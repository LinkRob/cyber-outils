'''Damage Dodge P''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can equip this badge by using two [[Badge Point|BP]], and while worn, Damage Dodge P allows Mario's partners to increase their defense points by one if they are using the defend action command in battle.

Damage Dodge P is found in two locations. The first of these badges is in [[The Great Tree]] in a red [[Question Block]] hovering over an invisible and hidden block in the room before the secret shop. Like [[Damage Dodge]], Damage Dodge P is found in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]]. The price is also just one hundred fifty [[coin]]s.

==Trivia==
*The [[Defend Plus P]] badge found in the [[Pirate's Grotto]] was originally a Damage Dodge P in the Japanese version.
*The Official Nintendo Player's Guide states on pages 60 and 108 that Damage Dodge P can be found in Pirate's Grotto, which is only true for the Japanese version of the game.
