'''Luigi's Mansion''' is a baseball stadium which appears in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It is also where the minigame [[Ghost K]] takes place.

==Description==
Luigi's Mansion takes place just outside the mansion of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|same name]]. Because it is haunted-themed, Luigi's Mansion can only be played at nighttime. The court's gimmick is the gravestones placed throughout the field. If a fielder approaches a gravestone, a [[ghost]] appears. There is tall grass within the field, and it can obscure the baseball if it lands there, making it harder for the fielders to find it. 

In Challenge mode, Luigi's Mansion can only be accessible after purchasing Luigi's [[Flashlight]] for 300 [[Coin]]s at the shop outside of Mario Stadium from one of the [[Pianta]]. It cannot be accessible at daytime.
