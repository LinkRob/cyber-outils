'''Holly Koopa''' is a [[List of characters|character]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. She is a female [[Koopa Troopa]] who appears on the board [[Shy Guy's Perplex Express]]. She appears to be rather wealthy and seems to be very conceited. If the player lands on the [[Event Space|Green Space]] in front of Holly, she asks the player if she is allowed to have a piece of [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] if they have any. If the player says "Yes," she pays them [[coin]]s in return. The number of coins she gives them is dependent on the cost of the Candy at the [[Candy Shop]]. If the player lands on the Green Space on top of the green passenger car, a [[Shy Guy]] appears, announcing that [[Bandit]] or [[Mowz]] has stolen Holly's Candy. The player must remember which car they last saw Bandit or Mowz riding in. If the player answers correctly, the Shy Guy awards them coins (20 coins in the [[Party Tent]], 10 in [[Star Battle Arena]]).

[[File:HollyKoopa.png|thumb|270px|left|Holly Koopa talking with [[Princess Daisy]]]]
Holly appears in the fourth stage of Star Battle Arena. Here, she plays a major part in the battle. On this stage, the player or the opponent wins by acquiring one [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]] from Holly. And the star is 50 coins (similar to the second stage [[Goomba's Booty Boardwalk]] and also similar to [[Pagoda Peak]] on Solo Cruise from ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' only the star is 100 coins). Whoever gets 50 coins and makes it there first gets a star from her (Holly). 

The Shy Guy conductor of the train states that he has a large portion of money and that he is a big fan of Holly; however, he is too shy to meet her. In Star Battle Arena, when the player meets the Shy Guy conductor, he gives the player ten coins to give to Holly.
