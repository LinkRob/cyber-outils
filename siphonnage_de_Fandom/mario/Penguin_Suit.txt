The '''Penguin Suit''' is a power-up item introduced in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==Main appearances==
===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[File:NSMBW Asset Model Penguin Suit.png|thumb|''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' model]]
This item is primarily found in [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]] and [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4]], and from then on it can be found in [[Toad House|Red Toad House]]s as well. The Penguin Suit allows players to turn into [[Penguin Mario|their Penguin form]]. [[Mario]] and [[Blue Toad]] have a blue body, [[Luigi]], [[Yellow Toad]], and [[Toadette]] have a navy blue body, and all have short, colored trousers. It gives players the ability to throw [[Ice Ball]]s at enemies, freezing them (just like the [[Ice Flower]]); this allows the frozen enemies to be used as platforms. In addition, the Penguin Suit lets players swim more efficiently underwater. The Penguin Suit also grants players the ability to slide on ice and snow indefinitely, on ground temporarily, and on water temporarily without sinking, as well as breaking any [[block]]s they collide with. Traction on ice is greatly improved as well. It is also able to defeat enemies, but will bounce off a sliding [[Cooligan]] or a [[Piranha Plant]] unhurt. It is introduced along with the [[Propeller Mushroom]] as the newest power-ups in the game.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U''===
The Penguin Suit reappears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and acts the same way it did in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', though the player can only get it through a Red Toad House in [[Superstar Road]]. Additionally, [[Penguin Bowling]] allows use of the Penguin Suit. It can also be used in the Penguin Pack from [[Boost Rush Mode]].

===''New Super Luigi U''===
In the expansion pack, ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', the Penguin Suit made more frequent appearances in courses including some outside Superstar Road.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', aside from its role in the two previous base games, the Penguin Suit can appear as a reward from [[Enemy Course]]s. [[Toadette]] and [[Nabbit]] also possess the Penguin Suit's swimming abilities, regardless of the form the former takes.

==Crossover appearances==
===''Mario & Sonic'' series===
==== ''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'' ====
The suit makes a cameo appearance in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' for the [[Wii]], as an unlockable outfit for [[Mii]]s.

==== ''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games'' ====
Like it’s predecessor, the Penguin Suit makes its return as a cameo appearance again as an unlockable outfit for Miis in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the [[Wii U]]. Penguin Suit can be available if the player upload results to the rankings from any event.

==== ''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'' ====
Like the previous two games, the Penguin Suit once again appears as cameo unlockable outfit for Miis in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' for the Wii U.

===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
The Penguin Suit appears in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'', representing the Water elemental orbs.

==Trivia==
*When a character obtains a Penguin Suit, the sound effect is slightly different from other power-ups. A sound similar to a duck quacking is played along with the normal power-up sound.
*In the Super Mario Mash-Up Pack in ''[[Minecraft]]'', Diamond Armor is replaced by the Penguin Suit.
*Concept art for ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' has the Penguin Suit as a [[List of New Super Mario Bros. Wii pre-release and unused content|Chicken Suit]].
