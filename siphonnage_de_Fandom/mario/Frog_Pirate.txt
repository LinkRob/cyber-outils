'''Frog Pirates'''<ref name=guide>''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide, page 127.</ref> are a species of frog in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They live in the swamps and wetlands of [[Yoshi's Island (Yoshi franchise)|Yoshi's Island]] and are found primarily in [[World 3 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 3]]. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] comes too close to one, it will lash out its tongue and try to steal [[Baby Mario]]. For this reason, Frog Pirates can become a nuisance as they can deplete Yoshi's [[Star Power]] rapidly so it’s recommended to tske them out quickly. Stepping on its head only disables it for a moment, so Yoshi should instead try to throw an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the frog or swallow it to truly defeat it. Spitting out a Frog Pirate will only make it fall off the screen, enabling it to respawn later.

The World 3 mid-boss [[Prince Froggy]] is based on this enemy, and unlike other bosses in the game, bears no visible differences to the enemy he is based on as he is never transformed by [[Kamek]] unlike most other bosses.

==Names in other languages==
*'''Chinese (Simplified):''' 呱呱蛙 (''Guāguā Wā''), meaning "Croaking Frog"
*'''German:''' Börki; diminutive form of ''Börk'' (frog's ribbit). The name is shared with the German name of the ''[[Yoshi's Story]]'' enemy [[Frog (Yoshi's Story)|Frog]].
*'''Italian:''' Ranocchio, meaning "Froggy"
*'''Japanese:''' ゲロゲーロ<ref name=Sho/><ref name=p16/> (''Gerogēro''); comes from「ゲロゲロ」(''gero-gero'', onomatopoeia for a frog's croaking). The name is identical to Prince Froggy's Japanese name.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese version of the game, Prince Froggy and Frog Pirates have the same name (''Gerogēro''). 
*In Prince Froggy's boss fight, it is implied that Frog Pirates like to eat [[Shy Guy|Shy-Guy]]s.
*Frog Pirates are among one of the few enemies from ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and [[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3|it’s remake]] to not return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.
*In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', there are creatures called [[Fleeper]]s that are reminiscent of Frog Pirates in a way, but look and act differently.
