'''Puzzle Panic''' is the last of [[Yoshi]]'s [[minigame]]s in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
Puzzle Panic is a harder variation of [[Puzzle Panel]], and it begins with a larger grid of panels. The player must match the Touch Screen picture with the top screen picture, and both pictures are made of panels with [[Super Mushroom|Mushroom]]s on the front and [[Cape Feather]]s on the back. Touching a panel with the [[stylus]] flips it over, as well as its surrounding panels. If the panels are flipped correctly, the two Yoshi statues on the left and right blast off, and the player advances to the next level. Subsequent levels require more panels to be flipped over.
