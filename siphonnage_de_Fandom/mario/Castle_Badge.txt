The '''Castle Badge''' is a [[badge]] featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Castle Badge can be bought from the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 75 [[coin]]s. Equipping it increases [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]'s [[Power|POW]] by 10. In its description, the Castle Badge is said to be for tourists.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Castle Badge is at a slightly higher price of 100 coins, and it becomes available at Beanbean Castle Town's clothes store after [[Queen Bean]] has been defeated. The Castle Badge can be sold for 25 coins. Aside from granting an increase in POW by 10, the Castle Badge also increases [[HP]] by 6.
