'''Shadow Mario Checks In''' is the seventh episode of [[Sirena Beach]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Objective==
This is one of several instances when [[Mario]] has to chase down [[Shadow Mario]] and spray him with [[F.L.U.D.D.|FLUDD]] enough times until Shadow Mario releases a [[Shine Sprite]] to collect.

The chase takes place in [[Hotel Delfino]], and as soon as Mario enters, Shadow Mario begins to run off. Shadow Mario moves between floors while being chased, excluding the attic, and he sometimes goes into open rooms. This episode specifically features [[Boo]]s who disguise themselves as Shadow Mario from afar, but reveal themselves if Mario comes close. A way to tell a disguised Boo apart is that it is slightly lighter than the actual Shadow Mario and does not carry a [[Magic Paintbrush]].

As usual, when Shadow Mario has been sprayed enough times, he falls over in defeat and leaves behind the episode's [[Shine Sprite]] for Mario to collect.
