The '''Super Mario 2021 Wall Calendar''' (also called '''2021 Super Mario Calendar''') is an officially licensed [[Nintendo]] product. It is a wall calendar designed by [[Celina Carvalho]] and published by Harry N. Abrams.

== Description ==
The wall calendar uses the format used in ''Super Mario'' games.

On the front cover, [[Luigi]], [[Mario]] and [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] are present from left to right.

On the April calendar section, [[Princess Peach]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]] and [[Toadette]] are present.

On the December calendar section, Mario, Luigi, Peach, Toad, Yoshi and [[Bowser]] wear their Christmas outfits with the fir tree present.
