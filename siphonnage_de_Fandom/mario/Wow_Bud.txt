'''Wow Buds'''<ref>https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/super-mario-bros-wonder-heres-a-few-tips-for-secret-seekers/</ref> are small bulbs appearing throughout ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. When touched, by either a player or another object, it activates and can give coins, move [[Warp Pipe|pipes]], reveal the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]], and do many other unexpected things, depending on the [[level]].
