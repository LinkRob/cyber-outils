The '''Necklace''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Necklace is worn by [[Madame Flurrie]] during the story. Flurrie loses it while [[Mario]] is in the [[Boggly Woods]], but Mario returns it to her after defeating the [[Shadow Sirens]]. Flurrie then joins Mario as the third member of his party.
