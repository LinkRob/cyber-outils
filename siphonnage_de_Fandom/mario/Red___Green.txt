[[File:Red and Green.jpg|thumb|Red (left) and Green (right).]]'''Red''' and '''Green''' are brothers, that operate the bridges in [[Yold Town]]. They both overly support their favorite colors. Their houses are just outside of the opposite sides of Yold Town. They got along well when they were young. Now, they get into arguments. Their designs are supposed to resemble [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].

==Red==
Red operates the wooden bridge from [[Mount Lineland]] to Yold Town. His favorite color is red. The color is found all over his house.

==Green==
Green operates the green suspension bridge from Yold Town to [[Yold Desert]]. His favorite color is green. The color is found all over his house. He seems to have a relationship with the [[Old Man Watchitt]] mayor of Yold Town.
