'''Quirk''' is a location in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Stars in Their Eyes]]."

==Description==
Quirk is a crimson planet. It has a desolate landscape consisting of red desert. However, there are several rivers, although they are filled with orange water. Quirk has low gravity, as [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] were shown to float upward when they stepped onto the planet's surface. The only inhabitants of Quirk are a green humanoid species of the [[Quirk (species)|same name]].
