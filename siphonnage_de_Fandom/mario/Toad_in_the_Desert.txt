The '''Toad in the Desert''' (also known as the '''Composer''') is a character in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
The Toad in the Desert is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] and a composer who lives in [[Dry Dry Outpost]] next to [[Little Mouser's Shop]]. He is very good in writing music, but admits he isn't as good without the [[Lyrics]]. During the events after Chapter 3, the [[Master Poet]] of [[Club 64]] in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] needed a composer to write his song with music keys. After getting the Lyrics from the Master Poet, [[Mario]] can then take it to Toad in the Desert who then turns the Lyrics into the [[Melody]].
