'''Flying Goombas''',<ref>M. Arakawa. Nintendo ''Mario Mania'' Player's Guide. Page 50.</ref> also named '''Winged Goombas''' or '''Paragaloombas''' are [[Galoomba]]s with wings, first appearing in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Profile==
Flying Goombas act much like [[Paragoomba]]s do; typically, they jump around using their wings, but some works such as the [[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World'' television series]] and ''[[Mario Party 3]]'' depict them as capable of full flight.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario World''====
[[File:SMW Flying Goomba Sprite.gif|frame|left]]
Flying Goombas first appear in ''Super Mario World'' and its [[reissue]], ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]'', where they alternate between walking and jumping around. Once [[stomp]]ed on, they change into wingless Goombas, like how Paragoombas turn into regular [[Goomba]]s when stomped. When walking, unlike normal Goombas, they will always walk towards the player's direction.

====''Super Mario Maker'' / ''Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS''====
Flying Goombas reappear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' under their current name and in ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', exclusively in the ''Super Mario World'' style, if the player puts [[wings]] on a Galoomba.

====''Super Mario Maker 2''====
Flying Goombas reappear in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. In addition to retaining their previous properties, by tapping and holding Paragaloombas in Course Maker, they can be changed into a winged, [[Goombrat]]-like [[Goombud]] variant.

===''Super Mario World'' television series===
[[File:SMW Cartoon Paragoombas.png|thumb|Para-Goombas in the cartoon episode "[[Fire Sale]]"]]
In the [[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World'' cartoons]], '''Para-Goombas'''<ref>"''Oh no! Para-Goombas!''" &ndash; Princess Toadstool, "Fire Sale"</ref> appear only once, in the show's series premiere, "[[Fire Sale]]," where several of them appear to attack [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Yoshi]]. This swarm of Para-Goombas is eventually defeated by Mario, who knocks them away after jumping through the air. These Para-Goombas appear to be an odd cross between Flying Goombas and the standard Para-Goombas of ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and later games, as they have the facial design and lack of stem of the former, yet their heads have a mushroom-like shape akin to the former.

===''Super Mario Adventures''===
In ''[[Super Mario Adventures]]'', a Flying Goomba makes a relatively minor appearance, being seen among the creatures sent by [[Bowser]] to attack [[Peach's Castle|Princess Toadstool's Palace]]. One is also seen among the attendees to Bowser's marriage to the hypnotized [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]].

===''Mario Party 3''===
In ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', Flying Goombas appear as obstacles in the minigame [[Ridiculous Relay]], slowing the player down when bumped into.

===''Mario Family''===
A Flying Goomba appears as a sewing pattern in ''[[Mario Family]]''.
