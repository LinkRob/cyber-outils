'''Picture Imperfect''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. Its name is a pun on the phrase "picture perfect."

==Gameplay==
Players have to remake the original picture of [[Mario]], [[Toad]], or [[Wiggler]] by hitting the blue [[! Block]] to stop the correct parts as they appear. Each set of parts flashes by more quickly than the last. The more accurate the picture is, the more [[point]]s the player will earn. The player(s) who has/have the most points at the end of the minigame win(s).
