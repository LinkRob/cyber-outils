'''Winged Bullies''' are a variant of [[Bully|Bullies]] in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. They can be created in Course Maker by giving a Bully a pair of [[wings]]. Winged Bullies have the ability to fly back onto ledges after being knocked off.
