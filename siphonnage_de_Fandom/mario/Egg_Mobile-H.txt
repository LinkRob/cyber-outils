The '''Egg Mobile-H''' is the first boss in the original ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog (16-bit)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'', appearing as [[Dr. Eggman]]'s [[w:c:sonic:Egg Mobile|Egg Mobile]] with a checkered wrecking ball attached to the underside. Its only ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]'' as the boss of the first stage of [[Tokyo Sky Flight]], where Dr. Eggman rides a giant version with his normal Egg Mobile nested inside.

At first, it swings the wrecking ball back-and-forth while flying around the screen, though after enough damage, the wrecking ball instead starts making full 360° loops around the machine. After the health bar has been depleted, the giant Egg Mobile-H is destroyed and Dr. Eggman escapes in his normal-sized Egg Mobile.
