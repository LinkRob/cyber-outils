'''World 1''' is the first [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. It is the only free world, and completing it unlocks [[World 2 (Super Mario Run)|World 2]].

==Levels==
===[[Up and Over]]===
Up and Over consists of [[Goomba]]s and Slope Slides. This level is a good introduction to slope sliding and vault jumping.

===[[Wall-Kicking It Underground]]===
Wall-Kicking It consists of Slope Slides, [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Goomba]]s, and Bounce Pads. The level is a good introduction to Wall-Kicking.

===[[Paratroopas in Mushroom Valley]]===
The level consists of [[Paratroopa]]s, [[Goomba]]s, and [[Mushroom Platform]]s. This level is a good introduction to using Paratroopas as platforms.

===[[Bowser's Castle Hangout]]===
This is the first castle you'll encounter. The area consists of [[Dry Bones]], [[Podoboo]]s, and a [[Fake Bowser|False Bowser]] at the end. The level is a simple guide to Bowser Battles, and when completed, gives you the necessary Toads required to get [[Blue Yoshi]] and [[Toadette]]. Beating the level again gives you 100 coins.
