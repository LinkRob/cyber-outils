The '''Gardener''' is a character who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Appearance==
The Gardener is an elderly [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with blue spots on his head. He also wears a green jacket.

==History==
The Gardener can be found in a lone house in [[Rose Town]]. The Gardener will tell a story on how he longed to get two items. [[Mario]] can give him the [[Seed (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Seed]] and [[Fertilizer]] to have the Gardener plant a large [[beanstalk]] leading to the very useful [[Lazy Shell]] weapon and armor.
