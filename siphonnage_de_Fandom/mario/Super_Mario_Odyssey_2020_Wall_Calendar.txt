The '''Super Mario Odyssey 2020 Wall Calendar''' (also called '''Super Mario Odyssey 2020 Calendar''') is an officially licensed [[Nintendo]] product. The wall calendar was designed by Celina Carvahlo and ABRAMS.

== Description ==
The stapled wall calendar is based off ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. The calendar is themed with various kingdoms found in ''Odyssey''. Most images feature Mario who is either in artworks or screenshots.
