'''Queen Hisstocrat''' is the mate of [[King Hisstocrat]] who appears as a boss in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. She acts the same as Hisstocrat except she attacks with fireballs as a pose to rocks and can also spew out fireballs from her mouth.

Queen Hisstocrat appears in [[Hisstocrat Returns]] that is located in [[World Bowser]], which is one of the two main boss levels that must be completed in order to progress to the final boss level. Is either the second/third-to-last or third/penultimate boss of that world and is overall either the fourteenth/third-to-last or fifteenth/penultimate boss of ''Super Mario 3D World''. She is later fought again in [[Boss Blitz]], alongside Hisstocrat, and can be fought as either the first, second, third, fourth/third-to-last or fifth/penultimate boss. 

==Physical description==
As her name suggest, Queen Hisstocrat is a pink version of Hisstocrat and when she attacks she has a pink minions, in addition to her having a pink cap with a diamond-shape belt, she also wears a larger silver colored crown on her head and has eye-lashes. Much like Hisstocrat previously, Queen Hisstocrat is in the ground so Player(s) never see the bottom side of her 

== Attacks ==
*'''Lava Throw''':  Queen Hisstocrat will emerge from the ground while fireballs are thrown in the air that will fall onto the player.
*'''Snake Hiss''': Queen Hisstocrat will make a music hiss and call multiple snakes to assist her in his battle, however she is only helping the player to attack him.
*'''Fireballs:''' At random directions, Queen Hisstocrat can spew out fireballs from her mouth.

== Battle ==
Strategy-wise, Queen Hisstocrat is fought very similarity to Hisstocrat, she battles alongside her smaller snake minions that can be used by [[Mario]] and the others to jump up to the large boss. Tho unlike Hisstocrat, Queen Hisstocrat can spew out fireballs, in addition to digging into the ground and send fiery balls at the player(s). Like Hisstocrat previously, she can also have her snake minions attack the player(s) directly. The [[Super Bell]] is useful here as it can be used to climb up easily and jump onto Queen Hisstocrat's head. After three hits, Queen Hisstocrat and her minions will spin a few times and explode.

== Trivia ==
* Given Queen Hisstocrat's name and how in the Boss Blitz level she is fought alongside Hisstocrat, its highly possible that she has a connection to Hisstocrat, tho its unknown as to what relationship they have, either be a son and mother relationship(which is highly possible given the fact that Queen Hisstocrat has more devastating attacks, has a larger crown, and how she has the word "Queen" in her name) or they are both a couple (husband and wife), one possibility is that both Hisstocrat and Queen Hisstocrat are siblings, or they could be friends.
* Queen Hisstocrat shares many parallel similarities to [[Fiery Dino Piranha]] of ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'':
** They are both bosses that have an appearance to a reptile (Fiery Dino Piranha: T-Rex, Queen Hisstocrat: Snake).
** They both are stronger versions of the previous boss fought earlier in the game (Fiery Dino Piranha: [[Dino Piranha]], Queen Hisstocrat: Hisstocrat).
** Both bosses can spew out fireballs from their mouths.
** If fought in order, they both serve as the penultimate bosses of that game.
* Queen Hisstocrat also shares many parallel similarities to [[Fiery Gobblegut]] from [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|''Super Mario Galaxy 2'']]:
** They are both bosses that have an appearance to a serpent reptile-like creature (Fiery Gobblegut: Dragon, Queen Hisstocrat: Snake).
** They both are stronger versions of the previous boss fought earlier in the game (Fiery Gobblegut: [[Gobblegut]], Queen Hisstocrat: Hisstocrat).
** Both bosses can spew out fireballs from their mouths.
** If fought in order, they both serve as the penultimate bosses of that game.
** Both also appear in a game that is a fallow-up/sequel to that previous game.
* Queen Hisstocrat shares some qualities to [[Pom Pom]], in that both are female versions of the previous boss (Pom Pom: [[Boom Boom]], Queen Hisstocrat: Hisstocrat) and both have a form of a projectile (Queen Hisstocrat: Fireballs, Pom Pom: Shurikens).
** Additionally, they both appear in ''Super Mario 3D World''.
* In the 2010 Xbox 360 video game made by Capcom, ''[[w:c:lostplanet:Lost Planet 2|Lost Planet 2]]'', there are a duo of bosses called [[w:c:lostplanet:Undeep|Undeep]], which they share many parallel similarities to:
** They both are large snake-like creatures that submerge underground.
** Both bosses can fire projectiles that is colored orange (Queen Hisstocrat: Fireballs, Undeep: Energy Bombs).
** They both serve as the penultimate bosses (assuming if Queen Hisstocrat is fought in order).
** Both bosses are fought as a duo (with Queen Hisstocrat being fought alongside Hisstocrat in the Boss Blitz level).
** They both appear in a game that serves as a fallow-up/sequel to the previous game.
