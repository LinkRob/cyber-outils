|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Wild Card''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Jester]]s.

==Effects==
When Wild Card is used, a bunch of cards is thrown at a single character dealing a set amount of damage. Wild Card is very similar to the other card-related move, [[Full House]].
