'''Blue Talking Flowers''' appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' in levels with [[ON/OFF Switch]]es. They appear when the ON/OFF Switch is set to off. If the ON/OFF switch is on, they appear as [[Talking Flower (Red)|red]]. Blue ones tend to have more cocky and/or mysterious personalities, often coming off as condescending when they assume that the player isn’t smart enough to complete puzzles.
