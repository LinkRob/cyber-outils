'''Rhomps''' are rolling species of [[Thwomp]]s which first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They are found in the sandslide area of the [[Slipsand Galaxy]]. If [[Mario]] is rolled over by one, he immediately loses a life. They are very similar to the [[Spindel]]s with similar shape, the same method of attack, and both are found in desert-like areas.
