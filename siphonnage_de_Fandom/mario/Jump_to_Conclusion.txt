'''Jump to Conclusion''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Opening==
[[File:Jump to Conclusion.png|thumb|200px|left|The opening scene]]
The [[finish line]] is shown, then the camera then quickly pans to the start as the [[Piranha Creeper]]s start moving.
{{br|left}}
==Gameplay==
The characters have to jump on a set of stairs consisting of 45 steps, each in their own lane, to the [[finish line]]. The bottom screen shows how many steps each character has before making it to the end. They can either do a small jump to advance one step up or a big jump to go two steps up; the reason for the two options is because Piranha Creepers appear near certain steps, and whoever jumps onto one is temporarily stunned. The first character to reach the finish line is the winner.

Jump to Conclusion and [[Dodge Fuzzy, Get Dizzy]] are the only Free-for-All minigames in ''Mario Party: Star Rush'' to have a record system. In the case of Jump to Conclusion, the record is the fastest time that the winner takes to reach the finish line.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|B}}: Big jump
*{{button|3ds|A}}: Small jump

==Flavor text==
*Instructions: ''Be first to jump your way to the finish line!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is derived from the saying "jump to conclusion," which means a hasty conclusion based on limited facts.
