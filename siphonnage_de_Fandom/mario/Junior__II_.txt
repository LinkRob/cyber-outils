'''''Junior (II)<ref>{{cite manual|game=Donkey Kong Jr. Math |language=English |page=5}}</ref>''''' (also known as '''''Pinkly Kong''''' or '''''Junior Kong''''' by fans) is the pink equivalent of [[Donkey Kong Jr. (character)|Donkey Kong Jr.]] controlled by player two in ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. Math]]'' and its re-releases, including the port featured on ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Doubutsu no Mori|Doubutsu no Mori]]'', its overseas conversion titled ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Animal Crossing (game)|Animal Crossing]]'', and its enhanced variation titled ''[[w:c:animalcrossing:Doubutsu no Mori e+|Doubutsu no Mori e+]]''. Here, Junior (II) and Donkey Kong Jr. compete to solve various equations of either addition, subtraction, multiplication, or division.

Junior (II) has all the same abilities as the main Donkey Kong Jr., and his only difference is his pink fur. In the game manual, due to the print being in black and white, both Juniors have the same sprite-based artwork. Unlike Donkey Kong Jr., Junior (II)'s relation to [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], let alone Donkey Kong Jr. himself, has never been established.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Junior (II) is named after Donkey Kong Jr. and has the number two written in Roman numeral form within parentheses, to indicate that he is Player 2's character. 

On the same page as Junior (II) in ''Donkey Kong Jr. Math''<nowiki/>'s instruction booklet, Donkey Kong Jr. himself is titled Junior (I), with a one written in roman numeral form. Given that the identifiers are used to denote who is controlled by which player, it is possible that the two Juniors actually share a name, given that the name Junior is ambiguously used throughout the manual.

==Trivia==
*It is unknown if his name is said "Junior Two" or "Junior the Second" due to the fact that ''II'' can mean either. 
*Junior (II)'s status as being the second player's character whose only distinction is its color is similar to how [[Luigi]] started out as a palette swap of [[Mario]] in ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''.
*It is possible that [[Donkey Kong]]'s pink costume in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' and pink fur option in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' are based of Junior (II).
