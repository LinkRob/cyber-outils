'''Hammer Throw''' is a move in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
Hammer Throw is a fielding move that can only be used by [[Hammer Bro]]. The player can have Hammer Bro perform a Hammer Throw by pressing {{button|wii|A}} when he is near a ball that is otherwise out of reach. In doing so, Hammer Bro throws a [[hammer]] at the ball, which it catches and returns to the Hammer Bro.
