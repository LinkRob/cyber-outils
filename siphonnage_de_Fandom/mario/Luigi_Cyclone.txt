'''Luigi Cyclone''' is the down special move used by [[Luigi]] in every {{series|Super Smash Bros.}} game. The move first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]''.

==Description==
The move is similar to [[Mario]]'s down special move, [[Mario Tornado]]. Upon using Luigi Cyclone, Luigi spins around, drawing in nearby opponents, before flinging them up into the air.
