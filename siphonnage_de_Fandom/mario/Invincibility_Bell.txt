The '''Invincibility Bell''' is a power-up item in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It can be obtained the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' segments of the game by scanning the [[Cat Mario]] [[amiibo]] and sometimes you’ll get it by scanning the [[Cat Peach]] [[amiibo]],but it’s random so don’t know when you’ll get it with her.

== Description ==
The Invincibility Bell is a variation of the [[Super Bell]], which it resembles but has white horizontal stripes connecting vertically on the front and back of it. Upon being used, an Invincibility Bell transforms [[Mario]] or another playable character into their [[White Cat Mario|White Cat]] form. The Invincibility Bell grants the same abilities as the [[Cat Mario|Cat]] form, in addition to [[Super Star|invincibility]] from most enemies and obstacles, in the same manner that the [[Invincibility Leaf]] grants not only the [[Super Leaf]]'s abilities but also invincibility.

The invincibility does not protect against [[lava]], [[Poisoned Water]], [[pit]]s, or being crushed. In ''Bowser's Fury'', it can protect Mario to the extent of being able to touch [[Fury Bowser]].
