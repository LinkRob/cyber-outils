'''Slow Go''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. [[Mario]] can wear this badge for zero [[Badge Point|BP]]. Wearing Slow Go slows Mario's movement in the overworld, although it does not affect his movement during battle.

== In ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ==
[[File:Slow Go (Paper Mario).png|frame|left]]
Slow Go is found in [[Dry Dry Ruins]]. The room in the ruins this badge is located in is the room that holds the [[Super Hammer]]; in which Mario can find a very secret room found on the way down to the spot with the hammer. Mario, standing near the wall on the north side, can fall down onto the railing of the room, and be able to traverse his way to the left wall. At the bottom of this wall, on the south side, Mario can find a hidden room with this badge in it by going through the wall. The Slow Go badge can be helpful while trying to walk past the sleeping [[Clubba]]s of [[Tubba Blubba's Castle]].

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
Mario can obtain this badge from [[Charlieton]]. Slow Go retains its zero BP cost, and still has no effect on Mario's movement in battle.
