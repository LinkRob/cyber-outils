'''Grinders''',<ref name=guide>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide, page 127, and ''Tetris Attack''.</ref> sometimes simply referred to as '''monkeys''',<ref>''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' stage titles and ''Yoshi's Island DS'' World 2 introduction.</ref> are [[Ukiki|monkey]] enemies that first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They share their name with a [[Grinder (obstacle)|certain sawblade enemy]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]''. They have the ability to climb [[vine]]s, and are one of the enemies who can directly steal [[Baby Mario]] from [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. However, some Grinders may run away from Yoshi upon sighting him. There are also several sub-types of Grinders called [[Seedy Sally]]s (which spit [[Melon]] seeds at Yoshi) and [[Short Fuse]]s (which drop grenades on Yoshi). They are commonly found in [[World 3 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 3]].

==History==
=== ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''/''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'' ===
[[Image:Grinder World.png|thumb|left|Yoshi approaches two fleeting Grinders.]]
Grinders are first seen in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and later appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. Grinders are usually found hopping around and often times flee from [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] as he approaches them (using the terrain to their advantage), Grinders can also climb vines and they tend to climb as high as possible to escape [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]. Some Grinders can also hop towards [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and steal [[Baby Mario]] from him forcing [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to retrieve the baby. Some Grinders are also seen swinging in chain forms attempting to hit [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] as he comes by. Grinders are also capable of swimming in water, upon falling in a lake of water, they attempt to float up to the surface and from here, they start swimming to land (Grinders cannot be beaten in water (unless Yoshi swallows one)). Grinders themselves can be beaten by being swallowed, a Grinder can also be stunned with an egg toss or a stomp and from here, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can beat the KO'd Grinder by running into it (upon being hit, the Grinder will turn a darker brown), there are two other types of Grinders called [[Seedy Sally|Seedy Sallies]]<ref name=guide2>Miller, Kent, and Terry Munson. ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' Player's Guide, page 128.</ref> and [[Short Fuse]]s<ref name=guide2/> (which spit [[Watermelon]] seeds and drop [[Needlenose]]s/[[Grenade]]s respectively, Grinders can be found all over [[World 3 (Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island)|World 3]].

===''Tetris Attack''===
[[Image:Grinder Confused.png|thumb|right|A Tetris Attack Grinder.]]
Grinders appear in ''[[Tetris Attack]]'', in this game, they are seen alongside [[Poochy]] as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s opponents. Due to [[Kamek]] brainwashing them with his magic, they tried to stop [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] on his adventure to beat [[Kamek]]. Once the Grinder is defeated, he and [[Poochy]] are freed from Kamek's dark magic and are unlocked as playable characters that help [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] on his mission.


=== ''Yoshi's Island DS'' ===
[[Image:Grinder Jungle.png|thumb|left|Yoshi near some Grinders.]]
Grinders also appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. In this game, they can be found in levels of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]]. They have a major role in [[Baby DK, the Jungle King!]] where they kidnap [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Peach]] forcing Yoshi and [[Baby Donkey Kong]] to rescue them. In this game, Grinders don't try to attack Yoshi instead, they constantly run away from him (sometimes climbing vines), additionally, running into them does nothing this time. In game, they also don't try to steal the baby from Yoshi at all, rather constantly running away from him making them more as an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]-supply in this game rather than an actual enemy. Grinders can be beaten immediately with swallows or [[DK Dash Attack]]s and can also be stunned with eggs (stunning a Grinder once again makes it a darker brown). A KO'd Grinder can be beaten by running into it. Grinders can also swim in lakes of water however this time, they can also swim below the water's surface. Grinders can also be made to appear in the [[Museum]] by hitting them with an egg, they appear in the Land Enemy Exhibit.

===''Yoshi's New Island''===
[[Image:Grinder Beach.png|thumb|right|A Grinder hoppingon the slippery sands as Yoshi approaches.]]
Grinders return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' retaining their behavior from previous games, in this game, they once again attack by jumping at [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and attempting to steal [[Baby Mario]] from him, they can also run away from him and climb across the vines to escape. Like in earlier games, Grinders can swim in lakes of water however this time, upon falling in, they will act like they're drowning before regaining momentum and swimming back to land. Grinders can be stunned with egg tosses and stomps and from here, they can be ran into for a quick defeat. Grinders can be slurped as well but unlike earlier games, they cannot be completely swallowed and turned into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] similar to a [[Bumpty]]. Grinders commonly appear in [[World 3 (Yoshi's New Island)|World 3]]. [[Seedy Sally]]s and [[Short Fuse]]s also return in this game.

===''Yoshi's Woolly World / Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''===
[[Image:Yarn Grinders.png|thumb|left|Yoshi (right) blends in with two Grinders.]]
Grinders are seen again in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. In these games, they can commonly be found in tropical-themed levels such as [[Junge of the Apes]]. They once again flee from [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] as he approaches them using the terrain to their advantage, Grinders once again cannot be swallowed and turned into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] (but they can still be slurped). Grinders can be beaten with [[Yarn Ball]]s or stomps, their relatives the [[Seedy Sally]]s and [[Short Fuse]]s also return in this game.

===''Yoshi's Crafted World''===
[[Image:Grinder Crafted.png|thumb|right|Yoshi approaching a Grinder.]]
Grinders also appear in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''. In this game, they can be seen in levels such as [[Jumping to Victory]] and [[Bombs Away on Pirate Island]], Grinders retain their behavior from previous games (fleeing from [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] as he approaches) and are commonly seen climbing on vines, Grinders can be beaten with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] tosses, stomps, or swallows. [[Short Fuse]]s also return, new sub-types of Grinders (resembling a [[Kong]]) called [[Uckykong]]s and [[Snow Ucky Kong]]s appear. Unlike earlier games, Grinders can be beaten by being spat into water.
