'''Body Slam''' is a move used by both [[Bombette]] and [[Madame Flurrie]] in the games ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' respectively. Both moves are basic attacks that require no [[Flower Point|FP]] to use, and involve the user thrusting her body into an enemy, meaning that attempting to use this attack on spiked enemies will cause damage to the partner.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario''===
To use Body Slam in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', the player must hold the {{button|n64|a}} button until the gauge fills and Bombette will slam into the enemy. This is useful when fighting the [[Koopa Bros.]]

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
To use Body Slam in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the player must move the cursor around onto the enemy and, after about 5 seconds, Flurrie will slam down onto the enemy. This can even hit ceiling enemies.

==Trivia==
*The different ways to use Body Slam references between the games.
**The way to use Body Slam in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' is the same as [[Admiral Bobbery]]'s [[Bomb]] from from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
**The way to use Body Slam in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' is the same how to use [[Lakilester]]'s [[Spiny Flip]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
*Other bosses, such as [[Tubba Blubba]], [[Macho Grubba]], and [[Bowser]] also use the Body Slam move.
*The Body Slam might be a reference to the move from the ''Pokémon'' games.
