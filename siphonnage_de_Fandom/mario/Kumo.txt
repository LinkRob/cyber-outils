'''Kumos''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. Kumos are large spider enemies that can only be found in levels of the [[Easton Kingdom]].

==History==
Kumos are often found hopping around attempting to damage [[Mario]]. A Kumo can be defeated by either jumping on it or tossing two [[Superball]]s at it. Defeating a Kumo earns the player 400 [[point]]s.
