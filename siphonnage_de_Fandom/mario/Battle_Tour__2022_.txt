'''Battle Tour''' is the eightienth tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']] which began on October 5, 2022 and ended on October 18, 2022. It was rerun from October 4, 2023 to October 17, 2023 as the game's hundred-and-sixth tour overall and the first tour following the end of new in-game content. It was rerun a second time from October 2, 2024 to October 15, 2024 as the hundred-and-thirty-second tour, and is being rerun a third time from October 1, 2025 to October 14, 2025 as the hundred-and-fifty-eighth tour.

The tour features Battle modes in single and multiplayer, mainly [[Balloon Battle]]. For this mode, battle tracks are introduced, featureing [[SNES Battle Course 1]] and [[Cookie Land]]. The tour also introduced food themes, featureing Chef Luigi and food themed karts and gliders. The tour also brought a remix track of [[Donut Plains 1 (track)|Donut Plains 1]] which is called [[RMX Donut Plains 1]].  This tour was also part of the autumn celebration event where players could recieve [[Green Speeder]] and [[Toad (Pit Crew)|Red Toad (Pit Crew)]].

Fromthe start of this tour, the player level has been increased to level 400 maximum, the tier rank has been increased to 99 maximum, the online player grade has been increased to S+9 maximum, the base-point cap for drivers, karts, and gliders has been increased, many items were added to the Daily Selects, and tour gifts now mainly contain free pipe launches. Premium Challenges++ have also been added to the game. The number of cups in each tour has also been raised to fifteen.

This was the first tour to not have the regular weekly pipes that could be fired by rubies and contained spotlight items, which were replaced by the Spotlight Shop introduced in this tour, including the removal of the spotlight items in the regular weekly pipe being raised to a higher shelf in the second or third course of cups during their featured tour, making Mii Racing Suits the only ones that can be raised to a higher shelf. Due to the major changes, the tutorial for new players was also altered.

== Trivia ==

* Due to this, every race course in the tour had all four variants appear, making this the first tour to feature all four variants of every race course that appeared in it.
* As of this tour's debut, a large number of drivers, karts, and gliders, including [[Kamek]], [[Tanooki Mario (character)|Tanooki Mario]], [[List of characters in Mario Kart Tour/Birdo|Birdo (White)]], the [[Tropical Truck]], the [[Goo-Goo Buggy]], the [[Luigi Parafoil]], and the [[Rose Paraso]]<nowiki/>l, are available in regular tour pipes, including the All-Clear Pipe.
* The eleventh set of [[Expert Challenges]] was made available at the start of this tour in 2022 and lasted until the end of the [[Holiday Tour (2022)|2022 Holiday Tour]].
