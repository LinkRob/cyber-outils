==Effects==
Emergency Rations are rather special items as they normally restore 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] (and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]) but while in the Flipside or Flopside Pit of 100 Trails, they restore 50 HP. In order to make an Emergency Ration, one must bring a [[Shroom Shake]] and a [[Fire Burst]] to [[Dyllis]]. Emergency Rations are very useful for one trying to complete the Pit of 100 Trails (especially the Flopside version as it must be completed twice).
