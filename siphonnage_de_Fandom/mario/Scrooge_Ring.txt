'''Scrooge Ring''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When the user equips the Scrooge Ring, they use only half the [[Flower Point|FP]] required when using a special attack. The Scrooge Ring can be obtained from [[Frogfucius' Student]] in [[Seaside Town]] for 50 [[Frog Coin]]s.
