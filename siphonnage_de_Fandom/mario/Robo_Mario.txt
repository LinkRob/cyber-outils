'''Robo Mario''' is a robotic counterpart of [[Mario]] who appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]''.

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
Robo Mario has a silver face, a black and silver body, a yellow nose, and yellow eyes. In his artwork, Robo Mario has the [[Gadd Science, Inc.]] logo on his cap, suggesting that he was created by [[Professor Elvin Gadd]].

==In ''Mario Kart Arcade GP''==
Robo Mario appears in the challenge game in Rainbow Cup, which occurs on [[Rainbow Coaster]]. The objective is to beat Robo Mario while dodging his attacks. There are no Item Boxes in this course, so while the player cannot attack Robo Mario, Robo Mario can spawn an infinite number of items to attack the player. When Robo Mario is in the lead, he drops Bananas to slow down the player. If the player is in the lead, Robo Mario either uses a [[Green Shell]] or a Black Shell to attack his opponent.

==In ''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''==
Robo Mario makes a reappearance, having the same role as in its predecessor. He is found on the challenge game on Rainbow Coaster.

== Trivia ==
*There is a discrepancy regarding the emblem on Robo Mario's kart—official artwork depicts the kart with [[Professor Elvin Gadd|Professor E. Gadd]]'s logo, while the in-game model shows it with [[Mario]]'s logo, because the same gold kart model is used for both Mario and Robo Mario.
