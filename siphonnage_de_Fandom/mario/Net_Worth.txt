'''Net Worth''' is a [[Co-op Minigame|Co-op]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. Its name is a pun of "net worth", a term in accounting.

== Opening ==
The camera shows [[Cheep Cheep]]s and [[Gold Cheep Cheep]]s swimming and jumping and the camera pans to the players with the fishing net.

== Gameplay ==
The players must catch as many [[Cheep Cheep|Cheep Cheeps]] to gain points. Regular Cheep Cheeps are worth one point while Gold Cheep Cheeps are worth three points. The players hold each corner of the net. The players must thrust the Joy-Con towards themselves to raise the net at the same signal. The Cheep Cheeps will be flicked to the inflatable pool.

== Ending ==
The Cheeps Cheeps they caught are swimming in the pool. The players do their victory animations for their success, or they do their losing animation for their loss.
