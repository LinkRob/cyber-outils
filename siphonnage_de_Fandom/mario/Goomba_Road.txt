'''Goomba Road''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Layout==
Goomba Road is a path leading from [[Goomba Village]] to [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] with many [[Goomba]]s found as enemies.

==History==
After finding the [[hammer]] and destroying the [[Yellow Block]] in Goomba Village, [[Mario]] must head east towards Toad Town through Goomba Road. While making his way down the path, Mario will come across two signs with a [[Mushroom (Paper Mario series)|Mushroom]] mounted on them, when read the first reveals a Mushroom while the second reveals a Goomba hidden behind the Mushroom. On this path, Mario encounters two Goombas, a [[Red and Blue Goombas|Red one and a Blue one]]. After the defeated pair flees, Mario continues forth and soon encounters the [[Goomba King]] in his [[Goomba King's Fortress|castle]]. The Goomba King and the pair of Goombas enter a fight with Mario. After the trio of Goombas are defeated, the Goomba King converses with the Red Goomba about the switch opening the bridge to Toad's Town.
