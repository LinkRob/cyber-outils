A '''Special Item''' is a number of items only found in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. Each Special Item is only usable by one or two certain characters. Although every Special Item can only be used by two characters, the unlockable [[King Boo]] and [[Petey Piranha]] can use any Special Item.

*[[Fireball]] and [[Green Fireball]]

*[[Yoshi Egg]]

''[[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]]:''
*[[Bowser Shell]]

''[[Wario]] and [[Waluigi]]:''
