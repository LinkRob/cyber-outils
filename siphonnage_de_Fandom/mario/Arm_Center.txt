*[[Starlow]]}}'''Arm Center''' is an area of [[Bowser]]'s right arm in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] visit this location on a quadrumvirate of occasions - the difficulty will progressively increase from task-to-task - in order to stimulate the muscles, resulting in an increase in strength, which will enable [[Bowser]]'s ability to perform various tasks and will result in the progression of the storyline.

The mini-game requires [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] to hit the balls of light back at [[Bowser]]'s arm muscles; [[Mario]] will have to hit the red balls with his hammer and [[Luigi]] will have to hit the green balls with his hammer.
