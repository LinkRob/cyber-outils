'''Snowy''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by [[Mallow]] once he reaches level 14.

==Effects==
When Snowy is used, a large snowman appears on the screen damaging all enemies for ice damage. This move is very useful against the [[Megasmilax]] fight in [[Bean Valley]]. Snowy is also useful against dealing with fire-based enemies especially those in [[Barrel Volcano]].

==Trivia==
*Snowy may be inspiration for the [[Snowman Doll]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' considering both summons a large snowman that damages all enemies on the screen for ice damage.
