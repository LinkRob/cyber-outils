'''Dinosaur Fish'''<ref>{{cite web |url=[:File:NoA Email VBWL names.png{{!}}Nintendo Customer Support]] |title=email |accessmonthday=May 1 |accessyear=2023 |last=MacFarlane |first=Brian |authorlink= |coauthors= |date=January 14, 2013 |format=screenshot|language=English}}</ref> is the first boss in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. He wields a spiked ball and chain for a weapon.

==In ''Virtual Boy Wario Land''==
[[File:Dinosaur Fish battle.png|thumb|left|The boss fight against the Dinosaur Fish]]
[[Wario]] fights Dinosaur Fish at the end of [[Stage 4 (Virtual Boy Wario Land)|Stage 4]]. At first, the Dinosaur Fish is at the cave in the background, where he tosses his spiked ball and chain across a pool at Wario three times. After this, Dinosaur Fish jumps across to where Wario is and swings his spiked ball and chain back and forth to try and hit Wario. Throughout the battle, Wario has to avoid Dinosaur Fish's spiked ball and chain.

To damage Dinosaur Fish, Wario must jump onto his head. Each time it is stomped, Dinosaur Fish retreats back into his cave, where he swings his ball and chain at Wario again. Wario can defeat Dinosaur Fish by jumping on his head a total of three times.
