'''Audrey Sourdive''' is a French voice actress. She provides the voice of [[Princess Peach (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Princess Peach]] in the European French dub of The Super Mario Bros. Movie. In addition, she voices [[Edge]] in the French version of ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.
