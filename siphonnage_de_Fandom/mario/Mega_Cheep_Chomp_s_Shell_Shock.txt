'''Mega Cheep Chomp's Shell Shock''' is a boss minigame in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''. It takes place in the [[Whimsical Waters]] and pits players against an oversized [[Cheep Chomp]] named [[Mega Cheep Chomp]]. As with the other mid-boss minigames, this minigame is available at a mid-boss fort, with a guarantee of appearing in the aforementioned Whimsical Waters.

== Introduction ==
The [[Mega Cheep Chomp]] surges from the water, snapping his jaws and surprising the player characters with his entrance. He then turns to face one character.

== Gameplay ==
{{sectstub}}
The characters will have to attack the Mega Cheep Chomp by shooting [[Koopa Shell|Koopa Shells]] at him. Each Koopa Shell that hits the creature will make him turn ninety degrees clockwise (causing him to face player 1 first, then 2, then 4, then 3, and back to 1) and gives one point. The Mega Cheep Chomp will charge at the character in his line of sight and make that character lose points one the timer runs out. This cannot be avoided. In the second section of the fight, some [[Cheep Cheeps]] start circling the Mega Cheep Chomp's location at the centre, acting as shields for his body and blocking one Koopa Shell apiece before getting knocked out. The Mega Cheep Chomp has 64 hit points.
{{Mario Party stub}}

== Ending ==
The Mega Cheep Chomp retreats into the water but then surges back up for a bit before his body explodes.

== Trivia ==

* This is one of four boss minigames throughout the entire [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'']] franchise where at least one character is bound to get hurt, the other three being [[Whomp Stomp]], [[Spike Strike]], and [[Chain Chomp Romp]]. Unlike the first three, which are from ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', this one is from ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.
