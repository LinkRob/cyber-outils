The '''Blue Key''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Blue Key is, as the name states, is a small blue key.

==History==
The Blue Key can be found in a room in [[Allergia Gardens]] that requires the use of [[Arty Wario]]'s blocks. The Blue Key can open the door of the same color to access a new area of Allergia Gardens. It is also one of two colored keys that can be found in the level with the other two being the [[Yellow Key]] and the [[Red Key (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Red Key]].
