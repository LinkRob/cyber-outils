'''Gold Medals''' are an item in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''.

==Description==
A Gold Medal is a type of [[Happiness Medal]] that [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] receives from a [[Yoshi Island Spirits|Spirit]] for completing a mission with a higher score than is required. A Gold Medal is essentially an upgrade over a [[Silver Medal]]. Collecting a Gold Medal also provides a better chance at winning the [[Bonus Chance!]]. If Yoshi has earned a Gold Medal in every [[level]], the player unlocks the Bonus Game [[Shuffle Mode EX]].
