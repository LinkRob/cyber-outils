'''Amy Rose''' is a pink female anthropomorphic hedgehog from the ''[[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' franchise who has a huge crush on [[Sonic the Hedgehog]]. She primarily makes her appearances in the ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' installments as one of the participants in the featured Olympic events for each entry as a playable character for Team Sonic. 

==In ''Mario & Sonic'' series==
===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games''===
[[File:Amy Rose M&SATOG artwork.jpg|thumb|x200px|left|Artwork of Amy for ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games'']]
In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', Amy appears as an All-Around character, featuring good skill and stamina stats, average speed, and below average power.

Amy utilizes some sort of heart power similar to Peach for her special moves. In the [[Wii]] version for Amy’s [[Dream Table Tennis (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games for Wii)|Dream Table Tennis]] special move, she laughs and hits the ball over the table covered with hearts with a "Go!". It hovers above the table for a second, then plummets down. This move is quite easy to return, but if it isn't it will probably knock the opponent over. In Amy’s [[fencing]] special move, she lunges forward, propelled by her heart power in one powerful jab.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''===
In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', Amy appears again as an All-Around character. Her special move has her spinning around with a bunch of hearts saying "Okay". In the Adventure Mode of the DS version, Amy is found in [[Sparkleton]] where she will join the player's team if the player beats her in [[Ski Shooting]] (Dream Biathlon). In [[Frostown]], there is a mechanism that requires Amy to hit it with her [[w:c:sonic:Piko Piko Hammer|Piko Piko Hammer]].

===''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games''===
[[File:Amy Gymnastics M&SATLOG artwork.png|thumb|x250px|Artwork of Amy for ''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'']]
Amy makes her next appearance in the Wii [[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|version]] of ''Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games'', returning as an All-Around type playable. Like the other female athletes, she has a new outfit worn for [[Gymnastics]] and [[Aquatics]] [[Event]]s, while keeping her winter outfit for Equestrian and wearing her sports outfit from the original game for all others. This is the first ''Mario & Sonic'' game to feature Amy being voiced by Cindy Robinson instead of Lisa Ortiz. In the game's opening, Amy appears with all of the other athletes at the start, as well as competing in [[Synchronized Swimming]] and [[100m Freestyle (Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games for Wii)|100m Freestyle]]. Amy also appears as one of the characters that can be spoken to in order to play minigames and win stickers in London Party mode.

In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|version]], Amy is included in the Girls group, meaning that she can only be played as in certain events. In the Story Mode, Amy first appears when she finds [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]] in [[Hyde Park]], and the pair are later attacked by [[imposter]]s, causing them to collapse. They are later found by [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Blaze the Cat|Blaze]] and three [[Toad (species)|Toads]]. After the purple Toad mentions seeing [[Rouge the Bat|Rouge]], the group find her and help her recover from being brainwashed. Rouge then leads them to the [[fog machine]] in Hyde Park, which Blaze destroys, and Amy, the rest of the girls, the Toads and Rouge decide to stay there to keep an eye on it. Amy is later mentioned by [[Knuckles the Echidna|Knuckles]] and [[Vector the Crocodile|Vector]], who explain to Sonic that they had come looking for [[Bowser]] on a tip from Amy. Amy appears to be calling [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], Sonic and [[Miles "Tails" Prower|Tails]] to offer support when they face Bowser and [[Dr. Eggman]], as well as appearing at the opening ceremony. In the bonus episodes, Amy appears to be helping Peach, Blaze and the Toads preparing the invitations for the Olympic Games, and challenges Blaze to an event so that she can deliver Sonic's invitation to him early. Although she wins the challenge, she stays behind anyway after Blaze accidentally scatters the invitations, and ends up rewriting Dr. Eggman's. When Daisy arrives, Amy tries to join her on a shopping trip, but after the pair are defeated in an event, they both stay behind to help finish the job. In the final episode, Bowser and Dr. Eggman arrive to try to get their invitations, but after Amy and Peach defeat them trying to make them wait, they decide to get them anyway. When Peach and Amy return, they find that Bowser and Dr. Eggman had left and assume that they found their invitations, and they head on a shopping trip with Daisy, Blaze and the Toads to celebrate the completion of the invitations. A [[List of badges in Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|badge]] of Amy can be obtained from the badge machine.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games''===
Amy returns in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]'', retaining her role as a playable All-Around type character. In Legends Showdown, she is in Area 2 with Knuckles, Wario and Waluigi, and shares victory animations with Sonic, Blaze and Peach.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games''===
[[File:Amy Rio 2016 Wii U Copacabana Beach.jpg|thumb|200px|Amy at Copacabana Beach in ''Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games'' for Wii U]]
Amy returns as a playable character in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games]]'' as once again an All-Around type. In the Nintendo 3DS version she is exclusive to 100m Freestyle and Rhythmic Gymnastics.

===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020''===
Amy reappears as a playable character in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''. Like other ''Mario'' and ''Sonic'' characters, she wears appropriate clothing for the events. In Chapter 3 of the Story Mode, Amy meets [[Luigi]] at the Olympic Stadium and asks him to compete in the 100m with her, but Luigi is searching for [[Miles "Tails" Prower|Tails]] (after she learned from Omochao). Amy tells Luigi that she can help him find Tails afterwards. After the match, Amy and Luigi head to the Karate Venue to find Tails.

She is also playable in [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020 - Arcade Edition|the arcade version]], as an All-Around character. Here, she has good technique, above average power, and average speed.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', there is both a collectible [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] and a [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|sticker]] of Amy.

===''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' / ''Wii U''===
In both ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'', there is an unlockable trophy of Amy.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Amy appears as a support [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]].
