'''World 1-4''' is the fourth and last [[level]] of [[World 1 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 1]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts on a path with a Fire Bar at the end above. The path ends at pillars within pits of lava, with the second pillar having the only ? Block with a power-up above it. After three more Fire Bars is a large pit with a set of stairs on the other end, leading to a narrow hallway with Fire Bars and a Koopa Troopa. The hall ends at a pit of lava with the platform on the other end having a Fire Bar. After crossing some more pits and pillars while dodging incoming Fire Breath, the player will arrive at the fake Bowser.
