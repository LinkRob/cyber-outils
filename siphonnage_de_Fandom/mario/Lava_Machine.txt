The '''Lava Machine''' is an object in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode, "[[Too Hot to Handle]]."

==Description==
The Lava Machine is a large, green furnace-like machine owned by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and [[Fryguy]]. Its purpose appears to be pumping out [[lava]].

==Episode appearances==
==="Too Hot to Handle"===
King Koopa and Fryguy, after they capture [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]], activate the Lava Machine with the intent of flooding the island of [[Waki-Waki]] with lava. Hearing King Koopa and Fryguy's plan, [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], after sending [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] to distract King Koopa, found some tools and began work on sabotaging the Lava Machine.

Later on, King Koopa is informed by Fryguy that Mario and Luigi have sabotaged the Lava Machine by clogging it. Hoping to fix the Lava Machine, King Koopa approaches it with Fryguy, Toad, and Princess Toadstool, only to see one of its pipes burst, causing lava to spew everywhere. Believing the situation to now be hopeless, King Koopa tries to escape with Princess Toadstool in his [[Fireproof Lava Boat]], but Mario manages to stop him from doing so.

The Lava Machine explodes eventually as a result of becoming completely clogged and backed up, unleashing a wave of lava. Mario, Luigi, Princess Toadstool and Toad manage to ride this wave to the beach of Waki-Waki on the Fireproof Lava Boat, while King Koopa makes his escape using a synthetic [[Mask Gate]].
