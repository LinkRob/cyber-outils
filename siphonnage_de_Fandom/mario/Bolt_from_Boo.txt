'''Bolt from Boo''' is a [[minigame]] from ''[[Mario Party-e]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a [[Ghost House]], with the background and platforms being designed after their appearance in ''[[Super Mario World]]''. The door and [[Big Boo]] sprites are also designed after their appearance from the game.

==Gameplay==
Two players take turns running [[Mario]] away from a Big Boo toward the exit by hitting the {{button|gba|A}} and {{button|gba|B}} buttons. Since the Big Boo is faster, Mario has to stop and turn around for Big Boo to retreat. If the Big Boo catches Mario, the challenge is over for that player. Whoever reaches the exit in the fastest time is the winner.
