'''Shoomps''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Shoomps are small [[Thwomp]]-like enemies with spikes on their sides.

===Behavior===
Shoomps slide from side to side. They may even slide into [[Poisoned Water|poison]] in their only level appearance, [[Another Uncharted Area: Swaying Ruins]].
