'''Body Check''' is a move in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}, appearing in both ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. Its function and the characters who can use it differs between the two games.

==In {{series|Mario Baseball}}==
===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
By rapidly pressing {{button|gcn|B}}, the runner can tackle a baseman, knocking the ball away from the baseman as they are about to catch it and allowing the runner to perform a close play. The move can be used by seven characters, including [[Wario]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Birdo]], [[Bowser]], [[Bowser Jr.]], [[Petey Piranha]], and [[Hammer Bro]].

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
Body Check is instead an an automatic action that occurs if the runner succeeds in a close play, which is done by pressing the correct button shown onscreen. Most characters can use the move except [[Blooper]], [[Boo]], [[King Boo]], [[Dry Bones]], and [[Monty Mole]], all of whom have a close-play ability of their own.
