'''ID UFO''' is a 2 vs. 2 mini-game from ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It can be unlocked with two players landing on the same-colour space and having its name pop up on the roulette wheel.

==Gameplay==
{{sectstub}}Two pairs of UFOs will be featured, one for each team. The players on each team must [[Ground Pound (move)|strike]] the panels that match the designs of said UFOs. A perfect match yields one point for that team. The UFOs vary in their design; some may resemble Saturn, some resemble hats, and still more designs exist, but the two that appear to be found by each team will have their designs featured on two of the panels for said team.

Striking an incorrect panel will cause time loss.

=== How to Win ===
The first team to five points wins. Failure to get five points within the one-minute time limit triggers a [[Tie (Mario Party)|tie]] if the numbers are same. If the numbers are different, the team with the most points wins.

==Cutscenes==

=== Intro ===
The red team gets startled when their ship is visited by a UFO, which flies off.

=== Ending ===
The winners celebrate while the floor collapses beneath the losers, dumping the latter into space.

==== Ties ====
A tie will be triggered by failing to get five points within the 1-minute time limit, causing both players to sulk as the host denies them the reward. Additionally, a tie can be triggered by both teams getting five points on the same frame, but this is extremely hard to pull off. If that happens, both teams celebrate. The referee will shout, ''"Draw!"'' in the former scenario, but in the latter scenario, she will remain silent after announcing, ''"Finish!"''

==Rules==

* '''Rules''':  ''"Find the panels that match the two UFOs. You and your teammate must then do a Ground Pound on both panels at the same time."''
* '''Advice''':  There are two pieces of advice for this minigame:
** ''"To get a point, you have to do a Ground Pound on your panel at the same time your teammate does a Ground Pound on his or hers."''
** ''"If you Ground Pound on the wrong panel, that panel will flash red."''
