'''Sir Flap-A-Lot''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is a red robot bird that has an attack of breathing blue fire at [[Wario]].

==History==
Sir Flap-A-Lots are only found in [[Blowhole Castle]] where they can be taken out by any attacks after a [[Spirit switch]] has been pressed to make them solid. Since they are flying, it is recommended to take them down with [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Their name is likely derived from the rapper {{w|Sir Mix-a-Lot}}.
