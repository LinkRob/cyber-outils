'''Drill Spin''' is a move which appears in some {{series|Super Mario}} games, most notably the [[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|''New Super Mario Bros.'' sub-series]]. It first appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Description==
In games featuring the Drill Spin, [[Mario]] or another playable character can perform a Drill Spin while twirling around in midair as the player holds Down on the D-Pad. As the name suggests, Drill Spin involves them twirling down rapidly in the manner of a drill, allowing them to reach the ground more quickly or even to break [[Brick Block]]s by twirling on top of them.

In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', twirling in midair occurs whenever Mario or [[Luigi]] jumps off a [[Spin Block (New Super Mario Bros.)|Spin Block]], during which they can then use their Drill Spin maneuver. The Drill Spin returns in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', where instead of being usable by jumping off a Spin Block, a character in their [[Propeller Mario|Propeller]] form can do a Drill Spin after charging up into the air to drill their way back down. In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'', and their [[reissue|remake]] ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'', the Drill Spin retains its role from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.
