The '''Shiver Shop''' is a location that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is an [[Item Shop]] in [[Shiver City]] that is run by a [[Bumpty]]. It is the first Item Shop to sell [[Shooting Star (item)|Shooting Stars]], and is known for selling other expensive items including a [[Life Shroom]]. When [[Mario]] becomes a suspect in the believed murder of [[Mayor Penguin]], he won't be able to buy any items until the mystery is solved.

==Trivia==
*The Shiver Shop is the third [[Item Shop]] where Mario temporary cannot purchase items from. The first is [[Shroom Grocery]] (when being taken over by a [[Shy Guy]] at the time) and the second is [[Boo's Shop]] (due to [[Lady Bow]] forbidding Mario from purchasing items until he agrees to help her out).
