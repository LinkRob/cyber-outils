'''Antasma X''' is the stronger counterpart of [[Antasma]]. Antasma C appears as a boss in the [[Battle Ring (Mario & Luigi: Dream Team)|Battle Ring]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
[[File:Antasma X (Bat State).png|thumb|220x220px|Antasma X in his bat form in Mario & Luigi: Dream Team.]]
Antasma X looks likes his [[Antasma|normal counterpart]], only with a blue cloak and skin, and bright blue eyes. In bat form, he has gold skin and wings, and pink eyes.

=== Powers and abilities ===
Antasma X's abilities are equivalent to [[Antasma]], but stronger.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' ==
Antasma X appears as a boss in the Battle Ring. His attacks remain the same, but he is stronger than his normal counterpart, Antasma.
