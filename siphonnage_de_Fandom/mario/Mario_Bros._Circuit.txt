'''Mario Bros. Circuit'''<ref name="trailer" /> is a track in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. It is the first track of the [[Mushroom Cup]], and the first track in the game.

== Layout ==
It is a desert-themed circuit themed after the {{w|Southwestern United States}}, with a sign at the starting line that says "Mario Bros. Circuit" and Yoshi's, a [[Yoshi]]-themed burger joint. There are {{w|butte}}s in the background shaped like mushrooms. [[Goomba]]s are obstacles.

== Trivia ==
*While previous games in the series had Mario and Luigi use their own circuits, in this game they share a track.
*There is a sign on the track based on the box art of ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''
*The track background resembles Radiator Springs from [[wikipedia:Pixar|Pixar]]'s ''[[wikipedia:Cars_(franchise)|Cars]]''.
