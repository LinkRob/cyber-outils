A '''Stinger''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Stingers are large, [[bee]] enemies commonly [[Land's End]], [[Bean Valley]], and [[The Trial Course]] within [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]]. They are a stronger variant of [[Buzzer]]s.

==Battle==
Stingers, in battle, mainly attack with their stingers. It is a rather weak physical attack, but they can use [[Thornet]], which can [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] a party member for [[Funguspike]] to transform one of them into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|mushroom]].
