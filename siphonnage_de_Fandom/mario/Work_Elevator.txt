The '''Work Elevator''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
The Work Elevator is a large flat [[platform]] with four arrow-emblazoned buttons that [[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in [[reissue|remake]]) can step on to move the platform around. If it bumps into something, the Work Elevator shakes a little and stops moving, after which it begins to move in the opposite direction. If the character falls off the Work Elevator, the Work Elevator respawns at its original location.

==In ''Super Mario 64'' / ''Super Mario 64 DS''==
The Work Elevator is found in a large room in the lower floor of [[Hazy Maze Cave]]. It plays a particular role in the mission [[Elevate for 8 Red Coins]], in which [[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in the remake) have to move the Work Elevator around to collect all eight [[Red Coin]]s.
