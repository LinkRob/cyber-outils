A '''Dryite''' is a race of [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Dryites are Toads that wear different color of tagelmust (blue, green, and yellow) that help them live in the [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|desert]].

==History==
Dryites can only be found in [[Dry Dry Outpost]] where they are located throughout the area alongside the [[Nomadimouse|Nomadimice]]. [[Goombario]] states that all Dryites in Dry Dry Outpost are adults (which is true as all of them resemble an adult Toad). The only named Dryite is [[Mr. E (Paper Mario)|Mr. E]] who is quite shy and rarely speaks.
