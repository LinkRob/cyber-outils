The '''Magikoopa Capsule''' is a [[Orb|capsule]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It can be thrown to a [[space]] on the board, or used on the player for ten [[coin]]s. Its effect summons [[Toady]], who swaps that player's capsules with an opponent's via a [[Roulette (Mario Party series)|roulette]].
