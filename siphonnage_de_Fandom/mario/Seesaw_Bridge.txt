'''Seesaw Bridge''', is the sixth level of [[Soda Jungle]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'', located outside the Haunted sub-area of Soda Jungle. It is unlocked via completion of [[Painted Swampland]] or [[Deepsea Ruins]], or via secret exit in [[Bridge over Poisoned Waters]] and its own completion unlocks [[Iggy's Volcanic Castle]]. 
==Layout==
This level takes place in a forest area covered with [[Poisoned Water]]. The level begins near a [[Wiggler]] on three [[? Block]]s, one of which contains a power-up. [[Seesaw Log Bridge]]s that tilt up and down are present, as well as a [[Roulette Block]]. A portion of the ground with Wigglers on it is found, along with more Seesaw Log Bridges. A [[River Piranha Plant]] is found in between Seesaw Log Bridges in the poison water. More Wigglers and a River Piranha Plant are found, followed by Seesaw Log Bridges that rotate in full circles. A row of River Piranha Plants is found below a structure of blocks, along with a [[Koopa Troopa]], a Wiggler, and a [[Red Ring]]. More River Piranha Plants are found between portions of ground, leading to the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. More Seesaw Log Bridges follow, with another Roulette Block found above a platform. More River Piranha Plants can be found in the poison water. A slope with Koopa Troopas on it is found, along with some River Piranha Plants in between Warp Pipes. More rotating Seesaw Log Bridges are found, leading to the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': A Warp Pipe is found to the left of two [[Wiggler]]s after passing the first set of Seesaw Log Bridges, hidden in a wall. The Warp Pipe leads to an underground area with the first [[Star Coin]], which can be reached using Seesaw Log Bridges partially submerged in poison water.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is found partly submerged in the poison waters. A thrown [[Koopa Shell]] is needed to get it.
*'''Star Coin 3''': At the area with two [[River Piranha Plant]]s between three Warp Pipes, the player should go down the rightmost pipe. After hitting the [[P Switch]], the player must then create a path of [[Hidden Block]]s to reach the third Star Coin.
