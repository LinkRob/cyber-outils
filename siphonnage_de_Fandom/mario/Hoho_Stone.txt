'''Hoho Stones''' are large [[Hoohoo Block]]s used by [[Dragohoho]] during his boss fight at [[Hoohoo Mountain]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. Dragohoho stands on a Hoho Stone to perform attacks on [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] more closely. A Hoho Stone has low [[HP]], and can be destroyed to take away Dragohoho's advantage when fighting the brothers.
