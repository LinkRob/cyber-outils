'''Treasure in the Ocean Cave''' is the third [[mission]] of [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in [[Super Mario 64]] and it's [[Reissue|remake]]: [[Super Mario 64 DS]].

== Description ==
To collect the third [[Power Star]] of [[Jolly Roger Bay]], [[Mario]] must swim under the sunken ship from [[Plunder in the Sunken Ship]] to find a hole in the Ocean wall. Once he swims through the hole, there will be an area with no water that Mario can walk around in that has [[Goombas]] and Falling Pillars. Once Mario dodges the Falling Pillars and Goombas, There will be a small platform containing four chests. Mario must open these chests in the right order or he will get electrocuted and he will lose one point of health. When they are opened in the correct order, a Star will spawn and Mario can collect it.

== Chest Order ==
The chests found in this mission must be opened in the correct order to obtain the Star. The order for these chests are :

* Top Chest
* Leftmost Chest
* Rightmost Chest
* Bottom Chest
