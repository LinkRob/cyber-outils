A '''Big Ninji''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
A Big Ninji has almost the same appearance as regular [[Ninji]]s, except it is larger.

=== Behavior ===
Like certain Ninjis, a Big Ninji only jumps in one spot, and it can harm the player's character upon contact.

==In ''Super Mario Advance''==
Like with most enemies, a Big Ninji can be picked up and thrown. Due to their large size, a Big Ninji takes longer to lift than the average-sized enemy. Either throwing a Big Ninji to the ground or defeating it reveals a [[small heart|Heart]], like with [[Mega Guy|Big Shy Guys]].
