The '''Mario Bros.''' or '''Super Mario Bros.''' is a group in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series. It was founded by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] who call themselves [[Plumber|plumbers]]. Other than fixing pipes, fittings systems and everything, they're also known as heroes of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. Since then, the duo group expand and gone through many adventures to deal with other dangers, traps, meeting new allies, friends and encounter deadly foes.

==History==

=== The Super Mario Bros. Series ===

==== [[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]] ====
The ''Mario Bros.'' franchise began in 1983 with the arcade game ''Mario Bros.'', which introduced Mario and Luigi as Italian-American plumbers working in the sewers of [[New York]]. Their objective was to eliminate creatures such as [[Shellcreeper|Shellcreepers]]—turtle-like enemies that would inspire future [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa Troopas]]. This game marked Luigi’s first appearance and established the dynamic of the brotherly duo.

==== [[Super Mario Bros.]] ====
In 1985, ''Super Mario Bros.'' was released for the Nintendo Entertainment System. This title redefined the Mario Bros. as heroes of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]], embarking on a quest to rescue [[Princess Peach]] from [[Bowser]], who had used dark magic to curse the [[Toad (species)|kingdom's inhabitants]]. Mario and Luigi traversed eight distinct worlds to restore peace and free the princess and the mushroom retainers from Bowser´s spell.

==== [[Super Mario Bros. 2]] ====
''Super Mario Bros. 2'' expanded the group dynamic beyond the brothers were [[Princess Peach]] who was in the previous game kidnapped and a new character named [[Toad (character)|Toad]] as playable characters. The story followed Mario's dream of a land called [[Subcon]], where he and his companions battled [[Wart]] to free the [[Subcon (species)|realm]] from his magical control. Luigi was portrayed with unique characteristics, such as a higher jump, which distinguished him from Mario in gameplay. Peach can floath in the air and Toad can be the fastest and has a superhuman strenght to pull turnips of with ease.

==== [[Super Mario Bros. 3]] ====
In ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] returned to their original roles as defenders of the Mushroom Kingdom. [[Bowser]] reappeared, accompanied by his seven children, the [[Koopalings]], who seized control of various regions. The brothers, supported by Toads through item-giving houses, traveled across multiple lands to defeat the Koopalings and Bowser, once again rescuing Princess Peach.

==== [[Super Mario World]] ====
Mario, Luigi and Peach head to vaccation on an [[Yoshi's Island (Super Mario World)|island]]. One day Peach suddendly vanished. The mario brothers discovered Bowser and the Koopalings took control, the mario brothers head to adventure to liberate the island and save Peach from Bowser´s grip once again. In their journey they meet a new character called [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] who confirmed them that Bowser took control of his [[Yoshi (species)|friends]] and the island. Yoshi then helps them along his way making him the third added member to the group. Thanks to the help from Yoshi, Mario retrieved peach once again from Bowser and saved the island allong with Yoshi´s friends.
