{{nihongo|'''Belcha'''|ベルチャ|Berucha}} is the first boss in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'' Belcha is a giant barrel with wooden teeth and black holes, with red eyes as well. Belcha is fought at the end of [[Lake Orangatanga]], where its [[Belcha's Barn|domain]] appears southwest of the lake.

==Battle==
To battle Belcha, [[Dixie Kong|Dixie]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] must start out with falling down a trapdoor to encounter Belcha. It then proceeds to burp out [[Knik-Knak]]s in [[barrel]]s at the Kongs to attack and the Kongs must continue throwing Knik-Knaks in Belcha's mouth. If missed, Belcha jumps forward, forcing the player to get closer to a spiky enemy in the [[Game Boy Advance]] version or just a pit in the [[SNES]] version. If the Kongs reach there and Belcha reaches the last rest of the floor, the Kongs lose a life. The Kongs must keep throwing Knik-Knaks in Belcha's mouth until Belcha's belch recoil makes it fall into a pit. Afterwards the Kongs can gain a [[Kremkoin]] as a reward.

==Trivia==
*Belcha is one of the two bosses in ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!'' to not reappear in ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'', the other one being [[Squirt]].
