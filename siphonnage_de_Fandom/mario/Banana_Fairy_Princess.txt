|race = [[Banana Fairy]]}}The '''Banana Fairy Princess''' is a character that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. She is the leader of the [[Banana Fairy|Banana Fairies]] of [[Banana Fairy Island]].

==History==
The Banana Fairy Princess can be found inside of Banana Fairy Island that only [[Tiny Kong]] can enter with her [[Mini-Monkey]] ability. The Princess is seen crying over the loss of the Banana Fairies due to [[King K. Rool]]'s appearance. Tiny agrees to help her and the Banana Fairy Princess gives Tiny a special [[Banana Fairy's Camera|camera]] and [[Banana Camera Film|film]] in order to capture the Banana Fairies in. Also, the Princess gives Tiny a special shockwave techique that can take down most enemies in one hit including [[Kasplat]]s.
