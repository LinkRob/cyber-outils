'''The Paraplonk Squad's Secret!''' is the fifth level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 16.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 6 [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s (Red), and 2 [[Starkiss]]. The enemy captain is a Koopa Paratroopa, and he knows [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 1 [[Koopa Troopa]], 3 Koopa Paratroopas, and 4 Starkiss. The enemy captain is a Starkiss, and he knows [[Chance]] and Deny.

The 3rd battle contains 2 Koopa Troopas, and 6 Koopa Paratroopas. The enemy captain is a Koopa Paratroopa, and he knows [[Rally]] and Deny. 

The final battle contains 3 Koopa Troopas, 1 Koopa Paratroopa, 3 Starkiss, and [[Corporal Paraplonk]]. The enemy captain is Corporal Paraplonk, and he knows Deny.

After the final battle, the player recruits Corporal Paraplonk.

The level can be replayed to recruit up to 1 Koopa Troopa, and or Koopa Paratroopa.
