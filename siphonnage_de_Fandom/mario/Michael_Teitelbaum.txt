'''Michael Teitelbaum''' is a New York-based author. Teitelbaum has written a large number of chapter books based on licensed properties, along with several non-fiction publications, primarily on baseball. He has also authored various ''[[Donkey Kong Country (series)|Donkey Kong Country]]'' chapter books.
