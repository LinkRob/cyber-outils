}}'''Line Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Line Jump is the line version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a blue spring boot with a blue/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 20/90 coins

Sell Price: 10/45 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Line Jump is first seen in [[Indigo Underground]], but can also found in [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Plum Park]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Vortex Island]], and [[Mustard Café]].
