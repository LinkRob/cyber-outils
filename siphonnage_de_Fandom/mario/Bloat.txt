'''Bloat''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. He is a member of the [[S.S. Chuckola Crew]] who served on the [[S.S. Chuckola]].

== Profile ==
Like his crewmates, Bloat is a skeleton. His voracious appetite, has caused him to grow too big, leading to his name.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'' / ''Bowser's Minions'' ==
Bloat is first seen where he was stuck to a wall after eating too much food and drinking too much [[Chuckola Cola]] (hence his nickname "Ostaf" ("Fatso" spelled backwards). One of the members asks Mario and Luigi to free him from the wall and the member will give up the [[Beanstar]] piece to them. They do so by blowing up the wall with dynamite and Bloat is excited to be free. However, a river of sand soon floods the ship and it eventually crashes into a large rock and sinks to the bottom of the ocean. It is unknown what happened to Bloat and the rest of the crew after this.
