[[File:Chemistry.png|thumb|An example of a good chemistry]]'''Chemistry''' is a system in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]'' that allows players to better organize a team for play. Certain characters have special connections with other characters, allowing them to preform moves like [[Buddy Jump]]s if they are placed next to each other in the lineup.{{stub}}
