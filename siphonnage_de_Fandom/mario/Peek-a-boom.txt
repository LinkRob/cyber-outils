A '''Peek-a-boom''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Peek-a-boom is a ninja-like enemy which wears a green uniform and glasses with light blue lenses.

===Behavior===
A Peek-a-boom starts off being invisible. If [[Wario]] comes closer, it appears with a cloud of smoke, but if he is too close, the Peek-a-boom vanishes again with more smoke. A Peek-a-boom attacks by throwing [[bomb]]s, only five of which can appear at once before it stops throwing them.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
A Peek-a-boom has a threat rating of two stars. Wario can sometimes use a Peek-a-boom's bombs to solve puzzles and therefore obtain [[treasure]]. If Wario uses an [[Earthshake Punch]] near a Peek-a-boom, he can pick it up and shake the Peek-a-boom.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Its name is a pun on "peek-a-boo" and an onomatopoeia for explosions, "boom."
