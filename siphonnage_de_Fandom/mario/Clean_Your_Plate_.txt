'''Clean Your Plate!''' is one of [[Young Cricket and Master Mantis]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
Using the [[Wii Remote]], the player has to tilt the plate so that the stream of water can splash onto the grime and wash it away. The first, second, and third levels feature one, two, and three spots of grime to clean, respectively.
