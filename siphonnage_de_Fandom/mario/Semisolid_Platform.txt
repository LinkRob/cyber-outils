'''Semisolid Platforms''', spelled '''Semi-solid platforms''' in ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', are a type of [[platform]] which appear in the platforming games, especially the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. Their first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance is in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'', released overseas as ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Description==
A Semisolid Platform is a platform that can be jumped onto from the bottom. They have a different design between most of their appearances, and some of these styles are placeable objects in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. [[Mushroom Platform]]s are a notable type of Semisolid Platform.
