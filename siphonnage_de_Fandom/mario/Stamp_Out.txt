'''Stamp Out''' is one of [[Wario-Man]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. 

==Gameplay==
The object is for the player to click on every empty circle. When an empty circle is clicked, a circular red stamp of [[Wario]]'s face appears. Once every circle is filled, the player wins. However, if the player either clicks on a stamped circle or does not fill in every empty circle on time, they lose at the microgame.
