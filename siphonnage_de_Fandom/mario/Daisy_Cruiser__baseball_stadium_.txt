'''Daisy Cruiser''' is a baseball stadium which appears in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It takes place on the ship of the [[Daisy Cruiser|same name]]. The minigame [[Blooper Baserun]] takes place here.

==Description==
At daytime, the gimmick is dining tables, similar to those from the ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' course, placed across the field. At nighttime, the tables do not appear, but rather [[Cheep Cheep]]s bounce around the field. [[Gooper Blooper]] makes an appearance about halfway through a nighttime game on Daisy Cruiser, leaning on one side of the ship to tilt the entire baseball field.

In the last cinematic of ''Mario Super Sluggers'', the Daisy Cruiser is seen to be an outdoor buffet with desserts and beverages. [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], her friends and others are at various tables, feasting after winning a decisive showdown against [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]].
