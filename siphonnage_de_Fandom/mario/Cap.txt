'''Caps''' are an object worn by certain characters in the {{franchise|Mario}}, most notably [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. A cap, specifically [[Mario's Cap]], first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'', but ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' is the first time where Caps played a role during gameplay.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario 64]]'' / ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''===
This game introduced the [[Metal Cap]] and the [[Wing Cap]]. It also introduced the fact that when [[Mario]] loses his cap, he cannot jump as high.

===''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''===
Like in ''Super Mario 64'', if Mario loses his cap, he has bad luck.
{{Sectstub}}

===''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''===
At the beginning of the game Mario loses his cap in a [[Bowser]] fight. [[Cappy]] finds it and replaces it throughout the course of the game. This whole game is pretty much centered around caps.

===''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''===
With the [[Parachute Cap]] [[Badge (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|badge]], [[Mario]] and others can float down slowly when midair.
