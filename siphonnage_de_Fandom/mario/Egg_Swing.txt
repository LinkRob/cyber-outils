'''Egg Swing''' is [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s [[Star Swing]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces his [[Egg Ball]] move from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', and his pitching move has similarly been replaced with [[Rainbow Ball]].

==Description==
Upon initiating Egg Swing, Yoshi lays a [[Yoshi Egg]] and then hits it. The Yoshi Egg then bounces around the field as a rainbow trails behind it. After bouncing two or three times, the Yoshi Egg stops, disappears, and the ball takes its place.

==Trivia==
*The Yoshi Egg can possibly bounce far enough to one side to go over the fence, which would count as a ground rule double.
