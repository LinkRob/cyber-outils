The '''Flame Flyer''' is a recurring [[kart]] in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''.

==Description==
The Flame Flyer is styled after a 1970s {{w|Plymouth Barracuda}}, with three exhaust pipes sticking out on both sides of the vehicle and a supercharger engine sticking out of the hood. It also has flame accents. It is often associated with [[Bowser]].

== In {{series|Mario Kart}} ==
=== ''Mario Kart Wii'' ===
[[File:FlameFlyer-Bowser.png|thumb|150px|left|Bowser's Flame Flyer in ''Mario Kart Wii'', colored dark green with yellow flames.]]
The Flame Flyer appears as a large-size [[kart]]. It is available from the start. It has great [[speed]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]], as well as good [[drift]] and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]] capabilities, but suffers from poor [[acceleration]] and [[handling]], followed by low [[Off-Road|off-road]] stats.

The Flame Flyer has the worst acceleration of any kart in the game, but if [[Waluigi]] or the large Mii's use the Flame Flyer, its acceleration will be tied with its [[bike]] counterpart, the [[Flame Runner]].
{{br|left}}
=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
[[File:MKT_Rock_Rock_Mountain_Gliding.jpg|thumb|200px|Bowser in the Flame Flyer racing with [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] on {{classic course|3DS|[[Rock Rock Mountain]]}} in ''Mario Kart Tour''.]]
The Flame Flyer appears in ''Mario Kart Tour '', where it is classified Super kart. It can be obtained through ruby pulls and purchasing from the item shop. Unlike in ''Mario Kart Wii'', the Flame Flyer can be used by any character, rather than being only restricted to heavyweights, and only uses Bowser's color scheme. The Flame Flyer uses the [[Off-Road (tire)|Off-Road]] tires from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and  ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.
