'''Wario Bros.''' is a [[microgame]] in the [[Anything Goes]] folder in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''

==Layout==
Wario Bros. takes place in ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]'', the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version in particular. Like [[Grow Wario Grow]], Wario Bros. is one of the microgames that is based on a classic [[Nintendo]] game while not being part of [[9-Volt]]'s microgame set.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Wario]], who has to obtain a [[Coin]]. In levels 2 and 3, Wario has to collect the Coin before [[Mario]] does. In level 3, Mario has the additional action of [[jump]]ing to try and collect the Coin. If done successfully, the player wins, but if unsuccessful, they lose.
