The '''Smithy Gang''' is an evil group of sentient weapons led by [[Smithy]] who have invaded the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and took over [[Bowser]]'s [[Bowser's Castle|Castle]] for their base of operations. They are the main antagonists of ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its upcoming [[Super Mario RPG|2023 remake]].
