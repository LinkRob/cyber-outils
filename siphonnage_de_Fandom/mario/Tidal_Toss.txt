}}'''Tidal Toss''' is a 1 vs. 3 mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It reappears in ''The Top 100'' and ''Superstars''.

==Gameplay==
===Single===
In this mini-game, the single player must try and knock the other players off the stage by jumping on the boat. The player could also use the [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] move to help send bigger waves. The higher the player jumps, the bigger the waves are. The single player wins if they manages to knock off the trio by the end of the time.

===Team===
For the trio, they must jump over the waves to stay on the stage. If the player is doing quick jumps, then the players should jump lightly to keep up with the speed. For the bigger waves, they must jump as high as they can. It should be noted that the water is always pushing off the stage which means the players must keep moving forward (but not so close to the boat) in order to not fall off. If at least one of the three players stays on the stage without being knocked off, they win.
