A '''Giant Vegetable''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]''.

==Description==
Giant Vegetable function similarly to regular [[vegetable]]s, except they can hit more enemies due to being a larger size.

==In ''Super Mario Advance''==
Like with other large objects that can be pulled from the ground, [[Toad (character)|Toad]] can pull out the Giant Vegetable the quickest due to being the strongest of the four characters.
