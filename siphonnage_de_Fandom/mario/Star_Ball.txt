The '''Star Ball''' is a large, translucent, capsule-like ball with a [[Power Star]] in it that appears in both ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. The objective with the Star Ball is to roll to the finish past the obstacles. You cannot dismount the Star Ball until you enter the ball-opener, where the ball will explode and you can claim the Power Star.
