'''Wavy Beach''' is the fifth world in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The beach-themed world is located in [[Vibe Island]] with many new enemies not seen in previous worlds being introduced here. The world also introduces [[Peach]] to the [[Subrella]] ability where she can swim underwater with [[Perry]] acting as a submarine. The main gimmicks of this world are the waves which Peach can ride, Beachballs which can be used to clear any blocks, and the Arrow Spears which fly out from their holes. The most common enemies are [[Blooper]]s, [[Hammer Bro]]s, and [[Cheep Cheep]]s. The boss of this world is the colossal [[Gooper Blooper]].
