'''Hard-Pressed''' is one of [[Mona]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of a red iron and must use it to iron out every wrinkle on a white shirt. The first, second, and third difficulty levels feature one, two, and three wrinkles, respectively.
