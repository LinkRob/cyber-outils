'''Special 8-1''' is the first level of [[Special 8]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is based off of [[World 1-2 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 1-2]]. 
==Layout==
The player starts next to a bridge with several [[Spike Trap|spikes]] on it. After the bridge is an area with several [[Spiny|Spinies]] and more spikes in it. At the end of this section is a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads outside, where there are two [[? Block]]s, the one on the right containing a [[Starman]], and the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Medal]]s==
*'''Star Medal 1:''' Floating above the spiked cylinder at the start of the level.
*'''Star Medal 2:''' Floating above the crumbling rock platforms.
*'''Star Medal 3:''' Floating next to the [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the exit. The player needs to [[Wall Jump]] to get it.
