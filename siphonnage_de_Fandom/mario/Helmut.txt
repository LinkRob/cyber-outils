'''Helmut''' is an aquatic enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''. It wears a spiked helmet to protect its head, so if [[Wario]] touches the spikes, he takes damage. A Helmut's vulnerable spot is its underside, and Wario can defeat a Helmut by charging at it from below.
