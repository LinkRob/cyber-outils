'''Stage Fright''' is a 1-vs.-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
The team members shoot balls out of their cannons, trying to hit the solo character, who successfully dodges the balls.

==Gameplay==
The team members have to knock the solo character off of the stage by shooting balls of varying colors at them. As demonstrated in the opening, the solo character has to avoid the balls shot at them. Every team member's cannon moves automatically, with the first team member's platform moving the slowest and the last team member's platform moving the fastest. The cannons are also lined up in rows, so it is possible for a team member to accidentally shoot at one of their teammates in front, temporarily stunning whoever it hit. The teams win if a ball hits the solo character, but the solo character wins if manage to dodge all of the balls within the 30-second [[time limit]].

==Ending==
If the team wins, their cannons line up in a formation as they team members perform their respective victory animation. If the solo character wins, they perform their victory animation while the team members do their respective losing animations.

==Controls==
===Solo===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move

===Team===
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Shoot balls

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''': ''Three players on moving platforms fire balls at the stage. One player has to run around the stage to avoid getting hit!''
* '''Advice''': ''Players on platforms who are hit by their own teammates will be temporarily stunned.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is derived from the phrase "{{w|stage fright}}," meaning to be scared of performing in front of a crowd.
