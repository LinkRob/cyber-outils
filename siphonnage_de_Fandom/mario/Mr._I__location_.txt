Found in the [[Horror Area]], [[Mr. I]] lives in a place called {{Nihongo|'''Mr. I'''|アイクン|Ai-kun}}. The location appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
Mr. I lives in the location named after him.
{{Spoiler|hide}}
The player can complete the [[Love That Princess!]] quest only if the player plays [[Princess Peach]] and accepts his request.
{{Endspoiler}}

During the [[Probably a Robbery?]] quest, Mr. I is one of the three suspects of the [[Koopa Bank (Mario Party Advance)|Koopa Bank]] robbery.
{{Spoiler|hide}}
Going to his place, he stated that he is not guilty but remembered the coins jingled.
{{endspoiler}}
{{spoiler|hide}}
In the [[Hearts A-Flutter]] quest, the player must visit him upon [[Flutter]]'s request.
{{Endspoiler}}
