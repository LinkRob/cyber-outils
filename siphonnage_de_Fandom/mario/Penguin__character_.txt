{{Nihongo|'''Penguin'''|ペンギン<ref>{{cite web|url=https://themushroomkingdom.net/mario-in-japan/mpartya|title=Mario Party Advance (GBA)|work=TMK|accessdate=2023-04-19}}</ref>|Pengin|Penguin}} is a penguin who lives in [[Shroom City]]. He appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Penguin is a blue [[penguin]].

=== Powers and abilities ===
* '''Penguin Physiology''': Although not seen using his own abilities, he is a penguin.

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
Penguin is in the [[Ice Game Room]], he loves playing games. If the player is willing to help Penguin's [[Engaging Game]] quest, he explained he is trying to win enough coins to buy a wedding ring for [[Pengwen]]. However, he kept losing coins. The player has to win at least 1 coin more than the player started with in the minigame, [[Drop 'Em]].

After winning the minigame, Penguin thanks the player and is able to buy the ring for Pengwen.

{{Spoiler}}
After the player completes Shroom City, Penguin still could not afford the wedding ring.
{{Endspoiler}}
