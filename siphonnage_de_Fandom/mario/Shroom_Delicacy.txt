}}'''Shroom Delicacies''' are items found only in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==

These rare mushroom-based meals are only created by [[Dyllis]] in [[Flopside]]. She will cook this item up for [[Mario]] so long as the two correct items are delivered to her. This item needs not only the basic [[Shroom Shake]] to be made, but also needs the rare [[Dayzee Tear]], a rare drop item from [[Crazee Dayzee|Crazee]] and [[Amazy Dayzee]]s. This item is not worth much for food as the Shroom Shake and this heal 10 HP. Like all other healing items in this game, it too cures poison if Mario or his crew suffer from it. Also the Tear does not heal much so it is not all that bad. This item also sells for a fairly nice price compared to its ingredients so it can be nice to make every now and then.
