'''Dragoneel Depths''' is the fifth level of [[Sparkling Waters]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
==Features==
{{Sectstub}}
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': In Fire form or Ice form, move or kill the third and fourth urchins away from the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': While avoiding the Dragoneel, go in the center of the second group of spinning platforms.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Have the Dragoneels destroy the Mega Urchin near the pipe on the left and go in it while avoiding the enemies to get the Star Coin on the other side.

==Hidden Luigi image==
Near the pipe leading to the third Star Coin shows a mini picture of standard Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' swimming.
