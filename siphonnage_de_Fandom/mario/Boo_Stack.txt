The '''Boo Stack''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
The Boo Stack is only found in the level [[The Engimansion]]. Mario finds the Boo Stack in a room where he opens a cabinet and 82 [[Boo]]s fall on him.

===Battle===
The Boo Stack favors rolling into [[Mario]]. Mario can take them out easily with the Shiny Infinite Jump found earlier in the level. Each time Mario lands a jump attack, this defeats a Boo, making this one [[Heart Point|HP]] per hit. After Mario defeats a Boo Stack, all 82 Boos become trapped into the [[Book of Sealing]].
