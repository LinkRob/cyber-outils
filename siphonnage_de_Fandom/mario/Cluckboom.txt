'''Cluckbooms''' (also called '''Bomb Chickens''' or simply '''Roosters''') are chicken-like enemies that appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They fly around and periodically drop explosive eggs.

== Description ==
Cluckbooms resemble chickens. They have green markings around their circular eyes, which have black pupils. Their wings are yellow, and the top of their head has a spiky mohawk-like comb. Their lower body doesn't have legs, forcing them to fly, instead having a cannon that can drop their bombs.

== History ==

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
Cluckbooms first appear in [[Dusty Dune Galaxy]]. They lay egg bombs that explode a few seconds after landing. They can be brought to the ground by using a [[Star Spin]] in their vicinity or shooting them down with a [[Star Bit]], causing them to lose control of their flight and plummet. Upon landing, they can be defeated with a ram for [[Star Bit]]s or stomped on for a [[Coin]]. Some Cluckbooms show up after a certain event is activated and must be avoided as they drop their explosive eggs, when the event ends, the Cluckboom simply leaves.

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
Cluckbooms can be found in [[Slipsand Galaxy]], where they behave the same as in the previous title. They once again drop explosive eggs on [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] which must be avoided and just as before to beat a Cluckboom, [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] must either ground it by shooting a [[Star Bit]] at it or by [[Star Spin]]ning at it accurately. This will ground the Cluckboom allowing the plumbers to get a clear chance to finish it off.
