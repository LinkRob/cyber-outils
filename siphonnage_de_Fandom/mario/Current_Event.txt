'''Current Event''', is the fifth course of [[Peach's Castle (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Peach's Castle]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. It is unlocked from completing [[Hot Cogs]] or [[Firefall Rising]] and its own completion unlocks [[The Final Battle (New Super Luigi U)|The Final Battle]]. The course's name is a pun on "electric current" and the phrase "current event", another term for news.  

==Layout ==
The level begins on a platform in the castle, with stairs going to a tilt lift. The lava begins rising, and a few [[Bowser Amp]]s are found. A [[Pipe Cannon]] follows, which leads to a platform above where another tilt lift is located. [[Parabomb]]s begin falling from above throughout the area, and two connected Bowser Amps are found just before the first [[Warp Door]]. A [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] is found to the right of the door. Another Pipe Cannon is found, which leads to a section of the ground with [[? Block]]s and a [[Bob-omb]]. A third tilt lift is found, leading through another section of Bowser Amps and Parabombs. Another Warp Door is found, with another Hidden Block containing a 1-Up Mushroom to the right of it. The [[Goal Pole]] is then found at the end in the final room.

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': At the beginning, the player must jump in between two Bowser Amps to get the first Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 2''': After going through the Pipe Cannon, the player must use the Parabomb and throw it at the Star Coin. The player can also have a Bob-omb land on the platform to lower it to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': On the third tilt lift, above the Bowser Amps in a V formation, the player can either jump off the Parabomb or time their jump to obtain the Star Coin.
