"'''Tutti Frutti, oh Mario'''", later alternately named "'''Tutti Frutti Mario'''," is a live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. The original version is the thirtieth live-action segment, and it was paired with ''[[The Legend of Zelda (television series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' episode "[[w:c:zelda:That Sinking Feeling|That Sinking Feeling]]," while the later version is recognized as the sixtieth live-action segment and was paired with "[[w:c:zelda:The Missing Link|The Missing Link]]," another ''The Legend of Zelda'' episode.

==Trivia==
*The title of "Tutti Frutti, oh Mario" is derived from the 1955 [[Little Richard]] song "{{w|Tutti Frutti (song)|Tutti Frutti}}," prominently featuring the lyric "Tutti Frutti, aw rooty" in its chorus.
