{{Nihongo|'''Fire Orb'''|ファイアボール|Faiabōru|Fireball}} is a special move used by [[Mario]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
It is Mario's first [[Fire (element)|Fire]]-type move used in the game and he learns it at level 3. To use this move, the player must repeatedly press the {{button|snes|y}} as Mario hurls fireballs at a single opponent. This move requires five [[Flower Point]]s to use. It is also the very first new move that Mario learns through leveling up.

With Fire Orb, Mario is able to damage a single enemy for fire damage. This is useful against insect and plant-based enemies but will barely do anything to other fire enemies.
