==History==
Shrooms are light blue-skinned [[Kremling]]s that are only found in [[Fungi Forest]]. They disguise themselves as regular, bouncy mushrooms and reveal themselves once a Kong gets close. Shrooms are rather weak enemies and can be taken out with any attack.
