'''Aquamentus''' is a large dragon from the ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' franchise. It first appears in the first ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'' game, and made its first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''[[Tetris DS]]''.

==In ''Tetris DS''==
During ''The Legend of Zelda''-based challenges, Aquamentus is among the characters from the first ''The Legend of Zelda'' to make a cameo by having an enlarged variation of its ''The Legend of Zelda'' sprite possibly appear on the Touch Screen.

==In {{series|NES Remix}}==
Aquamentus appears in Stage 5 of the ''The Legend of Zelda'' challenges. Here, the goal is for Link to collect a Triforce fragment, but he must first defeat Aquamentus in a boss battle, specifically within [[w:c:zelda:Level 1|Level-1]], without taking any damage.

==In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''==
If the player is playing as Link in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' style and the effect of boss music is triggered, an Aquamentus' sprite from ''The Legend of Zelda'' is briefly shown on-screen, replacing the [[Bowser Jr.]] emblem, although for final boss theme, this instead shows a sprite of [[Ganon]].
