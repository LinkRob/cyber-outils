'''Krystal''' is a character from the ''Star Fox'' series, in which she first appears in ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox Adventures|Star Fox Adventures]]''. Krystal is the love interest of [[Fox McCloud]]. Krystal made her first ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related appearance in ''[[Donkey Konga 3 JP]]'', in the form of a cameo. Krystal has also appeared in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.

==In {{series|Donkey Konga}}==
===''Donkey Konga 3 JP''===
[[File:Krystal DKa3 card.png|frame|left]]
Krystal makes a cameo appearance as one of the unlockable badges. It depicts her ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox: Assault|Star Fox: Assault]]'' artwork, although cropped to show her face. She makes another cameo in Multiplayer battle, in which her face is depicted on the Queen playing card. Like Krystal's badge, the card depicts her ''Star Fox: Assault'' artwork cropped to show only her face.

===''[[Donkey Konga 2]]'' (international)===
[[File:Krystal badge DKa2.png|frame|left]]
The same badge of Krystal in ''Donkey Konga 3 JP'' makes a reappearance in international copies of ''Donkey Konga 2'', having the same role as before.

==In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''===
Krystal is one of the characters present during the [[Lylat Cruise]] conversation, activated if any of the ''Star Fox'' fighters perform their [[Smash Taunt]] there. Aside from this, Krystal is also featured as a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] and as three [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Stickers]], depicting her ''Star Fox Adventures'', ''Star Fox: Assault'', and ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox Command|Star Fox Command]]'' artwork, respectively.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]''===
Krystal makes a cameo appearance as a collectible Trophy.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''===
Aside from retaining her Trophy cameo from ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'', Krystal also returns within the Lylat Cruise conversations.

===''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''===
[[File:Krystal SSB Ultimate.png|thumb|200px|Krystal's appearance in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'']]
Krystal is most prominently featured as an [[Assist Trophy]]. When summoned by a fighter, Krystal says "Try me!", and uses her staff to cast ice spells and swing at nearby opponents. It is possible for Krystal to be KO'd. When departing, she says "You're not ready yet!".

Just as before, Krystal returns in the Lylat Cruise conversations. There is also a Legend-class primary [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|spirit]] of Krystal, depicting her artwork from ''Star Fox Adventures''.
