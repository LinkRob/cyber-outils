'''Charlie''' is a human golfer in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]]. Wind does not affect his drives as much as any other player. Charlie wears a yellow shirt, a pair of jeans, and brown shoes. He appears to be a teenager who is possibly trying to become a great golfer (similarly to [[Plum]]). He is also a starting character along with [[Princess Peach|Peach]], Plum, and [[Baby Mario]]. Charlie's shots travel in a fade trajectory, meaning that they curve to the left. His max drive is 210 yards (192 meters).

Although Charlie does not make an appearance in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]], he is mentioned on the tournament board.
