'''Diamond City Elementary''', also named '''Diamond Elementary''', is a recurring location in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. It is an elementary school found near the center of [[Diamond City]], and is where [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]] go to school together.

Not much of the school is shown, although during 9-Volt's stage in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', he is studying math in class, expressing his disdain toward it, until [[Fronk]] suggests that he view it as a role-playing game.
