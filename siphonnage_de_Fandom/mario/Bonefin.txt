'''Bonefins''' are robotic skeletal fish that appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They resemble [[Fish Bone]]s but they are more robotic and metallic in appearance. Bonefins are minions of the colossal [[Kingfin]] and reside below the [[Bonefin Galaxy]]'s only planet with their leader.

==Appearances==

===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Bonefins appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' in the Bonefin Galaxy, which marks their only appearance. Kingfin summons them at different intervals with during his fight, with all of them rising from a hydrothermal vent on his world: after he is hit with one [[Koopa Shell]], he will summon two Bonefins to aid him in battle. The Bonefins travel the waters with their leader. They can be defeated with a shell; whenever they see [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]), they will charge directly at him and explode on impact. They don't home in on him and can be destroyed if they crash into a wall or if they reach the surface of the water. After three hits, Kingfin will summon four Bonefins to aid him. After Kingfin's defeat, the Bonefins disappear.

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
Bonefins have a cameo appearance in [[Super Mario Galaxy 2|''Super Mario Galaxy 2'']] in the [[Yoshi Star Galaxy]], where a fossil appears in their image on the fossil planet, but they do not appear otherwise. This fossil's head is shaped slightly differently compared to theirs.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and onward, Fish Bones behave very similarly to Bonefins but unlike the Bonefins, they don't explode.
