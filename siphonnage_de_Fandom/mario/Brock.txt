'''Brocks''' are a species of walking blocks that first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. The only Brock characters who appear are [[Broque Monsieur]], [[Broque Madame]], [[Broggy]], and the [[Blitty|Blitties]].

==Profile==
===Powers and abilities===
The Brocks have a unique ability to flip their heads upside down as shown twice by Broque. They are also very good carpenters  and gardeners as they seem to handle all of the construction and maintaining plant life on [[Pi'illo Island]].

==History==
The species itself (not the notable members) are rarely seen in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'', but it was not until ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' where they were more discovered. It turns out that they live in a region near the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] called the [[Pi'illo Kingdom]] where they live on Pi'illo Island peacefully with the [[Pi'illo]]s.

==Notable members==
*[[Broque Monsieur]]: The first Brock character that appeared in the series. He teaches Bowser how to use the [[Vacuum Shroom]] in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''. He also askes Bowser to gather the Blinkies for him. In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', he acts as a tour guide and the head of intelligence of Pi'illo Island.
*[[Broggy]]: Broque Monsieur's fateful pet. Broggy was first fought as a boss in ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' but soon becomes one of Bowser's special moves once he has found all of the Blitties. Broggy returns in ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' where he was sent by [[Dr. Snoozemore]] inviting Mario, Luigi, Peach, and the rest to the Pi'illo Kingdom.
*[[Brickle]]: The head carpenter of Pi'illo Island. He is a bit hot-tempered but can be grateful to people that help him out in need. He runs the [[Mushrise Park]] and his notable for his creation the [[Grobot]].
*[[Britta]]: The head proprietor of [[Dozing Sands]]. Britta is also hot-tempered always yelling at her employees to work faster. She craves adventure and wealth.
