'''Galrog''' is the second boss faced by [[Toad (character)|Toad]] in the VS COM mode of ''[[Wario Woods]]''{{'}}s [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version. Before battle, Galrog tells Toad that he is in training and asks Toad to go easy on him.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Galrog is a muscular cyclops with gray skin and a horn on his head.

===Etymology===
Galrog's name is likely a pun on {{w|Balrog}}, a monster from ''{{w|The Lord of the Rings}}''.
