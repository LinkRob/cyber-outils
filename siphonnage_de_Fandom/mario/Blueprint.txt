}}'''Blueprints''' are important items that appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. They can be obtained from defeating [[Kasplat]]s throughout the levels.

==History==
As stated by [[Snide]], the Blueprints are mainly used to help slow down [[King K. Rool]]'s [[Blast-o-Matic]]. This is confirmed when the [[Kong Family|Kongs]] make it to [[Hideout Helm]], the blueprints slow the Blast-o-Matic down by one minute each. There is a grand total of 40 blueprints in the game (8 for each Kong). After a blueprint is obtained, it must be taken to Snide at his [[Snide's HQ|HQ]] to also receive a [[Golden Banana]] as a reward.
