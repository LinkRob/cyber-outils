The '''DVD''' is a key item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The DVD in particular is of a limited-edition ''[[Toad Force V]]'' DVD, only fifty of which were ever produced.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
The DVD is a part of the quest [[DVD for Me]]. In it, [[Ninji (character)|Ninji]], a big fan of ''Toad Force V'', wants the DVD for his collection. He requests the player's character to retrieve a copy for him, mentioning that someone at [[Mushroom Condo]] has a spare copy. The character whom Ninji is referring to is [[Mushbert]], whom the player's character can visit in his floor of the Mushroom Condo. Mushbert is okay to give a DVD to player's character upon request, given that he apparently has all fifty copies of the DVD.

When the player's character gives Ninji the DVD, he is ecstatic, stating that there are only fifty copies of the DVD in existence. As a reward, Ninji gives the player the [[Tile Trial]] [[Gaddget]]. After this, Ninji proceeds to excitedly watch the DVD.
