The '''Ice Flower Cup''' is a cup in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Descripton==
The Ice Flower Cup is the second cup in the Star Open series, and because it has an [[Ice Flower]] as its symbol, the Ice Flower Cup is effectively a counterpart to the [[Flower Cup]], which has a [[Fire Flower]] symbol instead and is part of the World Open series. The Ice Flower Cup's stand is dark blue with a light blue diamond shape adorning the middle, the rims of the cup are also light blue, and a light blue snowflake-like shape is behind the Ice Flower.

==In ''Mario Tennis Open''==
The Ice Flower is an unlockable cup. It has one set for the starting round, three sets for the semifinals, and five sets for the finals. The Ice Flower Cup can be unlocked by completing the [[1-Up Mushroom Cup]]. The court used in the Ice Flower Cup is [[Penguin Iceberg]].
