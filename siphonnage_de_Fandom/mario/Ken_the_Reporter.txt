'''Ken the Reporter''' is a dog anchorman and news reporter in [[Diamond City]] in the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series. He first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Ken is a dog whose fur is colored orange and peach, although Ken's choice of clothing differs between his appearances. In ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', Ken wears a green bucket-shaped hat and suit, orange glasses, and black pants. In ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', Ken instead wears light blue-frame glasses, a white suit with a red tie. As of ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', Ken no longer wears a hat. In ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', Ken wears a red and white suit with a gray pair of headphones. In ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]'', Ken's fur was made redder, and his suit is entirely white.

===Etymology===
The word ken (犬) is the Japanese word for "dog."

==In the ''WarioWare'' series==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
Ken is first seen in the cutscene shown from turning on the game. Here, [[Wario]] sees Ken on the news, delivering a report about the in-universe [[Game Boy Advance]] game [[Pyoro (game)|Pyoro]]'s success and how video game sales are making their way up. Seeing a potential to make large profits, Wario is given the idea to start his own business, [[WarioWare, Inc.]].

Ken later makes another appearance during the ending scene of [[9-Volt]]'s story, when he reports that the [[Game Boy Advance SP]] has released; this prompts 9-Volt to go purchase one.

Ken is last seen in the ending cutscene. Three in-universe weeks after completing Wario's second stage, Ken asks if the game will really sell, followed by a scene of Wario earning lots of money.

===''WarioWare: Twisted!''===
Ken has taken a desk job at the local news agency, [[Diamond City Broadcasting Station, Inc.]], and he is instead named '''Anchorman Ken'''. Here, in the opening scene for [[Wario-Man]]'s story, Ken talks about how Wario has once again sold a massively popular game, with his new one surpassing all sales records.

===''WarioWare: Touched!''===
Ken apparently takes a job hosting [[Ear Candy]], a show on [[Diamond City]]'s local music network, and is named '''Ken the VJ'''. Ken is seen during [[Mona]]'s story, in which he reports the top five music artists of the week, with Mona taking the number one spot. Ken furthermore reports that Mona will be performing at the [[Hawt House]].

===''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''===
His title is now Ken the Reporter just like in his first appearance. Ken is shown at the end of Mona's story, reporting on a football game between the [[Diamond City Roughs]] and [[the Dinosaurs]] alongside [[Joe|Manager Joe]].
