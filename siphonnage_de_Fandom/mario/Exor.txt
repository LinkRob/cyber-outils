'''Exor''' is a major antagonist in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|Super Mario RPG]]''. He is a high-ranking member of the [[Smithy Gang]] being the one who crashes into the [[Star Road (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Road]] and [[Bowser's Castle]], shattering the former into several pieces, when he drops from the sky.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
While [[Mario]] is fighting [[Bowser]] in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]], Exor crashes through the castle, smashing the [[Star Road (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Star Road]] in the process. The Star Road was shattered into seven pieces that several prominent members of the [[Smithy Gang]] guarded some of them. Exor itself is a dimensional gate. He can teleport the entire Smithy Gang from their dimension called Smithy's factor to the dimension that Mario's world exists in.

Exor is actually made of four different entities. His right and left eyes are separate from each other and can both attack on their own. The mouth, which is called Neosquid, also attacks as a separate entity. The pommel (or counter-weight) of the sword is a skull shaped beast who is the only part of the sword actually named Exor.

==Battle==
Mario, [[Geno]], [[Mallow]], Bowser, and [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] attack Bowser's keep after its been taken over by Smithy's gang. After defeating a samurai named [[Boomer (boss)|Boomer]], the five are rushed to the top of the castle where Exor is waiting. One of the eyes, or both have to be stunned (killed) in order to attack Exor.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Exor's Japanese name, Karibā, is an abbreviation of the legendary sword {{w|Excalibur}}. It is possible that the English name Exor is based on a shortening of the word Exclalibur.

==Trivia==
*If the player times right the [[Geno Whirl]] attack after stunning the eyes, they can take out Exor in one hit, because it will do 9999 damage to him.
