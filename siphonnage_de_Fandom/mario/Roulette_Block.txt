The '''Roulette Block''' is a type of [[block]] which mainly appears in a few of the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]] installments, the first being ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
The Roulette Block quickly alternates in a set order between one of the items or power-ups, the contents of which vary between appearances. [[Mario]] or another playable character can hit a Roulette Block to have it release whichever power-up is displayed on it. The speed at which the Roulette Block cycles between items varies between its appearances, but it usually occurs in under one second.

In ''Super Mario World'', a Roulette Block switches between a [[Super Mushroom]] to a [[Fire Flower]], then a [[Cape Feather]], and lastly a [[Super Star|Starman]] before the cycle repeats.
