{{Species|name = Doogan|image = [[File:Doogan.png]]|caption = A Doogan in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand Year Door]]''.|first = ''[[Paper Mario]]'' (2001)|games = ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand Year Door]]''|related = Dog|members = [[Rowf]]<br>[[Rhuff]]<br>[[Dupree]]<br>[[Arfur]]}}'''Doogans''' are dog-like creatures from the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==History==
Doogans were not named until [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'']], but appeared before that in the form of the badge shop owner [[Rowf]] and his son [[Rhuff]] who only appeared in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', a vast majority of Doogans are thugs seen around [[Rogueport]] and the [[Glitz Pit]].

==Trivia==
* In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'', the majority of the Doogans aren't given names. However, [[Arfur]] and [[Dupree]] are the only exceptions to this.
