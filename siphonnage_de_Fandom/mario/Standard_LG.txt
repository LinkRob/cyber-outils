The '''Standard LG''' is a kart in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. This is one of three karts [[Luigi]] has, the others being [[Streamliner]] and [[Poltergust 4000]].

== Description ==
The Standard LG is the same style as [[Mario]]'s [[Standard MR]], but is colored green, has different stats and the emblem is Luigi's signature green "L", unless the player has their own personal emblem enabled.

During races, the CPU controlled Luigi may pick this kart to race with more frequently than his other karts.

In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', the [[Standard Kart M]] replaces this kart.

== In ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' ==
As mentioned above, the Standard LG has different stats from Mario's Standard LG. It has improved [[speed]], [[acceleration]] and [[handling]], but has weaker [[drift]]. In terms of hidden stats, it has improved [[Off-Road|off-road]] and [[Mini-Turbo]]. However most of its stats are weaker than Luigi's [[Poltergust 4000]].

Its speed is shared with [[Waluigi]]'s [[Standard WL]], its acceleration is equivalent to Mario's [[Shooting Star (kart)|Shooting Star]], its [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]] stat is equivalent to Mario's Standard MR, [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Wildlife]] and Waluigi's [[Gold Mantis]]. It shares its [[Items (stat)|items]] stat is shared with all other characters' [[Kart|Standard Karts]].

Also, just like with all other characters' Standard Karts, Luigi's Standard LG has different stats in [[Battle Mode]].
