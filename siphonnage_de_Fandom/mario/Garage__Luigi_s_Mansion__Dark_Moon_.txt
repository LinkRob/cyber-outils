The '''Garage''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is where [[Luigi]] first acquires the [[Poltergust 5000 (object)|Poltergust 5000]].

== In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon'' ==
Luigi first heads into the Garage during the first mission of Gloomy Manor. Luigi discovers the Poltergust 5000 in the hood of a car and equips himself with it. However, Luigi accidentally locks himself in but manages to find the key and exits the Garage.

Unlike most rooms in Gloomy Manor, the Garage only needs to be visited once during only one mission during the game. However, when playing though [[Poltergust 5000 (mission)|A-1: Poltergust 5000]] a second time after acquiring the [[Dark-Light Device]], Luigi can head back into the Garage where he can capture a [[Boo]] named Boogie Woogie by shining his Dark Light near the door.
