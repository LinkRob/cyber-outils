'''Bullet Bill''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He is a representative of his species of the [[Bullet Bill|same name]], and shares the same appearance as them.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Bullet Bill is a track and field athlete and owner of the [[Mushroom Stadium]]. The player's character can challenge him to a race, and if they win, Bullet Bill gives them the [[Mini Soccer]] [[Gaddget]].

In a different quest, Bullet Bill allows the [[Kamek Krew]] to perform at the Mushroom Stadium for free, being a huge fan of theirs.
