'''Tug-o-Dorrie''' is a battle mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
The minigame's name and gameplay are based on {{p|tug of war}}. Four characters face each other on high platforms held by giant plastic Dorries, colored red, blue, green, and yellow respectively. Each of the characters are holding rope connected in the middle to a bumper-like object. Each character has to pull their opponents off their platforms and into the pool below while avoiding being pulled off themselves. Whoever is the last character remaining is the winner, and whoever is remaining by the time the 60-second [[time limit]] is up is the winner. If all four characters are still remaining, the minigame ends as a [[draw]].

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|A}} (repeatedly): Pull rope
