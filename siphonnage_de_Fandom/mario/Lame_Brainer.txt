The '''Lame Brainer''' is an object that only appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Oh, Brother!]]"

==Description==
The Lame Brainer is a special helmet designed by [[Kooky von Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kooky von Koopa]] to turn its wearer into a mindless drone, complete with a hypnotic voice and red pupils. When the Lame Brainer is removed, its effects wear off.

==Episode appearances==
==="Oh, Brother"===
After Kooky successfully captured [[Mario]] with his [[Plumber Pole]], he uses Lame Brainer on Mario to put him under his control. Kooky demonstrates the Lame Brainer's effects on Mario to [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] by presenting Mario with a bowl of [[pasta]]. Although Mario wants the pasta at first, Kooky tells Mario that he hated pasta. Unable to think for himself, Mario then agrees with Kooky and rejects the pasta. King Koopa then asks for Mario to fix the broken [[Power Shower]] pipe, to which Mario obliges.

When [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] arrives to save Mario, King Koopa orders Mario to attack Luigi with a wrench. Luigi cannot reason with Mario, and at one point, Luigi aggravates Mario by pulling down on the Lame Brainer, his overalls, and his mustache. By doing this, Luigi bypasses the effects the Lame Brainer has on Mario, overloading it. As a result, the Lame Brainer pops off of Mario's head, freeing him from his trance, and onto Kooky's head, forcing him into behaving like a mindless drone instead. Mario takes advantage of the situation by ordering Kooky to attack King Koopa, whom Kooky then begins to chase out of the dungeon.
