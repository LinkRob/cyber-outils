'''Stretch Blocks''' (or '''Stretch Platforms'''), are [[block]]s  collected of five parts that always increase and decrease vertically or horizontally, in some cases rotate between the two. They first to be seen in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', where they work for as bridges in exact levels, and they are also comprise blocks that take after pale-colored versions of [[Rotating Block]]s. 

Stretch Blocks also to be seen in some World-e levels in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'': [[Slip Slidin' Away (Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3)|Slip Slidin' Away]] and [[Rich with Ropes]]. In the game, they actually use the same  color from ''Super Mario World''.

Stretch Blocks reappear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', where they appear at the end of [[World 9-2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-2]]. In the game, they are colored orange and go up or down as they increase and decrease.

Stretch Blocks make their most recent appearance in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' in [[Rock-Candy Mines]] as an basic fragment of navigating; themselves are red and blue, and they will increase or decrease turn on which colored switch is hit. They also appear in few levels with their ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' appearance. This time, however, they wink before they will increase or decrease.

Stretch Blocks debut in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' on the Mushroom Kingdom U stage. They have the same exact behavior as in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' and ''New Super Mario Bros. U''. Stretch Blocks to be seen in the [[Acorn Plains]] of the game.
