A '''Rotten Mush''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
This very old and disgusting [[Mushroom (healing item)|Mushroom]] restores only 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] when eaten by a character. Rotten Mushes can only be received from either [[Grate Guy]] or [[Knife Guy]] when playing one of their games. The Rotten Mush is one of the two "gag" Mushroom items that can be won, the other being the [[Moldy Mush]].
