'''Flying Fish''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Flying Fish are small, round, fish-like [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] either pink or green in color. They have yellow eyes, two stubby, short arms, and a thin, pointed tail at the end.

===Behavior===
Pink Flying Fish pop up in groups and fly around the place, trying to hit [[Luigi]]. Green Flying Fish act similarly except they move considerably faster than their pink counterparts.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Flying Fish only appear in the [[Courtyard (Luigi's Mansion)|Courtyard]] and [[Pipe Room]], the former having the pink type and the latter having the green type. If Luigi gets hit by a Flying Fish, he takes five [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage. Like [[Mouse (ghost)|mice]] and [[Bat (ghost)|bats]], Flying Fish have no HP counter, and they can be vacuumed into the [[Poltergust 3000]] instantly.

In the [[Hidden Mansion]] of PAL versions and the Nintendo 3DS remake, Flying Fish also appear in the [[Ball Room]] and [[Butler's Room]]. Aside from this, the Flying Fish in the Courtyard are replaced with the green type.
