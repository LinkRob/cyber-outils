'''Power Flowers''' are a type of flower that powers up [[Mario]] in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. They replace the three [[cap]]s from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

When [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] gets one, he can [[Fire Breath|breathe fire]]. When Mario gets one, either he'll turn into a balloon to float higher places or use a wing cap (others can use it in [[VS. Mode (Super Mario 64 DS)|VS. Mode]]). When [[Luigi]] gets one, he'll turn invisible and walk through fences and immune to enemy attacks (except lava or quicksand), similarly to the [[Vanish Cap]] in ''Super Mario 64''. When [[Wario]] gets one, his body becomes metal, he can walk underwater, and becomes invincible, similarly to the [[Metal Cap]] in ''Super Mario 64''.
