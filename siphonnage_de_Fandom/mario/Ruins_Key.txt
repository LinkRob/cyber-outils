The '''Ruins Key''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Ruins Key is found in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]] and can open locked doors within the ruins. [[Mario]] can find these keys by solving the various of puzzles found within the ruins.
