'''Team Eggman Nega''' is a group in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]''. It is headed by [[Eggman Nega]], who is assisted by [[Rouge the Bat]] and [[Dry Bowser]].

== In ''Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games'' ==
Eggman Nega, Rouge, and Dry Bowser are all bosses in [[Legends Showdown]]. Here, just as all of the playable characters are about to claim the Legend Trophy, the group ambushes them. The player's team then has to face all three of them in the Winter Sports Champion Race.
