'''Reclusa World''' is a level in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It is the sixth and last level in the game, following the [[Stormstar Sea]], which, while also a location, serves as the fifth level.

== History ==
After it hatches when [[Zokket]], in his last moments, donates his life energy to the egg that it is trapped in, [[Reclusa]] immediately erects its own lair, the [[Soli-Tree]], and goes on a rampage throughout [[Concordia]], with many of its victims being trapped in [[Reclusa Dream Flower|dream flowers]].

[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have to collect [[Bond (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Bond]]s from various persons throughout Concordia to bypass the barrier around Reclusa's fortress.

== Enemies ==
All enemies that were encountered earlier return but have been infused with [[Glohm]], with the exceptions of the [[Bob-omb]], [[Dry Bones]], [[Fire Bro]], [[Ice Bro]], and [[Spiny]], which vanish outright, and the [[Seedle]]s on [[Conductor Island]] (The ones in other locations and their variants contract the virus.). The Glohm-infected enemies are also changed according to the following:
*The enemies can be affected by gear and items that react to Glohm, such as the Anti-Glohm Specialist, and their attacks, if not avoided, can pass on Glohm infection, and their attacks occur at a faster speed.
*[[Seedle]] -- May create a spiked energy shield or explode into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*[[Guardarm]] -- Counterattacks after being attacked.
*[[Gorumbla]] -- May create a spiked energy shield.
*[[Soreboar]] -- Small specimens create a spiked energy shield while large specimens counterattack after being attacked.
*[[Bumbleshoot]] and Parashoot -- Counterattacks after being attacked.
*[[Invizilizard]] and Invizilizard R -- Explodes into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*Seedle R -- Does not give boosts to [[Speed (Initiative)|Speed]] to allies. May create a spiked energy shield or explode into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*Hydro Seedle -- Does not heal allies. May create a spiked energy shield and explodes into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*[[Capnap]] -- Explodes into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*[[Drillby]] and Drillby R -- Counterattacks after being attacked.
*[[Snaptor]] -- May create a spiked energy shield.
*[[Spikely]] -- May create a spiked energy shield.
*[[Mettozokettohei]] -- Creates copies of itself.
*[[Shower Fish]] -- Increases stats each turn.
*Togezokettohei -- Counterattacks after being attacked.
*Capnap R -- Does not heal allies that have taken damage, increases stats each turn, and explodes into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*[[Foopfly]] and Foopfly R -- Explodes into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*[[Magmad]] and [[Icead#Variants|spiked Icead]] -- Creates copies of itself.
*[[Curlup]] -- Increases stats each turn.
*[[Palooka]] -- Increases stats each turn.
*[[Piranha Plant]] -- Counterattacks after being attacked, does not perform any group attacks, and explodes into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*[[Spike]] -- Increases stats each turn.
*Ringuzokettohei -- May create a spiked energy shield.
*[[Millton]] -- Increases stats each turn.
*[[Goomba]] -- Creates copies of itself.
*[[Koopa Paratroopa]] -- Creates copies of itself.
*[[Magikoopa]] -- Increases stats each turn.
*[[Hammer Bro.]] -- Increases stats each turn.
*[[Boo]] -- Explodes into a fiery ring when destroyed.
*[[Shy Guy]] -- Creates copies of itself.
