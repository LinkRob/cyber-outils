'''Paris Promenade 3''' is one of the three layouts of [[Paris Promenade (combined)|Paris Promenade]] in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As its name implies, it is the third layout of Paris Promenade, and the only one to use the mid-east path fully. Its debut occured in the Paris Tour of 2021.

== Layout ==
Racers taking this layout start like the [[Paris Promenade|first route]], but they go around the north half of the roundabout with the Arc de Triomphe. After the road transitions into the south path, they glide over the Jardins du Trocadéro, though once they land, they turn left and head straight to the Luxor Obelisk from the south, turning right and taking the east exit. At the roundabout at the far east, the racers turn left, taking the north exit, then transition to the north path until they reach the northern terminus of the mid-east road. There, they turn left and approach the Luxor Obelisk from the south, taking a right turn and exiting the roundabout to the west to return to the start/finish line.

This route is used for the second half of Lap 1 and the entirety of Lap 2, but the south half of the roundabout with the Arc de Triomphe is usable just like in Paris Promenade. In Lap 3, the racers have the option to traverse this route in reverse after they take the north path out of the Arc de Triomphe or continue south onto Paris Promenade 1 in reverse, and as with the first two laps, both sides of the roundabout can be driven on.
