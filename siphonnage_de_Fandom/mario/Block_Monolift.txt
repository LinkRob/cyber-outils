'''Block Monolifts''' are an enemy in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Block Monolifts are a variant of [[Monolift]]s with a dark-blue body and entirely yellow eyes.

===Behavior===
Block Monolifts enter battle carrying blocks of ice containing various types of headgear that prevent Mammoshka's weakness being exploited. This can include a wooden pail, a slightly cracked blue helmet, or a spiked red helmet, each with varying degrees of effectiveness. Block Monolifts are very weak, being only at one [[Heart Point|HP]].

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
Block Monolifts only appear in [[Mammoshka]]'s battle in [[Mount Pajamaja]].
