'''Mini-Planet Daredevil Run''' is the second [[mission]] of [[Battle Belt Galaxy]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Objective==
Mini-Planet Daredevil Run is the [[Daredevil Comet]] equivalent of [[Mini-Planet Mega-Run]]. As such, the objective is the same, except [[Mario]] has to avoid taking any damage, otherwise he loses a life.
