The '''Manager''' is a boss who appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He is the second-to-weakest member of the [[Smithy Gang]] and works in [[The Factory]].

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
===Battle===
[[File:Manager SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
In battle, the Manager attacks mainly with physical attacks as his only special move is [[Spritz Bomb]]. He is accompanied by three [[Pounder]]s.

====Strategy====
The Manager doesn't have any weaknesses but his magic defense is rather low so [[Mario]]'s team should stick to magic attacks until he is defeated.

==Trivia==
*Before the Manager was defeated by Mario and his allies, he mentioned the creation of Mack 2 and Yaridovich 2, presumably referring to the [[Machine Made]] variants.
*His [[Psychopath]] thought is interesting as he stated he has been working for 25 years. This can be implied that the [[Smithy Gang]] has been around for that long (or even longer) before attempting to conquer Mario's world.
*The Manager resembles the [[Director]] except the Director is red in color.
