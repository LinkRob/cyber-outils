'''Jessica Lynn Chisum''' (born June 1, 1975) is an American voice actor and voice coach. She was the voice of [[Plum]] and [[Sherry]] in [[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]], as well as the voice of [[Birdo]] and [[Nina (Mario Tennis series)|Nina]] in [[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]].

* ''[[Mario Tennis (Nintendo 64)|Mario Tennis]]'' – [[Birdo]], [[Nina (Mario Tennis series)|Nina]]
