=== General ===
The placeholder graphics for the Castle Bridge tiles (from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''), which went unused in the previous game, can also be found in the game's files for this one too. The snow theme (both day and night) and sky theme even have unique bridge sprites! Strangely, almost every finished castle bridge design has brown bits at the bottom that are seemingly from the placeholder tiles. They are probably just a minor mistake done when Nintendo added the finished sprites.

== Unused Behaviors ==

==== Dying in the Hub World ====
If the player hacks the game to remove the invisible edge barriers in Story Mode and falls down the front, the screen will fade out and the game will bring Mario back to the middle of the hub world. This was likely intended as a failsafe if the player managed to glitch past the invisible walls.

=== Story Mode-Exclusive Parts ===
These objects cannot be placed from the Course Editor for unknown reasons as they are exclusive to the Story Mode. However, hacking it into the game shows more unused behaviors that were planned to be used in the final game. These elements can be messed with by hacking the game's files.

==== Stone ====
The list below explains what the stone object can do.

* The object can disappear completely if the player switches into the ''Super Mario Bros.'' game mode as that style doesn't allow the player to hold an item.
* The object can be enlarged unlike other objects which can be picked up, taking up a '''2×2''' tile space. Big Stones cannot be picked up or tongued by Yoshi, and do not crush the player despite their size.
* The object can be given Wings or a Parachute. Winged Stones can be stood on, and hitting a Winged Stone from below will not destroy the wings.
* The object can be placed inside [[Koopa Clown Car|Koopa Clown Cars]].
* When thrown, the object is able to kill most enemies in one hit, including the Angry Sun. Throwing the object four times at Bowser will defeat him.
* When placed into a Pipe or a Blaster, only one stone will be produced at a time. If the original stone disappears, another one will be produced.
* The object reacts to low gravity in the Night Sky and Night Airship themes.

==== Toad ====
Hacking Toad into the course of the game will result in him having his functionality be limited and broken. The list below explains the results in the course editor.

* They cannot be used in any style other than the ''New Super Mario Bros. U''.
* Their sprites are only visible when idle as they are invisible in the editor and when they are following the player.
* The most Toads the player can collect is 16. However the game stops the visual and sound effects for collecting more than 10 of them.
* Idle Toads cannot be enlarged, given Wings or a Parachute, tongued by Yoshi, or placed in Clown Cars or Lakitu's Cloud.
* They can be placed into Pipes, Blasters, ? Blocks, and Lakitu.
* They are immune to Grinders, Twisters, and Icicles.
* They do not interact with Trampolines.
* If the Toads touch the Moon itself, they will take instant damaged.

=== Clear Conditions ===
The file that contains the descriptions of the Clear Conditions ('''Message\ReferenceMsg_*\GoalCondition.msbt''') contains two used pieces of text for the Toad rescue Clear Conditions, labeled '''01''' ("Reach the goal with all [number] Toads in tow.") and '''03''' ("Reach the goal with [number] Toads in tow"), but none labeled '''02'''. Judging from the numbering of similar sets of strings, '''02''' would've most likely said "Reach the goal with at least [number] Toads in tow."
