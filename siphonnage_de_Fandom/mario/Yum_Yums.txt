The '''Yum Yums''' are a group of characters who serve as [[Mona]]'s pets in the [[WarioWare (series)|''WarioWare'' series]]. A few of them are often stored inside of her [[Mona's Scooter|scooter]], and prove useful whenever she gets into roadside conflict. Yum Yums first appear in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.
