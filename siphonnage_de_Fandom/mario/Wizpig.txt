}}'''Wizpig''' is the primary antagonist in ''[[Diddy Kong Racing]]'' and its remake, ''[[Diddy Kong Racing DS]]''. He resides in [[Future Fun Land]] and tries to take over [[Timber's Island]]. He also turns [[Drumstick the Rooster|Drumstick]] into a frog and brainwashes the island's four bosses.

[[Diddy Kong]], along with his friends, must stop Wizpig and restore Timber's Island to its regular state.

==Trivia==
*He rides on a giant rocket when he is challenged to the game's final race.
