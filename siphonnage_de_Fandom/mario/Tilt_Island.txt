'''Tilt Island''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It can be played at the [[Flipside Arcade]].

==Gameplay==
Tilt Island is played by holding the [[Wii Remote]] horizontally, like in the main game but unlike other minigames. The player can play as either [[Mario]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Bowser]], or [[Luigi]] and move their character by tilting the Wii Remote left and right accordingly.

The goal is for the character to catch falling desserts (including [[Peachy Peach]]es, [[Mango Juice]]s, and [[Fruit Parfait]]s) for [[point]]s and to try to collect enough to advance to the next round. However, they must avoid the [[Squig]]s, [[Spiny|Spinies]], and [[Spiky Tromp]]s that fall on the island. Enemies can be defeated for a number of [[point]]s, depending on the type of enemy. If the character either touches an enemy or is tilted off of the island, they lose a [[Extra life|life]]. There are three lives in total, so if the player loses all of them, it is a [[Game Over]]. The game has a total of twenty levels, and if the player clears all of them, they win.

Unlike other minigames in the Flipside Arcade, the player's performance in Tilt Island can be influenced by whom they play as. Peach and Bowser tend to be larger targets and move at a slower pace, leaving them a lot more prone to being damaged by falling enemies. Contrariwise, Mario and Luigi are smaller and faster.

===Bonus points===
Collecting only one type of dessert in a stage earns the player a bonus for that stage equal to the amount of points received from the desserts.

==Items==
There are also a few types of items that randomly fall out of the sky, and they can be collected randomly. Each item is worth 3000 points.

*[[Mushroom]]: Adds an extra life to the player's total.
*[[Flower Point|Flower]]: The player's character starts spinning and can knock away monsters for points.
*[[Super Star|Star]]: Clears all enemies from the screen.
