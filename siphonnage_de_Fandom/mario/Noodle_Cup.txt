'''Noodle Cup''' is [[Orbulon]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]] ''in the I.Q. folder. The microgame reappears in [[WarioWare Gold|''WarioWare Gold'']]'' ''as [[Mona]]'s microgame.

== Gameplay ==
The microgame uses the Amidakuji concept. The kettle faces the screen. The objective is that the player must pour water from the kettle to reach the noodle cup. The player wins if the water goes through the right pipe.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' There are three horizontal pipes.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' There are four horizontal pipes.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' There are five or six horizontal pipes.

=== ''WarioWare Gold'' ===
The microgame returns as [[Mona]]'s The background was updated to yellow. Instead of the kettle facing the screen, the kettle faces left.
* '''1st level difficulty:''' There are three horizontal pipes.
* '''2nd level difficulty:''' There are four horizontal pipes.
* '''3rd level difficulty:''' There are six horizontal pipes.
