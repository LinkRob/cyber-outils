'''A Dry Bone to Pick''' is the first level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 19.

== Layout ==
First, second  and final battle consists of seven [[Dry Bones]] and a Dry Bones as a captain.

In the first battle, the Dry Bones captain knows [[Deny]].

In the second battle, the Dry Bones captain knows Deny and [[Chance]].

In the final battle, the Dry Bones captain knows [[Rally]].

The level can be replayed to recruit up to 2 Dry Bones.

* [[Dry Bones]] (level 19-20)
