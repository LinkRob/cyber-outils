'''Wendy's Thwomp Castle''', or '''Frosted Glacier-Castle''', is the castle course of [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''. 
==Features==
It is unlocked by completing [[Peek-a-Boo Ghost House]], and its own completion unlocks [[All Aboard!]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': Go in the gap in between the second and third normal sized Thwomps and wall jump to avoid death.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Let the fifth normal-sized Thwomp land and then slide the wall and then wall-jump to the Star Coin before the Thwomp ascends back up.
*'''Star Coin 3''': By the last Mega Thwomp, wall-jump over the Thwomp, and then go into the small hole near where the Thwomp lands to a hidden passageway with the Star Coin above in the middle of the passageway.

==Hidden Luigi image==
After the fourth to last Mega Thwomp descends, look at the picture in that gap from the Thwomp of Super Luigi from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' jumping.
