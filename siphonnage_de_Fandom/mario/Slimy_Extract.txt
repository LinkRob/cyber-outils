The '''Slimy Extract''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.
==Effects==

Made from a [[Slimy Shroom]], this mysterious Shroom extract cures [[Cursya|curses]] and [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
