'''Doctor Vulko''', also simply known as '''Vulko''', formatted '''Dr. Vulko''' in-game, is a character from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. He is a Concordian doctor from Skorcheen Island.

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
Doctor Vulko is an elderly-looking Concordian with red hair and orange skin who wears fire-themed attire.

== History ==
Vulko makes his debut during the [[Brrrning Sea]] chapter when [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Snoutlet]] explore [[Skorcheen Island]] to cure [[Prince Chilliam]]'s [[Glohm]] infection (courtesy of [[Extension Corps|Ten]]). When the Bros. find him in his laboratory, they the path blocked due to the Connectar Flower bridge being out, so they reconnect Skorcheen to provide a path for Vulko to take to [[Shipshape Island]] from his laboratory. There, Vulko reveals his cure for the Glohm infection: an [[Ampberry]], which he feeds one to Chilliam. Chilliam, restored back to his old self, marries [[Princess Burnadette|Burnadette]].

Vulko makes another appearance in the [[Gulchrock Sea]] chapter, where his Ampberry supply is running low due to the large number of Glohm zombies. Mario and Luigi head off to Wayaway Island to find more Ampberries for him, which only grow there. After the Bros. get some Ampberries, Vulko resumes treating his patients.

Vulko is later one of [[Reclusa]]'s victims when it awakens, as he gets trapped in one of the flowers the fiend uses to trap its victims in dreams. Vulko is later freed along with the rest of the victims after the Bros. kill Reclusa.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Doctor Vulko is named after volcanoes. His name may also be influenced by the common name "Marco."
