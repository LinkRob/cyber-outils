'''Jump Man''' is an arcade game based on ''[[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]]'' seen in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''. The machine for the game is seen being played by [[Giuseppe]] in [[Punch-Out!! Pizzeria (Illumination)|Punch Out!! Pizzeria]]. The name is derived from "Jumpman," a name used for [[Mario]] in some early paratext associated with Donkey Kong.
