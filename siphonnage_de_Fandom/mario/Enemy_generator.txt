An '''enemy generator''' is an obstacle in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
Enemy generators are round, ornately decorated blue vase-like pods attached to the ground. Whenever [[Wario]] approaches an enemy generator, it begins to dispense both small and large [[Magon]]s, as well as [[Spiked Ball]]s in later courses.

Enemy generators are unaffected by most of [[Wario]]'s attacks, although an enemy generator eventually runs out of enemies. When this occurs, an energy generator appears to melt and releases six large [[coin]]s. Certain enemy generators can only be destroyed if Wario uses one of the spawned enemies to hit it using one of his [[Mad Move]]s. Certain enemy generators are indestructible, so they can neither be affected by a Mad Move nor can they melt.

==In ''Wario World''==
Enemy generators first appear in [[Greenhorn Ruins]] and appear in all subsequent levels, usually in areas where an enemy has to be used to complete a puzzle, such as near a [[Swirly Spinner]] or steel [[trapdoor (Wario World)|trapdoor]]. Like enemies in general, an Enemy generator respawns if Wario is away from it long enough.
