'''Chicken Ducks''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Chicken Ducks are large, fat birds who wear a metal headpiece on their head. In their artwork, Chicken Ducks are shown to have yellow feathers.

=== Behavior ===
Chicken Ducks can often be found asleep. If Wario awakens one, it flies straight upward to retreat from him. Chicken Ducks do not actively attack Wario nor can they damage him. If Wario throws an enemy at a Chicken Duck, it eats the enemy and flies upwards in a panic, dropping three [[10 Gold Coin]]s before flying offscreen.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
[[File:Chicken Duck WLSML3 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Chicken Ducks are first encountered in [[Course No.12]].
