'''Joe''' is a [[human]] golfer and a character who appears in the {{series|Mario Golf}}. He first appears in ''[[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Mario Golf]]'' for [[Game Boy Color]].

==Personality==
Joe has a showoff personality. He was formerly a disco pro before he became a golfer.

==In {{series|Mario Golf}}==
===''Mario Golf'' (Game Boy Color)===
Joe is one of six starter characters, alongside [[Azalea]], [[Kid]], [[Luigi]], [[Mario]], and [[Sherry]].

===''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' ([[Nintendo 64]])===
Although the game released beforehand, this is Joe's technical debut, as he could only become a playable character by using the [[Transfer Pak]] from the Game Boy Color game of the same name.

===''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''===
Joe is an unlockable playable character.
