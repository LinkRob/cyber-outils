'''Nina''' is a non-playable character who first appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. She is [[Yamamura]]'s assistant in [[Yamamura's Dojo]].

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Nina is a woman with short, brunette hair and a short ahoge, her eyes are dark green and there are freckles between her nose and eyes. Her skin is fair, and she is seen wearing a yellow ''Super Mario Maker 2'' shirt.

=== Personality traits ===
Unlike Yamamura, who is more solemn when it comes to teaching the player how to make courses, Nina often cracks jokes from time-to-time. She finds ways to entertain the audience to keep them engaged. Despite that, she also is interested in educating the crowd about the skills of becoming a successful course maker in the game.

== In ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' ==
Nina, along with Yamamura, teaches the player on how to construct courses in the game. 

== Trivia ==
* Nina seems to be a replacement of Mary O., who appeared in the original Super Mario Maker game.
