The '''Souvenir Shop''' is an [[Item Shop]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== In ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ==
The Souvenir Shop is located in [[Glitzville]] opposite of the [[Fresh Juice Bar]]. The Souvenir Shop sells exclusive items that cannot be bought anywhere else such as [[Point Swap]]s.
