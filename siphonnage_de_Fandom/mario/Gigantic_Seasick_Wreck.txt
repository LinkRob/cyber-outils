==Features==
This level is a shorter remix of [[Spooky Seasick Wreck]], but with [[Ty-Foo|Ty-Foos]] instead of [[Boo|Boos]].

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Near the start of the level, obtained by collecting 8 [[Green Coin]]s.
*'''Green Star 2''': On the second ship. To the right of the Warp Box that leads to the second phase, on a narrow walkway guarded by an [[Octoomba]].
*'''Green Star 3''':  In the narrow walkway section in the second area, about the end of the walkways. The player needs to use the [[Mega Mushroom]] to grab this Green Star.
