The '''Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant''' is an alternate form of the [[Piranha Plant]] from ''[[Super Mario-kun]]''.

== Biography ==
=== Boom!! One Shot Kill! A Plan to Join Forces ===
{{Main|Super Mario-Kun Vol 1 2}}
After [[Mario (Super Mario-kun)|Mario]] performed the "SNES Controller Nunchuck" against the Piranha Plants. A Piranha Plant transformed into the Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant. After it performed the "[[Iron Root Kick]]" onto Mario, it opened its mouth to eat the trio. Mario spot its weak point through the uvala. Mario and Yoshi's "[[Mario & Yoshi's Rolling Attack]]" puzzled the Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant and broke his head. Colossal Invincible Piranha Plant fell in defeat.
