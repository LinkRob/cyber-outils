The '''Darkmess Tentacle''' is a tentacle that was formed from the source of [[Darkmess Energy]]. It made its appearance in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''.

== Description ==
The Darkmess Tentacle is a black tentacle. According to the Jeanie, the player can reverse the ill-effects of the tentacle on the weather and purify its energy to use it to create a [[Warp Tunnel]].

== Etymology ==
Darkmess is a portmanteau of ''darkness'' and ''mess''.
