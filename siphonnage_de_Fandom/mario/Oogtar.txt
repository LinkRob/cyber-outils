'''Oogtar''' is a [[Cave People|cave boy]] resident of [[Dome City]] in [[Dinosaur World]]. He sometimes causes trouble and appears exclusively in the ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]'' television series. He wears a green leopard skin with orange spots and has blond hair. He acts as a replacement for [[Toad (DiC cartoons)|Toad]] in the show and is voiced by [[John Stocker]], who also voiced Toad.

==Profile==
Oogtar is a mischief-making Cave Person, as he pulls all sorts of pranks on everyone. [[Yoshi (DiC Cartoons)|Yoshi]] is seemingly his best friend, as evidenced by the fact that Oogtar gives Yoshi the nickname "Dino Dude" and him appearing with the other. He lives with a family of two unnamed parents and an unnamed sister.

He commonly speaks in broken sentences stereotypical of cavemen, usually characterized by the absence of articles, copulas, and verb conjugation. Some of his speech also resembles {{wp|beatnik}} slang, and he also frequently uses {{wp|illeism}} to refer to himself.

==History==
His first appearance was "[[Ghosts 'R' Us]]". Yoshi meets him in the [[Forest of Illusion (DiC Cartoons)|Enchanted Forest]] after [[Mario (DiC cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC cartoons)|Luigi]], and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] are kidnapped by the [[Magikoopa|Koopa Wizard]] [[Wizenheimer]]. Oogtar aids Yoshi in rescuing the three.

He has also appeared in other episodes like "[[Party Line]]" and "[[Rock TV]]". Three episodes which prominently feature Oogtar are "[[The Night Before Cave Christmas]]" and "[[A Little Learning]]" and the one above, "Ghosts 'R' Us". He appears in other episodes as well, but not as a significant character. However, in "Rock TV", it is revealed that Oogtar has a sister and parents.

==Concept and creation==
Oogtar was conceived by ''Super Mario World'''s story editor [[Phil Harnage]].<ref name="Tales">"[https://talesfromthecollection.com/phil-harnage-interview/ From Console to Cartoon: Phil Harnage on Writing DiC’s Video Game Classics]". Tales from the Collection. Retrieved July 1, 2025.</ref> Recalling the genesis behind the character in a 2025 interview, Harnage explained that Oogtar's inclusion was thought up by [[DIC Entertainment]] president [[Andy Heyward]], who felt that Yoshi was "too nice" to get into serious trouble and thought the show needed to give him a negative foil that would push him to do mischief.<ref name="Tales"></ref>
