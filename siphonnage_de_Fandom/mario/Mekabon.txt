'''Mekabons''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. Mekabons are small robots under [[Tatanga]]'s command that only appear in [[World 2-2 (Super Mario Land)|World 2-2]] of the [[Muda Kingdom]].

==History==
When a Mekabon spots [[Mario]], it will take off its head and attempt to hit him with it. Mario can defeat the Mekabons by jumping on them when their head is attached. In doing so, the player earns 800 [[point]]s.
