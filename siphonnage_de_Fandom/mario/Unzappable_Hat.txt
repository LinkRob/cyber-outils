An '''Unzappable Hat''' is an object in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Unzappables]]."

==Description==
Unzappable Hats are a type of hat that protects its wearer from all outside attacks. Several variations of the hat exist, but they all have the same effect. Unzappable Hats sold at the [[Gangster Supply Store]] in [[Crime Land]].

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
As part of his latest scheme, [[Al Koopone]] orders several Unzappable Hats from the Gangster Supply Store, and he orders [[Mouser]] and a [[Koopa Troopa]] to each try one and [[Tryclyde]] to try three, as he has three heads. Al Koopone then shoots the three of them with bullets from a [[Snifit]], scaring them, but the Unzappable Hats protect them from taking any damage. Wearing the Unzappable Hats, Al Koopone and the Koopa Pack go to rob the [[First Mushroom Bank]]. [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and his friends attempt to stop them with [[Vegetable]]s, but the Unzappable Hats protect the Koopa Pack from taking any damage.

Mario, [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]], and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] go to [[Koopa Klub]] and steal the Unzappable Hats, which are stored in the coat check room, to wear for themselves. When the group returns to confront Al Koopone again, their Unzappable Hats protect them from taking any damage from the Koopa Pack's Snifits. Because of the Unzappable Hats, Mario's group eventually manage to defeat the Koopa Pack and chase Al Koopone out of Crime Land.
