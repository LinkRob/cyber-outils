The''' Giant Robotic Bomb '''is an object in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It only appears in one stage in a single room. Towards the beginning of the level, [[Luigi]] enters the [[Workshop]] where he encounters two [[Greenie]]s with Hammers and Welding Masks banging on the bomb. To progress, Luigi must stun the bomb with his [[strobulb]] and the Greenies will be stunned by the resulting explosion. It resembles a large [[Robotic Bombs|Robotic Bomb]].
