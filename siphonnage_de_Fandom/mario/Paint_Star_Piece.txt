'''Paint Star Pieces''' are an item in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The five Paint Star Pieces are the pieces of the blue [[Mini Paint Star]] of [[Sacred Forest]], similarly in concept to [[Star Chip]]s in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. All Paint Star Pieces can only be collected after [[Mario]] defeats a certain enemy. Once Mario has collected all five Paint Star Pieces, he can repair the Mini Paint Star.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
The Mini Paint Star was taken apart by several [[Small Goomba Gang]]s, who then scattered the pieces throughout the level. Their locations are as follows:

#Held by a [[Small Cheep Cheep]], which Mario has to defeat to be able to obtain the Paint Star Piece.
#Located some place on the ground. At first, when Mario tries to collect this Paint Star Piece, some [[Small Goomba Wheel]]s proceed to attack him, and Mario has to defeat them before he can collect it.
#In the [[Old Sacred Tree]], which Mario has to hit with his [[hammer]] to make the piece fall. However, this also causes a Small Goomba Gang to fall out, and Mario has to defeat them before he can obtain the Paint Star Piece.
#In the middle of several Small Goomba Gangs, which Mario has to defeat before he can obtain the Paint Star Piece.
#On the ceiling of a cave, Mario must hit the wall with his hammer to make the piece fall. However, a [[Small Buzzy Beetle Gang]] falls along with the Paint Star Piece, and Mario has to defeat them before he can obtain the Paint Star Piece.
