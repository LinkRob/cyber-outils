'''Reclusa Dream Blocks''' are objects that can be found in [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. They only appear in the false [[Mushroom Kingdom]] that [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] get sent to. After the Bros. escape their trance, Reclusa Dream Blocks cannot be encountered again.

== Description ==
Reclusa Dream Blocks look like the [[Mystery Block]], but their question mark is inverted. Hitting a Reclusa Dream Block will not reap the standard rewards, but is necessary to progress.

== History ==
[[Mario (character)|Mario]] and his brother [[Luigi]] get put to sleep during their invasion of the [[Soli-Tree]] courtesy of [[Reclusa Dream Flower|Reclusa Dream Flowers]]. As they walk around the dream, which takes place in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] where a party is being thrown, they notice that some of the Mystery Blocks' question marks are inverted. Upon hitting the first of these weird blocks, the block collapses and a red mist pops out. Another weird block can later be found at the southeastern corner of the party. Upon hitting this block, [[Reclusa]] bursts from the remains, at which point [[Snoutlet]] realises that it is pulling something. After being caught again, it reveals what is happening. The block appears again in several more locations, after which Reclusa gives up and allows the Bros. to escape its trap.
