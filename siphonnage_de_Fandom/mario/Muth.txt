'''Muths''' are enemies resembling mammoths exclusively found in Chapter 5-1 in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.



==History==
They have 100 HP and an attack of 5. You get a lot of points for defeating them, so you should. The best way to defeat them is to use [[Bowser]]'s Flame Breath on them. They have stronger versions of them called a [[Mega Muth]] and a [[Dark Muth]]. Muths sometimes drop a [[Bone-In Cut]] and it's the only way to let one.
