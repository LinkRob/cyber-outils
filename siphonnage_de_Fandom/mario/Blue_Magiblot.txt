}}A '''Blue Magiblot''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Blue Magiblots are found mainly in [[Castle Bleck]] in all areas. Blue Magiblots mainly attack by hiding in the shadows and attacking with a magic blob attack when it's victim's guard is down. Blue Magiblots are often found with their other counterparts, the [[Red Magiblot]]s and the [[Yellow Magiblot]]s. In the Japanese version, there is also Green Magiblots.
