|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Mega Bomb''' is a special move used by [[Bombette]] in the game ''[[Paper Mario]]''. This is Bombette's strongest move and it requires 8 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. Bombette can use the Mega Bomb once she is upgraded to the [[Ultra Rank]].

==Usage==

To use Mega Bomb, the player must repeatedly press the {{button|n64|a}} button as Bombette walks toward the enemes (which is similar to [[Power Bomb]] except a little harder). Afterwards, Bombette will explode greatly hitting every enemy on the screen (including flying and ceiling enemies).

==Trivia==

*Mega Bomb consumes more FP than any other move in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
*Mega Bomb is comparable to [[Admiral Bobbery]]'s [[Bob-omblast]] move from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
