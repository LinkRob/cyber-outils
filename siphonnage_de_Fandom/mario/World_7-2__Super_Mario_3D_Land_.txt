'''World 7-2''' is the second [[level]] of [{World 7 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 7]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'' as well as the thirty-fourth one overall.

== Layout ==
World 7-2 features A lot of dangerous traps and snares than most levels in the game. At the start of the level, Mario must pass over several spiked bards and even a couple of Chain Chomps. Past the first room, Mario finds himself on a bridge of a castle, having to dodge spiked bars rolling down the swaying bridge. Past the bridge is a second inside portion of the level, featuring more Chomps, spiked bars, and platforms with retracting spikes. On the other side of the room is a door that leads to the final portion of the level. In this final area, Mario must jump over two swaying platforms, the latter of which sports a spiked bar. Upon jumping the second platform, Mario reaches the level's [[Flagpole]].

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] can be found by utilizing the red [[Exclamation Block]] above one of the first Chain Chomps of the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': Along the swaying bridge, Mario can find the second Star Medal on a small pair on blocks that upon standing on, will fall into the abyss below.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal is in the second inside portion of the level. The medal is easily seen between two fast-swinging spiked bars.
