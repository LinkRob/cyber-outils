'''Elite Pestnuts''' are enemies in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are a stronger variant of [[Pestnut]] with increased [[Power]] and faster [[Speed]].

==Battle==
Elite Pestnuts have higher stats than [[Pestnut]]s in the [[Chucklehuck Woods]]. They still behave the same way with faster speed. They cannot be stomped unless either Bro are wearing the correct clothing. With some damage, it will lose its spikes revealing a [[Beanie]] or [[Gold Beanie]] that is slowly floating and cannot attack. Unlike their weaker counterparts, they never appear in the overworld, and their presence only occur in battles with Bloopers in the [[Oho Ocean Seabed]].
