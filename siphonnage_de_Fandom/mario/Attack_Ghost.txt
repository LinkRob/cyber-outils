'''Attack Ghosts''' are enemies in ''[[Yoshi's Crafted World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Attack Ghosts are giant cardboard [[Boo Guy]]s with a red body, a gold scythe, and a [[Shy Guy]] mask with evil, purple eyes.

===Behavior===
An Attack Ghost's presence is not immediately evident, but when a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] approaches an Attack Ghost, it suddenly appears, marked by a ghoulish laughter, and proceeds to chase him down while rapidly swinging its scythe.

==In ''Yoshi's Crafted World''==
Attack Ghosts are only encountered in two levels, [[Haunted Maker Mansion]] and [[Cardstock Carnival]]. Attack Ghosts cannot be defeated, but a Yoshi can temporarily push one back by throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at it.
