'''''Super Mario''''' is a series of platforming games, generally 2D, within the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] starring [[Mario]]. They mostly revolve around the titular plumber who goes on many adventures to save [[Princess Peach]] from the evil [[Bowser]] and his [[Koopa Troop]], often with the help of his brother [[Luigi]].
[[File:World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.) Start.png|thumb|235x235px|''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', the series' origin title]]
It is the primary series of the flagship [[Nintendo]] franchise. ''Super Mario'' as a whole made its debut in the iconic 1985 [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] title, ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', and the latest so far is ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' released on the [[Nintendo Switch]] in 2023. There are 21 games released in the core series as of April 2025.

== Trivia ==

* The ''Super Mario'' series was technically co-developed with [[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' franchise]], as their [[Super Mario Bros.|first]] [https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda games] were made simultaneously by the same development team, and some games shared ideas between each other. Coincidentally, they also share the same amount of core installments released.
* This sub-series of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] has sold over 430 million copies worldwide so far, making it the sixth-best selling video game series, beaten by ''Tetris'', the ''Call of Duty'' series, Nintendo's own ''[[pokemon:Pokémon_(franchise)|Pokémon]]'', and ''Grand Theft Auto.''
