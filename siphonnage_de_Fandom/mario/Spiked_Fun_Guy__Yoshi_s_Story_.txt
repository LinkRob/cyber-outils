'''Spiked Fun Guys''' are a type of enemy encountered in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''. They have a purple, prickly, ball-shaped body. Despite this, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can still swallow a Spiked Fun Guy and convert it into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]].
