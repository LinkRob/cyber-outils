A '''Rat Funk''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Rat Funks are bipedal blue mice, and they have purple relatives named [[Alley Rat]]s.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Rat Funks are encountered in [[Kero Sewers]] and the [[Forest Maze]].

=== Battle ===
Rat Funks attack physically by slapping their tails at one of [[Mario]]'s party members. They can also use [[Poison (move)|Poison]] to try and [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] a party member. However, Rat Funks often flee than fight usually escaping from battle the first chance they get.

==== Strategy ====
Rat Funks are weak to fire meaning Mario can use moves like [[Fire Orb]] or [[Super Flame]] to quickly take them out before they flee from battle.
