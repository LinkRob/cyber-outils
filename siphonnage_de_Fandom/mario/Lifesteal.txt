'''Lifesteal''' is an effect that can happen in several games. It is first seen in [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga|''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'']] and returns in several other games.

== History ==

=== In the ''Mario & Luigi'' franchise ===

==== In ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'' ====
Lifesteal made its debut in [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'']], where [[Sunnycide]] can attempt to inhale the Bros. in his upright state; he can be fended off by smashing his face with a [[Hammer]] strike, but if he catches the target, he will heal 20 hit points on top of dealing damage.

==== In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' ====
Sockops can counter jump attacks by swallowing their would-be attacker in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', then digesting them with a Lifesteal effect, dealing ten damage once every 2/3 second, then healing by the same amount as the total damage.

==== In ''Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam'' ====
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', [[Dry Bowser]] has an attack where he tries to suck the Bros. up. [[Mario (character)|Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] must use their [[Hammer|Hammers]] to hit the [[Spiked Ball|spike balls]] and [[Dry Bones|Dry Boneses]] that Dry Bowser catches in his vortex; the former simply damaging him and the latter forcing him to end the attack entirely. Getting hit by any of these (or hitting the [[Bomb|Bombs]], which do not explode upon collision with the victim and serve no other purpose) will cause Dry Bowser to swallow the hero that got hit and devour him, draining health.
==== In ''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'' ====
In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', if the [[Gobblick]] catches [[Mario (character)|Mario]] or [[Luigi]] with his devour attack, the creature's health will be restored by a certain amount before he spits the victim at the opposite Bro.

=== In ''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'' ===
Lifesteal replaces the [[Vamp]] [[Super Effect]] from [[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle'']] in [[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope|''Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope'']]. [[Vampdash]] powers up his user's dashing charges to deal more damage and heal by an amount proportional to the damage. Another Spark named [[Vampastra]] can give the hero's weapon the Lifesteal Super Effect when wielded by that hero. [[Vampgeddon]] can also administer Lifesteal with his Skyrain Lifesteal ability.

[[Depleter|Depleters]], including Giant Depleters, can throw projectiles with Lifesteal properties.

== Trivia ==

* While Lifesteal does not appear in [[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|''Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle'']], any attack that hits a character who has the [[Vamp]] status effect behaves as though it has the Lifesteal property.
