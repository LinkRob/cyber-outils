'''Neon Heights''' is a [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Neon Heights - Mario Party 7 (Solo Board).png|thumb|left|Solo Cruise]]
Neon Heights is themed after various locations in the [[United States of America]]. It is largely based on {{wp|Las Vegas|Las Vegas, Nevada}}, and to a lesser extent, [[Hollywood]], [[California]]; {{wp|Cape Canaveral}}, [[Florida]]; and [[New York City]]. Set atop a skyscraper complex, the board features a theater district, a movie set, a space center, and a baseball diamond. There is also a [[Mario]] statue that resembles the [[Statue of Liberty]].

[[Shy Guy]]s run the attractions, while [[Top Hat Shy Guy]]s are in charge of the [[Orb Shop]]s. Neon Heights has three [[Treasure Chest]]s that appear on random [[space]]s. When a player reaches a chest, [[Koopa Kid]] offers to open it for 10 [[coin]]s. One chest contains a [[Star (Mario Party series)|Star]], another contains 20 coins, and the third contains a [[Bob-omb]] that blasts the player back to the start. Once the Star is found, three new chests appear in new locations.

[[Bowser]] appears to have some control over the board, as he and Koopa Kid claim that the chests and Stars belong to him. Additionally, Koopa Kid operates a toll gate, stopping characters and demanding 10 coins to unblock the path.

In Solo Cruise, the player's goal is to be first to get three of the five Stars hidden on the board. These Stars are located in five of the eight available chests (the other three contain Bob-ombs). To open a chest, players must pay Koopa Kid a fee that increases by five coins each time, starting at 5 coins and rising to a maximum of 40.

===Events===
[[File:MP7 NeonHeights2.jpg|thumb|Wario shooting Koopa Kid boards]]
There are many ways to earn coins on this board. The first is the movie set, the game's starting area. Every time a player passes by, the Shy Guy director will ask if they want to audition for a role (such as hero, heroine, sidekick, or villain, depending on their character). If they accept, they play a coin game where they have 10 seconds to shoot as many of the 15 Koopa Kid targets as possible. They get 1 coin from each Koopa Kid they smash. However, they must avoid the Toadsworth targets. Doing so, it ends the game. And the player gets nothing (no coins) When time runs out, the director comments on their performance and awards them coins equal to the number of Koopa Kid targets they shot. (If the player shoots all 15 Koopa Kid targets, they --on one turn with that-- get 15 coins. That is, provided that they shoot all of the targets.)

On the opposite side is the baseball field. Here, a Shy Guy challenges players to a home run contest. In this game, players hit baseballs launched from a machine. When time is up, the Shy Guy compliments the player and awards them two coins for every ball they knocked out of the park.

At the top of the board is the rocket ship. When a player lands on the [[Event Space|Green Space]] in front of it, a Shy Guy asks if they want to "go for the stars." If they accept, the player pumps fuel into the rocket for a limited time. The player then enters the ship and travels into the sky, collecting coins along the way and possibly earning a Star if they reach the top.

The final way to get coins is by landing on the Green Space in the middle of the board. A Shy Guy will let the player play a matching game, where the player earns coins for every pair of panels they match. Their turn continues until they miss. Matching all the panels awards the player a Star (or 20 coins in Solo Cruise), but picking the Bowser panel causes them to lose 10 coins. In a team game, the second player takes over if the first one misses, providing another chance to win the Star.

Finally, landing on the Green Space near the Mario statue or the Green Space to the right of the elevator above the baseball field summons a [[UFO]] that scrambles the locations of the chests. In Solo Cruise, the UFO abducts the player and moves them to a different spot on the board.

===Bowser Time===
During [[Bowser Time]] at Neon Heights, [[Bowser]] does one of four actions:

*Takes a souvenir photograph of all of the players, and makes them pay 10 or 20 coins each for it. Then, he leaves without giving anyone the photo. This also occurs on all other boards except for [[Bowser's Enchanted Inferno!]]
*Steals coins from all of the players and put them in the chest which contains twenty coins. If the chest containing the Star is opened before the chest that contains coins, then the extra coins are lost.
*Steals a Star from the player in first and puts it in the chest which contains a Star. Therefore, one of the chests contains two Stars until it is opened.
*Puts a [[Ztar|Dark Star]] in a chest, and the player opening the chest with the Dark Star loses one Star. This replaces the Bob-omb until the Dark Star is obtained.
