The '''Wardrobe Room''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Wardrobe Room is a room with various clothing furniture, such as a shoe rack, a hat rack, three light blue wardrobes, and a [[mirror]]. Some clothing can be seen hanging from the racks, and [[Luigi]] can use his [[Poltergust 3000]] to sway them back and forth.

==History==
In [[Area One]], once Luigi has cleared the [[Anteroom]], the vines obstructing the door to the Wardrobe Room disappears, leaving the room accessible. There are four [[Gold Ghost]]s, one of which has to be revealed from the leftmost wardrobe, and two [[Garbage Can Ghost]]s to vacuum up. There is also a [[Speedy Spirit]] hidden in the rightmost wardrobe, but like other Speedy Spirits, vacuuming it is not required. Once Luigi has vacuumed every ghost, the [[Key (Luigi's Mansion series)|key]] to the [[Study (Luigi's Mansion)|Study]] is spawned on an upper shelf to the left.

During the [[blackout]], occurring shortly after the start of [[Area Four]], [[Uncle Grimmly]] resides in the Wardrobe Room. Grimmly has taken the key to the [[Breaker Room]], and Luigi needs the key to restore the power. Luigi must vacuum Uncle Grimmly into his [[Poltergust 3000]] to reveal the [[Treasure chest (Luigi's Mansion series)|treasure chest]] with the key, allowing Luigi to restore electricity to the mansion.

In PAL releases and the Nintendo 3DS remake, the [[Hidden Mansion]] includes a [[Blue Twirler]] instead of two Garbage Can Ghosts, now being the first instance when Blue Twirlers are encountered.
