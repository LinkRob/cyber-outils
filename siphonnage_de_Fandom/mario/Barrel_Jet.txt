The '''Barrel Jet''' is the [[Defensive Power Shot]] of [[Diddy Kong]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''.

== Description ==
The Barrel Jet can be activated if the player triggers the Power Shot while the ball is behind Diddy Kong. Diddy will attach a special [[barrel]] to his back and fly around the court to fetch the ball.
