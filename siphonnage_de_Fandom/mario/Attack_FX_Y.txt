'''Attack FX Y''' is a [[badge]] which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge for zero points of his [[Badge Point|BP]], and by wearing the badge, some of Mario's attacks make a bell sound. [[Dazzle]] sells this badge for a very reasonable price of only one [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]. In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], in addition to bell sounds, the Attack FX Y Badge also has cricket sounds which was pulled from the original’s [[Attack FX R]] badge due to [[Hooktail]]'s weakness now being frogs rather sounds rather than cricket sounds, hence the badge found in [[Hooktail Castle]] is now the [[Attack FX G]] badge.
