A '''Yarikuri Obake''' (plural: Yarikuri Obake) is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Yarikuri Obake are a ghost variant of [[Pirate Goom]]s, and they carry around a spear with them.

===Behavior===
Like [[Boo]]s, a Yarikuri Obake only moves when [[Wario]] is not facing it.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
[[File:Yarikuri Obake WLSML3 sprite.png|frame|left]]
A Yarikuri Obake is first encountered in [[Course No.12]]. Yarikuri Obake are last encountered at the end of [[Parsley Woods]], in [[Course No.36]], where they assist [[Ghost (Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3)|Ghost]] during its boss fight. Yarikuri Obake cannot be defeated.
