'''Draglets''' are small, bat-winged dragon heads which appear as enemies in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. They are commonly seen as guards of the [[castle]] levels. Draglets usually float around their general areas but if they catch sight of intruders, they will spit a fireball. Draglets themselves aren't very durable and can be bested with most attacks.

==In ''Super Mario 3D Land''==
[[Image:Draglet Attacks.jpg|thumb|left|Mario meets a Draglet.]]
Draglets are first seen in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. In this game, they can be found in various castle-themed stages as well as volcanic areas. Draglets float around a set area and whenever they see [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]], they will spit a fireball, Draglets however can be defeated with basically any attack such as stomps or the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Super Leaf]], [[Boomerang Flower]], [[Starman]], [[Statue Leaf]], or [[Invincibility Leaf]]. Several Draglets can also be seen in the opening cutscene for [[World 8 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 8]] suggesting that they reside in the [[Koopa Kingdom]].
