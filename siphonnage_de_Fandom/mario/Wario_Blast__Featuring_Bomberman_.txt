'''''Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!''''' is a crossover game between the ''[[Wario (series)|Wario]]'' and the ''[[w:c:bomberman:Bomberman (series)|Bomberman]]'' series. It was developed by [[Hudson Soft]] and published by [[Nintendo]] and released on [[Game Boy]]. The game is based on the game ''Bomberman GB'' released in Japan in August 1994. [[Wario]] was exclusively added to Western releases, and aside from Wario being added, the game is mostly unchanged and plays like other ''Bomberman'' titles.

==Story==
The storyline is taken directly from the manual for the game<ref>1994. ''[[Wario Blast: Featuring Bomberman!]]'' instruction booklet. [[Nintendo|Nintendo of America]] (American English). Page 4.</ref>:

''[[Wario|WARIO]] INVADES THE LAND OF THE MADBOMBERS!''

Wario, in his constant search for new lands to conquer and treasures to gain, has found the extra-dimensional portal that links [[Mushroom Earth|his world]] with the [https://bomberman.fandom.com/wiki/Planet_Bomber Bomberland] one that [[Bomberman]] inhabits. Thinking that the [[Madbomber|Madbombers]] will make a nice addition to his legions of mindless minions (as well as a new source of funds for his nefarious schemes), Wario steps through the gate... and soon finds that the Madbombers are not quite the dim-wit-ted flunkies he originally thought them to be!

After finishing the game, depending on which character the player chose, Bomberman can be seen ride a motorcycle across a canyon, whereas Wario also appears, on a motorcycle. The two then chase each other back and forth indefinitely. The game would reset going back to the opening menu.

==Gameplay==
The player can choose to play as either [[Wario]] or [[Bomberman]], but this doesn't affect the gameplay. The gameplay is similar to other Bomberman games. The player has to blow up his or her enemies via the use of bombs to advance. The player maneuvers around a grid-like playing field, setting off bombs to blow up unsuspecting enemies and destroy walls to clear the way and to reveal occasional power-ups. The player wins by defeating the enemy character and the other Bombermen or False Warios.
