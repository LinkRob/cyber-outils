'''Red Bonus Game House''' is a building that appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
The '''Red Bonus Game House''' is a white Toad house with red stars.

== In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'' the Red Bonus Game House is a Special building with no rarity. The player can buy the house at the Shop. If the player touches the house, the player will be asked to play the Bonus Game. After the player completes the bonus game, the player cannot play the minigame until four hours pass.

=== Bonus Game ===
The player has 90 seconds the complete the bonus game. The player must enter one of the doors to reach the treasure chest. The first room contains two doors while the second room contains four doors. Upon entering the room, the player can grab as many coins as they can find. Treasure chests may have one [[Rally Ticket]], three Rally Tickets or nothing. Upon completing the bonus game, the player earn the collected coins and Rally Tickets.
