'''K9 Cannons''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
K9 Cannons appear to be a type of cannon in the shape of a [[Noobis]] that can be only found in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]]. They act as sentries where they will shoot energy balls at any intruder. [[Wario]] cannot defeat them in battle and they must be avoided.

==Trivia==
* Their name is a pun on the word "canine" considering they resemble dogs.
