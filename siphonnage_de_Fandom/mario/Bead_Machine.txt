'''Bead Machine''' is a [[Gaddget]] which appears in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked as a reward from [[Petal Guy]] by completing his quest [[Blossom of My Heart]].

==Gameplay==
The Bead Machine allows the player to create and customize a pattern of beads. Upon creating a design, the player can frame and save it. At the pattern viewer, if the player presses up or down, they can change the border color.
