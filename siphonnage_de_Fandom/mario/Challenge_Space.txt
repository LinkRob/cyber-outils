The '''Challenge Space''' (or an '''Exclamation Mark Space''') is a type of [[space]] found at least once or twice on every [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''. It is seen only in [[Star Battle Arena]].

Landing on this space will challenge the player to one of three [[minigame]]s: [[Stampede (minigame)|Stampede]], [[Fruit Picker]], or [[Pour to Score]]. If the player wins the minigame, they get to throw a dart at the dart wheel to earn five, ten, or twenty coins, depending on where the dart lands. If the player loses, they will not get an opportunity to throw a dart at the wheel.
