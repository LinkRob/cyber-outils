'''Mirror Ghosts''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mirror Ghosts are an invisible variant of [[Grabbing Ghost]]s, although a Mirror Ghost becomes visible while it is stunned by a [[flashlight]]. Mirror Ghosts have the same body as a Grabbing Ghost, with the only difference being that Mirror Ghosts are colored light blue.

===Behavior===
Mirror Ghosts act identically to Grabbing Ghosts in that it tries to sneak behind [[Luigi]] and grab him, throttling Luigi's movement. Like with [[Red Grabbing Ghost]]s and [[Cinema Ghost]]s, a Mirror Ghost damages Luigi while it chokes him, but regular Grabbing Ghosts cannot damage Luigi. 

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
Mirror Ghosts are only encountered in the [[Mirror Room]], where there are six of them, each having 20 [[Heart Point|HP]]. Even while Mirror Ghosts are invisible, their body remains visible on the [[mirror]]. When Luigi stuns a Mirror Ghost using his flashlight, he can start vacuuming it into his [[Poltergust 3000]].

Mirror Ghosts are similar to Cinema Ghosts, which are only visible by their silhouette the projector screen in the [[Projection Room]] unless Luigi stuns it using his flashlight. Mirror Ghosts and Cinema Ghosts both leave behind a dust cloud upon materializing.

In the PAL releases' and Nintendo 3DS version's [[Hidden Mansion]], Mirror Ghosts also appear in the [[Wardrobe Room]].
