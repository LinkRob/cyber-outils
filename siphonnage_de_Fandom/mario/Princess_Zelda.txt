'''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Princess_Zelda Princess Zelda]''', or simply '''Zelda''', is the eponymous deuteragonist of [https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda_(series) ''The Legend of Zelda'' franchise]. The love interest of the main protagonist [[Link]], she is the princess of the [https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Hyrule Kingdom of Hyrule] and one of the important members of its Royal Family. As the mortal incarnation of the Goddess Hylia and the wielder of the [https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Triforce_of_Wisdom Triforce of Wisdom], Zelda is normally the one who sends Link on his adventures to save the kingdom and defeat [https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Ganon Ganon], who often kidnaps her in a similar manner as [[Bowser]] does with [[Princess Peach]] (though in this case Ganon antagonizes Zelda for stealing her Triforce rather than trying to make her his queen). Although she has never appeared in any mainline ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games, she is playable in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' games since ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee|Melee]]''. Zelda herself is ''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' equivalent of Peach, and the two princesses are known to be friends with each other, as seen in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and other media related to both ''Mario'' and ''Zelda'' franchises.

== Profile ==
There are many different incarnations of Princess Zelda, complementary to Link. They have the same traits as being the princess of Hyrule, having magical powers with the control of the Triforce of Wisdom. Like Link, her age is very inconsistent throughout ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, as the incarnations of Zelda in the main games usually depict her as a child, but some cases have her as a teenager or young adult. While she is a damsel-in-distress in most occasions, she is also an active ally of Link.

== In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
===''Super Smash Bros. Melee''===
''Ocarina of Time'' incarnation of Princess Zelda made her ''Super Smash Bros.'' appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', marking Zelda's first debut into the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series.

===''Super Smash Bros. Brawl''===
[[File:BrawlZelda.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Artwork of Princess Zelda in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Zelda returns in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as a starter character, with her new design based on her appearance from ''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess''. She retains the ability to transform into Sheik from ''Melee'', now appearing as "Zelda/Sheik" on the character selection screen, and unlike in Melee, her starting form can be chosen. As with all other characters, Zelda received a Final Smash in Light Arrow, a powerful beam of light with near one-hit KO levels of knockback.

===''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U''===
Zelda returns as a playable character in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS and Wii U'', being first announced on December 26th, 2013. She retains her appearance from ''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'', but is no longer capable of transforming into Sheik, who has become a separate character entirely. As a result, she has a new down special move called Phantom Slash, where she summons a Phantom from ''The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks''.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
Zelda is a veteran fighter classified as fighter #17 in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. Compared to her previous design from ''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'', Zelda was rarely expressive; like other characters, Zelda is more expressive. Zelda has more buffs than nerfs on her moveset, her moves also improved KO potential. Her [[Final Smash]], Triforce of Wisdom replaced the Light Arrow. The Triforce of Wisdom seals an opponent, then blasts them away; she can knockout the opponent with 100%.

In the [[World of Light]] cutscene, Princess Zelda spoke her English line saying, ''"Stow your fear; it's now or never!"''. There is a cutscene of Mewtwo and she tried to reflect the beams with Nayru's Love and Confusion. Both of them were caught onto [[Galeem]]'s light beams along with many fighters sans [[Kirby]]. In the World of Light, Zelda can be found at the Sacred Realm of the Dark Realm at the bottom left corner taking place in a temple, the boulder blocks the path to Zelda. The player must defeat the spirit battle of Impa to clear the boulder, also leading to solve a clock-based puzzle. Zelda joining the party reveals the Triforce of Wisdom on the center of the map. Zelda's Classic Mode route is called Wisdom Prevails. Her opponents are villains or antagonists from various universes. Notably, [[Wario!|Wario]] and [[Bowser]] appear in Zelda's route, but Wario is an anti-hero. Ganondorf is the boss of her route, who transforms into [[Ganon]] upon his defeat.

==Trivia==
*Like [[Princess Peach]], Princess Zelda is among the most widely recognized and greatest female characters in video game history.
*[[Shigeru Miyamoto]] has stated that Princess Zelda's name originated from Zelda Fitzgerald, the wife of the acclaimed author, F. Scott Fitzgerald.
*Although Princess Zelda's romantic feelings toward [[Link]] and vice versa are not quite as explicit in comparison to Princess Peach's relationship with [[Mario]], she can be seen giving a kiss on the hero's cheek at the ending of the linked ''Oracle'' games.
*Zelda can be considered a reference to [[Princess Daisy]] and [[Rosalina]], as she is known to have a tomboyish personality and possess magical abilities.
*Unlike Princess Peach who had her first true game with ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' in 2005, Princess Zelda did not have her own until the release of ''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Echoes_of_Wisdom The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom]'' in 2024. However, their first playable roles occurred with ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' and ''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Zelda:_The_Wand_of_Gamelon The Wind of Gamelon]'' in 1988 and 1993, respectively.
**It must be noted that ''The Wind of Gamelon'' released for the Philips CD-i is not canon to the ''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' series. 
*When Princess Zelda's ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Ultimate]]'' design was demonstrated during the game's E3 2018 trailer, the Japanese version said that she is the incarnation from ''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_A_Link_to_the_Past A Link to the Past]''. Conversely, the English version, as well as the official website's English blog post focusing on Zelda, said she is the incarnation from ''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_A_Link_Between_Worlds A Link Between Worlds]''. To further complicate matters, Zelda's Classic Mode Spirit uses official artwork from ''A Link Between Worlds'', yet the English version of Palutena's Guidance regarding her in ''Ultimate'' contradicts both the English E3 trailer and blog post by stating that she is the incarnation from ''A Link to the Past''. This discrepancy was finally settled by [[Masahiro Sakurai]] during a Nintendo DREAMS Magazine interview, in which he stated that Princess Zelda's design in ''Ultimate'' is indeed a combination from both games.
**Due to ''A Link to the Past'' portraying her in-game sprite with brunette hair and a blue dress, in contrast to her appearance in the game's artwork, it is possible that this was the main reason as to why she is mostly referred to resemble her ''A Link Between Worlds'' incarnation.
*Zelda has more Enhanceable Spirits associated with her than any other character, with a total of 3.
*Nayru's Love, Din's Fire, and Farore's Wind were all originally used by Link in ''[https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Ocarina_of_Time Ocarina of Time]'', never by Princess Zelda herself. However, later spin-offs in the series such as ''Hyrule Warriors'' and ''Cadence of Hyrule'' depict her using these moves, likely influenced by their depiction in [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super'' ''Smash Bros.'']].
