'''''Yoshi no Cookie: Kuruppon Oven de Cookie''''' is a Japan-only [[Super Famicom]] game released in 1994. It is a variation of ''[[Yoshi's Cookie]]'' developed by National Human Electronics, but with an added game mode to promote their Kuruppon oven at the time. Only 500 copies of the game were ever produced.
