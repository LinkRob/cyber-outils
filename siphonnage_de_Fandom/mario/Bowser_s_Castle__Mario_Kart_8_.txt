'''Bowser's Castle''' is a course based on a castle with the same name. It appeared in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and reappeared in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''.

== Layout ==
It features a giant [[Bowser]] statue which punches the track to bend it. It is also the only Bowser Castle course to not have any [[Thwomp]]s. Before the race starts, the castle's portcullis rises up. [[Grey Bowser Statue]]s are present as the course's obstacles.
