'''Lullaby''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once [[Mario]] rescues [[Mamar]] from [[Tutankoopa]] during the events of Chapter 2. Lullaby also uses 2 [[Star Power|SP]] to use.

== Description ==
When using Lullaby, all enemies on the screen may be put to [[sleep]], similar to the [[Sleepy Sheep]], immobilizing them for a few turns. However, Lullaby may not work all the time and doesn't have any effect on any bosses.

==Trivia==
*The Lullaby move is actually useful against [[Kent C. Koopa]] as it always hits and puts him to sleep for four turns.
**Kent C. Koopa is also the only boss to be affected by the Lullaby.
