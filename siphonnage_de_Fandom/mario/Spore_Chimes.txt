|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Spore Chimes''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can be used by a few enemies in the game such as [[Ding-A-Ling]]s.

==Effects==
When Spore Chimes is used, a bell appears over the target and notes fly down on them turning them into a [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]]. Wearing accessories such as a Trueform Pin will prevent the affects of Spore Chimes.
