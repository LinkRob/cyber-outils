{{Nihongo|'''Craw-Daddy'''|ヤリクロウ|Yarikurō}} is a [[Dark Craw]] who fights in the [[Glitz Pit]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Appearance==
Like all Dark Craws, he has red eyes and different clothing from a regular [[Craw]]. The battle is the same process as any other battle with a Dark Craw, or the battle with [[Gus]]. Meaning this, the spear he holds can damage [[Mario]] if he jumps on it and he can use different techniques with his spear.

==History==
He is ranked 4th in the Glitz Pit. Being that, he is one of the last major league fighters [[Mario]], as the Great Gonzales, must battle. The Craw-Daddy is only fight-able if Mario first makes his way past [[The Fuzz]]. Upon his defeat, Mario will then gain access to [[Hamma, Bamma, and Flare]], a fighting team of three tactical [[Koopa Troopa|koopas]].
