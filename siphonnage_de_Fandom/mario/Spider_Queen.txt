}}The '''Spider Queen''' is a character that appears in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. Her body was mainly taken over by the [[Grouchy Possessor]] which served as the main boss of [[Gloomy Manor]] and the first major boss in the entire game.
==Personality ==
Once the Possessor Ghost has been defeated, the Spider Queen regains her senses. She isn't hostile at all toward [[Luigi]], and is probably happy that Luigi has freed her of the ghost's control. The Spider Queen also appears to rule over all of the [[Spider]]s in Gloomy Manor.
