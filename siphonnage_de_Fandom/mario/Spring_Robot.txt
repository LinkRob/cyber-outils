A '''Spring Robot''' (also called a '''Jump Beamer''') is an enemy that first appears in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They are spring-like enemies that shoot circular beams at the player. Spring Robots are purple with red markings on their sides, a head with a springy neck that gives them a resemblance to a [[Spring Topman]], and yellow eyes on the curved surface of their head with a bump on the opposite side of their head.
==Appearances==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Spring Robots make their debut appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', here, they first appear in [[Flipswitch Galaxy]], where one is found near a [[Power Star]]. They also appear in [[Battlerock Galaxy]] and [[Dreadnought Galaxy]]. Spring Robots are always found attacking in one spot, and if [[Mario]] touches their laser, he will get damaged. Mario must jump twice to expose the spring on the top of the robot's head, stunning it in the process. Mario can then jump onto the spring to bounce up to higher places that his normal jump cannot take him to under normal circumstances.
===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Spring Robots return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', in this game, only one is found in the entire game. It is located in the [[Space Storm Galaxy]].
