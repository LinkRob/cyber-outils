'''Mini-Z-Yuxes''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
These enemies are only found by fighting [[Z-Yux]]es. During battle, they are created by Z-Yuxes to protect themselves. [[Mario]] must defeat all of the Mini-Z-Yuxes before he damage the Z-Yux surrounded by the barrier created by the Mini-Z-Yuxes. Up to four Mini-Z-Yuxes can be in battle per each Z-Yux at one time.
