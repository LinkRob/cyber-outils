====[[Cranky Kong]]====
Cranky Kong is Donkey Kong's grandfather, the original Donkey Kong from the 1981 game, the widower of Wrinkly Kong and father of Donkey Kong Junior.
{{stub}}

====[[Donkey Kong Jr.]]====
Donkey Kong Jr. is Donkey Kong's father and the son of Cranky Kong and Wrinkly Kong. The relationship between Jr. and his son is unknown.

===Rivals===
====[[Mario]]====
[[File:Donkey Kong holding Pauline hostage.webp|thumb|[[Donkey Kong]] holding [[Pauline]] hostage, an issue that brings him and [[Mario]] in [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|conflict]].]]
Mario is Donkey Kong’s friendly rival and former arch enemy. They have had been rivalries at some point, it is implied in ''Super Smash Bros.'', and in [[Solid Snake]]'s codec conversation from ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', that their rivalry is friendlier in spin-off games.<ref>
'''Snake:''' Otacon, there's a gorilla wearing a tie here. He's huge.
'''Otacon:''' That's Donkey Kong. As you can tell, he's got strength to spare. He may be [[Kongo Jungle|king of the jungle]], but he [[DK's Tree House|lives in a house]] just like you or me. And he seems pretty smart—well, for an ape, anyway. The Donkey Kong who fought [[Donkey Kong (video game)|that epic battle]] with [[Mario]] was [[Cranky Kong|this guy's grandfather]].
'''Snake:''' That was a long time ago. What about this Donkey Kong? Does he get along with Mario?
'''Otacon:''' Nope, they're still at it. Seems like they're always competing in something—[[Mario Kart (series)|kart racing]], [[Sports game|sports]], you name it.
'''Snake:''' A chip off the old block...</ref> Despite this, Donkey Kong is good friends with Mario as he invites him with others in many sports, karts and party events. In ''[[Mario Party DS]]'', Mario and his friends freed Donkey Kong from being turned into a stone after they defeated [[Dry Bones]], though not thanking them. Unfortunately, are also times when Donkey Kong and Mario do not seem to hold grudges against each other from one another in spite of their [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|occasional conflicts]] due to his humiliation, such as stealing all [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Mario toys]], kidnap innocent [[Toad (species)|Toads]] and [[Pauline]]. Another part of Mario and Donkey Kong's friendship is how quickly Mario forgives Donkey Kong for remorseful consequences of his actions, even giving him a toy a few times.

Like Mario, Bowser is Donkey Kong's enemy. In the non-canon ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]: [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary|The Subspace Emissary]]'', Bowser was the mastermind behind of stolen [[Banana Hoard]] as he uses the Dark Cannon to turn Donkey Kong into a [[Trophy Stand|trophy]].
