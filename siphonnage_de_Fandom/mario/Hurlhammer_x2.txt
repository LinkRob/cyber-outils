|name=Hurlhammer x2 (Battle Card)}}'''Hurlhammer x2''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hurlhammer x2 is the line version of [[Mario]]'s "Hammer x2" attack. It is two of the [[Hurlhammer (Battle Card)|Hurlhammer]]'s stacked on top of each other, accompanied with a blue/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 50 coins (unpainted)

Sell Price: 25 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Hurlhammer x2 is first seen in [[Fortune Island]], but can also found in [[Mossrock Theater]] and [[The Emerald Circus]].
