The '''Vacuum Block''' is a type of [[Command Block]] item which appears exclusively in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. It is obtained by [[Bowser]], and allows him to use his Vacuum command during battle.
