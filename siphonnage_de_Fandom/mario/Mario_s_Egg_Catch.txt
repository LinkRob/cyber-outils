'''Mario's Egg Catch''' is a [[Super Mario Bros. Watch]] released by [[Nintendo]] in 1990. In the game you control [[Raccoon Mario]] by moving him left and right in order to catch the [[Birdo Egg|eggs]] that [[Birdo]] fires out.
