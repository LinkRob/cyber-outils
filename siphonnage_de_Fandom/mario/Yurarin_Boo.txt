A '''Yurarin Boo''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. Yurarin Boos are seahorse-dragon enemies only encountered in [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 2-3]] of the [[Muda Kingdom]]. Their leader is [[Dragonzamasu]].

==History==
Yurarin Boos are stronger versions of [[Yurarin]]s. They are nearly the same as Yurarins and even use the same sprite, but the difference with Yurarin Boos is that they attack [[Mario]] by shooting [[fireball]]s at him. Mario can defeat Yurarin Boos by shooting missiles at them from his [[Marine Pop]]. Like Yurarins, a Yurarin Boo yields 400 [[point]]s upon being defeated.
