'''Caveman Land''' is a location that makes a one-time appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Quest for Pizza]]."

==Description==
Caveman Land is based on [[Earth]] during the prehistoric era, although it features a variety of ecosystems, such as deserts, waterfalls, caves and forests. The main species that live here are [[Caveman|cavemen]], one of which is a medicine doctor named [[Mugga]], and another species native to the area is a cow-dinosaur hybrid named [[Cowazaurus]].
