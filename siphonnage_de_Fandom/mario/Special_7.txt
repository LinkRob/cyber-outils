'''Special 7''' is the seventh special world appearing in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]'', and the fifteenth world overall. The area in the background is pink in color and contains several trees of varying sizes, snowfall, as well as several scattered plateaus and many flowers near the map itself. There are six levels and a [[Mystery Box]].
