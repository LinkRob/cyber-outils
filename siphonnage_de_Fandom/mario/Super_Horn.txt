The '''Super Horn''' is an item that first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and reappears in [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]], ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart World]]''.  

== Description ==
The player can receive the Super Horn at 2nd to 6th place and when used creates a shockwave destroying everything caught in it and knocking over nearby racers as well. This can also be used to destroy a [[Spiny Shell]]. It can't stop the Blooper.
