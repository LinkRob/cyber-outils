'''King Theet''' is a character from ''[[Princess Peach: Showtime!]]'' He is the ruler of a kingdom in the play visited in [[The Castle of Thorns]].

== Physical Appearance ==
As a [[Theet]], King Theet lacks a mouth. He has black, beady eyes, and his nose is bulbous. His outfit is that of a king, with him having a crown, royal clothing, and a cape.

== History ==
On her way through [[The Castle of Thorns]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Swordfighter Peach|dressed as a swordsman]], receives a vision that King Theet has been captured, much to his knights' dismay. After the vision, she rushes forward to save him, fighting [[Sour Bunch]] knights, including one with big armour, en route. She eventually finds the King on top of his castle, and rushes forward to his aid. A [[Thorn Plant (Princess Peach: Showtime!)|Thorn Plant]] then emerges and attacks her, only to be butchered by the princess's sword. After he is rescued, the King reclaims his kingdom.
