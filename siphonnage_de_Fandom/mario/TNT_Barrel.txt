A '''TNT Barrel''' is an object in the {{franchise|Donkey Kong}}. It first appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]''.

==In ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''==
TNT Barrels reappear in this game as rather minor objects. TNT Barrels are mainly used against bosses as means to damage them. An enemy known as a [[Kaboom]] also hide inside TNT Barrels attempting to blow themselves up at a Kong.
