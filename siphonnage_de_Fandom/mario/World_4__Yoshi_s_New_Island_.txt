'''World 4''' is the fourth set of stages in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', and is located in a savanna. [[Kamek]] and [[Furious Fred de Fillet]] are the bosses of this world.  
==Levels==
*World 4-1: [[Hop 'n' Pop Till You Drop]] &ndash; This level features balloons.
*World 4-2: [[Underground Pokey Patrol]] &ndash; This level features [[Pokey]]s.
*World 4-3: [[Spin-Lift Drift]] &ndash; This level features the only [[Goomba]]s in the game.
*World 4-4: [[Fort Key Calamity]] &ndash; The fourth fort of the game.
*World 4-5: [[Metal Eggdozers on a Roll]] &ndash; This level features [[Metal Eggdozer]]s.
*World 4-6: [[Chomp Shark Chase]] &ndash; This level features [[Chomp Shark]]s.
*World 4-7: [[Flatbed Ferry Freefall]] &ndash; This level features [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]].
*World 4-8: [[Furious Fred de Fillet's Castle]] &ndash; The fourth castle of the game.
*World 4-S: [[See Poochy Run!]] &ndash; This level is centered around [[Poochy]].
