The '''Poltergust 4000''' is a kart that appears in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It debuted in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''.

== Description ==
It is the upgraded version of the [[Poltergust 3000]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]'', and shares some physical similarities to the [[Super Poltergust 3001]] from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', both built by [[Professor E. Gadd]].

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart DS''===
[[File:Poltergust4000.png|thumb|200px|left|Luigi in his Poltergust 4000 on the kart selection menu]]
The Poltergust 4000 serves as one of [[Luigi]]'s three exclusive karts. His others include the [[Standard LG]] and [[Streamliner]]. It offers decent [[Speed]], [[Acceleration]] and [[Handling]], as well as good [[Off-Road]] and [[Mini-Turbo]] (which two of those stats are hidden to the player), but it is fairly [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|Light]] and has low [[Drift]] and gets poor [[Items (stat)|Items]]. All it's stats except drift are slightly better than [[Mario]]'s [[Shooting Star]], making it more preferable.

Its speed is shared with the [[Gold Mantis]], making them both the fastest karts within the mediumweight class, even faster than the [[Rambi Rider]], which is a heavyweight kart, by one point. Its weight is equivalent to the [[Standard TD]], and the [[Banisher]].

During gameplay, only Luigi can use the Poltergust 4000. However, if the player completes all [[Engine Class|150cc]] Nitro or Retro Grand Prix cups, [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] and [[Waluigi]] (if he has already been unlocked) can use this kart. To let all other racers use this kart, the player must complete all cups in the 150cc Mirror Mode.

Luigi in the Poltergust 4000 is used as the staff ghost for {{Classic course|GCN|[[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Luigi Circuit]]}} and {{Classic course|GBA|[[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart: Super Circuit)|Luigi Circuit]]}}.

In pre-release versions of the game, the Poltergust 4000 had Professor Elvin Gadd's emblem on the kart, imply that had said character made it into the final build of the game, this would have been his signature kart.

===''Mario Kart Tour''===
[[File:MKT Luigi Celebrating.jpg|thumb|200px|right|Luigi celebrating in his Poltergust 4000 on {{Classic course|DS|[[Luigi's Mansion (track)|Luigi's Mansion]]}}]]
The Poltergust 4000 returns in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'', after a nearly 14-year absence, as part of the [[Halloween Tour]]. It is classified as Super kart. The game also introduces a icy-blue variant, simply called the [[Ice-blue Poltergust]], as part of the [[Ice Tour]].
