The {{Nihongo|'''Pokey Triplets'''|サンボ三兄弟|Sanbo Sankyōdai}} are a fighting team in the [[Glitz Pit]] that appears in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
This team consists of three regular [[Pokey]]s. Like most of the Glitz Pit teams, the Pokey Triplets were initially arrogant, but were defeated by [[Mario|the Great Gonzales]] and his team of partners. They place 18th in the Glitz Pit making them one of the weaker teams in the tournment.
