'''Frigid Bridges''' is a 4-player minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this minigame, the four players must try to build their ice bridges to reach the piece of land at the end. They must transport pieces of ice back and forth in order to help get to the land three times. However, there are [[Cheep-Cheep]]s in the water that jump out and attempt to knock the player into the icy water. If a player slips into the icy water or a Cheep Cheep knocks them in, a [[Lakitu]] will appear and carry the player out of the water. The player that builds the ice bridges and reaches the center first wins.
