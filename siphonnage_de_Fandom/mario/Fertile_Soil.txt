The '''Fertile Soil''' is a key item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
The Fertile Soil can be obtained from [[Posie]] of [[Flower Fields]]. It must then be used with the [[Magical Bean]] and the [[Miracle Water]] in order to grow a [[beanstalk]] to access [[Cloudy Climb]], where [[Huff N. Puff]] resides. However, [[Mario]] must have destroyed the [[Puff Puff Machine]] and freed the [[Sun]] before doing so.
