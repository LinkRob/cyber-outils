'''Mowtown''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. This minigame's title is a pun on the name of the record label {{wp|Motown}} owned by the Universal Music Group.

The only difference between day and night is that there are lit torches in the background at night.

There is a page based on this minigame in the [[Miracle Book]] called "Lawn Mowin' Fun" with [[Mario]] mowing the lawn. When the page is activated, Mario starts singing, [[Luigi]] himself starts mowing, [[Princess Peach|Peach]] catches the grass trimmings from Mario's mower, some [[Shy Guy]]s are shown hiding in one of the grass baskets, and the shrubs bloom. 

==Opening==
The players start up their lawnmowers and advance onto the lawn.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Mowtown night.png|thumb|At nighttime]]
The object of the minigame is to mow the most grass on a small area in a park. To start mowing, the player must press and hold the {{button|gcn|A}} button while moving around. The grass is automatically transported to the player's basket. The player with the most grass in their basket when time runs out wins. If everyone gets the same amount of grass, the minigame ends in a draw.

==Ending==
The four players stand on a freshly trimmed lawn in front of their baskets, with winning and losing animations playing as usual.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Change direction
*{{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Move forward and mow
*{{button|gcn|B}} &ndash; Move backward (won't mow)

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Tame the wildly overgrown lawn with your lawn mower! Whoever collects the most grass clippings wins!"''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"If you hit a wall or an opponent, back up and start mowing again."''
