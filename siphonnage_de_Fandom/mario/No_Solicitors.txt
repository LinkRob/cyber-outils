'''No Solicitors''' is a [[List of microgames in WarioWare Gold#Wario|Mash League introduction]] microgame in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.
==Gameplay==
The player has to repeatedly press {{button|3ds|A}} to keep the perpetrator from entering Wario's House. If the {{button|3ds|A}} button is not mashed fast enough, Wario will get flung away and the game is lost.
*'''1st level difficulty:''' The perpetrator is a businessman. He pushes the door slowly.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' The perpetrator is a beast with a dagger. It pushes the door somewhat hard.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' The perpetrator is a beast with a dagger. It pushes the door very hard.
