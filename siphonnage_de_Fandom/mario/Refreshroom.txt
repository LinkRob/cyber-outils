A '''Refreshroom''' is a special type of [[Mushroom]] found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. This unique item cures [[Shroomshock]], a state of unconsciousness. [[Mario]] has to find a Refreshroom for [[Toadsworth]], who fainted during the unveiling ceremony of [[Professor E. Gadd]]'s [[Prof. E. Gadd's time machine|time machine]]. The mushroom is in Toadsworth's room in [[Peach's Castle]]. Its appearance is similar to a [[1-Up Mushroom]], but the color of its mushroom cap is more forest green, and its spots consist of white stars. Its name is a combination of the words "refresh" and "mushroom".
