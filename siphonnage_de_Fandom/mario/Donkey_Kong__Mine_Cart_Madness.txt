'''Donkey Kong: Mine-Cart Madness''' is a steel roller coaster in the [[Super Nintendo World]] area at Universal Studios Japan and Universal Epic Universe. It is based on [[Nintendo]]'s [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|''Donkey Kong'' franchise]]. Mine-Cart Madness is known for simulating the feeling of jumping over tracks.
