'''SPD-UP''' is a positive status effect experienced in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. It stands for Speed-UP. In ''Partners in Time'', by eating a Blue Pepper, Mario and Luigi will increase [[Speed (Initiative)|Speed]], allowing them to attack before the enemies. The effect is indicated by an expanding blue circle of aura around Mario or Luigi. In ''Paper Jam'', Bowser and Paper Mario can get this status condition, getting it along with the bros using a SPD-Up Battle Card. All other games, only enemies can power themselves up with this condition. Rising blue streaks signal the power-up.
