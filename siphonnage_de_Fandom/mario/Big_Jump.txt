|name=Big Jump (Battle Card)}}'''Big Jump''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big Jump is the strong version of [[Mario]]'s [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a big brown boot with a orange/tan checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 15/60 coins

Sell Price: 8/30 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big Jump is first seen in [[Mondo Woods]], but can also found in [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Sacred Forest]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], and [[Vortex Island]].
