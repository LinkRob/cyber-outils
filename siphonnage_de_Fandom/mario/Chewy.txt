'''Chewies''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are purple [[Piranha Plant]]s that appear throughout [[Bean Valley]], and Chewies are also found in [[Land's End]], [[Belome Temple]], and [[The Trial Course]]. Chewies can also appear in the [[Sunken Ship]] as [[Hidon|Hidons]], disguising as treasure chests.

==Battle==
In battle, Chewies attack physically by biting. They are also capable of using moves like [[Drain]] and can use a nasty move called [[Pollen Nap]] that may put one of [[Mario]]'s party to [[sleep]].

===Strategy===
Chewies have no true weakness but they often appear in groups. It is recommended that [[Mallow]] uses a multi-hit move like [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] to damage all of them at the same time. Mario should focus using physical and Geno could also multi-hit using his [[Geno Blast]] or [[Geno Flash]] moves.
