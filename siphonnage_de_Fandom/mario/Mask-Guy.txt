'''Mask-Guys''' are an enemy featured in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. Upon sighting [[Wario]], a Mask-Guy attempts to charge into Wario and poke him with its sharp spear. Wario can defeat a Mask-Guy by either [[Ground Pound]]ing or [[jump]]ing on it or by [[Dash Attack|Barging]] into it from the side. Mask-Guys have a fiery variant named [[Flame Mask-Guy]].
