'''''Yoshi's New Island''''' is a game for the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. It was developed by [[Arzest]] and published by [[Nintendo]] in 2014. It is the third installment of the {{series|Yoshi's Island}}, following after ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. In 2015, ''Yoshi's New Island'' was [[reissue|re-released]] as a [[Nintendo Selects]] title in Europe, then in North America and Australia in 2016.

Despite being the third ''Yoshi's Island'' installment, ''Yoshi's New Island'' is significantly more based on the first game, ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and does not reuse any of the mechanics introduced in ''Yoshi's Island DS''. The story of ''Yoshi's New Island'' also takes place directly between after ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and before ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Development==
''Yoshi's New Island'' was announced at Nintendo Direct in April 2013. It was released in North America on March 14, 2014.

==Gameplay==
''Yoshi's New Island''{{'}}s gameplay remains the same from the other ''Yoshi's Island'' games, as a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] usually escorts babies, such as [[Baby Mario]], and eats enemies to use eggs as projectiles. There are two new types of eggs; one of them is [[Mega Eggdozer]]s, which destroy almost everything in it's path, that are obtained from big [[Shy Guy]]s/[[Lantern Ghost]]s and [[Egg Block]]s; the second new egg is the Metal Eggdozer, which allows you to sink underwater instead of floating on the surface as well as, instead of being chucked in the air, rolling on the floor, also obtained from metal Shy Guys and Lantern Ghosts as well as metal egg blocks; both of these eggs are capable of granting multiple 1-UPs by destroying a lot of destructible terrain, similar to the [[Mega Mushroom]] from [[New Super Mario Bros.]] being able to get 1-UPs by doing the same thing.
