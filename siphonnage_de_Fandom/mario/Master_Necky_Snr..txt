'''Master Necky Snr.''' is the sixth boss found in ''[[Donkey Kong Country]]'' appearing in [[Chimp Caverns]] who appears to be a giant [[Necky]] resembling [[Master Necky]], except for having a bigger appearance. 

His attacks are similar to Master Necky's, except that the number of coconuts Master Necky Snr. shoots increases after each hit with one on the first hit, two on the second, etc. [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] must avoid the coconuts and bounce on the [[tire]] to go on top of Master Necky Snr.'s head. He is only available for attacking for a few seconds after spitting out coconuts. The coconuts also go quicker and further than Master Neckys himself. The Kongs must jump on Master Necky Snr.'s head five times to defeat him causing them to go to [[Gangplank Galleon]] and fight [[King K. Rool]].
