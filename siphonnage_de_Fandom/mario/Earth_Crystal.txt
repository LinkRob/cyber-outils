The '''Earth Crystal''' is one of the four [[Elemental Crystals]] who fights alongside [[Culex]] in [[Monstro Town]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a sentient crystal associated with the element of earth. Like the other three Elemental Crystals, the Earth Crystal originates from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise, but in ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'', the Earth Crystal is from the dimension of [[Vanda]], like Culex.

The Earth Crystal can use deadly earth-based moves such as [[Boulder]] and [[Sand Storm]], and it is weak to [[Thunder (element)|Thunder]] attacks. The Earth Crystal has 3,200 [[HP]], the highest out of every Elemental Crystal, but has low [[Defense]] and [[Magic Attack]].
