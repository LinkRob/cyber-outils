'''Sir Hop-A-Lots''' are enemies that appear in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are versions of [[Sir Lance-A-Lot]]s that are green in color and ride around on pogo-sticks that serve as their means of transportation and a weapon.

==History==
Sir Hop-A-Lots first appear inside [[Carpaccio's Lab]] as one of the many enemies in the level. They also appear in [[Allergia Gardens]] presumably following their leader and creator, [[Carpaccio]], after he made off with the [[Crimson Diamond]]. As their name states, Sir Hop-A-Lots attack by hopping around attempting to land on [[Wario]]. They can be defeated with any attack and after being damaged, they will lose their pogo-sticks.
