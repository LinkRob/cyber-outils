'''Kooky Cookie '''is an item in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
[[Tayce T.]], a cook who lives in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] will make this item for Mario when Mario gives her a select two items. One of these items must be a [[Cake Mix]]. The other item used to make this delicious yet strange cookie must be a [[Koopa Leaf]], [[Maple Syrup]], or [[Stinky Herb]]. When used, this item heals 15 of [[Mario]]'s [[Flower Point|FP]], but it also gives him a special power; one of these various powers can be Electrification, Invisibility, or the disadvantageous sleepiness. A Kooky Cookie can be sold for 15 [[coin]]s.

Since the Kooky Cookie is one of the two items to give Mario invisibility, it is extremely helpful in battle. Also, the Kooky Cookie is much easier to get than [[Repel Gel]] as well, so Mario can use it during difficult battles. However, a Kooky Cookie also gives Mario sleepiness on occasion, so caution must be taken if Mario intends to use this item.
