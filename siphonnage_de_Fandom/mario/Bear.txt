'''Bears''' ('''Tamanoripū''' in Japanese) are an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Bear has the appearance of a short teddy bear, and it can always be seen riding around on a [[Beach Ball]].

===Behavior===
A Bear uses its Beach Ball to safely travel around on [[Spike (hazard)|spike]]-covered surfaces.

==In ''Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins''==
Bears only appear in [[Mario the Circus Star!]]. [[Mario]] can defeat a Bear by either [[stomp]]ing it or throwing a [[fireball]] at it as [[Fire Mario]]. Once a Bear has been defeated, Mario can use its Beach Ball to safely travel across the spikes on the floor and reach certain areas that are otherwise inaccessible.
