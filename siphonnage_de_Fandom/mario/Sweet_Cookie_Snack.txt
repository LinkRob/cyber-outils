The '''Sweet Cookie Snack''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Saffron]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. This recipe can be sold for 30 coins.

== Description ==
Sweet Cookie Snack is made up of three cookies all with different shapes. It is made by cooking a [[Cake Mix]]. This snack restores 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].

This recipe resembles that of the [[Big Cookie]] recipe made by [[Tayce T.]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', except the Big Cookie is missing the heart-shaped cookie in it. Also, it replaces the [[Zess Cookie]] from [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door|the previous game]].
