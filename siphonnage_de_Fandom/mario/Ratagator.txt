'''Ratagator''' is a character who appears in a few live-action segments of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. He is a hybrid between a sewer rat and an alligator, reflected by the fact that his name is a portmanteau of "rat" and "alligator." Ratagator is a friend of [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], and he tends to visit them at [[Mario Brothers Plumbing]].

For production of the show itself, Ratagator appears to be a hand puppet of sorts.
