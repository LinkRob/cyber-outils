'''Buzzers''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are [[bee]]s that reside in the [[Forest Maze]], and they have a stronger relative named [[Stinger]].

==Battle==
In battle, Buzzers are known for being one of the fastest enemies encountered early in the game meaning they will most often at times attack first. The Buzzers can attack physically with their stingers but can also use a deadly move called [[Thornet]] that may [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] one of [[Mario]]'s party.

===Strategy===
Buzzers, being insects, are weak to fire moves so Mario's [[Super Flame]] should deal high amounts of damage to them. Buzzers are also weak to ice but [[Mallow]] will most likely not learn [[Snowy]] at that part of the game.
