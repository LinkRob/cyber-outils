'''Don't Blow It''' is a [[Microgame]] found in the [[List of WarioWare: Touched! microgames#Total Drag|Total Drag]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' The name is a pun on the phrase where a person tells another not to mess up. This microgame always appears as the first microgame of the stage.

==Gameplay==
The objective of the game is to drag a lit match over the candles to light them on the cake. The player can blow into the [[Nintendo DS|DS]] mic to extinguish the candles after all of them are lit, though this is not a requirement of winning the microgame. This game has more time to complete than other microgames.
*'''1st level difficulty:''' There are 2 candles to light.
*'''2nd level difficulty:''' There are 3 candles to light.
*'''3rd level difficulty:''' There are 4 candles to light.

==Trivia==
*The description makes a reference to the song "{{wp|Happy Birthday to You}}" by saying, ''"Don't even think about singing that song."''
