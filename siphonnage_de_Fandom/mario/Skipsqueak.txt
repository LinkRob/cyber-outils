'''Skipsqueak''' is a mouse-like enemy which first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''/''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'' ===
Skipsqueaks made their first appearance in [[Really Rolling Hills]], then later on in [[Grumblump Inferno]], [[Cookie Cogworks]], and [[Rainbow Run]]. They are seen running on the rolling objects then jumping.

===''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]'' ===
Skipsqueaks different from the Skipsqueaks in ''3D World'', as they will try to jump right after Mario does instead of in a set rotation.
