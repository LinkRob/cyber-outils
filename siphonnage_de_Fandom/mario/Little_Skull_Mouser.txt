'''Little Skull Mousers''' are an enemy and a variant of [[Little Mouser]]s featured in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. It would later return in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Little Skull Mousers have the same body as regular Little Mousers—with their visible features including red feet, dark blue body, and a curled tail—but wear a skull on their head. 

===Behavior===
Unlike their parent species, Little Skull Mousers only [[jump]] in place and do not attempt to steal any [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] from a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. Breaking the skull worn by a Little Skull Mouser turns it into a regular Little Mouser.
