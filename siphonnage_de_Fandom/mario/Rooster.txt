'''Roosters''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are based on real-world roosters, which are male members of a [[Chicken (real world)|chicken]] species. The enemy Roosters in ''Wario Land II'' are quite tall, being around the same size as [[Wario]]. A Rooster has a sharp beak that it uses for self-defense.

===Behavior===
If Wario does not provoke a Rooster, it is not hostile. However, if Wario touches a Rooster, it pecks at him. While Wario is holding the [[hen]], specifically during the story [[Return the hen to her nest]], any nearby Roosters peck at Wario, as they are protective over the hen.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
Roosters most prominently appear during the the story "Return the hen to her nest." They are also encountered in the [[Defeat the giant spear man (Uncanny Mansion)|Defeat the giant spear man]] story of [[Uncanny Mansion]]. In either story, if a Rooster pecks at Wario, he loses [[coin]]s.

Wario's [[Dash Attack]] can only stun a Rooster temporarily. Wario can pick up a stunned Rooster, but this lowers his speed and jumping distance while he is doing so. Wario can only defeat a Rooster by [[Ground Pound]]ing onto it.
