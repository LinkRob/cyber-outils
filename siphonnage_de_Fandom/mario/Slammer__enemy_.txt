'''Slammers''' are an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Slammers are moderately big, bulky [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] with huge fists. These ghosts have a red color. They have very large foreheads along with what appears to be an underbite.

===Behavior===
Slammers mainly attack Luigi by clapping and using the shockwaves to harm him.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
Slammers first appear in the mansion [[Gloomy Manor]]. They are one of the ghosts that Luigi encounters throughout his journey. Slammers do not usually appear together, but they commonly appear with different types of ghosts, such as [[Greenie]]s. Slammers each have an [[Heart Point|HP]] of 30, and like many other regular ghost enemies, Luigi can use his [[Strobulb]] to stun a Slammer.
