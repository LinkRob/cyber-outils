'''Cow'''<ref>https://x.com/ign/status/1907780418247561520</ref> is a character appearing in [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']].

== Description ==
Cow is a cute, white bovine creature that resembles the [[Moo Moo]], down to having beady eyes and her body having a few black spots, with one around her right eye. Her nose has a ring run through it, and she wears a blue collar that has a bell hung from it. In the character select screen, Cow walks on all fours, but gameplay shows that she is still capable of driving and presumably walking upright.

== History ==

=== ''Mario Kart World'' ===
Cow was revealed in the Nintendo Direct for ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' on [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3pE23YTYEZM April 2, 2025], and is shown after [[Mario (character)|Mario]] drives his own car on a wall in a similar style to [[Anti-gravity]]. She can be seen riding a bike on a power line. Cow's emblem consists of a stylised depiction of a Moo Moo's face.

Cow was unlockable in version 1.0.0, but she was made available following a software update. She is a heavyweight driver.
