'''Hammer Throw''' is a recurring [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

== In ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ==
{{Q|Hits one enemy with the Hammer, no matter where the enemy is.|Description}}
Hammer Throw is located on the overpass before [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]]. If [[Mario]] strikes the tree by the overpass, a [[springboard]] will fall out of the tree. If Mario utilizes this springboard, he can gain access to the top of the overpass. On top of the overpass Mario will find a treasure chest which contains the Hammer Throw badge.

The Hammer Throw badge costs two [[Badge Point|BP]] and two [[Flower Point|FP]] to use. When equipped, Mario can choose to use the Hammer Throw attack to throw his [[Hammer]] to enemy damage on the enemy.

This badge is useful for injuring flying enemies who are spiked and enemies who cling to the ceiling.

== In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ==
[[File:Hammer ThrowTTYD.gif|left|TTYD Hammer Throw]]
The Hammer Throw badge costs two BP and two FP to use. It is found on the [[Twilight Trail]] in a hidden block. Mario can buy multiples of this badge for fifty [[Pianta Token]]s at the [[Pianta Parlor]].

==Trivia==
*There is a similar item to the Hammer Throw badge, the [[Dusty Hammer]], although it is a lot weaker.
