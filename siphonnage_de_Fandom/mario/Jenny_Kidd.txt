February 14, 1981)|website=[https://www.jennykidd.com]}}'''Jennifer Ann Kidd (née Schoone)''' (born February 11, 1983)  is an American singer and voice actress. She is best known for her role as young [[Pauline]] in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''.

She was cast as [[Pauline]] in the Nintendo Switch 2 video game Donkey Kong Bananza. She refers to her voice as youthful, energetic, and high pitched.
