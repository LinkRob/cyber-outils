*[[Mario]]|owner=[[Boddle]]}}The '''Yoshi Theater''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. It is the only known place in the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] where the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] are located. The Yoshi Theater is run by [[Boddle]], the younger brother of the famous [[Chuckola Cola]] maker [[Bubbles]].

==History==
[[Image:Yoshi Theater HD.png|thumb|right|Yoshi Theater as seen in the remake.]]
Mario and Luigi head to the Yoshi Theater to find the next [[Beanstar]] piece while [[Evelyn, Jose and Markus]] explored outside. They quickly find one but the Yoshi Theater owner Boddle wants to use the shiny piece for his banner. But Jose told him that they have to research for the town in the archeology. Boddle agrees to hand over the Beanstar piece but if only Mario and Luigi could give him special items called [[Neon Egg]]s. Boddle says that the Yoshis will only give up the Neon Eggs if they are fed [[Bean Fruit]] which can be found underground in certain areas over the Beanbean Kingdom. Boddle is nice enough to mark the areas on the map and Mario and Luigi head out to find them. Mario and Luigi return and does so and Boddle gladly hands over the Beanstar piece.

The Yoshi Theater is seen again at the very end of the game where it was revealed that the entire story was watched by the Yoshis on the movie screen.

==Trivia==
*In the theater itself, there are several posters that allude to other video game characters. [[Kirby]] from the Kirby series, [[Wario]], and [[Starfy|Stafy]] from "''The Legend of Stafy" series.''
*There is also a poster with Mario and Luigi on it which hints that this story is all a part of a movie (or was made into a movie).
*In the [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|remake]], these posters are replaced by ones referencing past ''Mario & Luigi'' Games a film, depicting [[Broque Monsieur]], [[Broque Madame]], [[Broggy]], and a [[Blitty]] to reference ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', a [[Shroob]] invasion from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', a [[Cheep Cheep]] in [[Twinsy Tropics]] to reference ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', and a film depicting [[Prince Dreambert]] to reference ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.
