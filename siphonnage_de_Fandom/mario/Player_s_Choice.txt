'''Player's Choice''' is a marketing label, introduced on May 20, 1996,<ref>May 16, 1996. [http://web.archive.org/web/19961222152530fw_/http://www.nintendo.com/newsroom/pr/051696f.html Nintendo Introduces Players Choice Games]. ''Nintendo''. Retrieved March 17, 2021.</ref> on select [[Nintendo]] games that have sold a certain number of copies. Player's Choice was originally only given to titles that had sold over a million copies before being reduced to 250,000 copies for the [[Nintendo GameCube]].<ref>https://web.archive.org/web/20070219174653/http://games.ign.com/articles/453/453311p1.html</ref> Player's Choice re-releases were typically sold at a lower price. Player's Choice was replaced by [[Nintendo Selects|Nintendo Select]] on the [[Wii]] up until the [[Nintendo Switch]].
