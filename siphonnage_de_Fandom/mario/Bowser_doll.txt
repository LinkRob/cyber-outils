The '''Bowser doll'''<ref>''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' Nintendo Player's Guide, page 34. "''Many a visitor to Rose Town Inn has chuckled at the sight of the innkeeper's young son playing with Mario and Bowser dolls.''"</ref> is a toy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The Bowser doll is owned by [[Gaz]], the son of the [[Rose Town innkeeper]]. Gaz likes to recreate [[Mario]]'s various adventures using his [[Mario doll]], [[Peach doll]], and Bowser doll. When Mario arrives in [[Rose Town]], he decides to join Gaz in playing with the dolls. Gaz decides that he will be "Mario" and that the real Mario will use the Bowser doll. During a pretend battle of the dolls, "Mario" loses to "[[Bowser]]," thus the Bowser doll must fight Gaz's favorite doll, [[Geno]]. However, the battle ends prematurely as Geno's Shooting Star Shot accidentally hits Mario, not the Bowser doll.
