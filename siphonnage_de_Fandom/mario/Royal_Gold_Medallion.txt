The '''Royal Gold Medallion''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Royal Gold Medallion is a gold medallion that, according to [[Wario]], has a king's face on it.

==History==
The Royal Gold Medallion can be found in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]] and is one of two medallions that the [[Royal gatekeeper]] requests [[Wario]] to find in order to speak with [[Poobah the Pharaoh]]. The Royal Gold Medallion can be found in a dark room at the bottom of the pyramid where Wario must climb upwards in the room until he reaches a purple treasure chest containing it toward the right.
