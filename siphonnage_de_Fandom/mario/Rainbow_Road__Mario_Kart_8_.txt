'''Rainbow Road''' is the final course of the [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. The course is set at a space station with high-tech sections.

== Layout ==
Rainbow Road takes place in a large space station with numerous high-tech themed sections and floating satellites, as well as an orbiting spacecraft resembling a [[Blooper]]. The space station is presumably owned by Mario Kart TV, as their logos can be seen throughout the satellites. Unlike previous Rainbow Roads, the track appear to be long lines of twisting solar panels, with the rainbow coming from cosmic reflection (this setting may be intentional, to differentiate this track from the [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 64)|N64 Rainbow Road]] in the [[Lightning Cup]]). Similar to ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', it takes place in atmosphere with Earth on the background.
== Behind the scenes ==
=== Creation and development ===
Since the course is located on a space station, the composers used futuristic sound effects to reflect the setting.<ref>[[Nintendo]] (June 2015). Mario Kart 8 Original Soundtrack. ''"This course is actually located on a space station and we've reflected that in the music by using futuristic-sounding effects."'' Retrieved on 2022-04-05.</ref>
