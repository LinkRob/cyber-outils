'''Goomba''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' and the game's representative for his species of the [[Goomba|same name]].

== ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
Goomba lives in the [[Goomba House]] in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. The player can choose to complete Goomba's [[Kind Goomba]] request.

Goomba explains to the player's character that several [[minigame]]s and [[Gaddget]]s have been scattered across the area. At one point, Goomba must be helped by giving him a ticket to the [[Train Station]], and the reward for doing this is the [[Egg Roll (Gaddget)|Egg Roll]] Gaddget.

{{Spoiler}}
At the end of the game, Goomba was assigned as Shroom City's guide.
{{Endspoiler}}
