'''Stinky Herbs''' are items which only appear in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== Effects ==
Each Stinky Herb restores 5 [[Flower Point|FP]], and they are commonly found throughout [[Flower Fields]]. Stinky Herbs are mainly found across the roadways and paths are stone walls. By walking along these walls, the plants fall off and sometimes will drop these items. The Stinky Herbs are used in several of [[Tayce T.]]'s recipes. Once such recipe being the [[Boiled Egg]] item. To cook that, Mario must need both a Stinky Herb and a regular [[Egg]] (which can only be found in a bush near the [[K64]] train of [[Mt. Rugged]]). The useful and crazy [[Kooky Cookie]] is another item Tayce makes, but she needs a [[Cake Mix]] in addition to the Stinky Herb. The [[Special Shake]] recipe can be enhanced by cooking it with this herb to create the helpful [[Healthy Juice]]; however, cooking the Stinky Herb by itself results in a [[Mistake]].
