The '''Hide-and-Seek Toad''' is a minor character from [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'']]. He is a young blue [[Mushroom Retainer]] who can be found throughout the mall of [[Toad Town]].

== Profile ==

=== Personality ===
Like many kids his age, the Hide-and-Seek Toad is shown to be fun-loving, but easily bored from his parents' shopaholic natures. His favourite game is hide-and-seek.

=== Appearance ===
The Hide-and-Seek Toad wears blue mushroom cap with white spots, a blue vest with golden linings, white trousers, and brown shoes. In the 3DS game, his cap is changed to white with blue spots.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'' ==
The Hide-and-Seek Toad can be found first leaving the badge shop out of frustration as his parents go shopping. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can talk to him to initiate a game of hide-and-seek, and must find him throughout the mall. Upon finding the Hide-and-Seek Toad five times, the Hide-and-Seek Toad rewards the Bros. the [[Small Shell]] item, which protects the wearer from damage from the first damaging hit.
