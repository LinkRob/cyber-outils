A '''Tooty Kamen''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are statue enemies that depict in the likeness the actual Tooty Kamen.

==History==
Tooty Kamens can be found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]] where they attempt to crush [[Wario]] should he comes near one. Wario can defeat one by jumping in it's head twice. The actual Tooty Kamen was said to be a kind and gentle king, despite him wanting to "destroy my enemies."
