'''Christopher Collins''' (August 30, 1949 – June 12, 1994) is a real person who did the original portrayal of [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] in ''[[King Koopa's Kool Kartoons]]''. Following an incident when one of the episodes had been filmed, Collins had been fired and replaced with [[Patrick Pinney]].
