'''Retry Clocks''' are rare items which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. They are only usable when [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] or [[Bowser]] fall in battle. If the player has a Retry Clock, they can choose to restart from the beginning of the battle with their [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Special Point|SP]] restored. Retry Clocks cannot be found in shops, but can be found in [[block]]s and can be earned by winning battles.
