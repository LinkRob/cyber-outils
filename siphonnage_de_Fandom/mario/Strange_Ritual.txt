'''Strange Ritual''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' [[Wario]] is its creator, and it can be found in his Spandex Challenge level.

== Layout ==
[[Wario]] and some other persons are dancing around a campfire in a ring. Their location is in shadow, with the circular region within the campfire's glow having reddish-orange ground

== Gameplay ==
To win, the player has to twist the Game Boy Advance so [[Wario]] will move. If Wario collides with any of the other members of the group, he will fall over and the player will lose. In the first level, the group goes one way without changing direction. In the second level, the group goes one way and then switches directions. In level 3, the line is small and the group switches directions constantly.

== Trivia ==

* The microgame's description references the phrase "When in Rome, do as the Romans do," which states that following local customs is wise in a foreign place.
