'''Cat Magikoopas''' are a cat variant of [[Magikoopa]] and an enemy in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Cat Magikoopas have a similar appearance to [[Magikoopa]]s, but with yellow fur, white tipped ears, and a yellow cat tail.
