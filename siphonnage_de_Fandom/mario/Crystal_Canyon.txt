'''Crystal Canyon''' is an unlockable stadium which appears in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Crystal Canyon is one of the two stadiums in ''Mario Strikers Charged'' to take place within a desert, with the other one being [[The Sand Tomb]]. The main gimmick of Crystal Canyon are the [[Giant Crystal]]s placed throughout the court. They reflect the sun's rays, which zap away one or two players from each team. Every character returns after a goal is scored on either side, but it is impossible to have a complete team as the Giant Crystals always zap away one of the characters.

Crystal Canyon is played on during the Finals of the [[Crystal Cup]] in the Road to the Crystal Cup mode. Here, the player can unlock Crystal Canyon, along with [[Diddy Kong]], by winning against Diddy in a best of 3 match.
