'''Jabbis''' are a mosquito-like species which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They are the archenemy of the [[Puni]]s and they all live in [[The Great Tree]].

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
During the events of Chapter 2 where [[Lord Crump]] and the [[X-Naut]]s have taken over The Great Tree, the Jabbis team up with them by building hives to block off the Punis. There are two different Jabbi hives: one that contains 10 Jabbis and another that contains 100 of them. After Mario and [[Punio]] frees the [[Puni Elder]], she will let 10 more Punis follow Mario and the Jabbis were taken down. After the rest of the Punis are freed, the 100 Jabbi army are defeated. If one returns to the rooms where they fought the Jabbis later in the game, there are Jabbis which Mario can interact with.

==Notable members==
*[[Jabble]]: A young Jabbi that is best friends with Punio. Unlike the other Jabbis, Jabble is friendly and actually joins the Puni's side.

==Trivia==
*Although the Jabbis cannot be battled directly, they can still be stunned by [[Madame Flurrie|Flurrie]]'s [[Gale Force]] move making it easier for the Punis to take them down.
**This also makes the Jabbis the only enemies in the game not to be fought in the traditional battle sequence.
