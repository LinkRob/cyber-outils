'''Water Hands''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Water Hand is colored blue like water, and its arm resembles a small, flowing waterfall.

===Behavior===
Water Hands hide in fast currents under small platforms. When [[Wario]] steps on it, a Water Hand attempts to grab Wario and get him to fall into the water. Water Hands have a relative named [[Sand Hand]], which similarly attempt to drag Wario into the quicksand.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Water Hands are only encountered in one [[level]], [[The Steep Canyon]].
