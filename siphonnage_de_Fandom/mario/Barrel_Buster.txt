'''Barrel Busters''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Barrel Buster is a [[barrel]] with a head poking out the top. In [[Shivering Mountains]], the head is made of snow, and in [[Mirror Mansion]], the head is made of cloth.

==In ''Wario World''==
Barrel Busters are only encountered while battling the [[Angler Mangler]]s and [[Terrible Portrait]]s. A Barrel Buster is required to defeat either enemy. To defeat either type of enemy, [[Wario]] first has to stun a Barrel Buster and pick it up. Wario then has to use a [[Piledriver]], specifically under an Angler Mangler's glowing orb or under an arm of a Terrible Portrait. After doing this, the Barrel Buster's head is shot up, hitting the enemy's weak spot.
