'''Tony''' is a character that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Being [[Don Pianta]]'s number-two guy, he is often given tasks such as taking care of the rival gang, the [[Robbo Gang|Robbos]], as seen in the early cutscene when [[Mario]] first talks to [[Toadsworth]]. Mario typically sees Tony along with other members of the gang at the top floor of the [[Pianta Parlor]]. Following Chapter 5, Don Pianta gives him the task of finding his daughter, [[Francesca]], however unfortunately he could not find her, so Don Pinta gives the job to Mario.
