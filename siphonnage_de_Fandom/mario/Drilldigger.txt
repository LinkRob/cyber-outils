The '''Drilldigger''' is an object in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==Description==
The Drilldigger is a large drilling machine on a rail system. It has two drills connected to yellow arms and a center platform that two characters can stand on. The left and right drills are green and red, corresponding to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s colors, respectively. When the [[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]] step on the Drilldigger, it begins moving in a single direction. Mario and Luigi use their [[Hammer]]s to hit a large button next to them, activating their drill: The button on Mario's side is red, and it activates the right drill, while Luigi's is green and activates the left drill. The drills are used mainly to destroy cracked boulders.

Use of the Drilldigger can be affected by Drilldigger Panels on the ground. One of them indicates where the rails split and which path is taken by the Drilldigger. The direction can be changed using the [[Dunk Hammer|Mole Mario]] technique by going underneath the panel and jumping out. The direction of the Drilldigger can be switched if Luigi's drill hits a green switch with a U-turn icon. One of the gray stations has a red button that Mario's drill can hit to stop the Drilldigger there.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
The Drilldigger is located in [[Dozing Sands]]. Mario and Luigi agree to use the Drilldigger to help [[Britta]] and [[Shelltop]]s in their construction project of creating a tourist attraction. The Mario Bros. are tasked with using the Drilldigger to destroy cracked boulders. One of the boulders is exceptionally large and contains [[Pi'illo]] pillows with [[Dream Portal]]s leading to [[Dreamy Dozing Sands]].
