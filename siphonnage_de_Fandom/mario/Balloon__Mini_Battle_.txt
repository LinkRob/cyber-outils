A '''Balloon''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
A Balloon is used in two [[Mini Battle]]s—[[Popping Balloons]] and [[Throwing Balloons]]—in which it is colored red and magenta respectively. Unlike most balloons, it does not have a lip at the bottom.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
In Popping Balloons, the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] has to [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] the Balloon containing the item before the [[Bandit]] does.

In Throwing Balloons, a Yoshi and a Bandit throw a balloon back and forth as it gradually expands. Each time before the Balloon is thrown, either side has to input a combination of buttons to throw it to their opponent. Whoever is holding the balloon when it pops loses the Mini Battle.
