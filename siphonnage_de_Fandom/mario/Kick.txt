'''Kicking''' is a recurring move throughout the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. It first appears in the [[arcade]] game ''[[Mario Bros. (video game)|Mario Bros.]]''. Kicking is one of the most basic physical moves, along with [[punch]]ing.
