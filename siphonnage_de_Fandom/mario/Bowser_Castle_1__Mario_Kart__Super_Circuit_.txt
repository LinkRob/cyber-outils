'''GBA Bowser Castle 1''' is the first of four Bowser Castle courses in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'', being the last course in [[Mushroom Cup]]. The course is quite short, having only a few [[Thwomp]]s, and many [[coin]]s. Although this is easy, there are not many [[Item Box]]es seen in the course. This course takes place inside the castle, the background proving it.

==In ''Mario Kart 7''==
Bowser Castle 1 appears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' as the 2nd course in the Shell Cup (between [[Luigi Raceway]] and [[Mushroom Gorge]]). This version remains the same for the most part, however a glide ramp emerges from the first jump shortly after 1st places crosses the line for the first time (other than at the race's start). This allows drivers to bypass the rest of the jumps and possibly glide all the way to the line (made easier with a [[Paraglider]]).

Although the map was graphically updated heavily, most other changes were small. On the game's launch, there was a serious bug on this course that allowed players to use an unintentional shortcut that shaved many seconds of time off of a players' [[lap]]. This, alongside similarly major glitches on [[Wuhu Loop]] and [[Maka Wuhu]], prompted a post-launch patch to the game that fixed all three of these issues.
