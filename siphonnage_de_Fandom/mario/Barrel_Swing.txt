'''Barrel Swing''' is [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s [[Star Swing]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces his [[Banana Ball]] from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', which he instead only uses for pitching.

==Description==
Upon initiating Barrel Swing, Donkey Kong pounds his chest a few times, and a [[barrel]] drops down from the sky. Donkey Kong then hits the barrel, causing it to roll across the field. If no fielder catches the barrel, it continues to roll. If a fielder tries to catch it, they become stunned for a while after catching the ball, and the barrel disappears. It is possible for Donkey Kong to score a home-run when using a Barrel Swing.
