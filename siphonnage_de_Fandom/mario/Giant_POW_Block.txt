'''Giant POW Block''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Advance]]''.

==Description==
A Giant POW Block is a larger variation of a [[POW Block]], and it has red borders. A Giant POW Block bounces upon hitting the ground, creating four tremors in the process before disappearing.
