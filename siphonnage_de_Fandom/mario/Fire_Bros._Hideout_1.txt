'''Fire Bros. Hideout #1''' is a secret level in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' found in World 4.

==Layout==
The level takes place in a square arena at night. The player's objective must defeat 3 [[Fire Bro.|Fire Bros.]] within 100 seconds.

==Green Star==
*'''Green Star 1''': Defeat the three Fire Bros. to get the [[Green Star]].
