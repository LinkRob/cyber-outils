These are all the possible combinations for the minigame Power-up Panels of <i>[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]</i> in each world. Green colors are indicated to be safe spots. There is also an open source tool [https://wischi-chr.github.io/MarioWiiPowerUps/ MarioWiiPowerUps] that helps finding the correct panel.
