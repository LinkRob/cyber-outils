The '''Thwomp Elevator''' is a character who appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. He is a giant [[Thwomp]] bearing a resemblance to [[Mr. Thwomp]] and is likewise found at [[Thwomp Volcano]].

He assisted [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] by going through volcano and is only stopped due to obstacle of a block in his path.

Last time the Thwomp Elevator is seen is on the [[Shroob Mother Ship|mothership]] after he was knocked out of volcano alongside [[Bowser]] and [[Baby Bowser|his younger self]].

Going back to Volcano afterwards has him nowhere to be found leaving him still stuck on the mothership. It is most likely [[Princess Shroob]] had him destroyed offscreen.
