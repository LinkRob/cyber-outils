'''Kopter''' is an unlockable playable character and a representative of the species of the [[Kopter|same name]]. He wears a black pilot cap with a propeller on top, beige pants, and a pair of googles; both of its eye pupils are red.

== In ''Donkey Kong Barrel Blast'' ==
In ''Donkey Kong Barrel Blast'', Kopter can be unlocked by completing the Topaz Cup by playing as a Kremling. Kopter and Wrinkly are the only rivals to be of different genders. Kopter has average boost and speed, and his agility is high:

* '''Boost:''' 3/5
* '''Speed:''' 3/5
* '''Agility:''' 5/5
