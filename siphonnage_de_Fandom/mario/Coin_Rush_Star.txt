{{Infobox Object|name=Coin Rush Star|image=File:Coin_Rush_Star.png|first=''[[Super Mario Run]]'' (2016)|latest=|games=}}[[File:Coin Rush Star Img.png|left|thumb|Small Toad about to grab a Coin Rush Star]]The '''Coin Rush Star''' is an item that debuts in the Toad Rally Mode in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. They have a blue star with silver eyes (which it is reverse from the [[Starman]]'s colors). Players need to fill up a gauge in the top center of the screen by collecting coins and performing certain moves. Once it is filled up, they will appear the meter and levitate over the player for a few seconds, before they dropping onto them and start a Coin Rush for an amount of time. However, it do not make the character affected invisible. The time of the Coin Rush can be increased when the coins are collected and moves are performed. 

During a Coin Rush, enemies will give twice as many coins when they are defeated, more coins and power-ups will come out of blocks, and the coins will come out of pipes.

A Coin Rush can also activated by collecting the [[Starman]], the Coin Rush will then last as long as the star does.
