The '''Down Under Land mayor''' is a character who makes his first and only appearance in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Crocodile Mario]]."

==Physical description==
The Down Under Land mayor is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] who wears black shoes, and a green top that may be a vest. He also has a mustache and a clear circle around on his right eye.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
He tells [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool]] (also known as Princess Peach), along with [[Toad]] to stop Kangaroo Koopa after he takes a [[Magic Statue]], which is used as protection for [[Down Under Land]]. This statue has the ability to repel crocodiles. 

The quartet arrive in Down Under Land too late. Crocodiles have attacked and are chewing up buildings. The only place that the crocodiles cannot reach is the roof the general store, where everyone including Toad, Princess, the Mayor and the Toads of the town scurry too. Mario and Luigi flee the crocodiles while promising the mayor they will recover the statue.

While Luigi and Mario are trying to thwart Kangaroo Koopa, the crocodiles continue their attack. When they chew up the general store's foundation to the point the building starts crumbling, the Mayor orders everyone to get to higher ground and everyone jumps to a nearby tree. Fortunately for the Toads, Mario and Luigi soon return with the statue. Princess Toadstool gives the statue a kiss, which seems to activate its magic powers and cause the crocodiles to leave Down Under Land. The Mayor thanks Mario and Luigi for saving the day.

== Trivia ==
He is voiced by Harvey Atkin, which was one of his few times he voiced a character other than Bowser.
