}}'''Sunshine Isles''' is a secret course in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. The player can access this course via a painting in the [[Rec. Room]]. This is also a [[Silver Star]] level. It's also the second level in the multiplayer VS. Mode.

== Layout ==
The course has a beach theme that is similar to the [[Delfino Plaza]] music from ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. There are a few small beach areas surrounding the main island. The lands are surrounded by water all around the areas, making the course small. The course has twenty-eight coins in total. With ten not being in enemies and eighteen in enemies. There is also the [[Koopa Shell]] the player can get by hitting a yellow [[Exclamation Block]], this Shell can come in handy when collecting the Silver Stars.

== Stars ==
This course only has one Star that can only be obtained by collecting 5 Silver Stars. The player can get Silver Stars by hitting [[Klepto]] (who is holding one of them in his talons), on the top of two of the palm trees, in a niche under the biggest island, and on top of the biggest island. Like last time, if the player gets hit by an enemy, he/she loses a Silver Star. The player must collect all five Silver Stars to summon a [[Power Star]] to collect on their journey to stop [[Bowser]].
