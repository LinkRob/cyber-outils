'''Beam Robota '''(ビームロボタ ''Bīmu Robota'') are enemies which appear in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''. They are blue robots and a variant of [[Spear-bot]]s that attack [[Wario]] by shooting beam blades at him, cutting Wario in half for a few seconds before he automatically returns to normal. Beam Robotas have a relative named [[Fire Robota]].
