A '''Magmite''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Magmites are a variant of [[Buzzy Beetle]]s with a rock shell, and they only appear in [[Moleville Mines]]. Magmites have stronger relatives called [[Magmus]], which they share an artwork image with, although Magmus have a more red-tinted sprite.

==Battle==
In battle, Magmites only attack by charging at one of [[Mario]]'s party members. Magmites are known for their high defense at the point when they are encountered.

===Strategy===
Although Magmites are weak against ice attacks, [[Mallow]] does not have [[Snowy]] at the point when Magmites are encountered. Magmites also resists Mario's Jump attacks, so the best way to handle Magmites is to attack them physically.
