'''X-Naut PhDs''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
They are [[X-Naut]] doctors that develop many of the X-Naut's arsenal of weaponry and advanced technology. Led by [[Sir Grodus]], they are a part of the X-Naut alliance and are found in the [[X-Naut Fortress]].

In battle, X-Naut PhDs can use various chemicals. They can attack either [[Mario]] and his partners or even use their chemicals on themselves or allies. Their attack is broken into five different attacks of different colors.
