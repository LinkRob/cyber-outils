'''Blitties''' are a species in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. They are living [[Brock]]s bearing the likeness of a cat.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story/Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
They are all found inside various enemies and can be retrieved by [[Bowser]] using the [[Vacuum Block]] and sucked the Blitty out of the enemy. If fifteen Blitties are found, [[Broque]] allows Bowser to give [[Broggy]] a walk, granting the special move [[Broggy Bonker]].

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
A Blitty makes a cameo in [[Yoshi Theater]] on a poster of a film with [[Broque Monsieur]], [[Broque Madame]], and Broggy.
