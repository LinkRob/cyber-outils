'''''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''''' is the [[Wii]] home console release of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', released in 2009 on the same day as the [[Nintendo DS]] [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|version]] in most countries. It follows after ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'', the Wii [[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|version]] in particular. It was created to coincide with the [[wikipedia:2010 Winter Olympics|2010 Winter Olympic games]] in Vancouver, Canada.

The game is similar to the original, but with different events, new characters and several big changes in gameplay and control schemes.
