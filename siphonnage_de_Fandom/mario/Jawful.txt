'''Jawful''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a strange enemy that wields a large fork as a weapon. Jawful is only encountered in [[Nimbus Land]]. It has a relative named [[Forkies]].

== Description ==
Jawfuls have a red body that resembles a tomato to some degree, with arms and legs coming directly from their main body and a green tuft of hair. In their right hand is a fork with three prongs.

==Battle==
Jawful is often found sleeping in the first few turns of the battle. After he has woken up, Jawful mainly attacks with his large fork. He does not posses any other skills or special attacks in battle.
