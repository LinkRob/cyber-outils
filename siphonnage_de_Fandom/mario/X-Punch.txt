==History==
The X-Punches are only seen in the [[Magnus von Grapple 2.0]] fight near the end of Chapter 7. They can be summoned by [[Lord Crump]] as his HP gets low. Just like with the X-Fists, the X-Punches act as independent air foes that can be damaged and defeated. The X-Punches have an HP of five and can deal nine points of damage which is very high. It is recommend to take out the X-Punches as soon as possible.
