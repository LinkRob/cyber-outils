|name=Big KO Hammer (Battle Card)}}'''Big KO Hammer''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Big KO Hammer is the big sideways version of [[Mario]]'s [[Hammer attack]]. It is a big yellow and blue hammer with a star in the center, accompanied with a yellow/pastel yellow checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 16/70 coins

Sell Price: 8/35 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Big KO Hammer is first seen in [[Plum Park]], but can also found in [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fortune Island]], [[Mustard Café]], [[Toad Trainworks]], [[Redpepper Volcano]], and [[Mossrock Theater]].
