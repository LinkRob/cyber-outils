The '''Sprixie Homeworld''' is a planet whose main landmark is the [[Sprixie Kingdom]]. It is similar in size and climate to [[Earth]] and the [[Mushroom Earth]]. As its name implies, it is where the [[Sprixie]] species originates from. [[Bowser]] and his [[Koopa Troop|forces]] invade the planet at the start of the game, and the [[Sprixie Princesses]] are caught in the aftermath. [[Mario]] [[Luigi|and]] [[Princess Peach|his]] [[Toad (character)|friends]] quit their walk to stop the Koopa King before his plans to take over the kingdom (and possibly the planet itself) get too far. It can also be seen in the background of [[World Star (Super Mario 3D World)|World Star]], [[World Mushroom (Super Mario 3D World)|World Mushroom]], and [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]].
