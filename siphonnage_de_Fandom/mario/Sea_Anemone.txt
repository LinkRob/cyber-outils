'''Sea Anemones''' is an underwater enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
They are stationary, invincible enemies which only appear in one level, [[Lots O'Fish]], and are even featured within its icon.

=== Behavior ===
Sea Anemones have large, stinging tentacles that they try to attack [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] with. Sea Anemone often guard common items, such as [[coin]]s or [[melon]]s, but must otherwise be avoided.
