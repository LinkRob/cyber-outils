The '''Trophy''' is a [[Stickers#Thing Stickers|Thing Sticker]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. 

== Description ==
The Trophy sticker is pretty large in size. It also has a brown base  and handles. It even has a human figure in a bowling stance at the top.

Unlike most Thing stickers, the Trophy sticker doesn't have any uses for the levels itself. It can, however, be used in battle where it throws its bowling ball to deal damage to enemies.
