'''Boss Rush''' is a gamemode in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' that allows the player to compete for points and beat all the bosses and mini bosses of ''Mario Party 9''.

==Gameplay==
The players need to fight all 12 boss battles (or 6 battles) to exit with exceptions of Diddy King and Donkey Kong Mini games. Once you get in, there's no way to exit until you fight all boss battles. After fighting all bosses, points are added depending to your rank in boss battles: five points for first, three points for second, two points for third and one point for fourth. Finally, Toad will announce the winner(s). Completing this mode gives you 10 party points.
