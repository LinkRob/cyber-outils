'''Bowser's Villa 8-9''' is the ninth level of [[Bowser's Villa]], in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. It is the final level of the game. 
==Layout==
The level starts off in a room with [[Koopa Troopa|Green Koopa]]s, and statues that the player needs to mimic. If the player does not mimic the statue, then the [[Security Thwomp]] in the background will ingest them, and put them back to the start of the room. At the top of the room is a [[Warp Pipe]].

The next section is a zip-wire section. Perry will automatically use his [[Slidebrella]] form. At the end of it is a Warp Pipe.

The next room has various enemies, before several [[coin]]s, a level for Toad Shot, and the goal gate.
