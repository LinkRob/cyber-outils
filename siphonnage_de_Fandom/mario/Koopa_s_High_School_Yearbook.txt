"'''Koopa's High School Yearbook'''" is a single-panel comic within the Nintendo Comics System series comics. It is from the ''Super Mario Bros. #2'' edition, and was released in 1990.

== Description ==
Hence the comic's title, it is a single page from [[Bowser]]'s high school yearbook. Shown on the page are three members that are of the same species as Bowser: a [[Koopa]] that wears glasses and is bullied quite often, a female Koopa that has a crush on Bowser, and Bowser himself. A fourth Koopa is partially seen, but the rest of the page has been ripped off, and thus, an additional image showing Bowser bullying the Koopa with the glasses is revealed.

On the single page are comments from Bowser's peers. Most of these comments express fear or loathing towards him, but one of them, which is implied to be from the female that has a crush on him, reveals that the extent of her relationship with him was that he once asked if she had finished eating her sandwich.
