'''Origami Piranha Plants''' are a variant of [[Piranha Plant]] featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. The main trait of an Origami Piranha Plant is that they are made out of origami, although they can also spit [[fireball]]s like a [[Venus Fire Trap]].
