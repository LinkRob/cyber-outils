{{nihongo|'''Tokyo'''|東京|Tōkyō}} is the capital city of [[Japan]] and a real-world city on [[Earth]]. It has made a few appearances in the {{franchise|Mario}}.

==In ''[[Mario is Missing!]]''==
[[File:Tokyo MIM.png|thumb|left|200px|The map of Tokyo in ''Mario is Missing!'' for SNES]]
Tokyo is one of the cities visited by [[Luigi]] in all versions of the game. In the [[MS-DOS]] and PC versions, Tokyo is part of the fourth level set. In the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] version, it is in the third, final level set. Lastly, in the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] version, Tokyo is in the second pair of levels alongside [[Paris]].

Like every other city appearing in the game, [[Koopa Troopa]]s have stolen artifacts from landmarks around Tokyo that Luigi has to return to their original places. This includes the [[Sensoji Temple Lantern|lantern]] from the [[Sensoji Temple]], a [[Sumo Apron]] from the [[Kokugikan Arena]], and an [[Great Buddha's Orange|orange]] from the [[Great Buddha of Kamakura]]. In the PC version, two other items are also stolen: a [[Mask from the Kabuki Theater|mask]] from the [[Kabuki Theater]], and [[Tong Tong|a panda]] from the [[Ueno Zoo]].

==Crossover appearances==
===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020]]''===
The entire game takes place in Tokyo, Japan, as it was the setting of the {{w|2020 Summer Olympics}} as well as the {{w|1964 Summer Olympics}}. In Story Mode, various landmarks of Tokyo are visited by the characters. Similarly, this is also the setting for its [[arcade]] counterpart, ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games Tokyo 2020 - Arcade Edition]]''.
