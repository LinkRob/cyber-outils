'''Red Spaces''' (or '''red spaces'''), known as '''minus spaces''' in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' and '''Lose Coins Spaces''' in ''[[Mario Party 10]]'', are [[space]]s in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]].

==Description==
When a player lands on a Red Space, they will lose [[coin]]s (three to twelve, depending on the game and the specific circumstances). In ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' and ''[[Mario Party 8]]'', a [[Bonus Star]] may be awarded to the player who lands on the most Red Spaces. Also, like with all of the other spaces in ''Mario Party 8'', the shape is different on each [[board]], including the tutorial. In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', Red Spaces have been replaced by [[Mini Ztar Space]]s. 

In ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', there are spaces with an identical appearance titled [[Danger Space]]s.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
In ''Mario Party 5'', these spaces are referred to as "minus spaces" in [[Eldstar]]'s explanation of them in the rules board and in a message in [[Bowser Nightmare]] after the effects of [[Bowser]]'s walk from a [[Event Space|? space]] wear off. However, they are instead referred to as "red spaces" during the [[Last Five Turns Event]] and the Party Mode results screen.
