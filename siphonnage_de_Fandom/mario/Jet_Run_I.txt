**[[Yellow Yoshi]]|image=noimage.jpg}}'''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a [[Badge Challenge]] course in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. This level has one [[Wonder Seed]] and is unlocked after completing [[Cosmic Hoppos]]. The completion of this course unlocks [[Pipe-Rock Plateau Special: Bounce, Bounce, Bounce]] in the [[Special World (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Special World]].

== Layout ==
This course is set entirely in the clouds. The course has many [[Semisolid Platform|Semisolid Platforms]] and at the start of the course, yellow arrows show the player when to jump. After the first two jumps, the yellow arrows disappear and [[Flower coin|flower coins]] replace them. Near the [[Checkpoint Flag]] is a [[Wow Bud]] that shows the player where to go next. 

== [[10-Flower Coin]] locations ==
The first coin can be found directly above the second gap of the course. The player can only reach it if the jump is made at the exact right timing.

The second coin can be found just before the [[Checkpoint Flag]], hovering above a pit.

The last coin can be found about halfway between the Checkpoint Flag and the [[Goal Pole]].{{SMBWNav}}
