'''Jerry''' is a character who appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. He is a male, cherry-red [[Bob-omb]] who joins [[Luigi]] on his [[Super Luigi|quest]].

==History==
Jerry is the second character to join Luigi on his quest. They adventure to [[Plumpbelly Village]] of the [[Waffle Kingdom]]. Luigi had to dress as a bride as an offering to [[Hizza]], the two-headed snake. Jerry was apparently disgusted by Luigi's appearance, but he helps Luigi defeat Hizza. Jerry then decides to continue following Luigi on his quest hoping to never see anyone else see Luigi in a dress. However, in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Jerry's dialogue has been altered slightly to him saying he will continue to follow Luigi throughout his quest so that no one will experience his clumsiness.
