'''Chain Chomp Wheel''' (known as '''Chain Chomp Roulette''' in Europe and Oceania) is a battle stage in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. It is shaped like a roulette wheel, and rotates counter-clockwise. It appears to be located in an aquarium, since some eels can be seen swimming outside of it, and there are also [[Mushroom]]-shaped plants there.

All the racers begin at the top of the stage, and fall straight back down it. A [[Chain Chomp]] rolls down the middle as an obstacle, which is a right pest since in the middle there is a giant star complete with some [[Item Box]]es.
