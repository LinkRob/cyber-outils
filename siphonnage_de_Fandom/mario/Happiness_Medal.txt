'''Happiness Medals''' are an item in ''[[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy]]''.

==Description==
Happiness Medals are medals that the [[Yoshi Island Spirits|Spirits]] of [[Yoshi's Island (location)|Yoshi's Island]] award [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] with for completing missions. To receive a Happiness Medal, Yoshi has to complete the tasks provided by a Spirit. A certain minimum number of Happiness Medals are required to access the next chapter.

There are two types of Happiness Medals, the [[Silver Medal]] and the [[Gold Medal (Yoshi Topsy-Turvy)|Gold Medal]]. Yoshi earns a Silver Medal if he gets a score near the requirement, and the player gets two chances in the [[Bonus Chance!]]. A Gold Medal is earned if Yoshi gets a score better than that required by the Spirits, and it also gives the player better odds at winning the Bonus Chance!.
