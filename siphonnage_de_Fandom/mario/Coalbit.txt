'''Coalbits''' are coal-based enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. Coalbits are only encountered in the [[Tunnel]], where they usually assist a [[Choomba]] during battle.
