'''Pro Goombas''', named '''Gu Goombas''' in the original game, are a stronger variant of [[Goomba]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario RPG (Nintendo Switch)|remake]]. They are only encountered in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]] toward the end of the game.

==Battle==
In battle, Pro Goombas tend to act confused and sometimes attack themselves and others. However, they can use the move [[Thornet]] that may [[Poison (status effect)|Poison]] one of Mario's party.

===Strategy===
Pro Goombas are rather weak enemies and even the simplest attack should finish them in one hit.

==Trivia==
*If [[Bowser]] is present in a battle against a Pro Goomba, they will run away from battle.
*Pro Goombas are one of few enemies in the game where two of its stats are the same (both their defense and magic defense are 66).
