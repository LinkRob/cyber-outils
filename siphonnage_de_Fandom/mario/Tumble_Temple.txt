'''Tumble Temple''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
The view is slowly adjusted from just above the [[lava]] to behind the characters while they each move onscreen to their respective positions.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Tumble Temple Perspective Mode.png|thumb|left|250px|Tumble Temple in Perspective Mode]]
The characters have to dodge [[Spiked Ball]]s and [[Giant Spiked Ball]]s, which fall through a field of pegs that stick in and out at random intervals. If a character touches a Spiked Ball or a Giant Spiked Ball, they are eliminated. As the minigame progresses, more Spiked Balls begin to roll down to where the characters are. After 30 seconds, the two spiky pillars begin to spin, increasing the speed of any Spiked Balls that touch them. Whoever is either the last character remaining is the winner. Alternatively, if the minigame reaches the end of the 60-second [[time limit]], any characters remaining by then are the winners.

Tumble Temple is one of the minigames that has a [[Reverse Minigame]] counterpart; the objective is the opposite, as the characters compete to have a Spiked Ball hit them first. Tumble Temple is also the first minigame played in [[Perspective Mode]].

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Pad}}: Move
*{{button|wii|2}}: Jump

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules (Normal)''': ''Dodge the Spiked Balls as they roll down the ramp. Be the last player standing to win!''
*'''Rules (Reverse)''': ''Be the first player to get smashed by the Spiked Balls that roll down the ramp.''
*'''Controls''': ''Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Press {{button|wii|Pad}} to move and {{button|wii|2}} to jump.''
