|activated by=Purchase of item}}The '''Hearty Latte''' is an item in [[Paper Mario: Color Splash|''Paper Mario: Color Splash'']]. This item costs 70 coins, and heals [[Mario]] to max health. It can be purchased in [[Prisma Café|Prisma Cafés]] located in [[Port Prisma]], [[Ruddy Road]], [[Fort Cobalt]], and any [[Roshambo Temple]].
