'''Da Vine Climb''' is a [[Donkey Kong (character)|DK]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Gameplay==
The goal of this mini-game is to climb vines and collect [[banana]]s in only ten seconds. However, the main obstacle is the spiders that are on the vines. If the player makes contact with a spider, it will stun the player for a short time, but can be dangerous as there is only ten seconds.

One banana will award one, two, or three coins, and a bunch of bananas will award five, ten, or fifteen coins.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|A}} ''(Hold)'' - Climb Vine
*{{button|gcn|B}} ''(Hold)'' - Descend Vine
*{{button|gcn|R}} - Move Right
*{{button|gcn|L}} - Move Left
