The '''Silver Card''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Silver Card allows [[Mario]] to play the Paper Game in the [[Pianta Parlor]]. Mario receives it by completing [[Pine T. Jr.]]'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]] titled "Help my Daddy!".
