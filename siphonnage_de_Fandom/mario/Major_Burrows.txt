==Profile==
===Physical description===
Major Burrows is a large furry Undergrunt who wears a spiky blue helmet that he protects his head with. When on his final phase, his fur and helmet turns red and his eyes turn full yellow. Defeating him causes him to get blank eyes, although those may presumably be eyelids.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Major Burrows is the only boss that appears in the [[Gusty Garden Galaxy]], fought in the [[Mission|levels]] The Dirty Tricks of Major Burrows, where he is seen chasing a [[Star Bunny]] around a tree. The Major returns to slake his revenge in Major Burrows' Daredevil Run, empowered by a [[Prankster Comet|Daredevil Comet]] so his attacks can instantly kill Mario, alongside every other dangerous object in the game.

==== Combat ====

===== Phase 1 =====
Initially, Major Burrows will be unaware of his planet being visited by [[Mario (character)|Mario]]. To stop him, Mario must use his [[Ground Pound]] skill to make Burrows jump out of the dirt and then attack him with a [[Star Spin]]. Doing so will cause the Major to take heed of Mario's presence and attack the plumber.

===== Phase 2 =====
Starting from the second phase, Major Burrows gets angry and chases Mario, leaving his original Star Bunny target to retreat up a tree. When Major Burrows gets close to Mario, his spiky head will emerge from the ground, when that happens, Mario must immediately use a Ground-Pound that will make him jump out but back in. This time, the Major will pop out of the ground and flee, and his rear end must be struck with a Star Spin (but if you're too slow, he'll jump back into the ground and chase after Mario again).

===== Phase 3 =====
Phase 3 is identical to phase 2, but this time, Major Burrows must be launched out of the ground twice, as the first time, he retreats back into the ground and charges furiously at Mario. After Major Burrows is defeated, the chased Space Rabbit will thank Mario and give him a [[Power Star]]. To make this battle easier, there is 6-Life Mushroom you can find and use.

==== Other Appearances ====
Major Burrows also appears in the credits, where he and Mario are shown fighting.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Major Burrows is now the third boss in the [[Boss Blitz Galaxy]]. Unlike last time however, he isn't seen chasing a frightened [[Star Bunny]] this time. When Burrows is defeated, he leaves behind a [[Launch Star]] that leads to [[Bouldergeist]].
