'''Stop Time for Red Coins''' is the sixth and final [[mission]] of [[Tick Tock Clock]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. It returns in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' as the penultimate mission of the course. It is intended to be completed by stopping the clockworks, done by entering the Tick Tock Clock [[painting]] with the minute hand on the "12."

==Layout==
[[File:Stop Time For Red Coins DS.png|thumb|left|In ''Super Mario 64 DS'']]
Near the player's starting position are [[rotating bar]]s scaling up the wall. If the clockworks are moving, these platforms will be spinning, making it difficult to collect the [[Red Coin]]s between the platforms. Once all the Red Coins are collected, the [[Power Star]] appears over the [[Star Marker]]. In the original game, the Power Star is placed much higher than usual so it is in line with a rotating bar. In the DS version, some of the rotating bars are placed differently and the Power Star is placed normally over the Star Marker.
