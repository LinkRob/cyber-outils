'''400m''' is a recurring event in the [[Mario & Sonic (series)|''Mario & Sonic'' series]], specifically the summer installments, in which the characters run 400m. The event first appeared in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Games]]'' for the [[Wii]].
