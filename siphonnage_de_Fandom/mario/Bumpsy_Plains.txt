{{InfoboxNeeded}}{{Image}}'''Bumpsy Plains''' is an area in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. It is northeast of [[Dimble Wood]], east of [[Blubble Lake]], south of [[Bowser Path]] , and north of [[Cavi Cape]] . There are three enemies in this area, the [[Filfit]] , the [[Sneed]] , and the [[Latiku King]].

==Appearance==
Bumpsy Plains has gray hard rocks for walls with a bright green floor. There are brown bricks to smash (as Bowser) or to jump on (as [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]].)

== Trivia ==
* It is the only area in the game to not be displayed on the World Map.
* It is also one area in the pairs that share music with another area.
