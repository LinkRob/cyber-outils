'''Goomba Mario''' is a [[Wonder Effect]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. When the player's character touches a [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]], there is a chance of them turning into their [[Goomba]] form.

As Goomba Mario, Mario wears his trademark cap, but resembles the regular [[Goomba]] otherwise. In this state, he moves more slowly and cannot jump. Mario will revert back to his regular form if he touches a [[Wonder Seed]].

== Trivia ==
* Because [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] do not have a hat or a mushroom top, they have a cracked [[Yoshi Egg]] on their head for their Goomba form.
** Nabbit still wears his costume and mask [[:File:Goomba Nabbit.png|in his Goomba form]]. He also has his ears. 
* The [[Goomba Mask]] is an item that makes the player attain a similar form, but the player is actually in a Goomba costume rather than a Goomba themself.
