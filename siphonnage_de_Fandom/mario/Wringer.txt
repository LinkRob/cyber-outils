|desc=Sells [[Thing]] cards to Mario for coins.}}The '''Wringer''' is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. This toad sells [[Things|Thing]] cards for a certain price, depending on the [[Things|Thing]].

==Location==
[[File:Wringer in PMCS screenshot.png|left|frameless|70x70px]]
He can be found in a barrell in the harbor district of [[Port Prisma]]. When [[Mario]] approaches the barrell to talk to him, the lid opens up. Talking to him for the first time gives a free [[Plunger]] away as a "free squeeze."
