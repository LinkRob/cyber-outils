'''Smash Egg''' is a [[Bros. Item]] which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.

==Description==
A Smash Egg resembles a [[Yoshi Egg]] but is patterned with yellow stars instead of spots. It somewhat resembles the [[Star Egg]] from ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. A Smash Egg can be bought at certain [[Item Shop]]s for 40 [[coin]]s and sold for 10 coins.

A Smash Egg is only used during battle, in which the two brother pairs—[[Luigi]] with [[Baby Luigi]] and [[Mario]] with [[Baby Mario]]—kick the Smash Egg at an enemy. They can do so up to six times. When a Smash Egg returns to either pair, the player must press each Mario Bros. pair's corresponding button ({{button|ds|A}} or {{button|ds|B}}) at the correct timing. If one of the pairs miss, they trip over and lose the Smash Egg. The babies can bounce the egg higher, forcing it to the other bro and increasing its power. One bounce increases the power by 25%, and two by 50%, at which it caps off.

There is a small chance of three different enemies dropping a Smash Egg: [[Pidgit]], [[Gnarantula]], and [[Love Bubble]], at 12%, 8%, and 12% respectively.
