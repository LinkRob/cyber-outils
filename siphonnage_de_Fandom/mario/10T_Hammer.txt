The '''10T Hammer''' is an item which first appears in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
The 10T Hammer is a type of [[Hammer]] and one of [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]'s special items. It consists of a brown handle and a large, black hammer head, having a nearly identical appearance to the Hammers in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, but with a light gray "10t" (indicating its weight of ten tons) on the side.

The 10T Hammer, like the regular hammer, is a battering item that involves the user swinging the 10T Hammer for approximately five seconds, damaging any racers within range. There is a similar item named the [[Thunder Stick]], which functions the same, but electrocutes its target.

==In ''Mario Kart Arcade GP''==
The 10T Hammer is in the challenge game of [[DK Cup]]. Here, the player's character of choice must use a 10T Hammer to knock down 15 [[Koopa Troopa]]s in [[DK Jungle (Mario Kart Arcade GP)|DK Jungle]]. This is the only instance in which the 10T Hammer can be used by a character aside from Donkey Kong, and the 10T Hammer is the only special item that can be used by anyone besides whoever normally owns the item.
