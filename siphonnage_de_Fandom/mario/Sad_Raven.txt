A '''Sad Raven''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sad Ravens are a variant of [[Raven]]s with a light blue body, and they are always seen crying a stream of water from their eyes, given that Sad Ravens are imbued with the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Sad Ravens grow briefly while crying, and they can become so loud, to the point of creating tremors that can stun [[Princess Peach|Peach]] for a few seconds.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Sad Ravens first appear in the [[level]] [[Hoo's Wood 2-4]].
