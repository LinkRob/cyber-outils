'''Ice Guys Finish Last''' is the seventh level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 13.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 2 [[Elite Troopea]]s and 6 [[Ice Bro]]s. The enemy captain is a Ice Bro.

The final battle contains 2 [[Lakipea]]s and 6 Ice Bros. The enemy captain is a Ice Bro.

No allies are gained after the final battle.

The player can replay the level to recruit up to 1 Ice Bro each time.
