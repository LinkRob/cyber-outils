'''Risky Whisker Island''' is an island located on [[Lake Lapcat]] in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Risky Whisker Island is one of the few islands surrounding the Wasteland [[Giga Bell]]. It is a large rectangular field of [[Donut Block]]s.
