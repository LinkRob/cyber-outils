'''Peridot Campgrounds''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Description==
The Peridot Campgrounds is a campsite located in the outskirts of [[Diamond City]].

==In ''WarioWare Gold''==
The Peridot Campgrounds is the setting of the [[Potluck Gang]] stage. In the opening cutscene, several employees of [[WarioWare, Inc.]] have gathered at the Peridot Campgrounds for a barbecue. Some of the characters bring along inedible food, and [[9-Volt]] starts to think that the potluck a bad idea when [[Ashley]] brought along [[Mandrake]] with her. [[5-Volt]] suggests playing cards until the food is ready.

In the ending cutscene for Potluck Gang, after the group has finished playing cards, [[Dribble]] suggests inviting [[Wario]] the next time they meet at Peridot Campgrounds, causing [[Ashley]] to blush. When 5-Volt says that the group will meet up again tomorrow, the group cheers.
