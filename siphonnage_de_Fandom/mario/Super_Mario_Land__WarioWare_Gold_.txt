'''Super Mario Land''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is part of [[5-Volt]]'s [[Nintendo Classics]] stage.

==Gameplay==
The microgame features a scenario from ''[[Super Mario Land]]'', where [[Superball Mario]] has to throw a [[superball]] at an enemy to defeat it. This can be attempted more than once, as long as Superball Mario defeats the enemy before [[Time limit|time runs out]].

Every difficulty level has a different possible enemy, becoming increasingly faster with each increased difficulty level. In Level 1, there is a [[Piranha Plant]], which emerges from a [[Warp Pipe|Pipe]] and does not move, allowing for more precise aiming. Level 2 has either a [[Goombo]] or a [[Bombshell Koopa]], although both of them move slowly. Level 3 has a [[Tokotoko]], which moves fast.
