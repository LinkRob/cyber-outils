'''World 7''' is the seventh world in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' It takes place in the sky, and is made up entirely of clouds, though some levels take place in overworld or mushrooms. To get to this world, the player must defeat [[Petey Piranha]] as [[Mini Mario]], or by taking the [[Warp Cannon|cannon]] in [[World 4 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 4]]. The boss is [[Lakithunder]], a [[Lakitu]] riding a thunder cloud. It has the most exits out of any world in the game, containing a total of fifteen exits. This is also the only world where the player needs to find at least one alternate exit to finish the world.
