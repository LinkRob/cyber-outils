'''Chuckola Bounce''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==Description==
Chuckola Bounce is played in the [[Winkle Colosseum]] in [[Chucklehuck Woods]] by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. The arena is a round room with [[Chuckorok]]s around the edge. It costs 500 [[coin]]s to play Chuckola Bounce.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
Mario and Luigi control either side of a Chuckola Board which is used to bounce the Chuckola Rocks that the Chuckoroks shoot at the player back at them. If a brother is hit by the Chuckola Rocks, they will spin around for a few seconds and they will lose time. When the snail resembling [[Winkle]] at the bottom left corner reaches the "Time's Up" sign at the bottom right corner, that game is over. Each Chuckorok hit is worth one point. At the end, the player's tally is added up and the total is their score. Occasionally, the Chuckoroks will spit out a Barrier Mushroom. If a brother touches this mushroom, he will be able to touch a Chuckola Rock and hit it back at a Chuckorok without spinning out. As Chuckoroks are hit, they become increasingly smaller, and therefore harder to hit.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', the game now has a time limit of sixty seconds (one minute), and the controls are different.
