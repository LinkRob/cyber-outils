'''Punch''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land II]]'' and a member of the [[Black Sugar Gang]]. They and [[Pirate Goom]]s are two of the enemies most commonly encountered by [[Wario]].

They wear a football helmets on their head for protection and red boxing gloves that they use to try and Wario, hence their name. If Wario is punched, he loses some [[coin]]s. Wario's [[Dash Attack]] can only defeat a Punch if it is upside-down.
