'''Love Me Not''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It can be obtained by completing [[Hearts A-Flutter]], a quest similarly related to love.

==Gameplay==
Love Me Not is a digitized petal-plucking [[minigame]] where players must pick petals from a flower to figure out whether their love interest loves them back. A petal can be plucked by pressing {{button|gba|A}}, usually once but occasionally twice, possibly to make the end result less predictable. Once every petal has been plucked, a result is shown stating whether the supposed person loves the player or not.
