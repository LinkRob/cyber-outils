'''Frosty''' is one of the [[snow spirits]] in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''. He partners with [[Mario]], [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] to save [[Winter Island]].

== In {{series|Mario & Sonic}}==
=== ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' ===
Frosty is the snow spirit of [[Frostown]], and he the first snow spirit whom [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]] encounter. After [[Bowser]] and [[Dr. Eggman]] captured the snow spirits, Frosty managed to escape and ask Mario and Sonic for their help to rescue the other snow spirits. Frosty is around with the whole cast, until Bowser and Dr. Eggman quickly capture him in a cage after Mario and Sonic free [[Blizza]]. In [[Blizland]], Frosty's cage is guarded by [[Dry Bowser]]. While Mario and Sonic challenge Dry Bowser in [[Curling Bowling]], Blizza tries to free Frosty. After Dry Bowser loses, Frosty is freed.
