The '''Propellion''' is an enemy from [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team|''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'']]. They can be found in the [[Dream World]] version of [[Mushrise Park]], aptly called "[[Dreamy Mushrise Park]]."

== Description ==
Propellions are pink dragon-fly shaped monsters. Their heads are circular and pink with bulbous eyes resembling those possessed by the [[Dream Stone's spirit]] and greyish-white spikes for noses. Their torso has yellow wings that spin around it to keep them aloft. Their lower body is pink with yellow stripes, ending in a "spike" that points downward.

== Behaviour ==
In the field, Propellions move at high altitudes, and will descend upon intruders into their territory, becoming vulnerable to a [[First Strike]]. If they miss, they may fly back up.

== Combat ==
In combat, Propellions cannot be struck by ground-based weaponry (the standard [[Hammer]], the [[3D Red Shell]], the [[3D Green Shell]], the shockwave of the [[Dropchopper]], and the [[Jet-Board Bash]]) [[Flying (Enemy type)|due to their flight abilities]]. The enemy has two attacks.

* For one attack, the Propellion will fly high and point its pointed bottom at the target, then perform a stomp attack at an angle. Since it descends at a diagonal angle, it can be dodged by jumping. Occasionally, it will fly over the target, signalled by descending at a more horizontal angle than usual. A Propellion that is performing this move can be stomped on if the target jumps with proper timing.
* The second attack involves it twirling across the ground towards the target, who must whack it with a hammer strike to avoid damage.

The Propellion is a Level 8 monster that has 42 [[Heart Point|hit points]], an [[Power|attack power]] of 56, a [[defense]] of 46, and a [[Speed (Initiative)|speed]] of 22, giving 8 [[Experience Point|experience]], 8 [[Coin|coins]], and possibly a standard [[Star Candy]] (10% of drops) upon death.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Name ===
Propellions derive their name from the word "propeller," referencing how the creatures' wings spin around their bodies, not unlike a helicpoter's propeller.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Propellions are called ''kaitenchū'' (written カイテンチュー), their name literally translating to "spin insect." Their Spanish and French names combine the respective translations of "spin" (referencing how their wings spin around their torsos) and "dragonfly" (referencing their design resembling one), with the former calling them ''Girálula'' (''girar'' ("to spin") + ''libélula'' ("dragonfly")), and the latter calling them ''Girollule'' (''girer'' ("to spin" and ''libellule'' ("dragonfly")). Portuguese refers to them as ''Helibelinha'', from ''hélice'' ("propeller") and ''libelinha'' ("dragonfly"). In Italian, the creature is called ''Zanzélica'', from ''zanzara'' (a translation of "mosquito") and ''élica'' (a translation of "propeller"). German refers to them as ''Propellos'', from ''Propeller'' ("propeller"). Their Russian name, ''Flyugoza'' (written Флюгоза), comes from ''flyuger'' ("windvane," perhaps referencing how their wings spin around their torsos; written флюгер) and ''strekoza'' ("dragonfly," which their design comes from; written стрекоза).

== Trivia ==

* Propellions are similar in their overworld behaviour to the [[Skipdrill]]; both species hover at high altitudes, coming down on the Bros. when they sense the latter nearby, and rising back up if they fail to strike, yet wandering aimlessly in all other scenarios. However, Propellions take less time to rise back up, since they do not get stuck in the ground.
* Propellions are the only enemies native to [[Mushrise Park]] that cannot be hit with ground-based weaponry at all. [[Antasma]] does not count as he is not native to the place, and neither do [[Fly Guy]] Rs since they are technically minions of [[Bowser]], and therefore intruders. [[Flibbee|Flibbees]] fall to the ground if struck in mid-air, and all other enemies are purely on the ground.
