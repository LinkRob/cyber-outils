'''Flip Out!''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', as one of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s microgames, and returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' as one of [[Wario Deluxe]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of a chef, who has to flip a pancake. To accomplish this, the player has to press {{button|gba|A}} to flip the pancake then tilt the system to move the chef and hold out the pan to where the pancake will land. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame, but if the pancake lands on the floor, they lose at the microgame.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', [[Wario]] is the chef, and the microgame was designed to use the {{button|3ds|pad}} rather than the gyro controls.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' Flipping the pancake launches it upward to eventually make its way down.
*'''Level 2:''' Flipping the pancake causes it to briefly float in the air before falling.
*'''Level 3:''' Flipping the pancake causes it to fly high into the air before falling.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the differences between the second and third difficulty levels were swapped.
