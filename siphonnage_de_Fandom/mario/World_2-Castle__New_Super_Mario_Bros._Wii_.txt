'''World 2-Castle''' is the final level of [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]].''

==Overview==
The level is unlocked after the player completes [[World 2-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2-4]], [[World 2-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2-5]], or [[World 2-6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 2-6]].

==Walkthrough==
This is an autoscrolling castle level filled with [[lava]] pits, and three-way corridors, of which only one is the correct path, much like in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. If the correct path is taken, a chime indicating so sounds and you will be able to go to the next part; if it is incorrect, a buzzer sounds and you will have to repeat the same area all over again, this process repeats until you eventually go down the correct path. In order to complete that area quickly, the player needs to take the upper route, the lower route, and the center route in that exact order. [[Podoboo]]s are also common in this castle, especially in the first section. If Mario or any of the other players stay too long between a wall and the edge of the screen, the autoscrolling level squishes them, instantly killing them and causing them to lose a life. There is also no [[Checkpoint Flag]] so be sure not to die as you progress through the Castle.

Roy Koopa is fought here for the second time. He attacks by popping out of giant pipes that [[Kamek]] produces with his magic before the start of the battle. When Roy jumps out of a pipe, he causes a tremor upon landing, which temporarily stuns any players on the ground so be careful. Mario also needs to avoid getting squished by Roy. There are two small gaps on the sides of the Pipes so you can continuously [[Wall Jump]] in between them to remain in the air as Roy shuffles through the Pipes, you can also lure him into hopping out from the far side this way and go for the stomp each time he lands. 

Roy is defeated after getting hit three times. Upon his defeat, the player gets the key to unlock [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 3]].

==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating above Mario and co. at the very start of the level. There are two ways to obtain it. Mario and co. can get on top of the nearby [[? Block]] and jump from it to the Star Coin, or take the middle path to be led to it instead (the former is quicker, the latter is easier).
*'''Star Coin 2''': At least a [[Super Mushroom]] is required for this one. To the right of the red [[Warp Pipe]]-emerging [[Piranha Plant]] is the second Star Coin floating high up between two walls, and it is blocked off by many [[Brick Block]]s. Mario and co. should destroy enough Brick Blocks to jump up or wall kick to the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The third Star Coin floats just over the [[lava]] in the area with several [[coin]]s, Brick Blocks, and [[Dry Bones]]. Mario and co. should activate the nearby [[P Switch]], and quickly grab the Star Coin over the new platform of Brick Blocks before the P Switch's effects wear off.
