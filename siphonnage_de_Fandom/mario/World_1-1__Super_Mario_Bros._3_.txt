'''World 1-1''' is the first level of [[Grass Land]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and its [[Reissue|remakes]] ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. The player is given a time limit of 300 seconds to complete it.

== Layout ==
This is a nature-themed level that features several bushes, platforms, [[coin]]s, and [[? Block]]s. The player will encounter common enemies such as [[Goomba]], [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, and more. This level introduces the [[Super Leaf]] power-up, which allows Mario or Luigi to transform into their raccoon counterparts.

The player begins near some [[? Block]]s and a single Goomba approaching them. Further up is a [[pipe]] containing a [[Venus Fire Trap]], or a normal [[Piranha Plant]] in the GBA remake. Ahead are several platforms where some [[Koopa Troopa]]s and some more Goombas are found. Ahead is a ? Block containing a power-up. If the player is already in super form, the item will emerge as a Super Leaf. With it, the player, after defeating the Goombas in their path, can get a running start and fly up towards a secret cloud area, where they can collect coins and be lead to a pipe leading to another area, where they can gather more coins, which is placed to form the number 3, most likely serving as a reference to the game.

On the regular path, below the cloud area, are some more platforms featuring a few [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. Ahead is an area where the player can activate a P-Switch to temporarily turn a stack of Brick Blocks into coins. Just up ahead is the pipe (which leads out of the secret area), followed by the darkness up ahead, where the goal is.
