'''Rainbow Mario''' is a special form of [[Mario]] that first appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. The powers of this form can be granted by obtaining a [[Rainbow Star]], it makes [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] invincible similar to the effects of a [[Starman]] but the effects of a Rainbow Star are insurmountable compared to those of a Starman. In addition to being invincible to all enemies and enemy attacks, the heroic plumber is also faster and leaves a rainbow trail behind, additionally, his jumps are also higher and longer. If Mario doesn't stop running while in this form, he'll get [[Star Bit]]s (but obviously, if he doesn't run or runs in small bursts, he won't get any star bits at all). All enemies killed while in this form will turn into masses of [[Star Bit]]s as well, while in this form, [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] can also break open objects that would normally require [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounds]] or [[Star Spin]]s (such as [[Crate]]s and [[Item Crystal]]s) just by simply running into them. Unfortunately, Mario/Luigi cannot combine this form with any other power-up; if Mario touches a power-up (such as a [[Fire Flower]]) while under the effects of a Rainbow Star, the other item will be kicked away off the screen. Most enemies killed by the Plumbers while in this form have unique animations upon leaving behind numerous [[Star Bit]]s in their wake. The music as well as Mario himself will increase in speed while in use as time goes by, making Mario get harder and harder to control until the effects run out. 

In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', some [[Teleporter (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|Teleporter]] Planets early on in the game have [[Rainbow Star]]s which allow the plumbers to easily exterminate each enemy from the triple [[1-Up Mushroom]] prize, additionally, some [[Romp Comet]] involve the plumbers having to kill all enemies in the area under the effects of a [[Rainbow Star]] within a time limit to get the [[Power Star]].

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', as Rainbow Mario, [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]]'s entire body changes color and leaves behind a rainbow trail in his wake. However in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', Mario/Luigi's entire body is just a glittery red but he still leaves a rainbow trail as he runs.
*The artwork for Rainbow Mario's artwork is similar to [[Invincible Mario]]'s artwork; Mario's poses in both artworks are identical, though he faces in different direction in each artwork.
*Rainbow Mario's theme is re-orchestration of the same theme of [[Wing Mario]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' as well as the [[Invincible Mario|invincibility]] theme from ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' which in turn originated from the [[Superstar Baby Mario]] theme in the game ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. 
*Due to the traits of invulnerability, changing colors, increased speed, and leaving after-images, this form is similar to [[w:c:sonic:Hyper Sonic|Hyper Sonic]] of the ''[[w:c:sonic:Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' series.
