A '''Flutter Orb''' (originally called a '''Wiggler Capsule''') is an [[Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 5]]'', ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', and ''[[Mario Party 7]]''. 

==History==
===''Mario Party 5''===
In ''Mario Party 5'', a Wiggler Capsule can be thrown onto a [[space]] on the [[board]], or used on the player for 20 [[coin]]s. Its effect is that of the [[Genie Lamp|Magic Lamp]], where [[Wiggler]] appears on a flower, asking if the player wants him to take them to the [[Star Exchange|Star Space]]. If the player says "Yes," Wiggler turns into a [[Flutter]], and he flies them to the Star Space.

===''Mario Party 6'' / ''Mario Party 7''===
In ''Mario Party 6'' and ''Mario Party 7'', players can use a Flutter Orb only on themselves. It costs 30 coins at an [[Orb hut]]. The effect remains the same. In ''Mario Party 6'', a Flutter Orb can be bought only on [[Towering Treetop]] and in [[E. Gadd's Garage]]. In ''Mario Party 7'', it can be bought only on [[Grand Canal]] and [[Bowser's Enchanted Inferno!]] This is most likely due to the fact that those boards are the only ones where the Star moves after each purchase.
