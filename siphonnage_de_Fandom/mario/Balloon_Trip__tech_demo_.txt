'''''Balloon Trip''''' is a [[tech demo]] for the [[Nintendo DS]] that was showcased at [[E3 2004]]. It is essentially a prototype of ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]'' as it features [[Baby Mario]] slowly traveling downward while having a balloon attached to him.
