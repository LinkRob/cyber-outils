'''Crisp Climb Castle''' is an island located on [[Lake Lapcat]] in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Crisp Climb Castle is one of the few islands surrounding the Ruins [[Giga Bell]]. It is a large castle-like structure reminiscent of a windmill, and at the top is a pool of water shaped like a cat's head and the [[Lighthouse (Bowser's Fury)|lighthouse]], the top of which is the highest point in Lake Lapcat.

[[? Block]]s in this area have snow on them that can be melted by throwing [[fireball]]s using the [[Fire Flower]].
