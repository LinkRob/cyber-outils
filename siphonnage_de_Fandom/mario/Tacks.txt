'''Tacks''' are an item which first appear in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]'' and return in both ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
Tacks are golden nails, and if a driver uses them, they scatter the Tacks behind their [[kart]]. If opponents drive over the Tacks, it can pop their tires, making it harder for them to control their [[Kart]].
