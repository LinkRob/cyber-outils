==History==
Muffy is a teenage [[Flip-Flopian]] that lives in [[Flipside]]. She is very optimistic girl that believes life should be short and sweet. After the defeat of [[Super Dimentio]], Muffy will mention that she spent all of her coins at the [[Flipside Arcade]] as she thought the world was ending.
==Trivia==
*Muffy's counterpart of [[Flopside]] is [[Lucy]]. Oddly, Muffy's name is Lucy in the PAL region while Lucy is called "Lacy."
