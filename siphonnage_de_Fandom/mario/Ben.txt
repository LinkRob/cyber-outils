'''Ben''' is a character in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
He is a humanoid dog with light brown fur and a white fur pattern on his face.

===Behavior===
Ben is a dog who has the job of being a reporter, like with [[Ken the Reporter]], [[Rocky the Reporter]], and [[Reporter John]].

==In ''WarioWare Gold''==
Ben is first seen reporting on ''[[Super Pyoro]]'', a newly released game starring [[Pyoro]]. He mentions that the game is selling well, inspiring [[Wario]] to make games for money. This scene directly parallels the opening of ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', where Wario decided to start his own company, [[WarioWare, Inc.]], upon seeing how well the ''Pyoro'' game sold.

Ben is also featured in one of the cards in [[Wario Kard]].
