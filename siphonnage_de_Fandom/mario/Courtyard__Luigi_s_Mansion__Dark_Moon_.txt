|first = ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''}}The '''Courtyard''' is an area in [[Haunted Towers]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is accessed from the [[Haunted Towers Entrance]] and leads to the [[Tower Lobby]] due west, the [[Hollow Tree]] due north, and the [[Hydro Generator]] due east. The Courtyard is also the most visited area in Haunted Towers.
==History==
During [[Haunted Towers#B-1: A Job for a Plumber|B-1: A Job for a Plumber]], Luigi must head into the Courtyard to gain access to the Hydro Generator.

During [[Haunted Towers#B-2: The Pinwheel Gate|B-2: The Pinwheel Gate]], Luigi can check an area to capture a [[Gold Greenie]].
