==Trivia==
*The victory poses in this game are the same ones in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.
*Wario's 2nd place animation is based on his 2nd place animation in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', with the difference here being that he has his right arm near his body while in Mario Party 9 he has his right arm in front of his face.
*The beginning of Wario's 3rd place animation is based on Toad's 4th place pose in ''Mario Party 9''.
*Wario's 4th place animation here has his eyes open and his eyebrows normal but his pupils missing, while in ''Star Rush'', his eyes are closed and his eyebrows are facing downwards.
*The beginning of Waluigi's 3rd place animation is based on his 3rd place animation in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. It also reuses Waluigi's 4th place animation's voice clip in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.
*Rosalina is the only character to not be sitting or lying down on the ground in 4th place.
