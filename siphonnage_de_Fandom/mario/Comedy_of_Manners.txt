'''Comedy of Manners''' is a [[Microgame]] coming from ''[[WarioWare: Move It!]]''. It uses the Big Cheese form for its gameplay.

== Layout ==
A lady wearing a pink dress controlled by the player stands close to the camera with her back to it, and another lady stands farther away. The two ladies are facing each other. On the second level, a third lady appears and will come closer once the other lady is greeted.

== Gameplay ==
The player must lift their Joy-Cons so their lady can lift the hem of her dress once the other lady lifts her dress's hem. If the player lifts too early, the lady will point at the hem of her dress as a signal that the player should lower their Joy-Cons. When the curtsy is properly returned, both smile, and the player's lady looks toward the camera. If the player's lady takes too long, the other lady becomes enraged and the player loses.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
*'''Level 1 (Easy)''': Only one lady needs to be greeted.
*'''Level 2 (Hard)''': Two ladies need to be greeted.
*'''Level 3 (Expert)''': Same as Level 2, but one lady fumbles the curtsy, resulting in a fake-out.
