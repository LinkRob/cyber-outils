'''Globe Gunners''' is a battle minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on a globe of [[Earth]]. Some real landmarks appear on the globe, including the {{w|Ayers Rock}}, an {{w|Easter Island}} {{w|Moai|statue}}, {{w|Egyptian pyramids}}, {{w|Las Vegas}}, {{w|Mount Fuji}}, and the [[Arc de Triomphe]]. The top screen shows a full map of Earth and an icon of each character's current location on the globe corresponds to the map.

==Gameplay==
The characters move around on a world globe, and each of them carry their own popgun-like rifle. The object for each character is to shoot the most blasts at their opponents within the 30-second [[Time Limit]]. Whoever shot their opponents the most is the winner, but if none of the characters score any [[point]]s, the minigame ends as a tie.

==Controls==
*{{button|ds|Pad}}: Move
*{{button|ds|A}}: Shoot
