A '''Glad Ptooie''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Glad Ptooies are a variant of [[Ptooie]]s imbued with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe, and Glad Ptooies resemble Ptooies, except they have a yellow head.

===Behavior===
Glad Ptooies are like regular Ptooies in that they continuously blow a [[Spiked Ball|ball]] above them. Glad Ptooies have a few added behaviors, namely how after tossing its ball up a few times, a Glad Ptooie tosses it another direction, where it goes to continue blowing it upward. If a Glad Ptooie does not have its ball, it tries to attack Peach by charging at her. If a Glad Ptooie hits a wall, it turns around, and if Peach is still nearby, it performs another attack. Otherwise, the Ptooie happily still moves back and forth.

Like with [[Glad Piranha Plant]]s, a Glad Ptooie's [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]] entry shows it performing a happy dance.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Glad Ptooies are first found in [[Hoo's Wood 2-4]], and make semi-recurring appearances throughout the following [[level]]s.

==Profiles==
*'''Glossary:''' ''Does a happy dance and throws balls in the air.''

==Trivia==
*Glad Ptooies are ironically encountered before the regular Ptooies, not counting the latter type's appearance in the [[Toad Jump]] minigame.
