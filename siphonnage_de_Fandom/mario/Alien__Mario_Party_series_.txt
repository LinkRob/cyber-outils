'''Aliens''' are non-playable characters in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Mario Party 5'' ===
Aliens make a brief appearance in [[ID UFO]], when the team wins.

=== ''Mario Party 6'' ===
[[File:MP6 Alien.png|left|thumb|214x214px|An Alien in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.]]
They appear on the board, [[Castaway Bay]] at night, but are absent during day.

=== ''Mario Party 7'' ===
Aliens appear on the board, [[Neon Heights]]. When the player lands on the [[Happening Space]], the Alien in the star-shaped windows UFO will swap the [[Treasure chest|Treasure chests]] in different places.
