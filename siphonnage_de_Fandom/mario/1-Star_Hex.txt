The '''1-Star Hex''' is a type of [[Hex]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Description==
A player with a 1-Star Hex can choose to place it on any [[Blue Space]] or [[Red Space]] within five spaces from their current location. When a player lands on it, a [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]] is distributed and the hex is destroyed. If opponents land on it, the sides of the hex will fold together on them, and a star will be taken from them and given to the player or team who placed it. If there are no stars to be taken, the hex will not have any special effect and will just be destroyed. If the "owner" of the space lands on it, they will receive five coins. This hex can be received in the [[Hex Area]] or stolen with a [[Snag Bag]].

In ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'', there is an item called the [[Star Steal Trap]] that acts like the 1-Star Hex.
