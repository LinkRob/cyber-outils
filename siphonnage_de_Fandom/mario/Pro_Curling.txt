'''Pro Curling''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', included as part of [[Jimmy T]]'s [[Sports]] folder.

==Layout==
A top-down view of an ice field with a circle marked on it is shown.

==Gameplay==
As a  man comes from the right with a curling brush, followed by a stone, the player must press {{button|gba|A}} repeatedly for the stone to slide across the ice and aim to rest it inside the circle. If done successfully, the player wins at the microgame. If the stone either stops before the circle or moves past it, the player loses. As the difficulty increases, the circle gets smaller, requiring more precise aiming to hit.

Each increased difficulty level makes the circle shorter, making it harder to meet the objective.
