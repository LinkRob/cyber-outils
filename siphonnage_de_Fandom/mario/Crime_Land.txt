'''Crime Land''' is a location in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[The Unzappables]]."

==Description==
Crime Land is a large city infamous for its crime rate, hence its name. It is based on 1920s Chicago, Illinois. Some of its known locations include Mario's Pasta Palace (formerly named [[Koopa Klub]]), a branch of the [[First Mushroom Bank]], and the [[Gangster Supply Store]] are located there.
