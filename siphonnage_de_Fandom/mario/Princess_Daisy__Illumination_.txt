'''Princess Daisy''' is a post credit character who appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''. She lands a knockout punch to a [[Ukiki (Illumination)|Ukiki]] and returns the stolen bag belonging to a female [[Whittle (Illumination)|Whittle]], who later thanks her.

==Trivia==
*Princess Daisy is:
**The second character (but the first to be shown) to be teased in a post-credits scene, after [[Yoshi (Illumination)|Yoshi]] in The ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie|Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.
**The second female character from the [[Mario (series)|video games]] to be introduced in the [[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|movie franchise]], following [[Rosalina (Illumination)|Rosalina]].
**The second character to appear in a movie media, first being the 1993 [[Super Mario Bros. (1993 film)|''Super Mario Bros.'']] live action film.
