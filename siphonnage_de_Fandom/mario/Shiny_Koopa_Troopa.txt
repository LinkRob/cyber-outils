'''Shiny Koopa Troopas''' are a rare species of [[Koopa Troopa]]s found in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. They have more health and deal more damage to Mario. Shiny Koopa Troopas exist in both green and red varieties, although the latter can only be fought if a [[Shiny Paratroopa]] has lost its wings.

Shiny Koopa Troopas also appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'', with the name '''Shiny Paper Koopa Troopa''', acting identically as in their debut appearance.
