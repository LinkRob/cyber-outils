==Description==
Mushroom Way is a grassy field with a few areas to explore, inhabited mainly by [[Goomba]]s and [[Koopa]]s.

==History==
After being summoned by [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Mario]] heads toward the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] on his way to meet the [[Chancellor]]. Mario must fight through Mushroom Way in order to reach the Kingdom. Along the way, Mario must save Toad as he is harassed by Goombas and Koopas on multiple occasion. Eventually, Mario comes across two [[Hammer Bro.|Hammer Bros.]] blocking his way. Mario defeats the duo, earns the [[Hammer]], his first equipable weapon, and heads away to the Mushroom Kingdom.
