'''Valley of Bowser 1''' is a [[level]] in ''[[Super Mario World]]'', more specifically the first level of [[Valley of Bowser]]. It consists of a maze-like structure and features enemies such as [[Chargin' Chuck|Lookout Chuck]]s, [[Mega Mole]]s, and [[Muncher]]s.
