'''Ostriches''' are recurring real-world in the [[Mario (franchise)|Mario]] and [[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]] franchises. They are tall birds with long necks and differently colored feathers. 

== In the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise ==
In the ''Mario'' franchise, ostriches are walking enemies that can damage the player. 

=== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' ===
In ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', there is an enemy called [[Ostro]], that works as a moving platform. Occasionally [[Shy Guy|Shy Guys]] will ride them. 

=== In ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' ===
In ''[[Mario Kart World]]'', ostriches can run through the track and block the path in the savannah region.

== In the ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' franchise ==
In the ''Donkey Kong'' franchise, ostriches have the ability to fly, despite not being able to do so in real life. [[Pauline]] notes this in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. 

=== In the ''[[Donkey Kong Country (series)|Donkey Kong Country]]'' series ===
The ostriches found on [[Donkey Kong Island|DK Island]], such as [[Expresso the Ostrich]], are ridable [[:Category:Animal Buddies|animal buddies]]. When riding them, you can fly into the air. 

=== In ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]'' ===
The ostriches found in the Forest Layer are sentient, speaking ostriches that usually work at the [[Eggshell Hotel]]. The [[Ostrich Elder]] is a large flightless ostrich with headphones and bananas for feathers. She can grant the player the [[Ostrich Bananza]], which allows [[Donkey Kong|DK]] to fly, glide, and shoot eggs.
