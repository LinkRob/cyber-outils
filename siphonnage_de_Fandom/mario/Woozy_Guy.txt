'''Woozy Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] which only appear in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platforming games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
In several of their appearances, Woozy Guys have the exact appearance of Shy Guys, and wear either a red, yellow, green, or purple cloak. In ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', Woozy Guys with purple cloaks do not appear in favor of ones wearing a dark blue cloak. In ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'', all Woozy Guys wear a purple cloak to distinguish them from Shy Guys.

===Behavior===
Woozy Guys are set apart from Shy Guys in that they move around by performing forward flips, not from walking. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can defeat a Woozy Guy using the same methods as for a regular Shy Guy, such as by eating a Woozy Guy to convert it into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]].
