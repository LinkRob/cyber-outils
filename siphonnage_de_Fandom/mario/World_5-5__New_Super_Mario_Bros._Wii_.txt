'''World 5-5''' is a level found in [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]] in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. 

==Layout==
In this autoscrolling sky level, the player first jumps on one of the backs of a giant green [[Jumbo Ray]]. They have platforms to help them just in case they lose a [[Jumbo Ray]]. There are also [[Propeller Block]]s that help them. There are a lot of [[Bullet Bill]]s in the level that are fired at the player from off-screen. After halfway, the player continues jumping on Jumbo Rays and avoiding Bullet Bills. Near the end, they can travel through a [[Warp Pipe]] that takes them to the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]], where the level ends.

==Aids==

*[[Jumbo Ray]]s - 22

==Enemies==

*[[Bullet Bill]]s - 14

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating high up in the area where the [[Bullet Bill]]s start to appear. Mario and co. should fall to it, then make it to safety either with the nearby [[Propeller Block]], or by jumping off a Bullet Bill.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is also floating in the area where the Bullet Bills begin to appear, albeit after the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]]. It can be obtained the same way as the first one, although a [[Propeller Suit]] is an additional option.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Near the end of the level, there are several [[coin]]s in the shape of a "W", near the final [[POW Block]]. Mario and co. should wait for a [[Jumbo Ray]] to fly under the coins, then activate the POW Block so that the third Star Coin will fall onto the Jumbo Ray and become obtainable.
