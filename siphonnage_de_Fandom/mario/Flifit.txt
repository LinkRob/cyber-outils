'''Flifits''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

== Description ==
Flifits are flowers with [[Snifit]] faces. They have yellow petals and a green stem with a leaf for a body.

== In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''/''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey'' ==
Flifit is fought by both [[Bowser]] and the Bros. In the field, they tend to take root, but chase after the heroes if approached. In combat, Flifits attack by spitting out [[Sneed]]s at the player, and the angle their shots go is determined by whether their head tilts back or not, with a backwards tilt implying the Sneed will be shot straight, and not tilting back involves them shooting their projectile into the ground, with the Sneed bouncing into the targeted character. If fought by the Bros., they indicate the target by pointing with the leaf. If they shoot the Sneed straight, the Sneed must be punched by Bowser, or jumped over by the bros. If they shoot the Sneed down, it will bounce up, and must be countered by ducking, if playing as Bowser, or using a [[hammer]], if playing as the bros.

Bowser can suck up the Flifit's petals using his vacuum attack, resulting in the Flifit being immobilized. However, a [[Snifit]] with wings will water the Flifit and revive it, later. Additionally, due to their nature as plants, Flifits take increased damage from fire-based weapons.

Flifits are Level 17 with 108 hit points, an attack power of 71, a defense of 55, and a speed of 35. When slain, they give 50 experience and drop 20 coins along with a Hot Drumstick and/or a Syrup Jar.
