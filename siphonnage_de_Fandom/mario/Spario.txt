'''Spario''' is a [[Microgame]] that can be found in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. It is in the Super Zero stage and is unlocked in free-play mode when the player completes it.

== Layout ==
[[Wario]] is standing in a grey void. He is wearing a coach outfit with his hands covered by gloves (which are referred to as "bags" in the description). Above him is a gauge that shows how many times the player has to punch, with grey gloves meaning remaining required punches and red gloves meaning successful punches.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to punch Wario's gloves, which can be done by tapping on them. Winning causes Wario to laugh. The number of times the player has to punch is equal to the difficulty level + 1 (2 for level 1, 3 for level 2, and 4 for level 3).

== Rules ==
''"Punch the bags to get in shape for the title fight! You gotta want it, kid!"''
