The '''Mad Hat Mk. III''' is a boss that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. It is the third in the line of the Mad Hat robots created by [[Count Cannoli]] and is fought in [[Carpaccio's Lab]].

== Battle ==
In the fight, the Mad Hat Mk. III only attacks with punches and sliding punches, but may occasionally throw its face. In order to damage it, [[Wario]] must duck under the sliding punch attack and punch the switch on it's back with [[Genius Wario]]. The Mad Hat Mk. III's hat will then fly off and Wario as [[Thief Wario]] can jump on it in order to damage it. Wario must do this two more times and Cannoli's creation will be defeated once more.
