'''Sonny''' is a playable character who only appears in ''[[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Mario Golf]]'' for the [[Nintendo 64]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sonny wears a cowboy-like hat, a red t-shirt with a blue collar and three blue buttons, light-gray pants, and has a black belt around his waist. Sonny has black hair. Like most of the human golfers, Sonny has brown shoes and a golfer's glove on his left hand, although Sonny's glove is red.
