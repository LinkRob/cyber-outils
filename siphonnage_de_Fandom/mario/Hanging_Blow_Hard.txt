A '''Hanging Blow Hard''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Hanging Blow Hards bear a similar appearance to its parent species, [[Blow Hard]], and an even closer one to its relative [[Drool Hard]]. Hanging Blow Hards have a light green body with yellow spots. The only difference a Hanging Blow Hard has from Drool Hard is a base from which its head stems, as it enables the Hanging Blow Hard to stick to a ceiling, as well as a long, light yellow tongue that can be used in the same manner as a swinging [[vine]].

===Behavior===
[[Baby Donkey Kong|Baby DK]] can swing from the tongue of a Hanging Blow Hard. While a Hanging Blow Hard's tongue is being held on to, it releases [[Needlenose]]s that descend down to the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] and Baby DK and explode upon reaching the bottom. As such, a Hanging Blow Hard's tongue must be swung from quickly.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
A Hanging Blow Hard is first encountered in [[Up the Creek]]. Two of them can be found in the botanical-enemy exhibit of the [[Island Museum]], where the Hanging Blow Hard to the right has its tongue swing faster than the Hanging Blow Hard on the left.
