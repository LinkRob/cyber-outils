|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''G'night''' is a special move used only by [[Bowyer]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. This move can also be used by Bowyer's [[Machine Made]] forms.

==Description==
When G'night is used, a bunch of sheep appears on an area.

==Effects==
With G'night, Bowyer is able to put one of [[Mario]]'s party to [[sleep]]. This can be countered by wearing a Wake Up Pin.

==Trivia==
*G'night acts similar to [[Sleepy Sheep]]s later in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as sheep appear on the screen and may put the victim to sleep.
