'''Shell Street''' is the first world of [[Challenge Road]] appearing in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. It has thirteen challenges. Shell Street is an urban-themed world with most challenges that take place in settings with clear-blue skies. After completing the thirteen challenges, the player will move to [[Chestnut Forest]].
