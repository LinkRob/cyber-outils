'''Hop-O-Matic 4000''' is the name of a vehicle used by both teams in the ''[[Mario Party 7]]'' minigame of the [[Hop-O-Matic 4000|same name]].

==Description==
The machine has four legs with suction cup feet, and a faceplate with extensions resembling antennae. The suction cups and faceplate are colored red and blue for the respective teams of the same color.
