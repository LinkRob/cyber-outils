The '''Back Space''' is a recurring type of [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', where it is known as a '''Reverse Space'''.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party 3''===
[[File:ReverseSpace.png|frame|left]]
Reverse Spaces only appear in Duel Mode boards. If a character lands on one, they are sent backward and cannot make any direct turns. If the character going in reverse has a partner behind them and runs into another character, then a battle occurs, with the partner from behind being able to attack the other player. On [[Backtrack]], landing on a Reverse Space actually changes the direction of the entire board. Although Reverse Spaces do not appear in [[Blowhard]], they are still shown on the board results screen.

===''[[Mario Party 9]]''===
[[File:Back Space MP9.png|frame|right|''Mario Party 9'' graphic]]
If a character lands on a Back Space, they get to roll the [[Dice Block|Normal Dice Block]] again, except they instead travel backward on the board. If a Captain lands on another Back Space in direct succession, they can still roll the Dice Block an extra time. [[Special Dice Block]]s cannot to modify these rolls.

===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
Back Spaces make a reappearance, retaining their role from ''Mario Party 9'', but are exclusive to the [[Mario Party (mode)|Mario Party]] mode. A different type of [[Back Space (fixed)|Back Space]] introduced in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' also appears, and these ones always send the character back a set number of spaces.
