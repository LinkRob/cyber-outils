'''Forget Me Not''' is a minigame in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. The player can play the minigame at the [[Flipside Arcade]].


== Gameplay ==
There are thirty rounds. The player's objective is to find the matching pairs of each card without making three mistakes. The minigame is over when the player makes three strikes. However, items may appear in one of the cards. The [[Mushroom]] removes the strike from the player's total. The [[Star]] allows the player to look at the unclipped cards.

After the game ends, the player will receive the number of tokens (Points ÷ 10,000).
=== Scoring ===
*Match a Pair: 6000
*Uncover an Item: 3000
