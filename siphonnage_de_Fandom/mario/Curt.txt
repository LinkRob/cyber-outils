'''Curt''' is one of the [[human]] tennis players featured in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]]. He is partnered with [[Bob]] in doubles. Curt is also an unlockable playable character, being of the Tricky type. Like [[Fay]], Curt can be unlocked by completing Singles in Senior Champ.
