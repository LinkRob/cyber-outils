'''World 2''' is the second set of stages in ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', it takes place in a forest with some haunted qualities. [[Kamek]] and [[Count Fang]] are the bosses of this world.
==Levels==

! width="20%" |Description

|World 2-1: [[Koopa Canyon]]
|A level which takes place on a cliffside of green grass. [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[Paratroopa]]s are featured prominently in this level. It is also the first appearance of the [[Metal Eggdozer]], a [[Mega Eggdozer]] which is heavier and helps in water.
|-
|World 2-2: [[Inside the Outside]] 
| A forest stage which introduces [[Little Mouser]]s. The main feature of this stage is [[Fake Yoshi]], a [[Shy Guy]] that mirrors the true Yoshi's movements.
|-
|World 2-3: [[Seesaw Scramble]] 
|A cliffside level which features [[Seesaw]]s for the first time. [[Stake]]s are also frequently found. In the second half of the level, volcanoes in the background will begin erupting, shooting out [[Flaming Chomp]]s that destroy some platforms. 
|-
|World 2-4: [[Beware the Boo Brigade]]
|The second fort of the game. It is full of ghastly creatures like [[Boo]]s, [[Big Boo]]s, [[Grim Leecher]]s, and [[Boo Blah]]s. Kamek's boss battle requires pounding on a giant stake the same color of the one he is standing on.
|-
|World 2-5: [[Lantern Ghost Grotto]]
|A level which takes place in a cave. Yoshi's goal is to find a [[Winged Cloud]] with a [[Key]], and a locked [[Door]] to go with it. [[Ball 'n' Chain]]s, [[Fang]]s and [[Lantern Ghost]]s appear as threats in this level.
|-
|World 2-6: [[Gusty Glory]]
|A level which takes place on various platforms with bottomless pits scattered around. [[Gusty|Gusties]] are a common threat in this level, as they are capable of knocking Yoshi into one of these pits. A [[Smiley Flower]] requires the use of some Gusties like a staircase. 
|-
| World 2-7: [[Hidey-Hole Hooligans]]
|A forest level which has walls that [[Wall Lakitu]]s hide behind to pelt Yoshi with [[Spiny Egg]]s, sometimes further secluded within houses made out of fragile material.
|-
|World 2-8: [[Count Fang's Castle]]
|The second castle level in the game. It has Fangs, a sewer section which introduces the [[Support Ghost]], and ends in a boss battle against [[Count Fang]]. 
|-
|World 2-S: [[All Aboard the Terrain Train]]
|The second special level in the game. This stage features a moving platform that Yoshi must stay on at all times. It plows through breakable material, the main threat being stuck in between the breakable material and terrain train.
