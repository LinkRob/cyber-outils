'''Ring Race''' is a bonus challenge in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. The player must pass through rings to gain points.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
'''Ring Race''' appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' as a bonus challenge. The player's objective is to pass through rings as many as possible to gain points. The stars ranked depends on the number of rings passed through. Even if the player does not pass through the ring, touching the ring still counts as a point. After completing three courses, the player may encounter a bonus challenge. If Ring Race is one of the bonus challenges, the game will choose the course, driver, kart and glider depending on the cup and tour.
