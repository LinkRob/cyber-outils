'''World 2-1''' is a level that appears in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. It is the first level of World 2, the [[Muda Kingdom]].

==Overview==
World 2-1 is mainly a beach-like level with some mountains in the background. The main enemies of this level are [[Honen]]s which are constantly seen jumping out of the water trying to bite [[Mario]]. There is also a [[Starman]] in this level where Mario can use it to mow through the Honens and [[Goombo]]s. This level also introduces [[Yurarin Boo]]s who are seahorse enemies that try to shoot fireballs at Mario.
