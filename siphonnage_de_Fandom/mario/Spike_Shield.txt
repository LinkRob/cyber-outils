'''Spike Shield '''is a [[badge]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. [[Mario]], while wearing this badge, is unaffected by spikes when using [[boots|jumping attacks]].

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
[[File:Spike Shield PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
Spike Shield is found in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]]. Near the very beginning of the dungeon, Mario finds a few coffins, and in the middle coffin not only has a [[Poison Pokey]] but also the Spike Shield. To wear a Spike Shield, Mario must use two [[Badge Point|BP]].

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
Spike Shield now costs 3 BP to use, one more than before. Given that some enemies with spikes are newly introduced, the Spike Shield can be considered more useful than in ''Paper Mario''. Spike Shield is found in [[Rogueport Sewers]], where it requires [[Yoshi (partner)|Yoshi]] or [[Vivian]] to obtain.
