The '''Dyllis Dynamite''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is made by mixing an [[Egg Bomb]] with a [[Dyllis Breakfast]]. Using this item hurts all enemies. This item is a replacement of the [[Zess Dynamite]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
