'''Pokipede Pass''' is a course of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
The level features many [[Pokipede]]s. Pokipedes travel on [[track]]s while snow tiles are all over the level.

Later in the level, there is a [[Wonder Flower]] blocked off by [[Hard Block]]s. Waiting for a few seconds will bring a pokipede that will break the hard blocks, which will make the wonder flower accessible. The [[Wonder Effect]] will cause a snowstorm and more and more snow tiles will cover the level.

==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s are obtainable in the following order:
#At the first [[? Block]] with an item, [[jump]]ing to the platforms to the left will lead the player to a 10-flower coin.
#Shortly after the first 10-flower coin, there is a wall made out of snow. Behind the wall is a whohoo and a 10-flower coin, the whohoo will destroy the wall and allow access to the 10-flower coin.
#During the wonder effect, a pokipede will destroy snow allowing access to a 10-flower coin.
