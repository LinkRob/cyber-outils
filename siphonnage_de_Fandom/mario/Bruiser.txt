The '''Bruiser''', known as the '''Growlster''' in the British English localizations of ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', is a [[kart]] appearing in the {{series|Mario Kart}}.

== Description ==
The Bruiser has a black body. There are spikes on the front sides. On the front center of the kart is a monster-looking face, with the eyes serving as headlights. There are four, light yellow-gold exhaust pipes. The character's emblem is located on the hood.

== ''Mario Kart'' series ==
=== In ''Mario Kart 7'' ===
[[File:MK7 Bruiser.png|thumb|185px|right|The Bruiser as it appears in-game.]]
The Bruiser makes its debut in the ''Mario Kart'' series, in the [[Nintendo 3DS]] title, ''Mario Kart 7''. The Kart can be unlocked by collecting a certain amount of coins.

The Bruiser gives a boost to [[speed]] and [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|weight]], but hinders [[acceleration]] and [[handling]]. The Bruiser is tied with the [[B Dasher]], [[Zucchini]] and [[Blue Seven]] for providing the highest speed boost. The Bruiser has a slightly higher acceleration boost than the B Dasher and Blue Seven, however.

The Bruiser is considered to be [[Wario]]'s personal kart in the game, as while controlled by a computer racer, he may often use this kart body. He is even seen using the Bruiser in promotional screenshots of the game. A computer-controlled [[Shy Guy]] may also use the Bruiser frequently.

Wario uses the Bruiser with [[Slim (tires)|Slim]] tires and [[Beast Glider]] for the Expert Staff Ghosts in courses [[Wario Shipyard]] and {{Classic course|DS|[[Waluigi Pinball]]}}.

=== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ===
[[File:MKT Pirate Tour Racers.png|thumb|185px|right|[[King Boo|King Boo (''Luigi's Mansion'')]] driving the Bruiser, racing with [[Dry Bowser]], [[Pauline]], [[Dry Bones]] and [[Bowser Jr.|Bowser Jr. (Pirate)]] during the [[Pirate Tour]].]]
The Bruiser returns in ''Mario Kart Tour'', after nearly eight years of absence. The Bruiser is equipped with Slim tires in the game.

== Crossover appearances ==
=== In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' ===
[[File:BruiserTrophy3DS.png|thumb|right]]
The Bruiser makes an appearance in ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS'' as a trophy. Wario is sat in the kart, equipped with [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires and Beast Glider.
