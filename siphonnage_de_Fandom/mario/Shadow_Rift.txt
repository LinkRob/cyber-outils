The '''Shadow Rift''' is an enemy from ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They are blobs of what appears to be shadowy antimatter or dark energy that look like space rifts.

== Description ==
Shadow Rifts are shapeless black blobs that can merge, move around, and/or split. Their bodies are framed by a white fuzzy outline.

== Behaviour ==
Shadow Rifts attack their prey via contact. Since [[Mario (character)|Mario]] cannot destroy them, he simply has to avoid the rifts. Mario will take damage if one swallows part or all of his body.

== In ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' ==
Shadow Rifts are seen in two stages. They first appear in [[Beware of the Rifts]] as the titular obstacle, and they later appear in [[The Final Test Wonder Gauntlet]].

== Trivia ==

* Rift Mario seems to be made from the same shadowy material that Shadow Rifts are made from.
