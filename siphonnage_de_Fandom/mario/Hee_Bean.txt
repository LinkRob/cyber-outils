'''Hee Beans''' are a yellow variation of [[bean]]s featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are used to brew [[Teehee Blend]] and [[Teeheespresso]] at the [[Starbeans Cafe]], with the former requiring only 25 Hee Beans to make whereas the latter requires 10 Hee Beans along with 15 [[Woo Bean]]s 

Hee Beans are the most rare out of all the beans, and they can only be obtained by either winning certain mini-games or rarely at [[Teehee Valley]].
