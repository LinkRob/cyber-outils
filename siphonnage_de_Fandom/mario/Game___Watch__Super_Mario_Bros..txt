'''''Game & Watch: Super Mario Bros.''''' is a full-color LED [[Game & Watch]] released in commemoration of the [[Super Mario Bros. 35th Anniversary]], and it was released internationally on November 13, 2020. It contains ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'', an alarm clock function, and a variation of ''[[Ball]]'' featuring [[Mario]] as the playable character.

''Game & Watch: Super Mario Bros.'' served somewhat as a revival of the Game & Watch line, as the most recent Game & Watch game prior to it is ''[[Mario the Juggler]]'', released in 1991. Following in the footsteps of ''Game & Watch: Super Mario Bros.'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' received its own full-color Game & Watch equivalent titled ''[[w:c:zelda:Game & Watch: The Legend of Zelda|Game & Watch: The Legend of Zelda]]''. Instead of including two games, ''Game & Watch: The Legend of Zelda'' includes three classic ''The Legend of Zelda'' games, ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda|The Legend of Zelda]]'', ''[[w:c:zelda:Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]'', and ''[[w:c:zelda:The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening]]''.
