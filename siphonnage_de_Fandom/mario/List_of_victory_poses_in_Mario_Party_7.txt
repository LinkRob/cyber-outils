{{Listnotice|Victory animations in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''}}
This applies to characters who have winning or losing animations.

== Information ==
The characters in this game now have more than one of each type of animation, whether it's winning or losing. The characters will only say their losing lines in Stick and Spin. 

In Coin and DK minigames, if the character gets coins or bananas, they strike their winning pose regardless of who has more. But if the character doesn't get any at all, they strike their losing pose.

In Donkey Kong free-for-all minigames, no matter how many bananas the players collect, Donkey Kong will always strike his winning pose.

In Bowser free-for-all minigames, if every player dies, then Bowser will strike his winning pose. But if at least one player survives, then Bowser will strike his losing pose. 

In duel minigames facing off against Donkey Kong or Bowser, they will strike their losing poses if the player wins in the minigames but strike their winning poses if they lose. If they lose in Bowser minigames, Bowser will crush the character they're using.

== Trivia ==
*This is the fourth Mario Party game in a row where everybody has the same victory animations. The differences, however, are subtle in this game, and each character has two winning animations.
*Mario's 2nd victory pose references his pose in the Mario Bros. commercial.
*Waluigi's losing pose is the only one that is shown on the back.
*Toad's winning poses don't have him opening his mouth until the end.
*Boo's losing pose references him getting scared when the player sees him in the platformers.
*Dry Bones' losing pose references his animation when the player stomps on him in the platformers.
