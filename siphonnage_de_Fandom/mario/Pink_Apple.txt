'''Pink Apple''' is an [[item]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
This item is located on the [[Overthere Stair]] in the game. The effects of the item include restoring five health points and cures a [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] status to one. It also transforms [[Princess Peach|Peach]] into [[Peachy Peach]] if she eats it, but only for a few seconds. Mixing this item with a [[Snow Bunny]] makes a [[Berry Snow Bunny]].
