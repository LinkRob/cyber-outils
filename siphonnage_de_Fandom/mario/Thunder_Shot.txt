'''Thunder Shot''' is the [[Special Shot]] used by [[Fly Guy]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Fly Guy perform the move by tapping out an uppercase "S" on the Touch Screen twice.

Fly Guy initiates Thunder Shot by dribbling the ball in the shape of an uppercase "S." The ball becomes white with electricity. Fly Guy pulls out a [[Spear Guy|spear]] and starts to spin the ball, which somehow shocks it with electricity and changes it to become entirely yellow. Lastly, Fly Guy uses the spear to throw the ball into the basket. If the ball enters the hoop, what appears to be a lightning strike hits through the basket, but only for visual presentation.

If Fly Guy performs Thunder Shot outside the three-pointer line, a teammate helps by dunking the electric ball after Fly Guy throws it with his spear.
