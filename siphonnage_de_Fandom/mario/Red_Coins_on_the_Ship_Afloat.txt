'''Red Coins on the Ship Afloat''' is the fourth mission of [[Jolly Roger Bay]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and the fifth mission in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. 

==Objective==
[[Mario]] (or [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Luigi]], or [[Wario]] in the remake) have to collect all eight [[Red Coin]]s, after which a [[Power Star]] appears above the [[Star Marker]] to be collected. Because the Power Star spawns above the Ship, the mission [[Plunder in the Sunken Ship]] has to be cleared first so that Mario can collect the Power Star.

==Locations==

* Four found inside clams throughout the level.
* One next to the crag with the [[Bob-omb Buddy]].
* One on top of the stone spire next to the same Bob-omb Buddy.
* Two on the sunken ship.
