'''Cloudy Climb''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Cloudy Climb is a small section above [[Flower Fields]]. As described by its name, Cloudy Climb is made up of many clouds that are used to climb into the sky. Mario gains access to this region only when he has obtained the [[Magical Bean]], [[Miracle Water]], and the [[Fertile Soil]] items so long as [[Sun (character)|The Sun]] is covering the skies of Flower Fields. With these items, Mario can call upon a beanstalk that will guide him into the clouds. This is also the location where [[Huff N. Puff]], the boss of Chapter 6, resides. Huff N. Puff is very formidable in battle, but after defeating him, Flower Fields is truly saved.

Cloudy Climb is also home to the exclusive [[S. Jump Charge]] badge. It is found in the upper area on a floating cloud that is accessible by climbing the near-by clouds. Cloudy Climb is riddled with yellow clouds and a puffy landscape. Clouds set up like stairs are the gateway into Huff n' Puff's domain.
