An '''orange flower''' is an enemy in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Orange flowers are flowers that have large, orange, leaf-like petals.

===Behavior===
Orange flowers hang from the ceiling, typically guarding a [[gem]], and if [[Luigi]] passes underneath an orange flower, it tries to slam down onto him. If the orange flower misses, it shrivels up temporarily.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
Orange flowers appear only in certain missions of the [[Haunted Towers]]. When an orange flower is in a shriveled up state, Luigi can defeat the orange flower by pulling it away from the ceiling using the [[Poltergust 5000]].
