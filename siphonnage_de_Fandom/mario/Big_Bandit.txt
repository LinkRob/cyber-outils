'''Big Bandits''' are enemies in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Big Bandits seem to be just larger and tougher forms of regular [[Bandit]]s. These guys are outfitted with a green cloth instead, and have slightly more attack and HP. With these new stats, they are also more skilled at thievery. Big Bandits, unlike Regular Bandits, can steal not only coins, but Items as well. Once stolen, these pesky thieves will run from combat. If they are fought within the [[Pit of 100 Trials]], [[Mario]] must fight them if one of them left.

These guys appear in the [[Glitz Pit]] regularly, and are battled in the minor league. One of the Big Bandits in the game befriends Mario and is kind of a snoop around the Glitz Pit. He searches the place, trying to solve some sort of mystery revolving around a conspiracy there.
