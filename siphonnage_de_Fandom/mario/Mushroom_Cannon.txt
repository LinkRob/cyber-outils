The '''Mushroom Cannon''' is the special item of some characters in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== Description ==
It has the typical shape of a cannon from this franchise, but it is red with white dots. It shoots [[mushroom]]s forward and the player has the possibility to use those mushrooms to gain much speed.
