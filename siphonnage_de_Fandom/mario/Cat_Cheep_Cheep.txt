'''Cat Cheep Cheeps''' are a variant of [[Cheep Cheep]] which first appear in ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
Their appearance is similar to a [[Cheep Cheep]] but they have red and white fur, white tipped ears and a red cat tail.
