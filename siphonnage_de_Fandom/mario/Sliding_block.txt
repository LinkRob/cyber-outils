The '''Sliding block''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Sliding blocks are a type of grounded [[? Block]] that slide across a surface in the manner of {{w|hockey puck}}s. Unlike most other [[block]]s, a sliding block can injure [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] upon contact. However, a sliding block can be broken with a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]], also causing it to release an item.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy''==
A sliding block exclusively appears on a planet in the [[Gold Leaf Galaxy]].
