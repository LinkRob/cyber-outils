'''Jumping Venus Fire Traps''' are a tropical variety of [[Venus Fire Trap]] and a fire-breating sub-species of the [[Jumping Piranha Plant]] appearing in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Advance 2: Super Mario World]]''. They behave similarly to a Jumping Piranha but when jumping, they spit two fireballs which go in separate directions and must be avoided. A Jumping Venus Fire Trap is the no more durable than a regular Jumping Piranha Plant and can be defeated with the effects of a [[Fire Flower]], [[Cape Feather]], or a [[Starman]]. Jumping Piranha Plants can be found in levels of [[Cookie Mountain]] and are also seen in [[Groovy]], [[Outrageous]], and [[Funky]]. Jumping Venus Fire Traps are rare enemies as only seven appear in the whole game.
