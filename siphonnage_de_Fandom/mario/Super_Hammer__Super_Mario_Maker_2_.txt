The '''Super Hammer''' is an item which only appears in ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. 

==History==
===''Super Mario Maker 2''===
Te Super Hammer is exclusive to the ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' style. It is unlocked after clearing the [[Meowser Showdown!]] level in Story Mode.

Picking a Super Hammer up transforms the character into their [[Builder Mario|Builder]] form, allowing them to use a hammer to break Hard Blocks as well as [[Thwomp]]s and any other breakable object, and place down up to five Builder Boxes at a time, which act like Crates. The hammer could also be used to defeat enemies as well.

It is worth noting that 7 hits are only necessary to defeat [[Meowser]] with [[Builder Mario]]'s hammer, compared to 20 with [[Fire Mario]]'s fireballs and [[Cat Mario]]'s scratch attack.
