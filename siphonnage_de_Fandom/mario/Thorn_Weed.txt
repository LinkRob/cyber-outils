'''Thorn Weeds''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''. They are metallic plants covered with spikes, a form that they became due to the power of the [[Wishstone]].

==History==
Thorn Weeds can only be found in [[Ancient Waterworks]] as a fairly common enemy in the area. Thorn Weeds often walk around an area until the spot [[Wario]] and attack by firing their spikes at him. Because they are made of metal and covered with spikes, Wario cannot damage them by jumping on them, Shoulder Bashing them, or even using [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers. [[Dragon Wario]]'s fire breath is the most ideal way of taking out Thorn Weeds. [[Genius Wario]]'s [[Ultra Hand]] can also be used to defeat Thorn Weeds.
