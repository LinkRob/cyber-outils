'''Wings''' are attached to various objects and creatures, allowing them to fly. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', being attached to a [[Koopa Paratroopa]]'s [[Koopa Shell|shell]].

==Description==
Wings do not always guarantee the ability to fly, especially if they are small. For instance, the [[Wing Cap]] in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' is like [[Mario's cap]] aside from having a pair of wings, allowing [[Mario]] to glide while in midair.

==In ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''==
Wings are items that act as the replacement of the [[Wing Cap]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', and touching it turns a character in to their [[Wing Mario|Wing]] form. Usually, only [[Mario]] can make use of the Wings, which appear within certain [[Cap Block|Red Blocks]] that would otherwise release a [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] for one of the other playable characters. Despite its name, the Wings have the appearance of a white feather, although they add two wings to [[Mario's Cap]] temporarily, while he is Wing Mario.

In VS mode, feathers appear within in two courses: [[Sunshine Isles]] and [[Battle Fort]]. They can be picked up by any character; for [[Luigi]] and [[Wario]], they add wings to their cap, like with Mario, but for [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], he grows wings on his back.
