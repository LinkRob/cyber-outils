'''Bikes''' are a new element in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' and are an alternate means of driving to [[Kart]]s. Although they feature the same basic attributes, there are differences. Bikes typically weigh less than karts; they are able to perform wheelies to boost one's speed for about four seconds (albeit at the cost of a reduced turning ability) but can be cancelled earlier by hopping or making a sharp turn; and Bikes are rendered incapable of performing Super Mini-Turbos, with the exception of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''. Similar to karts, Bikes are classified by weight. They're recommended for [[Time Trial]]s because they have superior turning and their ability to conduct wheelies, and also that they can do [[Aerial tricks|midair tricks]] too. The following is a list of all of the bikes in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. They appear in all weight classes.

== In Mario Kart series ==

=== ''Mario Kart DS'' ===
Despite Bikes not appear as a class of vehicles in ''Mario Kart DS'', one of [[Waluigi]]'s special [[Kart|karts]], the [[Zipper]], is a tricycle. Also, [[R.O.B.]]'s [[ROB-LGS]] has three wheels and therefore can also be considered a tricycle.
