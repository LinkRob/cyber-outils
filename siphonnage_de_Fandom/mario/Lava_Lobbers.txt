}}'''Lava Lobbers''' is a Duel minigame that appears in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Gameplay==
In Lava Lobbers, the players (seen inside a lava filled room --which is inside of a volcano) are on opposite tracks and are tasked to shoot each other down. (Each player has three health points.) The cannons in their carts can be aimed upwards or straight forward. The players must try to predict the other's move in order to get a good hit in. The player who shoots down the other three times wins. If no one shoots the other player three times within 30 seconds, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
*{{button|wii|Pad}} (Sideways) - Move
*{{button|wii|2}} - Shoot straight
*{{button|wii|1}} - Lob high
