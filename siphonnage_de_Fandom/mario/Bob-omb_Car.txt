==Description==
Bob-omb Cars are an explosive type of vehicle resembling a [[Bob-omb]]. Unlike every other type of vehicle, Bob-omb Cars can be fully destroyed for a short while, as they blow up upon colliding with a player or being hit with nearly any type of item, damaging racers in their vicinity. The only items that can hit a Bob-omb Car without detonating it are [[Banana Peel|Banana]]s and [[Fake Item Box]]es. 

In ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'', Bob-omb Cars have blank yellow eyes and a red base with blue wheels featuring gear pictures on the outsides. In ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', Bob-omb Cars had their color changed to purple, with their eyes now being white and glowing like headlights; their base has been simplified, and windup keys have been added on the backs of these cars to resemble actual Bob-ombs. ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' gives them a yellow wind-up key but otherwise reverts the Bob-omb Car back to their ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' design; this is reused in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe – Booster Course Pass]]''. Their [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']] design gives them red eyes and adds two "arms" that resembles the legs of a regular Bob-omb and yellow and black striped straps wrapped around their bodies. In all appearances, they have a flame on their "fuse," making them appear primed to explode.

The Bob-omb Car also bears a resemblance to the [[Mini Bomb Kart]], which appears in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

==History==
===''Mario Kart'' series===
====''Mario Kart: Double Dash!''====
[[File:Bob-omb Car MKDD model.png|thumb|left|160px|Model from ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]
In ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'', Bomb-cars appear on [[Mushroom Bridge]] and [[Mushroom City]]. Hitting a Bomb-car has the same effect as getting blown up by either a Bob-omb or a [[Spiny Shell (blue)|Spiny Shell]], causing the player to lose their item. After exploding, Bomb-cars respawn from certain hidden passages. On Mushroom Bridge, Bomb-cars appear from the pipe shortcut's entrance. In Mushroom City, the Bomb-car comes out of the alleyway on the left when players go down the hill and onto the left when someone hits the existing bomb on the lower level, and in the "Do Not Enter" alleyway on the upper level. If the player attempts to block the Bomb-car from coming out multiple times, it will reprogram itself to come out in an area that will evade the player, assuming they do not move their [[kart]]. The Bomb-car is also invincible from blowing up within the first three seconds of its respawn. Despite having a lit fuse, a Bomb-car never explodes on its own.

====''Mario Kart Wii''====
[[File:Bob-omb Car MKWii.png|thumb|right|160px|A Bob-omb Car in ''Mario Kart Wii]]
After being removed from {{classic course|GCN|Mushroom Bridge}} in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'', Bob-omb Cars reappear in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' on [[Moonview Highway]]. If a player hits a Bob-omb Car, they lose their item. Unlike in ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'', they respawn on the track itself, from a [[Warp Pipe]] just after the [[Finish line|starting line]], and they can now detonate immediately after respawning.

Bob-omb Cars also appeared on {{classic course|N64|[[Skyscraper]]}} in the first September 2008 tournament, and on [[Luigi Circuit (Mario Kart Wii)|Luigi Circuit]] in the first May 2009 tournament. In both cases, they all drove against the player and were the only obstacles for the player to evade.

====''Mario Kart Tour''====
Bob-omb Cars also appear in ''Mario Kart Tour'' on GCN Mushroom Bridge, Wii Moonview Highway, and both courses' T variants. In this game, they have their original ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' appearance but with windup keys like in their ''Mario Kart Wii'' appearance. If a player lands on top of a Bob-omb Car, they can perform a [[Jump Boost|trick]], which detonates the Bob-omb Car without hurting the racer in question.

====''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''====
[[File:BombCar model.png|thumb|left|160px|Model from ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'']]
Bob-omb Cars also appear in {{classic course|Wii|Moonview Highway}} in Wave 5 of the [[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe – Booster Course Pass|Booster Course Pass]] for ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', using their ''Mario Kart Tour'' appearance with reflections on their sides. In this version, Bob-omb Cars can only be found in the right lane going against the player. If a player uses a [[Boo]] item, they can pass right through Bob-omb Cars without detonating them.

Prior to the Bob-omb Cars' inclusion in the Booster Course Pass, a model for the Bob-omb Car existed within the game files of ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'', though it was removed in the 2.0.0 update. Unlike the Bob-omb Cars in the DLC, this Bob-omb Car was based on their design from ''Mario Kart Wii''.<ref>https://tcrf.net/Mario_Kart_8_Deluxe#Unused_Models.</ref>

====''Mario Kart World''====
Bob-omb Cars appear in ''[[Mario Kart World]]'' as obstacles. They have the same behavior as a Bob-omb Car from past appearances, except they now stop in Free Roam mode if a driver stops their vehicle in their path. Their Bob-omb bodies are also covered with yellow and black striped straps, and two "arms" that look like a regular Bob-omb's legs have been added. Detonating them causes a "BOOM!" onomatopoeia to appear.
