The '''Tenor Card''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is obtained from [[Toadofsky]] in [[Melody Bay]] after performing the [[Moleville]] blues song for him on the [[tadpole]]s. With the Tenor Card, [[Mario]] can purchase [[Megalixir]]s from the [[Juice Bar]].
