'''Jaxi Stands''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a bus stop-like location where [[Mario]] can call for a [[Jaxi]] in the [[Sand Kingdom]].

==Locations==
The Jaxi stands are spread through the entire Sand Kingdom of which the locations are listed here.

* In front of [[Tostarena|Tostarena Town]]
* Under the South Western Floating Island
* At the Tostarena Ruins Entrance
* Next to the Desert Oasis
* On top of the Jaxi Ruins
* Next to the painting that brings you to the [[Metro Kingdom]]
* On top of the Inverted Pyramid
* Near the hole where [[Knucklotec]] is/was stuck in the ground
* At the Tostarena Northwest Reaches
