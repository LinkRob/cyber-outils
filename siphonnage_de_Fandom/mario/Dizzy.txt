'''Dizzy''' is an ailment in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' role-playing games.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
[[File:DizzyTTYD.png|thumb|left]]
Dizzy first appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' where it is a rare ailment caused by enemies. This ailment is usually caused by [[Dark Koopa]]s lurking in the [[Toad Town Tunnels|sewers]]. If Mario is hit by this ailment, then his moves will sometimes miss. Also, Mario can cause Dizzy to enemies by using a [[Dizzy Dial]], the [[Dizzy Attack]], or the [[Dizzy Stomp]] badge. This also applies to ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', but in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', the Dizzy ailment is very rare.

==In {{series|Mario & Luigi}}==
Dizzy appears in all of the ''Mario & Luigi'' games where if the character is Dizzy, then they will lose that turn. The Dizzy ailment is caused by a specific attack or by getting damaged many times in a row or getting hit by something hard. The Dizzy ailment can be cured with [[Refreshing Herb]]s.

Examples of things that can cause dizziness include:

* [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can stun enemies with their hammers and an [[Advance Command|Advanced]] [[Cyclone Bros.]] attack. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'')
* An [[Oucher Glass]]'s sand ball can stun the Bros. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'')
* [[Popple]] can stun the Bros. via his steal attack, taking the target's [[Hammer]] and smashing him with it. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'')
* [[Hammer Bro|Hammer Bros]] can stun the Mario Bros. with hammers. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'')
* The stars that [[Cackletta]] summons during her second fight. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'')
* Mario and Luigi can stun enemies with the [[Smash Egg]] and [[Trampoline]] [[Bros. Attack|Bros. Attacks]]. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'')
* A [[Shroid]]'s spike ball can stun the Bros. if not countered properly. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'')
* The [[Elder Princess Shroob]] and the [[Shroobsworth|Shroobsworths]] can stun the Bros. with their meteors. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'')
* [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can stun enemies with their solo [[Hammer]] attack as well as some [[Special Attack|Special Attacks]] including [[Super Bouncer]], [[Spin Pipe]], and [[Magic Window]]. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'')
* [[Midbus]] can stun the Bros. with his ball. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'')
* The [[Junker]] can stun the Bros. with his [[Thwomp]] throw attack. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'')
* [[Bandit|Bandits]] can stun the Bros. with their cardboard bomb attack (''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'')
* [[Grumbell|Grumbells]] can stun the Bros. with their stomp attack. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'')
* [[Lob-omb|Lob-ombs]] can stun the Bros. with their bombs. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'')
* There is a Battle Plug called "Dizzyfying Attack" that Mario and Luigi can use to stun their enemies. (''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'')
