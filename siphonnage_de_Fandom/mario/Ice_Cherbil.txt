}}'''Ice Cherbils''' are enemies in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Ice Cherbils are bright blue [[Cherbil]]s that, instead of the regular sleep gas, shoot icy gas that freezes [[Mario]]. Their attack patterns are the same as other basic Cherbils or [[Poison Cherbil]]s. Ice Cherbils are found throughout [[Yold Desert]], the [[Overthere Stair]], and, like most enemies, the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].
