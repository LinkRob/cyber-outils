'''Nut Cases''' is a [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
[[Galoomba]]s collect acorns and settles crates with acorns on the tree stumps.

== Gameplay ==
Teams can win by collecting the most acorns by putting crates on their section for tree stumps. Players holding the crates will feel the Joy-Con  rumble. The stronger the rumble of the Joy-Con, the more acorns are in the crate. After the players place it on their five tree stumps or sixty seconds passed, the crates are opened and the acorns are counted.

== Ending ==
The winning team does their victory animations while losing teams are doing their losing animations behind the winning teams.

*{{button|ns|jc-right}} – Take/place box
