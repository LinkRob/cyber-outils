'''Chippies''' are round, mammal-like enemies which appear in ''[[Virtual Boy Wario Land]]''. They attack [[Wario]] by throwing either spears or spiked balls which explode upon hitting the ground. Chippies are only encountered within forested areas, [[Stage 5 (Virtual Boy Wario Land)|Stage 5]] in particular.
