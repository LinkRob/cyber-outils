'''World 7-5''' is the thirty-seventh level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
Throughout the level, Mario must be quick and agile as most platforms featured in World 7-5 are cut off by fast-moving saws along the borders of the platforms. Several [[Goomba]]s walk about most platforms as well. Across several fast-falling platforms, Mario finds a small opening between large blocks that he must sprint through to find the second half of the level. Upon exiting the opening, Mario finds a thing platform that spins around like a screw, and during the spinning of the platform, two saws begin to cut the platform. Past this spinning platform, Mario is ambushed by several Tail Goombas. Past a three-by-three plank of wood that is cut into each of its pieces, Mario finds the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|Checkpoint]]. Past a few more wooden platforms and a minor grassy plain, Mario lands on the last leg of the level, a large moving platform passing through several tall planks and buzz saws. At the end of the block's path, Mario must climb a small staircase of wooden blocks to reach the level's [[Flagpole]].

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is among the many blocks that are cut in front of the small opening near the middle of the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is found around the area with the large supplement of Tail Goombas.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal is located on the large wooden block near the end of the level, located on the far-left side of the path the block takes.
