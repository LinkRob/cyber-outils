The '''Binding Band''' is a treasure that appears in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''. It is a large wedding ring capable of fitting a person in its loop. The Binding Band is located in the [[Sand Kingdom]]'s Ice Cavern, where [[Knucklotec]] lives.
