'''Glide Ramps''' are an object in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''.

==Description==
Glide Ramps are a type of [[ramp]] that causes the character's [[kart]] to deploy its [[glider]] upon being used. Glide Ramps often appear before a long [[pit]], as racers can only travel across certain ones by [[glide|gliding]]. Their appearance is similar to a regular Boost Pad, albeit always blue and with white arrowheads perpendicular to the Glide Ramp's length (and parallel to the direction the player will be sent).

== Courses with Glide Ramps ==

=== ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' ===

* [[Toad Circuit]]: One appears after the racers get through the first lap just before the racers cross the bridge. This can open up a shortcut, though landing on the bridge will cause the shortcut to be bungled.
* [[Daisy Hills]]: Near the end, the racers will have to cross the river this way.
* [[Mario Circuit (Mario Kart 7)|Mario Circuit]]: When the racers exit Peach's Castle, they will be sent into a gliding section over the gap.
* [[Bowser Castle (Mario Kart 7)|Bowser Castle]]: There is one after the Starting Line that leads directly into the castle itself.
* [[Rainbow Road (Mario Kart 7)|Rainbow Road]]: Partway through the second section of the course, the racers will encounter one that leads onto the rings of a Saturn-esque planet, which have been made into a single solid circular platform. They take another to get off. In the final section, after the racers go through the rotating tunnel, they can hit a Glide Ramp to reach some boost-inducing rings.
