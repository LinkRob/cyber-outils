The '''Hammer Bros. Sphinx'''<ref>{{cite web| url = https://www.facebook.com/MarioKartEN/photos/566278000143464| title = Facebook| accessmonthday = June 29| accessyear = 2023| author = @mariokartEN| date = August 22, 2014| archiveurl = https://web.archive.org/web/20230629191853/https://www.facebook.com/MarioKartEN/photos/566278000143464 | archivedate = June 29, 2023| quote = "''The mysterious (and some say cursed) Hammer Bros. Sphinx looms over hapless kart drivers in Dry Dry Desert in Mario Kart 8. Take a lap … if you dare.''"}}</ref> is a landmark featured in desert-themed courses of the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

==Description==
The Hammer Bros. Sphinx is designed after the {{w|Great Sphinx of Giza}}, but with a [[Hammer Bro]] face. It only ever appears as a background element, not a location that can be explored, and it was only ever given an official name on a {{series|Mario Kart}}' Facebook post in 2014. According to the post, some speculate the Hammer Bros. Sphinx to be cursed, something that is not shown in the game.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart 8'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
The Hammer Bros. Sphinx appears in {{classic course|GCN|[[Dry Dry Desert (course)|Dry Dry Desert]]}}, where it replaces the regular sphinx statue from the original ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' iteration of the course. It can be seen near the bend following right after the oasis.

===''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''===
As of the 2023 [[Ninja Tour (2023)|Ninja Tour]], the Hammer Bros. Sphinx makes another background appearance, specifically within {{classic course|Wii|[[Dry Dry Ruins (course)|Dry Dry Ruins]]}}, where it replaces the [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] sphinx from the original ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' version, similar to how it replaced the regular sphinx for {{classic course|GCN|Dry Dry Ruins}}'s reappearance in ''Mario Kart 8''. In {{classic course|Wii|Dry Dry Ruins}}, the Hammer Bros. Sphinx can be found in the vicinity of the branching path between the [[finish line]] and the oasis.

==In ''[[Mario Tennis Aces]]''==
Small statues based on the Hammer Bros. Sphinx decorate the surroundings of the [[Bask Ruins]] court.
