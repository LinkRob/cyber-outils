'''Fruit Forecast''' is a [[2 vs. 2 Minigame|2-vs-2]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Opening ==
The camera zooms closer to the players that are standing on their platform of the tree. The baskets happen to appear on the teams' hands.

== Gameplay ==
The objective is to catch more fruit than the other team. Players above can catch the fruits. The players below catch fruit the players above have not caught. Teams earn a point per fruit they catch, a [[Spiked Ball]] may land on the player's basket and the player can barely move for a second but consciously get up afterward. None of the fruit fall after the time runs out.

== Ending ==
The camera slowly pans to the ground. Teams are found on the ground. The losing team does their losing animation behind the winning team that does their winning animation.
