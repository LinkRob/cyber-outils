A '''Jellien''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
Jelliens are only found in [[Outer Space]] and [[Outer Limits]] during Chapter 4 of the game. Jelliens mainly spend their times floating around into space until they spot someone. When a Jellien spots one of the heroes, they will attempt to bump into them causing damage. Jelliens can be easily dispatched of with [[Squirps]]' laser.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Jellien's name is a portmanteau of "jellyfish" and "alien."
