'''Onnanoko''' is a character who appears in ''[[Wrecking Crew '98]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Onnanoko is a little girl with short, dark brown hair and a matching eye color. She wears wears a red bow, a red apron, a white shirt, and red shoes.

It is revealed that Onnanoko is actually a ghost, and her Onnanoko grows a pair of wings and a halo.

===Personality===
Onnanoko likes playing, as with any typical young girl.

==In ''Wrecking Crew '98''==
Onnanoko is a hidden opponent fought in Stage B if [[Mario]] defeats [[Gotchawrench]] within three minutes. Before starting, Onnanoko says she has been alone for a long time and asks Mario if she can play with him. Mario accepts the offer, and when he wins, Onnanoko reveals her ghostly form before ascending. Winning against Onnanoko unlocks her as a playable character in both VS. Mode and Tournament Mode.

The fact that Onnanoko is not only undead but also claims to have been alone for a long time opens a dark suggestion that she had either been abandoned or lost.
