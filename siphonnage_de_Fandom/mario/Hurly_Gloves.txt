The '''Hurly Gloves''' are one of [[Bowser]]'s personal weapons in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They can be bought from the [[Shaman]] for 92 [[coin]]s. Equipping the Hurly Gloves increases Bowser's [[Attack]] power by 20. When using the weapon during battle, Bowser throws a [[Mario]] decoy at an enemy.
