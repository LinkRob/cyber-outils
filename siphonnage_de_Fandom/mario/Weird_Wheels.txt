'''Weird Wheels''' is a 1-vs-Rivals mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo character has square wheels, while the Rivals have triangular wheels. The faster you press the same button the faster you go ahead of your opponents.

===Solo===
For solo, you have four pictures of the directional pad. Where the red color marks on the arrow, press the same button. If you press the wrong button, you have to start over again.

===Rivals===
For all rivals, you have three pictures of your character and {{Button|wii|1}} and {{Button|wii|2}}. If it has the same character you play and has the number, you press the button shown on the screen. If you press the wrong button, nothing happens.

==Controls==
*Solo - Press any button on the D-Pad
*Rivals - {{Button|wii|1}} or {{Button|wii|2}}
