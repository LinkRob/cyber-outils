The '''300 SL Roadster''' is a downloadable [[kart]] body which first appears in the ''[[Mercedes-Benz x Mario Kart 8]]'' download pack for ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.

== ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''/''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' ==
300 SL Roadster is a downloadable kart part of the ''Mercedes-Benz x Mario Kart 8'' download pack for ''Mario Kart 8''.

In ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', the is unlocked at random by collecting coins. The kart body has high traction but poor air handling.
