'''Officer Kicksworth''' is an enemy in [[Wario: Master of Disguise|''Wario: Master of Disguise'']]. 

==History==
He appears in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]]. When [[Nigel B. Whistlebottom]] blows a whistle, he alerts other guards to come in the room. One of these guards is Officer Kicksworth. When he spots [[Wario]] he will try to attack by throwing a powerful punch or kick that will send him flying backwards. He cannot be defeated but can knocked out permanently.
