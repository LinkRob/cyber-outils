'''Black Rocks''' are obstacles which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. They are found throughout the [[Beanbean Kingdom]], serving as hurdles for [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]. They can only be obstructed by [[Ultra Hammer]]s. Several Black Rocks obstruct the entrance to [[Joke's End]], so Ultra Hammers are required to continue there.

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', they are colored light brown instead.
