'''Circuit Maximus''' is a 4-player mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Gameplay==
Each player must race around a circuit filled with [[Amp]]s. Some Amps will move, while others will stay in place. If a player runs into an Amp, they will be temporarily stunned. Whoever reaches the goal first wins the minigame.
