'''Dull Bones''' are a variant of [[Dry Bones]] featured as an enemy in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.  Dull Bones are typically weaker version of Dry Bones and, as their name implies they are duller in color. Dull Bones wear green shoes and once they are smashed they can't reassemble.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Dull Bones first appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and later go on to appear in it’s [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], in these games, they are the weakest enemy encountered and a much weaker version of the average [[Dry Bones]]. Dull Bones appear within [[Hooktail Castle]] as common enemies, many Dull Bones are littered about the Fortress these were once [[Koopa Troopa]]s that either died within the Fortress or fell victim to [[Hooktail]], some may reassemble and challenge [[Mario]] and his partners to duels, three Dull Bones later reappear in the [[Glitz Pit]] as a team of fighters making up the [[Dead Bones]] team. Dull Bones can also be found in the early floors of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]]. If there are few Dull Bones left in the fight, they can also construct other Dull Bones to help fight.

There is a unique Dull Bones, named [[Red Bones]], encountered twice throughout the game. 

===''Super Paper Mario''===
[[File:Dull_Bones_Card.png|thumb|left]]
Dull Bones reappear in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', in this game, they are found in and around the [[Underwhere]] alongside [[Dry Bones]] but unlike the previous game, Dull Bones are actually stronger than Dry Bones in this game. They throw bones and only deal four points of damage. They have 15 Hit Points, and aren't very strong. However, the Dull Bones can be easily taken down with [[Bowser]]'s fire breath.
