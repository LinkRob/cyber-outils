'''Zigzag Podoboos''' are a variety of [[Podoboo]]s introduced in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Zigzag Podoboos have essentially the same appearance as Podoboos.

===Behavior===
Zigzag Podoboos are always found outside the [[lava]], traveling diagonally as they bounce around the walls, floors, and ceilings of [[castle]] environments. They behave similarly to [[Boo Buddy Snake]]s in [[Ghost House]]s and the [[Fireball (Mario Bros. series)|red fireball]]s in the ''[[Mario Bros. (game)|Mario Bros.]]'' game. Unlike a standard Podoboo, if a Zigzag Podoboo falls into [[lava]], it never resurfaces.

==History==
===''Super Mario World''===
[[File:Zigzag Podoboo SMW sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''Super Mario World'', there is also one Zigzag Podoboo in [[Lemmy Koopa]]'s room and two in [[Wendy O. Koopa]]'s, assisting each [[Koopalings|Koopaling]] in battle.

===''Super Mario Maker'' subseries===
Zigzag Podoboos also appear in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]'', ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', and ''[[Super Mario Maker 2]]''. Here, they can be produced by applying wings to a standard Podoboo. Despite them having wings in these games, their behavior is reletively unchanged.
