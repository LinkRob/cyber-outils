The '''Fire Shot''' is [[Mario]]'s [[Special Shot]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Mario perform Fire Shot by tapping out a capital "M" twice. As Mario prepares the move, he dribbles the ball in said shape, lighting it on fire. Upon doing so, Mario uses one hand to throw the basketball to the hoop while flames trail behind it. If shot from inside the 3-point line, the ball lands in the hoop with a fiery explosion.

If Fire Shot is used from outside the 3-point line, the visuals do not change, but one of Mario's teammates needs to grab the ball and dunk it in.

[[Luigi]] has his own equivalent of the move titled [[Green Fire Shot]], which is almost the same except the flames trailing behind the basketball are instead light green.
