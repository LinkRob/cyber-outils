}}A '''Tomato''' (also called a '''Killer Tomato''') is an enemy that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==History==
The Tomatoes are only seen in [[Fungi Forest]] in the area with [[Funky's Store]].  The [[Worm (character)|Worm]] who lives in a large apple requests [[Chunky Kong]] to get rid of the Tomatoes somehow. The Tomatoes are very tough enemies and cannot be defeated by normal means. The only way to defeat the Tomatoes is to transform into [[Hunky Chunky]] and squish them with a [[Simian Slam]]. After doing so, the Worm will ask Chunky to take him to a safer area of Fungi Forest (knowing the Tomatoes will re-spawn).
