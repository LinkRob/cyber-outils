|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''Mini-Monkey''' is a special ability used by [[Tiny Kong]] in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Tiny can use this move once she buys a potion from [[Cranky Kong]].

==Usage==
In order to use Mini-Monkey, Tiny must jump into a barrel with her face on it. After this, Tiny will be shrunken to smaller size allowing her to fit through small entrances. This is required to enter [[Banana Fairy Island]] in order to speak with the [[Banana Fairy Princess]]. Mini-Monkey is also used in the [[King Krusha K. Rool|final fight]] with [[King K. Rool]] where Tiny must enter his shoe while small and shoot his toes with her [[Feather Bow]]. There are also a few other instances where Mini-Monkey can be used such as being used on [[Music Pad]]s which will call upon [[Squawks the Parrot]] who will carry Tiny up to a higher point.
