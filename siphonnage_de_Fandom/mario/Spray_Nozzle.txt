'''Spray Nozzle''' is the default nozzle for [[F.L.U.D.D.]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. This is the nozzle that the player will most likely find themselves using the most in this game.

== Description ==
The Spray Nozzle can be used to clean graffiti off of walls, stun enemies, and also annoy [[Pianta]]s when they are sprayed. This also makes an appearance in the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games. It appears in blue shooting dew drops at Mario, giving him a ride to death or back to a recognized area.

For the underwater missions for [[Noki Bay]], the Spray Nozzle can be used to move Mario left and right underwater.
