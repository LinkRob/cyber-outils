'''White Clubbas''' are a variant of [[Clubba]] which appear as an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They are encountered in both [[Shiver Mountain]] and the [[Crystal Palace]].

White Clubbas are much stronger than regular Clubbas, and have a lot more [[Attack]] power as well as a lot of [[Heart Point|HP]]. In contrast to their original counterparts, White Clubbas are a lot less lazy, and stay quick on the draw in case of any intruders walk by. [[Mario]] cannot bypass these enemies in the overworld. White Clubbas are vulnerable to [[Fire (element)|fire]]-elemental attacks.

In Crystal Palace, Mario is required to fight and defeat a few White Clubbas for the Clubba statues to clear out the Clubba statues on the other side. They are also loyal to the [[Crystal King]] referring to him as "great and honorable."
