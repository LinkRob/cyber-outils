The '''Shower Fish''' is an enemy that can be encountered in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. They are common on Allsand Island.

== Description ==
Shower Fishes are large blue fishes with pink lips. A shower head sprouts from the top of their head.

== Behaviour ==
As with most enemies, Shower Fishes chase after intruders into their territory.

=== Combat ===
In combat, Shower Fishes have two attacks, with their first attack having them stand in the centre and then jump onto a Bro. from there. Before she attacks, a Shower Fish will either kiss or sigh, with the former meaning she is targeting Mario and the latter is targeting Luigi. The target has to use his hammer to repel the attack; if this happens, the Shower Fishes will bounce off the hammer, flying higher above their target after the first two hits and retreating after the third counterattack.

Shower Fishes can also approach a Bro. to perform a bum rush, with the same tell as their first attack. They can give false tells to throw the target off, and the bum rush must be dodge by jumping. The player can tell when the Shower Fishes will perform the bum rush when their shower head glows.

==== Dried Shower Fishes ====
Occasionally, a Shower Fish will become dehydrated, taking on a slimmer, yellow form with a light instead of a shower head. The transformation is telegraphed if she breathes in a tired manner. This "dried" form has a different moveset, with one attack being to quickly perform a figure eight around the Bros. before closing in. This must be avoided by jumping. The second attack involves going in front of a target and charging power. When the enemy's light glows, a powerful slap that must be blocked with a hammer strike will be unleashed.

Dried Shower Fishes can revert back to their normal form, signalled by patting themselves.

==== Statistics ====
Shower Fishes are Level 17 monsters who have 248 hit points, an attack power of 62, a defense of 42, and a speed of 59 in their normal form and 70 in their dried state. Their defeat nets 154 experience and 54 coins.

== Variants ==

=== Glohm-infused Shower Fishes ===
Some Shower Fishes contract [[Glohm]] later. These have the same behaviour as their regular counterparts, but are even stronger and have the ability to raise their attack power. Three appear on Dropplet Islet as minor bosses guarding the Blocky Hammer Wear DX.

These Shower Fishes are typically of Level 40 with 1224 hit points, with an experience drop of 1695 and a coin drop of 253 when destroyed. However, the Dropplet Islet Glohm Shower Fish trio is of Level 42, with each one having 1364 hit points. When these are destroyed, they each grant 1923 experience and 275 coins for totals of 5769 and 825 apiece.

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
Shower fishes take their name from the shower head on their body.

== Nomenclature ==
In Japanese, Shower Fishes are called "''Shawā Uo''" (written シャワーウオ), with ''Shawā'' (written シャワー) coming from a transliteration of English "shower" and ''uo'' (written 魚 in Kanji, うお in Hiragana, and ウオ in Katakana) meaning "fish." Their Chinese name, ''Lín'yú'' (written 淋鱼) comes from the verb ''lin'yú'' ("to take a shower"; written 淋浴) and ''yú'' ("fish"; written 鱼). Spanish translates their name as ''Metamórpez'', from ''metamorfosis'' ("metamorphosis," perhaps due to their ability to change forms) and ''pez'' ("fish," referring to their design). Italian calls them ''Pesce Dóccia'', with ''dóccia'' meaning "shower"and ''pesce'' meaning "fish." In French, their name is ''Shampouïe'', from ''shampooing'' ("shampoo") merged with ''ouïe'' ("gill"). Korean translates their name as ''Syaweo Gogi'' (written 샤워고기), with ''gogi'' (written 고기) meaning "fish" and ''syaweo'' (written 샤워) being a transliteration of English "shower." For unknown reasons, they do not have a translated name in Dutch.
