'''Galaxy Bounce''' is the boss [[microgame]] of [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Ninja Scribble]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. The microgame has an extended variation featured as a souvenir, [[Orbit Ball (souvenir)|Orbit Ball]].

==Gameplay==
The object is to draw lines under [[Orbit Ball (character)|Orbit Ball]] to keep him bouncing upward. There are clouds in the sky, and if Orbit Ball passes through them, he is slowed down. If the ball makes it to space, it lands on a planet and give a thumbs-up, meaning the player has won the minigame. If Orbit Ball falls downward from missing a line, the player loses at the microgame.

The only difference between the difficulty levels is that they increasingly add more clouds.
