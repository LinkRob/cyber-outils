In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', [[Mario]] is immediately able to use it after attaining the [[Lucky Star (Paper Mario)|Lucky Star]] from [[Twink]] but in all later games, [[Mario]] has this ability by default.

==History==
Guard can be used to guard against an enemy's attacks to lessen damage. In order to use Guard, the player must press {{Button|gcn|a}} at the right moment to Guard against incoming attacks. All attacks are different in the game so the timing for Guarding will always be different. A successful Guard can also prevent [[Mario]] or his partners from being affected with a status ailment such as [[Poison (ailment)|Poison]].

In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', there is a different version of the Guard known as the [[Superguard]]. It works similar to Guard except it nullifies ''all'' damage and, if the enemy used a physical attack, deals back 1 point of damage to them. However, the Superguard is much more difficult to pull off and is recommend to stick with the regular guard when trying to defend against a status-inducing attack.
