'''Dreamy Sandoons''' are dreamy variants of [[Sandoon]]s which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', they are purple in color and usually come in large numbers, unlike regular Sandoons, Dreamy Sandoons cannot swallow [[Mario]] but they have the ability to make themselves taller and are also tougher than regular Sandoons.

==Battle==

Dreamy Sandoons appear in [[Dreamy Dozing Sands]] as common enemies. They are somewhat less intelligent than their normal counterparts, just wandering back and forth in the overworld occationally stretching their bodies. Striking the upper part of this variation counts as jumping on them. Their attacks are completely different, with their first one having them charge headlong and either stretch or retract their bodies as they advance, and their second one having them surround [[Mario]] who must block a cactus ball with a well-timed hammer strike. The Sandoon holding the cactus ball will be the one to throw it. If the Sandoons rotate, the formation will rotate accordingly, possibly changing the target. Their other attack involves them  In battle, Dreamy Sandoons appear in large hordes. They can attack by advancing towards Mario in vertical (from the player's point of view) yet sometimes crooked lines of up to six at a time. While doing this, the Dreamy Sandoons sink into or rise out of the sand, and Mario must jump over or on to them when they're close enough in order to avoid this.

The Dreamy Sandoons are Level 11 enemies with 38 [[HP]], 41 [[Power]], 42 [[Defense]], and 30 [[Speed]], and give 5 [[EXP]] upon defeat in addition to 2 [[Coin]]s and a 1% chance each of a [[Super Mushroom]] and a [[Boo Biscuit]].

==Trivia==
*Despite Dreamy Sandoons being more formidable than the regular Sandoons, their stats are much lower.
