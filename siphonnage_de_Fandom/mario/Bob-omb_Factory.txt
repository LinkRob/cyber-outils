The '''Bob-omb Factory''' is the second board map in story mode on ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.  Throughout the board, [[Bob-omb]]s can be seen being assembled and transported in the surroundings. This is slightly harder than [[Toad Road]] but it is not the hardest. The two bosses the player faces here are [[Whomp]] at the fort and [[Big Bob-omb]] beyond the Bowser gate. These two bosses can be a handful but once defeated the player will be able to move on [[Boo's Horror Castle]]. The people the player will be up against will be either [[Shy Guy]] or [[Kamek]] and the possibility of one or two other people. If the player is paired up with either [[Shy Guy]] or [[Kamek]], this ensures the player must become the superstar or tie to move on to the next board. This is also a board map available in party mode from the start.

==Spaces single path==
In the first section, 10 of the spaces are path of the conveyor belt, but only 5 of them will activate the conveyor belt, the rest 5 are normal spaces. In this board below, they're all called Event Space although it may changes to normal space at any time.

'''Side-by-side Zone Captain Event'''
Players split up. Then they take turns hitting a [[Dice Block]], simultaneously moving forward on their separate paths. When a player lands on an Event Space, a conveyor belt activates, moving the [[Mini Star|Mini Stars]] one row to the left. The captain should note the locations of Event Spaces and [[Mini Star|Mini Stars]] and plan everyone's route accordingly.
