'''Mecha Choice''' is a Free-for-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.
==Opening==
In this game, the four characters start running before the [[Mechakoopa]] and start with 3 door choices.

==Gameplay==
*Choose a safe route to escape the Mechakoopas. If you pick a dead end, you're out!
*This minigame is completely luck-based, there's no ultimate strategy here.
*The minigame ends in the following situations:
**If any of all players pick a safe route within the time limit through all of them.
**If 1, 2 or 3 players get locked up in a dead end.
**If all players get locked up in a dead end.

==Controls==
Hold the Wii remote sideways. Press A, 1, 2 to go to the respective door. You can change your door until time runs out.
