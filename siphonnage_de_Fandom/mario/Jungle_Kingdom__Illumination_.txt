The '''Jungle Kingdom''' is one of the main kingdoms of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]'' and neighbors both the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] and the [[Snow Kingdom (Illumination)|Snow Kingdom]]. It is populated by various [[Kongs]], including [[Donkey Kong (Illumination)|Donkey Kong]], [[Diddy Kong (Illumination)|Diddy Kong]], [[Dixie Kong (Illumination)|Dixie Kong]], [[Chunky Kong (Illumination)|Chunky Kong]] and the Kong Army under [[Cranky Kong (Illumination)|Cranky Kong]]'s leadership.
It first appears in the second act of the movie when [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] need aid from Cranky Kong and the Kong Army to save [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] from [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] and his Koopa Army at the [[Dark Lands]].

==History==
The Jungle Kingdom first appears when Mario, Princess Peach, and Toad approach the gate guarded by [[Sports Coat Kong|a huge blond guard Kong]].
