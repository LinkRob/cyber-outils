'''Spin''' could refer to:

*[[Spin Jump]] - A move utilized by [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] in various 2D games such as ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.
*[[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)]] - A move utilized by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] in every game of the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' series except ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''.
*[[Star Spin]] - A move utilized by [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] in the ''[[Super Mario Galaxy (series)|Super Mario Galaxy]]'' games with the help of [[Luma (character)|Baby Luma]] which allows them to navigate through areas and beat enemies.
