An '''Emoglobin''' is a kind of creature in [[Bowser's body]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. They offer hints and sometimes allow the player to save their game progress. One type heals [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] completely. There are also variations of Emoglobins including [[Evoglobin]]s, [[Nutsoglobin]]s, [[Liftoglobin]]s and [[Totemglobin]]s.
