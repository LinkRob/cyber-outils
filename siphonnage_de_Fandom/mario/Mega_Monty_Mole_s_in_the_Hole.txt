'''Mega Monty Mole's in the Hole''' is a boss minigame featuring [[Mega Monty Mole]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. It is a minigame styled after Whack-a-Mole.

==Gameplay==
The goal is to hit Mega Monty Mole several times until he is defeated. Mega Monty Mole pops out of one of the seven holes within the area at random. Each time a character hits Mega Monty Mole, they earn two points. However, Mega Monty Mole utilizes various explosive decoys, and if they hit a fake, they lose a point and are stunned.

In the second part of the fight, there are even more decoys to be wary of.

Whoever has the most points by the time Mega Monty Mole is defeated is the winner, just like with other boss minigames.
