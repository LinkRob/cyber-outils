The '''Boathouse''' is a small store run by two [[Raccoon]]s in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. It is located in [[Delfino Plaza]], and [[Mario]] can access the shop after defeating a [[Polluted Piranha Plant]] on the pier side of Delfino Plaza. This also opens the entrance to [[Ricco Harbor]] whose entrance is on the side of this building.

The Boathouse exchanges a [[Shine Sprite]] for every ten [[Blue Coin]]s. The interior of the store is very simple with just a counter, a shelf, and a giant Shine Sprite model on the shelf. The Boathouse is run by two [[Raccoon]]s. There are 240 blue coins for Mario to collect all around [[Isle Delfino]], so a total of twenty-four Shine Sprites are obtained here.
