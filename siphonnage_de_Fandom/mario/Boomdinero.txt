'''Boomdinero''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Boomdineros are a variant of [[Bandinero]]s wearing a protective armor, either gray or red in color, and the main body is in the shape of a cannon. Boomdineros also has shield-like arms that protect it from [[Wario]]'s [[jump]] and [[Dash Attack|dash attack]]s, bouncing Wario away if he attempts either move on one. A Boomdinero's armor also protects it from taking damage from [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]]. Boomdineros are heavier than other enemies, as seen from Wario's movement being slowed whenever he carries one.

===Behavior===
A Boomdinero attacks by shooting [[Spiked Ball|spiky ball]]s at [[Wario]] from the top, done in a similar manner to a cannon shooting out a cannonball. Silver Boomdineros are stationary, shoot only one spiky ball at a time, and take a while before reacting to Wario. Red Boomdineros walk from side to side, shoot spiky balls faster, and react more quickly. In [[Derailed Express]], there is a silver Boomdinero that has a different behavior of walking side to side, just as red Boomdineros do, but it does not fire spiky balls at Wario.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
Boomdineros have a three-star threat rating. Wario can temporarily stun a Boomdinero by performing an [[Earthshake Punch]] near one, then pick it up and shake the armor off. A Boomdinero's armor can also be destroyed by charging into it from a [[Max Fastosity Dasherator]]. Wario cannot defeat a Boomdinero by throwing it with power.

Boomdineros are summoned by [[Bloomsday]] during his boss fight.
