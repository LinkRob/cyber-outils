The '''Water Stone''' is an important item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
This mythical jewel allows water to flow out of it when it [[Mario]] places the stone in the hole of [[Lily (Paper Mario)|Lily]]'s empty pond. The Water Stone can be found by going though the grass maze to where [[Rosie]] is seen holding it. However, she is quite found by the appearance of the Water Stone and doesn't want to give it up unless Mario brings her something that she considers "more pretty" than the Water Stone. Mario manages to bring her a [[Crystal Berry]] from [[Posie]] and Rosie is excited to have it and gladly gives up the Water Stone.
