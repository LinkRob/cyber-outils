A '''Spite Pouch''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
Spite Pouches are small, orange bags with some powder on the inside.

==Effects==
By using a Spite Pouch, [[Mario]] or his partner is inflicted with the [[Payback]] status that counterattacks direct damage to opponents and inflicts half the damage toward them. For example, if Mario uses a Spite Pouch and gets attacked directly by a [[Spiked Goomba]], he will deal 1 damage back since its attack is 2. However, the Spite Pouch will not counter indirect attacks such as magical spells from [[Magikoopa]]s.
