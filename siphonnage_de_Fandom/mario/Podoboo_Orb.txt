The '''Podoboo Orb''' is an item in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. It costs five coins at the [[Orb Shop]]. Opponents that pass over a space containing the Orb summon Podoboos that immediately attack them, causing a loss of ten coins. It was functionally replaced by the [[Spiny Orb]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.
