The '''Thrice Candy''' is a [[Candy (Mario Party 8)|Candy]] in ''[[Mario Party 8]]''.

==Description==
The Thrice Candy is a red Candy with a picture of three yellow [[Dice Block]]s on the wrapper and replaces the [[Super 'Shroom Orb]] from past ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party]]'' games, acting identically to it. When used, it displays a special effect and lets the player hit three Dice Blocks at once instead of one. If the player rolls the same numbers, then they get a bonus 30 [[coin]]s (100 coins for three "7's"). Thrice Candies typically cost 20 coins but can sometimes cost only 15 coins during a bargain sale. They can be found on every board except for [[Bowser's Warped Orbit]].

As the Thrice Candy functions the same as [[Triple Dice]], the probabilities of rolling certain numbers are the same.
