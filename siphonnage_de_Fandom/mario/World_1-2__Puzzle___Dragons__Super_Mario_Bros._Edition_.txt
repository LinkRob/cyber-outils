'''World 1-2''' is the second stage in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. As the second stage, it is also very easy, having nothing noteworthy aside from it having slightly more diverse spawns than [[World 1-1 (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|1-1]].

== Encounters ==
Note: Enemies listed in bold will always appear, while enemies that are italicized have a chance to appear.
* '''Encounter 1''': '''1 [[Goomba]]''', ''1 Goomba, 1 Green [[Koopa Troopa]]'' (Only one of the 2nd and 3rd options will appear.)
* '''Encounter 2''': Same as Encounter 1.
* '''Encounter 3''': '''1 Green Koopa Troopa''', ''1 Green Koopa Troopa, 1 Goomba'' (Only one of the 2nd and 3rd options will appear.)
* '''Encounter 4''': (Boss) '''3 Green Koopa Troopas'''
