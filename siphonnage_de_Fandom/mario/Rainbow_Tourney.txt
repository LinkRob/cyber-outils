The '''Rainbow Tourney''' is the fourth and final tournament in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''. It can be unlocked by completing the [[Star Tourney]]. Rainbow Tourney consists of four courts: [[Sherbet Land (basketball court)|Sherbet Land]], [[Bloocheep Sea]], [[Pirate Ship]] and [[Rainbow Ship]]. Completing Rainbow Tourney unlocks the hard difficulty setting for all tourneys.

==Unlockables==
Aside from unlocking a hard difficulty setting, various other content can be unlocked by completing Rainbow Tourney on a certain difficulty or by obtaining a bronze, silver, or gold prize at minimum, depending on the item. Each of Rainbow Tourney's unlock tiers in normal difficulty is of a ''Final Fantasy'' character, while each unlock tier in hard mode is of an alternate costume for each of the ''Final Fantasy'' characters unlocked on normal difficulty setting.

*[[Ninja (character)|Ninja]]: Complete on normal difficulty with at least a bronze prize.
*[[White Mage]]: Complete on normal difficulty with at least a silver prize.
*[[Black Mage]]: Complete on normal difficulty with a gold prize.
*Black Mage alternate costume: Complete on hard difficulty with at least a bronze prize.
*Ninja alternate costume: Complete on hard difficulty with at least a silver prize.
*White Mage alternate costume: Complete on hard difficulty with a gold prize.
