'''Fleepers''' are a type of enemy which only appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Fleepers are yellow flea with proportionally large feet compared to the rest of their body. 

===Behavior===
Fleepers can jump very high, and can sometimes jump up to the [[Nintendo DS]]'s top screen. If a Fleeper lands on a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], he takes damage, causing the [[Countdown Timer]] to start. A Yoshi can defeat a Fleeper by either [[jump]]ing onto it or by throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at it.

===Etymology===
Their name is a portmanteau of "flea," referring to their species, and "leaper," referring to their main characteristic.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
Fleepers are first encountered in the level [[Boing!]]. Four of them can be found in the land-enemy exhibit of the [[Island Museum]].
