}}The '''Sweet Mystery Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. This is a candy-themed galaxy where [[Bulb Yoshi]] is used.
== Missions ==
=== Bulb Berry's Mysterious Glow ===
The first mission of the galaxy requires [[Mario]] to simply ride [[Bulb Yoshi]] across several platforms, making sure to grab a new berry before falling off. When the player reaches the second half of the level, they must activate several Flipswitches to reveal a [[Launch Star]] to the [[Power Star]].
=== Bulb Berry's Purple Coin Glow ===
The Purple Comet must be unlocked in order to select this mission. The player's goal is to collect all 100 Purple Coins. To collect them all, the player will need to use Yoshi and the Bulb Berry to open up new paths. If they manage to get all of the coins, they will earn a Power Star.

=== Green Star 1 ===
The player will redo the Bulb Berry's Mysterious Glow mission. The first green star is located under a Matter Splatter platform. To get it, the player must use Bulb Yoshi and stand on this platform. Then, they must wait until the Bulb Berry's effects wears off and fall to get the Green Star. This Star can also be reached by long jumping at it with Luigi.

=== Green Star 2 ===
The player will redo the Bulb Berry's Mysterious Glow mission. The second Green Star is located near the location of the Comet Medal on the Sweet Mystery Planet. To get it, the player has to perform a Flutter Jump towards the star located to the right of the medal.{{Super Mario Galaxy 2 Galaxies}}
