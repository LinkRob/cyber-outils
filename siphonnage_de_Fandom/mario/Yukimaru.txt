A '''Yukimaru''' (plural: Yukimaru) is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Yukimaru are snowman enemies on skis.

===Powers and abilities===
Yukimaru can spit out a stream of icy breath, which turns [[Wario]] into [[Frozen Wario]] upon contact.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
Yukimaru are encountered in [[Syrup Castle]], [[Invade Wario Castle]], [[Uncanny Mansion]], and [[Steal the Syrup's treasure!!|The Really Final Chapter]]. They can only be defeated if Frozen Wario slides into them.
