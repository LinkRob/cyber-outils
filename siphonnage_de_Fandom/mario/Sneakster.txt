The '''Sneakster''', known in Europe and Australia as the '''Nitrocycle''', is a type of [[bike]] that can only be used by middle-weight characters in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. Its kart counterpart is the [[Sprinter]].

== How to Unlock ==
It can be unlocked by getting a Star Rank on all [[Engine Class|100cc]] cups.

== Stats ==
* '''Speed''': High
* '''Weight''': Medium
* '''Acceleration''': Low
* '''Handling''': Low
* '''Drift''': Medium
* '''Off-Road''': Low
* '''Mini-Turbo''': Low
