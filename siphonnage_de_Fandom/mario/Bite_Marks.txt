'''Bite Marks''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is part of [[Wario Deluxe]]'s set.

== Layout ==
Bite Marks has a starkly different layout from its [[House Plant|inspiration]], but its gameplay is the same.

== Gameplay ==
The player has to tap on the blocks on the bottom jaw of a robot designed after [[Wario]] to allow the top jaw to fit.

=== Difficulty Levels ===
The first difficulty level requires a single block to break, with the last two requiring two. On the third level, some blocks must be tapped twice to break.

== Behind the Scenes ==
Bite Marks is based on [[House Plant]] from ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' The name comes from a "bite mark," which refers to a mark on a surface where something is bitten.
