'''Mel''' is a [[human]] tennis player who appears in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''. Her doubles partner is [[Chas]]. Mel's [[Offensive Power Shot]] is the [[Slider Shot]] while her [[Defensive Power Shot]] is the [[High-Jump Save]].
