The '''Cellar''' is a room in [[Gloomy Manor]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is the final area explored in Gloomy Mansion, and it is only accessible from the elevator in the [[Lobby]]. It is also where [[Luigi]] fights the [[Grouchy Possessor]] and obtains the first [[Dark Moon]] piece.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During [[Confront the Source|A-Boss: Confront the Source]], [[Professor E. Gadd|E. Gadd]] sends Luigi to the Cellar after discovering the Dark Moon piece is hidden from down there. Luigi reluctantly goes to the Cellar where he fights the large spider queen. During the battle, Luigi discovers that a strange, white ghost has possessed the body of the spider queen and heads back in. Luigi manages to capture the Possessor ghost into the [[Poltergust 5000]] and obtains the first Dark Moon piece.
