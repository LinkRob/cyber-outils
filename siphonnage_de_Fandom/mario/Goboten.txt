'''Goboten''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Goboten are seal-like creatures. As shown from their artwork, Goboten have a dark blue body, a pink nose, and a tan belly. They wear pink glasses, a pink vest, and a white and red-striped bandana on their head.

===Behavior===
If a Goboten spots [[Wario]], it throws a harpoon at him. If Wario is hit by the harpoon, he takes damage.

===Habitats===
Goboten are mainly encountered in underwater passages of [[Kitchen Island]].
