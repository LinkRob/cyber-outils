'''Bean Pants''' is a [[clothing]] item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They can only be worn by [[Mario]].

==In the games==
In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'', the Bean Pants can be bought from the clothes shop in [[Beanbean Castle Town]] for 100 [[coin]]s. Wearing it increases Mario's [[defense]] by 12.

In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'', the Bean Pants becomes available at the Beanbean Castle Town clothes store after [[Queen Bean]] has been defeated. The Bean Pants instead cost 160 coins and increase defense by 18. Bean Pants can be sold for 40 coins.
