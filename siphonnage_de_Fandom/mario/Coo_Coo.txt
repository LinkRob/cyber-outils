'''Coo Coos''' are a species of [[Chicken]] which first appear in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Coo Coos look like large, featherless chickens with two beady eyes, one of which has a star around it.

===Behavior===
Coo Coos typically attack [[Mario]] by dropping splotches of [[Goop]], in the fashion of [[Poop|bird poop]], when he is nearby. While the goop does little harm, it can be a slight hindrance from briefly slowing Mario.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
Coo Coos appear in a couple courses with the first being [[Bianco Hills]]. Coo Coos are seen flying around near the path to the [[Big Windmill]]. Coo Coos are later seen in [[Pianta Village]] where they are seen near the village area and drop blobs of [[Lava Goop]] this time.

Coo Coos pose little of a threat, as they can easily be defeated by being jumped onto. Upon being defeated, a Coo Coo drops a couple [[Coin]]s. If [[Mario]] uses [[F.L.U.D.D.]] to spray a Coo Coo, they drop Coins, as well as a [[1-Up Mushroom]] after some time. Said tactic is useful when going for the 100-Coin [[Shine Sprite]]s, as it also takes a Coo Coo to the ground to be stomped for more Coins.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Coo Coo's name could be based on the cuckoo, a type of bird and the sound made by a cuckoo clock. It could also be a play on the word "cuckoo," a way of describing someone who has gone crazy.

==Trivia==
*[[Cluckboom]]s, enemies that look similar to Coo Coos, appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', and its sequel, ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Their method of attacking Mario is by dropping explosive eggs on him, similar to a Coo Coo's method of dropping Goop on Mario.
