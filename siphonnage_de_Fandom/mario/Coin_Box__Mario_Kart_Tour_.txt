The '''Coin Box''' is a special item of certain drivers in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. It spits out a lot of [[coin]]s onto the track.

== Description ==
It appears as a skill of [[Mario|Mario (Hakama)]], [[Princess Peach|Peach (Vacation)]], [[Pink Gold Peach]], [[Pauline|Pauline (Party Time)]], [[Rosalina|Rosalina (Swimwear)]], [[Mario|Mario (Tuxedo)]], [[Yoshi|Yoshi (Gold Egg)]], [[Roving Racers|Gold Koopa (Freerunning)]], [[Dry Bones|Dry Bones (Gold)]], [[King Boo|King Boo (Gold)]], [[Bowser|Bowser (Santa)]], [[King Bob-omb|King Bob-omb (Gold)]], [[Kamek]], [[Meowser]], [[Chargin'_Chuck|Chargin' Chuck (Gold)]], [[Wiggler|Wiggler (Gold)]], [[Luigi|Luigi (Gold Knight)]], the [[Mii|Gold Mii Racing Suit]], and the [[Mii|? Block Mii Racing Suit]].

They discharge widespread forty coins toward the course when the player activates it. When a driver is leveled up to the point where they have Coin Box+, the Coin Box will sprinkle [[Red Coin|Red Coins]] as well. The Coin Box skill can only be found on High-End drivers, with a total of 19 drivers having it.
