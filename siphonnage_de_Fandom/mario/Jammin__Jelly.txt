'''Jammin' Jelly''' is an item in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]].

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Jammin'_Jelly_PM.png|frame|left]]
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', [[Mario]] can use Jammin' Jellies to recover fifty [[Flower Point|FP]]. Many [[recipe]]s require the Jammin' Jelly to be made. Mario cannot purchase these items anywhere in the game and there is only a limited number in the overworld; however, Mario can find an infinite number of Jammin' Jellies by finding silver [[Li'l-Oink]]s, although the drop rates are extremely rare. Jammin' Jellies can be cooked together with [[Ultra Shroom]]s to make the [[Jelly Ultra]], the best healing item in the game which restores 50 HP and FP.

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Mario can purchase these items in the shop in [[Rogueport Sewers]]. In addition, Mario can also win these items as Shop Point prizes or use the [[Pianta Parlor]]. Mario can use these items in new recipes as well, or simply use this item to recover fifty points of FP.
