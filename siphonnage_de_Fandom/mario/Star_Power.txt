'''Star Powers'''  (SP for short) are abilities in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Once [[Mario]] frees one of the seven [[Star Spirits]], he is given an ability from them. This ability will cost [[Star Energy]] to be used. When Mario frees [[Eldstar]] from the [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]] he is given two Star Spirits, [[#Focus|Focus]] and [[#Refresh|Refresh]].

== Abilities ==
=== Focus ===
Focus is one of the first two abilities that Mario learns. Given to Mario by Eldstar, this power does not cost Star Energy to use as it increases Mario's Star Energy when used.

=== Refresh ===
Refresh is the first ability that Mario learns that requires Star Energy. It is used to recover five [[Heart Point|HP]] and [[Flower Point|FP]]. This power costs Mario one point of Star Energy to use.
