'''Big Bullet Bill''' is a [[Bullet Bill]] similar in size to a [[Banzai Bill]]. Even though they are a larger variety of a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' enemy, Big Bullet Bills have only appeared in the {{series|Mario & Sonic}} of crossover games.

==In {{series|Mario & Sonic}}==
===''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''===
[[File: M&S Big Bullet Bill DS.png|thumb|left|200px|Big Bullet Bill blocking the way.]]
Big Bullet Bill appears as a rival boss and is similar in size to a [[Banzai Bill]]. The player meets him in [[Skeleton (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Wii)|Skeleton]] (Single Festival), [[Bobsleigh (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Wii)|Bobsleigh]] (Team Festival), and [[Blazing Bobsleigh]] (DS Adventure Mode).

Big Bullet Bill is first faced in the Skeleton Bobsleigh event in Festival mode. Since he can fly, he has no need for a bobsled and he can maneuver through the course with ease. If the player loses, Big Bullet Bill will fly proudly towards the sky, soon after [[Cream the Rabbit|Cream]] proclaims that the match was not fair because Big Bullet Bill could fly. If the player is the winner, Big Bullet Bill will fly away slowly towards and behind the crowd.

In the [[Adventure Tours]] mode of the Nintendo DS version, he appears at the end of [[Cubyrinth]], where he keeps [[Icy]] in a cage, and will only release the Snow Spirit if the player defeats him in [[Blazing Bobsleigh]].

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'' (Wii)===
Big Bullet Bills appear as enemies in the [[Dream Long Jump (Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games)|Dream Long Jump]] event. In this event, Big Bullet Bills goes across the screen in an attempt to knock down the players and make them fall in the water. Before a Big Bullet Bill passes through the screen, a warning screen appears to warn players of its arrival.

==Trivia==
*Big Bullet Bill is the only exclusive ''[[Mario & Sonic (series)|Mario & Sonic]]'' character to never made appearances in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series.
