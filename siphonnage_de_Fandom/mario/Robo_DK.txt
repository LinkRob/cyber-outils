'''Robo DK''' is a non-playable character who shows up [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']]. It appears on the track [[DK Spaceport]].

== Profile ==
Robo DK is an antagonist who is the robotic guardian of [[DK Spaceport]]. It has blue and white armour and a single black screen with two yellow dots that serves as his "eye." Vertical yellow tiles appear below his "nose" to form a "mouth," making him appear to be smiling.

==Main appearances==
=== In [[Mario Kart World|''Mario Kart World'']] ===
Robo DK is the main obstacle that menaces the racers in [[DK Spaceport]]. After the [[Glide Ramp]] that leads to the girder section, he jumps away to climb the top of the structure. As the racers ascend the structure, he tosses barrels down the track. These barrels act as obstacles and will squash any racer in their way. At the top, Robo DK throws even more barrels much like in the first layer, some of which may jump off-course to throw racers off, and the racers must pass between his legs to get past him and the girder segment.

== Trivia ==

* Robo DK and the DK Spaceport course reference the [[Donkey Kong (video game)|''Donkey Kong'']] arcade game. The way he acts is similar to [[Cranky Kong]] (who was then known as "Donkey Kong") in that game, and the girder section references how [[Mario (character)|Mario]] had to climb construction structures to reach Cranky in his quest to save [[Pauline]].
