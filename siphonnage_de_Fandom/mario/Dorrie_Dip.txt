'''Dorrie Dip''' is an Item [[minigame]] found in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It takes place in an area that resembles the [[Hazy Maze Cave]] from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Gameplay==
In the minigame, the player stands on [[Dorrie]]'s back and has to [[Ground Pound|ground-pound]] to get her to use her mouth to grab one of the items spinning around them. If Dorrie grabs [[Koopa Kid|Baby Bowser]] or the time limit reaches zero, the player gets nothing.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|A}} '''→''' {{button|n64|Z}} &ndash; Ground Pound

==Flavor text==
*'''Game Rules''' &ndash; ''"If you Ground Pound on Dorrie's back, she'll grab an item for you."''

==Trivia==
*The description for this minigame identifies Dorrie as a female, despite Dorrie being identified as a male in ''Super Mario 64'' by a [[sign]]. Similarly, the ''[[Nintendo Power]]'' and [[Prima Games|Prima]] strategy guides referred to Dorrie as a female.
