'''Flies''' are enemies introduced in ''[[Mario Paint]]''.

==History==
===''Mario Artist'' series===
====''Mario Paint''====
[[File:Fly MP sprite.png|frame|left]]
Flies are the main enemies in [[Gnat Attack]], a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Paint]]''. They are the only enemies in the game that are unable to cause any damage to [[Mario]]. They are the first enemies to appear in the game, and are the least threatening; just dashing across the screen when they come on. As they are unable to harm Mario's hand, Mario should concentrate on its more dangerous variants before swatting these insects.

====''Mario Artist: Paint Studio''====
Flies were planned to appear in a Gnat Attack minigame in ''[[Mario Artist: Paint Studio]]'', which was ultimately not implemented. They would have had a very different appearance compared to ''Mario Paint'', consisting in a brown body with yellow wings and a red proboscis.

====''WarioWare'' series====
Flies appear in several [[microgame]]s in the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series; this includes [[Mario Paint: Fly Swatter]] and [[Fly Swatter]] from ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', [[Fly Swatter (microgame)|Fly Swatter]] from ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and [[Mario Paint - Flyswatter]] from ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. 

===''Super Mario Maker''===
Flies randomly appear in the editing mode of ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. They buzz around the screen, and can be swatted with a fly swatter, and hitting them will trigger a recreation of the Gnat Attack minigame. Flies can also be spawned by shaking a [[Muncher]] and quickly trigger Gnat Attack. Additionally, on Fridays, the title screen animation has a fly attempt to carry away [[Builder Mario]]. The fly is swatted before it succeeds, dropping Builder Mario off of the screen. In English releases of ''Super Mario Maker'', Friday is referred to as "Flyday" until the fly is swatted. Because Gnat Attack is not available in ''[[Super Mario Maker for Nintendo 3DS]]'', Flies do not appear there or in [[Course Maker]], but a fly still appears during title screen animation for Friday.

===''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''===
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', flies appear as part of the Flies & Hand [[Assist Trophy]].
