A {{Nihongo|'''Vibe'''|きぶん|Kibun|Feeling}} is an emotion featured in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. Vibes can be forced on any living thing appearing in the game. Vibes also appear as a game mechanic.

== Description ==
There are four types of vibes that [[Princess Peach]] can use, [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]], [[Rage]], [[Gloom]], and [[Calm]], only one of which can be used at a time. Peach can use either of the vibes until her [[Vibe Gauge]] has been depleted. Contrarily, enemies continue using an infinite amount of a single vibe type until they are defeated.

== Trivia ==
*Contrary to their names, the Calm [[Heart Panel]] depicts a happy face while the Joy Heart Panel depicts a calm face.
