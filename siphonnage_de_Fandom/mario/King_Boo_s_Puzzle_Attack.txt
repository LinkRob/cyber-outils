'''King Boo's Puzzle Attack''' is a boss minigame in ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' in which the characters have to fight [[King Boo]]. In "Solo" mode, the mini-game is introduced by [[Bowser]] in [[Boo's Horror Castle]].

==Gameplay==
This is a 3 in a row puzzle used to attack [[King Boo]]. The game introduces [[Green Shell]]s, [[Red Shell]]s, [[Fire Flower]]s, [[Ice Flower]]s and rarely, [[Lightning]]s. Similar to ''{{w|Bejeweled}}'', the main part of gameplay is swapping the item tiles to have 3, 4 or 5 in a row, worth the respective number of same points.

As a bonus, 3 in a row lightnings are different from each other; they are worth +10 points and -3 points for your rivals.

===Rage===
When King Boo rages, the candles turn from blue to red to express his anger towards the player; he also includes a [[Boo]] to distract the player's cursor; now the player has to try to get the Boo off.

==​Controls==
Hold the {{System Buttons|Wii|Wiimote}} horizontally.

*{{System Buttons|Wii|2}} - Select
*{{System Buttons|Wii|2}} {{System Buttons|Wii|Pad}} - Swap
*Shake {{System Buttons|Wii|Wiimote}} - Ridding of the [[Boo]]
