'''Volleybomb''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In the [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, the player faces [[Salvo the Slime|Salvo]] in Volleybomb on the second floor of [[Duel Tower]].

==Gameplay==
Both characters each hold a [[hammer]], which they use to knock the [[Bob-omb]] at the other side. A character can either hammer the Bob-omb from the ground at an arc or spike the Bob-omb by [[jump]]ing and hammering at it. For computer-controlled opponents, some of them have their own tactics. If the opponent is [[Princess Peach|Peach]], she acts calm in the first two rounds, but in the third round, she moves and jumps around constantly. [[Luigi]] is the opposite, aggressive in the first two rounds, then calm in the third round.

If a character misses the Bob-omb with their hammer, it detonates on the ground, causing the character to lose a heart from their health bar. The Bob-omb can explode on its own after a while, in which case the character whose side it is on loses a heart. Both characters have three hearts in their health bar, and whoever loses all of them loses, ending the minigame and ranking the other character the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Pad}}: Move
*{{button|gba|A}}: Jump
*{{button|gba|B}}: Swing hammer

==Flavor text==
*Rules: ''Volley Bob-ombs and blast your foe! Earn 3 points to win!''

==Trivia==
*Volleybomb is based on the sport {{w|volleyball}}, of which its name is a pun on.
*The Bob-omb is always set to explode on the sixteenth hit, guaranteeing that the character who is not serving gets exploded if they do not achieve a double hit.
