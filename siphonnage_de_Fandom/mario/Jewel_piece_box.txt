'''Jewel piece boxes''' are an object in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Description==
A jewel piece box is a gold chest depicting [[Golden Diva]]'s face on the front. Every regular [[level]] has four jewel piece boxes, each containing a [[jewel piece]]. The location of each jewel piece box differs between difficulty settings.
