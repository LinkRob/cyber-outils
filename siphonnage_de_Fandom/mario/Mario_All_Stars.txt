'''''Mario All Stars''''' was a compilation television show produced by [[DiC Entertainment]] and released on cable television in 1994. It first aired on The Family Channel (now Freeform), then later in edited reruns on the USA Network. ''Mario All-Stars'' featured forty episodes of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', albeit only the animated segments, and all thirteen ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|''Super Mario World]]'' episodes with time-compressed footage. No episodes from ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' were used nor are any live-action segments of ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''.

The introduction to the show contained scenes from the ''Super Mario Bros.'' opening segment from ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' (where [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] are pulled down the [[Warp Zone|Warp Drain]] and roll into the [[Koopa Troopa]]s) as well as clips from the opening to ''Super Mario World''. The opening theme music is a slightly shortened variation of the ending credits theme from ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Trivia==
*''Mario All Stars'' was likely named after ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'', a compilation game featuring [[reissue|remakes]] of the original {{series|Super Mario}} installments for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]].
