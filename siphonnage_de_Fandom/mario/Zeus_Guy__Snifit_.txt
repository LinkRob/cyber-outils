'''Zeus Guys''',<ref>Williams, Drew. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' Player's Guide. Pages 31, 110, 135.</ref> also named '''Scorchits''',<ref>Black, Fletcher. ''Yoshi's Island DS'' PRIMA Official Game Guide. Pages 79, 239, 242, 243, 248, 303.</ref> or '''Fire Snifits'''<ref>''Yoshi's Island DS'' Internal Filename: FireSnifit</ref> are enemies in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. Zeus Guys are [[Snifit]]s with the ability to fire four balls of streamlined fire that harm a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]]. They mainly appear in the latter parts of the game as they first appear in [[Castle of Big Guy the Stilted]]. Zeus Guys mainly attack by walking slowly and breathing streams of fire capable of melting ice blocks (similar to [[Baby Bowser]]), they have two methods of breathing fire, the first involves them breathing the fire stream straightforward and the second involves them moving their head and breathing it up and down diagonally. Zeus Guys are later seen in [[At Last, Bowser's Castle!]], [[Welcome to Yoshi Tower!]], [[A Light in the Dark]], [[Hurry and Throw!]], and [[Let there be Light!]]. They can inadvertently help [[Yoshi]] out by melting unbreakable ice that even [[Baby Bowser]] cannot reach. Unlike most other types of [[Shy Guy]]s in ''Yoshi's Island DS'', they have a many-framed walking animation similar to their [[Snifit]] counterpart from ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', though the only times they are shown walking is when they are on platforms only slightly wider than themselves, essentially just causing them to turn around. When on larger amounts of ground, they stand in place instead. Some of them only shoot forwards, while others will swing their stream of fire upwards and downwards. Zeus Guys can damage [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] upon contact and can be defeated by either stomping on them, swallowing them, tossing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them, using [[Baby Donkey Kong]]'s Dash Attack, and Baby Bowser's fire breath.They can be made to appear in the [[Island Museum]] by tossing a [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them, they appear in the subterranean enemy exhibit.

==Trivia==
*Zeus Guys share their name with the [[Zeus Guy]]s from ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' however those Zeus Guys are a type of [[Bandit]]/[[Green Glove]] rather than a Snifit.
*During the [[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island/Beta elements|development]] of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', Snifits with the ability to breathe fire were considered.
