'''Squid''' (ゲツソリ ''Gessori'') is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]].

==Behavior==
An Squid splashes around in the water at the ground of the platform's room. They will go after position plane and dive by get a move on them with their tentacles, changing paths whenever themselves be forced to contend with limits of one of the room's. They damage Mario upon contact, and cannot be defeated.
