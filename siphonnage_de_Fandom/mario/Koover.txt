'''Koover''' is a [[Koopa Troopa]] and a minor character in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario''==
Koover has the same appearance as a regular Koopa Troopa, and he lives in [[Koopa Village]]. Koover is the Koopa who first greets [[Mario]] and [[Goombario]] when they first arrive in Koopa Village, and he warns them about the [[Fuzzy|Fuzzies]] taking the other Koopas' [[Koopa Shell|shells]]. Shortly afterward, Koover has his shell stolen by a Fuzzy. Once the Fuzzies have been defeated, Koover spends the rest of his time standing in front of the [[Toad House]].

Koover is also involved in a chain letter quest where he asks Mario and [[Parakarry]] to deliver a [[letter]] to [[Fishmael]] who is a fishing buddy of his. Fishmael then writes back the letter to Koover who then wants it delivered to [[Mr. E]] of [[Dry Dry Outpost]]. Koover later sends a letter to [[Kooper]] through the [[Post Office]] indicating they are friends.
