A '''Puppox''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Puppoxes are mechanical puppets only encountered in [[The Factory]]. They are a stronger variant of [[Remo Con]]s.

==Battle==
Puppoxes, in battle, are capable of using several physical attacks. However, they can use [[Eerie Jig]] to turn someone in [[Mario]]'s party into a [[scarecrow]] or [[Somnus Waltz]] to try and put them to [[sleep]].

===Strategy===
Puppoxes are weak to [[Fire (element)|fire]], so any of Mario's fire techniques are effective against them.
