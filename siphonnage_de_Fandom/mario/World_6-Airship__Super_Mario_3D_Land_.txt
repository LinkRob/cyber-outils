'''World 6-Airship''', also known as '''World 6-6''', is the thirty-second level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.

== Layout ==
The first [[Airship]] [[Mario]] lands on features a few spiked pillars that launch at the ship. At the second ship, Mario finds more spiked pillars, but inside, attempting to squish Mario. The third airship, like the second, has spiked pillars launching from the inside. With the level's automatic scrolling, Mario can get caught and squished by the level near these parts especially. Atop this winding path with spikes, Mario finds the [[Warp Pipe]] to [[Pom Pom]].

== Enemies ==
Enemies that appeared in World 6-Airship:
* [[Rocky Wrench]]
* [[Bob-omb]]

== Boss Battle ==
Pom Pom's room is filled with fire and seven rectangular platforms surrounded by these flames. She'll throw boomerangs at Mario like in previous confrontations. After each time she's hit, she goes into a rage, attempting to jump on Mario. After three hits, like the previous fights, she'll lose, and Mario can make his way to the [[Flagpole]] of the level.

== Star Medals ==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is behind the group of crates the at the beginning of the level.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal is obtained by hitting the purple blocks past the second group of spiked pillars in the level.
*'''Star Medal 3''': The final Star Medal is obtained using wall-kick techniques near the end of the level, past the final spike pillar.
