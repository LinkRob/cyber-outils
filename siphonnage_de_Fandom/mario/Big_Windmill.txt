The '''Big Windmill''' is a landmark location of [[Bianco Hills]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. The only source of its name is from a [[Pianta]].

==Description==
The Big Windmill is located on top of a spiraling stone path. It has four wooden blades, each one having a platform attached to it. The blades move slowly in a clockwise pattern, so [[Mario]] can stand on a platform and have it carry him up to the rooftop. There is an alcove in the center of the Big Windmill. The interior of the windmill is empty.

==History==
The Big Windmill only plays a notable role in Episode 2: [[Down with Petey Piranha!]]. Here, [[Petey Piranha]] can be found on the rooftop, and if Mario reaches Petey, a short cutscene plays, during which the rooftop cracks, and both Mario and Petey fall into the Big Windmill. While Petey is also seen atop the Big Windmill in Episode 1: [[Road to the Big Windmill]], Mario can use unconventional means to reach him there by skipping the episode's [[Shine Sprite]] when it appears to instead go to the Big Windmill to fight Petey instead, just like in Episode 2: Down with Petey Piranha!.
