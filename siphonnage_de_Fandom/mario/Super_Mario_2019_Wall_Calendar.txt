The '''Super Mario 2019 Wall Calendar''' (also called '''Super Mario 2019 Calendar''') is an officially licensed [[Nintendo]] product. It is a wall calendar designed by [[Celina Carvalho]] and published by Harry N. Abrams.

== Description ==
The wall calendar uses the format used in ''Super Mario'' games.

On the front cover, [[Luigi]], [[Mario]] and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] are present from left to right. Luigi's artwork is from ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'' that shows him with his common pose. The cover uses a ''[[Mario Party 9]]'' artwork of Mario holding the [[Star]]. It also used the ''[[Mario Party 8]]'' artwork of Toad running. On each month, the theme varies with different artworks. June and December are the only months that use the [[Mushroom]] or [[Star]] above the day.
