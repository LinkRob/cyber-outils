'''Coin Collectors''' is a [[Minigame|Special Game]] in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''. It takes place on [[Mario Classic Court]].

==Gameplay==
The gameplay involves the characters collecting 8-bit [[coin]]s. There are four player positions, although these can be changed by hitting a [[POW Block]] with a tennis ball. In Multiplayer mode, whoever finishes with the most [[point]]s is the winner.
{{sectstub}}

===Difficulty levels===
*Fever (Easy): Collect 1,000 points in one minute. In the ''[[New Play Control!]]'' version, this was changed to 500 points.
*Jackpot (Normal): Collect 4,000 points in two minutes. In the ''New Play Control!'' version, this was changed to 3,500 points.
*Gold Rush (Hard): Collect 10,000 points in three minutes. In the ''New Play Control!'' version, this was changed to 7,000 points.

==Items==
*[[Coin|Gold Coin]]: Worth ten points
*[[Red Coin]]: Worth fifty points
*[[Blue Coin]]: Worth a hundred points
*[[Fireball]]: KO's a character for three seconds
*[[Lightning]]: Minimizes other characters, decreasing their speed
*[[Mushroom (item)|Mushroom]]: Increases the character's speed
*[[Starman|Star]]: Makes the character invulnerable to fire and lightning
