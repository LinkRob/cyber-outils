The '''Mirror Room''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
The Mirror Room is a long room with two chandeliers, two candelabras, and a torch. There is a small drawer against the east wall, where a painting also hangs. A long [[mirror]] spans across most of the north wall, and it is the only mirror in the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]] that does not teleport Luigi to the [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion)|Foyer]] if he scans it with the [[Game Boy Horror]]; this is because the vines obstructing the door do not instantly disappear once [[Luigi]] has completed the room.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
The Mirror Room is accessed from the door on the right side of the [[Fortune-teller's Room]]. When Luigi enters the Mirror Room, the door behind him is sealed from white vines. There are five blue [[Grabbing Ghost]]s for Luigi to fight, but all of them are only visible in the reflection of the mirror. Once the [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] have been captured, the lights turn on, and a red [[treasure chest]] containing the [[Fire Element Medal]] appears. The door's vines do not immediately disappear once the lights turn on, so in order for Luigi to leave, he has to obtain the Fire Element Medal so that he can vacuum in a [[Fire Elemental Ghost]] from a nearby torch and use its effects to unleash fire onto the two candles in the room. Once this is done, the door is unsealed, and Luigi can exit the Mirror Room.

During the [[blackout]], six Grabbing Ghosts can be encountered in the Mirror Room.
