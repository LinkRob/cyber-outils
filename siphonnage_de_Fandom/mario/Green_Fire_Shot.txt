The '''Green Fire Shot''' is the [[Special Shot]] used by [[Luigi]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The Green Fire Shot is Luigi's equivalent of [[Mario]]'s [[Fire Shot]]. As its name suggests, the Green Fire Shot is almost the same as Fire Shot except the basketball has green flames trailing behind it, not orange, and the player can activate the Green Fire Shot by tapping out an uppercase "L" on the Touch Screen.
