'''Bowser's mother''' is a character with only a few appearances in Western ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' media. Her son is [[Bowser]]. Bowser's mother first appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Do You Princess Toadstool Take this Koopa...?]]" and returns in ''[[Mario's Time Machine|Mario's Time Machine Deluxe]]''.
