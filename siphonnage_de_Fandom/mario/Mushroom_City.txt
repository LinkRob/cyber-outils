'''Mushroom City''' is a course in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is the second course in [[Star Cup]] and is filled with [[traffic]]. Every car from [[Mushroom Bridge]] (a related course that is in [[Flower Cup]] in the game) is in this track. Mushroom City shares the same music with Mushroom Bridge, and at one point, Mushroom Bridge can be seen across a lake.

==Walkthrough==
The player starts off on a highway. As the player takes the exit he or she will be lowered into the city streets. The traffic lights appear only in [[Grand Prix]] mode. The traffic lights flash, as if they are not working. Mushroom City is busier than Mushroom Bridge, mainly because this is a city, and the bridge is just a famous part of the outskirts of the city. On the highway the player must stay in the middle of the street to avoid the cars. Some cars, though, cannot hurt the player if he or she is hit, though, but are instead cars that shoot out [[Dash Mushroom|Mushrooms]]. The two exits, where the cars appear, are considered out-of-bounds areas; if the player tries to go down this area, the screen will black out and they will be abducted by the [[Rescue Lakitu]] and dropped close to the Starting Line.

==Shortcuts==
Mushroom City has two shortcuts. One of these is a pink-colored (the other side of the path is purple though) path that is much like an alley, which cuts through a ton of track, putting the player farther ahead by quite a bit, while the other is a narrow dirt path to the side of the road just before the finish line; a [[Dash Mushroom]] can be used to ignore the dirt for the second shortcut.

==Other appearances==
*Mushroom City can be seen in 3 other courses. In Mushroom Bridge, it can be seen directly at the starting line. In [[Wario Colosseum]], you can see it barely through one of the colosseum's archways. In [[Rainbow Road]], when you fall off the edge of the track, you are able to see the city clearly, though throughout the level you can see the city just driving on the track. However, when playing spit-screen, all of these tracks removed their appearances of Mushroom City.
