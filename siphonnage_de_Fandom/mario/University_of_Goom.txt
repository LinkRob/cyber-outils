The '''University of Goom''' is a location mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
[[Goombella]] was a student at the University of Goom at some point prior to journeying with [[Mario]]. Goombella's teacher at the university was [[Professor Frankly]].
