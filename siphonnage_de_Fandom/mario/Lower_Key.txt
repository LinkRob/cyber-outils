The '''Lower Key''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Description==
The Lower Key is a small blue key that appears to have a shape of a dolphin ghost at the end of it (fitting the location it is found in).

==History==
The Lower Key is one of two keys that can be found in [[Blowhole Castle]] with the other being the [[Upper Key]]. The Lower Key is located inside of a purple treasure chest in the center room being guarded by three [[Buffy the Dolphin]]s. This key can be used to unlock a nearby door in a locked room that is dark.
