The '''Painting''' is an item which appears in PC releases of ''[[Mario's Time Machine (PC)|Mario's Time Machine]]''.

==Description==
It was painted by [[George Washington Carver]] and depicts a series of flowers.

==In ''Mario's Time Machine''==
[[Mario]] obtains the painting from one of Carver's students in [[Tuskegee]], during the year 1915. Mario gives the painting to [[Booker T. Washington]], who had been looking for it earlier.
