'''Foo Me Once''', titled '''Cloud Cover''' in PAL releases, is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] appearing in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Layout==
At the start, the game camera slowly scrolls past the seven platforms, each depicting a button. To the left of each button is a [[Foo]], which blows out a stream of clouds as the camera scrolls past. As a result, each of the paths where a platform is placed between is obscured by a stream of clouds.

==Gameplay==
At the start, the players have to memorize which buttons were on each on the platforms, from start to finish. Every round takes place before one of the platforms, where each character has three seconds to guess the button on that platform. Any characters who guess correctly jump onto the platform and continue on to the next round. However, if a character chooses an incorrect button or does not make a choice before time runs out, they are eliminated; if this is from guessing the incorrect button, the way they get eliminated is from failing to land onto a platform and therefore falling down a [[pit]]. Whoever is the last character remaining is the winner, but alternatively, if at least two characters make it to the goal at the end, anyone who made it there are the winners.

The minigame has an associated challenge named No Foolin', in which all four characters must reach the goal without guessing any of the buttons incorrectly.
