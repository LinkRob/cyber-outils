'''Power Bees''' are allies of the [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Story]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Power Bees have a yellow body, purple feet, black hands, black antennae with red tips, and a pair of light blue wings that have a dark purple outline. Despite their name, Power Bees more closely resemble a butterfly, mainly because of the wings on their back.

===Behavior===
Power Bees flutter around, and a Yoshi can eat one to restore health.
