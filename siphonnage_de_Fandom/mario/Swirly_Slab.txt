A '''Swirly Slab''' is an object in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Description==
A Swirly Slab is a circular platform with a spiral on it. If [[Wario]] performs [[Wild Swing-Ding]] while standing on a Swirly Slab, it rises, allowing Wario to reach certain higher and otherwise inaccessible areas.

==In ''Wario World''==
Swirly Slabs are first found in [[Horror Manor]] and return in [[Beanstalk Way]]. In Horror Manor, Swirly Slabs have the appearance of a metal disk, and in Beanstalk Way, a Swirly Slab is contained within flower buds that Wario can open by performing a [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] on it.
