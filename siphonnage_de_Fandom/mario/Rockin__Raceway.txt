'''Rockin' Raceway''' is a 4-player mini-game that appears in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''. It later returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the players must race on rocking horses to the finish line. They must try to maintain their speed and avoid losing all of their carrots or they will spin out. There are also two areas where power-ups are place to give the players infinite carrots allowing them to go fast without the worry of spinning out. This could only happen if they get the good carrot. If they get the bad carrot, they will spin out. The first player to reach the end of the finish line wins.

==Controls==
*{{button|n64|a}} - Rock forward
*{{button|n64|b}} or {{button|n64|z}} - Rock backward
Note: The player must switch from pressing {{button|n64|a}} and {{button|n64|b}} (or {{button|n64|z}}) in order to move the horse.

==Trivia==
*If there are multiple players close to the finish line when the winner crosses it, a replay will always be shown. However, if the player that finishes the minigame wins with a bigger lead over the other players, then there won't be a replay shown.
