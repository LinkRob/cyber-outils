'''A Banquet with Hisstocrat''' is the final level of World 3 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. Although it is classified as World 3, it serves more as a buffer between that World and [[World 4 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 4]].

== Layout ==
This level features the first time the player faces the boss [[Hisstocrat]]. Hisstocrat attacks by making rocks fall down and gets worms to help not him but you.

== [[Green Star]] ==
*'''Green Star 1''': Defeat [[Hisstocrat]] to get the Star.
