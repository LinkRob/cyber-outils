'''Cool Kong''' is an enemy in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Cool Kongs are a type of [[monkey robot]] that wear a pair of pointed, purple shades. They have a "C" on their necktie. Cool Kongs are about twice as large as [[Mini Mario (toy)|Mini Marios]], but their necktie is around the same height as them.

===Behavior===
A Cool Kong moves back and forth on a platform, usually ones inaccessible to the Mini Marios. If a Mini grabs a Cool Kong's tie, it shrinks to half its size, and in some cases causes the Cool Kong to travel under low platforms as well. If a Cool Kong is found in an open area, its lowered head can possibly serve as a safe platform for another Mini.

==In ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''==
Cool Kongs are first encountered in [[Magnet Mania (Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis)|Magnet Mania]]. A Mini Mario can grab a Cool Kong's tie and hold on to it for as long as desired as it moves around. The Minis are often required to hold on to a Cool Kong's tie, as this allows them to get across long obstacles, such as water and [[Spike (hazard)|spikes]]. A Cool Kong is often required to reach a [[Goal Door (Mario vs. Donkey Kong series)|goal door]].
