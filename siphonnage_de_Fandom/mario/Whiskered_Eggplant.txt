The '''Whiskered Eggplant''' is an item in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
The Whiskered Eggplant appears only in Challenge mode, where it is required for [[Waluigi]] to use his [[Star Skill]], the [[Liar Ball]]. The item costs 200 [[coin]]s to buy from the [[Yellow Toad]] at the shop, where it is only available if Waluigi is on the player's team.

The Whiskered Eggplant is a purple eggplant that sports a mustache resembling Waluigi's.

It is said that anyone who touches it “loses their lunch”.
