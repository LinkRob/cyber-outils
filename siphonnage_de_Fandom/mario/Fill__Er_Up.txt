'''Fill 'Er Up''' is a [[microgame]] in [[Ashley and Red]]'s [[Total Drag]] stage in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player has to drag the handles down so that the candy flows into the jar until it reaches the threshold, indicated by a red line with an up and down arrow on the right. After the jar is filled enough, the player must push the handles back up to close the hatch. If the jar is filled with the right amount of candy, the player wins. If the jar is filled past the threshold, it overflows, and the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level has a different type of candy, which become increasingly difficult to pair together.

*'''Level 1:''' There are large gumballs to drop into the jar.
*'''Level 2:''' There are medium jellybeans to drop into the jar.
*'''Level 3:''' There are small star candies to drop into the jar.
