"'''All Steamed Up'''" is the third live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'', and it is paired with the animated episode "[[Butch Mario & The Luigi Kid]]."

==Synopsis==
[[Sgt. Slaughter]] has a broken [[Steam-O-Matic]] steam cabinet, so he goes to [[Mario Bros. Plumbing]] to ask [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] to repair it.
