'''This Beans War''' is the second level of [[Hoohoo Mountain]]  in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 1. 

After the final battle, [[Captain Goomba (Mario & Luigi series)|Captain Goomba]] finds two more [[Goomba]]s who eagerly join his squad. He also doubts his leadership abilities, but he regains confidence as the Goombas compliment him.

==Layout==

Battle 1 consists of 6 [[Beanie]]s. The enemy captain is a Beanie.

The final battle consists of 7 Beanies. The enemy captain is a Beanie. 

After the final battle, the player recruits two more Goombas. However, they are only recruited for the first play through of the level.
