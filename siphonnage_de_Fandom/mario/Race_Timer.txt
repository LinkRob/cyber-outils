The '''Race Timer''' shows how much time one raced around a track in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. It is always at the top right hand corner of your game screen. The design of the timer changes from game to game. The value of the timer is in 1/100th of a second in the first 3 games, then 1/1000th of a second in later games.
