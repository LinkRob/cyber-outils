The '''Sleep Stomp''' is a [[badge]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge for two [[Badge Point|BP]], and he can use it as an attack in battle by using two [[Flower Point|FP]].

Sleep Stomp is found in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] as it is one of the many badges that [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] sells to [[Mario]]. This attack is just like most [[Boots]] attacks as it does not change the damage but instead inflicts a status ailment on the enemy. As its name would suggest, the Sleep Stomp puts an enemy [[Sleep|asleep]] upon impact.
