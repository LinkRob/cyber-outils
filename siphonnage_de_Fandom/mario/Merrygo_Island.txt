Found in the [[Color-Full Sea]] after the linking of [[Desolatt Island]], it is the sixth island to be linked to [[Shipshape Island]]. As its name implies, Merrygo Island features a theme park. [[Technikki]] makes her debut here.

== History ==

=== Background ===
According to the island's residents, [[Technikki]] constructed Merrygo Island's maze, setting her workshop near an exit. When [[Zokket]] and his [[Zokket Army|minions]] destroyed the former [[Uni-Tree]], sundering [[Concordia]], the maze was isolated and got turned into Merrygo Island. [[Princess Peach]] and [[Starlow]] were eventually brought here.

=== ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' ===
When the Bros. connect [[Desolatt Island]] to the new [[Uni-Tree]] on [[Shipshape Island]], [[Starlow]] arrives, claiming that she and [[Princess Peach]] have traveled to [[Concordia]]. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] then head over to Merrygo Island, where Princess Peach supposedly appears. Technikki is also encountered here, where she promises to assist the residents of Shipshape Island, and reveals that Peach is swarmed by enemies near the lighthouse, which the Bros. take care of. Mario and Luigi then link Merrygo to Shipshape.

The [[Monster Maniac]] can be found here with strengthened [[Krode|Krodes]] called Krode R's, and can be visited in one of the Monster Mania side quests.

== Enemies ==

* [[Snaptor]] (Some appear as minibosses.)

* Krode R (optional boss that the [[Monster Maniac]] summons)

All enemies contract [[Glohm]] when [[Reclusa]] [[Reclusa World|reawakens to conquer]] [[Concordia]].
