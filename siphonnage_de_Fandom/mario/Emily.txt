'''Emily''' is a character in the [[Mario Tennis (series)|''Mario Tennis'' series]]. She made her debut appearance in ''[[Mario Tennis (Game Boy Color)|Mario Tennis]]'' for the [[Game Boy Color]].

==In the ''Mario Tennis'' series==
In ''Mario Tennis'' for the Game Boy Color, Emily is an unlockable playable character of the Speed type, and her doubles partner is [[Kevin]]. She can be unlocked by winning against her in the Mario Tour by Singles in [[Island Open]].

Emily made a reappearance in ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]'' in which she is the coach of the Senior Class at the [[Royal Tennis Academy]].
