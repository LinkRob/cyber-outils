The '''Standard BW''' is an orange [[kart]] that belongs to [[Bowser]] in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. His other karts are [[Tyrant]] and [[Hurricane]]. This kart has the best speed and weight of all of the standard karts, while having the worst acceleration and handling, average drifting, and an average item stat.
