'''Feather''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When any user equips the Feather, their [[Speed]] is increased by 20. The item can be obtained in in [[Nimbus Land]] by avoiding getting pecked by a [[Dodo]].
