|name=Worn-Out Jump x3 (Battle Card)}}'''Worn-Out Jump x3''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.
==Description==
The Worn-Out Jump x3 is the weakest version of [[Mario]]'s "Jump x3" attack. It is three of the [[Worn-Out Jump]]'s stacked on top of each other. There is an orange/white striped background.
==Price==
Buy Price: N/A

Sell Price: 6 coins (unpainted)

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Worn-Out Jump x3 is first seen in [[Ruddy Road]], but can also found in [[Cherry Lake]], [[Indigo Underground]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Kiwano Temple]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Sacred Forest]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Violet Passage]], [[Vortex Island]], [[Fortune Island]], and [[Toad Trainworks]].
