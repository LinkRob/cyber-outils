'''Tree Stomp''' is a 1-vs-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Opening==
The camera "stomps" through the forest as stomping noises are heard. The solo player is shown getting shocked, and the camera zooms out to reveal the three other players in their tree stump robots.

==Gameplay==
In the minigame, the team controls giant tree stump robots modified for movement. The solo player must run around, avoiding contact with the team's machines. If two robots collide, they crash and malfunction for about two seconds before control is returned. Sometimes, [[Ukiki]]s throw [[Banana Peel|banana peel]]s onto the field, causing anyone who touches them to slip. Ukikis also throw [[Golden Banana|golden banana]]s, which speed up any character who touches them. The solo player wins if they survive for 45 seconds. The team players win if they eliminate the solo player.

==Ending==
If the solo player wins, the tree stump robots controlled by the team players break apart, and the camera zooms in on the solo player as they do a winning pose. If the team players win, they do winning poses.

==Controls==
===Solo (1 player)===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Move

===Group (3 players)===
* {{button|gcn|Stick}} &ndash; Change direction
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Forward
* {{button|gcn|B}} &ndash; Back

==Flavor text==
* '''Game Rules''' &ndash; ''"Three players control robots while the other player runs. The runner must stay alive for 45 seconds to win."''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"Grabbing a golden banana will make you faster, but be careful how fast you get going!"''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame's name is a pun on "tree stump."
