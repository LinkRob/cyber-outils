|games = ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''}}'''Triangle Trample''' is a special, musical technique used by [[Chunky Kong]] in the game ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''. Chunky can purchase this technique along with his triangle from [[Candy's Music Shop]] in [[Frantic Factory]].

==Usage==
Like with the other musical attacks, Chunky's Triangle Trample can be used on Chunky's music pads to trigger events or to defeat all enemies on the screen.
