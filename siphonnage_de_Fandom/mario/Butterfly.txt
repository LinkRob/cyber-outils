'''Butterflies''' are a real-world species of insects that have sometimes appeared in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] as well as the Yoshi games. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Print World]]''. Butterflies usually only appear in the background or as part of a scenery.
