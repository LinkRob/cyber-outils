A '''Mini Yoshi''' is a wind-up toy which appears in ''[[Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge]]''. It is designed after a [[Green Yoshi]].

==In ''Mini Mario & Friends: amiibo Challenge''==
Mini Yoshi becomes playable when the player uses a [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] [[amiibo]]. Like other playable Minis, Mini Yoshi has its own special ability, which is eating enemies such as [[Shy Guy]]s and [[Piranha Plant]]s by walking into them and sticking out its tongue at them, like a Yoshi itself.
