==History==
The Water Tablet is one of three tablets found in the [[Gap of Crag]], along with the [[Fire Tablet]] and the [[Stone Tablet]]. The Water Tablet can be found at the bottom of a small pool of water. The Water Tablet is then used along with the other tablets to summon the [[Pixl]], [[Cudge]], who is needed to proceed through the Gap of Crag.
