'''Rolling Rumble''' is a [[Coin Chaos]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''. It, [[Tiles and Tribulations]], and [[Goomba Gold Rush]] are the only Coin Chaos minigames available after the [[Coinathlon]] mode is unlocked.

==Gameplay==
The minigame is comparable to [[Bumper Balls]] from ''[[Mario Party]]'', as it involves each character rolling on their own ball, although they instead have to knock off opposing [[Shy Guy]]s on balls. Shy Guys on smaller balls are worth one coin, while Shy Guys on large balls are worth five coins. Occasionally, [[Koopa Troopa]]s on balls show up, and their ball contains a [[Red Coin]], which can be obtained by knocking off the Koopa Troopa. If a player character falls off the course, it takes time for them to respawn. When player characters retrieve an item box, a Koopa Troopa on an item box ball needs to be knocked off to obtain the item.

==Levels==
*Level 1: Shy Guys on small balls are very common, and a Shy Guy on a large ball only appears occasionally.
*Level 2: Koopa Troopas on Red Coin balls appear alongside more Shy Guys.
*Level 3: Many Koopa Troopas on Red Coin balls and Shy Guys appear.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|Stick}}: Move character
*Press and hold {{button|3ds|A}}: Build momentum
*Release {{button|3ds|A}}: Dash

==Flavor text==
*''Knock down your foes!''

==Trivia==
*In a possible oversight, the minigame instructions do not mention that the Control Stick is used to move the character.
