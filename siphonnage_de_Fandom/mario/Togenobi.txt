'''Togenobi''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''. They only appear in one [[level]], [[Mystic Lake]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Togenobi is a red octopus with four rotating tentacles, each carrying a mace.

===Behavior===
A Togenobi swings its tentacles around to try and hit [[Wario]].
