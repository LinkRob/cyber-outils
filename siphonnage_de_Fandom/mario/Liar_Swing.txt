'''Liar Swing''' is the [[Star Swing]] used by [[Waluigi]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces the [[Liar Ball]] from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', specifically for batting, as the Liar Ball is only used as Waluigi's Star Pitch. 

==Description==
Upon using the move, Waluigi forms his trademark Γ and hits the ball. The ball zig-zags across the field a few times before stopping, usually outside the foul line. Using the Liar Swing can be ineffective if an opponent catches the ball while it is moving. Unlike some other Star Swings, the Liar Swing cannot stun opponents.
