The {{Nihongo|'''Legion of Stationery'''|ブンボーぐんだん|Bunbōgundan|lit. Stationery Legion}}<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.nintendo.co.jp/switch/aruua/battle/|title=360° Battle!|work=Nintendo|language=Japanese|accessdate=2022-11-13}}</ref> is a group of giant sentient office tools allied with [[King Olly]] in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. They are the game's main bosses aside from King Olly himself, with five of them guarding the ends of the streamers wrapped around [[Peach's Castle]] while the sixth one serves as Olly's personal guard. They were originally inanimate tools belonging to the [[Paper Toad|Origami Craftsman]] before King Olly took them, enlarged them, and made them sentient.

== History ==
{{spoiler}}
After Mario defeats a member of the Legion of Stationery, their Collectible Treasure form will be available for purchase from a [[Rocky Wrench]] at a pier behind a red warehouse in [[Toad Town]]. Each member will also become available for a timed refight using a Bossotronic Fighter in the town's Battle Lab.

In a series of photos that scrolls through during the credits, the penultimate one shows all members of the Legion of Stationery in the Origami Craftsman's workshop in their original, inanimate, regular-sized forms.
{{endspoiler}}

==Trivia==
* Most of the members of the Legion of Stationery include a color in their design that corresponds with the color of the streamer that they are guarding.
** Guarding the red streamer, the lid of Colored Pencils' tin case is red.
** Guarding the blue streamer, the Rubber Band wears a few blue rubber bands across its body.
** Guarding the yellow streamer, Hole Punch has a yellow lever.
** Guarding the purple streamer, Tape's dispenser is purple.
** Guarding the green streamer, Scissors has green handles.
** Stapler is exempted from this rule since it does not guard a streamer, so it is colored entirely black.
