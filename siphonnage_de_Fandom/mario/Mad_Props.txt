'''Mad Props''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Gameplay==
Players use their hovercrafts through the winding river. The only obstacles that the characters have to avoid are [[rock]]s sticking out of the water. Handling of the [[boat]] is controlled by the left and right propellers: activating only the left propeller causes the boat to turn right, while using only the right propeller causes it to turn left. Using both propellers causes the boat to drive in a straight line. The tightness of the steering is determined by how hard the triggers are pressed: more force on the triggers turns the propellers faster and thus steering more. Whoever crosses the [[finish line]] first wins, but if neither character reaches the end within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|L}}: Control left propeller
* {{button|gcn|R}}: Control right propeller

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Use the boat's propellers to navigate through the white-water rapids.''
* '''Advice''': ''The harder you press, the faster the props will turn.''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The title of the minigame is a pun on the saying "mad props," meaning to give someone a lot of respect and credit, with the fact that "props" is short for "propellers."
