A '''Spring Ball''' is a type of object featured in the ''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]'' platformers. It first appears in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''.

==Description==
Whenever a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] bounces on a Spring Ball, it makes a noise and bounces him upward, functioning similarly to [[Springboard]]s from the [[Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series]]. Spring Balls come in two sizes, small and large, with the latter bouncing a Yoshi even higher. Spring Balls are often necessary to reach higher areas. A Spring Ball can sometimes be found within certain [[Winged Cloud]]s or on top of certain soft patches of ground, and in both cases, a Yoshi can reach the Spring Ball by throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at whatever it sits behinds.
