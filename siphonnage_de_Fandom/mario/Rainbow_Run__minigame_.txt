'''Rainbow Run''' is a 1-vs-3 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''.

==Gameplay==
The solo player on the narrow rainbow must get to the goal line, while three players on the clouds attempt to knock the rainbow runner off with [[cannonball]]s. If the solo player gets to the goal, that player wins, but if either the team of three knocks the rainbow runner down or time runs out, the team wins.

Although it has a different setting, Rainbow Run is identical in concept to [[Tightrope Treachery]] from the first ''[[Mario Party]]''. Unlike in Tightrope Treachery, team players' clouds automatically move instead of having players control them.

==In ''Mario Party 2''==
To get Rainbow Run, the player must buy 50 minigames from [[Woody]]. Rainbow Run does not appear in the [[Mini-Game Coaster]], along with [[Dungeon Dash]].
