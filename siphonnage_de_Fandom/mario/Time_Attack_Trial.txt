'''Time Attack Trial''' is one of the Time Attack challenges of [[Challenge Mode (New Super Mario Bros. U)|Challenge Mode]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It takes place on an original course, depicting an [[Acorn Plains]]-type setting.

Time Attack Trial has the simple platforming objective of [[Mario]] having to reach the [[Goal Pole]] within 35 seconds while avoiding enemies along the way, including [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Koopa Paratroopa|Paratroopas]], and [[Big Piranha Plant]]s.

==Medal requirements==
*'''Gold''': Complete in 21 seconds or less
*'''Silver''': Complete in 25 seconds or less
*'''Bronze''': Complete in 35 seconds or less
