'''Mini-Game Land''' is a mode in ''[[Mario Party 2]]''. It allows players to buy and play [[minigame]]s and minigame modes and check minigame records. In Mini-Game Land, players can buy minigames from [[Woody]] and then play them in the [[Mini-Game Park]]. The minigame modes are [[Mini-Game Stadium]], where players can play [[Mini-Game Battle]], [[Mini-Game Trial]] and [[Mini-Game Duel]], and [[Mini-Game Coaster]], where a player can choose a character for themselves and a partner to play 2 vs. 2 minigames and play them in turn as they go down the trail.
