A '''Mad Banzai Bill''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mad Banzai Bills are a variant of both [[Mad Bullet Bill]]s and [[Banzai Bill]]s, and they resemble the latter have angrier eyes and are engulfed in fire.

===Behavior===
A Mad Banzai Bill is imbued with the [[Rage]] [[vibe]], and it charges faster than regular Banzai Bills, similarly to how Mad Bullet Bills travel faster than their parent species, [[Bullet Bill]]. Mad Banzai Bills are fired off-screen, like with Mad Bullet Bills, meaning they have no corresponding [[Bill Blaster]]. 

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Mad Banzai Bills are first encountered in the level [[Ladida Plains 1-8]]. They are only found in special levels.
