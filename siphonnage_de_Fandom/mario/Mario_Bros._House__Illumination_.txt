'''Mario and Luigi's House'''  is the current home in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] that [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] reside within in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== History ==
=== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' ===
The house is seen at the end of the movie, where after [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]] defeat [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] in [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]], the two decide to move out of their [[Mario and Luigi's Family|family's]] [[Mario and Luigi's Apartment (Illumination)|apartment complex]] and take up residence in [[Mushroom Heights (Illumination)|Mushroom Heights]].

Here, Mario can be seen waking up in the house, before getting ready with Luigi and exiting to race to a [[Warp Pipe|personal pipe]] for their plumbing jobs.
