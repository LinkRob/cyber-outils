The '''Nimbus''' are a humanoid species with cloud-like bodies in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Their homeland is [[Nimbus Land]], a monarchy ruled over by [[King Nimbus]] and [[Queen Nimbus]]. The most notable member of the species is their son, [[Mallow]], who serves as one of [[Mario]]'s party members.
