'''Chicken!''' is a Duel minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
In [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]] mode, Chicken! is played against [[Mouser (Mario Party Advance)|Mouser]] on the third and final floor of [[Duel Tower]].

==Opening==
Both characters move out of the way as a [[Thwomp]] slams to the ground between them.

==Gameplay==
The object for each character is to try and stay under the Thwomp for as long as possible, but they must also avoid being squished by it. It is possible to tell how close the Thwomp is to falling by how large the shadow in the center of the tower is. Each character can try to trick their opponent by walking out of the shadow and going back in. If one character flees, their opponent has a chance to get out to win. The winner is determined by whoever either does not get flattened by the Thwomp or stays under it for a longer period of time. If both characters are flattened, the minigame ends in a [[draw]].

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|A}}: Flee
*{{button|gba|B}}: Fake out

==Flavor text==
*Rules: ''Thwomp's about to fall! Stay under it the longest, and you win!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name references the game of {{w|Chicken (game)|Chicken}}, which involves lasting for as long as possible without yielding to another competitor.
