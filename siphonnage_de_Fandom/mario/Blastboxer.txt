}}A '''Blastboxer''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearance==
Blastboxers are stronger versions of [[Boomboxer]]s and can mainly be found in [[Castle Bleck]]. Like it's other counterparts, Blastboxers attack with a powerful soundwave that can travel a far distance. They have rather high HP and attack so one should take caution of fighting them.
