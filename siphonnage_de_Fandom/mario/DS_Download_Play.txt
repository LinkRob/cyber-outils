'''DS Download Play''' is a local wireless communication feature between two or more [[Nintendo DS]] units. It is commonly used for multiplayer, but the system itself has also made use of it for its built-in PictoChat application. DS Download Play has been utilized as early as the launch title ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==In ''Mario Kart DS''==
This is a multiplayer feature used in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. In the game, there are two ways to play with more than one player. There is Normal mode, when multiple game cards wirelessly play, and Simple mode, or Download Play. In this mode, gameplay is very restricted, allowing only the courses in the [[Mushroom Cup]] and [[Shell Cup]]. You cannot change the [[Engine Class]]. In addition, only the [[Nintendo DS (Mario Kart DS)|Nintendo DS]], [[Tart Top]], and [[Pipe Plaza]] stages may be used. Also, players who don't own a game cartridge can only use [[Shy Guy]]. Download Play is also used to describe single-card play in other games for the Nintendo DS.
