"'''The Fish That Should've Gotten Away'''" is the first comic ever created in [[Valiant Comics]]'s ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]'' imprint and one of the ''[[Super Mario Bros. (Valiant Comics)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' comics. Its title is a play on the term "the one that got away," which is used in fishing to describe the loss of a catch.

==Plot synopsis==
Mario, while leaping over a lake from a pier, begins to bemoan the fact that he is being chased by a large horde of enemies. However, Mario remains optimistic, believing that nothing else can go wrong for him today.

Suddenly, after he stomps on a [[Clawgrip]] and avoids a [[Piranha Plant]], Mario is surprised to see a talking fish, who introduces himself as [[Stanley the Talking Fish]], and follows Mario. Oblivious to Mario's predicament with the dozens of nearby monsters, Stanley begins to pester Mario about his girlfriend, [[Barbara the Bush]]. Stanley asks Mario for advice about his relationship with Barbara, as some [[Trouter]]s begin to tell Mario to ignore the talkative Stanley, while trying to eat him.

Managing to reach some nearby land, Mario begins trying to get Stanley to go away while avoiding various creatures, with Stanley still wanting dating advice. Seeing a nearby [[Warp Pipe]], Mario jumps into it as Stanley still tries to talk to him.

Inside the Warp Pipe, Mario finds himself in a dark area filled with speaking trees and bushes, who, at the mention of Stanley, begin to swoon and ask Mario if he knows Stanley. Mario tells the nearby bushes that he knows Stanley; he taught him everything he knows.

==Notable mistakes and errors==
*Excluding [[Stanley the Talking Fish|Stanley]], the [[Trouter]]s featured in the comic have light-purple fins and lips, though later appearances depict the fins and lips as yellow. Additionally, the [[Gunion]]s in the comic are colored green instead of red, while the [[Fryguy]]s are colored completely yellow.
*In ''[[The Best of Super Mario Bros.]]'', the symbol on [[Mario Cap|Mario's cap]] changes from pink to blue throughout the comic.

==Trivia==
*According to an info page in ''[[Nintendo Comics System No. 1]]'', the story was drawn live in front of journalists during a January 1989 conference where [[Valiant Comics|Valiant]] announced the ''Nintendo Comics System'' line. Held at the defunct {{wp|Palladium (New York City)|New York Palladium}}, the artists drew on larger boards and used darker inks on the background to make the art more visible to the attendants.<ref>[https://archive.org/details/NintendoComicsSystemBookCollection/Nintendo%20Comics%20System%2001%20%281990%29/page/n39/mode/2up Nintendo Comics System Book Collection]</ref>
*Some of the enemies chasing [[Mario]] in the comic are [[Gunion]]s, making this the only ''[[Super Mario Bros. (Valiant Comics)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' comic to include any ''[[Super Mario Land]]'' enemies, outside of the ''[[Game Boy (comic)|Game Boy]]'' comics.
*At the start of the comic, Mario says that he is being chased by [[Spark]]s. Despite Mario saying this, no Sparks actually appear in the comic.
