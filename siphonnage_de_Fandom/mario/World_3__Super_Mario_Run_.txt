'''World 3''' is the third [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. It is the only free world, and completing it unlocks [[World 4 (Super Mario Run)|World 4]].

==Levels==

=== [[Big Spiny Blitz]] ===
A desert level with many Big Spinies and a Lakitu.

=== [[Bullet Bill Barrage]] ===
A sky level with Bullet Bills and Missile Bills.

=== [[Shell Me the Way!]] ===
A grassland level with many Koopa Troopas, Goombas and Brick Blocks.

=== [[Fire Bar Castle! Youch!]] ===
A castle level containing Fire Bars and Podoboos, as well as the game's third boss battle, against a Fake Bowser.
