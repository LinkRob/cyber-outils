'''Shhwonk Fortress''' is a fortress in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' where the [[Moon Stone]] and the [[Sun Stone]] are kept.

== History ==
It is located beyond [[Petalburg]] and a few smaller stone fortresses. The [[Sun Stone]] and the [[Moon Stone]] are found inside are used to open the pipe to [[Hooktail Castle]]. They say that long ago, Shhwonk Fortress was a place of relief from monster invasions for Petalburg residents. Past this, however, monsters invaded the fortress, and since then, traversing to them was actually outlawed without permission from [[Kroop]]. The fortress is estimated to be about a thousand years old, and seems to have been built around the time of the [[Thousand-Year Door]].
