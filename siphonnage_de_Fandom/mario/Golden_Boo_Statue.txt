The '''Golden Boo Statue''' is a key item in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Description==
The Golden Boo Statue is a symbol of the friendship shared by the three [[Boo]]s at the [[Boo Cemetery]]. They try to keep the Golden Boo Statue a secret from outsiders.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
At some point, the statue is stolen, and the quest [[True-Blue Boo]] involves figuring out who stole the Golden Boo Statue, which the Boos ask the player's character and [[Shroomlock]] to find. Since there are four Boos instead of the usual three, Shroomlock concludes that one of them has to be the thief. Once the player's character correctly identifies the false Boo, revealed to be [[Koopa Kid]], he loses his disguise and runs away, leaving the Golden Boo Statue behind. As thanks, the Boos reward the player's character with the [[Predictotron]] [[Gaddget]].
