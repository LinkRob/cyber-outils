'''Goma''' is a character who only appears in [[Leafy Greens]], a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Goma is a snail with a yellow body and a pink shell with a dark swirl on it. Its two eyes are represented as two white squares with a black outline hovering above its face, which has a visible mouth.

===Powers and abilities===
Goma has the ability to [[jump]], unlike other characters. If it eats a leaf, Goma grows to a massive size.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
[[File:Leafy Greens.png|thumb|left|200px|Goma's appearance in Leafy Greens in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']]
Goma stars as the playable character of the microgame Leafy Greens. Here, the object is for Goma to eat the leaf, which enlarges it to a massive size. If Goma falls down a [[pit]] or does not reach the leaf on time, the player loses at the microgame.

The only time Goma is named is in the microgame's corresponding info, which refers to Goma by its full title, "Goma the snail."

===''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''===
Because Leafy Greens, along with every other microgame in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', was ported to this game, Goma retains its exact role in Leafy Greens.
