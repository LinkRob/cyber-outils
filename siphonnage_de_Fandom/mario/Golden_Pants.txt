'''Golden Pants''' are a clothing item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. It can only be equipped on [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]], and doing so increases their defense by 48. The Golden Pants can be either obtained from the [[Thwomp Caverns]] or as an item dropped from [[Gold Koopeleon]]s.
