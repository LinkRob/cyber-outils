'''Toad's cousin''' is a turban-wearing [[Toad (species)|Toad]] character who only appears in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Mind Your Mummy Mommy, Mario]]." His job is to sell newspapers in [[Desert Land]]. He is one of [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]]'s cousins, along with [[Mushroom Marauder and Jake the Crusher Fungus]].
