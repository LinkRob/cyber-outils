'''Chikakos''' are robotic enemies featured in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Land''==
[[File:Chikako SML sprite.png|frame|left]]
Chikakos only appear in the final level of the game, [[World 4-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 4-3]]. Here, they are found floating up and down in the air. While Chikakos do attack [[Mario]] directly, they can be an annoyance as an obstacle. A Chikako can be defeated by shooting ten missiles at it using the [[Sky Pop]]. The player earns 800 [[point]]s whenever a Chikako is defeated.
