The '''Bottom of the Well''' is a location in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'', though it technically is not part of the mansion itself. It is cleared in the third Area.

==Layout==
The Bottom of the Well is the smallest area of the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|mansion]]. It is accessed from a ladder inside the well in the [[Courtyard]]. It has only one short path to the right, where the hidden side of a lion statue is, providing a look into the [[Secret Altar]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
When [[Luigi]] enters the location, he peers through the mouth of a stone lion into the Secret Altar, where he sees [[Mario]] trapped in a painting guarded by [[King Boo]]. After Luigi is seen trying to reach his arm out, he stops looking through the lion status, and a [[Ceiling Surprise]] appears behind him, or a [[Purple Bomber]] in the [[Hidden Mansion]] of PAL releases and the Nintendo 3DS version. It is the only ghost encountered, so after Luigi captures it, the [[Key]] to the [[Rec Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Rec Room]] appears.
