'''Vibe Tea''' is an item in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
Vibe Tea is a drink that permanently increases [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Vibe Gauge]] by a quarter of its original size whenever she drinks it.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
There are only four instances in which Peach can drink Vibe Tea during her journey. Vibe Tea initially costs 250 [[coin]]s, but it becomes increasingly expensive with each purpose, costing 500 coins the second time, 1,000 coins the third time, and 1,500 coins the fourth and final time.
