'''Splat-a-Stamp''' is a Free-for-All [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

==Opening==
Each character stands near their slingshot while applause is heard in the background. The camera then zooms in to the stamp.

==Gameplay==
Players have to use the [[stylus]] to fling paint balls onto a stamp to cover it with ink. The minigame randomly decides on the stamp, which can be either a [[Bullet Bill]], a [[Bob-omb]], or a [[Shy Guy]]. It is possible for other characters to override another character's ink with their own color instead. After 30 seconds, the [[time limit|timer]] runs out, and the ending follows.

==Ending==
[[File:Splat-a-Stamp_Results.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Results screen]]
The stamp floats into the air and flips upside down as a piece of paper with the stamp outline comes in. The paper is then stamped with the ink splatted onto it, and each character's percentage is calculated, ranging from as low as 0 to as high as 100. Whoever has the highest percentage is the winner.
