'''Balloon Bash''' is a multiplayer mode in ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''.

== Description ==
Balloon Bash is a mode where players must pop balloons and gain [[Star]]s.

The player must select their character before playing Balloon Bash. However, if the player places an [[amiibo]] on their [[Nintendo 3DS]], the amiibo character will bring a special item. The player must select a map before starting the game.

Balloon Bash's gameplay is similar to [[Toad Scramble]]. In Balloon Bash, all players roll regular [[Dice Block]]s no matter the character. If one player runs into a balloon, a minigame triggers. If two players run into balloons, a boss minigame will trigger. Balloon Bash ends after completing a certain number of rounds.

Like Toad Scramble, the game announces the Bonus Stars. Three Bonus Stars are chosen randomly. Depending on the criteria of the Bonus Star, the players will gain a Star. After three Bonus Stars are given, the game determines the player with the most stars and announces the player as the winner. Depending on the amount of stars earned, the player may earn a "Star Rush" or "Super Star Rush".
