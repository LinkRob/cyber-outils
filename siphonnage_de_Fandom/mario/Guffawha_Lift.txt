'''Guffawha Lifts''' are a type of platform found in [[Guffawha Ruins]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. These platforms are similar to [[Donut Lift]]s and function almost identically to them with the exception that they have statue faces imprinted on them rather than having a hollow center. 

==History==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
Guffawha Lifts are seen in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. In this game, they appear in [[Guffawha Ruins]] replacing the [[Donut Lift]]s found in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. Like the Donut Lifts from before, they will descend when [[Luigi]] steps on them so he must jump from platform to platform quickly. They are first seen along the ground and [[Luigi]] must hit a [[! Block]] to raise the Guffawha Lifts upward and from here, he must jump from platform to platform to get to the other side. Whenever [[Luigi]] stands on a Guffawha Lift, it descends so he must be quick or else he will have to repeat the process again.

==Trivia==
*Guffawha Lifts are the first variation of [[Donut Lift]] to be stoney in appearance as most variations are made of rubber or wood.
