'''Poison Pokeys''' are a variant of [[Pokey]] which appears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are poisonous, as stated by their name.

==Physical description==
Poison Pokeys are very similar in appearance to [[Pokey Mummy]]s from ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They are tall and have a light green body.

==In {{series|Paper Mario}}==
===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
Poison Pokeys can mainly be found in [[Riverside Station]] alongside [[Ruff Puff]]s and a few other enemies. They are also encountered on the lower floors in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].

===''Super Paper Mario''===
[[File:PoisonPokeyCard.png|thumb]]
Poison Pokeys can be found in [[Castle Bleck]] or, more specifically, the [[Castle Bleck Inner Sanctum|Inner Sanctum]].

==Battle==
Poison Pokeys mainly attack by slamming their bodies at Mario and lobbing their body segments at him as well. There is a chance that if Mario doesn't guard against the Poison Pokey's attacks, then it could possibly leave him [[Poison (status effect)|poisoned]].

===Strategy===
Unless he has the [[Spike Shield]] badge equipped, Mario should stick to using Hammer moves on Poison Pokeys. The Poison Pokeys are usually seen in groups so the Quake Hammer should be used. Also, having [[Koops]], [[Vivian]], or [[Bobbery]] as partners work as well (though Koops is a better choice).
