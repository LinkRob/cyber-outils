==Description==
Roto-Discs are strange, circular, plasma-ball-like objects that are commonly found in castles. They usually spin around an orb and require precision to avoid, like a [[Fire Bar]] or a [[Ball 'n' Chain]].

==Appearances==
==={{series|Super Mario}}===
====''Super Mario Bros. 3''====
[[File:Spinning Roto-Disc.gif|frame|left]]
In ''Super Mario Bros. 3'', Roto-Discs are the functional replacement of Fire Bars. They spin around an orb, and [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] must wait for the plasma ball to spin out of the way before proceeding or else he gets hurt. It is possible for Mario or Luigi to stand on the orbs. They appear only in [[fortress]] levels, often near pits of [[lava]], especially those with leaping [[Podoboo]]s, and in narrow hallways requiring even more precision to avoid. Roto-Discs are nearly invulnerable, but they can be destroyed if [[Statue Mario]] lands on top of one.

'''Duo-Roto-Discs'''<ref>''Nintendo Power'' Volume 13, page 59.</ref> can also be found, and they consist of two Roto-Discs circling the central orb. If Mario or Luigi destroys one, the other disappears.

====''Super Mario Land''====
Roto-Discs return as obstacles in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. They function the same way as in ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. They are commonly found in the [[Chai Kingdom]]. Roto-Discs are fully indestructible and cannot be destroyed by even the effects of a [[Starman]]. Furthermore, all Roto-Discs spin at the same speed and are uncommon in the game.

===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
[[File:Roto-Disc Classic.png|thumb|Mario near a Roto-Disc in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga'']]
Roto-Discs also appear in the [[Mini-Mario]] course of [[Bowser's Castle]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They are obstacles that send Mario back to the beginning of the area if they connect with him, and cannot be destroyed at all. Given that Roto-Discs only appear in a Mini-Mario course, [[Luigi]] never interacts with them.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[Wario's Woods]]'' for the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]], activating a combo causes an energy orb to appear and refill [[Birdo]]'s time gauge. The sprite used for the orb is the same as the Roto-Discs, only with a different palette.<ref>https://twitter.com/MarioBrothBlog/status/1298715131715567616</ref>
*A sign reading "ROTO DISC" appears in the 1993 ''[[Super Mario Bros. (film)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film. According to production designer {{wp|David Snyder|David L. Snyder}}, the Roto-Discs were imagined as the energy-conversion engines used in the electric [[Dinohattan]] cars.{{citation needed}}
*''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]'' also contains an enemy known as a [[satellite]] in the [[Mario Zone]], and it behaves in a similar fashion.
