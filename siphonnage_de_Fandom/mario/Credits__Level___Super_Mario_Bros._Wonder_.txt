'''The Credits''' is the last level of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]'' that you can play after beating [[The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage]]. It has many [[Wonder Effect|Wonder Effects]] in it. It, and [[The Final Battle! Bowser's Rage Stage|Bowser's Rage Stage]] are the only stages (not counting The Final-Final Test: Badge Marathon) without a [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]], as both of them have a Wonder Effect since the beginning.
