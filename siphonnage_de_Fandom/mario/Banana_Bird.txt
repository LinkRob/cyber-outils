The '''Banana Bird''' (''Bananus goldus flutterus'') is a species of banana-like bird which first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]''. They are found in caves and released when playing a "Simon Says"-like game with crystals.
