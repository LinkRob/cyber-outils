'''Piercing Blow''' is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear the Piercing Blow for one [[Badge Point|BP]], and in doing so, Mario can use this attack in battle for two [[Flower Point|FP]]. Piercing Blow is similar to the regular [[hammer]] attack, although this version pierces through enemy defenses.

The Piercing Blow is located in the [[Lovely Howz of Badges]], where it can be bought for 75 [[coin]]s.
