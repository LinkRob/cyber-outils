'''Doris 1''' is a house maid robot invented by [[Dr. Crygor]]. She first appears in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' and reappears in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare Gold''===
Doris 1 is first seen in [[Agate Forest]], where she states that she was abandoned by her creator, Dr. Crygor. When she sees Dr. Crygor, she begins to chase him through the woods. The player plays through Dr. Crygor's [[microgame]] set while this occurs. Whenever they win a microgame, Doris 1 trips over, allowing Dr. Crygor to briefly run further away before Doris 1 catches back up to him and the next microgame starts. If the player loses a microgame, Doris 1 is briefly seen hitting Dr. Crygor with her mop. In the ending, Doris 1 is later taken back to [[Dr. Crygor's Lab]].

===''WarioWare: Get It Together!''===
Doris 1 is first seen in the intro for [[High Tech]], where she cleans [[Dr. Crygor's Top Secret Factory]], being cheered on by [[Flatterbot]]s. In the intro for [[Penny's Mix]], Doris 1 can be seen in a field with some [[Alien Bunny|Alien Bunnies]] as [[Penny Crygor|Penny]] flies off with the [[Jet Tank One]].
