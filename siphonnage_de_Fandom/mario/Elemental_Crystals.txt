The '''Elemental Crystals''' are supporting bosses who fight alongside [[Culex]] appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. The group consists of the [[Fire Crystal]], the [[Earth Crystal]], the [[Water Crystal]], and the [[Wind Crystal]], all four of which are a recurring element from the ''[[Final Fantasy]]'' franchise, the flagship property of [[Square Enix|Squaresoft]], the game developers behind ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''.

==Battle==
In battle, each of the Elemental Crystal have their own unique spell to use.

===Fire Crystal===
{{main|Fire Crystal}}
The Fire Crystal, as it name states, is capable of using strong fire based moves such as [[Flame Wall]] and [[Flame (move)|Flame]]. Though it is weak to Ice-based moves, its magical attack is rather high as well as its health, but its defense is quite low. It is considered even more difficult to take out than the Water Crystal.

===Earth Crystal===
{{main|Earth Crystal}}
The Earth Crystal has the highest amount of defense out of the four Elemental Crystals, though, it has a rather low magical attack. Its HP is quite high. The Earth Crystal can use deadly earth-based moves such as [[Boulder]] and [[Sand Storm]], and it is weak to [[Thunder]] attacks.

===Water Crystal===
{{main|Water Crystal}}
The Water Crystal, even given its name, almost exclusively uses Ice-based moves, such as [[Blizzard (move)|Blizzard]] and [[Ice Rock]]. It has medium attack status but a rather high defense.

=== Wind Crystal===
{{main|Wind Crystal}}
The Wind Crystal has the highest [[Defense]] out of all the Elemental Crystals. It also has low [[HP]] but decent [[Attack]]. The Wind Crystal uses many thunder-based moves such as [[Static E!]] and [[Electroshock]]. It can also use the dreaded [[Petal Blast]] to turn one of [[Mario]]'s party into [[Mushroom (ailment)|Mushroom]].
