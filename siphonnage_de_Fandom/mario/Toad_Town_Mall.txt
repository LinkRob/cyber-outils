'''Toad Town Mall''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''.

==Description==
Toad Town Mall is a shopping area consisting of several shops, including the [[Badge Shop]], [[Gear Shop]], [[Item Shop]], [[Mushroom Ball Derby]], [[Star Shop]], [[Tea Kiosk]], and [[Toadles Boutique]]. Additionally, it is where the [[Toadley Clinic]] is located.
