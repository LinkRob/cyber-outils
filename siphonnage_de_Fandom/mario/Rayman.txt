'''Rayman''' is the main protagonist of the ''[https://raymanpc.com/wiki/en/Rayman_(series) Rayman]'' series by [[Ubisoft]]. 

== Biography ==
Rayman is a human/vegetable hybrid who has no arms, legs or neck, though he has hands, feet, and a head that are able to move independently from his body. Because of his lack of arms, Rayman is able to throw his fists in long-range punches at his enemies, and in some games is even able to project balls of energy from his hands. He is able to glide by spinning his hair like helicopter blades. He is typically found wearing white gloves, a red neckerchief on a purple body with a white ring in the center and yellow trainers. Rayman is well known for saving his world from various villains such as shape-shifting sorcerer, robot pirates, dark lums, and psychotic bunnies, also known as [[Rabbids]].

== History ==

=== ''Mario + Rabbids'' series ===
==== ''Rayman in the Phantom Show'' ====
In ''[[Rayman in the Phantom Show]]'', Rayman receives a golden ticket inviting him to the [[Space Opera Network]]. Arriving from a crashed rocket shell Rayman used to travel, he encounters a [[Stooge]] attacking him and then exclaims that he knew that the invitation was fishy, and that a [[Rabbid]] was behind it. After defeating other enemies, Rayman exits the Repository and heads to the [[Network Reception]]. There, he meets [[Rabbid Peach]], [[Rabbid Mario]], and [[Beep-0]], where they seem to be in the process of checking in.
