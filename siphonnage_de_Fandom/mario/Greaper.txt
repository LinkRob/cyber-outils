'''Greapers''' are ghost-like [[Shy Guy]]s which appear in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They resemble a cross between a Grim Reaper and a [[Shy Guy]]. They can be only found in the [[Sunken Ship]]. They also have a variant known as the [[Glum Reaper]].

==Battle==
In battle, Greapers mainly attack with their scythes. They are also capable of special moves such as [[Crystal]].

===Strategy===
Being ghosts, Greapers can be quickly taken out using a [[Pure Water]].
