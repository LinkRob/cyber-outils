The '''Pianta judge''' is a [[Pianta]] character who appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''.

==''Super Mario Sunshine''==
The Pianta judge presides over [[Mario]]'s court trial when he was accused of vandalizing [[Isle Delfino]] by a [[Shadow Mario|mysterious figure who looks like him]]. Around the end, when [[Princess Peach]] and [[Toadsworth]] try to attempt to object, but Pianta judge overrules them, pleads Mario guilty, and orders Mario to clean all of [[Isle Delfino]]. The Pianta judge then adjourns the court case. After Mario defeated [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]], restore all [[Shine Sprite|Shine Sprites]] at the [[Shine Gate]], the judge is seen dancing alongside other [[Pianta]]s and [[Noki|Nokis]] in [[Delfino Plaza]].

==Physical description==
The Pianta judge is a light green Pianta who wears glasses. The Pianta judge's white hair and mustache indicate that he is of elderly age.
