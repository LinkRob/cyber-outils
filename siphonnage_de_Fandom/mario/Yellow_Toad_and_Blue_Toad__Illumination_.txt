==History==
As [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] reach [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Peach's Castle]], Yellow and Blue Toads outside the door refuse to let them in, Toad distracts them using his frying pan to let Mario sneak in guards inside the castle. They were later, seen blowing their trumpet horns when Mario and Peach are leaving for the Jungle Kingdom.
