'''Nibbles''' are [[Chain Chomp]]-like fish which appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. They try to attack [[Mario]], although he cannot engage them in combat. Nibbles appear only in water areas, including rivers, ponds, the ocean, and even swimming pools.

== In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ==
Whenever Mario falls into the water, a Nibbles attacks him, dealing one [[Heart Point|HP]] of damage. Like most overworld obstacles that deal damage, Mario can be defeated by a Nibbles. If Mario is in his sailboat form, Nibbles do not try to attack him.

==Trivia==
*In the original ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', the Nibbles chomps at Mario right as he’s thrown out of the water however in the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], the Nibbles reveals itself a few seconds after Mario is thrown out.
