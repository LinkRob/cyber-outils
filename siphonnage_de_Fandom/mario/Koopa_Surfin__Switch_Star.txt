'''Koopa Surfin' Switch Star''' is the fifth mission of [[Dire, Dire Docks]] in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' only. The objective is to activate a [[Star Switch]] to spawn [[Power Star]] within a [[Star Sphere]] and then collect it before the timer expires, an objective shared with another mission in each of the main courses.

==Layout==
[[Wario]] is the only character who can complete the mission because he has to break a [[Black Brick]] to reveal a [[Koopa Shell]]. Upon activating the Star Switch, Wario has to get on the Koopa Shell to quickly travel over to the opposite end of the course and land on to the Star Sphere containing the [[Power Star]] so that he can obtain it before it disappears
