}}The '''Zip Zip''' ('''Rapide''' in Europe and Australia) is a medium-sized [[bikes|Bike]] in [[Mario Kart Wii|''Mario Kart Wii'']]. The bike has very good [[acceleration]] and [[handling]]. The bike also has very good [[Off-Road]]. The Zip Zip must unlocked by winning the [[Engine Class|100cc]] [[Lightning Cup]], or by playing 2,100 games. The bike's drift is not the best, but it has a good [[Mini-Turbos|Mini-Turbo]]. It is the staff ghost bike for [[Mario Circuit]] (Mario, Nin*==Kony) and DS Yoshi Falls (Yoshi, Nin*DoTak).
