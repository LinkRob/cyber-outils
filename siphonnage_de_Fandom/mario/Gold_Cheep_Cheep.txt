A '''Gold Cheep Cheep''' is a golden variant of a [[Cheep Cheep]] most frequently appearing in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

== In the ''Mario Party'' series ==
In their minigame appearances, Gold Cheep Cheeps are worth more points than a regular Cheep Cheep.

=== ''Mario Party 4'' ===
Gold Cheep Cheeps appear in [[Cheep Cheep Swap]]. If the player catches on of them, the player gains two points.

=== ''[[Mario Party 5]]'' ===
Gold Cheep Cheeps appear in [[Fish Sticks]]. If the player catches on of them, the player gains two points.

=== ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ===
There is a Gold Cheep Cheep who is a [[Coach (Mario Party Advance)|Coach]] in the [[Mushroom Pool]] at [[Seaside Area]].

=== ''[[Mario Party 10]]'' ===
Gold Cheep Cheeps appear in [[Cheep Cheep Leap]]. If the player hits one of them, the player gains three points.

=== ''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]'' ===
Gold Cheep Cheeps appear in the [[Coinathlon]] minigame, [[Cheep Cheep Reach]]. They are worth more coins than regular Cheep Cheeps.

=== ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' ===
Gold Cheep Cheeps appear in [[Net Worth]]. If the four players manage to lift the net at the same time with a Gold Cheep Cheep, it will count three points.

== In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' ==
Gold Cheep Cheeps will appear after the player touches the [[Gold Ring]]. Gold Cheep Cheeps will leave trails of [[coin]]s, but can still be defeated in the same manner as regular Cheep Cheeps, by shooting a [[fireball]] at it as [[Fire Mario]].

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
While Gold Cheep Cheep does not appear in ''Mario Kart Tour'', there is a [[kart]] based on it named [[Gold Cheep Charger]].
