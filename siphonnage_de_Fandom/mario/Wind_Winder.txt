'''Wind Winders''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Wind Winders are green and yellow creatures who wear opaque white glasses and use a propeller to hover in midair.

===Behavior===
Wind Winders blow gusts of wind at [[Wario]] to try and impede his progress or put him in the attack path of other enemies. Wind Winders cannot damage Wario directly.

==In ''Wario World''==
Wind Winders are only encountered in [[Beanstalk Way]]. Wario can perform a single punch or a [[Corkscrew Conk]] to ground and defeat a Wind Winder.
