'''iOS''' is an operating system created by Apple for their various products, most notably iPhones and iPads. The main competitor of the iOS is [[Android]]. As of the mid-2010s, [[Nintendo]] has started releasing games and software for iOS devices, a few of which are ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games.

==List of ''Mario'' games==
*''[[Super Mario Run]]'' (compatible with iOS 7 or higher)
*''[[Dr. Mario World]]'' (compatible with iOS 8.4 or higher)
*''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' (compatible with iOS 9 or higher)
