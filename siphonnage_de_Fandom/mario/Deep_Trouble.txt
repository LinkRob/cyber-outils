'''Deep Trouble''' is the first level of [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 9.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 8 [[Fuzzbush]]es. The enemy captain is a Fuzzbush.

The final battle contains 2 [[Troopea]]s and 6 Fuzzbushes. The enemy captain is a Fuzzbush.

No allies can be gained from this level.
