A '''Blue Virus''' is one of the three main color variations of [[Virus]], the other two being [[Red Virus]] and [[Yellow Virus]]. All three types debut in ''[[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]]'', and they usually appear alongside one another.

==In the [[Dr. Mario (series)|''Dr. Mario'' series]]==
===''[[Dr. Mario (video game)|Dr. Mario]]''===
[[Dr. Mario (character)|Dr. Mario]] can line up four pieces of a blue [[Megavitamin]] on a Blue Virus to destroy it.

==In the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]==
===In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''===
It is a component of the Virus enemy, alongside Red Virus and Yellow Virus. However, each of them have their own stats. Attacking a Red Virus turns it into a Blue Virus, which turns into a Yellow Virus if attacked itself.

==In the [[Mario Kart (series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]==
Blue Viruses appear in the ''Mario Kart Arcade GP'' subseries, in the form of two items, [[Wheel Virus]] and [[Dizziness Virus]]. It is the only Virus color variation to appear.
