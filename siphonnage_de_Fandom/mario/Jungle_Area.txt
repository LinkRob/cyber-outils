{{Nihongo|'''Jungle Area'''|ジャングルエリア<ref>{{cite web|url=https://youtu.be/LTUTO2AR7Fs|title=一人用マリオパーティアドバンスpart2|author=かげらいまどかK|work=YouTube|language=Japanese|date=2017-10-28|accessdate=2023-04-20}}</ref>|Jangurueria|Jungle Area}} is a grassy area in [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. It appeared in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.
== Description ==
Jungle Area is a grassy area full of trees, stumps and flowers. It consists of places including:
* [[Bowser Hideout]]
* [[Bowser Lab]]
* [[Dance Stage]]
* [[Jungle Game Hut]]
* [[Loch Dorrie]]
* [[Petal House]]
* [[Spear Thicket]]
* [[Ukiki House]]

== In ''[[Mario Party Advance]]'' ==
The Jungle Area is [[Princess Peach|Peach]]'s starting point in Shroom City.
