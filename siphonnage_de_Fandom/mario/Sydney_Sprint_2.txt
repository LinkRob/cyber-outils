'''Sydney Sprint 2''' is a race course from ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. As its name implies, it is the second layout of the [[Sydney Sprint]] course that takes place in Sydney, Australia.

== Layout ==
The racers start this route facing south in Port Jackson, visiting the Port Jackson Bridge and the Mary Booth Lookout Reserve. They go backwards down [[Sydney Sprint 1]], but rather than going through the 270-degree loop and heading southeast towards the Sydney Opera House, they divert onto a winding dirt road within the Mary Booth Lookout Reserve, tagging a glide ramp to get back to Port Jackson. They turn right and rejoin with Sydney Sprint 1 in reverse.

This route is used as the second half of the second lap of [[Sydney Sprint]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', with the first lap following Sydney Sprint 3.
