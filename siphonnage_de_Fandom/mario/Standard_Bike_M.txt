==''Mario Kart Wii''==
This bike can be ridden by medium weight characters. Including [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Princess Daisy]], [[Birdo]], [[Diddy Kong]], and [[Bowser Jr.]]
