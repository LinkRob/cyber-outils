'''World Castle''', also known as '''World 7''', is the seventh [[world]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. It comes after [[World 6 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 6]] and precedes [[World Bowser]]. World Castle is ruled over by the Red [[Sprixie Princesses|Sprixie Princess]].

== Layout ==
[[File:World Castle overview.png|thumb|250px|A map overview of World Castle]]
World Castle is mainly a lava-filed world featuring elements from [[Bowser's Castle]].

==Trivia==
*This is the first world of ''Super Mario 3D World'' that does not have a number in its title.
*This world is potrayed as the final world, despite world [[World Bowser|bowser]] being the true one.
