The {{Nihongo|'''B Dasher'''|Bダッシュ|B Dasshu|B Dash}} is a kart that appears in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart series]]''.

A similar kart appears in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', the [[Sprinter]] (also known as the [[B Dasher MK2]]). The [[Circuit Special]] from ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' also bears a striking resemblance to the B Dasher, so both the Sprinter and Circuit Special can be considered to be spiritual successors for the B Dasher.

== Description ==
The kart is shaped like a [[w:c:wikipedia:Formula One car|Forumla One race car]], colored white with a singular red stripe on the front. It has a window shield in front of the seat and steering wheel. It is also equipped with a large gray engine block at the rear of the kart, with four gray exhaust pipes.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
=== ''Mario Kart DS'' ===
[[File:B Dasher (Mario Kart DS).png|thumb|185px|right|Mario in his B Dasher in ''Mario Kart DS''.]]
The B Dasher debuted in ''Mario Kart DS'', as [[Mario]]'s exclusive kart in the game. His other karts include the [[Standard MR]] and [[Shooting Star]]. It may be his signature kart, as it was shown on the ''Mario Kart DS'' box cover.

The kart is balanced in all stats, except [[Weight (Mario Kart series)|Weight]] and [[Items (stat)|Items]], which are the highest stats. The kart offers good [[Speed]] and [[Acceleration]], with average [[Handling]] and [[Drift]].

It shares its speed with [[Princess Daisy]]'s [[Power Flower (Mario Kart DS)|Power Flower]]. It shares acceleration with [[Princess Peach]]'s [[Royale]]. Its weight is equivalent to [[Luigi]]'s [[Streamliner]] and [[Donkey Kong]]'s [[Standard DK]], but only if it is used by the latter. It shares its handling and drift with [[Waluigi]]'s [[Zipper]].

If Princess Daisy and Waluigi are unlocked, then completing all cups in the 150cc Nitro or Retro Grand Prix will give them the option to use the B Dasher, and all racers can use the B Dasher once all cups in Mirror Mode have been completed. Until then, only Mario can use the B Dasher.

A CPU-controlled Mario chooses the B Dasher to race in more frequently than his other karts.

On an interesting note, the B Dasher lacks its window shield. It is unknown if this was an unintentional mistake.

=== ''Mario Kart 7'' ===
[[File:Metal Mario MK7.png|220px|thumb|right|[[Metal Mario (character)|Metal Mario]] next to his B Dasher in ''Mario Kart 7'', whilst leaning on some [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires.]]
The B Dasher returns as a [[Retro Kart]] body in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It is noticeably more powerful than its original appearance in ''DS'', as it has more speed and handling, but hinders acceleration and off-road. It is among one of the most popular karts to use within the game.

A computer-controlled Mario has the tendency to use the B Dasher kart body, equipping it with [[Slick]] or [[Roller]] tires with the [[Super Glider]]. He may also equip the B Dasher with [[Mushroom (tires)|Mushroom]] tires along with either the [[Paraglider]] or [[Flower Glider]].

Equipping the B Dasher with the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires will resemble the original ''DS'' version.

The B Dasher, equipped with [[Slick]] tires and the Super Glider as Mario is used as the Expert Staff Ghosts for courses [[Mario Circuit]] and [[Mario Circuit 2|<small>SNES</small> Mario Circuit 2]].

=== ''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Deluxe'' ===
[[File:B_Dasher_MK8_screenshot.jpg|thumb|220px|right|[[Red Yoshi]] driving the B Dasher on [[Mario Circuit|<small>GBA</small> Mario Circuit]].]]
The B Dasher makes a return in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' in the first DLC pack. It gives a boost in speed and weight but decreases acceleration and traction. 

In the [[Nintendo Switch]] enhanced port, ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'', the B Dasher can be unlocked by collecting a random amount of coins. Also, the B Dasher acceleration and traction is slightly increased, whilst handling is decreased.

During races, a computer-controlled Mario may use this kart body with the [[Standard (tires)|Standard]] tires and Super Glider. Like ''7'', equipping it with Standard tires will also resemble the original version from ''Mario Kart DS''.

=== ''Mario Kart Tour'' ===
[[File:MKT Icon BDasher.png|thumb|185px|right|The B Dasher as it appears in ''Mario Kart Tour''.]]
The B Dasher appears in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Its appearance is different from previous installments, and uses the [[Pipe Frame]]s tires from this game. 

This kart was introduced during the [[New York Tour]]. It is classified as a High-End kart. It increases the duration of [[Dash Panel]] speed boosts and the points given for performing them.

The game also introduces four variants, such as the [[Black B Dasher]], [[Kabuki Dasher]], [[Red B Dasher]] and the [[Black Kabuki Dasher]]. These variants are also classified as High-End karts.

== Crossover appearances ==
=== ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' ===
[[File:SSBU B Dasher.png|thumb|right|170px|The B Dasher as a Support spirit in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate''.]]
The B Dasher makes an appearance in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' as an Ace-class support [[w:c:supersmashbros:Spirit|spirit]]. If a fighter equips the spirit, the spirit will slightly increase the power of the fighter's neutral special.

Like most spirit battles, the battle parameters are intended to match the spirit. In B Dasher's spirit's case, Mario's wedding costume from ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' matches the kart's default color scheme. The "Uncontrollable Speed" rule matches its low acceleration and poor traction.

== Trivia ==
*The name ''B Dasher'' may be a reference to the classic ''Mario'' games in which the B button was used to make the player move faster.
*The B Dasher is the first kart in the ''Mario Kart'' series to be influenced by the Forumla One racecar. The other vehicles influenced by the latter in the series are;
**The Sprinter debuting in ''Mario Kart Wii''.
**The Circuit Special debuting in ''Mario Kart 8''.
**The Black B Dasher, Kabuki Dasher, Red B Dasher, Black Kabuki Dasher, [[Carrot Kart]], [[Radish Rider]] and the many color variations of the '''B Dasher Mk.2''' in ''Mario Kart Tour''.
