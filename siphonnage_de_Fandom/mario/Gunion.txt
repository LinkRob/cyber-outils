'''Gunions''' are octopus-like enemies which appear in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''. They are only encountered in [[World 2-3 (Super Mario Land)|World 2-3]] of the [[Muda Kingdom]].

==History==
Gunions simply float around in the water, where they attempt to shoot [[Mario]] with balls. Mario can easily take out Gunions using missiles from his [[Marine Pop]]. Defeating a Gunion earns the player 800 [[point]]s.
