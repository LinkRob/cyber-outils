'''F-Zero''' is a [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''. It is one of [[9-Volt]]'s microgames, featured under the [[Nintendo Classics]] folder.

==Layout==
The microgame takes place in [[Mute City]], where the [[Blue Falcon]] and some other vehicles are racing.

==Gameplay==
The player must steer the Blue Falcon left or right to dodge obstacles until the timer runs out. If done successfully, they win the microgame. The Blue Falcon is nearly out of energy, so if it crashes into another racer or is against a wall for one second, the player loses the microgame.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels feature three, four, and five enemy racers respectively.
