'''Boat Mario''' is a transformation in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
Boat Mario is one of the four field transformations that can be used. [[Mario]] can use the Boat Mario transformation once he is cursed by the [[Black Chest Demon]] in the [[Pirate's Grotto]]. However, Mario must locate a Boat Mario pad in order to use the transformation. Boat Mario isn't used that much in the game due to its late appearance; however, it is put to good use in the Pirate's Grotto allowing Mario to cross over the waves and eventually reach [[Cortez]].

==Trivia==
*Boat Mario replaces [[Sushie]]'s swimming ability from ''[[Paper Mario]]''.
