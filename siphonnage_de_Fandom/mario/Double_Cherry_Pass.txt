'''Double Cherry Pass''' is a level of World 2 in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Near the first clear pipe, destroy the crate on the top-right corner to get the star.
*'''Green Star 2''': With at least one clone or additional player, be on the two giant P switches (all pressed together) after passing the first group of Blocksteppers.
*'''Green Star 3''': With at least three clones and/or other players, be on the platform by the flagpole that has a "4" on it to have it rise to the star.

== Stamp ==
[[File:Double_Cherry_Pass_Stamp.png|thumb]]

After the checkpoint, go to the three "?" blocks on the far left. With at least a clone or additional player, have one of them on the left "?" block and the other under that same block. Have the person under punching that block repeatedly to make the other go higher to reach the Fire Peach stamp.
