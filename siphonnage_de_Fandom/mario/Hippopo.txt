A '''Hippopo''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. They are strange, light blue, hippo-like creatures that are only encountered in [[The Factory]], often alongside [[Li'l Boo]]s and [[Ninja (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Ninjas]].

==Battle==
In battle, Hippopos are mainly capable of using many spells such as [[Mega Drain]], [[Blast]], and [[Sand Storm]].

===Strategy===
Hippopos are weak against thunder-based attacks meaning [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] and [[Shocker (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Shocker]] techniques are very effective against them.
