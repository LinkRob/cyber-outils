'''Fire Power'''<ref>''Super Mario 64 DS'' English instruction manual, page 19.</ref> is [[Yoshi]]'s [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] power-up in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

==Description==
Getting a Power Flower or eating fire allows Yoshi to [[Fire Breath|breathe fire]], though only the former lets him do so as much as he wants for a brief time.

The power-up itself has the most use in ice courses such as [[Cool, Cool Mountain]] and [[Snowman's Land]], due to its ability to melt blocks of [[Ice Block|Ice]]. It can also defeat any enemy on contact, making it very useful when there are enemies in Yoshi's way.

Yoshi's fire breath is required to complete two [[mission]]s: [[Frosty Slide for 8 Red Coins]] and [[Yoshi's Ice Sculpture]], since the other player characters—[[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Wario]]—cannot break blocks of Ice.
