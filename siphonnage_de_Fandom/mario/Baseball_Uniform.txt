The '''Baseball Uniform''' is an item introduced in the version 1.2.0 update of ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

==Description==
The Baseball Uniform is a white-and-blue striped uniform with a blue belt, and it resembles a typical baseball player's outfit. However, the Baseball Uniform has a red "M" on the front to indicate that it is an outfit of [[Mario]]'s. The Baseball Uniform is paired with the [[Batting Helmet]], which similarly resembles the helmet worn by a baseball player.

==In ''Super Mario Odyssey''==
The Baseball Uniform can be bought for 3,000 [[coin]]s at [[Crazy Cap]] after completing the main game.

==In ''Mario Kart Live: Home Circuit''==
The Baseball Uniform makes a cameo in [[Mushroom Fields]]' icon, which shows a rear view of [[Mario]] wearing it and the Batting Helmet while driving through the course.
