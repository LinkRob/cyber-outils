==History==
The Dramalama Plaque is the top prize that is earned from winning the Drama Slam in [[Jazzafrazz Town]]. [[Luigi]] noticed a piece of the [[Marvelous Compass]] was embedded inside of the Dramalama Plaque and decided to enter. Thanks from assistance from [[Hayzee]], Luigi was able to win the plaque and the piece of the Marvelous Compass.
