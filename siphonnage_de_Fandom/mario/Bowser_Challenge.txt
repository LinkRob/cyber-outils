'''Bowser Challenge''' is a bonus game found in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.
==Dialogue==
[[Bowser Jr.]] is the host of this bonus game.
*Hey, Dad! It's time for the Bowser Challenge!
*You'll play 10 Bowser Battles to see how many hearts you can get!
*You get a bonus of 3 hearts whenever you knock someone out, so play hard!
(Right before you play, you'll see the records of previous attempts. After that, Bowser jumps in the portal.)
After playing all Bowser Minigames:
*Yeah! That's my dad! Let's count how many hearts you collected!
*Next I'll announce the knockout bonus!
*All right, Dad! Let's change your throne to something a little more appropriate!
==Gameplay==
*The player will play 10 Bowser Minigames. Each time a player lose one heart will count as you collect one heart. If you knock a player out (lose all 6 hearts) you'll get a knockout bonus of 3 hearts.
*After completing all the minigames, you'll see your result and a box shows you how many players you knocked out, then add to your result.
*You can't choose COM players or COM difficulty. Only one human player will play with the GamePad. COM players can be anyone, sometimes even characters that you haven't bought yet.
*Each player will have 6 hearts, lives lose per attack depends on the minigame.
*The lowest rank is "Keep practicing" at less than 15 hearts, after that announcement Bowser holds his head sadly, because the throne is still just stone. The highest rank is "The King" requires the player to get 200 hearts or more, the throne now changes to gold, which is technically impossible without knocking players out, due to the fact that it's impossible to remove exactly 5 hearts from each player in certain minigames.
