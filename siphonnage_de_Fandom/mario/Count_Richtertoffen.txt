A '''Count Richtertoffen''',<ref name=website>''Wario Land 3'' website</ref> alternatively named '''Richtertoffen II''',<ref name=website/> is an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Count Richtertoffen are rotund creatures which wear a blue shirt, which has a black arrow design on it, and black pants.

===Behavior===
A Count Richtertoffen attacks by jumping up, then dropping to shake the ground or crush [[Wario]] to transform him into [[Flat Wario]] temporarily.

==In ''Wario Land 3''==
Wario encounters Count Richtertoffen in three different [[level]]s. He first encounters one in [[Out of the Woods]], followed by two more in [[Cave of Flames]], and lastly two more Count Richtertoffen in [[The Warped Void]]. Aside from this, Count Richtertoffen are featured in the Action Help menu to demonstrate Wario's new moves against a heavy enemy.

Once Wario has acquired the Super Grab Gloves from [[The East Crater]], he can lift a Count Richtertoffen. He can defeat a Count Richtertoffen from using either a [[Dash Attack]] or a [[Ground Pound|Smash Attack]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Count Richtertoffen and its alternate name are both a pun on the {{w|Richter magnitude scale|Richter scale}}, which is used to measure the power of earthquakes, as well as a pun on the pilot {{w|Manfred von Richthofen}}.
