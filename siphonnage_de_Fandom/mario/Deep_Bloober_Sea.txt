'''Deep Bloober Sea''' is a [[board]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Description==
Deep Blooper Sea is an underwater-themed board inhabited by [[Blooper]]s, as the name would imply, as well as [[Cheep Cheep]]s and other sea creatures. There are a few junctions here, but the main one is looked over by a [[Sushi]] in a submarine. Every time players get here, the shark makes them press one of four buttons before taking the path they chose. Three buttons do nothing, allowing safe passage, but one fires a torpedo at the player, blasting them to the opposite path. Additionally, the buttons do not reset until the incorrect one is chosen.

Landing on a [[Event Space|Happening Space]] near the giant [[Baby Blooper]] makes it call for its mother, whose huge tentacle takes the player to the other side of the board. [[Action Time]] occurs whenever someone lands on a Happening Space next to a giant vacuum [[Blowfish]]. The arrow on the Blowfish spins around, then points to the bottom, the front, or the right. Then, the Blowfish starts inhaling all players in front of it. Players need to tap the A button to swim against the suction. Anyone who fails is sucked into the Blowfish, then spat out in the direction the arrow points to, landing near a Sushi, [[Boo]], or back at the start.

==In ''Mario Party 3''==
In [[Story Mode]], the human player must go up against three opponents to get the Kindness [[Star Stamp]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Deep Bloober Sea's name is a reference to the original name of [[Blooper]]s and is a pun on the phrase "deep blue sea".
