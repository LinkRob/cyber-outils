A '''Flower Box''' is an item which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
Using a Flower Box increases the party's current [[Flower Point|FP]] by 5 and restores all FP upon being used the process. However, Flower Boxes are extremely rare, and can only be found by defeating certain bosses. The Flower Box restores the most FP out of any [[Flower (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|Flower]] item.
