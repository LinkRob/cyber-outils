==History==
The Rareware Coin must be collected in the game as it is needed alongside the [[Nintendo Coin]] in order to get to the final [[Boss Key]] in the game. The Rareware Coin can only be collected by playing [[Cranky Kong]]'s ''[[Jetpac]]'' game after collecting 15 [[Banana Medal]]s and picking it up whenever it appears.
