The '''Dyllis Dinner''' is a [[Recipes|recipe]] made by [[Dyllis]] in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. 

== Description ==
It is almost identical to the [[Zess Dinner]] from ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and even the [[Dyllis Lunch]]. The Dyllis Dinner is made by mixing a [[Fried Shroom Plate]] and the [[Meat Pasta Dish]], the upgraded [[Spaghetti|Spaghetti Plate]]. It restores 30 [[Heart Point|HP]], more HP than the two ingredients separately and cures [[Poison (ailment)|poison]].
