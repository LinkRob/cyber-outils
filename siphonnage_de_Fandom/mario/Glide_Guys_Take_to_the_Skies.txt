'''Glide Guys Take to the Skies''' is the sixth level of [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', an [[Orange Yoshi]] takes control in this level. Like [[Hit the M Blocks!]], this level is shown to take place at night once again retaining a good view of the sea. This level utilizes [[Baby Peach]]'s wind-floating abilities and primarily involves utilizing the wind to reach greater heights, this level also has several [[Glide Guy]]s which also utilize the wind and marks the debut of the [[Baseball Boys]] as well as the [[Koopa Troopa]]s in this game.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off the level, advance to the right and use the wind to fly skyward to the ledge above (going right is also an option but you will miss some key collectibles if you do) whilst avoiding the [[Glide Guy]]s, there is a [[Stork Stop]] here, you can use this to switch to [[Baby Mario]] to hit the [[M Block]] (and grab the [[Coin]]s by it) to grab the nearby [[Baby Coin]] and the [[Red Coin]] by it. After this, immediately switch back to [[Baby Peach]] as you will be needing her for the rest of the level, glide upward from here to reach the top ledge above the [[Baby Coin]] and toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the hovering [[Winged Cloud]] to earn yourself a [[1-Up]]. Continue to the right and grab the [[Coin]]s along the path of the right ledge, drop down to grab the rest of the [[Coin]]s, next defeat the [[Piranha Plant]] on the [[Pipe]] and then enter it to reach the underground cavern, this underground cavern has several [[Coin]]s along the bottom and heaps more stashed at the top, use the wind to glide up to the higher ledge, there are also two [[Fly Guy]]s in this room and one is holding a [[Red Coin]], either toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at this [[Fly Guy]] or swallow him or the Red Coin to obtain it. Upon collecting all the goodies, hit the [[Egg Block]] to fill your egg supply then exit through the [[Pipe]].

Upon reaching land, use the wind to reach the [[Smiley Flower]] in the air and advance to the left and grab the two [[Red Coin]]s above the [[Pointey]]s, next, move on back to the right and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to activate the wind, there is a [[Stork Stop]] and [[Egg Block]] to the right, you can use these to switch out to [[Baby Peach]] (if you haven't already) and grab some eggs and then glide up to the upper ledge, from here, dispose of the [[Pointey]] blocking your path and use the wind to glide even higher (this wind periodically stops so be sure to glide with it when it blows), after this grab the [[Red Coin]] in the [[Coin]] line and advance to the left ledge and hit the [[Winged Cloud]]s to grab some [[Coin]]s and a [[Smiley Flower]]. After this, move to the right and fill your egg supply with the [[Egg Block]], you will encounter a turf of [[Green Glove]]s and [[Slugger]]s (the [[Baseball Guys]] from ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''), these guys will either toss your eggs back at you or bat them away and they can hit you if you get to close to them so be careful. They can be defeated with either three stomps or a ground pound. Some rare [[Yellow Yoshi Egg]]s and [[Red Yoshi Egg]]s are also found here, you can use the eggs to hit the five hovering [[Winged Cloud]]s above, most of these [[Winged Cloud]]s contain [[Coin]]s, [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], or [[1-Up]]s but the one on the far end contains a [[Smiley Flower]] (using the [[Baseball Guy]]s egg-tossing abilities also works to hit the [[Winged Cloud]]s but their chances of hitting the clouds are lower than yours, be sure to also hit the [[Fly Guy]] holding the [[Red Coin]] above as well. After this, advance to the right and use the wind to reach the ledges, the left ledge leads to a [[Stork Stop]] and a vine which leads to a room with a huge stash of [[Coin]]s (there is nothing you can do about this room on your first visit as you need [[Baby Donkey Kong]]'s climbing abilities to reach this room but feel free to come back here once you unlock him by beating [[Baby DK, the Jungle King!]], don't worry, there are no [[Red Coin]]s or [[Smiley Flower]]s in this room), next advance to the right and use the wind (when it activates) to glide to the next area. 

Upon reaching the higher skies, tag the [[Middle Ring]] and you will come across a [[Morph Bubble]], hit it to transform into the [[Yoshi Helicopter]]. Fly around the room collecting the stashes of [[Coin]]s in the skies as well as in the niches whilst avoiding the [[Cloud Drop]]s be sure to search every niche as there are plenty of [[Coin]]s in this room, this area contains seven [[Red Coin]]s and one [[Smiley Flower]], there is also a [[Winged Cloud]] at the bottom lefthand niche which contains a 1-Up, once you're done, tag the [[Yoshi Block]] to transform back into normal Yoshi and continue onward. 

Back along the main path, in the next room, you will encounter some [[Koopa Troopa]]s walking along the path, swallowing a [[Koopa Troopa]] will allow you to use it's shell as a tossing tool for plowing through other foes, stomping on a [[Koopa Troopa]] will eject it from it's shell making it susceptible to attacks. Advance to the right and either use the [[Plaform Wheel]] to reach the other side or flutter there grabbing the [[Coin]]s and three [[Red Coin]]s along the path (as the distance is close enough to reach the other side with some flutter jumps) (below the platform is a flooring with several [[Piranha Plant]]s and [[Shy Guy]]s but these can easily be disposed of), the next [[Smiley Flower]] is on a "cheese" block platform by a [[Paratroopa]], grab it and continue onward, move down and continue to the right beating the [[Shy Guy]]s along your path, move to the path's end to grab a couple [[Coin]]s and then move back to where the wind is and glide skyward grabbing two [[Red Coin]]s in the [[Coin]] line, then advance to the left on the platform to grab the [[Coin]]s and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], next advance back to the right and use the wind to fly to the ledge, after this use the next wind gust you see to glide upward grabbing the last of the [[Red Coin]]s, the [[GOAL! Ring]] should be in sight now but before going to it, drop down and hit the [[! Switch]] and drop down to grab the [[Coin]]s that appear (and swallow any [[Shy Guy]]s that emerge from the [[Pipe]] for some [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]]), after grabbing the [[Coin]]s fly back up to the high ledge where the [[GOAL! Ring]] is and jump through it to finish the level.

==Baby Coin Location==
You will need [[Baby Mario]] for this [[Baby Coin]], near the beginning of the level, use the [[Stork Stop]] on the first ledge to switch to [[Baby Mario]] and hit the [[M Block]] on the neighboring platform to make a [[Flower Plant]] grow, climb up the plant and jump to the niche to grab the [[Baby Coin]].
