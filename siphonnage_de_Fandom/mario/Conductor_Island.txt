'''Conductor Island''' is a location in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', found in the [[Stormstar Sea]]. It is technically the first island to be visited, although its [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|lighthouse]] cannot be connected right away due to the path to the lighthouse being blocked off until [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] learn how to throw a Bro. Bomb with the [[Fire & Ice]] ability. The [[Great Conductor]] makes his home here.

==History==
After he and [[Luigi]] get dragged through a portal to [[Concordia]], [[Mario (character)|Mario]] ends up waking up on this island. Mario fears that his brother is lost, prompting the crimson-clad plumber to explore the island, where Luigi appears. Luigi is projected through more islands, leading Mario to a clearing where [[Shipshape Island]] can be seen. It is revealed that Luigi had landed on Shipshape Island, which Mario leaps toward. Luigi catches him, and the brothers reunite.

Later, upon the defeat of [[Glohm Bowser]], [[Zokket]] floods his fortress with a bond-breaking toxin called [[Glohm]], and the Bros. escape despite Zokket's taunts toward [[Connie]] regarding what happen. They wash ashore on Conductor Island, where they learn how to throw Bro. Bombs using [[Fire & Ice]]. There, they meet the [[Great Conductor]] himself, who tests their skills and allows them to connect the island.

==Layout==
===Starting Area===
The starting point is at the southwestern corner of the island, on a beach. A snaking path to the northeast leads to the Boulder Blockage area.

=== Boulder Blockage ===
Blocking off the path to the south side of the island (and by proxy the east side) is a boulder that cannot be destroyed on the first visit. On the second visit, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] learn the Bro. Bomb, allowing them to destroy the boulder.

=== Clearing ===
There is a clearing at the centre of the island. A path to the north leads to a lookout on the north side.

=== Dense Forest ===
The northwestern corner of the island has been blocked off due to a dense concentration of trees.

=== Southern Path ===
The south side of the island has a path that leads into a forest, accessible starting from the second visit due to the aforementioned boulder. At the southeastern corner of the island, the path curves north.

=== The Great Conductor's Forest ===
This area cannot be visited the first time, but is accessible from the second visit onward. The [[Great Conductor]] makes his home here.

=== The Lighthouse ===
Conductor Island's [[Lighthouse (Mario & Luigi: Brothership)|Lighthouse]] is found at the northeastern corner of the island, and is accessed after the Bros. pass the Great Conductor's challenge.
