'''Inamazu''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
From their sprite, Inamazu are shown to have an indigo body with antennae, large, pink lips, round hands, feet, and eyes, the latter three of which are light cream. There are two visible teeth from an Inamazu's upper lip. An Inamazu is shown to be wearing a bandana on its head.

===Behavior===
An Inamazu is an {{w|electric catfish}} in the literal sense that it can produce electricity from its antennas and shoot it at Wario.

==In ''[[Picross NP Vol. 7]]''==
An Imamazu makes a cameo as the "F" puzzle in Characters mode.
