'''Twist 'n' Out''' is a battle mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a flower-filled field in a meadow.

==Opening==
As the four characters are standing, a [[Tweester]] flies around and creates a tornado that surrounds them.

==Gameplay==
Each character is equipped with their own [[hammer]], and their object is to try and hit their opponents into the tornado. As time time goes, on the tornado becomes increasingly smaller. Whoever is the last character remaining is the winner. However, if every character is blown away, the minigame ends in a [[draw]].

==Ending==
If a character wins, the tornado disappears as the Tweester flies away. The losing characters fall from the sky, and all of them feel dazed. The cloudy day turns sunny, and the winner performs their victory animation as confetti falls onto them. If a tie occurs, all of the characters fall from the sky, dazed.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|stick}} - Move
*{{button|gcn|a}} - Swing [[hammer]]

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Stay in the eye of the tornado, and don't get knocked outside. The last one standing wins.''
* '''Advice''': ''Use the hammer to knock your opponents into the tornado. The direction they fly depends on when and where the hammer hits.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a pun on "{{w|Twist and Shout}}," a song first recorded by {{w|The Top Notes}} and later popularized by covers from prominent artists such as {{w|The Beatles}}.
