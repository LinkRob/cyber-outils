'''Flip Blocks''' (also known as '''Rotating Blocks''') are blocks that first appear in ''[[Super Mario World]], ''which take place of the [[Brick Block|Brick Blocks]] in said game. When the player hits one, it will rotate and the player can go through. [[Cape Mario]] can also spin into one from the side to spin it.If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] spin-jump on top of one, the block will be destroyed. These blocks can also contain items or coins.
