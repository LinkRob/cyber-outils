|textcolor = ffffff}}'''Refresh''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It can be used once [[Mario]] defeats the [[Koopa Bros.]] and frees [[Eldstar]]. This move only requires 1 [[Star Power|SP]] to use.
==Effects==
Using Refresh, Eldstar will appear and fly around Mario healing him with only 5 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 5 [[Flower Point|FP]] and curing any status ailment. However, at the point of later in the game, Refresh becomes rather useless and is only useful for curing status aliments whenever Mario doesn't have the necessary healing item with him.
==Trivia==
*A similar move, [[Sweet Treat]], is similar to Refresh except it has a chance to restore 10 HP and 10 FP making it a better healing move. This move can be seen in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.
