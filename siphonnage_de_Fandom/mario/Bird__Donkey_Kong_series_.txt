'''Birds''' are a recurring species in the original {{series|Donkey Kong}}. They first appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Jr. (video game)|Donkey Kong Jr.]]''. They are a variant of [[Nitpicker]]s, but have sometimes been named '''Nitpickers''' themselves.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
In many of their appearances, Birds resemble a raven.

===Behavior===
In ''Donkey Kong Jr.'', like the Nitpickers in the [[Jump Board Scene]], a Bird flies horizontally across the screen, descending progressively as it meets its margins. After performing four of these flights, a Bird goes offscreen. A difference with Birds is that they do not bombard [[Donkey Kong Jr. (character)|Donkey Kong Jr.]] with [[egg]]s. In the [[Atari 2600]] version, only one Bird flies left-to-right at the bottom of the stage. In the [[Intellivision]] version, Birds fly off the left or right side of the screen. In ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''{{'}}s namesake [[microgame]], [[Donkey Kong Jr. (microgame)|Donkey Kong Jr.]], Birds fly in a straight line from left to right, across the [[rope]]s.

In ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]], there is a bird nest where two Birds can spawn at a time. After it slowly rises from its nest, a Bird flies horizontally across the room, moving down a short distance and turning back upon reaching the screen boundaries. A Bird does three complete flights before fleeing outside of the stage. Unlike their original appearance, Birds now drop [[egg]]s to attack if it flies above [[Mario]]. In this game, both [[Hawks]] and the other [[Bird (Donkey Kong for Game Boy)|Bird]] enemy have an identical behavior to Birds.

==In {{series|Donkey Kong}}==
===''Donkey Kong Jr.''===
Birds are first encountered in the [[Chain Scene]]. Donkey Kong Jr. can defeat a Bird by dropping a [[fruit]] onto it, like with Nitpickers.

===''[[Donkey Kong Jr. Math]]''===
Only one Bird appears in the +-×÷ Exercise mode. The Bird hovers over the chains, showing which position Donkey Kong Jr. should fill, and if he made any mistakes, the Bird also points this out.

===''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' ([[Game Boy]])===

A single Bird nest makes an appearance in [[Stage 9-4]], a boss stage that resembles the Chain Scene from ''Donkey Kong Jr.''. Mario cannot defeat Birds, given that Stage 9-4 does not feature any objects that can be used against the Birds, so Mario instead has to avoid them.
