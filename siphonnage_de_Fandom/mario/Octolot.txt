'''Octolot''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Octolots are strange, octopus-like enemies encountered in the [[Forest Maze]]. They have stronger relatives named [[Octovader]]s.

==Battle==
Octolots, in battle, can use a lot of special attacks that can hinder [[Mario]]'s team. One of its signature attacks is [[Flame Wall]], which can damage everyone in Mario's party.

===Strategy===
Octolots are weak against both [[Jump]] and [[Fire (element)|fire]] attacks, so any of Mario's special attacks are effective against them.
