A '''Mad Mallet''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[Super Mario RPG|version]]. They are sentient hammer enemies that work for the [[Smithy Gang]]. Mad Mallets are only encountered in [[The Factory]].

==Battle==
Mad Mallets are only present in the battle with the [[Clerk]]. They only attack by pounding at one of [[Mario]]'s party members.

===Strategy===
Mad Mallets are weak to thunder attacks meaning [[Mallow]]'s [[Thunderbolt (move)|Thunderbolt]] or [[Shocker]].
