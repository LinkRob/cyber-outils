'''''New Super Mario Bros. Wii Coin World''''' is an arcade game themed after ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. It was developed and published by [[Capcom]], and the game was released in April 2011, exclusively in [[Japan]]. It has a slot machine mechanic in which winning a round earns the player a [[key]]. Obtaining five keys allows them to continue onward to fight [[Bowser]].
