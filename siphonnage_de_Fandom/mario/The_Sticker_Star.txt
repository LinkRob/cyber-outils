'''The Sticker Star''' is a distant Planet from the [[Mushroom Earth]] first mentioned in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', although the Planet itself is never seen, throughout the game [[Kersti]], who hails from the Sticker Star mentions the Planet several times.

==History==
Every year, the [[Sticker Fest]] is held in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] and the [[Sticker Comet]] arrives at the Kingdom to grant the wishes of all the citizens of the Mushroom Kingdom. the Sticker Star sends a representative ([[Kersti]]) along with the six [[Royal Sticker]]s on the Sticker Comet to the Mushroom Kingdom to grant everyone's wishes, one year, [[Bowser]] crashed the festival and attempted to steal the Sticker Comet for himself, this attempt failed and instead scattered the Royal Stickers across the Mushroom World with the sixth landing on Bowser's head turning him into a shiny, powerful monster. Kersti is also stuck to a sign in the ensuing chaos. When [[Mario]] finds Kersti after pulling her off the sign, she reveals that she needs to find the missing Royal Stickers or she won’t be able to return to the Sticker Star.

Throughout the game, Kersti mentions the Sticker Star, in these passing mentions, she reveals that the planet has some sort of civilization, she mentions how they learn [[Paperize|Paperization]] there starting in Elementary School along with mentioning her "Grandsticker" (possibly some form of teacher), Kersti even expresses amazement at the [[Sticker Museum]] in [[Decalburg]] mentioning how the Sticker Star doesn't have a Sticker Museum, in [[Water's Edge Way]], Kersti reveals that one Sticker told her that the Ions in Water make skin feel fresh. [[The Enigmansion]] also has a book written in a Sticker Language called [[Ancient Stickerish]] which Kersti admits she isn’t fluent with having flunked her Ancient Stickerish courses (skipped them in the European version). After the [[Royal Sticker]]s are found again and [[Bowser]] is stopped, the [[Sticker Fest]] is revived and Kersti is able to return to the Sticker Star.

==Trivia==
*The Sticker Star is one of the two prominent planets to appear in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' RPG, the first being the [[Shroob Planet]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''.
