'''Shady Paratroopas''' are a variant of [[Koopa Paratroopa]] as well as [[Shady Koopa]]s which appear as enemies in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''. Like Shady Koopas, they wear a light blue shell and a pair of sunglasses.

== History ==
While in the sky, Shady Paratroopas serve as nothing more than Paratroopas with more [[Heart Point|HP]]. However, if these guys are on the ground, they can perform the special attack that they are known for. These guys are found in a battle in the [[Glitz Pit]] and can be fought in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].
