'''Hexoskeleton''' is a boss [[minigame]] against [[Dry Bones]] in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Opening==
At the start, Dry Bones is shown standing at the edge of an octagonal arena. The player looks up in surprise. When the "Start!" prompt appears, Dry Bones gives a roar, his roar shaking the screen.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Hexoskeleton Dry Bones electrocuted.png|thumb|left|Dry Bones electrocuted from an attack]]
The player must defeat Dry Bones by performing [[Ground Pound]]s on [[magic switch]]es, each with a different color and pattern, located on the edges of a huge platform in the shape of an octagon. The flashing fire symbols on the map indicate which switches to hit and the order in which to ground-pound them. The player and Dry Bones have a [[Health Meter]]. The player has five lives, and Dry Bones has three. When the player ground-pounds the correct switch, Dry Bones' health is reduced. However, Dry Bones can attack by dropping [[Berry|berries]] to reduce the player's health, disabling some tiles, jumping around in an effort to squish the player, and throwing [[bone]]s. The player can use the berries to stun Dry Bones briefly.

After the first hit, the player needs to activate two more in order for the spell to work. On the top screen, the two magic switches flash up one by one. The first one flashing is the one that the player has to activate first. When the player activates it, a pink hex floats in midair. The player then has to Ground Pound the other magic switch to send another electrifying spell at Dry Bones.

When Dry Bones has one heart remaining, he throws more bones, and he starts to ground-pound to create a force that can hurt the player. After that, Dry Bones starts chasing the player with normal Ground Pounds. A big Ground Pound may leave a [[durian]], and if the player guides Dry Bones to it and he ground-pounds it, he is stunned for a slightly longer time than a normal berry would stun him. However, after recovering from a stun, Dry Bones starts to ground-pound the arena again. The player needs to activate three magic switches in the order shown on the top screen to launch another attack at Dry Bones and ultimately defeat him.
{{br}}

==Ending==
Dry Bones is frozen in his "taken damage" state while smoking from the magic blast, and then falls apart. Afterwards, the player celebrates near Dry Bones' remains.

==In ''Mario Party DS''==
In Story Mode, Hexoskeleton is the third boss minigame. After the player clears [[DK's Stone Statue]] and becomes the [[Superstar]], they can fight Dry Bones in Hexoskeleton. After defeating Dry Bones, they obtain the third [[Sky Crystal]] and can continue to [[Kamek's Library]].

Like other boss minigames, Hexoskeleton can be unlocked by the player for free play after they win it in Story Mode.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Its name is a portmanteau of "hex," which is a type of magic, and "{{wp|exoskeleton}}," which is the armor-like external skeleton of insects, crustaceans, and a few other kinds of animals. However, the first word could also be a pun on "hexagon," a type of shape, despite the board being shaped like an octagon.
