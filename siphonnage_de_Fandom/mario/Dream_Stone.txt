The '''Dream Stone''' is the main focus item of the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

== Usage ==
The Dream Stone can grant any wish to the one who possesses it.

== History ==
The Dream Stone was originally at [[Dozing Sands]] but [[Antasma]] and [[Bowser]] have already obtain it from Mario, Luigi, [[Prince Dreambert]], and [[Starlow]]. Antasma and Bowser then take the Dream Stone to the summit of [[Mount Pajamaja]] where they use it's powers to create [[Neo Bowser Castle]]. However, once the final battle nears, Bowser betrays Antasma and takes the Dream Stone for himself. Bowser attempts to use the Dream Stone's power but Starlow and [[Princess Peach]] soon destroy it. Not wanting to lose, Bowser [[Vacuum Mushroom|inhales]] the shattered pieces of the Dream Stone and transforms into [[Dreamy Bowser]]. Mario and Luigi defeat Dreamy Bowser and the care of the Dream Stone is left to [[The Zeekeeper]].

== Trivia ==
*The Dream Stone is very similar to the [[Star Rod]] from ''[[Paper Mario]]'' as they can both grant wishes, as well as powering [[Bowser]] up for the final battle.
