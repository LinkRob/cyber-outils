'''Pearl Beans''' are key items in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. One is a '''Red Pearl Bean''' while the other is a '''Green Pearl Bean'''.

== History ==
The [[Jellyfish Sisters]] taught [[Fire Tickle]] and [[Thunder Tickle]] techniques to [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] while underground. After that, they must give them the two Pearl Beans otherwise they must pay 100,000 coins for their lesson. Mario and Luigi can get the Green Pearl Bean if Luigi uses the Thunder Tickle technique behind Mario while completing the quest. They can also get the Red Pearl Bean by Mario using the Fire Tickle behind Luigi while searching for it. The two Jellyfish Sisters were pleased that the Mario Bros. got them the Pearl Beans and the Mario Bros. gained their new moves. Mario and Luigi returned back to the ground level of the [[Relaxation Room]].
