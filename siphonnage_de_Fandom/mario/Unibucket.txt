'''Unibucket''' is an object in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Description==
Riding in a Unibucket allows [[Wario]] to travel across white, slim cords. A Unibucket cannot ride on any other ground, and attempting this causes it to break. The player can move the Unibucket by tilting the [[Wii Remote]] and press {{button|wii|2}} to have it [[jump]]. The player can press up to have Wario to jump out the vehicle.

A yellow version with an exclamation mark, a punching glove, and a booster of the Unibucket appears in the boss level [[Hot Roderick Race]]. It features similar controls, except Wario cannot jump out the vehicle, and the player can press {{button|wii|1}} to use a boxing glove attack.
