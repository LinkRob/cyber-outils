The '''Dolphin Dasher''' is an unlockable medium-sized bike in ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]''. The kart resembles the [[Dolphin]]s from ''[[Super Mario World]]'', with additional color variants for each character. It is unlocked if the [[Engine Class|Mirror]] [[Star Cup]] is completed, or if the player completes 3,750 races.

== Description ==
[[File:MKW Dolphin Dasher.png|thumb|150px|right|[[Diddy Kong]]'s Dolphin Dasher in action in a [[Time Trial]] on [[Bowser's Castle (Mario Kart 64)|<small>N64</small> Bowser's Castle]].]]
The Dolphin Dasher has good [[speed]] and [[Off-Road|off-road]] capabilities, but slightly lacks in [[acceleration]], [[drift]] and [[Mini-Turbo|mini-turbo]].

Its [[kart]] counterpart is the [[Super Blooper (kart)|Super Blooper]].
