'''Hue-Down''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. Its title is a pun on {{w|hoedown}}.

==Layout==
The background consists appears to depict several [[Brick Block]] sprites from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. On the right side there is a color pad with nine colors: black, white, red, light green, dark green, yellow, peach, orange, and blue. Attached to the left of the color pad is a sprite from ''Super Mario Bros.''—either [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], a [[Goomba]], or a [[Super Mushroom]]—chosen by the microgame at random.

==Gameplay==
The player has to tap three colors on the color pad matching all three colors of the sprite so that it saps back into the color pad. If every correct color is correctly tapped, the player wins the microgame, but if they tap one wrong color, they lose at the microgame.
