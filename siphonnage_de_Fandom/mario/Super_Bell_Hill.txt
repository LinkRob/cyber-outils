{{Nihongo|'''Super Bell Hill'''|スーパーベルの丘|Sūpā Beru no Oka|Super Bell Hill}} (also known as '''World 1-1''') is the first level of [[World 1 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 1]] and the very first level in the game ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''.

== Layout ==
The features of this level are the standard grassy plains. There are also many trees shaped like a [[Super Bell]]. Climbing up the trees can give power ups and [[Coin]]s


== In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' ==
Super Bell Hill is the first level in the first world.
=== Green Stars ===
*'''Green Star 1''': Go out of the intersection of the third clear pipe going up.
*'''Green Star 2''': After passing the check point, chase down the nearby [[Rabbit]] to get the star.
*'''Green Star 3''': By the pipe leading to the stamp, climb the tallest bell tree and get the Big Rabbit for a [[Mega Mushroom]]. Hurry to the Giant Blocks to reach the star. Alternatively, use the gaps behind the first group and below the second group of Giant Blocks.

=== Stamp ===
[[File:Super_Bell_Hill_Stamp.png|thumb]]After the checkpoint, go into a pipe leading to a hidden area and jump into the clear pipe to get the stamp of [[Cat Mario]].

===Hidden Luigi image===
After dropping out of the clear pipe near the halfway point, the player can meet a Sprixie with a pair of binoculars who can show them a view of the goal's location. An 8-bit Luigi can be seen behind the fence near the bushes. However, Luigi disappears if players try to approach his hiding location.

== In ''[[Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker]]'' ==
[[Captain Toad]] can play the level in the Bonus Episode. Captain Toad cannot jump; the level added ladders, ramps, and [[Empty Block]]s.
=== Optional Mission ===
''"Grab the Golden Mushroom!"''
