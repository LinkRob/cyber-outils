The '''Super Mega Ultra Shroom '''is a fake item mentioned by [[Mimi]]. She shapshifted to Merlee and tried to convince and trick Mario to sign for the [[Pure Heart]] by mentioning the offer. However, Mario refuses other offers she tries to bribe him.
