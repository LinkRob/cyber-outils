The '''Sonic the Hedgehog fan''' is a teenage human character who appears in a commercial for ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]''. As can be inferred by his T-shirt, he is a fan of [[Sega]] and the ''[[Sonic the Hedgehog (franchise)|Sonic the Hedgehog]]'' games.

In a school pop quiz, he is given questions about the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] and [[wikipedia:Sega Genesis|Sega Genesis]], both being rephrased versions of the same question asking what provides more value between an SNES with bundled games and a Genesis without; while the rest of his class stops paying attention to the question, he is unable to answer, and asks for it to be repeated. Ultimately, he is punished with [[wikipedia:Writing lines|writing lines]], repeatedly writing the phrase "The Best Play Here".
