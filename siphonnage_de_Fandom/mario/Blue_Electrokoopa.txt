'''Electro Koopa''' is an enemy which only appears in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. They are a variant of a [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]] with an electrified shell.

== Profile ==
They appear in [[Pinna Park]], and are sometimes hanging from grids. They throw themselves inside their electric shells at [[Mario]], which deals one point of damage when it hits. To kill them, either stomp on them or spray them with [[F.L.U.D.D.]] while they're out of their shells. When an Electro Koopa is hanging on a grid underneath Mario, he can [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]. They can also be eaten by [[Yoshi]]s.

==Trivia==
*If an Electro Koopa's shell does not return, another respawns on it in a few seconds.
