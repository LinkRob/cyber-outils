'''Special 1-1''' is the forty-seventh level of ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It is the first level of the optional "special" worlds accessible only after beating the [[World 8 Bowser's Castle (Super Mario 3D Land)|final level]] of the game's story mode. Is also the first level with the [[Statue Leaf]] item.

==Layout==
The overall layout of Special 1-1 is based nearly entirely on [[World 2-1 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 2-1]]. However, the scenery has a dusk look to it, and a few actual changes to the level such as the binoculars originally placed at the beginning are replaced with a [[Statue Leaf]] or that the [[Goomba]]s from World 2-1 are replaced with [[Spiny|Spinies]]. A few [[Star]]s can be found in the level to quicken Mario's movements, allowing him to run right through enemies for an easy speed-run. The level's [[Flagpole]] is located where it was it was initially in World 2-1, however, several [[Prongo]]s have been placed all around the level.

*[[Prongo]]

==Star Medals==
*'''Star Medal 1''': The first [[Star Medal]] is found where the [[Toad]] from World 2-1 would have put it.
*'''Star Medal 2''': The second Star Medal can be found on the wall with the bouncing platforms. Mario must bounce to the left side of the wall in order to grab it.
*'''Star Medal 3''': At the end of the level, Mario can find a part of land that can take him to wooden boxes that will lead him to the final Star Medal.
