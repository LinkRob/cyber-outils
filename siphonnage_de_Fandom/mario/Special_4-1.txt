'''Special 4-1''' is the first level of [[Special 4 (Super Mario 3D Land)|Special 4]] in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''.  
==Layout==
The player starts out on a platform with a [[Roulette Block]] next to them, then the player moves onto an [[Elevator]] that starts to move upwards. Soon three [[Goomba]]s drop down, followed shortly after by three [[Tail Goomba]]s. The Elevator then move sideways and under a narrow platform with a [[? Block]] that contains multiple [[coin]]s on it. The Elevator then starts to rise again, and soon a [[Koopa Troopa]] and two [[Goomba Tower]]s drop down before the Elevator starts to move sideways again and under another narrow platform that has a ? Block on it that contains a [[Poison Mushroom]]. The platform then starts to rise again, and three [[Fire Piranha Plant]]s come into view, with a [[Coin Coffer]] also dropping down. The bottom left part of the Elevator and the part above then fall down, as does the top right section of the Elevator with the part below it. After the Fire Piranha Plants, three [[Bob-omb]]s drop down, and after they blow up, some [[Spiked Ball]]s are spawned. Shortly after, the sides of the Elevator fall away. The Elevator then rises through some [[Spike Trap|spike]]s and two [[Prongo]]s drop down, soon followed by another two Prongos. After this, the top and bottom sections of the Elevator fall away and shortly afterwards three [[Magikoopa]]s spawn; their attacks must be dodged three times before the Elevator reaches a [[Warp Box]], which leads to the final area. Here, the player must use three Elevators to reach the top of the [[Goal Pole]].
==Star Medals==
*'''Star Medal 1:''' Floating above the [[Spike Trap|spike]]s.
*'''Star Medal 2:''' Floating above the [[Mushroom Trampoline]].
*'''Star Medal 3:''' On top of the castle at the end of the level.
