'''Pleasant Path''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
[[Mario]] first visits this area while looking for the first [[Star Spirit]], [[Eldstar]]. Pleasant Path is a grassy area similar to [[World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. It features a pleasant brook, as well as [[Candy Cane]]s, which can restore Mario's [[HP]] if he walks around them a certain number of times. [[Koopa Village]] is near here, and [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]] is further down the road. [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] is to the west. Several enemies are found here: [[Goomba]]s, [[Koopa Troopa]]s, [[Paratroopa]]s, [[Paragoomba]]s, and [[Spiked Goomba]]s. [[Jr. Troopa]] is encountered here after Chapter 1 is completed, and [[Kent C. Koopa]] blocks the road after Chapter 5, but Mario does not have to fight him. Mario could either pay Kent C. Koopa 100 Coins or take the blue Warp Pipe located in [[Toad Town Tunnels]].
