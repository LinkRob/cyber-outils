'''Shroom Slide''' is a [[Gaddget]] in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. It is unlocked by completing the [[Find the Password]] quest.

==Description==
Similarly to [[Shroom Drop]], this Gaddget involves colored [[Mushroom (item)|Mushrooms]] traveling along a path. However, in the case of Shroom Slide, the Mushrooms travel along a board of randomly connected tracks, and the player can decide on the number of competing Mushrooms, anywhere from two to eight, and the number of winning landing places. Once every Mushroom has reached the goal, the Gaddget resets automatically.
