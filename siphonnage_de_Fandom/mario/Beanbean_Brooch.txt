The '''Beanbean Brooch''' is a key item in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

==History==
[[Lady Lima]] states that [[Cackletta]] forced [[Queen Bean]] to swallow the [[Belly Blech]] worm that makes her strong but furious all the time. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can cure Queen Bean with the [[Chuckola Reserve]] from the [[Chucklehuck Woods]]. Lady Lima gives the duo the Beanbean Brooch after they have defeated Queen Bean. Mario and Luigi can show the Beanbean Brooch to the [[Beanbean Guard]]s to continue their quest through [[Château de Chucklehuck]] to get the Chuckola Reserve and recover Queen Bean.
