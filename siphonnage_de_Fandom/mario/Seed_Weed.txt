'''Seed Weeds''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are walking plants that have a yellow bulb-like head and green weeds that act as their legs.

===Behavior===
A Seed Weed attacks by spitting seeds at [[Wario]] upon noticing him.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Seed Weeds can be found mainly in the [[Ancient Waterworks]], where they are common enemies. Seed Weeds are also found in [[Sneezemore Cave]], although only during the second special episode. Wario can easily dispatch of the Seed Weeds with any attack. Wario does not can damaged from a Seed Weed if he touches it.

Seed Weeds also appear in the fight with [[Ka-Bloom!]], who can produce them at will.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
*Their name is a pun of the word "seaweed."
