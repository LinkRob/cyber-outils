}}'''Power Smash''' is a [[badge]] in the [[Paper Mario series|''Paper Mario'' series]]. Power Smash gives [[Mario]] a stronger [[hammer]] attack in battle.

== Appearances ==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario]]'', Power Smash is found in [[Toad Town Tunnels]], where it is guarded by two [[Spiky Gloomba]]s on the western side of the sewers. The Power Smash is found by hitting the block with a jump pad on it, and it is in the chest located via the jump pad. Power Smash costs one [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear and two [[Flower Point|FP]] to use.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
In ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'', Power Smash gives Mario the same effects as its ''Paper Mario'' counterpart. It is the first badge obtained by [[Mario]] instead of [[Power Jump]] like in ''Paper Mario''. Power Smash is given to Mario by [[Professor Frankly]], and an unlimited number can be bought from the [[Pianta Parlor]] at 34 [[Pianta Token]]s each. By equipping multiples of the Power Smash, Mario can unleash a [[hammer]] attack stronger than [[Mega Smash]] from ''Paper Mario''.
