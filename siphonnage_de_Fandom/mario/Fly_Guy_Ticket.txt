The '''Fly Guy Ticket''' is a board item in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== In ''[[Super Mario Party]]'' ==
The Fly Guy Ticket can be obtained out of random from an [[Item Space]]. If the player uses the item, the [[Fly Guy]] will be summoned and the player can request an item they want the Fly Guy to steal the item from another player.
