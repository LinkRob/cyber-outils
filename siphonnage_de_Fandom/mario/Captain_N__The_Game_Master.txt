'''''Captain N: The Game Master''''' is an animated television series which ran from 1989 to 1991. While it is not a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' show, ''Captain N: The Game Master'' does have references to the franchise, and [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] appears in some of the episodes. The show was produced by [[DIC Entertainment]], and it is based on the ''Captain N'' comics in ''[[Nintendo Comics System]]''.

The show stars [[Kevin Keene]], a [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] gamer who is transported into [[Videoland]] to fight [[Mother Brain]] and other villains while allied by several protagonists of Nintendo Entertainment System games, such as [[Pit (character)|Pit]] and [[Mega Man]].
