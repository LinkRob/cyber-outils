'''Bunsen''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Robo Koopa (episode)|Robo Koopa]]." She is a robot from the city of [[Robo Land]] and a friend of [[Dr. Nerdnick]].

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Bunsen's name originates from {{w|Bunsen burner}}, a type of gas burner used as laboratory equipment.
