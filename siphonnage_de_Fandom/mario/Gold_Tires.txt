The '''Gold Tires''' ('''Gold Wheels''' in Australia and Europe) are unlockable wheels in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. They can be unlocked by getting 15,000 [[coin]]s, or by getting a [[star]] rank on every [[Grand Prix]] Cup in each [[Engine Class]]. 

Similar to the [[Slick]] wheels, the Gold Tires have some of the best [[speed]], but lack [[Off-Road|off-road]], having some of the worst. It also has some of the worst [[handling]] in the game, being tied with the Slick, [[Monster (wheels)|Monster]], and [[Red Monster]].
