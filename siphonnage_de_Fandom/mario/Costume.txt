In terms of [[Super Mario Odyssey]], a '''costume''' is a cosmetic item you can buy at the game's in-game store, [[Crazy Cap]], for either yellow coins or purple coins. Purple coins are regional coins that you can only use in the kingdom you find them in, and therefore the ones that cost purple coins '''can only be bought from those kingdoms.''' Every costume has 2 parts: A Cap and a Suit. However, the term "costume" returned in [[Yoshi's Crafted World]], where you can get cosmetics at the game's Gacha Machines.
