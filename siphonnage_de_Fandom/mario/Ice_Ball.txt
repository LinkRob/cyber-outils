'''Ice Balls''' (also spelt as '''Iceballs''') are balls of ice that are seen in several games of the ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' series. They are introduced in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''

==Description and Function==
Originally, Ice Balls looked like [[Fireball]]s, but they were blue. In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', Ice Balls had their design changed to spheres surrounded by misty ice. ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' redesigned them without the sphere. Later games reverted back to the ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' design.

Ice Balls function similarly to [[Fireball]]s but have the ability to freeze enemies instead. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], among others, gain the ability to shoot Ice Balls while under the effects of an [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]] (as [[Ice Mario]] and [[Penguin Mario]] respectively). Enemies such as [[Ice Bro]]s. and [[Venus Ice Trap]]s also have the ability to shoot Ice Balls, Ice Balls can freeze the same enemies a [[Fireball]] can but they can also freeze enemies that are immune to Fireballs such as [[Dry Bones]] and [[Bullet Bill]]s. Their ice element nature makes them very effective against fire-based enemies such as [[Podoboo]]s and [[Fire Snake]]s, which can instantly be destroyed by them while larger enemies can instantly break free from their ice cubes upon being frozen due to their large size. Ice Balls move similarly to fireballs, save for bouncing only once before a second impact with the ground destroys them, although both projectiles shatter upon hitting a wall.

==History==
===In the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' franchise===
====''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''====
[[Image:Ice Ball Toss.png|thumb|left|The Plumbers tossing their Ice Balls at a [[Snoozorb]].]]
Ice Balls make their debut in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', here, they can be used by [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], and [[Baby Luigi]] whenever they use an [[Ice Flower]]. The player needs to repeatedly press the button of the brother holding the big Ice Ball to grant it's full effect. When the Ice Balls hit enemies, they have a chance of inflicting [[POW-Down]], [[DEF-Down]], or [[SPD-Down]]. Ice Balls are essentially a recolor of Fireballs in this game.
{{br}}
====''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''====
Ice Balls return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' as misty clouds shot by the [[Icead]] enemy. This is the only game where their design is unique, not counting ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' since that game's ice ball design was based on the fireball.
{{br}}
====''Mario & Luigi: Brothership''====
Ice Balls return in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'', having reverted back to their ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' design (see below). Luigi can throw them using the [[Fire & Ice]] Bros. Move introduced in this game. [[Doom Bloom]]s can also spit Ice Balls when they attack.
{{br}}
===In the ''[[Super Mario (series)|Super Mario]]'' platformer games===
====In the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' franchise====
Ice Balls return in the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' games. In this game, they had their design changed, making them spheres surrounded by misty ice clouds.
=====''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''=====
[[Image:Ice Ball Battle.png|thumb|right|[[Ice Mario]] and [[Ice Bro.]] toss their Ice Balls.]]
In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', the heroes can attack with Ice Balls if they become [[Ice Mario]], Ice Luigi, Ice Blue Toad, or Ice Yellow Toad using an [[Ice Flower]], or [[Penguin Mario]], Penguin Luigi, Penguin Blue Toad, or Penguin Yellow Toad using a [[Penguin Suit]]. The Ice Balls can freeze enemies, including enemies that are immune to fireballs like [[Buzzy Beetle]]s and [[Dry Bones]] (however some enemies such as [[Thwomp]]s and [[Boo]]s are also immune to Ice Balls). Frozen enemies can be picked up and thrown, or destroyed by hitting a surface, ground pounding, jumping on them, tossing a shell or another frozen enemy, or touching them while under the effects of a [[Starman]], if left frozen for too long however, the enemy will break free. Airborne enemies will remain frozen in mid-air for a few seconds before plummeting and hitting the ground (the frozen enemy will shatter upon landing unless they were a couple feet above the ground when frozen) while frozen, the midair enemy can be used as a stepping stone, underwater enemies will float to the surface upon being frozen and never thaw ([[Urchin]]s however can break free quickly but become drowsy and descend to the ocean floor upon breaking free). Some enemies (particularly large enemies such as [[Super Piranha Plant]]s and [[Porcupuffer]]s) are immune to being frozen and break free instantly, but Ice Balls can dispel the [[Magic Projectile]]s of the [[Koopalings]], the [[Fireball]]s spat by [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]], as well as being able to destroy [[Podoboo]]s and [[Fire Snake]]s. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] can grab an Ice Ball with his tongue and spit it out, freezing any enemies in its path. [[Ice Bro]]s. attack with Ice Balls that damage Mario and temporarily freeze him, and can also freeze other enemies.
{{br}}
=====''New Super Mario Bros. U / New Super Luigi U / New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''=====
[[Image:Ice Ball Throw.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] tossing an Ice Ball at a [[Dry Bones]].]]
Ice Balls return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Luigi U]]'' and ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''. Just as before, they are used when the heroes turn into [[Ice Mario]] or [[Penguin Mario]] through the use of the [[Ice Flower]] or [[Penguin Suit]]. In this game, Ice Balls have a more crystalized appearance than before however they still have the same uses as before, they freeze enemies upon contact and just as before, larger enemies can break free instantly. Enemies such as [[Torpedo Ted]]s can also break free instantly, additionally, [[Boom Boom]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] can also be frozen but they too immediately break free upon being frozen. Just as before, fire-based enemies such as [[Podoboo]]s and [[Fire Snake]]s can be killed with an Ice Ball and Ice Balls can also be used to cancel out the [[Koopalings]] [[Magic Projectile]]s as well as [[Bowser]]'s fireballs, additionally, Bowser himself is immobilized for a second upon being hit with an Ice Ball. Enemies such as [[Ice Bro]]s. and [[Venus Ice Trap]]s also utilize Ice Balls in these games.
{{br}}
====In the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' games====
Ice Balls return in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' games, where they have their design changed to a recolour of a [[Fireball]], and Mario and his foes can use them as weapons against each other.
=====''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''=====
[[Image:Ice Ball Sticker.png|thumb|left|[[Mario]] tosses an Ice Ball at the [[Goomba]]s.]]
In ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'', Ice Balls are used by [[Ice Mario]] whenever he uses an [[Ice Flower]] [[Stickers|Sticker]], The Ice Balls have the same effects as the [[Fireball]]s and do about the same damage to ground-based enemies. The Ice Balls are very strong against fire-based enemies such as [[Fire Bro]]s; however ice-based enemies such as [[Snow Spike]]s and [[Ice Bro]]s are immune to the Ice Balls and the [[Bowser Snow Statue]] as well as [[Mizzter Blizzard]] can actually heal from the Ice Balls. Ice Balls have more power when the player uses a [[Shiny Ice Flower]] or a [[Flashy Ice Flower]].
{{br}}
=====''Paper Mario: Color Splash''=====
[[Image:Ice Ball Small.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] throws an Ice Ball at the [[Goomba]]s.]]
Ice Balls appear in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''; whenever [[Mario]] uses an [[Ice Flower]] [[Battle Cards|Card]], he turns into [[Ice Mario]] and has the ability to toss Ice Balls at certain enemies, enemies such as the [[Fire Bro.]] and the [[Podoboo]] are extremely weak to Ice Balls and take more damage from them while [[Ice Bro]]s. are virtually immune to Ice Balls. The Ice Balls are very strong against the [[Big Podoboo]] mini boss. Just as before, [[Ice Bro]]s. commonly attack by using Ice Balls.
{{br}}
=====''Paper Mario: The Origami King''=====
[[Image:Ice Ball Clash.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] tossing Ice Balls at the [[Tape (Paper Mario: The Origami King)|Tape]].]]
Ice Balls also appear in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]'' where they appear as one of [[Mario]]'s attacks, just as before when [[Mario]] uses an [[Ice Flower]] in battle, he turns into [[Ice Mario]] and is granted the ability to toss Ice Balls at his foes. These are powerful against fire-based enemies but ice-based enemies are immune to them. [[Ice Bro]]s. also retain the ability to toss Ice Balls as part of their attacks.
{{br}}
===In the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' franchise===
====''Mario Kart Arcade GP DX''====
[[Image:Ice Ball Drive.png|thumb|left|[[Ice Luigi]] holding an Ice Ball.]]
Ice Balls appear again in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''. In this game, they appear as [[Ice Luigi]]'s special item, Ice Balls can be thrown either forwards or backwards and can briefly freeze rivals for a few seconds causing them to fall behind greatly in the race, the Ice Balls can be useful for sending relentless rivals further back in the pack.
{{br}}
====''Mario Kart Tour''====
[[Image:Ice Ball FRENZY.png|thumb|left|[[Rosalina]] in an Ice Ball FRENZY.]]
Ice Balls appear in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''. Whenever a racer gets an [[Ice Flower]] from an [[Item Box]], they are given the ability to temporarily shoot three rows of Ice Balls at other racers similar to the [[Fire Flower]] with it's [[Fireball]]s however the Ice Balls are more powerful as they can briefly freeze racers and causing them to slide which makes them unable to move for a few seconds. Ice Balls are also seen during the [[Ice Bro]]'s. trick animations.
{{br}}
===In ''Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U''===
[[Image:Ice Ball Dunk.webp|thumb|right|[[Luigi]] throwing an Ice Ball.]]
In ''[[Super Smash Bros. for 3DS / Wii U]]'', the Ice Ball appears as [[Luigi]]'s second custom variation of the [[Fireball]] move. Although it doesn't travel anywhere near as fast as the standard Fireball, it boasts a longer range and has the ability to freeze opponents with higher damage percentages making some fights more feasible.
{{br}}
===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
[[Image:Ice Ball Mario.png|thumb|left|Mario holds an Ice Ball.]]
Ice Balls make a minor appearance in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'' where they are held by [[Ice Mario]], [[Ice Luigi]], and the [[Ice Bro]]s. They are also used as part of their attacks as well.
{{br}}
===''Mario Golf: Super Rush''===
[[Image:Ice Golf Ball.png|thumb|left|The Golf Ball as an Ice Ball.]]
The Ice Ball appears again in ''[[Mario Golf: Super Rush]]'', in this game, the Ice Ball is utilized by [[Luigi]] when he employs his [[Ice Flower Freeze]] [[Special Shot]]. Upon usage, his golf ball transforms into an Ice Ball which has the effect of freezing the area it lands on. In this frozen state, the friction of the golf ball is reduced, and nearby water and [[lava]] can also be frozen (but the Ice Ball itself cannot land in them). Even though characters traveling on foot within the frozen portions of water or lava are penalized, it still counts as a Penalty Area and incurs a stroke penalty if the golf ball lands on the ice. In the [[Target Golf]] mode, the Ice Ball can be obtained in multiplayer only and has the effect of freezing the surrounding area until the next shot is taken.
{{br}}
==Trivia==
*The Ice Ball has so far gone through three design changes, first appearing as a mere recolor of a [[Fireball]], then becoming a sparkly iced snowball, and finally a crystalized ball of ice.
