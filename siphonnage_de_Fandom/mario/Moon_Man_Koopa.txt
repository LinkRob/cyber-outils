'''Moon Man Koopa''' is an alias of [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Stars in Their Eyes]]."

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Moon Man Koopa shows King Koopa wearing a bubble-shaped space helmet over his head, and his cuffs do not have any spikes.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
Moon Man Koopa enslaves the residents of the planet [[Quirk (planet)|Quirk]] and takes over their own [[Quirk Factory]]. However, their plans were thwarted by [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] and [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] as usual.
