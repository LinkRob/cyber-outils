'''Chuboomba''' is a variant of [[Goomba]] and an enemy which appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. These creatures make their home in [[Cavi Cape]], but can also be found on [[Plack Beach]]. 

== Description ==
Chuboombas are Goombas with orange heads. Behind their back is a large white lollipop with a pink swirl.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''==
In the field, Chuboombas generally roam their territory. No Chuboombas can be found in the overworld in Plack Beach, but a Chuboomba may assist a [[Toothy]].

The Chuboomba is also the first enemy fought by [[Bowser]]. Its only attack is to ram into Bowser at speed. The Koopa King can counter this move with his [[Punch]], but if it exhales before charging, it will hesitate before impact, possibly throwing Bowser's timing off. If Bowser [[Vacuum Block|inhales]] the Chuboomba's lollipop, he regains health and the Chuboomba runs out of the battle, crying. However, this means Bowser does not gain any [[Experience Point|EXP]] from the battle. Chuboombas also appear in the tutorial for his different [[Special Attack]]s. If the Bros. fight a Chuboomba, the creature's charge can be dodged by jumping.

Chuboombas are Level 9 with 50 hit points, an attack power of 26, a defense of 31, and a speed of 7. Their defeat nets 18 experience with their loot drop containing 4 coins and possibly a Hot Drumstick.

== Behind the scenes ==
The Chuboomba's name comes from a portmanteau of "chubby" (or just "chub") and "Goomba".

==Trivia==
*Chuboomba is possibly related to [[Shrooboid Brat]] as both use lollipops to attack.
*Strangely, in the [[Brawl Attack]] instructions, the Chuboombas do not have their signature lollipop.
