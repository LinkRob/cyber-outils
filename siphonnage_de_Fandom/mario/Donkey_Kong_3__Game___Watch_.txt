'''''Donkey Kong 3''''' is a game for the [[Game & Watch]] based on the [[arcade]] game of the [[Donkey Kong 3|same name]]. It was released on August 20, 1984, around the same time as ''[[Donkey Kong 3: Dai Gyakushū]]'', an indirect sequel for ''Donkey Kong 3'' released only in Japan.
