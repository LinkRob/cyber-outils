'''Max Syrup Jars''' are items that appear in the [[Mario & Luigi (series)|''Mario & Luigi'' series]]. They first appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', in which they are named '''Max Syrups'''.

==Description==
Max Syrup Jars are a type of [[Syrup Jar]] item, and they restore more BP than some of the other Syrup Jar items.

==Appearances==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
[[File:Syrup MLSS sprite.png|frame|left]]
Max Syrup Jars are used in battle to refill a Bro's [[Bros. Point|BP]] completely. The Max Syrup Jar can be bought from the [[Castle Town Item Shop]] for 150 [[coin]]s, or 280 coins in the Japanese version.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''===
Using a Max Syrup Jar on Mario Luigi or Bowser restores up to 40 [[Special Point|SP]]. In the remake, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'', a Max Syrup Jar refills a Bro's BP entirely.

===''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''===
These items will heal 80 BP for either Bro in this game. They retain their abilities in'' [[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. They can be purchased from item shops later in the game.
