'''Chomp the Turtle''' is a character associated with the [[Munch a Bunch]] [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. He first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Chomp the Turtle is a turtle with a white silhouette and entirely black eyes. He has a wide mouth the usually remains open.

===Personality traits===
Chomp the Turtle's main trait is chomping down in whatever is in his way. 

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
[[File:Munch a Bunch WWIMM.png|thumb|left|200px|Chomp the Turtle in the ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' microgame Munch a Bunch]]
In Munch a Bunch, first appearing in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', Chomp has to eat two-dimensional shapes lined at his mouth. If done successfully, the player wins the microgame. Chomp the Turtle returns in the same form in the microgame's direct ports included in both ''[[Wario World]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', Chomp returns in a remake of the microgame Munch a Bunch. Aside from this, Chomp the Turtle is also a card in [[Wario Kard]], also the first time in which he is named.
