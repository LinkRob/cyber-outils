'''The Hoppycat Trial: Hop, Hop, and Awaaay''' is a course of [[Shining Falls]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.

==Layout==
The level features many [[hoppycat]]s. As this is the first level to feature the enemy, the level immediately demonstrates how the enemy works. At the start of the level is a block the player is encouraged to hit, there are hoppycats to the right of the block. Hitting the block by [[jump]]ing will also cause the hoppycats to jump, teaching the player how hoppycats work.

Later in the level is a long {{lw|? Block}} that must be hit with a hoppycat. Then will drop the [[Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will summon massive hoppycats that destroy ground. The player scale the level and use massive hoppycats to destroy ground blocking the path. Eventually the player will reach the [[Wonder Seed]] hidden behind ground. The [[Goal Pole]] is shortly after.

#To the right of a massive hoppycat is a 10-flower coin. The player must jump to allow access.
