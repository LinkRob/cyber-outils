==Effects==
In ''Super Paper Mario'', Mario can obtain many of these on his adventure. Although, it is not a badge in this game, but is a regular item. Once obtained, a Power Plus item can be used to automatically and permanently increase the attack power of Mario and his friends by 1 (2 for [[Bowser]]). There is a Power Plus in [[Flopside]]'s lowest basement. By going behind the warp pipe, Mario can open a big treasure chest. Inside is this item. Mario can also obtain these by trading 2500 Flipside Tokens at the Flipside Game corner. He can do this an unlimited amount of times.
