A '''Shymore''' (originally called '''Shyster''') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario RPG]]''. They are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] that work under [[Mack]] and the [[Smithy Gang]]. After [[Mario]] and [[Mallow]] get back the Frog Coin from [[Croco]], they return to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] where the town was being overrun by Shysters. Shysters are found bouncing around in homes and chasing [[Toad (species)|Toad]]s around. They are even more common in [[Princess Peach's Castle]]. Once Mack is defeated, all of the Shysters will leave.

There is a specific group of Shysters who fight alongside [[Mack]] named [[Bodyguard]]s. They use the same sprite as Shysters but have different stats.

==Battle==
In battle, Shymores mainly attack by hopping at either Mario or Mallow. They can also use a move called [[Drain]] that can deal moderate damage to Mario or Mallow.
