*''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''|time=100 seconds|located=[[Mushroom Kingdom]]}}'''Piranha Heights''' is the fifth level in the [[Acorn Plains]] area in the game ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''.
==Features==
The level features flat topped mushrooms that have different actions:
*Some do nothing, and are easy to cross.
*Some (Mostly the Pink) act like springs, and add a boost to your jumps.
*Some will bob up and down, and when you jump at the right time, you can avoid enemies.
The main enemy of the area is [[Piranha Plant|Piranha Plants]], that very in size.
==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': With Pink Baby Yoshi or the Flying Squirrel power up, get the Star Coin from the high platform to the right of the first conjoined pipes.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Use Pink Yoshi or the Flying Squirrel power up to jump on the right side of the second conjoined pipes.
*'''Star Coin 3''': Jump in the gap of the last two pipes and use the pipe on the right side to get to a hidden area. Use the mushrooms to get on the Luigi statue and get the coin.
