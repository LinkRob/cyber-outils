'''Bowser's Road''' is a song from ''[[Super Mario 64]]''. It plays in [[King Bowser]]'s [[Bowser in the Dark World|st]][[Bowser in the Fire Sea|ag]][[Bowser in the Sky|es]].

== Description ==
Its key is D minor and its tempo is between 60 and 70 beats per minute. The song's length is 15 bars in the main loop, with 37 beats of introduction.

The song is later reorchestrated for Bowser's [[Bowser's Star Reactor|reac]][[Bowser's Dark Matter Plant|tors]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' as well as his [[Bowser's Lava Lair|fortr]][[Bowser's Gravity Gauntlet|esses]] in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.
