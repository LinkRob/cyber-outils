'''Lighthouses''' are objects in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
Lighthouses are tall towers topped with cat ears. For each of the twelve major islands across [[Lake Lapcat]], there is one lighthouse, colored according to the region denoted by the [[Giga Bell]]s. All lighthouses are initially corrupted by the black [[goop]] seen throughout Lake Lapcat and covering [[Fury Bowser]]. While corrupted, their tops are covered in goop, and they emit a purple aura.
