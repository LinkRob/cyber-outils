'''Spring Feet I''' is a level of [[Fluff-Puff Peaks]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. It showcases the badge [[Spring Feet]].
==Layout==
The level demonstrates the mechanics of Spring Feet by having the player have bounce along for a bit then hit a {{lw|? Block}} that requires a higher than usual jump. After this, the level presents several blocks the player must bounce across. The player is then met by a [[lift]] moving left to right over a [[pit]]. Finally, the player is presented with a series of smaller lifts, then the [[Goal Pole]].
==Items==
The [[10-Flower Coin]]s can be obtained int he following order:
#A 10-flower coin is located above the first lift. The player must time their jump to go high enough to reach the 10-flower coin.
#After the second [[Checkpoint Flag]], there is a 10-flower coin in the air. To obtain the coin, the player must time their jump at the edge of the platform.
#Between the first two lifts in the series of lifts, there is a 10-flower coin in the air. The player must time their jump to obtain the 10-flower coin.
