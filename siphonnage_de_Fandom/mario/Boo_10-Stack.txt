A '''Boo 10-Stack''' is an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''. They are a variant of [[Boo]] made up of ten individual Boos, each having only one [[Heart Point|HP]], so a Boo 10-Stack's health corresponds to how many Boos are remaining. Boo 10-Stacks appear in both [[Lighthouse Island]] and [[Fortune Island]].
