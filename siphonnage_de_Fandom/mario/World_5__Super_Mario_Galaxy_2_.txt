|game=''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''|preworld=[[World 4 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)]]|nxtworld=[[World 6 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)]]}}'''World 5''' is the fifth world in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It contains six normal galaxies and one boss galaxy, as usual.

== Description ==

==== [[Space Storm Galaxy]] ====
This level revolves around mechanical objects and is somewhat based off the [[Dreadnought Galaxy|Dreadnought]] Galaxy and the [[Buoy Base Galaxy]] from [[Super Mario Galaxy]]. The galaxy has an abandoned rocketship-like object and a tower shaped like a Topman similar to [[Buoy Base Galaxy]]. Switches that slow down time first appear here.

==== [[Boo Moon Galaxy]] ====
This galaxy is stereotypical haunted mansion-themed. The level has poison that will make [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] lose a life instantly. [[Snake Block|Snake Blocks]] are found throughout the level. This is the only galaxy to involve use of the [[Boo Mushroom]]. Common enemies include [[Octoboo|Octoboos]] and [[Boo|Boos]].

==== [[Upside Dizzy Galaxy]] ====
This galaxy is similar to the [[Rightside Down Galaxy]]. This galaxy features heavy use of shifts in gravity. The direction of the gravity in this level can change depending on the direction of the arrows that are seen in the background. [[Goomba|Paragoombas]] are frequent enemies in this galaxy.

==== [[Fleet Glide Galaxy]] ([[Hungry Luma]]) ====
This level features many mechanical parts and takes place on a rocket ship. Just like [[World 2 (Super Mario Galaxy 2)|World 2]]'s [[Wild Glide Galaxy]], this galaxy involves [[Fluzzard]] gliding. Enemies here include [[Magmaargh|Magmaarghs]] and Bats. Many Balloons also appear scattered at different points throughout the galaxy.

==== [[Slipsand Galaxy]] ====
This level is desert-themed and features desert objects such as cacti. Sand Rivers and fireballs are the main obstacles in the level. A pyramid can be seen in the background, but serves no special purpose.

==== [[Shiverburn Galaxy]] ====
This galaxy is similar to the [[Freezeflame Galaxy]] from [[Super Mario Galaxy]], as it revolves around the elements of fire and ice. [[Octoomba|Octoombas]] and [[Elite Octoomba|Elite Octoombas]] are common enemies here.

==== [[Bowser Jr.'s Boom Bunker]] ====
[[Bowser Jr.]] is fought here, seen in the cockpit of the [[Boomsday Machine]]. The main enemies here are [[Boomerang Bro|Boomerang Bros]]. and Space Mines.
