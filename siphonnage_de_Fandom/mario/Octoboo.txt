'''Octoboos''' are enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Octoboos are a cross between a [[Boo]] and an [[Octoomba]]. They have white bodies with light gray spots, ghostly tails, blue antennae, and a spout. They also have glowing, yellow eyes.

===Behavior===
Octoboo attack identically to the [[Circling Boo Buddies]] from ''[[Super Mario World]]'', by circling around [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]].

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''==
[[File:Octoboo.png|thumb|left|200px|Three Octoboos in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'']]
Octoboos appear in [[Haunty Halls Galaxy]] and [[Boo Moon Galaxy]]. They are mostly undefeatable, and should be avoided.
