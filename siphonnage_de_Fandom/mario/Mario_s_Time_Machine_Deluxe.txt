'''''Mario's Time Machine Deluxe''''' is an [[Reissue#Enhanced ports|enhanced port]] of ''[[Mario's Time Machine (PC)|Mario's Time Machine]]'' released in 1994. It was one of two "Deluxe" re-releases of educational titles of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], with the other being ''[[Mario is Missing! CD-ROM Deluxe]]''. As the game was released on a CD-ROM, it was able to hold more content than the previous floppy disk release.

==Differences==
In a similar manner to ''Mario is Missing! CD-ROM Deluxe'', ''Mario's Time Machine Deluxe'' adds voice acting for its characters. The CD-ROM also features a [[Library]] application, which features [[Bowser's mother]] as the librarian. In addition, as the game was released a year later than the original DOS version, it is said to take place in 1994, rather than 1993. Animations also appear to have been modified or added.
