[[File:Jointtower.png|thumb|220x220px]]'''Joint Tower''' is an location in [[Bowser's body]] that appears in the game [[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story|''Mario &amp; Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story'']]. It is located right below the [[Energy Hold]] and is mainly located in Bowser's right leg: opposite of the [[Leg Outpost]].
==History==
Mario and Luigi visit Joint Tower after noticed something strange going on in that area. [[Toadbert]] is seen here and tells the Mario Bros. that if they activate Bowser's pressure points, then he could use the Body Slam. Mario and Luigi does so and Bowser can now Body Slam large switches and rocks. Bowser then escapes the [[Tower of Yikk]] using his new skill.
