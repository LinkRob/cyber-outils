'''Mask Koopas''' are a reskin of [[Koopa Troopa]]s featured in ''[[Super Mario World]]'' and its [[Game Boy Advance]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2]]''. All Koopa Troopas become Mask Koopas during [[Fall]], which occurs after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] have reached all 96 exits.

Mask Koopas behave the same as regular Koopa Troopas, and their only difference is their appearance. Instead of wearing a shell, Mask Koopas wear a mask in the likeness of Mario, but is more orange to match the Koopa Troopas' skin tone, and Mask Koopas wear a cap that is either green, red, blue, or yellow in color. During Fall, [[Red Koopa Troopa|Red]] and Green Koopa Troopas are replaced by Blue and Yellow Mask Koopas respectively.

In ''Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2'', there is a cutscene revealing how the Koopas became Mask Koopas. In it, Luigi is shown riding a hot-air balloon, where he throws masks down onto [[Dinosaur Land]]. Mario had stomped a Koopa Troopa out of its [[Koopa Shell|shell]], turning it into a [[Beach Koopa]], who walks up to a nearby mask and puts it on as a replacement.
