'''Batadons'''<ref>''Super Mario Land'' English instruction booklet. Page 14.</ref><ref>M. Arakawa. Nintendo ''Game Boy'' Player's Guide. Page 5.</ref> are enemies introduced in ''[[Super Mario Land]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Batadons are hopping carved stone heads with [[wing]]s behind their rectangular ears. In artwork for ''Super Mario Land'', they are depicted yellow-orange. In ''Mario Kart World'', Batadons are depicted light-yellow and have a more bottom-heavy shape, like the [[Tokotoko]].

==History==
===''Super Mario Land''===
[[File:Batadon SML sprite.png|frame|left]]
In ''Super Mario Land'', to defeat this enemy, [[Mario]] either has to [[jump]] on top of it, or shoot three [[Superball]]s at it. It acts much like a [[Fighter Fly|Fly]] or [[Kumo]], except it bounces higher, moves slower, and does not pause on the ground. Defeating a Batadon awards Mario with 800 points. Batadons can also pound through some platforms. They only appear in [[World 3-1 (Super Mario Land)|Worlds 3-1]] and [[World 3-3 (Super Mario Land)|3-3]]. Batadons closely resemble {{wp|moai}} heads, similarly to [[Tokotoko]]s, other rock-based creatures found in the [[Easton Kingdom]]; however, Batadons are the only ones with wings.

===''Mario Kart World''===
[[File:MKWorld Batadon.png|thumb|In ''Mario Kart World'']]
Batadons return in ''[[Mario Kart World]]''. They are featured in the open section of the [[Desert Hills]] course, where they replace the [[Pokey]]s in the original version.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario Land'' manga===
In the [[Super Mario Land (manga)|''Super Mario Land'' manga]], a Batadon is the first enemy encountered in Easton Kingdom, getting angry at Mario for ruining one of the statues and calling the Tokotoko and other Batadons for backup. However, the moai are easily tricked by Mario yelling "{{wp|Statues (game)|Red light!}}", making them stop in place. Only when Mario leaves do they realize that they were tricked and chase after him. After losing Mario due to him being in a disguise, they finally find him and jump towards him, failing to notice he was entangled in a web with lethal [[Suu]] venom about to be poured on him. Getting entangled in the web, the maoi free Mario with their jolts, and are hit by the venom in his place, melting down.

===''Super Mario-kun''===
In ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', some Batadons appear in [[Super Mario-kun Volume 3|volume 3]] alongside the Tokotoko and their boss [[Hiyoihoi]]. Apart from helping their leader and forming a giant rock with the moai, they do nothing relevant.
