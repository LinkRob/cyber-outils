'''World 6-4''' is a level from [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 6]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''. [[Monty Mole]]s are a common sight in this course. The player will also be able to use [[Yoshi]] here. Either Yoshi or a [[Propeller Mushroom]] is required for all three [[Star Coin]]s (Propeller Mushroom is recommended).

==Overview==
The course has rocky mounds which Monty Moles pop-out of. There are also weight-like platforms that will fall down if there is too much weight on one side. The Monty Moles can also cause the weights to tilt and fall. Right after the Checkpoint Flag the player can go below the next platform into a Warp Pipe and obtain a Star Coin.

==Walkthrough==
[[Mario]] begins the level near two [[? Block]]s, one of which contains a power-up. Two semi-solid platforms are found nearby, and [[Monty Mole]]s jump out when Mario approaches them. Two more ? Blocks are then found, one of which contains a [[Yoshi Egg]]. More Monty Moles and semi-solid platforms are encountered, along with bushes with [[berry|berries]] on them that can be eaten by [[Yoshi]]. More ? Blocks are then found, along with a [[Koopa Troopa]] and a [[Koopa Paratroopa]]. More semi-solid platforms are found, one of which having a [[P Switch]] in a block above it that causes a trail of [[Blue Coin]]s that lead to a [[Hidden Block]] containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] to appear. Balance Lifts are then encountered, along with more Monty Moles. After the second Balance Lift and a Koopa Paratroopa, the [[Checkpoint Flag|midway point]] is found. A Koopa Troopa and two ? Blocks are found, followed by three Balance Lifts over a pit. A [[Red Ring]] in found in between the first two. Mario then encounters a ? Block containing another Yoshi Egg, along with Monty Moles and three more Balance Lifts. A [[Warp Pipe]] is found, leading Mario to an area with a Balance Lift and the [[Goal Pole|flagpole]].
==Enemies==

* [[Monty Mole]]s - 20
* Red [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s - 3
* Red [[Koopa Troopa]]s - 3

==[[Star Coin]]s==
* '''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is floating above five Coins and a Koopa Troopa.
* '''Star Coin 2''': Directly after the Checkpoint Flag, there is a hidden area with Coins and a Green Pipe below normal ground. Entering the Pipe leads Mario and co. to an underground area with a POW Block which can be used to make the second Star Coin, along with several other Coins, fall to the ground and become obtainable.
* '''Star Coin 3''': Once reaching the cave area, above the first set of weight platforms is a hidden area, and the third Star Coin is right in the middle of it.
