'''Winter Island''' is the main setting of [[Adventure Tours]] mode in ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' for the [[Nintendo DS]]. The location consists of three towns, accessed in the order of [[Frostown]], [[Polastraits]], and [[Icepeak]]. The other island is [[Dream Island]], which has [[Sparkleton]], [[Cubyrinth]], and [[Blizland]].

== In game ==
In Adventure Tours's opening cutscene, [[Bowser]] and [[Dr. Eggman]] capture the [[snow spirit]]s, except [[Frosty]] as he managed to escape, and Bowser uses his [[Fire Breath]] to melt all of the snow. The reason that he and Dr. Eggman canceled the Olympic Winter Games is to host their own instead. [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog]], with the help of [[Toad]] and their other friends whom they meet along the way, go on a journey to rescue the snow spirits, who can bring snow to an entire area, so that the Olympic Winter Games can commence.
