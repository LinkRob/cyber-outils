The '''Boo Bell''' is an [[List of items|item]] in the [[Mario Party (series)|''Mario Party'' series]]. Its use is to ring for [[Boo]] and ask him to steal [[coin]]s or a [[Star (Mario Party series)|star]], depending on how much the player can afford. The Boo Bell first appears in ''[[Mario Party 2]]'', where it can be obtained in an Item [[Minigame]] only. In ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', it appears more frequently, as it can be purchased from [[Koopa Kid]]. The item returns in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'', ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]'', depicted with Boo's face imprinted.

The Boo Bell is replaced by [[Boo's Crystal Ball]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

In ''Super Mario Party Jamboree'', the Boo Bell can be purchased from the Boo Shop in [[Rainbow Galleria]] for 15 coins (or 7 coins during a flash sale, or 30 coins of the player is under the effect of a [[Markup Sticker]]). Outside of that, it can rarely appear from an [[Item Bag]] or an [[Item Space]].
