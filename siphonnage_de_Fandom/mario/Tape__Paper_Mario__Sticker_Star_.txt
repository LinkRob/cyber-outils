==Description==

Tape is located at the start of World 3-8: [[Tree Branch Trail]]. It can be found through the [[Secret Door]] at the start of the level. Tape looks like regular sticking tape. Its base is mainly blue in color. It takes up about one sticker space in the [[Sticker Album]].

==Notes==
[[File:TapeSecretDoor.png|256px|thumb|right|[[Mario]] next to the secret door leading to Tape.]]
Tape, in battle, can inflict Taped status on enemies causing them not to move for a couple of turns. The Tape is defintally helpful for the first phase of the final fight with [[Bowser]]. Also,  if the player hits the "A" button as soon the Tape appears on the screen, then not only it will inflict enemies with the Tapped ailment but also deals 25 damage.
