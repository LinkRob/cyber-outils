'''Wario Factory''' is one of the fourteen playable courts in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. It appears in all the four sports. It is owned by [[Wario]]. In dodgeball, there is a [[conveyor belt]] for each camp, which can be activated if players of the opposite team walk on red buttons. In hockey, two conveyor belts are placed near each goal, and transport several items on the court: green Wario boxes which are unbreakable and can't be opened, purple chests which contain 10 [[coin]]s if players shoot the puck on them, and large bombs which explode when touched by a player. [[Shy Guy]]s are the only spectators for this court.

Wario Factory also appears as a selectable court for the party game, [[Feed Petey]].
