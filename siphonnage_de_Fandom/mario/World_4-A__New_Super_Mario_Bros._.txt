'''World 4-A''' is a level found in the game ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]'' It can be unlocked by spending five Star Coins.

== Walkthrough ==
[[File:4-A NSMB.png|thumb|right]]
Starting off here, go right and jump to the next platform where you can hit a [[Question Block]]. Then, jump and swing from the vine to reach the following platform. Do this for the next several platforms, and then you will come to a [[Paddle Wheel]] in the [[Poisoned Water]] with several ledges around it. Jump on the side ledges to movel the wheel across the poisonous water, making sure to jump to avoid dying. You'll have to use those wheels to get across the next several stretches of water, so you will have lots of practice using them.

After the level's [[Checkpoint Flag|midpoint]], use the wheel and jump from the [[Paratroopa]] to reach the upper ledge. Move to the right with the next wheel, then jump onto the Paratroopas to reach the first [[Star Coin]] in the air. Continue to the dry platform on the right, and bounce from the green Paratroopa to a vine high above. Swing from it to reach the pipe to the right.

In the room the pipe leads you to, jump on the blocks to reach the top of the structure and go right. Step on the [[P Switch]] to turn the [[Brick Block]]s into [[Coin]]s. Kick the shells around to get lots of points and even an extra life or so. Also, make sure to collect the second [[Star Coin]] in the middle of the room. Go down the pipe at the right side to exit here.

When you land, go right and jump onto the pipes when the [[Piranha Plant]]s go back inside. Up ahead you will see three [[Warp Pipe|pipes]] with three [[Piranha Plant]]s inside them; wait for them to go down, then jump over and go down the lowest pipe. In the room below, wall jump to get the final [[Star Coin]]. Go back up through the rightmost pipe. When you're above ground again, go right into the exit pipe. Swing on the vine to reach the upper platform if you want, then jump to the [[Flagpole]].

== [[Star Coins]] ==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' Above the second poison river, hop on the paddle and move it to the right. The first Star Coin is in the air, grab it. 
*'''Star Coin 2:''' After the first Star Coin, move the next paddle a bit to the left, and jump on the [[Paratroopa]] to gain some height to reach the vine. Swing to a ledge which has a Pipe and enter it. The Star Coin is located in some bricks with several Koopa Troopas. Hit the [[P Switch]] on to to obtain your prize.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Near the end of the level is a set of 3 Pipes formed as a staircase with Piranha Plants hopping out, enter the lowest Pipe. The final Star Coin is located in this room, grab it and exit the room from the lowest Pipe while avoiding the Piranha Plants.
