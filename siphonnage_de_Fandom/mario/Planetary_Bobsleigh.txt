The '''Planetary Bobsleigh''' is an item in the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==Description==
The Planetary Bobsleigh is a sports equipment required for participating in the event [[Blazing Bobsleigh]].

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
The Planetary Bobsleigh is located in [[Cubyrinth]] during [[Adventure Tours]] mode. To obtain it, the player must defeat [[Silver the Hedgehog|Silver]] and [[Blaze the Cat|Blaze]] at [[Ice Hockey (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Nintendo DS)|Ice Hockey]] in [[Polastraits]], then go to the back area of Cubyrinth 1 with a robo-ball. They must then complete the [[minigame]] [[Stop the Robo-Balls!]] with Silver to access the treasure chest containing the Planetary Bobsled. This will allow Mario and Sonic to challenge [[Big Bullet Bill]] in [[Blazing Bobsleigh]].
