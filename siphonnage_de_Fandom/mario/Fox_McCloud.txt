'''Fox McCloud''' is the hero and main character of the ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox (series)|Star Fox]]'' series and the leader of a mercenary team known as [[w:c:starfox:Star Fox (team)|Star Fox]], consisting of Fox, [[Falco Lombardi]], [[Slippy Toad]], [[Peppy Hare]], and (for a time, in games before ''Star Fox Zero'') [[Krystal]]. He is a master of the Arwing, a compact battle ship, and is also proficient as a sharp-shooter on land with his Blaster. Fox and his team commonly have to rescue the [[Lylat Cruise|Lylat System]] from [[Andross]] and other evil entities, such as the [[w:c:starfox:Aparoid|Aparoid]]s in ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox: Assault|Star Fox: Assault]]''. Fox was created by Takaya Imamura.

==History==
===''Mario Golf''===
The names "Fox" and "McCloud" can be seen on the score chart in the [[Mario Golf (Nintendo 64)|Nintendo 64]] and [[Mario Golf (Game Boy Color)|Game Boy Color]] versions of ''Mario Golf''.

===''Mario Artist: Paint Studio''===
A 3D render of Fox from ''[[w:c:starfox:Star Fox 64|Star Fox 64]]'' can be used like a stamp in ''[[Mario Artist: Paint Studio]]''.

===''Mario Kart 8'' / ''Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
[[File:Fox-Mii.jpg|thumb|The Fox outfit for Miis.]]
As of version 3.0, Fox only makes a cameo in the Mii Racing Suit menu of ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''; likewise, his [[amiibo]] is compatible with the game. Placing it on the [[Wii U GamePad]] makes a custom Fox-themed outfit for players to equip upon [[Mii]]s available.

===''Super Mario Maker''===
Fox appears as a [[Costume Mario#92|Costume]] in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. Fox's costume can be unlocked by scanning his respective [[amiibo]] or randomly by completing the 100 Mario Challenge on Expert difficulty. Fox's transformation sound is his radio voice clips from the original ''Star Fox''. The stage clear theme is the "Mission Complete" theme from the original ''Star Fox'', while his death sound is the respective theme from ''Star Fox'', including the Arwing exploding. Finally, pressing {{button|wii|padup}} has Fox pose facing away from the screen, while General Pepper's "Good luck!" voice clip plays.

===Unused appearances===
Along with a couple of other Nintendo characters, Fox was also [[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga/Beta elements|supposed to appear]] in [[Starbeans Cafe]] instead of [[Professor E. Gadd]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' but was scarped.

==In other media==
===''Super Mario: Mario im Wunderland''===
[[File:Starfox-cn.png|thumb|Fox in "Super Mario: Verloren in der Zeit".]]
Fox's first appearance relevant to the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] is in the ''[[Club Nintendo (German magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Super Mario: Mario im Wunderland]]", introducing himself as "the fox from the TV spot". He lands on [[Earth]] with his spaceship and scares away [[Donkey Kong]], [[Wario]], and [[Bowser]], who attacked Mario, [[Toad]], [[Kirby]], and [[Yoshi]].

He can also be briefly seen in the comic "[[Super Mario: Verloren in der Zeit]]" from the same magazine, watching the Olympics with [[Peppy Hare]] and [[Falco Lombardi]].

===''Super Mario-kun''===
In [[Super Mario-kun Volume 7|volume 7]] of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'', Fox and Slippy appear in a side panel, wondering about where the Arwing stolen from the [[collector]] is.

===''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie''===
{{main|Fox McCloud (Illumination)}}
Fox is one of the characters featured in ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.
