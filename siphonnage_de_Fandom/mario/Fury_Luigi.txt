'''Fury Luigi''' is an enemy in the ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign of ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

== Physical description ==
This goopy creature has the base appearance of [[Luigi]] but with a black, goopy body and bright, red eyes. He can transform into a spiky ball.
