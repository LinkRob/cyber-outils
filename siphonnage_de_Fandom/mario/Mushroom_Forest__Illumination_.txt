'''Mushroom Forest'''<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/356208486587039745/1091461391417884762/20230331_120345.png|title=20230331_120345.png (4032×1960)|accessdate=2023-04-05|work=Discord}}</ref> is located in the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]]. The forest consists of red and blue mushrooms; some tall and some small. It appeared in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.

== History ==
[[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] warp into the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]] through a [[Warp Pipe]], with Luigi ending up in the [[Koopa Kingdom|Dark Lands]] and Mario in the Mushroom Forest. Mario meets [[Toad (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Toad]] and they head to the [[Princess Peach's Castle (Illumination)|Peach's Castle]].
