'''Kotsuheri''' are obstacles in ''[[Wario Land II]]''.

==Description==
Kotsuheri are small spiked objects with an imprinted skull emblem and a propeller on top, allowing the Kotsuheri to hover in midair. Some Kotsuheri float slowly from one point to another, while others circle in the air a lot faster. Kotsuheri are invulnerable to any attack, and contact with one causes [[Wario]] to lose [[coin]]s. If Wario touches a Kotsuheri while riding an owl, the owl flies away.

==In ''Wario Land II''==
Kotsuheri are mostly encountered in areas with narrow platforms and several [[pit]]s or in portions of a [[level]] where Wario needs an [[Owl (species)|owl]] to assist him.
