'''Red Piranha Plants''' are a variant of [[Piranha Plant]] featured in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]''. They resemble Piranha Plants, particularly using their sprite design from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'', but with a red head instead of dark green. Behaviorally, a Red Piranha Plant differs in that it moves faster and even continues its pattern of emerging from its [[Warp Pipe]] if [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] are standing on it.

Like regular Piranha Plants, Red Piranha Plants have the same animation of biting their teeth as they emerge up and down their Warp Pipe. A Red Piranha Plant can be defeated from a [[fireball]] thrown by [[Fire Mario]] or being touched while Mario is under the invincibility effects of a [[Starman]].

As of ''[[Super Mario World]]'', a Piranha Plant's head has mainly been colored red with white spots. They are not the same as Red Piranha Plants, which are merely distinguishable from their behavior alone.
