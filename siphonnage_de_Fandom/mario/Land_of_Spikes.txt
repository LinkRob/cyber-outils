'''Land of Spikes''' (or '''World 6-1''') is the first level of [[World 6 (Super Mario Run)|World 6]] in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''. 
==Layouts==
<tabber>
|-|Pink Coins = The level begins with many semi-solid platforms, along with some [[Spike]]s. Not long after the beginning, the player must use a back arrow block to reach a row of [[? Block]]s with a Spike on it, then jump onto a platform to obtain the first Pink Coin. Not too long after, the player will reach a "staircase" with three Spikes, and must reach the third consecutive Spike to obtain the second Pink Coin. After dropping down, the player will encounter a few more Spikes before reaching a [[cannon]] and being fired out just above a Spike, collecting the third Pink Coin in the process. Immediately after, the player must jump onto another semi-solid platform to get the fourth Pink Coin. After approaching a [[Pause Block]], the player will reach a [[Red Ring]], and must collect all [[Red Coin]]s to obtain a [[Super Star]], then enter another cannon to fire and collect the fifth and final Pink Coin. After several more Spikes, the [[Goal Pole]] appears.
|-|Purple Coins = The level begins with many semi-solid platforms, along with some Spikes. Not long after the beginning, the player must use a back arrow block to reach a row of ? Blocks with a Spike on it, then jump onto a platform with more back arrow blocks, then jump again and wall jump to obtain the first Purple Coin. Not too long after, the player will reach a "staircase" with three Spikes, and must reach the third consecutive platform and jump to obtain the second Purple Coin. After dropping down, the player will encounter a few more Spikes before reaching a cannon and being fired out just below a Spike, collecting the third Purple Coin in the process. Afterwards, the player must stick to the higher path, and jump past some Spikes to reach the fourth Purple Coin. Immediately after, the player will reach a Red Ring, and must collect all Red Coins to obtain a Super Star, then jump past a cannon and reach the top platform. They must then jump from that platform to obtain the fifth and final Purple Coin. After several more Spikes, the Goal Pole appears.
|-|Black Coins = The level begins with many semi-solid platforms, along with some Spikes. Not long after the beginning, the player must hit a [[Brick Block]] just below a Spike to reveal an [[! Switch]], then use a back arrow block to reach the row of Brick Blocks with the ! Switch on it to activate it, then jump onto a platform with more back arrow blocks, then jump again and wall jump to obtain the first Black Coin. Not too long after, the player will reach a "staircase" with three Spikes, and must pass the second consecutive Spike and make their way through the alcove to obtain the second Black Coin. After passing the area, the player will encounter a few more Spikes before reaching a cannon and being fired out just above the topmost Spike, collecting the third Black Coin in the process. Afterwards, the player must stick to the higher path, and jump past some Spikes to reach a Red Ring, collecting all of the Red Coins via a [[trampoline]] to obtain a Super Star, plowing through the topmost Spike and collecting the fourth Black Coin immediately after. The player must then enter another cannon to fire and plow through another Spike, collecting the fifth and final Black Coin. After several more Spikes, the Goal Pole appears.
