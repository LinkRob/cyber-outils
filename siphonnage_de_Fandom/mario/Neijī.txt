'''Neijī '''(ネイジー, ''Neijī'') is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Land 2: 6 Golden Coins]]''.

==Physical description==
It is a screw that first appears lodged in the ground. When Mario approaches, it will wind itself out and bounce around the screen, trying to drill him from above. It can be defeated with any attack.
