'''Bowzooka''' is [[Bowser]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Bowzooka appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Bowser's weapon. The player can change the skins of the Bowzooka. Bowser will be unlocked during the Spark Hunter, [[Bedrock]]'s battle, since Bowser owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
The Bowzooka can attack multiple opponents from a distance, and anything it hits results in an explosion, damaging or destroying any cover the enemy is hiding behind.
