'''Glide Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]] and an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Glide Guys bear the appearance of a Shy Guy but with an umbrella protruding from their head.

===Behavior===
They can glide in tough amounts of [[wind]], similar to [[Baby Peach]] can with her [[Peach's parasol|parasol]]. A Glide Guy floats for a few seconds in the air after jumping. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] jumps onto a Glide Guy, it loses its umbrella and becomes a regular Shy Guy, which can be defeated can then be jumped on again to be defeated. Furthermore, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] instantly swallow a Glide Guy to convert it into an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]].
