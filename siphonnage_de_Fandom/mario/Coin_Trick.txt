'''Coin Trick''' is an [[Accessory (Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars)|accessory]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
When a character equips the Coin Trick, the [[party]] earns twice the coins after battle. The item can be bought from [[Frogfucius' Student]] in the Frog Coin shop in [[Seaside Town]]. Purchasing Coin Trick costs 36 [[Frog Coin]]s, and the item can be sold for 18 regular [[coin]]s.
