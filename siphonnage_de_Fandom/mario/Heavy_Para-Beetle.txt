'''Heavy Parabeetles''' are a larger variant of [[Parabeetle]]s, being about three times their height. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' in [[World 7-6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 7-6]]. Heavy Parabeetles are not as strong, and they collapse if jumped onto.

Just like the standard Parabeetle, these can only be defeated by charging into it with a [[Starman]]. They'll only hurt the player if he runs into its face.
