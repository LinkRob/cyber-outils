The '''Three Beautiful Sisters''' are a group of characters who appear in ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]''. They consist of '''Kris T.''', '''Miss T.''', and '''Felissa T.'''

==Appearance==
All members of the Three Beautiful Sisters are [[Toad (species)|Toads]] but with different colors. Kris T. is the one with green spots and green clothing, Miss T. has orange spots wearing orange clothing, and Felissa T. has purple spots with purple clothing.

==History==
The Three Beautiful Sisters live in [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)|Toad Town]] and are always seen standing just outside of [[The Dojo]]. Kris T. is the oldest sister, followed by Miss T., and then Felissa T. who is the youngest. They often discuss things such as fashion and makeup. Miss T. is involved in the chain letter quest where [[Mario]] and [[Parakarry]] must deliver one to her from [[Mr. E (Paper Mario)|Mr. E]] of [[Dry Dry Outpost]] indicating they may be friends.
