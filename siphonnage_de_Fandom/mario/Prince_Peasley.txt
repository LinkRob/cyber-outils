'''Prince Peasley''' is a major supporting character in the game ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. He is the son of [[Queen Bean]], a boss and the rightful heir to the throne of the [[Beanbean Kingdom]].

==Profile==
===Personality===
Prince Peasley is highly narcissistic yet good intentioned at heart. He appears to be a brave and headstrong character who often goes off to lead the charge, although this sometimes gets him into difficult situations. He also seems to have become friends with [[Luigi]], as Luigi thinks very highly of him.

===Powers and abilities===
Prince Peasley rides around on a flying bean, which is about the size of a pillow.

==Biography==
Prince Peasley was transformed into [[Dragohoho]] by [[Cackletta]], then sealed into an egg and placed at the top of [[Hoohoo Mountain]]. This allowed Cackletta to take over the Beanbean Kingdom without interference.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''==
[[File:Prince Peasley meeting MLSS.png|thumb|left|Mario and Luigi meet Prince Peasley for the first time in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar'']]
Prince Peasley is first seen after [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] defeat Dragohoho, upon which he turns back to Prince Peasley. He introduces himself to Mario and Luigi and, in an act of gratitude, gives them [[Peasley's Rose]], which allow the brothers to enter [[Beanbean Castle]].

Later on, when the [[Beanstar]] shatters into four pieces and scatters all over the Beanbean Kingdom, Prince Peasley bets Mario and Luigi 99,999,999,999,999 [[Mushroom Kingdom]] [[coin]]s that he will find the Beanstar pieces before them. However, during Prince Peasley's quest, he gets swallowed by the [[Piranha Bean]]. During his quest for [[Bean Fruit]]s, Luigi unknowingly freed Prince Peasley by defeating the Piranha Bean, although Peasley had become a lot smaller from being eaten. As such, Luigi uses his [[hammer]] to hit Prince Peasley and return to him to normal height. As a reward, Prince Peasley gives Luigi the Bean Fruit he had been searching for.

Mario and Luigi manage to beat Peasley to his wager, but Prince Peasley kept to his word and handed them the coins. However, Peasley had bested the duo again, as Lady Lima revealed that the current exchange rate is equivalent to 99 coins in the Beanbean Kingdom.

Later on, it was Prince Peasley's idea to replace the Beanstar with a [[Fake Beanstar|fake one]] to deliver to Cackletta. This plan ended up failing because [[Fawful]] spotted the switch.

In the end, after Mario and Luigi have defeated Cackletta, Peasley shows up in Bowser's throne room and announces that the entire castle will explode in three minutes. After they all escape, Peasley, [[Queen Bean]] and Lima bid goodbye to their Mushroom Kingdom friends, but not before Peasley attaches a large gift package to [[Toad Express|Peach's plane]]: Bowser being trapped under gift-wrapping paper.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Like [[Lady Lima]], Peasley's name is derived from a vegetable: in his case, peas. His name can also be a pun on "peaceably," since he wants to defeat [[Cackletta]] and return peace to the Beanbean kingdom.
