'''Toxicomet''' is one of the [[Spark (Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope)|Sparks]] featured in ''[[Mario + Rabbids: Sparks of Hope]]''. It is recruited during the side quest The Quest For Challenge.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Toxicomet is a green Spark. He has blue eyes resembling a Rabbid's eyes and a Rabbid mouth and Rabbid ears out the top.

===Personality traits===
When Toxicomet is first seen, he possesses a cowardly streak. Toxicomet asks Alkementor to boost his spirit, but before Alkementor is able to help him, the forces of Darkmess possess the cauldron. After Alkementor gets his cauldron back, Toxicomet dives into the cauldron to drink the resulting potion, bolstering his spirit, and becomes brave.

===Powers and abilities===

Toxicomet can give his user the power to [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] enemies with his special skill attack. He is also able to give characters defense against the Poison effect greatly as well as provide a lesser damage resistance versus other effects.
==Name==
Toxicomet derives his name from the word "toxin," which refers to poisonous substances, and "comet."

=== In other languages ===
In Japanese, Toxicomet is called Tokishisuta (written トキシスタ), from "toxin" and "star." His Spanish name is Toxicosmo, from ''toxina'' (toxin) and ''cosmos'' (cosmos). In Portuguese, he is called Cosmotoxus, from ''cosmos'' (cosmos) and ''toxina'' (toxin). His Italian name is Velenova, from ''veleno'' (poison, venom) and ''nova'' (nova).
