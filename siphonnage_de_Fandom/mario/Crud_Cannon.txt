The '''Crud Cannon''' is an object in the ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' episode "[[Recycled Koopa]]."

==Description==
The Crud Cannon is owned by [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]], and its purpose is to launch garbage, specifically [[Koopa trash]], far distances.

==In other media==
===''The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3''===
===="Recycled Koopa"====
In his plot to take over the Real World, such as by turning every [[human]] into [[Koopa Zombie]]s, King Koopa uses the Crud Cannon from his [[Doom Ship]] to dump Koopa trash all over [[New York City]]. After [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] cleans the entire city with the [[Trash Smasher]], turning the populace and [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] back to their original forms, he and Mario modify the Trash Smasher so that it can fly, then they chase away King Koopa, who retreats with his Crud Cannon.
