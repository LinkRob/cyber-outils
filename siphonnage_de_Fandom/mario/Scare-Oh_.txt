}}'''Scare-Oh!''' is an enemy from ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
They are ancient Egyptian lion statues that can prevent attacks from above by deploying spikes on their heads and can also attack by releasing purple coin like objects at [[Wario]]. Scare-Oh!s are rather uncommon enemies in the game as two can be found in the [[Smithsnorian Museum]], two more in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]], and finally two as well in [[Allergia Gardens]]. They can be defeated by any of Wario's attacks.

==Trivia==
*The Scare-Oh!'s name is a pun of the words "scare" and "pharaoh."
