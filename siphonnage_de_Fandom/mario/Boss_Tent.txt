{{InfoboxNeeded}}[[File:Boss_Tent.jpg|right|250px]]The '''Boss Tent''' is a building located on [[Craft Island]] after defeating [[Baby Bowser]]. The Boss Tent provides faster versions of all the bosses in the game. Each boss is unlocked by defeating the previous one. After defeating a  group of four bosses, a new Yoshi design will be unlocked. The designs are Bronze Yoshi, Silver Yoshi, and Gold Yoshi.
