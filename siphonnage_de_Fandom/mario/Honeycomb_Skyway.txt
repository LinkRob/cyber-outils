==Features==
This level is unlocked by completing either [[Blast Block Skyway]] or [[Towering Sunshine Seaside]], and its own completion unlocks [[Spiky Spike Bridge Sneak]].
==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1''': Close to the starting the level, hovering above the floating block. The player has a [[Boomerang Flower]] to grab the first Green Star.
*'''Green Star 2''': The second Green Star is above a [[Clear Pipe]] in the first area with a Lava Panel.
*'''Green Star 3''': Close to the ending the level, hovering above a Lava Panel to the left. The player needs a [[Boomerang Flower]] to obtain this Green Star.
