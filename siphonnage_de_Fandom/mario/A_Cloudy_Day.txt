'''A Cloudy Day''' is the first level of [[Gwarhar Lagoon]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 15.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 5 [[Lakipea]]s and 3 [[Beanie]]s. Its enemy captain is the Lakipea.

The final Battle contains 5 [[Lakitu]] and 3 Beanies. its enemy captain is the Lakipea.

After the final battle, the player recruits a Lakitu.

The battle can be replayed to recruit up to 1 Lakitu.
