'''Starring Artist''' is a General minigame in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Layout==
The characters are standing on an observation deck overseeing several hills. The deck features a stone ground and gray railing. For a majority of the minigame, a clear, starry night sky is shown on both the top screen and Touch Screen. There are both five-pointed yellow stars and stars represented by white dots in the distant background, but the five-pointed yellow stars are closer and more prominent.

==Opening==
The characters walk to the observation stage. The camera then scrolls up to show the starry sky, and a demonstration of a constellation forming is shown.

==Gameplay==
In all three rounds, the characters have to try and recreate two constellations, each depicting either a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' enemy or species via the Touch Screen. To do so, they have to connect the stars with the [[Stylus]], and a thick, light blue outline indicates which of the stars have to be connected. At the end of a round, each character receives nine, six, three or zero [[point]]s respectively, awarded from the quickest to slowest at connecting the two constellations. Whoever has the highest score after the final round is the winner.

==Controls==
*{{button|3ds|Stylus}}: Draw line

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''' (American English): ''Use your stylus to copy the constellations you see on the top screen as quickly as possible.''
*'''Rules''' (British English): ''Take a look at the constellations on the upper screen, then try to recreate them by drawing lines between the stars.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's title is a pun on "{{w|starving artist}}," a phrase that refers to a person who focuses on their art over anything else.
