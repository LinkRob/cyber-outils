'''Mimics''' are enemies which appear in ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns 3D]]''. They are black bushes with legs and glowing yellow eyes. These enemies only appear in the Sunset Shore level. Mimics chase [[Donkey Kong]] and [[Diddy Kong]] and can hurt them on contact. These enemies can be taken out by a jump or rolling into them. They may surprise the player so that he or she might not have enough time to react and will probably get hit.
