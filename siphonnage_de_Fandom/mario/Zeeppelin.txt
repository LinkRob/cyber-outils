The '''Zeeppelin''' is a type of [[blimp]] and an object featured in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''.

==In ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''==
The Zeeppelin is used to transport [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach]], [[Toadsworth]], and some [[Toad (species)|Toads]] from [[Peach's Castle]] to [[Pi'illo Island]]. While aboard the Zeeppelin, Luigi falls asleep has a dream [[Antasma]] coming aboard the blimp. After this, Luigi dreams of the Zeeppelin crashes, even though outside his dream the Zeeppelin landed safely outside of [[Pi'illo Blimport]],

==Etymology==
*The name originates from the word [[Wikipedia:Zeppelin|zeppelin]], a rigid blimp invented in Germany, but with an extra "e" added, just as several other subjects have a "Zee-" prefix in their name.
