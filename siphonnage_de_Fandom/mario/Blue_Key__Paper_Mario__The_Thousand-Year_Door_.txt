The '''Blue Key''' is an item in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==In ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''==
The Blue Key is located in [[The Great Tree]], in a room that can be accessed after defeating ten [[Jabbi]]s. The Blue Key can unlock the blue cell where ninety [[Puni]]es are imprisoned, including [[Petuni]], increasing [[Mario]]'s Puni army from 11 to 101 members.
