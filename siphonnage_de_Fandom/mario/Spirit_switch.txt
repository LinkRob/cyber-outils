A '''Spirit switch''' is an object that appears in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==History==
Spirit switches are found only in [[Blowhole Castle]] where they are common objects of the level. When they are first seen, they are always deactivated. In order to activate a Spirit switch, [[Wario]] must shoot at them using [[Cosmic Wario]]'s lasers. After doing so, any transparent platforms will be visible and Wario can then ride on them. However, this also holds true for certain enemies that were transparent and harmless to Wario at first but will become hostile after a Spirit switch has been activated.
