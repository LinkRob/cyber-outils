'''Tubs''', alternatively named '''Basins''', are an item which exclusively appear in the [[arcade]] ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' games. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP]]''.

==Description==
Using a Tub on a character causes them to be whacked on the head with the Tub. In doing so, the racer is temporarily flattened as if a [[Thwomp]] landed on them, preventing them from being able to move around. It is possible to pick up a pair of three Tubs at once, specifically named Triple Tubs.
