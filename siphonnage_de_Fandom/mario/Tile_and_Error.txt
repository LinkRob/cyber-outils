'''Tile and Error''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place on a raised square platform with a grid of 16 tiles in the middle of a stadium.

==Gameplay==
Both characters on each team must [[Ground Pound (move)|ground-pound]] the tiles to change them to the team's color, either red or blue. A character can ground-pound the edges and vertices between tiles to change more than one at once, specifically up to four of them. It is possible for a character to stun their opponent by either [[jump]]ing onto them, [[punch]]ing them, [[kick]]ing them, or ground-pounding them. If a character falls off the platform, a [[Fly Guy]] rescues them and puts them back onto the stage. When the 30-second [[time limit|timer]] runs out is, the team with more tiles matching their color wins, but if there are eight tiles corresponding to each team's color, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
*{{button|gcn|A}}: Jump
*{{button|gcn|B}}: Punch
*{{button|gcn|A}} (in midair): Ground-pound
*{{button|gcn|B}} (in midair): Kick

==Game data==
*'''Rules''': ''Ground Pound the panels to flip over your team's color.''
*'''Advice''': ''Ground Pound the edge between 2 panels to change both of them at once.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on the phrase "trial and error," meaning for one to keep trying at something until they succeed.
