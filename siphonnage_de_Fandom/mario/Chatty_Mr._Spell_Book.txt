'''Chatty Mr. Spell Book''' is a character in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Chatty Mr. Spell Book is an old-looking, brown, living spell book with a skull on his cover.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
Chatty Mr. Spell Book appears in [[Ashley]]'s stage, [[The Loquacious Spell Book]], the title of which even references Chatty Mr. Spell Book.

In the stage's opening cutscene, when Ashley unsuccessfully attempts to convert a flower sprout into a [[Monster Flower|carnivorous plant]], Chatty Mr. Spell Book appears to try and help her. He appears in the intermissions between while the player plays through Ashley's set of [[microgame]]s.

In the ending cutscene, Chatty Mr. Spell Book tells Ashley how to create a carnivorous plant, happily declaring Ashley to be ready to try the spell. Following this, Chatty Mr. Spell Book disappears, and Ashley successfully turns a different sprout into an enormous flower monster.
