'''[[Mario]]''' is a hero archetype character with a simple personality. When his demeanour is shown, Mario is shown to be a brave, cheerful, and kind-hearted hero.

===[[Pauline]]===
Mario's former girlfriend and love interest, Pauline was kidnapped by [[Cranky Kong|Donkey Kong Sr.]] holding her hostage in a construction site, but Mario defeated and held him hostage until [[Donkey Kong Jr. (character)|Donkey Kong Jr.]] had to save him. Later, [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] kidnapped Pauline several times in bouts of childish temper tantrums, forcing Mario to save her once again. While no longer together, they are both clearly close friends, as Mario invited her to his ventures in the [[Mario Toy Company]] as a guest or partner and is protective of her when’s she’s kidnapped, and during the events of ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', Pauline as mayor, trusts Mario on his adventures on rescuing Peach.
=== [[Princess Peach]] ===
Peach is Mario's current love interest.<ref name="romantic1">''[[Mario Party 5]]'' "Cutest Couple" 2003</ref><ref name="romantic2">''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' [[Rosalina]]: "The villain who took your special one took her far from here, to the center of the universe. Once our Star Power is restored, the observatory will be able to go there. Then you can reunite with your special one..." 2007</ref><ref name="romantic3">[[Nintendo Today!]] [https://x.com/SJF1_3D/status/1948395785994793380 "Mario is always cheerful and bright. He loves Princess Peach and makes a great pair with his twin brother." (Nintendo of Japan 2025)]</ref> The official Mario Portal website as well as [[Nintendo Today!]] mention in the English translation of their character profiles that "Princess Peach and Mario are good friends and help each other out whenever they can". However, the corresponding official original Japanese versions of the same character profiles are more explicit and directly mention that "Mario loves Princess Peach" and that "Mario and Peach are very close, and rely on each other", ultimately confirming that they are much closer than "just friends" in an official capacity.<ref>[https://www.nintendo.com/jp/character/mario/characters/index.html  「ピーチ姫のことが大好きで」 Translation: "He loves Princess Peach"  (Nintendo of Japan 2025)]</ref><ref>[https://www.nintendo.com/jp/character/mario/characters/index.html  「マリオとはとても仲が良くて、お互いを頼りにしています」 Translation: "Mario and Peach are very close and rely on each other."  (Nintendo of Japan 2025)]</ref>
According to lead developer [https://nintendo.fandom.com/wiki/Yoshiaki_Koizumi Yoshiaki Koizumi] in [https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2017/06/17/marios-developers-on-hat-capture-and-peachs-love-for-mario.aspx an interview], Mario has been romantically interested in Peach as far back as the original 2D ''Mario'' games, hence why he's always willing to come to her aid and rescue when she needs it.

When [[Bowser]] kidnaps Princess Peach, Mario always rescues her and defeats Bowser. While Peach is captive, she helps Mario by sending letters with power-ups to Mario believing that Mario will rescue her. Peach calls Mario her knight in shining armor in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' while Peach is called Mario's special someone in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. They worry over each other when they are apart, and have been shown to have mutual romantic feelings for each other. During the ending to ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' he even attempts to ask for her hand in marriage, but gets turned down because he gets caught one-upping Bowser's proposal. Despite Peach being able to get somewhat annoyed with Mario at times like in the aforementioned incident, where she demands that Mario and Bowser stop fighting over her hand in marriage, it is very rare, and she forgives Mario afterwards. In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', when Mario is in need of rescue, Peach comes to his aid without a second thought and when finally rescuing him at the end of the game, she excitedly jumps into Mario's arms, happily calls his name, dances with him until he carries her in a [https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/bridal_carry bridal carry], and finally gives her a bouquet of flowers, all while hearts are shown.

Peach and Mario have also been partnered together in spin-off games many times and appear in merchandise with romantic undertones such as ''[https://store.line.me/stickershop/product/9350/en Super Mario's Relaxing Life]'' LINE stickers, which depict them sitting together staring at the sunset while a heart animation is shown.

===[[Rosalina]]===
Rosalina befriended Mario in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. She trusted him enough to help her restore the [[Comet Observatory]], and in return she helped him to defeat [[Bowser]]. They have since been trusted friends.
===[[Toad (character)|Toad]]===
Toad is shown to be Mario's good friend. Toad usually helps Mario with supporting roles and gives him advice. In the ''Mario'' cartoons, Toad was shown to be a close friend of both Mario and Luigi's, as he would commonly be seen assisting them in stopping King Koopa's evil plots. Toad even helped in domestic situations, alongside Princess Peach. Throughout the issues of the Nintendo Comics System, the comics often portrayed Toad to being Mario's loyal and trusted sidekick. In ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and it's [[Super Mario 64 DS|DS remake]], Toad completely trusted Mario throughout his adventure. In the ''Super Mario Galaxy'' games, Toad often relied on Mario to back him and his [[Toad Brigade|brigade]] up in various situations during the adventure.
===[[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]===
Yoshi is Mario's best friend. They have gone on many adventures together, not to mention he saved and helped Mario when he was a [[Baby Mario|baby]]. Mario occasionally rides Yoshi, although Yoshi has been known to be as good as Mario on his own two feet. Mario and Yoshi are often seen together trying to foil [[Bowser]]'s plans. Yoshi can eat enemies too, which helps Mario a lot on his adventures.

=== [[Baby Mario]] ===
Mario is on good terms with his past self Baby Mario. During the [[Egg Island]] [[Yoshi's New Island|incident]], Mario (disguised as "[[Mr. Pipe]]") helps Baby Mario and [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] on their journey giving them power ups and assist them on bosses. Baby Mario is unaware "Mr. Pipe" was actually his future self. 

Mario would later met his past self again during the [[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time|Shroob Invasion incident]]. After Baby Mario and [[Baby Luigi]] defeated the remaining [[Shroob|Shroobs]] in [[Hollijolli Village]], they'll meet their future adult selfs and then team up in order to save the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] past from the Shroobs. Mario spends much of the adventure holding Baby Mario in a piggyback. When Mario and Luigi were kidnapped by two brainwashed [[L33t Hamm3r Broz.|Hammer Bros.]] in a [[Vim Factory]], Baby Mario would save him. After [[Time hole|traveling throughout the past]], [[Princess Shroob|defeating]] [[Elder Princess Shroob|enemies]], and eventually rescuing [[Princess Peach]], Mario and his companions stopped the Shroob invasion. Before Baby Mario returns to the past, he happily gives Mario his hammer as a good-bye gift much to Mario pleasure by appreciate lifting Baby Mario up. 

Mario cares about Baby Mario showing him as a caring parental figure, however he does steps in if any of his teammates misbehave.

=== [[Baby Luigi]] ===
Like Baby Mario, Mario is in good terms with Luigi’s past self, [[Baby Luigi]]. While they never interacted much during ''Partners in Time'', in [[Bowser's Castle|Baby Bowser’s Castle]] Mario was able to convince Baby Luigi to let go of the chain and come down by using [[Stuffwell]] as a protective cushion. In an underground cave of [[Koopaseum]], when [[Luigi]] was angry at the babies crying after hitting a hammer, Mario to wakes up and gets furious at him. He would then go even more furious at Luigi for leaving Baby Luigi as he flees away from [[Petey Piranha]] being very scared and crying.

===[[Sonic the Hedgehog]]===
Sonic is the mascot of Nintendo's competitive company [[wikipedia:Sega|Sega]] and Mario's friendly rival. In Adventure Mode on the DS version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'', he joins with Sonic to save the [[snow spirits]] from [[Bowser]] and [[Dr. Eggman]] in [[Winter Island]], as well as on the 3DS version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Nintendo 3DS)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]],'' stopping them from using the fog in [[London]]. Since they share similarities in their heroism, it is safe to say it has made them good friends as Mario [https://www.mariowiki.com/List_of_Mario_quotes#Miscellaneous stated in response] that he likes Sonic and views him as a friendly archrival rather than an enemy. Mario and Sonic have only starred in 2 separate crossover game series together so far: [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'']] and [[Mario & Sonic (series)|''Mario & Sonic'']].

===[[Cappy]]===
Cappy is Mario's companion in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]].'' He can capture enemies to allow Mario to control them.
=== [[Luigi]] ===
Luigi is Mario's younger twin brother. Luigi usually joins and accompanies Mario on his adventures, serving as the secondary protagonist (although Mario has been known to go on adventures by himself). Through the years, Mario and Luigi have maintained a brotherly love for each other as shown in games like ''Luigi's Mansion'' and the ''Mario & Luig''i series. They also (try to) save each other whenever one of them needs the other’s help, notably, in the beginning of ''Mario + Rabbids: Kingdom Battle'', when Luigi got sucked into the vortex, Mario tried to help him twice, but to no avail. When he later hears his brother calling him for help, Mario immediately guesses Luigi is in trouble and rushes to his aid. In addition, the two of them are normally paired together in sports titles and spin-offs and although Luigi has shown to be slightly jealous of Mario at times (as he admits in ''Paper Mario''), he loves his brother very much and looks up to him as a positive role model. 

For his part, Mario seems to allow his sibling rivalry to get the best of him when competing directly with Luigi. When Luigi receives a trophy in ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', Mario slapped Luigi's back and stepped on Luigi's foot intentionally.

=== [[Papa Mario and Mama Mario|Mario's Parents]] ===
Mario and Luigi's supposed parents made a cameo at the end of ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', although the game does not show their faces or names. Furthermore, ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'' has stated that these two are not Mario and Luigi's parents, as the true parents appear at the end of the game.
=== [[Lock]] ===
'''Lock''' talked to Mario in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. Lock disliked men with mustaches and without them but easily falls in love with girls. After Mario saves the world, Lock considered that girls like boys that are heroes.

===[[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]]===
Mario and Donkey Kong's relationships were anti-heroic. Donkey Kong gets excited for something only for Mario showing up, starting a [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (series)|conflict]] against each other. Like [[Cranky Kong]], he kidnapped three [[Toad (species)|Toads]] and then later kidnapped Mario's ex-girlfriend, [[Pauline]] [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis|few]] [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Minis March Again!|times]], but after the [[Mini Land Theme Park]] [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Mini-Land Mayhem!|incident]], Donkey Kong's life has been spared as he recovers and becomes friendly to Mario and often participate in [[Sports game|sports]], [[Mario Kart (series)|racing]] and [[Mario Party (series)|party celebrations]] together in a friendly rivalry competition. In ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong: Tipping Stars]]'', Donkey Kong kidnaps Pauline once again for no reason as Mario goes to stop him. However, unlike before, they don't fight as it was revealed the whole kidnapping was a setup surprise brought up by Pauline, Donkey Kong and Toads, making Mario feeling a little confused.

Even after Mario defeats Donkey Kong, they both have gone through different emotions as seen when Mario gets angry at Donkey Kong for stealing Mini Marios and taking Toads prisoners, he begins to cry, but Mario conforts Donkey Kong by giving him a Mini Mario toy. In the [[Mario vs. Donkey Kong (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Mario doesn't get angry at him. When Mario freed Pauline for the second time and goes after her only for Donkey Kong backing him off again, they angerly stare at each other for few seconds as rather than kidnap and rescue Pauline for a third time, Mario gives Donkey Kong a [[Mini Pauline]]. This shows that Mario still cares about Donkey Kong and does not consider him an enemy.

=== [[Bowser]] ===
Bowser is Mario's nemesis and foremost enemy. Bowser and Mario have had a long history of antagonism and rivalry for Princess Peach. However, Mario almost always defeats Bowser one way or another. Bowser always tries to find a way to take Peach from Mario and have her all for himself. Bowser is jealous of Peach's friendship with Mario, as he admits in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. Occasionally, Bowser is on the same side as Mario when they shared a common enemy, as shown in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. Oddly enough, Bowser has saved Mario from a few obstacles, as shown in the last cinema in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''; Bowser stops a [[Bullet Bill]] from taking out Mario. Of course, Bowser's only reason for saving Mario is so that he needs a rival and he wants to destroy Mario himself. Mario (along with Luigi and Yoshi) is also one of the three main heroes that Bowser hates, as they always get in the way of his plans.

However, at the end of ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'', when Princess Peach "rejects" both Mario and Bowser, Mario is patting the back of Bowser's shell in sympathy (which is unprecedented), before kicking off on his face to get back onto the Odyssey before Peach leaves without them.
=== [[Wario]] ===
Mario realized '''Wario''' stole [[Mario's Castle]] while he was rescuing [[Sarasaland]] including [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]]; but Mario managed to get his castle back. They were also rivals in ''[[Mario & Wario]]'' with the help of [[Wanda]] guiding him. Furthermore, they get along in the ''[[Mario Party (series)|Mario Party series]]''.

One of Wario's tips in [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'']] states that Wario calls himself Mario's childhood friend and biggest rival.

=== [[Phantom (Rabbid)|Phantom]] ===
When Mario encountered the Phantom during the [[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|first rabbids incident]], The Phantom insulted him about Mario's history for the Phantom's hatred of him; his debut in [[Donkey Kong (video game)|Donkey Kong]], the way he slides down every [[Warp Pipe|Pipe]], although, Mario doesn't like being teased about his body, runs in fear crying "Mamma mia!", and whatever Peach sees in the plumber, himself. The Phantom also enjoys watching Mario Kart and the part where Mario's in first, until the [[Blue Spiny Shell]] hits him.

=== [[Foreman Spike]] ===

Mario's first ever rival, Foreman Spike was the boss of both Mario & Luigi during their time working for Spike in the Wrecking Crew games. Spike would end up sabotaging both Mario & Luigi's work for unknown reasons. However after some time both of the Mario brothers left him to work as plumbers, but it wouldn't be too long until Mario re-encountered Spike again who worked for Bowser only to end up beaten by Mario himself.
