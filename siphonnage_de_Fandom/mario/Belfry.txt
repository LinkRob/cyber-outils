The '''Belfry''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
The Belfry is a two-story room in the [[Old Clockworks]]. It can only be accessed by taking the north door inside of the [[Movements]]. The first part of the room contains several crates and a ladder, and the [[Belfry Clock]] is located at the top.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
In C-Boss: [[Showtime]], [[Luigi]] must go to the Belfry to obtain one of the [[Dark Moon]] pieces. When he attempts to climb the ladder to the top, Luigi is launched up by a spring onto the Belfry Clock, which the [[Overset Possessor]] possesses. After defeating the Overset Possessor, Luigi can claim the Dark Moon piece to end the mission.

In C-Bonus: [[Outlandish Interruption]], while the Belfry can be accessed, the springboard can no longer launch Luigi, nor can he climb the ladder.
