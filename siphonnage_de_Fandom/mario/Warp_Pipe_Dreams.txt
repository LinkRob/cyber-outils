'''Warp Pipe Dreams''' is a Duel [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
The camera shows the [[Warp Pipe]] goal platform out of the maze and then switches to the players' view.

==Layout==
The minigame takes place in a square maze with Warp Pipes in it. Both characters are in separated mazes made of [[Warp Pipe]]s, colored red for the first player and blue for the second player.

==Gameplay==
The object for each character is to escape the maze by going through Warp Pipes and eventually finding the right one to get into the center area, which both characters can enter. There is a larger green Warp Pipe that leads out of the maze. Whoever gets out of the maze first is the winner. If neither character escapes within a certain time limit, the minigame ends in a tie.

The minigame is somewhat comparable to one part of [[Dungeon Duos]] in ''[[Mario Party 4]]'', except said minigame involves both characters on each team having to find the right pipe to enter.

==Ending==
The winner is at the goal platform, performing their winning animation as the [[Ukiki]]s cheer.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|Stick}}: Move
* {{button|gcn|A}}: [[Jump]]

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Head for the goal by jumping in and out of Warp Pipes.''
* '''Advice''': ''Remember the pipes that you use so you don't double back on yourself.''

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
*The minigame's name is a portmanteau of "Warp Pipe" and the phrase "pipe dreams," referring to unrealistic hopes for something to occur.
