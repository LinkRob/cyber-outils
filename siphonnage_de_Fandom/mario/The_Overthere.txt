'''The Overthere''' is a heaven-like place in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'' where lost souls go when their game ends. [[King Grambi]] rules the Overthere.

== History ==
Before the lost souls can go to the Overthere, [[Queen Jaydes]] has to weigh their sins to approve them worthy of going there.

Mario goes to the Overthere to find the last [[Pure Heart]], which turns out to be [[Luvbi]]. The Overthere is populated by [[Nimbi]]s and is once invaded by [[Skellobit]]s.
