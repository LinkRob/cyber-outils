==History==
Ruins Keys are only found in [[Yold Ruins]] and there are three of them in total. One is in a small room with two [[Spiky Tromp]]s that requires the [[Flip]] technique to get to, another after hitting a switch with a [[Squig]], and the third is found behind a large staircase requiring the Flip technique to get to. All of these keys unlock the locked doors of Yold Ruins.
