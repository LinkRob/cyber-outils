'''World 1-Cannon''' is the first world [[Cannon]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'', and it leads to [[World 5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 5]]. In order to unlock this Cannon, you must find [[World 1-3 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1-3]]'s secret exit.
==Location==
The secret exit is located before the final [[Star Coin]] of the level. A [[Yoshi]] or [[Propeller Mushroom]] is needed to find it. Once Mario and Co. reach the part of the level in the image below, they must get onto the ledge (top-left) and then fly/flutter jump onto the top. The tunnel to the exit can be found by running to the right.[[File:1cannonnsmbw2.jpg|thumb|left|The secret exit is above this section of the level]]
