A '''Shogun''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Shoguns are ant-like insect creatures encountered in [[Land's End]] and [[Belome Temple]]. In Land's End, [[Mario]] must follow the Shoguns in the sand whirls in the ground in order to make it to Belome Temple and eventually [[Monstro Town]].

==Battle==
Shoguns are rather strong foes in battle that mainly attack by biting at one of Mario's party members. Shoguns can use [[Vigor Up!]] to increase their attack power more, and usually follow up with the deadly [[Carni-Kiss]] move that may take a party member out in one hit.

===Strategy===
Shoguns are weak to [[Ice (element)|ice]], so [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] or using an [[Ice Bomb]] is effective against them. Because Shoguns are very strong, it is recommended to take them out as quickly as possible.
