{{InfoboxNeeded}}[[File:Nintendo_Land_-_Luigi's_Ghost_Mansion_logo.png|thumb|308px]]'''Luigi's Ghost Mansion''' is a minigame found in ''[[Nintendo Land]]''. The minigame is based on ''[[Luigi's Mansion (video game)|Luigi's Mansion]]''. It revolves around A [[Mii]] playing a [[Ghost]], and Mii(s) dressed as [[Luigi]], [[Mario]], [[Wario]], or [[Waluigi]]. The players hunting the ghost will use flashlights and shine it on him/her, but the lights will go out soon and need to be replaced with batteries that will pop up. The ghost will hunt down the players at least three times. The game has five areas.
