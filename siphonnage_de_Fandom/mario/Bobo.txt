'''Bobo''' is a large bird in the {{series|Wario Land}}. It first appears in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]'' then returns ''[[Wario Land II]]''. In both games, Bobo is featured as the boss guardian of the [[SS Tea Cup]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bobo's most recognizable characteristic is the large skull emblem on his chest. In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3'', Bobo appeared to have black feathers due to the [[Game Boy]] system and some Japanese media (such as the ''[[Perfect Edition of the Great Mario Character Encyclopedia]]'') depicting him as a grayscale or monochromatic. In a ''Super Mario'' Kodansha issue and the [[Game Boy Color]] version of ''Wario Land II'', Bobo has purple feathers, a yellow beak, and a yellow belly. ''Wario Land II'' appears to depict Bobo as smaller yet still larger than Wario.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
[[File:BoboWL1.png|frame|left]]
During his battle, Bobo attempts to attack Wario by either swooping into him, spitting [[Watch]]es at him, or by trying to blow him off into a pit. To attack Bobo, Wario must jump on his head, which he can reach by jumping from the Watches. Once Wario has done this three times, Bobo is defeated.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Wario Land II''==
[[File:Defeat Bobo WLII.png|thumb|Wario fighting Bobo in ''Wario Land II'']]
Bobo is fought in chapter two's fifth and final story, [[Defeat Bobo!!]]. Here, Bobo's only direct attack involves it repeatedly swooping down at Wario then flying back up. Bobo can also conjure wind from the ground by flapping its wings. Wario has to [[crouch]], otherwise the wind blows him away out of the boss fight and to an earlier part of the level. Wario can attack Bobo by either [[jump]]ing on it twice or by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]]ing it, the latter being equivalent to two hits.

Every time Bobo takes two hits from a jump attack or one hit with a Ground Pound, Bobo changes its movements in its direct attack. After taking its first two hits, Bobo changes from performing a large and slow curve to moving in a w-like shape. After four hits, Bobo moves in three lines with 90 degree sharp turns. After taking six hits or being Ground Pounded three times, Bobo is defeated.

==In ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]''==
Bobo is one of the names featured  score chart, but it is not clear if it refers to Bobo from the ''Wario Land'' games.
