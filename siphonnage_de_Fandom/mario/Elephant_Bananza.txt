'''Elephant Bananza''' is a transformation of [[Donkey Kong]] that appears in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. It is one of the many Bananza forms he can transform into and is his fourth Bananza transformation, being granted to him by the [[Elephant Elder]] in the [[Tempest Layer]] after finding the [[Ancient Record]] for her.

==Physical description==
As Elephant Bananza, Donkey Kong is now a giant elephant who is very muscular. He is also mainly black in color with some purple hue on him as well as having yellow on his nails and the patters on his trunk. His tusks and his hair are also yellow on them and are meant to resemble bananas.

==Powers and abilities==
Elephant Bananza's main power is being able to inhale large amounts of terrain with his trunk. This form is extremely useful in clearing large amounts of terrain in a short amount of time as well as getting rid of harmful lava. This inhale ability can also work on certain enemies such as [[Crockoid]]s when they have lava protecting themselves. Elephant Bananza also has another unique ability in being able to store up any material and generate them as a chunk that can either be used to chuck at enemies or hold down cloud platforms. This ability is known as the Trunk Chunk. The chunks vary depending what Donkey Kong has previously inhaled with his Elephant Bananza form.

While very useful especially for terrain-heavy layers such as the [[Landfill Layer]], Elephant Bananza's main weakness is his limited breath. If Donkey Kong uses the inhale ability for too long, he will get tired and will need a few seconds to rest. Donkey Kong must use his inhale ability in careful doses to not wear himself out and leave himself wide open to attacks. Donkey Kong can upgrade his lung capacity in Elephant Bananza from the skill menu as well as increase the amount of chunks he can store. After Donkey Kong completes the Elephant Banazna Rehearsal during the post-game, he will be able to use the Golden Vacuum to turn the area he inhales into gold making it quite useful for increasing his gold supply in a short amount of time. 

== Trivia ==

* Uniquely, Donkey Kong is smaller than the actual elephants in the game with his Elephant Bananza active. All of the other Bananza transformations make him just as large as their base animal.
