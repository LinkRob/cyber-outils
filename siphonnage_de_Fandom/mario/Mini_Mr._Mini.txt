A '''Mini Mr. Mini''' is an item found exclusively in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
When used, a Mini Mr. Mini can briefly [[Tiny (ailment)|shrink]] enemies, which drops their Attack power. It can be purchased for ten [[coin]]s at [[Pungent's Shop]] in [[The Great Tree]]. It can also be dropped or used by enemies. This item is best for use against enemies with large Attack power.
