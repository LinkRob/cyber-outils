'''Battle Course 2''' is one of the four [[Battle Course]]s in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]''.

This course is lava-themed with it taking place inside of [[Bowser's Castle]]. The course design itself seems to be very similar to how it is in [[GBA Battle Course 3]]. The middle of the course has a chain-locked area with small squared-shaped holes in the metal. Four lava-shaped squares surround the chain-enclosed area. Four ramps surround the center.
