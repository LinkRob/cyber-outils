|name=Hopslipper x3 (Battle Card)}}'''Hopslipper x3''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hopslipper x3 is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s "Long Jump x3" attack.  It is three of the [[Hopslipper (Battle Card)|Hopslipper]]'s scattered on the edges of the card, accompanied by a green/white checkerboard background.
