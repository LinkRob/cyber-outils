The '''''New Super Mario Bros''''' series is a series of 2D ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]'' side-scrolling games for the [[Nintendo DS]], [[Wii]], [[Nintendo 3DS]], [[Wii U]], and [[Nintendo Switch]] consoles, having strong resemblances to the classic ''Mario'' games for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] and the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]] in the 1980's and 1990's. Some of Mario's voice clips are reused from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'', and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''.

All the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' games are almost alike, with new games adding some new levels that look virtually the same, some new power-ups, some old enemies from the older ''Mario'' games or some new enemies.

==Gameplay==
''New Super Mario Bros.'' games are a new take on classic side-scrolling ''Mario'' games. The games play very similar to the original ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]'' game. New power-ups, like the [[Mega Mushroom]], introduce new types of gameplay. In the ''New Super Mario Bros.'' games for Wii and Wii U, there can be up to four to five players (four on Wii, five on Wii U, including the new controller).

==Trivia==
*Out of the games of these series, only ''New Super Mario Bros.'' has featured mini-games (as most of them are from ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'', along with some new ones and have multi-player).
*All of the games' underground music is based on the underground music for ''Super Mario Bros.''
*All the Games in the series have [[Red Flagpole|Red-Flag Exits]].
*Starting in ''New Super Mario Bros Wii'' the [[Koopalings]] appear as bosses.
