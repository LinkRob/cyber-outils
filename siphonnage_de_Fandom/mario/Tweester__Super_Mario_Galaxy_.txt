'''Tweesters''' are [[tornado]]-like enemies in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They possess eyes and have debris spinning inside them, such as skulls and bones. Bone Twisters can be helpful at times, being used to traverse obstacles. Touching one also causes them to spin around. If the player then [[spin]]s using the [[Wii Remote]], the Bone Twister makes [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] go flying into the air and spins his arms around very quickly like a helicopter. Two Bone Twisters have spiked debris spinning around them, which damage Mario or Luigi if he makes contact with them. They appear only in the [[Dusty Dune Galaxy]] in the [[mission]]s [[Blasting through the Sand]] and [[Purple Coins in the Desert]].

Unlike the ''[[Paper Mario]]'' enemy of the same name, Tweesters sport a significantly different pair of eyes and are native to deserts.

Tweesters have their own ''Super Mario Galaxy'' trading card, which is also the source of their name.
