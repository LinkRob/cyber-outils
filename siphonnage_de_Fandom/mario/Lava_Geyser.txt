'''Lava Geysers''' (also called '''Vents''', '''Lava Spouts''', and '''Lava Fountains''') are eruptions of lava that occur periodically from [[lava]] and fiery pits. They first appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]'', and have been a recurring feature in ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related media ever since.

Lava Geysers usually appear in between gaps that [[Mario]] and friends are required to jump across. They are similar to [[Water Geyser]]s and [[Sand Geyser]]s with the biggest difference being they cannot be safely walked across and will burn those who touch them. In all games they appear in, Lava Geysers cannot be destroyed or dissipated, the heroes need to either wait for the Lava Geyser eruption to die down before continuing or jump over the Lava Geyser to avoid the inferno.

==In {{series|Mario vs. Donkey Kong}}==
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Retro.png|thumb|left|A Lava Geyser in ''Mario vs. Donkey Kong''.]]
Lava Geysers appear in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong]]''. In this game, they commonly appear in earlier levels of [[Fire Mountain (world)|Fire Mountain]] propelling platforms into the air allowing Mario to reach higher ground.
{{br|left}}
===''Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Mini.png|thumb|right|A Lava Geyser lifting up a platform in Lava Dome.]]
Lava Plumes appear again in ''[[Mario vs. Donkey Kong 2: March of the Minis]]'' in [[Lava Dome]]. In this game, they once again raise platforms into the air allowing [[Mini Mario]]s to jump on them them to reach higher platforms which can be useful for reaching high ledges however touching the Geyser itself will scorch a Mini Mario. In the [[Construction Zone]], Lava Geysers and their Platforms can be placed in the [[Lava Dome]] Kit as well as [[Special Kit 2]] and [[Special Kit 3]].

==In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''==
[[Image:Fury Volcano Activity.png|thumb|left|Peach near a Lava Geyser.]]
Lava Geysers appear in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'', in this game, they are found in various levels of [[Fury Volcano]] erupting from the cracked floorings periodically. Touching a Lava Geyser will cause damage. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can make use of the Lava Geysers by picking up a Tiki Platform and placing it on the Lava Geyser which can then be stood on to reach higher ledges.
{{br}}
==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Volcano Eruption.png|thumb|right|[[Mario]] near several Lava Geysers.]]
Lava Geysers return in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' appearing in the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]] and [[Bowser's Galaxy Reactor]]. In this game, they are an outright hazard and will burn you if you touch them. They erupt from lava in areas around the lava ocean or in between platforms which require a jump.

===''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''===
[[Image:Lava Plumes Wii.jpg|thumb|left|Lava Geysers from ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.]] 
Lava Geysers return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' commonly appearing in [[World 8 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8]]. In this game, they erupt from the Lava periodically and can scorch you if you touch them. Lava Geysers can be found in [[World 8-1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-1]], [[World 8-5 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-5]], [[World 8-7 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 8-7]], and [[World 9-6 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 9-6]]. In most levels, Lava Geysers periodically erupt out of the lava requiring you to wait for them to die down before continuing, in World 8-7, Lava Plumes must be jumped as you ride the [[Bonecoaster]]. Lava Geysers can also be seen around the World Map of World 8.

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Galaxy.png|thumb|right|Two Lava Geysers erupting from the Lava as seen in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2''.]]
Lava Geysers reappear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' retaining their role from the previous game, they can once again scorch [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) on contact and require timing or maneuvers to get around. They are seen in [[Bowser's Lava Lair]] the [[Shiverburn Galaxy]], the [[Melty Monster Galaxy]], and [[Bowser's Galaxy Generator]].

===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Lake.png|thumb|left|Lava Geysers in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.]]
Lava Geysers are seen again in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. In this game, they are seen erupting from the lava around various Castle levels elevating platforms allowing [[Mario]] (or [[Luigi]]) to jump on them to reach higher ledges, in some levels such as [[World 8-6 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 8-6]] and [[World 8-Bowser's Castle (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 8-Bowser's Castle]], Lava Geysers are an outright hazard and must be avoided at all costs. Lava Geysers can also be seen around the lakes of fire outside the level as well, a much larger Lava Geyser is also seen in [[World 5-2 (Super Mario 3D Land)|World 5-2]] erupting out of the lava down in the chasm below.

===''New Super Mario Bros. U/New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Deluxe.jpg|thumb|right|A Lava Geyser from ''New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe''.]]
Lava Geysers return in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' as well as ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]'' (they don't reappear in ''[[New Super Luigi U]]''). In these games, they only appear in [[Meteor Moat]]. Once again, touching these eruptions will cause damage. Also, the eruptions of the Lava Geysers are much shorter than they were in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'' making avoiding them more manageable.

===''Super Mario 3D World/Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''===
[[Image:Lava Plumes Battle.jpg|thumb|left|Lava Geysers as seen during the [[Bowser]] battle in ''Super Mario 3D World''.]]
Lava Geysers return in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]'' once again acting as they did in previous games, they appear in various lava-themed levels such as [[Bowser's Lava Lake Keep]]. Lava Geysers also appear during the boss fight with [[Bowser]] and his [[Bowser Mobile]] erupting from the lava below through the mesh parts of the roads. The Lava Geysers eruptions is also easier to time in this game than in previous games.

===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
[[Image:Lava Plumes Moon's Core.jpg|thumb|right|Lava Geysers as seen in ''Super Mario Odyssey''.]]
Lava Geysers appear in ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]'' commonly being found in the [[Luncheon Kingdom]] and the [[Moon Kingdom]] in the [[Underground Moon Caverns]]. In the Luncheon Kingdom, Pink Lava Geysers are seen all around the Kingdom around the boiling hot lakes, when Mario [[capture]]s a [[Podoboo]], he can safely touch a Lava Geyser without being hurt. [[Mario]] can also ride Lava Geysers to reach higher platforms while having captured a Podoboo. In the Moon Kingdom, since there are no Podoboos, Lava Geysers are an outright hazard, when Mario captures a [[Parabones]] to fly across the fiery lakes, Lava Geysers will erupt from the lava at which point he must go around. If the Parabones touches a Lava Geyser, it will disappear.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party DS''===
[[Image:Crater Crawl DS.png|thumb|left|A Lava Geyser erupting from the rising lava in Crater Crawl.]]
In ''[[Mario Party DS]]'', Lava Geysers are seen in the minigame [[Crater Crawl]]. During the minigame, players are climbing out of the volcano and every so often, Lava Geysers will erupt from the rising lava below, the players have to move out of the way as this happens. Any players scorched by one of the Lava Geysers are immediately eliminated from the game. The lava below starts bubbling to warn players where the Lava Geysers will erupt. As the minigame progresses, multiple Lava Geyser will start erupting giving the players less leg room whilst climbing the volcano crater, whoever reaches land first or is the last player remaining is the winner.
{{br|left}}
===''Mario Party 9''===
[[Image:Volcanic Lava Plumes.png|thumb|right|A Lava Geyser as seen in the background.]]
In ''[[Mario Party 9]]'', Lava Geysers are seen in the background for the [[Bowser Jr.]] minigame [[Bowser Pop]], in the minigame, they can be seen erupting from the [[lava]] sea in the background but have no impact on the minigame apart from aesthetic effect. The Lava Geysers' design in this game closely resembles how they looked in ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii''.

===''Mario Party: Island Tour''===
[[Image:Boiling Hot Lava Plumes.png|thumb|left|Lava Geysers seen in the boss intro.]]
In ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]'', Lava Geysers are seen in the boss battle minigame with [[Chain Chomp]], [[Chain Chomp's Lava Lunge]]. In the minigame, they can be seen erupting from the lava pool around the platform where the battle takes place. Two Lava Geysers are also seen in the minigame's introduction as the Chain Chomp makes his entrance however the Lava Geysers have no effect on the battle and are only there for aesthetic effect. In this game, the Lava Geysers look like how they did in ''Super Mario 3D Land''.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
Lava Geysers have been a primary obstacle in many lava-themed courses such as [[Bowser's Castle]]. In each of the games they appear in, Lava Geysers will knock those who drive into them high into the air.

===''Mario Kart Wii''===
[[Image:Lava Plumes MKW.jpg|thumb|left|[[Waluigi]] approaching some Lava Plumes in Bowser's Castle.]]
Lava Plumes are seen in various lava-themed courses of ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', the first ''Mario Kart'' game to feature them. They are first seen in [[Grumble Volcano]] where they erupt from the lava after parts of the track have crumbled apart possibly indicating that the eruptions are the cause for the track's crumbling. The Lava Plumes will begin to erupt from the former location of the ground, they can also erupt around the racers which they must maneuver around. Lava Plumes are also seen in [[Bowser's Castle]] where they erupt from the lava below the mesh tracks and from the ground near the end of the track. The eruptions of the Lava Plumes cause the ground to swell up forming mounds as they erupt allowing players to preform tricks off the mounds formed by the Lava Plumes while avoiding the Lava Plumes in the mound's center. Lava Plumes have also been added to the game's remake of ''[[Mario Kart 64|Mario Kart 64's]]'' Bowser's Castle erupting from the lava on the sides of the bridge and near the end jump on Laps 2 and 3.

Lava Plumes appear in in a Knex Set based on the game, also being the name used for the merchandise, and it involves [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] racing on a track dodging the Lava Plumes.

===''Mario Kart 7''===
[[Image:Lava Plume 3DS.jpg|thumb|[[Daisy]] driving by a Lava Geyser in ''Mario Kart 7''.]]
Lava Geysers return in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' appearing in [[Bowser's Castle]] erupting from the lava by the ramps making jumping the ramps a rough gamble. Lava Plumes are also seen in [[Big Donut]] where they erupt from the Lava and spin out those who glide into them sending them plummeting into the lava below.

===''Mario Kart 8/Mario Kart 8 Deluxe''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Chasm.png|thumb|left|[[Baby Peach]] avoids the Lava Geysers in Bowser's Castle.]]
Lava Geysers appear again in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' where they appear in [[Bowser's Castle]] at the gliding point near the track's end. They are seen erupting from the Lava below and scorch any racers who glide into them sending them plummeting down below. Lava Geysers are also seen in the games' remake of [[Grumble Volcano]] erupting from the lava in the areas where the crumpled ground once was.
{{br|left}}
==In ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''==
[[Image:Rumble Volcano.png|thumb|The Lava Geysers seen around the [[Rumble Volcano]] lifting up the platforms in ''Paper Mario: Sticker Star''.]]
Lava Geysers are seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]'' in the level [[Rumble Volcano]]. In this game, they are seen lifting the various platforms which are used to traverse the volcano. The Lava Geysers can elevate to any level allowing [[Mario]] to stop off on certain ledges to obtain items and more.

==In {{franchise|Yoshi}}==
===''Yoshi's Woolly World / Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World''===
[[Image:Lava Plume Smoke.png|thumb|left|A Lava Geyser as seen in ''Yoshi's Woolly World''.]]
Lava Geysers return in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' as well as ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''. In these games, they are commonly found in volcanic levels with [[lava]] such as [[Lava Scarves and Red-Hot Blarggs]]. Touching a Lava Geyser will cause a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to take damage, as usual, they erupt periodically from the lava below and cannot be destroyed. They can also erupt through certain platforms such as [[Donut Lift]]s. Lava Geysers appear to be more smoky in this game as well.
{{br|left}}
===''Yoshi's Crafted World''===
Lava Geysers can be found in [[Poochy's Magma Run]] and [[Mr. Geary's Factory]], acting the same as usual.
