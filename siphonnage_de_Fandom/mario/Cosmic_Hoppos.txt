'''Cosmic Hoppos''' is a course of [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
==Layout==
Most of the level is vertical. The player must use [[Hoppo]]s to scale the level and kill [[Smogrin]]s along the way.  

At the end of a level, there are two holes before the [[flagpole]]. These two holes can be filled with Hoppos and jumping on the right one will lead you to a secret area. In the area the player can obtain the [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will make Hoppos so bouncy, they send you into space. In space the player will have to avoid {{lw|Spike Trap}}s and Hoppos that have the chance of bumping the player into a spike.
==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
The first 10-flower coin is under the first {{lw|Checkpoint Flag}} that can be reached by bouncing off of a Hoppo that can be brought in from a ledge to the right.

The second 10-flower coin is present after the first checkpoint flag that can be reached by bouncing off of a Smogrin.

Shortly before the flagpole, there is a tunnel with a Hoppo. Going into the tunnel will enter the player into a secret area when Hoppos can be pushed into holes and bounced on to obtain the final 10-flower coin.{{SMBWNav}}
