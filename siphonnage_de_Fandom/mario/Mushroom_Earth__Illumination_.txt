The '''Mushroom Earth''' is a location that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series produced by [[Illumination]] and its spin-offs. It is an alternate version of the planet [[Earth (Illumination)|Earth]], home to the [[Mushroom World (Illumination)|Mushroom World]] continent and its kingdoms.
