The '''T-Rex''' (''Tyrannosaurus Rex)'' is a minor antagonist appearing in [[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie|''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'']]. It is based off and looks nearly exactly like the [[T-Rex]] from [[Super Mario Odyssey]]. After the chase, the T-Rex was shot and turned into a baby by the Super Scope Yoshi used, before turning Baby Mario, Baby Luigi, and Baby Toad back to their own grown-up selves.

==Trivia==
*Its roar shares the same roar from the ''Jurassic Park'' and ''Jurassic World'' franchises.
