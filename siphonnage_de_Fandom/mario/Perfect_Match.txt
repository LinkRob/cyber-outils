'''Perfect Match''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''. As one of [[Wario]]'s microgames, it appears under the Touch Training folder.

== Gameplay ==
As its name implies, Perfect Match gives the player a set of cards whose front sides are displayed. Afterwards, the cards flip and one flips back to show its front, and the player must find the card it matches. If the player selects that card, they win; otherwise, they lose. Level 1 features two pairs, while the higher two levels have three pairs apiece, with Level 3's cards appearing similar to each other.
