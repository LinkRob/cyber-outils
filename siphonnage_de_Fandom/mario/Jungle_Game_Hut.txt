The '''Jungle Game Hut''' is a location in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Description ==
The Jungle Game Hut is a gambling hall found in the [[Jungle Area]] of [[Shroom City (Mario Party Advance)|Shroom City]]. Its interior consists of large, leafy plants in the background. There are yellow slot machines to the left. In front of each machine is a stool resembling a tree, due to its green seat and brown cylinder. On the right side is a table that has several casino chips on it. Four stools with the same tree-like appearance surround the table. 

== In ''Mario Party Advance'' ==
The only character found in the Jungle Game Hut is [[Hoot]], who regularly goes to the Jungle Game Hut. Hoot eventually reaches a massive losing streak and incurs a giant debt. This gives the player the opportunity accept Hoot's quest, [[Debt's a Hoot]], in which they have to win the minigame [[Stop 'Em]] to help pay off his debt. The reward for clearing Stop 'Em is unlocking it in Party Mode.
