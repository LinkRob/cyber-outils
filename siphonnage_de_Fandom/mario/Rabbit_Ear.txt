A '''Rabbit Ear''' is an item in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
A Rabbit Ear is a small carrot with eyes with large rabbit ears and two faint, whisker-like markings to its left and right each. A racer can throw a Rabbit Ear backward onto an opponent. If an opponent is hit, they sprout rabbit ears and [[hop]] uncontrollably for seven seconds.
