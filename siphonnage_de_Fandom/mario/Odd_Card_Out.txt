'''Odd Card Out''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''. This minigame's name is derived from the phrase "odd one out." On the official website for ''Mario Party 6'', there is a minigame of the same name that is based on and played exactly like this minigame.

This minigame appears on a page in the [[Miracle Book]]. The page is named "Card Crazy." This features [[Daisy]] and [[Boo]]. Activating the page shows [[Brighton]] and [[Twila]] on the card and a [[Shy Guy]] popping up from behind the [[Goomba]]s.

==Opening==
[[File:MP6 Peach2.jpg|thumb|''Mario Party 6'' artwork of [[Princess Peach|Peach]] playing Odd Card Out]]
The players cheer as the camera passes by them, each of them holding up a sign with a random button on it.

==Gameplay==
The minigame takes place in a game show-like room with three screens. Players have to press {{button|gcn|A}}, {{button|gcn|B}}, or {{button|gcn|X}} depending on which of the three cards in front of them is different from the other two. The cards include common enemies, which are [[Koopa Troopa]]s, Goombas, [[Piranha Plant]]s, Shy Guys, and [[Bob-omb]]s. Some differences are easy for the players to notice (such as a red Bob-omb), but some are more subtle (such as the top of a Shy Guy's robe). Once someone picks a card, the round ends. If the player's choice is correct, they earn a point. However, the player is flattened by a [[Thwomp]] if the choice is incorrect, causing them to miss the next round. Victory goes to the player who earns two points first. If ten rounds pass without anyone reaching two points, the minigame ends in a tie.

==Ending==
The card screens all show one part of a rainbow as the winner's platform glows and the players perform their winning and losing animations. If the minigame ends in a tie, nothing else happens as the players perform their losing animations. If a player is still dazed by a Thwomp when the minigame ends, they do not perform their losing animation.
