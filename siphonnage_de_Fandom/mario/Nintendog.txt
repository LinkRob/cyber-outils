'''Nintendogs''' (also simply referred to as '''Dogs''') are a species of dogs starring in the ''[[w:c:nintendo:Nintendogs (series)|Nintendogs]]'' series of games introduced for the [[Nintendo DS]]. Their first appearance in a ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]''-related game is ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In ''Super Mario Odyssey''==
A Nintendog wearing a fedora, specifically a {{w|Shiba Inu}}, appears in the [[Metro Kingdom|Metro]], [[Seaside Kingdom|Seaside]], [[Moon Kingdom|Moon]], and [[Sand Kingdom]]s. If [[Mario]] play fetch with his cap enough, the Nintendog digs up a [[Power Moon]]. The Nintendog can also appear in the [[Odyssey]] once 250 Power Moons have been collected.
