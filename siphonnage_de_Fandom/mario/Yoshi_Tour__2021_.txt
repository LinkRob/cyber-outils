'''Yoshi Tour''' was the fortieth Tour in [[Mario Kart Tour|''Mario Kart Tour'']], that began on March 23, 2021 and ended on April 6, 2021.

This Tour featured [[Yoshi (Egg Hunt)]] which returned from [[Yoshi Tour (2020)]] along with [[Blue Yoshi]], [[White Yoshi]] and [[Birdo (Black)]] who where added as new drivers in this tour. This tour also introduced [[Snap a Photo]] as a new bonus challenge. 

The tour added R/T variants of [[Donut Plains 2 (track)|SNES Donut Plains 2]] and [[Royal Raceway|N64 Royal Raceway]].
