The '''Buckies''' are a group of three [[Noki]]s found in [[Pinna Park]] in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''. They are named '''Ducky''', '''Lucky''', and '''Plucky''', who have the appearance of a regular red, green, and blue Noki, respectively.

Though the Buckies do not return in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'', red, blue, and green are all a color option for the playable Noki.
