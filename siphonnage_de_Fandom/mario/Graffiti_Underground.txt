'''Graffiti Underground''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: The Origami King]]''. It is accessed from [[Toad Town (The Origami King)|Toad Town]], and it can be entered using a ladder.

To the right of the ladder is a red switch that can be hammered to turn the lights on. To the left of the ladder is the first [[Not-Bottomless Hole]]. At the bottom of the stairs is a yellow [[Toad (species)|Toad]], who introduces himself as the curator of [[Musée Champignon]] in Toad Town.

Following the tunnel, through the archway, [[Mario]] comes into another dark room, where he must hit the switch. There are twelve [[Origami Swooper]]s. To the right of the origami Swoopers is the 2nd Not-bottomless Hole. At the bottom of the next slight of stairs is a heart that restores 20 HP.

In the next area are two [[Origami Scaredy Rat]]s. To the left is the 1st question block, that contains a mushroom. As Mario continues to the right, he becomes cornered by 3 Origami Scaredy Rats, and 3 Origami Swoopers, whom he must fight.

In the next area, there is a pipe, that is only accessible through a lake of water. There is also a ladder that leads down to a dock over the water. Once you go over to the edge, and that start to make your way back, 4 Origami Scaredy Rats come out. Upon defeat, they drop a [[Magic Circle]]. Mario can use the Magic Circle to open a drainage pipe. Mario can now access the pipe to Peach's Castle, and collect the 3 coins, 2 10-coins, and the one 100-coin. There is also a chest which contains the No.16 Collectible Treasure Manhole Cover. This is the only collectible treasure in Graffiti Underground.  Above the pipe is a sign that says, "Graffiti Underground <>Gate to Peach's Castle<>."To the right of the pipe is a hidden tunnel that leads to another section of Graffiti Underground. There is the 3rd Not-bottomless Hole on the barricade between the once filled pool, and the sewer. On the barricade is a shadowed part of the ground that has the 2nd and last Question Block. The Question Block contains a bag of confetti. Down a flight of stairs is the 4th Not-bottomless Hole. 

Through a door is a room with two Origami Scaredy Rats. At the end of the room is the 5th and last Not-bottomless Hole, and the 2nd and last Toad, is in a crate.

More graffiti appears in the sewers as Mario progresses further into the game.
