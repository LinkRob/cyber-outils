'''Spinthra''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. Spinthras are large, spider-like enemies that can be found in [[Land's End]] and [[Bean Valley]]. They are a stronger variant of [[Arachne]]s.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
===Battle===
Spinthras mainly attack by biting a single member of [[Mario]]'s party. However, their main technique is to use the move [[Poison (move)|Poison]] in order to [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] a party member. They can also bite someone turning them into a [[scarecrow]].

====Strategy====
Spinthras are weak to ice meaning [[Mallow]]'s [[Snowy]] is effective against them. Using an [[Ice Bomb]] is also effective.
