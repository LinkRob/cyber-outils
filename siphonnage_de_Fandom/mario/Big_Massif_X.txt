=== <big>Big Massif X</big> ===


Big Massif X is a much stronger version of Big Massif. This boss is fought in the fourth level in the Battle Ring. The turn limit for him is 12 turns. You need to pay 250 coins to get in. You get 1,500 coins if you win.




Big Massif X has 1,901 HP, 291 Power, 226 Defense, and 104 Speed.
