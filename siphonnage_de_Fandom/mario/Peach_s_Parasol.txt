'''Peach's Parasol''' is a pink umbrella claimed to belong to [[Princess Peach]]. Princess Peach uses the parasol to glide and protect herself from hazards. Peach's Parasol debuts in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', in which it is used as a weapon. In its debut appearance, Peach's parasol is light blue, but has since been colored pink, matching her dress color.

==In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars'' ==
[[File:Parasol SMRPG sprite.png|frame|left]]
The Parasol is a [[List of weapons in Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars|weapon]] only usable by. The Parasol can be purchased from the shop in [[Seaside Town]] for 84 [[coin]]s after defeating [[Yaridovich]]. Equipping the Parasol increases Peach's [[Attack]] rating by 50.

== In {{series|Super Mario}}==
=== ''Super Mario Sunshine'' ===
[[File:Peach SMS.png|thumb|150px|left|''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' artwork of Peach with her parasol.]]
Peach uses her parasol in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' to protect herself from the heat. While she was in [[Corona Mountain]] with [[Bowser]] and [[Bowser Jr.]] (who was enjoying their vacation), she was using it while sitting on the rubber duck. After Mario defeats Bowser and Bowser Jr., all of them were falling, Princess Peach used the parasol for a soft landing; making it her first time to use it to glide.

=== ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' ===
After rescuing Peach in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', Peach will thank Mario. If the player tries to throw [[Star Bit]]s at Peach or jump on her, she guards herself using her parasol.

== In {{series|Mario Party}}==
=== ''Mario Party 3'' ===
''[[Mario Party 3]]'' is the only ''Mario Party'' game in which Peach uses her parasol. Here, Peach uses a parasol that resembles her own in the minigame [[Parasol Plummet]].

== In ''[[Super Princess Peach]]'' ==
{{Main|Perry}}
[[File:Perry.png|thumb|100px|Perry, an extraordinary talking parasol.]]
Princess Peach does not use her own parasol. Peach refuses to stay in the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] while [[Toadsworth]] begs her to not leave on her own, but gives her an extraordinary, talking parasol named '''Perry'''. Princess Peach uses Perry to transform into different forms. Peach uses him to glide over lengthy distances and guard herself. Princess Peach can pick up enemies and Perry can absorb them to fill up her [[Vibe Gauge]]. Peach can stun or defeat enemies with Perry. At the end of the game, Princess Peach hits Bowser sending flying him to the air. Perry unlocks the cage to save Mario and leaves with Peach, Mario, Luigi and other [[Toad (species)|Toads]].

== In ''Yoshi's Island DS'' ==
With Yoshi, [[Baby Peach]] assists by using her parasol to glide over lengthy distances.

== In ''Super Paper Mario'' ==
In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]'', Peach uses the parasol and becomes a playable character in [[Castle Bleck]], despite she has no use for it yet. Later, Peach joins Mario's party. The player must hold {{Button|Wii|2}} for Peach to use the parasol. Peach can use her parasol to glide over lengthy distances or fall gently. The player can also press {{Button|Wii|Paddown}} to shield herself. When enemies, bosses or objects comes in contact with Peach's parasol, she will not need inflict damage but both of them will bounce from each other. According to [[Tippi]]'s tattle, it is ideal that Peach shields herself with the parasol from the [[Rubee]]s used by [[Mimi]].

== ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)]]''==
{{Main|Peach's Parasol (Illumination)}}
Although Peach's parasol doesn't appear in the first film, it was seen in merchandise. The parasol eventually returns in the sequel.

== Crossover appearances ==
===[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]===
Since Princess Peach was introduced as a playable character in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', Peach's parasol has been a part of her movement. ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' is the first game in which Peach uses her parasol to glide slowly down onto the ground.

===''Animal Crossing'' series===
Peach's parasol appears as an [[w:c:animalcrossing:Umbrella|umbrella]] in the ''[[Animal Crossing]]'' series.
