'''Peppy Hare''' is a character from the [[Star Fox (series)|''Star Fox'' series]] who is a former member of the [[Star Fox (team)|Star Fox team]]. 

== History ==
=== Super Smash Bros. Melee ===
He appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' in the [[Corneria]] arena, and the [[Venom]] arena. He (along with [[Slippy Toad]], [[Falco Lombardi]], and [[Fox]]) will occasionally shoot energy bursts with his [[Arwing]] at the [[player]]s on the arena. He also has a trophy in the game.

=== ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' ===
Peppy also appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' as part of Fox's, Falco's, and [[Wolf O'Donnell|Wolf's]] [[Smash Taunt|smash taunt]] on the [[Lylat Cruise]] stage.

== Other media ==
=== Super Mario: Verloren in der Zeit ===
He made another appearance in the ''[[Club Nintendo (German magazine)|Club Nintendo]]'' comic "[[Super Mario: Verloren in der Zeit]]", here he is shown watching the Olympics with Fox McCloud and Falco Lombardi.

Peppy is one of the costumes in ''[[Super Mario Maker]]''. He is unlocked randomly by clearing the 100 Mario Challenge on Normal setting or higher.
