'''Glad Bloopers''' are enemies in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Glad Bloopers are a variant of [[Blooper]] imbued with the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe. Glad Bloopers look similar to Bloopers except they are a yellowish color and have purple eyewear.

===Behavior===
Because they are joyful, Glad Bloopers are less aggressive than regular Bloopers. Instead of bobbing at Peach like regular Bloopers do, Glad Bloopers swim in figure-eights.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Glad Bloopers are found in [[Wavy Beach]], [[Gleam Glacier]], and even [[Bowser's Villa]]. They first appear in the [[level]] [[Ladida Plains 1-9]]. [[Princess Peach|Peach]] can defeat a Glad Blooper by blowing air bubbles at them.
