'''Hoo's Wood 2-3''' is the third level of [[Hoo's Wood]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.
== Toads ==
*'''Toad 1''': Use the Joy vibe and jump on the grass. Jump on many green platforms and jump up to go in the pipe. Squat under and avoid the Ravens to reach the top.
*'''Toad 2''': Peach should cry to make the water wheel spinning. Get in the pipe to your left, then jump in the pipe on ground level. Get in the hidden pipe. After finding the ! Box, smash the green polka dot blocks.
*'''Toad 3''': Use the Joy vibe to spin the windmill, float up and go to the left and hit the ! Box.
== Puzzle Piece ==
*Keep climbing the hill and when on a flat surface, slide down to your right.
