'''5-Banana Coins''' are special coins which appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==In ''Donkey Kong 64''==
5-Banana Coins are very rare items that can only be found under [[DK Dirt Pile]]s, which require the [[Super Slam]] to remove. 5-Banana Coins are found throughout the courses, usually one per course. [[Angry Aztec]] and [[Fungi Forest]] are the only main courses that to have two 5-Banana Coins. Picking up a 5-Banana Coin gives five regular [[Banana Coin]]s to the [[Kong]] who picked it up.
