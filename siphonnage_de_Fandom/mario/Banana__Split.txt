'''Banana, Split''' is a Toad's Rec Room [[minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''. The minigame requires the player to have two [[Nintendo Switch]] systems.

== Opening ==
Toad gives minigame rules to the player.
== Gameplay ==
The player must play five rounds in which they match up the two systems to correctly align two halves of a banana as fast as possible. If the player makes an incorrect guess, the player is not penalized although they waste time. The player must join two systems to join the banana pieces. When the player slide their finger across both systems to confirm. The game ends after the player completes five rounds.

== Ending ==
The player performs their victory pose along the pile of [[banana]]s.

== In-game description ==
''"How fast can you clear five challenges?"''

== Etymology ==
[[File:Traditional banana boat.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Origin of the minigame's name.]]
'''Banana, Split''' is a play of ''banana split''. A banana split is an ice-cream based dessert served in a long dish called a boat.
