'''Love Tester''' is a [[souvenir]] in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'', located in the Doodads folder.

==Description==
The souvenir is used to test the compatibility of a couple or friendship. It is a reference to the toy of the [[w:c:nintendo:Love Tester|same name]], created in 1969 before Nintendo entered the video game market.

==Controls==
*{{button|gba|Tilt}} - Move the arrow
*{{button|gba|A}} - Turn machine on
*{{button|gba|L}} - Press button
*{{button|gba|R}} - Press button
