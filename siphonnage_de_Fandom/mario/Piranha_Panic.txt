'''Piranha Panic''' is a [[minigame]] in the {{series|Mario Baseball}}. It first appears in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and returns in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
The minigame takes place at [[Yoshi Park]]. The four participants in the game sit in four pipes with colored [[Yoshi Egg]]s behind them and three differently-colored [[Piranha Plant]]s in front, and their goal is to earn the most [[point]]s by hitting the Piranha Plants with their eggs.

==Gameplay==
===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
[[File:Piranha Panic Superstar Baseball.png|thumb|In ''Mario Superstar Baseball'']]
The goal is to throw the red, yellow, green and blue eggs into the Piranha Plant of the same color to earn points. If the wrong egg is thrown into a wrong colored Piranha Plant (e.g. Red Egg into Blue Piranha Plant), the Piranha Plant will proceed to spit a [[Fireball]] at the player who gave them the wrong egg, forcing them to duck. Occasionally, the players may find a [[Bomb]] amongst the eggs. If thrown at a Piranha Plant, the player will get three points. If thrown at a rival player (forcing them to duck for cover, as with fireballs), they will lose some points.

Sometimes, one of the Piranha Plants will duck down into its [[Warp Pipe]], with a different Piranha Plant appearing to take its place. Periodically, there is a giant Purple Piranha Plant that replaces the other three for a few seconds; it can eat all types of eggs, but spits fireballs at all players.

The player with the most points at the end of the 60-second time limit (90 in Special Mode) wins the game.

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
There are some gameplay differences. There are no longer any Bombs to throw. The big Purple Piranha Plant also does not return. There are also [[Red Shell]]s that appear occasionally, and they can KO a Piranha Plant in one hit.

There are also some differences with the rules. Players must prevent their Piranha Plants from reaching them by throwing balls at the plant. Each time the player hits Piranha Plant, their gauge fills, and once the gauge is at its maximum, the player can throw a bomb.

As a minor detail, the Piranha Plants are shown to sit in [[kart]]s that are the [[Piranha Pipes]] from ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

==In {{series|Mario Baseball}}==
===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
Aside from being one of the four player minigames, Piranha Panic has a role in [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]], where the player can challenge three [[Shy Guy]]s to a game of Piranha Panic.

==Nomenclature==
*'''Japanese:''' パックンパニック (''Pakkun Panikku''), meaning "Piranha Panic"
*'''Spanish:''' Pánico con Pirañas, translating to "Panic with Piranhas"
