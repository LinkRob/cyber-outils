'''Morph Bubbles''' are floating [[bubble]]s featured in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]], having first appeared in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and then returning in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. Touching a Morph Bubble allows a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to transform into the vehicle displayed within the Morph Bubble. During this, [[Baby Mario]] (or whichever other baby a Yoshi is carrying in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'') remains inside of a Bubble until Yoshi returns to his usual form, either from being forced back when the timer runs out (if the transformation is temporary) or by touching a [[Yoshi Block]].

In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]'', Morph Bubbles are replaced by [[Whirly Gate]]s.
