The '''Diamond Stone''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== Description ==
The Diamond Stone can be found in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]] in a room guarded by two [[Stone Chomp]]s and is needed to access the room where [[Tutankoopa]] resides in. The Diamond Stone must be used with the [[Lunar Stone]] and the [[Pyramid Stone]] and be placed in their correct pedestals in order to open the staircase leading to Tutankoopa's tomb.
