'''Waluigi Racer''' is an unlockable [[kart]] in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''. It is the signature kart of [[Waluigi]]. Waluigi Racer is indigo and has an upside-down L. The player can unlock it by finishing [[Engine Class|100cc]] [[Flower Cup]] in first place.

This kart has the same stats as [[Mario]]'s [[Red Fire]] and the engine sounds the same as Waluigi's default partner's, [[Wario]]. Waluigi Racer is better than the Red Fire because it recovers faster than it when hit by a [[Green Shell]] or a [[Red Shell]]. It is also designed after [[Waluigi]], with his signature colors and a design based on his nose and mustache.
