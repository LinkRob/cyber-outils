'''Mario Coin''' is a type of [[coin]] in ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP DX]]''.

==Description==
Mario Coins can buy and obtain special private [[kart]]s and items, although only from a player who has a [[Mario Card]].
