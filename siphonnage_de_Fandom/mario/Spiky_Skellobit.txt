A '''Spiky Skellobit''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''Super Paper Mario''==
Spiky Skellobits are powerful versions of [[Skellobit]]s that are found in the later levels of the game mainly in [[The Overthere]] and [[Castle Bleck]]. Like with Skellobits, they attack with a charging spear. Since they have spikes on their heads, Spiky Skellobits cannot be jumped on unless the player is riding on [[Carrie]].
