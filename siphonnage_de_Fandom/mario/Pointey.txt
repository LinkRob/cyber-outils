'''Pointeys ''' (also called '''Spring Plants''') are [[Pokey]]-like plants that appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'', they are seen residing in the ground but as [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] approaches, they stick their heads out and linger around bouncing [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] back if he hits them, the height of a Pointey can vary and this determines whether Yoshi can jump over them or has to defeat them to progress, despite this, Pointeys are completely harmless and can't cause any actual damage. Pointeys are commonly found in earlier levels of the game (such as those in [[World 1 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 1]] and [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]]) and appear scarcely in the latter levels. Pointeys cannot be swallowed but are not very durable at all and can easily be defeated with stomps, [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] tosses, or [[Baby DK]]'s Dash Attack, tossing an egg at a Pointey will make it appear in the [[Museum]].

==Trivia==
*A [[Hint Block]] seems to suggest that Pointeys emerge from the ground in spring.
*The Pointeys movements are similar to that of the [[Pokey]]s.
