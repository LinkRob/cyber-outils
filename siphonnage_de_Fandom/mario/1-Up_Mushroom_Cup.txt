The '''1-Up Mushroom Cup''' is a type of tournament in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''. It is the first cup for the Star Open tournament series, and it can be unlocked by completing the [[Special Cup|Champions Cup]] in the World Open series. For the 1-Up Mushroom Cup, the character compete on the [[Peach's Palace]] court.

The 1-Up Mushroom Cup appears to have been inspired by the [[Mushroom Cup]], since both have a type of [[Mushroom]] as a symbol and are the first cup of their respective tournament series.
