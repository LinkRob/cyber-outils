'''Smash Compactor''' is a Free-For-All mini-game in ''[[Mario Party 9]]''.

==Opening==
The four characters are on a platform, and each of them fall into a hole in the ground as the compactor slams down. Following this, the characters emerge back out.

==Gameplay==
With each round, the objective is to reach a glowing manhole lid in the ground before the opponents do. Characters can perform a few different actions, including [[jump]]ing, [[punch]]ing, and [[kick]]ing, to knock their opponents out of the way. Once the time to enter a safe zone is up, the lids open and the characters can enter their respective hole. Whoever has has not found a safe zone is squished by the compactor and eliminated from the minigame. Whenever this occurs, the eliminated characters float upward in a flattened state, eventually going offscreen, then they land onto a [[castle]]-like structure in the background, no longer in a flattened state. Every round has one fewer glowing hole than characters present, meaning at least one character is eliminated each round. Whoever is the last character remaining is the winner.

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Jump in an open hole as fast as you can. Anyone left after the doors close will get smashed!''
* '''Controls''': ''Hold the Wii Remote sideways. Press {{button|wii|Pad}} to move, {{button|wii|1}} to punch, {{button|wii|2}} to jump, and {{button|wii|1}} while jumping to kick.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame name is a pun on "trash compactor."
