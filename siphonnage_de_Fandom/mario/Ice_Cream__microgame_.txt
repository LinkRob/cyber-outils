'''Ice Cream''' is a downloadable [[microgame]] in ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' and ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. Ice Cream is the first of six microgames created by Nintendo of America's Treehouse, and it was exclusively released in North America on June 21, 2010.

==Layout==
There is a hand holding an ice cream cone inside of a house with yellow walls and a sand-colored ceiling. The walls appear to have black markings on them. A partially-visible framed photo is on the left wall. There is a window in the center, with a blue drape on each side, and outside the window is a round hill with light-green grass and a clear blue sky. There is a black spider on the ceiling.

==Gameplay==
The player has to launch the ice cream cone at the spider. If done successfully, the player wins, but if they miss the spider, the player loses at the microgame.
