'''★World 8-Bowser's Castle''' is the final [[level]] of ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. It is a harder variation of {{world-link|8|bowserp|World 8-Bowser's Castle (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)}}.

Like the normal of the level, it is long and difficult. The enemies are always consistent, are not always the exact same as before, and are much tougher to deal with. Upon completing the level for the first time, the player will unlock Super [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Dry Bowser]], and [[Bowser]], King of the Koopas as helpers and the credits will play. (The credits will play upon subsequent clears of the level as well.) 

== Encounters ==
All battles here will always have the same enemies.

*Encounter 1: 1 [[Boomerang Bro]], 1 [[Fire Bro]], 1 [[Ice Bro]] (In that order.)
*Encounter 2: 3 [[Bob-omb]]s
*Encounter 3: 1 Big Bone Piranha Plant
*Encounter 4: 1  [[Yellow Magikoopa|Yellow]], [[Green Magikoopa|Green]], [[Red Magikoopa|Red]], [[Blue Magikoopa|Blue]] and [[Purple Magikoopa|Purple]] Magikoopa (In that order.)
*Encounter 5: 1 [[King Bill]]
*Encounter 6: 1 [[Cheep Chomp]]
*Encounter 7: 1 [[Chain Chomp]] & [[Flame Chomp|Flame Chomps]]
*Encounter 8: 1 [[Thwomp]] and [[Thwimp|Thwimps]]
*Encounter 9 (Boss): 1 [[Dry Bowser]]
*Encounter 9 (If Dry Bowser has already been defeated): 1 [[Boom Boom]]

== Items ==
*[[Orb (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|Orbs]]: Fire, Water, Wood, Light, Dark, Heart
*? Block Items: None

== Trivia ==
*This is one of the very few levels in the Special Worlds that actually changes which enemies appear in the stage.
*After defeating Dry Bowser, Boom Boom here may randomly drop Spiky Shells or Grand Spiky Shells with 10 or more combos.
