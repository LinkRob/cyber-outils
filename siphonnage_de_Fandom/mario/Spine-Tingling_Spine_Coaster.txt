'''Spine-Tingling Spine Coaster''' is the first level in Superstar Road in New Super Mario Bros U as well as the first secret level. It is unlocked when the player collects every [[Star Coin]] in [[Acorn Plains]]. 

==Layout==
This level contains a [[Bonecoaster]], similar to [[Thrilling Spine Coaster]] from [[Rock-Candy Mines]], and has a background from the grassland stages in [[Acorn Plains]]. The level begins on a section of the ground, where a [[? Block]] containing a ? Block is found prior to the Bonecoaster. The Bonecoaster moves along the track, and [[Mega Fuzzy|Mega Fuzzies]] on circular tracks are found around the path. As the Bonecoaster speeds up, Mega Fuzzies are found on the same track, along with some that jump from tracks and more that are on circular tracks. A ? Block containing a power-up is found, followed by another ? Block containing a [[Starman]] at the top of a hill. More Mega Fuzzies follow then follow, and a [[Red Ring]] is reached after some jumps and hills. Multiple Mega Fuzzies follow, with two large groups of them appearing in the section leading to the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin is located between two Mega Fuzzies.
*'''Star Coin 2''': The second Star Coin is located below the Bonecoaster tracks. It moves to a higher location after a short period of time.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The final Star Coin is found near the end of the level when the Bonecoaster starts to alternate between two tracks. The player needs to be at the back of the coaster in order to get it.
