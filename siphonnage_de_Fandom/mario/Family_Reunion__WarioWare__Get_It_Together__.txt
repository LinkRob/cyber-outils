'''Family Reunion''' is one of the [[microgame]]s featured in [[Kat and Ana]]'s [[Nature]] level in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to locate the ducklings and return them to the mother duck. Whenever the character makes contact with a duckling, it automatically follows them until they are returned to the mother.

In Level 1, there is only one duckling that has to be reunited with its mother. In Level 2, there are two needing to be reunited. Level 3 also features two duckling but the maze is more complex.

In two-player mode, a third duckling is present in both Levels 2 and 3. A player's character can take a duckling from their opponent by making contact with it. If the microgame is won, whoever returns more ducklings to the mother duck receives an effort point.

==Mission==
*'''Mama's Got Questions:''' As 5-Volt, while playing Family Reunion in the Play-o-pedia, the player has to lead a duckling astray 15 times before reaching a [[Game Over]]. This mission is only doable in single-player mode.
