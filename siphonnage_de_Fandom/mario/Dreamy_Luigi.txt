}}|status=Alive|affiliation=[[Mario Bros. (group)|Mario Bros.]]}}'''Dreamy Luigi''' is a character that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. He is the dream counterpart of [[Luigi]] and assists Mario in the [[Dream World|dream world]].
==Appearance==
Dreamy Luigi is an exact replica of Luigi except that Dreamy [[Broque Monsieur]] states Dreamy Luigi as "more handsome" and "slightly taller" than the actual Luigi. However, this could imply on how Luigi thinks of himself as.

==Battle Style==
Unlike the regular Luigi, Dreamy Luigi doesn't physically fight alongside Mario during enemy or boss fights but he does after Mario lands a hit on enemies. He could also do several [[Bros. Attack]]s, named [[Luiginary Attack]]s, with Mario. Dreamy Luigi can also split into numerous of clones of himself which are called [[Luiginoid]]s.
