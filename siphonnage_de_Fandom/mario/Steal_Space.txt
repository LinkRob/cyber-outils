'''Steal Spaces''' are a type of [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party 10]]''.

==Description==
Steal Spaces resemble [[Event Space]]s in that they are dark green spaces with an exclamation mark, except a Steal Space's one is orange.

Steal Spaces function somewhat like [[Chance Time Space]] from earlier {{series|Mario Party}} installments. Whoever lands onto a Steal Space can steal 5, 10, or half of an opponent's [[Mini Star]]s; both the Mini Stars and the targeted opponent are determined by a spinner. In a two-player battle, whoever lands on the space spins the wheel solely to determine the number of Mini Stars to steal from their opponent.

==In ''Mario Party 10''==
One Steal Space can be found in [[Mushroom Park]], while two Steal Spaces are in [[Haunted Trail]], [[Whimsical Waters]], [[Airship Central]], and [[Chaos Castle]].
