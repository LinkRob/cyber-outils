'''Trick Tour''' was the forthy-forth tour of [[Mario Kart Tour]], that began on May 19, 2021 and ended on June 1, 2021. Just like the  [[Trick Tour (2020)]] and [[Mario vs. Luigi Tour (2020)]] this tour features mainly R/T variants tracks.

This Tour was the second time in a row to not add new [[Track|Tracks]], but introduced R/T variants of [[Choco Island 1|RMX Choco Island 1]] and [[Airship Fortress|DS Airship Fortress]]. This was also the first time not to introduce new [[Glider|Gliders]]. The tour introduced [[Builder Mario|Builder Toadette]] as a playable character from [[Super Mario Maker 2]] who is featured in the week 1 pipe along with [[Builder Mario|Builder  Toad]].

The menu's background featured the hills from RMX Choco Island 1R/T as the landmark for this tour.
