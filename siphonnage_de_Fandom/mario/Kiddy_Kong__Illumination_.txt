'''Kiddy Kong''' makes a cameo appearance in The Super Mario Bros. Movie. He accompanies the [[Kong]] army enlisted by [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]], [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]] and [[Toad (Illumination)|Toad]] to join the raid on [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]]. The Kong army races along [[Rainbow Road (Illumination)|Rainbow Road]] on their way to [[Bowser's Castle (Illumination)|Bowser's Castle]], but because of Bowser's prior knowledge of the attempted attack, they are ambushed by Bowser's army.
