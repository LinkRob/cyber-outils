'''Dragon Palace''' is a [[Battle Stage]] introduced in ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. It is designed similarly to the track [[Dragon Driftway]]. There is a big palace you can drive through, designed after traditional Old Asian temples. You can break through the windows of the palace and drive to the courtyard, which is made of cobblestone and has a large statue of Kung-Fu Lakitu on it, in a similar pose to that of the poster on the wall in ''[[Ribbon Road]].'' This pose is also a play on the cover for Dreamworks' Kung Fu Panda. There are also several statues of Dragons outside.
