'''Hanger Management''' is a 2-vs-2 and a Duel minigame which appears in ''[[Mario Party DS]]''.

==Layout==
The minigame appears to take place in the backyard of various homes.

== Opening ==
The characters shift on their hangers to avoid an obstacle.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Hanger Management MPDS duel.png|thumb|In Duel mode]]
Each team (or character in Duel mode) hangs onto their own hanger, which they steer along a wire to the end while avoiding various objects, marked by a red exclamation mark on a light yellow symbol. If a team or character bumps into an object, such as a shovel or a watering can, their movement briefly stops. A character can make their hanger go faster by balancing the hanger's weight. To avoid an object, the character or team has to lean on the side of the hanger opposite the obstacle, which is easier to perform in Duel mode.

Whoever reaches the finish line first wins. If both sides reach the finish line at the exact same time or if no one reaches the end within five minutes, the minigame ends in a tie. If both sides break a record, even when tied, the new record is counted, but "Tie" is shown right after the new record fanfare. In [[Score Scuffle]], Hanger Management ends once every character reaches the end.

==Ending==
Winners are shown on a table and do their victory pose. Losers are not shown.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The minigame title is a pun on the phrase "anger management."

==Trivia==
*While Hanger Management is not a 4-player minigame, Score Shuffle has an exclusive 4-player variation only playable there, being the only 2-vs-2 minigame that has an exclusive 4-player variation.
