A '''Gawbus''' is an enemy that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]].''

==History==
A Gawbus is a member of the [[Jawbus]] family that can be found in [[Castle Bleck]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Like with the Jawbus, a Gawbus mainly attacks by biting toward one of the heroes. A Gawbus' weak spot is the tail, like the others, and the best way to take them out is by using [[Mario]]'s [[Flip]] to get behind them and attacking the tail until it is defeated.
