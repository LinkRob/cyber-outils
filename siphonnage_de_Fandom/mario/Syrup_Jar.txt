The {{Nihongo|'''Syrup Jar'''|シロップ|Shiroppu|Syrup}} is an item debuting in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It returns in the {{series|Paper Mario}}, and {{series|Mario & Luigi}}.

== Description ==
In the ''Mario & Luigi'' series, the '''Syrup Jar''' strongly resembles a jar of [[Wikipedia:Syrup|syrup]]. Syrup Jars are round, with their syrup being gold in colour. [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] can obtain the Syrup Jar from [[? Block]]s or from enemies. Syrup Jars recovers [[BP]]<ref group="note">[[Bros. Point]]</ref> for any brother.

=== Variants ===
More powerful variants of the Syrup Jar can be found. These are Supersyrup Jars, Ultrasyrup Jars, and Max Syrup Jars, and they are rarer than their "standard" counterparts, with the Supersyrup Jar being more common than the Ultrasyrup Jar and the Max Syrup Jar being the rarest.

The standard Syrup Jar recovers 20 [[Bros. Point|BP]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', 10 [[Special Point|SP]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'', and 20 BP in both ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. For the [[Supersyrup Jar]], these values are 40, 20, and 40, respectively. The [[Ultrasyrup Jar]] restores 100 in ''Superstar Saga'', 30 in ''Bowser's Inside Story'', and 60 in ''Dream Team'' and ''Paper Jam'', respectively. Max Syrup Jars recover all Special Points in ''Superstar Saga'', 40 SP in ''Bowser's Inside Story'', 80 in ''Dream Team'' and ''Paper Jam''.

== In ''Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars''==
Syrups are items that can restore [[Flower Point|FP]]. Three types are found in the game: [[Honey Syrup]], which heals 10 FP, [[Maple Syrup]], which heals 40 FP, and [[Royal Syrup]], which maxes out FP.

== In ''[[Paper Mario]]''==
Syrups appear again in ''[[Paper Mario]]'', still, as items that recover FP. Honey Syrup and Maple Syrup return, however, Royal Syrup does not. [[Jammin' Jelly]] serves as the replacement for it.

== In {{series|Mario & Luigi}} ==
=== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''/''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions|Bowser's Minions]]''===
The '''Syrup''' appeared in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' along with the [[Ultra Syrup]] and [[Max Syrup]]. It recovers 20 BP to the user. Additionally, enemies and [[Queen Bean]] drop it too.<ref group="note">There are different possibilities of the enemy dropping the Syrup upon their defeat.
*Queen Bean (deflated left arm) - 100%
*[[Rex]] (unflattened) - 32.26%
*[[Spiky Snifit]] - 25.81%
*[[Chuck Guy]] ([[Chucklehuck Woods]]) - 22.58%
*[[Yo Bro]] - 9.68%</ref>

In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'', Syrup Jars recover 15 BP.
