'''Megahammer's Daredevil Run''' is a stage that appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It can be accessed by gaining the [[Comet Medal]] in its [[Bowser Jr.'s Mighty Megahammer|base mission]] plus another 26 medals in other galaxies's first missions (27 total). This causes a [[Prankster Comet|Daredevil Comet]] to visit [[Bowser Jr.'s Fearsome Fleet]]. 

==Layout==
[[Mario]] has to beat [[Bowser Jr.]]'s mighty [[Megahammer]] again, but without taking damage. The only planet visited is the Megahammer Planet. [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] is required for this mission, and the Megahammer retains his attack pattern from before.

==Trivia==
*The mission's name is erroneously written "Megahammer's Daredevil '''Bash'''" in the game.
