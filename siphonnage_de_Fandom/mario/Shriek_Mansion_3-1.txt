'''Shriek Mansion 3-1''' is the first level of [[Shriek Mansion]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Toads ==
*'''Toad 1''': Use the [[Joy (vibe)|Joy]] vibe to rise up and climb up the stairs to your right.
*'''Toad 2''': Jump in the purple pipe, climb up the ladders to the top, go to the right to reveal the hidden area and jump on the platform.
*'''Toad 3''': Fly up to the left with the Joy vibe to get in the area, then it'll appear to you a ! Box in front of you.
== Items ==
*'''Music Score''': Climb on the first ladder and jump to the left to reveal the hidden room.
