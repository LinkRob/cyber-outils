'''Super Mario's Relaxing Life''' is a series of animated stickers made by [[Nintendo]] and LINE merger. They were released on October 19th, 2017. The characters featured are: [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Princess Peach|Peach]], [[Princess Daisy|Daisy]], [[Toad (character)|Toad]], [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], [[Lakitu]], [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]], [[Bowser]], [[Bowser Jr.]] and [[Lemmy Koopa/Gallery|Lemmy Koopa]].
