The '''Old Photo''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

== History ==
The Old Photo can only be obtained after accepting one of [[Koopa Koot]]'s many favors. Koopa Koot wanted to relieve his younger days and asks [[Mario]] to retrieve the photo of him from a [[Boo]] in [[Boo's Mansion]]. If Mario heads to Boo's Mansion and talks to the first Boo there, he will give the Old Photo to Mario. However, before leaving, the Boo tells Mario that Koopa Koot is a selfish, old [[Koopa Troopa|Koopa]] who often tricks people to do his many favors and paying them a small amount of coins in return. Mario then returns to Koopa Koot to return the Old Photo to him and Koopa Koot awards Mario with a [[coin]].

== Trivia ==
*Oddly, even though the [[Boo]] says that Koopa Koot is selfish and only rewards people will small amounts of coins, Koopa Koot tends to give away good items such as the credits and even [[Star Piece (Paper Mario series)|Star Piece]]s.
