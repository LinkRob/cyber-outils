'''Krazy Kremland''' is the fourth world in ''[[Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]'' and the third in ''[[Donkey Kong Land 2]]''. This place is located on [[Crocodile Isle]]. This place appears to be an amusement park for [[Kremling]]s including [[Klank]]s themselves bulit by [[Kaptain K. Rool]].

In the second half of the amusement park in the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]], there are giant [[Kannon]] and [[Kruncha]] statues located in the park. There also appear to be marshes and bramble areas, showing that the amusement park was built on a place with marshes and brambly vines. There are also unsafe rollercoaster rides with many pitfalls, making visitors have to jump over the pitfalls to avoid losing a life. There are also [[Zinger]] hives located around here.
