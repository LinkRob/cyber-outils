'''Bombats''' are enemies in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bombats are purple bat-like creatures resembling a bomb. They have a pair of wings, eyes, a mouth, and a tail resembling the fuse of a bomb.

===Behavior===
The default behavior of a Bombat is to fly around without the intention of damaging [[Wario]]. If Wario damages a Bombat, it flashes red and moves toward Wario before exploding, damaging Wario if he is within the blast radius.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
Bombats are only encountered in [[Poobah the Pharaoh's Pyramid]], where twenty of them can be found.
