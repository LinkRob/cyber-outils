{{DISPLAYTITLE:List of minigames in ''Mario Party''}}
{{Listnotice|minigames from ''[[Mario Party]]''|For the main subject, see [[Minigame]]}}
There are 50 ''[[Mario Party]]'' [[Minigame|mini-games]] (56 if the different courses of some mini-games are included in this total), including 24 4-Player mini-games, ten 1-vs-3 mini-games, five 2-vs-2 mini-games, ten 1-Player mini-games, and an unlockable 1P Bumper Ball Maze mini-game. Certain mini-games are different from the others—instead of the basic rewards for mini-games played, the players get the number of Coins they collect.
