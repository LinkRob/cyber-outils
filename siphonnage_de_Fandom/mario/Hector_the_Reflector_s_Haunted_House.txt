'''Hector the Reflector's Haunted House''' is the fourth level of [[World 2 (Yoshi's Island DS)|World 2]] in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. It is the game's second castle and a [[Yellow Yoshi]] takes control in this level. This level takes place in a haunted castle filled with many traps and lava pools. The level is also home to some ghostly enemies such as [[Boo Guy]]s, [[Ghost Piranha]]s, and [[Invisighoul]]s. The boss in this level is [[Hector the Reflector]] a large [[Invisighoul]] who has been given the power of invisibility through [[Kamek]]'s magic.

==Walkthrough==
Starting off at the Castle's entrance, advance to the right and collect the [[Coin]]s along the path, beat the [[Boo Guy]] in your way or use him to obtain the [[Smiley Flower]] above, from here, continue hopping across the platforms collecting the [[Coin]]s, one of the [[Coin]]s on the final platform before the main floorings is a [[Red Coin]] so be sure to collect this one. Once you land on the flooring, watch out for the [[Donut Lift]]s as they're dangling above the [[lava]] so don't stand on them for too long, move to the right collect the [[Coin]]s on the platforms above ([[Boo Guy]]s spawn from the [[Pipe]] below so if you're low on [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s, you can slurp them up). A [[Ghost Piranha]] is also hovering above however these can be beaten with either an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] or a [[Boo Guy]]. Continue to the right and hit the [[Winged Cloud]] to form a bridge over the [[lava]], jump onto the [[Donut Lift]]s to grab the [[Coin]]s, each of the three [[Donut Lift]] sets have one [[Red Coin]] each totaling to three, watch out for the [[Ghost Piranha]]s as you cross the bridge and climb the stairs (they can be easily beaten with [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s, [[DK Dash Attack]]s, or by spitting a [[Boo Guy]] at them). After dropping down the stairs, smash the soft stone blockages to get under the stairs where three [[Red Coin]]s, a [[Coin]], and a [[Smiley Flower]] can be collected (just be careful to limit the time spent on the flooring under the stairs as the floor consists of [[Donut Lift]]s) once collected, advance to the right and enter the door.

In the next area, advance back to the right (there is an [[Arrow Wheel]] here but come back for it later) and be sure to hit the [[Winged Cloud]] above for some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], once done, enter the [[Pipe]] to the right. In this next room, you will find several chairs and vases that appear perfectly motionless but as you move along the corridor, several of them start floating in the air and will throw themselves at you (these furnitures are controlled by [[Invisighoul]]s, they can be eaten for [[Yoshi Egg|egg]]s however so they make a good egg supply), the room itself contains three upper niches each containing a [[Winged Cloud]], the first one contains some [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]], the second one contains a [[Smiley Flower]], and the third one contains a [[Key]] necessary for progressing through the level (if you fail to get all the [[Winged Cloud]]s and run out of [[Invisighoul]]s, leave the room and reenter, all the [[Invisighoul]]s will respawn). Once all the items have been collected, exit through the [[Pipe]] you came in from.

Upon entering the large room again, advance to the left and jump on the [[Arrow Wheel]] (the [[Arrow Wheel]]s are controlled by whichever direction the arrow is pointing in so you may need to hop off it to allow it to spin in your desired direction, they also bounce off walls rebounding their direction once they do). Guide the Arrow Wheel along the [[Coin]] path, there are three [[Red Coin]]s in this trail, one your near the top, take the left path and get an additional three [[Red Coin]]s here (totaling to six [[Red Coin]]s in this area) and grab the [[Smiley Flower]] in the niche, (from here, you can also guide it much higher to where the top ledge is, there's a [[Stork Stop]] here, you can switch to [[Baby DK]] and exit through the door to reach the area with the [[Baby Coin]]). After this, guide the [[Arrow Wheel]] to the upper righthand corner where the platform is and hit the invisible [[Winged Cloud]] in the corner (as it contains [[Star (Yoshi's Island)|Stars]]) and use the [[Key]] to unlock the door.

In this next area, advance to the right and tag the [[Middle Ring]], then swallow the [[Boo Guy]] here and toss the [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the [[Arrow Cloud]]s to collect the [[Coin]]s and two [[Red Coin]]s here, once done, the egg should hit the [[Boo Guy]] on the upper platform (be sure the [[Boo Guy]] is not anywhere on the slope or the egg will miss it) and the hit [[Boo Guy]] will roll into the [[Winged Cloud]] netting you a 1-Up, after this is done, advance to the right and on the [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]] (they start moving up and down when you stand on them so be sure not to slip into the [[lava]] amidst the moving [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]]) above the [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]] is a row of seven [[Coin]]s with four of them being [[Red Coin]]s, collect these and jump across the gap with the [[lava]] pit climb the stairs (but watch out for the [[Boo Guy]]s and [[Ghost Piranha]]s as you do), from here, use the next set of [[Flatbed Ferry|Flatbed Ferries]] to reach the upper platform, climb the stairs and avoid or beat the [[Ghost Piranha]]s, once at the top, toss an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at the niche above where some [[Coin]]s and a [[Red Coin]] await (if you're out, just slurp a [[Boo Guy]] that emerges from the nearby [[Pipe]]. Next, tag the [[Stork Stop]] and switch to [[Baby Mario]] (if you haven't already) and drop down to the lower ledge and use [[Baby Mario]]'s egg-throwing abilities to ricochet the egg off the wall so it hits the [[Arrow Cloud]], the egg will follow the track of [[Arrow Cloud]]s before finally hitting the [[Winged Cloud]], a staircase will be formed allowing you to reach the upper floor, beat the low-flying [[Ghost Piranha]] here and use an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] or a [[Boo Guy]] to obtain the [[Smiley Flower]] above, once done, exit through the door to the left.

In the next room, advance to the right and up the platforms. Watch out for the chairs and vases here, they're controlled by [[Invisighoul]]s and they will try to attack you so avoid or slurp them up for [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] because you're going to need them for the boss upahead, follow the path to the right and tag the [[Middle Ring]], pick whichever baby suits you at the [[Stork Stop]], and enter the Red Door.

===BOSS BATTLE: Hector the Reflector===
Upon entering the room, [[Hector the Reflector]] will show up, [[Kamek]] will then arrive and use his magic to make Hector invisible so as to only be seen in the mirror. The fight is divided by two screens, the top screen is where Yoshi's fight with Hector is and the bottom screen is the mirror, Hector is only visible in the mirror so you will need to check the mirror periodically to see where Hector is. Anyway, to beat [[Hector the Reflector]], pelt him with [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]], [[Invisighoul]]s also shown up in the fight armed with either chairs, vases, or tables to toss at you (these are also visible in the mirror), they can easily be swallowed to be turned into [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] if you're running low. Every time you hit Hector, he will shrink in size and also spit out a piece of furniture at one of the corners of the mirror breaking a bit of it off (for the fifth hit, he will hit the mirror's center), It's best to keep all eyes on Hector and come supplied with six [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] so that way, you can get Hector in a lock and conclusively shoot eggs at him each time he recovers. After six hits, Hector will shrink into nothingness and explode and the level is completed.

==Baby Coin Location==
Guide the [[Arrow Wheel]] to the highest flooring of the room, then switch to [[Baby DK]] at the [[Stork Stop]] here. From here, enter the door to the left and climb the chain and use either an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] or a [[DK Dash Attack]] to break open the soft stone blocking your way, once this is cleared, grab the [[Baby Coin|Baby DK Coin]] here and exit either through the [[Flipper]]s or through the door you came out from.

==Trivia==
*This level marks the only instance in the game where the two screens are utilized in a way other than expanding the game's screen (in this case, displaying the room and the room's mirror).
