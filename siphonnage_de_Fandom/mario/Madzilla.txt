'''Madzilla''' is a one-time character in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', in the episode "[[Dadzilla]]." He is an in-universe fictional [[Hollywood]] character and the star of his own movie series parodying ''{{w|Godzilla}}''. When [[Kootie Pie]] and [[Big Mouth]] see Madzilla, they believe him to be their real father, so they use their [[Magic Wand]]s to bring a mechanical version of Madzilla to life.
