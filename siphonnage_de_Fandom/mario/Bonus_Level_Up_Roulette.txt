The '''Bonus Level Up Roulette''' is a feature in the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' series first appearing in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'', whenever [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or anyone else [[Level Up|levels up]], their stats will increase however, they are given the option to choose one stat to provide bonus points to. The roulette is usually handled by a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] and players can move up or down to select their stat, if they want to pick another stat, they can cancel. The Bonus Level Up Roulette can effectively give the character an advantage in that particular stat, the highest possible number on the roulette depends on the game however to prevent a character from becoming too overpowered in a particular stat, constantly honing in on one stat every level up will often yield more 1's making it harder to fully exploit them, the higher numbers come back if the stat is left alone for a couple Level Ups, additionally, stats that players hardly focus on for certain characters tend to have much higher numbers. The Bonus Level Up Roulette has become a staple in the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' series appearing in nearly every game with the exception of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''.

==History==
===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga''===
[[Image:Level Up SS.png|thumb|left|The Bonus Level Up Roulette seen on [[Mario]]'s level up screen.]]
The Bonus Level Up Roulette first appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''. In this game, it appears whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] [[Level Up|levels up]], a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] will show up with a fishing rod holding the roulette and players can move down on the D-Pad to select their chosen stat, when they select their stat, the roulette will begin spinning really fast and when the player picks their chosen stat, the wheel will slow down before landing on a number, the number will determine the amount of points earned to the chosen stat, given this, the player's timing must be perfect to get a desired result, for certain Level Ups, some stats may not go up on their own, in these cases, the Bonus Level Up Roulette must be used to level these up if the player chooses, the highest possible result that can be attained from a Bonus Level Up is 5. If a player focuses on a stat too much for one brother, they will see less high numbers in the roll, this can be circumvented by leaving the stat for a couple Level Ups and focusing on other stats. If a player changes their mind about leveling up a stat, they can cancel by selecting B.

In the Japanese version a D-Pad appears beside the [[Fishin' Lakitu]] showing players that they could move the roulette to a stat of their choice, International releases did not have this feature.


===''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time''===
[[Image:Level Up PIT.png|thumb|right|The Bonus Level Up Roulette seen on [[Baby Mario]]'s level up screen.]]
The Bonus Level Up Roulette returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'' once again serving the same purpose as in the previous game, whenever [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]], or [[Baby Luigi]] [[Level Up]], they can choose one stat to apply bonus points to, just as before, a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] holds a fishing rod with the roulette, and when the player selects a stat, the wheel will start spinning fast however this time, the wheel stops the moment the player presses A to confirm, thereby locking in the number of points earned, just as before the more a player sticks to one stat for one brother, more 1's will appear in rolls for that stat while neglected stats reap higher numbers, in this game, the highest possible number of bonus points earnable for a stat is 6. Additionally, in this game, every stat will go up on their own except [[Stache]]. Stache stats will never go up on their own, players can only use the Bonus Level Up Roulette to level it up. In this game, if a player changes their mind about leveling up a stat, they can cancel with L as buttons A, B, Y, and X are now used to confirm.


===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story''===
[[Image:Level Up BIS.png|thumb|left|The Bonus Level Up Roulette seen on [[Bowser]]'s level up screen.]]
[[Image:Level Up BIS Giant.png|thumb|right|The Bonus Level Up Roulette seen on [[Bowser]]'s level up screen.]]
The Bonus Level Up Roulette returns in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' with similar functions to before but once again serving the same purpose. Whenever [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or [[Bowser]] [[Level Up]], they can choose one stat to give bonus points to, for Mario and Luigi, a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] holds a fishing rod with the roulette and for Bowser, a [[Boo]] with a fishing rod (a literal [[Fishin' Boo]]) holds the roulette, in this game, when a stat is chosen the wheel moves slightly slower than in previous games but precise timing is required to get the desired bonus points, like previous games, if the plumbers or Bowser focus too much on one stat, more 1's appear in rolls while the higher numbers appear for stats that are left alone for multiple levels, the higher numbers can be made to come back on stats by leaving them for a couple levels, in this game, the highest possible number of bonus points that can be earned from a bonus level up roulette is 9 (meaning 7 and 8 are also possible numbers as well) however triggering these high numbers requires leaving a stat alone for several multiple levels. Unlike the previous game, [[Stache]] ([[Horn (stat)|Horn]] for Bowser) will go up on most level-ups however it won't go up on some requiring a Bonus Level Up to earn stats for those levels. Just as before, if the player changes their mind about one stat, they can cancel their choice with L.

After rescuing him from the [[Nerve Cluster]] in [[Bowser's Body]], [[Toadbert]] will appear in [[Toad Square]] and provide advice on Bonus Level Ups.

If [[Giant Bowser]] is to get a Level Up, the menu looks slightly different, several buttons appear on the touch screen and the player can tap their desired choice with the stylus and stop the wheel with said stylus as well. Like a normal level up, players can cancel with L if they change their mind.

===''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team''===
[[Image:Level Up DT.png|thumb|right|The Bonus Level Up Roulette seen on [[Luigi]]'s level up screen.]]
The Bonus Level Up Roulette is seen again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', in this game, it functions identically to how it did in previous games, whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]/[[Dreamy Luigi]] [[Level Up|levels up]], they can choose one stat to apply bonus points to. Unlike previous games however, a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] no longer handles the roulette wheel this time due to [[Lakitu]]s being actual enemies in this game. Instead, the roulette is now a movable roulette wheel resembling a magnifying glass with two directional arrows above and below it. In this game, the slot machine moves about the same speed as in the previous game however timing it to get the desired numbers is significantly easier than in previous games. Just as before, if the plumbers focus too much on one stat, more 1's tend to appear in rolls while ones that are ignored tend to see higher numbers. The high numbers can return to worn out stats by leaving them for a couple levels. In this game, the highest amount of points that can be earned from a bonus level up is 5. Like ''Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time'', [[Stache]] will never go up in points on it's own, the Bonus Level Up Roulette is the only way to apply points to this stat (save for [[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team/Clothing|gear]]). Just as before, if the player changes their mind about one stat, they can cancel their choice with L.

A first in the series, upon [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]]'s first level up, [[Starlow]] gives a tutorial on how to use the Bonus Level Up Roulette.

Unlike the previous game, there is no custom roulette for the [[Giant Luigi]] battles as [[EXP]] is never earned in these battles.


===''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions''===
[[Image:Level Up SS DX.jpg|thumb|left|The Bonus Level Up Roulette seen on [[Mario]]'s level up screen.]]
The Bonus Level Up Roulette appears again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' after being absent from ''[[Mario & Luigi: Paper Jam]]''. In this game, like other games, whenever [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]]/[[Dreamy Luigi]] [[Level Up|levels up]], they can choose a stat to give bonus points to, it is once again handled by a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] holding a fishing rod with the roulette, in this game, upon the first Level Up, a box of text appears telling players they can add bonus points to a stat of their choice. In this game, the roulette moves significantly slower than in previous games making earning the desired point amounts heaps easier. Like previous games, if the plumbers focus too much on one stat, they will see more 1's in their rolls on that stat but this can be circumvented by going for a stat they level up less of which higher numbers tend to appear on. The high numbers can return to worn out stats by leaving them for a few levels. In this game, the highest amount of points that can be earned from a Bonus Level Up Roulette is 5 and just as before, if the player changes their mind about one stat, they can cancel their choice with L.


===''Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey''===
[[Image:Level Up BIS DX.png|thumb|right|The Bonus Level Up Roulette seen on [[Mario]]'s level up screen.]]The Bonus Level Up Roulette appears once again in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]''. In this game, whenever [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], or [[Bowser]] [[Level Up|levels up]], they can choose a stat to apply bonus points to, For Mario and Luigi, a [[Fishin' Lakitu]] holds a fishing rod with the roulette and for Bowser, a literal [[Fishin' Boo]] (a [[Boo]] a fishing rod) holds the roulette, the roulette remains identical to how it was in the previous game with the same roulette speed and the same easy timing. Just as before, honing in one stat too much will cause more 1's to appear in a roll but this can be fixed by moving to other stats which will cause the high numbers to appear again after a couple levels. Unlike the original, this time, the highest amount of earnable points from a Bonus Level Up Roulette is 5 rather than 9, just as before, if the player changes their mind about one stat, they can cancel their choice with L.

Just like the previous game, upon the first level up, the game will tell players they can add Bonus Points to a stat of their choice and just like the original, [[Toadbert]] in the [[Toad Square]] of [[Bowser's Body]] will provide advice on Bonus Level Ups once he is rescued from the [[Nerve Cluster]].

Like ''Mario & Luigi: Dream Team'' and unlike the original, [[Giant Bowser]] battles no longer provide [[EXP]] meaning there is no longer a unique Bonus Level Up Roulette for the Giant Battles in this game.


==Trivia==
*The Bonus Level Up Roulette is slightly similar to the [[Level Up]]s in the ''[[Paper Mario (series)|Paper Mario]]'' series where players can choose between applying bonus points to either [[HP]], [[FP]], or [[Badge Point|BP]].
*Prior to ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'', due to the lack of tutorials explaining how the Bonus Level-Up Roulette works, many players would just constantly solely level up their [[HP]] thinking it was the only stat that could be leveled up, ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' provides a full tutorial explaining how the Bonus Level-Up Roulette works and in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' the game, tells players upon their first [[Level Up]] that they can add bonus points to a stat of their choice.
**While Player's Guides and Prima Guides mentioned the feature, the games themselves did not until ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]'' (with the exception of the Japanese version of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' which has a D-Pad icon by [[Lakitu]] telling players they can move the direction of the roulette).
*Coincidentally, every single ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' game that features the Bonus Level Up Roulette to date is also every game that features [[Toadsworth]] and the [[Spin Jump (Mario & Luigi series)|Spin Jump]] ability.
