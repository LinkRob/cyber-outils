'''Dream Fencing''' is a [[Dream Event]] in the [[Wii]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]''. It is also where [[Eggman Nega]] is fought in a one-to-one match in [[London Party]], during the second match against him.

== Layout ==
Dream Fencing takes place at [[w:c:sonic:Ocean Palace|Ocean Palace]].

== Gameplay ==
The goal for each player is to deplete all of their opponent's health within two minutes. Knocking an opponent off the stage causes that opponent to lose a lot of their health. If a character is in danger of falling off, they can swing the {{button|wii|Wiimote}} and {{button|wii|Nunchuk}} (or swing the {{button|wii|Wiimote}} up and down, if playing with just a Wii Remote) to return on stage. However, a character that falls off can use a Special Thrust upon returning on the stage. If a character is knocked out, they can return as a bomb to attack the other players, similar to becoming a [[Mini Bomb Kart]] in Battle mode of ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''.

[[Egg Pawn]]s and [[Egg Flapper]]s sometimes appear on the stage. If a character touches them, they take damage. By performing a thrust, a character can knock them into their opponents, making them lose health.

Dream Fencing ends when there is only one player still standing. If there are two or more players still standing when the time is up, then the player with the most health left wins.
