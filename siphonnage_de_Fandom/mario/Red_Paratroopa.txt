'''Red Paratroopas''' are a recurring enemy in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Red Paratroopa is one of the color variations of [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s. The only difference with a Red Paratroopa's appearance from the standard green type is that Red Paratroopas wear a [[Red Shell]].

===Behavior===
In the 2D {{series|Super Mario}} installments, Red Paratroopas have a distinct behavior from the green type in that they usually fly up and down in a set path, rather than bounce forward or fly back and forth horizontally although in the ''[[New Super Mario Bros. (series)|New Super Mario Bros.]]'' series, both flying and bouncing Red Paratroopas can be seen. Similarly to other Koopa Paratroopas, stomping a Red Paratroopa turns it into a [[Red Koopa Troopa]].

==Trivia==
*Although green Paratroopas are the standard color variation of Koopa Paratroopas, various spinoff appearances featuring a playable Paratroopa, such as ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]'' and ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'', prioritize using the red type. In the {{series|Mario Baseball}}, both a green and a red Paratroopa are playable.
