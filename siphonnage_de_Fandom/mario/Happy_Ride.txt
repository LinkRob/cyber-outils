The {{Nihongo|'''Happy Ride'''|	ハッピーライド|Happī Raido|Happy Ride}} is a wedding-themed vehicle that debuted in [[Peach Tour (2020)|Peach Tour]] in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

== In ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' ==
The kart debuted in ''Tour'' as a new kart in Peach Tour. It reappeared in the [[Princess Tour (2023)|Princess Tour]] on the second week in the Spotlight Shop.
