'''Bubble Blowing''' is one of the four minigames in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. In the game, Mario must jump inside a bubble. The player must then use the star cursor to blow Mario around. This minigame can be played in the [[Bubble Breeze Galaxy]] and the [[Bubble Blast Galaxy]]. It is played both times over a hazardous substance. It has a small part in [[Gold Leaf Galaxy]]s [[The Bell on the Big Tree]] where all the [[Rainbow Notes]] must be collected around the big tree. Unlike the other galaxies, the player cannot lose a life if he or she falls.
