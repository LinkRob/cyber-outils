'''She-slime '''is an orange variant of a [[Slime]] and a playable character in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''. In the ''[[Dragon Quest]]'' franchise it is considered to be a slightly stronger variant of a regular [[Slime]].

==Profile==
She-slime isn't much different from the regular Slime, except when it comes to their color and strength. She-slime seems to be made out of a liquid substance too.

==In ''Mario Sports Mix''==
She-slime is a variation of [[Slime]] that can be unlocked by picking Slime 20 times in a sport. Unlike in the ''Dragon Quest'' games, She-slime only fills in here as an unlockable skin.
