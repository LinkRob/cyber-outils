'''Blue Peppers''' are items which appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]''. Using it raises the [[SPD-Up]] status for all characters for the remainder of the battle; as such, it is the speed-based counterpart to [[Red Pepper]]s and [[Green Pepper]]s. It costs 20 [[coin]]s to buy a blue pepper
