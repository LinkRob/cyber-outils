'''Negative Zone''' is [[Luigi]]'s [[Final Smash]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

== Description ==
When Negative Zone is used, Luigi emits a strange force field that causes random status effects. The force field is fairly large as it can take up nearly all of [[Final Destination]]. Anyone else that is trapped in the Negative Zone will suffer a random status effect such as sleep, slowness, or confusion. The move itself lasts a little over 10 seconds and Luigi can move on his own after he finishes dancing. Like [[Peach Blossom]], Negative Zone does not KO an opponent, but it will leave any foe trapped inside wide open to an attack.

== Trivia ==
*Negative Zone is the only Final Smash of a ''Mario'' character in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' to not return in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''.
**This move is replaced with [[Poltergust 5000 (Final Smash)|Poltergust 5000]].
**With Negative Zone being removed from later ''Super Smash Bros.'' titles, [[Peach Blossom]] and [[Daisy Blossom]] are the only Final Smashes that cannot KO an opponent.
*The origin of Negative Zone is though to be because of Luigi's sadness of being constantly in [[Mario]]'s shadow.
