'''Pestnuts''' are enemies found in the [[Chucklehuck Woods]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. They resemble a chestnut covered with spikes. Pestnuts have the ability to disguise their true form as a brown [[Chuckola Fruit]]. A stronger variation of them, named [[Elite Pestnut]] in the remake, appear in [[Oho Ocean Seabed]].

== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' ==
Pestnuts attack by steamrolling into their target. The Pestnut may roll into one of the Mario Bros. from the front, which can only be countered with the use of a hammer. The Pestnut may also attack from behind, which can only be dodged by jumping. Unless Mario or Luigi are wearing one of the Shiny Killer Jump [[Badge]] the attacker will take damage upon jumping on them. In the original Superstar Saga, jumping on Pestnuts to counter-attack their backside roll will still damage the Bros. even if they are wearing one.

After you drop their [[Heart Point|stamina]] to 0, Pestnuts will break, releasing either a [[Beanie]] or [[Gold Beanie]] who will then continue to fight. However, due to how Elite Pestnuts make their home in the ocean, defeating an Elite Pestnut actually does kill the Beanie/Gold Beanie inside.

Upon their defeat, Pestnuts give differing [[Experience Point|Experience rewards]] and Loot depending on their location. In the [[Chucklehuck Woods]] area, they will give 15 experience and drop 2 coins per kill along with a Mushroom or Super Mushroom. Their "elite" variation in the [[Oho Ocean Seabed]] gives 35 experience and 5 coins. Both variants take increased damage from [[Firebrand|fire-based attacks]], though only the Chucklehuck variant can be burned, with a 3 in 5 chance, while the elite variation is immune to the burn condition. The Chucklehuck variant has a 3 in 10 chance to be [[Dizzy|stunned]] from an attack that stuns enemies, while the elite variation has a 3 in 5 chance. Regardless their type, Pestnuts can have their [[POW-Down|stats lowered]] [[DEF-Down|quite]] [[SPD-Down|easily]].

==In ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' ==
Pestnuts also appear in ''Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions'' in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]''. Pestnuts fall under the classification of melee troopers, and attack (like they do in the regular game) by rolling into their opponents. Because of their spikes, if a Pestnut gets attacked from the side or above, the attacker will receive damage. Their special skill is Spin to Win. When used, they will roll into a minion three times. Pestnuts are weak against ranged minions, and are especially weak against [[Spear Guy]]s.
