'''Parachute Cap I''' is a [[Badge Challenge]] located in [[Pipe-Rock Plateau]]. It is unlocked after clearing [[Badge House in Pipe-Rock Plateau]], and contains one [[Wonder Seed]]. 

== Layout ==
The course has many pits and [[Semisolid Platform|Semisolid Platforms]], which make the badge necessary for completing the course. Also on the course are many [[Wow Bud|Wow Buds]] which spawn lifts and platforms to help the player. 

== [[10-Flower Coin]] locations ==
The first coin is found on top of a track with two platforms moving around it. The player needs to jump on one of said platforms and ride it to the top, and collect it.

The second coin is found right after the [[Checkpoint Flag]], above and to the right of two platforms moving side-to-side. The player has to jump off the higher platform and use the badge skill to obtain it.

The last coin is found directly above the [[Goal Pole|goal pole]].{{SMBWNav}}
