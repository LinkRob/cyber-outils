'''High Hoops''' is a recurring [[microgame]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', under [[Jimmy T|Jimmy T.]]'s [[Sports]] folder. The microgame later returns in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', in which it is also one of Jimmy's microgames.

==Layout==
The microgame is shown from a slightly distant side angle. In ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'', the man, basketball, and basketball hoop are all depicted with a black silhouette, and the basketball hoop is also floating in midair. The background is cream-colored, while the floor is light gray with an upper black linear gradient.

In ''WarioWare Gold'', the scene is now in full color, and is shown to take place in a gymnasium. A few of the background features include two light blue doors on the left side, a hardwood floor, and a white backboard that the hoop is attached to. The hoop is no longer floating in midair, and it has a red rim. The basketball player wears a red basketball uniform with a white line on the side of both the shirt and pants, black socks, and red sneakers that are slightly darker than his uniform. 

==Gameplay==
A man holding a basketball continuously [[jump]]s near a basketball hoop. When the man is at the peak of his jump, the player must press the A button to have him throw the ball into the hoop.

Each difficulty level places the basketball hoop higher than the one prior.

==Trivia==
*The microgame's name is a pun on the phrase "high hopes."
