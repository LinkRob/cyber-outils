}}The '''Space Junk Galaxy''' is a galaxy in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. It is the first galaxy in the [[Fountain]] and has a space/futuristic theme. Like its name implies, the Space Junk Galaxy contains lots of discarded space junk, including old rocket ships, floating debris, and various other objects and platforms. A lot of the objects are silver or blue, and there is a spaceship in it. Mario needs to have at least 9 Power Stars in his possession in order to access this area, including the Grand Star from [[Bowser Jr.'s Robot Reactor]].

The [[Pull Star]] is used much more here than in the other known galaxies. Also, [[Toad]]s in the [[Toad Brigade]] are on a big platform covered in crystals; if [[Mario]] frees the Toads trapped in the crystals, the main [[Starshroom]] will appear with a [[Sling Star]] that he can use to go to the next location. There is also a wooden planet shaped like [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]]'s head.

The last unique feature of this galaxy is that it has a path that builds itself when Mario approaches it, but crumbles after he leaves. This is later re-used in [[Bowser's Galaxy Reactor]], except the path crumbles after a few seconds automatically, and does not rebuild itself.

Mario fights two bosses in this galaxy, which are [[Kamella]] and [[Tarantox]].

== Missions ==
=== Major Missions ===

==== Mission 1: Pull Star Path ====
As the name suggests, there are a lot of [[Pull Star]]s, which Mario must use to make his way through the Galaxy. At the end Mario has to get 5 [[Silver Star]]s on a place full of small bits of path, which join together to make a path for Mario to go across.

Mario visits the following ares:

# Starting Planet
# Star Dust Rolling Step Planet (Mario can backtrack from this point by looking at the Pull Star between this planet in first person view and grabbing it from there.)
# G Capture Tutorial Zone 1
# Orb Planets
# Rocket Ship (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)
# G Capture Tutorial Zone 2
# Floating Tower Planet
# Assembly Block Planet
# Space Junk Area (Mario must backtrack to the Assembly Block Planet to get the [[Power Star]] after grabbing all five [[Silver Star|Silver Stars]].)

==== Mission 2: Kamella's Airship Attack ====
In this mission Mario has to go across [[airship]]s to fight [[Kamella]] after she brings her battle fleet close. He uses [[Green Shell]]s a lot in this mission.

Mario visits the following areas:

# Starting Planet
# Kamella's Airship Fleet (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)
## First Airship
## Asteroid Platforms
## Second Airship (Mario can backtrack from this point with a perfectly-aimed [[Long Jump]].)
## Third Airship (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)
## Kamella's Airship (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)

==== Mission 3: Tarantox's Tangled Web ====
Mario must traverse various planets until he finally breaks open Tarantox's planet and fights him. [[Sling Pod]]s are first seen here.

Once Mario finishes the level, the [[Sling Pod Galaxy]] will be unlocked via [[Hungry Luma]].

Mario visits the following areas:

# Starting Planet
# Star Dust Rolling Step Planet (Mario can backtrack from this point by looking at the Pull Star between this planet and the Starting Planet in first person view and grabbing it.)
# Small Maze Planet
## Outer Shell
## Maze Mantle (Mario can backtrack from this point with a [[Backward Somersault]].)
# Phantom Boss Spider Zone
## Grave Stone A (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)
## Grave Stone B (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)
## Abandoned Rocket Ship
## Grave Stone C (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)
## Tarantox's Lair (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)

=== Secret Star: Yoshi's Unexpected Appearance ===
After Mario feeds a Hungry Luma, it turns into the Yoshi Head Planet. On this planet, Mario must defeat all the [[Goomba]]s to get the [[Power Star]] to appear. A counter is kept for the number of times Mario jumps on Goombas consecutively, and if the count goes over 8 Mario will get 1-Ups. 

Mario visits the following areas:

# Starting Planet
# Star Dust Rolling Step Planet (Mario can backtrack from this point by looking at the Pull Star between this planet and the Starting Planet in first person view and grabbing it.)
# Small Maze Planet
## Outer Shell
## Maze Mantle (Mario must backtrack from this point with a [[Backward Somersault]] to continue with the secret star.)
# Yoshi Head Planet (Mario cannot backtrack from this point.)

=== Prankster Comets ===
As with all major Galaxies in the first ''Super Mario Galaxy'' (not counting boss Galaxies), there are two [[Prankster Comet|Prankster Comets]]: a Speedy Comet and (as usual) a Purple Comet.

==== Pull Star Path Speed Run (Speedy Comet in Orbit) ====
This mission is only accessible, unless already completed, if a [[Speedy Comet]] is in orbit. Mario must go through Pull Star Path under a time limit.

Mario visits the same areas he does in Pull Star Path, in the same order.

==== Purple Coin Spacewalk (Purple Comit in Orbit) ====
Mario must collect 100 [[Purple Coin]]s in the area at the end of Pull Star Path. The coins come in bunches of 10 on several platforms. It is also timed. This mission is only accessible, unless already completed, if a [[Purple Comet]] is in orbit.

The only areas Mario visits is the Space Junk Area; as the Purple Coins are in the Space Junk Area, and the Power Star appears on the Assembly Block Planet itself, he must backtrack to the Assembly Block Planet to get the star after getting all the Purple Coins.

== Layout ==
=== Starting Planet (Star Dust Start Planet) ===
The Starting Planet is a square planet whose top surface Mario starts his trek through the galaxy on. It has all-around gravity and slightly resembles the landing planet in the Space Junk Area. It has a metal frame attached to its core, which resembles the Star Dust Rolling Step Planet that comes immediately after it, but much shorter.

=== Star Dust Rolling Step Planet ===
This planet is visited in all missions except Kamella's Airship Attack and Purple Coin Spacewalk, and is also featured in the icon of the Galaxy. It resembles the Starting Planet's core, but much longer and without a frame, and has a cylindrical inward gravity field so Mario can walk all around its surface. A few crystals appear on the planet containing [[Star Bit|Star Bits]], though not in the Galaxy icon. In Pull Star Path, Pull Star Path Speed Run, and Tarantox's Tangled Web (and Yoshi's Unexpected Appearance due to it being a Secret Star accessed from Tarantox's Tangled Web), the planet is at the start of a [[Pull Star]] chain.

=== G Capture Tutorial Zones ===
Two asteroid fields appear in the Galaxy, filled with asteroids with the file name G Capture Tutorial Zone, hence their name. Their areas have a [[Pull Star]] chain in Pull Star Path, Pull Star Path Speed Run (by proxy due to Pull Star Path), Tarantox's Tangled Web (G Capture Tutorial Zone 1), and Yoshi's Unexpected Appearance (by proxy due to Tarantox's Tangled Web; G Capture Tutorial Zone 1 only).

==== G Capture Tutorial Zone 1 ====
This is an area visited in Pull Star Path, Pull Star Path Speed Run (by proxy due to Pull Star Path), Tarantox's Tangled Web, and Yoshi's Unexpected Appearance (by proxy due to Tarantox's Tangled Web). Close to the end of this section of the Galaxy is an L-shaped block with six [[Star Bit|Star Bits]] on it.

==== G Capture Tutorial Zone 2 ====
Visited only in Pull Star Path, this area follows the Rocket Ship. It has a more complex path formed by its asteroids, and three glass orbs with Pull Stars lead to it from the Rocket Ship. Close to the end is a disk-shaped asteroid that looks like a cookie.

===Orb Planets===
These planets are visited only in Pull Star Path and Pull Star Path Speed Run, and they are accessed via Pull Star chain from the Star Dust Rolling Step Planet. Hence their name, these Planets are three spherical glass planets. There are three Amps moving around them. They have a ring of metal around their surface and two metal plates on either side of their spherical surfaces. There are five Star Chips moving about on these planets, each of which have a spherical gravity field so Mario can walk around them. After collecting the Star Chips, Mario can use a Launch Star that appears between two of the planets to go to the Rocket Planet. One is featured in the icon.

Three similar-looking planets appear in the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]]'s first mission, The Sinking Lava Spire (and therefore Lava Spire Daredevil Run), just after Mario leaves the Starting Planet.

===Rocket Ship===
After his departure from the Orb Planets, Mario will land on a "planet" that is actually a big, long, red-and-tan space ship floating around in space. The rocket's loot consists mostly of just [[coin]]s. It is similar to [[w:c:pikmin:Captain Olimar|Olimar]]'s ship in terms of its design, possibly referring to the [[w:c:pikmin:Pikmin (series)|''Pikmin'']] franchise, but this has not been confirmed. A Pull Star chain connects it to the Floating Tower Planet. One of the two rockets appears on the Galaxy icon, presumably this one since it doesn't appear as damaged as the one in Tarnatox's Tangled Web.

After this planet are three glass orbs. They look similar to the Orb Planets that were visited earlier, even having inward gravity fields, but unlike the Orb Planets, these orbs have Pull Stars in their cores; Mario can still use the Pull Stars within the orbs to make his way forward, with the third one having a [[Checkpoint]].

===Floating Tower Planet===
This planet is a T-shaped planet that resembles a tower. It contains some Spoings. It is also where Mario finds the Toad Brigade, all of them trapped in a Crystal. After Mario frees them all, the Starshroom will appear. Various items and a Goomba are also trapped in crystals. On the top of the Starshroom is a Sling Star, which will take Mario to the Space Junk Area.

=== Assembly Block Planet and Space Junk Area===
The Space Junk Area contains the final planet of the Pull Star Path mission. There is junk platforms everywhere, and after making his way here, [[Mario]] needs to collect all 5 [[Silver Star]]s in order to make a [[Power Star]] and complete the mission. You can complete the mission by collecting the Silver Stars while the junk lets Mario stand on it. There is a very massive [[Black Hole]] under the platforms, and if you fall you get sucked into it. This area also takes place on 2 comets, the [[Purple Comet]] and the [[Speedy Comet]]. Both of those comets have the really massive Black Hole, and the latter's maze is altered.

===[[Kamella]]'s Airship Fleet Area===
Although it is bare in most areas, this area is infested with [[Airship]]s during Mission 2: Kamella's Airship Attack. In this Mission, Kamella invades. The airships have lots of Green Shells, Goombas, and boxes. There is even a secret room with a ton of Star Bits accessed by a Pull Star on one of the ships. She and the others supposedly retreat after this mission is completed, which may explain why it is empty.

=== Small Maze Planet ===
This of the Unknown Planets is a planet that is encountered shortly before the battle with Tarantox, and marks a checkpoint in Tarantox's Tangled Web. At first, Mario walks on top of it, which is a transparent crystal orb. Once he finds the passageway in, he can go under the crystal layer and explore underneath, where [[1-Up Mushroom]]s and other treats are waiting. Mario can still use his Star Pointer to grab [[Star Bit|Star Bits]], and also backtrack if it is his wish by jumping back out of the passage's entrance. A [[Launch Star]] inside the planet blasts Mario out of the glassy surface, sending him to the Grave Stone Planets. Pull Star Path has Mario pass by this planet en route to the first 

===Phantom Boss Spider Zone===
This area is where [[Tarantox]] makes his lair. It is rather sparse in matter and is only visited in Mission 3: Tarantox's Tangled Web. Few asteroids form a [[Sling Pod]] chain to the end.

==== Grave Stone Planets ====
These desolate planets appear en route to Tarantox's lair, their surfaces made of dark red and brown stone.

===== Grave Stone A =====
This planet is a planet mostly made of maroon, dark red, brownish rock. It has a sort of arch, as seen in the picture. This planet is an entrance to Tarantox's lair, and its gravity points downward towards a [[Black Hole]]. The [[A Tense Moment|pre-boss]] music begins when Mario lands on this planet, and the spider's Sticky Planet is nearby. A single [[Mushroom Retainer]] appears on this planet, trapped in the [[Sling Pod]] at the end, and must be launched out of the Sling Pod before Mario can use it. If the Mushroom Retainer is spoken to, they will explain that they are stuck.

This is the first planet in the game to have a Sling Pod and is the beginning of a long path of them.

===== Grave Stone B =====
This planet is a small square block with a Sling Pod that leads to the Abandoned Rocket Ship, and it has an inward gravity field despite its small size. If the player aims the Sling Pod correctly while they are trapped in it, the Mushroom Retainer on Grave Stone A will land on Grave Stone B. The Mushroom Retainer will vanish if the player does not aim correctly. However, the Mushroom Retainer can get trapped in the Sling Pod on Grave Stone B if their launch is aimed improperly, which can trigger a glitch where if Mario attempts to jump into the Sling Pod for his own use while not moving in a lateral direction, a conversation will happen with the Mushroomer yelling out about being stuck (which, despite being a bug, is actually possible in real life at that distance aside from the game taking place in space)<ref>https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qC-ypi33wMY</ref>. Upon being spoken to at Grave Stone B, the Mushroom Retainer thanks Mario for saving them.

===== Grave Stone C =====
This is the penultimate planet in the game, and has an inward gravity field, just like the Grave Stone B planet. It follows the Abandoned Rocket Ship.

====Abandoned Rocket Ship====
Just like the one in the first mission, here drifts a lonely rocket whose riders apparently died or abandoned ship. It is infested with spiders, but behaves otherwise like the first one. At its end is a Sling Pod that leads to Grave Stone C. It is unlikely the icon features this rocket, due to the rocket appearing there being not as damaged. See above for more details.

==== Tarantox's Planet ====
Tarantox's Planet is exceptionally large and looks like a giant version of the top of a Sling Pod. In fact, Mario uses a Sling Pod to get to the planet, breaking its shell open and revealing a giant sticky spider web, where he fights Tarantox, who is frozen inside. It has outward pointing gravity on the inside.

=== Yoshi Planet ===
The Yoshi Planet is a planet in the shape of a large, wooden Yoshi head that shoots red [[Steam Jet]]s out of its nostrils. It only appears in Yoshi's Unexpected Appearance. This appears to be the third reference to the Yoshi clan in ''Super Mario Galaxy'', the first being the Yoshi game file icon, and the second the Yoshi Egg Planet that was shown earlier.

When Mario arrives on this planet, the music changes to [[Space Fantasy]].

== Soundtrack ==
Aside from the standard jingles for when the mission starts, whenever Mario dies, and whenever he gets a [[Power Star]], the following songs play in this Galaxy:

* [[Space Junk Road]] -- Plays in most areas in the first mission up until [[Mario (character)|Mario]] frees the [[Toad Brigade]] and in the third mission until he leaves the Small Maze Planet.
* Kinopio Expedition Party -- Plays when Mario frees the Toad Brigade. The song fades when he uses the [[Launch Star]] to get to the Assembly Block Planet.
* [[Underground Theme|Space Athletic]] -- Plays in the Space Junk Area.
* [[Airship Theme|Airship Armada]] -- Plays in Kamella's Airship Attack until [[Kamella]] attacks.
* Boss Kamek -- Plays when Mario and Kamella fight.
* Star Get Chance -- Plays after Kamella, [[Tarantox]], and the Goombas on the Yoshi Head planet are defeated.
* [[A Tense Moment]] -- Plays when Mario lands on the first Grave Stone Planet.
* Dino Pakkun -- Plays when Mario fights Tarantox.
* [[Space Fantasy]] -- Plays when Mario lands on the Yoshi Head Planet.
* Speedy Comet/Hurry -- Plays in Pull Star Path Speed Run.
* Purple Comet -- Plays in Purple Coin Space Walk.
