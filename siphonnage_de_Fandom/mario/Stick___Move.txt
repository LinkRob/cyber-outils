'''Stick & Move''' is one of the [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', being a part of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[Sci-Fi]] folder.

==Layout==
There is a roof on the left and right sides of the screen each, and in between them are three thinner structures. One or more samurai stand in the gaps between roofs, continuously thrusting long poles upwards.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Tiny Ninja]], who has to make his way from the left roof to the right roof. To accomplish this, the player must press {{button|gba|A}} with good timing to have Tiny Ninja jump across without getting hit by any of the poles held by the samurais. If Tiny Ninja makes it across, the player wins at the microgame, but if Tiny Ninja is hit by a pole, the player loses at the microgame.

===Difficulty levels===
Each difficulty level adds one additional samurai.

*'''Level 1:''' There is one samurai at the fourth gap.
*'''Level 2:''' There are two samurais, located at the second and fourth gaps respectively.
*'''Level 3:''' There are three samurais, each located at the second, third, and fourth gaps but not the first one.
