'''Rocky Rope Race''' is a 2-vs.-2 [[minigame]] found in ''[[Super Mario Party Jamboree]]''.

==Opening==
The camera zooms up to the cave with the red team swinging through, before the screen splits, revealing the blue team.

==Gameplay==
Players must take turns swinging along a cave wall via a series of bars by letting go of one bar while their partner holds on to one and grabbing on to the next bar. If both players on a team let go of the bars, the team will fall, and both members will be brought back up by [[Lakitu]]s before resuming. The first team to reach the end wins. If three minutes pass, the minigame will end in a tie.

==Ending==
Outside the cave, the winning and losing teams perform their respective animations, with the losing players behind the winning players.

==Controls==
*Hold {{button|switch|jc-right}} to grab bar

==In-game text==
''Two players take turns swinging to the next bar along the route to the goal. Make sure your teammate has a firm grip on their bar before you let go of yours!''
