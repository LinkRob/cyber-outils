A '''Deburīna''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Deburīna is a dancing, female ballerina pig wearing a blue dress and a spiky tutu.

===Behavior===
Deburīna slowly dance towards [[Wario]] to try and damage him. If [[jump]]ed onto, a Deburīna falls to the ground and cries, but gets back up shortly afterward.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
[[File:Deburina WL4 screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|A Deburina, after emerging from a painting]]
Deburīna only appear in the level [[Doodle Woods]], where there are drawings of them on the walls. Some of these drawings come to life when Wario either gets near or walks under it.

Because of their spiky tutu, Deburīna cannot be attacked from the side while dancing, and if Wario attempts to attack a Deburīna, he takes damage. Wario can rather defeat a Deburīna by [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounding]] it. While a Deburīna is on the ground, it is also vulnerable to a side attack, such as a [[Dash Attack]].
