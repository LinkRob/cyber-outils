'''Windbags''' are an enemy which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Windbags are plants from which a peach-colored eggplant sprouts. A Windbag has a large circle-shaped mouth.

===Behavior===
Windbags use their mouth to blow strong gusts of wind on a Nipper Dandelion, from which four [[Nipper Spore]]s are blown out of. If a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] throws an [[Yoshi Egg|Egg]] at a Windbag, it dies out, but sprouts back after a few seconds.

==In ''Yoshi's Island DS''==
Windbags appear in as early as the first level, [[Ba-dum BUM!]]. Two Windbags can be found in the botanical-enemy exhibit of the [[Island Museum]].
