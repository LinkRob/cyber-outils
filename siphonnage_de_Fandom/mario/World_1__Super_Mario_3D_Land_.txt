'''World 1''' is the first world in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. There are four levels, a castle, a [[Mystery Box]], and a [[Toad House]].

== Descripton ==
World 1 is the first world in ''3D Land''. There is a total of seven levels in this world. The world is a grassland with trees and mountains.
