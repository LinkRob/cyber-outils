'''Snap Jaws''' are small, brown crocodile enemies which appear in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. They are often located around [[vine]]s, which they climb on using their jaws. Snap Jaws try to drop onto a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] to attack, and if Snap Jaw touches a Yoshi, the [[Countdown Timer]] is triggered.

Given that Snap Jaws are usually present around vines, they usually appear in [[level]]s in which [[Baby Donkey Kong|Baby DK]] is the main baby. Snap Jaws first appear in 2-7: [[Yoshi on Stilts]], and one is shown in the level icon for [[Use the Magnet to Get Rich Quick!]], in which it appears to be climbing down a series of vines.

Four Snap Jaws appear in the land-enemy exhibit of the [[Island Museum]].
