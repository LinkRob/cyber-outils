'''Drillbit Crabs''' are a crab enemy that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]'' and returning in [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[reissue|remake]], ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story + Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]'' and its exclusive side-game, [[Bowser Jr.'s Journey]]. Drillbit Crabs are encountered on [[Plack Beach]].

== Profile ==
Drillbit Crabs resemble a real crab, but their shell resembles a drill. Their main body is red.

== Appearances ==
In combat, Drillbit Crabs may attack by ramming their drill into a Bro. (This can be blocked by batting them away with the hammer.) or drilling from underneath and moving sideways, something that can be jumped over. The target (first target for the latter attack) can be spotted by how the Drillbit Crab tilts before it attacks, as it will always tilt toward said character before it starts its attack. [[Bowser]] can block both moves by punching it.

[[Pendril]]s sometimes assist Drillbit Crabs in battle.
