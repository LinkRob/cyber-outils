==Gameplay==
The Mine Cart Ride is a minigame that involves riding in a mine cart and going through a course to collect coins. This minigame is played by [[Diddy Kong]] at [[Jungle Japes]], [[Chunky Kong]] at [[Fungi Forest]], and [[Donkey Kong]] at [[Creepy Castle]].
