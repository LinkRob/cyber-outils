The '''Pyramid Stone''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
The Pyramid Stone can only be found in [[Dry Dry Ruins (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Ruins]] in a room guarded by two [[Stone Chomp]]s. The Pyramid Stone must be used along with the [[Diamond Stone]] and the [[Lunar Stone]] in order to access [[Tutankoopa]]'s room to rescue the second [[Star Spirit]].
