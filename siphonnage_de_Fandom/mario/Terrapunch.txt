|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Terrapunch''' is a special move that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It can only be used by [[Jagger]] and [[Terra Cotta]]s.

==Effects==
Terrapunch is a physical move that targets one character. When Jagger or a Terra Cotta use this move, they wind up a punch before unleashing it dealing twice the normal damage. It can be easily blocked, however.
