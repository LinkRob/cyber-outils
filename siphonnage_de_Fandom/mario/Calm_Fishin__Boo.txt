'''Calm Fishin' Boo''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. 

== Profile ==
===Physical description===
Calm Fishin' Boos are a variant of [[Fishin' Boo]] imbued with the [[Calm]] [[vibe]], but like with other Calm enemies, Calm Fishin' Boos share an appearance with their regular counterparts.

===Behavior===
At first, a Calm Fishin' Boo is asleep, but if [[Princess Peach|Peach]] moves by one too quickly, it wakes up and begins to attack like a regular Fishin' Boo.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Calm Fishin' Boos only appear in one [[level]], [[Shriek Mansion 3-5]]. In the [[Glossary (Super Princess Peach)|Glossary]], their name is spelled '''C. Fishing Boo'''.

==Game data==
*'''Glossary:''' ''Walk quietly by when it's sleeping.''
