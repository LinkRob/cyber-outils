'''Tuff Puffs''' are an enemy in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. They are smaller components of [[Huff N. Puff]] that break off his body during his boss fight. Tuff Puffs are very weak, as they have only 1 [[Heart Point|HP]], an [[Attack]] rating of 2, and a [[Defense]] rating of 0. Even though Tuff Puffs come in smaller and larger variants, their stats remain the same. The Tuff Puffs attack by using their [[HP Drain]] move on [[Mario]].
