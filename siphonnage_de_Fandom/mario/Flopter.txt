'''Flopter''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[Nintendo Switch]] [[reissue|port]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''. Here, they are mainly encountered in train, tank, and castle levels.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Flopter resembles an insect with a flower popping out of its body.

===Behavior===
A Flopter flies around the stage upon encountered. It then falls to the ground to try and attack [[Mario]] or another playable character.
