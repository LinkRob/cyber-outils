|games = ''[[Paper Mario]]''}}'''Starborn Valley''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. It is located within the [[Shiver Region]] and is where the [[Star Kid]]s are born. When [[Mario]] first arrives, the Star Kids aren't ascending into [[Star Haven]] due to the actions of [[Bowser]].

==Layout==
Starborn Valley is mainly a small town covered in snow. There is also three houses: an [[Inn]], a house with a [[Ninji]] inside, and [[Merle]]'s house.

==History==
After defeating the [[Monstar]], Mario and his friends arrive at Starborn Valley where they meet up with Merle. Merle instructs Mario to enter his house with him also apologizing for the Star Kids behavior earlier. Merle then explains to Mario that the last Star Spirit is being held at the [[Crystal Palace]]. He also gives Mario the [[Scarf]] as a key to getting to the palace and recommends Mario to head back to [[Shiver City]].
