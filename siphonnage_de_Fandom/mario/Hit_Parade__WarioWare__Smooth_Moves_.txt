'''Hit Parade''' is one of [[Penny Crygor|Penny]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Gameplay==
The object is to whack the moles, which are covered in black and have mischievous expressions, with the hammer by tilting the [[Wii Remote]] accordingly. If done successfully, the moles re-emerge from their holes in their normal state and say, "Hi!" If the player does not hit the required number of moles on time, they lose at the microgame.

The first, second, and third difficulty levels feature one, two, and three moles to hit, respectively.
