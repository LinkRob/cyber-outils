'''Nipper Dandelions''' are a type of plant consisting of a stem and four [[Nipper Spore]]s on top in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. Upon being blown by a gust of [[wind]], the Nipper Spores are released from the Nipper Dandelion and blown into the air.

In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'', Nipper Dandelions receive a passing mention from [[Viridi]] during the [[Palutena's Guidance]] conversation for [[Piranha Plant]]. Although this is the case, Nipper Dandelions do not bear any visual similarity to Piranha Plants, nor do they have any similar traits.
