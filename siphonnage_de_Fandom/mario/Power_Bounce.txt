'''Power Bounce''' is a [[badge]] in the {{series|Paper Mario}}. Wearing it allows [[Mario]] to perform consecutive [[Boots|jump]] attacks on a single enemy if the player presses {{button|n64|A}} repeatedly.

== In {{series|Paper Mario}} ==
=== ''[[Paper Mario]]'' ===
Power Bounce has a [[Badge Point|BP]] cost of two. Mario finds Power Bounce within [[Koopa Bros. Fortress]], in a cage in the middle of the fortress. To get in the cage, Mario has to pass through a small opening behind the cage, along the back wall.

This badge has a record set in [[Mario's Pad]]. On the chalkboard, at the bottom, there is a record for most consecutive Power Bounces.

=== ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' ===
Power Bounce has a slightly increased BP cost of three. It is found inside of a red [[Question Block]] in [[Hooktail Castle]].

Like Mario's Pad in ''Paper Mario'', Mario can check his record for most consecutive Power Bounces in this game by using the sign in front of the [[Pit of 100 Trials]].
