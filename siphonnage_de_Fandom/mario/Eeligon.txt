An '''Eeligon''' is an enemy which appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are a relative of [[Hooligon]]s, and Eeligons have a stronger dark variant named [[Dark Eeligon]]s.

== History ==
Eeligons are strange, alien-like enemies that can only be found in the [[Outer Limits]]. They spend their time hopping around into space. When an Eeligon spots one of the heroes, it will move toward them attempting to ram into them.
