The '''Visceral Vaporizer''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Visceral Vaporizer.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Visceral Vaporizer resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Visceral Vaporizer deals around 95 to 105 damage. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]].

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Visceral Vaporizer as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it when the player defeats [[Icicle Golem]].
