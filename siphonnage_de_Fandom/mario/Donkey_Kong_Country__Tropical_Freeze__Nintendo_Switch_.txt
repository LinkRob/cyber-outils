'''''Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze''''' is a [[Nintendo Switch]] platforming game released in 2018. It is a port of the [[Wii U]] game of the [[Donkey Kong Country: Tropical Freeze|same name]], and it was developed by [[Retro Studios]] and published by [[Nintendo]]. The most notable addition to the Nintendo Switch port is Funky Mode, in which the player gets to play as [[Funky Kong]].
