'''Tara Lynn Charendoff-Strong''' (born February 12, 1973) is a Canadian-American actress, voice-over artist, comedian, and singer, perhaps best known for voice acting in animated films and television. Her credited ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]''-relevant role is providing the voice for [[Iggy Koopa|Hop Koopa]] in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World]]'' animated series.
