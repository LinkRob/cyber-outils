}}'''Attack FX R''' is a [[badge]] that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Effects==
[[Mario]] can wear this badge for zero [[Badge Point|BP]], and wearing the badge makes some of Mario's attacks make a cricket sound.

The location of this badge can be quite tricky. It is only obtainable after Mario gets the ability to transform into a straight line of paper and can go through thin holes. With this new ability, Mario can by-pass the metal bars in prison cells in one of the rooms in [[Hooktail Castle]]. Inside one of these cages is this very [[badge]].

[[Hooktail]] hates crickets more than anything else in the world according to [[Kolorado]]'s deceased father. With this info and this badge, Mario can lower Hooktail's defense by one and attack by two after Mario attacks.

In the [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]], Hooktail's weakness is now Frogs, hence the Attack FX R Badge in the remake has been replaced with the [[Attack FX G]] badge which now has a frog sound effect, the Attack FX R badge can still be bought from [[Lovely Howz of Badges]] in the remake but the sound effects have been changed to that of [[Bowser]]'s which in the original was used for [[Attack FX P]]. The cricket sound effects were given to the [[Attack FX Y]] badge in the remake along with that badge’s bell sound effect seen in the original, unlike the original however, Hooktail no longer has a weakness to the Attack FX Y badge due to her new fear being frogs instead of crickets.
