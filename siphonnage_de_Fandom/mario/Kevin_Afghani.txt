'''Kevin Zachary Afghani''' (born November 9, 1996) is an American voice actor. He makes the voices of [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Wario]], and [[Waluigi]] following [[Charles Martinet]]'s retirement from voice acting.

==Voices==
*[[Mario]] (2023-present)
*[[Luigi]] (2023-present)
*[[Wario]] (2023-present)
*[[Waluigi]] (2024-present)
*[[Baby Mario]] (2025-present)
*[[Baby Luigi]] (2025-present)
*[[Baby Wario]] (2026-present)
*[[Baby Waluigi]] (2026-present)

==Roles==
* [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]] – [[Mario]] / [[Luigi]] / [[Rift Mario]]
* [[WarioWare: Move It!]] – [[Wario]] (English) / Mario / Luigi
* [[Super Mario Party Jamboree]] – Mario / Luigi / Wario / [[Waluigi]]
* [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]] – Mario / Luigi
* [[Mario Kart World]] – Mario / Luigi / Wario / Waluigi / [[Baby Mario]] / [[Baby Luigi]] / all corresponding variants
* [[Super Mario Party Jamboree: Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV|Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV]] – Mario / Luigi / Wario / Waluigi
* LEGO Super Mario – Wario
* [[Hello, Mario! (game)|Hello, Mario!]] – Mario
* It's Me, Mario! Stop Motion Shorts – Mario
* [[Mario Tennis Fever]] – Mario / Luigi / Wario / Waluigi / Baby Mario / Baby Luigi / [[Baby Wario]] / [[Baby Waluigi]]
