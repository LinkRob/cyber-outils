'''Ghost K''' is a [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It takes place at the [[Luigi's Mansion (baseball stadium)|Luigi's Mansion]] stadium.

==Gameplay==
The object is to throw baseballs at [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] from ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]''. If the ghost matching the player's color is hit, the player gets more points. Also, if the player throws successfully consecutively, a consecutive bonus is awarded. Occasionally, a bigger ghost appears which takes many hits to destroy, getting smaller as it gets hit. All players can hit it, but only the person who defeats it gets points. Ghosts holding stars, or rarely [[Bob-omb]]s, occasionally appear. Whoever hits them gets the item. Stars make the player invulnerable, while bombs stun him or her for a couple seconds.

==Controls==
*Wii Remote with Nunchuk: Tilt {{button|wii|Stick}} to select direction to throw baseball; swing {{button|wii|Wiimote}} to throw baseball.
*Horizontal Wii Remote: {{button|wii|Pad}} to select direction to throw baseball; {{button|wii|2}} to throw baseball
