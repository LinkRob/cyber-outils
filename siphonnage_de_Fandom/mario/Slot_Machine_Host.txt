The '''Slot Machine host''' is a character who first appears in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. She is runs the [[Slot Machine]]s in the game.

== Profile ==
The Slot Machine Host an honorable honest, much like the [[Mystery House Host]] is. She explains how the slots work and stays true to her word.
