'''World 3-1''' is the first level of [[World 3 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 3]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. The level is mainly made up of hills of sand. [[Cheep Cheep]]s that jump out of the water first appear here. This is the only beach-themed level on the main path of World 3 that does not take place underwater. 
==Layout==
The player begins on an orange platform at a beach. A [[? Block]] containing a [[Super Leaf]] can be found here. Floating [[Barrel]]s follow, which sinks when [[Mario]] is on it for too long. A [[Gold Ring]] appears here, which make coins follow behind Cheep Cheeps. More Cheep Cheeps follow, along with several more barrels. A row of [[Brick Block]]s can then be found, one of them containing a [[? Switch]] which makes coins to be released from a green [[Warp Pipe]] above. Another is a multi-coin block. Another hill of sand follows, along with the checkpoint. Rotating platforms and [[Piranha Plant]]s begin to appear. Another Gold Ring can be found on several orange platforms, along with more jumping Cheep Cheeps. After that, two ? Blocks appear, one of them having a [[Super Leaf]]. Following that is another set of rotating platforms with a Piranha Plant on one of them. A [[Roulette Coin Block]] can be found above a pipe with a [[Piranha Plant]] in it. Another pipe to the left of it leads to an underwater area with a Cheep Cheep and a ? Switch that releases coins from yellow Warp Pipes. One last Gold Ring appears, with more Cheep Cheeps along the way. Finally, a green Warp Pipe leads out of the area. In the last area, there are stone platforms and several more Cheep Cheeps which can help Mario reach the top of the [[flagpole]] not far away.
==[[Star Coin]]s== 
*'''Star Coin 1''': This [[Star Coin]] can be seen above three floating Barrels after the first Gold Ring. The player will need to hop off a Cheep Cheep to reach it.
*'''Star Coin 2''': Immediately after the checkpoint, the player should see a Piranha Plant beside several Brick Blocks and a ? Block with a [[Fire Flower]] above the rotating platforms. When the Piranha Plant returns to its pipe, Mario needs to wall kick off the pipe to find a hidden alcove with the Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': When Mario reaches the Coin Roulette Block, he should stand on the barrel on the right of the pipe below it. The barrel will drop and reveal the Star Coin.
