==Effects==
Despite what the description says, eating a Strange Chocolate gives the No Skills status rather than the sluggish status. That means that [[Mario]] cannot use his [[Flip]] ability, [[Princess Peach]] cannot float on her parasol, [[Bowser]] can't breath fire, and [[Luigi]] cannot use his Super Jump ability. This item can be made by giving [[Dyllis]] a [[Mild Cocoa Bean]] and a [[Poison Mushroom|Poison Shroom]]. It is not recommended to consume this item and should only be made to fill the recipes list.
