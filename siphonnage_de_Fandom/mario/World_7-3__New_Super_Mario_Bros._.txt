'''World 7-3''' is a level from [[World 7 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 7]] which appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

== Layout ==
This level takes place on a sky mountain, and revolves around [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] riding across a [[Mega Wiggler]]. The player will have to dodge enemies such as [[Goomba]]s that drop from the air and from [[Warp Pipe]]s, Green [[Koopa Paratroopa]]s, and [[Boomerang Bro]]s. The player will also have to use the Wiggler's jumps to get enough height to reach two of the three [[Star Coin]]s. The player can also find a [[Red Ring]], and [[? Block]]s that contain [[Starman]]s later on, which leave trails of [[Blue Coin]]s to collect.

== [[Star Coin]]s ==
* '''Star Coin 1:''' The first Star Coin is where the first Boomerang Bro is, on a row of [[Brick Block]]s.
* '''Star Coin 2:''' The second Star Coin requires the player to get a good jump when the Mega Wiggler bounces, then bounce of the Green Koopa Paratroopa that is in front of some blocks where the Star Coin is.
* '''Star Coin 3:''' The last is at the end of the level. To get it, Mario or Luigi must be in a form that is not Small or Mini Mario/Luigi. There are a stack of Brick Blocks, and the top one contains a [[Vine]], which must be [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pounded]] on, then the other Brick Blocks must be destroyed. The player just needs to climb down the vine and from below, the Star Coin is in sight, all that there is to do is to jump and collect it. The player can return back by taking the yellow [[Warp Pipe]].
