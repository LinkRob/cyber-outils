'''Shelly''' is an egg from which [[Birdo]] hatches in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a [[Birdo Egg]] with pink spots, based on the design of a [[Yoshi Egg]], and it has since become the basis of a Birdo Egg's design. However, during the battle, red splotches are on Shelly instead.

Shelly is fought during the first part of the boss fight against Birdo in [[Nimbus Castle]]. Shelly does not attack at all, and [[Mario]] and his party merely have to crack Shelly open, by depleting its [[Heart Point|HP]], to reveal Birdo, cuing the main part of the battle.
