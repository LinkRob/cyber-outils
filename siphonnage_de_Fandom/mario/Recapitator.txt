'''Recapitators''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are skeletal enemies with a few spikes on their head. Recapitators are featured in two color variations: gray and greenish.

===Behavior===
Similar to the [[Zombie]]s from other ''[[Wario Land (series)|Wario Land]]'' games, a Recapitator attacks [[Wario]] by throwing its own head, which travels on a straight path toward Wario before returning to the Recapitator like a boomerang. The spikes on a Recapitator's head protect it from Wario's [[stomp]] attack. If a Recapitator falls apart from Wario's [[Dash Attack]], it reassembles itself after a while, much like what [[Dry Bones]] from the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise can do.

The gray Recapitators do not move around, while the greenish ones walk from side to side on a platform and stop moving while trying to attack Wario.

==In ''Wario Land: Shake It!''==
To defeat a Recapitator, Wario must attack it with a Dash Attack or [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] while its head is away from the rest of the body. In doing so, the head passes the body and breaks when it hits a wall. Recapitators are not affected by [[Earthshake Punch]]es.

Both color variations of Recapitators are encountered in the level [[Boogie Mansion]]. In [[Large Fry Cook-Off]], only the greenish variant makes an appearance. Here, while a Recapitator's head is away from its body, it is possible for [[Large Fry]] to destroy the head using his charging attack. Shortly after Large Fry's attack, the body breaks apart on its own while waiting for the head to return.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
A Recapitator's name is a play on "decapitation," the removal of someone's head, but substitutes the "de-" prefix with "re-" to denote a return to a previous state; in other words, a Recapitator's name refers to the act of putting a head back on.
