The '''Skull Crate''' (also called the '''Sliding Crate''', the '''Skeleton Box''', or the '''Slide Box''') is a hazardous [[Crate]] found in ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' and ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]''. It can be found in [[Jolly Roger Bay]] once the Sunken Ship has been made afloat once more, as the Pirate Ship tilts, the Skull Crate will slide causing damage upon contact, while hazardous from the sides, it can be safely stood on if movement is kept to a minimum. Despite the Skull Crate looking breakable, it is purely indestructible and cannot be destroyed. There is only one Skull Crate found in the entire game.

==Trivia==
*It's unknown if the Skull Crate is a living object or just a sliding hazard.
