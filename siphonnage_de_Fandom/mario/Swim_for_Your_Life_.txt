'''Swim for Your Life!''' (also known as '''Superstar Road-3''') is an underwater level in [[Superstar Road]]. It appears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]'' and reappears in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U Deluxe]]''.

== Layout ==
The player starts the level on the ground surface. There is a pipe where the players can enter underwater.

Underwater, a [[Cheep Chomp]] will chase the players. Every [[? Block]] the player passes by contains a [[Fire Flower]] (replaced by a [[Super Mushroom]] if the player is in its [[Mini Mario|mini form]]), allowing them to defeat the Cheep Chomp. Despite defeating the Cheep Chomp, another Cheep Chomp will appear onscreen and continue chasing the player. The player will encounter [[Urchin]]s that can be defeated by the Cheep Chomp. The player can bait the Cheep Chomp to knock out the Urchins. The player can also take out the Urchins in their fire form. After surpassing the wall of Urchins, the player will encounter [[Cheep Cheep]]s. Until they come across a wall of [[Cheep Cheep]]s, the player will encounter [[Spiny Cheep Cheep]]s until the player can exit the pipe.

After exiting underwater, the player will return to the surface and encounter [[Brick Block]]s that allow the player to reach the top of the [[Checkpoint Flag]].

== [[Star Coin]]s ==
* '''Star Coin 1:''' Four Urchins are surrounding the Star Coin.
* '''Star Coin 2:''' An area with Cheep Cheeps and Urchins will have the Star Coin falling from the top of the screen.
* '''Star Coin 3:''' Reach the end of the underwater section, two Urchins are blocking the Star Coin.
