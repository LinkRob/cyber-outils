"'''Bonkers From Yonkers'''" is the seventh live-action segment of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the animated episode "[[The Great Gladiator Gig]]."

==Synopsis==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] is accidentally hit on the head with a pipe, giving him amnesia, so [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]] calls in [[Dr. Sigmund Fruitcake]] for help.
