'''Mario's Star''' is an item in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]].

==Description==
Mario's Star resembles either a [[Power Star]] or a [[Starman]], as it is a five-pointed star with a pair of black eyes.

==In ''Luigi's Mansion''==
{{Quote|Wh-what is this? If it isn't Mario's Star! What otherworldly beauty!|Madame Clairvoya, ''Luigi's Mansion''}}
Mario's Star is one of Mario's five missing items that [[Luigi]] has to find and present to [[Madame Clairvoya]], the other four being [[Mario's Glove]], [[Mario's Cap|Mario's Hat]], [[Mario's Letter]], and [[Mario's Shoe]].

Mario's Star appears in the [[Observatory]]. It is not seen at first, but after Luigi launches a [[Shining Ghost]] into the [[moon]], a solid path made of cosmic dust appears, and Luigi can walk all the way across to obtain Mario's Star on the other side. 

Collecting Mario's Star plays the exact sound effect from ''[[Super Mario 64]]'' for when the player selects a [[mission]].

==Trivia==
*Mario's Star is the only one of Mario's items to not appear in a different room in the Nintendo 3DS [[reissue|remake]]'s [[Hidden Mansion]].
