The '''Master Hall''' is a room in the [[Gloomy Manor]] in the game ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''. It is one of the largest rooms in Gloomy Manor and it leads to different other rooms including the [[Parlor]], [[Bedroom (Gloomy Manor)|Bedroom]], and [[Study (Gloomy Manor)|Study]].

==History==
During [[Gloomy Manor#A-2: Gear Up!|A-2: Gear Up!]], Luigi heads into the Master Hall in order to get into Parlor and Bedroom in order to retrieve the missing gears stolen by the [[Slammer]]s.

During [[Gloomy Manor#A-5: Sticky Situation|A-5: Sticky Situation]], Luigi has to clean up the Master Hall after it has been infested with spider webs. Luigi can light a ball of web on fire and burn down the larger webs that cannot be vacuumed into the [[Poltergust 5000]].
