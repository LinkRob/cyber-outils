'''Door Spirits''' are an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''. They also have a stronger variant named [[Silver Door Spirit]].

==Profile==
===Physical description===
They are brass-colored door knockers with a bulldog face that are attached to a door.

===Behavior===
Door Spirits can shoot bolts of electricity at [[Wario]].

==In ''Wario World''==
Door Spirits only appear in [[Horror Manor]], where they each guard door to another room. Wario can only enter one of these rooms if he defeats the Door Spirit guarding it, and to accomplish this, Wario has to hit the Door Spirit several times.
