A '''Spider Web''' is an object found in some forest levels of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] can climb and hold onto them, especially to move across [[Poisoned Water]] and sometimes to collect a [[Star Coin]] that is otherwise out of reach.
