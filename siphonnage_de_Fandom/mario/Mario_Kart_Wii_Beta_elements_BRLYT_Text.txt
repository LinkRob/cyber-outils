BRLYT files are responsible for menu layouts and have a section in them to place text. In the final game, it uses BMG files for text storage instead, which leaves the BRLYT files with a bunch of old and placeholder text, along with some being untranslated during it's development.

|As the text suggests, this means that the user would've originally not press the A button on the title screen but instead the + button (or START on a GameCube controller).
