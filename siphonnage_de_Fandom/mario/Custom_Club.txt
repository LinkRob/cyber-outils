'''Custom Clubs''' are an item in ''[[Mario Golf: Advance Tour]]''.

==Description==
As the name suggests, Custom Clubs are custom [[golf club]]s made by the Club Maker in the [[Custom Club Shop]]. [[Neil]] or [[Ella]] can have them created by exchanging a [[Custom Ticket]]. There are 15 different types of Custom Clubs, each altering the user's stats by various amounts. It is possible to transfer a Custom Club over to ''[[Mario Golf: Toadstool Tour]]'' using a [[Nintendo GameCube–Game Boy Advance Link Cable]].
