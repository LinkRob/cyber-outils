'''Kat and Ana''' are a recurring pair of kindergarten ninja sisters who appear in the {{series|WarioWare}}. They are among the characters employed by [[Wario]] at [[WarioWare, Inc.]]. Kat and Ana first appear in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', and they have also made crossover appearances in the {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Kat has pink hair with a single ponytail, while Ana has red-orange hair with two ponytails. Kat wears dark purple clothing with a cape of the same color while Ana wears light blue clothing with a cape matching their color.

===Personality===
Ana is timid, while Kat is a bit more bossy and the more dominant of the two. Both of them share a love for nature and animals, and their [[microgame]]s are usually themed as such.

==Biography==
In their manual bio for ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', it is stated that Kat and Ana are descended from the Iga ninja clan. Both of them live in an old-fashioned Japanese-style house in a forest.

Kat and Ana attend [[Diamond City Kindergarten]], separate from [[Diamond City Elementary]], just like how [[Japan]] in the real world separates kindergartens from elementary schools. Although the duo attend a different school than [[9-Volt]], Kat and Ana's diary on the Japanese ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' promotional website reveals them to be good friends with him.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
[[File:Kat and Ana WWMM.png|thumb|left|180px|''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' artwork]]
Kat & Ana host the [[Nature]] set of [[microgame]]s, in which the focus is on plants and animals alike. In the opening cutscene of Kat & Ana's stage, when a skeletal samurai demon named [[Boneheads]] takes over [[Diamond City Castle]]. Ana and her [[Ana's Crew|crew]] of five other ninjas try to stop Boneheads, but are unsuccessful because Boneheads' two lengthy swords. Ana tasks her messenger bird, [[Don the Sparrow|Don]], to send a letter to Kat calling for her assistance. The letter in particular reads "Ninja girl, save me!". Kat and her dog [[Shadow the Dog|Paw]] travel from afar, passing many Japanese landscapes along the way, until finally reaching the Castle.

When Kat enters the Castle, the player plays through the duo's microgame set. Whenever a microgame is completed, regardless of whether the player wins or loses, Kat goes through a sliding door to enter the next room. The chances are represented by four segments on Kat's sword part of the interface, so if the player loses at a microgame four times, it is a [[Game Over]].
[[File:Kat and Ana WWMM Nature ending.png|thumb|Both Kat and Ana with the Diamond City Castle's prince in their stage's ending cutscene in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']] 
In the end scene for Kat and Ana's stage, Kat reaches the top of Diamond City Castle. Paw transforms into a large sword, which Kat uses to slash Boneheads apart into pieces. After Boneheads was slashed, Kat then rescues Ana by cutting the rope that was tying her up. Following this, Kat and Ana go over to the [[Gelateria]], where they sit on the awning.

In the overall final cutscene, Kat and Ana are among the [[WarioWare, Inc.]] employees who are angry at Wario for not receiving any payment.

There is an unlockable minigame titled [[Jump Forever]], in which Kat and Ana, dressed in kimonos, swing a jump rope that Wario has to jump over. They gradually swing the jump rope more quickly.
{{br}}
===''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]''===
Kat and Ana perform in a television show called ''Super Show'' after [[9-Volt]]'s entrance. Under the headline "Karate", they show off their ninja techniques. It is not shown what they do next because Wario's television set bursts.

The duo are two of the ten selectable characters in the multiplayer games. Kat and Ana's minigame is called [[Wobbly Bobbly]], where each player's character tries to balance on a stack of upside-down turtles while playing microgames.

===''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''===
Kat and Ana were given a different microgame set titled [[Tap Out]], which features microgames requiring the player to subtly tilt the system and press the A button.

In the opening for their story, Kat and Ana are on a field trip with their class to [[Diamond Knoll]]. It first shows the teacher instructing Kat, Ana, and the other students to stick with their friend and not wander off. As Kat and Ana explore, Ana stops to look at a flower and quickly catches up with Kat, whom she finds poking a beehive. The hive falls, and a swarm of infuriated bees chase Kat and Ana During this is a short gameplay demo in which the player must repeatedly press A for Kat and Ana to run from the bees, up until they enter a wooden cabin.
[[File:Kat Ana intermission WW Twisted.png|thumb|left|Kat and Ana's appearance in the intermission of their stage in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'']]
In the cabin, Kat and Ana are relieved that they managed to escape the bees, but seconds later, they are ambushed by the [[Diamond Troll]] and go to fight it. During this, the player plays through Kat and Ana's series of microgames. 

In the ending for their story, Kat and Ana each use their sword to generate electricity and inflict their finishing move on the Diamond Troll. While the two are charging their attack, they are interrupted by two of their pets, [[Shuriken the Falcon|Shuriken]] and Shadow, who burst through the cabin to come and rescue them. The pets end up defeating the Diamond Troll when they slam into the ceiling by accident and fall onto the Diamond Troll. Kat says the whole experience was very fun. However, this is followed by Ana saying that they should return to their group, and she is called a "party pooper" by Kat. When the cabin doors open, Kat and Ana find that the same swarm of bees from earlier were waiting for them, and the scene concludes with Kat, Ana, Shuriken, and Shadow being chased by them in the background, during the sunset. Some time later, the four meet up with some of the other characters at [[Club Sugar]], although Shuriken and Shadow are not seen dancing with Kat and Ana inside.

===''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''===
[[File:Kat and Ana WarioWare Touched.png|thumb|x200px|Kat and Ana's reaction shown shortly after their initial anger at Numchuk in ''WarioWare: Touched!'']]
Kat and Ana's microgame set is [[Ninja Scribble]], which require the player to make scribbling movements using the Stylus. In the opening of the duo's story, Ana comes home wanting a snack, only to find that a bunch of bananas on the table are missing. Ana finds footprints leading out of the house, and she is determined to track down the banana thief. Just before Ana exits the house with her dog, Shadow, she leaves behind a note for Kat, which the player can write out with their Stylus. A few seconds after Ana and Shadow begin running into the woods, Kat and Shuriken catch up with them, having seen Ana's note.

As the girls and their pets make their way to the woods, the player plays through their set of microgames. Whenever the player wins a microgame, Kat and Ana are shown celebrating during the intermission, but if the player loses at a microgame, the girls become upset. The [[Game Over]] splash shows Kat and Ana walking happily with their pets. 

In the ending scene of Kat and Ana's story, they find the culprit, a monkey. The duo's initial anger is quickly replaced with endearment, as both Kat and Ana find the monkey very cute. The girls decide to keep the monkey and name him [[Numchuck]] as they cheerfully lift him up and down. Following this, Kat and Ana meet up with the other characters at the [[Hawt House]].

===''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''===
[[File:Kat and Ana dojo rescue WW Smooth Moves.png|thumb|left|200px|Kat and Ana arrive to come rescue the Diamond Dojo in ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'']]
Kat & Ana's stage is titled [[Evil Attacks Diamond Dojo!]]. Here, they have to defend the [[Diamond Dojo]] and the [[Sensei]] from the [[Elephant Ogre]] attacking it, a premise somewhat like that of ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgames$!''{{'}}s. As the girls make their way over to the dojo, Ana uses some sort of ninja magic on herself to transform into a turtle while also producing three duplicates, which represent the chances during the stage.

In the end, Ana discovers a [[Li'l Ogre]] playfully pulling her sensei's hair. Meanwhile, Kat duplicates herself and starts attacking the Elephant Ogre, until the Li'l Ogre yells to end the fighting. The Elephant Ogre's actual intention was to search for the Li'l Ogre. Kat waves goodbye to the Elephant Ogre as he leaves, but she suddenly notices that the actual Li'l Ogre is beside her and that the silhouette on the Elephant Ogre's hand is actually Ana.

Against the sunrise, the Elephant Ogre walks away, child in hand. Kat waves the big guy goodbye, until she notices that the baby is beside her, and that the Oni is actually walking away with Ana (both the baby Oni and Ana have the same silhouette).

In their story, Kat signs for the test which they have to eat the food they are given. They belong to the "Touch League" and their genre is "That's Life".

==In ''[[Wario World]]''==
[[File:Kat Wario World icon.png|frame|left]]
Kat makes a cameo in the form of an icon in [[Treasure Square]], representing the "WarioWare, Inc. selected 4" set in particular, which consists of an indefinitely looping [[Munch a Bunch]], with the number of shapes increasing for each level.

This technically marks the only time when Kat makes an appearance but not Ana, similarly to how another Treasure Square icon shows [[Dribble]] but not [[Spitz]].

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
====''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''====
[[File:Kat and Ana SSBB.jpg|thumb|left|Kat and Ana's appearance in ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'']]
Kat and Ana are featured together as an [[Assist Trophy]], and it is also their first time receiving a three-dimensional model. Upon being released, Kat and Ana soar across the stage and slash at opponents in an X formation using their swords. After doing this a few times, the duo leave. Aside from this, Kat and Ana are also featured together within their own [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]], and there is an individual [[Sticker (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)|Sticker]] of each sister.
{{br}}
====''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]''====
[[File:Kat and Ana SSB4 Wii U.jpg|thumb|200px|Kat and Ana's appearance in ''Super Smash Bros. for Wii U'']]
Kat and Ana reappear as an Assist Trophy in both games. They act the same as before, although they were redesigned after their appearance ''[[Game & Wario]]'', the latest ''WarioWare'' installment at the time of each game's release.

====''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''====
Kat & Ana do not return as an Assist Trophy, although there is a collectible Master-class [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]] featuring both of them.

=== ''[[Tetris 99]]'' ===
From September 17 to September 21, ''Tetris 99'' ran the 24th Maximus Cup which featured a ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]'' theme, where Kat is represented by the "T" tetromino.

When combined, their names form the word {{w|katana}}, a type of Japanese dual blade sword.
