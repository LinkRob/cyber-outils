"'''On Her Majesty's Sewer Service'''" is the twenty-second animated episode of ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]''. It is paired with the live-action segment is "[[9001: A Mario Odyssey]]." 

== Plot ==
[[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|Luigi]], and [[Princess Toadstool (DiC Cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] arrive in [[Spyland]], where Secret Agent [[James Blond]] has caught on to [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]]'s newest plan.  However, Koopfinger (as he calls himself in this episode) uses a ring to turn James Blonde into stone. Later, the trio is having lunch in a restaurant.  While Mario thanks the Princess for treating them to lunch she reveals that she didn't meet them for lunch but to ask for their help in stopping Koopfinger.  She then takes them to a secret facility where they meet with Agent N. Agent N gives them plumber specific spy gear such as a phone wrench, a laser drill, and a drain cleaner bomb.  They also get a spy car that resembles an old plumbers' truck.

Meanwhile, Koopfinger and his henchmen raid [[Fort Hard Knocks]]: the storage area for all of Spyland's coins. They easily manage to pillage a large sum of coins and start to make their getaway. However, the Marios catch up to them in their spy vehicle and give chase. Koopfinger attempts to ward them off with a [[Snifit]] and an [[Albatoss]], but both creatures are defeated by the Marios' spy car. However, a winged [[Birdo]] is called and manages to capture the Marios, spy vehicle and all.

Koopfinger takes the Marios to his secret hideout where he intends to get rid of the Marios. In order to decide their fate, Koopa plays the "[[Wheel of Misfortune]]. They "win" a trip to the Tunnel of Doom where they are confronted by various enemies such as a [[Goomba]], [[Piranha Plant]]s and a [[Spark]]. Using a grapple gun, they manage to escape.

Back on the surface, Koopfinger grows upset over the amount of money he's stolen and wants to go on another caper but the Marios surprise him.  During a fight where even [[Vampa White]] (the woman who spun the Wheel of Misfortune) participates, the Marios manage to fight off Koopfinger's henchmen. The Wheel of Misfortune is spun again with the fate of "Koopa Kaput" but he still manages to escape into a Warp Zone.

With Koopfinger defeated and James Blonde back to normal, the Marios receive commendations for their efforts.  Mario smells food and decides to have lunch, only to find out the food was actually exploding spaghetti.

== Trivia/Goofs ==
* The episode's title is a spoof of the 1969 {{w|James Bond}} film, ''{{w|On Her Majesty's Secret Service (film)|On Her Majesty's Secret Service}}''. It is also a spoof several Bond characters: [[Secret Agent N]] being a parody of {{w|Q (James Bond)|Q}}, the weapons maker; [[Koopfinger]] is a parody of the master villain {{w|Auric Goldfinger|Goldfinger}} Koopfinger's henchmen are parodies of well-known henchmen in the Bond films, such as a sharp iron-teeth Tryclyde is a parody of the mute assassin Jaws; and a mustached Mouser who throws his hat in the fight scene is a parody of {{w|Oddjob}}.
* This is the only episode of ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!'' in which [[Toad (DiC Cartoons)|Toad]] does not appear.
* In the Region 1 DVD release, this episode was relegated as a "bonus" episode (in Disc One) because its accompanied live-action segment was not included due for unknown legal reasons.
* This episode was not included in the 2012 re-releases of Volume 1 & 2 by [[NCircle Entertainment]] for unknown reasons.
* At one point Koopfinger claims he's using a Magic Potion but instead throws a bomb.
* This is the second time a Birdo is shown having the power of flight, the first being in "[[The Bird! The Bird!]]". This time, the wings are located on the creature's back instead of on its arms as in the original.
* Fort Hard Knocks is a spoof on {{w|United States Bullion Depository|Fort Knox}}.
* The first and only animated appearance of [[Porcupo]] and [[Spark]], enemies from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.
* After Mario and Luigi escape the Goomba and Spark from the Tunnel of Doom, the next scene goes back to King Koopa. Koopa Troopa's voice is the same as Triclyde when he first speaks to Koopfinger.
* The plumber's log is the James Bond's number, 007.
* Princess Toadstool has only six lines.
