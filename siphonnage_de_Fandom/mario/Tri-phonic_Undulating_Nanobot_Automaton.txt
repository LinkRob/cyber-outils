'''Tri-phonic Undulating Nanobot Automaton''', often abbreviated '''TUNA''' for short, is an object in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Description==
The TUNA is a machine created by [[Dr. Crygor]] that can make objects or even characters younger.

==In ''WarioWare: Touched!''==
In the opening cutscene of Dr. Crygor's story, [[Slightly Unscrewed]], Dr. Crygor first tests the TUNA out by placing an apple core into it, converting it into an [[apple]]. Dr. Crygor then goes inside the machine, followed by a gameplay demo of the player having to spin the wheel of the TUNA to activate it. The player plays through Dr. Crygor's microgames while the TUNA is upgrading Dr. Crygor, and the TUNA itself appears in the intermission shown between each microgame.
