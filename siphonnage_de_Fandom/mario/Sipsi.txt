'''Sipsi''' is a minor character that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Sipsi lives in [[Yold Town]] and is known as the "town gossp." She is often the first person to know the things that happen in Yold Town. She even gives advice about [[flip]]ping in certain areas. Sipsi can be talked to throughout the game to hear things about other characters.
