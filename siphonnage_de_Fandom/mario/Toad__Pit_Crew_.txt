'''Pit Crew Toads''' are a group of color variants of working [[Toad (species)|toads]] that made their first debut in [[Mario Kart 8|''Mario Kart 8'']] who serve to maintain and repair [[Kart|Karts]].

The colors are based on main characters of the game which include: [[Mario]] (Red), [[Luigi]] (Green), [[Princess Peach|Peach]] (Pink), [[Bowser]] (Yellow), [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] (Light-Blue) and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] (Blue).

== ''Mario Kart 8/Mario Kart 8 Deluxe'' ==
These toads can be spotted as background characters for the public in [[Mario Kart Stadium]].
