The '''Clerk''' is one of the bosses fought in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. He works at [[Smithy's Factory]], and is at the lowest rank of management there.

==Battle==
The Clerk fights [[Mario]]'s party with five [[Mad Mallet]]s. Like the [[Manager]] and the [[Director]], the Clerk's axe has the same symbol as the [[Blade]] airship.
