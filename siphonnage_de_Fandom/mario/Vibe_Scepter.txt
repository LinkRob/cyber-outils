The '''Vibe Scepter''' is an object in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
The Vibe Scepter is able to manipulate and control four emotions: [[Rage]], [[Joy]], [[Gloom]], and [[Calm]]. Princess Peach can use these emotions to turn them into her own powers.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
Bowser learns about [[Vibe Island]] and the Vibe Scepter. Bowser built his villa at Vibe Island. His underlings found the Vibe Scepter. Bowser ordered the [[Koopa Troopa]]s and a [[Goomba]] with the Vibe Scepter to infiltrate [[Princess Peach's Castle]] by his command and use it inside there. The [[Koopa Troop]] then bust in and capture [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Toad]]. Bowser requests the artifact to be given to him by one of his Goombas, when suddenly the Vibe Scepter overloads and changes everyone's vibe in [[Bowser Castle]].
