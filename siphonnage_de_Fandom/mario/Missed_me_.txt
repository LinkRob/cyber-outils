'''Missed me!''' is a move in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
The attack is only used by [[Mukumuku]]. Upon using the attack, Mukumuku burrows underground and reappears in a random spot on the battlefield. It looks around and tries to throw something, although is incapable of doing so. Afterward, Mukumuku returns to its usual spot.

==Trivia==
*Unlike most special attacks, Missed me!'s name is displayed only after being performed.
