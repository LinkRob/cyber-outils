'''Punch-Out!! Pizzeria''' is a location on [[Earth (Illumination)|Earth]] featured in ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie]]''.<ref name="smbplumbing">{{cite web|url=https://www.smbplumbing.co.nz/testimonials/|title=Super Mario Bros. Plumbing|date=2023|accessdate=2023-04-10}}</ref>

== History ==
A commercial for [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] plays there, and [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]] witness it. They also have a confrontation with [[Foreman Spike (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Spike]] there before being called for a job.

A reviewer, named anothercasserole, mentioned they saw [[Mario (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Mario]] and [[Luigi (The Super Mario Bros. Movie)|Luigi]]'s [[Super Mario Bros. Plumbing]] flyers hung up on the Punch Out!! Pizzeria.<ref name="smbplumbing"/>

== Trivia ==
=== References to video games and other media ===
* An arcade cabinet for a game named ''Jump Man'' that resembles [[Donkey Kong (video game)|''Donkey Kong'']], albeit with the main antagonist being a yeti, can be seen in the pizzeria.
* A picture of the Duck Hunt duck can be seen in the pizzeria.
* Various ''Punch-Out!!'' fighters also appear in pictures, such as [[Little Mac]], the protagonist of the series.
**This due to the pizzeria being based on the 1987 video game Punch-Out!! and the series of the same name.
