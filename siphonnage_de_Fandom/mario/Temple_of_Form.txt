The '''Temple of Form''' is a location in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==Description==
The Temple of Form is a temple found southwest of [[Diamond City]]. It is a Japanese-style building where the [[Form Baton]] is located, and a logo of the Form Baton is even displayed on front of the building. The Temple of Form and its surrounding area are owned by [[Joe]], who is the curator of the Temple of Form, which he decided to convert into an exhibit and tourist spot. The Temple of Form is inhabited by the [[Splunk]]s.

==In ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves''==
In Wario's stage, [[Mysterious Form Baton Found!]], Wario inadvertently discovers the Temple of Form after chasing a Splunk there, as the Splunk ran off with Wario's food. In the Temple of Form, Wario discovers the [[Form Baton]], which he takes for himself to make money off of its concept. When Wario takes the Form Baton, a boulder trap is triggered, and the player plays through Wario's [[microgame]] set as he runs from the boulder. In the end, Wario manages to escape.

In [[Orbulon]]'s story, [[The Secret of the Balance Stone]], Orbulon stumbles into the Temple of Form, where he takes the Form Baton and the [[Balance Stone]], the latter of which had fell down from space. When Orbulon does this, the Temple of Form begins to takes flight.

In the ending of [[Tiny Wario]]'s stage, [[Forever Form Baton]], the Splunks eventually catch up to Wario, demanding that he return the Form Baton. They chase Wario back into the Temple of Form, where Wario trips, and the Form Baton lands back to where it was originally found.

The Temple of Form is also featured on the main menu, and it is where the player can either play the [[microgame]]s available, watch the form explanations, and view the [[pose]] cards they have unlocked.
