'''Aerodent''' is the main boss  of the [[Topaz Passage]] in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'' and is one of the four main bosses in which the Player(s) can fight in any order after [[Spoiled Rotten]]. 

== Description ==
Aerodent himself is a grey mouse. He hides in a brown balloon that resembles a bear, with a necklace of blue gems.

==Battle==
[[File:WL4 Aerodent Battle.png|frame|left|Aerodent flipped upside down, exposing the vulnerable Rodent.]]
[[File:WL4 Aerodent Map.png|thumb|300px|Map of the level]]
During his boss fight, Aerodent hides with his balloon, protecting himself from [[Wario]]'s attacks. When fully inflated, this balloon is out of Wario's reach. From this safe vantage, Aerodent drops [[Pin enemy|pin enemies]] in an attempt to harm [[Wario]]. Wario can stun these enemies by jumping on their heads, flipping it upside down so its pin is pointing upwards. Throwing the enemy up at the bottom of the teddy bear will deflate it - it must hit the soft patch in order for a successful hit, as if it is too close to the leg area, the hit will be unsuccessful and Wario must repeat this process again.

From this point, Wario must ram the padded legs (which will flash to show where to hit). After that, the teddy bear will flip, with Aerodent poking his head out of the inflated area. Wario must then use his ram to strike Aerodent and damage him, causing the boss to fall out of his balloon and attempt to scramble back in. If Wario's head hits the inflated bear balloon, it will go up a little. If Wario does not touch Aerodent for a while or the balloon rises high enough, Aerodent will inflate his balloon and go upward again.

Aerodent also takes a large amount of damage from the [[Large Lips]], his HP dropping by 14.

Once Aerodent withstands enough damage, it begins to throw flaming sticks at Wario, setting the ground alight on contact. Wario will turn into [[Flaming Wario]] should he be burned, losing all control and wasting valuable time. After enough hits, Aerodent is defeated and Wario is rewarded with the creature's [[legendary treasure|Necklace]].

The time given to defeat the boss varies on the game mode.

*[[File:WL4-Normal Mode Sprite.png]]: the time given is 4:00.
*[[File:WL4-Hard Mode Sprite.png]]: the time given is 3:00.
*[[File:WL4-S-Hard Mode Sprite.png]] (unlockable): the time given is 2:00.

==''Super Mario-kun''==
Aerodent appears also as a boss in volume 28 of ''[[Super Mario-kun]]'' in the ''Wario Land 4'' arc. It can shoot fire at Wario, transforming Wario into Flaming Wario. It takes heavy damage when the [[shop owner]] uses the [[Large Lips]] move on it, and Wario deals the finishing blow to it.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
His name is a portmanteau of "aero", describing his airborne status, and "rodent".

== Trivia ==

* Aerodent is the only non-first/final boss in ''Wario Land 4'' who's name does not start with the letter '''"C"'''.
* Aerodent is the only boss in ''Wario Land 4'' that does not explode upon being defeat after his health bar is depleted, as he simply gets knocked off the screen, while the other bosses explode upon being defeat after their health bar are depleted.
* Aerodent has the second most Hit-Points out of any boss in ''Wario Land 4'', having sixteen Hit-Points.
