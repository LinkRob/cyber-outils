The '''Black Skull''' is a location in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
The Black Skull is [[Cortez]]'s pirate ship. It is where he rests and stores treasures such as the [[Sapphire Star]]. The ship has only two rooms: the room before the treasure room, where [[Cortez]] keeps all of his provisions, and the treasure room itself, where he is first introduced.

==History==
The Black Skull was permanently grounded long ago and docked deep in [[Pirate's Grotto]] until [[Mario]] and his partners arrive and defeat Cortez. It turns out that the source of the Black Skull's power is the [[Skull Gem]], which [[Flavio]]'s ancestors have stolen from Cortez. Flavio agrees to give Cortez the Skull Gem in exchange for use of his ship, [[S.S. Flavion|his own]] having been destroyed before. Soon after, [[Lord Crump]] attacks with the [[X-Naut ship]] and a second battle commences between the [[X-Naut]]s and Cortez's army of [[Ember]]s.
[[File:PMTTYD ship battle.png|thumb|200px|The Black Skull (right) collides with the [[X-Naut ship]] (left)]]
After the chapter, the Black Skull is docked in [[Rogueport]] and can take Mario to and from [[Keelhaul Key]] at any time; however, [[Unrevisitable area|Mario can no longer go inside]]. This is equivalent to the [[Whale (character)|Whale]] taking Mario to and from [[Lavalava Island]] in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. Despite being considered part of Pirate's Grotto by the [[Magical Map]], the interior of the Black Skull is given a unique location name of '''Pirate Ship''' by the location marker in the remake.
