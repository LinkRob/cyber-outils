|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Ink Blast''' is a special move used only by [[Blooper|Bloobers]] and [[King Calamari]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When Ink Blast is used, a large black ball of ink travels at its victim.

==Effects==
With Ink Blast, the user is able to cause moderate damage to a character.

==Trivia==
*This move is very similar to [[Gunk Ball]] except the attack is a tad lighter in appearance.
