The '''Silver Gearmo''' is a [[Gearmo]] who appears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. He lives in the [[Space Storm Galaxy]]. Much like how the [[Gold Gearmo]] from the [[Boulder Bowl Galaxy]] adores [[Goomba]]s, the Silver Gearmo has an affinity for [[Topman|Topmen]]. [[Mario]] must lure a [[Spiky Topman]] to him. This done, the Silver Gearmo will be pleased about his capture and Mario will obtain a [[Power Star]]. Like the Gold Gearmo, the Silver Gearmo appears on [[Starship Mario]] after clearing his mission.
