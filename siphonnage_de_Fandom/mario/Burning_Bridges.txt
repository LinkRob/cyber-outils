'''Burning Bridges''' is a Survivathon minigame from [[Super Mario Party Jamboree|''Super Mario Party Jamboree'']]. As a Survivathon minigame, it involves [[Bowser]] as an antagonist attempting to eliminate the player. Burning Bridges takes place on three bridges, while Bowser lingers in its background with a decrepit castle wall behind his back.

== Intro ==
The camera focuses on the first player's character before zomming out to show the entire area. [[Bowser]] pulls on a chain that causes [[Fireball|Fireballs]] to fire out of the [[Bowser Statue|Bowser Statues]], and the characters jump over them. The minigame then starts.

== Gameplay ==
To win, characters must jump over the fireballs that the statues shoot out of their mouths. The fireballs vanish upon colliding with each other. Over time, the fireballs increase in speed and their size starts to vary, which causes the window to vary. Whoever survives for the longest time wins the minigame.

== Controls ==
The A button is used to jump.

== Behind the Scenes ==
The name of the minigame comes from "bridge burning," which is an action involving ending a relationship, often unpleasantly.

== Trivia ==

* This is the only Survivathon minigames where Bowser does not interact with the characters during the minigame, directly or indirectly.
