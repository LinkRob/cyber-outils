'''Stamp By Me''' is a Battle [[minigame|mini-game]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
The first character stamps a piece of paper.

==Gameplay==
The objective for each character is use a giant hammer-like stamper to stamp pieces of paper as they pass by on either the left or right conveyor belt, both of which move at different speeds. If a paper is on the right conveyor belt, the player must press {{button|gcn|A}} for their character to stamp there, but if a paper is on the left, they must press {{button|gcn|B}} to stamp there. There are a total of 27 papers to stamp, and whoever stamps the most papers is the winner.

Stamp By Me is the final minigame in [[Decathlon Park]]. The objective is instead to stamp as many papers as possible to earn as many [[point]]s as possible, which can go up to 1,000.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|A}} &ndash; Stamp to the right
* {{button|gcn|B}} &ndash; Stamp to the left

==In-game text==
* '''Rules''' &ndash; ''"Stamp the pieces of paper as they zoom by you on the conveyor belt. Whoever stamps the most correctly wins!"''
* '''Advice''' &ndash; ''"The left and right conveyor belts move at different speeds. Keep this in mind to find the right stamping rhythm."''

==Trivia==
*The mini-game's name is a pun on "{{w|Stand by Me}}," the title of several works.
