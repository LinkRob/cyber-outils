'''5-Volt's House''', also known as '''9-Volt's House''', is the residence of [[5-Volt]] and [[9-Volt]] in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. The most commonly seen area of the house is 9-Volt's bedroom, which consists of various [[Nintendo]] memorabilia. 

== In {{series|WarioWare}}==
=== ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''===
[[File:WWIMM 9-Volt House.png|thumb|left|200px|9-Volt arriving home in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'']]
In the opening for [[9-Volt]]'s stage, 9-Volt leaves [[Diamond City Elementary]] and rides his skateboard back home.
{{br|left}}
=== ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' ===
[[File:9-Volt's_House WWTwisted.png|thumb|200px|The exterior of 9-Volt's House, as it appears during the night in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'']]
9-Volt's House makes an appearance in the opening cutscene, in which 9-Volt invites [[18-Volt]] to his home to play [[Nintendo Entertainment System]].
{{br|left}}
=== ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' ===
[[File:9-Volt's House WWTouched.png|thumb|left|x200px|9-Volt skateboarding away from his house in ''WarioWare: Touched!'']]
9-Volt's House is similarly featured as the setting of 9-Volt's stage, and is where 9-Volt and 18-Volt hang out to play video games.
{{br|left}}
=== ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''===
9-Volt's House appears in the opening of 9-Volt and 18-Volt's stage, [[The Multiplayer Test]]. In the opening, 18-Volt is invited play to 9-Volt's [[Game & Watch]]. However, 18-Volt accidentally breaks the Game & Watch, and 9-Volt screams at him to leave his house. After 18-Volt leaves, he goes to [[Toy Express]] to try and buy a replacement Game & Watch for 9-Volt.

=== ''[[Game & Wario]]''===
5-Volt's House is the main setting of 9-Volt & 18-Volt's minigame [[Gamer]]. 9-Volt's mode takes place inside his bedroom during nighttime, and he has to play video games without being caught by 5-Volt. In 18-Volt's mode, the house can be seen across the street.

=== ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'' ===
[[File:5-Volt's_House WW Gold.png|thumb|The exterior of 5-Volt's House in ''WarioWare Gold'']]
5-Volt's House appears at the start of 5-Volt's cutscenes. 5-Volt changes television channels and becomes bored. She was about to cook 9-Volt's dinner before his arrival until a muscular man appears on the screen. 5-Volt was convinced with [[Mr. Sparkles]]'s bodybuilding exercise and the game starts. Afterwards, 9-Volt arrived to their home. He was excited for his dinner, but he dropped his [[Nintendo 3DS]] that he could not reach, 5-Volt helped him get his Nintendo 3DS that amazed him with her skills.

===''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''===
5-Volt's House is first seen in 9-Volt's stage, [[Nintendo Classics]], in which 9-Volt and his [[Fronk]] pet play the former's [[Epic Augmented Reality Game]] in his bedroom past his bedtime unbeknown to 5-Volt.

Later, 5 Volt's House briefly appears in the intro cutscene for the [[Anything Goes]] level, in which the exterior of [[Wario's House]] briefly turns into 5-Volt's House as a result of the [[Mega Bug]]'s corruption.

The house makes a final appearance during the cast credits, in the background when 9-Volt, 18-Volt, 5-Volt, and [[13-Amp]] are credited.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
The [[Gamer (stage)|Gamer]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'' takes place in 9-Volt's bedroom during the nighttime, based on its appearance in ''Game & Wario''.
