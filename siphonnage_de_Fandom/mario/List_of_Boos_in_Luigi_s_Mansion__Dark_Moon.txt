{{DISPLAYTITLE:List of Boos in ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''}}
{{plagiarism}}
Like the Boos from ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'', the Boos from Dark Moon have names that are usually puns on phrases. However, the way to capture Boos is different in this game as Luigi has to use the Dark-Light Device to stun them, vacuum at their tongues and pull back to damage them, then suck them up in the Poltergeist 5000.
