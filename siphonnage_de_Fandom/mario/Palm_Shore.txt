'''Palm Shore''' is one of four [[Battle Stage]]s in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]''. It is related to [[Cheep Cheep Beach]], having a set of islands. The course has no obstacles except for one palm tree. The tide of the ocean surrounding the course considerably changes every few seconds, rising and falling. The farther the player drives into the water, the deeper it gets, and the character will slow down, making the him/her an easy target. The course's [[Item Box]]es are only in the center of the course.

The Mission 4-2 takes place in Palm Shore. Here, the object is for [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to hit 20 [[crab]]s in two minutes, but he can only use [[Green Shell]]s, [[Triple Green Shells]], and [[Bob-omb]]s.

This stage reappears in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'' as a retro battle stage.
