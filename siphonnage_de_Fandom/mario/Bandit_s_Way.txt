{{Nihongo|'''Bandit's Way'''|どろぼうロード|Dorobō Rōdo}} is a location that appears in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

== Description ==
Bandit's Way is a forest-like area with many enemies and flowers within.

== History ==
[[Mario]] and his new traveling companion [[Mallow]] chase [[Croco]] into Bandit's Way. They soon corner him and demand that he return Mallow's Frog Coin. Croco at first seems to comply before revealing his deception and starting a fight. Mario and Mallow defeat Croco and successfully retrieve Mallow's Frog Coin. The two decide to head back into the [[Mushroom Kingdom]] to complete their errand.
