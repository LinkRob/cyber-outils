'''Twin Swimmers''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare Gold]]''. It is found in the Touch league, being part of that league's That's Life! stage, which is hosted by [[Young Cricket]] and [[Master Mantis]].

== Gameplay ==
Twin Swimmers is similar to [[Perfect Match]] from ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'', but its aesthetic is different, and swimmers replace the cards. During the first phase of the microgame, the swimmers have their top halves shown for 3 seconds (assuming normal speed) and then retreat into the water. One sticks his or her top half out again, while the rest stick their bottom halves out. The player has to find the twin of the swimmer whose top half is sticking out. Selecting the wrong one will cause the correct twin to reveal himself/herself.
