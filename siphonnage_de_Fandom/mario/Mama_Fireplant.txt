'''Mama Fireplant''' is a character that appears in the ''[[Super Mario World (television series)|Super Mario World]]'' television series. She is a large, talking [[Piranha Plant]] who makes her first and only appearance in the episode "[[Fire Sale]]." Mama Fireplant lives on the outskirts of [[Dome City]] in a dome-house across the river with a panel in front that says, "Mama Fireplant the heat is on."

== Appearance ==

== History ==
[[File:Mama Fireplant singing.png|left|thumb|200x200px|Mama Fireplant singing, from "[[Fire Sale]]".]]

During "Fire Sale", Mama Fireplant was kidnapped by [[Wendy O. Koopa|Kootie Pie]] and her [[Red Koopa Troopa (DiC Cartoons)|Koopa Troopa]] henchmen, she screamed for help to which no one, but [[Yoshi (DiC Cartoons)|Yoshi]] heard it. Later, Kootie Pie had planned on using Mama Fireplant to heat-up her palace in [[Ice Land]], she also plans on making a fortune selling-off Mama Fireplant's fireballs to the [[Cave People|cave people]], without even knowing that she will eventually die in Ice Land's frigid climate, causing the entire Dinosaur Land to be flooded. She also has a song to sing during the episode called "[[Kootie Pie Rap]]."

Suddenly, [[Mario (DiC cartoons)|Mario]], [[Luigi (DiC cartoons)|Luigi]], [[Princess Toadstool (DiC cartoons)|Princess Toadstool]] and [[Yoshi (DiC Cartoons)|Yoshi]] arrive and managed to track Mama Fireplant to Kootie Pie's rapidly melting palace. After some trouble involving [[Porcupine Fish]], it was Yoshi who saved Mama Fireplant, using her pot as a sled to slide out of Kootie Pie's palace.

After escaping Ice Land, Mama Fireplant was returned safety to her home in Dome City, where she happily proceeded to throw a barbecue party in Yoshi's honor for her rescue.

== Personality ==
Unlike most [[Piranha Plant (DiC Cartoons)|Piranha Plants]] who are deadly and hunger for food, Mama Fireplant is actually nice. She aids the nearby inhabitants of [[Dome City]] by using her fireballs to give them heat. Despite her temper, Mama Fireplant actually cares for Cave People as she was worried, they could get freeze or worse, die herself in Ice Land if Kootie Pie doesn't send her back home. She even managed to grab the paralyzed Mario, Luigi and Princess Toadstool whereas Yoshi moved her pot as a sled to slide out. 

Leaving Ice Land with Yoshi's bravery made Mama Fireplant praise him, calling "little dino hero honey" for her rescue, awarding him for more barbecue and allow him to let use her own fire anytime.
