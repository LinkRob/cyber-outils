'''House Plant''' is a microgame from ''[[WarioWare: D.I.Y.]]'' It is in [[Orbulon]]'s folder.

== Layout ==
As its name implies, House Plant involves a house being set into the ground.

== Gameplay ==
To win this microgame, the player has to smash the blocks to make room for the house, whose base has four steel blocks dangling from it. If the player destroys an incorrect block or allows a steel block to land on a correct one, they are defeated.

== Behind the Scenes ==
House Plant's name comes from a "house plant," which refers to a plant that one keeps in his or her house.
