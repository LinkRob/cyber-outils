==History==
This Toad is first found in the foyer of Peach's Castle shortly after the [[Paper Toad|Paper Toads]] in the area are rescued, bragging about how lugging his sack around has made him physically stronger. When he notices that he is short one [[Mushroom]], Mario decides to give him a Mushroom of his own. Delivery Toad thanks him by giving him a [[1-Up Mushroom]] in return.

After rescuing all the Paper Toads via the [[Lakitu Info Center]] in the [[Sunbeam Plains]] village, Delivery Toad then becomes the basis of the mission [[Mushroom Foraging]], where [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Paper Mario (character)|Paper Mario]] must pick up all of the Mushrooms he drops three times.

Delivery Toad later visits the Lakitu Info Center location in [[Doop Doop Dunes]] after the requisite Paper Toads in the area are rescued to give Mario and co the [[paper trampoline]], as well as [[Trio Kite]]. He then visits the Lakitu Info Center location in [[Mount Brrr]] after the requisite Paper Toads in the area are rescued to give Mario and co the [[Trio Whirligig]].

As Mario, Luigi, Paper Mario, [[Princess Peach|Peach]], and [[Paper Princess Peach|Paper Peach]] glide down from Bowser's Castle, Delivery Toad is briefly seen pausing his delivery to watch them.

After Mario and co rescue the requisite number of Paper Toads needed to build the staircase to [[Neo Bowser Castle]], Delivery Toad reappears to relay a message from [[Toadette]].

Delivery Toad reappears during the [[Parade|victory parade]] in the credits, as the [[papercrafts]] pass through [[Twinsy Tropics]].
