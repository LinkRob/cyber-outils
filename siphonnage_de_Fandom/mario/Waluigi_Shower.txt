The '''Waluigi Shower''' is one of [[Waluigi]]'s birthday presents in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''.

==Description==
The Waluigi Shower is a portable shower. It is modeled after a [[Piranha Plant]], with a green pole resembling the stalk of a Piranha Plant and a red head with white spots. There are also five teeth on the bottom of the shower head. According to the item description, the Waluigi Shower spits flame, which suggests that it is more closely based on a [[Venus Fire Trap]].

==History==
Waluigi can obtain the item from [[Shy Guy]] by completing [[Shy Guy's Jungle Jam]] in Story Mode.

==Flavor text==
"''This is Waluigi's shower. It has good pressure...and spits flame!!''"
