'''Illusions''' are fake objects/items in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'', that once touched disappear into nothing. They can take the form of a [[Goal Pole|goal pole]], [[1-Up Mushroom]], and other things. 

== In ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' ==
Illusions make their only appearances in [[Shifty Boo Mansion]] of [[World 3 (Super Mario 3D World)|World 3]] and [[Shiftier Boo Mansion]] of [[World Flower (Super Mario 3D World)|World Flower]]. One of them in Shifty Boo Mansion conceals a [[Green Star]].
