'''Mathemortician''' is a 4-Player Mic [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]''.

==Opening==
The four characters stand in a row in a dark, haunted area. The first character walks up to the cannon to take a turn.

==Gameplay==
The characters have to say any of the single-digit numbers into the [[Nintendo GameCube Microphone|Mic]], except "0," to shoot at the [[Red Boo|Pink Boo]]s that float by the nine squares, each depicting a different number that correspond to the numbers that can be said the characters. If the Mic option is off, the numbers are instead selected from a blue window. Regular Boos are worth one [[point]], while those wearing crowns are worth three. Whoever ends with the most points win.

==Ending==
The players stand in a row as winners do their victory animations and losers do their losing animations. If everyone has the same score, everyone celebrates. If nobody has points, they sulk instead.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|mic}}: Say: "1," "2," "3," "4," "5," "6," "7," "8," "9"

==Flavor text==
*'''Rules''': ''Say a number into the Mic to fire at that square. If you hit a Pink Boo, you'll earn points.''
*'''Advice''': ''Pink Boos with crowns are worth 3 points! Hit them at all costs!''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name is a portmanteau of "mathematician," a person experienced in doing math, and "mortician," another word for "undertaker."
