'''Jars''' are a type of object found throughout [[Subcon]] in ''[[Yume Kōjō: Doki Doki Panic]]'' and its Western conversion ''[[Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. They are somewhat like the game's equivalent to [[Warp Pipe]]s, and several of the Jars lead into a room below. More general jars not associated with ''Super Mario Bros. 2'' have also appeared, such as in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]'' and ''[[Mario Pinball Land]]''.
