'''Discruptor''' is [[Rabbid Luigi]]'s default weapon in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]''.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Sparks of Hope]]'' ==
The Triple-Troll appears in ''Sparks of Hope'' as Rabbid Luigi's weapon. The player can change the skins of The Dukes. Rabbid Luigi will be unlocked once Rabbid Peach is saved from the Darkmess Manta, in order to unlock the Discruptor since Rabbid Luigi owns this weapon.

== Statistics ==
Luigi can attack up to three enemies with the Discruptor.
