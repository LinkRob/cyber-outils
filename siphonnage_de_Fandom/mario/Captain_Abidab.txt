'''Captain Abidab''' is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[20,000 Koopas Under the Sea]]." She is a sailor who lives in [[Happy Harbor]], being the only [[human]] amongst several [[Toad (species)|Toads]] to live there.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
Her name is derived from Captain Ahab, the whale-hunting sea captain from the novel ''{{w|Moby-Dick|Moby Dick}}''.
