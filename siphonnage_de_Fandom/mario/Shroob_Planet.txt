The '''Shroob Planet '''is the home planet of the [[Shroob]]s. It is only seen in the intro of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Partners in Time]]'', where the Shroobs spot the far away [[Mushroom Earth]] from their telescope.

The populace of the planet is likely deceased, as it is stated in the game that the planet was withering.

== Trivia ==
*The Shroob Planet is the only area [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[Baby Mario]] and [[Baby Luigi]] never went to visit in the game.
