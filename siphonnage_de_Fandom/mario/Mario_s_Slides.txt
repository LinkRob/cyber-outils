'''Mario's Slides''' is one of [[Mario]]'s minigames in ''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' and a Variety minigame in ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''.

== Layout ==
In ''Super Mario 64 DS'', the minigame takes place in-between two cliffs with a river a night, while in ''New Super Mario Bros.'', this was changed to an underground section with brown bricks, and sewage pipes on either end.

== Gameplay ==
In this minigame, a Mario head will be moving automatically from the top of the screen towards the bottom of the stage. There are lines present on the screen which [[Mario]] will move across, but the player will use the [[Nintendo DS]] stylus to draw more lines on the screen to direct Mario towards the [[Power Star]], and away from [[Piranha Plant]]s. The player can also increase Mario's speed of movement by tapping the downward arrows on the sides of the Touch Screen. If Mario lands in a Piranha Plant, the game will automatically end. Once the player collects five Stars, they clear a stage, and the screen resets to a new stage, with later stages having different ways to increase the difficulty.

In ''New Super Mario Bros.'', there is a multiplayer variation titled [[Vs. Mario's Slides]]. It is a 1-on-1 minigame, so it only supports two players. It plays mostly like the single-player version, but Mario will head back and forth between the two players.
