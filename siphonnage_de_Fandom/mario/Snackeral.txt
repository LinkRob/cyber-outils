A '''Snackeral''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario: Master of Disguise]]''.

==In ''Wario: Master of Disguise''==
These robot fish appear in [[Sweatmore Peak]], where they try to steal [[Wario]]'s [[Sweatmore Hotpants]] and run away. If Wario is in the lava without Hotpants, he will take damage unless he gets out of the lava or gets a game over. This enemy should be avoided or destroyed quickly. Since Snackerals can only be found within the lava, the only way to take them out is with [[Captain Wario (Wario: Master of Disguise)|Captain Wario]]'s missiles.
