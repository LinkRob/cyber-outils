}}'''Brolder Blockade is Back''' is a mini-boss level in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]''. The level's location is in [[World Castle]].

==Features==
The arena is similar to [[Lava Rock Lair]], and the enemies include [[Boss Brolder]], [[Splorch]]es, and other Brolders. Completion of this level unlocks [[Trick Trap Tower]].
== Layout ==
The stage is just a circular rocky platform surrounded by molten lava. It has three [[Brolder]] along with one Giant Brolder. After defeating all the enemies, collecting the Green Star completes the stage.

==Green Star==
*'''Green Star 1''': Defeat Boss Brolder to get the Star.
