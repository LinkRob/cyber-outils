|members = [[Flint Cragley]]}}'''Cragnons''' are strange, caveman-like creatures that appear in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Biography==
Cragnons live in the [[Land of the Cragnons]] where they worship a being known as "Big Rock Who Watches." They speak in their own unique language often adding the word "crag" to the beginning or end of words. Despite being this game's version of cavemen, they use several types of advanced technology such as televisions.
==History==
The Cragnons play a major role in Chapter 5. After [[Mario]], [[Princess Peach]], and [[Bowser]] end up in the Land of the Cragnons, they soon learn the Cragnons are being taken away by the [[Floro Sapien]]s. After speaking with the mayor, [[Marbald]], the heroes journey off to save them knowning a [[Pure Heart]] is with the leader.
