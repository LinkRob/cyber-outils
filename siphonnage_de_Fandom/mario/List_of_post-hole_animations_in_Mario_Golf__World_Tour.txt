== Trivia ==
*In Yoshi's Double Bogey animation, he makes his tired sound twice. Once when he hid in his egg and again after it broke.
*Despite having 16 Boos shown in Boo's Eagle animation, only 7 of them made a sound when combining with him.
*Toad's par animation is his only one that does not feature any other Toads.
*Rosalina's Bogey animation reveals her right eye.
