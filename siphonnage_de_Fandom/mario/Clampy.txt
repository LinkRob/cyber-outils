A '''Clampy''' is a variant of [[Clam]] and a recurring enemy species in the {{franchise|Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''.

==In ''[[New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]''==
They appear in [[World 1-4 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 1-4]] and a secret area in [[World 4-1 (New Super Mario Bros. Wii)|World 4-1]]. They trap characters in their mouths, giving them damage, and then releasing them. Most of them have [[coin]]s in their mouths as compared to ones that have [[1-Up Mushroom]]s to attract players to them. Clampies are indestructible and cannot be destroyed.

==In ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''==
Clampies can be seen in [[Cheep Cheep Lagoon]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. Similar to ''New Super Mario Bros. Wii'', Clampies have items in them to lure players toward them, such as [[Item Box]]es.

==In ''[[Mario Party 10]]''==
Clampies appear in the background of the board [[Whimsical Waters]]. Clampies also appear in the minigame [[Cheep Cheep Check]], where the solo character uses them to disperse large clusters of bubbles to try and distract the other characters from counting the [[Cheep Cheep]]s.
