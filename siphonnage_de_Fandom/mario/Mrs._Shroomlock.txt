'''Mrs. Shroomlock''' is a housewife married to [[Shroomlock]], whom she lives at [[Shroomlock House]] with in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Mrs. Shroomlock is a female [[Toad (species)|Toad]] with a white mushroom cap bearing magenta round spots. Her facial structure is narrower than ordinary Toads, and she wears plum lipstick around her mouth. She has plum purple afro hair in the rear of her head. Her attire features a purple dress in form of an apron that has a dark grape purple frill below the skirt area and a single pocket in pink color in the center of the dress. She is also equipped with a pure white blouse with short sleeves. Her legs are cloaked in white tights, and the footwear consists of purple pajama slippers.

===Personality===
One of Mrs. Shroomlock's main characteristics is gossiping, which can provide the player with hints if they visit her throughout various quests. The ending of ''Mario Party Advance'' states that she became addicted to infomercials.
