'''Ocean Small Turtles''' are an object in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Ocean Small Turtles are a type of platform bearing the design of a [[Koopa Troopa]]. They have a shell with a yellow rim and green sexagon panels on their back, a red head, and red appendages. The appendages on the Ocean Small Turtles cannot be seen until Mario uproots them by flooding the planet.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy''==
There are two Ocean Small Turtles, which can both be found at the [[Dreadnought Galaxy]]. At first, they are partially buried underground, but after [[Mario]] floods the planet by luring a [[Bullet Bill]] to break a vault, the Ocean Small Turtles emerge and start to move across the surface of the water. Mario can ride them to return to the main body of the planet.
