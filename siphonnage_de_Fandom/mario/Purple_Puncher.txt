'''Purple Punchers''', also known as '''Pink Mapu'''<ref name=Versus>Loe, Casey. Versus Books ''Luigi's Mansion'' Perfect Guide. Page 16.</ref> or '''bashers''',<ref name=Versus/> are a type of [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|Ghost]] featured in ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Purple Punchers are pink ghosts with yellow eyes and a small mouth. Despite their name, Purple Punchers have no purple attributes, similar to how [[Gold Ghost]]s are orange instead of golden. Purple Punchers are sometimes depicted a different color, such as in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'', in which they are light green.

===Behavior===
A Purple tries to attack [[Luigi]] by diving forward and charging a punch into him. If successful, Luigi loses ten [[Heart Point|HP]], and the Purple Puncher laughs before vanishing and slowly reappearing afterward.

==History==
===''Luigi's Mansion''===
[[File:Anteroom LM3DS.png|thumb|left|200px|''Luigi's Mansion'' (Nintendo 3DS) screenshot of a Purple Puncher and Gold Ghost in the Anteroom]]
In ''Luigi's Mansion'', Purple Punchers are the second type of normal ghost that [[Luigi]] encounters. As the name implies, these ghosts try to damage Luigi by giving a left or right hook to him; ten HP is lost for each successful uppercut punch. Luigi can stun Purple Punchers with his [[flashlight]], and then suck them in with his [[Poltergust 3000]]. They can also be hurt by any of the three elements.
[[File:Purple Puncher vacuuming pre-release screenshot.jpg|thumb|Early ''Luigi's Mansion'' screenshot of a Purple Puncher and a Gold Ghost being vacuumed, the former having 29 HP]]
In an [[list of Luigi's Mansion pre-release and unused content|earlier version of the game]], Purple Punchers had 30 HP each. However, this was changed to 20 in the final version for unknown reasons. This pre-release screenshot can be seen on page 19 of the ''Luigi's Mansion'' instruction booklet.

===''Mario Power Tennis''===
In ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'', Purple Punchers, along with several other ghosts, appear in the [[Luigi's Mansion Court]]. They also appear in [[Terror Tennis]], in which Purple Punchers are among the ghosts that the player's character has to keep contained in the portraits.

===''Mario Hoops 3-on-3''===
In ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'', light-green Purple Punchers are among the ghosts that appear in [[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|Luigi's Mansion]]. They they sometimes catch the ball, forcing the characters to try and retrieve it.

===''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2''===
[[File:Purple Puncher art blue MKAGP2.png|thumb|''Mario Kart Arcade GP 2'' sprite of a blue Purple Puncher art from Yoshi Park 2]]
In ''[[Mario Kart Arcade GP 2]]'', a blue and purple-colored Purple Puncher appears as of the ghost artworks in the Horror House section of [[Yoshi Park 2]].

===''Mario Sports Mix''===
Purple Punchers appear again in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]'' as spectators to the many sporting events. They also can be assistants to the players in the [[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Sports Mix)|Luigi's Mansion]] stage in the basketball sport.

==Crossover appearances==
===''Super Smash Bros.'' series===
The Purple Puncher, along with the [[Gold Ghost]] and the [[Blue Twirler]], are seen on a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros. series)|trophy]] that can be unlocked at random in both ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U]]''.
