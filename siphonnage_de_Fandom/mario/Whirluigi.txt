'''Whirluigi''' is the [[Offensive Power Shot]] used by [[Waluigi]] in both ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]'' and ''[[Mario Tennis: Power Tour]]''.

==Description==
Waluigi performs his signature Γ shape, then spins around very fast to create a cyclone around him, and then hits the ball with the cyclone. As a result, the ball greatly curves over the net, knocks the opponent back a few feet, and causes them to spin around very fast, temporarily hindering their movement.

==Etymology==
The name is a pun on "whirl," an action perform within the move, and Waluigi, who performs the move.
