'''Pyoro''' is a recurring character in the ''[[WarioWare (series)|WarioWare]]'' series. He first appears in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''. Pyoro also stars in his own [[DSiWare]] game, ''[[Bird & Beans]]''.

==Profile==
Most ''WarioWare'' games feature Pyoro as the star of his own unlockable [[minigame]], most of which can be unlocked by playing every [[microgame]] at least once. In [[Diamond City]] itself, Pyoro is a popular video game character.

===Physical description===
Pyoro is a red bird with a long tongue, which he uses to grab [[Bean (Pyoro)|Beans]], much like [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshis]] do to grab [[berry|berries]] and other items. Pyoro's name and design was inspired from {{w|Kyorochan}}, a bird who is the mascot of the {{w|Morinaga & Company|Morinaga}} cholocate "Choco-Ball."

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
[[File:Pyoro game case WWIMM.png|thumb|left|200px|Pyoro, as depicted on the fictional Game Boy Advance game starring himself]]
In the opening, Pyoro is seen on the box art for a fictional [[Game Boy Advance]] game starring Pyoro. Seeing the game's success drives [[Wario]] to form his company, [[WarioWare, Inc.]],

In the [[Pyoro (minigame)|Pyoro]] minigame, the player controls Pyoro as he snags falling vegetables with his tongue to prevent from destroying the floor. Pyoro's tongue shoots out diagonally, so he must stand a small distance away from the vegetable to catch it. If there is no more ground left or if Pyoro gets hit on the head, this results in a [[Game Over]]. There is another minigame titled [[Pyoro 2]], in which a yellow Pyoro has to spit seeds at the falling vegetables.
{{br|left}}

===''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''===
[[File:PYORO R.png|thumb|200px|Pyoro in the minigame PYORO R in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'']]
Pyoro stars in another unlockable minigame, [[PYORO R]], in which he has to defend flowers from incoming enemies. Unlike the previous game, Pyoro does not extend his tongue, but rather does so with his beak. Aside from this, Pyoro is also in charge of the Prize Machine, and whenever the player unlocks a [[souvenir]], a scene of Pyoro carrying it onscreen is shown.

===''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''===
Pyoro stars in [[Pyoro T]]. Like in ''WarioWare: Twisted!''{{'}}s PYORO R, the object is for Pyoro to once again defend the flowers in the garden from enemies, but Pyoro can now use his tongue to attack again, like in ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''{{'}}s Pyoro minigame.

Aside from appearing in minigames, Pyoro is also seen in the Options menu.

===''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''===
Pyoro stars within the minigame [[Pyoro S]]. Pyoro now has the ability to fly using his short, stubby wings. He flies through the air while spearing insects with his beak, and Pyoro has to avoid being knocked out of the air by them. Pyoro sometimes has to fight a spine-shooting, six-eyed circle monster. Pyoro can also eat [[apple]]s to earn [[point]]s and receive a power-up, which differs depending on the color of the apple: a blue apple extends Pyoro's tongue to become longer; an orange apple summons two small Pyoros to fly in formation and attack enemies; and a red apple gives Pyoro a flower barrier that can absorb bugs and make Pyoro grow larger temporarily.

===''[[Game & Wario]]''===
Pyoro stars in another minigame of his own, [[Bird (minigame)|Bird]].
{{sectstub}}

===''[[WarioWare Gold]]''===
Pyoro returns in the Pyoro minigame from ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' and stars in another minigame of his own, [[Super Pyoro]].
{{sectstub}}

===''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''===
Pyoro is an unlockable playable character. He plays a larger role than in the other ''WarioWare'' games. Pyoro is the final character to join [[Wario]] and the others on their journey.
{{sectstub}}

==In ''[[Bird & Beans]]''==
Pyoro is the starring character, and he appears in the remakes for both the Pyoro and Pyoro 2 minigames from ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
Pyoro has made a few cameo appearances within the series, including within the background of the [[Gamer (stage)|Gamer]] stage and as a [[Spirit (Super Smash Bros. Ultimate)|Spirit]] in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''.
{{sectstub}}

== Trivia ==

* Pyoro is similar to [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in many ways: 
* They are both based on animals.
* They both have long tongues they use to eat their favorite foods.
* Their favorite foods both come from plants.
