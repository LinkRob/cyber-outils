'''Mew''' is one of the first 151 [[Pokémon]] from the ''[[w:c:pokemon:Pokémon (franchise)|Pokémon]]'' franchise. It has made various appearances alongside [[Mario]] and other [[Nintendo]] characters in the crossover {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}, beginning with the first ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'', but Mew's first {{franchise|Mario}} appearance is in ''[[Mario Artist: Paint Studio]]'', where it cameos as a character stamp alongside the other 150 [[w:c:pokemon:Generation I|Generation I]] Pokémon.

== In {{series|Super Smash Bros.}}==
Mew has a rare chance of appearance from a [[Poké Ball]] upon throwing it.
