'''Eggplant Warriors''' are enemies in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical descriptions===
Eggplant Warriors are eggplant-like creatures, similarly to [[Spoiled Rotten]], except Eggplant Warriors are smaller. They are purple, have green feet, and wear headbands with knives attached to them.

===Behavior===
Eggplant Warriors behave identically to [[Totsumen]] in that they charge at [[Wario]] a few seconds after noticing him.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
Eggplant Warriors are encountered only during the boss fight against Spoiled Rotten.
