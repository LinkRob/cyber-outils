'''Garreg Mach Monastery''' is a stage that appears in the ''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' series. Its first and only appearance is in [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]], in which it appears in the 5th DLC pack along with [[Byleth]]. It is from the ''Fire Emblem'' universe.
