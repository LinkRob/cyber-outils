'''Banandium Gems''' (more commonly referred to as '''Bananas''') are the main collectibles in ''[[Donkey Kong Bananza]]''. They appear in all colors. For example, the 5-in-a-bunch variant is colored reddish orange. 

== Usage ==
Banandium Gems gives Skill Points, which can be spent on upgrades for [[Donkey Kong]] or his [[Bananzas]]. 

== Purchase ==
Donkey Kong can purchase a Banandium Gem from [[Chiptone]] at the [[Chip Exchange]] for 100 [[Banandium Chips]]. 

Donkey Kong can also purchase a [[Banandium Gem Map]] that reveals the location of a random Banandium Gem from [[Stufftone]] at the [[Stuff Shop]] for 1,000 [[Banandium Gold]].
