The '''Strudel Continent''' is a location that is only mentioned in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Strudel Continent is an area of the [[Waffle Kingdom]] that [[Luigi]] visited during his journey. [[Princess Eclair]] is known for living there and is one of two known continents of the Waffle Kingdom with the other being the [[Pudding Continent]].
