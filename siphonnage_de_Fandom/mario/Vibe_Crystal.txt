The '''Vibe Crystal''' (also called a '''Gem''' or '''Crystal''') is a collectible item in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

== Description ==
The Vibe Crystal is a blue gem. It strongly resembles the crystal worn on [[Peach]]'s dress. Princess Peach can collect the gem to refill the [[Vibe Gauge]]. Small ones fill a 40% of her meter while larger Vibe Crystals fill 80% of her Vibe Meter.
