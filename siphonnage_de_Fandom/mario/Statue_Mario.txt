'''Statue Mario''' is a form that [[Mario]] takes on upon acquiring a [[Statue Leaf]] or from using a [[Tanooki Suit]]. The form first appears in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Bros. 3'' / ''Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3''===
When the player gets the [[Tanooki Suit]] and turns into [[Tanooki Mario]], [[Mario]] turns into a statue for a short time before he turns back to Tanooki Mario. In the original ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]'', this form is excluive to Mario, whereas in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', [[Luigi]] can use it as well.

===''Super Mario 3D Land''===
It also appeared in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. Both Mario and Luigi can attain this form, but the player needs to get a [[Statue Leaf]]. Mario or Luigi can turn into a statue if the player presses {{button|3ds|l}} or {{button|3ds|r}}, while jumping in a similar fashion to do a [[Ground Pound]]. While in this form, Mario/Luigi will be invincible to most obstacles and will be able to defeat enemies that cannot normally be defeated if the player slams down on them from above. Also, Mario will be able to fool enemies into thinking he isn't actually there by turning into a statue to avoid damage.
