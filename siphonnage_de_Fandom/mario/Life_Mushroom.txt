'''Life Mushrooms''' are items which appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''.

==Description==
They have a red cap depicting a yellow star. A Life Mushroom provides [[Mario]] with three extra [[Health Point|life bars]] but if his health drops below four, they will disappear and his health meter will return to normal. Mario does not gain extra health if he is already affected by a Life Mushroom. Upon grabbing a second one, his health will restore to 6. In ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'', if Mario grabs a Life Mushroom while he has six health points, he earns a [[1-Up]].

A Life Mushroom is usually found before a boss battle, or in challenging levels. They can also be purchased from [[Lumalee]] for thirty [[Star Bit]]s.
