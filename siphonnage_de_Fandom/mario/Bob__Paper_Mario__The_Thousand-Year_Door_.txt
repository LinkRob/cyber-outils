'''Bob''' is a character that is only mentioned in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
Bob is only mentioned by his wife [[Maude and Flo|Maude]] in her conversation with Flo. This conversation can only be heard after [[Doopliss]] has been defeated. Being married to Maude who is a crow, it is certain that Bob is a crow as well.
