==Features==
{{sectstub}}

==Green Stars==
*'''Green Star 1:''' There is a wall constructed of destroyable blocks, and there is a POW block on top of it. Once you reach this, you throw a Bob-omb at it to destroy it. Alternatively, if you are playing on a Wii U GamePad, tap the POW block on your GamePad screen to detonate it.
*'''Green Star 2''': On one of the spinning platforms, there is a bunny. Chase the bunny and collect the star once you catch it.
*'''Green Star 3''': Just after the stamp, jump over the gap to the cloud canon. After you are launched complete the cloud board to collect the last star.

==Stamp==
[[File:Bob-ombs_Below_Stamp.png|thumb]]
Right between the second and third stars, there is a group of Goombas carrying bombs. Throw the bombs at a crack in the wall, and follow the sign to get the stamp.
