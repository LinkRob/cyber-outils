'''World 6-5''' is the fifth level in [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] of ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. 

==Layout==
[[Mario]] begins the level on a rocky platform nearby a [[Bowser Statue]]. A [[Seesaw]] can then be found, along with two [[Koopa Troopa]]s and [[? Block]]s. After another rocky platform, Mario can find a second Seesaw that has [[Spiked Ball]] on it. Mario then arrives at some more rocky platforms, along with more Bowser sculptures. A Seesaw with two Spiked Balls on it is encountered. The [[Checkpoint Flag]] can then be found, along with three ? Blocks and a Bowser sculpture. Two more Seesaws can be found, one with a Spike Ball on it. Another rocky platform with some blocks, a Spiked Ball, and a Bowser sculpture can be found, along with two Seesaws. Four Bowser sculptures can then be found, along with the [[Goal Pole]].

==Star Coins==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' The first star coin is located in a secret area. The player can access this area with the red pipe directly above the Seesaw with two [[Spiked Ball]]s on it.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' After the checkpoint, the second star coin is buried underneath [[Empty Blocks]]. [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] must tilt a Seesaw to lure a spiked ball to destroy the blocks.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Very close to the [[Goal Pole]], the third star coin is on the higher Seesaw between two spiked balls.
