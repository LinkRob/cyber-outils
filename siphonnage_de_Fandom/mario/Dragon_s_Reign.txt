'''Dragon's Reign''' is one of [[Dribble and Spitz]]'s [[microgame]]s, featured under the [[Fantasy]] folder, in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player's character has to defeat the dragon and retrieve the crown it drops. In two-player mode, whoever retrieves the crown receives an effort point.

The character listed as most suited for the microgame are [[Ashley]], [[Penny Crygor|Penny]], and [[Orbulon]]. Although Orbulon's [[Oinker]] cannot hit the dragon, it can attract the crown without havign to defeat the dragon. While Orbulon's tractor beam cannot hit the dragon, it can attract the crown without having to defeat the dragon.

===Difficulty levels===
*'''Level 1:''' The dragon is large and green. In single-player, it does not move, but in two-player mode the dragon moves slightly.
*'''Level 2:''' The dragon is smaller and yellow. In single-player, the dragon moves in slight patterns, but in two-player mode, it moves faster than the green dragon.
*'''Level 3:''' The dragon is even smaller and blue. In both modes, it moves around in erratic patterns.

==Mission==
*'''Mint-Condition Crown''': A single-player mission in which the player has to use all crew members to clear 18 consecutive rounds when replaying the microgame from Play-o-Pedia without letting the crown touch the ground.
