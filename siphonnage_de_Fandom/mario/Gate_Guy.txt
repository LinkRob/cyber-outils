'''Gate Guy''' is one of the Duel Boards in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Description==
Gate Guy is a small [[board]] named for the two [[Gate Guy (species)|Gate Guy]]s on the northern and southern parts of the board. The Blue Player Space is on the top left corner, whilst the Red Player Space is on the bottom right corner.

When a Gate Guy is approached, it always stops players to ask if they want to go through the path it blocks for 5 [[coin]]s. Paying the fee lets them cross the center of the board as a shortcut to their opponent or to the start spaces.

In [[Story Mode]], the player has to duel [[Wario]] (or [[Luigi]] if the player is Wario) on this board for the Wit [[Star Stamp]].
