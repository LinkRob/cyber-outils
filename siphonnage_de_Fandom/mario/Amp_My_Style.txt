'''Amp My Style''' ('''Beat the Buzz''' in British English) is a General minigame featured in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''. It takes place on an octagonal platform within a factory.

==Gameplay==
The objective is simply to avoid being touched by the electric currents linked between eight [[Amp]]s, the main feature and obstacle of the minigame, for 60 seconds. Amps form the electrical links as they move around. Any character who touches an Amp is electrocuted and loses the minigame, and any one who either survives to the end or whoever is the last one standing is ranked the winner.

*{{button|3ds|stick}} - Move
*{{button|3ds|A}} - Jump
