'''Big Frame Boos''' are an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Big Frame Boos are a variant of [[Frame Boo]]s, which they share a base appearance with, except they have a larger body.

===Behavior===
Big Frame Boos have the same behavior as a regular Frame Boo.

==In ''Yoshi's Woolly World''==
Like with Frame Boos, a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can stun a Big Frame Boo by using a [[Yarn Ball]] to turn it into a [[Boo]]-like [[balloon]], although a Big Frame Boo breaks free from it after a few seconds.
