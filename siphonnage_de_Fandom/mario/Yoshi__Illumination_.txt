'''Yoshi''' is a character that appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Movie (film series)|Super Mario Bros.]]'' film series and its spin-offs produced by [[Illumination]]. He is one of the film's two deuteragonists (alongside [[Luigi (Illumination)|Luigi]]) of ''[[The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]''.

== Profile ==

=== Physical description ===
Yoshi is a cute green dinosaur that helps his friend [[Mario (Illumination)|Mario]]. He has mainly green skin, but his stomach, chest, mouth, and underbelly are white. He has two big blue eyes, round cheeks, and a big round nose. On his nape, he has three red spikes. On his back has a red saddle or shell that can be used for riding him, and he has orange shoes on his feet with yellow soles. He has a long sticky tongue that can be used for swallowing food or enemies.

== Behind the scenes ==

=== Concept and creation ===
Yoshi was originally planned to physically appear in the movie having a major role but never made it in the final cut. == Personality ==
Yoshi is a friendly and cheerful dinosaur who loves to eat and be with friends...

== History ==

=== Film series ===

==== ''The Super Mario Bros. Movie'' ====
Yoshi appears as a green egg brought by a [[Shy Guy (Illumination)|Shy Guy]] as a gift for [[Bowser (Illumination)|Bowser]] and [[Princess Peach (Illumination)|Princess Peach]] during their wedding. After the [[Bomber Bill]] explosion due to [[Tanooki Mario]], sucking Bowser's flying fortress and everyone in the pipe, the green egg was transported on the underground sewers of [[Brooklyn (Illumination)|Brooklyn]]. In the post-credit scene, the egg begins to hatch as he screen cuts to black and Yoshi saying his name can be heard.

==== ''The Super Mario Galaxy Movie'' ====
As Mario and Luigi investigated a troubled [[Mysterious Pipe (Illumination)|Pipe]] in a pyramid inside the [[Sand Kingdom (Illumination)|Sand Kingdom]], they were frightened by Yoshi who just hatched, but the brothers saw that he was frightened from them too. Calming down, they befriended and promised not to hurt him. Back at the [[Mushroom Kingdom (Illumination)|Mushroom Kingdom]], Mario and Luigi loved how cute Yoshi's face  is, even though they couldn't resist it, but Toad had no idea Yoshi became part of the group, and was kinda jealous.
