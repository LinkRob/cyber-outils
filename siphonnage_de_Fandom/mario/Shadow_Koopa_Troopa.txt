The '''Shadow Koopa Troopa''' is a monster that can be encountered as an enemy in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They appear in [[Beware of the Rifts]], the fourth stage of the [[Fungi Mines]]. Shadow Koopa Troopas are called "NokonokoDark" in the game files.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
As their name implies, Shadow Koopa Troopas are shadow-infused Koopa Troopas with black bodies and red eyes. Their texture is identical to the [[Shadow Rift (Super Mario Bros. Wonder)|Shadow Rifts]] in the level [[Beware of the Rifts]], with what appears to be white stars. [[Rift Mario]] also has a similar texture
{{TBA}}

===Behavior===
Shadow Koopa Troopas are identical in their behaviour to regular Koopa Troopas; however, [[Mario (character)|Mario]] cannot defeat them normally, as contact with their body deals damage. However, their shadowy nature does not protect against his [[Fire Mario|Fire suit]]'s [[Fireball|Fireballs]]. Shadow Koopa Troopas are also instantly destroyed upon collecting the [[Wonder Seed]] in the level's [[Wonder Effect]].
{{TBA}}

==In game==
Shadow Koopa Troopas make their only appearance in the level Beware of the Rifts as [[Rift Mario]]'s minions; here, they are summoned when Mario grabs a [[Wonder Flower (item)|Wonder Flower]], resulting in five medals with Wonder Flower images popping up. When Mario collects the Wonder Seed, the Shadow Koopa Troopas will be destroyed instantly and Rift Mario will flee.

==Trivia==

* Shadow Koopa Troopas were originally going to wear glasses according to unused game files; these glasses do not appear in the final game.

==Nomenclature==
In Japanese, Shadow Koopa Troopas are called ''Kage Nokonoko'', which means "Shadow Koopa Troopa." Their file name is "NokonokoDark," with "nokonoko" being the Japanese name for the [[Koopa Troopa]].
