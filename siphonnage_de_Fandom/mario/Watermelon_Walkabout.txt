'''Watermelon Walkabout''' is a coastal-themed board available in Partner Party in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Description ==
Watermelon Walkabout has the same layout as [[Megafruit Paradise]]. Like Megafruit Paradise, the player can access the red and green pipes to move to another island.
