"'''Toddler Terrors of Time Travel'''" is the eighth episode of ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]''. It first aired on September 29, 1990.

== Plot ==
[[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]] and [[Kooky von Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|Kooky von Koopa]] travel back in time to prevent the [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] [[Luigi (DiC Cartoons)|brothers]] from discovering the Real World [[Brooklyn]] [[Warp Zone]] that first led them to the [[Mushroom Kingdom]].
