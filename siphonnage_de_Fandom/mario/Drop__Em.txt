{{Nihongo|'''Drop 'Em'''|コインドロップ|Koin Doroppu|Coin Drop}} is a gambling minigame in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''.

== Layout ==
The minigame is set in a peg maze, with a background designed after the plains theme in ''[[Super Mario Advance 4: Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

== Opening ==
Before the minigame starts, [[Lakitu]] appears as a referee when to tell the player to start the minigame.

== Gameplay ==
The player's objective is to time dropping the [[coin]] into the [[Question Block|? Block]]. Each coin the player drops, the player loses one coin. If it makes in the ? Block, the roulette determines how much coins the player receives. If the player misses, they have to restart again.

== Ending ==
There is no ending to the minigame until the player loses coins, which shows a sad expression of the player in an icon.

If they are completing a mission called [[Engaging Game]], there will be an ending after they complete the objective of the minigame. It shows a happy expression of the player in an icon as they win the minigame.
