'''Advisors''' (prototype name, '''Peas''') characters that appear in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]''.

==History==
The Advisors are the Beanish servants that worship the rule of [[Queen Bean]], and are the [[Beanbean Kingdom]] equivalents of [[Toad|Mushroom Retainers]]. When [[Cackletta]] attempted to usurp Queen Bean's rule, all the Advisors of the castle were imprisoned in the sewer system of the castle. They were saved and freed by [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]], who defeated the [[Belly Blech|possessed]] Queen Bean.
