'''Flip Tiles''' are objects in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''.

==Description==
Flip Tiles are yellow checkerboard tile [[platform]]s. They can be activated by stepping on them, causing the Flip Tile to flip downwards continuously.

==In ''Super Mario Galaxy''==
Flip Tiles appear only at [[Toy Time Galaxy]], on the 8-bit [[Mario]]–[[Luigi]] planet.
