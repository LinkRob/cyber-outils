'''Hypnotize''' is a song by the late Notorious B.I.G.

It’s instrumental plays while Yoshi explains how he got to the desert kingdom, playing over a flashback showing him in Brooklyn. It is one of the two only non-Mario game licensed soundtrack in the movie (along with “That’s Amore”), likely due to their frequent use in the first movie being criticized by audiences.
