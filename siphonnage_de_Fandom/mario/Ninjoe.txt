'''Ninjoes''' are purple creatures which appear as enemies in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They appeared during the Sammer Kingdom levels and aided the [[Sammer Guy]]s while fighting Mario and his allies. They, first the most part, were destroyed when [[The Void]] swallowed up the [[Sammer Kingdom]]. They appeared later in the [[Pit of 100 Trials]] in [[Flipside]]. Ninjoes attack by throwing ninja stars at the heroes. They can jump quickly making them highly evasive and hard to injure.
