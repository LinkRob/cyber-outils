'''Ukiwani''' are a type of enemy in ''[[Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Ukiwani are crocodile enemies that wear an inner tube. Their eyes are always half-closed. In their artwork, Ukiwani are shown to have a green body, with both their belly and eyelids being light green.

===Behavior===
Ukiwani lie in their inner tube at the surface of the lake's water. Whenever [[Wario]] is near a Ukiwani, it jumps straight up to try and bite him.

==In ''Wario Land: Super Mario Land 3''==
[[File:Ukiwani WLSML3 sprite.png|frame|left]]
Ukiwani are only encountered in [[Course No.29]].
