'''Melon Slices''' are common items which appear in ''[[Donkey Kong 64]]''.

==Summary==
The purpose of the Melon Slices is that collecting one restores a bit of HP for the Kongs. Four Melon Slices is needed to fill up a full watermelon that represents full health. The most common way to get Melon Slices is to defeat enemies. Defeating common enemies such as [[Zinger]]s will result in a single Melon Slice. Defeating tougher enemies such as [[Kosha]]s and [[Krobot]]s results in two Melon Slices. However, defeating enemies such as [[Klump]]s and [[Purple Klaptrap]]s doesn't result in Melon Slices but oranges instead.

There are also objects called [[Melon Crate]]s that wield four Melon Slices. However, they are much more rarer and only a few can be found in the entire game.

==Trivia==
*There is an easier way to restore health. The player simply needs to head inside a [[Tag Barrel]] and the Kong's melons is fully restored.
