The '''Sun Tower''' a fairly tall tower that is located in the [[Flower Fields]]. It makes it's only appearance in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Sun Tower is the home of the [[Sun]], which Mario and his party should go to the end of the northwestern path of the [[Flower Fields]], past the [[Bubble Plant]] and other patches.

To reach the Sun, [[Bombette]] is needed to destroy the rock that is blocking off the path leading up to the Sun. Afterwards, Mario can speak with the Sun who informs Mario that he is feeling unwell due to the numerous of clouds blocking off Flower Fields, rendering the Sun unable to shine. He then tells Mario he must destroy the [[Puff-Puff Machine]] which is the source of the dark clouds. After Mario does so, the Sun cheerfully brightens up and brings light back to Flower Fields. The Sun can still be spoken to at Sun Tower where he is grateful for Mario's help.
