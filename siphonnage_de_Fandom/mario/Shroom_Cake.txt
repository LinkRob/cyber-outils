The '''Shroom Cake''' is an item and a [[recipe]] featured in the [[Paper Mario (series)|''Paper Mario'' series]]. It makes its first appearance in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and later reappears in both ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]'' and ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==Appearances==
===''Paper Mario''===
[[File:Shroom Cake PM sprite.png|frame|left]]
The Shroom Cake can be made by [[Tayce T.]] by having her cook a [[Cake Mix]] and a [[Super Mushroom|Mushroom]] or a [[Super Shroom]]. The Shroom Cake can restore 10 [[Heart Point|HP]] and 10 [[Flower Point|FP]], which is useful during that point in the game (where the Cake Mix can be obtained at [[Shy Guy's Toy Box]] for the first time).

===''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''===
[[File:ShroomCakeTTYD.png|frame|left]]
Shroom Cakes retain their role from the previous game. A Shroom Cake can be created if [[Mario]] gives a [[Cake Mix]] with either a Mushroom or a Super Shroom to [[Zess T.]].

===''Super Paper Mario''===
Shroom Cakes make a reappearance, but now restore 30 HP and cures [[Poison (status effect)|poison]] making them a much more effective recovery item in this game than the previous two. In order to make Shroom Cakes in this game, one must give [[Dyllis]] of [[Flopside]] a Cake Mix and a [[Super Shroom Shake]].
