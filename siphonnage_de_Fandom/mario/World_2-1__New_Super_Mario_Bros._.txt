'''World 2-1''' is a desert level, and first level of [[World 2 (New Super Mario Bros.)|World 2]] of the game, ''[[New Super Mario Bros.]]''

==Walkthrough==
[[File:NSMB_ShellDash.png|thumb|160px|right|Gameplay of World 2-1.]]
At the beginning of the level, there are four [[Question Block]]s and a [[Pokey]]. [[Mario]] can jump over the [[Pokey]] by jumping off the [[Question Block|blocks]]. After this, there is a [[Spiny]] and another [[Pokey]], which can be jumped over by hitting the block near it. After the [[Pokey]] is jumped over, [[Mario]] will come across the first [[Star Coin]] of the level. There is a [[Pokey]] nearby it, so [[Mario]] must be careful. The [[Star Coin]] can be reached by jumping off the ledge. There is then a lift with a [[Pokey]] next to it. Past the lift, there are three more lifts with [[Pokey]]s on them. The third lift is much higher than the other ones, so [[Mario]] can jump below it when it is at its highest point to grab the second [[Star Coin]]. [[Mario]] should be careful in this area, as there is [[quicksand]]. Then there are two more lifts, one of which has a [[Pokey]] on it. There is a pit between the two lifts, so it is more dangerous than the other lifts. Then, [[Mario]] will pass the [[Midway Point]], and he will encounter a few [[Brick Block]]s and [[Question Block]]s. There are thinner platforms just past this, as well as lifts, pits, [[Pokey]]s, and the third and final [[Star Coin]]. In this area, there is a tiny [[Warp Pipe]], which can only be accessed if [[Mario]] has the [[Mini Mushroom]]. Inside it is a maze and some [[coin]]s. Past that, there is a large pit which has many lifts above it. These lifts are rapidly moving up and down. At the end of the pit, there is a [[Pokey]], and then a [[Warp Pipe|pipe]]. After the [[Warp Pipe|pipe]], there's a few [[Brick Block|blocks]] and a [[Pokey]]. If [[Mario]] hits one of the [[Brick Block|blocks]], he will find a [[P Switch|P-Switch]]. This switch turns all [[coin]]s into [[Brick Block|blocks]] and vice versa, making it possible for [[Mario]] to jump over the [[Pokey]]. After jumping over the [[Pokey]], [[Mario]] will slide down the [[flagpole]].

==[[Star Coins]]==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first [[Star Coin]] is located in mid-air. It can easily be obtained but take caution of the nearby [[Pokey]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': This [[Star Coin|coin]] will be above some [[quicksand]] in the air. However, a [[Pokey]] on a moving platform can cause some difficulties getting the [[Star Coin|coin]].
*'''Star Coin 3''': This [[Star Coin|coin]] is located on the bottom of the level, but two [[Pokey]]s on moving platforms can be a nuisance.
