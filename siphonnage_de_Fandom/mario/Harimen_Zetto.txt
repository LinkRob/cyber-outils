A '''Harimen Zetto''' is a stronger variant of [[Spiky]] and an enemy in ''[[Wario Land 4]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Harimen Zetto have almost the same appearance as Spikies except their shell is black instead of pink.

===Behavior===
Like Spikies, Harimen Zetto have a pattern of walking back and forth. [[Wario]] cannot flip over a Harimen Zetto from a [[Ground Pound (move)|Smash Attack]], unlike with Spikies, but this instead causes the Harimen Zetto to move toward Wario.

==In ''Wario Land 4''==
In Normal mode, Harimen Zetto only appear in [[Monsoon Jungle]], [[The Big Board]], and some [[pink area]]s. In S-Hard difficulty, Harimen Zetto replace most of the Spikies.

To flip a Harimen Zetto, Wario must either perform a Super Smash Attack nearby, throw either a [[rock]] or a regular enemy at it, or hit its underside from below. Wario can instantly defeat a Harimen Zetto by throwing a heavy enemy at it, such as a [[Ringosukī]]. Harimen Zetto can also be defeated instantly if eithger Wario touches it in either his [[Fat Wario|Fat]], [[Frozen Wario|Frozen]], or [[Zombie Wario|Zombie]] form or if he [[roll]]s into them. A Harimen Zetto yields three [[Silver Coin]]s upon being defeated, the most out of any other enemy. If Wario takes damage from a Harimen Zetto, he can lose as much as a [[Gold Coin]], depending on how many [[point]]s he has.
