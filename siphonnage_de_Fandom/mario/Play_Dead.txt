'''Play Dead''' is a [[Captain Command]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.

== Description ==
If the user uses Play Dead, any enemies that are currently attacking them instead go to attack some other ally.

== Flavor text ==
*'''In-game description:''' ''Feigns defeat on the battlefield so enemies will turn their attention elsewhere.''
