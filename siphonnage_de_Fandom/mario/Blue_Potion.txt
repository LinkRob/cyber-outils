The '''Blue Potion''' is a key item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
The Blue Potion is one of four potions that [[Princess Peach]] must use to make a potion to turn her invisible in the [[X-Naut Fortress]]. [[TEC-XX]] instructs Peach to follow the correct directions to make the invisibility potion.
