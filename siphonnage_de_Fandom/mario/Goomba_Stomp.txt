'''Goomba Stomp''' is an unlockable [[Extra Room]] minigame in ''[[Mario Party 4]]''. It can be unlocked by clearing [[Tree Stomp]] twice.

==Gameplay==
The character controls a mechanical machine appearing to be made from tree trunk. The object is for the character to use the machine to stomp on as many [[Goomba]]s as possible within a [[time limit]] of thirty seconds. The initial record is zero, so even stomping one Goomba counts as surpassing it. The player is ranked depending on how many Goombas their character stomped within the time limit.

==Controls==
*{{button|gcn|Stick}}: Change direction
*{{button|gcn|A}}: Move forward
*{{button|gcn|B}}: Move backward

== Flavor text ==
''Get in the robot and stomp on as many Goombas as you can within the time limit.''
