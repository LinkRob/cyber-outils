'''Slot Machine''' is a 1-player mini-game found in ''[[Mario Party]]''.

==Gameplay==
In this mini-game, the player has to match three images by hitting a ! Block.

The images shown on the slot machine will determine the amount of coins if you match three:

*'''Mario Hat''' – 20 coins
*'''Treasure Chest''' – 10 coins
*'''Fire Flower''' – 8 coins
*'''Mushroom''' – 6 coins
*'''Coin Bag''' – 5 coins
*'''Koopa Shell''' – 3 coins
*'''Coin''' – 1 coin
