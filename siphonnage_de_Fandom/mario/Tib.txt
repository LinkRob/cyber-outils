'''Tib''' is a minor character who appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==In ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''==
Tib is one of many characters that was imprisoned by [[Mimi]] during Chapter 2. Tib often has problems in his love life mainly due to him being clueless. However, he was eventually able to get a girlfriend before the events of the game and came to [[Merlee's Mansion]] to ask for [[Merlee]] to see the future for him and his girlfriend. However, he was captured by Mimi and forced to work. After Mimi was defeated, Tib and most of the other prisoners were set free.
