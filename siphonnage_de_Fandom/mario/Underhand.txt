|defeated = Invincible}}The''' Underhands''' are strange white hands with linked segments that seem to follow the player. They are only seen in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. They are found in the [[underwhere]] and wish to drag the spirits of the Underwhere deeper into the darkness. It is almost impossible to kill them. It is rumored that they may be stopped for good by using [[Barry]] the Pixl. They can be evaded by flipping into 3-D mode. They will still follow the player flipped however and they may be right on top of you when the player flips back to 2-D.

One can also avoid them by running far from it. Most Underhands are attached to a wall and will only move as far as they can stretch, but are able to pass through platforms. The underhands can be found in the [[River Twygz]] and in the [[Underwhere Road]]. They seem to dislike light for when torches are lit by the player. They disappear but reappear when the torch burns out. The Underhands in the underground part of the [[River Twygz]] will also disappear if water is drained but likewise will reappear if the water level rises again. Some of the Underhands in the River Twygz can float and will slowly move towards the player.
