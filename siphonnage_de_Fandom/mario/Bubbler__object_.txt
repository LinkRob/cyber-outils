'''Bubblers''', alternatively named '''air bubblers''', are an object in the {{series|Super Mario}}. They first appear in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and return in both ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Odyssey]]''.

==Description==
Bubblers are small, yellow pipes found underwater, and they create [[Bubble|air bubbles]], which can be used to replenish [[Mario]]'s [[Air Meter]] for while he is underwater.

==In {{series|Super Mario}}==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
Bubblers first appear in the [[Beach Bowl Galaxy]] and are later also found in [[Buoy Base Galaxy]], [[Drip Drop Galaxy]], [[Bonefin Galaxy]], and [[Deep Dark Galaxy]].

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
Bubblers first appear in the [[Cosmic Cove Galaxy]] and later appear in the [[Starshine Beach Galaxy]] and one section of the [[Battle Belt Galaxy]].

===''Super Mario Odyssey''===
A bubbler first appears in the [[Lake Kingdom]], where it creates bubbles in the underwater tunnel leading to the lake. Bubblers are later also found in [[Seaside Kingdom]], where they appear throughout.
