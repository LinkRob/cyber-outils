The '''Moon Stone''' is a key item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Description==
The Moon Stone has a self-explanatory name of being a stone that resembles the moon. More specifically, the Moon Stone resembles a crescent moon.

==History==
The Moon Stone can be found in [[Shhwonk Fortress]] along with the [[Sun Stone]]. The Moon Stone must be used with the Sun Stone in order to access [[Hooktail Castle]].
