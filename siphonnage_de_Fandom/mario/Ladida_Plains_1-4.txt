'''Ladida Plains 1-4''' is the fourth [[level]] of [[Ladida Plains]] in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''. The music changes back to its music from Ladida Plains 1-2 music.

== Layout ==
The player starts the level on some hills and in front of them is a [[Piranha Plant]] and a [[Springboard]] which can be used to get some [[coin]]s and a [[Crystal (Super Princess Peach)|crystal]]. After the hills there are two routes, the upper route leads to some [[Donut Block]]s, that can only be crossed by using the [[Gloom]] vibe and at the end of the Donut Blocks is a [[Toad (species)|Toad]]. On the lower route are some [[Goomba]]s and a [[Warp Pipe]] that leads to the next area.

In the next area is a [[Nipper Plant]] and another Springboard which can be used to get a crystal. After this is a wall, and the player will need to use the [[Joy]] vibe to get over it. As they reach the end of the area, the player can use the Joy vibe again to go to a small secret area to get lots of coins before going down the Warp Pipe. In this area is a giant Springboard in front of two gaps, the middle gap leads to a Toad, whilst the upper one leads to a [[Mad Green Koopa]] and another Warp Pipe.

In the next area is a [[Red Koopa Troopa]] and some wooden platforms, if the [[Rage]] vibe is used the player can go through the wooden platforms and get the final Toad. After this area is a Springboard, which can be used to access some floating platforms and a large amount of coins. There is also another wall that requires the Joy vibe to get over before the Warp Pipe. After exiting the Warp Pipe, there is a giant Springboard and if the player goes left they can collect a puzzle piece, going right will lead to some moving platforms and [[Bob-omb]]s, before a downhill section filled with Bob-ombs that leads to the end of the level.

== Collectibles ==
=== Toads ===
* '''Toad 1''': Use the Gloom vibe to run across the long blue falling platform.
* '''Toad 2''': Jump on the large green spring and go to the right on the second floor.
* '''Toad 3''': Rage to burn the bridge, jump over the pipe from your left.

=== Puzzle Piece ===
*Use the large green spring to take you to the higher place to the green grass and jump on the brown spring.
