The '''Ultibed''' is an important item that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It is soon acquired after [[Bedsmith]] has put together the five Zee Parts at [[Somnom Woods]].

==Usage==
The Ultibed is mainly used to help access the [[Dream World|dream world]] where the anicent Zeekeeper rests as Mario and the rest need help destroying the barrier of [[Neo Bowser Castle]]. The Ultibed must be slept on a place within the woods where the dream power swells and [[Dreambert|Prince Dreambert]] quickly states the [[Pi'illo Temple]].
