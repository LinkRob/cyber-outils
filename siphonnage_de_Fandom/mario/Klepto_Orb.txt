The '''Klepto Capsule''' is a [[Orb|capsule]] first introduced in ''[[Mario Party 5]]''. It can be thrown onto any space within ten spaces ahead of the player or it can be used on the player for ten [[coin]]s. Its effect summons [[Klepto]] to take the player to another player's space (which is determined by roulette). The Klepto Capsule returns in ''[[Mario Party 6]]'', where it is renamed the '''Klepto Orb'''. It costs five coins at the [[Orb Shop]]. Opponents that land on a space containing the Orb summon Klepto to take them back to the start of the board.

The Klepto Capsule and Klepto Orb have the same Japanese name, so they can be interpreted as the same subject, although the effect of the Klepto Orb differs in that instead of having Klepto take a character to a [[space]] with another character, it takes whoever landed on a [[space]] with a Klepto Orb back to the start of the board.
