'''Power Line Pigeons''' is one of [[Wario-Man]]'s [[microgame]]s in ''[[WarioWare: Twisted!]]''. A similar microgame titled [[Drop Zone]] went on to appear in ''[[WarioWare: Get It Together!]]''.

==Layout==
There is a light gray power line spanning most of the horizontal area. Light brown birds sit on the power lines. Below the power line is [[Wario]], whose sprites are reused from [[Wario's Adventure]], a boss microgame in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of [[Wario]], whom they can move left and right by tilting the system. The objective is for Wario to avoid any light brown splats of bird [[poop]] from dropping onto him. Small birds leave one dropping, while the big bird in level 3 leaves four droppings spread in a wave. In the event of a loss, Wario will have his face caked with poop.

===Difficulty levels===
Each increased difficulty level makes it harder for Wario to avoid the bird poop.

*'''Level 1:''' Five small birds fly in and try to poop on Wario.
*'''Level 2:''' Ten small birds fly in and try to poop on Wario.
*'''Level 3:''' Seven small birds and one big bird fly in and try to poop on Wario.
