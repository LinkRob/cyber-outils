The '''Hall of Hieroglyphs''' is the first level of the [[Entry Passage]] in ''[[Wario Land 4]]'', let alone the game in general. It is a tutorial level, and it features various hieroglyphs on the wall that teach [[Wario]] how to perform his various basic moves.
