The '''Superguard''' is a technique used by [[Mario]] and his partners in the game ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

== Description ==
In order to use the Superguard, the player must press {{Button|gcn|b}} in 3/60th of a second (2/30th of a second in the game's [[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door (Nintendo Switch)|remake]])when an enemy's attack is about to hit. If the Superguard is used correctly, not only Mario nor any partner won't take any damage but they will also counterattack for 1 point of damage (direct physical attacks only). However, the Superguard can be very tricky to use and it is recommended not to use it for status ailment-inducing attacks as the regular Guard can nullify them easier.

== Trivia ==
*One can make it through the entire game undamaged if they are extremely skilled with the Superguard.
