'''Gary''' is a minor character that appears in the game ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Gary is a [[Goomba]] that tried to escape [[Castle Bleck]] with another Goomba. The two of them soon meet up with [[Luigi]] and manages to fool the clueless plumber into being their bodyguards. However, the three of them run into a dead-end and Gary ends up getting hypnotized by [[Nastasia]] to serve [[Count Bleck]]. Soon, it happens to the other Goomba and [[Mr. L|Luigi as well]].
