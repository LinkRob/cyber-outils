'''Biff Atlas, the Bodybuilder''' is an optional [[portrait ghost]] in ''[[Luigi's Mansion]]'' and [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|its remake]].

==Profile==
===Personality===
Biff Atlas is a stereotypical jock who is cocky and loves to lift weights. Conversely, he also loves lilies.

===Physical appearance===
Biff has turquoise skin with a blond flattop and green eyes. His head is rectangular, his chin has a small cleft, and his body is very muscular. He wears a red tank top, red wristbands with yellow stripes, a brown belt with a gold buckle, and purple shorts that fit around his tail.

==''Luigi's Mansion''==
[[File:Biff Atlas LM.png|left|thumb|200px]]
Biff Atlas is found in the [[Rec Room]]. When Luigi enters, Biff is focused on lifting a barbell, choosing not to attack him. If Luigi wishes to capture Biff, he must first hit one of the three nearby punching bags toward Biff, while avoiding its backswing, which can hurt him. Doing this provokes Biff, who starts attacking Luigi by swinging his hands in rage. One of Biff's punches causes Luigi to lose twenty [[Heart Point|HP]]. Each time Luigi hits Biff with a punching bag, he becomes angrier and attacks quicker. After Biff is hit with punching bags three times, he collapses onto the floor, exposing his heart. This makes him vulnerable to the [[Poltergust 3000]], allowing Luigi to suck him up. Biff Atlas has 100 [[HP]]. If Luigi fails to capture him, Biff returns to weightlifting and Luigi must hit him with another punching bag to resume capturing him. After Biff is captured, the Rec Room lights up, and a green [[treasure chest]] appears which contains money.

Biff Atlas's portrait number in the [[Gallery (Luigi's Mansion)|Gallery]] is 16.

==Flavor text==
*'''[[Game Boy Horror]] profile:''' "''This kind body builder loves muscles and lilies. Why lilies? They symbolize purity.''"

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
His first name is a pun on "biff", a slang term meaning "to hit". His last name is based on [[wikipedia:Atlas (mythology)|Atlas]], a titan from Greek Mythology who was forced to hold up the sky.

==Trivia==
*A bust of Biff Atlas can be found in the [[The Artist's Studio]].
*Most of the time Biff Atlas is shown to be having a hard time lifting the barbell. If Luigi continues to leave him alone, he will eventually succeed in lifting it after a few attempts.
*In his platinum frame in the remake, the background heavily features lilies, referencing his stated fondness for them.
*[[Luigi]] can take damage if he stands under Biff Atlas when he puts his barbell down.
