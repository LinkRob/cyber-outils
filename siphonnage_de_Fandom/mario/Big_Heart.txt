A '''Big Heart''' is an item in ''[[Dance Dance Revolution: Mario Mix]]''.

==Description==
A Big Heart resembles a large heart in a container. It can be bought at any shop run by a [[Lakitu]] in the game's Story Mode for 170 [[coin]]s. Up to three Big Hearts can be carried at a time. If [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] has one in his possession and uses it before a dance, the dance meter completely fills up once it is empty. Even if the Big Heart is not necessary, it still disappears from the item inventory.
