{{Nihongo|'''Get Over It'''|ジャンプサバイバル|Janpu sabaibaru|Jump Survival}} is an unlockable [[Team Minigame|Team]] [[Minigame|minigame]] in ''[[Super Mario Party]]''.

== Description ==
=== Opening ===
The [[Grrrol]] rolls from the tunnel. The team players are shocked with the Grrrol, they all jumped over the Grrrol.

=== Gameplay ===
The team players must jump over the Grrrol while it attempts to touch them. Whomever the Grrrol touches, the player falls into the water. The last standing team wins.

=== Ending ===
The winning team poses their victory. The losing team poses their loss.

== Game data ==

== Trivia ==
*'''Get Over It''' comes from "get over it". The phrase means to accept their past and move on.
*The Japanese translation, ''Jump Survival'' connects to the minigame gameplay.
