A '''Bolt Beam'''<ref>Super Mario Encyclopedia, ''Super Mario Galaxy'' section, page 126</ref> is a type of obstacle that made its only appearance in the game ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]''. They are beam-shooting robots that have a screw on their heads.

==Appearances==
===''Super Mario Galaxy''===
In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', a single Bolt Beam appears in [[Buoy Base Galaxy]]. It is seen shooting circular beams of electricity that damage [[Mario]]/[[Luigi]] if he touches them. Their bolt can be removed, which opens up the inside of the planet they are stationed on. [[Ball Beamer]]s can often be seen near Bolt Beams, Ball Beamers however are indestructible and cannot be destroyed.
