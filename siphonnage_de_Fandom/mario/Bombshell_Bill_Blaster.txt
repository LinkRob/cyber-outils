'''Bombshell Bill Blasters''' are enemy cannons featured in both ''[[Paper Mario]]'' and ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==Appearances==
=== ''Paper Mario'' ===
Bombshell Bill Blasters can be quite tricky. They fire a [[Bombshell Bill]] every two turns, and stand idol every second turn. They won't launch a new bill if there's already one out, either. These cannons are powerful variants of regular [[Bill Blaster]]s, but are gold and have much more powerful stats. They also can't attack, much like the regular cannon. During a battle with these, if [[Mario]] is at a low enough level to receive [[Star Point]]s from the [[Bombshell Bill]]s, Mario can continue fighting, without damaging the Cannons to receive the maximum number of Star Points from a battle, 100. This trick can be done in ''[[The Thousand-Year Door]]'', but it isn't likely to receive Star Points from Bombshell Bills that late in the game.

=== ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door'' ===
They are found only in the final dungeon in the game, this one being the [[Palace of Shadow]]. Like their original ''Paper Mario'' versions, they take up a turn to do nothing, and use their next turn to launch a bombshell.
