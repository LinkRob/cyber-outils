'''Napple''' is a female golfer who appears in ''[[Mobile Golf]]''. Here, she is one of four characters playable from the start, along with [[Ken]], [[Thread]], and [[Lisa]].

==Physical appearance==
Napple is a girl with ginger hair arranged into a ponytail and a green eye color. She wears a pink shirt, a white skirt, and tennis shoes with a matching pink color.
