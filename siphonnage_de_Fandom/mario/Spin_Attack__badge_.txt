'''Spin Attack''' is a [[badge]] which only appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Spin Attack costs two [[Badge Point|BP]] to wear, and in doing so, [[Mario]] can use his spin ability to [[Dizzy|daze]] enemies in a first strike, paralyzing them for two turns.

The Spin Attack is obtained in [[Dry Dry Desert]], on a plateau that can only be reached from the assistance of a [[Tweester]].
