A '''Piranha Bud''' is the child-state of a [[Piranha Plant]] and the state that comes after a [[Nipper Plant]]. They resemble regular [[Piranha Plant]]s, with the exception being that they're mouths are smaller and they're overall smaller in size. They often appear alongside their adult forms and serve as a distraction to the heroes and a common pest. Many different kinds of Piranha Buds appeared throughout the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise. It is assumed that after some time, a Piranha Bud blooms into a [[Piranha Plant]].

==History==
===''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island / Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3''===
A subspecies of Piranha Buds called the [[Naval Bud]] appeared in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' as well as ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''. They are seen during the fight with their master [[Naval Piranha]] and are formed when [[Kamek]] uses his magic to make Naval Piranha larger.

===''Tetris Attack''===
Piranha Buds appear in ''[[Tetris Attack]]'' where they are seen fighting alongside [[Naval Piranha]]. They are encountered at the tenth level of Easy Vs. mode.

===''Paper Mario''===
A fiery variety of Piranha Bud called the [[Lava Bud]] appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]'' during the fight with [[Lava Piranha]] at [[Lavalava Island]]. They are often times on fire meaning [[Mario]] or his partners may suffer damage if they try to attack the Lava Buds up front. 

===''Yoshi's Island DS''===
A sub-type of Piranha Bud called the [[Bungee Bud]] appears in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]'' during the boss fight with [[Big Bungee Piranha]]. Bungee Buds are seen assisting the colossal [[Piranha Plant]] and tend to stretch out serving as a pest to [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]], when [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] loses the baby he is carrying, a Bungee Bud may reach out and hold onto the baby making it harder for [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] to reach him.
