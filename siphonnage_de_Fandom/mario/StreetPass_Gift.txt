A '''StreetPass Gift''' is a mechanic featured in ''[[Super Mario 3D Land]]''. It allows users to send each other gifts via a [[Nintendo 3DS]]'s [[StreetPass]] or [[SpotPass]] functionality, being named '''SpotPass Gift''' for the latter instance. If a [[Mii]] has been tagged by another Mii while in StreetPass mode, a StreetPass Gift from them can be obtained in a [[Toad House]]; all StreetPass Gifts are [[power-up]] items. There are five reward tiers for when consecutively tagged by the same Mii, each one yielding a higher reward and having a different present wrapping.
