'''Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge''' (also known as '''Mario Kart: Koopa's Challenge'''<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L9TzJVeptc0|title=Mario Kart: Koopa’s Challenge Ride|author=Theme Version|work=YouTube|date=2021-02-12|accessdate=2023-05-31}}</ref> in [[Universal Studios Japan]] is a 3D & 4D ride attraction found in the [[Super Nintendo World]] amusement areas. This ride requires supervision for people below 48" (122cm).<ref name=ush/> The theme is based on ''[[Mario Kart 8]]''.
