|games = ''Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door''}}'''Petal Meadows''' is a small area, east of [[Rogueport]], which leads to [[Petalburg]] and [[Hooktail Castle]]. It is featured in the game, ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
[[Mario]] first reaches this area by going through a certain [[Warp Pipe]] found near the beginning area of [[Rogueport Sewers]] after defeating the [[Blooper]]. The enemies found here include Goombas (as well as [[Goomba]] sub-species) and [[Koopa Troopa]]s. When Mario first enters the area, he first sees [[Hooktail]], flying to his castle here. Another not of interest here, is that this is the only place where a [[Horsetail]] can be. It is found using the Candy Canes like in ''[[Paper Mario (game)|Paper Mario]]'', but hitting with Mario's [[Hammer]] 10 times instead of running around and you get a Horsetail instead of hearts. When leaving Petalburg, [[Koops]] musters up some strength and joins Mario's crew.
