'''Daniel Robert Hennessey''' (born September 11, 1942 - November 13, 2024) was an American-Canadian voice actor and voice director in animation. His credited ''[[Mario (series)|Mario]]''-relevant role is providing the voice for [[Roy Koopa|Bully Koopa]] in ''[[The Adventures of Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' and ''[[Super Mario World]]'' animated series. He also voiced some of the cavemen. He even did the songs as well. Hennessey passed away on November 13, 2024 at the age of 82 due to complications associated with Parkinson's disease.

== Roles Outside [[Mario]] ==

* ''Little Bear -'' Father Bear
* ''Inspector Gadget'' - Chief Quimby
* ''The Raccoons -'' George Raccoon/Train Engineer Sid
* ''The Adventures of Tintin -'' Thomson 
* ''Police Academy -'' Zed and Tackleberry
* Brave Heart Lion - Care Bears
