A '''Precision Card''' is a type of [[card]] in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Precision Cards are only used in [[Kamek's Carpet Ride]] and [[Shy Guy's Shuffle City]], replacing the role that [[Dice Block]]s have in other boards. There are six types of Precision Cards, with numbers generally ranging from 1 to 6 to note how many spaces the user moves forward when using the card. In Shy Guy's Shuffle City, there is a Precision Card depicting a "0," and it does not move the user forward any spaces.

A Precision Card's colors vary depending on the board they are in. In Kamek's Carpet Ride, Precision Cards are have a blue border, and the number is a light blue gradient with a thick, black outline. In Shy Guy's Shuffle City, all Precision Cards have a pink border. A "0" Precision Card depicts the number as entirely but surrounded by the usual black outline. Precision Cards numbered 1-4 depict the numbers near identically to those in Kamek's Carpet Ride, except the numbers are larger. Precision Cards numbered either 5 or 6 are rainbow-colored, similarly to Power Precision Cards, and the number is displayed larger than the "5" and "6" Precision Cards in Kamek's Carpet Ride.

Precision Cards have a more powerful variation named [[Power Precision Card]], which can move opponents backward a few spaces.
