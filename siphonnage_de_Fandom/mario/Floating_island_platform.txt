'''Floating island platforms'''<ref>''Super Mario World: Super Mario Advance 2: Prima's Official Strategy Guide'', page 26.</ref> are objects in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
[[File:SMW Yoshis Island 4 Floating Island Platforms.png|thumb|left|200px|[[Mario]] crossing some floating island platforms]]
Floating island platforms are orange triangular rock [[platform]]s with flat grassy tops. They float on the surface of [[water]] and can be stood on by [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]], although by doing so, the floating island platform starts to sink into the water. When Mario or Luigi gets off a floating island platform, it rises back to the surface of the water, unless it has entered a [[pit]].

==''Super Mario World''==
In ''[[Super Mario World]]'', Floating Island platforms appear exclusively in [[Yoshi's Island 4]].
