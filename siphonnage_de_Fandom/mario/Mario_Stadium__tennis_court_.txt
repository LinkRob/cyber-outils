'''Mario Stadium''' is a tennis court in ''[[Mario Tennis Open]]''.

==Description==
It is where the basic [[Grass Court]], [[Hard Court]], or [[Clay Court]] takes place, all of which lack any gimmicks. As such, Mario Stadium takes over the role of [[Peach Dome]] from ''[[Mario Power Tennis]]''. Like with any other stadium court, spectators can be seen in the crowd, including [[Toad (species)|Toads]], [[Shy Guy]]s, and [[Koopa Troopa]]s.

==In ''Mario Tennis Open''==
All three of the Mario Stadium courts are available from the start. The Grass, Hard, and Clay variations of Mario Stadium all appear in the final World Open tournament, the [[Special Cup|Champions Cup]]: the Hard Court is in the starting round; the Clay Court is in the semifinals; and the Grass Court is in the finals.

Mario Stadium is also where the [[Ink Showdown]] Special Game is played.
