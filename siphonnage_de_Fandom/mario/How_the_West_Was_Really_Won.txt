'''How the West Was Really Won''' is [[Ashley]]'s microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''. The microgame name is derived from the film ''{{w|How the West Was Won (film)|How the West Was Won}}''.

==Gameplay==
The player will be engaging a showdown. The player may have the Wii Remote up, but they cannot shoot until "DRAW" appears onscreen. The player can shoot up to their opponent. As a result, the opponent's undergarments are shown.
*'''Level 1:''' More time to react.
*'''Level 2:''' Shorter time to react.
*'''Level 3:''' Short time to react.

==Trivia==
*Both cowboys wear boxer shorts with [[Super Mushroom]]s, [[Starman|Starmen]], and [[Fire Flower]]s, referencing ''[[Super Mario World]]''.
