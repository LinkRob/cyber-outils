'''Cheep Cheep''' is a character in ''[[Mario Party Advance]]''. He is a representative of his species of the [[Cheep Cheep|same name]] and has the same physical appearance as them.

==In ''Mario Party Advance''==
Cheep Cheep practices his swimming at the [[Mushroom Pool]], although he has a lack of confidence in his ability to swim. For the [[Swimmin' Wimp]] quest, [[Coach (Mario Party Advance)|Coach]] asks for the player's character to swim against Cheep Cheep in a race and deliberately lose by a short margin while still investing some effort. If done successfully, Cheep Cheep has a large surge in confidence, happily telling Coach that he won. As a reward, Coach gives the player a [[Desktop Golf]] [[Gaddget]].

At [[Sushi Cliff]], Cheep Cheep was one of three witnesses who saw [[Shroomlock]] being pulled off the cliff and into the ocean, the other two being [[Blooper (character)|Blooper]] and [[Dolphin (character)|Dolphin]]. Blooper is revealed to be the real culprit due to having tentacles, which neither Cheep Cheep nor Dolphin have (or any sort of arms).
