A '''Chester''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a small monster inside of a treasure chest that can only be fought in [[Bowser's Castle|Bowser's Keep]], behind one of the six doors in [[The Trial Course]].

Chesters resemble [[Pandorite|Pandorites]] in their treasure chest form, though their eyes have green eyelids, but their true form resembles a tan [[Ameboid]].

==Battle==
The Chester is a very tough opponent with very high [[Heart Point|HP]], attack, and defense. It can use strong spells like [[Flame Wall]] and [[Sand Storm]] and can even heal it's HP by a great margin with [[Mega Recover]].

===Strategy===
The Chester is weak against [[Jump]] attacks so [[Mario]] should use his strongest jump move, [[Ultra Jump]], against it. [[Geno]] should give Mario an extra boost with [[Geno Boost]] while [[Princess Peach|Princess Toadstool]] works on healing.
