'''Throw Me a Bone''' is a 4-Player [[minigame]] in ''[[Mario Party 6]]''.

==Opening==
A front view of the first character on their [[Chain Chomp|Chain-Chomp]] is shown, then the camera rotates around to behind the player as the screen splits into four sections. There are only aesthetic changes between the daytime and the nighttime. When this minigame is played in Decathlon Park, the [[Chain Chomp|Chain-Chomp]] that was watching the events follows the four players.

==Gameplay==
[[File:Throw Me a Bone night.png|thumb|left|Nighttime variety]]
The object for each character is to reach the goal at the end first. A character can get their Chain-Chomp to move toward the goal by throwing bones along the way. A purple arrow moving left and right appears in front of the player before they toss a bone. The player must {{button|gcn|A}} to stop the arrow, causing their character to throw a bone in the direction the arrow stopped, then the Chain-Chomp goes after the bone. If a character and their Chain-Chomp hit a column, both of them get stunned temporarily. Whoever crosses the [[finish line]] first is the winner.

Throw Me a Bone is the eighth minigame in [[Decathlon Park]]. Here, the objective is to get to the goal as fast as possible to earn as many points as possible, up to 1,000. The default record for this minigame in Decathlon Park is 60 seconds.

==Ending==
There are two different endings that can occur. The standard ending involves the winner performing their victory animation with the Chain-Chomp behind them. The alternate ending occurs if the player sets a New Record, and it involves the winner performing their victory animation as a bone is thrown across the screen from offscreen, then a Chain-Chomp goes after the bone.

==Controls==
* {{button|gcn|A}}: Throw a bone

==Flavor text==
* '''Rules''': ''Throw bones to coax the Chain-Chomp around obstacles and to the goal! Whoever crosses the goal first wins!''
* '''Advice''': ''The Chain-Chomp will chase after bones, so try to throw them into open spaces to avoid obstacles.''

==Trivia==
*The minigame's name comes from an expression used to request someone to go easy on or aid the speaker.
*There is a [[Miracle Book]] page based on Throw Me a Bone called "Fetch." If activated, the page shows [[Mario]] throw a bone as a herd of Chain-Chomps go after it. [[Koopa Kid]] can be seen riding on a Chain-Chomp.
