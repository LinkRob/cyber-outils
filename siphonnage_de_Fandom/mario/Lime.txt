'''Limes''' are a type of food item that appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
Limes are only found in a tree in the oasis of [[Dry Dry Desert (Paper Mario)|Dry Dry Desert]]. Using a Lime replenishes three of [[Mario]]'s [[Flower Point|FP]], unlike the [[Lemon]], which replenishes one [[Heart Point|HP]] and two FP. Limes can be cooked by [[Tayce T.]] to make a [[Tasty Tonic]], like with the Lemon. Limes can also be cooked to make [[Lime Candy]], [[Super Soda]]s, and [[Special Shake]]s. Unlike the Lemon, Limes have no other required uses (as the Lemon is required to have in order to see [[Moustafa]]).
