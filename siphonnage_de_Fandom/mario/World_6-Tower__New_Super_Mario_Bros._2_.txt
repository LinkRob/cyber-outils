'''{{world|6|towern2}}''' is the tower of [[World 6 (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World 6]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''. It primarily features dodging [[Burner]]s, some of which are [[! Block]]s that can be temporarily deactivated by hitting them causing them to stop. It is the last Tower in the game, as [[World Star (New Super Mario Bros. 2)|World Star]] does not feature one at all.

== Layout ==
This tower level features many [[Dry Bones]] and [[Burner]]s. Throughout most of the level, there will be Burners that will never turn off, forcing [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] to hit the gray [[! Block]] that activates the Burner. They are not turned off permanently though, they will reactivate after a shortwhile.

As the player traverses through the first half, avoiding Dry Bones and Burners and jumping through orange block platforms, after reaching the [[Checkpoint Flag]] they'll ride on [[Donut Lift]]s on tracks whilst trying to avoid more Burners and Dry Bones.

== [[Star Coin]]s ==
*'''Star Coin 1:''' The first Star Coin is in plain sight, but [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] cannot reach it unless they temporarily turn off the [[Burner]] that is blocking it.
*'''Star Coin 2:''' The player must enter the door left of the [[Checkpoint Flag]] (and turn off the Burner). The door leads to a large room with Burners and blocks. Above the arrow sign are two Hidden Blocks. One contains a 1-Up Mushroom. The second Star Coin is at the very top of the room.
*'''Star Coin 3:''' Just before [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] fight the [[Reznor]]s, there is a green [[Warp Pipe]] on the right wall that is blocked by a Burner. After deactivating it, the green pipe takes the player to a room with a Coin Roulette Block and circling Burners. There is a Hidden Block that contains a 1-Up Mushroom on the right of the room. The player must climb the Burners to find the third Star Coin.
