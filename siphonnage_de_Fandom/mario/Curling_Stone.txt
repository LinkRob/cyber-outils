The '''Curling Stone''' is a Bowling [[Stickers#Thing Stickers|Thing Sticker]] in ''[[Paper Mario: Sticker Star]]''. One is located in the [[Ice Flow]], where it slides down with the [[penguin]]s. Curling Stones can also be purchased from the [[Decalburg]] Thing shop.
