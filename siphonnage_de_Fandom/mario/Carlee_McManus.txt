'''Carlee Vianna McManus''' (born June 24, 1983) is an American actress and voice actress who portrays [[Lemmy Koopa]] in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', replacing [[Lani Minella]] from that game onwards (aside from Lani Minella's reused clips for ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' / ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Wii U]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]'').

Carlee McManus provides Lemmy with a voice that is higher in pitch in comparison to Lani Minella's portrayal of him.
