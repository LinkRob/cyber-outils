The '''Ice Skate''' is a power-up item featured in ''[[Super Mario 3D World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury]]''.

==Description==
[[File:Skating Goombas and Peach SM3DW screenshot.png|thumb|left|200px|A screenshot of two Skating Goombas moving after Peach, who is using her own Ice Skate, in ''Super Mario 3D World'']]
At first, a [[Skating Goomba]] is always found riding an Ice Skate, but [[Mario]] or another character can defeat it to start riding the Ice Skate themselves. The Ice Skate is based on the [[Goomba's Shoe]] from ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]'' in that it is a shoe power-up inhabited by a type of Goomba at first. An Ice Skate is yellow by default, but its color changes depending on who is using it: red for Mario, green for [[Luigi]], pink for [[Princess Peach|Peach]], blue for [[Toad (character)|Toad]], and lastly cyan for [[Rosalina]].

While utilizing an Ice Skate, a character travels smoothly and faster across the ice. However, a disadvantage of using the Ice Skate is the inability to stop moving while in it, possibly putting the character at risk of falling down a [[bottomless pit|pit]]. The player's character can exit an Ice Skate by crashing it into a wall.

==In ''Super Mario 3D World'' / ''Super Mario 3D World + Bowser's Fury''==
The Ice Skate makes its first appearance in [[Snowball Park]]. In the remake's ''[[Bowser's Fury]]'' campaign, Ice Skates also appear in [[Slipskate Slope]].
