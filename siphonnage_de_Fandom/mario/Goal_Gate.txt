'''Goal Gates''' are an object in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Description==
A Goal Gate is the level goal found at the end of every [[level]] in ''Super Princess Peach'', except boss levels. [[Princess Peach]] can enter a Goal Gate to complete the level she is in. Aside from this, a Goal Gate also consists of a roulette, which cycles between a [[Turnip]], [[Mushroom]], Flower, and [[Starman|Star]]. When Peach jumps at the roulette, [[Coin]]s are rewarded depending on which of them the roulette stops on: a Turnip rewards one coin to Peach; a Mushroom rewards 10 coins; a Flower rewards 30 coins; and a Star rewards 50 coins. If the Goal Gate lands on a Star seven levels in a row, a peach is shown instead, and it rewards a {{w|lucky number}} of 777 coins to Peach.

Goal Gates are possibly inspired by the [[GOAL! Ring]]s introduced in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', but the Goal Gate's use of power-up items is similar to the [[Goal (Super Mario Bros. 3)|Goals]] found in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.
