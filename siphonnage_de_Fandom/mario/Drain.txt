|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Drain''' is a special attack used by enemies and bosses in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''. It is a [[Fire (element)|Fire]] type more and a weaker version of [[Mega Drain]].

==Description==
Drain is a simple small fireball that travels quickly at its selected opponent.

==Effects==
With Drain, the enemy fires a fireball at a character dealing moderate damage.
