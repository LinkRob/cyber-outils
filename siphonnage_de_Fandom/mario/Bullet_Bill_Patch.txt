'''Bullet Bill Patch''' is an enemy in ''[[Yoshi's Woolly World]]'' and its [[reissue|remake]] ''[[Poochy & Yoshi's Woolly World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Bullet Bill Patch is a variant of [[Bullet Bill]] that is a two-dimensional patch of yarn designed to resemble the Bullet Bill sprite from ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''.

===Behavior===
If [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] unties a block of yarn containing a Bullet Bill Patch, the Bullet Bill Patch is released, and it travels toward Yoshi as if it were launched by a [[Bill Blaster]].

==In ''Yoshi's Woolly World''==
Bullet Bill Patches are first encountered in the level [[Snowy Mountain Lift Tour]]. Yoshi can defeat a Bullet Bill Patch by either stomping on it or by throwing a [[Yarn Ball]] at it.
