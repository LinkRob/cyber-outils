{{Infobox Game|title=Yoshi and the Mysterious Book|publisher=Nintendo|series=''[[Yoshi (franchise)|Yoshi]]''|release=May 21, 2026|genre=Platformer|image=File:Yoshi and the Mysterious Book American box art.jpg|caption=North American box art}}'''''Yoshi and the Mysterious Book''''' is a platformer game in the [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'' Series]]. It was revealed in the 9.12.2025 [[Nintendo Direct]] and released on May 21, 2026.

Yoshi jumps into a magical book named Mr. Encylopedia or for short Mr. E.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YluJneE1bMo Yoshi and the Mysterious Book - Nintendo Direct 9.12.2025] Retrieved on youtube.</ref> While inside the book, Yoshi and the world adopt an art style that resembles a hand-drawn picture book. Yoshi can carry other creatures on its back to gain abilities.

==Story==
While Yoshi is eating fruit from a tree, a sentient book known as Mister Encyclopedia (Mr. E for short) falls from the sky. As he cannot read his own pages, which hold information on various creatures, Yoshi uses Mr. E's magnifying glass to magically jump into his pages and help investigate.

==Trivia==

* This is the first [[Yoshi (franchise)|''Yoshi'']] game where:
** It is only released on the [[Nintendo Switch 2]].
** Have players not just platforming; they’re also documenting creatures.
** [[Bowser Jr.]] makes his first appearance instead of [[Baby Bowser]].
* The game’s slightly hand-drawn or sketchbook style evokes ''Yoshi’s Story''’s storybook intro but is more detailed and animated in a way that almost feels like stop-motion.
* This is the second ''Yoshi'' game to be set in the present timeline, first being [[Yoshi Topsy-Turvy|''Yoshi's Topsy Turvy'']].
