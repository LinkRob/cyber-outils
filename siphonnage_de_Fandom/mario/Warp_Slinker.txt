'''Warp Slinker''' is a kind of rare ghost that made their debut in [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']]. They are a variant of [[Slinker|Slinkers]] that only appear on floors that are multiple of 5 in the [[ScareScraper]] mode (except for the 5 floors mode). They have 250HP, which is more than double that of regular Slinkers' by 10. Their special ability is to grab Luigi and teleport him to a random location on the floor.

== Appearance ==
Unlike regular Slinkers that have purple body, Warp Slinkers have glowing cyan bodies. The rest of their physical features are same as regular Slinkers'.

== In [[Luigi's Mansion 3|''Luigi's Mansion 3'']] ==
When Warp Slinkers appear, they will most likely sneak up on [[Luigi]] like regular Slinkers. However, instead of grabbing Luigi and dealing damage like regular Slinkers, Warp Slinkers can grab Luigi and send him through a portal on the ground, which is similar to how Slinkers teleport [[Toad (character)|Toads]]. After going through the portal, Luigi will be teleported into a random room on that floor. The way to defeat Warp Slinkers is identical to that of defeating all forms of Slinkers, which is to flash and vacuum them up with the [[Poltergust G-00]], and use the [[Dark-Light Device]] to find them when they're invisible if needed. Like all forms of Slinkers and [[Hammer (Luigi's Mansion 3)|Hammers]], Warp Slinkers can be vacuumed and slammed by two players at once since they have two tails.
