}}'''Melon Guys''' are variants of Shy Guys which appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'' and its remake, ''[[Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3]]''.

== Profile ==
Melon Guys are taller than a regular Shy Guy, appearing stretched out. Their faces (or at least their masks) are stretched as well. Their arms and feet appear to be around the same size as a regular Shy Guy. They have some hair poking out from the top of their suit/costume.

== Appearances ==
===Yoshi's Island===
Melon Guys appear in a mere two levels in both the original and the remake. They cannot be defeated by being jumped on. When jumped on, they flatten temporarily and shoot a seed. These seeds can hit other enemies and damage/defeat them.

== Trivia ==
*Due to their appearance, they have also been referred to as 'Stretch Guys' or 'Stretches.'
*They are one of the rarest enemies in the game.
