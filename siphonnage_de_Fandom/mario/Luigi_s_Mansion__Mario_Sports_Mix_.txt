'''Luigi's Mansion''' is a court in ''[[Mario Sports Mix]]''.

==Description==
Luigi's Mansion takes place inside the [[Luigi's Mansion (location)|same name]]. Volleyball and Basketball are the only two sports that can be played here, and Luigi's Mansion is part the [[Flower Cup]] for both sports. [[Boo]]s also appear here as spectators and repeatedly make noises during a match.

In basketball, just like in the ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]'' iteration of the [[Luigi's Mansion (Mario Hoops 3-on-3)|court]], there are various [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghosts]] swarming around the court. A ghost may snatch the ball out of the player's hand and pass it to one of the opposing players. The basketball hoops appear in the form of chandeliers.

In volleyball, the ghosts may assist the character by striking the ball over the net on their behalf. The volleyball net is covered in a large spider web.
