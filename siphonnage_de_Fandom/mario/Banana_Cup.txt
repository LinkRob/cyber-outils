'''Banana Cup''' is a cup in the ''[[Mario Kart (series)|Mario Kart]]'' series. It appears in ''[[Mario Kart DS]]'' and ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 7]]'', ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'', and ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''. This cup holds Retro Courses. It succeeds [[Shell Cup]] and precedes [[Leaf Cup]]. It is the counterpart of the [[Flower Cup]].
