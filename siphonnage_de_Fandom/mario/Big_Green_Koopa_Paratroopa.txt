'''Big Green Koopa Paratroopas''' are a subspecies of [[Colossal Koopa Paratroopa]] first appearing in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]''. They are also, by extension, larger variants of [[Koopa Paratroopa]].

==History==
===''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition''===
Big Green Koopa Paratroopas appear in ''[[Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition]]'', in this game, they are Water-attribute enemies. In battle, in addition to attacking normally, they may occasionally use '''Big Shell''' to nullify Fire and Dark attribute damage for three turns, and may also use Frenzied Blows to deal a large amount of damage. When defeated, they may randomly drop [[Super Water Blocks]] of Green Koopa Paratroopas, while defeating them with 10+ Combo attacks guarantees they will drop [[Rare Fruit (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)|Rare Fruit]]s. As Water enemies, they take extra damage from Wood attribute attacks and less from Fire damage. Only three appear as enemies in the entire game: in the last regular battle in {{world-link|4|wendyair|World 4-Airship (Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition)}} and its Special World counterpart, and in the first battle of Course 2 of the Fixed Challenge mode in [[Score Attack]]. A Big Green Koopa Paratroopa also appears in the demo version of ''Puzzle & Dragons: Super Mario Bros. Edition'', as the boss of [[Intro]].

As teammates, Big Green Koopa Paratroopas gain a Light sub-attribute (being Water/Light teammates), and have an HP rating of 6/6, an ATK rating of 2/6, and a RCV rating of 3/6. Their Big Shell Skill changes any Fire and Wood Orbs on the player's Orb field into Water Orbs, while their Awoken Skill '''Health Boost''' raises the HP of the player's entire team. Big Green Koopa Paratroopas use [[Fruit (Yoshi food)|Fruit]]s and Rare Fruits to raise their Skill Levels. The player can create a Big Green Koopa Paratroopa by transforming a Green Koopa Paratroopa with five [[Super Mushroom]]s.

==Trivia==
*Despite their name, Big Green Koopa Paratroopas aren’t shown to be much bigger than a regular [[Paratroopa]] however it could be possible they are only slightly bigger as they’re still smaller than a [[Colossal Paratroopa]].
