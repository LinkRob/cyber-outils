{{image}}A '''Giant Eel''' is a large eel, spottable on underwater stages such as [[Koopa Cape]]. There, they swim around with the [[Cheep-Cheep]]s. They are also in [[Chain Chomp Wheel]] in the aquarium. They are red with yellow spots and a ridge on their backs. They are not a track hazard and do not affect the race.
