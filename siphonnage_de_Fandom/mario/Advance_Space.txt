'''Advance Spaces''' are a recurring type of [[space]] in the {{series|Mario Party}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Party: Island Tour]]''.

==Description==
Advance Spaces are [[Blue Space]]s depicting a plus sign and a number. Whenever a character lands onto an Advance Space, they move forward the same number of spaces as the number on the Advance Space.

==In {{series|Mario Party}}==
===''Mario Party: Island Tour''===
[[File:Dash Space MPIT 1.png|thumb|left|A +1 Dash Space in ''Mario Party: Island Tour'']]
'''Dash Spaces''' appear in every board except [[Banzai Bill's Mad Mountain]] and [[Rocket Road]]. In [[Bowser's Peculiar Peak]], they are known as '''Extra-Move Spaces''', although these have a negative effect because they move whoever lands on it closer to Bowser by a few spaces.

===''[[Mario Party 10]]''===
Advance Spaces are only in the [[Bowser Party]] mode, retaining the same function as in ''Mario Party: Island Tour''.

===''[[Mario Party: Star Rush]]''===
Advance Spaces make a reappearance, and are featured in the [[Balloon Bash]] and [[Mario Shuffle]] game modes.
