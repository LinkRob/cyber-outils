The '''Banana Ball''' is the [[Star Skill]] of [[Donkey Kong (character)|Donkey Kong]] in ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]'' and the [[Star Pitch]] of [[Diddy Kong]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''.

==Description==
When using Banana Ball while either pitching or batting, the baseball becomes a [[banana]] and curves greatly like a [[boomerang]] while being followed by a glowing yellow trail. In both games of the {{series|Mario Baseball}}, if a Banana Ball is pitched, the banana curves greatly from the batter before curving past them, so the batter must have precise timing to hit the banana. While batting a Banana Ball, it curves greatly across the field, making it hard for fielders to catch.

==In {{series|Mario Baseball}}==
===''Mario Superstar Baseball''===
In ''Mario Superstar Baseball'', the Banana Ball functions the same as Diddy Kong's [[Boomerang Ball]]. In [[Challenge Mode (Mario Superstar Baseball)|Challenge Mode]], if Donkey Kong is either the team captain or on the player's team, he can only use Banana Ball once the player has obtained the [[King Banana]].

===''Mario Super Sluggers''===
In ''Mario Super Sluggers'', the Banana Ball is usable by only Diddy Kong as a pitching move. The Banana Ball functions identically to Diddy's Boomerang Ball in ''Mario Superstar Baseball'', and thus Donkey Kong's Banana Ball while pitching.
