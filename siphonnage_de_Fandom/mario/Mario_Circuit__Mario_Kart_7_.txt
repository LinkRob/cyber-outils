'''Mario Circuit''' is the second track in the [[Flower Cup]] of ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. It later made a reappearance in ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]''.

==Layout==
The course begins with long, winding turns with flowers, grass, and pink-leaved trees lining the track. Racers will then bounce off of a [[Trampoline Mushroom|mushroom]] and continue on until they enter [[Princess Peach's Castle]], which was remodeled to accommodate the [[Grand Prix]]. After drifting through the spiraled passageway in the castle, racers will launch off of a [[glider]] ramp.

After that, there will be a dirt path with pipes, two of which have air that can boost your gliding. The dirt path has [[Giant Goomba]]s, which drop a [[Dash Mushroom|Mushroom]] upon being destroyed. There is a white ramp with a coin at the end that is best accessed gliding. After that, there is a pipe tunnel, similar to [[Mario Raceway]] in ''[[Mario Kart 64]]''. After that, the racers will emerge, leap over a small incline, and return to the race line.
