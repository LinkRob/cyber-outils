'''King Yellow Coin Coffers''' are Light/Light attribute enemies and are found as early as World 7-5, and may also appear in World 8-9. A King Yellow Coin Coffer is the boss of Special World course ★World 5-4, and Course 4 of the Fixed Challenge mode in Score Attack. As teammates, their Awoken Skill is '''Light Boost''', and may provide RCV+ bonuses when depowered.
