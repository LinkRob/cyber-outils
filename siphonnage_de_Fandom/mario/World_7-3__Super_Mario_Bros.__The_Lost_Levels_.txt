'''World 7-3''' is the third [[level]] of [[World 7 (Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels)|World 7]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros.: The Lost Levels]]'' and the twenty-seventh level overall in said game. In the original and ''[[Super Mario Bros. Deluxe]]'' versions, the level has a gray palette typically used in castles, whereas it uses normal grassland tiles in ''[[Super Mario All-Stars]]''.

==Layout==
World 7-3 consists of small platforms with [[Super Spring]]s placed on several of them. [[Wind]] will also be blowing throughout the level, making landings difficult. The third Super Spring has a [[Koopa Paratroopa]] above it that the player needs to avoid as they time their jump. After the fourth Super Spring are a group of platforms and pipes with [[Piranha Plant]]s. Halfway through the level, after the fifth Super Spring, is a [[? Block]] with a power-up. After the sixth Super Spring is a [[scale]] lift, and after the seventh Super Spring are small platforms with [[Fire Bar]]s and a Koopa Paratroopa between them before the [[Goal Pole]].
