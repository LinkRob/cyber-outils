The '''Purupuru''' is an enemy in ''[[Donkey Kong (Game Boy)|Donkey Kong]]'' for [[Game Boy]]. They are found in the Iceberg.

== Profile ==
=== Physical description ===
According to their artwork, Purupurus have greyish-white bodies with circular white eyes and black pupils and a grey mouth with white fangs, fin-like arms, and grey feet. Their design is akin to a seal.

=== Behavior ===
A Purupuru walks in circles around the environment at a moderate speed. If [[Mario (character)|Mario]] comes into contact with them, he will be pushed way. Mario can ride on them, but cannot pick them up or defeat them.

==In ''Donkey Kong'' (Game Boy)==
The Purupuru appears in Stage 7-3.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The Purupuru was named after the Japanese ideophonic word meaning "jiggly." The name of the enemy matches the Japanese name of the [[Wubba]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
