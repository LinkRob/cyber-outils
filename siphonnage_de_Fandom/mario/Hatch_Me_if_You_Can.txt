The '''Hatch Me if You Can''', called the '''Whichever Egg''' in Japanese, is a [[Special Attack]] of the [[Bros. Attack]] type that [[Mario]] can use in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]''. It costs 7 [[Special Point]]s to use, with its icon being a [[Yoshi Egg]].

== Procedure ==
Mario starts the attack by pulling the Yoshi Egg used in the attack from his pocket, and then tosses it high into the air. The egg will then fall down, so either Mario or Luigi (depending on which side the egg lands) has to hit it back into the air, followed by the opposite Bro. After the second hit, Mario and Luigi have to smash the egg into the target. A success causes the egg to break, after which a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshisaur]] will pop out and slam the enemy. The attack targets one enemy, despite the description having the targeting descriptor "All enemies."

== Behind the Scenes ==

=== Etymology ===
The attack's name comes from the phrase "Catch me if you can!"
