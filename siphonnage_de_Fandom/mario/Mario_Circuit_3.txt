Mario Circuit 3 is the fifth and final course of the [[Flower Cup]] in ''[[Super Mario Kart]]''. It is a tarmac circuit set in a grassland.

It reappears in ''[[Mario Kart: Super Circuit]]'' as the second course of the [[Extra Lightning Cup]], ''[[Mario Kart Wii]]'' as the first course of the [[Lightning Cup]], ''[[Mario Kart Tour]]'' starting from the [[Paris Tour (2019)|2019 Paris Tour]], and the [[Booster Course Pass]] of ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]'' as the second course of the [[Turnip Cup]].{{Mario Kart stub}}
