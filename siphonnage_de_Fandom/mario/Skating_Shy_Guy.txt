'''Skating Shy Guys''' are a variant of [[Shy Guy]]s which mainly appear in the {{series|Mario Kart}}. They first appear in ''[[Mario Kart: Double Dash!!]]''.

==Description==
Skating Shy Guys are ice skaters, hence their name. They can either be found ice skating in icy areas, serving as a hazard, or as goalies in ice hockey.

==In {{series|Mario Kart}}==
===''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!''===
Skating Shy Guys are featured as obstacles in [[Sherbet Land (Mario Kart: Double Dash!!)|Sherbet Land]], where they ice skate among the path and occasionally twirl into the air. If a racer drives into a Skating Shy Guy, they briefly spin out.

There are two color variations of Skating Shy Guys, blue and red. The only difference between them is that red Skating Shy Guys are always found together as a pair while blue Skating Shy Guys are always found skating alone. The course select icon for Sherbet Land depicts a blue Skating Shy Guy to the right and two red Skating Shy Guys to the left, in a further distance.

===''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' / ''[[Mario Kart 8 Deluxe]]''===
Skating Shy Guys return in <small>GCN</small> Sherbet Land, acting the same as in the course's ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' iteration.

==In {{series|Mario & Sonic}}==
===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]'' ([[Nintendo DS]])===
In the [[Ice Hockey (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for Nintendo DS)|Ice Hockey]] and [[Fever Hockey]] events, two Shy Guys wearing ice skates (although not explicitly defined as the Skating Shy Guys from ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'') serve as a goalie, one for each team. The color of the Shy Guy's robe matches the one associated with the team.

===''[[Mario & Sonic at the Sochi 2014 Olympic Winter Games]]''===
The Skating Shy Guys retain their role from the Nintendo DS version of ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games''.
