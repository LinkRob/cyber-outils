'''Gates''' are a mini-boss in ''[[Wario Land: Shake It!]]''. They appear in the underwater levels, guarding a [[Merfle Barrel]]. To destroy a Gate, [[Wario]], in the [[Subwarine]], has to repeatedly shoot missiles at it.
