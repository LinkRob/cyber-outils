'''Swimming''' is a recurring basic move in the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] and related media, especially the platforming games. It is first used in ''[[Super Mario Bros.]]''. The move allows for a character to move around underwater, although at varying speeds depending on the character or even power-up.
