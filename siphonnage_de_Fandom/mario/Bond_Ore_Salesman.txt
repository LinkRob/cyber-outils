The '''Bond Ore Salesman''' is a minor antagonist from [[Mario & Luigi: Brothership|''Mario & Luigi: Brothership'']]. He can be encountered in a side quest where [[Dorrie]] has become sick. The Bond Ore Salesman appears on [[Desolatt Island]].

== Profile ==

=== Physical Appearance ===
The Bond Ore Salesman is a tan-skinned Concordian. He wears a brown and green sleeveless top and dark brown trousers whose colour matches the back of his head. His eyes are dark brown, and an antenna sticks up out of the top of his head.

=== Personality ===
Quite uncharacteristically of most Concordians, who tend to be helpful, the Bond Ore Salesman is a sleazeball, quickly becoming a thorn in the heroes' side by overcharging his wares, and rather cowardly. As a result, the [[IDLE]] gang threatens to call more monsters to attack him unless they get the Bond Ore. However, his cowardly nature ultimately results in the gang getting his wares before the salesman runs off anyway.

== History ==

=== In ''[[Mario & Luigi: Brothership]]'' ===
The Bond Ore Salesman appears in the side quest It's Dorrie!, where [[Dorrie]] has become sick, but his injuries can be treated with Bond Ore. [[Mario (character)|Mario]] and [[Luigi]] head over to [[Desolatt Island]] to find the Bond Ore. The salesman refuses to part with his wares on the pretense that [[Princess Peach]] (who also came to Desolatt for the Bond Ore) is royalty (and therefore too rich to just pay five [[Coin|coins]]). She refuses, prompting him to try to claim her [[Peach's Crown|crown]]. Mario and Luigi attempt to buy the Bond Ore instead, much to the salesman's ire, only for him to come to the conclusion that they want in on this, which prompts the salesman to push his price up to 99 thousand coins. If the Bros. decline (or if they have too little money), the salesman becomes disappointed by the fact that they are broke as well. However, he gets caught by a [[Snaptor]], which has to be chased after and fought in a miniboss-esque scripted encounter (much to the group's horror). This particular Snaptor is of Level 15 with equal stats to the ones on [[Lottacoins Island]], albeit dropping Desolatt Scraps with a drop rate of 29% rather than Lottacoins Levers, and calls another identical Snaptor to the fight along with two [[Drillby|Drillbies]].

After the Bros. defeat the monsters, Ireen threatens to summon another Snaptor if the salesman does not hand over the Bond Ore. He complies, but then flees the scene. Mario, Luigi, Peach, Ireen, and Loog head back to [[Shipshape Island]] where they treat Dorrie's illness and he joins IDLE, gaining a similar scarf to Loog's.
