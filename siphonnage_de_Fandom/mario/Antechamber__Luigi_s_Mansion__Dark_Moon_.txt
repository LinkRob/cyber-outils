The '''Antechamber''' is a room in ''[[Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon]]''.

==Description==
The Antechamber is a room in the [[Old Clockworks]] consisting of a large hallway with three alcoves. There are several thick [[Spider Web]]s along the path, and the [[Tomb]] is at the end of the hallway. The Antechamber can be entered by taking the east door inside of the [[Quarry]].

==In ''Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon''==
During C-2: [[Underground Expedition]], [[Luigi]] has to go through the Antechamber to enter the Tomb at the end. Luigi has to pick up a torch with the [[Poltergust 5000]] to burn the spider webs along the way. Some [[treasure]]s sit within the alcoves: the first alcove has a [[Treasure Chest]] filled with [[coin]]s, [[bill]]s, and [[Gold Bar]]s as well as a Gold Bar inside a small hole; the second alcove has two Gold Bars and a spider web blocking off some [[Heart (item)|hearts]]; and the third alcove has four [[Gold Spider]]s.
