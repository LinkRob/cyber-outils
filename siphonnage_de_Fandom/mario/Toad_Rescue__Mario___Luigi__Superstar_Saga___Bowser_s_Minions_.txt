'''Toad Rescue''' is the ninth level of [[Teehee Valley]] in the ''[[Minion Quest: The Search for Bowser]]'' campaign of ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. The level of the enemy squad is 24.

== Layout ==
The 1st battle contains 3 [[Hammer Bro]]s, 4 [[Fire Bro]]s, and a [[Chargin' Chuck]]. The enemy captain is the Chargin' Chuck, and he knows [[Deny]].

The 2nd battle contains 1 Chargin' Chuck, 5 Fire Bros, and 1 [[Koopa Paratroopa|Koopa Paratroopa (Red)]]. The enemy captain is the Koopa Paratroopa, and he knows [[Rally]] and Deny.

The 3rd battle contains 3 Chargin' Chucks, and 5 Fire Bros. The enemy captain is a Chargin' Chuck, and he knows [[Chance]] and Deny.

The 4th battle contains 1 Chargin' Chuck, 3 Koopa Paratroopas (Red), and 4 Fire Bros. The enemy captain is a Koopa Paratroopa, and he knows Chance and Deny.

The final battle contains 2 Chargin' Chuck, 2 [[Spike]]s, 2 Fire Bros, 1 [[Magikoopa|Magikoopa (Red)]], and [[Morton Koopa Jr.|Morton]]. The enemy captain is Morton, and he knows Chance, Deny, and [[Call for Backup]]. Call for Backup brings a Koopa Paratroopa (Red).

After the final battle, the player recruits Morton (Lv. 24)

== Possible recruits ==
* [[Boomerang Bro]] (Lv. 24)
* Koopa Paratroopa (Red), (Lv. 24)
* Chargin' Chuck
* Fire Bro
* Hammer Bro (Lv. 24)
* Magikoopa (Red) (Lv. 24)
* Spike (Lv. 24)

== Trivia ==
* While there are no Boomerang Bros in the level, they can still be recruited.
* This is the first level to contain 5 battles.
