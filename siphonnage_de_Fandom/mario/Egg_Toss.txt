'''Egg Toss''' is a minigame in ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''.

==Gameplay==
The player takes control of a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], who has to shoot [[Yoshi Egg|eggs]] at targets floating above them. There are [[Egg Block]]s that the Yoshi can use to refill his supply of eggs.

Egg Toss has an Easy Mode and a Hard Mode, which can be unlocked by collecting all seven [[character coin]]s. In Easy Mode, Yoshi has thirty seconds to hit seven targets, while a few [[Goonie]]s try to interfere with him. In Hard Mode, Egg Toss's [[time limit]] was doubled to sixty seconds, or a minute, but what adds to the difficulty are the many [[Piranha Plant]]s, more Goonies, a [[Tap-Tap]], and two more targets for Yoshi to destroy.

Once every target is destroyed, the minigame ends, and Yoshi is awarded a number of [[1-Up]]s, with more being rewarded if he has managed to destroy every target sooner.
