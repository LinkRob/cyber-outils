==''Mario'' game appearances==
===''Mario Party'' series===
[[File:Vine with Me MP3.png|thumb|[[Wario]] and [[Luigi]] swinging on vines during Vine with Me]]
Hanging vines are a major focus of the duel minigame [[Vine with Me]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]'', where players swing over a forest floor inhabited by [[Piranha Plant]]s. Whoever reaches the end of the course first wins the minigame. Vine with Me subsequently appears in ''[[Mario Party: The Top 100]]'' and ''[[Mario Party Superstars]]''.

Vines are also the focus of the DK minigame [[Vine Country]] in ''[[Mario Party 7]]'', where the goal is to reach the top before [[Donkey Kong]]. Vine Country also appears in ''Mario Party: The Top 100''.

===''Luigi's Mansion 3''===
Swinging vines appear in the [[Garden Suites]] of ''[[Luigi's Mansion 3]]'', where Luigi must use them to reach opposing part so the room due to the destroyed staircase.
