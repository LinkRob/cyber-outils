A '''Triple Swing Lift''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario World]]''.

==Description==
A Triple Swing Lift is a type of [[lift]]. It is more specifically like a [[Single Swing Lift]], except a Triple Swing Lift is composed of three gray platforms, each attached to the pivot point, which is usually a [[Gray Block]], from a few light gray, circular segments. The direction that a Triple Swing Lift rotates in depends on which side [[Mario]] or [[Luigi]] is standing on, so it rotates clockwise if they are on the right side or counterclockwise if they are on the left side.
