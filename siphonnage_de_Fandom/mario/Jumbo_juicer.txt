The '''jumbo juicer''' is an object in ''[[Super Mario Sunshine]]''

== Description ==
The jumbo juicer is a giant pair of endlessly spinning cross-shaped juicer blades, which are zigzagged, making them sharp. The jumbo juicer processes [[watermelon]]s into smoothies. It is integrated into the roof of the [[Surf Cabana]] in [[Gelato Beach]], an anomaly for most juicer blades, which are used inside of blenders. The jumbo juicer is hazardous because if [[Mario]] touches it, he loses a unit of health.

==In ''Super Mario Sunshine''==
The jumbo juicer normally blocks the gap leading to the glass container inside the Surf Cabana, which contains the [[Shine Sprite]] of the eighth [[Mission|episode]], [[The Watermelon Festival]]. Mario can bypass the jumbo juicer and obtain the Shine Sprite by carefully pushing the giant watermelon across the [[bridge]] to the Surf Cabana, where it automatically enters the juicer; this process not only turns the watermelon into smoothies but also releases the Shine Sprite.

The jumbo juicer on the roof also serves another purpose. At some point, Mario must bring any type of [[fruit]] (excluding watermelons) to drop it into the jumbo juicer, which releases a hidden [[Blue Coin]].
