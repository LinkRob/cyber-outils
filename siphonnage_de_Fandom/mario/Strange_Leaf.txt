The '''Strange Leaf''' is an item which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
This very rare leaf is found only in the deepest reaches of the [[Forever Forest]]. At the far east exit of the forest, the Strange Leaf is found in the bush by the gateway that leads to [[Gusty Gulch]]. Eating a Strange Leaf restores 5 [[Flower Point|FP]]. It is also used in several recipes such as the [[Strange Cake]] or the [[Deluxe Feast]].

==Trivia==
*The Strange Leaf's appearance is very similar to that of a [[Stinky Herb]].
