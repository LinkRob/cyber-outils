'''DK Jungle''' is the first course in [[Special Cup]] in ''[[Mario Kart 7]]''. The course takes place in [[Jungle Hijinxs]], and is based on ''[[Donkey Kong Country Returns]]''. As the name applies, this course is owned by [[Donkey Kong]].

[[w:c:donkeykong:DK's House|DK's House]] is seen here, along with enemies like [[w:c:donkeykong:Tiki Goon|Tiki Goons]] and [[w:c:donkeykong:Screaming Pillar|Screaming Pillars]]. [[w:c:donkeykong:DK Barrel|DK Barrels]] can be found in the course, as well as frogs similar to [[w:c:donkeykong:Frogoon|Frogoons]], which can be seen when approaching the [[w:c:donkeykong:Golden Temple|Golden Temple]]. Like all other tracks, there is an area in the course where [[Glide|gliding]] is required, where the driver glides over a lake. If he or she falls, they will end up driving underwater until reaching shore again, which can slow the player down a little bit.

The song is based on the Jungle Hijinxs theme song; arguably the most famous ''[[Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' song.

The track returns in ''[[Mario Kart 8]]'' as the last track of the [[Banana Cup]].
