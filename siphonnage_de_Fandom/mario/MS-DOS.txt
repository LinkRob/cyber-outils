The '''MS-DOS''' (short for '''Microsoft Disk Operating System''') is an old computer system created by Microsoft that was released on August 12, 1981. The latest MS-DOS version is MS-DOS 8.0 for Windows ME and was released on June 19, 2000. Support for all MS-DOS versions before 7.0 was discontinued on December 31, 2006; however, MS-DOS versions 7.1 and 8.0 were discontinued on July 11, 2006.

Various ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' edutainment titles were released for it in the early to mid 1990s, such as ''[[Mario Teaches Typing]]'', as well as on [[PC]]s from back then in general.
