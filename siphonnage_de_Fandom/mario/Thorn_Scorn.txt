The '''Thorn Scorn''' is a [[Blaster (Primary weapon)|Blaster]] in ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]''. Mario can use the Thorn Scorn.

== Description ==
In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle|Kingdom Battle]]'', Thorn Scorn resembles a hand cannon. Like most Blaster weapons, it is an all-around weapon with average power and range. The weapon has medium cover damage and the user can attack from a medium range. The Thorn Scorn deals around 95 to 105 damage.

It has a low chance of triggering the Super Effect. However, there is a high chance that the Super Effect can trigger when aiming at [[Valkyrie]]s. When triggering the Super Effect, it can inflict [[Bounce (status effect)|Bounce]] dealing 131 damage.

== In ''[[Mario + Rabbids Kingdom Battle]]'' ==
Mario can use the Thorn Scorn as an option among the Blasters. He can obtain it when the player completes the Red Coin Challenge near the music area at [[Spooky Trails]].
