'''Rainbow Ball''' is the [[Star Pitch]] of [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] in ''[[Mario Super Sluggers]]''. It replaces his [[Egg Ball]] from ''[[Mario Superstar Baseball]]''.

==Description==
Upon initiating Rainbow Ball, Yoshi creates a rainbow path for the baseball to travel on. While traveling down the path, the baseball is invisible but becomes visible upon reaching the end. The batter has to be attentive to when the baseball reappears, otherwise they could risk getting a strike.
