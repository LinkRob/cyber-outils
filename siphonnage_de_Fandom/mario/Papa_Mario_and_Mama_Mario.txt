'''Papa Mario and Mama Mario''' are the father and mother of the [[Mario Bros. (team)|Mario Brothers]]. [[Yoshi]] delivered Mario and Luigi to their parents when they were babies, leaving them in front of their home doorstep.

==History==

=== [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'']] series ===

==== ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''/''Yoshi's Island: Super Mario Advance 3'' ====
Mario and Luigi's mother and father make their first video game debut appearance in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]'', both of whom are only seen their her lower half and hands. Once [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] rescues the [[stork]] and [[Baby Luigi]], the stork takes [[Baby Mario]] and Baby Luigi and goes to deliver them to their parents. The stork flies to an unnamed town surrounded by mountains and leaves the baby brothers on the doorstep of a [[mushroom]]-shaped house. The parents awake, turn on a light, open the door, and happily hold up Baby Mario and Baby Luigi, the latter of whom is held by Mama Mario.

==== ''Yoshi's Island DS'' ====
Mama Mario and Papa Mario make a short appearance in the opening sequence, featuring a short retelling of ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'', in which Baby Mario and Baby Luigi are seen in the hands of one of their parents.

==== ''Yoshi's New Island'' ====
The game takes place directly between after the events of ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island'' and before ''[[Yoshi's Island DS]]''. In the opening cutscene, it is revealed that the stork had delivered Mario and Luigi to the wrong parents. In the ending, the stork delivers the Mario Bros. to their real mother and father.
