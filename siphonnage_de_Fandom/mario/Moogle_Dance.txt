'''Moogle Dance''' is the [[Special Shot]] of [[Moogle]] in ''[[Mario Hoops 3-on-3]]''.

==Description==
The player can have Moogle perform the move by tapping out an "M" on the Touch Screen twice. Once this is done, Moogle initiates the shot by bouncing on the ball five times. It then rolls around in a circle on the ball twice. Moogle then takes the ball, which is now glowing white, and casts it toward the basket. When the ball lands into the hoop, multicolored [[fireworks]] appear.

If Moogle uses Moogle Dance outside the three-point line, the visuals are still the same, but a teammate is required to catch the ball and dunk it.

==Behind the scenes==
===Etymology===
The move is actually the [[w:c:finalfantasy:Moogle Dance|Moogle Dance]] move, first introduced as the [[w:c:finalfantasy:Dance (Final Fantasy VI)|Dance]] ability in ''[[w:c:finalfantasy:Final Fantasy VI|Final Fantasy VI]]''.
