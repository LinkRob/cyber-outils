'''Bellabel Park''' is the main setting of [[Super Mario Bros. Wonder – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Meetup in Bellabel Park|Meetup in Bellabel Park]], a part of ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''<nowiki/>'s Nintendo Switch 2 Edition. It was originally in ruin but was discovered by [[Captain Toad]] and then restored.<ref>Captain Toad & Talking Flower amiibo Detail<nowiki/>https://www.nintendo.com/us/amiibo/detail/captain-toad-and-talking-flower-super-mario-bros-wonder-series</ref> Inside Bellabel Park, players can play attractions in which they either play against each other or with each other.

== Sections ==

=== Attraction Central ===
[[File:Bellabel Park.png|left|thumb|153x153px|Bellabel Park in-game]]
Attraction Central is shaped exactly like a [[Bellabel Flower]]. You can play co-op attractions where you work together with other players or VS attractions, where you work against them. It is split into two major sections at the top. On the left is Local Multiplayer Plaza, in which you can play with up to four others on your same system or with GameShare. Local Multiplayer Plaza has at least 11 attractions, all manned by a [[Poplin]]:
