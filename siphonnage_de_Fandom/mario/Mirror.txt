'''Mirrors''' are generic objects featured in some ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' titles. One of its earliest prominent appearances is in ''[[Super Mario 64]]''.

==Description==
A mirror is a reflective surface that displays the reverse image of what is in front of it. As such, mirrors are typically used for personal grooming.

==History==
===''Super Mario'' series===
====''Super Mario 64''====
In ''[[Super Mario 64]]'', there is a large mirror against the northern wall of a room on the second floor of the [[Mushroom Castle (Super Mario 64)|Mushroom Castle]]. It shows a visible reflection of a [[Lakitu Bro]]. While the [[painting]] to [[Snowman's Land]] is hidden on the left wall, its painting is visible on the reflection of the mirror.

====''Super Mario 64 DS''====
''[[Super Mario 64 DS]]'' retains the same mirror on the second floor as the original version. This time, [[Luigi]] can use a [[Power Flower (Super Mario 64 DS)|Power Flower]] on a pedestal within the room to become [[Vanish Luigi]] and pass through the mirror to the other side, necessary for collecting a secret [[Power Star]] in a blank white room by entering the door into the room through the mirror's reflection and entering [[Chief Chilly Challenge]].

The first mirror is now found in the arena at the end of [[Big Boo Battle]]. [[Mario]] must look into the mirror for [[King Boo|Big Boo]] to appear, although he is only visible on the mirror.

===''Mario Kart'' series===
In the ''[[Mario Kart]]'' series, starting with ''[[Mario Kart 64]]'', the [[Mirror Mode]] is represented by a mirror as all the courses are mirrored.

===''Luigi's Mansion''===
[[File:Storage Room LM.png|thumb|left|200px|''Luigi's Mansion'' screenshot of the Storage Room's mirror, in which the switch for moving the wall can be seen]]
In ''[[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo GameCube)|Luigi's Mansion]]'' and its [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Luigi's Mansion (Nintendo 3DS)|version]], if [[Luigi]] examines a mirror using his [[Game Boy Horror]], its reflection begins to rotate, light-blue markings are shown from the Game Boy Horror display, then Luigi starts to spin, and is [[warp]]ed back to the [[Foyer (Luigi's Mansion)|Foyer]]. These mirrors can be used as shortcuts or escape routes when necessary instead of walking through the entire mansion. Nearly nearly every mirror functions identically, except the room-length mirror in the [[Mirror Room]], as there is a puzzle where Luigi has to use the [[Fire Element Medal]] to light the candles to unblock the door. There are some other rooms with a mirror, such as the [[Billiards Room]], which has a mirror nearly the size of the Mirror Room's, and the [[Safari Room]], where using the mirror is especially during the [[blackout]], as it can allow Luigi to skip several [[Ghost (Luigi's Mansion series)|ghost]] encounters. The first room to actually have a mirror is the Foyer, but examining its reflection only warps Luigi back to the same place. Both the [[Wardrobe Room]], [[Master Bedroom (Luigi's Mansion)|Master Bedroom]], and [[Storage Room (Luigi's Mansion)|Storage Room]] also have a mirror. In the Storage Room's case,

The [[Sealed Room]] is the only room where using the mirror is required, because the door is boarded up and Luigi cannot climb up the chimney, which is the only way to enter the Sealed Room. As such, Luigi has to use the mirror to escape the room. Despite this necessity, the game provides no hints about the hidden purpose of the mirrors.
