'''Game Guy Space''' is a [[space]] in ''[[Mario Party 3]]''.

==Description==
Game Guy Spaces are fairly rare on [[Party Mode]] boards but are common on [[Duel Mode]] boards. If a player lands on a Game Guy Space, [[Game Guy]] shows up, takes away the player's [[coin]]s, and forces them to play a [[Mini-game#Game Guy Mini-game|Game Guy Mini-game]]. The minigames are mostly luck-based, determining whether the player receives more coins back than before, usually double the original number, or if they lose every coin.

Landing on a Game Guy Space is one of three ways to play a Game Guy Mini-game, with the other two methods being to use a [[Lucky Charm]] or by accessing [[Game Guy's Room]] from Mini-game Mode, after clearing Story Mode.
