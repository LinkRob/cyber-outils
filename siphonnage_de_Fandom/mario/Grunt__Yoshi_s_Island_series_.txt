A '''Grunt''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. They are a relative of a [[Bandit]] who wear spiked helmets instead of masks. They appear in the levels, [[Marching Milde's Fort]] and [[Scary Skeleton Goonies!]]. All they do is to run back and forth. A [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]] can knock off their helmets by throwing an [[Yoshi Egg|egg]] at them, exposing the Grunt's lemon-shaped head. Whenever a Grunt is missing its helmet, it looks embarrassed and frantically runs back and forth. A Yoshi can swallow a Grunt and convert it into an egg. Somehow, whenever Yoshi spits a Grunt back out, it always gets back its spiked helmet.
