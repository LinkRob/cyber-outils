'''Red Card!''' is [[Diddy Kong]]'s [[Super Ability]] in ''[[Mario Strikers Charged]]''.

==Description==
Diddy can only use Red Card! when using his Special Item, which is depicted as his own head. Upon using the item, Diddy summons a beam of light in front of him, causing other characters who walk into it, regardless of team, to be sucked into the air and disappear for at least 15 seconds. In the rare chance of all opponents being caught within the beam of light, Diddy can perform his [[Mega Strike]] without any disruptions.

==Etymology==
Red Card! is named after "red card," a type of penalty card commonly used in football.
