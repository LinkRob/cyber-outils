'''Pi'illo Blimport''' is an area of [[Pi'illo Island]] that appears in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Dream Team]]''. It is the first area of Pi'illo Island that [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and the others visit.

== Description ==
Pi'illo Blimport is mainly a tourist site with many stands around. Many [[Brock|Brocks]] are also seen on here.

==History==
Mario, Luigi, [[Princess Peach]], and the others all finally land on Pi'illo Island of the [[Pi'illo Kingdom]]. They arrive at Pi'illo Blimport. Once they land, Luigi wakes up from a nightmare and falls unconscious on the ground. Later, they are greeted by [[Broque Monsieur]]&nbsp;who is evil at first, but after he would gladly be their tour guide. He plays a short game with Mario and the rest, and then soon leads everyone to [[Pi'illo Castle]].88
