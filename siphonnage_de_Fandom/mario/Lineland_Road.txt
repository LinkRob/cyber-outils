'''Lineland Road''' is a location in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is the first area in [[Lineland]] and serves as the main setting for Chapter 1-1.

==Layout==
Lineland Road appears as a plains-themed stage with many grass and hills around.

==History==
===Chapter 1-1: The Adventure Unfolds===
[[Mario]] and [[Tippi]] officially begin their adventure on Lineland Road. After fighting through some enemies, they eventually end up at [[Bestovius]]' house where he is actually charging 10,000 for the [[Flip|dimensional flip]] ability. Mario manages to talk down Bestovius to teaching the technique for free and is able to use Flip whenever he pleases. With this new ability, Mario and Tippi are able to make it through the rest of the level until they reach [[Mount Lineland]].
