The '''shop''' is run by [[Toad]] where the player can buy buildings and decorations in their Mushroom Kingdom. It appears in ''[[Super Mario Run]]''.

== Description ==
In ''[[Super Mario Run]]'', Toad runs the shop. The player can go to the shop to buy buildings and decorations. Objects are categorized into Building, Decoration and Special. Buildings may have criteria in order to unlock the buildings.
