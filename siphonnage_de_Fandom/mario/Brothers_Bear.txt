'''Brothers Bear''' (also known as '''Bear Family''') are a group of male friendly [[Bear]]s with the same bloodline which live in separate houses scattered across the lands from [[Northern Kremisphere|Kong Country]] and different functions.
==History==
===Background information===
Prior to the events of the game series, some older brothers provided services until they retired, for example: [[Barnacle]] was a sailor, [[Bazooka]] is a military veteran who fought in the [[List of implied events#Kremean War|Kremean War]] and [[Baffle]] was an '''F'''unky '''B'''ear '''I'''nvestigators agent.<ref>"'''Barnacle:''' ''Yes, I used to be a scuba diver but I've given it up.''" - ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble''</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 122 - "'''''BARNACLE BEAR:''' The old salty sailor bear, Barnacle, has retired to an island cabin in Lake Orangatanga, where he putters away his time with his extensive collection of sea shells.''"</ref><ref>"'''Bazooka:''' ''I fought in the Kremean War, don't you know, but I've retired from the service along with 'Big Bessie'.''" - ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble''</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 123 - "'''''BRIGADIER BAZOOKA BEAR:''' Kremean War veteran Bazooka Bear has been posted to the barracks on Mekanos to command the old artillery piece, Big Bessie.''"</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 125 - "'''''BAFFLE BEAR:''' Baffle Bears is retired agent of the FBI (Funky Bear Investigators) who now whiles away his time cracking codes and developing ciphers.''"</ref> Other brothers who are young started their professions, as: [[Bazaar]] is a shopkeeper, [[Brash]] is a athlete, [[Blunder]] is a librarian, [[Bramble]] is a botanist, [[K3]] is a climber, [[Barter]] is a pawnbroker, [[Benny & Björn]] are chairlifts owners and [[Boomer]] is a demolisher.<ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 122 - "'''''BRASH BEAR:''' Brash doesn't want anything from you, except perhaps for you to bow down and acknowledge him as greast athlete in the Northern Kremisphere.''"</ref> More other brothers as [[Blue]] and [[Bachelor]] has no professional occupations.

The quarrel between brothers was with Boomer, because several brothers exiled him to the underground for being considered dangerous due to his trajectory of destruction with bombs.<ref>"'''Boomer:''' ''The rest of the folks have stuck me up in here. Something about me being too dangerous or something.''" - ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble''</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 125 - "'''''BOOMER BEAR:''' Boomer Bear has been exiled to the underworld because of the destructive nature of his "toys".''"</ref>

In ''[[Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble!]]'', several brothers are visited by [[Dixie Kong]] and [[Kiddy Kong]] where they asked for services to the pair. Some of them suffer from the activities of the [[Kremling Kreeps]], others are received by [[Banana Bird]]s to take refuge in their homes and many need someone to help in the fulfillment of their tasks.<ref>"'''Bramble:''' ''Yes, I moved up here to get away from the smell of the machines, but it's been too dry to grow anything.''" - ''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble''</ref><ref>''Donkey Kong Country 3: Dixie Kong's Double Trouble'' Player's Guide, Nintendo, 1996, p. 5 - "'''''RAZOR RIDGE:''' One of the krueler Kremlings even disabled Björn Bear's Chairlifts!''"</ref> Their services were of great importance to Dixie and Kiddy's journey to save the Banana Birds and defeat [[K. Rool]] for the last time.

In ''[[Donkey Kong Land III]]'', only a Brother Bear appears.

==Profiles==
===''Donkey Kong Country 3: Diddy Kong's Double Trouble!''===
*<u>'''SNES Instruction booklet'''</u> (page 20): ''This time, in addition to being helped by the Kongs and your animal buddies, you can call on the services of the Brothers Bear, who live in cabins all around the world and sub-worlds. There are thirteen of these friendly characters, and each one of them offers you something totally different.''
*<u>'''SNES Player's Guide'''</u> (page 120): ''One of the features that sets Donkey Kong Country 3 apart from the previous games in the series is the exploration you need to do on the world map. In addition to going from place to place on the map, you'll need to go off the beaten track and interact with some of the inabitants of the Northern Kremisphere. The most prominent citizens are, of course, the Brothers Bear. You'll have to barter a wide variety of baubles with this band of bears to find everything in the game.''
*<u>'''''Nintendo Power'' vol. 89'''</u> (page 33): ''Business is hardly bearish for the bear family on the islands around Kong Country. Shop their stores and listen to their tales. A few give you advice or information while others need help solving a problem. If you assist the bears they'll help you on your quest.''
