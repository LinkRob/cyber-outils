|games = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'' (1996)}}'''Breaker Beam''' is very powerful attack used only by the [[Axem Rangers]] and [[Gunyolk]] in the game ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Description==
When the Breaker Beam is fired, a large, faded white light engulfs the screen.

==Effects==
With Breaker Beam, the foe can attack every one of [[Mario]]'s party for high damage. It can pierce defenses and is considered one of the strongest attacks in the game.

==Trivia==
*After using the Breaker Beam, the Axem Rangers must recharge before using it. Gunyolk, however, doesn't need to recharge to use this attack again.
