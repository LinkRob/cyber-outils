{{Merge|List of Gear in the Mario & Luigi series}}The '''Good Badge''' is a badge that can be obtained by [[Luigi]] in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Bowser's Inside Story]]''. It is found in the Treasure of e'erlasting wonder. This badge allows good to excellent hits to boost the meter. The meter's effect is low.
