'''Icepeak''' is a location in the [[Adventure Tours]] campaign of the [[Nintendo DS]] version of ''[[Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games (Nintendo DS)|Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games]]''.

==Description==
It is a mountain peak with various platforms, and cliffs as well as the home of the [[snow spirits|snow spirit]] [[Icy]].

==In ''Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games'' (Nintendo DS)==
Icepeak is the fifth town visited by [[Mario]] and [[Sonic the Hedgehog|Sonic]]. The cliffs cause them slide down levels in the mountain. The place also features many [[Warp Pipe]]s that take the player to underground chambers. One such chamber actually contains a sleeping Dark Chao, who will feel annoyed if awakened by talking to him. [[Toad]] theorizes that those chambers are where people go to sleep after the Olympic events are over. Should the heroes try talking to the Dark Chao again, [[Amy Rose]] will say they should just walk out silently and leave him be, as a way to apologize, an idea soon supported after they hear the Dark Chao mumbling asleep about them. An area of Icepeak also has a mission hosted by a [[Lakitu]], which can only be taken by [[Princess Daisy]]. Should she complete it, along with another mission in [[Blizland]] (from the same Lakitu), he'll then award them with the Meteor Curling Stone, required for playing [[Curling Bowling]] missions. The snow spirit [[Blizza]] is kept hostage in a cage here. While Blizza is successfully freed after [[Rouge the Bat]] is defeated in [[Figure Skating (Mario & Sonic at the Olympic Winter Games for DS)|Figure Skating]], [[Bowser]] and [[Dr. Eggman]] then immediately kidnap [[Frosty]] and take him to Blizland.
