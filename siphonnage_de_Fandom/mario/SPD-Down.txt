'''SPD-Down''' is a status effect that can hamper your [[Speed]], hence the ailment's name. It is the opposite of [[SPD-Up]], which increases the player's Speed. It appears in the ''[[Mario & Luigi (series)|Mario & Luigi]]'' franchise only. Blue streaks going down or a blue flash with a downward arrow may mark the ailment. Enemies may be hit if [[First Strike|ambushed]] via the hammer, but only in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''.
