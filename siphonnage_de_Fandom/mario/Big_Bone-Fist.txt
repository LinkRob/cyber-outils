'''Big Bone-Fist''' is an enemy in ''[[Wario World]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Big Bone-Fist is large skeleton arm with an attached hand.

===Behavior===
A Big Bone-Fist can use a few different attacks, one of which involves it grabbing [[Wario]] and then throwing him. While Wario is grabbed, the player can wiggle the Control Stick to make him escape. A Big Bone-Fist can also attack by shooting energy rockets and attempting to slam down on Wario.

==In ''Wario World''==
Only two Big Bone-Fists make an appearance, exclusively within [[Horror Manor]]. [[Wario]] must defeat them in order to progress further into the [[level]]. To attack a Big Bone-Fist, Wario must wait for it to do its slam attack and then [[Ground Pound (move)|Ground Pound]] the glowing blue weak spot on the back of Big Bone-Fist's hand. As a Big Bone-Fist takes damage, it fires more energy rockets. A Big Bone-Fist gets defeated once its weak spot has been Ground Pounded three times.
