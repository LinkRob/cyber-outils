'''S. Smash Chg.''' (short for '''Super Smash Charge''') is a [[badge]] which appears in ''[[Paper Mario]]''.

==Effects==
By equipping the S. Smash Chg., [[Mario]] can charge his [[hammer]] attacks to increase its power by three [[point]]s. The S. Smash Chg. requires 4 [[Flower Point|FP]] to use each time, but otherwise allows Mario to use it as many times as he wishes. S. Smash Chg. is a stronger version of [[Smash Charge]], and it can be used alongside it as well. The S. Smash Chg. badge can be bought at [[Rowf's Badge Shop]] for 100 [[coin]]s. This badge is a Jump counterpart to the [[S. Jump Chg.]] badge.
