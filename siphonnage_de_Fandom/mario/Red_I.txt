'''Red I''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''. It is a stronger, red variant of a [[Mr. I]].

==History==
A Red I is only encountered in [[Castle Bleck]], specifically [[Castle Bleck Entry]] and [[Castle Bleck Foyer]]. Red Is, much like Mr. Is, attack by firing a laser beam from its pupil. They cannot be defeated by normal means. The only way to defeat them is to use [[Mario]]'s [[Flip]] to flip into 3-D and run around them to make them dizzy which will defeat the Red I.
