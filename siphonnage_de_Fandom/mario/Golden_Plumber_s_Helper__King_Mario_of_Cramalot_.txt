The '''Golden Plumber's Helper''' is an item in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[King Mario of Cramalot]]."

==Description==
It is a golden plunger that was originally located in the [[Sacred Sink of Cramalot]], within the town of [[Cramalot]]. Legend says that whoever is able to remove the Golden Plumber's Helper from the Sacred Sink of Cramalot receives untold power and is crowned the new king of Cramalot; this legend is likely based on {{w|Excalibur}}.

==In ''The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!''==
At one point, [[Mario (DiC Cartoons)|Mario]] pulls the Golden Plumber's Helper from the Sacred Sink of Cramalot to try and defeat [[King Koopa (DiC Cartoons)|King Koopa]], who later takes it from him. Later, when Mario duels a King Koopa, who is wielding the Golden Plumber's Helper, he frees it from King Koopa's grasp using the [[Excalibur]].
