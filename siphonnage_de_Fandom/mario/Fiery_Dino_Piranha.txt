'''Fiery Dino Piranha''' is a boss fought in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'' and ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. Fiery Dino Piranha is similar in appearance to the original [[Dino Piranha]]. Unlike Dino Piranha's green and purple coloration, however, Fiery Dino Piranha has a red and black coloration. It has a dark red body and a head that is black with dark red spots. Its petals are also similar to those of Dino Piranha, but appear more singed. Fiery Dino Piranha's tail occasionally catches on fire during the fight, leaving a fiery trail in its path that hurts the player if touched. Also, later in the fight, Fiery Dino Piranha can shoot large fireballs at the player. It is also much faster than its normal counterpart, making it harder for [[Mario]] to reach its tail to attack. 

[[Fiery Gobblegut|The fiery version]] of [[Gobblegut]] from ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' is similar to Fiery Dino Piranha, as both are fiery versions of earlier bosses. The ''[[Super Mario Bros. Encyclopedia]]'' also treats the dark-colored [[King Kaliente]] in the same way.
==History==
===''Super Mario Galaxy'' ===
[[File:Fiery Dino Piranha SMG.png|thumb|left|225px|Fiery Dino Piranha]]
In ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', Mario battles Fiery Dino Piranha in the final level of the [[Melty Molten Galaxy]]. The beginning of the battle is much like that of the normal [[Dino Piranha]] fight, with [[Mario]] landing on top of the egg, cracking the egg and waking Fiery Dino Piranha. As Fiery Dino Piranha trots around, the bulb of the tail ignites and extinguishes in a steady rhythm, forcing Mario to wait until the tail is extinguished to attack and break the egg away.

Once it emerges from the egg, it spots Mario and chases him, similar to Dino Piranha. However, the bulb's fire still flickers on and off, still forcing Mario to wait until the tail is not on fire before striking.

After the first strike, Fiery Dino Piranha stomps in anger, and it continues chasing Mario, leaving a trail of fire behind its footsteps. After the second strike, it begins shooting fireballs out of its mouth, making it harder to maneuver around the planet to reach its tail, and the bulb's fire also flickers on and off in a faster pace. After Mario hits the tail a final time, it is defeated.
{{br|left}}

===''Super Mario Galaxy 2''===
[[File:Fierydinopirhanaegg.png|83px|thumb|Fiery Dino Piranha's Egg]]
Fiery Dino Piranha reappears in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]''. It is the last boss Mario must fight in the [[Boss Blitz Galaxy]]. The battle sequence is the same as in ''Super Mario Galaxy''. Since it appears in [[Throwback Throwdown|two]] [[Throwback Throwdown Speed Run|missions]], it is the only boss originating from ''Super Mario Galaxy'' that also appears in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' to be fought more times in the sequel than its predecessor.

If Mario dies while fighting it, he must fight [[Bouldergeist]] again due to the placement of the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. Unlike in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy]]'', heat waves are visible around its egg.

==Trivia==
*The Fiery Dino Piranha fight shares his battle theme with the [[Dino Piranha]] and [[Tarantox]] boss fights.
*The Fiery Dino Piranha is very similar to the second fight agents [[King Kaliente]], in that:
**They both are stronger versions of their normal counter-parts that is fought in [[Good Egg Galaxy]].
**They both are the main boss of a world themed on fire (King Kaliente: [[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor]] Fiery Dino Piranha: [[Melty Molten Galaxy]]).
**They both can launch out fireballs.
**They both serve as the final bosses of their role (King Kaliente: Guarding of the last Grand Star, Fiery Dino Piranha: Is the last Normal Boss Fight of the game).
*Fittingly enough, Dino Piranha is the first boss in ''Super Mario Galaxy'' (unless the reactor in the [[Gateway Galaxy]] is considered as one), while Fiery Dino Piranha is the penultimate boss (The final boss is [[Bowser]] from [[Bowser's Galaxy Reactor]]) of ''Super Mario Galaxy'', creating the feeling of having gone full circle once both have been defeated.
**Interestingly, in ''[[Super Mario Galaxy 2]]'', [[Fiery Gobblegut]] is also not only a stronger version of [[Gobblegut]] who is a boss of the first world, but is also potentially the penultimate boss of the game.
*Fiery Dino Piranha is very similar to [[Fire Gobblegut]], as both bosses are fiery versions of their original bosses: ([[Dino Piranha]] and [[Gobblegut]]), respectively.
*The official Prima guide for ''Super Mario Galaxy'' claims that Fiery Dino Piranha and Dino Piranha are related.
*Oddly enough, Fiery Dino Piranha is fought more in ''Super Mario Galaxy 2'' than in the original ''Super Mario Galaxy''. It is the only boss that returns in the sequel to do this, because the other four bosses in the Boss Blitz Galaxy are fought multiple times in the first game. (The Dino Piranha has to be fought with Speedy Comet conditions in the same galaxy, Major Burrows and the Bouldergeist have to be fought the second time with Daredevil Comet missions, and [[Bowser Jr.]] summons [[King Kaliente]] to his [[Bowser Jr.'s Lava Reactor|Lava Reactor]].
*In ''[[Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games (Wii)|Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games]]'', the original Dino Piranha gains the ability to breath fireballs like Fiery Dino Piranha.
