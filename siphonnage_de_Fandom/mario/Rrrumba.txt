'''Rrrumbas''' are an enemy with a giant breakable rock shell in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''. They only appear in the level [[Where the Rrrumbas Rule|Where the Rrrumbas Rule.]]

==Profile==
===Physical description===
A Rrrumba is a small, insect-like creatures that wear a large, boulder-shaped shell. Sparkling Rrrumbas contain coins or power-ups, and they can be collected when defeated.

===Behavior===
Rrrumbas roll on their shell down slopes. They can be defeated with a [[Ground Pound]] or with [[Drill Mario]].
