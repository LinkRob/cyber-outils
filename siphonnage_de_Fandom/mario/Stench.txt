|games         = ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''}}'''Stench''' is a status move that can be used by [[Straw Head]]s and [[Artichoker|Artichockers]] in ''[[Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]''.

==Effects==
With Stench, the user sprays it's target with green sprinkles causing [[Poison (ailment)|poison]]. This move can be avoided by wearing certain items such as a Safety Pin. This move looks similar in appearance to [[S'crow Funk]].
