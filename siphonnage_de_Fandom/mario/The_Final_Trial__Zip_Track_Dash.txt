'''The Final Trial: Zip Track Dash''' is a level of [[Shining Falls]] in ''[[Super Mario Bros. Wonder]]''.
__NOTOC__
==Layout==
The level features many [[Zip Track]]s. The level opens with a few long Zip Tracks where the player must jump over enemies and other obstacles. Afterwards the player will reach the [[Wonder Flower]]. The [[Wonder Effect]] will move the Zip Track below the player point up and give access to a new area.

===If Wonder Flower is collected===
Most of this path is comprised of Zip Tracks with tall walls and long jumps. The player will eventually be launched into the [[Wonder Seed]] shortly before the [[Goal Pole]]

===If Wonder Flower is not collected===
This path starts with two vertical and parallel Zip Tracks the player must jump across to avoid big [[Smogrin]]s. This is then shortly followed by a free fall into the [[Checkpoint Flag]]. This is then followed by tracks that move on [[track]]s when the player lands on them. Finally, the player must jump on a series of big Smogrin to reach the top of the Goal Pole.

==[[10-Flower Coin]] locations==
#Before the Wonder Flower, there is a 10-flower coin that is grabbed by falling onto another Zip Track with a 10-flower coin.
#After the parallel Zip Tracks on the path without the Wonder Flower, there is a 10-flower coin on the ceiling.
#On a track that leans down from a track, there is a 10-flower coin and the end of the Zip Track. This 10-flower coin is on the path without the Wonder Flower.
