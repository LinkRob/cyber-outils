The '''Big Bad Wolf''', or the '''Wolf''', is a one-time character who appears in the ''[[The Super Mario Bros. Super Show!]]'' episode "[[Little Red Riding Princess]]." Here, he tries to put a trap on [[Princess Peach]], like in Little Red Riding Hood.

However [[King Koopa]] has the trap set up and calls his [[Koopa Troop]]. King Koopa and his troop throw the wolf back into the forest. The Wolf jealous of Koopa, helps [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], and [[Toad (character)|Toad]] save the princess.

However he explains to Mario and his friends he is still evil and just wanted revenge on King Koopa.
