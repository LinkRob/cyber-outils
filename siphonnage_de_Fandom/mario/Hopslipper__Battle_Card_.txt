|name=Hopslipper (Battle Card)}}'''Hopslipper''' is a [[Battle Card (Paper Mario: Color Splash)|Battle Card]] seen in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
The Hopslipper is the normal version of [[Mario]]'s long [[Jump Attack|Jump attack]]. It is a green slipper with a spring attached to the bottom, with a green/white checkerboard background.
==Price==
Buy Price: 5/15 coins

Sell Price: 3/8 coins

== In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash'' ==
The Hopslipper is first seen in [[Ruddy Road]], but can also found in [[Cherry Lake]], [[Indigo Underground]], [[Bloo Bay Beach]], [[Daffodil Peak]], [[The Crimson Tower]], [[Sunglow Ridge]], [[Mondo Woods]], [[Marmalade Valley]], [[The Golden Coliseum]], [[Plum Park]], [[Cobalt Base]], [[Fort Cobalt]], [[Lighthouse Island]], and [[Toad Trainworks]].
