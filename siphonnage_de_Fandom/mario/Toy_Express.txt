'''Toy Express''' is a recurring location in the {{series|WarioWare}}. It may have first appeared in ''[[WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'', but Toy Express makes its first proper appearance in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]''.

==Description==
Toy Express is a toy store located on Interstate 301 in [[Diamond City]], where it is the main location where the city's residents go to purchase new video games and game consoles. The only time the store interior is seen is in ''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''.

==In {{series|WarioWare}}==
===''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!''===
An unnamed toy store, possibly Toy Express or a predecessor to it, appears in the background while [[Ken the Reporter]], who is holding a copy of [[Pyoro (game)|Pyoro]], reports on the game's success. This report prompts [[Wario]] to start his own game development studio, [[WarioWare, Inc.]]. The toy store also appears in ending cutscene, in which Ken reports on the success of Wario's new game.

===''WarioWare: Touched!''===
Toy Express appears in the opening cutscene of [[9-Volt]] and [[18-Volt]]'s stage, [[Retro Action]]. Here, a large crowd is seen outside the store anticipating the release of the [[Nintendo GameCube]] game [[36-Volt Man]]. The scene then cuts to 9-Volt and his friend [[18-Volt]] in front of the store with a copy of the game. The duo then head to [[5-Volt's House|9-Volt's House]] to play the newly-acquired game.

===''[[WarioWare: Smooth Moves]]''===
In the opening for 9-Volt and 18-Volt's stage, [[The Multiplayer Test]], when 18-Volt accidentally broke 9-Volt's [[Game & Watch]], the former goes to Toy Express to look for a replacement unit. As 18-Volt makes his way through the aisles, he cannot find any, but when he leaves Toy Express, 18-Volt notices a smaller video game store and goes there hoping to acquire a Game & Watch.

===''[[Game & Wario]]''===
Late in the game, after beating the game [[Pirates]], [[Reporter John]] is seen in front of Toy Express, reporting that the game is a success. During this, a line of customers are seen waiting in front of the store as well as a group of fans dressed in pirate outfits.
