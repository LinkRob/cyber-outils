'''Fliprus Lake''', or '''{{world|Frost|fliprus}}''', is the secret course of [[Frosted Glacier]] in ''[[New Super Mario Bros. U]]''. It is accessed via the secret exit of [[Swaying Ghost House]], and its own completion reveals a [[Spine Coaster]] that rides to [[Rock-Candy Mines]] and allows access to [[Fuzzy Clifftop]]. 
==Layout==
The level begins with a [[? Block]] containing a power-up, as well as a [[Fliprus]]. Some floating crates that can be used as platforms are found in the water, along with [[River Piranha Plant]]s. A section of icy ground follows, along with a [[Koopa Troopa]] and a Fliprus located on a floating crate. A Koopa Troopa is found on a ? Block containing a power-up, and is followed by a Fliprus. A River Piranha Plant and some more floating crates are found. A Fliprus is located on a vertical lift, with a Brick Block containing a [[1-Up Mushroom]] that can be accessed using a Koopa Troopa's shell following it. More Flipruses and River Piranha Plants are found in a section with more crates floating in the water. A Koopa Troopa is found on a ? Block containing a power-up. A section with more Flipruses, River Piranha Plants, and crates follows. Three more crates and a Fliprus are found before the [[Goal Pole]].
==[[Star Coin]]s==
*'''Star Coin 1''': The first Star Coin sits off-screen, above three moving [[Brick Block]]s and a red [[Koopa Troopa]].
*'''Star Coin 2''': Above the second Fliprus on a [[lift]], there is a green [[Warp Pipe]] (''pictured''). It takes Mario to a small area with two Flipruses and the second Star Coin.
*'''Star Coin 3''': The player must use floating crates to get the third Star Coin, found inside the last piece of ceiling of the level.
