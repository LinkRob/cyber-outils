'''Mini Paint Stars''' are items in ''[[Paper Mario: Color Splash]]''.

==Description==
Mini Paint Stars are the goal for every level, each having at least one, but some [[level]]s have more, serving as alternate exits. Mini Paint Stars are scattered across [[Prism Island]], and [[Mario]] must find them all to obtain all six [[Big Paint Star|Big Paint Stars]]. When obtained, they repaint a new area in the world map and unlock a new level, or an alternate pathway to an existing level. Collecting a Mini Paint Star also comes with rewards dropping from the sky. This includes paint, coins, and sometimes life hearts and roshambo tokens. The [[Coin|coins]] from this reward increases if more enemies were defeated. Mini Paint Stars have a larger counterpart known as [[Big Paint Star]]s, which are obtained after a [[Koopalings|Koopaling]] boss fight.

==In ''Paper Mario: Color Splash''==
Mini Paint Stars are generally found as a whole. However, [[Sacred Forest]]'s Mini Paint Star is scattered into five [[Paint Star Piece]]s, and [[Mario]] has to gather all of them to combine it into a Mini Paint Star.
