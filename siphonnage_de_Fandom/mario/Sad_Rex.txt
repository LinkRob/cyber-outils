A '''Sad Rex''' is an enemy in ''[[Super Princess Peach]]''.

==Profile==
===Physical description===
Sad Rexes are a variant of [[Rex]] and look nearly identical to it, except they are constantly crying due to being imbued with the [[Gloom]] [[vibe]].

===Behavior===
Sad Rexes usually sit in any location and weep, but whenever [[Princess Peach|Peach]] approaches a Sad Rex, it begins to cry and charges at her. If [[stomp]]ed, a Sad Rex shrinks and moves at the speed of a regular Rex.

==In ''Super Princess Peach''==
[[File:Sad Rex Approach.png|thumb|left|200px|Peach standing next to a Sad Rex]]
Sad Rexes first appear in the [[level]] [[Ladida Plains 1-9]], but they can mostly be found at [[Fury Volcano]]. After stomping a Sad Rex, Peach can then flip it over and pick it up to be thrown. Peach can defeat a Sad Rex by whacking it with [[Perry]], along with various other attacks as well.
