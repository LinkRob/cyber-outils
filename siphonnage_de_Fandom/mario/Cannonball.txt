'''Cannonballs''' are a recurring obstacle within the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' games. They are large black spheres shot from [[cannon]]s. Cannonballs first appear in ''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''.

==In the ''Super Mario'' series==
===''[[Super Mario Bros. 3]]''===
Cannonballs in ''Super Mario Bros. 3'' are fired from various cannons mounted on [[Bowser]]'s tanks, boats, and airships. Cannonballs in the game also have giant variations that are fittingly fired from giant cannons. Unlike [[Bullet Bill]]s, they are immune to [[Hammer Mario]]'s hammers.

==In the ''Mario Party'' series==
===''[[Mario Party 7]]''===
Cannonballs are featured in the 1-vs.-3 minigame [[Balloonatic]], in which the object for the three players is to shoot cannonballs at the solo player's balloons so that the solo player drops into the water.
