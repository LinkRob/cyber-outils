'''Solo Toadies''' are a variant of [[Toady]] featured in the [[Yoshi's Island (series)|''Yoshi's Island'' series]], and they first appear in ''[[Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island]]''. Solo Toadies generally wear a purple cloak, but there are also Solo Toadies that wear a light green cloak named '''Green Toadies'''.

==In ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''==
[[File:Green Toady SMW2 sprite.png|frame|left]]
In certain levels, when the [[Countdown Timer]] is activated, a Solo Toady captures [[Baby Mario]] while he is in his [[bubble]]. Solo Toadies do not specifically focus on trying to attack a [[Yoshi (species)|Yoshi]], but they try to keep Baby Mario and Yoshi separate for long enough until [[Kamek's Toadies]] arrive to kidnap Baby Mario, causing a life to be lost.

==In ''[[Yoshi Touch & Go]]''==
Green Toadies make a reappearance in the main mode, although they only attack [[Yoshi (character)|Yoshi]] instead of Baby Mario. In Time Attack mode, the purple Solo Toadies guard Kamek's Toadies.

==In ''[[Yoshi's New Island]]''==
Only the purple Solo Toadies make a reappearance, not the Green Toadies. The game reinstates their behavior from ''Super Mario World 2: Yoshi's Island''.
