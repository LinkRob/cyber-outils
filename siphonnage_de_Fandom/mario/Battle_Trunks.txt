The '''Battle Trunks''' are a type of key item featured in ''[[Paper Mario: The Thousand-Year Door]]''.

==History==
[[Mario]] takes on [[Jolene]]'s [[Trouble Center|trouble]], titled "Help wanted!". Mario goes to the [[Glitz Pit]] and picks up 20 Battle Trunks as Jolene requested. He returns them to [[Goomfrey]]. Mario receives 30 [[coin]]s for completing this trouble.
