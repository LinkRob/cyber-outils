The '''Whale''' is a character whose only appearance is in ''[[Paper Mario]]''. The whale is found at the [[Toad Town (Paper Mario)#Port|Toad Town port]].

== In ''Paper Mario'' ==
Before Chapter 5, [[Kolorado]] tells [[Mario]] that he wants to travel to the faraway island off the coast of the [[Mushroom Kingdom]]. However, he cannot obtain a boat in order to help him reach the island. Kolorado then mentions that all he found at the dock is some sort of creature in the water. Mario nears the creature and finds out that it is a whale by [[Spin Jump]]ing on it, walking it up.

While awake, the whale tells Mario that there's something inside of him and he wants it out. Mario enters the whale through its mouth and finds [[Fuzzipede]] inside. The two battle inside of the whale briefly, and upon its defeat, Mario and Fuzzipede are launched out of the whale. Relived, the whale takes Mario, Kolorado, and the rest of Mario's party to [[Lavalava Island]].

=== Inside the whale ===
The inside of the whale has two rooms. The first room is the tongue of the whale and is a very small room. The second room is a larger room down into the whale's body that is filled with water, fish, boxes, and bottles. Here, Mario finds Fuzzipede and the two battle inside this room. While inside of the whale, [[Watt]] is needed as the paths inside are dark. Even during the battle with Fuzzipede, it is dark, making Fuzzipede the only enemy that [[Goombario]] cannot use [[Tattle]] on.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{series|Super Smash Bros.}}===
The whale makes a cameo appearance in the [[Paper Mario (stage)|Paper Mario]] stage in ''[[Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]]''. Here, during the second phase of the stage, the whale can be seen lifting the [[S. S. Flavion]] from a stream of water blowing out of his blowhole.
