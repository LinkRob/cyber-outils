==History==
Skeet lives in [[Flipside]] on the third floor. He wants to be an architech and often stares at Flipside Tower every day. Before the events of Chapter 2, Skeet is the one who discovered [[Princess Peach]] after she was [[Dimentio|transported]] to Flipside.
