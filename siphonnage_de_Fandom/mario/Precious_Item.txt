The '''Precious Item''' is a key item that is not seen but mentioned in ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga]]'' and ''[[Mario & Luigi: Superstar Saga + Bowser's Minions]]''. It was mentioned during the [[Toad (species)|Toad]]'s sidequest. The Toad was on top of the shrub while talking to Mario, it can be obtained by walking south. When the player gives the Toad the Precious Item, he will give him the [[1-Up Mushroom]].
