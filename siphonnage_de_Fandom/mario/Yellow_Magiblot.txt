}}A '''Yellow Magiblot''' is an enemy that appears in ''[[Super Paper Mario]]''.

==History==
Yellow Magiblots are strange, blob-like enemies that can be found in [[Castle Bleck Interior]] and the [[Flipside Pit of 100 Trials]]. Yellow Magiblots are often found beside their relatives: the [[Blue Magiblot]]s and the [[Red Magiblot]]s. Yellow Magiblots attack with a blob of magic that can travel a far distance.
