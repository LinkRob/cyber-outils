The {{Term/Store|Subrosian Wilds|Subrosian Wilds|Series, OoS}} is an area in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 251}}</ref> It is located to the far west of [[Subrosia]]. Within the wilderness is a [[Treasure Chest]] containing the precious [[Blue Ore]], used to forge the [[Iron Shield]]. The area contains no other objects of note, aside from a [[Magic Ring]]. Its exploration is therefore optional.

==Geography==
In western Subrosia, the Wilds are part of a wooded area that also includes the [[Treasure Grove]] to the south. To the north it is bordered by [[lava]] spurting from the [[Subrosian Volcanoes]]. The area can be accessed from southwestern [[Subrosia Village]]. 

The inner Wilds are overrun by tall [[grass]] and lined by Subrosian [[tree]]s on all sides; a gap to the northwest constitutes the only entrance. However, the opening is barred by boulders and pitfalls that extend through the Wilds. Use of the {{Term|OoS|Magnetic Glove|link}} and {{Plural|OoS|"S" Stone|link}} are needed to navigate the area. Inside, just east of the opening, Link finds the treasure chest containing Blue Ore. Further in, he [[shovel]]s up the Magic Ring buried in the southwest corner. 

A green-robed [[Subrosian]] stands on the outer edge of the trees. He is determined to find the treasure which he knows is somewhere nearby.<ref>{{Cite|I know there's treasure around here somewhere, and I'm gonna find it!|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> It is in fact located just on the other side of the trees. Another blue-robed Subrosian can be found enjoying "volcano showers" on the northern edge of the wilderness.<ref>{{Cite|Volcano showers feel so great!|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref>
