'''''The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks Sheet Music''''' is a sheet music book for piano released in 2010. Containing 40 pages, the book features 22 songs from the 2009 ''Zelda'' game released on the [[Nintendo DS]].<ref>[http://zeldapower.com/index.php/downloads/sheet_music/the_legend_of_zelda_spirit_tracks_sheet_music.php Zelda Power - The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks Sheet Music]</ref>
