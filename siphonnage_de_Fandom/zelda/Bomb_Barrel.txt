{{Term/Store|Bomb Barrel|Bomb Barrels|Series, BotW|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref name="Tip">{{Cite|Bomb Barrels<br/>Shoot a fire arrow into a bomb barrel to make it explode. Tossing a burning weapon at one works too.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Bomb Stack|Bomb Stacks|NL|plural}},{{Exp Game|NL}}<ref>{{Cite|Bomb Stack|Monita|NL}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. {{Plural|Series|Bomb Barrel}} go unnamed in {{TWW}}, {{TP}}, {{PH}}, {{ST}}, and {{LCT}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb Barrel}} appear floating on the [[Great Sea]] near the [[Forsaken Fortress]] in {{TWW}}. Similar to {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb|link}}, though functionally similar in purpose to a maritime mine, {{Plural|TWWHD|Bomb Barrel}} surface then flash red with increasing rapidness as its fuse burns out, giving an indication as to when the {{Term|TWWHD|Bomb Barrel}} is about to explode. If in close proximity of a {{Term|TWWHD|Bomb Barrel}} when it detonates, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} will be thrown off his boat. In {{TWWHD}}, he is simply damaged instead. Direct collision with a {{Term|TWWHD|Bomb Barrel}} will also cause it to explode.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Plural|TPHD|Bomb Barrel}} appear in an outside room of the [[Goron Mines]] in {{TP}}. In this room, several [[Bulblin]] archers will shoot at {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} with fire-tipped {{Plural|TPHD|Arrow|link}}, which may detonate the {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb Barrel}} scattered across the rooms. {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb Barrel}} also appear in the [[Hidden Village]], which {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can use to shoot down the Bulblins around the Village.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Bomb Barrel}} appear as obstacles in the [[World of the Ocean King]] in {{PH}}. They can be detonated from a distance using the Cannon.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Bomb Barrel}} can be found inside a cave during the [[Three Trials]] in the [[Sand Realm]] in {{ST}}. They must be use in order to damage the [[Rocktite]]. Exploding a {{Term|ST|Bomb Barrel}} with the Cannon while the Rocktite is nearby forces its eye open, leaving it vulnerable to direct attacks.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} appear predominately around enemy encampments in {{BotW}}. If a {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} comes into contact with an open flame, it will detonate.<ref name="Tip"/> Alternatively, sufficient concussive damage to a {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} will cause it to break and explode. Link can use the [[Stasis]] [[Rune]] in conjunction with a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} on a Bomb Barrel to cause it to explode wherever it lands.<ref>{{Cite|Stasis<br/>Freezing a Bomb Barrel using Stasis and then attacking it will cause it to fly off and explode at its point of impact.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref> Anything flammable within the blast radius of a Bomb Barrel will catch fire when one explodes. It is possible to detonate nearby Bomb Barrels with the blast from another Bomb Barrel, causing a chain of explosions. Despite this, explosions from Bomb Barrels cannot detonate {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} also appear in [[Kah Okeo Shrine]], where they can be seen affixed to [[Balloon]]s. While attached to Balloons, {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} can be directed with a [[Korok Leaf]]. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to pick up a {{Term|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} that is attached to Balloons, the ropes connecting them will sever.

==Other Appearances==
==={{LCT|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|LCT|Bomb Barrel}} appear in certain stages in {{LCT}}, such as in the Hidden Village Bulblin shootout in [[Link's Crossbow Training#Stage 5|Stage 5]]. These {{Plural|LCT|Bomb Barrel}} can be used to {{Term|LCT|Link}}'s advantage, as detonating them will defeat any nearby Bulblin.
{{Ambig|End}}

==={{NL|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Monita|Esteemed visitors, please refrain from detonating explosives when your friends are nearby. But don't worry about these bombs--I'm mostly sure it's just a display unit.}}

{{Plural|NL|Bomb Stack}} appear in various Stages in {{NL}}'s {{BQ}}. They can be detonated with a strike from an {{Term|NL|Arrow|link}}. When detonated, {{Plural|NL|Bomb Stack}} damage anything in their blast radius.

{{Plural|NL|Bomb Stack}} also appear as a [[Prize]] in the {{NL|-}} Plaza. When interacted with, they explode before being replaced. The explosions from the Prize do not cause any damage.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Barrel}} are interactable hazards that can be detonated by various means, usually attacks from either {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} or monsters, damaging anything and anyone caught in its blast. When {{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Barrel}} in the stage are detonated, any monster caught in the blast with a possible {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point Gauge}} will have it exposed. Unlike {{BotW}}, they can be blown up with the {{Term|HWAoC|Remote Bomb|link}} {{Term|HWAoC|Rune|link}} via the {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Slate|link}}.

During the {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast|link}} combat segments, {{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Barrel}} are the weapon of choice for {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}.

{{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Barrel}} are also integrated into {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}}'s {{Term|HWAoC|Special Attack|link}}. If she has either none or two Signs absorbed, {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} uses just one that has been marked with a Sign, absorbing it upon the conclusion of the detonation. If she has three active Signs, she will use a whole stack of {{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Barrel}}, costing her her entire Sign stock, though she gains a Sign from the conclusion of the detonation.

In the {{PotA}} Downloadable Content pack, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} carry them on containers and throw them at enemies that get too close or from a distance. Unlike the {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Barrel}} in {{BotW|-}} and any point in the game, they are not damaged by their own {{Plural|HWAoC|Bomb Barrel|display=Barrels}}, save for exploiting a {{Term|HWAoC|Weak Point Gauge}} involving the {{Term|HWAoC|Remote Bomb}} {{Term|HWAoC|Rune}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
