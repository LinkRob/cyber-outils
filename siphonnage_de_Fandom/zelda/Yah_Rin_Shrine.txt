The {{Term/Store|Yah Rin Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Yah Rin Shrine}}<br/>A Weighty Decision}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Weighty Decision''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Yah Rin Shrine is located on the northwestern outskirts of {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Yah Rin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Yah Rin. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Yah Rin|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside Yah Rin Shrine, are a series of scales, which must be weighted down with metallic objects found inside the Ancient Shrine in order to raise Link to higher points within the Shrine. In order for Link to reach the Monk, he must traverse the weighted puzzles and approach the pagoda where Yah Rin waits.

Upon approaching Yah Rin, he will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Yah Rin|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Yah Rin will wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Yah Rin|BotW}}</ref>

Behind a gate in the first chamber of Yah Rin Shrine is a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} which holds a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword|link}}. A second, stone Treasure Chest in the room with two scales holds an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}}.
