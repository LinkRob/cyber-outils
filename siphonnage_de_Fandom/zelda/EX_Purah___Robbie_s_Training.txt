{{Term/Store|EX Purah & Robbie's Training||HWAoC}} is a level 58 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}},<ref>{{Cite|EX Purah & Robbie's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> added in the {{GoR}} [[DLC]] pack.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Purah & Robbie's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}} receive a reward of five {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Spicy Pepper|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Rice|link}}, 1,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}'s objective is to defeat 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within eight minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}} at first, although more spawn when {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}} reach 50 K0s. At 50 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} also spawns far away near the eastern edge of the map and starts moving toward {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}. The same happens again at 150 KOs, although the {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}} spawn in a different part of the map. The cycle continues at 200 KOs, and at 300, {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}} spawn instead of {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}}. At 340 KOs, {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos}} spawn in a different area of the map than the first group, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} does not appear. Once 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, the time limit is taken away and {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}} must then defeat each {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} that spawned. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once every {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I}} is defeated.
