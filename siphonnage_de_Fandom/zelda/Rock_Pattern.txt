{{Term/Store|Rock Pattern|Rock Patterns|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Piggyback">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 330 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Rock Pattern}} are geometric patterns of {{Plural|BotW|Rock|link}} that can be found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially finds a {{Term|BotW|Rock Pattern}}, it will be incomplete. Typically, the missing {{Term|BotW|Rock}} can be found in the direction of the empty spot.<ref name="Piggyback"/> By finding {{Plural|BotW|Rock}} nearby and placing them in any empty spots, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a hidden {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}}, which gives him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
