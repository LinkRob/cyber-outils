The {{Term/Store|Quaint Passenger Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Quaint Passenger Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Quaint Passenger Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Ancient Gold Piece|link}}, three {{Plural|ST|Ruto Crown|link}} and five {{Plural|ST|Star Fragment|link}}. Its description reads "Passengers like this carriage for its homey feel."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Quaint Passenger Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Stagecoach Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
