{{Name|Japanese|'''Azusa'''}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}.{{Name Ref|RTBToL}} She is one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s love interests.

==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Azusa}} is a train fanatic who first appears in {{Term|RTBToL|Page 5|link}}, behind the abandoned train station. She is taking photos of the garage where the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner|link}} is in, in that moment, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} approaches to talk to her, however, {{Term|RTBToL|Azusa}} gets scared, and runs away, leaving the place until the page is finished.

After obtaining the {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle Balloon|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} can return to the {{Term|RTBToL|Page 5}}, where {{Term|RTBToL|Azusa}} can be found in the basement of the garage behind the station. Since her hearts are locked from the beginning, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must give her first a train model found in {{Term|RTBToL|Page 9|link}}, in order to successfully start a {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push|link}}. After {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} wins her first heart, she will move to {{Term|RTBToL|Page 9}}, where she stays until the royal dance. {{Term|RTBToL|Azusa}} is seen in the end credits with the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner}}, now named the ''Azusa No. 2'', and been transformed to match her face instead of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}'s.
