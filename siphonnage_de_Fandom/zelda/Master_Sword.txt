== Overview ==
=== Origins and purpose ===
[[File:SS Goddess Sword Model.png|thumb|231x231px|The Goddess Sword, the original form of the Master Sword, from ''Skyward Sword'']]As seen in [[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|''Skyward Sword'']] where its origins are revealed, the Master Sword was originally known as the [[Goddess Sword|'''Goddess Sword''']], a weapon once used by the Goddess Hylia, and was later tempered into the Master Sword by the [[Link#Skyward Sword|Goddess's chosen hero]] and its spirit, [[Fi]], who bathed it in the three [[Sacred Flames]] located across the [[Founding of the Kingdom of Hyrule|land]] that would become the [[Hyrule|Kingdom of Hyrule]].<ref name="tempering">{{Cite Book|quote=All the while, Link continued with his task of tempering the Goddess Sword in the Sacred Flames of the Golden Goddesses. Imbued with the immense, sacred power of the Force, the Goddess Sword was transformed into the Master Sword. |book= HH |publisher= |page= 75}}</ref> [[Din's Flame]] in particular imbued the sword with the [[Power to Repel Evil]],<ref name="din's flame">{{Cite|The sacred red flame has imbued your blade with a divine power that repels evil.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref name="legendary">{{Cite|You got the Master Sword! The legendary blade with the power to repel evil...once wielded by the legendary hero himself!|N/A|TWW}}</ref> a power augmented after the Sword received the blessing of the descendant of Hylia, [[Princess Zelda]], which transformed the blade into the [[True Master Sword|'''True Master Sword''']].<ref name="bless" /><ref>{{Cite|The goddess has blessed your blade, and the Master Sword has at last achieved its ultimate form! The sword is now imbued with the mythical power to drive back demons, and only {{Color|SS Blue|Link}} may wield it!|N/A|SS}}</ref> As the sacred blade is empowered with purity and strength and therefore destined to wipe out the great evil that constantly threatens the land of Hyrule, it is the only weapon that can defeat [[Ganon]] in the many games it appears in, with [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|one exception]].<ref>{{Cite|It is none other than the Master Sword... The blade of evil's bane. It is the only sword that can banish Ganon from the world above!|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> In most core series games' plot or other media within the franchise where it is featured, the Master Sword seems to be a weapon of last resort by Hyrule when other methods to stop evil forces fail. Link also commonly wields this weapon in his [[Link/Mario-related media|other]] [[Link/Other Appearances|appearances]] from various media as well, alongside the [[Hylian Shield]], itself the main character's defense equivalent. 

The Master Sword is often seen to choose or accept its master by its own will, always [[Link|a hero]] who has gone through great trials that test his power, wisdom, and courage (much like the balance of the [[Triforce]]) to prove him worthy of wielding it by pulling it from its [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|pedestal]] where the blade rests. This may be the conscious choice of the sword's spirit, Fi, who sleeps eternally within the sword and only assists her heroic master.<ref name="fi" />

Despite being primarily associated with Link, few other characters in the series are also known to wield the Master Sword.

===Appearance===
[[File:ALttP Master Sword Artwork.png|right|frameless|295x295px|The Master Sword's debut appearance, as seen in ''A Link to the Past'']]
When the Master Sword was originally introduced in 1991 by official artworks of ''A Link to the Past'', the Sword's grip was red and its cross-guard yellow. However, in its pedestal, due to the hardware limitations of the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] and using a unique sprite as opposed to the one when on Link's hand where both the grip and guard were red and black, the Master Sword has a purple grip and blue guard.  Interestingly, the legendary sword's inventory sprite has its blade being distinctively blue with a white guard and red gem. Its most conventional appearance was clearly defined in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', with the purple grip and purple guard, though in subsequent games and the re-release of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', the purple has become more blue or indigo. In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', the grip and guard are royal blue as opposed to a deeper color. For the rest of the games, the Master Sword is merely a [[wikipedia:Longsword|bastard sword]] with a narrow {{Wp|ricasso}} and a wide, shallow {{Wp|Fuller (weapon)|fuller}} that runs almost the entire length of the blade, although in some appearances it has no fuller at all. The Triforce can be seen engraved in the lower part of the blade and a yellow gemstone is embedded in the cross guard. It is seen in ''The Wind Waker'' and ''Skyward Sword'' that the state of the gem and the cross guard are indicative of the sword's awakening and power. When Link acquires the unawakened Master Sword in the former and the first form of the blade in the latter, the gem in its cross guard is dark and the guard is retracted toward the grip. Once the Master Sword has been restored to its full power in both games, its cross guard regains its winged form, and the two gems on the guard regain their yellow color. In addition, the blade itself begins to glow with energy.

==Powers and Abilities==
[[File:OoT Master Sword Power to Repel Evil.png|thumb|233x233px|The Master Sword's signature holy power as seen in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
Widely known as Hyrule's secret weapon, the Master Sword's classic ability is the [[Power to Repel Evil|power to vanquish evil]]. Infused with the sacred flames provided by the [[Golden Goddess|Golden Goddesses]] and blessed with Hylia's power, the sword is effective even against evil ones possessing the Triforce. However, in order to keep the power to repel evil intact, two [[Sage|Sages]], known as the [[Sage of Earth]] and the [[Sage of Wind]], are in charge of praying to the gods and thus infuse the Master Sword with the gods' power, most notably in ''The Wind Waker''. <ref name="korl">{{Cite|The fact that the Master Sword lost the power to repel evil suggests to me that something has happened to the sages who infused the blade with the gods' power.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> Should something happen to these Sages, the Master Sword will weaken and lose its might to combat evil.

The sacred blade is also capable of destroying magical barriers<ref>{{Cite|The sword you hold in your hand is the one and only Master Sword! Now that you possess that blade, you can break the barrier at the castle. [...] Why, that's incredible! How easily it cuts through such dark magic!|Sahasrahla|ALBW}}</ref><ref name="BreakBarrier" /> and breaking curses that involve [[Shadow Crystal|powerful, dark magic]].<ref name="Quote1" /> In addition, it has the ability to lock away the remains of a [[demon]].<ref>{{Cite|The last remnants of {{Color|SS Blue|Demise}} are decaying slowly within the sword.|Impa|SS}}</ref><ref name="Demise's sealing" /> The Master Sword can also unleash [[Skyward Strike]]s by channeling heavenly energy.<ref>{{Cite|To gather energy from the heavens and charge up a {{Color|SS Red|Skyward Strike}}, {{Color|SS Green|point your sword skyward}}.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Even when low on power, the Master Sword can unleash Skyward Strikes through the use of lightning when it strikes the blade.

Specifically created to be used for good, only a true hero that is "pure of heart and strong of body" is capable of wielding the sacred blade (Link in virtually all cases),<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= It was so powerful that only one who was pure of heart and strong of body could wield it. |game= ALttP |page= 5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Master Sword! Only the true hero can wield this legendary weapon!|N/A|TP}}</ref> and as such, those with tainted hearts can't even touch this sword.<ref name="rauru">{{Cite|The Master Sword is a sacred blade which evil ones may never touch....|Rauru|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The blade of evil's bane. Those with evil hearts cannot touch it.|Inventory|TP}}</ref> If a hero who is too young to bear the title that comes with the Master Sword withdraws this blade from its [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|pedestal]], the Master Sword will seal away the hero's soul and awaken him when the bearer comes of age to wield it, as seen in ''Ocarina of Time''.<ref>{{Cite|Only one worthy of the title of "Hero of Time" can pull it from the Pedestal of Time....However, you were too young to be the Hero of Time.... Therefore, your spirit was sealed here for seven years.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|After you opened the door of time, the Master Sword sealed you away in the Sacred Realm...|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> However, if someone that is weak attempts to draw the Master Sword from its pedestal, they will lose their life, as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]''.<ref>{{Cite|If you sought to free the sword in any sort of weakened state, you would surely lose your life where you stand.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> The Master Sword also acts as a key to gain entrance to the [[Temple of Light]]. If the Master Sword is retrieved from its pedestal in the [[Temple of Time]], the portal to the [[Sacred Realm]] will open, eventually leading to the [[Triforce]].<ref>{{Cite|The Temple of Light, situated in the very center of the Sacred Realm, is the last stronghold against Ganondorf's evil forces. The Master Sword--the evil-destroying sword that you pulled out of the Pedestal of Time--was the final key to the Sacred Realm.|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Additionally, the bearer of the sword can travel through time to some extent by simply thrusting the Master Sword into the Pedestal of Time, similar to the [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword is a ship with which you can sail upstream and downstream through time's river... The port for that ship is in the Temple of Time...|Sheik|OoT}}</ref> The hero is able to travel backwards and forwards through the seven years of his life that were lost when he was kept in the Sacred Realm until he was old enough to hold the sword, in the case of ''Ocarina of Time''.

When in the [[Twilight]], the Master Sword itself can be infused with the power of the [[Sol]]s from the [[Twilight Realm]], allowing it to cut through the dark fog that exists in the realm.<ref name="sol">{{Cite|Light filled the Master Sword! Its blade glows with a golden light in the Twilight Realm. With it, you can cut through dark fog!|N/A|TP}}</ref>[[File:Link trusting the Master Sword into the Secret Stone..jpg|thumb|245x245px|Link using the Master Sword to deliver the final blow on the [[Demon Dragon]] (Ganondorf) in ''Tears of the Kingdom'', a common tactic in the series to end final battles]]Famously, the Master Sword is the weapon used by Link to end [[Final Boss]] battles in certain installments since ''Ocarina of Time'', mainly against Ganon.
[[File:ALttP Master Sword Lv3 Artwork.png|thumb|166x166px|The Master Sword in its strongest form, the Golden Sword, from ''A Link to the Past''|left]]
Even in its final form, as seen in {{ALttP}} and {{ALBW}}, the Master Sword can be further improved by [[Master Sword Lv2|smithing]] or [[Master Sword Lv3|magical means]] to inflict even greater damage. However, once its task is accomplished and the sword returned into its pedestal, it will revert back to its original shape, shown in the ending of both games as well as ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|BS Ancient Stone Tablets]]''.<ref>{{ALttP|-}} ending</ref> It is unknown why this occurs in these specific cases.

Overall, the Master Sword is one of, if not the most important weapon in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. However, it must be noted - despite being [[Link]]'s trademark [[Sword]], having "Master" in its name, and especially its essential role of defeating his archenemy [[Ganon]] (or other lead antagonists) in games it itself appears in and other media within the long-running franchise ([[comics]], [[manga]], etc.), which heavily suggests the Blade of Evil's Bane to be the ultimate weapon for the main protagonist story-wise, it is '''not''' technically such in terms of actual gameplay; Although indeed powerful in its own right, certain other weapons [[Weapon Strength|stronger than the sacred sword]] exist in various games. The [[Biggoron's Sword]], [[Silver Arrow]]s, and [[Light Arrow]]s are the best-known examples to be greater than the Master Sword in raw strength. Plus, as aforementioned, it can be optionally upgraded to be more potent than it normally is in rare cases, as seen as the Tempered/Golden Sword or using items that temporarily boosts damage such as the [[Elixir Soup]]. This applies to its spin-off appearances as well, specifically ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', as in that game, aside from the Biggoron's Sword, weapons such as the [[Megaton Hammer]], [[Great Fairy's Sword]], and even the [[Razor Sword]] surpasses the legendary blade in damage. Also, there is the [[Cane of Byrna]], which, although merely a non-attacking item in its source material, can deal vast amounts of physical damage through a Soul Charge. It is likely the [[Fierce Deity Sword]] by Link's infamous [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]] form in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' is considerably stronger than the Master Sword as well. Though in most cases whenever the Master Sword is weakened its damage output does not decrease as seen in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', the sacred blade undoubtedly lost much of its strength when it was unexpectedly [[Decayed Master Sword|shattered by Ganondorf]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', rendering the great weapon virtually useless not unlike a broken [[Giant's Knife]]. This also proves that the Master Sword itself is not as indestructible as often believed, so it can be reforged into a much stronger version of itself by sacred energy. Lastly and importantly, in spite of the Blade of Evil's Bane being capable of harming Ganon like other holy weaponry and is indeed a nuisance to the main antagonist, it does not always guarantee his death when defeated by this sword, as seen in ''Ocarina of Time'' (though he did perish by it a few times as [[Ganondorf]]), while in comparison the Silver Arrows can completely destroy him as his true beast form as seen in ''A Link to the Past'' and especially ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' where in the latter he dies instantly by bursting into ashes, consequently making these very [[Arrow|Arrows]] arguably the most effective [[item]] in Link's entire arsenal.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}/''The Adventure of Link''===
[[File:TLoZ Link Princess Zelda Artwork.png|thumb|234x234px|An example of an artwork from the [[Nintendo of America|NOA]] ''A Link to the Past'' strategy guide showing a blade on Link's hand appearing to be the Master Sword rather than the Magical Sword, based on ''The Legend of Zelda.'' The {{Term|TLoZ|Master Sword}} does not exist in the original {{TLoZ}} nor its [[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|sequel]], as both early [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] installments of the franchise had the [[Magical Sword]], its precursor. However, a sword closely resembling it can be seen on various artworks referring to {{TLoZ|-}} and ''The Adventure of Link'' in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past — Nintendo Player's Guide|The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past - Nintendo Player's Guide]]'' by [[Nintendo of America]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 4}}</ref> It should be noted that these are not canonical to the games' actual events.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:ALttP Master Sword Sprite 2.png|left|frameless]]
''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' marks the Master Sword's grand debut in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is the legendary blade that rests in the [[Lost Woods]] of northwestern Hyrule in a pedestal with ancient [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian]] inscribed on it.<ref>{{Cite|Take the three Pendants into the Lost Woods. The Master Sword awaits you there.|Sahasrahla|ALttP}}</ref> The ancient text says the hero with three symbols of virtue will wield it on cataclysm's eve, reading: "''The Hero's triumph on Cataclysm's Eve wins three Symbols of Virtue. The Master Sword he will then retrieve, keeping the knights' line true''."<ref>{{Cite|The Hero's triumph on Cataclysm's Eve Wins three symbols of virtue. The Master Sword he will then retrieve, Keeping the Knight's line true.|N/A|ALttP}}</ref>
[[File:ALttP Sacred Grove Artwork.png|thumb|230x230px|The Master Sword in its pedestal in {{ALttP|-}}]]
In order to defeat the evil wizard [[Agahnim]] that has taken over [[Hyrule Castle]], Link must obtain the Master Sword, as instructed by [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Sahasrahla]].<ref>{{Cite|The only weapon potent enough to defeat the wizard is the legendary Master Sword.|Loyal Sage|ALttP}}</ref> However, before the young hero can wield it, however, he must first acquire the three [[Pendants of Virtue]] that are scattered throughout the land.<ref>{{Cite|Not just anyone can use that weapon. Legends say only the Hero who has won the three Pendants can wield the sword.|Sahasrahla|ALttP}}</ref> After doing so, he retrieves the sacred blade from the [[Pedestal of Time]] within the Lost Woods ([[Fake Master Sword|fake swords]] can also be found in the forest to trick Link into finding the actual weapon). When obtained, the Master Sword replaces the [[Sword|Fighter's Sword]]. With it, he is able to destroy the electrical barrier that Agahnim had placed in the upper floor of Hyrule Castle. With the Master Sword in hand, Link eventually manages to defeat Agahnim twice in the game through a [[Dead Man's Volley]] battle, and soon after, [[Ganon]] himself. With Hyrule safe from evil and at peace after claiming the full [[Triforce]], Link places the Master Sword back in its pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|AND THE MASTER SWORD SLEEPS AGAIN... FOREVER!|N/A|ALttP}}</ref>
[[File:Link pulling Master Sword debut.png|thumb|229x229px|Link pulling the Master Sword for the first time]]
The {{Term|ALttP|Master Sword}} is twice as powerful as the Fighter's Sword and has a longer reach.{{Fact|ALttP}} When at full {{Term|ALttP|Life Gauge|link|display=health}}, it will also fire a {{Term|ALttP|Sword Beam|link}} when swung. In {{ALttP&FS}}, the Master Sword can also break {{Plural|ALttP|Sign|link}}.{{Verify|ALttP}}

During the course of the game, Link can actually upgrade the Master Sword. Its first upgrade is the [[Tempered Sword|'''Tempered Sword''']], which is acquired when the [[Dwarven Swordsmith]] is reunited with his partner on the outskirts of Kakariko Village.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Happy days are here again! You found my partner! ... We are very happy now... Drop by here again! At that time, we will temper your sword perfectly!|Dwarven Swordsmith|ALttP}}</ref> In the tempered shape, the Master Sword is two times stronger than its standard form, has a glowing red-orange blade and makes a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/LTTP/LTTP_Sword3.wav deep swing sound] as opposed to the usual [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/LTTP/LTTP_Sword2.wav slash sound]. Its second and last upgrade is the [[Golden Sword|'''Golden Sword''']], acquired by giving the Tempered Sword to the [[Cursed Fairy]] in the [[Mysterious Pond]] of the [[Dark World]] within the [[Pyramid of Power]]. In the golden shape, the Master Sword is four times stronger than its standard form, killing almost any enemy in one hit, has a golden blade and green hilt, has the ability to break jars and skull jars (in the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|GBA remake]]), and also make an [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/LTTP/LTTP_Sword4.wav even deeper swing sound] than the tempered form. Despite its great power, however, [[Silver Arrow]]s outclass the Golden Sword in raw strength. The Tempered and Golden Sword are entirely optional, however, as any version of the Master Sword alongside the Silver Arrows is needed to complete the game. 

Unlike all subsequent games in the series, the Master Sword itself does '''not''' defeat Ganon in ''A Link to the Past'', as the sacred blade can only stun him, while the Silver Arrows are required to outright destroy the main antagonist, needing four shots to kill him (this particular role would be reversed for the [[Light Arrow]]s starting in ''Ocarina of Time''), similar to ''The Legend of Zelda''. Although all forms of the Blade of Evil's Bane can damage Ganon during the final battle, a [[Spin Attack]] is required for its normal shape in the original [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] release as well as its remake, as only the upgraded variants can harm him with standard slash attacks, though the former deals less damage in comparison. Because of this disadvantage, fighting against the Dark Beast with just the traditional Master Sword would make the [[Final Boss]] encounter significantly more difficult. If [[Glitches in A Link to the Past|glitches]] or cheats are used to make Link duel his archenemy with the Fighter's Sword instead, that weapon is completely ineffective. 

Moreover, Link can obtain the magic of the [[Bombos Medallion|Bombos]] and [[Ether Medallion]] by holding the Master Sword up into the sky next to certain inscriptions around Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Hold up the Master Sword and you will get the magic of Ether.|N/A|ALttP}}</ref>

Ever since the Master Sword was pulled from the Pedestal of Time, it has become analogous with the [[Mythology in The Legend of Zelda Series#Legend of the Hero of Time|legend of the Hero of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|You got the Master Sword! The legendary blade with the power to repel evil...once wielded by the legendary hero himself!|N/A|TP}}</ref>
[[File:OoT Ganon's Defeat.png|thumb|279x279px|Arguably the most brutal defeat of Ganon by the Master Sword in the franchise, the Hero of Time delivers the finishing blow via stabbing him into his mouth resulting in a blood splatter]]
In this game, the Master Sword can only be used by Adult Link. It is twice as powerful as the [[Kokiri Sword]], but the [[Giant's Knife]]/[[Biggoron's Sword]] surpasses it by having double its power. However, although the Giant's Knife or Biggoron's Sword are the strongest sword in ''Ocarina of Time'' and can be used in the final battle like the [[Megaton Hammer]] (though the former would break apart after a few hits), they are still incapable of defeating Ganon, as only the Blade of Evil's Bane can deliver the final blow (otherwise the weapons simply misses). The finishing attack itself results in Link violently slashing Ganon's face multiple times before an unusual, rather vicious move anywhere in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, thrusting the Master Sword directly into his mouth with red blood being splattered (which was toned down to green in subsequent re-releases and the sword instead hitting Ganon's forehead in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|3DS remake]] due to [[Controversy in The Legend of Zelda Series|controversy]]). Surprisingly, despite the seemingly fatal blow and Princess Zelda claiming it could destroy him, the beast did '''not''' die, but flailed and roared in agony.

==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:Master Sword Pedestal.png|left|The Master Sword in Oracle of Seasons]]
The Master Sword makes a reappearance in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, fittingly as the most powerful sword in the game. The only way to unlock the Master Sword is to have finished one of the games, and to have started a [[Linked Game]] on the opposite game. When a certain [[Secret]] in the game is found and told to [[Farore (Oracle)|Farore]], she will upgrade the [[Noble Sword]] Link currently holds into the Master Sword. However, if Link still has the Wooden Sword, the Wooden Sword will become the Noble Sword, and the Master Sword will be found where the Noble Sword would have been.

Despite its name, the Master Sword in these games for the [[Game Boy Color]] is '''not''' the actual sword itself, but is simply the Noble Sword upgraded so its power is similar to that of the real Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= In {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, connecting {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}} upgrades the Noble Sword, giving it power akin to the Master Sword. |book= E |publisher= |page= 83}}</ref>

To get the Master Sword, Link must find the Old Woman in South Lynna who will tell the Clock Shop Secret. She instructs the player to dig around the Clock Shop located in Holodrum. The old man inside will ask the player to play a game wherein 12 beasts must be beaten within 30 seconds. 

If Link discovers the [[Linked Game/Secrets#King Zora Secret|King Zora Secret]] after completing the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}, a [[Zora]] in [[Hero's Cave]] will reward him with the Master Sword. However, if Link has already completed the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest}}, then the legendary blade will be found resting in its pedestal in the [[Lost Woods]] of northwestern [[Holodrum]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
If Link discovers the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Clock Shop Secret|Clock Shop Secret]] after completing the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest}}, the legendary blade will be given to him by the Old Woman in [[South Lynna]]. However, if the Secret is discovered before the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest}} is complete, then Link will have to bring the [[Broken Sword]] that was given to him by the [[Old Zora]] who lives at the [[Coast of No Return]] to [[Patch]] on top of [[Restoration Wall]] for repairs. If the ceremony is successful, Patch will restore the Broken Sword. This will give Link an upgraded version of his sword, depending on what he had when the ceremony began. If Link had the Wooden Sword, Patch's restoration will give him the Noble Sword. If Link had the Noble Sword, the restoration will give him the Master Sword.

To obtain the Master Sword if Patch repairs the Broken Sword into the Noble Sword, the player will need to use the Clock Shop Secret given to them by the Old Woman. By taking her secret back to Oracle of Seasons and obtaining the Master Sword in that game, they will get a new secret. This secret can be given to Farore in {{OoA|-}}, and the player will then obtain the Master Sword.

==={{ALBW|-}}===

The Master Sword in {{ALBW}} is the same legendary blade from {{ALttP|-}}, which is once again found inside of the Lost Woods. Much like in the Super NES title, the Master Sword is obtained by collecting the three [[Pendants of Virtue]]. One of the Pendants is given to Link by Princess Zelda, and the others are found in the [[Tower of Hera (A Link Between Worlds)|Tower of Hera]] and the [[House of Gales]]. The Master Sword is the only thing that has the power to break the Barrier surrounding Hyrule Castle, which is necessary for Link to face [[Yuga]] atop the Castle, and subsequently travel to [[Lorule]] for the first time. When obtained, it replaced the previously-used [[Forgotten Sword]].
[[File:ALBW Master Sword Lv2 Model.png|thumb|262x262px|Master Sword Lv2, the first upgrade in ''A Link Between Worlds'']]
Like ''A Link to the Past'', the Master Sword can be optionally upgraded twice by collecting pieces of [[Master Ore]] hidden throughout Lorule. By taking the Master Sword to the [[Blacksmith (A Link Between Worlds)|Blacksmith]] in Hyrule with two pieces of Master Ore, he will upgrade it to the [[Master Sword Lv2|'''Master Sword Lv2''']]. The Master Sword Lv2 is twice as powerful as the standard Master Sword and has a tempered, red blade, making it almost identical to the Tempered Sword from {{ALttP|-}}. When Link collects the remaining two pieces of Master Ore and takes them to the Blacksmith in Lorule, he will upgrade the sword again to the [[Master Sword Lv3|'''Master Sword Lv3''']]. This final upgrade of the Master Sword doubles its damage again, making it eight times as powerful as the [[Forgotten Sword]]. It also has a golden blade, making it almost identical to the Golden Sword from the {{ALttP|-}}. However, unlike the SNES title, the upgraded versions of the Master Sword do not affect its swinging sound effect when attacking enemies.

Despite its increased strength, the Master Sword Lv2's [[Sword Beam]] does the same base amount of damage as the Master Sword. The StreetPass [[Shadow Link]]s and [[Gramps]], the elderly man in [[Kakariko Village]], are able to wield the Master Sword Lv3. The former can be attributed to the Shadow Links being mere shades,<ref>{{Cite|"Their characters become Shadow Link characters in this world. As in ''A Link to the Past'', the Master Sword's Lv2 and Lv3 forms, although their increased strength gives Link an advantage, are not required to complete the game, as [[Yuga Ganon]] can be defeated with the blade in its normal form.

[[File:TotK Master Sword Model.png|thumb|305x305px|The Master Sword heavily damaged by the Gloom]]
When Link and Zelda reach the [[Imprisoning Chamber]] and the mummy that is [[Ganondorf]] reawakens, Link attempts to use the Master Sword to block a deadly Gloom attack targeted at the princess. However, the Gloom instead mangles his right arm, nearly killing the hero and severely decays the Master Sword. When Link tries to block another tendril of Gloom, in a shocking twist of fate, the sacred sword shatters into many pieces, with the tip of the blade flying towards and cutting Ganondorf's cheek. As Ganondorf himself begins to speak, he realizes that the sword he had just corroded and Link had broken on the Gloom tendrils was the legendary Blade of Evil's Bane that seals the darkness, expressing genuine disappointment at having beaten his fated adversary's best weapon so easily (since the invincibility that protected was gone again). As he causes [[Hyrule Castle]] to rise and Zelda to plummet down a deep chasm, Link tosses the now heavily damaged Master Sword aside in a futile effort to save the princess, but she gets sent back in time to the Ancient Hyrule.

When the Hero of the Wild comes to in the [[Room of Awakening]], the [[Decayed Master Sword|'''Decayed Master Sword''']] is stuck in a nearby rock, with only the hilt and the badly decayed base of the blade intact (much like a broken [[Giant's Knife]] in ''Ocarina of Time''). Link can pull it out with no problem, but in its current damaged state it has a pitiable base strength of just 1, cannot be used as a [[Fuse]]d Weapon, and will not recover energy when it runs out. At the end of the [[Temple of Time]] and his journey on [[The Great Sky Island]], Link discovers a glowing yellow orb. The Decayed Master Sword chimes, instructing Link to place it in the glowing orb. The effect of [[Recall]] is then played out as the sword is sent back countless millennia, where Zelda awaits to retrieve it.

The Decayed Master Sword communicates to Zelda that Link is safe and that it traveled back in time to find her and recover its strength. Now realizing why she was sent so far back in time, Zelda devises a plan to repair the Master Sword and render it able to stand against Ganondorf. Princess Zelda and [[Mineru]] discuss the sword and all the Great Deku Tree told the former about strengthening it with sacred light, with Mineru realizing Zelda intends to undergo the forbidden act of draconification to strengthen the sword in her light, Mineru protests, before ultimately forcing herself to accept Zelda's determination to sacrifice everything to defeat Ganondorf.

With their plan in place, Zelda and Mineru begin preparations to ensure Link would be sufficiently prepared to face the Demon King once more. Finally, with Mineru's spirit inside the [[Purah Pad]] and stowed inside a [[Steward Construct]], the rest of the Sages having promised their descendants would awaken to support Link when the time comes, and the Great Sky Island containing everything Link needs to get him started on his journey, Zelda swallows her [[Secret Stone]], takes the Decayed Master Sword, and transforms into the [[Light Dragon]], which proceeds to roam the skies for countless millennia until it reaches the present day.

Link may come across the corrupted Korok Forest during his journey and purge the Great Deku Tree of Gloom corruption, although Mineru will point Link to the Forest if he hasn't retrieved the sword when she gives him her vow. Link informs the tree that the Master Sword has disappeared. However, the Great Deku Tree senses the sword's presence, stating it appears to be in the sky, and moving at that.<ref>{{Cite|In the sky... I feel the Master Sword's presence strongly in this area. But...what could this mean? The Master Sword seems to be moving.|Great Deku Tree|TotK}}</ref>

Link can find the Master Sword embedded in a growth on the head of the Light Dragon. However, when he attempts to pull it, the Light Dragon, who has temporarily lost awareness of her previous identity as Princess Zelda, reacts violently and attempts to shake him off. Link needs at least two full [[Stamina Wheel]]s to hold on long enough for the Light Dragon to roar and climb above the clouds, where a golden sky awaits. 

The Light Dragon comes to a stop as the growth holding the Master Sword retracts, allowing Link to pull it out with no difficulty. The glowing sheen covering most of the sword peels away to reveal the restored blade, its inconsistent hue marking the severe damage it sustained, but otherwise fully restored and strong enough to stand against the Demon King (and invincible again). Alternatively, if Link hasn't recovered the sword by the final battle, when the Light Dragon intervenes to assist Link against the [[Demon Dragon]], the same sequence will play, beginning from when the growth recedes, before the battle proper. Link will not require the additional Stamina to retrieve the blade for this process.

The Master Sword in its restored state functions similarly to how it does in {{BotW|-}}, including its ability to regenerate itself after "breaking" (represented by the trait, "Revitalized Sword of Legend") with a few key differences. First of all, the damage output is still 30, but it's not shown on the weapons menu at all. It also retains a reawakened version that activates in the presence of Gloom-wielding enemies (i.e. most of the enemies in [[The Depths]], [[Gloom Spawn]]), but due to the relative abundance, this only increases its damage by 15 compared to the previous game's 30. Additionally, like most other weapons in the game its durability has been reduced. Finally, it can be used as a base for a Fused Weapon, with a few limitations: the fused material doesn't increase durability, and the added damage isn't increased in the presence of Gloom enemies. The fused material also cannot be recovered by [[Pelison]] because the Master Sword cannot be taken out of Link's inventory; reflecting this, it will only appear on the blade while it is in use as a green apparition, and when not in use, glowing green runes appear on the blade to indicate a fusion. During the final confrontation against Ganondorf, if Link has retrieved the blade, there will be an extra cutscene where he draws the blade in preparation for battle, and if the blade is in its recharge period, it would be fully restored immediately.

Gameplay-wise, the Master Sword as Link wields it during the intro of the game underneath Hyrule Castle has its own slot and stats. Using [[Glitches in Tears of the Kingdom|glitches]], it is possible to acquire it in a normal save: its name and description both read "MsgNotFound", it has a base attack power of 30 (the same as the revitalized sword), and is unbreakable, although it cannot be fused with any material without further glitches.

==Discrepancies==
Until the release of {{SS|-}}, the origin of the Master Sword was never seen and only alluded to; mistranslations from Japanese have further clouded the facts. For instance, according to the English manual of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', after [[Ganon]] entered the [[Sacred Realm|Golden Land]] and obtained the [[Triforce]], the people of [[Hyrule]] forged a special [[Sword]] resistant to magic, which could even repulse powers granted by the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Suspecting that Ganon's power was based on the Triforce's magic, the people of Hyrule forged a sword resistant to magic which could repulse even powers granted by the Triforce. This mighty weapon became known as the blade of evil's bane, or the Master Sword. |game= ALttP |page= 5}}</ref> The Seven Wise Men ([[Sage|Sages]]) searched for one worthy of wielding the Master Sword, but never found such a person, leaving them, with the aid of the [[Knight of Hyrule|knights of Hyrule]], to seal away Ganon instead.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= As the Seven Wise Men searched for a valiant person to take up the Master Sword, Ganon's evil army swarmed from the tainted Golden Land into Hyrule and attacked the castle. |game= ALttP |page= 6}}</ref> Although the mistranslated English manual states that the Master Sword was not created until after Ganon invaded the Golden Land, the original Japanese manual states that it was forged long before the [[Imprisoning War]].<ref name="Origin">{{Cite manual|quote=However, it could not be assumed that only a good person would get their hands on the Triforce. For that reason, the people of Hyrule were told by the gods to make something that would repulse any evil that may kidnap the Triforce: the blade of evil's bane.|game= ALttP|version= Japanese|page= 7}}</ref> The location of the blade was already forgotten by the time of the Imprisoning War, and no hero was found worthy of wielding it before [[Link]].<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= The Sages first had to search for the existence of the Master Sword and a hero to use it.|game= ALttP|version= Japanese|page= 7}}</ref> This notion that the Master Sword was forged to protect the Triforce is supported by the fact that [[The Goddess Hylia|Hylia]] (the first wielder of the [[Goddess Sword]]) was the Triforce's guardian.
[[Category:A Link to the Past Articles Needing Attention]]<!-- The quotes from the Japanese manual should contain the original Japanese text and use Template:Romanize to translate. Given our canon policy [[Guidelines:Canon]] it's questionable whether we should be using this material here at all. -->

Both the English and Japanese manuals of {{ALttP|-}} describe that the Master Sword was forged by the people of Hyrule by the order of God or Gods, plurality depending on the context of the original Japanese, and not necessarily by the Ancient Sages. Albeit implied in the Wind Waker, it isn't until {{TP|-}} that [[Princess Zelda#Twilight Princess|Princess Zelda]] officially mentions that the Ancient Sages crafted the Master Sword, specifying who are the people of Hyrule that made the weapon.<ref name="Quote1">{{Cite|Our world is one of balance... Just as there is light to drive away darkness, so, too, is there benevolence to banish evil. Head for the sacred grove that lies deep within the lands guarded by the spirit Faron. There you will find the blade of evil's bane that was crafted by the wisdom of the ancient sages...the Master Sword. The Master Sword is a sacred blade that evil can never touch. Evil cloaks you like a dark veil... and that blade is the only thing that can cleave it.|Zelda|TP}}</ref>[[File:ALTTP-Master Sword3.png|thumb|{{ALttP|-}}<nowiki/>'s ending, claiming the Master Sword forever sleeps, despite being reawakened again in almost all later games|240x240px]]
The ending of {{ALttP|-}} claims that the Master Sword "sleeps again, forever" after Link places it back in its shrine in the [[Lost Woods]], but {{ALBW|-}}, set after this game, shows Link once again taking up the Master Sword (plus nearly every later game since has it seen again), indicating that this line was merely metaphorical.

{{SS|-}}, which takes place at the beginning of the [[Zelda Timeline]] according to ''Hyrule Hystoria'', reveals the complete origin of the Master Sword, revealing that it was forged into its well-known current form from being the Goddess Sword, supposedly created by the goddess Hylia.<ref name="Goddess Sword" /><ref name="tempering" /><ref name="bless" /> And yet this too is innacurate, because the original Japanese text of ''Skyward Sword'' never mentions the goddess Hylia being the creator of the blade, only that the goddess "entrusted" the "strongest" weapon and Fi to the chosen hero, Link. From this it can be understood that the Ancient Sages still remain the original forgers of the weapon, having done so by the command of Hylia. Link later on tempered the Goddess Sword and made it the Master Sword. It isn't until ''Breath of the Wild'' that Goddess Hylia goes on to be credited as the creator of the sword.

== Reception ==
[[File:OoT3D Child Link pulling Master Sword.gif|left|thumb|267x267px|The Master Sword being pulled by Link in many games such as ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'' is one of the most famous scenes in the franchise and is similar to the Excalibur story]]
Since its first appearance in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', the Master Sword is widely known as one of the most recognizable swords, if not weapons altogether, in the video game industry, and the iconic moments of [[Link]] pulling and returning the sacred blade from its [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|pedestal]] throughout the majority of games and other media of the franchise are often compared to [[wikipedia:Excalibur|The Sword in the Stone]] (Caliburn) from the British mythologies of King Arthur (at a point in time before he got Excalibur from Nimue), with many fans calling the Master Sword a modern retelling of that story. Although not outright stated by [[Nintendo]], it is likely the Master Sword was inspired by the Excalibur (ironically, in the French release of ''A Link to the Past'', the Master Sword was named '''Excalibur'''). Many critics and fans alike also enjoy the sound of the legendary weapon's name or titles, claim it has a rather cool or unique appearance compared to different swords from [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] itself or most other media including when upgraded, and even widely considered it as arguably the greatest weapon in video game history, in spite of not being the strongest in the franchise. Some in the ''Zelda'' community believe the Blade of Evil's Bane (alongside the [[Hylian Shield]]) to be equally as important as Link himself. The Master Sword also inspired similar blades in some modern action-adventure/fantasy games.

[[Nintendo Power]] described the Master Sword as one of the best weapons in gaming, citing that it is more than just a powerful sword, but also integral to Link's adventures and development as a character. Ron Whitaker for ''The Escapist'' magazine commented "''The Master Sword doesn't look all that special. It's not that big, it doesn't have flames rising from it, and it doesn't look all that intimidating. But in the hands of the right person, it can save the world''".

===={{HW|-}}====
[[File:HW True Master Sword.png|200px|right|thumb|The True Power of the Master Sword being awoken in {{HW|-}}]]
The Master Sword, combined with the [[Hylian Shield]], appears as one of Link's unlockable weapons in {{HW}}. It is obtained by completing "[[The Sacred Sword]]" [[Scenario]] in [[Legend Mode]]. It performs similarly to Link's Hylian Sword moveset, although with a base attack power of 300, being much more powerful than most obtainable Swords. It also performs a wider and faster Spin Attack combo than the other Swords. After completing the "[[A War of Spirit]]" Scenario, Link can fire [[Sword Beam]]s with certain combos at full health. It is of the Light element.

Unlike other weapons, the Master Sword is one-of-a-kind and multiples cannot be obtained from weapon drops. In {{HW|-}} it has one [[Skill]] known as Evil's Bane, which is unlocked after collecting all Level 1, 2 and 3 Weapons found in Scenarios in Legend Mode and in the [[Adventure Map]] in [[Adventure Mode]] and then accumulating 25,000 KOs with the Master Sword. In {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}, it has two Skills, one being Evil's Bane, and the other being Exorcism. In {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}, Evil's Bane is unlocked in the same manner as in {{HW|-}}, but the KOs requirement is lowered to 10,000. In {{HW|L|-}}, Exorcism requires unlocking all of the Level 4 Weapons found in the [[Twilight Map]] and the [[Termina Map]] in Adventure Mode and then accumulating 25,000 KOs with the Master Sword while in {{HW|DE|-}}, all Weapons, including the [[Multi-Element Weapon]]s, must be collected but the KOs required is lowered to 15,000. The Evil's Bane Skill increases the Master Sword's base attack power by 200 and, in {{HW|-}}, also unlocks the Legendary seal for other Warriors' weapons that have that Skill available. In {{HW|L|-}}and {{HW|DE|-}}, the Exorcism Skill unlocks the Legendary seal and also increases the Master Sword's base power by an additional 400 points, bringing the total to 900. Unlocking the Evil's Bane skill awards the "True Master Sword" [[Medal]] in {{HW|-}}, while Exorcism does this in {{HW|L|-}}and {{HW|DE|-}}.

In the Legend Mode Scenarios, after obtaining the Sword, Link becomes cocky and overconfident in his abilities. In "A War of Spirit", while Link goes to face [[Cia]] at the [[Temple of Souls]], she uses his overconfidence against him by using the darkness in his heart to create [[Dark Link]]s to fight him repeatedly. With the aid of his allies, he is able to overcome these threats, and the bond shared between them awakens the True Power of the Master Sword. From that point, at full health, Link can fire Sword Beams with certain combos. After Link defeats Ganon in "[[Liberation of the Triforce]]", he returns the Master Sword to its pedestal at the [[Temple of the Sacred Sword]] to rest.

==Trivia==
*The Master Sword is the secondary symbol of {{TLoZ|Series}} after the [[Triforce]], being displayed in title logos starting in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' and appearing as [[Link]]'s main weapon in other games such as the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] and {{SCII}}. It is also by far the most popular and iconic [[Sword]] in the franchise's community.
*According to [[Takashi Tezuka]], he credited screenwriter Kensuke Tanabe for conceiving the popular scene when Link first obtains the Master Sword in ''A Link to the Past'' which became a staple for the franchise. It established the mythological importance of the blade within the series as the only sword with the power to repel evil, but also emphasized its symbolic significance. Tezuka also noted that at this moment the Master Sword recognizes Link himself as a hero. He explained that "''Our main aim was to show the birth of a hero in a scene fitting of The Legend of Zelda, and overlap this with a sense of achievement for the player that they have been recognized as a hero after having overcome many challenges.''"
*''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords|Four Swords]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap|The Minish Cap]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|Phantom Hourglass]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes|Tri Force Heroes]]'', and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]'' are the mainline games so far in the series where the Master Sword makes no appearance whatsoever. It should be noted, though, that the first two installments on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] predates its first appearance from ''A Link to the Past''.
**The ''Oracle'' games, in spite of its strongest Sword having its name, it's actually the Noble Sword.
*The design of the Master Sword is based on a bastard sword or longsword, a type of sword commonly used by European nations in the [[wikipedia:Middle_Ages|Middle Ages]].[[File:South Park TSOT Elf Blade.webp|thumb|154x154px|An example of a Master Sword-like weapon from pop culture, taken from ''[https://southpark.fandom.com/wiki/South_Park:_The_Stick_of_Truth South Park: The Stick of Truth]'']]
*Much like Link himself and perhaps the [[Hylian Shield]], due to the Master Sword's popularity, it is often referenced in [[The Legend of Zelda in Popular Culture|popular culture]] and can also be seen in a few other Nintendo games as an [[References to The Legend of Zelda in other media|Easter Egg]]. In terms of merchandise based on the franchise, it is one of the most successful.
*The [[Four Sword]] in the ''Four Swords'' games have similar abilities as the Master Sword.
*In {{TWW}}, according to the original Japanese text, the Master Sword was only sealing [[Ganondorf]]'s minions and not his magic, as can be observed in-game.<ref name="ganon" /><ref>{{Cite|{{Romanize|その剣は、魔を撃退する{{Color|TWW Vermilion|退魔の剣}} であると同時に、 我が魔族を封じていた、いまいましい{{Color|TWW Vermilion|封印}} そのものなのだよ！|Sono Ken wa, Ma o Gekitai suru Taima no Kendearu to Dōjini, Waga Mazoku o Fūjite ita, imaimashī Fūin sonomonona noda yo!|That sword is the Anti-Demon Sword that repels demons. However, it is also the annoying seal that was sealing my demon tribe.|3}}|Ganondorf|TWW|Japanese version}} <small>(Translation by [http://forums.legendsalliance.com/topic/14202-translation-of-japanese-game-texts/page-27#entry404295 jacensolo06])</small></ref>
*A different weapon known as a {{Few|Master Sword}} appears in ''{{Few|Fire Emblem: Mystery of the Emblem}}'', released shortly after {{ALttP|-}}. While it bears the same name and is a powerful weapon, it has a very different appearance to the weapon from {{TLoZ|-}} series. It also appears in the [[Nintendo DS]] remake, and in ''{{Few|Fire Emblem: Thracia 776}}'', where its appearance is closer to that of the ''Zelda'' blade. It also appears in ''{{Few|Fire Emblem Heroes}}'' under the ownership of Reinhardt: Thunder's Sword, where it is called "Meisterschwert".
*The Snake Killer weapon from the Japan-exclusive [[Game Boy]] title ''{{Wp|Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru|The Frog For Whom the Bell Tolls}}'' is quite similar to the Master Sword. The Snake Killer was pulled from the ground in an area called the "Ice Cave", and was necessary to defeat the Final Boss, Delarin, who was a snake. An ancient tablet regarding the Snake Killer had this inscribed on it: "When thou seeth thy true friend, the golden sword shalt awaken." When the spring bell has rung, and Richard turns back into a human, the Snake Killer begins to glow, and Prince Sable uses the sword to slay Delarin.[[File:LA Level 2 Sword Artwork.png|thumb|188x188px|The Koholint Sword from ''Link's Awakening'', which shares a similar appearance]]
*Although not canonically the Master Sword itself, the official artwork of the [[Koholint Sword|L-2 Sword]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'' strongly resembles it, and when obtained in-game, the blade appears from a downward position and is shown to possess great magical powers. It's unknown if the weapon was originally intended to be the Blade of Evil's Bane during development.
*The Master Sword when it glows with the [[Power to Repel Evil]] is sometimes compared to the popular [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lightsaber Lightsaber] weapon from the ''[https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars Star Wars]'' franchise by fans. This is especially true with its upgraded forms (even more so with the [[Master Sword Lv2|Tempered Sword]]) due to their colorful blades.
** In ''Super Smash Bros.'', playable fighters can use [[smashwiki:Beam_Sword|Beam Swords]], which such items strongly resemble Lightsabers. In the case of Link, he dual-wields both it and the Master Sword (or [[Kokiri Sword]] as [[Young Link]]) for dramatic effect.

* In Russian localization, the Master Sword is referred to as '''Supreme Sword'''.
* At the end of {{SS}}, because of the time travel involved, the [[Goddess Sword]] and Master Sword exist simultaneously. The Master Sword is within the [[Sealed Temple]], while the Goddess Sword is up in [[Skyloft]]. This likely explains its appearance in both ''Breath of the Wild'' and ''Tears of the Kingdom'', with the latter being having it as a reward for repairing the Giant Goddess Statue in the [[Forgotten Temple]] or as an unlockable after scanning the ''Skyward Sword'' Link [[amiibo]].
*''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'' are the only games so far in which the {{Term|Series|Master Sword}}, already seemingly at its full power (as opposed to its powered-down appearances in {{TWW|-}}, {{SS|-}}, {{BotW}}, and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''), can be permanently upgraded to deal even more damage.
*In {{FSAE}}, completing the [[Realm of Memories]] unlocks the Master Sword as Link's new sword. However, Link remains divided into four even after obtaining the sword, which implies that this sword is not the same as the true Master Sword, merely an upgrade of the conventional Four Sword, similar to the ''Oracle'' games. It is interesting to note that the Four Sword's original form, the [[Picori Blade]], greatly resembled the Master Sword.
[[File:Stone ganon.png|thumb|right|A rare occurrence of the arch-villain being eliminated by the sacred blade in the series, Ganondorf turns into stone upon the Master Sword being impaled into his skull, as seen in ''The Wind Waker''|232x232px]]
*Although most games in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series have the Master Sword simply wound or defeat [[Ganon]] or his incarnations such as [[Demise]] by a finishing blow followed by being sealed away, three titles so far - {{TWW|-}}, ''Twilight Princess'', and ''Tears of the Kingdom'', are very few exceptions where the Blade of Evil's Bane outright kills the main antagonist similar to how he was slain by the [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]] in [[The Legend of Zelda|''The Legend of Zelda'']] and ''A Link to the Past'' (although in these cases, he didn't battle Link in his true form, but as merely Ganondorf or the [[Demon Dragon]] in ''Tears of the Kingdom'').
**Link's victorious final blows onto his archnemesis in the aforementioned games as well as ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' by the Master Sword are often considered some of the most climactic [[Final Boss]] finishes of the ''Zelda'' franchise, and even the video game industry as a whole by fans.
*[[Dark Link]] in {{OoT|-}}, ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|A Link to the Past & Four Swords]]'', as well as the {{SSB|-}} games appear to be wielding the Master Sword (or its upgrades) himself, though it is likely due to gameplay mechanics.
*In the {{ALttP|Himekawa}}, it is said that the Master Sword was made by the [[Sages]] by instruction of the Gods.<ref>{{Cite|The King called the 7 Sages and the Guardian Knights to seal the origin of the evil flow. *Interestingly, the jingle of opening a large [[Treasure Chest]] in the 3D games is a heavily remixed version of the Master Sword's iconic theme.
*While the Master Sword is usually used in one hand, it is large enough to be used with two hands, as the weapon is that of a two-handed broadsword.
*In {{TP}}, the gem and round area above the grip of the Master Sword are engraved with box-like symbols reminiscent of Hylian. In {{TPHD}}, the symbols were removed from the gem, and the cross guard engravings were replaced with the {{Wp|Latin alphabet}} represented by equivalent characters from the {{Wp|Greek alphabet}}. The exceptions are 'j' and 'v', for which there are no Greek equivalents; other characters are used instead.
[[File:Master Sword symbol OoT.png|thumb|167x167px|The Eye Symbol on the Master Sword as seen in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
*A hidden detail of the Master Sword in ''Ocarina of Time'', while the weapon is on Link's hand, the [[Eye Symbol|Sheikah symbol]] is visible on the bottom of the blade just above its cross guard where its yellow gems should be. The same emblem is also visible on the Biggoron's Sword as well. It is unknown why such detail was added onto either sword's model in-game, as both never have the Eye Symbol in any official artwork, though it is possibly by memory limitations. This implies two separate models of the Master Sword were created for the original [[Nintendo 64]] version, as both gems are clearly visible when the blade is on its pedestal or knocked away from Ganon at the final battle.
*Due to the time travel elements in {{TotK|-}}, there are two Master Swords in existence at the same time in Hyrule -- the "original" Master Sword used by the past hero who sealed [[Calamity Ganon]] and then used by Link in {{BotW|-}}, and the Revitalized Master Sword possessed by the Light Dragon. Both swords exist simultaneously until the Master Sword is sent back in time at the Temple of Time, at which point only one Master Sword exists in Hyrule.
**''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets|Ancient Stone Tablets]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', and spin-off games are also cases where more than one Master Sword can be seen, though in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series and ''SoulCalibur II'', this occurs when a second Link is chosen and so forth.
*Although ''Ocarina of Time''<nowiki/>'s release predates ''Skyward Sword'' by 13 years, the Master Sword glowing before the final blow to defeat Ganon is possibly the spirit of [[Fi]] telling Link to finish the Demon King off.
*The [[Lokomo Sword]] from {{ST|-}} and [[Phantom Sword]] from {{PH|-}} both bear a striking resemblance to the Master Sword though their abilities are different. Both swords may have been forged to purposely resemble the Master Sword due to its legendary status acquired during Link the [[Hero of Time]]'s usage.[[File:SCII Soul Calibur sword near Link.png|thumb|217x217px|Link with Soul Calibur in ''SoulCalibur II'', a legendary sword in the ''Soul'' series comparable to the Master Sword]]
*Despite being unrelated to ''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' series or other [[Nintendo]] releases, the titular [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Calibur Soul Calibur] weapon in ''SoulCalibur II'', which Link encounters upon defeating [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Inferno Inferno] (the main source of [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge Soul Edge]) has powers strikingly similar to that of the Master Sword and Four Sword. Much like those sacred blades, this powerful holy sword can only be wielded by those who are pure of heart, making Link indeed suitable for its worth. Strangely, the ''Zelda'' main protagonist himself does not have an unlockable weapon based on it, unlike a few other characters like [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Xianghua Xianghua] or [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Siegfried Siegfried]. This is likely due to the fact that as Link already has the Master Sword in-game (alongside the [[Magical Sword]] and [[Great Fairy's Sword]]), it isn't necessary for him to have a second weapon designed to banish evil.
**Ironically, Link has a version of the evil Soul Edge to be unlocked (as with all other playable fighters) in its [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge_(Complete) terrifying complete form] that [[Soul Edge (Complete)|takes on the shape of his Master Sword and Hylian Shield]]. Though technically out of character for Link, it is excusable in that this weapon can be controlled by anyone who has a strong will regardless of their alignment, in which the Hero of Time himself clearly has expert disciplinary skills (as he is suggested to be a [[Knight of Hyrule|Hylian Knight]] according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]''), and he is also no stranger to dark powers, as seen with the [[Fierce Deity Mask|Fierce Deity's Mask]] in ''Majora's Mask''.
*In {{TotK|-}}, when fusing an object to the Master Sword, it is implied that the Master Sword itself absorbs the strength and ability of the object due to the fact that the blade will return to its normal shape with Zonai text appearing along the blade.
*During early development of ''Ocarina of Time'', the Master Sword appears to be a larger version of the Kokiri Sword and its scabbard is also entirely wooden, which applies to the aforementioned blade as well.
*The [[Gilded Sword]] in ''Majora's Mask'' bare a slight resemblance to the Master Sword, due to their similar shape and strength.[[File:LinkandZeldaWW.png|thumb|Princess Zelda holding the Master Sword in ''The Wind Waker''|302x302px]]
*Excluding Link's Shadow, [[Princess Zelda]] and [[Gramps]] are the only other characters besides Link to be seen holding the Master Sword in the series. For the former, she can be seen giving the sacred blade back to Link in ''The Wind Waker'' after it is knocked out of the young hero's hands by Ganondorf. Similarly, the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|Himekawa manga adaptation of {{OoT|-}}]] has Zelda lift and throw the Master Sword to Link during the final battle with Ganon. In {{BotW|-}}, she is seen inserting the Master Sword into its pedestal in the [[Korok Forest]] so that it may recover alongside Link for the next century. In {{TotK|-}}, she is seen again holding the sword when it travels back through time, when Zelda talks to [[Mineru]] regarding her plan, and also when she swallows her [[Secret Stone]]. As the [[Light Dragon]], Zelda wielded, healed, and empowered the Master Sword for thousands of years. None of those cases have the princess herself use the Master Sword in combat like Link, however. [[King Rauru]] may have also held the Master Sword after saving Link and replacing his decayed arm. When Link finally awakens, Rauru tells him that his Master Sword is leaning against some rubble. After the sword becomes shattered, he is seen dropping the sword in order to try and catch Zelda but fails. He himself is caught by Rauru's arm but does not have the Master Sword in either hand, meaning Rauru must have also grabbed the sword knowing its importance. In the case of Gramps in ''A Link Between Worlds'', he is shown wielding the sacred blade at its [[Master Sword Lv3|strongest form]] when Link duels him after getting all 50 Challenge Medals. Unlike Zelda, he indeed uses the Master Sword in battle.
*According to {{HH|-}}, both the hilt of the Master Sword and [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]] were created from [[Timeshift Stone]]s. This likely explains their time-based abilities and similar color.
*Like other [[Sword|Swords]] in the series, it is possible for the Master Sword to be stolen by enemies. For example, [[Ghirahim]] in ''Skyward Sword'' is capable of stealing the sacred blade while revisiting his first and second fights.
*In the Japanese version of ''A Link to the Past'', due to the possibility of upgrading the Master Sword in-game, some sources call the standard version of the Blade of Evil's Bane as '''Master Sword Level 1'''. This can also accurately apply to ''A Link Between Worlds''.
*Like the Biggoron's Sword, the Master Sword in most games of the franchise is said to be unbreakable. However, in the [[Nintendo Switch]] installments, as with the former, this is not always the case, which was met with criticism by some fans. The Master Sword can be restored by sacred energy that makes it even stronger.[[File:OOT Master Sword landing perfectly on ground near Zelda.png|thumb|254x254px|The blade of the Master Sword falling squarely onto the ground after Ganon knocking it off of Link's hand in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
*In the cutscenes leading to the final battle of ''Ocarina of Time'' and ''The Wind Waker'', which both games had the Master Sword being sent flying out of Link's hands by Ganon's beast and human forms, respectively, showed the sacred blade landing near Princess Zelda, with the latter case narrowly missing her head. In the former, the Master Sword can be seen hitting directly onto the ground in an identical position as if it was on its pedestal.
*As seen in ''The Wind Waker'' and ''Tears of the Kingdom'', Ganondorf is well-aware that the Master Sword is one of his major weaknesses due to its power to repel evil. In the former, he even warns the Hero of Winds that it is impossible to defeat him when the sacred blade is dull and powerless.
