{{Term/Store|Goronade||Series, OoA}} is an {{Term|OoA|Item|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|OoA|Goronade}} is given to {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} by the {{Term|OoA|Goron|link}} guarding the steps to the upper part of {{Term|OoA|Rolling Ridge|link}} in the past, in trade for the {{Term|OoA|Goron Vase|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What a fine Goron Vase! Please! Let me have that vase! I'll trade you some Goronade for it!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> {{Term|OoA|Link}} then must give the {{Term|OoA|Goronade}} to the {{Term|OoA|Goron|link}} running the {{Term|OoA|Big Bang Game|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm wide awake now! Thanks! I'm gonna open up shop! Want to give my game a try?|Goron|OoA}}</ref> so that he can then play the game and win the {{Term|OoA|Old Mermaid Key|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|OoA|Goronade}} seems to be a play on the popular energy drink "{{Wp|Gatorade}}", or simply a combination of 'Goron' and 'lemonade'.
