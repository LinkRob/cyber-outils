"{{Term/Store|The Ancient Rito Song||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Ancient Rito Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Rito Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Bedoli|link}} pacing on {{Term|BotW|Revali's Landing|link}}. She has been so happy now that {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} is no longer in danger that she began to sing an old song without realizing.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} has been appeased. It just sends my heart soaring! I didn't even realize I had started singing this old song.|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he overheard her,<ref>{{Cite|Did you hear me singing it?|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref> but she becomes embarrassed when he wants her to teach him the song.<ref>{{Cite|[Teach it to me!] Huh? Well that's embarrassing...|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bedoli}} starts the song, which describes the heart of a pillar that lights up when the sun is high.<ref>{{Cite|The pride of the Rito, pillar in the sky,<br/>its heart lights up, when the sun is high.|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref> However, she is unable to remember the rest of the song.<ref>{{Cite|...Uhhh. There's more to the song, but I forget the rest of the words.|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref> For the rest of the lyrics, she directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to seek out her sister, {{Term|BotW|Laissa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You should ask {{Color|BotW Blue|my sister}}, Laissa, if you want to know the rest.|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} begins.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has found {{Term|BotW|Laissa}}, he will ask her to sing the last half of the song.<ref>{{Cite|So Bedoli told you I would sing you the last half of the ancient Rito song? Her memory really is the worst...|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> She agrees to help, but first {{Term|BotW|Link}} must recite the half he learned from {{Term|BotW|Bedoli}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can help you...but there is a catch! Recite the first half from memory, and I will teach you the second half.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to successfuly recall the lyrics, {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} will keep her promise.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! You got every word right! Well, a promise is a promise. I'll teach you the last half of the song.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> The second half describes the pillar's heart shining on an incomplete path, but a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} would be able to stir its soul.<ref>{{Cite|The heart shines upon a path not whole,<br/>but a warming flame can stir its soul.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} remarks to {{Term|BotW|Link}} how profound it is.<ref>{{Cite|That's the second half of the ancient Rito song. Profound, isn't it?|Laissa|BotW}}</ref>

South of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}. From this angle, the hole in the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village's}} pillar looks like a heart. As the sun passes behind the pillar, it will cast a heart-shaped ray of light towards the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can light either a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} on top of the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, which will cause {{Term|BotW|Bareeda Naag Shrine|link}} to emerge from the ground. Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} has surfaced, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
