The {{Term/Store|Sanctum Observation Room||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 253}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Sanctum Observation Room}} is located on the third floor of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. From this vantage point, all of {{Term|BotW|Central Hyrule|link}} can be seen.<ref name="CaC"/> A hole in the center of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum Observation Room|display=Room}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} a view of the {{Term|BotW|Champions' Hall|link}} below.

Before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, those in the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum Observation Room}} were able to see all the way down to the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}. However, the top of {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|link}} has blocked this view. Unlike other parts of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum Observation Room}} has avoided much of the damage caused by {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/>
