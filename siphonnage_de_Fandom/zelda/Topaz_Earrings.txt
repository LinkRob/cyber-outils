The {{Term/Store|Topaz Earrings|Topaz Earrings|Series, BotW, HWAoC}} are a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Topaz Earrings}}<br/>Earrings made by Gerudo craft workers. They're made with topaz, a gem that harnesses the power of lightning to increase electricity resistance.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Topaz Earrings}}<br/>Earrings made by Gerudo craft workers. They're made with topaz, a gem that harnesses the power of lightning and gives the earrings a distinctive flash.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Topaz Earrings can be purchased in the [[Starlight Memories]] shop at [[Gerudo Town]] for 500 Rupees plus five [[Topaz]]es. When worn, they provide one level of Shock Resistance.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Topaz Earrings can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], though they cannot be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
