{{Term/Store|Martha's Landing||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Martha's Landing'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Martha's Landing}} is located between the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Faron Sea|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}}, {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Komo Shoreline|link}} sits to the west of {{Term|BotW|Martha's Landing}} and the {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach|link}} sits to the east. It is a long, irregular shaped beach with many {{Plural|BotW|Palm Tree|link}}. A nonfunctional {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} sits in the water, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can search to obtain {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}.

In the eastern edge of {{Term|BotW|Martha's Landing}}, there is a small section of beach that extends into the water. In the middle of this section, a {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus|link}} hides underground.

Next to the aforementioned {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} hides in a circle of {{Plural|BotW|Rock|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and gift {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed}} if he fills in the missing {{Term|BotW|Rock}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>

Far up in the middle of the beach near the cliffs, a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a bundle of 5 {{Plural|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}} is buried in the sand.
