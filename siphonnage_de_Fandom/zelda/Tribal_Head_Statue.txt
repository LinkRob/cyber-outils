'''Tribal Head Statues''' are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|PH, ST}} They are mostly involved in puzzles and riddles which {{Term|Series|Link|link}} must solve in order to gain access to new areas or treasures.

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} first encounters a {{Term|PH|Tribal Head Statue}} in the northern part of {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}. Here, he must activate the three statues scattered around and focus their light beam on the entrance of the {{Term|PH|Temple of Courage|link}}, which will grant the hero access to it.<ref>{{Cite|Only statues can urge the temple door open, so focus their light gaze on it!|N/A|PH}}</ref>

A single {{Term|PH|Tribal Head Statue}} is also located in {{Term|PH|Mercay Island|link}} near the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}}. To access it, {{Term|PH|Link}} must go through a cave which will take him to a higher portion of the island. Once again, Link can activate the statue's ray of light by hitting it with his {{Term|PH|Sword|link}}. By shining the beam parallel between the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King}} and {{Term|PH|Mercay Island}}, the {{Term|PH|Tribal Head Statue}} will reveal a bombable wall in the direction of its ray.<ref>{{Cite|A cliffside not so far from here has a hidden weak spot... Shine a beam parallel with a line between the temple and Mercay Tavern...|N/A|PH}}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, {{Plural|ST|Tribal Head Statue}} first appear in the {{Term|ST|Forest Sanctuary|link}}. They are located at each of the five sanctuaries scattered throughout the five different realms of {{Term|ST|New Hyrule|link}}. They are involved in {{Plural|ST|Puzzle|link}} that must be solved in order to gain access to the {{Term|ST|Lokomo|link}} sage that guards its respective sanctuary and realm. {{Plural|ST|Puzzle}} include connecting the two statues that are facing each other to solving a riddle concerning the {{Term|ST|Door of Friendship|link}}. 

There are also four giant {{Plural|ST|Tribal Head Statue}} placed around the {{Term|ST|Sand Realm|link}}, whose gazes cross where {{Term|ST|Link|link}} should bomb in the {{Term|ST|Sand Sanctuary|link}} to reveal the entrance that leads to {{Term|ST|Rael|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Find me by blasting the area where the gazes of the big statues meet.|N/A|ST}}</ref>
