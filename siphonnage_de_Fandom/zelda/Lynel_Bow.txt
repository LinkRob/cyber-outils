{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} used by regular {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}, particularly in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}. They become scarce as {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} tier up. However, in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} will use them instead, but they will still disappear with {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel}}. The only exception is the {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} on {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}}, which is always a regular {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}. Therefore, it may become the only source of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Bow}} in the world as the game goes on. 

{{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} make these {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} from rough metal.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can fire multiple {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} at a time, most commonly three, but occasionally one can be found that fires five {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} at once.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Bow}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and utilized by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are known to reflect the vicious nature of {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}} and are ideal for taking down fast-moving targets.<ref name="Inventory"/> 

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Bow}} conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will also sink if dropped in water, but can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, they do not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
