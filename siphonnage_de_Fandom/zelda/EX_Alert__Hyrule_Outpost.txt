{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Hyrule Outpost||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the first downloadable content pack, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Hyrule Outpost|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture a {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}} {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} to open a gate. The gate opens to an path containing a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} and some {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, while the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s next objective is to capture another {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} at the end of the road. The {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos}}, and when captured, a gate opens to an area containing the vicious monster. As the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights the vicious monster, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} will appear. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Herb|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Bird Egg|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
