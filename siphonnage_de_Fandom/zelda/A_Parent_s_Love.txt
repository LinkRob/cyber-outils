"{{Term/Store|A Parent's Love||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Parent's Love}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Tarrey Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} ocan come upon an argument between {{Term|BotW|Ruli|link}} and her husband {{Term|BotW|Hagie|link}} about their daughter {{Term|BotW|Hunnie|link}}. However, if he tries to speak to them, {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} will inform him that this is a personal matter and asks him to leave.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry... This is a very personal matter. Could you leave, please?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref>

To receive the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must listen in on {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} and {{Term|BotW|Hagie}}'s conversation through their open window. {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} laments that {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} is unwell and refuses to eat anything but cakes.<ref>{{Cite|Our daughter... The only thing she'll eat is {{Color|BotW Red|cake}}. Can't anything be done?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} recalls that they have searched everywhere for a cake that could make her better and wonders how much money they have spent.<ref>{{Cite|We've scoured the world, searching for a cake that would please her. None have. Imagine how much money we've spent...|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> His words anger {{Term|BotW|Ruli}}, who asks if he cares more about money or his daughter.<ref>{{Cite|Papa, excuse me!<br/>What's more important to you, money or our daughter?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} declares that she is desperate to find a cure for {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}}, even if she has to sell her soul to a monster.<ref>{{Cite|Even if I have to sell my soul to a {{Color|BotW Red|monster}}, I'll get my hands on a {{Color|BotW Red|cake}} that can cure our daughter!|Ruli|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can deliver a {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake|link}}, made with {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract|link}}, to {{Term|BotW|Ruli}}.<ref>{{Cite|What is... Is this a {{Color|BotW Blue|monster cake}}? I should give this to my daughter?|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> Though she is grateful to receive it, she notes its pungent odor.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much!<br/>Oh, wow. It's certainly quite pungent... Phew.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> However, she realizes she can not complain, as it could help {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} get better.<ref>{{Cite|I suppose I can't complain about things like that. If our daughter can get better thanks to this cake...|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> She decides to have her eat it right away.<ref>{{Cite|I'll try to get her to eat it right away.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll wake her up right away and try to get her to eat it.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref>

After eating the Monster Cake, {{Term|BotW|Hunnie}} gets her energy back, and is able to play outside again.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|monster cake}}... Our daughter wolfed it down with unbelievable gusto. It seems her health has returned to her!|Ruli|BotW}}</ref> As a way to thank him, {{Term|BotW|Ruli}} rewards Link with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't even know what to say-I'm just so thankful! Oh, I know. This isn't much, but please take it.|Ruli|BotW}}</ref>
