== Overview ==
[[File:Demise’s curse.png|thumb|295x295px|Screenshot of the Demise's speech of his curse upon his defeat in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'']]
The '''Curse of Demise''' is the notorious [[curse]] placed on [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] by [[Demise]] at the end of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]''<ref>{{Cite|Though this is not the end. My hate... never perishes. It is born anew in a cycle with no end! [...] Those like you... Those who share the blood of the goddess and the spirit of the hero... They are eternally bound to this curse. An incarnation of my hatred shall ever follow your kind, dooming them to wander a blood-soaked sea of darkness for all time!|Demise|Skyward Sword}}</ref>, immediately after his ultimate defeat by the hero. As the Demon King is dying after being struck down by the [[Master Sword]], Demise, impressed by Link's skills in the final battle, utters the powerful curse and warns him that an [[Ganon|incarnation of his hatred]] will forever follow his and Zelda's [[Hylian|kind]], destined to destroy the two special individuals carrying the bloodline of the Goddess [[The Goddess Hylia|Hylia]] and the spirit of the [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Legendary Hero]] protecting the [[Triforce]] - none other than the princess of [[Hyrule]] and Link - and if Link himself were to defeat (or even destroy) this incarnation, it will eternally reincarnate and the cycle repeats again, leading to all of the major events in [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Hyrule's history]]. Afterwards, the [[demon]] is sealed into the sacred blade to be slowly decayed into nothingness, ultimately killing Demise. It is the origin of Ganon and possibly other lead villains in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] such as [[Malladus]], [[Vaati]] and [[Bellum]], and is confirmed to be what caused [[Ganondorf]]’s descent into evil in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]]''. It is unclear whether or not Demise caused Link to reincarnate or himself simply placed the endless curse on the "Spirit of the Hero" itself.

== Trivia ==

* The "incarnation of my hatred" portion of the speech is different in the original Japanese version<ref>{{Cite|My hatred... The curse of the Demon Tribe... They shall continously go on reincarnating until the end of all times. [...] You people who possess the blood of the Goddess and the soul of hero shall... forever be unable to escape from this curse! This hatred and grudge... Its evolution shall forever painfully wander across this blood-stained "Dark Sea" along with you lowlifes forever!!|Demise|Skyward Sword — Japanese version, translated by Yamikawa of Zelda Universe}}</ref>, where it is worded more as a reminder of ''samsara'', the [[wikipedia:Buddhism|Buddhist]] cycle of life, death, and rebirth, regarding the respective rises and falls of good and evil. In this context, it comes off more as a warning that [[Demise]]'s death won't magically end the conflict between people and monsters, foreshadowing the rise of [[Ganon]], [[Vaati]], [[Malladus]], and many other great evils that would appear and terrorize [[Hyrule]].
* The curse is referenced in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess (Himekawa)|''Twilight Princess'' manga]] where the it was alluded to when [[Ganondorf]], the [[Gerudo]] form of Ganon, makes clear his motive for fighting [[Link]], and ultimately his suicide, to break the curse on him in his favor by ensuring his return to plague Hyrule in a later era. While each [[Manga|manga adaptation]] is self-contained and therefore not canon to the main series, this would canonically come to pass in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]'' when Ganondorf's reincarnation would defy Gerudo law and retrieve the [[Trident]] from the [[Pyramid]], becoming Ganon anew.
** This is further reinforced by unused text dummied out of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'' itself, which revealed that Ganondorf's dying words were meant to be a longer speech<ref>{{Cite|When one appears bearing the chosen power, so shall their antithesis invariably come into being. That is the destiny of those who bear the crests granted by the gods in which you believe. Each time a crest-bearer appears in this world, an era of bloodshed ever shall follow.|Ganondorf (Dummied out text)|Twilight Princess — Japanese version, translated by GlitterBerri}}</ref> than just a final statement of defiance.<ref>{{Cite|Do not think this ends here... The history of light and shadow will be written in blood!|Ganondorf|Twilight Princess}}</ref>
*** Similar to the Japanese version of Demise's speech, the officially-translated English version of the dummied-out text conveyed a slightly different message, where Ganondorf more directly alludes to how he is bound to return just as Link and [[Princess Zelda]] were, and also implies that the [[Golden Goddess|Goddesses]] themselves decreed this destiny.<ref>{{Cite|When the chosen ones appear... They are always born into this world in perfect balance. That is the destiny of the chosen. That is the fate decreed by your gods, the only path for those who bear their crests. When this world brings forth another marked as you are... Know too, that it shall also be visited by one of my blood.|Ganondorf (Dummied out text)|Twilight Princess — English version}}</ref>
