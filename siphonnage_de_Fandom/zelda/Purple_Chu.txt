{{Term/Store|Purple Chu|Purple Chus|Series, TP, TPHD}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref name=":0">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 170}}, 105</ref>

==Characteristics==
Purple Chu can be found in the [[Lakebed Temple]], [[Hyrule Field]], [[Snowpeak Ruins]], and [[Cave|Caves]]. Purple Chu are the result of a [[Red Chuchu|Red Chu]], [[Yellow Chuchu|Yellow Chu]], and [[Blue Chuchu|Blue Chu]] merging together.<ref name=":0" /> When Link attacks a giant Purple Chu with his sword, it will split into multiple smaller ones.<ref>''[https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Prima_Games_Twilight_Princess_Premiere_Edition_Strategy_Guide_Wii_Version Strategy Guide: Wii Version, Premier Edition]'' (Prima Games) pg. 220</ref> When defeated, they drop [[Purple Chu Jelly]], which Link can collect in an [[Empty Bottle]]. Drinking it will have a random effect on Link’s [[Life Gauge]].
