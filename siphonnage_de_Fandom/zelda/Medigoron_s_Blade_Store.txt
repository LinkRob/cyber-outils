'''Medigoron's Blade Store''' is a location in {{OoT}}. As its name implies, the store is owned by {{Term|OoT3D|Medigoron}} and it is where he forges and sells the {{Term|OoT3D|Giant's Knife}}.<ref>{{Cite|My Brotherrrr... Opened a new storrrre... It's Medigoron's Blade Storrrrrrrre... Howeverrrrr...  I am betterrrrrr at making bladessssss.|Biggoron|OoT}}</ref> It is located on the third floor of the {{Term|OoT3D|Goron City|link}}, but its entrance is initially blocked off by cracked walls that can be taken down with either {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb|link}} or the {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer|link}}.

The store itself is really cramped since {{Term|OoT3D|Medigoron}} takes up most of the room.<ref>{{Cite|They say that Medigoron didn't really think about his own size, so his store is really cramped.|Gossip Stone|OoT}}</ref>  The only things visible are a stove and a {{Term|OoT3D|Gossip Stone|link}}. {{Term|OoT3D|Medigoron}} only starts selling the {{Term|OoT3D|Giant's Knife}} for 200 {{Plural|OoT3D|Rupee|link}} during {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}'s adulthood.<ref>{{Cite|How about buying this knife for 200 Rupees?|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> Although the {{Term|OoT3D|Giant's Knife}} is twice as powerful than the {{Term|OoT3D|Master Sword|link}}, it will break after a few hits, greatly reducing its dealing power.<ref>{{Cite|It looks like there is a problem  with its durability... But still...|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can continue to buy the {{Term|OoT3D|Giant's Knife}} from {{Term|OoT3D|Medigoron}} until he obtains the {{Term|OoT3D|Biggoron's Sword|link}} from {{Term|OoT3D|Biggoron|link}}, at which point {{Term|OoT3D|Medigoron}} will stop selling the knife to the young hero.
