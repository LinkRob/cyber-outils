"'''Kakariko Village'''" is a recurring [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
"Kakariko Village" serves as the background music for the location in {{ALttP}} and {{OoT}}, [[Kakariko Village]]. In {{FSA}}, it serves as the background music for {{Term|FSA|Village of the Blue Maiden}}. A remix of the song also plays in {{TP}} when {{Term|TP|Link|link}} calms down {{Term|TP|Epona|link}} in {{Term|TP|Kakariko Village}}.

==Trivia==
*In {{TWW}}, the music that plays in {{Term|TWW|Windfall Island|link}} contains segments of "Kakariko Village".{{Fact}}
