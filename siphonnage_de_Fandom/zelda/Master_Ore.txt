{{Term/Store|Master Ore|Master Ores|Series, ALBW|plural}} are items in {{ALBW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ALBW|Master Ore}} can be found inside the {{Term|ALBW|Dark Palace|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Hideout|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Skull Woods|link}}, and the {{Term|ALBW|Sanctuary|link}} of {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}. They can be used to upgrade the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword|link}}. Each upgrade requires two chunks of {{Term|ALBW|Master Ore}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you can find two chunks of Master Ore, I'll temper that sword of yours.|Blacksmith|ALBW}}</ref> The first upgrade is done by the {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}, while the second upgrade is done by the {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith}} in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}.<ref>{{Cite|That sword's sharp, but I can sharpen it up MORE.|Blacksmith|ALBW}}</ref> Each time the Master Sword is upgraded, its attack power is doubled.<ref>{{Cite|With that Master Ore, I can strengthen your sword!|Blacksmith|ALBW}}</ref>
