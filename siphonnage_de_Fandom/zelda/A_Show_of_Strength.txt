{{Term/Store|A Show of Strength||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 36 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Show of Strength|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|A Show of Strength}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Lynel}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}}. Since both spawn at the same time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} can choose which one to approach first, although the other enemy will begin an assault on them soon afterwards. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
