==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
A Warm Doublet can be acquired from the {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} by either gifting him Spicy [[Meat and Seafood Fry]] or meeting him at the top of [[Mount Hylia]]. If the Warm Doulet has not been acquired from the Old Man before obtaining the [[Paraglider]], he will leave it in a chest in the [[Old Man's Cabin]]. The [[Ventest Clothing Boutique]] in [[Hateno Village]] also sells the Warm Doublet. When worn, it provides {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with one level of [[Cold Resistance]]. While the Warm Doublet can't be upgraded, it can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
