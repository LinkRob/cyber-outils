==Overview==
Ledo is at {{Term|TotK|East Reservoir Lake|link}} who tells {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} that he found blue stones at a nearby cave while swimming. He's interested in using one of these blue stones to repair {{Term|TotK|Mipha Court|link}}, but they're too heavy to carry them by himself and across the waters.

Link swam towards the lake's cave to see the blue stones for himself. Finding them, he manages to carry one back for Ledo. Excited to get one, Ledo thanked Link by giving him a {{Term|TotK|Zora Fabric|link}}.
