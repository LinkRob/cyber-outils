{{Term/Store|Fused Shadow|Fused Shadows|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> also known as '''Dark Power''' and '''Forbidden Power''',<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|TPHD Red|dark power}} rests in the {{Color|TPHD Red|temple}} deep within these woods. It is a {{Color|TPHD Red|forbidden power}}.|Faron (Spirit)|TPHD}}</ref> are quest items in {{TP}}. They hold the magical power used by the [[Dark Interlopers]] in the [[Interloper War]], who sought entry to the [[Sacred Realm]] to claim the [[Triforce]].<ref>{{Cite|Among those living in the light, interlopers who excelled at magic appeared. Wielding powerful sorcery, they tried to establish dominion over the Sacred Realm.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref>

==Locations and Quest Purpose==
The enormous power of the Interlopers was sealed away within the Fused Shadow by three of the four [[Light Spirit]]s, under the order of the [[Golden Goddesses|Goddesses]].<ref>{{Cite|It was then that the goddesses ordered us three light spirits to intervene. We sealed away the great magic those individuals had mastered.|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> Anything that comes in contact with the dark power radiating from the Fused Shadow is transformed, as were the bosses of the first three dungeons.<ref>{{Cite|The dark power you seek lies in the sacred grounds of the proud mountain dwellers. But already those grounds have been defiled, draped in shadow and seeded with evil. You must go to those sacred grounds and cleanse them.|Eldin (Spirit)|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A dark power rests in the temple deep within these woods. It is a forbidden power. Long, long ago, I and the other spirits of light locked it away. Because of its nature, it is a power that should never be touched by any who dwell in the light.|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> This is especially apparent in the [[Goron Mines]] where [[Darbus]], the patriarch of the [[Goron]]s, was transformed into [[Fyrus]] by this sinister power.<ref>{{Cite|But the moment Darbus reached out and touched the treasure...everything went wrong. He collapsed...and before our very eyes transformed into an unspeakable monster!|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref> [[Midna]] entreats [[Link]] to recover the Fused Shadows in return for her help in his quest.<ref>{{Cite|In exchange for my help, though, I need you to gather some things for me|Midna|TP}}</ref> There are three Fused Shadows that must be collected during the game and are held by the dungeon bosses [[Diababa]], Fyrus, [[Morpheel]], and later [[Zant]], of the {{Term|TPHD|Forest Temple|link}}, [[Goron Mines]], [[Lakebed Temple]], and [[Palace of Twilight]], respectively. However, there are four in all - Midna's helm is the fourth Fused Shadow. 

==Powers and Abilities==
[[File:TP Midna Using Fused Shadows.png|thumb|right|The Fused Shadows used to their full extent]]
The true power of the Fused Shadow is immense. By using just a fraction of its power, Midna obliterated [[Zant]], and when she dons the complete Fused Shadow, it transforms her into an enormously powerful spider-like creature, capable of piercing the otherwise impenetrable barrier around [[Hyrule Castle (Twilight Princess)|Hyrule Castle]] created by [[Ganondorf]]. However, the power of the Fused Shadows is less than that of the [[Triforce|power of the Goddesses]], as seen when Midna challenges Ganondorf and is soundly defeated. Ganondorf emerges from the encounter seemingly unscathed, crushing the fragment of the Fused Shadow that Midna wore as a helmet to symbolize his victory over her.

However, this may also be because the Fused Shadow's power may have withered over time. This can be assumed based on the damaged and cracked condition of the actual relic, and Zant's reference to it as "ancient and withered".<ref>{{Cite|Did you honestly mean to take an ancient and withered power like this and turn it against me?|Zant|TP}}</ref> Also, though Ganondorf is unharmed physically, he is no longer in the form he was in when he fought Midna; possibly because she weakened him.

It is unknown what ultimately became of the crushed fragment of the Fused Shadows. In the end credits, its pieces are seen lying half-buried in Hyrule Field.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, Midna wears a part of the Fused Shadow on her head. The complete Fused Shadow appears as part of her [[Focus Spirit]] Attack. When the attack is performed, the Fused Shadow will assemble on Midna, transforming her into a beast. She will then pierce her spear into the ground, returning her to normal afterward.

===''Animal Crossing: New Leaf''===
In ''Animal Crossing: New Leaf'', the Fused Shadow appears as part of the Midna's Mask headgear.

==Trivia==
*The back of Midna's helmet portion of the Fused Shadow contains an [[Eye Symbol]].
*In an interview in {{A&A}}, Yusuke Nakano said that the Fused Shadow piece on Midna's head was inspired by bronze pottery from the {{Wp|Shang Dynasty}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=[...] I wanted to search through some image resources not typically used in games. So I searched in various places and eventually ended up looking at bronze artifacts from the Shang dynasty. I felt like I could feel life within them, and their shape and form were really interesting.|book=A&A|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=420}}</ref>
*The Fused Shadow appears in {{BotW}} as a part of Midna's helmet, which is a piece of [[Armor]] exclusive to the first DLC Pack.
*While unconfirmed, it is likely that the design for the fused shadow was inspired by the armor worn by the villain [[w:c:conan: Thulsa Doom|Thulsa Doom]] in the film ''{{Wp|Conan the Barbarian}}''. Aside from aesthetic similarities, the E3 trailer featured a track from the film's soundtrack and the [[Hero's Spirit]] recites a line that closely resembles the thesis statement of the film.
