{{Term/Store|Yeta||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 277"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TPHD|Yeta}} is the wife of {{Term|TPHD|Yeto|link}}, who lives with him in the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}}. She became sick since she was given a piece of the [[Mirror of Twilight]] by her husband.<ref>{{Cite|Wife...look bad, uh? Not healthy since mirror...|Yeto|TP}}</ref> Upon first meeting {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Yeta}} agrees to help him locate the {{Term|TPHD|Bedroom Key|link}} to the room where the Shard is held; however, due to her sickness, she inadvertently sends him to {{Plural|TPHD|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the [[Ordon Pumpkin]] and [[Ordon Goat Cheese]] instead, both of which are given to {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} to add to and improve his Soup. After consuming the Soup with the added ingredients, {{Term|TPHD|Yeta}} becomes well enough to meet {{Term|TPHD|Link}} after he has obtained the {{Term|TPHD|Bedroom Key}} and leads him to the bedroom.<ref>{{Cite|I drink husband's soup, feel much better, uh. So I come find you.|Yeta|TP}}</ref> However, after she gazes into the Mirror, she becomes corrupted and transforms into the dungeon's boss, [[Blizzeta]]. Upon her defeat, she is returned to normal and freed from the Mirror Shard's influence.

Following Blizzeta's defeat, {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} enters the bedroom and rushes to her; after assuring her to forget about the beauty of the Mirror Shard and to focus on her own beauty instead. The two then embrace, releasing several [[Heart]]s and a [[Heart Container]].

She is an avid fan of snowboarding and gives Link a [[Piece of Heart]] if he can beat her in a [[Snowboard Race]] down [[Snowpeak|Snowpeak Mountain]].<ref>{{Cite|You...race me, uh?|Yeta|TP}}</ref> She is faster than her husband and prefers to take the shortcut down the mountain.
