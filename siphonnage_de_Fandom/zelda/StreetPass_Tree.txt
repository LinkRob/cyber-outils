The {{Term/Store|StreetPass Tree||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Apple|link}} {{Term|ALBW|Tree|link}} in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}} It is given to {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} as a gift from {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} completes his {{Term|Series|StreetPass|link}} setup by speaking to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}} in {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} commemorates the occasion by planting the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree}} just west of {{Term|ALBW|Link's House|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Today is a day for the history books. Why? Because you are making your {{Color|ALBW Red|StreetPass}} debut! I'd like to commemorate this special occasion with a gift.<br/>That gift is...an apple tree!<br/>I'll plant it in the garden near your house. Go take a look at it when you get a chance.|Gramps|ALBW}}</ref> The {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree}}'s main function is as a source of {{Plural|ALBW|Apple}}, which can be knocked off of the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree|display=Tree}} by dashing into it with the {{Term|ALBW|Pegasus Boots|link}}. Initially only three {{Plural|ALBW|Apple}} grow on the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree}}, and an additional {{Term|ALBW|Apple}} will appear for every three {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} met via {{Term|Series|StreetPass}}, up to a maximum of twenty {{Plural|ALBW|Apple}}. The rare and more effective {{Plural|ALBW|Green Apple|link}} can also be found growing on the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree}} on occasion.

The next time {{Term|ALBW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} after the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree}} has been planted, {{Term|ALBW|Gramps}} will ask if he has seen the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree|display=Tree}} yet,<ref>{{Cite|Say...have you gone to see that apple tree I gave you yet?|Gramps|ALBW}}</ref> though he seems to already know the answer and will be perturbed if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} does not respond honestly.<ref>{{Cite|Hmph... You're a bad liar.<br/>I planted it near your house. Go check it out sometime.|Gramps|ALBW}}</ref> The {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree}} is also present in Forest Arena during {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}} initiated with the {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} that appears next to the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Tree|display=Tree}}.
