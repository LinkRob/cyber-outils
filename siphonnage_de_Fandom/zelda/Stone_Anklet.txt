The '''Stone Anklet''' is an [[Accessory]] from {{EoW}}.
==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Stone Anklet|A heavy anklet that stabilizes balance. Wearing it reduces knockback.<ref name="Stone Anklet">{{Cite|A heavy anklet that stabilizes balance. Wearing it reduces knockback.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Stone Anklet can be bought at the [[Hyrule Castle Town Shop]] for 400 Rupees. Equipping them increases [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda’s]] knockback resistance.
