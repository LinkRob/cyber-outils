{{Term/Store|Reeza||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Reeza|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Reeza}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldier who guards the front entrance to the palace in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} at all times, alongside {{Term|BotW|Ploka|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Reeza}}, she says that {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, Chief of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}, is a kind person for allowing visitors during these tough times.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq. The {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} may be young, but she's a kind person who puts her people first.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|She told me to allow visitors though, even in these tough times...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Even in these tough times, the chief won't let any of her official duties slide, including things that feel unimportant.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|She's instructed me to go ahead and let people who wish to speak with her come in, regardless of subject matter...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the tough times she is referring to, {{Term|BotW|Reeza}} snaps at {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} problem and is none of his business.<ref>{{Cite|This is a Gerudo problem! It is none of your concern!|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> Reeza allows Link into the palace, but warns him not to get in the Chief's way.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, listen! Even though I'm letting you inside, that doesn't mean you can go getting in the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s way! Watch out!|Reeza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So you may enter, but with a word of warning. Do not get in the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s way!|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends the conversation, {{Term|BotW|Reeza}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to be rude to {{Term|BotW|Riju}}.<ref>{{Cite|Take care not to be rude.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Reeza}} after speaking to {{Term|BotW|Riju}} for the first time, she asks what makes {{Term|BotW|Link}} so special that {{Term|BotW|Riju}} trusts him to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What makes you so special that the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} would trust you with retrieving the {{Color|BotW Red|Thunder Helm}}?|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> She then warns him that the task is not simple,<ref>{{Cite|Hrmph. Regardless, I hope you are prepared. This task will not be a simple one.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> as the skilled {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=thieves|link}} slipped past all of the guards.<ref>{{Cite|The thieves were able to slip past all of our guards, so they're certainly not amateurs.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Reeza}} recommends that {{Term|BotW|Link}} goes to the barracks to learn more,<ref>{{Cite|You should head over to the {{Color|BotW Blue|barracks}} if you want to learn more...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> as the soldiers have been investigating the theft.<ref>{{Cite|The soldiers there have been investigating the theft for a while, so they should be able to help.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} calms Naboris, {{Term|BotW|Reeza}} talks about how much it stressed {{Term|BotW|Riju}} and {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} first appeared, a day didn't go by that the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Buliara}} didn't look stressed.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for lifting that burden from them.<ref>{{Cite|But now... Well, it's like years have been lifted from them. This is all thanks to you.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks her to talk about {{Term|BotW|Riju}}, she snaps at {{Term|BotW|Link}}, demanding he call her by her full title, Lady {{Term|BotW|Riju}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Treat her with due respect and call her Lady Riju. Her youth doesn't stop her from looking out for her people!|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to explain that {{Term|BotW|Riju}} has a softer side,<ref>{{Cite|She puts up a tough front most of the time, but she does have a softer side, too...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> but can only let it show in private due to her expectations of leadership.<ref>{{Cite|With all expectations of leadership, though, she can only let that side show when she's in private...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks her to talk about {{Term|BotW|Buliara}}, {{Term|BotW|Reeza}} is offended that {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not know about her.<ref>{{Cite|"Tell me about Buliara"?! How can you not know about her? It's an insult to think her name isn't recognized...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> She then explains that {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} is the best spear master in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and that she inspired her to train with the spear when she was young.<ref>{{Cite|Buliara is the best spear master in the whole town, of course!|Reeza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|She inspired me to take up the spear when I was young, but despite all my training, I'm not even close to her skill.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to say goodbye, {{Term|BotW|Reeza}} says she hopes that {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s ancestors are watching her.<ref>{{Cite|I only hope that all of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Riju}}'s predecessors are watching her now, and they see how commendable she's been.|Reeza|BotW}}</ref>
