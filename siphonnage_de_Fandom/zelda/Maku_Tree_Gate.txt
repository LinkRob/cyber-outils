The {{Term/Store|Maku Tree Gate|Maku Tree Gates|Series, OoS}} is an {{Term|OoS|Object|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref name= "Sign">{{Cite|Maku Tree Gate<br/>Show courage to gain passage!|Sign|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree Gate}} blocks the road to the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree|link}}, and may only be opened by displaying one's courage.<ref name= "Sign"/> In a Normal Game, {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} opens it with the {{Term|OoS|Wooden Sword|link}} from the {{Term|OoS|Hero's Cave|link}}. In a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Link}} already possesses the {{Term|OoS|Wooden Sword}} and may open the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree Gate|display=Gate}} right away.
