{{Term/Store|Twili Midna||Series, HW}} is a playable Warrior in {{HW}},{{Name Ref|HW}} first appearing in the [[Twilight Princess Pack]] DLC. She is the true form of [[Midna]] from {{TP}}. In {{HW|L}} she is unlocked as a Reward for Battle Victory in [[Adventure Map/Gerudo Desert (Hyrule Warriors Legends)#Battle A16|Battle A16]] of the [[Adventure Map]]. In {{HW|DE}}, she is unlocked as a Reward for Battle Victory in [[Adventure Map/Twilight Field (Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition)#Battle C6|Battle C6]] of the Adventure Map.

==Biography==
{{Stub|HW}}
Twili Midna is the form that Midna held prior to being cursed to resemble an imp by [[Cia]]. It is her true form, and can only be held by Midna by breaking the curse placed on her or by wielding the [[Shadow Crystal]]. She attacks using the [[Mirror (Item)|Mirror]], a Weapon based on the [[Mirror of Twilight]]. The Mirror is influenced by Midna's power to warp objects in Twilight Princess such as her own [[Zant's Hand]] for melee attacks, [[Sol]]s, [[Sky Cannon]] Link used to reach the [[City in the Sky]] to fires Sols, the Bridge of [[Kakariko Gorge]] and Twili Wolves that appear gigantic compared to her Imp form. Her [[Focus Spirit]] attack is a reenactment of the ending of Twilight Princess where she shattered the Mirror of Twilight, though in this case she apparently shatters reality itself.

Twili Midna appears in the "[[Her True Self]]" [[Scenario]] of [[Linkle's Tale]]. At some point after Link received the legendary Master Sword, Zelda and Midna leave their group to wander off to the Palace of Twilight, upon hearing a "mysterious darkness" has entered the domain. Coincidentally, [[Linkle]] happens to get lost there; the young villager suddenly finds a magical [[Shadow Crystal|black crystal]] that so happens to restore the imp Midna to her true form, Twili Midna.

When defeated in {{HW}}, Twili Midna drops the same [[Material]]s as her cursed form, dropping [[Midna's Hair]] as a silver Material and [[Midna's Fused Shadow]] as a gold Material. However, in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}, Twili Midna drops her own unique Materials when defeated. She may either drop her [[Twili Midna's Hairpin|Hairpin]] as a silver Material, or her [[Twili Midna's Robe|Robe]] as a gold Material.
