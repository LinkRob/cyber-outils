The '''Seismic Talus''' is the boss of the [[Suthorn Ruins]] in {{EoW}} and is the second overall boss of the game. This formidable adversary is a massive rock-like creature with a glowing purple core that players must target to defeat it.

==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
The Seismic Talus is a massive rock monster that emerges from the ground to attack with powerful slams. One of the stones on its body glows and acts as its weak point, which can be struck or pulled free with [[Bind]] to temporarily collapse the creature.

=== Strategy ===
The Seismic Talus is fairy straightforward and not at all too hard, to deal damage, Zelda must strike at the glowing rock-like orb that is on the creature, she can do so by either using an Echo that can deal damage or by using Bind to pull the Rock-like orb off of the Seismic Talus, through out the battle the Seismic Talus will preform three different attacks, it can either fire out it's arm at Zelda, slam down both it's hands or spin it's arms around like a propeller after either dealing enough damage or by pulling to glowing orb-like rock of the body, the Seismic Talus will collapse and Zelda can directly attack the orb by either using Echo Enemies, using throw-able Echos or by turning into Sword Form, after that the Seismic Talus will reform and will place the Rock-like orb on it's right shoulder, but otherwise, the battle is the same.

When down to the last bit of it's HP, the Seismic Talus will be enrage as it glows red, becoming stronger and will then place the Rock-like orb on the top of it's head, this phase of the battle is similar to the previous phase, but due to the location of the Rock-like orb now being on top of the Seismic Talus' head, Zelda can no longer have the Echos attack Rock-like orb and/or use Bind to grab onto the Rock-like orb normally, and will now have to wait until the Seismic Talus preforms the hand slam to either have the Echos attack Rock-like orb and/or use Bind to grab onto the Rock-like orb top pull it off and have the Seismic Talus collapse and allow Zelda to directly attack the orb by either using Echo Enemies, using throw-able Echos or by turning into Sword Form, Zelda must repeat this again to defeat the Seismic Talus.

==Trivia==

* The Seismic Talus is the only Non-Major Antagonist boss in ''The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom'' who is mandatory to fight first, where as the rest of [[Mogryph|the]] [[Vocavor|Non]][[Volvagia|-Major]] [[Gohma|Antagonist]] [[Skorchill|bosses]] can be fought in any order the Player chooses.
* The Seismic Talus has a slight resemblance to [[Ramrock]] from [[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|''The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages'']].
* The Seismic Talus has the distinct feature of being the very first boss in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series to be fought by Zelda.
