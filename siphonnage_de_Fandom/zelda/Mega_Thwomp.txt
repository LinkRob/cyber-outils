The {{Term/Store|Mega Thwomp||Series, LA, LADX}} is an enemy in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 192}}</ref> Its name and appearance is derived from the {{Smw|Thwomp}} enemies of the ''{{Smw|Mario (series)|Mario}}'' series.

==Characteristics==
A single {{Term|LANS|Mega Thwomp}} appears in an underground, side-scrolling passage of the {{Term|LANS|Key Cavern|link}}. It suspends itself over a pit of spikes in a hole in the ceiling, and can be reached by climbing a ladder on either side. Unlike most other [[Thwomp]]s, however, this enemy will not drop down if {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} passes beneath it while jumping over the spikes. The Mega Thwomp will drop when ramming into it with the {{Term|LANS|Pegasus Boots|link}} while equipped with the {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}}, causing it to crack and plummet helplessly to the floor. As the ledge to the next area is too high for {{Term|LANS|Link}} to reach on his own, the {{Term|LANS|Mega Thwomp}} can then be used as a harmless stepping stone for reaching the next area.
