{{Term/Store|Bomb-Master Cannon||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Bomb-Master Cannon}}
Cannon is the owner of the [[Bomb Shop]] on [[Windfall Island]]. If [[Link]] visits his shop early in the game, he acts rudely and disrespectfully towards the young hero, claiming to be the last maker of [[Bomb]]s in the world and refusing to sell them to those he disapproves of.<ref>{{Cite|My ancestors been making bombs for generations. Uh-huh. Now, seems like I'm the only person left in the world who still works in the bomb industry...|Bomb-Master Cannon|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And which means I don't go selling my work without approving of who I sell it to! And I don't approve of you! So beat it!|Bomb-Master Cannon|TWW}}</ref> As a result, he sells his products at ludicrous prices, with the lowest being 10,000 [[Rupee]]s for 10 Bombs.

During their quest to obtain [[Nayru's Pearl]], [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|Tetra's pirates]] pay a visit to Cannon, tying him up and gagging him in order to steal his Bombs, which they plan to use to break into [[Jabun]]'s cave. However, Link manages to sneak onto their [[Tetra's Ship|ship]] and take the Bombs for himself with [[Niko]]'s help. After being robbed by the pirates, Cannon goes back to running his shop, whilst adopting a kinder and more generous personality as well as more affordable prices on his merchandise.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, kindness is our policy here! We're the Bomb Shop for the world! ...We're always ready to serve!|Bomb-Master Cannon|TWW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Despite claiming to be the only person left in the bomb industry, [[Beedle's Shop Ship]] also carries bombs later in the game. However, this is because the Pirates sold [[Beedle]] some of the bombs they had stolen.<ref>{{Cite|Pirates sold me bombs for cheap!|Beedle|TWW}}</ref>
