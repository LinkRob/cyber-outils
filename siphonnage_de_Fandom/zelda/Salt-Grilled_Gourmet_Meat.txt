{{Term/Store|Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat|Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meats|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meats are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either [[Raw Gourmet Meat]] or a [[Raw Whole Bird]] together with [[Rock Salt]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat them to heal some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are considered to be lavish, making using a high-quality meats.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add extra ingredients while Cooking Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. They can be eaten to heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. When consumed, they'll replenish part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled when he eats these meats.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. These meals will grant Link a boost to his movement speed when he eats them.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. By eating them, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will receive one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. By eating them, the power of Link's attacks will be boosted.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be boosted when he eats these meals.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This lavish grilled dish makes liberal use of high-quality cuts of meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
