{{Term/Store|Minish Woods||Series, TMC}} is an area in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=On his way to the Picori's home in the Minish Woods, young Link met Ezlo, a man suffering from a curse.|book=HH|page=79}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Minish Woods}} is a forested region located in southeastern {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to two other areas: the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}} to the west and {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}} to the north.

===Inhabitants===
A large number of {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori}} live in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}} found here as well as various private mushrooms and ground cracks about the woods.  Notable inhabitants include {{Term|TMC|Festari|link}}, who speaks the human language, and {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} elder. Just north of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}} lies the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}, where {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} finds the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element}}. Just outside of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}} is the {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}}-maker and inventor {{Term|TMC|Belari|link}}'s house; after a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} has the opportunity to switch his {{Plural|TMC|Bomb}} out for {{Plural|TMC|Remote Bomb|link}}.

A few non-{{Term|TMC|Minish}} can be found here, as well. {{Term|TMC|Syrup|link}} the Witch resides in her {{Term|TMC|Syrup the Witch's Hut|link|display=Hut}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Great Butterfly Fairy|link}} who provides {{Term|TMC|Link}} with a larger {{Term|TMC|Wallet|link}} is found in the northern section of the forest. While not in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods}}, {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin|link}} must be accessed for the first time by traveling through the woods and using the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}}.

===Navigation===
As with the rest of the world in {{TMC|-}}, one must shrink and resize themselves with the {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} scattered across the forest. For example, {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized {{Term|TMC|Link}} is needed to enter the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village}} and interact with the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}.<ref>{{Cite|The world of the Minish is very small. You're far to big to meet them now [...] if we're to enter the village, we'll have to make you a touch smaller first.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> There are also small holes in the earth and logs that Link can only go through while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}. However, only human-sized {{Term|TMC|Link}} can safely traverse shallow water and high grass.<ref>{{Cite|But there is one thing you must know: being Minish-sized is full of dangers! Mere puddles at your normal size are bottomless swamps to the Minish.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> In addition to shallow water, there is deep water in the northern half of the woods that not even normal-sized {{Term|TMC|Link}} can cross; the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} found under the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} are required to swim over.

==Trivia==
*The name of {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods}} is notable as the only time that Hyruleans use "Minish" to describe the creatures; in all other instances "Picori" is used.<ref>{{Cite|Strange how, in the world of humans, only this forest has kept that name...|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
The word {{Romanize|チロリア|Chiroria}} from the Japanese name, {{Romanize|チロリアの森|Chiroria no Mori}}, can be translated to "Tyrolea", as in {{Romanize|チロリアンハット|Chirorianhatto}}, known in English as {{Wp|Tyrolean hat}}: a style of fedora headwear from the Tyrol of the Swiss Alps, traditionally made of felt, with a corded band, and trimmed with a feather. The appearance of this is reminiscent of the feathered tails and caps worn by the Minish themselves.
