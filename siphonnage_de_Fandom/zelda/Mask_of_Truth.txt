The {{Term/Store|Mask of Truth||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|CoH}} Its distinctive design prominently features a [[Sheikah]] [[Eye Symbol]].

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Mask of Truth can be borrowed from the [[Happy Mask Shop]] after completing the [[Mask Trading Sequence]]. Wearing the mask allows [[Link]] to speak to [[Gossip Stone]]s to learn new things about [[Hyrule]] and its inhabitants. The [[Happy Mask Shop Owner]] mentions that the mask was passed down by the [[Sheikah]].<ref>{{Cite|This is the Mask of Truth. It is a mysterious mask passed down by the Sheikah.|Happy Mask Shop Owner|OoT}}</ref>

If Link wears the Mask of Truth in the [[Forest Stage]] of the [[Lost Woods]], he will receive a [[Deku Nut]] upgrade.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That is the {{Color|MM Red|Mask of Truth}}, isn't it?</p><p>You have a frightful mask. But being able to see into people's hearts and minds seems useful...</p>}}

In {{MM}}, the Mask of Truth is given to Link by the [[Cursed Rich Man]] in the [[Swamp Spider House]]. According to him, he was told that he could become rich if he were to look inside, and was then given the mask. However, he became cursed as he went inside, and was turned into a [[Skulltula]]-like creature.<ref>{{Cite|You see, a while back, someone told me I could become rich and he gave me this mask. He said the instructions were inscribed somewhere in here, but when I went to look, I was cursed!|Cursed Rich Man|MM}}</ref> He tasks Link with defeating the 30 [[Gold Skulltula]]s in the house to lift the curse, and rewards him with the Mask of Truth as thanks.

Wearing the mask allows Link to speak with Gossip Stones.<ref>{{Cite|This mask allows you to hear and understand the wisdom of Gossip Stones and the thoughts of animals...|Inventory|MM}}</ref> Many Gossip Stones give clues on how to find other masks. The mask also imbues Link the ability to read animals' minds.<ref>{{Cite|The mask that can see into people's hearts and minds also seems to work on animals as well...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> This is mainly used in the [[Doggy Race]] to see which dog might win.<ref>{{Cite|Ruff! My four paws feel lighter than usual today!|Dog|MM}}</ref> The item description for this mask is the only direct reference to the Sheikah made in the game.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Mask of Truth! This is the same mask the Sheikah spoke of!|N/A|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, due to the lack of rumble feature and the [[Shard of Agony]], the Mask of Truth can locate hidden [[Cave|grottos]]. Wearing the mask causes the ground above the grotto to glow. However, this ability is never explained in the game.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}}, the Mask of Truth appears as a [[Costume]] for [[Impa]] that can be obtained in the [[Termina Map]] in [[Adventure Mode]]. The Mask of Truth also appears as an [[Item Card]] on the Termina Map. Using the Mask of Truth Item Card on a Map square activates [[Majora's Power]] on that Map square, giving Warriors a temporary defense boost in the next Battle on that square.
{{Noncanon|End}}
