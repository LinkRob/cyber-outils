{{Term/Store|Ruffian|Ruffians|Series|plural}} are {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}} in {{HW|L}}.{{Name Ref|HWDE}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|HWDE|Ruffian}} are powerful {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin|link}}. Their appearance and attack patterns are identical to their {{Term|HWDE|Big Blin}} counterparts, however their {{Term|HWDE|Morale|link}} is permanently boosted. They appear in the {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} "{{Term|HWDE|A New Disturbance|link}}" after the {{Term|HWDE|Helmaroc King|link}} is driven out of the {{Term|HWDE|Forsaken Fortress}}, and continue to spawn in the {{Term|HWDE|Forsaken Fortress}} {{Plural|HWDE|Keep}}, aside from the Enemy Base. They advance towards the Allied Base and the two {{Plural|HWDE|Keep}} surrounding it after appearing, and will capture the {{Plural|HWDE|Keep}} if they can.

When defeated, {{Plural|HWDE|Ruffian}} may drop {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin Hide|link}} or {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin Club|link}} as their Bronze and Silver {{Plural|HWDE|Material|link}}, respectively.
