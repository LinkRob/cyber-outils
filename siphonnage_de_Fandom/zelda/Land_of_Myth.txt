"{{Term/Store|Land of Myth||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}}" is the first {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} of the {{Term|HWDE|Ocarina of Time (Arc)|link}} arc of {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}} in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HWDE}} It takes place in {{Term|HWDE|Death Mountain (Hyrule Warriors)|link}}.

==Overview==
After going through one of the newly-created portals, [[Impa]], followed by [[Sheik]] and a small group of Hyrulean [[soldier]]s, arrive to [[Death Mountain]] during the era of the [[Hero of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|Leading a small force of Hyrulean soldiers, Impa stepped into one of the newly opened portals. The kingdom spread out before her seemed like the Hyrule she knew, yet it was somehow very different. Before long, she realized that they had traveled back to the era of the mythical Hero of Time. Awed, Impa began her search for the Gate of Souls. She sensed an evil presence on Death Mountain and set out for the ominous peak. And with her came the mysterious Sheik...|N/A|HW}}</ref> The Hyrulean Forces begin their search for the [[Gate of Souls]], but are interrupted when a [[fairy]] (named [[Navi]] in the Wii U version) appears and tells them that the [[Goron]]s, along with their chief [[Darunia]], have abducted [[Princess Ruto]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Listen! Princess Ruto is in trouble! She was abducted by the Gorons! Their leader Darunia used to be such a gentle soul. Someone named Zelda showed up and... He hasn't been the same since!|Navi|HW}}</ref> She elaborates that the once-gentle Darunia suddenly changed with the arrival of Princess Zelda. Although the allies' primary focus is finding and closing the gate, they quickly decide to help rescue the princess of the [[Zora]]s and find out Zelda's intentions.<ref>{{Cite|I'm concerned about our primary mission... But first we have to rescue Princess Ruto.|Sheik|HW}}</ref>

The Gorons, unwilling to talk with the Hyrulean Forces, immediately begin their attack against the allies by launching boulder attacks at the [[Allied Base]].<ref>{{Cite|They don't look like they're willing to talk... I guess we'll just have to force our way by.|Impa|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|One more step, and we'll flatten you with great, big boulders!|Goron Captain|HW}}</ref> Because the Gorons are using the West and East Boulder Keeps to launch their attacks, the Hyrulean Forces quickly capture them to prevent any further damage to their base.<ref>{{Cite|We have to stop the falling rocks. Perhaps capturing the Boulder Keeps is a good start...|Sheik|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wonderful! We've stopped the falling rocks! Our base should be safe for now.|Impa|HW}}</ref> However, Darunia becomes enraged that his people are being attacked and begins a rampage against Impa and her troops.<ref>{{Cite|Who dares attack the noble Gorons?! Whoever you are, prepare to battle a Goron Chieftain!|Darunia|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So, Darunia has appeared...? Very well. We must stop his rampage then.|Sheik|HW}}</ref> With Darunia mad with rage, the young [[Sheikah]] suggests that they knock the Goron Chieftain out to get some information regarding both Zelda and Ruto.<ref>{{Cite|We need him to calm down so we can get some answers... We must knock him out!|Impa|HW}}</ref>

Seeing himself overwhelmed by the strength of the Hyrulean Forces, Darunia temporarily retreats to the Chief Room to recover and calls for reinforcements.<ref>{{Cite|Not bad... I'll have to withdraw for now, but only so I can strike back twice as hard!|Darunia|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Big brother Darunia! We're on it!|Goron Captain|HW}}</ref> While the Gorons are being led by a group of [[Goron Captain]]s to reclaim both Boulder Keeps, Darunia emerges from his room seemingly without a scratch and begins to accompany his people in recapturing the keeps to launch more boulder attacks.<ref>{{Cite|YEEEEEES! I'm back to fighting strength! Brothers, we're marching out again!|Darunia|HW}}</ref> Impa and Sheik are surprised by the fast recovery of the Goron Chieftain and wonder if his base is hiding a secret to him replenishing his health.<ref>{{Cite|He looks like he hasn't a scratch on him... Will there be no end to this battle?|Sheik|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I wonder how he's replenishing his health... Is his base hiding some secret?|Impa|HW}}</ref> Sheik then devises a plan but requires the aid of Impa to secure the West Boulder Keep in order to execute it.<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to try something. First, I need to get into that keep...|Sheik|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hurry and capture that keep. We need it to put my plan into action|Sheik|HW}}</ref> Once Sheik makes it safely to the keep, she fires a boulder into the [[Enemy Base]] and destroys its gate, revealing the entrance to Darunia's room. The Goron infuriated Chieftain retreats to his chamber and commands all of the Gorons to attack the Allied Base since Sheik's attack ruined his premium [[Rock Sirloin]].<ref>{{Cite|Aaah! The premium rock sirloin I had hidden in my chambers! It's ruined! GRAH! You! I won't forgive you! Gorons! Move out!|Darunia|HW}}</ref> As part of this plan, the Gorons summon [[Bombchu]]s to the battlefield with the intention of blowing up the base;<ref>{{Cite|Let's blow up their base with a Bombchu!|Goron Captain|HW}}</ref> however, the Hyrulean Forces defeat the Bombchu Operators controlling the explosives and use them to conquer the remaining keeps, including the Enemy Base.<ref>{{Cite|Somebody must be controlling the Bombchu! We have to find and defeat whoever it is!|Impa|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Defeat the Bombchu Operators to take control of the Bombchus!|N/A|HW}}</ref>

When Impa and Sheik enter the Enemy Base, they find that two [[Big Poe]]s are controlling the Gorons and are the cause for their aggressive behavior.<ref>{{Cite|I feel as if the sorceress is behind this... Could her allies be controlling the Gorons?|Impa|HW}}</ref> Destroying the two monsters rids Darunia of the enchantments and decreases his power, allowing the allies to return the Chieftain's sanity by defeating him one last time.<ref>{{Cite|Defeat the Big Poes to remove Darunia's enchantments!|N/A|HW}}</ref> Not realizing his actions, Darunia quickly apologizes for the harm they had caused and releases Princess Ruto at the request of Sheik.<ref>{{Cite|I didn't realize what we were doing! Forgive us! Someone was twisting our minds!|Darunia|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Would you mind kindly releasing Princess Ruto?|Sheik|HW}}</ref>

After the Scenario finishes, Darunia becomes a playable character and his weapon, the [[Magic Hammer]], becomes available for use.
