'''Eyelid Pigs''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics==
{{Stub|FPTRR}}
Eyelid Pigs are the most basic of [[Pig]]s. They are cowardly and will run away from [[Tingle]] if they see him. They are found in [[Hometown Prairie]] and [[Cape Treasure]]. They may drop [[Minced Meat]] or [[Short Fur]] when defeated.

==Crossover appearances==
===''WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase''===
An Eyelid Pig appears in the microgame [[Mogitate Chinkuru]], exclusive to the Japanese version. Here, Tingle has to defeat it to win the microgame.
