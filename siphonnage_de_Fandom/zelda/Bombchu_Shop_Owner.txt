==Biography==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu Shop Owner}} runs the {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu Shop|link}} in the {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule Castle Town|link}}'s {{Term|OoT3D|Back Alley|link}} when {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} is a child. He exclusively sells {{Term|OoT3D|Bombchu|link}}, though his stock is in limited quantities, and every pack he sells are immediately labeled as sold out.
