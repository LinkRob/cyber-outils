"{{Term/Store|Koko's Specialty||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Koko's Specialty}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This is the final {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Koko}}, only activated when all her previous {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}} have been completed. {{Term|BotW|Koko}} has decided to make dessert to go along with dinner, settling on a {{Term|BotW|Honeyed Apple|link}} since her mother loved them.<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make for dessert? {{Big|OH!}} Koko will cook a {{Color|BotW Blue|honeyed apple}}! Mother loved those! It melts in your mouth and is sooo delicious!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> However, while she is checking her ingredients, she realizes that she is short on {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see... the ingredients are... an {{Color|BotW Blue|apple}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}... {{Big|Hrm? ...{{Color|BotW Blue|Courser bee honey}}? OH, NO!!}} Koko is all out of {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

Since {{Plural|BotW|Courser Bee|link}} attack people when they get close to their hives, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} has been unable to gather any {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey}} herself.<ref>{{Cite|The bees attack you when you get close to their hives. It's too dangerous for Koko to go get more...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She wanted to make {{Plural|BotW|Honeyed Apple}} for her departed mother, but because she can not, she feels like a bad daughter.<ref>{{Cite|Koko wanted to make some for Mother in heaven... But...Koko is a bad daughter who can't do anything right.|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} attempt to cheer her up, Koko admits that his words are sweet, but not what she needs.<ref>{{Cite|Your sweet words aren't helping... What Koko needs is some sweet {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}|Koko|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to offer her {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey}} when he does not have any in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will be able to tell he is lying.<ref>{{Cite|[Voilà! Honey!] Koko knows when someone is lying. Father isn't good at lying either...|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Koko}} is still beating herself up for being so forgetful when {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns later.<ref>{{Cite|Koko wanted to make a {{Color|BotW Blue|honeyed apple}}, but Koko forgot the {{Color|BotW Blue|courser bee honey}}?! Koko is a bad daughter who can't do anything right. Koko will never be like Mother...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives her the {{Term|BotW|Courser Bee Honey}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will become excited and asks him to wait while she makes the {{Term|BotW|Honeyed Apple}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wait... Is this... {{Big|courser bee honey!!}} Are you sure about this? Really? Thank you so much! Koko means it! Just stay right there a second!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Koko}} is quick to cook up the {{Term|BotW|Energizing Honeyed Apple|link}}, which brings back a memory of her mother.<ref>{{Cite|This... This is... Koko...remembers...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Whenever she had a fever, her mother would make a {{Term|BotW|Honeyed Apple}} just for her.<ref>{{Cite|When Koko had a fever, Mother would always cook this just for Koko. When Mother made her special dessert, she always put some of this in it.|Koko|BotW}}</ref> The memories push {{Term|BotW|Koko}} to start crying, and she promises her mother that she will continue to learn.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Waaah! Mother!!}} Koko... Koko...will do her best to try to be as good as you one day! Koko will get better and better! Koko promises!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Once she is able to pull herself together, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} admits that cooking her mother's dishes makes her feel better.<ref>{{Cite|Koko was able to re-create Mother's dishes! Koko...feels better now!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She thanks him and rewards him with the dessert,<ref>{{Cite|It's all thanks to you! Please, take this!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> ending the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
