'''Clora''' is a character in {{TFoE}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Meet Clora|author=Rob Dunlavey|published=|retrieved=|url=https://www.robd.com/cdi-1|title=CDi|site=https://www.robd.com}}</ref> She is a woman being held captive in [[Shipwreck Cliff]], and the daughter of [[Kulvan]].

==Biography==
Clora was captured and imprisoned aboard the ruined ship of Shipwreck Cliff. Link finds her inside the cabin. She proclaims him as her hero, rewarding him with a big kiss and nearly crushing him in a hug. She requests that he also save her father, who is also being held captive in Shipwreck Cliff. She tells him to "have a heart" and means it literally; Link is given an extra [[Heart Container#The Faces of Evil|Life Heart]] as well.
