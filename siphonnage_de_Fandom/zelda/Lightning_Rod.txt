{{Plural|BotW|Lightning Rod}} are one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are weaker counterparts to {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod|link}}. Most commonly, they are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. Their gems contain {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}.<ref name="Inventory" /> {{Plural|BotW|Lightning Rod}} are not made of flammable materials, and they can safely be used around {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They are also not made of magnetic materials and cannot be manipulated with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Lightning Rod}} do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they will float in sufficiently deep bodies of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Lightning Rod}} are imbued with the elemental energy of {{Term|BotW|Electricity}}, generating a ball of {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} that advances when swung. However, {{Plural|BotW|Lightning Rod}} are fragile and will break easily if they make direct contact with {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. They will also break simply from extended use. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} executes a {{Term|BotW|Spin Attack|link}} while wielding a {{Term|BotW|Lightning Rod}}, he will loose five blasts at once in different directions. They will temporarily lose their magical properties if used excessively, requiring a brief cooldown period before they can be used again.

==={{TotK|-}}===
The Lightning rod in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]] has now been replaced with the Topaz rod, the fusion of a magic rod and Topaz. The Topaz rod can also be made with any one handed fusion with Topaz, but with a significantly weaker effect.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HWAoC}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} are tools that can be wielded by all {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}, as well as by enemy {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. Located on the left bumper wheel with the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Rod|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Rod|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Apple|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} are limited charge attack {{Plural|HWAoC|Item|link}} that will shock anything in its attack radius. They can also help whittle away at exposed {{Plural|HWAoC|Weak Point Gauge|link}}. This attack radius can be widened if {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} are near metal crates or are in {{Term|HWAoC|Water (Terrain)|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} need {{Term|HWAoC|Electricity|link|display=Electric}}-elemental energy as fuel for its attacks, and the amount of charges the {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod|display=Rod}} has carry over into future battles, meaning completing battles with an empty {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} will have the {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} remain out of charge. The energy needed to power the {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} can be found by either slaying {{Term|HWAoC|Electricity|display=Electric}} {{Plural|HWAoC|Monster|link}}, such as {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe}}, or by smashing open crates.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} is obtained by slaying the player's first {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe}}, with the first one being found in the "{{Term|HWAoC|Road to the Ancient Lab|link}}" {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} help make the {{Plural|HWAoC|Weak Point Gauge}} of certain {{Plural|HWAoC|Monster}}, such as {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon]|link}}, drain faster. Being hit by the {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} while near or on a conductive surface will allow {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} to chain {{Plural|HWAoC|Weak Point Smash|link}} more consecutively.

While the {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}} innately has three charges, completing the "The Research of Rods" and "It Takes Two" {{Plural|HWAoC|Side Quest|link}} both reward {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} one additional charge to all Rods, including the {{Term|HWAoC|Lightning Rod}}.
