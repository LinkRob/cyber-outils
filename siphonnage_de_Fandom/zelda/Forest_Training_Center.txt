The {{Term/Store|Forest Training Center||Series, OoT3D}} is a location within the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Forest|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|The best place to go to learn some new skills is in the {{Color|OoT3D Green|Forest Training Center}}. It's on the hill just above here.|Kokiri|OoT3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
In the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Training Center}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} finds the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Sword|link}} as a child. It is located near the house of the {{Plural|OoT3D|Know-It-All Brother|link}}, and it contains several {{Plural|OoT3D|Rock|link}} and patches of {{Term|OoT3D|Grass|link}} that {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can use for practicing using the {{Term|OoT3D|Sword|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Targeting|link}}.

Within the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Training Center}} is a small hole called the {{Term|OoT3D|Hole of "L"|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Purple|Hole of "L"}}<br/>Let's go through this small hole! Stand in front of the hole and push {{Icon|OoT3D Circle Pad}} toward it. When the Action Icon shows "Enter," press {{Icon|OoT3D A}} to crawl into the hole. Pay attention to what the Action Icon says!|Sign|OoT3D}}</ref> which leads to a small maze housing a continuously rolling {{Term|OoT3D|Boulder|link}} and a {{Term|OoT3D|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Sword}}. If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} comes into contact with the {{Term|OoT3D|Boulder}}, he will take damage.

Once {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} obtains the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Sword}} from the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Training Center}}, he is able to go {{Term|OoT3D|Inside the Deku Tree|link}}, the first {{Term|OoT3D|Dungeon|link}}.
