==Locations and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Snowquill Trousers are a piece of [[Armor]] that can be bought for 550 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} at the [[Brazen Beak]]. When worn, they provide one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/> The Snowquill Trousers can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Snowquill Trousers, alongside the [[Snowquill Headdress]] and [[Snowquill Tunic]], compose the [[Snowquill Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides an Unfreezable [[Set Bonus]].
