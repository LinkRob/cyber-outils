==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Main Control Unit}} are located inside the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has activated all four {{Plural|BotW|Terminal|link}} inside them, he will be directed to the {{Term|BotW|Main Control Unit}} by the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|display=Divine Beast's}} respective {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} in order to reinstate their control.<ref name="Daruk"/><ref>{{Cite|You activated all the {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}}! Now you can start the {{Color|BotW Red|main control unit}}.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That was the last {{Color|BotW Red|terminal}}! Now you just need to start the {{Color|BotW Red|main control unit}}!|Revali|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well done! That was the last of the {{Color|BotW Red|terminals}}. Now...you'll need to start up the {{Color|BotW Red|main control unit}}!|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> A new {{Term|BotW|Marker|link}} will appear on his {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} that denotes its location.<ref>{{Cite|There should be a new glowing mark on your map. You're gonna need to make your way over to that.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There should be a new glowing mark on your map. Head there!|Mipha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I want you to take a good look at your map. There should be a new glowing point on there. Well?|Revali|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Take a good look at your map. Head for the glowing mark you see there.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to activate the {{Term|BotW|Main Control Unit}}, he will be stopped by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} beginning to swirl around it. As the {{Plural|BotW|Phantom Ganon|link}} appear, the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} will instruct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to defeat them in order to progress.<ref>{{Cite|Good luck, little guy! Go get 'im!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Regardless...I believe that you are well prepared for this moment. I have faith in you!|Mipha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can't believe I'm actually saying this... But you must avenge me, Link!|Revali|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Do not allow this to be your end. Fight for your life...and its death!|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} is victorious against these {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, he will have free access to the {{Plural|BotW|Main Control Unit}}. If he tries to activate it at this point, it will warn him that he will not be able to return inside the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} if he does so.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Once you activate the main control unit, you will not be able to reenter the interior of this Divine Beast.}}|Main Control Unit|BotW}}</ref> It then asks him if he is sure he wants to activate it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Do you still wish to activate it?}}|Main Control Unit|BotW}}</ref> Doing so will free the spirits of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}, allowing them to pilot their {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} once again.
