{{Term/Store|Tear of Light|Tears of Light|Series, TP, TPHD, ST, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}},{{Exp Game|TP, ST, HW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141 {{Exp Game|TP, ST}}}}</ref><ref name="HWDE Tutorial"/> also known as the '''Power of Light''',<ref>{{Cite|The black beast you slayed was a {{Color|TPHD Red|shadow being}}. It had come to seize the {{Color|TPHD Red|power of light}} I wield.|Ordona (Spirit)|TPHD}}</ref>{{Exp Game|TP}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They are light-infused magical teardrops that are scattered around various places. Upon their complete collection, they automatically perform their function whenever they are near something that requires their power.

==Locations and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the {{Plural|TPHD|Tear of Light}} are stored within the {{Term|TPHD|Vessel of Light|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Vessel of Light! It's crucial to collecting the Tears of Light.|N/A|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Gather...my scattered Tears of Light...that were stolen by shadow... Gather them...in this...|Eldin (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> They are needed to revive the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit of Light|link}} and dispel the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}} to return the land to its light-abundant state.<ref>{{Cite|When the vessel is full of tears, you can dispel the twilight and return the land to its light-filled state.|N/A|TP}}</ref> {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can only collect the Tears in {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link|display=Wolf form}} due to them being manifested within {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Insect|link}} that can only be detected upon the activation of his {{Term|TPHD|Sense|link|display=wolf Senses}}.<ref>{{Cite|All you have to do is find the dark insects and reclaim all the Tears of Light, right?|Midna|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|To see these evils that hold the Tears of Light, use your [X] senses.|N/A|TP}}</ref> After a {{Term|TPHD|Shadow Insect}} is defeated, it will reveal its {{Term|TPHD|Tear of Light}} within. Commonly, these {{Term|TPHD|Shadow Insects}} exist in pairs, making it easier for {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to locate the tears. To aid in his search, each {{Term|TPHD|Spirit of Light}} marks the location of each Tear on {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s map.<ref>{{Cite|The locations of my scattered Tears of Light are marked on your map....|Lanayru (Spirit)|TP}}</ref> As the game progresses, the distances between their locations and time spent collecting them increases. After filling the {{Term|TPHD|Vessel of Light}} with Tears, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} is immediately transported to the {{Term|TPHD|Spirit Spring|link}} and the {{Term|TPHD|Spirit of Light}} is awakened.<ref>{{Cite|The Vessel of Light is full of tears, and light has returned to this area!|N/A|TP}}</ref>

{{Plural|TPHD|Tear of Light}} can be found in the {{Term|TPHD|Faron Province|link}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Eldin Province|link}}, and in the {{Term|TPHD|Lanayru Province|link}}. Each province has 16 {{Plural|TPHD|Tear of Light}} to be collected, while only 12 must be collected in {{TPHD}}. However, the {{Plural|TPHD|Tear of Light}} can only be found in one province at a time. The {{Term|TPHD|Spirit of Light}} that requires them only marks the Tears on the map that reside within its province.<ref>{{Cite|With the last of my power...let me mark your map...with the locations...of the tears that have turned into...shadow insects...|Faron (Spirit)|TP}}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, each section of the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}} contains three {{Plural|ST|Tear of Light}} that must be collected to power up {{Term|ST|Link}}'s {{Term|ST|Sword|link}}. Once all three {{Plural|ST|Tears of Light}} have been obtained, {{Term|ST|Link}} can strike a {{Term|ST|Phantom|link}} in the back, which stuns it and allows {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}}'s spirit to possess it. After {{Term|ST|Link}} obtains the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword|link}}, he no longer needs to use the {{Plural|ST|Tear of Light}}. The {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword}} itself is infused with divine energy and can allow {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} to possess {{Plural|ST|Phantom}} in the same way {{Term|ST|Link}}'s previous sword did.

In the {{Term|ST|Dark Realm|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Tear of Light}} appear on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}}. If {{Term|ST|Link}} passes through one on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}, it gains temporary invincibility, as well as a significant speed boost whenever {{Term|ST|Link}} blows the whistle. With this upgrade, it is possible to destroy {{Plural|ST|Armored Train|link}}. The {{Plural|ST|Tear of Light}} here will eventually reappear after being collected.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
[[File:HW Tears of Light Sprite.svg|left]]
In {{HW}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Tear of Light}} appear as {{Plural|HWDE|Item Card|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|Twilight Map|link}} as part of the {{TP|-}} DLC Pack. They are used to dispel {{Term|HWDE|Twilight}} clouds from battle squares, thereby removing the battle's restrictions and revealing {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}}' locations. There is also the similar {{Term|HWDE|Tears of Twilight|link}}, which resets the Twilight clouds and are only available once all of the Twilight have been dispelled.
{{Noncanon|End}}
