{{Term/Store|Mr. Guard||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref name="Ghost">{{Cite|Hey. Mr. Guard... Have you heard about a black ghost wandering around town lately?|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref> He is the guard in charge of watching the [[Hytopia Castle]] entrance gate. He sometimes leaves his post to speak with the townspeople.

==Biography==
Mr. Guard can usually be found in front of the castle entrance gate. His duty is to guard the entrance and allow in only those who meet the [[King Tuft|King]]'s Tri Force Hero criteria.<ref>{{Cite|It is my sacred duty of insurmountable importance to guard the castle gate! I'm under orders to only allow through those who meet the surprisingly specific Tri Force Hero criteria!|Mr. Guard|TFH}}</ref> He is first seen refusing entry to the [[Faux Hero]], as he does not meet the right requirements to join the Witch-Hunting Brigade. However he immediately lets [[Link]] inside, who does meet the King's requirements.

Mr. Guard sometimes leaves his post to speak with the other townsfolk of [[Hytopia]], particularly the two women. He is informed by both women that a [[Princess Styla|black ghost]] has been lurking around the area, and is asked by both to join them in finding it and snapping a photo of it.<ref name="Ghost"/><ref>{{Cite|I really saw it! The black ghost! I really did! It was tall and dark, and it ran off when I tried to get closer. You've GOT to believe me!|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> When [[Ms. Drawers]] asks him to join her, he insists that he is too busy watching the gate to join her. However she says that she has seen him looking at her all day, implying he may be smitten with her.<ref>{{Cite|I've seen you staring at me all the long day, you rascal! Could it be... Have I ensnared your senses... ?!|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> However, he may simply have been staring at the spinach stuck in her teeth.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, I'm more concerned about that bit of food stuck in her teeth than this alleged ghost...|Mr. Guard|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I...wasn't able to tell her. About the spinach in her teeth. Did I fail her as a friend...?|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref> When [[Mrs. Pompadour]] invites him to catch the ghost, he says that he is already on a date with his best girl to find the ghost.<ref>{{Cite|Truth be told, I've got a date with my best girl to go ghost hunting later. If we spot a ghost, we're going to press Home at the same time to get some photographic evidence!|Mr. Guard|TFH}}</ref> The woman seems jealous of this.<ref>{{Cite|<small>Fine...</small>|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref>

After Link lifts the curse off of [[Princess Styla]] and the celebration is over, Mr. Guard can still be found in front of the castle entrance. He has been moved to tears by the [[Totem]] demonstrated by Link, King Tuft and [[Sir Combsly]]. He remains on duty even with the kingdom and princess saved.<ref>{{Cite|That totem was... PHENOMENAL!! I'm moved to *sniffle* tears! *sniff* Metaphorically, of course. *sob* I'm still on duty, noble hero. I mustn't let my emotions get the *SNIFFLE* best of me!|Mr. Guard|TFH}}</ref>
