The {{Term/Store|Royal Passenger Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Royal Passenger Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Royal Passenger Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Regal Ring|link}}, four {{Plural|ST|Dragon Scale|link}} and two {{Plural|ST|Ancient Gold Piece|link}}. It features a stately, golden design with an ornamental swan on top; its description reads "This car is opulent enough for a king!"

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Royal Passenger Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Golden Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to eight Hearts.
