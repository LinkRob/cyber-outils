"{{Term/Store|Into the Vortex||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Into the Vortex}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Rist Peninsula}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Amongst a set of ruins at the base of {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}} and an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}. The {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|display=Tablet}} reads that only a hero with the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} will be granted the trial on the {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula|display=Peninsula}}.<ref>{{Cite|Only a hero bearing this {{Color|BotW Red|ancient orb}} shall be granted the right of {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} on Rist Peninsula.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Once these words have been read, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

To complete this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must carry the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} to the spiraled tip of {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula}}. Along the way, he will encounter {{Plural|BotW|Water Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}. Once there, he must insert the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} into a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}, which will cause {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo Shrine|link}} to emerge from the ground. After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.<ref>{{Cite|You discovered the hero's trial by carrying the orb to the center of Rist Peninsula.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
