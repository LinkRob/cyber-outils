{{Term/Store|Revitalizing Potion|Revitalizing Potions|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Revitalizing Potions can be bought at the [[Potion Shop#Skyward Sword|Potion Shop]] for 30 [[Rupee]]s. They can be stored in empty [[Bottle]]s and restore the Shield gauge when drank in addition to restoring four [[Heart]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You got some {{Color|SS Gold|Revitalizing Potion}}! This potion {{Color|SS Red|fully replenishes}} your shield gauge and also restores {{Color|SS Red|four}} hearts!|N/A|SS}}</ref>

Revitalizing Potions can be upgraded at the potion shop by infusing them with various [[Bug]]s.
