{{Term/Store|Manny||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Manny|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} is a man who resides alone in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, often spending his days people-watching in front of {{Term|BotW|The Great Ton Pu Inn|link}}.<ref name="Checking">{{Cite|Checkin' everyone out today, too. The checkin' just never stops...|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> At night, he stands in front of the door to his house.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches to {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}}, he will find him mumbling to himself.<ref>{{Cite|*mumble mumble*|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> If spoken to, he explains that he is checking out the people who pass him by, which is what he does everyday.<ref name="Checking"/> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is staying at the {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ever stay at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}}?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} respond that he has, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} states that it makes no difference to him.<ref>{{Cite|[Yep.] Makes no difference to me, but...|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims no to have done so, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} will be confused.<ref>{{Cite|[Nope.] If you're not staying there, then...|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref>

Whatever his response, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} will declare that his loitering is actually a profession.<ref>{{Cite|I'm supposed to check out everyone who comes into town.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees his this, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} will be pleased.<ref>{{Cite|[Right, right.] Thought so.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that this is weird, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} calls him weird in response.<ref>{{Cite|[Sounds...weird.] YOUR job is weird. ...Whatever it is you do.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} claims that {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} has been peaceful lately, but he is aware that life outside the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|display=Village}} can be dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|I know things have been peaceful recently, but the outside world is still pretty dangerous, yeah?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he says that he has put himself in charge of making sure no suspicious individuals come by, but he accidentally reveals that he is watching for "beauties."<ref>{{Cite|So I gotta check and make sure if any beauties—er, I mean—suspicious folks have come around here.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref>

Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} claim that this job sounds difficult, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} will agree with him.<ref>{{Cite|[Sounds tough.] Yeah. It's a tough job! I'm over here makin' it look easy.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} comments on his mistake, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} will sigh.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|*sigh*}}}}|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> Either way, he claims that his actions can be explained by seeing {{Term|BotW|Prima|link}}, the proprietor of {{Term|BotW|The Great Ton Pu Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're lucky enough to stay at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}}, you'll see what I mean. Say hullo to Prima for me if you do.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He then quietly remarks that she does not know who he is.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Not that she even knows who I am...}}|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> Frustrated by this {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} decides to give her a gift.<ref name="Something">{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|This is tough... I have to give her SOMETHING...}}}}|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he knows what {{Term|BotW|Prima}} would like, but he claims he is doing so on behalf of a friend of a friend.<ref>{{Cite|What kind of gift do you think Prima at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}} would want? Just take a guess. It's not for me, you understand. It's for...my buddy's friend.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> After he finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|A Gift for My Beloved|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

Following this conversation, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to ask {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} questions.<ref>{{Cite|What is it?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You need anything else?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if he knows {{Term|BotW|Prima}}, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} scolds him for not referring to her casually, but he then inquires whether he has been to the {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Do you know Prima?] That's Ms. Prima to you! You ever been to the inn here?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that {{Term|BotW|Prima}} serves as the clerk for {{Term|BotW|The Great Ton Pu Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|Prima is the clerk over at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}}.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He then repeats his desire to give her a gift.<ref name="Something"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} questions {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}}'s loitering, he defends himself, claiming that he is preventing suspicious people from entering {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|["Checkin' out?"] There's weird, suspicious folks out there! So I gotta check everyone out to make sure no one dangerous gets through.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He believes that this is an important service for the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|display=Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm volunteerin' my own time because it's an important service for the village.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref>

After talking to {{Term|BotW|Prima}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} to share what he learned. While {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} is surprised, he is also jealous that {{Term|BotW|Prima}} spoke to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I talked to Prima.] What? Prima told you what she wants?! {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Prima...never even talks to me...}}}}|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He tries to be calm, but he then shouts and demands that {{Term|BotW|Link}} tell him what she likes.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* I mean, that's cool, that's cool... SO THEN, WHAT IS IT?!|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> When he learns that {{Term|BotW|Prima}} supposedly wants 100 {{Plural|BotW|Restless Cricket|link}}, he remarks how coincidental it is, since he also loves {{Plural|BotW|Restless Cricket}}.<ref>{{Cite|Prima wants {{Color|BotW Red|restless crickets}}? And {{Color|BotW Red|100 of them}}? Hahaha! What a small world! I love {{Color|BotW Red|restless crickets}}! I'm a connoisseur of them! {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|I think that's what that word means...}}}}|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> However, he is concerned about catching 100 of them, so he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to help him by collecting 10.<ref>{{Cite|But catching {{Color|BotW Red|100}} of them is gonna be tough. Think you could help me get started on those? How about you get me... {{Color|BotW Red|10}} of them? I'll get you something nice for your trouble.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has 10 {{Plural|BotW|Restless Cricket}}, he must return to give them to {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I got the goods!] You have some {{Color|BotW Red|restless crickets}} already?! Wow, that was fast!|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He quietly reveals that he has yet to catch a single {{Term|BotW|Restless Cricket|display=Cricket}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|I haven't even caught one yet...}}}}|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to hand over his {{Plural|BotW|Restless Cricket|display=Crickets}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, lemme see those {{Color|BotW Red|restless crickets}} you caught!|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He is impressed that {{Term|BotW|Link}} actually has them, as he thought he was being messed with.<ref>{{Cite|You really got them? I thought you were just messing with me.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> After confirming that there are 10 {{Plural|BotW|Restless Cricket}}, {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|OK, so... Hrm... Yup, that's {{Color|BotW Red|10}}. Thanks, I'll take 'em. A little somethin' for your troubles...|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He resolves to catch the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Restless Cricket}}, at which point he will use them to ask {{Term|BotW|Prima}} on a date.<ref>{{Cite|Right then! I'll go ahead and catch the rest of 'em. Then I'll take 'em to Prima, and we'll have that d-d-date... {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Oh, no! My s-s-s-stutter...}}}}|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have more questions he can ask {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|You need somethin'?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} if he is still checking people out, he will stumble through his words, claiming he is looking out for beauties, tourists, and suspicious individuals.<ref>{{Cite|[Are you...checkin'?] Just checking out the beauties—er, I mean tourists—er, I mean—suspicious folk! That's what I'm doing!|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> Since he mentioned beauties, he brings up {{Term|BotW|Prima}}, claiming that no one else could be as beautiful as her.<ref>{{Cite|Well...if we're gonna talk about beauties, though, no one can hold a candle to Prima, y'know?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He also claims to have heard that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} was beautiful, as well, and while she may have been able to compare to {{Term|BotW|Prima}}, he thinks this only speaks to how pretty they both are.<ref>{{Cite|They say the {{Color|BotW Blue|princess}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} who lived 100 years ago was super pretty, too. Maybe she coulda given Prima a run for her beauty, but that's the level we're talkin' about here.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref>

Following these statements, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} about {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}, and he is shocked that he does not know about her.<ref>{{Cite|[Princess?] Really?! Are you serious?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He explains that she was "swallowed up" by the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, and he attributes the peace in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} to her sacrifice.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|princess}} of Hyrule Castle who got swallowed up by the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Calamity}}! That's the only reason we got peace here now. We really owe that {{Color|BotW Blue|princess}}.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> After hearing this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask him about the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}, and {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} wonders whether he has been {{Term|BotW|Slumber of Restoration|link|display=sleeping}} in a {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link|display=cave}} for the past 100 years.<ref>{{Cite|[Great Calamity?] The {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Calamity}} was that awful thing from 100 years ago! You been asleep inside a cave or somethin'?!|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He has heard that is was a serpent or a black fog, but since he never saw it himself, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to make inquiries about it with the older residents of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Some people say it was some kind of {{Color|BotW Blue|serpent}}. Others say it was like some kind of {{Color|BotW Blue|black fog}} or something. I didn't see it myself, so I don't really know. But the old timers around here might know more about it.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Manny (Breath of the Wild)}} while carrying the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, he recognizes it as {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}}'s and wonders how she is doing.<ref>{{Cite|That's Purah's, right? I haven't seen her in a while. Wonder how she's doing...|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> Changing the subject, he brings up how many beautiful women reside in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}, bringing up {{Term|BotW|Prima}} as an example.<ref>{{Cite|This is totally unrelated, but this village has its shares of beauties, huh? Like Prima at the Ton Pu Inn.|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref> He claims that {{Term|BotW|Purah}} herself was likely just as beautiful when she was younger.<ref>{{Cite|I bet that Purah was a looker when she was young too, y'know?|Manny (Breath of the Wild)|BotW}}</ref>
