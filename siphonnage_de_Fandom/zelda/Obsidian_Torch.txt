{{Term/Store|Obsidian Torch|Obsidian Torches|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.

==Location and Uses==
Obsidian Torches are a type of {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}} that aid {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} in navigating caves and dungeons by lighting up dark areas and clearing stumps out of their path.<ref>{{Cite|Provides more visibility depending on your beat combo. Can burn stumps and light up braziers.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> The amount of visibility they offer depends on the hero's beat combo, increasing with each consecutive hit against an enemy and resetting when the combo is lost.

They can be found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} for 40 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.
