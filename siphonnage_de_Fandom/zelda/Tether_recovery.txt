A '''tether recovery''' is a type of [[recovery]] move in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]]. It first appears in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. A few fighters representing {{TLoZ|Series}} can perform a tether recovery.

The technique involves the fighter latching onto the side of an arena with an extendable item. If there is a ledge nearby, the tether snaps to it automatically. The player can press the standard attack button after grappling a ledge to pull their character over to there. If the ledge is occupied while using a tether recovery, it does not grab. If a player ledge-drops after tethering, they can re-tether.

There is a similar move in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'' for Link, Young Link, and Samus, the [[wall grapple]], essentially the predecessor of the tether recovery. However, the difference between the two moves is that a tether seeks out a ledge, while wall grapples activate upon touching a wall. In addition, throw-based tether recoveries, like wall grapples in ''Super Smash Bros. Melee'', no longer put characters in a [[helpless]] animation if they miss, although up special tether recoveries do (excluding [[Zero Suit Samus]]'s).

Many tether recoveries have a longer range than regular recovery moves and also home in on the ledge, allowing for better control. A disadvantage with tether recoveries is that they can be [[edgehog]]ged, causing the tether recovery to fail. If used correctly, the user can take their opponents down with them if the tether hits the opponent. None of the other characters can grab the ledge while a player is attached to it with a tether, making it a way to edgehog the other players. Tether recoveries cannot be used on ledges like drop blocks and icy edges.

Certain tether recoveries are usable while performing a [[grab aerial]], a self-explanatory technique where the fighter uses their grab move in midair.

==Relevance to ''The Legend of Zelda''==
[[Link]] can use his [[Clawshot]] as a tether recovery of the grab aerial type in {{SSBB}} and {{SSB4}}, replacing his ability from {{SSBM}} of wall-grappling with the Hookshot. In {{SSBU}}, Link cannot use any tether recovery, as he is no longer equipped with a Hookshot or Clawshot, neither of which appear in {{BotW}}, which Link's moves are based on.

[[Toon Link]] can use the [[Hookshot]] as a tether recovery of the grab aerial type from {{SSBB|-}} onward. [[Young Link]] can use the same weapon as a tether recovery in {{SSBU|-}}.

[[Sheik]] can perform a tether recovery in {{SSBB|-}} by using her [[Chain]], which is her side special move and not a grab aerial like the Hookshot and Clawshot.
