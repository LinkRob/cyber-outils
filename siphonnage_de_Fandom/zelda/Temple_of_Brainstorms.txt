The {{Term/Store|Temple of Brainstorms||Series, CoH}} is a dungeon in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Temple of Braintorms}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref> The {{Term|CoH|Temple of Brainstorms}} is included as part of the {{SotM}} expansion pack.

==Entering the Temple==
The {{Term|CoH|Temple of Brainstorms}} is located in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}} version of {{Term|CoH|Death Mountain|link}}. {{Term|CoH|Death Mountain}} must be crossed before the temple can be reached.

Upon entering the temple, the intruder's {{Plural|CoH|Item|link}} will be confiscated,<ref>{{Cite|INTRUDER DETECTED. INITIATING DUNGEON SEQUENCE. CONFISCATING INTRUDER'S ITEMS...|Synthrova|CoH}}</ref> and they will need to complete the temple before they may have them back.<ref>{{Cite|WELCOME, INTRUDER. IF YOU WANT YOUR POINTY STICKS BACK, THEN YOU WILL HAVE TO ESCAPE OUR MAZE.|Synthrova|CoH}}</ref>
