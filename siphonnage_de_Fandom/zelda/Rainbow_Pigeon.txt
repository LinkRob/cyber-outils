==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Rainbow Pigeon}}
Rainbow Pigeons are colorful birds that can be found in [[Hyrule Ridge]] and the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. Particularly large flocks can be found circling in the sky above the [[Seres Scablands]]. They're omnivorous, eating nuts, grains, insects, and worms.<ref>{{Cite|They're most fond of nuts and grain but won't pass up the occasional insect or earthworm if offered.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When one is killed, a Rainbow Pigeon will drop a [[Raw Bird Thigh]]. Their meat is noted to be of higher quality than a [[Wood Pigeon|Wood Pigeon's]].<ref>{{Cite|Their meat is of a higher quality than a typical wood pigeon's.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>
