The Membership Card is a [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Item]] from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{hatnote|”A Membership Card for The Sweet Spot, where you can eat cotton candy, the trendy treat. Every Deku Scrub wants one!” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="EoW Membership Card">{{Cite|A Membership Card for The Sweet Spot, where you can eat cotton candy, the trendy treat. Every Deku Scrub wants one!|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
When [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] and [[Tri]] arrive in the [[Faron Wetlands]] after a massive [[rift]] appears in the north, they discover the [[Deku Scrub|Deku Scrubs]] are strangely obsessed with “Cotton Candy”—a sticky treat made from spiderwebs spilling out of the rifts. The main source is a building just north of [[Scrubton]], known as [[the Sweet Spot]]. Tri confirms it’s a rift entry point, but two Deku Scrub guards block the way, demanding a membership card.

To get one, Zelda must close the rift at the Stilled Blossu’s House and then escape from the Deku Scrub Lockup. Once she does, Blossu rewards her with the card, granting access to the Sweet Spot.
