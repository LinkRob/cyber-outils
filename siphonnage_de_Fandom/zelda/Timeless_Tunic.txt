The {{Term/Store|Timeless Tunic||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Timeless Tunic is a fan favorite! Vintage at its finest, my dahling. Ooh! I'm feeling tres nostalgic now!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be tailored at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of three {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token|link}} and 100 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. It is available for purchase from the start of the game. Unlike all other {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}}, which appear as articles of clothing, the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}} changes {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s entire appearance into a faux-retro [[Wikipedia:Voxel|voxel]] look.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}} causes all of the game's music to resemble 8-bit [[Wikipedia:Chiptune|chiptunes]] in the style of the [[NES]] or the [[Game Boy]],<ref>{{Cite|Look like a hero of old with background music pulled from a beloved memory.|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> as well as altering {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s sound effects and voice clips to sound like they were produced by a classic video game console with limited audio capabilities. While only the {{Term|TFH|Links}}(s) wearing the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}} can hear the chiptune music, all other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} present can hear their altered sound effects. If a {{Term|TFH|Links}} wearing the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}} strikes the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|link}}, its songs will begin to play in the chiptune style, returning to normal when a different {{Term|TFH|Links}} strikes the Ball.

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}}. Though the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} appears in the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}}, it is impossible to collect the required {{Plural|TFH|Material}} in this mode.

==Trivia==
*8-bit variations of the game's soundtrack were originally created due to limitations of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] [[Download Play]] feature, as their file sizes are much smaller and easier to transmit than the game's regular music.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= This is related to the technical limitations of Download Play. Tri Force Heroes supports Download Play, which allows people to play the game even if they don’t own it by receiving data to Nintendo 3DS. There is a memory limit for Download Play, so music couldn't be transmitted normally. At first we removed the BGM, but as one would except it feels desolate without music. Therefore staff that was responsible of sound said: "If we created NES-like music, the data would become smaller."|name= Hiromasa Shikata|url= http://nintendoeverything.com/zelda-tri-force-heroes-cheer-outfits-idol-origins-how-the-timeless-tunic-came-to-be/|title= Cheer Outfit’s idol origins, how the Timeless Tunic came to be}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}} was then created as a way to let players hear these variations at any time.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= As we had created NES-like music under these circumstances, you'd want to listen to it when you like to and not just when using Download Play, and thus Timeless Tunic was born.|name= Hiromasa Shikata|url= http://nintendoeverything.com/zelda-tri-force-heroes-cheer-outfits-idol-origins-how-the-timeless-tunic-came-to-be/|title= Cheer Outfit’s idol origins, how the Timeless Tunic came to be}}</ref>
**Due to an oversight, if the {{Term|TFH|Links}} hosting a Download Play session wears the {{Term|TFH|Timeless Tunic}}, the other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} will be unable to hear his voice sounds.
