{{Term/Store|Maggie|Maggies|Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Maggie}}
Maggie was once the poor girl of [[Windfall Island]] who was then kidnapped and taken to the [[Forsaken Fortress]] by the [[Helmaroc King]].<ref>{{Cite|Ooh, hoo hoo! My sweet, sweet daughter! My only daughter! Poor Maggie... She was captured by a bunch of villains and taken to the Forsaken Fortress!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can believe it. After all, two girls from Windfall have already been kidnapped. Poor Maggie and Mila... It just breaks my heart to think about those two.|N/A|TWW}}</ref> According to [[Mila]], she has always been poor.<ref>{{Cite|And to make matters worse, for some reason I still can't figure out, that slob Maggie, who was the poorest girl in town, suddenly got filthy rich! Maggie, of all people! It makes me so mad that I want to do something terrible! Grrrr!|Mila|TWW}}</ref> When [[Link]] comes to rescue [[Aryll|his sister]], Maggie is liberated as well, and the pirate [[Tetra]] gives her back to [[Maggie's Father|her father]]. When Maggie showed her father the [[Skull Necklace]]s she had taken back from the Forsaken Fortress, he went off to sell them, allowing the girl and her father to become rich overnight.<ref>{{Cite|Why, she even returned with a huge load of lovely, glittering, delicious treasure. Thanks to her little incident, we've become filthy rich overnight! And I mean FILTHY!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It was by selling these for lots of cash that my father and I were finally able to escape from poverty!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> Maggie, although rich and living in the [[Auction House]], is still quite depressed.<ref>{{Cite|And to think that I would ever be able to live in such a beautiful, opulent house... It's like a dream come true! Hmmmm... But no! NO! It's a nightmare! Without HIM by my side, it's like a rainstorm rages in my heart...|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> She misses the [[Moblin]] named Moe,<ref>{{Cite|When I think of my precious little Moe... Troo di loo la la! I can't sleep at night... Why doesn't he write me back?|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> who apparently liked Maggie and gave her the necklaces.<ref>{{Cite|He was so kind to me in the Forsaken Fortress. That sweet boy who gave me all those expensive necklaces when I fled from that cursed island...|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> She asks Link to send a [[letter]] for her because her father won't allow her to leave the house.<ref>{{Cite|My father has been so strict with me lately. He won't let me out of the house! That's why I need you to take this letter -this note to Moe- and drop it into the postbox for me. Please, troo di loo la!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> A while later after Link sends the letter, her father kicks out [[Ilari]], the [[Postman#The Wind Waker|Rito postman]], who was trying to deliver [[Moblin's Letter|Moe's letter]] to Maggie,<ref>{{Cite|Silence! I'm telling you we don't allow postmen here! Begone! Begone!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't be ridiculous! I went through great trials and tribulations to go all the way to the  Forsaken Fortress and return with Moe's letter...|Ilari|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I came to deliver a letter addressed to Maggie from a Moblin named Moe, but as you saw, the girl's stubborn father won't permit me to give it to her.|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> so Link must find the postman and deliver Moe's letter to Maggie himself.<ref>{{Cite|I have an idea. That old fool may not permit postmen on his premises... But surely he would allow YOU in the place, would he not? Could you deliver this letter to Maggie in my stead?|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> In this letter, Moe writes that he loves Maggie so much that he could eat her "for dinner" in broken, poorly written sentences befitting a [[Moblin]].<ref>{{Cite|This is Moe. I... like... you... ...Ma...ggie... ...so...much...that... ...I...want...to... ...eat you...for dinner.|Moblin's Letter|TWW}}</ref> When she reads his letter, the young girl proclaims that it is a wedding proposal,<ref>{{Cite|Did you hear that? Those words? This means... I mean, it must mean... Can it be...?!? Is this his marriage proposal?|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> that she wants to marry Moe, <ref>{{Cite|It must be! At last, my feelings have been conveyed to Moe! At last he understands how I feel!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> and that she will set sail for the [[Forsaken Fortress]] the following day. She never does, however, for she says she could never leave her dear father behind.<ref>{{Cite|Whenever I think of my dear father, I lose my resolve to leave home...|Maggie|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|He's so infatuated with money and precious objects. And I am quite precious, you know. I guess I just have to stay here with him!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref>

Maggie is quite popular with the boys, but she has a tendency to prefer "wild" boys over others.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that Moe... I just adore Moe's messy handwriting. Ooh di loo la la! ♪ It's so dashing...and wild...and dangerous!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> This could explain her obsession with Moe the Moblin. 
[[File:MaggiesLetterB.png|thumb|142x142px|The backside of Maggie's letter to Moe to Moblin]]
She appears to be fond of [[Rabbit|rabbits]]. Maggie wears a [[Bunny Hood|rabbit-ear headdress]] and her letter to Moe is sealed with the likeness of a rabbit.

== Trivia ==
Like [[Mila]] and unlike [[Maggie's Father|their]] [[Mila's Father|fathers]], Maggie does not appear to recognize [[Link]] when he returns to [[Windfall Island]] to meet her.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Maggie}}'s Spanish and German name, Dolores, means "sorrowful", befitting her frugal life in the beginning of the game. In contrast, Mila's Spanish and German name means "fortunate".
