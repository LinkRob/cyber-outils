{{Term/Store|Frelly||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Frelly|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Frelly}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who manages the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref name="Shop">{{Cite|My name is Frelly. I run a shop in Gerudo Town where I rent out {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}}.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|link|display=Northwest Booth}} is managed by {{Term|BotW|Frelly}}'s mother, {{Term|BotW|Kohm|link}}.<ref name="Kohm"/> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can first encounter {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} just outside the front gate of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}. When Link speaks to her, Frelly asks if Link crossed the Desert on foot.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, wow... Don't tell me you crossed the Gerudo Desert on foot?|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells her that he came on foot, {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} comments that it must have been difficult and that it is common sense to travel the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} with a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Must have been hard to walk through the desert—especially having your feet sink into the sand. It's common sense to travel the desert with {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}} around here.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he rode there, {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} is impressed that a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} man has mastered {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} surfing.<ref>{{Cite|Wow... A Hylian voe who has mastered {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal}} surfing?|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} is speechless, pausing for a moment before saying that it must have been a challenge to catch a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|I didn't think it was possible. I'm speechless! I think I may need a moment... Phew. Anyway... I bet it was a real challenge to catch a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}, wasn't it?|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Frelly}} then introduces herself and explains that she rents out trained {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} for people who do not have the time to train their own.<ref name="Shop"/><ref>{{Cite|Most people can't take the time to train their own {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}}, so I rent out the ones I've trained!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> She assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she can answer any questions he has about {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} and that she is skilled at training them.<ref>{{Cite|If you need to know anything about {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}}, I should be your first stop.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you have any questions about {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}}, come talk to me. I'm quite skilled at training them!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}}, {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} explains that they are native to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert Gateway|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Sand seals}} are animals native to the Gerudo Desert Gateway.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> She then says that it is dangerous to cross the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} on foot, but one would breeze along by crossing with a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's quite dangerous to cross the desert on foot, but if you have a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}, you'll breeze right along!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> This has made them very important to the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Obviously, {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}} are incredibly important to the Gerudo people.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} how to catch a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, she tells him that they are sensitive to sound and that they must be approached very slowly.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Sand seals}} are especially sensitive to the sounds around them. The trick is to approach very, very slowly...|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> However, she points out that most {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} simply rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} to avoid the hassle of catching one.<ref>{{Cite|It's a pain in the neck, though, so most Gerudo just end up renting one instead.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks how to ride a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} tells him that he only needs a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, since {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} will simply go in whatever direction he steers them.<ref>{{Cite|Steering a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} is really easy—they'll go in whatever direction you want. But! You need a {{Color|BotW Red|shield}} to sand-seal surf, so don't forget about that.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> She recommends that the best way to learn is to try it.<ref>{{Cite|Just giving it a shot is best, but if there's anything you don't get, let me know! You know, the best way for you to learn how to do this is by just doing it!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} also tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come by her shop in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} if he has trouble catching one,<ref>{{Cite|If you're having a hard time catching one, come by my shop in Gerudo Town. I rent out sand seals and...oh.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> but quickly remembers that men are not allowed inside the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}'s walls.<ref>{{Cite|You're a voe... My shop is inside the town walls, so you won't be able to get in...|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} tells him to look for the big {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Statue|link}} to find her shop in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|My shop in Gerudo Town has a big {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal statue}} on it. Just look for that, and you'll find me!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth}}, she says that {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} are the best way to cross the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq! If you are hoping to cross the vast Gerudo Desert, {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal}} surfing is the way to do it!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> She offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} rental for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, but if he does not have any {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}, she will include a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}} for a total of 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's only {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to rent one! Need a shield, too? Just pay {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}! What do you say?|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} wishes to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, he will tell {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} to "seal the deal", which is a pun that confuses her.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's seal the deal!] What? Is that some kind of slang? {{Small|I wonder if it's just a thing outside Gerudo Town...}}|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> After hearing this, {{Term|BotW|Frelly}} herself will start to use {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} puns, such as telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it would be a "sealious mistake" to travel the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} without a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} and saying "seal you another time" when {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes a rental.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway... The Gerudo Desert is vast. It'd be a sealious mistake not to take a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} if you're traveling out of town.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Seal you another time! Sav'orq!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>
