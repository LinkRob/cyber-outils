The universe in which {{TLoZ}} takes place. Although the connection between the various lands, worlds, dimensions, and realms is often ambiguous, [[Encyclopedia|''The Legend of Zelda Encyclopedia'']] acts as the primary source for the structure of the world. What follows is a basic overview of the ''Zelda'' universe structure and layout. For extended overview regarding the actual history of the universe see the [[Zelda Timeline]], and for extended history, lore, and culture regarding locations, see their corresponding articles.

The universe is split into three distinct "planes": The world of '''The Heavens''' lie on a higher plane than the world called [[Hyrule]], and the lower plane of existence beneath both is the [[Underworld]]. Outside of these three planes, there also exist the [[Minish Realm]] and the inverted world of Lorule. 

==The Heavens==
The Heavens, also referred to as the Distant Nebula, is a higher plane of existence connected to the world of Hyrule, specifically the Light World, as well as directly to the Land of the Spirits of Good. Not much else is known about the Heavens, other than the fact that the [[Golden Goddesses]] likely reside there.

==The World of Hyrule==
The universe is home to the land called "Hyrule". Within Hyrule exist the [[Light World]] and the Land of the Spirits of Good, as well as connected parallels, such as the [[Twilight Realm]]. After the [[Great Flood]] occurs, the world of Hyrule becomes a giant ocean.

===[[Light World]]/The Great Sea===
[[File:Great Sea Chart.jpg|thumb|The submerged Kingdom of Hyrule in the Light World following the Great Flood]]
The Light World is a land within the world of Hyrule, home to the various countries such as [[Hyrule|The Kingdom of Hyrule]] and [[Hytopia]], as well as related realms and parallel worlds. [[The Sky]] is situated in the clouds above these countries within the Light World. Following the Great Flood, the Light World becomes the [[Great Sea]].  

===='''The Kingdom of Hyrule'''====
The Kingdom of Hyrule is a country set at the heart of the world of Hyrule, home to the [[Sacred Realm]]. The [[Dark World]], linked to the Sacred Realm and considered its "opposite", stands within Hyrule independently of the Light World, but is also considered "a second Hyrule".<ref>June 19, 2018. [https://www.darkhorse.com/Books/3000-358/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Encyclopedia-HC The Legend of Zelda Encyclopedia] - pp. 29</ref>

====='''[[The Depths]]'''=====
The Depths is a subterranean region located underneath the Kingdom of Hyrule. It was discovered by the [[Zonai]] and filled with mines to farm [[Zonaite]], an ore possessing qualities that are not yet fully understood. The Depths are also home to the ancient [[Goron]] city [[Fire Temple#Tears of the Kingdom|Gorondia]].

===='''Termina'''====
A parallel version of the Kingdom of Hyrule, [[Termina]], formed around the burdened heart and memories of the [[Skull Kid]], temporarily came into existence for three days (in limbo), and was ultimately erased from existence after the fourth day.<ref>June 19, 2018. [https://www.darkhorse.com/Books/3000-358/The-Legend-of-Zelda-Encyclopedia-HC The Legend of Zelda Encyclopedia] - pp. 36 - 37</ref> During its existence, the pathway to the world itself existed within Hyrule, specifically in the [[Lost Woods]].

===='''Hytopia'''====
A kingdom which depends on its fashion industry. It is north of the Kingdom of Hyrule.

===='''[[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]]'''====
Holodrum and Labrynna are separate regions that exist within the Light World alongside the Kingdom of Hyrule.

====='''[[Subrosia]]'''=====
Subrosia is a subterranean region located underneath the land of Holodrum.

===='''[[Koholint Island]]'''====
Koholint Island is a parallel world, which exists within the dream of the [[Wind Fish]] somewhere on the outer waters surrounding the Kingdom of Hyrule. After the Wind Fish awakens, this world ceases to exist.

===The Twilight Realm===
The Twilight Realm is a parallel version of the Light World, which exists "behind" it. 

=== The Neighboring Sea ===
After the [[Great Flood]], the Neighboring Sea now neighbours the [[Great Sea]]; the now flooded Kingdom of Hyrule. As the name suggests, this sea neighbors the submerged kingdom.

==== '''World of the Ocean King''' ====
The [[World of the Ocean King]] is an ocean realm that exists parallel to the Neighboring Sea.

===Open Sea===
A sea located in the world-wide ocean. It is an unknown distance away from the Great Sea, and the Neighboring Sea. The Open Sea is connected to the Land of the Spirits of Good.

===Land of the Spirits of Good===
A land that is connected to the Open Sea. The Land of the Spirits of Good is the land on which "[[New Hyrule]]" was founded, following the discovery of this continent by [[Tetra]].

===='''New Hyrule '''====
The new Kingdom of Hyrule founded by Tetra and her crew, upon the discovery of the Land of Spirits of Good and the establishing of friendly contact with the native [[Lokomo]].

==[[Underworld]]==
The underworld is on a lower plane of existence. It is likely the source of most of the evil within the world of Hyrule. It is connected directly to the Land of the Spirits of Good.

==Others==
Other dimensions and worlds that exist independent of the Heavens, Hyrule, and Underworld.

===Lorule===
[[File:ALBW Hyrule Lorule.png|thumb|[[Lorule Castle]], Lorule's inverted [[Hyrule Castle]]]]
The inverted dimension of Lorule is an exact mirror image of the world of Hyrule. It is unknown if Lorule is its own inverted dimension that is connected to Hyrule, or if it is itself contained within its own alternate universe.  

===='''The Kingdom of Lorule'''====
The [[Lorule|Kingdom of Lorule]] that exists within the world of Lorule. It is the inverted Lorule equivalent of the Kingdom of Hyrule.

===[[Minish Realm|The Minish Realm]]===
The world of the [[Minish]] race. The world is connected to the Light World. However, it does not exist within the same plane of existence. Instead, it exists independently on a level between the Heavens and Hyrule.
