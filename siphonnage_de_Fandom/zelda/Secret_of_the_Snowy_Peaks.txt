"{{Term/Store|Secret of the Snowy Peaks||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Secret of the Snowy Peaks}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Mount Granajh}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
There is a small {{Term|BotW|Ruined House|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Mount Granajh|link}} where {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can read a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} called the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Peak Log|link}}. It describes an unknown person's research into an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} hidden on the mountain.<ref>{{Cite|This is a log of my findings concerning the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} that sleeps on a snowy Gerudo peak.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> The author found an old text with instructions to cast a cold shadow onto the core of the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} when it glows.<ref>{{Cite|I come to this snowy mountain intending to find the {{Color|BotW Red|ancient}} shrine that was written about in an old text. "The {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} slumbers near the pedestal on a snowy Gerudo peak."|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> They believe that the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} referred to within is the one on {{Term|BotW|Mount Granajh}}.<ref>{{Cite|I believe I've arrived at the {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}} mentioned in the text.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref>

In the next entry, the author discusses another piece of text they have found that could help solve the mystery.<ref>{{Cite|On a brighter note, I've found an explanation in the source text that may be the key to this riddle.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> The text says to cast a "cold shadow" onto the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} while it is glowing.<ref>{{Cite|"When the snow mountain {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}} glows, cast a cold shadow onto its core."|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> The author recalls that the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} does glow for a short time each day, but they do not know how to make a cold shadow.<ref>{{Cite|I noticed a faint glow to the {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}} for a short while each day. But how to "cast a cold shadow onto its core"...? It seems that this calls for further research here.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref>

The author believes they are close to discovering the solution in the final entry.<ref>{{Cite|I can feel that I'm a hair's breadth away from unlocking the secrets hidden within the {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> They think that the "cold shadow" refers to a specific type of shadow,<ref>{{Cite|I'm certain that "{{Color|BotW Red|casting}}" and "{{Color|BotW Red|cold}}" refer to using a specific type of {{Color|BotW Red|shadow}}.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> but they are running out of food and must abandon their research.<ref>{{Cite|But I've run out of food entirely, and I doubt now that I ever had the tools to do the job...whatever they might be.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes reading the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Peak Log}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

The {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} referred to in the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Peak Log}} resides nearby on the side of {{Term|BotW|Daval Peak|link}}. To solve the riddle, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either use {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} on the pond near the edge of {{Term|BotW|Mount Granajh}} or stand at the edge holding a {{Term|BotW|Snowball|link}} at 16:00. When this is done, the shadow of either the {{Term|BotW|Snowball}} or {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} block will be cast onto the center of the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} and activate it. This will cause {{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma Shrine|link}} to rise from the snow,<ref>{{Cite|Casting a shadow on the mysteriously glowing pedestal revealed none other than an ancient shrine!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> which completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
