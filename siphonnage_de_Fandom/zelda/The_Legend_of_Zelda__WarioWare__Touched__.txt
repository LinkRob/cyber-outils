'''The Legend of Zelda''' is a microgame in ''[[WarioWare: Touched!]]'' Despite its name, the microgame is not only based on the first {{TLoZ}}, but also {{TAoL}}.

==Gameplay==
The microgame features levels based on ''The Legend of Zelda'' and {{TAoL}}, including the {{Term|TLoZ|Graveyard|link}}, inside a [[dungeon]], and the overworld. The object is to tap all the onscreen [[Fairy|Fairies]] to restore Link's health. If successful, Link will reveal himself and hold up a [[Nintendo DS]]. In {{TAoL|-}} setting, Link will simply walk past.

The later difficulty levels include enemies such as [[Ghini]]s, [[Moblin]]s, and [[Like Like]]s.

==Levels==
* '''Level 1:''' Catch one stationary Fairy.
* '''Level 2:''' Catch two moving Fairies from among a small group of enemies.
* '''Level 3:''' Catch two moving Fairies from among a large group of enemies.
