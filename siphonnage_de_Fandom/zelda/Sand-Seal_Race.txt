The {{Term/Store|Sand-Seal Race||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal race}} is a traditional Gerudo competition. The champion may even be there right now!|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally|link}} just south of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. It becomes available once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} frees the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} is gone, we can finally use the race course!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can enter the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Shabonne|link}}, who introduces him to {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} and {{Term|BotW|Tali|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} can be found next to {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} and {{Term|BotW|Tali}}, as well as an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} known as the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|link}}.

To complete "{{Term|BotW|The Undefeated Champ|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must beat {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s record of 1:30 to win the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tali's record is {{Color|BotW Red|1}}:{{Color|BotW Red|30}}! If you can beat that record, that {{Color|BotW Blue|trophy}} is yours.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} succeeds, he will be rewarded with the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, and can use it to reveal the {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo Shrine|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will end after he receives it. <ref>{{Cite|Beat the great Tali's time and walk away with the one and only trophy!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There's a legend shared among sand-seal racers that the grand trophy is the key to finding an ancient treasure.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> After completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can try to beat his own record for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|So do you wanna take a ride? Even as the champion, you'll need to pay {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} each time.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} succeeds, he will earn a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The best time so far is {{Color|BotW Red|[X]}}. If you can beat it, you'll be awarded 300 rupees!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref>

During the race, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must ride a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} through a course following a series of marked arches. There are seven of these arches between the starting and finishing arch, and for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to finish the race he must pass through all of them. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets off his {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} or leaves the course bounds, he will be disqualified.<ref>{{Cite|Ride the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} to compete for the best time while also going through the marked arches. If you get off your {{Color|BotW Red|shield or leave the course bounds}}, you're disqualified.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> Hitting an obstacle head-on with the {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, such as a rock in the sand, will cause {{Term|BotW|Link}} to fall off of his {{Term|BotW|Shield}}.
