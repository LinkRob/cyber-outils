The '''Ordonian Outfit''' is a type of {{Term|TPHD|Clothes|display=clothing|link}} {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} wears in {{TP}}.{{Name Ref|TPHD}}

==Location and Uses==
In the early portion of the game, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} wears the standard clothes of the {{Plural|TPHD|Ordonian|link}} of {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}. Most males of the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|display=Village}} wear a variation of this garb. There is nothing special about this outfit as it does not grant {{Term|TPHD|Link}} any extra abilities. 

Once the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Clothes|link}} are obtained, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} will not be seen wearing these clothes again, with a few exceptions: when sumo wrestling with {{Term|TPHD|Mayor Bo|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} wears the trousers from this outfit, and it also appears in various cutscenes. This is the only outfit of {{TP|-}} that does not include a hat, and becomes entirely replaced.
