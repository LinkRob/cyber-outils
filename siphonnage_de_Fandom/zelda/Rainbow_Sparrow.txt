{{Term/Store|Rainbow Sparrow|Rainbow Sparrows|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Rainbow Sparrow}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Rainbow Sparrow}}
Rainbow Sparrows are brightly colored birds, with more vivid coloring than their closer relatives.<ref>{{Cite|As the name suggests, their feathers are more colorful than other sparrows', which has made them a favorite among dilettantes.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Typical Rainbow Sparrows have purple wings, backs and tails, pink necks and tail tips, red heads, creamy throats and green bellies. They chiefly inhabit tropical forests within the [[Faron (Region)|Faron region]], such as the [[Faron Grasslands]].<ref>{{Cite|These small birds hail from the Faron region.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They also live in [[West Necluda]]. They are omnivores, and feed on a mixture of nuts and small insects.<ref>{{Cite|They feed on small rain-forest insects, but the omnivore in them loves nuts as well.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> A Rainbow Sparrow that's killed will drop a [[Chickaloo Tree Nut]].
