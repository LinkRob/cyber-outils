'''"Once Upon a Time Machine"''' is the twentieth episode of {{CN}}. It was first broadcast in the United States on November 17, 1990.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[Once Upon a Time Machine/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
[[File:Picnic.png|thumb|left|The heroes get ready to have a picnic]]
Kevin, Duke, Princess Lana, [[Link]], and [[Princess Zelda]] are getting ready for a picnic in [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=This is a great idea! To just kick back and have a picnic. Thanks for inviting us, Princess Zelda.|name=Kevin|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> However, their plans are interrupted when a swarm of [[Boon]]s begin to attack them.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Sounds like we have some uninvited guests... Boons!|name=Link|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> As the heroes are defeating all of the Boons, Count Gruemon and Doctor Gary are preparing to steal Kevin's Power Pad. When Kevin watches how Link defeats a Boon without a sword by just kicking it, he tries to copy him by taking off his belt with the Power Pad and Zapper and then kick the Boon, but he ends up tripping and not even kicking the bug.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Not bad! But I can top it. Even without this!|name=Kevin|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Kevin then turns around and sees that Count Gruemon has already taken his Power Pad and Zapper, who quickly runs to the inside of his time ship with Doctor Gary and flies off into a large warp hole. Kevin decides to go after them, and Link offers his help as the two heroes jump into the hole.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You're gonna need help. I'm going, too!|name=Link|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> They find themselves in an old west-like town, and after Kevin almost gets beaten up by a bull,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You callin' me weird, human?|name=Bull|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> he is saved by a cat named Pero. It is eventually revealed that Pero was sent back in time by Count Gruemon for saving a mouse from one of Gruemon's cats, and is still being persecuted for his crime.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Heh, that pig who stole your power-thingy sounds like Count Gruemon alright. The same swine who sent me back in time for saving a poor defenseless mouse from his hench cats.|name=Pero|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Pero then joins Kevin and Link on their quest since he has the Time Map that shows the warps to the different times in his video world that could help him get back to his own time.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It's a Time Map, showing the warps to the different times in my video world.|name=Pero|show=Captain N|episode=20}}</ref>

For most of the episode, Link, Kevin, and Pero travel through different eras of Pero's video game world as they are being persecuted by Count Gruemon and his two henchmen.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Then send that pesky cat and his humans to the bottom of the sea! Fire!|name=Count Gruemon|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Eventually the heroes reach the city of London, where they get on an airplane to help Pero reach another time warp to New York: the location of the time warp to his home.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ms. Liberty's Torch! The map says that's where I'll find my time warp home!|name=Pero|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> They land on the Statue of Libery's torch, which also happens to have Count Gruemon's time ship on the statue's crown.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=And Gruemon's time ship is parked right on her crown! Good work, Pero!|name=Kevin|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Link, Kevin and Pero confront Count Gruemon and quickly head to his time ship, but Doctor Gary calls a gigantic sea snake from the water depths.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Don't worry, my pet sea snake is hungry for supper!|name=Doctor Gary|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> As Pero is trying to make his way to the time warp on the statue's torch, the giant sea snake appears. Link begins to [[zap]] it with his sword to distract it meanwhile.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=My Hyrule weapon isn't doing much good! [?] I'll try to hold him off!|name=Link|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> However, before Pero can reach the time warp, Doctor Gary destroys it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=You're not going anywhere, my fine feline friend! Seal the time warm up, doctor!|name=Count Gruemon|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Pero quickly climbs down, pushes Link out of the way, and runs to the inside of Count Gruemon's time ship to begin to take off, which is where Kevin's Power Pad and Zapper were located.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The time ship is my only home ticket now!|name=Pero|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Kevin, angered that Pero was just looking out for himself, tries to fight off the sea snake by himself.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Power Pad or no Power Pad, I'm not letting that sea snake snack on my pals!|name=Kevin|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Pero then has a change of heart and throws Kevin his Power Pad and Zapper, allowing the young boy to defeat the sea snake.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I guess I'll never learn. First I save a mouse, then these guys.|name=Pero|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> Link, after witnessing Pero fly away, complains that he forgot to tell them how to get back to Hyrule, but just then, a part of Pero's Time Map falls down on Kevin's hands.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=But he forgot to show us how to get home to Hyrule!|name=Link|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref>

In Hyrule, Zelda and Lana had already called the rest of the N Team to go look for Link and Kevin.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ah, thanks for answering our call, fellas!|name=Princess Lana|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref> As Simon Belmont is looking for traces of the two heroes, he bumps into a Moblin. He is saved by Kevin, who defeats the creature without his Zapper since he does not want to solely depend on it.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=From now on, I'm keeping my options open.|name=Kevin|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=20}}</ref>
