{{Term/Store|Black Stalfos|Black Stalfos|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Black Stalfos|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|CoH|Black Stalfos}} are the strongest variety of {{Term|CoH|Stalfos|link}}. They move to the music on every other beat, crouching for one beat before hopping to an adjacent tile on the next beat. They always carry shields in order to protect them from frontal attacks, though they can be easily knocked off with any {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}}. They only appear in caves and dungeons, and can be summoned by a {{Term|CoH|Black Sarcophagus|link}}.
