"{{Term/Store|A Royal Recipe||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Royal Recipe}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Riverside Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Gotter|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}. There, {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} will explain that his ancestor was a chef for the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} and that he wishes to taste one of the {{Plural|BotW|Recipe|link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|display=Royal Family}} once enjoyed.<ref name="Cookbook">{{Cite|If you head north through Hyrule Field, you'll see the castle surrounded by that foul aura. It has long been a den of monsters, but in its glory days it was surely a thing of wondrous beauty. It was in that very castle in those very days that my ancestor... Hmm, my grandfather's grandfather, I believe... Anyway, he worked as the castle's appointed chef! I couldn't believe it myself! Oh, how I wonder what sorts of cuisine members of the royal court enjoyed in those good ol' days... As a child, I would often suffer meager meals, all while dreaming of the beautiful dishes that might have graced my plate. Oh, if only things had been different! Gourmet spiced meat, seafood curry, mushroom risotto, egg pudding... Oh, my, I think I'm salivating. And it's my pursuit of those childhood dreams that explains the fabulous body you see here! These days, I enjoy my time working at these stables, sharing my tales of beauty with any traveler willing to listen. But I still wonder what could have been. Anyway, excuse me... I seem to have caught myself prattling on. But looking upon your perfectly symmetrical face and discussing meals of beauty has rekindled my desire. If you should happen to set foot inside Hyrule Castle, could you find it in you to keep an eye out for a cookbook? There must be one lying around somewhere in there! I'm simply dying to finally sample the food of the royal court.|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Gotter}}, these can be found inside the {{Plural|BotW|Castle Library Book|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref name="Cookbook"/>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} reads either {{Term|BotW|Castle Library Book}} inside the [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)#Library|Library]], he can learn the {{Plural|BotW|Recipe}} for {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Royal Family's Secret Recipe #1<br/>The Princess's Favorite: {{Color|BotW Red|Fruitcake}}.<br/><br/>Boil any two (or more) pieces of fruit with Tabantha wheat and cane sugar for a good while.<br/><br/>Eating this dish will apparently clear your head and help you focus on things such as ancient technology research.|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Royal Family's Secret Recipe #2<br/>The Chancellor's Favorite: {{Color|BotW Red|Monster Cake}}<br/><br/>Stew monster extract, Tabantha wheat, cane sugar, and goat butter for a spell.<br/><br/>It's a dangerous dish that makes your head fuzzy and may even motivate you to plan evil schemes!|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} to inform him of the discovery, who will ask him to prepare the dish.<ref>{{Cite|You what?! You really discovered a royal cookbook?|Gotter|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Could I bother you further to prepare the dish for me? I must sample it!|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, it is not necessary to infiltrate Hyrule Castle and read either book in order to recreate the cakes. Regardless of whether the {{Plural|BotW|Castle Library Book}} have been read, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} with either a {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}} or a {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} will ask to try it. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has both cakes in his possession, the {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake}} will be prioritized. Upon receiving either {{Term|BotW|Cake|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} will eat it and describe their flavors.<ref>{{Cite|[Give him fruitcake.] This... This is the fruitcake said to be eaten by the princess of Hyrule...|Gotter|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Give him monster cake.] This monster cake is said to have been a favorite of the minister of Hyrule.|Gotter|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Simply...delicious... How could I possibly describe this experience? A refreshing aroma reminiscent of galloping across these majestic planes on horseback... No, that undersells the dish's complexity. It fills me with that heart-thumping intensity that seizes your very soul when you're spotted by a Guardian!|Gotter|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It tastes...beautiful... How can I put it into words? It's like biting into a Chuchu... No, no, that would be disgusting and dangerous. It stimulates the brain, much like being pummeled by a Hinox!|Gotter|BotW}}</ref>

Upon finally having experienced his dream of tasting one of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Recipe}}, {{Term|BotW|Gotter}} will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} and present him with a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A truly elegant dish! I never thought I would taste something quite so thrilling. I really must thank you for giving me such a beautiful meal! Here, take this. For your trouble.|Gotter|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
