{{Compendium/Extract|Ancient Bladesaw}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bladesaw}} can be made by providing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} with 15 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Screw|link}}, 5 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shaft|link}}, 2 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core|link}}, and 1,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Exchange for Ancient Bladesaw?|Ancient Oven|BotW}}</ref> They are made using {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, and their unique rotating blades give them a strong cutting power.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are considered {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}, an archetype of equipment created by {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>

Being made of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bladesaw}} will deal additional damage when used against {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}-type {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, are unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They do float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, however, and they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}.
