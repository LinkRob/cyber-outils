{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Crusher}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} favored by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Crusher}} are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}}. Though they are more of a lump of metal, they can be lethal in the hands of a {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are most commonly used by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Crusher}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. However, they are unable to be burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Additionally, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Crusher}} will sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
