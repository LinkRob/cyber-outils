The {{Term/Store|''Sheikah Sensor Notes''||Series, BotW}} are an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Sensor Notes|N/A|BOtW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Sheikah Sensor Notes}} are a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} written by {{Term|BotW|Symin|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Sensor Notes<br/>Author: Symin|Sheikah Sensor Notes|BotW}}</ref> and they're found on his desk in the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. They provide detailed instructions for using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor|link}} and its upgrade.
