The {{Term/Store|Shada Naw Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shada Naw Shrine}}<br/>The Red Giveaway}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Red Giveaway''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Shada Naw Shrine}} is located north of {{Term|BotW|Selmie's Spot|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shada Naw|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shada Naw. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Shada Naw|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Shada Naw Shrine features an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, three fans, a moving platform, a metal block, and a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} is activated by the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, it raises a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} up to {{Term|BotW|Shada Naw}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. The fans blow the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} from the stone platform over the moving platform, where it then rolls towards a ramp that leads to the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} will drop if the moving platform is not next to the wall of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} when it is blown that way. The fans will also blow the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} past the ramp, causing it to fall off the side if it is not stopped. Another metal block, hidden in the wall under the ramp, can be partially moved out of the wall.

In order to complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be standing on the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} when the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} activates the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be raised up to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, allowing him to speak to {{Term|BotW|Shada Naw}}.

{{Term|BotW|Shada Naw}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial, and they give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shada Naw|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shada Naw|BotW}}</ref>

In a small nook above one of the fans is a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Great Frostblade}}
