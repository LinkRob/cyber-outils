The {{Term/Store|Bomb Garden||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|🠉 Bomb Garden<br/>🠊 Eddo's Garage|Sign|PH}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|PH|Bomb Garden}} is located on {{Term|PH|Cannon Island|link}}. It is a fenced-in field of {{Plural|PH|Bomb Flower|link}} situated southwest of {{Term|PH|Eddo's Garage|link}}. {{Term|PH|Link}} must pass through the {{Term|PH|Bomb Garden}} in order to reach {{Term|PH|Eddo|link}} through the back door of his {{Term|PH|Eddo's Garage|display=Garage}} in order to obtain the {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}}.

South of the fences surrounding the {{Term|PH|Bomb Garden|display=Garden}} is a {{Term|PH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|PH|Red Rupee|link}}, valued at 20 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}.
