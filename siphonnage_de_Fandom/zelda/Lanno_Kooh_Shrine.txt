The {{Term/Store|Lanno Kooh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Lanno Kooh Shrine}}<br/>Lanno Kooh's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Lanno Kooh's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Lanno Kooh Shrine is located in the northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}, under a rock formation, downriver of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Headspring|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hebra Falls|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, Link can fell a tree and ride it downriver through the Hebra Headspring and Hebra Falls until it reaches the Shrine. Alternatively, Link can attempt to swim through the cold water of the river surrounding the Shrine without succumbing to the extreme cold.

As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Lanno Kooh|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Lanno Kooh. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Lanno Kooh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Lanno Kooh Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Lanno Kooh. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}. As Link approaches Lanno Kooh, he will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the Shrine,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Lanno Kooh|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Lanno Kooh|BotW}}</ref>
