==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Dark Tunic is a piece of [[Armor]] that can be purchased from [[Kilton]] at his [[Fang and Bone]] shop after all [[Divine Beast]]s have been freed. It cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], nor can it be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Dark Tunic, alongside the [[Dark Hood]] and [[Dark Trousers]], compose the [[Dark Series]]. When the full set is worn, it provides a Night Speed Up bonus. Also, characters get scared when they see [[Link]] wearing it.

The Dark Tunic design is a straight color-swap of the [[Tunic of Time]]. In the same fashion, the Dark Hood and Dark Trousers are modeled after the [[Cap of Time]] and the [[Trousers of Time]] respectively.
