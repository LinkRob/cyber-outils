"{{Term/Store|The Korok Trials||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Korok Trials}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Great Hyrule Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to the {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} elder, {{Term|BotW|Chio|link}}. He reveals that the {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} have come together to prepare a series of trials for him.<ref>{{Cite|Us Koroks all got together and prepared some {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} just for you, Mr. Hero!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed all of the trials, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} believes he will be able to pull the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}} from its {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link|display=Pedestal}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you pass the veeeery tough {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} we've prepared...pulling out the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}} will be a piece of cake! ...Probably!|Chio|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already pulled out the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} insists that the trials will help him get stronger.<ref>{{Cite|Although you may not need them, since you already pulled the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}} out and all... But these {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} will help you grow even stronger than you are now!|Chio|BotW}}</ref>}} There is also a tough trial designed by {{Term|BotW|Chio}} himself.<ref>{{Cite|Among them is an extra-tough one designed by me, the elder of {{Color|BotW Blue|Korok Forest}}.|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if wants to test himself in the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So will you test your mettle in the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trials}}?|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to do it, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} informs him that if keeps doing whatever people ask him to do, he is going to get conned eventually.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll do it!] Ya know, if you keep doing everything everyone asks of you without question, you're gonna get conned eventually...|Chio|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}}, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} will comment that he is ride.<ref>{{Cite|[Korok Trials?] Ah, you need to think about it first... Wise boy...|Chio|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Chio}} explains that, by visiting all of the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that are part of the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can be considered a true grown-up.<ref>{{Cite|Long story short—you need to visit all the shrines in the Korok Forest so that you can be considered {{Color|BotW Red|a real grown-up}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}} themselves are based on the those of the legendary hero, and every {{Term|BotW|Korok}} must undertake them.<ref>{{Cite|It's a ceremony that every Korok must undertake, and it's based on the trials of the legendary hero himself!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Chio}}, {{Term|BotW|Hestu|link}}, several other {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}, and even {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}} have completed the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}},<ref>{{Cite|I've done it...and so has Hestu...and him over there, and her too, and even the Great Deku Tree himself has done it!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> though {{Term|BotW|Chio}} retracts that last statement and admits it was a lie.<ref>{{Cite|OK...that last part was a lie. He's pretty rooted.|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Chio}} introduces the three {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}: the "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Second Sight|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial one!}} In the southwest forest: {{Color|BotW Red|Trial of Second Sight}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> "{{Term|BotW|The Lost Pilgrimage|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial two!}} In the northwest forest: {{Color|BotW Red|The Lost Pilgrimage}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> and "{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial three!}} In the eastern forest: {{Color|BotW Red|The Test of Wood}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Chio}} asserts that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can overcome anything if he is capable of completing the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you can overcome the Korok Trials, you can overcome anything!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} have also prepared prizes for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We've also prepared some wonderful prizes for you!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to show them what he is made of,<ref>{{Cite|Now show us what you're made of!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> before asking if he will make an attempt.<ref>{{Cite|Well? Will you do it?|Chio|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already completed all three {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Chio}},<ref>{{Cite|All right, do your be—st... You've already completed all of the {{Color|BotW Red|trials}}?!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> he will give him the prize immediately.<ref name="Treasured Item">{{Cite|Ah! I know that look. You want me to give you something since you visited all the places, don't you? ...I guess I have no choice. I'll give you my treasured item.|Chio|BotW}}</ref>}} When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed all three {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Chio}}, who will be amazed by his feats.<ref>{{Cite|*whistle*<br/>That's amazing! You're so cool!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Realizing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants a reward, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} will give him his prized possession, which is three {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}.<ref name="Treasured Item"/> He encourages eating {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} cooked with {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle}} to soothe aches and pains.<ref>{{Cite|Try eating a dish cooked with that {{Color|BotW Blue|big hearty truffle}}... It'll soothe all of your aches and pains and then some! I'm not pulling your branch here! Just try it for yourself!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
