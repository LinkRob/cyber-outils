The {{Term/Store|Phantom Sword||Series, PH}} is the only weapon that can harm [[Bellum]] and [[Phantom]]s in {{PH}}. It is composed of the {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)|link}} and a blade forged from three types of [[Pure Metal]]s: Crimsonine, Azurine, and Aquanine.<ref>{{Cite|The Phantom Sword is no ordinary sword... When forged, normal metal can never handle that kind of sacred power! Aquanine... Azurine... And Crimsonine... These three pure metals must be forged together to create the Phantom Sword.|Zauz|PH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
[[File:Oshus Phantom Sword.png|thumb|left|Oshus fuses the Phantom Hourglass with the blade of the Phantom Sword]]
After rescuing {{Term|PH|Tetra|link}} from the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} is told by {{Term|PH|Oshus|link}} to find the three kinds of {{Plural|PH|Pure Metal|link}} and take them to the legendary blacksmith {{Term|PH|Zauz|link}} to forge the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword|link}} in order to defeat {{Term|PH|Bellum|link}}. The {{Plural|PH|Pure Metal}} include {{Term|PH|Crimsonine|link}}, found in the {{Term|PH|Goron Temple|link}}, {{Term|PH|Azurine|link}}, found in the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}}, and {{Term|PH|Aquanine|link}}, found in {{Term|PH|Mutoh's Temple|link}}. After {{Term|PH|Zauz}} forges the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword}} with the three {{Plural|PH|Pure Metal}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} must complete the blade by returning to {{Term|PH|Oshus}}, who will fuse the {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)|link}} with the blade itself.<ref>{{Cite|Rare metals and mystical sands are fused together. And so the Phantom Sword is born. With that, you can defeat Bellum.|Oshus|PH}}</ref> By doing so, the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword}} gains the ability to control time for a short period.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm. I cannot call it truly complete without a handle. Take this blade to the Ocean King. He will add the Sand of Hours. Only he can empower the Phantom Sword with the ability to control time.|Zauz|PH}}</ref>

The {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword}} is the only weapon capable of slaying the {{Plural|PH|Phantom|link}} that patrol the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}}, and thus, the only weapon capable of defeating {{Term|PH|Bellum}}. Since the {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)}} is incorporated into the hilt, it makes the wielder immune to the life-draining curse of the temple. In combination with a {{Term|PH|Phantom Sphere|link}}, it can stop time temporarily. {{Term|PH|Link}} uses this ability in his battle with {{Term|PH|Bellum}}, using it to freeze time so he can get behind {{Term|PH|Bellum}} and attack his eye.

When {{Term|PH|Link}} goes to talk to {{Term|PH|Zauz}} about the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword}}, the blacksmith explains that his ancestors defended {{Term|PH|Ocean King}} in an age when great evil lurked the sea, and in order to combat those monsters, his people would forge their own weapons.<ref>{{Cite|You see, for generations, our ancestors lived to serve the Ocean King. They defended the Ocean King in an age when great evil lurked in the sea. My people forged our own weapons and fought in epic battles. You seek one of those great weapons. An artifact called the Phantom Sword.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> One of these weapons was the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword}} itself, and {{Term|PH|Zauz}} even mentions that he had one, although he lost it at some point.<ref>{{Cite|I no longer have it...|Zauz|PH}}</ref> Thus, it is implied that {{Term|PH|Zauz}}'s people forged many {{Plural|PH|Phantom Sword}} for battle, and that perhaps the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword}} is not the only weapon that can vanquish {{Term|PH|Bellum}} and the {{Plural|PH|Phantom}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{PH|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
During the {{PH|Himekawa}}, Link is told by Grandpa Oshus that the blacksmith Zauz can forge a weapon to defeat Bellum.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Visit the blacksmith Zauz, who lives in the Northwestern sea. He will teach you how to defeat Bellum.|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 133}}</ref> Although, when [[Linebeck]] is captured by Bellum, [[Jolene]] is sent to Zauz instead.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Jolene, I need to ask you a favor... Can you find Zauz the Blacksmith and ask how to defeat Bellum?|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 155, 156}}</ref> When Zauz has completed the Phantom Sword, Jolene brings the weapon to Link.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Sorry to make you wait. Here, Link! The only weapon that can defeat Bellum... The Phantom Sword!|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 179}}</ref> Link uses the Phantom Sword to defeat Bellum once and for all.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}}, the Phantom Sword appears as the second tier of [[Toon Link]]'s [[Light Sword]] weapon paired with the [[Shield of Antiquity]] from {{ST}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The Phantom Sword resembles the [[Master Sword]] in many ways - the major differences between them are the symbol of the [[Oshus|Ocean King]] appearing in place of the Triforce emblem on the blade as well as the cross-guard featuring a different design, in which the Master Sword's yellow jewels are replaced by the Phantom Hourglass.
**The music that plays when Link receives the Phantom Sword is also the same as that of the Master Sword.
*Despite the fact that this sword is the second sword in the game, it's no stronger than [[Oshus's Sword]]. As such, Link must use [[Leaf]] to increase the power of his sword.
