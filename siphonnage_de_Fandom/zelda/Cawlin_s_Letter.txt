{{Term/Store|Cawlin's Letter||Series, SS}} is an item in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Cawlin's Letter is a love letter written by [[Cawlin]] to [[Karane]]. If [[Link]] walks near the restroom in the [[Knight Academy]] at night, he will hear a mysterious moaning.<ref>{{Cite|Oooooh... Somebody... Please, somebody help me...|Phoeni|SS}}</ref> If he tries to open the door, someone inside will ask for paper.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh... {{Color|SS Red|Paper}}... Please... Somebody bring me {{Color|SS Red|paper}}... {{Color|SS Red|Bring me some paper}}... {{Color|SS Red|Any kind of paper at all}}... Ooooh...|Phoeni|SS}}</ref> Later, Link can talk to Cawlin near the restroom to receive his letter, which he must deliver to Karane.<ref>{{Cite|So listen up... Your mission is to take this piece of {{Color|SS Red|paper}} to a certain person! It's a very important piece of {{Color|SS Red|paper}}! Make SURE you deliver it.|Cawlin|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So listen up! The person you must give it to is a girl... A senior student... Her name is {{Color|SS Blue|Karane}}.|Cawlin|SS}}</ref> If Link looks towards the restroom, Cawlin will reprimand him for thinking of using it as toilet paper.<ref>{{Cite|You fool! How dare you! This is no ordinary piece of paper! It's a {{Color|SS Red|letter}}! A very special one at that! I put my heart into every word... I guess you could say it's sort of like a... All right, fine! It's a love letter! And that is why you will not, under any circumstances, EVER allow it to be used as toilet paper! Do you hear me?!|Cawlin|SS}}</ref>

If Link delivers the letter to Karane, she will jokingly consider going out with Cawlin and then wonder what [[Pipit]] thinks.<ref>{{Cite|I have to say, he really put his heart into it and everything... I don't know... Maybe I should consider going out with {{Color|SS Blue|Cawlin}}. ...Hah! As if! Sigh... I wonder what {{Color|SS Blue|Pipit}} would think if he knew about this...|Karane|SS}}</ref> Following this, if Link tells Pipit about the letter, he will interrupt a meeting between Karane and Cawlin and proclaim his love for her.<ref>{{Cite|Wait! I think you should go out with me instead! I mean...er...I want to ask you out!|Pipit|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When you told me about the letter, it was at that moment I finally realized. I realized that...I love her. I thought to myself, "I can't let anyone else take her from me!"|Pipit|SS}}</ref> Afterwards, Karane accepts his love, causing Cawlin to run away crying. Pipit then gives Link five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s for helping them get together.<ref>{{Cite|If you hadn't told me, I might never have even noticed my own feelings for her. I'm so grateful. I don't know how I can ever thank you!|Pipit|SS}}</ref> Cawlin can later be found crying in [[Groose]]'s room.

Link can alternatively give the letter to [[Phoeni]]. Afterwards, she can be found in Groose's room, stroking Cawlin while he sleeps, having been moved by the letter he wrote.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... You're the one from the other day... It's good to see you again... Actually, thanks to you, I met my true love... Yes, him... It all started with that letter that you gave me... Those beautiful words of affection caused me to fall hopelessly in love... Ahh, my heart is bursting with thoughts of him... I feel so excited, so cheerful, so full of life...|Phoeni|SS}}</ref> She will then give Link five Gratitude Crystals for helping her fall in love with Cawlin.
