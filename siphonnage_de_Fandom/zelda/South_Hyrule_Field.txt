==Features and Overview==
{{Map:South Hyrule Field}}
{{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field}} is located in southern {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to three other areas: the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}} to the east, {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} to the north, and the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}} to the west.

{{Term|TMC|Link's House|link}} lies in the center of {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field}}. {{Term|TMC|Tingle|link}} can also be found on a ledge to the east. After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}} appears west of {{Term|TMC|Link's House}} that leads to the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}}.
