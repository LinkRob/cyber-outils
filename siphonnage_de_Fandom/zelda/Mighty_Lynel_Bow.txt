{{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, though, {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}} use {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Bow}} instead of their Blue-Maned counterparts. They will become rare as {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} tier up, but the {{Term|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel}} in the {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} will always remain the same. Therefore, it can become the only source of {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Bow}} later in the game. {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Bow}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}}, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

The bowstrings of {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Bow}} are noted to be made of a metal so tough that regular {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} have trouble drawing them.<ref name="Inventory"/> When they are drawn, however, they can fire three arrows at once. Since they are made of metal, they conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Bow}} also sink if they are dropped in water, but can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, they do not burn if when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} do.
