{{Term/Store|Wortsworth||Series, TotK}} is a character in {{TotK}}. He gives Link the [["Messages from an Ancient Era"]] quest, in which he translates 13 Ancient Hyrulean [[Stone Tablet#Tears of the Kingdom|Stone Tablets]] written by the [[High Maiden]] of [[King Rauru]] and [[Queen Sonia]], whenever Link shows him a picture of one taken with his [[Purah Pad|Purah Pad's]] [[Camera|Camera Rune]].

==Biography==
Wortsworth is a surveyor who lives in [[Lookout Landing]] and is a member of the [[Zonai Survey Team]]. After the Great Hyrule Sky Stone Tablet falls into [[Lookout Landing]], Wortsworth will move to [[Kakariko Village]] to translate the other 12 tablets.
