==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Shock Arrow}}
{{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} which can be used with any {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}. While they most commonly appear in singles, they can also be found in bundles.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Shock Arrow x5}}<br/>A bundle of five shock arrows.<br/>Shock arrows break apart on impact, channeling electricity into nearby objects. Very effective against metals and moisture.|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Shock Arrow x10}}<br/>A bundle of 10 shock arrows.<br/>Shock arrows break apart on impact, channeling electricity into nearby objects. Very effective against metals and moisture.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow}} immediately dissipate into a burst of electricity after impact.<ref name="Inventory"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} or an enemy is shot by a {{Term|BotW|Shock Arrow}}, they are electrocuted and stunned for a short period of time. Therefore, they're noted to be effective against wet opponents and metal.<ref name="Inventory"/> In addition, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is using any equipped items while shocked, they are dropped to the ground. They can be dropped by archer [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemies]] or found inside {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}.

The following locations sell Shock Arrows:
* The [[Arrow Specialty Shop]] in [[Gerudo Town]]
* The [[General Shoppe]] in [[Korok Forest]]
* The [[Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)]]
* [[Shaillu]]'s arrow store in [[Kara Kara Bazaar]]
* The fish market in [[Lurelin Village]]
* [[Jini]] at the [[Mounted Archery Camp]] (only during rain)

{{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow}} deal an additional 20 damage to the attack power of the {{Term|BotW|Bow}} being used. When shot at a body of water, they create a damaging area of effect around where the {{Term|BotW|Shock Arrow}}'s point of impact. {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow}} also inflict extra damage when used in the {{Term|BotW|Rain}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you use a {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrow}} when it's raining, it does extra damage.|Daz|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} can sometimes be found using them. {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow}} can send surges of electricity through conductive objects, such as metal crates and metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} are known to be susceptible to damage from Shock Arrows.<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, as an aquatic race, we Zora are terribly vulnerable to electricity.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> [[Seggin]], however, is noted to be more resistant to them than the average Zora, and is even able to use them.<ref>{{Cite|Seggin, who is quite shock resistant for a Zora, hit one with a {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrow}}. Sure enough, it slowed the water a bit.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref>

In order to complete the [[Main Quest]] "[[Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|Divine Beast Vah Ruta]], Link must use Shock Arrows when he faces the [[Divine Beast Vah Ruta|Divine Beast of the same name]] with [[Sidon]].<ref>{{Cite|We need you to use {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}} to get those orbs working properly again!|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> Muzu received the task of locating the Shock Arrows, but he will leave before telling anyone where they are.<ref>{{Cite|I tased Muzu with finding the {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}} we will need to appease {{Color|BotW Blue|Vah Ruta}}.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> Hearing that [[Mipha]] was in love with Link, Muzu refuses to reveal their location unless he can prove it.<ref>{{Cite|If you are truly Lady Mipha's beloved, then prove it! Do so and I will tell you how to get those {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}, as well as anything else you wish to know.|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> Once he's convinced, he'll reveal that the arrows can be found in the territory of the {{Term|BotW|Lynel|link}} on [[Ploymus Mountain]].<ref>{{Cite|That tall mountain over yonger... It is called {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}, and there you will also find {{Color|BotW Blue|Shatterback Point}}. A terrifying creature has made its home up there. This awful beast shoots volley after volley of {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}. Even a single one could be fatal to a Zora.|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> He estimates that Link will require at least twenty to face the beast.<ref>{{Cite|In order to appease the Divine Beast, I estimate that you will need...hmm... {{Color|BotW Red|at least 20}} {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}.|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> After they've been collected, Link can move on to the battle with Divine Beast Vah Ruta.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Shock arrows as a unique arrow type, have been removed from [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the kingdom]], to make way for the addition of [[Fuse]], which allows players to make their own Shock arrows, by Fusing a Arrow with a [[Shock Fruit]] or any other Elemental material. Another easy source of Shock/Lightning Arrows is with [[Key_Item#Tears_of_the_Kingdom|Vow of Riju]], which allows 1 Lightning Based Arrow every 10 seconds.
