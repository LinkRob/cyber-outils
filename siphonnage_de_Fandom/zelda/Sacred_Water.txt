{{Term/Store|Sacred Water|Sacred Waters|Series, SS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 137 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
Sacred Water is pure water found only at the [[Skyview Spring]]. After being attacked by [[Ghirahim]],<ref>{{Cite|Shortly before your arrival, I was attacked by a pompous creature calling himself {{Color|SS Blue|Ghirahim}}.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]] must heal in a [[Water Basin|basin]] of Sacred Water. When [[Link]] first encounters Faron, she is in need of another dose of Sacred Water in order to fully recover. Link must then bring a [[Bottle]] of Sacred Water to Faron,<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, the healing properties of the sacred water have diminished, and I am now in need of another dose to freshen up my healing bath. {{Color|SS Red|Bring me the container of fresh sacred water}} that I require.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> restoring her powerful true form, and thus opening the way to the [[Ancient Cistern]]. When embarking on this task, new and more powerful enemies appear within the [[Skyview Temple]], with one door been resealed by enemies, forcing Link to find the [[Key]] again. In order to access the Skyview Spring, Link must defeat a trio of [[Stalfos]] in the boss chamber.
