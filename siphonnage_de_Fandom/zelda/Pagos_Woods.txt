The {{Term/Store|Pagos Woods||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Pagos Woods'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Pagos Woods are located in the [[Faron Grasslands]].

[[Kass]] can be found here in search of the origin for an [[Ancient Song]]. If [[Link]] listens to Kass's Song, he will receive the [[Shrine Quest]], "[[The Serpent's Jaws]]".
