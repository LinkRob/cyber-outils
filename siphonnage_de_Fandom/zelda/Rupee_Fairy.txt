{{Term/Store|Rupee Fairy|Rupee Fairies|Series, HW|plural}} are items found in {{HW}}.<ref name="Tutorial">{{Cite|If you capture a Rupee Fairy, enemies will start dropping a higher number of Rupees for a limited amount of time.|Tutorial|HW}}</ref>

==Locations and Uses==
Rupee Fairies are dropped by [[Fairy Hunter]]s in the ''"Win the Rupee Competition"'' challenge battles found in  the [[Termina Map]], [[Master Wind Waker Map]], and the [[Grand Travels Map]] in [[Adventure Mode]]. When a Warrior collects a Rupee Fairy, enemies defeated by the Warrior will drop more [[Rupee]]s for one minute.<ref name="Tutorial"/> If the Fairy Hunter is defeated by [[Rogue Force]]s, the Rogue Forces will collect the Rupee Fairy and their [[Morale]] will increase for two minutes.
