==Features and Overview==
Manbo's Pond is the "private resort" of {{Term|LANS|Manbo|link}} the {{Term|LANS|Fish|link}}. [[Link]] may [[Warping|Warp]] directly to the pond once he's obtained the [[Ocarina]], by playing "[[Manbo's Mambo]]" while standing in the overworld.

The pond itself is not noteworthy, save for a nearby shop owned by [[Crazy Tracy]]. The shop offers a free health refill, and the [[Potion#Secret Medicine|only potion]] Link can buy in {{LA|-}}. She charges 28 [[Rupee]]s for the first dose, and then 42 Rupees for each subsequent application. She will also refill all of Link's [[Heart]]s to the maximum, as a courtesy.
