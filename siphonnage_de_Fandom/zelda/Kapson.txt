{{Term/Store|Kapson||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kapson|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kapson}} is an elderly {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} and a retired priest of {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|May the kindness of Goddess Hylia be ever with you...|Kapson|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Retired">{{Cite|Link... I have retired from my role as a priest. I am now living my life free from the material concerns of this world.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> He can initially be found inside the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But back in the domain, there is {{Color|BotW Red|Kapson}}, who was a priest in the past. {{Color|BotW Red|Kapson}} should be in the {{Color|BotW Red|sleeping chamber}}. Why don't you go visit him?|Gaddison|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kapson}}, he will be happy to see a traveler and begin to welcome him to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, well! It is always nice to have a visitor here. Thank you for journeying to our fair {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}...|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> He recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}} after a moment, at which point he expresses anger at him for the death of {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... Wait a moment... You... You're Link! The perpetrator who forced Lady Mipha to join his crusade 100 years ago, only to be destroyed by {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}! As though I could ever forget that face.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that many of the elderly {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} still remember {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}, and as a result, they hate him for what happened to her.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that the elderly here who loved Lady Mipha still remember... and they still resent you.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} claims to hate {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s actions, not {{Term|BotW|Link}} himself, and he believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can be forgiven.<ref>{{Cite|I, however, resent the deed and not the person. I also believe in allowing the mistakes of the past to wash away.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kapson}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he has retired from his role as a priest.<ref name="Retired"/> Ever since his retirement, {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} has found that his life has become boring.<ref>{{Cite|However... Ever since I retired, each day is more boring than the last... Spending day after day in idleness, only to pass from this world soon...|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> He worries that he will soon pass away.<ref>{{Cite|Spending day after day in idleness, only to pass from this world soon... Link... As I'm sure you are well aware, the afterlife lays claim to all. We never know when it will beckon.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to introduce him to any engaged couples he meets, as he would like to officiate one more wedding.<ref>{{Cite|If during your travels you meet any {{Color|BotW Red|engaged couples}}, I would like you to introduce them to me. Joining two souls is a true honor, one I shall stake my life on. Just one more wedding...and I shall be fulfilled.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find a priest to officiate his wedding among the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite|They want to hold a ceremony, so he wants you to find a {{Color|BotW Red|Zora}} who can officiate. Even though this Zora technically won't be working for Hudson's company, he would be much more comfortable if the {{Color|BotW Red|Zora's name ended in "son."}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can then return to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} to inform {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} of this opportunity.<ref>{{Cite|[Get a load of this...] I have been waiting to hear those words. Now, please tell me the specifics...|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} is not far from their current location, {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} decides to leave for {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} right away.<ref>{{Cite|As for the location... Hmmm... {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}} in {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}}... {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} isn't too far from here. It would please me to hurry there right away.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> He is eager to be able to work again.<ref>{{Cite|No need to worry about me. I have always had confidence in my fins and feet! It has been a long while since I have had work. I am eager to put my skills to good use!|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> He invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come visit him once he is settled in.<ref>{{Cite|You should come as well. I will start my preparations in {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}} in the meantime.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> At this point, he then departs from {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.

{{Term|BotW|Kapson}} will officiate {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} and {{Term|BotW|Rhondson|link}}'s wedding in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|*cough cough* *ahem* Silence please, everyone. We shall now begin. We are gathered here today to join Hudson and Rhondson in matrimony.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> Once the ceremony begins, {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} asks {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} if he is willing to take {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}} as his wife,<ref>{{Cite|Hudson... Before the eyes of these witnesses and before those of Goddess Hylia... Do you take Rhondson to be your wife, to have and to hold, in good times and in bad?|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} agrees to.<ref>{{Cite|I do.|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> Next, {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} asks the same question of {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}}, with the added stipulation that she name their children in accordancw with the {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction|link}} naming guidelines.<ref>{{Cite|Excellent. And Rhondson... Before the eyes of these witnesses and before those of Goddess Hylia... Do you take Hudson as your lawfully wedded husband, in sickness and in health? And do you also vow to...name your children in accordance with the Bolson Construction naming guidelines?|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}} objects, claiming that these are not traditional vows.<ref>{{Cite|Wait a moment... Those vows are not traditional by anyone's standards!|Rhondson|BotW}}</ref> After being convinced, {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}} will take the vow.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Well, those are the guidelines...}} I do.|Rhondson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} then asks the gathered crowd to help him greet the newly married couple.<ref>{{Cite|Excellent. {{Big|*cough cough*}} And so, dearly beloved, please help me in greeting this newly married couple.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> He wishes them happiness as they begin their lives together.<ref>{{Cite|I wish this new couple nothing but the utmost happiness as they set out on this journey of marital bliss!|Kapson|BotW}}</ref>

Once he arrives in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} will take charge of its {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link... Are you in need of a good sleep and a hearty meal?|Kapson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, humble servant led here by destiny... I can provide you with one meal and some lodging. Come and speak with me at the counter.|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kapson}}'s {{Term|BotW|Inn}} does not charge any {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} for a stay and does not offer any superior bedding options, though he will wake {{Term|BotW|Link}} up at his preferredtime of day.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] I thought so. Thanks to the benevolence of the Goddess Hylia, I can offer you a place to stay. When would you like me to wake you?|Kapson|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kapson}} while atop any of the furniture in his {{Term|BotW|Inn}}, he will pray to {{Term|BotW|Hylia}} that she smites him for his insolence.<ref>{{Cite|Ohmmm... Goddess Hyliaaaa... Please cast your divine judgment down upon the humble servant who speaks to me from atop my furniture...|Kapson|BotW}}</ref>
