"{{Term/Store|Slumbering Power||Series, BotW}}" is the thirteenth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Slumbering Power}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #13}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Memory}} is unlocked at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}}, located within {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}. In it, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} stands with his back to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}, guarding her as she stands in the water of the spring. {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} prays to the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}, saying she comes seeking help regarding her sealing power.<ref>{{Cite|I come seeking help... regarding this power that has been handed down over time...|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She asserts that prayer will awaken her power, but her words turn sour as she states this is what she's been told her whole life.<ref>{{Cite|Prayer will awaken my power to seal Ganon away... Or so I've been told all my life...|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} sighs. She claims that her grandmother heard voices from the spirit realm, and she recounts that her mother, the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|link}}, told her she would develop the power.<ref>{{Cite|Grandmother heard them—the voices from the spirit realm. And Mother said her own power would develop within me.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} laments that she has yet to unlock her power.<ref>{{Cite|But I don't hear...or feel anything!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She recounts how her father, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}}, tells her to stop wasting time trying to be a scholar.<ref>{{Cite|Father has told me time and time again... He always says, "Quit wasting your time playing at being a scholar!"|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

With bitterness, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} curses {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}}, throwing her hands down in the water.<ref>{{Cite|Curse you...|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Despite spending every day of her life praying and pleading to ancient spirits, she has never received a sign from any holy powers.<ref>{{Cite|I've spent every day of my life dedicated to praying! I've pleaded to the spirits tied to the ancient gods... And still the holy powers have proven deaf to my devotion.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> As the memory ends, she asks {{Term|BotW|Hylia}} to tell her what is wrong with her,<ref>{{Cite|Please just tell me... What is it...? What's wrong with me?!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> causing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to turn back towards her.
