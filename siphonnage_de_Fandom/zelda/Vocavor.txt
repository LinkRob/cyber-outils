'''Vocavor''' is the boss of the [[Jabul Ruins]] in {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
Vocavor is the boss of the [[Jabul Ruins]], appearing as a massive aquatic monster somewhat resembling a cross between a mosura fentoni and a shark. It attacks by creating powerful [[Whirlpool|whirlpools]] and striking with its armored body, though the growths on its back are vulnerable. As the battle continues, Vocavor summons larger whirlpools and grows more dangerous. After defeating it, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] frees the captured [[Tris]] and receives a [[Heart Container]].
