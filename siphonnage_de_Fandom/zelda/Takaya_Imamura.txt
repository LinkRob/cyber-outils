{{Infobox Staff|image=File:Takaya Imamura.webp|caption=|position=Art Director, Object Designer|gender=Male|birthday=April 10, 1966|birthplace=Takarazuka, Hyogo, Japan}}'''Takaya Imamura''' is a video game designer, manga artist and educator, who worked at [[Nintendo]] from 1989 to 2021. 

He worked on [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|''The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask'']] as the Art Director, and assisted the development of [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|''The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past'']] as an Object Designer.

Within the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda's serie'']], he's especially the one who created the character of [[Tingle]] and designed the [[Moon]] and the [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]] from ''The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask''.
