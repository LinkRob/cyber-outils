The {{Term/Store|Kolomo Garrison Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kolomo Garrison Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Kolomo Garrison Ruins are located in [[Hyrule Field]]. They sit on a small plateau that juts into the neighboring [[Lake Kolomo]].

If [[The Master Trials]] DLC pack is installed, a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing {{MM|Item}} can be found buried in a pile of debris at the southern corner of the Ruins as part of the "[[EX Treasure: Ancient Mask]]" [[Side Quest]].

A [[Korok]] can be found by lifting a [[Rock]] hidden under a pile of leaves in the Garrison Ruins.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
