{{Term/Store|Broom|Brooms|Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} are recurring items and objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 119">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Broom}} go unnamed in {{ALttP}}, {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, {{OoS}}, {{OoA}}, {{ALBW}}, and {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The {{Term|ALttP|Sweeping Lady|link}} is seen using a {{Term|ALttP|Broom}} in front of her house in {{ALttP}}. In the Game Boy Advance version of {{ALttP|-}}, the {{Term|ALttP|Apprentice|link}} can be seen holding a {{Term|ALttP|Broom}}.

==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LANS|Broom}} is the tenth item in the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}} in {{LA}}. It is received from {{Term|LANS|Mr. Write|link}} as thanks for delivering him {{Term|LANS|Christine|link}}'s {{Term|LANS|Goat's Letter|link}}.<ref name="E 119"/> The {{Term|LANS|Broom}} can then be given to {{Term|LANS|Grandma Ulrira|link}} in {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}} to replace the old {{Term|LANS|Broom}} she wore down from overuse.<ref>{{Cite|YAHOO! I worked too hard and now my broom is worn to the handle!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|YAHOO! YAHOO! A new broom?! For me? It is, isn't it?!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> In exchange, she gives {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} the {{Term|LANS|Fishing Hook|link}} she found while sweeping near the river bank.<ref>{{Cite|Okay! In return you can have this fishing hook I found when I swept by the river bank!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Term|OoT3D|Kotake|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Koume|link}} are seen flying on {{Plural|OoT3D|Broom}} in {{OoT}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Term|MM3D|Kotake}} and {{Term|MM3D|Koume}} are seen flying on {{Plural|MM3D|Broom}} in {{MM}}.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Term|OoS|Kotake}}, {{Term|OoS|Koume}}, and {{Term|OoS|Maple}} are seen flying on {{Plural|OoS|Broom}} in {{OoS}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Term|OoA|Kotake}}, {{Term|OoA|Koume}}, and {{Term|OoA|Maple}} are seen flying on {{Plural|OoA|Broom}} in {{OoA}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Term|ALBW|Irene|link}} is seen flying on a {{Term|ALBW|Broom}} in {{ALBW}}. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can call {{Term|ALBW|Irene}} by ringing the {{Term|ALBW|Bell|link}}. He can then hang on to {{Term|ALBW|Irene}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Broom}} to be transported to any activated {{Term|ALBW|Weather Vane|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Ivee|link}} can be seen sweeping with a {{Term|BotW|Broom}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|East Wind|link}} shop in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} in {{BotW}}.
