The {{Term/Store|Deku Temple||Series, FPTRR}} is the third [[Dungeon]] in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Entrance to the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Temple|display=Temple}}==
The {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Temple}} is found in the [[Deku Forest (First Continent)|Deku Forest]] on the [[First Continent]], and is actually the interior of the [[Great Deku Tree|Deku Tree]]. [[Tingle]] ventures in to the temple to find the [[Life Dew]], which will cure the Deku Tree's sapling. It requires 1500 [[Rupee]]s to open the main gate.

==Themes and Navigation==
There are three {{Plural|FPTRR|Sub-Boss|link}}, each found in their own rooms. This dungeon's main boss is [[Bana Bana]], a three-headed carnivorous plants who is protecting the water that Tingle needs to obtain.
