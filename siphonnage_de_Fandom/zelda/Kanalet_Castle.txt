{{Term/Store|Kanalet Castle||Series, LA, LADX}} is a mini-dungeon in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 234 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Kanalet Castle|author= |published= |retrieved= September 3, 2019|url= https://www.zelda.com/links-awakening/koholint-island/|title= Koholint Island – The Legend of Zelda ™: Link’s Awakening game for the Nintendo Switch™ system – Explore the island!|site= Zelda.com|type= }}</ref> It is located near the center of {{Term|LADX|Koholint Island|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LADX|Kanalet Castle}} was once owned by {{Term|LADX|Prince Richard|link}}, but rebellious servants kicked him out; now {{Term|LADX|Prince Richard}} is only interested in retrieving the {{Plural|LADX|Golden Leaf|link}} he left behind.<ref>{{Cite|Salutations! You wouldn't know by the look of me, but I used to live in the castle! My servants went berserk and I was forced to flee to my villa... So, you want the key to Ukuku Prairie, do you? I may be able to help you... Let's make... a deal, shall we? I want you to retrieve the Golden Leaf I left behind in the castle when I fled...|Prince Richard|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Prince Richard}} gets {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} to acquire these {{Plural|LADX|Golden Leaf}} by defeating monsters and solving puzzles. However, the front gate of {{Term|LADX|Kanalet Castle}} is up and thus impassable. {{Term|LADX|Link}} must give {{Plural|LADX|Banana|link}} to {{Term|LADX|Kiki|link}} to make a bridge across the moat. He can then enter via an underground passage under the castle walls. Once within {{Term|LADX|Kanalet Castle}}, {{Term|LADX|Link}} can press a {{Term|LADX|Floor Switch|link}} to open the gate, allowing an easy escape and access if he wishes to return.<ref>{{Cite|Hunh? It sounds like the castle gate opened! You can easily leave the castle now!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> In order to get each Golden Leaf, Link must battle a Mad Bomber to claim the first leaf, and next, Link must scare a Crow outside of the castle in order to defeat it and claim the second leaf. For the third of them, Link must enter Kanalet Castle and do battle with two Darknuts and a Zol. After they are defeated, Link can go and use a bomb to destroy a cracked wall, revealing a Darknut that has the fourth leaf. The {{Term|LADX|Boss|link}} of this mini-dungeon is a {{Term|LADX|Ball and Chain Soldier|link}}, who battles with {{Term|LADX|Link}} for the final {{Term|LADX|Golden Leaf}}.

In the southwest corner of {{Term|LADX|Kanalet Castle}}'s moat, {{Term|LADX|Link}} can dive and find a {{Term|LADX|Piece of Heart|link}}.
