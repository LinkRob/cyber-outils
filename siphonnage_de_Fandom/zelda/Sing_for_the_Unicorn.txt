'''"Sing for the Unicorn"''' is the fifth episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}} to be broadcast, but the second in production order. It first aired in the United States and Canada on October 6, 1989.

==Plot==
:''See also: [[Sing for the Unicorn/Episode transcript|Episode transcript]]''
It is evening in {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule|link}}. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian|link}} is enjoying the night air while {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} tries to swing in to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}}'s room with a little help from the king.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Uh...I was just...gonna swing in through your daughter's window and try to get her to kiss me.|name= Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Not a chance. [...] No. You need to take flowers, THEN you might succeed. Yes. Girls are crazy about flowers.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> When he lands in her room, the princess is impressed by the flowers that the young hero brought and Zelda offers him a big kiss, which {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} gladly accepts.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Flowers? For me? Aw, how sweet! You're sweet, Link. Clumsy, but sweet. So for you, a biiig kiss!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> However, Zelda then hears {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian}}'s cry for help and rushes off to her window to see that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}, who is riding on the back of a mysterious {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn|display=flying unicorn|link}}, has begun his assault on the castle and is after the king.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ganon's after daddy!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} launches himself over to where {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} and the King are, but he is promptly attacked by {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Vire|link}}. By the time the young hero finishes them off with his {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Crissword|link}} and {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} has already begun to fly away,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Farewell, hero!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> but {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} jumps off and manages to grab onto the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}}'s tail. Just then, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} zaps {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, who loses his grip and begins to fall to his death. Before giving up hope, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} spots four {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Vire}} below and retrains his fall by grabbing two of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Vire}}' feet. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} zaps him again, causing {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to fall even further to his death, but survives with Zelda's help. 
[[File:Sing (Sing for the Unicorn).png|thumb|right|A mysterious warrior attacks {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda]]
In the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} imprisons the King in a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|section=Pit Room|display=room with a slowly-disappearing floor|link}} as punishment until Zelda turns {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule}} and the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Triforce|link}} of Wisdom over to him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Now, King Harkinian is my prisoner, and soon, Hyrule will be all mine! Take him to the pit room. [...] What they had better do is turn the kingdom and Triforce over to me in one hour...or you'll be falling for a long, long time!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Meanwhile, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda enter the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}} but are attacked by {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Armos|link}} when the princess accidentally touches one of the statues.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Now stay quiet and don't touch the statues. [...] THAT'S why!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> The heroes defeat the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Armos}} with the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zelda's Bow|display=bow|link}} but a trap door opens beneath them and plunges them deeper into the dungeon where {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos|link}} await them.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Stalfos!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} easily zaps several of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}}, but one of them sneaks up from behind and knocks his sword off when more {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} ambush the young hero.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Princess, help!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Although Zelda is helpless since she lost her bow in the previous room fighting the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Armos}},<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I lost my bow in the other chamber!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> a mysterious warrior luckily appears and defeats the rest of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} using her [[wikipedia:sai (weapon)|sai]] while targeting {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} as well in the process.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Link! What's that creature?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} attempts to shoot the warrior with a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam}} but realizes that it is a human since the beam did not cause it to vanish.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I don't know, princess. But I'm gonna zap it! [...] Ow! Hey, that's no magic creature! It's a real person, like us!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Zelda then grabs the warrior and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} removes its mask, revealing that it is a woman who does not speak {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda's language.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=She doesn't speak our language.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Zelda is able to communicate with her, revealing that her name is {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing|link}} and is trying to rescue her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}} that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} stole.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Her name is Sing. She's from a land far away. Ganon stole her unicorn and she's trying to rescue it.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} agrees to help her and uses a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bomb|link}} to blast his way into {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon's Lair|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Well, we WERE on a rescue mission anyway. So, let's make it a party! C'mon!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> 

Due to the blast that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} caused, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} hears the group coming and begins to head their way.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=What? What's that?|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and the others fight a pair of {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Lynel|link}} at the entrance, and the young hero picks up a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Recorder|display=magic whistle|link}} after he defeats one of them.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Ah, a magic whistle! This could be useful.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda}} run ahead and cross the bridge, where they see two {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}} guarding the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}}. After {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} defeats them and gets her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}} back,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Good job, Sing! We've got your unicorn.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Armos}} appears with a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bomb}} in hand; however, Zelda throws her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Boomerang|link}} at its hand, which causes the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bomb}} to explode near him, thus killing the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Armos}}. Because of the explosion, a hole opens beneath where the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Armos}} was and reveals the place where the King is imprisoned. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} then begins to make his way to where {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} and Zelda are, but before he reaches the end of the bridge, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} appears before him. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} is able to zap him once with his {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam}}, but the evil wizard uses his magic to cut the bridge, which the young hero manages to hold on to while it swings him to the other cliff. Just as {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} is about to deliver the final blow,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Farewell, Link!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Zelda defeats {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon}} using her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Boomerang}} while {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} and her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}} rescue the king from his fall in the pit room.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Daddy! Sing! You saved him! Thank you, thank you!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Seeing the happy ending, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} blows the magic whistle to call upon a whirlwind that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Warping|display=warps|link}} them out of the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld}}.

The episode ends with {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} waving goodbye to the King, Zelda and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} as she's riding away on her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}}.

==Trivia==
*This episode establishes that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam}} cannot harm a normal person. Presumably, this was done to tone down the violence in the series, much like how {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} never uses his sword to strike or stab any enemies.
*At some point in the preview for this episode, the word "zip" can be seen instead of the usual zipping animation.
