{{Term/Store|Onox||Series, OoS}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 196}}</ref> also known as '''General Onox''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 215}}</ref> is one of two main antagonists of the ''Oracle'' games alongside [[Veran]] and is the [[Final Boss]] of the ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Seasons]]'' title. He calls himself the "General of Darkness",<ref name="General"/> and wishes to ruin the land of [[Holodrum]] in accordance with the wishes of his master, [[Twinrova]]. To do this, Onox is tasked with the burden of capturing the [[Din (Oracle)|Oracle of Seasons]] and submerging the [[Temple of Seasons]].<ref>{{Cite|As you know, if I imprison the Oracle of Seasons and bury the temple that houses the Season Spirits, the seasons of Holodrum will be cast into chaos, the bountiful gifts of nature will rot, and all living this will perish!!! That is the world of darkness that I long for!|Onox|OoS}}</ref> Although Onox appears to be a giant in solid armor closely reminiscent of an [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckle]], his true form is that of a demonic [[dragon]], summoned from the Dark Realm.<ref name="dragon">{{Cite|Gwah hah hah! I was summoned from the Dark Realm by Twinrova! See my true form! Feel the might of a dark dragon!|Onox|OoS}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Onox|The General of Darkness is behind the chaos caused by the disruption of Holodrum's seasons. Onox has great power and uses his mighty flail ti crush all who oppose him. He has cast a barrier of shadow around his castle and plans to gather the powers of destruction from the dying lands of Holodrum.}}Onox’s early history is much unknown but he does briefly mention that Twinrova summoned him from the Dark Realm (whether or not he is referring to the Dark Realm featured in Spirit Tracks or not is unclear).[[File:Gorgonfirst.png|167x167px|left]]
By capturing [[Din (Oracle)|Din]], the Oracle of Seasons, and encasing her in a crystal, Onox is able to submerge the [[Temple of Seasons]] into [[Subrosia]] and throw the flow of the seasons into chaos,<ref>{{Cite|Down, Temple of Seasons!!! Spring, summer, fall, winter... Fall into chaos!|Onox|OoS}}</ref> resulting in the damage of the land and the weakening of the [[Maku Tree]].<ref>{{Cite|What's to be done, Link? With the seasons in chaos, the land is robbed of the very essence of nature, draining all of might!|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> To counter these events, [[Link]] sets out on a quest to obtain the [[Essences of Nature]], which allow him to enter [[Onox's Castle]] on the [[Northern Peak]] to face him and revert his deeds.<ref name="Northern Peak"/>

After obtaining every Essence of Nature, Link goes to the Northern Peak, where he uses their power to bust into Onox's Castle, breaking the protective barrier he had placed upon its entrance to bar intruders. Link faces General Onox in two segments, the first in his natural form, using the comatose Din within a [[crystal]] to shield himself from Link's attacks. Before the young hero can defeat Onox, however, he reveals his second, actual form to him - a huge and ferocious dragon.<ref name="dragon"/> After ultimately defeating Onox, rather than cursing his own fate, he mocks Link as he dies, revealing that the Flame of Destruction has been lit, and Twinrova's grand scheme is one step closer to fruition.<ref>{{Cite|Gurrrgh...no... It's too late. My orders were to capture Din and deliver the destructive power of a seasonless land to Twinrova. Now, as the Flame of Destruction, that power is set to devour this land! Gah, hah, hah... |Onox|OoS}}</ref>

If Link has already saved [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]] from [[Veran]] in {{OoA}}, when he reaches Onox at the top of the Onox's Castle, the villain will reveal to Link that [[Princess Zelda]] is already being prepared for sacrifice, so that [[Ganon]] himself can return.<ref>{{Cite|It's too late, Link. Zelda--our sacrifice--is in our grasp! Soon no one will be able to stop the return of Ganon. Even you shall be destroyed--by Onox!|Onox|OoS}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
[[File:OoS Onox Dark Dragon Sprite.png|thumb|Onox as his true form|211x211px]]
Being the last boss of ''Oracle of Seasons'', Onox expectedly deals out massive physical damage with his attacks. His armor also blocks normal attacks with the [[Sword]], so Link must use [[Spin Attack]]s to get through. Onox is capable of bringing down a barrage of {{Plural|OoS|Falling Rock|link}} from the ceiling, and summoning multiple {{Plural|OoS|Gale Wind|link}}. Partway into the battle, Onox will summon [[Din (Oracle)|Din]] still trapped in a crystal as his shield. The [[Rod of Seasons]] must be used to knock Din away, or Link will be electrocuted whenever he attacks Onox. Despite this, Din, will always drift back to Onox eventually. After Onox is defeated again, he will transform into his more powerful dragon form. From here, Link must jump onto Onox's hands, then attack the gem on his head in order to kill him once and for all.

==Other Appearances==

==={{OoS|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} reprises his role as the main antagonist of the ''Oracle of Seasons'' manga where he is depicted as a ruthless knight who is on the search for the Oracle of Seasons, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din|link}}. In the manga, he gains the ability to directly sap the life energy of the landscape and any lifeforms that he touches, leaving behind barren land and lifeless husks. Though he is also able to hypnotize beings into violent, selfish manifestations of themselves, such as turning the once-honorable boxing champion [[Blaino]] into a bully that steals opponents' weapons after they're defeated.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Wait! I remember not long ago... a big guy showed up dressed head to toe in armor. And then... |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 113}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Your punches snapped me out of it. Good going! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 113}}</ref> Like in the game, he captures {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} in order to put the seasons into chaos and sink the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Temple of Seasons|link}} into {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Subrosia|link}}, though he plays a much more active role in barring {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} from completing his quest by sending monsters to do his bidding, such as placing {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Medelock|link}} in the sunken {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Temple of Seasons}} and sending out an army of minibosses such as {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Aquamentus|link}} and {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Syger|link}} on the mountainside in order to prevent {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} from reaching the castle. His main goal is to turn Holodrum and its inhabitants into a lifeless wasteland and keep {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} around as the only mote of beauty in his world.<ref name="Dark World">{{Cite Book|quote= Holodrum's seasons are running wild... Its fertility has been stolen, and soon all life will perish. Truly a land of the dead. This is the dark world that I desire. |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref><ref name="Beauty">{{Cite Book|quote= ''You'', {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}}, will be the one mote of beauty in my desolate world. |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref> Despite wearing the armor of a knight, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} disregards the ideals of chivalry and honor, such as mercy and protecting the innocent, as just childish naivete and argues that brute strength is all the really matters.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Such an idealist! Such naivete will get you killed in a world where brute strength is all that ''really'' matters. |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 45}}</ref>

He first appears while riding horseback along with two guards wandering on the road, where {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}}, feeling frustrated about running away from {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} when she offered him to dance, accidentally tosses a branch over his shoulder, which lightly hits and bounces off the head of {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}}'s armored horse. When {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} tries to apologize for the mishap, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} interrupts him and orders the guards to kill him. In response, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} questions why he would attack while he's in the middle of apologizing, with {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} retorting that he holds no mercy for anyone that tries to oppose him, no matter how small and insignificant.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= No! There is no mercy for ''anyone'' who gets in my way. Not even a single ant! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 45}}</ref> This prompts {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}}, who is training to be a knight, to chastise {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} for his unknightly behavior and defeats his guards.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= You're a jerk! Why're you wearing a knight's armor? A ''real'' knight protects the weak from bullies like you! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 52}}</ref> With them defeated, General {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} takes it upon himself to unleash his power, the ability to drain life and power from all living creatures, leaving behind death wherever he steps. Though to {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}}'s surprise, he is also able to spread and manipulate the dark magic to his will, catching {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} off-guard as he tries to outrun his magic but fails as he has his energy drained from him. While on the ground helpless, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} takes the opportunity to grab him by the head and boast about his unique ability before sapping {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}}'s life and continuing his search for the oracle.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Do you see? ''That's'' the power of the General of Darkness. It saps all manner of power, destroying all life. |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 49}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} stumbles upon the barren land that {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} left behind, lamenting at how far his destruction has spread across the land, only to find {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} nearby.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= This is terrible! The General of Darkness made these marks. Has he come this far? How much barren land does he have to spread before he'll stop? |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 52}}</ref> At first, she assumes that he is dead though quickly finds that only his life energy was drained, allowing her to undo the damage that {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} had caused by breathing life back into {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} with the help of the Season Spirits.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= N-no thank goodness, he ''has'' had his life energy drained... but it's not too late to breathe it back into him! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 53}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Spirits, lend me your strength. Fill him... with all the life that you can! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 53}}</ref><ref name="No Doubt">{{Cite Book|quote= This is the result of someone receiving aid from the Season Spirits. We'll find you soon enough, o Goddess... |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 59}}</ref> However, this turns out to be a trap as {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} later returns to the spot where he left {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}}, noticing life returning to the area, giving away the oracle's presence nearby.<ref name="No Doubt"/> He sends his minions to scout the area, and they report back to him on {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}}'s disguise as a traveling performer, and that her troupe is headed to the Temple of Seasons.

He is encountered again at the Temple of Seasons, where he strikes the temple with lightning in order to force {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} out of the building and demands her to come with him to his castle. When she refuses and orders him to stop polluting the land, he makes an attempt to grab her only to be stopped by {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}}, much to his surprise as he had presumed {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} to have perished from their last fight.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Meddler... I thought you were dead! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 84}}</ref> {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} tries to fight {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} in order protect {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}}, but quickly finds himself to be of no match as he has to constantly dodge {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}}'s ball and chain, and is knocked to the ground after a feeble attempt to deflect the weapon with his sword. When {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Impa|link}} tries to intervene, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} strikes her down with his fist. Before {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} can finish him off, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} agrees to leave but only on the condition that {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} isn't harmed.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Wait!! I'll go. Just don't hurt that boy! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 89}}</ref> {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} happily agrees and spirits {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} away in a giant tornado as {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} watches on in horror.

Should anyone go there... |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= General {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}}!! {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} has killed Medelock! |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 143}}</ref> At this news, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din}} snarks that it now seems that it isn't the land that's going to perish, but {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= It seems as though ''you'' may be the one about to perish. |book= OoS (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 143}}</ref> This prompts him to angrily swing his ball and chain at the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Keese}}, narrowly missing it, and demand it to relay the message to his troops as soon as possible in order to stop {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} from reaching the castle.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Don't just sit there flapping your wings! When {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} reaches the castle, he and {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} have a face-off, though this time {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} manages to best {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} by slicing off the chest pieces of his armor. At first, she chastises {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Link}} for being on the losing end, but when she learns of {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}}'s dirty fighting tactics, she changes her tune and calls out {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|General Onox}} for giving evil a bad reputation.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= He doing ''what''?! ==Trivia==
*Onox, when in his humanoid appearance, is possibly the only other example of a [[Gerudo]] male in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] beside [[Ganondorf]].
*Like [[Veran]]'s shoulder dress, Onox's brazen armor is also emblazoned with the [[Crest of the Gerudo]], reflecting his allegiance with [[Twinrova]].
*Onox's reaction upon first meeting [[Link]] in the beginning of the game will vary depending on whether the player started a new game or a linked save. In the former, he merely tells Link to move aside before breezing past him. In the latter, he'll recognize the young hero as being the one who destroyed Veran, while boasting that he's stronger than her.
*In concept artwork, Onox is depicted with a two handed battleaxe, similar to those used by [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckles]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', with a flail attached.
*In the initial [[Nintendo Power]] coverage for ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]]'', Onox was referred to by his Japanese name of '''Gorgon'''.
*He is considered to be one of the hardest [[Final Boss|Final Bosses]] of the franchise.
*Before their second phase, Koume or Kotake incorrectly refer to Onox as "Knox".
*The strategy to defeat Onox, or rather, his Dark Dragon form by jumping onto his hand and then slashing at a specific weakpoint on his face, bears a resemblance to [[w:c:MegaMan:Sigma|Sigma]] (more specifically his [[w:c:MegaMan:Wolf Sigma|Wolf Sigma]] form) in ''Mega Man X'' from the ''Mega Man'' series. As both ''Mega Man X'' and the ''Oracle'' duology were created by [[Capcom]], the resemblance was most likely intentional.

==Nomenclature==
Onox's name may be a phonic reference to the gem {{wp|onyx}}, a hard mineral known for its black variety. His Japanese name, Golgon or, more likely, Gorgon, refers to a [[w:Gorgon|same name race of creatures from the Greek myths]], a notable member being [[wikipedia:Medusa|Medusa]] who also inspired the [[Medusa Head|boss]] of [[Sword & Shield Maze]].
