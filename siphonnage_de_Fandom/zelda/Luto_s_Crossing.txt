==Features and Overview==
Luto's Crossing is a bridge that spans over the [[Zora River]] in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]].

==Nomenclature==
Luto's Crossing derives its name from [[Laruto]], the [[Zora]] [[Earth Sage]] from {{TWW}}.
