The {{Term/Store|Blacksmith's Wife||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 356 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith's Wife}} is the wife of the {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith (A Link Between Worlds)|display=Blacksmith|link}} and the mother of {{Term|ALBW|Gulley|link}}. She is upbeat and kindhearted, offering to make {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} breakfast after her husband had scolded him for being late to work.<ref>{{Cite|Did you even have breakfast yet? Soon as you come back, I’ll make sure you eat before work!|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref> After {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} traps {{Term|ALBW|Osfala|link}} in a {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Gulley}} goes missing. She and her husband grow increasingly concerned over his disappearance,<ref>{{Cite|Gulley! Where are you?! My son's got to be all right. He's just GOT to be, Link!|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref> until {{Term|ALBW|Link}} rescues him from the {{Term|ALBW|Dark Palace|link}}. In her relief, she plans on making {{Term|ALBW|Gulley}}'s favorite dessert to celebrate his return.<ref>{{Cite|I'm still so terribly worried about my little Gulley, Link! Wh-what did you say? Oh... That's good...! I'm so relieved...! But he can't come back yet? Well, that's all right. So long as he's fine. I can't let him see me like this, that I almost lost all--! But of course I knew he'd come back. I'll make his favorite dessert. I'll have something so yummy waiting for him that he'll know everything's OK!|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Next time you see him, let my boy know I'll have his favorite dessert waiting for him when he comes home!|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref>

==={{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}===
After {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|display=Hilda|link}} rescues {{Term|ALBW|Link}} from {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}}, the princess leaves him in the middle of a road near the Lorulean Blacksmith's shop. The Lorulean {{Term|ALBW|Blacksmith's Wife}} brings him back to her home to save him from the dangers in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}. Once she sees that the young hero is in good health, she kicks him out of her home,<ref>{{Cite|How does this kid expect to survive two seconds in Lorule if he's—? Well, well, well...Lookit this! Oh, I'm sorry. Did I wake you? How was your nap?! Don't even know why I brought you back. Shoulda just left you there passed out in the middle of the road...Now that you're awake and I see you're OK, you can see yourself to the door. Go on now. Scoot!|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref> disgusted that she committed nice acts. Humorously, she mentions that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} was mumbling the name {{Term|ALBW|Gulley}}, her Hyrulean counterpart's son, but she dismisses it as an unacceptable name.<ref>{{Cite|Whaddaya want this time?! Here I am, taking all this time just to help folks out...Since when did I get so...nice?! The nerve...Passing out right in the middle of the road and then mumbling that name... What was it? Gulley? Whoever heard of such a name? Even if I had kids, I would never name one Gully! Now go on! Scoot!|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref> After her husband sees the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword Lv2|link}}, she becomes confused by his change of mood and asks if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} was the cause of the vicissitude.<ref>{{Cite|What is this sudden mood change? He's really worked up! You wouldn't know anything about this, would you?|Blacksmith's Wife|ALBW}}</ref> However, once {{Term|ALBW|Link}} obtains the materials for her husband to forge him the {{Term|ALBW|Master Sword Lv3|link}}, her passion for her husband is reignited at seeing his own returned vitality.
