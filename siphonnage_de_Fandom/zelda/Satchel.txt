{{Term/Store|Satchel||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Satchel}} sits on a chair on the upper floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}} throughout the game. He is the father of {{Term|TMC|Joel|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Zill|link}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

{{Term|TMC|Satchel}} wonders about {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} and his search for the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey, what do you know about that {{Color|TMC Red|light force}} stuff? People are saying it's some kind of treasure that can grant you any wish. Anyway, now the king's in a big huff trying to find it. It's strange, though. He never struck me as the greedy type before. It's almost like he's a different person now...|Satchel|TMC}}</ref> though this quickly turns to criticism of the {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|King}}'s greed as the game progresses.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Green|king}} is just terrible lately! Things are going crazy around here! Would you believe a guard came into my house the other day? It’s true! And get this! He demanded that I give him some kind of "{{Color|TMC Red|force}}"!|Satchel|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, he explains how to navigate the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}} using its {{Plural|TMC|Sign|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Normal people can't enter the {{Color|TMC Green|Royal Valley}}. There's a cryptic forest that leads into it, and people get lost there. The secret to finding your way through is reading all the signs! You'll never make it unless you read each one carefully.|Satchel|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, and after {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Satchel}} notices the difference, though he does not want to scare the children.<ref>{{Cite|I can't say this in front of the kids... I don't want to alarm them, you know? But...{{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule Castle}} has been looking pretty weird lately. I can't say exactly what it is, though. It's just...different. Strange.|Satchel|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Satchel}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure! Let's see how well they fit!|Satchel|TMC}}</ref>
