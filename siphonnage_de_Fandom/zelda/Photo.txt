==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, during the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}, [[Mr. Write]] receives a fake Photograph of {{smw|Princess Peach}} from [[Christine]]. In {{LA|DX}}, the [[Photographer]] can take Photos of [[Link]] in various situations, which are saved in [[The Travels of Link]] album inside the [[Camera Shop]].

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:Pictography.png|thumb|right|200px|Pictograph of Great Bay from {{MM|-}}]]
In {{MM}}, sepia-toned Pictographs can be taken with the [[Pictograph Box]]. They can be given to the [[Swamp Tourist Center Guide]] in exchange for prizes. If [[Link]] shows him a Pictograph of [[Tingle]] or the [[Deku King]], he will receive a [[Piece of Heart]]. Link can also show a Pictograph of a [[Gerudo Pirate]] to the [[Fisherman#Majora's Mask|Fisherman]] at the [[Great Bay Coast]] in exchange for the [[Seahorse]].

In {{MM3D}}, Link is able to take full color Pictographs.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, the Picto Box can only take black and white Pictographs. Once Link upgrades it to the Deluxe Picto Box by completing [[Lenzo's Research Assistant]] sidequest, he is able to take full color ones. [[Lenzo]] also offers Link '''Legendary Pictographs''', which are special Pictographs that depict subjects that cannot be captured on film by Link, with the exception of {{Term|TWW|Ganondorf|link}}. He can purchase them for 50 [[Rupee]]s in {{TWW|-}} or 150 in {{TWWHD}}; however, Legendary Pictographs can only be purchased after obtaining the Deluxe Picto Box. In {{TWWHD|-}}, they are in full color with altered poses. The Legendary Pictograph that is offered changes with each phase of the moon.

At [[Windfall Island]], Link can receive various [[Treasure Chart]]s by showing [[Kamo]] a Pictograph of the full moon, [[Minenco]] a Pictograph of herself, and [[Pompie and Vera]] a Pictograph of Lenzo talking with [[Minenco]]. He can also receive a [[Piece of Heart]] by showing a Pictograph of [[Anton]] to [[Linda]] and vice versa.

Link can show a color Pictograph of a character or enemy to [[Carlov]] at the [[Nintendo Gallery]] in exchange for a [[Figurines in The Wind Waker|Figurine]] of the subject. Despite Carlov only accepting color Pictographs, he accepts any sepia-toned Legendary Pictograph.

{{TWWHD|-}} also features a "selfie" mode in which Link can include himself in the Pictograph. By holding the left stick in certain directions, Link can display various expressions. Also, a golden Carlov icon appears over Pictographs that are suitable for turning into Figurines.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, several Photos of [[Hena]] and her siblings can be seen inside her hut in the [[Fishing Hole]]. A Photo of the [[Fishing Hole Man]] from {{OoT}} also appears, with [[Hena]] claiming that he may be her ancestor.<ref>{{Cite|That's one of Hyrule's legendary master fisherman! Look, he's got a Hylian loach, doesn't he? He just may be one of my ancestors! Of course, I can't exactly PROVE that... It's kinda just wishful thinking on my part|Hena|TP}}</ref> A Photo of Link can also be added after catching the [[Hylian Loach]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Link can obtain a Camera from the [[Photo Bro]] at the [[Miiverse Gallery]]. With it and while in the [[Drablands]], Link can take single Photo per level, which is saved in the gallery's album. Link can then talk to the Photo Bro to post any of the Photos onto Miiverse.
