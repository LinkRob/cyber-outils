{{Term/Store|Irch Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Irch Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Irch Plain is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. A small marsh can be found on the southwestern side of the Plain. At the edge of this marsh is a wagon, which can be searched for [[Material]]s.

Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] shows [[Link]] some of [[Hyrule]]'s wildlife including the rare [[Silent Princess (Item)|Silent Princess]] and attempts to make Link taste a [[Hot-Footed Frog]].<ref>{{Cite|Zelda shows Link some of the rare types of wildlife in Hyrule. She explains her admiration for a flower called the silent princess. She also catches something unexpected and inspiring!|Recovered Memory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There's one! Oh! And another! The flowers we have in Hyrule aren't just beautiful... They're also quite useful as ingredients for a variety of things. ...Ah... This one here is called the silent princess. It's a rare, endangered species. Despite our efforts, we can't get them to grow domestically yet. The princess can only thrive out here in the wild. All that we can hope... is that the species will be strong enough to prosper, on its own. Is that what I think it is?! Look at this! I don't believe it, but I actually caught one! This delicacy is known to have very, very potent effects under the proper circumstances. Ta-da! Research from the castle shows ingesting one of these can actually augment certain abilities. We wouldn't be in a controlled environment out here, and with your level of physical fitness... you'd be a perfect candidate for the study! Go on! Taste it!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Consequently, Irch Plain is the site of the [[Recovered Memory]], "[[Silent Princess (Memory)|Silent Princess]]", which Link can recall by visiting the Plain after speaking with [[Impa]] and receiving the "[[Captured Memories]]" [[Main Quest]].

A [[Korok]] can be found by lifting a [[Rock]] atop a [[Tree]] on a slight hill on the Plain. A second Korok can be found by standing beside a [[Pinwheel]] atop a well in the northern reaches of the Plain and shooting the [[Balloon]]s that appear.
