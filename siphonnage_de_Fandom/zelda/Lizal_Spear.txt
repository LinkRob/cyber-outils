{{Plural|BotW|Lizal Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, bearing the ripping edge characteristic of {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} design.<ref name="Inventory"/> These designs are common among other pieces of {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}}, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are wielded by low-tier {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}, most commonly in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} also use them on occasion. Because of their low durability, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Spear}} do not last very long in combat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Due to their metallic spearheads, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Spear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked back up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They cannot burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
