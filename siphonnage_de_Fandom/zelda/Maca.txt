{{Term/Store|Maca||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Maca|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Maca}} is a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} that lives in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. They can be found outside the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Great Deku Tree's Navel|link}}, where they can provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with information about the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}} and {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, Mr. Hero! We're so excited you're here! If you need to know anything about these woods, just ask Maca! Or you can climb up here if you want to speak to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Deku Tree}}.|Maca|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ah! Mr. Hero! I watched you pull out the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}! If you want to know more about the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}, leave it to Maca!|Maca|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did you need something else?|Maca|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you have trouble with anything in these woods, just ask Maca!|Maca|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes {{Term|BotW|Maca}}, they will advertise their services.<ref>{{Cite|Leave it to Maca!|Maca|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}, {{Term|BotW|Maca}} proclaims that its highlights are {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[About the forest...] The highlights here in {{Color|BotW Blue|Korok Forest}} are the Great Deku tree and the sword pedestal, of course!|Maca|BotW}}</ref> They then quietly remark that {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}} likely is not happy to have been turned into a tourist trap.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Although the Great Deku Tree probably isn't happy about becoming a tourist trap.}}}}|Maca|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Maca}} explains that the {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} decorated the {{Term|BotW|Great Deku Tree's Navel}} for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, including an {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|General Store|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We've really decked out the inside of {{Color|BotW Blue|the Great Deku Tree}} in anticipation of your arrival. You'll find a place to rest and a bunch of other useful things in there!|Maca|BotW}}</ref>

Moving on to the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Maca}} states that it is where the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} rests.<ref>{{Cite|And then there's the {{Color|BotW Blue|sword pedestal}}! The {{Color|BotW Red|sword that seals the darkness}}, which needs no introduction, rests here.|Maca|BotW}}</ref> Since the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|display=Pedestal}} is located in the center of {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}, {{Term|BotW|Maca}} claims that standing on it will give one a sense of most of the paths located within.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|sword pedestal}} is the center of the forest... If you stand on the pedestal and look around, you'll be able to understand most of the paths in the forest!|Maca|BotW}}</ref> In addition to this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the {{Plural|BotW|Bean Lamp|link}} to navigate the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}} as well.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|bean lamps}} act as landmarks. You'll want to familiarize yourself with them to make it easier to find your way around.|Maca|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever find himself lost in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}, {{Term|BotW|Maca}} tells him to return to the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|display=Pedestal}} to reorient himself.<ref>{{Cite|And if you get turned around, you can always head on back to the {{Color|BotW Blue|sword pedestal}}!|Maca|BotW}}</ref>

Next, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Maca}} about the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|[About the sword...] The sword that seals the darkness... You want to know more about the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}}?|Maca|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Maca}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} may lose power if overused, but it will regenerate over time.<ref>{{Cite|Well the {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Sword}} is a very special weapon, but it can lose its power if you overuse it. When that happens, don't panic. All you have to do is wait for its power to regenerate.|Maca|BotW}}</ref> They suggest only using it for fighting evil, as opposed to trivial matters like opening bottles or cans.<ref>{{Cite|I probably wouldn't use it for trivial things like opening bottles or cans. Just for smiting evil and stuff.|Maca|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} want to know more, {{Term|BotW|Maca}} directs him to {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Great Deku Tree can tell you more.|Maca|BotW}}</ref>

Finally, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about leaving the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}, {{Term|BotW|Maca}} questions why he would want to leave such a perfect place.<ref>{{Cite|[How do I leave?] You want to leave the forest? But why? It's perfect here!|Maca|BotW}}</ref> However, they then reveal that they are joking, and they understand that {{Term|BotW|Link}} would need to leave the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|display=Forest}} to save {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Just kidding! I know you have to leave sometime. You've got to save Hyrule!|Maca|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Maca}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that past the {{Term|BotW|Sign|link}} east of the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword}}, he will find an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you head east from the {{Color|BotW Red|sword pedestal}}, there will be a sign on your right. Climb up the hill on the right side of the sign, and you'll see an {{Color|BotW Red|ogre tree}}.|Maca|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters its mouth, he will be taken back to the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you walk into that tree's mouth, you'll *whoosh* be taken to the entrance of the woods.|Maca|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} set this up because they do not like traversing the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods}}.<ref>{{Cite|We set it up this way because even us Koroks don't like to walk through the Lost Woods if we can avoid it.|Maca|BotW}}</ref> At this {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}} bearing a note from {{Term|BotW|Maca}}, where they assure the reader that they will not be eaten by the {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Note from Maca:<br/>Just climb into this tree's mouth to leave the forest. I promise it won't eat you.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref>
