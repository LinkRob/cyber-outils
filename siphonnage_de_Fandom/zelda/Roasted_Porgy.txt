==Location and Uses==
Roasted Porgies are made with {{Plural|Series|Armored Porgy|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Mighty Porgy|link}} are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. They also have a chance of being dropped by Blue {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} when felled by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. They have a delicious, crisp skin and a soft and fluffy texture.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one restores one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.
