The {{Term/Store|Monk|Monks|Series, BotW|plural}} are a group of characters in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Monk">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am a humble monk, blessed with the sight of the Goddess Hylia and dedicated to helping those who seek to defeat Ganon.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They are ancient people who reside at the end of {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, awaiting the arrival of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} in order to bestow him with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|Monk}} are members of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} clan who are devout worshipers of the Goddess, {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}}.<ref name="Sheikah"/> They are gifted with {{term|BotW|Hylia}}'s sight, and solely seek to aid {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} in combating {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref name="Monk"/> They each reside on a {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} inside their respective {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, which they have designed to serve as trials of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s resolve.<ref>{{Cite|You have proven to possess the resolve of a true hero. I am Kalm Yu'ogh, the creator of this trial.|Kalm Yu'ogh|BotW}}</ref> Upon completion of their respective {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}}, they give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} and bless him in {{Term|BotW|Hylia}}'s name before disappearing, having fulfilled their purpose.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|With your arrival, my duty is now fulfilled. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, allow me to bestow this gift upon you... Please accept this Spirit Orb. [...] May the goddess forever smile upon you.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

Every known {{Term|BotW|Monk}} is unique, each having their own article of clothing or jewelry. They may also have their own ''{{Wp|Mudra}}'', often in some variant of a triangular pattern to represent the [[Triforce]]. They all follow a minimal manner of dress, and have {{Plural|BotW|Crest of the Sheikah|link}} tattooed upon their foreheads.

== Trivia==
*The {{Plural|BotW|Monk}} seem to be based on real-world {{Wp|Buddhist mummies}}, which are monks who, through their purity, succeeded in preserving their bodies through death. More specifically, they seem to be based on the Japanese act of {{Romanize|''{{Wp|Sokushinbutsu}}''|即身仏}}, wherein monks practice austerity to the point of death and self-induced mummification. This aligns with the {{Plural|BotW|Monk}}' behavior of meditating within sealed {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}} while withering away.
*Each of the seven {{Plural|BotW|Monk}} at the end of the [[Trial of the Sword]] have posed their arms in a way that mimics the poses of the seven [[Sage]]s when they were sealing {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Okay, so I just finished the Trial of the Sword and immediately noticed the 7 monks are representing the 7 Sages. |author= kamuilyn |published= April 16, 2018 |retrieved= April 18, 2018 |url= https://www.reddit.com/r/Breath_of_the_Wild/comments/8cl9gl |title= Okay, so I just finished the Trial of the Sword and immediately noticed the 7 monks are representing the 7 Sages. : Breath_of_the_Wild|site= Reddit|type= }}</ref>
