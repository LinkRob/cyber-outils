{{Term/Store|Mystery Seed|Mystery Seeds|Series, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132 {{Exp Game|OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>{{Fact}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Mystery Seeds|You never know what to expect when you use these seeds. It's said that placing Mystery Seeds on particular objects will earn you hints related to your quest.}}
In {{OoS}}, {{Plural|OoS|Mystery Seed}} are {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed|link}} that grow on {{Plural|OoS|Mystery Tree|link}}. Their effect is randomized among all of the other {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed}}'. They are also capable of bringing {{Plural|OoS|Owl Statue|link}} to life, turning [[Buzz Blob|Buzz Blobs]] into [[Cukeman|Cukemans]], and revealing the "black beast" that guards the [[X-Shaped Jewel]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Mystery Seeds|You never know what to expect when you use these seeds. It's said that placing Mystery Seeds on particular objects will earn you hints related to your quest.}}
In {{OoA}}, {{Plural|OoA|Mystery Seed}} are {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed}} that grow on {{Plural|OoA|Mystery Tree}}. Their effect is randomized among all of the other {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed}}'. They are also capable of bringing {{Plural|OoA|Owl Statue}} to life, turning [[Buzz Blob|Buzz Blobs]] into [[Cukeman|Cukemans]], and undermining {{Term|OoA|Veran|link}} while she possesses the body of another.
