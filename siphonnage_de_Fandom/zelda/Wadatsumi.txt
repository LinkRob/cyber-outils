{{Term/Store|Wadatsumi||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 289}}</ref>

==Biography==
Usually, Wadatsumi is a resident of [[Papuchia Village]]. However, one day, he and the other men of the village were kidnapped by pirates and taken to the [[Pirate Hideout]], just north of the village.

After [[Link]] receives a [[Force Gem#Spirit Tracks|Force Gem]] from [[Niboshi]] in Papuchia Village, [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] leading to the Pirate Hideout will be revealed, making it accessible for Link to enter. Inside the hideout, Link will find Wadatsumi behind bars asking for the young hero's help. In order to rescue the prisoner, Link must partake in what is sort of a minigame: he will have to defeat as many [[Miniblin]]s as possible with his [[Bow]] and [[Arrow]]s as they walk across the upper part of the [[Nintendo DS|DS]] screen while Link stays on the other side of the hideout. Eventually, once enough Miniblins have been destroyed, Wadatsumi and Link will get on a small type of [[Mine Cart]] to escape the hideout. As they make their way out, Miniblins will continue to follow the pair. On both occasions, if Link takes too long to defeat a Miniblin, it will throw a cage at the young hero, and if it's not destroyed using an arrow, it will capture Link, ending the rescue. At the end of the hideout is a [[Big Blin]] that will give a massive hit to the pair if he is not defeated before the escape cart reaches him. 

After escaping the hideout, Wadatsumi asks Link to take him back to his wife in Papuchia Village.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a woman back home in Papuchia Village, and I really miss her... Hm? You say you know my girl? She's one fine woman, isn't she? Heh heh heh! Oh, but that reminds me... What are you doing all the way out here anyway? Cruising on your train, you say? Now that's good timing! How about giving me a lift to Papuchia Village?|Wadatsumi|ST}}</ref> After the young hero takes him back to the village, Wadatsumi will reward Link with a Force Gem, which establishes more Spirit Tracks in the Ocean Realm and makes way to several shortcuts in the realm. After rewarding Link with a Force Gem, the young man will immediately depart for his wife's house,<ref>{{Cite|Wahoooo! Back home at last! And it hasn't changed a bit! I thought I'd never see this place again after those filthy pirates snatched me! Thanks so much for rescuing me, bro! OK then! I'm gonna go see my girl! I bet she's dying to see me! See you later!|Wadatsumi|ST}}</ref> where he will find out that [[Orca (Spirit Tracks)|his wife]] has left him for [[Dovok]].<ref>{{Cite|Who is this guy?!|Wadatsumi|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This MAN is my husband!|Orca|ST}}</ref> After the young hero follows him out of his ex-wife's house and talks to him, the former prisoner will inform Link that he has decided to move on from his wife,<ref>{{Cite|Looks like she's moved on... I guess I need to move on too, huh?|Wadatsumi|ST}}</ref> and that he is considering to visit the [[Wise One]] to receive his  fortune.<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh heh... Maybe the Wise One will tell my fortune too...|Wadatsumi|ST}}</ref>
