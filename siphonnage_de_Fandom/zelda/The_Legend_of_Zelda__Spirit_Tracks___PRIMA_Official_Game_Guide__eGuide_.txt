'''''The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks — PRIMA Official Game Guide (eGuide)''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{ST}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The 224-page eBook has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks — PRIMA Official Game Guide]]," containing a complete walkthrough of {{ST}} and a listing of {{Plural|ST|Item|link}}, collectibles, {{Plural|ST|Character|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Enemy|link}}, and {{Plural|ST|Boss|link}}. It also contains an overview of the game's {{Term|ST|Multiplayer|link}} mode. 

It was offered as a digital download from Prima and is a digitized version of the physical book, albeit with a white background and non-essential artwork removed for ease of printing. The eGuide has searchable text and a hypertext-enabled table of contents. Each page also has a hypertext-enabled list of sections and relevant subsections.

The guide had a free preview containing partial snippets of various chapters. These include an overview of past game entries, an explanation of controls, and {{Term|ST|Item}}, {{Term|ST|Character}}, and {{Term|ST|Enemy}} lists.<ref>{{Cite Web|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20101229174427/http://www.primagames.com/catalog/promo_image/9780307467683_1549.pdf|site=primagames.com (archived)|title=The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks (eGuide) - Free Preview|type=PDF|author=Prima Games|retrieved=October 1, 2022}}</ref>
