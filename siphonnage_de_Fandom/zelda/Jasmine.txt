{{Term/Store|Jasmine||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Jasmine}} is seated outside {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is given a gold {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} by the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}. When spoken to, she will give {{Term|TMC|Link}} a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} as a reward for noticing her.<ref>{{Cite|I feel I should give you something to reward your abilities of perception! Call it a "Master of Sur-Prize"!|Jasmine|TMC}}</ref> She is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}, don't you? Well, OK! Let's give it a try.|Jasmine|TMC}}</ref>
