==Biography==

=== {{BotW|-}} ===
{{Term|BotW|Hagie}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who moves to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} with his family after the completion of the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. During the day, he sits at the edge of town, overlooking {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Torin Wetland|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} asks what his deal is.<ref>{{Cite|What's the deal? You're...|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that he is a traveler, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} remarks that he does not normally speak with "commoners" and that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is an exception.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm a traveler.] I normally don't have time to mingle with commoners...but I have a feeling about you. I'll make an exception this time.|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> Based on his clothing, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} guesses that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is in need of money.<ref>{{Cite|Your clothes...<br/>I'm guessing you're a little hard up for cash. Am I right?|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he does not need {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} grunts and the conversation ends.<ref>{{Cite|[No.] Hmpf...|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he is, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} appreciates his honesty.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] I appreciate your honesty.|Hagie|BotW}}</ref>

Immediately, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} offers {{Term|BotW|Link}} a down payment of 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|So for the down payment... I'd say {{Color|BotW Blue|100 rupees}} sounds fair, yes?|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} is confused by this, {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} forces him to take the money.<ref>{{Cite|[A...down payment?] I don't care about the details. Here, take it. Hand!|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> By taking the money, {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts {{Term|BotW|Hagie}}'s request.<ref>{{Cite|Hoh ho ho!<br/>So you accept, then? Excellent!|Hagie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Hagie}} reveals that there are two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Torin Wetland}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, listen to what I have to say. === {{TotK|-}} ===
After the events of [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]], Hagie was hired to work for [[Hudson Construction]]. He was put in charge of the Railcar leading from [[Tarrey Town]] to the [[Hudson Construction Site]]. While [[Hudson]] was busy preparing for his daughter's, [[Mattison]], move to [[Gerudo Town]], Hagie started charging people 20 [[Rupee]]s to use the Railcar, despite the fact that Hudson allows everyone to use it for free. He also stole a large glowing rock from the construction site, thinking it was valuable. Hagie also starts the [[Adventure_Log#Shrine_Quests|Shrine Quest]] "[[Rock for Sale]]", where he tries to sell the rock to [[Link]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s, but [[Ruli]] would catch him selling the rock and forces him to sell the rock for 50 Rupees instead. 

During the [[Side Quest]] "[[Mattison Independence]]", [[Mattison]] would try to use the Railcar to see [[Hudson]] at the Hudson Construction Site, but she couldn't use the Railcar since she couldn't pay Hagie 20 [[Rupee]]s. Link would have to obscure Hagie sight to allow [[Mattison]] to sneak past him and use the Railcar.

After "[[Mattison Independence]]" is complete, Hudson would start focusing on work again, forcing Hagie to stop charging people 20 [[Rupee]]s to use the Railcar. Hagie would also comment how Hudson would keep calling him "Hagieson", despite the fact that Hagie would constantly tell [[Hudson]] that his name is Hagie.
