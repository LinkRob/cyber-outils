The {{Term/Store|Reception Room||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 249}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Reception Room}} is an interior room of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. Each wall of the {{Term|BotW|Reception Room}} has a doorway, making it a hub for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} as he explores.<ref name="CaC"/> The northern doorway leads deeper into the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|display=Castle}}, though it is covered by rubble. The eastern door leads into the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors|link}}, towards the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Square Spiral Staircase|link}}. The western doorway also leads into the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors}}, though it is blocked by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}.

The southern exit of the {{Term|BotW|Reception Room}} leads into the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room|link}}. To access the {{Term|BotW|Observation Room}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must destroy the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Reception Room}}. This will clear away the {{Term|BotW|Malice}} covering the doorway.
