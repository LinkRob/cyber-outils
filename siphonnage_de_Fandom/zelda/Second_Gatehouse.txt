The {{Term/Store|Second Gatehouse||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Second Gatehouse'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Second Gatehouse}} is a small building located along the path to the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}}, continuing from the {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse|link}}. Much like the {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse}}, it is flanked by two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Turret|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Second Gatehouse}}, the portcullises at the entrance and exit will close and a {{Term|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}} will descend from the ceiling. Upon its defeat, the portcullises will be raised once again. 

After the {{Term|BotW|White-Maned Lynel}} has been defeated, it will drop a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}. A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} on the upper level on the {{Term|BotW|Second Gatehouse}} contains five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. The conical spire roof of the Second Gatehouse has broken in at places, giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} access to the interior. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
At the top of the {{Term|BotW|Second Gatehouse}}'s spire, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}}. Interacting with them will cause a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} to appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
