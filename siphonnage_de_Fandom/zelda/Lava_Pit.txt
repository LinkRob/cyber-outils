{{Term/Store|Lava Pit|Lava Pits|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|It looks like there are many}} lava pits {{Color|OoT3D Light Blue|around here, so watch your step!}}|Navi|OoT3D}}</ref> Lava Pits go unnamed in {{MM}}.

==Location and Uses==
Lava Pits are shallow pools of [[Lava]] which will damage [[Link]] if he comes into contact with them.

==={{OoT|-}}===
Lava Pits can be found in [[Dodongo's Cavern]], [[Goron City#Ocarina of Time|Goron City]], and the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}}. If Link touches a Lava Pit, he will sustain damage in quarter-[[Heart]] intervals.

==={{MM|-}}===
Lava Pits can be found in [[Snowhead]] and the [[Stone Tower Temple]]. [[Goron Link]] is capable of walking through Lava Pits without taking any damage.
