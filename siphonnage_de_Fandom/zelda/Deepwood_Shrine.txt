The {{Term/Store|Deepwood Shrine||Series, TMC}} is the first {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} within {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> It is a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-size {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} located behind the lost city of the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} deep within the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}. It was once a meeting place between {{Term|TMC|Minish}} and humans.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
[[File:TMC Deepwood Shrine Artwork.png|thumb|160px|left|The {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}}]]
Due to {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} opening the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|display=Shrine}} is now infested with monsters and has been abandoned by the nearby {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}.

When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} comes to the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}} in search of a way to reforge the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link|display=Elder}} tells the young hero to seek out the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}, one of which is hidden in the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|display=Shrine}}. The {{Term|TMC|Gentari|display=Elder}} gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} permission to enter the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|display=Shrine}}, guarded by the village priest, {{Term|TMC|Festari|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|display=Shrine}} shows many signs of being long abandoned, due to the large amount of cobwebs lining the walls. The main room in the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} houses a large {{Term|TMC|Barrel|link}} mounted horizontally on a pole. {{Term|TMC|Link}} must first remove obstructions at the ends of the pole and then run inside the barrel to rotate holes into position allowing access to the other parts of the dungeon. Most of the puzzles in the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|display=Shrine}} involve pushing pots or statues onto switches or using the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to open passageways. The bottom floor is flooded with water and {{Term|TMC|Link}} must use a [[Lily Pad]] to navigate, using the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar}} as the motor. It is infested with {{Plural|TMC|Sluggula|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Red Pesto|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Puffstool|link}}.
