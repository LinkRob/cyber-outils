'''Palace of Wind: The Shadow Chateau''' is a term used in the {{FS|Himekawa}}.

==Overview==
During one of the bonus pages, [[Shadow Link]] creates a blog with the name ''Palace of Wind: The Shadow Chateau''. This is one of three bonus stories that makes fun of Shadow Link's inability to have contact with sunlight. The one featuring his blog is simply called ''Shadow Link's Blog'', while the other ones are called ''About That Time'' and ''Shopping Online''.

During the story about the blog, the narrator mentions that he is unable to go out in daylight and that he instead makes stuff up. The example being used during the story is that he went to an amusement park with his girlfriend.
