==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Rubies are rare {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} occasionally obtained from breaking {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}. They are also dropped by various types of {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus|link}}, Silver enemy variants and {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Blademaster|link}} when killed. They're sold at [[Ore and More]] in [[Tarrey Town]] and by [[Kairo]] at [[Foothill Stable]]. Rubies are said to contain the power of fire within them, granting warmth in colder temperatures when used in {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can sell {{Plural|BotW|Ruby}} for 210 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} in most {{Plural|BotW|Shop|link}}, though the merchant [[Ramella]] will occasionally pay more for them in groups of ten after completing the [[Side Quest]] "[[The Jewel Trade]]".<ref>{{Cite|By the way, do you have {{Color|BotW Red|10 rubies}}?|Ramella|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You'll get 2,100 rupees if you sell them at a store...or you can trade them to me for {{Color|BotW Red|2,300 rupees}}.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Ruby}} are required to purchase the {{Term|BotW|Ruby Circlet|link}} from "{{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shop}}. The description of the {{Term|BotW|Snowquill Headdress|link}} reveals it is adorn with a {{Term|BotW|Ruby}}.

Rubies are used by {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} to upgrade certain pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor}}. They're needed for all tiers of the Ruby Circlet and the [[Hero Set]], while five are needed for the final tier of each piece of the [[Snowquill Set]].

*Total for '''Ruby Circlet''': 22 (2, 4, 6, 10)
*Total for '''Hero Set''':  57 (1, 3, 5, 10) {{Exp|For Each Armor Peice in the Set|x3}}
*Total for '''Snowquill Set''':  15 (0, 0, 0, 5) {{Exp|For Each Armor Peice in the Set|x3}}

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, Rubies look like their versions from Breath of the Wild. They can be bought at the store in the Gerudo Desert, or be found in gray treasure chests in main quests, as well as be obtained through first-clear rewards. They can be sold to most Merchants for 210 Rupees, but [[Dayfah]] will buy them for 525 Rupees.
{{Noncanon|End}}
