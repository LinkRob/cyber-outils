{{Term/Store|Zumi||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Zumi|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Zumi}} can always be found at {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}}, either next to the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} outside or next to {{Term|BotW|Akrah|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Onya|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Garshon|link}} in the rain. When talked to in the afternoon, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that he must be tired from his trip and wonders if he is a leviathan researcher.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my, you must be tired from your long trip! Are you another of those {{Color|BotW Red|leviathan}} researchers? You don't look the type.|Zumi|BotW}}</ref> Late at night, she is glad to see link {{Term|BotW|Link}} because she thinks traveling on dark roads is dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! Good seeing you so late at night! It's just common sense that traveling dark roads at night is dangerous!|Zumi|BotW}}</ref> Regardless of the time, she mentions ruins behind {{Term|BotW|Mount Drena|link}} that are always dark, and says that no one who goes there ever returns. As a result, she warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} and advises him to stay away from the ruins.<ref>{{Cite|But on the other side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Drena}}, northeast of this stable, there are {{Color|BotW Red|ruins that are dark even during the day}}! But no one who has gone there has ever come back! So you'd best forget about going there!|Zumi|BotW}}</ref>
If it is raining, she mentions how dreadful the weather is and that all she can do is relax in the stable.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! This weather is dreadful! Nothing to do in this weather but stay here at {{Color|BotW Blue|{{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable}}}} and relax.|Zumi|BotW}}</ref>
