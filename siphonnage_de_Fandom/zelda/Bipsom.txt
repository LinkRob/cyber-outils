{{Term/Store|Bipsom||Series, OoA (Wessel)}} ({{Term/Store|Pip||Series, OoA (Wessel)}} for short) is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Bipsom">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= OoA (Wessel)|publisher= |page= 52}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Bipsom}}'s in-game name is decided by {{Term|Series|Link|link}}.<ref name="Name">{{Cite|I am {{Color|OoS Blue|Blossom}}. This is my first child--a healthy baby boy! But I am having trouble choosing a name. Can you help me think of one? What would you call him?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> Although {{Term|Series|Bipsom}} is the official name for the character, it is impossible for {{Term|Series|Link}} to name him "Bipsom", as the name does not fit within the five-character limit.}} He is the son of {{Term|Series|Bipin|link}} and {{Term|Series|Blossom|link}}. His personality and path in life develop as a result of {{Term|Series|Link|link}}'s decisions.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=|author= Drenn |published= January 5, 2018 |retrieved= January 20, 2018 |url= http://wiki.zeldahacking.net/oracle/Bipin_and_Blossom's_son |title= Bipin and Blossom's son |site= ZeldaHacking Wiki |type=}}</ref>

==Biography==
===Infancy===
[[Link]] can first encounter Bipsom inside [[Bipin's & Blossom's House]], which is a location in both [[Horon Village]] in {{OoS}} and [[Lynna City]] in {{OoA}}. Bipsom is first seen as an infant in the arms of his mother, Blossom, with his father, Bipin, running around back and forth nearby. If Link speaks to Blossom, she explains that she is having trouble thinking of a name for her baby and asks Link if he can think of one.<ref name="Name"/> After naming the child, Blossom asks for confirmation of Link's chosen name.<ref>{{Cite|You would name him {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> Link can say "No" to modify the name. Once Link says "Yes," Blossom agrees to call him that name and tells Link to visit them anytime.<ref>{{Cite|It's a fine name! That's what I'll call him! He seems happy to have it! Come visit us anytime.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's a fine name! I shall call him so! He seems happy to have it. Come visit us anytime.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link speaks to Blossom again, she expresses hope that her son will grow up strong and healthy like Link.<ref>{{Cite|I hope {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} will grow strong and healthy, like you.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link speaks to Bipin after naming his son, he remarks that it is a fine name and that he hopes he will grow up to be a fine child.<ref>{{Cite|So, the name is decided! {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}, it is! It's a fine name! I hope he grows to be a fine child!|Bipin|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So, the name is decided! {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}, huh? It's a fine name! I hope he grows to be a fine child!|Bipin|OoS}}</ref> The name that Link gives the baby may affect his personality and life path. If Link visits the family to complete each step of their story line, their son can develop into a child over the course of the game and continue to grow into adulthood throughout the following [[Linked Game]] or [[Hero's Secret]].

==== Rupees for Doctor ====
[[File:OoX Bipsom Sick Sprite.png|left|40px]]
After Link collects two [[Essence]]s and has named Bipin's and Blossom's son, Link can revisit the family and talk to Blossom to find that their son is ill and needs a doctor.<ref name="Doctor">{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} looks a little strange. I want to take him to a doctor, but we have no Rupees... Could you please lend us some?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> Blossom explains that they do not have enough [[Rupee]]s to see a doctor and asks if Link would lend them some.<ref name="Doctor"/> If Link says "No," Blossom says she understands.<ref>{{Cite|I understand...|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says "Yes," Blossom asks him how much he will lend them.<ref>{{Cite|Really? How much can you lend us?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link chooses an amount of Rupees that is higher than what he currently has, then Blossom will tell him that he does not have enough.<ref>{{Cite|It looks like you don't have enough Rupees.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link gives Blossom 1 Rupee, she says that his sympathy is enough.<ref>{{Cite|Link! You are no rich man. Your sympathy will do fine.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link gives her 10 Rupees, she thanks him and says she will have to find a way to earn the rest of the Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks... I'll have to do something about the rest...|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link gives her 50 Rupees, she thanks him and says that it should be just enough.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you. This should be just enough.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link gives her 150 Rupees, she thanks him and calls him a "true samaritan."<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Link! You are a true samaritan!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link leaves and re-enters the house and speaks to Blossom, she thanks him again and says that she can take her baby to the doctor now.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Now I can take him to the doctor.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> The amount of Rupees Link gives can affect the outcome of the child's personality and life path. Once Link has walked around the [[Overworld]] for at least 8 screens, he can return to Blossom to find that the baby is no longer ill.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} has gotten better! Thank you!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref>

==== Sleep Advice ====
After Link has collected four Essences and has learned that the baby is no longer sick, he can speak to Blossom to find that the baby is not sleeping enough.<ref>{{Cite|My baby never sleeps. Link, how can I get him to sleep?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref name="Sleep">{{Cite|My baby never sleeps. How can I get him to sleep, Link?|Blossom|OoA}}</ref> Blossom asks Link how she can get him to sleep.<ref name="Sleep"/> Link can either advise her to sing or play with the baby.<ref>{{Cite|I like to sing! I'll try it!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I see! If we play, he'll get all tired out. Thank you!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link speaks to her afterward, she comments about how her son is a "darling baby."<ref>{{Cite|Well? Isn't he a darling baby?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> The decision that Link chooses here affects the outcome of the child's personality and life path.

===Childhood===
Once Link has obtained six Essences and given Blossom advice for getting the baby to sleep, he can visit the family to find that their son has grown into a child.<ref>{{Cite|See! {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} has grown!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> The child can develop one of three personality types based on the choices that Link made previously. If Link advised Blossom to play with him to get him to sleep, then the child will become "hyper."<ref name="Hyperactive">{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} is so hyperactive!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link advised Blossom to sing him to sleep, then the outcome of his personality is determined by the amount of Rupees Link gave Blossom for the doctor. If Link gave her either 1 Rupee or 10 Rupees, then the child will become "bouncy."<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} is such a curious child.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link gave her 50 Rupees, then the child will become "shy."<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} is too shy.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link gave her 150 Rupees, then the child may become "shy" or "hyper" based on what Link named him.

After Link defeats [[General Onox]] in {{OoS|-}} or [[Veran]] in {{OoA|-}}, the family moves away to either [[Labrynna]] or [[Holodrum]] depending on the game.<ref>{{Cite|Off to Labrynna! Bipin & Blossom|Sign|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Off to Holodrum! Bipin & Blossom|Sign|OoA}}</ref> After defeating Onox in {{OoS|-}}, Bipsom and his parents can be seen in a small scene in Horon Village as the four [[Seasons]] are being restored back to normal. Bipsom continues to develop in the following Linked Game or Hero's Secret. However, if he is an adult and Link progresses to a Hero's Secret, the story line is reset back to the beginning when their son was an infant. When Blossom asks for Bipsom's name again, his name from the previous game is automatically typed out already in the typing screen, but Link is able to rename him. Upon first visiting the family after they have moved, Link can speak to Blossom, who asks if Link remembers naming their son and comments that he is doing well.<ref>{{Cite|Link! We meet again! Do you remember naming our son, {{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}}? He's quite well!|Blossom|OoA}}</ref> If Link speaks to their son, he asks if Link remembers him.<ref>{{Cite|Where have you been, Link? Do you remember me? It's {{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}}! Mom and Dad are doing well, too!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're Link? I'm {{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}}! No way!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey! Link! Remember me? It's {{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}}! All right! We meet again!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref>

==== Blossom's Question ====
After Link has spoken to the child before and then walked around the Overworld for at least 8 screens, he can return to the family to advance their story line further. If Link speaks to Blossom, she comments about their son's personality and asks how Link was as a child,<ref name="Hyperactive"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} is very shy. What kind of child were you, Link?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} is such a curious child. What kind of child were you, Link?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> first asking if he was energetic or hyper.<ref>{{Cite|Were you a hyperactive child, Link?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|An energetic one?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Energetic?|Blossom|OoA}}</ref> If Link says "No," Blossom asks if he was quiet.<ref>{{Cite|Quiet, perhaps?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says "No" again, she asks if he was weird.<ref>{{Cite|Were you weird?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says "No" three times in a row, she says guesses that Link's personality was hard to sum up in one word and will repeat the dialogue is Link speaks to her again.<ref>{{Cite|I guess it's hard to sum it up in a word.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> Once Link says "Yes" to one of the questions, Blossom comments about his answer.<ref>{{Cite|Oh? I bet you were quite a mischievous boy.|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Really? You must have been cute!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You were? I never would have guessed...|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> Link does not have to speak to Blossom at this point in order for the family's story line to progress, but his responses to her influence Bipsom's life path.

==== Child's Question ====

===== Bouncy Child =====
[[File:OoX Bipsom Bouncy Sprite.png|left|40px]]
As a "bouncy" child, he has rosy cheeks and constantly bounces between the two beds in the House. If Link speaks to him, he introduces himself and describes his name as "groovy."<ref>{{Cite|My name is {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}. Isn't it groovy?|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> His mother believes that, although he is a strange child, he will become a man of great deeds.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}} is a strange child, but he'll be a man of great deeds!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> After Link has spoken to the child before in a Linked Game or Hero's Secret and then walked around the Overworld for at least 8 screens, he can speak to him again to receive a question. He asks Link what came first, the chicken or the egg.<ref>{{Cite|Which was first, the chicken or the egg?|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says the "Chicken," the child questions how the chicken hatched.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Then how did that chicken hatch?|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says the "Egg," the child questions who laid the egg.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Then who laid that egg?|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> Afterwards, no matter what personality he has, the child says that he cannot wait to grow up.<ref name="Grow Up"/> The responses that Link gives the child influences his life path as an adult.

===== Shy Child =====
[[File:OoX Bipsom Shy Sprite.png|left|36px]]
As a "shy" child, he wears glasses and runs away from Link if he approaches him. If Link talks to him, the child stutters as he introduces himself, only to run away after realizing that he spoke to a stranger.<ref>{{Cite|I... I am called {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}... ! ! ! ! ! ! Oops! I spoke to a stranger!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> After Link has spoken to the child before in a Linked Game or Hero's Secret and then walked around the Overworld for at least 8 screens, he can speak to him again to receive a question. He asks Link if he is strong.<ref>{{Cite|Are you strong?|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says "Yes," he says that he will be strong too, so he can protect his parents.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be strong, too, so I can protect my mom and dad!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says "No," the child is surprised and wonders if it bothers Link.<ref>{{Cite|Huh??? And it doesn't bother you?!?|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> Afterwards, no matter what personality he has, the child says that he cannot wait to grow up.<ref>{{Cite|I can't wait to be a grown-up.|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I can't wait to be a grownup.|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> The responses that Link gives the child influences his life path as an adult.

===== Hyper Child =====
[[File:OoX Bipsom Hyper Sprite.png|left|40px]]
As a "hyper" child, their son will have long hair and constantly runs around the room. If Link speaks to him in {{OoA|-}}, the child explains that he was named by a "great man," referring to Link.<ref>{{Cite|I am called {{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}}. They say I was named by a great man. Isn't it a cool name?|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> In {{OoS|-}}, perhaps as a development mistake, the child instead states the same quote that the musician Bipsom says when telling Link that he is writing a song about him.<ref>{{Cite|I, {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}, am writing a song to praise Link, the great hero!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> After Link has spoken to the child before in a Linked Game or Hero's Secret and then walked around the Overworld for at least 8 screens, he can speak to him again to receive a question. He will ask Link if he has a girlfriend.<ref>{{Cite|Do you have a girlfriend?|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says "Yes," he says that he likes someone too.<ref>{{Cite|...Actually, I have someone I like, too, but don't tell!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> If Link says "No," he speculates that Link is not ready for one.<ref>{{Cite|...I guess you aren't ready.|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> Afterwards, no matter what personality he has, the child says that he cannot wait to grow up.<ref name="Grow Up">{{Cite|I can't wait to grow up!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref> The responses that Link gives the child influences his life path as an adult.

===Adulthood===
Once Link has obtained two Essences and answered the child's question, he can visit the family to find that the child has grown into an adult and chosen his path in life. If Link speaks to Blossom, she asks if he remembers naming their son and comments about how he turned out.<ref>{{Cite|Link! Do you remember naming our son, {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}?|Blossom|OoS}}</ref><ref name="Fine Man">{{Cite|He's grown into a fine man!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> Their son can become a traveler, an aspiring hero, an arborist, or a musician. If he is either an aspiring hero or a traveler, Blossom says that he has grown into a fine man.<ref name="Fine Man"/> If he is an arborist, she says that he is a master arborist.<ref name="Arborist">{{Cite|He's a master arborist, too!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If he is a musician, then she says that he is a famed musician.<ref name="Musician">{{Cite|He's a famed musician now!|Blossom|OoS}}</ref> If Link speaks to Bipsom, he also says a unique phrase depending on his life path.<ref>{{Cite|My name is {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}! Isn't it a cool name? Yeah, dude!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref><ref name="Hero">{{Cite|My name is {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}. I am training every day to be a hero like you, Link!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I, {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}, have decided to take the same tree-lined path as my father!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref><ref name="Writing">{{Cite|I, {{Color|OoS Blue|[Name]}}, am writing a song to praise the great hero, Link!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref>

====Traveler====
[[File:OoX Bipsom Traveler Sprite.png|left|44px]]
Bipsom can become a traveler in a Linked Game or Hero's Secret. He wears sunglasses, has spiky hair, and can be found singing either in bed or on a table. Though his mother is worried that he idles his time away,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}}...He's grown up, but he idles his time away. What am I to do?|Blossom|OoA}}</ref> Bipsom decides to travel the world once Link has collected four Essences and spoken to Bipsom before as an adult.<ref>{{Cite|I'm gonna save my money and go out into the world!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> Bipsom asks Link for a contribution of Rupees for his travels.<ref>{{Cite|So can you make a contribution?|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> If Link refuses, Bipsom calls him stingy.<ref>{{Cite|You're stingy, dude.|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> If Link accepts, Bipsom asks how much he wants to contribute.<ref>{{Cite|How much can you contribute?|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> If Link does not give him any Rupees, he gets upset and says that he cannot buy anything.<ref>{{Cite|I can't buy anything, dude! Thanks!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> If Link gives him 50 Rupees, Bipsom is disappointed with the low amount.<ref>{{Cite|That's it, dude? ...Uh, thanks!!!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> If Link gives him 100 Rupees, he is appreciative and exclaims that he will never forget this favor.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks, dude! I'll never forget this favor!|Bipsom|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks, dude! I'll never forget this!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref>

After collecting six Essences, Link can talk to Bipsom to find that he has returned from building character on his travels.<ref>{{Cite|Link, what's up? I've been out in the world building character! ====Musician====
[[File:OoX Bipsom Musician Sprite.png|left|86px]]
Bipsom can become a famed musician in a Linked Game or Hero's Secret.<ref name="Musician"/> Bipsom can only become a musician if Link advised Blossom to sing to Bipsom to get him to sleep as a baby. He can always be found singing and playing his guitar. Bipsom's mother enjoys his songs.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard {{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}}'s songs? They're quite nice.|Blossom|OoA}}</ref> He is writing a song about Link and his deeds.<ref name="Writing"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoA Blue|[Name]}} said he is writing a song about your deeds!|Blossom|OoA}}</ref> After Link collects four Essences and has spoken to Bipsom before as an adult, Bipsom's song is almost finished, but he needs to know whether Link values love or courage more.<ref>{{Cite|My song is almost finished! Once Link has collected six Essences, Bipsom's song is finished and he asks Link if he will listen to it.<ref>{{Cite|Finally! My song is now finished!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> If Link says "No," Bipsom invites Link to come listen anytime.<ref>{{Cite|Really..? Come listen anytime!|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> If Link says "Yes," Bipsom will play Link the song. If Link told Bipsom that he values love more, then Bipsom will play the [http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4VurbyQdx8#t=54m19s theme] that is heard when rescuing [[Princess Zelda]]. If Link chose courage, he plays the [http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r4VurbyQdx8&feature=youtu.be&t=1h3m41s melody] that is heard after rescuing either [[Din (Oracle)|Din]] or [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]]. No matter what song is played, Bipsom asks Link how the song was and Link's Life Energy is restored.<ref>{{Cite|How was it?|Bipsom|OoA}}</ref> The song continues to play until Link leaves the house. Link can return to Bipsom anytime to hear the song again and restore his Life Energy.
