==Features and Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala}} region is a rather large highlands region located in north-east {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, home of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Akkala}} is said to be the place where {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}} fell during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In a real sense, the kingdom of Hyrule met its doom here.|Nell|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= The Akkala region, with beautiful colored leaves, is said to be the last land where the Hyrule Kingdom collapsed, in which {{Typo|many|much}} pristine wildness remains.|magazine= Nintendo Magazine|publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021|page= 16}}</ref>
