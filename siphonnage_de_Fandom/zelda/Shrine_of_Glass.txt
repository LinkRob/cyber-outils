{{Term/Store|Shrine of Glass|Shrines of Glass|Series, CoH|plural}} are objects in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|SHRINE OF GLASS!|N/A|CoH}}</ref> They were introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Glass}} can appear in {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}}. When interacted with, {{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Glass}} turn all of the character's items into glass, excluding their {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}}. Unlike other shrines, none of the effects of the {{Term|CoH|Shrine of Glass}} are removed when the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} is left.

When destroyed, the shrine drops a random glass weapon (for most characters), a {{Term|CoH|Glass Spear|link}} (for {{Term|CoH|Impa|link}}) or a {{Term|CoH|Diamond|link}} (for characters that can't use weapons).
