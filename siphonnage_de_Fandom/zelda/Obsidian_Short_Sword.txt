==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Obsidian Short Sword}} are a type of {{Term|CoH|Short Sword|link}} whose damage increases as {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}'s beat combo rises, beginning from 1 and increasing to 2 and finally 3, then resetting back to 1 when the combo is lost. Like all {{Plural|CoH|Short Sword}}, they can only be wielded by Link, and can only be acquired either after he infuses the Short Sword with {{Term|CoH|Obsidian|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an already-infused one in {{Term|CoH|Death Mountain|link}} is opened by Link.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Obsidian Dagger|link}}.
