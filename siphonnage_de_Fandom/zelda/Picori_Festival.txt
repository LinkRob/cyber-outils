The {{Term/Store|Picori Festival||Series, TMC}} is an event in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 78}}</ref>

==Overview==
It is an annual celebration which commemorates the coming of the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}},<ref>{{Cite|So as not to forget our gratitude, we hold this festival each year.|Marshall|TMC}}</ref> who helped drive evil away from the land by granting the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men|link}} the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But then the Picori came from the sky, bringing humans powerful magic. With the magic light force and the Picori Blade, the beasts were repelled.|Marshall|TMC}}</ref> The tale details that the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} show themselves every 100 years, when the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|link}}, the link between their world and the world of men, opens,<ref>{{Cite|The sanctuary is a strange realm, trapped between two worlds. It is the bridge between the Minish world and the Human world. The door to the sanctuary opens only once every hundred years!|Melari|TMC}}</ref> and only to children.<ref>{{Cite|The Picori do not show themselves to anyone but children.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref> Held in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|display=Festival}} includes a {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}} wherein the winner is allowed to touch the sacred {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}}.<ref>{{Cite|Say, wasn't there going to be a {{Color|TMC Blue|sword-fighting tournament}} at the castle? I wonder who won this year...|Princess Zelda|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whoever wins the competition wins the honor of touching the sacred blade.|Minister Potho|TMC}}</ref> During {{TMC|-}}, the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|display=Festival}} is the one hundredth annual occurrence since the last time the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} showed themselves.<ref>{{Cite|They say that every hundred years, the Picori come to Hyrule... And this is the 100th year, to the day, of the last time the Picori were here!|Anton|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} wins the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|display=Tournament}} and shatters the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}}, causing the evil it had sealed away to return to plague {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}.

In the beginning of {{TMC|-}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} attends the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|display=Festival}} with {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}}. At the center of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival}} lie four stands: {{Term|TMC|Brocco|link}}'s vegetable stand, {{Term|TMC|Beedle|link}}'s fruit stand, {{Term|TMC|Pina|link}}'s minigame stand, and a final stand where {{Term|TMC|Marshall|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}} resident, tells of the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} legend. To the north of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival}} stand two people from the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}} ({{Term|TMC|Gale|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Hailey|link}}), and a {{Term|TMC|Vassal|link}} stands by the {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}.

Shortly after completing {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival}} will have already ended, and the town square returns to a normal marketplace.
