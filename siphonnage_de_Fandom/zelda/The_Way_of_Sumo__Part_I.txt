==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TPHD|The Way of Sumo, Part I}} is found within the basement of {{Term|TPHD|Mayor Bo|link|display=Mayor Bo's}} house. It is the first in a series of three instructional posters on {{Term|TPHD|Sumo Wrestlin'|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|The Way of Sumo, Part I|display=''Part I''}} details the victory conditions and basic procedure of {{Term|TPHD|Sumo Wrestlin'}}.

The poster is attached to a rough plank notice board near the Sumo ring.
