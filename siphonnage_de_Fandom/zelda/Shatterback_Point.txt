{{Term/Store|Shatterback Point||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Shatterback Point'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Shatterback Point is a cape jutting out from [[Ploymus Mountain]] over the [[East Reservoir Lake]] in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. It is a popular diving spot, despite its dangers, and is often used by the [[Zora]] thrill-seeker, [[Gruve]].<ref>{{Cite|[Diving spot?] My favorite diving spot of late is {{Color|BotW Blue|Shatterback Point}} at the tip of {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. I can scarcely put it into words...the joy of your own reflection when diving into {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}} from there... However, a {{Color|BotW Blue|Lynel}} does reside on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. If you go, be prepared for the worst.|Gruve|BotW}}</ref> To deter would-be divers, a [[Sign]] cautioning travelers was placed at its tip,<ref>{{Cite|JUMP AT YOUR OWN RISK!|Sign|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Laflat|link}} has begun searching for [[Picture]]s of the [[Lynel]] atop Ploymus Mountain to scare anyone whom Gruve may have goaded into diving.<ref>{{Cite|There's a red-maned monster called a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} that lives on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. I REALLY need a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. It can be a picture of any red-maned {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. Not just the one on the mountain. So what do you say? Can you get me a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Understood!] I am glad that was settled so quickly. See, there's this fool in the domain who goads people into jumping off of {{Color|BotW Blue|Shatterback Point}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. It's an incredibly dangerous jump, and this guy pressures people into jumping off by questioning their courage. He's a real bottom-feeder. If this keeps up, someone is surely going to wind up horribly injured! Does that answer your question, Link? I'll leave the details to you. Just bring me a {{Color|BotW Red|picture of Lynel}} so I can describe them in terrible detail. Thank you!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> Shatterback Point is one of the locations that is affected by the [[Rain]] generated by {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.
