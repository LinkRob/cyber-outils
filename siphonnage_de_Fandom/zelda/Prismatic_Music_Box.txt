The '''Prismatic Music Box''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Prismatic Music Box|A music box with a lovely, seven-colored cylinder. It plays beautiful, bright tones.<ref name="Prismatic Music Box">{{Cite|A music box with a lovely, seven-colored cylinder. It plays beautiful, bright tones.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The Prismatic Music Box is the reward for completing the medium [[Flag Race]] in 25 seconds. It is required to make the [[Gizmol]] [[Automaton]] and to complete the [[Performance Artist!]] [[Side Quest]].
