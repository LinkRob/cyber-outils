{{Term/Store|Tingle Statue|Tingle Statues|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=141 {{Exp Game|TWW, TWWHD}} }}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Tingle Statue}} go unnamed in {{HW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue}} are hand-crafted gold statues made by {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle|link}}'s brother, {{Term|TWWHD|Knuckle|link}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can find five of these {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue|display=Statues}} by {{Term|TWWHD|Bomb|link|display=Bombing}} specific spots in {{Plural|TWWHD|Dungeon|link}}.

In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWW|Tingle Statue}} can only be found by connecting a [[Game Boy Advance]] to the [[Nintendo GameCube]] and deploying a {{Term|TWW|Tingle Bomb|link}} with the {{Term|TWW|Tingle Tuner|link}}. They can be located by using the Game Boy Advance's screen. When a {{Term|TWW|Tingle Statue|display=Statue}} is detected, {{Term|TWW|Link}} must pay {{Term|TWW|Tingle}} to use a {{Term|TWW|Tingle Bomb}} to expose the {{Plural|TWW|Treasure Chest}} in-game. Regular {{Plural|TWW|Bomb}} cannot be used for this purpose in any versions of {{TWW|-}} prior to {{TWWHD|-}}. In {{TWWHD}}, there is no indication for these {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue|display=Statues}}, as the {{Term|TWW|Tingle Tuner}} was replaced with the {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Bottle|link}}. However, the {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue|display=Statues}} still exist in the exact same locations, and regular {{Plural|TWW|Bomb}} can be used to reveal their {{Plural|TWW|Treasure Chest}}.

After being found, the {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue|display=Statues}} will automatically appear at a cluster of stone pedestals on {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Island|link}}. By talking to {{Term|TWWHD|Ankle|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} is paid 50 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} for each one found, and an additional 500 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}} if all five of them are found. In {{TWWHD|-}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Knuckle|link}} will also appear on {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Island}} once all five {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue|display=Statues}} are found.

The {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue}} can be struck and tossed into the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}} at anytime. Nobody will suspect {{Term|TWW|Link}} as the vandal if he attacks the {{Plural|TWW|Tingle Statue}} unless the {{Term|TWW|Tingle Tuner}} is in use. In this event, {{Term|TWW|Tingle}} will tell {{Term|TWW|Link}} to stop. If {{Term|TWW|Link}} continues, {{Term|TWW|Tingle}} will eventually hit {{Term|TWW|Link}} with a {{Term|TWW|Tingle Bomb}}. Throwing a {{Term|TWW|Tingle Statue}} into the {{Term|TWW|Great Sea}} will cause {{Term|TWW|Tingle}} to immediately use a {{Term|TWW|Tingle Bomb}} on {{Term|TWW|Link}}, whether or not he has hit one of them already.

The {{Plural|TWWHD|Tingle Statue}} also each contain a hint to the location of a renewable source of 100 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}}. However, this source no longer exists in {{TWWHD|-}}.
