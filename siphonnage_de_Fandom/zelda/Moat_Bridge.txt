{{Term/Store|Moat Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Moat Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Moat Bridge is located at the northwestern border of the [[Hyrule Castle Town Ruins]] where the [[Hyrule Castle Moat]] feeds into the [[Regencia River]] in [[Hyrule Field]].

In [[Master Mode]], a wooden platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s can be found on the southern side of the Bridge. Atop this platform, [[Link]] can find a wooden [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Royal Guard's Sword]].

On the northern side of the Moat Bridge, a [[Circles|Circle]] of [[Lily Pad]]s lays right below the bridge. If Link falls into the Circle, a [[Korok]] will appear to him and gift him a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
