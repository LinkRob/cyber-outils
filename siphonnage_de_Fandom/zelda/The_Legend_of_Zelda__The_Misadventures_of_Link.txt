'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Misadventures of Link''''' is a comedy miniseries based on [[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD'']], produced by [[Nintendo]] using the {{wp|Machinima|machinima}} technique of synchronizing video footage from a video game using customized camera angles and other in-game modifications. Episodes of the series were periodically available to stream exclusively on the {{nw|Nintendo Video}} service for the [[Nintendo 3DS]]. The episodes were later made available to stream on Nintendo's official YouTube channel. Released as promotion for ''The Wind Waker HD'', the series follows [[:en:Link#The_Wind_Waker|Link]] throughout various scenarios of the game.

==Production==
The videos served both as entertainment and as advertisements for {{TWWHD}} and {{ALBW}}. The title and logo of the series is likely a play on {{TAoL}}. The first episode, "Tunnel Vision," appeared on November 1, 2013, with each new episode appearing six days after the previous one. The last episode, "Pearl of Pain!," was released on January 6, 2014.

''The Misadventures of Link'' is based on Link's adventures from {{TWWHD|-}}. Despite initial reports that suggested that the series were to be animated, much like {{TLoZ|TV Series}}, as many articles used the word cartoon to describe the series,{{Fact}} it was shown to be a {{wp|Machinima|machinima}} series. While most machinimas are fan-made and unofficial, ''The Misadventures of Link'' was an official [[Nintendo]] production, with {{TWWHD|-}} game engine being used along with custom camera angles and facial expressions, although some episodes were just in-game cutscenes with slight modifications.
