{{Term/Store|Revali's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 6 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Revali's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Revali's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Wildberry|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}, although a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawns alongside more of them once 100 of them are killed. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} is defeated, more {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin}} spawn. After 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, a gate opens and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawn. The time limit is also taken away, so the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} are defeated.
