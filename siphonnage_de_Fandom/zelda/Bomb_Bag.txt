{{Term/Store|Bomb Bag|Bomb Bags|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, OoS, OoA, TWW, TWWHD, TMC, TP, TPHD, PH, ST|plural}} are recurring items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 118 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM, TWW, TMC, TP, PH, ST, SS}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |publisher= Nintendo |page= 83}}</ref> They first appear in {{OoT|-}}.

==Location and uses==
Bomb Bags are satchels in which [[Link]] can carry Bombs. They are often obtained as a Dungeon Item that give Link access to Bombs and allow him to continue his quest,<ref name="OoT3D Bag">{{Cite|You found a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Bomb Bag}}! This bomb-holding bag is made from a Dodongo's stomach!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> though in some cases, the Bombs themselves are treated as Link's essential item,<ref>{{Cite|You got ten {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}}!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> while the Bomb Bag they come with is either shown in his inventory or merely alluded to.<ref name="OoA Upgrade">{{Cite|You can now carry 30 {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}}. And I've filled your bag. Farewell...|Fairy|OoA}}</ref>

Upgrades to the Bomb Bag, which increase the amount of Bombs they can carry, are quite commonplace across Link's adventures. Most games featuring the item also have several subsequent Bomb Bags for Link to discover, which act as upgrades to the initial Bomb Bag and can usually be collected in any order.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Big Bomb Bag}}! Now you can carry more bombs, up to a maximum of {{Color|OoT3D Yellow|30}}!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Biggest Bomb Bag}}! Now you can carry up to {{Color|OoT3D Yellow|40}} Bombs!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref>

In earlier games, such as {{TLoZ}}, {{ALttP}} and {{LA}}, Bombs are available to be found or purchased without the need for a Bomb Bag, as they are not tied to Link's progression. Link's Bomb capacity can still be increased by the blessings of other characters, such as the [[Old Man]], [[Venus]], and the [[Mad Batter]], respectively.<ref>{{Cite|Then I will increase your carrying ability so you can carry 15 Bombs at maximum. This is just a small happiness I can give to you.|Venus|ALttP}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
The Bomb Bag in {{MM}} can be purchased from the [[Bomb Shop]] in [[West Clock Town]] for 50 [[Rupee]]s. If Link saves the [[Old Lady from Bomb Shop|Old Lady]] from [[Sakon]]'s attack during the night of the [[First Day]], then the Big Bomb Bag will replace the regular Bomb Bag in the Bomb Shop's stock; otherwise, it will appear for sale at the [[Curiosity Shop]] on the night of the [[Final Day]] for a higher price. The Big Bomb Bag can be purchased without owning the regular Bomb Bag first.

The [[Business Scrub]] in [[Goron Village]] will sell the Biggest Bomb Bag to [[Goron Link]] for 200 Rupees, as long as Link trades in his Big Bomb Bag.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give you my {{Color|MM3D Red|Biggest Bomb Bag}}, regularly priced at {{Color|MM3D Purple|1,000 Rupees}}... In return, you'll give me your {{Color|MM3D Red|Big Bomb Bag}} and just {{Color|MM3D Purple|200 Rupees}}!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> Otherwise, the Biggest Bomb Bag sells for 1,000 Rupees, which is more than Link can carry at any given time.<ref>{{Cite|If you don't have a {{Color|MM3D Red|Big Bomb Bag}}, I have to sell it for {{Color|MM3D Purple|1,000 Rupees}}.|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}===
In both {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, the existence of a Bomb Bag is only alluded to when acquiring an upgrade to Link's Bomb capacity.

In {{OoS|-}}, after obtaining Bombs in the [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]], Link can purchase a Big Bomb Bag at the Subrosia Market.<ref>{{Cite|A {{Color|OoS Red|Big Bomb Bag}}<br/>I'll trade for {{Color|OoS Red|10 Bombs}} and {{Color|OoS Red|50 Ore Chunks}}. How about it?|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> Despite the item's name, the upgrade is represented by a red Bomb which immediately explodes all around Link, magically granting an increase in the number of Bombs he can carry.

Another Bomb Bag upgrade can be obtained through the use of [[Linking Secret]]s. If Link has defeated [[Onox]] and begun a [[Linked Game]] of {{OoA|-}}, he will eventually meet a young girl at the [[South Shore]] who asks him to pass on her [[Linking Secret#Pirate_Secret|"lucky" secret]] to an unlucky person in [[Holodrum]],<ref>{{Cite|I forgot to tell my lucky {{Color|OoA Red|secret}} to that guy who went to {{Color|OoA Blue|Holodrum}}. He's so unlucky.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> hinting that she refers to a [[Piratian]].<ref>{{Cite|I just hope he doesn't meet with disaster and end up a bag of bones...|N/A|OoA}}</ref> Back in {{OoS|-}}, if Link tells this Secret to one of the remaining Piratian in the [[House of Pirates]], he will be rewarded with an increased Bomb Bag capacity—but only if he possesses the "lucky" number of 777 [[Ore Chunk]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Thank'ee! Now I'm lucky! I'd like to give ya somethin' lucky in return... But yer luck seems to 'ave run out... What shall we do? Aye! I know! Get 777 {{Color|OoS Red|Ore Chunks}}! Sounds lucky, don't it? Then I'll share my luck with ya!|Piratian|OoS}}</ref> If Link's adventure in {{OoS|-}} is the [[Linked Game]] instead, he can meet a friendly [[Moblin]] in the [[Great Moblin's Keep|Great Moblin's Ruined Keep]] who tells him a Secret related to the [[Tokay]] people.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee! {{Player Name}}! I'm no enemy! I heard a rare {{Color|OoS Red|secret}}. Do you want to hear it?|Moblin|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|OoS Red|secret}} is related to the Tokay people in {{Color|OoS Blue|Labrynna}}! Understand?|Moblin|OoS}}</ref> Passing on this Linking Secret to the [[Wild Tokay|Wild Tokay Museum]] curator in {{OoA|-}} will inspire him to revive the [[Wild Tokay]] ceremony.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! That's the legendary Wild Tokay {{Color|OoA Red|secret}}! Now I want to bring back the Wild Tokay! Can you help?|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> For completing the mini-game, he rewards Link with a Bomb Bag capacity upgrade and another Secret to access the upgrade in [[Holodrum]].<ref>{{Cite|I'll teach you the {{Color|OoA Red|secret}} to carrying more {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}} in {{Color|OoA Blue|Holodrum}}, too.|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> Back in {{OoS|-}}, Link can tell this Secret to [[Farore (Oracle)|Farore]] to receive the upgrade in that game.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, instead of Link upgrading his Bomb Bag to be larger and larger, he can collect multiple Bomb Bags in his inventory. Each Bomb Bag can only hold one type of Bomb at a time, so collecting extra Bags gives Link not just extra carrying capacity, but the ability to hold more varieties of explosives as well.

Obtaining the [[Giant Bomb Bag]] doubles the carrying capacity of all of Link's Bomb Bags, though the Giant Bomb Bag itself does not appear in the Collection.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, there are no traditional Bombs at all. After acquiring the Bomb Bag in the [[Earth Temple]], [[Link]] can store [[Bomb Flower]]s and use them at will, giving him the same abilities as Bombs. In addition, [[Small Bomb Bag]] are in unlimited supply; after completing the Earth Temple, Link can purchase as many [[Small Bomb Bag]]s as he can carry, though only those he stores in the [[Adventure Pouch]] will contribute to his Bomb capacity.

Each Small Bomb Bag can be [[Upgrading|upgraded]] at the [[Scrap Shop]] to store up to 15 extra Bombs. With up to eight spots in the Adventure Pouch, this gives Link a maximum capacity of 130 Bombs, provided that he equips no other items.

==Trivia==
*In {{OoT|-}}, the Bomb Bag is made from a [[Dodongo]]'s stomach.<ref name="OoT3D Bag"/> This may have influenced the design of the Giant Bomb Bag in {{TP|-}}.
*In {{OoT3D|-}} and {{MM3D|-}}, the clasp around the Bomb Bag's opening is embossed with the [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian]] text {{Romanize|{{H|OoT|ホム}}|Bomu}}, or "Bomb".
*In {{TMC|-}}, the Great Mayfly Fairy asks Link if he threw a golden Bomb or a silver Bomb into her spring, and rewards him with a [[Large Bomb Bag|Big Bomb Bag]] for his honesty. This scene was recreated from a near-identical scene in {{OoA|-}}, which in turn was likely based on an ancient Greek fabled known as {{Wp|The Honest Woodcutter}}.
