{{Term/Store|Turtle Rock||Series, LA, LADX}} is a {{Term|LA|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 210 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref> At first it is actually a part of the {{Term|LA|Turtle Rock|link}} {{Term|LA|Dungeon|link}}, where it is seemingly made of stone and is blocking the entrance to the {{Term|LA|Dungeon}}, akin to the entrance of the eponymous {{Term|LA|Dungeon}} in {{ALttP}}. However, by playing the {{Term|LA|Frog's Song of Soul|link}}, the stone will break and the head will come to life and will try to attack {{Term|LA|Link|link}} by extending its neck to slam into him, similar to the behavior of {{Term|ALttP|Trinexx|link}} in {{ALttP|-}}. Unlike {{Term|ALttP|Trinexx}}, {{Term|LA|Turtle Rock (Boss)}} can be harmed by simply attacking with a {{Term|LA|Sword|link}}, and is relatively weak. Once {{Term|LA|Turtle Rock (Boss)}} is destroyed, the entrance to the {{Term|LA|Dungeon}} is revealed.

In the Switch version, although its overall role was essentially unchanged, the battle was altered to have its head retain its stone carapace upon awakening, requiring the player to destroy the rocky shell via bombs before commencing with the standard battle.

==Trivia==
*Despite only being a {{Term|LA|Sub-Boss}}, {{Term|LA|Turtle Rock (Boss)}} has a theme unique to it, including a special introduction as it comes to life.
*To date, {{Term|LA|Turtle Rock (Boss)}} is the only {{Term|LA|Boss}} named after the {{Term|LA|Dungeon}} it is fought at. In addition, until Echoes of Wisdom, it was the only Mini-Boss fought at the entrance to a dungeon.
*Rather uniquely, touching Turtle Rock's neck can cause damage even when it is dying.
