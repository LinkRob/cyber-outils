The {{Term/Store|Blue Bracelet||Series, FSA}} is an {{Term|FSA|Item|link}} in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117 {{Exp Game|FSA}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|FSA|Blue Bracelet|Increase your defensive powers and decreases damage you take by half.}}
[[File:FSA Blue Bracelet Sprite.png|left]]
The Blue Bracelet is contact-activated item that, when found in a stage, cuts all damage [[Link]] receives in half. However, per every three-stage-region, there is only one Blue Bracelet, hidden within the overworld of one of the three stages. Like all items found in {{FSA}}, the Blue Bracelet's power only operates in and during the stage it was found in, not the entire level, as one may hope.
