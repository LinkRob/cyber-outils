{{Term/Store|Giant|Giants|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are characters in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 11}}</ref>

==Biography==
Two {{Plural|TPHD|Giant}} appear in the {{Term|TPHD|Event of Giants|link}} in the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove|link}}, guarding the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}}. They are awakened by {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} howling "{{Term|TPHD|Zelda's Lullaby|link}}", and ask that he solve their puzzle in order to enter the true {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove}}.<ref>{{Cite|We are guardians of this land... Guide us to where we once stood. Only then can you enter the true Sacred Grove...|Giant|TP}}</ref> Once {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} has completed the puzzle, the {{Plural|TPHD|Giant}} will yield passage to the location of the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword}}.

A third {{Term|TPHD|Giant}} stands in front of the {{Term|TPHD|Door of Time|link}}; when {{Term|TPHD|Link}} returns to the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove}} during his search for the {{Plural|TPHD|Mirror Shard|link}}, he thrusts the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}} into the {{Term|TPHD|Pedestal of the Master Sword|link}}, causing the {{Term|TPHD|Giant}} to disappear and granting him access to the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time (Dungeon)|link}}.
