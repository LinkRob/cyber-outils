==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Term|MM3D|Cremia|link}} drives a {{Term|MM3D|Donkey}} to pull along her {{Term|MM3D|Wagon}} loaded with bottles of {{Term|MM3D|Milk|link}}. After defending {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}} from the {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} can escort {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} as she drives her {{Term|MM3D|Donkey}} through {{Term|MM3D|Milk Road|link}} to make her {{Term|MM3D|Milk}} delivery.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Donkey}}
{{Plural|BotW|Donkey}} are used as pack animals for traveling merchants in {{BotW}}. They can be found on the road with certain travelers or resting at {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Donkey}} will always follow the merchant they're traveling with.

According to the traveling merchant {{Term|BotW|Cambo|link}}, the scientific name for {{Plural|BotW|Donkey}} is ''equus donkus''.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, dear. You mean you weren't asking me to pontificate upon the charms of the equus donkus, as they are know?|Cambo|BotW}}</ref>
