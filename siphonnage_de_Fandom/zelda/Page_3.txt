==Features and Overview==
Page 3 is a large corn farm, and is home to [[Peter]], [[Sensei]], and [[Corn Boy]]. [[Buriki]] has crash-landed her spaceship in the field, and will join Tingle's party when he replaces her batteries and oils her. The [[Slingshot#Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love|Slingshot]] can be found here. After Buriki fails to take off in her spaceship, Peter takes the trio to the forest on [[Page 4]] via his tractor.
