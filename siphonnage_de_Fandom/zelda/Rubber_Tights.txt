The {{Term/Store|Rubber Tights|Rubber Tights|Series, BotW}} are a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Rubber Tights}}<br/>These invaluable tights are resistant to electricity. They're made with something called "rubber," an ancient technology that no longer exists in this modern age.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Rubber Tights}}<br/>These invaluable tights are made with something called "rubber," an ancient technology with origins shrouded in mystery.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Users==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Rubber Tights can be acquired in the [[Qukah Nata Shrine]] after completing the [[Shrine Quest]] "[[A Song of Storms]]" in [[Calora Lake]]. When worn, they provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/> They, along with the [[Rubber Helm]] and [[Rubber Armor]], compose the {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor (Set)|link}} set, which makes Link Unshockable when worn. Rubber Tights can be both enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
