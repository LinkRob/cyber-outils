The {{Term/Store|Bomber's Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Bomber's Ring<br/>Set two Bombs at once|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Bomber's Ring can be obtained by getting a perfect score at the platinum level of the [[Goron Dance]] minigame during the past in {{OoA}}. It needs to be noted that the Bomber's Ring is not a guaranteed reward, the ring received can also be the [[Protection Ring]].

The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it allows [[Link]] to set two [[Bomb]]s at once.<ref name="Description"/> The Bomber's Ring can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
