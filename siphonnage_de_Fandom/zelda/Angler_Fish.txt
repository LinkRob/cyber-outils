The {{Term/Store|Angler Fish||Series, LA, LADX, LANS, OoA}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 163 {{Exp Game|LA, OoA}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LADX|Angler Fish}} is the {{Term|LADX|Boss|link}} of {{Term|LADX|Angler's Tunnel|link}} in {{LA|-}}. It is the {{Term|LADX|Nightmare|link}} guarding the {{Term|LADX|Surf Harp|link}}. The {{Term|LADX|Angler Fish}} is fought underwater and charges at {{Term|LADX|Link|link}}, making rocky tiles fall down from the ceiling upon hitting a wall. {{Plural|LADX|Angler Fry|link}} will also appear to aid it. {{Term|LADX|Link}} can defeat it by slashing its light with the {{Term|LADX|Sword|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
The {{Term|OoA|Angler Fish}} is the {{Term|OoA|Sub-Boss|link}} of {{Term|OoA|Jabu-Jabu's Belly|link}} in {{OoA}}. It is battled in one of the underground, side-scrolling areas of the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}}, with a bumpy ground that {{Term|OoA|Link}} must use the {{Term|OoA|Roc's Feather|link}} to traverse. The {{Term|OoA|Angler Fish}} will move back and forth through the room while continually bouncing off the ground, up to the height of the ceiling. It will periodically fire harmful, slow-moving bubbles toward {{Term|OoA|Link}}, which can bounce off the room's surfaces and accumulate. These bubbles can be popped with the {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}} and will sometimes drop {{Plural|OoA|Heart|link}} or {{Plural|OoA|Scent Seed|link}}; otherwise they will eventually pop on their own.

{{Term|OoA|Link}} must use the {{Term|OoA|Seed Shooter|link}} to fire {{Plural|OoA|Scent Seed}} at the {{Term|OoA|Angler Fish}}, deflating it and dropping it to the ground, where {{Term|OoA|Link}} can damage it by striking its light with his {{Term|OoA|Sword}}. It will then reinflate and repeat the process several times. After taking enough damage, the {{Term|OoA|Angler Fish}} will be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} and creating a portal just outside the underground room that leads to the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}}'s entrance.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler Fish}} appears as the boss of the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Angler's Tunnel|link}}. While fighting it, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} breaks his {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Sword|link}} after stabbing its head, defeating it in the process.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Nomenclature==
The {{Term|OoA|Angler Fish}} is named after the real-life {{Wp|Anglerfish}}, which it strongly resembles.
{{Nomenclature}}

==Trivia==
*Although the boss has no given gender, it is to be noted that it possessing light lures implies that it is female due to female angler fish in real life being the only ones to have light lures.
