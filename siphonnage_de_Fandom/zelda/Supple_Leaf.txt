{{Term/Store|Supple Leaf|Supple Leaves|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 140"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Supple Leaf}} are one of the rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Cacto Clothes|link}}.<ref name="E 140"/> Unlike most {{Plural|TFH|Material}}, they cannot be obtained from [[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Levels]] in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}, purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}}, or won in {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}. Instead, {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} can receive one as a reward from {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}} for completing all {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} in the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Ah yes, I hear you have conquered a {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands challenge}}... Why didn't you say something sooner! So modest! The mark of a true hero, I say. As promised, your reward for besting the {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands challenge}} of the Woodlands. Use it well!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> or as one of the random prizes for winning a {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} battle in the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands}}-based arena.{{Fact|TFH}}

Following the Ver. 2.1.0 update, {{Term|TFH|Link}} can also receive a {{Term|TFH|Supple Leaf}} by speaking to the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}}, who, for every ten {{Plural|TFH|Hero Point|link}} collected, will grant the young hero a {{Term|TFH|Material}} of the lowest value required to craft an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}. The odds of winning one in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum}} have also been increased.{{Fact}}

{{Plural|TFH|Supple Leaf}} can also be sold to the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} for 600 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} apiece.
