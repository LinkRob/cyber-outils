The {{Term/Store|Owl||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Manual|LA|Owl|This mysterious owl follows you throughout your adventure. He seems to know much of the island and the world in general, including the Wind Fish. But is he friend or foe?}}
The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} appears to {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} as soon has he finds his {{Term|LANS|Sword|link}} in {{Term|LANS|Toronbo Shores|link}}.<ref name="First Quote">{{Cite|Hoot! Hoot! So you are the lad who owns the sword... Now I understand why the monsters are starting to act so violently... A courageous lad has come to wake the Wind Fish... It is said that you cannot leave the island unless you wake the Wind Fish... You should now go north, to the Mysterious Forest. I will wait for you there! Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite manual|quote= You set out in search of your sword and other gear that might have washed up on the beach with you. As you stood in the surf with your recovered sword, a strange owl suddenly appeared and hooted this riddle: "Awaken the Wind Fish and all will be answered."|game= LA|page= 4}}</ref> He appears again on multiple occasions to share his words of wisdom.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= LA|page= 28}}</ref> The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} is greatly concerned of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish|link}}'s eternal slumber and upon seeing {{Term|LANS|Link}}, believes that he could be the one who would awaken the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}.<ref name="First Quote"/>

Giving {{Term|LANS|Link}} directions in the form of riddles,<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! How many Instruments have you gotten so far? When you play the Instruments in front of the Egg, the Wind Fish will wake and you will leave this island. Now, you must hasten to the Yarna Desert! The dark, monstrous inhabitants of the sand will show you the way! Hoot Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref> the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} sends him to various {{Plural|LANS|Dungeon|link}} of the isle to retrieve the {{Plural|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! Take the key and go to the Tail Cave. Retrieve the Instrument that is hidden there! Go now! The Wind Fish is waiting! Hooot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref> which are all needed to awake the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}.<ref name="Instrument">{{Cite|Hoooot! That is an 'Instrument of the Sirens!' I have to admit, at first I did not believe you were real... That Instrument, along with the seven others in the set, has the power to wake the Wind Fish! You must collect them all! I was instructed to give you directions... Your next goal is north, in Goponga Swamp!! Hoot, indeed!|Owl|LADX}}</ref> The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} is also constantly reminding {{Term|LANS|Link}} that the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} is watching his progress and awaits to be awakened.<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! Ho, brave lad, on your quest to wake the dreamer! Welcome to the Mysterious Wood! Much of mystery you will find on this uncharted Koholint island! I'm afraid you may find it a trifle difficult to leave the island while the Wind Fish naps. ...By the by, have you ever visited the Tail Cave, which is south of the village? Go there with the key you find in this forest... The Wind Fish is watching...Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref>

Upon getting the {{Term|LANS|Full Moon Cello|link}} from {{Term|LANS|Tail Cave|link}}, the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} would once again appears.<ref name="Instrument"/> He mentions that at first he did not believe that {{Term|LANS|Link}} was real, suggesting that he believed that he was actually a part of the dream world, as {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}} is a mere illusion dreamed up by the sleeping {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}.<ref name="Instrument"/> However, since {{Term|LANS|Link}} had gotten the {{Term|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens}}, it would seem that the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} now believes that {{Term|LANS|Link}} is in fact real. When explaining what the {{Term|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens}} is, the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} also mentions that he was given instructions to guide {{Term|LANS|Link}}.<ref name="Instrument"/>

After getting the fifth {{Term|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens}}, the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} will then instruct {{Term|LANS|Link}} to the {{Term|LANS|Southern Face Shrine|link}}, where ancient ruins speak of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! There are two shrines, one to the north, the other to the south. First, head south, where ancient ruins speak of the Wind Fish... You will learn much there...|Owl|LADX}}</ref> After defeating the {{Term|LANS|Armos Knight|link}} there, {{Term|LANS|Link}} discovers some writing and a drawing on the wall. The wall reveal that {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island}} is a dream of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} and by awakening him would make the island disappear. The wall depict a drawing of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} as well as the {{Term|LANS|Owl}}. The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} outside reassures {{Term|LANS|Link}} by telling him that no one is really sure whether the foretelling is true or not, and the only way to find out would be to awaken the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}. The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} then tells {{Term|LANS|Link}} to trust his feelings and then flies off.<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! I see you have read the relief... While it does say the island is but a dream of the Wind Fish, no one is really sure... Just as you cannot know if a chest holds treasure until you open it, so you cannot tell if this is a dream until you awaken... The only one who knows for sure is the Wind Fish... Trust your feelings... Someday you will know for sure...|Owl|LADX}}</ref>

The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} is also quite aware of the "{{Term|LANS|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}", the song which needs to be played with the eight {{Plural|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens}} to awaken the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}. After {{Term|LANS|Link}} completes {{Term|LANS|Eagle's Tower|link}}, he encounters {{Term|LANS|Marin|link}}, who often sings the "{{Term|LANS|Ballad of the Wind Fish}}" in the {{Term|LANS|Tal Tal Mountain Range|link}}. After saving her from some monsters, the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} thinks that she could have been trying to awaken the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} with her song,<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! That girl sang her song in front of the Egg! Her 'Ballad of the Wind Fish' is a song of awakening! Did she actually intend to wake the Wind Fish?! The next Sirens' Instrument is in the west. Play your melodies so the unliving stones might hear! Show your courage! The Wind Fish waits for you! Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref> which seems to be true as {{Term|LANS|Marin}} had revealed earlier her wish of becoming a {{Term|LANS|Seagull|link}} and hoped that the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} would make her wish come true someday.<ref>{{Cite|They say the 'Ballad of the Wind Fish' is a song of awakening. I wonder, if the Wind Fish wakes up, will he make my wish come true?|Marin|LADX}}</ref>

After getting all of the {{Plural|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens}}, {{Term|LANS|Link}} goes in front of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish's Egg|link}} and plays the "{{Term|LANS|Ballad of the Wind Fish}}" on the {{Plural|LANS|Instrument of the Sirens}}. By doing so, an opening cracks open in the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish's Egg}} and the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} flies down, telling {{Term|LANS|Link}} to go inside.<ref>{{Cite|The time has come... The Wind Fish awaits... Enter the Egg... Hoot! Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref> After completing the maze and defeating the final {{Term|LANS|Nightmare|link}} inside, a stairway opens which takes {{Term|LANS|Link}} to the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}'s room. There, the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} appears to {{Term|LANS|Link}} for the final time.<ref name="Wind Fish">{{Cite|Hoot! Young lad, I mean... Link , the hero! You have defeated the Nightmares! You have proven your wisdom, courage and power! ... ... ... ... As part of the Wind Fish's spirit, I am the guardian of his dream world... But one day, the Nightmares entered the dream and began wreaking havoc. Then you, Link , came to rescue the island... I have always trusted in your courage to turn back the Nightmares. Thank you, Link ... My work is done... The Wind Fish will wake soon. Good bye...Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref> The {{Term|LANS|Owl}} then reveals that he was in fact part of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}'s spirit. As the spirit of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}, the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} was also the guardian of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}'s dream world.<ref name="Wind Fish"/> He also explains that {{Plural|LANS|Nightmare}} had invaded the dream world and had probably been the cause of the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}}'s inability to awake.<ref name="Wind Fish"/> As the {{Term|LANS|Wind Fish}} is about to awaken, the {{Term|LANS|Owl}} thanks {{Term|LANS|Link}} one last time and wishes him farewell before fading away.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl}} first appears after {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} finds his {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Sword|link}}, asking if he is the messenger of awakening that has come to awaken the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish|link}}. After {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} retrieves the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Full Moon Cello|link}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl}} introduces him to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Felicia|link}} to assist and look after him. Throughout {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}}'s journey in {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|link}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl}} serves as his guide, telling him where his next destination is.
{{Noncanon|End}}
