The {{Term/Store|Bridge of Eldin||Series, BotW}},{{Exp Game|TP, BotW}}{{Fact}}<ref>{{Cite|Bridge of Eldin|Map|BotW}}</ref> also known as '''Eldin Bridge''',{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|He should be at {{Color|BotW Red|Eldin Bridge}}.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the Bridge of Eldin is a bridge found north of [[Kakariko Village]]. It crosses a deep gorge where [[Zora's River]] runs through, essentially connecting the [[Eldin Province]] to a path leading to the [[Lanayru Province]].<ref name=quote>{{Cite|I would try to find the {{Color|TP Red|path}} beyond the {{Color|TP Red|Bridge of Eldin}} that leads to Lanayru Province.|Gor Coron|TP}}</ref> It also serves as the main access road to the [[Hidden Village]].<ref name=quote/> 

After [[King Bulblin]] suddenly kidnaps [[Colin]] from Kakariko Village, he makes his way to the Bridge of Eldin, with [[Link]] in pursuit. Here, the two engage in horseback combat in which Link comes out victorious and rescues Colin from the [[Bulblin]]'s grasp. Once the young hero clears the [[Goron Mines]] and crosses this Bridge en route to the next province, a significant portion of the Bridge is removed by [[Zant]]'s [[Shadow Beast]]s, thus blocking the path. Defeating the beasts causes a [[Twilight Portal]] to be created, but it cannot be used due to the Bridge's removal.

Link eventually finds what appears to be the removed section of the Bridge of Eldin in the {{Exp|GameCube version; eastern part in the Wii version|western part}} of the [[Gerudo Desert]], right on the [[Gerudo Mesa]]. With the aid of [[Midna]], the young hero [[warp]]s back to the area where the Bridge of Eldin was removed, taking the section with them.<ref>{{Cite|What is this thing? It does seem to be man-made. I wonder what it is? Why is it there? It doesn't look like it's been there long, does it?|Midna|TP}}</ref> However, if Link did not defeat the Shadow Beasts that appeared after the Bridge was removed, the portal will not appear and thus they will be unable to warp the missing section of the Bridge. Once the portal is created, Midna will place the section back where it belongs, allowing Link to cross the Bridge again.

Once Link receives the [[Ancient Sky Book]] from [[Shad]], he will have to return to the Bridge of Eldin in order to receive a [[Ancient Sky Book#Sky Characters|Sky Character]] from the [[Owl Statue]] situated in the northern section of the bridge. Moreover, by transporting the statue to the other side of the Bridge and situating it exactly below a certain ledge will allow Link to jump over and obtain a [[Piece of Heart]].

A [[Golden Bugs|male Golden Phasmid]] can also be found in the northern portion of the Bridge of Eldin.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, the Bridge of Eldin is a bridge that crosses over [[Death Mountain]]'s lava moat in the [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]] Region, connecting Death Mountain with [[Eldin Canyon]]. It is denoted as Death Mountain Marker #8.<ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #8<br/>Bridge of Eldin|Sign|BotW}}</ref> A road leading from [[Goron City]] paves the way to the Bridge through the [[Abandoned North Mine]] and [[Stolock Bridge]]. On the western side of the Bridge of Eldin is a [[Cannon]], one of many stationed around Death Mountain that the [[Goron]]s use to drive away [[Divine Beast Vah Rudania]].<ref>{{Cite|I can't tell ya how many times we've used the {{Color|BotW Red|cannon}} to chase off that fiend.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref>

The Bridge is initially inaccessible as [[Bludo]], the Gorons' Boss, has drawn the Bridge to prevent Vah Rudania from crossing over.<ref>{{Cite|The problem is...good ol' Boss raised the {{Color|BotW Red|Bridge of Eldin}} to make sure that mean Divine Beast couldn't get over here.|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> After Link saves [[Yunobo]] a second time from a pair of [[Black Moblin]]s at the Bridge's Cannon, and informs him of Bludo's inability to come and use the Cannon, the two decide to attack Vah Rudania themselves so that Link can board it.<ref>{{Cite|WHAT?! You're gonna try to board the Divine Beast? Who do you think you are...{{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}}? It's dangerous in there, goro! <small>Though I guess if you weakened it first, it might be possible...</small> But...why risk it?! [...] Hmm. Well, I don't really get it, but I'm sure you have your reasons, goro...|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> Yunobo uses [[Daruk's Protection]] and instructs Link to fire him at the Bridge using the Cannon to lower it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! It occurs to me that YOU know how to use the cannons! Don't you, goro? <small>Maybe we CAN get across after all...</small> So let's just do what I do to help {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} sometimes. That means I'm gonna be the cannonball! And you'll launch me, goro! [...] I've got {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk's Protection}}, goro! My ancestor's help can't possibly fail me!|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So rest easy, got it? Well, go on then! Take aim at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bridge of Eldin}} and fire away, goro!|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> From there on, the Bridge becomes accessible.

==Spinoff appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{LCT|-}}===
In {{LCT}}, the Bridge of Eldin appears as a Defender Mode scenario in [[Link's Crossbow Training#Level 6|Level 6]]. Link is chased by [[Bokoblin]]s, some riding on [[Bullbos]] and firing flaming arrows. [[Kargarok]]s and Bokoblins will also chase after him. Link's mission is to safely cross the Bridge.

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub|HW}}
In {{HW}}, the Bridge of Eldin appears as part of the [[Twilight Field]] stage. In the [[Land of Twilight]] [[Scenario]], Midna appears as an enemy fighting [[Lana]]. During the scenario, Midna will appear on the Bridge of Eldin.
{{Ambig|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{TP}} during the horseback portion of the battle against [[Ganondorf]], the Bridge will appear destroyed regardless of whether or not it was repaired.
*In some copies of {{TP|-}}, there is a game breaking glitch which prevents Link from advancing to the Lanayru Province. If the game is saved and then quit after the Bridge has been destroyed, but before entering the [[Twilight]], Link will appear on the south side of the bridge after opening the file, making it impossible to continue through the game.
