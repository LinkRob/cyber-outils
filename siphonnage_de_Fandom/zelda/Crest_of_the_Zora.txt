The {{Term/Store|Crest of the Zora |Crests of the Zora|Series}}, is a recurring [[Symbol]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is comprised of three scales, alluding to the [[Zora]] race's connection to water.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 48}}</ref> It also resembles both the [[Zora's Sapphire]] and the symbol of [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]].
