The {{Term/Store|Ze Kasho Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ze Kasho Shrine}}<br/>Ze Kasho Apparatus}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Ze Kasho Apparatus''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Ze Kasho Shrine}} can be found on a ridge above the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he will hear its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ze Kasho|link}}, introduce the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ze Kasho. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Ze Kasho|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
At the start of the {{Term|BotW|Ze Kasho Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle|link}} and a platform with {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes|link}} all across it. Below this platform is a bottomless pit. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes interacts with the {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}}, he will be able to move the platform. However, the {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}} will also move along rails as the platform is tilted. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must tilt the platform in a certain way so that he has a path through the {{Term|BotW|Floor Spikes}}.

The next room has several lasers shooting across it, in addition to two black platforms embedded in the ground. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits the nearby {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}}, the platforms will flip themselves, which temporarily blocks the lasers. While they are blocked, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to move forward. However, the platforms will rotate too quickly for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to cross in one movement. In order to cross entirely, he must again hit the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} by throwing a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} or [[Remote Bomb]], or shooting it with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}.

At the end of this passageway, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear|link}} can be found on a high platform to the right of the exit door. To acquire the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must again hit the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} with a ranged attack and quickly jump onto the nearby platform that rotates along the walkway. Once the platform stops at its highest point, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to glide to the platform that holds the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}.

In the final room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must again take control of a {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}}. In this one, he must roll three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} to hit three {{Plural|BotW|Floor Switch|link}}. Once all are activated at the same time, the door to {{Term|BotW|Ze Kasho}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} will open permanently.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Ze Kasho}} will commend him for overcoming the trial and give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ze Kasho|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before disappearing.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ze Kasho|BotW}}</ref>
