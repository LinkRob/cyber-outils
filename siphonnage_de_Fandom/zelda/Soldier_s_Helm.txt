==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Soldier's Helm is a part of the [[Soldier's Set]], in combination with the [[Soldier's Armor]] and [[Soldier's Greaves]]. It's sold at [[Ventest Clothing Boutique]] in [[Hateno Village]]. It can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] and upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. It offers no effect aside from defense.
