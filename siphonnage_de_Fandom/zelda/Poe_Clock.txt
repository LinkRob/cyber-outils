==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|OoA|Poe Clock|It is said that one who is able to help a wandering spirit move on to the afterlife will be given this strange clock in return. Surely someone, somewhere, needs a clock.}}
The {{Term|OoA|Poe Clock}} is given to {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} by {{Term|OoA|Allan|link}} in {{Term|OoA|Yoll Graveyard|link}} next to a {{Term|OoA|Rock|link}} near the {{Term|OoA|Spirit's Grave|link}}. If {{Term|OoA|Link}} moves the headstone blocking the {{Term|OoA|Poe|link}}'s tomb, it will give {{Term|OoA|Link}} the {{Term|OoA|Poe Clock}}.
