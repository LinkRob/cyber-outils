'''''Nintendo Sound History Series: Zelda The Music''''' (サウンドヒストリーシリーズ「ゼルダ ザ ミュージック」) is a compilation album of music from various ''Zelda'' games, including {{TLoZ}}, {{TAoL}}, {{ALttP}}, {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, {{TWW}} and {{FSA}}. The album has 70 tracks and a running time of 1 hour, 18 minutes. It was released in Japan in 2004 by '''Scitron Digital Content Inc.''' as part of the ''Nintendo Sound History Series''.
