{{Term/Store|Nice Bomb|Nice Bombs|Series, ALBW|plural}} are items in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ALBW|Nice Bomb}} are an upgrade to {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb|link}}. The upgrade can be obtained after collecting at least 10 {{Plural|ALBW|Lost Maiamai|link}} and then returning them to {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}. The {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb}} first must be bought from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio|link}} in order to upgrade them. {{Plural|ALBW|Nice Bomb}} have a larger blast radius and cause more damage to {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} than {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb}}.
