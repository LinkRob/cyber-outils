{{Term/Store|Keye||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Keye|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Keye}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} child who resides within {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. During the day, he plays near {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|link}}. At night, he and his brother, {{Term|BotW|Tumbo|link}}, rest in the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Zzz... Zzz...}}|Keye|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially speaks to {{Term|BotW|Keye}}, he will ask who he is.<ref>{{Cite|Um... Who are you?|Keye|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that he is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Keye}} will not be very impressed.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm a Hylian.] Oh...|Keye|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is unable to answer, {{Term|BotW|Keye}} will accuse him of copying how he speaks.<ref>{{Cite|[Um...] Hey! Don't copy me!|Keye|BotW}}</ref> Upon getting a closer look, however, he realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Now that I have a closer look, I see you're a Hylian...|Keye|BotW}}</ref>

Knowing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}}, {{Term|BotW|Keye}} then asks if he came from far away.<ref>{{Cite|So if you're a Hylian, did you come from far away?|Keye|BotW}}</ref> He starts to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} the reason for his question, but he decides not to continue, since his father, {{Term|BotW|Fronk|link}}, told him not to talk to strangers.<ref>{{Cite|I ask because my... ... Oh, never mind. My dad told me not to talk to strangers.|Keye|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to ask him what is wrong, {{Term|BotW|Keye}} will claim that it is not the business of a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite|[What's wrong?] It's not a Hylian's business...|Keye|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Keye}} standing in front of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}} with {{Term|BotW|Laruta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you need something?|Keye|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what he is doing, {{Term|BotW|Keye}} explains that he is practicing to use a spear.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Spear practice...|Keye|BotW}}</ref> He does a jump to demonstrate his abilities, but he laments that he is unable to reach the {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|display=Statue's}} pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|Hyah! But I can't even jump high enough to reach Lady Mipha's {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}.|Keye|BotW}}</ref> In frustration, he admits to having seen the pedestal somewhere else before.<ref>{{Cite|Urgh, that {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}! I feel like I've seen it somewhere before...|Keye|BotW}}</ref> He thinks there may have been a similar pedestal at {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}}, and he wants to be able to jump high enough to pierce it.<ref>{{Cite|Was there a pedestal at {{Color|BotW Blue|Veiled Falls}}? I want to be able to jump high enough to {{Color|BotW Red|pierce}} that {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}}...|Keye|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Mei|link}} has returned to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, {{Term|BotW|Keye}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he hopes he will help his family if something happens to them again.<ref>{{Cite|Mom... She went far away for work... Mister... If something else happens to us, I hope you'll be around to help again...|Keye|BotW}}</ref>
