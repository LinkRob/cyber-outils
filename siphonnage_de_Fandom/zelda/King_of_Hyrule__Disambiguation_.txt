The King of Hyrule refers to the current ruling monarch of the Kingdom of [[Hyrule]], usually father to [[Princess Zelda]]. Given that the games in {{TLoZ|Series}} span a large period of time, there have been a number of individually identifiable Kings of Hyrule:

*'''[[King of Hyrule]]''', the recurring character type seen across the series, also featuring Kings without their own pages.
*'''[[King of Hyrule (The Adventure of Link)]]''', an unnamed King of Hyrule who ruled Hyrule with the united Triforce at his command and presided over a golden age of the kingdom before his death.
*'''[[Prince of Hyrule]]''', the son of the aforementioned king, who inherited his throne and title.
*'''[[Royal Family of Hyrule#A Link to the Past|Un-named King]]''' in {{ALttP}} as shown in the credits.
*'''[[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]]''', the final King of old Hyrule in the adult timeline.
*'''[[King Daltus|Daltus]]''', the King of Hyrule during {{TMC}}.
*'''[[King Gustaf|Gustaf]]''', an ancient King of Hyrule who appears in {{TMC}}, long after his death.
*'''[[King Rhoam|Rhoam Bosphoramus Hyrule]]''', the Last King of Hyrule in {{BotW}}.
*'''[[King Rauru]]''', the First King of Hyrule in {{TotK}}.
*'''[[King Harkinian|Harkinian]]''', the name given to the King of Hyrule who appeared in [[The Legend of Zelda (TV Series)]], the [[Philips CD-i Games]], and the [[Valiant Comics]].
*'''[[King of Hyrule (The Shadow Prince)]]''', the King of Hyrule in {{TSP}}.
