{{Plural|BotW|Radiant Shield}} are mid-grade {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} made by the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}}. They are presented to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} warriors once they achieve the rank of captain, and they are considered to be a direct upgrade to {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This is seen with {{Term|BotW|Teake|link}}, the captain of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} guard, who carries one on her back. In addition, they are also known for their incredible {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} abilities.{{Fact|BotW}} Their opulence is rivaled only by their capabilities in combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and utilized by the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

There are four {{Plural|BotW|Radiant Shield}} scattered throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, with a fifth added in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}.{{Where}} One can be found inside the secret cave in the mountain east of the {{Term|BotW|Sho Dantu Shrine|link}}, along with other pieces of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear}}. 

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shield}}:
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

{{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} will comment on {{Plural|BotW|Radiant Shield}} if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her with one equipped. She reveals that they are very rare, and she practically never sees them anymore.<ref>{{Cite|That's a radiant shield! You wouldn't believe how rare those things are! You practically never see them anymore...|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> Unlike other shields, the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shield}} can {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|display=Shield Surf}} on stone.

Due to their metal compositions, {{Plural|BotW|Radiant Shield}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. However, they can be manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
