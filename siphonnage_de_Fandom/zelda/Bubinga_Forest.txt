{{Term/Store|Bubinga Forest||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Bubinga Forest'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Bubinga Forest is located in [[West Necluda]], just east of the [[Dueling Peaks Stable]] and south of [[Blatchery Plain]]. A majority of the Forest grows on sloped terrain, with the [[Squabble River]] curving around its southern base.

One [[Korok]] is found using a [[Natural Hiding Spot]] under a [[Rock]] at the top of a large [[Tree]] in the northwest corner of the Forest. Another Korok is found near the center of the Forest, by stepping on a rock with a pinwheel on it and shooting the three [[Balloon]]s that appear.
