The {{Term/Store|Bird Lover||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Bird Lover can be found under the bridge just east of [[Link's House]]. He lives in a tent under the bridge, which he prefers over a normal house. As his name suggests, he is fond of [[bird]]s, and many of them flock around him. Walking up to him makes them fly off, causing him to question why [[Link]] would do that. Speaking with him further can award Link with a [[Bottle]], which the Bird Lover describes as causing "too much excitement" for him.

==Trivia==
*The Bird Lover appears to be based on the Camper found at the same place in {{ALttP}}.
*He bares a strong resemblance to [[King Harkinian|King Harkanian]] from the Zelda CD-i games.
