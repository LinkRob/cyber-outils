{{Term/Store|King Zora||Series, ALttP, OoT, OoT3D, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228,{{Exp Game|ALttP}} 238,{{Exp Game|OoT}} & 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> He is the leader of the [[Zora]] race.

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:ALttP King Zora Sprite.png|left]]
In {{ALttP}}, the King Zora of the [[River Zora]] species is found at [[Zora's Waterfall]]. Unlike his kin, he is a friendly character with a generous, predictable laugh.<ref>{{Cite|Great! Whenever you want to see my fishy face, you are welcome here.|King Zora|ALttP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wah ha ha! What do you want, little man?|King Zora|ALttP}}</ref> He will sell the [[Flippers]] to [[Link]] for a pricey 500 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Wah ha ha! But I don't just give flippers away for free. I sell them for 500 Rupees a pair.|King Zora|ALttP}}</ref> As a bonus, he tells Link that he can use the [[Whirlpool Waterway]]s to [[Warping|warp]] from one body of water to another in [[Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|I will give you a free bonus with your purchase. I will let you use the  magic water ways of the sea folk which link lakes and rivers. When you see a whirlpool, dive into it. You never know where you'll surface! Wah ha ha!|King Zora|ALttP}}</ref> The young hero must meet him and purchase Zora's Flippers<ref>{{Cite|You bought Zora's Flippers!|N/A|ALttP}}</ref> in order to complete his quest; if Link does not have enough Rupees for the Flippers, the King will kindly encourage him to find more before returning.<ref>{{Cite|Wade back this way when you have more Rupees... Wah ha ha! I'll see you again!|King Zora|ALttP}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, King Zora sits on his throne at the helm of [[Zora's Domain]], guarding passage to the great [[Lord Jabu-Jabu]], the patron deity of the Zoras.<ref>{{Cite|Zora's Fountain is just beyond King Zora's throne. That is where Lord Jabu-Jabu swims. But... unless you have King Zora's permission, you can't go to Zora's Fountain.|Zora|OoT}}</ref> Only when Link presents him with a [[Letter in a Bottle|letter]] from his missing daughter, [[Princess Ruto]], who claims to be inside Lord Jabu Jabu, does he grant the young hero passage to [[Zora's Fountain|Lord Jabu Jabu's lair]] to rescue her.<ref>{{Cite|Ho, this letter! It's from Princess Ruto!! Hmmm...Let's see... She's inside Lord Jabu-Jabu? That's not possible! Our guardian god, Lord Jabu-Jabu, would never eat my dear Princess Ruto! But since that stranger, Ganondorf, came here, Lord Jabu-Jabu has been a little green around the gills... The evidence seems clear. Of course, you'll go find Ruto. You can pass through here to the altar  of Lord Jabu-Jabu. I'll keep this letter. You keep the bottle it was in. Take it  respectfully! Please find my dear Princess Ruto immediately... Zora!|King Zora|OoT}}</ref> When the [[Hero of Time]] ventures to Zora's Domain, he finds the Zora monarch and the rest of the region frozen solid: a curse placed on the region that stems from [[Morpha]]'s control of the {{Term|OoT3D|Water Temple|link}}, the source of their water. Freeing King Zora from the [[Red Ice]] with [[Blue Flame|Blue Fire]] after completing the {{Term|OoT3D|Ice Cavern|link}} will grant Link a free [[Zora Tunic]] as a gift.<ref>{{Cite|Oh--I've come back to life! Was it you who saved me? Don't be nervous! It looks like you have a hard time breathing underwater. As an expression of my  gratitude, I grant you this tunic. With this, you won't choke underwater.|King Zora|OoT}}</ref> If Link already has the special tunic, he will offer him a kiss instead, which the hero refuses.<ref>{{Cite|What?! You already have one? You're ready for anything, aren't you! With my heartfelt thanks...how about a KISS?! What? You don't want it?! Oh well...|King Zora|OoT}}</ref>  

Incidentally, it appears that, according to in-game dialog, King Zora and [[Biggoron]] are good friends and have known each other for a long time.<ref name="Biggoron">{{Cite|Talk about a nostalgia trip! That reminds me of my childhood friend, Biggoron!|King Zora|OoT}}</ref> During the ending sequence of {{OoT|-}}, King Zora is shown to be one of the few characters not celebrating the victory over [[Ganon]]'s defeat, instead sitting next to a sorrowful [[Mido]], both depressed at the disappearance of Ruto and [[Saria]] (as they were not aware of them being [[Sage|Sages]]). However, as the lights of five the Six Sages fly above including Princess Ruto and Saria themselves, their heads raise skywards in recognition as they soar towards [[Death Mountain]].

{{HH}} states this King Zora's full name as '''Zora De Bon XVI'''.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The king Link meets in this era is known as Zora De Bon XVI. His daughter is Princess Ruto.|book=HH|page= 88}}</ref>

==={{OoA|-}}===
[[File:King Zora Past.png|left|72px|King Zora in the past]]
In {{OoA}}, {{Term|OoA|King Zora}} resides in {{Term|OoA|King Zora's Palace|link}}. The Zora are at first leaderless, as King Zora died 400 years ago due to an illness caused by an overwhelming amount of sea pollution. To save him, Link must travel to the past and give him a [[Magic Potion]]. After returning to the present, King Zora will grant Link permission to enter Jabu-Jabu if the young hero cleanses the seas, handing over the [[Library Key]] as well. It's uncertain is if he recognizes him or not.
==={{TP|-}}===
King Zora in {{TP}} is long dead. He was [[Rutela]]'s husband and the creator of the [[Zora Armor]].<ref name=armor>{{Cite|During his lifetime, my husband created garments specifically for the chosen hero, garments that house the abilities of the Zora.|Rutela|TP}}</ref> His grave is located in the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard]] and is where Link is given the Zora Armor by the ghost of Queen Rutela.

It is thought by some fans this is the same King Zora as in {{OoT|-}}, due to a quote from an interviewer talking to the developers.<ref name=Quote1>[http://www.thehylia.com/index.php?subaction=showfull&id=1173582355&archive=&start_from=&ucat=19& Nintendo Dream: Eiji Aonuma Interview 1]: ''"There’s  a reference to King Zora (the king of the Zora race in Ocarina of Time;  his official name is Do Bon, the third), and there are some pictures of the man from the fishing pond (the owner of a fishing business near Lake Hylia in Ocarina of Time)."''</ref> Queen Rutela also states how her husband created "garments specifically for the chosen hero" that would allow him to breathe underwater, hinting at the [[Zora Tunic]] in {{OoT|-}}.<ref name=armor/> However, there is no indication that King Zora of the [[Nintendo 64]] installment actually crafted these tunics, and only offers his as a gift while they can be bought in the [[Zora Shop]].

==={{EoW|-}}===
In {{EoW}}, two different Zora rulers, one for the River Zoras [[Dradd]] and one for the Sea Zoras [[Kushara]], each command their respective tribe. Contrary to prior Zora rulers being referred to with royal titles, Dradd and Kushara are instead Chiefs.

==Trivia==
* Wearing the [[Goron Mask]] while speaking to King Zora reveals that [[Biggoron]] and King Zora are best friends, and have known each other since childhood.<Ref name="Biggoron"/>[[File:MM Toto Model.png|thumb|Toto, King Zora's counterpart from {{MM|-}}]]
* The King Zora in {{OoT}}, has a [[Terminan]] counterpart in {{MM}} named [[Toto]], who is the manager of [[The Indigo-Go's]]. In comparison, he is much smaller and wears a cane. His daughter [[Princess Ruto]] also has a counterpart, [[Lulu]], who is its lead singer.
* Unlike Ruto who becomes excited when [[Link]] wears the [[Zora Mask]], King Zora will react in disgust, saying he is making a mockery of him.
* The infamous sequence in which King Zora very slowly moves aside in {{OoT|-}} so as to allow Link entry to [[Zora's Fountain]] alongside making his comical "''[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_KingZora_Slide2.wav Owink!]''" sound is possibly one of the most exaggerated cutscenes in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], lasting about 30 seconds long. It has become a meme among fans who played the game and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|remake]].
* In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', King Zora's eyes are red like in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]'' artwork. In the original [[Nintendo 64|N64]] version, however, they were purple like Ruto's.
* When Princess Ruto gives Link the [[Hero of Time]] the [[Spiritual Stone of Water|Zora's Sapphire]], she tells him off-screen not to tell her father, as the [[Spiritual Stone]] also serves as the "Zora's Engagement Ring" as explained by her unseen mother. This implies she was not supposed to become engaged to others not of her race (as Link himself is a [[Hylian]]). Despite this, King Zora is never aware of it.
** In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|{{OoT|-}} manga]] by [[Akira Himekawa]], she tells her father that she had given the Zora's Sapphire to someone but not saying it was Link, in which King Zora reacts in shock.
** If Link were to actually marry Princess Ruto, the ''Zelda'' series' main protagonist would become his son-in-law.
