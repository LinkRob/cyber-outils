The{{Term/Store|Temple Remains||Series, OoS}} are a location in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 251}}</ref> It is part of [[Holodrum]]'s northern mountain range, located between [[Tarm Ruins]] and [[Goron Mountain]]. The Temple Remains are the former locale of the [[Temple of Seasons]], which [[General Onox]] caused to sink into [[Subrosia]] when he captured [[Din (Oracle)|Din]]. The General of Darkness has his [[Onox's Castle|castle]] built on the western peak of the remains, known as [[Northern Peak]].<ref>{{Cite|I sense evil on the Northern Peak, where the Temple of Seasons rested! Onox has cast a shadow on the Northern Peak. I think you will find him there.|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref>

==Navigation==
[[File:OOS VolcanoEruption 2.png|thumb|left|The volcanic eruption fills the remains with lava]]
[[File:SubrosiaPortal.png|thumb|right|[[Portal (Object)|Portal]] to [[Subrosia]] atop the remains]]
The Temple Remains are the last area of Holodrum to be explored by Link. At the very top of the remains there is a turret containing a [[Portal (Object)|Portal]] to [[Subrosia]]. This portal leads to the totally isolated [[Sword & Shield Maze]], the dungeon containing the final [[Essence of Nature]].

At first, parts of the remains are impossible to navigate due to several trees blocking an opening that leads inside the ruins' walls. By changing the season to autumn, pitfalls in the ruins become covered by leaves.<ref name="Guide 52">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 52}}</ref> This allows Link to walk up the western side of the remains. By then changing the season to winter and walking across a snowdrift, Link gains access to the first portal, which leads to a previously unexplored area of the [[Subrosian Volcanoes]].<ref name="Guide 52"/> Inside a cave, the young hero encounters a [[Subrosian]] throwing various items into a pit.<ref name="Guide 53">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS|publisher= Nintendo|page= 53}}</ref> Link must throw a bomb into the pit, causing all the volcanoes in Subrosia to erupt.<ref name="Guide 53"/> When Link returns to Holodrum, he discovers that the ruins have now been filled with lava.<ref name="Guide 53"/> The magma—in addition to several [[Tektite]]s and [[Darknut]]s roaming the walls—makes navigating the ruins treacherous, but it also destroys the obstructive trees blocking Link's path. By changing the season to summer, Link can use [[vines]] to climb up the eastern side and go inside the ruins through the opening.<ref name="Guide 53"/> Travelling through the cavernous interior of the remains (with use of the [[Roc's Cape]] and [[Magnetic Glove]]s) eventually leads to a staircase that will take Link to the top of the ruins. After fighting off a few [[Lynel]]s, Link discovers a pair of [[Fairy|fairies]] and the final portal to Subrosia inside the turret on the west side. On arrival in Subrosia, the young hero finds himself in front of the [[Sword & Shield Maze]].<ref name="Guide 53"/>

The eruption also weakens a section of a wall in the remains. Bombing it opens up a cave with a [[Piece of Heart]] inside.<ref name="Guide 53"/>
