{{Term/Store|Telephone Booth|Telephone Booths|Series, LA, LADX|plural}},{{Exp Game|LA}}<ref>{{Cite|Telephone Booth|Atlas of Koholint Island|LADX}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Phone Booth|Phone Booths|HWL, HWDE|plural}},{{Exp Game|HWL}}<ref name="HWDE Tutorial">{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Grandpa Ulrira}}: Use this on a Phone Booth to uncover hidden treaasures.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> are recurring locations in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Telephone Booth|Things would be very inconvenient even on a small island like Koholint without telephones. These phones are free, but whom do you suppose you can call?}}
{{Plural|LANS|Telephone Booth}} can be found all across {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}. They are communication devices used to speak with {{Term|LANS|Old Man Ulrira|link}}, who will give {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} various hints on how to progress on his quest.<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Hello! It's me, Ulrira! Ask me anything about the island! If you get lost, give me a call! Bye! CLICK!' |Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref>

When using the phone inside {{Term|LANS|Old Man Ulrira}}'s house, {{Term|LANS|Link}} dials the number of someone calling himself the {{Term|LANS|Bucket Mouse|link}}. After hanging up, a message appears saying that {{Term|LANS|Link}} must have dialed the wrong number.<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! BRRING! CLICK! Yeees! It's the Bucket Mouse! Thanks for calling! ...Well... CLICK!' ??? ... You must have dialed a wrong number... |Bucket Mouse|LADX}}</ref>
