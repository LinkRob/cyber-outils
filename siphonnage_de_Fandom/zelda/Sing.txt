'''Sing''' is a character appearing in the episode "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing for the Unicorn}}" of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. She is from a far away land who has come to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Hyrule|link}} to rescue her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn|link}} after it was stolen by {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}}.<ref name=sing>{{Cite Episode|quote=Her name is Sing. She's from a land far away. Ganon stole her unicorn and she's trying to rescue it.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Her weapon of choice is the [[wikipedia:sai (weapon)|sai]].

==Biography==
[[File:Sing (Sing for the Unicorn).png|thumb|left|Sing's first appearance in the episode]]
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} first meet {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} when they are in the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}} fighting {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos|link}}. When a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}} grabs {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} from behind, a mysterious warrior suddenly appears and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zap|link}}s the foe, along with the rest of the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Stalfos}}, using her {{Wp|sai}}. However, she soon begins to target {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} as well.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Link! What's that creature?|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Although the young hero attempts to shoot the warrior with a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam}}, he realizes that the disguised attacker is a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Human|link}} since the beam did not cause it to vanish.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I don't know, princess. But I'm gonna zap it! [...] Ow! Hey, that's no magic creature! It's a real person, like us!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Zelda then grabs the warrior and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} removes its mask, showing that it is a woman who does not speak {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} and Zelda's language.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=She doesn't speak our language.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Zelda is able to communicate with her and reveals that her name is {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}}, who is trying to rescue her unicorn that {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}} stole.<ref name=sing/> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} agrees to help her and uses a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Bomb|link}} to blast his way into {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon's Lair|link}}, where together they continue to look for {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}}'s {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}} as well as {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian|link}} who was captured earlier on.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Well, we WERE on a rescue mission anyway. So, let's make it a party! C'mon!|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> 

Eventually, the heroes find that the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}} is being guarded by {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}}, which {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} easily defeats by zapping them away.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Good job, Sing! We've got your unicorn.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> With her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}} rescued, she uses it to save {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian}} from his doom in a so-called pit room.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Daddy! Sing! You saved him! Thank you, thank you!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> With everyone now safe, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} uses a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Recorder|display=magic whistle|link}} to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Warping|display=warp|link}} everyone out of the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Underworld|link}}, including {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}}. At the end of the episode, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sing}} waves goodbye to the King, Zelda and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} as she's riding away on her {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Unicorn}}.
