"{{Term/Store|Special Delivery||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Special Delivery}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Bank of Wishes}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Finley|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes|link}} with her {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|link}}. She asks the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|display=Letter}} to carry her thoughts.<ref>{{Cite|Carry my thoughts...to him...|Finley|BotW}}</ref> When she notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Finley}} asks him if he also has a wish for the water fairy,<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Do you have a wish for the water fairy too?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> but she realizes this is not the case since he does not have a letter.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... I suppose not. If you did, you'd have a letter to send.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, she deduces that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be a servant to the water fairy.<ref>{{Cite|But wait, does that mean you're... a servant of the water fairy?!|Finley|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms her thoughts, {{Term|BotW|Finley}} dismisses it and tells him not to make fun of her because she is cute.<ref>{{Cite|That can't possibly be so... Please don't make fun of me just because I'm a cute girl.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Since he is a traveler,<ref>{{Cite|If you're a traveler, that's lucky for me! OH, WOW! If that's the case, I have an important request for you.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Finley}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to follow her {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} and find out who picks it up, as she would like to know.<ref>{{Cite|Once I release it, I'd like you to follow my {{Color|BotW Red|love letter}} and get to know whoever picks it up. Once you do that, please report back to me so I can find out what kind of person he is...|Finley|BotW}}</ref> She can not go herself, since she promised {{Term|BotW|Kodah|link|display=her mother}} she would not stray too far from {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I know, I know. You're probably thinking, "Why not just go yourself, lazy girl?!" I'm right, aren't I? The thing is, I promised my mother I wouldn't venture out too far.|Finley|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Finley}} begs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find the person who picks up the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}}.<ref>{{Cite|So I'm begging you. Please... Find the person who picks up this letter. OK?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Once he agrees, {{Term|BotW|Finley}} prepares to throw the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|display=Letter}} into the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}} and asks if he is ready.<ref>{{Cite|[Fine...] Tee hee! Thank you! Excellent! OK, I'm going to throw the letter in now! Are you ready?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the container holding the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} breaks easily, so he should be careful handling it.<ref>{{Cite|These letter containers {{Color|BotW Red|break easily}}, so please be careful.|Finley|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} is cast into {{Term|BotW|Zora River}}, and so {{Term|BotW|Link}} is tasked with keeping it safe on its journey. On its path are [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemies]] that may destroy it, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either defeat or avoid. The {{Term|BotW|Love Letter|display=Letter}} can also get stuck at several points along the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|display=River}}, which similarly must be destroyed or avoided.

If the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} is broken or lost, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Finley}} at the {{Term|BotW|Bank of Wishes}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I can't see the letter.] You lost sight of my {{Color|BotW Red|love letter}}, didn't you?|Finley|BotW}}</ref> Having predicted this, {{Term|BotW|Finley}} made a copy,<ref>{{Cite|Luckily, I thought something like that might happen, so I made an extra.|Finley|BotW}}</ref> which she will throw into the {{Term|BotW|Zora River}} as well.

Eventually, the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}} will arrive at the camp of {{Term|BotW|Sasan|link}} at the mouth of the {{Term|BotW|Zora River|display=River}}, who asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has ever been to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry for the random question, but have you ever been to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}?|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> He asks how far it is from his camp,<ref>{{Cite|I see... Is it far from here?|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> and when he hears that it is, he introduces himself.<ref>{{Cite|[It's a long ways.] I see... Oh! Sorry. I forgot to introduce myself. I am Sasan, the traveling merchant.|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> On his way to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, he caught sight of {{Term|BotW|Finley}}'s first love letter,<ref>{{Cite|I was on my way to Zora's Domain... but a {{Color|BotW Red|love letter}} in this inlet caught my eye. The writer seems to be a lovely Zora named Finley.|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> and since then, he has been sending letters back to her via travelers.<ref>{{Cite|I've been corresponding with her by giving my replies to travelers heading to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}.|Sasan|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Sasan}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he was following the {{Term|BotW|Love Letter}},<ref>{{Cite|So...you were following this letter?|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> revealing that he lied about his appearance in his own replies.<ref>{{Cite|Hm, so she asked you to come down here and see what kind of person I am. Is that it? Well, earlier I lied when I wrote to her that I was a buff traveler. She said she wanted to meet me, but...|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> Though he wants to meet {{Term|BotW|Finley}}, if he does so now, she would figure out his lie.<ref>{{Cite|Even though I'm dying to meet her, I can't do it. I've really blow it. If I had known this would happen—that I'd fall for her—I never would've lied...|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s encouragement, {{Term|BotW|Sasan}} finds the motivation to go see her.<ref>{{Cite|[Love reigns supreme.] Love reigns supreme... Hmm. I suppose it does. Ya know, hearing that does make me feel a little better. OK! It's time for me to take a chance and try to meet her...|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> On his journey to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}, {{Term|BotW|Sasan}} hopes to train up his muscles.<ref>{{Cite|She's waiting in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}. That's what her letter says, anyway. So I'll achieve two goals at the same time and train up my muscles on the way there! I'll get super beefy! Yeah, that's the ticket! I'll run all the way to the domain! Catch ya later!|Sasan|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Sasan}} leaves his camp, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can head to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} to find him.<ref>{{Cite|We'll meet again at {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}—I'm sure of it!|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> He and {{Term|BotW|Finley}} both recognize him,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! It's you! Hey, Sasan! This is the guy who made sure my letter reached you safely!|Finley|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! It's you! As you can see, I somehow made it to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}.|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> They thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for bringing them together,<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I managed to work up the courage to finally meet Finley. At least, that's how I see it.|Sasan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much for introducing me to my soul mate!|Finley|BotW}}</ref> and they give him a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Sasan and I discussed it, and we decided we just HAD to thank you the next time we saw you. So here... This is from both of us. Thank you for everything!|Finley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Finley}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Sasan}} to work out and build up his muscles,<ref>{{Cite|Sasan! Be sure to swim and climb and lift and stuff. A LOT. That will help you build up those muscles you promised me!|Finley|BotW}}</ref> which he agrees to.<ref>{{Cite|As you wish...my darling... I'll try my best...|Sasan|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} ends.
