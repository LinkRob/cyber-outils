{{Term/Store|Ancient Fin|Ancient Fins|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 115"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Ancient Fin}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Dunes|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Ancient Fin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Ancient Fin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Ancient Fin}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Ancient Fin}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Ancient Fin}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Dunewalker Duds|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 115"/>
