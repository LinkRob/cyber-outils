{{Term/Store|Mr. Tudor||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref name= "Tips"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TFH|Mr. Tudor}} can be found next to the small libraries in each of the {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}} lobbies. He gives {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} tips on venturing out into the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. He also appears in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}} lobby, although the only tip he can offer there is how to {{Term|TFH|Blacklist|link}} other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}. When the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are traveling between {{Plural|TFH|Stage}} in a {{Term|TFH|Level}}, {{Term|TFH|Mr. Tudor}} briefly appears on-screen to give them some advice.

{{Term|TFH|Mr. Tudor}} has dreamed of being a renowned writer since he was a young boy.<ref>{{Cite|Hear this! The tutor at the castle dreamed of being a renowned writer when he was a lad. To create imaginative worlds with the stroke of a pen —that was his dream! He still writes what he sees everyday. Perhaps part of the joy of dreaming is the chase...|Bearded Baron|TFH}}</ref> He is the writer of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Diary|link}} under the pseudonym of '''Four Eyes'''.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305}}</ref>

At the conclusion of the game's ending, {{Term|TFH|Mr. Tudor}}'s hat and glasses are seen resting on a table alongside the storybook which had, just prior, illustrated the events of the game's story.

==Nomenclature==
Tudor is likely a corruption of "tutor", a person who gives private instructions. Tudor is also a name of Welsh origin, which was the Welsh form of the old Celtic name ''Teutorigos'', which meant "ruler of the people." It was notably the surname of five monarchs of England. Four Eyes is a common epithet for a person who wears glasses. Four Eyes's name in the UK, Bob-cut Four-eyes, further references {{Term|TFH|Mr. Tudor}} as he wears a bob-cut hair style.
