{{Term/Store|Patricia||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Patricia}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Best Sand Seal"/>

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Patricia}}
{{Term|BotW|Patricia}} is {{Term|BotW|Riju|display=Lady Riju|link}}'s favorite {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be there waiting for you with my favorite sand seal, Patricia.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She is blue and has a bow in her hair. {{Term|BotW|Patricia}} was a gift to {{Term|BotW|Riju}} from her mother before she passed.<ref name="Gift"/>

{{Term|BotW|Patricia}} can be found in the southwestern corner of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, just to the left of {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s throne. If offered fruit, she will share her wisdom with {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. She likes {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} in particular.<ref name="Wildberries"/> Her messages can only be translated by those who can truly communicate with her,<ref>{{Cite|Patricia has a mysterious power... If you feed some fruit to her, she will deliver divine messages. But the messages can only be understood by those who are able to truly communicate with her.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> usually the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Padda|link}}. She is fond of puns involving the word "Seal".<ref>{{Cite|Look to the top of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Highlands}} to sea... SEE... the path to a dragon. *sigh* I've spared you the seal pun.|Padda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Patricia}} and {{Term|BotW|Riju}} assist {{Term|BotW|Link}} in getting near the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} while disabling its legs with {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} to access the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} has finally been calmed! It sounds like our {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} and Patricia played a big part in the whole adventure, too!|Lorn|BotW}}</ref>

==={{TotK|-}}===
When {{Term|TotK|Gerudo Desert|link}} has been plagued with a severe sandstorm, Riju has been frequently taking Patricia out as her steed outside the Gerudo Desert. She was first taken to the {{Term|TotK|North Gerudo Ruins|link}} where Riju started practicing with her lightning strike attack, an ability she recently learned. During the sandstorm, Patricia was currently unavailable at her pen. Once the sandstorm lifted, Patricia was returned back to her pen.

Like the previous game, Patricia can be fed with {{Plural|TotK|fruit|link}} to share divine knowledge.<ref>{{Cite|Please, offer her some fruit. In return, she'll share a reading with you!|Padda|TotK}}</ref> For some reason, if fed with a {{Term|TotK|Splash Fruit|link}}, she says a series of three numbers;<ref>{{Cite|Though if you give her <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">splash fruit</font>, you'll almost certainly get a strange, number-filled reading.|Padda|TotK}}</ref> the meaning of the numbers is unknown to Gerudo people.

The numbers that Patricia communicates are in-game coordinates that correspond to various objectives. That last number triplet that Patricia says is "-0249, 1041, -2438", and that is the coordinates of the penultimate boss fight area, where Demon King Ganondorf is.
