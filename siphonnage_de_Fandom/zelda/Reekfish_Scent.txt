The {{Term/Store|Reekfish Scent||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an object in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Reekfish}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish Scent}} is the fourth {{Term|TPHD|Scent|link}} learned by {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} and it replaces the {{Term|TPHD|Poe Scent|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish Scent}} is learned in {{Term|TPHD|Zora's Domain|link}} near the {{Plural|TPHD|Mother-and-Child Rocks|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish Scent}} is colored red, and it is picked up from a live {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish|link}} after {{Term|TPHD|Fishing|link}} for one. The scent is used to track down the {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish}} that {{Term|TPHD|Yeto|link}} took to make a {{Term|TPHD|Simple Soup|link|display= Soup}} for his sick wife, {{Term|TPHD|Yeta|link}}, and also used to correctly navigate the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak|link}} base without getting lost in the blizzard. {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} can take a more direct route than {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} is capable of following, so {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} will need to find the trail again after a few detours. {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} can finally catch up with {{Term|TPHD|Yeto}} at the beginning of the {{Term|TPHD|Snowboard Race|link}} at {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Top|link}}.
