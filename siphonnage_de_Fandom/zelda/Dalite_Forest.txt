{{Term/Store|Dalite Forest||Series, BotW}}is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Dalite Forest'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Dalite Forest is located in the [[Hyrule Ridge]]. It is a large forest that is bisected by a road that leads to [[Nima Plain]]. A pond sits along the southwestern reaches of the Forest, separated by a small rock hill.

Along the path that divides the Forest, two [[Korok]]s can be found. The first one is found by offering an [[Apple]] at the statues on the roadside, while the second one can be found by hitting an [[Acorn]]-shaped {{Term|BotW|Vase|link}} suspended by a chain on a tall [[Tree]] further on the path. Two additional Koroks can be found near the pond by lifting a [[Rock]] atop a Tree and by hitting a second Acorn-shaped {{Term|BotW|Vase}} suspended on the inside of a hollow rock formation.
