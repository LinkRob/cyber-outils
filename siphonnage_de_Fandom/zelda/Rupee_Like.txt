{{Term/Store|Rupee Like|Rupee Likes|Series, FS, TMC, PH, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 201 {{Exp Game|FS, TMC, PH, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|Series|Rupee Like}} are variations of {{Plural|Series|Like Like|link}}, with {{Plural|Series|Rupee|link}} of varying denominations attached to stalks at their tops.

==={{FS|-}}===
Rupee Likes debut in Four Swords. They hide under the Rupees at the top of their stalks, which they use as a lure to try to ambush and eat any [[Link]]. The color of the Rupee on their stalk determines how many Rupees it steals from a Link at a time. They can be very annoying if not dealt with quickly, especially in the later levels, since they will spit a Link out only if he breaks free or if all of the Rupees are stolen, and you need Rupees in order to pass levels or revive fallen Links. Additionally, Rupee Likes are very durable comparing to most other enemies.

Most of the time if a piece of Rupee spawns out of nowhere, it is very likely to be a Rupee Like.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Rupee Like}}
{{Plural|TMC|Rupee Like|link}} hide under the {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} at the top of their stalks and use it to lure {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} in {{TMC}}. They act pretty much like their debut appearance in Four Swords. They appear in various areas and come in three types: {{Plural|TMC|Green Rupee Like|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Blue Rupee Like|link}}, and {{Plural|TMC|Red Rupee Like|link}}. The color of the {{Term|TMC|Rupee}} indicates how many {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} they drain at a time from {{Term|TMC|Link}}. {{Plural|TMC|Rupee Like}} appear be shorter than {{Plural|TMC|Like Like|link}}, not counting their stalks, and can only engulf {{Term|TMC|Link}} up to his neck. When they have captured him, they hold his head with their stalks.

If a {{Term|TMC|Rupee Like}} engulfs {{Term|TMC|Link}}, it will suck his {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} away and continue to do so until he struggles free or if he runs out of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}. {{Plural|TMC|Rupee Like}} are commonly found hiding in a large group of {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} and are usually found at a corner or the middle of the formation. The {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} they carry on their stalks are real and can be claimed among the undigested {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} when they are defeated. {{Plural|TMC|Rupee Like}} will continue to chase down {{Term|TMC|Link}} if he has no {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}}, though they deal no damage.

One method to avoid falling prey to a {{Term|TMC|Rupee Like}} is to collect free-standing {{Plural|TMC|Rupee}} with items such as the {{Term|TMC|Boomerang|link}} or the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Rupee Like}} are very large in {{PH}}, appearing in the {{Term|PH|Isle of Ruins|link}}. As with {{Plural|PH|Like Like|link}}, they can draw {{Term|PH|Link|link}} in from a distance like a vacuum. They appear in the maze-like area before the water level is lowered with the {{Term|PH|King's Key|link}}, and they will appear within the maze if the water level has already been lowered.

As in {{TMC}}, {{Plural|PH|Rupee Like}} attempt to lure {{Term|PH|Link}} with the {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}} at the top of their stalks; the color of the {{Term|PH|Rupee}} determines how many {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} are stolen from {{Term|PH|Link}} at a time. They will spit him out if he breaks free or if he runs out of {{Plural|PH|Rupee}}, although it is possible for him to break free before any {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} are stolen.

They will still try to attack {{Term|PH|Link}} even if he has no {{Plural|PH|Rupee}}. Unlike {{TMC|-}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} will take one half of a {{Term|PH|Heart|link}} of damage when he is released by the {{Term|PH|Rupee Like}}, but the damage is taken when he lands on the ground. If {{Term|PH|Link}} hits the ground after being released and falls into water, the extra damage will apply, dealing a total of one {{Term|PH|Heart}} of damage. Also, {{Plural|PH|Rupee Like}} can hide back in the ground if {{Term|PH|Link}} is far enough away, using their {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} as lures again, and they will attack if {{Term|PH|Link}} re-approaches.

If {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} are stolen from {{Term|PH|Link}} by a {{Term|PH|Rupee Like}}, he can regain them if he defeats the {{Term|PH|Rupee Like}} quickly.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Rupee Like}} have slightly larger shadows in comparison to normal {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} in {{ALBW}}. As usual, a Rupee Like will reveal themselves when Link draws near. A Rupee Like will try to eat him if it's facing him when he's close enough. Rupee Likes cannot chase Link if he's on shallow water, but they can still eat him if he's close enough to dry land. Unlike the other games it's appeared in, all Rupee Likes will steal a set amount of Rupees from Link regardless of the color of the Rupee on their stalks, before spitting him out; always stealing ~20 Rupees each time, and Link will take only a quarter of a heart of damage when he's spat out. The amount of Rupees stolen can be decreased if Link struggles if the player moves the circle pad of the Nintendo 3DS. Also, Link can also get hurt by simply touching the Rupee Like when it doesn't try to eat him. This actually deals more damage then being spat out after being eaten, similarly to the Anti-Kirbies in Link's Awakening, but it only deals half a heart of damage. They will always drop a {{Term|ALBW|Green Rupee|link}} upon defeat, even if the {{Term|ALBW|Rupee Like}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Blue Rupee Like}}, {{Term|ALBW|Red Rupee Like}}, or Purple {{Term|ALBW|Rupee Like}}. Even after defeat, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} cannot regain {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} that have been stolen from him. {{Term|ALBW|Green Rupee Like|display=Green}}, {{Term|ALBW|Blue Rupee Like|display=Blue}}, and {{Plural|ALBW|Red Rupee Like}} appear all throughout {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, while the Purple {{Term|ALBW|Rupee Like}} only appears in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Rupee Rush|link}} {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game|link}}.
