The {{Term/Store|''Journal of Various Worries''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Journal of Various Worries|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Journal of Various Worries}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} detailing the troubled thoughts of a number of villagers in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} is the chief of the {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|display=Village}}, the book is located on a counter in {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link|display=her home}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can read the {{Term|BotW|Book}} to get a glimpse at future {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} that will become available.

The first entry is by {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}}, who signs it as "The Bow Master". It details how he decided to choose his precious {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}} over his wife, {{Term|BotW|Rola|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, my wife left me today. The last thing she said to me was: "What's more important, the Cuccos or me?!" I chose the Cuccos.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref> 

Next is an entry by {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}}, who is concerned over the appearance of {{Term|BotW|Pikango|link}} and his search for the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A painter unfamiliar to any of us has arrived in the village. He claims he is searching for the Great Fairy's Fountain, but that's near where the village's spirit protector resides. We must make sure the outsider doesn't learn of the location of the fountain...or the two shrines near it.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref> Although he attempts to remain anonymous, he signs the entry as an "Anonymous Father of Two". As {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} is the only man in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}} with two children, the signature exposes him.

After this, there is an entry written by {{Term|BotW|Rola|link}}. She signs her worry as a "Lonely Arrow Girl" and notes how {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be a fine archer, hoping he comes by her shop.<ref>{{Cite|The new guy in town looks like he really knows how to tune a bow. I hope he comes by and nocks an arrow for me. It's been WAAAY too long.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref>

Following {{Term|BotW|Rola}}'s entry is {{Term|BotW|Lasli|link|display=Lasli's}}, signed as "Struggling Shop Girl". She expresses her dissatisfaction with her life after the murder of her boyfriend, despite her job keeping her occupied.<ref>{{Cite|Day in, day out, I'm outside barking people into the shop. I don't mind, really. It's good money, and busying myself helps to numb the pain.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lasli}} writes that {{Plural|BotW|Sunset Firefly|link|display=Fireflies}} are the only thing to truly make her happy, but she has been banned from walking at night and finds herself despairing without them.<ref>{{Cite|But the only thing that really helps me feel better is walking among the fireflies. Unfortunately, we've now been asked not to walk around at night because it's dangerous. I don't know how I can cope without the fireflies...|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref>

The final entry in the {{Term|BotW|Journal of Various Worries|display=''Journal''}} belongs to {{Term|BotW|Paya|link}}, who signs the entry using the fruit that gave her her name, "Papaya".<ref>{{Cite|Well... My name is Paya. Th-the reason I was named Paya is because I have a birthmark shaped like a papaya seed...|Paya|BotW}}</ref> She brings up the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|link}} handed down in her family, writing that she has been hearing voices concerning its purpose.<ref>{{Cite|There is a precious heirloom that has been passed down through my family for generations. Recently, I've begun to hear voices telling me that the heirloom has an important destiny.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref> Although she is fearful, {{Term|BotW|Paya}} feels that this destiny is swiftly approaching.<ref>{{Cite|It's going to happen soon. It makes me nervous, but I can feel it in my bones.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref>
