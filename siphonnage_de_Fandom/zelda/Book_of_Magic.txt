The {{Term/Store|Book of Magic||Series, TLoZ}},{{Exp Game|TLoZ}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 383{{Exp Game|TLoZ}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Magic Book''',{{Fact}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Locations and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Magic wand|This is the wand that Wizzrobe uses. Wave it to let loose magic spells. What's more, if Link picks up the Magic Book and learns some new spells, he can chant some fiery spells and send out flames.}}
In {{TLoZ}}, the Book of Magic is an item that [[Link]] equips to add the power of [[Fire]] to the [[Magical Rod]].<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= This is the wand that Wizzrobe uses. Wave it to let loose magic spells. What's more, if Link picks up the Magic Book and learns some new spells, he can chant some fiery spells and send out flames.|game= TLoZ|page= 26}}</ref> In addition to the normal [[magic]] beam that shoots from the Magical Rod itself, the Book of Magic allows it to also emit Fire once its beam makes contact with an enemy, object or wall, causing more damage to enemies. Like the Fire emitted from the [[Blue Candle|Blue]] and [[Red Candle]], it deals a damage of [[Weapon Strength#The Legend of Zelda|1]] on certain enemies. The Book is found in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}} of the First Quest and {{Term|TLoZ|Level 4|link}} of the [[Second Quest]].

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, the Magic Book is a minor quest item. The book belongs to [[Iris]], the novice magician trapped within the [[Dark World]]. Iris was practicing her magic in the [[Village of the Blue Maiden]] when she teleported herself to the Dark World, accidentally leaving her Magic Book behind in the [[Light World]]. The [[Links]] can use the [[Roc's Feather]] to jump over the pit in the long house east of the [[Seeker's Guild]] headquarters and meet [[Mage|Iris' teacher]] who has found her book lying unguarded. The teacher requests the Links to give the book back to her apprentice in the Dark World.<ref>{{Cite|That little brat, Iris! I can't believe she'd leave such a valuable book just lying around... Hey... Aren't you the lads who freed [[Vaati]]? What good timing. You know how to get to the [[Dark World]], do you not? If you see my apprentice, a witch named Iris, please give her this book.|Mage|FSA}}</ref>

== Trivia ==
[[File:TLoZ Book of Magic (JP).png|168x168px|thumb|As strongly implied from the cross on the Book of Magic, it was originally known as the Bible, as seen in the initial Japanese release of {{TLoZ|-}}]]
* The Book of Magic is actually the '''[[wikipedia:Bible|Holy Bible]]''', as it was called in the original Japanese [[Family Computer Disk System]] version of {{TLoZ|-}} and the [[wikipedia:Christian_cross|Christian cross]] on its official artwork is clearly visible. The name was changed in the international release (as well as the later 1994 [[Nintendo Entertainment System|Famicom]] version) due to [[Nintendo of America]]'s strict policy of avoiding real-life religious material. The cross on the book's sprite, as well as [[Link]]'s [[Shield]] including the [[Magical Shield]], however, were left intact. In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]'', it is completely removed. As it is indeed the Bible, this strongly implies the [[Fire]] gained from this item for the Magical Rod in the very first game of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] was [[wikipedia:Holy_Fire|Holy Fire]] from the [[wikipedia:Holy_Spirit_in_Christianity|Holy Spirit]].
