The {{Term/Store|Boss's House||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 300}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Boss's House}} is the residence of {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}}, the boss of the {{Term|BotW|Goron Group Mining Company|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link|display=Goron City's}} {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} is located outside of the building, at which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can exchange {{Plural|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Heart Container|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Stamina Vessel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It appears you have claimed {{Color|BotW Red|four}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}}. In exchange for {{Color|BotW Red|four}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}}, I will amplify your being. So tell me what it is that you desire.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} can be found either inside the {{Term|BotW|Boss's House|display=House}} or just outside.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}, he can speak to {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} outside of his {{Term|BotW|Boss's House|display=House}}. {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} expresses his frustration towards {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Drat! That blasted {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}!}}|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> explaining that it has worsened the eruptions from {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|With {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}} runnin' wild recently, Death Mountain's eruptions have gotten real bad.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> In his anger, {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} ends causing his back pain to flare up.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|But it always comes back!! Arrrgh...}} Youch... Owwowow...|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> He sends {{Term|BotW|Link}} to fetch {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey... If ya happen to see {{Color|BotW Red|Yunobo}}, will ya let him know I'm lookin' for him?|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> which begins the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} helps {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}},<ref>{{Cite|Yunobo told me all about how you helped him, brother.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> he must return to the {{Term|BotW|Boss's House}} to receive three {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Bludo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a reward for ya. Hylians like you need this sorta thing, right?|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> He decides to go meet {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} at the {{Term|BotW|Bridge of Eldin|link}} to deal with {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}},<ref>{{Cite|OK then. Well, I took my painkillers, so I guess it's time to meet up with Yunobo.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> but his plans change when his back pain returns.<ref>{{Cite|Owww... Arghhh... I'm afraid I won't be goin' anywhere today.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> Instead, he has {{Term|BotW|Link}} find {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} to cancel their plans for the day.<ref>{{Cite|I hate to put you on the spot, but... can ya please go tell Yunobo that I'm gonna have to cancel today?|Bludo|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} will bring {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Boss's House}} at {{Term|BotW|Bludo}}'s request.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Oh! That's right}}! I talked to {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} about you, and he said to bring you to him! An order from {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} can't be refused. Move it, goro!|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> To thank him,<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, right! I've still gotta thank you, brother!|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep the contents of a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} found inside his {{Term|BotW|Boss's House|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's some {{Color|BotW Blue|treasure}} in my house. Grab it. Keep it. It's yours.|Bludo|BotW}}</ref> Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker|link}}.
