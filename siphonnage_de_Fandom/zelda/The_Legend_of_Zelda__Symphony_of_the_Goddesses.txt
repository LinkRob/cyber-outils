'''''The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses''''' was a concert series that followed [[The Legend of Zelda 25th Anniversary Symphony|''The Legend of Zelda'' 25th Anniversary Symphony]] concerts in 2011. Unlike the 25th Anniversary concerts, which only played in three cities, the ''Symphony of the Goddesses'' toured various cities across the world from 2012 to 2017, with the exception of 2014.

{{OTOC}}

==Development==
[[File:Zelda Symphony Stephen Colbert.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Promotional art from The Late Show with Stephen Colbert]]
Like the 25th Anniversary concerts, the ''Symphony of the Goddesses'' was produced by Jason Michael Paul Productions, Inc. teamed up with [[Nintendo]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Nintendo has teamed up with Jason Michael Paul Productions, Inc. to provide video game fans with the ultimate musical experience for The Legend of Zelda.|url=http://press.nintendo.com|title=Dallas Symphony Orchestra to Play ‘Symphony of the Goddesses’ on Jan. 10; retrieved Feb. 25 2012|site=Nintendo Press}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Jason Michael Paul Productions, Inc. and Nintendo proudly present "The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses," a concert event that will take audiences of all ages on a thrilling orchestral adventure through one of the video game industry's most beloved franchises.|url=http://dallassymphony.com/season-tickets/single-tickets/special-concerts-events/productions/the-legend-of-zelda.aspx|title=January 10 The Legend of Zelda; retrieved Feb. 25 2012|site=Dallas Symphony Orchestra official site}}</ref> Various pieces from the premiere concerts were carried over to the tour, including {{TWW}} and {{TP}} Symphonic Movements. Both of these pieces form part of the new four-movement symphony recounting the classic storylines from {{OoT}}, {{TWW|-}}, {{TP|-}}, and {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=The name "Symphony of the Goddesses" refers not only to the concert program but also to the new four-movement symphony recounting the classic storylines from The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time™, The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker™, The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess™ and The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past™.|url=http://radiohyrule.com/2012/01/05/symphony-of-the-goddesses-movements-revealed-new-segments-announced|title=Symphony of the Goddesses Movements Revealed; New Segments Announced; retrieved Feb. 25 2012|site=Radio Hyrule}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote=The highlight of this new musical program is a full four-movement symphony that will guide fans through a musical retelling of key chapters from The Legend of Zelda franchise.|url=press.nintendo.com|Dallas Symphony Orchestra to Play ‘Symphony of the Goddesses’ on Jan. 10; retrieved Feb. 25 2012|site=Nintendo Press}}</ref> The concert featured music from games that were not played at the 25th Anniversary concerts, including {{LA}}, {{MM}}, and {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quoe=Fans can also expect to hear stirring orchestral renditions of music from other series games, including The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening™ and The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask™.|url=http://radiohyrule.com/2012/01/05/symphony-of-the-goddesses-movements-revealed-new-segments-announced|title=Symphony of the Goddesses Movements Revealed; New Segments Announced; retrieved Feb. 25 2012|site=Radio Hyrule}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote=The other aspect of the program is more modular, so you can expect to hear highlights from other favorite moments in the Zelda pantheon that may not be covered by the 4-movement symphony, not to mention a lot of the great music found in the peripheral titles, like Majora's Mask, or the handhelds like Spirit Tracks, Link’s Awakening or the Oracle duology.|url=http://www.destructoid.com/zelda-symphony-of-the-goddesses-dates-and-details-218894.phtml|title=Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses Dates and Details; retrieved Feb. 25 2012|site=Destructoid}}</ref> Just as in two of the previous concerts, the pieces were accompanied by synchronized scenes from the ''Zelda'' games, projected behind the orchestra.

The concert series were divided in three seasons. The second one, dubbed "[[Second Quest]]," not only included some songs from the previous season, but also some new ones, including songs from games that were absent previously such as {{ST|-}}. Also, some of the scenes projected behind the orchestra were updated, such as the ones from "{{TWW|-}} Symphonic Movement" which were replaced by scenes from {{TWWHD}} instead of the original game. The third season, dubbed "Master Quest," also featured songs from {{ALBW}} and scenes from {{MM3D}}. During the 2017 tour, songs from {{BotW}} were also included.

On October 13, 2015, the concert made an appearance in television during The Late Show with Stephen Colbert. The presentation featured a ''Zelda'' series medley, including music from {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= October 15, 2015|url= http://www.cbs.com/shows/the-late-show-with-stephen-colbert/video/7199215F-7957-48B3-FD24-640E9B219B45/performance-by-the-legend-of-zelda-symphony-of-the-goddesses/|title= Performance By The Legend Of Zelda: Symphony Of The Goddesses|site= CBS|type=}}</ref>

Later, a book of sheet music adapted from the concert, entitled [[The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses (Sheet Music)|Symphony of the Goddesses]], was produced and released by the sheet music company Alfred.

===Pre-Concerts===
To promote the first two seasons of concerts, Nintendo held two special pre-concert events at the Nintendo World Store in New York. These events included a mini-concert featuring various songs exclusive to them, including from games absent in the ''Symphony of the Goddess'' concerts such as {{TAoL}}. The first event was held on September 14, 2012, played by a small orchestra.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= September 15, 2012|retrieved=August 8, 2014|url= http://youtu.be/U3uW26gIRXY|title= Zelda Symphony of the Goddesses Nintendo World Store Mini-Concert|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref> The second one was held on October 20, 2013, played by a string quartet.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= October 21, 2013|retrieved=August 8, 2014|url= http://youtu.be/0XKP0eGY9lg|title= The Legend of Zelda: Symphony of the Goddesses Pre-Concert (Videri String Quartet) at Nintendo World|site= YouTube|type=}}</ref>

==Concerts==
The first concert was in Dallas, Texas on January 10, 2012, which sold out in record time.<ref name= "Dallas">{{Cite Web|quote="The Legend of Zelda™: Symphony of the Goddesses" with the Dallas Symphony Orchestra sold out in record time.|url=https://www.dallassymphony.com/season-tickets/single-tickets/special-concerts-events/productions/the-legend-of-zelda.aspx|title=January 10 The Legend of Zelda; retrieved Feb. 25 2012|site=Dallas Symphony Orchestra official site}}</ref> The last concert was in Salt Lake City, Utah on December 30, 2017. At its first appearance in Mexico City on September 3, 2013, the Symphony reached its highest attendance with over 10,000 attendees.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= This is been our biggest show to date. Mexico has filled more seats than any Zelda Symphony concert to date.|name= Jeron Moore|url= http://youtu.be/RtGyq7nnBFo?t=2m34s|title= TloZ: Symphony of the Goddesses Mexico D.F 3/09/2013 parte 13/17}}</ref>
