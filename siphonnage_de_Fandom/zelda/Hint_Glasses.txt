The {{Term/Store|Hint Glasses|Hint Glasses|Series, ALBW|plural}} are an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Hint Glasses}} are received from the {{Term|ALBW|Fortune-Teller|link}} north of {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can see the future in my crystal ball. And I have seen that there is a gift in your near future. Very near. As in...NOW! Those are the Hint Glasses.|Fortune Teller|ALBW}}</ref> When equipped, they give {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} the ability to see {{Plural|ALBW|Hint Ghost|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Those glasses allow the wearer to behold things amazing and strange!|Fortune Teller|ALBW}}</ref> though his surroundings will appear darker. This allows him to access hints if stuck at a certain area in exchange for a [[Play Coin]].<ref>{{Cite|For if you're in a place where you feel hopeless and stuck, look at the world through those glasses. You may just catch a glimpse of a Hint Ghost. Such ghosts give you tips and tricks from the great beyond.|Fortune Teller|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you find yourself stuck on a puzzle, a Hint Ghost will give you a hint in exchange for a Play Coin.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

The {{Term|ALBW|Hint Glasses}} can also be used while [[Wall Merging|Wall Merged]], and can be seen on {{Term|ALBW|Link}} in his {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}} form.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|ALBW|Hint Glasses}} bear a striking resemblance to [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groucho_glasses Groucho glasses].
