{{Term/Store|Smog||Series, OoA}} is the recurring [[Boss]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. He first appears as the boss of the [[Crown Dungeon]] in {{OoA}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 204}}</ref> and reappears as the boss of the [[Eastern Palace|Eastern Temple]] and [[Hidden Ruins]] in {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==

==={{OoA|-}}===
====Appearance====
Smog appears as a gaseous, horned face that challenges [[Link]] to a deadly game,<ref>{{Cite|It's time for our little game! I break apart. If you can force me back together and blow me away, it ends! But before you do, I shall take a bit of your soul!!! Now begin!|Smog|OoA}}</ref>

====Battle====
His battle plays out over four rounds that combine traditional combat with puzzle-solving elements. Each round begins with an arrangement of solid blocks appearing in the room, and Smog dividing itself into several smaller forms. These pieces of Smog attach themselves to the solid blocks and move around their perimeters like [[Spark]]s, immune to harm from any of Link's weapons; instead Link must use the [[Cane of Somaria]] to create new blocks that divert the pieces' paths, forcing them to collide and recombine into the original Smog. To aid in redirecting the pieces, some of the existing blocks can be pushed once, and the pieces can be momentarily slowed down by striking them with the [[Sword]]. If Link's use of the Cane of Somaria causes any of the pieces to detach from the blocks, they will vanish and reappear in their original positions. At any point, Link can step on a [[Floor Switch]] in the northwest corner of the room to reset the blocks and the pieces of Smog to their original positions.

In the first and third rounds, Smog divides into two pieces, while in the second and fourth rounds, it divides into three. Recombining two of the three pieces forms a slower-moving, medium-sized Smog which must then be combined with the third piece. The small and medium pieces of Smog will periodically spit blue fireballs toward Link, which can be dispelled with the Sword. When Smog is returned to its original form, it will detach from the solid blocks and slowly float in pursuit of Link, spitting lightning bolts that electrocute Link on contact. Smog is otherwise vulnerable to Link's Sword in this form and will be defeated by several strikes, setting up the next and more difficult round. After defeating Smog in the fourth round, it will leave behind a [[Heart Container]], and the door to the [[Sacred Soil]] will be opened.

==={{EoW|-}}===
====Appearance====
Smog, like in his previous appearence, challenges the protagonist to a game, who is now [[Zelda]]. He is now a greenish color, and his horns are smaller and now yellow. He now also has the power of electricity.

====Eastern Palace====
He first is found in the Eastern Temple (Formely Eastern Palace), and is warned by a nearby NPC, and is the main antagonist of the side-quest [[Let's Play a Game]]. His battle is incredibly similar to his prior one, however he is now immune to the sword, and there is no Floor Switch. After his defeat, he swears revenge.

====Hidden Ruins====
Smog once again plays a role in another sidequest. The sidequest [[Cotton Candy Hunt]] has many [[Deku Scrubs]] believe Smog to be a living cotton candy, and they ask Zelda to find him. He is located in the Hidden Ruins, and this time his boss takes place in a 2D section, but otherwise remains the same.
