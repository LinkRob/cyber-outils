The {{Term/Store|Regal Necklace||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Regal Necklace can be obtained at the [[Isle of the Dead]] from [[Brant]]'s tomb. It is used to calm the [[Cyclone]] preventing access to the [[Isle of Ruins]].<ref>{{Cite|King Mutoh, the holder of the Aquanine, is not here. He is on the island that is guarded by a rock wall, the Isle of Ruins. You'll need that Regal Necklace to get there. That will calm the cyclone at the entrance, so that your boat may pass.|Brant|PH}}</ref> Once obtained, the [[SS Linebeck]] is able to dock at the isle in order to search for the final [[Pure Metal]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 268}}</ref>
