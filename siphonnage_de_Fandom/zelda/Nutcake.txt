{{Term/Store|Nutcake|Nutcakes|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Nutcake}}<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Nutcake}} are a kind of {{Term|BotW|Cake|link}} that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either an {{Term|BotW|Acorn|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Chickaloo Tree Nut|link}} together with {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}. Eating them will restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} have a pleasant texture and a simple sweetness, both of which come from the use of nuts.<ref name="Inventory"/>

At {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Misa's Recipe Book|link}}, which contains a recipe for {{Term|BotW|Nutcake}}. {{Term|BotW|Misa|link}} first instructs the reader to purchase {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter}}, and {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar}} from her {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|First, you'll want to buy {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|cane sugar}} here at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Slippery Falcon}}.|Misa's Recipe Book|BotW}}</ref> She then says to throw them into a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} with nuts of one's choice, though she prefers {{Plural|BotW|Acorn}}.<ref>{{Cite|Toss these ingredients in a pot alongside the nuts of your choice to make a tasty {{Color|BotW Blue|nutcake}}! I prefer {{Color|BotW Blue|acorns}} myself.|Misa's Recipe Book|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can add a fifth ingredient when making {{Term|BotW|Nutcake}}, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Nutcake''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Nutcake}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hearty ingredient. When eaten, all of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} will be healed, and he will gain temporary {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Nutcake''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Nutcake}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an energizing ingredient. These {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} can be eaten to refill part of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Nutcake''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Nutcake}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an enduring ingredient. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Nutcake''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Nutcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hasty ingredient. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can eat them to have his movement speed increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Nutcake''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Nutcake}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a spicy ingredient. Eating them will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Nutcake''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Nutcake}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an electric ingredient. If eaten, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Shock Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Nutcake''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Nutcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a mighty ingredient. When consumed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will gain a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Nutcake''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Nutcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a tough ingredient. They can be eaten to increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Nutcake''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Nutcake}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a sneaky ingredient. Eating these {{Plural|BotW|Cake}} will provide a boost to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Nutcake''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Nutcake}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Forest nuts give this cake a pleasant texture and a simple, understated sweetness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a chilly ingredient. When eaten, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
