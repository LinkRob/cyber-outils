The {{Term/Store|Big Green Chuchu||Series, TMC}} is the {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} of {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 166}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}} is also known as the '''Giant ChuChu''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 32}}</ref> However, as this name contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}}
The {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}} slips its way inside the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} while {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is inside exploring its depths. The {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}} is a normal-sized {{Term|TMC|Chuchu|link}}, but it is considered to be huge by {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized creatures. In order to defeat the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must use the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to suck jelly from the base of the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}}'s body. Once enough has been sucked out, the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}} will lose its balance and fall over, becoming susceptible to attacks from the {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword|link}}.

The reward for defeating the {{Term|TMC|Big Green Chuchu}} is a {{Term|TMC|Heart Container|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}}.
