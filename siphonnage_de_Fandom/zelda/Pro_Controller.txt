'''Pro Controllers''' are controllers for the [[Nintendo Switch|Nintendo Switch.]]

== Overview ==
The Pro Controller first launched alongside the [[Nintendo Switch]], and can be used with many of the games that the Nintendo Switch has to offer.

=== Special Editions ===
Since it's release there have been many special editions for the Pro Controller.

==== [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]] ====

* [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom]] Edition

==== Splatoon ====

* Splatoon 2 Edition
* Splatoon 3 Edition

==== Monster Hunter ====

* Monster Hunter Rise: Sunbreak Edition
*Monster Hunter Rise: Special Edition

==== Xenoblade Chronicles ====

* [[Xenoblade Chronicles 2]] Edition

==== [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]] ====

* [[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate]] Edition
