'''''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Re-Arranged Album]]''''' is one of the official soundtracks for {{OoT}}. It was released in December 22, 1999 and was arranged by MOKA. Composed of 12 tracks, the album remixes some of the songs from {{OoT|-}}.
