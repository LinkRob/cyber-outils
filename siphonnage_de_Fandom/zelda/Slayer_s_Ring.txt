The {{Term/Store|Slayer's Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Slayer's Ring<br/>1000 beasts slain|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Slayer's Ring is received from [[Vasu]] after defeating 1,000 enemies. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. It has no effect when worn. The Slayer's Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
