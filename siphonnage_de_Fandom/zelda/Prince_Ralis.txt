{{Term/Store|Prince Ralis||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 277 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Ralis is the prince of the [[Zora]] people, and son of [[Rutela]]. He tried to travel to [[Hyrule Castle]] to plead to [[Princess Zelda]] for help when [[Zora's Domain]] froze over, but he was attacked by twilit monsters sent by [[Zant]] and rescued by [[Ilia]].<ref name="danger"/><ref>{{Cite|Bless her heart... She found this poor boy collapsed in the road, so she did all she could to save him.|Telma|TP}}</ref> He became ill because he had spent too long away from water, and required emergency transport to [[Kakariko Village]] to be treated by [[Renado]].<ref>{{Cite|I've heard of a shaman in Kakariko Village, in the Eldin lands, who's tended Gorons and Zoras.|Telma|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You ready to roll, Link? You've got to protect these young ones on the way to Kakariko!|Telma|TP}}</ref>  Soon after Link clears the [[Lakebed Temple]], Ralis wakes up and recovers from his illness. However, he does not often socialize with the people of Kakariko Village and constantly prays at [[King Zora]]'s grave in the [[Kakariko Village Graveyard|Graveyard]]. If Link visits him, Ralis will tell the young hero that he had seen him in a dream that his mother sent him.<ref>{{Cite|My mother came to me in a dream... She showed me your image. She spoke of a youth who would save our domain and steer my fate. One named Link.|Prince Ralis|TP}}</ref> He is reluctant to become the King of the Zoras, but throughout the story he grows in confidence with [[Link]]'s help.<ref>{{Cite|Link... I have so little confidence... What can I do? I could not even carry out one simple task for my mother... I am so unlike her... I doubt I am even qualified to rule... Link... What would my mother have thought of me? I still have so little confidence in myself... What can a pitiful Zora like me do? I am not sure I can be of any use at all...... ...Can I?|Prince Ralis|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|As I look at you, I get a sense of what my mother wanted, what I should be doing now... I will return to my village.|Prince Ralis|TP}}</ref> He is an excellent [[Reekfish]] angler,<ref name="Prince"/> and gifts Link the [[Coral Earring]] required to catch one.<ref>{{Cite|It must be a reekfish. That is a rare specimen found only in the Zora village. It feeds on a valuable type of coral, and without that bait, even our best fishers cannot catch one. Look closely, Link... My earring is made of this coral. I would like to give it to you.|Prince Ralis|TP}}</ref> Catching a Reekfish is essential to the game's progression; when Link catches one, it will give off a scent that leads to [[Snowpeak]]. Link can use this scent to guide him through the harsh blizzards of Snowpeak in [[Wolf Link|his wolf form]] and seek out the first shard of the [[Mirror of Twilight]].<ref>{{Cite|I want to investigate further...but I can't even see the path through this blizzard, yeah? I'm sure you're here because you're curious about the mountain, but you'd best not go without a plan.|Ashei|TP}}</ref>

After giving Link the Coral Earring, Ralis returns to Zora's Domain and rules over the Zoras. If Link visits him, he will welcome him and urge him to make himself at home.<ref>{{Cite|I think all of us carry heavy hearts, but I will do my best to see that our village's wounds heal as quickly as possible. Come see us anytime, Link. You will always be welcome here.|Prince Ralis|TP}}</ref> During the end credits, Ralis finds the spirit of his mother and continues to rule over the Zoras, but in a much happier state.

==Trivia==
*During the period in the game when Link returns to [[Ordon Village]] to ask [[Mayor Bo]] how he bested the [[Goron]]s, if he goes into the other villagers' houses either before or after he speaks to Mayor Bo, Ralis' Theme will be playing in the background. This only occurs during this segment of the game.
*The pendant around Ralis' neck resembles the [[Zora's Sapphire]] from {{OoT}}.
