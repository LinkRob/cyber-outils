The {{Term/Store|Iron Shield|Iron Shields|Series, OoS, OoA, SS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, the {{Term|OoS|Iron Shield}} can be earned by offering the {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Smithy|link}} a piece of {{Term|OoS|Hard Ore|link}} and a {{Term|OoS|Wooden Shield|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link}} can also obtain the shield via a {{Plural|OoS|Secret|link}} in a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game|link}}.

The {{Term|OoS|Iron Shield}} defends {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} against some attacks that the {{Term|OoS|Wooden Shield|link}} cannot, but not as many as the {{Term|OoS|Mirror Shield|link}}, the most powerful shield in the game.

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, the {{Term|OoA|Iron Shield}} can be obtained on {{Term|OoA|Crescent Island|link}} after the {{Term|OoA|Tokay|link}} steals {{Term|OoA|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|OoA|Item|link}} when he first arrives on their island. {{Term|OoA|Link}} must regain his arsenal one-by-one by speaking with each {{Term|OoA|Tokay}} individually. One {{Term|OoA|Tokay}} thief can be found in a cavern on the south edge of the island, which can only be reached by swimming with the [[Mermaid Suit]]. Instead of returning the {{Term|OoA|Wooden Shield|link}} that {{Term|OoA|Link}} originally lost, this {{Term|OoA|Tokay}} will instead return an upgraded {{Term|OoA|Iron Shield}}. If the {{Term|OoA|Iron Shield}} is ever eaten by a {{Term|OoA|Like Like|link}}, a new one can be bought from any shop for 50 {{Plural|OoA|Rupee|link}}.

When not playing a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game|link}}, the {{Term|OoA|Iron Shield}} is the best {{Term|OoA|Shield}} that is possible to obtain. When playing a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game}}, the more powerful {{Term|OoA|Mirror Shield|link}} can be obtained.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, the {{Term|SSHD|Iron Shield}} bears two symbols that resemble footprints, possibly those of a {{Term|SSHD|Loftwing|link}}. It can be bought from the {{Term|SSHD|Gear Shop|link}} at the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} for 100 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} after the {{Term|SSHD|Skyview Temple|link}} is completed.<ref>{{Cite|Ahaa! I've got some {{Color|SS Red|new}} gear in stock to show you, friend. Available now, you can purchase an {{Color|SS Gold|Iron Shield}} and a {{Color|SS Gold|Small Seed Satchel}}. I hope they meet with your approval!|Rupin|SS}}</ref><ref name="SS Cost"/> Compared to the {{Term|SSHD|Wooden Shield|link}}, the {{Term|SSHD|Iron Shield}} is more durable and does not burn when it comes in contact with {{Term|SSHD|Fire|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's much more durable than a wooden shield, and I'm happy to report that it won't burst into flames!|Rupin|SS}}</ref><ref name="SS Obtain">{{Cite|You got an {{Color|SS Gold|Iron Shield}}!<br/>This sturdy metal shield is unaffected by fire, but watch out for electrical attacks!|N/A|SS}}</ref> However, when it comes into contact with {{Term|SSHD|Electricity|link}} it can conduct it well and will shock {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I suppose I should warn you that it won't protect you from electricity, which could come as a nasty...shock.|Rupin|SS}}</ref><ref name="SS Obtain"/> This can be negated if {{Term|SSHD|Link}} performs a {{Term|SSHD|Shield Bash|link}} just as the {{Term|SSHD|Electricity}} connects. It can be upgraded by {{Term|SSHD|Gondo|link}} to increase its durability.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|SS Black|A strengthened Iron Shield with improved durability.}}|N/A|SS}}</ref> The {{Term|SSHD|Iron Shield}} is superseded by the {{Term|SSHD|Sacred Shield|link}}, which repels {{Term|SSHD|Fire}}, {{Term|SSHD|Electricity}} and {{Term|SSHD|Curse|link}} attacks.
