The '''Unicorn''' is a character featured in the episode "[[Sing for the Unicorn]]" of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. Belonging to [[Sing]], it was stolen from her by [[Ganon]]. The evil magician uses it to fly around [[Hyrule]] and capture [[King Harkinian]], taking him as a hostage in the [[Underworld]].<ref name=sing>{{Cite Episode|quote=Her name is Sing. She's from a land far away. Ganon stole her unicorn and she's trying to rescue it.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> 

When [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] see Ganon riding the creature, they begin to question where he got the unicorn from since "beautiful creatures aren't his style."<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=I still wonder where Ganon got that flying unicorn. Beautiful creatures aren't his style.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> Eventually, the young duo bump into Sing in the Underworld, who explains that she is trying to rescue her unicorn that Ganon stole from her.<ref name=sing/> The heroes find that the Unicorn is being guarded by [[Moblin]]s, which Sing easily defeats with her {{Wp|sai}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Good job, Sing! We've got your unicorn.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref> With her unicorn rescued, she uses it to save King Harkinian from his doom in a so-called pit room.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Daddy! Sing! You saved him! Thank you, thank you!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=5}}</ref>

At the end of the episode, Sing waves goodbye to the King, Zelda and Link as she's riding away on her unicorn.
