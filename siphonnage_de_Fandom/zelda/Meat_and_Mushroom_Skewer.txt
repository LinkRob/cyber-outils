{{Term/Store|Meat and Mushroom Skewer|Meat and Mushroom Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Meat and Mushroom Skewers are meals made by cooking any piece of meat with {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The regular version can be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}, which offer no status effects, or by using multiple Mushrooms with different effects. When consumed, they'll heal several of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Meat and Mushroom Skewers are made by grilling Mushrooms with steak or bird meat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Several different variations of Meat and Mushroom Skewers can be made by using different ingredients:

* '''Hearty Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}. Eating these skewers will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}}. They can be eaten to restore a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}. When eaten, they completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}}. If they're eaten, they'll temporarily boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}. Consuming these skewers will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}}. Eating them temporarily increases the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}}. Link's defense will be increased by eating these skewers.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}}. If eaten, they increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meat and Mushroom Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meat and Mushroom Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A filling dish made by grilling various mountain ingredients with either steak or bird meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}}. They grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Chilly"/>
