{{Term/Store|Crab Stir-Fry|Stir-Fries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Crab Stir-Fry}}<br/>The Goron spice used in preparing this crab pairs perfectly with the flavor of the meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Crab Stir-Fries are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Goron Spice]] and any type of {{Term|BotW|Crab|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. A regular Crab Stir-Fry can be made by either using two different types of Crabs or by adding a third ingredient with a contradictory effect. When eaten, it will restore seven {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Alternatively, they can be sold for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. The Goron Spice used is known to pair perfectly with the flavor of the crab meat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Three different variations of Crab Stir-Fry exist, correlating with the three different types of Crabs available:

* '''Energizing Crab Stir-Fry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Crab Stir-Fry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The Goron spice used in preparing this crab pairs perfectly with the flavor of the meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. Eating one will replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They heal five Hearts, but they sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Crab Stir-Fry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Crab Stir-Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The Goron spice used in preparing this crab pairs perfectly with the flavor of the meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}. When consumed, Link's attack power will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Mighty"/> They heal two Hearts in his Life Gauge and sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Tough Crab Stir-Fry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Crab Stir-Fry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The Goron spice used in preparing this crab pairs perfectly with the flavor of the meat.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. If eaten, Link will have a higher defense for a short period of time.<ref name="Tough"/> They also heal for five Hearts, and they can be sold for 30 Rupees.
