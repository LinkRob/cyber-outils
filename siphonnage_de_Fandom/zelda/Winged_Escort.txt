{{Term/Store|Winged Escort||HWAoC}} is a level 43 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Winged Escort|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Winged Escort}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Prime Meat|link}}, and ten  {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Salmon|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}'s objective is to escort a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} with {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}} to a Targeted Position. They begin at the bottom of the map, and must take an almost linear path to the position in the north. Many {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} stand in their way, as not far from the starting position, the three encounter two {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} and a small group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}}. Then, after defeating them, they soon find a {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus|link}}, along with six {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Pebblit|link}} found further down a nearby path. Afterwards, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} and a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}} are found just before reaching a bridge located about halfway to the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' destination. On the other side of that bridge are more {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice-Breath Lizalfos}} that surround two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}. Afterwards, all that is in their way are two groups of four {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}}, although they are easy to defeat. An {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Lynel}} spawns once the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} reaches the Targeted Position, and must be defeated to complete the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}.

A {{Term|HWAoC|Korok|link}} can be found in the northern area of the map above the Targeted Position, and when talked to, awards {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}} with a {{Term|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}.
