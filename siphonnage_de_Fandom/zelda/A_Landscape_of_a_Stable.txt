"{{Term/Store|A Landscape of a Stable||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Landscape of a Stable}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Foothill Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Inside {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Mayro|link}} admiring a painting of the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wish I had this kind of artistic skill...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> When he notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he asks if he also came to look at the painting.<ref>{{Cite|Have you come to look at this picture too? It's so beautiful... I could spend hours just looking at it.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> It was supposedly made by {{Term|BotW|Ozunda|link}}, the owner of {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|This painting was made by Ozunda, the owner of this stable, or so I hear.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> He appreciates the majesty of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} in the background and the southern angle of the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|display=Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|That volcano in the background... It's so majestic. And the stable is subtle in the scenery. I think the {{Color|BotW Red|stable was painted from a southern direction}}... The composition is just so impressive...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.{{Note|{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl Shrine}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Mayro}}. In this scenario, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be automatically completed once {{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains it.}}

By heading southwest from {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will come upon {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl Shrine|link}}. Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} has been activated, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
