The '''Game Boy Player''' is an accessory for the [[Nintendo GameCube]]. It plugs into the "Hi-Speed Port" on the bottom of the GameCube. The Player contains actual Game Boy Advance hardware but it requires a boot disc to run. All [[Game Boy]] and [[Game Boy Color]] titles and nearly all [[Game Boy Advance]] titles can be played on the Game Boy Player; there are exceptions such as the ''Game Boy Advance Video'' series which cannot be played on the Game Boy Player, however (to prevent users from recording the videos to VHS and other recordable media).

==Trivia==
* An issue of ''[[Nintendo Power]]'' mentioned that a player could play all Zelda games released up to that point on the Nintendo GameCube if the player owned both the [[The Legend of Zelda: Collector's Edition|Collector's Edition]] and a Game Boy Player.
