{{Term/Store|Jiahto||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Jiahto|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} historian who studies and preserves the history of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref name="Jiahto">{{Cite|I am a historian who studies Zora history and preserves it for future generations.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He spends his days in the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}} examining the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|link}} at its rear.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} before calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, he will find him lost in thought.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Hrmm... Hmph...|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks him what is wrong, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will initially believe him to a be a traveler.<ref>{{Cite|[What's wrong?] Hmm... A traveler, eh?|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He explains that the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument}} contains the history of the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} as told by {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}, which he is rereading so he can record it in a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Upon this stone monument is the proud history of the Zora, as dictated by King Dorephan himself many years ago. I plan to record it all in a book, so I am reading it again.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} expresses that {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}}'s plan sounds difficult, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will finally recognize him.<ref>{{Cite|[That sounds rough.] ... ... {{Big|Link?! Is that really you?}}|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He blames {{Term|BotW|Link}} for stealing {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} from the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Hylian...who stole away our Lady Mipha! And what's more, the Champion who...|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He proclaims that he will never be able forgive {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I...I cannot forgive you! Not ever!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> No matter how {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} accuses him of not understanding what he has done.<ref>{{Cite|Do you not understand the gravity of what you have done?!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He explains that not only did the the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} fail to stop {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, they also caused {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s death.<ref>{{Cite|Not only did you Hylians fail to stop {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}} 100 years ago... But you went and stole our Lady Mipha from us!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> Due to this, he refuses to forgive the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}} for what happened.<ref>{{Cite|I will never forgive you awful Hylians! Not ever!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

Despite his prior statement, after {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will admit to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he misjudged him.<ref>{{Cite|Link... It seems that...I was wrong about you.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> Now that he can see the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} aiming at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, he is confident that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} will still be able to assist in defeating {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Seeing {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}'s form there... Even though we lost Lady Mipha, it seems she can still help you fight against {{Color|BotW Red|Calamity Ganon}}...|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} apologizes for not properly introducing himself earlier,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I am sorry I did not introduce myself sooner. I am Jiahto.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> and he describes his role as a historian to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref name="Jiahto"/> However, he is concerned by the condition of the {{Term|BotW|History of the Zora, Part Seven|link}}, as it has become deteriorated over time.<ref>{{Cite|Even so, this stone monument that King Dorephan supposedly wrote... It's torn to pieces now.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He is worried that the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} are in a similar condition.<ref>{{Cite|Most likely, the {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} outside of the domain are in the same awful condition.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} reiterates his desire to transcribe their contents into a {{Term|BotW|Book}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking about collecting the contents of all the stone monuments and compiling them into a book.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

Due to {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}}'s age, though, traveling to all 10 {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} will take some time for him.<ref>{{Cite|But with these old legs and fins, traveling to all {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} will take a great deal of time.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he could find the 10 {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monuments}} and relay their contents to him.<ref>{{Cite|That is why... Link... I'd like to ask a favor of you. I'd like you to find all {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} around {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} and tell me what they say.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} is relieved that the {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} will be assisting him.<ref>{{Cite|[OK.] Oh! Thank you! You truly are a lifesaver! How reassuring that the Champion will search for those monuments for me!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that one of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} details the location of a treasure, which he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep should he find it.<ref>{{Cite|I have it on good authority that among the stone monuments, one has the location of some {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}} written on it. If you happen to find said {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}}, feel free to keep it. It will most likely be something that a Zora wouldn't need.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} expresses his belief in {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Yes, you'll find the {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} around {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}... I believe in you. Inf act, I'm counting on you!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> after which the "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

During the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} to have him share the location of one of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} that he has not read yet.<ref>{{Cite|What is wrong? Are you unable to find all of the stone monuments? I can perhaps tell you the location of a {{Color|BotW Red|stone monument you have yet to investigate}}...|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will randomly select one of the {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monuments}} to share the location of.<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me.] OK. I'll try to remember... {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Roll, roll the dice... *toss*}}}} Got it! It's in this area here...|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has read all 10 {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}}, he can return to the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber}} to inform {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} of what he has learned.<ref>{{Cite|Link... It would seem you have checked all of the stone monuments for me! Now then, please tell me what was written on them!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> After hearing the contents, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} remarks that many of the details of the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} have been recorded, and thanks to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he has been able to gather all of that information.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... The history, culture, folklore, and various heroic acts of the proud Zora people... It's all here! Eureka! Thanks to you, I've compiled the precious information from those stone monuments!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for helping him and reqards him with a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link, thank you for taking on such an arduous task! Please, allow me to reward you!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} again, he will express his gratitude for reading all of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmph... Link, eh? It was an arduous task you took on for me... I humbly bring my fins together in gratitude!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He feels responsible to ensure that {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}}'s words are passed on for future generations.<ref>{{Cite|I, Jiahto, shall take the responsibility of passing on King Dorephan's inscriptions to future generations!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> In addition, he promises to include a description of all {{Term|BotW|Link}} did in his future {{Term|BotW|Book}}, as well.<ref>{{Cite|And, Link... A description of your heroic contribution shall be included, as well!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>
