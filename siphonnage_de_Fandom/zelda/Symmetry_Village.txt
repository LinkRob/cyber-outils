{{Term/Store|Symmetry Village||Series, OoA}},<ref name="E">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 111 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> also known as '''[[Symmetry City]]''' in the present,<ref name="E"/> is a village in the northwestern mountains of [[Labrynna]], in {{OoA}}. The most prominent feature of the village is its symmetric design, creating a perfect mirror image over the y-axis.

==Overview==
As its name would suggest, the entire village is symmetrical, and each inhabitant of the village has their own twin. In the village lies an active volcano, inside of which the [[Skull Dungeon]], home to the fourth [[Essences of Time|Essence of Time]] can be found. Inside the [[Middle House]], which serves as the town hall for the village, lies the [[Tuni Nut]], the village's sacred symbol that holds the village in balance.<ref>{{Cite|The Tuni Nut is the city's symbol.|N/A|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Tuni Nut: The nut that balances all.|Inventory|OoA}}</ref>

In the basement of the Middle House resides [[Thomas]], who plays a role in the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}. [[Link]] can trade him the [[Dumbbell]] for his [[Cheesy Mustache]].

==Story==
In search of the fourth [[Essences of Time|Essence of Time]], Link travels to the Nuun Highlands where a bridge crosses over to the [[Talus Peaks]] where the city is found. However, the bridge will not be built upon Link's arrival. At the request of [[Mutoh]], the head [[Carpenters|carpenter]], Link gathers up all the workers on break,<ref>{{Cite|Those lazy, worthless fools! Just as we're buildin' this bridge to the Symmetry City Ruins, they go off on break! I wait and I wait, but they don't come back!|Mutoh|OoA}}</ref> with the help of his animal companion (either [[Ricky]], [[Moosh]], or [[Dimitri]], depending on certain choices Link has made on his adventures). The workers will then build the bridge, allowing Link to enter the city. 

When Link arrives in Symmetry City for the first time, he finds the city in a state of {{Term|OoA|Symmetry City Ruins|link|display= ruin}}, infested with [[Podoboo Tower]]s. Link then travels to the past to Symmetry Village to find the cause of the problem, at which point he notices the volcano inside the village erupting, wreaking havoc upon it. He then learns that the [[Tuni Nut]], the village's sacred symbol, has broken. This causes instability in the village, thus erupting the volcano.<ref>{{Cite|When the Tuni Nut broke, the city fell out of balance, and the volcano started erupting.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> To restore balance to the village, [[Link]] must bring the Tuni Nut to [[Patch]] atop [[Restoration Wall]] to have it fixed.<ref>{{Cite|A guru named Patch lives at the top of Restoration Wall. I think he can fix the broken Tuni Nut, but I could never climb that wall.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> Once the nut is fixed and placed on its pedestal in the [[Middle House]], peace will return to the village. The volcano becomes inactive, and the present-day Symmetry City will no longer be in ruins. In the present, the raging volcano is replaced by a waterfall, parted in the middle by the entrance to [[Skull Dungeon]].<ref>{{Cite|Huh? The cave entrance? It was covered by lava from the volcano long ago! Maybe if the volcano calms down, you may be able to get in.|N/A|OoA}}</ref>
