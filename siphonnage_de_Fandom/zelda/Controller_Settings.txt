The {{Term/Store|Controller Settings||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} menu is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Controller Settings|Settings|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Controller Settings}} allow the player to change the settings of their controller.<ref name="Settings"/> The {{Term|HWDE|Controller Settings}} menu is a submenu of the {{Term|HWDE|Settings|link}} menu. The first setting depicts whether the player's controller will rumble while playing.<ref>{{Cite|Rumble|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Turn controller rumble on or off.|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The second setting regards the vertical camera controls.<ref>{{Cite|Vertical Controls|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref> This allows the player to control the camera vertical by either pressing up to move the camera up normally, or to invert the controls and press down to move the camera up.<ref>{{Cite|Adjust vertical camera options for R.|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The third setting is similar to the second setting, but this instead changes the camera horizontally.<ref>{{Cite|Horizontal Controls|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Adjust horizontal camera options for R.|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The final option will take the player to the {{Term|HWDE|Button Settings|link}} where they can map their own controls to their preference.<ref>{{Cite|Assign and set button functions.|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The defaults for these settings are rumble to be on, and both the vertical and horizontal camera controls will remain standard. The player can quickly return to these default settings by pressing the button below the {{Term|HWDE|Button Settings}}.<ref>{{Cite|Return to Default|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Reset the current state and return settings to default?|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The final button will save the players settings and take them back to the {{Term|HWDE|Settings}} menu.<ref>{{Cite|Confirm Settings|Controller Settings|HWDE}}</ref>
