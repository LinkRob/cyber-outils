{{Term/Store|Stamina Potion|Stamina Potions|Series, SS, SSHD|plural}} are {{Plural|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref name="Obtention"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSHD|Stamina Potion}} can be bought at the {{Term|SSHD|Potion Shop|link}} for 50 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}}. They can be stored in {{Plural|SSHD|Empty Bottle|link}}.

When consumed, a {{Term|SSHD|Stamina Potion}} completely refills its user's {{Term|SSHD|Stamina Gauge|link}} and reduces the speed at which it depletes for three minutes.<ref name="Obtention"/>

{{Plural|SSHD|Stamina Potion}} can be upgraded into {{Plural|SSHD|Stamina Potion +|link}} for a fee of 20 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Infusion Shop|link}} by infusing them with the following {{Plural|SSHD|Bug|link}}: three {{Plural|SSHD|Volcanic Ladybug|link}}, two {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Stag Beetle|link}}, two {{Plural|SSHD|Gerudo Dragonfly|link}}, and a {{Term|SSHD|Faron Grasshopper|link}}.
