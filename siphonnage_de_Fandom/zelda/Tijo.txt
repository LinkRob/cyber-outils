{{Term/Store|Tijo||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244}}</ref>

==Biography==
Tijo is the drummer of the [[Zora]] band [[The Indigo-Go's]] and is [[Mikau]]'s roommate.<ref>{{Cite|This is the room of Mikau and Tijo, the guitarist and drummer for the Zora band, The Indigo-Go's.|Zora|MM}}</ref> He has broken the ladder that leads to Mikau's upper-half of the room and promises to start going on a diet.<ref>{{Cite|Um... Don't get mad, but I want you to hear this... I tried to climb up the other ladder to your practice area, and it broke. I'm gonna go on a diet now, so just forgive me, OK?|Tijo|MM}}</ref> He goes on to tell [[Zora Link]] that he knows the reason the ocean has been getting weird is due to problems at the [[Great Bay Temple]]. Whenever this happens, the Zora descendant who is been protecting the temple will have trouble befall them, implying that [[Lulu]] has been having problems because she is the supposed descendant.<ref>{{Cite|There's a rumor that the ocean is getting weird 'cause there's trouble off shore at Great Bay Temple. And they say when that happens, something bad will befall the Zora descendant who's been protecting the temple. Do you catch my drift? And lately, Lulu has been looking kind of weird. Maybe Lulu's the...|Tijo|MM}}</ref>

Tijo leaves his room to practice during the [[Band Rehearsal|rehearsal]] and is last seen during the [[New Day|ending credits]] where he and the rest of the band members are playing at the [[Milk Bar]] for the [[Carnival of Time]].

==Trivia==
*A model of one of Tijo's drums can be seen behind [[Carlov]] in the [[Nintendo Gallery]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''.
*Likely an [[Cameos in The Legend of Zelda|Easter Egg]], the music Tijo plays on his drum kit upon entering his room is the [[cave]] theme from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]''.
