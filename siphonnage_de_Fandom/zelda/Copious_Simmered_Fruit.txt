{{Term/Store|Copious Simmered Fruit|Copious Simmered Fruits|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Copious Simmered Fruit}}<br/>The flavors of the various fruits in this simmered dish exist in perfect harmony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Copious Simmered Fruits by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} four different fruits together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made by using at least two fruits with differing status effects. Eating them will heal some of Link's {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The flavors of the fruits used in these meals exist in perfect harmony.<ref name="Inventory"/>

If Link uses an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}} when making Copious Simmered Fruits, the fourth fruit that he uses will grant the dish a status effect. This can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Copious Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Copious Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The flavors of the various fruits in this simmered dish exist in perfect harmony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}}. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Hasty Copious Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Copious Simmered Fruit}}<br/>>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The flavors of the various fruits in this simmered dish exist in perfect harmony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}. Link's movement speed will receive a boost when he eats these.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Copious Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Copious Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The flavors of the various fruits in this simmered dish exist in perfect harmony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}}. These fruits will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] if he eats them.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Copious Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Copious Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The flavors of the various fruits in this simmered dish exist in perfect harmony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}}. By eating them, Link can gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Copious Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Copious Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The flavors of the various fruits in this simmered dish exist in perfect harmony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}. If they're eaten, they'll grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Chilly Copious Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Copious Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The flavors of the various fruits in this simmered dish exist in perfect harmony.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}. These meals can be eaten to provide Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
