==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Gina}}
{{Term|TMC|Gina}} is a {{Term|TMC|Ghini|link}} found in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, in the grave to the right of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}'s. Her tomb is accessed by {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Dampé|link}}, and a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 100 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}} can be found against the back wall. {{Term|TMC|Gina}} is lonely,<ref>{{Cite|Ah, so you moved that gravestone, and now you can go outside... But I would get lonely here by myself, so could you come see me sometime?|Gina|TMC}}</ref> and enjoys {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s company.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you came back to visit! I'm so happy! I know this is a grave, but please, make yourself feel at home!|Gina|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with her twice;<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Yeah, OK.|Gina|TMC}}</ref> the first time causes a fallen {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}} to rise in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}, giving {{Term|TMC|Link}} access to a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing 100 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}}. The second {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} opens a waterfall in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, leading to a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} containing 200 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell}}.
