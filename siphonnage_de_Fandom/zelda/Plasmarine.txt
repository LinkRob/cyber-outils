{{Term/Store|Plasmarine||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} of {{Term|OoA|Jabu-Jabu's Belly|link}}, the seventh {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=E |publisher= |page=198}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|OoA|Plasmarine}} is a jellyfish-like creature that is battled in an underwater room, where {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} is unable to attack using his {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}}. {{Term|OoA|Plasmarine}} is susceptible to the {{Term|OoA|Long Hook|link}} and will alternate between its blue form and its red form each time {{Term|OoA|Link}} uses the item on it. {{Term|OoA|Plasmarine}}'s method of attack is to swim quickly to {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s location, stopping briefly to either shoot an electric ball toward him, or try to electrocute him on the spot. The electric ball produced is always the same color as the {{Term|OoA|Boss}}'s present form; {{Term|OoA|Link}} must use the {{Term|OoA|Long Hook}} to both displace the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} and switch its color, so that it gets hit by its own attack. As {{Term|OoA|Plasmarine}} takes damage, it will begin to fire more electric balls in succession. After taking enough damage, it will be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Heart Container|link}} and opening the door to the {{Term|OoA|Rolling Sea|link}}.
