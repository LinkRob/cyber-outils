{{Term/Store|Mane|Manes|Series, BotW}} are [[Object]]s in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you have a horse, we can help you change its {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|mane}} style.|Canni|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Manes are cosmetic changes that [[Link]] can make to his [[Horse]]s. There are a total of 10 different Manes that can be styled.

Link can change his Horse's Mane at certain [[Stable]]s. He must bring the Horse to the worker feeding the Horses to the side, who will ask if he is interested in changing his Horse's Mane or [[Horse Gear]].<ref>{{Cite|If you're interested, may I help you change your horse's {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|mane}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So you'd like me to change the {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|mane}} on this little one again, correct?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> Manes can only be changed if Link has a high bond with his Horse,<ref>{{Cite|Wow! This one's eyes are positively sparkling with affection! I can tell this little one is just head over heels for you. That settles it! I'll gladly help you out with some {{Color|BotW Blue|gear}}!|Canni|BotW}}</ref> otherwise the worker will refuse to help.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Oh? Your horse doesn't seem to be tamed. At all. Did you just catch it, by any chance? Please spend some time showing this sweetie you care, and then come back.|Canni|BotW}}</ref>
