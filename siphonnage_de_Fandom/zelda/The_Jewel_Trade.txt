"{{Term/Store|The Jewel Trade||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Jewel Trade}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Goron City}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has freed the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, he can encounter the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} merchant {{Term|BotW|Ramella|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her, she calls him bold for flirting with her in the middle of the day.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq. Flirting in the middle of the day, are we? Bold.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Ramella}} is disappointed if {{Term|BotW|Link}} refutes this.<ref>{{Cite|[That's not what I—] Oh. That's too bad.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ramella}} explains that she is originally from the {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm Ramella. As you can see, I'm from the Gerudo region.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> she came to {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} to find {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I came for the {{Color|BotW Blue|jewels}} that are to be found in Goron City.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|display=Death Mountain's}} eruptions have now stopped, she has begun restocking on {{Plural|BotW|Gem}}.<ref>{{Cite|The eruptions on {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}} have finally come to a stop. As a result, we've started restocking {{Color|BotW Blue|jewels}}.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ramella}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has any {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}, as she is in need of it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Do you have some {{Color|BotW Red|amber}} too? I need a whole lot of it.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> If he brings her 10, {{Term|BotW|Ramella}} promises pay him more compared to the usual selling price at stores.<ref>{{Cite|Bring me {{Color|BotW Red|10}} of them, and I'll buy them for more than a shop would pay!|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has 10 pieces of {{Term|BotW|Amber}}, {{Term|BotW|Ramella}} will ask him to sell to her,<ref>{{Cite|In any case, do you have {{Color|BotW Red|10 pieces of amber}}? If so, will you sell them to me?|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> offering him 500 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as payment.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, 10 pieces of amber... Sell them to the shop, and you'll get 300 rupees. Or sell them to me for {{Color|BotW Red|500 rupees}}!|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} sells to her, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete. From this point on, {{Term|BotW|Ramella}} will offer to pay extra whenever {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings her 10 pieces of a randomly chosen {{Term|BotW|Gem}}.<ref>{{Cite|I need to procure other {{Color|BotW Blue|jewels}} too, so keep bringing me what you find. Bring me {{Color|BotW Red|10}} at a time, got it? As for what kinds of jewels I want to buy, well... That's likely to change {{Color|BotW Red|every once in a while}}.|Ramella|BotW}}</ref>
