{{Term/Store|Judo||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>

==Biography==
Judo is one part of the twin dancing group, the [[Rosa Sisters]], which itself is a part of the [[Gorman Troupe]]. Alongside her sister, [[Marilla]], she dances as part of the [[Gorman Troupe]] for the annual [[Carnival of Time]]. She and Marilla hail from an unspecified village in [[Termina]].<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I'll blame it on the troupe leader and go back to the village... But Judo wouldn't like it.|Marilla|MM3D}}</ref>

During the daytime, Judo can be found pacing in the halls of the [[Stock Pot Inn]]. There, she considers potential moves that she can add into the dance performance,<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm... Ah, well, that won't work... No... That's too basic...|Judo|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We need a new step... Maybe with more jumping...|Judo|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Marilla likes this choreography, but she doesn't get into it at all...|Judo|MM3D}}</ref> and worries whether they will be ready in time.<ref>{{Cite|We have to be ready without any rehearsal this time... Every time...|Judo|MM3D}}</ref> At night, Judo can be seen practicing her dance with Marilla in [[West Clock Town]].

==Trivia==
*In {{MM3D}}, if Gorman informs the Rosa Sisters of the concert's cancellation after Link teaches them Kamaro's Dance, they will still ask him to attend their performance. This may be due to a development oversight.
