The {{Term/Store|Water Medallion||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an important item in {{OoT}}.<ref name="Get">{{Cite|You received the {{Color|OoT3D Dark Blue|Water Medallion}}! Ruto awakens as a Sage and adds her power to yours!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Water Medallion}} is the {{Term|OoT3D|Sage Medallion|link}} that {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} gains after defeating {{Term|OoT3D|Morpha|link}} in the {{Term|OoT3D|Water Temple|link}}.<ref name="Get"/> It is given to him by the newly awakened {{Term|OoT3D|Sage|link}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Ruto|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can tell that nothing will stop you in your quest for justice and peace... You must take this {{Color|OoT3D Dark Blue|Medallion}}... Take it respectfully!|Princess Ruto|OoT3D}}</ref> Upon obtaining this item, the [[Zora]] princess tells the [[Hero of Time]] that although she still loves him dearly, their childhood engagement by her giving him the [[Spiritual Stone of Water|Zora's Sapphire]] must be put into an indefinite hold due to her duty, and bestowed her powers onto him as a symbol of their relationship.

== Trivia ==
*In pre-release versions of {{OoT|-}}, the Water Medallion was called the '''Ice Medal''' due to the Water Temple originally planned to be ice-themed, and its emblem resembling ice. Equipping it with arrows would have resulted in the [[Ice Arrow]]s, while its use as a Medallion would have created waves of ice surrounding Link, akin to [[Din's Fire]].{{Fact|OoT}}
