==Features and Overview==
Marot Mart is a shopping center owned by {{Term|BotW|Marot|link}} that is located in [[Zora's Domain]]. It bears two [[Shop]]s: the [[Coral Reef]] and the [[Hammerhead]].

===Coral Reef===
{{Main|Coral Reef}}
The Coral Reef is a [[General Store]] that is operated by [[Cleff]].<ref>{{Cite|[The general store?] Our general store has been in business for over 100 years. It has a long and honorable history. The shopkeep, Cleff, is really serious. But his younger sister, Tona, is head over fins for Prince Sidon! Kee hee!|Marot|BotW}}</ref>

===Hammerhead===
{{Main|Hammerhead}}
The Hammerhead is a workshop operated by {{Term|BotW|Marot|link}}'s grandfather, {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[The workshop?] The old man in the workshop, his name is Dento... He's my grandpa! He's the one who fixed Mipha's weapon, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}.|Marot|BotW}}</ref>
