{{Term/Store|Uten Marsh||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Uten Marsh'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Uten Marsh is located in [[East Necluda]] between the [[Necluda Sea]] and [[Mount Floria]]. The Uten Marsh is a very shallow pond which only goes up to [[Link]]'s leg, but Link is still able to use the [[Cryonis]] Rune in the water. The marsh is dense with plant life and a few [[Tree]]s grow throughout the area.

In the middle of the Uten Marsh, a [[Blue Hinox]] sleeps on a small area of land the water doesn't reach. This Hinox is part of a [[Shrine Quest]] called "[[The Three Giant Brothers]]", where three Hinox brothers each have a [[Ancient Orb]] hanging around their neck. This Hinox that sleeps in the marsh is given the title; "''Hinox (Middle Kin)''" as this Hinox is the middle of the three brothers. Along with the Ancient Orb, a [[Dragonbone Boko Spear]] and a [[Dragonbone Boko Club]] also hang from the Hinox's neck. 

A short distance southwest from the sleeping area of the Hinox, a stone chest holding a bundle of five [[Ice Arrow]]s can be found underneath a tree.
