===Event Procedure===
The trading sequence is available at any time after the {{Term|TWWHD|Delivery Bag|link}} has been received. {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}}'s first {{Term|TWWHD|Decoration|link}} is a {{Term|TWWHD|Town Flower|link}} from {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}}, gifted to the young hero in the hopes that by trading it, his shop will increase its products. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can then trade this to the {{Plural|TWWHD|Wandering Merchant|link}} on the {{Term|TWWHD|Mother & Child Isles|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Greatfish Isle|link}}, and {{Term|TWWHD|Bomb Island|link}}. After every trade, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} will lose his original {{Term|TWWHD|Decoration}} and some {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} in exchange for another {{Term|TWWHD|Decoration}}. Because of the {{Term|TWWHD|Merchant's Oath|link}}, the {{Plural|TWWHD|Wandering Merchant}} will send the {{Term|TWWHD|Decoration}} they traded to {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari's Shop|link}}. After {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} retrieves all of the flower {{Plural|TWWHD|Decoration}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Zunari}} will give {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} the {{Term|TWWHD|Magic Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes! Young master, look! Already, my shop has a wide variety of products to choose from! It is a cornucopia! Thanks to you, young master, every morning the postman comes with packages from the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|traveling salesmen}} you traded with. At last, my humble stall has the appearance of a full-fledged store! My dear! Your unflagging dedication has strengthened my determination to live my life for the success of my business! Master, you are the savior of my stall! I must thank you from the very bottom of my heart. This is my most prized possession—next to my hoodie coat, of course! It is none other than the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Zunari}} family treasure... Please accept it!|Zunari|TWWHD}}</ref> At the end of the sequence he can trade the {{Term|TWWHD|Shop Guru Statue|link}} to the merchant of {{Term|TWWHD|Greatfish Island}} to receive a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}}.

With the availability of the new products in {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can decorate the entire island with them. If he does so, {{Term|TWWHD|Sam|link}}, who is sitting on the bench near {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo|link}}'s house, will reward him with a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart}} for brining joy to the island.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Windfall just seems to be filled with joy lately... Don't you think? Say, you've been cooperating with the volunteers, have you? You know what I'm talking about? Decorating the town? Oh, really? ...You have? Wow, it never fails to amaze me how kind and thoughtful folks can be. ...I know! I'll return your kindness with some of my own!|Sam|TWWHD}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*This trading sequence—possibly all the trading sequences of the series—may be inspired by the Japanese legend of the {{wp|Straw Millionaire}}, a Buddhist folk tale about a peasant who became a millionaire through a series of successive trades.
