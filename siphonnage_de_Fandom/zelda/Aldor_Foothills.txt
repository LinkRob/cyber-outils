The {{Term/Store|Aldor Foothills||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Aldor Foothills'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Aldor Foothills}} act as a southern foundation for {{Term|BotW|Mount Drena|link}}, directly west of {{Term|BotW|Lake Mekar|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}. They consist of multiple levels of rocky terrain that slowly increase in steepness. The lower area of the {{Term|BotW|Aldor Foothills|display=Foothills}} is patrolled by a {{Term|BotW|Blue Lynel|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} sleeps on the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Aldor Foothills}} within a clump of {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}. It carries a {{Term|BotW|Royal Bow|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Royal Claymore|link}} around its neck.

A single {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Aldor Foothills}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must destroy the pottery hidden in a fallen {{Term|BotW|Tree}}, which is located near the ruins at the base of the {{Term|BotW|Aldor Foothills|display=Foothills}}. When this is done, the {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will reveal itself and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
