The {{Term/Store|Rabbit Net||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Rabbit Net|link}} is given to {{Term|ST|Link|link}} by {{Term|ST|Bunnio|link}} upon visiting the {{Term|ST|Rabbitland Rescue|link}}. It is used to catch {{Plural|ST|Rabbit|link}} that are scattered throughout the land of {{Term|ST|New Hyrule|link}}. The {{Term|ST|Rabbit Net}} is not a regularly usable {{Term|ST|Item}}; it can only be used while {{Term|ST|Link}} is aboard the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}, and it is automatically readied when the young hero fires a cannonball at a {{Term|ST|Boulder|link}} or a {{Term|ST|Barrel|link}} with a {{Term|ST|Rabbit}} hiding behind it. He will then have ten seconds to deploy the {{Term|ST|Rabbit Net}} onto the {{Term|ST|Rabbit}} while it hops around the screen. As the {{Term|ST|Rabbit Net}} drops slowly in comparison to the {{Term|ST|Rabbit}} in question, a careful technique must be used to anticipate the {{Term|ST|Rabbit}}'s next resting point in order to catch it. Once captured, they can be taken to the {{Term|ST|Rabbitland Rescue}} for a cumulative reward.
