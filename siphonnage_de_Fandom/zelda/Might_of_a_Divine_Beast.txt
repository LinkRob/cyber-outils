{{Term/Store|Might of a Divine Beast||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Might of a Divine Beast|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Might of a Divine Beast}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} as its pilot. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Swift Violet|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Armored Porgy|link}}, and 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}'s objective is to defeat 4,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within three minutes. The different {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Lynel|link}}, many {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} scattered along a path that makes up the entire map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 4,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
