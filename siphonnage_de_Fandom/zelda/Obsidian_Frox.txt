The '''Obsidian Frox''' is a sub-boss in {{TotK}}. These [[Frox]]es are the second strongest variant and have a black or obsidian colored body. They also use the same moves as the other Froxes except belly flop you 2 times instead of 1 or 3. These Froxes are also able to detect you from far distances such as any other one and are found next to [[Zonaite]] deposits and drop Obsidian Frox fangs. They also drop crystalized charges which you can use for [[Energy Cell]]s and are much more resillient, having 1350 HP.
