==Location and Uses==
[[File:Ant (M).png|left]]
[[File:Ant (F).png|left|47px]]
The male and female Dayfly are both located within the {{Term|TP|Gerudo Desert|link}}. They can be given to [[Agitha]] in exchange for different items, such as a bigger [[Wallet]], [[Rupee]]s, or her [[Miiverse Stamp]] in {{TPHD}}. If collected a second time, they will drop a [[Heart]].
