The {{Term/Store|House of Skulltula||Series}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 240}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The House of Skulltula is a gray building located in the southeast part of {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}. The inside of the house is bare and covered by cobwebs, ruined furniture can be found as well as a fireplace, a book with the [[Sheikah]] symbol and various portraits of spiders. According to an [[Professor Shikashi]], the extremely rich family who used to live within became cursed due to their greed.<ref>{{Cite|Folks around here tell of a fabulously rich family that once lived in one of the houses in this village... But they say that the entire family was cursed due to their greed! Who knows what might happen to those who are consumed by greed...|Professor Shikashi|OoT}}</ref> When [[Link]] first enters the house, he finds it is inhabited by the [[Cursed Rich Man]] and his five sons who have taken the shape of [[Skulltula]]s due to a curse bestowed upon them by the [[Gold Skulltula|Spiders of the Curse]]. The Cursed Rich Man goes on to explain that the only way their curse will be lifted is if all of the [[Gold Skulltula]]s around [[Hyrule]] are destroyed and if their Gold Skulltula Token is collected.<ref>{{Cite|We all look like this because of the spider's curse. If every Spider of the Curse in the entire world were destroyed, the curse would be broken.|Cursed Rich Man|OoT}}</ref> 

The Cursed Rich Man persuades Link to help them by promising the young hero great riches if he and his sons are all freed from their curse.<ref>{{Cite|If you break the curse on my family, we will make you very rich...|Cursed Rich Man|OoT}}</ref> As such, Link must set out and seek out 100 Gold Skulltula Tokens to restore the family back to its former state. The young hero will earn their grateful awards each time he destroys a certain amount of Gold Skulltulas.<ref>{{Cite|The curse has been broken! Thank you! Here's a reward for you!|Hylian|OoT}}</ref>

Once Link has collected all 100 Gold Skulltula Tokens, all the family members will return back to their original form, and the Rich Man will award the young hero with a [[Huge Rupee]] worth 200 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You have received a Huge Rupee! It is worth a whopping 200 Rupees!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> Link can receive as many Huge Rupees as he wants each time he leaves the house and returns to talk to the Rich Man.

==Trivia==
*It is possible to strike any of the cursed family members with the sword in their back. This will result in them crying in pain and getting stunned for a moment. When they are no longer stunned, they will spin around once. If Link approaches them, they will attack him. After that, they revert to normal. Leaving the house and then re-entering also returns any of them back to their normal behavior.
*Similarly to the rich family cursed by the Skulltula, in {{TP}}, Link must save a man named [[Jovani]] who has sold his soul to the [[Poe]]s for untold riches. Link can also lift the curse by obtaining 60 [[Poe Soul]]s.
