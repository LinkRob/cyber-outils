{{Plural|BotW|Pot Lid}} are lids of large soup pots that can be used as {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} in combat. It is said that they smell of poultry,<ref name="Inventory" /> likely due to being used for {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}. They are also considered to be quite {{Term|BotW|Durability|link|display=Durable}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can often be found next to {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} at {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}} or other settlements, such as {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. They can also be found in some {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}, where they are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, and rarely, {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}.

According to {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Training Journal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} once used a {{Term|BotW|Pot Lid}} to defeat a {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} by deflecting its laser after it began acting erratically during an experiment.<ref>{{Cite|I hear one of those ancient machine things went nuts during some test and shot out deadly beams! With true Goron spirit, Link grabbed a pot lid from the ground and deflected the blast. He nailed the thing in its weak spot and saved the day.|Daruk's Training Journal|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Pot Lid}} will burn and eventually disintegrate if they touch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or are equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Pot Lid}} cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
