==Biography==
==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Zill and Friends}}

{{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)}} is a little girl in {{TMC}} who resides with her family, {{Term|TMC|Emma|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Lolly|link}}, in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. She is first seen during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, blocking the way forward until {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains a {{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}}. She mentions that she has never seen any {{Plural|TMC|Minish|link}} before.<ref>{{Cite|I've never seen the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}} before...|Candy (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> She encircles {{Term|TMC|Link}} along with the {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|link}} when he returns from the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}, extolling the virtues of {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Blue|Fusing Kinstones}} is a blast!|Candy (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, she can be found standing in front of the {{Term|TMC|Fountain|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Lolly|link}}, asking to see the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, I wanna see the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}}, too! Can I? Can I? Huh? Can I?|Candy (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)}} moves to the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, where she runs around with {{Term|TMC|Lolly}} and {{Term|TMC|Zill|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey!!! Wait up!|Candy (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> Here, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's do it!|Candy (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> causing a shoal to rise in {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}; it gives {{term|TMC|Link}} access to a {{Term|TMC|Secret Cave|link}} once he has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}.

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)}} moves to stand outside the newly-opened {{Term|TMC|Chest Mini-Game Shop|link}}, bemoaning the fact that she cannot enter.<ref>{{Cite|I wanna play games, too!|Candy (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> She moves back outside, wandering north of the {{Term|TMC|Fountain}}, after {{Term|TMC|Link}} sees the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. Here, she asks if a {{Term|TMC|Ghost|link}} is in the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Well|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is there really a ghost in the well?|Candy (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Candy (The Minish Cap)}} moves with her friends to the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} receives a golden {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} from {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}. By the time {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, she returns to the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn}} and remains there for the remainder of the game.
