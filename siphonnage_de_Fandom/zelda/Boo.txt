As spooky ghosts go, this one's pretty cute!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Boo}} were referred to as {{Term/Store|Boo Buddy|Boo Buddies|LA, LADX|plural}} in all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}} and as '''Boo Buddles''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 99}}</ref> However, as these contradict the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, they are not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} They are directly based on the {{Smw|Boo}} enemies from the ''{{Smw|Mario (series)|Mario}}'' series.

==Characteristics==
Boo Buddies only appear in a single dark room in the [[Bottle Grotto]], housing the [[Power Bracelet]]. Unlike their ''Mario'' counterparts, which are typically shy and hide when stared at, they do not shy away from [[Link]] and actively pursue him in the darkness of the room. When struck with the [[Sword]] they disappear, only to reappear elsewhere and continue their assault. They can only be defeated by lighting one of the [[torch]]es in the room with [[Magic Powder]]; doing so will weaken them and turn them dark as they flee in defeat. At this point, Link can strike them with his blade to destroy them, or wait until they have left, upon which point the [[Treasure Chest|chest]] containing the Power Bracelet will appear.

==Trivia==
*Using light to weaken or defeat enemies occurs in many later ''Zelda'' games. A number of enemies, such as [[ReDead]]s and [[Nocturn]] (a very similar ghost enemy), can be defeated in this way.
*In the Nintendo Switch version of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Link's Awakening]]'', Boos are voiced by {{Smw|Sanae Suzaki}}, who voices them since ''{{Smw|Mario Power Tennis}}''.
*Boo was designed by Takashi Tezuka, who remarked that his wife is normally described as quiet and shy, but she one day exploded in a fit of rage, maddened by all the time Tezuka spent at work. That later gave Tezuka the idea of "putting his wife in the game", where Boo first appeared in ''Super Mario Bros. 3''.

==Nomenclature==
Boo Buddy's Japanese name, Teresa, comes from the Japanese word {{Romanize|照れる|Tereru||1}}, meaning "to be shy", fitting their bashful nature.
