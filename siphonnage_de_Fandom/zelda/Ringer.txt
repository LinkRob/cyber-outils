A {{Term/Store|Ringer|Ringers|Series, SS|plural}} are animals in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ringers are tiny animals that gather around Link if he skydives from a high altitude above Pumpkin Landing.|book= HH |publisher= |page= 36}}</ref> It is a type of flying [[Squirrel]] that can be encountered in groups when skydiving to an island in the [[Sky]] from great heights.

==Characteristics==
The Ringers are harmless creatures that come in various colors: orange, red, green, blue and purple, and usually appear in groups of ten. If [[Link]] touches them as he is diving, they will begin to form a circle around the young hero. Touching all of the Ringers before landing on the island will yield a reward of 20 [[Rupee]]s.
