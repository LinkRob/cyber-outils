{{Term/Store|Jora||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Jora|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Jora}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who travels the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} alongside his friend, {{Term|BotW|Dillie|link}}. The pair can be found under attack by two {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, during which time he states he is too pretty to die.<ref>{{Cite|Aaaaah! Noooo! I'm too pretty to die!|Jora|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} saves them, {{Term|BotW|Jora}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Chilly Egg Pudding|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Where'd you learn how to do THAT?! And more importantly, can you teach me? Here, I'll give you this!|Jora|BotW}}</ref>

Once he has been saved, {{Term|BotW|Jora}} will express his excitement to find his future wife in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Time to hit Gerudo Town in style! Wouldn't want to disappoint my future wife by running late!|Jora|BotW}}</ref> He and {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} will then begin to scout their surroundings.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Jora}}, he will explain that they are traversing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} in order to find {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're on our way to Gerudo Town. Course, we need to make it out of this huge desert, first.|Jora|BotW}}</ref> While he has been relying on {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} for directions, {{Term|BotW|Jora}} believes that they will be popular once they arrive.<ref>{{Cite|But Dillie knows the way, so we should be find. Once we get there, we're hitting the big time!|Jora|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Dillie}} then reveals that he has been following {{Term|BotW|Jora}},<ref>{{Cite|Uh, I thought you knew the way. I was following you, right? Uh-oh...|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> at which point {{Term|BotW|Jora}} asks when they decided he was the leader.<ref name="Leading">{{Cite|I'm leading the way?! What?! When did we decide this?|Jora|BotW}}</ref>

Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Dillie}}, he will try to cheer {{Term|BotW|Jora}} up by reminding him that they will be in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} soon.<ref>{{Cite|You OK, buddy? C'mon, there's no use crying over spilled milk. We're headed to Gerudo Town!|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> He offers to let {{Term|BotW|Jora}} lead the way,<ref>{{Cite|I'll even let you lead the way!|Dillie|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Jora}} is anxious about this.<ref name="Leading"/>
