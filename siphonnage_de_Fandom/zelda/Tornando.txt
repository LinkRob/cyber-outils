'''Tornando''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Tornando|This mischievous monster cloaks itself in a miniature tornado and bursts out from sandstorms to knock foes backward.<ref name="Tornando">{{Cite|This mischievous monster cloaks itself in a miniature tornado and bursts out from sandstorms to knock foes backward.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Tornandoes are [[frog]]-like monsters engulfed in a wind [[tornado]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Tornando [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and it costs two Echo energy to summon. Tornandoes are required to complete the [[Tornado Ghost?]] [[Side Quest]].
