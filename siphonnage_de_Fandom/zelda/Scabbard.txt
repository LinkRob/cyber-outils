{{Term/Store|Scabbard|Scabbards|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|OoT3D, MM3D, TWWHD, TPHD, PH, ST, SSHD}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Scabbard}} are a type of sheath used to hold [[Sword|Swords]]. In the franchise and other media, specifically the 3D games (as they are not visible in 2D titles), [[Link]] normally has a scabbard for his swords. The most ornate scabbards tend to be for the most advanced sword, which is usually, but not always, the [[Master Sword]].
[[File:Link back view (Switch games).webp|left|thumb|204x204px|Link's Master Sword scabbard on his back as seen in the [[Nintendo Switch]] games ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]''/''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'']]
Link himself, in spite of being a master swordsman, infamously wears his scabbards clipped onto a shoulder belt instead of at the hip. This, along with the choice of keeping his [[Shield|Shields]] on his back further behind such equipment, although unrealistic, creates his signature image that is instantly recognized, according to [[Nintendo]]. For most swords, the scabbards are automatically changed with them. Though a select few share the same scabbard.  Some scabbards have mouths, the name for the opening of the scabbard, that matches the sword’s guard. These parts of the sword form what is known as a chape, which when fitted at the mouth makes a seal when the sword is in the scabbard. The swords and scabbards that have these attributes tend to be the most ornate.

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{OoT}} introduced scabbards to the core series. The scabbard changes depending on Link's age, so the [[Kokiri Sword]] had one scabbard and the [[Master Sword]], [[Giant's Knife]] and [[Biggoron's Sword]] shared a different one. In {{OoT3D}}, the latter two blades were given a unique scabbard to match their much longer appearance, and a strap was added to Link's model as shown in official artwork (the original [[Nintendo 64]] version lacks it due to graphical limitations).

==={{MM|-}}===
{{MM}} had a scabbard for all the normal [[Controller Buttons|B-button]] swords. The powerful [[Great Fairy's Sword]] and [[Fierce Deity Sword]] lacked scabbards. Unlike ''Ocarina of Time'', these scabbards are notable for having very accurate sizes in regards to the sword they hold. The [[Gilded Sword]]'s scabbard is absolutely massive and sometimes clips the ground as Link moves. It is also the first in-game appearance of Link's shoulder-strap.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{TWW}} had two scabbards, one for each of the swords. All the stages of the [[Master Sword]] used the same scabbard. These scabbards followed the style of {{TWW|-}} of being cel shaded smaller in size.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{TP}} had scabbards for two of the three swords. The [[Wooden Sword]] was only held by straps instead of a full scabbard. These scabbards have more detail in them than any other game before or since. Sometimes other characters carry scabbards for their own swords. One notable scabbard here is the one for the smaller sword of a [[Darknut]] in the game. When the Darknut pulls out this sword, its scabbard gets thrown to the side. There is also the scabbard that [[Ganondorf]] uses to carry the [[Sword of the Sages]] during the final battle, which appears to be made out of tape put together in the center.

===Out-of-Game Scabbards===
Scabbards have technically existed as early as ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', with the introduction of them being on Link's back occurred in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]''. These games, alongside ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'', {{FS}}, {{FSA}}, and {{TMC}} do not depict Link with a scabbard in-game, usually because his sword is always drawn and those installments are in 2D graphics. However, Link is depicted with a scabbard in some artwork for the games. His scabbard in ''The Legend of Zelda'', ''A Link to the Past'' and {{LA|-}} appears as a dark brown with a red jewel at the bottom. The scabbards seen in {{FS|-}}, {{FSA|-}} and {{TMC|-}} appear identical to the Hero's Sword's scabbard seen in {{TWW|-}}.

== Trivia ==
* It's likely the idea of [[Link]] wearing scabbards on his back was not originally planned by the franchise's debut in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', as official artwork of the earliest games until ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' had them on his hip or carrying the equipment on his hand.[[File:Hero's Spirit scabbard.png|thumb|361x361px|The hidden scabbard on the Hero's Spirit as seen in ''Twilight Princess'']]
* Although often missed by players, in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', the [[Hero's Spirit]], who is the restless soul of the legendary [[Hero of Time]] (the Link of the N64 games), actually wears a scabbard and is briefly seen when he teaches Link, the Hero of Twilight, the seven [[Hidden Skill|Hidden Skills]]. In this case, he properly carries it on his hip rather than on his back. The scabbard itself is a worn-out version of the [[Master Sword]]'s, despite the blade he uses is completely different.
* In the original [[Family Computer Disk System]] TV commercial for ''The Legend of Zelda'' in Japan, during the animation of when Link is [[Game Over|defeated]] and shown speaking in frustration toward the viewers, a scabbard is visible on him, and it's correctly carried on the hero's hip. This would technically mark the first appearance of the scabbard itself in any ''Zelda'' media.
* In ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', whenever Link uses any of his unlockable weapons, the scabbard he wears for his Master Sword seemingly vanishes, even with the [[Mirror Shield]] where he wields the Blade of Evil's Bane. It is unknown why it occurs, though it is presumably by graphical limitations. The aforementioned weapons themselves appear attached onto the character's back like a scabbard when not in use, which can look rather comical when equipped with the [[Biggoron's Sword]], [[Megaton Hammer]], [[Cane of Byrna|Cane of Bryna]] or [[Bug Net]] (easily noticeable when using the [[Boomerang]]).[[File:Early Octorok.jpg|thumb|216x216px|An early scabbard seen in the beta version of ''Ocarina of Time'']]
* During early development of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the [[Kokiri Sword]] and Master Sword's scabbards both appear to be entirely made of wood.
* Exceptionally long swords such as the Biggoron's Sword are known to have Scabards with lengths about as much as Adult Link's own height, as showcased in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]''.
* Reflecting the rather impractical nature of Link carrying his scabbards on his back in a real-life combat scenario due to impossible geometry, aside from exposing his body while reaching back, there have been some instances of cosplayers playing as the main character himself having trouble unsheathing the Master Sword, as they would at times get stuck or hurting their arms or shoulders due to the length of the blade, and is even more difficult putting it back in without taking the scabbard itself off. ''[https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby_(series) Kirby]''/''[[Super Smash Bros. (series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'' creator Masahiro Sakurai even gave insight about Link's animations in the latter series were the hero lowers his sheathe in order to fit his weapon cleanly into the scabbard, unlike [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' series]] where he simply contorts his wrist.
