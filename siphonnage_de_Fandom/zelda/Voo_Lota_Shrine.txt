The {{Term/Store|Voo Lota Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Voo Lota Shrine}}<br/>The Winding Route}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''The Winding Route''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine}} is located in at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}, west of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is initially buried beneath the ground, but once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has completed the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}} singing there on her own.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Grrrrrr! My sisters are dumb DUMMIES!}} They promised we'd rehearse together, but NO ONE showed up!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to remind her sisters of their rehearsal,<ref>{{Cite|Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal? They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you.|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> which begins the "{{Term|BotW|Recital at Warbler's Nest|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

In {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Genli|link}} by the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, who demands {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière|link}} before practicing her singing.<ref>{{Cite|Hurry! Hurry! {{Big|I NEED {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} in my belly!}} I know {{Color|BotW Blue|Kheel}} wants me to practice singing with her, but I just can't. Not until I get my {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> The rest of her and {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}'s sisters went to gather ingredients for her,<ref>{{Cite|My sisters already left to get {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} ingredients. I told them I wouldn't sing until I get it. I didn't expect them to actually make it for me, though!|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can find all three remaining sisters throughout {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}. {{Term|BotW|Cree|link}} is found in {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}}, where she will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}} she bought if he promises to make {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}} for {{Term|BotW|Genli}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I give you this {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}, will you make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli? She should be in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}}.|Cree|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kotts|link}} is found fishing at the ponds leading into {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}},<ref>{{Cite|Hiah! HIYAH! I'm fishing for {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}}!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> having forgotten about practice.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? What are you doing here? What? Rehearsal? AHHH! I totally forgot! I was too focused on catching these fish.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> She will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please take this {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} and go make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Finally, {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}} is found on a ledge overlooking {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}}. Since she finished gathering her ingredient early, she decided to start practicing.<ref>{{Cite|What? Singing rehearsal at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's next}}? I didn't forget! It's just that Genlie really wanted some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}, so my sisters and I have been looking for ingredients. I finished early, so I was practicing here to kill time.|Notts|BotW}}</ref> She will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} her {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}} so he can make {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well then...I guess I'll give the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}} to you! Please make the best {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli!|Notts|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has sent the three sisters to {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Genli}} at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooks}} {{Term|BotW|Salmon Meunière}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} recognizes the smell and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to let her have some.<ref>{{Cite|You're going to let me have some, right? RIGHT?!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> If he gives it to her, {{Term|BotW|Genli}} decides to keep her promise and head to {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? My promise to my sisters? I suppose I shouldn't break my promise...|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find all five sisters singing a song. While standing on the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}, he can use a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}} to blow through holes in the rocks nearby, in correlation to the sisters song. When this is done, {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine}} will appear.<ref>{{Cite|You solved the mystery behind the Rito Village song and Warbler's Nest, uncovering an ancient shrine in the process! The sisters, thrilled to practice their songs and find a secret, went back to Rito Village.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Voo Lota. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Voo Lota|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
In front of {{Term|BotW|Link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Ladder|link}} extends high into the air. Atop the {{Term|BotW|Ladder}}, a short tunnel leads to a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} walks close to the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}, the doorway he passed through will close behind him. Hitting the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} will open the floor from underneath {{Term|BotW|Link}} and plunge him towards {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to escape by {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=Paragliding}} into a fan, which will blow him to a platform and a locked gate which conceals the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. To open the portcullis, he must jump west of the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} and {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paraglide}} to another platform, stopping before he hits a wall of {{Plural|BotW|Floor Spikes|link}}. He must then walk to the end of the path, where a fan can blow him upward to another platform.

Again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must jump and glide over and then under more {{Plural|BotW|Floor Spikes}} and land on another platform. On this platform, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}} sits next to a wall of spikes. He then makes his way back to the original platform and unlock the gate blocking the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

Upon approaching the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota}} will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trail speaks the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Voo Lota|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Voo Lota}} wishes him well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Voo Lota|BotW}}</ref>

On the last platform before gliding back and unlocking the door, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found on a separate platform near containing a {{Term|BotW|Flameblade|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} diverts his course for this {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, he will be able to make his way back to the main path via a few fans.
