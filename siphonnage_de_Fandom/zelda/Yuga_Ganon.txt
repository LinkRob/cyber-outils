{{Term/Store|Yuga Ganon||Series, ALBW}} is the joint form of {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Ganon|link}} that serves as the final {{Term|ALBW|Boss|link}} of {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW|publisher= Prima|page=286}}</ref>{{Note|The beast is only referred to as "Yuga" or the "Demon King" in-game. The name "Yuga Ganon" comes from the [[Prima Games|Prima]] Official Strategy Guide.}} The beast results from {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} fusing with {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}} after the former resurrects the latter, and takes on the combined appearances and abilities of the two.

==Biography==
In {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} uses the descendants of the [[Sage#A Link Between Worlds|Seven Sages]] along with {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda|link}} to resurrect the Demon King and holder of the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Power|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}}, who {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} claims will help him attain true beauty.<ref>{{Cite|You've arrived just in time! A splendid collection, don't you think? All Seven Sages, and Zelda herself... But even they pale in comparison to my NEXT artful creation. ME! I will use the Sages to reach the one who shall help me attain true beauty. I mean, of course... GANON!|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref> After releasing {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}} from his seal in darkness, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} proceeds to fuse with {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}}'s form and the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Power}},<ref>{{Cite|Behold! The Triforce of Power! Join with me! Let us destroy all ugliness in this world or any other. We shall be TRULY superior!|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref> transforming into a being that takes on the physical attributes of both {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} and {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}}. Now possessing the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Power}}, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} attacks {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}}, but his attack is restrained by the magic of a mysterious princess named {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|link|display=Hilda}}, who informs {{Term|ALBW|Link}} that she can only hold {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} at bay for so long and welcomes the young hero to her kingdom, {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Hero of Hyrule, I can hold the beast at bay for so long. It should prove enough time to bid you a most sorrowful welcome to my kingdom. Welcome to Lorule. My name is Princess Hilda and I have failed you in every way.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> She informs the young hero that she knew of {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}}'s plot to abduct {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}'s Sages and {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}, but had failed to stop him, allowing {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} to use them to siphon {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}}'s power in order to gain enough power to destroy both {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}.<ref>{{Cite|I knew Yuga planned to slip into your world to abduct Zelda and the Sages. I...I couldn't stop him. Now he has used them to summon the Demon King and siphon his power. Yuga's appetite will soon consume our worlds. Hyrule and Lorule-the beast's for the taking.|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|display=Hilda}} asks {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to awaken his full potential in order to defeat {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} by stealing back the {{Plural|ALBW|Painting|link}} of the Seven Sages, which have been sent to different locations throughout {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}} as a backlash from {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}}'s transformation.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid I must ask you to do what I cannot-defeat the beast. To do that, you most awaken your full potential, Hero of Hyrule. I sense that the paintings of Hyrule's Seven Sages are being sent to the far corners of Lorule. You must steal those paintings back. The secret to Yuga's defeat lies in uniting your friends!|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> 

After rescuing the Seven Sages and attaining the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Courage|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Impa|link}} reveals to {{Term|ALBW|Link}} that {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}} is returning and that he must travel to {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}} to stop the {{Term|ALBW|Ganon|display=Demon King}}. After reaching the top of {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} finds {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}}, who tells him the legend of how {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}} fell into its current state. Upon finishing the tale, {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|display=Hilda}} steals the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} from {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} and summons {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}}, ordering the beast to steal the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Courage}} from {{Term|ALBW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|As the Princess of Lorule, it is my duty to save my kingdom. So I know you'll understand when I say... I must have your Triforce of Courage!|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yuga! I command you! Seize the Triforce of Courage from him! Lorule shall be reborn!|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> but {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} is defeated in battle. {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}}, still believing that her court wizard is loyal to her, orders him to give his {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Power}} to her.<ref>{{Cite|Give me your Triforce of Power, Yuga! We mustn't be defeated! If we are, Lorule will be lost forever! Come now, obey me! Give it to me at once!|Hilda|ALBW}}</ref> However, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} reveals that he has no concern for {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}'s fate, and merely planned to remake {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}} in his own image, using {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}} in his plans.<ref>{{Cite|Ha ha ha ha... My dear, sweet, deluded Hilda...! What care have I to save your crumbling kingdom. It's no better than that hideous Hyrule. When the Triforce is mine, I plan to remake Lorule in MY image. Now you, my dark beauty, must serve your purpose.|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} promptly turns {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}} into a {{Term|ALBW|Painting}} and absorbs her along with the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Wisdom}}, transforming into an even stronger beast. Stating that he will soon take his rightful place among the gods,<ref>{{Cite|Ha ha ha! I shall soon take my rightful place among the gods! And then the beauty of destruction will rain down upon this world! Now, I'll be taking that last piece of the Triforce from you, worm! How far you've wriggled! But at last you know your true destiny-to give me what's MINE!|Yuga|ALBW}}</ref> {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} engages {{Term|ALBW|Link}} in a final battle for the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Courage}}, but he is defeated and finally destroyed, releasing {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|display=Hilda}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce}} pieces.

==Characteristics==
===First Phase===
In the first phase of the fight, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} attacks in a similar way to how {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}} does in {{ALttP}}. {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} frequently warps around the arena, and does so more often as he takes damage. In the first stages of the fight, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} mainly attacks by thrusting his [[Trident]] at {{Term|ALBW|Link}}, or slashing the young hero with it, much like a {{Term|Series|Sword|link}}. Both attacks can be avoided by running out of the way fast enough, but extra caution should be taken when attempting to attack {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} with the {{Term|ALBW|Sword}}, as it will make {{Term|ALBW|Link}} more vulnerable to {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}}'s slash attack. The safest way of hitting {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} without getting hit is to dodge his Trident attacks, then quickly attack him from behind to avoid being struck should {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} attack soon thereafter. Eventually, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} will start throwing his Trident at {{Term|ALBW|Link}}, and occasionally warp out of the arena to launch energy balls from the sky. These energy balls appear immediately above {{Term|ALBW|Link}}, so the young hero must keep running to avoid them all. Eventually, the fight will end, and the second phase will begin.

{{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda}} casts a ring of dark magic around the room prior to the fight, preventing {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|link}}.

===Second phase===
In the second phase of the fight, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} transforms {{Term|ALBW|Princess Hilda|display=Hilda}} into a {{Term|ALBW|Painting}} to absorb her power and that of the {{Term|ALBW|Triforce of Wisdom}}. {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} will initiate the fight by throwing a blue ball of energy at {{Term|ALBW|Link}}, which he must reflect back at him in a game of {{Term|ALBW|Dead Man's Volley|link}}, in which the {{Term|ALBW|Bug Net|link}} can also be used as in earlier games. After several exchanges between them, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} will be struck by his own magic and get stunned, then {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|display=merge}} into the wall as a {{Term|ALBW|Painting}}. {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda}}, speaking from her {{Term|ALBW|Painting}} on the wall, will then give {{Term|ALBW|Link}} the {{Term|ALBW|Bow of Light|link}}, so he can attack {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} while {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|display=merged}} inside the wall. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|display=merge}} into the wall and shoot {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} directly with the {{Term|ALBW|Bow of Light}}. The evil beast will block the {{Term|ALBW|Arrow}} and try to stop it in its track, exposing his back. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must take this chance to {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|display=emerge}} out of the wall, get behind {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} and shoot him in the back, forcing him out of the wall and rendering him vulnerable to his {{Term|ALBW|Sword}} slashes. {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} will then warp out of the arena, and cause numerous fireballs to shoot out of the walls. These fireballs will turn into bats, which {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must avoid. This strategy must be repeated twice more during the fight, but with key differences: {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} will respond to the second {{Term|ALBW|Arrow}} fired at him by charging violently and rapidly at him. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must quickly {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|display=emerge}} out of the wall and back in to shoot him before he ends the charge. The third time around, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} will instantly block any {{Plural|ALBW|Arrow}} fired at him, and turns around too fast for {{Term|ALBW|Link}} to quickly {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|display=merge}} behind his back. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must then shoot away from him so the {{Term|ALBW|Arrow}} will fly all the way across the wall and hit {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}}. This will force him out of the wall one last time and render him vulnerable to the finishing move, which can be dealt with any weapon that inflicts damage.

==Trivia==
*The weapon wielded by {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} appears to be a combined version of {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}}'s Trident and {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}}'s staff-brush.
*The music which plays during the first phase of the {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} battle is a remixed version of the battle with {{Term|ALBW|Ganon}} from {{ALttP|-}}.
