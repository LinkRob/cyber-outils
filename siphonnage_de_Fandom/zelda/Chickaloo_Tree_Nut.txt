==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
[[Link]] can obtain Chickaloo Tree [[Nut|Nuts]] by killing any [[Sparrow]], searching [[Tree]]s and the floors of forests, or in the waterways of [[Zora's Domain]]. Chickaloo Tree Nuts are commonly used as [[Food]] and can be eaten raw to restore a quarter of a [[Heart]]. They can be roasted in [[Fire]] to create [[Roasted Tree Nut]]s, or they can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] as part of a [[Recipe]]. Chickaloo Tree Nuts are also used in the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] as an ingredient for [[Crimson Dye]].

When [[Cooking]], either Chickaloo Tree Nuts or [[Acorn]]s are needed to make the following [[Food]]s:
