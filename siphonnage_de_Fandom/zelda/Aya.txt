{{Term/Store|Aya||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aya|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Aya}} is a worker at the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}. She sits on a stool under the {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} next to a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|link}} at all hours of the day. When spoken to, she will seem depressed.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh...*|Aya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks her what is wrong, she quietly mutters about the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Is something wrong?] {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|The}} {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}}{{Color|BotW Gray|...}}}}|Aya|BotW}}</ref> Upon questioning, she will explain that her life has been dull since she got married, and she previously used to pass the time by staring at the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}.<ref name="Married"/> However, she reveals that the director of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}, has not lit the {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} in a while.<ref>{{Cite|But the {{Color|BotW Blue|director}} at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} hasn't lit the fire in so long.|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to relight the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, at which point {{Term|BotW|Aya}} asks if he can retrieve it in under three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll light it for you.] You? Hmm... That's one possibility, I guess. How fast can you do it? Do you think you can bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} to this {{Color|BotW Blue|lantern}} in under {{Color|BotW Red|three minutes}}?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the {{Term|BotW|Restoring the Blue Flame|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, in which {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will charge {{Term|BotW|Link}} 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}-handling fee.<ref>{{Cite|Great! But I'll be taking {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} as a horse-handling fee.|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She does this because she does not see how else he could complete the challenge within the time limit.<ref>{{Cite|Well, you're going to use a horse, right? I don't see any other way to do it that fast. So yeah, {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}.|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She also makes a joke before sending {{Term|BotW|Link}} out, pretending to count down and then telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} this is what she will say before he leaves.<ref>{{Cite|Alrighty... On your mark...get set... {{Big|Three... Two... One... Go!}} See, I'll say that, and then you'll start.|Aya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will be excited and give him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Unbelievable! It's back! The {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} is really back! I really ought to do something to thank you, huh?|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

The next time the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} goes out, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will ask Link to bring it back in one minute.<ref>{{Cite|How fast can you do it? Do you think you can bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} to this {{Color|BotW Blue|lantern}} in under {{Color|BotW Red|one minute}}?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> After this is done, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} goes out a third time, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to relight the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} in less than twenty seconds.<ref>{{Cite|How fast can you do it? Do you think you can bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} to this {{Color|BotW Blue|lantern}} in under {{Color|BotW Red|20 seconds}}?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> The reward for doing this is a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}. On subsequent visits, however, she will only be able to give {{Term|BotW|Link}} 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can try anyway, but the reward is still the same {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}.|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

If the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} is extinguished while it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will remark that {{Term|BotW|Link}} should not retrieve it due to the poor weather.<ref>{{Cite|The weather's pretty lousy, though, so maybe not.|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Aya}} is happier while the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} is lit. She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if there is anything as pretty as the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, but she does not think there is.<ref>{{Cite|Is there anything in all the world as pretty as the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}}? I'm thinking...no?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She also finds the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} to be soothing, and wants to know if {{Term|BotW|Link}} thinks the same.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh...* Good old {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}}. It's so soothing to stare at, don't you think?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> In addition, she will warn {{Term|BotW|Link}} against putting it out,<ref>{{Cite|Don't put it out!|Aya|BotW}}</ref> grumbling angrily if he does so.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|I told you not to put it out...}}}}|Aya|BotW}}</ref>
