'''''Zelda'' no Video''' is a 2003 Japanese documentary on the history of {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features==
The documentary begins with the presentation of {{TWW}}, the most recent game in the series during the year of its release. Afterwards, a retrospective of ''The Legend of Zelda'' series is presented, including all games released up to {{TWW|-}}, even including {{AST}}. Throughout the documentary, interview excerpts with different personalities from the video game industry and media are shown, telling their memories of ''The Legend of Zelda'' series throughout the years. These personalities include game analysts, representatives from {{wp|Famitsu}} and [[Capcom]], {{Wp|Scha Dara Parr}} MC Bose, and members of [[Nintendo]], including the series director [[Eiji Aonuma]] and the series creator [[Shigeru Miyamoto]].

During the epilogue, each personality describes their thoughts on the "''Zelda'' essence," concluding with a lengthy interview with Miyamoto. As a bonus after the credits, a "secret [[Triforce]] scene" is shown, featuring the Japanese ''Zelda'' rap commercial for {{ALttP|-}}.
