The {{Term/Store|Ruvo Korbah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ruvo Korbah Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength+}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength+''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah Shrine}} is located on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} south of the {{Term|BotW|Temple of Time|link}}. It is one of four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that appear on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}} as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must defeat the various {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} that appear at this location during the trial.<ref>{{Cite|The One-Hit Obliterator, as the name suggests, can defeat enemies in one-hit. However, the wielder will also be defeated in one hit. Defeat the monsters at the locations marked on the map within the Great Plateau.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> These include a {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} riding {{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}. After the last {{Term|BotW|Enemy}} is defeated, {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah Shrine}} will rise up from beneath the ground. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ruvo Korbah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Ruvo Korbah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah Shrine}} first appears to be like any other Major Test of Strength, with a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} appearing to fight {{Term|BotW|Link}} and a small room in the back unlocking upon its defeat. However, only a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a bundle of ten {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} are found in the room instead of the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} stands on the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}, the central platform on which the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} entered on will descend back down, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} entry to a lower section of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. Here {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter several {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}}, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, and another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Small Key}} opens a locked portcullis, beyond which lies the final room of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}. Here, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat four different {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}} as they shoot lasers at him from afar.

Once all of the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}} have been defeated, the portcullis in the back of the room opens, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah}} grants him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triump over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ruvo Korbah|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ruvo Korbah|BotW}}</ref>
