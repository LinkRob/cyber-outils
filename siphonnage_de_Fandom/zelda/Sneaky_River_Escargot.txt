==Location and Uses==
Sneaky River Escargot can either be made by roasting a [[Sneaky River Snail]] near {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or found as a drop after defeated a Blue {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}}. Their meat is supposed to be soft and chewy, and pulling it out of the shell whole provides one with a sense of accomplishment.<ref name="Inventory"/> Eating one restores one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.
