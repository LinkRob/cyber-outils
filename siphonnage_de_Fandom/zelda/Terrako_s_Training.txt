{{Term/Store|Terrako's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Terrako's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Terrako's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Core|link}}, one {{Term|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} spawn, and {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}}'s objective is to defeat 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. After reaching 370 kills and defeating both of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}}, a gate opens, and two more {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} spawn. Once the objective is reached, {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} spawns and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} is defeated.
