"{{Term/Store|The Armies of Ruin||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}}" is the first {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} of the {{Term|HWDE|Prologue|link}} arc of {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}} in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|The Armies of Ruin}}|Select Scenario|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin}}" is the first {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}} that is unlocked and is the start of the {{Term|HWDE|War Across the Ages|link}}. "{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin}}" takes place in {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Field (Stage)|link}}.

"{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin}}" begins with {{Term|HWDE|Zelda|link}} having a dream of playing the {{Term|HWDE|Goddess's Harp|link}} in a grassy field. Suddenly, the sky turns from light blue to dark purple and the ground begins to shake, followed by an unknown dark substance that starts chasing after {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}}. {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} drops her {{Term|HWDE|Goddess's Harp}} and runs, the {{Term|HWDE|Goddess's Harp}} sinking into the dark substance. The substance catches up with {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} and overwhelms her, then she wakes from her dream. {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} tells the alarmed {{Term|HWDE|Impa|link}} about the recurring nightmare,<ref>{{Cite|Impa...the same nightmare keeps haunting me...|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref> to which {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} states that it could be a dark omen for {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule|link}}<ref>{{Cite|An omen, perhaps, of dark times ahead for Hyrule...|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} states that they must find the reborn spirit of the hero, {{Term|HWDE|Link|link}}, to help protect {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|We must find the reborn spirit of the hero.|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> Heading outside, {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} and {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} look down at the {{Term|HWDE|Trainee|link|display=Soldiers in training}}, to which {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} states that they will find no hero there and should look elsewhere.<ref>{{Cite|We will find no fully realized heroes here... We'd be wiser looking elsewhere.|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} notices one {{Term|HWDE|Soldier}} in training with more skill than the rest, {{Term|HWDE|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 82}}</ref> Before she can think further, a {{Term|HWDE|Soldier}} runs up to {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} and reports that a horde of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}} is marching towards {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A horde of monsters is marching toward Hyrule Castle!|Soldier|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} orders {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} to prepare the armies for battle and leaves.<ref>{{Cite|Impa, prepare the troops for battle!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref>

When the {{Term|HWDE|Dark Forces|link}} begin their siege upon {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Hyrulean Forces|link}} intercept them in {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Field (Stage)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hordes of monsters began laying siege to the kingdom of Hyrule. Princess Zelda led a valiant defense against hopeless odds.|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> As a {{Term|HWDE|Trainee}}, {{Term|HWDE|Link|link}} defies his orders to stay out of the confrontation and runs into battle.<ref>{{Cite|News of the Hyrulean army's imminent defeat reacheed the cadets at the knights' training yard. As the elite troops fell in battle, Hyrule's trainees were left to wait helplessly inside the castle walls. Despite the odds, one young knight took up is simple training sword and chose to fight. This brave young man was named Link. Compelled to act, he rushed to the monster-ridden battlefield to assist Zelda and defend Hyrule.|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> Fighting breaks out throughout {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Field (Stage)}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Blue|Hyrulean Forces}} are fighting in multiple locations!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> with large amounts of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}} worrying {{Term|HWDE|Impa}}.<ref>{{Cite|Where did all these monsters come from?|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} searches for {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}}, but finds that she is separated by a wall of {{Plural|HWDE|Boulder|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We can't get to Princess Zelda with those boulders in the way!|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Link}} appears and quickly fells many of the lesser {{Term|HWDE|Dark Forces}} with ease, surprising {{Term|HWDE|Impa}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? A new recruit? Who is this soldier, anyway?|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|HWDE|Link}} encounters a {{Term|HWDE|Fairy|link}}, who hides within {{Term|HWDE|Link}}'s body and introduces herself as {{Term|HWDE|Proxi|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Someone! Help! I'm too little to fight monsters!|Proxi|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Proxi}} enthusiastically attempts to maintain a conversation with {{Term|HWDE|Link}}, only to conclude that though he speaks little.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! I'm Proxi! And who are you? Link, huh? Unusual name! What's it mean? Where are you from? Nice sword! Man of few words, huh? That's okay--I'll come along and do the talking!|Proxi|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Link}} and {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} search for a way to rid of the {{Plural|HWDE|Boulder}} and rescue {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}}. After defeating enough {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Abandoned Fort Gate|link}} opens revealing the {{Term|HWDE|Volga|link|display=Dragon Knight}}, {{Term|HWDE|Volga}}.

{{Term|HWDE|Link}} and {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} fight with {{Term|HWDE|Volga}} to keep him back from {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|Don't let the dragon knight through!|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Volga}} accepts their challenge with a claim that he will win.<ref>{{Cite|If you're ready to join you ancestors, then I accept your challenge!|Volga|HWDE}}</ref> As {{Term|HWDE|Volga}}'s health reaches the halfway point during the battle, he will stop the fight and attempt to destroy {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} and {{Term|HWDE|Link}} with a blast of fire.<ref>{{Cite|How noble. Enjoy your shared grave.|Volga|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|Volga}} becomes shocked after learning that the two were protected by {{Term|HWDE|Link}}'s {{Term|HWDE|Triforce|link}} and decides to flee from the fight.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Red|Volga}} has retreated!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You haven't beaten me. You've merely hidden behind your shields, human and otherwise.|Volga|HWDE}}</ref> Annoyed, the {{Term|HWDE|Wizzro|link|display=Dark Wizard}} opens the {{Term|HWDE|Abandoned Fort|link}} and sends out another wave of {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is Volga slacking off again? Fine! Send out the next unit!|Dark Wizard|HWDE}}</ref> A {{Term|HWDE|Goron Captain|link}} pleads for assistance as he is attacked by a {{Term|HWDE|Lizalfos|link}} and a legion of {{Plural|HWDE|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We can't hold them anymore! Somebody, help!|Goron Captain|HWDE}}</ref> Once {{Term|HWDE|Link}} rescues the {{Term|HWDE|Goron Captain}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance|link}} will open<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|HWDE Green|Mine Entrance}} is open!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> and the {{Term|HWDE|Goron Captain}} thanks {{Term|HWDE|Link}} and recommends that he takes the treasured {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb|link}} within it.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks a ton! Hey, you should take the treasure in that mine as a reward!|Goron Captain|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Head toward the interior of the {{Color|HWDE Green|mine}} and get the {{Color|HWDE Green|treasure}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> After the {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb}} are obtained, {{Plural|HWDE|Bokoblin}} will seal the {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance}} with {{Plural|HWDE|Boulder}}, which must be destroyed using the newly-obtained explosives.<ref>{{Cite|Destroy the boulders and rendezvous with {{Color|HWDE Blue|Zelda}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With those bombs, we should be able to break the boulders and proceed to Princess Zelda!|Impa|HW}}</ref> Just outside the {{Term|HWDE|Mine}}, a smaller {{Term|HWDE|Boulder}} can be destroyed with the {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb}} to reveal a {{Term|HWDE|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 375 {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 84}}</ref> The {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb}} are also necessary to destroy the {{Plural|HWDE|Boulder}} blocking the {{Term|HWDE|West Field Keep|link}}, which leads to the area that {{Term|HWDE|Princess Zelda|link}} is defending. After {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} captures the {{Term|HWDE|West Field Keep}}, the two areas will reconnect so {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} and {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} can reunite.<ref>{{Cite|Capture the West Field Keep! This will let us reconvene with out allies. Hurry!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Princess! Thank goodness! I fear I'd never have reached you if not for a brave trainee!|Impa|HW}}</ref> When {{Term|HWDE|Link}} joins with {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}}, she recognizes him as the promising trainee that she observed in {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle}} prior to the assault on {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|You! I saw you in the training yard!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref>

Suddenly, more {{Term|HWDE|Dark Forces|link}} appear and the {{Term|HWDE|Central Keep|link}} and the {{Term|HWDE|SW Bridge Gate|link}} open.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Red|Dark Forces}} have received reinforcements! The {{Color|HWDE Red|Central Keep}} and the {{Color|HWDE Green|SW Bridge gate}} are open!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> After reuniting, {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} commands her troops to capture the {{Term|HWDE|Central Keep|link|display=Central}} and {{Term|HWDE|East Field Keep|link|display=East Field Keeps}} and to close their respective gates. A {{Term|HWDE|Hylian Captain|link}} will declare that his forces can capture the {{Term|HWDE|East Field Keep}} on their own and requests that someone else captures the {{Term|HWDE|Central Keep}} in his place.<ref>{{Cite|Leave the East Keep to us!<br/>Someone take the central one!|Hylian Captain|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Wizzro||display=Dark Wizard}} orders his troops to kill {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} and the {{Term|HWDE|Hyrulean Forces}} and advance towards the {{Term|HWDE|Castle Keep|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Attack! Destroy! Massacre! Kill the royal whelp before I grow impatient!|Dark Wizard|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Red|Dark Forces}} are advancing toward the {{Color|HWDE Blue|Castle Keep}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> Once the two {{Plural|HWDE|Keep}} are captured, their gates to the north will become sealed off preventing travel in both directions. Before they are closed, the {{Term|HWDE|Hyrulean Forces}} may make their way northward and capture the {{Plural|HWDE|Keep}} to the north, including the {{Term|HWDE|Enemy Base}}, where the {{Term|HWDE|Wizzro||display=Dark Wizard}} is situated. If he is attacked, he will retreat, saying that he has other matters to take care of.<ref>{{Cite|I have no time for these vermin! There are preparations that need my attention!|Dark Wizard|HWDE}}</ref> A side mission will also start when a {{Term|HWDE|Guard Troop|link}} appears and moves to the {{Term|HWDE|W. Rockface Keep|link}}, to which {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} orders to defeat the {{Term|HWDE|Guard Troop}} and take the {{Term|HWDE|Keep}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Red|Guard Troop}} appeared and is advancing towards the {{Color|HWDE Red|W. Rockface Keep}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We must not let the enemy fortify their hold on that keep. Hurry! Take it at once!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref>

After the {{Term|HWDE|Central Keep}} and {{Term|HWDE|East Field Keep}} have been captured, {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} will travel to the {{Term|HWDE|Fairy Fountain|link}} to call upon the {{Term|HWDE|Great Fairy|link}} for help.<ref>{{Cite|We have forced the invaders into the north! Now we must call upon the Great Fairy! I must put our plan in motion. Soldiers in training! Follow me to the Fairy Fountain!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref> However, the entrance to the {{Term|HWDE|Fairy Fountain}} is blocked by a cracked wall, which must also be destroyed with the {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb}}.<ref>{{Cite|If only we had a bomb... We'd have that wall down in a moment...|Zelda|HW}}</ref> During this time, the {{Term|HWDE|Dark Forces}} may break through the barriers from the northwest and east, requiring the Hyrulean Forces to divert their attention to stop them.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|HWDE Green|Rockface Checkpoint}} is open! {{Color|HWDE Red|Dark Forces}} are advancing!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We must seal the northern gate to stop the dark forces from getting to the castle!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref> Once the wall is destroyed, {{Term|HWDE|Link}} and {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} may pray to the {{Term|HWDE|Great Fairy}} together by using the newly-charged {{Term|HWDE|Magic Circle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Blue|Zelda}} has charged the {{Color|HWDE Green|Master Circle}}! It's ready to use!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Great Fairy}} enhances the {{Term|HWDE|Hyrulean Forces}}' {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb}} and detonates them across the battlefield to greatly weaken the {{Term|HWDE|Dark Forces}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|HWDE Green|Magic Circle}} dealt a devastating blow to the enemy forces!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|HWDE|Hyrulean Forces}} receive a {{Term|HWDE|Morale|link}} boost<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Blue|Hyrulean Forces}} gained morale after their attack on {{Color|HWDE Red|Dark Forces}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> and {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} leaves the battlefield.<ref>{{Cite|Please, Princess Zelda, you must pull back. We can manage the battle from here.|Impa|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I understand. May the goddesses protect you!|Zelda|HWDE}}</ref>

In response to this, the {{Term|HWDE|Wizzro||display=Dark Wizard}} will summon {{Term|HWDE|King Dodongo|link}} in the {{Term|HWDE|North Square|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hiya ha ha! This is where it gets interesting! Unleash King Dodongo!|Dark Wizard|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How did the wizard transport a creature of that size to the battlefield so quickly?!|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> {{Term|HWDE|King Dodongo}} will begin his rampage south towards {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle}} and so must be defeated quickly. After {{Term|HWDE|King Dodongo}} is defeated {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} compliments the army.<ref>{{Cite|Well fought, troops. This is why we never give up.|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> A {{Term|HWDE|Soldier}} informs {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} that the {{Term|HWDE|Wizzro||display=Dark Wizard}} has taken {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle}} and merely used {{Term|HWDE|King Dodongo}} as a distraction.<ref>{{Cite|General Impa, terrible news! The enemy wizard has seized the castle!|Hylian Soldier|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So, the beast was just a distraction... Where is Princess Zelda? Is she safe?|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> With {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Castle}} under the control of the {{Term|HWDE|Wizzro||display=Dark Wizard}}, {{Term|HWDE|Zelda}} disappears, prompting {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} to employ {{Term|HWDE|Link}}'s help in locating her,<ref>{{Cite|We can't locate Princess Zelda. I pray she's safe... I must ask for your help in finding her. I trust you are willing? Link, be a hero worthy of wearing this tunic.|Impa|HWDE}}</ref> granting him the {{Term|HWDE|Hero's Clothes|link}} as a symbol of his worthiness.

Upon completing "{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin}}" for the first time, {{Term|HWDE|Impa}} becomes a playable {{Term|HWDE|Warrior|link}} and her {{Term|HWDE|Weapon|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Giant's Knife|link}}, becomes available for use. Along with {{Term|HWDE|Impa}}, {{Term|HWDE|Free Mode|link}}, {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, and the {{Term|HWDE|Gallery|link}} unlock.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 86}}</ref>
