{{Term/Store|Caladbolg||Series, CoH}} is an item in {{CoH}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|CoH|Caladbolg}} is a legendary {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}. It is locked inside a blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} located on the roof of {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}}, which can be accessed after stepping on the switch in the courtyard in order to open a door to a room full of {{Plural|CoH|Wizzrobe|link}} and a {{Term|CoH|Ball 'N Chain Guard|link}}, then going up the stairs inside. Like other {{Plural|CoH|Long Sword|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Caladbolg}} has a hit range of two spaces at the cost of the use of {{Term|CoH|Link}}'s {{Term|CoH|Shield}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got {{Color|CoH Yellow|Caladbolg}}! ==Trivia==
*{{Term|CoH|Caladbolg}} is the sword of Fergus mac Róich from the Ulster Cycle of Irish mythology. Spelled Caladcholg, it is also associated with the more obscure Ulster hero Fergus mac Léti, suggesting a conflation of two legends. It was said to be a two-handed sword that made a circle like an arc of rainbow when swung, and to have the power to slice the tops off hills and strike down an entire army. {{Term|CoH|Caladbolg}} in {{CoH|-}} is shown to have a gradient of colors from the base to the tip referencing its rainbow swing in the mythology.
