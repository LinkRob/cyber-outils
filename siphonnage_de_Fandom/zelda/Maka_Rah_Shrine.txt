The {{Term/Store|Maka Rah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Maka Rah Shrine}}<br/>Steady Thy Heart}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Steady Thy Heart''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Maka Rah Shrine}} is in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, in a cave on the western edge of {{Term|BotW|Lake Kilsie|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} can be seen through a crack in the wall near the {{Term|BotW|Lake Kilsie|display=Lake's}} western dock. To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must find another entrance to the cave in the cliffside further northeast. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters, he is greeted by the its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Maka Rah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Maka Rah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Maka Rah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
In the first room of {{Term|BotW|Maka Rah Shrine}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight|link}}, one lit and one unlit are on either side of a portcullis. When both {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight}} are lit, the portcullis will rise, leading to room with a rotating hexagonal column partially covered in spikes above a pool of lava.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} carefully crosses the column, he can press a {{Term|BotW|Switch|link}} at the back of the room to make the room's back wall rise, revealing a room with four {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} in a shallow pool of water. A {{Term|BotW|Switch}} on a ledge at the back left corner of the room will lift a portion of that room's back wall revealing another {{Term|BotW|Switch}} and a portcullis.

Activating the {{Term|BotW|Switch}} will cause the portcullis to rise and unleash a large {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boulder|link}}, sending it rolling through a wall of wooden boxes and towards {{Term|BotW|Link}}. The corridor behind the wall of boxes will pass another pool of lava and lead to yet another {{Term|BotW|Switch}} and portcullis. Again, the {{Term|BotW|Switch}} will make the portcullis rise and send a large {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boulder}} hurtling down a ramp towards {{Term|BotW|Link}}. A new {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boulder}} will drop if the previous one lands in the pool of lava behind the {{Term|BotW|Switch}}.

In order to complete the trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must find a way past the large {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boulder}} to reach the top of the ramp, where {{Term|BotW|Maka Rah}} awaits. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Maka Rah}}, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Maka Rah|BotW}}</ref> They wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Maka Rah|BotW}}</ref>

On a ledge at the back right of the room with the pool of water is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing ten {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}} can be found on a ledge above the wall of wooden boxes behind a row of cracked blocks. Another metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Core|link}} can be found in a small nook just before the final {{Term|BotW|Switch}} above the second pool of lava.
