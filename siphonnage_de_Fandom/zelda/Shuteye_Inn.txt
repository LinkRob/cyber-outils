The {{Term/Store|Shuteye Inn||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shuteye Inn|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Shuteye Inn is an [[Inn]] in [[Kakariko Village]]. It is located across from Impa's house, adjacent to the pond that surrounds a [[Goddess Statue]]. [[Ollie]] is the skittish [[Sheikah]] proprietor who runs the front desk.<ref>{{Cite|Ack! Are you...a g-guest? I suppose so... {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|You s-scared me half to death...}}}}|Ollie|BotW}}</ref> The Inn offers the usual regular bed for 20 [[Rupees]] that will restore all of Link's [[Hearts]], as well as a [[Cucco]] feather-stuffed bed for 40 Rupees that will also grant an {{Term|BotW|Extra Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well... The regular bed is {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. And...th-the soft bed is {{Color|BotW Red|40 rupees}}, I guess.|Ollie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[The difference?] The soft bed is stuffed with premium Cucco feathers...or something. I think. Sleep on that, and... Um... Ya know... You'll be more energized than usual.|Ollie|BotW}}</ref>

At night, [[Pikango]] the traveling artist will retreat to the corner of the lobby to sleep, mumbling about his "journey on the horizon."<ref>{{Cite|My journey...is on the horizon...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref> On some nights or during inclement weather, {{Term|BotW|Brokka|link}}, a traveler, and {{Term|BotW|Bugut|link}}, a traveling merchant, sit at the Inn's table if they happen to be passing through the Village.
