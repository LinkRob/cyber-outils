The {{Term/Store|Blue Mail||Series, ALttP, LANS, ALBW}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW, LANS}}<ref name="E 117"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|LANS Blue|Sturdy blue mail}}|N/A|LANS}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Blue Clothes||Series, LADX}},{{Exp Game|LADX}}<ref name="E 117"/> are a recurring type of [[Defense]] [[Tunic|Clothes]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:BlueMail Sprite.png|left|40px]]
The Blue Mail is the second Mail [[Link]] can wear in [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|{{aLttP|-}}]], and is the first defense upgrade. It is hidden in a [[Big Chest]] in the [[Ice Palace]] of the [[Dark World]]. When Link wears it, the damage taken from enemies is reduced by half of what he normally takes.{{Fact}} Additionally, it turns Link's cap yellow. It is removed once Link obtains the [[Red Mail]] in [[Ganon's Tower (A Link to the Past)|Ganon's Tower]], and if it is skipped, then the young hero will not wear it if he returns to find it. The Red Mail itself further reduces damage to a quarter of what Link usually takes.

==={{LA|DX|-}}===
[[File:LADX Blue Clothes Sprite.png|left|frameless]]
In the ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|DX]]'' version of {{LA}}, the Blue Clothes halves the damage Link takes, similar to the effect of a [[Guardian Acorn]], except the effect is permanent as long as the tunic is worn, much like the Blue Mail from {{aLttP|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|You've got the Blue Clothes! Your damage will be reduced by half!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> They are accessible only after completing the special [[Color Dungeon]] hidden in the [[Graveyard]],<ref name="E 117" /> where the [[Fairy Queen]] will allow Link to choose between the Blue or [[Red Clothes]]. Once he selects one of the special tunics, he can switch to the other by speaking to the Fairy Queen again. However, he can never regain the original [[Green Clothes]]. Grabbing a Guardian Acorn with the Blue Clothes equipped quadruples Link's defense, making him rather durable. The Red Clothes, on the other hand, doubles Link's attack power, making it the permanent version of the [[Piece of Power]].

==={{ALBW|-}}===
[[File:ALBW Blue Mail Icon.png|left|frameless]]
The Blue Mail in {{ALBW}} is typically found in a Big Chest hidden in the [[Swamp Palace (A Link Between Worlds)|Swamp Palace]] in [[Lorule]], but it can also be obtained later inside [[Lorule Castle]]. Whichever chest is not opened first will then contain the [[Red Mail]]. As in {{aLttP|-}}, wearing the Blue Mail will reduce the damage Link takes by half<ref>{{Cite|You got the blue mail! This mail cuts damage by half! Sturdy mail that provides great defense.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>, while the Red Mail further reduces down to a quarter.
