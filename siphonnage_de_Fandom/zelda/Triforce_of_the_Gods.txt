'''''Triforce of the Gods''''' may refer to any of the following:

*'''''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past#Japanese Version|The Legend of Zelda: Triforce of the Gods]]''''', the Japanese name for {{ALttP}}.
*'''''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds#Japanese Version|The Legend of Zelda: Triforce of the Gods 2]]''''', the Japanese name for {{ALBW}}.
*'''''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Triforce of the Gods]]''''', a common title for the [[BS-X Broadcasting System]] port of {{ALttP|-}}.
*'''''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (Himekawa)]]''''', the Japanese name for the {{ALttP|-}} manga by [[Akira Himekawa]].
*'''''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (Cagiva)]]''''', the Japanese name for the {{ALttP|-}} manga by [[Ataru Cagiva]].
*'''''[[The Legend of Zelda: Triforce of the Gods (Board Game)]]''''', a board game based on {{ALttP|-}}.
