{{Term/Store|Monster Horn|Monster Horns|Series, SS, SSHD, ALBW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132 {{Exp Game|SS, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
Monster Horns are instruments of animal bone carried by [[Bokoblin]]s.<ref>{{Cite|This well-crafted horn is made of sturdy animal bone. You might find a use for this as a material.|Collection|SS}}</ref> They can be stolen from Bokoblins by using the [[Whip]]. Link can also win them by making between 20 to 27 cuts in the [[Clean Cut]] game at [[Bamboo Island]]. The [[Moonlight Merchant]] will occasionally sell Monster Horns for 100 [[Rupee]]s. In his home at night, [[Rupin]] will occasionally buy Monster Horns for 30 Rupees.

Monster Horns are necessary for [[Upgrading]] the [[Divine Shield]] into the [[Goddess Shield]], the [[Small Quiver]] into the [[Medium Quiver]], and the Medium Quiver into the [[Large Quiver]] at the [[Scrap Shop]].

==={{ALBW|-}}===
<!-- You got a monster horn! Ouch. Watch that you don't hurt yourself on that thing!-->
Monster Horns can be found by opening [[Treasure Chest]]s or by defeating enemies in {{ALBW}}. In exchange for 10 Monster Horns and 100 [[Rupee]]s, the [[Witch]] will brew Link a [[Yellow Potion]]. Alternatively, Link can sell Monster Horns to the Witch for five Rupees apiece.
