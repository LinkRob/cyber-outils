==Features and Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, the House of Books is located south of [[Kakariko Village]]. The [[Book of Mudora]] can be found on the top of a bookshelf, and can be knocked down by [[Dash Attack|dashing]] into it with the [[Pegasus Boots]].

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Atlas Of Koholint Island, Auto Map and Memo Guide Book, Dark Secrets And Mysteries Of Koholint, Fun With Bombs, How to Handle Your Shield Like a Pro!, Secrets Of The Whirling Blade, Selecting The Item That's Right For You, The Properties of Warp Points}}
In {{LA}}, the Village Library contains several books that explain basic controls, the [[Atlas of Koholint Island]] that lets [[Link]] see the full map of [[Koholint Island]], and [[Dark Secrets And Mysteries Of Koholint]], which gives directions to navigate inside the [[Wind Fish's Egg]].

In {{LA|DX}}, an additional book can be found atop of a bookshelf. In order to access it, Link must knock it down by dashing into the bookshelf using the Pegasus Boots. Link can then read it to learn the instructions needed to access the [[Color Dungeon]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, the {{Term|OoS|Eyeglass Island Library}} is mentioned by a {{Term|OoS|Ghini}} as part of the {{Term|OoS|Library Secret|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Main|Eyeglass Island Library}}
In {{OoA}}, the Eyeglass Island Library is located in the [[Zora Seas]]. It can only be opened by using the [[Library Key]]. Inside, Link can find the [[Book of Seals]] in the present and [[Fairy Powder]] in the past. The [[Mirror Shield]] can also be obtained from here through a [[Linked Game]].

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Royal Hyrule Library}}
In {{TMC}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}} is home to the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} elder {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}}. In order to reach it, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must find the three missing {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}. When found, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} will store the books back on their bookshelves so {{Term|TMC|Link}} can climb them and reach {{Term|TMC|Librari}}, who will lead him to the location of the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}. When {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} takes over {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Vassal|link}} blocks the entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Library}} is a two-story room found on the eastern side of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. Both floors are used for {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} storage, while two subfloors are connected to stairways and are used to access different parts of the {{Term|BotW|Library}} and the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Corridors|link}}. On the first floor, the western exit is blocked by {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}, and it can only be accessed by defeating the {{Term|BotW|Glowing Eyeball|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|East Passage|link}}.

Due to {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}'s influence, the {{Term|BotW|Library}} has fallen into disrepair and much of the architecture of the {{Term|BotW|Library}} has fallen apart.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref> Portions of the {{Term|BotW|Library}} are covered in {{Term|BotW|Malice}}, further inhibiting travel throughout the room. Segments of the {{Term|BotW|Library}}'s roof have given way, allowing travel between its interior and the exterior of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}.

The {{Term|BotW| Library}} is the resting place of the {{Plural|BotW|Castle Library Book|link}}. The first issue can be found resting on a table in the center of the {{Term|BotW|Library}}'s ground floor. The second is located on a podium on the western side of the second floor. These {{Plural|BotW|Book}} detail recipes favored by the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Royal Family's Secret Recipe #1<br/>The Princess's Favorite: {{Color|BotW Red|Fruitcake}}.|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Royal Family's Secret Recipe #2<br/>The Chancellor's Favorite: {{Color|BotW Red|Monster Cake}}.|Castle Library Book|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Library}} also contains several hidden rooms and passages behind metallic {{Plural|BotW|Bookshelf|link}}, which can be moved using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. One of these {{Plural|BotW|Bookshelf}} can be found on the eastern side of the {{Term|BotW|Library}}, and it reveals a secret passageway leading to the {{Term|BotW|Docks|link}}. Behind a {{Term|BotW|Bookshelf}} on the opposite wall, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find an abandoned excavation site, which reveals a portion of the {{Term|BotW|Astral Observatory|link}}'s walls. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Stone Smasher|link}}, an {{Term|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Rare Ore Deposit|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}. The northern wall of the {{Term|BotW|Library}} holds a {{Term|BotW|Bookshelf}} that hides the {{Term|BotW|King's Study|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} may read {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam's Journal|link}}.

==Other Appearances==

When Link returns to Kakariko to find clues about the remaining two [[Pendants of Virtue]],<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Leaving Sahasrahla, Link returns to Kakariko Village to look for clues to the location of the remaining two pendants...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> he finds the library has been set on fire by [[Agahnim|Agahnim's]] soldiers.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The House of Books is ablaze! The soldiers must have laid torch to it!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> Link learns that the [[Librarian]], who he must speak to according to Sahasrahla,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He's inside that inferno?!
