==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}} are found within the {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm|link}} of each respective region. The {{Term|SSHD|Tears of Farore|link}} are found in {{Term|SSHD|Faron Woods|link}}, the {{Term|SSHD|Tears of Din|link}} in {{Term|SSHD|Eldin Volcano|link}}, the {{Term|SSHD|Tears of Nayru|link}} in {{Term|SSHD|Lanayru Desert|link}}, and the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}} in {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link}}. To leave a {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm}} and obtain a new {{Term|SSHD|Item}} required to locate the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Flame|link}} and later the {{Term|SSHD|Triforce|link}}, {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} must collect 15 {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}} to be stored in the {{Term|SSHD|Spirit Vessel|link}}. However, {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian|link|display=Earth}} and {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Guardian|link}} patrol the area and chase after {{Term|SSHD|Link}}. As such, he must use the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}} to stun the creatures for 90 seconds while he continues to look for the rest of the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|SS Gold|Sacred Tear}}! This object's power halts the movements of the Guardians for just {{Color|SS Red|90 seconds}}. Collect all 15 to pass the trial!|N/A|SS}}</ref> If {{Term|SSHD|Link}} is spotted by one of the guardians, the remaining tears will shoot a light into the sky to reveal their locations until one is collected. This effect can be duplicated by collecting a {{Term|SSHD|Light Fruit|link}}, which lasts for 30 seconds.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|SS Gold|Light Fruit}}! This mysterious fruit enables you to see beacons over the positions of Sacred Tears for {{Color|SS Red|30 seconds}}!|N/A|SS}}</ref>
