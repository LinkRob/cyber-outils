'''Zelda's Bow'''<ref name=HH>{{Cite Book|quote= Zelda's Bow / The weapon used by Zelda during the final battle with Ganondorf. Her arrows were created from the transformed Light Spirits.|book=HH|page= 196}}</ref> is a recurring weapon in {{TLoZ|Series}}. In games such as {{TWW}}, {{Term|Series|Princess Zelda|link}} mainly uses a {{Term|Series|Bow}} with the {{Plural|Series|Light Arrow|link}} to help {{Term|Series|Link|link}} fight {{Term|Series|Ganon|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:Princess Zelda with bow.png|left|80px]]
{{Term|TWW|Princess Zelda}} from {{TWW}} is the first Zelda in the series who fights along {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s side in his battle against Ganondorf. She does not have her own Bow, but uses {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s {{Term|TWW|Bow}} and {{Plural|TWW|Light Arrow}} to cover {{Term|TWW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll use this bow to cover you! Attack him as best you can!|Princess Zelda|TWW}}</ref> During the battle, however, Ganondorf hovers over to Zelda and knocks her unconscious, but after a while, she wakes up and aids {{Term|TWW|Link}} once again by aiming a {{Term|TWW|Light Arrow}} at {{Term|TWW|Link}}'s {{Term|TWW|Mirror Shield|link}}, which bounces off to Ganondorf and allows {{Term|TWW|Link}} to deal the final blow.

==={{TP|-}}===
[[File:TP Link and Zelda.jpg|300px|thumb|right|Zelda with her Bow in {{TP|-}}]]
In {{TP}}, after Zelda calls upon the {{Plural|TP|Spirit of Light|link}} to aid her in defeating Ganondorf, she obtains the {{Plural|TP|Light Arrow}} and the Bow of Light with which to wield them.<ref name=HH/> Zelda uses the Bow of Light to aid {{Term|TP|Link}} in the horseback part of their battle against Ganondorf. By shooting the {{Plural|TP|Light Arrow}} from her Bow of Light, Zelda can stun Ganondorf, giving {{Term|TP|Link}} a chance to strike.

Since the princess is not seen with anything to carry the Arrows in, it is assumed that the special Bow of Light actually produces the {{Plural|TP|Light Arrow}} instead of it requiring ammunition.

==={{ST|-}}===
Like in {{TWW|-}}, Princess Zelda in {{ST}} does not have her own Bow, but she uses {{Term|ST|Link}}'s {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}} during his battle against {{Term|ST|Malladus|link}}. By tapping the {{Term|ST|Bow}} button in the touch screen, {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} can be controlled in order to shoot a {{Term|ST|Light Arrow}} at {{Term|ST|Malladus}}'s back.

==={{BotW|-}}===
[[File:BotW Twilight Bow Icon.png|right]]
{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s bow from {{TP}} may be obtained in this game, by scanning {{Term|Series|Princess Zelda}}'s {{SSB|-}} series amiibo. It has a low chance of spawning and can only spawn after having completed one Divine Beast. The {{Term|BotW|Twilight Bow|link}} fires {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow}}, which are unlimited in supply until the bow breaks. {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow}} have the same power as a normal arrow. {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} fired from the bow shoot entirely straight with virtually unlimited effective range. The bow has 30 power and 100 durability.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
===''The Legend of Zelda'' TV Series===
[[File:Bow (Cold Spells).png|Zelda using a Bow in the animated series|thumb|right]]
In {{TLoZ|TV Series}} loosely based on the first ''Zelda'' game, the princess often accompanies Link on his adventures and is seen being quite skilled in the use of a Bow, which seems to be her weapon of choice since she uses it in most of her battles. The Bows used by Zelda fires beam-like arrows instead of the usual arrows. Interestingly, Zelda is capable of making the Bow magically appear in her hands instead of having to take it out from her pocket.

Zelda is first seen skillfully wielding a Bow in "[[The Ringer]]", where Link gives her a Bow dropped by a [[Stalfos]] so that she may help him fight off a horde of Stalfos that have surrounded them.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=A measly three Stalfos? Hah! Take this, princess.|name=Link|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=The Ringer}}</ref> In "[[Cold Spells]]", the young hero once again gives the princess a Bow, which she uses to defeat various magically-possessed buckets and blankets. Zelda uses the Bow later on in the episode to shoot an arrow at Ganon, seemingly weakening him and allowing Link to deliver the final blow. Zelda is last seen wielding a Bow in "[[Sing for the Unicorn]]" that she uses to defeat an [[Armos]] that had grabbed Link. 

Other than the Bow, Zelda also seems to be fond of using the [[Crossbow]] and is seen using it twice in the animated series.

===''Captain N: The Game Master''===
Zelda is once again seen skillfully wielding the Bow in {{CN}}. The princess carries this Bow wherever she goes, as she can make this Bow magically appear in her hands, much like the ''Zelda'' animated series. Most of the enemies that Zelda encounters in the show can easily be defeated with just one arrow from her Bow. In "[[Quest for the Potion of Power]]" and "[[Once Upon a Time Machine]]", Zelda uses a golden Bow capable of shooting beam-like arrows; however, in "The Trojan Dragon", she wields a blue Bow that shoots regular arrows instead.

===''The Legend of Zelda'' (Valiant Comics)===
[[File:Zelda's Bow (VC).png|thumb|right|Zelda using her Bow in the comic]]
In the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}, Zelda is portrayed as a proficient archer much like her animated series counterpart.<ref name=bow>{{Cite Book|quote=Yow! She meant it! But...Zelda '''never''' misses...|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [Queen of Hearts]}}</ref> Although the princess at one point fights using a sword and shield, she confesses that she prefers the Bow since she is unable to fire a sword beam correctly.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It would sure help if I had  a  Bow! I can't fire a sword blast worth a darn!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=8 [To the First Power]}}</ref> When wielding a Bow, Zelda never misses her target and is capable of easily defeating enemies with it,<ref name=bow/> as seen in ''[[To the First Power]]'' where she defeats a [[Gohma]] and later a [[Wizzrobe]] with a single shot. 

Zelda is next seen using her Bow in ''[[The Legend of Zelda (Valiant Comics)/Issue 4#Queen of Hearts|Queen of Hearts]]'', when she goes to help Link and Queen Seline fight off a horde of enemies. She appears and shoots an arrow at a magic container being guarded by a Wizzrobe,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Get away from my friends, you creep!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=9}}</ref> breaking it and restoring Seline's powers. In ''[[Day of the Triforce]]'', Zelda wields her trusty weapon to defeat Ganon's minions that have attacked the [[Water Town of Saria]]. In this story, Zelda is shown to be capable of making her Bow magically appear in her hands, similar to the animated series.

==={{TCT|-}}===
Being [[Princess Zelda]]'s main weapon of choice, she uses the Bow to defeat any enemies she encounters during her adventures and prefers using it over [[Zelda's Dagger|her dagger]].

Throughout her adventure in {{TCT}}, Zelda often uses the Bow to defeat a variety of enemies, from [[Tektite]]s to [[Gibdo]]s and even [[Octorok]]s.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=In a split second, she notches one of the strange arrows into her bowstring and takes aim on the nearest gibdo. It's close enough for her to smell its rotting cloth bandages.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=18}}</ref> In the final battle against [[Ganon]], Zelda fires an [[arrow]] at the evil wizard to pin his robe to a trunk, allowing Link to hurl the [[Spear]] at his hand and eventually defeat him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=As Ganon holds up yet another huge ball of fire, Zelda slips an arrow from her quiver and lets it fly. Ganon is so busy trying to stop Link that he doesn't see the arrow until it's too late. It catches his robe and pins it to the tree trunk.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=118}}</ref>

The only times Zelda uses her dagger instead of the Bow is when she has run out of arrows or she's fighting an enemy that is immune to arrows, such as the [[Boomerang Demon]]s.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Drat!' she yells. 'Arrows are no good against these monsters.' She draws her dagger to fend off the weird flying goblins.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=65}}</ref> Other than that, the Bow proves to be an invaluable weapon in the course of her journey, often times saving her from various dangerous situations, such as when she defeated [[Madame Grusha|Madame Grusha's]] army of [[Moblin]]s.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=In a flash, Zelda lets fly two arrows, piercing two two closest moblin. The others pour into the small room over the bodies of their fallen comrades, slobbering and jabbering in their awful tongue.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=27}}</ref>
