The {{Term/Store|Wind Fish's Egg||Series, LA, LADX}} is an important landmark and the final [[Dungeon]] in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 148}}</ref>{{Note|This Dungeon is also referred to as the '''Egg of the Wind Fish''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 18}}</ref> However, as {{E}} supersedes the guide, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Wind Fish's Egg}} was referred to as the '''Wind Fish Egg''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Wind Fish Egg}}<br/>Found high on Koholint Island's Mount Tamaranch, the Wind Fish Egg is a mysterious structure constructed by the Wind Fish itself to serve as a secure hibernation chamber. During Link's quest to wake the Wind Fish, he bravely entered the Wind Fish Egg and fought an intense series of battles against dark and shadowy enemies.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160308171202/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/w.jsp#WindFishEgg|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the names given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} It is the gigantic [[Egg]] that sits on top of [[Mount Tamaranch]],<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Hello, this is Ulrira speaking! Link, it's time for you to face the Egg on Mt. Tamaranch! Be careful, Link ! Bye! CLICK!'|Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> and is said to be where the [[Wind Fish]] sleeps.<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Hello, this is Ulrira speaking! Link, it's time for you to face the Egg on Mt. Tamaranch! Be careful, Link ! Bye! CLICK!'|Quadruplet|LADX}}</ref> To get to the egg, [[Link]] must climb up a flight of stairs near [[Goponga Swamp]], but he won't be able to open the way inside until he has collected all eight of the [[Instrument of the Sirens|Siren Instruments]]. The egg itself is white with several pink spots and can be seen from many parts on the island. The Wind Fish's Egg is commonly seen in a beam of light surrounded by a cloudy ring, which is prominent in the title screen of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|Link's Awakening DX]]'' and the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Switch remake]].

==Overview==
After completing [[Eagle Tower]], [[Marin]] can be found on a broken bridge on [[Tal Tal Heights]] as a result of a kidnapping.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Link ! Some monsters put me up here! What should I do?! I'm afraid of heights!!|Marin|LADX}}</ref> After rescuing her, the {{Term|LANS|Owl|link}} reveals that she attempted to awake the Wind Fish by singing the [[Ballad of the Wind Fish]] in front of the egg.<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! That girl sang her song in front of the Egg! Her 'Ballad of the Wind Fish' is a song of awakening! Did she actually intend to wake the Wind Fish?! The next Sirens' Instrument is in the west. Play your melodies so the unliving stones might hear! Show your courage! The Wind Fish waits for you! Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref>

The Wind Fish's Egg is the ninth and final dungeon in the game. Shortly after obtaining the final instrument, Link will be redirected to the egg.<ref>{{Cite|...EGG.... The Egg on the mountain calls!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> The young hero then plays the Ballad of the Wind Fish by using his [[ocarina]] along with the other instruments. When the song ends, part of the egg's shell suddenly cracks open and reveals an entrance, allowing Link to go inside.<ref>{{Cite|Hoooot! The Wind Fish sleeps long and dreamily in the Egg above... When you play the eight Siren Instruments in front of the Egg he will awaken. This, my friend, is the only way for you to leave the island! Hoo!|Owl|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The time has come... The Wind Fish awaits... Enter the Egg... Hoot! Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref> Unlike most other dungeons in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], the design of the inside of the egg is very simple, lacking any decoration, making it rather mysterious.

After falling through a pit at the entrance (which is very dark), the egg contains an endless maze similar to the recurring [[Lost Woods]]. To correctly navigate it, Link must follow a specific sequence of directions. However, this sequence is randomly set whenever a new game file is made. To determine the correct sequence, the Hero of Legend must read a book titled "Dark Secrets And Mysteries Of Koholint" inside the library in [[Mabe Village]].<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! This is Ulrira! You're lost in the Egg? Hmmmm. No sir, I can't help you on that one. How about the library? And hey, don't stop calling me because I didn't know one little answer! CLICK!'|Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> The book has very fine print that can only be read with the aid of a [[Magnifying Lens]],<ref>{{Cite|Gasp! Wha-What's this! ... ... You can't read the tiny print without the aid of a magnifying glass...|N/A|LADX}}</ref> so it is necessary to complete the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}. However, it is still possible to successfully navigate the maze without reading the book or having the Magnifying Glass in Link's possession (though this is extremely difficult to do without a guide).

After successfully clearing the maze, Link will come upon a second pit. By dropping down, he will encounter the [[Final Boss]] and the last of the [[Shadow Nightmare|Nightmares]]. Once they have been ultimately defeated, a stairway will appear and will lead Link to The Wind Fish's room, where the Owl and the Wind Fish itself await him and the game ends.

==Trivia==
*The concept for the world ending when a large egg breaks on top of a mountain, was an idea originally planned for [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|''A Link to the Past'']].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=To tell you one of my personal experiences, I worked on the overall concept, storyline and structure of The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past and there were some ideas that didn't make it. Out of these ideas, there was one I really wanted to see in a game and we finally decided to use it as one of the basic concepts for the setting of The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening a few years later (this was, in fact, that the world ends when a massive egg breaks on top of a mountain).|url=http://www.officialnintendomagazine.co.uk/57137/features/monkey-men-talking-to-michael-kelbaugh-and-kensuke-tanabe/|title=Wii U Feature: Monkey Men: talking to Michael Kelbaugh and Kensuke Tanabe|site=Official Nintendo Magazine}}</ref>
*Although the official artwork and title screen of ''Link's Awakening'' suggests the Wind Fish's Egg to be at the center of [[Koholint Island]], the game's map shows it located at the top area.[[File:Pink Yoshi egg.webp|thumb|212x212px|A pink Yoshi Egg or Birdo Egg from the ''Mario'' series, which the Wind Fish's Egg is based on]]
*The Wind Fish's Egg closely resembles the [[mariowiki:Yoshi_Egg|Yoshi Eggs]] seen in the ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Super Mario]]'' and ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Yoshi_(franchise) Yoshi]'' franchises. Ironically, such eggs of the [[Yoshi|character]]'s [[mariowiki:Yoshi_(species)|species]] with pink dots exist, though this is more commonly associated with the [[mariowiki:Birdo_Egg|Birdo Egg]]. In comparison, the massive egg in the ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|Zelda]]'' title has more spots around it.
**On a similar note, a [[Yoshi Doll]] can be obtained in a shop for the [[Trading Quest]].
