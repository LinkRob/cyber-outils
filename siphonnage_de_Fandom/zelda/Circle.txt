{{Term/Store|Circle|Circles|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}} used to reveal {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 330">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 330 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Circle}} can be found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} in two different varieties, a circle of {{Plural|BotW|Rock|link}} jutting out of a body of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} or a circle of {{Plural|BotW|Lily Pad|link}} floating on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}. Nearby a {{Term|BotW|Circle}} made of {{Plural|BotW|Rock}}, normal {{Plural|BotW|Rock}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is able to carry will always be found. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} throws one of these {{Plural|BotW|Rock}} so that it lands in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Circle}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear to give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Piggyback 330"/> For {{Plural|BotW|Circle}} of {{Plural|BotW|Lily Pad}}, they will always appear underneath a ledge overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can jump off of. If he dives into the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Circle}}, this will also cause a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} to appear.<ref name="Piggyback 330"/>
