{{Term/Store|Squiddy|Squiddies|Series, PH, ST, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name= "Encyclopedia"/>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Squiddy}} were referred to as '''Jellyriches''' in an official [[Miiverse]] post for {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= One day a member of the level production team created the Jellyrich. |name= Mikiharu Oiwa |url= https://web.archive.org/web/20171104195205/https://miiverse.nintendo.net/replies/AYMHAAACAAADVHkosazxNA |title= Tri Force Heroes Miiting part 2}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Plural|PH|Squiddy}} occasionally pop out of the sea while {{Term|PH|Link|link}} is sailing aboard the ''{{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}}''. If they are hit with the {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}}, they will shoot up into the air and dispense a single {{Term|PH|Rupee|link}}. If left alone, they will slowly fall back into the sea. If hit enough, they will eventually split into two. Up to three {{Plural|PH|Squiddy}} can appear at the same time; once they are allowed to disperse and one drops into the sea, one of the remaining {{Plural|PH|Squiddy}} will be able to split again.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, {{Plural|ST|Squiddy}} are only found in the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}}, where they behave identically to the ones in {{PH|-}}. {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can use the {{Term|ST|Cannon|link}} of the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} to strike them and collect {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Squiddy}} appear on or close to dry land, hovering close to the ground. Physically, they resemble a blue jellyfish adorned with {{Term|TFH|Rupee|link}}-like patterns. One {{Term|TFH|Squiddy}} can be found in every {{Term|TFH|Level|link}}. They can be attacked with the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}, causing them to bounce upward and dispense Green {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}}. After being juggled several times, they will start to drop Blue {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} and eventually Red {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}}. At this point, striking the {{Term|TFH|Squiddy}} once more will cause it burst into a shower of {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}}. {{Plural|TFH|Squiddy}} will bounce more quickly the more they are hit, and if allowed to touch the ground without being struck quickly enough, they will burst without dispensing any {{Plural|TFH|Item}}. If the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} are low on health, {{Plural|TFH|Squiddy}} will drop up to four {{Plural|TFH|Heart}} before dropping {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} as normal.

{{Plural|TFH|Squiddy}} can be safely struck with the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}, and explosions from {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}}. All other {{Plural|TFH|Item}}, including {{Plural|TFH|Sword Beam|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}'s pillars, will cause a {{Term|TFH|Squiddy}} to burst instantly.
