{{Term/Store|Lizalfos Chieftain|Lizalfos Chieftain|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|}} is a character in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Red|Lizalfos Chieftain}}|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> He was first introduced in the Wii U version's ''Master Quest Pack'' DLC as part of ''Cia's Tale'' in Legend Mode, and was later added to parts of the main story in ''Hyrule Warriors Legends'' and ''Hyrule Warriors Definitive Edtion''.

==Characteristics==
The Lizalfos Chieftain is the leader of the Lizalfos tribe dwelling in the Eldin Caves. He is much larger and better-equipped than the standard Lizalfos, but otherwise fights the same. He is loyal to Volga and reveres him like a deity. Whenever Volga appears, the Lizalfos and Dinolfos Chieftains are often there with him. When Cia appears in the Eldin Caves attempting to recruit Volga to her army, this angers the Lizalfos Chieftain and he leads his tribe, assisted by the Dinolfos Chieftain's tribe, to attack Cia. However, Cia defeats them and forcefully recruits them to her army. Following this, the Lizalfos Chieftain battles the Hyrulean Army many times, but is always defeated by Link or another warrior. During Cia's last stand against the Hyrulean Army, the Lizalfos Chieftain flees the battlefield in terror in the face of the overwhelming odds. His fate afterwards is unknown.
