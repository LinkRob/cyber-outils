The {{Term/Store|Nice Fire Rod||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Once Link acquires the three [[Pendants of Virtue]], he can buy the [[Fire Rod]] from Ravio for 1200 Rupees. Once he owns the rod, Link can then find and bring 10 [[Lost Maiamai|Lost Maiamais]] to [[Mother Maiamai]] to get her to upgrade the [[Fire Rod]] into the '''Nice Fire Rod'''.

The '''Nice Fire Rod''' is much the same as the  [[Fire Rod]], except that it is enhanced to produce a much taller and wider pillar of flames which travels a further distance and deals more damage.
