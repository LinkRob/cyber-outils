The {{Term/Store|Full Moon Cello||Series, LA, LADX, LANS, HWL, HWDE}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 124"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Full Moon Cello}}: Use this on the search screen to fill the Special Attack gauge.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The Full Moon Cello is one of the eight [[Instruments of the Sirens]] needed to awaken the [[Wind Fish]].<ref name="E 124"/> It can be found at the end of [[Tail Cave]] after defeating its boss, [[Moldorm]].<ref name="E 124"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, after defeating {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Moldorm|link}} inside {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Tail Cave|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} finds the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Full Moon Cello}} inside the next chamber. After gathering all eight {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Instrument of the Sirens|link}}, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Full Moon Cello}} plays the "{{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}" alongside the other instruments while {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} plays his {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ocarina|link}} in oder to open the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish's Egg|link}}.

==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWL Full Moon Cello Sprite.png|left|40px]]
In {{HW|L}}, the Full Moon Cello appears as an [[Item Card]] in the [[Koholint Island Map]] of [[Adventure Mode]], which is included in the {{LA|-}} DLC Pack. It gives the Warrior a maxed out [[Special Attack]] Gauge during the battle as a Bonus Rule.
{{Noncanon|End}}
