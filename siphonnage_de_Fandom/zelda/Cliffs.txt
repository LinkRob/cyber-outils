{{Term/Store|Cliffs||Series, CoH}} is a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cliffs|Map|CoH}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Cliffs|link}} make up a single region that consists of a mountainous biome full of hills and stalagmites. Though their placement in the world is random, the {{Term|CoH|Cliffs}} always appear near {{Term|CoH|Death Mountain|link}} along the northern side of {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}.

{{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss}} such as {{Plural|CoH|Hinox|link}} and {{Plural|CoH|Black Hinox|link}} can be found here.
