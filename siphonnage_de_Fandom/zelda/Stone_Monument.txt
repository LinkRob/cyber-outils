{{Term/Store|Stone Monument|Stone Monuments|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Most likely, the {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} outside of the domain are in the same awful condition.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} were commissioned by {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} as an attempt to preserve {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} history.<ref>{{Cite|What an honor it was to receive a personal request from King Dorephan to craft a historical stone monument!|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> The reason this was done in stone and not paper was because paper could be damaged by {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 274}}</ref> At first, the commission was meant to be a single monument, but due to the sheer amount of writing {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} wanted preserved, his original text was split among seven {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}}.<ref>{{Cite|I did not realize how much content he'd give me, though... It certainly exceeded the line limits of a single monument. I suppose I could have just shortened the text, but it felt wrong to tamper with our great king's words. Thankfully, I was able to split it all between seven monuments to ensure that every word was preserved.|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> Two additional monument were created to recount stories of {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} and {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link|display=Prince Sidon}}.<ref>{{Cite|While I was at it, I thought...why not add two of my own? And so I created one for King Dorephan and one for Prince Sidon.|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref> The unnamed stonemason who made the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} also made one dedicated to themselves, though they forgot to sign it.<ref>{{Cite|Why, indeed! My achievement surely deserves remembrance too. That is how a commission of one became 10. Of course, having increased the number of monuments, I had to find places for them all... That proved difficult. Still, it is worth it! So long as I remember to sign these monuments, my name will be remembered forever. As it should be.|Memoir of a Gifted Stonemason|BotW}}</ref>

After calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can speak to {{Term|BotW|Jiahto|link}} to find out that he wants to compile the contents of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} into a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking about collecting the contents of all the stone monuments and compiling them into a book.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> However, due to his age, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}} himself and to relay what they say.<ref>{{Cite|But with these old legs and fins, traveling to all {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} will take a great deal of time. That is why... Link... I'd like to ask a favor of you. I'd like you to find all {{Color|BotW Red|10 stone monuments}} around {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}} and tell me what they say.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts the task, {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}} will be relieved that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find the {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Thank you! You truly are a lifesaver! How reassuring that the Champion will search for those monuments for me!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> He also reveals that one of the monuments has the location for a piece of treasure written on it, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep said treasure should he find it.<ref>{{Cite|I have it on good authority that among the stone monuments, one has the location of some {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}} written on it. If you happen to find said {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}}, feel free to keep it. It will most likely be something that a Zora wouldn't need.|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> This activates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}".

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} reads all of the {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Jiahto}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link... It would seem you have checked all of the stone monuments for me!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he'll give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link, thank you for taking on such an arduous task! Please, allow me to reward you!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref>
