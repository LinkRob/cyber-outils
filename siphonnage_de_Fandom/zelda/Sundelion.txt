A Sundelion is "a wild plant that grows in the sun at high attitudes. Soaked in sunlight, its petals can restore health depleted by gloom when used in cooking" from [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom]].

Sundelions are primarily found in [[Sky Islands|Sky islands]], but also can be found in [[Eldin Canyon]] and [[Hyrule Field]].

When [[cooking]] with Sundelions, each Sundelion used in a dish restores 3 [[hearts]] lost to [[gloom]].

Each Sundelion sells for 8 [[Rupee|Rupees]] at any merchant.
