"{{Term/Store|21||Series, TMC}}" is a {{Term|Series|Song|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Track: 21|N/A|TMC}}</ref> This song is a remix of the battle theme from the Japanese version of ''Zelda II: The Adventure of Link''.

==Overview==
"{{Term|TMC|21}}" plays in several battles, most of them {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss|link}}:
* The {{Term|TMC|Madderpillar|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}
* The two {{Plural|TMC|Spear Moblin|link}} summoned by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}}
* The {{Plural|TMC|Spiny Chuchu|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}}
* The {{Term|TMC|Darknut|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}
* The {{Term|TMC|Red Dark Nut|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}
* The {{Term|TMC|Red Dark Nut}} in the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}
* The {{Plural|TMC|Darknut}} and {{Plural|TMC|Red Dark Nut}} in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}
It appears as the twenty-first track on the {{Term|TMC|Phonograph|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}}.
