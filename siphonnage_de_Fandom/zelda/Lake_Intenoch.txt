{{Term/Store|Lake Intenoch||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lake Intenoch|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Lake Intenoch is a large [[Hot Spring]] lake in southeastern [[Eldin Canyon]]. The Lake is part of a river that flows toward the northeast of a deep gorge that surrounds the Lake. It is partially an extension of [[Lake Ferona]] to the west, another Hot Spring lake. The two Lakes are split by the road that paves through [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]]. Unlike Lake Ferona, whose water levels varies between shallow and deep, Lake Intenoch is mostly deep water. As a Hot Spring, the water is warm and will gradually recover Link's health as he stands or swims in it.

[[Broca Island]] is the Lake's sole landmark, which lies at the far northeastern end of the Lake.
