{{Term/Store|Crazy Tracy's Health Spa||Series, LA, LADX}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 234 {{Exp Game|LA, LADX}}}}<br/>{{Cite|Crazy Tracy's Health Spa|System|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Crazy Tracy's Health Spa}} was referred to as '''Crazy Tracy's House''' in a quiz on the [[Play Nintendo]] website.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= May 17, 2020|url= https://play.nintendo.com/activities/skill-quizzes/zelda-links-awakening-wind-fish-riddle-quiz/|title= Zelda: Link’s Awakening Wind Fish Riddle Quiz - Play Nintendo|site= Play Nintendo|type= }}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LANS|Crazy Tracy's Health Spa}} is located near the {{Term|LANS|Goponga Swamp|link}}. It can be reached from anywhere in the overworld map by playing "{{Term|LANS|Manbo's Mambo|link}}", as the {{Term|LANS|Song|link}} {{Term|LANS|Warping|link|display=Warps}} {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} to {{Term|LANS|Manbo's Pond|link}} in her front yard.

===Wares and Pricing===
{{Term|LANS|Crazy Tracy|link}}'s prices are determined by the amount of enemies defeated prior to entering her shop.
