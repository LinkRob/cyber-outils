The {{Term/Store|Lizalfos Gear|Lizalfos Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear}} is a collection of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}}. It is found in three tiers of increasing quality and durability.

Basic {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear}} is most commonly found in the possession of regular {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}. It is the product of low-quality metalwork, which makes {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} of this tier relatively fragile and weak.<ref name="Lizal Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Lizal Spear}}<br/>The brutal, ripping edge of this weapon’s spearhead is a distinctly Lizalfos design. Its low durability doesn’t lend itself to extended use.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Lizal Shield}}<br/>A common shield found among the Lizalfos. It's made of metal, but its sloppy craftsmanship offers poor durability.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The second tier of gear is typically wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}}. It includes additional metal that gives it greater durability, and also possess greater attacking power.<ref name="Forked Boomerang">{{Cite|{{Big|Lizal Forked Boomerang}}<br/>Blue Lizalfos in particular like this weapon. It has one more blade than the Lizal boomerang to give it additional cutting power, and it still returns when thrown.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Reinforced Lizal Shield}}<br/>This Lizal shield has been strengthened by adding a different type of metal to the mix. The edge is lined with spikes, so handle with care.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The final tier of weaponry is used by wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Black Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lizalfos|link}}, and is further strengthened and reinforced by metal enhancements.<ref name="Tri-Boomerang">{{Cite|{{Big|Lizal Tri-Boomerang}}<br/>More blades means more attack power! It can be used as a boomerang, but all those blades makes that a bit more dangerous. Carried by Black Lizalfos seasoned in battle.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Forked Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Forked Lizal Spear}}<br/>Skilled Lizalfos warriors tend to favor this spear. What it lacks in piercing power, it makes up for with the brutal wounds its dual ripping blades will inflict.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Steel Lizal Shield}}<br/>This Lizal shield is adorned with several metal shells as a means of reinforcement. Its defensive capabilities are high, but its weight requires a skilled soldier to bear.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

One-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} within this archetype are notable for their curved blades, which give them a {{Term|BotW|Boomerang|link}}-like shape that allows them to return their owner when thrown.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Lizal Boomerang}}<br/>A curved sword favored by the Lizalfos. It can be used to attack directly or can reportedly be thrown like a boomerang.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Higher-tier variants of these weapons posses more forked blades, but still return when thrown.<ref name="Forked Boomerang"/><ref name="Tri-Boomerang"/> {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos}} spears are instead distinguished by blades lined with hooks and barbed edges, designed to tear and rip at foes.<ref name="Lizal Spear"/><ref name="Forked Spear"/><ref name="Enhanced Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Enhanced Lizal Spear}}<br/>Judging by the harpoon-like spearhead of this Lizalfos-made spear, the Lizalfos use it for fishing as well as combat. Try not to get caught on the wrong end of its barbs.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They are sometimes used for fishing in addition to combat.<ref name="Enhanced Spear"/> {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos}} {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} are fashioned from the flexible branches of a type of waterside {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}}, increasing the spring of the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} and thus the strength with which it projects {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}.<ref name="Strengthened Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Strengthened Lizal Bow}}<br/>A Lizal bow with a grip reinforced by metal. The body is made from the branches of a flexible tree that grows near water, which offers some serious destructive power.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The grip of basic {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} is reinforced with the bones of large {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}}; higher tiers are instead metal, which covers most of the highest-quality {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} used by skilled {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos}} marksmen.<ref name="Strengthened Bow"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Lizal Bow}}<br/>A wooden bow created by Lizalfos. It's reinforced by the bones of a large fish – a marked improvement over any standard wooden bow.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Steel Lizal Bow}}<br/>This bow is wielded by lizalfos who are expert marksmen. The metal that reinforces much of the weapon adds some additional weight but offers heightened durability.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear}} is formed from metal, all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} within this archetype sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and can be manipulated through {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. The exception to this are the {{Term|BotW|Lizal Bow|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Strengthened Lizal Bow|link}}, which are constructed primarily of wood. As a result of this, they will float in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}, do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}, cannot conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity}}, are capable of burning, and are not magnetic.
