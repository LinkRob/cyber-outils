'''Giant Squids''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Giant Squids are large, purple cephalopods. They are very powerful, and can inflict two and a half [[Heart]]s of damage on [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. If Zelda has two or less [[Celestial Sign]]s, Giant Squids are invincible.

The first Giant Squid is encountered in [[North Seacoast Plain]], and two more are encountered in the [[Shrine of Water]].
