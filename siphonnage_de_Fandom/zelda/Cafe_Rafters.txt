The {{Term/Store|Cafe Rafters||Series, TMC}} are a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Cafe Rafters}} are located above {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. They are only accessible when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, requiring the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}} to be used on a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} inside the {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|display=Cafe}}. They lead towards the {{Term|TMC|Shop Rafters|link}} via a wooden plank connecting {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe}} to {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}}.

Several {{Term|TMC|Minish}} reside in the {{Term|TMC|Cafe Rafters}}, both {{Term|TMC|Town Minish|link}} and visiting {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}};<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Green|Forest Minish}} have come to visit.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> some may {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link}}.
