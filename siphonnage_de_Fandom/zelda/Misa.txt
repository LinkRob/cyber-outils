{{Term/Store|Misa||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Misa|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Misa}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} woman who owns {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. She is assisted by her son {{Term|BotW|Fyson|link}} before he leaves for {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}},<ref name="Mom"/> and she wants him to take over the {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} one day.<ref>{{Cite|My mom said she wants me to take over the family store someday...<br/>Can you believe that?!|Fyson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Misa}} can always be found at the back of {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon}}, and she welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} when he walks inside.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome!|Misa|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, she will repeat her greetings to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Slippery Falcon}}, the legendary Rito general store! You look beat! Working hard, eh?|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hello...and welcome to the Rito general store, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Slippery Falcon}}.|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey! Welcome to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Slippery Falcon}}!|Misa|BotW}}</ref> She then asks what he needs from her.<ref>{{Cite|Was there something you needed?|Misa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon}}'s stock, {{Term|BotW|Misa}} explains that they provide everything from self-defense {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} ingredients.<ref>{{Cite|[What do you sell?] We carry just about everything—from items for self-defense to everyday groceries.|Misa|BotW}}</ref> She encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to buy her ingredients, and she allows him to read her {{Term|BotW|Misa's Recipe Book|link|display=''Recipe Book''}} to learn how to use them.<ref>{{Cite|By all means, please peruse the {{Color|BotW Blue|book of recipes}} there, and buy some of our fine ingredients!|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can find recipes that use our featured ingredients in the {{Color|BotW Blue|book of recipes}} over there.|Misa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes a purchase at {{Term|BotW|The Slippery Falcon}}, {{Term|BotW|Misa}} will thank him.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, sir. Thank you!|Misa|BotW}}</ref> If he buys the entire stock of one product, {{Term|BotW|Misa}} promises to replace it soon.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! We'll make sure to stock up on that.|Misa|BotW}}</ref> Like other vendors, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also sell his own {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Misa}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Here to sell.] Sure thing. Show me what you're selling.|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anything else you want to sell?|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Any other items you'd like me to take off your hands?|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Choose the items you want to sell!|Misa|BotW}}</ref> When she buys one of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Item}}, she gladly gives him his {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and thanks him.<ref>{{Cite|OK, here you go!|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK!|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you very much!|Misa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks so much!|Misa|BotW}}</ref> At the end of their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Misa}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return.<ref>{{Cite|Come back soon!|Misa|BotW}}</ref>
