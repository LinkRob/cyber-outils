"{{Term/Store|Get in the Groove||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Get in the Groove}}<br/>05/04/2021 |author= Nintendo |published= May 4, 2021 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/9e3701bf/index.html |title= Event: Get in the Groove |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Get in the Groove}}" is an event wherein a greater number of either singing or dancing {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits with slick moves and impressive pipes are coming to the Spirit Board in this event!|author= Nintendo |published= May 4, 2021 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/9e3701bf/index.html |title= Event: Get in the Groove |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
