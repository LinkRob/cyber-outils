{{Term/Store|Miss Vai Battle Pageant||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 65 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Miss Vai Battle Pageant|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Miss Vai Battle Pageant}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given ten minutes to defeat five {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. The first is {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}}, and the second {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}}, who appears after her defeat. {{Term|HWAoC|Riju|link}} does the same, and {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} appears when {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} loses a little bit of her health. The fifth {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}}, appears after either {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} or {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} is defeated. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches the objective, {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} appears wearing the {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Outfit|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when he is defeated, and can be cleared using {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Hestu}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Great Fairy}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Monk Maz Koshia}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}}, or {{Term|HWAoC|Battle-Tested Guardian}}. If one of the five default {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} above is also the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} completing the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, then they are replaced by a {{Term|HWAoC|Great Fairy}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Veil|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Top|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Sirwal|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
