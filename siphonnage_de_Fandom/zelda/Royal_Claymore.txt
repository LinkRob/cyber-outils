{{Plural|BotW|Royal Claymore}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear|link}} series. They are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} intended to be wielded by the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Being struck by one of these {{Plural|BotW|Royal Claymore|display=Claymores}} is said to destroy one's body and their resolve.<ref name="Inventory"/> They have a base attack damage of 52.

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Claymore}} are scattered throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. They can be found on the necklaces of {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Stalnox|Link}} can also drop {{Plural|BotW|Royal Claymore}} when they are defeated, particularly the one within {{Term|BotW|Lockup|link}}. Other high-tier enemies, such as {{Plural|BotW|Silver Moblin|link}}, make use of them.

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Claymore}} can also be found in the following locations:
* {{Term|BotW|Woodland Tower|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Nero Hill|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}
* Various {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Coliseum Ruins}} (If only three {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} have been completed)

When the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, they have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Claymore}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

If a {{Term|BotW|Royal Claymore}} is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, it will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} due to being made of metal. Similarly, they are capable of conducting {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, and they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They are incapable of burning, which means they can be exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} without issue.

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Claymore}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear|link}} equipment archetype, an intricate set of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} designed for the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>
