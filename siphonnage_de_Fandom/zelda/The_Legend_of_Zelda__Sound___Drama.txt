'''{{TLoZ|Colon}} ''Sound and Drama''''' is the official soundtrack of {{ALttP}}. The first CD features eight synthesized arrangements of songs from the game and a 17-minute "Sound Drama" performed in Japanese, and the second CD contains 40 original MIDI tunes from both {{TLoZ}} and {{ALttP|-}}. It was released on June 22, 1994 and published by Sony Records.
