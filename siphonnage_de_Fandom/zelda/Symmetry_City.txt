{{Term/Store|Symmetry City||Series, OoA}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 111}}</ref> previously the {{Term|Series|Symmetry City Ruins|link}}, is a location in {{OoA}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|OoA|Symmetry City}} is situated in the northeastern mountains of [[Labrynna]]. The most prominent feature of the city is its symmetric design, creating a perfect mirror image over the y-axis. Each inhabitant of the city has their own twin. In the city lies the entrance to [[Skull Dungeon]], home to the fourth {{Term|OoA|Essence of Time|link}}. In the past, it featured a [[Middle House]], which served as the town hall, where the [[Tuni Nut]], {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|link}}'s sacred symbol that held the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|display=Village}} in balance, was located.<ref>{{Cite|The Tuni Nut is the city's symbol.|N/A|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Tuni Nut: The nut that balances all.|Inventory|OoA}}</ref> Nowadays, in the place where the Middle House once stood, grows a {{Term|OoA|Gale Tree|link}}, where Link can gather his first [[Gale Seed]]s. The tree also provides easy [[warp]] access to the city, which can otherwise only be reached by a bridge spanning the [[Nuun Highlands]]. 

In search of the fourth [[Essences of Time|Essence of Time]], Link travels to the Nuun Highlands where a bridge crosses over to the [[Talus Peaks]] where the city is found. However, the bridge will not be built upon Link's arrival. At the request of [[Mutoh]], the head [[Carpenters|carpenter]], Link gathers up all the workers on break,<ref>{{Cite|Those lazy, worthless fools! Just as we're buildin' this bridge to the {{Color|OoA Blue|Symmetry City Ruins}}, they go off on break! I wait and I wait, but they don't come back!|Mutoh|OoA}}</ref> with the help of his animal companion (either [[Ricky]], [[Moosh]], or [[Dimitri]], depending on certain choices Link has made on his adventures). The workers will then build the bridge, allowing Link to enter the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry City Ruins}}.

When Link arrives in Symmetry City for the first time, he finds the city in ruins, infested with [[Podoboo Tower]]s. Link then travels to the past to {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village}} to find the cause of the problem, at which point he notices the volcano inside the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|display=Village}} erupting, wreaking havoc upon it. He then learns that the [[Tuni Nut]], the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|display=Village}}'s sacred symbol, has broken. This causes instability in the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|display=Village}}, thus erupting the volcano.<ref>{{Cite|When the Tuni Nut broke, the city fell out of balance, and the volcano started erupting.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> To restore balance to the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|display=Village}}, [[Link]] must bring the Tuni Nut to [[Patch]] atop [[Restoration Wall]] to have it fixed.<ref>{{Cite|A guru named Patch lives at the top of Restoration Wall. I think he can fix the broken Tuni Nut, but I could never climb that wall.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> Once the nut is fixed and placed on its pedestal in the [[Middle House]], peace will return to the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|display=Village}}. The volcano becomes inactive, and the present-day Symmetry City will no longer be in ruins. In the present, the raging volcano is replaced by a waterfall, parted in the middle by the entrance to [[Skull Dungeon]].<ref>{{Cite|Huh? The cave entrance? It was covered by lava from the volcano long ago! Maybe if the volcano calms down, you may be able to get in.|N/A|OoA}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Inhabitants of Symmetry Village claim to hear music flowing down from the mountains surrounding the {{Term|OoA|Symmetry Village|display=Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sometimes I hear music flowing down from the mountaintop, but I wonder why.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> In reality, the music comes from [[Tokkey]], who is researching a tune for the [[Harp of Ages]] inside a cave on [[Talus Peaks]].
*Also, there are two people who live on opposite sides of the city in the present who are not identical twins. One claims he is too small, the other, too big.<ref>{{Cite|What a mess...
Do I have to be bigger?|N/A|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What a mess... Do I have to be smaller?|N/A|OoA}}</ref>
*There are two BGMs for Symmetry City depending on its current state. Its BGM prior to the Tuni Nut being fixed is the music used shortly after Nayru/Din are abducted by the respective main antagonist of the ''Oracle'' games, and its BGM after the Tuni Nut is restored is a calm remix of the Symmetry Village theme.
