"{{Term/Store|The Apple of My Eye||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Apple of My Eye}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Rito Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arrives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, he can meet {{Term|BotW|Juney|link}}. She is visiting {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} on her honeymoon, though she finds herself disappointed by the town.<ref>{{Cite|I'm here on my honeymoon. But this town is seriously a hole.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Juney}} thinks she would have more fun in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, but {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}} makes her want to return home.<ref>{{Cite|In Gerudo Town, they've got jewelry to buy. Zora's Domain is always a hoot with all of the weird, spiritual fish people... But here...I just want to go home already.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> She is outraged that this is the place her husband, {{Term|BotW|Jogo|link}}, chose to visit,<ref>{{Cite|Give me a break already! I can't believe my husband brought me here for our honeymoon...|Juney|BotW}}</ref> and she threatened him with a divorce unless he brought her a {{Term|BotW|Baked Apple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|He already apologized, but if he doesn't want a divorce, I told him he'd better bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}}.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} hears this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

In order to help ease {{Term|BotW|Juney}}'s bad mood, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must bring her a {{Term|BotW|Baked Apple}}.<ref>{{Cite|Distracting her from her troubles with a {{Color|BotW Red|baked apple}}, her favorite snack, is one way to lighten the mood.<br/><br/>Bring Juney a {{Color|BotW Red|baked apple}} and save her marriage!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> They can be made by dropping an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} next to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has one, {{Term|BotW|Juney}} will be able to smell it on him.<ref>{{Cite|*sniff sniff*<br/>I know that smell! You've got a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}}!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> She asks for it, promising a present in return.<ref>{{Cite|Can I have {{Color|BotW Blue|one}}? I'll give you a present!|Juney|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives it to {{Term|BotW|Juney}}, she will gift him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure thing.] You serious? Cheers! Just hold out your hand.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> Though she wishes her husband would be the one to bring her {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple}},<ref>{{Cite|Now if I could only get my husband to cook me a {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apple}} every once in a while.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring her more in the future.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a second stomach and a hollow leg for {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}}. Bring me as many as you possibly can.|Juney|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
