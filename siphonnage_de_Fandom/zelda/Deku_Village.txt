{{Term/Store|Deku Village||Series, CoH}} is a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Deku Village}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref> It is the starting area for {{SotM}} and is accessible in other {{Plural|CoH|Mode|link}} through the dirt behind {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} in the {{Term|CoH|Lost Woods|link}} cave.

==Features and Overview==
The village is ruled by the {{Term|CoH|Deku King|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Deku Village, home of the great and powerful Deku King|Sign|CoH}}</ref> The southeast house contains a chest with either the {{Term|CoH|Lute|link}} or three Diamonds, depending on whether it was Skull Kid or another character that began inside. The large central building houses the {{Term|CoH|Deku King}}, and the southwestern building contains the Deku Shooting Gallery. In [[Symphony of the Mask]], there is a wall of trees to the south preventing exit until obtaining {{Term|CoH|Mask Magic|link}} and hitting the switch in the southwest.<ref>{{Cite|You're not thinking of going outside the village, are you, friend? If you want to leave, you'll need to find a way past my safety gate first!|Deku Scrub|CoH}}</ref> 

==Trivia==
*When starting {{SotM}}, it will be raining and continue to rain until after obtaining the {{Term|CoH|Skull Mask|link}} and speaking to the {{Term|CoH|The Great Deku Tree|link}}. If {{Term|CoH|Deku Village}} is first encountered in a different {{Term|CoH|Mode}}, however, it will already be sunny.
**It is possible to obtain {{Term|CoH|Mask Magic}} and keep the {{Term|CoH|Deku Village}} in a rain state. By avoiding speaking with the {{Term|CoH|Great Deku Tree}} after obtaining the {{Term|CoH|Skull Mask}}, the {{Term|CoH|Petal Trimmer|link}} can still be found and given to the {{Term|CoH|Deku King}} in exchange for Mask Magic.
