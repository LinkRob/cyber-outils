The {{Term/Store|Master Craftsman's Son||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239{{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Master Craftsman's Son appears as a depressed deadbeat.<ref name="Wander"/> While [[Link]] is a child, the Master Craftsman's Son can be found underneath the tree near the entrance to [[Kakariko Village]] at night. He is the [[Master Craftsman]]'s son and the [[Cucco Lady]]'s brother.<ref name="Wander"/><ref name="Cojiro">{{Cite|Its name is Cojiro, and it used to be my brother's Cucco. Its blue body is quite charming. It's so cute! Since my brother has gone, it's strange, but Cojiro has stopped crowing.|Cucco Lady|OoT}}</ref> He also appears to be the [[Granny]]'s grandson, but it is also possible that she is his mother.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT|publisher= Nintendo|page= 114}}</ref> When Link speaks to the Master Craftsman's Son, he will comment on how he finds everyone, including his own mother and father, to be disgusting.<ref>{{Cite|People are disgusting. My own father and mother are disgusting. You must be disgusting, too!|Master Craftsman's Son|OoT}}</ref>

The Master Craftsman's Son is part of the {{Term|OoT3D|Trading Quest|link}}. When Link is an adult, he can talk to the Cucco Lady after waking [[Talon]] with the [[Pocket Cucco]], resulting in her giving Link a blue [[Cucco]] named [[Cojiro]]. She mentions that the Cucco used to belong to her brother, and ever since he disappeared, Cojiro has stopped crowing.<ref name="Cojiro"/> Link can then find the Master Craftsman's Son in the [[Lost Woods]] sleeping beside a tree stump where a [[Skull Kid]] would play his flute. When Link shows him Cojiro, he will wake up and be surprised that someone else has been able to tame the Cucco. Taking this as a sign that Link is a "nice guy," he asks the hero to deliver an [[Odd Mushroom]] to the old lady in the [[Granny's Potion Shop|Potion Shop]].<ref>{{Cite|Cojiro? Why? Normally only a nice guy like me can tame you... Which means... You... You must be a nice guy! Must be! You must be!! Please Mr. Nice Guy! Please! Deliver this stuff to the old hag in the potion shop in Kakariko Village!|Master Craftsman's Son|OoT}}</ref>

Upon showing the Odd Mushroom to the Potion Shop Lady, she calls the Master Craftman's Son a "bum" for going into the Lost Woods, and asks Link that if he sees "that fool", to give him the [[Odd Potion]].<ref>{{Cite|That bum! He had to go into the forest... [...] If you see that fool, give this to him. It is the strongest medicine I have ever produced. However, this potion will not work on a monster... They say that there is no medicine that can cure a fool... I guess that's true...|Granny|OoT}}</ref> However, when Link returns to the forest to give the Master Craftman's Son the medicine, [[Fado (Ocarina of Time)|Fado]] is there instead. She explains that due to the fabled curse of the woods, he is gone and has become a [[Stalfos]].<ref>{{Cite|That guy isn't here anymore. Anybody who comes into the forest will be lost. Everybody will become a Stalfos. Everybody, Stalfos.|Fado (Ocarina of Time)|OoT}}</ref>
