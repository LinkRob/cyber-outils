{{Term/Store|Stal Skull|Stal Skulls|Series, TFH|plural}},{{Exp Game|TFH}}<ref name="E 139"/> also known as {{Term/Store|Stalfos Skull|Stalfos Skulls|ST|plural}},{{Exp Game|ST}}<ref name="E 139"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Stalfos Skull}} are a common item of {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}. They can be found inside {{Plural|ST|Jar|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest|link}}, or under piles of leaves. They are also occasionally dropped by {{Term|ST|Stalfos|link}} and {{Plural|ST|Stalfos Warrior|link}} upon defeat. Like most {{Plural|ST|Treasure}}, {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can also buy them from certain {{Plural|ST|Shop|link}}, or win them with a {{Term|ST|Prize Postcard|link}}.

{{Term|ST|Link}} can sell {{Plural|ST|Stalfos Skull}} for 50 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} each, or trade them with {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} in exchange for {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}}. {{Plural|ST|Stalfos Skull}} are included in the cost of the {{Term|ST|Wooden Cannon|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Skull Engine|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Skull Cannon|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Skull Passenger Car|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Skull Freight Car|link}}, and the {{Term|ST|Garden Freight Car|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Stal Skull}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Dunes|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes|link}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Stal Skull}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Stal Skull}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Stal Skull}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum}}''', ''"Only Bombs-no swords!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Stal Skull}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Stal Skull}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple}}''', ''"Don't pop any balloons!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Stal Skull}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Stal Skull}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Dunewalker Duds|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 139"/>
