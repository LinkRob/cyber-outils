'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time''''' is an upcoming action-adventure game published by Nintendo. It is the second remake of the 1998 Nintendo 64 video game, following ''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D'' (2011) for the Nintendo 3DS. It is scheduled to be released for the Nintendo Switch 2 in 2026.
