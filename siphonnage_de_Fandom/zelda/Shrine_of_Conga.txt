{{Term/Store|Shrine of Conga|Shrines of Conga|Series, CoH|plural}} are objects in {{CoH}}. They were introduced as part of the {{OO}} [[Downloadable Content]] update.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Shrine of Conga}} appear in {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}} in both {{Term|CoH|Dungeon Mode|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Story Mode — Octavo|link}}. While active, the rhythm is altered by adding an extra beat between the third and fourth of every 4 beats, and the shrine drops several items when activated. The effects of shrines last until the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} is left, the {{Term|CoH|Dungeon}} {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} is defeated, or the affected character falls in battle.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, look! A {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! {{Color|CoH Blue|Shrines}} are useful, but they often have a tricky {{Color|CoH Blue|side effect}} that you may not like. Bump into it if you want to take a chance and activate the {{Color|CoH Blue|shrine}}! Shrine effects stay active until you leave the dungeon, defeat the dungeon boss, or are defeated.|Trill|CoH}}</ref>

When destroyed, the shrine drops a [[Diamond]].
