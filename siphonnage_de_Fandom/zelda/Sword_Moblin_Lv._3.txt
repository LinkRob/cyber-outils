'''Sword Moblin Lv. 3''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Sword Moblin Lv. 3|A veteran Moblin that has mastered using its huge sword and shield to cut down all opponents.<ref name="EoW Sword Moblin Lv. 3">{{Cite|A veteran Moblin that has mastered using its huge sword and shield to cut down all opponents.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Sword Moblin Lv. 3 are stronger versions of [[Sword Moblin|Sword Moblins]] that have significantly higher health, deal more damage, and only appear in the [[Eternal Forest]] and [[Hebra Mountains]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which costs five Echo energy to summon.
