{{Term/Store|Nyave||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282}}</ref>

==Biography==
A cowardly member of the "Maritime Defense Force", Nyave can be found sailing in a ship in the southwestern part of the [[World of the Ocean King]]. When [[Link]] enters the ship, Nyave can be found lying on the ground, pretending to be dead, as [[Miniblin]]s and a [[Geozard]] run around. If Link defeats them and rescues Nyave, he will reward him with several [[Treasure]]s he picked up.{{Note|If Link has a maximum of 99 for all the Treasures except for the Regal Ring, Nyave will only give him Regal Rings. However, if Link has 99 of all the Treasures including Regal Rings, Nyave will give him [[Big Green Rupee]]s instead.}} Nyave has a brother, [[Nyeve]], who is a fan of Link. Nyave wants to be a cook like his older brother in order to get married.

Nyave is part of the [[Phantom Hourglass Trading Sequence|game's trading sequence]]. By giving him the [[Guard Notebook]] he lost, Link will receive a [[Wood Heart]].

==Trivia==
*In the German translation, Nyave is named ''Naiv'' (meaning ''naïve''), while Nyeve is left unnamed.

==Nomenclature==
Nyave and his brother's names are plays on the words ''knave'' and ''naïve'', respectively.
