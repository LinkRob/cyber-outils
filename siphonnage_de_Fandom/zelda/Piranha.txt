{{Term/Store|Piranha|Piranhas|Series, LA, LADX, LANS, OoS, OoA, FS|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 198 {{Exp Game|LA, OoS, OoA, FS}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
Piranhas in {{LA}} are green [[fish]] with black dorsal regions who hide underwater. Whenever [[Link]] is nearby, they will quickly surface and jump towards him in an attempt to damage him. They can be found in [[Goponga Swamp]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
Piranhas in {{OoS}} resemble a yellow variant of the Piranhas in {{LA|-}}, behaving in the same manner as their past counterpart. They can be found in the [[Eyeglass Lake]].

==={{FS|-}}===
Piranhas in {{FS}} are red with a silvery dorsal region. They attack in much the same way as their past incarnations. They can be found in [[Talus Cave]]. In {{FSAE}}, they appear in the {{LA|-}} Stage of the [[Realm of Memories]].

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Piranha|Long fins and remarkable ferocity define this underwater predator. It pursues its prey relentlessly.<ref name="EoW Piranha">{{Cite|Long fins and remarkable ferocity define this underwater predator. It pursues its prey relentlessly.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Piranhas are highly aggressive fish enemies that can be found throughout the [[Faron Wetlands]]. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Piranha [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of two.
