{{Term/Store|Ramrock||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Boss}} of the {{Term|OoA|Ancient Tomb|link}}, the eighth {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=E |publisher= |page=199}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} is a levitating stone head that comes to life when {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} gets near. It is battled in four distinct phases. Much of its movement consists of sweeping back and forth along the rear wall in a long, rectangular room. In the southern corners of the room are regrowable [[Bush]]es that can be cut to replenish {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s supply of {{Plural|OoA|Bomb|link}} and {{Plural|OoA|Scent Seed|link}}.

{{Term|OoA|Ramrock}}'s first method of attack is to fire its fists at {{Term|OoA|Link}} as he passes into its line of sight. {{Term|OoA|Link}} can use his {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}} to strike the fists back at the {{Term|OoA|Boss}}. A direct hit from one of its own fists will damage {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}}, otherwise they will simply rejoin the head when they reach the wall. After three hits, the next phase will begin.

For the second phase, {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} summons a new pair of hands designed for crushing. It detaches from the rear wall and begins to chase after {{Term|OoA|Link}}. Every few seconds, {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} stops in place and slams its hands together in an attempt to crush {{Term|OoA|Link}}, who must lay or throw a {{Term|OoA|Bomb|link}} directly between its hands. {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} will crush the {{Term|OoA|Bomb}}, taking damage from the explosion. If {{Term|OoA|Link}} can do this three times, the battle will enter the next phase.

{{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} begins the third phase by returning to the rear wall, discarding its hands again, and summoning two shields that protect its front and sides from any attack. It will attack {{Term|OoA|Link}} by periodically firing a large energy ball at him, or by firing unavoidable lasers from its eyes as it sweeps across the room. {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s only counter-attack is to use the {{Term|OoA|Seed Shooter|link}} to bounce any {{Term|OoA|Mystical Seed|display=Seed}} off the walls, hitting {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} in the back. Four hits with the {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed|display=Seeds}} will end this phase.

For the final phase, {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} loses the shields and gains two miniature cannons for arms, each one carrying a heavy {{Term|OoA|Ball|link}} attached by a chain. When {{Term|OoA|Link}} enters the {{Term|OoA|Boss}}'s line of sight, it will fire one of the {{Plural|OoA|Ball}} at him, causing great damage if it hits. In the brief time before {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} retracts the {{Term|OoA|Ball}} and continues its assault, {{Term|OoA|Link}} must grab the other {{Term|OoA|Ball}} using his {{Term|OoA|Power Glove|link}}, pull it back to its full extension, then release the {{Term|OoA|Ball}}, sending it hurtling into the {{Term|OoA|Boss}}. After doing this three times, {{Term|OoA|Ramrock}} will finally be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Heart Container|link}} and opening the door to the {{Term|OoA|Falling Star|link}}.
