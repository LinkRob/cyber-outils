The {{Term/Store|Shora Hah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shora Hah Shrine}}<br/>Blue Flame}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Blue Flame''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Shora Hah Shrine}} is located inside the crag of the {{Term|BotW|Isle of Rabac|link}}. It can be reached by taking a {{Term|BotW|Mine Cart|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}}, which follows a rail that curves toward the {{Term|BotW|Isle of Rabac|display=Isle}}. This {{Term|BotW|Mine Cart}} is propelled using {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}. Alternately, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} can also be reached by gliding down to it using the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} from a nearby elevated point. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shora Hah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shora Hah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Shora Hah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Shora Hah Shrine}} consists of a single massive chamber filled with a pool of {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} beneath its walkways. Inside the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} encounters a metal block with {{Plural|BotW|Floor Spikes|link}} on its bottom. This block is connected to vertical rails, and it can be hoisted up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must then pass underneath it while it is raised. On the other side is an unlit {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|link}}}}. The left path leads to two raised platforms that can be reached by making a bridge by moving the steel blocks across the rail. On one of the platforms is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Bow|link}}.

The other platform has a lit {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} bearing a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must bring to the unlit {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} using a wooden {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}}, or an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}. Lighting the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} below opens a barred door to another walkway, in which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must avoid {{Term|BotW|Water|link}} streams to light another {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}}. This activates a moving platform across from it.

From the walkway, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must light an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} and fire it at the moving platform's {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} to make stairs raise. These stairs lead to a {{Term|BotW|Switch|link}} that lowers a cylindrical ramp that drops a giant {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boulder|link}}. The ramp leads to an enclosed area with a group of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}}. Two {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} here collectively contain a {{Term|BotW|Great Flameblade|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}. Lighting the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} here opens the way to another area with three more {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}}. Two of the {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestals}} have {{Term|BotW|Water}} spouts that will douse the flames if lit, requiring that {{Term|BotW|Link}} suspends one with {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} and quickly lights both of them.

The following area has three {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}}, two of which are on moving platforms that move between {{Term|BotW|Water}} streams. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must light both moving {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestals}} at once by firing a lit {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} when they are close. Successfully doing so creates {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to glide to the next area. There, {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds two more {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestals}}. One is on a movable metal block on a rail that must be moved to the other end, but has a {{Term|BotW|Water}} spout in its path. {{Term|BotW|Link}} may suspend the {{Term|BotW|Water}} spout with {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} and quickly move the block over with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}.

Alternately, it is possible to move the block over first and then light its {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} with a lit {{Term|BotW|Arrow}}. This brings the lit {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} to the final walkway, which can otherwise only be reached by riding the {{Term|BotW|Updraft}} in-between. Two nearby {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} sit on ledges which can be reached by using the {{Term|BotW|Updraft}}. They collectively contain a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Sword|linl}}.

The final walkway has three {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}. The walkway leads to another torch and a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} containing 10 {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}. Just ahead of the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} are a group of seven more {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} that have {{Term|BotW|Water}} spouts pointed at them. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must light all seven {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestals}} at once by using a {{Term|BotW|Spin Attack|link}} with a lit {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} in order to open the final door.

At the very end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} is the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Shora Hah}}, who presents {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shora Hah|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shora Hah|BotW}}</ref>
