The '''Square Jewel''' is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
The Square Jewel is found inside a cave behind a bombable wall in [[Spool Swamp]] accessed only during [[Winter]]. In a [[Linked Game]], it swaps locations with the 100 [[Rupee]]s found in a cave behind another bombable wall beneath [[Eyeglass Lake]] during [[Summer]]. It is used in conjunction with the other three Jewels to open the door leading to [[Tarm Ruins]].
