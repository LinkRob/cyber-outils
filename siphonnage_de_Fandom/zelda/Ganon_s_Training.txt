{{Term/Store|Ganon's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ganon's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Ganon's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, except for a single {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}}. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} is killed, two more of them spawn alongside more {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} reaches 160 KOs, more {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} spawn. After 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, a gate opens and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} spawn alongside many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}. The time limit is also taken away, so the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} are defeated.
