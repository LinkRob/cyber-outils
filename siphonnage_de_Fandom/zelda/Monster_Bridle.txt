==Location and Uses==
Monster Bridles are a type of {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} that can be purchased at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for 399 {{Plural|BotW|Mon|link}}. These Bridles were handmade by the shop's proprietor, {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}}, though he had a more monstrous {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} in mind when he made them.<ref name="Inventory"/>

They can be equipped on Horses at either the {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Monster Bridle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> Like other {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear|link}}, Monster Bridles can't be used by {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
