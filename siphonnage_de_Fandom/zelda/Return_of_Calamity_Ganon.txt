"{{Term/Store|Return of Calamity Ganon||Series, BotW}}" is the fifteenth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Return of Calamity Ganon}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #15}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The "{{Term|BotW|Return of Calamity Ganon}}" {{Term|BotW|Memory}} can be unlocked at the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - East Gate|link}}. It opens up on the day of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}'s 17th birthday, with the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} awaiting {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}'s return from the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|link}} at the top of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}. As they make their return, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} is clearly despondent.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} reach the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - East Gate|display=East Gate}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} asks what happened and not to keep them in suspense.<ref>{{Cite|Well? Don't keep us in suspense. How'd everything go up there on the mountain?|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} solemnly shakes her head, clearly upsetting the other {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}. {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} steps forward to ask if she truly felt nothing,<ref>{{Cite|So you didn't feel anything? No power at all?|Revali|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} quietly apologizes for.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry, no.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} declares that they should move on,<ref>{{Cite|Then let's move on.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> assuring {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} that she has done all she could and that feeling sorry for herself will not help.<ref>{{Cite|You've done all you could. Feeling sorry for yourself won't be of any help.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She reminds {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} that the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom}} was not her last shot and that anything could spark her [[Triforce|sealing power]].<ref>{{Cite|After all, it's not like your last shot was up there on Mt. Lanayru. Anything could finally spark the power to seal Ganon away.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> She affirms that they just need to keep looking.<ref>{{Cite|We just have to keep looking for that...thing.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} looks to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, whose eyes are on {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}. She tries to give {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} some advice, though at first she struggles with what to say.<ref>{{Cite|If I may... I thought you... Well, I'm not sure how to put this into words... I'm actually quite embarrassed to say it.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> She reveals that she has been thinking about what she does while she is {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Grace|link|display=healing}} people, and she tries to explain what she thinks about when she uses her power.<ref>{{Cite|But I was thinking about what I do when I'm healing. You know, what usually goes through my mind... It helps when I think—when I think about—|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> However, she is interrupted by the ground shaking.

Using {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link|display=his Gale}}, {{Term|BotW|Revali}} flies straight up to see {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} under siege by {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}. The rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} quickly realize what has happened.<ref>{{Cite|It's here.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is it, then...|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> Mipha asks if they are sure,<ref>{{Cite|Are you sure?|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> to which {{Term|BotW|Revali}} responds that he is positive.<ref>{{Cite|Positive.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} realizes that {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} has awoken.<ref>{{Cite|It's awake... Ganon!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Daruk}} addresses the group, claiming they will need everything they have to take the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}} down and commanding the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} to return to their {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's stop wasting time! We're gonna need everything we got to take that thing down! Now Champions! To your Divine Beasts! Show that swirling swine who's boss!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He further states that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be meeting {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} head on, so their assault needs to be unified.<ref>{{Cite|Link will need to meet Ganon head on when we attack! This needs to be a unified assault!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} back to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}},<ref>{{Cite|Little guy! You get to Hyrule Castle!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> much to {{Term|BotW|Revali}}'s annoyance. He assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can count on them for support, but reiterates it is up to him to defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can count on us for support. But it's up to you to pound Ganon into oblivion!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} places her hands on {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} shoulders, trying to convince her to retreat somewhere safe.<ref>{{Cite|Come. We should go. We need to get you someplace safe.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> But {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} refuses, stepping away from {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} and insisting that although she may not have much use on the battlefield, there is something she can do to help.<ref>{{Cite|No! I am not a child anymore! I may not be much use on the battlefield... But there must... There must be something I can do to help!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Memory}} ends with the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} gazing up at {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} storm, {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} rubbing at the back of his head.
