{{Term/Store|Monkton||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Monkton|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Monkton}} is an retired resident of {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}}.<ref name="Monkton">{{Cite|A traveler, eh? Can you spare a minute to listen to a bored, old retiree tell a story?|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> During the day, he paces around the interior of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}. At night, he sleeps upright in one of the chairs inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} was a child, there were not many kids who lived around {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heh, to be honest, growing up out in the middle of nowhere... Well, there weren't really other kids around.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> His only friends were {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)|link}}, whom he would explore with.<ref>{{Cite|My only friends were {{Color|BotW Blue|Harry}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Mary}}. I remember playing with {{Color|BotW Blue|Harry}} out in the maze and {{Color|BotW Blue|Mary}} out by the ruins...|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Monkton}} was told to avoid the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|link}} by the adults at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me about Harry.] There's an {{Color|BotW Red|enormous maze}} beyond the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Tundra}} just north of here. I don't know who built it or when. The adults always said not to go near it. They said it was too dangerous there.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} convinced {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} to enter the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|display=Labyrinth}} to test his courage, but he never saw him again.<ref>{{Cite|One day, I urged {{Color|BotW Blue|Harry}} to go into the maze as a test of courage. I...I never saw him again.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} realized that the adults were right when they said that the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|display=Labyrinth}} is dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|I learned the hard way that it really is dangerous there. Children have no real sense of danger, I suppose...|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>

The adults also warned {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} to avoid the ruins of the {{Term|BotW|Forgotten Temple|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}, as it was also supposedly dangerous<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me about Mary.] Maybe you've seen the big canyon to the southeast. There are some secret ruins down at the base of that valley. I don't know who built them or when. The adults always said not to go near. They said it was too dangerous there.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} still took {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} to investigate the ruins.<ref>{{Cite|One day {{Color|BotW Blue|Mary}} and I went out there to explore and search for the ruins.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} slipped and fell into the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|display=Canyon}}, and {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} never saw her again.<ref>{{Cite|In the blink of an eye, {{Color|BotW Blue|Mary}} lost her footing. She slipped and tumbled down the cliff.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> That day, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} realized that curiosity could also be the cause of tragedy.<ref>{{Cite|I...I never saw her again. Sometimes a child's innocent curiosity can cause tragedies that can never be taken back.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches him, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} asks if he would like to listen to a story.<ref name="Monkton"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires about the story, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} claims that he has been reminiscing on his childhood recently.<ref>{{Cite|[What kind of story?] When you get to be my age, you start reminiscing about all the memories from your childhood.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> Even in his old age, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} is still heartbroken over losing {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}}.<ref>{{Cite|To this day, the mere memory breaks my heart.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That memory will haunt me until my dying day...|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>

After listening to both stories, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he overcame his grief over {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} as he grew older.<ref>{{Cite|As I grew older, I learned to overcome my grief over losing them. You can't change the past, only the future.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> He then states that two of their descendants are located in the stall next to {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}}, revealing that both {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}} were {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Harry}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Mary}} never made it back, but two of their descendants are in that stall over there. My only friends out in these parts were horses, you see. I suppose that's still true to this day.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> While he may no longer grieve for them, {{Term|BotW|Monkton}} is still plagued by his memories of {{Term|BotW|Harry (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Mary (Breath of the Wild)}},<ref>{{Cite|I just can't relax...|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> thinking about them even while he sleeps.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Harry... Mary...}}|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>
