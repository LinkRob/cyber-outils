{{Term/Store|Sparrow|Sparrows|Series, BotW|plural}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 164}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Plural|BotW|Sparrow}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Bird|link}} found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. They are registered within the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} under six different sub species. All {{Plural|BotW|Sparrow}} are small in size and will flee from {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if they spot him. They drop a {{Term|BotW|Chickaloo Tree Nut|link}} when killed.

==Types of Sparrows==
===Common Sparrow===
{{Main|Common Sparrow}}
{{Plural|BotW|Common Sparrow}} are green in color, with a light orange chest and white underbelly. They are the most common {{Term|BotW|Sparrow}} and live in grasslands, forests, and occasionally villages. They are omnivores and eat a diet of nuts and insects.<ref>{{Cite|Their diet consists of nuts and small insects.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

===Red Sparrow===
{{Main|Red Sparrow}}
{{Plural|BotW|Red Sparrow}} are herbivorous birds that live in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}, consuming a diet of nuts and wild plants.<ref>{{Cite|These small birds live in the Hebra region and eat mainly nuts and wild plants.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They are deep grey in color with a white chest, and bright red face from which they presumably get their name. These red facial feathers are often used by {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} for decorations.<ref>{{Cite|Their bright-red feathers are often used as decorations.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

===Blue Sparrow===
{{Main|Blue Sparrow}}
As their name implies, {{Plural|BotW|Blue Sparrow}} are blue in color with a lighter underbelly, a white chin and yellow cheeks. They are most commonly found by the watersides of the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}, bathing in the shallows and eating the leaves of the plants that grow there.<ref>{{Cite|They get most of their food from leaves and plants that grow near water. If it's safe enough, they can be spotted bathing in puddles or shallow rivers.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

===Rainbow Sparrow===
{{Main|Rainbow Sparrow}}
The most colorful of all the {{Plural|BotW|Sparrow}} and a favorite among dilettantes,<ref>{{Cite|As the name suggests, their feathers are more colorful than other sparrows', which has made them a favorite among dilettantes.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Rainbow Sparrow}} have purple bodies, a red head, a green underbelly and a pink and cream neck. They can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Faron (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}, where they consume an omnivorous diet of nuts and small, rainforest insects.<ref>{{Cite|They feed on small rain-forest insects, but the omnivore in them loves nuts as well.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

===Sand Sparrow===
{{Main|Sand Sparrow}}
Well adapted to the hot, arid climate of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sand Sparrow}} are known to burry themselves in the sand as a means of avoiding predators. <ref>{{Cite|Sometimes they can be spotted half-buried in the sand, hiding perfectly still as a means of evading predators.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They are largely dark gray in color with a white underbelly, red head and orange cheeks.

===Golden Sparrow===
{{Main|Golden Sparrow}}
Much like other {{Plural|BotW|Bird|link}} found throughout the hot and volcanic {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Golden Sparrow}} have evolved heat resistant feathers to cope with the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Their down is resilient to burning, an evolutionary trait produced by the harsh volcanic environment.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> In appearance they are largely gray in color with a white underbelly and golden head, from which they get their name. They are insectivores and live off of small insects they find hidden in rocks.<ref>{{Cite|They subsist on small insects that hide inside rocks.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>
