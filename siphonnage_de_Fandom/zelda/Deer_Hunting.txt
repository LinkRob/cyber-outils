{{Term/Store|Deer Hunting||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Dantz">{{Cite|About that {{Color|BotW Red|deer hunting}}... No time like the present, right? Go get started, and I'll call you once your {{Color|BotW Red|minute}} is up, OK?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
{{Term|BotW|Deer Hunting}} is operated by {{Term|BotW|Dantz|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Hateno Pasture|link}}, which is located in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Dantz|link}}, he realizes that he is a traveler.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Good day, stranger! Hrm? Are you some kinda wanderer?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Since he looks capable of using a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would be willing to help him.<ref>{{Cite|You look handy with a bow. Mind helping a poor farmer out?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> He explains that {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest|link}} has become overpopulated by {{Plural|BotW|Deer|link}}, which is destructive to the environment.<ref>{{Cite|So what I need is for you to hunt some {{Color|BotW Red|deer}} out in {{Color|BotW Blue|Retsam Forest}}. They're overpopulated these days, and it's destroying the forest.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a one minute time limit to hunt as many of the {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} in the {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest|display=Forest}} as he can.<ref>{{Cite|That's why I was hoping you could hunt all the {{Color|BotW Red|deer}} you can...in {{Color|BotW Red|one minute}}.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts this task, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} asks if he can head out right away.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Can...can you go right away?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} for hunting tips before starting the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} suggests using a strong {{Term|BotW|Bow}} that will take down a {{Term|BotW|Deer}} in one shot..<ref>{{Cite|[Is there a trick to this?] A trick? Well, I guess it helps to have a strong bow to take 'em down with one shot.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> He also says that {{Term|BotW|Link}} should use {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} to sneak up on the {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} before attacking.<ref>{{Cite|And of course, you want to get close enough to the deer to line up your shot without them noticing you.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to start the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} will remind him that he is counting on him.<ref>{{Cite|What a trooper! I'm counting on you!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will then be transferred to {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest}}, where {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} explains that he will call him once his minute is up.<ref name="Dantz"/> During this time, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to hunt as many {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Buck}} as he can. Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} stray too far from the {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} will end the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} and call him back.<ref>{{Cite|You've gone too far! Come back!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Once the minute is up, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} will yell for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return.<ref>{{Cite|That's time! Come back, wanderer!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

Back at {{Term|BotW|Hateno Pasture}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} will review how many {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} hunted. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} failed to get a single {{Term|BotW|Deer}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} will refuse to give him a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? You didn't even get ONE? Sorry, but no results, no reward.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} hunted one to three {{Plural|BotW|Deer}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} offers his thanks and rewards him with a {{Term|BotW|Green Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for doing your part! Here's a reward for you...|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Next, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} killed four to six {{Plural|BotW|Deer}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} calls him vicious with his {{Term|BotW|Bow}} and gifts him a {{Term|BotW|Blue Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're downright vicious with that bow, wanderer! Here's a reward for you...|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} hunts between seven to nine {{Plural|BotW|Deer}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} is impressed by his initiative and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I knew you were a go-getter, but this is more than I ever dreamed of! Here's a reward for you...|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} conquers all 10 {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} in {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} tells him that it is possible to be too good at hunting and rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got 10 deer?! Easy there, wanderer. I didn't think I'd be saying this, but there's such a thing as being TOO good a hunter. I won't skimp on it this time!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

After giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} his reward, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} reiterates that they need to get the {{Term|BotW|Deer}} population under control.<ref>{{Cite|The thing with these deer, though... We really have to get their numbers under control.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would be willing to head into the {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest}} again.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, wanderer, can I ask you to go again?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} is disappointed and tells him to return if he changes his mind.<ref>{{Cite|Oh. Well, shucks. Let me know if you ever change your mind.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>
