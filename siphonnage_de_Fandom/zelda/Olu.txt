{{Term/Store|Olu||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']].<ref>{{Cite|Olu|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Olu|N/A|ToTK}}</ref>

==Biography==
In ''Breath of the Wild'', Olu is a [[Gerudo]] who can be found right outside of [[Hotel Oasis]]. She works for the hotel, clapping her hands and advertising the Hotel to passing visitors. If [[Link]] speaks to Olu, she encourages him to relax his body and mind at Hotel Oasis.<ref>{{Cite|Are you exhausted from your travels, ma'am? Please consider Hotel Oasis!|Olu|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Relax your body and mind at Hotel Oasis!|Olu|BotW}}</ref> She recommends the Hotel's spa plan and cites it as the number one way to feel relaxed and beautiful<ref>{{Cite|I recommend checking out our unique... {{Color|BotW Red|spa plan}}! Ranked number one in ways to feel relaxed and beautiful!|Olu|BotW}}</ref>, telling Link to go inside and ask [[Romah]] for more information.<ref>{{Cite|Step inside and speak with Romah for more information!|Olu|BotW}}</ref>

In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', Olu now works as an apprentice under Romah and can be found inside the hotel after Link and [[Riju]] save Gerudo Town.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Olu}} is apprenticing under {{Color|BotW Red|Romah}}. She's the only vai who has ever had the opportunity to inherit Romah's special technique.|Jules|ToTK}}</ref> She needs to practice her technique, so offers Link a spa plan for 25 rupees. Some of the time, this provides 3 temporary hearts and a temporary stamina wheel; the rest, it only restores Link's hearts.<ref>{{Cite|I'm really only an apprentice, so there's still a lot left to learn. Oh, but even so, I can guarantee that it will make your body feel as good as new. Don't worry about that! But—and this is what I'm still working on—if I get things just right... you'll feel a boost of energy {{Color|BotW Red|just like}} with the owner's spa plan! The apprentice spa plan is only {{Color|BotW Red|25 rupees}} too! That's {{Color|BotW Red|half}} of what the owner's plan costs, you know!|Olu|ToTK}}</ref>
