The {{Term/Store|Ocarina of Wind||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Ocarina of Wind! North, south, east, and west are all just a breath away!|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|Play the ocarina to call up Zeffa and be carried to any Wind Crest you’ve found. Choose your destination and press the A Button to confirm it.}}
The {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind}} is obtained at the top of the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}} after defeating {{Term|TMC|Mazaal|link}}. By playing it, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can call {{Term|TMC|Zeffa|link}} to [[Warping|travel]] to any {{Term|TMC|Wind Crest|link}} he has found.<ref>{{Cite|Aha!! So that mysterious mark we've been seeing is a Wind Crest. If you play the Ocarina of Wind, Zeffa comes to pick you up and fly you away. That means we can have Zeffa carry us to any other Wind Crest we've seen. Are you following me, Link? Don't hurt your brain thinking about it. Just give it a try, and you'll see what I'm talking about.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The tune played on the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind}} is the same as the one played on the {{Term|TLoZ|Recorder|link}} in {{TLoZ}}.
*The color of, and hole placement on, the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind}} is the same as the {{Term|OoT|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}}, though there is no known connection between the two.
