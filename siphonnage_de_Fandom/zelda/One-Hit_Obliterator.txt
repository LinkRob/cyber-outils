The {{Term/Store|One-Hit Obliterator||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|One-Hit Obliterator}}<br/>A weapon that defeats foes with one hit and causes the user to die from one hit. It loses its sheen and power after two consecutive uses, but will eventually regain both.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
<!-- A weapon that defeats foes with one hit and causes the user to die from one hit. It loses its sheen and power after two consecutive uses, but will eventually regain both. -->
{{Compendium/Extract|One-Hit Obliterator}}
[[File:BotW One-Hit Obliterator Icon.png|left]]
The {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} is an unbreakable {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} that can instantly defeat enemies in a single hit. However, it also renders its user vulnerable to single strikes as well.<ref name="Inventory"/> The {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} can only be used twice at any given time. When its power is depleted, it will stop glowing for a few seconds to recharge.<ref>{{Cite|You can use this weapon's ability only twice within a set period of time...only when it is glowing. When enough time passes, the weapon will regain its sheen along with its power.|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> During this time, its attack power is reduced to 1 and it will not recharge while it is swung.

While the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} is in {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, he cannot switch to another melee {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, nor can he regain health. Any health regained through the use of {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} will quickly disappear after {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves the {{Term|BotW|Inventory}} screen. The {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} can safely strike an opponent's {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} without losing its charge. While at full charge, the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} can fell {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} in one hit, whereas its uncharged state requires 40 hits to fell a {{Term|BotW|Tree}} and 12 to reduce its log to {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} is found inside the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} after {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} to the terminal it was found in. After doing so, the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} will appear opposite the terminal inside the chamber. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} grabs hold of the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}}, his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} will reduce to a mere quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}. Upon regaining his composure, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}}, calls out to him.<ref>{{Cite|I am Maz Koshia. I now address the one who wishes to master a Divine Beast...|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that while undergoing the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial|link}}, he must steel himself with dedication, as its volatile properties render its use highly dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|To undertake this task requires unwavering dedication. That weapon defeats foes with one hit. However, the reverse is also true, as its wielder will also fall to a single strike.|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} then directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to his task of clearing four marked locations on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}, where groups of enemies must be cleared.<ref>{{Cite|When enough time passes, the weapon will regain its sheen along with its power. At the four marked locations within the Great Plateau...defeat all monsters present to prove your power.|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia}} also warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he leaves the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}} at any time before the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial|display=Trial}} is complete, the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} will leave him and return to its pedestal in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you are not up to the challenge, leave this plateau. The weapon will return here, and the trial will end.|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes {{Term|BotW|Etsu Korima Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rohta Chigah Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ruvo Korbah Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita Shrine|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} will split into four glowing orbs and scatter to different areas of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You completed the trials of the Great Plateau! After conquering the last shrine, the One-Hit Obliterator split into four glowing orbs and scattered to different parts of Hyrule.|EX The Champions' Ballad|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator}} then returns to its pedestal in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}. However, it can no longer be taken from its pedestal and having fulfilled its duty, rests peacefully.<ref>{{Cite|It has fulfilled its duty. It slumbers peacefully...|N/A|BotW}}</ref>
