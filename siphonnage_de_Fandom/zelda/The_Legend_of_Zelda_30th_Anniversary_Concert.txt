'''''The Legend of Zelda'' 30th Anniversary Concert''' was a series of concerts performed at Japan in 2016 as part of [[The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary|''The Legend of Zelda'' 30th Anniversary]]. The Tokyo concert on October 16, 2016 was released as a [[The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary Concert (Album)|live album]] on February 15, 2017.

==Concerts==
The 30th Anniversary Concerts featured appearances by the staff involved in {{TLoZ|Series}}, including [[Shigeru Miyamoto]], [[Eiji Aonuma]], [[Koji Kondo]], and [[Takashi Tezuka]]. They were conducted by Takemoto Taizo. During performance of the Hyrule Field theme from {{OoT|-}}, attendees could use the different colored program sheet to choose which of the three versions of the theme should be played.<ref name= "NE">{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Brian|published= October 3, 2016|retrieved= October 4, 2016|url= http://nintendoeverything.com/lots-of-zelda-talk-breath-of-the-wild-original-planning-documents-at-30th-anniversary-concert-in-japan/|title= Lots of Zelda talk, Breath of the Wild, original planning documents at 30th anniversary concert in Japan|site= Nintendo Everything|type=}}</ref>

Attendees to the concerts needed to enter a lottery to win a chance to purchase a ticket.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= We say lucky because you'll have to enter your name in a lottery in order to buy any of the tickets regardless of level.|author= KK Miller|published= July 3, 2016|retrieved= October 4, 2016|url= https://www.japantoday.com/category/entertainment/view/nintendo-to-stage-legend-of-zelda-30th-anniversary-concerts|title= Nintendo to stage 'Legend of Zelda' 30th anniversary concerts|site= Japan Today|type=}}</ref> With each ticket, a set of commemorative pins were also included.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Attendees can choose to purchase a Master Sheet ticket which has an original design and a commemorative pin set for 12,000 yen (£87.50), S seat tickets for 9,000 yen (£65.50) or A seat tickets at 7,500 yen (£54.50).|author= Alex Seedhouse|published= June 30, 2016|retrieved= October 4, 2016|url= http://www.nintendo-insider.com/2016/06/the-legend-of-zelda-30th-anniversary-concert-in-japan/|title= The Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary Concert Resounds Across Japan|site= Nintendo Insider|type=}}</ref>
