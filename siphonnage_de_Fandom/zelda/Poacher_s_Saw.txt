The {{Term/Store|Poacher's Saw||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}} and its remake, {{OoT3D}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
[[Link]] can obtain the Poacher's Saw by returning to where the [[Master Craftsman's Son]] initially was in the [[Lost Woods]] only to find him gone instead. [[Fado (Ocarina of Time)|Fado]] explains that he has turned into a [[Stalfos]]. By giving her the [[Odd Poultice]], she will let him take the Poacher's Saw the Master Craftsman's Son left behind.

==Trivia==
*The Poacher's Saw is a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_saw ''ryoba'' (両刃)], a Japanese double-edged saw meant for carpentry.
*It is unknown why this item is called a "Poacher's Saw" when it belongs to the [[Master Craftsman]] and is meant for woodworking carpentry.
