|caption =      Bowser

{{smw|Bowser}} is the arch-nemesis of Mario and the King of Koopas,<ref>{{cite|Well, he is the king of Koopas. It's only natural he'd be slow. But that's only because he's the heaviest fighter here--by far. He's a powerhouse of destruction. Careful he doesn't flatten you.|Colonel Roy Campbell|Super Smash Bros. {{clear}}
===Captain Falcon===
{{ImageCaption
|image =        File:SSB4 Captain Falcon.png
|size =         100px
|align =        left
|caption =      Captain Falcon
|captionalign = center
}}
[[smashwiki:Captain Falcon|Captain Falcon]], also known as "F-Zero pilot number 07,"<ref>{{cite|Good eye, Snake! He's F-Zero pilot number 07!|Otacon|Super Smash Bros. Brawl}}</ref> is the main protagonist of the ''F-Zero'' series, [[Nintendo]]'s fast-paced futuristic racing video game franchise. Only his machine actually was shown in game, portrayed by a two-dimensional sprite - not until ''F-Zero X'' would his character be conveyed by a computer-generated image. The ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' games are the only times he has appeared outside the ''F-Zero'' series, aside from his helmet and car or in his anime, and the first time that he himself became a playable character. His signature move, the "[[smashwiki:Falcon Punch|Falcon Punch]]," has become the subject of many parodies.
{{clear}}

===Diddy Kong===
{{ImageCaption
|image =        File:SSB4 Diddy Kong.png
|size =         100px
|align =        right
|caption =      Diddy Kong
|captionalign = center
}}
{{smw|Diddy Kong}} is Donkey Kong's best pal. He's famous for his peanut popgun and rocketbarrel jetpack. Diddy is a playable in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''. He has a major role in [[The Subspace Emissary]], in which he is teamed up with Fox for a large portion of the game. Diddy competed with Link's third place score in ''[[Cameos of The Legend of Zelda#Donkey Kong|Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]]''.
{{clear}}

===Donkey Kong===
{{ImageCaption
|image =        File:SSB4 Donkey Kong.png
|size =         100px
|align =        left
|caption =      Donkey Kong
|captionalign = center
}}
{{smw|Donkey Kong}} is a gorilla and the star of the ''[[smw:Donkey Kong (franchise)|Donkey Kong]]'' video game series.  He made his debut in the original ''[[smw:Donkey Kong (game)|Donkey Kong]]'' arcade game as the main antagonist with Mario as the protagonist. He has also appeared in many ''Mario'' games. Donkey Kong is a playable fighter in every game of the {{SSB|-}} series. DK enjoys playing ''Zelda'' songs on his bongos, and he has played them in all 3 of his ''[[smw:Donkey Konga|Donkey Konga]]'' games (in different regions), and even has a ''Zelda'' noise-set and roulette game for them.
{{clear}}

===Dr. Mario===
{{ImageCaption
|image =        File:Dr. Mario.png
|size =         100px
|align =        right
|caption =      Dr. Mario
|captionalign = center
}}
Mario has assumed many roles over the years, one of them being {{smw|Dr. Mario}}. With his supply of [[smw:Megavitamin|Megavitamin]]s and {{smw|Nurse Peach}} at his side, no disease is incurable for him. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', Dr. Mario is very similar to his normal counterpart. His Megavitamins, replacing Mario's fireballs, are somewhat more powerful than the {{smw|fireball}}s. 
{{clear}}

===Falco===
{{ImageCaption
|image =        File:SSB4 Falco.png
|size =         100px
|align =        left
|caption =      Falco Lombardi
|captionalign = center
}}
[[starfox:Falco|Falco's]] past is mysterious, and he always avoids conversation of what he did before joining the team. Falco is seen as the finest pilot of the [[starfox:Star Fox (team)|Star Fox team]], possessing a high skill level when at the controls of an [[starfox:Arwing|Arwing]]. Shortly after the story of ''[[starfox:Star Fox 64|Star Fox 64]]'', he left the team. Where he went and why is unknown, but he does reappear again at the end of ''[[starfox:Star Fox Adventures|Star Fox Adventures]]'' to save Fox, and subsequently returns to the Star Fox team. Falco is not a playable character in the original ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' game.  However, he is present in the sequels, ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', and needs to be unlocked in both.
{{clear}}

===Fox===
{{ImageCaption
|image =        File:SSB4 Fox.png
|size =         100px
|align =        right
|caption =      Fox McCloud
|captionalign = center
}}
[[starfox:Fox|Fox]] is the leader and captain of the Star Fox Team. Fox is the main character of the [[starfox:Star Fox (series)|''Star Fox'' series]]. He and his team are all Pilots who defend the galaxy from multiple threats. Fox is also one of the characters in the original ''[[Super Smash Bros.]]'' game for the [[Nintendo 64]] and its sequel, ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]''. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' Fox's moveset remains the same, except for a few changes in appearance such as his "[[smashwiki:Fox Illusion|Fox Illusion]]" move. He is shown piloting his Arwing in the cutscenes and remains a large part of the storyline.
{{clear}}

===Ganondorf===
{{ImageCaption
|image =        File:SSB4 Ganondorf.png
|size =         100px
|align =        left
|caption =      Ganondorf
|captionalign = center
}}
{{main|Ganondorf}}
Ganondorf in the ''Super Smash Bros.'' series is an extremely slow but powerful character. His jumps are very low, making reaching high points difficult. His moveset is copied heavily from Captain Falcon, and the moves have been slowed down greatly; however, if they connect with their opponent, the effect is likely to be devastating. Appearing in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' as a stat-modified clone of Captain Falcon, ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'' attempts to remove this connotation by continuing to slightly alter both Ganondorf and Captain Falcon, making them seem slightly less like each other.
{{clear}}

===Ice Climbers===
The [[Ice Climbers]], Nana and Popo, are two best friend mountain climbers from the [[NES]] game ''Ice Climber''. They are playable from the start of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'' and return in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

It has many attacks, each one quite devastating. Though not as powerful as {{Ssb|Crazy Hand}}, the left hand as Master Hand is the right, the same rules apply: as its HP lower, its attacks become stronger and more frequent. Master Hand is prone to long periods of inactivity while Crazy Hand is fighting with it, and it does not attack as frequently as Crazy Hand. In ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', Tabuu uses the Master Hand as a puppet to do his bidding. The Master Hand at one point tries to kill Tabuu, but is quickly defeated. It is unknown whether he survived. Master Hand faced players at the end of the Classic Mode. If the player was able to defeat Classic Mode's numerous challenges under harsher settings, Crazy Hand would appear. He also appeared alongside Crazy Hand in {{Ssb|Event Match #50: Final Destination Match}}.
