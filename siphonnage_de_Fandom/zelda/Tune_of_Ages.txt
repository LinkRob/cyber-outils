The "{{Term/Store|Tune of Ages||Series, OoA}}",<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97}}</ref> also known as the '''"Tune of Time"''',{{Name Ref|OoA}} is the third tune that Link learns for the Harp of Ages in {{OoA}}.

==Location and Uses==
[[Nayru, the Oracle of Ages|Nayru]] teaches it to the young hero when he rescues her from [[Veran]]'s grip.

The Tune of Ages unlocks the full power of the [[Harp of Ages|Harp]]. It allows Link to freely travel between the past and present ages. When he plays this tune, he immediately reverts to the opposite age, no matter the location. However, if he cannot land in that space in the opposite age, he will be sent back. For instance, if he plays the tune in the Present and there's a tree in the Past, Link will be sent back to the Present (and vice-versa).
