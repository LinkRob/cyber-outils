{{Term/Store|Simmered Fruit|Simmered Fruits|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Simmered Fruit}}<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Simmered Fruit}} are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} different fruits together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. There are two ways to make this dish's default version. First, either {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}} must be used without any status-altering fruits. Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use multiple fruits with contradicting status effects. If consumed by Link {{Plural|BotW|Simmered Fruit}} will restore his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} by at least one {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. They have a sweet taste, and the fruits are simmered until tender.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can use certain fruits, in addition to extra ingredients, to make different variations of Simmered Fruits:

* '''Hearty Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}} or other hearty ingredients. By eating them, Link can have all of his Hearts healed and his maximum Hearts temporarily increased.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using energizing ingredients. When eaten, they will refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using enduring ingredients. Link can eat these fruits to completely restore and overfill his Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}} or other hasty ingredients. If Link eats them, Link's movement speed will be increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}} or other spicy ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}} or other electric ingredients. They can be eaten to give Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}} or other mighty ingredients. These fruits will boost the power of Link's attacks when eaten.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using tough ingredients. Eating these meals will increase Link's defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using sneaky ingredients. When eaten, they'll increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Simmered Fruit''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Simmered Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This sweet dish is made by heaping tasty fruits into a pan and simmering until tender.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} or other chilly ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
