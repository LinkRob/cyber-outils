The {{Term/Store|Shae Katha Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shae Katha Shrine}}<br/>Shae Katha's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Shae Katha's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Shae Katha Shrine}} is located behind the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}. However, the door leading to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is originally closed. {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Pagos Woods|link}}, where he will ask if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} wants to hear an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" passed down in the area.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His song hints that a Shrine is at the Spring, which is inside ruins shaped like a {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Where the forest {{Color|BotW Blue|dragon}} splays its jaws,<br/>A {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} sleeps with noble cause.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Listening to the song will begin "{{Term|BotW|The Serpent's Jaws|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

At the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pray to the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}, it will ask him to offer one of {{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Scale|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|Offer {{Color|BotW Red|Farosh's scale}}, received from the golden spirit, to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Courage}}.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link}} will drop one of its {{Term|BotW|Farosh's Scale|display=Scales}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} manages to strike its body. When one of {{Plural|BotW|Farosh's Scale}} is dropped into the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Courage}}, a wall behind the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue}} will open, revealing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} and completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your path has shown itself. Now go forth.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shae Katha|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shae Katha. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Shae Katha|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Shae Katha Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to {{Term|BotW|Shae Katha}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Thunderspear|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Shae Katha}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shae Katha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Shae Katha}} will wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shae Katha|BotW}}</ref>
