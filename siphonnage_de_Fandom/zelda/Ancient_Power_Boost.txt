It provides extra external energy, increasing the power of runes.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==

More often than not, the basic Ancient Power Boost has a much higher Attack than a basic Shiekah Slate.
Ancient Power Boosts can be obtained by beating certain Challenges, or by opening certain Treasure Chests
