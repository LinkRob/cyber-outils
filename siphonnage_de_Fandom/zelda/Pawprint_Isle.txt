{{Term/Store|Pawprint Isle||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (B5) is one of the isles of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==

This island is unique in that it has ''two'' separate [[Secret Cave]]s. The first cave, which can be accessed by crawling inside the small structure found in the middle of the island, contains an underground passage hiding a [[Piece of Heart]], a [[Joy Pendant]], many [[ChuChu]]s, and a Purple [[Rupee]]. The second cave is accessible only after Link collects the [[Hookshot]], since it is located in one of the island's "fingers". Inside it the young hero gains, after defeating numerous [[Wizzrobe]]s, a Silver Rupee. Also, there is a second Piece of Heart that can be obtained from the bottom of the sea by using one of the [[Treasure Chart]]s found inside the [[Tower of the Gods]].
