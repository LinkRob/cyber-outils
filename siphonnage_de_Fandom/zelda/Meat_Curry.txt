{{Term/Store|Meat Curry|Meat Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meat Curry}}<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Meat Curries are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Raw Meat]], [[Hylian Rice]], and [[Goron Spice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. They can be eaten to restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The heat from the Goron Spice allows one to enjoy the savoriness of the meat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding more ingredients to Meat Curry, several different variations can be made:

* '''Hearty Meat Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meat Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meat Curry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meat Curry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. They replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} when consumed.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meat Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meat Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Eating these curries will completely refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meat Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. If eaten, they'll increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meat Curry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one temporary level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meat Curry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Link gains one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when he eats these curries.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meat Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. They offer Link a boost to his attack power when consumed.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meat Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meat Curry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When eaten, they increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meat Curry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The heat from the spice allows you to enjoy the large portion of the meat's savoriness.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. Link will be granted one level of [[Heat Resistance]] if he eats these curries.<ref name="Chilly"/>
