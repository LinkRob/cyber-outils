The {{Term/Store|Nice Boomerang||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Nice Boomerang}} can be obtained by upgrading the {{Term|ALBW|Boomerang|link}} after finding and returning 10 {{Plural|ALBW|Lost Maiamai|link|display=Lost Maiamais}} to {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}. The upgrade has a farther range and faster speed than the {{Term|ALBW|Boomerang}}, and also allows {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} to throw up to three {{Plural|ALBW|Boomerang}} at once.<ref>{{Cite|Nice! The boomerang is more powerful now! Try it out to see its stronger effects! You can now throw three in a row! Speed and flying distance are also improved!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> Like the {{Term|ALBW|Boomerang}}, it can be used to stun and defeat {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}}, collect items from a distance and activate {{Plural|ALBW|Switch|link}}, and can be thrown in any direction that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} is facing.
