{{Term/Store|Maggie's Letter||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Letter is obtained and used during a sidequest available in [[Windfall Island]] after rescuing [[Aryll]] in [[Forsaken Fortress]]. The letter can be obtained after {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie|link}} returns to Windfall Island and becomes rich. She tells Link about [[Moblin#The Wind Waker|Moe the Moblin]], who supposedly is in love with her,<ref>{{Cite|When I think of my precious little Moe... Troo di loo la la! I can't sleep at night... Why doesn't he write me back?|Maggie|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|He was so kind to me in the Forsaken Fortress. That sweet boy who gave me all those expensive necklaces when I fled from that cursed island...|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> and gives him this letter to be deposited in the postbox.<ref>{{Cite|My father has been so strict with me lately. He won't let me out of the house! That's why I need you to take this letter -this note to Moe- and drop it into the postbox for me. Please, troo di loo la!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref> After doing this and waiting until the next day, Link returns to the [[Auction House]] and finds [[Maggie's Father]] arguing with [[Ilari]], one of the [[Rito]] [[Postman|postmen]].<ref>{{Cite|Silence! I'm telling you we don't allow postmen here! Begone! Begone!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't be ridiculous! I went through great trials and tribulations to go all the way to the Forsaken Fortress and return with Moe's letter...|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> Once the postman leaves, Link goes to the [[Cafe Bar|Coffee Shop]]; in there, he finds Ilari and is requested by him to give [[Moblin's Letter|Moe's Letter]] to Maggie (since Ilari himself couldn't).<ref>{{Cite|I came to deliver a letter addressed to Maggie from a Moblin named Moe, but as you saw, the girl's stubborn father won't permit me to give it to her.|Ilari|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I have an idea. That old fool may not permit postmen on his premises... But surely he would allow YOU in the place, would he not? Could you deliver this letter to Maggie in my stead?|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> Bringing this letter to Maggie will result in Link being rewarded with a [[Piece of Heart]]. 

Humorously, the message written in Moe's letter suggests that he likes Maggie so much that he wants to ''eat'' her, yet the girl misinterprets this as a marriage proposal.<ref>{{Cite|This is Moe. I... like... you... ...Ma...ggie... ...so...much...that... ...I...want...to... ...eat you...for dinner.|Moblin's Letter|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did you hear that? Those words? This means... I mean, it must mean... Can it be...?!? Is this his marriage proposal?|Maggie|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It must be! At last, my feelings have been conveyed to Moe! At last he understands how I feel!|Maggie|TWW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The front of the letter reads "Majū Tou" (Forsaken Fortress) and "Mō Sama He" (To Mr. Moe) in [[Hylian_Language|Modern Hylian]]. The {{wp|Japanese honorifics|Japanese suffix "-sama"}} indicates great respect and is closer to "lord" than Mr., confirming Maggie's affection for Moe.
*The back reads "Magī Yori" (From Maggie).
*It is unknown how Maggie's Letter was delivered to Moe or how Moe was able to respond, as no Moblins appear in the Forsaken Fortress after [[Valoo]] attacks [[Ganondorf]], yet it is only after this that Maggie's Letter can be delivered.
