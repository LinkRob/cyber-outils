The {{Term/Store|Sun Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 37}}</ref> also known as the '''Sun's Mask''',<ref>{{Cite|His {{Color|MM3D Red|storage}} for the things he's stolen is on the other side of this {{Color|MM3D Red|rock door}}. The Sun's Mask is surely in there.|Kafei|MM3D}}</ref> is an object in {{MM}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Sun Mask was created by [[Kafei]] for use as his ceremonial wedding mask. It is stolen by [[Sakon]],<ref>{{Cite|But on my way there, a man hanging around the gate--a prancing man in a white shirt--he stole my precious {{Color|MM3D Red|wedding ceremony mask}}!|Kafei|MM3D}}</ref> so [[Link]] and Kafei must infiltrate his [[Sakon's Hideout|Hideout]] to retrieve it. Combining the Sun Mask with the [[Moon Mask]] results in the [[Couple's Mask]].
