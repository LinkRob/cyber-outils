{{Term/Store|Blue Ore|Blue Ores|Series, OoS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117}}</ref> Along with {{Term|OoS|Red Ore|link}}, it is one of the components for making {{Term|OoS|Hard Ore|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoS|Blue Ore|link}} is in a chest in the southwestern corner of the {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Wilds|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} must use the {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove|link}} to cross the pitfalls over to the spot where the chest is.

When {{Term|OoS|Link}} has both the {{Term|OoS|Red Ore}} and {{Term|OoS|Blue Ore|link}}, he brings them to the {{Term|OoS|Great Furnace|link}} to have them smelted into {{Term|OoS|Hard Ore|link}}.
