==Biography==
Richard is the pet dog of a {{Term|Series|Mamamu Yan|link}}. At night when {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} is a child, {{Term|OoT3D|Richard (Ocarina of Time)}} will run away, causing {{Term|OoT3D|Mamamu Yan}} to beg {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to look for him.<ref>{{Cite|I can't find my puppy anywhere! I need you to look for it! Please!|Mamamu Yan|OoT3D}}</ref> If {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} succeeds in returning {{Term|OoT3D|Richard (Ocarina of Time)}} home, the woman will reward him with a {{Term|OoT3D|Piece of Heart|link}} the first time he accomplishes this and a {{Term|OoT3D|Blue Rupee|link}} all subsequent times.<ref name= "Richard 2"/>

At some point during {{Term|OoT3D|Link}}'s seven year absence, {{Term|OoT3D|Richard (Ocarina of Time)}} passed away. He is remembered fondly by her owner, who tells {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} that {{Term|OoT3D|Talon|link}} reminds her of {{Term|OoT3D|Richard (Ocarina of Time)}} before mentioning what a famous dog he used to be.<ref name= "Richard 1"/>
