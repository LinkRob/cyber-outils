The {{Term/Store|Masked Ship||Series, PH}} is a shop in {{PH}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|PH|Masked Ship}} is managed by {{Term|PH|Beedle|link}}'s alter ego, {{Term|PH|Beedle's Assistant|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 106}}</ref> It appears instead of {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship|link}} from 10am to noon on weekends and 10pm to midnight on weekdays, according to the [[Nintendo DS]] clock.

When {{Term|PH|Link}} first boards the {{Term|PH|Masked Ship}}, {{Term|PH|Beedle's Assistant}} pretends that have not already met on {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship}}. He claims to be holding a liquidation sale on account of the store's imminent closure in seven days.<ref>{{Cite|OHHHHHH! Welcome! I don't think we've met before! Nope! But I know all about you! I close up shop in {{Color|PH Red|seven days}}! Liquidation sale!|Beedle's Assistant|PH}}</ref> This turns out to be a marketing ploy, as the next day the shop reopens and hosts a "grand reopening sale" with prices no different from before.<ref>{{Cite|OHHHHHHH! Welcome! I've remodeled! Welcome to our grand reopening sale! Take a look around! ...I didn't lie! I closed, redid the interior, and the shop is reborn!|Beedle's Assistant|PH}}</ref>
