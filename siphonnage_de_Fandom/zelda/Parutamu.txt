{{Term/Store|Parutamu|Parutamu|Series, TAoL|plural}} are enemies in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref> They are a stronger variant of the [[Stalfos]], distinguished by the horned helmet they wear.

==Characteristics==
[[File:TAoL Parutamu Red Sprite.png|left]]
Like Stalfos, Parutamu are equipped with a sword they use for attacking with, and a shield to block [[Link]]'s attacks. In addition, their horned helmets also protects them against Link's [[Down Thrust]] attack as well. Although stronger than the Stalfos, their attack strategy remains the same, as they will only use the shield to protect their upper body and attack occasionally. As they never move their shields, their legs are vulnerable to attacks from the [[Sword]] while crouching. In some areas, like Stalfos, Parutamu will drop from above while performing a Down Thrust. Both red and blue Parutamu appear, with the blue being the stronger variety. They appear in the [[Island Palace]], [[Maze Island Palace]] and [[Three-Eye Rock Palace]].
