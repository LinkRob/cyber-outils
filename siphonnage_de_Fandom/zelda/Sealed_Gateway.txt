The {{Term/Store|Sealed Gateway||Series, TFH}} is a Level in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=160 }}</ref> It is the first Level of the [[Drablands#Fortress|Fortress]] area.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|TFH|Sealed Gateway}} is the entrance and the castle grounds of the Fortress Area, and is where the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} begin their infiltration of the base. The Level starts from ground Level, where the fortress walls are heavily guarded by numerous [[Soldier (Enemy)|Soldier]] enemies. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} eventually make their way inside and then to the fortress's balcony at the end, where the [[Bomb Storage]] Level takes place.

In '''Stage 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} begin at a clearing guarded by two [[Sword Soldier]]s, where the entrance is barricaded by an iron gate. Two [[Boomerang]]s and a [[Gust Jar]] can be found at the front of the gate. The gate will lower, and the Stage's Triforce Gateway will open, once the items are taken. Along the rest of the way, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} encounter [[Spear Soldier]]s. A giant, cracked block stands in the way of the fortress bridge, but can be blown up by using two [[Bomb Flower]]s that grow nearby. More Sword Soldiers and a Spear Soldier are encountered on the bridge. A [[Switch]] must be struck to lower the next gate, opening the way to the Triforce Gateway.

In '''Stage 2''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} enter the fortress's courtyard. The heroes walk into an open yard, but are immediately trapped inside as a [[Ball and Chain Soldier]] appears. Defeating it lowers the gates, leading to another yard where the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} then fight Sword Soldiers, Spear Soldiers and [[Bomb Soldier]]s. The Spear and Bomb Soldiers attack from ledges, requiring that the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to throw each other over to fight them. After they are all defeated, the gate to the Triforce Gateway lowers.

In '''Stage 3''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear on a platform standing over the fortress moat. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must cross this moat to the other side to get to the next Triforce Gateway. They come across two colored walls: one yellow and one purple. The yellow wall is raised and blocks the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' progress, but can be lowered by striking the yellow Switch nearby. This will turn the Switch purple, and will lower the yellow wall and raise the purple one. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must alternately rise and lower these two walls in order to cross by striking the Switch. One Link can strike the Switch twice to allow the other two to cross. For the third Link to cross, he can stand on the lowered yellow segment, then hit the switch to raise it. On the other side is a [[Squiddy]] and two Soldiers, plus another set of the yellow and purple walls. To cross these walls, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can form a [[Totem]] and throw each other over. A switch on the other side can be hit to lower the walls for the third Link. More Soldiers are encountered on this side, and Soldiers across the moat will also attack by throwing spears and bombs over. On this side is a [[Treasure Chest]] sitting on a ledge, which can be reached by lowering the surrounding yellow wall.

The purple wall to the left must be lowered, and on the other side the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} find another switch and a raised yellow wall leading to a ledge with two {{Plural|TFH|Jar|link}} and a pair of Chests. One Chest contains a {{Term|TFH|Purple Rupee|link}} while the other contains three [[Heart]]s. To cross the moat, two of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must stand on the lowered purple platform, while the other {{Term|TFH|Links}} strikes the Switch to raise it. On the other side is a lowered yellow platform, which one {{Term|TFH|Links}} must be propelled over using the Gust Jar. That Link can then carry the other one over using the Boomerang. Once both {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are over the gap, the switch can be struck again to raise the yellow platform and bring them closer. The attacking Spear Soldier and Bomb Soldier from before are encountered here. Two Switches must be pressed down to make stairways appear, allowing the third {{Term|TFH|Links}} to reunite with the others. The Triforce Gateway lies just ahead.

In '''Stage 4''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} enter inside the fortress. The heroes ride a moving platform up, where a couple of Bomb Soldiers attack from the ledges. A Chest nearby can be opened for a {{Term|TFH|Red Rupee|link}}. At the very top is a gap with two Switches on the other side, reachable by propelling two of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} over with the Gust Jar. These switches create a bridge for the third {{Term|TFH|Links}}. The heroes then reach a narrow walkway over a pit, blocked by two weights attached to ropes. These weights can be swung by using the Gust Jar on them from the right side, allowing one of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to cross over to a switch that opens a wall on the left side. The {{Term|TFH|Links}} can then use the Boomerang to carry the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} over from this opening. Once all three {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are reunited, two Ball and Chain Soldiers appear to fight them. Once they are defeated, the doors leading to the fortress's balcony and Triforce Gateway opens.
