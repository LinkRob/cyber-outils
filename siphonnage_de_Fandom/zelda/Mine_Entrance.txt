The {{Term/Store|Mine Entrance||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} is a location in {{HW}}.<ref name="Battle Log">{{Cite|The {{Color|HWDE Green|Mine Entrance}} is open!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance}} is found in the northwest corner of {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Field (Stage)|link}}. In "{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance}} must be opened in order to retrieve the {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb|link}} from the {{Term|HWDE|Mine|link}}, which are needed to destroy the {{Plural|HWDE|Boulder|link}} blocking the way to {{Term|HWDE|Zelda|link}} and the rest of {{Term|HWDE|Hyrule Field (Stage)}}.<ref>{{Cite|Destroy the boulders and rendezvous with {{Color|HWDE Blue|Zelda}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> Around the {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance}} is a {{Term|HWDE|Goron Captain|link}} and a {{Term|HWDE|Lizalfos|link}} fighting with each other, with the {{Term|HWDE|Goron Captain}} in trouble.<ref>{{Cite|We can't hold them anymore! Somebody, help!|Goron Captain|HWDE}}</ref> Helping the {{Term|HWDE|Goron Captain}} by defeating the {{Term|HWDE|Lizalfos}} will open the {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance}} allowing access to the {{Term|HWDE|Mine}}, in turn allowing the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} to retrieve the {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb}}.<ref name="Battle Log"/><ref>{{Cite|Thanks a ton! Hey, you should take the treasure in that mine as a reward!|Goron Captain|HWDE}}</ref> In {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance}} can be found closed off, unable to be opened.
