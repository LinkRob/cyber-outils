The concept goes unnamed in several titles,{{Exp Game|TLoZ, TAoL, MM3D, FSA, PH, ST, SS, ALBW, BotW}} in some of which {{Plural|Series|Key|link}} are the only {{Plural|Series|Dungeon Item}}.{{Exp Game|FSA, PH, ST, BotW}}

==Overview==
{{Stub}}
{{Plural|Series|Dungeon Item}} obtained in a given dungeon are displayed only when inside that dungeon, often next to the open {{Term|Series|Dungeon Map|link}} on the {{Term|Series|Map|link}} screen. The most common {{Plural|Series|Dungeon Item}} are {{Plural|Series|Small Key|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Boss Key|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Dungeon Map|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Compass|link}}, in that order.

== Location and Uses ==

=== {{MM|-}} ===
In [[Stone Tower Temple]], the [[Giant's Mask]] can only be used during the fight with [[Twinmold]] and has no effect outside of the dungeon.{{Fact}}
