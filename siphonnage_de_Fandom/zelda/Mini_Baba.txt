==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= That's a {{Color|MM White|Mini Baba}}. It won't attack unless you get too close to it, so watch your step!
}}

{{Plural|MM3D|Mini Baba}} are short plants which stick out of the ground. They are the miniature versions of {{Plural|MM3D|Deku Baba|link}}. They will not attack unless {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} comes within proximity of them. A simple {{Term|MM3D|Sword|link}} strike is sufficient to defeat them, and they usually yield {{Plural|MM3D|Deku Nut|link}} when defeated.
