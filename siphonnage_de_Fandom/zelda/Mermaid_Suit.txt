The {{Term/Store|Mermaid Suit||Series, OoA}} is an item  in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131}}</ref> It is found in [[Mermaid's Cave]].

==Location and Uses==
The Mermaid Suit is an upgrade for the [[Zora's Flippers]]. The Mermaid Suit permits [[Link]] to swim in deep water without drowning. It also allows him to swim faster and against currents by tapping the direction Link wants to go.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|OoA Red|Mermaid Suit}}! Now you can swim in deep waters. Press {{Icon|OoA Control Pad}} to swim, {{Icon|OoA B}} to dive and {{Icon|OoA A}} to use items.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> Furthermore, he can use it to dive under the water to explore the seabed, flooded passages, and submarine grottos. This ability is essential in the navigation of the underwater [[Zora Village]], as well as the next dungeon located there, [[Jabu-Jabu's Belly]].

Underwater, Link can still use the [[Switch Hook]], the [[Seed Shooter]], and his [[Sword]].

==Trivia==
* Many Zoras in Zora Village, including [[King Zora]] believe Link is a mermaid because of the suit.<ref>{{Cite|We don't get many mermaids visiting us! This is Zora Village!|Zora|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Do you need me, great mermaid?|King Zora|OoA}}</ref>
