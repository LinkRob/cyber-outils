'''''The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds — Official Digital Strategy Guide for 3DS''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{ALBW}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The guide has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Prima Official Game Guide]]," containing a complete walkthrough of {{ALBW}}, a listing of [[Characters in A Link Between Worlds|characters]], {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}}, and {{Plural|ALBW|Boss|link}}, maps, and a checklist of collectibles.

It is served using Prima's in-browser digital guide service and has a single sample chapter on quest {{Plural|ALBW|Item|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Prima Games|url=https://eguides.primagames.com/games/legend-zelda-link-between-worlds/guides/the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-between-worlds-eguide|site=eguides.primagames.com|title=The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Official Digital Strategy Guide for 3DS|retrieved=October 1, 2022}}</ref> Prior to March 1, 2020, the guide was available for 20 gold points as a [[My Nintendo Rewards|My Nintendo Reward]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= October 1, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/rewards/50748a14bcd46d58|title= Online Access - The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Prima Official Game eGuide|site= my.nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>
