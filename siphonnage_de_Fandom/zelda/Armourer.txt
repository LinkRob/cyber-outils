The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Armourer||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Armourer|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|FPTRR|Armourer}} lives in a house in the eastern half of {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town|link}}, where he constantly polishes a sword. Through {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}}'s adventure, he can be bribed into sharing information and weapons can be sold to him for relatively high prices. 

It is later revealed that the {{Term|FPTRR|Armourer}} is {{Term|FPTRR|Aba}}'s father, instead of {{Term|FPTRR|Junglo|link}} as {{Term|FPTRR|Aba}} had believed.
