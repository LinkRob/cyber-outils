{{Term/Store|Mayor Plen||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|OoA|Mayor Plen}} is the mayor of {{Term|OoA|Lynna City|link}}. He lives in {{Term|OoA|Mayor Plen's House|link}}. If spoken to, he gives {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} some information about the history of the City.<ref>{{Cite|I am Plen, the mayor here in Lynna City. Lynna has a long history reaching back hundreds of years. Back then, it was Lynna Village--not a city.|Mayor Plen|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am Plen, the mayor here in Lynna City. Long ago, Queen Ambi, who ruled these lands, suddenly changed into a cruel and heartless person. Our records say it was because a girl who served her was taken away by something. I wonder... What really happened?|Mayor Plen|OoA}}</ref>

In a {{Term|OoS|Linked Game|link}} of {{OoS}}, a golden {{Term|OoS|Subrosian|link}} will appear in {{Term|OoS|Lava Lake|link}}. He will tell {{Term|OoS|Link}} a secret meant for {{Term|OoA|Mayor Plen}} back in {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}}.<ref name= "Secret"/> When {{Term|OoA|Link}} tells the secret to the Mayor, he will give {{Term|OoA|Link}} a {{Term|OoA|Magic Ring|link}}, which, once appraised by {{Term|OoA|Vasu|link}}, will become the {{Term|OoA|Spin Ring|link}}. In a {{Term|OoA|Linked Game}} of {{OoA}}, {{Term|OoA|Mayor Plen}}'s grandmother will appear in his home. She has a [[Linked Game/Secrets#Ruul Secret|Secret]] for {{Term|OoA|Link}} to tell to {{Term|OoS|Mayor Ruul|link}} in {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you remember Mayor Ruul back in Holodrum's Horon Village? I want you to tell this secret to Mayor Ruul, understand?|Human|OoA}}</ref>
