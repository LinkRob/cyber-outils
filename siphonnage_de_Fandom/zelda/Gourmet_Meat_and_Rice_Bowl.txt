==Location and Uses==
Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowls are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Raw Gourmet Meat]], [[Hylian Rice]], and [[Rock Salt]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. They can be eaten to restore several {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The meats used in this dish are carefully selected to ensure their quality.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Additional ingredients can be used when making Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowls, creating several different versions of the meal. These versions are:

* '''Hearty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they'll replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. They both refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link a boost to his movement speed for a short time.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. They grant Link low-level [[Cold Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If they're eaten, they grant link a low-level [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Eating this meal will temporarily boost the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They increase Link's defensive abilities if they're eaten.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When eaten, they increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} temporarily.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Gourmet Meat and Rice Bowl}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Only the most carefully selected cuts of high-quality meats go into this dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding cold ingredients. Eating them will grant Link a low-level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
