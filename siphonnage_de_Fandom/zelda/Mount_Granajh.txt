{{Term/Store|Mount Granajh||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mount Granajh'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mount Granajh is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. There is a small, ruined shack on the mountain where Link can read an authorless journal titled the ''[[Mountain Peak Log]]''. It describes someone's attempts at discovering an [[Ancient Shrine]] nearby. Reading it will give Link the "[[Secret of the Snowy Peaks]]" [[Shrine Quest]]. After completing the Shrine Quest, the [[Suma Sahma Shrine]] will reveal itself on the mountain.
