==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Moblin Horn}} are dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stalmoblin|link}} upon defeat. They are useful as ingredients in creating various {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Gray [[Dye]] as well as sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} in exchange for {{Plural|BotW|Mon|link}}.

The [[Fireproof Armor]] set requires Moblin Horns, in addition to a [[Fireproof Lizard]], to upgrade each piece to its first tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Fireproof Armor''': 6 (2, 0, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
