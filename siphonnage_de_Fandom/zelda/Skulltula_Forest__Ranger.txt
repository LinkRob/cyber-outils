{{Term/Store|Skulltula Forest: Ranger||Series, LCT}},<ref>{{Cite|Skulltula Forest: Ranger|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> also known as '''Level 4-3''',<ref>{{Cite|Level 4-3|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> is a {{Term|LCT|Stage}} in {{LCT}}. It is the third Stage of {{Term|LCT|Level 4|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
In {{Term|LCT|Skulltula Forest: Ranger}}, {{Term|LCT|Link|link}} has 90 Seconds to explore the woods and defeat all 18 {{Plural|LCT|Skulltula|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Move using the Control Stick, and defeat the 18 Skulltulas.|Stage Select|LCT}}</ref> {{Term|LCT|Link}} can locate the {{Plural|LCT|Skulltula}} by using his Radar, as the {{Plural|LCT|Skulltula}} appear as red dots on it. 13 of the {{Plural|LCT|Skulltula}} walk on the ground, while 2 climb the walls of the woods and the last 3 hang down from {{Plural|LCT|Tree|link}} on strings. Each {{Term|LCT|Skulltula}} must be shot twice with the {{Term|LCT|Crossbow|link}}, or once with a {{Term|LCT|Bomb Arrow|link}}, to be killed. Shooting a {{Term|LCT|Skulltula}} with a {{Term|LCT|Bomb Arrow}} will also kill any {{Plural|LCT|Skulltula}} around the originally shot {{Term|LCT|Skulltula}}. Upon defeating all eighteen {{Plural|LCT|Skulltula}}, {{Term|LCT|Link}} will gain 500 points times the number of seconds he has left to complete the {{Term|LCT|Stage}}.

Other {{Plural|LCT|Target|link}} that give {{Term|LCT|Link}} points when shot can be found throughout {{Term|LCT|Skulltula Forest: Ranger}}. To the right of the starting area, three {{Plural|LCT|Wooden Box|link}} sit on a wooden balcony. Continuing forward from the {{Plural|LCT|Wooden Box}} and along the wall, {{Term|LCT|Link}} will come across two {{Plural|LCT|Deku Baba|link}} near a small wooden platform. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} continues following the wall, the ground will slope upwards and {{Term|LCT|Link}} will find four {{Plural|LCT|Deku Baba}} at the top. Three of the {{Plural|LCT|Deku Baba}} arise from the ground, while one hangs down from a nearby {{Term|LCT|Tree}}. A small wooden balcony overlooking the bottom of the slope holds eight {{Plural|LCT|Jar|link}} and four {{Plural|LCT|Bomb Barrel|link}}. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} shoots any of the {{Plural|LCT|Bomb Barrel}}, they will blow up and destroy any unbroken {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} and other {{Plural|LCT|Bomb Barrel}} on the balcony. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} continues forwards directly from the starting area, he will find a small hollow in a large {{Term|LCT|Tree}} filled with ten {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} suspended in the air by strings. {{Term|LCT|Link}} can shoot the {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} directly or he can shoot the strings the {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} are attached to, causing the {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} to fall to the ground and break. The {{Term|LCT|Jar}} sitting in the middle of the hollow not attached to any strings will also make an {{Term|LCT|Orange Rupee|link}} appear when shot. Continuing forward from the hollow and slightly left, a {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow|link}} stands up high on top of a large {{Term|LCT|Tree}} branch. Straight ahead of the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}, a small {{Term|LCT|Skeleton|link}} rests on the ground next to a large {{Term|LCT|Tree}}. These {{Plural|LCT|Skeleton}} can be found behind the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}}, and {{Term|LCT|Link}} can find another {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} by following the wall left of the starting area. This {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} against the wall will give {{Term|LCT|Link}} the same points as the other {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}}, but an {{Term|LCT|Orange Rupee}} will appear when this {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} is destroyed. If {{Term|LCT|Link}} walks past the {{Term|LCT|Scarecrow}} and to the very far wall, then travels right along the wall, he will run into three {{Plural|LCT|Deku Baba}}. Two of the {{Plural|LCT|Deku Baba}} rest on the forest floor, while one {{Term|LCT|Deku Baba}} hangs down from an overlooking {{Term|LCT|Tree}} branch. The last optional {{Term|LCT|Target}} {{Term|LCT|Link}} can shoot for points are the small {{Plural|LCT|Skull|link}} that are spread throughout the woods. Some {{Plural|LCT|Skull}} can be found near the beforementioned bone piles and some rest near {{Plural|LCT|Tree}}. One particular {{Term|LCT|Skull}} sits with a group of three other {{Plural|LCT|Skull}} a short distance past the {{Term|LCT|Skeleton}} near the starting area wall. This {{Term|LCT|Skull}}, sitting in the middle of the group, will cause an {{Term|LCT|Orange Rupee}} to appear when destroyed.
