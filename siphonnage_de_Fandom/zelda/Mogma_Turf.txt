The '''Mogma Turf''' is a location in {{SS}}.{{Name Ref|SS, SSHD}}

==Skyward Sword==
In Skyward Sword, the [[Mogma]]s live here. [[Bokoblin]]s moved in recently and the Mogmas hate them. Because of this, the Mogmas mistake Link for a [[Green Bokoblin]], and call him a "Green Monster". The Mogmas block ways that Bokoblins can come in through. Many locations of the volcano are considered parts of the Mogma turf, [[Fire Sanctuary (Dungeon)|Fire Sanctuary]], [[Earth Temple]], and Eldin Volcano.
