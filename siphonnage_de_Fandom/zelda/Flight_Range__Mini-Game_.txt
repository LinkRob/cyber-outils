The {{Term/Store|Flight Range||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 332}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
During the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}} in the hut at the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}}, where he can offer his assistance.<ref>{{Cite|[I can help you.] Help me? With what exactly?|Teba|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Teba}} is initially skeptical that he can help take down {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}},<ref>{{Cite|[Vah Medoh...] {{Big|Let me get this straight...}} Some random Hylian wants to help me bring down Divine Beast Vah Medoh? I'm not buying it.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> he will eventually relent once he sees {{Term|BotW|Link}} is serious.<ref>{{Cite|[Then let's get started.] Are you serious? HA! Well, far be it from me to talk you off the ledge.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Before they leave, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} insists on testing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to see if he is prepared to face the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...So I'm going to need to test you before I take you with me.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Teba}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} warriors use the {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}} to practice their aerial archery.<ref>{{Cite|We Rito warriors use the {{Color|BotW Red|updraft}} in this ravine to hone our aerial {{Color|BotW Red|archery}} skills.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Since they are so strong, though, someone who is flightless could use a cloth such as the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to float up.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|updrafts}} are so strong here that a person could just open up a cloth over their head and float up easily.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he tasks {{Term|BotW|Link}} with destroying five of the {{Plural|BotW|Target|link}} within three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Now let's see what you're capable of. You'll have to maneuver through the air and destroy {{Color|BotW Red|five of the targets}} set up in this valley {{Color|BotW Red|in three minutes}}.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks for advice, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} tells him that using a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} in the air is similar to using one on the ground.<ref>{{Cite|[Any pointers?] Using a bow in midair is just like using a bow on the ground.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> He suggests using a {{Term|BotW|Bow}} that is made for shooting from a long range.<ref>{{Cite|But you'll probably want a bow suited for long-range engagement.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is unable to complete the challenge, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} says he will be useless against {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|Use your arrows to destroy {{Color|BotW Red|five targets in three minutes}}. If you can't do that, you don't stand a chance against {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells {{Term|BotW|Teba}} he is ready, the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} will begin.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm ready.] Then go destroy {{Color|BotW Red|five targets}} with your arrows.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> There are a total of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Target}}, all of which are painted a glowing blue that make them easy to spot from a distance. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has shot five {{Plural|BotW|Target}}, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} will call him back to the hut.<ref>{{Cite|OK! Come on back!|Teba|BotW}}</ref> There, he calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} a skilled archer, saying it was as though time stopped every time he shot an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're...a skilled archer. It was as if time stopped with every arrow you let fly.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Now that he has seen {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s abilities, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} allows him to help take down {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you really want to help me bring down {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}, it's now or never.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

After "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)}}" has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} decides to take {{Term|BotW|Tulin|link}} with him to the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}}.<ref>{{Cite|My wounds are just about healed, so I guess it's time to take Tulin to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Flight Range}}.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> He hopes that training there will help {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} grow into a warrior like {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm hoping Tulin grows up to be a distinguished warrior like Master Revali.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> Before they leave, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come play with them at the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, guy!<br/>Come play with us at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Flight Range}} sometime!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> At the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}}, {{Term|BotW|Teba}} will once again mention that he is training {{Term|BotW|Tulin}}.,<ref>{{Cite|It's you... Haven't seen you in a while. Now that {{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}'s calmed down, I've started training Tulin again.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to show {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} how he handles a {{Term|BotW|Bow}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you have the time, would you mind showing Tulin how you handle a bow?|Teba|BotW}}</ref> noting that it could set example for him.<ref>{{Cite|Show Tulin how you handle a bow. It could set a good example for him.|Teba|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Tulin}}, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if his father was telling the truth when he said {{Term|BotW|Link}} was good with a {{Term|BotW|Bow}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Hey, Link! [Hey, Tulin!] You're Link, right? My dad told me about you! Also, he said you're REAL good with a bow! Is that true?|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> Whether {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms or downplays his skill, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} requests he show off.<ref>{{Cite|[Of course!] Wow! That's so neat! You gotta show me!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I'm all right.] Nuh-uh! Dad said you were great, and Dad never lies, so it must be true! Come on, show me!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} explains that he will count down while {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to break as many {{Plural|BotW|Target}} as he can.<ref>{{Cite|I'll count down, and you'll break all the {{Color|BotW Red|targets}} before time's up!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> If he does so, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} promises him a prize.<ref>{{Cite|When you break a bunch, I'll give you a prize!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} falls too far down into the chasm or starts to leave the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}}, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} will tell him to come back quickly.<ref>{{Cite|Uh-oh, you went too far! Come back real quick!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> Similarly, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} during the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}, he will ask if he is quitting.<ref>{{Cite|What's wrong? Are you quitting?!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he is, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} will be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] Is that really all you got?|Tulin|BotW}}</ref>

When time runs out, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} will call {{Term|BotW|Link}} back to the hut.<ref>{{Cite|Time's up! Come back!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} will be excited if {{Term|BotW|Link}} destroys all 20 {{Plural|BotW|Target}} before running out of time.<ref>{{Cite|Oooooh! Look at that!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> He will then review {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s performance.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} breaks less than five {{Plural|BotW|Target}}, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} is unimpressed and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to show him something cooler next time.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Um...what was such a big deal about that? Next time, make sure you show me something a lot cooler, OK?|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> Next, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} breaks between five and nine {{Plural|BotW|Target}}, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} notes that while {{Term|BotW|Link}} had some trouble, he still learned a lot from his performance, and he gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|You had some trouble, but that's OK. I have trouble too sometimes. I still learned a lot watching you! Oh, {{Color|BotW Blue|Dad}} told me to give you {{Color|BotW Blue|this}} to thank you. Come back and show me more anytime!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} breaks between 10 and 15 {{Plural|BotW|Target}}, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} will be impressed and give him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That was nifty, Link! Oh, {{Color|BotW Blue|Dad}} told me to give you {{Color|BotW Blue|this}} to thank you. Come back and try again anytime!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} manages to break between 15 and 19 {{Plural|BotW|Target}}, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} will be excited by this feat, rewarding {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} and promising to remember his technique.<ref>{{Cite|Yaaay! I knew you could do it, Link! I'll remember the way you shoot when I practice! Oh, {{Color|BotW Blue|Dad}} told me to give you {{Color|BotW Blue|this}} to thank you. Come back to show me your neat tricks again anytime!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} breaks all 20 {{Plural|BotW|Target}}, {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} will be amazed, and he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}} as a prize.<ref>{{Cite|You're amazing, Link! When you fired your bow, it was like time stopped! Oh, {{Color|BotW Blue|Dad}} told me to give you {{Color|BotW Blue|this}} to thank you.|Tulin|BotW}}</ref>
