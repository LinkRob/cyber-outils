'''Shiho Fujii''' is a composer at [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
Fujii was born in 1984<ref>[https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/wii/mario25th/3/0 Iwata Asks: Super Mario 25th Anniversary (EN)]</ref><ref>[https://www.nintendo.co.jp/n10/interview/mario25th/vol4/index.html Iwata Asks: Super Mario 25th Anniversary (JP)]</ref>. After graduating from the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senzoku_Gakuen#College_of_Music Senzoku Gakuen College of Music]<ref>[https://www.senzoku.ac.jp/music/active_graduate/9168 Senzoku Gakuen graduate interview (in Japanese)]</ref>, she joined Nintendo in 2007<sup><ref name=":2">[https://www.nintendo.co.jp/jobs/interview/2013/fuji.html 2013 Nintendo recruit interview (in Japanese)]</ref></sup><ref>[https://web.archive.org/web/20190511111741/https://www.nintendo.co.jp/jobs/keyword/20.html 2016 Nintendo recruit interview (in Japanese; archived)]</ref>. She has composed for many famous games, including ''Animal Crossing: City Folk'', ''New Super Mario Bros. U'', and ''Splatoon''.
