"{{Term/Store|Lorule Castle||Series, ALBW}}" is a recurring {{Term|Series|Song|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Track|author= n.a. |track= Lorule Castle |discography= The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds Original Soundtrack |publisher= Nintendo |year= 2015 |format= CD }}</ref> "{{Term|Series|Lorule Castle (Theme)}}" goes unnamed in {{TFH}}.

==Overview==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
"{{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle (Theme)}}" plays inside the eponymous location, {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle|link}}. The song has five variations which are heard as the castle's chambers are completed.

"{{Term|ALBW|Lorule Castle (Theme)}}" is in the key of A minor and begins with a staccato violin rhythm, followed by a woodwind melody which borrows notes from dorian and harmonic minor scales, producing some major chords where they would otherwise be minor chords. The second section contains a portion of "{{Term|ALttP|Hyrule Castle (Theme)}}" from {{ALttP}} played in reverse,<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Adam Koopmans |published= February 25, 2014 |retrieved= November 2, 2015 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQLhoZ7kMQk |title= Hyrule/Lorule Castle Themes Played Back to Back, Reversed, and Together |site= YouTube |type= Video}}</ref> and the third section reprises the main melody with additional arrangement.

Once {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} has completed one of the castle's four main chambers, the song gains several string and wind instruments, playing the main melody one octave lower with stronger harmonies and counter-melodies. After completing two chambers, the song gains snare percussion.

When {{Term|ALBW|Link}} completes the third chamber, the song's structure changes by adding many instrumental flourishes and a repetition of the main melody in the key of C minor, followed by a new second section that strongly features a portion of "{{Term|ALBW|Enter Ganon|link}}", foreshadowing the {{Term|ALBW|Boss|link}} battle against {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon|link}}. When all four chambers are completed, a vocal chorus will be heard throughout the song.

==={{TFH|-}}===
"{{Term|TFH|Lorule Castle (Theme)}}" is one of the 23 playable {{Plural|TFH|Song|link}} from the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Lobby Ball|link}}. This version is based on the first variation from {{ALBW|-}}.
