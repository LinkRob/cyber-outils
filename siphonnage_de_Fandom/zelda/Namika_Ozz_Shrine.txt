The {{Term/Store|Namika Ozz Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Namika Ozz Shrine}}<br/>A Modest Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Modest Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Namika Ozz Shrine}} is located in a large hollowed {{Term|BotW|Tree Stump|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Crenel Hills|link}}, which found in the eastern part of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by the its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Namika Ozz|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Namika Ozz. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Namika Ozz|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Namika Ozz Shrine}} features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}}, wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear+|link}}, will rise from a pit to challenge him. In order to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}}.

Once the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} has been defeated, the portcullis in the back of the chamber will open, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to access the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Namika Ozz}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Namika Ozz|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Namika Ozz}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Namika Ozz|BotW}}</ref>

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Frostspear|link}}.
