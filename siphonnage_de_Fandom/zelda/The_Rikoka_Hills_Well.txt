The Rikoka Hills Well <ref>Rikoka Hills Well - [[Map]] ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of Kingdom)]]''</ref>, also known as Moza's Hideaway Restaurant, is a reoccurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

It is also one of the 58 [[Well|wells]] needed for the [[Where Are The Wells?|"Where are the Wells?"]] [[Side Adventure]]. 

==Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The well found next to a [[Ruined House|ruined house]] on the eastern of Rikoka Hills.

==={{TotK|-}}===
The well has an underground chamber now, and became a restaurant owned by '''[[Moza]]'''.

By jumping into the well, [[Link]] will startle Moza <ref><q>''"Huh? Are you...a customer?! Welcome to Moza's Hideaway Restaurant, home of the ultimate dish! Truth is, we haven't officially opened. You must have a good nose! I mean, you just couldn't stay away from these tempting aromas, right?" -'' [[Moza]] ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of the Kingdom)]]''</q></ref><ref>"''Welcome to Moza's Hideaway Restaurant, home of the ultimate dish! Truth is, we haven't officially opened. You must have a good nose! I mean, you just couldn't stay away from these tempting aromas, right?''" - Moza ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of the Kingdom)]]''</ref>. By interacting with her, you will start the [[Side Quest]] '''[[The Ultimate Dish?|"The Ultimate Dish?"]]''' 

After completing the quest, Link can come back<ref>''"Ah! A customer! Can't get enough of the ultimate dish? I have a special announcement for customers like you. Moza's Hideaway Restaurant is opening its doors to the public! Bring me your failed dishes anytime, and I'll make them ultimate! Please visit Moza's Hideaway Restaurant whenever you need that ultimate flavor!" -'' Moza ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of the Kingdom)]]''</ref> to Moza whenever a [[Dubious Food|Failed Dish]] and/or [[Rock-Hard Food|Rock hard food]] is created through [[Cooking]]<ref>''"So, are you ready to turn your failed food into the ultimate dish? If you give me failed dishes, I can work my magic." -'' Moza ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of the Kingdom)]]''</ref> by paying 10 [[Rupee|Rupees]], she will then turning it<ref>''"Chop it! Pound it! Steam it! Throw in a Hyrule bass! Boil it! Cook it! Add that raw meat! <small>Now just a pinch of the secret extract, aaand...</small> Done! Prepare to be amazed!"'' - Moza ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|(Tears of the Kingdom)]]''</ref> into [[Monster Stew]].
