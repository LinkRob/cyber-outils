The {{Term/Store|Radiant Set||Series, BotW}} is a recurring [[Armor Set]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 180}}</ref>{{Fact}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} set consisting of the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Mask|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shirt|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Radiant Tights|link}}. All three pieces can be bought at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} for 2400 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and 9 {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}} in total after "{{Term|BotW|The Secret Club's Secret|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} has been completed. Painted using {{Term|BotW|Dye}} made from crushed {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}}, the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set}}'s skeletal markings glow when worn at night.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Radiant Mask}}<br/>A special mask for members of the Gerudo Secret Club. Painted with dye made from crushed luminous stones, it gives off a lurid, mysterious glow in the dark.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Radiant Shirt}}<br/>This shirt is sold at the Gerudo Secret Club. It glows with a strange pattern in the dark due to a dye made from crushed luminous stones.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Radiant Tights}}<br/>These tights are sold in the Gerudo Secret Club. They're painted with dye from crushed luminous stones, which gives them an eerie glow in the dark.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set}} can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and can be {{Term|BotW|Dye|link|display= Dyed}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.

If all three pieces of the set have been upgraded twice at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, wearing the set grants a "Disguise" {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus|link}} against {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stalmoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Stalizalfos|link}}. The upgraded set also provides a "Bone Atk. Up" bonus, increasing attack power when wielding {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} made of bone.
