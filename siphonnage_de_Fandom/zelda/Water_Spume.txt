{{Term/Store|Water Spume|Water Spumes|Series, SS|plural}} are enemies in {{SS}}.<ref name="E 212">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 212}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Water Spumes are a water-based variety found swimming in the [[Lanayru Sand Sea]] in the past.<ref name="E 212"/> Water Spumes attempt to capsize [[Skipper]]'s boat by shooting balls of water. They can be defeated with a single cannon shot.
