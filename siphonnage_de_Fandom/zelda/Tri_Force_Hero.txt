The {{Term/Store|Tri Force Hero|Tri Force Heroes|Series, TFH|plural}},<ref name="Ages">{{Cite|In the kingdom of Hytopia, the legend of the {{Color|TFH Blue|Tri Force Heroes}} has been passed down through the ages.|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> also known as the '''Totem Heroes''',<ref>{{Cite|O ye who bear the attributes of the legendary Totem Heroes!|Sign|TFH}}</ref> are characters mentioned in {{TFH}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, possessing the attributes of the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}}, fulfill the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero|display=Heroes}}' role during the course of their adventure.

==Biography==
The legend of the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}} has been passed down through generations in the kingdom of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}},<ref name="Ages"/> and tells of three heroes from parts unknown who band together to defend the kingdom from peril. These heroes are said to triumph over hardships with their {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} maneuvers and ability to [[Communication Icon|communicate without words]], owing to their unbreakable bond,<ref>{{Cite|The bond between the three is such that they can communicate thoughts and emotions without a word. They will totem. They will triumph. They will overcome all hardships.|Book|TFH}}</ref> and are recognized for possessing the {{Term|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage|link}}:
[[File:TFH Heroic Attributes Promotional Artwork.jpg|150px|right|thumb|Promotional image showing the {{Term|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage}}]]
#Pointed ears.<ref>{{Cite|Pointy ears that reach skyward, ever alert for the call of friends in need...|Book|TFH}}</ref>
#Thick sideburns.<ref>{{Cite|Attractive yet ethereal sideburns that inspire allies to greatness...|Book|TFH}}</ref>
#Side-parted hair.<ref>{{Cite|And perfectly parted hair that smells of lavender and friendship.|Book|TFH}}</ref>
The citizens of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} believe in the legend and have placed the {{Term|TFH|Tri Force Hero Statue|link}} at the center of town, in honor of {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}} past and future.

When {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}} is cursed by the [[Lady Maud|Drablands Witch]] and all of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}}'s stylishness is threatened, {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}} issues a call for heroes to save the kingdom from its despair.<ref>{{Cite|The king, too, was in agony. As only chosen heroes may enter the witch's domain, the despicable Drablands, King Tuft issued a call to kingdoms near and far for a hero to save the day.|Book|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}, while passing through {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} on his travels, is informed of the situation by {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's Apprentice|link}}, who believes {{Term|TFH|Link}} may be one of the fated heroes. {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}} is immediately impressed by {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s heroic traits and enlists the young hero into the {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I must say...I've seen many would-be heroes in my time. Too many, in fact. But none of them boasted the perfect trifecta of heroic features as you do. We're happy to have you on our side!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> though he cannot remember the full reason for recognizing these traits.<ref>{{Cite|Ears pointed skyward to hear the call to arms! Epic sideburns to inspire allies! And side-parted hair to... Erm, keep enemies on their toes! Or was it something about lavender... I can't recall that bit at the moment.|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> Alongside [[Links|other heroes]] brought forth by the {{Term|TFH|Match Master|link}}, or through the use of {{Plural|TFH|Doppel|link}} to divide his soul among three vessels, {{Term|TFH|Link}} ventures into the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}} to fulfill the roles of the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}}.

According to {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}}, the legend of the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero}} also dictates that the heroes will bring everlasting peace and style to {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} by overcoming all challenges in front of them.<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of {{Color|TFH Blue|Tri Force Heroes}}, there's more to the {{Color|TFH Blue|legend}} than most people know. The story goes... "When the {{Color|TFH Blue|Tri Force Heroes}} overcome all challenges, Hytopia will be blessed with everlasting peace and style."|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> Believing {{Term|TFH|Link}} to be hero of legend, the king implores him to fulfill his destiny by besting the {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Naturally, I believe YOU to be the {{Color|TFH Blue|hero}} of legend, destined to lead our fair kingdom to eternal greatness! As such, I must earnestly encourage you to best all {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands}} challenges and become a true {{Color|TFH Blue|Tri Force Hero}}!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref>
