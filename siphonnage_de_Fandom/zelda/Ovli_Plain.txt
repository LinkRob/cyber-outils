{{Term/Store|Ovli Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ovli Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Ovli Plain}} is located northwest of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} in [[East Necluda]].

A {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus (Luminous)|link}} can be found embedded in the ground north of the {{Term|BotW|Ginner Woods|link}}.
