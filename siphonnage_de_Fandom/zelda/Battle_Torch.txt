==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Battle Torch}} are a type of {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}} that can be used to fight enemies, dealing 1 damage to any enemy that hits {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Like any other {{Term|CoH|Torch}}, they also aid in navigation by lighting up dark areas and clearing {{Plural|CoH|Tree Stump|link}} out of the hero's path. However, using the {{Term|CoH|Torch}} will cause it to eventually burn out unless the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation|link}} is equipped, though it is impossible to deal damage to an enemy without breaking the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation}} due to its fragile nature.

They can be found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} for 30 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.
