The {{Term/Store|Customer||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
The Customer is found at the counter of [[Kakariko Village]]'s [[Milk Bar]]. If talked to, he will snap at [[Link]] for bothering him, but then apologizes immediately afterward, stating that he did not mean to be rude.<ref>{{Cite|Whaddya want? Can't a guy just drink his milk in peace? Ah, sorry. Don't mean to be a grump.|Customer|ALBW}}</ref> He goes on to explain that he was simply thinking about using a magnifying glass for reading maps.<ref>{{Cite|I'm just thinking about magnifying glasses-how I'd read maps with 'em.|Customer|ALBW}}</ref> However, he also says that his eyesight is now so bad, not even a magnifying glass can aid him.<ref>{{Cite|But my eyesight's going, and now not even a magnifying glass helps!|Customer|ALBW}}</ref> If talked to again, he will give the young hero a tip about using the zoom-in and zoom-out buttons on the map.<ref>{{Cite|Ever tried looking at a map with a magnifying glass? It's sort of dizzy fun, going all (+)(-)(+)(-) with 'em.|Customer|ALBW}}</ref>
