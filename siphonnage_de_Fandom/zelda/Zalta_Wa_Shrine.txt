The {{Term/Store|Zalta Wa Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Zalta Wa Shrine}}<br/>Two Orbs to Guide You}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Two Orbs to Guide You''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Zalta Wa Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Breach of Demise|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. It is southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Ridgeland Tower|link}} and on the north side of the path. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters, he is greeted by {{Term|BotW|Zalta Wa|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} who designed the eponymous {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Zalta Wa. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Zalta Wa|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Zalta Wa Shrine}} has one large chamber with a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}} in the center of the room that controls the {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} embedded in the ground beside it. On the left side of the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} is a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}}, which will open the small room on the left side with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} inside of it. However, stepping off of the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} will close the door. On the right side is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} balanced on a pillar next to another {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}.

By shooting the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can knock it into the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Doing so will open the door in the back of the chamber, which reveals another {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}. While standing on the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can throw this {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into the room with the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Doing this causes the structure above the room in the back to move in and out.

While standing on the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can hit the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} to launch himself into the structure. Following the path up from here, he will eventually come upon the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Zalta Wa}}.

Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Zalta Wa}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial, and they present him with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Zalta Wa|BotW}}</ref> They will then wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Zalta Wa|BotW}}</ref>

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow|link}} can be found on top of the enclosed room with the pedestal. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can reach this {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} by first activating the moving structure above the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}, then by flying down with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} from the platform above.

==Nomenclature==
The Shrine's name, ''Zalta Wa'', is a corruption of ''Sato'''ru''' '''Ta'''ki'''zawa'''''. [[Satoru Takizawa]] is the art director for {{BotW}}.
