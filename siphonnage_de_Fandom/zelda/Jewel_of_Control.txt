The '''Jewel of Control''' is an item appearing in the "[[Hitch in the Works]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. It is made so that anyone who wears it obeys [[Ganon]]'s orders, who plans to put this around [[Princess Zelda]]'s neck so that she will marry him and make him ruler of [[Hyrule]].<ref name=jewel>{{Cite Episode|quote=I wanted that princess. But once I place this Jewel of Control around her neck, the princess will do anythingI say--even "Marry me!" Aha ha ha ha! And make ME ruler of all Hyrule! Aha ha ha ha!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> 

When the [[Moblin]]s bring Zelda to the [[Underworld]], Ganon places the Jewel of Control around the princess and welcomes her as his future bride, which makes her agree immediately to marry him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=What?! If you think I'm gonna marry you, pigface, you're--absolutely right. I WILL marry Ganon.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> Link then eventually makes his way into Ganon's lair and interrupts the ceremony, only to discover that Zelda does not want to be rescued as she is still under the jewel's control.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=No. I'm marrying Ganon.|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref> After defeating the evil wizard, Link takes Zelda back up to the overworld where, upon noticing the Jewel of Control, she promptly takes it off.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Oh, what happened? And where'd this stupid necklace come from? |name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=10}}</ref>
