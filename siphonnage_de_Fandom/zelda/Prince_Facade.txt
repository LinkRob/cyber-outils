'''Prince Facade''' is a character in {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. He is a knight from the land of Arcadia and appears only in the episode "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|The White Knight|link}}", which is named after him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I am Prince Facade from Arcadia. I have traveled the world and viewed its wonders but none have been your match in beauty.|name= Prince Facade|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> Although the prince is brave and strong, he is known to be vain and will not fight if it means it will ruin his image or mess up his clothes.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= So...Prince Facade, the sickenly handsome face of Hyrule, is he. Hmm...this could be most fortunate. I've dealt with Prince Facade before, and I know his weakness. He's brave enough, but he's vain, very vain! Proud of his looks. He'd never let himself get dirty, not even to save the princess!|name= Ganon|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> His weapon of choice seems to be a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Crossbow|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} first appears when {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} is struggling to defeat an {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Octorok|link}}. Easily and elegantly, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} defeats the monster with his {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Crossbow}}. The Octorok leaves behind a Rupee, which Link eagerly claims until Facade said the Octorok was killed by his weapon. Facade easily impressing {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} and sweeping her off her feet. Zelda, fascinated by the prince, immediately forgets all about her compatriot, only remembering her manners knowing that Link was standing right next to her. She introduces herself to Facade then says the dirty man standing next to her is "Stink". An enraged look causes her to giggle and correct herself that his proper name is Link.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Uh, Link! Where are your manners? Stand up and meet Prince Facade! This is my friend, uh, well, an acquaintance--somebody I know slightly--his name's Stink.|name= Princess Zelda|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} offers Zelda a ride back to the castle as they leave {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to walk back there.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Oh, I'm sure you'll be fine, Link! [...] See you back at the castle, Link!|name= Princess Zelda|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> When they arrive back at the castle, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian|link}} and Zelda are fascinated by Facade and his tales of heroism, with the princess not caring about {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} anymore and not considering him to be in the prince's class. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} even told Zelda that he has battled {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Ganon|link}} himself (something that Ganon himself later confirms to his minions, albeit with a less flattering description of the prince).<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Well, there's Link, of course, but he's not in the prince's class. Prince Facade is wonderful.|name= Princess Zelda|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, feeling left out,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I swear... It's like Zelda doesn't even know I exist anymore.|name= Link|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> decides to try and match Facade, and attempts to dress more like him.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= No! I'll handle it myself. Did you get those clothes I wanted? [...] Zelda wants a prince, she'll get a prince.|name= Link|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> However, when he makes his appearance in front of Zelda and the king, he embarrasses himself and causes everyone to laugh at him, promptly causing {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}} to storm out of the castle humiliated and angry.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= She laughed... she laughed at me! She laughed! [...] Save your breath, Spryte! I'm gone! If Zelda wants her Triforce protected, her prince precious can do it. I just quit!.|name= Link|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> He mounts his horse and prepares to emigrate from Hyrule in search of other lands that may need a hero, telling [[Catherine]] that Facade has replaced him. 

Meanwhile, Facade continues to impress Zelda with his stories of saving a city from monsters.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= And as the final creature vanished, I realized that I had saved the city at last!|name= Prince Facade|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> As they are about to kiss, {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Tektite|link}} arrive and attack Facade while {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Moblin|link}} throw Zelda out the window, where a {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zola|link}} readily catches her. The Zola carries Zelda out of the castle, with Facade in pursuit, but when the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zola}} jumps into the water, Facade hesitates on the bank, not wanting to get his clothes wet and muddy.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Oh, mud... No, I'm afraid not. I DO have a reputation to maintain.|name= Prince Facade|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> Zelda screams at him to save her, but he refuses, saying that heroes do not get dirty.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Sorry, princess. No can do, I'm afraid. These are my hero's clothes, and heroes do not simply get dirty.|name= Prince Facade|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link}}, who was still near enough to hear Zelda's screams, rushes back to get past the prince and defeat the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Zola}} with three {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Sword Beam|link}}, thus rescuing Zelda.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Hey, it's a dirty job, princess, but somebody's gotta do it.|name= Link|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref>  She thanks Link for saving him and asks him to escort her home. {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Prince Facade}} quickly tries to calm the angry Zelda by making up excuses,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= Zelda, you're safe! I'm so glad, really. I would have done it myself, but you know how it is.|name= Prince Facade|show= The Legend of Zelda|episode= 3}}</ref> but she refuses to listen and makes him fall in the mud. As Link and Zelda ride off, a dirty Facade laughs to himself.

== Trivia ==

* Prince Facade's name has to do either with how much he cares about his appearance, or him not being as perfect of a hero as he seems.
* He and [[Charles of Moria|Sir Charles]] from the non-canon adventure books have some similarities superficially. Both are princes who seem like they can replace Link as heroes, only to not do so due to their vices. The difference is that Facade, while too vain, is still a hero, while Charles is [[Ganon|something much worse]].
