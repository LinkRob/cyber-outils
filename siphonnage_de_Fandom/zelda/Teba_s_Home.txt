{{Term/Store|Teba's Home||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Kaneli">{{Cite|If you're curious, {{Color|BotW Blue|Teba's home}} is {{Color|BotW Blue|right next door}} to my own humble abode.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Teba's Home}} is located near the top of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, right next door to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House|link}}.<ref name="Kaneli"/> It overlooks {{Term|BotW|Revali's Landing|link}} as well. It houses {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Saki|link}}, and their son, {{Term|BotW|Tulin|link}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} starts the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, he will have to seek out {{Term|BotW|Saki}} to learn the location of {{Term|BotW|Teba}}. He can find her in {{Term|BotW|Teba's Home}}, from where she was able to overhear his conversation with {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Forgive my intrusion, but I overheard you speaking with the {{Color|BotW Blue|elder}}.|Saki|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Teba}} left for the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My husband headed to a place called the {{Color|BotW Red|Flight Range}}.|Saki|BotW}}</ref> She points out {{Term|BotW|Revali's Landing}} to him, explaining that he can get to the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range}} from there.<ref>{{Cite|If you take off from {{Color|BotW Blue|Revali's Landing}}, it's a straight shot down to the {{Color|BotW Red|Flight Range}}.|Saki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Saki}} explains that it is a memorial for the {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}, which triggers the "{{Term|BotW|Revali's Flap|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Revali's Landing is a memorial for the Rito Champion, Revali... So named in the hope that none might forget the events of that horrible day...|Saki|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, he will be able to find {{Term|BotW|Teba}}, {{Term|BotW|Saki}}, and {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} in their {{Term|BotW|Teba's Home|display=Home}}. {{Term|BotW|Saki}} and {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} are both grateful to have {{Term|BotW|Teba}} home safe and sound.<ref>{{Cite|To have my husband home safe and sound... I cannot begin to express my gratitude.|Saki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Dad}} finally came back from work!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Teba}} has promised to take {{Term|BotW|Tulin}} flying as soon as he has healed from his injures.<ref>{{Cite|But it sounds like he got hurt... But he says he's gotten a lot better, so he'll be able to take me flying again soon!|Tulin|BotW}}</ref>

When {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack is active, {{Term|BotW|The Diary of Revali|link}} will appear inside {{Term|BotW|Teba's Home}}. According to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}}, he entrusted it to {{Term|BotW|Teba}} since he admires {{Term|BotW|Revali}}.<ref>{{Cite|However, not just anyone has the right to read something of that nature... That's why I left {{Color|BotW Blue|Teba}} in charge of it, since he so admires {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Revali}}.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Teba}} then decided that it would be safest in his {{Term|BotW|Teba's Home|display=Home}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Teba}} took the diary to {{Color|BotW Blue|his home}} for safekeeping.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref>
