The {{Term/Store|Bombers Secret Society of Justice||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a gang of six boys who strive to do good in their home town of [[Clock Town]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Bombers Secret Society of Justice forever!|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Bombers Secret Society of Justice}} was referred to as the '''Bombers Gang''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Bombers Gang}}<br/>The Bombers Gang has existed in Clock Town for many years. The gang consists of young boys with little else to do than cause trouble and goof around. Whenever a member of the Bombers Gang decides to quit the group and make something of himself, a new recruit is added. Generally, the Bombers Gang has always consisted of five members.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160312172522/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/b.jsp#BombersGang|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} '''Jim''', their leader, wears a red bandanna, whereas the hideout guard wears a yellow bandanna and the rest wear blue ones. They are good friends with the [[Professor Shikashi|astronomer]] in the [[Astral Observatory]];<ref>{{Cite|The old astronomer at the observatory outside town is the only one who'll spend any time playing with us kids.|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] needs to befriend the gang in order to get to the observatory.<ref>{{Cite|We Bombers have our hideout at the observatory. You have to take a secret route to get to it. Oh! Don't be telling Jim in North Clock Town that I told you that!|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM}}</ref>

==Biography==
[[File:Bombers.jpg|right|thumb|200px|Five members of the Bombers]]
Prior to the events of {{MM}}, the Bombers gang befriended the [[Skull Kid]], but later cast him out due to his evil motives.<ref>{{Cite|Once, we let some kid who wasn't human join our gang, and, boy, did we ever regret it!|Jim|MM}}</ref> As a result, they only allow humans to join their gang,<ref>{{Cite|If only you were human... Then I could give you an original Bombers' Notebook and make you a member...|Jim|MM}}</ref> provided that they can pass the test. Because of their previous affiliation with the Skull Kid, they also recognize [[Tatl]] and ask Link why is she with him, but only if the young hero is in [[Zora Link|Zora]] or [[Goron Link|Goron]] form.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! A Zora! Do you live in the ocean? What's it like? Why is Tatl with you? Why? Huh? Why?|Bomber|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! A Goron! Do you live in the mountains? Why is Tatl with you? Why? Huh? Why?|Bomber|MM}}</ref>

After popping the balloon Jim is firing at in [[North Clock Town]], [[Link]] is challenged to a game of [[Hide-and-Seek]].<ref>{{Cite|All right! Line up, guys! If you can find all five of us by tomorrow morning, I'll teach you the code!|Jim|MM}}</ref> The five Bombers can each be found during this game in the area of Clock Town they are normally located, save for one — while he is normally found in [[West Clock Town]], he will be found in [[North Clock Town]] during the game of hide-and-seek. The Bombers hiding in [[East Clock Town]] will sometimes hide under one of the two crates near the [[Milk Bar]]. If the young hero can find and catch all five Bombers within the time limit, he will be awarded with the secret passcode to the Bombers' hideout and the [[Bombers' Notebook]], provided that he is in Hylian form.<ref>{{Cite|You did it! So what's your name, guy? Link, huh? [...] I'm making Link a Bomber! You guys don't mind, right?|Jim|MM}}</ref> An alternative way to receive the Bombers' Notebook is to learn the passcode, rewind to the [[First Day]], enter into their hideout, come back out and speak with Jim.<ref>{{Cite|Nobody but Bombers know that code! So why do you know it? Listen, guy! You're pretty good! You managed to figure out a code that only we know! Now that's something! I like you!|Jim|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, the notebook is received from the [[Happy Mask Salesman]], who found it after Skull Kid dropped it.<ref>{{Cite|It appears to be one of those fancy notebooks the local brats carry around. Perhaps the imp dropped it?|Happy Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref> Also, the location of some Bombers was changed, with a member hiding in each of the five areas of Clock Town. Some of them now also hide under crates. Jim now also mentions that the usage of [[Deku Nut]]s is forbidden.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and Deku Nuts aren't fair game. They're bright and hurt our eyes, so you can't use them, OK? Got that?|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> Despite this, there isn't any penalty on Link for using Deku Nuts in the hide-and-seek game.

==Trivia==
*When he was younger, [[Kafei]] was once a member of the Bombers.<ref>{{Cite|Kafei? I can recall when he was in the Bombers. Don't see his face much anymore, though...Well, what could he be doing?|Professor Shikashi|MM}}</ref>
*If Deku Link shoots at them with the [[Bubble Blast]] in the hide-and-seek game, they will stop for a brief moment.
*The [[Hyrule]]an counterpart to Jim and the other Bombers is the [[Graveyard Boy]] from {{OoT}}, although only Jim has the same hairstyle as the Graveyard boy.
*[[Titch]], [[Chiro]] and [[Dante]] from {{FPTRR}} are part of a secret society of young children, likely in reference to the Bombers.
