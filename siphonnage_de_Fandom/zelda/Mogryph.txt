'''Mogryph''' is the boss of the [[Gerudo Sanctum]] in {{EoW}} and is one of the two bosses in which the Player can fight in any order of the first act.

==Characteristics==
Mogryph is the boss of the [[Gerudo Sanctum]], appearing as a fearsome creature that combines traits of a [[mole]] and a gryphon. It burrows beneath the sand to ambush targets and launch sweeping sand attacks, later taking to the air to thanks to the wings on it's back to create [[Tornado|tornadoes]] and perform diving strikes. After defeating Mogryph, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] frees the captive [[Tris]] and earns a [[Heart Container]].

==Strategy==
'''Phase One:'''

•	'''Sand Attacks:''' '''Mogryph''' begins by emerging from the sand to launch ranged sand attacks. Avoid standing directly in front to evade these assaults or by jumping above the sand attacks. 

•	'''Charging Attack:''' After several sand attacks, '''Mogryph''' burrows underground and charges toward you. Position yourself behind one of the statues in the arena to cause '''Mogryph''' to collide with it, stunning the boss. 

•	'''Counterattack:''' While '''Mogryph''' is dazed, switch to Swordfighter Form and unleash attacks. Repeat this process to progress to the next phase.

'''Phase Two:'''

•	'''Aerial Assaults:''' '''Mogryph''' gains the ability to fly, introducing new attacks such as creating tornadoes. Stay near the edges of the arena to dodge these whirlwinds. 

•	'''Dive Attacks:''' The boss dives into the sand, swimming beneath the surface before emerging to attack. Watch for ripples in the sand to anticipate its location and move aside to avoid damage.

•	'''Statue Strategy:''' Place a Statue Echo on one of the solid sand platforms to lure '''Mogryph''' into colliding with it during its dive attacks, stunning the boss. Attack while it’s incapacitated. 

'''Additional Tips:'''

•	'''Gerudo Sandals:''' Equip the [[Gerudo Sandals]] [[accessory]] to prevent sinking in quicksand during the battle. 

•	'''Bind Ability:''' Utilize the Bind Ability to pull '''Mogryph''' out of the sand, making it vulnerable to attacks.

==Trivia==

* Mogryph's name is a combination of '''"Mole"''' and '''"Gryphon"'''.
* Mogryph has many similarities to both [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Moley Moley] and [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Mrs._Moley Mrs. Moley] of the ''Kirby'' series.
** They both are large moles that submerge from underground.
** They both have similar attacks of launching out projectiles and(in the case with Mrs. Moley) dive bombing.
** They both serves as the main bosses of underground-based areas.
