{{Term/Store|Thieves' Town||Series, ALBW}} is a location in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 114 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref> Located on the western edge of [[Lorule]], it is the counterpart to [[Hyrule]]'s [[Kakariko Village]]. It is very similar in appearance to the [[Village of Outcasts]] from the [[Dark World]].

==Features and Overview==
The settlement is home to a group of thieves who have a [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|hideout]] on the northern edge of the town. The leader of the thieves, [[Stalblind]], possesses the portrait of the [[Sage]] [[Osfala]]. The [[Thief Girl]] was also imprisoned in the Thieves' Hideout,<ref>{{Cite|You're not the thief girl, are ya? I heard you were supposed to be locked up inside a cell somewhere!|Masked Follower|ALBW}}</ref> and will help [[Link]] in return for being freed. 

It is also home to a few [[Masked Followers|masked villagers]], and their leader the [[Masked Elder]]. Most of the masked followers found in town gather in a field located to the west, and speak of becoming monsters to be saved.<ref>{{Cite|The only way to get saved is to become a monster! C'mon! Get saved! Mumbo jumbo, mumbo jumbo...|Masked Follower|ALBW}}</ref>   

The state of the Lorulean people in town is less then desirable to some,<ref>{{Cite|Look around, kiddo. It's all thieves and creepy masked folks in this village.|Captain|ALBW}}</ref> though some can still be found trying to make the best of things.<ref>{{Cite|I don't mind it so much. At least there's still a strong musical culture in this town.|Veteran Thief|ALBW}}</ref>

Numerous [[Moblin]]s patrol the town, and many [[Hyu]]s are seen floating around.
