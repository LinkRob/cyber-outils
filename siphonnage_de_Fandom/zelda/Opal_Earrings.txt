The {{Term/Store|Opal Earrings|Opal Earrings|Series, BotW, HWAoC}} are a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Opal Earrings}}<br/>Earrings sold at Gerudo jewelry shops. They contain opal, a gem that harnesses the power of water to increase your swimming speed.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Opal Earrings}}<br/>Earrings sold at Gerudo jewelry shops. They contain opal, a gem that harnesses the power of water and casts a cool, elegant incandescence when held up to light.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Opal Earrings can be purchased in the [[Starlight Memories]] shop at [[Gerudo Town]] for 200 Rupees plus eight [[Opal]]s. When worn, they provide one level of Swim Speed Up.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Opal Earrings can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], though they cannot be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
