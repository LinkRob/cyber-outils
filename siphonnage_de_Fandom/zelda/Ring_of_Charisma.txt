{{Term/Store|Ring of Charisma|Rings of Charisma|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Ring of Charisma}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Ring of Charisma}} are equipment that cuts the prices of shop items in half when worn by either {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Reduces the cost of items for sale in shops around Hyrule.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> If the price of an item is an odd number, such as {{Plural|CoH|Bomb|link}} which typically cost 25 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}, the halved price is simply rounded up from 12.5 to 13 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee}}.

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain minibosses.

==Trivia==
They share their name and function with the Ring of Charisma item from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
