'''Skoras''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Skoras are strong, scorpion-like enemies that appear only in the [[Forest of Canvula]]. Skoras have very fast and erratic movements, and they shoot [[Fireball]]s to attack. A Skora can inflict two [[Heart]]s of damage on [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], but if Zelda has at least four [[Celestial Sign]]s, a Skora inflicts only a Heart and half of damage.

Skoras are invincible by default. If Zelda has five Celestial Signs, she can defeat a Skora by hitting it with her [[Wand]] seven times. If she acquires the sixth Celestial Sign, Zelda only needs to attack a Skora three times to defeat it.
