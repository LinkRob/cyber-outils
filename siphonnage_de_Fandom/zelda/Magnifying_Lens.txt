The {{Term/Store|Magnifying Lens||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 131"/>{{Note|This item is also referred to as the '''Magnifying Glass''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 21}}</ref> However, because {{E}} takes precedence, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
The Magnifying Lens can be found inside a cave to which the entrance is revealed by inserting the [[Scale]] into [[The Mourning Mermaid]] in [[Martha's Bay]].<ref name="E 131"/> The Magnifying Lens can be used to find things not normally visible to the human eye,<ref name="E 131"/> making it similar in function to the [[Lens of Truth]]. Using it allows Link to encounter a [[Goriya]] with whom he can trade certain items for a [[Boomerang]] in [[Toronbo Shores]].<ref>{{Cite|I found a good item washed up on the beach... I'll trade it to you for what you have in your B Button...|Item Trader|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Boomerang in exchange for the item you had.|N/A|LADX}}</ref> The Magnifying Lens also allows Link to read the book found in the [[Library]] in [[Mabe Village]] with the fine print that contains the answer to the puzzle in the [[Wind Fish's Egg]].<ref name="E 131"/> As such, it is almost required to complete the game; it is possible to skip the Magnifying Lens by testing all the possible paths through the egg.

In {{LA|DX}}, the Magnifying Lens also allows Link to see a friendly [[River Zora|Zora]] in a house in [[Animal Village]].<ref name="E 131"/> At this point, the [[Photographer]] will come in and take a picture of the two together.<ref>{{Cite|Hi there! It's me, the photographer! You say Zora is in the house? I HAVE to take a picture of that. I'll call it 'I Found Zora.'|Photographer|LADX}}</ref> Afterwards, the Zora will tell Link how to find Goriya.<ref>{{Cite|If you keep me a secret, I'll tell you something useful. Go to Toronbo Shores, use the magnifying glass and you will find someone like me.|Zora|LADX}}</ref>
