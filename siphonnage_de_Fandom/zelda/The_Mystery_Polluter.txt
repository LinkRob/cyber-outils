"{{Term/Store|The Mystery Polluter||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Mystery Polluter}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is one of four that must be completed as part of "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}". When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}} in the empty plot near {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}. Though she is keeping it a secret from {{Term|BotW|Lorn|link|display=her mother}}, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} is planning on starting a fruit garden in the plot.<ref>{{Cite|Heehee! I'm still keeping it a secret from Mama, but I'm growing a fruit garden here!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> She has not yet figured out what she wants to grow,<ref>{{Cite|I haven't figured out what I want to plant here yet, though...|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> but she promises to share her crop with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I grow a lot, I'll share some of it with you, OK?|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} has been stopped, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} crying in the empty plot.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... Vai-from-out-of-town... Sav'aaq... *sniff*|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> She explains that she was planning an orchard for the plot,<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to plant an orchard here...|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> which she even began to prepare and clean.<ref>{{Cite|I picked up trash... I pulled the weeds... I tilled the ground... I did all the stuff you're supposed to do to make the earth happy...|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Trash|link}} has begun to pile up in the water trough, which means she can not use the water for her plants.<ref>{{Cite|But... But... The {{Color|BotW Red|trash}}... All this {{Color|BotW Red|trash}} keeps getting washed down into the water trough, and now I can't use the water there anymore. And if I can't use the water anymore... my orchard is doomed!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> When she is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} begins.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} rinds flowing down into the trough through the upper waterway. By following them, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can discover the source of the polution, a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} woman named {{Term|BotW|Calyban|link}} who is eating {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon}}. She is annoyed that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is interrupting her enjoyment of her snack,<ref>{{Cite|What's with you? How can I enjoy my snack with you staring at me, huh?! Buzz off!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> but she becomes outraged when she is accused of polluting the waterways.<ref>{{Cite|[Actually...] What? Garbage?!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> Even though she knows that {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} is upset,<ref>{{Cite|A small vai is having trouble?|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} sees no reason to change her ways.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, sure, you spring this on me out of nowhere and expect me to change MY lifestyle. Well, no. I do what I want!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref>

The look on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s face ends up convincing {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} to change her mind.<ref>{{Cite|Aww... Don't look at me like that! Fine! FINE! I get it!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> Stating that altruism is for suckers,<ref>{{Cite|But I need something from you in return. Altruism is for suckers.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} reveals that she would like to try a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Just once...I'd like to eat a {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> She refuses to stop eating so many {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon}} unless {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings her a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}}.<ref>{{Cite|You bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}, and I can hold off on eating so many melons.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> She advises {{Term|BotW|Link}} that they grow in the cold parts of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Wildberries}} grow in the {{Color|BotW Red|snowy areas of the Gerudo Highlands}}.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> and due to the danger of the area, they rarely appear in the market.<ref>{{Cite|It's a dangerous region, so the fruit is kind of rare and hardly ever shows up at market...|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> Realizing there is no point in only having one,<ref>{{Cite|Ah. Now that I think on it, there's barely a point if you only bring me one...|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} decides to ask for 10.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see... Yes, I'll need at least {{Color|BotW Red|10}}...|Calyban|BotW}}</ref>

Once Link has collected 10 {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} for {{Term|BotW|Calyban}}, he can return to deliver them to her. {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} is amazed at how delicious they are,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|SWEEEETNESS!}} So that's a {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}...|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> noting that there is no rind for her to toss away.<ref>{{Cite|It's delicious! And no rind to toss! What a magical fruit!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> She says that she may stop eating other fruit entirely if she has {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I have these, I might just give up on other fruit entirely!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> Realizing how selfish she has been,<ref>{{Cite|But...now I feel bad for the little vai. Now that my craving's been satisfied, I realize just how horribly selfish I've been... Shame on me.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} leaves to help {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} clean up the rinds.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to go help clean up the trash! I've really caused a lot of trouble...but now I'm going to make it right! Sarqso!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> 

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Dalia}}, {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} has already cleaned up her {{Term|BotW|Trash}} and left.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! You! Listen to this! Some other vai I've never met before helped me clean up the trash! Amd now the water trough is completely back to normal!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for telling someone who could help about the trash.<ref>{{Cite|You told her about the trash, didn't you? Sarqso!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> She gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon}} that was given to her by {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} as a thank you for caring about her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I also got a hydromelon from the lady who cleaned it up! I'll share! It's a sarqso for caring about me!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>

Since {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} also gave {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yeah, the lady who helped clean gave me some wildberries!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> she has decided to grow them in the plot.<ref>{{Cite|So now my field is gonna be a wildberry patch!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> She welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come eat the {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} in her garden,<ref>{{Cite|You can come eat the berries anytime you like! It'll be great!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> which completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. Just as {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} says, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can come back later to find that three {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}} plants have grown there.
