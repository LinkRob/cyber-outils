'''Horriblins''' are a common enemy type in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom]]''. They appear in many places around Hyrule, often in caves. After the Upheaval, these monsters began appearing in Hyrule's cave systems. They have sturdy horns that are perfect for crushing rocks. Horriblins are often seen clinging to the ceiling of their homes, throwing rocks at [[Link]], should they spot him. A single [[Arrow]] is enough to knock the monster down, allowing Link to engage it in melee combat. Horriblins tend to wield spears as their weapons of choice, often [[Fuse|Fused]] with another object.
