The '''Rocket''', is a [[Zonai Device]] in {{TotK}}.

== Locations and Uses ==
{{Manual|TotK|Rocket|A Zonai device that produces powerful thrust. It packs a punch but burns out quickly.}}
Rockets are [[Zonai Device]]s in {{TotK}}. They can be found on the [[Sky Islands]], as well as from a 'pachinko-style' Zonai Machine in exchange for [[Zonai Charge|Zonai Charges.]]<ref name=":0">[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2EMZzW7hj0M&ab_channel=Zeltik I Played Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom (Hands-On Preview) - Zeltik]</ref>

When attached to objects using [[Ultrahand]], it can be used to propel objects into the sky,<ref name=":0" /> and can additionally be attached to [[Link]]'s arm to lift him into the air.<ref>[https://youtu.be/WBELOrZvgNI?t=226 The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom - Official Trailer #3]</ref> Despite these uses, they do not last very long.<ref name=":0" />

== Details ==
Action: Provides a strong, brief thrust in the direction pointed in.
Notes:
# When activated, rockets travel in a straight line, regardless of gravity.
# Each rocket uses up a third of a battery over a 5 second duration.
# All rockets have a 5 second lifespan.
# All rockets cost 3 zonaite using Autobuild.
# Rockets are able to move Hover Stones, which cannot be moved by fans.
* Shield Fuse: Launches Link into the air.
* Melee Fuse: Increases damage by 5.
* Melee Fused Throw: Propels the item forward.
