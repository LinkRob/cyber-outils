The {{Term|BotW|Twilight Bow}} is a rare {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} that was once used by {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> It can be rarely acquired from a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawns after scanning the {{SSB|-}} series {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, though only after completing at least one of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. It is said to contain the power of the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit of Light|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Like the {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light}} from {{TP|-}}, it fires {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Twilight Bow}} shares many characteristics with the {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light}}; they both fire {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow}} resembling those seen in {{TP|-}}, and both have the greatest range of any {{Term|BotW|Bow}}. However, unlike the {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light}}, the {{Term|BotW|Twilight Bow}} has a base attack power of 30 and durability of 100.
