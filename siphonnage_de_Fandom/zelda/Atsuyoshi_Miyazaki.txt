'''Atsuyoshi Miyazaki''' is a voice actor. He is the current Japanese voice actor for [[Master Kohga]], having voiced him since {{BotW}}. In {{BotW}}, he voiced Kohga in all regions. He was formerly affiliated [[Ken Production]] but is currently freelance.
