==Characteristics==
Boulders are rocks that fall in random patterns when [[Link]] is traveling through certain mountainous regions of [[Hyrule]], commonly appearing on [[Death Mountain]]. They are often unpredictable and will inflict damage to Link if he comes in contact with them. The boulders are like in the Indiana Jones movies when Indy in the first movie escapes the temple by escaping the rolling boulder.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Rock|These rocks crumble off Death Mountain and fall down to attack Link. He can't destroy these natural nuisances.}}
Rocks first appear in {{TLoZ}}, in which they fall from Death Mountain.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Boulders appear on the lower western region of Death Mountain in the [[Light World]]. They are much larger and can inflict a [[Heart]] of damage.  It is easier to travel further up the mountain by traveling through the caves to avoid them.

==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, Boulders appear, similar to their original appearance, on the east of the [[Tal Tal Mountain Range]]. Boulders are also used as weapons by the [[Stone Hinox]] in {{LA|DX}} when it slams the ground.

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Boulders appear on Death Mountain, but rather than bouncing down the Mountain, they roll down it once Link has become an adult. Boulders also appear in [[Gerudo Valley]] and certain [[dungeon]]s. Sometimes they roll around areas such as [[Ganon's Castle]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Boulders behave like they do in {{OoT|-}} when climbing the [[Stone Tower]]. A gray variant can be found in the [[Deku Shrine]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, Boulders appear on [[Restoration Wall]].

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Boulders appear on Death Mountain. They show up in caves where they can be pushed, where they also roll around and can be dropped into pits to make them safe to cross. [[Hinox]]es throw massive Boulders at the Links.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Boulder}}
In {{TMC}}, Boulders appear on [[Mount Crenel]] and function similarly to their {{ALttP|-}} incarnation.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, rolling Boulders appear on the [[Isle of Ruins]].

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Boulders appear in the [[Fire Sanctuary]]. Static varieties are found in other locations and can be pushed to solve puzzle, or destroyed by {{Plural|ST|Wrecker Phantom|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Stub}}

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Boulders sometimes roll down the path instead of [[Snowball]]s in the [[Snowball Ravine]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
Boulders in {{BotW}} are sometimes used in puzzles to reveal [[Korok]]s that are scattered across [[Hyrule Kingdom]] where Link must push a Boulder into a hole some distance away.<ref name="Piggyback 330"/> Sometimes the holes are at the bottom of a slope with the Boulders at the top, and sometimes they are on level ground and must be launched using [[Stasis]]. A variation of this puzzle involves using [[Magnesis]] on an [[Iron Ball]] to get it into the hole.<ref name="Piggyback 330"/> After at least one Boulder makes it into the hole, a Korok will appear and give Link a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> Other Boulders reprise their roles as obstacles in the game, appearing in the Shrines and other places.

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{stub|TotK}}
Boulders return as obstacles in {{TotK}}. They can also be [[Fuse]]d with a [[Tree Branch]] to create a [[Long Stick]].

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Boulder|A hard rock that's very heavy, but you can move it if you really push.<ref name="EoW Boulder">{{Cite|A hard rock that's very heavy, but you can move it if you really push.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Boulders are heavy objects that can be created after [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] learns their [[Echo]]. Once learned, she can summon them anywhere using the [[Tri Rod]]. They can be used to sink objects, block hazards, and solve puzzles.
