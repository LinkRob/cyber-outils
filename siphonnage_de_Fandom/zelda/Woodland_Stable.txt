The {{Term/Store|Woodland Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''Woodland Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Woodland Stable is a [[Stable]] that is situated along the border between [[Eldin Canyon]], [[Hyrule Field]] and the [[Great Hyrule Forest]].<ref name="Woodland Stable">{{Cite|This here {{Color|BotW Blue|Woodland Stable}} borders the {{Color|BotW Blue|Eldin region}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Field}}. It's a central fixture for travelers. Also the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Hyrule Forest}}, which is where the stable got its name, is right over yonder.|Ashe|BotW}}</ref> It sits along the road in northern [[Hyrule]] that paves between [[East Hyrule Castle]] and Eldin Canyon. Although technically part of Eldin Canyon, it is in close proximity to the woodlands of the Great Hyrule Forest, giving it its name.<ref name="Woodland Stable"/> The Stable sits right beside [[Pico Pond]], where the [[Mirro Shaz Shrine]] is located, making it a convenient [[Travel Gate]] to the Stable. The Woodland Stable contains every amenity characteristic to Stables, including [[Horse]] services, lodging, and a [[Cooking Pot]] for [[Cooking]]. Beside the Stable is a pen that keeps several {{Plural|BotW|White Goat|link}}, guarded by a [[Hylian Retriever]]. On the opposite side of the pen is a deck with several tables and [[Barrel]]s. A [[Double Axe]] can be found resting against a stump nearby, and a Soldier's Broadsword can be found underneath a tree north of the Double Axe. A [[Soldier's Spear]] can be found laying against the rock wall leading toward the Shrine.

The Stable is run by [[Kish]], who mans both its Horse services and lodging. A girl at the Horse pen named [[Breen]] will also assist Link with changing his Horse's Gear or Mane. As with all other Stables, [[Beedle]] can occasionally be found here selling his wares.

An elderly worker at the Stable named [[Ashe]] assists travelers with giving directions and explaining the local area. He will give directions to Eldin Canyon, Hyrule Field, and the Great Hyrule Forest as well as their features when spoken to.<ref>{{Cite|Do you want me to tell you about the surrounding areas?|Ashe|BotW}}</ref>

The painter [[Pikango]] can be found here and directs Link to where the [[Recovered Memory]] "[[Subdued Ceremony]]" is located, at the [[Sacred Ground Ruins]] in front of [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] to the southwest of the Stable.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... This shows {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} in the distance. They say there used to be a stage where knighting ceremonies were performed, just south of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. It's in ruins now, but... I'm pretty certain that's what's in this picture.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

A child named [[Shamae]] requests that Link attaches "balloons" to one of the Barrels in front of her so that they may fly into the sky, initiating the [[Side Quest]] "[[Balloon Flight]]".<ref>{{Cite|Hey, mister! Just once in my life, I want to see {{Color|BotW Red|balloons}} float right up into the sky! If you'll help me realize my {{Color|BotW Red|balloon}} dream, I'll give you a treasure I got from the sky as thanks!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Now put some {{Color|BotW Red|balloons}} onto a barrel and make it float up high!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> Shamae will give Link a [[Star Fragment]] as thanks for fulfilling her request.

A man named [[Molo]] seated at one of the deck's tables mentions that there are rumors of abandoned treasures and weapons to be found in Hyrule Castle,<ref>{{Cite|Listen, if you wanna get anywhere in this life, you gotta cash and cachet. And the only way to do that in this day and age is to go to—stay with me here—{{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. I know—it sounds crazy. But rumor has it that it's full of abandoned treasure and weapons! They're just sitting in there, free for the taking! Well...if you can get past all the bloodthirsty monsters, of course.|Molo|BotW}}</ref> and hopes to plunder it for himself despite the dangers there.<ref>{{Cite|You're right! I can't let a smoking-hot rumor like this go to waste! I just gotta act on it, bud!|Molo|BotW}}</ref> He also mentions that there is an [[Ancient Shrine]] located at its northern [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)#Docks|Docks]], referring to the [[Saas Ko'sah Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|There's docks on the north side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}, see, and I hear you can get inside the castle from there. There's also an ancient {{Color|BotW Blue|shrine}} somewhere around there. Smells like treasure to me. Stinks like it, even!|Molo|BotW}}</ref>
