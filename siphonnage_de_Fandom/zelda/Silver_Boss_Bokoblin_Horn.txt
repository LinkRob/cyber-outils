'''Silver Boss Bokoblin Horn''' is an item in {{TotK}}. It can be obtained by defeating [[Silver Boss Bokoblin]]. The item appears eventually at some Boss Bokoblin spawn points after enough in-game progression.

Silver Boss Bokoblin Horns are an uncommon monster part that can be fused to your weapons and shields to give your attacks and shield parries incredibly high damage while also sporting the appearance of the disk-shaped horn itself. You can also attach these powerful horns to arrows, use them as an ingredient to cook slightly longer-lasting elixirs or dye your armor at the Hateno Dye Shop. However, these horns are best suited for fusing with weapons which will be essential for defeating powerful monsters.

==Data==
Fuse Attack Power: 37

* Fusion Effect: Melds a Silver Boss Bokoblin Horn to your weapon, increasing its attack power by 37 and giving your weapon the appearance of the horn itself.
* Attachment Effect: Adds a Silver Boss Bokoblin Horn to the next arrow fired from your bow, increasing its attack power by thirty-seven.
* Shield Fusion Effect: Melds a Silver Boss Bokoblin Horn to your shield, increasing its attack power by thirty-seven and causing your shield parries to damage any monster in melee range.

Unique Cooking Effect: Elixir Effect Duration +00:40
