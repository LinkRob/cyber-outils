{{Term/Store|Verona||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Verona}} is the mother of {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Jim|link}}.<ref name="E 271"/> She is first seen at the southern edge of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}} during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where she enjoys watching everyone have fun after working hard to prepare.<ref>{{Cite|Phew... Preparing for the festival left me absolutely exhausted... Still, I'm just enjoying watching people have fun.|Verona|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, she can be found standing near the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Well|link}} talking to {{Term|TMC|Keeley|link}} about how exciting the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}} was.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see the tournament? It was incredible! I was out of my seat and cheering before I even knew it! I can't wait for next year's tournament! When's it supposed to be?|Verona|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} impersonates {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Verona}} notices his strange behavior,<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TMC Green|king}} doesn't seem like himself these days... It's weird.|Verona|TMC}}</ref> and the worsening of his mood.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know... The {{Color|TMC Green|king}}'s mood just keeps getting worse and worse.|Verona|TMC}}</ref>

Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} has encountered the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Verona}} relocates to the {{Term|TMC|Girl's House|link}}, located in the southeastern portion of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, where she begins preparing dinner.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I suppose I'd better get dinner started! Hmm hm hmmm!♪|Verona|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} acquires the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} and monsters begin appearing around {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}, {{Term|TMC|Verona}} comments on how scary it is.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh, scary... Well, as long as nothing else happens...|Verona|TMC}}</ref>

She is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}, don't you? Well, OK! Let's give it a try.|Verona|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TMC|Verona}} likely gets her name from the city of Verona, the setting of William Shakespeare's ''[[wikipedia:Romeo and Juliet|Romeo and Juliet]]''.
