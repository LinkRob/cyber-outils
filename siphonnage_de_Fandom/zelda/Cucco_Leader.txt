The {{Term/Store|Cucco Leader||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 277}}</ref>

==Biography==

The {{Term|TP|Cucco Leader}} is a {{Term|TP|Cucco|link}} who is the leader of all the {{Plural|TP|Cat|link}} who live in the {{Term|TP|Hidden Village|link}}.

If {{Term|TP|Link|link}} speaks to the {{Term|TP|Cucco Leader}} while in {{Term|TP|Wolf Link|link|display=wolf}} form they will ask {{Term|TP|Link}} to find and speak with the twenty {{Plural|TP|Cat}} scattered throughout the village.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TP Green|Oh... Welcome... This is the forgotten village... 30... No, more like 20... That's how many cats have made it back here to the village... So perhaps you could...you know...just...play with them?}}|Cucco Leader|TP}}</ref> If {{Term|TP|Link}} does this he will be rewarded with a {{Term|TP|Piece of Heart|link}} for him to collect before he leaves.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TP Green|You talked to all of them! Thanks... Now your prize is}} {{Color|TP Red|in front of the old lady's house}}{{Color|TP Green|... Make sure you pick it up before you leave...}}|Cucco Leader|TP}}.</ref> If {{Term|TP|Link}} leaves the {{Term|TP|Hidden Village}} before he has spoken to all the {{Plural|TP|Cat}} he will have to start again from the beginning when he next returns.
