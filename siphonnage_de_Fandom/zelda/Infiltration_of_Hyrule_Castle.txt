The {{Term/Store|Infiltration of Hyrule Castle||Series, FSA}} is the final stage of [[Near the Fields]] in {{FSA}},{{Name Ref|FSA}} within after both [[The Field]] and [[The Swamp]].

Unlike many of the levels in {{FSA|-}}, the Infiltration of [[Hyrule Castle]] is more of a [[Stealth]] mission than an exploratory level. The four [[Links]] must carefully sneak into Hyrule Castle in search of the [[Maiden#White Maiden|White Shrine Maiden]], evading the bright spotlights that scour the grounds. While careful footwork is the best strategy here, any loud sound such as busting open a [[Jar|pot]] will alert the [[searchlight]]s; it is jail time for the Links if they are to be caught beneath its beam. Though it may seem the cell is inescapable, the [[key]] to unlock its door is located directly across the hall in a pot, reachable with the [[Boomerang]]. An ingenious strategy for evading the spotlights is to use the small moat surrounding the castle to swim beneath the surface, away from the beam's gaze. Interestingly, this avenue will also help Link in bolstering his [[Force Gem]] count, as there is an abundance of Force Gems beneath the surface of the water. 

Upon entering the castle, the Links must face the many [[Ghini]] and [[Whisp]]s that stalk the halls, as well as [[Big Poe (Boss)|Big Poe]], the guardian of the room where the [[Maiden#White Maiden|White Shrine Maiden]] lay incarcerated. In terms of enemies outside the castle, only [[Dark Link|Shadow Link]], [[Keese]], and [[Whisp|Red Whisp]] exist.
