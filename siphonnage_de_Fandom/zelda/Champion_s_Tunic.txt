==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Champion's Tunic is obtained from [[Impa]] in [[Kakariko Village]] after [[Link]] relives one of his [[Recovered Memories]].<ref>{{Cite|Now then... Let me see. Aha! This is, without a doubt, the {{Color|BotW Red|Camera}} that Princess Zelda used 100 years ago. ... It could be that if you visit the locations within those {{Color|BotW Red|pictures}}, you will be able to restore some of your lost memories. Come back here once you have tried going to at least one of those locations. There is something I wish to give to you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now that you have seen some of the princess's memories, you must have started to remember certain things... Here. I will hand this over to you now, before I forget.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> The Champion's Tunic mirrors the signature blue cloth worn by the other [[Champion]]s, which are decorated with the symbol of the respective tool each Champion must wield against [[Calamity Ganon]]. As Link is the [[Hylian]] Champion, it is marked with the silhouette of the [[Master Sword]]. According to its description, it is only given to individuals who have won the respect of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].<ref name="Inventory"/>

When worn, the Champion's Tunic gives Link the ability to see an enemy's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} with a total numerical amount of their remaining health.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Champion's Tunic has the highest potential defense of any [[Armor]] present in the game, with a maximum defense of 32. It cannot be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], nor can it be sold, though it can be upgraded at a [[Great Fairy Fountain]]. The Champion's Tunic is not part of any [[Armor Set]], though it consistently appears matched with the [[Hylian Trousers]] in promotional media, in each of Link's Recovered Memories and at the end of the game if Link has recovered all of his lost Memories. Similarly, the two aformentioned pieces of Armor are also paired with the [[Hylian Hood]] when Link is seen on horseback in promotional media.
