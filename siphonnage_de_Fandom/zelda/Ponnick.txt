{{Term/Store|Ponnick||Series, TotK}} is a character in {{TotK}}.

==Biography==
Ponnick is a member of the [[Zonai Survey Team]] and lives in [[Lookout Landing]]. He is among the surveyors to research the Hyrule Field Chasm and accompanied [[Robbie]] to the Depths. When Link arrives at the bottom of the chasm, Ponnick is found at a small campsite and reveals that Robbie had went ahead into the darkness, leaving him worried.
