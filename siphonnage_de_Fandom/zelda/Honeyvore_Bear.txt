{{Term/Store|Honeyvore Bear|Honeyvore Bears|Series, BotW}}s are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Honeyvore Bear}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract}}
{{Plural|BotW|Honeyvore Bear}} are powerful animals that inhabit the wildernesses of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} during the {{Term|BotW|Era of the Wilds|link}}. They can be found in areas throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}, but are particularly common in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Honeyvore Bear}} have primarily brown fur, with golden-brown muzzles. They are known to have a strong taste for {{Term|BotW|Honey|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As their name implies, they have a natural love of honey.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Honeyvore Bear}} are highly territorial animals, and will attack anyone who crosses into their territories.<ref>{{Cite|They'll attack anyone who wanders into their territory regardless of the wanderer's weaponry.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> However, they can also be ridden and tamed like a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}},<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= If you distract its attention and sneak up, you might be able to ride it.|magazine= Nintendo Magazine|publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021|page= 16}}</ref> although they cannot be registered at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} can also be found riding {{Plural|BotW|Honeyvore Bear}} in certain locations, such as in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. When killed, they drop {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat|link}}.
