{{Term/Store|Anti-Lightning Training: I||HWAoC}} is a Level 9 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Lightning Training: I|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Lightning Training: I}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Zapshroom|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Safflina|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfin Trout|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} within five minutes, which is surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}}. Once the {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}} is defeated, two more {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}} spawn alongside {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} and the time limit resets to five minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when they are defeated.
