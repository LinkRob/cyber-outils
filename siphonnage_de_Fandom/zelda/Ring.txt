The {{Term/Store|Ring|Rings|Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Wizzro]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}}. It's also where he was sealed before and after the events of the game, but while free Wizzro uses its power as a way to cast his spells.

==Variations==
The default Ring is the '''Blue Ring''', which is based on its [[Blue Ring|namesake]]. The second form of the Ring is the '''Red Ring''', which is based on its [[Red Ring|namesake]]. The third form is the '''Magical Ring'''.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Wizzro cackles, sinking into the ground as slimy, cycloptic fish-like creatures leap from a pool of dark magic in the ground. The magic compresses into a ball and explodes, blowing enemies away, while the giggling Wizzro re-emerges from the ground. This attack deals damage in a wide area around Wizzro and carries no Element.{{verify}}
