'''Lake Boruna''' is a location in {{ZA}}.{{Name Ref|ZA}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|ZA|Lake Boruna}} is a large lake that compasses most of the {{Term|ZA|Vendoss}} swamp, where the river flowing from the {{Term|ZA|Forest of Torian|link}} empties into. Around the {{Term|ZA|Lake Boruna|display=Lake}}, {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|link}} can walk along the marsh, but will not find anything there. A man named {{Term|ZA|Faust|link}} in the swamp village runs a boat ferry service, and will ferry {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda}} to the {{Term|ZA|Mystery Island|link}} in the middle of the {{Term|ZA|Lake Boruna|display=Lake}} for two {{Plural|ZA|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hello there, young lady! I am Faust the boatman and for two Rubies I offer a tour to Mystery Island. What's on Mystery Island? If I told you, it wouldn't be a mystery.|Faust|ZA}}</ref>

{{Term|ZA|Mystery Island}} is a small, lonely island of mostly sand. Its sole feature is a single {{Term|ZA|Palm Tree}}, where by its base a {{Term|ZA|Crystal Heart|link}} can be found.
