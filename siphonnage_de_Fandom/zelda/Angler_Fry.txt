{{Term/Store|Angler Fry|Angler Fries|Series, LA, LADX|plural}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{LA}}.<ref name= "E 163">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 163 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Angler Fries appear during the battle against the [[Angler Fish]]. They do not actively attack [[Link]], but swim around the area aimlessly, hurting him if he touches them. Angler Fries require one hit from the [[Sword]] to be defeated.
