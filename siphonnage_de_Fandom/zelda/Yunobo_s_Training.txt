{{Term/Store|Yunobo's Training||HWAoC}} is a level 41 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yunobo's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}'s objective is to defeat 600 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}} at first, which all surround a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} in the center. When he reaches 150 KOs, a line of {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}} spawns in front of a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}}, which both approach {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches 400 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} spawns, along with {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} that surround {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}. Once 600 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} ends.
