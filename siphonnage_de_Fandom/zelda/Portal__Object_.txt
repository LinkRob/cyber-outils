{{Term/Store|Portal|Portals|Series, OoS, OoA, TMC, CoH|plural}} are magical gateways that usually serves either as a mode of transportation or a means of transformation within {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |publisher= BradyGames |page= 12{{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |publisher= BradyGames |page= 8{{Exp Game|OoA}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's right! I can turn you tiny anytime you want! Well, that is, if there's a stump or some other kind of {{Color|TMC Blue|portal}} nearby...|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>{{Name Ref|ALttP, OoT, MM, TWW, FSA, TP, PH, ST, ALBW}} There have been multiple examples of portals seen throughout the series. They can serve as gateways to other worlds or dimensions as seen in {{OoT}} and {{MM}}, as methods of quick transportation as seen in {{TP}}, or as a means of transformation in order to explore new places or things as seen in {{TMC}}. The use of portals has steadily grown as the series has progressed, and new uses for them have been known to be developed with each new addition to the series.

==Overview and Uses==
Portals have many uses throughout the series, assisting Link along in his quests against evil by enabling him to meet his needs. At times, he must perform certain tasks to make certain portals available to be used. Typically however there two main uses for portals that have repetitiously been applied throughout the series thus far: transportation and transformation.

Of all potential uses to which portals have been put throughout the series, none has been more prevalent than the ability of transportation. Portals have served as methods of transportation or gateways to permit entry into certain places. This ability has constantly been reused with the development of new games, with portals of varying power and ability acting as methods of quick transportation for Link to places he seeks to reach. They have also been known to be used for transporting Link out of the various dungeons he investigates throughout the series, thus saving the time of having to trek all the way back through the dungeon to reach the exit once the objective has been achieved. Portals have also served as modes of transportation for Link to enter new worlds or dimensions that otherwise would be unreachable by foot or any other mode of transportation for that matter.

Another use for portals that has arisen in the series (though largely on only one occasion) has been to transform Link in some manner from his normal form, or in this case his size. This was first exhibited in {{TMC}}, where certain objects found throughout the Overworld turned out to be portals that would allow Link to shrink to the minuscule size of the Minish in order to explore the world the way they were able to. Though they did not really change Link's body in any way in the end, the portals featured at the end of the adult dungeons in {{OoT|-}} and each dungeon in {{TP|-}} both caused a physical change for Link that included such things as being encased inside a crystal or transforming into Twilight matter in order to be warped out of the dungeon. It remains to be seen whether any new aspects of the end-dungeon portals will be developed for future games, or whether there will be other transformation features.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Minish Portal}}
Four different {{Plural|TMC|Portal (Object)}} appear in {{TMC}}. Three of them, colored blue, red, and green, appear exclusively in {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}}. They appear after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} has reached a certain checkpoint within the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}, and serve as a way for him to return to the entrance and maintain his progress. Blue {{Plural|TMC|Portal (Object)}} appear earlier in {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon}}, with red {{Plural|TMC|Portal (Object)}} appearing later on, and green {{Plural|TMC|Portal (Object)}} only appearing after {{Term|TMC|Link}} defeats the {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}.

The fourth variant of {{Plural|TMC|Portal (Object)}} is a singular {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)}} that appears in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} (near {{Term|TMC|Link's House|link}}) after {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} goes through this {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)}}, it will transport him to a limited section of the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}, where he will have an opportunity to save {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}}.

Other objects in throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} are called {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to shrink down to the size of the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, Red Portals are created by [[Zant]]'s evil magic and send [[Shadow Beast]]s to fight Link. Once the Shadow Beasts are defeated, the portal then turns blue. [[Midna]] can [[Warping|Warp]] Link to these portals from any location. Zant can also create portals to summon various monsters of Twilight, and it is implied that [[Ganondorf]] may use them to teleport.{{Fact}} They also bear the [[Twilight Emblem]].

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, Twilight Portals appear as part of [[Midna]]'s, [[Twili Midna]]'s, and [[Zant]]'s attacks. Midna and Twili Midna both conjure yellow Twilight Portals to summon Shadow Wolves to their aid. Zant instead conjures red Twilight Portals to perform a number of attacks, including summoning [[Zant's Hand]]s and [[Zant Mask]]s.

Twilight Portals, known as '''Warp Portals''',<ref>{{Cite|A handful of {{Color|HW Green|warp portals}} exist on the Twilight Adventure map. Using them skillfully will be the key to smooth adventuring. You will not be able to use red warp portals. Win the battle to turn the portal green and gain access to its warping powers. Use the {{Color|HW Green|Ooccoo}} Item Card on green portals to warp to other areas.|Tutorial|HW}}</ref> appear as part of the [[Twilight Map]] from the {{TP|-}} DLC Pack. Red Warp Portals are scattered across the Twilight Map. When a battle square with a red Warp Portal is completed, the Warp Portals on that battle square and somewhere else on the Map turn green. An [[Ooccoo]] [[Item Card]] can then be used on the green Warp Portal to transport the Warrior to the other battle square, unlocking that square if it had not yet been unlocked.
{{Noncanon|End}}
