==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|White Goat}}
{{Plural|BotW|White Goat}} are domesticated {{Plural|BotW|Goat|link}} often found grazing at certain {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}} and [[Hateno Pasture]]. They are herbivorous and will eat any fruit, nut, vegetable, or flower if presented  with them. They will also consume any [[Mushroom]]s given to them. Their milk is used to make [[Goat Butter]] and their wool is used to make clothes.<ref>{{Cite|These goats are raised for their multiple uses. Their milk can be drank or made into butter, and their soft fur is used to make clothes.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>
