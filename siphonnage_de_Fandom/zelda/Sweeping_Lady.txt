The {{Term/Store|Sweeping Lady||Series, ALttP}} is a character in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 388}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Sweeping Lady can be found sweeping in front of her house, located west of the [[Bug-Catching Kid]]'s house in [[Kakariko Village#A Link to the Past|Kakariko Village]]. She assures [[Link]] that she believes him despite the rumors of him kidnapping [[Princess Zelda]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link. The rumors say you kidnapped the Princess, but I still trust you.|Sweeping Lady|ALttP}}</ref> In her house, there are four [[Cucco]]s and a secret room.

If Link sprinkles [[Magic Powder]] on her, she turns into a [[Fairy]]. If Link leaves the area and returns, she will be in her human form again, still able to be transformed.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 131}}</ref>
