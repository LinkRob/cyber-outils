The {{Term/Store|Royal Family's Tomb||Series, OoT}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|Royal Family's Tomb|Sign|OoT}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The large headstone at the back of the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village Graveyard|link}} conceals the entrance to the {{Term|OoT3D|Royal Family's Tomb}}. It is flanked by two smaller headstones referencing the famous {{Term|OoT3D|Composer Brothers|link}} of {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}}. The entrance to the Tomb can only be opened by playing "{{Term|OoT3D|Zelda's Lullaby|link}}" in front of the headstone, proving a connection with the {{Term|OoT3D|Royal Family|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We inscribed it on the royal tombstone. As a messenger of the Royal  Family, you should show your Royal credentials on top of the  Triforce mark...|Composer Brothers|OoT}}</ref>

The inside of the Tomb is infested with {{Plural|OoT3D|Keese|link}} and {{Plural|OoT3D|ReDead|link}}, along with pools of {{Term|OoT3D|Toxic Water|link}}. Located at the end is another tombstone inscribed with a poem written by the Composer Brothers.<ref>{{Cite|The rising sun will eventually set, A newborn's life will fade. From sun to moon, moon to sun... Give peaceful rest to the living dead. This is an interesting poem... huh?|N/A|OoT}}</ref> Right below it is the secret melody of the deceased composers, the "{{Term|OoT3D|Sun's Song|link}}."<ref>{{Cite|Something is inscribed on the tombstone... It's the secret melody of the Composer Brothers!|N/A|OoT}}</ref>
