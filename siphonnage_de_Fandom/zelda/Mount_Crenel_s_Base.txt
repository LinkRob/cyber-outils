{{Term/Store|Mount Crenel's Base||Series, TMC}} is an area in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel's Base}} is located in western {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to two other areas: {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} to the north and {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}} to the southeast.

{{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel's Base}} is the only place to obtain {{Term|TMC|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water|link}}, which {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} needs to climb further up {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} itself. Behind a destructible wall, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can find a {{Term|TMC|Business Scrub|link}} that tells him the location of an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}},<ref>{{Cite|A friend of mine in a {{Color|TMC Green|cave in the south of Trilby Highlands}} has a handy tool. If you want to climb {{Color|TMC Green|Mount Crenel}}, I suggest you go buy it from him.|Business Scrub|TMC}}</ref> needed to hold the {{Term|TMC|Mt. Crenel Mineral Water|display=Mineral Water}}.
