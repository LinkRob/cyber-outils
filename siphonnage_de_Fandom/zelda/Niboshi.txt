{{Term/Store|Niboshi||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 289}}</ref> She sells fresh [[Fish]] in [[Papuchia Village]].

==Biography==
After [[Link]] completes the [[Fire Temple]], he can talk to Niboshi, who will mention that she is in need of [[Mega Ice]] in order to keep her fish stock fresh. The young hero must purchase [[Mega Ice]] from [[Noko]] in [[Wellspring Station]] and take it back to the saleswoman. If he brings her at least 10 units of Mega Ice, she will reward the young hero with a [[Force Gem#Spirit Tracks|Force Gem]] that reveals more [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] in the [[Ocean Realm]], opening the way to the [[Pirate Hideout]] north of the village.

Link can buy 20 fish for just 50 [[Rupee]]s. If the young hero manages to bring at least 10 fish to Lucia in [[Hyrule Castle Town]], he will receive another Force Gem that recovers more Spirit Tracks in the Ocean Realm. It is not necessary to bring Mega Ice to Niboshi in order to buy fish; in fact, the woman will not accept the ice until after the Fire Temple has been completed, but she will sell Link fish as soon as he has the [[Freight Car]].
