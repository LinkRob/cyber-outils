'''Rune''' is an elf who has only appeared in the original {{TLoZ|Mishouzaki}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Biography==
{{Stub}}
He had an affair with {{Term|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Princess Zelda|link}}, despite the animosity between Elves and {{Plural|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Hylian|link}}. This affair resulted in Zelda becoming pregnant with {{Term|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Link|link}}.

Rune perished after being mortally wounded by one of {{Term|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Ganon|link}}'s {{Plural|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|Demon|link}}. Before his death, he passes his {{Term|TLoZ (Mishouzaki)|White Sword|display=sword|link}} on to someone who he thought could bring it to his son, who he had never met.
