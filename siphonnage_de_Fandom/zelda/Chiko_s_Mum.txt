{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Chiko's Mum||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Name"/>{{Name Ref|TMTP}} She lives in {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town|link}} with her daughter, {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}}.

==Appearances==
==={{FPTRR|-}}===
When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} returns {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}}'s {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Doll|link}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum}} will reward him. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can ask her for information several times. By speaking to her throughout the game, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can obtain two perfume {{Plural|FPTRR|Recipe|link}}: {{Term|FPTRR|Recipes|section=One Love|display=One Love|link}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Recipes|section=Endless Love|display=Endless Love|link}}. She also offers important advice on how to complete the {{Term|FPTRR|Town Guard|link}}'s storyline. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} gives enough {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} to the wishing well and {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}} sees the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy|link}} that emerges from it, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can ask for a reward up to 30,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} from {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum}}. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can sell several {{Plural|FPTRR|Item|link}} to {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum}} for good prices.

==={{TMTP|-}}===
In {{TMTP}}, {{Term|TMTP|Chiko's Mum}} is a fortune teller.
