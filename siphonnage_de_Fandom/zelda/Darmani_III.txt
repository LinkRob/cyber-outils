{{Term/Store|Darmani III||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 197}}</ref> also known as '''Darmani the Third''',<ref>{{Cite|I am Darmani the Third. The blood of proud Goron heroes runs in me.|Darmani|MM}}</ref> is a character in {{MM}}, and is the [[Termina|Terminan]] counterpart of [[Darunia]].

==Biography==
In life, Darmani III was a renowned [[Goron]] hero. He was considered the greatest warrior of his tribe and possible heir to the {{Term|MM3D|Goron}} throne.<ref>{{Cite|This feels strange for me to say, but when I was alive, I was a renowned warrior and veteran.|Darmani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Goron who makes calm decisions, yet boasts courageous determination and can take care of all shall be the patriarch! The chosen one, Darmani...is you! There could be no other!|Goron Elder|MM}}</ref> He suffered a terrible accident when he ventured to [[Snowhead Temple]] to find and stop the [[Goht|culprit]] that was causing the devastating winter that threatened his people. On his way to the {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead Temple|display=Temple}}, {{Term|MM3D|Darmani III|display=Darmani}} was knocked off the path and into the deep valley below by the blizzard caused by [[Biggoron]] and unfortunately lost his life.<ref>{{Cite|I was fine until I marched off to Snowhead by myself, hoping that I could drive off a demon. It had been wreaking havoc on Goron Village...Then the blizzard at Snowhead blew me into the valley...And now...Here I am...|Darmani|MM}}</ref> 

In his death, Darmani was doomed to haunt his somber grave in the [[Goron Graveyard]]. He became full of regrets and felt sorrow for failing his people. Although he cannot normally be seen with the naked eye as he is now a spirit, [[Link]] can find Darmani with the [[Lens of Truth]] at the [[Goron Village]], where he will tell the young [[Hero of Time]] to follow him to the Goron Graveyard (where he becomes fully visible) before explaining why he died. Link then must heal his spirit by playing the "[[Song of Healing]]" to free him from his regrets. Upon doing so, Darmani III sees visions of when he was a famous hero and the Gorons cheering him on, alongside Link himself. Before passing on, {{Term|MM3D|Darmani III|display=Darmani}} decides to leave his own powers in the [[Goron Mask]] for the fellow [[Hylian]] hero, which allows Link to transform into [[Goron Link]] when worn.

When spring returns to {{Term|MM3D|Snowhead|link}} upon the defeat of {{Term|MM3D|Goht|link}}, the [[Goron Elder]] announces to Link his plans to step down as patriarch of the tribe and hand the position over to {{Term|MM3D|Darmani III}}, whom he mistakes Goron Link for due to his resemblance.<ref>{{Cite|This time, I, the protector of this tribe for so many years, choose to yield the seat of tribal patriarch to a younger one. The Goron who makes calm decisions, yet boasts courageous determination and can take care of all shall be the patriarch! The chosen one, Darmani...is you! There could be no other! ...What do you think? If it's you, everyone would happily accept you as the patriarch.|Goron Elder|MM}}</ref>

{{Term|MM3D|Darmani III}} also enjoyed the {{Plural|MM3D|Goron Races|link}} while alive and was a strong contender, as revealed by the [[Elder's Son|Goron Elder's son]].<ref>{{Cite|...You used to like racing more than you liked gobbling up a third helping of rock sirloin...|Goron Elder's Son|MM}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Darmani III has a large scar on his stomach that is not featured on [[Goron Link]]. However, it is visible on the statue left behind when {{Term|MM3D|Link}} plays the "{{Term|MM3D|Elegy of Emptiness|link}}" as a {{Term|MM3D|Goron}}. This may be a battle scar, or perhaps the very wound from when he fell to his death.
**His own scar resembles that of [https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Sagat Sagat]'s from [[Capcom]]'s ''[https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Street_Fighter_series Street Fighter]'' fighting game series, whom has his on his chest by [https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Ryu Ryu]'s powerful [https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Shoryuken Shoryuken] in the events of the [https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Street_Fighter_(game) original game].
***The bead necklace Darmani wears is also similar to that of [https://streetfighter.fandom.com/wiki/Akuma Akuma]'s from said series, who, like Darmani himself, is a dark skinned muscular warrior.

* Darmani shares a similar fate with [[Mikau]] in that both are heroes of a non-[[Hylian]] race that have sadly perished through accidents and have given their souls onto Link through [[transformation]] [[Mask|Masks]] to make him fulfill their final wishes.
* Darmani, unlike other Gorons including Goron Link, has gray skin rather than the usual light brown. He also has many tattoos around his body.
* Darmani III being a Goron hero who died and is filled with regrets is coincidentally similar to the Hero of Time himself, who became the [[Hero's Spirit]] in {{TP}}.
* It is likely he was told by [[Kaepora Gaebora]] about Link's presence, and Darmani tells the young hero that the "Soaring one" would come and heal his sorrows.
* Darmani as a Goron hero himself is likely a reference to Darunia from {{OoT}} being a descendant of such as explained by [[Link (Goron)]].
