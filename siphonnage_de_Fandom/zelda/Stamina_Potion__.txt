==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSHD|Stamina Potion +}} are an upgraded version of the regular {{Plural|SSHD|Stamina Potion|link}}. They can be infused from a regular {{Term|SSHD|Stamina Potion}} for a fee of 20 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Infusion Shop|link}} and require the following {{Plural|SSHD|Bug|link}}: three {{Plural|SSHD|Volcanic Ladybug|link}}, two {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Stag Beetle|link}}, two {{Plural|SSHD|Gerudo Dragonfly|link}}, and a {{Term|SSHD|Faron Grasshopper|link}}.

When consumed, a Stamina Potion + prevents its user's [[Stamina Wheel#Skyward%20Sword|Stamina Gauge]] from depleting for three minutes.<ref name="Obtention" />
