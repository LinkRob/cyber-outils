==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|SSHD}}
A {{Term|SSHD|Small Seed Satchel}} is obtained from {{Term|SSHD|Bucha|link}} alongside the {{Term|SSHD|Slingshot|link}} and can hold 10 {{Plural|SSHD|Deku Seed|link}}. Additional {{Plural|SSHD|Small Seed Satchel}} can be bought from {{Term|SSHD|Rupin|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}, but each one takes up a slot in the {{Term|SSHD|Adventure Pouch|link}}, making a maximum of eight {{Plural|SSHD|Seed Satchel}} possible to hold at once. 

A {{Term|SSHD|Small Seed Satchel}} can be obtained by activating a {{Term|SSHD|Goddess Cube|link}} on {{Term|SSHD|Eldin Volcano|link}} and the {{Term|SSHD|Goddess's Treasure Chest|link}} can be found west of {{Term|SSHD|Fun Fun Island|link}}.
