{{Term/Store|Mountain Minish|Mountain Minish|Series, TMC|plural}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref> also known as '''Mountain Picori''',<ref>{{Cite|Mountain Picori|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> are a type of {{Term|Series|Minish|link}} in {{TMC}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Mountain Picori}}
The {{Plural|TMC|Mountain Minish}} are a group of seven {{Plural|TMC|Minish|link}} that followed {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}} to {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|These seven students followed Melari from Minish Village to Mount Crenel.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> They now live in {{Term|TMC|Melari's Mine|link}}, where they hunt for {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} and other ores and minerals by digging into the walls.<ref>{{Cite|He lives with his seven apprentices in Mount Crenel, which contains all the mineral riches they will ever need.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Green|Master Melari}} and his seven apprentices can help you. They live in the mountains, where they can find all the ore they need.|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref>

Four of the {{Plural|TMC|Mountain Minish|link}} have {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Yeah, sure. Why not?|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref>

The {{Plural|TMC|Mountain Minish}} often sing the {{Term|TMC|Ting Tong|link}} song,<ref>{{Cite|The Ting Tong song can be sung only by full-grown mountain men! It warms the heart, doesn't it? Fills you with energy? Try singing a round!|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ting tong, tong along!♪ Deep down, dig down!♪ Dig joy in the ground!♪|Mountain Minish|TMC}}</ref>
