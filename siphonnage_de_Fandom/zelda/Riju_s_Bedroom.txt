{{Term/Store|Riju's Bedroom||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= Dark Horse Books |page= 321}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom}} is the room in which {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} stays, at the top floor of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}}. Inside can be found {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Chief's Diary|link}}, and several {{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom}} at night, he will find her playing with her {{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy}}.<ref>{{Cite|What should we play?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, she will become embarrassed and tell him to ignore the {{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy|display=Toys}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what?! The sand-seal stuffed animals? Don't worry about them! Just... Just ignore them, OK?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She then scolds {{Term|BotW|Link}} for entering someone's room so late at night.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, town hero or no, you shouldn't just come wandering into someone's room at this time of night!|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

During {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|link}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom}}. According to {{Term|BotW|Riju}}, it was discovered after {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} was calmed.<ref>{{Cite|You wish to speak of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}}? Hm, what an interesting request. After {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} was tamed, we located {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa's diary}}. Since you're here, I shall tell you something from it.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> When asked about the {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|display=''Diary''}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} explains that it is on the sofa in her room, and since {{Term|BotW|Link}} was friends with {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}, she allows him to read it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa's diary}} is in {{Color|BotW Blue|my room}}. Since she trusted you, I'm certain she wouldn't mind you reading it.|Riju|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa's diary}} is on the sofa. Since she trusted you, I'm certain she wouldn't mind you reading it.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>
