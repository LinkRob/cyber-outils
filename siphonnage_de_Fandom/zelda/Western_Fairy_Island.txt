{{Term|TWWHD|Western Fairy Island}} is a small, heart-shaped island located in quadrant C1 of the Great Sea. The entrance to a shell on the island is blocked by a ring of fire, though by hitting a plunger with the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}}, the fire will recede, allowing Link to enter and receive a [[Quiver]] upgrade from the Great Fairy within.
