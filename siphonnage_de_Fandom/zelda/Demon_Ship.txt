{{Term/Store|Demon Ship|Demon Ships|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Demon Ship|Shipyard|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Stone Ship|book= E |publisher= |page= 284}}</ref> The name refers to both the individual {{Term|PH|Ship Part|link}} and the complete Ship.

==Location and Uses==
==={{Term|PH|Ship Part}}===
{{Plural|PH|Demon Ship}} are {{Plural|PH|Hull|link}} belonging to the completed {{Term|PH|Demon Ship}} of the same name. They are said to ferry evil creatures.<ref>{{Cite|A scary ship said to ferry the foulest of evils.|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> Just like most {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can collect multiple {{Plural|PH|Demon Ship}}. They can be obtained through various methods:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Methods}}

Attaching three, six, or all eight {{Term|PH|Demon Ship}} parts to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} will increase its stamina to five, six, or seven {{Plural|PH|Heart}}, respectively. {{Plural|PH|Demon Ship}} can be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} for either 50, 150, or 800 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}. Their value is determined randomly when the [[Saving|Save]] file is created, and is the same for all {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} set.

===Ship===
The {{Term|PH|Demon Ship}} is the name given to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} once it has been outfitted with the following {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Demon Ship}}

The {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} can be attached to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} at any time by visiting the {{Term|PH|Shipyard|link}} on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}. For every three parts of the {{Term|PH|Demon Ship}} used, the durability of the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} is increased by one Heart. When all eight parts are used together, they increase its durability by three Hearts, up to a total of seven Hearts. Aside from this boost in durability, the {{Term|PH|Demon Ship}} is merely a change in appearance to suit the player's personal tastes.
