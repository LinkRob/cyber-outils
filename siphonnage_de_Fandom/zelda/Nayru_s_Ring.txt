{{Term/Store|Nayru's Ring|Nayru's Rings|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Nayru's Ring}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Nayru's Ring}} are equipment that act as the ring version of {{Term|CoH|Nayru's Love|link}} spell, protecting their users from damage and can reflect any attack back at the enemy, but can be equipped by either {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Reflects damage done to you from any enemy. One use only.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> But unlike {{Term|CoH|Nayru's Love}}, they can only be used once and will disappear from the hero's inventory after being activated.

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or slain minibosses.
