The '''Kakariko Village Shop''' is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP, AST}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} can access the {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village Shop}} in the south-central area of {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village}}. The building is a small, windowless hut with {{Plural|ALttP|Cucco|link}} roaming the yard. The {{Term|ALttP|Hooded Shopkeeper|link}} inside sells {{Term|ALttP|Link}} few {{Plural|ALttP|Item}} and {{Plural|ALttP|Shop}} ran by identical shopkeepers selling identical wares can be found on {{Term|ALttP|Death Mountain}} and near {{Term|ALttP|Lake Hylia}}. The {{Plural|ALttP|Red Potion}} at all three {{Plural|ALttP|Shop}} sell for 150 {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee|link}}, slightly higher than the 120 {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee}} at the {{Term|ALttP|Witch's Hut|link}}.
