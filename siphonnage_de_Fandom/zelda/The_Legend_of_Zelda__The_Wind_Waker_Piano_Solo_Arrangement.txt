'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker Piano Solo Arrangement''''' (ゼルダの伝説風のタクトオリジナル・サウンド・トラックス) is a sheet music book arranged for the piano. Released on April 30, 2003<ref name=quote>[http://benippon.com/en/legend-of-zelda-wind-waker-original-soundtrack-piano-solo-arrangement-book-sheet-music Benippon.com]</ref>, it contains 200 pages of sheet music from {{TWW}}.<ref>[http://zeldapower.com/index.php/downloads/sheet_music/the_legend_of_zelda_-_wind_waker.php Zelda Power - The Legend of Zelda: Wind Waker]</ref>
