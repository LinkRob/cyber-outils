The {{Term/Store|Check Skulltulas||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} screen is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Check Skulltulas|Warrior Info|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Check Skulltulas}} screen can be accessed from the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior Info|link}} menu before starting a battle or during a battle. The {{Term|HWDE|Check Skulltulas}} screen allows the player to read what objectives must be fulfilled to make a {{Term|HWDE|Gold Skulltula|link}} appear during the battle.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 5}}</ref> Every {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} will have a {{Term|HWDE|Gold Skulltula}} that will appear after 1,000 {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}} are defeated.<ref>{{Cite|KO 1,000 enemies!|Check Skulltulas|HWDE}}</ref> If a {{Term|HWDE|Gold Skulltula}} has been defeated, the {{Term|HWDE|Check Skulltulas}} menu will put a golden stamp reading "Got it!" on the {{Term|HWDE|Gold Skulltula}} objective.<ref>{{Cite|Got it!|Check Skulltulas|HWDE}}</ref> If a battle in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}} or {{Term|HWDE|Challenge Mode|link}} without a {{Term|HWDE|Gold Skulltula}} is played, the option to go to the {{Term|HWDE|Check Skulltulas}} menu will appear in the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior Info}} menu, but selecting it will do nothing.
