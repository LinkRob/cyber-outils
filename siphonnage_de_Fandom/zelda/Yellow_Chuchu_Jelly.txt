==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP|-}}, Yellow Chu Jelly oozes from [[Yellow Chu]]s when they are defeated. It can be scooped up into a [[Bottle]] and functions exactly like [[Lantern Oil]], refilling the [[Lantern]] fully when used. Yellow Chu Jelly will also immediately refill the Lantern if it is used to scoop up the Jelly. Yellow Chu Jelly is the only [[Chu Jelly]] that cannot be drunk by [[Link]] when stored in a bottle.

==={{BotW|-}}===
Yellow Chuchu Jelly is dropped by [[Electric Chuchu]]s when killed. It is also dropped by other Chuchus if these are killed with electric weapons, and can be created when regular Chuchu Jelly is exposed to electricity. When struck by physical attacks, it releases a spherical electrical pulse. Like other {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}}, they can be combined with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Yellow [[Dye]] or sold to [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] for [[Mon]].

The {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor (Set)|link}} set requires Yellow Chuchu Jellies to be upgraded to its first and second tiers at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
