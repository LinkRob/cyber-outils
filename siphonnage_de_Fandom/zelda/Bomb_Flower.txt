{{Term/Store|Bomb Flower|Bomb Flowers|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, OoS, OoA, TWW, TWWHD, FSA, PH, ST, SS, SSHD, TFH|plural}}, also named '''Bomb Plants''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW |publisher= Prima |page= 10}}</ref> are plants with an explosive fruit that serves as a substitute for [[Bomb]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 118 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM, OoS, OoA, TWW, FSA, PH, ST, SS, TFH}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So what then? You here to pick {{Color|SSHD Gold|bomb flowers}}?|Kortz|SSHD}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
In most games, the fruit of the Bomb Flower will explode after a short time once picked from the plant. Just like Bombs, they flash red when near the point of detonation. Bomb Flower fruits detonate automatically in the presence of fire or when caught in an explosion, which may cause a chain reaction should there be other Bomb Flowers nearby. Slashing it with [[Link]]'s [[Sword]] or piercing it with an [[Arrow]] also cause its explosion. 

Earlier versions of the Bomb Flower are sensitive to light, and only grow in darker places.<ref name="dark">{{Cite|They usually grow only in dark places, so Bomb Flowers that grow in a place like this are extremely rare.|Goron|OoT}}</ref> In later reincarnations, Bomb Flowers seem to have become more tolerant of sunlight, as they are more common throughout the world. In certain appearances, Bomb Flowers are considered a special token of the [[Goron]]s, who tend the flowers in the mountainous region in which they appear. 

==Mainline appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Bomb Flowers first appear in {{OoT}}, appearing exclusively on [[Death Mountain]]. Referred to by the [[Goron]]s as their "special crop," Bomb Flowers are said to be "mining plants," and the fruit of the flower is the raw material for regular [[Bomb]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Those plants growing over there are Bomb Flowers. They are "mining plants" that grow only on this mountain. The flower's fruit is the raw material for bombs.|Goron|OoT}}</ref> When [[Link]] is a child, Bomb Flowers are too heavy for him to lift without the aid of the [[Goron's Bracelet]].<ref>{{Cite|If you have the Goron's Bracelet, even a little kid like you could easily pick it with (A).|Goron|OoT}}</ref> Although very rare on the mountain itself, Bomb Flowers grow wildly within [[Dodongo's Cavern]], as the warmth and dim lighting presents ideal conditions for their growth.<ref>{{Cite|Because the light inside is very dim, the Bomb Flowers, a plant unique to this mountain, grow like crazy in there!|Goron|OoT}}</ref><ref name="dark"/> Therefore, it causes some distress among the Gorons when Dodongo's Cavern becomes infested with "ancient creatures", the [[Dodongo]], making the caverns a dangerous place and affecting the Goron's Bomb Flower harvest.<ref>{{Cite|Ancient creatures have infested the Dodongo's Cavern! We've had a poor harvest of our special crop, Bomb Flowers!|Darunia|OoT}}</ref> Above the entrance to the cavern, near [[Goron City]], stands a lone Bomb Flower, and a Goron devotedly protecting this rare flower from the sun.<ref>{{Cite|I'm standing here to shade the Bomb Flowers from the sun.|Goron|OoT}}</ref> With the Goron Bracelet, young Link is able to throw the fruit of the Bomb Flower down to the entrance of Dodongo's Cavern, which has been blockaded by boulders. The Bomb will destroy the boulders, allowing Link to enter.

Additionally, Bomb Flowers can be found next to most doors and in hallways in Goron City, where they are required to blow up the rocks blocking the warp to the [[Lost Woods]]. They are also seen growing in the [[Fire Temple]], suggesting they can even survive in volcanic temperatures. Their fibers are claimed to be one of two materials from which [[Goron Tunic]]s are made, the other being [[Dodongo]] scales, in order for them to be heat-resistant.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Fashioned from a sporty blend of Dodongo hide and Bomb Flower fibers, the Goron Tunic allowed Link to survive the intense heat generated within Death Mountain Crater.|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160308195651/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/g.jsp|title=The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia|site=Zelda.com|type=jsp}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Bomb Flowers act in exactly the same way as in {{OoT|-}}, except Link no longer requires the Goron Bracelet to pick them up. They make a minor appearance in the [[Woodfall Temple]] and [[Snowhead Temple]], where they can be used to solve puzzles if Link has not obtained the [[Bomb Bag]] or [[Blast Mask]]. They can also be used as a way to stun [[Odolwa]], as well as distract and kill the moths that he sends after Link. In the [[Goron Race]]s, Bomb Flowers appear as obstacles, and will explode if [[Goron Link]] makes contact with them, slowing him down.

==={{OoS|-}} / {{OoA|-}}===
Bomb Flowers appear once again in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, and are important to each of the games' plots. However, in {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, they are portrayed as much larger, powerful Bombs reminiscent of [[Powder Keg]]s. Although Bomb Flowers are mentioned to grow in abundance in the areas in which they appear, only one is seen in either game. It is also said that they take a long time to grow, unlike in {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}} where they grew back almost instantly.<ref>{{Cite|Bomb Flowers grow slowly over a long period of time.|Goron|OoS}}</ref>

In {{OoA|-}}, Bomb Flowers appear once again as the pride of the Goron people, who live on [[Rolling Ridge]].<ref name="famous">{{Cite|A young boy brought Bomb Flowers to Rolling Ridge. Thanks to him, Bomb Flowers are now the product that makes Rolling Ridge famous.|Goron|OoA}}</ref> In the past age, Link brought a single Bomb Flower to the Gorons, who used it to free the [[Goron Elder]] from under a heavy boulder.<ref>{{Cite|The Elder is buried alive! But Gorons alone can't dig him out! If we had the legendary Bomb Flower, we could blast the rocks away...|Goron|OoA}}</ref> After the ordeal, the Gorons proceeded to plant their newly acquired Bomb Flowers all across Rolling Ridge.<ref>{{Cite|We shall take good care of the Bomb Flowers you brought us. We shall fill the peaks with them!|Goron Elder|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Elder is traveling around Rolling Ridge planting Bomb Flowers!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Due to Link having introduced the Bomb Flower to the Goron race, Link was credited with bringing the Bomb Flower to the Gorons.<ref name="famous"/> Centuries later, the [[Great Moblin]] builds his [[Moblin's Keep|Keep]] on Rolling Ridge and hoards the Goron's Bomb Flowers. Infiltrating the fortress, defeating the Great Moblin and subsequently destroying his keep allows the Gorons to grow their famous Bomb Flowers freely once again.<ref>{{Cite|Is the Great Moblin gone? Now we can grow Bomb Flowers without a care in the world!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> As thanks for his help, the Gorons give Link a Bomb Flower of his very own.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know who you are or where you're from, but thank you for getting rid of the Great Moblin. That wretch was keeping us from growing Bomb Flowers. Now we can grow Bomb Flowers without any worries! Take one as a sign of our thanks.|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Link brings this Bomb Flower to the past, thus creating a stable time loop.

In {{OoS|-}}, a Bomb Flower is sought after by the [[Subrosian Smelter]], who wishes to blow up a rock blocking the path to the [[Temple of Seasons|Tower of Autumn]] in order to obtain some [[Ore]]. Although many Bomb Flowers are said to grow in [[Subrosia]], all the other Bomb Flowers are not yet in bloom.<ref>{{Cite|I want to break this ore-filled rock, but it's too hard. A Bomb Flower could break it, but they aren't in bloom. Some guy down south says he saw one, though.|Subrosian Smelter|OoS}}</ref> It can be found in a portion of [[Lava Lake]] in the south that can only be accessed with [[Roc's Feather]].<ref>{{Cite|I've seen a Bomb Flower. I saw it at Lava Lake down south.|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
Bomb Flowers in {{TWW}} seem to be more common than in past appearances and no longer require shaded areas to grow. They can be found on several islands, most notably on [[Dragon Roost Island]], where they are said to act as a dangerous obstacle to the [[Rito]].<ref>{{Cite|As you're from off-island, allow me tell you about the Dragon Roost. Even in the best of times, it's a dangerous place with all those bomb flowers growing everywhere.|Rito|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We used to have to worry only about bomb flowers when we went to get our scales.|Rito|TWW}}</ref>. Particular withered Bomb Flowers can be found along the banks of the [[Dragon Roost Pond]], and need to be watered (using a [[Bottle]] filled with [[Water]]) to grow into usable Bombs.<ref>{{Cite|The pond came from this bubbling spring. It was such a beautiful little pond... Now it's so arid that even the bomb flowers have completely dried up.|Rito|TWW}}</ref>

==={{FSA|-}}===
Bomb Flowers are common items in {{FSA}}. They serve as a substitute for regular Bombs when these cannot be obtained until later on in the level. Stages with Bomb Flowers include: [[Hyrule Castle (Four Swords Adventures)|Hyrule Castle]], [[Eastern Temple]], [[Death Mountain Foothills]], [[The Mountain Path]], [[The Swamp]], [[Temple of Darkness]], [[Desert of Doubt (Stage)|Desert of Doubt]], [[Frozen Hyrule (Stage)|Frozen Hyrule]], [[Temple of Ice]], and [[Palace of Winds]].

==={{PH|-}}===
Bomb Flowers in {{PH}} resemble the ones seen in {{TWW|-}}, and continue to serve as early substitutes for Bombs. Bomb Flowers in these games are seemingly more fragile, as, unlike past appearances, they automatically detonate on contact with a Sword or an Arrow, something which would have previously triggered a delayed reaction. 

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Bomb Flowers are similar to their appearance in {{PH|-}}. A particular type of Bomb Flower appears in the [[Battle Mode]], and it permanently flashes red and will only explode once thrown.

==={{SS|-}}===
[[File:SS Bomb Render.png|60px|right]]
In {{SS}}, Bomb Flowers appears somewhat smaller than in their past appearances. Also, when Link picks one up, a line will appear from the Bomb, and then curve to the floor, showing the path the Bomb Flower will take once thrown by Link. Bomb Flowers can also be picked up and stored for later in Link's inventory once he has obtained the Bomb Bag, essentially becoming Bombs. They are very common and grow all throughout [[Hyrule|The Surface]], being able to survive in extreme environments such as [[Lanayru Desert]].

[[File:Big Bomb Flower.png|175px|right|thumb|The Big Bomb Flower]]

The Bomb Flowers used on the [[Groosenator]] are about three times the size of the common variety. These special Bomb Flowers can be found in a small alcove next to the track, which can be accessed until the third fight with [[The Imprisoned]]. They can be picked up and thrown, but not stored.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Main|Big Bomb Flower}}
In {{ALBW}}, a special type of Bomb Flower known as the [[Big Bomb Flower]] appears in southwest [[Lorule]]. It can be borrowed from the [[Bomb Flower Store]] south of the [[Thieves' Town]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s, and is necessary to access the [[Swamp Palace (A Link Between Worlds)|Swamp Palace]]. A second Big Bomb Flower later appears in [[Lorule Castle]], where it must be used to access a room containing a [[Moldorm (Boss)|Moldorm]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, Bomb Flowers function the same as in past games, exploding only a few seconds after being plucked, and regrowing soon after. They appear the same size as normal Bombs, but if one is picked by Link while he is wearing the [[Big Bomb Outfit]], the Bomb Flower doubles in size, and is given an increased blast radius and damage output. Even if the Bomb Flower is picked by a Link not wearing the Big Bomb Outfit, it enlarges if the Link wearing it holds it before it explodes.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, Bomb Flowers can be found growing in caves across the Kingdom. They are placed into Link's inventory, and can be fused to arrows or weapons. They bear a similar appearance to earlier installments of the series and can explode whenever a weapon strikes them or when they are shot from a bow. While they can be found in any cave, they are often found more frequently in [[Sky]] island caves and [[The Depths]]. If they're used or placed in extreme heat environments where Link takes damage, they will explode immediately.

==In other media==
==={{PH|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In the {{PH|Himekawa}}, Bomb Flowers are found growing on the Isle of Gust. When [[Cyclok]] attacks [[Linebeck]], Link, [[Ciela]], and the girl they found, Linebeck attempts to return to [[SS Linebeck|his ship]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'm going back to the ship!|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 87}}</ref> The captain holds onto a Bomb Flower to avoid being sucked into the cyclone. He and the Bomb Flower are pulled into the winds and the plant detonates. Inspired by this, Link decides to throw the Bomb Flowers into the swirling wind.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I know... I'll throw one of these into the swirling wind!|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 87}}</ref> Using the Bomb Flowers, he successfully explodes Cyclok. 
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Spinof appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Bomb Flowers can be found lined against walls in certain [[Keep]]s in {{HW}}. They can be used to blow up enemies in Keeps when Warriors attack them or push enemies into them. Once detonated, Bomb Flowers will not grow back. When equipped with the [[Gauntlets]], Link can pick up and toss Bomb Flowers towards enemies. Two types of Bomb Flowers appear; the most prevalent type resembles the Bomb Flowers from {{OoT}} whereas Bomb Flowers in [[Skyloft (Stage)|Skyloft]] and the [[Sealed Grounds (Hyrule Warriors)|Sealed Grounds]] resemble their iterations from {{SS}}.

Bomb Flowers do not appear as objects in Keeps in {{HW|L}}, appearing only when used by the [[Groosenator]]. However, Bomb Flowers make a reappearance in {{HW|DE}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
