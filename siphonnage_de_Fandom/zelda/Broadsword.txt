==Location and Uses==
==={{ZA|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The {{Term|ZA|Broadsword}} is not a physical {{Term|ZA|Sword|link}}, but rather the name of a Spell. It allows {{Term|ZA|Zelda|link}} to shoot flying {{Plural|ZA|Sword}} from her {{Term|ZA|Wand|link}}, and is received from {{Term|ZA|Sir Basil|link}}. It is particularly effective against certain knights in the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Strength|link}}, who can only be defeated from this {{Term|ZA|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|display=Spell|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|CoH|Broadsword}} are {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} in {{CoH}}. They deal damage to any {{Term|CoH|Enemy|link}} in the three spaces in front of the wielder, and will attack enemies to the side even if there is no enemy directly in front. The legendary version of the {{Term|CoH|Broadsword}} is {{Term|CoH|Fragarach|link}}, which deals 2 damage and pierces {{Term|CoH|Armor|link}}.
{{Ambig|End}}
