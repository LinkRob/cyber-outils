==Characteristics==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Link and Link's Shadow.png|thumb|232x232px|Link vs. Link's Shadow, in {{TAoL|-}}]]
{{Term|TAoL|Link's Shadow}} appears as the [[Final Boss]] of {{TAoL}} after [[Link]] defeats [[Thunderbird]] in the [[Great Palace]]. He is an evil refection of Link, and serves as the final test before the hero can obtain the [[Triforce of Courage]] and wake [[Princess Zelda]]. Link's Shadow is the most difficult opponent to appear during Link's second quest to save [[Hyrule]], as he is notoriously capable of mirroring his every move with the exception of [[Item|items]].

The strategy for Link's Shadow is to attack him where his guard is down. Another, rather infamous strategy that many mistaken for a glitch is to duck in the very left corner of the arena and repeatedly stab when he gets close. However, this is actually a flaw from the enemy's AI exclusively in the international release of the game and is discouraged by some players, as it takes away the challenge.

The Shadow's appearances in Event Matches during {{SSBM}} and {{SSBB}} of the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] are largely based upon this battle. This battle also formed the basis of the Streetpass Battle Mode of {{ALBW}}, marking only the second time that music from {{TAoL|-}} returned to use in another [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'']] game, the first being in {{TMC}}.

Since {{OoT}}, Link's Shadow became popularly known as [[Dark Link|'''Dark Link''']]. Some games have a variant known as [[Shadow Link]], but this powerful enemy does not originate from Link's own shadow. Ironically, [[NES Remix 2]] refers to Link's Shadow as "Shadow Link".

==Trivia==
[[File:TLoZ Link Kneeling Artwork 3.png|thumb|The original artwork used for Link's Shadow from {{TLoZ|-}}]]
*The official artwork of Link's Shadow in {{TAoL|-}} is a darkened version of one of [[Link]]'s poses from {{TLoZ}}.
*Link's Shadow was inspired by the shadow of Disney's [https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Peter_Pan_(character) Peter Pan], whom Link himself is based on according to [[Shigeru Miyamoto]].
*In his final battle in ''The Adventure of Link'', Link's Shadow dodges or blocks most of the attacks that Link attempts but is vulnerable when he jumps. This flaw can be taken advantage of by simply standing in a corner and repeatedly swinging Link's [[Sword]] toward Link's Shadow. The dark hero will attempt to jump at his counterpart and keep running into the attacks, making a rather very difficult battle seem less challenging. However, this does not work in the original Japanese [[Family Computer Disk System|Famicom Disk System]] version, as the A.I. was simplified when the game was transferred to cartridge format for the international release.
*Although Link's Shadow in itself has not reappeared in any {{TLoZ|Series}} games (not counting [[Dark Link]] or [[Shadow Link]]), a similar concept for this antagonist was utilized for the [[Shadow Nightmare]] final boss in {{LA}}, which featured the titular bosses, depending on the version, either emerging from under Link (original and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|DX]]'' versions) or emerging from Link in a cutscene ([[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|''Switch'' version]]), implying they were in part formed from Link's inner nightmares. In addition, Dark Link's damage animation in {{OoT}} was reused from Link's Shadow's defeat animation.
