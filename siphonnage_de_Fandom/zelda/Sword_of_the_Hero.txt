The [[Sword of the Hero]] is an [[Item]] in {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Sword of the Hero}}<br/>A sword once wielded by a hero in an ancient age. When it’s grasped, a strange sense of nostalgia washes over you. Take it when going alone would otherwise be dangerous.|Inventory|TotK}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub}}
The Sword of the Hero is located inside a chest near a giant hollowed tree in [[The Depths]] near [[Dalite Forest]] and [[Lightroot|Muzasu Lightroot]]. If it breaks, [[Link]] can obtain a replacement by offering 100 [[Poe Soul]]s to any [[Bargainer Statue]] after owning the sword at least once. It can also be obtained by scanning a {{TLoZ}} [[Link]] [[Amiibo|amiibo]] statue using the [[Amiibo (Rune)|Amiibo Rune]]. It resembles the [[Sword#The Legend of Zelda|Sword]] wielded by Link in {{TLoZ|-}} and is noted to feel "nostalgic" when wielded.
