{{Term/Store|Lukan||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lukan|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
=== {{BotW|-}} ===
{{Term|BotW|Lukan}} first appears in {{BotW|-}} as a minor {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} NPC who can be found traveling throughout the {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)|link}}. In particular, she travels between {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}}, stopping at {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} along the way. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first meets {{Term|BotW|Lukan}}, she notes that he looks like a regular traveler, but she is unsure.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmmm... You look like your everyday traveler, but how can I be sure?|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> Lukan admits that she is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} soldier in disguise as a {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Don't tell anyone...but I'm not merchant. I'm a Gerudo Town soldier in disguise.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> Asking {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has heard of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link|display=Yiga Clan}}, {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} explains that they have been causing trouble in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, and some of them have disguised as travelers to ambush people.<ref>{{Cite|Ever heard of the Yiga Clan? They're a group of thieving bandits who have caused all sorts of trouble back in town.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I got a tip that some of them go incognito as travelers to ambush the unwary.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> The reason that {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} herself is disguised is so she can catch them when they try to attack her.<ref>{{Cite|That's why I'm dressed as a simple merchant. My mission is to blend in and stay on guard in case they strike.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> She believes that, since they were bold enough to infiltrate {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, they will likely strike travelers as well.<ref>{{Cite|They were bold enough to strike at Gerudo Town, so I wouldn't be shocked if they struck at travelers, too...|Lukan|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}, she says that the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga Clan}} is bound to attack if anyone at the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} lets their guard down.<ref>{{Cite|If anyone at this stable lets their guard down, those Yiga Clan jackals are bound to show their true colors.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} will spend her time carefully watching for suspicious people who may be {{Term|BotW|Yiga}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have to keep a careful eye out for any suspicious characters.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Those Yiga Clan jackals will pounce on anyone who lets their guard down. I can't afford to relax for even a moment.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> Sometimes, {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} be found sitting inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, though she claims that it is a bad place to rest due to the danger of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga Clan}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is a nice place to relax in the middle of a long journey, huh? WRONG! That's what the Yiga Clan WANTS you to think!|Lukan|BotW}}</ref>

As the day breaks, {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} notices that the {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} is starting to increase.<ref>{{Cite|It's getting warm.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> During the day, she remarks that it is "broiling."<ref>{{Cite|Another broiling day...|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> In the evening, she will be relieved that the weather is cooler.<ref>{{Cite|It's finally cooling off...|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> At night, she comments on how cold it is.<ref>{{Cite|Tonight's chilly again.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref>

In the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to go near the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, or else he will be struck by its {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq! Don't blame me if you go wandering the deserts and {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s lightning strikes you down.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're still hanging around here? Don't blame me when {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s lightning fries you to a crisp.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> She then proclaims that the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}} know better than to go near the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We Gerudo know better than to go anywhere near that thing.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} has heard that {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, the chief of the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}, might be doing something about {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|display=Naboris}}, but that it is still smart to stay away until then.<ref>{{Cite|I heard our chief might be doing something about {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}, but...until she does, be smart and stay away.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} also tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|display=Naboris}} has caused many problems since it showed up.<ref>{{Cite|Ever since {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} showed up again, we've had no end of problems here.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> She continues to say that everyone avoids the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, and she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} should get somewhere safe before its {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} strikes him.<ref>{{Cite|Anyone who knows anything about the desert, like my people, take pains to avoid the thing.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You should get to safety somewhere like Kara Kara Bazaar before {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s lightning strikes you down.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} again, she is surprised that he is still there, saying not to blame her if he is struck by {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're still here? Don't you understand the dangers?|Lukan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sa'oten! Don't blame me if {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s lightning strikes you down.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref>

After the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} is still dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} has stopped terrorizing the desert, but that doesn't mean it's a safe place now. Not even for us Gerudo.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> She tells him to make sure he is well-equipped before he journeys the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|display=Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you intend to travel the desert, be sure that you're equipped for the journey.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> She also warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} about the dangers of sandstorms in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, and she tells him to avoid them if he values his life.<ref>{{Cite|If you're going into the desert, traveler, listen well. There are sandstorms out there so thick you can't tell vai from voe. Don't get caught up in one if you value your life!|Lukan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|One warning if you're planning to go into the desert: there are sandstorms out there so thick you can't tell vai from voe. Don't get caught up in one if you value your life!|Lukan|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}} or {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, she claims that she has been watching her surroundings and that there is no one suspicious in the area.<ref>{{Cite|I've been watching, and I feel safe saying there's no one suspicious around here. Still, I'll keep my eyes open.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> In {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, she is paranoid that the last of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} could attack at any moment.<ref>{{Cite|I've been watching, and I feel safe saying there's no one suspicious around here. That doesn't mean I can relax, though. The surviving Yiga Clan could strike at any moment. I'll keep my eyes open.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The survivors of the Yiga Clan could show their faces at any moment. We have to be ready when they do.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> She will also tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldiers have been working to root out all surviving {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga Clan}} members, and she is doing her part as well.<ref>{{Cite|But the other soldiers are working day and night to root out the surviving Yiga Clan jackals. I've got to do my part too.|Lukan|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Lukan}} is attacked by [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemies]] while traveling, she will draw her {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} to defend herself.<ref>{{Cite|Come on!|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves her, she will thank him.<ref>{{Cite|I wouldn't have made it out of that without your help. Sarqso!|Lukan|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Chilly Mushroom Risotto|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have to thank you somehow. Is this enough?|Lukan|BotW}}</ref>

=== {{TotK|-}} ===
Lukan appears again in {{TotK}}. Her character model from the previous game is unchanged, and she retains her [[Gerudo Scimitar]] and [[Gerudo Shield]] as well. 

She is now found in South [[Hyrule Field]], just north of [[Lake Hylia]]. She walks between the north gate of the [[Bridge of Hylia]] and the base of the [[Great Plateau]], taking the path that runs next to Scout's Hill around the [[Forest of Time]] Chasm and through the [[Outpost Ruins]]. When she reaches the endpoint of her route at either the Great Plateau or the Bridge of Hylia, she will pause briefly and stand still before turning around and walking in the opposite direction. 

Much of her dialogue concerns the [[Yiga Clan]], and it seems that her purpose, as in the last game, is to search for Yiga members who may be hiding in plain sight with nefarious goals.<ref>{{Cite|I fear the Yiga Clan are hiding in plain sight, slipping in among innocent passersby to evade detection. This means I can trust no one I meet...Even a kind, wrinkly old vai may in fact be a Yiga assassin in disguise.|Lukan|TotK}}</ref>  She continues to put forth a vigilant attitude, never letting her guard down.<ref>{{Cite|I hate to cast undue suspicion on innocents, but that may be the cost of vigilance.|Lukan|TotK}}</ref>

Lukan has several situational dialogue bubbles that appear over her head at different times. Multiple of these dialogue phrases relate to the warning she gives [[Link]] when he talks to her around Lake Hylia - that members of the Yiga Clan are disguising themselves amongst fruit in the woods around [[Faron (Region)|Faron.]]<ref>{{Cite|Not even fruit is safe!|Lukan|TotK}}</ref> When she passes a platform with [[Hudson Construction|Hudson Construction Restoration Materials]], she will comment on them.<ref>{{Cite|They build houses, right?|TotK|Lukan}}</ref> When she walks by the Forest of Time Chasm, she will advise Link to be careful.<ref>{{Cite|Take care around chasms.|Lukan|TotK}}</ref> If it is raining, she will remark on it, and if it is storming, she will give a general warning about lightning.<ref>{{Cite|Ah...Rain.|Lukan|TotK}}</ref> 

If Link chooses to talk to her around the Forest of Time Chasm, she will remark that hiding in chasms seems like something the Yiga Clan would do, and furthermore, that suspicious figures have been seen in the chasms, though she isn't sure that they are in fact Yiga<ref>{{Cite|Hiding out in chasms where no one is looking - where sunlight doesn't reach - would be exactly the Yiga Clan's style.|Lukan|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sadly, we have too little information to know whether the suspicious figures seen in the chasms are Yiga. For now we will have to keep a watchful eye.|Lukan|TotK}}</ref>.  Talking to her later will possibly yield similar dialogue in which she warns Link to keep his guard up if he ever goes near a chasm.<ref>{{Cite|There's a rumor that the chasms everywhere have become hiding places for the Yiga Clan. If you ever go near a chasm, be sure to stay on your guard.|Lukan|TotK}}</ref> This is a hint towards the Yiga's true presence within the [[The Depths|Depths]]. 

If Link saves Lukan from an enemy, she will gift Link a food item as a show of gratitude.<ref>{{Cite|Well, then! A show of gratitude is in order. Will you take this?|Lukan|TotK}}</ref> These items include the [[Carrot Cake|Hasty Carrot Cake]], which grants 4 hearts, as well as a low-level speed boost that lasts 5:40, and the Meat Skewer, which also grants 4 hearts. 

Like many NPCs, Lukan will seek shelter along her usual path if it begins to rain. Talking to Lukan while she is sheltering from the rain will yield a unique dialogue in which she comments that it rarely rained in her home of [[Gerudo Town]], so sheltering from the rain feels exciting.<ref>{{Cite|It seldom rained in Gerudo Town, so sheltering from the rain like this still feels novel and exciting. I could stay here for ages, listening to that pitter-patter and never tire of it. Even after the rain stops, I might never move from this spot...|Lukan|TotK}}</ref>
