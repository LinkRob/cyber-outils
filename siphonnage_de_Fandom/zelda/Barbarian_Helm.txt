==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Barbarian Helm is a piece of [[Armor]] located in the [[Tu Ka'loh Shrine]] at [[Lomei Labyrinth Island]]. After obtaining it, [[Granté]] will begin selling the piece of Armor for 4,000 [[Rupee]]s. According to its description, the Helm was worn by the warriors of an ancient warlike tribe from the [[Faron (Region)|Faron]] [[Region]].<ref name="Inventory"/> When worn, it provides one level of Attack Up. The Barbarian Helm can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Barbarian Helm, alongside the [[Barbarian Armor]] and [[Barbarian Leg Wraps]], compose the [[Barbarian Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Charge Atk. Stamina Up [[Set Bonus]].
