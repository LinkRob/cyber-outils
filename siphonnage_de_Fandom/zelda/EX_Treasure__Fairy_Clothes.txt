"{{Term/Store|EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Outpost Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal|link}}, which is rumored to be in the {{Term|BotW|Outpost Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a journal that places the locations of the treasure somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Outpost Ruins}}...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

Inside {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal}} are clues to the locations of eight {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, each containing a priceless relic stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The Journal states the green garments of the {{Term|Series|Tingle|link|display=man who wished to be a fairy}} can be found at the ruins of a trading outpost, a prison and a village next to popular farmland.<ref>{{Cite|The green garments of the man who wished to be a fairy are hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where trade flourished}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where sinners were imprisoned}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the village next to farmland where many people once gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> 

{{Term|BotW|Tingle's Hood|link}} can be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} buried in the middle of the {{Term|BotW|Exchange Ruins|link}} The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing {{Term|BotW|Tingle's Shirt|link}} is buried near the ruins of the {{Term|BotW|Castle Town Prison|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Tingle's Tights|link}} are in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} buried under the remains of a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} in the southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Mabe Village Ruins|link}}. Each {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be dislodged from the ground using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} within. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed once all three pieces of {{Term|BotW|Tingle's Set|link}} have been obtained.<ref>{{Cite|You found the treasure that was hidden at Exchange Ruins, Castle Town Prison, and Mabe Village Ruins. You obtained {{Color|BotW Red|Tingle's Outfit}}!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
