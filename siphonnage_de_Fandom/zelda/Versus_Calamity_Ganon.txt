{{Term/Store|Versus Calamity Ganon||Series, HWAoC}} is a [[Challenge]] in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Stub}}
Versus Calamity Ganon is one of the final challenges in the game, and the challenge could be unlocked when the challenge [[Versus Astor]] is complete.

Versus Calamity Ganon is time-restricted, and the player will have 12 minutes to defeat [[Astor]], [[Harbinger Ganon]], and ultimately, [[Calamity Ganon]]. The challenge has a recommended level of 84, and once completed, Calamity Ganon will become a playable character.
