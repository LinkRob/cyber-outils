The {{Term/Store|Mad Batter||Series, ALttP, LA, LADX}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228 {{Exp Game|ALttP}} & 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref> He sleeps in altars found in underground caves, and [[Link]] may awaken him by sprinkling [[Magic Powder]] on these altars. The bat creature will then curse Link to punish him for having been released, but he inadvertently helps Link out by providing upgrades as his curse.

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, his altar is found in a hole next to the [[Dwarven Swordsmith]]s' [[Smithery]]. The hole can be reached by pounding the stake blocking the way with the [[Magic Hammer]] or by using the [[Magic Mirror]] in the Dark World to travel immediately above the well, and then jumping down. Once inside, [[Link]] can go north to an altar and sprinkle the Magic Powder. The Mad Batter will appear, who will both scold and thank Link for waking him.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Blast you for waking me from my deep, dark sleep! ...I mean, thanks a lot, sir! But now I will get my revenge on you. Get ready for it! ...Err, is that OK with you, sir?|Mad Batter|ALttP}}</ref> This confusing shift in his dialogue continues until he finally casts a spell on Link, halving his [[Magic Meter]].<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh heh! I laugh at your misfortune! Now your magic power will drop by one-half! Congratulations! Now, do you best, even though I'm sure it won't be enough! Have a nice day! See you!|Mad Batter|ALttP}}</ref> In reality, this causes Link to consume half the magic power he normally would with pertaining items.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 125}}</ref>

==={{LA|-}}===
[[File:LADX Mad Batter Sprite 2.png|left|28px|The Mad Batter in his ball of light form]]
The Mad Batter shows up in three different locations throughout {{LA}}: the [[Mysterious Woods]], [[Martha's Bay]], and [[Tal Tal Heights]]. The Mad Batter serves the same purpose wherever he is seen: to increase Link's inventory. His altars are always found underground and look like lamps. When Link tosses Magic Powder on them, the creature appears in a ball of light form before taking a bat-like form. Upset about being awakened from his sleep, the Mad Batter speaks in a purposely confusing way.<ref name="Ready">{{Cite|Hey, Kid! You woke me up from a fine nap!! ...Thanks a lot! But now, I'll get my revenge! Are you ready?!|Mad Batter|LADX}}</ref> The creature will triple Link's capacity for carrying [[Bomb]] and [[Arrow]] and double that of his Magic Powder,<ref>{{Cite|Okay, I'll let you carry more Bombs! He He He! Are you ready?!|Mad Batter|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Fine, I'll let you have more arrows! Heh Heh! Are you ready?!|Mad Batter|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll let you carry more Magic Powder! He He! Are you ready?!|Mad Batter|LADX}}</ref> thinking he's troubling the hero by burdening him with more equipment.<ref>{{Cite|Heh Heh Heh! You deserve it! Now look at all that junk you have to carry! Hah! Take care! See you again!|Mad Batter|LADX}}</ref> Link can choose whether to be "punished" or not, as the creature will ask "Are you ready?!" before he casts a spell.<ref name="Ready"/> However, if Link declines, the Mad Batter will only change the option of what equipment he will increase.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 91}}</ref>

In {{LA|NS}}, the Mad Batter is called '''Li'l Devil''' and appears more demonic;{{Name Ref|LANS}} however, his role remains virtually the same. His color was changed to a more muted brownish-purple color.
