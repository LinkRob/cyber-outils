{{Term/Store|Laruto||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is the previous [[Sage|Sage of Earth]] in {{TWW}}.<ref name="E 259"/> She belongs to the [[Zora]] race, born in [[Zora's Domain]]<ref>{{Cite|Laruto - Birthplace: Zora's Domain|Nintendo Gallery|TWW}}</ref> before the [[Great Flood]], and the only one of her race to appear in the game. Prior to its events, she is murdered by [[Jalhalla]] under orders from [[Ganondorf]] after the latter managed to circumvent the seal of the Goddesses and reached the surface of the ocean.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Hundreds of years passed, and Ganondorf, who should have been sealed at the bottom of the ocean along with Hyrule, was resurrected once again. He gave an order to his minions to attack the Wind and Earth temples, killing the two sages whose prayers gave the Master Sword the ability to destroy evil.|book=HH|page=124}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|After his defeat at the hands of the Hero of Time, Ganondorf was sealed away... but not for all time. He was revived, and he returned to Hyrule in a red wrath. He attacked this temple and stole my soul, knowing that he had to remove the power contained in that enchanted blade.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref> After her death, her spirit remained in the Earth Temple until she was replaced by her descendant, [[Medli]].

==Biography==
Laruto had served as the Sage of Earth, praying that the power to repel evil would remain within the Master Sword. Following her assassination at the hands of Jalhalla, Laruto's spirit remained in the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]], awaiting a successor.<ref>{{Cite|For an age, I offered my prayers here in the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Earth Temple}}, praying that the power to repel evil would ever remain within the Master Sword.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref> When [[Link]] visits the temple, Laruto appears before him, requesting that he find the new sage.<ref>{{Cite|In order to return the power to repel evil to your sword, you must find another to take my stead in this temple and ask the gods for their assistance.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref> Laruto gives Link a couple of hints regarding how he can recognize the new sage and teaches him the [[Earth God's Lyric]],<ref>{{Cite|You must find the one who carries on my bloodline... The one who holds this sacred {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|instrument}}...|Laruto|TWW}}</ref> the song that will awaken the new sage.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|song you just conducted}} is one which will {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|open the eyes}} of the new {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|sage}} and awaken within that sage the melody that will carry our prayers to the gods.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref>

Link leaves the temple and finds the new sage: [[Medli]]. He plays the Earth God's Lyric for her, causing her to lose consciousness. In her "dream", she is visited by Laruto and the duo play the Earth God's Lyric together. When Medli wakes up, she requests Link to take her to the Earth Temple.<ref>{{Cite|Just now, a sage spoke to me. She spoke so gently... There's something... something that I must do. Link... Thanks to you, I've been awakened to the knowledge that I'm a sage of the Earth Temple. There's actually something that I can do to help this world. It's incredible... I bet my teacher knew all about this... Link, please... You must take me to the Earth Temple.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> 

Laruto's last appearance is when Link defeats Jalhalla. When Medli plays the Earth God's Lyric, Laruto appears next to her and plays along. She disappears again when the [[Master Sword]] has regained the first half of its power, finally freed from her obligations.

==Trivia==
*Like several other characters, Link cannot take a [[Photo|pictograph]] of Laruto. To get her figurine, he must obtain her [[Legendary Pictograph]] from [[Lenzo]].<ref name=Quote1/>
*She seems to be named after, and possibly related to, [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]], the Sage of Water from [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]].

==Nomenclature==
The name Laruto could be a combination of the word lament, which is "a song of passionate sorrow or grief", and Ruto, another Zora sage. The name Laruto seems to be reference to the name [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]], which belonged to the Zora princess in {{OoT}}, who was also a sage.
