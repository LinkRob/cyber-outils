{{Term/Store|Mama's Cafe||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mama's Cafe|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe}} is located in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. In the {{Term|TMC|Cafe Rafters|link}} above the {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|display=Cafe}} live some of the {{Term|TMC|Town Minish|link}} holding a similar bar, who are being visited by some of the {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|link}} can be found here, and will {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}. Also, there is a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized bridge leading from the {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|display=Cafe}} into the back of {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}}, which allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to gain access to the back of the shop where he obtains the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|link}} to give to {{Term|TMC|Fifi|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}.

In this place, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can find {{Term|TMC|Gepper|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Mama|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Baris|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Jasmine|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Chai|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Breve|link}}, with whom he can fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}}. Outside, he can fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|June|link}}. At the end of the game, {{Term|TMC|Herb|link}} is seen outside the {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|display=Cafe}} and rewards {{Term|TMC|Link}} if he has collected all the {{Plural|TMC|Figurine|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|link}}.

==Trivia==
*While {{Term|TMC|Mama}} is the name of the proprietress of {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe}}, it's also a term to refer to a proprietress of a bar in Japan.
*The names of many of the people of {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe}} are related to drinks, similar to the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}'s relation to weather.
**''[[Wikipedia:Jasmine|Jasmine]]'' is a plant used in tea. Jasmine's Japanese name, {{Romanize|ブルマン|Buruman}}, is a blend of coffee.
**''Breve'' comes from [[wikipedia:Caffè breve|caffè breve]], a latte. Breve's Japanese name, {{Romanize|キリマン|Kiriman}}, is another blend of coffee.
**''[[Wikipedia:Malasa chai|Chai]]'' is a tea. Chai's Japanese name, {{Romanize|チーノ|Cīno}}, is a shortening of the Japanese spelling of cappuccino.
**''Baris'' comes from [[Wikipedia:Barista|barista]]. Baris's Japanese name, {{Romanize|モカ|Moka}}, comes from [[Wikipedia:Cafe mocha|mocha]].
**An ''herb'' is used for making tea. Herb's Japanese name, {{Romanize|オーレ|Ō Re}}, is the Japanese spelling of "[[wikipedia:Café au lait|au lait]]".
