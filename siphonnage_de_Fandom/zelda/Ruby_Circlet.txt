==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Ruby Circlet can be purchased in the [[Starlight Memories]] shop at [[Gerudo Town]] for 500 Rupees plus three [[Ruby|Rubies]]. When worn, it provides one level of Cold Resistance.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Ruby Circlet can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], though it cannot be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
