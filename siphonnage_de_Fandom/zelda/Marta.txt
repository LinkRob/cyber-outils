{{Term/Store|Marta||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Marta|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Marta}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She wields a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first enters the {{Term|BotW|Barracks}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Marta}} training with a practice dummy alongside {{Term|BotW|Smaude|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her, she reveals that this training was instigated by {{Term|BotW|Babi|link}}, who is watching the two of them.<ref>{{Cite|*pant pant* Babi's training is really tough, but...|Marta|BotW}}</ref> However, since the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} thieves were able to slip past her and steal the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}, she does not feel like she can complain.<ref>{{Cite|It's our fault the thieves were successful. We have no room to complain.|Marta|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives {{Term|BotW|Teake|link}}'s permission to learn about the theft from the soldiers, {{Term|BotW|Babi}} refuses to allow him to speak to {{Term|BotW|Marta}} and {{Term|BotW|Smaude}}, claiming they are in the middle of training.<ref>{{Cite|As you can see, we are in the middle of spear training! Teake's permission or no, I would ask that you not interfere!|Babi|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has recovered the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, {{Term|BotW|Marta}} will be relieved that they can finally take a break.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you bringing back the chief's heirloom, we can finally take a break...|Marta|BotW}}</ref> However, she expresses concern that {{Term|BotW|Babi}} has special training ready for them after they repair their {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Spear}}.<ref>{{Cite|In theory, anyway. More likely, once we finish repairing our spears, we'll have to get started on more special training. {{Small|*sigh* Babi's always got more special training...}}|Marta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Marta}} also feels that {{Term|BotW|Babi}} is working too hard, and she hopes that she never becomes as intense as her.<ref>{{Cite|Babi is working way too hard, even though the chief's heirloom is back! I hope I never become as intense as her...|Marta|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
