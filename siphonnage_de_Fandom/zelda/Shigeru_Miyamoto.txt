'''[[wikipedia:Shigeru_Miyamoto|Shigeru Miyamoto]]''' is a Japanese video game designer, artist and producer, and the creator of {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Biography==
During his childhood, Miyamoto enjoyed exploring the countryside and hillsides around his home, which inspired the creation of ''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' franchise.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= When I was younger, I grew up in the countryside of Japan. And what that meant was I spent a lot of my time playing in the rice paddies and exploring the hillsides and having fun outdoors. When I got into the upper elementary school ages — that was when I really got into hiking and mountain climbing. There's a place near Kobe where there's a mountain, and you climb the mountain, and there's a big lake near the top of it. We had gone on this hiking trip and climbed up the mountain, and I was so amazed — it was the first time I had ever experienced hiking up this mountain and seeing this big lake at the top. And I drew on that inspiration when we were working on the Legend of Zelda game and we were creating this grand outdoor adventure where you go through these narrowed confined spaces and come upon this great lake. And so it was around that time that I really began to start drawing on my experiences as a child and bringing that into game development.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20150619152426/https://www.npr.org/sections/alltechconsidered/2015/06/19/415568892/q-a-shigeru-miyamoto-on-the-origins-of-nintendos-famous-characters|title= Q&A: Shigeru Miyamoto On The Origins Of Nintendo's Famous Characters}}</ref> He joined [[Nintendo]] in 1977, where he began developing early [[wikipedia:Arcade_game|arcade games]] such as ''[[wikipedia:Sheriff_(video_game)|Sheriff]]'' and ''[[mariowiki:Donkey_Kong_(video_game)|Donkey Kong]]'' (his first major hit), and through his long and highly successful professional career, would become one of the most accomplished and influential video game designers in the world and is primarily responsible for making the company widely known as it is today (Nintendo before Miyamoto's employment was a small Japanese card/toy manufacturer). Shortly before starting development for ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]'', Miyamoto began developing ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' as a launch title for the [[wikipedia:Famicom_Disk_System|Famicom Disk System]] (an add-on for the Japanese version of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]]).<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= First, we started making The Legend of Zelda, and then we started Super Mario Bros. The Legend of Zelda was for the Family Computer Disk System, so we decided to finish up Super Mario Bros. first.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= https://www.nintendo.com/nes-classic/the-legend-of-zelda-developer-interview/|title= The Legend of Zelda Developer Interview}}</ref> Since then, he has worked as the producer of every game in the ''Zelda'' series. He is also the creator of other iconic Nintendo franchises such as ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'', ''[https://donkeykong.fandom.com/wiki/Donkey_Kong_(series) Donkey Kong]'', ''[[fzero:F-Zero_(series)|F-Zero]]'', ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_(series)|Star Fox]]'', and ''[[pikmin:Pikmin_Wiki|Pikmin]]'', as well as ''[https://nintendogs.fandom.com/wiki/Main_Page Nintendogs]'', and played important roles in the creation of ''[[metroidwiki:Metroid_(series)|Metroid]]'' and ''[[pokemon:Pokémon_Wiki|Pokémon]]''. In 2002, Shigeru Miyamoto served as one of Nintendo's representative directors as an executive and was promoted to the title of Fellow at Nintendo in 2015. He continues to work at Nintendo to this day.

==Trivia==
*Shockingly, Shigeru Miyamoto grew up with no television or toys as a child. He created his own toys and learned to rely on his own imagination.
*As Miyamoto is such an invaluable asset to [[Nintendo]] for decades, unlike most other employees, the company does not allow him to walk or ride a bike to work, but rather drive, instead. This is because they fear he might get hit by a car on his way to his job.
*Shigeru Miyamoto never expected that he would become a highly accomplished video game designer when he joined Nintendo in the late 1970s. When asked about his talent, he himself stated "''I feel that I have been very lucky to be a game designer since the dawn of the industry. I am not an engineer, but I have had the opportunities to learn the principles of game [design] from scratch, over a long period of time. And because I am so pioneering and trying to keep at the forefront, I have grown accustomed to first creating the very tools necessary for game creation.''"
*''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|The Adventure of Link]]'' is the only ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]'' game Miyamoto considers a failure,<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Compared to Legend of Zelda, Zelda II went exactly what we expected... All games I make usually gets better in the development process, since good ideas keep coming, but Zelda II was sort of a failure...|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= https://www.nintendoforums.com/articles/40/super-play-magazine-interviews-shigeru-miyamoto-about-zelda|title= SUPER PLAY MAGAZINE INTERVIEWS SHIGERU MIYAMOTO ABOUT THE LEGEND OF ZELDA}}</ref> due to the limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] hardware.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= I think specifically in the case of Zelda II we had a challenge just in terms of what the hardware was capable of doing, [...] So one thing, of course, is, from a hardware perspective, if we had been able to have the switch between the scenes speed up, if that had been faster, we could have done more with how we used the sidescrolling vs. the overhead [view] and kind of the interchange between the two. But, because of the limitations on how quickly those scenes changed, we weren't able to. The other thing, is it would have been nice to have had bigger enemies in the game, but the Famicom/NES hardware wasn't capable of doing that. Certainly, with hardware nowadays you can do that and we have done that, but of course nowadays creating bigger enemies takes a lot of effort.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= https://kotaku.com/shigeru-miyamotos-bad-game-514017583|title= Shigeru Miyamoto's 'Bad' Game}}</ref> It is also his least favorite game of all time that he has worked on.
**He also dislikes the art style of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' as well.
*Miyamoto himself played the mandolin in ''The Wind Waker''<nowiki/>'s title theme.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= You may recall that the opening sequence to The Wind Waker starts off with a mandolin that's played and that's actually sampled from Mr. Miyamoto playing.|name= Eiji Aonuma|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20121026024638/http://www.ign.com/articles/2004/05/20/interview-eiji-aonuma?page=3|title= The Legend of Zelda producer talks about the game, the franchise, the past and the future.}}</ref>
*As with many (but not all) fans, Miyamoto has admitted he disliked [[Navi]] the [[Fairy]].
*Due to his legendary status, Shigeru Miyamoto has often been described by many as the video game equivalent of [[wikipedia:Walt_Disney|Walt Disney]] and [[wikipedia:Stan_Lee|Stan Lee]], and arguably Nintendo's most important employee in history.
*The 1978 arcade hit ''[[wikipedia:Space_Invaders|Space Invaders]]'' is known to have inspired Shigeru Miyamoto to the video game industry.
*During the development of ''[[mariowiki:Donkey_Kong_(video_game)|Donkey Kong]]'', Miyamoto did not have his own bathtub. He could only wash himself at Nintendo's tub - using the very same hot water the company's factory used to produce their handfuda playing cards in the late 19th century.
*Surprisingly, Miyamoto's favorite video game character is '''not''' [[Mario]], [[Link]], or any other of his creations, but rather Namco's mascot, [https://pacman.fandom.com/wiki/Pac-Man Pac-Man].
*Shigeru's favorite games are often older ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series titles from the NES and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] era, specifically ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]'', ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros._2|Super Mario Bros. 2]]'', and ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_World|Super Mario World]]''. He also heavily enjoys ''[[wikipedia:Pokémon_Go|Pokemon GO]]''.
**His favorite ''The Legend of Zelda'' series game is ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'', as he said he and his team had fun developing it and he was even playing it the whole time. According to himself, ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]'' is the best ''Zelda'' title rather than ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.
**In terms of third-party games, according to ''Kotaku'' in 2020, Miyamoto is interested in ''[https://pacman.fandom.com/wiki/Pac-Man_(series) Pac-Man]'' and ''[[wikipedia:Tetris|Tetris]]''.
*Ironically, although Shigeru Miyamoto is universally known as one of the greatest video game designers and producers, he actually spends very little time playing video games himself. In his free time he prefers to play music on the guitar, mandolin, and banjo, as well as rearranging furniture. In addition, before creating ''Donkey Kong'', he initially wanted to become a manga artist.[[File:Tezuka Miyamoto and Kondo.jpg|thumb|207x207px|Miyamoto with Tezuka (left) and Kondo (right) during a ''Super Mario Maker'' promotion in 2015]]
*Fellow longtime Nintendo employees [[Takashi Tezuka]] and [[Koji Kondo]] are among Shigeru Miyamoto's best friends.
*When Miyamoto initially applied for a job at Nintendo, he wanted to be a toy designer, but was rejected. According to himself, "''I'' ''had wanted to to make toys. So I applied to Nintendo wanting to be designer, but...they weren't hiring any designers at the time."''
*Despite being an important figure in Japan and the rest of the world, Miyamoto doesn't appear on Japanese television, as to minimize his chance of being recognized. Because of this, more foreign tourists than Japanese people approach him. He also usually does not sign autographs to fans due to concern that he would be inundated (though he still allows pictures). Although he does know and speak some English, Shigeru is not fluent in this language and prefers to speak in Japanese during interviews.
*Miyamoto has made several quotes well-known to the public, such as "''Video games are bad for you? That's what they say about rock and roll.''", and "''A delayed game is eventually good, but a rushed game is forever bad.''"
*Fitting for his work in the ''Nintendogs'' games, Miyamoto himself is in fact a semi-professional dog breeder.
*Shigeru Miyamoto is a knight in the French order of arts and literature. He was officially inducted into the order in 2006, becoming only the 106th person on record to receive this honor. 
*Shigeru has claimed if Nintendo were to ever get into robotics, the first thing he will do is to create a giant Donkey Kong robot. Robot boss enemies of the character have been seen in various ''Donkey Kong'' series games.
*In 2025, Miyamoto stated that he is stepping down from the ''Mario'' series, as he hopes "to stay healthy until its 50th Anniversary (in 2035)". He revealed in an interview that he had now entrusted most of the work of new games to the younger generation, although he will still play the first 30 minutes of projects to ensure they continue to feel "like ''Mario''" to himself.
