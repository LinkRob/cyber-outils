==Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Slumber of Restoration}} is a feature of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} that was discovered shortly following the excavation of the aforementioned {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|display=Shrine}}.<ref name="Research Notes">{{Cite|I spoke with Purah about the Shrine of Resurrection we discovered earlier.<br/><br/>As we speculated, this particular shrine is, in fact, a medical facility with the power to heal.<br/><br/>It also has a long-term stasis function that can be activated and maintained until healing is complete.<br/><br/>In the war against the Calamity 10,000 years ago...were the injuries so great as to necessitate such a facility?<br/><br/>If so, I will remain uneasy until we have made all adjustments necessary to restore it to full working order.<br/><br/>I can only pray that even if Calamity Ganon returns, our battle will not require the Shrine of Resurrection's power...|Zelda's Research Notes|BotW}}</ref> When an individual is placed within the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}'s bed, they will slowly be healed by the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|display=Shrine}}.<ref name="Research Notes"/> If an individual's injuries are severe enough, they can be placed in a long-term stasis known as the {{Term|BotW|Slumber of Restoration}} wherein their body is healed but they do not age.<ref name="Research Notes"/>
