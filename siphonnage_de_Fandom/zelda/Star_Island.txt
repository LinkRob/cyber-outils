{{Term/Store|Star Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (A2) is one of the islands of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==

Located just east of the [[Forsaken Fortress]], Star Island is surrounded by explosive barrels hidden under the waves which surface as [[Link]] approaches them. Once Link reaches the island safely, he finds several large, stone boulders. Underneath one is a secret grotto, while others contain [[Rupee]]s, items and an army of [[Miniblin]]s. The cave houses several enemies, and after successfully defeating all of them, Link receives a [[Piece of Heart]].

This island is also the location [[Oakin]], one of the [[Korok]]s, chose to plant one of the [[Great Deku Tree]]'s seeds, and it is one of the islands where [[Blue ChuChu]]s can be found.
