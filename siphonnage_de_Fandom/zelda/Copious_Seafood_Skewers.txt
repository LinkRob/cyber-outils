{{Term/Store|Copious Seafood Skewers|Copious Seafood Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Copious Seafood Skewers}}<br/>It's just a whole heap of stuff shoved on to a skewer, but it's still a pretty tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Copious Seafood Skewers can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} four different types of seafood in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they'll heal some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Despite being a heap of random items shoved onto a skewer, they're known to be a tasty dish.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Because of their specified recipe, very few variations of Copious Seafood Skewers exist. They can only be made by combining a {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}}, and two different types of seafood that grant the same effect. These variations are as follows:

* '''Hearty Copious Seafood Skewers''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Copious Seafood Skewers}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>It's just a whole heap of stuff shoved on to a skewer, but it's still a pretty tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using any combination of {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}. When eaten, it will fully heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Copious Seafood Skewers''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Copious Seafood Skewers}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>It's just a whole heap of stuff shoved on to a skewer, but it's still a pretty tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. If they're eaten, they'll refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Mighty Copious Seafood Skewers''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Copious Seafood Skewers}}<br/>Grants a mid-level attack-power boost.It's just a whole heap of stuff shoved on to a skewer, but it's still a pretty tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using any combination of {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}. Eating one will grant Link a temporary boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Copious Seafood Skewers''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Copious Seafood Skewers}}<br/>Grants a mid-level defense boost.<br/>It's just a whole heap of stuff shoved on to a skewer, but it's still a pretty tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using any combination of {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. When they're eaten, Link's defense will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Copious Seafood Skewers''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Copious Seafood Skewers}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>It's just a whole heap of stuff shoved on to a skewer, but it's still a pretty tasty dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}. Eating them increases Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period of time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>
