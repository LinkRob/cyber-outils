Numerous '''manga based on ''The Legend of Zelda'' series''' have been published. {{Wp|Manga}} are comics created in Japan and typically, but not always, span multiple chapters or issues. There have been many different manga based on {{TLoZ|-}} created by multiple authors, mostly following the plot lines of various games, albeit often with condensed plots, the introduction of new characters, and occasionally the addition of original side-stories. The manga adaptations are generally considered to be non-canon to the games.

The vast majority of {{TLoZ|-}} manga were only published in Japan and do not see release in English or other languages. Most {{TLoZ|-}} manga released in English have been the adaptations by [[Akira Himekawa]], along with the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}.{{Note|{{ALttP|Ishinomori}} was originally created for and serialized in the North American magazine {{Wp|Nintendo Power}}, and can be considered to be an {{Wp|original English-language manga}}. It was eventually released in Japan following its completion.}}

{{Wp|Manhua}} are the Chinese equivalent of Manga. Only one {{TLoZ|-}} manhua has been produced, {{OoT3D|Ming|-}}.

{{Wp|Yonkoma|4-koma}} are a type of manga that divide their story into four-panel sections, similar to a comic strip. They typically prioritize gag comedy over standard story development.
