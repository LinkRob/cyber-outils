'''Automatons''' are creature-like constructs that can be created after assisting [[Dampé|Dampe]], in {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
Automatons are mechanical constructs created by Dampé and given to [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] after helping with “Inspiration”. These machines serve as mechanized versions of Enemies, unlike [[Echo|Echoes]], Automatons do not require a summoning cost; however, they must be wound up with the [[Clockwork Key]] before activation. If an Automaton is destroyed, it cannot be used again until it is repaired by Dampé, which requires either [[Rupee|Rupees]] or [[Monster Stone|Monster Stones]].
