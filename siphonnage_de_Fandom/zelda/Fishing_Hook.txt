The {{Term/Store|Fishing Hook||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 123"/>

==Location and Uses==
The Fishing Hook is the eleventh item in the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You exchanged {{Icon|LADX Broom}} for the fishing hook {{Icon|LADX Fishing Hook}}! What will the fishing hook become?|N/A|LADX}}</ref> It is received from [[Grandma Ulrira]] in [[Mabe Village]] after giving her the [[Broom]],<ref name="E 123"/> which she found while sweeping near the river.<ref>{{Cite|Okay! In return you can have this fishing hook I found when I swept by the river bank!|Grandma Ulrira|LADX}}</ref>

The Fishing Hook is later given to the [[Fisherman]] found beneath the bridge in [[Martha's Bay]] to replace the one he lost.<ref>{{Cite|It's no use, little buddy! A fish took my hook... I keep casting my line into the water, but I haven't got a bite... I thought this would happen...|Fisherman|LADX}}</ref> In exchange for the Hook, the Fisherman promises to give Link his next catch,<ref>{{Cite|Oh! What is that you have in your hand? It's not a fishing hook, is it? You had better let me have it. I'll give you my next catch if you let me have it...|Fisherman|LADX}}</ref> which turns out to be the [[Necklace]].<ref>{{Cite|The {{Icon|LADX Fishing Hook}} became a necklace {{Icon|LADX Necklace}}! L-l-lucky!|N/A|LADX}}</ref>
