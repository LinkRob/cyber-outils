{{Term/Store|Librari||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Librari}}
{{Term|TMC|Librari}} is the elder of the {{Term|TMC|Town Minish|link}}. He lives inside a book in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, and is the twin brother of the {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}} elder {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link}}.<ref name="E 270"/> He carries a feather with him, which came from a {{Term|TMC|Cucco|link}} he fought when he was younger.<ref>{{Cite|Those large wings that he is so proud of are trophies from a duel with a cucco as a young Minish.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

In order for the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} to open, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must speak to the {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}} who lives near the {{Term|TMC|Wind Crest|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have heard rumors that one Minish actually did find a way in, long ago... That Minish is {{Color|TMC Green|Elder Librari}}, and he is in semi-retirement in the town library. The library should be opening soon. Perhaps you can go find him.|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref> Despite learning that {{Term|TMC|Librari}} once entered the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, how he did so remains a mystery until {{Term|TMC|Link}} encounters him in person.<ref>{{Cite|And how did that {{Color|TMC Green|Librari}} get into the {{Color|TMC Green|Temple of Droplets}}, anyway?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I just can't figure out how {{Color|TMC Green|Librari}} got into the {{Color|TMC Green|Temple of Droplets}}. I'm going crazy with curiosity!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

After speaking to {{Term|TMC|Jotari|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must retrieves three overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}; when they are re-shelved by {{Term|TMC|Sturgeon|link}}, he gains access to {{Term|TMC|Librari}}'s home. While speaking to {{Term|TMC|Librari}}, the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} elder triggers a trapdoor, causing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to fall down a hole and face a trial to learn {{Term|TMC|Librari}}'s secret.<ref>{{Cite|Now...OPEN, secret mystery panel!! Wohoho! Wasn't that fun? Now then... It's up to you to pass this {{Color|TMC Blue|trial}} safely and recover the {{Color|TMC Red|item}} you require.|Librari|TMC}}</ref> After passing the trial and defeating a pair of {{Plural|TMC|Scissors Beetle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}, allowing him to {{Term|TMC|Swimming|link|display=Swim}} and {{Term|TMC|Diving|link|display=Dive}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm! Yes, yes, yes! Why, it all makes sense now, doesn't it, boy? {{Color|TMC Green|Librari}} used these {{Color|TMC Red|flippers}} to swim to the entrance to the {{Color|TMC Green|Temple of Droplets}}.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Librari}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Yes, please!|Librari|TMC}}</ref> causing a {{Term|TMC|Golden Octorok|link}} to appear in the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}} is closed. {{Term|TMC|Librari}} can still be found elsewhere, however; after {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with the {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}} in the northeastern house of {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}} for the second time, a crack appears near the {{Term|TMC|Wind Crest}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia}}. The crack serves as the entrance to an obstacle-filled passageway that leads to an island at the center of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia}}, where {{Term|TMC|Librari}} can be found. He is astonished that {{Term|TMC|Link}} managed to find him, and gives him a full {{Term|TMC|Heart Container|link}} as a reward. <ref>{{Cite|Hey! How'd you find me, kid? You're a real humdinger! So, since you found me, I'm going to give you {{Color|TMC Red|my number-one treasure}}!|Librari|TMC}}</ref> If spoken to afterwards, he will tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} the location of the three {{Plural|TMC|Great Fairy|link}} across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So, have you met the {{Color|TMC Green|Great Fairy}} of {{Color|TMC Green|Minish Woods}}? She lives right off that little side path!|Librari|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|And have you met the {{Color|TMC Green|Great Fairy}} of {{Color|TMC Green|Mount Crenel}}? She lives behind a wall that you have to break open with a bomb.|Librari|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What about the {{Color|TMC Green|Great Fairy}} near the {{Color|TMC Green|Royal Crypt}}? Have you met her? She lives behind a wall that you have to break open with a bomb.|Librari|TMC}}</ref>
