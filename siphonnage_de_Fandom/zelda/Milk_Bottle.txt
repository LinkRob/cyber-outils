==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|MM3D|Milk Bottle}} are large containers of {{Term|MM3D|Milk|link}} used by {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}}. Six {{Plural|MM3D|Milk Bottle}} appear atop {{Plural|MM3D|Barrel|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Barn|link}} on the {{Term|MM3D|Second Day|link}} if {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} successfully protects the {{Plural|MM3D|Cow|link}} from the {{Plural|MM3D|Ghost (Majora's Mask)|link}} on the night before.{{Verify|MM}} During the "{{Term|MM3D|Protect the Milk!|link}}" {{Term|MM3D|Event}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must shoot {{Plural|MM3D|Arrow|link}} at the masked {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Brothers|link}} to keep them from breaking the three {{Plural|MM3D|Milk Bottle}} on {{Term|MM3D|Cremia|link}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Carriage|link}}.<ref name="Pursuers"/> Each {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bottle}} breaks after three hits.
