The {{Term/Store|Mayor's Residence||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite|Mayor's Residence|Map|MM3D}}</ref> also known as the '''Mayor's Official Residence''',<ref>{{Cite|Mayor's Official Residence|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> is a location in {{MM}}.

==Features and Overview==
Situated in [[East Clock Town]], the Mayor's Official Residence is the office of [[Mayor Dotour]] and is also the home to his wife, [[Madame Aroma]], and son, [[Kafei]]. It is open to the public from 10 AM to 8 PM.<ref>{{Cite|The Mayor's Official Residence / Open to the public from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.|Sign|MM}}</ref> The building does not function only as a home, however; it is also where the mayor does his mayoral duties and holds meetings.

There is a [[Receptionist]] in the lobby of the Residence who will direct visitors to one of two rooms. On the left is the mayor's office, wherein he does important tasks such as signing papers, holding meetings and making sure that the citizens of [[Clock Town]] are happy.<ref>{{Cite|The room on the left is the Mayor's room... It sounds like they're having some kind of meeting, I think.|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> To the right is the drawing room, which doubles as Madame Aroma's office.<ref>{{Cite|The room on your right is the drawing room and it's also Madame Aroma's office.|Receptionist|MM}}</ref> Madame Aroma and [[Toto]] can be found here. When spoken to for the first time, Madame Aroma will confuse Link with the expert person-finder she hired and give him [[Kafei's Mask]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear, are you on a field trip? ...Or are you the expert person-finder I hired?|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> Adjoined to the drawing room is the family's bedroom, where [[Kafei's Diary]] can be found.

A discussion is being held in the mayor's office during most of [[Link]]'s three days in [[Termina]]. The meeting's topic is whether to mandate an evacuation notice to the townsfolk and whether the [[Carnival of Time]] should still take place or not. [[Mutoh]], the head of the [[Carpenters|Carnival Committee]], is strongly against mandatory evacuation, but [[Captain Viscen|Viscen]], the [[Soldier]]s' captain, is in favor of it.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Mayor and Carnival Committee Members, please order those who remain to evacuate!|Viscen|MM}}</ref>

If Link wears the [[Couple's Mask]] during the meeting, the Mayor will be reminded of the marriage arrangement and conclude that being with their families is more important than continuing the meeting,<ref>{{Cite|The Couple's Mask. What a nice reminder...So, a young couple has been married. ...Yes, yes, everyone. We are all worried for our families. Why don't we end this meeting? |Mayor Dotour|MM}}</ref> and he will decide against the mandatory evacuation. Instead, he concludes that the decision to stay in town or seek shelter is for each family to decide on their own.<ref>{{Cite|This will do. Whether you're stubborn and will stay and guard your family, or if you'd prefer to run far away and seek shelter... That is for people to decide on their own.|Mayor Dotour|MM}}</ref> He will then reward Link with a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Adults are all so stubborn... It is shameful. Here is a token of my gratitude.|Mayor Dotour|MM}}</ref>

Meanwhile, Madame Aroma and Toto are in the drawing room canceling all the performances they had prepared for the Carnival of Time due to the [[Lulu|singer]] of [[The Indigo-Go's]] losing her voice.<ref>{{Cite|The opening performance I've asked you to do... They've canceled! Just now!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref>
