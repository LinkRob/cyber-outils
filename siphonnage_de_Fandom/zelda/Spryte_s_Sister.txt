'''Spryte's Sister''' is a character appearing in the "{{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairies in the Spring|link}}" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. As her name states, she is the sister of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte}} and daughter of {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Oberon}}, the king of {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy}}.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That's right! Spryte's my sister. And you must be Zelda. And Link.|name=Spryte's Sister|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref>

==Biography==
When {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte's Sister}} realizes that the near-completed {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Royal Water Park|link}} is taking water from the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy Spring|link}} water supply, she begins to create {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Water Monster|link}} to keep their spring from draining.<ref name=water>{{Cite Episode|quote=We didn't know who you were. All we knew was somebody  was draining our spring.|name=Spryte's Sister|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> However, {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Link|link}} are unaware of the motives behind the water creatures' appearance at the water park, which has scared all of its workers away.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Never saw anything like it, your majesty. Scared off all my crew and took the whole place!|name=Workers' Boss|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref> When the young heroes accidentally arrive at the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy Spring}}, Zelda notices that the beams creating the monsters are coming from a tree and, upon further investigation, discovers that the monsters were being created by {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte's Sister}}. After the {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Fairy}} introduces herself, she reveals to the young heroes her reasons for creating the monsters; however, once {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Harkinian|link}} explains to {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|King Oberon}} that they will not use the spring's water supply anymore and instead opt for using magic ponds, all is forgiven and {{Term|TLoZ (TV Series)|Spryte's Sister}} stops creating the {{Plural|TLoZ (TV Series)|Water Monster}} so that the construction of the water park can finally be completed.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That's right, my dear. I'm afraid our water park was hooked into the fairy water supply. And the fairy king here got  worried. Perfectly natural for them to defend it, eh? [...] Not a bit, my dear! We'll just use magic ponds to move  our water. Once all the pools are full, we won't need to use any more!  I was just explaining that to Oberon here. He's not worried anymore.|name=King Harkinian|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=11}}</ref>
