{{Term/Store|Boe|Boes|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are enemies in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Main|Black Boe, White Boe}}
{{Plural|MM3D|Boe}} are shuffling, hazy balls that take two forms: the {{Plural|MM3D|Black Boe|link}}, which thrives in the darkness and beneath shadow, and the {{Plural|MM3D|White Boe|link}}, which flourishes in high-intensity light and under snow-like conditions. Both types are not very threatening when isolated, and can be dispelled easily with a single sword swipe. However, these floating fiends tend to cluster in groups, and in such situations, {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} may have to switch fighting tactics to deal with them accordingly.
