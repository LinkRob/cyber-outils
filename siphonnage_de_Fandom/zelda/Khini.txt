{{Term/Store|Khini||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Khini|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Khini}} is a worker at {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. Throughout the day, he stands in front of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} and welcomes travelers.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome!|Khini|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Helloooo! Welcome to {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stable}}! The premiere stable of the east!|Khini|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to him, {{Term|BotW|Khini}} offers to sell him an {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bun|link}}, which he promises will increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s energy.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, good sir... The outside is crunchy while the inside is chewy. Take one bit, and your {{Color|BotW Red|energy will go through the roof}}! Go on! Try one of our {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala buns}}! They're a delicacy straight from the {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stable}}!|Khini|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Care to buy an {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala bun}}?|Khini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to take one, {{Term|BotW|Khini}} explains that he will need to eat it immediately, since the ingredients go bad quickly.<ref>{{Cite|[Sounds yummy.] Thank you soooo much! Oh, one thing to note... {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala buns}} go bad fast since they're made of fresh ingredients. So you'll have to eat it here, that way we can ensure its quality. That OK?|Khini|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to eat it at the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Khini}} then asks for 10 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as payment.<ref>{{Cite|[OK.] Thank you soooo much! That'll be {{Color|BotW Red|10 rupees}}, please!|Khini|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has payed for the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bun}}, {{Term|BotW|Khini}} tells him to close his eyes and open his mouth.<ref>{{Cite|Heheh, thanks for your business! Now then, close your eyes and open your mouth {{Big|wiiiiiiiiiiiide...}}|Khini|BotW}}</ref> He then walks {{Term|BotW|Link}} through the process of chewing and swallowing the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bun|display=Bun}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now just MUNCH 'N' CRUNCH! {{Big|And down the hatch it goes...}}|Khini|BotW}}</ref> On occasion, eating one will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} extra {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}}. Afterwards, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he thought about it.<ref>{{Cite|So how was it?! Amazing, right?|Khini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} did not receive extra {{Term|BotW|Stamina}}, {{Term|BotW|Khini}} will notice and remarks that this is odd.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? That's odd. Your {{Color|BotW Red|energy}} didn't {{Color|BotW Red|go through the roof}}?|Khini|BotW}}</ref> Either he, he encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to visit him again in the future.<ref>{{Cite|Come back soon!|Khini|BotW}}</ref>
