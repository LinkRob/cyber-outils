{{Term/Store|LD-301N Skipper||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a robotic character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 295}}</ref> It is one of a race of [[Ancient Robot]]s that are found primarily in the [[Lanayru Province]].

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= This unit's designation is {{Color|SS Blue|LD-301N Skipper}}. It is the leadership model of this series of artificial life-forms. The original purpose of this series of robots was mining. It is the supreme model of the mass-produced robots.
}}

{{clear|left}}

Skipper was once the captain of the [[Sandship]], a vessel that protected [[Sacred Flames|Nayru's Flame]].<ref>{{Cite|Who am I, brrzrrt...? I am the proud skipper of the ship that protects {{Color|SS Red|Nayru's Flame}}, phweep!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> It once had a family with at least one child and an admiring crew.<ref>{{Cite|That house holds many dear memories of my family, vrrm. I wonder when I will be able to go home, zrrt. I really want to see my family again, phweep...|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Dear Dad, Good luck at work, vrrm!|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Dear Captain, Thanks for everything, zrrt! Yours, the Crew, vrrrrrm.|N/A|SS}}</ref> After its ship was taken by the pirate {{Term|SS|LD-002G Scervo|link}}, Skipper's crew were taken hostage and Skipper was thrown overboard.<ref>{{Cite|It was the day of the storm, phoo-weep... My crew and I were navigating the seas as usual, vrrm... And then those brutes, bzzt... the pirates, suddenly attacked us, brrzt. They were after {{Color|SS Red|Nayru's Flame}}, zzpt. My crew was imprisoned...vrrrt... and I was thrown into the sea!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> Eventually, the crew succumbed to the wear of time and broke down. [[Link]] first meets Skipper when he activates a [[Timeshift Stone]] on a boat near Skipper's deactivated body, reanimating the robot. After hearing of Link's quest to find the Sacred Flames, Skipper enlists his aid in locating its lost ship.<ref>{{Cite|You say you're searching for {{Color|SS Red|Nayru's Flame}}, bzzt? Oh... So you need {{Color|SS Red|Nayru's Flame}} to find someone important to you, vrrrm...? In that case, you should help me search for my ship and crew, phweep!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref>

They search for clues across the [[Lanayru Sand Sea]], recovering an [[Ancient Sea Chart|accurate map]] of the past Lanayru Sand Sea from Skipper's home at [[Skipper's Retreat]].<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... There's something weird about that map of yours...vrrm. If you're going to navigate these treacherous waters, then you need a proper {{Color|SS Gold|sea chart}}, doo-weep! There is a {{Color|SS Gold|sea chart}} in my shack at {{Color|SS Blue|Skipper's Retreat}}, bzzzt. So first you need to get on this boat and set a course for {{Color|SS Blue|Skipper's Retreat}}!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> Link also rides a mine cart through the vast [[Lanayru Shipyard]] where the Sandship was built,<ref>{{Cite|The Shipyard is where we used to build our ships, vrrt. My ship was built there too, vweep! If you go to the Shipyard, you might find some clues to the location of my ship, brrzrrt!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> and finds the remnants of the ship at the [[Pirate Stronghold]],<ref>{{Cite|So my ship wasn't here, zrrt? Then there is only one place left for us to look for clues, vrrm... Brrzrrt. This could be scary, but... we'll head to the stronghold of the pirates who stole my ship, phweep! The pirate captain is a mechanical maniac, zrrt! Worse, he has plenty of evil underlings too, vrrm! I never wanted to lay my optical receptors on him again, brrzrrt. But... If we want to take the ship back, then we have no choice, phoo-weep!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> which [[Fi]] analyzes for [[Dowsing]].<ref>{{Cite|You know the place? Some kind of thing called {{Color|SS Red|dowsing}}, vrrm? That's amazing, phoo-weep! This time we'll definitely find my ship, voo-whooot!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref> After they find the Sandship and Link activates its Timeshift Stone, Skipper will commandeer the ship and ask Link to find its crew who have been held hostage for many years.<ref>{{Cite|Now the ship looks just like it used to, phoo-weep! That makes me so happy, vrrm! Now I just need you to go rescue my {{Color|SS Blue|crew}}, vweep! I'm pretty sure they're being held in the {{Color|SS Blue|brig}} inside the ship, brrzrrt!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref>

After Link has defeated the Abyssal Leviathan [[Tentalus]] who attacked and destroyed the ship, Skipper and its crew rebuild the Sandship and continue to sail, still protecting the Sacred Flame hidden on board.

==Trivia==
*Skipper seems to be the only Ancient Robot who recognizes its death if it steps out of the area affected by the Timeshift Stone.<ref>{{Cite|But if you think about it, they should all be dead, brrzrrt! The only reason I'm up and about like this is because of that Timeshift Stone on the boat, phoo-weep!|LD-301N Skipper|SS}}</ref>
*Despite {{Term|SS|LD-301N Skipper}}'s crew being unable to pass through the electrical barrier of their cell, {{Term|SS|LD-301N Skipper}} somehow manages to bypass one to speak to {{Term|SS|Link}}.
