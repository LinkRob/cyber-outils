{{Term/Store|Bamboo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Bamboo">{{Cite|All right, Bamboo, my totally gnarly and radical horse! We're finally ready to hit the mountain for some {{Color|BotW Red|shield surfing}}!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Bamboo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} belonging to {{Term|BotW|Baumar|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler.<ref name="Bamboo"/><ref>{{Cite|Just hit the northern passage from here, man, and you'll find {{Color|BotW Blue|Serenne Stable}}. Let's kick it, Bamboo!|Baumar|BotW}}</ref> The two of them travel a portion of the {{Term|BotW|Hilltop High Road|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} so that {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} can go {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.<ref name="Bamboo"/><ref name="Excellent"/>

Like many {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Bamboo}} is outfitted with the standard {{Term|BotW|Stable Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Stable Saddle|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Baumar}} shows a lot of affection for {{Term|BotW|Bamboo}}, referring to them as "totally gnarly", "radical", and "most excellent".<ref name="Bamboo"/><ref name="Excellent"/>
