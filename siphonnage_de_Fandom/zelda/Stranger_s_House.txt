The {{Term/Store|Stranger's House||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Stranger's House}} is located along the western edge of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, near the exit to the {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}} resides within it, and {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with him, creating a {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}} in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Link}} can use to travel to the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}.
