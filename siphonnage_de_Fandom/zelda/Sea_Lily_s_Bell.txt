==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
The Sea Lily's Bell is one of the eight [[Instruments of the Sirens]] needed to awaken the [[Wind Fish]].<ref name="E 137"/> It can be found at the end of [[Key Cavern]] after defeating its boss, [[Slime Eye]].<ref name="E 137"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub|HWDE}}
In {{HW}}, the Sea Lily Bell appears as the Lv. 1 tier of [[Marin]]'s [[Bell#Hyrule Warriors|Bell]] Weapon.
{{Noncanon|End}}
