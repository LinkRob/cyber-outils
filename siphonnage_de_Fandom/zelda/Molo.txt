{{Term/Store|Molo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Molo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Molo}} is {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who is a regular at the {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}. During the day while the {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} is clear, he sits out at the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}'s patio drinking from a mug. When it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}}, he will instead sit at the table inside. At night, he sleeps in one of the {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|*snooooooore*|Molo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} wakes him up, {{Term|BotW|Molo}} asks who he is and why he would disturb him at such a time.<ref>{{Cite|Who are you? Who shows up at a time like this?|Molo|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks with {{Term|BotW|Molo}}, he complains that he is unsure of what to do.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh! I just don't know what to do!|Molo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Molo}} thanks him for nothing.<ref>{{Cite|[See you around.] Thanks for nothing, bud.|Molo|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what his issue is, {{Term|BotW|Molo}} explains that the only way to move forward in life is to be rich and admired.<ref>{{Cite|[What's wrong?] Listen, if you wanna get anywhere in this life, you gotta have cash and cachet.|Molo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Molo}} believes that the only way to achieve this is to steal the abandoned treasure and {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} inside {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}, though he does acknowledge how dangerous this would be.<ref>{{Cite|And the only way to do that in this day and age is to go to—stay with me here—{{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. I know—it sounds crazy. But rumor has it that it's full of abandoned treasure and weapons! They're just sitting in there, free for the taking! Well...if you can get past all the bloodthirsty monsters, of course.|Molo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} encourages {{Term|BotW|Molo}} to enter {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}, he will be inspired to do so.<ref>{{Cite|[Go for it.] You're right! I can't let a smoking-hot rumor like this go to waste! I just gotta act on it, bud!|Molo|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him not to try, {{Term|BotW|Molo}} states that he is unable to let a rumor of this magnitude go to waste.<ref>{{Cite|[Take it easy...] Take it easy? And let a rumor like this go to waste? Nah, I gotta go big...|Molo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Molo}} has heard that one can enter {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}} through the {{Term|BotW|Docks|link}}<ref>{{Cite|There's a docks on the north side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}, see, and I hear you can get inside the castle from there.|Molo|BotW}}</ref> He is also interested in the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that is supposedly nearby, referring to {{Term|BotW|Saas Ko'sah Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's also an ancient {{Color|BotW Blue|shrine}} somewhere around there. Smells like treasure to me. Stinks like it, even!|Molo|BotW}}</ref>

Of the people {{Term|BotW|Molo}} knows who have gone to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}, none have returned, which he believes is due to them being too busy now that they are rich.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, no one I know who's gone to {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} has come back. Probably because they're too busy being rich!|Molo|BotW}}</ref> Once he has formulated his plan to enter the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|display=Castle}}, he thinks he will be able to live a comfortable life.<ref>{{Cite|I just need to come up with a solid plan. Then I'll be living the good life!|Molo|BotW}}</ref>
