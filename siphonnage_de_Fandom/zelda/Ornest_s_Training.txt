{{Term/Store|Ornest's Training||Series, TotK}} is a {{Term|TotK|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TotK}}.

==Location and Rules==
Ornest's Training is located southwest of {{Term|TotK|Tabantha Great Bridge|link}} on a western cliffside of {{Term|TotK|Tanagar Canyon|link}}. The location is identifiable by a tall tower off the cliffside with a hanging platform. There are two people present: Tabult the {{Term|TotK|Goron|link}} retainer, and the host, Ornest the {{Term|TotK|Rito|link}}. It costs 20 {{Plural|TotK|Rupee|link}} to play.<ref>{{Cite|I charge <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">20 rupees</font> so I can keep Tabult on retainer. So, think you can <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">return</font> the balls?|Ornest|TotK}}</ref>

Ornest says that the tower is used for his training.<ref>{{Cite|See that impressive tower back there? We built that baby just for this.|Ornest|TotK}}</ref> His routine consists of catching balls in the air and dropping them into the hanging platform, constantly swaying. Tabult at the tower<ref>{{Cite|The guy operating the tower is <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Tabult</font>. I keep him on retainer so I can train!|Ornest|TotK}}</ref> will throw balls to be caught.<ref>{{Cite|Tabult will lob <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">balls</font> at you. The game is to <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">return</font> them to the moving basket.|Ornest|TotK}}</ref> Up to 12 balls will be thrown, each with varying colors. Green balls are worth one points, red balls are worth three points.<ref>{{Cite|You'll be faced with a total of 12 balls coming at you.<br/>Getting a green ball is worth <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">one point</font>. But a red ball nets you <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">three</font>!|Ornest|TotK}}</ref> As the training is designed for him — a Rito — for non-Rito i.e: Link's case, he is allowed to use whatever means to catch the balls in the air.<ref>{{Cite|I don't see a featherless guy like you doing it my way though.<br/>So I'll let you use any tools or mysterious powers you've got going for you to <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">return</font> them.|Ornest|TotK}}</ref>

Once the all the balls are thrown, the game ends. The reward for playing the game are {{Plural|TotK|Rupee|link}}. The more points scored based on the balls caught, the higher the payout.
