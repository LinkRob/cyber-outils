{{Term/Store|Beetle|Beetles|Series, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA, TMC|plural}} are recurring [[enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 165 {{Exp Game|LA, OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, Beetles appear from a hole in the ground south of [[Kanalet Castle]] whenever [[Link]] gets close. They can be defeated with a single [[Sword]] slash, but endlessly continue appearing from the hole.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, Beetles can appear when Link digs a hole using the [[Shovel]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, Beetles can appear when Link digs a hole using the Shovel.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Beetle}}
In {{TMC}}, Beetles appear under [[Rock]]s and [[Grass]] or when digging with the [[Mole Mitts]]. If they grab Link, he will not be able to move until he swings his Sword.

There are also red-hued Beetles that only appear when Link fights [[Mazaal]] in the [[Fortress of Winds]].

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Beetle|This small monster isn't very strong but can overwhelm the unwary in great numbers.<ref name="EoW Beetle">{{Cite|This small monster isn't very strong but can overwhelm the unwary in great numbers.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Beetles are small, weak enemies that emerge from [[Beetle Mound|mounds]], relying on their numbers to overwhelm foes. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Beetle [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of one.

==Nomenclature==
The Beetle's Japanese name, "Zoro Zoro", is derived from the Japanese onomatopoeia for insects skittering around.
