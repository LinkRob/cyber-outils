'''Vicente''' is a character in the {{MM|Himekawa}}.

==Biography==
Vicente is first shown with [[Osun|Master Osun]] and his fellow students making swords in their castle. One of the students ask when the [[Link|famous swordsman]] is going to arrive.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= When's that famous swordsman going to get here?|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 14}}</ref> Vicente responds that he should arrive that day at anytime and that he must be pretty good if House Taburi recommends him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Today. Anytime now. Must be pretty good if House Taburi recommends him.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 14}}</ref> Vicente adds that although he is coming to their knightly order for lessons, it would be great for their reputation to beat him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= He's coming to our knightly order for lessons...But it'd be great for our reputation if we beat him.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 14}}</ref> Osun says it woud be great for the swordsman if he beat them instead.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Maybe. But...It'd be great for him if he beats us!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 14}}</ref> Vicente does not believe a wandering swordsman could accomplish such a feat and asks what his name is.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= A wandering swordsman beat us? Are you kidding?! What's his name?|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 14}}</ref> Osun says the swordsman's name is Link.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 15}}</ref> A man announces that Link is out by the gate.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= He's here! Out by the gate!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 15}}</ref> Vicente wants to see him first so he leads the group dashing through the door.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= All right! I wanna see him first!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 15}}</ref> When Vicente's group gets to the gate, he does not see anyone.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Huh? There's no one here!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 15}}</ref> He then looks down to see Link. A student says that the boy, Link, must be Link's squire.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That must be his squire.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 16}}</ref> Vicente scoffs, asking if Link ran off and left him behind.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ha! Did Link run off and leave you behind?|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 16}}</ref> The boy says he is Link and asks to be taken to Osun, their leader.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'm Link. Take me to Osun, your leader.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 16}}</ref> Vicente and his group stare in confusion before breaking into an uproar of laughter.  Vicente laughs that Link chickened out and left behind the kid pretending to be him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Hear that? He chickened out and ran, leaving behind this kid...Pretending to be him.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 17}}</ref> Link desperately tries to convince them of the truth.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Really, I'm Link. Osun is expect-ing me.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 17}}</ref> Vicente tells the child not to press his luck and asks why they should take him seriously without proof.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Look, kid, don't press your luck! Why should we take you seriously without proof-|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 17}}</ref> Link holds up the [[Ocarina of Time (Object)|Ocarina of Time]], baffling Vicente and the others when he sees the [[Triforce|mark of the Hylian Royal Family]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Th-That's...The mark...Of the Hylian Royal Family!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 17}}</ref> Osun arrives and tells Vicente to never judge a swordsman by appearance alone and welcomes Link to their castle.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Never judge a swordsman by appearance alone... Welcome to our castle, Link.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 17}}</ref> Vicente answers yes sir to his mentor<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Yes, sir!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 17}}</ref> and stands shocked when Osun says they may learn a lesson from the boy.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Ha! I expect we'll be learning from you.|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 19}}</ref>

While Vicente duels Link, he falls to the ground with his sword shattered in pieces. He questions how he can beat Link with a broken sword.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Rats! How can I beat him with a broken sword?|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher=VIZ Media|page= 18}}</ref> A student reminds Vicente that is broke after Link beat him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Vicente, it broke after he beat you!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 18}}</ref> Vicente is amazed at Link since he is not only beating them but also holding his own against Master Osun.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can't believe it. That kid isn't just beating us...He's holding his own against Master Osun!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 19}}</ref> Once Link leaves, Vicente stomps off to go train.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I'm gonna go train!!|book= {{MM|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 21}}</ref>
