"{{Term/Store|The Hero's Cache||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Hero's Cache}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kitano Bay}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Kitano Bay|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} playing his bandoleon on a stone pillar.<ref>{{Cite|Why, hello! I certainly didn't expect to see anyone else up here.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has met him before, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will comment on how fortunate they are to have crossed paths again and asks if he can to see the ocean.<ref>{{Cite|How fortunate to cross paths with you again! Did you come to see the sea?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He explains that the strange pillar they are standing on is the subject of an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}," and he and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|This oddly shaped rock pillar we're standing on is the subject of a song. Would you like to hear it?|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} sings a song that recounts the words of an [[Link#The_Hero_10.2C000_Years_Prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|ancient hero]].<ref>{{Cite|An ancient hero spoke these words:<br/>"One day I'll return to fighr evil."|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The hero left a cache at "17 of 24", and the pillar points toward its location.<ref>{{Cite|"My cache is at {{Color|BotW Red|17 of 24}},<br/>This rock will point toward its retrieval."|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When he is done singing, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} speculates that the cache could be referring to a stash of money,<ref>{{Cite|"My cache?" Could it be a hidden stash of money?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which he believes is possible since it was left by the hero from 10,000 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|If it was left by the ancient hero that defeated the Calamity 10,000 years ago, it must be a big amount!|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} luck in deciphering the riddle,<ref>{{Cite|Good luck figuring it out. May the light illuminate your path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

At five in the evening, which is the seventeenth hour of the day, the shadow of the pillar will point towards a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the water. Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pull the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} up. Inside is a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}, obtaining which will end the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You've solved the riddle of the ancient verse that Kass recited and earned a gold rupee for your trouble.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
