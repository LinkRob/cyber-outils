'''Ganondorf's Steed''' is the horse that {{Term|Series|Ganondorf|link}}, [[Ganon]]'s human form, rides in {{OoT}}, {{TP}} and {{TotK}}, as well as ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]''.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=HH|page=148}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Ganondorf's Steed Model.png|180px|left]]
The first appearance of Ganondorf's Steed in {{OoT}} is during [[Link]]'s nightmare prior to {{Term|OoT3D|Navi|link}} awakening him at the very beginning of the game. The horse itself is a black {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo|link}} stallion with glowing red eyes and mane and tail that match the coloration of [[Ganondorf]]'s hair.<ref>{{Cite|They say that the horse Ganondorf rides is a solid black Gerudo stallion.|Gossip Stone|OoT}}</ref> Aside from a few flashbacks, Ganondorf rides this horse while pursuing {{Term|OoT3D|Impa|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Zelda|link}} as they flee from {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule Castle|link}} on horseback after the young [[Hero of Time]] gathers the three [[Spiritual Stone|Spiritual Stones]]. The Steed no longer appears when Ganondorf becomes the Evil King during Link's adulthood, as presumably it has no further use for the archvillain. 

The horse is equipped with warhorse armor, called {{Wp|Barding|barding}}, including a plate champron, to protect the horse's face, a full criniere, to protect the neck, a sturdy peytral, to protect the chest and shoulder region, and a red and gold cloth {{Wp|Caparison|caparison}}.

[[Phantom Ganon]], the [[boss]] of the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}, rides an identical stallion, although after being hit by some [[Arrow|Arrows]] by Link, the steed abandons its master. It is unknown if this particular stallion is a ghost or the actual horse Ganondorf himself rode. Just before the beginning of the battle, the stallion emits [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_GanondorfsHorse_Snort.wav a brief snort] that alerts Link to it and its master's presence in a deliberate echo to their first face-to-face meeting seven years prior.

==={{TP|-}}===
The Steed in {{TP}} compared to {{OoT|-}} has a much thicker build to fit Ganondorf's size, muted color scheme, and new armor design, including a croupiere to protect the horse's hindquarters, a smaller peytral, and without a colorful caparison. The horse plays a larger role in this game as {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}}'s mode of transport while engaged in a horseback battle with Link and {{Term|TPHD|Zelda}}. In the prelude to the battle, Ganondorf was shown to ride the horse shortly after Hyrule Castle was obliterated by [[Midna]] in a self-sacrificial effort to stop the King of Evil, destroying Midna's [[Fused Shadow|helmet]] in the process, before charging at Link and Zelda with the intention of running them down. However, Zelda, by praying to the Light Spirits, ended up being teleported out with Link at the last minute to gain the [[Light Arrows|means]] to banish evil. When the King of Evil is struck down by Zelda's Light Arrows, he falls off his horse with the horse falling to its side shortly afterward. However, it disappeared by the time the dust settled and Ganondorf got up, leaving its fate ambiguous.

Aside from its role in the final boss battle, the Steed also made a brief appearance in the Six Sage's recollection of Ganondorf and his past as a "demon thief" where it reared and neighed as Ganondorf was riding it amist a sea of flames.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Ganondorf's Steed is seen once again in {{TotK}}, having a more demonic appearance with a larger horn, [[Gloom]] eyes, and armor, in addition to the same red mane as its owner. Unlike in previous games, it is only seen once when Ganondorf is leading his army of monsters to destroy [[Hyrule]] to make his new kingdom.<ref>{{Cite|Rise. Rise my servants. Sweep over Hyrule with the power I have granted you. Eliminate this kingdom and her allies. LEAVE NO SURVIVORS!|Ganondorf|TotK}}</ref>

==={{HWAoI|-}}===
In ''[[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment]]'', Ganondorf's Steed reprises its role from ''Tears of the Kingdom'', with the game elaborating that the scene where Demon King Ganondorf rode it and gave orders to wipe out Hyrule occurred around the time Rauru and the other Sages arrived at the Temple of Time.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, {{Term|HWDE|Ganondorf's Steed}} briefly appears in {{Term|HWDE|Ganondorf}}'s intro. It is ridden into battle shortly before {{Term|HWDE|Ganondorf}} dismounts it by levitating. It looks nearly identical to {{Term|HWDE|Epona|link}} rather than being a {{Term|HWDE|Gerudo}} stallion.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{MM3D}}, a toy horse that resembles Ganondorf's Steed can be found on the upper floor of the ranch house at [[Romani Ranch]].
*The manual for [[The Legend of Zelda Chess Set]] refers to Ganondorf's Steed (which plays as the [[wikipedia:Knight_(chess)|Knight]] at the villain side) as Phantom.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= '''Epona & Phantom'''|product= The Legend of Zelda Chess Set|page= 3}}</ref>
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', the Steed shares the same [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_GanondorfsHorse_Snort.wav snorting] and [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_GanondorfsHorse_Neigh.wav neighing] sound as adult [[Epona]] and other horses, but is slightly sped-up, likely to distinguish it from them.
*Ganondorf's Steed in {{OoT|-}}, as seen in cutscenes, notably gallops at a more aggressive pace than how most other horses normally run, much like [[Princess Zelda]]'s white horse in the same game.
*[[Link]]'s encounters with Ganondorf's Steed as both child ([[Hyrule Field]]) and adult ([[Forest Temple]]) in ''Ocarina of Time'' had himself hearing its snort and turns around by surprise, with [[Ganondorf]] riding on it (though the latter case is actually [[Phantom Ganon]]).
