The {{Term/Store|Spare Key||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref> also known as the '''Lon Lon Ranch Key''',<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TMC Red|Lon Lon Ranch key}}! Now you can open the ranch door!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> is an item in {{TMC}}.

==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} visits {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}, he discovers that {{Term|TMC|Talon|link}} lost his key and cannot get inside his {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|link|display=House}}, leaving his only {{Term|TMC|Spare Key}} inside and locking both him and {{Term|TMC|Malon|link}} out.<ref>{{Cite|I lost my key, and I can't get inside. And the {{Color|TMC Red|spare key}}'s inside the house!|Talon|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What a pain. And I was thinking of going into town to sell {{Color|TMC Red|Lon Lon Milk}}.|Malon|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} must enter {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|link}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized in order to retrieve the {{Term|TMC|Spare Key}}, which is inside a {{Term|TMC|Jar|link}}. After showing the key to {{Term|TMC|Talon}}, he will thank the young hero, allowing him to pass through his {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|display=House}} whenever he needs to reach {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Our one and only spare key! I don't know how you got this, but I can't thank you enough! Tell me your name, sonny. {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}? That's a great name. If you ever need to go to {{Color|TMC Green|Lake Hylia}}, feel free to cut through our house.|Talon|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You really helped me out, sonny. If you ever want to go to {{Color|TMC Green|Lake Hylia}}, use the shortcut out behind our ranch.|Talon|TMC}}</ref> This also allows {{Term|TMC|Malon}} to go to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} and start selling {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Milk|link}} for 100 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}. Now, I can finally get back into my house. Well, I'd better be going. I have a lot to do before I can leave town.|Malon|TMC}}</ref>
