{{Term/Store|Calyban||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Calyban|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
Calyban is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who can first be found eating {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} on top of the walls of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she will tell him that she cannot eat with Link staring judgmentally at her and tells him to go away.<ref>{{Cite|What's with you? How am I supposed to enjoy eating with you staring at me? Buzz off.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I mean it! I can't eat with your judgmental eyes on me! Go away!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref>

Calyban plays a role in the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Mystery Polluter|link}}", which begins with the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} child, {{Term|BotW|Dalia|link}}, crying because a water trough is unusable due to a flow of {{Term|BotW|Trash|link}} in it.<ref>{{Cite|All this {{Color|BotW Red|trash}} keeps getting washed down into the water trough, and now I can't use the water there anymore.|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon}} rinds flowing down into the trough through the upper waterway and must follow where they are coming from to find the source of the pollution. This leads Link to Calyban, who is throwing the rinds into the waterway.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Calyban}}, she rudely insists that she can do what she wants.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, sure, you spring this on me out of nowhere and expect me to change MY lifestyle. Well, no. I do what I want!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> Stating that altruism is for suckers,<ref>{{Cite|But I need something from you in return. Altruism is for suckers.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} refuses to stop eating melons unless {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings her ten {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}, and I can hold off on eating so many melons.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, I'll need at least {{Color|BotW Red|10}}...|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} are often found in cold places, such as the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected ten {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} and returns to give them to {{Term|BotW|Calyban}}, she is amazed at how delicious they are.<ref>{{Cite|It's delicious! And no rind to toss! What a magical fruit!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} says that she may stop eating other fruit entirely if she has {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I have these, I might just give up on other fruit entirely!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> She then realizes how selfish she has been and leaves to go help {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} clean up the melon rinds.<ref>{{Cite|Now that my craving's been satisfied, I realize just how horribly selfish I've been... Shame on me.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm going to go help clean up the trash!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Dalia}}, {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} has already cleaned up her {{Term|BotW|Trash}} and left.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! You! Listen to this! Some other vai I've never met before helped me clean up the trash!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon}} that was given to her by "the lady who cleaned it up" as a thank you for caring about her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I also got a hydromelon from the lady who cleaned it up! I'll share! It's a sarqso for caring about me!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} sitting in the shade nearby with {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon}} and {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon}} rinds. She says that she gave the {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} to {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} to apologize and because she will always prefer {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon}}.<ref>{{Cite|I gave those {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberries}} to the little vai to apologize for causing her so much trouble with my rinds. Besides, I guess I prefer {{Color|BotW Blue|hydromelons}} to anything, and I always will.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref>
