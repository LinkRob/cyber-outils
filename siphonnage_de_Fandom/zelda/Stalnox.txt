{{Term/Store|Stalnox|Stalnoxes|Series, BotW|plural}}<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Stalnox}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> are recurring bosses in {{TLoZ}} series, being a skeletal version of a [[Hinox]].

== Characteristics ==
=== {{BotW|-}} ===
{{Compendium/Extract|Stalnox}}
A Stalnox is a monstrous boss the size of a tree that can only be found during the night, between 9:00 PM and 5:00 AM. It will not become active if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is too close, or is watching it, however. If encountered during the day, it will simply be a pile of bones and will not come to life to attack Link. If Link engages a Stalnox in battle and morning arrives, the Stalnox will dig underground, fleeing the fight until the next night, at which point it can be battled again.

Like its living counterpart, the Stalnox pursues Link, attempting to pound him to the ground or catch him between its hands. It is also able to pick up trees to use as weapons. Uniquely to the Stalnox, it can remove its ribs, collarbone, and even an arm to throw at Link. It is also able to dig up [[Bomb Barrel]]s to hurl, which explode on contact. Shooting an [[Arrow]] into its eye will stun the boss as he holds it in pain, giving Link the chance to attack. However, when its health is past half, shooting the eye ball will detach it from its socket which will cause the Stalnox's body to drop into a pile of bones for a few moments. During this time, Link can attack the eyeball or even pick it up and throw it further away from the body, eventually, however, it will return to the Stalnox. Shooting the eyeball with a [[Bomb Arrow]] will automatically remove the eye no matter how much health the boss has. Once the eye has lost all of its health, or if the Stalnox and the eye are not reunited before the morning comes at 5:00am, the Stalnox is automatically defeated. Once dead, the Stalnox will drop [[Hinox Tooth|Hinox Teeth]] and rare weapons.

The Stalnox fought within Hyrule Castle will reward Link with the [[Hylian Shield]] once it has been killed. Apparently, defeating it was once a test for Royal Guards to become knights.<ref>{{Cite|- Test of the Royal Guard -<br/>Strike down the giant foe to become recognized as a knight of the kingdom.|Sign|BotW}}</ref> Link can only receive the Hylian Shield a single time by defeating this Stalnox and must purchase another from [[Granté]] in [[Tarrey Town]] if it is broken or lost.

Link must defeat every Stalnox in the [[Overworld]] as part of obtaining [[Kilton]]'s [[Medal of Honor]] for defeating every Hinox.

=== {{TotK|-}} ===
Stalnoxes return in {{TotK}} and they are nearly identical to their cameo in {{BotW|-}}. However, like many enemies in {{TotK|-}}, they can now fight using their [[Stalnox Horn|new horns]], which are made of several [[Stalkoblin]] heads. One can be found near the initial end of the [[Hidden Royal Passage]], while more can be found in various locations, such as [[The Depths]]. Stalnoxes are also among the various monsters [[Ganondorf]] summons to conquer Hyrule in the past, such as [[Moblin]]s, [[Bokoblin]]s [[Boss Bokoblin]]s, [[Lizalfos]], and their living counterparts.
