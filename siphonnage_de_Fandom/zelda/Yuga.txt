'''Yuga''' is an evil sorcerer and the main antagonist of {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''Yuga'''|book= E |page= 213}}</ref> At the beginning of the game, Yuga hunts the descendants of the [[Seven Sages]] from {{OoT}}/{{ALttP}}, as well as [[Princess Zelda]], in an attempt to resurrect [[Ganon]] and use his power to rule the worlds of [[Hyrule]] and [[Lorule]], and ultimately take his place among the gods.

Yuga's trademark attribute is that he can [[Wall Merging|transform himself and others into two-dimensional art]]. With his magic wand, he can trap people within picture frames, turning them into paintings that he can simply pick up and run off with.

==Biography==
===Background===
Long before the events of {{ALBW|-}}, Lorule was a beautiful kingdom similar to Hyrule; however, the kingdom had lost its Triforce in a fierce civil war, due to the Royal Family destroying it rather than seal it away, and slowly began to decay. Generations later, Yuga was serving [[Princess Hilda]] when he discovered a crack in Lorule's sacred realm that led to Hyrule. Upon learning of the existence of another, relatively intact Triforce in this realm, Hilda and Yuga formed a plan to obtain the sacred relic and use it to restore Lorule, despite knowing that doing so would doom Hyrule to Lorule's fate.<ref name="crack"/> As Hilda was not capable of merging so as to fit in the crack, she sent Yuga to Hyrule so as to start the first phase of their plan - namely, to abduct the sage's descendants and Zelda. Secretly, however, he intended to backstab Hilda to fulfill his own personal agenda to utilize the Triforce to ascend to godhood and remake existence into his image, and was using Hilda, with only Ravio seeing through Yuga's intentions.<ref>{{Cite|''"2 Days to Go. She's being duped. Doesn't she realize that? He's just a leech. There's no choice but to go."''|Diary|ALBW}}</ref>

== Physical Description ==

=== Lorulean ===
Yuga is a Lorulean with pale white skin, he has dark almond-shaped eyes, vermilion eyeshadow and false purple eyelashes under them, pointy ears, and long, curly, poppy-red hair.

He mainly wears a long purple and red cape, he wears a black tunic with long sleeves, with triangular edges and the Lorule symbol in the center, he wears baggy pants with blue and dark blue stripes and golden shoes, he has a golden headdress on head with a red gem and wears diamond earrings.

=== Yuga Ganon Form ===
After merging with Ganon, Yuga took on the demonic swine-like appearance of the former, but still retained his curly red hair, baggy pants.  Additionally, he began to wear a ruff with the symbol of Lorule around his neck.

== Personality ==
Yuga, being a painter, is obsessed with art, in fact his obsession is such that he uses his magic to transform beautiful beings or people into paintings, showing a sadistic side by wanting to make everything beautiful into a painting.

Yuga proved to be quite manipulative since he manipulated Princess Hilda to steal the triforce of Hyrule, only to later betray her and keep it for himself. However, Yuga was so arrogant that he always looked down on beings he considered inferior, like Link, which ironically caused his death.

== Abilities ==
Yuga is a powerful sorcerer, who prefers to use his magic skills to fight and has demonstrated great magical feats such as conjuring lightning bolts, creating doubles of himself and shooting energy bolts from his staff. His most prominent ability is his power to turn beings and objects into paintings, being able to make his drawings come to life and attack on command, transform himself into paint and escape through narrow places.

After fusing with Ganon his magical abilities increased incredibly, being able to shoot bolts of energy from his hands and the ability to transform himself and others into paint without the need to use his staff, but he gained a great vulnerability to the [[Magic|divine magic]].

==In battle==
Yuga is fought three times over the course of the game. He is first encountered as the first boss of the game in the [[Eastern Palace]], then later as the boss of [[Hyrule Castle]], serving as the fourth boss, and one last time in the two-part final battle of the game, at the end of [[Lorule Castle]], serving as the twelfth/final boss of the game.

===Eastern Palace Encounter===
{{quote|I don't have time to coddle would-be heroes. But since you insist...I will oblige.|'''Yuga''' before fighting Link in the Eastern Palace}}

As the first boss battle of the game, Yuga is at his weakest. He can summon two [[Green Sword Soldier]] to add him in battle, and attacks by either summing energy pillars or firing lighting beams at Link, both of which are summed and/or fired from the wall, and hiding in the wall as a painting when the young hero gets too close. He is too fast to run towards him directly, so the only way to hit him to use the [[Bow]] to shoot an arrow at him. This will stun him, making him vulnerable to sword slashes or more arrows. After taking enough damage, he will get angered and become faster, and start moving along the wall in a more unpredictable way, his attacks are also more aggressive, from summoning three Green Sword Soldiers, summing the energy pillars from both sides of the walls, and can unleash two lightning beams from the walls, but the strategy to defeat him remains the same. Eventually the battle will end, and Link will obtain the ability to become a drawing himself.

===Hyrule Castle Encounter===
{{quote|I have what I've come to Hyrule for, so I refuse to let you delay my departure any further.|'''Yuga''' before attacking Link in Hyrule Castle}}

As the boss of Hyrule Castle, Yuga is much more formidable. He attacks by creating two copies of himself and launching powerful spells at Link, which the young hero can avoid by standing between the lighting pillars in the arena. Link must hit the real Yuga to stun him, making him vulnerable to more damage. If Link hits a fake Yuga, the copy will turn into a Soldier and the real Yuga will launch a powerful spell. The real Yuga can easily be recognized by his glowing staff. When Yuga takes enough damage, he will once again become much faster and move more erratically, and his spells will have a wider range.

===Lorule Castle's Throne Room Encounter===
{{Main|Yuga Ganon}}
Yuga is fought in a joint form with Ganon as the final boss of the game. There are two stages to the fight.

==Trivia==
*Yuga is the only boss of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds]]'' to be fought on more then one occasion.
*As he is leaving the Sanctuary, Yuga can be heard humming his own theme tune. This is similar to a scene early on in {{TP}}, when [[Midna]] can be heard humming a little of her own theme tune. In {{SS}}, Ghirahim also hums his own theme tune during the ritual to revive [[Demise]].
*Yuga is likely the Lorulean counterpart of Agahnim from ''A Link to the Past'', based on his clothing and role in the story, such as both betraying the royal family in their respective games.
**Given how Hilda and Ravio have similar appearances to Zelda and Link, respectively, it's also implied that he was also the Lorulean counterpart to Ganon's original form, [[Ganondorf]], due to sharing a similar appearance to the latter (notably the red hair and long Gerudo nose). This is further reinforced in ''Super Smash Bros. Ultimate'' and ''Hyrule Warriors'', where the former game had Ganondorf act as the puppet fighter for Yuga, and the latter had Yuga dropping Ganondorf's spoils if defeated.
*Yuga has many similarities to Ghirahim from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword]]'', they both serve as recursing bosses and are also the first bosses to be fought, and both have similar goals to bring back the antagonist that has been sealed, though unlike Ghirahim who serves as the secondary antagonist and serves as both the first, seventh and eleventh/penultimate boss of that game, Yuga serves as the main antagoinist and serves as the first, fourth and twelfth/final boss of the game.
*Immediately before trapping Hilda in a painting during the final battle, Yuga Ganon is shown adopting a pose that resembles Ganon's game over appearance in the Overseas versions of ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]]''.
*Yuga's form of launching projectiles is unique, as unlike some of the previous major antagonist/bosses who launch them from either their hands, chest, mouth, tail, eyes and/or weapons, Yuga launches his projectiles from the walls.
*Yuga's pondering how Link escaped his fate of being a painting only to dismiss the matter was reused to an extent with Ganondorf in {{TotK}} immediately prior to the Phantom Ganon boss fight at Hyrule Castle, where he expressed similar surprise at how Link survived his earlier attack on him, before ultimately dismissing the matter. The main difference is that Ganondorf deduced [[King Rauru's Right Arm|the means]] in which Link ultimately survived, and Ganondorf's dismissing Link was due to overconfidence at Link dying before the next sunrise.

Yuga's name is derived from the Japanese word {{Romanize|油画|Yuga}}, which means "oil painting", a reference to his magical abilities.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= The name "Yuga" is derived from the Japanese word for oil paintings because he has the ability to use magic to turn people into paintings.|name= Eiji Aonuma|url= https://www.facebook.com/notes/the-legend-of-zelda-uk/eiji-aonuma-qa-on-the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-between-worlds/379446052188331|title= Eiji Aonuma Q&A on The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 302}}</ref>
