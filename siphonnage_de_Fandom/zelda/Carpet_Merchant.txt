The {{Term/Store|Carpet Merchant||Series}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref name="sign">{{Cite|All those reckless enough to venture into the desert--please drop by our shop. {{color|OoT Yellow|Carpet Merchant}}|Sign|OoT}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|OoT|Carpet Merchant}} is a salesman who sits on a flying carpet and runs a shop in the {{Term|OoT|Haunted Wasteland|link}}.<ref name="sign"/> {{Term|OoT|Link|link}} can access his flying carpet by either using the {{Term|OoT|Hover Boots|link}} to cross onto it or simply jump and press the attack button at the last second to gain a little extra momentum which makes him land on the carpet. He refuses to tell {{Term|OoT|Link}} he sells {{Term|OoT|Bombchu|link}} until paid.<ref>{{Cite|I won't tell you what it is until I see the money....|Carpet Merchant|OoT}}</ref>

During the ending sequence the {{Term|OoT|Carpet Merchant}} can be seen floating on his carpet with the {{Term|OoT|Bean Seller|link}} and the {{Term|OoT|Lake Scientist|link}} at {{Term|OoT|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}.

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese version, the Carpet Merchant's dialogue is written in a mix of kanji and katakana to convey that his pronunciation is stilted, and is not fully familiar with the language. In the English version, he speaks fluently.
** In the French translation of the original N64 version, the Carpet Merchant speaks French with a heavy Arabic accent, substituting most e's for I's and making several grammatical mistakes, and he was given the name Sabib al Mamoud, which is a corruption of the Arabic phrase and name "Sahib al-Mahmoud", meaning "The companion of the praised one". This was changed to standard French in the 3DS remake and his exact name is no longer mentioned, only his title is. This was likely due to differing values between the time periods.
