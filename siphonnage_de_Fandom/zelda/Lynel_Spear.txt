{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} made and used by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, however, {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} make use of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Spear}}, since regular {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} wielding {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Spear}} are not present. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> While their crescent shape makes them unbalanced and difficult for {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} to use, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} are able to swing them efficiently.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are most commonly found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}, though they can only be obtained by defeating {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Spear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Finally, they are unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
