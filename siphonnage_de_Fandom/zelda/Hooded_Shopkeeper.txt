{{Term/Store|Hooded Shopkeeper|Hooded Shopkeepers|Series, ALttP|plural}} are recurring characters in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228{{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|AST}}

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Hooded Shopkeeper}} are green-clad women who sell {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} a few {{Plural|ALttP|Item|link}} in the {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village Shop|link}}, in the {{Term|ALttP|Lake Hylia Shop Cave|link}}, and in the {{Term|ALttP|Death Mountain|link}} Shop. Their {{Term|ALttP|Red Potion|link}} price is slightly higher than that of the {{Term|ALttP|Witch's Hut|link}}.

The {{Term|ALttP|Apprentice}} is also a hooded woman, but she wears yellow and manages the {{Term|ALttP|Magic Shop}}. If {{Term|ALttP|Link}} talks to her, she gives him a sample of {{Term|ALttP|Red Potion}}, effectively restoring all of his {{Plural|ALttP|Heart Container|link}}. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of the game, the {{Term|ALttP|Apprentice}} looks more like {{Term|ALttP|Maple|link}}.
