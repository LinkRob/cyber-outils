The {{Term/Store|Master's Plaque||Series, OoS}} is a quest item in {{OoS}}.<ref name="E 131">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131}}</ref> It belongs to the [[Master Diver]] in [[Sunken City]], who uses the plaque as part of his test to prove [[Link]] worthy of the [[Flippers|Zora's Flippers]].<ref name="E 131"/>

==Location and Uses==

The plaque is found inside a cavern above the waterfall (which can only be accessed with the help of [[Dimitri]]) next to the one where the Master Diver resides. Here Link will find the test that the Master Diver spoke of: Link must hit the four eye statues found in the cave simultaneously to reveal a staircase leading to a [[Treasure Chest]] where the Master's Plaque, proof of Link's completion of the test, is located.<ref>{{Cite|I am the master diver! To become my pupil, go to the next cave and open all four eyes in a single stroke! And bring back proof that you have done so!|Master Diver|OoS}}</ref> To solve the puzzle, Link must push the four [[block]]s into a position where they can all be hit by a single [[Spin Attack]].

Once Link has obtained the plaque, he can bring it back to the Master Diver, who will give Link the [[Flippers]] as promised.
