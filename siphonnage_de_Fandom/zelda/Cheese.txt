'''Cheese''' is a type of dairy product existing both in real life and in {{TLoZ|Series}}, although the latter features only variations of cheese. Cheese in {{TLoZ|Series|-}} is not as common as in its relative [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]] but has still made some appearances nonetheless.

==Mainline appearances==
The first cheese to appear is [[Ordon Goat Cheese]], particularly in {{TP}} and its remake {{TPHD}}. It lacks a resemblance to actual cheese.

The second cheese to appear is [[Hateno Cheese]] in {{TotK}}. It bears a much closer resemblance to a block of cheese in real life, and many cheesy dishes can be created from it.
