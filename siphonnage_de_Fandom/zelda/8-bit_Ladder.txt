The '''8-bit Ladder''' is a [[Weapon]] in {{HW}} and its reissues, {{HWL}} and {{HWDE}}. It is a [[Weapon]] for [[Sheik]] in the Master Quest downloadable pack. The 8-bit Ladder is the 8-bit equivalent of Sheik's [[Harp]], and it is statistically-identical to the [[Triforce Harp]]. The 8-bit Ladder is modeled after the [[Ladder]] sprite from {{TLoZ}}.
