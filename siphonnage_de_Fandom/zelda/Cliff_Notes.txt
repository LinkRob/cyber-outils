The {{Term/Store|Cliff Notes||Series, MM}} are an object in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Nintendo |page= 31}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Cliff Notes}} were referred to as the '''Skull Kid's Cleff''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 36}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{Guide|MM|Nintendo}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} The {{Term|Series|Cliff Notes}} go unnamed in {{MM3D}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|MM3D}}
The {{Term|MM3D|Cliff Notes}} are a set of musical notes arranged into a {{Term|MM3D|Song|link}} and set into a wall in the northern part of {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field|link}}. By playing the {{Plural|MM3D|Song}} on the wall, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can receive {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}}.
