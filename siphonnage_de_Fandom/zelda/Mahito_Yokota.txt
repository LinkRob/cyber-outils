'''Mahito Yokota''' is a sound designer employed at [[Nintendo EAD]]. Though his most celebrated work is featured in ''{{smw|Super Mario Galaxy}}'' and its {{smw|Super Mario Galaxy 2|sequel}}, Yokota's work extends to {{TLoZ|Series|-}} games too. He often works alongside [[Koji Kondo]]. Together they composed the music for {{TP}}<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Yes. I was in charge of the orchestral songs for The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess as well.|url=http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/0/0|site=iwataasks.nintendo.com; retrieved November 26 2013}}</ref> and most recently {{SS}}. He also directed the sound of {{OoT3D}}, in which his goal was to recreate Kondo's music for the [[Nintendo 3DS|3DS]] system<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=One was The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D and the other is The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword.|url=http://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/#/3ds/zelda-ocarina-of-time/0/0|site=iwataasks.nintendo.com; retrieved November 26 2013}}</ref>.
