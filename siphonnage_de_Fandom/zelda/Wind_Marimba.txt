==Location and Uses==
The Wind Marimba is one of the eight [[Instruments of the Sirens]] needed to awaken the [[Wind Fish]].<ref name="E 143"/> It can be found at the end of [[Catfish's Maw]] after defeating its boss, [[Slime Eel]].<ref name="E 143"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWL Wind Marimba Sprite.png|left|40px]]
In {{HW|L}}, the Wind Marimba appears as an [[Item Card]] in the [[Koholint Island Map]] of [[Adventure Mode]], which is included in the {{LA|-}} DLC Pack. It increases the amount of [[Rupee]]s earned.
{{Noncanon|End}}
