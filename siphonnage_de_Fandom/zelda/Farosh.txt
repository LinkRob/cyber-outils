{{Term/Store|Farosh||Series, BotW}} is a [[Dragon]] in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Farosh}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Farosh}}
{{Term|BotW|Farosh}} is one of three roaming Dragons in [[Hyrule]], the others being {{Term|BotW|Naydra|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}. While she cannot be defeated, she does not actively try to harm {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets too close, he will be damaged by the electricity surrounding her body, unless he possesses a full set of [[Rubber Armor]] that has been enhanced twice by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} or the [[Thunder Helm]] for the [[Unshockable]] [[Set Bonus]]. {{Term|BotW|Farosh}} will drop different {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots her with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}. Depending on where {{Term|BotW|Farosh}} is shot, she will drop a [[Shard of Farosh's Horn]], [[Shard of Farosh's Fang]], [[Farosh's Claw]], or [[Farosh's Scale]]. After being shot, the dragon will disappear until the next in-game day.

Farosh's Scale can be dropped into the [[Spring of Courage]] to reveal a hidden {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.
