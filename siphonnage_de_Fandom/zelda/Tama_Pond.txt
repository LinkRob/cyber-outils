{{Term/Store|Tama Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Tama Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Tama Pond is located east of [[Rito Village]] and west of [[Hebra Tower]] in the [[Tabantha Frontier]]. It is a small oval shaped pond with a flat rocky terrain on the north side, and a small cliff on the southern side.

On the southern side of Tama Pond, a [[Korok]] is hiding in a circle of [[Lily Pads]].
