{{Term/Store|Pumpkin Soup||Series, SS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=120 {{Exp Game|SS}}, 127 {{Exp Game|SS}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|SSHD, HWDE}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
Pumpkin Soup is sold at the [[Lumpy Pumpkin]] and is known all across [[The Sky]] for its ability to restore [[Heart]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Their famous pumpkin soup is popular in Skyloft for its nutrient content and revitalizing properties.|Fi|SS}}</ref> However, the soup will become cold if not consumed within five minutes after purchasing it, which decreases its healing capabilities. It will restore eight Hearts when hot and four Hearts when cold.

One of [[Link]]'s tasks requested by [[Pumm]] is to deliver a sample of hot Pumpkin Soup to [[Eagus]]. Later on, when Link needs to find the location of the [[Triforce]], he is told to ask [[Levias]] for help. [[Owlan]] tells Link that Pumm makes a yearly offering of Pumpkin Soup to Levias, but due to the [[Thunderhead]], he was unable to do so the current year. Once Link tells Pumm everything, he proceeds to cook a massive batch of Pumpkin Soup so large that [[Scrapper]] is required to transport it inside the Thunderhead.

In {{HW}}, during the "[[Land in the Sky]]" scenario, Link must assist [[Fi]] in transporting a batch of Pumpkin Soup in order to gain assistance from Levias. During this instance, Levias pushes a small floating island to make a bridge, allowing [[Forces|allied Forces]] to advance into enemy territory. The Pumpkin Soup is also used after the [[Great Fairy]] is called, sending [[Fairy|Fairies]] to give Levias another serving of the soup. Levias, rejuvenated by the soup, then attacks [[Volga]] and severely weakens him.

[[File:HWDE Pumpkin Soup Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Pumpkin Soup appears as a Drink-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. It is imbued with the Fire {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Pumpkin Soup can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}
