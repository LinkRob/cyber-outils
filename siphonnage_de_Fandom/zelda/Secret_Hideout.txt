The {{Term/Store|Secret Hideout||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|MM3D Red|code}} is {{Color|MM3D Red|XXXXX}}! The entrance to our {{Color|MM3D Red|secret hideout}} is in {{Color|MM3D Red|East Clock Town}}!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|MM3D|Secret Hideout}} is a section of the {{Term|MM3D|Astral Observatory|link}} that the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|link}} use as their headquarters. The entrance to the {{Term|MM3D|Secret Hideout}} is located in {{Term|MM3D|East Clock Town|link}} and is guarded by a lone {{Term|MM3D|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|display=Bomber}}. This {{Term|MM3D|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|display=Bomber}} will only let individuals pass into the {{Term|MM3D|Secret Hideout}} if they know their secret five-digit code.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to pass through here, you have to say the secret code!|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Umm... That's right. If you know the {{Color|MM3D Red|code}}, then you're a member, right? OK, here you go!|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|MM3D}}</ref>

{{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} can only obtain the code after passing {{Term|MM3D|Jim|link}}'s test.<ref>{{Cite|We Bombers have a {{Color|MM3D Red|hideout}} that leads to the {{Color|MM3D Red|observatory}} outside town. You need a {{Color|MM3D Red|code}} to get in. Maybe I'll tell you what it is!|Jim|MM3D}}<br/>{{Cite|But don't think you're getting it that easily! I can't just tell you what the code is... You'll have to pass my test first.|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> He challenges {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to find himself and four other {{Term|MM3D|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|display=Bombers}}, who will hide throughout {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town|link}}, before sunrise.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|MM3D Red|five}} of us will run and hide in different places all over town. If you can catch {{Color|MM3D Red|all}} of us {{Color|MM3D Red|before sunrise}}, we'll teach you the secret code!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> Once all five {{Term|MM3D|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|display=Bombers}} have been located, they will reveal the code to {{Term|MM3D|Link}}, though they will only tell him once.<ref>{{Cite|But I'll teach you the {{Color|MM3D Red|code}}, just like I promised. I can tell you only {{Color|MM3D Red|once}}, though, so pay close attention!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> The code is randomized in each playthrough.

A {{Term|MM3D|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|MM3D|Silver Rupee|link}} sits behind a cracked wall across the waterway to the side of the entrance.
