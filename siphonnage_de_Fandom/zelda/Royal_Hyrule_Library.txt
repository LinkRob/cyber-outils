The {{Term/Store|Royal Hyrule Library||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Royal Hyrule Library. Although the library is well loved, many forget to return their books, causing no end of trouble for the librarians.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Figurine|TMC|Library}}
Situated in the northwestern corner of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}} remains closed until after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} receives the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}} and uses it to [[Warping|warp]] to the {{Term|TMC|Wind Crest|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I came here to check out a book. They're supposed to be open by now, I wonder what's going on...|Klaus|TMC}}</ref> There, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must talk to the {{Term|TMC|Forest Picori|link}}, who tells him that to enter the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, he must speak to {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}}, as he is the only {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} who managed to make his way into the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|display=Temple}}. The {{Term|TMC|Minish}} also tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}} should be opening shortly, indicating that he may enter it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, ho! A visitor! How rare! But you have found me, the wisest of the wisest of the wise! You what? You want to enter the temple you can see in the lake? They call that place the Temple of Droplets. I have heard rumors that one Minish actually did find a way in, long ago... That Minish is Elder Librari, and he is in semi-retirement in the town library. The library should be opening soon. Perhaps you can go find him.|Forest Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, the elder Librari once found an entrance to the Temple of Droplets. And now, he lives in the town library! And this is all the wisdom of the wisest of the wise I have to offer to you!|Forest Minish|TMC}}</ref> 

When the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}} reopens, {{Term|TMC|Link}} is welcomed by {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|link}}, who recognizes him as {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}}'s grandson.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the library! hey! Aren't you Smith's grandson, Link? Enjoy your browsing!|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}} is in charge of {{Term|TMC|Library Book}} returns, which has proved problematic for her.<ref>{{Cite|Although the library is well loved, many forget to return their books, causing no end of trouble for the librarians.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Paige|link}} introduces herself as the person responsible for checking out books, declaring that the book returns are not her problem, though she still sympathizes with {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}}'s predicament.<ref>{{Cite|I'm in charge of checking out books, so it's not my problem. Still, I feel really bad for the girl over there in charge of returns...|Paige|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Klaus|link}} can be found inside the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} wondering what he should check out.<ref>{{Cite|Wonder what I should check out today.|Klaus|TMC}}</ref>

On the second floor of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}} waits {{Term|TMC|Sturgeon|link}}, who tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that the books have been moving seemingly of their own accord, unaware of the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} influence.<ref>{{Cite|Sometimes, these books move on their own! I've seen it with my own two eyes! An' don't tell me I'm crazy! I ain't crazy!|Sturgeon|TMC}}</ref> Sturgeon is a potential target for [[Kinstone Fusion]], and if {{Term|TMC|Link}} successfully fuses {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with him, the pond in the northwestern section of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle Garden|link}} will dry up, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to descend the newly-revealed stairway into the chamber to receive the {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} housed within.

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} becomes {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized and enters the bookcase that {{Term|TMC|Sturgeon}} is standing by, he will discover a {{Term|TMC|Minish}} settlement within the bookcase. A {{Term|TMC|Town Minish|link}} will tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} that because some humans have checked out books and failed to return them, Elder {{Term|TMC|Librari}} is stranded on the upper levels of the bookshelf. She asks for {{Term|TMC|Link}} to find out where they are from {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}} and retrieve them.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey, hey! This is no good! No good at all! The humans have checked some books out of the library! Thanks to them, none of us can get back to Elder Librari... How will he get by all alone? I'm so worried... I'm sure the librarian knows something about where those books went... I wish I were big. Why, I'd walk up and ask her where those books went!|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link}} can also fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with this {{Term|TMC|Town Minish}}, causing a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} to appear in the playground garden of the {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}. Another {{Term|TMC|Town Minish}} will complain that the books are missing, but celebrates the fact that because of it, she does not have to listen to {{Term|TMC|Librari}}'s "boring old stories".<ref>{{Cite|We can't get up the shelf, because some human checked that book out! But at least we don't have to listen to Elder Librari's boring old stories!|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref>

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} speaks to {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}}, she will direct him to {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}}, who is overdue on {{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? The books missing from the bookshelf on the second floor? Yes, they've been checked out for quite a while. They're VERY overdue! Take A Hyrulean Bestiary, for example. Been gone for ages. Checked out by a girl with a cat. She said she had a new cat at her house. Wanted to learn more about it, she did.|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns {{Term|TMC|A Hyrulean Bestiary}}, {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}} will thank him and send him after {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Look at that! Our long-lost copy of A Hyrulean Bestiary! Link, are you returning this book?! Thank you so much! I can't begin to describe how much I'm looking forward to putting this back!|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> which is in the possession of {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The next overdue book is... Let's see... Ah, yes! Of course...Legend of the Picori! It's been out forever! It was checked out by a somewhat absentminded scholar, I believe...|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref>

The third book, {{Term|TMC|A History of Masks|link}}, was checked out by {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen|link}}.

Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} retrieves the three books and returns them to {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)}}, she will give them to {{Term|TMC|Sturgeon}}, the person in charge of putting books back on the shelves.<ref>{{Cite|You know, Link, you've now returned all of our overdue books! Now I can finally get that bookshelf on the second floor in order! Hey, Sturgeon! Books to shelve!!|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> With this done, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can now climb the books as a {{Term|TMC|Minish}}, speak to {{Term|TMC|Librari}}, and obtain the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} after passing his trial.<ref>{{Cite|Wohoho! Wasn't that fantastic? Now then... It's up to you to pass this trial safely and recover the tool you need. Oh, what fun! I do so love the real nitty-gritty of adventuring!|Librari|TMC}}</ref>

Inside the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can fuse {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Paige}}, as well as with {{Term|TMC|Klaus|link}}, an elderly man found either inside or in front of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}}.

After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} takes over {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} and turns it into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Vassal|link}} will block the entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}}.
