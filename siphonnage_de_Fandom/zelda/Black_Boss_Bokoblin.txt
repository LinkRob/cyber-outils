== Appearance ==
[[Boss Bokoblin|Boss Bokoblins]] started appearing after the Upheaval. They are powerful enemies who are found leading a group of [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]]. The Black Boss Bokoblin are even more powerful than the [[Blue Boss Bokoblin|Blue variant]] make them an even tougher opponent. They are black colored with yellow marking all over their body and their horn is a double axe with two purple banners indicating their leadership. They also have a lot of attacks. Including commanding minions to chuck rocks, and charge attack. They can also ram Link with their horns too...
