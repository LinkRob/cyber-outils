{{Term/Store|Bago-Bago|Bago-Bago|Series, TAoL|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 164 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Bago-Bago hide in the rivers beneath bridges until [[Link]] gets close. They swoop out of the water and, after landing, move along the ground and attempt to swarm him. They will shoot [[Rock]]s at Link from their mouths, even when leaping. As they appear on bridges, they are likely to knock Link into the water or lava below, causing him to lose a life. They drain 10 [[Experience]] points on contact.
