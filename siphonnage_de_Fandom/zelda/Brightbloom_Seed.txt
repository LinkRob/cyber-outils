'''Brightbloom Seeds''' are a material in {{TotK}}. They are used to illuminate dark areas. They can be found in caves and other underground areas. It has a Fuse Attack Power of 1.

<blockquote>
A seed that's often found in caves or wells. When struck, it will take root and bloom, giving off faint light from a small flower.
</blockquote>

==Locations and Uses==
==={{TotK}}===
They are mostly useful in the [[The Depths|Depths]]. Since the Depths are dark, the Brightbloom Seeds allow you to see around you far better.

[[Frox|Froxes]] and [[Little Frox|their small counterparts]] can eat them, but this can be used against them as a distraction. They can drop such seeds as well.
