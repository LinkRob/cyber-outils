The {{Term/Store|Bareeda Naag Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Bareeda Naag Shrine}}<br/>Cannon}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Cannon''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an [[Ancient Shrine]] in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Bareeda Naag Shrine}} is located on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} just south of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}. It can only be accessed by completing "{{Term|BotW|The Ancient Rito Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can start the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Bedoli|link}} after completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. He can ask {{Term|BotW|Bedoli}} to teach him the song she is singing, which she will agree to.<ref>{{Cite|[Teach it to me!] Huh? Well that's embarrassing... *ahem* The pride of the Rito, pillar in the sky, its heart lights up, when the sun is high.|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref> However, she has forgotten half of the lyrics, so she sends him to her sister {{Term|BotW|Laissa|link}} to learn the rest.<ref>{{Cite|...Uhhh. There's more to the song, but I forget the rest of the words. You should ask {{Color|BotW Blue|my sister}}, Laissa, if you want to know the rest.|Bedoli|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laissa}} will only provide the rest of the lyrics to {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he can recite what {{Term|BotW|Bedoli}} taught him.<ref>{{Cite|I can help you...but there is a catch! Recite the first half from memory, and I will teach you the second half.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to successfully repeat the song for her, {{Term|BotW|Laissa}} will honor her promise.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! You got every word right! Well, a promise is a promise. I'll teach you the last half of the song. The heart shines upon a path not whole, but a warming flame can stir its soul.|Laissa|BotW}}</ref>

South of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} can be found. When the sun hits the pillar that {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} is standing upon, the hole will cast a heart shape onto the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, causing it to start glowing. When this happens, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must light a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}}, at which point {{Term|BotW|Bareeda Naag Shrine}} will unearth itself.<ref>{{Cite|The lyrics in the ancient song from the Rito Village were actually a clue to lead to the entrance of a shrine!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he will hear the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} {{Term|BotW|Bareeda Naag|link}} introducing him to the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Bareeda Naag. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}.|Bareeda Naag|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Immediately inside {{Term|BotW|Bareeda Naag Shrine}} is a large {{Term|BotW|Cannon|link}} that spherical {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} can be inserted into. Detonating the {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|display=Bomb}} will send an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Cannon}} towards a target on the other side of the room. Several moving platforms act as obstacles for this target.

Hitting the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} against the target will turn it blue, and it will open the door blocking the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Bareeda Naag}}, they will commend him for overcoming the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero...}}|Bareeda Naag|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Bareeda Naag|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bareeda Naag}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Bareeda Naag|BotW}}</ref>

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found behind four cracked blocks near the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. Inside this {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} is a {{Term|BotW|Falcon Bow|link}}. Past the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find another {{Term|BotW|Cannon}}. While there are still moving platforms blocking the target, the target itself now moves back and forth as well. Hitting this target will open another door, behind which is a second stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.
