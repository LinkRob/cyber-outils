{{Term/Store|Stolock Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Stolock Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Stolock Bridge is a long, rickety bridge in [[Eldin Canyon]] that crosses overhead [[Goron City]]. The Bridge crosses the two ledges between the [[Abandoned North Mine]] and the continuing road that ultimately leads to [[Death Mountain]]. The Bridge can easily be reached by following the road through the Abandoned North Mine, which leads to a tunnel inhabited by [[Rock Octorok]]s before finally the Bridge itself.
