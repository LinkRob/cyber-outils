{{Term/Store|Sir Frosty|Sir Frosties|Series, ST|plural}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 203}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Sir Frosties are commonly found in the [[Snow Realm]] and often appear near tracks as the [[Spirit Train]] passes. They typically appear in pairs and resemble simple snowmen, with a round body and a smaller head of snow. When [[Link]] approaches, a Sir Frosty launches its head toward the Spirit Train to deal damage, then regenerates it to continue attacking. They can be easily defeated using the [[cannon]], but require two hits—one to destroy the head and another to destroy the body. The head can also be destroyed midair with the cannon, preventing it from hitting the train.
