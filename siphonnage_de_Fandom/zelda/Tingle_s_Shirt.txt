{{Term/Store|Tingle's Shirt||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Tingle's Shirt}}<br/>There was once a man who claimed to be the very reincarnation of a fairy. He treasured this shirt, and so will you. After all, it's a rather rare find. Kooloo-Limpah!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It's only available if [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack is active.

==Location and Uses==
Tingle's Shirt can be found as part of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes]]". It's buried in the ruins of the [[Castle Town Prison]] and can be lifted from the ground with [[Magnesis]]. After obtaining it, [[Granté]] will begin to sell Tingle's Shirt in [[Tarrey Town]]. It's part of [[Tingle's Set]] along with [[Tingle's Hood]] and [[Tingle's Tights]]. Tingle's Shirt offers no additional effects on its own or as part of a set. It can't be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} or [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
