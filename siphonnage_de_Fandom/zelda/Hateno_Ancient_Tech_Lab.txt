The {{Term/Store|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab||Series, BotW}}  is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab is now set as a travel gate.}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> It is north of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} and is owned by {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} researchers {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Symin|link}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|BotW}}
The {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}} is located on a hill just north of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. It appears to be similar in design and material to the houses and {{Plural|BotW|Silo|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}, but has {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} inhabitants instead of {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}. There is a large {{Term|BotW|Telescope|link}} attached to the body of the building. It is the main objective for the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Locked Mementos|link}}"

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first encounters the Lab, it will appear on his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, but will not be available as a {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}} point. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the Lab, he is greeted by Purah. She instructs him to obtain a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} to power up the Lab's {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, the Lab is made functional once again and becomes a {{Term|BotW|Warping}} point. The Lab's {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} also regains its functionality, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to restore the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.

After speaking to {{Term|BotW|Purah|linK}} about {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} may trade in certain {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}} to her in exchange for upgraded {{Plural|BotW|Rune}}. Three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Screw|link}} gets him the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor +|link}}, three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shaft|link}} gets him {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb +|link}}, and three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core|link}} can get him {{Term|BotW|Stasis +|link}}. After purchasing the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor +}} ability and completing the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Sunshroom Sensing|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} may buy pictures in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}} from {{Term|BotW|Symin|link}}.

Inside {{Term|BotW|Purah's Room|link}} at the top of the Lab, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can read {{Term|BotW|Purah's Diary|link}}. However, if he reads her diary, she will pretend to take away his ancient {{Plural|BotW|Rune}}.
