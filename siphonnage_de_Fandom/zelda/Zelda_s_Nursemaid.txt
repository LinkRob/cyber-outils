'''Zelda's Nursemaid''' is a character in {{AST}}.{{Name Ref|AST}} She is {{Term|AST|Princess Zelda|link}}'s caretaker.

==Biography==
{{Stub|AST}}
{{Term|AST|Zelda's Nursemaid}} appears in {{Term|AST|Hyrule Castle|link}}, where she watches over {{Term|AST|Princess Zelda}} and keeps her safe. During an optional sidequest in the first week, the {{Term|AST|Player|link}} may rescue {{Term|AST|Princess Zelda}} and escort her back to the {{Term|AST|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} where she stays behind with her {{Term|AST|Zelda's Nursemaid|display=Nursemaid}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|AST|Zelda's Nursemaid}} shares a similar role to {{Term|Series|Impa}}, who has consistently been {{Term|Series|Princess Zelda}}'s nursemaid in the series. The {{Term|AST|Zelda's Nursemaid|display=Nursemaid}} in {{AST|-}}, however, is left unnamed and may not be {{Term|Series|Impa}}.
