The {{Term/Store|Temple of Fire||Series, PH}} is the first {{Term|PH|Dungeon}} that {{Term|PH|Link}} visits in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 156}}</ref> It is located on the {{Term|PH|Isle of Ember|link}}. It protects the {{Term|PH|Leaf|display=Spirit of Power}}.<ref>{{Cite|This temple protects the Spirit of Power.|N/A|PH}}</ref>

==Entrance to the {{Term|PH|Temple of Fire|display=Temple}}==
The {{Term|PH|Temple of Fire}} is located on the higher levels of the {{Term|PH|Isle of Ember|link}}'s mountain, which can only be accessed after {{Term|PH|Link|link}} has rescued the {{Term|PH|Fortune Teller|link}}, {{Term|PH|Astrid|link}}, and has his fortune told by her.<ref>{{Cite|To open the summit gate, you need what the fortune-teller has to offer.|Gossip Stone|PH}}</ref> When {{Term|PH|Link}} first arrives at the {{Term|PH|Temple of Fire|display=Temple}}'s entrance, it is sealed shut. To open it, he has to blow out the two candles on either side of the door.<ref>{{Cite|Blow out the flames to illuminate the path ahead.|N/A|PH}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
As the {{Term|PH|Temple of Fire}}'s name implies, it is themed around {{Term|PH|Fire|link}}. There are numerous pits of {{Term|PH|Lava}} which can hamper {{Term|PH|Link}}'s path, as well as moving bodies of {{Term|PH|Fire}} that can burn him.<ref>{{Cite|... Oh, no! There are flames everywhere! You better not touch them...|Ciela|PH}}</ref> The {{Term|PH|Dungeon}} has five floors in all and features a small variety of enemies. Many of the puzzles in the {{Term|PH|Dungeon}} involve the {{Term|PH|Dungeon}}'s primary {{Term|PH|Item}}, the {{Term|PH|Boomerang|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You got the boomerang! This item follows the path you draw on the screen!|N/A|PH}}</ref> sometimes requiring {{Term|PH|Link}} to hit {{Plural|PH|Crystal Switch|link}} in a certain order. The {{Term|PH|Boss}}, who is supposedly the cause of the volcano erupting,<ref>{{Cite|The volcano has stopped erupting, Link! I'll bet it's because you defeated all the monsters, Link!|Ciela|PH}}</ref> is a flame sorcerer by the name of {{Term|PH|Blaaz|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Blaaz, Master of Fire|N/A|PH}}</ref> After clearing the {{Term|PH|Dungeon}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} will receive a {{Term|PH|Heart Container|link}} and the first of the three {{Plural|PH|Spirit|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I am Leaf, Spirit of Power! I serve the Ocean King. I owe you thanks for defeating that evil wizard. You broke the seal, so now I'm free!|Leaf|PH}}</ref> {{Term|PH|Leaf|link}},<ref>{{Cite|It's the Spirit of Power, Leaf!|Ciela|PH}}</ref> who explains that he was locked away with the {{Term|PH|Ocean King}} a long time ago, and wants to join {{Term|PH|Link}} in order to find out what happened to him.<ref>{{Cite|I was locked away with the Ocean King years ago. But now I can't feel the Ocean King's presence...I wonder what happened to him? If I come with you, maybe I can find out. If you need it, I will gladly lend a hand!|Leaf|PH}}</ref>
