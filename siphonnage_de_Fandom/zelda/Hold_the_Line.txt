{{Term/Store|Hold the Line||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold the Line|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hold the Line}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must capture four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} while defending two Allied Strongholds. A {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, or {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} spawns at each uncaptured {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} after certain amounts of time and attempts to capture the Allied Strongholds. Also, after one {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} Turret appears in each enemy {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. After two are captured, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}} spawn on opposite sides of the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Korok Seed|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
