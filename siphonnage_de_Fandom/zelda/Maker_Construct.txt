'''Maker Constructs''' are friendly [[Construct|constructs]] from {{TotK}}.

==Characteristics==
A total of seven Maker Constructs reside on the [[Great Sky Island]]. Of these, four are positioned near rafts and are capable of providing [[Link]] with an [[Energy Cell]] upon interaction.
