{{Term/Store|Aris Beach||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aris Beach|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Aris Beach}} is located along the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. It is south of {{Term|BotW|Ubota Point|link}} and {{Term|BotW|North Aris Beach|link}}.

In {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can receive the "{{Term|BotW|Take Back the Sea|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Sebasto|link}} explains that monsters have taken over many of the {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|display=Village's}} best fishing spots, including {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Monsters}} have taken over the best fishing spots. You used to be able to pull in a good haul at {{Color|BotW Blue|Aris Beach}}, but these days you'd be lucky to leave there with your life.|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears tough, he asks him to clear out the monsters.<ref>{{Cite|You look pretty tough.<br/>Think you could go take care of them?|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} he is referring to is located just off the eastern coast of {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach}}. The monsters that inhabit it are {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Blue Moblin|link}}. To access it, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can either swim or take the {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} that can be found on the {{Term|BotW|Clarnet Coast|link}}. After all of the monsters have been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to access the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|display=Stronghold's}} {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Halberd|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Sebasto}} will reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} for completing his request.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks, mate. I suppose I should give you this to show my appreciation.|Sebasto|BotW}}</ref>

A smaller {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}} can be found on the western end of {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach}}, consisting of a {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} and two {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} here has four {{Plural|BotW|Boko Spear|link}} staked into the ground surrounding it, three of which bear {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Bass|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} at this {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|display=Stronghold}} contains five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}.

Several other {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found in the sand and waters surrounding {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach}}. The contents of these {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chests}} are a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Topaz|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Giant Boomerang|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.
