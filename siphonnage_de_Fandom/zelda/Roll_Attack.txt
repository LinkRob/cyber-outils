The {{Term/Store|Roll Attack|Roll Attacks|Series, TMC}} is a recurring ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Roll Attack|Tiger Scroll|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{FSA|-}}===
If the {{Term|FSA|B Button|link}} is pressed as {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} finishes {{Term|FSA|Rolling|link}} in {{FSA}}, he will perform a {{Term|FSA|Spin Attack|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can obtain a {{Term|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}} to learn a {{Term|TMC|Sword Technique|link}} known as the {{Term|TMC|Roll Attack}} in {{TMC}}; it is taught to him by {{Term|TMC|Grayblade|link}}. To use it, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must {{Term|TMC|Rolling|display=Roll|link}} and swing his {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}} just before the {{Term|TMC|Rolling|display=Roll}} finishes.<ref>{{Cite|Roll! Then swing your sword as you start to get up!|Tiger Scroll|TMC}}</ref> If he does so, he will slide forward with his {{Term|TMC|Sword}} outstretched, and will perform a {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack}} if he runs into an enemy or item.

==={{TP|-}}===
Rather than a {{Term|TPHD|Spin Attack|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} performs a forward thrust after rising from a {{Term|TPHD|Rolling|link|display=Roll}} in {{TP}}. Unlike most of the {{Term|TPHD|Sword}} techniques in the game, it is not a {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}}; it can be used from the moment {{Term|TPHD|Link}} picks up a {{Term|TPHD|Sword}}.
