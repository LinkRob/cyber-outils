{{Term/Store|Spirit Battle|Spirit Battles|Series, SSBU|plural}} are special Battles in {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite|Spirit Battles<br/>When you defeat a puppet fighter, you'll free the spirit trapped within it. Free as many spirits as you can!|N/A|SSBU}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{#section:Spirit|Var}}
Spirit Battles are accessible either via the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} or through [[Adventure Mode]].

After a Spirit Battle, the Fighter must shoot the Puppet Fighter, which is encased in a rotating shield, in order to obtain the Spirit. Shooting the Shield will cause that portion of the shield to break. Upon completing the Spirit Battle again, any portions where the shield has been destroyed will stay missing, allowing for the Fighter to more easily strike the Puppet Fighter inside.
