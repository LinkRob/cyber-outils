{{Term/Store|Black Tektite|Black Tektites|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Black Tektite|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Black Tektite}} are the strongest variety of {{Term|CoH|Tektite|link}}, being capable of dealing two {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}} of damage to {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. Like other {{Plural|CoH|Tektite}}, they move to the beat of the music but possess their own unique movement pattern, crouching on one music beat before jumping twice to attack the heroes on the second and third beats. They can only move diagonally and jump on and off objects standing one block tall.
