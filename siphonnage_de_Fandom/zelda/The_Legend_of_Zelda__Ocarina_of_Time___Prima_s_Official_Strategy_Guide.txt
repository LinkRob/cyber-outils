'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — Prima's Official Strategy Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{OoT}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 144 pages and contains a complete walkthrough of {{OoT}}, including top-down dungeon maps and an abundance of screenshots. The first few pages contain a quick retrospective on previous games in the series. The guide contains information on collectibles and secrets such as {{Plural|OoT|Heart Container|link}}, {{Plural|OoT|Gold Skulltula|link}}, {{Plural|OoT|Fairy|link}}, and {{Plural|OoT|Magic Bean|link}}. It also has a complete chart of {{Plural|OoT|Item|link}}, including cost, location, and a short description for each.
