{{Plural|BotW|Serpentine Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by members of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} tribe.<ref name="Inventory"/> Spear masters are able to catch opponents with their crescent shape and deliver brutal cuts.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Serpentine Spear|display=Spears}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear|link}} equipment archetype, consisting of all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created and utilized by the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref> They are found in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, particularly around {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, as well as in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}.

Since {{Plural|BotW|Serpentine Spear}} are made of metal, they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are also unable to burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. If they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they will sink, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
