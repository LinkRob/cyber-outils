{{Term/Store|Rist Peninsula||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.{{Fact}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula}} spans from the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Sea|link}} on the eastern edge of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. It is a spiraled sandbar. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} places an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} at the center of the peninsula, the {{Term|BotW|Ritaag Zumo Shrine}} will rise up from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|Only a hero bearing this {{Color|BotW Red|ancient orb}} shall be granted the right of {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} on Rist Peninsula.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref>

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
It is where the last [[The Dragon's Tears|Dragon Tear]] is located.

==Trivia==
* The spiral shape of Rist Peninsula bears a strong resemblance to the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiral_Jetty Spiral Jetty], an earthwork sculpture created by Robert Smithson in 1970.  It is unknown if this was intentionally done by Nintendo.
* {{Term|BotW|Rist Peninsula}}'s name may be an anagram of the word ''stir'', as it features a spiral pattern.  This is supported by the fact that its Japanese name is an anagram of ''uzumaki'', the word for spirals or whirlpools.
