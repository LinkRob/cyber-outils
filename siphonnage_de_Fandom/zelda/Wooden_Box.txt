==Location and Uses==
A crate is a hollow box that often comes in two sizes. Link can lift and throw the smaller variety in order to bust it open, but he must roll into or set an explosive by the larger variant to achieve the same effect. In {{BotW}}, Link can also set the crates on [[fire]] to release the contents, but doing so will cause any food [[material]]s to become roasted. Link can climb atop the larger boxes without breaking them to gain a broader view of the surrounding area or to gain extra height in a jump. The larger crates also come in an indestructible variety which can be pushed, allowing Link to use them to reach higher areas or to uncover hidden passages. If anything, single recovery hearts or nominal [[Rupee]]s can be found inside these boxes. However, in {{OoT}}, crates often hide [[Gold Skulltula]]s. Smaller crates, as seen in {{OoT|-}}, {{MM}}, and {{TWW}} can also be used to activate and sustain a Switch. In {{TWW|-}}, the smaller variants can float on top of water, which allows them to be used as miniature platforms in the [[Tower of the Gods]].

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Wooden Box|A large, wooden box made of planks that is light enough to float on water.<ref name="EoW Wooden Box">{{Cite|A large, wooden box made of planks that is light enough to float on water.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Wooden Boxes are objects found across [[Hyrule]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Wooden Box [[Echo]] after using the [[Tri Rod]] on one, allowing her to summon Boxes as needed. They can block wind, be used as platforms, and float on water.
