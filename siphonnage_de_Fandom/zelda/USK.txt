The '''Unterhaltungssoftware Selbstkontrolle''' is the organisation that is responsible for video game ratings in [[Wikipedia:Germany|Germany]] and parts of [[Wikipedia:Austria|Austria]] where they enforce the Jugendschutzgesetz (JuSchG) Protection of Young Persons Act. In Australia it is the Australian Classification Board ([[ACB]]), in Europe it is the Pan-European Game Information ([[PEGI]]) and in North America it is the Entertainment Software Rating Board ([[ESRB]]).
