==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Titanium Short Sword}} are a stronger variant of {{Term|CoH|Short Sword|link}} that always deal 2 damage to a single enemy. Like all {{Plural|CoH|Short Sword}}, they can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, but can only be acquired either after he infuses a Short Sword with {{Term|CoH|Titanium|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if he opens the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an already-infused one at the north end of {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Titanium Dagger|link}}.
