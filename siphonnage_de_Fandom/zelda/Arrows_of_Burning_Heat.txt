{{Term/Store|Arrows of Burning Heat||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Arrows of Burning Heat}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins at the {{Term|BotW|The Curious Quiver|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, after speaking to {{Term|BotW|Rola|link}} while in possession of a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Rola}} will note that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be a fine archer and will let it slip that she finds such men very attractive.<ref>{{Cite|That's right, my good man! I knew it the moment I saw ya... I had you figured for one heck of an archer. And I'm head over heels for a fine slice of man that can use a bow!|Rola|BotW}}</ref> She asks him to show her some of his skill, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can either accept or decline.<ref>{{Cite|So how about it? Why not take out that bow and show me something special?|Rola|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, she makes her disappointment known and tells him she would have given him something for his time. <ref>{{Cite|[Um... No, thanks.] Oh. That's a shame... If you had entertained me, I was going to reward you with something more than worth your time.|Rola|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rola}} will continue to fawn over {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he speaks to her after refusing her quest and is startled out of her daydreaming.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm... An archer with a strong build... I could look at you all day! ...Oh! Sorry--my imagination ran away with me. Did you need something?|Rola|BotW}}</ref> Should he decide to leave again, {{Term|BotW|Rola}} bemoans her lack of luck.<ref>{{Cite|[Never mind.] Where can I find the perfect man to show me a feat of skill...|Rola|BotW}}</ref>}}

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts her request, {{Term|BotW|Rola}} explains that she would like him to light the torches around the {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|display=Village's}} {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[You got it!] I like your attitude! All you gotta do is {{Color|BotW Red|light}} the {{Color|BotW Red|torches by the Goddess Statue}}. Think you're up for it?|Rola|BotW}}</ref> If he is unsure about what or where she means, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Rola}} to direct him to it.<ref>{{Cite|[Goddess Statue?] You really don't know about the {{Color|BotW Red|Goddess Statue}}? It's by the {{Color|BotW Red|spring}} in front of Lady Impa's home. I don't know who put it there, but it's been there forever.|Rola|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rola}} becomes uncertain if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|display=Statue}}, however.<ref>{{Cite|So...are you gonna do it or not?|Rola|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} fully accepts the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Rola}} fawns over him once more.<ref>{{Cite|The thought of a young hunk drawing a bow just makes my day!|Rola|BotW}}</ref> Should he return to her before completing the quest, {{Term|BotW|Rola}} will be sure to remind him of it.<ref>{{Cite|Don't go forgetting our promise now.|Rola|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|More importantly... You better not forget the promise you made!|Rola|BotW}}</ref> She will be upset if {{Term|BotW|Link}} forgets about the task,<ref>{{Cite|[Promise?] You forgot already?! You were going to {{Color|BotW Red|light}} the {{Color|BotW Red|torches}} by the {{Color|BotW Red|Goddess Statue}}. I'm looking forward to it, so don't mess it up!|Rola|BotW}}</ref> but is still clearly excited.<ref>{{Cite|Heh, my heart's practically singing... I haven't felt this way in years.|Rola|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} lights the torches as instructed, he must return to {{Term|BotW|Rola}} in {{Term|BotW|The Curious Quiver}}. She admits that she was watching him and is very impressed.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey, kid! I saw you!}} You were amazing! Boy, what a shot in the heart that was!|Rola|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} lit the torches without using {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rola}} will be disappointed, but she reckons that a man will always go against a woman's expectations.<ref>{{Cite|Although... What I really wanted was for you to light the fire using an arrow... But I suppose a man is ultimately doomed to betray a woman's expectations. Don't you think?|Rola|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s inconsiderate actions remind her of {{Term|BotW|Cado}}.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't seen him in a while...for various reasons...but you being inconsiderate reminds me of my husband.|Rola|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Rola}} takes the moment to mention how her estranged husband {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}} is also a great archer.<ref>{{Cite|My husband's a fine archer himself... but we're living apart right now. This feeling... It's been so, so long.|Rola|BotW}}</ref> She expresses her excitement with by giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|I'm so excited right now!}} That was really something special to see. In fact, here... A token of my thanks.|Rola|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rola}} mutters how she wishes her husband had continued with his archery, ending the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|If only my husband hadn't become obsessed with {{Color|BotW Red|Cuccos}} and had kept up with his archery skills... If only...}}|Rola|BotW}}</ref>
