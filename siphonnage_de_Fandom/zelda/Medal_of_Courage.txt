{{Term/Store|Medal of Courage|Medals of Courage|Series, FS|plural}} are {{Plural|FS|Item|link}} in {{FS}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= ALttP&FS|page= 37}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After completing a {{Term|FS|Stage|link}}, a {{Term|FS|Medal of Courage}} is awarded to the {{Term|FS|Link|link}} that obtained the greatest number of {{Plural|FS|Rupee|link}}. The total number of {{Plural|FS|Medal of Courage|display=Medals}} won can be seen on the {{Term|ALttP|Player Select Screen|link}}. In {{FS}}, obtaining ten {{Plural|FS|Medal of Courage}} unlocks the {{Term|ALttP|Lumberjack's Riddles|link}} in {{ALttP}}. In {{FSAE}}, collecting five {{Plural|FS|Medal of Courage}} in {{Term|FS|Multiplayer|link}} unlocks the {{Term|FS|Hero's Trial|link}} {{Term|FS|Stage}}.
