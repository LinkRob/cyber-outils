{{Term/Store|Aryll||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> She is the younger sister of [[Link]], specifically the Hero of Winds incarnation from the {{TWW}} and {{PH}}.

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:Link and Aryll.jpg|thumb|269x269px|Link and Aryll at the start of the former's adventure in {{TWW|-}}|left]]
Aryll is [[Link]]'s younger sister who lives with him and their [[Link's Grandma|Grandma]] on [[Outset Island]]. Much like her older brother, the Hero of Winds, whom she slightly resembles, Aryll is a very kind, mature and thoughtful person who is eager to help others, especially her Grandma. She often calls Link, “Big Brother.”<ref name="carry" /><ref>{{Cite|Her current goal is to learn how to fetch water so she can help out her grandmother.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> One of her favorite hobbies is to hang out at Outset Island's lookout and play with the [[Seagull]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Hee hee hee! This is my FAVORITE spot to gaze out at the sea. When I play with the gulls, I call it "Aryll's Lookout."|Aryll|TWW}}</ref> Her [[Telescope]] is her most cherished possession, but she lends it to Link as a special gift for his birthday.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to give you my most treasured belonging...but just for one day!|Aryll|TWW}}</ref> Soon after, she and Link notice a giant bird known as [[Helmaroc King]] soaring in the skies while carrying [[Tetra]] in its talons. [[Tetra's Pirate Crew]] successfully hit the Helmaroc King with a cannon ball, causing Tetra to fall into the nearby [[Fairy Woods]]. Aryll urges her brother to go and rescue the pirate girl.<ref>{{Cite|Link, this is so terrible! That girl fell into the forest! You have to go help her!|Aryll|TWW}}</ref> After Link does so, the giant bird mistakes Aryll for Tetra,<ref>{{Cite|And unless my eyes fail me, the young girl who was just kidnapped from this island also has long ears, does she not? Much like YOU do, Miss Fearsome Pirate. My point is that the bird mistook that poor girl for you, and that's why it grabbed her!|Quill|TWW}}</ref> taking her away to the [[Forsaken Fortress]].<ref>{{Cite|I may as well tell you that the bird that kidnapped both you and Link's sister has made its foul nest to the north, on the heights of the Forsaken Fortress.|Quill|TWW}}</ref> It then becomes Link's sworn priority to save her.

After some persuasion, Tetra allows Link to come with her on her [[Tetra's Ship|ship]] and travel to the Forsaken Fortress in an attempt to rescue his sister. whom he sees is imprisoned with two other girls, [[Mila]] and [[Maggie (The Wind Waker)|Maggie]]. However, Link is captured by the Helmaroc King before he can get to Aryll, and the bird launches him off into the [[Great Sea]], after he sees [[Ganondorf]], the monster's master, for the first time.

It is soon revealed that the siblings are very close. When the [[King of Red Lions]] rescues and meets Link, the young hero sincerely admits to the boat that he will do anything to rescue his younger sister and defeat Ganondorf, even if it means risking his own life. It became clear that the Hero of Winds' adventure in {{TWW}} is merely revenge for Aryll's kidnapping.
[[File:Link saving Aryll.png|thumb|263x263px|Tetra interrupting Aryll's reunion with Link after being rescued in {{TWW|-}}]]
Later, Link returns to the Forsaken Fortress and is finally able to rescue Aryll. However, before the two could reunite with heartwarming affection, Tetra interrupts him about the Helmaroc King and her crew of pirates take Aryll away and she promises to return her safely to Outset Island, while Link himself is forced to settle his business with the evil bird that kidnapped his sister and eventually Ganondorf.<ref>{{Cite|If you're worried about your sister...don't be. I promise I'll deliver her safely back to your little island. Normally, I'd expect a huge reward for something like this...but this time, it's my treat!|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> As such, Aryll spends the rest of the game traveling with the band of pirates, sending her older brother letters from time to time.<ref>{{Cite|How have you been, Big Brother? I'm here on the pirate ship writing you this letter. Isn't that neat?|Aryll|TWW}}</ref> Aryll also has a change of wardrobe during her stay with the pirates: instead of her usual light blue tropical dress, she changes to a dark purple one with a skull near the bottom. She also wears this dress during the [[Second Quest]].
[[File:TWW Aryll emotional goodbye.png|thumb|276x276px|Aryll, fighting back tears, waving goodbye to her older brother Link at the very end of {{TWW|-}}]]
Aryll is seen again at the end of the game aboard the pirates' ship after Link and Tetra surface from the now-flooded [[Hyrule]] following the ultimate defeat of Ganondorf (in which Link finally avenges his sister). Then, at post-credits, with her and Grandma emotional, she appears one last time waving goodbye to her brother before Link and Tetra leave for good in search of new lands, leading to the events of {{PH}}, in which Aryll herself is never seen or even mentioned in this game. Therefore, it is unknown what lies of Aryll later in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Adult Timeline]].

==Trivia==
[[File:BotW Link's Father Concept Art.jpg|thumb|Unused concept art of Link's little sister in {{BotW}}, from ''Creating a Champion''.|189x189px]]
*Aryll, due to her cute appearance in resemblance to [[Link]] and brave behavior, is beloved among [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] community, and many fans wished to see her return in a future game.
*A piece of artwork for {{BotW}} included in the Japanese version of ''[[Creating a Champion]]'' is labeled {{Romanize|家族|kazoku|family|2}} in Japanese and features {{BotW|-}}'s Link with a young girl, presumably a little sister like Aryll. This art is not included in the English version of the book, and the character never appeared in the final version of the game. It is unknown if she would have been a new version of Aryll or have been named something else.
**[[Linkle]] from ''[[Hyrule Warriors Legends]]'', the female doppelganger of Link, was also originally going to be the hero's younger sister, but this was rejected by [[Eiji Aonuma]], as it would conflict with Aryll.

* Aryll shares the same voice actress as her brother in {{TWW}} - [[List of Voice Actors|Sachi Matsumoto]].
** She is also the voice of the [[Skull Kid]] from {{MM}}.
* Aryll being the damsel-in-distress in {{TWW|-}} mirrors [[Princess Zelda]]'s role in the early games of the ''Zelda'' series and even the title itself.
* Being Link's sibling, it is possible that Aryll possesses the spirit of the [[Hylia's Chosen Hero|Legendary Hero]] as well, or at least a fraction thereof. Alongside having a rather courageous and kind personality like him, her letter she gives to her brother late in the story says she wants to become strong like him and not to worry about her.
* In {{PH}}, Aryll is referenced by [[Nyeve]] when Link trades the [[Hero's New Clothes]] for the Telescope. He jokes that it was originally a gift that he had borrowed from his younger sister.<ref>{{Cite|This is something valuable that I borrowed from my little sister...Ahhh ha ha! Just kidding.|Nyeve|PH}}</ref>Aryll's Trophy in the North American version of ''Super Smash Bros. Brawl'' erroneously claims her Telescope is marked with Turtles instead of Seagulls. This error occurred due to the Japanese word for seagull (カモメ) being misinterpreted as the Japanese word for turtle (カメ). This was corrected in the PAL version.[[File:TWW Aryll fighting enemies (hack).png|thumb|245x245px|A modified gameplay of {{TWW|-}}, showing Aryll in her skull dress fighting [[Miniblin|Miniblins]]]]
* Although not intended by [[Nintendo]], with use of ROM hacking, ironically, it is possible to replace Link with Aryll as the protagonist of {{TWW|-}}. Due to her model being switched from her brother's, however, Aryll appears as his height and her eyebrows are in a lighter shade of yellow. She shares the same voice clips as Link but is higher pitched to match her original role. Humorously, the actual NPC remains in the game, essentially making two of her exist and the character rescuing herself.

==Nomenclature==
Aryll's name may come from the word Amaryllis, which is a red flower, referencing the pattern on Aryll's dress. In the character design notes for Aryll inside the [[Zelda Box|''Zelda Box'']] book, [[Eiji Aonuma]] says that Aryll (アリル, ''Ariru'') was originally going to be named ''Mariru'' (マリル) to be similar to [[Marin]] (マリン) from {{LA}} and [[Malon]] (マロン, ''Maron'') from {{OoT}}.<ref>[http://zeldawiki.org/File:Aryll_Character_Design_Notes.png Aryll design notes]</ref> As such, certain similarities between Aryll and Marin can be noted, such as their affinity for Seagulls, and the red hibiscus in Marin's hair that appears on a pattern on Aryll's dress.
