==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Tall Tails}}" is an event wherein a greater number of tailed [[Spirit]]s randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits with long tails are trailing their way to your Spirit Board! Defeat them in battle to earn more SP than usual.<br/><br/>The Legend-class Omega Ridley and the Articuno, Zapdos, & Moltres spirits will appear at set times. Be sure to check them out! |author= Nintendo |published= May 1, 2019 |retrieved= May 3, 2019 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/48a8c1ee/index.html |title= Event: Tall Tails - Super Smash Bros. Ultimate |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary and Ace Spirits befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
