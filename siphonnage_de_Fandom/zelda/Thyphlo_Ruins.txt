The {{Term/Store|Thyphlo Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Thyphlo Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Located in the northern portion of the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins}} are surrounded by a murky lake and are shrouded in darkness, making navigation impossible without a light source. The {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins}} can be entered from any side, which will trigger a short cutscene and place {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} accordingly in the island. The entrance is located on the western side, connected to the Drenan Highlands by a bridge.

Upon entering, a voice speaks to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and informs him of an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} deep within the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Traveler from beyond these woods, you are now faced with a trial... Find the shrine hidden in these dark ruins...}}|Ketoh Wawai|BotW}}</ref> However, he must navigate the darkness within in order to reach it. This begins the "{{Term|BotW|Shrouded Shrine|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. Near the entrance is a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} that can be used if {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not carry one.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes his way through the darkness, he will find many {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight|link}} that can be lit. These {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight}} serve as hints to find the way to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} near a set of unlit {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight}}, he will spot an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} tethered to the necklace of a {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can defeat the {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}} in order to obtain the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}}. Alternatively, he can steal the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|display=Orb}} from the {{Term|BotW|Hinox}}'s necklace, avoiding the fight. Placing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} in the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} will reveal the {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai Shrine|link}} and complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you stole the object from the Hinox hidden in the depths of the ruins and placed it on the pedestal, a shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
