The {{Term/Store|Subrosian Dance Hall||Series, OoS}} is a location in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Subrosian Dance Hall|Map|OoS}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Dance Hall}} is a dancing hall run by the {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Dance Instructor|link}}. It is where all the dancing in [[Subrosia]] takes place. [[Link]] can win many {{Plural|OoS|Item|link}} by playing the {{Term|OoS|Mini-Game|link}} were he must stay with the tempo and not run into the other dancers. Every time he is successful, the tempo will speed up next time. If successful, Link can obtain the [[Boomerang]] and a [[Strange Flute]] as a prize.

==Trivia==
*In the {{OoS|-}} multi-path book, the {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Dance Instructor|link}} is the only female [[Subrosian]], as [[Rosa]] is not present.
*In a [[Linked Game|Linked]] {{OoA}} game, there are two Subrosians in [[Rolling Ridge]] in the past that claim that they will bring the dance back to Subrosia, suggesting that the [[Goron Dance]] inspired the Subrosian Dance.
