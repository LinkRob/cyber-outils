The {{Term/Store|Genie||Series, LA, LADX}} is the {{Term|LANS|Nightmare|link}} of {{Term|LANS|Bottle Grotto}}, the second dungeon in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 179}}</ref> It guards the {{Term|LANS|Conch Horn}}.

==Characteristics==
[[File:LADX Genie Sprite.png|left]]
The battle against the {{Term|LANS|Genie}} is relatively simple, but the {{Term|LANS|Genie}}'s fireballs can cause a problem if {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} doesn't pay attention. When the fight begins, {{Term|LANS|Link}} must wait for the {{Term|LANS|Genie}} to enter his bottle and then quickly stun the bottle using his {{Term|LANS|Sword}}. {{Term|LANS|Link}} cannot hurt the {{Term|LANS|Genie}} as long as he has his bottle to protect him, so {{Term|LANS|Link}} must first destroy the bottle. In order to do this, {{Term|LANS|Link}} must use the {{Term|LANS|Power Bracelet}} to pick up the bottle and hurl it against the back wall. Then he must quickly run to the bottom wall and get ready to dodge. Three throws (two in the French and German versions) will destroy the bottle. 
[[File:LADX Genie's Bottle Sprite.png|left]]
The rest of the fight is all dodging and swordplay. {{Term|LANS|Link}} must pay attention and do his best to avoid the fireballs (the {{Term|LANS|Roc's Feather|link}} comes in handy here) while attacking the {{Term|LANS|Genie}} with his {{Term|LANS|Sword}}.

When {{Term|LANS|Link}} defeats the {{Term|LANS|Genie}}, he receives a {{Term|LANS|Heart Container}} and the {{Term|LANS|Conch Horn}}.

==Trivia==
*In the DX version, the {{Term|LADX|Genie}}'s speech has been extended to give additional clues how to defeat him. Originally, he only said ''"NYAH NYAH! You can't hurt me as long as I have my bottle!"'', but in the DX version, once inside his bottle, he also says ''". . . .! I can't move! But I am still all right. Your little sword won't break this bottle!"''.
