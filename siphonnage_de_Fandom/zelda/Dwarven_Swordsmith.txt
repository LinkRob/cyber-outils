The {{Term/Store|Dwarven Swordsmith|Dwarven Swordsmiths|Series, ALttP|plural}} are twin characters in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The {{Plural|ALttP|Dwarven Swordsmith}} can be found to the southeast of {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village|link}}. One of the smiths remarks that he can only temper {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}'s sword when his partner returns.<ref>{{Cite|If my lost partner returns, we can temper your sword, but now, I can't do anything for you.|Dwarven Swordsmith|ALttP}}</ref>

[[File:LttP darkworldsmith.gif|left]]
In the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}, the missing {{Term|ALttP|Dwarven Swordsmith}} can be found just south of the {{Term|ALttP|Village of Outcasts|link}} near the {{Term|ALttP|Digging Game|link}}. He is stuck in a {{Term|Series|Frog|link}}-like form due to the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World}}'s magic, and he is surrounded by black stones which can only be lifted with the {{Term|ALttP|Titan's Mitt|link}}. It is required to rescue him in order to get the {{Term|ALttP|Super Bomb|link}}.

[[File:LttP lightworldsmith.gif|left]]
{{Term|ALttP|Link}} can then use his {{Term|ALttP|Magic Mirror|link}} to transport both him and the {{Term|ALttP|Dwarven Swordsmith}} back to the {{Term|ALttP|Light World}}, reuniting the two {{Term|ALttP|Dwarven Swordsmith|display=Dwarves}}. As a reward, they will offer to temper {{Term|ALttP|Link}}'s {{Term|ALttP|Master Sword|link}} into a {{Term|ALttP|Tempered Sword|link}}.
