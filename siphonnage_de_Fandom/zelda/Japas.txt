{{Term/Store|Japas||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244}}</ref>

==Biography==
Japas is the bass player of the [[Zora]] band, [[The Indigo-Go's]].<ref>{{Cite|This room belongs to Japas, the genius bassist of the Zora Band.|Zora|MM}}</ref> He and [[Mikau]], the guitarist, like to write and compose their own music, but [[Evan]], the bandleader and songwriter, always condemns them for doing so.<ref>{{Cite|Today, Japas and I had a jam session. He's not good with words, but he writes great riffs.|Mikau|MM}}</ref><ref name=songs>{{Cite|Were you guys secretly writing a song behind my back again? Isn't songwriting the job of the bandleader? What, you're not happy with my songs?|Evan|MM}}</ref> If [[Link]], as [[Zora Link]], reads [[Mikau's Diary]], he will learn a new riff written by them. He can then play it in a [[Jam Session]] with Japas, who will complete the song.<ref>{{Cite|If you feel up to it, let's have a little jam session.|Japas|MM}}</ref> If the [[Hero of Time]] plays the full song for Evan, he will receive a [[Piece of Heart]]. This is the same song that is played during the band's [[Band Rehearsal|rehearsal]] and their eventual performance at the [[Carnival of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Mikau... That song Evan wrote sounds just like the one we played at our jam session, doesn't it?|Japas|MM}}</ref>

He is last seen during the [[New Day|ending credits]] where he and the rest of the band members are performing at the [[Milk Bar]] for the Carnival of Time.
