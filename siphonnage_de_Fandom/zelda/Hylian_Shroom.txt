==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hylian Shroom}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom}} are common {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} found near {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat one to restore half a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be cooked into [[Toasty Hylian Shroom|Toasty Hylian Shrooms]], which heal three-quarters of a {{Term|BotW|Heart}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Toasty Hylian Shroom}}<br/>A Hylian shroom toasted to perfection. The taste and fragrance are amplified, restoring more health to its consumer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], they are also used to create Red [[Dye]].

In a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, Hylian Shrooms are among the mushrooms that can be used to make [[Cream of Mushroom Soup]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], [[Fragrant Mushroom Sauté]], [[Fruit and Mushroom Mix]], [[Glazed Mushrooms]], a [[Meat and Mushroom Skewer]], a [[Mushroom Omelet]], [[Mushroom Rice Balls]], [[Mushroom Risotto]], a [[Mushroom Skewer]], [[Copious Mushrooms Skewers]], [[Salt-Grilled Mushrooms]], and [[Steamed Mushrooms]].
