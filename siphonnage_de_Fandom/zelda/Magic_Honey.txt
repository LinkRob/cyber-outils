The '''Magic Honey''' is an {{Term|TCT|Item|link}} in {{TCT}}.

==Location and Uses==
Located in the {{Term|TCT|Beekeeper's Hut|link}}, it is one of the three items required to free {{Term|TCT|Link}} from the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}} as dictated by the {{Term|TCT|Fairy's Scroll|link}}.

{{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} can only obtain the {{Term|TCT|Magic Honey}} from {{Term|TCT|Conly|link}} the beekeeper if she offers him a flask full of {{Term|TCT|Fairy Flower Sap|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Zelda takes the flask out of her pouch, uncorks it and waves it gently under the beekeeper's nose. He grabs for it, but she snatches it away. 'Not so fast,' she says. 'Let's trade.' |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 70}}</ref> After {{Term|TCT|Conly}} receives the flower sap, he will allow her to enter his hut and retrieve as much {{Term|TCT|Magic Honey}} as she desires as well as take some of his {{Plural|TCT|Magic Bee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The little man eyes the flask hungrily. 'What do yer want?' he asks. 'Honey? Take whatever you need.' And he waves to a row of honey pots along one wall. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 56}}</ref>

Once {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda}} reaches the room where {{Term|TCT|Link}} is imprisoned, she must pour the {{Term|TCT|Magic Honey}} into the holes that appear on top of the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}} after the {{Term|TCT|Handprint}} has been placed.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Crack! A dozen small holes appear in the top of the crystal right under the beam of light. 'Look at that,' Zelda whispers. 'It looks like a honeycomb. The honey!' Quickly she pours the magic honey into the holes. The thick brown liquid begins to steam and bubble. |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 75}}</ref> This will cause the {{Term|TCT|Magic Honey}} to steam and bubble, which is where the {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|display=Princess}} must use the {{Term|TCT|Magnifying Glass}} and hold it above the {{Term|TCT|Magic Honey|display=Honey}}, thus breaking the {{Term|TCT|Crystal}} open.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= 'Heat!' Zelda cries. She takes the magnifying glass and holds it in the light above the honey. Under the heat of the magnified sunlight, the crystal begins to turn colors--first blue, then gold, then red. Bbrr-r-room! With a terrific crash, the crystal splits open! |book= TCT |publisher= Archway |page= 75}}</ref>
