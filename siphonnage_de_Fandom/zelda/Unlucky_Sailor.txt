The {{Term/Store|Unlucky Sailor||Series, OoS}} is a character in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Charm for the Unlucky Sailor|N/A|OoS}}</ref>

==Biography==
After the Piratians leave Subrosia, a few of them get left behind. In a [[Linked Game]], one of the Pirates, named the Unlucky Sailor,who seems to believe strongly in luck, believes that the ship would never have crashed (and thus he would have never been left behind) if he knew the [[Linked Game/Secrets#Pirate Secret|secret to being lucky]].<ref>{{Cite|If I'd known the secret to bein' lucky, the boat wouldn't 'ave sunk and I wouldn't 'ave been left behind.|Unlucky Sailor|OoS}}</ref> Link obtains said secret from a young girl found south of the [[Black Tower]] in [[Labrynna]] in the past,<ref>{{Cite|I forgot to tell my lucky secret to that guy who went to Holodrum. He's so unlucky. I just hope he doesn't meet with disaster and end up a bag of bones... Can you help me?|N/A|OoA}}</ref> and can then tell to the Unlucky Sailor. The Unlucky Sailor will then upgrade the maximum number of [[Bomb]]s Link can carry (up to 50 if he has obtained the previous upgrade, up to 30 if not), but only when he has exactly 777 [[Ore Chunk]]s in his [[inventory]].<ref>{{Cite|Thank'ee! Now I'm lucky! I'd like to give ya somethin' lucky in return... But yer luck seems to 'ave run out... What shall we do? Aye! I know! Get 777 Ore Chunks! Sounds lucky, don't it? Then I'll share my luck with ya!|Unlucky Sailor|OoS}}</ref> The Unlucky Sailor will also give Link the secret to carrying more Bombs in [[Labrynna]].
