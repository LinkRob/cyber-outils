{{Term/Store|Anti-Ice & Lightning Training||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 44 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Ice & Lightning Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Ice & Lightning Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given ten minutes to defeat three {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}}, which are all scattered around the map and spawn at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Tireless Frog|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
