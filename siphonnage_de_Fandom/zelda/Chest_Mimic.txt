{{Term/Store|Chest Mimic|Chest Mimics|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Chest Mimic|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Chest Mimic}} are enemies that pose as {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} in order to ambush {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. They tend to lie dormant and unmoving in order to keep their disguise, staying still until one of the heroes is standing directly next to them, which will prompt them to awaken and chase the heroes on every beat until slain.
