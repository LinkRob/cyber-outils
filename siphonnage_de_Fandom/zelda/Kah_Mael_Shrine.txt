The {{Term/Store|Kah Mael Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kah Mael Shrine}}<br/>Drop and Rise}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Drop and Rise''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Kah Mael Shrine}} is located on {{Term|BotW|Tingel Island|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is hidden in a hole that is covered with a large rock slab. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon|link}} to raise the slab up, or he can use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to knock the slab out of the way. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can then drop down in the hole to access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}. Once he enters, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kah Mael|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kah Mael. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kah Mael|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Kah Mael Shrine}}, he will find a large scale with stairs leading up to them. Initially, the left side of the scale is lowered, while the right side is raised. The {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} can be seen on the platform behind the scale. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a metallic {{Term|BotW|Barrel|link}} atop the left side of the scale. In addition to this, a metal box is present on a wooden platform to the right of the scale, which is suspended by ropes.

Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can move the {{Term|BotW|Barrel}} between the two sides of the scale. When it is dropped on the raised part of the scale, it will fall, while the opposite side will rise. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} drops the {{Term|BotW|Barrel}} on one side while standing on the other, he can use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} to move the metal box down to the scale. Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can shoot the ropes supporting the wooden platform, which will cause the box to fall to the ground.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} drops the box onto the other side of the scale while he is lowered, he will be launched into the air. From here, he will be able to use his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. {{Term|BotW|Kah Mael}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kah Mael|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kah Mael|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} launches himself into the air, he can choose to Paraglide down onto a platform on the righthand side of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. Here, he can open a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.
