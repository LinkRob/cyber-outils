The {{Term/Store|Tunic of the Hero||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Tunic of the Hero}}<br/>Legend has it this armor was once cherished by a hero from ancient times. Its design is rather simple, but it's such a traditional look.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Tunic of the Hero is a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the 30th Anniversary 8-Bit Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of the Hero. They can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Tunic of the Hero, alongside the [[Cap of the Hero]] and [[Trousers of the Hero]], comprise the [[Hero Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from the original ''[[Legend of Zelda]]''. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
