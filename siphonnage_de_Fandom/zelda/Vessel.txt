The {{Term/Store|Vessel|Vessels|Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After obtaining the [[Train Cars#Spirit Train|Trusty Freight Car]] from [[Kagoron]] in [[Goron Village]], [[Link]] can speak with the [[Wise One]] in [[Papuchia Village]] and tell her that he's been having bad luck. She responds by offering Link a vessel for 300 [[Rupee]]s. If he buys it, he must take it to the [[Snow Sanctuary]] while avoiding getting hit on the [[Spirit Train]] and talk to [[Steem]].  If the train runs into anything, the vessel will break, and Link must start over.

When Link finally arrives with the vessel, Steem graciously thanks him for giving him a new decoration for the drab sanctuary. Steem rewards the young hero for his efforts with a [[Force Gem]].<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for the vessel! I'm all aflurry with gratitude!|Steem|ST}}</ref> After [[Steem]] places the vessel in the sanctuary, Link can break it with his [[sword]]. If he does, Steem will be shocked and demand a new one.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what is this?! What do you plan to do about this?!|Steem|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You'd better bring me a replacement vessel to make up for the way you acted!|Steem|ST}}</ref> When Link speaks to [[Princess Zelda]] before boarding the train to leave, she suggests that they replace the vessel in order to "make it up to him."<ref>{{Cite|Steem was really angry at us... I hate to say it, [Link]... But it was your fault for breaking that vessel. We have to make it up to him.|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> Link can buy another vessel from the [[Wise One]] and return it to the sanctuary. Steem will reward Link for his thoughtfulness with a [[treasure]].<ref>{{Cite|It's true that you broke the vessel, but you did replace it. I should reward you for your thoughtfulness. Well, since you worked so hard, let me give you this.|Steem|ST}}</ref>
