{{Term/Store|Forsaken Fortress||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (A1) is the first dungeon [[Link]] visits in {{TWW}} and is [[Ganondorf]]'s initial stronghold.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref> It is an ominous island, and most people tend to avoid it.<ref>{{Cite|Even if I were to consider it... Lately, I've heard nothing but evil rumors about this Forsaken Fortress...|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> The stone fortress is well-guarded with cannons, searchlights, and hordes of [[Miniblin]]s, [[Bokoblin]]s, and [[Moblin]]s, who patrol the area and attack any would-be invaders.<ref>{{Cite|Listen up, Link... There's a monster running the searchlight up there! If you can slay the monster, then just maybe it'll shut the searchlight down.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> The [[Helmaroc King]] makes his home on the Forsaken Fortress as Ganondorf's servant.<ref>{{Cite|The Monstrous Helmaroc King. Habitat: Forsaken Fortress. Master: Ganondorf.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> It is also the place where Link's younger sister, [[Aryll]], is held prisoner along with [[Mila]] and [[Maggie (The Wind Waker)|Maggie]] until they are rescued.<ref>{{Cite|I may as well tell you that the bird that kidnapped both you and Link's sister has made its foul nest to the north, on the heights of the Forsaken Fortress.|Quill|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I...was once the richest little debutante in this town. Did you know that? But one day, a monstrous bird came and took me away to a terrible place called the Forsaken Fortress, where I was locked up and held captive. Oh, it was awful!|Mila|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ooh, hoo hoo! My sweet, sweet daughter! My only daughter! Poor Maggie... She was captured by a bunch of villains and taken to the Forsaken Fortress!|Maggie's Father|TWW}}</ref>

Before the [[Great Flood]], the Forsaken Fortress was the [[Gerudo's Fortress]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 59}}</ref> [[Tetra]] briefly mentions to Link that the fortress previously belonged to another clan of pirates.<ref>{{Cite|There are all sorts of strange rumors about this place. What I do know is that long ago, it used to be the hideout of a no-good group of pirates we used to compete with... But they were just small-time. Now, the place looks like it's pretty dangerous.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Fortress==
The dungeon is located in the far northwest quadrant of the [[Great Sea]], and it is where [[Ganondorf]] has constructed his temporary lair by using a portal from Hyrule under the sea.<ref>{{Cite|I see... So this is the portal through which Ganon was able to seep into the unsuspecting world above...|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> Link is taken there by [[Tetra]] and her [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|Pirate Crew]],<ref>{{Cite|I'll call you when we reach the Forsaken Fortress, so until then, just go talk to Niko and do whatever he tells you.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Even if I were to consider it... Lately, I've heard nothing but evil rumors about this Forsaken Fortress... Tell you what. If you come back with something like that, we'll let you stow away on our ship.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> but he has to visit the place alone with the [[King of Red Lions]] later on.

Whilst inhabited by Ganondorf and his minions, the Fortress is held in a perpetual night. If it is approached during the daytime, the day cycle will rapidly accelerate until nightfall. The [[Song of Passing]] will also have no effect until Link leaves the Island sector, similar to Ganondorf's rainfall curse during the quest to obtain the [[Nayru's Pearl]].

===First Visit===
[[File:Forsaken Fortress TWW.jpg|thumb|left|The inside of the Fortress|216x216px]]
During his first visit, Link is catapulted to the Fortress by [[Tetra]] and her [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|Crew]]. Upon landing, due to a miscalculated shot, he accidentally loses his [[Hero's Sword]] and is left with only his [[Hero's Shield]] to defend himself.<ref>{{Cite|Your sword landed all the way up there? Shoot! I'm sorry! I apologize! I guess my aim was off by a little bit.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref>

As he cannot directly attack enemies, Link must practice stealth while navigating the fortress, as there are [[Moblin]]s and [[Searchlight]]s ran by [[Bokoblin]]s. They patrol the Fortress and throw intruders in one of the jail cells if spotted. If he is caught, Link is able to escape the cell through a small hole in the wall. He can also navigate around without being seen by hiding under [[Barrel]]s (though he will be caught again if Moblins or the searchlights see him move).

In order to reach the room where [[Aryll]] is being held captive, Link must travel to each tower and use [[Boko Stick]]s to defeat the Bokoblins operating the Searchlights. Doing so will shut the Searchlights down.<ref>{{Cite|Those searchlights are a real pain, huh? You're going to have to deal with them somehow, I'm afraid... If you can slay the monster, then just maybe it'll shut the searchlight down.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> Once Link is outside the room where Aryll is imprisoned, he gets his Sword back and fights a Shield Bokoblin. Before Link is able to save his sister and two other girls, [[Mila]] and [[Maggie (The Wind Waker)|Maggie]], unfortunately, the [[Helmaroc King]] swoops in, grabs him with his beak, and brings him before [[Ganondorf]]. Ganondorf himself then tells the massive bird to throw Link at sea, who ends floating unconscious in the [[Great Sea]].

===Second Visit===
[[File:Miniblinattack.jpg|thumb|273x273px|right|Link beset by [[Miniblin]]s in the fortress]]
After Link finds the [[Master Sword]], he pays a second visit to the Fortress, where he confronts [[Phantom Ganon]] to gain the {{Term|TWWHD|Skull Hammer|link}}, then the Bokoblins who once more use the Searchlights as well as Moblins, and finally the dreaded Helmaroc King to finally rescue his sister and the girls from [[Windfall Island]], with the unexpected help of Tetra and the pirates.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Well, never mind about that. For now, we'll just take these girls. I'm sure their fathers back in town will pay a handsome reward for their return. If you're worried about your sister...don't be. I promise I'll deliver her safely back to your little island.|Tetra|TWW}}</ref> Although he succeeds in the rescue mission this time, he now fails to defeat Ganondorf in order to avenge Aryll because the Blade of the Evil's Bane lost its [[Power to Repel Evil|holy power]].<ref>{{Cite|You cannot defeat me with a blade that does not sparkle with the power to repel evil! What you hold is useless. Go back to the world below, and tell that to the pathetic fools who made this blade! Its power is gone, and its edges are dull!|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref> When Link removed it from its pedestal in [[Hyrule Castle]], he lifted the seal that prevented Ganondorf from taking control of [[Hyrule]] again;<ref>{{Cite|Foolish child. While that sword is indeed the blade of evil's bane, at the same time, it has long played another role... You see, it is also a sort of key... a most wretched little key that has kept the seal on me and my magic intact! By withdrawing the blade, you have broken that seal...|Ganondorf|TWW}}</ref> Nevertheless, after the help of Tetra, [[Quill]], [[Komali|Prince Komali]] and [[Valoo]], the Fortress is eventually abandoned and the enemies (Ganondorf included) now move to Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|Ganon has not shown himself above the seas since Valoo unleashed his fiery wrath upon him. And what's worse, there is not a creature stirring in his base of operations in this world...the Forsaken Fortress.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> After Link clears the [[Wind Temple]], it's revealed that the Fortress is indeed uninhabited (though few minor enemies still exist if entered).

As Link has his Sword during the second visit, he is able to defend himself and does not need to use stealth as in the first visit. Moblins will now simply attack him, instead of bringing him to the jail. Also, Searchlights that are trained on Link will cause [[Bomb]]s from cannons to be fired at him and will also attract [[Miniblin]]s. The Hero of Winds can still be imprisoned if he is caught by the [[Floormaster]] that appears in a certain area of the Fortress, although the switch that opens Link's cell can be activated from the inside using the [[Bow]] in the event that this happens.

===Third Visit===
If Link visits the Fortress after Ganondorf is attacked by Valoo, he will find that the curse of endless night has lifted and the Fortress is devoid of enemies, save for several [[Keese]] and [[Rat|Rats]]; the [[Fishman]] later claims that the Fortress has indeed been completely abandoned. Contrary to popular belief, the [[Heart Container]] dropped by the Helmaroc King will also appear near where Link obtained the Skull Hammer at this time, if he failed to collect it after slaying the boss.

Later on, while Link is exploring [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]] in Hyrule, he is given the opportunity to open a portal of darkness linking it to the Forsaken Fortress, allowing the young hero to return to the surface of the sea quickly and conveniently, should he so desire. It was actually through this portal that Ganondorf was able to escape the sealed kingdom originally and wreak havoc on the inhabitants of the [[Great Sea]].

== Boss Battle ==
[[File:TWW Helmaroc King Model.png|center|thumb|412x412px]]
The [[Helmaroc King]] is the dungeon's [[Boss]], a major servant of Ganondorf, and is the very monster that kidnapped Link's younger sister Aryll (and previously Tetra) early in the game, therefore the secondary antagonist. Essentially a giant [[Kargarok]], it is fought during the second visit after Link rescues Aryll. In the start of the battle, as the tower containing the prison cells is being flooded, the enemy follows and attacks Link as he climbs to the top, avoiding Bokoblins along the way. The massive bird then attempts to block him from passing, where Link must use the Skull Hammer onto its helmet, temporarily knocking it off the platform. In the actual battle, the Helmaroc King has three attack patterns. In the first, it lands on the ground in front of Link where it walks and then tries to impale him with its beak, but becomes jammed to the floor if it misses, leaving itself vulnerable to a Skull Hammer attack by Link, causing a crack to its helmet. The second is it flys around the tower attempting to run over Link with its claws (which can potentially and comically make it crash to a wall). A third one is it floats in the air and flaps its wings to blow the young hero into the large spikes surrounding the arena.

When the Helmaroc King's helmet is struck four times by the Skull Hammer, it shatters, revealing its face and will now become vulnerable to the Master Sword or other items. The strategy is still the same, though the boss itself becomes more frantic. After enough hits, the Helmaroc King unleashes one final screech in agony and then explodes into a purplish smoke, killing it, and leaves behind a Heart Container as a reward.

==Trivia==
[[File:Sheikah Eye TWW.png|right|40px]]
*A modified version of the [[Sheikah]] [[Eye Symbol]] makes a small appearance near the top of the Forsaken Fortress.
*The Forsaken Fortress in the Japanese version of the game is called '''Demon Beast Island'''. Despite its name, [[Ganon]] as his true form is not physically present in {{TWW}} (though [[Puppet Ganon]] in its first form resembles him).
*This is also one of the only two [[Dungeons in The Wind Waker|Dungeons]] without a [[Warp Jar]]. The other is [[Tower of the Gods]].
*This [[Dungeon]] is unique in that, like [[Skull Woods (A Link to the Past)|Skull Woods]] in {{ALttP}}, it has multiple entrances and exits, given the fact that only the interior is considered to be the dungeon itself (as seen in the [[Map#Dungeon Map|Dungeon Map]]), and the exterior territory belongs to the [[Great Sea]] overworld. For the same reason, this fortress is the only dungeon in {{TWW|-}} whose boss is fought and defeated outside.
*This is the only dungeon (besides [[Ganon's Castle|Ganon's Tower]]) where an exit portal does not appear after the boss is defeated.
*Also on the initial infiltration of the tower's interior under the "Mjtower" level, compared to the other one, [[Link]] spawns at the top of the tower on each visit, whereas the entrance doorway leads back to the tower's entrance where he obtains the [[Hero's Sword]] dropped at, and the seals used to prevent water from leaking in, as well as the [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]] being absent. Entering [[Ganondorf]]'s ship here also traps Link instead.
*Even when the ship is destroyed after infiltrating the fortress the second time, it wasn't affected on the first visit map because it was designed to allow Link to head over to [[Windfall Island]] after his attempt to save Aryll, due to how the layout was made. A dark portal that links to the Dungeon also doesn't appear on this map of Forsaken Fortress.
*The tune that plays as Link is thrown in jail also plays in {{FSA}} in the [[Infiltration of Hyrule Castle]], when the [[Links]] are thrown in jail after being coincidentally spotted by the [[Searchlight]]s.
*Despite [[Seagull]]s appearing near Aryll's cell room, Link is not able to control them using a [[Hyoi Pear]].
*If a cheating device is used to make Link prematurely have his [[Sword]] and other weapons ([[Bow]], [[Skull Hammer]], etc.) in the first visit, [[Moblin|Moblins]] do not arrest Link when they spot him and will simply attack. However, he can still be put into jail if he is seen by the Searchlights.
