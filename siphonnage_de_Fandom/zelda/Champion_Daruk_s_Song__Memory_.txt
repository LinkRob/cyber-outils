"{{Term/Store|Champion Daruk's Song||Series, BotW}}" is the second {{Term|BotW|EX Recovered Memory|link}} in {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Champion Daruk's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|EX Recovered Memory #2}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|Champion Daruk's Song (Memory)}}" is triggered upon completion of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, after listening to {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}'s recently composed {{Term|BotW|Champion Daruk's Song (Song)|link|display=song}} about the {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪Recall {{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}'s Champion of flame,<br/>before his fated moment finally came.♪<br/>♪This is the tale of Great {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}'s task,<br/>and the princess who came to ask.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} starts as a meeting between {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} as the pair walk along the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|link}} through {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. They are accompanied by two {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Daruk}} agrees to pilot {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}'s fight against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Count me in, tiny princess. I'm the fearless Daruk, after all! And if Hyrule needs my help, I'll gladly lay down my life.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Daruk!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> He remarks that there has been a significant increase in monsters attacking people,<ref>{{Cite|Not sure if it's Calamity Ganon's fault or what, but I hear monsters have been attacking people more than ever lately.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> and he suggests that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} avoid being outside {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} without a horde of {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} watching out for her.<ref>{{Cite|Times like these...you shouldn't leave the castle without a horde of mighty Gorons to watch your back.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} in turn says that {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} sounds like her {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link|display=father}}, adding that the {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|display=King}} is assigning a {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=knight}} to follow her around wherever she goes.<ref>{{Cite|You sound like Father. He's assigning a knight to watch over me wherever I go.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When she states that the top contender is the most accomplished swordsman in all of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}},<ref>{{Cite|I hear the top contender is the most accomplished swordsman in all of Hyrule.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} realizes that she is probably talking about {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Huh, that could only be... Hm? What the...|Daruk|BotW}}</ref>

Before {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} can relay his thoughts, however, he spots a group of {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} rushing angrily towards a spot just beside the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Road|display=Road}}.<ref>{{Cite|They really are all over the place these days.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> Once he realizes someone is in danger,<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Is someone being attacked?|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} tells {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} to stand back and then charges at the {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cowards! Wait here, Princess.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Zelda}}'s bodyguards step in front of her to keep her safe, {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} launches two {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} far away, then dispatches the rest of the group, all with just a few mighty swings of his {{Term|BotW|Boulder Breaker|link}}.

As the dust settles and the remaining {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} flee in terror, {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} laughs triumphantly as {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} runs up behind him.<ref>{{Cite|Hmph! Spineless little cuccos! No monster stands a chance when fighting me, the almighty Daruk!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> She then notices what the {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} were attacking: a {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Zelda}} beckons the animal,<ref>{{Cite|You're safe.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> then gives it some pats and hugs, she congratulates {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} for saving it.<ref>{{Cite|It seems our friend here was the one being attacked. Precious boy. You saved his life!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, she realizes that {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} is now quivering in fear at the sight of the dog. The {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever|display=Retriever}} then barks once at {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}, who yelps fearfully and covers himself with his {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Protection|link|display=barrier}}.

A moment later, the group sees the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever}} off,<ref>{{Cite|Good riddances...and stay safe. I'm sorry ya had to see that side o' me.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} sheepishly admits that he was chased by {{Plural|BotW|Dog|link}} a lot when he was younger and that the sight of them still makes him panic today.<ref>{{Cite|As a kid, dogs always chased me. I still panic when I see one o' those critters.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> An amused {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} remarks that "(She) never imagined the Great Daruk would have a weakness".<ref>{{Cite|I never imagined the Great Daruk would have a weakness.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} then sheepishly asks whether or not {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} is some kind of {{Term|BotW|Dog}} monster.<ref>{{Cite|So tell me...Princess...|Daruk|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Yes?|Princess Zelda|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Calamity Ganon isn't, uh... some kind of a dog monster, is he?|Daruk|BotW}}</ref>{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}, after staring back at {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} in astonished silence for a moment, breaks into a giggling fit, which {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} sheepishly joins in on as the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} ends.
