The {{Term/Store|Captain's Hat||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Captain's Hat}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is a splendid thing. It earns the respect and sworn allegiance of legions of soldiers.</p>}}

The Captain's Hat is obtained after [[Link]] awakens and defeats [[Captain Keeta]]. Link must awaken Captain Keeta from where he rests near the [[Ikana Graveyard]] using the "[[Sonata of Awakening]]." After he defeats Captain Keeta, he will submit to Link's power,<ref>{{Cite|Wait! It is my loss! Sheathe your weapon...|Captain Keeta|MM}}</ref> and tell him of the defeat he suffered while serving as the commander of the Ikana Army.<ref>{{Cite|I commanded the Ikana Army of Ikana Kingdom atop the hill. I am called Skull Keeta. Since being shamed by loss in a battle within my kingdom... I have waited here for one to come and awaken my soul.|Captain Keeta|MM}}</ref> After making his final request, he shows the hero a [[Treasure Chest]] containing the Captain's Hat and takes his leave.<ref>{{Cite|Young swordsman who has awakened and deftly defeated me... I shall rely on your power to fulfill my request. I ask you to take my soul, which rests in the fiercely burning flame... And convey my words to my men, who, even in death, remain loyal to me. Tell them the war has ended... Then I shall be able to drift quietly into sleep... Captain, sir! May I take leave, sir?|Captain Keeta|MM}}</ref> Link can then use the [[Hookshot]] or jump across the bridge guardrails to claim it.

The Captain's Hat is designed to resemble Captain Keeta, and as such, displays itself as a symbol of authority. When Link wears it, he is immediately mistaken for Captain Keeta. This causes [[Stalchild]]ren to not attack Link, and also allows him to communicate with them and take command.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Captain, sir! It's been a long time since we've seen you!|Stalchild|MM}}</ref> This can be used in the Ikana Graveyard to open the graves that lead [[Beneath the Graveyard]]. In here, Link can find the "[[Song of Storms]]," a [[Piece of Heart]], and a [[Bottle]] (or another Piece of Heart in {{MM3D}}). Link can also use the Captain's Hat in the [[Oceanside Spider House]] to obtain another Piece of Heart by solving the secret puzzle of the house.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Captain, sir! It's been a long time since we've seen you! As you have ordered, sir, we are investigating the secrets of this place.|Stalchild|MM}}</ref>

The Captain's Hat can also be used to make [[ReDead]]s dance, ignoring Link.

==Trivia==
*If Link wears the Captain's Hat during the battle with [[Igos du Ikana]], he will think that Link is Captain Keeta. The game will go into a short cutscene before returning to the battle.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM|publisher= Nintendo|page= 134}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! Keeta! Is it not Captain Keeta?!? ...............But you're so... ...T-Tiny!!! I was nearly fooled by what you have done...|Igos du Ikana|MM}}</ref>
*The Captain's Hat is one of six masks that are necessary to complete the game, the others being the three transformation masks, the [[Garo's Mask]], and the [[Gibdo Mask]].
