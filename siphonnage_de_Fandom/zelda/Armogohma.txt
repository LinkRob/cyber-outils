Armogohma can shoot fiery lasers from her eye. She can also lay dozens of eggs, which hatch into [[Baby Gohma]]s. In order to stun Armogohma, [[Link]] must shoot her eye with the [[Bow]]. Afterwards, Link must bring one of the massive statues in the room to life with the [[Dominion Rod]]; doing so allows him to make the statue swing its fist onto Armogohma's carapace. After three hits from the statues, Armogohma's body will explode. Afterwards, her "eyeball" reveals itself to be a smaller spider on its own, which tries to escape along with some of her spawn. Armogohma can be defeated with a single [[Sword]] strike, three [[Arrow]] shots, a single [[Bomb Arrow]], or a pair of attacks from the [[Spinner]]. She can additionally be defeated by using one of the statues controlled by the Dominion Rod, flattening it in the process.

Following Armogohma's vanquishment, Link obtains a [[Mirror Shard]] and a [[Heart Container]].

==Trivia==
*In the [[Zelda HD Experience|''Zelda'' HD Experience]], Armogohma appears fighting against Link in an environment reminiscent to the Temple of Time.

==Nomenclature==
Armogohma's name is a portmanteau of the word "armor" and [[Gohma]].
