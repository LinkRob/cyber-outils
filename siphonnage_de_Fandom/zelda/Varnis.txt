{{Term/Store|Varnis||Series, ALBW}} is the name of one of the 20 possible {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} that can appear in {{ALBW}} without being met by [[StreetPass]].<ref>{{Cite|It's Varnis!<br/>This {{Color|ALBW Blue|Shadow Link}} has come via {{Color|ALBW Red|StreetPass}} and awaits battle!|Shadow Link|ALBW}}</ref>{{Note|All Shadow Links claim to have come via StreetPass, even when they are pre-generated.}}

==Overview==
Once {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} has spoken to {{Term|ALBW|Gramps|link}} and enabled {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link Battle|link}}, a new signpost signifying the arrival of a {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link|link}} will appear at the {{Term|ALBW|StreetPass Meadow|link}} after every five hours of gameplay, regardless of whether or not the player has received a [[StreetPass]] tag. {{Term|ALBW|Varnis}}, having the lowest overall statistics of all the pre-generated {{Plural|ALBW|Shadow Link}}, is often the first {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} to be met by this method.

{{Term|ALBW|Varnis}} wears a {{Term|ALBW|Green Tunic|link}} and wields a {{Term|ALBW|Forgotten Sword|link}} and no {{Term|ALBW|Shield|link}}. He is tied with {{Term|ALBW|Grisa|link}} for having the smallest {{Term|ALBW|Life Gauge|link}} (three {{Plural|ALBW|Heart}}), he has the fewest Medals won (zero), and is the only pre-generated {{Term|ALBW|Shadow Link}} to carry only one {{Term|ALBW|Item}}: a {{Term|ALBW|Rented Bow|link}}. He has the lowest possible bounty at 50 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.
