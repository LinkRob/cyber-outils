{{Term/Store|Spectacle Island||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (B3) is one of the islands of the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features==
{{Fishman|Beware the night when the crescent moon faces up! That's all I can tell you, small fry.}}
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>Spectacle Island</p><p>East of there is [[Windfall Island]]. I tried to enter my best drawing in the [[Windfall Auction|auction]] there. But I got rejected at the door! Why is there a dress code at an auction?</p>
}}

The island is where the [[Barrel Shoot]] mini-game is located. [[Link]] can play it by walking up to the cannon on top of the tower, where he will find [[Salvatore]]. Playing this game can result in obtaining a [[Heart Container|Piece of Heart]] for the first successful run, [[Treasure Chart]] #17 for the second win, and 100 [[Rupee]]s for every success after that. This mini-game, however, can only be played during the day since Salvatore will be asleep during the night.

This island is also one of the recurring destinations of the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}}.

==Trivia==
*Spectacle Island is a reference to [[Spectacle Rock]] from the older games in {{TLoZ|Series}}.
