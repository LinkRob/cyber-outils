The {{Term/Store|Beaver Race|Beaver Races|Series, MM3D}} is a {{Term|MM3D|Mini-Game|link}} in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|You won the Beaver Race! This was added to your {{Color|MM3D Red|notebook}}!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> It remained unnamed until {{MM3D}}.

==Location==
The {{Term|MM3D|Beaver Race}} is located in the {{Term|MM3D|Waterfall Rapids|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Zora Cape|link}}. Once there, {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}} must talk to the {{Term|MM3D|Little Brother Beaver|link}} swimming around in the water. He then challenges {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} to compete against him, which leads to the first of two {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Race|display=Races}}.

==Rules==
The first {{Term|MM3D|Beaver Race}} against the {{Term|MM3D|Little Brother Beaver|link}} consists of swimming through 20 underwater rings in under two minutes. Upon succeeding, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must then race against the {{Term|MM3D|Big Brother Beaver|link}}, but this time with 25 rings. Successfully completing both {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Race|display=Races}} rewards {{Term|MM3D|Link}} with a {{Term|MM3D|Bottle|link}}. Racing again against the {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver}} during the same three-day-cycle yields a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}} from them. The rules for the first {{Term|MM3D|Beaver Race|display=Race}} are the same, except the time limit is shortened to one minute and 50 seconds. Once {{Term|MM3D|Link}} wins against the {{Term|MM3D|Little Brother Beaver}}, he will have to race against the {{Term|MM3D|Big Brother Beaver}} with the same time limit, but with 25 rings instead. The {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart}} is rewarded upon the completion of both {{Plural|MM3D|Beaver Race|display=Races}}.

The layout of the {{Term|MM3D|Beaver Race|display=Race}} contains two intersections at which the path spliths into two before merging again after a certain distance. Depending on the day, only one of the two paths is accessible for the racers at each of these intersections, the other being blocked off: during the {{Term|MM|First Day}}, the racers will have to take the right-hand path at the first intersection and the left-hand path at the second intersection; during the {{Term|MM|Second Day}}, they will have to take the left-hand path and the right-hand path, respectively; and during the {{Term|MM|Final Day}}, they will have to take the left-hand path at both intersections.
