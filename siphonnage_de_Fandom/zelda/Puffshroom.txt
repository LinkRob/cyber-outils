'''Puffshrooms''' are [[Material|Materials]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom]]''.

== Locations and Uses ==

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
Puffshrooms are [[Material|Materials]] that explode in a puff of smoke when struck - [[Enemy|Enemies]] will be temporary blinded, so [[Link]] can [[Stealth|stealthy]] attack them with a [[Sneakstrike]].
