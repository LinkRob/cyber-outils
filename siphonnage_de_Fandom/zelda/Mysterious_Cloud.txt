{{Term/Store|Mysterious Cloud|Mysterious Clouds|Series, TMC|plural}} are objects in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mysterious Cloud|Kinstone Fusion|TMC}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Mysterious Cloud}} were referred to as '''Cloud Pedestals''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 68}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game in {{TMC}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Cloud}} are only found in the lower {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}. There are five of them, each holding a {{Term|TMC|Gold Kinstone|link|display=Gold Kinstone Piece}}. {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with them. Each {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} activates one of five {{Plural|TMC|Pinwheel|link}}; when all five are active, a large {{Term|TMC|Tornado|link}} will appear to take {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}}
