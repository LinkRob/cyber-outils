{{Name|Japanese|Monpe}} is a field worker in {{RTBToL}}.

==Biography==
She can be found on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Page 7|link}}, and {{Term|RTBToL|Page 8|link}}, working in a flower field at the entrance of Vegetable Village. Similar to most of the girls in the story, {{Term|RTBToL|Monpe}} will avoid speaking with {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} until he gives her enough gifts in a {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push|link}}. {{Term|RTBToL|Monpe}} has several girls who are working for her, and on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 8}}, one of them is a disguised {{Term|RTBToL|Yamori|link}}.
