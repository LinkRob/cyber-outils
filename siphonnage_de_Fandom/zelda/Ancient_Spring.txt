==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spring}} are recoverable items from defeated {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. They are also sold by the travelling merchant [[Teli]] at the price of sixty {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. They and all other {{Plural|Series|Ancient Material|link}} were made by the ancient {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}} to use in their {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spring}} float when placed in water and never lose tension, no matter how many times they are compressed.<ref name="Inventory"/>

The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} requires {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spring}} in order to make certain pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}. They also count as {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, so they can be used in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. They are also accepted at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Gray Dye|link}}, or they can be sold to the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} in exchange for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spring}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
