The {{Term/Store|Cheval Rope||Series, OoA}} is a quest item in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Cheval Rope}} was referred to as '''Cheval's Rope''' in {{Guide|OoA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 3}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in , it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} Along with the [[Flippers]], this item was made by [[Cheval]], in the hopes of somehow helping the people of the world.<ref>{{Cite|Before I die, I want to finish my work on flippers for swimming and rope that won't decay in water. I hope to aid all the people of the world by inventing such things!|Cheval|OoA}}</ref> The rope, along with the Flippers, can be retrieved from [[Cheval's Tomb]] in the present time. The Cheval Rope is water-resistant,<ref>{{Cite|You got Cheval Rope! This sturdy rope won't rot when wet.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> and is needed by [[Rafton]] so he can build [[Link]] a [[Raft]],<ref>{{Cite|If I only had rope that won't decay in water, I could make my raft.|Rafton|OoA}}</ref> which is used to travel to [[Crescent Island]].
