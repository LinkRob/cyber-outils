The {{Term/Store|Failed Execution of Ganondorf||Series}} is an event in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 11}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|Series|Failed Execution of Ganondorf}} took place several years following the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]] ending of {{OoT}} and the events of {{MM}}, in which the King of Evil, [[Ganondorf]], was captured and put on trial for his crimes against the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] before being sentenced to [[wikipedia:Capital_punishment|capital punishment]]. The results of this execution would make way for [[Shadow Invasion|Zant's Invasion of Hyrule]]. The story of the execution is told to [[Link]] and [[Midna]] by the [[Sage]]s in {{TP}}.

==A Legendary Hero's Warning==
[[File:OoT Ending.png|thumb|265x265px|Link warns Princess Zelda about Ganondorf's true plans, as seen at the ending of {{OoT|-}}]]
After being completely sent back to his childhood at the conclusion of {{OoT|-}}, the young [[Hero of Time]], with the memory of his previous adventure still intact and remaining the bearer of the [[Triforce of Courage]], returned to [[Hyrule Castle]] to warn [[Princess Zelda]] about Ganondorf's conspiracy. This prevented the events of the game from ever unfolding again, for he knew the [[Gerudo]] King was about to launch an assault against [[Hyrule]] in order to find the [[Sacred Realm]] and steal the [[Triforce]] if not stopped at once.<ref name="Aonuma" /><ref>{{Cite|He was the leader of a band of thieves who invaded Hyrule in the hopes of establishing dominion over the Sacred Realm.|Sage|TP}}</ref> Because the castle have been alerted by Link about Ganondorf's betrayal to the [[King of Hyrule]]'s alliance, the Royal Family were able to thwart the villain's plot before it came to fruition. After the failure of his attempted invasion due to his plans being prematurely revealed, Ganondorf was ultimately arrested and taken prisoner.<ref>{{Cite|But he was blind...In all of his fury and might, he was blind to any danger, and thus was he exposed, subdued, and brought to justice.|Sage|TP}}</ref> Because it was believed Ganondorf would only continue to do more evil should he be let free, he was then sentenced to death at the [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]].<ref name=Aonuma>{{Cite Person|quote=In the last scene of Ocarina of Time, kids Link and Zelda have a little talk, and as a consequence of that talk, their relationship with Ganon takes a whole new direction. In the middle of this game [Twilight Princess], there's a scene showing Ganon's execution. It was decided that Ganon be executed because he'd do something outrageous if they left him be. That scene takes place several years after Ocarina of Time.|name=Eiji Aonuma|url=http://www.thehylia.com/index.php?subaction=showfull&id=1173582355&archive=|title=Nintendo Dream: Eiji Aonuma Interview}}</ref>

==Execution of the King of Evil==
[[File:Ganondorf's Execution.jpg|thumb|left|Ganondorf is impaled with the Sword of the Sages ([[Wii]] version)|245x245px]]

Several years following the events of the Hero of Time's adventures in Hyrule and [[Termina]] and leading up to {{TP|-}}, Ganondorf's death sentence was to be carried out. The ancient {{Plural|TPHD|Sage|link}} chained him in the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Chamber|link}} and produced a [[Sword of the Sages|sword of pure light]], brandished by the Sage of Water, which was then used to impale the King of Evil directly into his chest, hitting vital organs and creating a glowing wound.

[[File:Ganon's execution.png|thumb|right|The Triforce of Power on Ganondorf's hand, making him survive his execution (Wii version)|249x249px]]
At this moment, Ganondorf was on the verge of death. However, somehow, the symbol of the [[Triforce of Power]] suddenly appeared on the back of his right hand, preventing him from dying.<ref name="divine prank">{{Cite|Yet...By some divine prank, he, too, had been blessed with the chosen power of the gods.|Sage|TP}}</ref> With his strength restored by the essence of the [[Golden Goddess|Goddess]] [[Din (Goddess)|Din]], Ganondorf, full of rage, managed to break free of his bonds. The Sages were shocked to see the Triforce symbol resonating on his hand, not understanding how he could have attained the power of the gods.<ref name="divine prank"/> Ganondorf promptly rushed forward, obliterating the Sage of Water into thin air with his fist. He then removed the Sword of the Sages from his chest and began to wield it as his own weapon.

Shocked by their capital punishment for Ganondorf being botched, the Sages activated the [[Mirror of Twilight]] as a last resort, which proceeded to pull the Gerudo King into the [[Twilight Realm]]. Though this would not kill the archvillain, it imprisons and prevents him from invading Hyrule again for a time, therefore sealing Ganondorf away. His spirit would then roam the Twilight Realm, searching for a way to return to the world of light and conquer it. He revitalized himself using the lingering anguish and hatred from among the Twili that managed to bleed through the Twilight Realm's dimensional fabric.<ref>{{Cite|Your people have long amused me, Midna. To defy the gods with such petty magic, only to be cast aside... How very pathetic. Pathetic as they were, though, they served me well. Their anguish was my nourishment. Their hatred bled across the void and awakened me. I drew deep of it and grew strong again.|Ganondorf|TP}}</ref>

With Ganondorf surviving his execution, the Hero of Time's efforts of having him eliminated for good was ultimately in vain.

==Trivia==
*The music that plays when [[Ganondorf]] breaks free from his bounds, is a remix of [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|{{OoT|-}}]]'s escape from [[Ganon's Castle]].
*Despite the events of the execution taking place only a few years after the events of {{OoT|-}} and {{MM}}, Ganondorf appears significantly older than in the [[Nintendo 64|N64]] games, similar to {{TWW}}, due to the story being told in {{TP}}.
**The [[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess (Himekawa)|Himekawa manga adaptation]] elaborates that his {{TP|-}}-era appearance was the result of him having to reconstitute his body after it had been obliterated during the sealing process.
*Ganondorf being sealed to the [[Twilight Realm]] by the [[Sage|Sages]] after surviving an otherwise fatal blow and then returns back to [[Hyrule]] is coincidentally similar to the ending of {{OoT|-}}, where he was defeated by [[Link]] the [[Hero of Time]] as his monster form [[Ganon]] before being sealed to the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]] and then break his imprisonment in the events of {{TWW|-}} in the Adult Timeline.
[[File:TP manga Ending Blow.png|thumb|208x208px|The Ending Blow as seen in the {{TP|-}} manga, where the Master Sword stabs Ganondorf's chest just like the Sages did]]
*An ironic twist of fate from Ganondorf's attempted execution with the [[Sword of the Six Sages|Sword of the Sages]] is that Link, the Hero of Twilight, during the final battle, would actually kill the King of Evil via an [[Ending Blow]] onto the very same area of his chest with the [[Master Sword]] by the gaping wound.
*A likely reason why the Six Sages from {{OoT|-}} never performed Ganondorf's execution is because the ones who are the Hero of Time's childhood friends were never awakened as such in the Child Timeline (nor [[Princess Zelda]] herself), and the Ancient Sages long before them were still active and continued to the events of {{TP|-}}. It's unknown why [[Rauru]], who himself is indeed an ancient sage in the former game's story, was not present in the latter.
