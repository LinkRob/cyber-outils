The {{Term/Store|Bombers' Notebook||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 118}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
The {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}} is given to {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} by Jim, the leader of the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice]], after he completes their [[Hide-and-Seek]] test.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Bombers' Notebook. It contains the words we live by! Read it over!|Jim|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Bombers' Notebook! It allows you to keep track of people's schedules.|N/A|MM}}</ref> The {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}}, which can hold twenty entries, keeps track of the schedules of characters in need. Every time {{Term|MM3D|Link}} makes a promise, a Promise Sticker is added to the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}}.<ref>{{Cite|Promise to help them. Mark promises with Promise Stickers. Never be late with fulfilling your promises.|Jim|MM}}</ref> If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} solves a character's problem, he will receive a reward and a Happy Sticker will be added to the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whenever you solve someone's problem, it makes you happy, so a Happy Sticker will be added to your book.|Jim|MM}}</ref> Once an entry is fully completed, a Happiness Seal is placed next to it.<ref name="seal">{{Cite|When you're thanked for easing someone's troubles, take note! By helping someone out, you'll receive a happiness seal. Try to please everyone!|Sign|MM}}</ref> A picture of the character appears on the left column of the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}}, while its schedule appears in the adjacent row.<ref>{{Cite|People are shown in the left column and their schedule appears in the adjacent row.|Sign|MM}}</ref> Blue bars show when a notable event will occur.<ref>{{Cite|Blue bars show people's schedules and when notable events occur. Something will happen at the time shown!|Sign|MM}}</ref>

In {{MM3D}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}} is instead given to {{Term|MM3D|Link}} by the [[Happy Mask Salesman]], who says the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}} was most likely dropped by the [[Skull Kid]].<ref>{{Cite|It appears to be one of those fancy notebooks the local brats carry around. Perhaps the imp dropped it?|Happy Mask Salesman|MM3D}}</ref> It includes entries for every character that gives {{Term|MM3D|Link}} an item and for ongoing and rumored events. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can also set an alarm to a specific time so [[Tatl]] reminds him of any given event.

==Stickers==
===Promise Stickers===
[[File:Promise Sticker 01.png|right]]
Promise Stickers are shaped like exclamation marks. They are added to the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}} each time {{Term|MM3D|Link}} makes a promise or appointment, or each time he completes a significant event.<ref>{{Cite|An exclamation mark is added to your notebook when you make a promise or appointment! Be sure to confirm what you need to do!!|Sign|MM}}</ref> Details regarding the promise or event are displayed when the sticker is highlighted. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} travels back in time after receiving a sticker, it will be grayed out until {{Term|MM3D|Link}} makes the promise or completes the task again.

===Happy Stickers===
[[File:Thanks Sticker.png|right]]
[[File:Mask Sticker.png|right]]
When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} receives an item such as a {{Term|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}} as a prize or as thanks, a ribbon-shaped sticker is added to the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers' Notebook}}. When he receives a {{Term|MM3D|Mask|link}}, a {{Term|MM3D|Mask}}-shaped sticker is added instead. Details regarding the item and the means by which it was earned are displayed when the sticker is highlighted.

===Happiness Seal===
[[File:Happiness Seal.png|right]]
When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} completes all available objectives for a certain character, a Happiness Seal is added to that entry.<ref name="seal"/> This includes obtaining all previous Promise and Happy Stickers for that character as well.

==Trivia==
*In the N64 version, the jingle that plays when {{Term|MM3D|Link}} obtains the {{Term|MM|Bombers' Notebook}} is the regular "item obtained" tune. In {{MM3D}}, it is the same tune that plays when {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} obtains a {{Term|MM3D|Mask|link}}.
*In the N64 version, you can delay getting the Bombers' Notebook until much later in the game. When you receive it, it will have no entries filled out, even if you have talked to the NPCs or completed their corresponding side quest. If you had already completed an NPC's side quest before receiving the Bombers' Notebook, you will receive the corresponding Happiness Seal when you talk to the NPC again.
