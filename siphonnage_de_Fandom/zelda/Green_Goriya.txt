{{Term/Store|Green Goriya|Green Goriyas|Series, ALttP, ALBW|plural}} are enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 183 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Green Goriya}} first appear in the {{Term|ALttP|Palace of Darkness|link}} and reappear in later {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} {{Plural|ALttP|Dungeon|link}} such as {{Term|ALttP|Ganon's Tower|link}}. They copy {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}'s movement pattern, similar to {{Plural|Series|Arm-Mimic|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Mask-Mimic|link}}, moving in the same direction as {{Term|ALttP|Link}}. {{Plural|ALttP|Green Goriya}} can be cornered and destroyed with the {{Term|ALttP|Master Sword|link}} and are especially weak to the {{Term|ALttP|Bow|link}}.

In {{ALttP|-}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Green Goriya}} make a single appearance in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World|link}} in a cave in {{Term|ALttP|Death Mountain|link}}. In {{ALttP&FS}}, they were replaced with {{Plural|ALttP|Eyegore|link}} in the {{Term|ALttP|Light World}}, thus they only appear in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World}} in that version.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Green Goriya}} appear mainly in {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon|link}}, as well as the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower|link}}. In the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower}}, four {{Plural|ALBW|Green Goriya}} appear on Floor 16, along with three {{Plural|ALBW|Red Goriya|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must fight the enemies on an Icy Floor in order to proceed to the next floor.
