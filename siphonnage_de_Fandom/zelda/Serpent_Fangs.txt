{{Term/Store|Serpent Fangs|Serpent Fangs|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 137"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Serpent Fangs}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Frozen Plateau|link}}''', ''"Don't get hit by snowballs!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Serpent Fangs}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Frozen Plateau}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Serpent Fangs}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Serpent Fangs}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Snowball Ravine}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Serpent Fangs}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Silver Shrine|link}}''', ''"Win without using a sword!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Serpent Fangs}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Serpent Fangs}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Serpent Fangs}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka|link}}.<ref name="E 137"/>
