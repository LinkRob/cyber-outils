{{Term/Store|Linkle's Compass|Linkle's Compasses|Series, HWL|plural}} are items in {{HW|L}}.{{Name Ref|HWL}}

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub}}
Linkle's Compass is an item passed down for generations, gifted to [[Linkle]] by her grandmother believing that she will grow up to be a hero.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= She has aspired to be a hero since she was little, and believes that the compass she received from her grandmother, which has been passed down for generations, is the symbol that proves it.|name= Yosuke Hayashi|url= http://www.ign.com/articles/2015/12/08/hyrule-warrior-the-story-of-how-linkle-became-a-star|title= HYRULE WARRIOR: THE STORY OF HOW LINKLE BECAME A STAR}}</ref> It bears some mysterious power as it glows a bright light in response to Linkle's actions.

Linkle's Compasses are also Gold [[Material]]s which are dropped by Linkle in {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}.
