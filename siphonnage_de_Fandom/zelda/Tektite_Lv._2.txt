'''Tektite Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Tektite Lv. 2|While it has a thick shell and sharp leg spikes, the single, glaring, red eye is the dominant feature of this Tektite variant.<ref name="Tektite Lv. 2">{{Cite|While it has a thick shell and sharp leg spikes, the single, glaring, red eye is the dominant feature of this Tektite variant.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Tektite Lv. 2 is a tougher variant of the standard [[Tektite]], with higher health and damage. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Tektite Lv. 2 [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
