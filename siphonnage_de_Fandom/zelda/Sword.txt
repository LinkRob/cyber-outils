== Location and uses ==
[[File:OoT Link Spin Attack Artwork.jpg|left|thumb|283x283px|''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' artwork of Link using his [[Kokiri Sword]] to slash and defeat two [[Deku Baba|Deku Babas]] with the [[Spin Attack]] technique ]]
Swords are [[Link]]'s primary offensive weapon used to fight and slay [[Enemy|enemies]], defeat antagonists, parry attacks and sometimes solve [[Puzzle|puzzles]]. Like its defense counterpart, the [[Shield]], there are a wide variety of swords, some of which are stronger than others. The Sword is a common and importantly recurring item in every game of the ''Zelda'' franchise. The blade completes Link's hero-like appearance, along with any Shield he happens to wield at the time. Most Swords Link has at the beginning of the games are weak and resemble daggers in some cases ([[Kokiri Sword]], [[Hero's Sword]], etc.), but later in the quest are replaced with more advanced or longer blades, or become [[Upgrading|upgraded]] to inflict greater damage. In gameplay, it's almost always equipped to the [[Controller Buttons|A or B button]]. While the majority of swords in the series appear to be typical [[wikipedia:Middle_Ages|medieval]]-based blades, certain ones are infused with [[magic]] and play an important role, such as the [[Master Sword]] or [[Magical Sword]].
[[File:TPHD Master Sword Model.png|thumb|181x181px|The Master Sword, the trademark sword of the franchise]]
The best-known Sword in the series is the legendary Master Sword, which is Link's signature weapon since ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'' and is often required to defeat [[Ganon]].

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
First appearing in {{TLoZ}}, the Sword can be obtained in a [[Cave]] accessed from the starting screen. Inside, [[Link]] finds an [[Old Man]] who tells him to take the Sword because it is dangerous to travel alone.<ref name="TLoZ Old Man">{{Cite|IT'S DANGEROUS TO GO ALONE! TAKE THIS.|Old Man|TLoZ}}</ref> The Sword has two additional color variants which appear when Link wears either the [[Blue Ring]] or the [[Red Ring]]. The Sword can damage almost every enemy, but it is one of the weakest items in the game. However, the Sword can be replaced by the [[White Sword]] and then the [[Magical Sword]], which each deals twice the damage of its predecessor. A Sword that is designed like a {{Wp|rapier}} is depicted on the title display of the game. It is not confirmed to be any of the Swords that can be obtained in gameplay.

The Fighter's Sword is given to [[Link]] by his [[Link's Uncle|Uncle]] along with the [[Fighter's Shield]] as he enters [[Hyrule Castle]]. Link uses the Fighter's Sword until he obtains the [[Master Sword]].

During the credits, Link's Uncle is shown using the Fighter's Sword again.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Sword|This trusty blade is your main weapon. You can use it to attack enemies or cut the grass. Rumor has it that there is a more powerful Sword hidden on Koholint Island.}}
The Sword in {{LA}} is the first Sword that Link can obtain. It is from Link's [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|previous adventures]] and has his name engraved on it.<ref>{{Cite|You found your Sword! It must be yours because it has your name engraved on it!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> Link finds it on [[Toronbo Shores]] shortly after waking up after his crash on [[Koholint Island]]. By bringing 20 [[Secret Seashell]]s to the [[Seashell Mansion]], Link can replace it with the [[Level 2 Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|I am the spirit of the mansion. I have been waiting for someone to overcome the darkness. Find all the secret shells and go through the gate to receive the ultimate sword!|Spirit of the Mansion|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

By holding his Sword out, Link can move while he keeps facing the same direction.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= LA|page= 18}}</ref> If he then releases his Sword, Link will perform a [[Whirling Blade]] attack,<ref>{{Cite|'The Whirling Blade technique has been handed down from generation to generation by the family of the hero. To use it, hold down the Sword Button and build up your power. When you have enough, you can release the Button! Can you master this?'|Secrets Of The Whirling Blade|LADX}}</ref> which deals more damage than a regular Sword blow.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= LA|page= 19}}</ref>

Link can also hit walls with his Sword to determine whether or not they can be destroyed using [[Bomb]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Well that was a surprise! Hey! I'll tell you a secret! Use your sword to poke at dungeon walls... If you hear a hollow clank, you can break the wall with a Bomb!|Talking Timber|LADX}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
The Sword is used by the [[Links]] in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Exp|Sword|剣}} |author= Nintendo |published= n.d. |retrieved=August 23, 2017|url= https://www.nintendo.co.jp/3ds/ea3j/world/index.html |title= ゼルダの伝説　トライフォース３銃士 : 冒険ははるか長い道のり {{!}} ニンテンドー3DS {{!}} 任天堂 |site= Official Japanese Site |type= Image }}</ref> It changes appearance when certain [[outfit]]s are worn, such as the [[Sword Suit]], the [[Sword Master Suit]], the [[Dapper Spinner]], the [[Hero's Tunic]], [[Ninja Gi]], the [[Spin Attack Attire]] and the [[Tri Suit]]. While wearing the [[Fierce Deity Armor]], the Sword becomes the [[Fierce Deity Sword]]. In addition, the Sword becomes a [[Telescope]] while wearing [[Linebeck's Uniform]], and a parasol while wearing the [[Lady's Ensemble]].

The Sword in {{BotW}} can be obtained randomly from a [[Treasure Chest]] dropped by scanning {{TLoZ|-}} [[Link]] [[amiibo]] with the [[Amiibo (Rune)|amiibo]] [[Rune]]. It is described as a Sword that was once wielded by a hero in an ancient age.<ref name="BotW Inventory"/> Its appearance resembles its design throughout the artwork of {{TLoZ|-}}.

Being made of metal, the Sword will conduct [[Electricity]] attract [[Lightning]] during [[Thunderstorm]]s. It will sink if it is dropped in [[Water (Terrain)|Water]], but it can be picked up again and manipulated using the [[Magnesis]] Rune. It is also unable to burn when exposed to [[Fire]] or a [[Scorching Climate]].

In "[[Stinging a Stinger]]" of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}, [[Sleezenose]] offers [[Link]] a Sword as a sign of gratitude for having saved him. ==Trivia==
*Although [[Link]] is by far the most popular recurring character in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] to use a sword as their main weapon, due to the [[Medieval elements in The Legend of Zelda series|medieval-like setting]] of the franchise, there are others. Aside from some enemies such as [[Stalfos]] or [[Darknut|Darknuts]], [[Ganon]] and even [[Princess Zelda]] are also known to have swords in certain games and other media (though the latter has never yet been seen using one in combat).[[File:TLoZ Sword-Giving Old Man.png|thumb|217x217px|The iconic Sword-giving Old Man quote from ''The Legend of Zelda'']]
*In {{TLoZ}}, when the [[Old Man]] gives Link the Sword at the very beginning of the quest, he tells the young hero the line, "''IT'S DANGEROUS TO GO ALONE! TAKE THIS.''"<ref name="TLoZ Old Man" /> This quote has become a [[Iconic quotes in The Legend of Zelda series|famous line]] throughout the ''Zelda'' fanbase, even being referenced in later media of the franchise or popular culture, like {{HW}}, where [[Impa]] references the line when talking to Princess Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|Princess Zelda, it's dangerous to go alone. Take me!|Impa|HWDE}}</ref>
**In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'', the line is also referenced in the Sword's [[Inventory]] description.<ref name="BotW Inventory" />

* In the original ''The Legend of Zelda'', due to hardware limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]], the starting Sword's hilt (alongside [[Arrow|Arrows]], Princess Zelda's dress, etc.) shares the same color palette as Link's [[Clothes|tunic]]. If he obtains the [[Blue Ring|Blue]] or [[Red Ring]], it will also become whitish blue or red, respectively. It even turns gray when Link [[Game Over|dies]].
* Link in the 3D games and other media is well known to carry any of his Swords on his back due to his [[scabbard]] (and [[Shield]]) being positioned there. However, although this is not realistic as they are supposed to be worn on the hip, this is an intentional choice by [[Nintendo]] to make the character be instantly recognized.[[File:OoT Giant's Knife breaking.png|thumb|233x233px|The Giant's Knife's blade being shattered in ''Ocarina of Time'', something that is considered startling during release]]
* The [[Giant's Knife]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' is the first Sword in the mainline series to break or wear down from regular use. It is succeeded by the [[Razor Sword]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' and the many blades Link obtains in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. The former weapon in the [[Nintendo 64]] title breaking apart after a few hits was met with considerable shock among fans at the time, as the ''Zelda'' franchise itself is heavily associated with swords and adds a feeling of being virtually defenseless (plus the weapon's high cost of 200 [[Rupee|Rupees]] makes obtaining and destroying it rather frustrating). Even the [[Master Sword]] was [[Decayed Master Sword|shattered]] in ''Tears of the Kingdom'' when Link attempted to protect Princess Zelda from [[Ganondorf]]'s vicious [[Gloom]] attack after being near-fatally wounded himself.
** In another case from the same game, Ganon knocks away Link's Master Sword during the final battle, forcing him to use different items to fight his archnemesis before retrieving it back. This marks the first time in the franchise Link was to lose a Sword.
** The earliest media in the series of any sword breaking is seen in the Japanese commercial of the original [[Family Computer Disk System|Famicom Disk System]] version of ''The Legend of Zelda'', where a defeated Link is shown speaking to the audience as his blade splits in half.
* Among the most powerful Swords used by Link in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series are the [[Master Sword Lv3]], [[Magical Sword]], [[Biggoron's Sword]], [[Fierce Deity Sword]], and more.
* Although the Sword is arguably the most important item within the franchise during gameplay, in ''The Legend of Zelda'', due to the total absence of linearity, it is possible for Link to progress and complete [[Dungeon|dungeons]] throughout his adventure without having the signature weapon at all. However, he cannot defeat the [[Final Boss]] - Ganon himself, in [[Level 9|Level-9]]; alongside the [[Silver Arrow|Silver Arrows]] for the final blow, it is required for him to have any of the three blades (Sword, [[White Sword]], or Magical Sword) to be able to harm his archenemy, as other items such as [[Bomb|Bombs]] or the [[Magical Rod]] are ineffective.[[File:MM beta Kokiri Sword model.png|thumb|The beta Kokiri Sword in ''Ocarina of Time''|254x254px]]
* During development of ''Ocarina of Time'', the [[Kokiri Sword]] was originally in a wooden texture, resembling the basic Sword from ''The Legend of Zelda''.
* The Sword can be considered the equivalent of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series' [[mariowiki:Super_Mushroom|Super Mushroom]], as these are the main items used by the main protagonists to help them progress levels.
* In ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', Link uses seven different Swords. Although most of them are from the N64 installments where the [[Hero of Time]] was playable, the Magical Sword is included and two more are exclusive to the game - the [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Armos_Series Armos Series] (based on the otherwise unused weapons wield by [[Armos]] in ''Ocarina of Time'') and the evil [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge Soul Edge] taking on a [[Soul Edge (Complete)|shape resembling his Master Sword alongside the Hylian Shield]].
** Notably, the main protagonist also uses the [[Megaton Hammer]], [[Cane of Byrna]], and [[Bug Net]] like swords in-game, which appears especially impractical for the latter item. However, in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds|A Link Between Worlds]]'', the Super Net upgrade is able to damage and even defeat enemies. It is possible this particular item was inspired by the Namco fighting game's usage.
