'''Lizalfos Lv. 3''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Lizalfos Lv. 3|This massive Lizalfos is native to the Faron Wetlands. It is resistant to fire and is also at home underwater.<ref name="EoW Lizalfos Lv. 3">{{Cite|This massive Lizalfos is native to the Faron Wetlands. It is resistant to fire and is also at home underwater.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Lizalfos Lv. 3 are stronger, Blue-skinned variant of the standard [[Lizalfos]] and [[Lizalfos Lv. 2]], boasting resistance to fire and the ability to swim underwater.

The Lizalfos Lv. 3 also comes in a [[Dark Echo|Dark variant]] apon coming in contact with a [[Tecuum]].
