"{{Term/Store|The Royal Guard's Gear||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Royal Guard's Gear}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Riverside Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|The Royal Guard's Gear}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} offered by {{Term|BotW|Parcy|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveller who resides at the {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first meets her, she asks him if he has ever been to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, explaining that it had fallen to ruin over 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Have you gone to the giant castle north of here? That's Hyrule Castle. It's said to have fallen to ruin over a hundred years ago.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> She used to sneak into the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} herself looking for ancient treasure, only to stop more recently due to the environmental hazards that surround the area.<ref>{{Cite|I used to sneak in there to salvage ancient treasures, but it's too dangerous to go in there now. These days it's surrounded by an eerie mist and savage machine-like creatures.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> Back then, {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} was looking for equipment from the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|link}}, though she was never able to find any.<ref>{{Cite|Back when it was safer, I went in looking for this ultra-rare equipment that once belonged to the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard}}. It's legendary among collectors, but I was never able to find any...|Parcy|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} must retrieve at least one piece of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series}} from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} to show to {{Term|BotW|Parcy}}. Upon seeing one of the {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}}, she will recognize it and ask to see it.<ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that bow you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that big sword you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that shield you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that spear you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that sword you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> After looking at the {{Term|BotW|Item}}, {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} and express a desire to find one herself.<ref>{{Cite|This is...incredible. Thank you. You have shown me something truly spectacular. I need to go out and find one of these myself.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> As a token of her appreciation, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a token of my appreciation.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> Once this is done, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
