{{Term/Store|Malice Guardian|Malice Guardians|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{HWAoC}}. They are [[Guardian Stalker|Guardian Stalkers]] that have been heavily corrupted by [[Malice]].

==Characteristics==
Malice Guardians behave incredibly similarly to Guardian Stalkers, except their abilities involve Malice. They can slam two of their feet on the floor to create the same attack that the Elemental Guardians can use (but for Malice Guardians, they have malice enhanced lasers that will instantly break it's weak point if deflected). However, they have much more health - usually taking around three weak point smashes before being defeated - and their legs can take much more damage before breaking.
