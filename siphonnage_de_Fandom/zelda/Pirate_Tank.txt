{{Term/Store|Pirate Tank|Pirate Tanks|Series, ST|plural}} are [[Miniblin]] tanks in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 198}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Pirate Tanks only appear after [[Link]] rescues [[Wadatsumi]] from the [[Pirate Hideout]]. When Link is carrying a passenger on the [[Spirit Train]], Pirate Tanks will appear and attempt to kidnap them. Pirate Tanks can be defeated by shooting them with the [[Cannon]]. If a pirate tank, reaches the train, Miniblins will board the train and try to carry off the passenger to their [[Pirate Hideout|Hideout]], if the route to hideout is not unlocked, this will instead lead to an instant [[Game Over]].
