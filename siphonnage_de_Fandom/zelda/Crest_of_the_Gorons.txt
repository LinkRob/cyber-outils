The {{Term/Store|Crest of the Gorons|Crests of the Gorons|Series}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Symbol|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 47}}</ref> It is used to represent the {{Term|Series|Goron|link}} tribe and depicts a stylized image of the {{Term|OoT3D|Goron's Ruby|link}} from {{OoT}}.

==Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The {{Term|OoT3D|Crest of the Gorons}} appears tattooed on {{Term|OoT|Darunia|link}}'s arm in {{OoT}}.

==={{TWW|-}}===
The {{Term|TWWHD|Crest of the Gorons}} appears on {{Term|TWWHD|Darunia|link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Stained Glass|link}} in {{TWW}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Crest of the Gorons}} appears on {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}'s clothing in {{BotW}}.
