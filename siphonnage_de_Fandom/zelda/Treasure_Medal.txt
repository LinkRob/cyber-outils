The {{Term/Store|Treasure Medal||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 141}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Treasure Medal is a token-like item that is found in a [[Goddess Cube Chest]], which is unlocked after finding the [[Eldin Volcano]] [[Goddess Cube]] it is keyed to, with said Cube being found near the [[Bokoblin]] settlement next to the [[Earth Temple]]. The Chest itself is found on a minor sky island southeast of [[Beedle's Island]].

The Treasure Medal boosts the chances of slain monsters dropping [[Treasure]]s whenever it is kept in the [[Pouch|Adventure Pouch]].
