The {{Term/Store|Red Juggler||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 245">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM, MM3D}}}}</ref> The {{Term|Series|Red Juggler}} goes unnamed in {{OoT}}.{{Note|In the {{MM}} section of {{E}}, the twins are individually named "Red Juggler" and "Blue Juggler".<ref name="E 245"/> These names are not used in the {{OoT}} section, where they are instead collectively named the "Twin Jugglers",<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref> despite the fact that they are never seen juggling in-game.}}


==Biography==
The {{Term|Series|Red Juggler}} is a member of the {{Plural|Series|Twin Jugglers|link}} along with his brother, the {{Term|Series|Blue Juggler|link}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
The Red Juggler is shown as something of a bully who is constantly mocking others, particularly the Blue Juggler, and is always laughing over something, usually someone else.

When Link first arrives in [[Hyrule Castle Town]], the Red Juggler laughs at the Blue Juggler, calling him an idiot for getting stuck in the drainpipe resulting in the castle increasing its security. <ref>{{Cite|I wanted to see Princess Zelda! I avoided the guards... Swam through the moat... I was almost there when they caught me!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wha-ha-ha! What a crazy guy! Can you believe this guy was crazy enough to try to sneak into the castle to see Princess Zelda? All because of this idiot, they've tightened security at the castle. Wha-ha-hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

Seven years later, the Red Juggler has fled to Kakariko village and is sheltered by an [[:File:OoT GenerousWoman2.png|unnamed generous woman]] who also lodges [[Talon]] (when he is driven away from [[Lon Lon Ranch]]), [[Professor Shikashi]], [[Mamamu Yan]], and an unnamed bearded man. The Red Juggler still bullies the Blue Juggler and  disbelieves his claim of seeing [[Dampe]]'s ghost with the [[Hookshot]].<ref name="Dampé 1">{{Cite|I tell you, I saw him! I saw the ghostly figure of Dampé the gravekeeper sinking into his grave. It looked like he was holding some kind of treasure!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref><ref name="Dampé 2">{{Cite|Wah hah hah hah hah hah! He must have been seeing things! Wah hah hah hah hah hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref> He will also make fun of Link for wearing [[Kokiri Clothes]] if he visits him at night.<ref>{{Cite|Wah hah hah hah hah hah! Those are some funny-looking clothes, man!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>
