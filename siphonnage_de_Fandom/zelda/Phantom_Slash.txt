'''Phantom Slash''' is the down special move of [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] in {{SSB4}} and {{SSBU}}. It replaces Zelda's [[Transform]] move from {{SSBM}} and {{SSBB}} due to her and [[Sheik]] being made separate [[fighter]]s.

==Overview==
Based on Zelda's ability to possess [[Phantom]]s in {{ST}}, Phantom Slash involves Zelda summoning a Phantom to dash forward and attack anyone in the path. The Phantom's presence is only temporary, but the player can charge the attack to have the Phantom traveling greater distances. If the attack is fully charged, the Phantom follows its attack by swinging opponents upward.
