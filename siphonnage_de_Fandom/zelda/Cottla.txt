{{Term/Store|Cottla||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cottla|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Cottla}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} girl residing in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} with her sister {{Term|BotW|Koko|link}} and their father {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}}.<ref name="Daughters"/> {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} and her sister {{Term|BotW|Koko}} wake up at 5:00 AM, with {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} claiming her mother is hiding.<ref>{{Cite|Mommy's hiding! Hmmm, maybe she's over there!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially asks {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} if she would like to play, she will refuse, claiming that she and her mother are already in the middle of a game.<ref>{{Cite|NO!! I'm playing with Mommy right now. You'll just have to wait your turn!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> She will then head up to the {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine|link}} to search for the her mother.<ref>{{Cite|Mommy's hiding...but I'll find her!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> By 8:00 AM, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} gives up and goes to meet with the rest of her family.<ref>{{Cite|Daddy! Big Sis! I'm waaaiiiting!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref>  At 8:30 in the morning, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} will sit at the base of a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} near a fork in the road, wondering if her other family members will come by.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder...if they'll be here soon...|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> At around 9:00 AM, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will join her. After {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks with {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} for the first time, {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} will start to join them at this time.

While they sit together, {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} shares stories with his children.<ref>{{Cite|I hear a woman crying...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Any moment I can spend with my girls is a gift. I always end up boring them with the same stories, though.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} tells them about how one can hear a woman sobbing in the woods past the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}},<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} right above this hill, guarding the Sheikah tribe. Sometimes from {{Color|BotW Red|the forest behind it}}, you can hear... {{Big|a woman sobbing!}} ...Heh! That's the story I'm telling the children right now, anyway.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> but he is actually just trying to scare them away from the woods, which have become full of {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Actually, the location I speak of is where the village's {{Color|BotW Blue|guardian spirit}} resides. But lately monsters have been lurking there, and it's become quite dangerous. I'm scaring my daughters so they won't go near that forest...}}|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} only becomes excited by the story instead of being fearful.<ref>{{Cite|It works on Koko, but for some reason, those stories just excite Cottla...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

At 11:00 AM, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} and {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} will get up and go their separate ways, running through {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}} as {{Term|BotW|Dorian}} returns to work. Even in the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} can be found running about.<ref>{{Cite|I looove the rain! Yay, a race!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her during this time, she will declare that she is bored and wonder when dinner will be.<ref>{{Cite|When's food?! I'm BORED!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> Should he immediately say goodbye, she will be disappointed.<ref>{{Cite|But...I'm bored...|Cottla|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} if she would like to play with him, she will be excited at once, causing her to ask what he would like to play.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|YAAAY!}} What'll it be? What'll it be?|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> For games, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can choose between Tag or Hide-and-seek. Choosing Tag excites {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} further and she begins to run in place.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|YAAAY!}} I'll start running!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> Choosing Hide-and-seek makes {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} declare that she will hide while {{Term|BotW|Link}} seeks her.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|YAAAAY!}} I'll go hiiiide!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> Before she leaves, she lets {{Term|BotW|Link}} know that they will have to stop the game at dinnertime.<ref>{{Cite|But I've gotta go home to Sis when it's dinnertime, OK?|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> Selecting a game and agreeing to play begins the "{{Term|BotW|Playtime with Cottla|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} catches {{Term|BotW|Cottla}}, she will pout.<ref>{{Cite|Boo! You found me!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> However, she is excited to have had so much fun.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|THAT WAS SO FUN, THOUGH!}}|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} her secret treasure, which is {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is my secret treasure... But here, you can have it! Crunchy crunchables!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref>

At around 4:30 PM, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} will sit at one of the tables near {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Koko}} has been {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So hungry!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> While sitting, she will complain about wanting dinner.<ref>{{Cite|Dinner! DINNER!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can still ask if she would like to play, but she is too focused on dinner to want to.<ref>{{Cite|NO!! I've gotta chow down with Big Sis now! You're just gonna have to wait!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> During the "{{Term|BotW|Cooking with Koko|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} mentions that {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} loves {{Plural|BotW|Hot Buttered Apple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|OH!}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Hot buttered apple}} sounds good! Mother used to make it all the time! Cottla loves it too!|Cottla|BotW}}</ref>

7:00 PM is when both {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} and her sister {{Term|BotW|Koko}} head home for the night.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going home...|Cottla|BotW}}</ref> While asleep in her {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cottla}} dreams about her parents.<ref>{{Cite|Mommy...|Cottla|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Daddy...|Cottla|BotW}}</ref>
