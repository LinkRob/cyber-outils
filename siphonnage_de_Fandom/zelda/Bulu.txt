{{Term/Store|Bulu||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Anouki}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref> He resides in {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|ST|Bulu}} lives in a house in the southeast portion of {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}}. When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} visits the village for the first time, he is tasked by {{Term|ST|Honcho|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Anouki}} chief, to pair up all of the {{Plural|ST|Anouki}} in order to put together a village watch due to all the monsters that have begun to appear after the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}} vanished. {{Term|ST|Bulu}} comments to {{Term|ST|Link}} he dislikes {{Plural|ST|Anouki}} who grow out their facial hair.<ref>{{Cite|Just remember this! I can't stand Anoukis who grow out their facial hair.  They think they're so great! I mean, who do they think they are! Bah!|Kofu|ST}}</ref> Due to his preferences, {{Term|ST|Link}} ends pairing him up with an {{Term|ST|Anouki}} named {{Term|ST|Yeko|link}}.
