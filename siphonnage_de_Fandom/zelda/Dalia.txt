{{Term/Store|Dalia||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dalia|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dalia}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} child who lives in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. At night, she sleeps at {{Term|BotW|Lorn's Fruit Stand|link}}, which is a {{Term|BotW|Stall|link}} owned by her mother, {{Term|BotW|Lorn|link}}.<ref name="Mama"/> According to {{Term|BotW|Lorn}}, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} loves {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake|link}}, so she makes it every once in a while.<ref>{{Cite|My kid really loves {{Color|BotW Blue|fruitcake}}, so I make it every once in a while.|Lorn|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first enters {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} and the other three children speaking to each other as a group as the sun sets. {{Term|BotW|Kalani|link}} asks them if they are in love.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you guys! Are you in love?|Kalani|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Makure|link}} then responds that she wants to attend {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}}'s class when she grows up and get married.<ref>{{Cite|When I'm all growed up, I will be! I'll go to Miss Ashai's class, and I'll become in love and I'll get married!|Makure|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pearle|link}} then comments that {{Term|BotW|Ashai}} is unmarried and wonders what that means.<ref>{{Cite|Mom says it's really strange that Miss Ashai is unmarried but teaches her class on voe. I wonder what unmarried means...|Pearle|BotW}}</ref> Lastly, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} says that she heard being in love is the sweetest thing in the world, but she does not think she can handle something sweeter than berries.<ref>{{Cite|I heard that being in love and being married is the sweetest thing in the world! But I really like berries, and those are really sweet. I don't know if I can handle something sweeter than that!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>

After this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} in the empty plot near {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}. She asks what someone from out of town is doing in a place like this.<ref>{{Cite|What's a vai from out of town doing in a place like this?|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what she is doing, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} reveals that she is planning on starting a fruit garden in the plot, though she is keeping it from her mother for now.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Heehee! I'm still keeping it a secret from Mama, but I'm growing a fruit garden here!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> Once the garden is made, she will be able to eat as mush fruit as she wants.<ref>{{Cite|Then I can eat all the fruit I want until my tummy gets filled right up!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> However, she has not yet decided what she will plant.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't figured out what I want to plant here yet, though...|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} promises to share some of what she grows with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If I grow a lot, I'll share some of it with you, OK?|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} and started "{{Term|BotW|The Thunder Helm|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, he can find {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} crying in the empty plot. {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} explains that she wanted to plant an orchard in the plot, going so far as to pick up the {{Term|BotW|Trash|link}}, pull the weeds, and till the ground.<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to plant an orchard here... I picked up trash... I pulled the weeds... I tilled the ground... I did all the stuff you're supposed to do to make the earth happy...|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} from the nearby trough is unusable due to a flow of {{Term|BotW|Trash}} into it.<ref>{{Cite|But... But... The {{Color|BotW Red|trash}}... All this {{Color|BotW Red|trash}} keeps getting washed down into the water trough, and now I can't use the water there anymore.|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> Without the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}, her orchard will not be able to flourish.<ref>{{Cite|And if I can't use the water anymore... my orchard is doomed!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> After she is finished speaking, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive "{{Term|BotW|The Mystery Polluter|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} finds out that the source of the {{Term|BotW|Trash}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} named {{Term|BotW|Calyban|link}}, who enjoys eating {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}} on the walls of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and throwing the rinds into the waterway. This pollutes the water trough that {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} for her orchard. {{Term|BotW|Link}} convinces {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} to stop by giving her 10 {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}, and I can hold off on eating so many melons.|Calyban|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes, I'll need at least {{Color|BotW Red|10}}...|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} receives her {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}}, though, she feels bad for Dalia, so she helps clean up the trash.<ref>{{Cite|Now that my craving's been satisfied, I realize just how horribly selfish I've been... Shame on me. I'm going to go help clean up the trash! I've really caused a lot of trouble...but now I'm going to make it right! Sarqso!|Calyban|BotW}}</ref> 

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Dalia}}, {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} has already cleaned up her {{Term|BotW|Trash}} and left.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! You! Listen to this! Some other vai I've never met before helped me clean up the trash!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Hydromelon}} that was given to her by "the lady who cleaned it up" as a thank you for caring about her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I also got a hydromelon from the lady who cleaned it up! I'll share! It's a sarqso for caring about me!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dalia}} claims that {{Term|BotW|Calyban}} also gave her some {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}}, which has inspired her to make her garden into a {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}} patch.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yeah, the lady who helped clean gave me some wildberries! So now my field is gonna be a wildberry patch!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} he can come eat the {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}} anytime he likes, after which "{{Term|BotW|The Mystery Polluter}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.<ref>{{Cite|You can come eat the berries anytime you like! It'll be great!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can come back later to find that three {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}} plants have grown there. According to {{Term|BotW|Dalia}}, when she planted the first {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}}, more began to sprout from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Wildberries}} are amazing! Yeah! Yeah! When I buried one in the ground... {{Big|SHLOOP! SHLOOP! SHLOOP!}} More and more sprouted up, on after the other!|Dalia|Botw}}</ref> Despite her work clearing up the garbage and preparing the garden, she thinks the first {{Term|BotW|Wildberry}} must have been magic.<ref>{{Cite|I worked so hard to clean up the rubbish and make the earth here happy, but... I think that first {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}} must have been a MAGIC {{Color|BotW Blue|wildberry}}!|Dalia|BotW}}</ref> In her sleep, she even talks about eating {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I'm gonna eat a lot of wildberries...}}|Dalia|BotW}}</ref>
