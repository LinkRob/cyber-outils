{{Term/Store|Mastering Remote Bombs||HWAoC}} is a Level 4 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}. It is unlocked after completing {{Term|HWAoC|The Battle of Hyrule Field|link}} and is used to introduce to the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} the {{Term|HWAoC|Remote Bomb|link}} rune of the {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Slate|link}}.

At the start of the challenge, the player is notified of the quest of killing 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}, preferably with {{Plural|HWAoC|Remote Bomb}}. After that, a gate opens to an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}, which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} has to capture. After it is captured, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}} appears. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}} is defeated. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}},  five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Screw|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Gear|link}}.
