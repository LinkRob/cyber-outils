The {{Term/Store|Bean Seller||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238 {{Exp Game|OoT}} & 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Bean Seller sells [[Magic Bean]]s, which can grow into magic leaves that are able to carry [[Link]] to otherwise inaccessible areas. He can be found sitting beside [[Zora's River]], where he constantly feasts on the beans he carries in his sack. Once {{Term|OoT|Link}} has purchased his first {{Term|OoT|Magic Bean}} he will explain how to plant them in patches of {{Term|OoT|Soil|link}}<ref>{{Cite|Set a bean to (C) and use it on the dirt right here.|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref>. These Magic Beans are not very popular at first and are relatively cheap,<ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... How about some Magic Beans? They aren't selling very well... How about... 10 Rupees for one piece?|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref> but each time Link buys a pod of beans, the price for the next pod jumps by 10 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... How about some Magic Beans? Well, they're not that popular yet. How about... 20 Rupees for one piece?|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref> The Bean Seller's notions about their popularity changes gradually until he calls them "legendary."<ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... How about some Magic Beans? They are getting to be quite popular... 30 Rupees for one piece.|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... How about some Magic Beans? They're all the rage! 40 Rupees for one piece.|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... We have the popular Magic Beans! You'll regret it if you don't buy them now! 50 Rupees for one piece. (Just for now.)|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... These are the super-popular Magic Beans! In case you're wondering, they'll soon be sold out! Super price! 60 Rupees for one piece!|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... We have the super-rare Magic Beans! This could be your last chance! Special price! 70 Rupees for one piece!|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... We have the legendary Magic Beans! I'll sell them only to you! Super price! 80 Rupees for one piece!|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... Do you want...Magic Beans?! They aren't cheap, but...do you still want them? I can't let them go for less than 90 Rupees apiece!|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref> Eventually, due to Link's patronage, he believes that they are so popular that he refuses to part with them for under 100 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Chomp chomp chomp... We have...Magic Beans! Do you want them...huh? Huh? 100 Rupees for one piece! Keyahahah!|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref> After the last Bean is bought, he says that he is sold out of Magic Beans.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, too bad! We're sold out! Chomp chomp...What? Oh, these beans are not for sale!|Bean Seller|OoT}}</ref>

During the ending sequence the {{Term|OoT|Bean Seller}} and the {{Term|OoT|Lake Scientist|link}} can be seen with the {{Term|OoT|Carpet Merchant|link}} floating on his carpet at {{Term|OoT|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Bean Seller hides in a [[Cave|Grotto]] within the [[Deku Palace]].<ref>{{Cite|Need {{Color|MM3D Red|bean}} from {{Color|MM3D Red|bean seller}}. Live {{Color|MM3D Red|beneath}} palace garden.|Monkey|MM3D}}</ref> Unlike his {{OoT}} counterpart, his Beans sell for 10 Rupees no matter how many Link buys, with the first pod being free.<ref>{{Cite|Well...chomp, chomp... You're the first customer I've had in a looooong time! Do you need any {{Color|MM3D Red|Magic Beans}}? They sprout {{Color|MM3D Red|leaves}} as soon as you {{Color|MM3D Dark Blue|water}} them. That they do, they do. Mmm, I'll give you {{Color|MM3D Red|one}} free sample, so try planting it in some {{Color|MM3D Red|soft soil}}. You can always buy more.|Bean Seller|MM3D}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, the Bean Seller informs Link that he can enter the [[Outer Garden]] from within his Grotto, and that he must water the Magic Beans in order for them to sprout.<ref>{{Cite|You can get to the {{Color|MM3D Red|Outer Garden}} from over by the fountain. Find the {{Color|MM3D Red|soft soil}} and theeeere you can use it. You can plant the Beans whenever you want, but if you don't {{Color|MM3D Dark Blue|water}} 'em, their {{Color|MM3D Red|leaves}} won't grow.|Bean Seller|MM3D}}</ref>
