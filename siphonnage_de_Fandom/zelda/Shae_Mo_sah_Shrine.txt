{{Term/Store|Shae Mo'sah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shae Mo'sah Shrine}}<br/>Swinging Flames}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Swinging Flames''', is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah Shrine}} is located on a cliff in the northern part of {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}, along the path to the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} can be reached by taking the upward path from the {{Term|BotW|Boss's House|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shae Mo'sah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Shae Mo'sah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah Shrine}} consists of a number of connected rooms. The first room has a barred entrance to the next chamber, which is operated by a nearby {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} that must be kept pressed to open it. Directly above this room is a suspended lamp in an opening of the ceiling, which can be dropped by snapping its cable and thus burn the vines beneath it. Burning the vines will also destroy a wooden platform holding a stone {{Term|BotW|Barrel|link}}, which can be used to press down the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}.

The second room is opens to a much larger chamber that is inhabited by two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} and one {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}. Past the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}, a ramp leads to the third chamber. Within is another {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}, which lowers a lamp on a chain when activated. This lamp can be knocked back by shooting it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} or using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, which will set the wall's vines aflame, releasing a small {{Term|BotW|Iron Ball|link}}.

Around the room's corner is another {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}. When it is activated, it will open a barred door, allowing the {{Term|BotW|Iron Ball}} to slip through and roll a wheel. Doing this will open another door to the next room. In this next room is another ramp leading back to beginning of the third chamber and a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Small Key}} opens a door to the left of the first ramp. In the fourth chamber, there is another {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} that lies on a raised platform, and pressing it will lower two lamps. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must swing one of these lamps using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} and then cut its cable to light the vines underneath. This drops another stone {{Term|BotW|Barrel}} from a wooden platform. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use this {{Term|BotW|Barrel}} to press down the second {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}, opening the portcullis at the back of the chamber. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also use {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}} while he is standing on it, which keeps the portcullis open long enough for him to pass through.

The final chamber of {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah Shrine}} contains the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah}}. When spoken to, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shae Mo'sah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shae Mo'sah|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found to the left when {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the second room. When it is opened, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will obtain a {{Term|BotW|Stone Smasher|link}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be found past the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout}}, and it contains a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}. Finally, a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found in the third chamber, below the chute that deposits the lamp. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find 10 {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}.
