'''Needle Storm''' is a recurring ability of [[Sheik]]'s in the {{SSB|series}}. It first appears in {{SSBM}}, followed by {{SSBB}}, {{SSB4}}, and {{SSBU}}.

==Overview==
When using the move, Sheik can charge it to get up to six needles, and the player can release the B button to have her throw all of the needles, each inflicting little damage to whomever they hit. In {{SSB4}} and {{SSBU}}, Needle Storm is charged by pressing the B button, and the player can attack by pressing the button again.

Exclusively in {{SSB4|-}}, Needle Storm has two variations: '''Penetrating Needles''', in which three three needles are thrown, capable of going through them and inflicting electric damage, and '''Paralyzing Needles''', in which Sheik immediately throws a needle that can stun whomever it hits.
