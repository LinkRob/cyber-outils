'''Tangler Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Tangler Lv. 2|Lighting up its surroundings with a head-mounted lamp, this scowling fish charges straight ahead.<ref name="Tangler Lv. 2">{{Cite|Lighting up its surroundings with a head-mounted lamp, this scowling fish charges straight ahead.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Tangler Lv.2 is a tougher variant of the standard [[Tangler]], with higher health and damage. After defeating one, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Tangler [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of two.
