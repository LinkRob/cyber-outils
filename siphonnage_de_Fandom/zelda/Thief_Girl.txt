The {{Term/Store|Thief Girl||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Thief Girl is first found imprisoned in the [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|Thieves' Hideout]].<ref name="Rhyme"/> She was imprisoned after discovering the location of the portrait of [[Osfala]].<ref>{{Cite|Well, I may have "accidentally" learned where the boss hid it... maybe.|Thief Girl|ALBW}}</ref> After [[Link]] rescues her from her prison, she takes him to the location of Osfala's portrait.<ref>{{Cite|You're looking for that painting, yah? That one of the kinda younger... elder-looking fella? I'll show you where it's hidden.|Thief Girl|ALBW}}</ref> However, as they are trapped inside the hideout, Link must complete the dungeon with the Thief Girl's help.

After escaping the hideout, she leads Link to a house right of the Thieves' Hideout entrance where the portrait is hidden. She then leaves {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}} to hide in a cave south of the {{Term|ALBW|Vacant House|link}}. Inside the cave, she wears a mask and gives Link [[Rupee]]s as an additional reward for rescuing her.

==Trivia==
*The Thief Girl may be inspired by [[Ghanti]] from [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (Himekawa)|''A Link to the Past'' manga]] by [[Akira Himekawa]]. They share a similar appearance and role in the story, being thieves who wind up becoming Link's allies.
*When receiving Rupees from her, the Thief Girl says "How about we just keep this a secret to everybody, okay?" This is a reference to the iconic quote from {{TLoZ}}, [[Iconic Quotes from The Legend of Zelda#"It's a secret to everybody"|"It's a secret to everybody."]]
*The mask that the Thief Girl wears resembles a [[Pikku]].
