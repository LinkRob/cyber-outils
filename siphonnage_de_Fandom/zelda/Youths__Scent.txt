The {{Term/Store|Youths' Scent||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an object in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Youths'}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TPHD|Youths' Scent}} is the first {{Term|TPHD|Scent|link}} learned by {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}. It is learned in the {{Term|TPHD|Eldin Province|link}} of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} while it is covered in {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Youths' Scent}} is colored yellow, and it is picked up from {{Term|TPHD|Talo|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Sword|link}}, dropped when they were seized by {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Youths' Scent}} leads {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} into {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village|link}}, where he finds the {{Term|TPHD|Talo}}, {{Term|TPHD|Malo|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Beth|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Colin|link}} along with {{Term|TPHD|Renado|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Luda|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Barnes|link}}, hiding from the {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast}} and {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Insect|link}}.
