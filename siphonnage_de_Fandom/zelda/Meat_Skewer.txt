{{Term/Store|Meat Skewer|Meat Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meat Skewer}}<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Zesty Meat Skewer|Zesty Meat Skewers|Series, HWAoC|plural}},{{Exp Game|HWAoC}}<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Zesty Meat Skewer}}<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Meat Skewers can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} one to three pieces of meat together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they'll restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These skewers are juicy and filling snacks that are made with grilled meat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding more ingredients, different variations of Meat Skewers can be made:

* '''Hearty Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. They heal all of Link's Hearts when eaten and will temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meat Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they'll refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. These skewers will both refill and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when they're eaten.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Link gains an increase to his speed when he eats these.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] temporarily.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]] if they're consumed.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. If eaten, the power of Link's attacks will be boosted.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They'll increase Link's defense when he eats them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating these skewers will boost Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A juicy, filling snack made by grilling small chunks of meat on a skewer.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
