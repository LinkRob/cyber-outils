==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Bright-Eyed Crab}}
{{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab}} can be found on freshwater shores in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. Many of them live in the area surrounding {{Term|BotW|Ne'ez Yohma Shrine|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. They are also sold by the traveling {{Plural|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Chumin|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Bugut|link}}.

Eating a {{Term|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab}} raw will restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They can also be roasted by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make {{Term|BotW|Blackened Crab|link}} or chilled by {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} to make {{Term|BotW|Frozen Crab|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} one in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} will result in a meal with {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}}-restoring effects.<ref name="Inventory"/>
