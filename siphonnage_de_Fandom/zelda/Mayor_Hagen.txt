{{Term/Store|Mayor Hagen||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Mayor Hagen}}

{{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} is the presiding mayor of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, and lives in his {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House|link|display=House}} near {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aherrrm herrmmm! I am {{Color|TMC Green|Hagen}}, mayor of this fair town! Mayors are usually very important and busy folk, I'll have you know...But I still have a hobby, of course! I collect unusual masks! I just love them! They're beautiful! Simply magnificent, I tell you!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> He claims not to have done anything as mayor, yet the town runs smoothly.<ref>{{Cite|I think I've finally got a good grasp of how things run in {{Color|TMC Green|Hyrule}}. Things run so smoothly around here, and I haven't even done a thing! I wonder how much better this place would be if I actually did anything!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} first encounters {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}}, {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen|display=Hagen}} brags to {{Term|TMC|Link}} about owning a {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin|link|display=Lakeside Cabin}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. He tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that he goes there to practice his hobby of {{Term|TMC|Mask|link}} making.<ref>{{Cite|I hope I don't sound like I'm bragging, but I have a cabin at {{Color|TMC Green|Lake Hylia}}. It's a gorgeous little place where I go to enjoy my hobby, mask making.|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> Behind one of the {{Plural|TMC|Mask}} in his house live two {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Dashing|link}} into the wall with the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}} will cause the {{Plural|TMC|Mask}} to fall off, revealing the small hole where the {{Plural|TMC|Minish|link}} live. When broken, the {{Plural|TMC|Mask}} will sometimes yield {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Minish}} in his office will give insight into living in his home.<ref>{{Cite|The person living here is the mayor, very important you know. Doesn't it all just sound so...official?|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The mayor likes strange masks. Sometimes, he even makes his own.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> 

{{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} has constructed a shelter in his garden leading beneath {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} in preparation for the event that monsters should suddenly attack the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|display=Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aherrm!! That hole in the garden leads to a shelter of sorts. But it's not like I'm trying to run off and save myself alone or anything! Just keep that in mind!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> The shelter itself contains puzzles that require the use of the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} or {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}} in order to fully navigate it. Immediately beside the {{Term|TMC|Ladder|link}} leading down into the shelter is a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} that contains a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}.

While {{Term|TMC|Link}} is trying to gain {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}}'s assistance in reaching the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, he seeks out three overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}} for the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}. After he returns the first two, {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|link}} tells him that {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} had checked out the third and final book, {{Term|TMC|A History of Masks|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And the last book we still need is {{Color|TMC Red|A History of Masks}}. I think it was checked out by {{Color|TMC Green|Hagen}}, the mayor. How could he keep a library book so long! He's a public official!|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} asks {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} about the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}}, he suggests that {{Term|TMC|Link}} ask the {{Plural|TMC|Mask}} on his wall.<ref>{{Cite|Aherrm! Yes! That's me, {{Color|TMC Green|Hagen}}. What's that? You want a book? {{Color|TMC Red|A History of Masks}}? And you say I checked it out, do you? Yes, indeed I did... Or wait... Did I? You know, these masks see everything that goes on in my private life. Maybe you should just ask my masks!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> While the {{Plural|TMC|Mask}} themselves do not speak, the {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish}} living behind them will tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} that they saw {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} going to his {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's Lakeside Cabin|display=Cabin}} with the {{Term|TMC|Library Book|display=Book}} in hand,<ref>{{Cite|The mayor took off for his {{Color|TMC Green|cabin by the lake}} clutching a {{Color|TMC Red|book}}.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> and likely left it there.<ref>{{Cite|When the {{Color|TMC Green|mayor}} came back, he didn't have that {{Color|TMC Red|book}} anymore. I'm sure he just left it at his {{Color|TMC Green|lakeside cabin}}. He's pretty forgetful like that.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns the book and obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Librari}}'s {{Term|TMC|Trial|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} will attribute his success to {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen|display=Hagen}}'s own advice.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh, you seem to have found the book, safe and sound and in fine shape! But only because you listened to my masks like a good little boy, right? War har har har!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref>

As the game progresses, {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} continues to express his interests in both {{Plural|TMC|Mask}} and {{Plural|TMC|Library Book}} about them.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going to have to pick up volume 2. That last book was just getting good!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm thinking of ways to increase the library's budget to buy more books. Especially the ones about masks, you know.|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> Although he continues to avoid work,<ref>{{Cite|I may look somewhat idle to you, but no, sir! I will be working terrifically hard as soon as you leave. You can bet on that! Hoho!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> he takes responsibility for {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} and its citizens after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} and circumstances worsen in the area. He orders the people to the shelters,<ref>{{Cite|These are the times that really test my mettle as a mayor, I tell you. Hyrule is my responsibility to the last. Everyone to the shelters!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> and after {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, he reinforces this order while trusting {{Term|TMC|Link}} to save them.<ref>{{Cite|Monsters are coming! Everyone stay indoors! Oh, that doesn't apply to you, my boy! You go get 'em, {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! Your sword and bravery will save the day!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}} is one of the people with whom {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Oh, please, please, yes!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} successfully {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's so pretty! A perfect fit! Now, I know we'll get lucky!|Mayor Hagen|TMC}}</ref> a puddle in {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}} dries up to reveal a staircase. The stairs lead down into a cave housing a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest}} that contains a {{Term|TMC|Big Wallet|link}}, which increases {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Rupee}} capacity.

==Trivia==
*If {{Term|TMC|Link}} shoots an {{Term|TMC|Arrow|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}}, it will stick into him instead of passing through him. He is the only character in {{TMC}} to whom this will happen, and he will not respond to it in any way.
*{{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} has a natural distrust for {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen}}, and believes that his office is hiding a secret.<ref>{{Cite|That mustachioed guy strikes me as somewhat unreliable. There's something fishy about this room, too.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>
