The {{Term/Store|Moruge Forest||Series, TLoZ (Valiant Comics)}} is a forest found in [[Hyrule]], and is the home to [[Bagu]].{{Name Ref|TAoL}}<ref>{{Cite|He's come all the way from the Moruge Forest to visit us!|Link|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)}}</ref> It is first mentioned in an issue of the [[Valiant Comics]], when Bagu visits the [[North Palace]].

==Features and Overview==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
[[File:TAoL Bagu's House Overworld.png|thumb|left|An explanation on how to find Bagu's House]]
In {{TAoL}}, the name of the forest that serves as home to Bagu is not given, but a [[Bot]] living in [[Saria Town]] refers to it as the "Woods N of River".<ref>{{Cite|LET ME BE! MASTER IS IN WOODS. N OF RIVER|Bot|TAoL}}</ref> It is a densely wooded region situated directly to the north of the Water Town of Saria, next to the [[Moruge Swamp]]. Bagu lives in a house in the middle of the forest, and when [[Link]] meets him, he gives Link a note which must be shown to the [[River Man]] to freely cross the bridge in Saria Town.
