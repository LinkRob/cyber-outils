{{Term/Store|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|Sheikah Slate Pedestals|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 223}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} are {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal|link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} can be placed into. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} awakens in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}, the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} will guide him to take the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} from the nearby {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}}.<ref>{{Cite|That is a {{Color|BotW Red|Sheikah Slate}}. Take it. It will help guide you after your long slumber.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Doing so will open the door to the next chamber, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to progress.

Every {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} is outfitted with a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} underneath. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} will instruct him to place the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} inside it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Place the Sheikah Slate in the pedestal.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> This will activate the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau Tower|display=Tower}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Tower activated.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> causing all of the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} to rise out of the ground.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Please watch for falling rocks.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This tower and others just like it have erupted across the land, one after another. It is almost as though...a long-dormant power has awoken quite suddenly.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} hold onto the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} while {{Plural|BotW|Guidance Stone}} distill information to it. {{Plural|BotW|Guidance Stone}} on top of {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower}} will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} regional {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} data.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Distilling local information...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Regional map extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> Similarly, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can retrieve {{Term|BotW|Map}} information inside {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling map of Divine Beast.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> In {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} aid in the extraction of {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Rune extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

At the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} will use the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} to aid in the process of repairing the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Camera, Hyrule Compendium, and album missing files confirmed. Starting repair.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|A rune has been added.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Repair complete.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can place his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} in the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} to upgrade his {{Plural|BotW|Rune}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Slated for Upgrades|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Enhancing Sheikah Sensor.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Enhancing Remote Bomb rune.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Enhancing Stasis rune.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, once "{{Term|BotW|Sunshroom Sensing|link}}" has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can have {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} entries added using the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated.<br/>Adding picture to Hyrule Compendium.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

While {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack is active, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} will instruct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}.<ref>{{Cite|Go to the Shrine of Resurrection and return the Sheikah Slate to its terminal.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When he does this, the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial|link}} will be activated.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate and Champion verification complete. Activating the Divine Beast Tamer's Trial.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Map}} will then open, with four {{Plural|BotW|Marker|link}} now displayed.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Associated locations have been marked on the map.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} will then rise from the ground, and the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} will instruct him to take it to begin the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Take hold of the provided weapon to begin the trial.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref>

Later in the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, after {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed all four of the trials across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}, {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}} will tell him to return to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I am Maz Koshia. I must commend you for conquering the trial thus far. The door to the final trial is now open at the Shrine of Resurrection. Go, and face your destiny.}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> There, he will be instructed to place the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal}} to reveal a new trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Return the Sheikah Slate to the terminal you first obtained it from. Do so, and the final passageway will reveal itself...}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} will then cause the chamber to descend,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Descending...<br/>Please beware of tremors.}}|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|BotW}}</ref> bringing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}.
