The {{Term/Store|Northwest Gate||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|northwest gate}} of Gerudo Town.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Northwest Gate}} is one of three gates that serve as an entrance to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. It is guarded by {{Term|BotW|Laine|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier. Past the {{Term|BotW|Northwest Gate|display=Gate}} is the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|link}}, which is operated by {{Term|BotW|Kohm|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Laine}} at the {{Term|BotW|Northwest Gate}}, she will warn him against traveling the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}} during a sandstorm, as travelers can easily become lost.<ref>{{Cite|We caution all travelers to avoid the sandstorms. If you're caught in a sandstorm, you'll lose your way. Even us Gerudo have trouble navigating them.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She warns him that both thieves and priceless treasures are hidden in sandstorms.<ref>{{Cite|The danger of the sandstorms makes the desert the perfect hiding place for thieving bandits and priceless treasures.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> After receiving permission from {{Term|BotW|Teake|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to ask {{Term|BotW|Laine}} about the {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Karusa Valley?] So you're the Hylian that Captain Teake was talking about? The thieves' hideout is in {{Color|BotW Red|Karusa Valley}}.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She will point him towards the {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|display=Valley}} from the {{Term|BotW|Northwest Gate}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can't see {{Color|BotW Red|Karusa Valley}} from here, but it's in that direction.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can also ask about the treasure in the sandstorm.<ref>{{Cite|[Treasure?] Having said that, I don't really know the details either.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laine}} points out the nearby {{Term|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|link}}, explaining that there are more of them throughout the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There are {{Color|BotW Red|statues of swordswomen}} scattered across Gerudo Desert. They're remnants of ages long past.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> There are rumors that the {{Term|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|display=Statues}} lead towards an ancient treasure, but she dismisses this as superstition.<ref>{{Cite|People talk about the statues pointing to the hiding place of ancient treasure... but that's just superstition.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She also considers it too risky, considering the dangerous monsters in the area.<ref>{{Cite|Even if it was true, there are dangerous monsters in the sandstorms.<br/>I wouldn't risk it.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> This begins "{{Term|BotW|The Silent Swordswomen|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.
