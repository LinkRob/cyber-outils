{{Term/Store|Eeno|Eenos|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are enemies in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref> They are squat, snowy creatures that reside in cold regions.

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= That's an {{Color|MM White|Eeno}}.<br/> It's persistent!<br/> ...And it's susceptible to heat!
}}

Eenos slide across the ground in amorphous puddles and emerge unexpectedly when they sense movement nearby. When [[Link]] approaches one, it will quickly rise up and begin to pelt him with snowballs. Some Eenos are larger than others, and can toss snowballs larger than the average person. When attacked, these creatures will split into three smaller Eenos. [[Fire Arrow]]s will defeat any type of Eeno in a single attack, causing them to melt.

Their faces slightly resemble those of [[Goron]]s.
