'''Alligaroos''' are [[enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Alligaroos are crocodile-like humanoids encountered in [[Vendoss]] and the northern area of [[Seacoast Plain]]. Alligaroos move around quickly while roaring. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can defeat an Alligaroo by hitting it a few times, ranging from as many as six if Zelda has zero [[Celestial Sign]]s to as little as three hits if Zelda has five or six Celestial Signs.
