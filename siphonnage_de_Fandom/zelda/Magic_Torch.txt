{{Term/Store|Magic Torch|Magic Torches|Series, CoH|plural}} are {{Plural|CoH|Item|link}} in {{CoH}}, introduced in the {{SotM}} [[DLC]] pack.{{Name Ref|CoH}}

==Location and Uses==
Magic Torches are a type of {{Term|CoH|Torch|link}} that offer maximum visibility. {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid|link}} can use to see the items inside {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}, bushes, columns of dirt, and pots without the need to break them open.<ref>{{Cite|Provides amazing light and reveals the location of enemies, NPCs, and items. Slowly consumes stamina. Can burn stumps and light up braziers.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> Like other torches, they also allow visibility within dark areas and burn away stumps at the cost of durability (unless the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation|link}} is equipped). However, unlike other torches, the Magic Torch inflicts a passive stamina drain on the user.

They can be found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} or bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} for 75 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}}.
