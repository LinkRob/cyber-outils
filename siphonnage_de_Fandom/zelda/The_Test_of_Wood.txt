"{{Term/Store|The Test of Wood||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Test of Wood}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|The Test of Wood}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} is one of three that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must complete as part of "{{Term|BotW|The Korok Trials|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find this {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|link}} on the eastern edge of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}. There, he will find {{Term|BotW|Damia|link}}, who has been expecting him.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hero guy! I was told you'd be coming.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He asks if he is prepared for the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood|link}}, as it is supposed to be the hardest of the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|But do you really think you're ready for the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}?! Some say it's the hardest of the Korok Trials.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> However, he explains that all {{Term|BotW|Link}} has to do is make it to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} while equipped with the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But it's not complicated—all you need to do is equip this {{Color|BotW Red|Korok weapon set}} and reach the shrine at the back of the area.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} again if he is ready to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready to try it?|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to try, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will encourage him.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm ready.] All right. Go get 'em!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he will be disqualified if his {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}} breaks or is unequipped.<ref>{{Cite|You have the keep the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}, {{Color|BotW Red|bow}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|shield}} equipped the whole time. If they break or you unequip them, you're out!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have the {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} space for all of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear}}, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will tell him that he needs to make room before they can begin.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Your inventory is full! You need to make some space for a sword, a shield, and a bow before we can get started.|Damia|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} is then given a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Sword|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Damia}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that his goal is the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}},<ref>{{Cite|All you have to do is reach the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} in the back of the area. Off you go!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> before sending him off on the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}.

Along his journey, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter {{Plural|BotW|Keese|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fire Keese|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Electric Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fire Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Water Octorok|link}}. They must be either avoided or defeated in order to continue on with the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}. After {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through {{Term|BotW|Mido Swamp|link}} and approaches {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} will stop him and shout that he has reached his goal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gooooooooal!}}|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Damia}} flies down next to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and congratulates him for completing the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}}.<ref>{{Cite|CONGRATULATIONS!<br/>You've passed the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> He claims that happiness awaits him inside {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well done, hero man!<br/>Happiness awaits you at the shrine.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} want to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood}} again, {{Term|BotW|Damia}} says to let him know before flying away.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yeah! If you ever want to do the trial again, just let me know. See ya!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} can enter {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine}}.
