In {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Sword}} are a common, weak sword often used by travelers to defeat small {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Though it is fairly durable, it is unreliable due to its low attack power.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are often found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}, either abandoned or in use by {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}. 

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Sword}} can also be obtained by using the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to scan the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|SSB|Toon Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} ({{TLoZ}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Toon Link}} ({{TWW}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|TP|Link|link}} ({{TP}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|SS|Link|link}} ({{SS}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} (Rider) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

During "{{Term|BotW|The Weapon Connoisseur|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nebb|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him several different {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} to see. The first {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} he asks for is a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm, where should we start? Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|traveler's sword}}? I've seen it in books, but never in real life before! If you find it, please let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for bringing him a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Whoooa! The real thing is really the real deal!}} Thank you! Take this as a token of my thanks!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Sword}} are made from metal and will therefore attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they will also conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are magnetic, which means they can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. If they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they will sink.

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Sword}} art part of the {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear|link}} archetype of equipment, which is known to be used by travelers for self-defense.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
