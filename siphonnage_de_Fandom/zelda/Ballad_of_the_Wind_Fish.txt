The "{{Term/Store|Ballad of the Wind Fish||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}}" is a recurring song in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120{{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{LA|-}}===
The "Ballad of the Wind Fish" is also known as a song of awakening in {{LA}},<ref>{{Cite|Hoot! That girl sang her song in front of the Egg! Her 'Ballad of the Wind Fish' is a song of awakening! Did she actually intend to wake the Wind Fish?! The next Sirens' Instrument is in the west. Play your melodies so the unliving stones might hear! Show your courage! The Wind Fish waits for you! Hoot!|Owl|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|PLAY THE EIGHT INSTRUMENTS! PLAY THE SONG OF AWAKENING!!|Wind Fish|LADX}}</ref> for it has the ability to awaken sleeping people. A girl named [[Marin]], who lives in [[Mabe Village]] of [[Koholint Island]], can be seen in the village's square singing the song. Later on during the adventure, Marin will accompany [[Link]] around the island, and will help him by singing the "Ballad of the Wind Fish" to the sleeping [[Walrus]] who blocks the entrance to the [[Yarna Desert]], which will wake him up and have him move out of the way. Afterwards, Marin will be found in the square of [[Animal Village]], surrounded by Animals and still singing the song. When Link obtains the [[Ocarina]], he can talk to Marin either in Mabe Village or in Animal Village, and she will teach him the song. Playing the song on the Ocarina is necessary to open the [[Wind Fish's Egg]] after acquiring all eight [[Instrument of the Sirens|Instruments of the Sirens]]. After ultimately defeating the [[Shadow Nightmares]], Link plays the "Ballad of the Wind Fish" one last time to awaken the Wind Fish, making the island disappear.

The "Ballad of the Wind Fish" can be heard multiple times on certain occasions. In the opening scene of the game, some notes of the song can be heard playing in the background. Part of the song also plays when Link acquires any of the Eight Instruments of the Sirens. A remixed version of the song is used as the [[Southern Face Shrine]] theme. The song also plays during the final scene, the credits, the secret ending (''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|DX]]'' and [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Switch]] versions only), and the [[Game Over]] screen.

==={{MM|-}}===
The "Ballad of the Wind Fish" is a popular song by [[The Indigo-Go's]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Indigo-Go's is the popular group that we Zora are so proud of. They've put out a lot of songs. One of their hits is "Ballad of the Wind Fish." You know that song, don't you? Huh? You don't? Really? Even one that famous?|Toto|MM}}</ref> It is vastly different from the "Ballad of the Wind Fish" in {{LA|-}}. 

While in the [[Milk Bar]], [[Toto]], the manager of the Indigo-Go's, asks Link to play the song as a sound check,<ref>{{Cite|We were scheduled to do a show, but it got canceled. This place has a reputation for being a great live venue, so it's a shame. I'd like to do a sound check, though. I just want to see what it would have been like... Hey, you! Could you help me with a performance?|Toto|MM}}</ref> with each part of the song played by one of Link's transformations. [[Gorman]], who also sits in the Milk Bar, becomes nostalgic upon hearing the song and, as thanks for playing it, rewards Link with the [[Troupe Leader's Mask]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
The "Ballad of the Wind Fish" from {{LA|-}} is one of the many tunes the [[Lucky Lobby Ball]] plays when hit with the [[Sword]] in {{TFH}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In volume 1 of the {{LA|Cagiva}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Marin|link}} sings the "{{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ballad of the Wind Fish}}" for {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} while at {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Toronbo Shores|link}}. According to her, it is a song passed down {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|link}} and is loved by the animals at {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Animal Village|link}}. In volume 2, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} plays the song on the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ocarina|link}} alongside the eight {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Instrument of the Sirens|link}} in order to open the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish's Egg|link}}. After the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Wind Fish|link}} appears, he asks {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} to play the "{{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ballad of the Wind Fish}}" so they wake up together. As {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} plays the song, the inhabitants of {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island}} sing along while the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|display= Island}} disappears.

==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, part of [[Marin]]'s moveset when wielding the [[Bell#Hyrule Warriors|Bell]] includes her singing part of the "Ballad of the Wind Fish", creating a blast of water that damages enemies. Her pre-fight intro features her singing part of the ballad.
{{Noncanon|End}}
