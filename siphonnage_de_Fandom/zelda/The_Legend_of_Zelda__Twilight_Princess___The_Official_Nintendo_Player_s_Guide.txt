'''''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — The Official Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a guidebook released by [[Nintendo Power]] for {{TP}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 192 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{TP}}, as well as secrets and lists of collectables such as {{Plural|OoT|Piece of Heart|link}}.
