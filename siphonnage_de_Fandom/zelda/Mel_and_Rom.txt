'''Mel and Rom''' are two characters that make an appearance in the {{OoS|Himekawa}}. They are twins and also members of the {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Troupe of Performers|display=Troupe of Traveling Performers|link}} alongside {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Din (Oracle)|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Impa|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Parot|link}}, {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Jovan|link}} and {{Term|OoS (Himekawa)|Rishu|link}}. Their specialty seems to be playing tambourine.
