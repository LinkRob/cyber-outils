==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, a {{Term|SSHD|Wooden Mop}} can be found inside the {{Term|SSHD|Knight Academy|link}} in {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link}}. It is found leaning against the wall inside the {{Term|SSHD|Restroom|link}}. When examined, a message appears stating that it's used to keep the room clean.<ref>{{Cite|Keep it squeaky clean in here.|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

{{Compendium/Extract|Wooden Mop}}
{{Plural|BotW|Wooden Mop}} are commonly used to clean up around households in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Therefore, they can be found in villages such as {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} and ruins within {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. Despite their intended purpose, {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Mop}} have a sturdy construction, giving them some merit in combat.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being made of {{Term|BotW|Wood|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Mop}} will float when dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They are also unable to conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, but they will burn when exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Wooden Mop}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Daily Use Goods|link}} equipment archetype, which consists of tools used for the upkeep of settlements throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
