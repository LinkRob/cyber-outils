'''Brace Yourself Games''' is a Canadian video game development studio. They are known for the release of their first game, ''{{Wp|Crypt of the NecroDancer}}'', a {{Wp|roguelike}} rhythm game.<ref name="cotndpress" /> Their second game, {{CoH}}, is a crossover spin-off of ''NecroDancer'' and {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|retrieved=June 15, 2019|url=https://www.nintendo.com/games/detail/cadence-of-hyrule-crypt-of-the-necrodancer-featuring-the-legend-of-zelda-switch/|title=Cadence of Hyrule - Crypt of the NecroDancer Featuring the Legend of Zelda for Nintendo Switch - Nintendo Game Details|site=Nintendo}}</ref>
