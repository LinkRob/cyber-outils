==Location and Uses==
Moblin Trophies are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's [[Scenario]] battle or a [[Challenge]], with a Trophy earned for every [[Moblin]] kill (be they regular, [[Blue Moblin|Blue]], [[Black Moblin|Black]], [[Silver Moblin|Silver]], [[Fire Moblin|Fire]], [[Ice Moblin|Ice]], and [[Electric Moblin|Electric]]) a Warrior obtains.

Moblin Trophies can be used to either complete [[Side Quest#Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Quest]]s or be used in the Hyrule Blacksmith Guild workshop to de rust Rusted Weapons or remove Seals on a weapon.
