{{Term/Store|Joute||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Joute|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Joute}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who travels with his {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} between {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. He carries a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}. At both of his stops, he momentarily rests under a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} before continuing his journey. If it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}}, he will seek shelter near {{Term|BotW|Fort Hateno|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Equestrian Riding Course|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Joute}} likes {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} more than he does people.<ref>{{Cite|I love horsies. Love 'em more than people. People smell funny and talk all the time, but not horsies. Horsies just ride.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> In particular, he holds much affection for his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, referring to him as his "best horsey."<ref>{{Cite|My best horsey at my side.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> He keeps track of his hunger as they travel together,<ref>{{Cite|My horse's belly is full...|Joute|BotW}}</ref> and he makes remarks about needing to buff his {{Term|BotW|Saddle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Need to buff my saddle...|Joute|BotW}}</ref> During the night, he is happy to be able to see the stars with his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stargazin' with horsey...|Joute|BotW}}</ref> When it is {{Term|BotW|Rain|display=Raining}}, {{Term|BotW|Joute}} will inform {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that both he and his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} dislike such {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}}, so he tries to avoid it when he can.<ref>{{Cite|I don't care much to go out on rainy days. Me and my horsey don't like getting wet.|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My horsey's feeling blue... He gets a little sensitive on rainy days.|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Rain's got horsey damp...|Joute|BotW}}</ref>

As he travels, {{Term|BotW|Joute}} will have many {{Term|BotW|Horse}} facts to share with {{Term|BotW|Link}}, even if {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not care to hear them.<ref>{{Cite|Here's the truth, straight from the horse's mouth: the only thing I'm good for is horses and horse facts.|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You probably don't care for horsey facts, but that's all I've got to offer, so hold on to your...well, you know.|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm warning you, I only talk horse talk. Not, like, talking the way horses talk. I mean talking about horses.|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So you want some information, do you? Well, if you don't mind it bein' about horses, I'm your man!|Joute|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the secret to holding on to a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} is to approach it from behind quietly and hop on quick.<ref>{{Cite|The secret to holding on to your horses, by the way, is to approach them quietly from behind and then hop on quick!|Joute|BotW}}</ref> He recommends petting the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} after getting on them "to let them know you care."<ref>{{Cite|Once you're on, give 'em a little pet to let them know you care.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does catch a wild {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Joute}} to register it at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}, as they will give him a {{Term|BotW|Saddle}} and {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you catch a wild horsey, you should take it to a stable. Once you register it, you'll get a saddle and bridle.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> He also claims that {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} like {{Plural|BotW|Carrot|link}} and suggests buying them at {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Horses like carrots. So maybe go buy some over in {{Color|BotW Blue|Kakariko Village}} and try feeding them to a horse.|Joute|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Joute}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} vary in the speed and power, so it is important for him to keep their strength in mind.<ref>{{Cite|There are several types of horses, for instance. Fast ones. Powerful ones. Get to know what your horse is good at.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> He suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} choose a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} that suits his own needs.<ref>{{Cite|There are all kinds of horses, so get to know yours, and find one that's suited to what you need.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} will get tired if they run all the time, {{Term|BotW|Joute}} advises {{Term|BotW|Link}} to an eye on his {{Term|BotW|Horse}} to make sure it is alright.<ref>{{Cite|Horses get awful tired of runnin'. You can't just dig in your spurs all the time. Use your eyes to see you they're doing.|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Horses get tired after galloping for a long time. Take a moment to check in and see how your horsey's doing!|Joute|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Joute}} has a song to describe the different types of legendary {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, the lyrics of which encourage the listener to go catch them.<ref>{{Cite|♪White horsies, giants, and bony ones too. They're out there for catchin', and that's what you should do...♪|Joute|BotW}}</ref> Though he describes it at slightly inaccurate, he believes it is a catchy tune.<ref>{{Cite|That's my slightly inaccurate but highly lyrical song about the legendary horses. It'll get stuck in your ear real good!|Joute|BotW}}</ref> In order to make it work as a musical, he had to make it seems as though there were multiple of each legendary {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|You like my song about the legendary horses? There's only one of each, but I took some liberties to make it a musical...|Joute|BotW}}</ref>

In general, {{Term|BotW|Joute}} cares more about {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} than any local attractions.<ref>{{Cite|You want me to point out some local areas of interest? Sorry, but I only talk about horses. All day, every day.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> However, he does tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} about the {{Term|BotW|Equestrian Riding Course}}, which is in ruins.<ref>{{Cite|Although...if you head east from here, you'll find the ruins of the old horse training ground.|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you head east from here, you'll find the ruins of the old horse training ground.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> While it was once an important location, it is now suffering from an infestation of [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|monsters]].<ref>{{Cite|It's pretty important. Shows up on maps and everything. Sounds like it's got a bit of a monster infestation goin' on now...|Joute|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sounds like it's got a bit of a monster infestation goin' on now, though...|Joute|BOtW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Joute}} will share that {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} are not the only {{Plural|BotW|Animal|link}} that will allow someone to ride them.<ref>{{Cite|We all know horsies are special...but they're not the only animals that'll let people ride them.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> While he does not think it would be worth it to ride anything else, he believes {{Term|BotW|Link}} may be interested.<ref>{{Cite|I wouldn't waste my time on anything but a horse, but...you look like the type of person who doesn't discriminate.|Joute|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to try to ride any {{Term|BotW|Animal}} that looks mountable.<ref>{{Cite|Horsies aren't the only animals you can ride. If you see an animal you think you can ride, why not give it a shot?|Joute|BotW}}</ref>
