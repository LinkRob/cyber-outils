==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Vegetable Risotto is made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}}, and either a {{Term|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}} or any {{Term|BotW|Carrot|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version is made by using ingredients with contradicting status effects. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat them to heal some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Due to this dish's sweetness, it has a mild flavor.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of vegetable used, Link can make the following variations of Vegetable Risotto:

* '''Enduring Vegetable Risotto''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Vegetable Risotto}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The sweetness of the ingredients gives this risotto a mild flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. By eating them, Link can restore and overfill his {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Vegetable Risotto''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Vegetable Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The sweetness of the ingredients gives this risotto a mild flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}. They can be eaten to grant Link a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Tough Vegetable Risotto''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Vegetable Risotto}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The sweetness of the ingredients gives this risotto a mild flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Fortified Pumpkins. When eaten, these risottos will increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>
