{{Term/Store|Ultra Ball Haul||Series, TotK}} is a {{Term|TotK|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TotK}}.

==Location and Rules==
Ultra Ball Haul is located in {{Term|TotK|Tanagar Canyon|link}}, below the {{Term|TotK|Tabantha Great Bridge|link}}. A {{Term|TotK|Goron|link}} named Modar hosts the game. It costs 20 {{Plural|TotK|Rupee|link}} to play.

Before the game begins, Link has unlimited time to prepare, primarily to build a vehicle using nearby construction parts and/or {{Term|TotK|Zonai Devices|link}} to transport the ball. During the setup phase, Link is not allowed to move the ball several meters away from its original position.<ref>{{Cite|Uh, kid? The startin' spot is right here in front of me. Takin' the ball too far away is just askin' for trouble.|Modar|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! It's against the rules ta move the ball that far away before I say go! Back to your starting spots!|Modar|TotK}}</ref> He is allowed to use the ball as part of constructing a vehicle as long it remains within the starting area. Once Link is ready to begin the game, he will need to talk to Modar to start the game.<ref>{{Cite|Heya, kid! Are you ready? [All ready!] Now we're rollin'! OK, get to your mark!|Modar|TotK}}</ref>

The objective of the game is to carry the target ball as far away from its original position within the allotted time. Link is allowed to whatever means to carry ball away. If the ball is somehow lost or destroyed, the game ends in a failure.

The rewards for completing the game are {{Term|TotK|Rupee|link}} payouts. The farther the ball is carried away, the higher the reward.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Your score didn't wow me, but I'll still give ya a little something.|Modar|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There ya go! That's pretty good, kid! Bet ya can do even better next time too!|Modar|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Amazing! Ya got that ball far! I knew you were special, kid. That was a ball haul fer the ages. Now here, this is for you!|Modar|TotK}}</ref>
