==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Guts}} are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that are dropped by defeated {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Black Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, as well as by {{Plural|BotW|Golden Bokoblin|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}. They are also available to purchase at {{Term|BotW|High Spirits Produce|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. They convulse on their own occasionally.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Guts}} can be used with other ingredients to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, they can also be used to make {{Term|BotW|Purple Dye|link}}, or they can be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Guts}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
