The {{Term/Store|Blacklist|Blacklists|Series, TFH}} is a feature in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= The Blacklist|game= TFH|page= 12}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}} acts as a registry of players that {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} will not be connected with during online [[Multiplayer]]. {{Term|TFH|Link}} can learn of the feature by speaking to {{Term|TFH|Mr. Tudor|link}}, who recommends it as a means to deal with "false heroes".<ref>{{Cite|In the {{Color|TFH Red|online}} world, at times you may encounter false heroes. These pretenders, not having the heart of a true hero, may behave rather unheroically in the {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands}}. I recommend you register such false heroes on your {{Color|TFH Red|blacklist}}.|Mr. Tudor|TFH}}</ref> By touching and holding the {{Term|TFH|Hero Panel|link}} of the target player and pressing either the A or B button, a prompt appears onscreen to provisionally add them to the {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}}. This can be performed inside the [[Hytopia Castle|Multiplayer lobby]] or at any time during a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}}, aside from cut-scenes or [[Saving]]; following the Ver. 2.0.0 update, a player can even be {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}}ed after they exit from a game in progress.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Users will be able to add other users to their blacklist if they exit the game through the pause menu during the game.|retrieved= Feb. 5, 2022|url= https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/15571/~/how-to-update-the-legend-of-zelda%3A-tri-force-heroes|title= How to Update The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes|site= Nintendo Support}}</ref> If {{Term|TFH|Link}} himself exits from a game, he will be warned by the {{Term|TFH|Match Master|link}} of the potential to be {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}}ed.<ref>{{Cite|Young hero... Might I have a word?<br/>[...]<br/>Lately, more and more false heroes have been added to {{Color|TFH Blue|blacklists}}... It's rather unfortunate.|Match Master|TFH}}</ref>

If a player has been provisionally {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}}ed, then once the team disbands, which will occur automatically after a {{Term|TFH|Level}} concludes, another onscreen prompt will ask to confirm or cancel that player's permanent addition to the {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}}.

Up to 100 players can be added to the {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}} before any new additions begin to overwrite the oldest additions.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= You can register up to one hundred players on your blacklist. When you go over a hundred, the oldest entries will be overwritten.|game= TFH|page= 12}}</ref> There is no way to view the contents of the {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}} or remove individual players, though the entire {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}} can be cleared at once from the Settings menu on the game's title screen. Players on the [[Nintendo 3DS]] Friend List cannot be {{Term|TFH|Blacklist}}ed.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= You cannot register a friend on your blacklist.|game= TFH|page= 12}}</ref>
