{{Term/Store|Blue Bubble|Blue Bubbles|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 169 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TWW, TWWHD}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
As a variant of the {{Term|TLoZ|Bubble|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Bubble}} are animated {{Plural|TLoZ|Skull|link}} that appear in the {{Term|TLoZ|Second Quest|link}} of {{TLoZ}}. They can be found in {{Term|TLoZ|Level 4|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 5|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 6|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 7|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ|Level 8|link}}, and {{Term|TLoZ|Level 9|link}}. Like {{Plural|TLoZ|Bubble}}, they move in erratic patterns and are invincible to all attacks. When touched, {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Bubble}} do not inflict damage to {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} and will instead dispel the curse that disables {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} from using his {{Term|TLoZ|Sword|link}} after touching a {{Term|TLoZ|Red Bubble|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= |magazine= Nintendo Power|issue=1|date= July/August 1988|page= 31}}</ref> Because this is one of the only ways for {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} to end a curse from a {{Term|TLoZ|Red Bubble}} (other than drinking a {{Term|TLoZ|Blue Water of Life|link}} or visiting a {{Term|TLoZ|Great Fairy|link}} in the {{Term|TLoZ|Overworld|link}}), {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Bubble}} are often found near {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Bubble}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Blue Bubble}}<br/>Guard against its blue flame with your shield!
}}

In {{OoT}}, Blue Bubbles are animated skulls surrounded by blue fire. They can be found in the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}, hovering in place in certain rooms. If Link approaches them, they will aggressively fly toward him. Their flames act as a barrier that protects them from most attacks. Link can defend himself with a [[Shield]] to extinguish their flame and knock them to the ground.<ref>{{Cite|Guard against its blue flame with your shield!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> On the ground, they helplessly hop around and attempt to escape Link while vulnerable. If Link places a [[Bomb]] near a Blue Bubble, they will be attracted to it, circling around the Bomb until it explodes.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= That's a {{Color|MM White|Blue Bubble}}!<br/> Quick! Run! Don't let it curse you! If it comes after you, defend yourself to block it!
}}

Blue Bubbles in {{MM}} are animated skulls surrounded by a blue flame. They can be found throughout various places in [[Termina]] during the night, such as [[Termina Field]] and [[Ikana]]. They will aggressively chase after Link if he comes near them, cursing him if they come into contact. If Link becomes cursed, he will be unable to use his {{Term|MM3D|Sword|link}} for a short period of time. However, the curse can be removed immediately by playing the "[[Song of Storms]]". Link can defend himself with a Shield to extinguish their flame and leave them vulnerable.<ref>{{Cite|That's a '''Blue Bubble'''! Quick! Run! Don't let it curse you! If it comes after you, defend yourself to block it!|Tatl|MM}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Tingle
|quote1= <p>It's an evil '''Blue Bubble'''!</p><p>Fan the curse away with the Deku Leaf!</p>
}}

In {{TWW}}, Blue Bubbles are floating skulls surrounded by curling blue flames. Blue Bubbles fly around at their own leisure, but immediately chase after Link when they spot him, attempting to catch him on their fire. They emit a cackling laughter whenever they spy or attack him. Their fire serves as a protective barrier that shields them from Link's attacks. These flames, however, can be blown away with the [[Deku Leaf]], causing the Bubble to lose all power as it falls to the ground as a regular skull, where it can be attacked normally.<ref>{{Cite|Fan the curse away with the Deku Leaf!|Tingle|TWW}}</ref> They can also be shot down with the [[Hero's Bow]] as well. Blue Bubbles curse Link when they come into contact with him. This sets Link on fire and prevents him from using any items so long as the flames remain. These flames can be instantly removed by stepping into a [[Light]] source. Blue Bubbles appear in the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]]. The temple's [[Blue Fog]] has an identical effect.
