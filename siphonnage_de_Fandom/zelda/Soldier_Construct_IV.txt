'''Soldier Construct IV''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{TotK}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name=Soldier Construct IV|desc= A sophisticated variant of the combat construct made by the Zonai with ancient techniques and magic. Unlike lower-ranked Soldier Constructs, its body sports a striking golden color. Comes equipped with excellent attack power and endurance, not to mention a majestic decoration on its head.|locs= Unknown|loot= Zonai Charge, Large Zonai Charge, Soldier Construct IV Horn}}
The Soldier Construct IV is an upgraded version of the [[Soldier Construct III]]. As well as being the strongest [[Soldier Construct]] of all, it is very strong and will only appear once [[Link]] finishes all of the temples. It can spawn on the surface as well as on the sky islands, but it won't spawn on the Great Sky Island. It is completely gold in color with a horn that resembles a spinning saw blade. If shot in the eye it will take double damage and fall down, giving you a chance to inflict some damage.
