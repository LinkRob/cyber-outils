The {{Term/Store|Magic Gear|Magic Gear|BotW, Series}} is an archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear}} is a set of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are made in areas of great elemental power, such as {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}.<ref name="Flameblade">{{Cite|{{Big|Flameblade}}<br/>This magical sword was forged in the lava of Death Mountain. It leaves white-hot flames in its wake when the blade glows red.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Frostblade">{{Cite|{{Big|Frostblade}}<br/>A magical sword forged in the frigid mountains of the Hebra region. When the blade glows blue, enemies struck by it will become frozen.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Thunderblade">{{Cite|{{Big|Thunderblade}}<br/>A magical sword forged and refined by lightning from the Hyrule Hills. Attack when the blade glows red to expel flames.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Great Frostblade">{{Cite|{{Big|Great Frostblade}}<br/>This magic-infused greatsword was forged by smelting ore found in the Hebra Mountains' permafrost. Attack when the blade glows blue to expel freezing air.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Great Thunderblade">{{Cite|{{Big|Great Thunderblade}}<br/>This magic-infused greatsword was forged by the Hyrulean royal family using lightning from the Hyrule Hills. Attack when the blade glows golden to expel lightning.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Flamespear">{{Cite|{{Big|Flamespear}}<br/>A magical spear forged in the magma of Death Mountain. Attack when the blade glows to expel powerful flames.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Frostspear">{{Cite|{{Big|Frostspear}}<br/>A magical spear forged from ancient ice taken from the Hebra Mountains. Attack when the blade glows blue to chill the air and freeze your foe.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Thunderspear">{{Cite|{{Big|Thunderspear}}<br/>A magical spear that contains thunder from Thundra Plateau in its tip. Attack when the blade glows with a golden light to unleash an electrical attack.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They come in three varieties: flame, frost, and thunder. Flame weaponry was made by ancient {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} smiths using {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}}, and they set {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} when used.<ref name="Flameblade"/><ref name="Great Flameblade"/><ref name="Flamespear"/> Frost weaponry was made using ancient {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} and ore from the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains}} and will freeze {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} when used.<ref name="Frostblade"/><ref name="Great Frostblade"/><ref name="Frostspear"/> Lastly, thunder weaponry was forged by the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} using lightning from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge}} and {{Term|BotW|Thundra Plateau|link}}, and they will electrocute any {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} that they are used against.<ref name="Thunderblade"/><ref name="Great Thunderblade"/><ref name="Thunderspear"/>

Since they is made of metal, pieces of {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear}} can attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Finally, they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

The reason for the setting is unknown in Tears of the Kingdom, but it was deleted due to [[Fuse]] as a factor outside of the setting
