The {{Term/Store|Bait Bag||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a bag in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 116 {{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> It holds [[Hyoi Pear]]s and [[All-Purpose Bait]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Nintendo|page= 24}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Bait Bag is decorated with the face of a pig,<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Nintendo|page= 10}}</ref> and can hold up to eight pieces of animal feed (either individual Hyoi Pears, bunches of up to three All-Purpose Bait, or a mix of the two). It is purchased from [[Beedle]], the traveling merchant that is seen throughout the [[Great Sea]], for 20 [[Rupee]]s. Food purchased from Beedle is automatically put in the bag, giving Link the  choice to equip any piece of food stored within, rather than the bag itself. Restocking the Bait Bag can also be done with the [[Hand-Me-Down Tingle Tuner]].
