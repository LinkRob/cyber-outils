The {{Term/Store|Monster Lady||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Monster Lady}}
The {{Term|TMC|Monster Lady}} stays in {{Term|TMC|Percy's House|link}} while {{Term|TMC|Percy|link}} himself was away for a long journey. When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} enters the poet's {{Term|TMC|Percy's House|display=House}}, the {{Term|TMC|Monster Lady}} asks him to not turn on the lights, since she prefers the darkness.<ref>{{Cite|A dark room settles my soul...I say. Do not turn on the lights...I say.|Monster Lady|TMC}}</ref> Turning on both {{Plural|TMC|Torchlight|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}} reveals the {{Term|TMC|Monster Lady}} is actually a {{Term|TMC|Bow Moblin|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Bow Moblin}} asks {{Term|TMC|Link}} to not tell anyone its true identity and gives him 50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, dear. You found me out! Don't tell anyone!|Bow Moblin|TMC}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The act of using light to reveal the {{Term|Series|Monster Lady}}'s true form as a monster is a reference to {{Term|ALttP|Blind the Thief|link}} from {{ALttP}}.
