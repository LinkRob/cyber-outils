The {{Term/Store|Practical Cannon||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Practical Cannon|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Practical Cannon}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} and the first {{Term|ST|Cannon|link}} acquired for the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}. Before {{Term|ST|Link|link}} travels to the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}, {{Term|ST|Alfonzo|link}} warns the young hero of an increase in monsters in the area and suggests that the duo stop by their home town of {{Term|ST|Aboda Village|link}}. There, {{Term|ST|Alfonzo}} attaches the {{Term|ST|Practical Cannon}} to the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}. This allows {{Term|ST|Link}} to fire {{Plural|ST|Cannonball|link}} while engineering the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|display=Train}}.

The description of the {{Term|ST|Practical Cannon}} reads "You can be sure that any shot fired from this cannon will hit home."
