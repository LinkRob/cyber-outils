The {{Term/Store|Well||Series, LA, LADX}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 21}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|LADX|Well}} is located in the western part of {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}}, north of the {{Term|LADX|Village Library|link}}. {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} can only access it by clearing out the {{Plural|LADX|Grass|link}} atop the cliff in vicinity, then jumping down. A {{Term|LADX|Piece of Heart|link}} can be found in the puddle of {{Term|LADX|Water (Terrain)|link}} near to a staircase leading out.

If {{Term|LADX|Link}} jumps down the {{Term|LADX|Well}} while {{Term|LADX|Marin|link}} is following him, she will fall on him. In {{LA|DX}}, if {{Term|LADX|Link}} has met the {{Term|LADX|Photographer|link}}, the latter will appear and take a {{Term|LADX|Photo}} of that moment for {{Term|LADX|The Travels of Link|link}} which he will call "{{Term|LADX|Heads Up!|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|Hi! I'm the photographer! What a great photo moment! I'll call this 'Heads Up!'|Photographer|LADX}}</ref>
