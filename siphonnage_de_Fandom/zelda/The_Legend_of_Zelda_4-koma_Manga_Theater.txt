''{{Name|Japanese|The Legend of Zelda 4-koma Manga Theater}}'' is a [[4-koma]] series published by {{Wp|Enix}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The series consists of eight anthology volumes of comedic {{TLoZ|-}} 4-koma by various authors. The first three volumes cover {{ALttP}}, volumes four and five focus on {{LA}}, and volumes six through eight cover both.
