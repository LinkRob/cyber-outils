{{Term/Store|Blue Darknut|Blue Darknuts|Series, TLoZ, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 173 {{Exp Game|TLoZ, OoS, OoA}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Blue Darknut}} were referred to as '''Blue Knights''' in {{Guide|OoA|BradyGames}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |publisher= BradyGames |page= 50 |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Darknut}} are stronger than their {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Darknut|link|display= Red counterparts}}. They are able to shield themselves from any attack and are thus invulnerable if attacked from the front.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= The knight who lives in the labyrinths. He has lots of attacking power. He repels Link's attacks from the front with his shield.|game= TLoZ|page= 36}}</ref> {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} can harm them by attacking them from the side or behind with either his {{Term|TLoZ|Sword|link}} or a {{Term|TLoZ|Bomb|link}}. {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Darknut}} cannot be stunned by the {{Term|TLoZ|Boomerang|link}} and they cannot be harmed by the {{Term|TLoZ|Magical Rod|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Arrow|link}}, or {{Term|TLoZ|Fire|link}}. They have a health of eight. When defeated, {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Darknut}} may drop either a {{Term|TLoZ|Heart|link}}, one {{Term|TLoZ|Rupee|link}}, or a {{Term|TLoZ|Fairy|link}}.{{Note|If {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} defeats 10 enemies without getting hit or teleporting with the {{Term|TLoZ|Recorder|link}}, then the 10th enemy defeated will drop five {{Plural|TLoZ|Rupee|link}}. However, if the 10th enemy is defeated with a {{Term|TLoZ|Bomb|link}}, then it will drop four {{Plural|TLoZ|Bomb}}. If {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} defeats 16 enemies without getting hit or teleporting, then the 16th enemy will drop a {{Term|TLoZ|Fairy|link}}. After the 16th enemy, {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} must get hit and reset the counter to zero in order to achieve this effect again. Defeating {{Plural|TLoZ|Armos|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Like Like|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Keese|link}}, or {{Plural|TLoZ|Gel|link}} will not be counted.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= n.a. |published= n.d. |retrieved= August 17, 2017 |url= http://redcandle.us/The_Legend_of_Zelda#Forced_drops |title= Forced Drops |site= redcandle.us}}</ref>}}
