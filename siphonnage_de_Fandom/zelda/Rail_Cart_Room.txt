==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|OoS}}
The {{Term|OoS|Rail Cart Room}} is a room on the first floor of the {{Term|OoS|Dancing Dragon Dungeon|link}}. It contains a {{Term|OoS|Zol|link}} and a {{Term|OoS|Mine Cart|link}} on a track leading out of the {{Term|OoS|Rail Cart Room|display=Room}}, as well as {{Plural|OoS|Stair|link}} leading to the second floor.
