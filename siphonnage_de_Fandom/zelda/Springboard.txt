{{Term/Store|Springboard|Springboards|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW |publisher= Prima |page= 12}}</ref> {{Plural|Series|Springboard}} go unnamed in {{PH}}. Though the means of activating them in each game is different, their basic function is to launch [[Link]] into the air and help him reach higher areas.

==Appearances==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Springboards appear in the [[Wind Temple]] and [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]]. Link can use them by climbing atop one while wearing the [[Iron Boots]] to press it down; taking the boots off will send him flying into the air. In addition to reaching ledges and areas above him, Springboards can also give Link a boost in altitude for use with the [[Deku Leaf]].

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, Springboards are found primarily in [[Mutoh's Temple]], as well as the last few floors of the [[Temple of the Ocean King]]. Link can activate them by using the [[Hammer]]. Springboards can also be used to move [[boulder]]s between different elevations, which is required in some areas. They serve as vital during the battle against [[Eox]], the boss of Mutoh's Temple, allowing Link to reach and attack the various pressure points situated on its body.
