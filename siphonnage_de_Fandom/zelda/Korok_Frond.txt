'''Korok Fronds''' are [[Material]]s in {{TotK}}.

== Location and uses ==
Korok Fronds can be attached to Link's equipment via the [[Fuse]] ability. A weapon fused with a Korok Frond will create a gust of wind every time the weapon is swung. Attaching a Korok Frond to an [[Arrow]] will also create a gust of wind when the [[Arrow]] is fired. Korok Fronds can be obtained randomly by chopping down trees in the [[Sky Islands]] and the regions of [[Hyrule]].
