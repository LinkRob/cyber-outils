The {{Term/Store|Strange Brother|Strange Brothers|Series, OoS|plural}} are characters in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250}}</ref>

==Biography==
They are a couple of mischievous {{Plural|OoS|Subrosian|link}} who trick {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} into trading {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather|link}} for {{Term|OoS|Fool's Ore|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's that? It's so weird. I'll trade you this ore for it! Thanks!|Strange Brother|OoS}}</ref> {{Term|OoS|Link}} has to follow them through the {{Term|OoS|Treasure Grove|link}} where they bury his {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather}} to get it back, but they will hide if they see {{Term|OoS|Link}} following them. {{Term|OoS|Link}} can return and follow the {{Plural|OoS|Strange Brother}} to their secret spot again to dig up more of their buried treasure. This treasure includes {{Term|OoS|Ore Chunks|link}}, {{Plural|OoS|Magic Ring|link}}, or {{Plural|OoS|Gasha Seed|link}}. The {{Term|OoS|Pot|link}} in the upper right side of their house contains up to 100 {{Plural|OoS|Ore Chunk}}

==Trivia==
*When the Strange Brothers realize they are being followed, they will run over trees, lava, and their house to get away.
