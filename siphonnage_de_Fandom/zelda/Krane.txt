{{Term/Store|Krane||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Krane|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Krane}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} who stands watch at the western entrance to {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. He wears a {{Term|BotW|Forehead Protector|link}} and carries a {{Term|BotW|Cobble Crusher|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Krane}} first meets with {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he greets him as "Master Link."<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Well, lookee here... Welcome, Master Link!}}|Krane|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks how he knew who he was, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} appears confused, before he expresses his hope that {{Term|BotW|Link}} made it past the {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb|link}} without injury.<ref>{{Cite|[How did you know?] What do ya mean? More importantly, I hope ya made it past all those {{Color|BotW Blue|magma bombs}} without injury!|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He admits that not many tourists have visited {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} since the {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb}} started falling, so he has been addressing guests as the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} to pass the time.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, we haven't had too many visitors here since those {{Color|BotW Blue|magma bombs}} started fallin' from the sky. I'd be bored to tears if I didn't pass the time pretendin' to greet the long lost Hylian {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}, heh.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He explains that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was the name of the {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} who fought alongside the {{Term|BotW|Goron}} {{Term|BotW|Champions}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, in case ya didn't know, Link is the little {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} who fought alongside the Goron {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}, Daruk.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Krane}} directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}} if he wants to know more.<ref>{{Cite|If ya wanna hear more, talk to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}}. He lives further in town.|Krane|botW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Krane}} then asks what has brought {{Term|BotW|Link}} to {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there! What brings ya all the way out here?|Krane|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him again and answers that he is visiting for a different reason, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} will notice the change and become confused.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|I thought you said you were here for some "divine bees" earlier? Maybe I'm crazy...}}}}|Krane|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Huh? Didn't you say somethin' about being here for pleasure before?}}}}|Krane|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Huh? Didn't you say somethin' about being here for business earlier?}}}}|Krane|BotW}}</ref>

When told that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is there for a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} admits that he does not know much about that.<ref>{{Cite|[A Divine Beast.] Huh. Sorry, I don't really know anything about all that business.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He believes that {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} would know something about it.<ref>{{Cite|If you're looking for info, I bet the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} knows a thing or two about it.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> The next time {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells him that he is there for a {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, will mishear him as saying he is there for a "diving feast," which he does not know about.<ref>{{Cite|[A Divine Beast.] I don't know anything about a "{{Color|BotW Blue|diving feast}}" or whatever...|Krane|BotW}}</ref> However, he adds that {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} has been driving away a lizard monster with his {{Plural|BotW|Cannon|link}}, referring to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I do know {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} drives a lizard monster away with a {{Color|BotW Red|cannon}}, so you should ask him if you wanna know more about that.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Bludo}} lives in the deepest part of town.<ref>{{Cite|By the way, {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} is the old guy that lives in the deepest part of town.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives this answer to {{Term|BotW|Krane}} after calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} complains that it so much chaos.<ref>{{Cite|The "divine beat"? Oh! You mean that lizard monster, don't you? That thing caused such a ruckus for us!|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He is grateful that {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}} was able to bring peace back to {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to {{Color|BotW Blue|Yunobo}}, peace has finally been restored!|Krane|BotW}}</ref>

Next, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that he is there for pleasure, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} says that he reasons do not matter and welcomes him to {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, whatever brought ya here doesn't really matter. Welcome, brother!|Krane|BotW}}</ref> Due to the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Goron City|display=City}}, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} has not seen many {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}} as of late.<ref>{{Cite|Because of this extreme heat, I actually haven't seen any {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylians}} around lately...|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He considers it their loss, though, and he encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to enjoy his visit.<ref>{{Cite|Welp, their loss! Enjoy your stay, little guy!|Krane|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tells {{Term|BotW|Krane}} a second time that he is there for pleasure, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} wonders whether he is equipped for the heat.<ref>{{Cite|[Pleasure!] Well, just visiting is well and good, but... can you really bear this heat?|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that there are goods in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} that could help him adjust, such as {{Term|BotW|Fireproof Armor|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Ripped and Shredded|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Fireproof Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There are some goods that might suit a {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}} tourist like you. If that doesn't do it for ya, I also saw someone sellin' {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof elixir}}... You should go check it out.|Krane|BotW}}</ref>

Finally, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can tell {{Term|BotW|Krane}} that he is in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} for business, which causes {{Term|BotW|Krane}} to recall that it used to be easy to find {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} in town, even though the {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} find them disgusting.<ref>{{Cite|[Business.] Business, eh? In the old days, you could easily find {{Color|BotW Blue|jewels}} in Goron City. To us, they're just gross snacks that are hard to eat. Yuck!|Krane|BotW}}</ref> Though it sounds outlandish to him, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} shares that there are people who buy them for high prices.<ref>{{Cite|Sounds nuts, but lots of people actually buy those abominations at a high price. Crazy, I know. But it's true!|Krane|BotW}}</ref> In fact, he claims that it is the main source of income for {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't get it, but that's why the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} and Goron City make lots of money sellin' that weird food to crazy folks.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} again tells {{Term|BotW|Krane}} that he is there for business, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} shares that he heard there are many valuable {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} on {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Business.] I hear there are some valuable jewels on {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}.|Krane|BotW}}</ref> Since no one goes there anymore, {{Term|BotW|Krane}} believes that anyone who goes there to dig up {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} would not face much competition.<ref>{{Cite|No one goes near there these days, so you could probably go dig it all up without much competition.|Krane|BotW}}</ref>

He paces back and forth, talking about {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} to himself.<ref>{{Cite|Lord Daruk...|Krane|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} how he is dealing with the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate}}, warning him that it will not cool down when the sun sets.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there. I'm followin' the Great {{Color|BotW Blue|Daruk}}'s footsteps too!|Krane|BotW}}</ref> He has heard that {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} fought a huge {{Term|BotW|Monster|link}} at this location, and he wonders what it looked like.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, I hear {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk}} defeated a {{Color|BotW Red|huge monster}} at {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Darman}}.
