{{Term/Store|Sub-Weapon|Sub-Weapons|Series, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are {{Plural|HWDE|Item|link}} in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 84}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|HWDE|Sub-Weapon}} are a group of {{Plural|HWDE|Item}} that the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} obtain over different {{Plural|HWDE|Scenario|link}}. {{Plural|HWDE|Sub-Weapon}} include {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Bow|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Boomerang|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Hookshot|link}}, and the {{Term|HWDE|Hammer|link}}. The {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb|link}} are obtained during "{{Term|HWDE|The Armies of Ruin|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} after rescuing a {{Term|HWDE|Goron Captain|link}} from a {{Term|HWDE|Lizalfos|link}}, to which the {{Term|HWDE|Mine Entrance|link}} will open.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|HWDE Green|Mine Entrance}} is open!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> At the bottom of the {{Term|HWDE|Mine|link}}, a {{Term|HWDE|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Plural|HWDE|Bomb}} can be found.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks a ton! Hey, you should take the treasure in that mine as a reward!|Goron Captain|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Bow|link}} is obtained during the "{{Term|HWDE|The Sorceress of the Woods|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}} at any time in the bottom left corner of the {{Term|HWDE|Faron Woods (Hyrule Warriors)|link}}. The {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} are required to use the {{Term|HWDE|Bow}} to defeat the {{Plural|HWDE|Deku Baba|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Use the {{Color|HWDE Green|bow and arrows}} to defeat the {{Color|HWDE Red|Deku Babas}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Boomerang|link}} is obtained during "{{Term|HWDE|The Sorceress of the Valley|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}} when the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} need to cut down some {{Plural|HWDE|Wall of Vine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a Wall of Vines blocking our path! Isn't there some way to get rid of it...?|Lana|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Boomerang}} can be found in a {{Term|HWDE|Treasure Chest}} that appears after capturing the "{{Term|HWDE|Western Room|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Keep|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 97}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Hookshot|link}} is obtained during "{{Term|HWDE|The Shadow King|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}} when {{Term|HWDE|Zant|link}} destroys the bridges in the {{Term|HWDE|Palace of Twilight|link}}, trapping the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior}} inside. The {{Term|HWDE|Hookshot}} appears in a {{Term|HWDE|Treasure Chest}} after capturing the "{{Term|HWDE|Upper Level East|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Keep}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 101}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Hammer|link}} is obtained during the "{{Term|HWDE|A New Disturbance|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario}} on a small ledge south of the "{{Term|HWDE|Bomb Shop|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Keep}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= HWL |publisher= Prima |page= 143}}</ref>
