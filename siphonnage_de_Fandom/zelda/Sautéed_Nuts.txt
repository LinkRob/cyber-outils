{{Term/Store|Sautéed Nuts|Sautéed Nuts|Series, BotW|plural}} are [[Item]]s in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Sautéed Nuts}}<br/>These sautéed tree seeds are the perfect snack for the busy adventurer on the go!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[Link]] can make Sautéed Nuts by [[Cooking]] at least one [[Acorn]] or [[Chickaloo Tree Nut]] in a [[Cooking Pot]]. Eating these meals will restore some of Link's [[Heart]]s to his [[Life Gauge]]. They're considered to be the perfect snack for busy adventurers.<ref name="Inventory"/>

No variations of Sautéed Nutss can be made.
