The {{Term/Store|Settings||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} menu is an interface in {{HW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Settings}}|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|HWDE|Settings}} menu allows the player to adjust the settings of {{HW|-}} to their preferences.<ref name="Info"/> The {{Term|HWDE|Settings}} menu can be accessed from the {{Term|HWDE|Select Mode|link}} menu. The menu consists of three submenus, the {{Term|HWDE|Game Settings|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Controller Settings|link}}, and the {{Term|HWDE|Volume Settings|link}}. The {{Term|HWDE|Game Settings}} allow the player to change various settings about the gameplay,<ref>{{Cite|Change various game settings.|Settings|HWDE}}</ref> the {{Term|HWDE|Controller Settings}} allow the player to change the settings regarding their controller,<ref>{{Cite|Change rumble settings and control options.|Settings|HWDE}}</ref> and the {{Term|HWDE|Volume Settings}} allow the player to change the settings of the sound.<ref>{{Cite|Set volume levels for BGM and sound effects.|Settings|HWDE}}</ref> The final button will save the players changes and return to the {{Term|HWDE|Select Mode}} menu.<ref>{{Cite|Confirm Settings|Settings|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Confirm changes and return to previous screen.|Settings|HWDE}}</ref>
