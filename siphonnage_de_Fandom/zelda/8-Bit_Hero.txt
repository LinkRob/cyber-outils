'''8-Bit Hero''' is one of the microgames in 9-Volt's and 18-Volt's Retro Action stage in ''[[WarioWare|WarioWare: Touched!]]''

==Gameplay==
Both the top and bottom screens each display a nearly identical arrangement of pixels of a zoomed-in 8-bit protagonist sprite from a certain [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] game. In terms of gameplay, the only actual difference between each difficulty level is that later ones slightly extend the width of the grid. The player has to tap the pixel on the Touch Screen that differs from the one on the Touch Screen to win. Only the second level is themed after {{TLoZ}}.

Level 1 shows a 5×3 area (15 pixels), and tapping the odd pixel reveals the sprite to be of [[mariowiki:Small Mario|Small Mario]] from ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]'', who then appears in the iconic starting area of [[mariowiki:World 1-1 (Super Mario Bros.)|World 1-1]].

Level 2 shows a 6×4 area (24 pixels). When the odd pixel is tapped, the sprite is revealed to be of Link from {{TLoZ|-}}, who is shown in the [[Overworld]], in an area with a [[Red Octorok]] and two [[Blue Octorok]]s.

Level 3 shows a 7×5 area (35 pixels). When the odd pixel is tapped, the sprite is revealed to be of Popo from ''Ice Climber'', who is then shown in the first screen of the game.
