The {{Term/Store|Moblin Cave||Series, LA, LADX}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 32}}</ref> also known as the '''Baw Wow Dungeon''',<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= |magazine= Nintendo Power|issue= 50|publisher= |date= July 1993|page= 60}}</ref> is a mini-dungeon in {{LA}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|LADX|Moblin Cave}} is located on the edge of {{Term|LADX|Tal Tal Heights|link}}. It has cracked flooring in places. It is controlled by a gang of {{Plural|LADX|Moblin|link}} led by the {{Term|LADX|King Moblin|link}}. Early in {{Term|LADX|Link|link}}'s quest to wake the {{Term|LADX|Wind Fish|link}}, the {{Term|LADX|King Moblin}} and his henchmen kidnap {{Term|LADX|BowWow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|AIEEEEEEEE! It's terrrrible! My BowWow was dognapped by... Mo-mo-MOBLINS!! OHHH! AHHHH! Please! Somebody help my poor BowWow!! |Madam MeowMeow |LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link}} travels to the {{Term|LADX|Moblin Cave}} and defeat the {{Plural|LADX|Moblin}}. He then battles the {{Term|LADX|King Moblin}}, who recognizes that he is there to save {{Term|LADX|BowWow}}.<ref>{{Cite|You must be an assassin sent by Madam MeowMeow to rescue the mutt! You came here to get me, but it is I who will get you!! |King Moblin|LADX}}</ref> {{Term|LADX|Link}} defeats the {{Term|LADX|King Moblin}}, saving {{Term|LADX|BowWow}}.<ref>{{Cite|You've saved BowWow! What a fearsome beast! |N/A|LADX}}</ref> This is necessary for him to enter the second {{Term|LADX|Dungeon|link}}, {{Term|LADX|Bottle Grotto|link}}, as the {{Plural|LADX|Goponga Flower|link}} blocking the entrance can be eaten by {{Term|LADX|BowWow}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} arrives to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Moblin Cave}} after hearing from {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Madam MeowMeow|link}} that {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|BowWow|link}} was kidnapped by {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Moblin|link}}. After defeating a group of {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Moblin}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} reaches the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|King Moblin|link}}, who attacks him with his {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Bow|link}}. After evading several {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Arrow|link}} and tackles from the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|King Moblin}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} proceeds to defeat him with a {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Sword|link}} slash. After his victory, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} enters the chamber where {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|BowWow}} was held and saves her.
{{Noncanon|End}}
