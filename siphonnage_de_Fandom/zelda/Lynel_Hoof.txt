==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Lynel Hooves are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that can be found by defeating all types of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}. They're heavier than horse hooves and can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] with critters to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] also accepts Lynel Hooves as an ingredient for Black [[Dye]], but they can also be exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]].

The final riddle [[Walton]] tells during the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[Riddles of Hyrule]]" asks {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to bring him an item that starts with "H" and ends with "oof". <ref>{{Cite|If stepped on by this, you will go poof! {{Color|BotW Red|It starts with an H and ends with an oof}}!|Walton|BotW}}</ref> If Link places a Lynel Hoof in front of him, Walton will cheer that he figured it out.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Lynel hoof!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for deciphering all the riddles, Walton will give Link a [[Diamond]].<ref>{{Cite|I knew you could do it, Mr. Hero! Here is your reward!|Walton|BotW}}</ref>

Two pieces of [[Armor]] require Lynel Hooves in order to be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. The [[Barbarian Set]] needs them for its second and third tiers, while the [[Soldier's Set]] needs them for its final tier.

* Total for '''Barbarian Set''': 18 (0, 2, 4, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Soldier's Set''': 6 (0, 0, 0, 2) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
