The {{Term/Store|Gibdo Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 125 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> It resembles the face of a [[Gibdo]].

==Location and Uses==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Gibdo Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is a fine mask. It is filled with the love of a father and child.</p>}}

The Gibdo Mask is given to [[Link]] by [[Pamela's Father]] after he lifts the curse cast upon [[Ikana River]] in [[Ikana Canyon]]. Upon entering the [[Music Box House]], the hero finds Pamela's Father in a state of half-mummification.<ref>{{Cite|This mummy looks different... Sorta like it's here waiting for its human heart to be healed.|Tatl|MM}}</ref> By playing the "[[Song of Healing]]," her father will be healed, and the mummified portion of him encased in a mask.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Gibdo Mask! This mask was once the hideous face of Pamela's father.|N/A|MM}}</ref>

When worn, the Gibdo Mask causes [[Gibdo]]s and [[ReDead]]s to recognize Link as one of their own, and so they will not attack him. Link can also talk to Gibdos while wearing the mask, which is necessary to explore [[Beneath the Well]], which is, at first, the only way to enter the [[Ancient Castle of Ikana]]. ReDeads outside of the well cannot be talked to, but the mask will cause them to dance,<ref>{{Cite|It seems the ReDeads that appear in Ikana Castle start dancing when the Captain's Hat, Gibdo Mask or Garo's Mask are worn. But that does not change things much...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> rendering them inoffensive.

In {{MM3D}}, the Gibdo Mask can also be used to catch an Ancient Fish in the [[Swamp Fishing Hole]].<ref>{{Cite|Ancient Fish. Lives in the swamp. Often referred to as a living fossil. Said to feel a certain kinship with mummies.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Gibdo Mask is one of six masks that are necessary to complete the game, the others being the three transformation masks, the [[Garo's Mask]], and the [[Captain's Hat]].
*If Link wears the Gibdo Mask instead of Garo's Mask, the [[Poe Collector]] will still grant him access to Ikana.<ref>{{Cite|That is the mask of those who roam this world with the most lingering regrets! Use it to ask them their regrets... Perhaps you may be able to save the souls that wander beyond here. Yee-hee-hee!|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref> However, Link must have already entered the cursed land at least once prior to this in order to have obtained the mask.
* In the original Japanese version,{{clarify|MM}}<!--Does this mean only N64 v1.0, or is it saying "original" in contrast to the localized versions--> Redeads that were Gibdos burned by the Fire Arrows will still attack Link when wearing the Gibdo Mask.{{verify|MM}}
