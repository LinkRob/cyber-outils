{{Term/Store|Master Kohga's Training||HWAoC}} is a level 47 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Master Kohga's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Term|HWAoC|Mighty Bananas|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within eight minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, and more spawn as {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} defeats them. At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, only one {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}} is present, but another spawns alongside more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 50 KOs. This happens again at 150 KOs. Once 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} spawns and the time limit is taken away. All four of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} must be defeated for the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} to end.
