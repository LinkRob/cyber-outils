{{Term/Store|Quest History||Series, ALttP}} is an interface in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite|QUEST HISTORY|Credits|ALttP}}</ref>

==Overview==
The Quest History is the last part of the credits in A Link to the Past.  When it comes up, it shows the number of times [[Link]] died (and/or in the SNES version only, the number of times the player has saved the game) in each of the major dungeons.
