==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Armored Carp}}
{{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} found in bodies of freshwater throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. Large deposits of calcium within their scales cause them to be as hard as armor.<ref name="Inventory"/> Because of this, {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} them into a dish will grant the consumer a boosted defense.<ref name="Inventory"/> Once caught, they can be eaten raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} worth of health, or they can be cooked into {{Term|BotW|Roasted Carp|link}} or frozen into {{Term|BotW|Frozen Carp|link}}.
