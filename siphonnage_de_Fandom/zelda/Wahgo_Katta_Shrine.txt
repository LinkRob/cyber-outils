The {{Term/Store|Wahgo Katta Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Wahgo Katta Shrine}}<br/>Metal Connections}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Metal Connections''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Wahgo Katta Shrine}} is located northwest of {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}, along the western bank of {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Wahgo Katta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Wahgo Katta. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Wahgo Katta|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Wahgo Katta Shrine}} consists of a large, single chamber with a stack of three metallic blocks next to a platform. There is also a metal slab on top of another platform. The {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} is located on a ledge high above the main floor of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.

To navigate {{Term|BotW|Wahgo Katta Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to reposition the blocks so they are stacked beneath the platform with the slab. From here, he can {{Term|BotW|Climbing|link|display=Climb}} up to the metal slab. With the metal slab available to him, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can reposition the metal blocks again so they are beneath the platform containing the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. He can then place on the edge of the platform, causing one of its ends to tilt down and rest on tops of the blocks. {{Term|BotW|Link}} will now be able to {{Term|BotW|Climbing|display=Climb}} up to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

Upon approaching the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Wahgo Katta}} will present {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Wahgo Katta|BotW}}</ref> Following this, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Wahgo Katta|BotW}}</ref>

A stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} is located on the top of the platform that the climbable metal blocks rest against. To access it, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must {{Term|BotW|Climbing|display=Climb}} up the blocks. When the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} is opened, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}.
