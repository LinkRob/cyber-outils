{{Term/Store|Black Sarcophagus|Black Sarcophagi|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= Black Sarcophagus |game= CoH |system= Nintendo Switch |version= 1.0.0 |country= United States of America}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Black Sarcophagus}} are the strongest variety of {{Plural|CoH|Sarcophagus|link}} that can summon undead enemies in order to attack {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. They can summon {{Term|CoH|Black Stalfos|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Black Stalfos Rider|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Black Gibdo|link}}. They only appear in caves and dungeons. 

==Trivia==
They share their name, summoning ability, and color scheme with the Black Sarcophagus enemies from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
