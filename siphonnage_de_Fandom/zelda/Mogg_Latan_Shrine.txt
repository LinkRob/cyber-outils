The {{Term/Store|Mogg Latan Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Mogg Latan Shrine}}<br/>Synced Swing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Synced Swing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Mogg Latan Shrine}} is located near the peak of {{Term|BotW|Satori Mountain|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mogg Latan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Mogg Latan. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Mogg Latan|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Mogg Latan Shrine}} features several isolated platforms that are suspended over a bottomless gap. At the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} is a metallic {{Term|Series|Swinging Platform|link}}, which is constantly moving back and forth. This {{Term|Series|Swinging Platform|display=Platform}} connects the platform that the {{Term|BotW|Elevator Platform|link}} drops {{Term|BotW|Link}} on and the second platform nearby. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} rides the {{Term|Series|Swinging Platform}}, he will be able to jump onto the second platform.

Another {{Term|Series|Swinging Platform}} is located between the second platform and the third platform. However, this {{Term|Series|Swinging Platform|display=Platform}} does not move. Using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can lift the {{Term|Series|Swinging Platform|display=Platform}} towards himself, giving it the inertia to move back and forth. He can then ride it to the next structure.

The third and fourth platforms can be connected by a stone bridge. However, the end of this bridge that connects to the third platform is suspended in the air by ropes. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot the ropes with {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, which will cause the bridge to fall. He can then cross onto the fourth platform.

To the left side of the fourth platform are several {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder|link}}, which are suspended in the air by chains and block the path forward. With {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can move the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}}, but he must avoid touching them. {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} can be used to freeze one of the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}} in time.

Past the {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a slanted rail with a metallic platform connected to it. Using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can drag the platform towards himself, at which point he can ride it as it falls back into its original place. At the end of the rail are several more metallic platforms, all of which are connected to horizontal rails. By configuring them into a staircase with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to climb up onto the final platform.

At the top of the staircase is a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}} and a pair of metal doors, the latter of which is blocked by a wooden slab and covered in leaves. When the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} is hit, two lanterns will fall from the ceiling nearby. By moving one of the lanterns with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}}, freezing it with {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} while it is moving towards the leaves, and then breaking the rope, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can light the leaves on {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Fire}} will spread to the wooden slab, freeing the metal doors. Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can shoot the leaves with a {{Term|BotW|Fire Arrow|link}}, attack them with a {{Term|BotW|Fire}}-based {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, or create a spark by striking a piece of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} nearby, all of which will have the same result.

Using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} to push the metal doors open will reveal the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Mogg Latan}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will commend him for completing the Trial and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Mogg Latan|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Mogg Latan}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Mogg Latan|BotW}}</ref>

On the right side of the fourth platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. When it is opened, he will find a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Spear|link}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} can be found behind the last {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boulder}}, which is not necessary to pass. It contains a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow|link}}. The final {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is located behind the metallic platforms that can be made into a staircase. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} opens it, he will receive a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.
