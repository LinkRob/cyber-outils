==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSHD|Air Potion +}} are an upgraded version of the regular {{Plural|SSHD|Air Potion|link}}. They can be infused from a regular {{Term|SSHD|Air Potion}} for a fee of 20 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Infusion Shop|link}} and the following {{Plural|SSHD|Bug|link}}: two {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Stag Beetle|link}}, two {{Plural|SSHD|Skyloft Mantis|link}}, and two {{Plural|SSHD|Lanayru Ant|link}}.

When consumed, an {{Term|SSHD|Air Potion +}} completely refills its user's {{Term|SSHD|Oxygen Gauge|link}}, and prevents it from depleting for three minutes.<ref name="SSHD Obtention"/>
