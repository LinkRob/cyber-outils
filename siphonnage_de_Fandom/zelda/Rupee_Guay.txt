{{Term/Store|Rupee Guay|Rupee Guays|Series, SS|plural}} are [[Enemy|Enemies]] in {{SS}}.<ref name="E 201">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 201 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
[[Rupee Guay]]s fly around the [[Sky]] carrying [[Rupee]]s of different values. [[Link]] can snatch the Rupees from the Rupee Guays by crashing into them while flying on the [[Crimson Loftwing]], but only if Link reaches them in the set amount of time.<ref name="E 201"/> If the time expires, the Rupee Guay will fly away from Link. Rupee Guays are bigger than normal Guay, but can damage Link on contact.
