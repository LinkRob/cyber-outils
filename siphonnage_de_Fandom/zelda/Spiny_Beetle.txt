==Characteristics==
Spiny Beetles are usually found sheltering under stones or bushes. In early appearances, they are harmless creatures, though in later appearances they will actively attack [[Link]]. While Link can destroy them with his [[Sword]], if he removes the object that they are hiding under, they will scurry around wildly, bumping into Link and either knocking him back or causing damage.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Spiny Beetles in {{ALttP}} are harmless creatures that will move away from [[Link]] when he gets near, revealing their location. While Link can destroy them with his [[Sword]], if he removes the bush or stone that they are hiding under by lifting it, they will scurry around wildly, dropping green [[Rupee]] and bumping into Link. If they come into contact with him, they will not cause damage, but will knock him back. If Link picks up a Rupee they drop, they will drop another. They will continue to dash around for a short while before disappearing, leaving behind a single Rupee. If Link obtains a certain number of [[Green Rupee]]s, they will drop a [[Red Rupee]].

==={{LA|-}}===
Spiny Beetles in {{LA}} hide beneath bushes and rocks, attacking Link when he approaches. They do not continually drop [[Rupee]]s as in {{ALttP}}, though they are likely to have [[Fairy]]s hidden in the object they are underneath, and may drop them when defeated.

==={{OoS|-}} / {{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, Spiny Beetles behave and appear identically as in {{LA}}.

==={{FS|-}}===
The Spiny Beetles that hide under [[Grass]] appear as enemies in {{FS}}. A Link can instantly defeat a Spiny Beetle by slashing it with his [[Sword]] once.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Spiny Beetle}}
Spiny Beetles in {{TMC}} hide beneath [[Grass]] and [[Rock]]s, charging at [[Link]] when he approaches. They are found in the [[Minish Woods]], in the [[Trilby Highlands]], and on [[Mount Crenel]], as well as within [[Cave]]s across [[Hyrule]]. If they hide beneath a Rock, [[Link]] cannot use his [[Sword]] to harm them until he has learned the [[Rock Breaker]] technique.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Hoarder|Naturally camouflaged, this creature hides underground until an unwary foe approaches the mushrooms on its back.<ref name="EoW Hoarder">{{Cite|Naturally camouflaged, this creature hides underground until an unwary foe approaches the mushrooms on its back.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Hoarders are [[insect]]-like creatures that camouflage themselves with a glowing mushroom on their backs, illuminating dark areas. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Hoarder [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.

==Trivia==
*Despite their name, they do not appear to have any spines or spikes on their bodies at all. The name may stem from the spike-like appearance of a bush on top of these creatures in {{LA}}, or potentially from their spike-like feet.
*The [[Nintendo Power]] Player's Guide for {{LA|-}} confuses these enemies with [[Beetle (Enemy)]]s and [[Spiked Beetle]]s, stating that they can only be defeated by flipping them over with a [[Shield]]. This information is inaccurate, and may also be the cause for their seemingly inaccurate name, as Spiked Beetles are based on {{smw|Spiny|Spinies}} from the {{smw|Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series}}.
