The {{Term/Store|Troupe Leader's Mask||Series, MM3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|HWDE}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142 {{Exp Game|MM3D}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Troupe Leader's Mask}} was referred to as the {{Term/Store|Circus Leader's Mask||MM}} in all versions of {{MM}} prior to {{MM3D}}. However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Circus Leader's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>That is a fine mask. It is filled with the feelings of tenderness left behind in the back of one's heart.</p>}}

The Troupe Leader's Mask is given to [[Link]] by [[Gorman]] inside the [[Milk Bar#Majora's Mask|Milk Bar]]. Once Link enters by using [[Romani's Mask]], he meets [[Toto]], manager of the [[Zora]] band [[The Indigo-Go's]]. If Link aids him by playing the "[[Ballad of the Wind Fish#Majora's Mask|Ballad of the Wind Fish]]" using the musical instruments of each of his four forms,<ref>{{Cite|We were scheduled to do a show, but it got canceled. This place has a reputation for being a great live venue, so it's a shame. I'd like to do a sound check, though. I just want to see what it would have been like... Hey, you! Could you help me with a performance?|Toto|MM}}</ref> [[Gorman]] will be moved by the tune.<ref>{{Cite|That melody...It brings back so many memories! [...] I entered show business because I heard this song at the Carnival of Time long ago... By doing performances, I thought maybe I'd get to meet the singers of that song...|Gorman|MM}}</ref> He then gives Link the mask to show his gratification.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry I booed you... Please, take my mask.|Gorman|MM}}</ref>

When worn, the mask cries and has a calming effect on people related to Gorman. When Link wears it in the presence of the [[Gorman Brothers]], the mask affects them emotionally, sapping all desire to be rude out of them.<ref>{{Cite|Grrrr... We're the notorious Gorman Brothers! We can't get all sappy over stuff like this! I'm sorry, but take that mask off. I can't get anything done when you gaze at me with that mask on.|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...It's no use. When you look at me with that thing on...I can't talk to you like that...|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> During the segment in which Link must defend [[Cremia]]'s [[Milk]] shipment to get Romani's Mask, wearing the Troupe Leader's Mask prevents the bandits from attacking. However, Romani's Mask is required to get the Troupe Leader's Mask in the first place.

In {{MM3D}}, from 6 am to 12 pm on the [[Second Day]], if [[Link]] wears the Troupe Leader's Mask and talks to [[Gorman]] while he is sleeping in the [[Stock Pot Inn]]'s bunk room, Gorman requests Link bring him [[Mystery Milk]] from [[Gorman Brothers|his brothers]] at their ranch on [[Milk Road]];<ref>{{Cite|Hey, sonny... Bring me some sweet nectar, will ya? You know the stuff... that special milk my bros make that instantly heals an upset stomach...|Gorman|MM3D}}</ref> however, he will not request any milk if Link cheered him up by playing the "[[Ballad of the Wind Fish]]" in the [[Milk Bar]] the night [[First Day|before]]. After receiving the request from Gorman, if Link talks to the Gorman Brothers from 12 pm to 6 pm while wearing the Troupe Leader's Mask, they will give him the Mystery Milk. After 2 minutes (actual time), the Mystery Milk spoils and turns into Moldy Milk.<ref>{{Cite|Mystery Milk has turned into Moldy Milk...|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yuck! Something seriously stinks! It must be that Mystery Milk you're carrying! Has it already gone bad? Gross. Hate to say it, but nobody in their right mind would accept that stuff anymore.|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> If Link successfully delivers it to Gorman from 12 pm to 6 pm,<ref>{{Cite|Bring it once it's past noon. Got it?|Gorman|MM3D}}</ref> Gorman will let Link keep the empty [[Bottle]].<ref>{{Cite|Now be a champ and toss this old bottle for me. Or don't. For all I care you can keep it to store bugs and junk in. Whatever suits you.|Gorman|MM3D}}</ref> After 6 pm, he will not acknowledge that Link has the milk. While Link has the Mystery Milk or Moldy Milk, [[Tatl]] won't let him soar with the "[[Song of Soaring]]" or transform into a [[Goron Link|Goron]].<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hold on a minute! You're not planning on flying with that Mystery Milk, are you? If you fly while carrying that, you'll spill it all over the place and make a huge mess! Nobody wants that! I think you should walk instead. Carefully!|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, hold on a minute! You're not planning on rolling with that Mystery Milk, are you? If you roll while carrying that, you'll spill it all over the place and make a huge mess! Nobody wants that! I think you should walk instead. Carefully!|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> If Link plays the "[[Song of Time]]" while he has the bottle of Mystery Milk or Moldy Milk, the item will be lost.<ref>{{Cite|... That's it. I am absolutely sick of us having to carry that milk around! Use that one song of yours to rewind time and make it as though we never had it to begin with! Please, I beg you!|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref>

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, the Troupe Leader's Mask appears as a costume for [[Zant]] in the {{MM|-}} DLC pack.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{MM|-}}, this is one of the few masks that can be obtained more than once, along with the [[Couple's Mask]]. In {{MM3D|-}}, Gorman instead gives Link a [[Fishing Hole Pass]] if he plays the Ballad of the Wind Fish after obtaining the Troupe Leader's Mask.
*The Troupe Leader's Mask is one of the only four dynamic masks in the game (along with the [[Great Fairy Mask]], the [[Blast Mask]], and the [[Bunny Hood]]).
