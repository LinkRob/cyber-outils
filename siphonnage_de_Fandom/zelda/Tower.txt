The '''Tower''' is a location featured in {{TSP}}. It is a tower situated in the most remote part of {{Term|TSP|North Castle}}, "up a long, narrow, spiral staircase" where prisoners are kept.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Tower is in the most remote part of North Castle, up a long, narrow, spiral staircase.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=62}}</ref><ref name=tower>{{Cite Book|quote='Link, I've heard enough. Your petty envy has become more than a nuisance. It's time you were taught some manners. I hereby banish you to the Tower for three days, effectively immediately!'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=62}}</ref>

{{Term|TSP|Link|link}} is taken to the {{Term|TSP|Tower}} by the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|link}}'s orders after {{Term|TSP|Link}} accuses {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|link}} to be evil without having any {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|display=proof|link}} to back it up.<ref name=tower/> Before the {{Plural|TSP|Soldier|link}} take him to the {{Term|TSP|Tower}}, {{Term|TSP|Impa|link}} approaches {{Term|TSP|Link}} and reminds him that he has the means to escape the tower; however, before she can tell him what it is, the guards hurriedly take the young hero to the cell.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Remember, you have the means to get out,' she whispers. 'It's--' 'Alright!' the second guard says irritably. 'That's enough of that. Come on, Link. Let's go. To the cell with you!'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=63}}</ref> At this point in the book, {{Term|TSP|Link}} will have three choices to pick from. He can either use the {{Term|TSP|Magical Clock|display=Clock|link}}, the {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle|link}}, or use neither.

If {{Term|TSP|Link}} uses the clock inside the {{Term|TSP|Tower}}, it will halt time for everyone except him and give the young hero an opportunity to escape the {{Term|TSP|Tower}}'s prison cell by using a toothpick to push the key out of the keyhole.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=But Link now recognizes this clock, and he knows it's anything but ordinary. It can stop time for everyone but its bearer for a short while. [...] Link takes a toothpick from his pocket and goes to work, trying to push the heavy key out of the lock.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=55, 56}}</ref> {{Term|TSP|Link}} then slides a paper under the door to drag the key back in, which he uses to unlock the door and escape the prison cell just as the clock is about to return time to its normal pace.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link slides a piece of paper under the door, trying to catch the key on it. [...] Then he's got the key onto the sheet of paper. Link grabs it and fumbles to unlock the door. [...] The door swings open.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=62}}</ref>

By using the {{Term|TSP|Golden Whistle}}, it will make a piercing sound, which makes the whistle to instantly shatter in {{Term|TSP|Link}}'s hands. However, inexplicably, a black vortex closes in on him, and when {{Term|TSP|Link}} wakes up, he is standing in the {{Term|TSP|Triforce Room|link}} with the king and {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda|link}}, meaning he has successfully traveled back in time and giving him another chance to travel to the {{Term|TSP|Water Town of Saria|link}} and obtain the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The sound is piercing. Link's ears start to ring, and he has the dizzy feeling that the room is spinning around him. Dimly he notices that the whistle has burst to pieces in his hands. Then a black vortex closes in on him. When Link comes to, he's standing in the Triforce room with the king, Charles and Zelda.[...] '...I hereby dub you a knight of the Triforce,' the king tells Charles, beaming. 'Oh, deja vu,' groans Link. 'I could swear this has happened before.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 109}}</ref>

However, if the young hero does not use either of those items or does not have them, {{Term|TSP|Link}} will gaze out the {{Term|TSP|Tower}} window to see Charles riding away, and just as he is disappearing into the woods, his horse turns into a giant, winged lizard. The page ends with a Game Over as {{Term|TSP|Link}} realizes that Charles is {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}} himself and has escaped with the {{Term|TSP|Triforce|link}} of Wisdom.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Just as Charles is disappearing into the woods, his horse turns into a giant, winged lizard! Then Charles turns around in the saddle. Even at this distance, Link can see his skull-like face and burning white eyes. [...] 'Oh, no! Charles is Ganon himself!' he moans. 'And he's gotten away with the Triforce!'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=28}}</ref>
