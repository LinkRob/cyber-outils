{{Term/Store|Medium Bomb Bag|Medium Bomb Bags|Series, ST, SS, SSHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|You've upgraded to the {{Color|SSHD Gold|Medium Bomb Bag}}! It can hold {{Color|SSHD Red|10}} extra bombs. Carry it with you when you need extra firepower.|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can obtain three {{Plural|ST|Bomb Bag|link}} in any order. They are found at the following locations:
#Purchased at {{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop|link}} for 500 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}}.
#Rewarded for beating the current record in the [[Whip Race]] {{Term|ST|Mini-Game}} in {{Term|ST|Whittleton|link}}.
#Rewarded for completing Level 2 of the {{Term|ST|Take 'Em All On!|link}} {{Term|ST|Mini-Game}}.

Whichever {{Term|ST|Bomb Bag}} is the second to be collected will become the {{Term|ST|Medium Bomb Bag}}, which allows {{Term|ST|Link}} to carry 20 {{Plural|ST|Bomb}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, a {{Term|SSHD|Medium Bomb Bag}} is a supplemental {{Term|SSHD|Bomb Bag|link}} that increases {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}}'s {{Term|SSHD|Bomb|link}}-holding capacity by 10, as long as the {{Term|SSHD|Medium Bomb Bag}} is stored in the {{Term|SSHD|Adventure Pouch|link}}.

A {{Term|SSHD|Medium Bomb Bag}} can be created by [[Upgrading]] a {{Term|SSHD|Small Bomb Bag|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}}, at a cost of one {{Term|SSHD|Lizard Tail|link}}, three {{Plural|SSHD|Ornamental Skull|link}}, one {{Term|SSHD|Blue Bird Feather|link}}, and 50 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}}. They can also be sold at the {{Term|SSHD|Item Check|link}} for 80 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee}}, or upgraded further into {{Plural|SSHD|Big Bomb Bag|link}}.
