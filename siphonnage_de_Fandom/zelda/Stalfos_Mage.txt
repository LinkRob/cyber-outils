{{Term/Store|Stalfos Mage|Stalfos Mages|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Stalfos Mage|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==

{{Plural|CoH|Stalfos Mage}} are a magical variety of {{Term|CoH|Stalfos|link}}. They move to the beat of the music on every other beat, their robes flashing one beat before attack and move onto an adjacent tile. When {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} stands two spaces in front of them, they will flash again on one beat and growl, signaling that they are about to summon wind to pull the heroes towards them on the next beat. Once the heroes are successfully pulled in, they will attack by lunging towards them on the third beat. They're one of the many enemies that can be summoned by a {{Term|CoH|Sarcophagus|link}}.

==Trivia==
*They behave identically to the Skeleton Mage from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.

*They also look nearly identical to the Shrouded Stalfos from ''[[Link's Awakening]]'' and the ''Oracle'' games.
