{{Term/Store|Frozen Hyrule||Series, FSA}} is the first stage in the level of the [[Frozen Hyrule|same name]] in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 267}}</ref> It is mainly the fields of Hyrule, but have been covered in [[snow]], and some passages have been blocked by walls of [[ice]]. There is also a trail where blizzards cause damage, which will require finding holes to take cover in before continuing on. The boss for the stage is [[Manhandla]], which is also the boss for [[The Coast]].

Extensive use of the [[Fire Rod]] is necessary to progress past Frozen Hyrule's [[Snow Wall]]s, melt hazardous [[Snowball]]s, and thaw frozen [[Force Gem]]s.
