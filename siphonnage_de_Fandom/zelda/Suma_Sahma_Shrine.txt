The {{Term/Store|Suma Sahma Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Suma Sahma Shrine}}<br/>Suma Sahma's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Suma Sahma's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma Shrine}} is initially buried beneath the {{Term|BotW|Snow|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Mount Granajh|link}}. In the ruins atop the mountain, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Mountain Peak Log|link}}, recounting the attempts of an unknown individual to unearth the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is a log of my findings concerning the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} that sleeps on a snowy Gerudo peak.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> Reading it begins the "{{Term|BotW|Secret of the Snowy Peaks|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Mountain Peak Log}} refers to a nearby {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}}, saying to cast a cold shadow onto it when it glows.<ref>{{Cite|I come to this snowy mountain intending to find the {{Color|BotW Red|ancient shrine}} that was written about in an old text. "The {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} slumbers near the pedestal on a snowy Gerudo peak." I believe I've arrived at the {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}} mentioned in the text.|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|On a brighter note, I've found an explanation in the source text that may be the key to this riddle. "When the snow mountain {{Color|BotW Red|pedestal}} glows, cast a cold shadow onto its core."|Mountain Peak Log|BotW}}</ref> The glow occurs at 4:00 PM, at which point {{Term|BotW|Link}} can cast the shadow of either a {{Term|BotW|Snowball|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} block onto it. This causes {{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma Shrine}} to rise from the ground, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Casting a shadow on the mysteriously glowing pedestal revealed none other than an ancient shrine!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma|link}}, who says he has already proven his worth by arriving at the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine...I am Suma Sahma. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Suma Sahma|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma Shrine}} is one large room with no puzzles or {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}, only a straight path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma}}. There is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the center of the room containing a {{Term|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar|link}}. As a reward for arriving to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Suma Sahma}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Suma Sahma|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Suma Sahma|BotW}}</ref>
