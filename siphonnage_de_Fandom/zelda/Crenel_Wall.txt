The {{Term/Store|Crenel Wall||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Crenel Wall (Watch out for falling rocks)|Sign|TMC}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Crenel Wall}} is located in the northwestern section of {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}. It is comprised of a large climbable wall with a few ledges extending out from it. In order to ascend the wall, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must acquire the {{Term|TMC|Grip Ring|link}} from a {{Term|TMC|Business Scrub|link}} elsewhere on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel}}.

The {{Term|TMC|Great Mayfly Fairy|link}} can be found at a {{Term|TMC|Fairy Fountain|link}} in the southeastern corner of the {{Term|TMC|Crenel Wall}}, behind a wall that {{Term|TMC|Link}} can destroy with a {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}}. In the northwestern corner of the {{Term|TMC|Crenel Wall|display=Wall}}, the {{Term|TMC|Crenel Hermit|link}} lives inside a cave; nearby, another cave accessed with the {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts|link}} contains a {{Term|TMC|Mysterious Wall|link}}.
