The {{Term/Store|Blowing Wind||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The [[Blowing Wind]] is the reward for completing the [[Ancient Ruins]], and is guarded by [[Manhandla]]. It is the sixth of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Sweet fruit is born when pollen is carried by the [[Blowing Wind]]!"<ref>"Sweet fruit is born when pollen is carried by the [[Blowing Wind]]!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]])</ref>
