{{Term/Store|Herb||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Herb}} is a rich {{Term|TMC|Figurine|link}} collector who owns the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}}, the largest house in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Not only am I a collector of figurines, but I'm also rich! Filthy, stinkin' rich!|Herb|TMC}}</ref><ref name="Complete">{{Cite|!? Whooooooooooooaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!!!! You did it! I can't believe you did it! This is...a totally complete set! How incredibly awesome! And you even got the legendary Carlov Medal!!! It shines with a beautiful light... It's every collector's dream! What you've shown me here... It's just... It's just amazing. Here, feel free to go in my house and take whatever you want in thanks... 'Cuz, you know, I'm rich! Hahaha!|Herb|TMC}}</ref> He does not appear until after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} has seen the spirit of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}; from that point until the end of the game, he can be found sitting at a table outside {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}}.

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} collects 130 {{Plural|TMC|Figurine|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Herb}} will reward him by letting him inside the {{Term|TMC|Music House}},<ref name="Complete" /> which contains a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}, three {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} with 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} in each, and a {{Term|TMC|Phonograph|link}}.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure! Let's see how well they fit!|Herb|TMC}}</ref>
