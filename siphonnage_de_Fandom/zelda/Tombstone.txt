==Location and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Stub|TLoZ}}

==={{LA|-}}===
Tombstones are found within the [[Graveyard]]. Touching a Tombstone will spawn a [[Ghini]] or a [[Giant Ghini]]. A single Tombstone can be found west of the Graveyard. It is the resting location of the [[Ghost (Link's Awakening)|Ghost]].

In {{LA|DX}}, the Tombstones need to be pushed in the correct order described in the book atop the bookshelf in the [[Village Library]] in order to unveil the [[Color Dungeon]].

==={{TMC|-}}===
Twelve {{Plural|TMC|Tombstone}} are scattered throughout the northern portion of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}. The top-left {{Term|TMC|Tombstone}} can be pushed aside to reveal a cave with a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}. The top-right {{Term|TMC|Tombstone}} will move aside after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Dampé|link}}, revealing a cave with {{Term|TMC|Gina|link}} inside. Finally, the top-center {{Term|TMC|Tombstone}} will move aside when {{Term|TMC|Link}} uses the {{Plural|TMC|Flashing Spot on the Floor|link}} (initially covered by {{Plural|TMC|Rock|link}}) to simultaneously press down three {{Plural|TMC|Floor Switch|link}}; this grants him access to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}.

Inside the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt}}, a {{Term|TMC|Tombstone}} emblazoned with the symbol of the {{Term|TMC|Triforce|link}} marks the grave of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}.
