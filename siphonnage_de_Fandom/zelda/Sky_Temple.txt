{{Term/Store|Sky Temple||Series, TFH}} is the last {{Term|TFH|Levels in Tri Force Heroes|display=Level|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=161 }}</ref> It is the fourth and last Level of the {{Term|TFH|Sky Realm}} area.

==Themes and Navigation==
As with the other Levels in the {{Term|TFH|Sky Realm|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple}} takes place in the sky high above the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. It mostly consists of floating platforms that float above the sky's orange clouds, which are filled with numerous enemies that the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} must defeat to proceed. All eight {{Plural|TFH|Item|link}} encountered during the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' travels are provided for use here, providing many different strategies. At the very end is a gorgeous palace where {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}} can be found.

In '''Stage 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} begin at a platform containing a wide assortment of items: including the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Gripshot|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Hammer|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Bow|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must pick three of the eight {{Plural|TFH|Item}}. After making their choice, the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}} on another platform glows, and a translucent, disk-shaped lift appears close by. This lift will drop the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to a grassy island with a pair of {{Term|TFH|Mini-Margo|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Totem Deku|link}}. If the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} did not pick the {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}}, then a {{Term|TFH|Bomb Flower|link}} is provided in the center to defeat the {{Plural|TFH|Mini-Margo}} with. After they are defeated, another disk platform appears which drops the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} onto an island with a lake. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} fight a horde of {{Plural|TFH|Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Water Tektite|link}}, and a {{Term|TFH|Keeleon|link}}. Their defeat creates another lift to an icy platform where the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} face {{Plural|TFH|Freezard|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, and {{Plural|TFH|Terrorpin|link}}. {{Plural|TFH|Bomb Flower}} are provided for the {{Plural|TFH|Freezard}} and {{Plural|TFH|Terrorpin}} if the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} do not have the appropriate items. The lift that appears takes the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}.

In '''Stage 2''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} again appear on a platform with the eight different {{Plural|TFH|Item}}, which the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can freely swap their items for if they choose. A disk platform drops the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} into a pit with lava pools. Here the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} face a horde of {{Plural|TFH|Fire Keese|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Green Kodongo|link}}, and gold {{Plural|TFH|Spear Soldier|link}} riding {{Term|TFH|Armos|link}} statues. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are also provided with their own {{Term|TFH|Armos}} to help defeat the {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} with. After their defeat, a gate opens to the next platform with a disk lift. This drops the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} into a quicksand-filled pit with {{Plural|TFH|Stalfos|link}}, {{Plural|TFH|Morth|link}} and a {{Term|TFH|Gibdo|link}}. After the {{Plural|TFH|Stalfos}} and {{Term|TFH|Gibdo}} are defeated, another lift appears. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are then dropped into a pit filled with {{Plural|TFH|Ghini|link}} and a {{Term|TFH|Ball and Chain Soldier|link}} wielding a fiery ball and chain. The pit immediately turns pitch dark as soon as they fall in. The {{Term|TFH|Ball and Chain Soldier}} provides some light, but the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can also light their way by lighting the torches with the {{Term|TFH|Fire Gloves}}. After all {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}} are defeated, the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} appears.

In '''Stage 3''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear on yet another platform containing the eight {{Plural|TFH|Item}}. A staircase with green {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} leads to a circular platform, where immediately the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are intercepted by a group of gold {{Plural|TFH|Sword Soldier|link}} and Spear Soldiers. Because there are no railings around the platform, it is possible to knock them off using the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}}. Their defeat opens a gate to another platform where more gold Soldiers fight them, of which one is a {{Term|TFH|Bomb Soldier|link}} and the other a {{Term|TFH|Ball and Chain Soldier}}. The final staircase leads to the palace entrance where a pair of flying {{Plural|TFH|Super Aeralfos|link}} appear. After they are defeated, the giant gate opens to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}.

'''Stage 4''' is where the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} fight {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|section=Strategy|display=The Lady|link}} inside the temple.
