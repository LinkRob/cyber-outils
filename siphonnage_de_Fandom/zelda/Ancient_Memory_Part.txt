==Locations and Uses==
{{Stub|HWAoC}}
The Ancient Memory Part is a weapon used by {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}} and it is the default and most basic weapon of one of the two weapon types used by Zelda, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. The default Ancient Memory Part has an attack damage of 5, and it could be fused with other weapons used by 
Zelda to make stronger weapons.

The Ancient memory part can be acquired in the chests that appear after a strong foe is defeated or an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} is captured in a {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} or {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} with {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}} being one of the selected characters, or when said scenarios and challenges are completed with Zelda being selected.
