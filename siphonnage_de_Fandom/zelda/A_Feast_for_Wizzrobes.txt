{{Term/Store|A Feast for Wizzrobes||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 34 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|A Feast for Wizzrobes|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|A Feast for Wizzrobes}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to defeat six {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}}, six {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Wizzrobe|link}} are split into four groups. The first contains two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}}, the second contains two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe}}, the third contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe}}, the fourth contains three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}}, and the fifth contains one {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe}}, one {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe}}, and one {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe}}. Each one of the first four groups can only be confronted by defeating the group before it, as there is a closed gate that opens each time one of the groups is defeated. The fifth group spawns when the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 13 KOs. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Opal|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
