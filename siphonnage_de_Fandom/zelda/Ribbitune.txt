'''Ribbitune''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Ribbitune|An amphibious crooner whose golden croaks draw other monsters to it, which can be useful as a trap.<ref name="Ribbitune">{{Cite|An amphibious crooner whose golden croaks draw other monsters to it, which can be useful as a trap.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Ribbitune are [[frog]]-like monsters that attract enemies by singing songs. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Ribbitune [[Echo]] with the [[Tri Rod]], and it costs two Echo energy to summon.
