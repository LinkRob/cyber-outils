"{{Term/Store|The Royal White Stallion||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Royal White Stallion}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Outskirt Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet {{Term|BotW|Toffa|link}}, who asks if he has seen a {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}} on his journies.<ref>{{Cite|A traveler... Tell me, have you seen a {{Color|BotW Red|pure-white horse}} anywhere?|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> Long ago, his grandfather told him that he saw the {{Term|BotW|White Horse}} that belonged to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} grazing on {{Term|BotW|Safula Hill|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A long time back, my grandfather told me that he once saw the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} grazing on {{Color|BotW Blue|Safula Hill}}...|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> Recently, a customer at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} also claims to have seen the {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The thing is...just the other day, one of our customers said he saw a {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Safula Hill}} recently...|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Toffa}} believes may be a descendant of the original.<ref>{{Cite|Could it be a relative of the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} from my grandfather's day?|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him the {{Term|BotW|White Horse}} if he happens to find it,<ref>{{Cite|Well, if you happen to see a {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} on your travels, do you think you could bring it back to me?|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> in addition to registering it at a {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! If you see the {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}, please {{Color|BotW Blue|register it}} at a stable and bring it to me.|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> He also warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that, while it is a beautiful {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, it can be difficult to catch.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Safula Hill}} supposedly has a beautiful, pure-white coat without any mottling. I hear it's very elusive, though. Good luck.|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.{{Note|It is possible to begin and end this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} immediately by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Toffa}} after registering the {{Term|BotW|White Horse}}. He will be shocked by its presence and the validity of his grandfather's stories.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You— That {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}! My grandpa's stories were true? The {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} I dreamed of is right before my eyes!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Toffa}} then gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Royal Bridle}} and {{Term|BotW|Royal Saddle}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's something I'd like to present you with! These are the royal family's saddle and bridle! The royal saddle and bridle are custom fit for this {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref>}}{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tames the {{Term|BotW|White Horse}} before accepting the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} from {{Term|BotW|Toffa}}, he will implore {{Term|BotW|Link}} to register the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} at the reception desk right away and speak to him after.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You— That {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}! You can {{Color|BotW Blue|register that beauty}} at this stable's reception desk. Go {{Color|BotW Blue|register}} the {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} and then come right back!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can {{Color|BotW Blue|register your horse}} here at the stable's reception desk! Talk to me again after you've {{Color|BotW Blue|registered}} your {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref>}}

As {{Term|BotW|Toffa}} said, the {{Term|BotW|White Horse}} can be found near the {{Term|BotW|Sanidin Park Ruins|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Safula Hill}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} requires at least two full {{Plural|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} in order to tame the {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, but once this has been done, he can ride it back to {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}}.

After registering the {{Term|BotW|White Horse}} at a {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Toffa}} will grow excited over the fact that his grandfather's stories were true.<ref>{{Cite|What? You brought the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}}? Well I'll be... Grandpa wasn't lying!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> He says that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has made his life worthwhile.<ref>{{Cite|Son, you just made my life. Staying alive in this godforsaken land may just have been worth it after all.|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives him the {{Term|BotW|Royal Bridle|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Royal Saddle|link}}, which was handed down to him by his grandfather and custom fitted for a {{Term|BotW|White Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've got a surprise for you. My grandpa handed this down to me, and now I'm handing it down to you. I present to you...the royal family's saddle and bridle! Custom fitted for the white royal horse!|Toffa|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear|link}} is immediately placed upon the {{Term|BotW|White Horse}}, at which point {{Term|BotW|Toffa}} recalls the stories his grandfather told him.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} that {{Color|BotW Blue|Princess Zelda}} rode. The one my grandfather told me about when I was little...|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> He heard that the sight of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} riding her {{Term|BotW|White Horse}} was a beauty beyond words,<ref>{{Cite|The sight of the princess riding atop the {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} was supposed to be a beauty beyond words...|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> and he states that the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has must be its grandchild.<ref>{{Cite|There's no doubt about it, this {{Color|BotW Red|horse}} is the grandchild of that hose so long ago. So beautiful... So pure...|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
