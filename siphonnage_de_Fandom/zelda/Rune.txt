Distilling Rune...|N/A|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}} also known as {{Term/Store|Sheikah Rune|Sheikah Runes|Series, HWAoC|plural}},{{Exp Game|HWAoC}}<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Runes|Quests|HWAoC}}</ref> are recurring abilities added to the {{Term|Series|Sheikah Slate|link}} in {{TLoZ|series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Runes are special objects controlled by the [[Sheikah Slate]] that can affect the world around {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. Runes are primarily obtained from the [[Ancient Shrine]]s scattered across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. To obtain a Rune, Link must insert the Sheikah Slate into a pedestal to extract them.<ref name= "Rune"/> Upon extraction, Link can access that Rune's abilities by pressing the L Button and selecting the Rune.
