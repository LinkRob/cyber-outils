{{Term/Store|Nocturn|Nocturns|Series, ST|plural}} are a ghost-like enemy in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 194}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The enemy is encountered in the [[Tower of Spirits]] on the third and sixth visits. They will hover out of reach in the darkness, but when they suddenly get exposed to the light, they fall to the ground, weakened. This can easily be done by lighting up the torches around with the [[boomerang]] or by using [[Princess Zelda]] to possess a [[Torch Phantom]] to move around with a light. Once this is done, Princess Zelda or [[Link]] can attack the Nocturn. After obtaining a Bow of Light, Link can shoot a Nocturn in its back with a fully charged Light Arrow.

==Nomenclature==
Its name is derived from the word "nocturnal", referring to the fact that they live in the dark and resent the light.
