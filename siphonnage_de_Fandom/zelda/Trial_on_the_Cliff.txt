"{{Term/Store|Trial on the Cliff||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial on the Cliff}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|North Lomei Labyrinth}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|link}} in the northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Qaza Tokki|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to make his way through the maze, as he will receive the reward of a blessing.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You who have reached this point... Proceed onward through the maze and you will receive a blessing as reward...}}|Qaza Tokki|BotW}}</ref> Hearing this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin. From the center of the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|display=Labyrinth}}, {{Term|BotW|Qaza Tokki Shrine|link}} can be seen in a central chamber, surrounded by portcullises. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must navigate the maze to find an entrance into this chamber.

While journeying through the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth|display=Labyrinth}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter pools of {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} blocking his path. He will also be attacked by monsters, such as {{Plural|BotW|Blue Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Black Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Octorok|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Chuchu|link}}. There are also many {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} from which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can gather supplies, such as {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.

In the northern corner of the {{Term|BotW|North Lomei Labyrinth}}, hidden tunnels can be found that lead {{Term|BotW|Link}} to a ladder. At the end of this ladder is an elevated tunnel going towards the center of the labyrinth, which leads to a small platform above the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. By dropping down into the chamber, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to activate {{Term|BotW|Qaza Tokki Shrine}}, which completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
