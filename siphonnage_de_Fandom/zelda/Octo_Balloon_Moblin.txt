A '''Octo Balloon Moblin''' is a Enemy in [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity]]. It was added in the Guardian of Remembrance DLC.

== Characteristics ==
A Octo Balloon Moblin is a Moblin of any color that floats a bit off the ground thanks to 3 [[Sky Octorok|Sky Octoroks]] attached to it. It also has a basket filled with [[Octo Balloon|Octo Balloons]] that it uses to regenerate its Sky Octoroks if they all deflate(Which may happen as you deflect its attacks) The Sky Octoroks may or may not be trained, as they tend to use their attacks when the Moblin gives the signal. Its attacks are as follows.

A flying kick forwards(Short range)

A flying kick forwards(Long range)

Firing a rock forwards from one Sky Octorok

Firing a rock forwards from all Sky Octoroks

Firing a metal weapon forwards from the middle Sky Octorok

Spinning around in a attempt to drag you into it

Spinning around while firing rocks from the Sky Octoroks<br />
