{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Claymore}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} known for their low durability.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}, either abandoned or being used by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. They also have a chance of appearing in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn from scanning the {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

If a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Claymore}} is placed in front of a {{Term|BotW|Rock Octorok|link}}, it will inhale the sword and then spit out its cleaned counterpart.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> There is a 50% chance for a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Claymore|link}}, a 35% for a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Claymore|link}}, a 10% chance for a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Claymore|link}}, and a 5% chance for a {{Term|BotW|Royal Claymore|link}}.

Since they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Rusty Claymore}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They are also unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Claymore}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which consists of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that have been left exposed to the elements.<ref name="CaC"/>
