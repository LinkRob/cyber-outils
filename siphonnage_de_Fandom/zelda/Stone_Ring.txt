==Location and Uses==
The {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring}} are a group of 12 circular stones with holes in their middles that can be found on the {{Term|BotW|West Hyrule Plains|link}}. They are mentioned in an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" about the area,<ref>{{Cite|Peculiarly enough, these rocks are apparently mentioned in an ancient song from the area.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} will offer to sing for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} appearing when two {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring}} are threaded together by an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When a single arrow threads two rings,<br/>the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} will rise like birds on wings.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Listening to this song will activate "{{Term|BotW|The Two Rings|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

To solve the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot a singular {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} through two {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring}}. A pair of {{Plural|BotW|Stone Ring}} that line up with each other can be found on the southwestern corner of the {{Term|BotW|West Hyrule Plains}}. After shooting an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} through these two {{Term|BotW|Stone Ring|display=Rings}}, {{Term|BotW|Sheem Dagoze Shrine|link}} will rise from the ground, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|An ancient shrine appeared when you loosed a single arrow through two ring-shaped rocks.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
