==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Peahat Larva}}<br/>Defend with your shield!
}}

Peahat Larvae are miniature spawn of Peahats in {{OoT}}. They are emitted by a sleeping [[Peahat]] at night or by a Peahat that has flown into the air. They attack from above and continuously chase [[Link]] if not avoided or dispatched. The [[Shield]] can fend off these enemies by blocking their assault. Peahat Larvae will die if they land; if they are not killed, the Peahat Larvae will eventually get tired and return to their parent plant.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= {{Color|MM White|{{Typo|Pea Hat Larva|Peahat Larva}}}}... You don't know about it? If it comes at you, get in the defensive position to knock it back!
}}

Peahat Larvae are emitted by the single [[Peahat]] found in a [[Secret Grotto]] in [[Termina Field]] in {{MM}}. They continuously chase Link if not avoided or dispatched. The Shield can fend off these enemies by blocking their assault. Peahat Larvae will die if they land.
