"{{Term/Store|The Lost Pilgrimage||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Lost Pilgrimage}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Korok Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|The Lost Pilgrimage}}" is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} that are needed to complete "{{Term|BotW|The Korok Trials|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. In the northwest portion of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} named {{Term|BotW|Tasho|link}}, who appears concerned.<ref>{{Cite|Going alone? I dunno...|Tasho|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to them, they recognize him as the hero and ask if he is planning on attempting the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You! You're Mr. Hero, right? You're gonna do the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trial}}, yes, yes?|Tasho|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms he is doing the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Tasho}} has a favor they would like to ask of him.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure.] I knew it!<br/>I'd like to ask you a favor, then!|Tasho|BotW}}</ref> Another {{Term|BotW|Korok}}, {{Term|BotW|Oaki|link}}, has just set off on the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|My little Oaki just now set off to do the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trial}}...|Tasho|BotW}}</ref> While he insisted on going on his own, {{Term|BotW|Tasho}} is worried about him.<ref>{{Cite|Normally, I'd go along, too, but Oaki insisted on going alone. I let Oaki go, but...well, I'm just getting worried!|Tasho|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, {{Term|BotW|Tasho}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to secretly keep an eye on {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} while he attempts the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|Could you please follow Oaki without being seen? I want to be sure nothing bad happens...|Tasho|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} begins.

In order to find the end of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must silently follow {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} through the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets too close to {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} or draws attention to himself, {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} will initially believe he is a ghost.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|KYAAH! A ghost!}}|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> However, once he realizes {{Term|BotW|Link}} is alive, he becomes angry that he was followed.<ref>{{Cite|...Wait, you're not a ghost!<br/>Were you following me? I said I could do this by myself!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> He insists on starting over again on his own.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh, and now I'm not by myself... I'll have to do it all over again.|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> Back at the start of the path, {{Term|BotW|Tasho}} notes that {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} tends to get nervous on occasion.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh, Oaki saw you, huh?<br/>Oaki can get a little nervous or antsy sometimes, that's for sure...|Tasho|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} is planning on leaving again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have another chance to follow him all the way.<ref>{{Cite|Luckily, Oaki is planning on going to do the trial again, so you've got another chance to stealthily follow. That's OK, right? It's OK? Good! I'm counting on you!|Tasho|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} follows {{Term|BotW|Oaki}}, they will express fear over being alone,<ref>{{Cite|What was that?|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK... I'm a little bit scared.|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> though he attempts to calm their nerves by assuring himself or inspecting the scenery.<ref>{{Cite|Everything is A-OK!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oooh, pretty flowers!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This isn't so bad...|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm not scared one bit.|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> On his journey, {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} will also make remarks about familiar landmarks or his memory of the path.<ref>{{Cite|I remember this tree!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is the right way...|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Halfway there!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm almost there!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> Certain occurrences, such as {{Plural|BotW|Tree Branch|link}} falling from {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, will cause {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} to turn around, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must be prepared for.

After passing through a giant fallen {{Term|BotW|Tree}}, {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} will become scared that someone else is there,<ref>{{Cite|Is... is someone there?|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> and he will retreat back down the path when he thinks they have seen a ghost.<ref>{{Cite|AHH! A ghost!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> In this instance, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the {{Term|BotW|Tree}} to hide from {{Term|BotW|Oaki}}. When he realizes it was simply a shadow, he continues his {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh. It was just a shadow...|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, as {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} reaches the end of the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}, he passes by a pack of {{Plural|BotW|Maraudo Wolf|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either dispatch or evade them without alerting {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} to his presence.

Once {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} has reached {{Term|BotW|Daag Chokah Shrine|link}}, he will cheer about his victory.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! There's the shrine|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Woo! I've finally made it!|Oaki|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Yay! I made it! I finished the {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}!}}|Oaki|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to reach {{Term|BotW|Daag Chokah Shrine}} without following {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} by slipping ahead of them and following the path.}} Finally noticing {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} recognizes him as the hero.<ref>{{Cite|Wha? Who's there?<br/>Hey! Are you that hero guy? You know—Mr. Hero?|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> He excitedly share with {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was able to complete the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}} on his own.<ref>{{Cite|Guess what, Mr. Hero! I did the trial all by myself! Aren't I brave?|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> Realizing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} likely intends to utilize the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yeah! You're probably here for the shrine.|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} allows him to go ahead and look.<ref>{{Cite|The Great Deku Tree said that the monk's blessing is waiting in the shrine ahead! Why don't you go take a look?|Oaki|BotW}}</ref> After he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
