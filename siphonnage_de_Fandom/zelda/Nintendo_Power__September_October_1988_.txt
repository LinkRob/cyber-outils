'''Nintendo Power (September/October 1988)''' is a [[:Category:Magazines|magazine]] published by [[Nintendo]].

==Zelda Content==
===Insider's Calendar===
The calendar features multiple dates and events related to Nintendo.
* '''December 17th''': "First 1,000,000 Zeldas sold (1987)"
* '''March 22nd''': "Princess Zelda awakens"

===Counselor's Corner (p. 64-65)===
The article provides answers to four questions about {{TLoZ}}:
* '''"In the First Quest of The Legend of Zelda where is Level Seven?"'''
* '''"How do I get into Level Nine and what special items can I find inside?"'''
* '''"In the Second Quest of The Legend of Zelda how do I find the Letter?"'''
* '''"I've looked everywhere but I can't find the Raft. Where is it?"'''

===Pak Watch (p. 85)===
The article provides news on the development of upcoming Nintendo titles. Among these news, there is a letter written by {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}} addressed to {{Term|TAoL|Princess Zelda|link}}:
:'''''Dear Zelda,'''<br/>I'm here in Kyoto with my programmer Mr. Miyamoto, and I'm afraid I have some bad news to tell you. Are you sitting down? (That's right, you're lying down) Anyway, they tell me I will not be able to begin my journey until the new year. It has something to do with computer chips or something. I know we hoped to spend the holidays together, but I'm sure you can hold on a little bit longer. I miss you desperately.<br/>Yours forever,<br/>LINK"''

===NFL Players Challenge Tyson for World Championship!! (p. 95)===
The article features interviews of {{Wp|Eric Dickerson}}, {{Wp|Ron Morris}}, and {{Wp|Sean Jones}}. In the interview of Eric Dickerson, it is mentioned that he has played {{TLoZ|-}}.

===TOP 30 (p. 102-104)===
The section features the 30 most popular [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] games according to a poll held by [[Nintendo]]. {{TLoZ|-}} is in first position with 2090 points. {{TAoL|-}} is in 17th position with 225 points.{{Note|name= TAoL TOP 30|The game has received votes despite not being released yet.}}

The points for {{TLoZ|-}} go as follow:
* '''Player's Picks''': 1360 points {{Small|(First position)}}
* '''Pro's Picks''': 470 points {{Small|(First position)}}
* '''Dealer's Picks''': 260 points {{Small|(Third position)}}

The points for {{TAoL|-}} go as follow:
* '''Player's Picks''': 111 points {{Small|(13th position)}}
* '''Pro's Picks''': 114 points {{Small|(11th position)}}
* '''Dealer's Picks''': N/A
