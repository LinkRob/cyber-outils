Mountainous regions are very high and are usually Fire or Ice themed. The element of Earth is also present in these places.

Several named '''Mountains''' have appeared throughout ''The Legend of Zelda'' series.

*'''[[Death Mountain]]''', the fore-running Mountain of the series and has appearances in nearly all titles involving Hyrule.
*'''[[Fire Mountain]]''', one of the many islands of {{TWW}}
*'''[[Goron Mountain]]''', an area in the northern part of Holodrum in {{OoS}}. 
*'''[[Mount Crenel]]''', the prominent mountain featured in {{TMC}}.
*'''[[Mount Tamaranch]]''', Koholint Island's central Mountain in {{LA}}.
*'''[[Mt. Cucco]]''', the northeastern most mountain of {{OoS|-}}.
*'''[[Northern Peak]]''', a small mountain holding Onox's Castle in {{OoS|-}}.
*'''[[Rolling Ridge]]''', located in the northern part of Labrynna in {{OoA}}.
*'''[[Snowhead]]''', exclusively in {{MM}}, this is one of the few snow covered mountains.
*'''[[Snowpeak]]''', another snow covered mountain, appears in {{TP}}.
*'''[[Tal Tal Mountain Range]]''', the mountain range spanning Koholint Island in {{LA|-}}.
*'''[[Talus Peaks]]''', Labrynna's western most mountain range in {{OoA|-}}.
*'''[[Veil Falls]]''', refers to the waterfall cascading the unnamed mountain of {{TMC|-}}.
