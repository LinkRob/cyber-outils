The {{Term/Store|Deplian Badlands||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Deplian Badlands'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>
==Breath Of The Wild==
===Features and Overview===
The Deplian Badlands are a barren landscape in the [[Eldin Mountains]] range. The Deplian Badlands are littered with dead Trees and volcanic soil, leaving the land unfit to bear any significant vegetation. The Deplian Badlands lead eastward up a slope. At the top of this slope rest some unnamed ruins, which bear several dead Trees and one large, living Tree. Atop the lone Tree is a [[Pinwheel]], which will spawn five [[Balloon]]s throughout the surrounding ruins when approached. Destroying these Balloons will cause a Korok to appear. [[Ore Deposit]]s can be found north and south of these ruins.

At the base of the slope at Deplian Badlands' eastern border, Link can find a [[Magnesis]]-themed [[Korok]] puzzle. Completing the puzzle will cause a Korok to appear, who will then grant Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
