'''Oinker Wizards''' are pig-like enemies that appear in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They are a variant of the more common {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker|link}}. They shoot different projectiles at {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} that can stun him for a second, and try to keep their distance at same time, making it complicated to attack more than one at a time.

==Areas==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}} are only found one the {{Term|FPTRR|Second Continent|display=Second|link}} and {{Plural|FPTRR|Third Continent|link}}. They are first encountered in {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plain|link}}, and after that also in the in the {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins|link}}. They accompany the regular {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}} behind a closed gate of their camp, which can only be opened with the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Oinker Snout|display=Oinker Snout|link}}.

==Characteristics and Variations==
===Oinker Wizard===
Regular {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}} only appear on the {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plain|link}}, together with a pair of {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker|section=Snow Oinker|display=Snow Oinkers|link}}. They guard the stolen {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Gold Lamé Socks|display=Gold Lamé Socks|link}} of {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum|link}}. They shoot fireballs at {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} which are relatively hard to dodge, but can be blocked completely by the {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguards|display=Bodyguard|link}}. When they are defeated, they may drop a {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Hand-Sewn Robe|display=Hand-Sewn Robe|link}}.{{clear}}

===Senior Oinker Wizard===
[[File:FPTRR Senior Wizard Oinker Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Senior {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}}''' only appear in the first area of the {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}} camp in {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins|link}}. A pair of them joins the fight in the last phase together with some {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker|section=Small Oinker Captain|display=Small Oinker Captains|link}}. They shoot purple energy balls which do more damage and push {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} further away than the regular fireballs of their cousins in {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plain}}.{{clear}}

===Yang Illusion Master Oinker Wizard===
[[File:FPTRR Yang Illusion Master Oinker Wizard Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Yang Illusion Master {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}}''' appears only in the final fight with the [[Oinker Chief]]. They are summoned normally in the battle when at least one {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker|section=Oinker Bodyguard|display=Oinker Bodyguard|link}} is still present, otherwise the {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} would be called instead. They shoot energy balls that can set {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} on fire for a small amount of time, and they are generally faster than normal {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}}. When they are defeated they may drop a valuable {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Magical Hat|display=Magical Hat|link}}.{{clear}}

===Yin Illusion Master Oinker Wizard===
[[File:FPTRR Yin Illusion Master Oinker Wizard Sprite.png|left]]
Like its counterpart, the '''Yin Illusion Master {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}}''' only appears in the final battle against the Oinker Chief. The only difference between the two wizards is that this one shoots blue energy balls that can freeze {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} for a short time, making him more vulnerable to the attacks of the other {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}}. This {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}} can also drop the Magical Hat.
