==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|FS|Armor Seeds|These mystical seeds increase your defense power.}}
{{Plural|FS|Armor Seed}} can be found by cutting {{Term|FS|Grass|link}}. When picked up, {{Plural|FS|Armor Seed}} will double {{Term|FS|Link|link}}'s defensive power until the end of the current {{Term|FS|Stage|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The mystical seeds you collect will boost your power by up to two levels. {{Color|FS Orange|Razor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Razor Seed}} add sword power. {{Color|FS Orange|Armor Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Armor Seed}} add defense. {{Color|FS Orange|Pegasus Seeds}} {{Icon|FS Pegasus Seed}} add speed.|Stray Fairy|FS}}</ref> Only two {{Plural|FS|Armor Seed}} for each one of the {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} can be active at a time.
