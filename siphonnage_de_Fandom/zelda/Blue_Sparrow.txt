{{Term/Store|Blue Sparrow|Blue Sparrows|Series, BotW|plural}} are [[Creature]]s in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Blue Sparrow}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Blue Sparrow}}
Blue Sparrows chiefly inhabit wetlands and watersides within the [[Lanayru (Region)|Lanayru region]], such as the [[Lanayru Wetlands]], the [[Zora River]], and {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This breed of sparrow is most often seen in the Lanayru region.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They're also found in and around the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. They feed chiefly on waterside plants. They often bathe in shallow water when safe, but are skittish and easily startled.<ref>{{Cite|They get most of their food from leaves and plants that grow near water. If it's safe enough, they can be spotted bathing in puddles or shallow rivers.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When killed, Blue Sparrows drop a single [[Chickaloo Tree Nut]].
