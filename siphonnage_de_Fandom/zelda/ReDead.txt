{{Term/Store|ReDead|ReDeads|Series, OoT, OoT3D, TWW, TWWHD, TFH|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoT, TWW, TFH}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 176 {{Exp Game|OoT3D}} & 241 {{Exp Game|TWWHD}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|CoH}} also known as the '''Lurkers in the Dark''',{{Exp Game|OoT}}{{Name Ref|OoT3D}} are recurring [[enemy|enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
Redeads are tall, [[zombie]]-esque monsters. While their appearance changes from game to game, their behavior has remained relatively the same - namely staying in place and using a petrifying scream to freeze nearby prey in their tracks, including [[Link]]. While the victim is stunned, they will walk over and attack, typically by locking them in place and biting them.

ReDeads share many similarities with [[Gibdo]]s, which also behave identically in {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, and {{TP}}.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|ReDead}}<br/>Its gaze will paralyze you. If it bites you, tap any button to escape!
}}

In {{OoT}}, its first appearance, ReDeads are brown, shriveled humanoids who wear [[Spooky Mask|wooden masks]]. They usually appear in small groups or sometimes alone in dead or ruinous places, such as tombs or dungeons, and make their presence known with [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_ReDead_Moan.wav periodic, low-pitched moaning]. ReDeads are encountered the earliest inside the [[Royal Family's Tomb]], where they gather in a chamber with poison water leading to the room where [[Link]] learns the [[Sun's Song]]. They can later be found in [[Hyrule Castle Town]]'s ruined market place when he becomes the [[Hero of Time]], possibly as the town's deceased residents, as well as at the [[Bottom of the Well]] and inside the [[Shadow Temple]].

ReDeads can either be found idly standing slightly hunched over or sitting crouched down in a fetal position until Link approaches, at which they will rise and slowly shuffle towards him. When the young hero is within their range, they will temporarily paralyze him with a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_ReDead_Scream.wav bone-chilling shriek], swiftly jump onto his back if close enough, and gradually drain his health by squeezing and biting him, inflicting half a [[Heart Container|Heart]] every second. Link will then have to struggle free with a series of A and B-Button presses (one button alone also works). Once released from their hold, Link can either strike back with the [[Sword]] in retaliation or run away to attack from a safer distance. ReDeads, however, have a flaw in that they are blind; Link can walk by slowly or with the [[Iron Boots]] equipped to pass by unnoticed. If he takes out or puts away any weapon, or runs near them, they will hear him and attempt to paralyze him with their vivid gaze and scream. Interestingly, Link cannot become paralyzed in certain areas with a fixed camera position, namely Castle Town's market, possibly because of the way the camera usually zooms in when the monsters use their gaze attack, which would ruin the effect of the pre-rendered background.

ReDeads are quite slow, both in motion and recovering from an attack, making them fairly easy to slay, particularly if Link can get behind them, though it requires a considerable amount of hits with the [[Kokiri Sword]] or [[Giant's Knife|Broken Giant's Knife]]. A single [[Spin Attack]] or [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]] with the [[Biggoron's Sword]] will defeat it immediately. When a ReDead is slain, any other ReDeads in the area will walk towards and crouch beside the fallen enemy until its body fades away, ignoring Link and making killing the whole group easier. This behavior is never explained in the game, although plausible explanations could be that they are mourning its death or even consuming its corpse.

ReDeads in {{OoT|-}} are quite susceptible to the Sun's Song, which will paralyze them in a coat of light for one minute when played. They are also weak to [[Din's Fire]]. For some unknown reason, however, [[Fire Arrow]]s will not damage them.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= {{Color|MM White|ReDead}}...<br/>If it catches you in its paralyzing glare, you won't be able to move. Shake it off if you get caught!
}}
[[File:ReDeadOoTMM.jpg|thumb|Dancing ReDeads manipulated by the Captain's Hat from {{MM|-}}|229x229px]]
In {{MM}}, ReDeads appear mostly the same as in {{OoT|-}}, although with several differences, particularly in their behavior and weaknesses. In this [[Nintendo 64|N64]] game, ReDeads will continue to utilize a paralyzing scream and slowly approach Link when he comes close. However, a [[Gossip Stone]] explains that Redeads were part of a dancing troupe of [[Ancient Castle of Ikana|Ikana Castle]] in their living days.<ref>{{Cite|The ReDeads in Ikana were once members of the castle's dancing troupe...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> As such, if the young Hero of Time wears the [[Garo's Mask]], [[Gibdo Mask]], or the [[Captain's Hat]], the ReDeads will assume no living beings are around and dance instead of attacking him. While wearing any one of these masks, Link can freely attack the ReDeads with ease, so long as he keeps the mask on.

ReDeads in {{MM|-}} are quite weak to beams of [[light]] reflected off of the [[Mirror Shield]], which will kill them instantly. They also retain their weakness to fire, and, unlike the game's prequel, can be defeated with Fire Arrows.

Gibdos can have their bandages burned away to reveal a ReDead underneath.

In {{TWW}}, ReDeads appear significantly different. Their skin is turquoise, and they have little to no fat in them, leaving them as almost skeletal. They wear large, gold hoop earrings, and their lips have rotted away to reveal their gums underneath. Typically, their eyes are pitch-black, but when they turn hostile and attack, red, glowing pupils appear. 

They mainly appear in the [[Earth Temple#The Wind Waker|Earth Temple]], as well as in the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship|link}}, the [[Savage Labyrinth]], [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]], and in the sewer tunnels of the [[Private Oasis]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW|publisher= Prima|page= 31}}</ref> Their method of attack is similar to their earlier incarnations, where they scream to stun Link and grab him when he gets too close. They can occasionally be found hiding in coffins that pop open when Link approaches them or shines light on their surface. Like those of {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}}, these ReDeads will proceed to bite Link repeatedly when they grab him, draining him of hearts until the player rapidly presses A and B to free himself. They do not appear to be blind, however, as they will look up at Link whenever he moves close to them; in fact, while their eyes are initially black and lifeless, they glow red when they scream and attack. They cannot be fooled by walking slowly, and will always attack once Link comes within range.

As in {{MM|-}}, they are weak to light. However, this only stuns them instead of killing them. Link can use the Mirror Shield or [[Medli]]'s [[Rito Harp|harp]] to reflect light onto them, allowing him to quickly attack before they become mobile again. It is possible to interrupt their paralyzing scream if Link times a [[Jump Attack]] properly or throws [[Bomb#The Wind Waker|Bomb]]s near them ahead of time. ReDeads are invulnerable to items like the [[Boomerang#The Wind Waker|Boomerang]], [[Hookshot#The Wind Waker|Hookshot]], and normal [[Arrow]]s. Like most other enemies in the game, however, they can be instantly killed by [[Light Arrow|Light Arrows]].

In the Earth Temple, ReDeads do not attack or acknowledge Medli even when Link is controlling her with the [[Command Melody]].

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, ReDeads<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TFH|publisher= Prima|page= 164}}</ref> appear in the [[Lone Labyrinth]] and [[Grim Temple]] of [[The Ruins]]. They appear and behave slightly differently from their past incarnations, as their clay-like bodies are partially melted to the ground, which somewhat resembles [[Dead Hand]], but become fully bodied when stunned. They also wear bright, blue masks. They are able to dissolve their bodies into a puddle to quickly move around the area. When one of the [[Links]] draws near, they emit a petrifying cry, which travels in a shock wave radius. Any of the Links caught in this shock wave will become petrified to the ReDead's scream. The monster will then approach the nearest Link and proceed to grab and repeatedly bite him, which will drain the Links' shared health. The other Links can come to their teammate's rescue by attacking the ReDead, which will free the trapped Link.

Under most circumstances, ReDeads can only be attacked once they are stunned by an item, such as the [[Gust Jar]] or the [[Gripshot]]. This allows the Links to attack them during this brief period. They can also be attacked from afar with the [[Fire Gloves]].

==={{SSB|-}} series===
===={{SSBM|-}}====
[[File:SSBM ReDead.jpg|thumb|200px|Official screenshot of Link fighting two ReDeads in {{SSBM|-}}]]
In {{SSBM}}, ReDeads appear mainly as enemies in the [[Underground Maze]] in Adventure Mode. At first, a ReDead limps around, but upon noticing a fighter, it moves after them. If a ReDead latches on to a fighter, they damage them increasingly. The player can free their fighter by mashing buttons on the [[Nintendo GameCube Controller]]. If attacked for long enough, ReDeads lie on the ground idly, and while a ReDead is in this state, a fighter can defeat them using further attacks. However, if left in this state for long enough, a ReDead eventually rises again. ReDeads that are killed may sometimes drop a random [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|trophy]]. They can be knocked out, netting 800 points from the ReDead KO {{ssb|List of Bonuses|bonus}}.

In Versus Mode, a ReDead can rarely appear from [[smashwiki:Party Ball|Party Balls]], [[smashwiki:Crate|Crates]], and other types of containers. These ReDeads are a darker brown color than in Adventure, but are not behaviorally different from their lighter counterpart.

According to their [[Trophies in Super Smash Bros. Melee|Trophy]] in {{SSBM|-}}, ReDeads from {{OoT|-}} are not actually undead people who had inhabited [[Hyrule Castle Town]] at one point, but ghastly forms created by magic.<ref>{{Cite|At first, Link was shocked at the state of the town, and the apparent lack of survivors. He later learned that the townsfolk had evacuated to safety, and that the ReDeads were just magic animated into hideous humanoid shapes.|Trophy|SSBM}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* In the Japanese releases, ReDeads are known as '''ReDeat'''.
* As revealed in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', like [[Gibdo|Gibdos]], ReDeads have the ability to speak if [[Link]] talks to them wearing the [[Gibdo Mask]].
* The [[Dead Hand]], a [[Sub-Boss]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', is similar to ReDeads (and Gibdos) in that it is a zombie-like enemy capable of slowly walking, grabbing, and biting Link to inflict damage.
* The infamous scream of the ReDead in the [[Nintendo 64]] installments (''Ocarina of Time'' and ''Majora's Mask'') is an edited, slightly shorted version of a [https://soundeffects.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Ideas,_HUMAN,_SCREAM_-_SCREAM:_FEMALE,_HORROR_03 stock sound effect] from The General Series 6000 Sound Effects Library.
* Comically, in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'', when [[Medli]] is following Link and a ReDead sees him and unleashes its paralyzing scream as he was running, she will continue to move forward in a straight line until Link regains control again.
** In the same game, when a [[Stalfos]] is present with this enemy, it is possible for that foe to easily defeat a ReDead when it attempts to attack Link with its spiked club but is within reach of it, especially when the Stalfos is performing its spinning swing, striking it repeatedly.
