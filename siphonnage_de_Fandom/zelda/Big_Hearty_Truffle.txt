==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Big Hearty Truffle}}
{{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle}} are a more rare yet more effective version of the regular {{Term|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}. Though they were once regular {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}, being left unpicked for years and has caused them to grow larger.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be found growing at the base of trees in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}.

Like all hearty-type foods, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle}} can be {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to restore {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and increase his maximum health, though it increases the heart count more than a regular Hearty Truffle.<ref name="Inventory"/> It can be eaten for three {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, more than the normal {{Term|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}. {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle}} exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} become {{Plural|BotW|Toasted Big Hearty Truffle|link}}. They can be used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} to create {{Term|BotW|Black Dye|link}}.
