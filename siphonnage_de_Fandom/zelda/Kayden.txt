{{Term/Store|Kayden||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kayden|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kayden}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} who runs the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. He is married to his childhood friend, {{Term|BotW|Kodah|link}}, and the two of them have a daughter, {{Term|BotW|Finley|link}}, together.<ref name="Kodah"/>

When {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} is first approached by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, he will recognize him from his visits to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Wait... It can't be... Y-you're Master Link! From 100 years ago!|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> However, he does not stay on the topic for long, and he will return to his duties tending to the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* Sorry. Back to work I go.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} has been appeased, {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} where he has been for the past 100 years.<ref>{{Cite|Not to be rude by assaulting a customer with questions, but... Where have you been all this time?!|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> He will also begin to inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} about of his marriage to {{Term|BotW|Kodah}}, but he cuts himself off to remain professional.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that in the last 100 years...my childhood friend, Kodah, and I...|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> He appears to be relieved that the {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} has ended.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, I can finally see a break in the weather. Now then...|Kayden|BotW}}</ref>

Through the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn}}, {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} offers a place for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to rest.<ref>{{Cite|We're always happy to provide some sound sleep to weary travelers. Welcome to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Seabed Inn}}! Would you like to rest here?|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn}} is capable of providing a regular {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Water|link}} {{Term|BotW|Bed}} for guests to sleep in.<ref>{{Cite|Our normal bed will cost you {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}. Our blissful water bed is {{Color|BotW Red|80 rupees}}.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Water}} {{Term|BotW|Bed}}, {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} explains that it is a {{Term|BotW|Bed}} made of {{Term|BotW|Water}}, which makes it cool and comfortable.<ref>{{Cite|[Water bed?] You wish to know about our water bed? Well, as the name implies...it is a bed filled with water. It conforms to the shape of your body, so it's like being wrapped up in a cocoon as you sleep. Very calming. Oh, and its incredible cooling effect promises a level of sleeping comfort you simply can't find elsewhere. Perhaps you would like to try it?|Kayden|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to stay at the {{Term|BotW|Seabed Inn|display=Inn}}, {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} asks him what time he wants to wake up.<ref>{{Cite|How long would you like to sleep?|Kayden|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|How long would you like to sleep on the blissful water bed?|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses the time, {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} tells him to sleep well.<ref>{{Cite|OK then. Please sleep well until morning...|Kayden|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK then. Please sleep well until noon...|Kayden|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK then. Please sleep well until nightfall...|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} wakes up, {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} notes that he appears to have had a good sleep.<ref>{{Cite|Good morning. You look like you slept well.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good afternoon. You look like you slept well.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You seem to hae slept well. It's nighttime now.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} utilized the {{Term|BotW|Water}} {{Term|BotW|Bed}}, {{Term|BotW|Kayden}} will also add in the hope that he enjoyed it.<ref>{{Cite|Good morning. I hope you enjoyed the blissful water bed.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You appear to have slept well. I hope you enjoyed the blissful water bed.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I see you slept soundly. I hope you enjoyed the blissful water bed.|Kayden|BotW}}</ref>
