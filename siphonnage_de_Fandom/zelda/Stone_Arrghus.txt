{{Term/Store|Stone Arrghus||Series, FSA}} is the {{Term|FSA|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|FSA|Eastern Temple|link}} in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 207}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Stone Arrghus}} was referred to as '''Stone Arrghuss''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Stone Arrghuss}}<br/>Link encountered Stone Arrghuss while exploring the Eastern Temple. This large one-eyed creature was made up entirely of rocks.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311084700/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/s.jsp#StoneArrghuss|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Term|FSA|Stone Arrghus}} appears covered by small {{Plural|FSA|Rock|link}}. In order to attack it with the {{Term|FSA|Sword|link}}, the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} must use the {{Term|FSA|Boomerang|link}} to pull the {{Plural|FSA|Rock}} off. Once all of them have been destroyed, {{Term|FSA|Stone Arrghus}} will start attacking the {{Plural|FSA|Links}}. When defeated, it disintegrates and releases the yellow {{Term|FSA|Maiden|link}}.
