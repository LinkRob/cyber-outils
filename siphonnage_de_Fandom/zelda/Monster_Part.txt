{{Term/Store|Monster Part|Monster Parts|Series, BotW|plural}} are a recurring class of {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can cook up an elixir by mixing a {{Color|BotW Blue|bug part}} and a {{Color|BotW Blue|monster part}} together. Elixirs can be made in pots, just like food, and with valuable ingredients, the elixir's effect will get stronger! You can also add {{Color|BotW Blue|acorns}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|Chickaloo tree nuts}} to impact your elixir with healing properties. However... I Aurie Taamu, am deathly afraid of insects and would never even think of touching them. Fortunately, there are regular meals that you can cook which will produce the same effects as elixirs.|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 1|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Material|link}} dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} when they are defeated.<ref name="Piggyback 23">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|page= 23|publisher= Piggyback|edition= Expanded Edition|year= 2018}}</ref> Higher-ranking {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} can be dropped by higher-ranking {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}}, where {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Horn|link}} can be dropped by all {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}, but {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Guts|link}} will only be dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} or higher. {{Plural|BotW|Material}} dropped from {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} that can deal damage to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} are not considered {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} (i.e. {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Maraudo Wolf|link}}).

{{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} can be sold in {{Plural|BotW|Shop|link}} and to traveling merchants all around {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} for varying prices; rarer {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} will sell for higher amounts of {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}}, the proprietor of the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}, accepts {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} in exchange for his currency, {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}.

===={{Term|BotW|Armor}} Upgrades====
Several {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} upgrades also require {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part}} in order to be completed by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}.
