{{Term/Store|Missing in Action||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Missing in Action}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can begin this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by talking to {{Term|BotW|Sesami|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}}. He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has seen his friends, four travelers named {{Term|BotW|Oliff|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Flaxel|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Canolo|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Palme|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, did you happen to see any of my friends? They're four travelers named {{Color|BotW Blue|Oliff}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|Flaxel}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|Canolo}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|Palme}}...|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> They were traveling along the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path|link}} when they were ambushed by {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We were all walking together when some monsters suddenly jumped us near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Koukot Plateau path}}.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} ended up running all the way to the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}, but when he turned around, he realized his friends were not following him.<ref>{{Cite|I took off running and didn't stop until I got here. I thought my friends were  right behind me, but when I turned around, they were nowhere to be found.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> He is concerned that they may have been harmed by monsters,<ref>{{Cite|You don't think the monsters could have... No, they're fine. They'll be back any second now.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> and so he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to direct his friends to the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} if he sees them.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, if you happen to run into any of my friends on the road, please let them know Sesami is waiting for them here.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Sesami}}'s friends along the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path}}. Each one is being attacked by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, and they must be saved before {{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to them. After talking to each of them, they will join {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} at {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}.

{{Term|BotW|Oliff}} is found fighting two {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin}} on the scaffolding near {{Term|BotW|Joloo Nah Shrine|link}}, along {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves him, he will praise him for his fighting.<ref>{{Cite|Wh-what wonderful form! I've never seen anyone fight like that!|Oliff|BotW}}</ref> He introduces himself, and while he claims to be a decent fighter, he was caught off guard by the monsters.<ref>{{Cite|My name is Oliff. I'm a decent fighter, but I was caught off guard while travelling with my friends...|Oliff|BotW}}</ref> Since he was outnumbered, he thought it would be unwise to challenge them.<ref>{{Cite|The monsters vastly outnumbered us, so I thought it unwise to challenge them.|Oliff|BotW}}</ref> He is grateful that {{Term|BotW|Link}} showed up when he did, as he does not like to think of what would have happened to him.<ref>{{Cite|I hate to think what would've happened had you not shown up. By the Goddess, I'm grateful...|Oliff|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Oliff}} is glad to hear that {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} is safe at {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}, at which point he goes to join him.<ref>{{Cite|Sesami is waiting for me? Ah, I'm glad to hear he's safe. I shall hurry off to the Gerudo Canyon Stable where he waits.|Oliff|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Canolo}} can be found on the scaffolding that bridges {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau}} and {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru}}. She is not sure at first that she has been saved,<ref>{{Cite|Wait...<br/>Am I...saved?|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> but when she hears that {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} is waiting at {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}, she immediately expresses anger toward him.<ref>{{Cite|At least I'm safe now. No thanks to Sesami.|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> She blames him for running away as soon as the monsters attacked them, abandoning his friends in the process.<ref>{{Cite|As soon as the monsters showed up, he ran away as fast as he could and left us for dead. Who does that? Especially to a girl!|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> Before leaving for the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, she claims that she will not forgive him for this.<ref>{{Cite|He's at Gerudo Canyon Stable? If he thinks I'm going to forgive him for this, he's got another thing coming!|Canolo|BotW}}</ref>

On the southeastern edge of {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Flaxel}} fighting two {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin}}. Upon rescuing {{Term|BotW|Flaxel}}, she expresses her gratitude for his help,<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for the assist, pal. That could've been the end of me.|Flaxel|BotW}}</ref> though she admits that she will be sore the next day.<ref>{{Cite|Boy, I'm gonna be sore tomorrow. Filthy bottom-feeders...|Flaxel|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Flaxel}} hears that {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} has been at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}} instead of helping her,<ref>{{Cite|So Sesami was kicking his feet up in the stable while I was being tortured by those sorry sacks?!|Flaxel|BotW}}</ref> she decides to go there and give him a piece of her mind.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I'm going to go give him a piece of my mind, all right. A piece of my fist too!|Flaxel|BotW}}</ref>

Finally, {{Term|BotW|Palme}} is found under attack by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin}} near the arch between {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau}} and {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru}}. At first, he is shocked to see that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to defeat his assailants.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? I'm alive? But...the monsters... Did you get rid of them?|Palme|BotW}}</ref> He calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} incredible and says that he may be braver than {{Term|BotW|Sesami}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's incredible! YOU'RE incredible! You might even be braver than Sesami!|Palme|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Palme}} is surprised when he hears that {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} stayed at the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}} instead of coming to save him.<ref>{{Cite|Sesami...is waiting at the stable? He didn't come to save me?|Palme|BotW}}</ref> He wonders whether {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} lied to him about his mastery of the martial arts, but quickly decides that it must have been a misunderstanding.<ref>{{Cite|But he said he'd mastered the martial arts. Did he...lie to me about that? Nah...this must be a misunderstanding.|Palme|BotW}}</ref>

After rescuing all four of {{Term|BotW|Sesami}}'s friends, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}. {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} tells him that he is glad to see his friends are safe, and he thanks him by rewarding him with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As soon as they all got back safely, a wave of emotion washed over me. Thank you. Thank you so much. I want you to have this.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> This event helped him realize the importance of friendship,<ref>{{Cite|In a way, I'm kind of glad all of this happened. It's helped me realize just how important friendship is.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> and he expresses that while opening up to others makes a person more vulnerable, it is the only way to give life value.<ref>{{Cite|Opening up to others might make you more vulnerable, but it's also the only way to give your life any value.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> In a comical twist, {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} bluntly reveals that he and his friends will be parting ways because caring for others is an emotional liability.<ref>{{Cite|That said, caring for others is an emotional liability, so my friends and I have decided to part ways.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> In reality, {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} has no idea what lesson to take away.<ref>{{Cite|Not really sure what the takeaway is here...|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

==Trivia==
* All of the characters involved in this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} are named after types of {{Wp|Vegetable oil}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} is named after {{Wp|Sesame oil}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Oliff}} is named after {{Wp|Olive oil}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Canolo}} is named after {{Wp|Canola oil}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Flaxel}} is named after {{Wp|Flaxseed oil}}.
** {{Term|BotW|Palme}} is named after {{Wp|Palm oil}}.
