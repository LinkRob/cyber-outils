The {{Term/Store|Veteran Thief||Series}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref|ALBW}}

==Biography==
The Veteran Thief is encountered inside his house in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}. He is friendlier than most of the townspeople, greeting [[Link#A Link Between Worlds|Link]] kindly, and telling him about the song he sings when spoken to.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Hey there, little guy! Sorry. I was just singing out loud. It's an old tune. You probably wouldn't know it.|Veteran Thief|ALBW}}</ref> His tendency to sing in front of complete strangers is actually to the hero's advantage, however, as his song contains part of the rhyme required to enter the [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|Thieves' Hideout]].<ref>{{Cite|♪ Many years ago, I felt so free... Thought I chose the thief's life, but it really chose me. ♪ Every time I say I'm a-leavin', this accursed life sends me right back to thievin'!|Veteran Thief|ALBW}}</ref>

He also comments that Thieves' Town may be too rough for the young hero.<ref>{{Cite|Waddya think of this place? Little too rough for ya, eh?|Veteran Thief|ALBW}}</ref> He however, states that he does not mind the rough lifestyle of the settlement, as there remains a strong traditional emphasis on music in their culture.<ref>{{Cite|I don't mind it so much. At least there's still a strong musical culture in this town. It's a tradition with us!|Veteran Thief|ALBW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Veteran Thief's house corresponds to the same location as the {{Term|ALBW|Bee Guy|link}}'s house in [[Hyrule]]. 
*Two paintings of a [[Rupee]] and a [[Heart]] can be found in the Veteran Thief's house. Link can [[Wall Merge|merge]] behind the Rupee painting to receive 1 Rupee; however, the painting of the Heart cannot be reached as it is above his height.
