==Location and uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the Large Bomb Bag is an upgrade to the original [[Bomb Bag]] that increases its carrying capacity from 20 [[Bomb]]s to 30. There are two ways to obtain the Large Bomb Bag. One way involves entering [[Goron City]], as child [[Link]], and using a Bomb to stop the [[Hot Rodder Goron]]'s rolling while the Goron is inside the northern alcove. He will reward Link for his "courage" with the Large Bomb Bag.<ref>{{Cite|All right! I'll give you this in honor of your courage!|Hot Rodder Goron|OoT3D}}</ref> Alternatively, the Large Bomb Bag can be won from the [[Bombchu Bowling Alley]] if it is the second price.

Whichever upgrade is acquired second will become the [[Giant Bomb Bag]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the location of the Large Bomb Bag depends on whether or not Link saves the [[Old Lady from Bomb Shop]] from [[Sakon]]'s attack in [[North Clock Town]] on the night of the [[First Day]]. If the Old Lady is saved, the Large Bomb Bag can then be purchased from the [[Bomb Shop]] for 90 [[Rupee]]s. Otherwise, it will become available at the [[Curiosity Shop]] for 100 Rupees on the [[Final Day]].

Although the Large Bomb Bag acts as an upgrade to the regular [[Bomb Bag]], increasing its capacity from 20 Bombs to 30, it can be purchased without owning the Bomb Bag first.

==={{OoS|-}}===
The Large Bomb Bag in {{OoS}} can be purchased from [[Subrosia Market]] for 50 [[Ore Chunk]]s and 10 [[Bomb]]s. Despite its name, the Large Bomb Bag is actually just a red Bomb that explodes all around Link as he holds it, magically increasing his Bomb-carrying capacity by 20.

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, the Large Bomb Bag is an upgrade given to Link by a [[Fairy]] in present-day [[Talus Peaks]], after he throws a Bomb into her pond and then answers her question truthfully.<ref>{{Cite|You are an honest person. I'll increase the number of {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}} you can carry to reward you. Hee-yah!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> The upgrade is represented by several Bombs exploding all around Link, magically increasing his Bomb-carrying capacity by 20. The Fairy will also fill his Bomb Bag; however, it is unclear if this refers to an already-existing Bomb Bag, or one given to him by the Fairy.<ref>{{Cite|You can now carry 30 {{Color|OoA Red|Bombs}}. And I've filled your bag.|Fairy|OoA}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, Link can obtain three Large Bomb Bags in any order. Each one upgrades the Bomb-carrying capacity of his [[Bomb Bag]]; the first one to 30, the second to 50, and the third to 99. They can be found in the following locations:

#Purchased at [[Stockwell's Shop]] for 600 [[Rupee]]s (not available in the European version).
#Given to Link by the [[Great Mayfly Fairy]], after he throws a Bomb into her [[Great Fairy's Fountain|Fountain]] and responds to her question truthfully.
#Inside a [[Treasure Chest]] in the [[Wind Ruins]] that appears after [[Kinstone Fusion|fusing]] [[Kinstone]]s with [[Belari]].

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Link can obtain three [[Bomb Bag]]s in any order. They are found at the following locations:
#Purchased at [[Beedle's Air Shop]] for 500 [[Rupee]]s.
#Rewarded for beating the current record in the [[Whip Race]] mini-game in [[Whittleton]].
#Rewarded for completing Level 2 of the [[Take 'Em All On!]] mini-game.

Whichever Bomb Bag is the third to be collected will become the Large Bomb Bag, in which Link can carry up to thirty Bombs.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, a Large Bomb Bag is a supplemental Bomb Bag that increases [[Link]]'s [[Bomb]]-holding capacity by 15, as long as the Large Bomb Bag is stored in the [[Adventure Pouch]].

A Large Bomb Bag can be created by [[Upgrading]] a [[Medium Bomb Bag]] at the [[Scrap Shop]], at a cost of three [[Lizard Tail]], four [[Jelly Blob]]s, two [[Bee Larva|Hornet Larvae]], one [[Golden Skull]], and 100 [[Rupee]]s. They can also be sold at the [[Item Check]] for 150 Rupees.
