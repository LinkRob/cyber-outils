The {{Term/Store|Shovel|Shovels|Series, ALttP, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA, FSA, PH, CoH}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, LA, OoS, OoA, FSA, PH}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138{{Exp Game|ALttP, LA, OoS, OoA, FSA, PH}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Deluxe Shovel''',{{Exp Game|LA}}<ref>{{Cite|Deluxe Shovel<br/>'''200''' Rupees!|Town Tool Shopkeeper|LADX}}</ref> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is used to dig up treasures and, occasionally, even enemies from beneath the soil. The Shovel may also be used to unearth secret holes that lead to otherwise unreachable passages.

==Appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Shovel|Use this to dig holes in the ground. You cannot dig in some surfaces. You need this shovel to find the Flute.}}
In {{ALttP}}, the Shovel is given to Link by the [[Flute Boy]] in the [[Haunted Grove]].<ref>{{Cite|Then I will lend you my shovel. Good luck!|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> Its primary use is to find his hidden [[Flute]].<ref>{{Cite|Before I came here I buried my flute there, along with some flower seeds. Will you try to find it for me?|Flute Boy|ALttP}}</ref> After finding the Flute, it will replace the Shovel. In the [[Dark World]]'s [[Digging Game]] located southwest of the [[Village of Outcasts]], the game keeper will lend Link a Shovel usable only for the duration of the [[Mini-Game]].<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to the treasure field. The object is to dig as many holes as you can in 30 seconds. Any treasures you dig up will be yours to keep.|Digging Game Keeper|ALttP}}</ref>

In the [[Game Boy Advance]] [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|version]], the Shovel has its own item slot and is thus usable throughout the game.

In either case, using the Shovel on a tile of plain land will dig a hole. Most of the time, the hole contains nothing, but occasionally, Link may find rupees and hearts. The Digging Game even contains a Piece of Heart which can be dug up from random locations within the game area. Using the Shovel on a tile impossible to dig, or a hole already dug, will make a "hard object" sound, indicating that there is nothing to dig.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Shovel|The Shovel is useful for digging holes in the ground. Who knows what kind of buried treasure you might find? Keep in mind that some parts of the ground are too hard to dig.}}
The Shovel can be purchased from the [[Town Tool Shop]] in [[Mabe Village]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s in {{LA}}. It is used to find the [[Slime Key]] at the end of the [[Pothole Field]]. It can also be used to find some of the [[Secret Seashell]]s which are scattered across [[Koholint Island]]. It, among other items, can also be traded at the end of the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}} to obtain the [[Boomerang]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Shovel|Use the Shovel to dig holes in the ground or to clear away snow. You might be able to find buried treasure, so dig holes in many different places.}}
{{Stub|OoS}}
The Shovel is required to find the Star-Shaped [[Ore]] needed to enter the Summer Tower of the [[Temple of Seasons]] in {{OoS|-}}. Link can also dig up enemies such as [[Podoboo]]s.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Shovel|Use the Shovel to dig holes in the ground or to clear away snow. You might be able to find buried treasure, so dig holes in many different places.}}
In {{OoA}}, Link obtains the Shovel from a worker inside the [[Black Tower]]. He can use it to remove dirt piles. In {{OoS}}, Link obtains the Shovel from [[Holly]] after entering her home from the chimney. He can use it to remove the snow around her house.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Manual|FSA|Shovel|Dig in soft ground to find items.<br/>{{Big|Lev. 2}}<br/>If you listen carefully, you will hear a sound around areas with Force Gems or areas where there is a covered hole.}}
In {{FSA}}, the Shovel can be upgraded to the Level 2 Shovel by throwing it to a [[Great Fairy]]. The upgraded Shovel differs from the original in that it chimes whenever Link stands over treasure, a hidden passage, or other hidden secrets.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} can find the {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer|link}}'s Shovel in the man's hideaway on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|PH Red|shovel}}! Tap a spot to dig there. Dig in suspicious places.|N/A|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This must be the place that {{Color|PH Blue|Romanos}}'s dad called his hideaway. But I haven't seen any clues about how to get through the foggy passage!|Ciela|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Isn't that my husband's shovel? That's OK, young man. You found it, so you should keep it. What? A second hideaway? Well, come to think of it, he often went off with that shovel. He would mutter something about four spots...and an intersection of some sort?|Maronie|PH}}</ref> It is mostly used to uncover hidden passages. It can also be used to unearth special, but not always marked, digging spots containing valuable treasures such as big Rupees or treasure maps.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page=12}}</ref> It can also unearth [[Updraft]]s that, when unblocked, can elevate Link to higher elevations.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page=15}}</ref> The Shovel is also used in the Digging Game on [[Harrow Island]], where Link can dig up ten treasures for the price of 50 Rupees.

==={{FPTRR|-}}===
[[File:FPTRR Shovel Sprite.png|left]]
In {{FPTRR}}, [[Tingle]] can buy the Shovel from [[Duke]] in the [[Lon Lon Meadow]]. When equipped, the weight of the Shovel will cause Tingle to walk slower. To dig, Tingle must be tapped on the touch screen. Only soft ground can be dug. It can be used to dig up multiple items, such as [[Ingredient]]s, [[Treasure Chest]]s, and [[Rupee Good]]s.

==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Plural|CoH|Shovel}} are equipment that allow {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} to dig through walls and mounds of dirt within {{CoH}}, revealing {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} and opening up new pathways in order to navigate their way through dungeons.<ref>{{Cite|Allows you to dig through walls. It's fragile and not that special.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> However, they have a limited lifespan as using them to dig costs durability, and using up a {{Term|CoH|Shovel}}'s durability completely will cause it to wear out and disappear from the hero's inventory. The durability of a {{Term|CoH|Shovel}} can be preserved indefinitely with the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation|link}} when it is equipped.

There are different types of {{Plural|CoH|Shovel}} with their own special abilities, including the {{Term|CoH|Auto-shovel|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Battle Shovel|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Glass Shovel|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Shovel of Courage|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Shovel of Stamina|link}}.

The basic {{Term|CoH|Shovel}} can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} for 10 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} and slain enemies, though a free {{Term|CoH|Shovel}} will always appear in the cave next to {{Term|CoH|Link's House|link}}, having originally been left behind by {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} after waking {{Term|CoH|Link}} up from his enchanted slumber.
