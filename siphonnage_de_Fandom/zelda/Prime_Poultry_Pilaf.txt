{{Term/Store|Prime Poultry Pilaf|Prime Poultry Pilafs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Prime Poultry Pilafs are meals that are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Raw Bird Thigh]], [[Hylian Rice]], a [[Bird Egg]], and [[Goat Butter]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Eating them will heal some of the {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These pilafs are a favorite in the region of [[Gerudo (Region)|Gerudo]], and the rice used permeates the savory taste of the poultry.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add a fifth ingredient to the Cooking Pot when making Prime Poultry Pilaf, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hearty ingredient. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an energizing ingredient. Consuming them will restore part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an enduring ingredient. These meals will completely replenish and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hasty ingredient. Link can eat them to have his movement speed increased.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a spicy ingredient. Eating these pilafs will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an electric ingredient. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a mighty ingredient. Link's attack power will be boosted when he eats these pilafs.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a tough ingredient. They will grant Link a boost to his defense if he consumes them.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a sneaky ingredient. When eaten, they will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Prime Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Prime Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The rice permeates the savory taste of the poultry in this Gerudo-region favorite.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a chilly ingredient. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
