{{Term/Store|Zelkoa Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Zelkoa Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Zelkoa Pond}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, south of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Zelkoa Pond}} is small pond that feeds into a creek running through {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}. 

Next to the pond, a large {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} grows tall. On the top of this {{Term|BotW|Tree}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} is hiding under a rock. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} lifts this rock, the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and gift {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You Found Me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
