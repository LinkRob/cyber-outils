'''''Captain Rainbow''''' is a game for the [[Wii]]. It features various [[Nintendo]] characters, including [[Crazy Tracy]] from {{LA}}. [[Link]] (from {{TP}}) and {{smw|Mario}} can be seen in photos above her throne. Captain Rainbow also makes an appearance in ''[[Ripened Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love|Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love]]'', as both a show on TV and a dungeon in DunJon.

==Characters Appearing in Captain Rainbow==
*Nick a.k.a. Captain Rainbow
*Birdo (Catherine) from ''Super Mario Bros. 2''
*Hikari from ''Shin Onigashima''
*Ossan from ''Golf''
*Little Mac from ''Mike Tyson's Punch-Out!!''
*Mappo from ''GiFTPiA''
*[[Piranha Plant]]s from ''Super Mario Bros.''
*Takamaru from ''The Mysterious Murasame Castle''
*[[Crazy Tracy]] from {{LA}}
*Famicom Wars Soldiers from ''Famicom Wars''
*Tao from ''Chibi-Robo'' and ''GiFTPiA''
*Devil from ''Devil World''
*Lip from ''Panel de Pon''
