{{Term/Store|Zelda's Letter|Zelda's Letters|Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} receives {{Term|OoT3D|Zelda's Letter}} from the {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Zelda|link|display=Princess}} herself in the {{Term|OoT3D|Castle Courtyard|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|One more thing... Take this letter... I'm sure it will be helpful to you.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Zelda's Letter}} is required to make the {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper|link}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}} open the gate that leads to the {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain Trail|link}}. After {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} gives the {{Term|OoT3D|Zelda's Letter|display=Letter}} to the {{Term|OoT3D|Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper|display=Gatekeeper}}, he will open the gate, which will remain open for the rest of the game.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, this is... This is surely Princess Zelda's handwriting! Well, let's see... Hmmm... OK... "This is Link... He is under my orders to save Hyrule." Wah ha ha ha hah! What kind of funny game has our Princess come up with now?! OK, OK, all right.  You can go now... Just be careful, Mr. Hero! Wah hah ha ha hah!|Death Mountain Trail Gatekeeper|OoT}}</ref>
