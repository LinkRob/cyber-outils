"{{Term/Store|A Wife Washed Away||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Wife Washed Away}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After appeasing {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Fronk|link}} speaking to himself.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|They say {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has calmed down, and yet...}}|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} interrupts his thoughts, which causes {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} to apologize.<ref>{{Cite|[Go on...] Ah! It is you, Link! I am sorry... I was talking to myself.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks him what is wrong, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} reveals that his wife {{Term|BotW|Mei|link}} went out fishing after {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|display=Vah Ruta's}} rampage started,<ref>{{Cite|[What's wrong?] The truth is... My wife Mei left and hasn't come back yet. After the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}} started rampaging, she went out to go fishing like everything was normal.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> but even after everything has settled, she has not returned.<ref>{{Cite|And now {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} has settled down, but she still hasn't returned...|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} fears the worst and laments not stopping {{Term|BotW|Mei}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid the worst has happened... If only I had stopped her from leaving! Oh, what do I do?!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to go find {{Term|BotW|Mei}}, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} is grateful and asks him to bring her home.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll find her!] Link... You saved Zora's Domain, and now you're going to help me find my Mei, as well? You are a true Champion! I am sorry to put this burden on you, but please... Please bring her home!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} where he thinks {{Term|BotW|Mei}} could be, who then reveals she went fishing in {{Term|BotW|Zora River|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Where could she be?] The day {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}} started raging, she said she was going fishing in {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}}.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> Due to the river swelling, she may have been swept downstream.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}'s rage caused the river to swell. She may have been swept downstream!|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Ledo|link}} and the others did not see her when they went searching for a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fronk}} believes she may be beyond the river.<ref>{{Cite|However, if Ledo's group didn't see anything, maybe she passed through {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora River}} and is beyond there...|Fronk|BotW}}</ref> In the worst case scenario, {{Term|BotW|Mei}} could be in {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Worst case scenario...she could have been carried all the way to {{Color|BotW Red|Lake Hylia}}.|Fronk|BotW}}</ref>

On one of the small islets surrounding {{Term|BotW|Hylia Island|link}} within {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Mei}}. She will either be swimming in the water or walking around on land, singing about her catch to herself.<ref>{{Cite|♪Big catch, big catch...♪|Mei|BotW}}</ref> If he speaks to her, she will remark that she has caught many fish that day.<ref>{{Cite|We got a big haul today! Fishing is so much fun!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks her if she knows {{Term|BotW|Fronk}}, which causes her to realize she forgot to return home.<ref>{{Cite|[Do you know Fronk?] Hm? Fronk? Yes, he's my darling. My love! Why, is something wrong? ... ... {{Big|WAAAAAAH!!}} My darling! I'm sooooorry! I completely forgoooooot! I got carried awaaaaay!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> In her rush to return to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} her catch, which is five {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I-I-I've gotta get back home right away! Here, you can have these!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has them, she returns to her home.<ref>{{Cite|OK! Now they're all yours. Now then... Time for me to go home to {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora's Domain}}! See ya!|Mei|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
