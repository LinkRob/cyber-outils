{{Term/Store|''Misko's EX Journal 2''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Misko's EX Journal 2|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} that was written by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}} and can only be found after {{TCB}} DLC Pack has been purchased and downloaded. It can be found underneath the wooden wreckage of the eastern part of {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Book}} is important for several {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} that become available when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} leaves the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. It is needed to progress through "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Merchant Hood|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was a traveling merchant's hood.<br/><br/>Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Garb of Winds|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was the blue garb of the Hero of Winds.<br/><br/>Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Usurper King|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was the helmet of the Usurper King.<br/><br/>Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> and "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Dark Armor|link}}".<ref>{{Cite|The royal family of Hyrule collected historically significant outfits, but they were stolen by a bandit named Misko. One of the items stolen was armor belonging to a dark entity.<br/><br/>Somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Red|Deya Village Ruins}} is a journal that places the location of this treasure...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> At first, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will question the newly-found {{Term|BotW|Book}}, asking himself if he has seen it here before.<ref>{{Cite|Was this book always here?|Link|BotW}}</ref> When read, it tells of {{Term|BotW|Misko}} stealing the six treasures from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} and scattering them throughout the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}, the locations of which are also written:<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref>
