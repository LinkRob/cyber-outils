The {{Term/Store|Milk Bar Owner||Series, ALBW}} is the name of two characters in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=300 }}</ref> They run the [[Milk Bar]]s in their respective worlds of [[Hyrule]] and [[Lorule]]. 

==Biography==
===Hyrule===
The Hyrulean Milk Bar Owner is the proprietor of the Milk Bar in [[Kakariko Village]]. When [[Link]] first speaks to the Milk Bar Owner, he provides the young hero with gossip about the town.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone in the village is talking about the castle, you know. And how the elder left his house for the first time in forever! Who knew Sahasrahla could walk so far! Strange days, people are saying. And they're probably about to get even stranger!|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref> He then offers to sell Link some fresh, chilled [[Milk]].<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! We just got some fresh milk, already chilled!|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref>

After Link obtains the [[Letter in a Bottle]] in [[Lake Hylia]], the Milk Bar Owner will be surprised that he knows about [[Premium Milk]]. Once Link explains that the letter is from a man on [[Death Mountain]], he will give the young hero a glass of it in exchange for the letter and ask that he give it to the [[Bouldering Guy]] up on Death Mountain,<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! Care for an icy-cold glass of--! Whuh? You heard about premium milk? How'd you find out about the good stuff? I see... A message in a bottle. And from someone stranded up on the mountain? Could only be one guy. He's a tricky customer. Sorry to ask, but could you take this premium milk to him? I bet it would help him heal up in no time.|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref> who is a regular customer.<ref>{{Cite|He's a regular at my establishment who loves exploring the mountain. Last time he was in here, I think he said his next expedition would take him east of the Tower of Hera. I can't afford to lose his business. Get that to him, OK? I'm really counting on you, friend.|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref> When Link returns to the Milk Bar after delivering the Premium Milk, he thanks the young hero and offers him a half-price discount on all Milk purchases.<ref>{{Cite|You delivered it safe and sound? Thanks a bunch. I don't know how I can ever repay you... How about this? From now on, you only need to pay half price for milk. You're my favorite customer now!|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome, young sir! Care for a glass of milk? Special deal, only 10 Rupees.|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref>

===Lorule===
The Lorulean Milk Bar Owner runs the Milk Bar in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}. Unlike his Hyrulean counterpart, he is extremely unfriendly, telling any potential patrons to leave. He reveals to Link that he has grown more inhospitable due to the influx of customers in his bar, seeking refuge from the monsters that plague Lorule.<ref>{{Cite|Whaddya want?! Scram, brat! What with all the monsters running around, more and more folks are taking refuge in here. Customers everywhere... IT REALLY GETS MY GOAT!|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref> The Milk Bar Owner simply desires to be left alone to drink Milk in peace.<ref>{{Cite|I just want to drink a little bit of milk in peace! Why can't everyone just leave me alone and scram?!|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Hyrulean Milk Bar Owner significantly resembles [[Talon]], as well as [[Mr. Barten]], whom he may be based on. Furthermore, the Milk he sells is said to come from [[Lon Lon Ranch]].<ref>{{Cite|Restores five hearts. Straight from the noted Lon Lon Ranch.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref> The Lorulean Milk Bar Owner on the other hand greatly resembles [[Ingo]], and shares his sour disposition.
