The '''Bottle of Magic''' is an item featured in the "[[Quest for the Potion of Power]]" episode of {{CN}}. Located inside the [[Island Palace]], it is found by Game Boy after a strange creature accidentally destroys one of the palace's statues. Game Boy notices the strange bottle and Kevin picks it up in case it could be used.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Potential energy source detected.|name=Game Boy|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=A bottle of magic. Good work. This could come in handy.|name=Kevin|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref>

Later on in the episode, after Ganon has been resurrected with the [[Potion of Power]], Kevin pours the contents of the Bottle of Magic on to Link's [[Magical Shield]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote= I gotcha. The magic bottle Game Boy found. I don't know what it does, but I sure hope it works.|name=Kevin|Show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref> This gives the same effect as if the young hero had used the [[Reflect Spell]] from {{TAoL}}:<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=That reflect magic is awesome stuff!|name=Kevin|show=Captain N: The Game Master|episode=Quest for the Potion of Power}}</ref> when Ganon throws a fire beam at Link, he uses his shield to reflect the magic back at Ganon, successfully destroying the evil wizard.
