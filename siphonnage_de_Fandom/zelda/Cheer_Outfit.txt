The {{Term/Store|Cheer Outfit||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Cheer Outfit increases the energy gauges of your teammates. All for one and such and such.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}. It becomes available for purchase once the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} arrives in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, at a cost of three {{Plural|TFH|Frilly Fabric|link}}—a {{Term|TFH|Material|link}} available only from the Street Merchant—and 100 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} dresses {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} in a cheerleader's uniform consisting of a pink crop top and pleated skirt, and styles his hair into a long ponytail. In areas where {{Term|TFH|Link}} can't use his {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}, he will carry pink pom-poms instead.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit}} increases the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge|link}} by 50% for both of the wearer's [[Links|allies]],<ref>{{Cite|Increases the energy gauge of all allies by 50%. Spread the cheer!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> but has no effect on the wearer themselves. If two or more {{Plural|TFH|Links}} in a trio wear the {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit}}, then all three heroes receive a boost to their {{Plural|TFH|Energy Gauge}}. However, these boosts do not "stack"; no single {{Term|TFH|Link}} can receive an {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge}} boost greater than 50%.

In a single-player game, the {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit}} functions the same as the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gear|link}}, increasing the {{Term|TFH|Energy Gauge}} that is shared between {{Term|TFH|Link}} and his two {{Plural|TFH|Doppel|link}}.

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Cheer Outfit}}. Though the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} can appear in the {{Term|TFH|Catalog|link}}, it is impossible to collect the required {{Plural|TFH|Material}} in this mode.
