{{Term/Store|Sanctuary Mask|Sanctuary Masks|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 137"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Sanctuary Mask}} are rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Lone Labyrinth|link}}''', ''"Adventure in the dark!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sanctuary Mask}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sanctuary Mask}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Grim Temple}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Sanctuary Mask}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 2,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Sanctuary Mask}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack Attire|link}}.<ref name="E 137"/>
