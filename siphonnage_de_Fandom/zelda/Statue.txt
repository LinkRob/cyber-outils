{{Term/Store|Statue|Statues|Series|plural}} are recurring objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|TLoZ}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|game=ALttP&FS |guide=Prima |page=179 {{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref>

==Overview==
While many {{Plural|Series|Statue}} are simply decorative, some are able to be moved or destroyed by {{Term|Series|Link|link}} in a variety of ways in order to press {{Plural|Series|Switch|link}} or open new pathways. Other {{Plural|Series|Statue}} are capable of autonomous movement and serve as {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}}.

There are also a number of specific and notable {{Plural|Series|Statue}} in the series.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Carver's Statue, Gargoyle Statue}}
In the remake of {{ALttP&FS}}, three {{Plural|ALttP|Wooden Statue|link}} appear as rewards during the {{Term|ALttP|Lumberjack's Riddles|link}}. Additionally, the {{Term|ALttP|Gargoyle Statue|link}} serves as the entrance to {{Term|ALttP|Thieves' Town|link}}.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Mermaid Statue, Owl Statue}}
In {{LA}}, the {{Term|LANS|Mermaid Statue|link}} covers a {{Term|LANS|Cave|link}} containing the {{Term|LANS|Magnifying Glass|link}}. Additionally, {{Plural|LANS|Owl Statue|link}} appear throughout {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Owl Statue, Quill Statue}}
In {{MM}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Owl Statue|link}} are used in conjunction with the "{{Term|MM3D|Song of Soaring|link}}" to transport {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} across {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}, and to {{Term|MM3D|Saving|link|display=Save}}. In {{MM3D}}, similar objects called {{Plural|MM3D|Quill Statue|link}} also appear.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Main|Owl Statue}}
In {{OoS}}, {{Plural|OoS|Mystery Seed|link}} can be given to {{Plural|OoS|Owl Statue|link}} in exchange for advice on nearby puzzles.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Main|Link Statue, Owl Statue}}
In {{OoA}}, {{Plural|OoA|Mystery Seed|link}} can be given to {{Plural|OoA|Owl Statue|link}} in exchange for advice on nearby puzzles. Additionally, various {{Plural|OoA|Link Statue|link}} appear in {{Term|OoA|Lynna City|link}} after {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|OoA|Veran|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Mysterious Statue, Stone Statue}}
In {{TMC}}, there are three {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Statue|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}} with whom {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} golden {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}. Statues resembling {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|link}} appear outside {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}, with one inside being movable to access a portion of the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Underground|link}}.

A number of {{Plural|TMC|Statue}} appear in {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} to assist in solve puzzles, or to serve as decoration. In {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, there are a number of {{Plural|TMC|Stone Statue|link}} that hinder {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s progress, and can only be destroyed by reflecting their own cannonballs back at them.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Main|Horned Statuette, Link Statue, Mechanical Statue, Owl Statue, Wooden Statue}}
In {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can use the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod|link}} to control {{Plural|TPHD|Mechanical Statue|link}} and {{Plural|TPHD|Horned Statuette|link}}. {{Plural|TPHD|Statue}} are important in {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}'s quest to help {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}}; six {{Plural|TPHD|Owl Statue|link}} hold the missing characters from the {{Term|TPHD|Ancient Sky Book|link}}, and the {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Statue|link}} is an {{Term|TPHD|Item|link}} used to restore {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}}'s memory.

A {{Term|TPHD|Link Statue|link}} depicting {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} also appears at the final floor of the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Shadows|link}}.

In {{TFH}}, the {{Term|TFH|Tri Force Hero Statue|link}} allows the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} to {{Term|TFH|Saving|link|display=Save}}. The {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga|link}} also allows them to temporarily transform into a {{Term|TFH|Statue}}.
