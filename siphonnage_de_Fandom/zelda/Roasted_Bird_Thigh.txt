==Location and Uses==
Roasted Bird Thighs are the result of [[Raw Bird Thigh]]s that have made contact with [[Fire]]. Roasted Bird Thighs are said to have crisp skin and juicy meat inside.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> Black {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} can drop them upon their deaths. [[Link]] can restore two and a quarter {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} by eating a Roasted Bird Thigh.
