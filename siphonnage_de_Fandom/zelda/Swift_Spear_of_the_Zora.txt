{{Term/Store|Swift Spear of the Zora||HWAoC}} is a level 25 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Swift Spear of the Zora|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Swift Spear of the Zora}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}'s objective is to reach a Targeted Position within four minutes, which is an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} on the other side of the map. There are two {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} between {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} and the Targeted Position, both with gates around them that close when {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} enters and open when the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured. The path from {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}'s starting position to the first {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}, including the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} itself. The path from the first {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} to the second is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}} in the same way, although instead of a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} must defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} to capture the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} instead. The {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawns when {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} enters the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}. There is also a fork in the path just before the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, and if {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} goes in the other direction, she will find an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. The path from the second {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} to the Targeted Position is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}}, except for a skull {{Term|HWAoC|Monster Stronghold|link}} containing a small group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Treasure Chest|section=Skull Chest|link}}. The chest opens when all of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}} are defeated. When {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} reaches the Targeted Position, which is another {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, four {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawn and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos}} as well, and the gates around it do not close when {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} enters. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once all four {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} are defeated.
