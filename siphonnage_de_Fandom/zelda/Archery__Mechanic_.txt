{{Term/Store|Archery||Series, BotW}} is a puzzle mechanic in {{BotW}} used to reveal {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}}.<ref name="Piggyback 330">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 331 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Term|BotW|Archery (Mechanic)}} is a type of {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} puzzle that appears all across the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. It often takes the form of a lone {{Term|BotW|Balloon|link}} floating in a hard-to-reach spot, and if {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots the {{Term|BotW|Balloon}} with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear. Sometimes, a {{Term|BotW|Pinwheel|link}} will instead be found, which summons a group of {{Term|BotW|Balloon}} for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to shoot when he stands next to it.<ref name="Piggyback 330"/> Sometimes an {{Term|BotW|Acorn|link}} or other random objects are used instead of a {{Term|BotW|Balloon}}, but the shooting aspect remains the same with each.<ref name="Piggyback 330"/> Whenever {{Term|BotW|Link}} reveals a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} with any of these methods, the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
