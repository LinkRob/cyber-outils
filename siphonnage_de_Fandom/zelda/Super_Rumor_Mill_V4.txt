{{Term/Store|''Super Rumor Mill V4''||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|Super Rumor Mill V4|N/A|BotW}}</ref> also known as '''''Super Rumor Mill EX: Volume 4''''',<ref>{{Cite|Super Rumor Mill EX: Volume 4|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}} series, found at the {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}. In it, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} describes the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} and where some of their treasure can be found inside of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|From the moment I wake to the moment I sleep, my mind is full of rumors. Even my dreams are gossipy! Traysi here! You fine folks keep up with dream rumors too, don't you? Well, this one's so good it might as well be a dream. Listen up, history buffs! This tidbit is about the brave soldiers who were tragically wiped out during the Great Calamity. The royal guards who protected the royal family! Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. They say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on {{Color|BotW Red|the second floor}}. I do hope one day {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} is restored to its former glory, complete with royal guards. Hmm... I wonder when a royal guard in shining armor will come sweep me off MY feet... Traysi's Recommendation: ★★★★★|N/A|BotW}}</ref> Reading it is required for the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}".
