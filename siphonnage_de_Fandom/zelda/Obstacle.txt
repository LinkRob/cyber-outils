==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Obstacle}} are used as part of the {{Term|BotW|Obstacle Course|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Blynne|link}} runs in {{Term|BotW|Fural Plain|link}}. There are ten in total, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must jump over with his {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} to clear.<ref>{{Cite|It's an obstacle course, but on horseback! There are 10 obstacles to navigate. Best time wins.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> According to {{Term|BotW|Blynne}}, the best way to clear the {{Plural|BotW|Obstacle}} is to be able to control one's {{Term|BotW|Horse}} well.<ref>{{Cite|The difficult part about this is the fact that you can't just try to recklessly ride as fast as you can. In order to clear the obstacles on the path, you have to be able to control your horse very well.|Blynne|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} ends once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has cleared all ten {{Plural|BotW|Obstacle}} and returns to {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When you clear the last one, ride back here. Simple, right?|Blynne|BotW}}</ref>
