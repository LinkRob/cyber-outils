The {{Term/Store|Recruit Uniform||Series, ST}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref> also known as the '''Hero's Clothes''',{{Name Ref|ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Recruit Uniform}} is the same uniform that [[Soldier|guard]]s wear.<ref>{{Cite|You got a recruit uniform! Wearing this, you can pose as a soldier and move freely about the castle!|N/A|ST}}</ref> It is given to Link by [[Princess Zelda]] so that he can disguise himself and be able to pass by the guards unnoticed as the two escape from [[Hyrule Castle]].<ref>{{Cite|All right, please put this on right away. It's a recruit uniform. The guards are starting their patrols, so getting past them won't be easy. But if you wear this, they'll think you're one of them!|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> It serves as a replacement to the [[Engineer's Clothes]], which Link can reobtain later in the game when he obtains 15 of the 20 stamps in [[Niko]]'s [[Stamp Book]]. There is no functional difference between Link's outfits in this game, and switching between the two can be done at any time by talking to Niko.
