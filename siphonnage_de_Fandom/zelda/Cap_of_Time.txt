==Location and Uses==
The '''Cap of Time''' is a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the Legend of Zelda 30th Anniversary Ocarina of Time Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Cap of Time. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Cap of Time, alongside the [[Tunic of Time]] and [[Trousers of Time]], comprise the [[Hero of Time Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from [[Ocarina of Time]]. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
