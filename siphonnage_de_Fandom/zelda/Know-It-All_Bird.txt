A {{Term/Store|Know-It-All Bird}} is a recurring animal in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the house of the wise {{Color|OoS Blue|Know-It-All Birds}}. See them if you have questions!|N/A|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Umm, even this Know-It-All Bird knows little of essences.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> A group provides [[Link]] with helpful tips about gameplay. These guys have blue, green, red, or orange feathers and there are 10 of them in total. They reside in their {{Term|OoS|Know-It-All Birds' Hut|link|display=Hut}} in [[Lynna City]] in {{OoA|-}} and [[Horon Village]] in {{OoS|-}}. They ask if Link wants to learn about subscreens, [[saving]], [[map]]s, [[Mystical Seed]]s, [[Spin Attack|hero's skill]], [[shield]] tactics, [[bomb]]s, items, seasons and Essences. Even though they know nothing about the Essences, they still ask [[Link]] if he wants to learn about them.
