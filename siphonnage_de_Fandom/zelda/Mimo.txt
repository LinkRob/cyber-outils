{{Term/Store|Mimo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mimo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mimo}} is a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} who can be found on top of {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}. He does not appear until the "{{Term|BotW|Stranded on Eventide|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine|link}} have been completed.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 340}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} operates the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches him, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} asks what he is doing so high up.<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing all the way up here?|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} answers that he is seeking adventure, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} reasons he must be drawn to heights.<ref>{{Cite|[Seeking adventure!] You must be drawn to heights.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} claims that this is something they have in common, since {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}} is the only place where he can hear the {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You and I have that in common. It is only from here that I can properly hear the wind.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that he is in search of treasure, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} tells him that the only object in their vicinity is the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I heard there's treasure.] Then you are in for disappointment. The only object to be found here is this strange building.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} then explains that he came to {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}} to find freedom.<ref>{{Cite|What is my purpose here, you ask? I suppose...I am here to find my freedom. In a manner of speaking.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

Mimo wants to live free and unrestricted as the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} does, and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to live the same way.<ref>{{Cite|I want to live as the wind does-free and unrestricted. To bide here and listen to its voice offers a taste of that freedom. Don't you long to be like the wind too?|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that he does, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} starts to feel a connection with him.<ref>{{Cite|[With all my heart.] I think we will be fast friends. I've never felt such kinship with a Hylian before.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to fly, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} believes he is still capable of undergoing his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your style of wing is different than mine but similar enough that I think you could undergo my {{Color|BotW Red|training to become the wind}}.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the training, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} explains that he wants to find the freedom of the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} by copying its movements.<ref>{{Cite|[Train to what?] The wind is stormy or calm by turns but always moves as it will I seek to emulate its freedom by mimicking its movements.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> To achieve this, he has created the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}}, which involves flying through {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)|link}} that are suspended in the air.<ref>{{Cite|To that end, I have devised a training program that involves flying through rings suspended in the air.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} promises that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be enriched if he undertakes this training.<ref>{{Cite|Should you choose to undertake this training, I promise that you will find it enriching.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} seems unsure about participating, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} asks to be left alone so he can hear the {{Term|BotW|Wind}}'s voice.<ref>{{Cite|[I don't know...] I see. There is a gulf in our thinking on the matter, then. Please leave me in peace so that I may hear the wind's voice.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} will be happy.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll do it!] It lifts my spirits to hear of your keen interest.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> To set up the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} requires a payment of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I require {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to set up a course for you to train. Is that acceptable?|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Once paid, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to wait on {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}} while he makes his preparations.<ref>{{Cite|You should wait here while I prepare the course for you.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

On his return, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} apologizes for taking so long, but he informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}} is ready for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to fly through.<ref>{{Cite|That took longer than I expected. My apologies. But it is ready now for you to fly whenever you are ready.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} recommends that {{Term|BotW|Link}} start out with the route to their left, as the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} is calmer in that location.<ref>{{Cite|I recomend...the route over there. It can be enjoyable to waft along on a calm breeze.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> However, he claims the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} will welcome {{Term|BotW|Link}} regardless of what direction he takes.<ref>{{Cite|But the choice is ultimately yours. Whatever you decide, the wind will welcome you.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} will begin.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} either lands on the ground or flies through all of the {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} will come and retrieve him.<ref>{{Cite|Hm. Stay there and I will come collect you, my flightless friend.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} passed through less than 20 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} admits that he may have overestimate {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s skills, but he thinks practice could help him improve.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? How many rings did you achieve? {{Color|BotW Red|XX}}? I appear to have overestimated your skill a bit, it seems. You may be unaccustomed to flying. But this is nothing a bit of practice on my course can't correct.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> After reaching between 20 and 24 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} states that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not yet one with the {{Term|BotW|Wind}}, but he still gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm...{{Color|BotW Red|XX rings}}. A fair showing, but you are not yet one with the wind. Still, I suppose you deserve this participation reward.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through 24 to 29 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} claims that he handles his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}} well, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You achieved {{Color|BotW Red|XX rings}}, hm? Very good. You take to your strange wings quite well. Here, this is for you.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} flies through between 30 and 49 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} states that even a {{Term|BotW|Rito}} could not have scored so well, and he gives him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|You achieved {{Color|BotW Red|XX rings}}?! That is wonderful! Your score is unsurpassed, even by we Rito! You have truly become one with the wind, and so I must insist that you accept this reward.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through at least 50 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} suggests he may be a {{Term|BotW|Rito}} in disguise, and he rewards him with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|XX}}?! Unbelievable... Are you certain that you're not a Rito in disguise? To see you soar was a rare treat. Here, you deserve to be rewarded in kind.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} asks if he wants to try another round.<ref>{{Cite|Are you satisfied for the day? Or would you like to train again?|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} before he leaves that he will be there to pick him up if he gets tired.<ref>{{Cite|I will be ready to swoop in whenever you tire of flying, so do not worry yourself on that account.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} accuses him of being like a {{Term|BotW|Cucco|link}}, but he still looks forwards to seeing {{Term|BotW|Link}} again.<ref>{{Cite|[Nah, I'll pass.] Yes, you are much like a {{Color|BotW Blue|Cuccoo}}. First you fly, and now you cluck. Most intriguing. I look forward to seeing you again.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Mimo}}, he recognizes him and asks if he is interested in training.<ref>{{Cite|Greetings, my Hylian friend. Have you returned to {{Color|BotW Red|train to become the wind}}?|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>
