{{Term/Store|Chief-of-the-Dunes Battle||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 37 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Chief-of-the-Dunes Battle|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Chief-of-the-Dunes Battle}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must defeat 900 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. Multiple {{Plural|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain|link}} and Gerudo Soldiers aid the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} in his or her mission. At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, there is only a large group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}, but a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} spawns after the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 50 KOs. Alongside the {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}}, many {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}} spawn, and continue to spawn as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeats them. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 250 KOs, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Moblin}} spawn alongside even more {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}}. Eventually, as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} gains more KOs and moves throughout the map, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} spawn as well. At 500 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} and a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} spawn. Like before, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}} spawn in large numbers as the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} gains more KOs. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reaches 900 KOs, a {{Term|HWAoC|Molduga}} spawns. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Molduga}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Voltfruit|link}}, 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
