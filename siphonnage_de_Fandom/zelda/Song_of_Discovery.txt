The "{{Term/Store|Song of Discovery||Series, ST}}" is a song in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The song is played on Link's [[Spirit Flute]]. It is learned from a [[Air Stone]] located in the center of [[Anouki Village]]. When played, it reveals hidden items or treasure chests buried in the ground.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Song of Discovery! Play it to find hidden objects.|N/A|ST}}</ref> This function is similar to that of the [[shovel]] in {{PH}} and other games in the series, as well as [[Wolf Link|Wolf Link's]] digging ability in {{TP}}.
