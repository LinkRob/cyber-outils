{{Term/Store|Daruk||Series, BotW, SSBU}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Title"/>{{Name Ref|SSBU, HWAoC}}

==Personality==
Courageous at heart, Daruk is extremely dedicated to defeating the Calamity Ganon and protecting his people. In battle, he is loud and ferocious - although he does have a calm side to him as well during peaceful moments, such as telling the malevolent creature to "open wide" as he fired his Divine Beast's laser at Hyrule Castle. In life, Daruk was a fast friend to [[Link]] and it was the latter's strength and appetite that he demonstrated that won him over as the Goron Champion himself also had those traits. 

Daruk is also revealed to suffer from cynophobia (fear of dogs), much due to having been attacked by them often as a child and still fears them to this day, and he seems to be embarrassed about having this trait.
