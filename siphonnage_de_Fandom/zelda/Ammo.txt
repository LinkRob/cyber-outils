==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|BotW}}
{{Term|BotW|Ammo}} is the ammunition used for {{Plural|BotW|Cannon|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Darunia Lake|link}}. While it was originally used for mining {{Term|BotW|Ore|link}}, it was abandoned along with the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|display=Mine}} following {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}'s rampage. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} puts {{Term|BotW|Ammo}} back into use by firing the {{Plural|BotW|Cannon}} with {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}}, in order to fetch {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Vault|link}}.
