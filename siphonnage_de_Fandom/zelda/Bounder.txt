'''Bounders''' are enemies in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Bounders are small fish found swimming in certain bodies of water. They attack by aimlessly spitting balls of water in any of the cardinal directions. Bounders most commonly appear in the [[Plain of Donora]], but [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] also encounters them in the [[Forest of Canvula]]. Some Bounders are found outside of water, where they flop around.
