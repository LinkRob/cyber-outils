The {{Term/Store|Korok Mask||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Korok Mask}}<br/>A mask inspired by those fun-loving Koroks. It shakes when one is hiding nearby. It's a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Location and Uses==
Obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}} is the objective of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Strange Mask Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it there's an item called the Korok Mask that has the power to alert its wearer when a Korok is hiding nearby.<br/><br/>Apparently the {{Color|BotW Red|Woodland Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare mask.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As part of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must read the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V1|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}. According to {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}} is inhabited by a spirit.<ref>{{Cite|They say if you pour enough love into an object, a spirit will inhabit it. Something that creepy's just gotta be true!<br/><br/>And apparently, this Korok Mask really is inhabited by a spirit!|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref> She has also heard that it occasionally shakes and makes noises when worn, which she theorizes may be due to hidden friends nearby.<ref>{{Cite|If you wear it, it supposedly shakes and makes adorably weird noises every now and then.<br/><br/>Maybe it does that to tell you when friends are nearby and you can't see them? Who knows!|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref> Finally, {{Term|BotW|Traysi}} shares that it is supposedly hidden in the hollow of a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} in a forest that makes people lose their way.<ref>{{Cite|Some say the treasure chest containing this mask is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in a tree hollow in a forest that makes people lose their way}}.<br/><br/>Hmm, sounds like a tough place to find something! Luckily, word has it there's a convenient trick for finding your way...|Super Rumor Mill V1|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}} can be found in a DLC {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} south of the pair of stationary {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}}' entrance. It is located in the mouth of an {{Term|BotW|Ogre Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Mask}} in the spooky tree's hollow within the Lost woods!<br/><br/>The Korok Mask will inform you when a Korok is hiding nearby.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}}, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}} will begin selling it for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.{{Note|Prior to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Updates|Version 1.3.3 Update]], {{Term|BotW|Granté}} would not sell any {{Term|BotW|Armor}} that is exclusive to the {{Term|BotW|Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass|link}}. This meant that the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask}} could not be replaced if sold, which was noted in the description of the Mask.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Korok Mask}}<br/>A mask inspired by those fun-loving Koroks. It shakes when one is hiding nearby. If sold, this rare find can't be replaced.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The Korok Mask's description was changed in the Version 1.3.3 Update as the Mask could now be replaced.<ref name="Inventory"/>}} When worn, the {{Term|BotW|Korok Mask|display=Mask}} shakes when a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} is hiding nearby.<ref name="Inventory"/>
