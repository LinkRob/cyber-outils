'''Cat Statues''' are Objects from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Cat Statue|A lithe statue with a mark on its forehead and rounded features. Its cunning eyes stare into the distance.<ref name="Cat Statue">{{Cite|A lithe statue with a mark on its forehead and rounded features. Its cunning eyes stare into the distance.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Cat Statues are large cat statues found in the [[Gerudo Sanctum]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]]. They cost one [[Echo]] energy to summon.
