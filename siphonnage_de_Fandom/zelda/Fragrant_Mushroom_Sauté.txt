{{Term/Store|Fragrant Mushroom Sauté|Fragrant Mushroom Sautés|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}} are a meal that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Mushroom|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Regular Fragrant Mushroom Sautés can be made by using a {{Term|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}} in the recipe or by using multiple mushrooms with differing effects. Eating them will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} missing {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The aroma of these dishes make mouths water.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}} are favored by {{Term|BotW|Nellie|link}}, a resident of [[Gerudo Town]], who makes it whenever she is feeling tired.<ref>{{Cite|My mom often said that dishes with mushrooms were good for nursing serious headaches. So... when I'm feeling tired, I always make {{Color|BotW Blue|fragrant mushroom sauté}}. I love that curry flavour! It's a simple dish that you can make if you have some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|mushrooms}}.|Nellie|BotW}}</ref>

Based on the types of Mushrooms used, several different variations of Fragrant Mushroom Sauté exist:

* '''Hearty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}}. When eaten, they will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}. They completely refill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Grants a mid-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}}. If they're eaten, they'll temporarily boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}}. Consuming one will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}. Eating them gives Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}}. When eaten, they'll temporarily increase the damage of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}}. If they're eaten, Link's defense will be boosted for a short period of time.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}}. Eating one increases Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a time.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Fragrant Mushroom Sauté''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fragrant Mushroom Sauté}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The fragrant aroma of this sautéed spice and mushroom dish makes your mouth water.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}}. When consumed, Link will temporarily gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
