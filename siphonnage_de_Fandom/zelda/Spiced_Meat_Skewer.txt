{{Term/Store|Spiced Meat Skewer|Spiced Meat Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Spiced Meat Skewers can be {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=cooked}} by combining {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. By eating them, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can replenish some of his lost {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The Goron Spice covers up the scent of the meat, which allows its flavor to shine.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add extra ingredients while making Spice Meat Skewers, which results in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these meals will heal all of [[Link|Link's]] Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be partially refilled if he eats them.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. When eaten, they'll completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Link will gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}} when he eats these meals.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If they're eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. By eating them, Link's defense will be increased.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be boosted when he eats them.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Spiced Meat Skewer''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Spiced Meat Skewer}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A special Goron spice covers up the scent of the meat, allowing its flavor to shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
