==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
[[File:BotW Thunder Helm Key Item Icon.png|left]]
The Thunder Helm is a legendary helmet belonging to Lady [[Riju]], Chief of the [[Gerudo]]. It's been passed down as an heirloom amongst the Gerudo, and is traditionally worn only by their chief.<ref name="Inventory"/> It is a cultural treasure of the Gerudo people and holds some of the [[Champion]] [[Urbosa]]'s power.<ref name="Treasure">{{Cite|Well then, let's see how serious you are. This helm is a cultural treasure. To earn it, you must gain the trust of our people.|Riju|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Thunder Helm">{{Cite|You want this {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}} that badly? I bet you do. This special helm still holds some of Urbosa's power.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> The Thunder Helm was stolen by the [[Yiga Clan]] prior to the events of {{BotW}}. During the "[[Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|Divine Beast Vah Naboris]]" [[Main Quest]], Link is tasked with recovering the Helm for Riju by infiltrating the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]].<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps if this person were first to prove his worth by recovering your stolen Thunder Helm...|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> After defeating the leader of the Yiga Clan, [[Master Kohga]], Link will find the Thunder Helm in a [[Treasure Chest]].

Once Link returns the Thunder Helm to Riju, she will wear it and trigger one of Link's [[Recovered Memory|memories]]. With the Thunder Helm now in their possession, Link and Riju initiate a plan to get Link inside the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}. They ride {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} near Naboris so that Link can disable its feet with [[Bomb Arrow]]s. Riju wears the Thunder Helm to this encounter, which protects her by absorbing the [[Lightning]] of Naboris. Link must stay close to Riju to avoid getting struck by it.<ref>{{Cite|You'll have to stay close if you don't want to be shocked.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

After Link has entered Naboris and regains control of it, the Thunder Helm can be found resting on a small pedestal beside Riju on her throne. When Link interacts with the Thunder Helm, Riju will offer to lend it to Link.<ref>{{Cite|And if you truly desire it, then perhaps I can lend it to you...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> However, she then says that Link must earn it by gaining the trust of her people.<ref name="Treasure"/> Riju agrees to lend Link the Thunder Helm in exchange for resolving the Gerudo Town people's problems.<ref>{{Cite|I want you to address their concerns and bring a resolute peace to our town.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> This being the [[Side Quest]], "[[The Thunder Helm]]". In order to satisfy Riju's terms, Link must complete the four Side Quests in Gerudo Town that pertain to helping the people that live there. This includes "[[Tools of the Trade]]", "[[The Mystery Polluter]]", "[[Medicinal Molduga]]" and "[[The Search for Barta]]."

Once Link has done all four Side Quests and returns to Riju, she tells Link that she has heard of his actions in delivering happiness to her people.<ref>{{Cite|I have heard of your recent actions. You have returned smiles to the faces of my people...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, Riju keeps her promise and lends Link the Thunder Helm.<ref>{{Cite|And if it can help a true friend in need, we will gladly lend you this sacred treasure.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Riju assures Link that he will be able to use the Thunder Helm to its fullest potential and unlock the power of Urbosa that lies within.<ref>{{Cite|I know that you, more than any other, will be able to use this {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}} to its fullest potential... I believe you can unlock the power of Urbosa that lies within.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> While wearing the Thunder Helm, Link will gain three points of defense and will not take any damage from Lightning strikes.

The Thunder Helm has a defense guard point of 3 and cannot be upgraded by the {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
{{Main|Lightning Helm}}
A diary in the Yiga Clan Hideout reveals that despite the Yiga Clan losing the original one, they managed to analyze it before Link stole it back, allowing the researcher of the Clan to craft a new one. After multiple failed attempts (involving the researcher getting struck by lightning a lot) they succeeded, but the diary implies that the researcher died shortly afterward from the injuries. The Yiga Clan created Thunder Helm is located in the Yiga Clan Hideout, where Link must complete a minigame hosted by a [[Yiga Blademaster]] involving defeating 9 [[Yiga Footsoldier|Yiga Footsolider]]s in 1:00. It has the same properties as the original.

==Trivia==
*Due to an oversight, the Thunder Helm can be permanently lost if Link receives it while his Armor inventory is full. Though Link will "receive" the item, it will not appear in the inventory nor will it return to its pedestal, and it cannot be obtained from speaking to Riju again.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo3DSChannel|published= June 9, 2017|retrieved= July 26, 2017|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZwkgncIs3C0|title= You Can Permanently Lose the Thunder Helm in Zelda Breath of the Wild!|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref>
*The Thunder Helm is one of three pieces of equipment that will not break Link's disguise if worn with the Gerudo Set, the other two being the [[Snow Boots]] and [[Sand Boots]]. The Helm, the Gerudo top and either of the boots can be worn together.
**As a possible nod to the unique connection with the Gerudo Set in Breath of the Wild, when acquired in the Yiga HQ, the Yiga-made version also doesn't disrupt the Yiga Set's disguise in their base in Tears of the Kingdom.
*Most Gerudo in Tears of the Kingdom will recognize the Thunder Helm if Link approaches them with it on, but will not talk about it if Link talks to them.
