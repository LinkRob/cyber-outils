'''Mayor Cravendish''' ({{IPA|ˈkɹævəndɪʃ}} {{Respell|KRA|vən-dish}})<ref name="Chained">{{Cite|It's awful! Mayor Cravendish has been chained in the tower!|Harbanno|TWoG}}</ref> is a character in {{TWoG}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|Turn to the chains above Mayor Cravendish, who will give you the magical lantern.|Sla naar de kettingen boven Mayor Cravendish, die je de magische lantaarn geeft.}}|magazine=CD Interactief|volume= 2|issue= 11|date= November 1995|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=31}}</ref> He is the mayor of the town of {{Term|TWoG|Sakado|link}}, {{Term|TWoG|Gamelon|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|TWoG|Mayor Cravendish}} is the mayor of {{Term|TWoG|Sakado}}. He, along with several other residents,<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=A panic is rolling through Sakado! The Mayor and some of the other villagers are missing. Could Ganon be kidnapping more innocent people? You'd better get to work.|game=TWoG|page=10}}</ref> were captured by the {{Term|TWoG|Iron Knuckle (Boss)|link}} and imprisoned in {{Term|TWoG|Tykogi Tower|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh my! It looks like everyone's been taken to Tykogi Tower.|Impa|TWoG}}</ref> {{Term|TWoG|Impa|link}}'s {{Term|TWoG|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} shows his capture. Before he is taken, Cravendish declares their kidnapping illegal.

Inside {{Term|TWoG|Tykogi Tower}}, {{Term|TWoG|Princess Zelda|link}} is informed by {{Term|TWoG|Harbanno|link}} that {{Term|TWoG|Mayor Cravendish}} been imprisoned on the Tower's top floor.<ref name="Chained"></ref> Zelda defeats the Iron Knuckle guarding the Tower's dungeon, and finds the Mayor inside bound by chains. Embarrassed, Cravendish politely asks Zelda to cut the chains with her {{Term|TWoG|Sword|link}}. He was able to conceal a {{Term|TWoG|Magic Lantern|link}} in his coat during his imprisonment, which he gives to Zelda in gratitude for rescuing him.

{{Term|TWoG|Mayor Cravendish}}'s house is located in the back alley of {{Term|TWoG|Sakado}}, as indicated by a portrait of himself in his house. However even after he is freed, {{Term|TWoG|Mayor Cravendish}} does not return to {{Term|TWoG|Sakado}} and is not seen again.

== Etymology ==
Mayor Cravendish may be named for the [[wikipedia:Cavendish_family|Cavendish family]], a British noble family formally founded in the 14th century which has remained prominent for 600 years. Alternatively, he may be named for [[wikipedia:Cavendish,_Suffolk|Cavendish]], a village in Suffolk. In addition, "craven" means contemptibly lacking in courage, or cowardly.
