{{Term/Store|Vulture Vizier||Series, TFH}} is the {{Term|TFH|Boss}} of {{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}, which serves as the {{Term|TFH|Sub-Boss}} of {{Term|TFH|The Dunes|link}}, in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 211}}</ref> It is a gigantic, bulky {{Term|TFH|Vulture|link}}.

==Characteristics==
The {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} fight {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier}} atop a tilting platform above a pit of sand. The platform will constantly tilt to the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' weight, as well when {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier}} slams on it. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must take care not to slide off the platform, while also moving to make the platform tilt to where they need to be. The bird will fly away and perch on one of the outside railings. It will attack the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} by taking a deep breath in and then blowing streams of flames across the platform. Using the tilting of the platform to reach the {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier|display=Vulture}}'s height, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must stun it using either the {{Term|TFH|Gripshot|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}}, or the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}, and attack it with the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier|display=Vulture}} will immediately react to the hit by pecking at the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}, also striking the tilting platform and shifting it wildly. The {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier|display=Vulture}} will eventually fly over to the other railing; the railing on the east side is slightly higher, making it more difficult to reach the {{Term|TFH|Boss}}.

Eventually {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier}} will fall off the railing and into the sand, however it will shortly reappear carrying a stone statue in its talons. It will drop this statue onto one side of the platform, forcing it to tilt downward until the weight falls off. {{Plural|TFH|Jar|link}} containing {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} may also be dropped as the {{Term|TFH|Boss}} flies across. {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier}} will then perch down momentarily before flying off to grab another statue. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can attack it while it is perched. After several more hits, {{Term|TFH|Vulture Vizier}} will fall onto the center of the platform and frantically breathe flames as the platform tilts back and forth. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must quickly attack it during this period, eventually defeating it.
