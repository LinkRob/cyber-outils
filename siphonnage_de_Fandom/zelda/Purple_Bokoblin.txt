==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Purple Bokoblin}} are the strongest variety of {{Term|CoH|Bokoblin|link}} that only appear in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}. Despite their strength, they share the same beat pattern as other Bokoblins by raising their arms up for one beat before moving on the following beat.
