{{Term/Store|Ami||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Ami|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Ami}} is a child who lives at the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}} on the edge of the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}}. His brother {{Term|BotW|Izra|link}} will direct {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to him for directions, stating it is great to have a little brother who pulls his weight.<ref name="Izra"/> During the day, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} can be found watching {{Term|BotW|Yolero|link}} practice with his "Master {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}}" by the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. If it rains, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} will seek shelter in the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|display=Stable}}. At night, he sleeps in one of the {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} inside.<ref>{{Cite|Zzz...|Ami|BotW}}</ref>

The first time {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Ami}}, he will sigh.<ref>{{Cite|Hah...|Ami|BotW}}</ref> When asked what is wrong, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} explains that while his older brothers get to play all day long, he is forced to stay by the {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}} and give directions.<ref name="Ami"/> {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires further, at which point {{Term|BotW|Ami}} reveals that the {{Term|BotW|Stable Association|link}} requires each {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} to have an individual ready to give directions to visitors.<ref>{{Cite|[Directions?] Yeah. Chapter four, article nine of the Stable Association Regulations states: "Each stable must have personnel ready to give directions to visitors at all times."|Ami|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Ami}} is the only resident of {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}} with a grasp on geography, this job has fallen to him.<ref>{{Cite|And somehow I got stuck with this dumb job instead of playing like my brothers. I guess that's what I get for being the only person at Wetland Stable with a grasp on geography...|Ami|BotW}}</ref>

On subsequent visits, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} proclaims that his life is a joke, before he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he wants to know about the surrounding area.<ref>{{Cite|My life is a joke. OK, let's get this over with. What do you want to know about the surrounding area?|Ami|BotW}}</ref> When asked what is to the east, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} will explain that {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} is about the only thing in that direction.<ref>{{Cite|[What's to the east?] Zora's Domain.<br/>That's about it.|Ami|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the west, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} mocks {{Term|BotW|Link}} and explains {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} is that way.<ref>{{Cite|[What's to the west?] Great question, Mr. Perceptive. Hyrule Castle.|Ami|BotW}}</ref> He  also shares that he has heard about a hidden escape route from the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} called the {{Term|BotW|East Passage}}, which was supposedly made for the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a hidden escape route from Hyrule Castle called the {{Color|BotW Red|East Passage}}. I hear it leads directly into the castle. It was made for the old royal family though, so who knows if it's still usable.|Ami|BotW}}</ref> To the north, he explains that there is {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} and other things.<ref>{{Cite|[What's to the north?] You know—{{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}} and some other junk.|Ami|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to leave, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} will ask if he wants to know what is to the south.<ref>{{Cite|You want to know what's south of here?|Ami|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} denies him, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} says that he will see him around.<ref>{{Cite|[No, thanks...] See you around...|Ami|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Ami}} reveals that he wishes he knew but he has never been able to leave {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable}}.<ref name="South">{{Cite|[Yeah.] Me too, but I never get to leave this stable. If you really want to know, you should go ask at {{Color|BotW Blue|Riverside Stable}}.|Ami|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to ask around at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}} if he wants to know.<ref name="South"/>
