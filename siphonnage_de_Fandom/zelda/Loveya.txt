{{Name|Japanese|Loveya}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Loveya}} first appears on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6|link}}, where he helps {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} make a shortcut. After that, he offers {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} advice on {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push|link}}ing, and gives him some free {{Plural|RTBToL|Item}} to try it out with. After {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} uses up those {{Plural|RTBToL|Item}}, he can purchase more from {{Term|RTBToL|Loveya}}'s shop. He keeps his shop open on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 7|link}} and {{Term|RTBToL|Page 8|link}}, as well. On {{Term|RTBToL|Page 10|link}}, he offers to sell {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} level 2 {{Plural|RTBToL|Item}}, which will work for gaining a second heart with {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}'s love interests.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|RTBToL|Loveya}}'s Japanese name, {{Romanize|ラブ屋|Rabu Ya||3}}, means "Love Dealer", but can also phonetically be represented as "Love {{exp|Slang for 'You'|Ya}}". Additionally, the "Ya" may be a phonetic pun on the Japanese word for "arrow", {{Romanize|矢|Ya||3}}, fitting his position as Cupid.
