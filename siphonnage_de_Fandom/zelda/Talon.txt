{{Term/Store|Talon||Series, OoT, OoT3D, OoS, TMC}} is a [[Wikipedia:Recurring character|recurring character]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239,{{Exp Game|OoT}} 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Talon is in charge of [[Lon Lon Ranch]] in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yep, I'm Talon, the owner of Lon Lon Ranch. I went to the castle to deliver some milk, but I sat down here to rest, and I guess I fell asleep...|Talon|OoT}}</ref> He is lazy, and a very heavy and long sleeper.<ref name="asleep"/> After [[Ganondorf]] acquires the [[Triforce of Power]] and turns [[Hyrule]] into an evil wasteland, he appoints Talon's underling, [[Ingo]], to take charge of the Ranch (who behaves the opposite to Talon). During [[Link]]'s adulthood, after Ingo busts Talon from the Ranch, he can be found sleeping in one of the houses in [[Kakariko Village#Ocarina of Time|Kakariko Village]].<ref>{{Cite|Look at the bearded man sleeping over there. I bet even someone like him has had his share of troubles in the past...|Hylian|OoT}}</ref> Link can only wake him up by using [[Cojiro]] during the [[Trading Quest]]. When he regains control of his Ranch, he tells Link that he will dedicate himself to hard work from that point onward.<ref>{{Cite|I'm gonna turn over a new leaf and work real hard from now on.|Talon|OoT}}</ref> Talon has a young daughter named [[Malon]], to whom he entrusts his finest horse, [[Epona]].<ref>{{Cite|My name is Malon! My dad owns Lon Lon Ranch!|Malon|OoT}}</ref>

When the [[Hero of Time]] is a child, Talon allows Link to play {{Term|OoT3D|Talon's Chicken Game|link}} in which he tosses three special [[Cucco]]s he call "Super Cuccos" into a crowd of normal Cuccos and asks Link to find those specific three.<ref>{{Cite|Well how about a little game? These three Cuccos I have here are special Super Cuccos! I'm going to throw these Cuccos into that there gaggle of normal Cuccos. If you can pick out these three special birds from among the normal Cuccos within the time limit, I'll give you something good.|Talon|OoT}}</ref> The prize is a [[Bottle]] full of Lon Lon [[Milk]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh...! I'm proud to present to you a sample of our very own Lon Lon Milk. You'll be energized the moment you drink it! After you drink it, you can bring back the bottle and buy a refill, anytime you want!|Talon|OoT}}</ref> After Link wins the game, Talon will jokingly ask the young hero if he wants to marry Malon.<ref>{{Cite|How'd you like to marry Malon? Haw haw! I was just kidding! Just kidding! I think you're a little young for that, aren't you?|Talon|OoT}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
Talon and Malon live in a chicken farm north of [[Horon Village]] in {{OoS}}. As part of the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}, Link must wake up Talon sleeping on [[Mt. Cucco]] with the [[Megaphone]]. Talon then gives Link a [[Mushroom]] as thanks.

==={{FSA|-}}===
Talon appears in the [[Near the Fields]] level in {{FSA}}. After escorting Malon safely home, Talon tells of recent troubles in the area and bemoans the only safe thing for them to do is stay in the house all the time. As gratitude, he allows the [[Links]] to use horses to traverse the area past their house.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Talon}} and his daughter Malon both operate Lon Lon Ranch east of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|This small ranch lies just outside Hyrule Town. Father-and-daughter team Talon and Malon run it, and they sell the finest milk in Hyrule!|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

Link first encounters {{Term|TMC|Talon}} after speaking with the {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store|link}}. He and {{Term|TMC|Malon}} are locked out of their {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|link|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|Honestly! Where did Dad go this time?!? I leave for just a few minutes, and he locks up the ranch and takes off!|Malon|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I lost my key, and I can't get inside. {{Color|TMC Red|And the spare key}}'s inside the house!|Talon|TMC}}</ref> Link helps them by shrinking to {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} size and going into their {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|display=House}} to find the {{Term|TMC|Spare Key|link}}, which is inside a {{Term|TMC|Jar|link}}. This gives him access to {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}} and the northern portion of {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}, where {{Term|TMC|Syrup the Witch's Hut|link}} is located.<ref>{{Cite|You really helped me out, sonny. If you ever want to go to {{Color|TMC Green|Lake Hylia}}, use the shortcut out behind our ranch.|Talon|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Talon}} stays in his {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|display=House}} near {{Term|TMC|Elsie|link}} for the remainder of the game.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? You got it!|Talon|TMC}}</ref>

==Development==
In an {{OoT|-}} developer's interview posted on {{wp|Shigesato Itoi}}'s website, 1101.com, script writer Toru Osawa says that {{Term|OoT3D|Talon}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Malon}} from the [[Nintendo 64]] title are respectively based on {{Term|LANS|Tarin|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Marin|link}} from {{LA}} and it is intended that players who have played the previous game on [[Game Boy]] will recognize the reference.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= {{Exp|夢を見る島」（ＧＢ版）のときに出てきた、タリンとマリンという父親と女の子の、元になる親子も今回登場しますし、前からゼルダシリーズをやってくれているひとに わかってもらえるといいなあ、と思っています。これってひょっとしたら、あのときのあれかなって、考えてもらえたら、ぼくらも嬉しい。（スクリプトディレクター・大澤　徹さん）|A father and daughter named Marin and Tarin that were introduced in Link’s Awakening for GameBoy also appeared in this Zelda game. We’re hoping that people who’ve played the Zelda series from the very beginning will recognize them. If you wonder “is this a reference to then?” we’ll be happy.}}|name= Toru Osawa|url= http://www.1101.com/nintendo/nin1/nin1-12.htm|title= Ocarina of Time Developers Interview (Japanese)}}</ref> {{HH}} even states that it is likely that {{Term|Series|Tarin}} is formed from Link's memories of {{Term|Series|Talon}}.

==Trivia==
* {{Term|Series|Talon}}'s wife is never seen in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, although he does mention her in {{OoT|-}} when Link talks to him while wearing either the {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Mask|link}} or {{Term|OoT3D|Goron Mask|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that reminds me of my wife... On second thought, it doesn't... It doesn't look like her at all!|Talon|OoT}}</ref>
* Whenever Talon is woken up by Link in ''Ocarina of Time'', he speaks a certain quote: "''What in tarnation? Can't a person get a little shut-eye around here?''". This has become somewhat of a meme among fans of the game.[[File:SM64 Mario sleepy.webp|thumb|221x221px|[[Nintendo]]'s mascot, Mario, is whom Talon resembles and even sleeps like the iconic plumber]]
* Talon's appearance, along with [[Ingo]], are based on [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], respectively, who are the series' main protagonists. In an {{OoS|-}} artwork, {{Term|OoS|Talon}}'s hat even has Mario's "M" logo on it. Additionally, there is an emblem of [[Bowser]], whose character is the main antagonist, on {{Term|Series|Talon}}'s scarf, which is also present on Malon (as well as [[Romani]] and [[Cremia]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''). {{Term|OoT3D|Talon}}'s sleeping pose and snoring in {{OoT|-}} are also based on Mario's iconic sleeping from ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_64|Super Mario 64]]''.
**In ''[[mariowiki:The_Super_Mario_Bros._Movie|The Super Mario Bros Movie]]'', {{Term|Series|Talon}}'s character design is also used for Mario and Luigi's father, [[mariowiki:Mario_and_Luigi's_father|Papa Mario]].
