'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D''''' is a [[Manhua]] by [[Akira Himekawa]] that was released on October 27, 2012. It was included in the manual for {{OoT3D}} in Hong Kong and Taiwan. It is loosely based on the story of {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo Taiwan|published= |retrieved= November 16, 2021|url= https://www.nintendo.tw/zelda/zelda_comic_for_HP.pdf|title= The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|site= Nintendo Taiwan|type= PDF File}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo Hong Kong|published= |retrieved= November 16, 2021|url= https://www.nintendo.com.hk/zelda/zelda_comic_for_HP.pdf|title= The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|site= Nintendo Hong Kong|type= PDF File}}</ref>
