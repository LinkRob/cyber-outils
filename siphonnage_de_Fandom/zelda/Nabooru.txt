{{Term/Store|Nabooru||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} (pronounced {{IPA|nəˈbɔər.uː}} {{Respell|nə|BOAR|oo}}<ref>{{Cite|Nabooru...  Legend of the Gerudo, celebrated over ages. And you, who were named to honor her—you are a legend as well.|Urbosa|BotW}}<br/>Urbosa pronounces the "r" as an {{Wp|alveolar flap}} <span class="IPA">[ɾ]</span>.</ref>) is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref name="E 239"/> She is the second-in-command of the [[Gerudo]] with [[Ganondorf]], the human form of [[Ganon]], being her only superior. She has a [[Gerudo Warrior|second-in-command]] herself, who is the Gerudo who granted [[Link]] the permission to enter the [[Gerudo's Fortress]] freely.<ref name="Exalted"/> Nabooru is highly respected by the other Gerudo,<ref name="Quote 3">{{Cite|She's just a little girl, but she commands a lot of respect among the Gerudo, Kotake...|Koume|OoT}}</ref> who are unaware of her rebellion against Ganondorf. During the events of {{OoT|-}}, she becomes the new [[Sage|Sage of Spirit]] and aids the [[Hero of Time]] in his battle against Ganon.

==Biography==
Link meets Nabooru in the [[Spirit Temple]] as a child, having used the [[Requiem of Spirit]], which he learned in the future, to reach the [[Desert Colossus]]. At this time, Nabooru is already in opposition to Ganondorf's wicked behavior.<ref name=":0">{{Cite|First of all, let me introduce myself. I'm Nabooru of the Gerudo. I'm a lone wolf thief. But don't get me wrong! Though we're both thieves, I'm completely different from Ganondorf. With his followers, he stole from women and children, and he even killed people! A kid like you may not know this, but the Gerudo race consists only of women. Only one man is born every hundred years... Even though our laws say that lone male Gerudo must become King of the Gerudo, I'll never bow to such an evil man!|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref> She intends to infiltrate Ganon's followers' headquarters there, but is unable to without the [[Silver Gauntlets]], which are out of reach beyond a very small opening.<ref name=":1">{{Cite|I want to ask you a favor... Will you go through this tiny hole and get a treasure that's inside? The treasure is the Silver Gauntlets. If you equip them, you can easily push and pull very heavy things! No, no, no, kid! Don't even think about taking this treasure for yourself! The Silver Gauntlets won't fit a little kid like you if you try to equip them! I want you to be a good boy and give them to me! Ganondorf and his minions are using the Spirit Temple as a hideout. Only the Silver Gauntlets will allow me to sneak deep into the temple. Once there, I'm going to steal all the treasure inside and mess up their plans! How about it? Will you do it?|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref> Through their conservation, she asks Link to get the gauntlets for her, promising to "do something great" for him in return.<ref>{{Cite|If you can successfully get the Silver Gauntlets... I'll do something great for you!|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref> Once the young hero acquires the Silver Gauntlets, [[Twinrova|Koume and Kotake]], Ganondorf's surrogate mothers and evil witches, discover and capture Nabooru, and she orders Link to leave to safety.<ref name=":2">{{Cite|Yeearggh! Let me go!! You, you fiends! Ganondorf's minions! Link! Get out of here! Now! These witches! They're using black magic on me!|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref> 

When Link returns to the temple as an adult, Nabooru has been brainwashed.<ref name="Quote 4">{{Cite|Then we should brainwash her again! Hee hee hee!|Koume|OoT}}</ref> While brainwashed, the high-ranking Gerudo was used to lead activities contrary to the values she previously held.<ref>{{Cite|When Nabooru was a lone wolf thief, she never stole from the weak, but... Now, Nabooru's gang of thieves stands for cruelty and brutality. She claims to be a chivalrous robber, but...no way!|Carpenter|OoT}}</ref> During this intervening period, Nabooru is apparently rumored to brainwash people herself.<ref>{{Cite|The leader of the gang of thieves is Nabooru. She is experimenting in brainwashing many people at the ruins in the desert.|Carpenter|OoT}}</ref>
[[File:Nabooru Iron Knuckle.webp|thumb|252x252px|Nabooru, brainwashed as a deadly Iron Knuckle by Twinrova, in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
Toward the end of the Spirit Temple, Link finally confronts Kotake and Koume, apparently fussing over a strange [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckle]].<ref>{{Cite|Iron Knuckle - Something's strange... This is not an ordinary enemy!|Navi|OoT}}</ref> They command it to attack him and vanish. Upon its defeat, the Iron Knuckle's armor falls off to reveal Nabooru inside with the spell lifted. Although she appears to be free at last, Kotake and Koume reappear and quickly capture her a second time before retreating to the [[boss]] room ahead.<ref name="Quote 4"/><ref name="Quote 3"/><ref>{{Cite|Well, well... Looks like she's back to normal... Koume...|Kotake|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Maybe we should make her work for the great Ganondorf for a little while longer! Ho ho ho!|Kotake|OoT}}</ref> After their defeat, Nabooru is truly freed and she awakens as the Sage of Spirit, giving Link the [[Spirit Medallion]], after regretting her mistakes as a respected Gerudo.<ref>{{Cite|I was brainwashed by those old witches and used by Ganondorf to do his evil will... But isn't it funny? That a person like me could turn out to be the Sage of Spirit! And now, I'm going to fight them as one of the six Sages! Heh heh... I'm going to pay them back for what they did to me!|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref> <ref name=":3">{{Cite|Kid... No... Link, the Hero of Time! Instead of keeping the promise I made back then, I give you this Medallion! Take it!|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You received the Spirit Medallion!! Nabooru awakens as a Sage and adds her power to yours!|N/A|OoT}}</ref>

When Link ultimately defeats Ganon, Nabooru and the other five Sages (alongside [[Princess Zelda]]) combine their powers to seal Ganondorf away into the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]]. During the ending, she can be seen overlooking a celebrating [[Hyrule]] on [[Death Mountain]] with the other Sages, namely [[Saria]], [[Darunia]], [[Princess Ruto]], and [[Impa]].

==Other Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In the {{OoT|Himekawa}}, Nabooru's role is slightly different from the game. She is under the influence of Koume and Kotake as well, but is not transformed into an Iron Knuckle. She is sent to spy on [[Sheik]] which results in her combating Link. He then frees her from the influence which allows her to help release the other Gerudo. She also gives Link the [[Mirror Shield]], which she claims she stole a while back.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ...I stole it from this temple a while ago|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 60}}</ref> After [[Princess Zelda]] is captured by Ganondorf, Nabooru explains that the Gerudo race consists only of women, and that the first male born after one hundred years has the right to be chief. Nabooru decides to fight Ganondorf with Link, commenting that there are some things even a master thief should never steal.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... I'm gonna teach that Ganondorf that there are some things even a master thief should never steal!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 69}}</ref> After the two battle monsters, [[Rauru]] speaks with both Link and Nabooru, revealing that Nabooru has been awakened as the sage of spirit. <ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The seal is released... Awaken as the sage of spirit!|book= OoT (Himekawa)|publisher= |volume= 2|page= 71}}</ref> Together the sages, including Nabooru, create a bridge to [[Ganon's Castle]]. 
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*Nabooru is the first specific [[Gerudo]] in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] to be an ally of [[Link]].<ref name=":0" /><ref name=":1" /> Another one who aids the main protagonist would not appear in the franchise until ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]]'' with [[Riju]], 19 years later.
*''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' contains unused text from Nabooru, wherein she remarks that Link is an enemy of [[Ganondorf]] and must die.<ref>{{Cite|Great Ganondorf's...Enemy...Must die...|Nabooru|OoT}}</ref> This dialogue was most likely to be used when fighting her as an [[Iron Knuckle (Boss)|Iron Knuckle]].
*Much like Ganondorf, likely due to the fact that she is a Gerudo, although she does know Link's name as she says it twice when she was captured by [[Twinrova|Koume and Kotake]] before correcting herself by saying "Hero of Time" when obtaining the [[Spirit Medallion]], Nabooru refers to him as "kid" even as an adult, due to his youth.<ref name=":2" /><ref name=":3" />
*The unnamed [[Gerudo Warrior]] in [[Gerudo's Fortress]] who gives Link the [[Gerudo Token]] to access the [[Haunted Wasteland]] is Nabooru's own second-in-command.[[File:OoT Nabooru's liking of Link message.png|thumb|214x214px|The first half of the message Nabooru says to Link after obtaining the Spirit Medallion, implying a crush on the hero]]
*Although Nabooru is not in a direct romantic relationship with the Hero of Time unlike [[Saria]], [[Princess Ruto]], [[Malon]], or [[Princess Zelda]], she does express a possible love interest to Link in that she had no idea how handsome he would become as an adult, and admitted she should've kept the "promise" she made to him when he was a child, implying a fifth female in the love triangle next to [[Navi]].
*Unlike the standard Iron Knuckles, when defeated, Nabooru's armor breaks apart entirely and crumbles to the ground.
[[File:Unmasked Iron Knuckle.png|thumb|212x212px|An Iron Knuckle with the helmet and upper armor removed via debug cheats, revealing Nabooru's beta head]]
*Interestingly, the hidden Gerudo head inside the regular Iron Knuckles' helmets in both ''Ocarina of Time'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' is actually a leftover of what was supposed to be Nabooru's own head during development before it was changed in the final release. Although this was removed in their [[Nintendo 3DS]] remakes in-game, it still exists in the data files.
*Nabooru's in-game clothing is colored white, despite being shown as pink in official artwork.
*Fitting her second-in-command role, in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|''Ocarina of Time'']] manga, Nabooru, when she was brainwashed by Twinrova, is shown to be a more proficient fighter than the common Gerudo, rivaling Link's skills. Despite this, she is unarmed in the original [[Nintendo 64]] game.
**In ''Ocarina of Time'' itself, Nabooru as an Iron Knuckle is especially dangerous, on-par with Ganon (himself the [[Final Boss]]), as unlike normal ones, she has the ability to instantly counterattack Link in the event the hero hits her from behind, making the use of [[Nayru's Love]] recommended for most players.

==Nomenclature==
Nabooru is most likely a variation of the Japanese name Noboru, meaning "rise" or "ascend".<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/noboru |site= behindthename.com |title= Given Name NOBORU}}</ref> Nabooru's name, like several other characters from {{OoT|-}}, is taken from a [[Nabooru Town|town]] in {{TAoL}}.
