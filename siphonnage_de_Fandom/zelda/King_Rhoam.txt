{{Term/Store|King Rhoam||Series, BotW}} '''Bosphoramus Hyrule''' is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Leader">{{Cite|I was King Rhoam Bosphoramus Hyrule. I was...the last leader of Hyrule. A kingdom which no longer exists.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>{{Fact|HWAoC}} He is the former [[King of Hyrule (Disambiguation)|King]] of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} and the first person that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} encounters after awakening in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}, under the guise of a simple '''Old Man'''.

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} was once the king of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} and the father of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}, one century prior to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s awakening in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}. Most of the information on {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}'s life is detailed in {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam's Journal|link}} and in {{Plural|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.

When his daughter was born, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} decided to name her {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}, as is the tradition of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Today, as the sun rose and a new day was born, my daughter, too, joined this sweet world. In keeping with the traditions of the royal family, I have decided to name her...Zelda.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> Following such an occasion, he decided to begin writing down his life story.<ref>{{Cite|I am not a man accustomed to frivolous musings, but now seems as good as a time as any to begin my royal memorandum.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref>

Prior to the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, a fortune teller predicted the revival of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, so {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} ordered the excavations that uncovered the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stories of Ganon were passed from generation to generation in the form of legends and fairy tales. But there was also...a prophecy. "The signs of a resurrection of Calamity Ganon are clear. And the power to oppose it lies dormant beneath the ground." We decided to heed the prophecy and began excavating large areas of land. It wasn't long before we discovered several ancient relics made by the hands of our distant ancestors. These relics, the Divine Beasts, were giant machines piloted by warriors. We also found the Guardians, an army of mechanical soldiers who fought autonomously. This coincided with ancient legends, oft repeated throughout our land.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= A fortune teller predicts the revival of Calamity Ganon. King Rhoam begins searching for ancient relics. The four Divine Beasts are uncovered.|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 362}}</ref> As more {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} was revealed, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} became certain that the prediction would come true.<ref>{{Cite|Reports keep arriving regarding the excavation of relics. The fortune teller's predictions seem to be coming true. Calamity Ganon was not a figure of fable, or even a legend. He actually existed in our great land of Hyrule.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> While he was concerned that investigating the relics would take time, he noticed that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} displayed an interest in research.<ref>{{Cite|We must investigate all the relics, learn as much as we can. But understanding the Divine Beasts alone will take time... Zelda's eyes lit up like a wildfire when I told her about the relics... I must admit, she has a knack for research.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> During this process, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} learned about about a [[Princess Zelda#Ten_Thousands_Years_prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|princess]] with a sacred power and her [[Link#The_Hero_10,000_Years_Prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|appointed knight]] who was chosen by the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We also learned of a princess with a sacred power and her appointed knight, chosen by the sword that seals the darkness.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> With the power of the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, they were able to seal {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|It was they who sealed Ganon away using the power of these ancient relics.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} was six years old, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}'s wife, the {{Term|BotW|Queen of Hyrule|link}}, unexpectedly died.<ref name="Queen"/> Even when she was alone with {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} never cried, which {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} assumed was because he taught her to be a valiant and steady princess.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda never cried, never faltered. Not even during the royal funeral or later when she and I were alone with our grief. I must assume her strength is a result of us repeatedly informing her of her duty to be a valiant and steady princess.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Witnessing her strength gave {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} hope that he would be able to raise her on his own and prepare her for the future.<ref>{{Cite|For a child of merely six years of age, her conduct was truly that of a born leader. Her strength gives me hope. From now on, I must raise her all alone... Now, only I remain to prepare her for her difficult future as princess of Hyrule.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, he pushed {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} to unlock her {{Term|BotW|Triforce|link|display=sealing power}}, though he believed her to be held back by her grief for her mother.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda finally reported back after her visit to the fountain. It seems her sacred sealing power has still yet to awaken. It has been a year and three months since her mother passed. Perhaps she is held back by heartache too deep to heal.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} wanted to let her wait until she was ready, but since {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s revival was imminent, he ordered her to keep training.<ref>{{Cite|If the Ganon prophecy wasn't looming over our heads, I would tell her to take her time... To wait until she was ready. But our situation is dire and leaves no room for weakness—even on behalf of my beloved daughter. My heart breaks for Zelda, but I must act as a king, not a father. I must order her to train relentlessly at the fountain.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref>

[[File:BotW The Champions' Ballad.png|thumb|left|250px|King Rhoam appointing the Champions]]
In preparation for {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s return, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} appointed four skilled individuals, known as {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}}, to pilot the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref>{{Cite|It was clear that we must follow our ancestors' path. We selected for skilled individuals from across Hyrule and tasked them with the duty of piloting the Divine Beasts. With the princess as their commander, we dubbed these pilots Champions—a name that would solidify their unique bond.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> In "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Memory)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} presides over the inauguration of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, warriors! I'd like to thank you for joining me here today, and for your bravery in accepting this...fateful task.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He officially bestows their sacred garb to them, including {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Champion Tunic|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I officially appoint you Hyrule's Champions and bestow upon you this sacred garb.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He continues, explaining that the blue color is representative of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule}}, and it has been passed down for generations.<ref>{{Cite|That blue is a symbol of the Royal Family, one that has been passed down for countless generations.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} reveals that the garments were crafted by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}, whom he reminds of her duty as a daughter of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|display=Royal Family}}.<ref>{{Cite|Those garments you now wear were all crafted by my daughter, Zelda. Zelda. I trust you with the task only a daughter of the royal family can fulfill.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He instructs her to lead the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} and protect {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}} from {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lead our Champions, Princess. And together, protect our kingdom from the threat of Calamity Ganon.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

In "{{Term|BotW|Father and Daughter|link}}," {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} comes upon {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} and {{Term|BotW|Link}} on the bridge between her study and room.<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing out here, Zelda?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} admits to assessing the results of the {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} experiments and begins to explain their usefulness,<ref>{{Cite|I... I was assessing the results of the experiment with the Guardians. These pieces of ancient technology could be quite useful against the—|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} interrupts her to say that he already knows this.<ref>{{Cite|I know that. They are essential to Hyrule's future, and our research demands that we keep a close eye on them.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He reminds {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} that she has a duty as a princess that she has yet to fulfill.<ref>{{Cite|However, as the princess, you currently have a crucial unfulfilled responsibility to your kingdom.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} asks {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} when she is going to stop treating awakening her power as a childish game.<ref>{{Cite|Let me ask you once more... When will you stop treating this as some sort of childish game?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He claims that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} is wasting her time, as she needs to dedicate every moment to unlocking her power.<ref>{{Cite|And now you are here wasting your time. You need to be dedicating every moment you have to your training. You must be single-minded in unlocking the power that will seal Calamity Ganon away.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> It will never matter if Link beats Ganon if she cannot seal him.

{{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} refuses to accept any more excuses from {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|No more excuses, Zelda!|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He accuses her of running away from her duty, and as a result forbids her from researching {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stop running away from your duty. As the king, I forbid you to have anything to do with these machines from this moment on and command you to focus on your training.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Courtyard|link}}, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} reveals that, among the gossip mongers, she is known as an heir to a throne of failure.<ref>{{Cite|Do you know how the gossip mongers refer to you? They are out there at this moment whispering amongst themselves... that you are the heir to a throne of nothing... nothing but failure.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He informs her that it is her destiny to prove them wrong.<ref>{{Cite|It is woven into your destiny that you prove them wrong.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> After assuring that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} understands, he returns to the interior of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Do you understand?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yes. I understand.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Later, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} would reflect on this encounter in his {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam's Journal|display=''Journal''}}.<ref>{{Cite|I was told Zelda went off to research ancient technology, so I had no choice but to confront her about it.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> He claimed that the reason that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s power had not awakened yet was due to her playing at being a scholar.<ref>{{Cite|The reason her sacred powers still won't awaken is because she's spending all her efforts playing at being a scholar!|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> However, he admitted to understanding her struggles, as she had been left without any guidance on her powers for 10 years.<ref>{{Cite|In truth, I understand Zelda's feelings. Painfully so. She lost her mother, her teacher, before she could learn from her. Ten pointless years of self-training, without so much as a book or note to help find her way...|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> Not only did those in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} talk behind her back, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} recognized that scolding her only drove her to hide away in her research.<ref>{{Cite|Those in the castle talk behind her back. And I, her only family, scold her for her shortcomings. No wonder she wishes to hide away in her beloved relic research. I'd love nothing more than to console her...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Even if it meant she would hate him, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} resolved to keep pushing her to fulfill her duty.<ref>{{Cite|But I must stay strong. She MUST fulfill her duty, just as we all must. Even if she comes to despise me.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} was at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|link}}, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} wrote that it would likely be her last chance.<ref>{{Cite|I have been told my Zelda went to the Spring of Wisdom... This will likely be her last chance. If she is unable to awaken her power at Lanayru, all hope is truly lost.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> In the event that she would yet again fail to unlock her powers, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} planned on welcoming her back kindly.<ref>{{Cite|If she comes back without success, then I shall speak kindly with her. Scolding is pointless now.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> After 10 years of training, he could now see that her powers would awaken in some other way.<ref>{{Cite|I forced 10 years of training on her... and after all that, it seems her power will stubbornly awaken some other way.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> He even considered encouraging her research into {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} in the hope that it could give her the answers he was unable to.<ref>{{Cite|Perhaps I should encourage her to keep researching her beloved relics. They may just lead her to answers I can't provide.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> As he wrote, he anxiously awaited his daughter's return.<ref>{{Cite|For now, I sit anxiously, more a father than a king in this moment. I sit and await my daughter's return.|King Rhoam's Journal|BotW}}</ref> Before he could express his guilt to his daughter, however, {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} awakened, destroying {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} and killing {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Calamity Ganon revives. The Guardians and the four Divine Beasts are taken over, and Hyrule Castle Town is destroyed. King Rhoam is killed.|book= CaC |publisher= |page= 364}}</ref><ref name="Great Calamity">{{Cite|The Great Calamity was merciless... It devastated everything in its path, lo, a century ago. It was then that my life was taken away from me.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Since then, he has remained on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} in the form of a {{Term|BotW|Spirit|link}}.<ref name="Spirit"/>

Following {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s resurrection, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} assumes the persona of an {{Term|BotW|Old Man|link}} living alone atop the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first meets the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}}, he is seen camping in an alcove just east of the entrance of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}. The {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} follows {{Term|BotW|Link}} throughout "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. He repeatedly exhibits a knowledge of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}'s capabilities as well as {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} culture and technology.<ref>{{Cite|It has been quite some time since I have seen that {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}... Long ago, a highly advanced tribe known as the Sheikah inhabited these lands. The great power of their wisdom saved this kingdom time and time again. But their ancient technology disappeared long ago... Or so it is said.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> He requests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} bring him the {{Plural|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} from within the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in exchange for his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}, the means of escaping the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Old Man explained that there are a total of four Shrines on the plateau. He said that he will give you the Paraglider once you have acquired the Spirit Orb from all the of the Shrines.<br/>He told you to survey the plateau from the top of the tower to find the remaining shrines. He also pointed out that you can use your Sheikah Slate if you need to travel quickly.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

[[File:BotW King Rhoam Introduction.png|thumb|right|250px|King Rhoam revealing his true identity to Link]]
After {{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains all four {{Plural|BotW|Spirit Orb}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} will meet {{Term|BotW|Link}} outside of the final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. There, he promises to tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} the truth about who he is, asking for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to meet him at the spot where the four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} intersect before mysteriously fading away.<ref>{{Cite|With this, you have now acquired all of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}} from the shrines on this plateau. Oho ho! Extraordinary! That means...it is finally time. Link, it is finally time for me to tell you everything. But first... Imagine an X on your map, with the four shrines as the end points. Find the spot {{Color|BotW Red|where those lines intersect}}. I shall wait for you there. Do you understand? Where two lines connecting the shrines would cross... There...I will...be waiting...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> The location at the intersection of the four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} is the {{Term|BotW|Temple of Time|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pray to the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}} to receive either a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel|link}}. Upon doing so, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} will speak to {{Term|BotW|Link}} from the rooftop of the {{Term|BotW|Temple of Time}}, urging him meet him there.<ref>{{Cite|The blessing of the Goddess has made you that much more resilient, I see... Here I am... Get up here—quickly!|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the rooftop tower of the {{Term|BotW|Temple of Time}}, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} will congratulate him on his progress and announce that he will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} his true identity.<ref>{{Cite|Well done there, young one! Now, then... The time has come to show you who I truly am...|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> Here, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} reveals to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}, the leader of the former Kingdom of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}.<ref name="Leader"/> As he does so, the {{Term|BotW|Old Man}} begins to shine brightly and assume his true form as {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}.

{{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} reveals to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he lost his life during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}.<ref name="Great Calamity"/> He explains that he chose not to bear his true form while {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s memories were still fragile so as not to overwhelm him.<ref>{{Cite|I did not think it wise to overwhelm you while your memory was still fragile. So rather than that, I thought it best to assume a temporary form.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to forgive him for this deception.<ref>{{Cite|Forgive me.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Now, though, he believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is ready to hear what happened 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|I think you are now ready. Ready to hear what happened 100 years ago.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} shares the events leading up to the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}} with {{Term|BotW|Link}}. At the end of the story, he admits that the princess he spoke of was his own daughter, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref name="Daughter"/> He also reveals to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was her knight, but he was defeated while trying to protect her.<ref>{{Cite|And the courageous knight who protected her right up to the very end... That knight was none other than you, Link. You fought valiantly when your fate took an unfortunate turn.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> As a result, {{Term|BotW|Link}} was taken to the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}, which brought him back after 100 years.<ref>{{Cite|And then, you were taken to the Shrine of Resurrection. Here you now stand revitalized, 100 years later.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the voice he has been hearing since he woke up was that of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|The words of guidance you have been hearing since your awakening are from Princess Zelda herself.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} remains in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, where she calls out for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s help.<ref>{{Cite|Even now, as she works to restrain Ganon from within Hyrule Castle, she calls out for your help.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}'s power is draining and will soon be unable to restrain {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|However, my daughter's power will soon be exhausted. Once that happens, Ganon will freely regenerate himself and nothing will stop him from consuming our land.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} feels guilty since he was not able to save {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}}, but he still asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to save {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} and destroy {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Considering that I could not save my own kingdom, I have no right to ask this of you, Link... But I am powerless here... You must save her...my daughter. And do whatever it takes to annihilate Ganon.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

In the century since the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}, {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} has managed to keep his control over the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}.<ref>{{Cite|Somehow, Ganon has maintained control over all four Divine Beasts, as well as those Guardians swarming around Hyrule Castle|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> As it would be reckless for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go directly to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} suggests that he make his way to {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I believe it would be quite reckless for you to head directly to the castle at this point. I suggest...that you make your way east, out to one of the villages in the wilderness. Follow the road out to Kakariko village.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Once there, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} instructs him to meet with the elder, {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}}, who will be able to tell him more about his task.<ref>{{Cite|Follow the road out to Kakariko Village. There you will find the elder, Impa. She will tell you more about the path that lies ahead.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} to find its exact location, though he still gives him directions to go west after he passes through the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Consult the map on your Sheikah Slate for the precise location of Kakariko Village. Make your way past the twin summits of the Dueling Peaks. From there, follow the road as it proceeds north...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} then grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}, fulfilling his promise to allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to safely leave the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|Go on... Here is the {{Color|BotW Blue|paraglider}}, just as I promised.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Receiving this officially completes "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau}}." He believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} will now be able to safely descend from the cliffs of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}.<ref>{{Cite|With that, you should be able to safely fly off the cliffs surrounding this area.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Having served his duty, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} bids {{Term|BotW|Link}} farewell and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|And...I think that's it. I've told you everything I can... Link... You must save...Hyrule...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Once he is gone, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive the "{{Term|BotW|Seek Out Impa|link}}" and "{{Term|BotW|Destroy Ganon|link}}" {{Plural|BotW|Main Quest}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} eradicate {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} and the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} are seen floating above {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}. They disappear before the pair turns to face them.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|start}}
{{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} retroactively debuted in {{HWAoC|-}}, where he is seen readying the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian|link}} army into battle. He reminds {{Term|HWAoC|Princess Zelda|link}} to focus on her duty of awakening her powers, rather than studying the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Divine Beast|link}}. He is also seen with a sword during the fight against their own {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}, which {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}} recruited to his side by corrupting them.

{{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} is thought to have died from the blast of {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} lasers, but it was really {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam|display=Rhoam}} himself who used the device called the Ancient Guardian Shield, given to him by {{Term|HWAoC|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}. This turned the tables on the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian}}, and the {{Plural|HWAoC|Royal Guard|link}} were able to retreat from {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}. He is later unlocked as a playable character, where he is able to switch to his old man guise in battle to change to a different move set.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{TotK|-}}===
Rhoam was briefly mentioned by Zelda in her first memory scene, taking place immediately after her disappearance, where she identified herself as his daughter as well as his status as the king of Hyrule. This alongside [[King Rauru|Rauru]]'s confusion due to his being the king of Hyrule caused her to realize she traveled back in time to the era of Hyrule's founding.

==Nomenclature==
King Rhoam's first name, ローム (''Rōmu'') in Japanese, may be a corruption of [[wikipedia:Rome_(disambiguation)|Rome]], an ancient Mediterranean city-state, and due to its spelling it may also come from the English word "roam" which means to wander or travel freely and with no specific destination, one of the main selling points of Breath of the Wild. Bosphoramus may be taken from the [[wikipedia:Bosporus|Bosphorus strait]] (once territory of the Eastern Roman empire), known for its clear blue waters which match King Rhoam's colour scheme.
