==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Igneo Talus Titan}}
The {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan}} bears a striking resemblance to {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus}}, but is significantly larger and much stronger than its common counterpart. It is involved in one of the three trials of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Daruk's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, in which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must "stop a titan of molten stone."<ref>{{Cite|♪Crawling flame on a mighty peak.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Rudania}}'s Champion, never weak.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|stop of titan of molten stone}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|follow rings of light alone}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|survive lava's fiery fate}}.<br/>Champion, trials await!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Champion|link}} {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} originally fought the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan}} to pass the same trial.<ref name="Krane"/>

The {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan}} appears at the northern shore of {{Term|BotW|Lake Darman|link}}, where it lies in the {{Term|BotW|Lake Darman|display=Lake}}'s {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}} and emerges as it is approached. Its characteristics and attack patterns are for the most part identical to the common [[Igneo Talus]]; its body is encased in molten rock and it attacks by launching its arms at [[Link]], which are then shortly regrown. It can also attack by slamming its arms into the {{Term|BotW|Lava}}, creating a fiery splash. Its heated body also creates {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} of hot {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} around it, which [[Link]] can use to glide over with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to its weakness: the [[Ore Deposit]] protruding from the top of its head. However, coming in direct contact with the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan|display=Titan}}'s molten body will catch [[Link]] on {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Thereby, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can attack the Titan with {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} to cool it down and stun it temporarily, or approach it while wearing the {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}} if it has been enhanced twice by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} for the {{Term|BotW|Fireproof|link}} {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus}}. Notably, the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan}} does not leave {{Term|BotW|Lake Darman}}, requiring that {{Term|BotW|Link}} glides over if he intends to strike its weakness directly.

After the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan}} is defeated, the {{Term|BotW|Kamia Omuna Shrine|link}} rises from the ground nearby, thereby completing the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan|display=Titan}}'s trial.

Like the {{Term|BotW|Molduking|link}}, the defeat of the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus Titan}} does not count towards its respective {{Term|BotW|Medal of Honor|link}}.
