{{Term/Store|Daily Drills: Rito||HWAoC}} is a level 14 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daily Drills: Rito|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Daily Drills: Rito}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any            {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Salmon|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to reach a Targeted Position within four minutes. Many {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier}} with both melee and ranged {{Plural|HWAoC|Weapon}} stand and will appear in his or her way. When about halfway there, a gate closes and a {{Term|HWAoC|Rito Captain|link}} spawns, who must be defeated to reopen it. The same happens again directly in front of the Targeted Position, although with two {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Captain}} instead of one. Past the gate, at the Targeted Position, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} will find {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} surrounded by a group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Soldier}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} is defeated.
