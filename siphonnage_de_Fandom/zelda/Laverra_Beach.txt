{{Term/Store|Laverra Beach||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Laverra Beach'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Laverra Beach}} is a location found within the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}, running from the western side of the mouth of the {{Term|BotW|Menoat River|link}} bordering the {{Term|BotW|Faron Sea|link}}. It is a predominately sandy beach with the occasional patch of gravel, with segments of rocks and grass to the west and cliffs to the north. A {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} base lies just off the shore.

Four {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found off the shore of {{Term|BotW|Laverra Beach}}. Three of these are wooden and can be found floating near the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos}} camp, and by the eastern end of the beach. The fourth is metal and lies on the ocean floor towards the central point of the beach. Several {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}} can be found along the norther cliff face.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find five {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} along {{Term|BotW|Laverra Beach}}. One can be found be completing the star shaped pattern of rocks at the eastern end of the beach, a second by examining a moving patch of green light towards the beaches centre, a third under a rock {{Term|BotW|Link}} can access by blowing up a weak portion of the cliff to the right of a {{Term|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit}}, a fourth up a palm tree at the beaches centre, and a fifth under a rock on a ledge towards the top of the cliff to the west.
