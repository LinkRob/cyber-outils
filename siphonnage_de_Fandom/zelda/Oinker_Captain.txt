The '''Blue Oinker Captain''' and the '''Red Oinker Captain''' are large pig-like enemies that appear in {{FPTRR}}, and are boss-like variants of the more common {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker|link}}. Together, they guard the second area of the large {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}} camp in {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins}}. Both Captains are only armored lightly and attack much more aggressively with their pairs of scimitars than their shorter cousins.

==Battle==
In the first battle phase, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} and his {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}} can't reach the Captains as they are standing on higher ground in different corners of the room. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} has to pick bombs from the plants in the opposite corners and throw them at one of them to start the next phase. To interrupt this attack, the {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker Captain}} constantly throw broken {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} orbs at {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} which make him drop the ticking bomb. In addition, they will regularly throw three {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker|section=Pubarian|display=Pubarians|link}}, which are even more distracting.

When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} successfully hits a Captain with a bomb, it will jump down into the battle area and start a devastating attack. The Blue {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Captain}} will unleash a {{Term|FPTRR|Spin Attack|link}} that last for several seconds, and the Red {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Captain}} will perform multiple jumping attacks which will remove a serious amount of 1,128 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}} from {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} each time one hits him. The Captain will eventually become exhausted and vulnerable for a short amount of time.

Even with the strongest {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, it will still take three successful attempts to defeat one of the Captains this way. The other will continue throwing the orbs during the whole fight until one of them is defeated.

They both have a high chance of dropping one of the most valuable items, the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=999-Man Slicer|display=999-Man Slicer|link}}.
