The {{Term/Store|Kema Kosassa Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kema Kosassa Shrine}}<br/>A Major Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Major Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Kema Kosassa Shrine is located on the {{Term|BotW|Risoka Snowfield|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he's greeted by its monk, {{Term|BotW|Kema Kosassa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Kema Kosassa. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Kema Kosassa|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Kema Kosassa Shrine features a large primary chamber, from which a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} rises from a pit once Link gets near. In order to complete the Trial, Link must defeat this Guardian Scout.

Once it is defeated, a portcullis leading to Kema Kosassa will rise allowing Link to speak to him. He will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kema Kosassa|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kema Kosassa|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.
