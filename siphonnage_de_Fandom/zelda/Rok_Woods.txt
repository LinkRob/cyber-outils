The {{Term/Store|Rok Woods||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rok Woods|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Rok Woods are a large, wooded area south of the [[Skull Lake]] in [[Deep Akkala]]. The Rok Woods are filled with autumn-tinted [[Tree]]s which hide a wide array of [[Creature]]s and [[Forest Octorok]]s. [[Ore Deposit]]s can be found along the rock walls on the western side of the Woods.

A [[Korok]] can be found underneath a [[Rock]] hidden under a [[Rock Slab]] to the west side of the Rok Woods. A second Korok can be found in the forest, under a Rock atop a large Tree near the center of the grove.
