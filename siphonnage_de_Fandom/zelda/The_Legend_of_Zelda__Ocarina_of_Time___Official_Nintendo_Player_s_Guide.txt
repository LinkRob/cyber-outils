'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — Official Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Nintendo Power]] for {{OoT}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 128 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{OoT}}, as well as secrets and lists of collecables such as {{Plural|OoT|Piece of Heart|link}}. It also features a preface written and signed by [[Shigeru Miyamoto]].
