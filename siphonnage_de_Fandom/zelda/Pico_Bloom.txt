{{Term/Store|Pico Bloom|Pico Blooms|Series, TMC|plural}} are objects in {{TMC}}.<ref name="Cultivate">{{Cite|This is where we cultivate Pico Blooms. The {{Color|TMC Red|Picolyte}} sold in the human town is made from the {{Color|TMC Blue|nectar of our plants}}.|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Pico Bloom}} are flowers found exclusively {{Term|TMC|Inside the Barrel House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}. They are grown for their nectar, which is used to make {{Term|TMC|Picolyte|link}}.<ref name="Cultivate"/> More {{Plural|TMC|Pico Bloom}} can be grown if {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} gives to the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} {{Term|TMC|Inside the Barrel House}} the items he requires.<ref>{{Cite|You see that plant with the little bud? We're working on ways to improve it! I need {{Color|TMC Red|milk}} to continue my improvements on this Pico Bloom.|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref> Once a new bud blooms, {{Term|TMC|Beedle|link}} will begin selling {{Term|TMC|Picolyte}} of the same color at {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}.



How to get The Pico Bloom to bloom is leave the Minish Village and kill 15 enemies. You can fast travel with the ocarina back to the Minish Village. Go back in the the barrel and they will bloom.
