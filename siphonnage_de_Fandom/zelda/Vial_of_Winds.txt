The '''Vial of Winds''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
[[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] receives the Vial of Winds from [[Glebb]] in the [[Plain of Andor]] after Zelda brings her back water. The Vial of Winds is used in conjunction with the [[Raft]] in the [[Seacoast Plain]] port to reach the [[Shrine of Water]].
