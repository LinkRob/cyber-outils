'''Rapids''' are fast-moving currents of [[Water (Terrain)|water]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and uses==
Although Rapids push [[Link]] the opposite direction, he still has a modicum of control.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Stub|LA}}
In {{LA}}, Rapids are a feature in the [[Rapids Ride]] mini-game.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Stub|OoT}}
In {{OoT}} the currents of [[waterfall]]s and [[Zora's River]] serve to speed up Link's swimming pace.
