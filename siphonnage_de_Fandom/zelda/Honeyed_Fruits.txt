==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Honeyed Fruits are dishes made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Courser Bee Honey]] with any fruit, aside from {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}}, in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The regular version can be made by using fruits with an effect that counteracts that of the Courser Bee Honey. When eaten, they'll restore some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. This dish combines the sweetness of Courser Bee Honey with the sourness of fruits.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Only one variation of Honeyed Fruits can be made:

* '''Energizing Honeyed Fruits''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Honeyed Fruits}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A dish that combines the thick sweetness of honey with the acidity of sour fruits.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using neutral fruits, such as {{Plural|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}}. Eating them will refill a part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>
