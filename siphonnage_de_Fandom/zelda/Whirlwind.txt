==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
The {{Term|ST|Whirlwind}} can be found in the {{Term|ST|Forest Temple|link}}. It has the ability to pick up and move small items, like {{Plural|ST|Small Key|link}}. It can also blow around and stun lighter enemies,<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= The whirlwind shoots forward and stuns the monster! |game= ST |page= 25}}</ref> and send {{Term|ST|Phantom Zelda|link}} flying through short distances. The {{Term|ST|Whirlwind}} can be used as a propeller when {{Term|ST|Link}} moves through water while being on a {{Term|ST|Block|link}}, as well as extinguishing {{Plural|ST|Torch|link}}.

When {{Term|ST|Link}} is holding the {{Term|ST|Whirlwind}}, it brings up a line with which he can aim. {{Term|ST|Link}} must then blow into the {{Term|ST|Whirlwind}} to send a gust of {{Term|ST|Wind|link}} across the screen.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Choose a direction with the stylus, then blow! |game= ST |page= 25}}</ref> The longer he blows, the farther it travels, dying after a few seconds.
