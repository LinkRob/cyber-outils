{{Name|Japanese|Iona}} is a character in {{RTBToL}}, and one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s love interests.{{Name Ref|RTBToL}}

==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Iona}} works at the seed store in Vegetable Village. {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} first meets her in {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6|link}} at the entrance of the village. When she sees {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}, she runs off and locks herself within the seeds store. {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} will not be able to further interact with her until {{Term|RTBToL|Page 7|link}}, where he can give her gifts in a {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push|link}}. After her first heart is unlocked, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} and friends ask her for a plant-based fuel required for the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner|link}} to work. She accepts to help them harvest the {{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo|link}} fruits, however, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must need to find dust from the wings of the perfect butterfly as fertilizer, as well as the actual Seeds of {{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo}} and proper soil to plant them.

After some events, {{Term|RTBToL|Iona}} is finally able to plant the seeds, but, due to an error with the fertilizer, the tree grows extremely fast, transforming it into a monster.
Once the battle is over, {{Term|RTBToL|Iona}} thanks the group for saving the village, and gives them the fuel for the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner}}. She stays at the train station, and is seen again in {{Term|RTBToL|Page 14|link}} at the royal dance.
