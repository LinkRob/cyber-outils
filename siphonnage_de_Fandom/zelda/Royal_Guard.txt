=== Adventure of Link ===

The [[Swordsman]], [[Jermafenser]] was once the captain of the Royal Guard before being chosen to guard [[Midoro Palace]] for the [[King of Hyrule]]. <ref name="E 186">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 186 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
The {{Plural|OoT3D|Guard}} seen in the throne room in {{OoT}} wear royal purple breeches under their plate armor and graves. They also have more ornamental helmets with golden tips and purple spears. Several guards are depicted guarding the {{Term|OoT3D|King of Hyrule|link}} in his throne room, which can be seen through the window in the {{Term|OoT3D|Courtyard|link}}. If {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} shoots a {{Term|OoT3D|Deku Seed|link}} at the western window in the {{Term|OoT3D|Courtyard}}, a {{Term|OoT3D|Royal Guard}} will scold him. <ref>{{Cite|Hey you! Don't cause any trouble!|Soldier|OoT3D}}</ref> 

Ultimately they were either killed or forced to retreat when {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf|link}} overthrew the castle. Several broken spears and helmets can be found around the entrance to {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Castle|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
Several {{Plural|TPHD|Guard}} with ornate shields protected {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}}'s last stand against the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}} invasion. When the {{Plural|TPHD|Shadow Beast|link}} stormed the throne room, the {{Plural|TPHD|Guard}} were violently slain.

In certain areas of {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} can {{Term|TPHD|Sense|link}} the ghosts of the {{Term|TPHD|Royal Guard}}. They will point in the direction that he needs to go, or towards points of interest.

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW|-}}, the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard}} wear an {{Term|BotW|Armor Set}} known {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set|link}}. They wear a blue and red {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Uniform|link}} with white gloves, a beret style {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap|link}}, and white {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots|link}}. In one of the {{Plural|BotW|Memory|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} regains, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard}} are shown standing around {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} during his ceremony to entitle the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}}. During Recovered Memory #11 "[[Shelter from the Storm]]", while talking to Link as they wait for a storm to pass beneath a tree east of [[Scout's Hill]] near the [[Bridge of Hylia]], {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} mentions {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has dedicated himself to the path of a knight of the Royal Guard like his father, before asking him what he would do if he realized one day that he realized he just wasn't meant to be a fighter but everyone said he had to be one due to being born into a family of the Royal Guard, would he have chosen a different path. According to [[Purah's Diary]], Link was the youngest member to be appointed to Hyrule's Royal Guard, which she refers to as the "Imperial Guard of Hyrule Castle." 100 years after the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, Link is the only known surviving member of the Royal Guard and after {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon|link}}'s defeat he returns to his role as Princess Zelda's personal knight and bodyguard. Unlike other members of the Royal Guard, due to his status as the hero chosen by the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}} and the Hylian Champion, Link wears the {{Term|BotW|Champion's Tunic|link}} and [[Hylian Trousers]] instead of the Royal Guard Set. However one Royal Guard Set survived the Great Calamity, which Link can acquire during the "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. During said quest, Link must read {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4|link}} at the [[Riverside Stable]]. The author [[Traysi]] reveals that according to a former maid of Hyrule Compend Castle, the surviving Royal Guard Set can be found within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} and that she hopes one day that the kingdom is restored as she hopes that a Royal Guard in shining armor will come a sweep her off her feet.<ref name="SRMEXV4">{{Cite|Super Rumor Mill EX: Volume 4 [Read.] From the moment I wake to the moment I sleep, my mind is full of rumors. Even my dreams are gossipy! Traysi here! You fine folks keep up with dream rumors too, don't you? Well, this one's so good it might as well be a dream. Listen up, history buffs! This tidbit is about the brave soldiers who were tragically wiped out during the Great Calamity. {{Big|The royal guards who protected the royal family!}} Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. They say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on {{Color|BotW Red|the second floor}}. I do hope one day {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} is restored to its former glory, complete with royal guards. Hmm... I wonder when a royal guard in shining armor will come sweep me off MY feet... Traysi's Recommendation: ★★★★★|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref> 

Several {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Armor}} of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard}} are scattered around the ruined {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard}} used {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Claymore|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Uniform|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots|link}}.
