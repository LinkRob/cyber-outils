The {{Term/Store|Stick||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 140"/>{{Name Ref|LANS}}{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Stick}} was referred to as the '''Wooden Stick''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Wooden Stick}}<br/>During his adventure on Koholint Island, Link made Kiki the Monkey very happy by giving him the Bananas. In return, Link received the Wooden Stick. Link made similar trades like this with many of the island's inhabitants, and in the end his kindness was rewarded {{Typo|withthe|with the}} Magnifying Glass.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160308171202/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/w.jsp#WoodenStick|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the names given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
[[File:LANS Stick Text Icon.png|left]]
The Stick is the fifth item in the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You found a stick a monkey left behind... You take it!|N/A|LADX}}</ref> The Stick is obtained at [[Kanalet Castle]] after Link gives the [[Bananas]] to [[Kiki]] the [[Monkey]], when it is found left behind on the bridge that the Monkeys built to let Link cross the moat in thanks for his kindness.<ref name="E 140"/>

Once Link obtains the [[Sea Lily's Bell]] from the [[Key Cavern]], he finds Tarin near a beehive in [[Ukuku Prairie]], who asks if he can borrow the Stick.<ref>{{Cite|Oh?! Link , I see ya have a nice stick... Can I borrow it for a second?|Tarin|LADX}}</ref> Tarin uses it to knock the beehive down, but angers the [[Bee]]s and is chased away, leaving a [[Honeycomb]] behind that Link takes as the Trading Sequence's next item.<ref name="E 140"/>
