The {{Term/Store|Sarjon Woods||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Sarjon Woods'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Sarjon Woods are located in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} between [[Guchini Plain]] and [[Floria River]]. The Sarjon Woods are mostly grown on a raised plateau with many layers each containing trees. The Sarjon Woods' Plateau is surrounded by Floria River on the north and east, and by a path on the west and south.

One [[Korok]] can be found hiding in the Eastern Sarjon Woods. Below the plateau, a big tree sprouts out of the ground. Atop this tree is a [[Korok Balloon]], which [[Link]] can pop to make the Korok appear and obtain a [[Korok Seed]].<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
