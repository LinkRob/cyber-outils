{{Term/Store|Purple Peahat|Purple Peahats|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Purple Peahat|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Purple Peahat}} are stronger variants of the {{Term|CoH|Peahat|link}} enemy, possessing more health and attack power then their weaker counterpart. They also have their own beat pattern, capable of moving in every direction on every single beat of the music. While largely stationary enemies, they will fly away whenever {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} gets too close and will move erratically in any given direction for several beats before landing down to rest.
