{{Term/Store|Ore and More||Series, BotW}} is a shop in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ore and More|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Ore and More}} is a stall in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} that sells a small selection of {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} at a high price. It is opened when the {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} {{Term|BotW|Greyson|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Pelison|link}} move to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}, the latter of whom runs the stall.<ref>{{Cite|My little brother, Pelison, even started an ore shop. Check it out when ya get a chance!|Greyson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pelison}} states that the {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} he sells all come from the boulders that {{Term|BotW|Greyson}} breaks in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|display=Town}}, which are rich in {{Plural|BotW|Ore}} due to the area's close proximity to {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Once we started breakin' up the rock here, we found that it's rich in {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}}. So I've decided to open up shop! I'm not sure why we have so much ore here, but maybe it's because we're close to {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}. Anyway, these here are freshly mined. Be sure to buy a whole bunch!|Pelison|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pelison}} operates the stall at all times.

In {{TotK}}, the stall no longer exists, as Greyson mans Tarrey Town's railcar service and Pelison runs the Break-A-Part Shop.
