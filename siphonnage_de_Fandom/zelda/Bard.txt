The {{Term/Store|Bard||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300}}</ref> It is the name of two characters in [[Hyrule]] and [[Lorule]], both who reside in the [[Milk Bar]]s of their respective worlds. 

==Biography==
===Hyrule===
The Hyrulean Bard spends his time in the Milk Bar in [[Kakariko Village#A Link Between Worlds|Kakariko Village]]. For a fee of 10 [[Rupee]]s, he will play various songs alongside the [[Flute Boy]].<ref>{{Cite|In these dark days, you've got to keep your spirits up. A song will do you a world of good. How about a song to go with your milk? Just 10 Rupees.|Bard|ALBW}}</ref> He is a friend of the Flute Boy and explains to [[Link]] that the child dislikes speaking to strangers.<ref>{{Cite|It's like I told you, pal. He doesn't like strangers. Just the way it is.|Bard|ALBW}}</ref>

===Lorule===
The Lorulean Bard resides in the Milk Bar of {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}. He asks Link if he had met the [[Stalblind|boss]] of the town, but states he would not waste any time on him. He mentions that the boss is busy stashing away the [[Painting]] he got, which the Bard figures must be worth a fortune. He also knows about the [[Thief Girl]], who was imprisoned after discovering the Painting. Inspired by the recent events in town, he had composed a small song and asks Link if he wants to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|That whole thing kinda inspired me, actually. Wanna hear my latest ditty?|Bard|ALBW}}</ref> The song he sings contains part of the rhyme needed to enter the [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|Thieves' Hideout]].<ref>{{Cite|♪ Oh ho ho! Wrong place, wrong time, and the boss will be glad to put you back in line! ♪ A smart thief fears the boss's wrath! I'd rather be on a cliff walkin' a narrow path!|Bard|ALBW}}</ref>
