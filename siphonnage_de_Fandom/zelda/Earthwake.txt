'''Earthwake''' is an ability in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It first appears in {{BotW}} and returns in {{TotK}}.

==Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, Earthwake is used by [[Yiga Blademaster]]s and [[Monk Maz Koshia]] during combat and sends some earth at [[Link]] during the battle.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, Link can use the Earthwake skill by going into the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]] after visiting the three branches ([[Akkala Ancient Tech Lab]], southern base of [[Mount Drena]], [[Old Man's Cabin]]) getting the Yiga Armor Set, and doing Yiga training and defeating several Yiga in under a minute. A Blademaster will give Link the [[Earthwake Manual]], and after that, Link can also enter the room that he was not allowed in until he got training.

To use Earthwake, the player has to hold down the Y button while Link does not have a weapon equipped. Earthwake has no recharge, so it can be reused as many times as possible while inflicting little damage.
