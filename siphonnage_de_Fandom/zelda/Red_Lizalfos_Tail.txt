==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Red Lizalfos Tails are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by [[Fire-Breath Lizalfos]] upon their deaths. Fire-Breath Lizalfos can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and the region of [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]]. Their flavor makes them unsuitable for consumption, but they can be combined with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] accepts Red Lizalfos Tails in exchange for [[Mon]], but they can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Red [[Dye]].

Ten Red Lizalfos Tails, as well as five {{Plural|BotW|Ruby|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Snowquill Set]] to their final tiers at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Snowquill Set''': 30 (0, 0, 0, 10) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
