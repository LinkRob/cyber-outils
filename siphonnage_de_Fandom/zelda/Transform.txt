'''Transform''' is the down special move of both [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] and [[Sheik]] in {{SSBM}} and {{SSBB}}.

==Overview==
The move involves Zelda transforming into Sheik or vice versa. If either character gets hit while in the process of using Transform, the move is canceled.

In {{SSBB|-}}, Zelda or Sheik can use Transform while [[Kirby]]'s [[Final Smash]], {{ssb|Cook Kirby}}, is active to avoid being dragged into the cauldron.

Transform does not return in {{SSB4}} and {{SSBU}}, since both games include Zelda and Sheik as entirely separate fighters. Instead, Zelda has [[Phantom Slash]] as her side special move, while Sheik's is [[Bouncing Fish]].
