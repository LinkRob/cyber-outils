The {{Term/Store|Bombchu Shop||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a [[Shop]] in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238}}</ref>

==Features==
Located in the [[Back Alley]], it specializes in selling [[Bombchu]] in various quantities and prices. It is only opened at night. The Bombchu on the left side all come in quantities of 10, while the ones on the right contain 20 Bombchu. If [[Link]] buys all of the Bombchu available, the shop will not restock its merchandise.<Ref>{{Cite|This is a new type of bomb! It's available only in limited quantities, and when I'm sold out, that's it!|Bombchu Shop Owner|OoT}}</Ref>
