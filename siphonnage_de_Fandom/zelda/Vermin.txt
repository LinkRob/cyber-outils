'''''Vermin''''' is a single-screen game in the {{wp|Game & Watch}} series of handhelds, originally developed and released by [[Nintendo]] in 1980. In it, the player wields a pair of hammers to protect their garden from an onslaught of moles burrowing through the ground.

In celebration of the 35th anniversary of {{TLoZ|Series}}, a "Link Version" of ''Vermin'' is included in the {{G&WTLoZ}} collection. This version replaces the protagonist Mr. Game & Watch with {{Term|Series|Link|link}}, and the moles with {{Plural|Series|Octorok|link}}.
