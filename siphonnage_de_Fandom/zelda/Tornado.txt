'''Tornadoes''' are recurring [[object]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|There is a {{Color|TMC Blue|tornado}} atop this house. Enter it to go to the {{Color|TMC Green|Palace of Winds}}.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref> Tornadoes go unnamed in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
==={{TMC|-}}===
Tornados are three large vortices that appear at [[Veil Springs]], atop the [[Cloud Tops]], and inside the [[Palace of Winds]]. They propel [[Link]] upwards into the [[Sky]], allowing him to reach the Cloud Tops, the Palace of Winds, and the [[Gyorg Pair]] respectively.

The [[Crimson Cyclone]]s that appear in the Cloud Tops function identically to Tornadoes in that they launch Link upwards into a new area, but they are smaller and take the appearance of red [[Updraft]]s.

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{ZA|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:ZA Tornado Sprite.png|frame|left]]
A few Tornadoes appear only in the [[Shrine of Air]]. They are small moving twisters that wander aimlessly. Unlike most obstacles, Tornadoes cannot be dispatched with the [[Wand]], and Tornadoes can only be quelled by casting the [[Calm]] [[Spell]] on it three times.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|The three blue tornadoes can only be defeated with the Calm Spell.|De drie blauwe tornado's kunnen alleen worden verslagen met de Kalmerende Spreuk.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 3|issue= 12|date= December 1996|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=41}}</ref> Tornadoes can inflict a [[Heart]] of damage on [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], or just half a Heart if she has five or six [[Celestial Sign]]s.
{{Noncanon|End}}
