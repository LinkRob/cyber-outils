{{Term/Store|Prankster Poe|Prankster Poes|Series, TFH|plural}} are {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TFH|publisher= Prima|page= 18}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TFH|Prankster Poe}} appear in the {{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion|link}}, the {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}, and the [[Frozen Zone|Frozen]] and [[Shadow Zone|Shadow Zones]] of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}. They closely resemble the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, and even wear similar hats. {{Plural|TFH|Prankster Poe}} attack by sneaking up to the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} and grabbing them to throw them off the central platforms they are located at. They move quickly, requiring the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} to strike back before they can sneak up to them. {{Plural|TFH|Prankster Poe}} are initially translucent, but become clearer and are briefly stunned when they are struck. When they are whole, they can be harmed, and a second strike of the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} will damage them. After one {{Term|TFH|Prankster Poe}} is damaged, it will disappear and then reappear behind the {{Term|TFH|Links}} who attacked it, hoping to sneak in again before that {{Term|TFH|Links}} can react. They take several strikes of the {{Term|TFH|Sword}} to defeat. In the {{Term|TFH|Illusory Mansion}}, they appear as a group that ambush the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} in the {{Term|TFH|Level}}'s final {{Term|TFH|Stage}}. In the {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple}}, only two are fought, along with a pair of {{Plural|TFH|Chasupa|link}}.
