The {{Term/Store|Bird Statuette||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SSHD Gold|Bird Statuette}}!<br/>It proves your victory today.<br/>Wait till Zelda sees this!|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
At the beginning of the game, {{Term|SS|Link|link}} must participate in the {{Term|SS|Wing Ceremony|link}}. In this minigame, which will be used to teach {{Term|SS|Link}} how to fly properly, {{Term|SS|Link}} must catch a golden {{Term|SS|Loftwing|link}} that is carrying the {{Term|SS|Bird Statuette}} to become a senior at the {{Term|SS|Knight Academy|link}} and be one step closer to graduating.<ref>{{Cite|Good morning, [Link]! Good luck in the Wing Ceremony today! Just think: if you win today, you'll become a senior like us and you'll be a very big step closer to graduating as a knight.|Karane|SS}}</ref> Although {{Term|SS|Groose|link}}, {{Term|SS|Cawlin|link}}, and {{Term|SS|Strich|link}} will also try to catch it, {{Term|SS|Link}} ends up claiming the statue for his own, winning the race.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Bird Statuette! It proves your victory today. Wait till Zelda sees this!|N/A|SS}}</ref> The {{Term|SS|Bird Statuette}} is then used in the ceremony atop the {{Term|SS|Statue of the Goddess|link}} with {{Term|SS|Zelda|link}} so that she may offer it to the goddess {{Term|SS|Hylia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Link], hand me that Bird Statuette you grabbed in the race. I must offer it to the goddess.|Zelda|SS}}</ref>
