The {{Term/Store|Blue Juggler||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 245">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM, MM3D}}}}</ref> The {{Term|Series|Blue Juggler}} goes unnamed in {{OoT}}.{{Note|In the {{MM}} section of {{E}}, the twins are individually named "Red Juggler" and "Blue Juggler".<ref name="E 245"/> These names are not used in the {{OoT}} section, where they are instead collectively named the "Twin Jugglers",<ref name="E 238"/> despite the fact that they are never seen juggling in-game.}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|Series|Blue Juggler}} is a member of the {{Plural|Series|Twin Jugglers|link}} along with his twin brother, the {{Term|Series|Red Juggler|link}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Blue Juggler}} is a timid man who is constantly recounting his experiences with oddities and mysteries on {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule|link}}, only to mocked by the {{Term|OoT3D|Red Juggler|link}}.<ref name="Dampé 1">{{Cite|I tell you, I saw him! I saw the ghostly figure of Dampé the gravekeeper sinking into his grave. It looked like he was holding some kind of treasure!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref><ref name="Dampé 2">{{Cite|Wah hah hah hah hah hah! He must have been seeing things! Wah hah hah hah hah hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

When {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} first visits {{Term|OoT3D|Castle Town|link}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Blue Juggler}} tells him that he tried to sneak into the {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule Castle|linK|display= Castle}} and speak to {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to see Princess Zelda! I avoided the guards... Swam through the moat... I was almost there when they caught me!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> Whilst he made it past the {{Plural|OoT3D|Soldier|link}}, he got stuck in the drain hole due to his size and was caught. <ref>{{Cite|I found a small drain hole on the right side of the castle that I thought I could sneak into, but I got stuck there.|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> As a result, the security was increased and the {{Term|OoT3D|Red Juggler}} mocked him.<ref>{{Cite|Wha-ha-ha! What a crazy guy! Can you believe this guy was crazy enough to try to sneak into the castle to see Princess Zelda? All because of this idiot, they've tightened security at the castle. Wha-ha-hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

When {{Term|OoT3D|Impa|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Princess Zelda}} leave {{Term|OoT3D|Hyrule Castle}} on horseback pursued by {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf|link}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Blue Juggler}} reveals he was almost kicked by {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf's Steed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I almost got kicked by that black horse!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> This leads to the {{Term|OoT3D|Red Juggler}} calling him a coward.<ref>{{Cite|Look at that guy--he's so scared! What a wimp! Ha ha hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

Seven years later, the {{Term|OoT3D|Blue Juggler}} has fled to {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}} during {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf}}'s reign and lives in {{Term|OoT3D|Impa|link}}'s old house. He saw {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé|link}}'s ghost descending into his {{Term|OoT3D|Dampé's Grave|link|display= Grave}} with the {{Term|OoT3D|Hookshot|link}} in his hand, but was again dismissed and labeled timid by the {{Term|OoT3D|Red Juggler}}.<ref name="Dampé 1"/><ref name="Dampé 2"/>

When Kakariko village was set fire, the Blue Juggler was among the villagers who helped put out the flames.<ref>{{Cite|The fire almost consumed us all! But together, we all were able to put out the fire! Pretty good work, don't you think?|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

When {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} clears the {{Term|OoT3D|Shadow Temple|link}}, the {{Term|OoT3D|Blue Juggler}} sees him fly out of the temple surrounded by a blue light and tries to tell everyone about it.<ref>{{Cite|There he is! It's him! He's the one who dropped from the sky surrounded by a blue light! Now do you believe me?|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref> However the {{Term|OoT3D|Red Juggler}} labels this a delusion of his.<ref>{{Cite|Wah hah hah hah hah hah! He must have been seeing things! Wah hah hah hah hah hah!|Red Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

In his final appearance, he laments no one has believed him for the past seven years.<ref>{{Cite|Nobody believes what I've been telling them all these past seven years!|Blue Juggler|OoT}}</ref>

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Stub|MM3D}}
In {{MM}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Blue Juggler}} is a mirthful juggler who get along well with his twin.<ref>{{Cite|You see, we're entertainers. We must keep people smiling. No matter how grim things get, we must always be optimistic!|Twin Jugglers|MM}}</ref>
