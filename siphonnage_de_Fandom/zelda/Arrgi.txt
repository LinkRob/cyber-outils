{{Term/Store|Arrgi|Arrgi|Series, ALttP, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP |publisher= Nintendo |page= 154}}</ref><ref name="Encyclopedia"/> {{Plural|Series|Arrgi}} go unnamed in {{AST}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Arrgi}} appear as polyps in {{ALttP}}. They surround and protect {{Term|ALttP|Arrghus|link}} from any damage. {{Plural|ALttP|Arrgi}} can jump off {{Term|ALttP|Arrghus}} to attack and distract {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}. He can pull them away from {{Term|ALttP|Arrghus}} with the {{Term|ALttP|Hookshot|link}} and then slash them with the {{Term|ALttP|Sword|link}}. After all the {{Plural|ALttP|Arrgi}} are defeated, {{Term|ALttP|Link}} can start attacking {{Term|ALttP|Arrghus}} directly.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Arrgi}} appear as eyes surrounded by gelatin in {{ALBW}}. They surround and protect {{Term|ALBW|Arrghus|link}} from any damage. {{Plural|ALBW|Arrgi}} can jump off {{Term|ALBW|Arrghus}} to attack and distract {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}}. He can pull them away from {{Term|ALBW|Arrghus}} with the {{Term|ALBW|Hookshot|link}} and then slash them with the {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}}. After all the {{Plural|ALBW|Arrgi}} are defeated, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can start attacking {{Term|ALBW|Arrghus}} directly.
