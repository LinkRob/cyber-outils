==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Nefarious Ne'er-do-wells}}" is an event in which a greater number of antagonist {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= A collection of crooks are up to no good on the Spirit Board in this event!|author= Nintendo |published= June 23, 2020 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/8685e85a/index.html |title= Event: Nefarious Ne'er-do-wells |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
