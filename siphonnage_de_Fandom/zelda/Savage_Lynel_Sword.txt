{{Compendium/Extract|Savage Lynel Sword}}
{{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Sword}} are used by {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lynel|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lynel|link}}. As it takes time for {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} to scale up, {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Sword}} may not be available until later in the game. However, the {{Term|BotW|White-Maned Lynel}} in the {{Term|BotW|Second Gatehouse|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} does not scale, meaning it will always carry a {{Term|BotW|Savage Lynel Sword}}. They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are created and utilized by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> The blades of this {{Term|BotW|Savage Lynel Sword|display=Sword}} are said to be strong enough to cut down any foe.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Since they are made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Sword}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. If they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Sword}} will sink.
