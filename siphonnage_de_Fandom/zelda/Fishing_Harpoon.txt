{{Plural|BotW|Fishing Harpoon}} are tools used by fisherman to catch large {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found around {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}, though they are also used by {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}. Because of their sharp spearheads, {{Plural|BotW|Fishing Harpoon}} can be used as {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Because of their metal spearheads, {{Plural|BotW|Fishing Harpoon}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Fishing Harpoon}} will not burn.
