{{Term/Store|Sturgeon||Series, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258 {{Exp Game|TWW}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Sturgeon}}
In {{TWW}}, Sturgeon is a wise, elderly man who lives on [[Outset Island]] with his granddaughter, [[Sue-Belle]], on the upper floor of a home where his younger brother, [[Orca]], also resides.<ref>{{Cite|The hooligan I'm referring to is my younger and much less intelligent brother, who lives just downstairs...|Sturgeon|TWW}}</ref> Whereas his brother will spend the entire day studying the art of fighting,<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, the only thing he DOES study is the art of fighting.|Sturgeon|TWW}}</ref> Sturgeon will lock himself in his room and study all day, expanding his knowledge.<ref name="knowledge"/> Although very wise, Sturgeon has a very hot temper,<ref>{{Cite|Ah! My brother upstairs is very high-strung.|Orca|TWW}}</ref> and is hardly accommodating.<ref>{{Cite|He is quite wise, but with that big head of his, he is hardly...accommodating.|Orca|TWW}}</ref> He is easily angered by Orca's training,<ref name=hooligan>{{Cite|Such a racket! Can't you keep quiet!? You've knocked everything off my shelves, you dratted hooligan!|Sturgeon|TWW}}</ref> and often refers to his brother as a "hooligan".<ref name=hooligan></ref><ref>{{Cite|You just be sure to take the time to read it all through very carefully. If you don't, you'll surely end up like that useless hooligan of a little brother of mine who lives downstairs.|Sturgeon|TWW}}</ref> Even though he despises swordfighting, he was actually once an accomplished swordsman himself.<ref>{{Cite|Surprisingly, Sturgeon was an accomplished swordsman in his youth.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref>

At the beginning of the game, Sturgeon will teach Link the game's basic controls, such as [[Targeting System|L-Targeting]]. In Sturgeon's room, Link can see papers tacked to the walls, titled "[[Sturgeon's One-Point Lessons]]". These articles contain useful information on basic game mechanics, such as [[Heart]]s, [[Saving]], the use of [[Map|Area Maps]], and so on.

In his youth, Sturgeon set out with his brother on an arduous quest to collect ten [[Knight's Crest|Knight's Crests]], which would allow them to learn a powerful new sword technique.<ref>{{Cite|Any courageous soul who gathers ten of these crests can learn a powerful new sword technique!|Orca|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Both my brother and I dreamt of learning this technique in our youth. We worked so hard to collect the crests...|Orca|TWW}}</ref> However, even after many long years and adventures beyond count, they were unable to even approach finding ten crests, and they both grew old.<ref>{{Cite|But it took many long years and adventures beyond count before we even approached finding ten of them, and we both grew old...|Orca|TWW}}</ref> If Link brings presents 10 Knight Crests to Orca, he will teach Link the technique that they were never able to master: The {{Term|TWWHD|Hurricane Spin|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Sturgeon}} is found on the upper floor of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}. He is convinced that some of the books from the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} move on their own,<ref>{{Cite|Sometimes, these books move on their own! I've seen it with my own two eyes!|Sturgeon|TMC}}</ref> though it is simply the {{Term|TMC|Library Minish|link}} who live there. After {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} returns all three overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Sturgeon}} moves to put them back on the shelf with incredible speed.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, yes! Here I am! I'm here! I'll have these books put away lickety-split!|Sturgeon|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library}} closes, and {{Term|TMC|Sturgeon}} is absent for the remainder of the game.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure, why not?|Sturgeon|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Sturgeon}}'s name comes from the large fish called a {{Wp|Sturgeon}}. His brother, {{Term|Series|Orca|link}}, is also named after a sea creature.
