The {{Term/Store|Royal Engineer's Certificate||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer's Certificate}} is given to {{Term|ST|Link|link}} by {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|link}} during his {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer|link}} graduation ceremony.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, I'd completely forgotten that today was the graduation ceremony. Huh, I didn't know they gave engineer certificates to little kids like you...|Soldier|ST}}</ref> It certificates one as a full-fledged {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got your royal engineer's certificate! Now you're a full-fledged engineer!|N/A|ST}}</ref>

The text on the {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer's Certificate}} reads "I hereby recognize our newest royal engineer. Work hard, for we all rely on you."<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 40}}</ref>
