{{Term/Store|Dr. Left's House||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= Insert Map}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Biologist's Home||OoS}},<ref>{{Cite|Biologist's Home|Map|OoS}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The {{Term|OoS|Biologist's Home}} is located in the southeastern area of {{Term|OoS|Horon Village|link}}. A {{Term|OoS|Bed|link}} and several bookshelves may be found inside, along with {{Term|OoS|Dr. Left|link}}. He will give {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} the {{Term|OoS|Cuccodex|link}} should he light the {{Term|OoS|Torchlight|link}} next to the {{Term|OoS|Book|link}} that the {{Term|OoS|Dr. Left|display=Doctor}} is reading.<ref>{{Cite|Splendid! I can finally focus! Here! Take this {{Color|OoS Red|Cuccodex}}!|Dr. Left|OoS}}</ref>

A portion of the eastern wall is {{Term|OoS|Cracked Wall|link|display=Cracked}}. If {{Term|OoS|Link}} blows it up with a {{Term|OoS|Bomb|link}}, he will be able to exit the {{Term|OoS|Biologist's Home}} through a side room. Outside, in {{Term|OoS|Dr. Left}}'s yard, is a pool of {{Term|OoS|Water (Terrain)|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link}} can cross it when it freezes during {{Term|OoS|Winter|link}}, or using the {{Term|OoS|Zora's Flippers|link}} or the {{Term|OoS|Roc's Cape|link}}, in order to reach a {{Term|OoS|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 20 {{Plural|OoS|Rupee|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House}} is located along the western edge of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, near the staircase. {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left|link}} can be found inside, as well as some {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} is the owner of an overdue {{Term|TMC|Library Book|link}} titled {{Term|TMC|Legend of the Picori|link}}, which {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must locate and return to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The next overdue book is... Let's see... Ah, yes! Of course...Legend of the Picori! It's been out forever! It was checked out by a somewhat absentminded scholar, I believe...|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|TMC}}</ref> Before {{Term|TMC|Link}} has to find the overdue {{Term|TMC|Library Book}}, {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left}} will not allow him into his {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House|display=House}}, as he is absorbed in his incessant study of the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What is all of this!? I'm busy, don't you see that? Do not disturb my research! If you have no business, begone! You heard me!|Dr. Left|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Plural|TMC|Power Bracelet|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Fountain|link}}, he is able to move the bookshelves within {{Term|TMC|Dr. Left's House}}, helping him obtain the overdue {{Term|TMC|Library Book}}.
