{{Term/Store|Deep Woods, Deep Shadow||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 67 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Deep Woods, Deep Shadow|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Deep Woods, Deep Shadow}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} are given eighteen minutes to defeat 12 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. Only 10 can be found at first, are split up into groups and found in corners of the map. The first is a group of two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}, the second is a lone {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox|link}}, the third is a {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus|link}} pair, and the fourth is a group of three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}. Although they do not have to be defeated, many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}} are scattered around the map as well. The eleventh {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}} is a a {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox|link}} that spawns when the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox}} is defeated, and the last two are {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}, who spawn in the center of the map once the first 10 are defeated. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any four {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Radiant Mask|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Radiant Shirt|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Radiant Tights|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
