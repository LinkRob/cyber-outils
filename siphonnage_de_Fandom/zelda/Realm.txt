*'''[[Realm of the Heavens]]''', the eighth and last level of {{FSA}}.
*'''[[Forest Realm]]''', the southwestern quadrant of the re-established kingdom of [[Hyrule]] in {{ST}}.
*'''[[Snow Realm]]''', the northwestern quadrant of the re-established kingdom of Hyrule in {{ST}}.
*'''[[Ocean Realm]]''', the southeastern quadrant of the re-established kingdom of Hyrule in {{ST}}.
*'''[[Fire Realm]]''', the northeastern quadrant of the re-established kingdom of Hyrule in {{ST}}.
*'''[[Sand Realm]]''', located in the upper portion of the Ocean Realm in the re-established kingdom of Hyrule in {{ST}}.
*'''[[Dark Realm]]''', the realm where the [[Demon Train]] is located in {{ST}}.
*[[Illusory Realm|'''Illusory Realm''']], a realm Link accesses in the [[The Champions' Ballad|Champion's Ballad]] [[Downloadable Content|DLC]] in order to fight the [[Blight Ganon|Blight Ganons]] once again in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]].''
