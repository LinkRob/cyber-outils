The '''Red Bow''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Red Bow used to belong to the [[Red Knight]], but it was stolen from him long ago. is sold by a [[merchant]] in the [[Shrine of Strength]] for 50 [[Rupee]]s. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] is required to purchase the Red Bow for the Red Knight to appear.
