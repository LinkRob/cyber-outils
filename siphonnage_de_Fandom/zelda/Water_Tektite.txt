{{Term/Store|Water Tektite|Water Tektites|Series, ALttP, LA, LADX, LANS, OoS, OoA, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 212 {{Exp Game|ALttP, LADX, OoS, OoA, ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Appearing only in the {{Term|ALttP|Swamp Palace|link}} in {{ALttP}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Water Tektite}} usually live in groups on shallow and deep water and move in diagonal motions towards {{Term|ALttP|Link}} as he attempts to get past. They usually appear in groups of three. While not that dangerous on their own, not being overly strong, they often appear alongside the more dangerous {{Plural|ALttP|Pirogusu|link}} and {{Plural|ALttP|Kyameron|link}} and other traps.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Plural|LANS|Water Tektite}} move in diagonal motions and appear in the {{Term|LANS|Angler's Tunnel|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Catfish's Maw|link}}, but do not actively home in on {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} like those in the previous game. They are unable to jump and can never leave the water.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Plural|OoS|Water Tektite}} appear out in the open in {{OoS}}, found primarily around {{Term|OoS|Spool Swamp|link}}, skating around {{Plural|OoS|Goponga Flower|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Plural|OoA|Water Tektite}} can be found throughout the {{Term|OoA|Mermaid's Cave|link}} in {{OoA}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Water Tektite}} appear solely in the {{Term|ALBW|Swamp Palace|link}} of {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} in {{ALBW}}. While they don't appear in as many numbers as those in {{ALttP}}, their behavior is similar. They are a little weaker than their counterparts in {{ALttP|-}}, not causing as much damage on contact. They appear largely identical to their original form, but now have some antennae or eyestalks on top of the main part of their body.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Water Tektite}} in {{TFH}} are identical to their appearance in {{ALBW|-}}. They are found in the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}} and in the {{Term|TFH|Flooded Zone|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}.
