The '''Mountain Passage''' is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mountain Passage|N/A|PH}}</ref>

==Features==
Situated in {{Term|PH|Mercay Island}}, the {{Term|PH|Mountain Passage}} is a small passage of two floors that is infested with basic {{Plural|PH|Enemy|link}}. It offers a few basic puzzles that {{Term|PH|Link}} will encounter throughout the rest of his journey, such as pulling levers in a certain order or obtaining {{Plural|PH|Small Key|link}} from monsters.

{{Term|PH|Link}} is required to go through the {{Term|PH|Mountain Passage}} at the beginning of the game to get to the other side of {{Term|PH|Mercay Island}} due to the bridge being broken. To get to the Passage, {{Term|PH|Link}} must go through a small path west of {{Term|PH|Oshus|link}}'s house. Once the young hero successfully clears the Passage, he will exit right above the {{Term|PH|Mercay Tavern|link}}. Since the bridge eventually gets fixed, the {{Term|PH|Mountain Passage}} becomes unnecessary to access the east side of the Island.
