The {{Term/Store|Hero's Charm||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an item in {{TWW}}.<ref name="E 127">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127}}</ref> It is a mask that reveals an enemy's remaining health.<ref name="E 127"/>

==Location and Uses==
In {{TWW}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Charm}} from {{Term|TWWHD|Mrs. Marie|link}} by giving her an additional 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Joy Pendant|link}} after having accepted the {{Term|TWWHD|Cabana Deed|link}} for 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Joy Pendant}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Are you giving these to me as well? But I told you I was perfectly satisfied with the {{Color|TWW Red|20}} you gave me before! [...] To thank you, I have to give you my most precious of precious belongings! I shall give you...this very special piece of {{Color|TWW Red|jewelry}}! Now, close your eyes, dear... This {{Color|TWW Red|item is so rare}}, there is not another like it anywhere in the world!|Mrs. Marie|TWW}}</ref> In {{TWWHD}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Charm}} is in a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} at the bottom of the {{Term|TWWHD|Savage Labyrinth|link}}.

The {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Charm}} is equipped and unequipped from the {{Term|TWWHD|Quest Status Screen|link}}. When [[Targeting System|targeting]] an enemy with it equipped, their life gauge is displayed as a horizontal red meter above them. The mask also functions during most boss battles, only with a longer life gauge across the bottom of the screen. The {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Charm}} cannot show life gauge for {{Term|TWWHD|Puppet Ganon|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Ganondorf|link}}, both of which are defeated by special attacks rather than by gradual depletion of health.

The {{Term|TWWHD|Hero's Charm}} consists of two conjoined [[Sheikah Eye]]s whose stems curl back to form mandibles. The stems are joined by a set of teeth which cover the wearer's mouth. The teeth are curved upward in a haunting smile.
