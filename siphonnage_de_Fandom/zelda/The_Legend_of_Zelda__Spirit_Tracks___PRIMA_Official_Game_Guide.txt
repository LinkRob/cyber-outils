'''''The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks — Prima Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{ST}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 319 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{ST}}, a listing of {{Plural|ST|Item|link}}, collectibles, characters, {{Plural|ST|Enemy|link}}, and {{Plural|ST|Boss|link}}, as well as an overview of the game's {{Term|ST|Multiplayer|link}} mode.
