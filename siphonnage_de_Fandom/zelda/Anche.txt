{{Term/Store|Anche||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anche|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Anche}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who manages the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|link}}.<ref name="Manage">{{Cite|Seeing as we live in a desert, it's pretty important stuff for us Gerudo... So I manage this place 24 hours a day.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> She is often seen counting the {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}}, and she carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar|link}} on her person. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to her, she explains that the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}} stores the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} that is collected from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is an {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}} used for storing the ice brought down from the mountains.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Anche}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} is important for the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} people.<ref name="Manage"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Anche}} while she is in {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}} during the night, she tells him to come back tomorrow and that her work is done for the day.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|...Who are you? My work's...done for the day... If you...need something... come back...tomorrow...}}|Anche|BotW}}</ref>

During "{{Term|BotW|The Perfect Drink|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Furosa|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to talk to {{Term|BotW|Anche}} about obtaining {{Term|BotW|Ice}} for {{Term|BotW|Pokki|link|display=Pokki's}} drink.<ref name="Furosa"/> She also mentions that {{Term|BotW|Anche}} goes to bed early, so {{Term|BotW|Link}} should speak to her while it is bright out.<ref>{{Cite|You should also know that she heads to bed quite early. If you plan to go, you'll need to get there while it's bright out.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> When the situation is explained, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|I see... Well, if Furosa wants ice, then you should bring her some. You're permitted to take some ice.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> She reminds him to move quickly due to the heat, since {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} can not do anything with {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You've taken the heat into account, right? If you move too slowly, the ice will surely melt. Furosa has no use for water...|Anche|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will be diappointed if the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} melts,<ref>{{Cite|...You've gotta be kidding me.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> though she accepts {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s mistake and allows him to take another block.<ref>{{Cite|Even the Gerudo make mistakes transporting ice, sometimes. Just grab another block and try again.|Anche|BotW}}</ref>

After completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, speaking to {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will cause her to ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to once again deliver {{Term|BotW|Ice}} to {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} for her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Talk about great timing! Can you go deliver ice to Furosa again?|Anche|BotW}}</ref> While she usually delivers the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} herself,<ref>{{Cite|Usually I'm the one who's supposed to deliver it, but...|Anche|BotW}}</ref> she explains that the nearby monsters are preventing her from leaving the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lately there've been more and more monsters skulking around here, so I can't be away from the icehouse.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Anche}} charges {{Term|BotW|Link}} 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} for the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} and explains that {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will reimburse him.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, before you carry the ice, I'll have to ask you to pay the {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}} for the ice in advance, though... Once you've delivered it, Furosa should be able to reimburse you for the cost of the ice.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has paid the fee, the {{Term|BotW|Carry the Ice!|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} will begin. {{Term|BotW|Link}} is timed while delivering the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} and will be rewarded with extra {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} if he delivers it quickly enough.<ref>{{Cite|And if you deliver it quickly, Furosa will probably reward you for the effort, too.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts the task, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} expresses joy and reiterates that {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will be waiting for him at the end of the nearby ruins.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! That's the spirit! So you're heading to the same place as before...|Anche|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|There's a group of ruins on the way to {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}—you know those? Furosa should be at the exit nearest to town.|Anche|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to melt a formation of {{Term|BotW|Ice}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}} or if {{Term|BotW|Anche}} detects any {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will scold him for damaging her supply before kicking him out.<ref>{{Cite|You! What's the idea, bringing that fire into the icehouse?! Get out of here with that! You're gonna ruin our supply!|Anche|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey! Enough of that!}} If that ice melts, you better believe you'll be sorry! Get out!|Anche|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Anche}} detects {{Term|BotW|Fire}} but cannot find the culprit (such as if {{Term|BotW|Link}} sets the {{Term|BotW|Fire}} while she is asleep and then leaves), she will angrily ask who is responsible for putting it there.<ref>{{Cite|Fire?! WHO DID THAT?!|Anche|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is caught enough times, she will inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she will not be held responsible for the punishment being brought to him and demands that he leave.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|YOU ARE SO DEAD!}} {{Small|Go ahead, tear up the ice room... I won't be held responsible for what happens next...}} {{Big|GET OUT!!}}|Anche|BotW}}</ref>
