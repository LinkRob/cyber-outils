==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Fang}} are {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}} upon defeat. They are worn down and dull, but the material is hard.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Fang}} can be used with other ingredients to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Mon|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} can use {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Fang}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Light Yellow Dye|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Fang}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
