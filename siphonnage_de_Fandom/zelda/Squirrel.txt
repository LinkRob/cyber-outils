{{Term/Store|Squirrel|Squirrels|Series}} are recurring [[Animals]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 63 {{Exp Game|Series}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:ALttP Squirrel.gif|left]]
In {{ALttP}}, Squirrels appear frolicking near the [[Pedestal of Time]] in the [[Lost Woods]]. In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of the game, [[Link]] must catch a Squirrel as a part of the [[Lumberjack's Riddles]].<ref>{{Cite|It munches on nuts...|Q. Bumpkin|ALttP}}</ref>

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, Squirrels appear in the [[Ordona Province]]. Once Link returns to [[Ordon Village]] in his [[Wolf Link|Wolf form]], one Squirrel tells him to talk with other animals to gather information.<ref>{{Cite|If you run into any trouble, try talking to the animals in town. We animals understand one another, so you'll be fine!|Squirrel|TP}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Squirrels reside in the Lost Woods, near the Pedestal of Time. They run away when Link moves close to them. In the ending, Squirrels are seen gathering around [[Gulley]] in the [[Haunted Grove]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bushy-Tailed Squirrel|link}} can be found in wooded areas. They can be hunted down by using the [[Bow]]. When defeated, they drop a single [[Acorn]].<ref>{{Cite|They tend to live in forests and subsist on acorns and other nuts.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{OoA|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{OoA|Himekawa}}, Link and [[Impa]] enter a forest in search of the Oracle of Ages, [[Nayru]]. They are stopped by the forest animals, including a Squirrel, that them to go back.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Go back. Go back. Go back.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 22}}</ref> The animals let the two pass after realizing Link is a [[Hylian]] of the old blood.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= His ears are pointed. He's a Hylian. A Hylian of the old blood!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 23}}</ref> A Squirrel listens to Nayru sing and as she speaks with Impa and Link. When 
it is learned that Impa is possessed by [[Veran]], the Squirrel and other animals charge her. The charge does nothing against Veran and she possesses the oracle. After Veran uses Nayru to travel to Labrynna's past, the Squirrel watches as Link picks up the [[Harp of Ages]] and talks to [[Ralph]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
