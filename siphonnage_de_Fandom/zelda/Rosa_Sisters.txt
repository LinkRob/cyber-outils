The {{Term/Store|Rosa Sisters||Series, MM}} are a group in {{MM}}.<ref name="Notebook">{{Cite|Rosa Sisters, twin dancers Members of the Gorman Troupe|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Rosa Sisters are twin dancers in the [[Gorman Troupe]] who are visiting [[Clock Town]] to participate in the annual [[Carnival of Time]].<ref name="Notebook"/> They are one of the twenty characters in the game featured in the [[Bombers' Notebook]], though they are both covered under one entry. The Bombers' Notebook does not mention their names, but by talking to the sisters at the [[Stock Pot Inn]], it is revealed that Judo is the name of the one in the red dress, while Marilla is the one with the blue dress. 

The Rosa Sisters can be found during the day walking around the [[Stock Pot Inn]]. During the night, they are found practicing their dance moves in [[West Clock Town]]. They seem troubled as they will have to perform their choreography at the Carnival of Time without any prior rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|We have to be ready without any rehearsal this time...Every time...|Judo|MM}}</ref> Moreover, their dance is not finished yet,<ref>{{Cite|Doo-doo-dooo... Don't speak to her right now. She hasn't finished her new dance for the town's carnival Doo-da-daa... Even though the song is finished.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> and they cannot decide on what other moves should be added to the dance in order to not bore the audience.<ref>{{Cite|Judo's very good at it...Maybe we should add some spins... If we do a poor job of extending it, the audience will get bored.|Marilla|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We need a new step...Maybe with more jumping...|Judo|MM}}</ref> They also decided not to change the song since [[Guru-Guru]], the troupe's musician, could get angry at such decision.<ref>{{Cite|We shouldn't change the song now... Guru-Guru is scary when he gets mad...|Marilla|MM}}</ref> Marilla also thinks about blaming [[Gorman]], the troupe leader, and going back to her village, but realizes her sister Judo would not approve of her decision.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I'll blame it on the troupe leader and go back to the village... But Judo wouldn't like it.|Marilla|MM}}</ref>

By wearing [[Kamaro's Mask]], the mask filled with the dancing spirit of [[Kamaro]], [[Link]] can teach the Rosa Sisters the choreography of the deceased dancer. The twins are impressed by the dance moves and quickly learn the choreography, joining Link in the routine and calling him their master.<ref>{{Cite|You are our master!|Rosa Sisters|MM}}</ref> The twins thank him with a [[Piece of Heart]] and ask him to go to their performance.<ref>{{Cite|Master! Please come to our performance!|Rosa Sisters|MM}}</ref> Even though, they are bothered at the fact that their troupe leader will not watch their dance.<ref>{{Cite|Although the dance for the carnival is ready, the troupe leader won't come to view it.|Guru-Guru|MM}}</ref> They will also have to perform their moves without even seeing the stage.<ref>{{Cite|We're pretty prepared, but our troupe leader seems to be acting strange for some reason. Well, we haven't even seen the stage yet...It looks like we're going straight to the big show without any rehearsal.|Rosa Sisters|MM}}</ref> The Rosa Sisters are last seen during the ending credits performing the choreography taught to them by Link at the [[Milk Bar#Majora's Mask|Milk Bar]]. The visible audience is only a [[Soldier]], presumably the one from West Clock Town who used to watch them practice.

==Trivia==
*In {{MM3D}}, if Gorman informs the Rosa Sisters of the concert's cancellation after Link teaches them Kamaro's Dance, they will still ask him to attend their performance. This may be due to a development oversight.
