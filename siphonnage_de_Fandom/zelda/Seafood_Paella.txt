{{Term/Store|Seafood Paella|Seafood Paellas|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Paella}}<br/>No fisherman's birthday bash would be complete without this top-shelf seafood dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Seafood Paella}}<br/>No fisherman's birthday bash would be complete without this top-shelf seafood dish.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Seafood Paellas are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}}, and either a {{Term|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}} or an {{Term|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}} together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= {{Color|NM Brown|'''Recipe Data'''<br/>1 Hearty Blueshell Snail, 1 Hylian Rice, 1 Goat Butter, 1 Rock Salt, 1 Mighty Porgy (or an Armored Porgy)}} |magazine= Nintendo Magazine |publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021 |page= 17}}</ref> Their default version can be made by using both types of Porgies in the recipe. By eating them, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can heal twelve of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are considered top-shelf and are common in the birthday celebrations of fishermen.<ref name="Inventory"/>

When Link speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kiana|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, she will ask her son {{Term|BotW|Kinov|link}} what she should make for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|What should I cook for tonight's dinner?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Kinov responds that she should make Seafood Paella,<ref>{{Cite|Make your {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}! It's so good and yummy and great!|Kinov|BotW}}</ref> to which Kiana agrees.<ref>{{Cite|That settles it! I'll make some {{Color|BotW Blue|seafood paella}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> However, since she's low on ingredients, she asks Link to retrieve and Hearty Blueshell Snail and Goat Butter for her.<ref>{{Cite|We seem to be a bit short on ingredients though. We'll need {{Color|BotW Red|goat butter}} and a {{Color|BotW Red|hearty blueshell snail}}.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, I know! Could you go an get me the ingredients we're missing?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> When Link returns with the ingredients, Kiana will ask for them.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You have the ingredients! Can I have them?|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> She tells Link she'll start cooking and gives him 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|All right, I'll take those, then. Now to start cooking. Here's a little something...|Kiana|BotW}}</ref> Kiana also invites Link to eat with her family since he brought her the ingredients, giving him Seafood Paella as well.<ref>{{Cite|Since you found the missing ingredients, why don't you eat with us? It'll be done soon.|Kiana|BotW}}</ref>

Because the required ingredients are of contradicting effects, no variations of Seafood Paella can be made.
