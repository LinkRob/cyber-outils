The {{Term/Store|Takama Shiri Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Takama Shiri Shrine}}<br/>Dual Purpose}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Dual Purpose''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri Shrine}} is found in the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, though it is initially buried beneath the sand. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} accessed as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} by riding a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} through a series of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} across the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens}} in a certain amount of time. The worker at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Essa|link}}, can be found in near the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens}} attempting to recruit wild {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}}.<ref>{{Cite|Frelly asked me to go recruit some wild {{Color|BotW Red|sand seals}}.|Essa|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} can either or rent a trained {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} from {{Term|BotW|Frelly|link}} or grab a wild one.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|sand seals}} in town were all trained by Frelly. They're faster, and they'll wait for you to return to them. By comparison, the ones outside who haven't been trained will just run off the moment you dismount.|Essa|BotW}}</ref> The first {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}} can be found nearby.

While riding through the {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be attacked by several {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}}. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} successfully passes through the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, the {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri Shrine}} will rise from beneath the ground nearby. Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is offered a trial by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Takama Shiri. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Takama Shiri|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds several metal cubes and platforms attached to rails that he can slide back and forth using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. Throughout the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must connect electrical circuits by moving the metal objects to the correct spot. Doing so will activate {{Plural|BotW|Switch|link}} that open gates to the next room.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} cannot stand on the metal objects while current flows through them without being electrocuted unless he wears the full {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor (Set)|link}} set or the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}. Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can make his way to the end of the room on non-conducting metal objects before completing the puzzle to avoid getting electrocuted.

In the third room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can activate a {{Term|BotW|Switch}} that leads to a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Bow|link}}. After solving the third room, {{Term|BotW|Link}} gains access to the final room of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, where he meets the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri}}.

{{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s resourcefulness and rewards him with {{Term|BotW|Naboris's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Naboris's Emblem.}}|Takama Shiri|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to collect all three of {{Plural|BotW|Naboris's Emblem}} to reveal a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Takama Shiri|BotW}}</ref>

If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits during "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song}}", {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that two of {{Plural|BotW|Naboris's Emblem}} remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Takama Shiri|BotW}}</ref> If it is the second {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} he visits, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will tell him that one remains.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Takama Shiri|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third and final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} he visits, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will tell him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Naboris...}}|Takama Shiri|BotW}}</ref>
