"{{Term/Store|By Firefly's Light||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|By Firefly's Light}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has completed both "{{Term|BotW|Find the Fairy Fountain|link}}" and "{{Term|BotW|Flown the Coop|link}}", he can speak to {{Term|BotW|Lasli|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, who complains she has to head home straight away instead of searching for {{Plural|BotW|Sunset Firefly|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* They say the roads are dangerous at night, so I have to come straight home without searching for {{Color|BotW Red|fireflies}}...|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> She misses them and has become sick of going without them.<ref>{{Cite|I miss them so much. And the night sky... Now it's the same tired thing every night. I'm so sick of it...|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lasli}} wishes that the {{Plural|BotW|Sunset Firefly}} could come to her so she could see them,<ref>{{Cite|If only the {{Color|BotW Red|fireflies}} would come to play here so I could still see them...|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> which begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

To satisfy {{Term|BotW|Lasli}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must gather five {{Plural|BotW|Sunset Firefly}}. They can be found around the bridge nearby, though they can be obtained from anywhere. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lasli}} with {{Term|BotW|Sunset Firefly|display=Fireflies}} in his {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}}, she notices their glowing and demands to see them.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? That glowing... Could it be you caught some?! Show me, show me!|Lasli|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} releases five {{Plural|BotW|Sunset Firefly}} in her home, {{Term|BotW|Lasli}} will stand and express her happiness.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|...Oh! Wow!}}<br/><br/>Look at all those {{Color|BotW Red|fireflies}}!|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> To express her gratitude, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you... This is all I have to offer, but please accept it with my gratitude!|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> She then comments on how pretty their glow is, describing it as magical.<ref>{{Cite|They glow so prettily... It's magical...|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says that {{Term|BotW|Lasli}} is also pretty, she will thank him.<ref>{{Cite|[You're pretty too.] Heehee! Thanks!|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} reminds her of their short lifespan, she becomes frustrated and remarks that boys are unromantic.<ref>{{Cite|[They don't live long.] Ugh! Boys are SO unromantic...|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.
