{{Term/Store|Laflat||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Laflat|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Laflat}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} who serves as the chief secretary for the {{Term|BotW|Zora}} royal family.<ref name="Laflat">{{Cite|Oh, where are my manners? I am Laflat. I am the chief secretary for the royal family.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, she can be seen inspecting {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|link}} for damage in the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The damage is minimal...|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} cautiously asks if he is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you...a Hylian?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> Despite how he answers, she will then introduce herself and her role to him.<ref name="Laflat"/> After a moment, she asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is curious about {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}}.<ref>{{Cite|... Are you curious about this statue of Lady Mipha?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims that he is, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} explains that the statue depicts {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}, the former {{Term|BotW|Zora}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes, I'm curious.] If you do not mind...please allow me to explain. This is a statue of the former Zora Champion. Her name was Lady Mipha.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> According to her, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} was both kind and beautiful, and she was capable of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Grace|link|display=healing}} wounded soldiers.<ref>{{Cite|She was as kind as she was beautiful... They say her ability to heal wounded soldiers was beyond compare.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> Though they are old now, these soldiers still remember and love {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref>{{Cite|Those soldiers she healed...they are old and decrepit now. But they remember. For this reason, the elders of the domain all love Lady Mipha dearly.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to speak to them if he wants to know more.<ref>{{Cite|If you would like to know more, I suggest you try talking to one of them.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} can be found near the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}} gazing out at {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, she asks him to confirm his identity.<ref>{{Cite|You are Link...right?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} wonders why she wants to know, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}} left {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} shouting that he would stop {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Hylian}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Why do you ask?] Prince Sidon ran off through the eastern gate. He was shouting something like... "I'm off to stop the Divine Beast! Link the Hylian is coming with me!"|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he plans on ascending {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain}} in search of {{Plural|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I assume that means you're going up {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}} in search of {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms this, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} reveals that she has a request for him.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] If so, I have a request that is... somewhat related.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She explains that there is a monster called a {{Term|BotW|Lynel|link}} that lives on top of {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a red-maned monster called a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} that lives on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} needs a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of this {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} or a similar one.<ref>{{Cite|I REALLY need a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. It can be a picture of any red-maned {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. Not just the one on the mountain.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he can get this {{Term|BotW|Picture}} for her.<ref>{{Cite|So what do you say? Can you get me a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts her request, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} will be relieved they settled things so quickly.<ref>{{Cite|[Understood!] I am glad that was settled so quickly.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She further explains that there is an {{Term|BotW|Gruve|link|display=individual}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} who is encouraging others to jump into the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Shatterback Point|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|See, there's this fool in the domain who goads people into jumping off of {{Color|BotW Blue|Shatterback Point}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. It's an incredibly dangerous jump, and this guy pressures people into jumping off by questioning their courage.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} is afraid that, if he continues pressuring people, someone may end up injured.<ref>{{Cite|He's a real bottom-feeder. If this keeps up, someone is surely going to wind up horribly injured!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She needs the {{Term|BotW|Picture}} to show the rest of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} how scary the {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} is, in hopes that hearing of its description will deter them from approaching {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain}}.<ref>{{Cite|I need a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} to show everyone how scary they are. That way, maybe people will stop going up there. Does that answer your question, Link? I'll leave the details to you. Just bring me a {{Color|BotW Red|picture of a Lynel}} so I can descrive then in terrible detail. Thank you!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> When she finishes speaking, the "{{Term|BotW|Lynel Safari|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of a {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} to show it to {{Term|BotW|Laflat}}, who asks him how his mission is coming along.<ref>{{Cite|Link! How is that {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} mission coming along?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs her that he has a {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} requests that he show it to her.<ref>{{Cite|[I have your picture.] Oh, wow! Please show me!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> Upon seeing the {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}, she will at first scream in shock, but will quickly compose herself.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|WAAAH?!}} *ahem* My apologies... That sound was not very dignified, was it? P-pardon me... I'm all better now. That {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}... How terrifying!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She believes that anyone who sees this {{Term|BotW|Picture}} would avoid visiting {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain}}.<ref>{{Cite|One look at this, and I doubt anyone would get any reckless ideas about visiting {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laflat}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}}, revealing her plan to tell the children of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} about the {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! I'll tell the children of the domain about this. Maybe that will scare them away from the mountain.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a pair of {{Term|BotW|Zora Greaves|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, right! Now for your reward...|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that they were once made by skilled craftsmen on behalf of an ancient {{Term|BotW|Zora}} king, but they are now fairly common.<ref>{{Cite|These {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora greaves}} were once made by skilled craftsmen by order of an ancient Zora king. Now they're fairly common.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Term|BotW|East Reservoir Lake}} was finished, the {{Term|BotW|Zora Greaves}} were made to give to visiting {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}} as a sign of friendship.<ref>{{Cite|As you may know, {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}} was created in a collaboration between Hylians and the Zora. After {{Color|BotW Blue|East Reservoir Lake}} was finished, we made Zora greaves as a token of friendship for Hylians who came to visit.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

However, when the number of {{Plural|BotW|Hylian}} visiting {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} dwindled, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}}'s father was forced to close his {{Term|BotW|Armor Shop|link}}.<ref name="Father"/> The pair she gave {{Term|BotW|Link}} was the last one left in his stock room.<ref>{{Cite|These Zora greaves here were the last pair left in the stock room when he closed up shop.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She explains that wearing them could help a {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} {{Term|BotW|Swimming|link|display=Swim}} like a {{Term|BotW|Zora}}, which is why they would be wasted on her.<ref>{{Cite|Wearing these, even a Hylian can swim as well as a Zora. That said, these would be wasted on me. They are only useful for a Hylian like you.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> After she is done talking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

{{Term|BotW|Laflat}} can be seen in the crowd of {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} congratulating {{Term|BotW|Link}} after he calms {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}. Afterwards, she tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that all of the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}, including {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}, are grateful for his help.<ref>{{Cite|All of us, as well as Lady Mipha... We're all so grateful to you, Link.|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>
