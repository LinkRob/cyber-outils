{{Term/Store|Electric Guardian|Electric Guardians|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
Electric Guardians are Guardians enhanced with electricity. Their attacks are as follows:

* Firing a laser
* Swinging a leg so their claws can scratch their victims
* Jumping up, then slamming down, causing a huge electric surge
* Spinning their legs then charging forward as they scratch
* Rearing back with two feet up, then slamming them down, causing a small lightning storm around them
