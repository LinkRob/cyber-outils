'''Level-8''' is the final {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon|link}} in {{BSTLoZ}}.

==Entrance==
The {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon|link}} entrance is revealed by {{Term|BSTLoZ|Bomb|link}}ing the left rock of {{Term|BSTLoZ|Spectacle Rock|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon|link}}'s layout is that of an arrow pointing downward. It contains three {{Plural|BSTLoZ|Mini-Boss|link}}: a {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dodongo|link}}, {{Term|BSTLoZ|Patra|link}} and {{Term|BSTLoZ|Aquamentus|link}}. The {{Term|BSTLoZ|Book of Magic|link}} and the {{Plural|BSTLoZ|Silver Arrow|link}} are located within the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Dungeon}}. Two blue {{Plural|BSTLoZ|Gohma|link}} serve as the final {{Term|BSTLoZ|Boss|link}}, guarding the final piece of the {{Term|BSTLoZ|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} and a {{Term|BSTLoZ|Heart Container|link}}.
