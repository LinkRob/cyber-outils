{{Term/Store|Jotari||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Jotari}} is a {{Term|TMC|Library Minish|link}} who lives in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}. According to {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}}, they departed from the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}} prior to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|display=Library}}'s books being borrowed and were unable to return to visit him.<ref>{{Cite|Wohohoho! Oh? A visitor after many long weeks! Even {{Color|TMC Green|Jotari}} has been away so long, I've grown almost...lonely. ...But what is it? What do you need?|Librari|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} must speak to {{Term|TMC|Jotari}} to learn that people have checked out various {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The humans have {{Color|TMC Blue|checked some books out of the library}}! Thanks to them, none of us can get back to {{Color|TMC Green|Elder Librari}}... [...] I'm sure the {{Color|TMC Green|librarian}} knows something about where those books went... I wish I were big. Why, I'd walk right up and ask her where those books went!|Jotari|TMC}}</ref> This triggers {{Term|TMC|Maggie (The Minish Cap)|link}} to tell {{Term|TMC|Link}} about the overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|display=Books}} and who checked them out. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns the {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|display=Books}}, {{Term|TMC|Jotari}} will give him 50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} as thanks.<ref>{{Cite|So you brought the books back? Good for you! Thank you! Please take this as a sign of our thanks!|Jotari|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Jotari}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to fuse pieces? Do you think you have one that matches mine?|Jotari|TMC}}</ref> Rather than {{Term|TMC|Jotari}}'s name displaying on the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}} screen, it simply refers to them as a {{Term|TMC|Library Minish}}.<ref>{{Cite|Library Minish|Kinstone Fusion|TMC}}</ref>
