==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SSHD|Small Bomb Bag}} are supplemental {{Plural|SSHD|Bomb Bag|link}} that allow {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} to carry an extra five {{Plural|SSHD|Bomb|link}} at time, as long as the {{Term|SSHD|Small Bomb Bag}} is stored in his {{Term|SSHD|Adventure Pouch|link}}.

{{Plural|SSHD|Small Bomb Bag}} are in unlimited supply; after {{Term|SSHD|Link}} completes the {{Term|SSHD|Earth Temple|link}}, he can purchase as many as he wishes from the {{Term|SSHD|Gear Shop|link}} in {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} for 150 {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} apiece. Another {{Term|SSHD|Small Bomb Bag}} can be obtained from a {{Term|SSHD|Goddess's Treasure Chest|link}} on the {{Term|SSHD|Isle of Songs|link}}.

Each {{Term|SSHD|Small Bomb Bag}} can be individually [[Upgrading|Upgraded]] to a {{Term|SSHD|Medium Bomb Bag|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}}, increasing their {{Term|SSHD|Bomb}} capacity by another five.
