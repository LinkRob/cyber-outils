The '''Turakawak Shrine''', designated by the name ''Stacking A Path'', is a [[Shrine of Light]] in {{TotK}}. This shrine is special, as its [[Lightroot]], the [[Kawakarut Lightroot]], is the only Lightroot to be slightly off on its coordinates of the corresponding shrine. Its exact coordinates are: (-3496, -0198, 0066) (of the shrine).

== Completion ==
You start off in a room with a metal block and an upper area. Walk up to the metal block sitting in front of [[Link]]. Use [[Ultrahand]] to move it next to the wall and climb up it to reach the raised floor.

You’re now on a higher floor and there is a second block there. Use [[Ultrahand]] on the block you just climbed up on and stack it on top of the other metal block. Use [[Ultrahand]] to attach it to the other block so it forms a stable structure comprising of two blocks.

There is a hidden [[Treasure Chest]] you can get now. It is well hidden. It is technically on the floor above but it is only accessible via this area. Look up towards the ceiling and you will see a metal grate hanging above the lower area.

Use [[Ultrahand]] and place the two stacked blocks underneath the metal grate. Climb up the blocks.

Once you climb up, you’ll be high up enough to use the [[Ascend]] ability on the grate above you. Use [[Ascend]] and arrive in the room with a [[Treasure Chest]]. Open it to get a magic rod.

Exit the area with the [[Treasure Chest]] using [[Ascend]]. You’re on a higher floor now. Backtrack to the lower room where you made the tower of two blocks. You’ll need them to complete the [[Shrine of Light|Shrine]].

Once you are back in the room where you stacked the blocks, grab them once again and place them so they line up with the ladder that leads to the higher room you just came from.

Climb up the blocks and up the ladder to get into the higher room. Using [[Ultrahand]], grab the two stacks of blocks and bring them onto the upper level.

This room has a third block, except for this one you can’t climb. Use it to build a path up to the end of the shrine.

You can’t stack the three blocks right up on top of each other since you can’t climb on one of the blocks. Use [[Ultrahand]] to attach it to the side of the climbable block. You will need to place it low enough so [[Link]] can jump up onto it from the other block without climbing.

If you are having trouble balancing the structure, prop the structure up against walls to help give it additional support.

Climb up and go collect your [[Light of Blessing]].
