{{Term/Store|Riju's Training||HWAoC}} is a level 40 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Riju's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Riju's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Truffle|link}}, one {{Term|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. Only {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} are seen at first, but a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}} spawn when {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} reaches 40 KOs. When 90 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}} spawn. When {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} reaches 140 and 180 total KOs, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} spawn. When the objective is reached, a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} spawns and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Riju}} defeats the {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}}.
