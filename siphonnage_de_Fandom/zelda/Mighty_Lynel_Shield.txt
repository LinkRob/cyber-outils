{{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Shield}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} made by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They have been reinforced by both armor and many blades.<ref name="Inventory"/> Compared to {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Shield|link}}, they are stronger in both defense and offense, and they are capable of tearing through armor when deflecting.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are part of the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} use these {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Shield|display=Shields}}, however {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}} will use these {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Shield|display=Shields}}. They become scarce as {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} tier up, though the {{Term|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|First Gatehouse|link}} will always stay the same, making it a permanent source of {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Shield}}.

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Lynel Shield}} conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a thunderstorm, and will also sink if dropped in water. They can also be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
