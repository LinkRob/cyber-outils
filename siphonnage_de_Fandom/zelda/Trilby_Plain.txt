{{Term/Store|Trilby Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Trilby Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Trilby Plain is a small clearing to the southeast of the [[Woodland Stable]], nestled close to the foothills of [[Trilby Valley]]. Its most notable characteristics are the grove of trees that surround the road paving through, and the shallow pond in the center.

A concealed [[Korok]] runs about the Pond, who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for catching him.

==Nomenclature==
Trilby Plain is named after the [[Trilby Highlands]] from {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= Trilby Highlands, a location from The Minish Cap.|game= BotW|publisher= Piggyback|page= 351}}</ref>
