'''''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'' manga''' was a [[manga]] story that ran from 2016 to 2023 by [[Akira Himekawa]] loosely based on the 2006 [[Nintendo GameCube]]/[[Wii]] title ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]''. The series was first released on February 8, 2016 in Shogakukan's Manga One app in Japan.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The Legend of Zelda Twilight Princess manga series will start from February 8th on Japanese comic application Manga One by Shougakukan.|author= Akira Himekawa|published= February 2, 2016|retrieved= February 2, 2016|url= https://www.facebook.com/AkiraHimekawa.4legswing/photos/a.267691966710054.1073741828.267514720061112/840101082802470/?type=3|title= Twilight Princess News|site= Facebook|type=}}</ref> Each chapter was originally being serialized each Monday every two weeks, then was later changed to the last Monday of every month, with some exceptions. English, German, Spanish, Italian, and French versions are also being published.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The book of Twilight Princess Vol.1 will be released June 24th in Japan. It will also be released in English, German and Spanish.|author= Akira Himekawa|published= June 22, 2016|retrieved= June 22, 2016|url= https://www.facebook.com/AkiraHimekawa.4legswing/posts/928610253951552|title= Twilight Princess Vol.1|site= Facebook|type=}}</ref> In total, there are 11 volumes.

Himekawa started working on the manga in 2006, but it was never published at the time due to the game's CERO "B" rating in Japan, which is equivalent to the "E10+" (Everyone 10+) rating by the [[ESRB]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Back in December 2006, the original Twilight Princess received a CERO B rating – ages 12 and up – in Japan, which was why the manga stopped publishing in Shogakukan’s Gakunenshi magazines (seems to be aimed at grade schoolers, hence the termination).|author= Brian|published= January 31, 2016|retrieved= February 1, 2016|url= http://nintendoeverything.com/zelda-twilight-princess-manga-is-happening/|title= Zelda: Twilight Princess manga is happening|site= Nintendo Everything|type=}}</ref>

==Characters Exclusive to the Manga==
===Midna's Father===
{{Main|Midna's Father}}
Midna's Father is shown in the manga's first chapter and is spoken of by his daughter, [[Midna]].

===Darpa===
{{Main|Darpa}}
Darpa is a prideful boy who had trained to be a swordsman alongside Link in his childhood.

===Rioma===
{{Main|Rioma}}
Rioma is Link's childhood friend and fellow knight in training.

===Zeu===
{{Main|Zeu}}
Zeu is another knight in training and friend of Link's from his younger days.

== Trivia ==

* Some combat scenes of the manga, such as when [[Link]] battles [[Ganondorf]], depicts graphic violence beyond the level of any mainline [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] game. This is possibly a reference to ''Twilight Princess'' being known as one of the darker installments and the game itself rated "T" (Teen) by the ESRB, which counts as PG-13.
* Although ultimately an illusion, the [[Hero's Spirit]] (who is formerly Link the [[Hero of Time]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'') is shown assisting Link the Hero of Twilight, [[Princess Zelda]], and [[Midna|Midna]] in fighting Ganondorf at the climax, which does not occur in the game.
* Midna's relationship with Link in this media is shown more explicitly in comparison to the original game and is even romantic.
