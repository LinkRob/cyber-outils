'''Cameos''', also known as '''Easter Eggs''', are extras or references intentionally programmed into a game by the developers. Throughout {{TLoZ|Series}} there have been many Easter Eggs, such as images, characters, and even enemies taken from other [[Nintendo]] franchises, usually ''Super Mario'' (as both were originally created by the same development team). They can seldom be from its own series or other media as well. Although Easter Eggs do appear in modern titles of the ''Zelda'' series, they were more common in the older games.

*The [[Rupee]] sprites are modeled after the Gold Bars in ''Clu Clu Land''.
*The sprites of [[Block|Blocks]] are from [https://www.mariowiki.com/Super_Mario_Bros. ''Super Mario Bros''.].

=={{ALttP|-}}==
*Several houses in [[Kakariko Village#A Link to the Past|Kakariko Village]] have a portrait of {{Smw|Mario}}.
*[[Chain Chomp]]s from the {{Smw|Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series}} appear as powerful enemies in {{Term|ALttP|Turtle Rock|link}} and the [[Palace of the Four Sword]].
*The [[Fairy Fountain (Theme)|Fairy Fountain]] theme (also the File Select theme) is a remix of the {{Smw|Water Land}} theme from ''{{Smw|Super Mario Bros. 3}}''.
*The music of when [[Soldier|Soldiers]] chase Link in Kakariko Village is a remix of the boss theme from ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Super_Mario_Bros._2 Super Mario Bros. 2]''.

*[[Tarin]] resembles Mario, as they look similar, like mushrooms, and turn into raccoons.
*The [[Cucco Keeper]] resembles {{smw|Luigi}}.
*[[Christine]]'s love letter to [[Mr. Write]] includes a photograph of {{smw|Princess Peach}}.
*A [[Yoshi Doll]] can be obtained as a prize in the [[Trendy Game]].[[File:LA Piranha Plant.png|thumb|Link encountering a Goomba and Piranha Plant]]
*Many enemies from the {{smw|Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series}} make an appearance as foes, including [[Blooper]]s, [[Bombite|Bob-ombs]], [[Boo Buddy|Boos]], [[Chain Chomp]]s, [[Cheep-Cheep]]s, [[Goomba]]s, [[Piranha Plant]]s, [[Pokey]]s, [[Mask-Mimic|Shy Guys]], and [[Thwomp]]s.
*In {{LA|NS}}, statues of ''Mario'' enemies can be obtained. The pedestal for the [[Blooper Figure]] says "Squids are the hot new thing with kids today" which is a reference to the ''[https://nintendo.fandom.com/wiki/Splatoon_(series) Splatoon]'' series.
*[[Mamu]] the frog is {{smw|Wart}} from ''{{smw|Super Mario Bros. 2}}'', the main antagonist of said game. In fact, Mamu is also Wart's Japanese name.
*An enemy called [[Anti-Kirby]] (called simply Kirby in Japan) appears in [[Eagle's Tower]]. It attacks by inhaling and is colored pink in {{LA|DX}}, much like the [https://kirby.fandom.com/wiki/Kirby actual character].
*[[Sea Urchin]]s resemble {{wk|Gordo}}s from the {{wk|Kirby (series)|''Kirby'' series}}.
*[[Mr. Write]] is based on Dr. Wright from ''[[wikipedia:SimCity|SimCity]]'', who is a cameo of ''SimCity's'' creator Will Wright. The music in his house is a remix of Dr. Wright's theme.
*[[Prince Richard]] is the hero's rival from ''Kaeru no Tameni Kane ha Naru'', a Japan-only game for the [[Game Boy]]. [[Richard's Villa]] music is a remix of the ''Kaeru'' overworld theme. The [[frog]]s inside his house also appear in the game.
*Totaka's Song can be heard in Richard's Villa after the song loops for several minutes.<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eRkqcEJihGU Totaka's Song in Link's Awakening]</ref>
*In the French version, if Link calls from the phone in [[Grandpa Ulrira]]'s house, {{Wp|Madonna (entertainer)|Madonna}} will answer him.<ref>{{Cite|DRING DRING! DRING! Hello! Madonna à l'appaouïelle. No darling... CLIC! Tu as dû faire un faux numéro!|Telephone|LA}}</ref>

=={{OoT|-}}==
[[File:Malonpendant.jpg|thumb|right|245x245px|Malon's [https://www.mariowiki.com/Bowser Bowser] brooch]]
*[[Talon]] and [[Ingo]] resemble Mario and Luigi, respectively.
*[[Malon]] and [[Talon]] wear a brooch in the shape of {{smw|Bowser}}'s face.
*Various portraits of mainline characters from the ''Super'' ''Mario'' series can be found in [[Princess Zelda's Courtyard]]. These include a portrait of Mario, Luigi, {{smw|Yoshi}}, Princess Peach, and Bowser. The images themselves are taken from ''Super Mario 64'' and ''Mario Kart 64''.
*On the second floor of [[Dodongo's Cavern]], there is a sign carved out of stone that was reused from the one in {{smw|Princess Peach's Castle}}'s courtyard from ''{{smw|Super Mario 64}}''.[[File:OoT Arwing.png|thumb|Link fighting against an Arwing|223x223px]]
*An [[Arwing]] from ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_64|Star Fox 64]]'' can be found inside the game's files to combat [[Link]]; it was used to code {{Term|OoT|Volvagia|link}}'s movement.
*The [[Stone of Agony]] closely resembles the [[Nintendo 64]]'s Rumble Pak, which is ironically used to make said accessory rumble when Link is near hidden holes.

==={{OoT3D|-}}===
*Princess Zelda's Courtyard's portraits were replaced by a replica of a level from ''{{smw|New Super Mario Bros.}}''.
*Posters featuring artwork from {{SS}} can be found in [[Lon Lon Ranch]], [[Gerudo's Fortress]], and the [[Bombchu Shop]].
*In the [[Happy Mask Shop]], at the far right, one of the masks strongly resemble [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Maul Darth Maul] from ''[[wikipedia:Star_Wars|Star Wars]]''.

*The [[Milk Bar Owner]] in Hyrule resembles Mario, and the Milk Bar Owner in Lorule resembles Luigi.

=={{HW|-}}==

=={{TFH|-}}==
[[File:TFH Fitting Room Hammerwear.jpg|thumb|193x193px|The green Link in the Hammerwear Outfit]]
*A picture of [[Makar]] can be found hanging among the pictures of the [[Miiverse Gallery]].
*The [[Fire Gloves]], which allow the Links to throw fireballs from their hands, function identically to the {{Smw|Fire Mario}} form in the {{Smw|Super Mario (series)|''Super Mario'' series}}. They also produce the same sound effect from ''{{Smw|Super Mario Bros.}}''
*The [[Hammerwear]] [[Outfit]] is designed after a {{Smw|Hammer Bro}} from the ''Super Mario'' series.
**If the blue Link or red Link wears the the Hammerwear Outfit, it resembles a {{Smw|Boomerang Bro}} or a {{Smw|Fire Bro}}, respectively. [[File:TFH Cozy Parka Render.png|thumb|153x153px|Link wearing the Cozy Parka]]

* Various outfits from earlier ''Zelda'' games, as well as other less-known Nintendo franchises are also available. For example, his [[Cozy Parka]] is designed after [https://www.ssbwiki.com/Ice_Climbers Popo] from ''[[wikipedia:Ice_Climber|Ice Climber]]''.

=={{FPTRR|-}}==
*Princess Zelda makes an appearance.
*The Mini-Game select screen is designed like the character select screen from ''Super Mario Bros 2''.
*[[Lon Lon Meadow]] is named after Lon Lon Ranch.
*A man who resembles Ingo is found around Lon Lon Meadows.
*The Tingle Tuner resembles a Nintendo DS.{{Fact}}
*Tingle's fight against [[Captain Stalfos]] resembles the gameplay of the {{wp|Punch-Out!!|''Punch-Out!!'' games}}.

== {{BotW|-}} ==

* All toponyms are references within the ''Zelda'' series, and many locations exist in a ruined state.
* [[Satori Mountain]] is named for [[Satoru Iwata]].
* [[Wizzrobe|Wizzrobes]] act like Magikoopas, specifically from ''[https://www.mariowiki.com/Super_Mario_Galaxy Super Mario Galaxy]''.
* [[Brigo]] may be based on {{Wp|Kevin Briggs}}.
* [[Revali]] is based on Falco from the ''Star Fox'' series.

== ''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity'' ==
* [[Impa]]'s hand motions are from the ''[[wikipedia:Naruto|Naruto]]'' anime series.
* Mipha's traversal is swimming over land, in reference to an exploit from {{BotW|-}} that Sidon can be captured from the Vah Ruta battle.
* Sidon's Remote Bomb attack is "Hole In One" from ''{{Wp|Rhythm Heaven Fever}}''.
