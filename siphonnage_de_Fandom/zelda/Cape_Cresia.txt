{{Term/Store|Cape Cresia||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Cape Cresia'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Cape Cresia is located around the [[Necluda Sea]] in [[East Necluda]]. it is a long {{wp|peninsula}} that stretches out into the Necluda Sea. As the land shrinks in width, Cape Cresia eventually turns into [[Soka Point]].
