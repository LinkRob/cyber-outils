{{Term/Store|Meghyn||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Meghyn|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who travels {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} searching for {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}} with her sister, {{Term|BotW|Nat|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're traveling all over to find hearty truffles. I know, I know—it's dangerous for two folks to travel on their own these days. You don't have to tell me twice!|Nat|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} encounters them, the sisters will be under attack by a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} and {{Term|BotW|Nat}} can be encountered in the {{Term|BotW|Ginner Woods|link}}, south of {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}}, and in the {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Woods|link}}.

While she is being attacked, {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} will run away in fear.<ref>{{Cite|Oh nooooo!|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yikes! Help!|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Somebody, please!|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}} has been defeated, she will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|Phew...|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hoh...|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That was close...|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, she she thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his help by giving him {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That was exciting...up until it turned terrifying. Thanks for all your help.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Just when I was sure I knew how I was going to die... The world is full of surprises. Like you!|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, she states that {{Term|BotW|Nat}}'s idea to search for {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}} is the reason they got in danger.<ref>{{Cite|It was my sister's harebrained idea to hunt for truffles here that got us into this mess.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} again, she claims that she never wanted to go hunting for {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}, blaming {{Term|BotW|Nat}} for their predicament.<ref>{{Cite|I never even saw them coming! This is exactly why I didn't want to go truffle hunting. Lousy Nat...|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> After both {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} have been defeated, she is surprised that she and {{Term|BotW|Nat}} have survived.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe we're alive.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> She recalls that the {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} appeared out of nowhere.<ref>{{Cite|We were minding our own business, hunting for truffles, and then wham! Monsters out of nowhere.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> She harbors some resentment towards {{Term|BotW|Nat}}, who refuses to let them return home without {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}.<ref name="Sister"/> If {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} was knocked unconscious by a {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin}}, she continues to blame {{Term|BotW|Nat}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ooooh... I blame my sister entirely for this. "Let's go hunt for truffles!" she says. "It'll be fun!" she says.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} while {{Term|BotW|Nat}} is still in danger, {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} will be concerned for her sister.<ref>{{Cite|It's not too late for my sister, right? Right?!|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nat! Oh, this is just perfect...|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref>

While they are scouring for {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}, {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} remarks that she wants to go home.<ref>{{Cite|I want to go home...|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> She recalls that she told {{Term|BotW|Nat}} it would be risky to go hunting.<ref>{{Cite|I told Nat that our luck would run out in about five minutes flat if we went truffle hunting here.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> She claims that {{Term|BotW|Nat}} becomes unreasonable when she thinks about {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|But when those visions of rupees start dancing in her head, there's no getting through to her anymore.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nat}} tells {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} to return to their search, reminding her that they will not return home until they find {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}.<ref>{{Cite|Less talking, more hunting! Remember, Meghyn, nobody goes home until we find those hearty truffles!|Nat|BotW}}</ref> With a sigh, {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} agrees with her.<ref>{{Cite|*sigh* She's right. The sooner we find those truffles, the sooner this will all be over with.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Nat}}, she tells him about her and {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}}'s journey and reasoning behind searching for {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}, claiming there is no reward without a risk.<ref>{{Cite|Hearty truffles aren't your run-of-the-mill mushrooms you find growing on half the trees in the forest. They're a gourmet treat that soothes your fatigue and brings you back up to tip-top shape! I know that it's dangerous for the two of us to be out here without protection. We've been in our share of scrapes. But there's no reward without risk, right?|Nat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} becomes angered by this, standing up and telling {{Term|BotW|Nat}} not to make it sound as though they only care about {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nat! Try not to make it sound so much like we only care about the money!|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> She reminds {{Term|BotW|Nat}} that whatever money they make is not worth it if they do not survive to spend it at home.<ref>{{Cite|It's not worth anything if we don't make it back home to spend it, you know?|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nat}} argues back, stating they will either practice fighting or find {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle}}, and she considers both of these scenarios to be a win.<ref>{{Cite|It'll be fine! Either we get a little practice fighting, or we find more hearty truffles. Win-win, right?|Nat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Meghyn}} becomes despondent, claiming that {{Term|BotW|Nat}} will be the death of her.<ref>{{Cite|You are going to be the death of me.|Meghyn|BotW}}</ref>
