The {{Term/Store|Comfork||Series, ALttP (Ishinomori)}} is an item in the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP (Ishinomori)}} It is a crystal tuning fork that, when vibrating, allows people to communicate with other beings that are in different worlds.

==History==
After Link frees the [[Maiden]] from the [[Palace of Darkness]], she thanks the young hero and grants him the Comfork.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Thank you, Link! Because of you I can escape this Crystal prison! Now, here is something that will help you--the Comm-Fork!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=16}}</ref> Later, the Comfork begins to ring and Link sees an image of Sahasrahla and the rest of his friends back in Kakariko.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=What's that noise? [...] Sahasrahla!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> Although at first Sahasrahla disregards Link's voice as having been his  imagination, Sahasrahla and the others eventually communicate with Link  and discover that the Comfork allows them to speak with each other even  though they are on different planes of existence.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I can '''see''' them...when the Comfork rings! The girl in the crystal wasn't kidding when she said it might be helpful!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Ahhh,  it must be the magical comfork of legend! The vibrations of this  crystal tuning fork can distort time and space... While the comfork  vibrates, you can communicate with beings on different planes of  existence...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref>

Later, when Link and [[Epheremelda]] realize that the [[Swamp Palace (A Link to the Past)|Swamp Palace]] is completely dried up, the young hero uses the Comfork to call Sahasrahla for help and ask him to investigate the area in the Light World.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Maybe Sahasrahla has an idea! I'll call him with the Comfork!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=11}}</ref> Using the device permits Link to eventually enter the inner portion of the palace and free another maiden.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=We found a large pool of water surrounded by statues, but nothing else of interest...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=12}}</ref>
