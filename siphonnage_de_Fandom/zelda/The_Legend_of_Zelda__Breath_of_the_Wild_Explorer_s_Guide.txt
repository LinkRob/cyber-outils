'''''The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Explorer's Guide''''' is a book released with the Explorer's Edition and Starter Pack of {{BotW}}.

==Features==
The books features 100 pages. The first four pages provide a summary of {{TLoZ|Series}}, as well as introducing [[Link]], [[Princess Zelda]], and [[Ganon]]. The following section is a guide to various mechanics in {{BotW}}, such as using {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}} and performing {{Term|BotW|Perfect Dodging|link|display=Perfect Dodges}}. After that is a guide on how to complete the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. The last page gives information on the Expansion Pass.
