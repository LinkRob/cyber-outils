The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Arena||CoH}} is a {{Term|CoH|Dungeon|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Gerudo Arena}}|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Arena}} is located in the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}} version of the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Desert|link}}, and is accessible right away. There are five floors of {{Plural|CoH|Enemy|link}} to get through before reaching {{Term|CoH|King Dobongo|link}} and obtaining the {{Term|CoH|Bongos|link}}. The layout of the arena floors varies from seed to seed, but not from death to death like other {{Plural|CoH|Dungeon|link}}. It is highly recommended to bring potions into the arena,<ref>{{Cite|Make sure you're well prepared if you're heading into the arena. The shop behind me sells potions, if you have a bottle to hold them.|Gerudo|CoH}}</ref> as it must be completed in one run from bottom to top. Completing the arena and talking to the guard on the entry floor will open up the {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|CoH|Hero's Medallion|link}}.
