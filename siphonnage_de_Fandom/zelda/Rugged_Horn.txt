{{Term/Store|Rugged Horn|Rugged Horns|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 136"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Rugged Horn}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Volcano|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Blazing Trail|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Rugged Horn}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Hinox Mine|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Rugged Horn}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Hinox Mine}}''', ''"Don't pop any balloons!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Rugged Horn}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Den of Flames|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Rugged Horn}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Den of Flames}}''', ''"Avoid the volcanic rocks!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Rugged Horn}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Fire Temple|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Rugged Horn}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Rugged Horn}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Boomeranger|link}} {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 136"/>
