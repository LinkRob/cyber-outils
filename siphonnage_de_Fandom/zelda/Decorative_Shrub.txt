'''Decorative Shrubs''' are [[Object|objects]] from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{Manual|EoW|Decorative Shrub|An ornamental plant hardy enough to support a person on its bushy leaves.<ref name="Decorative Shrub">{{Cite|An ornamental plant hardy enough to support a person on its bushy leaves.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Decorative Shrubs are objects found across [[Hyrule]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Decorative Shrub [[Echo]] after using the [[Tri Rod]] on one, allowing her to summon shrubs as needed. They can block wind, be used as platforms, and will burn if placed near fire.
