'''Minister Lefte''' is a character from {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
Minister Lefte first appears in the [[Throne Room]] of [[Hyrule Castle]], where she discusses strategies to address the [[Rift|Rifts]] with the King of Hyrule and [[General Wright]]. After they finalize a plan, a rift opens, engulfing all three. [[Dark Echo|Dark versions]] of them soon emerge from the void, accusing [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] of causing the Rifts. Later, while exploring the Stilled Suthorn Forest, Zelda encounters '''Minister Lefte''', who ended up there due to the earlier events. After completing the [[Suthorn Ruins]], she offers to guide Zelda to a location marked on the map by [[Impa]]. Lefte leads Zelda to meet [[Lueburry]], Impa's brother. Throughout Zelda's journey to resolve the Rifts in the [[Gerudo]] and [[Zora]] regions, Minister Lefte remains a helpful presence. she stand on the left from the kings view (hence the name "Minister Lefte").
