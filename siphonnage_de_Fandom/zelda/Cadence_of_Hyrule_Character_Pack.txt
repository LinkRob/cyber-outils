The {{Term/Store|''Cadence of Hyrule Character Pack''||Series, CoH}} is the first downloadable content pack for {{CoH}}.<ref name="Game Details"/> It is available as a standalone DLC pack or as part of the {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Season Pass|link}}.

==Overview==
The {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Character Pack}} introduces five playable characters: {{Term|CoH|Aria|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Frederick|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Impa|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Shadow Link|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Shadow Zelda|link}}.<ref name="Game Details"/> The {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Character Pack|display=''Character Pack''}} also includes {{Term|CoH|All Character Mode|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Mystery Mode|link}}, which were previously features of {{CoH}}{{'}}s parent game, ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
