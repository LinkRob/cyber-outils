'''''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — PRIMA Official Game Guide''''', published by [[Prima Games]] in 2006, is an officially licensed strategy guide for ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]''. It is available in Collectors and Premier versions for both the [[Nintendo GameCube|GameCube]] and [[Wii]].<ref>[https://primagames.com/ Prima Games]</ref>
