The '''Island of Hyron''' is a location featured in the {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}}. It is home to the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Fifth Palace|link}}. To be able to reach the island, many dangerous places must be crossed, including a desert, a ravaging sea, and dark caves.<ref>''And so, the journey begins. Across the murky swamps...and dark, forbidding caverns. Through blazing days in the desert. Frigid nights in a city of tombs...and across a ravaging sea.'' - N/A (''Valiant Comic Issue #1'')</ref> A man (presumably a Hylian {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Soldier|link}}) is disguised as an {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Iron Knuckle|link}}, and was charged by a past king to protect the treasure that rests there.<ref>''Long ago, the old king charged to protect the palace from those who are '''unworthy'''.'' - Knight (''Valiant Comic Issue #1'')</ref> 

==History==
When {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Princess Zelda|link}} realizes that the only way to keep the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Triforce of Wisdom|link}} is by going to a faraway land with the sacred relic in hand, she decides to go to the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Fifth Palace|link}} located on the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Island of Hyron}} and retrieve the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Flute|display=Magic Flute|link}} from there, which will {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Warping|display=Warp|link}} her to a distant location away from {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Ganon|link}}.<ref>''In the Fifth Palace of Hyrule is a magic flute that will transport me to a distant place. Ganon will never find me'' - Zelda (''Valiant Comic Issue #1'')</ref><ref>''The Fifth Palace is on the Island of Hyron!'' - Link (''Valiant Comic Issue #1'')</ref> Upon reaching the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Island of Hyron}}, the young duo ventures into the palace, where the Blue Iron Knuckle challenges them but loses due to {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Link}}'s skill with a blade. The knight surrenders, and shows them the way to the {{Term|TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|Flute|display=Magic Flute}} to give to Zelda. <ref>''By reaching the Fifth Palace and defeating me, you have proved that you deserve the treasures within.'' - Knight (''Valiant Comic Issue #1'')</ref>
