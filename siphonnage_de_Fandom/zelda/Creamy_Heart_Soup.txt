==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Creamy Heart Soups are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Hydromelon]], a [[Voltfruit]], [[Fresh Milk]], and either a [[Hearty Radish]] or [[Big Hearty Radish]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Soups made with a regular Hearty Radish heal eight {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} and sell for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. Soups made with Big Hearty Radishes heal thirteen Hearts and sell for 80 Rupees. Unlike many other {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}, there are no variations of Creamy Heart Soup that grant additional affects. These soups are known to be sweet, and sharing them is believed to bring two people closer together.<ref name="Inventory"/>

During her cooking classes in [[Gerudo Town]], [[Ashai]] will mention that she was planning on making Creamy Heart Soup.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... I was planning on {{Color|BotW Red|creamy heart soup}} for the lesson today, but I'm being a bit forgetful, it seems.|Ashai|BotW}}</ref> However, when reciting the recipe to Link, she'll name every ingredient but the Hearty Radishes, claiming she can't remember them.<ref>{{Cite|I know that I need some {{Color|BotW Blue|fresh milk}}, one {{Color|BotW Blue|voltfruit}}, one {{Color|BotW Blue|hydromelon}}, and two heart-shaped vegetables...|Ashai|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I just can't recall what the heart-shaped vegetable was...|Ashai|BotW}}</ref>
