{{Term/Store|Carrot Cake|Carrot Cakes|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Carrot Cake}}<br/>Even those who don't like carrots tend to enjoy the mild sweetness of this cake.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Carrot Cake}} are type of {{Term|BotW|Cake|link}} made by combining {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, and either an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Regular {{Plural|BotW|Carrot Cake}} can be made by using one of each carrot, which can be eaten to restore seven {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Their mild sweetness makes them enjoyed by even those who dislike {{Plural|BotW|Carrot|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Two variations of {{Term|BotW|Carrot Cake}} exist, depending on which kind of carrot is used to make them:

*'''Enduring Carrot Cake''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Carrot Cake}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Even those who don't like carrots tend to enjoy the mild sweetness of this cake.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using an {{Term|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. Eating one will restore and overfill {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/> They'll also heal {{Term|BotW|Link}} for five {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} and can be sold for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.

*'''Hasty Carrot Cake''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Carrot Cake}}<br/>Grants a mid-level movement speed boost.<br/>Even those who don't like carrots tend to enjoy the mild sweetness of this cake.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using a {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}. Eating one will grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} a mid-level speed boost.<ref name="Hasty"/> They heal {{Term|BotW|Link}} for three {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}, but they can also be sold for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.
