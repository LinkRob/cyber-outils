{{Plural|BotW|Thunderblade}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} forged and refined by {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} from the hills of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Magic Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} with elemental properties.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Whenever the blade shines gold, any {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} struck by it will be electrocuted.<ref name="Inventory"/> These swords are commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}.

Despite their elemental nature, {{Plural|BotW|Thunderblade}} can still conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} due to being made of metal. However, this also means that they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. While they sink if they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they are also unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

The reason for the setting is unknown in Tears of the Kingdom, but it was deleted due to [[Fuse]] as a factor outside of the setting
