{{Term/Store|EX Evacuating Hyrule||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 66 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Evacuating Hyrule|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the second wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must escort a {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} to a Targeted Position near the southwest corner of the map. After walking a short distance, a large {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}} appears, stopping the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}}'s movement. When it is defeated, a {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain|link}} is discovered at the northern edge of the map, and {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} runs to escort her to another Targeted Position found in the northwest. Like the first captain, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian Scout I|link}} appear next to the {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain}} soon after she starts moving. When one of the scouts is destroyed, a {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Captain|link}} appears in the east and has to reach a Targeted Position in the map's southeast corner. {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}} is the one who escorts him, and like the other two, a large {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}} appears and attacks him soon after he is discovered. More {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} appear to attack each captain before they reach the Targeted Position, with the {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} being assaulted by an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Guardian|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, and a pair of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain}} is attacked by a {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} pair, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Guardian|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}}, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Captain}} by a {{Term|HWAoC|Blizzrobe|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Thunder Wizzrobe|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}}. When all three captains reach their positions, a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox|link}} spawns, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is cleared once it is defeated.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Impa}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Armoranth|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cool Safflina|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
