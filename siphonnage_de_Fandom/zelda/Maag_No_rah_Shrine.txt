The {{Term/Store|Maag No'rah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Maag No'rah Shrine}}<br/>Maag No'rah's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Maag No'rah's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Maag No'rah Shrine}} is hidden in cave in the cliff-face of {{Term|BotW|Upland Lindor|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}. The cave is south of a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} containing {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Maritta Exchange Ruins|link}}, and it is concealed by a cracked rock that can be destroyed by explosives.

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Maag No'rah|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Maag No'rah. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Maag No'rah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Maag No'rah Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Maag No'rah}}. Along this path is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, worth 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Maag No'rah}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Maag No'rah|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Maag No'rah}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Maag No'rah|BotW}}</ref>
