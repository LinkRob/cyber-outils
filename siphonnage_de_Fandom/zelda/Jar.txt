{{Term/Store|Jar|Jars|Series, MM, MM3D, BotW, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}},{{Exp Game|MM, BotW, HW}}<ref>{{Cite|Ocean, #4: Underwater Jar|Visions|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 276}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Jar}}: Use this on switches to reveal hidden staircases.|Tutorial|HWDE}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Vase|Vases|TWW, TWWHD|plural}},{{Exp Game|TWW}}<ref>{{Cite|You will need {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|magic power}} to use my leaf. You can find Magic Jars in {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|grass clumps}} and {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|vases}} and after you defeat {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|monsters}}.|Deku Tree|TWWHD}}</ref> and {{Term/Store|Pot|Pots|ALttP, LA, LADX, FPTRR|plural}},{{Exp Game|ALttP, LA, EoW, FPTRR}}<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= {{Big|Pots}} |game= ALttP |page= 35}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have you used the {{Color|FPTRR Orange|pot}} yet?|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. {{Plural|Series|Jar}} typically drop common items such as {{Plural|Series|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Rupee|link}} when broken. They are a common sight in {{Plural|Series|Dungeon|link}}, {{Plural|Series|House|link}} and sometimes {{Plural|Series|Cave|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Reorg}}
{{Lacking Sources}}
{{Manual|ALttP|Pots|These are scattered throughout the dungeons. These pots come in handy, because you can pick them up and throw them at the enemy to cause damage. You can also find hearts in some pots, and some hold arrows and bombs (note that the contents of a used pot will not be replaced until you leave the dungeon). Also, some pots hide switches that open doors.}}
When broken, {{Plural|Series|Jar}} may reveal items such as {{Plural|Series|Rupee}}, {{Plural|Series|Heart}}, {{Plural|Series|Fairy|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|Series|Bomb|link}} and sometimes {{Plural|Series|Magic Jar|link}}. Depending on the game, they may also conceal other items or objects as well, such as {{Plural|Series|Bee|link}} and even {{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo|link}} in {{TP}}. {{Plural|Series|Jar}} can be broken either by picking them up and throwing them, by {{Term|Series|Roll Attack|link|display=Rolling}} into a wall with a shelf that houses them, or by slashing them with the {{Term|Series|Sword|link}} or another item; however, in some games, only higher leveled {{Plural|Series|Sword}} can break {{Plural|Series|Jar}}, or may require certain techniques to do so such as the {{Term|TMC|Rock Breaker|link}} in {{TMC}}. {{Plural|Series|Jar}} may also sometimes be used as weights and may be placed or pushed onto a {{Term|Series|Switch|link}} to activate them. The {{Plural|Series|Jar}} in {{LA}}, {{OoS}}, and {{OoA}} require the use of the {{Plural|Series|Power Bracelet|link}} to pick up and push them.

While {{Plural|Series|Jar}} are mainly used to conceal Items, they may also be thrown at enemies as a form of attack. In games like {{ALttP}} and {{LA|-}}, {{Plural|Series|Jar}} deal a significant amount of damage when thrown and can break through most defenses, instantly defeating a variety of enemies that would otherwise require multiple strikes of the {{Term|Series|Sword}} to defeat.

While there are many benefits to breaking {{Plural|Series|Jar}} in the series, {{Term|Series|Link|link}} may also be punished in certain circumstances for breaking a {{Term|Series|Jar}}. In {{TWW}}, if {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} breaks the {{Term|TWWHD|Jar}} that {{Term|TWWHD|Sue-Belle|link}} carries on her head and talks to her, she will force him to pay her ten {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}} in damages. Likewise, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} will also be forced to pay for every expensive {{Term|TWWHD|Jar}} he breaks in the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House|link}}. In {{TP|-}}, it is possible for {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to be kicked out of {{Term|TPHD|Hena|link}}'s {{Plural|TPHD|Fishing Hole|link}} after multiple attempts to break the {{Plural|TPHD|Jar}} inside.<ref>{{Cite|C'mon... Can't you try to control yourself? I'm gonna have to ask you to leave. [...] That's it! Enough! You're being a jerk! Get OUT!|Hena|TP}}</ref> She will only allow him back inside once he has apologized.

In some {{Plural|Series|Dungeon}} or worlds, {{Plural|Series|Jar}} are replaced by similar objects that have an identical use, such as the {{Plural|Series|Skull|link}} in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} of {{ALttP|-}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} in {{ALBW}}.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Pots|Earthen pots are a common sight in the dungeons. If you wear the Power Bracelet, you can lift these pots and throw them at enemies. Some pots have hearts or other items inside; others hide switches beneath them.}}
{{Term|LANS|Link|link}} needs to acquire the {{Term|LANS|Power Bracelet|link}} in order to be able to lift {{Plural|LANS|Jar}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oof! This looks heavier than heavy! Your current strength won't cut it...|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

In versions prior to {{LA|NS|-}}, {{Term|LADX|Link}} must not only acquire the {{Term|LADX|Power Bracelet}}, but also equip and use it to lift {{Plural|LADX|Jar}}.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Pot|A simple pot of a common type. It is big enough for a person to fit inside.<ref name="EoW Pot">{{Cite|A simple pot of a common type. It is big enough for a person to fit inside.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Jars are objects found across [[Hyrule]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Jar [[Echo]] after using the [[Tri Rod]] on one, allowing her to summon jars as needed. She can hide inside them to sneak past enemies or throw them to create distractions.

==Varieties==
===Gust Jar===
{{Main|Gust Jar}}
[[File:TMC Gust Jar Artwork.png|left|120px]]
Appearing in {{TMC|-}}, {{TFH|-}}, and {{SS|-}} {{Plural|Series|Gust Jar}} allow {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} to blow wind and even suck dust, objects, and smaller enemies.

==="Don't drop the pot!"===
{{Main|Drablands Challenge#Don't drop the pot!}}
[[File:TFH Drop the Pot Icon.png|left]]
In {{TFH}}, one of the {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} in certain Levels requires the {{Plural|TFH|Link|link}} to safely transport a special {{Term|TFH|Jar}} to the end of the Level. This {{Term|TFH|Jar}} rests on a pedestal when the Level begins, and must be picked up in order for the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}} to appear. For the Level to be completed, the {{Term|TFH|Jar}} must be placed or thrown onto another pedestal at the end of the fourth Stage. Like other {{Plural|TFH|Jar}}, this one breaks when thrown to the ground, or when dropped by a {{Term|TFH|Link}} as he takes damage, which causes the {{Plural|TFH|Link}} to fail the challenge. It can be safely thrown between the {{Plural|TFH|Link}} if necessary.

===Expensive Vases===
{{Main|Extremely High-Class Bone-China Vase}}
Found only in {{TWW|-}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House|link}} and the cabana in the [[Private Oasis]], expensive {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar}} of blue and white decorate the first floor and the fireplace and bookshelves in the cabana. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} breaks one of these {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar}} while {{Term|TWWHD|Mila's Father|link}} owns the house, he will not be allowed to leave until he pays the necessary amount of {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}} needed to replace the {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar}}. When ownership of the {{Term|TWWHD|Auction House}} passes to its new owner, {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie's Father|link}}, after {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} defeats the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King|link}} at the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can break as many of these {{Plural|TWWHD|Jar}} as he desires without a single fine; they will be replaced the next time he enters the mansion.

===Flying Pot===
{{Main|Flying Pot}}
{{Plural|Series|Flying Pot}} are a recurring obstacle that commonly appear in {{Plural|Series|Dungeon}}. When approached, they come alive and fly at {{Term|Series|Link}} to attack him and break on impact. When broken, these {{Plural|Series|Jar}} may contain items usually found in normal {{Plural|Series|Jar}}, such as {{Plural|Series|Rupee}} and {{Plural|Series|Heart}}.

===Warp Pot===
{{Main|Warp Pot}}
Appearing only in {{TWW|-}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Warp Pot}} allow {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Warping|link|display=Warp}} to other unblocked {{Plural|TWWHD|Warp Pot}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Dungeon|link}}. They are useful for quickly navigating in a {{Term|TWWHD|Dungeon}}, and some {{Plural|TWWHD|Dungeon}} may have more than one pair.

===Water Pot===
{{Main|Water Pot}}
{{Plural|Series|Water Pot}} are large {{Plural|Series|Jar}} filled with water. They are mainly used for extinguishing or cooling fire and magma, turning the latter into a temporary solid platform. They appear in {{TWW|-}} and {{FSA}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{LCT|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|LCT|Jar}} appear as common shooting targets in {{LCT}}. They are worth five points when shot. Broken {{Plural|LCT|Jar}} may sometimes release {{Plural|LCT|Rupee|link}} into the air, which can be shot at for extra points.
{{Ambig|End}}

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Colored {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} may be broken to reveal item pick-ups in {{HW}}. The type of item dropped depends on the color of {{Term|HWDE|Jar}}: red {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} drop {{Plural|HWDE|Heart|link}}, brown {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} either drop {{Plural|HWDE|Rupee|link}} or the temporary upgrades of sub weapons, yellow {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} drop {{Plural|HWDE|Force Fragment|link}}, and green {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} drop small or large {{Plural|HWDE|Magic Jar|link}}. These {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} are mainly found within {{Plural|HWDE|Keep|link}}, but may also rarely be found out on the battle field beneath {{Plural|HWDE|Boulder|link}}.

In {{HW|L}}, brown {{Plural|HWDE|Jar}} in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}} may also contain a {{Term|HWDE|Companion Fairy|link}} or a delicious {{Term|HWDE|Fairy Food|link}} as treasure in certain {{Plural|HWDE|Battle}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
