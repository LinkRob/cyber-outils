{{Term/Store|Molida's Store||Series, PH}},<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Molida's store!|Shopkeeper|PH}}</ref> also known as '''Molida Island Shop''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page=100}}</ref> is a shop in {{PH}}.

Situated in [[Molida Island]], Molida's Store offers a variety of common items for sell, such as [[Bomb]]s and [[Potion]]s. If [[Link]] hasn't yet bought a [[Wooden Shield]], he can buy one from Molida's Store for just 80 [[Rupee]]s. After the young hero has cleared the [[Temple of Courage]], the store will put up a [[Quiver]] upgrade for a price of 1000 Rupees. The upgrade will depend upon whether the young hero has already obtained a Quiver from [[Romanos]]'s minigame.

Like all the other stores, Molida's Store also offers a random [[Treasure]]. Only one Treasure is available at any time, and the one it offers is random. After Link buys one Treasure from the store, it will take one day for the shop to restock with a new, random Treasure.
