==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Armored Porgy}}
{{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy}} are saltwater {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} that can be found in the waters of the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Sea|link}}. They are also sold by {{Term|BotW|Mubs|link}} at the fish market in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}} and by the traveling {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Chumin|link}}. As is the case with both {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy}} and {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, they can be chilled by {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} to make {{Plural|Series|Frozen Porgy|link}} or burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Porgy|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat one raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They have hard scales with a compound that, when {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}}, will make a meal that will temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s defense.<ref name="Inventory"/>
