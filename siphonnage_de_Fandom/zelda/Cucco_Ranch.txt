{{Term/Store|Cucco Ranch||Series, ALBW}},<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Cucco Ranch!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> also known as '''Dodge the Cuccos''',<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= Dodge the Cuccos|game= ALBW|system= Nintendo 3DS|version= English|country= US}}</ref>{{Note|This name does not appear in-game but can be found in the game's text data.}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{ALBW}}. Cucco Ranch can be found just south of [[Kakariko Village]] in [[Hyrule]], and is operated by the [[Cucco Girl]]. [[Link]] must speak to the Cucco Girl, who will charge a different amount of [[Rupees]] depending on which difficulty he selects. The aim of the game is to dodge [[Cucco]]s flying overhead for the duration of 30 seconds in order to win a prize.<ref>{{Cite|If you can dodge all of the Cuccos for 30 seconds, I'll give you a special prize!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> The time and amount of Cuccos will increase with the difficulty of the selected mode. If Link is touched by a Cucco, he automatically loses the game.

==Modes and rewards==
There are four modes of difficulty, ranging from 'Egg', to 'Endless'. The 'Egg' level costs 20 Rupees, and is the easiest of the four levels. After completing the Egg level, Link is rewarded with 40 Rupees and unlocks the second level. The 'Chick' level costs 30 Rupees, and features large Cuccos to add to the difficulty of the game. If Link beats this level, he is rewarded with 60 Rupees,<ref>{{Cite|You're no fledgling, that's for sure! The Cuccos are hopping happy! Here's your prize—60 Rupees!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> and unlocked the 'Rooster' level. Upon surviving the Rooster level, Link is rewarded with 150 Rupees<ref>{{Cite|That was amazing! I was on the edge of my seat all the way! Oh yeah, the Cuccos asked me to thank you. Here's a gift—150 Rupees!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> and a Piece of Heart.

He also unlocks the 'Endless' level, in which he must survive as long as possible with no time limit, and costs 10 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Endless level? The one where you keep dodging and dodging to see how long you can keep it up for? Got 10 Rupees to spare?|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> If Link is able to withstand the Cuccos for 100 seconds, he will get Rupees as a prize.<ref>{{Cite|Whooie! That was some spectacular footwork! By the looks of it, you can take on bigger challenges than that. Your prize is 100 Rupees!
|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> Every few minutes, the amount of Rupees if Link is hit by any Cuccos increases, but if the 999.99 second limit is reached, the Cucco Girl will stop Link and comment about how he managed to dodge all the Cuccos for such an amount of time, giving him the title of the Cuccomaster and 3000 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, oh, wowie-wow! You dodged every single Cucco? You know what that makes you? The Cuccomaster!|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> A large Cucco will then appear outside the Ranch which only comes out in a blue moon,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, look! The Giant Cucco came to meet you as well! He usually doesn't like strangers... The Giant Cucco only comes out once in a blue moon, you know. Go talk to him, why don't you?|Cucco Girl|ALBW}}</ref> throwing hearts at Link if he gets close and talks to it. This is also shown in the end credits.<ref>{{Cite Web|url=https://miiverse.nintendo.net/posts/AYQHAAABAAABUaW4vYLd0Q|author=avengah|published=December 12, 2013|title=Screenshots of Cucco Ranch Endless mode completion|site=Miiverse}}</ref>
