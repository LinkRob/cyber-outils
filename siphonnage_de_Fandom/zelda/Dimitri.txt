{{Term/Store|Dimitri||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250{{Exp Game|OoS}} & 254{{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> He is one of the three [[Link's Partners|animal companions]] Link can partner with in {{OoA}} and {{OoS}}, the other two being [[Ricky]] and [[Moosh]].

==Characteristics==
Dimitri is a talking red [[Dodongo]] who can swim up waterfalls and against currents.<ref>{{Cite|I hear there's a creature that can swim up waterfalls! What kind of creature is it?|Hylian|OoS}}</ref> He attacks enemies by biting them. He is summoned by playing [[Strange Flute|Dimitri's Flute]], though this will not work everywhere.<ref>{{Cite|Your flute's song fell flat.|N/A|OoS}}</ref> Link can pick him up and throw him with the [[Power Bracelet]]. 

The landscapes of certain regions in [[Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna]] change depending on which animal companion Link befriends. With Dimitri, these regions become flooded with water to match his swimming abilities.

==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:Dimitri Spool Swamp.png|thumb|right|Link saves Dimitri from a group of Moblins]]
In {{OoS|-}}, Link will eventually have to venture into [[Spool Swamp]] for the next [[Essences of Nature|Essence of Nature]] after completing the [[Snake's Remains]] dungeon. During this time, Link can find and acquire a [[Strange Flute]]. One of the Strange Flutes can be acquired by winning a dance round in the [[Subrosian Dance Hall]], yet only before Link helps Ricky the kangaroo. While heading into Spool Swamp, Link will encounter one of the potential animal helpers, Ricky. [[Blaino]], who lives nearby, has taken away Ricky's [[Ricky's Gloves|boxing gloves]], and Link must fight him in a boxing round and defeat him to win back the gloves. When the gloves are returned, Ricky will offer to help Link by transporting him to Spool Swamp.

If Link has won the Strange Flute from the Subrosian Dance Hall earlier, he will eventually encounter Dimitri being attacked by the [[Great Moblin]]'s henchmen.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee! I found somethin' weird! If we catch it and sell it, we'll be rich!!! And the Great Moblin will be very pleased!!!|Moblin|OoS}}</ref> Link pays the henchmen 50 [[Rupee]]s and another 30 Rupees to leave Dimitri alone.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee! Let it go? Then you can pay us for it! 50 Rupees!|Moblin|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You're smart! But 50 Rupees is too cheap! Give me 30 more!|Moblin|OoS}}</ref> Dimitri then thanks Link and joins him, turning the Strange Flute into Dimitri's Flute.<ref>{{Cite|You saved me! I'm Dimitri. What about you? Link? That's a weird name. But starting today, Link is my friend. If you play that Strange Flute, I'll try to come find you.|Dimitri|OoS}}</ref>

If Link has [[Strange Flute|Ricky's Flute]] or [[Strange Flute|Moosh's Flute]], he will encounter Dimitri in [[Sunken City]]. Link finds a group of children harassing Dimitri, claiming to be the Safety Patrol. By giving one of the children all of the [[Bomb]]s that Link has, the children will flee. As thanks, Dimitri will temporarily join Link and allow him to ride him, which is necessary to maneuver the flooded city until Link gets the [[Flippers|Zora's Flippers]].<ref>{{Cite|You saved me. I am Dimitri. You can't swim? I can even swim up waterfalls. You helped me, now I help you.|Dimitri|OoS}}</ref> The city has a couple of waterfalls which only Dimitri can climb. One of the waterfalls leads to the [[Master Diver]] who will test Link in getting the [[Master's Plaque]], with the reward being the Zora's Flippers. Shortly after Dimitri will leave.<ref>{{Cite|The monster went off somewhere.|Safety Patrol|OoS}}</ref> Link can find the Safety Patrol later talking about Dimitri, considering him as a patrol member.<ref>{{Cite|And we were gonna let it join the Safety Patrol, too.|Safety Patrol|OoS}}</ref>

If Link does have Dimitri's Flute however, all Link needs to do is call Dimitri into the city rather than saving him from the city's children.

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA|-}}, Link first encounters Dimitri washed ashore on [[Crescent Island]] after completing the [[Moonlit Grotto]]. Two [[Tokay]] are hustling around Dimitri, planning on eating him.<ref>{{Cite|We found this red fish. We're dying to savor its exciting flavor.|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> The Tokay propose giving Dimitri to Link for [[Ember Seed]]s. They run off after tasting its intense flavor. After Link saves him, Dimitri offers to take him where he wants to go.<ref>{{Cite|Are you going somewhere? I can give you a ride.|Dimitri|OoA}}</ref> After taking Link back to Labrynna's mainland, Dimitri leaves.  

When Link tries to later venture into [[Symmetry Village|Symmetry City]], he will encounter the [[Stray Fairy|Fairies]] from the [[Fairies' Woods]], who ask Link to help an animal who got lost in their woods. The animal will either be Moosh, Ricky or Dimitri depending on whether Link has any of the Strange Flutes or not. Dimitri will be one of the animals if Link bought the Strange Flute from the shop in [[Lynna City]].<ref>{{Cite|A red, lizard-lookin' animal got lost in our forest and can't get out!|Fairy|OoA}}</ref> If Dimitri is found, he will decide to join Link and gives him his flute. Dimitri's swimming ability is used in the [[Nuun Highlands]], which helps Link locate the three lazy [[Carpenters]] needed to extend the bridge to the ruined city.<ref>{{Cite|Those lazy, worthless fools! Just as we're buildin' this bridge to the Symmetry City Ruins, they go off on break!|Mutoh|OoA}}</ref> Dimitri, however, will not accompany Link to the city as he cannot withstand the high temperatures.<ref>{{Cite|Too hot! It's boiling hot here! You go alone, Link!|Dimitri|OoA}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Despite being a Dodongo, Dimitri's abilities have little in common with the rest of his species, being at home in aquatic environments and seeming to have a much lower heat tolerance, as shown if Link tries to enter the ruins of Symmetry City on his back. He also never displays any ability to breathe fire and is the only known Dodongo to have speech capabilities.
*Owing to his swimming abilities, Dimitri is the only one of Link's animal friends to not promptly leave Link when he reaches the Sunken City's entrance (as Moosh leaves due to his fear of water, while Ricky leaves him due to being disgusted by the smell of medicine).
