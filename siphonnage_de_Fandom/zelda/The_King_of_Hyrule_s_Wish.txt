==Overview==
"{{Term|BotW|The King of Hyrule's Wish}}" plays during a cutscene at the end of "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}" quest. It starts as a slow rendition of "{{Term|Series|Ganon's Theme|link}}" on piano, eventually swelling to become "{{Term|Series|Zelda's Lullaby|link}}" with elements of "{{Term|Series|Overworld BGM|link}}" from {{TLoZ}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= {{Exp|Ganon's Theme|ガノンのテーマ}} |book= {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}} booklet |publisher= Nintendo Co., Ltd. |page= 17}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= {{Exp|Princess Zelda's Theme|ゼルダ姫のテーマ}} |book= {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}} booklet |publisher= Nintendo Co., Ltd. |page= 17}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= {{Exp|Ground BGM|地上BGM}} |book= {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}} booklet |publisher= Nintendo Co., Ltd. |page= 17}}</ref>
