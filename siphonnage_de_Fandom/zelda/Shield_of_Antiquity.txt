The {{Term/Store|Shield of Antiquity||Series, ST}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 138">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138 {{Exp Game|ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
The Shield of Antiquity is obtained from [[Niko]] after collecting 10 [[Stamp (Item)|Stamps]] in {{ST}}.<ref name="E 138"/> Unlike the regular [[Shield]], it cannot be swallowed by [[Like Like]]s.<ref name="E 138"/>

The Shield of Antiquity belonged to the Hero of Winds, one of Niko's old friends.<ref>{{Cite|That shield once belong to an old friend of mine from my adventuring days.|Niko|ST}}</ref><ref name="E 138"/> It is the same [[Wooden Shield#Phantom Hourglass|Wooden Shield]] used by [[Link]] in {{PH}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
The Shield of Antiquity appears paired with the [[Phantom Sword]] from {{PH|-}} as the second tier of [[Toon Link]]'s [[Light Sword]] Weapon in {{HW}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
