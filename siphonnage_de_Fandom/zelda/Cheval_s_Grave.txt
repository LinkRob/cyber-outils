'''Cheval's Grave''' is a location in {{OoA}}.{{Name Ref|OoA}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|OoA|Cheval's Grave}} is the final resting place of the famed inventor {{Term|OoA|Cheval|link}}, who was responsible for the creation of a special {{Term|OoA|Rope (Object)}} that does not dissolve in water, named {{Term|OoA|Cheval Rope}},<ref>{{Cite|You got Cheval Rope! This sturdy rope won't rot when wet.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> as well as the {{Term|OoA|Zora's Flippers}}.<ref>{{Cite|Before I die, I want to finish my work on flippers for swimming and rope that won't decay in water.|Cheval|OoA}}</ref> The {{Term|OoA|Cheval's Grave|display=Grave}} is located in present {{Term|OoA|Labrynna|link}}'s {{Term|OoA|Yoll Graveyard|link}}. {{Term|OoA|Cheval}}, who wanted to leave his mark on society and create things that would continue to help people long after his death,<ref>{{Cite|I hope to aid all the people of the world by inventing such things!|Cheval|OoA}}</ref> was buried along with his inventions.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Link! Are you looking for something, or what? Huh? Cheval? I know that name. Yeah! I've seen a grave in our own time that has that name on it.|Ralph|OoA}}</ref> Before he died, had his watery tomb transformed into a sort of test course meant to only yield access to his inventions to one worthy to wield them.<ref>{{Cite|I shall entrust my invention only to one with both might and light feet.|Cheval|OoA}}</ref> His {{Term|OoA|Cheval's Grave|display=Grave}} is discovered by {{Term|OoA|Link}}, who comes in search of both inventions in order to reach and navigate the regions around {{Term|OoA|Crescent Island|link}} while on his quest to find the eight {{Plural|OoA|Essences of Time|link}}. 

The area surrounding {{Term|OoA|Cheval's Grave}} is littered with pitfalls, which {{Term|OoA|Link}} can only traverse with the help of {{Term|OoA|Moosh|link}}. As such, it is impossible for {{Term|OoA|Link}} to return to the {{Term|OoA|Cheval's Grave|display=Grave}} unless he obtains {{Term|OoA|Strange Flute|display=Moosh's Flute|link}}.
