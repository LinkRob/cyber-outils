The {{Term/Store|Wand of Gamelon||Series, TWoG}} is an item in {{TWoG}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Wand of Gamelon is a relic that is kept inside of the {{Term|TWoG|Shrine of Gamelon|link}}. It is being guarded by {{Term|TWoG|Omfak|link}}, one of [[Ganon]]'s minions. The Wand of Gamelon is briefly mentioned by {{Term|TWoG|Myra|link}}, who states that it is the only item that can defeat Ganon.<ref>{{Cite|This light will penetrate the dark around Ganon, but only the wand can defeat him. Good luck!|Myra|TWoG}}</ref>

The Wand of Gamelon defeats Ganon in a single shot by throwing it at him. In the following cutscene, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] waves the Wand to create magic golden chains that wrap around Ganon. These chains then imprison him inside of a book.
