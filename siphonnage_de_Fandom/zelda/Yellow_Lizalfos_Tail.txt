==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Yellow Lizalfos Tails are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by [[Electric Lizalfos]], which can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and [[Hyrule Ridge]], when killed. Though their smell makes them unable to be cooked, Yellow Lizalfos Tails can be mixed with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be exchanged with [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] for [[Mon]] or used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Yellow [[Dye]].

The {{Term|BotW|Rubber Armor (Set)|link}} set requires Yellow Lizalfos Tails for its third and fourth upgrades at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
