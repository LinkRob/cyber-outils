The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Gerudo Tower|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Gerudo Tower is a [[Sheikah Tower]] that is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. The Gerudo Tower is an exceptionally tall Sheikah Tower that rests within a deep valley. Once Link reaches the top of Gerudo Tower, he can activate the Tower,<ref>{{Cite|Sheikah Tower activated.|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> filling in the [[Sheikah Slate]]'s [[Sheikah Slate#Map|Map]] of the area surrounding the Gerudo Highlands, the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower Region|link}}. [[Kass]] sits on top of Gerudo Tower playing an accordion, and will give Link the [[Shrine Quest]] "[[Sign of the Shadow]]".

Hidden underneath a mound of rocks at the bottom of the spiraling path sits a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] that contains five [[Bomb Arrow]]s. A second metallic Treasure Chest containing five [[Fire Arrow]]s can be found further along the path.
