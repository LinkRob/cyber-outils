{{Term/Store|Fire Hinox|Fire Hinoxes|Series, HWAoC|plural}} are enemies in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
Fire Hinox are brown Hinoxes with orange sideburns and yellow runes on their body signifying elemental powers. In addition to having all the moves of the [[Black Hinox]], Fire Hinoxes have original moves of their own such as the ability to ignite a log and shoot beams of pure flame when they swing it. Fire Hinox can spit a fireball which will continue to bounce off the ground until it hits a target at which point the projectile will explode and cause the burning effect. 

Additionally the Hinox's regular strong attack now have an area of effect that can give out the burning status effect to those that get hit.
