The {{Term/Store|Tokay Cave||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA |publisher= BradyGames |page= 77{{Exp Game|OoA}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The entrance to {{Term|OoA|Tokay Cave}} is located on {{Term|OoA|Crescent Island|link}}, through the {{Term|OoA|Tokay|link}} idol that serves also serves as the entrance to the {{Term|OoA|Moonlit Grotto|link}} in the present. The {{Term|OoA|Tokay Cave|display=Cave}} is opened by placing the {{Term|OoA|Tokay Eyeball|link}} in the idol's empty eye socket, which {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} can obtain by giving {{Term|OoA|Cap'n|link}} the {{Term|OoA|Zora Scale|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank'ee! Now we can escape these seas! Take this as a sign o' me thanks! It's the jewel called the {{Color|OoA Red|Tokay Eyeball}}! It's said to be the thing that opens the way to the {{Color|OoA Blue|ancient tomb}}. I don't know if it's true or not, though.|Cap'n|OoA}}</ref>

When {{Term|OoA|Link}} enters the {{Term|OoA|Tokay Cave|display=Cave}}, a mysterious voice will sound, requesting that he display his power, wisdom, and courage.<ref>{{Cite|Show your Courage, Wisdom and Power. The road to the past shall then open.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> {{Term|OoA|Link}} then makes his way through three trials. He first must complete a combat test with {{Plural|OoA|Gibdo|link}}, to show his power. Next is a maze of {{Plural|OoA|Rock|link}} he must complete while being pursued by a {{Term|OoA|Chaser|link}} and {{Plural|OoA|Blade Trap|link}}, to show his courage. Finally, he has to make his way through an invisible floating maze using {{Plural|OoA|Block|link}} from the {{Term|OoA|Cane of Somaria|link}}, to show his wisdom.

After besting the {{Term|OoA|Tokay Cave|display=Cave}}'s trials, {{Term|OoA|Link}} goes through an area with {{Plural|OoA|Ladder|linK}} and pools of {{Term|OoA|Water (Terrain)|link}} before exiting through an underwater cave opening. He finds himself in the {{Term|OoA|Sea of No Return|link}}, and can access the {{Term|OoA|Ancient Tomb|link}} by traversing yet another maze.
