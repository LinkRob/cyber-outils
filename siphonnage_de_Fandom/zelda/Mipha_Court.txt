'''Mipha Court''' is a location in {{TotK}}.

==Features and overview==
Mipha Court is built on the summit of [[Ploymus Mountain]]. It features [[Zora]] architecture and pools leading up to [[Mipha's Statue]] on the very top of Ploymus Mountain. 

Mipha Court was constructed some time between the events of {{BotW}} and {{TotK|-}}, with Mipha's Statue being moved here in order for a statue of [[Link]] and [[Sidon]] to be put in its place. After the [[Upheaval]], it was the only area in [[Zora's Domain]] to still have fresh water, with some Zora saying it was a blessing from [[Mipha|Princess Mipha]] herself. The Ihen-a Shrine is also located here allowing an easy way to travel to Mipha Court. 

In Mipha Court, after [[Yona]] convinces [[Sidon]] to assist [[Link]] in investigating the sludge contaminating Zora's Domain and the [[Lanayru (Region)|surrounding region]], the Sludge Like emerges from the ground and ambushes the group. Link and Sidon fight it together. It is covered in sludge, which makes it invulnerable. Link and Sidon fought it together and vanquished it.
