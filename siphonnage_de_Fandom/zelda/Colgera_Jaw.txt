'''Colgera Jaw''' is an [[Item]] in {{TotK}}. 

== Location and uses ==
{{Stub|TotK}}
Colgera Jaws can be obtained after defeating [[Colgera]] in the [[Wind Temple#Tears of the Kingdom|Wind Temple]]. If [[Link]] enters [[The Depths]], he can visit specific areas where certain [[Boss]]es, such as Colgera, can be challenged to a rematch. Colgera will drop one Colgera Jaw any time it is defeated in The Depths. These Jaws can be used with the [[Fuse]] ability to either add considerable [[Weapon Strength|strength]] to a weapon, or to damage enemies when [[Parry]]ing with a [[Shield]].
