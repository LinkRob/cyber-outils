{{Term/Store|Kiki||Series, ALttP, LA, LADX}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228 {{Exp Game|ALttP}} & 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
Kiki shows up when [[Link]] is wandering through the maze of brush on his way to the [[Palace of Darkness]], the first [[Dark World]] dungeon in {{ALttP}}. Kiki loves [[Rupee]]s more than anything else in the world and asks the hero for 10 Rupees, after which he will follow Link.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Kiki the monkey, ki ki! I love Rupees more than anything. Can you spare me 10 Rupees?|Kiki|ALttP}}</ref> If Link is hurt by the enemies encountered while Kiki is following, the monkey will run back to the maze and Link will need to give him another 10 Rupees to get him to follow again. When both Link and Kiki reach the palace entrance, Kiki tells Link he can open the door in exchange for 100 Rupees. If Link pays him, Kiki looks around for a bit, then jumps on the roof and hits a [[Switch]], opening the door. Once Kiki opens the entrance, the door will remain open. If Link declines to pay either time, Kiki will run off and Link must return to the maze to find him.

==={{LA|-}}===
Kiki is found southeast of [[Kanalet Castle]]'s moat in {{LA}}. She complains that she is hungry.<ref>{{Cite|Chi-kiita! Chi-kiita! Kiki the monkey! Hungry! Kiki the monkey!|Kiki|LADX}}</ref> When Link gives her the [[Bananas]] as part of the {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}, she calls several other Monkeys to build a bridge to access Kanalet Castle.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|LADX Bananas}}! {{Icon|LADX Bananas}}! Oooh! Ooh! Kiki! Monkeys! Come! Repay him! Kiki!|Kiki|LADX}}</ref> After building the bridge, they leave behind a [[Stick]], the next item in the Trading Sequence.<ref>{{Cite|You found a stick a monkey left behind... You take it!|N/A|LADX}}</ref>

If Link approaches Kiki while with [[BowWow]], Kiki and BowWow instigate a fight.<ref>{{Cite|Kiiiki! What?! All right, mutt! Let's battle!!|Kiki|LADX}}</ref> After a while, Kiki runs away by jumping across the moat.
