{{Term/Store|Shrine of Light|Shrines of Light|Series, TotK|plural}} are dungeon-like puzzles in {{TotK}}.

==Overview==
Shortly after founding [[Hyrule]], [[King Rauru]] and [[Queen Sonia]] created the Shrines of Light to protect the kingdom from monsters. The shrines sealed the monsters away, making them unable to be resurrected by the [[Blood Moon]], or by any other means.

Every Shrine on [[Hyrule|The Surface]] has a [[Lightroot]] at the same XZ coordinates in [[The Depths]], with its name spelled backwards, the only exception is [[Turakawak Shrine]], where its Lightroot equivalent is slightly off.

Like the [[Ancient Shrine]] in {{BotW}}, each Shrine of Light contains a series of traps and obstacles to be overcome as part of a trial. Oftentimes, the subtitle of the Shrine will give a hint as to the solution of the trial/puzzle.

Shrines of Light are appended by their respective (x, y, z) coordinates (exact location of travel gate).

==Trivia==
* The names for the Shrine of Light, in Japanese, are anagrams of the name of the locations in {{wp|Kyoto}}.
** Examples listed below:
*** In-isa (インイサ) is an anagram of Saiin (サイイン / 西院), location in {{Wp|Ukyō-ku, Kyoto}}.
*** Kisinona (キシノナ), anagram of Nashinoki (ナシノキ / 梨木), is derived from Nashinoki Shrine (梨木神社), a real-life shrine located in {{wp|Kamigyō-ku, Kyoto}}.
*** Gutanbac Shrine is named Gutanbachi (グタンバチ) Shrine in Japanese, with Gutanbachi being an anagram of Tanbaguchi (タンバグチ / 丹波口), location in {{wp|Shimogyō-ku, Kyoto}}.
** Coincidentally, the layout of all the shrines are identical to those location in Kyoto.
