The {{Term/Store|Book of Koridai||Series, TFoE}} is an item in {{TFoE}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Book of Koridai is a sacred relic that is kept inside of the Shrine of Koridai in {{Term|TFoE|Glutko|link}}. It is being guarded by the monster {{Term|TFoE|Glutko (Boss)|link}}, one of [[Ganon]]'s minions. Earlier in his adventure, Link meets the reader {{Term|TFoE|Aypo|link}} in {{Term|TFoE|Nortinka|link}} who offers to read the Book's secrets if Link brings it to him.<ref>{{Cite|If you bring me the Book of Koridai, I will gladly read the secret verse.|Aypo|TFoE}}</ref> When Link brings the Book of Koridai to {{Term|TFoE|Aypo}}, he reveals that the Book's sacred power is the only thing that can defeat Ganon.<ref name="Ganon">{{Cite|Listen, such is the power of the prince of darkness that he can kill with a single look! Attacks against Ganon will prove fruitless unless Link attacks with the sacred book.|Aypo|TFoE}}</ref>

The Book of Koridai defeats Ganon in a single hit by throwing it at him. It magically imprisons Ganon inside its pages, turning him into an illustrated page.

The item's name comes from the word "book" meaning a bound set of blank sheets for writing in, most likely in reference to the pages it contains.

==Trivia==
*In {{TWoG}}, a book that appears identical to the Book of Koridai appears when [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] uses the {{Term|TWoG|Wand of Gamelon (Item)|link}} to defeat Ganon. The wand creates a chain that binds Ganon before he is similarly imprisoned in the book.
