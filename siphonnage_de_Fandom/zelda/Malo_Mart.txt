{{Term/Store|Malo Mart||Series, TP, TPHD}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Malo Mart}}|N/A|TP}}</ref><ref>''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — PRIMA Official Game Guide|PRIMA Official Game Guide]]'' (Prima Games) pg. 120</ref> also known as '''Kakariko Goods''',{{Name Ref|TPHD}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}. Its flagship store, located in [[Kakariko Village]], is owned and operated by [[Malo]].

==Features and Overview==
==={{TP|-}}===
Malo Mart was originally a general store in Kakariko Village known as '''Kakariko Goods''' its original owner being an unknown woman. The store was left abandoned after its original owner was attacked by a [[Shadow Beast]] and presumably transformed into one of them.<ref>{{Cite|Remember the lady from the general store? Just one of those things attacked her, and a whole gang from town went to save her! And what happened? She was already gone, and there were TWO monsters waitin'! ...You connectin' the dots? That means that if we get attacked by them, then we'll be...|Barnes|TP}}</ref> 

When Malo finds the abandoned store, he decides to reopen the shop as his own.<ref>{{Cite|I decided to take on this unmanned shop...|Malo|TP}}</ref> Although he can be a bit rude,<ref>{{Cite|Time is money. If you're done shopping, quit wasting both.|Malo|TP}}</ref> he likes to keep his prices low and serve his customers well, hoping to be a financial "hero."<ref>{{Cite|I may not be a hero, but maybe I can help people with heroic business deals.|Malo|TP}}</ref> Malo Mart is the only place where [[Link]] can purchase the [[Hawkeye]] and the [[Hylian Shield]].

After Link obtains the [[Master Sword]], Malo Mart proposes a Townspeople-Relief Plan to stop shops in [[Hyrule Castle Town (Twilight Princess)|Hyrule Castle Town]] gouging prices.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Welllllcome...to Malo Mart!}} I have heard from a reliable source that shops in Hyrule Castle Town are gouging prices, crushing folks under the thumb of commerce! Well, fear not, Brudda, for Malo Mart has proposed a {{Color|TPHD Red|townspeople-relief plan}} as an answer to the lamentations of the common man! Phase One: {{Color|TPHD Red|Repair}} the broken {{Color|TPHD Red|bridge}} leading from {{Color|TPHD Red|east Castle Town}} to the {{Color|TPHD Red|field}} in order to improve traffic! Phase Two: {{Color|TPHD Red|Buy}} that {{Color|TPHD Red|Castle Town shop}} and sell {{Color|TPHD Red|products}} to the town at {{Color|TPHD Red|reduced prices}}! You have heard me right, Brudda!|Gor Ebizo|TPHD}}</ref> However, Malo Mart lacks the funds to enact this Plan so [[Gor Ebizo]] asks Link to donate to Malo Mart's cause,<ref>{{Cite|There is a problem, however... The finances for the execution of this glorious plan are...lacking! Please, Brudda! Offer some financial support to aid in the purchase of the Castle Town shop! For the benefit of the people!|Gor Ebizo|TPHD}}</ref> first asking for 1000 [[Rupee]]s to fix the bridge between [[Hyrule Field#Twilight Princess|Hyrule Field]] and East Castle Town.<ref>{{Cite|We are {{Color|TPHD Purple|1000 Rupees}} short of funding for the bridge repairs... We need that bridge for a steady flow of goods to Castle Town... We MUST bring the cost of goods down in Castle Town, Brudda! Could you please help support this worthy cause?|Gor Ebizo|TPHD}}</ref> Upon receiving enough Rupees to fix the Bridge, Gor Ebizo celebrates and hopes Link will continue to fund the Plan while Malo notes that the shops will soon regret their price-gouging.<ref>{{Cite|Glorious day! We have collected enough funds to {{Color|TPHD Red|finance repairs}} to the {{Color|TPHD Red|bridge}} connecting {{Color|TPHD Red|east Castle Town}} to {{Color|TPHD Red|Hyrule Field}}, Brudda!|Gor Ebizo|TPHD}}<br/>{{Cite|Those {{Color|TPHD Red|corrupt, price-gouging shops}} in {{Color|TPHD Red|Castle Town}} are officially on notice! Soon they will rue!|Malo|TPHD}}<br/>{{Cite|We hope you will continue your {{Color|TPHD Red|financial support}} of our efforts to sell {{Color|TPHD Red|products}} in the town at {{Color|TPHD Red|discounted prices}}, Brudda!|Gor Ebizo|TPHD}}</ref> For the next part of the Plan, Malo Mart needs 2000 Rupees to buy [[Chudley's Fine Goods and Fancy Trinkets Emporium]] in Castle Town.{{Fact|TP}} If Link delivers the [[Hot Springwater]] to the [[Goron]] near the newly repaired Bridge, Malo Mart manages to save up Rupees, reducing the amount of Rupees needed to buy Chudley's Fine Goods and Fancy Trinkets Emporium to 200 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to all, the bridge is built, the trade route is secure, and we saved money for the economic development of Castle Town! But in order to reach our Castle Town development goal, we still need {{Color|TPHD Purple|200 Rupees}}!|Gor Ebizo|TPHD}}</ref>

After funding the takeover of Chudley's Fine Goods and Fancy Trinkets Emporium, the [[Malo Mart Castle Branch]] opens up in Hyrule Castle Town, which is run by a now much more cheerful [[Chudley]].
