{{Term/Store|Rolf||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Rolf}} is {{Term|TMC|Romio|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Dog|link}},<ref name="E 271"/> and resides in the {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, where he remains for the entirety of the game.

As soon as {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}, he is able to become {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} and speak with {{Term|TMC|Rolf}}. {{Term|TMC|Rolf}} will complain about the heat produced by the fireplace and the fact that there are more people in the {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|display=House}}, adding to the heat.<ref>{{Cite|It's too hot! Arf... {{Color|TMC Blue|The fireplace is too hot...}} Now there are even more people in here, and it's getting too hot to bear! What will it take to cool this place down!? Pant, pant, pant!|Rolf|TMC}}</ref> He will also warn {{Term|TMC|Link}} to be careful because he is so small, stating that not all {{Plural|TMC|Cat|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Dog}} are as nice as him and that they might attack {{Term|TMC|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You ought to be careful when you're running around! You're awfully tiny! And not all dogs and cats are nice, like me. Woof! Some of them might even take a swipe at you, arf! Rarf!|Rolf|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} uses {{Term|TMC|Water|link}} to put out the fire in the fireplace, {{Term|TMC|Rolf}} will thank him and express his annoyance at {{Term|TMC|Romio}} spending time with {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks, arf! You put out the fire. But I'm still miffed at my master! I can't believe how long he's been hanging out with his girlfriend!|Rolf|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Rolf}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|Rarf! Arf arf! You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Rarf arf!|Rolf|TMC}}</ref>
