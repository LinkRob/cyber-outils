{{Term/Store|Podoboo Tower|Podoboo Towers|Series, OoS|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 198 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Podoboo Tower}} were referred to as '''Lava Monsters''' in {{Guide|OoS|BradyGames}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |publisher= BradyGames |page= 55{{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} {{Plural|Series|Podoboo Tower}} go unnamed in {{OoA}}. They are a ''The Legend of Zelda'' series-exclusive variant of the [[mariowiki:Lava Bubble|Lava Bubbles]], also named Podoboos, of the [[mariowiki:Mario (series)|''Mario'' series]].

==Characteristics==
==={{OoS|-}}===
Podoboo Towers appear in [[Subrosia]], predominately at [[Lava Lake]], in {{OoS}}. When they emerge, they stay in place and occasionally release fireballs at [[Link]] to push him into the lava. After a short while, they will sink back from where they came and soon resurge. Digging up earth with the [[Shovel]] in Subrosia may sometimes dig up a Podoboo Tower. They can be defeated with [[Sword]] slashes.

==={{OoA|-}}===
Podoboo Towers infest the burning ruins of [[Symmetry City]] in {{OoA}}. When they emerge, they stay in place and occasionally release fireballs at Link. Like in {{OoS|-}}, they can be defeated with Sword slashes. Once Link fixes the [[Tuni Nut]] and Symmetry City is restored, Podoboo Towers will disappear from the City.
