==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Chillfin Trout}}
Chillfin Trout can be caught by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} in the waters of the [[Tabantha Frontier]] and [[Hebra Mountains]]. The [[Coral Reef]] in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} also sells them. They can be eaten raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Similar to other trout, Chillfin Trout become [[Roasted Trout]] when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} and [[Frozen Trout]] when chilled by [[Ice]]. When {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}}, {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout}} provides {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>
