The {{Term/Store|Lynna City Comedian||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref>{{Note|This character was also referred to as '''Subel''' in the {{OoA|Wessel}} and simply as the '''Comedian''' in {{A&A}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= {{OoA|Wessel|-}}|publisher= Scholastic|page= 30}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 211}}</ref> However, because {{E}} retconned these terms, they are not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} 

==Biography==
The Lynna City Comedian can be found in [[Lynna City]] in the present, standing with his [[Sidekick]] just outside of the house with the blue roof. The Comedian is very fond of the jokes he tells, but it seems he is the only one who appreciates them.<ref>{{Cite|So what kinda candy's never on time??? Choco-late! Isn't that a sweet joke!|Comedian|OoA}}</ref> Even his own Sidekick will disapprove of the Comedian's jokes.<ref>{{Cite|That joke goes against our aesthetics, ya know...|Sidekick|OoA}}</ref>

The Lynna City Comedian plays a role in the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}. [[Link]] can trade him the [[Cheesy Mustache]] to aid him with his comedy.<ref>{{Cite|I need something that will make people laugh. I wonder if a fake mustache would work?|Comedian|OoA}}</ref> In exchange, the Comedian will tell Link the [[Funny Joke]],<ref>{{Cite|OK, buddy, I'm gonna teach you the funniest gag I know! OK... Here goes!|Comedian|OoA}}</ref> which can then be told to [[Dekadin]] to continue the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest}}. With the Comedian's new mustache, his Sidekick asks if he plans on restarting his comedy routine anew.<ref>{{Cite|So, you wanna start over from scratch?|Sidekick|OoA}}</ref>
