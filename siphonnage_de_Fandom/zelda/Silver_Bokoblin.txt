{{Term/Store|Silver Bokoblin|Silver Bokoblins|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Silver Bokoblin}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Improve}}
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Silver Bokoblin}}
The Silver Bokoblin is much more clever, powerful, and dangerous than the other three lower-tiered [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]]. Silver Bokoblins tend to use similar weapons as the [[Black Bokoblin|Black Bokoblins]], including the [[Dragonbone Boko Club|Dragonbone Boko Clubs]], [[Dragon Bone Boko Bow|bows]], and [[Dragonbone Boko Shield|shields]].

The Silver Bokoblin retains all the skills other Bokoblins have, like kicking and avoiding [[Remote Bomb|remote bombs]]. New to this Bokoblin is the faster reaction time and use of more powerful attack patterns. When first encountered, they tend to use a double slash attack and will use a slower single slash attack sometimes too. They will also use their jump and swinging attacks more often, lasting much longer than the weaker Bokoblins.

Silver Bokoblins are much stronger than all its Bokoblin variants and have been influenced by [[Ganon]]'s dark magic.<ref>{{Cite|They have been influenced by Ganon's fiendish magic, so they are stronger than even the Black Bokoblins.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>, only being outclassed by the Golden Bokoblins introduced during Master Mode. They carry stronger [[Weapon]]s than other Bokoblins and may drop [[Bokoblin Horn]]s, [[Bokoblin Fang]]s, [[Bokoblin Guts]], [[Amber]], [[Opal]], [[Topaz]], [[Ruby|Rubies]], [[Sapphire]]s, and [[Diamond]]s when defeated.

You would be foolish to call these Silver Bokoblins a mere nuisance. They have been influenced by the Demon King's fiendish magic, so they are stronger than even the Black Bokoblins. It's said that the Demon King's power marked their silver bodies with purple patterns. The magical glow at the tip of their horns may look funny at a glance, but don't take these creatures lightly, or the joke will be on you.

They are practically the same as the Breath of the Wild Bokoblins in that they are stronger than usual and drop gems on death, but they have bigger, more unique horns. They are almost identical to other species of Bokoblin in Tears of the Kingdom, except they have four major differences: 1. They do much more damage and have more health. 2. They are silver and purple. 3. They have stronger horns with glowing baubles at the end, and 4. They tend to spawn with stronger weapons. Overall, they are practically buffed Bokoblins.
