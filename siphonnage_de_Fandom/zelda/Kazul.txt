{{Term/Store|Kazul||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kazul|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kazul}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler who rides on {{Term|BotW|Horse|link|display=Horseback}} between {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}. He carries an {{Term|BotW|Emblazoned Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}.

On his journey, {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} will occasionally comfort his {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shush...|Kazul|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whoa there...|Kazul|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now, now...|Kazul|BotW}}</ref>

As he travels, {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} advice and warnings.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there, adventurer! A little word of advice for you...|Kazul|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey there, adventurer! A word of warning for you...|Kazul|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey there, adventurer! A little treasure tip for you...|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> On his way to {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} is about the same distance from their current location.<ref>{{Cite|I'm on my way to Riverside Stable, a bit southwest from here. Southeast about the same distance from here is Kakariko Village! Check your map and you should find it easy enough.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> He also suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} bring a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} and a {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} if he is also going to {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there adventurer! It's quite a journey to the Riverside Stable from here. You could walk there if you HAD to, but if you want to be prepared, bring a detailed map and a horse, at least.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's quite a journey to the Riverside Stable from here... You could walk there if you HAD to, but if you want to be prepared, bring a detailed map and a horse, at least.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kazul}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to head to {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} if he is looking for adventure, directing him towards {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there, adventurer! You look like you could use an adventure! If you head northeast from here, you'll find Akkala. Do you have Akkala on your map? Look for a lake in the shape of a skull. If it's adventure you're after, look no further.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does go to {{Term|BotW|Akkala}}, {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} suggests that he stock up on supplies near the base of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're headed for Akkala, you're gonna need some basic equipment! Death Mountain is just to the north of here. I'd recommend you head for its base first and see about stocking up.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> While he is stopped, he will quietly tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} about the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}, which sells special wares at night.<ref>{{Cite|People are trying to rest here, but I've got stuff to share, so I'd better do it nice and quiet. You ready, adventurer? If you find a shop called Fang and Bone, keep an eye out for the special wares you can only buy at night...|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to visit the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone}} should he ever find himself at {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake}}, as it would be an interesting experience.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there, adventurer! Were you considering a trip to Skull Lake? If you go, promise me you'll check out Fang and Bone. As shops go, it's definitely...interesting.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref>

In addition, {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} will warn {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be careful if he enters {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} in search of treasure, since he has never been anywhere so dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|You might get it into your head to go check out Hyrule Castle, but I've never encountered a more dangerous place... If you head there hoping to find treasure, be careful that you don't lose your life!|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> However, he has heard of a secret entrance to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}, which is located through the inlet behind the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've heard of a secret way into Hyrule Castle. You can find your way in through the inlet behind castle town.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> He reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that this is simply a rumor, and {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} even suspects it could have been a story that was spread to catch thieves.<ref>{{Cite|It's just a rumor, mind you. For all I know, it's a story spread to catch out thieves trying to break in.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} also knows of a secret cave on the west side of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}, but none who have entered have returned.<ref>{{Cite|I've heard tell of a secret cave on the west side of Hyrule Castle! There are many who've tried to get in through the cave to hunt for treasure, but there are none who have returned!|Kazul|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kazul}} recommends that {{Term|BotW|Link}} sell off any extra {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} he has, because they are worth a fair amount of {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey there, adventurer! You can probably get a pretty good price for any ore you sell. If you've got some spare lying around, it's probably worth your while to sell it off and pocket the rupees!|Kazul|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does this enough, {{Term|BotW|Kazul}} explains that he will be able to afford pricier gear.<ref>{{Cite|On your travels, you may stumble across various kinds of ore. I recommend you collect it! Ore will fetch you a good price if you sell it, which makes it a nice way to afford some of the pricier gear you'll find.|Kazul|BotW}}</ref>
