The {{Term/Store|Qua Raym Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Qua Raym Shrine}}<br/>A Balanced Approach}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Balanced Approach''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Qua Raym Shrine}} is located on an islet in {{Term|BotW|Goronbi Lake|link}} in western {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. The {{Term|BotW| Ancient Shrine|link}} can be reached by making a bridge using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to manipulate the nearby iron crates and drop them into the {{Term|BotW|Lava|link}}. Alternately, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can glide to it with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} from a higher elevation. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Qua Raym|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Qua Raym. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Qua Raym|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Qua Raym Shrine}} consists of a single chamber with two sets of large scales. Across from the scales is a locked cell where three metal blocks are kept. The {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} is on a raised level above the center set of scales, but this platform is blocked by a stack of bombable blocks that can only be reached by raising the scale next to them. This scale in turn has a a set of {{Plural|BotW|Floor Spikes|link}} directly above it. {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s main objective is to manipulate the two scales to reach the bombable blocks and finally the Monk, but while also avoiding the spiked ceiling.

The scales leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} are initially halfway raised. The left scale holds a wooden crate that can be burned away, by lighting an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} on fire from one of the {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}}. Removing this weight drops the adjacent scale closest to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, which holds two {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}}. This {{Term|BotW|Small Key|display=Key}} is used to open the cell with the three steel blocks, which can then be carried using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} and used to manipulate the scales.

To blast away the bombable blocks, Link must place a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} on the closest scale and then place a steel block on the other scale, thereby raising the {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|display=Bomb}} close enough to destroy the blocks. The {{Plural|BotW|Floor Spikes}} above this scale prevents {{Term|BotW|Link}} from simply riding it to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. To resolve this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must place one steel block on this scale to keep it from pressing against the ceiling, with the other two blocks being placed on the adjacent scale. When this is done, the scale closest to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} will be raised.

Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Qua Raym}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Qua Raym|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Qua Raym}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Qua Raym|BotW}}</ref>

The other set of scales lead to another {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} on a raised platform. As with the other two scales, they can be ridden by placing enough weight on one side to raise {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the platform. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Claymore|link}}.
