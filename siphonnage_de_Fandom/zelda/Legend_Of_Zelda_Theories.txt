== Timeline Theories ==
The Legend of Zelda Timeline, released alongside the Hyrule Historia book in 2011 sparked debate among fans wanting explanations for the more unexplained events. 

=== Downfall Timeline Origin Theories ===

==== The Wish Theory ====
This theory proposes that the Hero of Time originally lost to Ganon. The Downfall Timeline was created by Link in A Link to the Past when he wished to undo all of Ganon’s evil. This causes the Hero of Time to win the fight against Ganondorf, creating the other two timelines.

=== The Unknown Split Theory ===
This proposes that the Hero of Time losing was the first major anomaly or difference to the original timeline after an unseen timeline split and not what ''caused'' the split.

=== The Minish Cap Split Theory ===
The main plot point of this theory is that in The Minish Cap, if Link takes too long in the final room of Hyrule Castle, Vaati kills Zelda, causing the split. This is a believable theory because it has a unique “game over” screen.
