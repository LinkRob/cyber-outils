The {{Term/Store|Lanayru Tower||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|Lanayru Tower|Map|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''North Tower''',<ref>{{Cite|But then, like, I heard a voice...coming from the {{Color|BotW Red|north tower}}...|Eryck|BotW}}</ref> is a location in {{BotW}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|BotW}}
The Lanayru Tower is a [[Sheikah Tower]] that is located in the [[Lanayru Wetlands]]. It is situated on a large, multi-level hill surrounded by a monster encampment. [[Gruve]] can be found on the top of the tower, having been stranded there when the towers awakened. Activating the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Tower|display=Tower}} will distill a {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Tower Region|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.
