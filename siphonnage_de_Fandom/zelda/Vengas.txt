{{Term/Store|Vengas|Vengas|Series, ST|plural}} are enemies in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 211}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Vengas are found in the {{Term|ST|Forest Temple|link}}. If [[Link]] kills them, they will explode into clouds of poisonous gas which cannot be walked over. The gas can be removed with the [[Whirlwind]], an item found in the Forest Temple. A Vengas is potentially dangerous to Link because of the gas they produce, and the time Link has to run away before the gas is released.
