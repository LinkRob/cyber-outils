"{{Term/Store|The Champions' Ballad||Series, BotW}}",<ref name="Ballad">{{Cite|If you would, please listen to this special song... The Champions' Ballad...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> previously known as the '''Unfinished Song''',<ref name="Kass">{{Cite|My teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}} mentions such {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}}, and now one has appeared before my eyes...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> is a {{Term|BotW|Song|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Overview==
The first time {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches a {{Term|BotW|Monument|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} will remark that a {{Term|BotW|Monument}} is mentioned in the Unfinished Song by his teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}}.<ref name="Kass"/> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} expresses curiosity, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} explains that his teacher was unable to complete the song before he passed away.<ref name="Unfinished">{{Cite|[Unfinished song?] Yes. The song is mostly complete. However... My teacher left this world without finishing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} has made it a goal for himself to complete the Unfinished Song, so he has decided to visit the locations that the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} wrote about.<ref>{{Cite|I wish to weave the missing pieces together and finish the song. That's why I'm roaming the areas mentioned in the lyrics. Ah, sweet joy! How glorious to sing this song in the very land it describes...|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

The Unfinished Song is composed of several key verses that were written by the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}.<ref name="Unfinished"/> These verses were written in honor of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} and the trials they faced.<ref>{{Cite|...So? Did the {{Color|BotW Blue|verse}} move you? It was written in honor of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} challenging the trial.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> They are dedicated to {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Revali}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Urbosa}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will offer to sing these verses for {{Term|BotW|Link}} in their respective locations.<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to sing it for you...|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

While journeying and completing the Unfinished Song, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will write several independent songs dedicated to the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}}. These are "{{Term|BotW|Champion Daruk's Song (Song)|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Daruk}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor him in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> "{{Term|BotW|Champion Mipha's Song (Song)|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor her in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> "{{Term|BotW|Champion Revali's Song (Song)|link}}",<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Revali}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor him in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and "{{Term|BotW|Champion Urbosa's Song (Song)|link}}."<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Urbosa}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor her in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> As he writes, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} begins to feel closer to the departed {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}.<ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that celebrates the softer side of Champion Daruk, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that captures the determination of Champion Mipha, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that highlights the hidden feelings of Champion Revali, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that honors the heart of Champion Urbosa, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> In turn, he gets closer to finishing the song.<ref>{{Cite|I was struck by a realization...that the missing parts of the song can only be finished by my teacher's pupil. By me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I feel much more confident that I can do those missing lyrics justice.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The end result is not quite clear yet...but I'm beginning to feel a stirring of inspiration about those verse.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At the end of his travels, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} decides that the perfect setting to the song would be {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} in the distant past.<ref>{{Cite|The missing {{Color|BotW Blue|verses}} of an ancient song... I think the perfect setting would be {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} in the distant past.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}, he will exit the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}} to find {{Term|BotW|Kass}} overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} reveals that he has would like to sing the completed song for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I happen to have the perfect song for someone as esteemed as you. I would love to sing it for you. My teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}... the one I told you about... I finally completed the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}!|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He considers their current location perfect for singing the song.<ref>{{Cite|This lofty location is the perfect stage to sing this special song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The final song depicts the inauguration of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|It takes place 100 years ago... at {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. Yes, the very castle you can still see over yonder. It's about the magnificent inauguration of the chosen Champions.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to listen to him sing this song, which he has entitled "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Song)}}".<ref name="Ballad"/> Listening to the song causes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to remember "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Memory)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.

Once "{{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad (Song)}}" has been played for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} recounts that the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} told him to write songs that transported their listener to a different moment in time.<ref>{{Cite|My teacher always advised me to write songs that transport the listener to the moment in time you're singing of.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} now believes that he understands that lesson, as well as the true power of music.<ref>{{Cite|Now...I finally feel I understand what he meant...and the true power of music.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>
