{{Term/Store|Taming the Savage Lynel||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 57 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Taming the Savage Lynel|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Taming the Savage Lynel}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given ten minutes to defeat three monsters. At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, only a {{Term|HWAoC|Lynel|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} are present, but a {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Lynel}} spawns when one of them is killed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all three of them are defeated. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
