The '''[[wikipedia:Nintendo_64|Nintendo 64]]''' (ニンテンドー64, ''Nintendō Rokujūyon'', NINTENDO64), commonly abbreviated as '''N64''', is a 64-bit [[wikipedia:ROM_cartridge|cartridge]]-based video game console. It was released in June 1996, is [[Nintendo]]'s third system released internationally, and the first to display 3D graphics. It is also the final home console to use true ROM cartridges. The N64 is also the first Nintendo system to have four controller ports as opposed to two from the previous [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] and [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]]. Part of the [[wikipedia:Fifth_generation_of_video_game_consoles|32-bit era]] alongside the [[Game Boy Color]], it competed against the [[wikipedia:PlayStation_(console)|Sony PlayStation]] and [[wikipedia:Sega_Saturn|Sega Saturn]], and it is the most technologically advanced home console of its day in spite of using outdated cartridges while its rivals use CDs for storing games.

One of the most critically acclaimed systems of all time like its predecessors, some of the most popular and best selling games of the N64 include ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_64|Super Mario 64]]'' (all-time seller), ''[[mariowiki:Mario_Kart_64|Mario Kart 64]]'', ''[https://goldeneye.fandom.com/wiki/GoldenEye_007_(Nintendo_64) GoldenEye 007]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time]]'', ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_64|Star Fox 64]]'', ''[https://perfectdark.fandom.com/wiki/Perfect_Dark Perfect Dark]'', ''[[fzero:F-Zero_X|F-Zero X]]'', and more. Its library is also known for introducing many spin-off games in the Nintendo franchises such as the [[mariowiki:Mario_Party_(series)|''Mario Party'' series]], ''[[smashwiki:Super_Smash_Bros._(series)|Super Smash Bros.]]'', ''[https://donkeykong.fandom.com/wiki/Diddy_Kong_Racing Diddy Kong Racing]'', and ''[[pokemon:Pokémon Stadium|Pokémon Stadium]]''.

It sold 32.92 million units worldwide and was discontinued in 2002.

The Nintendo 64's predecessor is the Super Nintendo Entertainment System, and it was proceeded by the [[Nintendo GameCube]].

== Trivia ==

* The slogans of the N64 are "Get "N" or Get Out!" and "Change the System", making it the first unique console promotion since the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]], [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|SNES]], and [[Game Boy]] (excluding "Play It Loud!" for the latter).
* The Nintendo 64's original retail price in 1996 was $199.99, which is equivalent to $400.26 as of 2024.
* The launch titles of the N64 are ''Super Mario 64'', ''Pilotwings 64'', and a Japan-only game called ''Saikyō Habu Shōgi''.
* The Nintendo 64, despite being successful in North America, was a commercial failure in Japan and Europe, as those regions strongly favored the Sony PlayStation and having a lack of RPG titles.
* Though the Nintendo 64 had an [[wikipedia:Nintendo_64_controller|innovative controller]], it is notoriously known for having a fragile analog stick, which would cause movement problems during gamplay when damaged from overuse, commonly when positioning forward. A widely known case of this occurring was by players of the original ''[[mariowiki:Mario_Party|Mario Party]]'' game, where it contains mini-games involving intensely rotating it many times (which can cause blisters in hands due to hard plastic). This has caused many third-party companies to create aftermarket versions to help eliminate these issues.
* The two critically acclaimed ''Zelda'' games released on the N64, ''Ocarina of Time'' and ''Majora's Mask'', are the system's 4th and 12th best-selling games, respectively, with 7.6 and 3.36 million sold. For comparison, its highest seller, ''Super Mario 64,'' sold 11 million (though this game was a pack-in launch title).
* Although the [[wikipedia:Atari_Jaguar|Atari Jaguar]] in 1993 claims to be the first ever 64-bit console, it was made with a 16/32-bit CPU with two dissimilar additional processors that supposedly add up to 64, but it's overly complex design proved to be a huge flaw. The Nintendo 64, however, was built with an actual 64-bit central processor, making it the first true home console at this level.[[File:OoT Stone of Agony Render.png|thumb|The Stone of Agony item strongly resembles the N64's Rumble Pak]]
* The [[Stone of Agony]] in ''Ocarina of Time'', which is used to help [[Link]] find [[Dig Cavern|Dig Caverns]], is based on the [[wikipedia:Rumble_Pak|Rumble Pak]], one of the Nintendo 64's accessories.
* According to [[Shigeru Miyamoto]], the reason why the Nintendo 64 has four controller ports is because it is Nintendo's first system powerful enough to handle a 4-player split screen without significant slowdown.
** The N64 is actually the second home console ever released with four controller ports, as the unsuccessful [[wikipedia:Atari_5200|Atari 5200]] in 1982, predates it by 14 years.
** Surprisingly, the Nintendo 64 is '''not''' the first console by Nintendo to have 4-player support; Very few Super NES games such as ''[[wikipedia:Street_Racer_(1994_video_game)|Street Racer]]'' or ''[[wikipedia:Top_Gear_3000|Top Gear 3000]]'' are capable of such gameplay through the use of an adapter known as the [[wikipedia:Multitap|Multitap]].
* Despite the N64 having an advanced 64-bit processor running at 93.73MHz (PS1 in comparison runs at 33.86MHz), virtually all of its games are set to run at 32-bit like its rivals due to storage limitations of using cartridges.
* Although the [[animalcrossing:Animal_Crossing_(series)|''Animal Crossing'' series]] was introduced to international audiences on the [[Nintendo GameCube]], it actually made its debut on this console in Japan, with its first installment named ''[[animalcrossing:Dōbutsu_no_Mori_(game)|Dōbutsu no Mori]]'' (''Animal Forest'').
* The N64 is capable of playing a select few Game Boy or [[Game Boy Color]] cartridges, through the use of the [[wikipedia:Transfer_Pak|Transfer Pak]] in certain games of its own. No [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'' series]] games were featured for the assessory, as the Nintendo-related that did come to use were for the ''[[pokemon:Pokémon_(franchise)|Pokemon]]'' and ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Super Mario]]'' franchises.
* The Nintendo 64 was originally going to be called "'''Ultra Famicom'''" in Japan, but was changed to be the first Nintendo home console to have a universal name and model design. Its prototype name in 1995 was also called "'''Ultra 64'''".[[File:N64 Fire Orange special edition model.jpg|thumb|240x240px|An example of a "Fire Orange" Nintendo 64 limited edition unit]]
* The N64 is known to be the first Nintendo home console to have official special edition models and controllers which most were made in different transparent colors such as "Jungle Green", "Fire Orange" or Atomic Purple".
** The "Atomic Purple" in particular would also be used for the Game Boy Color.
* Despite its success, the N64 had a [[wikipedia:List_of_Nintendo_64_games|relatively small library of games]] released for the system - 388 in total, due to the difficulty of producing 3D hardware for cartridges (which contain very little storage memory) in an era where CD-ROM technology became prominent and lack of 3rd-party support. For comparison, the earlier NES and Super NES had 1000+ (716 Western) and 1,755, respectively. Its main rival, the PlayStation, received 7,918 games, making it by far the best-selling console of that generation of video games (102.49 million units sold).
** Three games of its library require the Expansion Pak for them to run on the console. They are: ''[https://donkeykong.fandom.com/wiki/Donkey_Kong_64 Donkey Kong 64]'', ''Majora's Mask'', and ''Perfect Dark''.

* The [[Nintendo Switch]] in 2017 is often believed to be the next home console since the Nintendo 64 to use cartridges. However, the console itself is a hybrid and the media it uses for its games, carts (which are the same as the [[Nintendo DS]] family), although they can be attached onto the system, they are considered distinct from conventional ROM cartridges.
