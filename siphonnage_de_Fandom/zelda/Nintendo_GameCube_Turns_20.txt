"{{Term/Store|Nintendo GameCube Turns 20||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: Nintendo GameCube Turns 20}}<br/>09/07/2021 |author= Nintendo |published= September 7, 2021 |retrieved= August 16, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/7b5c665e/index.html |title= Event: Nintendo GameCube Turns 20 |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|Nintendo GameCube Turns 20}}" is an event wherein a greater number of {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} that debuted on the [[Gamecube]] randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits of characters first introduced in Nintendo GameCube games are angling their way on to the Spirit Board in this event!|author= Nintendo |published= September 7, 2021 |retrieved= August 16, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/7b5c665e/index.html |title= Event: Nintendo GameCube Turns 20 |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
