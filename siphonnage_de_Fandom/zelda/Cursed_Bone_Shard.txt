The {{Term/Store|Cursed Bone Shard|Cursed Bone Shard|Series, HWAoC|plural}} is a {{Term|Series|Weapon|link}} used by {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity_Ganon|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Cursed Bone Shard}}<br/>A screw that has been contaminated by Malice. On top of its innate strength, it is capable of repairing itself if broken, possibly as a result of Calamity Ganon's influence.|Weapons|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==

The Cursed Bone Shard can be acquired whenever a {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} or a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} is completed by {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity_Ganon|link}}, and it can also be acquired when a powerful foe or an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} is defeated when Calamity Ganon participates in the scenario or challenge. Like many other characters, the default Cursed Bone Shard of {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity_Ganon|link}} has an attack damage of 11. The Cursed Bone Shard can be fused with other {{Plural|HWAoC|Weapon|link}} used by Calamity Ganon.
