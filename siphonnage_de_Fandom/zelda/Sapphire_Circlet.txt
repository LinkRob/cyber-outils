==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Sapphire Circlet can be purchased in the [[Starlight Memories]] shop at [[Gerudo Town]] for 800 Rupees plus three [[Sapphire]]s. When worn, it provides one level of Heat Resistance.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Sapphire Circlet can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], though it cannot be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
