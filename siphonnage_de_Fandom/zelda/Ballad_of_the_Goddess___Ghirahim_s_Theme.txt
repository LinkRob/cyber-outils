The "{{Term/Store|Ballad of the Goddess / Ghirahim's Theme||Series, SSBfWU, SSBU}}" is a [[Song]] in {{SSB4|}} and {{SSBU}}.

==Overview==
"Ballad of the Goddess / Ghirahim's Theme", as the title suggests, is a medley combining remixes of [[Ghirahim]]'s theme and the "[[Ballad of the Goddess]]" from {{SS}}.

It is the 8th track in disc 2 of ''[[Ssb:Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS/Wii U|Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS/Wii U: A Smashing Soundtrack]]''.

==={{SSB4|-}}===
"Ballad of the Goddess / Ghirahim's Theme" appears as a track in {{SSB4}}. In the [[Nintendo 3DS]] version it plays in [[Ssb:Smash Run|Smash Run]], while in the [[Wii U]] version it plays on the [[Skyloft]] Stage.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"Ballad of the Goddess / Ghirahim's Theme" returns as a track in {{SSBU}}. It plays on any ''Zelda'' related Stage.
