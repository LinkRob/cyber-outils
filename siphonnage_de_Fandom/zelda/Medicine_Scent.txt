The {{Term/Store|Medicine Scent||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an object in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Medicine}} Scent!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TPHD|Medicine Scent}} is the fifth and final {{Term|TPHD|Scent|link}} learned by {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} and it replaces the {{Term|TPHD|Reekfish Scent|link}}. It is learned in the {{Term|TPHD|Medical Clinic|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} after {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} gives {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville|link}} the {{Term|TPHD|Invoice|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Medicine Scent}} is colored green, and it is picked up by a puddle of spilled medicine. The {{Term|TPHD|Medicine Scent}} is used to find the {{Term|TPHD|Wooden Statue|link}}, which was stolen after it had gotten some medicine on it while it was in {{Term|TPHD|Dr. Borville}}'s possession.<ref>{{Cite|Look, lad, if I'd been able to sell the {{Color|TPHD Red|wooden statue}} the {{Color|TPHD Red|young lady}} had, I would've been able to get the money...
