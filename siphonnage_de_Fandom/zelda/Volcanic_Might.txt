{{Term/Store|Volcanic Might||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Volcanic Might|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Volcanic Might}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} as its pilots. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, twenty {{Plural|HWAoC|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to defeat eight {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Talus|link}} within four minutes. Large amounts of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Rock Octorok|link}}, and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} can also be found attacking Vah Rudania. {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} reinforcements also appear, when five of the eight {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Talus}} are defeated and as Vah Rudania crawls around the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once all eight {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Talus}} are defeated.
