{{Term/Store|Anti-Lightning Training: III||HWAoC}} is a Level 41 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Lightning Training: III|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Lightning Training: III}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Thunderwing Butterfly|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Darner|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat three {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Guardian|link}} within five minutes. Only one can be seen at first, but the second spawns after the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} reduces the first to slightly more than half health. The third {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}} spawns after the second is brought down to the same amount. When all of them are killed, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel|link}} spawns and the time limit changes to three minutes. When it is killed, {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon}} appears and the time limit resets again to five minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon}} is defeated.
