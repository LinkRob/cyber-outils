The {{Term/Store|Album||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} function in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Album}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially obtains the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, it is in a damaged state.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|The Sheikah Slate has been damaged.}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Strange... It doesn't seem like you have the {{Color|BotW Red|basic runes}}... I don't understand why they're missing. There must be some reason...|Symin|BotW}}</ref> He can have it restored by {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And to help the one true hero, I, the one and only Purah, will restore the {{Color|BotW Red|basic functions}} missing from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> However, she is unable to do so until the furnace outside the lab has been lit with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, which will activate the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Could you please bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} in town? Oh, and also use it to light our {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}}? This sweet little {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} will start working once you do that, allowing us to restore your missing {{Color|BotW Red|runes}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} will remove the lock on the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} and allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use it.<ref>{{Cite|Hokay then, {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} repair... underway! {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} lock removed! Now...let's do this thing!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Once it confirms the missing data, the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} starts to repair the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Camera, Hyrule Compendium, and album missing files confirmed. Starting repair.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Album}} and other functions will be restored.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Repair complete.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

Once she examines the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} notices some landscape {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Album}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, it's not just a pretty girl in these images. Yes, there are also some {{Color|BotW Red|old landscape photos}} stores in the {{Color|BotW Blue|album}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> She realizes that they must have been taken by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I knew Princess Zelda had made frequent use of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Camera}} feature, but this...|Purah|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} knight, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} surmises {{Term|BotW|Link}} may have been with her when they were taken.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Linky! It dawns on my that you were Princess Zelda's appointed knight, which means... there's a strong possibility that you {{Color|BotW Red|accompanied}} her to all of the {{Color|BotW Red|places where these pictures were taken}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> She believes that they may be able to him him recover his missing {{Term|BotW|Memory|link|display=Memories}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...right. The "lost memory" thing could prove troublesome. But don't lose hope! It just means these pictures could be the missing piece to help restore your lost memories!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Due to her theory, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} go see {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}}, as she knows a lot about {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|If my hunch is correct, I suggest you check in with {{Color|BotW Red|Impa}}. She knows more about Princess Zelda than even I do!|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} confirms that the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} in the {{Term|BotW|Album}} were taken by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! This is, without a doubt, the {{Color|BotW Red|Camera}} that Princess Zelda used 100 years ago.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She believes that, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} were to visit the locations depicted, he may be able to recover some of his memories.<ref>{{Cite|It could be that if you visit the locations within those {{Color|BotW Red|pictures}}, you will be able to restore some of your lost memories.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Captured Memories|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

Any {{Term|BotW|Picture}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes are stored within the {{Term|BotW|Album}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Pictures created are saved in the album.}}|Camera|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes a {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} asks if he would like to save or delete it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Save in album?}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref> If he deletes it, it will be lost forever. There are a total of 48 slots for {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} in the {{Term|BotW|Album}}.
