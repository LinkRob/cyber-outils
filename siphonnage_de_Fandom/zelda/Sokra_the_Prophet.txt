{{Term/Store|Sokra the Prophet||Series, OoS}} is a character in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250}}</ref>

==Biography==
Sokra is a wandering prophet who points [[Link]] in the right direction when it comes to the [[Temple of Seasons]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm a wandering prophet, Sokra. Ahh...I feel it! Spring, summer, autumn, winter! The four spirits are calling you! Make no mistake! The Temple of Seasons is still hidden in this world!|Sokra the Prophet|OoS}}</ref> He also tells Link how to use the [[Rod of Seasons]]. He is very peaceful and usually spends his time sleeping close to the eastern exit of [[Horon Village]] or playing his mandolin while singing.
