The '''Gloom's Lair''' {{Exp Game|TotK}} is a location introduced in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. Situated deep beneath the [[Imprisoning Chamber]], it is most notably the place where [[Ganon]]dorf hides out following his return, until Link and the five Sages finally face him to prevent the destruction of [[Hyrule]].
