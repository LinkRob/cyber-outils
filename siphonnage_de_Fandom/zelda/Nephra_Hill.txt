{{Term/Store|Nephra Hill||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Nephra Hill'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Nephra Hill is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. [[Joloo Nah Shrine]] sits on the southern edge of Nephra Hill.

There are multiple circles of rocks that Link can complete in order to solve a Korok puzzle and gain a [[Korok Seed]].

==Nomenclature==
Nephra Hill derives its name from ''nephrite''.
