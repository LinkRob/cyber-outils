{{Term/Store|Boar|Boars|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring [[Animals]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{BotW}} features {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Red-Tusked Boar|link}}. They are commonly found in {{Plural|BotW|Forest|link}}. When encountered, they will either flee from {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} or charge at him in defense. When defeated, {{Plural|BotW|Woodland Boar}} drop {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}, while {{Plural|BotW|Red-Tusked Boar}} drop {{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat|link}}, which can be eaten or cooked to restore {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} will sometimes chase after {{Plural|BotW|Boar}} that wander near them, occasionally killing and eating them.

==Other Appearances==
===Philips CD-i Games===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{TFoE}} and {{TWoG}}, {{Plural|TFoE|Boar}} appear as unnamed enemies. They are short, fast enemies that run in a linear path and will fall to a single sword hit. However, due to their small size, striking one may become difficult if [[Link]] or [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] are even slightly on an incline. In {{TFoE|-}}, they can be found in the [[Harlequin Bazaar]]. In {{TWoG|-}}, they inhabit [[Aru Ainu]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
