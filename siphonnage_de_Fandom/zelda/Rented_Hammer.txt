The {{Term/Store|Rented Hammer||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Hammer<br/>Bang down any pegs that block your way! Enemies too—wham!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Hammer}} is used to push pegs and {{Plural|ALBW|Mole|link}} into the ground, as well as to defeat certain {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} such as {{Plural|ALBW|Deadrock|link}} and {{Plural|ALBW|Terrorpin|link}}. It is necessary for progression in the {{Term|ALBW|Tower of Hera|link}}. It is rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for fifty {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Hammer}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Hammer|link}} for 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Hammer|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
