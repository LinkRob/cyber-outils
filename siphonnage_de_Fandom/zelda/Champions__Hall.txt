{{Term/Store|Champions' Hall||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 252}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Champions' Hall}} makes up the second floor of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. Previously, the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}} could be seen from the center of the {{Term|BotW|Champions' Hall|display=Hall}}, but that view is now blocked by {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/> As a result of this, it has also been covered in {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}}. Despite this, spiral staircases on either side still allow {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to access the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, which is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can read in {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4|link}} that a part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard|link}}'s equipment can be found on the second floor.<ref>{{Cite|Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. They say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on {{Color|BotW Red|the second floor}}.|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Champions' Hall}}, he will find an EX {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} at the foot of a statue of a knight. After opening it, he will receive the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap|link}}.
