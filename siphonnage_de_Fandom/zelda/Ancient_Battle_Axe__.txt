{{Compendium/Extract|Ancient Battle Axe++}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++}} are {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout IV|link}} in "A Major Test of Strength" {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. They also have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} spawned by scanning the {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. They are {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe|link}} that have been enhanced with {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} to reach peak performance, beyond the limits of metal weaponry.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Due to {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++}} being made of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, they will deal additional damage if they are used against {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. They will not burn when exposed to a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. However, they are unable to be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. They float when dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe++}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a collection of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
