{{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod}} are one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are superior counterparts to {{Plural|BotW|Lightning Rod|link}}, commonly wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Thunder Wizzrobe|link}}. Their gems contain {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod}} are not made of flammable materials, and they can safely be used around {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They are also not made of magnetic materials and cannot be manipulated with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod}} do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity}}, and they will float in sufficiently deep bodies of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod}} are imbued with the elemental energy of {{Term|BotW|Electricity}}, generating three balls of {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} that advance when swung. However, {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod}} are fragile and will break easily if they make direct contact with {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. Thunderstorm Rods will also break simply from extended use.<ref name="Inventory"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} executes a {{Term|BotW|Spin Attack|link}} while wielding a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod}}, he will loose eight blasts at once in different directions. When thrown with the [[R Button]], {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod}} will not leave {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s hand, and they will instead produce three balls of {{Term|BotW|Lightning}} that will advance in the direction they are thrown. They will temporarily lose their magical properties if used excessively, requiring a brief cooldown period before they can be used again.
