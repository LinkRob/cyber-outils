{{Term/Store|Laruta||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Laruta|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Laruta}} is a {{Term|BotW|Zora}} child who can be found in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. For most of the day, spends her time with {{Term|BotW|Tona|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tula|link}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}}, where they talk about {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So dreamy!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Starting at 4:00 PM, she begins to run around the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}} while singing the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song|link}}."<ref>{{Cite|♪A light scale...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪It's hiding...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪A trial awaits...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> At night, she sleeps in one of the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|display=Chamber's}} pools.<ref>{{Cite|It's time for bed. I'll see you later.|Laruta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Zzz... *mumble*|Laruta|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Laruta|link}}, she sings part of the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}}."<ref>{{Cite|♪La dee dooo!♪ ♪Splits the feet... of a {{Color|BotW Red|Veiled Falls}} sight...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|♪La la laaaa!♪ ♪Your {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} awaits... It's glowing bright...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Noticing {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} asks who he is.<ref>{{Cite|Who are you?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds that he is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will become excited.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm a Hylian.] A Hylian?! Cool!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims to be a swordsman, will be impressed bt him.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm a swordsman.] A swordsman! A traveling one?! So cool!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Despite how he responds, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} still believes that {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} is much cooler.<ref>{{Cite|But, ya know, {{Color|BotW Red|Prince Sidon}}... is much cooler! Hee hee!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref>

The next time {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Laruta}}, she will introduce herself and ask if he wants to talk.<ref>{{Cite|♪Hellooo!♪ I'm Laruta! Wanna talk?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Is there anything else you'd like to ask me about?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}}," {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} explains that she loves to sing, especially the song from the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[That song?] I just looooove singing! I even remember the song from the Champion Festival!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> She then asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to hear it.<ref>{{Cite|...Do you want to hear it?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will recite the song for him.<ref>{{Cite|[I want to hear it.] OK! I'm gonna sing it now! The Champion Festival Song! ♪A gift from the sky... a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪ ♪Splits the feet... of a {{Color|BotW Red|Veiled Falls}} sight...♪ ♪Your {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} awaits... It's glowing bright...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} declines her offer, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will be upset.<ref>{{Cite|[Not right now.] Pfffft!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Next, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about {{Term|BotW|Sidon|display=Prince Sidon}}, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} claims that he is handsome and what every girl dreams of.<ref name="Laruta"/> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} says goodbye to him.<ref>{{Cite|[Good-bye.] Bye-bye!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laruta}} is among the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}} present when {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for appeasing {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}. Afterwards, she can be found standing in front of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain Square|link}}. When spoken to, she will start humming part of the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}}."<ref>{{Cite|♪La dee dooo!♪ ♪A gift from the sky... a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> However, she stops when she recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}} as the {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} who helped {{Term|BotW|Sidon}}, calling him handsome.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! You're the Hylian who helped our Prince Sidon! You're sooo handsome!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Although, she believes that {{Term|BotW|Sidon}} is even more handsome.<ref>{{Cite|Your looks still don't compare to Prince Sidon, of course...|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Due to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s appearance, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} decides to keep speaking with him..<ref>{{Cite|But you're not half bad! In fact... I wouldn't mind talking with you for a bit! Hee hee. Wanna talk? |Laruta|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} once again asks about the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}}," {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will respond the same as before, including singing the song for him.

When she finishes singing, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} states that she does not understand the meaning of the song, even though she plans on singing it at the next {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival}}.<ref>{{Cite|I don't really get what the song's about, but I'm gonna sing it at the next Champion Festival!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> She explains that {{Term|BotW|Trello|link}} is in charge of organizing the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|display=Festival}}, but he has not started preparing yet.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Trello is supposed to get the festival together, but he hasn't done anything for it yet.|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} wonders what is wrong with him, theorizing that he forgot this was his responsibility.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder what's wrong with him... Did he forget that's his job?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> Once she finishes speaking, "{{Term|BotW|The Ceremonial Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} about the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}}" after completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will yet again remark that {{Term|BotW|Trello}} is in charge of the event.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! That reminds me! Trello is in charge of planning the Champion Festival.|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> She has heard that {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}} is going to create a replica of the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}} that was used by {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They're even gonna have Dento make the same spear Mipha has!|Laruta|BotW}}</ref>
