The {{Term/Store|Letter-Entrusting Old Man||Series, TLoZ}} is a character in {{TLoZ}}.<ref name="E 219"/>

==Biography==
The {{Term|TLoZ|Letter-Entrusting Old Man}} is found in a northeast cave of {{Term|TLoZ|Hyrule|link}}. He will provide {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} with the {{Term|TLoZ|Letter|link}} and ask him to deliver it to the {{Term|TLoZ|Old Woman|link}},<ref>{{Cite|SHOW THIS TO THE OLD WOMAN.|Old Man|TLoZ}}</ref> a childhood friend of his.<ref name="E 219"/>
