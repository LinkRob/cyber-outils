The {{Term/Store|True Master Sword||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an {{Term|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref name="E 142">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
After {{Term|SSHD|Princess Zelda|link}} blesses the {{Term|SSHD|Master Sword|link}} at the {{Term|SSHD|Temple of Hylia|link}}, it becomes the {{Term|SSHD|True Master Sword}}. This extends the cross-guard's position outward while adding a white glow to the blade. With this final transformation, the {{Term|SSHD|True Master Sword}} becomes able to harm and defeat {{Term|SSHD|Demise|link}}. Additionally, the {{Term|SSHD|Skyward Strike|link}} charges faster, deals more damage, and increases its range.<ref name="E 142"/> In {{Term|SSHD|Hero Mode|link}}, the {{Term|SSHD|True Master Sword}} can charge {{Plural|SSHD|Skyward Strike|link}} instantly.{{Fact|SSHD}}
