{{Term/Store|Nyave's Boat||Series, PH}}{{Name Ref|PH}} is an {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{PH}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|PH}}{{Term|PH|Nyave's Boat}} is a {{Term|PH|Traveler's Ship|link}} owned by {{Term|PH|Nyave|link}}. It can be found in the southwestern quadrant of the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}}. When Link boards the ship, he finds Nyave passed out in the center of the cabin, with various enemies, including {{Plural|PH|Miniblin|link}} and a {{Term|PH|Geozard|link}}, attacking the ship. When Link clears the ship of foes and talks to Nyave, the guard stands up and claims to have overslept<ref>{{Cite|Oh no! Oh, golly! I must've overslept!|{{Term|PH|Nyave|link}}|{{PH}}}}</ref>. He then gives Link some {{Term|PH|Treasure|link}}. 
Nyave's Boat is also part of the {{Term|PH|Trading Quest|link}}. If Link talks to Nyave while holding the {{Term|PH|Guard Notebook|link}}, he will trade it for the {{Term|PH|Wood Heart|link}}.
