"{{Term/Store|Legendary Rabbit Trial||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Legendary Rabbit Trial}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Great Hyrule Forest}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Peeks|link}}, who reveals that he prepared a trial for him.<ref>{{Cite|Peeks thought of a trial that will make Mr. Hero stronger! You won't have to fight, and it's not scary!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> He wants to see a {{Term|BotW|Blupee|link}}, even if it is just a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|BUT you'll need to be extra careful! So... I wanna see a {{Color|BotW Red|Blupee}}! It's a blue, glowy, hoppy thing that glows in the dark! Just about Peeks's size! I just really want to see a {{Color|BotW Red|Blupee}}! Even if it's just a picture of one!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to help, {{Term|BotW|Peeks}} will be grateful.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll help you out.] I knew you were a nice guy, Mr. Hero! Thanks for listening to Peeks's request!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> While he does not know where to find a {{Term|BotW|Blupee}}, he has heard that creatures like them have been spotted throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So...I don't know where they live, but I heard there are little blue glowing critters hopping around Hyrule.|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Peeks}} is curious about the glow of {{Plural|BotW|Blupee}} and asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} feels the same.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently they're called {{Color|BotW Red|Blupees}}! I wonder how glowy they are! Aren't you curious?|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> He promises a gift if {{Term|BotW|Link}} can show him one.<ref>{{Cite|If you show me a {{Color|BotW Red|Blupee}}, I'll be sure to thank you with a gift! Good luck!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Blupee}} can be found in the forest surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, as well as in other woodlands. They also appear on {{Term|BotW|Satori Mountain|link}} on nights that the {{Term|BotW|Lord of the Mountain|link}} appears. Using the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can take a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of a {{Term|BotW|Blupee}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, he can return to show it to {{Term|BotW|Peeks}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Mr. Hero! Did you bring it for me?|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Peeks}} is shocked by its appearance, saying it is even more beautiful than he could have imagine.<ref>{{Cite|W-w-wait a minute! Is that a real {{Color|BotW Red|Blupee}}?! Wow! It's even more beautiful than {{Color|BotW Blue|Peeks}} imagined!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> Having seen a {{Term|BotW|Blupee}}, {{Term|BotW|Peeks}} is content.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks so much, Mr. Hero! Now I'm content!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh! And here's a litle thank-you gift! Peeks didn't forget!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref>

When he hands {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Rupee|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Peeks}} realizes that he might find himself in need of money.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yeah! Mr. Hero needs rupees sometimes, right?|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> He suggest that {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoot {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} at {{Plural|BotW|Blupee}} if he is ever desperate, as they drop {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} when hit.<ref>{{Cite|If you desperately need rupees...you should shoot an arrow at a {{Color|BotW Blue|blupee}}! I hear they drop lots of rupees if you hit them.|Peeks|BotW}}</ref> Before the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} ends, {{Term|BotW|Peeks}} once again expresses how happy he is to have seen a {{Term|BotW|Blupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man! I'm so glad I got to see something so cool! Peeks is so happy right now!|Peeks|BotW}}</ref>
