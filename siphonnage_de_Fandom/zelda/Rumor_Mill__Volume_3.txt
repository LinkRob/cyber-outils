==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill: Volume 3}} is found on a table at {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}. In it, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} details rumors about {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}}, saying they can be found under rocks, out in the wild, or in strange corners.<ref>{{Cite|They could be under a rock, or way out in the wilds, or even just in a suspicious nook that seems slightly not right...|Rumor Mill: Volume 3|BotW}}</ref> Since she finds it creepy, this rumor gets three out of five stars.<ref>{{Cite|Real eerie, huh?
