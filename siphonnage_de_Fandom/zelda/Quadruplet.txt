The {{Term/Store|Quadruplet|Quadruplets|Series, LA, LADX|plural}} are characters in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 233"/>

==Biography==
The {{Plural|LADX|Quadruplet}} are four children that live in {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}}. They provide {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} with tips. Two of them (named {{Term|LANS|Kidoh|link}} and {{Term|LANS|Joonya|link}} in the remake) can be seen playing with a ball near the {{Term|LANS|Village Library|link}}, one is found near the {{Term|LANS|Dream Shrine|link}}, and the last can be found near the {{Term|LANS|Trendy Game|link}}. When {{Term|LADX|Link}} returns to {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village}} after completing {{Term|LANS|Tail Cave|link}}, Kidoh and Joonya will alert him that {{Term|LANS|BowWow|link}} has been kidnapped.
