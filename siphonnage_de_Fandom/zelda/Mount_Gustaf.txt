{{Term/Store|Mount Gustaf||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mount Gustaf'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mount Gustaf is located in [[Hyrule Field]]. The ruins of an unnamed hamlet sit along the northern side of the mountain. A grass trail cuts the center of the mountain and continues onto an overpass over [[Regencia River]].

At the base of the mountain near the aforementioned ruins is a [[Natural Hiding Spot]] for a [[Korok]]. [[Link]] will need the [[Remote Bomb]] to discover its location.
