The {{Term/Store|Jump Thrust||Series, TAoL}} is a skill taught to [[Link]] by a [[Knights of Hyrule|Hyrulean Knight]] in [[Darunia Town]] in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 109 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Jump Thrust}} was referred to as the '''Up Thrust''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Up Thrust}}<br/>The Up Thrust is a special battle technique made famous by Link in which a warrior jumps into the air and strikes his enemy with a sword during the ascent.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160319120853/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/u.jsp#UpThrust|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Overview==
Since the door to the Knight's house is locked from the inside, the young hero must use the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Jump|Jump spell]] to jump from roof to roof and eventually enter the house by going down its chimney. At the end of the basement, the knight teaches Link his new skill upon talking to him.<ref>{{Cite|WHEN YOU JUMP PRESS UP TO STAB.|Hylian Knight|TAoL}}</ref>

When Link uses this skill, he leaps into the air and thrusts his [[Sword]] straight up, attacking enemies directly above him. This is especially useful against flying enemies such as [[Ache]]s, and also for clearing out blocks in [[Palace]]s after getting the [[Power Glove|Handy Glove]].

The second skill thought to Link in the game, the [[Down Thrust]], is the polar opposite, as the hero thrusts his Sword downwards, instead.

==Crossover appearances==

==={{SSB|-}} series===
In the {{SSBS}}, Link, [[Young Link]], and [[Toon Link]] can only perform this attack while they are in the air: by pressing up on the Control Stick and then the Smash button. This attack can inflict up to 16% of damage.

==={{SCII|-}}===
:''How to perform: {{SC2|up}}[[File:OpenParenthesis.png]]{{SC2|ul}}[[File:Comma.png]]{{SC2|ur}}[[File:CloseParenthesis.png]]{{SC2|va}}''
In {{SCII}}, the Jump Thrust is one of [[SoulCalibur II/Command List|Link's Commands]], although it goes by the name of '''Rocket Stab'''. It is considered to be a mid-attack and can be performed by pressing up on the Control Pad, and then the vertical attack button. Using this attack will allow Link to deal up to 28 HP worth of damage. 
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* Despite its prominent usage in spin-off games, {{TAoL|-}} remains the only main installment of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] where the Jump Thrust is seen.
* In the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], [[Link]] uses both of his hands to perform the special technique. However, in {{TAoL|-}} as well as ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', only one hand is used. Therefore, the fighting game by Namco is accurate to its mainline appearance.
