'''Hydrozol''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Hydrozol|A Zol variant that grows when exposed to water. Its liquid surface makes it immune to fire.<ref name="EoW Hydrozol">{{Cite|A Zol variant that grows when exposed to water. Its liquid surface makes it immune to fire.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Hydrozols are blue, water-based variants of [[Zol|Zols]] that grow in size when exposed to Water. Their completely resistant to Fire, and possess greater strength than regular Zols. Hydrozols have an [[Echo]] cost of two when summoned. 

They have counterparts in the [[Ignizol]], and to a lesser extent, the [[Buzz Blob]]. A [[Dark Echo|Dark]] Hydrozol can appear after coming into contact with a [[Tecuum]].
