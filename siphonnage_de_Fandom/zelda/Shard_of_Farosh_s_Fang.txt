==Location and Uses==
Shards of Farosh's Fang are acquired by shooting the dragon [[Farosh]] in the face, at which point it will glow and fall on the ground. Farosh can be found flying through [[Lake Hylia]], [[Lake Floria]], and the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. If Shards of Farosh's Fang are used in a [[Cooking Pot]], the resulting meal will always be [[Dubious Food]]. The Shards can also be used as an ingredient to make Light Yellow [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] in [[Hateno Village]].

At a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, two Shards of Farosh's Fang and five {{Plural|Series|Energetic Rhino Beetle|link}} are needed to upgrade the [[Cap of the Wild]] to its third tier. An additional Shard can be used to upgrade the [[Fierce Deity Boots]] to their third tier.

* Total for '''Cap of the Wild''': 2 (0, 0, 2, 0)

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Boots''': 1 (0, 0, 1, 0)
