{{Term/Store|Mushroom Spores|Mushroom Spores|Series, SS|plural}} are a recurring item in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
[[Link]] can obtain Mushroom Spores by scooping them up in a [[Bottle]] after slicing any [[mushroom]] found in [[Faron Woods]], [[Deep Woods]], [[Skyview Temple]], and on certain islands in [[The Sky]]. Link receives a Bottle with Mushroom Spores from [[Parrow]] so he can give them to [[Orielle]], who is missing after her [[Loftwing]] gets injured.<ref>{{Cite|What?! You found my sister?! Her bird's injured and can't fly?! That's terrible! In that case, take her this as quick as you can! These are {{Color|SS Gold|Mushroom Spores}}. They will heal the bird's injury.|Parrow|SS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWDE Mushroom Spores Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Mushroom Spores appear as Weird-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. They are imbued with the Lightning {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Mushroom Spores can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}
