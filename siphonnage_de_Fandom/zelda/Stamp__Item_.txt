{{Term/Store|Stamp|Stamps|Series, ST}} are items in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ST|Stamp (Item)}} can be collected by {{Term|ST|Link|link}} in his {{Term|ST|Stamp Book|link}} at every {{Term|ST|Stamp Station|link}}.

Upon collecting enough {{Plural|ST|Stamp (Item)}}, {{Term|ST|Niko|link}} rewards {{Term|ST|Link}} with the following:
*'''10 {{Plural|ST|Stamp (Item)}}:''' {{Term|ST|Shield of Antiquity|link}}
*'''15 {{Plural|ST|Stamp (Item)}}:''' {{Term|ST|Engineer's Clothes|link}}
*'''20 {{Plural|ST|Stamp (Item)}}:''' {{Term|ST|Great Spin Attack|link}}

Each {{Term|ST|Stamp (Item)}} is labeled in {{Term|ST|Hylian (Language)|link}}. Most of the labels are the name of the location where the associated {{Term|ST|Stamp Station}} is located.
