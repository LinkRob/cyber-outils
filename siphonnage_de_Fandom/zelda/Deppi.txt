{{Term/Store|Deppi||Series}} is a character in {{FSA}}.{{Fact}} It is a {{Term|FSA|Deku Scrub|link}} that makes its home in the {{Term|FSA|Forest of Light|link}}.

==Biography==
As requested by its partner, {{Term|FSA|Dekki|link}}, the {{Plural|FSA|Link|link}} must bring {{Term|FSA|Deppi}} three items in a specific order during their journey in the {{Term|FSA|Lost Woods (Four Swords Adventures)|link}}, the items being either a {{Term|FSA|Stone|link}}, a {{Term|FSA|Sign|link}}, a {{Term|FSA|Bomb|link}}, an Oil {{Term|FSA|Pot|link|display=Pot}}, {{Term|FSA|Bottled Water|link|display=Water}}, or Grass. If the {{Plural|FSA|Link}} bring the wrong item they will be "fired",<ref>{{Cite|You ready? I'm gonna name three items, and I want you to remember each of 'em! I ain't repeating myself!! Then you go into the woods, nab 'em, and bring 'em back. And in order, you hear!?|Dekki|FSA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So go take all those things I told you about and get 'em to Deppi in that order! You make any mistakes, and you're fired! Now, get movin'! Time's a-wastin'!|Dekki|FSA}}</ref> but if they manage to bring the items in the right order, {{Term|FSA|Deppi}} tells them to bring the good news to {{Term|FSA|Dekki}}, who will reward the {{Plural|FSA|Link}} with a {{Term|FSA|Power Bracelet|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's all of them! Your job's finished! Now give Dekki the good news|Deppi|FSA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nice work! You're not as worthless as you look, you know that? That's it! Job's done! You did well, so I'm gonna give you a special reward! A Power Bracelet! Put this on, and you can move even the heaviest things!|Dekki|FSA}}</ref>
