The {{Term/Store|Rented Tornado Rod||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Tornado Rod<br/>Fly up and hover in the air! Also gives nearby foes a whirl!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Tornado Rod}} is used to lift {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} into the air, allowing him to catch gusts of wind and land on moving platforms. It also will stun {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} for a short time if used near one. It is rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for fifty {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Tornado Rod}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}} for 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Tornado Rod|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
