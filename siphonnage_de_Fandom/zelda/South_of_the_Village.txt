{{Term/Store|South of the Village||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a location in {{LA}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LANS|South of the Village}} is located to the south of {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}}. It contains the entrance to {{Term|LANS|Tail Cave|link}}. Two {{Plural|LANS|Secret Seashell|link}} can be found here. The path in {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village}} continues through the western part of this region before stopping at a cliff.
