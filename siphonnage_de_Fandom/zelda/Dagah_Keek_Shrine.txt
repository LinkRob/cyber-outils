The {{Term/Store|Dagah Keek Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Dagah Keek Shrine}}<br/>Dagah Keek's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Dagah Keek's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek Shrine}} is near the pool at the bottom of {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}. Initially the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} is buried underground. It is possible for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to gain access to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} at any time. However, after freeing {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, he can get hints about the Shrine by talking to {{Term|BotW|Laruta|link}}, who can be found singing in front of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|♪La dee dooo!♪ ♪Your {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} awaits... It's glowing bright...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref>

When asked about the what she was singing, {{Term|BotW|Laruta}} will mention that is sung during the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I just looooove singing! I even remember the song from the Champion Festival! ...Do you want to hear it?|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> She will sing {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song|link}}, which suggests there is a Trial near {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls}},<ref>{{Cite|[I want to hear it.] OK! I'm gonna sing it now! The Champion Festival Song! ♪A gift from the sky... a {{Color|BotW Red|scale of light}}...♪ ♪Splits the feet... of a {{Color|BotW Red|Veiled Falls}} sight...♪ ♪Your {{Color|BotW Red|trial}} awaits... It's glowing bright...♪|Laruta|BotW}}</ref> beginning the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Ceremonial Song|link}}".

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can get more information by talking to {{Term|BotW|Trello|link}}, who has accidentally dropped the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|link}}, a replica of the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident|link}}, on the way to have it examined by {{Term|BotW|Dento|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} is used in a festival to honor Champion Mipha... The Champion Festival's {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}... Well, it's a fake. I planned to have that {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} examined by Dento, but on the way there, I dropped it off the bridge. It doesn't really matter. It's a fake, after all... I'll have to ask Dento to make a new one. If someone happens to pick it up, they can just keep it.|Trello|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds the {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident|display=Trident}} and brings it back to {{Term|BotW|Trello}}, he claims that it has the same power as the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} and can lead a hero to the Trial mentioned in the {{Term|BotW|Champion Festival Song}}.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Red|ceremonial trident}} you're holding... In addition to its combat use, it has the same power as the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}}. Originally, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}} was meant to lead the hero to a {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}... At least, according to the {{Color|BotW Red|legend}}. I believe the Champion Festival Song's {{Color|BotW Red|lyrics}} mention this... If you find a child who loves songs, he or she might know it.|Trello|BotW}}</ref>

Later, {{Term|BotW|Trello}} will remember the lyrics and note that there is a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls}} like the one {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}} is on.<ref name="Veiled Falls">{{Cite|Oh, well... After you left, I remembered the legend. A gift from the sky...a scale of light... Splits the feet...of a Veiled Falls sight... Your trial awaits...it's glowing bright... ... At {{Color|BotW Blue|Veiled Falls}}, there is a pedestal just like the one Lady Mipha's statue rests upon.|Trello|BotW}}</ref>{{Clarify}}<!-- How/when does he remember the lyrics --> {{Term|BotW|Trello}} then states that the legend implies that the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} should be thrust into the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, replicating the pose of {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Statue}}, and although nothing happened when they tried this, he thinks it might work for someone with the {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}'s talents.<ref>{{Cite|The legend indicates that you must thrust the {{Color|BotW Blue|Lightscale Trident}} into the same place as the statue of Lady Mipha. We tried to do just that long ago, but nothing happened. Perhaps someone with the Champion's talents might have better luck...|Trello|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Veiled Falls}}, the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} will activate if {{Term|BotW|Link}} thrusts either the {{Term|BotW|Lightscale Trident}} or {{Term|BotW|Ceremonial Trident}} straight down into it by attacking while in the air. Activating the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} causes the {{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek Shrine}} to rise from beneath the ground, completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You thrust the spear that Trello dropped into the pedestal hidden in Veiled Falls, causing an ancient shrine to appear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is proof of his worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Dagah Keek. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Dagah Keek|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek Shrine}} features no puzzles, simply a direct path to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek}}. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, which is worth 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches {{Term|BotW|Dagah Keek}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Dagah Keek|BotW}}</ref> and will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Dagah Keek|BotW}}</ref>
