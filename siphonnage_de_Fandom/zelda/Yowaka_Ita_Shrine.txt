The {{Term/Store|Yowaka Ita Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Yowaka Ita Shrine}}<br/>Collected Soul}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Collected Soul''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita Shrine}} is found in the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. It is one of four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that appear on the Great Plateau as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. In order to access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must defeat the various {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}} that appear within the {{Term|BotW|Forest of Spirits|display=Forest}} during the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|The One-Hit Obliterator, as the name suggests, can defeat enemies in one-hit. However, the wielder will also be defeated in one hit. Defeat the monsters at the locations marked on the map within the Great Plateau.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After the last {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} is defeated, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} will rise up from beneath the ground. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Yowaka Ita. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Yowaka Ita|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita Shrine}} features one large chamber with three platforms. The first platform has a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}} directly in front of the entrance. The {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita}} is behind a portcullis beside the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. Further into the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, the platform slopes downward and two {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder|link}} and two small {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} continually roll down the slope, bouncing off of obstacles in their path before falling off the bottom of the platform.

At the bottom of the platform are two thin metal bridges to the second platform, which bears a large {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} in front of a portcullis and a large metal bucket. The third platform is floating off to the side of the second platform and slopes downward toward the second platform. On the third platform, three small {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder|link}}, a large {{Term|BotW|Boulder}}, a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, and a big {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} are continually rolling down ramps and then down the slope. One part of the platform has a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}}, which pushes up anything that gets stuck on it.

To complete the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the metal bowl. It can then be used to catch the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb}} and bring them to the second platform. put the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into the slot near the entrance of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}. The small {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} can be brought back to the {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} at the entrance, opening the portcullis that blocks the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. {{Term|BotW|Yowaka Ita}} will congratulate {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial, and they present him with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Yowaka Ita|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then wish him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Yowaka Ita|BotW}}</ref>

The metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} on the third platform can be caught using the metal bowl. Opening it will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a bundle of five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. By inserting the large {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into its respective {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}} on the second platform, a nearby portcullis will open. Inside is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow|link}}.
