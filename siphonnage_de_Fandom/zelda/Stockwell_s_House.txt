{{Term/Store|Stockwell's House||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House}} is located in the northern area of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} lives within it while he is not at his {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link|display=Shop}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}},<ref>{{Cite|My master, {{Color|TMC Green|Stockwell}}, has been coming home far too late to play with me...|Fifi|TMC}}</ref> along with his {{Term|TMC|Dog|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Fifi|link}}. It is only accessible after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Spare Key|link}} and passes through {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}.

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} shrinks down to {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} size and attempts to take the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link|display=Bottle}} of {{Term|TMC|Dog Food|link}} behind {{Term|TMC|Stockwell}} in his {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|display=Shop}}, he will allow {{Term|TMC|Link}} to keep the {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle}} if he goes to {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's House}} and feeds {{Term|TMC|Fifi}} the {{Term|TMC|Dog Food}}.<ref>{{Cite|That bottle contains food for my dear little dog, Fifi... What? You just want the {{Color|TMC Red|bottle}}? That's highly unusual... Well, fine. If you feed Fifi, then I guess {{Color|TMC Blue|you can have the bottle}}.|Stockwell|TMC}}</ref>
