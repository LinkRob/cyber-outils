The {{Term/Store|Vault||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Drak">{{Cite|Yunobo...went to the {{Color|BotW Blue|vault}} to fetch some painkillers. But now that ya mention it, I suppooooose he never came back.|Drak|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Vault}} is a part of the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}. In order to approach the {{Term|BotW|Vault}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} requires at least two levels of {{Term|BotW|Flame Guard|link}}.

During the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Drak|link}} about {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}}'s location while he is searching for him.<ref>{{Cite|[Do you know Yunobo?] Why's a stranger like yoooou askin' about Yunobo?|Drak|BotW}}</ref> He will inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he saw {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} head towards the {{Term|BotW|Vault}} in order to retrieve {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}}'s painkillers, but he has yet to return.<ref name="Drak"/>

The entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Vault}} will initially covered by rubble. {{Term|BotW|Link}} will need to use {{Term|BotW|Bludo}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Cannon|link}} to clear away the rubble, after which he will be able to access the {{Term|BotW|Vault}} and find {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}}. Afrer he has calmed down, {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for saving him.<ref>{{Cite|Were you the one who broke through that crag to rescue me, goro? Thanks, brother!|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> He explains that a {{Term|BotW|Magma Bomb|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} caused a rock slide, which left him trapped.<ref>{{Cite|I came to get some painkillers for Boss, but a magma bomb caused a rock slide, sealing the entrance, goro.|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> Now that he is free, he will leave the {{Term|BotW|Vault}} to deliver the painkillers to {{Term|BotW|Bludo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, I've gotta run! But do come by and see the {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}} later, OK? He may be gruff, but he always repays a favor. I'm sure there's a {{Color|BotW Red|reward}} in it for you. OK then! Good-bye for now, goro!|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref>

Two {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found inside the {{Term|BotW|Vault}}. They contain a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} and a bundle of 10 {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found hiding in a {{Term|BotW|Natural Hiding Spot|link}} on top of the {{Term|BotW|Vault}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} lifts the {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
