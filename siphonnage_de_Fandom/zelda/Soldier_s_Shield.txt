{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Shield}} are mid-grade {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} known to have once been used by the guards of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} intended for soldiers of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> They boast a moderately high defense rating as well as a decent durability. They can be found almost anywhere throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}}, though an easy spot is {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tundra|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Shield}} is sold by {{Term|BotW|Russ|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Trilby Plain|link}} for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|soldier's shield}}? A reliable choice for combat or the slopes, sure. How's about {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}?|Russ|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys it, {{Term|BotW|Russ}} calls him a bona fide {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link|display=Shield Surfer}}, and he then shares the legend of {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks, my man! Now you're a bona fide shield surfer! By the way, have ya heard about the legend? Pretty epic. The legend of the shield-surfing world is {{Color|BotW Blue|Selmie}}! When {{Color|BotW Blue|Selmie}} boarded a shield, nothin' could stand in her way. Everybody who saw her tear it up was practically hypnotized! That's raw skill, man. She retired young and became a living legend. They say she settled down in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}} to the northwest. If you're serious about shield surfing, maybe you should track her down. {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Actually, lots of folks have tried to meet her...and they all ran into trouble along the way.}}}}|Russ|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} also gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Shield}} for completing the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} with a time between 2:00 and 2:15.<ref>{{Cite|A shiny new record deserves a shiny new shield. Here y'go!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Shield}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} ({{TLoZ}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Toon Zelda|link}} ({{TWW}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|TP|Link|link}} ({{TP}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|-}} Series {{Term|SS|Link|link}} ({{SS}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Despite their appearance, {{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Shield}} have a core made of wood, and therefore can burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> In addition, the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} will not work on them, and they cannot conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. Finally, they will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
