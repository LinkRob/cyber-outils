{{Term/Store|Cooking with Koko||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Cooking with Koko}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After helping {{Term|BotW|Koko|link}} with her {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} by completing the "{{Term|BotW|Koko's Kitchen|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, she will have another dilemma the next time {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} talks to her at the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. She has decided to make {{Plural|BotW|Hot Buttered Apple|link}} like her mother used to,<ref>{{Cite|What should Koko make now... {{Big|OH!}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Hot buttered apple}} sounds good! Mother used to make it all the time! Cottla loves it too! Let's see... The ingredients are... some {{Color|BotW Blue|apples}} and...{{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}?|Koko|BotW}}</ref> but finds that she is completely out of {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|OH, NO!!}} Koko is out of {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She calls herself a bad daughter and believes she will never be like her mother.<ref>{{Cite|Koko is a bad daughter who can't do anything right. Koko will never be like Mother...|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} should comfort her rather than helping, {{Term|BotW|Koko}} will shrug off his words.<ref>{{Cite|[Cheer up!] Koko doesn't need your sympathy. Koko needs some goat butter!|Koko|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also offer her {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter}}, but she can tell he is lying, as her father {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}} is also bad at it.<ref>{{Cite|[Voilà! Goat butter!] Koko knows when someone is lying. Father isn't good at lying either...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> Once she is done speaking to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Goat Butter}} is sold at {{Term|BotW|High Spirits Produce|link}} nearby, though it is not necessary to buy it from there. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Koko}}, he finds her lamenting the fact that she forgot the {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter}} and calling herself a bad daughter.<ref>{{Cite|Koko wants to cook a {{Color|BotW Blue|hot buttered apple}} but forgot the goat butter?! Koko is a bad daughter who can't do anything right... Koko will never be like Mother...|Koko|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers her {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|display=Butter}}, she will be surprised that he went so far for her and asks him to stay put while she cooks the meal.<ref>{{Cite|Is this... {{Big|goat butter?!}} Are you sure about this? Really? Thank you so much! Koko means it! Stay right there for a second!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Koko}} is very pleased with her {{Plural|BotW|Hot Buttered Apple}} and offers one to {{Term|BotW|Link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm! What a yummy smell! And that hint of butter makes all the difference! Oh! Oh! And the apples are superbly sweet too! Here, take some. It's really something else.|Koko|BotW}}</ref> She states that she is determined to improve her cooking, ending the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|Koko will work hard to get better and better at cooking!|Koko|BotW}}</ref>
