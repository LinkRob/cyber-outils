{{Plural|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye}} are small {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}}-made {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} found in both {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They also have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn when the {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Sheik|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye}} were designed to decrease the wielder's blind spots without sacrificing too much in the way of defense.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Shield}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by the modern {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye}} will neither conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} nor attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They will not sink if are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they cannot be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, as with all wooden shields they will burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
