==Location and Uses==
The Knight's Bridle is a type of {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} that can be obtained by {{Term|BotW|Jini|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Mounted Archery Camp|link}}. They're given when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} breaks 20-22 targets during the {{Term|BotW|Horseback Archery|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tell you what... You break {{Color|BotW Red|more than 20}} targets, and I'll hand over my {{Color|BotW Red|horse gear}}.|Jini|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hm...{{Color|BotW Red|[X] targets}}... You've got some fire in you, young man. The best archers say {{Color|BotW Red|targets seem to freeze when they concentrate on them}}. I think you could reach that level too. Here's a gift to help you on your travels. Hope you find a good use for it.|Jini|BotW}}</ref> Before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} gave these Bridles to their {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Despite claiming that they provide additional armor to a Horse's head and neck,<ref name="Inventory"/> they're purely cosmetic.

Workers at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}} are able to equip Link's Horses with the Knight's Bridle.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Knight's Bridle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> However, they can't be placed on {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
