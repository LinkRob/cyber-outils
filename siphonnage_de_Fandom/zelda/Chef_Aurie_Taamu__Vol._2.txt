{{Term/Store|''Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 2''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 2|Dina|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 2}} is the second in a series of cookery books written by {{Term|BotW|Aurie Taamu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This collection of Aurie Taamu's notes is so amazing! I feel like I learn something new each time I read it.|Dina|BotW}}</ref> It can be found in the room where {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}} holds her {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} class, where it is used by the students who attend it.
Unlike the {{Term|BotW| Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 1|link|display=first}} volume in the series, {{Term|BotW|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 2}} is located too high up for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to read.<ref>{{Cite|It's too high up to read...|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 2|BotW}}</ref> As a result he must wait for the nightly arrival of {{Term|BotW|Dina|link}}, who will offer to read it for him.<ref>{{Cite|That book was written by an amazing chef, Aurie Taamu. {{Small|Just between us... He was the voe that our teacher was in love with once. Or so I hear...}} If you can't reach it, would you like me to read it for you?|Dina|BotW}}</ref> It details the best ways to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cook}}, the use of {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract|link}}, and selling meals.

{{Term|BotW|Chef Aurie Taamu, Vol. 2}} has a light green cover that is decorated with dark green decoration. It is left open on a page showing a diagram on chopping {{Plural|BotW|Carrot|link}}.
