{{Term/Store|Shell Apartment|Shell Apartments|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shell Apartment|Shipyard|PH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|PH|Shell Apartment}} are {{Plural|PH|Bridge|link}} belonging to the {{Term|PH|Tropical Ship|link}} set of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. They smell of the ocean, but are still very comfortable.<ref>{{Cite|Top creature comforts combined with the smell of ocean air.|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> Just like most {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can collect multiple {{Plural|PH|Shell Apartment}}. They can be obtained through various methods:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Methods}}

Attaching three, six, or all eight {{Term|PH|Tropical Ship}} parts to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} will increase its stamina to five, six, or seven {{Plural|PH|Heart}}, respectively. {{Plural|PH|Shell Apartment}} can be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} for either 50, 150, or 800 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}. Their value is determined randomly when the [[Saving|Save]] file is created, and is the same for all {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} set.
