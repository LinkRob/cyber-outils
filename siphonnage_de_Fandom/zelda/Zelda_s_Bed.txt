Zelda's Bed is an [[Echo]] from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{hatnote|”There's no better place to sleep than your own bed from your own castle.” — [[Notebook]]<ref name="Zelda's Bed">{{Cite|There's no better place to sleep than your own bed from your own castle.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Zelda's Bed is an Echo that can be used to bridge gaps and reach otherwise inaccessible areas. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can regain health by sleeping in Zelda's bed, or any other bed found in {{EoW|-}}.
