'''Sneakstrikes''' are recurring abilities in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda Series]].<ref>'Sneakstrike' - N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]])([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref>
==Breath of the Wild==
Sneakstrikes can be performed by sneaking behind an enemy and attacking it while it is unaware of [[Link]]'s presence. Another way of performing a sneakstrike is by attacking a sleeping enemy. Successfully executing a Sneakstrike will deal 8 times the damage by Link's equipped melee weapon.<ref>{{Cite|Sneak up on an enemy and unleash a melee attack to do some major damage.|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

== Tears of the Kingdom ==
A new method included involves striking a [[Puff Shroom]] (even if the enemy all ready knows of your presence), causing a cloud of white fog to appear. In the fog, one can then run around the enemy and strike from behind (it appears as though sneaking is not necessary).<ref>[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I0-nzrEft4w Mr. Aonuma's Tears of the Kingdom Gameplay Demonstration] - YouTube, published March 28, 2023.</ref>
