{{Term/Store|Whip Bird|Whip Birds|Series, ST}} are {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 63}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ST|Whip Bird}} are found at a variety of stations, mostly in the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}}. They are black-feathered and have pink beaks and legs. {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can latch onto the wooden bars they carry with the {{Term|ST|Whip|link}} and they will carry him in the air along a pre-determined path, often granting access to higher areas that he would otherwise not be able to reach. {{Plural|ST|Whip Bird}} can be called directly to {{Term|ST|Link}} if he plays the {{Term|ST|Song of Birds|link}}.
