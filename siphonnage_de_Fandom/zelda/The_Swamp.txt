{{Term/Store|The Swamp||Series, FSA}} is the second {{Term|FSA|Stage|link}} of the {{Term|FSA|Near the Fields|link}} {{Term|FSA|Level|link}} in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Big|The Swamp}}'''|N/A|FSA}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
At the entrance to the swamp, {{Term|FSA|Kaepora Gaebora|link}} arrives and warns the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} of the poison lands ahead. {{Term|FSA|Kaepora Gaebora}} says {{Term|FSA|Vaati|link}} poisoned the swamp and drove out its regular inhabitants; all that remain are monsters. The grassy terrain of {{Term|FSA|The Swamp}} is pockmarked with poisonous pools of {{Term|FSA|Toxic Water|link}} and lined by rocky cliffsides. The cliffsides have many {{Term|FSA|Cave|link}} systems interconnecting many parts of {{Term|FSA|The Swamp}}. These {{Term|FSA|Cave}} systems come in especially handy when {{Term|FSA|Dark Link|display=Shadow Link|link}} turns up with his {{Term|FSA|Bomb|section=Big Bomb|display=Big Bombs|link}}, serving as life-saving retreats.

[[File:The Swamp FSA.png|thumb|left|200px|The {{Term|FSA|Graveyard|link}} located north of {{Term|FSA|The Swamp}}]]
Further into the area, {{Term|FSA|Dampé|link}} tends to a cemetery. {{Term|FSA|Dampé}} tells of a {{Term|FSA|Dark Mirror|display=mirror|link}} in which a dark tribe was sealed away long ago. {{Term|FSA|Dampé}} indicates that it rested in the {{Term|FSA|Lost Woods|display=Forest of Light|link}} which he says is now the Forest of Dark. The four {{Plural|FSA|Links}} access this area by lifting large {{Plural|FSA|Tree|link}} from a nearby path with the {{Term|FSA|Power Bracelet|link}}.
