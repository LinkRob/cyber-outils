{{Term/Store|Link: Two-Handed Weapons||HWAoC}} is a Level 10 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link: Two-Handed Weapons|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Link: Two-Handed Weapons}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}, and with only a Two-Handed {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}} equipped. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Restless Cricket|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s objective is to defeat 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are Hylian {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|link}}, except for three {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} that spawn at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. Once 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} spawns and the time limit is taken away. {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} must be defeated for the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} to end.
