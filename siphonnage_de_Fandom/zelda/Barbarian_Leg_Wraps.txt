==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Barbarian Leg Wraps are a piece of [[Armor]] located in [[Qaza Tokki Shrine]] at the center of the [[North Lomei Labyrinth]]. After obtaining them, [[Granté]] will begin selling the piece of Armor for 4,000 [[Rupee]]s. According to their description, the Leg Wraps were worn by the warriors of an ancient warlike tribe from the [[Faron (Region)|Faron]] [[Region]].<ref name="Inventory"/> When worn, they provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with one level of Attack Up. The Barbarian Leg Wraps can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Barbarian Leg Wraps, alongside the [[Barbarian Helm]] and [[Barbarian Armor]], compose the [[Barbarian Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Charge Atk. Stamina Up [[Set Bonus]].
