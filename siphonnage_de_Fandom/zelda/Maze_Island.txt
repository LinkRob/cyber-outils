{{Term/Store|Maze Island||Series, TAoL, PH}} is a recurring geolithic labyrinth in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 224 {{Exp Game|TAoL}} & 284 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Appearances and Features==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
{{Term|TAoL|Maze Island}} is a rather large island in [[Eastern Hyrule]] that, as its name suggests, is a natural labyrinth. Narrow passageways formed by impassable rock walls, rivers with sparse bridges, and hidden pitfalls make travelling on this island both dangerous and time-consuming. Maze Island's single landmark is the {{Term|TAoL|Maze Island Palace|link}}, which {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}} visits during his quest to wake the sleeping {{Term|TAoL|Princess Zelda|link}}.

The {{Term|TAoL|Child|link}} from the {{Term|TAoL|Darunia Town|link}} can be found here on the east side of the island, while a hidden {{Term|TAoL|Magic Container|link}} rests in the west.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH|-}}, {{Term|PH|Maze Island}} can be found in the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link|display= Northeastern Sea}}; it is initially unmarked. It contains three increasingly difficult mazes: Beginner, Normal, and Expert. Link is required to strike all the {{Plural|PH|Gossip Stone|link}} that appear once the maze opens to complete each level. Completing the Beginner difficulty (four Gossip Stones) gets Link a {{Term|PH|Wisdom Gem|link}}, and finishing the Normal difficulty (eight Gossip Stones) nets him a {{Term|PH|Treasure Map|link}}. Only by completing Expert (fourteen Gossip Stones) will the island relinquish its {{Term|PH|Heart Container|link}}.

There is also a {{Term|PH|Wisdom Gem}} inside a chest accessible by bombing a wall, as well as three Big Green {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}} hidden under the ground.
