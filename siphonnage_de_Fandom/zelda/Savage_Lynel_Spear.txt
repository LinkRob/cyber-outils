{{Compendium/Extract|Savage Lynel Spear}}
{{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Spear}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lynel|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lynel|link}}. Due to the axe-like design of their spearheads and their exceptional weight, they have extreme destructive power.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly used in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Spear|display=Spears}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Spear}} conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, though they cannot burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in water, but can be manipulated and picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
