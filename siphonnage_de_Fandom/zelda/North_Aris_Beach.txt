{{Term/Store|North Aris Beach||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Stalnox">{{Cite|North Aris Beach<br/>{{Big|Stalnox}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|North Aris Beach}} is located north of {{Term|BotW|Aris Beach|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Stalnox|link}} can be found in the northern part of {{Term|BotW|North Aris Beach|link}}.<ref name="Stalnox"/> It is inactive during the day, but it will attack {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if approached at night. In addition to regular {{Term|BotW|Stalnox}} drops, this one will also leave behind a {{Term|BotW|Royal Halberd|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Great Flameblade|link}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found buried in the sand on {{Term|BotW|North Aris Beach}}. After pulling it from the ground with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can open it to receive a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}.
