{{Term/Store|Blatchery Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Blatchery Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Blatchery Plain is located in [[West Necluda]] between the [[Dueling Peaks]] and [[Fort Hateno]]. Blatchery Plain is a flat section of land covered in tall [[Grass]] that is surrounded by high cliffs and the [[Squabble River]]. In the center of the Blatchery Plain, a {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} sits near some pillars and ruins. [[Celessa]] can be found walking the [[Road]] through the Plain, talking about the battle that took place in the Plain years before.<ref>{{Cite|An ancient battle took place here… Even just being near a place so full of tragic memories is painful.|Celessa|BotW}}</ref> Another traveler, [[Agus]] walks along the road with her {{Term|BotW|Donkey|link}}. She will sell various things to Link if he talks to her.<ref>{{Cite|I'd love it if you bought something, but, I mean, I'm happy just to show you what I've got for sale. The rest is up to you!|Agus|BotW}}</ref> Two travelers, [[Joute]] and [[Toma]], can be seen riding along the road on horseback.

During the second [[Great Calamity]], Blatchery Plain was overrun with [[Guardian#Breath of the Wild|Guardian]]s that were making their way to Fort Hateno.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 385}}</ref> After Link fell in battle to a Guardian, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]'s powers awoke and she destroyed most of the surrounding Guardians.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 384}}</ref> Consequently, the remains of several Guardians litter the Plain and can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s.

If Link defeats all the monsters in the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}, a stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Opal]] will unlock for Link to obtain.
