==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Carrot Stews are made in {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} using [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Fresh Milk]], [[Goat Butter]], and either a [[Swift Carrot]] or an [[Endura Carrot]]. Regular Carrot Stew is made by either using both types of carrots or by using a filler as a fifth ingredient. Consuming one will restore eleven {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They sell for 70 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} if a carrot and an additional ingredient are used and for 120 Rupees when both carrots are used. These stews are left to simmer for a long time, which brings out the carrots' sweetness.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Using either type of carrot will result in one of two different variations of Carrot Stew:

* '''Enduring Carrot Stew''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Carrot Stew}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This simple stew sat simmering for a long time to bring out the sweetness of the carrots.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using an Endura Carrot. Eating one of these will completely replenish and overfill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/> They also heal seven of his Hearts and sell for 100 Rupees.

* '''Hasty Carrot Stew''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Carrot Stew}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This simple stew sat simmering for a long time to bring out the sweetness of the carrots.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using a Swift Carrot. When consumed, Link's speed will temporarily increase.<ref name="Hasty"/> They also restore four Hearts to his Life Gauge, but they can also be sold for 40 Rupees.
