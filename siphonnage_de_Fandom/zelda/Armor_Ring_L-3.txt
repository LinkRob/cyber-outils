==Location and Uses==
The Armor Ring L-3 can be found at the end of the [[Hero's Cave]] in a [[Linked Game]] of {{OoA}}. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it decreases damage taken by [[Link]] while also decreasing [[Sword]] damage.<ref name="Description"/> The Armor Ring L-3 can be transferred to {{OoS}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
