{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Bow}} are weak ranged {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} commonly used by travelers for protection.<ref name="Inventory"/> Though they do not deal much damage, they can be used to attack {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} at a distance.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are most commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}, where they are either wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} or hidden in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}.

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Bow}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} (Archer) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Bow}} are flammable, and they will therefore burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. However, they are not magnetic, so they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, but they will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Traveler's Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear|link}} archetype of equipment, which are all said to be used to defend travelers throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> A {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Bow}} is part of an unnamed set of equipment used by {{Term|BotW|Link}} in promotional media, alongside a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield|link}}.
