{{Term/Store|Prime Meat Curry|Prime Meat Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Prime Meat Curries can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Raw Prime Meat]], [[Hylian Rice]], and [[Goron Spice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can eat these curries to have some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Since high-quality meat is used when making these meals, they have a deeper taste than other curries.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add additional ingredients when making Prime Meat Curry, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When they're eaten, all of [[Link|Link's]] Hearts will be healed, and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. These curries will refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} if they're eaten.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Consuming these will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Link will receive a boost to his movement speed by eating these.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. If these curries are eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. These curries will increase Link's attack power when they're consumed.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Link's defense will be boosted if he eats these.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When they're eaten, Link will receive a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Prime Meat Curry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Prime Meat Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The high-quality meat in this curry has given it a deeper taste than most other curries.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
