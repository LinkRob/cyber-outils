"{{Term/Store|A Brother's Roast||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Brother's Roast}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Goron City}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Bladon|link}} in his home, where he is wondering where someone went.<ref>{{Cite|Where did he go?|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches him, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} complains that his brother has yet to return.<ref>{{Cite|My reckless brother hasn't come back yet. What in the world is he thinkin'?|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about his brother, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} explains that he is proud of {{Term|BotW|Gonguron|link}}, but he went to investigate "the secret of the hero" and never returned.<ref>{{Cite|[Brother?] My little brother, Gonguron. I'm really proud of him, but... He said he was gonna go dig up {{Color|BotW Red|the secret of the hero}}, and then he just took off!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} ran away, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} says that, if he were, he would not have said he was searching for the "secret of the hero".<ref>{{Cite|[Did he run away?] No way! If you're gonna run away, you wouldn't first tell your bro you're gonna dig up the {{Color|BotW Red|secret of the hero}}!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> With sadness, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} wonders what is happening with {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}}.<ref>{{Cite|I really don't know what in the world got into him...|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What in the world is goin' on with that kid...|Bladon|BotW}}</ref>

Once again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} a question. If he asks where {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} went, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} shares his belief that he went to {{Term|BotW|Gorko Tunnel|link}},<ref>{{Cite|[Where did he go?] I'm certain my little brother went off to {{Color|BotW Blue|Gorko Tunnel}}, southwest of {{Color|BotW Blue|Gorko Lake}}.|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> which the {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}} gave up on mining due to its extremely hard bedrock.<ref>{{Cite|The bedrock in that tunnel is incredibly hard, so us Gorons gave up tryin' to dig it out ages ago.|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires about the "secret of the hero", {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} explains that legends state it is hidden behind that bedrock.<ref>{{Cite|[Secret of the hero?] Southwest of {{Color|BotW Blue|Gorko Lake}}, there's an abandoned spot called {{Color|BotW Blue|Gorko Tunnel}}. Behind that hard bedrock, there lies not just ore...but the {{Color|BotW Red|secret of the hero}}! ...Or so the legend goes. True or not, it makes for a good story. I heard it from my dad's cousin's neighbor's uncle.|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> Ultimately, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} wishes that {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} would give up on his dreams and help out with work.<ref>{{Cite|Instead of chasin' dreams, I'd prefer to see him face reality and help us out with our important Goron work...|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives at {{Term|BotW|Gorko Tunnel}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} collapsed in front of the bedrock.<ref>{{Cite|*cough* *wheeze* Phew! I'm spent.|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> He wishes to keep mining the rubble in order to find the secret of the hero, as he believes he will become famous if he does so.<ref>{{Cite|To think that somewhere behind all this rubble is the {{Color|BotW Red|secret of the hero}}... I know I'll become really great and important if I can find it!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> However, he is not strong enough to continue, and his stomach has been rumbling.<ref>{{Cite|My only problem is that I don't have the strength to go on. My belly's empty and rumblin' like mad! What to do...|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> Just then, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Gorko Tunnel|display=Tunnel}},<ref>{{Cite|Brother! So this is where you've been!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> where he reveals he traced his brother's steps to find him.<ref>{{Cite|I tried to think like my brother and do what he would do so I could find him...|Bladon|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}}'s brother, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} asks for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s assistance in helping him.<ref>{{Cite|I need you to do me a favor! As a brother, I can't just sit idly by. I have to help him!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go down the hill to the base of {{Term|BotW|Gortram Cliff|link}} and retrieve and {{Term|BotW|Rock Roast|link}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|Exit here and head {{Color|BotW Red|down the hill}} to Gortram Cliff. There you can find {{Color|BotW Red|rock roasts}} rollin' around. I need ya to please grab one of 'em for me.|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> While he would like to get it himself, but his "Goron shoulder" condition, which he does not wish to discuss, prevents him from doing so.<ref>{{Cite|I'd like more than anything to go there and get it myself. It's just... Well, I have a chronic condition. Yeah. It's called Goron shoulder. Very serious. I'd rather not talk about it.|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he is relying on {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him a {{Term|BotW|Rock Roast}}.<ref>{{Cite|Needless to say, I expect a lot from you! Please don't let me down!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref>

The path from {{Term|BotW|Gorko Tunnel}} to the base of {{Term|BotW|Gortram Cliff}} is filled with {{Plural|BotW|Red Chuchu|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}. They can be either avoided or defeated when traversing this path. {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} will also roll in front of {{Term|BotW|Link}}, which will knock him down if they hit him. To secure a safe path, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can defeat all of the {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} that he will have to face and trigger all of the {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} beforehand, which will allow him to walk straight to {{Term|BotW|Gorko Tunnel}} with no issues.

Upon {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s return with a {{Term|BotW|Rock Roast}}, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} will beckon him over to drop it in the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Finalyl! I've been waitin' on ya! Go on—drop the roast in the pot. Quickly!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> He declares that this {{Term|BotW|Rock Roast}} he has ever seen and immediately decides to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} it.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm... It's amazing! This is the finest {{Color|BotW Red|rock roast}} I've ever laid eyes on! I'm gonna cook it up right now!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> The result is a {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I did it! I made a {{Color|BotW Red|deliciously grilled rock roast}}!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> the scent of which causes {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} to sit up.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Am I hunger-hallucinating, or do I really smell {{Color|BotW Red|deliciously grilled rock roast}}?!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} offers the {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast}} to {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} to help him feel better.<ref>{{Cite|Brother! It's ready! You're gonna eat it and LOVE IT, and everything will be as it should be again!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref>

Since it looks so tasty, {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} decides to eat the {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast}} right away.<ref>{{Cite|Look at this! It looks so tasty! I NEED to eat it right away!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> He enjoys the gritty, crunchy texture,<ref>{{Cite|Yummy! Delicious! Incredible! That texture! That crunch! Ooooh, and that gritty feeling as it glides down my throat!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> especially after it has been seared to perfection.<ref>{{Cite|OH! Yes! Can't forget that savory flavor of condensed minerals seared in high heat to perfection! It's a taste explosion!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast}} restores {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}}'s strength,<ref>{{Cite|It's restoring my strength! I'm like a phoenix reborn and ready to fly! In fact, I AM Death Mountain!!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> and he compliments his brother's {{Term|BotW|Cooking}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your cookin' is the best of the best, brother!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> Fully energized, {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} decides to return to mining the bedrock.<ref>{{Cite|Now I'm full and my belly is happy! Let's do this!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref>

With a shout about his {{Term|BotW|Goron}} power, {{Term|BotW|Gonguron}} is able to hit the bedrock hard enough to break it,<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Graaaaghhh! GORON POWER, GOOOO!!!}}|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> revealing {{Term|BotW|Kayra Mah Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|WOW! There it is! The {{Color|BotW Red|hero's secret}} has revealed itself to me!!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> However, both he and {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} note that it is similar to {{Term|BotW|Shae Mo'sah Shrine|link|display=something else}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Weird... I feel like I've seen somethin' like this before in Goron City...}}|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So this is the {{Color|BotW Red|secret of the hero}}, eh? {{Small|Hmm... It looks oddly familiar...}}|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Bladon}} is proud of his brother for unearthing the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, what an amazing discovery! That's my brother for ya!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> The two of them celebrate together, and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} is completed.
