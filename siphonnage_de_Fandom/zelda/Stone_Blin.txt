'''Stone Blins''' are {{Plural|HWDE|Enemy|link}} in {{HW|L}}.{{Name Ref|HWL}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|HWDE|Stone Blin}} are stronger variants of {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin|link}}. They have an alternate color scheme to {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin}} and wear stone armor. They also wield a stone club instead of the {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin}}' metal club. Like {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin}}, they swing their club downwards and side to side. They also do a power swing to a side across their bodies like the {{Plural|HWDE|Big Blin}}, only with an extra swing outward in the opposite direction before exposing its {{Term|HWDE|Weak Point Gauge|link}}. Lastly, like the lesser counterpart, it blocks with its club as well as charges a downward swing over its head where the {{Term|HWDE|Warrior|link}} can deny it with the {{Term|HWDE|Hammer|link}} item attack, causing it to fall on its back.

In "{{Term|HWDE|A New Disturbance|link}}", a {{Term|HWDE|Stone Blin}} known as the {{Term|HWDE|Boss Blin|link}} is the leader of the monster forces trying to take over the {{Term|HWDE|Forsaken Fortress (Hyrule Warriors Legends)|link}} from the {{Term|HWDE|Helmaroc King|link}}. After driving the Giant Bird away, the {{Term|HWDE|Boss Blin}} starts summoning {{Plural|HWDE|Ruffian|link}} from the three outer {{Plural|HWDE|Keep|link}} of the Fortress to attack the {{Term|HWDE|Hyrulean Forces|link}}' Allied Base in {{Term|HWDE|Windfall Island|link}}. After acknowledging the outer {{Plural|HWDE|Keep}} are under the Hyrulean Forces' control, the {{Term|HWDE|Boss Blin}} will then summon a {{Term|HWDE|Gohma|link}} to attack the Allied Base, making it a priority to defeat. Once {{Term|HWDE|Gohma}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWDE|Boss Blin}} will become enraged and will not flinch from regular attacks. 

When defeated, {{Plural|HWDE|Stone Blin}} may drop {{Plural|HWDE|Stone Blin Buckler|link}} or {{Plural|HWDE|Stone Blin Helmet|link}} as their Bronze and Silver {{Plural|HWDE|Material|link}}, respectively.
