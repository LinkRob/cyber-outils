{{Compendium/Extract|Ancient Battle Axe}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} often wielded by the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} found within {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> In particular, they can be found in {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}} designated by the Trial name "A Minor Test of Strength." Scanning the {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe}}. They were forged using {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, and while they are powerful, their unique shape makes them less durable.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Since {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe}} are made from {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, they will deal extra damage when they are used against {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}}. They do not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they cannot be manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. They will, however, float on {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}} in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>
