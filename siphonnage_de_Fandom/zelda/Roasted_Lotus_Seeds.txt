{{Plural|BotW|Roasted Lotus Seeds}} are the result of [[Fleet-Lotus Seeds]] that have made contact with [[Fire]]. Roasted Lotus Seeds are said to have a soft and flaky center inside their outer shell.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> When eaten, they restore three-quarters of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}}.
