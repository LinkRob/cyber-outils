{{Term/Store|Trial of the Ancients||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Trial of the Ancients}} is located in an {{Term|HWAoC|Ancient Shrine|link}} on the {{Term|HWAoC|Great Plateau|link}}, and is unlocked after completing the {{Plural|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} "{{Term|HWAoC|The Trial of Stones|link}}," "{{Term|HWAoC|The Trial of Monsters|link}}," and "{{Term|HWAoC|The Trial of the Mighty Foe|link}}." In the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given three minutes to defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Monk Maz Koshia|link}}. When first cleared, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} rewards the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} with an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Star Fragment|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}. Completing this mission also unlocks the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} "{{Term|HWAoC|Monk's Training|link}}" and unlocks {{Term|HWAoC|Monk Maz Koshia}} as a playable {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}.
