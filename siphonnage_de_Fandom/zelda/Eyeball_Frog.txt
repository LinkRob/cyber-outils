The {{Term/Store|Eyeball Frog||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 122}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can obtain the {{Term|OoT3D|Eyeball Frog}} by taking {{Term|OoT3D|Biggoron|link}}'s {{Term|OoT3D|Prescription|link}} to {{Term|OoT3D|King Zora|link}}.<ref name="King Zora">{{Cite|Ooh... This is... Well... Hmmm... Hmmm... {{Color|OoT3D Red|Eyedrops}}... You might say we have them, you might say we don't... We do have the ingredients. If you take the {{Color|OoT3D Red|ingredients}} to the doctor at the Lakeside Laboratory, he can make the drops for you.|King Zora|OoT3D}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} must then bring it to the {{Term|OoT3D|Lake Scientist|link}} at the {{Term|OoT3D|Lakeside Laboratory|link}} within three minutes of obtention, or else it will spoil.<ref name="King Zora"/><ref>{{Cite|But you need to deliver them fresh... Can you make it {{Color|OoT3D Red|before they spoil}}?|King Zora|OoT3D}}</ref> If Link tries [[Warping]] while carrying the Eyeball Frog, it will spoil instantly.
