The {{Term/Store|Shae Loya Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shae Loya Shrine}}<br/>Aim for the Moment}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Aim for the Moment''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Shae Loya Shrine}} is located on a ridge just south of {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shae Loya|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shae Loya. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Shae Loya|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside {{Term|BotW|Shae Loya Shrine}} is a {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}} that repeatedly pushes a giant {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} upwards before catching it. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} to stop the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} while it is in the air, and then he must strike it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} to knock it off of its path. From here, it will land in the nearby {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}, which in turn activates a second {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s side of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.

This {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} will repeatedly jut up and down. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} is sent into the air by this {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}, he will be able to see an alcove containing a {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch|link}}. Shooting this {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} will raise the portcullis blocking the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the {{Term|BotW|Shae Loya}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will commend him for completing the Trial, and they give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shae Loya|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Shae Loya}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shae Loya|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} rests on a wooden shelf above the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, which is suspended by ropes. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot the ropes in order to make the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} fall to floor level. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Falcon Bow|link}}. There is a second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} behind the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} on the opposite side of the shrine. After being sent into the air by the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} on his side, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} to drift over to the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}.
