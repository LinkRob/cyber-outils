{{Term/Store|Armor|Armor|Series, BotW}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Clothes|link|display=Clothing}} and protective covering that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can wear in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Armor|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Armor|Inventory|TotK}}</ref> Each piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} has a numeric rating that contributes to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s overall Defense. The higher {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s Defense, the less damage he will take from {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} attacks.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Armor}} serves a variety of purposes, primarily to provide {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with defensive padding, allowing him to endure more damage. Damage numbers are shown numerically in {{BotW|-}}. Each number of damage normally deals a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}. For example, an attack with a power of 4 will damage {{Term|BotW|Link}} by one full {{Term|BotW|Heart}}. By wearing {{Term|BotW|Armor}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can decrease the amount of damage he takes. Each piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} has a numeric rating that contributes to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s overall Defense. The higher {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s Defense, the less damage he will take from {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} attacks. Wearing {{Term|BotW|Armor}} subtracts the attack's strength value by {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s total defense value. Wearing {{Term|BotW|Armor}} totaling a defense level of three or higher will reduce that attack's damage to a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart}}, and no matter his defense, {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes at least a quarter of a {{Term|BotW|Heart}} of damage from any attack. {{Plural|BotW|Armor}} often pair with other pieces to form {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set|link}}.

Certain pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} also provide {{Plural|BotW|Effect|link}} that can boost {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s attack, movement speed on {{Term|BotW|Snow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sand|link}}, through {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}, or while {{Term|BotW|Climbing|link}}, boost his stealth capabilities, or protect him from the environment. Environmental hazards include {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, heat, {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}, and the cold. Most clothing can be enhanced up to four times by the revived {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}, with the number of available upgrades dependent on how many {{Plural|BotW|Great Fairy}} have been restored. Each upgrade comes with a certain cost of {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set}} that have been enhanced at least twice may provide {{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has no {{Term|BotW|Armor}} on, some characters will pass comment.<ref>{{Cite|Aren't you...a bit chilly?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I hope you'll permit me a question... If I may be so bold...are you not chilly?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref>

Extra pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} were added in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Downloadable Content|Expansion Pass DLC]], and some pieces can also be gained by scanning certain [[Amiibo]] on the [[Wii U]] and [[Nintendo Switch]] consoles.
