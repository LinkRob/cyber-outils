The {{Term/Store|Rainbow Bridge||Series, OoT}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT |publisher= Nintendo |page= 104}}</ref>It is a temporary bridge conjured using the power of the Six [[Sage]]s in order to help [[Link]], the [[Hero of Time]], reach the final dungeon, [[Ganon's Castle]].<ref>{{Cite|We six will gather our power to create a bridge to the castle where Ganondorf dwells...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref>

==Features and overview==
The bridge is created by the [[Sage]]s when Link arrives at Ganon's Castle after [[Princess Zelda]]'s capture in order to rescue her from [[Ganondorf]]'s clutches. [[Rauru]] calls out to Link once he gets to the front of the castle gates, and tells Link the way to save Zelda.<ref>{{Cite|Bring down the six barriers, and save Princess Zelda!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Using their combined power in the form of colored orbs, the Sages then create a glass-like bridge resembling a [[wikipedia:Rainbow|real rainbow]], allowing the Hero of Time to proceed and enter the castle. Link himself can be seen checking its surface by taking one step and having a close look before gameplay resumes.

When the castle eventually collapses following Ganondorf's defeat and during the final battle against [[Ganon]] himself, the Rainbow Bridge is no longer visible by the former entrance.

In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'', the Rainbow Bridge was redesigned to have different textures and guardrails to prevent Link from accidentally falling off its edges.

== Trivia ==
* The color of the Rainbow Bridge in the original [[Nintendo 64]] game is based on the colors representing the Six Sages.
* The Rainbow Bridge's appearance is likely inspired by the [[mariowiki:Mario_Kart_(series)|''Mario Kart'' series]]' [[mariowiki:Rainbow Road|Rainbow Road]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], as such courses are made out of translucent rainbow colored roads.
