The '''Bow of Might Lv. 2''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
{{hatnote|”An enhanced bow capable of fighting off the monsters of the rifts.” — [[Key Items in Echoes of Wisdom|Key Items]]<ref name="Bow of Might Lv. 2">{{Cite|An enhanced bow capable of fighting off the monsters of the rifts.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
The [[Bow]] of Might Lv. 2 can be upgraded to [[Bow of Might Lv. 3|Lv. 3]] by using 20 [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]] at [[Lueburry|Lueburry’s]] House, significantly increasing its damage.

==Trivia==
* When Link infiltrated [[Suthorn Ruins]] at the start of the game, this was the form the Bow of Might took.
