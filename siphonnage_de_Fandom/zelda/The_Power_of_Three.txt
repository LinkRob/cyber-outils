{{Term/Store|The Power of Three||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Power of Three|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|The Power of Three}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon|link}} as its pilots. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, and 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

The {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat 5,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within seven minutes. The different {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Water Octorok|link}} and many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} scattered around {{Term|HWAoC|Cephla Lake|link}}. A large group of {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} also spawns at the bottom of the map when Vah Ruta walks to a certain point. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 5,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
