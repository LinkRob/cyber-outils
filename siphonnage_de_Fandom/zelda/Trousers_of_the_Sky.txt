The {{Term/Store|Trousers of the Sky||Series, BotW}} are an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trousers of the Sky}}<br/>Legends say these trousers were once worn by a hero who appeared from the sky riding a mighty bird. It was the official garb of a certain order of knights.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
It can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the Skyward Sword Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Trousers of the Sky. They can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Trousers of the Sky, alongside the [[Cap of the Sky]] and [[Tunic of the Sky]], comprise the [[Hero of the Sky Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from [[Skyward Sword]]. When it has been enhaced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
