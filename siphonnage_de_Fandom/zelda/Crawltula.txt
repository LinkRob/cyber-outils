'''Crawltula''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Crawltula|A spidery monster that does not spin webs but does walk on walls and ceilings as easily as flat ground.<ref name="Crawltula">{{Cite|A spidery monster that does not spin webs but does walk on walls and ceilings as easily as flat ground.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Crawltulas are [[spider]]-like creatures that can be found crawling on walls and ceilings throughout [[Hyrule]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn the Crawltula [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
