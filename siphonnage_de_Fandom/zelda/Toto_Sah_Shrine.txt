The {{Term/Store|Toto Sah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Toto Sah Shrine}}<br/>Toto Sah Apparatus}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Toto Sah Apparatus''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Toto Sah Shrine}} is located south of the {{Term|BotW|Hickaly Woods|link}}, behind a bombable wall along the river. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Toto Sah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Toto Sah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Toto Sah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Toto Sah Shrine}} consists of several {{Plural|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle|link}}, in which platforms must be rotated to create pathways to the exit. The first test {{Term|BotW|Link}} comes across is a simple rectangular platform. The second features a suspended stairway, in which the center section slides along a rail and must be aligned with the others to create a path. The third {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}} features a complex floating structure that holds a sliding stairway leading to a locked room with the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. This structure must be oriented as a pathway. There is also a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} on the structure containing a {{Term|BotW|Small Key|link}}, which is needed to open the door blocking the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

Upon completion of the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Toto Sah}} will present {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for his resourcefulness.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Toto Sah|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fades away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Toto Sah|BotW}}</ref>

The platform connected to the first {{Term|BotW|Motion Control Puzzle}} can be flipped upside down to reveal a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}. Inside this {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} is a {{Term|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye|link}}.
