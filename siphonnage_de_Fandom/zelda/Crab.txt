{{Term/Store|Crab|Crabs|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring creatures in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Crab|Figurine|TWWHD}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Crab}} are small, helpless creatures that are found near the {{Term|TWWHD|Water|link}} on islands across the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}}, usually on {{Term|TWWHD|Sand|link}} but sometimes in {{Term|TWWHD|Grass|link}}. They are the smallest creatures found in {{TWW|-}}, and are completely harmless. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} gets too close to one, it will scatter and disappear into the ground, then resurface once {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} is no longer nearby. {{Plural|TWWHD|Crab}} are found throughout the beaches of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea}}, but are very common on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}}.

{{Figurine|TWW|Crab}}

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, three varieties of {{Plural|BotW|Crab}} appear throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. These types are the {{Term|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. They are commonly found throughout {{Term|BotW|Necluda|link}}, but can be found on mostly all beaches and rivers in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}. They can be used in {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} to grant different effects to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} depending on the {{Term|BotW|Crab}} used in the dish. {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}} will raise defense when cooked in a meal, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}} will raise attack when cooked in a meal, and {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}} will restore {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} when cooked in a meal. {{Plural|BotW|Crab}} can also be eaten raw to restore one of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link|display= Hearts}}.
