{{Term/Store|Coliseum: Intermediate||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 40 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Coliseum: Intermediate|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Coliseum: Intermediate}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given five minutes to defeat five {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalmoblin|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox}}. Only two {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalmoblin}} spawn at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, although another spawns after one of them is killed. When the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} kills a second one, two more {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalmoblin}} spawn, and after one more is killed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Stalnox}} spawns. When all six of those {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Radish|link}}.
