The {{Term/Store|Snowfield Stable||Series, BotW}} is a [[Stable]] in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.{{Fact}}

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable}} lies in the middle of the road between the [[Great Hyrule Forest]] and the [[Tabantha Frontier]] that runs through the {{Term|BotW|S. Tabantha Snowfield|link}} in the [[Tabantha Tundra]]. In a small crater to the west is the [[Rin Oyaa Shrine]], which makes a convenient [[Travel Gate]] to the Stable when activated. The Snowfield Stable contains every amenity characteristic to Stables, including [[Horse]] services, lodging, and a [[Cooking Pot]], owned by [[Danton]],<ref>{{Cite|If you plan to roam the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra Mountains}}, you should make some food to help you withstand the extreme cold. Feel free to use my cooking pot outside. Going to a snowy mountain unprepared is unacceptable for a true adventurer!|Danton|BotW}}</ref> for [[Cooking]]. There are three [[Hylian Retriever]]s at the {{Term|BotW|Snowfield Stable|display=Stable}}. Two are black and white and stay near to the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} and in front of {{Term|BotW|Varke|link}}. The third is bluish gray and stays next to the boarded {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}. Two of the Retrievers will lead {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to hidden treasure if Link befriends them by feeding them three pieces of meat or fruit when they are happy. The black and white Retriever near the Cooking Pot will lead Link to a metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} southwest of the Stable containing a [[Star Fragment]]. The bluish gray Retriever next to the boarded Horses will head north of the Stable to a Chest with a [[Feathered Spear]]. A poster showing a [[Recipe]] for [[Spicy Mushroom Rice Balls]] hangs on the northern wall inside the Stable.

The Stable is run by {{Term|BotW|Varke}}, who mans both its Horse services and lodging. As with all other Stables, [[Beedle]] can occasionally be found here selling his wares. Two other merchants, {{Term|BotW|Kanny|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Yammo|link}} can also occasionally stop by one of the Campfires to sell their wares.

{{Term|BotW|Juannelle|link}} can be found at the back of the Stable. She traveled to the north looking for a {{Term|BotW|Stalhorse|link}} but can't cope with the freezing [[Temperature]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I've traveled to the east and the west in my great hunt for information, and now I find myself here in the freezing north... I heard there was a {{Color|BotW Red|Stalhorse}} in this area, but I can't take it any longer! It's too cold to look anymore!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> If Link agrees to find it for her, the "[[Stalhorse: Pictured!]]" [[Side Quest]] will begin.<ref>{{Cite|[A Stalhorse?] {{Big|Beep-beep!}} You just set off Juannelle's intuition sensors! You're interested in the {{Color|BotW Red|Stalhorse}} too, huh? I just wanted to see this creepy {{Color|BotW Red|Stalhorse monster}} once with my own eyes! If it really does exist, it'd be such a big scoop! But... Well, I can handle all kinds of dangers and hazards, but this cold... Brrr, I can't stand it! Can you handle this task for me? I'll even reward you! All I need is a picture! Or some notes!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I can do it.] Good answer! There's a rumor it can be found with {{Color|BotW Blue|Stalkoblins}} in the {{Color|BotW Blue|North Tabantha Snowfield}}. Some have reported it aaaaall the way on the other side of the snow field to the east, right in front of those bizarre {{Color|BotW Blue|ruins}}. Good luck! I'm really counting on you!|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref> Upon retrieving a picture of a Stalhorse and showing it to her, she will reward Link with a [[Silver Rupee]], valued at 100 [[Rupee]]s. {{Term|BotW|Juannelle}} will also give information on some [[Thyphlo Ruins|pitch-black ruins]] and the far edge of the [[Hebra Mountains]].<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me about the ruins.] Light is unable to enter these ruins for some reason. It's said that you can't see an inch ahead, even during the day! There must be some mysterious power behind this phenomenon, don't you think? I can only wonder at what's hiding within those ruins... The bored retiree, [[Monkton]], will wander around inside the Stable, telling stories to anyone who will listen.<ref>{{Cite|A traveler, eh? Can you spare a minute to listen to a bored, old retiree tell a story?|Monkton|BotW}}</ref>

When the [[Weather]] is clear, [[Danton]] and [[Harlow]] can found next to Danton's Cooking Pot. Danton is obsessed with the [[Sword that Seals the Darkness]] while Harlow believes there's more to being a hero than the sword you wield.<ref>{{Cite|It's every dreamer's dearest dream, isn't it? Having a sword that shoots light all like WHAM and mows down all kinds of monsters like KAPOW would be amazing! I mean, even the name—{{Color|BotW Red|the sword that seals the darkness}}—is just the coolest thing ever!|Danton|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ugh, why do some people equate the unnecessary pursuit of power with some childish dream of adventure? That sword may be good for slaying monsters, sure, but it's not like a sword alone makes a hero. Ya know?|Harlow|BotW}}</ref>

Occasionally, travelers will stop to rest at the Stable. The [[Shield Surfing|Shield Surfer]] {{Term|BotW|Baumar|link}} will dismount from his Horse, [[Bamboo]], and stand by one of the Campfires on his way to the Hebra Mountains. The search for the [[Dragon's Road]] brings [[Nazbi]] and his Horse, [[Dauntless]], to the Stable.<ref>{{Cite|My name is Nazbi, treasure *clack-clack* hunter and *chatter-chatter* adventurer extraordinaire! This cold... *chatter-clack* I can't stop my *clack-clack* teeth from chattering... My search for the {{Color|BotW Red|dragon's road}} has frozen over. *chatter-clack* All I can do is wait out the cold right here. Heigh-ho, Dauntless, my *clack-clack* cold-yet-noble steed! *clack-chatter*|Nazbi|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Nazbi}} will dismount from Dauntless and wait by a Campfire before heading back towards [[Serenne Stable]] and eventually [[Tabantha Bridge Stable]] to resume his search. {{Term|BotW|Falmark|link}}, an older Shield Surfer, may rest at a table inside the Stable during his journey to the Mountains. {{Term|BotW|Ronn|link}} will travel to the Stable, searching for a Dragon.<ref>{{Cite|It's said that in order for me to come face to face with a real, live dragon, I need the blessing of the Goddess Hylia.|Ronn|BotW}}</ref> He will dismount from his Horse and rest at a Campfire before continuing his search and heading back to [[Serenne Stable]].
