'''Blue Iron Knuckles''' are a color variant of [[Iron Knuckle]] and [[enemy|enemies]] in {{TAoL}}. They throw knives and try to hit at Link but also are skilled in blocking attacks. Link fights a Blue Iron Knuckle in different palaces, including the [[Island Palace]], the [[Maze Island Palace]], the [[Palace on the Sea]], and the [[Three-Eye Rock Palace]]. Blue Iron Knuckles are usually guarding entrances or other types of things such as doors and keys.
