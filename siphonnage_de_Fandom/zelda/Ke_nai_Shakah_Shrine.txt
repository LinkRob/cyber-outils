The {{Term/Store|Ke'nai Shakah Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ke'nai Shakah Shrine}}<br/>A Modest Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Modest Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Ke'nai Shakah Shrine}} is hidden in a cave behind cracked rocks in {{Term|BotW|Ulria Grotto|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. These rocks can be destroyed by {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=Paragliding}} into {{Term|BotW|Ulria Grotto}} from above, then shooting them with {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} while in midair. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ke'nai Shakah|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ke'nai Shakah. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Ke'nai Shakah|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Ke'nai Shakah Shrine}} features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}} wielding an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe+|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword+|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge him. In order to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.

Once the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III}} has been defeated, the portcullis in the back of the room will rise, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} access to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. When he interacts with it, {{Term|BotW|Ke'nai Shakah}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ke'nai Shakah|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ke'nai Shakah|BotW}}</ref>

In addition to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} can be found behind the portcullis. This {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}}.
