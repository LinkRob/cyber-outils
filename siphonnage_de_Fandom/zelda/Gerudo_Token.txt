The {{Term/Store|Gerudo Token||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>{{Note|In all versions of {{OoT|-}} prior to {{OoT3D}}, the Gerudo Token was known as the '''Gerudo's Membership Card''' when obtained and the '''Gerudo's Card''' when viewed in the inventory.<ref>{{Cite|You obtained the {{Color|OoT Red|Gerudo's Membership Card}}!<br/>You can get into the Gerudo's training ground in their hideout.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> This was retconned to "Gerudo Token" in {{OoT3D|-}}.}}

==Location and Uses==
The Gerudo Token can be obtained from the [[Gerudo Warrior]] after sneaking past all the [[Gerudo]] guards and freeing the four [[Carpenters]]. Though the Gerudo do not usually grant membership to men,<ref>{{Cite|We were really interested in joining their all-female group, but they locked us up like this just because we're men!|Ichiro|OoT}}</ref> [[Link]]'s skill earns their respect, admitting him into their group.<ref>{{Cite|I've seen your fine work. To get past the guards here, you must have good thieving skills. I used to think that all men, besides the great Ganondorf, were useless...but now that I've seen you, I don't think so anymore! [...] Say, you must want to become one of us, eh? All right, then! You're in from now on! Take this. With it, you will have free access to all areas of the fortress!|Gerudo Warrior|OoT}}</ref>

The Gerudo Token allows Link to walk freely around [[Gerudo's Fortress]] without being thrown into prison.<ref>{{Cite|From now on, you're free to come and go as you please through the gate to the desert.|Gerudo Warrior|OoT}}</ref> It also grants him access to the [[Haunted Wasteland]], which leads to the [[Spirit Temple]]. Additionally, it enables Link to participate in the [[Horseback Archery Range]] and the [[Gerudo's Training Ground]].

==Trivia==
*The star and moon symbol in the membership card was replaced with the [[Crest of the Gerudo]] in later versions of the game.
