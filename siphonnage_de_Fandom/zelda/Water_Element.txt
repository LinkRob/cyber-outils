The {{Term/Store|Water Element||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Water Element}} is the third of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}} obtained by {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, found within the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. After defeating the {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|display=Element}} and returns to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} to infuse it into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}}, turning it into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Three Elements)|link}} and allowing him to split himself into three rather than two.
