{{Term/Store|Blue Moblin|Blue Moblins|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Blue Moblin}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TLoZ, TAoL, OoS, OoA, TWWHD, TotK}}

==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
Blue Moblins first appear in [[The Legend of Zelda]], being seen in forest areas like the [[Lost Woods]]. They act the same as Moblins, but can withstand more hits.

==={{TAoL|-}}===
Blue Moblins later reappear in [[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link]], being the strongest Moblin in the game. Like the other Moblins in {{TAoL|-}}, they attack by throwing their spears or jabbing Link with them, while also draining his experience points if they land a hit on him.

==={{OoA|-}}===

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Blue Moblin}} are first found as a {{Term|TWWHD|Miniboss|link}} in {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Cavern|link}}, then appearing as a common {{Term|TWWHD|Enemy|link}} afterwards. They are fought the same way as regular {{Plural|TWWHD|Moblin|link}}, but do not wield {{Plural|TWWHD|Lantern|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Blue Moblin}}
Reappearing for the first time since ''The'' ''Wind Waker'', Blue Moblins are found all over [[Hyrule]] and are now tall and heavy, similar to the normal Moblins.<ref>{{Cite|These heavyweight monsters can be found all over Hyrule.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They are tougher variants of [[Moblin]]s and often have stronger Moblin {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, though they are now outclassed by [[Black Moblin]]s and [[Silver Moblin]]s.<ref>{{Cite|They're much tougher than their standard counterparts and often have much stronger weapons equipped.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When defeated, they drop [[Moblin Horn]]s, [[Moblin Fang]]s, and [[Moblin Guts]].

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Stub|TotK}}
{{Plural|TotK|Blue Moblin}} reappear in {{TotK|-}}. While they look and act the same, they have [[Blue Moblin Horn|Blue Moblin Horns]] on their heads.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Blue Moblins reappear in {{HWAoC}}, first seen in [[The Yiga Clan Attacks!]]. Like Moblins in this game, they attack with either their fists or weapons like [[Moblin Spear]]s, [[Moblin Club]]s, and any metal weapons, which can be countered with [[Stasis]], [[Cryonis]], and the [[Magnesis]] Shiekah Runes, thus exposing their [[Weak Point Gauge]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
