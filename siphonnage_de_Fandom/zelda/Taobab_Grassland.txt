The {{Term/Store|Taobab Grassland||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Taobab Grassland'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland}} is a savanna located in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. It sits south of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and north of the [[Oseira Plains]]. The {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland}} boasts several {{Wp|Adansonia|baobab}} {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, which are unique to the valley containing the {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland}}, Oseira Plains, and the [[Nautelle Wetlands]]. The {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland}} is the only location that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey there! It's not every day that I see someone all the way out here! We're doing an ecological study on the wild animals in the area. We'd heard that there was a {{Color|BotW Red|giant horse}} living in this region. Apparently it can't be found anywhere else... That was all we needed to hear! We headed this way to investigate right away!|Straia|BotW}}</ref> which is necessary for completing the "[[Hunt for the Giant Horse]]" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Straia|link}} at the [[Mounted Archery Camp]], he will direct {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland}} and ask for its capture and retrieval for study.<ref>{{Cite|[Leave it to me.] Ah, you're a lifesaver! They say that the {{Color|BotW Red|giant horse}} lives around the {{Color|BotW Blue|Taobab Grassland}}. That'd be northwest of here.|Straia|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found by destroying a clay {{Term|BotW|Pot|link}} dangling from a chain on a {{Term|BotW|Tree}}'s branch. A second {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found by popping the [[Balloon]] at the top of a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} in the southern reaches of the {{Term|BotW|Taobab Grassland|display=Grassland}}.
