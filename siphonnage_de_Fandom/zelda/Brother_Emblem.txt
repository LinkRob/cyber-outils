The {{Term/Store|Brother Emblem||Series, OoA}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> also referred to as the '''Brotherhood Emblem''',<ref name="Obtention">{{Cite|You got the Brotherhood Emblem! Now you are a Goron, too!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> is a quest item that appears {{OoA}}.

==Location and Uses==
The Brother Emblem is given to [[Link]] by the [[Graceful Goron]] after getting a high score on the [[Goron Dance]] in either the present or the past.<ref>{{Cite|If you dance well, I'll give you a Brotherhoood Emblem!|Graceful Goron|OoA}}</ref> Receiving the Brother Emblem makes Link an honorary Goron,<ref name="Obtention"/> allowing him to bypass the Goron guarding the way to [[Rolling Ridge]].<ref>{{Cite|Huh?!? That's a Brotherhood Emblem! You understand the passion of a Goron's heart! You may pass!|Goron|OoA}}</ref>
