The {{Term/Store|Crown Key||Series, OoA}} is an item in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Crown Key is needed to open the [[Crown Dungeon]]. It is given to [[Link]] by the [[Goron Elder]] after Link frees him from a gigantic [[Boulder]],<ref>{{Cite|Did you bring us the Bomb Flower? Sorry to trouble you. This is the shiny key I found on Northern Peak. I never found the keyhole, so I'll give this to you.|Goron Elder|OoA}}</ref> using the [[Bomb Flower]] he earned after defeating the [[Great Moblin]].<ref>{{Cite|The Elder is buried alive! But Gorons alone can't dig him out! If we had the legendary Bomb Flower, we could blast the rocks away...|Goron|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't know who you are or where you're from, but thank you for getting rid of the Great Moblin. ... Now we can grow Bomb Flowers without any worries! Take one as a sign of our thanks.|Goron|OoA}}</ref>
