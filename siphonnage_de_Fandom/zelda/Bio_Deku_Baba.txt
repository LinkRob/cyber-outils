==Characteristics==
==={{MM|-}}===
{{Tatl
|quote1= {{Color|MM White|Bio Deku Baba}}...<br/> Just calm down and aim carefully! And don't blame me if one hit doesn't get rid of it!
}}
[[File:MM BioDekuBaba.png|80px|left]]
Bio Deku Babas are similar to their cousins, [[Deku Baba]]s, but instead of growing from the ground, they live underneath [[Lily Pad]]s. They can be found in the [[Great Bay Temple]] and in [[Secret Grotto]]es. When cut off from their lily pads, they will crawl around at the bottom of the pool they live in. They will stay there and wait for [[Link]] at the bottom, but if he is there, they will immediately attack. They can be defeated by the use of projectiles such as [[Zora Link]]'s Double Cutters. If Link attempts to stand on the lily pads, the Bio Deku Baba will flip it and damage him. If the Bio Deku Babas' heads are hit instead of the vine, they will die instantly instead of falling to the bottom.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Bio Deku Baba|Capable of swallowing whole anything that approaches it, this fearsome plant is vulnerable to having its head pulled.<ref name="EoW Bio Deku Baba">{{Cite|Capable of swallowing whole anything that approaches it, this fearsome plant is vulnerable to having its head pulled.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Bio Deku Babas are a variant of Deku Baba capable of swallowing [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] whole. After defeating one, Zelda can learn the Bio Deku Baba [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which has an Echo cost of three.
