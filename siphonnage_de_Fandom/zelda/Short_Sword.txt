==Location and Uses==
Short Swords are {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} exclusive to {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, acting as his starting weapon. They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}}, dealing only 1 damage to a single enemy in front of the user and can be either found or upgraded with various materials. One Short Sword can always be found at {{Term|CoH|Link's House|link}} being guarded by an {{Term|CoH|Octorok|link}}, regardless if {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} wakes up Link or {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}} first.
