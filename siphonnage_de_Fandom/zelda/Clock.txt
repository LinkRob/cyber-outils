==Appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Magical clock|The enemy stops moving when Link has this. Mind you, it works only for the screen he's on at the moment.}}
Clocks are items dropped by enemies in {{TLoZ}}. If [[Link]] picks up a Clock, the young hero will begin flashing and all enemies currently on the screen will immediately freeze in place indefinitely, vulnerable to Link's attacks.<ref name="E 120"/> If Link leaves the screen he is on, the effect will end. Even if Link touches the frozen enemies, he cannot be harmed by them while a Clock is in effect.<ref name="E 120"/>

Clocks can be dropped by blue [[Octorok]]s, blue [[Moblin]]s, red [[Goriya]]s, [[Gibdo]]s, [[Vire]]s, red [[Darknut]]s, red [[Wizzrobe]]s, blue [[Lynel]]s, {{Plural|TLoZ|Blue Tektite|link}}, red [[Leever]]s, [[Stalfos]], [[Zol]]s, [[Ghini]]s, [[Wallmaster]]s, [[Pols Voice]]s, blue [[Lanmola]]s, and [[Rope]]s.

==Other Appearances==
==={{TSP|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:Magical Clock TSP.png|left]]
In {{TSP}}, the Clock makes another appearance, serving the same function as in {{TLoZ|-}}. By moving the small knob left of the numeral XII, the clock will halt time for anyone except its bearer, but as soon as both hand knobs reach XII, time will go back to its normal pace.

The Clock is first introduced in the book when [[Impa]] gives Link the clock after the young hero decides to wait and not confront [[Charles|Charles of Moria]]. The Clock is not used in the book unless Link accuses Charles of being evil without having any [[Mirror of Truth|proof]] to back it up, in which case he will be sent to a jail cell in the [[Tower]]. He uses the clock to halt time and proceeds to utilize a toothpick to push the cell key out the keyhole; afterward, the young hero slides a paper under the door to drag the key back in, which he uses to unlock the door just as the clock is about to return time to its normal pace. Link then quickly heads out the door.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The 8-bit Clock appears as a Weapon in {{HW}} and {{HW|DE}}, as a [[Zora Scale]] for [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]]. In {{HW|-}}, it is a Lv.★ Weapon, with identical stats to the Lv.3 Water Dragon Scale, unlocked from the Termina Map. In {{HW|DE|-}}, it is not a separate Weapon but a replacement for the Lv.4+ Sun Dragon Scale when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu; the Sun Dragon Scale is unlocked from the Koholint Island Map.

== Trivia ==

* The original {{TLoZ}} remains the only installment where the Clock appears in the main series.
* The Clock in {{TLoZ|-}} is a parallel to the [[mariowiki:Super_Star|Super Star]] power-up from the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, as Link becomes invulnerable to enemies and his sprite flashes as long as it is in effect.
** The fellow flagship [[Nintendo]] series does however have a similar item of its own called the [[mariowiki:Stop_Watch|Stop Watch]], where enemies would temporarily freeze in place.
