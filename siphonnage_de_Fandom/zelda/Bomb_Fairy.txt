{{Term/Store|Bomb Fairy|Bomb Fairies|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Bomb Fairy|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Bomb Fairy}} appear in dungeons, where they will fly towards {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. If struck by a {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}}, they will explode on contact. However, if they are allowed to approach the heroes without being struck, {{Plural|CoH|Bomb Fairy}} will heal them for half a {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}}. {{Plural|CoH|Bomb Fairy}} can also be caught in {{Plural|CoH|Bottle|link}} and become temporary allies. When released, they will fly in a straight line and explode upon making contact with something.

{{Plural|CoH|Bomb Fairy}} are based on Pixies from ''[[wikipedia:Crypt of the NecroDancer|Crypt of the NecroDancer]]''.
