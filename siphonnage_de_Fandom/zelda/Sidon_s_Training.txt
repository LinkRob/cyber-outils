{{Term/Store|Sidon's Training||HWAoC}} is a level 41 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sidon's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Sidon's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Tireless Frog|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} at first, although many {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Lizalfos|link}} spawn when {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches 100 KOs. When he reaches 200 KOs, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos|link}} spawn, along with an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches 300 KOs, an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} spawns in the southeastern corner of the map and advances toward {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Moblin}} is defeated.
