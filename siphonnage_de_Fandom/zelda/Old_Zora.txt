The {{Term/Store|Old Zora||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref> He is the final step in the trading sequence in order to get the [[Noble Sword]]. He lives in a cave near [[Coast of No Return]] in the past.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 118}}</ref> He mentions to [[Link]] that he misses the smell and sound of the sea, and that he used to be the best Zora musician back in his younger days.<ref>{{Cite|How I miss the smell and sound of the sea... Back in my day, I was the best Zora musician. If I had an instrument, I could relive my youth.|Old Zora|OoA}}</ref> Upon hearing this, Link gives him the [[Sea Ukulele]], and in return the Old Zora thanks him with the [[Broken Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|Well I give you something old... This is the Hero's Sword that a great hero gave my ancestors long ago!|Old Zora|OoA}}</ref> After being told the sword is broken, the old Zora will hurriedly change the subject to how pleasant the Ukulele sounds.<ref>{{Cite|...What's that? It's broken..? ...Ah-Ahem! Ahh! Umm... What a great sound!|Old Zora|OoA}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The Old Zora mentions that he is capable of fixing anything, but whether he actually can do this is never confirmed.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! What fun! I don't even want to think about any Broken Swords, you know? ...No!!! I mean, I have the power to fix anything, so I have to do it.|Old Zora|OoA}}</ref>
