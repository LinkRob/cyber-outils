{{Term/Store|Salt-Grilled Mushrooms|Salt-Grilled Mushrooms|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Salt-Grilled Mushrooms are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The default can be made either by using {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}, which offer no status effects, or by using two types of Mushrooms with different effects. Eating them will restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These meals are noted to be simple, their preparation only requiring one to lightly salt Mushrooms before grilling them.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on both the type of Mushroom added and the inclusion of other ingredients, the following variations of Salt-Grilled Mushrooms can be made:

* '''Hearty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, they will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}} or other energizing ingredients. They can be eaten to refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}} or other enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel can be completely restored and overfilled by eaten these meals.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}} or other hasty ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}} or other spicy ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when they're eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}} or other electric ingredients. Link can eat them to receive one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}} or other mighty ingredients. By eating them, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}} or other tough ingredients. When Link eats them, he will gain a boost to his defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will be boosted if he eats these meals.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Salt-Grilled Mushrooms''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Salt-Grilled Mushrooms}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A basic mushroom dish made by lightly salting mushrooms and grilling them.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}} or other chilly ingredients. They can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
