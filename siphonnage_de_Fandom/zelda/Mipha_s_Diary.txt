{{Term/Store|''Mipha's Diary''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mipha's Diary|N/A|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Mipha's Diary}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} that was used by {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} to document her personal thoughts. It will appear in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, on a table in {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link|display=King Dorephan's}} {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}} after {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} begins "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}". The contents of the {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Diary|display=''Diary''}} tell of her affection towards {{Term|BotW|Link}} as well as her inauguration as {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} and pilot of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}} casually brings up {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Diary}} at first, using it to demonstrate how {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} fell in love with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|As {{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha's Diary}} plainly states...it was not long before she developed strong feelings for you.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> When asked about it, he explains that the {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Diary|display=''Diary''}} was just recently found, which brought him joy.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, of course. {{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha's Diary}} was found just recently. Its discovery brought me much joy. It is on {{Color|BotW Blue|that table over there}}.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> He allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to read it, claiming that he is sure {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} would forgive him if he did.<ref>{{Cite|I do not mind if you choose to read it. I am sure {{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha}} would forgive you anything.|King Dorephan|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses to face {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon|link}} again, {{Term|BotW|Mipha}} may yell at {{Term|BotW|Link}}, warning him against doing so.<ref>{{Cite|Link. If you happen to, uh... That is to say, if you should find my diary somewhere... {{Big|DON'T YOU DARE READ IT!}}|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> She is surprised by her sudden outburst, but still hopes {{Term|BotW|Link}} will not read her {{Term|BotW|Mipha's Diary|display=''Diary''}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! I did not mean to raise my voice. But I do hope you will respect my wish...|Mipha|BotW}}</ref>
