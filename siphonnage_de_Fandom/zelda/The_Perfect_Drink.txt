"{{Term/Store|The Perfect Drink||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Perfect Drink}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|East Barrens}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
On an outcropping in the {{Term|BotW|East Barrens|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Pokki|link}} collapsed on the terminal outside of {{Term|BotW|Misae Suma Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'd just found the shrine too...|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> She is dehydrated and unable to move.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh, my throat... It's so dry... I can't move a muscle...|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> Believing that this is the end of her life, {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} wishes she could have one last {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If this really is the end... I wish I could have one last taste... One sip... One sip of an ice-cold {{Color|BotW Red|Noble Pursuit}}...|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> With this wish, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

At {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Furosa|link}} for a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Noble Pursuit?] You want to try our signature drink, {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Since she is out of {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}}, though, she is unable to make one.<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, I need a huge amount of ice to make that drink, and I'm fresh out right now.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Due to how strong the drink is, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} refuses to sell one to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, it's a really strong drink. Even if I could make it, I wouldn't sell it to a little vai like you.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs her that the drink is actually for {{Term|BotW|Pokki}},<ref>{{Cite|[Actually...] What'd you say? Pokki's collapsed?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> While she wishes she could help, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} is unable to make a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}} for {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} without {{Term|BotW|Ice}},<ref>{{Cite|Hmm. I want to help her out, but I can't make a you-know-what without ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> and she is unable to retrieve any from the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's ice at the {{Color|BotW Red|icehouse}}, but my legs aren't in the best shape for hauling ice...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} on {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}'s behalf.<ref>{{Cite|[Should I get it for you?] Are you serious? You'll get it? That'd be a huge help.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} is grateful for this, as {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} is her best customer.<ref>{{Cite|Pokki is my best customer, after all. I wish I could get the ice myself.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}} is north of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}},<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, the {{Color|BotW Red|icehouse}} is north of town.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> and if he explains the situation to {{Term|BotW|Anche|link}}, she will allow him to take some.<ref>{{Cite|Anche manages it, but I think if you explain the situation, she'll let you have some ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} goes to bed early, so {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to visit during the day.<ref>{{Cite|You should know that she heads to bed quite early. If you plan to go, you'll need to get there while it's bright out.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} decides it would be too much to make {{Term|BotW|Link}} carry the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} all the way to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh, young lady... Carrying ice all the way through the desert and then all the way to town? I can't let that stand?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> so she plans on taking the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} from him at the ruins entrance.<ref>{{Cite|If you bring the ice to the {{Color|BotW Red|entrance to the ruins}}—between town and the icehouse—I can take it from there.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} goes to the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will allow him to retrieve {{Term|BotW|Ice}} for {{Term|BotW|Furosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Actually...] I see... Well, if Furosa wants ice, then you should bring her some. You're permitted to take some ice.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> She reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he moves too slowly, the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} will melt into {{Term|BotW|Water|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} has no use for.<ref>{{Cite|You've taken the heat into account, right? If you move too slowly, the ice will surely melt. Furosa has no use for water...|Anche|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will then be given a block of {{Term|BotW|Ice}}, which he must carry through the ruins. Keeping the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} in the shadows will prevent any melting. If it does melt, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will be disappointed,<ref>{{Cite|...You've gotta be kidding me.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> though she will comfort {{Term|BotW|Link}} and allow him to take another block.<ref>{{Cite|Even the Gerudo make mistakes transporting ice, sometimes. Just grab another block and try again.|Anche|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will be waiting for {{Term|BotW|Link}} at the end of the ruins.<ref>{{Cite|Over here...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} is not wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} will notice he is a man, but she does not care.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? You're...<br/>Well, whatever... I don't care who you are, as long as I get my ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>}} She is grateful that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to bring her the {{Term|BotW|Ice}} in such good condition.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa. No doubt about it, that's definitely ice {{Color|BotW Red|from the icehouse}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Looks like the ice is in good shape. Sarqso.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> With it, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} believes she can make the best {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}} ever.<ref>{{Cite|I'll be able to make a {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}} with this. It may be the best I've ever made...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With this, I'll be able to make the ultimate {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to inform {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} that it is waiting for her.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but could you please let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Tell her that I made the best one ever and I've got it waiting for her...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I've got one more favor to ask. Could you go and let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Just tell her the best drink ever is waiting for her...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} hears this, {{Term|BotW|Furosa}} thinks she will come running.<ref>{{Cite|Knowing her, she'll probably come running when she hears that there's an amazing drink with her name on it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What? No, of course this will work. Knowing her, if she hears a delicious drink is waiting, she'll come running.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Misae Suma Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} is convinced that this is where she will die.<ref>{{Cite|I can't take it anymore... I'm going to rot here...|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> Hearing about the best {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit}} ever,<ref>{{Cite|[Actually...] Huh?|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pokki}} will run back to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you say "best {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}} ever"? Pfft, I am GONE!|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to access {{Term|BotW|Misae Suma Shrine}}.
