{{Plural|BotW|Phrenic Bow}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} passed down through the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} tribe.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref> By concentrating before pulling the string, the user can enter a first person view that allows them to shoot distant targets as easily as those nearby.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Phrenic Bow}} are most commonly found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in and around {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. There is also a {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} that uses a {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow}} at the skull rock base north of {{Term|BotW|Sahasra Slope|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}}. Finally, they have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} that spawn when the {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Sheik|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} archers, such as {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}}'s bodyguard {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}}, utilize {{Plural|BotW|Phrenic Bow}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. They have a wooden construction, and thus they will burn if exposed to a source of {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. As a result of this, however, they do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they will float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They also cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.

{{Term|BotW|Cado}} is equipped with a {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can take a picture of with the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to add its entry to the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}}. Additionally, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find two more mounted inside of {{Term|BotW|Cado}}'s {{Term|BotW|House|link}}, though their model is part of the environment and the {{Term|BotW|Camera}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} does not register them.

==Trivia==
*Due to a glitch, {{Term|BotW|Cado}}'s {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow}} may become unequipped when he is not on guard duty. However, it can be easily fixed by sitting at a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} or sleeping at the {{Term|BotW|Shuteye Inn|link}}.
