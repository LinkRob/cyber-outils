'''''The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds'' Original Soundtrack''' is the original soundtrack for {{ALBW}}. It was available on Club Nintendo in Japan in 2014 and in Europe in 2015.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Reece Heather|published= September 11, 2014|retrieved= May 3, 2015|url= http://www.zeldauniverse.net/2014/09/11/a-link-between-worlds-official-soundtrack-now-available-on-japanese-club-nintendo/|title= A Link Between Worlds official soundtrack now available on Japanese Club Nintendo|site= Zelda Universe|type=}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Morgan Lewis|published= January 26, 2015|retrieved= May 3, 2015|url= http://www.zeldauniverse.net/2015/01/26/a-link-between-worlds-original-soundtrack-now-available-on-european-club-nintendo/|title= A Link Between Worlds soundtrack now available on European Club Nintendo|site= Zelda Universe|type=}}</ref> The soundtrack features 2 discs with 89 tracks plus 16 [[Milk Bar#A Link Between Worlds|Milk Bar]] musician pieces. Additionally, it includes an insert with liner notes from the composer, Ryo Nagamatsu, and 12 score excerpts.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Explore the music of The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds with this double-CD soundtrack, featuring 89 tracks, plus 16 Milk Bar Musician pieces. The insert includes liner notes from the game’s composer, Ryo Nagamatsu, and 12 score excerpts for you to perform at home.|author= |published= |retrieved= May 3, 2015|url= http://stars.nintendo-europe.com/products/the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-between-worlds--original-soundtrack-290|title= The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds – Original Soundtrack|site= Club Nintendo Europe|type=}}</ref>
