{{Term/Store|Blue Gel|Blue Gels|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Blue Gel|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Blue Gel}} are slime-like enemies that appear after a {{Term|CoH|Blue Zol|link}} is defeated. Unlike their {{Term|CoH|Zol|link}} parent, their movement is not restricted to a given set of tiles. Therefore, they will actively chase {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, but are slow as they can only move on every other music beat.
