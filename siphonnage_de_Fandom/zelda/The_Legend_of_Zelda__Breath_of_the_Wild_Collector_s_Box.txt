{{Term/Store|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of The Wild Collector's Box||Series, BotW}} is a merchandise bundle manufactured by [[CultureFly]] and licensed by [[Nintendo]].<ref name="CultureFly"/>

==Overview==
The {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of The Wild Collector's Box}} contains seven different items relating to {{BotW}}. The cover of the box features the logo of {{BotW|-}} along with artwork of the five {{Plural|BotW|Champion|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}. The sides of the box are also decorated with artwork of {{Term|BotW|Link}} shooting an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}. Inside, the box contains a messenger bag, a glass water bottle, a journal, a pen, four pins, a snack box, and a large sticker. The messenger bag sports the "Z" from of the logo for {{BotW|-}} on the front and an array of {{Plural|BotW|Royal Crest|link}} on the inside. The glass water bottle features the same "Z" from the messenger bag on its silicone covering. The journal's tag claims to contain 100 lined pages. However, this is incorrect, and the journal contains 120 lined pages, plus the front and back pages. A string for bookmarking a page appears at the top and a strap appears at the back for holding the book closed. The ballpoint pen features designs of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, and {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}. The four pins each feature one of the {{Plural|BotW|Champion}}, excluding {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Each pin features the official artwork of the {{Term|BotW|Champion}} it represents. The bento-style snack box is designed after the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} and has two sections inside with latches to close it. Finally, the large sticker includes again all of the {{Plural|BotW|Champion}} excluding {{Term|BotW|Link}} from their official artworks.
