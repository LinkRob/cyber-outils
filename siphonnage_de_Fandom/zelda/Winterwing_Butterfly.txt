==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Winterwing Butterfly}}
Winterwing Butterflies can be found flying in [[Hyrule Ridge]] and the [[Tabantha Frontier]]. In order to catch them, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must be sneaking, or else they will fly away. Alternatively, they can be purchased from [[Beedle]] at the [[Gerudo Canyon Stable]] and [[Tabantha Bridge Stable]]. When combined with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a [[Cooking Pot]], the resulting food will be a [[Chilly Elixir]].<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] as an ingredient to make Blue [[Dye]].
