{{Term/Store|Strata||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 107}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Strata}} is a member of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}, and can be found at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}. He first appears when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} visits the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} through a {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}} in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}}, and blocks the main entrance of the building. After reaching the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} via {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Strata}} can be found next to the {{Term|TMC|Portal (Object)|link}}, where he recognizes {{Term|TMC|Link}} and explains where they are.<ref>{{Cite|Ohh, if it isn't old {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! My quest to match Kinstones has taken me all the way up here. I'm not sure you've noticed yet, but we're on top of the clouds! The Wind Tribe lives here! They protect the path to the {{Color|TMC Green|Palace of Winds}}, where the {{Color|TMC Red|Wind Element}} lies!|Strata|TMC}}</ref>

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} does not {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato}} and save {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} before arriving at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} from the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}}, {{Term|TMC|Strata}} will not show recognition towards {{Term|TMC|Link}}; he will still explain where they are, albeit in a more subdued manner.<ref>{{Cite|In this tower dwell the people of the Wind Tribe. We left your surface world long ago to serve a greater duty. We now protect the gate to the {{Color|TMC Green|Palace of Winds}}, where the {{Color|TMC Red|Wind Element}} lies.|Strata|TMC}}</ref>
