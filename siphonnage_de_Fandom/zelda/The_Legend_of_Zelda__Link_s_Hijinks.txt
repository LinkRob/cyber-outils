'''''The Legend of Zelda: Link's Hijinks''''' is a gag [[manga]] series based on {{TLoZ|Series}}. The series was drawn and written by Seitaro Komatsu and was originally published and serialized in the Japanese magazine ''{{Wp|CoroCoro Comic|Bessatsu CoroCoro Comic Special}}'' (別冊コロコロコミックSpecial).<ref name= "English webcomic">{{Cite Book|quote= Tingle is happy to present the following gag comic based on The Legend of Zelda series. He is, he is! This is based on the original comic by Mr. Komatsu, which was published in a Japanese manga magazine called CoroCoro Comics Special. Please enjoy it! Kooloo-limpah!|book= LH|publisher= Play Nintendo|page= 1}}</ref>

==General Information==
On February 13, 2015, to coincide with the international release of {{MM3D}}, a webcomic based on {{MM|-}} was added to the Japanese Nintendo News site, consisting of two parts.<ref name= "Japanese webcomic"/> The second part was posted on February 25, 2015. An official English translation of the webcomic was posted on the Play Nintendo site on August 7, 2015.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Thanks to the hard work of many, Nintendo just published an original Legend of Zelda webcomic!|author= Alison Rapp|published= August 7, 2015|retrieved= August 9, 2015|url= https://twitter.com/mnemosynekurai/status/629732580875636737/photo/1|title= Alison Rapp on Twitter|site= Twitter}}</ref> The second part was posted later.

The {{MM|-}} webcomic follows the misadventures of [[Link]] and his efforts in avoiding his responsibility of saving [[Termina]]. The first part takes place just moments before the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]] crashes into the land. Link uses the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} to turn back time, but only so he can keep playing on his [[Nintendo 3DS]]. The second part has [[Tatl]] introducing Link to masks in an attempt to get him to stop, but this backfires when he plays with the masks and forgets all about the Moon. At the end of the second part you were able to print your favorite mask(s) you can't on play nintendo but thanks to someone they have been recovered here https://linksliltri4cezeldacollection.weebly.com/play-nintendo---printable-majoras-mask-3d-papercraft-2015.html.
