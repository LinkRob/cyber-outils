{{Term/Store|Plain Crepe|Plain Crepes|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Plain Crepe}}<br/>The simplicity of this dish lets the flavor of its ingredients shine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Plain Crepes can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Fresh Milk]], [[Cane Sugar]], [[Tabantha Wheat]], a [[Bird Egg]], and [[Goat Butter]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they will restore some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Because this dish is so simple, the flavor of the ingredients is able to shine.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Since Plain Crepes require five ingredients to make, no variations of them exist.
