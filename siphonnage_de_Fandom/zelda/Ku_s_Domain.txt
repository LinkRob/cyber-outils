{{Term/Store|Ku's Domain||Series, ALBW}} is a location in {{ALBW}}.

==Features and Overview==
Ku's Domain is a lake located in the northeast region of [[Lorule]], counterpart of [[Zora's Lake]] in [[Hyrule]]. It is located directly south of Lorule's [[Death Mountain]], whose runoff creates the huge waterfall that forms the northern border of Ku's Domain.

Upon entering Ku's Domain, [[Link]] will be attacked by a group of [[Ku]]s, who will either chase after Link on land, or attack him from the water by shooting fireballs. After defeating them all, a [[Rupees#Silver Rupee|Silver Rupee]] pops out of the water. Every subsequent time Link returns to Ku's Domain and defeat all Ku's, he will get a [[Rupees#Red Rupee|Red Rupee]] instead.

A lost [[Maiamai]] can also be found underwater to the west.
