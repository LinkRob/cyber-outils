{{Term/Store|Volcon||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Volcon|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Volcon}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron}} who stands outside of the {{Term|BotW|Rollin' Inn|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, where he directs travelers to the lodge.<ref>{{Cite|A traveler! Perfect! Hey, why don'tcha stay here and rest your weary head?|Volcon|BotW}}</ref> Volcon helps run the Inn alongside {{Term|BotW|Tray}},<ref>{{Cite|Stay at my inn, friend!|Volcon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|HEY! We've got a customer comin' through!|Volcon|BotW}}</ref> and gives Goron-style massages to its customers.<ref>{{Cite|But to make up for that, I can offer you a mostly pain-free massage session. You'll feel as loose as gravel afterward! Yep, I think you should definitely stay here, friend!|Volcon|BotW}}</ref> If asked, {{Term|BotW|Volcon}} explains the inn to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and describes it as a distinguished {{Term|BotW|Goron}} establishment with a lovely view of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, although blocked by {{Term|BotW|Bludo|link}}'s house.<ref>{{Cite|This is the distinguished Goron establishment, {{Color|BotW Blue|Rollin' Inn}}! See that! What a lovely view 
of {{Color|BotW Blue|Death Mountain}}! Er...that's what I'd like to say, anyway. But {{Color|BotW Blue|Boss}}'s house is kinda in the way.|Volcon|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, he offers to massage {{Term|BotW|Link}} as compensation and invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to stay at the inn.<ref>{{Cite|But to make up for that, I can offer you a mostly pain-free massage session. You'll feel as loose as gravel afterward!|Volcon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yep, I think you should definitely stay here, friend!|Volcon|BotW}}</ref>

After the {{Plural|BotW|Magma Bomb|link}} stop falling following the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}'s liberation, {{Term|BotW|Volcon}} becomes hopeful that droves of tourists will start coming to {{Term|BotW|Goron City}} and stay at the Inn.<ref>{{Cite|A traveler! Perfect! Hey, did ya notice that the {{Color|BotW Blue|magma bombs}} have stopped fallin' from the sky? And that means... Well, hordes of tourists should start rollin' in soon, which means business is set to explode! I'll be busy as a rollin' stone in no time! If you're itchin' for a massage without a reservation... now's your last chance! So how about it? Why don'tcha stay here and rest your weary head?|Volcon|BotW}}</ref>
