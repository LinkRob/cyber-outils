==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>I have recorded information on this creature.</p><p>His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Bucha}}. He is the elder of the herbivorous Kikwi race. He can hide objects in the structure on his back.</p>
}}

Bucha is the elder of the [[Kikwi]]s of [[Faron Woods#Skyward Sword|Faron Woods]]. He is extremely large, towering over his fellow Kikwis and [[Link]].<ref>{{Cite|Kweeeee-heee... I am {{Color|SS Blue|Bucha}}, the Kikwi Elder. I saw the way you spotted me through my ingenious camouflage. Kweee, you are clearly a master woodsman.|Bucha|SS}}</ref>

When he and Link first meet, he is asked if he had seen Link's missing friend [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], to which Bucha recalls having been with her.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, the girl creature with the blond hair. She looked like one of your kind. Yes, she was with me, kweeee.|Bucha|SS}}</ref> However, Bucha is more concerned about his missing Kikwis, who all went into hiding after a large number of [[Bokoblin]]s started to appear throughout the Woods.<ref>{{Cite|But now is not the time for such talk... The woods are full of monsters. I am worried about my missing tribe. Kweeee... The shock and worry has made my memories of the girl quite hazy...|Bucha|SS}}</ref> He asks Link to help him find his Kikwis and to come to back to him after finding them all, at which point he might remember Zelda's whereabouts.<ref>{{Cite|I am worried sick for my fellow Kikwis. If you could calm my fears, I might be able to remember more about the girl you seek went of to, kwee.|Bucha|SS}}</ref> After finding all of the Kikwis, Link returns to Bucha, who is extremely grateful for Link's assistance. He remembers that Zelda had headed to the [[Skyview Temple]] in the [[Deep Woods]].<ref>{{Cite|Kwee hee hee, I feel much less worried now! I believe I just remembered where that young lady went! I think she headed down that way toward the temple, kwee...but be sure to take great care if you follow her. That area is crawling with monsters.|Bucha|SS}}</ref> He also gives Link his Kikwi heirloom, the [[Slingshot]], as a reward for helping them, saying it will help him get to the Temple without a hassle.<ref>{{Cite|As a reward for finding all my fellow Kikwis, I will give you a precious Kikwi heirloom.|Bucha|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|With a {{Color|SS Gold|slingshot}}, a clever young man like you should be able to find a way to the temple.|Bucha|SS}}</ref>

After going through the Skyview Temple, Link can find Bucha and all of the other Kikwis hiding in a well-supplied valley, guarded from the monsters inhabiting the Woods. Later in the game, Bucha comments on Link's [[Water Dragon's Scale]], saying it will give him access to a large number of abilities he has not possessed before.

While Faron Woods is submerged underwater, Bucha can be found on a large [[Lily Pad]] where he gives Link helpful hints on how to find all of the [[Tadtone]]s hidden around the flooded forest. Bucha also suggests Link uses his [[Dowsing]] ability to find the hidden Tadtones.

==Nomenclature==
Bucha's name may be derived from {{Wp|kombucha}}, a type of fermented tea. Bucha's name may also be derived from {{Romanize|部長|Buchou}},<ref>{{Cite Web|url= http://www.jisho.org/words?jap=%E9%83%A8%E9%95%B7&eng=&dict=edict|title= 部長 or buchou in Japanese|site=Denshi Jisho — Online Japanese dictionary}}</ref> which generally translates to "Chief" or "Director".
