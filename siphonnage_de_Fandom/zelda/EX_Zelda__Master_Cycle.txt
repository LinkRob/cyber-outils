{{Term/Store|EX Zelda: Master Cycle||HWAoC}} is a level 5 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Zelda: Master Cycle|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game’s {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Zelda: Master Cycle}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}, and with only a {{Term|HWAoC|Master Cycle|link}} as her {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} receives a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|High-Yield Ancient Furnace|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Maintenance Oil|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Machine Lubricant|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Curing Agent|link}}.

For the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}'s objective is to defeat 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within three minutes. There are only about 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} at first, which are all {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} has at least 100 KOs, the timer resets to three minutes and she is given the same task to defeat 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}. A group of about 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin}} spawns again as well, although in a different part of the map, and with the addition of a small amount of {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}. When she reaches that objective, the same thing happens again, although there is a handful of {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} instead of {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu}}. When {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} reaches her third objective, the cycle ends and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawn instead. The time limit is also taken away. Once the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
