{{Term/Store|The Royal Escort||HWAoC}} is a Level 27 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Royal Escort|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|The Royal Escort}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Tireless Frog|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Prime Meat|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s objective is to escort {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}} to a Targeted Position. At first, there are only {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}} and a single {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} on the map, but as {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} moves closer to the Targeted Position, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}}, and multiple {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} spawn. As {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} moves even further, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}}, and more {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier}} spawn to prevent {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} from succeeding. Nothing else spawns after they are defeated, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} reaches the Targeted Position.
