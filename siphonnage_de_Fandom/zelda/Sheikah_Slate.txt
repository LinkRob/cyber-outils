The {{Term/Store|Sheikah Slate|Sheikah Slates|Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Sheikah Slate}}<br/>A mysterious tablet with a glowing center. You've never seen this device before, and yet...there's something familiar about it.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|SSBU}}

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} is a multipurpose tablet bearing the {{Term|BotW|Crest of the Sheikah|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> It was named by [[Purah]], since its original name remains undiscovered<ref name=":1">''The stone relic we discovered has been named. We are calling it the Sheikah Slate. We have not found any mention of a name for this object in the records we have unearthed so far... Nevertheless, Purah insisted we call it the Sheikah Slate, as the relic is a slate made by the Sheikah tribe. Feels a bit on the nose to me, but it was not a fight I thought I could win. — [[Zelda's Research Notes]] ([[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]])''</ref>. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} obtains it shortly after being awakened by a disembodied voice in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection|link}}. Though {{Term|BotW|Link}} has no recollection of seeing it before, he finds it familiar.<ref name="Inventory"/> He must first use it to get out of the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hold the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} up to the pedestal. That will show you the way.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} was made specifically for the chosen hero.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 402}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} can be used to access the {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, which displays the current {{Term|BotW|Temperature}} and time, and any {{Term|BotW|Pin|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Stamp|link}} placed. It also grants access to any of the many {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} located across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must activate the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah Tower}} to extract the local {{Term|BotW|Map}} to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|Distilling local information...|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Regional map extracted.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} as a {{Term|BotW|Scope}} when the R Stick is clicked down.<ref>{{Cite|Did you know about the {{Color|BotW Blue|scope}} on your {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}?|Old Man|BotW}}</ref> In addition to providing a closer view, the {{Term|BotW|Scope}} ability provides a first-person view from which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can set {{Plural|BotW|Pin}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} is able to receive {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}}, a form of information that bestows the device a number of different abilities. They serve as a replacement for traditional items such as {{Plural|BotW|Bomb|link}}. These {{Plural|BotW|Rune}} can be obtained and installed onto the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} at the four initial {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} found atop the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, the {{Term|BotW|Album|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}} features are unavailable at the beginning, as they are encrypted in {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} text.<ref>{{Cite|The Sheikah Slate data has been damaged|N/A|BotW}}</ref> They are restored when the tablet is brought to {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. Included in the {{Term|BotW|Camera}}'s {{Term|BotW|Album}} are 12 {{Plural|BotW|Photograph}} which were taken by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} a century prior. Upon completion of the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}} gifts {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.

Before the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}, [[Impa]], [[Robbie]] and {{Term|BotW|Purah}} went on a research expedition.<ref>''Impa introduced me to Purah and Robbie, other respected members of her tribe.''

''Tomorrow I embark on an excavation with them. — [[Zelda's Research Notes]], page 1 ([[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]])''</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} was unearthed by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} and {{Plural|BotW|Purah}}.<ref name=":0">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 374}}</ref> Purah then named it 'the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}'.<ref name=":1" /> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} was then used to activate the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}.<ref name=":0" /> While there are no records of where the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} was originally discovered, the mechanism accepting the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}} in the {{Term|BotW|Shrine of Resurrection}} consists of only a pedestal, thus presenting a strong argument that it was discovered there.<ref>''[[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild – Creating a Champion|Creating a Champion]]'', Dark Horse Books, pg. 375</ref>

==Other Appearances==
==={{SSBU|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Stub}}
The {{Term|SSBU|Sheikah Slate}} makes an appearance in {{SSBU}} as part of {{Term|SSBU|Link}}'s [[Champion Tunic]] outfit and his [[Tunic of the Wild]]. It is mostly only used to summon out a round {{Term|SSBU|Remote Bomb}} and to detonate it.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
The {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Slate}} first appears in the {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}}, "{{Term|HWAoC|The Battle of Hyrule Field|link}}", in {{HWAoC}}, when [[Impa]] drops it after being chased by a horde of {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}. The Sheikah Slate then awakens the {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link|display=Diminutive Guardian}} that was on the field lying dormant, and activated a [[Sheikah Tower]] in the middle of the battlefield.

The Sheikah Slate is mostly used in battles to use {{Plural|HWAoC|Rune|link}} such as [[Cryonis]] or [[Magnesis]], and the [[Sheikah Sensor]] can also be used to find out where objects needed to complete side quests could be found. In addition, Princess Zelda, from the start up until halfway through the [[Scenario]], "[[Each Step Like Thunder]]", primarily uses the Sheikah Slate to compensate for her lack of sealing power during this time to take part in various battles.  

The Sheikah Slate has several important roles, such as getting information from the memory of the Diminutive Guardian, and activating the Sheikah Towers all over [[Hyrule]], helping them to gain full knowledge of the land, such as monster outposts and the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]].
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In shape, the {{Term|Series|Sheikah Slate}} resembles a [[Nintendo Switch]] or a [[Wii U]] GamePad, referencing the consoles on which {{BotW|-}} was released. According to [[Eiji Aonuma]], it was designed to resemble a Wii U GamePad.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=(Playing the game last week, the controls felt great on Switch. The Sheikah Slate looks a bit like the Hyrule version of the Wii U GamePad, and looks like it could be controlled in that way - was that the intention?) '''Aonuma:''' Yes, initially that's true - we kind of pictured the Sheikah Slate as being reminiscent of a Wii U GamePad, but also to be honest with you I think the Sheikah Slate's size and appearance resembles a Nintendo Switch quite strongly. So we think that comparison works for both versions of the game, and we're happy with it. And the fact we didn't have to change the appearance of the Sheikah Slate in game... we were very grateful for that.|author=Eiji Aonuma|published=on an unknown date, updated on Jan 19, 2017|retrieved=May 4, 2025|url=https://www.eurogamer.net/zelda-breath-of-the-wild-boss-eiji-aonuma-on-the-large-extra-burden-of-moving-to-nintendo-switch|title=The big Zelda: Breath of the Wild interview|site=Eurogamer}}</ref>
*In the demo version of the game, used at E3 2016, the Sheikah Slate featured a health sensor that was built into the first-person scope ability. When pointed at a mob for 1 second it revealed their number of hit points.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= June 14, 2016|retrieved= February 25, 2021|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ze05xdORvYs&start=768| title= The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild - Exploration Gameplay - Nintendo E3 2016|site= YouTube|type=}} </ref> A similar ability was added to the [[Champion's Tunic]] in the final release.
*Link is able to make images of himself at any time, as long as he has the [[Camera]] [[Rune]]. Bizarrely, he does not hold the Sheikah Slate in any manner, instead it seems to hover mid-air, which appears to be gameplay convenience, rather than an actual ability of the Sheikah Slate.
