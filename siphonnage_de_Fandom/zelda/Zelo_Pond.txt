{{Term/Store|Zelo Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Zelo Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Zelo Pond is a medium-sized pond to the southeast of the [[Woodland Stable]], sitting right next to the foothills of [[Trilby Valley]] and the river that runs between [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]] and [[Hyrule Field]]. A sandbank runs between the Pond and river, creating an isthmus between the two bodies of water. Several [[Luminous Stone Deposit]]s can be found along the rock face.

A [[Korok]] is hidden in the Pond's water, who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding it.
