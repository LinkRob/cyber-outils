{{Term/Store|Whip|Whips|Series, ST, SS, HW, HWL, HWDE|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142 {{Exp Game|ST, SS}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HW}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
The {{Term|ST|Whip}} can be found in the {{Term|ST|Ocean Temple|link}} in {{ST}}. Its main use is to swing from poles and for puzzles that require the transfer of certain {{Plural|ST|Sword}} from one place to another. It can also be used to remove thorns from {{Plural|ST|Tree|link}} that block {{Term|ST|Link|link}}'s path as well as being able to hold on to flying propellers to travel to otherwise unreachable areas. When summoning {{Plural|ST|Bird|link}} by playing the "{{Term|ST|Song of Birds|link}}", {{Term|ST|Link}} can use the {{Term|ST|Whip}} to latch on to the wooden bars they hold, which allows him to be carried by them.

The {{Term|ST|Whip}} can attack certain {{Plural|ST|Chuchu|link}} that {{Term|ST|Link}} cannot attack with his {{Term|ST|Sword|link}}. It can also take the {{Term|ST|Shield|link}} and armor away from certain enemies. The {{Term|ST|Whip}} is required during the fight against {{Term|ST|Phytops|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Temple}}, where it is used to pick up and throw thorns to damage the creature's weak point.

The {{Term|ST|Whip}} can also be used in the {{Term|ST|Whip Race|link}} minigame in {{Term|ST|Whittleton|link}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
The {{Term|SS|Whip}} can be found in the {{Term|SS|Ancient Cistern|link}} in {{SS}}. A ball of light is found at the end of the {{Term|SS|Whip}}, which can attach to many things. It is also possible to hang on poles and certain branches with the {{Term|SS|Whip}} and swing in the air with it. It can also be used to attach to the hooked thorns of a {{Term|SS|Lily Pad|link}} to flip it over, to pull out-of-reach levers, and to uproot {{Plural|SS|Peahat|link}} to later be used with the {{Term|SS|Clawshot|link}}. {{Plural|SS|Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|SS|Heart|link}} can be grabbed with the {{Term|SS|Whip}} and pulled to {{Term|SS|Link}}. As a weapon, it can be used to defeat weaker enemies, stun larger ones, and to dismantle the {{Term|SS|Ancient Cistern}}'s {{Term|SS|Boss}}, {{Term|SS|Koloktos|link}}. The {{Term|SS|Whip}} can be used to [[Thievery|steal]] {{Plural|SS|Monster Horn|link}} from {{Plural|SS|Bokoblin|link}}. To use the {{Term|SS|Whip}}, the [[Wii]] Remote must be flicked.
