==Location and uses==
The Deku Seed Bullet Bag is obtained alongside the [[Fairy Slingshot]] when found [[Inside the Deku Tree]]. It can initially hold up to 30 [[Deku Seed]] bullets.<ref name="Description"/> Link can receive bigger Deku Seed Bullet Bags by scoring three perfect bullseyes in the target practice at the [[Lost Woods]] and by hitting all 10 [[Rupee]] targets in the [[Shooting Gallery]] at the [[Market]].<ref>{{Cite|Cool! You're great! You scored three perfect bullseyes! I have to give a neat present to such a wonderful person! Please take it!|Deku Scrub|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wonderful! Bravo! Perfect! Here's a fantastic present!|Shooting Gallery Owner|OoT3D}}</ref> Link can do these in any order but receives a '''Big Deku Seed Bullet Bag''', which can hold 40 Deku Seeds,<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Big Deku Seed Bullet Bag}}. This bag can hold up to {{Color|OoT3D Yellow|40}} slingshot bullets.|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref>{{Note|In all versions of {{OoT|-}} prior to {{OoT3D|-}}, the Big Deku Seed Bullet Bag was known as the '''Deku Seeds Bullet Bag''' when obtained and the Bullet Bag when viewed in the Equipment Subscreen.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|OoT Red|Deku Seeds Bullet Bag}}. This bag can hold up to {{Color|OoT Yellow|40}} slingshot bullets.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> This was retconned to "Big Deku Seed Bullet Bag" in {{OoT3D|-}}.}} from whichever he does first. The Bullet Bag Link receives from whichever he does second is the '''Biggest Deku Seed Bullet Bag''' which can hold 50 Deku Seeds.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Biggest Deku Seed Bullet Bag}}!
