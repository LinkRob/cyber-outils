{{Term/Store|Clavia||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Clavia|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Clavia}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} resident of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. She can often be found on the dock behind the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, thinking to herself. At night, she sleeps in the {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|link}} with her family.<ref>{{Cite|Zzz...zzz...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> During {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, she can be found taking stock in her {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|display=House}}. She greets {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} when they speak,<ref>{{Cite|Mornin'.|Clavia|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good day.|Clavia|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Evenin'.|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> and she occasionally remarks on her life in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|display=Hateno}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh, Hateno...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first meets {{Term|BotW|Clavia}}, it takes her a moment to realize that he is a traveler.<ref>{{Cite|... You're a traveler, aren't you?|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> She is eager to share something with him, but if Link decides to leave instead, she considers it a shame.<ref>{{Cite|[See you later.] Oh, dear... What a shame.|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to listen to her, {{Term|BotW|Clavia}} will point to the cliffs of {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}} and explain that she found a note in an old {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} at her {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|display=House}}. <ref>{{Cite|[What is it?] You see those three trees up on top of that snowy mountain there? I found a small letter stuck between the pages of this old book at my house...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> The note holds cryptic instructions to a trial.<ref>{{Cite|It said, "When the three summit trees become one, turn your back to them and head toward the sea. A trial encased in stone awaits you there.”|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> Although {{Term|BotW|Clavia}} admits that she does not understand the words, she guesses that the note refers to {{Term|BotW|Afromsia Coast|link}} and excitedly thinks of the possibility of treasure waiting there.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know what that means! But maybe it means we have to head to {{Color|BotW Blue|Afromsia Coast}}, which is just east of those three trees on the mountain! For the "trial {{Color|BotW Red|encased in stone}}..." bit, I wonder if you get some kind of treasure for doing that...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> However, she is quick to recall with dismay that she has parental responsibilities and must remain in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}.<ref name="Child"/> Instead, she enlists {{Term|BotW|Link}} to venture out and collect the reward for her, beginning the "{{Term|BotW|Secret of the Cedars|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But you can do that, right?! You'd be great at that! Go on! Head out there and bring us back that treasure!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Clavia}} later after completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}. She will hesitantly ask if he has found the treasure the note spoke of.<ref>{{Cite|Did you happen to find the treasure... by any chance?|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> Realizing that it was rude to push this errand on {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she asks if he might be open to sharing the reward.<ref>{{Cite|I guess it probably wasn't fair of me to expect you to do all of the exploring and adventuring... But maybe we could split the treasure seventy-thirty or something like that?|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> Regardless of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s response, {{Term|BotW|Clavia}} will admit that she was only joking with him and that she is satisfied without it.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, I'm just kidding! I'm plenty satisfied with what I have in my life as it is. The key is to always live within your means, you know?|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> She is disappointed by the "treasure" being an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, as there is already one in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Beyond the three summit trees... there was only a {{Color|BotW Blue|shrine}}, huh? We have our very own shrine here in the village, so I'm not so impressed...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref>

As with other villagers in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|display=Hateno}}, {{Term|BotW|Clavia}} will react to the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her while carrying it. She recognizes it as the one {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} uses,<ref>{{Cite|Oh? That {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}}... Is that the same as the one Purah has?|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> and she suggests he hurry along with his delivery.<ref>{{Cite|You'd better hurry and take that {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} over to Purah's.|Clavia|BotW}}</ref>
