The {{Term|BotW|Spring-Loaded Hammer}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hammer|link}} that can be obtained from {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}, for 199 {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}. It can only be purchased after {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has freed at least one {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}'s control. When spoken to after doing so, {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} will announce the development of the {{Term|BotW|Spring-Loaded Hammer}} and describe its effects.<ref>{{Cite|Da da...DAAAAAAAA! I've developed a new piece of {{Color|BotW Blue|monster gear}}! I call it the... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Spring-loaded hammer}}!}} Whack a small monster with this thing, and...BOING! It'll launch the monster into the distance! It's really useful for getting rid of pesky monsters that you don't feel like fighting. Plus, it's just fun to use!|Kilton|BotW}}</ref> Unlike other {{Plural|BotW|Hammer}} in {{BotW}}, the {{Term|BotW|Spring-Loaded Hammer}} is held with one hand.

When the {{Term|BotW|Spring-Loaded Hammer}} is used, the fourth hit from a chain of regular attacks will send certain {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} flying.<ref name="Inventory"/> As it has little strength, it is primarily meant for entertainment. The {{Term|BotW|Spring-Loaded Hammer}} is not magnetic and cannot be retrieved with {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}}. This also means that it will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and it will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. When dropped into {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, it will float on the surface. When exposed to an open flame, such as a {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, it will catch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}.
