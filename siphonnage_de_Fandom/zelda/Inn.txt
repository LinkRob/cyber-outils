==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Happy Hearth Inn}}
The only {{Term|TMC|Inn}} in {{TMC}} is the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. Staying there costs {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}, but {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} will have his {{Term|TMC|Life Gauge|link}} restored and receive a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} as a gift for staying.

The {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn}} is also the location where the three {{Plural|TMC|Oracle|link}} can be found before they rent out {{Plural|TMC|Vacant House|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Gorman|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
There are numerous {{Plural|BotW|Inn}} across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} in {{BotW}}. {{Plural|BotW|Inn}} are typically found in major settlements, where they offer guests regular {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} ranging from free to 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} a night to restore {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, or more expensive {{Plural|BotW|Bed}} which grant {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart|link}} in addition to restoring {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge}}. {{Plural|BotW|Inn}} that charge 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} for their expensive {{Plural|BotW|Bed}} will grant a single {{Term|BotW|Extra Heart}} whereas {{Plural|BotW|Inn}} that charge 80 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} per night come with additional services and grant two {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}}. However, the {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} granted from {{Plural|BotW|Inn}} are not successive and can only restore to the level of {{Term|BotW|Extra Heart}} that they are intended to grant. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has any {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina|link}} while resting on an 80 {{Term|BotW|Rupee}} charge, the active {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} for his {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina}} will also be completely filled regardless of the original amount of {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina}} that he possessed.

{{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}} also double as {{Plural|BotW|Inn}}, and each {{Term|BotW|Stable}} offers {{Plural|BotW|Bed}} ranging from 20 to 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}. The {{Term|BotW|Great Deku Tree's Navel|link}} and the unnamed {{Term|BotW|Inn}} in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}} are the only {{Plural|BotW|Inn}} which do not charge {{Term|BotW|Link}} to stay the night.<ref>{{Cite|Zzz... Oh, Mr. Hero. Welcome to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Deku Tree's Navel}}. I made a bed out of leaves just for you, Mr. Hero! Take as long as you like with yawn and the ZZZs!|Pepp|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|[How much?] Oh, I don't want your rupees! You're the great Mr. Hero! I prepared this bed for you ages ago! All I want is to help you get some sleep.|Pepp|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|I was waiting for so long...I got really *yawn* sleepy...|Pepp|BotW}}</ref> However, they do not offer any expensive bedding options and cannot grant any {{Plural|BotW|Extra Heart}} or {{Term|BotW|Extra Stamina|display=Stamina}}.
