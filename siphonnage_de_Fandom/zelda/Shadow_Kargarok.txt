{{Term/Store|Shadow Kargarok|Shadow Kargaroks|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}},<ref name= "Encyclopedia"/> also known as '''Monstrous Birds''',<ref>{{Cite|What... What are these {{Color|TPHD Red|monstrous birds}}? What in the world is happening here?|Soldier|TPHD}}</ref> are enemies in {{TP}}.

==Characteristics==
Shadow Kargaroks are [[Kargarok|Kargaroks]] that have been transformed by the effects of the [[Twilight]]. They attack in the same way as normal Kargaroks, although their bodies are black, their heads are concave, and they make siren-like sounds. 

[[Wolf Link]] first encounters Shadow Kargaroks on the [[Castle Rooftops]], but they can also be found in other [[Twilit]] regions. In the Twilit lakebed of [[Lake Hylia]], a [[Shadow Bulblin]] uses the [[Hawk Grass]] to summon [[Twilit Carrier Kargarok]]. After defeating the Bulblin, the Kargarok carries Link and [[Midna]] to [[Upper Zora's River]].<ref>''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess — PRIMA Official Game Guide|PRIMA Official Game Guide]]'' (Prima Games) pg. 170</ref>
