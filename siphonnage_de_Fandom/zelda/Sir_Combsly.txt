{{Term/Store|Sir Combsly||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} is a servant of {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}} and commander of the {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade|link}} of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Allow me to introduce myself. I am {{Color|TFH Blue|Sir Combsly}}, commander of the {{Color|TFH Blue|witch-hunting brigade}} for the great kingdom of Hytopia.|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> He once had a slickback hairstyle, but it has since collapsed.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Exp|魔女討伐隊の司令官。正義感が強くあつい男。昔はオールバックだったが、 今は髪形が崩れている。|The Commander of the Witch Subdue Corps. A passionate man with a strong sense of justice. Although he used to have slicked back hair, his hairstyle has now collapsed.}}|author= Nintendo|published= September 16, 2015|retrieved=September 24, 2015|url= http://www.nintendo.co.jp/3ds/ea3j/storychara/characters.html|title= ゼルダの伝説　トライフォース３銃士 <nowiki>|</nowiki> ニンテンドー3DS <nowiki>|</nowiki> 任天堂|site= Zelda.co.jp}} <sup>(Translation: [http://zeldauniverse.net/forums/index.php/Thread/178050-The-Legend-of-Zelda-TriForce-Heroes-Gameplay-spoilers-beware/?postID=5657575#post5657575 kaialone])</sup></ref>

After {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} is spotted by {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's Apprentice|link}} and recognized to have the same features as the fabled {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Hero|link}}, he is sent to meet with {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}}. {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} recruits him into the {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade}} upon seeing that his features perfectly match the legendary {{Term|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? That face... Those ears... You there! I must speak with you! Pointy ears... Check! Sideburns... Check! And finally... hair parted to the side... CHECK! IT'S HIM!!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Excellent. As expected, soldier. I must say... I've seen too many would-be heroes in my time. Too many, in fact. But none of them boasted the perfect trifecta of heroic features as you do. We're happy to have you on our side!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> He gives him some {{Term|TFH|Hytopian Silk|link}} so he can tailor his uniform, the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic|link}}, at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Consider yourself enlisted. As a token of welcome, I will supply you with one firm handshake and one Hero's Tunic. Here are the necessary materials. Take them to Madame Couture at once, and she'll make you the outfit!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref>

{{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} will congratulate {{Term|TFH|Link}} whenever he opens a new {{Term|TFH|Area|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. After completing the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}} {{Term|TFH|Area}}, he will inform {{Term|TFH|Link}} that he has been summoned by {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}}. {{Term|TFH|Link}} can also speak with {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} whenever he wishes to change his name that is displayed during [[Multiplayer]].

After the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} defeat {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}} and craft the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble|link}} needed to lift {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}'s curse, {{Term|TFH|Link}} enters the Castle wearing the {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}}, but {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} does not recognize him at first.<ref>{{Cite|Did you need something? Huh? No? Maybe? Wait a dash... Isn't that... Ah, {{Player Name}}! Is that the outfit Madame spoke of?|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} had told him of the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} earlier, and he tells {{Term|TFH|Link}} to lift the curse off of {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}} right away. After {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}}'s lift is cursed, the town holds a big celebration. {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} demonstrates his strength during the celebration by lifting both {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} and {{Term|TFH|Link}}, forming a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}}. After the festivities are over, he tells {{Term|TFH|Link}} that he should go see {{Term|TFH|King Tuft}} for his promised reward.

==Nomenclature==
Sir Combsly's name most likely derives from a {{wp|comb}}, a fashion-oriented object for styling hair.
