{{Term/Store|Wisdom Gem|Wisdom Gems|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref name="E 143"/>

==Location and Uses==
By collecting 10 {{Plural|PH|Wisdom Gem}}, the {{Term|PH|Neri|link|display= Spirit of Wisdom}} increases {{Term|PH|Link|link}}'s defense while allowing him to stun weaker enemies by walking into them. Getting all 20 quadruples the defense ratio. If the {{Term|PH|Neri|display= Spirit of Wisdom}} is equipped, less time will be lost from the {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)|link}} when hit by a {{Term|PH|Phantom|link}}; 15 seconds with ten {{Plural|PH|Wisdom Gem}}, or only five seconds with all 20.
