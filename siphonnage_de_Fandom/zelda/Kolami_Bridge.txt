{{Term/Store|Kolami Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Kolami Bridge South'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Kolami Bridge is located in the [[Tabantha Frontier]] between [[Strock Lake]] and [[Tanagar Canyon]]. Kolami Bridge is a thin long strip of logs tied together across a long gap. Along the bridge, a great wind blows hard sometimes east and sometimes west. [[Link]] can use the [[Paraglider]] to glide down the west wind to Strock Lake quickly. Or he could use the east wind to glide across Tanagr Canyon. Down under the bridge, although there are many enemies, there are some [[Luminous Stone Deposit]]s for Link to harvest.

Underneath the bridge, a [[Korok]] can be found hiding under a rock behind a Bombable Wall is the northwest corner of the area.
