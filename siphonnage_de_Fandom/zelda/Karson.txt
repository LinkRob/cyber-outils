{{Term/Store|Karson||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Karson|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

== Biography ==
{{Term|BotW|Karson}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} resident of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. He has recently finished his trial period for {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction|link}}, making him an official employee.<ref>{{Cite|My trial period is done, and they finally hired me as an official member of the Bolson family!|Karson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] I just started at Bolson Construction. Name is Karson!|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

Initially, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} can be found attempting to demolish the {{Term|BotW|Link's House|link|display=abandoned house}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} alongside {{Term|BotW|Hudson|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Workin', as you can see! This old house has been abandoned. Everyone in Hateno Village agreed we should demolish it.|Karson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Phew... This house isn't looking any less demolished despite all the effort... |Karson|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that the former owner of the house left to serve at {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} and never returned.<ref>{{Cite|The old owner apparently went off to {{Color|BotW Blue|the castle}} to report for service. Never came back, never wrote, so away it goes!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} comments that this seems like hard work, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} claims that he would not want it any other way.<ref>{{Cite|[Hard work, huh?] Yep! And I wouldn't want it any other way, to be honest with ya.|Karson|BotW}}</ref>  If {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to buy the house, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} will direct him to his boss, {{Term|BotW|Bolson|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'd buy this place!] Well, I'm not into real estate, so I can't really help you out there. If you're really interested in this place, you're gonna need to talk to the {{Color|BotW Blue|boss}}.|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Karson}} admits to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was hired by {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction}} due to his name ending in "-son."<ref>{{Cite|I'm so glad my name happens to end in "son," y'know?|Karson|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} explains that {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction}} has a policy of only hiring people whose names end in "-son."<ref>{{Cite|[...Son?] Yep, you heard me right! Apparently, {{Color|BotW Red|your name}} has to end in "{{Color|BotW Red|son}}" to work at Bolson Construction.|Karson|BotW}}</ref> he claims that {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} has been considering expanding his business, but he does not know where he is going to find enough people fitting the name requirement.<ref>{{Cite|The boss there was saying he's really looking into expanding the business. But I'm wondering—just how many folks are out there with {{Color|BotW Red|names ending in}} "{{Color|BotW Red|son}}"? Maybe lots?|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to buy the {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}} from {{Term|BotW|Bolson}}, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} will cease work and sit underneath a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} nearby, awaiting {{Term|BotW|Bolson}}'s orders.<ref>{{Cite|I got the order to stop from the boss, so demolition is on hold for now...|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He appears relieved to receive a break.<ref>{{Cite|[That's great.] Hoo...it sure is.|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He will admits to {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he finds construction work to be hard, which is why he idolizes {{Term|BotW|Hudson}}'s ability to work quickly.<ref>{{Cite|Demolition is seriously hard work. But there are folks like Hudson who could bust through a house in one day... The guy's my hero.|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He then reveals that he overheard {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s deal with {{Term|BotW|Bolson}}, and he asks if he is serious about buying the {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|So... I overheard you talking to the boss. Are you really gonna buy the house?|Karson|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he is, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} will be surprised, especially since {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be younger than him.<ref>{{Cite|[Yep, buyin' it!] {{Big|Whoa! No way!}} That's incredible. And you even look younger than me...|Karson|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} claims that he will not buy the {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}}, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} is still impressed that he considered it, and he wonders how much demolition he will have to do in order to make that kind of money.<ref>{{Cite|...But you were considering it? How many houses would I have to demolish to get that kind of dough?!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> Despite {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s answer, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} decides to keep working hard to build his own place in the world.<ref>{{Cite|Not that it matters. We all have our own place to build. I'll just keep workin' hard the best way I know how!|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the purchase of his {{Term|BotW|Link's House|display=House}}, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} will thank him for doing so.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, yeah, thanks for the big purchase!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He is impressed someone so young was able to spend so many {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Seriously, though. Well done! Where'd a kid like you get that kind of dough?|Karson|BotW}}</ref> Due to this, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} has received a sizable bonus.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I'm getting a plum bonus!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> With it, he plans on getting a meal with his girlfriend, {{Term|BotW|Sophie|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Me and my girlfriend are gonna grab a tasty meal!|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Karson}} will take note when {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} leaves for {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Hudson just left not too long ago.|Karson|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks where he went, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} reveals that he is in search of new and creative ways to expand the business {{Term|BotW|Bolson Construction}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Where?] Looking for new and creative ways to expand the business, he said.|Karson|BotW}}</ref> Though he wants to help him, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} believes he still has much to learn.<ref>{{Cite|I wanna help him out, but I'm still green. Part of getting better in life is realizing how much you have to learn!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Karson}} still has many things he hopes that {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} will teach him, and so he hopes they will meet again soon.<ref>{{Cite|There was tons more that I was still hopin' to learn from him. Hope I get to see him again soon...|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

Throughout the "{{Term|BotW|Hylian Homeowner|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} will assist {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} in renovating {{Term|BotW|Link's House}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm with you!|Karson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Been waiting!|Karson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Right on!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> After all of the renovations are complete, {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} has {{Term|BotW|Karson}} help him install the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Furniture|link}} into {{Term|BotW|Link's House}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, since you bought everything, I threw in some free {{Color|BotW Blue|furniture}}! Yeah! Least I could do! {{Big|Karson! Look lively!}}|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> The two of them will then proceed to take a break.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I am TUCKERED. I couldn't swing a ball-peen hammer right now, let alone an axe... Break time, baby!|Bolson|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|From the Ground Up|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to invite {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} and {{Term|BotW|Karson}} to his wedding in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, we'll have to invite guests in order to hold a ceremony... [Guests?] Well, I'm talking about my boss, Bolson, and my subordinate, Karson. [I see.] However, I've got to prepare for the ceremony, so my hands are full... Could you invite them for me?|Hudson|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} informs {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} of the wedding, he and {{Term|BotW|Karson}} will depart for {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} together.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Karson, we're headed out!}}|Bolson|BotW}}</ref> Before he leaves, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} remarks that he and {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} have been waiting for this day.<ref>{{Cite|Finally! We've been waiting for this day!|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

After arriving in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Hudson}} states that he feels relaxed now that he has met with {{Term|BotW|Hudson}}.<ref>{{Cite|I just met with Hudson, so now I'm feeling pretty relaxed!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has met {{Term|BotW|Hudson}}'s fiancée, {{Term|BotW|Rhondson|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That reminds me, have you already met Hudson's fiancée?|Karson|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Karson}} considers her to be a beauty, and he is impressed that she makes all of the clothes that she sells. <ref>{{Cite|She's a real beauty... And apparently she's an amazing tailor too! All those clothes she sells? She makes them herself!|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He shares that his girlfriend {{Term|BotW|Sophie}} also works at an {{Term|BotW|Armor Shop|link}}, but her father is the one who actually makes all of the clothes.<ref name="Girlfriend"/> However, he realizes he has gotten on a tangent.<ref>{{Cite|I guess it doesn't matter...|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

During the wedding ceremony, when {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} rebukes {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}}'s claim that the vows are not traditional, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} argues that she likely did not mean traditional in the way he is thinking.<ref>{{Cite|I don't think she means traditional in that sense of the word.|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He is also seen applauding once the couple has been declared married. Afterwards, he and {{Term|BotW|Bolson}} will return to {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Karson! We're going home!|Bolson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK!|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

After returning to {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Karson}} will wonder what it is that {{Term|BotW|Rhondson}} likes about {{Term|BotW|Hudson}}.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder what it is that {{Color|BotW Blue|Rhondson}} likes about Hudson...|Karson|BotW}}</ref> He theorizes that it could be {{Term|BotW|Hudson}}'s silky hair or his chiseled physique.<ref>{{Cite|Is it his astoundingly silky hair?|Karson|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Is it his chiseled physique?|Karson|BotW}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
The first half of Karson's name is a variation of the Scandinavian name Karr, which means "reluctant", "curly haired", and "obstinate".<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/ka10rr/submitted |site= behindthename.com |title= Given Name KARR}}</ref>
