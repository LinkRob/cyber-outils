The {{Term/Store|South Akkala Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''South Akkala Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

According to Zelda, it was destroyed during the [[Great Calamity]], but fully rebuilt sometime afterward<ref>''"South Akkala Stable... Many, many Guardians swept through this area, and though the building was destroyed, the workers were all able to get to safety. No doubt that is thanks to the soldiers of Akkala Citadel buying them time... But now the Stable Association has rebuilt the stable, and you can hardly see any traces of the past destruction."'' – [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] (''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'')</ref>.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. West of the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} is the {{Term|BotW|Ze Kasho Shrine|link}}, which stands high on a cliff overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}. The {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}} contains every amenity characteristic to {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, including {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} services, providing lodging, and a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}. It also contains some farmland, with {{Term|BotW|Highland Sheep|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}}. Several crates are left outside which can be broken for small items. Inside the livestock pen, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}} can be befriended who leads {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Claymore|link}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Stable}} is run by {{Term|BotW|Dmitri|link}}, who mans both its {{Term|BotW|Horse}} services and lodging. As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} can occasionally be found here selling his wares.

A traveler named {{Term|BotW|Rex|link}} can be found at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, who asks Link about the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}, denying its existence if he doesn't have the Sword, and calling it a fake if Link has already obtained the Sword.

The player may speak to {{Term|BotW|Tenne|link}}, a woman who will tend to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Horse}} and suit them with {{Term|BotW|Horse Gear|link}} if its bond has reached its maximum.

Outside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} is a worker named {{Term|BotW|Jana|link}}, who is in charge of the flower patch and provides the "{{Term|BotW|Little Sister's Big Request|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. A woman named {{Term|BotW|Kaifa|link}} tends to the livestock on the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}’s farm.

There is a recipe for {{Term|BotW|Egg Tart|link}} on the wall inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} and, if the {{Term|BotW|The Master Trials|link}} DLC is installed, the second volume of {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}} can be found on the a table.
