==Biography==
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} is a reworked {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}. It was originally a {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} that was used in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} but was taken by {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} during the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I returned to Kakariko Village to collect the remaining Guidance Stone, then I immediately set out for Akkala.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} was later remodeled into the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} to automate the process of refining {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}} for research in order to lessen the load of {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}'s daily routine.<ref>{{Cite|It's difficult to say how much progress I made on my research. The routine demanded so much focus, day after day. But then I had a clever thought! I realized I could automate the entire refinement process! It took significant work, but I was able to successfully remodel the Guidance Stone. Thanks to my effort, it can refine any ancient material inserted into it and offer something in return.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> He named the machine {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}} after his first love.<ref>{{Cite|I named this machine Cherry, after the first girl I ever loved...|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref>

Eventually, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} had an idea that could further increase {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}}'s capabilities by automating the manufacturing process alongside the refining process, as well as giving it the ability to speak.<ref name="Manufacture"/> The process of upgrading {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}} was extremely difficult, and {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} even had to leave the idea for a short time.<ref>{{Cite|Automating the manufacturing process was extremely difficult. It took me decades to figure it out. I'll spare you the details of all the things that went wrong... But I will say it was complex enough that I had to put my desires to remodel Cherry on the back burner for a while.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> It wasn't until {{Term|BotW|Jerrin|link}} arrived that she was able to improve {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}}'s designs and give it a voice alongside the ability to manufacture ancient equipment.<ref>{{Cite|I would have been lost in my work even then... But around that time, a young woman named Jerrin arrived. She'd first trained with Dr. Purah, then Lady Impa had sent her to aid me. She quickly proved herself quite capable. She carried the blue flame from Tumela Heights and even reworked and improved my designs for Cherry.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks to Jerrin's redesign, Cherry was soon crafting ancient soldier gear perfectly and speaking beautifully.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> At some point during this time, {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} and {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} got married.<ref>{{Cite|Her intellect, her spirit... In hindsight, is it any surprise that we eventually ended up married?|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> After a while, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} realized that his interactions with {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}} and him calling it {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}} was hurting {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}}, so he took it upon himself to remove it's advanced voice programming and rename it to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}}.<ref>{{Cite|She spoke better than any machine I'd ever heard. It would have been hard to tell she wasn't a native Hylian. If I said, "Good morning," she'd respond with a sweet, "Good morning, Robbie." And she was helpful! "Cherry, where'd I leave my book?" "It's the third book from the right on the second shelf, Robbie." She also knew to ask if she needed something. "Robbie, could you increase the furnace heat a little?" I'd always go personally to fetch more blue flame from Tumlea Heights. I didn't realize our interactions hurt someone... That someone...was Jerrin. Perhaps it was due to her namesake, but my exchanges with Cherry hurt Jerrin. Despite the fact that she knew Cherry was only a machine, she still felt uneasy with my interactions with her. She was in tears when she told me. She knew it made little sense, and yet she couldn't help how she felt. I was devastated. I hadn't even realized the pain I was causing. Although Jerrin had designed Cherry, she was now being hurt by her own creation. We agreed to restore Cherry to her original, mechanical way of speaking. I took it upon myself to make the changes. From then on, I was careful to not refer to her as Cherry. Doing so would anger my wife. Instead, I called her... ancient oven.|Robbie's Memoirs|BotW}}</ref> From then on, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} vowed to stop calling the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|display=Cherry}}" as it would make {{Term|BotW|Jerrin}} mad.<ref>{{Cite|My wife...isn't as crazy about her as I am. Yes, when I call her {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}, my wife gets angry and throws things. That's why I call her the ancient oven now.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} 100 years after the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}}, the {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}} outside the lab is extinguished, making the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} nonfunctional.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}...erm, the ancient oven... creates ancient soldier gear. But I fear that she's out of sorts at the moment. Hm. I'm afraid someone will have to bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Term|BotW|ancient furnace}} again.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> During the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Robbie's Research|link}}," {{Term|BotW|Link}} can carry the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} and relight the {{Term|BotW|Furnace}} to activate the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}}, giving him access to the shop.
