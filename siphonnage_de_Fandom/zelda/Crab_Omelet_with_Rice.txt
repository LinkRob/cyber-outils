{{Term/Store|Crab Omelet with Rice|Crab Omelets with Rice|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Crab Omelet with Rice}}<br/>The fluffy crab legs pair perfectly with the rice for a truly scrumptious dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Crab Omelets with Rice are made by combining [[Hylian Rice]], a [[Bird Egg]], [[Rock Salt]], and any {{Term|BotW|Crab|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Regular Crab Omelets with Rice can only be made by using two different types of Crabs in the recipe or by using an ingredient with a differing effect. These types of omelets restore eight {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Alternatively, they can be sold for 70 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. The fluffy meat inside of the crab legs are known to pair perfectly with the rice.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Three different variations of Crab Omelets with Rice exist, correlating with all three different types of Crabs:

* '''Energizing Crab Omelet''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Crab Omelet with Rice}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The fluffy crab legs pair perfectly with the rice for a truly scrumptious dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using a {{Term|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}. Consuming one will partially refill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They restore six Hearts to his Life Gauge, but they can also be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Crab Omelet''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Crab Omelet with Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The fluffy crab legs pair perfectly with the rice for a truly scrumptious dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using a {{Term|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}. When eaten, they'll temporarily boost Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They also restore nine Hearts to his Life Gauge, and they sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Tough Crab Omelet''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Crab Omelet with Rice}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The fluffy crab legs pair perfectly with the rice for a truly scrumptious dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using an {{Term|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}. If they're consumed, they increase Link's defense for a short period of time.<ref name="Tough"/> They heal Link for seven Hearts and sell for 60 Rupees.
