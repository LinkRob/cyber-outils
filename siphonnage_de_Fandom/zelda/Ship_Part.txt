{{Term/Store|Ship Part|Ship Parts|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 313}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} consists of eight customizable {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}}: {{Term|PH|Prow|link}}, {{Term|PH|Anchor|link}}, {{Term|PH|Hull|link}}, {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}}, {{Term|PH|Handrail|link}}, {{Term|PH|Bridge|link}}, {{Term|PH|Chimney|link}}, and {{Term|PH|Wheel|link}}. The ninth {{Term|PH|Ship Part}}, the {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm}}, cannot be changed. While traveling on the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}}, the {{Term|PH|Life Gauge|link}} appears green instead of red to denote the stamina of the Ship rather than {{Term|PH|Link|link}}'s health.

Attaching new {{Term|PH|Ship Part}} to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} can increase its stamina from it initial four {{Plural|PH|Heart}}. When using three, six, or all eight {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the same set, its stamina is increased to five, six, or seven {{Plural|PH|Heart}}, respectively. For every two {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the {{Term|PH|Golden Ship|link}} set used, its stamina is increased by one {{Term|PH|Heart}}, up to a maximum of eight {{Plural|PH|Heart}}. Docking the Ship at any {{Term|PH|Island|link}}'s port replenishes all the Ship's stamina.

{{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} are obtained through various methods:
<section begin="Methods"/>
* Purchasing them from {{Term|PH|Beedle|link}} and other {{Plural|PH|Shop|link}}.
* Finding them in {{Plural|PH|Treasure Chest|link}}.
* Salvaging them using the {{Term|PH|Salvage Arm|link}}.
* Winning them from {{Plural|PH|Mini-Game|link}}.
* Trading them in {{Term|PH|Freedle|link}}'s boxes.<section end="Methods"/>
{{Term|PH|Link}} can collect multiple instances of the same {{Term|PH|Ship Part}}, up to 99 of each.

Most {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} can be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} on {{Term|PH|Mercay Island}}. All {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} in the same set have the same value, and the value of each set is randomly selected from three possibilities at the creation of the [[Saving|Save]] file: 50,<ref name="50 Rupees"/> 150,<ref name="150 Rupees"/> or 800 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}.<ref name="800 Rupees"/> The only exceptions are the {{Term|PH|Golden Ship}} parts, which are always valued at 1,500 {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} apiece,<ref name="1,500 Rupees"/> and the default {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}}, which are valued at zero {{Plural|PH|Rupee}} and cannot be sold.<ref name="0 Rupees"/> Once a {{Term|PH|Ship Part}} has been sold, its value is recorded on the {{Term|PH|Collection Screen|link}} menu.
