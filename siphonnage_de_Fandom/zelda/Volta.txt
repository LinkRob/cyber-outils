==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ZA|Volta}} are bird-like men that inhabit the later sections of the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Air|link}}. They can be first encountered if {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|link}} pushes one of two blocks in one of the rooms, causing them to appear, however more {{Plural|ZA|Volta}} will be encountered later in the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Air|display=Shrine}}. They are hardy enemies with good endurance, and take three hits of the {{Term|ZA|Wand|link}} to be defeated.
