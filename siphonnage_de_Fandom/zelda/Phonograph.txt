{{Term/Store|Phonograph|Phonographs|Series, OoS|plural}} are recurring items and objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
A Phonograph can be seen in {{OoT}} being played by [[Guru-Guru]] who resides in the [[Kakariko Windmill|Windmill]] in [[Kakariko Village]]. Guru-Guru later uses his Phonograph to teach an adult [[Link]] the [[Song of Storms]].

==={{MM|-}}===
An identical Phonograph reappears in {{MM}} alongside Guru-Guru in his room at the [[Stock Pot Inn]], and the [[Laundry Pool]]. It also appears in the [[Milk Bar]] during the credits.

==={{OoS|-}}===
[[File:OoS Guru-Guru.gif|right|Guru Guru and the Phonograph]]
The Phonograph is the tenth and final item in the {{OoS}} {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}. It is given to Link by Guru-Guru in exchange for helping the [[Windmill]] spin faster with the [[Engine Grease]].<ref>{{Cite|Engine Grease? Will that make it go quickly?|Guru-Guru|OoS}},</ref> When played for the [[Deku Scrub]] in the [[Lost Woods]], he will give Link instructions on how to navigate the Lost Woods and find the [[Noble Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|Dee! What a tune! I love it! I'll teach you the way to my Secret Spot! If temperatures rise as you go far to the west, you'll find it!|Deku Scrub|OoS}}</ref> After Guru-Guru gives the Phonograph to Link, he pulls out a spare one to resume playing himself.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, a {{Term|TMC|Phonograph}} can be found inside the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. It allows {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to listen to music.

Two tracks on the {{Term|TMC|Phonograph}} ("{{Term|TMC|2|link}}" and "{{Term|TMC|6|link}}") do not play anywhere else in the game. There are also two music tracks that play in-game that do not appear on the {{Term|TMC|Phonograph}}; the first plays in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} before {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} reach {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} to attend the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}} (a similar song to "{{Term|TMC|27|link}}"), and the second is a short fanfare that plays when {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} first leaps onto {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s head (an excerpt of the series' main theme, "{{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda (Theme)|link}}").
