{{Term/Store|Lenzo||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 258{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
In his earlier days, Lenzo sailed throughout the [[Great Sea]],<ref>{{Cite|You see, in my younger days I was tireless, sailing far and wide across the Great Sea, snapping pictographs wherever I went...|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> taking pictures of mystical beings and strange sights,<ref>{{Cite|This foul ship, veiled in mist and fading in and out of sight like something of another world... Yes, my young sir... 'Tis none other than the Ghost Ship!|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> but he has now settled on [[Windfall Island]]. Lenzo has been studying [[Pictograph]]s and everything about them ever since he was young.<ref>{{Cite|You see, young sir, I have been studying Picto Boxes since I was but a lad.|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> When [[Link]] first meets him, Lenzo is reluctant to trust him due to the fact that one of his [[Picto Box]]es was stolen from him,<ref>{{Cite|Once, long ago, I rented a room out to a traveler for a single night. The next dawn, my precious Picto Box was stolen...|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> so prompts the young hero to leave. However, when Link shows him the Picto Box he found, the pictograph master urges him to look around his gallery upstairs, and even asks him to become his [[Lenzo's Research Assistant|Research Assistant]] to develop a box with "incredible pictography capabilities."<ref>{{Cite|My lifelong dream of creating a box with incredible pictography capabilities is at long last nearing fruition. Young sir, I hesitate to ask, but... Could you perchance become my research assistant?|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref>

After Link helps him with his research material, Lenzo tells him that he has been researching pictographs with color.<ref>{{Cite|My assistant, Link! Allow me a brief query. Do you perchance know about pictographs with color? I have researched this concept for many years, and at long last I understand it! A stronger light is needed to imbue color in the pictograph. No normal light will do. In fact, normal light must be overcome! Color pictography requires a light of the very brightest intensity!|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> If Link brings him a [[Forest Firefly]] from the [[Forest Haven]], Lenzo will improve the young hero's Picto Box and make it so that it can take color pictures.<ref>{{Cite|...Why, I feared the secret light had been extinguished forever! Is that truly one of the long-fabled Forest Fireflies?! Oh, glory! [...] For your efforts, I shall place this one inside your box! Come on now, hand me your Picto Box, if only for a moment...|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh-ho! Now, those pictographs of yours that have forever lacked the scintillating palette of life should henceforth show the world as it was meant to be in living color!|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> In {{TWWHD}}, the Forest Firefly is not required, as Lenzo gives Link the Picto Box directly after completing all the research tasks.<ref>{{Cite|You have completed all of the tasks I set you to. My doubts about you have been all cleared up! I shall hereby be proud to call you my assistant, Link! And... No assistant of mine will be seen going around taking pictographs in monochrome! Not in a million years! So here. Allow me to present you with this Picto Box.|Lenzo|TWWHD}}</ref>

Lenzo also sells Legendary Pictographs,<ref>{{Cite|I am willing to pass on to you one of these treasured Legendary Pictographs.|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> which Link can buy for 50 [[Rupee]]s per photo.<ref>{{Cite|This is, after all, a very precious thing. I cannot just give it away for free, can I? What do you say, young sir? Will you pay 50 Rupees for it?|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> [[Minenco]], the woman who has a keen interest in pictography, can be often found discussing the topic with Lenzo.

==Nomenclature==
Lenzo's Japanese name, Genzō, is a pun on the Japanese word for {{wp|Photographic processing|film developing}}, {{Romanize|現像|Genzō}}. Likewise, his English name is likely a reference to a {{wp|camera lens}}. His Japanese title is also a reference to the {{wp|magic lantern}}, an early image projector.
