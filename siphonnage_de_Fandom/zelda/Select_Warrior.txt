The {{Term/Store|Select Warrior||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE}} menu,{{Exp Game|HW}}<ref>{{Cite|Select Warrior|N/A|HWL}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|HWDE Red|Select Warrior}}}}|N/A|HWDE}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Character Select||Series, Soulcalibur II}} menu,{{Exp Game|Soulcalibur II}}<ref>{{Cite|CHARACTER SELECT|N/A|Soulcalibur II}}</ref> is a recurring interface in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Overview==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub|HWDE}}
In Hyrule Warriors, players will be able to choose a specific amount of characters from the ones that are available to use in the Select Warrior screen.

==={{SCII|-}}===
{{Stub|Soulcalibur II}}

=== ''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity'' ===
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, once the player presses the [[Scenario]] they would like to play. Although there is no title for the page, when the player first presses the characters, the game will give an introduction as to how to change the character. Most of the time, the characters that can be chosen are 1 to 2 characters.
