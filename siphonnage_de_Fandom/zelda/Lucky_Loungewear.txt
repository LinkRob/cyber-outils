The {{Term/Store|Lucky Loungewear||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Lucky Loungewear improves luck on the battlefield. You'll occasionally evade attacks without even trying!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Lucky Loungewear}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|TFH|Sweet Shroom|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Blin Bling|link}}, one {{Term|TFH|Gohma's Eye|link}} and 200 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. When wearing the {{Term|TFH|Lucky Loungewear}}, {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} will automatically dodge 25% of hits. Instead of taking damage, {{Term|TFH|Link}} will simply bounce off of the enemy or trap while the word "Lucky!" appears onscreen and a chime is heard. The {{Term|TFH|Lucky Loungewear}} does not protect him from falls, [[Quicksand]], or [[Lava]].
