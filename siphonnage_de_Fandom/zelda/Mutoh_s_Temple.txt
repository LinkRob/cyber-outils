{{Term/Store|Mutoh's Temple||Series, PH}} is the seventh dungeon in {{PH}}.<ref name="E 156">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 156}}</ref>

==Entrance to the Temple==
The temple is located on the northeastern part of the [[Isle of Ruins]], and serves as the location of the pure metal [[Aquanine]]. The entrance is available for the one who encounters the four knights of the [[Cobble Kingdom]] (one of them resting on the [[Isle of the Dead]]) and solves an ancient riddle.<ref name="E 156"/>

==Themes and Navigation==
[[File:Hammer Switch Corridor.png|thumb|left|Link pressing several switches with the [[Hammer]], as he moves through a passage.]]
The temple includes numerous obstacles that can be harmful for the careless explorer, including hidden trap holes, arrow shooters, rolling rocks, and water that can be risen or lowered manually. Anyone surviving these traps will find the temple's item, which is the [[hammer]]. This handy tool is useful to press rusty switches, and many of the puzzles revolve around it, especially favoring puzzles which involve flipping tiles.<ref>{{Cite|The path will open when mighty attacks change the color of the tiles.|Stone Tablet|PH}}</ref> The temple's boss is [[Eox]].

The dungeon's design is richer and more detailed than that of the other temples in the [[Isle of Ruins]], and is also one of the largest in the game in terms of size (only surpassed by the [[Temple of the Ocean King]]). The temple contains the sleeping spirit of [[King Mutoh|Mutoh]], the king of the ancient [[Cobble|Cobble Kingdom]], who grants Link the Aquanine as thanks from helping them rest in peace.

It is optional whether Link completes this temple before or after the [[Goron Temple]] and/or the {{Term|PH|Temple of Ice|link}}.
