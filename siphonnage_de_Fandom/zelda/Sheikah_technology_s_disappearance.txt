== Intro ==
In [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild|Breath of the Wild]], [[Ancient Technology|Sheikah Technology]] is incredibly prominent, and after the events of the game, with the threat of [[Calamity Ganon|Ganon]] neutralised, the [[Ancient Technology|Sheikah Technology]] should be safe to use. However, in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]], a game set only a few years later, it is completely absent, other than a few exceptions: 

* [[Skyview Tower|Skyview Towers]]. These are quite obviously made using [[Ancient Technology|Sheikah Technology]].
* The [[Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|Ancient Tech Labs]]. These have remnants of the [[Ancient Technology]].
* [[Lookout Landing]]. The switch that activates the [[Skyview Tower|Skyview towers]] is made from recycled [[Sheikah]] Tech
{{Theory}}

== Theories ==

=== The Dismantlement Theory ===
This theory suggests that the [[Hyrulean|people of Hyrule]] destroyed the [[Ancient Technology|Sheikah Tech]] to stop [[Calamity Ganon]] from taking control over the tech, should he return. This theory is by far the most popular. It falls apart when you realise that they destroyed titanic machines, being the [[Divine Beast|Divine Beasts]], in only a few years, along with the gigantic towers surrounding [[Hyrule Castle]]. Another point against this is that there is nowhere that they could have put the dismantled parts. 

=== The Failsafe Theory ===
[[File:FldObj OpField PurificationUnit A 01.jpg|border|thumb|197x197px|The Purification Unit]]
This can also explain why [[Ancient Technology|the Technology]] is absent in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]. It promotes the idea that when [[Ganondorf]] blasted the Inverted Pyramid above the Imprisoning Chamber, it caused the [[Ancient Technology|Sheikah Tech]] to vanish to attempt to maintain the seal. This means that after Ganondorf’s imprisonment, the Sheikah constructed it as a failsafe, should he break free. Known in the files as “FldObj_OpField_PurificationUnit_A_01” The term “[[Purification Unit]]" implies that the [[Gloom]] leaking from [[Ganondorf]] is somehow being purified into something else.

=== The Monk Theory ===
This theory suggests that the [[Monk|Monks]] and [[Ancient Technology|Sheikah Tech]] are intertwined. After all the [[monk]]’s bodies crumbled away, including [[Monk Maz Koshia|Maz Koshia]], the technology simply ceased to exist or disappeared in some other way such as crumbling in a similar way as the Monks do.
