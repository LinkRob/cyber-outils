'''Vapora'''<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|To remove the blue rays of light blocking the way north, Zelda must kill the Vapora.|Om de blauwe lichtstralen te verwijderen die de toegang naar het noorden blokkeren, moet Zelda de Vapora doden.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 3|issue= 12|date= December 1996|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=41}}</ref> are the mini-bosses of the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Air}} in {{ZA}}. They are cloud-like monsters that take the shape of a face.

==Characteristics==
{{Term|ZA|Vapora}} is first encountered early in the Shrine after the stretch of falling {{Plural|ZA|Ice Crystal|link}}. Another one is encountered at the Shrine's half-point, where it must be defeated to dispel a {{Term|ZA|Barrier|link}} blocking the path ahead. {{Term|ZA|Vapora}}'s only method of attack is to move around the room and release small clouds at {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|link}} to hurt her. The first {{Term|ZA|Vapora}} will only fire one cloud aimed at Zelda, whereas the second {{Term|ZA|Vapora}} will release three clouds that spread out as they travel. These clouds can be blocked with the {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Magic Shield|display=Magic Shield|link}} however. The enemies have high endurance, and take several hits of the {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Wand|display=Wand|link}} to defeat. Their large size and aimed attacks, as well as the Shrine's slippery floors, can make them a difficult enemy to fight. While projectile {{Term|ZA|Spells of Zelda's Adventure|display=Spells|link}} can be used to attack them from afar, it is not as effective as the Wand.

Once Zelda defeats the second {{Term|ZA|Vapora}}, it leaves behind the {{Term|ZA|Items in Zelda's Adventure|section=Rug|display=Rug|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
Vapora is derived from {{Wp|vapor}}.
