'''''The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes — Official Digital Strategy Guide''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TFH}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The guide has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes — Official Guide]]," containing a complete walkthrough of {{TFH}}, detailed maps, strategies for {{Plural|TFH|Boss|link}}, and a listing of collectibles.

It is served using Prima's in-browser digital guide service and, unlike most other Prima eGuides, doesn't have sample chapters.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Prima Games|url=https://eguides.primagames.com/games/legend-zelda-tri-force-heroes/guides/the-legend-of-zelda-tri-force-heroes-eguide|site=eguides.primagames.com|title=The Legend of Zelda: TriForce Heroes Official Digital Strategy Guide|retrieved=October 1, 2022}}</ref>
