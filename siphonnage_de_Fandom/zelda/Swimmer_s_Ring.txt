The {{Term/Store|Swimmer's Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Swimmer's Ring<br/>Swimming speed {{Icon|OoA Up Arrow}}|Inventory|OoA}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Swimmer's Ring is received from the [[Master Diver]] at [[Sunken City]] after telling him the [[Diver Secret]] and passing his test. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. When worn, it increases [[Link]]'s swimming speed.<ref name="Description"/> The Swimmer's Ring can be obtained in {{OoA}} by telling the secret received from the Master Diver to [[Farore]], or by transferring it by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
