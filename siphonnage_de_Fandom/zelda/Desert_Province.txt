The '''Desert Province''' is a province of the {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} overworld in {{TP}}.{{Name Ref|TPHD}}

==Features and Overview==
The province is only accessible after {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} has given {{Term|TPHD|Auru's Memo|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Fyer|link}} of {{Term|TPHD|Lake Hylia|link}}. This will make the {{Term|TPHD|Oasis Flight|link}} available to the young hero, and while he will only be able to go the first time for free, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} will have to pay a fee from then on. Other than {{Term|TPHD|Warping|link}} to the Desert Province, taking the {{Term|TPHD|Oasis Flight}} is the only manner in which {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can reach the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that prison is condemned, and even the road leading to the desert is impassable.|Auru|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oasis flight...right? So here you go, one of our secret options not available to the general public...|Fyer|TP}}</ref>

The Desert Province consists entirely of the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert}}. It is home to the Arbiter's Grounds and the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror Chamber|link}}, through which {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can enter the {{Term|TPHD|Twilight Realm|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals|link}} is located here as well at the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Mesa|link}}; however, its entrance is initially blocked by a piece of the {{Term|TPHD|Bridge of Eldin|link}} that must be removed before being able to access the interior of the cave. One of the six {{Plural|TPHD|Owl Statue|link}} is also found in this province.
