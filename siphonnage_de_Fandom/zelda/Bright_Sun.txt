The {{Term/Store|Bright Sun||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The [[Bright Sun]] is the reward for completing the [[Poison Moth's Lair]], and is guarded by [[Mothula]]. It is the third of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Young shoots grow quickly under the warm rays of the Bright Sun!"<ref>"Young shoots grow quickly under the warm rays of the [[Bright Sun]]!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons)]]</ref>
