The Dawn of '''A New Day''', is the fourth day in {{MM}}, the events of which are shown during the ending credits, taking place after the [[Final Day]].{{Name Ref|MM, MM3D}}

==As a Bad Ending==
{{Main|Terminan Apocalypse}}

If [[Link]] fails to defeat [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]] before the [[Moon]] ends up falling towards [[Termina]], a cutscene showing its destruction appears.

==As the Ending Credits==
If Link succeeds in destroying the main antagonist Majora and save Termina, during the dawn of A New Day, several characters Link has helped in the past get some much needed closure. The first event showcased is that of the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}}, now free of the power of Majora's Mask, reconciling with the {{Term|MM3D|Four Giants|link}}. During this scene, the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}} also befriends Link (who apparently remembers him from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''), and the {{Term|MM3D|Happy Mask Salesman|link}} recovers Majora's Mask. After bidding the [[Hero of Time]] farewell, the {{Term|MM3D|Happy Mask Salesman}} enigmatically vanishes. Link himself leaves via [[Epona]] to seemingly enter Clock Town after [[Tatl]] sternly tells him to go on about his business (before becoming emotional afterwards and saying "Thank you").

The following is a list of events as they occur chronologically during the Ending Credits of ''Majora's Mask''.

*{{Term|MM3D|The Indigo-Go's|link}} perform the jam session song written by {{Term|MM3D|Japas|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Mikau|link}} for the {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time|link}} in the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar|link}} Latte. Link appears as {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link|link}} to partake in the performance. Among those seen enjoying this portion of the show are {{Term|MM3D|Honey and Darling|link}}, and the {{Term|MM3D|Rosa Sisters|link}}. The incorrect sync of the music with the character animations make it seem as though {{Term|MM3D|Japas}} is playing the guitar instead of the bass.
*A boat of four {{Term|MM3D|Gerudo|display=Gerudo Pirates|link}} are seen seafaring on the waters of {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay|link}} near the {{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab|link}} under a rainbow adorned sky. The captain of the skiff is pointing forwards during their first pass by the screen but, humorously, is pointing backwards during their second pass going the opposite direction. The other pirates are shown patrolling their {{Term|MM3D|Pirates' Fortress|display=Fortress|link}} as usual.
*The disembodied, ghostly heads of {{Term|MM3D|Igos du Ikana|link}} and his {{Term|MM3D|King's Lackeys|display=Lackeys|link}} are seen in the throneroom of {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Castle|link}}, singing and arguing, respectively.
*A part of {{Term|MM3D|Goron Village|link}} is shown, though nobody is seen there.
*The inhabitants of the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Palace|link}}, minus the {{Term|MM3D|Deku Butler|link}}, are seen standing cordially with the {{Term|MM3D|Monkey|link}} in their throne room. The {{Term|MM3D|Deku Princess|link}} curtsies, and the {{Term|MM3D|Monkey}} crosses his arms (waves in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'').
*''If the {{Term|MM3D|Postman's Hat|link}} is collected:'' The {{Term|MM3D|Postman|link}}, free of his {{Term|MM3D|Postman's Hat|display=Hat|link}} and backpack, is seen running freely through {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field|link}}'s eastern region.
*''If {{Term|MM3D|Kamaro's Mask|link}} is collected:'' The {{Term|MM3D|Rosa Sisters}} dance at the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar}} Latte using the form taught to them by {{Term|MM3D|Link}} using {{Term|MM3D|Kamaro's Mask}}. The visible audience is only a {{Term|MM3D|Soldier|display=Guard|link}}, presumably the one from {{Term|MM3D|West Clock Town|link}} who used to watch them practice.
*''If the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy's Mask|link}} is collected:'' The five {{Plural|MM3D|Great Fairy|link}} of {{Term|MM3D|Termina}} are shown floating together in their complete forms.
*''If {{Term|MM3D|Romani's Mask|link}} is collected:'' {{Term|MM3D|Romani|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}} are shown at their ranch. {{Term|MM3D|Romani}} practices shooting her bow, popping three ghost-shaped balloons, earning much praise from her older sister, and she triumphantly runs a hand through her hair.
*''If the {{Term|MM3D|Blast Mask|link}} is collected:'' The {{Term|MM3D|Bomb Shop Owner|link}} and his [[Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|mother]] are seen embracing in {{Term|MM3D|West Clock Town}} in front of their [[Bomb Shop|Shop]].
*''If the {{Term|MM3D|Bunny Hood|link}} is collected:'' {{Term|MM3D|Grog|link}} is seen with his gaggle of [[Cucco|Cuccos]].
*''If the {{Term|MM3D|Troupe Leader's Mask|link}} is collected:'' The {{Term|MM3D|Indigo-Go's}} perform again, but {{Term|MM3D|Zora Link}} is absent (presumably now leaving the land). Among those watching are some {{Plural|MM3D|Zora|link}}, some {{Plural|MM3D|Carpenter|link}}, and {{Term|MM3D|Gorman|link}}.
*''If the {{Term|MM3D|Bremen Mask|link}} is collected:'' A final sweep of the {{Term|MM3D|Milk Bar}} shows a large crowd listening to the musical talents of {{Term|MM3D|Guru-Guru|link}}, including a Guard, {{Term|MM3D|Shiro|link}}, some {{Plural|MM3D|Zora}}, a {{Term|MM3D|Carpenter}}, {{Term|MM3D|Gabora|link}}, some {{Plural|MM3D|Cuccoo|link}}, and {{Term|MM3D|Honey and Darling}}.
*{{Term|MM3D|Pamela|link}} runs into the arms of her {{Term|MM3D|Pamela's Father|display=Father|link}} outside of their {{Term|MM3D|Music Box House|link}}, rejoicing in a {{Term|MM3D|Gibdo|link}}-free {{Term|MM3D|Ikana|link}}, though the river is dry even if it was restored on the final cycle. ''(While it may be assumed that this scene is linked to collecting the Gibdo Mask, it is not, and said mask is mandatory to collect on a typical playthrough regardless).''
*''If the {{Term|MM3D|Couple's Mask|link}} is collected:'' At long last, {{Term|MM3D|Kafei|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}} have their {{Term|MM3D|Anju and Kafei's Wedding|display=wedding ceremony|link}}. Among those present to witness the union are {{Term|MM3D|Mayor Dotour|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Madame Aroma|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Mutoh|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Cremia}}, {{Term|MM3D|Captain Viscen|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Man from Curiosity Shop|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Anju's Mother|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Anju's Grandmother|link}}, and {{Term|MM3D|Tingle|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Anju}} is shown in her beautiful wedding dress wearing a string of pink pearls, but the adult {{Term|MM3D|Kafei}} is not shown (the camera strongly implies it is his point of view). {{Term|MM3D|Tingle}} cries and throws confetti.
*''If the [[Mask of Scents]] is collected:'' The {{Term|MM3D|Deku Butler}} is seen kneeling before the bizarre withered tree beneath the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Tower|link}}, quivering. Many believe this tree may in fact be his missing dead {{Term|MM3D|Deku Butler's Son|display=Son|link}}.
*Finally, Link, no longer in Termina, is shown riding away on {{Term|MM3D|Epona|link}} through the foggy woods where he first encountered the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}}, searching for [[Navi]] once again. Upon a stump sitting under a ring of light is a primitive drawing of Link, the {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid}}, {{Term|MM3D|Tatl}} and {{Term|MM3D|Tael|link}}, and in the background the Four Giants, waving, by the Skull Kid himself as a remembrance for the young [[Hero of Time]]. {{Term|MM3D|Saria's Song|link}} is briefly heard after a few moments, then the game ends.

== Trivia ==

* Unlike the [[First Day|First]], [[Second Day|Second]], and [[Final Day|Final]] Days, which their introduction screens shows a black background with white letters, the New Day have this in the opposite manner. It also does not show what time remains due to the Moon being sent away (or destroyed in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'').
* During the credits, if the aforementioned Masks are not collected, the screen will be in black with the required item shown until the next sequence.[[File:MM 4th Day Glitch Moon.png|thumb|262x262px|The Moon during the 4th Day glitch]]
* Through a [[Glitches in Majora's Mask|glitch]], specifically the "4th Day Glitch", it is possible to explore Termina in the New Day with unlimited time, though when executed, the [[Moon]] will appear extremely far away (its model turned tiny in reality), and Clock Town will be empty, therefore disabling all events related to the [[Bombers' Notebook|Bomber's Notebook]]. The sky and weather will appear the same as in the [[Terminan Apocalypse]] regardless of day or night, except that the clouds do not swirl.
** When this glitch is active and Link is underwater, bizarre colors can be seen due to the nature of the environment.
* As ''Majora's Mask'' was originally planned to have a seven-day cycle rather than three, it is likely that the New Day is the eighth day during the game's development. In addition, if debugs are used to reveal it, it was originally going to be called "'''Thursday'''".
