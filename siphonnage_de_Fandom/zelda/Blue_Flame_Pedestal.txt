{{Term/Store|Blue Flame Pedestal|Blue Flame Pedestals|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 289}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} are a special type of {{Term|BotW|Torchlight|link}} that can only be lit with a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, which can be retrieved from the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}}. However, in {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}, these {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestals}} serve as the sources of {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame}}. Their lights can be extinguished by {{Plural|BotW|Ice Arrow|link}} or with a gust of wind from a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}}.

The paths between both the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} and the nearest {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} are lined with {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}}. These {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestals}} have a protective roof, which keeps the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} safe from {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} or other environmental hazards. {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} make use of the {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} to aid in his retrieval of the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} for the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab's}} {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Blue flame}} makes it sound fancy, but it's really just the same as a regular flame. If it gets wet, that's that. It'll help to light the village {{Color|BotW Blue|lanterns}} you'll see on your way back here and to {{Color|BotW Red|avoid steep inclines}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

The only {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that utilizes {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} as part of its trial is {{Term|BotW|Shora Hah Shrine|link}}, located on the {{Term|BotW|Isle of Rabac|link}}. Within, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must light several {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} in order to progress. In addition, the interior of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}} is dark when {{Term|BotW|Link}} first enters, save for two lit {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}}. He must use these {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame}} to navigate the darkness and activate {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.
