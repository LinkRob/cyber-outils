{{Term/Store|Thomas||Series, OoA}} is a character in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 254}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Thomas}} was referred to as the '''Thin Man''' in {{Guide|OoA|Nintendo}} and the '''Unbalanced Man''' in the {{OoA|Wessel}}.<ref name="Thin Man"/><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= {{OoA|Wessel|-}}|publisher= Scholastic|page= 30}}</ref> However, as these contradict the name given in {{E}}, they are not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Biography==
[[File:OoA Thomas Sprite.gif|left]]
Thomas resides in the basement of the [[Middle House]] the center of [[Symmetry Village]] in the past. He seems to be very self-conscious about his slight build and has somewhat of an obsession with "getting the girls."<ref>{{Cite|I'm so skinny... I wish I could change...|Thomas|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Get the girls... Get the girls...|Thomas|OoA}}</ref>

Thomas wishes to gain weight by working out. However, since he only has enough training equipment for one side,<ref>{{Cite|I'm so skinny... I wish I could change...I know! I'll work out! Except... I have only one Dumbbell...|Thomas|OoA}}</ref> and training one arm at a time goes against his policy,<ref>{{Cite|Working one arm at a time... It goes against my policy...|Thomas|OoA}}</ref> he will be unable to do so. [[Link]] can give him the [[Dumbbell]] received from [[Mamamu Yan]] as part of the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}, allowing Thomas to exercise both his arms while keeping the town symmetrical. In return, he gives Link the [[Cheesy Mustache]].
