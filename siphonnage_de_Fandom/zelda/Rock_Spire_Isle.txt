The island is a long slender island with the path to a [[Secret Cave]] being blocked by rocks. To destroy the rocks, [[Link]] needs a couple of [[Bomb]]s and good timing when throwing them; alternatively, Link can use his ship's cannon to blow them up from afar. At the end of the path there is a grotto and, inside, Link must use a [[Boko Stick]] to light up the room and destroy all the [[Keese]]. Once the last one is destroyed, a [[Treasure Chest]] will appear containing Treasure Chart 37.

There is a somewhat different kind of [[Beedle]] shop around the isle called the "Masked Beedle" Shop. Link needs a wallet upgrade in order to buy most of the items Beedle sells here, which include a [[Heart Container|Piece of Heart]] (950 Rupees), [[Treasure Chart]] #4 (900 Rupees), and a Bottle (500 Rupees).

Two other Pieces of Heart can be obtained here. One of them is found by taking down two armed boats floating around the isles: one of them will drop 100 Rupees, and the other one will leave a Piece of Heart. The second one is found at the bottom of the sea by using Treasure Chart #2 from [[Windfall Island]].
