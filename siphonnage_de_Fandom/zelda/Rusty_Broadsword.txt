{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Broadsword}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} known to break easily due to their age.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can commonly found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Eldin Canyon|link}}, where they either have been abandoned or are wielded by low-level {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. One spawns in a stone on a small island in a lake near the {{Term|BotW|Temple of Time|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. They also have a chance to appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that spawn when the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Rock Octorok|link}} will inhale {{Plural|BotW|Rusty Broadsword}} when they are placed in front of them, spitting back out a cleaned counterpart.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref> There's a 50% chance for a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}, a 35% chance for a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Broadsword|link}}, a 10% chance for a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Broadsword|link}}, and a 5% chance for a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Broadsword}} are made of metal, so they can attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} and can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. If they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, they will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} do.

{{Plural|BotW|Rusty Broadsword}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} that have become deteriorated.<ref name="CaC"/>
