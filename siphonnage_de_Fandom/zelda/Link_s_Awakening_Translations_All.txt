The following page is a list of the names of characters, bosses, enemies, locations, and items appearing in {{LA}} in Japanese, English and German.
*Empty cells indicate there is no given name for the particular individual/location/item in that language.
*Many names in the German version are grammatically incorrect.
*The names of most enemies, bosses, and mini-bosses are never mentioned in-game. Their English and Japanese names are sourced from the official Nintendo Player's Guide and [[Shogakukan]] guide, respectively. The German names are sourced from the German version of the "[[wikipedia:Super Game Boy|Super Game Boy]] Player's Guide" published by the official {{smw|Club Nintendo}} magazine, the German equivalent of [[Nintendo Power]]. Club Nintendo and its editor in chief, Claude M. Moyse, are credited for the German localization of {{LA|-}}.
