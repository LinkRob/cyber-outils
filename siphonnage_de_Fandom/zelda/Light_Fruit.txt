{{Term/Store|Light Fruit|Light Fruits|Series, SS|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWL}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
Light Fruits only appear scattered throughout the [[Silent Realm]]s. Whenever [[Link]] collects one of these, [[Beacon]]s will appear over the locations of all [[Sacred Tears]] for 30 seconds.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|SS Gold|Light Fruit}}! This mysterious fruit enables you to see beacons over the positions of Sacred Tears for {{Color|SS Red|30 seconds}}!|N/A|SS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
In {{HW|L}}, Light Fruit appear as one of the Plant Fairy Food items, used in the [[My Fairy]] Dining Room to level up Fairy companions. It is associated with the element of {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Light}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
