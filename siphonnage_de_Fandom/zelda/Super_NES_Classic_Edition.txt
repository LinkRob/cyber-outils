The '''[[wikipedia:Super_NES_Classic_Edition|Super NES Classic Edition]]''', also known as '''SNES Classic''' or '''SNES Mini''', is a small-scale emulator-based version of the [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System]] with twenty-one pre-installed games, released in 2017 (original console was 1991), meant to be novelty item and a modern way to play older titles without an original Super NES.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The Super NES Classic Edition system<br/>looks and feels just like the original ’90s<br/>home console, except it’s super small.<br/>Play 20 classic Super NES games plus<br/>*gasp* the never-before-released Star<br/>Fox™ 2 game!|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 18, 2021|url= https://www.nintendo.com/super-nes-classic/|title= SNES Classic Edition - Official Site - Super Nintendo Entertainment System|site= Nintendo|type= }}</ref> It was manufactured and released by [[Nintendo]] for a MSRP of US$79.99 as a successor to the [[NES Classic Edition]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= <b>Only</b> US<b>$79.99</b><br/>{{Small|MSRP*}}|author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= November 18, 2021|url= https://www.nintendo.com/super-nes-classic/|title= SNES Classic Edition - Official Site - Super Nintendo Entertainment System|site= Nintendo|type= }}</ref> The Super NES Classic Edition is also famously known for being the first offical release of ''[[wikipedia:Star_Fox_2|Star Fox 2]]'', a completed, but previously-unreleased entry in the ''Star Fox'' series.
