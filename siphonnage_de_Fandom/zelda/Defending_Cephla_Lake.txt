{{Term/Store|Defending Cephla Lake||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defending Cephla Lake|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Defending Cephla Lake}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, with {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon|link}} as its pilot. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

For the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}'s objective is to defeat 3,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within four minutes. The different {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} include {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Hinox|link}}, many {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Wizzrobe|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Water Octorok|link}} scattered around the map. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 3,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
