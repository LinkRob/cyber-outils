The {{Term/Store|Cave of Ordeals||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an optional {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''Twilight Princes'''<br/>The Great Fairy is found in the Cave of Ordeals and reigns over all fairies as their queen.|book= E |page= 23{{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
[[File:Cave of Ordeals.jpg|thumb|right|300px|A floor inside the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals}}]]
The {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals}} is located below the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Mesa|link}}, and can be accessed any time after restoring the {{Term|TPHD|Bridge of Eldin|link}}, as [[Warping|warping]] the Bridge back to its original location will reveal the Cave's entrance. The {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} contains a total of fifty floors; however, to make it into the deeper floors, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} will need several [[Items in Twilight Princess|Items]] from further along in the game. Within the Cave, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} has to fight his way through hordes of enemies, and only by defeating each enemy within a floor will he be able to progress to the next floor.

Enemies here never drop {{Plural|TPHD|Heart|link}}, but they drop much more {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} than usual.

After every ten floors, there is a [[Great Fairy Fountain#Twilight Princess|Fountain]] in which the {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy|link}} appears and rewards {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s efforts by releasing {{Plural|TPHD|Fairy|link}} into the respective {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit Spring|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, Hero, to the Cave of Ordeals. I am the Great Fairy. I reign over all fairies of this realm as their queen. In praise of your efforts thus far, I will release fairies to Ordon Spring.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref> before offering to Warp him outside to the surface.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to give up the fight for now and go reequip yourself, I can return you to the surface. You need merely to speak to me.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref> Upon reaching the final floor, the {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy}} there will reward the young hero with a {{Term|TPHD|Bottle|link|display=Bottle}} full of {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears|link}}.<ref name="Fairy">{{Cite|It is impressive that you have made it this far. Your trust in your skills is your greatest asset. You cleared many ordeals... How impressive. In praise of your courage, I will give you this blessing. Drink this and you should be able to clear any difficulties that you might encounter ahead.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref> The {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy}} will also grant {{Term|TPHD|Link}} unlimited {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} from the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit Spring}}, but only when he does not already have some in a {{Term|TPHD|Bottle}}.<ref name="Tears">{{Cite|If ever you need my assistance again, visit any spring.|Great Fairy|TP}}</ref>

In addition to ordinary enemies, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can also find three {{Plural|TPHD|Imp Poe|link}} in total on certain floors. Once they are defeated, the {{Plural|TPHD|Imp Poe}} will no longer appear on their respective floors. Certain floors also contain recovery {{Plural|TPHD|Heart|link}} or valuable {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} which can be found using {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}'s [[Sense]]s.

===Rewards===
*Reaching the 10th floor releases {{Plural|TPHD|Fairy}} into {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Spring|link}}.
*Reaching the 20th floor releases {{Plural|TPHD|Fairy}} into {{Term|TPHD|Faron Spring|link}}.
*Reaching the 30th floor releases {{Plural|TPHD|Fairy}} into {{Term|TPHD|Eldin Spring|link}}.
*Reaching the 40th floor releases {{Plural|TPHD|Fairy}} into the {{Term|TPHD|Spring of the Spirit Lanayru|link}}.
*Reaching the 50th floor fills a {{Term|TPHD|Bottle|display=Bottle}} with {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}}.<ref name="Fairy"/> It also allows {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to collect more {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears}} by revisiting any of the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit Springs}}, so long as he does not already have some in a {{Term|TPHD|Bottle|display=Bottle}}.<ref name="Tears"/>

==Trivia==
*The Cave of Ordeals plays the same role as the [[Savage Labyrinth]] from {{TWW}}. The main difference being that the Cave of Ordeals requires the main items from the [[Lakebed Temple]], {{Term|TPHD|Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|link}}, [[Snowpeak Ruins]], [[Temple of Time]], and the [[City in the Sky]] in order to explore all of the floors and fully clear it. Another difference is that the Cave of Ordeals is completely optional, while the first 31 rooms of the Savage Labyrinth must be completed in order to progress in the game.
*For unknown reasons, [[Jovani]] knows of the Cave of Ordeals and that it is the source of Great Fairy Tears, of which he also has a bottle. He is the only character in the game to hint at its existence and the reward at the end.
*The second time completing the Cave, on the last ledge before the {{Term|TPHD|Great Fairy}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Postman|link}} can be found squatting down trying to read a {{Term|TPHD|Letter|link}}.
*If the appropriate floors are cleared, fairies will still appear at the appropriate springs during the ending credits.
*A cheat used for debugging purposes was accidentally left in the North American [[Wii]] 1.0 release of {{TP|-}}. If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} uses the {{Term|TPHD|Gale Boomerang|link}} to extinguish the {{Plural|TPHD|Torchlight|link}} on almost any floor of the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals}}, the following room's door will be opened without the need to defeat any enemies. This cheat was disabled in all other regions and revisions of the game.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Cave of Ordeals Debug Routine ||retrieved=Nov. 8, 2021 |url=https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Twilight_Princess/Debug_Content |title=The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess/Debug Content |site=The Cutting Room Floor}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|author=jgc8fan |published=Jan. 10, 2007 |retrieved=Nov. 8, 2021 |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cu54BFmwbzQ |title=Twilight Princess Cave of Ordeals Boomerang Trick |site= YouTube|type= }}</ref>
