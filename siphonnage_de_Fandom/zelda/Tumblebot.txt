'''Tumblebots''' are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Tumblebots are a variant of [[Bot]]s with a dark blue body and red eyes. They are found rolling around in the [[Plain of Andor]] and the [[Plain of Donora]]. They are one of the weakest enemies, and can be quickly defeated by [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. Tumblebots inflict only half a [[Heart]] of damage.
