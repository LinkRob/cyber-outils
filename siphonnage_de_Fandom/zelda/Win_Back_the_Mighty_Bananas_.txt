{{Term/Store|Win Back the Mighty Bananas!||HWAoC}} is a level 36 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Win Back the Mighty Bananas!|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Win Back the Mighty Bananas!}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}' objective is to capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} within eight minutes. The {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} are separated by a closed gate near the center of the map, giving the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} in the south access to one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} and the other having access to two. The northernmost {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is where the {{Plural|HWAoC|Mighty Bananas|link}} are found, protected by {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}}. The other northern {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalkoblin|link}}, and the southern one is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Stalizalfos|link}}. When an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} spawns in the south, and upon its defeat, the gate in the center of the map and in front of the last {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} open. When a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} enters the last {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} recaptures the second one and has to be defeated to capture it again. After all three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster}} appear, and the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} are given three minutes to defeat them. {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} spawns when they are, and the timer changes again to four minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeat him.
