The '''Volcanic Talus''' is the [[Sub-Boss]] of [[Summit Cave]] in {{EoW}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
The Volcanic Talus is a flame-covered variant of the [[Seismic Talus]] encountered during the [[A Mountainous Mystery]] [[Side Quest]], whose flames can be extinguished with water or similar effects.
