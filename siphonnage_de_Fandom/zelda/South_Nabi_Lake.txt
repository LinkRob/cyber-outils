{{Term/Store|South Nabi Lake||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''South Nabi Lake'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
South Nabi Lake is located in [[West Necluda]]. At the southwest part of the Lake, there is a small wooden dock with a [[Raft]]. East of this Raft is a skull-shaped {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}}.

A metal [[Treasure Chest]] containing [[Opal]] can be found submerged next to the dock. Another sunken Chest can be found between the dock and the small island, this one containing a [[Soldier's Bow]]. Two additional Chests hidden in the water can be found east of the island. One contains a [[Soldier's Spear]] while the other houses an [[Amber]].

There is a small elevated island in the middle of the lake that has a single [[Rock]] placed in the center. A [[Korok]] can be found at this [[Natural Hiding Spot]].
