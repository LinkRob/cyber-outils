{{Term/Store|Mount|Mounts|Series|plural}} are a recurring mechanic in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref}} They are {{Plural|Series|Animal|link}} or enemies whose movements are manually controlled for the purposes of [[Transportation]], combat, or playing {{Plural|Series|Mini-Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 205}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Riding that horse would be much faster than hoofing it, so let's try and grab it! A wild horse like that, you probably {{Color|TP Red|mount}} with {{Icon|TPHD A}} and then hang on for dear life with {{Icon|TPHD Left Arrow}}{{Icon|TPHD L Stick}}{{Icon|TPHD Right Arrow}} until you can get it to run straight. Simple!|Midna|TP}}</ref> Some {{Plural|Series|Mount}} can be summoned with a special {{Term|Series|Call|link}}. In some games, {{Term|Series|Link|link}} can unseat an enemy's {{Term|Series|Mount}} and use it himself. In other games, he can only use his own.

{{Term|Series|Epona|link}} is the most common of {{Term|Series|Link}}'s personal {{Plural|Series|Mount}}. In general, flying by {{Term|Series|Cucco|link}} is the most frequently recurring form of animal-based transportation in {{TLoZ|Series}}. 

==Appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Flying Rooster}}
In {{LA}}, {{Term|LA|Link}} revives the {{Term|LA|Flying Rooster|link}}. It then follows the hero around {{Term|LA|Koholint Island|link}}. Picking up the {{Term|LA|Flying Rooster}} causes {{Term|LA|Link}} to fly just above the ground, allowing him to retrieve the {{Term|LA|Bird Key|link}} in {{Term|LA|Tal Tal Heights|link}}. When {{Term|LA|Link}} completes {{Term|LA|Eagle's Tower}}, he can no longer use the {{Term|LA|Flying Rooster}} – it has gone to live in the {{Term|LA|Henhouse|link}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|Cucco, Horse}}
In {{OoT}}, when {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} (child or adult) jumps off a ledge carrying a {{Term|OoT3D|Cucco|link}}, he will glide slowly downward. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can use this technique to gather the {{Term|OoT3D|Cucco Lady|link}}'s {{Plural|OoT3D|Cucco}} in {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Village|link}} for a {{Term|OoT3D|Bottle|link}}. Lone {{Plural|OoT3D|Cucco}} at {{Term|OoT3D|Zora's River|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Valley|link}} can be used to obtain {{Plural|OoT3D|Piece of Heart|link}} nearby.

Adult {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can mount the saddled {{Plural|OoT3D|Horse|link}} at {{Term|OoT3D|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}. A {{Term|OoT3D|Horse}} canters when its rider digs their heels into its sides. It gallops when whipped. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can whip his {{Term|OoT3D|Horse}} up to 6 times in succession to maintain a gallop. The number of remaining whippings is represented as {{Plural|OoT3D|Carrot|link}} which regenerate over time. A galloping {{Term|OoT3D|Horse}} can jump over fences, but only if approaching the fence head-on.<ref>{{Cite|Approach the horse quietly and stand by its side. Press {{Icon|OoT3D A}} to mount up. Use {{Icon|OoT3D Circle Pad}} to make it run. Once it starts running, press {{Icon|OoT3D A}} to tap it with the whip. It will speed up! When you want to jump over a fence, accelerate and hit it head on! If you try to jump over a fence at an angle, the horse won't like it, and it won't jump. After the horse comes to a complete stop, press {{Icon|OoT3D A}} to dismount. Now, have fun!|Ingo|OoT3D}}</ref>

{{Term|OoT3D|Link}} wins {{Term|OoT3D|Epona|link}} from {{Term|OoT3D|Ingo|link}} by beating him in two horseback races. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} escapes {{Term|OoT3D|Lon Lon Ranch}} on {{Term|OoT3D|Epona}}'s back by making her jump over the outer fence. {{Term|OoT3D|Epona}} can also jump across the broken bridge over {{Term|OoT3D|Gerudo Valley|link}}. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} needs her in order to play at the {{Term|OoT3D|Horseback Archery Range|link}} and to hunt {{Plural|OoT3D|Big Poe|link}}, who only appear while {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} is on horseback. {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} can draw his {{Term|OoT3D|Bow|link}} while on horseback, but cannot use his other weapons. He is also invincible while mounted on {{Term|OoT3D|Epona}}. 

{{Term|OoT3D|Princess Zelda|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Impa|link}} ride a white {{Term|OoT3D|Horse}} out of {{Term|OoT3D|Castle Town}} as {{Term|OoT3D|Ganondorf|link}} purses them on a black stallion. {{Term|OoT3D|Phantom Ganon|link}} rides an identical stallion.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Cucco, Epona, Snapper (Turtle)}}
In {{MM}}, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} rides a young {{Term|MM3D|Epona}} without a saddle, which he was unable to do in {{OoT|-}}. {{Term|MM3D|Skull Kid|link}} steals {{Term|MM3D|Epona}} from Link in the {{Term|MM3D|Lost Woods|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must retrieve her from {{Term|MM3D|Romani Ranch|link}} in order to jump the fences in {{Term|MM3D|Termina Field|link}} barring the entrances to {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Region|link}} and {{Term|MM3D|Ikana Region|link}}. {{Term|MM3D|Link}} also needs her in order to race the {{Plural|Series|Gorman Brothers|link}} at the {{Term|MM3D|Gorman Track|link}}. During the {{Term|MM3D|Protect the Milk!|link}} event, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} defends {{Term|MM3D|Cremia|link}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Carriage|link}} against the masked {{Plural|Series|Gorman Brothers}} as they attempt to attack the {{Term|MM3D|Carriage}} on horseback.

One of the {{Term|MM3D|Bombers Secret Society of Justice|link|display=Bombers}} in {{Term|MM3D|West Clock Town|link}} uses a {{Term|MM3D|Cucco}} to glide away from {{Term|MM3D|Link}} when playing {{Term|MM3D|Hide-and-Seek|link}}. 

The {{Term|MM3D|Sub-Boss}} {{Term|MM3D|Gekko|link}} mounts is a {{Term|MM3D|Snapper (Turtle)|link}}.

==={{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}}===
{{Main|Animal Companion, Flying Cucco}}
In {{OoA}}, {{Term|OoA|Link}} mounts one of the three {{Plural|OoA|Animal Companion|link}} to traverse the {{Term|OoA|Nuun Highlands|link}}. Each {{Term|OoA|Animal Companion}} has an attack move as well as a special ability. {{Term|OoA|Ricky|link}} can leap up cliffs and over holes, {{Term|OoA|Moosh|link}} can fly, and {{Term|OoA|Dimitri|link}} can swim against currents and up waterfalls.

In {{OoS}}, {{Term|OoS|Link}} mounts all three {{Plural|OoS|Animal Companion}}, but as in {{OoA|-}} he can only summon the one whose {{Term|OoS|Flute}} he owns ({{Term|OoS|Ricky's Flute|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Moosh's Flute|link}}, {{Term|OoS|Dimitri's Flute|link}}). Their special abilities are needed to traverse {{Term|OoS|Holodrum Plain|link}} ({{Term|OoS|Ricky}}), {{Term|OoS|Mt. Cucco|link}} ({{Term|OoS|Moosh}}), {{Term|OoS|Sunken City|link}} ({{Term|OoS|Dimitri}}), and {{Term|OoS|Natzu|link}} (whichever one Link can {{Term|OoS|Call|link}}). 
 
In {{OoS|-}}, {{Term|OoS|Link}} also uses a {{Term|OoS|Flying Cucco|link}} (similar to the {{Term|LA|Flying Rooster}} from {{LA|-}}) to reach the eastern summit of {{Term|OoS|Mt. Cucco}} where the {{Term|OoS|Spring Banana|link}} tree is located.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Main|Epona}}
In {{Term|FSA|The Field|link}} stage, the {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} instantaneously mount {{Plural|FSA|Horse}} when one of them picks up a {{Term|FSA|Carrot|link}}. The {{Plural|FSA|Horse}} are needed to jump fences and can also trample enemies and {{Term|FSA|Grass|link}}. They disappear after a certain amount of time,{{Clarify|FSA}} unless the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} pick up another {{Term|FSA|Carrot}} before then. 

{{Plural|FSA|Horse}} also appear in the {{Term|FSA|Mini-Game}} {{Term|FSA|Bucking Broncos|link}}. 

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Main|Bullbo, Cucco, Epona, Kargarok, Golden Cucco, Twilit Carrier Kargarok}}
{{Incomplete|type=section|reason=Bullbo mechanics}}
In {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} mounts {{Term|TPHD|Epona}} to herd {{Plural|TPHD|Goat|link}} at {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Ranch|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Epona}} disappears when a band of {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin|link}} mounted on {{Plural|TPHD|Bullbo|link}} attack {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}. Their leader, {{Term|TPHD|King Bulblin|link}}, mounts {{Term|TPHD|Lord Bullbo|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Epona}} reappears in {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village|link}} after throwing off a pair of {{Term|TPHD|Bulblin}} riders. Link subdues his frenzied horse and regains the ability to {{Term|TPHD|Call|link}} her with {{Term|TPHD|Horse Grass|link}}, and later the {{Term|TPHD|Horse Call|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can draw his {{Term|TPHD|Bow}} and swing his {{Term|TPHD|Sword}} while mounted on {{Term|TPHD|Epona}}. Unlike previous games, he is not invincible while mounted and can even be knocked off {{Term|TPHD|Epona}} by an attack. Likewise, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} can knock {{Term|TPHD|Bulblin}} riders off their {{Term|TPHD|Bullbo}}. When {{Term|TPHD|King Bulblin}} kidnaps {{Term|TPHD|Colin|link}}, a mounted battle ensues between {{Term|TPHD|Link}} and {{Term|TPHD|King Bulblin}} defended by {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} then defeats {{Term|TPHD|King Bulblin}} in a joust. Later on in the game, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} again mounts {{Term|TPHD|Epona}} to fend off mounted {{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin}} attacking the {{Term|TPHD|Wagon|link}} carrying {{Term|TPHD|Telma|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Kakariko Village}}. At the end of the game, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} fights {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf|link}} on horseback. {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} summons {{Plural|TPHD|Phantom Rider|link}} to attack {{Term|TPHD|Link}}.

{{Plural|TPHD|Bulblin}} always ride two to a {{Term|TPHD|Bullbo}} — one steers the animal while an archer sits behind. In {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert|link}}, where {{Term|TPHD|Link}} cannot call {{Term|TPHD|Epona}}, Link can ride {{Plural|TPHD|Bullbo}} and use their charging ability to destroy the wooden fences in the {{Term|TPHD|Bulblin Camp}}.

{{Plural|TPHD|Cucco|link}} can once again be used to glide to otherwise unreachable places.{{Where|TP}} In particular, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} uses the {{Term|TPHD|Golden Cucco}} to cross the gorge in {{Term|TPHD|Faron Woods|link}}, in order to reach the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove|link}} on the other side. The {{Term|TPHD|Flight-by-Fowl|link}} {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game}} features {{Term|TPHD|Cucco}}-gliding as well.

After {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} kills the {{Term|TPHD|Twilit Carrier Kargarok|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Shadow Bulblin|link}} rider, {{Term|TPHD|Midna}} can mount the beast and steer it up {{Term|TPHD|Zora's River|link}} with {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} in its claws. The same is true for the giant {{Term|TPHD|Kargarok|link}} used to play {{Term|TPHD|Fruit-Pop-Flight|link}}.
