The {{Term/Store|Road to Mountain Village||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an area in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Road to Mountain Village|Map|MM3D}}</ref> Located directly north of [[Termina Field]], it serves as the main passageway to the [[Mountain Village]] and the general [[Snowhead]] region.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|MM3D|Road to Mountain Village}} is a fairly insignificant place within the scope of [[Link]]'s adventure. There are no items to be found, no {{Plural|MM3D|Piece of Heart|link}} hidden anywhere, and no inhabitants. The only interesting feature to be found is a [[Gossip Stone]] which delineates the potential applications of [[Gold Dust]], in addition to providing Link with the knowledge of its market value at the [[Curiosity Shop]].<ref>{{Cite|The gold dust won as the prize in the Goron Races in spring can be used by the smithy to forge a sword. Or it can be sold to the smithy for 40 Rupees, but the Curiosity Shop will buy it for 200 Rupees...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref>

Additionally, the pathway is notable for being the first location in the game where [[Bomb]]s (and by extension, a [[Bomb Bag]]) are required in order to proceed. As an alternative, Link may use the [[Blast Mask]] to eliminate the ploughs of snow barricading the way to the [[Mountain Village]].
