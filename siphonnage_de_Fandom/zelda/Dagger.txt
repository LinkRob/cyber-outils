==Location and Uses==
==={{ZA|-}}===
In {{ZA}}, {{Plural|ZA|Dagger}} can be fired by casting the eponymous Spell. They are particularly effective against {{Plural|ZA|Wizzrobe|link}}. They cost 2 Rupees to use. The Spell is given by [[Characters in Zelda's Adventure#Krebb|Krebb]] in the Mobilin's Head Inn after talking to him twice.

==={{FPTRR|-}}===
<!--"The blade's deadly glint shows that this is the real thing. Handle with care!"-->
In {{FPTRR}}, the {{Plural|FPTRR|Dagger}} can be found behind the western pirate gate of [[Cape Treasure]]. It is unlocked by interacting with the Pirate insignia behind the eastern gate. The [[Armourer]] will pay 400 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} for it.

==={{CoH|-}}===
In {{CoH}}, the {{Term|CoH|Dagger}} is {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}'s and {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}}'s starting {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}}. They deal 1 damage to a single enemy in front of the user, and can only be used by {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} and {{Term|CoH|Cadence}}.
Like all weapons in Cadence of Hyrule, the Dagger can be found with or infused into one of 6 materials:
*{{Term|CoH|Titanium Dagger|link}}: Deals 2 damage to a single enemy in front of the user.
*{{Term|CoH|Ruby Dagger|link}}: Deals 1 damage to a single enemy in front of the user. After defeating 5 enemies, restores one half heart.
*{{Term|CoH|Emerald Dagger|link}}: Deals 1 damage to a single enemy in front of the user, and poisons that enemy.
*{{Term|CoH|Obsidian Dagger|link}}: Deals 1, 2, or 3 damage to a single enemy in front of the user. This weapon's damage increases as your combo increases.
*{{Term|CoH|Glass Dagger|link}}: Deals 3 damage to a single enemy in front of the user. If you take damage while this weapon is equipped, it breaks.
*{{Term|CoH|Jeweled Dagger|link}}: Deals 4 damage to a single enemy in front of the user.
