The '''Dark Tower''' is a location featured in {{TCT}}. Situated in the main street of the {{Term|TCT|Town of Ruto|link}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=She goes out to the main street of Ruto and looks around. Looming over everything is a dark, evil-looking tower with five curved stone spikes on its top.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=24}}</ref> it is the resting place of {{Term|TCT|Ganon|link}}'s {{Term|TCT|Handprint}}, which is one of the three items necessary to free {{Term|TCT|Link|link}} from the {{Term|TCT|Crystal|link}} as dictated by the {{Term|TCT|Fairy's Scroll|link}}.

==Overview==
When {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} enters the {{Term|TCT|Dark Tower}}, she will be greeted by a {{Term|TCT|Darknut}}, who promptly attacks her upon sight.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Welcome!' an inhuman voice whispers from the shadows. 'Who's there?' Zelda demands, pulling back on her bowstring. A tall, armored figure steps into the moonlight. 'A darknut!' Zelda whispers. Without another word, the evil knight charges, his shield held high, his long blade slicing the air.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=49}}</ref> By shooting an {{Term|TCT|Arrow|link}} at the {{Term|TCT|Darknut}}'s back, she defeats the foe and climbs up the tower.

After a long climbing of stairs, {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} comes out at the very top of the spire to find herself above {{Term|TCT|Ruto Town|link}}. The princess begins to think that the {{Term|TCT|Darknut}} was there for a reason, and that it must have been guarding something important.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Zelda trudged up the stone staircase. After a long climb, she comes out at the very top of the spire. All Ruto is beneath her. 'That darknut was here for a reason,' she says. 'It was guarding something. What?'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=49}}</ref> Just then, she sees a {{Term|TCT|Handprint}} pressed into the stone near the battlement. Realizing that it is {{Term|TCT|Ganon}}'s mark, she thinks of the {{Term|TCT|Fairy's Scroll}} riddle and figures out that {{Term|TCT|Ganon}}'s touch is the coldest thing in the world.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Her eyes travel over the battlement and stop on a strange object set in the floor. [...] 'A handprint!' she says in surprise. 'Pressed into the stone. And that mark!' [...] 'That's Ganon's mark! It must be his handprint.' Then she thinks of the riddle. 'What's colder than ice?' she says. 'Ganon's hand! His touch is the coldest thing in the world.'|book= TCT|publisher= |page=44}}</ref> Using {{Term|TCT|Zelda's Dagger|display=her dagger|link}}, Zelda makes the handprint come free from the floor and heads out of the tower and into the {{Term|TCT|Tavern|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=With the point of her dagger, Zelda carefully priest at the stones around it until the handprint comes free. She slips the print into her pouch.|book= TCT|publisher= |page=44}}</ref>
