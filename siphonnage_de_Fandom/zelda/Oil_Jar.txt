==Location and Uses==
==={{TotK|-}}===
Oil Jars can be bought at {{Term|TotK|The Slippery Falcon|link}} in {{Term|TotK|Rito Village|link}}. It cannot be eaten raw, but they can be used in a {{Plural|TotK|Cooking Pot|link}} to make certain dishes. Oil Jars can also be thrown into a {{Term|TotK|fire|link}} to create an updraft.

When [[Cooking]], Oil Jar is used for creating dishes as a frying additive. Specifically, it's needed to make [[Crunchy Fried Rice]], [[Deep-Fried Drumstick]], [[Deep-Fried Roast]], [[Deep-Fried Thigh]], and [[Fragrant Seafood Stew]].
