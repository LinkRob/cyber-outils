'''One-eye Lizards''' are enemies in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|FPTRR|One-eye Lizard}} appear in the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Garden}}. They are aggressive and will attack {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} if they spot him by popping their large eye out of their head and striking him with it. Once hit, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} will take damage and be briefly caught in red goo. {{Plural|FPTRR|One-eye Lizard}} have an endless supply of eyes with which to attack {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}.
