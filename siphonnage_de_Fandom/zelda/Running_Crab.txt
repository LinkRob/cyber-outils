{{Term/Store|Running Crab|Running Crabs|Series|plural}} are aggressive {{Wp|coconut crab}}s commonly found in various shoreline and sandy areas of {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics and Variations==
===Running Crab===
The standard '''Running Crab''' is a dangerous foe early on in the game on [[Sunshine Seashore]], aggressively chasing [[Tingle]] down and ganging up on him where possible. Despite this, they are still relatively weak and are easily dealt with when Tingle obtains a [[Bodyguard]]. Running Crabs yield [[Minced Meat]] and sometimes even drop [[Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland#Marine Gem|Marine Gem]]s.

===Blue Running Crab===
[[File:FPTRR Blue Running Crab Sprite.png]]
'''Blue Running Crabs''' appear in [[Cape Treasure]] and [[Lon Lon Meadow]]. They are largely identical to their red cousins, but are somewhat stronger. The [[Ground Shell]] and [[Amethyst]] can be obtained from the blue variety.

===Stealing Sand Crab===
[[File:FPTRR Stealing Sand Crab Sprite.png]]
There is only one '''Stealing Sand Crab''' in the game, found far later in the game in the [[Fairy Garden]]. Unlike those fought earlier, this enemy is cowardly and will flee [[Tingle]]. It has stolen the [[Blazing Herb]], one of the five Herbs that Tingle must recover to restore the fountains. Despite the name, it has no apparent relation to [[Sand Crab]]s.

==Nomenclature==
The name {{Romanize|カケアシガニ|Kakeashigani}} is a play on the Japanese words meaning "running fast" and {{Romanize|ヤシガニ|Yashigani}}, meaning "coconut crab."
