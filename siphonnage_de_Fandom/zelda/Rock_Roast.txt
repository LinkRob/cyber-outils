{{Term/Store|Rock Roast|Rock Roasts|Series, BotW|plural}} are objects in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Favor">{{Cite|I need you to do me a favor! As a brother, I can't just sit idly by. I have to help him! Exit here and head {{Color|BotW Red|down the hill}} to Gortram Cliff. There you can find {{Color|BotW Red|rock roasts}} rollin' around. I need ya to please grab one of 'em for me.|Bladon|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Rock Roasts appear in the [[Shrine Quest]], "[[A Brother's Roast]]". After finding [[Gonguron]] collapsed in [[Gorko Tunnel]], his brother, [[Bladon]], asks [[Link]] to find Rock Roasts near [[Gortram Cliff]] to help him.<ref name="Favor"/> In order to bring a Rock Roast uphill to Gorko Tunnel, Link must evade a series of [[Boulder]]s, [[Fire Chuchu]]s, and [[Moblin]]s.

Upon retrieving a Rock Roast for Bladon, the [[Goron]] will [[Cooking|Cook]] it using the nearby [[Cooking Pot]], making a {{Term|BotW|Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast|link}}. (similar to [[Raw Gourmet Meat]])<ref>{{Cite|I did it! I made a {{Color|BotW Red|deliciously grilled rock roast}}!|Bladon|BotW}}</ref> The smell from the Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast will wake Gonguron from his stupor.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Am I hunger-hallucinating, or do I really smell {{Color|BotW Red|deliciously grilled rock roast}}?!|Gonguron|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Brother! It's ready! You're gonna eat it and LOVE IT, and everything will be as it should be again!|Bladon|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite|Look at this! It looks so tasty! I NEED to eat it right away!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> Eating the Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast restores Gonguron's strength, allowing him to hurriedly break through the nearby wall of hard rock, revealing [[Kayra Mah Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|Yummy! Delicious! Incredible! That texture! That crunch! Ooooh, and that gritty feeling as it glides down my throat! OH! Yes! Can't forget that savory flavor of condensed minerals seared in high heat to perfection! It's a taste explosion! It's restoring my strength! I'm like a phoenix reborn and ready to fly! In fact, I AM Death Mountain!! Your cookin' is the best of the best, brother!|Gonguron|BotW}}</ref> After this, Rock Roasts will no longer appear at Gortram Cliff.

Rock Roasts cannot be destroyed with the [[Remote Bomb#Breath of the Wild|Remote Bomb]] [[Rune]]. Link is unable to cook one should he take one to a different [[Cooking Pot]] or places it on [[Aji]]'s [[Protein Palace|grill]], meaning Bladon is the only way to turn it into a Deliciously Grilled Rock Roast.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, rock roasts can be found in the East and West {{Plural|TotK|Restaurant Caves|link}} after completing the main quest {{Term|TotK|Yunobo of Goron City|link}}. They appear as objectives for the [[Side Quest|side quests]] [[Meat for Meat]] and {{Term|TotK|Rock Roast or Dust|link}}. It can also be {{Term|TotK|Fuse|link|display=fused}} to a weapon to turn it into a hammer. It has a fuse power of nine.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Rock Roast|Considered a Goron delicacy, this meal is beyond the chewing power of the average Hylian.<ref name="EoW Rock Roast">{{Cite|Considered a Goron delicacy, this meal is beyond the chewing power of the average Hylian.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Rock Roasts are chunks of rock commonly eaten by Gorons, found in the [[Rock-Roast Quarry]] on [[Eldin Volcano]]. During the [[Rock-Roast Quarry (Quest)|Rock-Roast Quarry]] [[Main Quest]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn the Rock Roast [[Echo]], which costs one Echo energy to summon.
