The {{Term/Store|Broken Picori Blade||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119}}</ref> also known as the '''Broken Picori Sword''',<ref>{{Cite|Broken Picori Sword|Quest Status|TMC}}</ref> is an item in {{TMC}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} receives the {{Term|TMC|Broken Picori Blade}} from {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} breaks the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} to open the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|link}} and turns {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} to stone. {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that the restored {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} is the only thing that can break the curse, and asks him to begin his search in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}.

After obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} smithy {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}} is able to reforge the {{Term|TMC|Broken Picori Blade}} into the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}}.
