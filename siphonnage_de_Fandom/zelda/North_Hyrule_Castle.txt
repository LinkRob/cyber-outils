{{Term/Store|North Hyrule Castle||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|North Hyrule Castle|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
North Hyrule Castle is located north of the [[Hyrule Castle Moat]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. A wagon in the northeast portion of Hyrule Castle North can be searched for [[Material]]s. Piles of logs that can be chopped for [[Wood]] can be found throughout the area.

A [[Blue Hinox]] can be found resting at the center of North Hyrule Castle. In [[Master Mode]], it is replaced with a [[Black Hinox]].
