{{Term/Store|Blue Water of Life|Blue Water of Life|Series|plural}},{{Exp Game|Series}}<ref name="E 142">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142 {{Exp Game|Series, TLoZ}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Life Potion|Life Potions|TLoZ|plural}}{{Exp Game|TLoZ}}<ref name="E 142"/> or '''Water of Life''',<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Drink the Water of Life to fully replenish your health. ==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Water of life|Drink this and all of Link's LIFE hearts will turn red. Red water turns blue when Link sips it. Drink the blue water and that's the last of the water.}}
Life Potions are blue, bottled drinks that [[Link]] can purchase for 40 [[Rupee]]s from any [[Old Woman]] in a [[Cave]] after he has shown one of them the [[Letter]]. If Link drinks a Life Potion, it will restore all of his [[Heart]]s.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Drink this and all of Link's LIFE hearts will turn red.|game= TLoZ|page= 25}}</ref> Life Potions can also be obtained by drinking a [[Red Water of Life|2nd Potion]].<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Red water turns blue when Link sips it. Drink the blue water and that's the last of the water.|game= TLoZ|page= 25}}</ref>

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{ZA|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:ZA Life Potion Sprite.png|left]]
In {{ZA}}, [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can purchase Life Potions from a number of [[Merchant]]s around [[Tolemac]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s. They fully restore Zelda's health when consumed. She may only carry one Life Potion at a time.
{{Noncanon|End}}
