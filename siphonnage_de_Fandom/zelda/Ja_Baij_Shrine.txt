The {{Term/Store|Ja Baij Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ja Baij Shrine}}<br/>Bomb Trial}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Bomb Trial''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Ja Baij Shrine}} is located in the ruins of the {{Term|BotW|Eastern Abbey|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} initially found on the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau}}, it must be completed as part of "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Old Man explained that there are a total of four Shrines on the plateau. He said that he will give you the Paraglider once you have acquired the Spirit Orb from all the of the Shrines.<br/>He told you to survey the plateau from the top of the tower to find the remaining shrines. He also pointed out that you can use your Sheikah Slate if you need to travel quickly.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is guarded by {{Plural|BotW|Decayed Guardian|link}} and surrounded by large walls. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can climb the wall or use the {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}'s laser to destroy the bombable wall blocking the entrance. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ja Baij. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Ja Baij|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
After entering {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain the {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} by placing his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}} below the nearby {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling Rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Rune extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> This {{Term|BotW|Rune}} is needed in order to progress. Immediately, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will need to use his {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb}} to destroy the cracked walls blocking his path.

After this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find himself in a hallway with two branches. Exploding the cracked walls on the left will lead to a {{Term|BotW|Ladder|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} will encounter a moving platform with several cracked walls on the other side. By placing the square {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} on the platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can activate it once it is close to the walls, allowing him to ride the platform to the other side.

On the other side of the platform is a large chamber, from which {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} can be seen. However, it is located a floor above {{Term|BotW|Link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Ladder}} leading up to it is blocked by walls. Four {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}} are found in this room. Two on the left exchange an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}}, demonstrating how {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} can be used to to push {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} forward. By placing a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} in the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} on the right, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can explode it when it reaches the walls, clearing the path to {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij}}.

At the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij}} will be waiting for {{Term|BotW|Link}}. If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed, {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij}} will explain that they are a {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of {{Term|BotW|The Goddess Hylia|link}}, dedicated to aiding those who fight {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the resolve of a true hero. I am Ja Baij, the creator of this trial. I am a humble monk, blessed with the sight of Goddess Hylia and dedicated to helping those who seek to defeat Ganon.}}|Ja Baij|BotW}}</ref> With {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arrival, {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij}}'s purpose is fulfilled, and the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|With your arrival, my duty is now fulfilled. In the name of Goddess Hylia, allow me to bestow this gift upon you... Please accept this Spirit Orb.}}|Ja Baij|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ja Baij|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ja Baij}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ja Baij|BotW}}</ref>

In the hallway at the beginning of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can go right to find another wall. Destroying this wall will reveal a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Claymore|link}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can only be accessed by using the {{Term|BotW|Launcher}} on the left side of the final chamber to send {{Term|BotW|Link}} across the room. Inside, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}.
