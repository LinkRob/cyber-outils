{{Term/Store|Red Puffstool|Red Puffstools|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Red Puffstool|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Red Puffstools are a type of {{Term|CoH|Puffstool|link}}. Like other Puffstools, they are stationary and cannot move from their position, but differ in that they do not release any spores. When hit by a {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} or projectile, they will crouch for one beat and then explode on the next, damaging all within their immediate vicinity before dying.
