The {{Term/Store|Mask Shop||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mask Shop|Map|OoA}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Mask Shop is located in [[Nuun Highlands]], west of [[Lynna City]]. Inside, [[Link]] can find a starving [[Happy Mask Salesman]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm so hungry my stomach hurts. AHH! I'M HUNGRY! Pardon me. I just thought you could help.|Happy Mask Salesman|OoA}}</ref> In exchange for the [[Tasty Meat]], Link can receive the [[Doggie Mask]] from the Salesman.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? Hand it over... Food at last... Chomp, chomp................ Why are you staring at me? Oh, you WANT something? How greedy! How mean! You won't leave until you get something? Fine. You can have this.|Happy Mask Salesman|OoA}}</ref>
