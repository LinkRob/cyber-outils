==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Large Block}} appear in three sizes in {{TMC}}, and require multiple {{Plural|TMC|Links|link}} to move them (at least two {{Plural|TMC|Links}} are needed to move a 2 x 2 {{Term|TMC|Large Block}}, at least three are needed to move a 3 x 3 {{Term|TMC|Large Block}}, and four are needed to move a 4 x 4 {{Term|TMC|Large Block}}). To do this, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must be able to copy himself on {{Plural|TMC|Flashing Spot on the Floor|link}}; the {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Two Elements)|link}}, {{Term|TMC|White Sword (Three Elements)|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}} allow him to create copies of himself (one copy, two copies, and three copies respectively). As long as the original {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is making direct contact with a {{Term|TMC|Large Block}} and each space along one side of the {{Term|TMC|Large Block}} is covered by the {{Plural|TMC|Links}}, it can be pushed like a normal {{Term|TMC|Block|link}}.
