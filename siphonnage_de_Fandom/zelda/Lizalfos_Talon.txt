==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Lizalfos Talons are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by all types of {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} and [[Stalizalfos]] upon their defeat. They can be combined with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make an [[Elixir]].<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be sold to [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] for [[Mon]] or given to the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Gray [[Dye]].

The [[Zora Set]] requires five Lizalfos Talons, as well as five {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}}, to upgrade each piece to their second tiers at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Zora Set''': 15 (0, 5, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
