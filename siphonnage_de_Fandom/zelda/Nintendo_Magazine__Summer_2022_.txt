'''Nintendo Magazine (Summer 2022)''' is a [[:Category:Magazines|magazine]] published by [[Nintendo]]. It is the fifth issue in the {{NM}} series and included both Japanese and English versions.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Excerpts from the Nintendo Magazine 2022 Summer (Digital edition) are also available in English. |author= @Nintendo |published= July 29, 2022 |retrieved= September 26, 2022 |url= https://twitter.com/Nintendo/status/1552932651623137281 |title= 任天堂株式会社 |site= Twitter |type= }}</ref>

==Zelda Content==
===Enjoy the Summer Wilderness with Pokémon and Zelda! (p.16-17)===
This section highlights {{BotW}} and {{Pokémon|Pokémon Legends: Arceus}} as two games where you can explore nature.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= Summer vacation is a great time to explore nature. Let's explore some activities you can enjoy in these games! |magazine= Nintendo Magazine |publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Summer 2022 |page= 16 (English)}}</ref> This section includes some images from in {{BotW|-}}, including a reused image from the Winter 2021 issue of {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} shooting a {{Term|BotW|Woodland Boar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= |magazine= Nintendo Magazine |publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021 |page= 18 (English)}}</ref> Other images include {{Term|BotW|Link}} standing atop {{Term|BotW|Mount Hylia|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} in front of the {{Term|BotW|Old Man's Cabin|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} using {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} on a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} to lift it out of some {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and finally {{Term|BotW|Link}} aiming a {{Term|BotW|Shock Arrow|link}} at a few {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}} and an {{Term|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}. Also included in this section are some artworks of {{Term|BotW|Copious Mushroom Skewers|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Gourmet Poultry Curry|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Mighty Porgy}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}.

This section details how metal {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found submerged underwater and shows how {{Term|BotW|Magnesis}} can be used to raise them up and out.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= Why don't you ride a raft and search for treasure chests lying at the bottom of the sea? Let's become a treasure hunter today! |magazine= Nintendo Magazine |publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Summer 2022 |page= 17 (English)}}</ref> It also details a trick to catching {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}}, explaining how using a {{Term|BotW|Shock Arrow}} will electrocute all {{Term|BotW|Fish}} in a large radius and make them easy to pick up.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= Fish swim freely in the sea. You can grab them by hand, of course, but you can also use a Shock Arrow to electrocute them before you catch them. |magazine= Nintendo Magazine |publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Summer 2022 |page= 17 (English)}}</ref>
