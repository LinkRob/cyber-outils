{{Term/Store|Ploymus Mountain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Ploymus Mountain'''|Map|BotW}}</reF>

==Features and Overview==
Ploymus Mountain is a mountain in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]] which overlooks the [[East Reservoir Lake]]. It is inhabited by a lone [[Lynel]], which has caused issues with the residents of [[Zora's Domain]] due to its tendency to loose [[Shock Arrow]]s.<ref>{{Cite|That tall mountain over yonder... It is called {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}, and there you will also find {{Color|BotW Blue|Shatterback Point}}. A terrifying creature has made its home up there. This awful beast shoots volley after volley of {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}. Even a single one could be fatal to a Zora.|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> In order to stop the flow of water coming from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, Sidon instructs Link to go to Ploymus Mountain and collect any stray Shock Arrows that the Lynel has shot.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Aha! You must mean that {{Color|BotW Blue|Lynel}}! He is a man-beast, that one!}}|Sidon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That beast does indeed wield {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}. That is certainly one way to collect them quickly. He is vicious, to be sure. But I am certain Link will rise to the challenge.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> At Ploymus Mountain, Link can find a total of 31 Shock Arrows stuck to the bark of [[Tree]]s, in the ground, and even in the rocks. He can get an extra 20 if he kills the Lynel. Ploymus Mountain is one of the locations that is affected by the constant [[Rain]] generated by Divine Beast Vah Ruta.

Ploymus Mountain is also where the [[Zora]], {{Term|BotW|Laflat|link}}, directs Link in search of a [[Picture]] of a red-maned Lynel as part of the "[[Lynel Safari]]" [[Side Quest]].<ref>{{Cite|There's a red-maned monster called a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} that lives on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. I REALLY need a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. It can be a picture of any red-maned {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. Not just the one on the mountain. So what do you say? Can you get me a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> During the "[[EX Champion Mipha's Song]]" [[Main Quest]] from [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack, Ploymus Mountain is where the [[Mah Eliya Shrine]] rises from the ground after completing one of the Trials which requires Link to ascend the waterfalls at [[Mikau Lake]] and [[Lulu Lake]].

A [[Korok]] can be found underneath a [[Rock]] along the northern slope of Ploymus Mountain.
