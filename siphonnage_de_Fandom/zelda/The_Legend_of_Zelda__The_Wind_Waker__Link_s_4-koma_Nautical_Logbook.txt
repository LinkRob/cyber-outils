''{{Name|Japanese|The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker: Link's 4-koma Nautical Logbook}}'' is a [[4-koma]], a humorous series of vertically aligned comic panels in groups of four, that depicts several funny scenarios that may have happened during [[Link]]'s journey. Unlike other [[manga]]s, ''Link's Logbook'' does not retell the story of {{TWW}} since it skips most of the dungeons and scenes, such as Link meeting [[Tetra]]. However, the manga does follow in chronological order.
