== Characteristics==
These skull-like enemies usually blend in with their surroundings, acting as regular [[skull]]s that can be picked up and thrown by [[Link]]. They will flash and begin pursuit if Link walks too close to them. Stals can also hide under grass, although this is an uncommon case. They will actively follow Link unless he can run away from them or destroy them with weapons such as the [[Sword]]. They appear to be the Dark World counterparts of [[Armos]], due to their mannerisms.
