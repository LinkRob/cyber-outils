{{Term/Store|Switch Mole|Switch Moles|Series, TFH|plural}} are traps in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= SwitchMole |game= TFH |system= 3DS |version= 2.1.0 |country= United States}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Switch Moles pop up randomly from a group of holes on the ground. The [[Links]] must hit them with a [[Magic Hammer]] in order to open the path forward. Switch Moles can appear in groups, with electricity surrounding some of them. They can be stunned temporarily with the [[Boomerang]] before being hit with the Magic Hammer.
