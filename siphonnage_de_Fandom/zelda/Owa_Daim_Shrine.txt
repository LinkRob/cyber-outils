The {{Term/Store|Owa Daim Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Owa Daim Shrine}}<br/>Stasis Trial}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Stasis Trial''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Owa Daim Shrine}} is located atop a climbable precipice just off the southern edge of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}}. It is one of four {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must complete during "{{Term|BotW|The Isolated Plateau|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Old Man explained that there are a total of four Shrines on the plateau. He said that he will give you the Paraglider once you have acquired the Spirit Orb from all the of the Shrines.<br/>He told you to survey the plateau from the top of the tower to find the remaining shrines. He also pointed out that you can use your Sheikah Slate if you need to travel quickly.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Owa Daim|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Owa Daim. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Owa Daim|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
At the entrance of {{Term|BotW|Owa Daim Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|link}} beneath it to his left. When he places his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Pedestal|display=Pedestal}}, the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} will authenticate it and begin to distill a {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Sheikah Slate authenticated. Distilling rune...}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> A drop of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}} will then fall onto the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, unlocking the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Rune extracted.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

Immediately in front of {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a rotating slab attached to a spinning gear. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} uses {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} on the gear while the slab is horizontal, he will have a short amount of time to walk across it. There is a path upwards on the second platform, but a {{Term|BotW|Boulder|link}} consistently rolls down it. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} on the {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} when it is at either the end or the beginning of the path, allowing him to walk upwards without behind hit.

On the third platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find an {{Term|BotW|Iron Sledgehammer|link}}. Another {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} blocks the path between {{Term|BotW|Link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits the {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} while it is under the effects of {{Term|BotW|Stasis}}, it will be launched forward when the {{Term|BotW|Rune}} wears off. After this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to approach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}.

If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed, {{Term|BotW|Oman Au}} will explain that they are a {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of {{Term|BotW|The Goddess Hylia|link}}, dedicated to helping those who fight {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the resolve of a true hero. I am Owa Daim, the creator of this trial. I am a humble monk blessed with the sight of Goddess Hylia and dedicated to helping those who seek to defeat Ganon.}}|Owa Daim|BotW}}</ref> With {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arrival, {{Term|BotW|Oman Au}}'s duty is now fulfilled, and the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|With your arrival, my duty is now fulfilled. In the name of Goddess Hylia, allow me to bestow this gift upon you... Please accept this Spirit Orb.}}|Owa Daim|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Owa Daim|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Owa Daim}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Owa Daim|BotW}}</ref>

Behind the spot that the {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} falls from is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can access it while the {{Term|BotW|Boulder}} is under the effects of {{Term|BotW|Stasis}}. Inside, he will find a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Shield|link}}.
