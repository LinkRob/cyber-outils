The {{Term/Store|Shadow Barrier||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an object in {{OoT}}.<ref name="Dispelled">{{Cite|The {{Color|OoT3D Purple|Shadow Barrier}} is dispelled! Please save the princess!|Impa|OoT3D}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Shadow Barrier}} is one of the {{Term|OoT3D|Six Evil Barriers|link}} found {{Term|OoT3D|Inside Ganon's Castle|link}} that protects {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon's Tower|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link...can you hear me? It's Rauru, the Sage. We six will gather our power to create a bridge to the castle where Ganondorf dwells... The castle's keep, which is known as Ganon's Tower, is protected by {{Color|OoT3D Red|six evil barriers}}. Bring down the six barriers, and save Princess Zelda!|Rauru|OoT3D}}</ref> In order to dispel the {{Term|OoT3D|Shadow Barrier}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} must enter its corresponding room, denoted by the {{Term|OoT3D|Shadow Medallion|link}} over its door, and free the {{Term|OoT3D|Impa|display=Sage of Shadow}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Impa|link}}.<ref name="Dispelled"/>
