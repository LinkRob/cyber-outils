{{Term/Store|Safula Hill||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Safula Hill'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Safula Hill is located in the [[Hyrule Ridge]]. The [[Sanidin Park Ruins]] are located atop the Hill, and the {{Term|BotW|White Horse|link}} can be found roaming the center of the Hill. The White Horse is necessary to complete [[Toffa]]'s request in the [[Side Quest]] "[[The Royal White Stallion]]".<ref>{{Cite|A traveler... Tell me, have you seen a {{Color|BotW Red|pure-white horse}} anywhere? A long time back, my grandfather told me that he once saw the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} grazing on {{Color|BotW Blue|Safula Hill}}... The thing is...just the other day, one of our customers said he saw a {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Safula Hill}} recently... Could it be a relative of the royal family's {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} from my grandfather's day? Well, if you happen to see a {{Color|BotW Red|white horse}} on your travels, do you think you could bring it back to me?|Toffa|BotW}}</ref> [[Glendo]] occasionally travels along the road that runs across the Hill on his [[Horse]].

Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] and [[Link]] across Safula Hill on the way to the [[Sanidin Park Ruins]]. Zelda thanks Link for his advice for calming her White Horse and notes that the White Horse wears the [[Royal Saddle]] and [[Royal Bridle|Bridle]] very well.<ref>{{Cite|"Be sure to take the time to soothe your mount... That's the only way it will know how you truly feel." Your advice was quite helpful—thank you. This little one and I are getting along quite well now. At first, I wasn't sure if I should outfit him with all of the royal gear. I thought maybe he should have to earn it first. But it works! He wears it like a true natural. I'm trying to be a bit more empathetic. Benefit of the doubt, you know?|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

A [[Korok]] can be found by giving an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}} to the {{Term|BotW|Offering Spot|link}} at the statues by the [[Tree]] on the Hill's southern peak.
