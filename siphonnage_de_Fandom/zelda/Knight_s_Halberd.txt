{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Halberd}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that have been modeled after {{Plural|BotW|Axe|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They were used by {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} who were skilled in mounted combat before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> In the present, they are commonly used by low-tier {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

Scanning the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} has a chance to drop a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Halberd}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Young Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Halberd}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are also unable to be burned by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Halberd}} will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Halberd}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} used by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>
