{{Term/Store|Ruined House|Ruined Houses|Series, BotW|plural}} are locations in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 341}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|Ruined House}} are the remains of homes once belonging to {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} before the return of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref name="CaC"/> Due to this, they are most common among ruins in {{Term|BotW|Central Hyrule|link}}, though they can also be found among all destroyed {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} settlements in other {{Plural|BotW|Region|link}}. Since their destruction during the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, these buildings have fallen into further disrepair, leaving only portions of their walls and foundations.<ref name="CaC"/> {{Plural|BotW|Ruined House}} come in two varieties: wood and stone.
