==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TPHD|Auru's Memo}} is given to {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} by {{Term|TPHD|Auru|link}} after getting the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}}. When talking to him, if {{Term|TPHD|Link}} answers "yes" to going into the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert|link}}, he will give him the memo. When shown to {{Term|TPHD|Fyer|link}}, it allows {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to take the {{Term|TPHD|Oasis Flight|link}} into the desert.<ref>{{Cite|Hrrrm... From old Auru, huh... Ah well, I guess I oughta do what the old coot says...
