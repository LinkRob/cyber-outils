{{Term/Store|Stone Slab Fragment|Stone Slab Fragments|Series, LA|plural}} are {{Plural|LA|Item|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 140"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|LA|Stone Slabs and Fragments|If you place the stone slab fragment into the stone slab, you will be able to get a hint about how to conquer the dungeon.}}
{{Plural|LA|Stone Slab Fragment}} are missing pieces of {{Plural|LA|Stone Slab|link}} that can be found in every {{Term|LA|Dungeon|link}} on {{Term|LA|Koholint Island|link}}. Each {{Term|LA|Stone Slab|display=Slab}} resides on a wall and holds a message, but cannot be read because a part of it is missing.<ref>{{Cite|Something is inscribed on the stone slab, but you can't read it because a piece is gone.|N/A|LA}}</ref> {{Term|LA|Link|link}} must explore the {{Term|LA|Dungeon}} until he finds a {{Term|LA|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the missing {{Term|LA|Stone Slab Fragment}}. He can then return the {{Term|LA|Stone Slab Fragment|display=Fragment}} to the {{Term|LA|Stone Slab|display=Slab}}, revealing a hint about a {{Term|LA|Puzzle|link}}, an enemy, a {{Term|LA|Sub-Boss|link}}, or the {{Term|LA|Dungeon}} {{Term|LA|Boss|link}}.

In {{LA|DX}}, they are replaced by {{Plural|LANS|Stone Beak|link}}.
