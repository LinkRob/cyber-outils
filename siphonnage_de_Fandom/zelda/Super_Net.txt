The {{Term/Store|Super Net||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Super Net}} is obtained after {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} has acquired the [[Bug Net]] and successfully completes the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower|link}}'s advanced level a second time. Like the normal {{Term|ALBW|Net}}, it can be used to catch {{Plural|ALBW|Item}} and put them in {{Plural|ALBW|Bottle|link}}; however, it can actually be used to damage [[Enemies in A Link Between Worlds|enemies]] and deals eight times the damage.

== Trivia ==
[[File:SCII Bug-Catching Net Model.png|thumb|175x175px|The Bug Net as seen in ''SoulCalibur II'', paired with the [[Hylian Shield]]]]

* The Super Net's ability to harm enemies like a [[Sword]] was possibly inspired by the Bug-Catching Net in the Namco fighting game ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'' ([[Nintendo GameCube]] version only), where Link indeed uses such seemingly impractical item like a blade against his opponents.
