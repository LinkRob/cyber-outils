{{Term/Store|Lumber|Lumber|Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 130}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Lumber is for sale in [[Whittleton]] for only 50 [[Rupee]]s. However, Link is unable to afford it if he does not have the Freight Car yet, and by the time he does, the seller needs [[Goron Iron]] to polish the tools he uses to cut the trees and find his merchandise. The Goron Iron can be purchased in [[Goron Village]] for 100 Rupees and brought to Whittleton to solve this problem.

With the lumber available, Link can carry it to [[Anouki Village]] to help the [[Anouki]] build a fence. Even then, however, the young hero still has to bring [[Kenzo]] from the {{Term|ST|Trading Post|link}} so that the fence build can start. The reward for these deeds is a [[Force Gem]] that activates [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] giving access to the [[Snowdrift Station]].
