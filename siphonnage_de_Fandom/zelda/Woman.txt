The {{Term/Store|Woman||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Biography==
===Hyrule===
The Woman lives in a house situated in the northeast corner of [[Kakariko Village#A Link Between Worlds|Kakariko Village]]. She seems to enjoy peace and quiet, preferring to stay inside her house rather than joining in the bustle of the village.<ref>{{Cite|The village is really bustling today, don't you think? I think I'll stay inside where it's nice and quiet!|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> However, soon afterward, she becomes bothered by strange noises coming from her roof.<ref>{{Cite|So... I've had a hard time sleeping lately. I've been hearing the strangest little cries from my roof. I've never heard anything like it before. It's keeping me up all night.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have you heard those strange little sounds coming from my roof? I wonder what's making that noise.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> To aid her, [[Link]] must have obtained the [[Power Glove]], then by using a [[Cucco]], he must land on her roof and lift the stone to reveal a [[Maiamai]], and talk to the Woman again. She will thank Link and reward him with a [[Rupee|Red Rupee]].<ref>{{Cite|Remember those strange little cries I was hearing from my roof? Well, can you believe it? Those sounds stopped! What? You took some creature off of it? Thanks, Link! Now I can get a good night's rest again. Please take this reward.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> Afterward, she admits that she misses the Maiamai's crying.<ref>{{Cite|How strange... I miss those little cries coming from my roof. It was a sweet sound, now that I think of it.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref>

===Lorule===
The Lorulean Woman's personality is in direct contrast to her kindly Hyrulean counterpart. She is aggressive and seems to enjoy picking fights and arguing. When spoken to, she will ask Link if he thinks she looks strong.<ref>{{Cite|Uh, hey. Think I look strong?|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> If he answers "Sure", she will ask suspiciously if he is lying, telling him that his ears twitched, and that hers twitch whenever she lies, which she hints to be often.<ref>{{Cite|You lying? Cuz your ears twitched real funny there. Mine do that when I lie. They twitch ALL the time. Anyway, I may not look it, but I'm a regular at the Treacherous Tower.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> If he answers "No", she will tell him that she is, in fact, quite weak, but will also state that she is in training at the [[Treacherous Tower]] to become stronger.<ref>{{Cite|You call it like you see it, eh? I'm sorry to say you're right... I'm weak. So... so weak... I'm training, though. I'm trying to become a regular up at the Treacherous Tower.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> She will then express surprise that Link has never heard of the Treacherous Tower, and tell him that it is on the summit of [[Death Mountain#A Link Between Worlds|Death Mountain]].<ref>{{Cite|Whaddya mean, you've never heard of the Treacherous Tower?! Well, you know Death Mountain? It's right on top of that. It's where all the tough guys go for fun.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What, you've never heard of the Treacherous Tower? Well, you know Death Mountain? It's right on top of that. The place attracts some rough customers.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's up on top of Death Mountain. Just getting there is something of an achievement, if you ask me.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> She then warns him about judging people on appearance alone.<ref>{{Cite|Be careful about going around judging people by their appearance, boy. If I were stronger, I'd teach you a lesson!|Woman|ALBW}}</ref> She also advises him to try out the Treacherous Tower if he thinks he is skilled enough.<ref>{{Cite|Got faith in your sword arm, boy? Go to the Treacherous Tower, and put your money where your mouth is.|Woman|ALBW}}</ref>
