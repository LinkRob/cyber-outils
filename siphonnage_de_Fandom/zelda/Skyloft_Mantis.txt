{{Term/Store|Skyloft Mantis|Skyloft Mantises|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Species name: {{Color|SS Blue|Skyloft Mantis}}<br/>Sensitivity to human presence: 70%<br/>Usable as a potion ingredient.</p><p>Be aware that this insect is surprisingly agile when it is fleeing, so you must approach it cautiously.</p>
}}

Skyloft Mantises can be found at [[Skyloft]]. They can be caught by using the [[Bug Net]]. Skyloft Mantises can be used to upgrade [[Potion]]s at the [[Potion Shop]] or sold to [[Strich]] at the [[Knight Academy]] during nighttime. They hide to ambush their prey with their sharp arms to quickly devour them. Skyloft Mantises prefer darkness, so they usually hide under [[Pot]]s.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= It takes cover and ambushes its prey, pierces insects that get near with is sharp arms, and quickly devours them. It prefers darkness and often hides inside pots.|book= E |publisher= Dark Horse Books|page= 66}}</ref>
