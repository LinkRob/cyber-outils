{{Term/Store|Mama||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Mama}} is the owner of {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}}, and remains at the bar inside throughout the entire game. She tells {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} that he can hear interesting gossip in the {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|display=Cafe}}.<ref>{{Cite|We hear all sorts of interesting gossip in here. Stop by from time to time.|Mama|TMC}}</ref> She and {{Term|TMC|Link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure, kid.|Mama|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a shoal rising at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, granting {{Term|TMC|Link}} access to a {{Term|TMC|Dig Cavern|link}}.
