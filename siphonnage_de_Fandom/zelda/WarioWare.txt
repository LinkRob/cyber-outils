'''''WarioWare''''' is a series of video games published by [[Nintendo]]. It is a series within the ''[[mariowiki:Wario (franchise)|Wario]]'' franchise, spun off from the ''[[mariowiki:Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise. The games are centered around [[Wario]] and his company named WarioWare, whose task is to manufacture microgames. Many of these are ''Zelda''-themed, and all appear in {{smw|9-Volt}}'s sets of microgames, which are based on various Nintendo retro games. Most of the installments' microgames have three difficulty levels, with variations at each level.

''WarioWare: Snapped!'' and ''Game & Wario'' are the only ''WarioWare'' games with no references to ''The Legend of Zelda''.
