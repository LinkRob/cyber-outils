{{Compendium/Extract|Ancient Bow}}
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bow}} are obtained from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Exchange for Ancient Bow?|Ancient Oven|BotW}}</ref> They can be bought for 1,000 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, 15 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Spring|link}}, 10 {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Gear|link}}, and one {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}. They can only be purchased after {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has activated the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}. They are the result of {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}'s research into {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This is common among pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}, the equipment archetype that {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bow}} belong to.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>

While {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bow}} have high power and very high durability, their most notable feature is their immense range. {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bow}} fire {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} in a straight line for quite some distance before the {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} become affected by gravity, making them ideal for hitting far-off targets.<ref name="Inventory"/> However, they also have a slow draw speed, being only 70% of a typical {{Term|BotW|Bow}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bow}} are one of only two {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} to have an altered {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} gravity stat, the other being {{Plural|BotW|Silver Bow|link}}, though the gravity reduction from {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bow}} is significantly higher than the latter.

Like other {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Bow}} will fold into a more compact shape on {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s back when he's not using them. Being made with {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, nor will they attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during storms. In addition, they will also float if they are dropped in water.
