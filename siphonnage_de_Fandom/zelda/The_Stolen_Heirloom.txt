==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Paya|link}} crying on the floor near {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}}. She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she only took her eyes off an object for a second, and now she does not know what to do.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Link}}... I swear I only took my eyes off it for a second! What should I... What should I do?|Paya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to tell her to stay positive, {{Term|BotW|Paya}} will angrily tell him that the situation is bad.<ref>{{Cite|Keep my chin up?<br/>Do you even realize how bad this is?!|Paya|BotW}}</ref> She reveals that the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|link}} has been stolen,<ref>{{Cite|[What's wrong?] The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah heirloom}}... {{Big|The {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} has been stolen!}}|Paya|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|When Link asks Paya what's wrong, she technically has two lines of dialog, depending on how Link responds to her initial distress. Both have Paya say "''The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah heirloom}}...''" - however, the branch in which Link tells Paya to "keep your chin up" has the incorrect spelling of {{Typo|Shiekah|Sheikah}}.}} just after she discovered its secret.<ref>{{Cite|And just when I had finally discovered the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}}'s secret...|Paya|BotW}}</ref>

With {{Term|BotW|Paya}} distraught, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} recites a legend about the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}}, which states a hero will be able to receive a "blessing of antiquity."<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|"The hero, as chosen by the Sheikah heirloom, will be gifted the blessing of antiquity."}}|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Paya}} believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is this hero, and so she has been researching the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom|display=Heirloom}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|Paya thinks "{{Color|BotW Red|The hero, as chosen by the Sheikah heirloom}}" is you, so she stayed up all night researching the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Through this research, she concluded that "the blessing of antiquity" is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} must be used to unlock.<ref>{{Cite|She came to the conclusion that "{{Color|BotW Red|the blessing of antiquity}}" refers to one of those {{Color|BotW Red|ancient shrines}}. And that the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} is the {{Color|BotW Red|key that opens the shrine}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> However, it was stolen before they were able to inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} of {{Term|BotW|Paya}}'s findings.<ref>{{Cite|And just before we were able to tell you this, the thing is stolen!|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Paya}} has been cleaning the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} everyday,<ref>{{Cite|I had been cleaning the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} regularly every day from morning to dusk.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> so she deduces it must have been stolen late at night.<ref>{{Cite|Someone must have stolen it during the deepest hour of the night.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> While outsiders are not permitted inside of {{Term|BotW|Impa's House}}, {{Term|BotW|Paya}} does not believe any of the villagers would steal from them.<ref>{{Cite|The place if off-limits to outsiders...
