==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Bigger Bullet Bag}} is an upgraded form of the {{Term|OoT3D|Bullet Bag|link}}. {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can obtain this bigger version by scoring three perfect bullseyes in the target practice in the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods|link}}, or by hitting all 10 {{Term|OoT3D|Rupee|link}} targets in the {{Term|OoT3D|Shooting Gallery|link}} at the {{Term|OoT3D|Market|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cool! You're great! You scored three perfect bullseyes! I have to give a neat present to such a wonderful person! Please take it!|Deku Scrub|OoT3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wonderful! Bravo! Perfect! Here's a fantastic present!|Shooting Gallery Owner|OoT3D}}</ref> {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} may do these in any order, but will always receive a {{Term|OoT3D|Bigger Bullet Bag}} after completing the first one.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Big Deku Seed Bullet Bag}}.
