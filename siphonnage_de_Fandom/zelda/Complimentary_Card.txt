The {{Term/Store|Complimentary Card||Series, PH}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{PH}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|PH|Beedle|link}} will send the {{Term|PH|Complimentary Card}} attached to a {{Term|PH|Letter|link}} once {{Term|PH|Link|link}} has accumulated 50 points on his membership card for {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship|link}}.<ref name="Points"/> The {{Term|PH|Complimentary Card}} can be given to {{Term|PH|Beedle}} for an opportunity to compliment him, by speaking into the [[Nintendo DS]] microphone.<ref>{{Cite|Well then, compliment away!<br/><br/>{{Color|PH Red|Speak up}}! I'm waiting for my compliment!|Beedle|PH}}</ref> {{Term|PH|Beedle}} will act amused and embarrassed by the compliment, thanking {{Term|PH|Link}} for his kind words.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh?<br/><br/>Hey!<br/><br/>Hey hey!<br/><br/>Ohh ho ho!<br/><br/>Hey!<br/><br/>Ohhhh ho ho!<br/><br/>Yuk yuk yuk yuk...<br/><br/>I'm a little embarrassed...<br/><br/>......|Beedle|PH}}</ref> However, if nothing is spoken into the microphone, {{Term|PH|Beedle}} will be annoyed, but the {{Term|PH|Complimentary Card}} will not be expended.<ref>{{Cite|Umm... I don't hear anything...<br/><br/>You can be kind of mean when you want, huh?|Beedle|PH}}</ref>
