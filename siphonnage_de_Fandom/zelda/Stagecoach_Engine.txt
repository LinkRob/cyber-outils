The {{Term/Store|Stagecoach Engine||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stagecoach Engine|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Stagecoach Engine}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Ancient Gold Piece|link}}, three {{Plural|ST|Dragon Scale|link}} and eight {{Plural|ST|Bee Larvae|link}}. It features two {{Wp|Carousel}}-like horses that rotate to simulate galloping, and its description reads "This train is sure to get people's attention."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Stagecoach Engine}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Stagecoach Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts. Using the {{Term|ST|Stagecoach Engine}} also changes the {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}}'s sound to that of a horse neighing.
