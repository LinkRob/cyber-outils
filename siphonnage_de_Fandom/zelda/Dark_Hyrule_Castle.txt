{{Term/Store|Dark Hyrule Castle||Series, TMC}} is the result of {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}'s power and is the sixth and final {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|TMC|Link|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} emerge from the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} after completing the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}} and inadvertently letting {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} discover the location of the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} to discover that {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} has been altered by {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s magic. {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} changes the original {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}}, turning its inhabitants into stone and bringing in many of his fiercest monster minions. The initial portion of this {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} takes place in the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}'s basement, where {{Term|TMC|Link}} must free the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}'s staff and {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s petrification spell with the power of the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword}}. Once {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} has been freed, he gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} a {{Term|TMC|Small Key|display=Key}} that allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to escape from the basement and take on the rest of the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}, as well as allowing him to exit the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} and prepare if desired. This is the final {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} of {{TMC}}, and as such has no direct in-game reward, although completing it initiates the ending, and visiting {{Term|TMC|Biggoron|link}} after having completed the game at least once (there is an option to save after the end of the credits) allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to create the {{Term|TMC|Mirror Shield|link}}.

{{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle}} is among the larger {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon}} in the game, with several floors. It includes puzzles from every prior {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}, as well as some new varieties, such as sets of floor tiles that must each be stepped on exactly once. As many of the routes in the castle have been blocked off, progressing through the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}} requires flying around the outside of the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} with {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} at some points. There are multiple {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss}}. Early on, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must fight a {{Term|TMC|Red Dark Nut|link}} to proceed. Somewhat later, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must fight a {{Term|TMC|Black Knight|link}}, the strongest of all {{Plural|TMC|Dark Nut|link}}, to open the way to the room containing the {{Term|TMC|Big Key|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Big Key}} is located behind a block that must be pushed by four {{Term|TMC|Link}} clones but is itself blocked by four key blocks. Each of the four {{Plural|TMC|Small Key}} required is guarded by one or two {{Plural|TMC|Dark Nut}} located in one of the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}'s four corner towers. These {{Plural|TMC|Dark Nut}} appear after the {{Term|TMC|Black Knight}} is defeated. For some reason, the symbol on the banners of {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle}} resembles the {{Term|Series|Crest of the Sheikah|link}}, though it is most likely a reference to {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}, whose monstrous shape in this and other games has a single immense eye.

Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} enters the central keep through the {{Term|TMC|Big Key}} door, a countdown starts. As {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} announces, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must interrupt {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s extraction ceremony before a bell tolls three times, or {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} will acquire the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} will die, and the game will end.<ref>{{Cite|So, you've come to stop my little ceremony. Well, you're too late. A mere three more chimes of the bell will bring the ceremony to its end! And with the third toll of the bell, I will become like a god! And your precious Princess Zelda will be nothing more than cold, dead, stone.|Vaati|TMC}}</ref> Along the way to {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} encounters a {{Term|TMC|Ball and Chain Soldier|link}}, a group of ten {{Plural|TMC|Purple Keaton|link}}, and a group composed of two {{Plural|TMC|Red Dark Nut}} and a second {{Term|TMC|Black Knight}}. The first two tolls of the bell are scripted to occur as soon as {{Term|TMC|Link}} passes through the doors following the first two challenges, but the third battle is in fact timed (although the game gives no indication that it is different from the others) and if {{Term|TMC|Link}} takes too long fighting the {{Plural|TMC|Dark Nut}} the bell will toll for a third time, resulting in a [[wikipedia:Failure|Game Over]].

Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} makes it to {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} in time, {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} calls on the incomplete power he has been able to extract from {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} thus far and the game's final battle commences.<ref>{{Cite|I have not yet drained all of the light force housed in Princess Zelda. But what I have should suffice... I shall be transformed! Unstoppable! But let me first attend to the pesky worms who would trifle with me...|Vaati|TMC}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|TMC|Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Purple Keaton|link}} appear with different palettes in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle}}, but although generally this implies a stronger or in some way different enemy, these are functionally identical to their counterparts found elsewhere in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}, having the same amount of health, dealing the same amount of damage to {{Term|TMC|Link}}, and exhibiting the same behaviors.
*Unlike the other {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss}} in the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}, and in {{TMC|-}} in general, the first {{Term|TMC|Black Knight}} has the background music normally heard during fights with main {{Plural|TMC|Boss|link}}. It shares this trait with the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}.
*The {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}'s theme music is a remixed version of the {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule Castle}} theme from {{ALttP}}.
