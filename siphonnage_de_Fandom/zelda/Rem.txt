{{Term/Store|Rem||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Rem}}

{{Term|TMC|Rem}} is a resident of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, and owns {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store|link}}. He has a habit of falling asleep while working on shoes, only to find them to be completed when he wakes up. This leads him to believe that he is skilled in crafting shoes while asleep.<ref>{{Cite|Lately, whenever I take a nap, all my shoes are done when I wake up! You know, if I can make shoes in my sleep, I must be in the right job!|Rem|TMC}}</ref> However, his shoes are really completed by the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} who live in {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store}}, and can be seen working while he sleeps.

When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is unable to cross the {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}, he must seek {{Term|TMC|Rem}}'s help to create the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}};<ref>{{Cite|Say, have you been to {{Color|TMC Green|Rem's Shoe Shop}}? His shoes really are the best. I'm sure they could help you on your quest. Why don't you give him a visit?|Smith|TMC}}</ref> however, {{Term|TMC|Rem}} has fallen into a deep sleep and the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} have taken to crafting the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots}} themselves. Despite this, {{Term|TMC|Rem}} is needed to add the finishing touches to the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|display=Boots}},<ref>{{Cite|Ho, ho! You need {{Color|TMC Red|Pegasus Boots}}? Why, you're just in time! We made a pair just now while {{Color|TMC Green|Rem}} was sleeping. But only {{Color|TMC Green|Rem}} can put the finishing touches on them...|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref> so {{Term|TMC|Link}} must retrieve a {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Syrup|link}} in her {{Term|TMC|Witch's Hut|link|display=Hut}} in the northernmost section of {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ha, ha! That sleepyhead {{Color|TMC Green|Rem}} is nearly impossible to wake up... If you want to wake him, you'll need to get an item from {{Color|TMC Green|Syrup}}'s hut. It's a little bit of a trek, but we'll mark it on your map for you.|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ho, ho! We've done all we can with these shoes. Just face the sleepy snoozer and press {{Icon|TMC R}}... That'll pop his eyes right open! POP!|Syrup|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If I recall correctly, and I guarantee that I do, my boy... After {{Term|TMC|Link}} awakens {{Term|TMC|Rem}} with the {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom}}, {{Term|TMC|Rem}} finishes the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots}} and gives them to {{Term|TMC|Link}} free of charge.<ref>{{Cite|Whaa--! What is that terrifyingly bad smell!? A wake-up mushroom?! I should have guessed! So you woke me up? Thanks, I just remembered I have something urgent to do! ?! Huh? What now?! {{Term|TMC|Rem}} is the author of a {{Term|TMC|Library Book|link|display=Book}} titled {{Term|TMC|Rem's Anthology for Sleepless Nights|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There seem to be many interesting books in here... {{Color|TMC Blue|Hyrule on 10 Rupees a Day}}, {{Color|TMC Blue|So Your Son Wants to Be a Hero}}... {{Color|TMC Blue|It Could Be Verse!}}, by Percy, and {{Color|TMC Blue|Rem's Anthology for Sleepless Nights}}. ==Trivia==

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Rem}}'s name derives from "[[Wikipedia:Rapid eye movement sleep|R.E.M.]]", also known as rapid eye movement, a stage of deep sleep.
