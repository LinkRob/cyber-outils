{{Term/Store|Kaifa||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kaifa|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} is a young {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman who works at {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}. Her duty is to take care of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Highland Sheep|link}}.<ref name="Kaifa"/> During {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, she seeks shelter under one of the {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} behind the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|This rain... My poor sheep...|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What depressing weather we're having, and so early in the day...|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}}, she asks if he is a customer.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... You're a customer...right?|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has confirmed that he is, she will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] OK, good.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} explains that a suspicious individual known as {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} has been appearing all throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lately a suspicious person named {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}} has been showing up in {{Color|BotW Red|various regions across the land}}.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> He is supposedly the owner of the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}, which she has heard only appears during the night.<ref>{{Cite|They say he's the owner of something called the {{Color|BotW Blue|Fang and Bone}}. It only appears at {{Color|BotW Red|night}}, or so they say.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> Due to the rumors, the nearby {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} has hired security, and {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be careful.<ref>{{Cite|It seems {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stable}} up north is hiring soldiers for protection because of the rumors about {{Color|BotW Red|Kilton}}. Be careful out there.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} again, she becomes nervous and asks if he needs something.<ref>{{Cite|Umm... {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|Again...}}}} Do you need something?|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask her about {{Term|BotW|Kilton}} again, and she admits that she does not feel secure, as the only man at {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}} is her father, {{Term|BotW|Dmitri|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Other than Father, it's just women here. I don't feel as secure as I'd like to.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> She also thinks they should hire soldiers like the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} did.<ref>{{Cite|We should probably do that, too...|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} who she is, she will be thrown off guard.<ref>{{Cite|[Who are you?] Me?! You can't ask me out of the blue like that... I'm not mentally prepared for this!|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> She then apologizes to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm...I'm sorry.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> When he asks her a second time, she takes a moment before introducing herself.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|*inhale* *exhale*}} Hi. I'm Kaifa.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> She explains that she is the eldest daughter at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, but after that she is unable to continue.<ref>{{Cite|I'm the eldest daughter. And... I'm...I'm sorry.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} where they are, and she explains that they are at the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|[What is this place?] This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|South Akkala Stable}}.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> Due to the large number of {{Term|BotW|Highland Sheep}} housed at the {{Term|BotW|Stable}}, many customers refer to it as a {{Term|BotW|Highland Sheep|display=Sheep}} {{Term|BotW|Stable}} instead of a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} {{Term|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|People usually say stuff like, "Isn't this more of a {{Color|BotW Blue|sheep stable}} than a horse stable?"|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} is not good at raising {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, she can do nothing to help the fact that they have had a lot of {{Term|BotW|Highland Sheep}} births.<ref>{{Cite|I may be bad at raising horses, but I can't help the fact that we've had a lot of sheep births here!|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kaifa}} begins to shout as she wonders what is wrong with having a lot of {{Term|BotW|Highland Sheep|display=Sheep}}.<ref>{{Cite|Who cares anyway? What's so wrong about that?!|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> After a moment of silence, she apologizes to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for her outburst.<ref>{{Cite|... ... *ahem* I'm sorry. It seems I've done it again...|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref> She will later claim that she does not care if the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} is referred to as the Akkala Sheep Stable.<ref>{{Cite|I really don't care if you start calling this place the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala Sheep Stable}}. Really. See. If. I. Care.|Kaifa|BotW}}</ref>
