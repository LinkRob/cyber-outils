{{Term/Store|Letter|Letters|Series, TLoZ, LA, LADX, LANS, OoT, OoT3D, FSA, ST|plural}} are recurring items and objects in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129 {{Exp Game|TLoZ, LA, OoT, FSA, ST}}}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Letter}} was referred to as the '''Letter to a Little Old Lady''' in {{T&T}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TLoZ|guide= Tips & Tactics|page= 13}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} They are the most common means of long-distance communication between characters.

{{Plural|Series|Letter}} are most often sent via {{Term|Series|Mail Delivery|link}} and delivered by the {{Term|Series|Postman|link}}. {{Term|Series|Link|link}} is occasionally tasked with mailing {{Plural|Series|Letter}} to a {{Term|Series|Postbox|link}}. Sometimes he personally {{Plural|Series|Delivery|link|display=Delivers}} certain {{Plural|Series|Letter}}. {{Plural|Series|Letter}} to be mailed or hand-delivered go in the {{Term|Series|Inventory|link}}. In many games, various characters mail {{Plural|Series|Letter}} to {{Term|Series|Link}}, sometimes enclosing items for him. In some games the {{Plural|Series|Letter}} are kept on the {{Term|Series|Collection Screen|link}} (specifically the {{Term|Series|Letters|link}} submenu) and can be read any time. In other games the {{Plural|Series|Letter}} are discarded after being read.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
[[File:TLoZ Letter Sprite.png|left]]
In {{TLoZ}}, [[Link]] can receive the Letter from an [[Letter-Entrusting Old Man|Old Man]] in a northeast cave of [[Hyrule]]. When Link shows the Letter to any [[Old Woman]], they will sell him [[Blue Water of Life|Blue]] and [[Red Water of Life|Red]] Waters of Life. The Letter reads:<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 221}}</ref>

{{Quote|This heroic lad is an acquaintance of Impa. Please give him aid.|Letter}}

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Main|Goat's Letter}}
In {{LA}}, {{Term|LANS|Christine|link}} asks {{Term|LANS|Link}} to deliver the {{Term|LANS|Goat's Letter}} to {{Term|LANS|Mr. Write|link}}.

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Main|Letter in a Bottle, Zelda's Letter}}
{{Stub|OoT}}
In {{OoT}}, a [[Letter in a Bottle]] can be found in [[Lake Hylia]]. It is a Letter from [[Princess Ruto]] that must be delivered to [[King Zora]] in order to gain access to [[Zora's Fountain]].

[[Zelda's Letter]] is given by [[Zelda]] to verify [[Link]]s work is for the [[Royal Family]]. Showing this to the the [[Hyrulean Soldier]] at the [[Kakariko Village]]s entrance to [[Death Mountain]] will open the gate. After showing the [[letter]] the [[Happy Mask side quest]] will begin.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Letter to Kafei, Special Delivery to Mama}}
{{Stub|MM}}
At the beginning of the {{Term|MM3D|First Day|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Kafei|link}} mails a letter to {{Term|MM3D|Anju|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Postman}} refuses to tell {{Term|MM3D|Anju}} which {{Term|MM3D|Postbox}} he collected it from. Later on in the {{Term|MM3D|Anju's Anguish|link}} {{Term|MM3D|Event}}, Link mails {{Term|MM3D|Anju}}'s {{Term|MM3D|Letter to Kafei|link}}.

On the {{Term|MM3D|Night of the Second Day|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Postman}} can be seen mailing a letter to himself.<ref>{{Cite|Last night, I wrote a letter to myself and deposited it in a post box... ==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Father's Letter, Moblin's Letter, Maggie's Letter, Note to Mom}}
In {{TWW}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} obtains four {{Plural|TWWHD|Letter}} for {{Term|TWWHD|Delivery|link}}. The first is {{Term|TWWHD|Father's Letter|link}} to {{Term|TWWHD|Komali|link}}. Afterward, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can mail the {{Term|TWWHD|Note to Mom|link}} for {{Term|TWWHD|Baito|link}}. He can do the same for {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie|link}} with {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie's Letter|link}}. He also delivers the reply—{{Term|TWWHD|Moblin's Letter|link}}—back to {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie}} as her father will not allow {{Term|TWWHD|Ilari|link}} to deliver it.<ref>{{Cite|I have an idea. That old fool may not permit postmen on his premises... But surely he would allow YOU in the place, would he not? Could you deliver this {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|letter}} to {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Maggie}} in my stead?|Ilari|TWW}}</ref> All of these {{Plural|TWWHD|Letter}} are stored in the {{Term|TWWHD|Delivery Bag|link}} in the {{Term|TWWHD|Inventory|link}}.

{{Term|TWWHD|Link}} himself receives {{Plural|TWWHD|Letter}} from other characters through the wiggling {{Plural|TWWHD|Postbox|link}} of the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service|link}}. The {{Plural|TWWHD|Letter}} always enclose an item, either {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}}, [[Chart]]s, or {{Plural|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}}. Once read, the {{Term|TWWHD|Letter}} is discarded and {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} obtains the item that came with it. 

The {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|TWWHD|Mini-Game}} has {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} sorting letters for the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service}}. His pay depends on the number of letters he can sort within the time limit.
