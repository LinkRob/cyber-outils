{{Term/Store|Zora Egg|Zora Eggs|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are items in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143}}</ref> They were laid by [[The Indigo-Go's]] singer, [[Lulu]]. 

==Location and Uses==
One late night, [[Lulu]] heard a strange sound in her room, and when she opened her eyes, saw [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirates]] entering her room. Despite her attempts to fight back, they were able to steal her eggs under guidance of the [[Skull Kid]].<ref>{{Cite|I heard a sound late at night, and when I opened my eyes, I saw strangers in my room. I tried to fight, but they were able to steal my precious eggs.|Lulu|MM}}</ref> Her close friend (if not love interest) and fellow band member, [[Mikau]], tried to take back the eggs that were taken by the thieving women by going to their [[Pirates' Fortress|fortress]]. However, he failed and ultimately ended up floating half-dead near the [[Great Bay Coast]].

According to [[Lake Scientist|the professor]] in the {{Term|MM3D|Marine Research Lab|link}}, it takes about one to three days for the Zora eggs to hatch after they are laid. Since the eggs are highly sensitive to changes in the temperature, the professor set aside an aquarium where the Eggs would be placed until they were hatched.<ref>{{Cite|Zora eggs are very sensitive to changes in the temperature. the only way the eggs can hatch is if they're placed in this aquarium water, which I set aside for them long ago.|Professor|MM}}</ref> If they are not kept in the specially-made aquarium, the Zora Eggs may die, for the recent abnormal weather in the [[Great Bay]] region has caused the ocean temperature to significantly rise and become murky.<ref>{{Cite|But if they aren't kept in this aquarium until then...they may die. The reason for this is that the recent abnormal weather has caused the ocean temperature to rise in this region.|Professor|MM}}</ref>

[[Link]] must infiltrate the Pirates' Fortress and recover four of the seven eggs that were stolen by the Gerudo. The three remaining eggs can be found in the murky waters of [[Pinnacle Rock]], where many [[Deep Python]]s guard the Zora Eggs in their deep underwater caves (as well as a [[Seahorse]]).<ref>{{Cite|Go find the other three eggs before those sea snakes eat them!|Aveil|MM}}</ref> As [[Zora Link]], the enormous serpents must be defeated before the eggs can be reached. Link can keep the Zora Eggs inside empty [[Bottle]]s.
[[File:MM Baby Zoras Model.png|thumb|The Zora Egg hatchlings]]
When the seven Zora Eggs are finally reunited inside the aquarium, they hatch and show Link the notes of the "[[New Wave Bossa Nova]]". This new melody must be played in front of Lulu in order for her to recover her voice. It will also awaken the guardian [[Giant Turtle]] of Great Bay, who will take the young [[Hero of Time]] to the [[Great Bay Temple]].

==Trivia==
*It is possible to sell a Zora Egg to the [[Curiosity Shop]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s each. However, this is not recommended if the "[[New Wave Bossa Nova]]" is not yet learned, as selling any of the eggs makes it impossible for [[Link]] to complete the sidequest during the current three-day cycle.
*The occurrence of the eggs hatching and forming eighth notes within a song is likely a visual pun on the Japanese word, {{Romanize|おたまじゃくし|Otamajakushi}}, which means "tadpole", but can also mean "musical note".
*When the Zora Eggs hatch when placed in the aquarium, they make a distorted, high-pitched version of the sound effect when [[Mario]] transforms into [[mariowiki:Raccoon_Mario|Raccoon Mario]] or any of his power-up Suits when collecting the required items in ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros._3|Super Mario Bros. 3]]'', from the ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise.
*While it is clear [[Lulu]] is the mother of the baby [[Zora|Zoras]] that hatch from the seven Zora Eggs, some fans speculated that [[Mikau]] is the father due to their apparent romantic relationship.
