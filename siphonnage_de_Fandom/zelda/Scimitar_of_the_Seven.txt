Used alongside the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven}} was a {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} used by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|display=Scimitar}} belongs to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created and utilized by the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> It is said that {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}'s movements resembled a dance while she used the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|display=Scimitar}} in battle.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} will gift {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} both the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven}} and the {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker}} after completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I want to offer you these as thanks for your help. I trust you'll accept them.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She believes that, since {{Term|BotW|Link}} was friends with {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}, they should be in his possession.<ref>{{Cite|These pieces of equipment are priceless treasures of the Gerudo people. They were once worn by {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Since you and {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa|link}} were friends, it's only right that you have them.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> They can be found in a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} loses the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven}}, he can go to {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}} to replace it, though it will cost him one {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}, five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|To make a {{Color|BotW Red|Scimitar of the Seven}}, I'll need {{Color|BotW Blue|one diamond}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|five flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo scimitar}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven}} is made of metal, and will therefore attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. This also means that it is capable of conducting {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. It will sink if dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but it can be picked up again using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. In addition, it will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.


==Other Appearances==

{{Stub|HWAoC}}
Alongside the {{Term|HWAoC|Daybreaker|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Scimitar of the Seven}} is exclusively wielded by {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} in combat. She wielded it against the {{Term|HWAoC|Yiga|link|display=Yiga Clan}} after exposing her impersonator, {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga|link}}, who was responsible for the {{Plural|HWAoC|Gerudo|link}} attacking {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Princess Zelda|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}} while she was away. She attempts to swipe at {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga|display=Kohga}}, but they flee before they can strike. She also points it at the {{Plural|HWAoC|Monster|link}} on her way to {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}. She uses it to battle {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} inside {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.
