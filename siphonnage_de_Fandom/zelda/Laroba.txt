{{Term/Store|Laroba||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Laroba|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Laroba}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} who travels between the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}} via the {{Term|BotW|Shadow Pass|link}}. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Laroba}} left the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} in search of a man to marry.<ref>{{Cite|I've traveled from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}} in the southwest all the way here to {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} on my journey to find a partner.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Some people travel the world for glory, for money, for thrills... But me, I do it for love.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Take me, for instance—my goal is to find a fine, strapping voe! Best of luck with whatever your goal is, kid!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> To further this goal, she sizes up every man she meets to gauge his eligibility.<ref>{{Cite|I size up every voe coming and going down the road for eligibility.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> Since they are all different ages, sizes, and races, she has difficulty making a decision, though she acknowledges that she likely appears creepy to them.<ref>{{Cite|They're different ages, sizes, sometimes even races, so it's hard to make a call. I probably look like a creep to them...|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She discourages {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} from pursuing her, though, claiming he is too young.<ref>{{Cite|Easy there, though, kid. You're way too young for me.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref>

During her journey, {{Term|BotW|Laroba}} has enjoyed meeting people from all over the world and getting to hear their perspectives.<ref>{{Cite|The best parts of it have been getting to meet at the peoples of the world and hearing their thoughts and perspectives.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> In particular, she appreciates that she can meet a variety of people in {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Zora|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Goron|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Rito|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You there! Hylian kid! It's nice to meet you. Out here in Akkala, we have all sorts: Zora, Goron, Rito, you name it.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She is glad that they are able to meet with each other, as they were more isolated in the past.<ref>{{Cite|Time was, we didn't mix much, but things change. And I'm glad they have!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She also encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to talk to the people he meets, no matter what they look like.<ref>{{Cite|That's my advice to you: don't be shy with the people you meet, no matter what they look like|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> On occasion, {{Term|BotW|Laroba}} remarks that {{Plural|BotW|Goron}} are hardy or that {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} are good folk.<ref>{{Cite|Gorons are hardy folk...|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hylians are good folk...|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She also likes those who can {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}},<ref>{{Cite|Cooks are the best folk.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> although she is curious about other travelers.<ref>{{Cite|Travelers are so curious.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laroba}} often gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} tips when it comes to using {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}}. For example, she informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he should aim for the heads of monsters and the eyes of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You any good with a bow, kid? If you can hit a mark, then try aiming for heads with monsters and eyes with guardians.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When using a bow, you want to aim for the head with monsters and the eyes with Guardians.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She encourages him to keep practicing his aim.<ref>{{Cite|Keep practicing your aim, and someday you might even pierce the heart of—monsters. I obviously meant monsters.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} to always stock up on {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, especially since life can always throw him for a loop.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! Even the best bow is useless if your quiver runs dry, right? I mean, what else are you going to do with it? Life has a funny way of throwing you for a loop, and it's not always as nice as bumping into a cutie out of nowhere. Make sure you stock up on arrows before something bumps into you. Get me?|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> In addition to keeping an eye on his {{Term|BotW|Quiver|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laroba}} also advises that {{Term|BotW|Link}} carry extra {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} in case his current one snaps.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! I'm sure you know by now that your quiver runs dry quick, but do you remember to keep an eye on your bow? It's best to keep a sturdy backup or two, just in case the one you're using snaps at the worst possible moment.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laroba}} claims to take care of her own {{Term|BotW|Bow}}, despite the fact that she does not visibly carry one.<ref>{{Cite|I keepy my most important weapon well-oiled and tightly strung. Otherwise, how will I ever pierce anyone's heart?|Laroba|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} ever finds himself in a battle, {{Term|BotW|Laroba}} suggests that he should decide if he fights or runs based on the condition of his {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! I'm sure you've fought your share of the riffraff, but have you ever run up against the really big ones? Stand and fight? Cut and run? It all depends on what condition your weapons are in, really. If your quiver runs dry and all your weapons break in the middle of a fight... Well, I hope you have a backup plan.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She also shares a rumor she heard about a {{Term|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link|display=rare set of arrows}} that are hidden deep in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Someone I met a while ago told me about a rare set of arrows deep inside {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. It might be something to remember if you ever find yourself exploring the place.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Remember to look for a rare set of arrows if you ever find yourself exploring the castle.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laroba}} proposes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} set a goal to reach on his {{Term|BotW|Map|link}}, occasionally checking to ensure he is on the right path.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! Are you trying to get someplace? Do you have a map? If you do, then it's good to set a goal to reach and then check your map every so often to get your bearings.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's good to set a goal to reach and then check your map every so often to get your bearings.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> In addition, she points out {{Term|BotW|Skull Lake|link}} on his {{Term|BotW|Map}}, explaining that he can encounter the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} there at night, if he has the stomach for it.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! Ever heard of a thing called {{Color|BotW Red|Fang and Bone}}? Check your map for a pond in the north that looks like a skull. If it's not on there yet, you'll know it when you see it. That pond at night is where you'll run into {{Color|BotW Red|Fang and Bone}}, if you've got the stomach for it.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laroba}} is troubled when it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}}, proclaiming that she will never get used to it.<ref>{{Cite|I'll never get used to rain.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> However, she also considers it a way to cool off when she gets too flushed while staring at a man.<ref>{{Cite|The rain helps cool me off when I get too flushed from staring at voe.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Laroba}} is threatened by an [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemy]], she will flee in terror.<ref>{{Cite|Somebody, please!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nooo!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yikes!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yikes! Help!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh nooooo!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> While she is in danger, she can claim that her stomach is tied in knots or that she has had enough mortal peril.<ref>{{Cite|Close calls like this get my stomach all tied up in knots.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|OK, that was enough mortal peril for one day.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, she may comment that she likes {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s style or that she owes him.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, kid! I like your style!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks for stepping in, kid. I owe you!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> She might also promise him a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}} if she ever returns to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whew. Kid, if I ever make it back to Gerudo Town, the first glass of Noble Pursuit is on me.|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> Compared to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she thinks she looks like a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} pup.<ref>{{Cite|Next to you, kid, I might as well be a sand-seal pup!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Tough Poultry Curry|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I owe you big-time, and I always repay my debts!|Laroba|BotW}}</ref> On subsequent rescues, she hands out {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.
