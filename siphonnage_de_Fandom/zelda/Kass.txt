{{Term/Store|Kass||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kass|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Kass}} is a minstrel who travels throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} in search of the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}".<ref name="Kass">{{Cite|Allow me to introduce myself. My name is Kass. I'm a bard traveling the land in search of ancient songs.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He has left his family and his hometown of {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} to fulfill the final wish of his teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}}. This journey is documented in {{Term|BotW|Kass's Journal|link}}, which can be found in a hut on {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|"I leave here a record of my search for the ancient songs of Hyrule."<br/>- {{Color|BotW Blue|Kass}}|Kass’s Journal|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first encounters {{Term|BotW|Kass}}, he will recognize the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} that he is carrying, though he excuses himself for prying.<ref>{{Cite|Greetings, traveler! How about a song? Th-that there...on your hip! ...No, I'm sorry—it's nothing. I didn't mean to pry.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if he is a bird, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} is shocked to hear that he has never seen a {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}} before.<ref>{{Cite|[Are you a...bird?] Have you... never met a Rito before? Odd.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He then introduces himself to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks if he has heard of the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}."<ref name="Kass"/><ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Red|ancient songs}} of Hyrule?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He goes on to explain that the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}" are dedicated to the [[Link#The_Hero_10,000_Years_Prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|ancient hero]] who defeated {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} 10,000 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Ancient songs—songs that sing the praises of a hero who beat back the Calamity in an age past.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

During his journey, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} can be found at several {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}}. There, he plays a version of [[Epona's Song]] that harmonizes with the regular "{{Term|BotW|Stables|link}}" theme.<ref name="Stable">{{Cite|But according to my teacher, it was a song performed by the ancient hero to call to his horse companion.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At these {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, he will offer to sing an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song}}" for {{Term|BotW|Link}} that recounts the previous sealing of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, which was taught to him by the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}.<ref>{{Cite|I happen to know a song about the ancient hero. It was passed down to me by my teacher. Do you care to hear it?|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anyway, I should return to my craft.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After singing the song for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} thanks him and welcomes him back to listen to it again.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for listening. Please come back and hear it again some time.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has seen the nearby {{Term|BotW|Kaya Wan Shrine|link}}, as he knows a lot about {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We meet again! Small kingdom! Did you see the {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} near this stable? I know quite a bit about the {{Color|BotW Red|shrines}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he learned about them from the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}, as he was a member of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} tribe that often accompanied {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} as she surveyed ruins.<ref>{{Cite|You see, my teacher was the court poet for the Hyrulean royal family and also a member of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah tribe}}. He was well versed in ancient civilizations and surveyed ruins in the company of the princess, it seems.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} recounts that the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} was always happy when he shared these memories with him.<ref>{{Cite|Those memories seemed to bring him a great deal of joy when he would speak of them.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

Upon meeting {{Term|BotW|Link}} at {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} informs him that there are many theories about the origin of the song he plays at {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, we meet yet again! How are you holding up? In case you were wondering about the song I perform at the stables, there are many theories about its origin.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He heard from the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} that it was a song played by the ancient hero to call his {{Term|BotW|Epona|link|display=horse companion}}.<ref name="Stable"/> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back and listen to it.<ref>{{Cite|If you ever feel like hearing it, feel free to drop by.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will be impressed that he made it so far, as even he struggled to make it there by flying.<ref>{{Cite|Good to see you again! I trust you're keeping well? You've come quite some distance. Even I, blessed with the gift of flight, struggled a bit to make it here.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He recounts that the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}, who served the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}, was forced to flee {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} when the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}} struck.<ref>{{Cite|...My teacher was the court poet in the employ of the Hyrulean royal family. One hundred years ago, he came face-to-face with the Calamity at {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} and was forced to flee.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He imagines that it was a much more dangerous journey than his own.<ref>{{Cite|It was a perilous journey—very different from my own travels, I imagine. Danger was his constant companion...|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

When spoken to at {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will confess that he is homesick.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, we meet again! I'm glad to see you in one piece. We're actually very close to my hometown of Rito Village, and I must admit I'm feeling incredibly homesick.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He admits that he had to leave his wife, {{Term|BotW|Amali|link}}, and their children behind when he set out on his journey, and he hopes they are doing well.<ref name="Rito"/> However, he reveals that he is unable to return until he fulfills his promise to the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}.<ref>{{Cite|But you see, I cannot go home until I fulfill my promise to my teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about his promise, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will begin to explain, but he then decides now is not the time to reveal his promise.<ref>{{Cite|[Promise?] My promise to... On second thought, now is not the time.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that after he learns all of the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}," he will share his promise with him in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|But if I'm able to learn all of the ancient songs...I will tell you of my promise back in my hometown.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass}} can also be found in several locations in the wild. Hints for these locations can be found in {{Term|BotW|Kass's Journal}}, which documents all of the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}" that he knows of. At each of these locations, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will receive a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} after listening to an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song}}" from {{Term|BotW|Kass}}. They are as follows:

* "{{Term|BotW|The Serpent's Jaws|link}}" in the {{Term|BotW|Pagos Woods|link}}
* "{{Term|BotW|The Two Rings|link}}" on the {{Term|BotW|West Hyrule Plains|link}}
* "{{Term|BotW|The Crowned Beast|link}}" on {{Term|BotW|Rabia Plain|link}}
* "{{Term|BotW|Sign of the Shadow|link}}" at {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower|link}}
* "{{Term|BotW|A Song of Storms|link}}" at {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake|link}}
* "{{Term|BotW|Master of the Wind|link}}" at {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon|link}}
* "{{Term|BotW|The Hero's Cache|link}}" at {{Term|BotW|Kitano Bay|link}}
* "{{Term|BotW|Under a Red Moon|link}}" at {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}

In his {{Term|BotW|Kass's Journal|display=''Journal''}}, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} pledges to continue his journey until he can pass the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}" on to the {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|These are all of the ancient songs that I have discovered. I will continue my journey, hoping the day will come when I can pass them on to the Champion of Hyrule.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} solves the riddles in each of the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}," {{Term|BotW|Kass}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, where he will perform with his bandoneon next to {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm taking a moment's rest from my tireless search for the ancient songs to perform in my hometown.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The final page in {{Term|BotW|Kass's Journal}} will also change, revealing that he is pleased to have shared all of the "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}" with {{Term|BotW|Link}}, fulfilling his promise to the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have finally managed to pass all of the ancient songs to the Champion of Hyrule. My quest is complete, I now return to {{Color|BotW Blue|Rito Village}}.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> During the day, he will be joined by his daughters, {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kotts|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Genli|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}}, who sing along as he plays.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, we meet again! When visited at night, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} reveals that he has a song that he would like {{Term|BotW|Link}} to listen to.<ref>{{Cite|...By the by, I've something I must ask of you. Is that all right?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} thanks him and says that he wishes to talk about his teacher, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}.<ref>{{Cite|[That's fine.] Thank you ever so much. {{Term|BotW|Kass}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that his teacher was a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} poet who lived in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}} before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity}} struck.<ref>{{Cite|My teacher was of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah tribe}}. He was the court poet for the Hyrulean royal family.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> During this time, the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} fell in love with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}, who he was close in age with.<ref>{{Cite|At the time, there was a beautiful princess in the royal family, quite close to my teacher in age, apparently. He poured that belief into a song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} then plays that song for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, entitled the "{{Term|BotW|Song of the Hero|link}}."<ref>{{Cite|And that song is what I need you to hear.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

After finishing the song, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} continues his story, explaining that the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}} witnessed {{Term|BotW|Link}} sacrifice himself for {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} while fleeing the {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Calamity}}.<ref>{{Cite|My teacher fled the Calamity and returned to his hometown of Kakariko Village. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kass}} again, he will express his gratitude that he listened to the "{{Term|BotW|Song of the Hero}}" for him.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for listening to my teacher's song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back at night if he wants to hear it again, as it is the only time his daughters leave him alone.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to hear the song again, you'll need to come back at nighttime. ==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Kass}} is potentially derived from ''{{Wp|cassowary}}'', a type of large, flightless bird, though he largely resembles a Blue-and-yellow Macaw. 
The name Kasshīwa is a play on the Japanese word "[https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%81%8B%E3%81%97%E3%82%8F kashiwa]", which originally referred to a certain breed of chicken but is now used more widely in western Japan to refer to various kinds of poultry dishes.
