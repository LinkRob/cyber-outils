The {{Term/Store|Goron Dance||Series, OoA}} is a {{Term|OoA|Mini-Game|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hi, Link! The Goron Dance is all the rage in these mountains! But the Subrosian Dance is way cooler. These Gorons have a ways to go!|Rosa|OoA}}</ref>

==Features==
The {{Term|OoA|Goron Dance}} is located in the {{Term|OoA|Goron Dance}} Hall, which is held inside the base of {{Term|OoA|Rolling Ridge}}. The dance is hosted by a {{Term|OoA|Goron|link}} who instructs seven {{Plural|OoA|Goron}} within the dance hall. The minigame can be played in both the present and the past. In the present, it costs 10 {{Plural|OoA|Rupee}} to play, while in the past, it costs 20 {{Plural|OoA|Rupee}}.

The minigame involves the {{Plural|OoA|Goron}} and {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} following the moves of the dance instructor by moving left or right, or by striking a pose. However, {{Term|OoA|Link}} has to keep up with the instructor's rhythm. If {{Term|OoA|Link}} moves too late or too quickly, he will miss and lose his chance of getting a perfect score. Each round consists of eight moves.

The {{Plural|OoA|Goron}} who dance professionally are called "Graceful Dancers," and that only people who dance well can be considered a {{Term|OoA|Goron}} within the {{Term|OoA|Rolling Ridge}} {{Term|OoA|Goron}} tribe.<ref>{{Cite|One must know our dance if he is to be called a Goron. If you dance well, I'll give you a Symbol of Brotherhood!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> In the past, the dances are instructed by a red {{Term|OoA|Goron}}, who is the first generation of Graceful Dancers.<ref>{{Cite|I am the first generation of Graceful Dancers!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> In the present, a blue {{Term|OoA|Goron}} instructs the dancers, who is the fifth generation.<ref>{{Cite|I am the fifth generation of Graceful Dancers!|Goron|OoA}}</ref>

The prize for dancing with no more than two flaws is the {{Term|OoA|Brother Emblem|link}}, which proves {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s worth to the {{Plural|OoA|Goron}}. After acquiring the {{Term|OoA|Brother Emblem}}, the {{Term|OoA|Goron}} who blocks the stairway from "non-brothers" allows {{Term|OoA|Link}} to pass.<ref>{{Cite|Do you have a passionate heart? Only proven Goron brothers may pass. Be off!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|OoA|Link}} can dance again to win {{Plural|OoA|Rupee}}, {{Plural|OoA|Gasha Seed|link}}, and {{Plural|OoA|Magic Ring|link}}.
