{{Term/Store|Ciela||Series, PH}} is [[Link]]'s [[Fairy]] [[Link's Partners|companion]] in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Stub|PH}}
{{Term|PH|Ciela}} first meets Link when he lands on {{Term|PH|Mercay Island|link}}, where she explains to him that she lost her memory.<ref>{{Cite|I lost my memory...a long time ago.|Ciela|PH}}</ref> She then takes him to her grandpa, {{Term|PH|Oshus|link}}. Eventually, she and {{Term|PH|Link}} begin to travel together along with {{Term|PH|Linebeck|link}}, whom she does not like much and often fights with. Prior to the game's beginning, {{Term|PH|Ciela}} was attacked by {{Term|PH|Bellum|link}}, which caused her to lose her power and memories and wash up on {{Term|PH|Mercay Island}}'s beach.<ref>{{Cite|Long ago, Bellum attacked you, stealing your power and memories...|Oshus|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When I woke up on this beach, Grandpa rescued me!|Ciela|PH}}</ref> After completing the {{Term|PH|Temple of Courage|link}} in {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}, another Fairy much like {{Term|PH|Ciela}} appears. {{Term|PH|Link}} goes to {{Term|PH|Oshus}}, who then uses his staff to fuse the new {{Term|PH|Fairy}} and {{Term|PH|Ciela}} together. This makes her memories come back, revealing that she is the Spirit of Time and Courage.<ref name=title/>

In the final scene before the credits, {{Term|PH|Ciela}} says her goodbyes to {{Term|PH|Link}} and {{Term|PH|Linebeck}}. She eventually gains respect for {{Term|PH|Linebeck}} as he does for her.

===Abilities===
By collecting 10 {{Plural|PH|Courage Gem|link}} and offering them at {{Term|PH|Spirit Island|link}}, {{Term|PH|Ciela}} can allow {{Term|PH|Link}} to perform a {{Term|PH|Sword Beam|link}}. Collecting 20 gems doubles the width of the beam. In the final boss fights, {{Term|PH|Ciela}} provides {{Term|PH|Link}} with {{Plural|PH|Phantom Sphere|link}}, allowing him to temporarily freeze time.

==Non-Canon Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{PH|-}} Manga===
In the {{PH|Himekawa}}, {{Term|PH|Ciela}}'s lost memories take the form of a little girl. {{Term|PH|Link}} and {{Term|PH|Linebeck}} first encounter her on the {{Term|PH|Isle of Gust|link}}. {{Term|PH|Link}} comments that she seems like an empty shell and {{Term|PH|Linebeck}} warns him that she might be a threat to them. However, the girl is soon attacked by {{Term|PH|Crayk|link}} and {{Term|PH|Link}} departs to {{Term|PH|Molida Island}} to rescue her. Once {{Term|PH|Link}} defeats {{Term|PH|Crayk}}, the girl responds by thanking them. {{Term|PH|Link}} expects to see the {{Term|PH|Ciela||display= Spirit of Courage}}, but {{Term|PH|Oshus}} appears and tells {{Term|PH|Link}} that she is already there. {{Term|PH|Link}} turns to the girl and asks if it is her, but {{Term|PH|Oshus}} tells him that she is not the {{Term|PH|Ciela||display= Spirit}}. He then tells {{Term|PH|Ciela}} that he is sorry that he hid the truth for so long and {{Term|PH|Ciela}}'s memory rejoins with her, creating the {{Term|PH|Ciela||display= Spirit of Courage}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Term|PH|Ciela}}'s vocal sounds are identical to those of {{Term|OoT|Navi|link}} from {{OoT}}, though they are heard less frequently.
*During the final battle with {{Term|PH|Bellum}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} cannot use the {{Term|PH|Sword Beam}} due to {{Term|PH|Ciela}}'s capture at the hands of {{Term|PH|Bellum}}.
*{{Term|PH|Ciela}} is depicted with three colorations: white at the beginning of the game, yellow after her true identity is revealed, and blue on official artworks, the blue color also being identical to Navi.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|PH|Ciela}} is similar to "cielo", the Spanish and Italian word for "sky" (although the name can also connotate "heaven"). In addition, "ciel" is the French word for "sky". This is appropriate given the nature of {{Term|PH|Ciela}}'s character to fly around the screen.
