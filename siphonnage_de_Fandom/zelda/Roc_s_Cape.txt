The {{Term/Store|Roc's Cape||Series, OoS, FS, TMC}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 136 {{Exp Game|OoS, FS, TMC}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|Roc's Cape}} is an item that allows {{Term|Series|Link|link}} to jump and glide over holes and between platforms. 

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, it is an upgraded version of the {{Term|OoS|Roc's Feather|link}} hidden in the {{Term|OoS|Explorer's Crypt|link}}, allowing him to cross wider gaps and evade enemy attacks by granting him the ability to glide across three spaces and double jump.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|OoS Red|Roc's Cape}}! Press and hold the button to do a double jump!|N/A|{{OoS}}}}</ref> Usage of the {{Term|OoS|Roc's Cape}} is paramount in traversing the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon|link}} and in the fight against {{Term|OoS|Gleeok|link}}, allowing {{Term|OoS|Link}} to become airborne in order to evade {{Term|OoS|Gleeok}}'s stomping attack.

==={{FS|-}}===
{{Manual|FS|Roc's Cape|This magical mantle allows you to jump. Press the A Button in midair to spread your Roc's Cape and coast a little farther.}}
The {{Term|FS|Roc's Cape}} is also an item in {{FS}}, notably found in {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace|link}} and the [[Sea of Trees]]. It allows Link to cross chasms and rivers without falling in them. Pressing B after pressing A will make Link do a Down Thrust which kills all enemies and destroys any grass near the area where it was performed. 

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, it is hidden in the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}. It is crucial for navigating the palace and for fighting against the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Pair|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} can use the {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape}} to ring the bell in the center of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, causing a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} to fall from it.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TMC|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{TMC|Himekawa}}, {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} a {{Term|Series|Map|link}} of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} fastened with a {{Term|TMC|Cucco|link}} feather. The feather is magic, and can draw maps. When {{Term|TMC|Link}} is attacked by the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Pair}} above the clouds, the feather transforms into the {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I... I'm flying! The feather turned into a cape! Cool!|book= TMC (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 142}}</ref> However, it can still turn back into the magic feather.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The [[Magic Cape#Zelda: The Wand of Gamelon|Magic Cape]] of {{TWoG}} has a near identical use to the {{Term|Series|Roc's Cape}}. Like the {{Term|Series|Roc's Cape}}, it is also associated with a feather item.

==Nomenclature==
The {{Term|Series|Roc's Cape}} is believed to be named after the {{wp|Roc (mythology)|Roc}}, a mythical bird of enormous size and strength.
