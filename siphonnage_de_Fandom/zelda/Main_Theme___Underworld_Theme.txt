"'''Main Theme / Underworld Theme'''" is a Song in {{SSB4}}.

==Overview==
"Main Theme / Underworld Theme" is comprised of two songs from the original {{TLoZ}}, the overworld theme and the dungeon theme. In {{SSB4|3DS|-}}, it plays on the Spirit Train Stage, and in {{SSB4|Wii U|-}} it plays on the Skyloft Stage.

"Main Theme / Underworld Theme" is the ninth track in disc 2 of ''{{Ssb|Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS / Wii U ♪—A Smashing Soundtrack—|Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS/Wii U: A Smashing Soundtrack}}''.
