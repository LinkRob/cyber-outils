{{Term/Store|Dexivine|Dexivines|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are plant-like enemies in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 175}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Dexivines are small, purplish tentacles that often hide in grass and will emerge from the ground when [[Link]] approaches, attempting to grab onto him and drain his [[Magic Meter|magic power]]. Although they are not themselves dangerous, both dungeons in which they are found see extensive use of the [[Deku Leaf]], an item which requires magic to use, so they can be detrimental to Link's progress if they are not destroyed quickly. Fortunately, Dexivines can easily be dispatched with a swing from the sword, though they will grow back in a matter of seconds and can never truly be killed, so it is best not to linger for too long in an area infested with these troublesome vines.

The [[Forbidden Woods]] has a small area where the Dexivines spawn, and the [[Wind Temple]] also has a number of deep canyons where Dexivines lie in wait for Link to miscalculate a jump amongst the platforms high above. They tend to live hidden in grass.

Dexivines are not recognized by [[Carlov]] and do not have a figurine in the [[Nintendo Gallery]].
