'''Captain Olimar''' is the main protagonist of the ''[[Pikmin (series)|Pikmin]]'' series. He does not appear in any games of {{TLoZ|Series}}, but he has fought either with or against [[Link]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], and [[Ganondorf]] within the {{SSB|Series}} since ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''.

==Background==
He is a tiny alien from the planet Hocotate and appears human-like but has pointed ears and a bulbous nose.  He is actually about the size of a quarter in relation to a human.  Olimar is an astronaut for the interstellar transportation company Hocotate Transportation Company. He crash lands on the planet Earth and befriends creatures known as [[Pikmin]]. The Pikmin help Olimar repair his ship before he runs out breathable air and dies due to substances in the air that are poisonous to him.

==''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]''==
{{noncanon}}
[[File:Pikmin and Olimar screenshot SSBB.jpg|thumb]]
Olimar is a playable character alongside his Pikmin helpers. Olimar needs to have pikmin that he can pluck out of the ground to attack.  How many Pikmin and what types of Pikmin he plucks has a big effect on the match.  The Pikmin will follow Olimar wherever he goes and, like in the games, will even die for him.

Olimar is a anagram of the name [[Mario]] and is actually a bit similar to him.
