{{Term/Store|Copious Meat Skewers|Copious Meat Skewers|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Copious Meat Skewers}} can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a minimum of four different types of meat together. If consumed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} it will restore his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} by between ten and twenty {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, depending on the quantity and quality of the {{Plural|BotW|Ingredient|link}} that were used to make it. The preparation of Copious Meat Skewers is simple, consisting only of shoving meat onto a skewer.<ref name="Inventory"/>

A fifth ingredient can be added to Copious Meat Skewers, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hearty ingredient. When eaten, [[Link|Link's]] Hearts will be completely healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an energizing ingredient. Eating these skewers will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an enduring ingredient. They completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when consumed.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hasty ingredient. Link's movement speed will be increased by eating these skewers.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a spicy ingredient. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an electric ingredient. Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when he eats them.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a mighty ingredient. When eaten, Link will receive a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a tough ingredient. If consumed, Link's defense will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a sneaky ingredient. They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Copious Meat Skewers''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Copious Meat Skewers}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Just shove a bunch of meat on to a skewer and you're good to go.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a chilly ingredient. When these skewers are eaten, Link gains one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
