The "{{Term/Store|Inverted Song of Time||Series, MM, MM3D}}" is a recurring [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:MM3D Inverted Song of Time Icon.png|left]]
The "Inverted Song of Time" lets [[Link]] slow the flow of [[time]]. When played, all events related to the flow of time will occur at only 30% of their original speed, making completing Dungeons or other story-related events much easier. Events that are not directly involved with the flow of time, such as an enemy attacks or the rate at which Link can move, are not affected. In {{MM3D}}, playing the "Inverted Song of Time" causes all events directly related to the flow of time to occur at half of their normal speed. As its name implies, the song is the "[[Song of Time]]" merely played backwards.<ref>{{Cite|If you play that mysterious song backward, you can slow the flow of time. And if you play each note twice in a row, you can move a half day forward in time!|Scarecrow|MM}}</ref> Link can learn about the "Inverted Song of Time" by talking to a [[Scarecrow]]; however, it will not be saved in the Quest Status screen.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
The "Inverted Song of Time" appears as an [[Item Card]] in the [[Termina Map]] in [[Adventure Mode]] in {{HW}}. It is represented by an upside down [[Ocarina of Time (Item)|Ocarina of Time]]. When the "Inverted Song of Time" is used, 10 extra hours are added to the time counter.
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* It is likely that the Inverted Song of Time is the replacement for the originally planned seven-day cycle of {{MM|-}}, specifically the original N64 version, due to the rate of the slowed flow of time running at approximately 1/3 of the normal speed.
