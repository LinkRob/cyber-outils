The {{Term/Store|North Lomei Labyrinth||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|'''North Lomei Labyrinth'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Enormous Maze''',<ref>{{Cite|[Tell me about Harry.] There's an {{Color|BotW Red|enormous maze}} beyond the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Tundra}} just north of here. I don't know who built it or when.|Monkton|BotW}}</ref> is a location in {{BotW}}.

==Features and Overview==
North Lomei Labyrinth is located in the [[Hebra Mountains]].<ref>{{Cite|I have info on the pitch-black ruins and on what's at the far edge of the Hebra Mountains. Light is unable to enter these ruins for some reason. It's said that you can't see an inch ahead, even during the day! There must be some mysterious power behind this phenomenon, don't you think? I can only wonder at what's hiding within those ruins… But finding whatever it is would certainly make you famous! Where is it? It should be past Mount Drena if you're heading toward the Eldin Mountains.|Juannelle|BotW}}</ref>
Upon entering the Labyrinth, [[Link]] hears the voice of the [[Monk]], [[Monk#Qaza Tokki|Qaza Tokki]], telling him that he shall receive a blessing at the end of the Labyrinth,<ref>{{Cite|You who have reached this point... Proceed onward through this maze, and you will receive a blessing as reward.|???|BotW}}</ref> initiating the [[Shrine Quest]] "[[Trial on the Cliff]]". Link can see an [[Ancient Shrine]] behind portcullises in the center of the maze. To navigate the maze, Link can climb across walls to avoid touching the [[Pool of Malice|Pools of Malice]] that cover the Labyrinth's northern path. Throughout the Labyrinth, Link can find several [[Treasure Chest]]s containing a [[Silver Rupee]], a [[Gold Rupee]], an [[Edge of Duality]], 5 [[Ice Arrow]]s and a [[Knight's Shield]]. Link can also find an [[Enhanced Lizal Spear]], a [[Meteor Rod]] and a [[Hunter's Shield]] near a [[Lizalfos]] camp in the labyrinth's eastern corner. A [[Knight's Bow]] can be found in a small tunnel above the camp. Once Link reaches the end of the Labyrinth, he will find a walkway that lets him drop into the chamber with the [[Qaza Tokki Shrine]], completing the Shrine Quest.

On the cliffs surrounding the Labyrinth, [[Stalkoblin]]s can be found at night riding {{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse|link}}. [[Stalmoblin]]s may also rise up out of the ground at night.
