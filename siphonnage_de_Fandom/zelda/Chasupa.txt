{{Term/Store|Chasupa|Chasupa|Series, ALttP, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring flying eyeball enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 391 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Chasupa}} behave much like {{Term|ALttP|Keese|link}}, waiting for {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} to come within their proximity, at which point they begin flying towards him. {{Plural|ALttP|Chasupa}} appear in {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule Castle Tower|link}}, and later in the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}}, such as inside the {{Term|ALttP|Thieves' Town (Dungeon)|link}}. The {{Term|ALttP|Dark World}} {{Plural|ALttP|Chasupa}} appear identical, though they are notably stronger, being able to withstand more hits and cause more damage.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Chasupa}} appear predominately in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} and behave much in the same way as they did in {{ALttP|-}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}} is the only [[Hyrule]]an dungeon in which they appear.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, {{Plural|TFH|Chasupa}} appear and behave identically to those in {{ALBW|-}}. They appear in three [[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Levels]] of {{Term|TFH|The Ruins|link}} (excluding {{Term|TFH|Palace Noir|link}}) and in the first Floor of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}' {{Term|TFH|Shadow Zone|link}}. {{Plural|TFH|Chasupa}} require three hits from a standard {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} to defeat, but can be killed instantly with a single hit from the {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}}.
