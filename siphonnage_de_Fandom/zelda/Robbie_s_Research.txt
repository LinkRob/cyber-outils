==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} uses the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to take a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}}, she will ask him to retrieve some ancient {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} for her.<ref>{{Cite|You don't have to worry about this until after you see Impa, but I want you to bring me back some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient materials}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> She says he can deliver them either to her or {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}, her fellow researcher at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bring them either to {{Color|BotW Red|me}} or to {{Color|BotW Red|Robbie}} at the {{Color|BotW Red|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she promises him "something good" from both of them,<ref>{{Cite|As if my joy weren't enough, if you bring us {{Color|BotW Red|ancient materials}}, we'll give you {{Color|BotW Red|something good}} as a reward.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> which begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

At the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can speak to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I...it's... An...it's...|Ancient Oven|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} and {{Term|BotW|Jerrin|link}} catch him doing so.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! Hm. That's too bad. If you WERE the legendary Link, there would be something important I'd need to tell you.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> and he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to show the wounds he suffered 100 years ago to prove his identity.<ref>{{Cite|But how can I determine with full certainty whether or not you are the true hero? Yes, how indeed? Hm? Hm, hm, yes. If you can show me the wounds you {{Color|BotW Red|body}} suffered 100 years ago, that should prove you are truly Link.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>

I'm afraid someone will have to bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} again...|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|BotW|Link}} can activate the {{Term|BotW|Furnace}} before interacting with {{Term|BotW|Robbie}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} in {{Term|BotW|Akkala}} is located on top of {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}. The path between the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} and the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} is filled with {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}. Due to how common {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}} is in {{Term|BotW|Akkala}}, there are also {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|link}} lining the pathway. Once he returns to the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can light the {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}} outside, activating the {{Term|BotW|Travel Gate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab has been added to your map as a travel gate.}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

Inside, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} will excitedly inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} is once again operational, attributing it to the power of his love.<ref>{{Cite|You! Link! Listen up, hm! My darling {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} is back to normal! Such is the power of love... My love made {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} well again!|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> He grows disappointed upon being told that it was {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s doing,<ref>{{Cite|[The furnace is lit.] ... Oh... Well, thank you. Yes, quite.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> but gifts him with three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}} to express his gratitude.<ref>{{Cite|Now then, please take this as a gift of my gratitude that {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} is fixed!|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he made them using {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} technology and his own upgrades, making them effective against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|They are made using secret Sheikah technology. With the addition of my upgrades, they have morphed into... Anti-Calamity weapons! So to speak... Needless to say, they are an incredibly powerful type of ancient soldier gear. I like to call them... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Ancient arrows}}!}}|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} is capable of making {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow}} and other types of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear}},<ref>{{Cite|In addition to {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient arrows}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} can make all kinds of ancient soldier gear.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> though they require {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to manufacture.<ref>{{Cite|If you're looking to acquire some, give your {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient materials}}... {{Small|and rupees}}...to {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} are needed to fund the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}}'s research.<ref>{{Cite|Ever since the fall of Hyrule Castle, we haven't had much in the way of funds for our research.
