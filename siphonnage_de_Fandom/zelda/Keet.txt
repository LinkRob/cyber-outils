{{Term/Store|Keet||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 294}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Keet}}, and oddly enough for {{Color|SS Blue|Skyloft}}, he's a very normal boy. He's known to have an interest in {{Color|SS Blue|Kina}}, the waitress at the Lumpy Pumpkin
}}

Keet is a young man who regularly frequents the [[Lumpy Pumpkin]] and is found there throughout the majority of the game, save for one instance where he can be found in the [[Skyloft]] Plaza before [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] is kidnapped. He is one of the two audience members, the other being [[Rusta]], who watches [[Link]] and [[Kina]]'s duet.

According to [[Fi]], he is known to have an interest in Kina.<ref>{{Cite|He's known to have an interest in {{Color|SS Blue|Kina}}, the waitress at the Lumpy Pumpkin.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Keet's name is shortened from {{Wp|parakeet}}, a type of parrot.
