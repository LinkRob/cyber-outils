<noinclude>{{TLoZ|colon}} {{ST}} uses the form of [[Hylian (Language)|Hylian text]] introduced in {{TWW}}, which is based on Japanese {{Wp|Kana|kana}}.

Due to the linguistic differences between English and Japanese, many translations can be highly contextual and open to interpretation. The translations listed below should be considered the best available estimates.</noinclude>

Any errors found in the in-game text are retained in the '''Hylian''' column, but corrected in the '''Transliteration''' and '''Meaning''' columns.
