{{Term/Store|Lost at Sea Station||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}, which houses the Mini-{{Term|ST|Dungeon|link}} of the same name.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 290}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Lost at Sea Station}} was referred to as '''Lost at Sea Temple''' in {{Guide|ST|Prima}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= ST|publisher= Prima|page= 270}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Entrance to the Station==
Situated on an island in the southeast corner of the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Lost at Sea Station}} can only be accessed once {{Term|ST|Link|link}} gives five pieces of {{Term|ST|Dark Ore|link}} to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}}. As a sign of gratitude, he will reward the young hero with a {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}} that reveals the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} to the Station.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, this is more than enough! Thanks, kid! Let me shake your hand!|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref>

When arriving at the {{Term|ST|Lost at Sea Station}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} encounters a cliff face and little else at ground level. To reach the top of the cliff, where numerous {{Plural|ST|Bird|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Crow|link}} and {{Plural|ST|Cucco|link}} await, he must play the {{Term|ST|Song of Birds|link}} to call a {{Term|ST|Bird}} carrying a trapeze, and use the {{Term|ST|Whip|link}} to grapple onto it. At the top, a hidden cave can be found by activating a {{Term|ST|Beacon|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Song of Light|link}}, then {{Term|ST|Bomb|link|display=Bombing}} the rear wall where the {{Term|ST|Beacon}}'s light shines.

Within the cave, there is nothing but a {{Term|ST|Gossip Stone|link}}; however, when it is activated with the {{Term|ST|Song of Awakening|link}}, it will tell {{Term|ST|Link}} of a new temple, based on a "legendary temple" from a distant land.<ref>{{Cite|This temple is a replica of a legendary temple in a distant kingdom. Many treasures are hidden within. And to protect these treasures are many Phantoms.|Gossip Stone|ST}}</ref> If Link agrees to enter the temple, he will be transported into it, thereupon unable to use his {{Term|ST|Sword|link}} or {{Term|ST|Bow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you wish to enter, you cannot use your sword or bow. Do you wish to proceed?|Gossip Stone|ST}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Within the {{Term|ST|Lost at Sea Station}}'s temple, {{Term|ST|Link}} will encounter all four types of {{Plural|ST|Phantom|link}} while he completes the objectives on each floor, which are based on various floors from the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}} from {{PH}}. The {{Plural|PH|Phantom}} cannot be defeated or possessed, as {{Term|ST|Link}} is unable to use his {{Term|ST|Sword}} and his {{Term|ST|Bow}}. Completing a floor's objective will unlock a door to the next floor.

After completing the final floor, {{Term|ST|Link}} will come to a {{Term|ST|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|ST|Regal Ring|link}}, alongide a {{Term|ST|Portal (Object)|link}} that returns him to the {{Term|ST|Gossip Stone}} in the {{Term|ST|Lost at Sea Station}}. He can return to the temple and replay it as many times as he wants, earning himself an indefinite amount of {{Plural|ST|Regal Ring}}.
