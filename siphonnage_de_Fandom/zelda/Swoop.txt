{{Term/Store|Swoop||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Sub-Boss}} of the [[Wing Dungeon]] in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 73}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|OoA|Swoop}} appears as a red, {{Term|OoA|Vire|link}}-like creature with wings protruding from its head. {{Term|OoA|Swoop}} flutters above {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} while following him around the room, occasionally attacking by slamming down onto the floor. {{Term|OoA|Link}} must dodge the attack and strike {{Term|OoA|Swoop}} with his {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}} before it takes off again, leaving a hole in the floor that will take {{Term|OoA|Link}} to the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}}'s basement should he fall into it. After several hits, {{Term|OoA|Swoop}} will speed up its attacks and begin to slam onto the floor several times in a row, though it can be countered in the same manner. When {{Term|OoA|Swoop}} is defeated, a {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} and a portal to the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}}'s entrance will appear.
