==Biography==
Anutu lives in a small hut in Crater Cove. Earlier he had caught a [[Gohma]], yet claims it is a crab and the biggest he has ever caught. [[Link]] corrects him, and Anutu praises him for his knowledge by giving him the [[Power Sword]], which can shoot [[Sword Beam|Power Blast]]s. If spoken to again, Anutu gives Link his encouragement, hoping to soon return to fishing once [[Ganon]] is defeated.
