The {{Term/Store|Gut Check Rock Challenge|Gut Check Rock Challenges|Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|You signed on for the hard-core training regimen of the three Goron Blood Brothers. They call it the {{Color|BotW Red|Gut Check Rock Challenge}}! Talk to Bayge when you're ready.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}} is hosted by {{Term|BotW|Bayge|link}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}} atop the apex of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock|link}}. There, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find him sitting in front of {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine|link}}, though he refuses to allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} entry due to how sacred it is to them.<ref>{{Cite|I AIN'T MOVIN' A MUSCLE, BROTHER! Not a single, well-defined muscle! This is a special platform! Sacred even! Only real Goron men, as decided by us three Goron Blood Brothers, are allowed.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} agrees to move if {{Term|BotW|Link}} can clear the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|SO WHAT ARE YA WAITIN' FOR, BROTHER?! GET TRAININ'!}} Yeah that's it! {{Color|BotW Red|If you can clear our hard-core training program, I'll move outta your way}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bayge}} explains that the objective of the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Challenge}} is to climb to the apex of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} beginning from the bottom in under three minutes, all while collecting at least 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} along the way.<ref>{{Cite|Startin' from the bottom, ya gotta climb back up here within the time limit. BUT! Don't fool yourself into thinking it's only a climbin' challenge, little man. You've gotta {{Color|BotW Red|pick up rupees}} as ya go! Get up the cliff within the time limit, and grab a {{Color|BotW Red|passable number of rupees}}. Do that, and you pass the test!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You've gotta collect {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} as ya climb. A hard-core Goron man should be able to handle that no problem!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} runs out of time or gives up, he will have to return all of the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} that he collected.<ref>{{Cite|If ya run outta time or decide to give up halfway through your climb... {{Big|You'll have to return all the rupees ya picked up! That's life, brother.}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> This begins "{{Term|BotW|The Gut Check Challenge|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. He then informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it costs 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} per attempt.<ref name="Bayge"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} of varying amounts are found along the rockface and on the wooden platforms along {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}}. {{Plural|BotW|Green Rupee|link}} appear towards the bottom, but {{Plural|BotW|Blue Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Red Rupee|link}} are found higher up. To reach them, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must climb the rockface. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} climbs further up the pinnacle, more valuable {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} will begin to appear. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use the {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} around the sides of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} to reduce the need to climb, but doing so will skip large portions of the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge|display=Challenge}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes more than three minutes climbing {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will stop him when the timer runs out.<ref>{{Cite|Huh> What do ya think you're doin', brother?! Ya went over the time limit!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He does not allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} he has already gathered, since he has not earned them.<ref>{{Cite|I know it seems harsh, but I can't let ya keep those rupees! Ya didn't earn 'em.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will be waiting if he wants to try the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}} again.<ref>{{Cite|Tough luck, my miniscule friend. But I'll be waitin' if ya wanna take the challenge again. Don't let me down!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Similarly, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves the boundaries of the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will stop him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|WAIT. ONE. MINUTE. BROTHER!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He is disappointed that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is running away from his training.<ref>{{Cite|What are ya doin?! You're runnin' away? From training? From a challenge?! Unthinkable! Unimaginable! Uncool!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} disqualifies {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s attempt and takes back the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} he has collected.<ref>{{Cite|I'm afraid I'm gonna have to disqualify ya this time. That means you've gotta gimme back the rupees ya picked up.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Like before, he tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will be waiting for his next attempt.<ref>{{Cite|But hey, I'll be here waitin' if ya ever wanna try the challenge again!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes his climb, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will end the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|GOOOOOAAAAL!!}}|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> After the game is finished, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will check how many {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected.<ref>{{Cite|Nice goin', brother! Now let's see how many rupees ya scored.<br/>Looks like ya got...|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} collected at least 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} says that he has passed and declares him the fourth {{Term|BotW|Goron Blood Brothers}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|UNBELIEVABLE!!}} You've passed our training with the grit of a true Goron, my brother! In fact...you've earned the right to call yourself...the fourth Goron Blood Brother!!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|UNBELIEVABLE!!}} You've passed our training with the grit of a true Goron, my brother!! Now that you've managed to clear our extreme training AND the {{Color|BotW Red|contest of endurance}}... You've earned the right to call yourself... the fourth Goron Blood Brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pass by him and access {{Term|BotW|Gorae Torr Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|As promised, you are now allowed to stand on this sacred platform of Goron manliness. You're honored, I know.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will also be able too keep all of the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} he has obtained. However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} collected less than 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} says he did not do good enough.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|NO! Not good enough, brother!}} Ya didn't pick up enough rupees! Not even close, tiny person.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> If he wants to try again, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} invites him to return.<ref>{{Cite|But hey, I just know if ya try it again, you'll find your inner Goron and prevail! I'll be waitin' for ya, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

Once the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} to replay the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}} at any time. This time, however, the cost of entry has increased to 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref name="Rupees"/> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} explains that the previous price was for new recruits, but that offer has expired.<ref>{{Cite|[Did the price go up?] That was the new recruit price! That offer has expired, brother. I have my reasons... Protein doesn't buy itself, little man.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> The goal is still to collect at least 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} has to return any he has collected if he does not succeed.<ref>{{Cite|You've gotta collect {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} as you train. But if you're bringin' the heat like a true Goron, that should be no problem. Even if ya reach the goal within the time limit, without those rupees, you fail. So don't fail, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} succeeds in this version of the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} will be impressed by him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Unbelievable!}} You annihilated our training like it was a mere warm up, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He is glad to see that he was right about {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s strength.<ref>{{Cite|I knew I was right about you! You've got the body of a bitty baby but the heart of a Goron warrior!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> As a reward for passing the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Challenge}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Rushroom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|For passing the Gut Check Challenge, I'm gonna award ya with this fancy {{Color|BotW Blue|rushroom}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} to try the {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Challenge|display=Challenge}} as many times as he wants.<ref>{{Cite|Take this training as many times as ya want, brother! In fact, I'll be waitin' here for ya to challenge yourself again!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

===Super Gut Check Challenge===
After {{Term|BotW|Link}} clears the initial {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he is ready for the harder version, known as the '''Super Gut Check Challenge'''.<ref>{{Cite|And good news, fourth brother! That means you can do the GUT CHECK CHALLENGE all you want! As a Goron Blood Brother, you're ready for the HARD stuff! Just lemme know when you're pumped enough to try it!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} next approaches {{Term|BotW|Bayge}}, he says that the regular {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}} would be too easy for him.<ref>{{Cite|You already beat {{Color|BotW Red|Gut Check Challenge}}, so that wouldn't be enough of a trial for a brother like you.|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Instead, he offers the {{Term|BotW|Super Gut Check Challenge}}.<ref>{{Cite|I recommend our hardest training program of all time. I call it... {{Color|BotW Red|Super Gut Check Challenge}}!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} which program he would like to try.<ref>{{Cite|So? Which program will it be?|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses the {{Term|BotW|Super Gut Check Challenge}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} informs him that it costs 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref name="Super"/> This program requires {{Term|BotW|Link}} to gather at least 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|You've gotta collect {{Color|BotW Red|300 rupees}} as you train! Go on, show me how much of a Goron you really are!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he reaches the top of {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}} without enough {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, he will fail.<ref>{{Cite|Even if ya reach the goal within the time limit, without those rupees, you fail. So don't fail, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref>

Similar to the regular {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock Challenge}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Plural|BotW|Green Rupee}}, {{Plural|BotW|Blue Rupee}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Red Rupee}} as he climbs. However, the {{Term|BotW|Super Gut Check Challenge}} introduces {{Plural|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, which can be found higher up on {{Term|BotW|Gut Check Rock}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} finishes the course with at least 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, {{Term|BotW|Bayge}} rewards him with an {{Term|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|For passing our famous Super Gut Check Challenge, I'm gonna reward ya with an {{Color|BotW Blue|endura shroom}}! It's yours, brother!|Bayge|BotW}}</ref> Like before, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is allowed to keep all of the {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} that he gathered.
