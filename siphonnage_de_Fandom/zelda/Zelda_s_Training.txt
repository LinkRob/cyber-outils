{{Term/Store|Zelda's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 12 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Zelda's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}} also spawns to fight alongside {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}. Once the objective is reached, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawn and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} are defeated.
