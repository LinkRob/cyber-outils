"{{Term/Store|EX Champion Urbosa's Song||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Champion Urbosa's Song}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|East Gerudo Mesa}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
After finishing the trials of the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|One-Hit Obliterator|link}} will split into four glowing orbs and scatter to different areas of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link|display=Hyrule}}, revealing four ancient structures called {{Plural|BotW|Monument|link}}. One of the {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} appears at {{Term|BotW|East Gerudo Mesa|link}}, southeast of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Monument}}, he will hear the voice of a {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, who explains that each pillar displays a {{Term|BotW|Map|link}} that reveals the location of a trial that must be undertaken in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|I applaud your courage in coming here. The locations that will be revealed to you are of trials that will enhance the power sleeping within. Do not let your guard down...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who is nearby, will make a remark about either the flash of light or the {{Term|BotW|Monument}}, depending on how many {{Plural|BotW|Monument}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already visited.<ref>{{Cite|What was that...flash of light? Perhaps it was my imagination playing tricks on me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did that {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} just start glowing? I suppose that proves I didn't imagine it the first time.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|stone monument}} has survived for 10,000 years. What secrets does it hold...|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That light... Is it a reaction to someone nearby? Is it me...or is it...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When he {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he is at first confused why someone else would venture to such a high altitude.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? How strange to see someone else at this ample altitude. I wonder what brought him here...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} apologizes to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for voicing his thoughts aloud, as he has become used to speaking to himself.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! How embarrassing. I'm afraid traveling on my own encourages me to talk to myself on occasion.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} notes that the {{Term|BotW|Monument}} is the same one mentioned in the {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|link}} of his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|Look! As expected, there's one here too! One of the {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} mentioned in my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If this is the first {{Term|BotW|Monument}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has visited, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will explain that it was mentioned in an {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}} written by his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|This {{Color|BotW Blue|monument}}... I must admit it has taken me by surprise! My teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}} mentions such {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}}, and now one has appeared before my eyes...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} explains that it is missing its key verses.<ref>{{Cite|[Unfinished song?] Yes. The song is mostly complete. However... My teacher left this world without finishing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He is now on a journey to finish the song.<ref>{{Cite|I wish the weave the missing pieces together and finish the song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>}} He is happy to be able to sing so close to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, such a rush of happiness I feel at being able to sing while the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} watches over us...|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

Part of the {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song}} references the trials that {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} overcame.<ref>{{Cite|The song my teacher left behind... There's {{Color|BotW Blue|a verse}} about the trials that {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Urbosa}} overcame.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He then proceeds to sing him the song, which provides instructions for completing all three trials.<ref>{{Cite|♪Lightning king of the desert.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s Champion, the stalwart.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|fight the brute of the sand}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|chase rings upon the land}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|throw the orb underground}}.<br/>Champion, trials abound!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} was moved by his performace.<ref>{{Cite|...So? Did it move you?|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...So? Did the {{Color|BotW Blue|verse}} move you? It was written in honor of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} challenging the trial.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His research into legends about the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} has allowed him a deeper understanding of who they were,<ref>{{Cite|By steeping myself in legends of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Champions}}, I'm beginning to form a deeper understanding of them.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which will in turn help him with completing the key verses.<ref>{{Cite|This will prove invaluable in writing the {{Color|BotW Blue|key verses}} of the song.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} feels as though the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} are speaking to him through the song and asking for its completion.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... It's as though the Champions are speaking to me through the song... I hear their silent plea for its completion. That's why I must learn more about the Champions of Hyrule's past...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He has been able to hear tales about {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} from {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Luckily, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo}} chief, {{Color|BotW Blue|Riju}}, and her guard, {{Color|BotW Blue|Buliara}}, shared some precious tales of their Champion with me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> though he had to leave his {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link|display=comfort zone}} in order to meet them.<ref>{{Cite|I'm grateful I was able to visit {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}}, even though I had to... well, leave my comfort zone a bit.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can complete the trials in any order. By defeating the {{Term|BotW|Molduking|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|East Barrens|link}}, he will unlock {{Term|BotW|Keive Tala Shrine|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} surfing through a series of {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}} will reveal {{Term|BotW|Takama Shiri Shrine|link}}. Finally, retrieving an {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}} and throwing it into the pit outside will unearth {{Term|BotW|Kihiro Moh Shrine|link}}.

Each of the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} reward {{Term|BotW|Link}} with one of {{Plural|BotW|Naboris's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Naboris's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} will then inform {{Term|BotW|Link}} that collecting all three {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|display=Emblems}} will unlock a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They will tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} how many more trials he has to complete before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> After completing all three shrines, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} of the final {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} will instruct him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Naboris...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives, he will once again hear the voice of a {{Term|BotW|Monk}}. The voice informs him that in exchange for {{Plural|BotW|Naboris's Emblem}}, he will be taken to the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}}, a world within his memories, to face a trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To the one who approaches this Divine Beast... In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial. In exchange for Naboris's Emblems... you will be granted the chance to tread through the realm of memories.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Without determination, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find this trial to be unforgiving.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Those who lack determination will find this trial unforgiving.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} also reminds him to take this world seriously, as its truth is deeper than it appears.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Do not take this place lightly, nor dismiss it as merely a world within your mind. The truth is much deeper than you know.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} will then find himself inside {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|display=Naboris}}, where the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} reiterate that this realm was made from his memories.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|This illusory realm was created from the depths of your memories...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} that he will fight is a product of his own fear, which he must overcome during this trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|The enemy you shall face is a product of the fear that dwells within. This battle is a trial of the soul. You must emerge victorious using only the tools provided.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> With this, {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} appears again.

{{Term|BotW|Link}}'s equipment and belongings will be temporarily changed to the following:
* {{Term|BotW|Desert Voe Set|link}} (upgraded twice)
* The {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|link}}
* The {{Term|BotW|Daybreaker|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Edge of Duality|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Voltfruit|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Zapshroom|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}

After defeating the phantasm again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will return to the exterior of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}. He finds {{Term|BotW|Kass}} there,<ref>{{Cite|Well, well. Though our aims are different, our feet took us to the same destination. I speak figuratively, of course. I travel by wing rather than by foot.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> who explains that hearing anecdotes about {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} has inspired him to write a song for her,<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Urbosa}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor her in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which brought him to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That is why I felt inclined to roost here, near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}, which {{Color|BotW Blue|Urbosa}} herself mastered.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He then sings "[[Kass 'Champion Urbosa's Song'|Champion Urbosa's Song]]".<ref name="Urbosa's Song">{{Cite|♪Recall {{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}}'s Champion of sand,<br/>before she was lost to the land.♪<br/>♪Chief {{Color|BotW Blue|Urbosa}}'s heart was strong,<br/>so the princess came along.♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> This allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to visualize the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} from 100 years ago.<ref name="Urbosa's Song"/> 

After the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}} ends, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} notes that, while {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} was a fierce warrior, she was also a good friend to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Though she was a fierce warrior, the Gerudo chief was also a loving friend of Hyrule's princess.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} what he thought of the song,<ref>{{Cite|So? What do you think of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Urbosa's Song}}?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which he explains allowed him to feel closer to his teacher.<ref>{{Cite|By writing a song that honors the heart of Champion Urbosa, I feel closer than ever to my dear teacher.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will then hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}, who calls him an impressive voe and allows him wield {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury|link}} more often.<ref>{{Cite|I must say, you are an impressive voe. You may now wield Urbosa's Fury far more often.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> The skill will be upgraded to {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury +|link}}, which reduces its recharge time to four minutes. She leaces the duty of protecting {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You are the pride of Hyrule, hero. Take care of our princess. I'm leaving that honor in your capable hands.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> After receiving the upgrade, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} is completed.
