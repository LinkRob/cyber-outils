The ghost of a young girl named Phoeni appears as a hand in the toilet of the [[Knight Academy]] at night asking for paper. Once [[Link]] receives [[Cawlin's Letter]], he can choose to deliver it to [[Karane]] or give it to Phoeni.<ref name="Guide 165">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS|publisher= Prima|page= 165}}</ref> If he chooses to give it to Karane and visits Phoeni afterwards, she will tell Link she no longer needs paper before vanishing.<ref>{{Cite|Please forget about the paper... Good-bye...|Phoeni|SS}}</ref> If he chooses to give the Letter to Phoeni, she will believe the letter was meant for her,<ref>{{Cite|It's good to see you again... Actually, thanks to you, I met my true love... Yes, him... It all started with that letter that you gave me...|Phoeni|SS}}</ref> giving Link five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s in return. Afterwards, Phoeni's hand can be found stroking [[Cawlin]]'s head while he sleeps.<ref name="Guide 165" />

==Trivia==
*The concept of a hand in the toilet is based on Japanese ghost stories where a hand grabs from beneath the toilet whenever someone sits in the bathroom.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= It's actually something from a Japanese ghost story. Not a specific ghost story.
