==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Solder's Armor, along with the [[Soldier's Helm]] and [[Soldier's Greaves]], comprise the [[Soldier's Set]]. The set can be purchased at [[Hateno Village]]. It appears to be composed of a pair of pauldrons, a pair of gauntlets, and a full breastplate, all of which is made of steel. It has no effect aside from increasing defense, but it can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] and enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}.
