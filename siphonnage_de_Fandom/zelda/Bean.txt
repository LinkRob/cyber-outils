==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Bean}} are objects found throughout the world of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. They are the seeds of beanstalks,<ref name="Planted"/><ref>{{Cite|Hey, listen to this! You remember that big bean I planted? Well, would you believe it finally sprouted! I wonder where it leads!|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref> which can be found in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}, on {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}}, in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}, and in {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. They can only be made to grow with the magic from a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I planted a bean near my house, but it just doesn't want to grow...|Forest Picori|TMC}}</ref>
