{{Term/Store|Stockwell's Shop||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref name="Vassal">{{Cite|The town is quiet enough, but past the gates, there are monsters everywhere! You should visit {{Color|TMC Green|Stockwell's shop}} and get outfitted before you go out there.|Vassal|TMC}}</ref> As its name suggests, it is owned by {{Term|TMC|Stockwell|link}} and is located in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. This is the only shop where {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can buy a {{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}} in case his gets swallowed by a {{Term|TMC|Like Like|link}}. There are also {{Term|TMC|Bomb Bag|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Quiver|link}} upgrades that can be bought. An additional {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} can be acquired if {{Term|TMC|Link}} shrinks down to {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} size and enters the back of the store, though {{Term|TMC|Stockwell}} will ask him to feed his dog {{Term|TMC|Fifi|link}} before he keeps it.
