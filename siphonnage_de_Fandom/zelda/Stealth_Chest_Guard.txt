==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Stealth Chest Guard is a piece of [[Armor]] which can be purchased at {{Term|BotW|Enchanted|link}} in [[Kakariko Village]]. According to its description, the [[Sheikah]] fabrics used to make the Chest Guard suppress the rustling of clothes.<ref name="Inventory"/> When worn, it provides one level of Stealth Up. The Stealth Chest Guard can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Stealth Chest Guard, alongside the [[Stealth Mask]] and [[Stealth Tights]], compose the [[Stealth Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a [[Night Speed Up]] [[Set Bonus]].
