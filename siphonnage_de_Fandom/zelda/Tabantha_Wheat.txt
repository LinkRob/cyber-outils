==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Tabantha Wheat can be collected by cutting grass in the [[Tabantha Frontier]]. Alternatively, it can be purchased at [[The Slippery Falcon]] in [[Rito Village]] or from the travelling merchant [[Yammo]]. Eaten raw, Tabantha Wheat will restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Tabantha Wheat is one of the ingredients that can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Light Yellow [[Dye]].

When {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Tabantha Wheat}} is needed to make stews and breads. In particular, it's necessary to make the following {{Plural|BotW|Food}}:
