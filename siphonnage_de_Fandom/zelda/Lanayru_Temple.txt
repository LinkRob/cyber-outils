The '''Lanayru Temple''' is a [[Dungeon]] in {{EoW}}.

==Entrance to the Temple==
The Lanayru Temple is unlocked at the end of "The Holy Mount Lanayru" quest line, which itself is only unlocked after beating [[Hyrule Castle]]. To reach the temple, players must make it to the top of [[Mount Lanayru]] and traverse the [[Stilled Holy Mount Lanayru]], at which point they will arrive at the dungeon.

==Themes and Navigation==
The Lanayru Temple is an ice-themed dungeon, befitting its mountainous origins. The player was move carefully across slippery floors, avoid icy enemies, and be careful when wading through the freezing cold waters.

The dungeon's central gimmick is a series of large air conditioning units; the player must activate them to either melt or freeze over certain rooms from different locations, sometimes even switching between the two conditions to solve puzzles. This all culminates in one large, interconnected puzzle required to get a snowball to hit a switch and unlock the Big Key.
