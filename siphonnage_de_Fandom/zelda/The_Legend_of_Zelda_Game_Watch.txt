'''''The Legend of Zelda'' Game Watch''', referred to as '''''The Legend of Zelda'' 8321''' in the instruction manual, is a combined game and wristwatch released by {{Wp|Nelsonic Industries}}. It was released in 1989 for the U.S. and 1992 for the U.K..

It is commonly confused with {{ZG&W}}. It is considered the poorer of the two LCD games because the screen is too small to reasonably allow much strategy or movement. The playing area is even smaller than that of the [[Game Boy]] titles in the series.

==Watch==
{{ImageCaption|image=File:Zelda watch.png|size=250px|align=left|caption=A black version of the watch.|captionalign=center}}
The [[The Legend of Zelda (Series)|''Zelda'']] watch was sold in pink, black, white or red. {{Wp|Nelsonic Industries#Game Watches|A number of other watches}} based on [[Nintendo]] licenses were also made, in addition to licenses from other companies. In total, twelve million ''The Legend of Zelda'' Game Watches were sold, making it commercially successful.

==Game==
===Gameplay===
It is a top-down game loosely based on the original {{TLoZ}} and includes such familiar foes as [[Aquamentus]] and [[Keese]] (here referred to as dragon and bat, respectively), as well as the exclusive [[Iron Ball]]s.

The game consists of four dungeons with four rooms each.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= There are 4 caves in each level and 4 levels for a total of 16 caves.|game= TLoZGW|page= 1}}</ref> In each room, [[Link]] has to collect a [[boomerang]] to defeat the [[Iron Ball]]s. After all Iron Balls are defeated, he can get a sword to defeat the [[Keese]], which first appear in the second dungeon. As a reward, he gains a [[key]] to the next room. When entering it, though, he loses his found items and has to recollect them, and if he does not make it to the next room within ten seconds of grabbing the key, 10 points will be deducted every second until they reach 0, at which point the [[Game Over|game is over]].<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= You have 10 seconds to get Link through the door. For every second after that, 10 points will be deducted from your score until all points are lost and game is over. |game= TLoZGW|page= 1}}</ref> The last two rooms of every dungeon also have [[Heart]]s to replenish Link's [[Life Gauge|health]]. Once all the enemies in the fourth room are dealt with, the dungeon's [[boss]] (which is always [[Aquamentus]]) appears. To defeat him, Link has to collect a [[Bomb]] and place it in front of Aquamentus.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= Link must destroy the dragon by picking up a bomb (S1) at the top left corner and dropping the bomb (S1) near the Dragon. Link must get away before the bomb explodes or else Link loses all his lives.|game= TLoZGW|page= 1}}</ref> Following his victory, a [[Triforce]] fragment appears, which allows Link access to the next dungeon. After collecting all four pieces of the Triforce the game resets to level one.

===Story===
====Plot Synopsis====
Link enters a cave unarmed to collect the pieces of the [[Triforce]]. He travels through four dungeons with four caves in each, while being attacked by [[Iron Ball]]s, [[Keese]], and at the end of each dungeon, [[Aquamentus]]. After reaching the end of the fourth dungeon, Link reassembles the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= When the Dragon is destroyed a triangle will be seen in the bottom right corner. Pick it up (S1) and it will be added to the Triforce triangle of the top right.|game= TLoZGW|page= 1}}</ref>

==Trivia==

*32 years later, in 2021, another [[Game & Watch: The Legend of Zelda|Game & Watch version of ''The Legend of Zelda'']] would be made. However, this one is a direct port of the [[The Legend of Zelda|NES original]] as opposed to the more primitive gameplay of the Game & Watch game.

*This game has the smallest [[Life Gauge]] of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] - five [[Heart Container|Heart Containers]].
