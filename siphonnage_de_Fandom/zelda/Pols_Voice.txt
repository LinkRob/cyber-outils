{{Term/Store|Pols Voice|Pols Voices|Series, LA, LADX|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 120}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Pols Voice|A ghost with big ears and a weak point ‒ he hates loud noise.}}
In {{TLoZ}}, Pols Voices are ghosts with big ears, whiskers, and a tooth-filled maw underneath them, resembling a [[rabbit]]. They move and bounce around dungeon rooms and although they can be defeated by [[Sword]] attacks, they are especially vulnerable to [[Arrow|Arrows]], which will die from a single hit.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TLoZ|guide= Nintendo|page= 11}}</ref> When defeated, Pols Voices may drop either a [[Heart]], one or five [[Rupee]], or a [[Clock]].{{Note|If {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} defeats 10 enemies without getting hit or teleporting with the {{Term|TLoZ|Recorder|link}}, then the 10th enemy defeated will drop five {{Plural|TLoZ|Rupee|link}}. However, if the 10th enemy is defeated with a {{Term|TLoZ|Bomb|link}}, then it will drop four {{Plural|TLoZ|Bomb}}. If {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} defeats 16 enemies without getting hit or teleporting, then the 16th enemy will drop a {{Term|TLoZ|Fairy|link}}. After the 16th enemy, {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} must get hit and reset the counter to zero in order to achieve this effect again. Defeating {{Plural|TLoZ|Armos|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Like Like|link}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Keese|link}}, or {{Plural|TLoZ|Gel|link}} will not be counted.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= n.a. |published= n.d. |retrieved= August 17, 2017 |url= http://redcandle.us/The_Legend_of_Zelda#Forced_drops |title= Forced Drops |site= redcandle.us}}</ref>}} Despite being cute-looking, these enemies are among the most dangerous, as Pols Voices often appear in groups, can inflict two [[Heart Container|Hearts]] when coming into contact with [[Link]], their bouncing movements make them difficult to hit with Swords, and are resilient with 10 health points, requiring ten blows with the Wooden Sword, five with the [[White Sword]], or three with the [[Magical Sword]] to eliminate them.
[[File:Famicom controllers.webp|thumb|219x219px|The 2P Famicom controller (bottom) serves as a unique weakness for the Pols Voice, thanks to the built-in microphone ]]
The game's instruction manual states that Pols Voices hate loud noises.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= A ghost with big ears and a weak point - he hates loud noise.|game= TLoZ|page= 36}}</ref> This is a reference to the original Japanese version of the game, where they can only be immediately defeated by talking into the second controller's microphone.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Cinemassacre|published= June 7, 2015|retrieved= February 15, 2017|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fJ7Q0xhEo24|title= The Legend of Zelda (Famicom) James & Mike Mondays|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref> As the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] controllers do not feature a built-in microphone, their weakness was changed to Arrows; however, the manual's description was translated literally and not edited to reflect this.

==={{LA|-}}===
Pols Voices appear in [[Bottle Grotto]] and {{Term|LANS|Face Shrine|link}} in {{LA}}. They are primarily vulnerable to Arrows, though they take several shots to defeat. They can also be quickly defeated by throwing [[Pot]]s at them or by using [[Bomb]]s. Playing the [[Ballad of the Wind Fish]] on the [[Ocarina]] will defeat them instantly.

Pols Voices can be temporarily stunned with [[Magic Powder]]. After they have been stunned, they can be picked up and thrown using the [[Power Bracelet]].

==={{OoS|-}}===
Pols voices appear in [[Snake's Remains]], [[Poison Moth's Lair]], and the [[Sword & Shield Maze]] in {{OoS}}. They also appear inside the [[Hero's Cave]] in a [[Linked Game]]. They can be destroyed with [[Pot]]s and [[Bomb]]s, and playing the [[Strange Flute]] and its variants ([[Dimitri's Flute]], [[Ricky's Flute]] and [[Moosh's Flute]]) will cause them to explode.

==={{OoA|-}}===
Pols Voices appear in [[Moonlit Grotto]] in {{OoA}}. They can be destroyed with [[Pot]]s and [[Bomb]]s, and playing the [[Harp of Ages]] or the [[Strange Flute]] and its variants ([[Dimitri's Flute]], [[Ricky's Flute]] and [[Moosh's Flute]]) will cause them to explode.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, Pols Voices only appear in the [[Temple of Courage]]. By blowing into the [[Nintendo DS]] microphone, they get stunned, leaving them open for an attack.<ref>{{Cite|Creatures that are sensitive to sound dwell in the sands ahead. So, walk without sound and bring as little attention to yourself as possible.|Ciela|PH}}</ref> Pols Voices attack by flipping forward and attempting to attack Link with their large mouths hidden under their bodies, unlike earlier games. Their appearance in this game is much closer to the original ''The'' ''Legend of Zelda'' official art than their in-game 2D sprites up to this point.

=== ''Echoes of Wisdom'' ===
A Pols Voice doll appears in a house at Hyrule Castle Town in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]''.

==Other Appearances==

=== '''''BS The Legend of Zelda''''' ===
{{Noncanon}}
Pols Voices appear in ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]'', where they serve the same purpose as in the original game. They will be instantly defeated if the [[Player]] uses the [[Ocarina]].

==={{ZA|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
Pols Voice appears in {{ZA}} as a [[Sub-Boss]] in the [[Shrine of Illusion]] and has a much more sinister appearance than in the mainline games resembling a [[demon]]. The best method to defeat Pols Voice is to attack it with the [[Noise]] spell, although it's also possible to defeat it by attacking it with [[Princess Zelda]]'s [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Wand|Wand]] for two minutes in real time.
{{Noncanon|End}}
==Trivia==
*Ironically, despite the international release of ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' have Pols Voices completely vulnerable to [[Arrow|Arrows]], they are immune to them in the original Japanese version, making the enemies more difficult to defeat without access to the Famicom's second controller microphone.
*After selling the [[Bunny Hood]] to the [[Running Man]] in {{OoT}}, he mentions that with its ears, he might hear the voices.<ref>{{Cite|I bet with those long ears you can hear the voices...|Running Man|OoT}}</ref> This might be a reference to Pols Voices.
*Due to the hardware limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]], if [[Link]] were to obtain the [[Blue Ring|Blue]] or [[Red Ring]] in ''The Legend of Zelda'', the whiskers of a Pols Voice will become a very light blue or red, respectively. This is due to them sharing the same color palette as the hero's [[Green Clothes|tunic]] (alongside certain characters and items).
*Although Pols Voices are in fact ghosts but closely resemble a rabbit, ironically, in the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Switch remake of ''Link's Awakening'']], they are known as "''Hase''" in the German release, which translates to '''Bunny'''.
