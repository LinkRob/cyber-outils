The {{Term/Store|Cradle||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have not seen a {{Color|TPHD Red|cradle}} come floating by here, have you?|Uli|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TPHD|Uli|link}} made the {{Term|TPHD|Cradle}} when {{Term|TPHD|Colin|link}} was born.<ref>{{Cite|I made it when my first child was born, and I have cherished it all these years.|Uli|TPHD}}</ref> At some point, it was taken by a {{Term|TPHD|Monkey|link}}, and {{Term|TPHD|Uli}} begins searching for it along the banks of the river in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} is able to recover it by using {{Term|TPHD|Hawk Grass|link}} to send a {{Term|TPHD|Hawk|link}} to take it from the {{Term|TPHD|Monkey}}. Upon returning the {{Term|TPHD|Cradle}} to {{Term|TPHD|Uli}}, she will ask {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to carry it back to her house, where she gives him a {{Term|TPHD|Fishing Rod|link}}.
