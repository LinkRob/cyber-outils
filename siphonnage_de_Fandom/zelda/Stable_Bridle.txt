==Location and Uses==
Stable Bridles are the default {{Term|BotW|Bridle|link}} giving to {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} when they're registered at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}. They can be replaced by different Bridles {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} obtains throughout his journey, but they can always be equipped again by having the Horse's equipment changed at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref name="Canni"/> However, when registered at Stables, both {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}} will receive their own unique Bridles, and they can't be equipped with the default one.
