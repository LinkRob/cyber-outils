{{Term/Store|Hotfeather Pigeon|Hotfeather Pigeons|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Hotfeather Pigeon}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Hotfeather Pigeon}}
Hotfeather Pigeons are rare {{Plural|Series|Pigeon|link}} that can be found in areas surrounding {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}, particularly the [[Eldin Canyon]] and [[Eldin Mountains]].<ref>{{Cite|This rare breed of pigeon lives near Death Mountain.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> While their feathers are fireproof and used to make clothing, their meat will burn when exposed to a [[Scorching Climate]].<ref>{{Cite|Their fireproof feathers are valuable as clothing material. Their meat, on the other hand, is not fireproof, and it'll burn up as soon as it's struck.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They drop a [[Raw Bird Thigh]] when they're killed.
