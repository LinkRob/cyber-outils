{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} that were once favored by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} who defended {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Their metal construction grants them a superior durability, and they are known to be reliable while firing.<ref name="Inventory"/> Commonly, they are used by many {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}.

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} (Archer) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Knight's Bow}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped outdoors during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. In addition, they sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. They also do not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Knight's Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> Many of the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} in this grouping were used by the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule}} prior to the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.
