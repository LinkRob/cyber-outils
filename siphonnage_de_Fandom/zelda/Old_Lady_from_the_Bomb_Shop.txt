The {{Term/Store|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop||Series, MM, MM3D}},{{Exp Game|MM}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref> also known as the '''Old Lady from Bomb Shop''',{{Exp Game|MM}}<ref>{{Cite|Old Lady from Bomb Shop<br/>The Bomb Shop owner's mother. She's in charge of keeping the place well stocked. The shop is open all day every day.|Bombers' Notebook|MM3D}}</ref> is a character in {{MM}}.

==Biography==
The Old Lady is one of three people [[Link]] can meet at the [[Bomb Shop#Majora's Mask|Bomb Shop]] in [[West Clock Town]], along with her son, who is the [[Bomb Shop Owner|Owner of the Bomb Shop]], and a [[Goron]], who sells [[Powder Keg]]s. On the night of the [[First Day]], the Old Lady enters [[North Clock Town]], carrying a sack full of [[Bomb Bag|Big Bomb Bag]]s. At midnight [[Sakon]] appears and robs the old woman.<ref>{{Cite|Stop! Thief!!! Give the old lady her luggage back!!!|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Stop! Thief!!! I'm just an old lady--get my luggage back!!!|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM3D}}</ref> Link can attack Sakon with his sword to make him drop the sack. Attacking him with any other weapon makes the sack explode. After he has left, the Old Lady will thank Link and reward him with the [[Blast Mask]].<ref>{{Cite|Yes, I must thank you. It's a dangerous mask, but maybe you could use it to throw your own festival fireworks show.|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref> A Big Bomb Bag will also be up for sale at the Bomb Shop the next day if she is saved, otherwise it will go up for sale at the [[Curiosity Shop]] for a higher price on the [[Final Day]].

The Old Lady is a firm believer in the [[Four Giants]]. After Link helps her, she credits the Giants for having looked out for her.<ref>{{Cite|A nice fella helped me out, so we can finally sell Big Bomb Bags! Once again, the Giants are looking out for me!|Old Lady from the Bomb Shop|MM}}</ref>
