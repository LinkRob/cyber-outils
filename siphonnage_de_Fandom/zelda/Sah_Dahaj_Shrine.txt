The {{Term/Store|Sah Dahaj Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sah Dahaj Shrine}}<br/>Power of Fire}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Power of Fire''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Sah Dahaj Shrine}} is hidden within a gorge directly north of {{Term|BotW|Cephla Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sah Dahaj|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sah Dahaj. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Sah Dahaj|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Sah Dahaj Shrine}} consists of three connected rooms. The first room has a pair of torches among a growth of leafy vines, one of which has fallen. These vines conceal a passage in the wall to the next room. The vines can be burned away by using the fire from the torches; the fallen torch can be knocked toward the vines using a Weapon.

The passage leads to another chamber where in the corner are more vines and a suspended lamp overhead, as well as a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}. The lamp's cable can be snapped by striking it, thus burning the vines below and revealing another passage.

The third chamber has a sealed door and a {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} enclosed in a barred cell. Above the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|display=Switch}} is a slab suspended by two crates. The crates can be destroyed by igniting the vines that lead to them, thus dropping the slab and activating the {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch}}. This opens the door.

Through the door is the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Sah Dahaj}} presents Link with a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Sah Dahaj|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Sah Dahaj|BotW}}</ref>

In the room is before the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} suspended by a wooden platform with vines leading to it. Burning the vines will destroy the platform and drop the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}}, which contains a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Bow|link}}.
