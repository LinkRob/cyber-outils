==Characteristics==
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|White Bubble}}<br/>Aim for it when it stops!
}}

{{Plural|OoT3D|White Bubble}} are only found in a single room on the fourth floor of the {{Term|OoT3D|Spirit Temple|link}}. They will circle the mirror that {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} needs to rotate to solve the chamber's puzzle. They do not attack directly and will instead fly around and eventually stop in one spot, at which point they can be defeated. {{Plural|OoT3D|White Bubble}} are the only {{Plural|OoT3D|Enemy}} that aren't present in {{MQ}}.
