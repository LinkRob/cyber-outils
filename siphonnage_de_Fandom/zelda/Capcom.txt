'''Capcom''' is the only third-party video game development company to have developed [[The Legend of Zelda (Series)|{{TLoZ|-}}]] titles. In collaboration with [[Nintendo]], they designed {{OoS}}, {{OoA}}, {{FS}}, and {{TMC}}.

Capcom is known for its numerous popular franchises such as ''Mega Man'', ''Street Fighter'', ''Devil May Cry'' and ''Resident Evil''.

=={{TLoZ|-}} Games Developed by Capcom==
==={{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}}===
Capcom's first contributions to the ''Zelda'' series were {{OoS}} and {{OoA}} for [[Game Boy Color]]. The development of these titles was a joint venture between Capcom and Nintendo,<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages and Oracle of Seasons are the result of a collaboration between game developers from both Nintendo and Capcom.|author= |published= |url= http://web.archive.org/web/20050311025108/http://www.nintendo.com/gamedev?gameid=m-Game-0000-314|title= The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons Interview Part I|site= Nintendo of America|type= Internet Archive}}</ref> lead by Capcom game director Yoshiki Okamoto. According to Okamoto, the development team struggled early on without Nintendo producer [[Shigeru Miyamoto]]'s guidance. Miyamoto later became involved in the project at Okamoto's request.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= [F]or the first little while I had left the team totally alone [without Miyamoto's help] because I figured they'd easily be able to do this much by themselves. So I left them alone, and for the first year we did nothing but lose lots of money. . . . It's been taking up money for ages now, with all the people we've brought in. So I came in, and I saw that nothing was working out, and I went up to Miyamoto and was like, 'Help me!'|name= Yoshiki Okamoto|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20050311025108/http://www.nintendo.com/gamedev?gameid=m-Game-0000-314|title= The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons Interview Part I}}</ref> Rather than beginning development with gameplay design, as was Miyamoto's convention, the team began by using Capcom subsidiary Flagship to create the game scenarios (i.e. story and characters). This resulted in gameplay design difficulties; several aspects of the games had to be redone.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= Mr. Miyamoto normally creates the game scenario (story and characters) after the initial game play is designed. If the action part of the game is solid, the scenario can be developed from there. We started by using the Capcom scenario creation company, Flag Ship, to create the scenario first. Then, we created maps and started developing the game. I don't believe that worked. . . . Using that system, the team had to redo both the scenario and the maps several times to make all the elements fit.|name= Yoshiki Okamoto|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20050311025108/http://www.nintendo.com/gamedev?gameid=m-Game-0000-314|title= The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons Interview Part I}}</ref>

Despite initial concerns, {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}} received a positive reception from fans and media alike. Capcom has been praised for their contributions to the ''Zelda'' series.{{Fact}}

===''A Link to the Past & Four Swords''===
{{Main|The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords}}
Capcom returned in 2002 to create the [[Game Boy Advance]] remake of {{ALttP}}. On the same game cartridge as this remake came an original ''Zelda'' game created by Capcom, the multiplayer-only {{FS}}. {{FS|-}} introduced important plot elements, namely the eponymous {{Term|FS|Four Sword|link}} and the villain {{Term|FS|Vaati|link}}. These would reappear in the later titles {{FSA}} and {{TMC}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|The Legend of Zelda: The Minish Cap}}
Capcom's next and most recent ''Zelda'' game came in 2004 with the release of a prequel to {{FS|-}}, {{TMC}}, for the [[Game Boy Advance]]. 

In reference to their earlier works, the three {{Plural|FS|Oracle|link}} make a cameo appearance in this game. Link can help each of them to find {{Plural|TMC|Vacant House|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. However, one of them will never receive a new house as a third one never becomes available. This may be an unfinished gameplay element. Alternatively, it could be a deliberate reference to {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}' [[The Legend of Zelda: Mystical Seed of Courage|unreleased third title]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Tourian Tourist|published= October 14, 2009|retrieved=March 7, 2014|url= http://touriantourist.blogspot.com/2009/10/third-oracle-house-in-minish-cap.html|title= Third Oracle House in The Minish Cap|site= Hyrule Blog|type=}}</ref>

Capcom has not been involved in the development of any ''Zelda'' game since {{TMC|-}}. {{PH|-}} was the first handheld ''Zelda'' game since {{LA}} to have not been developed by Capcom.

== Keiji Inafune ==
{{wp|Keiji Inafune}}, the longtime creator in charge of the ''{{wp|Mega Man}}'' series for Capcom, has also long been a fan of the ''Zelda'' series. His all-time favorite game is {{ALttP}}. Inafune created the ''{{wp|Mega Man Legends}}'' series as an homage to ''Zelda'', and those games incorporate a great deal of ''Zelda''-style gameplay elements. {{OoT}}, released soon after the first ''Legends'' game, has a 3D gameplay interface strikingly similar to ''Legends''.  Inafune would later become directly involved in the development of the ''Zelda'' series at Capcom as the producer of {{TMC}}.<ref>[http://www.1up.com/features/4-ways-mega-man-legends-ocarina 4 Ways Mega Man Legends Beat Ocarina of Time to the Punch]</ref>
