{{Term/Store|Mysterious Wall|Mysterious Walls|Series, TMC|plural}} are objects in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mysterious Wall|Kinstone Fusion|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Wall}} are spots within {{Plural|TMC|Secret Cave|link}} with which {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}}.

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone}} with these {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Wall|display=Walls}}, the {{Plural|TMC|Goron Digger|link}}' task of breaking through a cave at {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch}} will progress, and after six {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusions}} with six different {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Wall}}, along with the farmer {{Term|TMC|Eenie|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can obtain an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}} at the end of the cave.
