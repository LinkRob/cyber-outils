'''Soldier Construct III''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{TotK}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name=Soldier Construct III|desc= An upper-tier combat construct made by the Zonai with ancient techniques and magic. It faithfully obeys commands and tries to root out any being it deems to be an enemy. The decoration on its head, designed to intimidate foes, becomes a powerful weapon when swung around.|locs= Lanayru Sky Archipelago,
Thunderhead Isles|loot=  Zonai Charge, Soldier Construct III Horn}}
Soldier constructs III are a type of [[construct]] that are quite powerful and can be found on the surface but more commonly on [[Sky Islands|sky islands]]. however they can not be found on the great sky island. they are green in color with a gold horn that resembles a serrated executioners cleaver.
