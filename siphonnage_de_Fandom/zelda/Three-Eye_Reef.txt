{{Term|TWWHD|Three-Eye Reef}} is a minor island located west of {{Term|TWWHD|Greatfish Isle|link}}, at D1. It has three round reefs in the center, giving it its name. After the {{Term|TWWHD|Helmaroc King|link}} is defeated in {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Warship|link}} and wall-mounted {{Plural|TWWHD|Cannon (Enemy)|link}} will appear at the island. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} destroys all of them, a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} appears containing a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}}.

A {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}} can also be pulled from the bottom of the sea by using Treasure Chart 38, obtained from the {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Auction|link}}.
