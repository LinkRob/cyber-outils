{{Term/Store|Gate of Time|Gates of Time|Series, SS|plural}} are recurring objects and items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 72}}</ref> Each gate is capable of creating a portal through time to travel to the era just after the Goddess [[Hylia]]'s fight with [[Demise]].<ref>{{Cite|That is a Gate of Time. It is said to transport whoever passes through its arch to a different time.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You stand in the past, ages before your own time. Here the goddess Hylia has only just sealed away Demise... And little time has passed since the goddess sent the outcropping of rock into the sky that would one day become Skyloft.|Impa|SS}}</ref> The Gate of Time located in the [[Temple of Time]] was constructed by Hylia herself.<ref name="ToTGateOfTime"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{SS|-}}===
The Gate of Time looks like a giant gear being spun by two smaller gears in both upper corners. It has 12 teeth and a symbol on each tooth, bearing resemblance to the hour indicators on a clock. The [[Triforce]] can be seen in the center of the gear surrounded by the [[Crest of Din]], the [[Crest of Nayru]] and the [[Crest of Farore]] and what appears to be musical notation. When the gate is touched, a spiral of hollow, blue gear shapes appear to indicate that it is open, and the original glassy surface reappears when it is closed.

The first Gate of Time [[Link]] encounters is in the Temple of Time in the [[Lanayru Desert]].<ref name="ToTGateOfTime">{{Cite|This is the Temple of Time. Within it is the sacred Gate of Time made by the goddess, bzzt.|Ancient Robot|SS}}</ref> [[Impa]] and [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] are about to enter its threshold when [[Ghirahim]] appears and attacks. Link intervenes after Impa is overwhelmed by Ghirahim's power and Zelda entrusts the [[Goddess's Harp]] to him just before they both escape, destroying the Gate of Time behind them.<ref>{{Cite|You were successful in meeting her at the Temple of Time, but our discourse was cut short by Ghirahim, who attempted to attack Zelda. During Ghirahim's attack, Zelda entrusted you with her harp and then ran from the area through a strange gate. In order to accomplish her own mission, Zelda entrusted her harp to you and fled through a Gate of Time.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

Link travels to the [[Sealed Grounds]] where the [[Impa|old woman]] reveals the existence of a second Gate of Time. [[Ghirahim]] learns about the second Gate of Time from a drawing in the [[Fire Sanctuary (Skyward Sword)|Fire Sanctuary]].<ref>{{Cite|Look at these old drawings! Until I found these, I was...upset about that little stunt the goddess's guard dog pulled at the Gate of Time. [...] [T]hese drawings suggest the existence of a second Gate of Time.|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> To activate it, Link must temper the [[Goddess Sword]] into the [[Master Sword]] by obtaining the three [[Sacred Flames]] hidden in the Faron, Eldin, and Lanayru provinces.<ref>{{Cite|When the three sacred flames have imbued your sword, only then will the Gate of Time open for you.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|A second Gate of Time exists, but in order to activate it you will need to enhance your sword.|Fi|SS}}</ref> Eventually Link is successful in this endeavor, though his efforts necessitate resealing [[The Imprisoned]] on multiple occasions, and meets Zelda in the distant past. After she enters a deep slumber, Link uses the gate again to return to the future and quest after the [[Triforce]]. He uses the gate during this mission to plant the [[Life Tree Seedling]] in the past and recover the [[Life Tree Fruit]] in the present to revive [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]].

After Link wishes upon the Triforce for the destruction of [[Demise]] in the present, Ghirahim abducts Zelda and takes her through the gate in an attempt to revive Demise in the past. Link and [[Groose]] follow through the gate to rescue Zelda, and after they succeed, they use the gate a final time to return to the future.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
In {{HW}}, the Gate of Time appears in the [[Sealed Grounds (Hyrule Warriors)|Sealed Grounds]] Stage in the Hall of Time [[Keep]]. It can be clearly seen after Link and Fi close the [[Gate of Souls]] in "[[Sealed Grounds (Hyrule Warriors)#Sealed Ambition|Sealed Ambition]]".

It is also appears as [[Lana]]'s level one Summoning Gate weapon.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In {{HWAoC}}, the portal opened by [[Terrako]] in the opening cutscene has [[Sheikah (Language)]] around the edges that reads "Gate of Time Open".

==Trivia==
*The pattern on the gate can be seen on the game's [[Gallery:Box Art|box art]]. On the boxart, the words "Wisdom", "Nehru", "Power", "Din" and "-rol" can be seen written in {{TP}} [[Hylian Language|Hylian]]. This text has been removed from active Gates of Time, but can be faintly seen on the broken gate in the [[Temple of Time]].
*In Japanese both the [[Door of Time]] and the [[Gate of Time]] share the same name, {{Romanize|時の扉|Toki no Tobira|Gate of Time|2}}.
*Upon being activated, the Gates of Time somewhat resemble the [[Mirror of Twilight]].
*At one point in {{TP}}, Link must walk through a special door in order to be transported into the past and enter the [[Temple of Time (Dungeon)|Temple of Time]], and must then go through the same door to return to the present, thus making the door virtually identical in function to the Gates of Time.
