==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Fortified Pumpkins are one of three foodstuffs favoured by the [[Sheikah]] race, with the others being [[Swift Carrot]]s and [[Mighty Bananas]]. They are primarily and exclusively cultivated by the Sheikah farmer [[Olkin]], who sells 8 from his field in [[Kakariko Village]]. They can also be purchased from the travelling merchant [[Cambo]] or dropped by defeated {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}}.

Eaten raw, {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin}} restore half of a {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. They can also be roasted by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to create {{Plural|BotW|Baked Fortified Pumpkin|link}}. When cooked in a dish, the resulting food temporarily provides {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} with superior defense.<ref name="Inventory"/> At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], Fortified Pumpkins can be used to create Orange [[Dye]].

Olkin sells his pumpkins from his field because his rival, [[Steen]], refuses to have them stocked at his [[Trissa|wife]]'s [[High Spirits Produce|shop]]. There are nine pumpkin plants in the patch, yet only eight bear fruit, with Olkin tending to the ninth; this is likely where Mellie gets the Fortified Pumpkins to cook. During the evening, Olkin's wife [[Mellie]] cooks him dishes containing many fortified pumpkins,<ref>{{Cite|My wife's tough dishes are made from {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkins}}. They're out of this world. There are A LOT of {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkins}} in that recipe, I tell ya what.|Olkin|BotW}}</ref> which Olkin feels is way better than {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My wife's {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkin}} dishes are the best. Way better than a barrel o' carrots, that's for sure!|Olkin|BotW}}</ref> Some may also be purchased from [[Cambo]] as he leaves Kakariko Village, implying he bought some from Olkin, however this does not reduce Olkin's stock.

As part of the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "[[Riddles of Hyrule]]", [[Walton]] will ask [[Link]] to bring him Kakariko Village's specialty fruit, which has rock-hard flesh.<ref>{{Cite|Kakariko Village's specialty fruit! It's {{Color|BotW Red|rock-hard}} flesh is quite resolute!|Walton|BotW}}</ref> If Link returns with a Fortified Pumpkin, Walton will be excited he figured the riddle out and move on to the next one.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|{{Color|BotW Red|Fortified pumpkin!}} That's it! THAT'S IT! You got it!}}|Walton|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, Fortified Pumpkins are necessary to make [[Pumpkin Stew]], [[Tough Meat-Stuffed Pumpkin]], and [[Pumpkin Pie]]. A Fortified Pumpkin, an [[Endura Carrot]], or a [[Swift Carrot]] are needed to make [[Veggie Cream Soup]], [[Vegetable Risotto]], and [[Vegetable Curry]].
