==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Big Flag}} are markers found throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} claims to have left {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} at the bases of those in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}, their purpose being to assist hikers on their journey.<ref>{{Cite|I've left a few things behind on the mountain trails and at the base of the flags planted. If you're in trouble, spot a flag and head toward it. Hopefully these items will be of use to you!|Selmie's Notes|BotW}}</ref> She also uses a pair of them as a goal in the Advanced Course of the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.<ref name="Selmie"/> Another {{Term|BotW|Big Flag}} is used to mark the goal for {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It couldn't be simpler ta play! Whack the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} and try to knock it inta the hole at that flag.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> A third {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}, the {{Term|BotW|Footrace|link}}, uses a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag}} as its goal as well.<ref>{{Cite|The goal is the {{Color|BotW Red|flags on top of the mountain}}! There's only two of us, so the race ends once someone reaches them!|Konba|BotW}}</ref>

Several {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag}} have {{Plural|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}} on top of them. These {{Term|BotW|Big Flag|display=Flags}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Passeri Greenbelt|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Garrison Ruins|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Town Ruins|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|East Post Ruins|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|South Nabi Lake|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} interacts with the {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|display=Leaves}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
