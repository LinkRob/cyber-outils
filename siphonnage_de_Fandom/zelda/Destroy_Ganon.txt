"{{Term/Store|Destroy Ganon||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Destroy Ganon}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Great Plateau}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} receives this {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} from {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} after learning of the events 100 years ago. Though he is ashamed that he could not save his own kingdom,<ref>{{Cite|Considering that I could not save my own kingdom, I have no right to ask this of you, Link... But I am powerless here...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} asks that {{Term|BotW|Link}} save {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} and do whatever it takes to annihilate {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You must save her...my daughter. And do whatever it takes to annihilate Ganon.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|display=Quest}} begins alongside "{{Term|BotW|Seek Out Impa|link}}" once {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}} fades away.

At any time, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can choose to infiltrate {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} can be found in {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|link}} inside the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}}. If they have not yet been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be forced to fight {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} one by one. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has defeated them, or if they have already been defeated, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} calling out to him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|... Link...}} Link...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She informs him that her power is giving out and she can no longer hold back Ganon.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry...but my power isn't strong enough... I can't hold him...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} laser erupts from the {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|display=Cocoon}}, wildly shooting around the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ganon's Cocoon|display=Cocoon}} explodes, and {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} falls through the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum}}'s floor and into the {{Term|BotW|Astral Observatory|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} glides down with the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}, landing right in front of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed any of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} will begin their attack on {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}. {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} announces that his moment for revenge has finally come for revenge, launching a laser from {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now my moment has finally come. Brace yourself, Ganon, for the sting of my revenge!|Revali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} says that this is her and {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link|display=Divine Beast Vah Ruta's}} final opportunity and they will not fail, just as {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|display=Vah Ruta}} fires its laser.<ref>{{Cite|This will be our final opportunity. We will not fail!|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} cheers {{Term|BotW|Link}} on and tells {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}} to open wide, directing {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} to fire its own attack.<ref>{{Cite|Let's go, little guy! Now! Open up wide, Ganon!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}, atop {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, notes that this moment is 100 years in the making, and she warns {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} to hold on as she snaps her fingers and activates the laser.<ref>{{Cite|A hundred years in the making... Hold on, Princess. Our moment has arrived!|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}' blue beams flood into the {{Term|BotW|Astral Observatory}}, raining upon {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}. For each {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} that has been reclaimed, one-eighth of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon's}} health will be depleted.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s second form, it flees from the {{Term|BotW|Astral Observatory}}. A golden light encompasses {{Term|BotW|Link}} and teleports him to {{Term|BotW|Windvane Meadow|link}} with his {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}.{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have a tamed {{Term|BotW|Horse}}, the one that appears will be the same one that appears in his {{Plural|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.}} As the {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} swirls, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} repeats the history of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, saying he was born out of a dark past and is an ancient evil that is reborn time and time again.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|... Ganon...}} Ganon... Ganon was born out of a dark past. He is a pure embodiment of the ancient evil that is rebord time and time again...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Assuming the form of {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon|link}}, he has given up on reincarnation.<ref>{{Cite|He has given up on reincarnation and assumed his pure, enraged form.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> If he is allowed to go free, the destruction would be unlike anything seen before.<ref>{{Cite|If set free upon our world, the destruction will be unlike anything ever seen before.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

To face {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} entrusts {{Term|BotW|Link}} with the {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I entrust you with the Bow of Light—a powerful weapon in the face of evil.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She notes that while {{Term|BotW|Link}} may not yet have recovered all of his strength or his memories,<ref>{{Cite|Link...you may not yet be at a point where you have fully recovered your power or all of your memories...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> he does not need to have remembered his courage, since he never forgot it.<ref>{{Cite|But courage need not be remembered... For it is never forgotten.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

With the {{Term|BotW|Malice}} covering {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon}}'s body, {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s attacks are unable to harm him.<ref>{{Cite|That energy covering Ganon's body is called Malice. None of your attacks will get through as he now is...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} will attempt to hold it back as much as she can, instructing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to attack any glowing points.<ref>{{Cite|I will hold the Malice back as much as I can, but my power is waning. Attack any glowing points that you see!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Shoot a glowing area!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link}} continues to circle and shoot at {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon|display=Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} notes that his power is weakening.<ref>{{Cite|Ganon's power is weakening!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's working|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When all of the glowing spots have been shot, an eye appears on {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon}}'s head, the core of his being.<ref>{{Cite|Link! Look up there!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's the very core of Ganon's being! Do what you must, Link!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} flies up into the air with his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} commands him to shoot now.<ref>{{Cite|Now, Link!|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots the eye, {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon}} stuggles as {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} suddenly emerges from inside him. When she lands on the ground, she begins to use her power to seal him away. He transforms back into his pig-like cloud form as he tries to flee and fight against {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}. She catches it in an expanding ball of light, which disappears quickly, sealing {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} away. With this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} is completed.
