==Characteristics==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Wild Pig}}
{{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} are generally peaceful animals that struggle in vain when picked up in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]''. They come in three varieties: pink, black, and pink with black spots. [[Link]] can catch and hold a {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} if he gets close to one, by either sneaking up or running at it.<ref name="TWW Pigs"></ref> He can also lure one close by spreading {{Term|TWWHD|All-Purpose Bait|link}} on the ground next to a {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}}.<ref>{{Cite|You could also distract it with bait, I guess.|Abe|TWWHD}}</ref> {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} love to eat, and if they are fed {{Term|TWWHD|All-Purpose Bait}}, they might dig up {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Morth|link}}, and {{Plural|TWWHD|Item|link}} from the ground, including a {{Term|TWWHD|Piece of Heart|link}}. Like [[Cucco|Cuccos]], if a {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} is attacked too many times, even hit by enemies, it will turn red in anger and relentlessly retaliate against Link by ramming him like a raging bull. If there are any nearby {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}}, they will also attack him, similarly to the {{Term|Series|Cucco's Revenge Squad|link}}. The {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} will chase Link until he either perishes, enters a building or goes to a place where they can't reach him, like on top of a high object or in a body of water. While an angry {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} is charging after the Hero of Winds, it will destroy any {{Term|TWWHD|Grass|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Vase|link}} and even {{Plural|TWWHD|Enemy|link}} in its way. When hit, Link will lose one [[Heart Container]] in damage.

Before Link visits the {{Term|TWWHD|Forsaken Fortress|link}} for the first time, the young hero can assist {{Term|TWWHD|Abe|link}} and capture a {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} for his wife, {{Term|TWWHD|Rose|link}}, who will thank him with 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]] after it is placed in their pen.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Thank you! Thank you so much! I've been thinking of getting a pet pig for a while now. The boys will be SO happy when they get home. You sweetie! Ok, Link... Hold out your hand. This is your reward! Don't spend it all in one place!|Rose|TWWHD}}</ref> Link can capture the two other {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island|link}} and receive additional increments of 20 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee}} for each of them.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you SO much for bringing us yet another family member. Here's a little something for you!|Rose|TWWHD}}</ref> When the young hero returns to {{Term|TWWHD|Outset Island}} after placing all three {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} in the pen, he will find that only one, very large {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} is present. If Link speaks to {{Term|TWWHD|Rose}}, she will claim two other {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} ran away though [[Zill]] implies the family actually ate them for dinner.<ref>{{Cite|It was delicious!|Zill|TWW}}</ref> The remaining fully grown {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} is [[Link (Pig)|named after Link]], and it can be provoked as well. Although it is normally quite slow in movement, it runs just as fast as its smaller counterparts when it charges, and because of its size, it is significantly more dangerous - dealing three [[Hearts]] of damage if Link is hit by it. After Link has obtained the {{Plural|TWWHD|Power Bracelet|link}}, he can pick the giant {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}} up and carry it, but his walking will be inhibited as though he is wearing the {{Term|TWWHD|Iron Boots|link}}.

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese release, they are known as '''Wild Pig'''.
*One of the {{Plural|TWWHD|Pig}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}} releases gas when {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} picks it up, with little, green clouds periodically coming out of its rear.
*If Link carries a Pig for a particularly long time, it will eventually begin attacking him.
*When Pigs are attacking Link, the [[enemy]] battle theme begins to play in the background music until they stop charging.[[File:TLoZ Ganon Million Publishing Artwork.png|thumb|Artwork of Ganon from ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'', where he resembles a humanoid pig]]
*[[Ganon]], Link's archenemy and main antagonist of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]], is often associated with Pigs, as he appears to be an anthropomorphic boar in most games. However, the Dark Beast himself is actually a [[Demon]].
