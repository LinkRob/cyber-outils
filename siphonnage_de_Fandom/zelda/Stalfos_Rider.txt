{{Term/Store|Stalfos Rider|Stalfos Riders|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= Stalfos Rider |game= CoH |system= Nintendo Switch |version= 1.0.0 |country= United States of America}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Stalfos Rider}} are a special variety of {{Plural|CoH|Stalfos|link}} that pursue {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} while riding on top of skeletal beings that bear a passing resemble to [[Stalhorse]]s. Their steeds allow them to move on every beat of the music, making them faster than a normal {{Term|CoH|Stalfos}} until their mount is slain, which will ground the rider and turn him into a normal Stalfos. They only appear in caves and dungeons, and are one of the few enemies that can be summoned by a {{Term|CoH|Sarcophagus|link}}.

==Trivia==
They behave identically to the Skeleton Knight enemies from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
