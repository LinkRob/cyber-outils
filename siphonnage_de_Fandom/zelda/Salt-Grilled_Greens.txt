{{Term/Store|Salt-Grilled Greens|Salt-Grilled Greens|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Salt-Grilled Greens are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any vegetable, herb, or flower with {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}}, which offers no status effects. Eating these meals will heal some {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Salt-Grilled Greens are known to be beneficial to one's health, being made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Based on the type of greens used, Link can make the following variations of Salt Grilled Greens:

* '''Hearty Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When they're eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using energizing ingredients. By eating these greens, part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} will be refilled.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or other enduring ingredients. These meals will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}, or other hasty ingredients. Link can eat them to receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}} or other spicy ingredients. If they're eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}} or other electric ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}} or other mighty ingredients. Consuming these greens will boost the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}} or other tough ingredients. Link's defensive abilities will be increased if he eats these.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. They can be eaten to boost Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Salt-Grilled Greens''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Salt-Grilled Greens}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A health-boosting dish made with leafy greens and a touch of salt.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}} or other chilly ingredients. When eaten, they will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
