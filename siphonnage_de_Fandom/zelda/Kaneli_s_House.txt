{{Term/Store|Kaneli's House||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 309}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House}} is the highest house in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, located at the end of the wooden path that twists around the spire. It is next door to {{Term|BotW|Teba's Home|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're curious, {{Color|BotW Blue|Teba's home}} is {{Color|BotW Blue|right next door}} to my own humble abode.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}}, the chief of the {{Term|BotW|Rito|link}}, inside at all hours of the day.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} in his {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House|display=House}}, he will begin to welcome him to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, a visitor. Welcome to...|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> However, he is caught off guard when he recognizes the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} has,<ref>{{Cite|... Oh. OH! That object on your hip... Could it be...|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> which he asks him about.<ref>{{Cite|Forgive me. Where are my manners? I am Kaneli, elder of Rito Village. Now then...is that not a {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} dangling from your hip?|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Once he realizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is capable of boarding the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}},<ref>{{Cite|That means you must be a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}} like Master Revali—one of the few able to board {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}!|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} asks him to stop the rampage of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that I know you have the blood of a {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion}}, there is something I must ask of you. How shall I put this... I would like you to deal with {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}. The giant beast circling above us.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Agreeing to do so begins the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, he must return to {{Term|BotW|Kaneli's House}} to tell him the news.<ref>{{Cite|You have done it! And you survived, at that! You conquered {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}!|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} has prepared a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, the contents of which he allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to take.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, yes! Of course! I must reward you properly! Feel free to take what you find in that treasure chest. You will get more use out of it than I.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> Inside is the {{Term|BotW|Great Eagle Bow|link}}.
