"{{Term/Store|EX Teleportation Rumors!||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|EX Teleportation Rumors!}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|South Akkala Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TMT}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} is obtained upon leaving the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} and loading the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, which can be done by {{Term|BotW|Warping|link}}, reloading a save file, dying, or leaving an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. When the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}} is activated, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be tasked with finding a copy of the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}}, which is located at the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|South Akkala Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare item...|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is possible to activate the second stage of this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} by reading the {{Term|Series|Super Rumor Mill V2}} without triggering any of the loading conditions for its activation.}}

The {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V2|link}} is located on a table inside {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable}}. In the book, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} discusses the dream of teleportation.<ref>{{Cite|Don't you make it a point to dig up rumors while traveling? In any case, today's topic of discussion is... {{Big|The Dream of Teleportation!}}|Super Rumor Mill V2|BotW}}</ref> She reports on a rumor that an ancient medallion, said to have the power to teleport its owner to wherever it was placed.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently, there was once an ancient medallion that could instantly teleport you to wherever you placed it...|Super Rumor Mill V2|BotW}}</ref> It is said be found in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} under a labyrinth in northeast {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hear the treasure chest containing this tool is located beneath a labyrinth in northeast Akkala.|Super Rumor Mill V2|BotW}}</ref>

In the vast chamber beneath {{Term|BotW|Lomei Labyrinth Island|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found on the southern wall. Upon opening the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will obtain the {{Term|BotW|Travel Medallion|link}} and complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You managed to reach Lomei Labyrinth Island and found the {{Color|BotW Red|Travel Medallion}} in a secret underground room!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>
