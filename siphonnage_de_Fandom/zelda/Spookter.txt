{{Term/Store|Spookter||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 270"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Spookter & Spekter}}
{{Term|TMC|Spookter}} is {{Term|TMC|Spekter|link}}'s brother.<ref name="E 270"/> He lives in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, where he blocks a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized entrance to {{Term|TMC|Anju's Henhouse|link}}. He attempts to scare {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} whenever spoken to,<ref>{{Cite|Buggedy-buggedy-BOO! Did I scare ya?|Spookter|TMC}}</ref> but gradually becomes more and more disinterested in scaring people,<ref>{{Cite|Sigh. I guess my heart’s just not in it. Maybe I’m not cut out for this...|Spookter|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Still nothin’, huh? Yeah, I’m just about ready to hang up my sheet and leave the ghost business.|Spookter|TMC}}</ref> eventually admitting to being scared himself after {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that everyone’s gone... I’m kind of scared.|Spookter|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Spekter}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Spookter}} disappears from the game,<ref name="Paradise">{{Cite|I don't know why I've been such a nasty little prankster all the time... All of a sudden, I feel like a weight's been lifted off my shoulders! I feel like I've made up for all my awful deeds! Haha! Hey, {{Color|TMC Green|Spookter}}! See you in paradise!|Spookter|TMC}}</ref> allowing {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to enter the {{Term|TMC|Anju's Henhouse|display=Henhouse}} while {{Term|TMC|Minish}}-sized.
