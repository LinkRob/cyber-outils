==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Porgy Meunière can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} either an {{Term|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}} alongside [[Goat Butter]] and [[Tabantha Wheat]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. The regular version can only be made in different ways. First, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can utilize both types of Porgy when cooking. Second, he can use another ingredient that has a different effect than the Porgy used. When eaten, these meunières will restore some of [[Link|Link's]] {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These dishes are popular among resident of coastal regions.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Two different variations of Porgy Meunière exist:

* '''Mighty Porgy Meunière''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Porgy Meunière}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Popular among residents of coastal regions, this juicy porgy is a delish dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Mighty Porgies. Eating these meals will grant Link a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Porgy Meunière''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Porgy Meunière}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Popular among residents of coastal regions, this juicy porgy is a delish dish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using Tough Porgies. Link can eat them to increase his defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>
