{{Term/Store|Ra||Series, TAoL}} is an enemy in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 199}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
These enemies, resembling the [[Stone Statue|dragon-head decorations]] adorning the walls of some corridors in [[Palace]]s, only appear in Palaces. They fly straight ahead and move in a wavelike pattern as they move across the room. They are temporarily paralyzed when stabbed with a sword, and eventually destroyed after being stabbed enough times.
