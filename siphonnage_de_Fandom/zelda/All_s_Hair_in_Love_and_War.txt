"{{Term/Store|All's Hair in Love and War||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: All's Hair in Love and War}}<br/>09/08/2020 |author= Nintendo |published= September 8, 2020 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/65ee2b9f/index.html |title= Event: All's Hair in Love and War |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|All's Hair in Love and War}}" is an event wherein a greater number of long-haired {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits with long and luscious locks are coming to the Spirit Board in this event!|author= Nintendo |published= September 8, 2020 |retrieved= August 13, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/65ee2b9f/index.html |title= Event: All's Hair in Love and War |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
