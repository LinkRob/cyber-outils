The {{Term/Store|Paraglider Course||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 340}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}} only becomes available once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} clears {{Term|BotW|Korgu Chideh Shrine|link}}. After this, {{Term|BotW|Mimo|link}} will appear at the top of {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}. He has come to the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|display=Island}} to live freely and unrestricted, like the {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What is my purpose here, you ask? I suppose...I am here to find my freedom. In a manner of speaking. I want to live as the wind does—free and unrestricted. To bide here and listen to its voice offers a taste of that freedom.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he also longs to be like the {{Term|BotW|Wind}}.<ref>{{Cite|Don't you long to be like the wind too?|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s ability to fly is different than his own, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} believes it is similar enough to undergo his training, which is supposed to help one become the {{Term|BotW|Wind}}.<ref>{{Cite|Your style of wing is different than mine but similar enough that I think you could undergo my {{Color|BotW Red|training to become the wind}}.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} explains that he wishes to emulate the free movement of the {{Term|BotW|Wind}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Train to what?] The wind is stormy or calm by turns but always moves as it will. I seek to emulate its freedom by mimicking its movements.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> To achieve this goal, he has created a training program that consists of flying through rings that float in the air.<ref>{{Cite|To that end, I have devised a training program that involves flying through rings suspended in the air.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> He promises that if {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to try his training, he will find it rewarding.<ref>{{Cite|Should you choose to undertake this training, I promise that you will find it enriching.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to attempt his training, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} will be happy,<ref>{{Cite|It lifts my spirits to hear of your keen interest.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> though he asks for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to set up the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}}.<ref name="Mimo"/> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has paid, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} asks him to wait while he prepares the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course|display=Course}}.<ref>{{Cite|You should wait here while I prepare the course for you.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> After this is done, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} apologizes for taking so long and explains that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can start whenever he is ready.<ref>{{Cite|That took longer than I expected. My apologies. But it is ready now for you to fly whenever you are ready.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> He recommends taking off to the left, as {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to drift along a calm breeze.<ref>{{Cite|I recommend...the route over there. It can be enjoyable to waft along on a calm breeze. But the choice is ultimately yours. Whatever you decide, the wind will welcome you.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

During the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, a total of 70 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)|link}} will appear on {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island}}. Using {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}} and his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must maneuver himself to pass through as many {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}} as possible without touching the ground.{{Note|{{Term|BotW|Link}} can land on top of {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}} without facing any repercussions.}} The game ends when {{Term|BotW|Link}} either falls out of the sky or passes through all 70 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, at which point {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} will return him to {{Term|BotW|Koholit Rock}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmmm. Stay there and I will come collect you, my flightless friend.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Mimo}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a reward depending on how many {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}} he has passed through. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} totaled 19 or less {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} realizes he overestimated {{Term|BotW|Link}} and tells him to keep training on the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}} to strengthen his ability.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? How many rings did you achieve? {{Color|BotW Red|19}}? I appear to have overestimated your skill a bit, it seems. You may be unaccustomed to flying. But this is nothing a bit of practice on my course can't correct.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> He does not give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a reward. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} clears between 20 and 24 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} says he still has progress to make before he is one with the {{Term|BotW|Wind}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm...{{Color|BotW Red|20 rings}}. A fair showing, but you are not yet one with the wind.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> For his participation, he gifts {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Still, I suppose you deserve this participation reward.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Next, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through 24 to 29 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} notes that he takes to his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider}} well and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You achieved {{Color|BotW Red|25 rings}}, hm? Very good. You take to your strange wings quite well. Here, this is for you.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} pass between 30 to 49 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} informs him that even {{Plural|BotW|Rito|link}} do not score so well, declaring him one with the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} and rewarding him with a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You achieved {{Color|BotW Red|30 rings}}?! That is wonderful! Your score is unsurpassed, even by we Rito! You have truly become one with the wind, and so I must insist that you accept this reward.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through at least 50 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} asks if he is a {{Term|BotW|Rito}} in disguise.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|70}}?! Unbelievable... Are you certain that you're not a Rito in disguise?!|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> For being able to see him fly like that, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|To see you soar was a rare treat. Here, you deserve to be rewarded in kind.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>

After giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} his reward, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} asks if he would like to attempt the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course}} again.<ref>{{Cite|Are you satisfied for the day? Or would you like to train again?|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> During any subsequent attempts, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} will remind {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will be there to swoop in if he gets tired.<ref>{{Cite|I will be ready to swoop in whenever you tire of flying, so do not worry yourself on that account.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> However, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides not to go again, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} compares him to a {{Term|BotW|Cucco|link}}, first flying before clucking.<ref>{{Cite|[Nah, I'll pass.] Yes, you are much like a {{Color|BotW Blue|Cucco}}. First you fly, and now you cluck. Most intriguing.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he anticipates {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s return.<ref>{{Cite|I look forward to seeing you again.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref>
