The {{Term/Store|Wake-Up Mushroom||Series, TMC}} is an item in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom}} is a white-stalked {{Term|TMC|Mushroom|link}} with a red cap and yellow spots. It gets its name from its foul stench, which is strong enough to wake anyone up.<ref>{{Cite|Whaa--! What is that terrifyingly bad smell!? A wake-up mushroom?! I should have guessed! So you woke me up? Thanks, I just remembered I have something urgent to do!|Rem|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can purchase one from {{Term|TMC|Syrup|link}} for 60 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} after his first visit to the {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes! A fine choice, my dear! That's a limited-time article! One whiff, and you're wide-awake! That is why it's called a {{Color|TMC Red|wake-up mushroom}}! Would you like to buy it for 60 Rupees?|Syrup|TMC}}</ref> It can then be taken to {{Term|TMC|Rem|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} to wake him from his sleep. As thanks, {{Term|TMC|Rem}} gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}} that he was working on.<ref>{{Cite|?! Huh? What now?! There are more new shoes! You probably won't believe this, but... I have a strange habit of making shoes in my sleep! So you must have come here because you want some of my shoes, right? Tell you what: because you were nice enough to wake me, you can have 'em! Hold on a sec, though. I just want to finish this one last bit...|Rem|TMC}}</ref>
