{{Term/Store|Water and Fire||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Improve|January 2021}}
After having hope that they might be able to save {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} from the possessed {{Plural|HWAoC|Divine Beasts|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}} run to save them. {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha|link}} is about to get killed by {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} about to be killed by {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}}. However, {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon|link}} arrives from the future to protect {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}, while {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo|link}} arrives to protect {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}}. Meanwhile, Link and Zelda arrive on the scene, and first attempt to charge directly in to save Mipha, but there's Malice in the way. {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} defeat monsters and the {{Plural|HWAoC|Eye of Malice|link}} behind them to destroy the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice|link}} surrounding {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, and go inside. They fight {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} starts to get ready to use {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}'s water to make the lava around {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} disappear. After defeating an {{Term|HWAoC|Igneo Talus|link}} that was in the way, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} charge to Divine Beast Vah Rudania, defeat a Fire Lynel, then go inside the {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}} and successfully defeat {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}}.

After this, the second battle starts, where {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} uses {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Rudania}} to help the defensive forces by destroying the bridges the {{Plural|HWAoC|Guardian|link}} are crossing, and by destroying their headquarters.
