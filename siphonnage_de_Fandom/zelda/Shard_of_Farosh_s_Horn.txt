==Location and Uses==
Shards of Farosh's Horn can be gathered by shooting at the horns of the dragon [[Farosh]], who can be found at [[Lake Hylia]], [[Lake Floria]], and the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. The Shard will glow as it falls to the ground.

Meals [[Cooking|cooked]] using a Shard of Farosh's Horn will have a buff lasting thirty minutes. At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], Shards of Farosh's Horn can be used to create Light Yellow [[Dye]].

Several {{Plural|Series|Armor|link}} require Shards of Farosh's Horn to be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. One Shard and two [[Lynel Guts]] are needed to upgrade the [[Barbarian Armor]] and [[Fierce Deity Boots]] to their final tiers. Two Shards of Farosh's Horn are needed to upgrade the [[Champion's Tunic]] to its second tier. Lastly, two Shards of Farosh's Horn and one [[Star Fragment]] are required to upgrade the [[Cap of the Wild]] to its fourth and final tier.

* Total for '''Barbarian Armor''': 1 (0, 0, 0, 1)

* Total for '''Cap of the Wild''': 2 (0, 0, 0, 2)

* Total for '''Champion's Tunic''': 2 (0, 2, 0, 0)

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Boots''': 1 (0, 0, 0, 1)
