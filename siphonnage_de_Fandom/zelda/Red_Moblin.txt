==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In {{TLoZ}}, {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Moblin}} are common enemies found throughout {{Term|TLoZ|Hyrule|link}}. They keep to the wooded areas, such as the {{Term|TLoZ|Lost Woods|link}}, and throw spears to attack. These spears can be blocked with the most basic {{Term|TLoZ|Shield|link}}.

Not all {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Moblin}} are enemies, however. Some friendly {{Plural|TLoZ|Red Moblin}} hide in secret caves across {{Term|TLoZ|Hyrule}} and give {{Term|TLoZ|Link|link}} {{Plural|TLoZ|Rupee|link}} when spoken to, uttering the [[Iconic Quotes from The Legend of Zelda|iconic phrase]] "It's a secret to everybody" in the process.<ref>{{Cite|IT'S A SECRET TO EVERYBODY.|Red Moblin|TLoZ}}</ref>

==={{TAoL|-}}===
In {{TAoL}}, {{Plural|TAoL|Red Moblin}} appear leaner and muscular. Although their master, {{Term|TAoL|Ganon|link}}, is deceased, they continue to serve him and patrol the {{Term|TAoL|Overworld|link}} of {{Term|TAoL|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Hyrule was on the road to ruin. The power that the vile heart of Ganon had left behind was causing chaos and disorder in Hyrule. What's more, even after the fall of Ganon, some of his underlings remained, waiting for Ganon's return.|game= TAoL|page=3}}</ref> {{Plural|TAoL|Red Moblin}} are common to the wooded areas of {{Term|TAoL|Hyrule}}, although some can be found on bridges or caves. They attack by either jabbing or throwing their spears.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= A little devil that lives mainly in the forest. He attacks Link by throwing spears, but he moves slowly.|game= TAoL|page= 28}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{CoH|-}}===
[[File:CoH Red Moblin Sprite.png|left]]
{{Plural|CoH|Red Moblin}} are a stronger variant of the {{Term|CoH|Moblin|link}}. Their movement is restricted to the beat of music, and have a set attack pattern that forces them to move every two beats, crouching for one beat before jumping on the next, harming anyone that stands in their way. Their main weapon is the spear, which they may throw at {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}} when they get close enough. They always attack within a pattern, pausing on one beat to aim their spear before throwing it on the second beat, and having it land on the third. Once landed, they will make their way towards retrieving their spear unless it has been destroyed, picked up by another Moblin, or the hero using the {{Term|CoH|Power Glove|link}}. Without their spear, they will search for another one or if there are not any around, they will proceed to attack the heroes directly. Their spears are only temporary weapons, which can be throw with the Power Glove in order to deal damage to other enemies.
{{Noncanon|End}}
