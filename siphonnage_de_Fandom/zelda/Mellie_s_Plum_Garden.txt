{{Term/Store|Mellie's Plum Garden||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mellie's Plum Garden<br/>No Tresspassing|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. It is located just East of {{Term|BotW|Steen|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}} farm, down the slight decline. It houses nine plum {{Plural|BotW|Tree}}, all of which were planted by {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} on the day {{Term|BotW|Mellie|link}} was born.<ref>{{Cite|These plums here were planted by Impa on the day I was born. They're special to me, so I keep a close watch on them.|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> As such, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} has cared for and tended to them all her life to the point where she considers them an extension of herself, therefore she forbids everyone from entering, including {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|HEY! What are you doing?! These plum trees... I've been raising them my whole life. They've grown as I've grown. They're... a part of me. When these trees eventually rot and fall away... so will I. That's why I've painstakingly raised them with all the love I can muster. So as long as I'm around, there's no way I'll let you mess with my plum garden!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Whether they were planted specifically to commemorate {{Term|BotW|Mellie}}'s birth isn't specified; {{Term|BotW|Impa}} planted {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden}} despite the poor weather,<ref>{{Cite|I was told it rained the day I was born. Fitting... that it would be raining the day my soul died too...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> but the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} possess clothing that lets them work in the rain.<ref>{{Cite|I wish this rain would take a hint and lay off for a while... The folks in Kakariko Village have really amazing clothes that let them work out in the rain with no problem. Amazing! But only the Sheikah people can wear 'em, so they won't sell us any...|Sagessa|BotW}}</ref> Should all of {{Term|BotW|Mellie}}'s plum {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} get destroyed, {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} considers her life worthless.<ref>{{Cite|WHAT AM I SAYING? THIS IS NO TIME FOR A HISTORY LESSON! M-my plum trees... My plum trees are all gone! Those trees were my life... I suppose today's the day this old lady's life loses its meaning...|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> During the day, and the {{Term|BotW|Flown the Coop|link}} {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, one of {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}} can be found here, but cannot be retrieved when {{Term|BotW|Mellie}} is around.
