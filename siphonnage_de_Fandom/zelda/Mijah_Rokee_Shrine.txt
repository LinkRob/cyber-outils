The {{Term/Store|Mijah Rokee Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Mijah Rokee Shrine}}<br/>A Modest Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Modest Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee Shrine}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}. It is initially buried underground and requires certain conditions to be met before it rises and becomes accessible.

On top of a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}, who reveals he knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" about the area.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His song describes the {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} asking one to stand bare under a bleeding moon.<ref>{{Cite|When the moon bleeds and the fiends are reborn / The monks will invite you as they have sworn.<br/>But first you must stand on the pedestal bare / With nothing between you and the night air.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> This activates the "{{Term|BotW|Under a Red Moon|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

According to the "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song}}", the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} will only rise if {{Term|BotW|Link}} stands on the nearby {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} while wearing no {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} on the night of a {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}}. If he does so, {{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee Shrine}} will rise from underground, allowing entry.<ref>{{Cite|After you took off all your clothing and stood on top of the pedestal on the night of the blood moon, an [sic] the ancient shrine appeared.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, {{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee|link}}, who introduces the combat Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Mijah Rokee. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Mijah Rokee|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee Shrine}} features a large primary chamber, in which a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout III|link}}, wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Sword+|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Shield+|link}}, rises from a pit once he approaches. In order to complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat this {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.

Once the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} is defeated, a portcullis leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee}} will rise, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pass through. The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Mijah Rokee|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Mijah Rokee|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the portcullis contains a {{Term|BotW|Frostblade|link}}.
