'''Gloom's Approach''' is a [[Dungeon]] in {{TotK}}. 

== Entrance to the Approach ==
{{Stub}}
Gloom's Approach is the final area leading to [[Ganondorf]] and is located in [[The Depths]]. Its original purpose was to serve as a pathway to the [[Imprisoning Chamber]] where Ganondorf was originally sealed.

== Themes and navigation ==
{{Stub}}
Compared to the other dungeons in {{TotK|-}}, Gloom's Approach is almost entirely linear in design. It is divided up into several sections, a few of which were visited by [[Link]] and [[Zelda]] during the prologue.

=== Gloom's Approach ===
{{Stub}}
A cave in The Depths that can only be accessed via the [[Hyrule Castle Chasm]].

=== Forgotten Foundation ===
{{Stub}}
{{Main|Forgotten Foundation}}
These ruins contain ancient murals on the walls that depict the events leading up to the [[Imprisoning War]].

=== Imprisoning Chamber ===
{{Stub}}
{{Main|Imprisoning Chamber}}
This large chamber originally served as the prison in which [[Ganondorf#Tears of the Kingdom|Ganondorf]] was sealed by [[King Rauru]]. It now contains a large chasm that leads down into Gloom's Lair.
