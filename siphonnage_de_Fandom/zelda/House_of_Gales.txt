The {{Term/Store|House of Gales||Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}} in {{ALBW}}. It is one of the earlier {{Plural|ALBW|Dungeon}} located in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Zora's Flippers|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}} are required to access it, and its {{Term|ALBW|Boss}} is {{Term|ALBW|Margomill|link}}.

==Entrance to the Dungeon==
The {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales}} (along with the {{Term|ALBW|Tower of Hera|link}}) can be accessed after completing the first {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}, {{Term|ALBW|Eastern Palace|link}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}} is found in the center of {{Term|ALBW|Lake Hylia|link}} in southeastern {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}}. To enter it {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} needs the {{Term|ALBW|Zora's Flippers|link}}, obtained from {{Term|ALBW|Oren|link}} after returning the stolen {{Term|ALBW|Smooth Gem|link}}; and the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}}, which can be rented from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for 20 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|ALBW|Link}} travels to the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales}} in order to acquire the {{Term|ALBW|Pendant of Wisdom|link}}. A number of new enemies are encountered inside, such as {{Plural|ALBW|Bubble|link}}, {{Plural|ALBW|Bari|link}} and {{Plural|ALBW|Heedle|link}}, the latter being the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Sub-Boss|link}}. Navigating the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales}} requires extensive use of the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod}}. It must be used to hover onto moving platforms and travel on wind currents created by fans. These must be activated by hitting stepping on {{Plural|ALBW|Floor Switch|link}} or striking {{Plural|ALBW|Crystal Switch|link}} with the {{Term|ALBW|Bow|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Boomerang|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Hookshot|link}} or {{Plural|ALBW|Bomb|link}}. The fans are used to ascend floors and to hover across gaps. However, they can be dangerous as some lead to pitfalls. The {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales}} also contains a few {{Term|ALBW|Fire|link}} hazards – the first of the game. They can be extinguished using the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod}} or the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Rod|link}}.

The {{Term|ALBW|Boss}} of the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}, {{Term|ALBW|Margomill|link}}, is fought on a platform surrounded by a large pit. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must use the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod}} and his {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}} to defeat it. Afterwards, he acquires a {{Term|ALBW|Heart Container|link}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Pendant of Wisdom}}.

The {{Term|ALBW|Compass|link}} is in the western room of the first floor. It can be accessed by {{Term|ALBW|Wall Merging|link}} onto the vertically moving red block and then emerging on the northernmost platform that it reaches. The {{Term|ALBW|Big Key|link}} can be found on the southeastern outer wall of the {{Term|ALBW|Dungeon}}, accessed by dropping down from the southern central platform on the second floor. The {{Term|ALBW|Treasure Chest|link|display=Chest}} will appear after activating the {{Term|ALBW|Lever|link}} on the far right.
