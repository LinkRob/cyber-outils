The {{Term/Store|Bomb-Shop Man||Series}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 300}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Bomb-Shop Man is the proprietor of the [[Bomb Flower Store]] in [[Lorule]]. He lends out the [[Big Bomb Flower]] that grows in the yard next to his shop, which [[Link]] can borrow for 200 [[Rupee]]s. The Bomb he sells can be used to destroy large boulders, such as the one blocking the entrance to the [[Swamp Palace (A Link Between Worlds)|Swamp Palace]]. The Bomb-Shop Man also appears in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}} to prevent Link from taking the Bomb to the [[Thieves' Hideout (A Link Between Worlds)|Thieves' Hideout]] - he seems to be able to appear suddenly no matter which entrance Link attempts to use.
