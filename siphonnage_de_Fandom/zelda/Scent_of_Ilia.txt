The {{Term/Store|Scent of Ilia||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an object in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|TPHD Red|Scent of Ilia}}!|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TPHD|Scent of Ilia}} is the second {{Term|TPHD|Scent|link}} learned by {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} and replaces the {{Term|TPHD|Youths' Scent|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} learns the {{Term|TPHD|Scent of Ilia}} in the {{Term|TPHD|Lanayru Province|link}} of North {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule|link}} while it is covered in {{Term|TPHD|Twilight|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Scent of Ilia}} is colored pink, and the scent is picked up from {{Term|TPHD|Ilia|link}}'s purse. The scent leads {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link}} along a winding path through {{Term|TPHD|Hyrule Castle Town|link}} and into {{Term|TPHD|Telma's Bar|link}}, where he finds {{Term|TPHD|Telma|link}} and an amnesiac {{Term|TPHD|Ilia}} with a sick {{Term|TPHD|Zora|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Prince Ralis|link}}.
