'''''The Legend of Zelda''''' is a gamebook published by [[Shogakukan]] on December 20, 1997.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The Legend of Zelda|book= {{TLoZ|gamebook}}|publisher= Shogakukan|page= Cover}}</ref> It is based on {{ALttP}}, specifically the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} portion. There is a heavy emphasis on the {{Plural|ALttP|Dungeon|link}} of the game.

==Story==
The prologue recounts {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link|link}}'s backstory, how he lost his parents when he was young and was raised by his aunt and [[Link's Uncle|uncle]] in {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Kakariko Village|link}}. As the book begins, {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link}} is atop the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Pyramid of Power|link}} in the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Dark World|link}}, reflecting on his triumph over {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Agahnim|link}} and acquisition of the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Master Sword|link}}. {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Sahasrahla|link}} communicates with {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link}} through the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Magic Mirror|link}}, directing him to the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Swamp Palace|link}}.

What follows is a fairly straightforward but abbreviated adaptation of the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Dark World}} segment of {{ALttP}}. {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link}} conquers the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Swamp Palace}}, {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Thieves' Town (Dungeon)|link}}, and {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Misery Mire (Dungeon)|link}}, rescuing two {{Plural|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Maiden|link}} in each. After being released from their {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Crystal|link}} prisons, their spirits enter {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link}}'s body and advise him as he continues on his journey. When {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link}} then clears {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Turtle Rock|link}}, {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Zelda|link}} appears and directs him to {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Ganon's Tower|link}}.

After fighting through the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Ganon's Tower|display=Tower}} and defeating {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Agahnim}}, {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link}} goes back to the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Pyramid of Power|display=Pyramid}} to confront {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Ganon|link}}. He defeats him and finds the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Triforce|link}}, and wishes for peace to return. An epilogue shows the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Light World|link}} at peace, the transformed {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Dark World}} denizens restored, and {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Link}} returning the {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Master Sword}} to its pedestal and flying off with {{Term|TLoZ (Gamebook)|Zelda}} atop the [[Flute Boy's Bird|weathervane bird]].
