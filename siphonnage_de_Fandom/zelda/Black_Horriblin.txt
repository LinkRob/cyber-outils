A '''Black Horriblin''' is a variant of a [[Horriblin]] that is the 2nd strongest of all of the horriblin variants. They preferably use a [[spear]] [[Fuse|fused]] to  another spear, or with a [[sword]]. they drop a [[Black Horriblin Horn]] and a [[Horriblin Fang]], but rarely [[Horriblin Guts]]. These can be found in the [[The Depths|Depths]], and in [[Cave|caves]] on the [[Hyrule|surface]].
