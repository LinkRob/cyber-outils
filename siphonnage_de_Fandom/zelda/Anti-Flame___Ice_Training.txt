{{Term/Store|Anti-Flame & Ice Training||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 45 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Flame & Ice Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Flame & Ice Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given ten minutes to defeat an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Lynel|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox}} is located in the southern part of the map, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Lynel}} in the upper-right hand corner. For one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} to reach either of them, they must open a gate by defeating a {{Term|HWAoC|Wizzrobe|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} of the same element. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when they are both killed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Luminous Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Thigh|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
