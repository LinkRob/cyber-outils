==Other Appearances==
==={{CR|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:crazytracy.png|right|x200px]]
Crazy Tracy appears as a cameo in {{CR}},<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= n.d.|retrieved= July 27, 2016|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20150226214943/http://www.nintendo.co.jp/wii/r7cj/guide/traycy.html|title= トレイシー|site= Nintendo.co.jp|type= Archive}}</ref> behaving drastically differently from her {{LA|-}} iteration. She is usually just referred to as Tracy. Like other characters in {{CR|-}}, she is heavily caricatured, having overly large breasts. 

Tracy resides in a mansion on Mimin Island where she can be often found sitting or her throne. She enjoys using her feminine wiles and her wish is to enslave all men in the world. Tracy stores various things in her cleavage, including strawberry {{Wp|Pocky}}s, which she can be seen nibbling. She has portraits of {{Smw|Mario}}, Link from {{TP}}, and an empty portrait frame above her throne. At night, she can be found powdering her face in front of her mirror or taking a bath behind a shower curtain.

The protagonist of {{CR|-}}, Nick, can take part in a quiz hosted by Tracy. He can also help her enslave Takamaru, who actually have problems with seeing and even just thinking of women since he gets very easily excited, but eventually manages to endure the look of Tracy. A portrait of Takamaru pops up between the Mario and Link portraits after Tracy has enslaved him. Furthermore, the Famicom Soldiers seem to be fans of Tracy, since they can be occasionally seen privily watching Tracy in her mansion.
{{Noncanon|End}}
