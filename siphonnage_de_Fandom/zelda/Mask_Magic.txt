{{Term/Store|Mask Magic||Series, CoH}} is an ability in {{CoH}} that can only be used by {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Mask Magic}} ability! It's automatically assigned to {{Icon|CoH L}} and only {{Icon|CoH Skull Kid}} can use it.|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|CoH|Mask Magic}} is obtained after obtaining the {{Term|CoH|Skull Mask|link}} and finding the {{Term|CoH|Petal Trimmer|link}} to return to the {{Term|CoH|Deku King|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh yeah, the {{Color|CoH Blue|Deku Tree}} gave me this to hold on to. He said to give it to you when {{Color|CoH Yellow|the time is right}}.|Deku King|CoH}}</ref> It allows {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid}} to use the power contained within the masks he finds.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Blue|Skull Kid}} can now unlock the special power contained in his masks! Use {{Icon|CoH L}} to use a magic attack. The attack changes depending on the currently equipped mask.|N/A|CoH}}</ref>
