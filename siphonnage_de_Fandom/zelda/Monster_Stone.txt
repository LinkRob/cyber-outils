The '''Monster Stone''' is an item from {{EoW}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Monster Stone|Strange stones that form in monster bodies. They're considered a collectible among enthusiasts.<ref name="Monster Stone">{{Cite|Strange stones that form in monster bodies. They're considered a collectible among enthusiasts.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Monster Stones are rare items dropped by enemies. [[Princess Zelda]] can trade five of them to [[Dampé]] for the [[Clockwork Bangle]], or use them in place of [[Rupee|Rupees]] when repairing [[Automaton]]s.
