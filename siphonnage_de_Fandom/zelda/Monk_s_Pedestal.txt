{{Term/Store|Monk's Pedestal|Monk's Pedestals|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 219}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} are located at the end of {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, serving as the goals. They are square platforms that are encased in a blue energy. Inside, they contain the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} that created the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}} they reside in.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} touches the {{Term|Series|Crest of the Sheikah|link}} on a {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, the blue energy surrounding it will shatter. The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} within will then speak to him, commending {{Term|BotW|Link}} for being able to complete the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the resolve of a true hero.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero...}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruins. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rise.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then grant {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, allow me to bestow this gift upon you... Please accept this Spirit Orb.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> They will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find himself outside of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}. When {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that have previously been completed are revisited, the blue energy and {{Term|BotW|Monk}} of their {{Plural|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}} will remain absent. However, interacting with these {{Plural|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|display=Pedestals}} will still transport {{Term|BotW|Link}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you ready to exit this shrine?|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

16 additional {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} are available as part of {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack. At the end of these {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrines}}, their {{Plural|BotW|Monk}} will gift {{Term|BotW|Link}} either a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb}} as normal, {{Term|BotW|Naboris's Emblem|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Rudania's Emblem|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ruta's Emblem|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|link}}, depending on which {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}} is associated with.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Naboris's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Rudania's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Ruta's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Medoh's Emblem.}}|Monk|BotW}}</ref> At the end of the {{Term|BotW|Final Trial|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will approach {{Term|BotW|Monk Maz Koshia|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}, where he reveals that a battle against him will be his final task.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have proven to possess the power of a true hero. In the name of Goddess Hylia... I offer this final trial!}}|Monk Maz Koshia|BotW}}</ref> He will then {{Term|BotW|Warping|link|display=Warp}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} away.

==Trivia==
* {{Plural|Series|Monk's Pedestal}} bear several examples of {{Term|Series|Sheikah (Language)|link}} text. The tile in front of the {{Plural|Series|Monk's Pedestal|display=Pedestals}} reads "Goal."<ref>{{Cite|{{Script|Sheikah|GOAL}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> In addition to this, their roofs bear the words "Goal Point," which repeats around their sides.<ref>{{Cite|{{Script|Sheikah|GOALPOINT}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref>
