==Biography==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW|-}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Girl}} lives in {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}}, usually found playing in a field with {{Plural|ALBW|Cucco|link}} to the east of the {{Term|ALBW|Weather Vane|link}}. Before {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} turns {{Term|ALBW|Seres|link}} into a painting, the {{Term|ALBW|Girl}} cannot be found in {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Girl}} will ask {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} if he has seen any {{Plural|ALBW|Fairy|link}}, and stating that he might be able to catch one with a {{Term|ALBW|Net|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you seen any of those {{Color|ALBW Blue|fairies}}, Link? You know, I bet you could nab one with a {{Color|ALBW Blue|net}}!|Girl|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It'd be real neat if you could put a {{Color|ALBW Blue|fairy}} in a {{Color|ALBW Blue|bottle}}...|Girl|ALBW}}</ref> After {{Term|ALBW|Link}} completes the {{Term|ALBW|Eastern Palace|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}} places a barrier over {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Girl}} will question the color of the sky over {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|The sky over the castle is a weird color. Why's that?|Girl|ALBW}}</ref> After {{Term|ALBW|Gulley|link}} is turned into a painting by {{Term|ALBW|Yuga}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Girl}} will inquire where he has gone.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't seen {{Color|ALBW Blue|Gulley}} for a while... Where's he gone to?|Girl|ALBW}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH|-}}, {{Term|TFH|Mimi}} lives in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}. She is friends with the {{Term|TFH|Beret Boy|link}} and can often be found playing with him. She hears news from her mother that a {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} is coming to town, and is excited for his arrival. After his arrival, {{Term|TFH|Mimi}} becomes preoccupied with finding a rare blue bird in town, and hopes to take a picture of it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TFH Blue|Mimi}} can't find any blue birds! Not anywhere! Where are they? Where, where? {{Color|TFH Blue|Mimi}} wants to take a picture of a blue bird to show to everyone so they can live happily ever after!|Mimi|TFH}}</ref> She also becomes preoccupied with a book called the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Diary|link}},<ref name="Diary"/> and offers to read it to {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} when she has it. After {{Term|TFH|Link}} lifts the curse off of {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Mimi}} says she is no longer interested in finding the blue bird and is instead going to look for something else that is blue.<ref>{{Cite|Mimi doesn't care about blue birds anymore. Mimi's gonna find something blue and live happily ever after!|Mimi|TFH}}</ref> She ultimately decides to stay with the {{Term|TFH|Beret Boy}}, who is overjoyed by her decision.<ref>{{Cite|Mimi! I'm so glad you finally know how I feel about you!|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref>
