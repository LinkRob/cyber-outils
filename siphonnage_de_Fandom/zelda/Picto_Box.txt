The {{Term/Store|Picto Box||Series, TWW, TWWHD}},{{Exp Game|TWW}}<ref name="E 134">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 134 {{Exp Game|MM, TWW}}}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Pictograph Box||MM, MM3D}},{{Exp Game|MM}}<ref name="E 134"/> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Pictograph Box is a sepia-toned camera which Link obtains from the [[Swamp Tourist Center]] after rescuing [[Koume]] from the [[Woods of Mystery]]. It can hold only one pictograph at a time.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= The Pictograph Box is an advanced piece of Terminan technology. It is capable of saving one snapshot at a time.|author= |published= |retrieved= March 4, 2016|url= http://web.archive.org/web/20160308110429/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/p.jsp|title= The Great Hyrule Encycopedia|site=Zelda.com|type=}}</ref>

The [[Swamp Tourist Center]] runs a Pictograph Contest while the swamp is poisoned. If Link submits a standard pictograph from inside the swamp, Link can choose between a free boat tour or 5 Rupees; if he submits a pictograph of a Big Octo, he can choose between a free boat tour or 20 Rupees; if he submits a pictograph of [[Tingle]] (the [[Swamp Tourist Center Guide]]'s son) or the [[Deku King]], he will be rewarded with a [[Piece of Heart]].

The [[Fisherman]] of [[Great Bay]] requests Link a pictograph of a [[Gerudo Pirate]] in exchange for the [[Seahorse]] that he captured near [[Pinnacle Rock]]. In [[Zora Hall]], a [[Zora]] requests a pictograph of [[Lulu]]; if Link gives him a picture including her face, he will give him 20 Rupees.

In {{MM3D}}, the Pictograph Box now takes pictographs in full color.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, the Picto Box allows Link to take and store three black and white pictographs at a time. It is found inside a [[Treasure Chest]] at the end of the crawlspace maze in [[Town Jail|Tingle's jail cell]] on [[Windfall Island]]. In {{TWWHD}}, the storage is increased to twelve images. The Picto Box also has a new feature which enables Link to take self-photographs while making any of nine facial expressions. Link can send pictographs to Miiverse via [[Tingle Bottle]]. Pictographs found inside Tingle Bottles can be saved in the Picto Box for later use in the [[Nintendo Gallery]]. The Deluxe Picto Box is not required to save color pictographs from Miiverse.

====Deluxe Picto Box====
{{Main|Deluxe Picto Box}}
[[File:TWWHD Deluxe Picto Box Artwork.png|70px|right]]
The Deluxe Picto Box is an upgraded version of the original Picto Box which allows Link to take pictographs in full color. Link needs to catch a [[Forest Firefly|firefly]] from [[Forest Haven]] to obtain the Deluxe Picto Box.

In {{TWWHD|-}}, Link starts with the Deluxe Picto Box, eliminating the need of the Forest Firefly sidequest.
