{{Term/Store|Veil Falls||Series, TMC}} is an area in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Veil Falls}} is a series of cliffs surrounding a large {{Term|TMC|Waterfall|link}} located in northeastern {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. It connects to three other areas: {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}} to the south, {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}} to the southwest, and {{Term|TMC|Veil Springs|link}} to the north.

As a waterfall, water is abundant in {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} are used when traversing the base of the waterfall. In order to progress further, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must enter a cave that is blocked by a stone doorway with a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone|link}} slot; the other piece is obtained from {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I grant to you this Kinstone... Seek the source of the flow in Hyrule... Only then will your path open...|Gustaf|TMC}}</ref> At the very top of {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls}} is {{Term|TMC|Veil Springs|link}}, which houses a {{Term|TMC|Tornado|link}} that serves as the entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}, the location of the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So! This was what they meant by all of that "source of the flow" business! Well, this waterfall certainly is the source of all Hyrule's water. According to Gustaf, the final element lies somewhere behind the falls. We've dawdled long enough, Link! Let's go find that element!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Splitblade|link}}, one of the nine {{Term|TMC|Blade Brothers|link}}, can be found at a southern cave, and after unblocking the entrance by {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}, he will "teach" {{Term|TMC|Link}} how to charge his {{Term|TMC|Split Gauge|link}} faster.<ref>{{Cite|Mnnh! Now I will teach you how to fill your Split Gauge faster than ever!|Splitblade|TMC}}</ref>
