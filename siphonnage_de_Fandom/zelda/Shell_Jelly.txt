{{Term/Store|Shell Jelly|Shell Jellies|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref name="E">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 203}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Shell Jellies are [[Chu]]-like monsters that encapsulate and protect [[Chu Worm]]s, making them both invulnerable to attacks. To defeat them, [[Link]] must either drag out and kill the Chu Worm using the [[Clawshot]], or blast them with a [[Bomb Arrow]].<ref name="E"/><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref> After getting separated, Chu Worms will attempt to hop back into Shell Jellies.
