==Location and Uses==
Shards of Dinraal's Fangs can be collected from the dragon, [[Dinraal]]. If Link shoots Dinraal's teeth with an arrow, a fang will drop onto the land, appearing as a glowing light. Like other dragon materials, Link can only make one fang drop with each encounter with the dragon. Using Dinraal's Fangs in a [[Cooking Pot]] will result in [[Dubious Food]]. They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Crimson [[Dye]].

Two Shards of Dinraal's Fangs, as well as five {{Plural|Series|Energetic Rhino Beetle|link}}, are needed to upgrade the [[Trousers of the Wild]] to their third tier. Another Shard of Dinraal's Fang can be used to upgrade the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Mask|link}} to its third tier.

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Mask''': 1 (0, 0, 1, 0)

* Total for '''Trousers of the Wild''': 2 (0, 0, 2, 0)
