{{Term/Store|Demon|Demons|Series|plural}} are a recurring race of monstrous beings that are usually of evil alignment in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 98">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 98 {{Exp Game|Series}}}}</ref> Although demons are inherently evil and they exude a dark aura that attracts monsters and radically changes the behavior of [[Animal]]s, very few exceptions exist.<ref>{{Cite|There's no denying that when I was a demon, I must have radiated a fiendish power into my surroundings. Now that I'm human, it seems that most-unpleasant aura has completely faded from this place, like an odious aroma in the wind! And goodness me, I couldn't be more elated! Now everyone can finally live in peace, and it's all thanks to you.|Batreaux|SS}}</ref> They desire to have control over the world and thus constitute a significant threat to [[Hyrule]] and its people. Usually within the series, [[Ganon]] is known as the master of demons as the Prince of Darkness. However, some games have different lead antagonists who are also demons themselves. Often, [[Link]] and sometimes [[Princess Zelda]] fight and destroy these creatures in order to save Hyrule or other lands.

The terms "demon" and "monster" are sometimes used interchangeably, but generally demons are born from the source of evil while monsters are creatures corrupted by dark magic. Demons command and create evil, while monsters do their bidding.<ref name="E 98"/>

In gameplay, most demons are simply [[Enemy|enemies]] or [[Boss|bosses]] but certain ones are also [[Final Boss|Final Bosses]].

==Characteristics==
===History===
==={{SS|-}}===
During the [[Era of the Goddess Hylia]], the Demon race emerged from the earth, desiring the [[Triforce]], originating from their leader [[Demise]].<ref name="eternal">{{Cite|This eternal being has conquered time itself. It is the source of all monsters.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref name="origin">{{Cite Book|quote=The origin of evil that threatens the world. Legends say that Demise's appearance changes depending on the era and those who bear witness to him.|book=E|page=99}}</ref> Demise desired to rule the world with the enormous power that the Triforce would grant.<ref name="HH 71">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH|publisher= |page= 71}}</ref> [[Hylia]] protected the Triforce and took the surviving people to the sky, hiding the Triforce there as well.<ref name="HH 71"/> Hylia and the remaining tribes on the surface fought against Demise and managed to seal him away;<ref name="HH 71"/> however, the goddess knew the seal would not last, so she decided to extinguish Demise with the power of the Triforce.<ref name="HH 71"/> However, gods cannot use the power of the Triforce, so she abandoned her status as god to be reborn as a human.<ref name="HH 71"/>

When Hylia was eventually reincarnated as a human girl named Zelda, Demise's servant [[Ghirahim]] made himself the Demon Lord and set his plans to revive the Demon King in motion. Ghirahim sent demon hordes on the surface to locate Hylia's mortal incarnation.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Around the same time, Demise sent his follower Ghirahim to the Surface, where Ghirahim gave himself the title of lord. Through secret plotting and scheming, he unleashed demon hordes on the land in an attempt to find the goddess and resurrect the Demon King Demise.|book=HH|page=73}}</ref> Eventually Ghirahim succeeded in reviving Demise; however, Link defeated Demise and the demon race disappeared from the earth.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=After arriving in the past, Ghirahim performed the ritual to resurrect the Demon King Demise. Link followed, battling and defeating Ghirahim, but he was too late. Demise was reawakened! After an intense battle, Link defeated the demon. Upon his termination, the Demon King’s hatred was absorbed and sealed in the Master Sword. The demons on the Surface no longer terrorized its inhabitants.|book=HH|page=76}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
During the Force Era, demons returned and almost covered the world in darkness. However, the [[Hero of Men]] used the [[Light Force]] and the [[Picori Blade]] to seal all the demons in a treasure chest, restoring peace.<ref>{{Cite|A long, long time ago... when the world was on the verge of being swallowed by shadow... The tiny Picori appeared from the sky, bringing the hero of men a sword and a golden light.|N/A|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Using the sword, the Hero of Men sealed the evil beings away in the Bound Chest and brought peace to the world once again.|book=HH|page=78}}</ref> Years later, seeking the [[Light Force]], [[Vaati]] opened the treasure chest, accidentally unleashing the demons upon Hyrule once more.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Vaati believed the Light Force was in the Bound Chest. He broke the Picori Blade to unseal the chest and found that it did not contain the Light Force at all. The evil that had been sealed inside poured forth from the chest.|book=HH|page=79}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
In the Era of the Hero of Time, [[Ganondorf]] successfully infiltrated the [[Sacred Realm]] and took the [[Triforce of Power]]. As the Sacred Realm reflects the heart of the one who takes the Triforce, it became a dark world infested by demons, known as the Evil Realm.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Sacred Realm is a mirror that reflects the hearts of those who set foot in it. An evil heart will turn the realm into a living hell. A pure heart will transform it into paradise. Using the Triforce of Power, Ganondorf became the Demon King. The Sacred Realm was distorted into a nightmarish world where demons ran amuck.|book={{HH}}|page=87|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref>

==={{ALttP|-}}===
In the Era of Light and Dark, [[Agahnim]] used his powers to send people, including the [[Maiden|Maidens]], to the [[Dark World]]. Anyone sent there became a demon based on their heart, except Link, who possessed the qualities of a hero and instead became a [[Bunny Link|pink bunny]] (until he found the [[Moon Pearl]], which allowed him to remain in human form in the Dark World), and the Maidens became trapped in [[Crystal|crystals]].<ref name=AgahnimDarkWorld>{{Cite Book|quote=Those who stepped on the Magical Warp Tiles that served as entrances would be lost to the Dark World, transformed into demons. Link, who possessed the qualities of the Hero, did not become a demon, but was instead turned into an animal (in this case, a pink bunny). He managed to obtain the Moon Pearl, which prevented him from being transformed while in the Dark World, and the Magic Mirror, which allowed him to return to the Light World.|book={{HH}}|page=95|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref> 

==={{OoA|-}}===
At some point after the defeat of Agahnim, demons infested [[Labrynna]], and attacked [[Impa]] shortly before Link awoke to help her.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Next, Link alighted in the unfamiliar land of Labrynna. He came to the aid of Impa, who was surrounded by demons. Impa had other troubles, however, finding herself unable to move a boulder with the crest of the royal family on it.|book={{HH}}|page=99|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref> 

==={{LA|-}}===
Demons also appeared in the [[Wind Fish]]'s dream, and attempted to prevent Link from ending the dream by waking the Wind Fish.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The mysterious owl that appeared before Link informed him that the only way to escape the island was to awaken the Wind Fish. As he collected the Instruments of the Sirens, Link uncovered a shocking truth. The world in which he had found himself existed within the Wind Fish's dream, and waking the Wind Fish would cause the island to disappear in a stream of bubbles. The demons had attacked Link in order to preserve themselves and the existence of their island home.|book={{HH}}|page=103|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref>

==={{TLoZ|-}}===
In the Era of Decline, following his resurrection, Ganon attacked the kingdom of Hyrule with his demon army and stole the Triforce of Power.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Demon King has been defeated countless times by heroes in the past. He is thought to have been resurrected after many years.|book=HH|page=106}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There came a day when the demon army led by the Demon King Ganon invaded Hyrule and stole away the Triforce of Power.|book=HH|page=106}}</ref> 

==={{TAoL|-}}===
Although Link managed to destroy Ganon, his followers remained in the area planning to resurrect their master by sacrificing Link and sprinkling his blood over the ashes of the Demon King.<ref name="Ganon's Resurrection"/>

==={{TP|-}}===
In the Twilight Era, [[Zant]] was given magical powers by Ganondorf, which he used to transform the [[Twili]], the inhabitants of the [[Twilight Realm]], into demons.<ref name=TwiliDemon>{{Cite Book|quote=Ganondorf was sent to the Twilight Realm. There, the Demon King granted the ambitious Zant magical powers and began plotting to unite the Light World and the Twilight Realm into a single realm known as the Dark World. Zant's influence steadily increased. Clouds of darkness covered the Twilight Realm, and the Twili began transforming into demons.|book={{HH}}|page=114|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref> He used this army of demons to attack Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The peace that had reigned in the Light World for hundreds of years was broken by Zant's army of Shadow Beasts.|book=HH|page=114}}</ref>

==={{FSA|-}}===
In the Shadow Era, a reincarnation of Ganondorf used the [[Dark Mirror]] to create demonic copies of [[Link]], known as [[Shadow Link]]s. If this mirror is used by an evil person, it reflects their heart and creates demons.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Dark Mirror: A mirror that reflects the evil heart of its bearer, spawning demons.|book=HH|page=119}}</ref> This new Ganondorf used the power of the [[Trident]] to turn the [[Knights of Hyrule]] into demons.<ref name=KnightDemon>{{Cite Book|quote=Ganondorf then utilized the power of the Trident to defeat the Knights of Hyrule one by one as they rushed to the rescue, transforming them into demons. He then placed a curse on the Royal Jewels they held.|book=HH|page=120}}</ref> With the knights now demons and the Royal Jewels turned into gates to the [[Dark World]], demons returned to Hyrule again.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The now demonic Knights of Hyrule and their Royal Jewels were scattered across the kingdom by Shadow Link and his kin. The Tower of Winds also disappeared from sight. With the Royal Jewels transformed into gateways to the Dark World, demons began appearing in the land of Hyrule once again.|book={{HH}}|page=120|publisher=Dark Horse Books}}</ref>

==={{TWW|-}}===
In the Era of the Great Sea, the [[Forsaken Fortress]] (known as the Demonic Beast Island in Japanese) served as Ganondorf's base, and was inhabited by demons.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The resurrected Ganondorf's base of operations. It is crawling with large numbers of demons.|book=HH|page=124}}</ref>

==Abilities==
===Transformation===
It is possible for demons to turn into humans. In {{SS}}, [[Gratitude Crystal]]s can be used to turn [[Batreaux]] into a human.<ref>{{Cite|All the {{Color|SS Gold|Gratitude Crystals}} that you collected helped him turn from a demon into a human.|Fi|SS}}</ref>

Likewise, humans can turn into demons. Ganondorf, originally a [[Gerudo]], became the Demon King Ganon - his true form, after claiming the [[Triforce#Triforce of Power|Triforce of Power]] during the events of {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Using the Triforce of Power, Ganondorf became the Demon King.|book=HH|page=87}}</ref>  

Anyone [[Agahnim]] sent to the [[Dark World]] became a demon, except Link, who possessed the qualities of a hero and instead became a pink bunny (until he found the [[Moon Pearl]], which allowed him to remain in human form in the Dark World).<ref name=AgahnimDarkWorld/>

The reincarnation of Ganondorf in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]'', also originally a [[Gerudo]], became the Demon King after claiming the [[Trident]], similar to the original. This Ganondorf then turned the [[Knights of Hyrule]] into demons with the Trident's power.<ref name=KnightDemon/>

Other races can also become demons. [[Vaati]], who was originally a [[Minish]], became a demon after draining the [[Light Force]].<ref>{{Cite|The sorcerer Vaati took this form after draining the power of the light force from Princess Zelda. The evil beams from his eyes are devastating.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> 

The [[Twili]] were turned into demons by [[Zant]], using the magical powers bestowed upon him by Ganondorf.<ref name=TwiliDemon/>

===Possession===
Demons have demonstrated the ability to possess other creatures. Both Ganon and [[Malladus]] have [[possessed Zelda]]; both have also demonstrated the ability to exist as a disembodied spirit. [[Bellum]] also possessed Linebeck; turning him into a giant Phantom.

==Demon Kings==

[[Demise]], who had existed eternally,<ref name="eternal"></ref> was the origin of evil and the source of all monsters.<ref name="origin"></ref> The {{HH}} references other demon kings that existed prior to {{SS|-}}, meaning that there were more alongside Demise, prior to being wiped out during the ancient wars.<ref name="DemonKings">{{Cite Book|quote=Not all residents of the Surface are friendly. Some are the subordinates of the demon kings from ancient wars. Since the demise of the demon kings, their numbers have multiplied, and now they are destroying the Surface.|book=HH|page=59}}</ref>{{Note|Japanese nouns can generally be used to refer to both singular and plural subjects, so it is possible that the original author was referring only to a single demon king in a single ancient war, namely, Demise in the [[Ancient Battle]]. Or as {{E}} notes, Demise's various appearances change from era to era.}} 

Demise, [[Ganon]], and Malladus have all held the title of Demon King. Demon kings, due to their immense power, are generally depicted as requiring specialized weapons such as [[Silver Arrow]]s or the [[Master Sword]] to kill, as both weapons have holy power. Rather than getting killed, demon kings can be merely imprisoned (which happens most often with Ganon/Ganondorf). Due to the inability to permanently eliminate the demon king (due to the existence of resurrection), the title is seemingly retained after death.<ref name="Ganon's Resurrection">{{Cite Book|quote=Ganon's Resurrection: It is said that Ganon can be resurrected by sacrificing Link, the one who defeated him, and sprinkling the Hero's blood over his ashes.|book=HH|page=107}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The twin witches known as Twinrova were plotting ways to perform the ceremony that would resurrect the Demon King. For the resurrection ceremony to occur, it was necessary to light the Flame of Destruction, the Flame of Sorrow, and the Flame of Despair, derived from the chaos, sorrow, and hopelessness of humanity. They would also need to offer up a sacred sacrifice.|book=HH|page=98}}</ref>

== Trivia ==
*The term "[[wikipedia:Demon|demon]]", based on certain real-world religious beliefs, especially [[wikipedia:Christianity|Christianity]], are supernatural malevolent and grotesque entities and are the minions of [[wikipedia:Devil_in_Christianity|the Devil]], in eternal rebellion against [[wikipedia:God_in_Christianity|God]]. In the context of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'']], demons are likewise vulnerable to holy elements, while [[Ganon]] and [[Demise]] play a similar role to that of the Devil as intelligent Demon Kings and have no regard of life.[[File:MM3D Fierce Deity Link art (close up).png|thumb|201x201px|Link as the demonic-looking Fierce Deity in an artwork of ''Majora's Mask 3D'']]
*[[Link]]'s [[Fierce Deity Link|Fierce Deity]] form from {{MM}}, is referred to as a "Demon God" in the French localization as well as its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (Himekawa)|manga adaptation]] by [[Akira Himekawa|Akira Himeawa]]. Despite its rather ferocious appearance, it is technically a [[Deity]], and Link himself uses its power specifically to defeat Majora and rescue [[Termina]].
*[[Batreaux|Batreuax]] in {{SS}} is a very rare example of a demon that is not evil.
*In the First Quest of {{TLoZ}}, [[Level 7|Level-7]], the seventh [[Dungeons in The Legend of Zelda|Dungeon]], is known as Demon. Its map is shaped like such a creature facing to the right with its mouth open, appearing to be roaring.[[File:OoS Onox Dark Dragon Sprite.png|thumb|154x154px|Onox's true form shares many features of a demon]]
*Despite [[Onox|General Onox]]'s final form being technically a [[dragon]], his ferocious appearance closely resembles that of demons.
*Although the English version of ''Hyrule Historia'' clearly states that Ganon is Demise's reincarnation, the Japanese version only suggests a connection between the two beings while leaving it ambiguous as to their precise ties to each other.
*Ganon's appearance in ''[[Zelda's Adventure]]'', a title from the unsuccessful [[Philips CD-i]] series, has many physical features of that of actual demons according to various religious depictions than in other games.
* [[Bowser]], the main antagonist of the [[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], and the character, Ganondorf, who he's allied with in ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl]]'', has a title named 大魔王 (''Daimaō''), meaning "Great Demon King". However, this might be a mistranslation from Japan, as he is supposed to be called "Great Sorcerer King".
* Although not part of the ''Zelda'' franchise, the main antagonist of ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Inferno Inferno], the spirit of [https://soulcalibur.fandom.com/wiki/Soul_Edge Soul Edge], is said to be a demon himself. Link when using the unlockable [[Soul Edge (Complete)]] technically uses his body in the form of a [[Sword]] and [[Shield]] resembling a wicked version of the [[Master Sword]] and [[Hylian Shield]].
