{{Term/Store|Purple Gargoyle|Purple Gargoyles|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Purple Gargoyle|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Purple Gargoyles are statues that appear within {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}} and the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}}. As statues, they cannot move, though they will spit fireballs at anyone who passes by, dealing damage to everyone except {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. In the {{Term|CoH|King|link}}'s throne room, a castle guard notes that the fireballs seem to be harmless to anyone wearing purple clothing.<ref>{{Cite|The other night, I walked by a statue that shot out purple fireballs. I don't know why, but they didn't even burn my purple pajamas!|Guard|CoH}}</ref>
