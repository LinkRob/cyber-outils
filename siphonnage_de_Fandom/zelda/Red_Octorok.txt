==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
The Red Octorok is an {{Term|FSA|Enemy|link}} that can be found in the very beginning of the game. If {{Term|FSA|Link|link}} touches it, half a {{Term|FSA|Heart|link}} will be taken. The same amount of damage will be inflicted if he touches the {{Term|FSA|Rock|link}} that it spits out.
