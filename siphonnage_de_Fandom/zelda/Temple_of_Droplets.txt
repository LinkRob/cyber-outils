The {{Term/Store|Temple of Droplets||Series, TMC}} is the fourth {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> It is a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-size temple in a block of [[ice]] in the middle of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, accessible with the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}}. The exterior of the temple itself resembles a {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}, which {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} will fall into upon shrinking down.

==Themes and Navigation==
[[File:TMC Temple of Droplets Artwork.png|thumb|left|190px|{{Term|TMC|Link}} finding the {{Term|TMC|Big Key|link}} frozen within a block of ice]]
The temple is largely frozen over, with certain items encased in [[Ice#Ice Blocks|ice blocks]] and many floors slick with ice. The temple has many skylights that can be opened to let in [[Light Beam|sunbeams]] which are used to melt some of the ice. Enemies encountered in the temple include {{Plural|TMC|Mulldozer|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Pesto|link}} and {{Plural|TMC|Sluggula|link}}. Oddly enough, the B1 and B2 floors both resemble pots on the map.

The {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets}} has three {{Plural|TMC|Sub-Boss|link}}: a {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu|link}} and a pair of {{Plural|TMC|Madderpillar|link}} in the dark. The item is the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}}, which is obtained after defeating the {{Term|TMC|Big Blue Chuchu}}. The {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern}} can be used to light {{Plural|TMC|Torch|link}}, to light up dark rooms, and to melt ice, all of which are frequent puzzle elements in the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}.

Like the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets}} has portions that are filled with water. As before, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must steer a [[Lily Pad]] through the network of waterways. This is necessary despite having the Flippers because some destination banks are too steep to climb onto from the water itself.

The {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} is an ordinary sized {{Term|TMC|Octorok|link}}, but it looks like a {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok|link}} from the perspective of a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} first encounters this {{Term|TMC|Boss}} frozen solid early in the temple, but fully unlocking a skylight will defrost it, along with the nearby frozen {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}}. Defeating the {{Term|TMC|Big Octorok}} allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to recover the {{Term|TMC|Water Element}}, which it swallowed.
