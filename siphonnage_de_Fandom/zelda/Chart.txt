{{Main|Sea Chart, Treasure Chart, Triforce Chart, Special Chart}}

In {{TWW}}, there are four main types of {{Plural|TWWHD|Chart}}. {{Plural|TWWHD|Sea Chart|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Treasure Chart|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Triforce Chart|link}}, and {{Plural|TWWHD|Special Chart|link}}.

{{Plural|TWWHD|Triforce Chart|link}} are {{Plural|TWWHD|Chart}} leading to a piece of the {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce of Courage|link}}. Because they are written in {{Term|TWWHD|Hylian (Language)|link|display=ancient Hylian}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} is unable to read them and must bring them to {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle|link}} to be deciphered, at the price of 398 {{Plural|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} each.{{Fact|TWWHD}} There are a total of eight {{Plural|TWWHD|Triforce Chart}} in {{TWW|-}}, but only three appear in {{TWWHD}}.{{Fact|TWWHD}}
