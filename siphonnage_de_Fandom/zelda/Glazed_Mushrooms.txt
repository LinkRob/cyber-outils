==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Glazed Mushrooms are meals made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}} together with [[Courser Bee Honey]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can only be made by using a {{Term|BotW|Mushroom}} that has a differing effect to the Courser Bee Honey. Eating this meal will heal a few {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The Courser Bee Honey gives the Mushrooms a sweet and complex taste with a savory finish.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Only one variation of Glazed Mushrooms exists:

* '''Energizing Glazed Mushrooms''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Glazed Mushrooms}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The honey in this mushroom dish gives it a sweet, complex taste and a savory finish.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using either {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}}. If eaten, they'll replenish a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>
