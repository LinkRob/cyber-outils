'''''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD — Official Digital Strategy Guide''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TPHD}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The guide has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD — PRIMA Official Game Guide]]," containing a complete walkthrough of {{TPHD}}, detailed maps, and a listing of {{Plural|TPHD|Item|link}} and collectibles.

It is served using Prima's in-browser digital guide service and has six sample chapters. These chapters contain basic player actions as well as overviews and partial walkthroughs of {{Term|TP|Ordon Village|link}} and the {{Term|TP|Forest Temple|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Prima Games|url=https://eguides.primagames.com/games/legend-zelda-twilight-princess-hd/guides/the-legend-of-zelda-twilight-princess-hd-eguide|site=eguides.primagames.com|title=The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Official Digital Strategy Guide|retrieved=October 1, 2022}}</ref> Prior to March 1, 2022, the guide was available for 20 gold points as a [[My Nintendo Rewards|My Nintendo Reward]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= September 25, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/rewards/e9e56558b5c57b3d|title= Online Access - The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD eGuide|site= my.nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>
