{{Term/Store|Ice Rose|Ice Roses|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 128"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Ice Rose}} are rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Silver Shrine|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Ice Rose}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}''', ''"Don't get hit by snowballs!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Ice Rose}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Ice Temple}}''', ''"Transport the orb quickly!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Ice Rose}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 1,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Ice Rose}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga|link}}.<ref name="E 128"/>
