==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Common Sparrow}}
Common Sparrows are a widespread and ubiquitous species of bird, and can be seen throughout Hyrule. They chiefly inhabit forests and grasslands, but sometimes enter [[Hylian]] settlements.<ref>{{Cite|As the name suggests, these birds are rather common around Hyrule. They mainly live in plains or forests but sometimes venture out to villages.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They are omnivorous, and feed primarily on nuts and small [[insect]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Their diet consists of nuts and small insects.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When they're killed, they drop {{Plural|BotW|Chickaloo Tree Nut|link}}.
