'''Ancient Bow and Arrow''' is the [[Final Smash]] of [[Link]] in {{SSBU}}. It replaces his [[Triforce Slash]] attack from previous games.

==Overview==
The Ancient Bow and Arrow attack is like [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] and [[Sheik]]'s Final Smash, [[Light Arrow]]. It involves Link drawing an [[Ancient Bow]] and firing an [[Ancient Arrow]] straight in front of him. The Ancient Arrow inflicts a lot of damage to whoever it hits and sends them flying away.
