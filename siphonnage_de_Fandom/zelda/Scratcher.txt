{{Term/Store|Scratcher||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Scratcher}} is {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Cat|link}}.<ref name="E 271"/> While attempting to locate {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is tasked with retrieving overdue {{Plural|TMC|Library Book|link}}, one of which is in the possession of {{Term|TMC|Julietta}}. From inside {{Term|TMC|Julietta}}'s {{Term|TMC|Girl's House|link|display=House}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must make his way up onto the top of the {{Term|TMC|Bookshelf|link}} while avoiding {{Term|TMC|Scratcher}}. {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} warns him of the {{Term|TMC|Cat}}, claiming that it looked at him as if it wanted to eat him.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Did you see that cat looking at me?! I know it wants a piece of me!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} makes it past {{Term|TMC|Scratcher}}, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} expresses his relief.<ref>{{Cite|Waaah! That really startled me! Everything's scary when you're small!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>

Despite this, while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, {{Term|TMC|Scratcher}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|Rowr! Would you like to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Mrow...|Scratcher|TMC}}</ref>
