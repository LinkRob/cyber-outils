==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|Series|Shock Switch}} are small sphere- or obelisk-shaped {{Plural|Series|Switch|link}} that can be activated by being slashed by a {{Term|Series|Sword}} or hit by another weapon. They will then change color. {{Plural||Shock Switch}} are usually a two-position toggle with effects that last until they are hit again, but single-use and timed varieties also exist. Sometimes several {{Plural||Shock Switch}} will be connected to the same puzzle, each one acting as a toggle. They frequently move barriers and walls into new positions and affect everything in the {{Term|Series|Dungeon}}, rather than just the immediate area like other {{Plural|Series|Switch}}. In some cases, a {{Term|Series|Bomb|link}} must be used to activate a {{Term|Series|Shock Switch}} after a time delay so {{Term|Series|Link}} can get ready for its effect.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Crystal Switches|In the dungeons there are blue and orange blocks which rise and fall into the floors. These will often block your path. Strike the Crystal Switch with your sword or some other weapon to make the blocks retract. When the Crystal Switch is red, the orange blocks will retract. When the the Crystal Switch is blue, the blue blocks will retract.}}
In {{ALttP}}, shock switches are used mainly for Barrier block puzzles used throughout dungeons. When link hits one and it changes color, the blocks will either lift or recede into the ground, changing the path link can take.

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Crystal Switches|In some dungeons, cubical barriers may prevent you from advancing. You can make these blocks rise and fall by applying force to the Crystal Switches with your sword or other items. Note that each switch will affect every block in the dungeon.}}
In {{LA}}, shock switches retain many of their properties from a link to the past, and are used in barrier block puzzles throughout dungeons.

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Shock Switches are used in several dungeons and mini dungeons, most notably the {{Term|OoT|Fire Temple|link}}, where they are frequently used to solve puzzles and open {{Term|OoT|Goron|link}} cells throughout the dungeon.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, hit switches are used in several dungeons and mini dungeons, notably the [[Ancient Castle of Ikana]] where they are used to solve puzzles and preserve progress throughout the dungeon.

==={{TMC|-}}===
Two varieties of {{Plural|TMC|Shock Switch}} appear in {{TMC}}, often in {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} or cave systems in areas such as {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}. Both can be activated if {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} strikes them with a {{Term|TMC|Sword|link}}, a {{Term|TMC|Bomb|link}} or {{Term|TMC|Remote Bomb|link}}, an {{Term|TMC|Arrow|link}} or {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Boomerang|link}} or {{Term|TMC|Magical Boomerang|link}}, or by firing a {{Term|TMC|Sword Beam|link}} or {{Term|TMC|Peril Beam|link}} at them. The gray variety, once struck and activated, turn yellow and their effects are permanent. While some appear on their own, they often come in groups and must be struck simultaneously to activate their effect. The colored variety are either blue or red, and act as a toggle that alternates between the two colors as they are struck. Both varieties of {{Plural|TMC|Shock Switch}} often activate bridges or cause various {{Plural|TMC|Object|link}} to appear, such as {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}.
