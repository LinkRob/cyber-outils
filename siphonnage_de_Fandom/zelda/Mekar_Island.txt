{{Term/Store|Mekar Island||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|'''Mekar Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]].''

==Features and Overview==
Mekar Island is a small island located in [[Lake Mekar]]. Mekar Island sits directly West of [[Korok Forest]] and the [[Lost Woods]]. Mekar Island is devoid of life, hosting only a dead [[Tree]] in its center. If [[Link]] steps on the Island at night a group of various Stal enemies will rise and attack him.

In Tears of the Kingdom, the island remains relatively unchanged. However, there is now a metal [[Treasure Chest|chest]] buried in the ground next to the tree that contains a [[Forest Dweller's Spear]]. More significantly, when Link steps foot on the island a group of [[Gloom Spawn]] will appear, and a [[Phantom Ganon]] will appear when the Spawn are defeated.
