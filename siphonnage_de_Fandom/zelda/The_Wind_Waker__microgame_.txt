'''The Wind Waker''' is a ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|The Legend of Zelda]]''-themed microgame in the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series. It first appears in ''WarioWare: Smooth Moves'', as part of 9-Volt and 18-Volt's shared stage, "The Multiplayer Test," and returns in ''WarioWare Gold'', now as only one of 18-Volt's microgames.

==Gameplay==
The Wind Waker features a scenario from {{TWW}} where [[Link]] is riding the [[Deku Leaf]] outside the [[Forest Haven]]. Link must ride the [[wind]], and the player must have Link land on a plateau before time runs out. If either time runs out or Link misses the platform, the player loses.

==Levels==
* '''Level 1:''' Link will be near land.
* '''Level 2:''' Link will be far from land.
* '''Level 3:''' Link will be very far from land.

==Profiles==
