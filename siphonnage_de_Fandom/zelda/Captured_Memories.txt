"{{Term/Store|Captured Memories||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Captured Memories}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Kakariko Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} after repairing his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} will ask to see the repairs.<ref>{{Cite|Now then... Let me see.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She recognizes the {{Plural|BotW|Picture|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Album|link}} as those taken by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} 100 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! This is, without a doubt, the {{Color|BotW Red|Camera}} that Princess Zelda used 100 years ago.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She suggests that, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} visits the locations depicted in the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}}, he may be able to recover some of his {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link|display=Memories}}.<ref>{{Cite|... It could be that if you visit the locations within those {{Color|BotW Red|pictures}}, you will be able to restore some of your lost memories.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She asks that he try to find at least one of these places before returning to her {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link|display=House}}, as she wants to give him something.<ref>{{Cite|Come back here once you have tried going to at least one of those locations. There is something I wish to give to you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} begins.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has reclaimed at least one {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}}, {{Term|BotW|Impa}} will be able to tell that he is beginning to remember certain things.<ref>{{Cite|Now that you have seen some of the princess's memories, you must have started to remember certain things...|Impa|BotW}}</ref> Before she can forget to do so, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} his {{Term|BotW|Champion's Tunic|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Here. I will hand this over to you now, before I forget.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> which she kept safe by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} request.<ref>{{Cite|This is something of yours that I have been keeping safe, by request of the princess.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Impa}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Champion's Tunic|display=Tunic}} was made for him when he became a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This clothing was specially made for you when you became a Champion. Please handle it with care.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} travels throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, he can find more of his {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memories}}. After completing "{{Term|BotW|Find the Fairy Fountain|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Pikango|link}} at certain {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}} and towns, where he can give hints to the locations shown in the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}}.

* At {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Ground Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... This picture shows {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} in the distance. They say there used to be a stage where knighting ceremonies were performed, just south of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. It's in ruins now, but...I'm pretty certain that's what's in this picture.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies {{Term|BotW|Lake Kolomo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... That's {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Kolomo}}, isn't it? It's an oddly shaped lake west of here. There's a forest on the west side of it. Yes, I can see some trees here as well. It must be over there.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Columns|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, these are...the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient columns}}. If you cross the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Great Bridge}} over there, there's a large cliff to the south. The ruins are atop that cliff.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm. This picture... Oh! Isn't this the {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}}? It's an oasis just outside Gerudo Town. If you head southwest from here, you can't miss it.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies a location looking down on the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... I can see {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} and the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Hyrule Forest}}, which neighbors it to the north. Looking down on the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Hyrule Forest}} from the southeast, you can see scenery like this.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies a location north of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... Here you can see {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} in the distance. So this must be {{Color|BotW Red|north of the castle}}. The south side of the castle is where Castle Town used to be, but I don't see any buildings here, which means... Yes, indeed, I'd say this is likely on the north side, across the castle moat. It should be {{Color|BotW Red|southeast}} of this stable.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies a location east of {{Term|BotW|Scout's Hill|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Ah, these {{Color|BotW Red|two tiny stone statues}} depicted here...and that faint view of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bridge of Hylia}} in the distance... From this angle, I think perhaps this is the northeast short of {{Color|BotW Blue|Lake Hylia}}. Possibly {{Color|BotW Blue|Scout's Hill}} or perhaps a little further east of there.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies the cloisters surrounding {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Oh, yes, this is {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. It appears to be a view of it from before the Great Calamity. Rather than the castle, it focuses on the surrounding cloisters. What a fascinating composition for a picture...|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... I know I've seen that spring and that {{Color|BotW Red|Goddess Statue}} before... There was a place like this at the ruins in {{Color|BotW Blue|North Akkala Valley}}, to the west of here.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies the {{Term|BotW|Sanidin Park Ruins|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! That horse statue! This is at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sanidin Park Ruins}}. Yes, there are some park ruins on {{Color|BotW Blue|Safula Hill}} to the {{Color|BotW Red|northwest}} of here. You'll find this horse statue there.|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Road - East Gate|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A large gate wuth a snowy mountain beyond it... Oh, I know that place! There is a big mountain east of this village... It's called {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Lanayru}}. At the base of {{Color|BotW Blue|Mount Lanayru}}, on the west side of {{Color|BotW Blue|Naydra Snowfield}}, lies that gate. I'm almost certain!|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

* At {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Pikango}} identifies the forest northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Bottomless Swamp|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That forest... I get the feeling I've seen it before. I can't say for certain, but I believe this may depict the forest just south of here. No... Wait... Maybe it's the forest on the other side of the river, northeast of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Bottomless Swamp}}?|Pikango|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has recovered all of his {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memories}} from the {{Term|BotW|Album}}, he must then return to {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}} to tell {{Term|BotW|Impa}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, well. You've returned. What is it?|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She is initially shocked to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has managed to visit all twelve locations so quickly,<ref>{{Cite|No... All twelve pictures already? You've visited every place?!|Impa|BotW}}</ref> but she then decides it is time to reveal the location of the final {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Then I suppose it's time. I will reveal the location of the final memory.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Impa}} stands, she reveals that {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} instructed her to wait to show the {{Term|BotW|Final Picture|link}}, which is hanging on the wall of her {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|display=House}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here is what happened... Lady Zelda gave me specific instructions. She asked me to wait to show you this. This is the final picture.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if the {{Term|BotW|Picture}} is familiar to him, revealing that the location is only half a day away from {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Does it look familiar? From this village, you should be able to get there in a half day's time.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> She sends him out to recover his final {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now go, child. Seek out what Lady Zelda has shown you.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Final Picture}} depicts a scene in {{Term|BotW|Ash Swamp|link}}, filled with deactivated {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds this location, he will be able to claim his final {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}}: "{{Term|BotW|Zelda's Awakening|link}}". After recalling the {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|display=Memory}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} calling out to him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|... Link...}} Link.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She states that he has recovered all of his memories of herself and the {{Plural|BotW|Champions}} from 100 years ago,<ref>{{Cite|You have recovered all of your memories of us from 100 years ago...|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> and she is now waiting for him inside {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am here...inside Hyrule Castle.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> He is now ready to defeat {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It is now time for you to defeat Ganon.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}} ends.
