"{{Term/Store|The Master Sword||Series, BotW}}" is the eighteenth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Master Sword}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #18}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"The Master Sword" memory begins immediately when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} draws the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}} from its [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|pedestal]] within the [[Korok Forest]].

The memory begins with a white screen, where {{Term|BotW|Zelda|link}} can be heard telling the Master Sword its master will return, and until then it will rest safely here.<ref>{{Cite|Your master will come for you. Until then, you shall rest safely here.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> As the white fades, the Master Sword is seen laying across its pedestal in the forest. Though the [[Slumber of Restoration]] within the [[Shrine of Resurrection]] will rid Link of his memories, Zelda begs the sword to trust that Link will come back.<ref>{{Cite|Although the Slumber of Restoration will most certainly deprive him of his memories, please trust me when I say that I know he will arrive before you yet again.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

The Great Deku Tree interrupts Zelda, asking her what she plans to do next.<ref>{{Cite|If I may be so bold...what is it you are planning to do next, Princess?|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> Looking back down at the Master Sword, Zelda reveals that she heard it speak to her and with determination she shares that her role is unfinished.<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword... I heard it speak to me. It seems that my role is unfinished. There is still something I must do.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> The Great Deku Tree is able to sense great strength in her dedication.<ref>{{Cite|I sense there is great strength in your dedication.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref>

Zelda tries to ask the Great Deku Tree to relay a message to Link when he returns for the Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite|Great Deku Tree, I ask of you, when he returns, can you play relay this message... Tell him I—|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, the Great Deku Tree cuts her off, saying any words she has for him would sound much better coming from her.<ref>{{Cite|Now, then... Words intended for him would sound much better in the tones of your voice, don't you think?|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> Because of this, Zelda smiles at him and agrees.<ref>{{Cite|Yes.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

Taking the Master Sword in her hands, Zelda drives it into its pedestal, leaving it there as she continues on her journey.
