==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Lynel Horns are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by all types of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} upon their defeats. They're too hard for most blades to scratch, but they can be combined with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make White [[Dye]] or exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]].

The [[Barbarian Set]] requires Lynel Horns to be upgraded to its first and second tiers at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Barbarian Set''': 12 (1, 3, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
