The {{Term/Store|Bakery||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Bakery}} is located in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} and is owned by {{Term|TMC|Wheaton|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Pita|link}}. They are known for their pastries that hold prizes inside, as well as the taste of the pastries themselves.<ref>{{Cite|The bread Wheaton makes has got to be the best bread in Hyrule!|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Welcome! You've come to the one and only bakery of Wheaton and Pita! Our delicious brioche, which may or may not have something special inside, is a filling and fulfilling bargain at only 20 Rupees!|Pita|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can purchase these and have a chance to find {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} inside.<ref>{{Cite|There was a Kinstone piece inside your tasty treat! What incredible luck!|N/A|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nothing this time... Too bad!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} has taken over {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}} and sent the soldiers after the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}}, business starts to go poor for the couple.<ref>{{Cite|Fewer and fewer people are coming to buy bread... I can't believe how dangerous it's gotten around here...|Pita|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I like eating leftovers, but I just wish business were going better for them...|Town Minish|TMC}}</ref>
