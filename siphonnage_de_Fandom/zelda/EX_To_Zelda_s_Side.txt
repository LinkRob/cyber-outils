"{{Term/Store|EX To Zelda's Side||Series}}" is a {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Overview==
{{Improve}}
EX To Zelda's Side covers the arrival of Terrako and how it ended up in Hyrule Field from Mount Daphnes. Before the mission starts, a cutscene plays, which covers the journey of the Malice that becomes Harbinger Ganon and a bit of where Terrako arrived. The [[Scenario]] starts with Terrako(labeled the Mysterious Guardian) at the top of the map and unable to Dodge, Run,  use Attacks and Strong Attacks or use Rods or Apples. When you arrive in a area with enemies however, Terrako opens up and you can start attacking. However, like with King Rhoam in {{Term|HWAoC|EX The Princess and the King|link}}, and Master Kohga in the first half of {{Term|HWAoC|EX The Yiga Clan's Retreat|link}}, Terrako has not got the Sheikah Slate or it's associated Runes.
