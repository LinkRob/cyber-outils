{{Term/Store|Sidon vs. the Octoroks||HWAoC}} is a level 49 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sidon vs. the Octoroks|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Sidon vs. the Octoroks}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon|link}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Palm Fruit|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Sanke Carp|link}}, and five {{Plural|HWAoC|Octo Balloon|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}}'s objective is to defeat 80 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within four minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Water Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Forest Octorok|link}}, and {{Plural|HWAoC|Rock Octorok|link}} at first, all scattered along a single path. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon}} reaches this objective, a closed gate at the end of the path opens, a {{Term|HWAoC|White-Maned Lynel}} spawns, and the timer resets to four minutes. When the {{Term|HWAoC|White-Maned Lynel}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
