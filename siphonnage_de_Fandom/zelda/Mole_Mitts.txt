The {{Term/Store|Mole Mitts|Mole Mitts|Series, TMC|plural}} are an item in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 80}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts}} are found in the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}. They allow {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to dig through sand and cloud barriers and to open sealed {{Term|TMC|Cave|link}} entrances. They can also be used to dig in the ground to find items such as {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}, {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}, and sometimes even {{Plural|TMC|Beetle (Enemy)|link}}. The {{Term|TMC|Mole Mitts}} are necessary for defeating the {{Term|TMC|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds}}, {{Term|TMC|Mazaal|link}}.
