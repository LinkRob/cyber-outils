The {{Term/Store|Akkala Falls||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Akkala Falls|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Falls}} are a series of three {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall|link}} located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. They flow from {{Term|BotW|South Lake Akkala|link}}, which is fed by {{Plural|BotW|Waterfall}} from {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Falls}} themselves supply {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
At the top of the central {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Circle|link}} of stones in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}}. When he throws a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} into this {{Term|BotW|Circle}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
