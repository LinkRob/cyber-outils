{{Term/Store|''The Legend of the Hylian Loach: Twilight Fish''||Series, TP, TPHD}} is an {{Term|TPHD|Object|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's the ultimate fish book: The Legend of the Hylian Loach: Twilight Fish!|Hena|TP}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TPHD|The Legend of the Hylian Loach: Twilight Fish}} is a {{Term|TPHD|Book|link}} found in {{Term|TPHD|Hena|link}}'s house in the {{Term|TPHD|Fishing Hole|link}}. It gives a description of the behavior and habitat of the Loach, mentioning that they prefer the shallow waters and love [[frog]]s. Moreover, the book explains that they are known to be antsy and prefer the summertime over wintertime.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, the loach... Like bass, loaches prefer the shallows. They love frogs, but they're antsy, so they'll bolt when humans are around. They're not very active in cold weather either, so the best season to catch 'em in is summer!|Hena|TP}}</ref> According to Hena, the book is about a young Hylian Loach and its quest to save Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite|That book's a real tearjerker, too! It's about how a young Hylian loach saves Hyrule... I've read it so many times, I can almost recite it.|Hena|TP}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* The title of {{Term|TPHD|The Legend of the Hylian Loach: Twilight Fish}} is a parody of {{TP}}'s title.
