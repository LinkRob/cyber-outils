The {{Term/Store|Malo Mart Castle Branch||Series, TPHD}},<ref>{{Cite|Malo Mart Castle Branch|N/A|TPHD}}</ref> also known as the '''Castle Town Malo Mart''',<ref>{{Cite|Welcome! Welcome! Grand opening sale going on now at {{Color|TPHD Red|Castle Town Malo Mart}}!|Chudley|TPHD}}</ref> is a location in {{TP}}.

==Features and Overview==
The Malo Mart Castle Branch is an offshoot of [[Kakariko Village]]'s [[Malo Mart]] that replaces the decadent [[Chudley's Fine Goods and Fancy Trinkets Emporium]] in the southwest portion of [[Castle Town Central Square]] once [[Malo]] accrues enough money to buy it from [[Chudley]]. The shop boasts low prices and is very lively with energetic music, shoppers, and a kitschy decor. Once it opens, many of the citizens of Castle Town start carrying Malo Mart bags. Inside, Link can buy the [[Magic Armor]], which was also sold at Chudley's Fine Goods and Fancy Trinkets Emporium at an unpayable price.

In {{TPHD}}, the [[Malo Mart Stamp]] is added to the store as well. It temporarily replaces the [[Arrow]]s on the shelf, which return once the Stamp is bought.
