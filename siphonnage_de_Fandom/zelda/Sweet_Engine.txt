The {{Term/Store|Sweet Engine||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sweet Engine|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Sweet Engine}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Palace Dish|link}}, two {{Plural|ST|Mystic Jade|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Ancient Gold Piece|link}}. Its design is based on a {{Wp|Roll cake|roll cake}} with a candle-shaped chimney, and its description reads "This train looks good enough to eat! But that would be a bad idea."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Sweet Engine}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Sweet Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts. Using the {{Term|ST|Sweet Engine}} also changes the {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}} to a series of playful-sounding {{Wp|Major chord|major chords}} with added twinkling effects.
