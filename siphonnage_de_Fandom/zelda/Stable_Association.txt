The '''Stable Association''' is a recurring organization in {{TLoZ|Series}}. 

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
The Stable Association was established many years before the events of the [[Great Calamity]] for the purpose of aiding travelers for their long journeys across the land. This includes providing beds and shelter when rest is needed, registering and boarding Horses, and giving directions to those who may be looking for nearby landmarks or settlements. Every Stable found across [[Hyrule#Breath of the Wild|Hyrule]] is owned and maintained by members of the Stable Association. 

==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Stub|TotK}}
The Stable Association, like Stables in general, is largely the same in {{TotK|-}} as it was in {{BotW|-}}. One key addition, however, is the introduction of [[Pony Point]]s. Link earns Pony Points by utilizing various services found at each Stable, such as resting in a bed. Pony Points can be used to redeem various rewards, such as the [[Towing Harness]] or the [[Sleepover Ticket]].
