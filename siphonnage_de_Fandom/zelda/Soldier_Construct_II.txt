'''Soldier Construct II''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{TotK}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Compendium|name=Soldier Construct II|desc= A mid-tier combat construct made by the Zonai with ancient techniques and magic. It faithfully obeys commands and tries to root out any being it deems to be an enemy. The heavy decoration on its head can be swung like an axe in slash attacks.|locs= Hebra Mountains,
Rising Island Chain|loot=  Zonai Charge, Soldier Construct II Horn}}
The '''Soldier Construct II''' is a stronger version of the [[Soldier Construct I]]. A creation of the [[Zonai]], it can be found on [[Sky Islands]]. It will not appear in the [[Great Sky Island]] if [[Link]] has not beaten any [[temple]] yet. It has a stronger weapon than its predecessor and sometimes can have a fused weapon.
