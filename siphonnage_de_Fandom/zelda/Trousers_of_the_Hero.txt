==Location and Uses==
The Trousers of the Hero are a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the 30th Anniversary 8-Bit Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Trousers of the Hero. They can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Trousers of the Hero, alongside the [[Cap of the Hero]] and [[Tunic of the Hero]], comprise the [[Hero Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from the original [[Legend of Zelda]]. When it has been enhaced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
