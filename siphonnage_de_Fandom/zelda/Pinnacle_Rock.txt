{{Term/Store|Pinnacle Rock||Series, MM, MM3D}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 246}}</ref> also known as the '''Sea Snakes' Lair''',<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Sea snakes' lair}} ahead.<br/>Due to the murky water, caution is advised for all who enter.|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> is a location in {{MM}}.

==Features and Overview==
Pinnacle Rock is found within the [[Great Bay Coast]]. It is full of mist and signs of warning found on the sea bottom. Beyond the signs, Pinnacle Rock houses the lair of the [[Deep Python]]s, which consists of a deep hole in the sea filled with predators, all of them sea serpents.<ref>{{Cite|Here in the depths of Pinnacle Rock live many dangerous sea snakes...|Seahorse|MM}}</ref> There are eight Deep Pythons within, and three of them have the [[Zora Egg]]s that [[Mikau]] had been frantically searching for. Two of the serpents have chests containing 20 [[Rupee]]s, one with a lost [[Seahorse]], and the two remaining have nothing.

The easiest way for [[Link]] to get through the murky waters of Pinnacle Rock is by using the help of the friendly Seahorse located inside the [[Fisherman's Hut]]. Once Link has given the [[Fisherman]] a [[Pictograph]] of a [[Gerudo#Majora's Mask|Gerudo Pirate]], he receives the Seahorse in a [[Bottle]]. The aquatic being wishes to go to Pinnacle Rock, so after it is dropped near the entrance, the Seahorse guides Link to the inner part of the serpent-infested sea.<ref>{{Cite|Help me... Please, take me back to the waters near Pinnacle Rock...|Seahorse|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please follow me closely without making any missteps.|Seahorse|MM}}</ref> There, he explains to the young hero that his friend has been trapped by a sea snake, and asks Link for his help.<ref>{{Cite|My friend is trapped here. Link, can you please find a way to rid the area of all the sea snakes and help my friend?|Seahorse|MM}}</ref> While Link makes his way to the bottom of Pinnacle Rock trying to find the three Zora Eggs, it is recommended that he defeats each of the Deep Pythons he encounters along the way, for the only way to save the Seahorse is to defeat each of the serpents within. The two Seahorses will reward Link with a [[Piece of Heart]] once they are reunited.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, Link. This is a symbol of my deepest gratitude.|Seahorse|MM}}</ref>

However, it is possible to get through Pinnacle Rock without the Seahorse's assistance. The way to do so is:
*Stop at the first sign, go to the second one on the right, go one forward, head to the left until another sign comes into sight, one up, one right and then into the lair.

Getting out this same way is quite difficult. It is far easier for Link to simply swim out a random way and magically wind up back at the entrance to Pinnacle Rock.
