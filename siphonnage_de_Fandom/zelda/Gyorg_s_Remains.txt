{{Term/Store|Gyorg's Remains|Gyorg's Remains|Series, MM, MM3D}} are an item in {{MM}}.<ref name="E 126">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|MM3D|Gyorg (Boss)|link}} is the gargantuan masked fish {{Term|MM3D|Boss|link}} of {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|link}}.<ref name="E 126"/> When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} cleanses the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple}} for the first time and acquires {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg's Remains}}, the third {{Term|MM3D|Guardian Deity|link}} is freed and meets {{Term|MM3D|Link}} in the [[Chamber of Giants]],<ref>{{Cite|You seized {{Color|MM3D Red|Gyorg's Remains}}! In doing so, you've freed the innocent spirit that was imprisoned within this dark mask.|N/A|MM3D}}</ref> where {{Term|MM3D|Tatl|link}} implores the {{Plural|MM3D|Guardian Deity|display=Giants}} to intervene on behalf of the world on the condition that she and {{Term|MM3D|Link}} free the last {{Term|MM3D|Guardian Deity}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|Hey, listen. We want you to lend us your power! If you just leave things as they are, something terrible will happen to this world! Surely, you're the only ones who can stop it! That's what Tael was trying to tell us! "Help our friend." I get it. We have to help the last one! Then promise us this... You'll cooperate with us...}}|Tatl|MM3D}}</ref> The {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg's Remains|display=Remains}} allow {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to {{Term|MM3D|Warping|link|display=Warp}} directly to the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} room from the {{Term|MM3D|Great Bay Temple|display=Temple}}'s entrance.<ref>{{Cite|Ye who hold my remains... Return to the appointed place to face me...|N/A|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg's Remains}} are not required for this; simply challenging {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg}} once will activate the {{Term|MM3D|Portal (Object)|link}} so {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can try again without having to go back through the {{Term|MM3D|Dungeon}}.

{{Term|MM3D|Gyorg's Remains}} are worn by one of the {{Plural|MM3D|Moon Child|link}}. They can also be seen hanging on one of the walls of the {{Term|MM3D|Boss}} chamber inside the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}} during the battle against {{Term|MM3D|Majora's Mask (Item)|link}}. The {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg's Remains|display=Remains}} eventually detach from the wall and attack {{Term|MM3D|Link}} by shooting energy at him. They can be destroyed with any weapon.

==Trivia==
*The icon for {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg's Remains}} in {{MM3D|-}} uses {{Term|MM3D|Gyorg}}'s blue appearance from that game; however, the smaller icon seen on the file selection screen retains it's original red appearance from {{MM|-}}.
