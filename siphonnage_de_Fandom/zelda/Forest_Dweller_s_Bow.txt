{{Plural|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} made by {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} for use by {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> As a result, they can commonly be found in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, but they can be found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}} as well. These {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> They are crafted from flexible wood, and the bowstring is made from sturdy vines.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow}} are capable of firing three {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} at once in trade for only dealing a smaller amount of damage per hit. They have a slightly slowed {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} flight speed compared to other {{Plural|BotW|Bows}}, with {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} fired from them travelling roughly 11% slower.

A {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow}} is one of the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} during "{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, with which he must fight his way to the {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But it's not complicated—all you need to do is equip the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok weapon set}} and reach the shrine at the back of the area.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> The other two weapons are the {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Sword|link}}. If any of these {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} are switched out or break, the trial is over and must be restarted.<ref>{{Cite|You have to keep the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}, {{Color|BotW Red|bow}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|shield}} equipped the whole time. If they break or you unequip them, you're out!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow}} will burn if they touch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or if they are equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, they cannot be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
