The {{Term/Store|Milk Bar|Milk Bars|Series, MM, MM3D, PH, ALBW}},<ref>{{Cite|Milk Bar|Map|MM3D}}</ref>{{Name Ref|PH, ALBW}} also known as '''Latte''',{{Exp Game|MM}}<ref>{{Cite|The special Romani-bred cows are the source of Chateau Romani, the most desired of forbidden milk! It is a mystical milk that fills you with magic power... If you seek the dreamy milk of Chateau Romani, please visit Latte, near Clock Town's East Gate.|Cremia|MM3D}}</ref> is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is a drink restaurant that primarily serves [[Milk]].

==Features and Overview==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Milk Bar is referred to both as the Milk Bar and as '''Latte'''.<ref>{{Cite|Romani's Mask Wear it with [C] to show you're a member of the Milk Bar, Latte.|N/A|MM}}</ref> The bar, which is located in [[East Clock Town]], is run by [[Mr. Barten]],<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Barten was happy to get his first delivery in quite a while!|Cremia|MM}}</ref> and is open from 10 PM to dawn.<ref>{{Cite|Latte / The Members-Only Milk Bar / Open from 10 p.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> The bar has a reputation for being a great live venue, so the [[Zora]] band, [[The Indigo-Go's]], had planned to do their [[Carnival of Time]] performance on the Milk Bar stage.<ref>{{Cite|We were scheduled to do a show, but it got canceled. This place has a reputation for being a great live venue, so it's a shame.|Toto|MM}}</ref> However it was cancelled when [[Lulu]], the band's singer, lost her voice.

The Milk Bar is a members-only establishment, and as such, it refuses entry and service to anyone during opening hours who does not have proof of membership in the form of [[Romani's Mask]].<ref>{{Cite|This bar is for members only. Those without proof of membership will be refused entry.|Mr. Barten|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Patron Guidelines for the  Milk Bar, Latte: We offer our customers limited- run milk. Thus, we are a members- only establishment. Those who do not have proof of membership will be refused service.|N/A|MM}}</ref> There are two drinks offered at the Milk Bar: regular Milk, which costs 20 [[Rupee]]s, and the special vintage Milk [[Chateau Romani]], at a price of 200 Rupees per serving.<ref>{{Cite|Our most popular, of course, is Chateau Romani. It's a vintage milk. It comes from Romani Ranch, and the current price is 200 Rupees!|Mr. Barten|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] cannot order anything unless he is wearing Romani's Mask when speaking to the bartender.<ref>{{Cite|Sir...It's the rules. Please wear your Romani's Mask inside the bar.|Mr. Barten|MM}}</ref>

By speaking to The Indigo-Go's manager, [[Toto]], near the stage, Link can participate in a sound check for the band.<ref>{{Cite|I'd like to do a sound check, though. I just want to see what it would have been like... Hey, you! Could you help me with a performance?|Toto|MM}}</ref> Link is required to play the "[[Ballad of the Wind Fish#Majora's Mask|Ballad of the Wind Fish]]" by donning each [[Transformation#Majora's Mask|transformation]] [[mask]] and playing their specific part in the song as orchestrated by the manager. By doing so, Link can receive the [[Troupe Leader's Mask]] from [[Gorman]], who happens to be listening to the performance.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry I booed you... Please, take my mask.|Gorman|MM}}</ref>

Link can also find [[Madame Aroma]] drinking in the bar from 6:00 PM on the Night of the [[Final Day]]. If Link received the [[Special Delivery to Mama]] during the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest, he can give it to her to receive a [[Bottle]] full of Chateau Romani.<ref>{{Cite|This!!! It's from Kafei! Correct? Correct? Wonderful! You really are an expert! Yes, yes, I'm sorry. My thanks. Yes, yes, it is your job, after all. I'm sorry. At this point in time, I can give you only something like this. Actually I wanted to give this to you sooner... Really!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref> In {{MM3D}}, Link is unable to enter unless he has Romani's Mask, preventing him from delivering the letter until he has assisted Cremia in delivering Milk.

During the ending credits, the members of the [[Gorman Troupe]] and The Indigo-Go's can be seen performing at the Milk Bar as part of the performances for the Carnival of Time.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Main|Mercay Tavern}}
In {{PH}}, a small Milk Bar can be located on [[Mercay Island]], though nothing can be purchased from it; however, Link can receive a [[Power Gem]] from the bartender. It is said that [[Linebeck]] usually stops at it.<ref>{{Cite|The captain usually drops anchor at the milk bar, but I haven't seen him in days. Perhaps the bar owner knows where Linebeck has vanished to.|N/A|PH}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, a Milk Bar can be found in both [[Kakariko Village]] and {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}}. The [[Milk Bar Owner]] from [[Hyrule]] sells Milk for 20 Rupees a Bottle, and later for 10 Rupees after saving his regular customer, the [[Bouldering Guy]], with a Bottle of [[Premium Milk]]. A duo of musicians, comprising of a [[Bard]] and a [[Flute Boy#A Link Between Worlds|Flute Boy]], play a song for 10 Rupees. These songs are selected at random each time, and are a guitar and flute remixes of recurring songs from ''The Legend of Zelda'' series. 

In [[Lorule]], the Milk Bar Owner simply desires to be left alone to drink Milk in peace and does not sell Milk to Link.<Ref>{{Cite|I just want to drink a little bit of milk in peace! Why can't everyone just leave me alone and scram?!|Milk Bar Owner|ALBW}}</Ref>
