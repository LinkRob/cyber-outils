'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD — Official Digital Strategy Guide for Wii U''''' is a digital strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TWWHD}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The guide has the same contents as "[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD PRIMA Official Game Guide]]," containing a complete walkthrough of {{TWWHD}}, a listing of {{Term|TWWHD|Locations in The Wind Waker|display=islands|link}}, and a checklist of {{Plural|TWWHD|Item|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Song|link}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Mini-Game|link}}, and collectibles.

It is served using Prima's in-browser digital guide service and, unlike other Prima eGuides, doesn't have sample chapters.<ref>{{Cite Web|author= Prima Games|url=https://eguides.primagames.com/games/legend-zelda-wind-waker-hd/guides/the-legend-of-zelda-the-wind-waker-eguide|site=eguides.primagames.com|title=The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD Official Digital Strategy Guide for Wii U|retrieved=September 25, 2022}}</ref> Prior to August 1, 2019, the guide was available for 20 gold points as a [[My Nintendo Rewards|My Nintendo Reward]].<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo|published= |retrieved= September 25, 2022|url= https://my.nintendo.com/rewards/cd5fab57f2b35484|title= Online Access - The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD Prima Official Game Guide|site= my.nintendo.com|type= }}</ref>
