{{Term/Store|Auto-shovel|Auto-shovels|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Auto-shovel}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Auto-shovel}} are items that allow {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} to dig through walls and mounds of dirt, but with the bonus of automatically digging walls directly adjacent to the heroes as they move.<ref>{{Cite|Allows you to dig through walls by moving adjacent to them.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> Like other {{Plural|CoH|Shovel|link}}, using them to dig costs durability, and using up a {{Term|CoH|Shovel}}'s durability completely will cause it to wear out and disappear from the hero's inventory unless the {{Term|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation|link}} is equipped.

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}.
