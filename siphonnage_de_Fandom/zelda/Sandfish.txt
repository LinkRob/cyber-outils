{{Term/Store|Sandfish|Sandfish|Series, TFH|plural}} are {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 201}}</ref> They are skeletal {{Term|TFH|Fish|link}} that appear to be the dry-land counterpart of the {{Term|TFH|Skullfish|link}}.

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TFH|Sandfish}} appear in the {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} {{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}. They swim freely in {{Term|TFH|Quicksand|link}}, occasionally jumping in an attempt to bite one of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}. They can be attacked with the {{Term|TFH|Sword}} during this leap, however, the {{Term|TFH|Links}} attempting this must take care that he does not remain on the sand for too long, or he will sink. They can also be defeated much more quickly by "beaching" them with the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}} and hitting them. This strategy is recommended, since it is less time-consuming, but the {{Term|TFH|Links}} must be careful not to be standing on the {{Term|TFH|Quicksand}} while using the {{Term|TFH|Water Rod}}. In some areas, the {{Term|TFH|Sandfish}} will quickly respawn once defeated.
