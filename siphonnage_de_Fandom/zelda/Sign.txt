==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Stub|ALttP}}

==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, {{Plural|LADX|Signpost}} indicate the way,<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|LADX Right Arrow}} Tail Cave {{Icon|LADX Down Arrow}} Toronbo Shores|Signpost|LADX}}</ref> and sometimes warn adventurers of potential dangers.<ref>{{Cite|Beware of Sea Urchins! Don't touch them with your bare hands!|Signpost|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|DANGER! Keep out! (Except BowWow)|Signpost|LADX}}</ref> In the {{Term|LADX|Ukuku Prairie|link}}, there is a {{Term|LADX|Signpost Maze|link}} that consists of 14 {{Plural|LADX|Signpost}} that {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} must read in a specific order to open the entrance to {{Term|LADX|Mamu|link}}'s house. Every time a {{Term|LADX|Signpost}} is read, it will direct {{Term|LADX|Link}} to the next one.<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|LADX Down Arrow}} GO THIS WAY|Signpost|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|LADX Up Arrow}} GO THIS WAY|Signpost|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|LADX Right Arrow}} GO THIS WAY|Signpost|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|LADX Left Arrow}} GO THIS WAY|Signpost|LADX}}</ref> Should he fail to read the good {{Term|LADX|Signpost}}, {{Term|LADX|Link}} is met with a message informing him that he must start all over again.<ref>{{Cite|TRY AGAIN FROM THE START|Signpost|LADX}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Plural|OoT3D|Sign}} are predominately used to denote locations, though they can also be placed in specific areas to inform travelers of features or dangers in the immediate area. {{Plural|OoT3D|Sign}} can be destroyed with a {{Term|OoT3D|Sword|link}} or with {{Plural|OoT3D|Bomb|link}}, at which point the remains of the sign will fall to the ground. These remains can be manipulated with the {{Term|OoT3D|Megaton Hammer}}, being forced to bounce around when in the vicinity of its shockwaves. Damaged {{Plural|OoT3D|Sign}} can be repaired with "{{Term|OoT3D|Zelda's Lullaby|link}}".

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Plural|MM3D|Sign}} in {{MM}} function similarly to their {{OoT}} counterparts. When damaged, {{Plural|MM3D|Sign}} can be repaired with the "{{Term|MM3D|Song of Healing|link}}".

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Sign}} serve one of three purposes in {{TMC}}: they give direction to other areas of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Icon|TMC North}} Hyrule Town<br/>{{Icon|TMC West}} Castor Wilds {{Icon|TMC East}} Minish Woods|Sign|TMC}}</ref> display specific location names,<ref>{{Cite|Syrup the Witch's Hut|Sign|TMC}}</ref> or provide "warnings" that double as hints for {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} to explore further.<ref>{{Cite|No bomb throwing|Sign|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Under construction. All digging strictly prohibited!|Sign|TMC}}</ref> There are also a series of {{Plural|TMC|Sign}} in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}} that give {{Term|TMC|Link}} some specific and some vague directions towards the {{Term|TMC|Graveyard|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Up|Sign|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Same as two before|Sign|TMC}}</ref>

There are a total of 31 {{Plural|TMC|Sign}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
Wooden {{Plural|ST|Sign}} appear throughout {{Term|ST|Hyrule|link}} in {{ST}}, and in some locations, {{Plural|ST|Stone Tablet|link}} also double as {{Plural|ST|Sign}}. Each [[Village]] features a large {{Term|ST|Sign}} which displays the {{Plural|ST|Treasure|link}} that {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can win by submitting {{Plural|ST|Prize Postcard|link}} on that day, as well as a blurb about the latest {{Term|ST|Battle Mode|link}} results.

In the {{Term|ST|Overworld|link}}, various {{Plural|ST|Sign}} are seen alongside the {{Plural|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} which indicate to passing {{Plural|ST|Train|link}} that they should perform certain actions, such as changing gears or sounding their {{Term|ST|Whistle|link}}. If {{Term|ST|Link}} has a passenger aboard the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} and does not heed these {{Plural|ST|Sign}}' instructions, the passenger will become displeased.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you missed the slow sign! Pay attention, kid!|Passenger|ST}}</ref>
==={{TotK|-}}===

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Sign|A simple sign made of wooden planks. These stand all over Hyrule.<ref name="EoW Sign">{{Cite|A simple sign made of wooden planks. These stand all over Hyrule.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Signs are wooden posts found throughout [[Hyrule]]. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], allowing her to summon them for one Echo energy. Signs display various messages and can be set on fire.
