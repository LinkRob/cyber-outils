{{Term/Store|Jim||Series, MM, MM3D, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245{{Exp Game|MM}} & 271{{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> 

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Jim is the leader of the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice]]. He wears a red bandanna to distinguish from the other members and uses a blowgun,<ref>{{Cite|... Whaddya want, shrimp? I'm busy practicing with my blowgun!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> which he can be seen shooting at a large blue-colored balloon styled after [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]] in {{Term|MM3D|North Clock Town|link}}. He is a tough acting kid who is strict about the membership of his justice seeking group the Bombers,<ref>{{Cite|Don't mess with me!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> due to previous events with the [[Skull Kid]]. Though he comes off as a troublemaker at first, his actions and intentions are actually kind-hearted.<ref>{{Cite|If you're really a Bomber, don't forget to solve people's problems with pride!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> {{Term|MM3D|Jim}} is also an observant child, whom will sometimes react to [[Link|Link']]<nowiki/>s [[Mask|Masks]] when he knows information about an individual.<ref>{{Cite|What? Oh, a mask? I've seen a real Keaton near the slide!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I used to see him at the Laundry Pool a lot|Jim|MM3D}}</ref>

He seems to run his group with pride, and has little patience for people he would consider weak.<ref>{{Cite|Act your age! Don't be a crybaby!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref> In order to be considered a member of his group, Link must win at a game of [[Hide-and-Seek]] to prove himself worthy.<ref>{{Cite|All right! Line up, guys! If you can find all five of us by tomorrow morning, I'll teach you the code!|Jim|MM}}</ref> If the young [[Hero of Time]] is not in his normal [[Hylian]] form while obtaining the code to their [[Secret Hideout]], {{Term|MM3D|Jim}} will begrudge the fact that he isn't allowed into his gang.<ref>{{Cite|Did you memorize the {{Color|MM3D Red|code}}? If only you were {{Color|MM3D Red|human}}...then I could make you a {{Color|MM3D Red|Bombers}} member... Too bad!|Jim|MM3D}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, Jim is a young boy who attends {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}}. He is the younger brother of {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}},<ref name="Brother"/> and the son of {{Term|TMC|Verona|link}}. He enjoys reading books and pretending that he is a hero on an adventure.<ref>{{Cite|I like reading books. I get to pretend I'm the hero and run around having adventures and stuff!|Jim|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} transforms {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}} into {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Jim}} returns home with {{Term|TMC|Julietta}} and {{Term|TMC|Verona}}, where he stays for the remainder of the game. Unlike {{MM|-}}, he is not wearing a red bandanna.

{{Term|TMC|Jim}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's do it!|Jim|TMC}}</ref>
