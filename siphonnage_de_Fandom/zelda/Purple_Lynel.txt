{{Term/Store|Purple Lynel|Purple Lynels|Series, CoH|plural}} are {{Plural|CoH|Sub-Boss|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Purple Lynel|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Purple Lynel}} are a stronger, blue-and-purple-colored variety of {{Term|CoH|Lynel|link}}, possessing more health and attack power than their weaker counterparts. However, they still have the same attack pattern as they move to the beat of the music on every second beat, always crouching before hopping onto an adjacent tile, and drawing their bow when {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} walk within their line of sight. Once their bows are drawn, they will fire {{Plural|CoH|Arrow|link}} on the following beat, dealing damage to any heroes or enemies that may be in the way. Afterwards, they will reposition themselves until they are able to take aim again. These {{Plural|CoH|Arrow}} can pass through {{Plural|CoH|Torch|link}} and catch fire.

They can be found in caves, dungeons, outside the entrance of the {{Term|CoH|Lost Swamp|link}}, and within the {{Term|CoH|Grassland|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Forest|link}} biomes across {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}.
