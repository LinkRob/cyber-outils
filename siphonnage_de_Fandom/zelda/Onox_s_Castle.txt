{{Term/Store|Onox's Castle||Series, OoS}} is a {{Term|OoS|Dungeon|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 151}}</ref>

==Overview==
===Entrance to {{Term|OoS|Onox's Castle}}===
Onox's Castle is the lair of [[General Onox]], the final or ante-penultimate boss of {{OoS|-}}. [[Link]] must use the Huge Maku Seed here to break the barrier.  The entrance to the castle is guarded by [[Darknut]]s, [[Lynel]]s, and a [[Ball and Chain Soldier]].

===Themes and Navigation===
In the castle, Link fights a variety of enemies, including the mini-boss [[Facade]]. At the end, he fights Onox who has captured [[Din (Oracle)|Din]], the Oracle of Seasons. Although pathways are open, seemingly to allow Link to bypass enemy fights, the [[Warping#Dungeon Warping|enchanted area]] warps Link back to face the foes.
