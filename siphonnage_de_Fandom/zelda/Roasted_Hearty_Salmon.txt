==Location and Uses==
Roasted Hearty Salmons are the result of [[Hearty Salmon]] that have been exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. They have a nice aroma and edible skin.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} sometimes drop them when killed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. Eating one restores four and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to Link's {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}.
