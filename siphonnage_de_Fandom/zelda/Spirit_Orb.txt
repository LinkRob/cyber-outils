{{Term/Store|Spirit Orb|Spirit Orbs|Series, BotW|plural}} are [[Items in Breath of the Wild|items]] in {{BotW}}.<ref name= "Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Spirit Orb}}<br/>A symbol of courage given to those who have overcome the challenges of a shrine.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Spirit Orbs are symbols of courage obtained from the [[Sheikah]] [[Monk]]s after completing their [[Ancient Shrine]]s.<ref name= "Inventory"/> The center of every Spirit Orb contains the [[Goddess Crest]]. [[Link]] can pray to [[Goddess Statue]]s to upgrade either his total [[Heart]]s or [[Stamina Vessel]]s in exchange for four Spirit Orbs.<ref>{{Cite|In exchange for {{Color|BotW Red|four}} {{Color|BotW Blue|Spirit Orbs}}, I will amplify your being.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> There are a total of 120 Spirit Orbs to be obtained in the base game, which allows Link to receive 30 upgrades. An additional four Spirit Orbs can be obtained from Ancient Shrines that appear on the [[Great Plateau]] during the "[[EX The Champions' Ballad]]" [[Main Quest]] as part of [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack, which will allow Link to receive one additional upgrade.
