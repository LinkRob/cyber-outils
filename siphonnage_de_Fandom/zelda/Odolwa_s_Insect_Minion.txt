{{Term/Store|Odolwa's Insect Minion|Odolwa's Insect Minions|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are [[Enemy|enemies]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 196}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Odolwa's [[Insect]] Minions appear during the battle against [[Odolwa]] in [[Woodfall Temple]]. Odolwa can summon them alongside [[Moth]]s to swarm [[Link]]. They are attracted to fire, so Link can toss a [[Bomb]] to distract them. When defeated, they leave a [[Heart]] behind.
