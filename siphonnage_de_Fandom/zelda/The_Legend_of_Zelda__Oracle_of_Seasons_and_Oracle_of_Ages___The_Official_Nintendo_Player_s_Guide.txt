'''''The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons and Oracle of Ages — The Official Nintendo Player's Guide''''' is a guidebook released by [[Nintendo Power]] for {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 128 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of for both {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, lists of collectibles such as {{Plural|LA|Piece of Heart|link}}, a detailed enemy listing, and other tips and secrets.
