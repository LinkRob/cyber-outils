The {{Term/Store|Tough Beetle||Series, SS, SSHD}} is an {{Term|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141 }}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|SSHD|Tough Beetle}} is the second optional (and third and final overall) upgrade to the {{Term|SSHD|Beetle|link}}, enabling it to fly much farther than before.<ref>"''You've upgraded to the Tough Beetle! Your Beetle can now fly farther than ever before!''" ''—'' N/A ''([[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword HD|Skyward Sword HD]])''</ref> It may be purchased from the {{Term|SSHD|Scrap Shop|link}} in {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} for {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|SSHD|Treasure|link}}.

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|SSHD|Bokoblin|link}} are afraid of the {{Term|SSHD|Tough Beetle}} and will run away from it. {{Plural|SSHD|Red Bokoblin (Archer)|link|display= Archer Bokoblins}} will attempt to shoot it at first sight.
*The {{Term|SSHD|Tough Beetle}} cannot be used during the {{Term|SSHD|Horde Battle|link}}.
