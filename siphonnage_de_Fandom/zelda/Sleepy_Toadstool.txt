==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|LA|Sleepy Mushroom|This is but one of many types of mushrooms that grow in the fine tropical climate of Koholint Island. Witches use this mushroom as an ingredient for magic powder. Eating this mushroom, or any other, without the advice of an expert is not recommended.}}
The Sleepy Toadstool is an optional item [[Link]] can gather in the [[Mysterious Woods]]. If he brings it to [[Syrup]], she will make him some [[Magic Powder]]. The Sleepy Toadstool can cause unusual effects when eaten.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 17}}</ref> [[Tarin]], who has a fondness for [[Mushroom]]s, had eaten one while at the Mysterious Woods and was transformed into a raccoon.<ref>{{Cite|The last thing I kin remember was bitin' into a big juicy toadstool... Then, I had the darndest dream... I was a raccoon! Yeah, sounds strange, but it sure was fun! |Tarin|LADX}}</ref> Link uses the Magic Powder to turn him back to normal.
