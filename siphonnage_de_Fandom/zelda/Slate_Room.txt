==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|OoA}}
The {{Term|OoA|Slate Room}} is located within the {{Term|OoA|Ancient Tomb|link}}. Inside is a pedestal with slots for four {{Plural|OoA|Slate|link}}. {{Term|OoA|Link|link}} must collect the {{Plural|OoA|Slate}} and insert them into the openings in the pedestal to proceed within the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon|link}}.
