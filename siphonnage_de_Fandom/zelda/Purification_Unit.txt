== Overview ==

[[File:Purification Unit.jpg|thumb|The Purification Unit in the game’s files]]
The Purification Unit is a large, pyramid like shape attached to the ceiling of the [[Imprisoning Chamber]]. The name “Purification Unit” comes from the game’s files where it is known as “FldObj_OpField_PurificationUnit_A_01”. The unit itself is a grey, inverted pyramid with constellations similar to that of a [[Ancient Shrine|Sheikah Shrine]]. In the opening scene, [[King Rauru|Rauru]]’s energy and  [[Ganondorf]]’s [[Gloom]] are seemingly seeping into the Purification Unit. The Purification Unit seems to be purifying the [[Gloom]] as it says in its name. The Purification Unit seems to have been added after the Imprisoning War by the [[Hylian|Hylians]] as it was not there in either [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]’s [[Memory|memories]] or [[Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment|Age of Imprisonment]]. However, the fact still stands that the Purpose of the Purification Unit is unknown.
