{{Term/Store|Urbosa's Fury +||Series, BotW}} is an ability in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Urbosa's Fury +}}<br/>Champion Urbosa's divine gift. Summons powerful lightning to the surrounding area. Trigger it with a charge attack by holding {{Icon|BotW Y}}. It now requires less time to recharge.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack.

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury +}} is an enhanced form of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury|link}}, which was a divine gift that was given to the late {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} {{Term|BotW|Champion|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury +}} is functionally identical to {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury}} as grants its controller the ability to summon {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} in their immediate area and deals the same base 150 points of damage to enemies, though the time required to recharge it is reduced to four minutes.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Active again in: 04:00}}|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Like {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury}}, the enhanced ability also stuns enemies and can cause them to drop their equipment.

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Fury +}} by overcoming {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}'s part of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Tamer's Trial|link}} in the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. To do so, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must face three Trials and defeat {{Term|BotW|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} in combat in the {{Term|BotW|Illusory Realm|link}}. Upon doing so, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}'s spirit speaks to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, commending him and informing him that he may now use her power more frequently.<ref>{{Cite|I must say, you are an impressive voe. You may now wield Urbosa's Fury far more often. You are the pride of Hyrule, hero. Take care of our princess. I'm leaving that honor in your capable hands.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref>
