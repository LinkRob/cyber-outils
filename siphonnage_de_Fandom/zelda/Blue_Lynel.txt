==Characteristics==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
Blue Lynels are semi-common {{Plural|FSA|Enemy|link}} that can be found in the northeast portion of Hyrule, mostly in the mountain range. Touching one will take two Hearts from {{Term|FSA|Link|link}}. The do not appear horse-like when viewed from the front, but they will start to look like themselves when viewed from the side. Just like {{Term|FSA|Link|link}} at full {{Plural|FSA|Heart|link}}, Blue Lynels attack by throwing a {{Term|FSA|Sword|link}}. It takes 6 hits to defeat it with a wooden sword and they have twice as much collision damage compared to the red ones. When defeated, they can drop one {{Term|FSA|Rupee|link}}, a heart, four {{Plural|FSA|Bomb|link}} or a {{Term|FSA|Clock|link}}.
