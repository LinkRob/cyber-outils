The {{Term/Store|Temple of Time||Series, TP, TPHD}} is the sixth {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 155}}</ref>

==Entrance to the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time (Dungeon)||display= Temple}}==
{{Term|TPHD|Link}} must first travel to the {{Term|TPHD|Sacred Grove|link}} and return the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}} to its pedestal, thus revealing the door to the past. Entering the door brings {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to the past where he can again drive the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword}} into its pedestal, creating stairs leading to the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} has eight floors, which lead upward like a tower, and also contains many large, mosaic windows on its upper floors, allowing the light from the outside to shine through. However, despite seemingly being a peaceful setting, the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time}} is inhabited by a large number of enemies, including {{Plural|TPHD|Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|TPHD|Beamos|link}}. Many of the statue enemies, like the {{Term|TPHD|Beamos}} and {{Plural|TPHD|Blade Trap|link}}, are designed in a more sleek and elegant appearance, similar to the look of the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time}}. Upon reaching the eighth floor of the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} enters combat with the {{Term|TPHD|Middle Boss|link}} of the dungeon, a {{Term|TPHD|Darknut|link}}. After defeating the armored foe, he is rewarded with a sacred rod known as the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod|link}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod}} holds the power to animate and move certain objects, such as statues. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} uses the item to control a large statue, equipped with a {{Term|TPHD|Hammer|link}}, which he uses to solve many of the puzzles throughout the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} and obtain the {{Term|TPHD|Boss Key|link}}. Making his way back to the entrance to the {{Term|TPHD|Temple of Time}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} places the statue in its original position opposite of its counterpart, which reveals a pathway to the {{Term|TPHD|Boss|link}} chamber.<ref>{{Cite|Link... Don't you think there's something odd about that statue? In the grove and in the temple entry, there were always matching ones on each side, but there's only one here... Why don't you use your senses to take a look around? I knew it... There was one more here originally.|Midna|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We need to find the other statue in this temple that matches that one.|Midna|TP}}</ref> 

The {{Term|TPHD|Boss}} of the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} is known as {{Term|TPHD|Armogohma|link}}, and is defeated with the use of the {{Term|TPHD|Dominion Rod|link}} and the large statues surrounding the chamber. Upon defeating it, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} is rewarded with the third shard of the {{Term|TPHD|Mirror of Twilight|link}} and a {{Term|TPHD|Heart Container|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TWWHD|Tower of the Gods|link}} from {{TWW}} has many similarities to this {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}}, such as both being towers, similar music, statues which can be controlled, numerous {{Plural|Series|Beamos|link}} and {{Plural|Series|Armos|link}}, a {{Term|Series|Darknut|link}} {{Term|Series|Mini Boss}}, and a room in each temple with a balance scale that requires the weight of statues to manipulate.
*The music in the {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon}} is a remix of the {{Term|TPHD|Secret Grotto|link}}/{{Term|TPHD|Inside the Deku Tree|link}} music from {{OoT}}.{{Verify|TP}}
*A symbol that bears resemblance with the {{Term|OoT3D|Light Medallion|link}} from {{OoT|-}} can be found inside the dungeon.
*The Dominion Rod is not required to obtain the Big Key for this dungeon if Link uses the two available statues, as well as both Helmasaur helmets.
*It is possible to get fairies from the giant transport bells by hitting them with the [[Ball and Chain]].
