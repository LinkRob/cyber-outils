The {{Term/Store|Akkala Citadel Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Akkala Citadel Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins}} are the remains of a fortress built to protect the {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} {{Term|BotW|Region|link}} from all conceivable threats,<ref>{{Cite|That's the {{Color|BotW Red|Akkala Citadel Ruins}}. Long ago, at the peak of Hyrule's power, they built a fortress to protect Akkala.|Nell|BotW}}</ref> and to protect {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} as a whole from invaders coming from the sea to the east.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 386}}</ref>  However, the events of the second {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}} forced the remains of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}'s leaderless army to retreat to the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel|link}}, where they were assailed by {{Term|BotW|Guardian (Breath of the Wild)|link|display=Guardians}} under {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}'s control.<ref>{{Cite|It was said to be unassailable, but during the Great Calamity, Hyrule Castle fell... The army had no royalty to lead them. With no other choice, they fell back to this citadel to make their last stand.|Nell|BotW}}</ref> Unable to match the sheer power of the ancient {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} technology, the soldiers fell in battle, marking the decisive end to the Kingdom of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, a concentrated assault from the out-of-control Guardians spelled the end for this fortress, too. In a real sense, the kingdom of Hyrule met its doom here.|Nell|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote= The Akkala region, with beautiful colored leaves, is said to be the last land where the Hyrule Kingdom collapsed, in which {{Typo|many|much}} pristine wildness remains.|magazine= Nintendo Magazine|publisher= |volume= |issue= |date= Winter 2021|page= 16}}</ref> A century later, a traveler named {{Term|BotW|Nell|link}} decided to visit the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins}} to pay respects for his relatives who fell during the battle. However, as the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins}} are still inhabited by {{Term|BotW|Guardian (Breath of the Wild)|display=Guardians}}, {{Term|BotW|Nell}} is unable to complete his journey.

Atop a broken balcony on the northern side of the Ruins, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find several {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} as well as a metal door covering a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} under which a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found. On the wall above this point, a second {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found hiding under a {{Term|BotW|Rock}} beneath a pile of dead leaves. A third {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found underneath a {{Term|BotW|Rock}} beside a row of {{Term|BotW|Cannon|link}} south of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Tower|link}}.
