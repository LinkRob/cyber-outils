{{Term/Store|Bombchu Bowling||Series, CoH}} is a location in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to Bombchu Bowling! It's 30 rupees per game.|Gerudo|CoH}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
This {{Term|CoH|Mini-Game|link}} gives the contestant 50 beats - roughly 16 seconds - to hit cardboard cutout targets based on {{Plural|CoH|Enemy}} from Crypt of the Necrodancer. Earning at least ten points rewards them with a {{Term|CoH|Piece of Heart|link}} the first time, and 20 {{Plural|CoH|Rupee|link}} on subsequent attempts. Earning at least 15 points rewards them with a {{Term|CoH|Big Bomb Bag|link}}, and 50 Rupees on subsequent attempts.
