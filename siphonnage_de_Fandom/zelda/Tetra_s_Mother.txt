'''Tetra's Mother''' is a character mentioned in {{TWW}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|TWWHD|Tetra's Mother}} was the captain of a {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra's Pirate Crew|display=pirate crew|link}}, but by the time the events of {{TWW|-}} take place, she has already passed away. Before her departure, she entrusted {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra|link}} with a piece of the {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce|section=Triforce of Wisdom|display=Triforce of Wisdom|link}} and instructed her to guard it with her life.<ref>{{Cite|Your mother passed this down to you and instructed you to treasure it...and to guard it with your life.|Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule|TWW}}</ref> {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra}}, following in her mother's footsteps, became the new leader of the pirate crew, who all respect her despite her being much younger than them.<ref>{{Cite|You're probably wondering why we treat young Miss Tetra with so much respect when she's clearly so much younger than us, aren't you, now?|Nudge|TWW}}</ref>

Inside {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra}}'s cabin of her {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra's Ship|display=Pirate Ship|link}}, a portrait of {{Term|TWWHD|Tetra's Mother}} can be seen, along with a picture of the legendary {{Term|TWWHD|Link|section=Ocarina of Time|display=Hero of Time|link}}.
