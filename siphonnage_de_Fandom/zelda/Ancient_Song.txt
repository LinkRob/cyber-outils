The "{{Term/Store|Ancient Song|Ancient Songs|Series, BotW|plural}}" are a series of {{Plural|BotW|Song|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Red|ancient songs}} of Hyrule?|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
The "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}" are dedicated to the [[Link#The_Hero_10,000_Years_Prior_to_Breath_of_the_Wild|ancient hero]] who defeated {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} 10,000 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Ancient songs—songs that sing the praises of the hero who beat back the Calamity in an age past.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Court Poet|link}} made it his goal to collect these songs to aid {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} after he awoke from the {{Term|BotW|Slumber of Restoration|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|He would seek the songs of the hero who sealed the Calamity away in an age past...so they could be passed on to the knight once he returned.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> His desire was that the songs would help {{Term|BotW|Link}} rescue {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|All so that the princess might be saved... Those were my teacher's last words, passed on now through me.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After his passing, his student {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} continued his work.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} encounters {{Term|BotW|Kass}}, he will often have an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song}}" to share with him. At {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}, he sings a song that is dedicated to the defeat of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} 10,000 years ago.<ref>{{Cite|I happen to know a song about the ancient hero. It was passed down to me by my teacher. Do you care to hear it?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> There are also several "{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Song}}" that are regional, and they provide instructions to revealing hidden {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> These songs are located near the {{Term|BotW|Pagos Woods|link}},<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Pagos Woods}}." It seems that a song has been passed down through the ages in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Pagos Woods}} area of Faron. The song references mythical creatures such as dragons and giant serpents. I wonder if such creatures ever really existed. Or perhaps they still exist today!|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|West Hyrule Plains|link}},<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of the {{Color|BotW Blue|West Hyrule Plains}}." On the south side of the West Hyrule Plains, there is a bridge called {{Color|BotW Blue|Jeddo Bridge}}. Near this bridge, there are a number of circular rocks with holes in their centers. Peculiarly enough, these rocks are apparently mentioned in an ancient song from the area. It sounds rather odd, but I hope to find out more about this ancient rock song.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Rabia Plain|link}},<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Rabia Plain}}." Apparently {{Color|BotW Blue|Rabia Plain}} in the northern part of Hateno is home to an ancient song about beasts. Rabia Plain is northeast of {{Color|BotW Blue|Kakariko Village}}. I'll likely stop by the village during my search for the song.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Tower|link}},<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Tower}}." I've heard word there is an ancient song about a tall structure called {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Tower}}. I don't know where this Gerudo Tower is, but mysterious towers have popped up all around Hyrule recently. This {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Tower}} may be the tower that appeared in the Gerudo region.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Calora Lake|link}},<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Calora Lake}}." Apparently there is an ancient song about the giant boulder in {{Color|BotW Blue|Calora Lake}} in the Faron Region. I hear that area is prone to violent thunderstorms. I'll need to prepare before I go searching for the song.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Horon Lagoon|link}},<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Horon Lagoon}}." I hear there is an ancient song about a place in Lanayru called {{Color|BotW Blue|Horon Lagoon}}. The winds in {{Color|BotW Blue|Horon Lagoon}} are supposed to be very intense. I may need to travel the area on foot.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Washa's Bluff}}." I've heard whispers of an ancient song about the "night of the red moon" traditionally sung in {{Color|BotW Blue|Washa's Bluff}}. No one quite knows why the red moon rises. Or what happens in the span of that night.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} listens to these songs, he will receive a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. An additional regional song exists around {{Term|BotW|Kitano Bay|link}}, which grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} instead.<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Kitano}}." It seems there is an ancient song about an oddly shaped stone pillar in the open water of {{Color|BotW Blue|Kitano Bay}}. It details something that was hidden there before the Great Calamity to be claimed by a lost hero. I'm not sure what's hidden there, but if it's meant for a lost hero, it must be worth a fortune!|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} is clearing the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|EX The Champions' Ballad|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, he can encounter {{Term|BotW|Kass}}, who has come to learn about another "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song}}."<ref>{{Cite|As for me, I came to this spot to pursue an {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient song}} about a hero at this very plateau.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He will offer to play the song for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In any case, would you like to hear my song?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After playing it for {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he reveals that he found it among the {{Term|BotW|Court Poet}}'s old notes.<ref>{{Cite|Curious, indeed. As it were, I perused my teacher's old note...and found that song there.|Kass|BotW}}</ref>
