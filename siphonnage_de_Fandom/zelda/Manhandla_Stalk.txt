'''Manhandla Stalks''' are enemies in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Characteristics==
[[File:HWDE Manhandla Stalk Mini Map Icon.png|left]]
While {{Term|HWDE|Manhandla}} makes an appearance in {{HW}}, {{Plural|HWDE|Manhandla Stalk}} also appear as individual {{Term|HW|Manhandla}} stems that emerge in various locations in {{Plural|HWDE|Scenario|link}}, more commonly in {{Term|HWDE|Adventure Mode|link}}, where they can appear in groups of two but appear more commonly in groups of three. From the location that they sprout, they will barrage either the opposing Bases or opposing {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} from afar. If a {{Term|HWDE|Warrior}} approaches the Stalk, it will fire at them. Large numbers of {{Plural|HWDE|Manhandla Stalk}} can deal high amounts of damage to the Allied Base, so defeating them is a priority. {{Plural|HWDE|Manhandla Stalk}} can be defeated instantly with the {{Term|HWDE|Boomerang}}, and unlike {{Term|HWDE|Manhandla}} itself, the Stalks can be damaged by other {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon|link}}. {{Plural|HWDE|Manhandla Stalk}} do not drop {{Plural|HWDE|Weapon|link}} when defeated.

In "{{Term|HWDE|The Sorceress of the Valley|link}}", {{Term|HWDE|Manhandla}} will retreat when its health is down to half and reemerge as five Stalks that must be defeated. Defeating these stalks will make {{Term|HWDE|Manhandla}} reappear in its entirety.
