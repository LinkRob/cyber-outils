{{Term/Store|Hero of the Rito||HWAoC}} is a level 52 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hero of the Rito|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Hero of the Rito}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Nightshade|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Cool Safflina|link}}.

At the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}'s objective is to save two {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Captain|link}}, with one being in the northernmost area of the map and one in the east. The one in the north is being attacked by a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, while the one in the east is being attacked by two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} and more {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin}}. They will both be found by {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}} to already have less than half of their total health, and are saved by killing their main attackers (the {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin}}). After both of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Captain}} are saved, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel}} spawns in the lower end of the map and advances toward {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lynel}} must be defeated for the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} to be completed. In addition to these {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, many {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos}} are scattered around the center of the map. After the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is cleared, one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Rito Captain}} begin to compliment {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}}, and then is surprised to see that it was {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}} who saved them.<ref>{{Cite|Superb as always, Master Rev... Teba?! Well, that is a surprise!|Rito Captain|HWAoC}}</ref>
