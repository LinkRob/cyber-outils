The '''Spearfish''' are {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} appearing in {{TFoE}} and {{TWoG}}. These fish enemies have the ability to fly, similar to {{Term|TWWHD|Seahat|link}} (which {{Plural|Series|Spearfish}} predate). Their only means of attack is to fly straight into either {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} or {{Term|TWoG|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}}. 

In {{TWoG|-}}, {{Plural|TWoG|Spearfish}} appear only in {{Term|TWoG|Shutoy Lake|link}}. They hop on the water instead of flying.
