{{DISPLAYTITLE: Dungeons in {{TAoL|-}}}}
This is a list of all "palaces" appearing in {{TAoL}}. It is worth noting that though many of these dungeons are located in specific biomes, the dungeons themselves do not seem to conform to any particular theme or element, similar to the dungeons of {{TLoZ}}: the architecture of every dungeon is a simple brick color swap, and aside from the varying enemies from dungeon to dungeon, the overall vibe given off by each dungeon is similar.

As {{TAoL}} is predominantly a side-scrolling game, each dungeon has an entrance above ground, usually adorned by a statue of an [[Iron Knuckle]], that leads to a lift that descends into the dungeon itself.
