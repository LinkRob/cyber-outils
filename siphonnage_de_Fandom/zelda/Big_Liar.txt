{{Name|Japanese|Big Liar}} is the second boss in {{RTBToL}}. He is the god of the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami}} and resides dormant in a cave at the end of the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|link}}. {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} needs an offering in order to enter his lair, where {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi|link}} is being held captive. He is encountered on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 11}}.

==History==
{{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki|link}}, and {{Term|RTBToL|Lion (Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love)|display=Lion|link}} enter {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar}}'s chamber in {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village}} in an attempt to rescue {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}}, after he was kidnapped by {{Term|RTBToL|Segāre|link}}. Once they free {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}}, who was chained to a stalagmite, {{Term|RTBToL|Segāre}} appears, and calls up {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar}}. However, the monster eats {{Term|RTBToL|Segāre}} before turning towards {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}.

==Characteristics==
Rather than a fight, {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar}} asks {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} multiple questions as a test if he and his friends want to leave alive. If {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} gives an incorrect answer, he will be transformed into a goat-like animal, resulting in a game over. {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must first offer {{Term|RTBToL|Milk|link}} to calm him down. {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar}} will then posses {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}} to speak directly with {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}, and the quiz will start. 
Through {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}}, he will ask a total of ten questions but, since they are in a village where everyone speaks in lies, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must give the opposite answer. At the end, {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar}} will give {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}} his golden medal, and return {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}} to her normal state. Finally, {{Term|RTBToL|Liar|link}} will come to rescue {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} and his friends.
