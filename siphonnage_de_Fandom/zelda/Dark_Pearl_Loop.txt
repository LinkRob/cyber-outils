==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Dark Pearl Loop}} are an item of {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}. They can be found in {{Plural|PH|Treasure Chest|link}}, bought in shops, or given to {{Term|PH|Link|link}} as a gift from {{Term|PH|Nyave|link}}. They can be sold to {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} or, for a greater sum, to the {{Term|PH|Ho Ho Tribe|link}} on certain days.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Dark Pearl Loop}} are an item of {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}. They can be found inside {{Plural|ST|Jar|link}} and {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest}}. They can also be bought from shops or won from a {{Term|ST|Prize Postcard|link}}. {{Term|ST|Link}} can sell {{Plural|ST|Dark Pearl Loop}} for 150 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} each, or trade them with {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} in exchange for {{Term|ST|Train Car|link|display=Train Parts}}.
