{{Term/Store|Flower Trail|Flower Trails|Series|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW |publisher= Piggyback |page= 331 |edition= Expanded Edition |year= 2018}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Flower Trail}} are a type of hiding spot for {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} that come in two varieties. The first is a series of flowers that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must follow. The second is a set of flower groupings, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must touch in rising order. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the end of the {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
