The {{Term/Store|Festival Tower||Series, MM}} is an object in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Answer me this... Pick one of the three choices... Once it's completed, how {{Color|MM Red|tall}} will the {{Color|MM Red|festival tower}} at the carnival be?|Keaton|MM}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Festival Tower}} was referred to as the '''Scaffold''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 16}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} The {{Term|Series|Festival Tower}} goes unnamed in {{MM3D}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub}}
The {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower}} is a four-story structure created to celebrate the annual {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time|link}} and located in {{Term|MM3D|South Clock Town|link}}. When {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} arrives in {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower}} stands at only one story high as the {{Plural|MM3D|Carpenter|link}} have not completed its construction. Though the {{Plural|MM3D|Carpenter}} worry about completing it in time,<ref>{{Cite|Heigh-ho, heigh-ho! It looks like another all-nighter. I wonder if that'll get finished.|Bremor|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Heigh-ho, heigh-ho! It's gonna be another all-nighter like I figured. I wonder if it'll get finished on time.|Bremor|MM3D}}</ref> they will continue its construction until the {{Term|MM3D|Night of the Final Day|link}}, at which time they will flee from the {{Term|MM3D|Moon|link}}. A poster outside the {{Term|MM3D|Treasure Chest Shop|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|East Clock Town|link}} announces that the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Town Carnival Executive Committee|link}} is seeking workers to help with the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower}}'s construction.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Urgently Recruiting!}}<br/>Workers wanted to build the festival tower for the carnival.<br/>See Mutoh.|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> Each night, {{Plural|MM3D|Torch|link}} are added to the base of the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower|display=Tower}}, though they are absent the following days.

For the entirety of the {{Term|MM3D|First Day|link}}, the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower}} resembles a simple one-story scaffold with {{Plural|MM3D|Wooden Box|link}} inside. Additionally, one of {{Term|MM3D|Mutoh}}'s apprentices will stand atop the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower|display=Tower}} and stare at the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, apprentice! Don't just stand around lookin' at the sky all day!|Mack|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Huh? The moon?|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's gotten bigger again. Th-this is bad...|N/A|MM3D}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} clears the {{Term|MM3D|Oceanside Spider House|link}} at any point before the {{Term|MM3D|Dawn of the Second Day}}, the apprentice will flee to the {{Term|MM3D|Oceanside Spider House|display=House}}. They cannot be found standing atop the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower}} at any point thereafter until {{Term|MM3D|Link}} returns to the {{Term|MM3D|Dawn of the First Day|link}}.}}

By the time of the {{Term|MM3D|Dawn of the Second Day|link}}, the {{Plural|MM3D|Carpenter}} complete the second story of the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower|display=Tower}}, dressing it in striped, orange cloth. Throughout the entire {{Term|MM3D|Second Day|link}} and the first half of the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day|link}}, {{Term|MM3D|Bremor|link}} will pace on its roof while holding a wooden beam.<ref>{{Cite|Bremor! Don't be standin' 'round lookin' at yer feet all day!|Mack|MM3D}}</ref> The apprentice that stood atop the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower|display=Tower}} can no longer be found there, having fled in search of shelter.

On the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day}}, banners advertising the {{Term|MM3D|Carnival of Time}} are draped over the four sides of the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower|display=Tower}}. Additionally, a {{Term|MM3D|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|MM3D|Purple Rupee|link}}, valued at 50 {{Plural|MM3D|Rupee|link}}, can be found at its top. During the {{Term|MM3D|Night of the Final Day}}, {{Term|MM3D|Mutoh}} will stand in front of the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower|display=Tower}} and curse his apprentices who fled.<ref>{{Cite|Cowards! All of you! Not a one of you stayed! Disgusting excuses for apprentices, the lot of you! Hmph! I'll have a fine carnival without you... If you're gonna fall, then fall already!|Mutoh|MM3D}}</ref> If {{Term|MM3D|Deku Link|link}} speaks to him during this time, he apologizes for it being incomplete and wishes that he could scare away the {{Term|MM3D|Moon}} away.<ref name="Bridge">{{Cite|Hey, Deku kid! You waitin' for us to finish the bridge from the festival tower to the {{Color|MM3D Red|Clock Tower entrance}} up there? Sorry! My apprentices panicked and ran away! This is all we get for a festival tower. It's not quite tall enough, is it? Sheesh! Even if we went to the top of the festival tower, we wouldn't be able to get up to the Clock Tower entrance! I apologize for all this. I wish I could scare that moon away!|Mutoh|MM3D}}</ref>

When completed, the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower}} is intended to stand at four stories high and connect to the {{Term|MM3D|Clock Tower|link}} by a bridge.<ref name="Bridge"/> The {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower}} is one of the possible subjects of the {{Term|MM3D|Keaton's Quiz|link}}, in which the {{Term|MM3D|Keaton|link}} asks how many stories the {{Term|MM3D|Festival Tower|display=Tower}} will be upon its completion.<ref>{{Cite|Once it's completed, how tall will the festival tower at the carnival be?|Keaton|MM3D}}</ref>
