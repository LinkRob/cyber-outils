==Biography==
{{Term|LANS|Suhni}} is one of {{Term|LANS|Mamasha|link}}’s and {{Term|LANS|Papahl|link}}’s five children.<ref name="Papahl" /> With the exception of his baby sibling, {{Term|LANS|Suhni}} and his brothers look almost identical to each other, a fact that sometimes confuses their mother.<ref>{{Cite|With four boys who look alike, even I get confused sometimes...|Mamasha|LANS}}</ref>

If {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} wins the {{Term|LANS|Yoshi Doll|link}} from the {{Term|LANS|Trendy Game|link}} shop {{Term|LANS|Suhni}} will be waiting for him outside, where he will explain that he and his brothers have been trying to win it forever as their mother is desperate to have one.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! You won the {{Color|LANS Pink|Yoshi Doll}}! We've been trying to do that forever! Our mom is really desperate to have one.|Suhni|LANS}}</ref>

If {{Term|LANS|Link}} speaks to {{Term|LANS|Suhni}} he will apparently ask him how long they he and the other residents have been living on the island, a question {{Term|LANS|Suhni}} will not understand, stating that the concept of 'when' makes his head hurt.<ref>{{Cite|Dude! You're asking me when we started to live on this island? What do you mean by "when"? Whoa! The concept just makes my head hurt!|Suhni|LANS}}</ref>
