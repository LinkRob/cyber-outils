The {{Term/Store|River of the Dead||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''River of the Dead'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|River of the Dead}} was referred to as the '''Hylia River''' in the E3 2016 demo for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Hylia River'''|Map|BotW}}<br/>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo |published= June 14, 2016 |retrieved= June 8, 2020 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ze05xdORvYs?t=123 |title= The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild - Exploration Gameplay - Nintendo E3 2016 |site= YouTube |type= Video}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in the final build of the game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]]. Additionally, the name "{{Term|Series|Hylia River|link}}" was given to another location in {{BotW|-}}.}}

==Features and Overview==
The River of the Dead is a body of [[Cold Water]] located on the western portion of the [[Great Plateau]]. The River of the Dead is fed by a waterfall originating from [[Mount Hylia]]. Due to its altitude and the presence of Cold Water, the air around the River of the Dead is afflicted by a [[Freezing Climate]], which will damage [[Link]] if he remains in the vicinity without any [[Cold Resistance]]. The River of the Dead flows west and leads off of the Great Plateau, ending in a waterfall leading to the base of the Plateau. Near this waterfall is a [[Bokoblin]] camp on a platform built around a Tree. Link can sever ropes suspending a ramp to gain access to the camp. Just before the waterfall is a damaged bridge linking both sides of the River. To cross it, Link can complete the gap by using the [[Magnesis]] [[Rune]] to move the metallic door leaning on the stone arch's remains. A [[Raft]] can be found docked near the center of the River of the Dead. Scattered on the riverbank are [[Tree]]s, patches of [[Grass]], unlit [[Campfire]]s and [[Guardian]]s' remains. A [[Woodcutter's Axe]] can be found set in a Tree near the remains of a house, which contains a [[Korok Leaf]] and a [[Cooking Pot]].

A cave can be found behind the waterfall that feeds the River. Housed inside the cave are two [[Treasure Chest]]s which contain a [[Spiked Boko Bow]] and a bundle of five [[Arrow]]s, respectively. Two more Treasure Chests can be found in the waters of the River itself, containing a single [[Amber]] each.

Under the bridge at its western waters, a [[Korok]] can be found,<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]].
