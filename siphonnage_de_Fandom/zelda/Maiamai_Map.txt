The {{Term/Store|Maiamai Map||Series, ALBW}} is an object and interface in {{ALBW}}.<ref name="Mother Maiamai">{{Cite|Thanks! Mother Maiamai finds you quite a kind child. Here—take this. It will help you know if my tykes are nearby. Oh, yes—upon the {{Color|ALBW Blue|Maiamai Map}} you can surely rely!|Mother Maiamai|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[File:Maiamai Map.png|thumb|left|40px|Maiamai Map Icon]]
The {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai Map}} is obtained from {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}} after {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} offers to help her locate all 100 {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai|link|display=Lost Maiamais}}.<ref name="Mother Maiamai"/> It works in both {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}. By tapping the {{Term|ALBW|Maiamai Map}} icon on the touchscreen, the overworld map will be divided into areas—nine in {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule}} and seven in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}—displaying the number of remaining {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai|display=Lost Maiamais}} in each one.<ref>{{Cite|Go on—tap the {{Color|ALBW Blue|Maiamai Map}} icon on your Touch Screen! Those numbers tell you how many of my little Maiamais are in each area. At least until you rescue some, and then it will tell you only how many more you have yet to find! Now please, go and search for all my Maiamais!|Mother Maiamai|ALBW}}</ref>
