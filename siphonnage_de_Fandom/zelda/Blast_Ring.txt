The {{Term/Store|Blast Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Blast Ring<br/>Bomb damage {{Icon|OoS Up Arrow}}|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Blast Ring can be found on the [[Western Coast]] in {{OoS}}. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it increases [[Bomb]] damage.<ref name="Description"/> The Blast Ring can be transferred to {{OoA}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
