{{Term/Store|Jacobi||Series, OoA (Himekawa)}} is a character in the {{OoA|Himekawa}}.{{Name Ref|OoA (Himekawa)}}

==Biography==
Prior the events of the [[Manga]], {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi}} lived with his {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Roperi's Mother|link|display=wife}} and young daughter, {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Roperi|link}}. When {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Queen Ambi|link}} began to construct the {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Black Tower|link}}, citizens of {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Labrynna|link}} where taken as slaves to build it, including {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi}}.<ref name="Family"/> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Roperi}} has worried about him since then.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I...I want to go, too! I'm worried about my dad...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 89}}</ref> 

{{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi}} is first seen during a rebellion against building the tower.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Destroy the tower! It's brought us only suffering!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 114}}</ref> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Sir Raven|link}}, who has arrived to quell the violence, calls out to {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Jacob!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 115}}</ref> Raven says he told him to wait until the signal was given and asks if he is aware of how worried {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Roperi}} is.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Are you crazy! I told you to wait until the signal was given! Do you know how worried Roperi is about you?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 115}}</ref> {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi}} apologizes and says they cannot stop it now.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Sorry, Raven. But we can't stop it now!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 115}}</ref> Meanwhile at her home in the hidden village, {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Roperi}} hopes her father will come home in time to see her {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Mystery Tree|link|display=flower}} bloom.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I hope dad comes home in time to see the flower bloom.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 118}}</ref> 

{{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi}} is last seen at the manga's end, reunited with his family after the defeat of {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Ganon|link}}.
