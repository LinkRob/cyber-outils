{{Term/Store|Bee Larva|Bee Larva|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}},{{Exp Game|TP}}<ref name="E 116">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 116 {{Exp Game|TP, ST, SS}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Bee Larvae|Bee Larvae|ST|plural}},{{Exp Game|ST}}<ref name="E 116"/> and {{Term/Store|Hornet Larvae|Hornet Larvae|SS|plural}},{{Exp Game|SS}}<ref name="E 116"/> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
To obtain {{Term|TPHD|Bee Larva}} in {{TP}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can knock down any of the {{Plural|TPHD|Beehive|link}} located in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Village|link}} and {{Term|TPHD|Hena|link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Fishing Hole|link}}. After the {{Plural|TPHD|Bee|link}} are defeated, the remains of the {{Term|TPHD|Beehive}} can be scooped up into an empty {{Term|TPHD|Bottle|link}}. Each scoop contains ten {{Plural|TPHD|Bee Larva}}. {{Term|TPHD|Bee Larva}} can also be purchased from {{Term|TPHD|Sera's Sundries|link}} for 10 {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}}.

In addition to being used as fishing bait, the {{Plural|TPHD|Bee Larva}} can also be eaten by {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to recover a quarter of a {{Term|TPHD|Heart|link}} per {{Term|TPHD|Bee Larva}}. {{Term|TPHD|Link}} reacts with disgust when consuming {{Term|TPHD|Bee Larva}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, {{Plural|ST|Bee Larva}} are a common of {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}. They can be found inside {{Plural|ST|Jar|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest|link}}, or under piles of leaves, as well as occasionally being dropped when a {{Term|ST|Beehive|link}} is destroyed. They can also be bought from shops or won from a {{Term|ST|Prize Postcard|link}}.

{{Term|ST|Link|link}} can sell {{Plural|ST|Bee Larva}} for 50 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} each, or trade them with {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} in exchange for {{Term|ST|Train Car|link|display=Train Parts}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, {{Plural|SS|Hornet Larvae}} are {{Plural|SS|Treasure}} used to [[Upgrading|Upgrade]] items at the {{Term|SS|Scrap Shop|link}}­.
