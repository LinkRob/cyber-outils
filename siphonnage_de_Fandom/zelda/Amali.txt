{{Term/Store|Amali||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Amali|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Amali}} lives in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}} with her five daughters {{Term|BotW|Kheel|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Notts|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Kotts|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cree|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Genli|link}}, and is the wife of {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}}. Before the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} has been appeased, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} can be found on the platform near the {{Term|BotW|Akh Va'quot Shrine|link}} with {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}. {{Term|BotW|Amali}} muses that {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} insists on worrying her.<ref name="Mother"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks what is the matter, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} explains that her youngest daughter {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} wants to go to {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest|link}} and will not stop talking about it.<ref name="Kheel"/> Her five daughters have been getting needier by the minute ever since the "beast", Vah Medoh, appeared.<ref>{{Cite|I have five daughters, and ever since that {{Color|BotW Blue|beast}} appeared, they have been getting needier by the minute.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> At times like this she wishes {{Term|BotW|Kass}} was around.<ref>{{Cite|At times like this, I wish he was around...|Amali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Amali}} wishes she could be a kid again and think only of herself, but acknowledges that as a parent she has to be bigger than her childen.<ref>{{Cite|I wish I could be a kid again and think only of myself. But that is the thing about being a parent-you have to be bigger than your children.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Amali}} tells {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} to watch her language when she calls {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} a big jerk.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} is a big jerk!|Kheel|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Kheel! Watch your language!|Amali|BotW}}</ref>

At night, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Amali}} by the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}'s {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)|link}}. She asks him who he usually {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=cooks}} for.<ref>{{Cite|Traveler...who do you usually cook for?|Amali|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} says he cooks for himself, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} hopes she did not ask too personal of a question.<ref>{{Cite|I hope that wasn't too personal a question.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> When she cooks, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} wonders about the future of the village and her family.<ref>{{Cite|When I cook dinner for my daughters, I sometimes wonder what the future holds for this village...for my family.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> Her thoughts are dark, but they are lighter once she eats.<ref>{{Cite|My thoughts tend to grow a bit dark then, but they lighten as soon as I satisfy my hunger.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She encourages him to use the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}.<ref>{{Cite|So go ahead and use the pot. A full stomach will do you well.|Amali|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} has been appeased, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} is still on the platform, this time calling for {{Term|BotW|Kheel}}.<ref>{{Cite|Kheel!! Where are you?!|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She turns to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and says that she cannot find {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} anywhere and wonders where she could be.<ref>{{Cite|I can't find Kheel anywhere! Where could she be?|Amali|BotW}}</ref> Amali considers the possibility that she could have gone to {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} to practice her singing.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe she went to {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}} to practice her singing...|Amali|BotW}}</ref> However, it is far away and {{Term|BotW|Amali}} worries whether she could have made it there on her own.<ref>{{Cite|But that's so far away. Could she have made it on her own? Oh, I'm so worried...|Amali|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Find Kheel|link}}."

Once {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} has been found, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} is relieved that she is at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}} after all.<ref>{{Cite|You found Kheel?! So she's at {{Color|BotW Red|Warbler's Nest}} after all. Phew... She really had me worried!|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as thanks for letting her know.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for letting me know. Please, take this for your trouble.|Amali|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|50 rupees. Its violet glow fills your heart with generousity.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Amali}} feels much better now that she knows where {{Term|BotW|Kheel}} is and thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} again.<ref>{{Cite|I feel much better now that I know where she is. Thank you.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> It sets her mind at ease to know where her children are.<ref>{{Cite|It sets me mind at ease to know where my children are. Thank you.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> Her daughters are gathering the ingredients necessary to make {{Term|BotW|Salmon Muenière|link}}, since {{Term|BotW|Genli}} will not sing with her sisters until she has some.<ref>{{Cite|Kheel's sisters are gathering the ingredients necessary to make {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}. Apparently Genli won't sing with the rest of them until she eats some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> She hopes they stay safe as they are all out gathering ingredients.<ref>{{Cite|So they are all out gathering ingredients. I hope they are staying safe.|Amali|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Amali}}'s daughters finish their recital at {{Term|BotW|Warbler's Nest}}, they return to her in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's tell Mom!|Notts|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Last one to mom is an unhatched egg!|Notts|BotW}}</ref> She can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Kitchen (Location)}}, where she hopes her children are not causing {{Term|BotW|Link}} too much grief.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link. I hope my daughters are not causing you too much grief today.|Amali|BotW}}</ref> Upon {{Term|BotW|Kass}}'s return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Amali}} is inside her home and thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for bringing her husband back.<ref name="Husband"/> She says he is singing with his daughters at his usual place and that they could use an audience if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has some time to spare.<ref>{{Cite|He is singing with his daughters at his usual place. I am sure they could use an audience if you have some time to spare.|Amali|BotW}}</ref>
