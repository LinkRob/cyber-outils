King Gustaf is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Gustaf, Royal Spirit}}
{{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} was an ancient king of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} and a member of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} countless ages ago and the earliest known {{Term|Series|Hylian|link}} king in the [[Zelda Timeline#Chronology of the Zelda series|history of Hyrule]], ruling at some point in the early days of {{Term|Series|Hyrule}}'s {{Term|Series|Force Era|link}}. He was very fond of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}, and even ages after his passing, he seeks to keep the peace of the kingdom of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}} secure from beyond the grave.<ref>{{Cite|The spirit of an ancient king of Hyrule who wishes to secure peace in his land from beyond the grave. He was very fond of the people of the Wind Tribe.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

Many ages after his death, he appears to {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} as a ghostly figure after {{Term|TMC|Link}} retrieves the {{Term|TMC|Water Element|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} at {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}. He tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} his identity as an ancient king and instructs the hero to stand before him,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Blue|... ... O young one... Child who seeks to aid the princess of Hyrule... My name is Gustaf... I was king of Hyrule, countless ages ago. Stand before me... Only then will the path open to you.}}|Gustaf|TMC}}</ref> indicating that {{Term|TMC|Link}} must enter the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}} where he rests.<ref name="Mark"/>

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} reaches {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}'s tomb in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt}}, {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} gives him a gold {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} and instructs him to use it at the {{Term|TMC|Source of the Flow|link}} in order to proceed beyond the stone door at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... ... {{Color|TMC Blue|O young one... Child who seeks to rescue my descendant, Zelda... Welcome... My name is}} {{Color|TMC Green|Gustaf}} {{Color|TMC Blue|. I was king of Hyrule countless ages ago...}}|Gustaf|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Blue|I grant to you this}} {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone}}{{Color|TMC Blue|... Seek the}} {{Color|TMC Green|source of the flow}} {{Color|TMC Blue|in Hyrule... Only then will your path open... Save}} {{Color|TMC Green|Zelda}}{{Color|TMC Blue|... All of Hyrule is counting on you...}}|Gustaf|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hmm... Fascinating! An ancient king of Hyrule? How strange... And the {{Color|TMC Green|source of the flow}}? What do you suppose that could mean?|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> This allows {{Term|TMC|Link}} to reach the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} where the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}} resides, allowing him to find the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} that they protect.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TMC|-}} (Himekawa)===
{{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} appears in the {{TMC|Himekawa}} while {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is searching through the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}, and is hostile to the hero in an attempt to preserve {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}'s peace. {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} stops after {{Term|TMC|Link}} drops a {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} from his {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, recognizing it as an artifact from his allies, the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} defeats the {{Term|TMC|Gyorg Pair|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Siroc|link}} tells him that {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} was a benevolent ruler who went to the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} in search of their agricultural knowledge. {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} used that knowledge in conjunction with the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} to spread prosperity throughout {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}; however, {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}'s aide sought the {{Term|TMC|Light Force}} for himself and assassinated {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}}. In his will, {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} granted ownership of the {{Term|TMC|Light Force}} to the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}}, who left the surface world with it out of mourning.

While he was alive, {{Term|TMC|Gustaf}} also {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fused}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} as a sign of friendship.
{{Noncanon|End}}
