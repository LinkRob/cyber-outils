{{Term/Store|Dark Ore Mine||Series, ST}} is a location in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 290}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The mine is situated northeast of the [[Fire Realm]], but [[Link]] only gains access to this [[Train Station|station]] after he receives a [[Force Gem#Spirit Tracks|Force Gem]] from [[Ferrus]] as a sign of gratitude for taking him to the [[Ocean Realm]]. Once this is done, the Force Gem will reveal [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] to the mine.

The Mine is inhabited by three [[Goron]]s who mine the nearby caves for [[Dark Ore]]. As mentioned by the Gorons, since Dark Ore disappears when exposed to sunlight, they must mine the ore while working in complete darkness so as to keep it intact.<ref>{{Cite|The Dark Ore we mine here disappears when exposed to sunlight. So we work all day in the dark like this. I so need to go out and get some sun!|Goron|ST}}</ref>

Here, Link can purchase ten pieces of Dark Ore for 200 [[Rupee]]s, five of which he can take to [[Linebeck III]] in the {{Term|ST|Trading Post|link}} and receive a Force Gem from him to gain access to [[Lost at Sea Station]] in the [[Ocean Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|Dark Ore is 200 Rupees. Want to buy some?|Goron|ST}}</ref>

East of the inner area is a buried treasure hid by [[Kagoron]], who eventually sends Link a letter with a map that pinpoints this treasure.{{When|ST}}
