{{Term/Store|Link: One-Handed Weapons||HWAoC}} is a Level 10 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link: One-Handed Weapons|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Link: One-Handed Weapons}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}, and with only a One-Handed {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}} equipped. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Whole Bird|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hot-Footed Frog|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s objective is to defeat 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within four minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are Hylian {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Soldier|link}}. {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Captain}} spawn throughout the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} as well, and sometimes must be defeated for more {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} to spawn. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
