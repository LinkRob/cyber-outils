The {{Term/Store|Shee Venath Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shee Venath Shrine}}<br/>Twin Memories}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Twin Memories''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Shee Venath Shrine sits on a ledge underneath the peak of the northern {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link|display=Dueling Peak}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shee Venath|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shee Venath. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Shee Venath|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The Shee Venath Shrine is linked to the {{Term|BotW|Shee Vaneer Shrine|link}}, which is located on the southern Dueling Peak. Inside both Shrines are 5×5 grids of circular slots and five {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} in pre-determined slots. In order to reach the Shrine's Monk, Link must lift the Ancient Orbs and place them into the slots corresponding to the Shee Vaneer Shrine.<ref>{{Cite|The shrines atop these peaks share a connection—their memory the answer to the other's question. Commit to memory before you start, lest, a failure, you will depart.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> An elevator and viewing platform enable Link to get an aerial view of the layout of the Shrine.

[[File:BotW Shee Venath Shrine Puzzle Solution.png|thumb|The solution to the puzzle]]

After Link places the Ancient Orbs in the proper slots, the portcullis leading to Shee Venath will rise. Upon speaking to Shee Venath, the Monk will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Shee Venath|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Shee Venath will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Shee Venath|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Serpentine Spear|link}} can be found inside a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that sits on a ledge in the wall opposite the viewing platform. It can be reached by {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=Paragliding}}.
