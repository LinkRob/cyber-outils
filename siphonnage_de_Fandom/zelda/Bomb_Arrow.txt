They are the result of combining [[Bomb]]s with [[Arrow]]s, thereby making for an explosive variety of Arrows. Bomb Arrows are shot like normal Arrows, but when the Arrow reaches its target, the Bomb explodes.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, Bomb Arrows are makeshift items that [[Link]] can shoot by equipping both the Bow and Bombs and using them at the same time. Bomb Arrows can be used as a stronger projectile attack against enemies, but at the cost of a Bow and Arrow.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, to use a Bomb Arrow, the Bow must first be equipped, then the ZR Button ([[Wii U]]), R Button ([[Nintendo GameCube]]), or Z Button ([[Wii]]) must be pressed when any kind of Bomb is selected in the [[Inventory]]. Bomb Arrows consume both a single Bomb and a single Arrow per shot. They can be fired to create distant blasts in high and low places.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...And you can combine bombs and arrows to make bomb arrows for when you need ranged explosives!|Barnes|TP}}</ref> They can be used to blow up distant [[Rock]]s, which is required to progress in the [[Lakebed Temple]]. Bomb Arrows have to be used quickly because if Link holds the Bomb Arrow to the Bow for too long, the Bomb Arrow explodes, inflicting damage upon him.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Bomb Arrows are obstacles fired by [[Bulblin]]s instead, which they do in the [[Overworld]] as Link rides the [[Spirit Train]]. Link can use the [[Cannon]] to destroy Bomb Arrows headed his way.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Bomb Arrow}}
In {{BotW}}, Bomb Arrows appear as a type of Arrow that can be equipped to any [[Bow]]. They also appear in bundles.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Bomb Arrow x5}}<br/>A bundle of five bomb arrows.<br/>The explosive powder packed into the tips of these arrows ignites on impact, dealing big damage to anything caught in the blast.|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Bomb Arrow x10}}<br/>A bundle of 10 bomb arrows.<br/>The explosive powder packed into the tips of these arrows ignites on impact, dealing big damage to anything caught in the blast.|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Bomb Arrow x20}}<br/>A bundle of 20 bomb arrows.<br/>The explosive powder packed into the tips of these arrows ignites on impact, dealing big damage to anything caught in the blast.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> They're dropped by some archer [[Enemies in Breath of the Wild|enemies]] or found inside [[Treasure Chest]]s. [[Bokoblin]]s and [[Lizalfos]] sometimes use Bomb Arrows. In [[Master Mode]], one [[Moblin]] that uses Bomb Arrows can be found near [[Spectacle Rock]] and one [[White-Maned Lynel]] using Bomb Arrows can be found on the [[Great Plateau]]. Before facing [[Divine Beast Vah Medoh]], [[Teba]] will also give Link twenty Bomb Arrows.

The following locations sell Bomb Arrows:
* The [[Arrow Specialty Shop]] in [[Gerudo Town]]
* The [[East Wind]] in [[Hateno Village]]
* [[The Slippery Falcon]] in [[Rito Village]]
* The [[Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)]]
* [[Shaillu]]'s arrow shop in [[Kara Kara Bazaar]]
* [[Jini]] at the [[Mounted Archery Camp]] (when it's not raining)

Bomb Arrows explode on impact and add an additional 50 damage to the attack power of the Bow being used. If an enemy is hit indirectly with the explosion alone, they are dealt 50 damage no matter what Bow is used. They're designed to destroy monsters, and they ignite on impact with an enemy.<ref name="Inventory"/> The explosion of a Bomb Arrow can light enemies and flammable objects on [[Fire]]. Like all Arrows, a shot to the head of an enemy deals double the amount of damage. They do not explode in the [[rain]], as the water causes them to fizzle out, but they still function like normal Arrows. Instead of disappearing after their detonation, Bomb Arrows bounce off objects while it is raining, and can be picked up and fired again. They will not penetrate and stick to any objects like [[Tree]]s. Bomb Arrows cannot be shot within the scorching temperatures of the area around [[Death Mountain]], as the Arrows explode immediately and damage Link after the Bow is drawn.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Bomb arrows as a unique arrow type, have been removed from {{TotK}} in favor of [[Fuse]], which allows players to make their own Bomb arrows, by Fusing a Arrow with a [[Bomb Flower]], or any other explosive.

{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
[[File:Giant Bomb Arrow Linkle HW.jpg|thumb|Linkle firing a giant Bomb Arrow in {{HW|-}}]]
[[Linkle]] uses Bomb Arrows as part of the moveset for her [[Crossbows]] Weapon in {{HW}}. Like their appearance in {{LCT}}, she may charge a shot before firing a Bomb Arrow at enemies. She can also unleash a barrage of Arrows and Bomb Arrows. During her Crossbows Weapon's [[Focus Spirit Attack]], Linkle fires a giant Bomb attached to an Arrow before smashing it to the ground with a fiery kick.

In the {{ALBW|-}} DLC Pack, [[Ravio]] uses Bombs attached to [[Light Arrow]]s as part of the [[Special Attack]] for his [[Rental Hammer]] Weapon.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*During development of {{ALttP}}, Bomb Arrows were planned to appear. They were set to be shot by pressing the A and B Buttons simultaneously when the Bow and Bombs were equipped at the same time. The idea was scrapped after [[Shigeru Miyamoto]] requested that Link should always have the Sword equipped. This idea was later implemented in {{LA|-}}.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= We also thought about having these weapons combine, say for example, having the Bow & Arrows set to the A Button and a Bomb to B Button so that when you use them together (i.e. press both the buttons), Link would shoot an arrow with a bomb attached. In the end we didn’t use this in A Link To The Past, as Shigeru Miyamoto requested that Link always have the sword equipped. We were able to implement this system in the next title, Link’s Awakening, though.|name= Takashi Tezuka|url= http://nintendoeverything.com/nintendo-on-zelda-link-to-the-past-scrapped-idea-working-with-snes-master-sword-importance-more/|title= Nintendo on Zelda: Link to the Past – scrapped idea, working with SNES, Master Sword importance, more}}</ref>
