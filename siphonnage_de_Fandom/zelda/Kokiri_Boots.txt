The {{Term/Store|Kokiri Boots||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
The Kokiri Boots are [[Link]]'s standard footwear in {{OoT}}. He starts the game with them. As an adult, he can equip the [[Iron Boots|Iron]] and [[Hover Boots]] as well, giving him special abilities. He can equip back the Kokiri Boots at any time. They are the only {{Term|OoT3D|Boots|link}} with standard traction and the ability to {{Term|OoT3D|Autojump|link}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
Link wears the same Kokiri Boots and clothes from {{OoT|-}} in {{MM}}; however, they cannot be replaced or unequipped.
