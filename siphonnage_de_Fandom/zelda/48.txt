"{{Term/Store|48||Series, TMC}}" is a {{Term|Series|Song|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Track: 48|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|TMC|48}}" plays twice: first when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} wakes up from being attacked by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} in the stained glass chamber of the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}}, and second when {{Term|TMC|Link}} and {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} flee to the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary}} when {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}} begins crumbling upon the defeat of {{Term|TMC|Vaati Transfigured|link}}. It appears as the forty-eighth track on the {{Term|TMC|Phonograph|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}}.
