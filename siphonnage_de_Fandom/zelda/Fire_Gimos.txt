{{Term/Store|Fire Gimos|Fire Gimos|Series, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 177 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Term|ALBW|Fire Gimos}} can be found in {{Term|ALBW|Turtle Rock|link}} in {{ALBW}}. Although {{Term|ALBW|Fire Gimos}} can be harmed with the {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}}, this reflects damage on {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} if struck directly. They must be defeated either by hitting them with the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Rod|link}} and then striking them once they are frozen, or by using the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}} to temporarily put out their flames.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Term|TFH|Fire Gimos}} appear in the {{Term|TFH|Fortified Zone}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} in {{TFH}}. They can be stunned with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}, leaving them open to attacks from the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}}. They can also be blown off the main platform with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}.
