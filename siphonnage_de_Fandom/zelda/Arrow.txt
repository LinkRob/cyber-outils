Its shaft was carved from the wood of a sturdy tree.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|This Item was also referred to as '''Wooden Arrow''' in the manual for {{TLoZ|-}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Bow, Wooden Arrows and Silver Arrows|game= TLoZ|page= 23}}</ref> However, because {{E}} takes precedence, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} They're sharp projectiles that are shot with a [[Bow]]. Many varieties of arrows exist throughout the ''Zelda'' series since the very first game, {{TLoZ|-}}, including [[Silver Arrow]]s, [[Fire Arrow]]s, [[Ice Arrow]]s, [[Light Arrow]]s, [[Bomb Arrow]]s, [[Shock Arrow]]s, and [[Ancient Arrow]]s.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Bow, and wooden and silver arrows|Link can use them only if he has both the bow and an arrow. For each arrow Link shoots, he loses one ruby. Oh, by the way, there are some enemies that can be disposed of using only the bow and arrow. So be alert!}}
Arrows can be shot using the Bow in {{TLoZ}}.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Link can use them only if he has both the bow and an arrow.|game= TLoZ|page= 24}}</ref> [[Link]] cannot fire arrows until he has purchased them from a [[Merchant]]. After Link has done so, he will be able to shoot Arrows. Unlike all later games, due to the lack of an ammo counter, Arrows consume one [[Rupee]] per shot. Arrows deal 2 damage to enemies that can be inflicted by them. [[Pols Voice]]s are weak to Arrows and will be easily defeated by them in one hit. [[Gohma]]s can only be defeated with Arrows hitting when their eyes are open.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Oh, by the way, there are some enemies that can be disposed of using only the bow and arrow. So be alert!|game= TLoZ|page= 24}}</ref> [[Silver Arrow]]s also appear in the game, which deal 4 damage to enemies and must be used as the final blow on [[Ganon]] to destroy him.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
Arrows appear in {{ALttP}} and can be shot using the Bow. Link can purchase arrows at [[shop]]s, acquire them from defeated enemies, or obtain them from [[Treasure Chest]]s. Link can increase the amount of arrows he can carry by spending Rupees at the [[Pond of Happiness]]. The Silver Arrows also return and must be used to defeat Ganon. These arrows are capable of destroying any enemy with a single shot.

==={{LA|-}}===
Arrows appear in {{LA}} and are shot by using the Bow. If Link equips [[Bomb]]s at the same time as the Bow, he can fire the Bombs with Arrows.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 105}}</ref>

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, arrows are shot using the [[Fairy Bow]], which can only be used while Link is an adult. The [[Hero of Time]] can purchase arrows from shops or [[Business Scrub]]s if he has the Bow.<ref>{{Cite|Knock it off! Leave me alone, and I will sell you Arrows! 30 pieces 70 Rupees they are!|Business Scrub|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can buy arrows at a shop only if you have a bow. Have you got one, mister?|Fado (Ocarina of Time)|OoT}}</ref> Arrows can also be found by cutting grass, defeating enemies, or within small [[Treasure Chest|Treasure Chests]]. Link can carry more arrows by buying larger [[Quiver]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You upgraded your quiver to a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Big Quiver}}! Now you can carry more arrows—{{Color|OoT3D Yellow|40}} in total!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> Link can shoot arrows while riding [[Epona]], and also shoot them through the [[fire]] of lit [[torch]]es to light other torches or melt ice. [[Fire Arrow]]s, [[Ice Arrow]]s, and [[Light Arrow]]s make their debut in {{OoT|-}}, with the latter replacing the Silver Arrows (though they function the opposite). Light Arrows are required to progress through [[Ganon's Castle]] and defeat [[Ganondorf]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, arrows are shot with the [[Hero's Bow]], which is the main item of the [[Woodfall Temple]]. They function the same as in {{OoT|-}}. Link can purchase Arrows from Shops, find them by cutting grass, or acquire them from defeated enemies. Link can shoot arrows while riding on Epona and fire them through the flames of lit torches to light other torches. Fire Arrows, Ice Arrows, and Light Arrows return as central [[Dungeon]] items.

==={{FS|-}}===
In {{FS}}, the [[Links]] do not collect arrows, but can shoot them with the various bows found throughout the game.

==={{TWW|-}}===
Arrows are shot in {{TWW}} with the use of the Hero's Bow. Arrows can be purchased from shops, found by cutting grass, or acquired from defeated enemies. Link can shoot arrows while sailing the [[Great Sea]] on the [[King of Red Lions]]. Light Arrows also appear in {{TWW|-}} as a crucial weapon against Ganondorf.

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, the Links shoot arrows from the various bows found throughout Hyrule.

==={{TMC|-}}===
Arrows can be shot in {{TMC}} by using the Bow. Arrows can be purchased from shops, found by cutting grass, or acquired from defeated enemies. Link also obtain the Light Arrows from [[Gregal]], after saving him from an evil spirit.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, arrows are shot with the Hero's Bow. Link can purchase arrows at shops, find them by cutting grass, or acquire them from defeated enemies. Link can also pick up arrows that land on the ground when enemies miss their mark. Like in {{OoT|-}} and {{MM|-}}, Link can fire arrows while riding Epona. He can expand his capacity of arrows by beating the [[STAR Game]] in [[Hyrule Castle Town]], acquiring larger quivers from [[Purlo]] as rewards. [[Bomb Arrow]]s appear for the first time in {{TP|-}} and can be used to destroy rocks or inflict high amounts of damage to enemies.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, Link can fire arrows with his bow.

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Link can fire arrows by using the bow.

==={{SS|-}}===
Arrows can be shot with a bow in {{SS}}. They can be purchased at the [[Gear Shop]] in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}. Additionally, if enemies fire arrows at Link and they hit the ground, he can pick them up. Link can increase the amount of arrows he can carry by equipping a quiver to his [[Adventure Pouch]].

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, Link does not collect arrows, but can still shoot them with a bow. Like all items in the game, Link uses up his [[Energy Gauge]] to fire arrows.

==={{TFH|-}}===
In {{TFH}}, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} shoot {{Plural|TFH|arrow}} from {{Plural|TFH|bow}} that can be found in various [[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Level]]s. Firing them uses up their {{Plural|TFH|Energy Gauge}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Arrow}}
In {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} are commonly found and are made from sturdy {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be found as bundles.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Arrow x5}}<br/>A bundle of five common arrows.<br/>The shafts of these arrows were carved from the wood of a sturdy tree.|N/A|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Arrow x10}}<br/>A bundle of 10 common arrows.<br/>The shafts of these arrows were carved from the wood of a sturdy tree.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> They can be used to strike {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} from afar with any {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}, except for the {{Term|BotW|Twilight Bow|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light|link}}, which only shoot {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow|link}}. Shooting an {{Term|BotW|Enemy}} in the head with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} will inflict double damage.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Critical Hits}}<br/>Hitting a monster or enemy in the head with an arrow deals double the normal amount of damage.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref> If an {{Term|BotW|Enemy}} is scouting on a lookout post, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can shoot them in the head to deal 10x damage to them.{{Fact}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} can shoot {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} while riding a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} and from atop the {{Term|BotW|Master Cycle Zero|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} will penetrate and stick to certain {{Plural|BotW|Object|link}}, such as wood, and certain {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Shroom|link}}.{{Which}} {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} can also be lit on fire and used to spread {{Term|BotW|Fire}} to flammable {{Plural|BotW|Object}}, such as grass, wood, and {{Plural|BotW|Torch|link}}.

* {{Term|BotW|The Curious Quiver|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}
* {{Term|BotW|Shaillu|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Arrow}} {{Term|BotW|Shop}} in {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}

==={{TotK|-}}===
Arrows return in ''Tears of the Kingdom'', now with the ability to be [[Fuse|fused]] with Materials, and because of this all previous arrow types were removed from the game, such as Bomb Arrows and Fire Arrows.

==Trivia==
*Due to the graphical limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]], in {{TLoZ|-}}, Arrows, alongside [[Princess Zelda]]'s dress, Merchants' clothing, the whiskers of a Pols Voice, the [[Raft]], [[Boomerang]], and even the [[Sword]], as they each use the same palette as [[Hero's Clothes|Link's tunic]], will change color from green to a very pale blue or red if he equips the [[Blue Ring]] or [[Red Ring]], respectively.
*In {{TLoZ|-}}, the spears that the [[Moblin]] enemies throw at Link are actually arrows.
