{{Term/Store|Slime Minion|Slime Minions|Series, PH|plural}} are enemies in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 204}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Slime Minions appear during the fight against [[Bellum]]. They can be spit by him or can drop from the ceiling. Slime Minions usually appear in groups and will attack [[Link]] to distract him. They can be defeated with a single swing of the [[Sword]].
