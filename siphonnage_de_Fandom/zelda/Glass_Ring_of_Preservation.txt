{{Term/Store|Glass Ring of Preservation|Glass Rings of Preservation|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Glass Ring of Preservation}}|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Glass Ring of Preservation}} are items that prevents all other equipment from losing durability, allowing its wearer to use limited-use items indefinitely but only as long as the ring is equipped.<ref>{{Cite|Very fragile but prevents all other equipment from losing durability while equipped.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref> However, like all glass items, the ring will break as soon as its wearer takes damage, forcing the heroes to tread carefully around hazards and enemies in order to keep it intact.

They can either be bought from a {{Term|CoH|Shopkeeper|link}} or found as loot from red {{Plural|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}}.
