The {{Term/Store|Shield||Series, TAoL}} is an ability in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 108}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Shield (Magic)}} was referred to as '''Shield Spell''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Shield Spell}}<br/>When a traditional shield isn't available, the Shield Spell can create a protective barrier in a pinch. Link was known to use the Shield Spell when times were rough.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311084700/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/s.jsp#ShieldSpell|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Overview==
[[File:TAoL Link Shield Sprite.png|left]]
The {{Term|TAoL|Shield}} magic is an ability that increases Link's defensive abilities, reducing the damage he takes by half.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Raises Link's life level temporarily. Reduces damage inflicted by the enemy by half.|game= TAoL|page= 43}}</ref> Link's tunic turns red to show that it is in effect, and it wears off as soon as Link leaves a room and the scene changes. It is one of the first magic Link obtains, and can be found in an [[Old Man]]'s house in [[Rauru Town]].
