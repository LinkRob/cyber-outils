The {{Term/Store|Great Moblin's Keep||Series, OoA}},{{Exp Game|OoA}}<ref name="E">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 251 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 255 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Moblin's Keep||OoS}},{{Exp Game|OoS}}<ref name="E"/> is a recurring mini-dungeon in {{TLoZ|Series}}. Once destroyed, the keep will become known as the '''Ruined Keep'''.<ref>{{Cite|Ruined Keep|Map|OoS}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoA}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, the Moblin's Keep is built on a rise surrounded by [[cannon]]s, west of [[Sunken City]]. When Link enters the vicinity of the keep, the [[Great Moblin]] will appear on the rise, and will start firing [[Bomb]]s at Link. The keep itself can be accessed from the bottom left corner of the area, reached by passing through [[Natzu]]. Once he has infiltrated the keep, Link will engage in battle with the Great Moblin. Once the Great Moblin is defeated, the keep will be destroyed. Afterwards, the Great Moblin and his [[Boarblin|minion]]s will retreat to an abandoned house in Sunken City. At the ruined keep, Link will find a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Piece of Heart]]. In a [[Linked Game]], a friendly Boarblin also appears carrying the [[Tokay Secret]]. Link can blow up the house that the Great Moblin and his minions move into by using a Bomb or [[Ember Seed]]s on the pile of Bombs in the room. The house will be rebuilt after a certain amount of time.

The Moblin's Keep is one of the few areas of [[Holodrum]] which has no [[Tree#Tree Stump|season stumps]] and remains in the same season at all times. Unlike in {{OoA|-}}, destroying the keep is not required to complete the game.

==={{OoA|-}}===
[[File:OoA Moblin Keep.png|right|thumb|Moblin's Keep from {{OoA|-}}|140px]]
In {{OoA}}, the Great Moblin builds his keep on [[Rolling Ridge]] in the present.<ref>{{Cite|Some weird building suddenly appeared here on Rolling Ridge! Some guy called the Great Moblin built it, but it's in our way!|Goron|OoA}}</ref> The area becomes overrun with [[Moblin]]s, harassing the [[Goron]]s of Rolling Ridge.<ref>{{Cite|Rolling Ridge is in an uproar now with the Great Moblin here.|Goron|OoA}}</ref> Because of the keep, the Gorons are unable to grow their special [[Bomb Flower]]s,<ref>{{Cite|Ever since that big pig--the Great Moblin--came, we haven't been able to grow the Gorons' famed Bomb Flowers.|Goron|OoA}}</ref> which the Great Moblin is stealing for his own use. [[Link]] must infiltrate the keep and defeat the Great Moblin in order to destroy the keep. Once destroyed, the Gorons will thank Link for his help and reward him with a Bomb Flower of his own.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know who you are or where you're from, but thank you for getting rid of the Great Moblin. That wretch was keeping us from growing Bomb Flowers. Now we can grow Bomb Flowers without any worries! Take one as a sign of our thanks.|Goron|OoA}}</ref> The Bomb Flower can be used to free the [[Goron Elder]] from under a [[boulder]] in the past.<ref>{{Cite|The Elder is buried alive! But Gorons alone can't dig him out! If we had the legendary Bomb Flower, we could blast the rocks away...|Goron|OoA}}</ref> The Elder will then give Link the [[Crown Key]] to open the [[Crown Dungeon]]. After the keep is destroyed, a passageway to the entrance of the dungeon will be revealed.

The Moblin's Keep is also referred to as the "Great Moblin's Palace."
