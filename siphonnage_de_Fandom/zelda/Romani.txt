{{Term/Store|Romani||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a character in {{MM}} and is the [[Termina|Terminan]] child counterpart of [[Malon]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>

==Biography==
Romani is a young girl who helps her elder look-alike sister, [[Cremia]], take care of [[Romani Ranch]], the premier supplier of Milk for the [[Milk Bar]]. She is kept busy by her dedication to keep the Ranch and the [[Cow]]s safe from the [[Ghost (Majora's Mask)|Ghosts]].<ref>{{Cite|Romani was practicing... For tonight...Tonight... They are coming... They... They come at night every year when the carnival gets closer. They come riding in a bright, shining ball, and many of them come down...to the barn... My older sister won't believe me... But Romani must protect the cows!|Romani|MM}}</ref> However, her older sister does not believe her, forcing Romani to look for help elsewhere. On the [[First Day]], she recruits [[Link]] to help her defend the Cows and teaches him "[[Epona's Song]]" to help him train for the attack.<ref>{{Cite|The operation begins tonight at two. I'll be waiting in the barn. Don't be late!|Romani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You two work perfectly together! I'll teach Romani's horse-calling song to you, Grasshopper. Now you two keep getting along, and go practice some more!|Romani|MM}}</ref> She is proficient in the use of a [[Bow]] like Link, although she does not actually help during the ghost invasion as she must stay inside the barn to guard the Cows. Once the Cows are saved, she gracefully thanks Link with a [[Bottle]] full of Romani [[Milk]] and calls him a "little hero" before heading to bed.<ref>{{Cite|Here's Romani's thanks. When you drink it, put your hand on your hip and take a big gulp like we do here at the ranch! [...] See you later... Little hero.|Romani|MM}}</ref> Romani later reveals that she does not tell Cremia about the recent Ghost attack so as to not worry her.<ref>{{Cite|About last night... It's a secret from my sister. I don't want to worry her!|Romani|MM}}</ref> The [[Second Day|next day]], Romani, impressed by his heroic talent, asks for the young [[Hero of Time]]'s assistance again for next year, but then quickly comes up with the idea of having Link live at the Ranch with them instead, allowing him to borrow Romani's bed and claiming that her sister will be happy too.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Grasshopper, can I ask you to help next yea... .....! I know, Grasshopper! Why don't you just live at the ranch? I'll lend you Romani's bed. My sister will be happy, too! It's a great plan! So...It's decided!|Romani|MM}}</ref>

Romani is shown to have a fun-loving and active personality, constantly jogging outside her house with her pet [[Dog]] during the day. She is not afraid to speak her mind, as demonstrated when she calls Link "cute" and "Grasshopper" the first time she meets him.<ref>{{Cite|Ohh. This is my first time meeting a foreigner... You're cute.|Romani|MM}}</ref> She also likes to refer to herself in third person, and it is implied by her actions and by Cremia that Romani is not aware of the [[Terminan Apocalypse|fate]] that is about to befall [[Termina]]. She wishes to go to the [[Carnival of Time]], an event that she has never attended, but her older sister won't allow her to go until she turns into an adult, much to her dismay.<ref>{{Cite|Romani wishes she could go to the Carnival of Time just once! I've never been! My sister always tells me that I'll be an adult...someday!|Romani|MM}}</ref> However, on the night of the [[Final Day]], Romani is excited at the fact that her older sister is finally going to let her try some of the luxurious [[Chateau Romani]], although she is rather confused since Cremia had told her to wait until she became an adult (though it is strongly implied Cremia wanted to make Romani happy before their doom).<ref>{{Cite|It's "Chateau Romani." It's the first time I get to drink it! Until now, my sister always said "Wait until you're an adult." But why now?|Romani|MM}}</ref> Romani will also receive a [[Romani's Mask|Mask]] handmade from Cremia, for her older sister has finally acknowledged her as an adult.<ref>{{Cite|You've become an adult now, Romani...I see it in you. I'm acknowledging it. [...] Well, yes, I'll make [a mask] for you...|Cremia|MM}}</ref>

Romani is last seen during the game's ending showing off her Bow skills as her older sister cheers her on.

==Trivia==
*When Romani first meets [[Link]], she gives him the nickname "Grasshopper" because he [[Green Clothes|wears green]] and apparently patters when he walks.<ref>{{Cite|Link? That's a nice name, but... Hmm... Well, all right then. How about... Grasshopper? That's the name Romani gives you. See, you're wearing green clothes, and you patter about when you walk, so Grasshopper it is!|Romani|MM}}</ref> This is the same name [[Agitha]] gives to the Hero of Twilight incarnation in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, boy! I thought you were a giant grasshopper!|Agitha|TP}}</ref> It is also likely a reference to [[Malon]] calling Link "Fairy Boy" due to his [[Kokiri]]-like clothing and having [[Navi]] the [[Fairy]] on his side in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''.[[File:Romani Day2.png|thumb|265x265px|Romani's disorientated state]]
*If Link were to fail preventing the [[Ghost (Majora's Mask)|Ghosts]] from reaching the barn where she and the [[Cow|Cows]] hide on the [[First Day]], they will be abducted and taken to the sky, and disappear. Romani, somehow alive and sent back from the ordeal, will return to [[Romani Ranch]] on the [[Second Day]]. However, from this point onward, she had become very disorientated, to the point Romani has trouble speaking or remembering anything. It is unknown what the Ghosts had done to her, though it's likely they have erased her memory. On the [[Final Day]], she can also be seen occasionally thrashing and grasping her head. In addition, [[Cremia]] will be heartbroken not only for the loss of her specially-bred Cows (destroying her business), but also seeing her younger sister in such a horrible, untreatable condition.
**Even in her unhealthy state, during the Final Day, Romani still allows Link to improve on his [[Horseback Archery|horseback archery]] if he so desires. However, she only refers to it as practice and even questions herself as to what he is practicing for.<ref>{{Cite|But... This... What is this practice for? ...Romani...can't remember...|Romani|MM}}</ref>
***Strangely, when Link performs the horseback archery practice and then goes to talk to Romani, she appears completely healthy as if she had never been captured by the Ghosts. This is a developer oversight, as when it is finished, she reverts into her poor state again.
*Romani tells Link that Cremia has a crush on [[Kafei]]. Due to this, several characters believe that the reason for Kafei's disappearance is that he had run off with her older sister, despite his approaching wedding with [[Anju]].
*Like her [[Hyrulean]] counterpart Malon (as well as [[Talon]]), as her model was reused, Romani and her sister Cremia wear brooches of the face of [[Bowser]], the main antagonist of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Super Mario]]'' franchise.
*Before doing the Milk delivery quest on the second night, if Link talks to Romani inside the house first, she will offer that Link stays and lives with them (likely due to his heroic actions), saying she will lend the young Hero of Time her bed. She will only do this if he agrees to be her assistant on the First Day.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Grasshopper, can I ask you to help next yea... ...! I know, Grasshopper! Why don't you just live at the ranch? I'll lend you Romani's bed. My sister will be happy, too! It's a great plan! So it is decided!|Romani|MM}}</ref>
*When Romani attempts to shoot with her [[Bow]], of course lacking any [[Arrow|Arrows]], the "out of ammo" sound effect heard is the one [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Slingshot_Twang.wav used for the Fairy Slingshot] from ''Ocarina of Time'' rather than what is actually from [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Bow_Twang.wav Link's own Hero's Bow].
**Ironically, they are technically the same audio file, but the [[Slingshot]] version is slightly sped-up from the Bow's.
*Although Romani is remarkably similar to her counterpart Malon as a child, she can be distinguished by her mouth being slightly open and is even more energetic in behavior.
*In the demo version of {{MM|-}}, Cremia refers to [[Madame Aroma]] as her and Romani's aunt.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Ah... You. Did my aunt put you to work?|author= Liliana|published= February 26, 2011|retrieved= April 12, 2015|url= https://tcrf.net/Proto:The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Majora%27s_Mask/Preview_Demo/Text_differences|title= Proto:The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask/Preview Demo/Text differences|site= The Cutting Room Floor|type=}}</ref> In the final release, she simply calls her a middle-aged woman.<ref>{{Cite|Ah... You. Did some middle-aged woman put you to work?|Cremia|MM}}</ref>
*Romani and Cremia were supposed to appear in the {{MM|Himekawa}} but were cut, as Himekawa could not fit them in the story. This omission is acknowledged in a side note at the end of the manga adaptation.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=We couldn't work them into the story no matter how we tried, so we're putting them here... Cremia and Romani.|book= MM (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= }}</ref>
*Being Malon's counterpart, her singing when she reteaches Link "[[Epona's Song]]" is identical, and is also the same as [[Lulu]]'s.

==Nomenclature==
Romani is most likely named after the [[wikipedia:Romani people|Romani people]] of Europe.
