The phrase '''Twilit''' is a prefix given to any being or object that has been engulfed in [[Twilight]] by [[Zant]], King of the Twilight. Twilit beings will always be hostile towards [[Link]] and his affiliates. The most notable Twilit creatures are each of the bosses that appear during the course of {{TP}}.

In modern English, the word "Twilit" means "lighted by or as if by twilight," referring to the light of the setting sun. In this context, calling a being consumed by Twilight as "Twilit" is more than appropriate.
