The {{Term/Store|Kokiri Shop||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a location in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT3D|publisher= Prima|page= 20|edition= eGuide}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Shop}} is a shop located in the [[Kokiri Forest]] that sells items from the surrounding forest it is located in.<ref>{{Cite|This shop… It sells things you can get in the forest for free! Tee hee!|Kokiri|OoT3D}}</ref> Like all dwellings and houses in Kokiri Forest, the store is a hollowed tree. However, it has a red line painted around the top of it; along with an awning on which a [[Kokiri]] girl is perched, and a flat roof. At the beginning of his journey in {{OoT}}, [[Link]] must visit this shop in order to purchase the [[Deku Shield]] for 40 Rupees, which, along with the [[Kokiri Sword]], he needs to pass Mido and meet with the [[Great Deku Tree]].
