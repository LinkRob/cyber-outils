==Biography==
Mila is a wealthy girl from [[Windfall Island]] who was kidnapped by the [[Helmaroc King]] and taken to the [[Forsaken Fortress]].<ref>{{Cite|...It's difficult to tell. Yes. Difficult. You see, my one and only daughter, Mila, was taken away from me to a frightening place called the Forsaken Fortress... I have asked many to rescue her, but it seems to be a difficult voyage to that cursed place...|Mila's Father|TWW}}</ref> During [[Link]]'s second trip to the Forsaken Fortress in his attempt to rescue his sister [[Aryll]] again, [[Tetra]] and her [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|crew of pirates]] take Mila and {{Term|TWWHD|Maggie|link}} back to Windfall Island. When Mila returns, she learns that [[Mila's Father|her father]] spent their riches paying Tetra in hopes of bringing her back to the island safely.<ref>{{Cite|But one day, a monstrous bird came and took me away to a terrible place called the Forsaken Fortress, where I was locked up and held captive. Oh, it was awful! My father spent every last Rupee in his coffers in an attempt to get me rescued.|Mila|TWW}}</ref> Being saddened and angered by her new life of poverty (the reverse happening for Maggie and [[Maggie's Father|her own father]]), Mila assumes work at [[Zunari's Shop]] to try and make money for the family.<ref>{{Cite|Now, every day, from morning until night, I'm busy working for the open-air shop. So, as you can see, at least I'm trying to settle into my poor lifestyle.|Mila|TWW}}</ref><ref name="Figurine"/> However, she becomes impatient and sneaks out at night to steal the money out of [[Zunari]]'s private safe.<ref>{{Cite|I-I'm not a thief! I swear! I haven't stolen anything! Well, yet.|Mila|TWW}}</ref> Link must follow her and intervene as she attempts to open Zunari's safe, catching her in the act and forcing her to give up her life of crime, earning an [[Empty Bottle]], which she claims it has washed up the shore.<ref>{{Cite|It's true... I know I'm quibbling over nothing... But being so poor weakens a person's very soul... But...it's time I quit making silly excuses for myself!|Mila|TWW}}</ref> Afterwards, instead of thieving, she tells the Hero of Winds that she spends her evenings working at an unspecified island, indicating that she is no longer present on Windfall Island at night.<ref>{{Cite|I'm done with all that sneaking around in the dark. In fact, I head off to a faraway island to work in the evenings now, so I won't be seeing you at night anymore!|Mila|TWW}}</ref>

Prior to her life of a squalor, Mila had a very bossy and overbearing disposition,<ref name="Figurine"/> but later become more humble and polite after accepting her new lifestyle.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to you, sir! This way to Windfall's outdoor shop, chock-full of fun and happy products!|Mila|TWW}}</ref>

== Trivia ==

* Like [[Maggie (The Wind Waker)|Maggie]] and unlike [[Maggie's Father|their]] [[Mila's Father|fathers]], Mila does not seem to recognize [[Link]] when he returns to [[Windfall Island]] to meet her.
* Mila's "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Mila_ShhShhh.wav ''Shh! Shhh!'']", due to the way she sounded, has some fans like to make fun of it by believing she said "''Shushy!''".
* The sidequest of Link having to sneak behind Mila to stop her stealing from [[Zunari]]'s safe is a popular meme among fans of [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] community. This is because whenever she says ".''..Who's that?''", the young hero [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Link_Meow.wav meows like a cat] to prevent himself from being spotted, with many claiming to be one of the cutest or most humorous actions by the main protagonist.
** Ironically, in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]'', there is a [[Cat]] [[Link (Cat)|sharing his name]] who is owned by [[Sera]], coincidentally like the [[Link (Pig)|pet Pig]] owned by [[Abe]] and [[Rose]] as well as a [[Link (Goron)|certain Goron]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''.

==Nomenclature==
In the Slavic languages, the word for "dear" or "precious" is ''mila'', which reflects Mila's character. In contrast to her European Spanish and German names meaning "fortunate", or Maggie's Spanish and German names mean "sorrowful".
