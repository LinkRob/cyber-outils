{{Term/Store|Carmine Pearl|Carmine Pearls|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 119"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Carmine Pearl}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Sky Realm|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Floating Garden|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Carmine Pearl}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Floating Garden}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Carmine Pearl}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Deception Castle|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Carmine Pearl}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Dragon Citadel|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Carmine Pearl}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Carmine Pearl}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make {{Term|TFH|Linebeck's Uniform|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Robowear|link}}.<ref name="E 119"/>
