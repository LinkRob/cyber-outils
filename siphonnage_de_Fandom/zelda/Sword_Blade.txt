The {{Term/Store|Sword Blade||Series, PH}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|PH|Sword Blade}} is an unfinished and unusable form of the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword|link}}, the only weapon capable of defeating {{Term|PH|Bellum|link}} and the {{Plural|PH|Phantom|link}}. It is forged by {{Term|PH|Zauz|link}} after {{Term|PH|Link|link}} brings him the three {{Plural|PH|Pure Metal|link}}; {{Term|PH|Aquanine|link}}, {{Term|PH|Azurine|link}}, and {{Term|PH|Crimsonine|link}}. As the forging of the {{Term|PH|Sword Blade}} is a lengthy process, {{Term|PH|Link}} must leave {{Term|PH|Zauz}} to the task for a time.<ref>{{Cite|I've never seen metal like this. I'll bet I can forge a mighty sword with them. But it will take some time.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> It is at this point the young hero receives a {{Term|PH|Letter|link}} from {{Term|PH|Jolene|link}} challenging him to a final battle.

After defeating {{Term|PH|Jolene}} and returning to {{Term|PH|Zauz|link}}'s house, the {{Term|PH|Sword Blade}} is completed and presented to {{Term|PH|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aha! Yes... Yes, that's it. It's complete! It's...perfect! Please take it, Link!|Zauz|PH}}</ref> {{Term|PH|Ciela|link}} is confused by the blade's lack of a handle,<ref>{{Cite|Huh? This sword doesn't have a handle to hold it with? It's still not complete?|Ciela|PH}}</ref> until {{Term|PH|Zauz}} suggests bringing it to {{Term|PH|Oshus|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Take this blade to the Ocean King. He will add the Sand of Hours.|Zauz|PH}}</ref> who fuses the {{Term|PH|Sword Blade}} with the {{Term|PH|Phantom Hourglass (Item)|link}} itself to create the {{Term|PH|Phantom Sword}}.
