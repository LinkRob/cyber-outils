The '''Mole''' is a character in {{AST}}.{{Name Ref|AST}}

==Biography==
The {{Term|AST|Mole}} appears once per week at a random location of the {{Term|AST|Overworld|link}} at specific times. If found, it tells the {{Term|AST|Hero of Light|link}} about the location of a {{Term|AST|Treasure|link}}. It then proceeds to burrow down, moving to the {{Term|AST|Treasure}}'s location. In said area, the {{Term|AST|Mole}} appears next to a hole leading to an underground cave. Inisde, six {{Plural|AST|Treasure Chest|link}} containing several {{Plural|AST|Rupee|link}} can be found. The {{Term|AST|Hookshot|link}} is needed to reach all chests in weeks 2 and 3. Each time the {{Term|AST|Mole}} is found, 10,000 points are added to the score of the current week.
