The '''Wand of Power''' is an item appearing in the episode "[[The Missing Link]]" of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. Used by [[Ganon]], the wand shoots a powerful spell that captures its victim within the [[Evil Jar]].<ref name=wand>{{Cite Episode|quote=Eliminate that heroic pest! I want Zelda. My new Wand of Power will allow me to zap her into my Evil Jar!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=12}}</ref> When Ganon arrives at [[North Castle|North Castle's]] courtyard, he fires the spell at [[Princess Zelda]], but she deflects with the [[Triforce of Wisdom|Triforce of Wisdom's]] magic,<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Blast! She deflected my power zap!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=12}}</ref> which causes the spell to bounce around and eventually captures Link instead. Ganon decides that capturing Link is satisfactory and leaves back to the [[Underworld]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Well, not quite what I planned, but it will do!|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=12}}</ref> Although the Wand of Power was able to send Link's body to the Evil Jar, it did not capture his spirit since the spell was weakened as it was bouncing around.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=The zap, of course! All that bouncing around it did must have  weakened it before it hit you! It got your body but not your spirit!|name=Princess Zelda|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=12}}</ref> This leaves Link to wander around the castle and the underworld as a ghost while Zelda works to recover his body.
