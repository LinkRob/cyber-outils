==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill: Volume 4}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill|link}} series, found at {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}. In this volume, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} details a rumor about {{Plural|BotW|Shooting Star|link}}, and how there may be a {{Term|BotW|Star Fragment|link|display=treasure}} waiting where one lands.<ref>{{Cite|And I've heard that there is a treasure waiting where these stars have landed.|Rumor Mill: Volume 4|BotW}}</ref> This rumor receives four out of five stars.<ref>{{Cite|Traysi's Recommendation: ★★★★☆|Rumor Mill: Volume 4|BotW}}</ref>
