The {{Term/Store|Keive Tala Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Keive Tala Shrine}}<br/>Big or Small}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Big or Small''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an [[Ancient Shrine]] in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Keive Tala Shrine is found in the {{Term|BotW|East Barrens|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} as part of the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}". To access the Shrine, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Molduking|link}} in the East Barrens. Upon its defeat, the Shrine will rise from beneath the ground on the side of a nearby mesa. Upon entering the Shrine, Link is offered the trial by the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Keive Tala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Keive Tala. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Keive Tala|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
To complete the Shrine, Link must connect four unconnected parts of a circuit on the wall to the left. Link can use the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} and three metallic crates within the Shrine to connect the circuits. He may also use various conductive items from his inventory. Once the circuit is complete, a gate to the end of the Shrine will open.

Link can complete the circuit even further past the gate by lining up four platforms with a gyroscope puzzle. Doing so successfully opens the gate to an additional small room with a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Radiant Shield|link}}.

At the end of the Shrine, Link meets the Monk Keive Tala, who commends his resourcefulness and rewards him with one of {{Term|BotW|Naboris's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero.}}|Keive Tala|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Naboris's Emblem.}}|Keive Tala|BotW}}</ref> The Monk then tells Link to collect all three of Naboris's Emblems to reveal a new challenge.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Keive Tala|BotW}}</ref> If this is the first Shrine Link visits of the three in the "EX Champion Urbosa's Song" Quest, the Monk tells Link that two Shrines remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Keive Tala|BotW}}</ref> If it is the second Shrine he visits, the Monk will tell him that one remains.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Keive Tala|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third and final Shrine he visits, the Monk will tell him to go to the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Naboris...}}|Keive Tala|BotW}}</ref>
