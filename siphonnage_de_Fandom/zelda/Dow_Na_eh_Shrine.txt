The {{Term/Store|Dow Na'eh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Dow Na'eh Shrine}}<br/>Three Boxes}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Three Boxes''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Dow Na'eh Shrine}} is located behind the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade|link}} in {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. A pathway on the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Promenade|display=Promenade}} leads directly into a cave which houses the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by the its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dow Na'eh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Dow Na'eh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Dow Na'eh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Inside {{Term|BotW|Dow Na'eh Shrine}} rests a gigantic weighted {{Term|BotW|Floor Switch|link}} surrounded by a pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} by the giant {{Term|BotW|Switch}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} that contains an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}} can be found positioned on an elevating pillar. A third {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} can be found on a wooden shelf on the wall, suspended by two ropes. These ropes can be shot with  {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} to make the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} fall into the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)}} below, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} to retrieve it and the {{Term|BotW|Zora Sword|link}} inside.

In order for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}, he must position all three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} onto the gigantic Switch and compress it with their combined weights. This will release a portcullis to a small room housing a large, metal box. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use this metal box in conjunction with a nearby {{Term|BotW|Launcher|link}}, which periodically raises itself, to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}} bearing {{Term|BotW|Dow Na'eh}}. The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Dow Na'eh|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Dow Na'eh will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Dow Na'eh|BotW}}</ref>
