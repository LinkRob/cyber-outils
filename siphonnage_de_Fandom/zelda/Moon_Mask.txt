The {{Term/Store|Moon Mask|Moon Masks|Series, MM, MM3D}} is an object in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 37}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Moon Mask is a ceremonial mask created by [[Anju]] for use as her ceremonial wedding mask. It is kept by Anju on a mannequin with her wedding dress inside her room at the [[Stock Pot Inn]]. Combining the Moon Mask with the [[Sun Mask]] results in the [[Couple's Mask]].
