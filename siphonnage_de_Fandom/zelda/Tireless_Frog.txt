==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Tireless Frog}}
Tireless Frogs can be found near ponds in the [[Lanayru Great Spring]] and [[Hyrule Ridge]] during the rain.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be combined with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make an [[Enduring Elixir]], which will temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} when consumed.<ref name="Inventory"/> At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], Tireless Frogs can be used to make Crimson [[Dye]].
