The {{Term/Store|Three Noble Attributes of Courage|Three Noble Attributes of Courage|Series, TFH}} are the distinguishing features said to be possessed by the heroes of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When three heroes heroically gather, the Triforce gate will open. So it is written. Right here. You just read it. Hytopia's fated heroes shall embody the Three Noble Attributes of Courage. Pointy ears that reach skyward, ever alert for the call of friends in need... Attractive yet ethereal sideburns that inspire allies to greatness... And perfectly parted hair that smells of lavender and friendship. The bond between the three is such that they can communicate thoughts and emotions without a word. They will totem. They will triumph. They will overcome all hardships. That is why they are the three of legend... The {{Color|TFH Blue|Tri Force Heroes}}.|Book|TFH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
A Hytopian prophecy foretells of the {{Plural|TFH|Tri Force Heroes|link}}, a trio of adventurers who will bring perpetual peace and style to the kingdom.<ref>{{Cite|But I believe. I must! I believe the legendary Tri Force Heroes will appear once more and save our kingdom! Speaking of Tri Force Heroes, there's more to the legend than most people know. The story goes... "When the Tri Force Heroes overcome all challenges, Hytopia will be blessed with everlasting peace and style." Naturally I believe YOU to be the hero of legend, destined to lead our fair kingdom to eternal greatness!|King Tuft|TFH}}</ref> They are identifiable as possessing the {{Term|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage}}: their pointed ears, sideburns, and side-parted hair. These are all features of the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}, thus making them suitable candidates to be the legendary heroes.

{{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's Apprentice|link}} notices {{Term|TFH|Link|link}}'s ears and hair, and sends him to meet {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}} inside {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}} to become a hero.<ref>{{Cite|YOU!! You must be...the hero! The one fated to save our dear Princess Styla!|Madame Couture's Apprentice|TFH}}</ref> {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} also recognizes that {{Term|TFH|Link}} possesses the {{Plural|TFH|Three Noble Attributes of Courage}}, and recruits him into the {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade|link}} to free {{Term|TFH|Hytopia}} and {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}} from the curse of {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's this? That face... Those ears... You there! I must speak with you! Pointy ears... Check! Sideburns... Check! And finally... hair parted to the side... CHECK! IT'S HIM!!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The combination of these three features is not exclusive to the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}. Several characters throughout the series also possess these attributes, such as {{Term|TWWHD|Princess Zelda|link}} from {{TWW}}.
