The {{Term/Store|Ancient Battle||Series, SS}} is one of the earliest battles in [[Hyrule]]'s history, prior to its founding as a kingdom,<ref name="before">{{Cite Book|quote=Before the Kingdom of Hyrule and the royal family exist, the goddess Hylia is reborn as a mortal to the headmaster of the Knight Academy. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=16 }}</ref> and was originally depicted in the introductory cutscene of {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 71}}</ref> The conflict erupted when the Demon King known as [[Demise]] broke out of the depths of the earth with his armies and attempted to steal the [[Triforce|ultimate power]] from the goddess [[Hylia]]. The aftermath of the battle resulted in the creation of both [[Skyloft]] and the [[Sealed Grounds]].

==Overview==
The [[Golden Goddesses]] created the [[Triforce]]. They limited use of the relic's powers to mortals, supposedly as a means to inspire hope amongst them.<ref>{{Cite|The gods created the Triforce, yet they specifically designed it so that their own kind could never use its power. Somehow, I think that may have been their way of giving hope to all the mortal beings of the land.|Zelda|SS}}</ref> They ordered the goddess [[Hylia]] to safeguard it and the world.<ref>{{Cite|Such was the might of the ultimate power that the old ones placed it in the care of the goddess.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=After the three goddesses departed, the Triforce and the world was entrusted to the goddess Hylia. |book=E |publisher= |page=16 }}</ref> For an unspecified period, Hylia ruled over the [[Surface]], and the various races that existed at that time. This era of peace ended when a fissure opened in the earth, from which an army of demons was led by the Demon King [[Demise]].<ref>{{Cite|One dark, fateful day, the earth cracked wide and malevolent forces rushed forth from the fissure. They mounted a brutal assault upon the surface people, driving the land into deep despair... They burnt forests to ash, choked the land's sweet springs, and murdered without hesitation.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Evil originated in ancient times, emerging from great fissures in the earth. Demons crawled up, led by the powerful Demise, carrying with them an evil in direct opposition to everything the goddesses had created. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=99 }}</ref> Demise sought to obtain the Triforce to conquer the world and to this end he ordered his armies to destroy all in their path.<ref>{{Cite|In his thirst to make the world his own, Demise readied a massive army of monsters for war. He sought to take the Triforce for himself by force.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Hylia's great responsibility was soon tested as evil beings emerged from the ground, seeking the Triforce. Called demons [...], they served an evil power so great it was known only as "Demise". |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=12 }}</ref>

The goddess Hylia, fearing for her people, the [[Hylian]]s, gathered them on an outcropping of rock, placed the [[Triforce]] inside the [[Sealed Temple|Temple of Hylia]] and raised it and the Hylians into the [[Sky]] to keep them out of Demise's reach.<ref>{{Cite|To prevent this great power from falling into the hands of the evil swarming the lands... the goddess gathered the surviving humans on an outcropping of earth. She sent it skyward, beyond the reach of the demonic hordes. Beyond the clouds.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Without the power to fight, Hylia's people could only cry out for help to the goddess. She answered, cutting out a portion of their land and, along with the Triforce, moving her people to safety high above.|book=E|publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]]|page=12}}</ref> This floating island along with others became the new home of the Hylians, known as [[Skyloft]].<ref>{{Cite|The goddess feared for her people. She used her power to send both them and the Triforce into the sky on a slice of earth she cut away from the land. This floating rock became the new home of our people. In time, it came to be known as Skyloft.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The islands in the sky where the surviving humans dwell became known as Skyloft. |book= HH |publisher= |page= 72}}</ref> She also created a cloud barrier to separate the realm of the Sky from the Surface and thus prevent Demise's armies from pursuing her people.<ref>{{Cite|Until now, a cloud barrier created by the goddess has separated the world you know from the one below.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The land and sky were then separated by a sea of clouds. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=12 }}</ref>

Hylia and the remaining races on the Surface fought and sealed Demise's evil forces away.<ref>{{Cite|With the humans safe, the goddess joined forces with the land dwellers and fought the evil forces, sealing them away.|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Five races other than the Hylians existed during the age of the goddess Hylia. Those five races stood with the goddess Hylia to fight the demon hordes and the Demon King Demise and remained on the surface after he was sealed away. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=54 }}</ref> Hylia imprisoned and sealed away Demise in what became known as the [[Sealed Grounds]].<ref>{{Cite|After a long and fierce battle, the goddess, Hylia, succeeded in sealing away Demise.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He was imprisoned in the Sealed Grounds after a fierce battle with the goddess Hylia. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=174 }}</ref> The Seal that now bound him, stripped him of his true physical form, forcing into the form of [[The Imprisoned|an abomination]] capable of great destruction, should he ever escape.<ref>{{Cite|Stripped of his true physical form by the seal that binds him, he takes the shape of an abomination. But even in his hideous state, he's more than capable of devouring this land if we allow him to do what he desires.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Sealed by the goddess Hylia, Demise revives twice in Skyward Sword. The first time, he returns as a twisted, incomplete monster known as the Imprisoned. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=293 }}</ref>

To prevent Demise from escaping the seal, Hylia created the sword spirit [[Fi]], bound to the [[Goddess Sword]], which was to be passed on to her chosen hero, [[Link]],<ref>{{Cite|In order to put an end to the demon king, Hylia devised two separate plans and set them both into motion. First, she created Fi. She made the spirit that resides in your sword to serve a single purpose: to assist her chosen hero on his mission.|Zelda|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote={{color|E Green|Fi}} A sword spirit created by the goddess Hylia. She resides inside the Goddess Sword, and calls Link "Master" after he pulls the blade from its pedestal. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=293 }}</ref>. She entrusted three spirit dragons, [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]], [[Eldin (Dragon)|Eldin]], and [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]], as well as the sky spirit [[Levias]], to pass on the four parts of the [[Song of the Hero]] to the appointed hero, so he could open the Temple of Hylia and recover the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=In the time of {{SS|-}}, three spirit dragons watched over the Surface: the water dragon Faron, guardian of forests and sources of water; the fire dragon Eldin, overseer of volcanic lands; and the thunder dragon Lanayru, who had domain over the deserts. In addition to being entrusted with the land by the goddess Hylia, alongside the sky spirit Levias,  their role was to pass on the four parts of the Song of the Hero to the hero. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=22 }}</ref> Hylia constructed two [[Gate of Time|Gates of Time]] out of [[Timeshift Stone|Timeshift Stones]], mined in the [[Lanayru Mining Facility]], for Zelda and Link to travel between the ancient past and their present.<ref>"''This is the Temple of Time. Within it is the sacred Gate of Time made by the goddess, bzzt.''" — [[Ancient Robot]] ({{SS}})</ref> Hylia ordered [[Impa]] to travel to the future via a Gate of Time to protect [[Zelda]] and help her remember her former life, by praying at the [[Earth Spring]] and the [[Skyview Spring]].<ref>"''Zelda, at the command of the goddess, I passed through the Gate of Time. I did so to protect you and aid the fight to prevent the world's destruction.''" — '''Impa''' ({{SS}})</ref><ref>"''The spirit maiden who descended from the clouds must travel to two sacred places to purify her body. You stand in one of these places: Skyview Spring. The other is known as the Earth Spring.''" — [[Fi]] ({{SS}})</ref> She created the [[Goddess Wall|Goddess Walls]] to aid her chosen hero.<ref>"''There is no mistaking it! This symbol must mark one of the goddess walls left for the hero by the goddess!''" — [[Gorko]] ({{SS}})</ref> The Goddess Hylia passed away, gave up her divinity so she could transfer her soul to a mortal — Zelda, the daughter of the [[Knight Academy|Knight Academy's]] Headmaster [[Gaepora]]<ref name="before" /><ref>{{Cite Book|quote={{color|E Green|Zelda}} Daughter of Gaepora, the headmaster at the Knight Academy. She later faces her destiny to be awakened as the goddess Hylia.|book=E|publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]]|page=293}}</ref>— and, in future, use the power of the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite|Her second plan...was to abandon her divine form and transfer her soul to the body of a mortal. ...She made this sacrifice, as you have likely guessed, so that the supreme power created by the old gods could one day be used. For while the supreme power of the Triforce was created by gods, all of its power can never be wielded by one. Knowing this power was her last and only hope, the goddess gave up her divine powers and her immortal form.|Zelda|SS}}</ref>
