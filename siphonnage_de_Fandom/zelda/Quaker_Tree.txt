The '''Quaker Tree''' is a tree mentioned in {{TSP}}. It only appears in the book after {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|link}} decides to fight the {{Term|TSP|Darknut|link}} that ambushes {{Term|TSP|Princess Zelda|link}}, {{Term|TSP|Link|link}} and him with the sword. When the fight is over, Charles's sword accidentally slices into the roots of a Quaker Tree after the sword sinks into the ground.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The sword, caught underneath his massive body, sinks into the ground. R-R-R-UMMMMBLE! The earth around Link's feet shakes. Oh, no! Charles's sword must have sliced into the roots of a quaker tree!|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 12}}</ref> Due to the tree's earthquake, the group falls into an {{Term|TSP|Underground Tunnel|link}}, which is a maze filled with deadly {{Term|TSP|Stalfos|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book| quote=The next thing knows, he, Zelda and Chalres have dropped twenty feet into an underground tunnel.|book= TSP|publisher= |page= 12}}</ref>
