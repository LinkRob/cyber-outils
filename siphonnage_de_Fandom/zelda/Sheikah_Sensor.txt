The {{Term/Store|Sheikah Sensor||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sheikah Sensor}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor}} is used to locate {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It can be unlocked either by activating any {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Tower|link}} after leaving the Great Plateau or in the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} by repairing the Sheikah Slate. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is near an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor}} will beep louder.

Once upgraded, the Sheikah Sensor becomes the Sheikah Sensor +, and can be used to detect objects other than shrines. This functionality can be used to search for any object you've got an entry for in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium|link}}. Once this has been unlocked, {{Term|BotW|Symin|link}}. will ask to see the Sheikah Sensor, and the "{{Term|BotW|Sunshroom Sensing|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will become available.
