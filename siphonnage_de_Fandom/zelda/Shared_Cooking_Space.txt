The {{Term/Store|Shared Cooking Space||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 265}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Shared Cooking Space}} is located in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, across the stream that flows from {{Term|BotW|Zelkoa Pond|link}}. It boasts two {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can use for {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}. There are also three {{Plural|BotW|Jar|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Barrel|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} that can be broken for supplies.

When it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=rains}} during the day, {{Term|BotW|Aster|link}} will take refuge in the {{Term|BotW|Shared Cooking Space}}, where she will pretend to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cook}}.
