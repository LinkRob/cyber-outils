The '''Demon King's Bow''' {{Exp Game|TotK}} is a [[Weapons in Tears of the Kingdom|weapon]] introduced in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']]. It is most notably the bow of [[Ganon]]dorf himself, which is also wielded by his darkest servants such as [[Phantom Ganon]].

== Characteristics ==

The Demon King's Bow deals higher damage the greater the maximum number of [[heart]]s [[Link]] has. When Link has a significant number of hearts, Demon King's Bows deal the highest damage out of all single-shot bows in ''Tears of the Kingdom'', though they are still weaker per volley than higher-tier multi-shot bows, such as [[Savage Lynel Bow]]s. Demon King's Bows are the only Gloom-based weapon type that Link can use without having the negative affects of the Gloom.

Unlike most weapons, Demon King's Bows cannot be upgraded by [[Rock Octorok]]s. 

== Location ==

The Demon King's Bow has a high chance of being dropped by Phantom Ganon after defeat.
