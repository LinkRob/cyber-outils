The {{Term/Store|Angular Isles||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} (G5) are islands in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 260}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==

The Angular Isles are two islands created out of blocks. [[Link]] can ascend the larger island by moving blocks and climbing on them. At the top is a [[Treasure Chest|chest]] containing a [[Piece of Heart]]. To reach the second island, Link can either latch onto its tree with the [[Hookshot]] or glide there with the [[Deku Leaf]]. This island has a [[Secret Cave]] with a [[Puzzle#Block Puzzle|block puzzle]] that can only be completed with the [[Mirror Shield]]. The prize for completing the puzzle is a [[Treasure Chest|chest]] containing 200 [[Rupee]]s.

Although the isles are uninhabited at the time of {{TWW|-}}, they are the birthplace of the [[Great Fairy|Great Fairies]]<ref>{{Cite|Great Fairy — Birthplace: Angular Isles|Nintendo Gallery|TWW}}</ref> and [[Salvage Corp|Salvage Corp.]]<ref>{{Cite|Salvage Corp. — Birthplace: Angular Isles|Nintendo Gallery|TWW}}</ref> It is also home to many {{Plural|TWWHD|Gyorg|link}}, meaning that travel through the waters surrounding the island can be difficult for the unprepared traveler. Lastly, a Piece of Heart can be unveiled from the sea after finding and opening a [[Treasure Chart]] found in [[Forbidden Woods]].

==Trivia==
*The Angular Isles have the smallest total area of any island in the [[Great Sea]], excluding the three archipelagos ([[Five-Star Isles]], [[Seven-Star Isles]] and [[Star Belt Archipelago]]).

==Nomenclature==
While the Japanese name for the island uses {{Romanize|カク|kaku}}, which means "angle" or "square", a fitting description of its blocky appearance, the word {{Romanize|カクカク (赫赫, 赫々)|kakukaku}} means "brilliant", "bright", or "glorious". This place, being the birthplace of the Great Fairies, makes this secondary meaning have notable significance.
