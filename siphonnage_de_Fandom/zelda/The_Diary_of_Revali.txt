{{Term/Store|''The Diary of Revali''||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|The Diary of Revali|N/A|BotW}}</ref> also known as '''''The Diary of Revali, the Rito Legend''''',<ref>{{Cite|Read {{Color|BotW Blue|The Diary of Revali, the Rito Legend}}?|The Diary of Revali|BotW}}</ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|The Diary of Revali}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} that was used by {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} to document his personal thoughts. The {{Term|BotW|Book}} can only accessed with the purchase of {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack. When downloaded, the {{Term|BotW|Book}} will be in {{Term|BotW|Teba's Home|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, on the desk to the left. The {{Term|BotW|The Diary of Revali|display=''Diary''}} contents tell of {{Term|BotW|Revali}}'s desire to prove his worth to the people of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, the development of his special technique that would become known as {{Term|BotW|Revali's Gale|link}}, his inauguration as {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} and pilot of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}, and his personal dislike for {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} being selected as the one who would challenge {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} face-to-face.

Asking {{Term|BotW|Kaneli|link}} about {{Term|BotW|Revali}} will cause him to share that the {{Plural|BotW|Rito|link}} recently found {{Term|BotW|The Diary of Revali}}, and they are grateful to have one of his personal items.<ref>{{Cite|We recently found {{Color|BotW Blue|The Diary of Revali}}. We were all excited to locate such a personal item of the Champion's.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> He left {{Term|BotW|Teba|link}} in charge of the {{Term|BotW|Book}} to ensure not just anyone reads it.<ref>{{Cite|However, not just anyone has the right to read something of that nature. That's why I left {{Color|BotW Blue|Teba}} in charge of it, since he so admires {{Color|BotW Blue|Master Revali}}|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref>. When asked about the {{Term|BotW|The Diary of Revali|display=''Diary}}, {{Term|BotW|Kaneli}} states that it was found at the {{Term|BotW|Flight Range|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Revali}} adored,<ref>{{Cite|Hoo hoo... The historic {{Color|BotW Blue|Diary of Revali}} was recently found at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Flight Range}} our Champion so adored.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Teba}} took it to his {{Term|BotW|Teba's Home|display=Home}} for safekeeping.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Teba}} took the diary to {{Color|BotW Blue|his home}} for safekeeping.|Kaneli|BotW}}</ref>

After defeating {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon|link}} again, {{Term|BotW|Revali}} may comment on this diary.<ref>{{Cite|You may happen upon something called "The Diary of Revali, the Rito Legend."|Revali|BotW}}</ref> He alleges that he is not, in fact, the author, and that not a single word should be believed.<ref>{{Cite|Don't believe a word of it! I'm not the author. Misleading title, I know. Got it?|Revali|BotW}}</ref>
