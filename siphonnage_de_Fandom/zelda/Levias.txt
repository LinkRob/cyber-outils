{{Term/Store|Levias||Series, SS}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 189}}</ref> also known as '''The Great Spirit Levias''',<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 295}}</ref> is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}. He is a wise, [[whale]]-like Sky Spirit who has served as the protector of the skies for ages and lives in the [[Thunderhead]].<ref>{{Cite|Surely you're familiar with Levias, the great sky spirit who has guarded our realm for ages? Levias has served as the protector of our skies for a long time, and his knowledge of our world is encyclopedic, to put it mildly.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If I'm correct, Levias is the one inside the thunderhead. But...that's not the Levias that we all know. That's...a monster!|Owlan|SS}}</ref> Levias has a particular fondness for [[Pumpkin Soup]], which [[Pumm]] always offered on a sky isle that had a perpetual rainbow above it, before the Thunderhead engulfed it.<ref name= "Soup">{{Cite|Every year I offer him an entire basin full of my pumpkin soup! But lately a huge thunderhead has been brewing there, and I haven't yet made this year's offering. I tell you, I feel horrible about it!|Pumm|SS}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{SS|-}}===
Before the goddess [[Hylia]] transferred her soul into [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|the body of a mortal]], she appointed Levias as the guardian of the skies.<ref>{{Cite|I am the great spirit they call Levias. Before she passed from this world so long ago, the goddess Hylia appointed me as warden of the skies.|Levias|SS}}</ref> She also left the Sky Spirit a hint as to the location of the [[Triforce]], which was one of the four parts of the "[[Song of the Hero]]," a melody that would unlock the final trial for the [[Link#Skyward Sword|chosen hero]].<ref name= "Song">{{Cite|Oh, but the goddess did entrust me with a hint as to the Triforce's location. The clue is a song, meant to be played on the harp you hold. It is known as the Song of the Hero. The song is the key to revealing the secret location of the Triforce. The goddess split this song into four parts. She entrusted one part to me and the other three to the dragons of the land.|Levias|SS}}</ref> However, at one point, Levias was corrupted by evil forces and became plagued with [[Bilocyte]], who possessed the Sky Spirit and caused him to attack anyone who came near.<ref name="malice"/>

After learning of Levias from the Headmaster [[Gaepora]], [[Link]] speaks to [[Owlan]] and learns that although Levias can be found in the Thunderhead, he has become possessed by Bilocyte and will not listen to reason. Owlan tells the hero to talk to Pumm, who makes annual Pumpkin Soup offerings to Levias.<ref name= "Soup"/> Link then places a basin of Pumpkin Soup on an island inside the Thunderhead to lure the Sky Spirit out of the sea of clouds and, with the aid of his [[Loftwing]] and the newly-learned technique [[Spiral Charge]], removes the parasites. Upon the removal of the last visible parasite, a Bilocyte occupying Levias' blowhole emerges. Link removes this parasite as well, and upon hearing that Link was searching for the Triforce, Levias informs him that the four parts of the "Song of the Hero" are needed to find it. Levias then instructs Link to track down the three surface dragons, [[Faron (Dragon)|Faron]], [[Lanayru (Dragon)|Lanayru]], and [[Eldin (Dragon)|Eldin]], to learn their respective parts of the melody.<ref name= "Song"/> Once he has done this, Levias imparts the final part of the "Song of the Hero" to Link.<ref>{{Cite|When they've taught you what they know, come to me and I will complete the song with my own voice!|Levias|SS}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==

==={{HW|-}}===
Levias appears in the "[[Land in the Sky]]" [[Scenario]] in {{HW}}. After Link finds [[Fi]], he must protect her in order to deliver two batches of Pumpkin Soup to Levias, who in exchange pushes a mass of earth in order for the Warriors to reach new areas on the map. After meeting the [[Great Fairy]], she gives another batch of Pumpkin Soup to Levias, who attacks [[Volga]] in order to weaken him.

==Nomenclature==
Levias' name is derived from {{wp|Leviathan}}, a sea creature mentioned in Biblical texts. Fittingly, the modern Hebrew form of the word means "whale".
