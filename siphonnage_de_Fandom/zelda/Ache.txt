{{Term/Store|Ache|Aches|Series, TAoL|plural}} are bat enemies appearing in {{TAoL}}.<ref name="E 162"/>

==Characteristics==
{{Manual|TAoL|Ache|A bat that lives in dark places. Usually, it hangs upside down from trees or in caves.}}
In {{TAoL|-}}, Aches usually appear in a blue variety found commonly in caves, and sometimes in [[Tree]]s.<ref name="E 162"/> Their method of attack is to hang from the branches or ceiling, and swoop down in a pointed arc towards {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}}, returning to their roosting point. This makes them hard to hit and avoid, but they are rather weak and more of an annoyance than a real threat. 

Purple Aches are slightly different from their more common counterparts. Called "Eyes of [[Ganon]]" by townsfolk,<ref>{{Cite|EYES OF GANON ARE EVERYWHERE BE CAREFUL|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref> Aches can disguise themselves in the villages of [[Hyrule]] as ordinary [[Hylian]]s. Talking to any moving NPCs in [[Saria Town]] or [[Darunia Town]] have a chance to transform into an Ache in disguise (including speaking the same NPC multiple times), and they often reveal themselves and attack if Link talks to them, striking unexpectedly. Not only this, but purple Aches can vary wildly in strength; they usually do minor damage and take a single strike to defeat, but sometimes they may be much stronger and able to withstand a huge number of hits, similar to [[Bubble]]s.

In [[Nabooru Town]], a lone red Ache roosts on the ceiling of one building. It will not attack, and if {{Term|TAoL|Link}} jumps up he can speak to it, but it will not say anything. If Link persists, it will break its silence and reveal a secret to him.<ref>{{Cite|YOU ARE PERSISTENT FIND HEART OVER OCEAN|Ache|TAoL}}</ref>

The [[Acheman]] is a close relative to Aches.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TLoZ|-}} TV Series===
An Ache makes a brief appearance in "[[The Ringer]]" episode of {{TLoZ|TV Series}}. Like the rest of Ganon's minions, the Ache is capable of speech. When Ganon arrives disguised to the [[Magician's Contest]] being held in [[North Castle]], he turns a bird into an Ache and orders it to go see who is guarding the [[Triforce#Triforce of Wisdom|Triforce of Wisdom]].<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=Quickly, fly to the tower if anyone guards the Triforce of Wisdom.|name=Ganon|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref> The Ache then flies off to the tower where the Triforce and Link are located and sees that Link and [[Spryte]] are next to the sacred relic. The flying foe then returns to Ganon and informs him that the young hero is guarding the Triforce.<ref>{{Cite Episode|quote=It's guarded by Link.|name=Ache|show=The Legend of Zelda|episode=1}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}
