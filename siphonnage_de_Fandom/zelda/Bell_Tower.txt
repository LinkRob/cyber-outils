The {{Term/Store|Bell Tower||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 252}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Bell Tower}} is a part of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. It is located above the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum Observation Room|link}}, and its {{Term|BotW|Bell}} was designed to ring when a chain is pulled.<ref name="CaC"/>

Inside the {{Term|BotW|Bell Tower}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. Once it is opened, he will receive five {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Arrow|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Bell Tower}} using {{Term|BotW|Archery (Mechanic)|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots the {{Term|BotW|Balloon|link}} near the {{Term|BotW|Bell Tower}}'s ceiling, a {{Term|BotW|Korok}} will reveal itself and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
