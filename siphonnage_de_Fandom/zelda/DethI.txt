{{Term/Store|DethI||Series, LA, LADX}} (spelt <span style="font-family:serif">DethI</span>) is a {{Term|LADX|Boss|link}} in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 202}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|LADX|DethI}} was also referred to as '''Death Eye''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 112}}</ref> However, because it contradicts the name given in {{E}}, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
{{Term|LADX|DethI}} consists of a large pulsating mass with a single, usually closed eye from which two dark, shadowy arms with huge spiked tips extend outwards. Using the {{Term|LADX|Roc's Feather|link}}, {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} can jump over the arms to avoid being hurt once they begin to swing and avoid the movement of the central body. Occasionally, the eye will open during the battle, upon which {{Term|LADX|Link}} must strike it using the {{Term|LADX|Bow|link}} or the {{Term|LADX|Boomerang|link}} (or the Magic Rod in {{LA|NS}}). After depleting half of its health, the swinging arms speed up and start moving in an erratic pattern, with one arm moving faster than the other. 16 (10 in {{LA|NS|-}}) direct shots in the eye with the {{Term|LADX|Bow}} or a single one with the {{Term|LADX|Boomerang}} will defeat it (in {{LA|NS|-}}, the Boomerang can no longer defeat DethI in one hit). Once it is defeated, the {{Term|LADX|Wind Fish|link}} will awaken.

==Trivia==
*The design of {{Term|Series|Vaati|link}}'s final form in {{FS}} and {{TMC}} may have been inspired by {{Term|LADX|DethI|link}}.
*In versions prior to {{LA|NS}}, {{Term|LA|DethI}}'s musical theme was the same as the other {{Plural|LA|Mini-Boss|link}}. This was changed in {{LA|NS|-}}, where it was given its own musical theme.
* Some sources, such as the Prima Official Strategy Guide, have misinterpreted the spelling of its name as Dethl, with a lowercase L instead of a capital I.
