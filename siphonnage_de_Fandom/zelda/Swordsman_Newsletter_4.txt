==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}} becomes available for purchase after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman|link}}, inspiring {{Term|TMC|Stamp|link}} to enlist {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} in creating the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter}} series.<ref>{{Cite|I really wish I could do more to help people... Wait! I've got it! I'll ask {{Color|TMC Green|Swiftblade}} to start up a newsletter for adventurers! That'll be a great way to provide a useful service for everyone... Hm. It's going to be a lot of work, though. I should get {{Color|TMC Green|Marcy}} to help me.|Stamp|TMC}}</ref>

The {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter 4}}, the fourth issue, can be purchased for 200 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Post Office|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains at least four {{Plural|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}}.
