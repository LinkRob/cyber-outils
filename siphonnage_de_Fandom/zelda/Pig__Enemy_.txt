'''Pigs''' appear as enemies in {{FPTRR}}. There are several different varieties of {{Plural|FPTRR|Pig}}. What appears to be their snout is actually their face, with their eyes in the "nostrils". They appear to be closely related to {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker|link}}.

==Variations==
===Eyelid Pig===
'''Eyelid Pigs''' (or simply '''Eyelids''') are the most basic of the pigs. They are cowardly and will run away from {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} if they see him. They are found in {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure}}. They may drop {{Term|FPTRR|Minced Meat|link}} or {{Term|FPTRR|Short Fur|link}} when defeated.

===Flying Pig===
[[File:FPTRR Flying Pig Sprite.png]] 
'''Flying Pigs''' are a rather passive enemy that can only be attacked when they land and they can only be found at the {{Term|FPTRR|Sunshine Seashore}}. They may drop {{Term|FPTRR|Minced Meat}} or {{Term|FPTRR|Short Fur}} when defeated.

===Gold Pig===
[[File:FPTRR Gold Pig Sprite.png]] 
'''Gold Pigs''' are a rare variant of the Eyelid Pigs that appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure}}. They drop 500 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} when defeated.

===Diver Pig===
[[File:FPTRR Diver Pig Sprite.png]]
'''Diver Pigs''' are found in the {{Term|FPTRR|Steamy Marsh}} and will run away from {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} if they see him. They may drop {{Plural|FPTRR|Baby Shark|link}} or {{Term|FPTRR|Pink Quartz|link}} when defeated.

===Flaming Pig===
[[File:FPTRR Flaming Pig Sprite.png]]
'''Flaming Pigs''' are an aggressive enemy found on {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma}}. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} must throw a bottle of water onto them to be able to attack them. They may drop {{Term|FPTRR|Marbled Meat|link}} or {{Plural|FPTRR|Gold Gem|link}} when defeated.

==Nomenclature==
The Japanese name for these enemies generally consist of puns on the word {{Romanize|ブタ|Buta}}, meaning "pig." For example, Eyelid Pigs are known as {{Romanize|マブタ|Mabuta}}, meaning "Eyelids," while Diver Pigs are known as {{Romanize|ダイブタ|Daibuta}}, a combination of Buta and {{Romanize|ダイバー|Daibā}}, meaning "diver."
