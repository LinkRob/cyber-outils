The '''Triforce Room''' is a location featured in {{TSP}}. Located in {{Term|TSP|North Castle}}, it is the resting place of the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom|link}}, where it is safely kept away from the hands {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}}. In order to hide the Triforce, there are three false {{Plural|TSP|Triforce Room}} scattered around the castle: the path from the throne room to the true {{Term|TSP|Triforce Room}} passes over exactly ten diamonds.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There are false Triforce rooms located in North Castle, as well as the true Triforce room where the Triforce of Wisdom is hidden. The path from the throne room to the true Triforce room passes over exactly ten diamonds.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=78}}</ref> 

The {{Term|TSP|Triforce Room}} is first visited in the book after the {{Term|TSP|King of Hyrule|link}} decides to show {{Term|TSP|Charles of Moria|link}} the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=He beckons to Sir Charles. 'To cement the new bond between our kingdoms, we'd like you to see something we call the Triforce of Wisdom.'|book= TSP|publisher= |page=23}}</ref> The room is described to be "stunning," with walls that are "coated with glittering gems--diamonds, emeralds, and jade, all set in elaborate designs. The floor is pure white marble," and a "golden chandelier hangs from the ceiling.<ref>Page 77</ref> It is also here that the king dubs Charles a knight of the {{Term|TSP|Triforce|link}}, asking him to protect it and to keep it away from {{Term|TSP|Ganon|link}}'s hands.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote='Wonderful! I hereby dub you a knight of the Triforce,' says the king, beaming.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=78}}</ref> Later on in the book, after {{Term|TSP|Link|link}} uses the {{Term|TSP|Mirror of Truth|link}} to reveal that Charles is in fact {{Term|TSP|Ganon}} in disguise, Charles will manage to steal the {{Term|TSP|Triforce of Wisdom}} from the {{Term|TSP|Triforce Room}} and escape with it into the {{Term|TSP|Midoro Swamp|link}}, where {{Term|TSP|Link}} must follow him to defeat him and bring peace back to {{Term|TSP|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Charles inches toward the Triforce, snatches it from its stand and then drags Zelda out the door.|book= TSP|publisher= |page=23}}</ref>
