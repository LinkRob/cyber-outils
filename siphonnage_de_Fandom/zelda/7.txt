"{{Term/Store|7||Series, TMC}}" is a {{Term|Series|Song|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Track: 7|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|TMC|7}}" plays four times during {{TMC}}; first when {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} attacks the {{Plural|TMC|Vassal|link}} guarding the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|link}} during the award ceremony for the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}}, second when {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} appears and summons a pair of {{Plural|TMC|Spear Moblin|link}} to attack {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} after they have infused the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} for the first time, third when {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} possesses {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, and fourth when {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} learns the location of the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} from the stained glass in the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} before knocking {{Term|TMC|Link}} unconscious.

It appears as the seventh track on the {{Term|TMC|Phonograph|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}}.
