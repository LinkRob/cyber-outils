The {{Term/Store|Silver Scale||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, [[Link]]'s own abilities allow him to dive for no more than three seconds, or in distance, three meters. When he reaches [[Zora's Domain]], he can participate in the [[Diving Game]]. If he manages to collect all of the Blue [[Rupee]]s underwater before time runs out, he will be rewarded with the '''Silver Scale''', which enhances Link's diving capabilities to up to 6 meters.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Silver Scale! Jump into the water and press (A). You can dive deeper than you could before.|N/A|OoT}}</ref> It is required to enter [[Inside Jabu-Jabu's Belly]].
