'''[https://kaeru-no-tame-ni-kane-wa-naru.fandom.com/wiki/Prince_Richard Prince Richard]''' is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Richard|N/A|LANS}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Richard (Link's Awakening)}} was referred to as {{Term/Store|Prince Richard||LA, LADX}} prior to {{LA|NS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{LA|NS|-}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}} He originates from the 1992 Japan-only [[Game Boy]] title ''[[wikipedia:Kaeru_no_Tame_ni_Kane_wa_Naru|Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru]]'' (''The Frog For Whom the Bell Tolls''), where he is the rival and childhood friend of its main protagonist [https://kaeru-no-tame-ni-kane-wa-naru.fandom.com/wiki/Prince_of_Sabl%C3%A9 Prince of Sablé].

==Biography==
{{Term|LADX|Prince Richard}} is a noble swordsman who once lived in {{Term|LADX|Kanalet Castle|link}}, but his servants went berserk and kicked him out.<ref>{{Cite|Salutations! You wouldn't know by the look of me, but I used to live in the castle! My servants went berserk and I was forced to flee to my villa... So, you want the key to Ukuku Prairie, do you? I may be able to help you... Let's make... a deal, shall we? I want you to retrieve the Golden Leaf I left behind in the castle when I fled...|Prince Richard|LADX}}</ref> He then moved a little ways off in a small house, [[Richard's Villa]]. Richard is only interested in getting his five {{Plural|LADX|Golden Leaf|link}} back and asks [[Link]] to retrieve them.<ref>{{Cite|Smashing! To tell you the truth, there are five leaves, and I want them all! Oh, you'll need to do some digging, so buy a shovel! Are you off, then? Good luck!|Prince Richard|LA}}</ref> The young hero receives the {{Term|LADX|Slime Key|link}} buried in the {{Term|LADX|Pothole Field|link}}, the garden in back of {{Term|LADX|Richard's Villa}}, for gathering the five {{Plural|LADX|Golden Leaf}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh! Tres Bien! I see you have recovered all of the leaves! Now, move this box and you will find your reward! |Prince Richard|LADX}}</ref>

The overworld theme ''The Frog For Whom the Bell Tolls'' is used as the theme for Richard's Villa. Inside, there are many {{Plural|LADX|Frog|link}}, which is also a reference to his game of origin.

==Trivia==
*He is one of several guest characters in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'' from a different [[Nintendo]] franchise to appear in a [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'']] game.
*The Japanese video game where Prince Richard debuted is not very well-known overseas, and therefore many Western ''Zelda'' fans do not know who the character is.
**Ironically, the game engine of ''Kaeru no Tame ni Kane wa Naru'' is an early version of what would be used for ''Link's Awakening''.
*Richard shares a similarity with [[Link]] alongside several key characters from the ''[[fireemblem:Fire_Emblem_(series)|Fire Emblem]]'' series such as [[fireemblem:Marth|Marth]] in that he is a heroic swordsman who lives in a medieval fantasy setting.
*If Link waits in Richard's Villa and lets the tune continue for about 2 minutes and 30 seconds, "Totaka's Song" will eventually begin playing.
*If Link attempts to talk with {{Term|LADX|Prince Richard}} before returning {{Term|LADX|BowWow|link}} to {{Term|LADX|Madam MeowMeow|link}}, he will shoo him away, telling the young hero to return without the [[Chain Chomp]].<ref>{{Cite|Ahem! Really, I must insist that you not bring that awful beast in here! Leave that creature outside and then we can talk! Good Bye!|Prince Richard|LADX}}</ref>
*In the [[photo]] with Prince Richard in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|Link's Awakening DX]]'', Link can be seen showing the [[wikipedia:V_sign|V sign]], a gesture famously used by [[Mario]], Nintendo's mascot and main character of the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' series.
*He is referenced in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], specifically ''[[Super Smash Bros. Brawl|Brawl]]'' and ''[[Super Smash Bros. Ultimate|Ultimate]]'', where he appears as a Sticker and Spirit, respectively. 
*Richard's sprite bears a resemblance to Elite Four/Champion [[pokemon:Lance|Lance]] from the early ''[[pokemon:Pokémon_(franchise)|Pokémon]]'' series games. 

==Nomenclature==
Richard means "brave power". It is built from the Germanic name elements ric, which means "rule" and "power", along with hard, meaning "hardy" and "brave".<ref>{{Cite Web|url= https://www.behindthename.com/name/richard |site= behindthename.com |title= Given Name RICHARD}}</ref>
