{{Term/Store|Dorian||Series}} is a character in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dorian|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
Dorian is a [[Sheikah]] who guards [[Impa's House]] in [[Kakariko Village]]. He carries an [[Eightfold Blade]]. Before [[Link]] meets with [[Impa]], Dorian will stay at his post at all hours of the day.

In the past, Dorian was a member of the [[Yiga]], but he gave this up when he met his wife and had his daughters, [[Koko]] and [[Cottla]].<ref name="Yiga">{{Cite|I am...a member of the Yiga Clan. Or I used to be, anyway. But then I met the most incredible woman who blessed me with two amazing children.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Dorian tried to change his ways and leave the Yiga for the [[Sheikah|Sheikah tribe]] but the Yiga killed his wife in retaliation.<ref>{{Cite|And so I tried to change my ways. Tried to leave the clan. But the Yiga... They took my wife's life as punishment.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> With the lives of his children on the line Dorian continued to serve as an informant within the Sheikah for the Yiga.<ref name="Informant">{{Cite|I couldn't let them hurt my children, so I used my position as gatekeeper to gather information for the Yiga. I told them about you and about the village in exchange for safety.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

In the [[Journal of Various Worries]], Dorian expresses concern over [[Pikango]]'s arrival in Kakariko Village.<ref name="Journal"/> Dorian fears that, in his search for the Great Fairy Fountain, Pikango will discover the Ancient Shrines near it. Dorian signs the Journal as "Anonymous Father of Two" in an attempt to remain anonymous.<ref name="Journal"/>

When Link first approaches Impa's House, Dorian and his fellow guard [[Cado]] will take a defensive stance and demand he identify himself.<ref>{{Cite|You there! Who are you?! How dare you trespass upon {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Impa}}'s abode!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> However, once they notice the [[Sheikah Slate]] in Link's possession, the two relax their stances and apologize.<ref>{{Cite|Please forgive us for behaving so rudely.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He will explain that [[Impa]] informed them about the swordsman and asks that Link head inside to meet with her.<ref>{{Cite|Of course we have heard the legends from Lady Impa herself. Please, friend... Go ahead and step inside.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Cado and Dorian will step aside to allow Link passage and Dorian reveals that for ages Impa has been waiting for his return.<ref>{{Cite|Lady Impa has been waiting on you for a long time.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> While he wishes to speak to Link as well, he asks that the hero first see the elder.<ref>{{Cite|There is much I would like to tell you as well... But first, please hurry and see what Lady Impa has to say.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

Dorian implores Link to use the [[Sheikah]] [[Kakariko Village|Village's]] resources to his advantage.<ref>{{Cite|If you need anything, please find it here in our village. The {{Color|BotW Blue|clothing shop}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|materials shop}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|general shop}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|inn}} should be able to provide everything you need.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He also mentions the [[Ta'loh Naeg Shrine]] on a hill above his settlement,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and just between you and me, there's also a {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} in the hills of this village.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> as well as the [[Great Fairy Fountain]], which he refers to as the location of Kakariko Village's [[Cotera|Guardian Spirit]].<ref>{{Cite|They say up and behind those hills resides Kakariko's {{Color|BotW Blue|guardian spirit}}. I hear she's incredibly beautiful.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Dorian admits that does not dare to try and contact the [[Great Fairy]] himself, but recommends that Link search for her.<ref>{{Cite|I'm too intimidated to go see for myself, though... It's said that she offers a special blessing to those that visit her... You should go there and see for yourself.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> If Link speaks to him at night, he asks what h 's doing up so late and tells him to spend the night at the [[Shuteye Inn]].<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing up so late? Monsters roam around at night. It's way too dangerous to wander around. There's an {{Color|BotW Blue|inn}} in this village. Why not go rest there until morning?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

Following Link's meeting with Impa, Dorian told this Yiga about his awakening.<ref name="Informant"/> Filled with grief, Dorian eventually decided to cut ties with the Yiga completely,<ref>{{Cite|But my grief over losing my wife consumed me, and I finally decided to cut ties with the Yiga completely!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> but the Yiga threatened the lives of Impa and Paya as well.<ref>{{Cite|But the Yiga are pure evil. They told me they would kill both Impa and Paya if I didn't play along!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

Once Link has spoken to Impa, Dorian and Cado will begin to take shifts guarding Impa's House. Cado will return to his post at 11:00 in the evening, allowing Dorian to go home at midnight to his daughters, [[Koko]] and [[Cottla]].<ref>{{Cite|Ah, Master Link... It's gotten so late.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> At home, Dorian will take a seat and remove his umbrella hat. He explains that his wife was murdered by the [[Yiga]].<ref name="Wife"/> He mourns her death and admits his great displeasure as his inability to protect her.<ref>{{Cite|If only... If only I had been able to stop them...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Eventually, Dorian will fall asleep in his chair.

At 8:00 AM, Dorian will wake up and go to tell his daughters stories.<ref>{{Cite|I'm off to meet my daughters. I'm going to tell them stories about our village under the tree my wife so loved.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> However, he admits that those stories are always the same.<ref>{{Cite|My stories are always the same though. I hope they don't mind!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> If Link speaks to the family while they are under the favorite [[Tree]] of Dorian' wife, Dorian will introduce his girls.<ref name="Daughters"/> While he loves their time together, he admits that he only ends up repeating stories to them.<ref>{{Cite|Any moment I can spend with my girls is a gift. I always end up boring them with the same stories, though.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> If Link asks about these stories, Dorian will share one of them.<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|BotW Red|shrine}} right above this hill, guarding the Sheikah tribe. Sometimes from {{Color|BotW Red|the forest behind it}}, you can hear... {{Big|a woman sobbing!}} ...Heh! That's the story I'm telling the children right now, anyway.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Quietly, he will admit to Link that he is talking about where the Kakariko Village's [[Cotera|Guardian Spirit]] is and that he is trying to keep the girls from going there.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Actually, the location I speak of is where the village's {{Color|BotW Blue|guardian spirit}} resides. But lately monsters have been lurking there, and it's become quite dangerous. I'm scaring my daughters so they won't go near the forest...}}|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> However, it only seems to work on Koko, as Cottla instead gets excited.<ref>{{Cite|It works on Koko, but for some reason, those stories just excite Cottla...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> At 11:00 AM, the girls run off and Dorian returns to Impa's House.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Well, if it isn't Master Link. I'll go back to guarding Lady Impa's home now. The {{Color|BotW Blue|Yiga Clan}} has been snooping around, so please be careful, Master Link.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

When the [[Sheikah Heirloom]] has been stolen from [[Paya]], Dorian asks Link go and comfort her.<ref>{{Cite|Someone snuck in and stole Lady Paya's most prized possession... I'm sure Lady Paya would feel a little more at ease if she saw Master Link's face.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> According to Paya, Dorian and Cado claim not to have seen anyone aside from the residents of Kakariko Village.<ref>{{Cite|I checked with Dorian and Cado, but they said they didn't see anyone other that the villagers around.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> While "[[The Stolen Heirloom]]" [[Shrine Quest]] is active, Dorian wonders who could have stolen the Heirloom, recalling that he saw nobody suspicious.<ref>{{Cite|But who could have stolen the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}}? There were some people visiting the manor, but they were all known villagers. I don't remember seeing anyone suspicious.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Link can ask who visited Impa's House, to which Dorian tells him [[Lasli]] and [[Mellie]] came by.<ref>{{Cite|[Who came by?] The only people who came by yesterday were... {{Color|BotW Blue|Lasli}} from the clothing shop and {{Color|BotW Blue|Grandma Mellie}} from the plum garden. That's about it. I didn't see anyone else.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

At 10:00 in the evening, Dorian will make his way to the woods past Ta'loh Naeg Shrine. If Link speaks to him during this time, Dorian is startled by his presence.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Well if it isn't Master Link. What are you doing wandering around in the dead of night?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He struggles to come up with a reason why he is out, but he eventually claims that he was patrolling Kakariko Village for the thief.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Me? Um... I was just... The village... Yes! I was patrolling the village! The thief could be hiding anywhere, you know!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> However, he decides to return to his post, as leaving Cado alone is dangerous.<ref>{{Cite|But I should probably get back to my post. Leaving Cado alone on guard duty is dangerous. Stay safe out there.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> If spoken to again, Dorian warns Link that he could be mistaken as the thief for walking around so late at night.<ref>{{Cite|Well, if it isn't Master Link... You should really stop stalking about at night like this, or someone may mistake you for the thief. P-please do be careful, Master Link.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

During his walk, Dorian wonders how "they" could have done this and states he must do something.<ref>{{Cite|How could they?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I must do something.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> In the woods, he comments about how quiet it is,<ref>{{Cite|Sure is quiet out here...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> but he hears something as he approaches the [[Pedestal]] past the bridge.<ref>{{Cite|What was that?!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He decides he must be imagining things,<ref>{{Cite|Must be imagining things.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> but once he is over the bridge, he demands an unknown entity to show themself.<ref>{{Cite|Show yourself!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

When Link approaches, Dorian repeats his demand.<ref>{{Cite|I know you're there! Show yourself!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> A [[Yiga Blademaster]] appears on the Pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|You don't have to shout. I'm right here.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> Dorian accuses the Blademaster of stealing the Sheikah Heirloom,<ref>{{Cite|So you're the ones who stole the heirloom!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> which the Blademaster confirms, noting that it seems special to Kakariko Village.<ref>{{Cite|Heh... Such anger. It seems this thing is truly special to the fools of this village.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> Dorian questions why they would steal it,<ref>{{Cite|Why would you steal it?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> but the Blademaster tells him not to play dumb and that he knew what he was getting into.<ref>{{Cite|Don't play dumb, Dorian. You knew what you were getting yourself into when you decided to leave the organization.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> He informs Dorian that by leaving the Yiga, he has forfeited his life.<ref>{{Cite|You forfeited your life the moment you left.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> After telling them about the Heirloom, his usefulness has come to an end.<ref>{{Cite|You did well to tell us about the heirloom. Unfortunately, your usefulness has come to an end, as must you. But first...|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> However, the Blademaster notices Link nearby, choosing to attack him first.<ref>{{Cite|It appears we have an audience!|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref>

After the Yiga thief is defeated, Dorian expresses shock that he was followed.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe I was being followed.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he used to be part of the Yiga Clan himself before he met his wife.<ref name="Yiga"/> Link can now use the [[Sheikah Heirloom|Heirloom]] to open the [[Lakna Rokee Shrine]]. Dorian offers his apologies and asks that Link keep what happened a secret for his children's sake.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link, I... I'm very sorry for all of this. I know it's a selfish request, but do you think you could keep what really happened a secret? I truly don't deserve mercy, but I don't want my children, Koko and Cottla, to suffer anymore.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Dorian is one of several characters that will hit Link back if he attacks them with a [[Weapon]].<ref>{{Cite|Please, stop!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> These attacks deal no damage.
*In {{TotK|-}}, Dorian will, for a moment, mistake Link for a Yiga Assassin if he is approached while wearing the [[Yiga Set]].<ref>{{Cite|Why are you wearing an awful outfit like that?<br>My insides turned to ice for a moment there.|Dorian|TotK}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Dorian's namesake comes from, and is only one letter off of, durian. However he has no connections to the in-game item, the [[Hearty Durian]].
