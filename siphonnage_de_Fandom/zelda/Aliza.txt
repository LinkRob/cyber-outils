{{Term/Store|Aliza||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Aliza|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Aliza}} is a resident of {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}. She can be found standing beneath a large {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} just east of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}, which she refers to as sacred.<ref name="Sacred">{{Cite|So it was true! I met the hero of Hyrule under this sacred tree!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> There, she diligently waits to meet the hero of legend, whom she is smitten with. Until {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks with her, she will stand beneath the {{Term|BotW|Tree}} regardless of the {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Time|link}} of day. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, she will preemptively speak to him.<ref>{{Cite|You're finally here...|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} first speaks with {{Term|BotW|Aliza}}, she initially believes him to be the legendary hero.<ref name="Sacred"/> Regardless if {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms or denies this, she will backtrack on her assertion and doubt his status when she notices he is not carrying the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Uh... What?] So you're not the hero of legend? I was so sure... ...But now that you mention it, you're way too scrawny to be the hero. And you don't have the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}}.|Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Yes, I am the hero.] I knew it! At long last I— ...Wait a minute! You can't fool me! Everyone knows the hero of Hyrule wields the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}}! You don't have it!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} without any {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, she will realize that he is npt carrying any and dismiss him, claiming that the hero is always equipped with the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|...But now you mention it, you're way too scrawny to be a hero. You don't even have a sword!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I knew it! At long last I— Wait a minute! You don't even have a sword. You can't be the hero of legend! Nice try, buddy. The hero of Hyrule always carries the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}} with him. Plus he's way tougher looking...|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>}} Once she realizes her mistake, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to send the hero her way if he ever encounters him on his travels.<ref>{{Cite|If you happen to meet the hero who wields the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}} during your travels... Tell him I'm waiting for him here.|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> Even if {{Term|BotW|Link}} tries to stop talking to her without acknowledging her belief, she will still make this request.<ref>{{Cite|[Bye.] Wait! If you happen to meet the hero who wields the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}} during your travels... Tell him I'm waiting for him here.|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>

After speaking to {{Term|BotW|Aliza}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives the "{{Term|BotW|My Hero|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, prompting him to return to Aliza once he has found the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can further interrogate Aliza about the "legendary hero" and the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, did you meet the hero of Hyrule that wielded the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}}?|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> When asked about the hero, {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} explains that he appears with the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} whenever {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} threatens {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Legendary hero?] Oh, come on... You know—the hero who saves Hyrule whenever the Calamity takes hold? He carries the {{Color|BotW Red|legendary sword}} on his back like it's no big thing. How cool is that?|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires about the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, she will inform him that the stories she heard of it say that it was put to rest in a {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link|display=forest}}, awaiting its owner's return.<ref>{{Cite|[Legendary sword?] The stories say that the sword sleeps somewhere in the forest...waiting for the hero to awaken it. Oh, I wish I could meet him...|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>

After acquiring the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} and state his status as the hero.<ref>{{Cite|[I am he.] WHAT?! So...it WAS you!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already acquired the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Aliza}}, she will discover that he is the hero regardless of how {{Term|BotW|Link}} reacts in return to her.<ref>{{Cite|[Uh... What?] Don't try to hide it! That sword there is proof enough… you are the hero I've been waiting for!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Yes, I am the hero.] I knew it was you! That sword leaves no room for doubt. I'm so happy to finally meet you!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Bye.] Wait! I knew it was you! That sword leaves no room for doubt. I'm so happy to finally meet you!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>}} She states that the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} he carries is proof of this.<ref>{{Cite|That sword is proof enough... I'M SO HAPPY RIGHT NOW!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> As a gift, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Star Fragment|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This... is a little something I've been saving for you. Please take it!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} is truly excited about meeting {{Term|BotW|Link}}, her mood completely drops after rewarding their encounter, having lost her excitement over meeting him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man. It feels SO good to check that off the bucket list! But...now that I've met you, some of the excitement's gone. I mean, you're not even my type... Sorry, buddy. Well, toodle-oo!|Aliza|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to {{Term|BotW|Aliza}} later on, she scolds him for talking to random strangers instead of doing his job.<ref>{{Cite|You must be really busy, you know, saving the world and all. So instead of spending your time chatting with random strangers, maybe think about doing your job, yeah?|Aliza|BotW}}</ref> During the evening, she sits beside {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable}}'s {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. At midnight, she heads inside the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} to sleep in one of its {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}}.
