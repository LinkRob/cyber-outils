'''''The Legend of Zelda: Best Collection''''' is a sheet music book compilation arranged for the piano. It contains 192 pages featuring ''Zelda'' music from {{TLoZ}}, {{ALttP}}, {{OoT}}, {{MM}}, and {{TWW}}.<ref>[http://zeldapower.com/index.php/zelda-sheet-music/242-the-legend-of-zelda-best-collection.html Zelda Power - The Legend of Zelda: Best Collection]</ref> It was released on August 20, 2008.<ref name=quote/>
