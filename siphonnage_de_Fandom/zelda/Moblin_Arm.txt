{{Plural|BotW|Moblin Arm}} are detached {{Term|BotW|Moblin|link}} bones that continue to move after being separated from the body.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Stalmoblin|link}} after being defeated by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. They can be used as two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, but are very brittle and will easily break.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Moblin Arm}} are made of bone, which means they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, can not be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, and will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
