==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Zant's Helmet is a piece of [[Armor]]  introduced in the "[[EX Treasure: Usurper King]]" [[Side Quest]] from [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC pack. It is a priceless relic that was once kept safely within the walls of [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]], but was later stolen by [[Misko]]. Misko hints at the whereabouts of Zant's Helmet in [[Misko's EX Journal 2]] in the [[Deya Village Ruins]],<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> claiming that it rests among dead [[Tree]]s in [[Tobio's Hollow]].<ref>{{Cite|The helm of the self-proclaimed King of Shadows is {{Color|BotW Red|in the swamp of the valley of dead trees, south of Dueling Peaks}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The metallic [[Treasure Chest]] containing Zant's Helmet can be found between two dead Trees in the middle of a [[Bottomless Bog]] in Tobio's Hollow. In order to retrieve it, Link must use the [[Magnesis]] [[Rune]] to pull it from the ground.

When worn, Zant's Helmet grants [[Link]] the [[Unfreezable]] bonus, preventing Link from being frozen. However, Link will still take damage from exposure to frigid environments and freezing water even while wearing Zant's Helmet. It cannot be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], nor can it be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] in [[Hateno Village]].

Zant's Helmet is said to be much like the Helmet that [[Zant]] wore in the [[Era of Twilight]].<ref name="Inventory"/> After initially finding Zant's Helmet, Link can purchase more from [[Granté]] in [[Tarrey Town]] for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
[[File:HW Zant's Helmet Icon.png|right]]
In {{HW}}, Zant's Helmet is a Gold [[Material#Hyrule Warriors|Material]] that can be dropped by [[Zant]] upon defeat. In {{HW|-}}, [[Agitha]] and [[Medli]] can use Zant's Helmet to create various [[Badge]]s in the [[Badge Market]]. In {{HW|L}}, only Agitha can use Zant's Helmet in the Badge Market. Zant's Helmet can also be used to create various mixtures in the [[Apothecary]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
