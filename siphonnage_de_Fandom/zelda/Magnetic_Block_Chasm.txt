The {{Term/Store|Magnetic Block Chasm||Series, OoS}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= BradyGames |page= 94 {{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> also known as the '''Magnet Chasm''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= BradyGames |page= 91 {{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> is a location in {{OoS}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|OoS|Magnetic Block Chasm}} is a room on the second basement level of the {{Term|OoS|Explorer's Crypt|link}}. It consists of a large {{Term|OoS|Pit|link}}, with several spinning {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Block|link}} floating above it. Also within the room are several {{Plural|OoS|Block|link}}, a {{Term|OoS|Shock Switch|link}} that reverses the direction the {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Block}} spin, and two {{Plural|OoS|Trampoline|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} must use the {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Glove|link}} to "stick" himself to the {{Plural|OoS|Magnetic Block|display=Blocks}} and cross the {{Term|OoS|Magnetic Block Chasm|display=Chasm}} to continue further into the {{Term|OoS|Dungeon|link}}.
