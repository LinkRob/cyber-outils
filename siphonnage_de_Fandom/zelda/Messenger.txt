{{Term/Store|Messenger|Messengers|Series|plural}} are types of {{Plural|HWAoC|Captain (Hyrule Warriors)|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Characteristics==
{{Improve}}
Messengers come in three types; Enemy Messengers, which will cause you great annoyance through the enemies they spawn if you neglect to kill them, Allied Messengers, which will summon allies for your team, and Critical Messengers, which must be kept safe at the risk of losing the mission.
