{{Term/Store|Goron Ore|Goron Ores|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 126"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Goron Ore}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Volcano|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Blazing Trail|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Goron Ore}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Blazing Trail}}''', ''"Pop all balloons!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Goron Ore}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Hinox Mine|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Goron Ore}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 250 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Goron Ore}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Boomeranger|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Goron Garb|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 126"/>
