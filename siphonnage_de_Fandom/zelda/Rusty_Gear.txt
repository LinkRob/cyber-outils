The {{Term/Store|Rusty Gear|Rusty Gear|BotW, Series}} is an archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear}} consists of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that were once used by {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Rusty Spear}}<br/>A rusty polearm likely used by knights from an age past. The spearhead is in bad shape due to prolonged exposure to the elements, so its durability is low.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Rusty Shield}}<br/>It's likely this rusty old shield once belonged to a knight. It still has some defensive capabilities, but its usefulness has been worn down by time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Over time, lack of maintenance and exposure to the elements have decayed the quality of these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, causing them to become brittle and lowering their {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}.<ref name="Spear"/><ref name="Shield"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Rusty Broadsword}}<br/>This once-fearsome sword has seen better days. It can do some damage in the right hands but also breaks quickly.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Rusty Claymore}}<br/> A two-handed sword not properly cared for. Although it can be used as a weapon, its durability is very low. Don't expect it to last for more than a few strikes.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

If a piece of {{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear}} is placed in front of a {{Term|BotW|Rock Octorok|link}} while it is inhaling air, it will swallow the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} and pause to crunch it in its mouth.<ref name="CaC"/> After this, it will shoot it back out as a cleaned counterpart.<ref name="CaC"/> After being cleaned, there is a 50% chance that the item will be its corresponding item in the {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Gear|link}} archetype, a 35% chance that it will be of the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear|link}}, a 10% chance that it will be of the {{Term|BotW|Knight's Gear|link}}, and a 5% chance that it will be of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear|link}}.

As {{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear}} is made from metal, all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} in this series sink when placed in {{Plural|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they can be manipulated through the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a piece of {{Term|BotW|Rusty Gear}}.
