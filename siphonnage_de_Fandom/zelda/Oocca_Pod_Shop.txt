The {{Term/Store|Oocca Pod Shop||Series, TP}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 379}}</ref> Situated in the [[City in the Sky]], it is the only shop in the dungeon and can be found on the first floor. 

==Features and Overview==
After [[Link]] and [[Ooccoo]] arrive to the City in the Sky, Ooccoo rushes to the Oocca Pod Shop to check for survivors.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh, goodness... Brave adventurer, you won't believe it! There's a dragon raging outside the city walls... Ooh, I'm terribly worried about everyone... I'm going to check in that shop for survivors!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref> Inside, Link can find an array of items that can be bought before adventuring through the dungeon itself. Ooccoo will once again offer to accompany Link; however, unlike other times where she could transport him out of the dungeon, Ooccoo will only be able to transport Link to the inside of the Oocca Pod Shop instead.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh! But you're planning on walking around the city and taking it in, right?! I'm worried about the others and this is an emergency! I can't just stand around waiting! Please take me, too! I won't help you warp to the surface, but I WILL bring you back to this shop anytime!|Ooccoo|TP}}</ref>

The Oocca Pod Shop is notable in that it is the only shop in the entire game that is located inside a dungeon, making it easy for Link to restock his inventory at any time while traversing the dungeon.
