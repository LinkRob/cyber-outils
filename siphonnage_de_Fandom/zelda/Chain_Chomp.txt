'''[[mariowiki:Chain_Chomp|Chain Chomps]]''' (also known as '''BoWWows''') are recurring [[Enemy|enemies]] and [[Animal]]s in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|FS}}}}</ref> Chain Chomps are from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], making their overall debut in ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario Bros. 3|Super Mario Bros. 3]].'' In the ''Zelda'' series, they make their first appearance in {{aLttP}} and reoccur in a few subsequent titles. 

Despite their prime association with ''Mario'', Chain Chomps were originally conceived as foes for {{TLoZ|-}} franchise,<ref name=":0">{{Cite Person|quote= We’d had concept art for Bow-Wow lying around for awhile. We’d put it aside thinking we might make use of it if we could, but someone discovered it and ended up using it for their own purposes.|name= Takashi Tezuka|url= http://www.glitterberri.com/a-link-to-the-past/the-men-who-made-zelda/|title= The Men Who Made Zelda – Staff Interview}}</ref> similarly to [[Fire Bar]]s. [[Takashi Tezuka]], who was working on games for the ''Super Mario'' series at the time and formerly the co-director of the latter, allegedly decided to put it in {{aLttP|-}} as a joke.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Originally, Chain Chomp was a character from the ''Mario'' universe. But somehow, he made a guest appearance in a 2D ''Zelda'' game. I hear it started because Mr. Tezuka, who was working on ''Mario'' games back then as well, jokingly put one in a ''Zelda'' game.|published=August 4, 2014|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3_DZtTR3zu0?t=16m18s|title=Hyrule Warriors Direct|site=YouTube}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Much like in the ''[[mariowiki:Mario_(franchise)|Mario]]'' series, Chain Chomps are dog-like creatures in the shape of an iron ball with large fangs, usually attached to a chain to keep them from charging, hence their name. Chain Chomps are commonly depicted as being chained to a post, limiting their movement when they attempt to lunge at [[Link]]. In all of their appearances within the ''Zelda'' series, unlike ''Mario'', they are invincible enemies that have no means of being defeated.

In ''Super Mario'', they are widely known for their signature "[https://soundeffects.fandom.com/wiki/Sound_Ideas,_DOG,_ROTTWEILER_-_MEDIUM_DOG,_BARKING,_ANIMAL woof-woof]" sound. In {{TLoZ|-}}, however, they tend to be silent, with the exception of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]''. In ''A Link to the Past'', they use the generic sound of [[Soldier (Enemy)|Soldiers]] brainwashed by [[Agahnim]], which coincidentally also charges at Link.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
[[File:ALttP Chain Chomps.png|thumb|left|200px|Chain Chomps, as seen in {{ALttP|-}}]]
In {{aLttP|-}}, Chain Chomps are found as enemies in {{Term|ALttP|Turtle Rock|link}}, as well as in the [[Palace of the Four Sword]] in the [[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords|GBA remake]]. In both dungeons, they are chained to posts and periodically lunge at Link to attack. They are invulnerable to all forms of attack and inflict three [[Heart Container|Hearts]] worth of damage regardless of whatever [[Clothes|Mail tunic]] the young hero wears, making them among the most dangerous foes in the game. In Turtle Rock, there are only two Chain Chomps found in one room guarding two [[Shock Switch|Shock Switches]], but in the Palace of the Four Sword, numerous Chain Chomps line the entrance hallway, all chained to [[torch]]es.
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, Chain Chomps are docile Animals, unlike [[Mutt|Mutts]]. Three of them are kept as pets by [[Madam MeowMeow]] in [[Mabe Village]]. Two of her Chain Chomps are small, with one kept indoors and another, named [[YipYip]], inside the doghouse next to her house. In this game, the Chain Chomps have the ability to speak, specifically YipYip.

==={{FS|-}}===
[[File:FS BowWow Item Information.png|thumb|Green Link obtaining BowWow in Vaati's Palace, as seen in {{FS|-}}|240x240px]]
In {{FS}}, a Chain Chomp,<ref>{{Cite|You got a Chain Chomp! He eats anything! Beware the mad dog!|N/A|FSAE}}</ref> known as BowWow in the original version,<ref>{{Cite|You got BowWow! He eats anything! Beware the mad dog!|N/A|FS}}</ref> can occasionally be obtained as an usable item in [[Vaati's Palace]]. When used, the Chain Chomp appears tethered to a chain that the Link who has it carries. Like the BowWow from {{LA|-}}, it lunges at and eats enemies whole within its reach, and is able to attack enemies that the [[Links]] otherwise cannot get to. It is, however, also hostile towards the other Links and will attack them as well if nearby. Much like actual enemies, [[Rupee]]s will spill out each time the other Links are attacked by it. The Chain Chomp can be put away by pressing the item's button again.

In {{FSAE}}, a Chain Chomp can be found as one of the items in the [[Realm of Memories]] during the [[Goponga Swamp]] section of the {{LA|-}} area. It is necessary to attack a lone [[Octorok]] that is outside of the Links' reach, in order to reveal a chest containing a [[Key]].

==={{FSA|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Four Swords Adventures|Four Swords Adventures]]'', a lone Chain Chomp appears in [[Kakariko Village (Four Swords Adventures)|Kakariko Village]], north of the settlement in a burning field. As with {{aLttP|-}}, it is an indestructible obstacle that is chained to a post and charges at the Links.

== In the ''Mario'' series ==
[[File:SM64 Chain Chomp.webp|thumb|243x243px|A Chain Chomp as seen in ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_64|Super Mario 64]]'', on the game's first course, [[mariowiki:Bob-omb_Battlefield|Bob-omb Battlefield]]]]
In their source material despite being originally intended for {{TLoZ|-}},<ref name=":0" /> Chain Chomps are one of the many enemies in the ''Super Mario'' games sent by [[Bowser]] to hinder and harm [[Mario]] and [[Luigi]] from protecting the [[mariowiki:Mushroom_Kingdom|Mushroom Kingdom]] and rescue [[Princess Peach]]. They made their first appearance in the 1988 [[Nintendo Entertainment System]] title ''Super Mario Bros. 3''. As in the ''Zelda'' games, they are large, black dog-like spherical monsters with large fangs that periodically charge at the protagonists and chomping their massive jaws, making the famous "woof-woof" sound in the 3D ''Mario'' titles. Although most are rather small, some Chain Chomps come at gigantic sizes or colors such as red, making those variants more dangerous. As with other enemies, in 2D-based games, if the Mario Bros (or other playable protagonists) touch them, they will lose power-ups or shrink down to their Small form, and in three-dimensional installments can inflict high damage. In certain games, when chained to a post, they can actually break free to attack if the player waits long enough or uses the [[mariowiki:Ground_Pound|Ground Pound]] technique to force them out.

Interestingly, in ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_RPG:_Legend_of_the_Seven_Stars|Super Mario RPG: Legend of the Seven Stars]]'', Bowser can use a Chain Chomp as a weapon, which is shared for the [[mariowiki:Koopalings|Koopaling]] [[mariowiki:Iggy_Koopa|Iggy Koopa]] in later games, specifically ''[[mariowiki:New_Super_Mario_Bros._Wii|New Super Mario Bros. Wii]]'' and ''[[mariowiki:New_Super_Mario_Bros._2|New Super Mario Bros. 2]]''.

==Trivia==
*Although Chain Chomps for [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] in Japan are known as '''Kerubin''', they are named '''Wanwan''' in the ''[[Mario (franchise)|Mario]]'' franchise, a reference to their signature barking sounds.
*The idea of the Chain Chomp came from [[Shigeru Miyamoto]]'s childhood experience when a fierce dog lunged towards him and almost bit him. However, the dog was tethered by a chain, holding it back.
*Although appearing to have iron-like skin, [[Madam MeowMeow]] in {{LA}} mentions that BowWows have fur coats.<ref>{{Cite|Ho ho ho! My BowWow is so proud of his fine fur coat!|Madam MeowMeow|LADX}}</ref>
*The Chain Chomp was originally going to appear in {{OoT}} but was scrapped late in development, according to an interview with Miyamoto.
*The Chain Chomp in {{FSA|-}} uses recycled sound bytes from ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_64|Super Mario 64]]''.
