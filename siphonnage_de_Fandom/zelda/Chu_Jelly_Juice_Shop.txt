The {{Term/Store|Chu Jelly Juice Shop||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a location in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hello, hello, and hello again! This is the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Chu Jelly Juice Shop}}! We use only 100% natural {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Chu Jelly}} here, I assure you!|Doc Bandam|TWWHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly Juice Shop}} is located next to the mansion of {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island}}. It specializes on using {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly|link}} to make {{Plural|TWWHD|Potion|link}} and it is run by the eccentric {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our restorative juices are specially made with plentiful portions of Chu Jelly!|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref> At first, the {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly Juice Shop|display=Shop}} only offers the {{Term|TWWHD|Red Potion|link}}. When {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} gives him 15 {{Term|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly|display=Green|link}} and {{Term|TWWHD|Blue Chu Jelly|link}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam}} will be able to make {{Term|TWWHD|Green Potion|display=Green|link}} and {{Plural|TWWHD|Blue Potion|link}}, respectively.<ref>{{Cite|Gasp! Is that...? Could it possibly be?!? Did you, by chance, bring me Chu Jelly?!? It's quite clever of you to be bringing such quality ingredients to me!|Doc Bandam|TWW}}</ref> Every time {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} gives him five more {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Potion}}-maker will give the young hero a free sample of the new {{Term|TWWHD|Potion}}, either a {{Term|TWWHD|Red Potion|display=Red}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Green Potion|display=Green}}, or {{Term|TWWHD|Blue Potion}}, depending on what type of {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}} {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} gives him. Even if he has already made that type of {{Term|TWWHD|Potion}}, he will still give {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} a free sample.
