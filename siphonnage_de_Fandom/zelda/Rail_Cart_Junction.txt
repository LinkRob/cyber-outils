The {{Term/Store|Rail Cart Junction||Series, OoS}},<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= BradyGames |page= 51 {{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> also known as the '''Mine Cart Junction''',<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoS |guide= BradyGames |page= 52 {{Exp Game|OoS}} |edition= Pocket Guide}}</ref> is a location in {{OoS}}.

==Features and Overview==
{{Stub|OoS}}
The {{Term|OoS|Rail Cart Junction}} is a room on the first floor of the {{Term|OoS|Dancing Dragon Dungeon}}. It contains three {{Plural|OoS|Stalfos}} and a {{Term|OoS|Blade Trap|link}}, as well as a {{Term|OoS|Mine Cart|link}} on a track and a {{Term|OoS|Rail Switch|link}} with which to control it.
