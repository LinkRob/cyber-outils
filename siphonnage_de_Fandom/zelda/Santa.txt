==Biography==
{{Term|OoS|Santa}} is mentioned only once, by {{Term|OoS|Holly|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} is forced to enter her {{Term|OoS|Holly's House|link|display=House}} via the chimney due to the snow covering her walkway, and she mistakes him for {{Term|OoS|Santa}}.<ref name="Holly"/>

==Trivia==
* In reality, {{Wp|Santa Claus|Santa}} is a mythical figure that comes down people's chimneys on Christmas Eve to leave them presents. {{Term|OoS|Holly}} likely mistakes {{Term|OoS|Link}} for {{Term|OoS|Santa}} because he too entered through the chimney.
** In the British English version of the game, {{Term|OoS|Holly}} instead mistakes {{Term|OoS|Link}} for a {{Wp|Chimney sweep|chimney sweep}}.<ref>{{Cite|AHH!!! You startled me! Winter sets in all of a sudden, then down my chimney comes... You're no chimney sweep!|Holly|OoS|British English version}}</ref>
* {{Wp|Holly}} is a plant often associated with Christmas. {{Term|OoS|Holly}}'s name may be a reference to this.
