==Overivew==
{{Term|FSA|Terrorpin Hockey}} was created by a [[Deku Scrub]] at the stage called [[Frozen Hyrule]]. The [[Links]] have to flip over the [[Terrorpin]]s that will glide around on the ice and then simply hit them once to make 2 red [[Force Gem]]s (worth 20 Force Gems each) pop out.<ref>{{Cite|The rules are simple. You send Terrorpins spinning and then cut them!|Deku Scrub|FSA}}</ref> If a Link hits them when they are standing still on the ice, he will most of the times get nothing. And if he gets hit by one when it is gliding around on the ice, he will lose plenty of Force Gems.
