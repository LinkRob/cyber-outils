'''Zelda 2''' is one of 18-Volt's microgames in ''[[WarioWare|WarioWare: D.I.Y. Showcase]]''. It is based on {{TAoL}}.

==Gameplay==
[[Link]] has to raise his [[Shield]] to defend himself against a [[Goriya]]'s [[Boomerang]]s. If done successfully, the player wins, otherwise they lose.
