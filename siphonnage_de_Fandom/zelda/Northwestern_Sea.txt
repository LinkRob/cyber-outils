The {{Term/Store|Northwestern Sea||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 284}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|PH|Northwestern Sea}} is a quadrant in the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}} located in the northwest. The {{Term|PH|Northwestern Sea}} is initially cursed with a thick fog generated by the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} that causes all {{Plural|PH|Ship}} to become lost.<ref name="Fog">{{Cite|What?! What happened?! We were chugging along, making headway there... Bah! You think maybe... Could it be the influence of the Ghost Ship? Now what did I hear about that...oh, yeah! It's always surrounded by infernal fog! And that fog leads ships horribly astray! I think it's best that we beat a hasty retreat, Link! Maybe someone on one of the nearby islands knows a way through this fog!|Linebeck|PH}}</ref> It can only be accessed by navigating a specific route through the western side of the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea|link}}. After pursuing the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} through the fog and losing their way, {{Term|PH|Linebeck}} suggests that someone from a nearby {{Term|PH|Island}} in the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea}} might know the route.<ref name="Fog"/> The safe route through the fog can be found in the {{Term|PH|Old Wayfarer|link}}'s cave on {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}}.

The {{Term|PH|Northwestern Sea}} contains the {{Term|PH|Isle of Gust|link}}, {{Term|PH|Bannan Island|link}}, the {{Term|PH|Uncharted Island|link}}, and {{Term|PH|Zauz's Island|link}}. After rescuing all three {{Plural|PH|Fairy|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} is able to pursue and board the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}} to find {{Term|PH|Tetra|link}}'s petrified body.

{{Term|PH|Beedle|link}}'s {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship|link|display=Shop Ship}} can occasionally be found wandering the waters of the {{Term|PH|Northwestern Sea}}, though it will eventually leave for another quadrant of the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King}}. After obtaining the {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} can speak to a {{Term|PH|Golden Frog|link}} between {{Term|PH|Bannan Island}} and the {{Term|PH|Isle of Gust}} to learn the corresponding symbols that allow him to {{Term|PH|Warping|link|display=Warp}} to this location.<ref>{{Cite|Ooof! Nice shot, buddy! The big frog spread the world about you! So, want to know what to draw on the {{Color|PH Red|Cyclone Slate}} to fly right here? Here goes! Pay attention!|Golden Frog|PH}}</ref>

Once she has been encountered in the {{Term|PH|Southwestern Sea|link}}, the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck}} has a chance of running into her in all quadrants of the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King}}, including the {{Term|PH|Northwestern Sea}}. While {{Term|PH|Zauz|link}} is forging the {{Term|PH|Sword Blade|link}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} will receive a letter from {{Term|PH|Jolene}} challenging him to a duel.<ref>{{Cite|Little boy in green! So you got me, and got me good! Humiliation burns hot in my pirate blood! And so, my mind is haunted with this one, searing word... {{Color|PH Red|REVENGE!}} Yes! I demand a rematch to see who's truly the strongest on the sea! I've sharpened my skills since last we clashed. I am unbeatable! All will fall beneath my blade. Oh, yes. Somewhere on these vast seas our paths will cross again. If you have any honor at all, you will meet me in battle one last time! The mighty She-Pirate, Jolene|Jolene|PH}}</ref>
