{{Term/Store|Mastering Cryonis||HWAoC}} is a Level 8 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mastering Cryonis|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Mastering Cryonis}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} used to introduce to the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} the {{Term|HWAoC|Cryonis|link}} rune of the {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Slate|link}}. It can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillshroom|link}}, and ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillfin Trout|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must defeat 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}}. After that, a gate to an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} opens, which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture. After capturing it, a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} appears. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} is defeated. Since the  point of this {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} is to teach the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} how to use the {{Term|HWAoC|Cryonis}} rune, both the {{Term|HWAoC|Bokoblin}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin}} {{Plural|HWAoC|Boss}} perform attacks that can be countered using {{Term|HWAoC|Cryonis}}.
