{{Plural|BotW|Steel Lizal Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} mostly found in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}, where they are used by {{Plural|BotW|Black Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lizalfos|link}}. Elemental {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} such as {{Plural|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} also use them. The {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}} that wield them are known to be expert marksmen.<ref name="Inventory"/> While the metal reinforcements of {{Plural|BotW|Steel Lizal Bow}} increase their durability, they also end up adding weight.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created and utilized by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Due to their metallic components, {{Plural|BotW|Steel Lizal Bow}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Finally, {{Plural|BotW|Steel Lizal Bow}} will not burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
