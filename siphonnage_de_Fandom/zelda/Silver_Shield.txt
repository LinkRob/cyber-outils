{{Plural|BotW|Silver Shield|link}} are {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}}-made {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} known for their intricate ornamentation.<ref name="Inventory"/> It is believed that true masters of these {{Plural|BotW|Silver Shield|display=Shields}} can redirect attacks like a rock redirecting rushing water.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Silver Shield}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Zora}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref>

There are five scattered across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, a majority of which are in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} and in and around {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}. An additional two are stowed away in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}. They are found at the following locations:
* The west bridge of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}
* Within a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} underneath the throne room of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}
* Inside a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} in the middle of {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}}, off the west side of {{Term|BotW|Dah Hesho Shrine|link}}
* In a wooden {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} at the southeast shore of {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}}, north of the lone {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}}.
* A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} just off the west coast of {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}

{{Plural|BotW|Silver Shield}} are made of metal, and will therefore conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Shield}}. Though they sink in water, they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
