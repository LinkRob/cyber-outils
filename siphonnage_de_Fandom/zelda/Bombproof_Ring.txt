The {{Term/Store|Bombproof Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Bombproof Ring<br/>No damage from your own Bombs|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Bombproof Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s, stumbling into [[Maple]], or through a [[Ring Fortune]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be obtained by getting a perfect score at the gold level of the [[Goron Dance]] minigame. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it prevents damage from [[Link]]'s own [[Bomb]]s.<ref name="Description"/> The Bombproof Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
