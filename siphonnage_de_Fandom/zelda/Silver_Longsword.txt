{{Plural|BotW|Silver Longsword}} are two-handed swords made by the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}}, in spite of their preference for {{Plural|BotW|Spear|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Their unique design have made them among the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> This {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} shares its elegant, silver style with the other pieces of {{Term|BotW|Zora Gear|link}}, such as the {{Term|BotW|Silverscale Spear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 173}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Silver Longsword}} are commonly found around the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Silver Longsword}} can be found behind the {{Term|BotW|Ne'ez Yohma Shrine|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Silver Longsword}} are made of metal and as such cannot be burned when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, nor will they float in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. However, they do attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
