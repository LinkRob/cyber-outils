{{Term/Store|Mysterious Statue|Mysterious Statues|Series, TMC|plural}} are objects in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mysterious Statue|Kinstone Fusion|TMC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Statue}} are only found in the southwestern corner of {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}}, near the entrance to the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}}. There are three of them, each holding a gold {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with them. Each {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusion}} will cause one of the {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Statue}} to raise up and slam onto the ground, causing cracks to appear in the large boulder to the south; when all three {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fusions}} have been performed, the boulder shatters, allowing {{Term|TMC|Link}} to enter the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins}}.
