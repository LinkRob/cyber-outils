The '''15-Second Game''' is a [[Mini-Game]] in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP |guide= Nintendo |page= 43}}</ref>

==Location and rules==
The 15-Second Game is a [[Race (Mini-Game)|racing mini-game]] that takes place in the southern part of [[Kakariko Village]] in a yard next to the [[Quarreling Brothers]]' house. [[Link]] must [[Bomb]] the left wall of the house and walk outside to access the game. A woman informs him that he will get a reward if he reaches the end of the track in fifteen seconds or less.<ref>{{Cite|If you can reach the goal within 15 seconds, we will give you something good. Ready, Set... ...Go! |Hylian|ALttP}}</ref> Doing so, Link earns a {{Term|ALttP|Piece of Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This try your time was XX minutes XX seconds. You qualified! Congratulations! I present you with a piece of heart!|Hylian|ALttP}}</ref> If he plays again, the man at the end of the maze tells him he has no more rewards to give.<ref>{{Cite|I don't have anything more to give you. I'm sorry!|Hylian|ALttP}}</ref>

If Link enters the maze from the [[Dark World]] using the [[Magic Mirror]], the owners will not let him participate until he properly begins the game.{{Fact}}

==Trivia==
*In {{ALttP|-}}, the [[Sign]] in the yard will tell Link that if he has enough time to read it, he should go straight to the goal.<ref>{{Cite|If you have enough time to read this sign, you should go to the goal immediately!|Sign|ALttP}}</ref>
