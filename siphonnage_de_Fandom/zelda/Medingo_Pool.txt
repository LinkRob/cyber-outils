{{Term/Store|Medingo Pool||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Medingo Pool'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Medingo Pool is a small pool of lava in southeastern [[Eldin Canyon]]. It is situated right beside the road that paves the way toward the [[Southern Mine]] and [[Goron City]]. The Pool is connected by a narrow strait that flows from [[Darb Pond]] north. The Pool and the road is patrolled by a [[Guardian Stalker]].

On an isolated shore is a giant skull that conceals a [[Korok]] inside, who will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.
