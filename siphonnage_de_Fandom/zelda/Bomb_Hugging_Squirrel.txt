'''Bomb Hugging Squirrels''' are enemies in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} They appear in {{Term|FPTRR|Steamy Marsh}}. As their name implies, they are {{Plural|FPTRR|Squirrel|link}} that have taken to curling up on {{Term|FPTRR|Bomb Fruit}}. They usually spend their time sleeping on the fruit; this is the best time to attack. Attacking at any other time runs the risk of the {{Term|FPTRR|Squirrel}} setting off the bomb. The {{Term|FPTRR|Bomb Hugging Squirrel}} will explode with the bomb, causing damage to {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}}. They drop {{Term|FPTRR|Bomb Fruit}} when defeated.

Some {{Plural|FPTRR|Squirrel}} also appear on the Squirrel Wheel in the {{Term|FPTRR|Steamy Marsh}}. They will run on the wheel when touched, but they will not attack and cannot be harmed.
