{{Term/Store|Poultry Pilaf|Poultry Pilafs|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Poultry Pilafs are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Raw Bird Drumstick]], [[Hylian Rice]], a [[Bird Egg]], and [[Goat Butter]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Eating them will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The Hylian Rice is sautéed in poultry broth, and the trick to making these meals is to cook them on low heat until the rice is fluffy.<ref name="Inventory"/>

In [[Ivee's Diary]], Link can find a recipe for Poultry Pilaf.<ref>{{Cite|Today's Recipe:<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Poultry Pilaf}}|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref> [[Ivee]] lists the ingredients as a few Raw Bird Drumsticks, a little bit of Goat Butter, a suitable amount of Hylian Rice, and some Bird Eggs.<ref>{{Cite|Ingredients<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Raw Bird Drumstick}} (just toss a few in)<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Raw Bird Drumstick}} (actually, a few more)<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Goat Butter}} (measure out "a little")<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian Rice}} (one "suitable amount")<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Bird Eggs}} (a few should be fine)|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref> She asserts that the accuracy of measurements doesn't matter, since it only adds to the flavor.<ref>{{Cite|Note: Inaccurate measurements only make the dish for de-lish!|Ivee's Diary|BotW}}</ref>

Link can add a fifth ingredient when making Poultry Pilaf, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hearty ingredient. When eaten, Link has all of his Hearts healed and gains temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an energizing ingredient. Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}} can be partially refilled by eating these.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an enduring ingredient. Eating these pilafs will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a hasty ingredient. They can be eaten to boost Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a spicy ingredient. These pilafs grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding an electric ingredient. Link gains one level of [[Shock Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a mighty ingredient. When eaten, they will increase the power of Link's attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a tough ingredient. Eating them will boost Link's defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a sneaky ingredient. If consumed, Link will gain a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Poultry Pilaf''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Poultry Pilaf}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>Sautéed Hylian rice steamed in poultry broth. Cook on low heat until the rice is fluffy.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding a chilly ingredient. Eating these pilafs will grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
