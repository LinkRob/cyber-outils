{{Term/Store|Link||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is an [[Animal]] character in {{TWW}}.{{Note|In reality, the {{Term|TWWHD|Pig}}'s name matches whatever the player chooses as the hero {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s name.}}

==Biography==
Link is [[Abe]] and [[Rose]]'s pet [[Pig]].<ref name="Pet"/> The hero [[Link]] can catch a wild Pig for them before departing [[Outset Island]] at the beginning of the game, receiving a [[Red Rupee]] in exchange.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Thank you! Thank you so much! I've been thinking of getting a pet pig for a while now. The boys will be SO happy when they get home. You sweetie! OK, Link... Hold out your hand. This is your reward! Don't spend it all in one place!|Rose|TWW}}</ref> Link the Pig will be placed in a small pen near Abe and Rose's house, along with two other pigs Link can optionally give to Rose in exchange for a Red Rupee as well. Rose named the Pig after the hero as an honor.

When the Hero of Winds returns to Outset Island, the Pig will have fully grown and will be the only one left in the pen. While Rose claims that the other two pigs ran away, Zill implies that the family actually ate them.<ref>{{Cite|It was delicious.|Zill|TWW}}</ref> The color of the grown Link the Pig will depend on the color of the Pig the young hero originally caught. If no Pig was caught or at least one black pig was, Link will then be black. If only pink and speckled pigs were caught, he will be pink, and if only speckled pigs were caught, he will be speckled. Eventually, Link the Pig will have grown so much that he can only be lifted with the [[Power Bracelet]] and Link the hero can only move at the same pace as wearing the [[Iron Boots]]. He can then be taken to the black soil facing [[Mesa]]'s house where he will dig up a [[Piece of Heart]] if fed with [[All-Purpose Bait]].

Like other Pigs, Link will angrily charge and ram the [[Hylian]] Link if harmed by the latter. Despite being normally very slow, when chasing the main protagonist, he runs just as quickly as regular pigs. He will continue chasing and attacking until Link the Hero of Winds either dies, falls in water, or leaves the area. Unlike other members of his species, however, Link the Pig is capable of dealing three [[Heart]]s worth of damage with each hit due to his massive size (therefore six Hearts in [[Hero Mode]] of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD|The Wind Waker HD]]''), making this animal rather more dangerous than most [[Enemy|enemies]].

== Trivia ==

* Link the Pig is one of few creatures in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] who shares the name of the [[Link|main protagonist]]. The others are the [[Link (Goron)|Goron named after him]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', and [[Sera]]'s [[Link (Cat)|pet Cat]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]]''.
**Ironically due to Link the Pig's species, pigs are generally associated with Link the hero's archnemesis [[Ganon|Ganon,]] who appears to be an anthropomorphic boar, though he is actually a [[demon]].

* Link the Pig's three Heart damage onto the hero is the highest amount inflicted in the game.
