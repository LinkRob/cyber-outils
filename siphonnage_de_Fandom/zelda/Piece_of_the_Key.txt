==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
The Goron Elders [[Gor Amoto]], [[Gor Ebizo]], and [[Gor Liggs]] each give Link one of the three Key Shards after talking with him. When combined, they form the [[Nightmare Key|Big Key]] for the [[Goron Mines]].

==={{SS|-}}===
The five Pieces of the Key are found buried around [[Eldin Volcano]], and are found via [[Dowsing]]. Together they are used to open the door to the [[Earth Temple]].
