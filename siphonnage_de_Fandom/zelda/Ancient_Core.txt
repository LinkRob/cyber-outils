==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core}} are a rare drop from {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} in {{BotW}}. They can also appear in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} found in the overworld, within {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, and as [[amiibo]] rewards. Like other {{Plural|Series|Ancient Material|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core}} were made by the ancient {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}} 10,000 years ago. It is used as a power source for machines.<ref name="Inventory" />

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can give three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core}} to {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Slated for Upgrades|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, which will upgrade his {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You'll need {{Color|BotW Red|three ancient cores}} to power up {{Color|BotW Blue|Stasis}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, they can be used at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} as ingredients to create various pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}, including the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ancient Short Sword|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ancient Bladesaw|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Ancient Spear|link}}. They also count as {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, which means they can be used with critters in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} accepts {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core}} as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Gray Dye|link}}, or they can be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} in exchange for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}}

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Core}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
