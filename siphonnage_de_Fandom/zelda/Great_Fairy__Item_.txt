The {{Term/Store|Great Fairy||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Link]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Water}}.

Unlike Link's other Weapons, control shifts to the [[Great Fairy]] as Link is placed inside a giant [[Bottle]] the Great Fairy carries. Link is momentarily released from his Bottle when certain attacks are executed, in which the Great Fairy and Link both combine their attacks. 

==Variations==
The Lv.1 Great Fairy is the '''Great Fountain Fairy'''. The Lv.2 Great Fairy is the '''Great Forest Fairy''' and the Lv.3 Great Fairy is the '''Great Sky Fairy'''.

The Lv.★ form of the Great Fairy is the '''8-Bit Fairy''', which is only available in {{HW|-}} and {{HW|DE}}. The Weapon models the Great Fairy after the [[Fairy|Faires]] from {{TLoZ}}. In {{HW|DE|-}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Fairy replaces the Great Fairy of Tempests when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.

The Lv.4 Great Fairy is the '''Great Sky Fairy +''', which is only available in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. She appears identical to the Great Sky Fairy but is a much stronger Weapon.

The Lv.4+ Great Fairy is the '''Great Fairy of Tempests''', which only appears in {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}. As a Multi-Element Weapon, the Great Fairy of Tempests is also associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Lightning}}. She wears an orange recolor of the Great Sky Fairy's outfit and has orange hair.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
The Great Fairy takes to the sky, approaching the [[Moon (Object)|Moon]]. She directs it to crash into the ground, which crushes enemies beneath it. The attack deals damage in a circular area ahead of the Great Fairy.

===Focus Spirit===
{{Main|Focus Spirit}}
Upon activation, the Great Fairy twirls in the air and bursts with Water energy. The Focus Spirit Attack has the Great Fairy summon a miniature fountain with a wave of Fairies and blows them at her enemies. When the Focus Spirit Gauge is depleted, she flies up and releases Link from his Bottle, then comes flying past Link's way as Water energy explodes in her path, before scooping Link back into his Bottle.
