'''Turtles''' are [[enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
[[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] encounters Turtles in the [[Whirlpool]] of the [[Shrine of Water]]. Although they are enemies, Turtles have lots of [[health]], and they can take away up to a [[Heart]] and a half of Zelda's health.
