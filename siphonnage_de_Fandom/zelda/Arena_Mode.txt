{{Term/Store|Arena Mode||Series, CoH}} is a {{Term|CoH|Mode|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Arena Mode|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|CoH|Arena Mode}} is an endless version of the {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Arena|link}}, a series of increasingly-difficult combat challenges for the chosen character to face. There is no way to save between floors; if the character dies, they must start over.<ref>{{Cite|Play an infinite arena mode, where the enemies get harder and harder.<br/>If you're defeated, you'll have to start over!|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref> A {{Term|CoH|Boss|link}} will appear every six floors.
