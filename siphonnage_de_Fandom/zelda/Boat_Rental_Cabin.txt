The {{Term/Store|Boat Rental Cabin||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|-ZORA'S RIVER TO LAKE HYLIA-<br/>Ride downriver the FUN way!<br/><br/>{{Color|TP Green|BOAT RENTAL CABIN}}<br/><br/>Rental Fee: {{Color|TP Purple|20 Rupees}}|Sign|TP}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TPHD|Boat Rental Cabin}} is a house located near the upper portion of {{Term|TPHD|Zora's River|link}}. From it, {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can rent a boat for twenty {{Plural|TPHD|Rupee|link}} to play the {{Term|TPHD|Rapids Ride|link}}, a {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game|link}} run by {{Term|TPHD|Iza|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I USED to rent boats here, but then we had a rockfall and it dammed the river up.|Iza|TP}}</ref>

When {{Term|TPHD|Link}} first enters the {{Term|TPHD|Boat Rental Cabin}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Mini-Game}} is not playable because of several {{Plural|TPHD|Rock|link}} blocking the {{Term|TPHD|Zora's River|display=River}}. {{Term|TPHD|Iza}} gifts {{Term|TPHD|Link}} {{Plural|TPHD|Arrow|link}} and a full {{Term|TPHD|Bomb Bag|link}}, and he may clear out all of the {{Plural|TPHD|Rock}} by combining them into {{Plural|TPHD|Bomb Arrow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give you some bombs and arrows, so you just put 'em together and shoot bomb arrows at that thing.|Iza|TP}}</ref> The first obstacle is visible from the interior of the {{Term|TPHD|Boat Rental Cabin|display=Cabin}}; it has only one complete wall and is open to {{Term|TPHD|Zora's River}} on the inside.
