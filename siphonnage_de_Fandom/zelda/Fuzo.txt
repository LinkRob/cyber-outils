{{Term/Store|Fuzo||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 282 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Stub|PH}}
Fuzo is the apprentice of [[Eddo]].<ref>{{Cite|Hello, there! You've come to Eddo's Garage...that is, {{Color|PH Blue|Master Eddo}}'s shop! I'm Fuzo, his apprentice! And you? Oh, nice to meet you, Link. What? You want a cannon? Sorry, little guy, but we don't have any ready to sell. Not yet. Master's working on one though, so he's withdrawn to his workroom. Yup! He's gone and locked himself in there until he's done concentrating! If you really, really, really want a cannon, then go speak with the master. You can go around the island to reach the back door. Dangerous, though! The back door's never locked, so that's your ticket to meet the master! I'll be happy to open the gate that leads to the rest of the island... Follow me!|Fuzo|PH}}</ref> He can be found on [[Cannon Island]] at [[Eddo's Garage]]. He awards [[Link]] prizes for [[Battle Mode]] accomplishments. For every four [[Battle Mode#Big Plays|Big Plays]] earned in Battle Mode, Fuzo will reward Link with a [[Ship Parts#Golden Ship|Gold Ship Part]]. Since only 16 Big Plays are possible, the maximum number of rewards is four. While Link is searching for Eddo, Fuzo tells him that he needs to go around the Island's bomb garden to get to him.

==={{PH|-}} Manga===
In the {{PH|Himekawa}}, [[Link]] first meets Fuzo when he lands on [[Cannon Island]] and asks if any cannons are available for purchase.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Excuse me! Do you sell cannons?|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 64}}</ref> Fuzo introduces himself as [[Eddo]]'s apprentice and explains that Eddo is currently using them all to construct a new cannon.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Welcome! Sorry, but we don't have any cannons for sale now. Master Eddo took them all in back in order to make a new one. I'm just the apprentice, Fuzo. Nice to meet you.|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 64}}</ref> Link requests that Fuzo asks Eddo for one of the cannons,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=... Will you ask your master for us?|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 64}}</ref> but Fuzo suggests that Link should do it himself, as Eddo can be difficult to persuade.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Hmm... Master can be a little difficult. You should... ask him yourself...|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 64}}</ref> Fuzo then attempts to open the door to Eddo's work space, only to find that it is locked.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Hmm? It's locked again.|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 64}}</ref> Fuzo explains that Eddo sometimes stays in the room for extended periods of time.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= When he's working, sometimes he stays in there for a long time.|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 64}}</ref> Link and [[Ciela]]<!--who says it?--> suggests that he should shout or bang on the door,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Why don't you yell... or bang on the door?!|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 65}}</ref> but Fuzo refuses and says that Eddo is frightening when his work is interrupted.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= He's scary when you interrupt his work.|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 65}}</ref> Fuzo tells Link to try the back door,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Try the back door. Walk around the island and you'll be out back.|book= {{PH|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 65}}</ref> causing Link to hurry away. Fuzo, now alone, says that he was going to warn him about the [[Bee#Phantom Hourglass|Beehive]] that is there and remarks about how impatient Link is.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Gee, I was gonna tell him... to watch out for the beehive!
