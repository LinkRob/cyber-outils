The {{Term/Store|Maag Halan Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Maag Halan Shrine}}<br/>Maag Halan's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Maag Halan's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
Maag Halan Shrine is located in {{Term|BotW|Mido Swamp|link}} in the northeast corner of the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must first talk to {{Term|BotW|Damia|link}} at the entrance of the [[Korok Trial]], who will give Link the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}".

The Quest requires Link to travel a path set before him using the entire Korok Weapon Set: a {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Sword|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Shield|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Forest Dweller's Bow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hero guy! I was told you'd be coming. But do you really think you're ready for the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}?! Some say it's the hardest of the Korok Trials. But it's not complicated—all you need to do is equip this {{Color|BotW Red|Korok weapon set}} and reach the shrine at the back of the area. Are you ready to try it?|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Once Link reaches the end of the path without any of his equipment burning or being broken, the Shrine will reveal itself, completing the Quest.

As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan|link}}, who states that merely being able to enter the Shrine is proof of Link's worth.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Maag Halan. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Maag Halan|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
Maag Halan Shrine features no puzzles, simply a direct path to Maag Halan. Along this path is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Giant Ancient Core|link}}. As Link approaches Maag Halan, the Monk will give Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for reaching the Shrine,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Maag Halan|BotW}}</ref> and will wish Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Maag Halan|BotW}}</ref>
