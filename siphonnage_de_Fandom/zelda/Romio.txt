{{Term/Store|Romio||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref name="E 271"/>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Young Couple}}
{{Term|TMC|Romio}} lives on the eastern side of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, south of the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}, in a purple {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|link|display=House}}. {{Term|TMC|Romio}}'s {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|display=House}} is connected to the {{Term|TMC|Girl's House|link}}, where his girlfriend {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}} lives, by a small board that {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} can walk across.

{{Term|TMC|Romio}} is first seen during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}; it is his first, and he wonders where the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} are.<ref>{{Cite|This is my first ever {{Color|TMC Blue|Picori Festival}}! So...where are all the {{Color|TMC Green|Picori}}, anyway?|Romio|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Romio}} can be found inside his {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|display=House}}, where he remains for the rest of the game.

{{Term|TMC|Romio}} will mention that his sister, {{Term|TMC|Berry|link}}, is at {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}},<ref>{{Cite|What do you want? My sister's not here. She's at school.|Romio|TMC}}</ref> and comment on {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s hat (actutally {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}) and its strange appearance.<ref>{{Cite|You have a weird hat. Where can I get one of those?|Romio|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} possesses {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Romio}} mentions the intelligence of his {{Term|TMC|Dog|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Rolf|link}}, and admit that he spoils him.<ref>{{Cite|My dog's real smart. And a little spoiled, too.|Romio|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Romio}} disregards the {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|display=King}}'s strange behavior, claiming it has nothing to do with his love with {{Term|TMC|Julietta}}.<ref>{{Cite|They say the king's been weird lately. But that doesn't really have anything to do with our love...|Romio|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} has obtained the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Berry}} returns to the {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|display=House}}, and {{Term|TMC|Romio}} mentions that she is supposed to stay indoors.<ref name="Berry"/>

{{Term|TMC|Romio}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure! Let's see how well they fit!|Romio|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TMC|Romio}}, along with his girlfriend {{Term|TMC|Julietta|link}}, are a reference to a famous {{Wp|William Shakespeare}} play, ''{{Wp|Romeo and Juliet}}''.
