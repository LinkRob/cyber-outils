"{{Term/Store|Resolve and Grief||Series, BotW}}" is the third {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Resolve and Grief}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #3}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
This {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory}} can be unlocked within the grove of {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Lake Kolomo|link}}. It begins with {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}, looking down at the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} and walking through the {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} with {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} trailing her. She recounts her plans for them to head to {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, where she wants to make adjustments to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} so {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} can use it as easily as possible.<ref>{{Cite|From here, we'll make our way to Goron City. Then, we'll need some adjustments on that Divine Beast so Daruk can manage it as easily as possible.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She states that while {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} can get it to move, it is obvious there is more to learn.<ref>{{Cite|He's figured out how to get it to move... However, it's apparent that we still have much more to learn.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She reasons that since the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} were made by people, they should be able to figure out how they work.<ref>{{Cite|But to think, that Divine Beast was actually built by people... That means we should be able to understand how it works and how to use it to our advantage.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} comments that, while they do not know much about the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast}}, they are the best hope for defeating {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|These Divine Beasts...so much we don't know... But if we want to turn back the Calamity Ganon, they're our best hope.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} appears to be saddened and discouraged by her own words, stopping in her tracks. She turns back to {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks him how proficient he is with the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tell me the truth... How proficient are you right now, wielding that sword on your back?|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She recounts a legend of an [[Fi|ancient voice]] that resonates within the blade, and she asks whether {{Term|BotW|Link}} can hear it.<ref>{{Cite|Legend says that an ancient voice resonates inside it. Can you hear it yet...hero?|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>
