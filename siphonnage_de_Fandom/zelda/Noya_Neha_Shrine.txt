The {{Term/Store|Noya Neha Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Noya Neha Shrine}}<br/>A Minor Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Minor Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Noya Neha Shrine}} is located on the far end of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle West|link}}, an island in the northern part of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. It is found inside of a cave, the entrance to which is blocked by thorns. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will have to burn them away with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} before he can reach the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Noya Neha|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in the shrine... I am Noya Neha. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Noya Neha|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Noya Neha Shrine}} features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters this chamber, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}}, wielding a {{Term|BotW|Ancient Battle Axe|link}}, will rise from the pit below. To reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}.

Once the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}} has been defeated, the portcullis at the back of the room will open. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can approach {{Term|BotW|Noya Neha}} to receive a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Noya Neha|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Noya Neha|BotW}}</ref>

Behind the portcullis is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Knight's Shield|link}}.
