{{Term/Store|Razor Sword|Razor Swords|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}} are recurring [[Sword]] [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It exclusively appears in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{MM|-}}===
The Razor Sword is obtained by upgrading the [[Kokiri Sword]] at the [[Mountain Smithy]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|All right... To reforge your sword, it will cost you 100 Rupees. It'll be ready at sunrise. You'll have to let us hold onto your sword until then. Keep in mind that after you use your reforged sword 100 times, it will lose its edge and it'll be back to its original sharpness...|Zubora|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] must leave his Kokiri Sword at the smithy for an entire day before the Sword is ready. Once completed, the Razor Sword features an odd-looking double-blade with the center  resembling that of an actual razor, alongside having increased range and twice the strength. However, its edge is prone to chipping and eventually will dull after hitting objects or enemies a certain number of times. Although brittle, the Razor Sword is significantly more durable in comparison to the [[Giant's Knife]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''. After one hundred strikes, as explained by [[Zubora]], the blade will break apart, and then become the Kokiri Sword once again.<ref>{{Cite|Your Kokiri Sword has been strengthened and forged into a Razor Sword! This new, sharper blade is a cut above the rest. Use it up to 100 times without dulling its superior edge!|N/A|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Kokiri Sword you went to the trouble of sharpening is now just a plain sword again. Take it back to the swordsmith to reforge it.|N/A|MM}}</ref>

Alongside the dulling issue, this upgrade is not permanent, since if the young [[Hero of Time]] plays the [[Song of Time]] to go back to the [[First Day]], the Razor Sword will revert back to the Kokiri Sword. However, the Razor Sword can be further upgraded to the [[Gilded Sword]], which will not revert back to the starting weapon and has three times its damage.

==Other appearances==
==={{SCII|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{SCII}}, the Razor Sword is one of Link's weapons. Paired with the [[Hylian Shield]], the Razor Sword can be bought in the Regulus Shop for 600 Gold while playing the Weapon Master Mode. When Link equips the Razor Sword, his attack goes up to 125% while his defense stays the same. However, due to the Sword's short reach, Link will take 15% block damage compared to the usual 10%.<ref>{{Cite|Good offense offset by a short reach and a penetrable guard.|N/A|Soulcalibur II}}</ref>

== Trivia ==
* In the Japanese version of the game, the Razor Sword is called '''Feather Sword'''.
* The Razor Sword is rarely used by fans who played ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' and its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|remake]], due to the weapon itself being temporary unlike most other [[Sword|Swords]], prone to breaking, and can be upgraded to the permanent [[Gilded Sword]].
* As the Razor Sword deals double the damage of the [[Kokiri Sword]], it technically has the same strength as the [[Master Sword]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time.]]''
** In ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', however, although not canon to the series, while shorter in reach, actually inflicts greater damage than the Blade of Evil's Bane.
* The Razor Sword shares the [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_GiantsKnife_Break.wav same sound effect] as the [[Giant's Knife]] from ''Ocarina of Time'' when its blade breaks apart in ''Majora's Mask''. Unlike the aforementioned sword, however, this particular weapon is not visibly seen being shattered.
* The message seen when the Razor Sword's blade becomes dull and reverts back the Kokiri Sword after [[Link]] strikes 100 times reads "''The Kokiri Sword you went to the trouble of sharpening is now just a plain sword again. Take it back to the [[Mountain Smithy|swordsmith]] to reforge it.''"
