'''Steward Constructs''' are a friendly variant of the [[construct]] characters in {{TotK}}. Their purpose is to work and teach those who ask them questions. They can primarily be found on the Great Sky Island which serves as the tutorial section of the game. They can also be found on various [[Sky Islands]] throughout [[Hyrule]] as well but a few can be found on both the [[Surface]] and the [[Depths]].

== Story ==
According to [[King Rauru]], the Steward Constructs were the first kind the [[Zonai]] built. Some became so skilled that they surpassed them, and the people of [[Great Sky Island]] came to learn from them instead.<ref>:{{Big|Rauru}} at the exit of [[Pondside Cave]]<br>The {{Color|BotW Red|Steward Constructs}} were the first to be built. After that, we crafted others suited to different roles. <!-- -->{{Color|BotW Red|Culinary Constructs, Maker Constructs...}} You'll find all sorts of them still active in this place. <!-- -->Some became so skilled within their specialties that they surpassed us. We began to learn from them instead.<!-- --> You should take some time out to talk to them. They can teach you many things you might not yet know.</ref>

When [[Link]] awakens on the Great Sky Island after [[Rauru]] replaces Link's arm with his own, a Steward Construct meets him outside the [[Room of Awakening]] and hands him the [[Purah Pad]] which had been in its care for thousands of years at that point due to [[Princess Zelda]] traveling back in time and handing it to a Steward Construct to put away and take care of until the appropriate time. Later, another Steward Construct gives Link an [[Energy Cell]] which allows Link to use energy from the cells to power [[Zonai Devices]] outside of the [[Shrine of Light|Shrines of Light]].
