{{Term/Store|Toppo|Toppo|Series, ALttP}} are recurring enemies in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 210}}</ref>{{Name Ref|CoH}}

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Plural|ALttP|Toppo}} hide in {{Term|ALttP|Grass}} and leap from it straight into the air before dropping back down. They are found in the {{Term|ALttP|Great Swamp|link}} and have large ears and bear two sharp claws. They are weak, and can be defeated by any weapon in {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}}'s arsenal. However, removing a patch of {{Term|ALttP|Grass}} they are about to jump out of or land in will cause the {{Term|ALttP|Toppo}} to crash to the ground and lie there helplessly.{{Note|If {{Term|ALttP|Link}} cuts the grass around a {{Term|ALttP|Toppo}}, it may become stranded on the ground without even leaping.}} If {{Term|ALttP|Link}} approaches a stunned {{Term|ALttP|Toppo}}, it will give him either a {{Term|ALttP|Heart|link}}, {{Plural|ALttP|Arrow|link}}, {{Term|ALttP|Bomb|link}}, a big {{Term|ALttP|Magic Jar|link}}, a {{Term|ALttP|Fairy|link}}, or {{Plural|ALttP|Red Rupee|link}} and call him a thief before disappearing in a skull-shaped puff of smoke.<ref>{{Cite|All right! Take it, thief!|Toppo|ALttP}}</ref> Defeating them normally will only yield {{Plural|ALttP|Heart}} or {{Plural|ALttP|Green Rupee|link}}. They are one of the few monsters that can speak. {{Plural|ALttP|Toppo}} will no longer appear in the {{Term|ALttP|Great Swamp}} after the gate to the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} begins to open at {{Term|ALttP|Hyrule Castle|link}}.

A Toppo will jump out of the ground when a certain amount of rupees and treasures are on-screen, and will run to collect them.
