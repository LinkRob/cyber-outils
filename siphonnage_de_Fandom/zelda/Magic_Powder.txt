==Location and Uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Manual|ALttP|Magical Powder|This magical concoction has unpredictable effects on certain things. Try sprinkling it on a bush to cause a strange chemical reaction. Its effect on other living things is said to be even stranger.}}
[[File:ALttP Magic Powder Inventory Sprite.png|32px|left]]
In {{ALttP}}, Magic Powder can be obtained from [[Syrup]] after giving her the [[Magic Mushroom]], which can be found in the [[Lost Woods]]. Once [[Link]] gives her the mushroom, he must leave the area and return. Then, [[Witch's Assistant|her assistant]] ([[Maple]] in {{ALttP&FS}}) will give him the Magic Powder inside the [[Magic Shop]].

Magic Powder can dissolve [[Bush]]es,<ref name="ALttP Bushes"/> turn [[Buzz Blob]]s into [[Cukemen]], and turn [[Bubble]]s and [[Rabbit Beam]]s into [[Fairy|Fairies]]. It can also be used on various enemies, turning them into a [[Slime]]. Magic Powder can also be used to awaken the [[Mad Batter]] inside a well near the [[Dwarven Swordsmiths]]' [[Shop]]. When sprinkled on the altar, it summons the Mad Batter, who rants at Link before doubling his [[Magic Meter]].

==={{LA|-}}===
{{Manual|LA|Magic Powder|This Powder, made from the Sleepy Mushrooms of Koholint Island, has mystical powers. Try to sprinkle it on many things to test its effects. If you find a Sleepy Mushroom, take it to a witch, and have her change the mushroom into Powder for you.}}
In {{LA}}, Link can first obtain Magic Powder by giving the [[Sleepy Toadstool]] to the [[Witch]], which will turn it into Powder.<ref name="LA How to Obtain"/> It can also be won at the [[Trendy Game]]. It can be used to light [[Torch]]es, defeat certain enemies, and remove the curse put on [[Tarin]] that turned him into a raccoon. The Magic Powder initially has a maximum capacity of 20 uses, although this can be increased to 40 by the [[Mad Batter]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
In {{HW|L}}, Magic Powder appears as an [[Item Card]] for the [[Koholint Island Map]] in the Link's Awakening Pack DLC. It is used to light torches which removes locks on certain adjacent squares so that they can be opened normally.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In {{ALttP|-}}, if Link sprinkles Magic Powder onto the [[Cucco]] inside the house of one of the [[Informant Woman]] in [[Kakariko Village]], it will become a woman and scold Link for teasing the other Cuccos.<ref>{{Cite|Cluck cluck... What?! You turned me into a human. I can even speak! Aha, it must be you who is always teasing my friends. The Weathercock is always watching you harass them.|Cucco|ALttP}}</ref> If he sprinkles Magic Powder on the [[Sweeping Lady]] in front of her house, she turns into a Fairy.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALttP|publisher= Nintendo|page= 131}}</ref>
*In {{LA|-}}, if Link sprinkles Magic Powder on a small pile of bones in the [[Yarna Desert]], it will offer advice on where to find a [[Piece of Heart]] in the area.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh... Yess... That dust was so refreshing... For that, I'll tell you a hint! Use a Bomb in a place where the sand swallows you... There is a treat on the other side of the wall... Bye!|N/A|LADX}}</ref>
*In {{LA|NS}}, a glitch can be performed where the player sprinkles {{Term|LANS|Magic Powder}} on the {{Term|LANS|Raccoon|link}} and immediately pauses. The game will turn black and white and {{Term|LANS|Link}} will start rapidly losing hearts. The player can then pause the game about half a frame from the death sequence and restart the game, causing the game to crash. This crash will apparently break your [[Nintendo Switch|Switch]] console.{{Fact|BotW}}
