{{Term/Store|Pipit||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Pipit|N/A|SSHD}}</ref> He is the resident {{Term|SSHD|Bird|link}} expert of {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link}} and an acquaintance of {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm something of a bird researcher around here. So, you know, if you ever find yourself in need of some bird expertise, come see ol' {{Color|SSHD Blue|Pipit}}.|Pipit|SS}}</ref> He is a senior student at the {{Term|SSHD|Knight Academy|link}} and wears a yellow tunic, the senior uniform of the previous year.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH|publisher= |page= 73}}</ref>

{{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} has a strong sense of honor and duty and is dedicated to his work. He was the winner of the previous year's {{Term|SSHD|Wing Ceremony|link}}, winning in a close race.<ref>{{Cite|The Wing Ceremony's about to begin, right? Remember when Pipit won the last one right at the last second? It was soooo close. Now, that was a race...|Karane|SS}}</ref> He patrols around the {{Term|SSHD|Knight Academy|link}} at night with the aim of keeping {{Term|SSHD|Skyloft|link}} safe, but also to help finance his education.<ref>{{Cite|It's true. I'm working the night patrol to earn some money!|Pipit|SS}}</ref> His family does not seem to be well off financially, which has led to a strained relationship with his mother {{Term|SSHD|Mallara|link}}, who spends the {{Plural|SSHD|Rupee|link}} he gives her paying {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} to clean her home instead of buying food and doing the cleaning herself.<ref>{{Cite|How could I not be mad?! It's been obvious lately that you've been giving someone Rupees to clean the house! I gave you that money so you could buy some bread! If you keep spending money like this, I won't have any money to go to the Knight Academy! I didn't take that job patrolling just so you could live a life of luxury! You've got to stop doing this!|Pipit|SS}}</ref>

{{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} has a romantic interest in classmate {{Term|SSHD|Karane|link}}, which is revealed if {{Term|SSHD|Link}} gives her {{Term|SSHD|Cawlin's Letter|link}} and tells {{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} about it. Wishing to know what she intends to do,<ref>{{Cite|Let's just say you had an interest in how that relationship turned out. Do you think it would be best to go and ask her about it? I mean... That isn't to say that I have a personal interest here, though. I'm just interested in the emotion of love...in general...|Pipit|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} realizes he loves her and rushes over to find her just as she is giving {{Term|SSHD|Cawlin}} a reply.<ref>{{Cite|And, um... Wh-what's your...answer?|Cawlin|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} intervenes and asks her to go out with him instead,<ref>{{Cite|Wait! I think you should go out with me instead! I mean...er...I want to ask you out!|Pipit|SS}}</ref> to which she hesitantly accepts in an effort to prevent breaking {{Term|SSHD|Cawlin}}'s heart.<ref>{{Cite|Errr... I want to go out with... Pipit...|Karane|SS}}</ref> 

After that, they will begin dating. {{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} thanks {{Term|SSHD|Link}} for helping him find true love and gives him five {{Plural|SSHD|Gratitude Crystal|link}}. {{Term|SSHD|Karane}} will admire {{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} even more,<ref>{{Cite|My guy is going out on patrol again tonight. It's not even required of him--it's just something he feels he has to do... Just thinking about him makes my heart start pounding like I just ran up a flight of stairs!|Karane|SS}}</ref> and thanks {{Term|SSHD|Link}} for his role, but she still feels sorry for how things turned out for {{Term|SSHD|Cawlin}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hee hee! Thank you, Link. Thanks to you, I have an amazing new boyfriend! But I feel kind of bad about Cawlin, you know?|Karane|SS}}</ref> Afterwards, by sitting on a bench seat in the front row of the classroom, {{Term|SSHD|Link}} can listen to {{Term|SSHD|Karane}} and {{Term|SSHD|Pipit}} discussing their relationship as well as how {{Term|SSHD|Karane}} wants to visit his mother,<ref>{{Cite|I guess I should hustle over and say hello to your mother too. Uhhhh, that's moving a bit too fast, don't you think?|Pipit|SS}}</ref>
