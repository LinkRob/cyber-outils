{{Term/Store|Red Water of Life|Red Water of Life|Series|plural}},{{Exp Game|Series}}<ref name="E 142">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 142 {{Exp Game|Series, TLoZ}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|2nd Potion|2nd Potions|TLoZ|plural}},{{Exp Game|TLoZ}}<ref name="E 142"/> are items in {{TLoZ}}.{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Red Water of Life}} were also referred to as '''Water of Life''' in the manual.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Drink the Water of Life to fully replenish your health. ==Locations and Uses==
{{Manual|TLoZ|Water of life|Drink this and all of Link's LIFE hearts will turn red. Red water turns blue when Link sips it. Drink the blue water and that's the last of the water.}}
[[File:TLoZ Potion Shop.png|thumb|left|A {{Term|TLoZ|Shop}} selling a {{Term|TLoZ|2nd Potion}}]]
2nd Potions are red, bottled drinks and a counterpart of [[Blue Water of Life]]; both become available for purchase from an [[Old Woman]] in a [[Cave]] after [[Link]] has shown one of them the [[Letter]], and the 2nd Potion costs 68 [[Rupee]]s. Alternatively, 2nd Potions can obtained from [[Old Man|Old Men]] who offer Link a choice between a [[Heart Container]] and a 2nd Potion.<ref>{{Cite|TAKE ANY ONE YOU WANT.|Old Man|TLoZ}}</ref> However, if Link chooses the 2nd Potion, he will permanently miss the Heart Container. If Link drinks a 2nd Potion, it will restore all of his [[Heart]]s and transform into a [[Life Potion]].
