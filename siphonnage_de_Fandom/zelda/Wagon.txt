==={{BotW|-}}===
The remains of {{Plural|BotW|Wagon}} can be found scattered across [[Hyrule]] in {{BotW}}. {{Plural|BotW|Wagon}} primarily appear along the roadside and can be searched for [[Material]]s.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Wagons appear as pre-built vehicles at certain stations, primarily near {{Plural|TotK|stable|link}}. They can be towed with {{Plural|TotK|horse|link}} equipped with a {{Term|TotK|Towing Harness|link}} to pull them or modified with {{Term|TotK|Zonai Devices|link}} or other objects for specific features. Most available wagons at said stations are free to use, but there are some personally owned by individuals that cannot be taken.

In a few situations, Link is required to escort certain people in wagons to progress. Once asked that Link will escort them, they will board the wagon waiting to reach their destinations. If the wagon falls at an unsafe angle, the passenger(s) will panic and leave, resulting in Link and his passengers' position being reset.

The {{Term|TotK|Stable Troupe|link}} owns a personal wagon used for their travels. {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} often use them to escort them to reawaken sleeping {{Plural|TotK|Great Fairy|link}}.
