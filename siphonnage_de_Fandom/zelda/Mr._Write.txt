{{Term/Store|Mr. Write||Series, LA, LADX}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Mr. Write|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Manual|LA|Mr. Write|He loves reading and is always writing letters. People say he is writing to a charming young woman in a city far away. Doesn't he look a little bit familiar?}}
Mr. Write is a lonely letter-writer who sends countless letters to various people, but never gets any replies.<ref>{{Cite|Well... I pretty much stick to myself, me and my letters... My name's Write! The only thing I don't like about my hobby is that I never receive a response... |Mr. Write|LADX}}</ref> He lives in a house on the border of the [[Mysterious Woods]].<ref>{{Cite|I would like you to take this letter to a Mr. Write who lives on the border of the Mysterious Forest, please! |Christine|LADX}}</ref> He is part of the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}. Fittingly, the item [[Link]] delivers to him is a [[Letter#Link's Awakening|Letter]] from [[Christine]], a resident of the [[Animal Village]].<ref>{{Cite|Please! I really must insist you have this {{Icon|LADX Letter}}! |Mr. Write|LADX}}</ref> He gives Link a [[Broom]] in exchange, seemingly because he has nothing better to offer.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm... She's so beautiful... I must give you something for your trouble... Hmm... Well, it looks like all I have is this broom... how'll that be? |Mr. Write|LADX}}</ref> When the letter is delivered, it is revealed that it is a love letter, and that Christine has enclosed a photograph.<ref>{{Cite|What's this?! A letter for me?! I'm so happy! ...And look! The letter came with a photograph! |Mr. Write|LADX}}</ref> Although Mr. Write believes the photo to be of Christine herself, it is actually an image of {{Smw|Princess Peach}} from the {{Smw|Mario (series)|''Mario'' series}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Broom as your reward from Mr. Write! But that photo was not of... |N/A|LADX}}</ref> This is a reference to long distance relationships, since she is lying about what she looks like and Mr. Write believes he is writing to a beautiful woman.<ref>{{Cite|Hello! I'm writing back to Christine now! |Mr. Write|LADX}}</ref>

[[File:LADX Bird Sprite.png|left]]
Mr. Write owns an orange [[Bird]] (also the color of Christine's bow) while Christine owns a green bird (also the color of Mr. Write's hair).

==Trivia==
* Mr. Write is based on the near-identical advisor Dr. Wright from the SNES port of ''SimCity'', himself an avatar of ''SimCity'' and ''The Sims'' creator (and real human being) Will Wright.<ref>https://gamehistory.org/simcity/</ref> An adaptation of his theme from that game plays in his home.<ref>https://nintendo.fandom.com/wiki/Dr._Wright</ref>
** The SNES ''SimCity'' port itself replaces the generic "disaster" event monster with Bowser, fitting neatly with the other Mario franchise references littered throughout the dream world of Link's Awakening.
* A similar character later appeared in {{OoS}}, going by the pun of a name [[Dr. Left]]. The latter also a makes a returning appearance in {{TMC}}.

==Nomenclature==
His name is a reference to the fact that he loves writing letters.
