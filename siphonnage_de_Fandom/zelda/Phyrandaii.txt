==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TFoE|Phyrandaii}} are flaming, wheel-like {{Plural|TFoE|Enemy|link}} that either fly through the air towards {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} in {{TFoE|-}}, or roll along the ground, often in small groups of two to three, in {{TWoG|-}}. The {{Plural|TWoG|Phyrandaii}} that roll will only move forward, and will not turn around and give chase should {{Term|TWoG|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}} manage to jump over one.

Under most circumstances, Phyrandaii are invincible enemies that cannot be harmed. In {{TFoE|-}} only, {{Term|TFoE|Link}} can throw {{Plural|TFoE|Snowball|link}} to dispatch them, as all other attacks will have no effect on them. As they are treated as flying {{Plural|TFoE|Enemy}} in {{TFoE|-}}, the {{Term|TFoE|Bell|link}} can also be rung to stun them for a short period of time. As {{Plural|TFoE|Snowball}} are not present in {{TWoG|-}}, they cannot be defeated and instead must be avoided. Zelda can easily pass them by unharmed using the {{Term|TWoG|Magic Cape|display=Magic Cloak|link}}
