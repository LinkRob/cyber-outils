{{Term/Store|Tyto||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a character in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tyto|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= His name is {{Color|SS Blue|Tyto}}. He is a member of a highly civilized race of moles called the Mogma. All Mogmas have a deep and abiding love of treasure.
}}

Tyto is a blue-haired [[Mogma]]. When Link enters [[Eldin Volcano]], he can dig Tyto out of his burrow with the [[Digging Mitts]], frightening the Mogma.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! You scared me silly! You can't just start digging right above my head, you know?|Tyto|SS}}</ref> Tyto then dismisses Link, stating that it is impossible to stop digging when what could be found is unknown.<ref>{{Cite|But I get where you're coming from. You never know what you're going to find when you dig, so it's impossible to stop!|Tyto|SS}}</ref> He tells Link about what he might find while digging with the Digging Mitts.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|SS Red|Rupees}} are the most common treasure you'll dig up, but sometimes you might even score {{Color|SS Red|Eldin Ore}} or some {{Color|SS Red|hearts}}!|Tyto|SS}}</ref> The Mogma encourages the hero to dig whenever there is an opportunity.<ref>{{Cite|So if you run across a hole and you've got the time, dig, dig, dig!|Tyto|SS}}</ref> If Link speaks to Tyto again, he will warn Link about [[Chuchu|red, blobby things]] that will sometimes come from burrows.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hey! Sometimes these {{Color|SS Red|red, blobby things}} will pop out too. Disgusting! Be careful to not let'em grab you.|Tyto|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Tyto’s name is derived from the metal {{Wp|titanium}}.

Tyto’s and [[Nackle]]'s names were interchanged in the North American, Latin American Spanish and French localizations. His Japanese name, {{Romanize|チッタ|Chitta}}, is derived from {{Romanize|チタン|chitan}}, Japanese for {{Wp|titanium}}. His name is also derived from titanium in the Canadian French and German versions.
