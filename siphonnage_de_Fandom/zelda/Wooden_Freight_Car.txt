The {{Term/Store|Wooden Freight Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wooden Freight Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Wooden Freight Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Wood Heart|link}}, two {{Plural|ST|Demon Fossil|link}} and one {{Term|ST|Pearl Necklace|link}}. Its description reads "This wooden car catches your eye, but it's easy on the wallet."

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Wooden Freight Car}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Wooden Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to six Hearts.
