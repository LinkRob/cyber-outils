The {{Term/Store|Fairy Queen||Series, LANS, FSA}},{{Exp Game|LANS, TWW, FSA}}<ref>{{Cite|Fairy Queen|N/A|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 233 {{Exp Game|TWWHD}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 23 {{Exp Game|FSA}}}}</ref> also known as the {{Term/Store|Queen Fairy||OoA}},{{Exp Game|OoA}}<ref name="OoA">{{Cite|Please listen, young one! You may not believe this, but I am the Queen Fairy, guardian of this sea! The curse of an evil one named Veran has made me appear so!|Queen Fairy|OoA}}</ref> the {{Term/Store|Queen of the Fairies||TWW, TWWHD}},{{Exp Game|TWW}}<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 23 {{Exp Game|TWWHD}}}}</ref> and the '''Queen of Fairies''',{{Exp Game|TWW}}<ref name="TWW">{{Cite|Today I have prepared a pictograph of the Queen of Fairies.|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref> is the Fairy that rules over all of the [[Fairy|Fairies]] and [[Great Fairy|Great Fairies]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. The {{Term|Series|Fairy Queen}} goes unnamed in {{LA|DX}}. They are known to be much more powerful than the Great Fairies, and as such, they can empower [[Link]] with abilities that no Great Fairy can bestow upon the young hero.<ref name="Figurine"/> Various Fairy Queens have appeared throughout the series, with their appearances changing drastically between each game.

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Venus}}
[[File:ALttP Venus Sprite.png|left]]
In {{ALttP}}, a Fairy Queen named Venus lives in the [[Pond of Happiness]] located inside a cave on an island in the middle of [[Lake Hylia]].
{{Clear}}

==={{LA|DX|-}}===
[[File:Thefairyqueen ladx.png|right|thumb|The Fairy Queen of {{LA|DX|-}} in her fountain]]
In {{LA|DX}}, a [[Great Fairy]] who is proclaimed as the Fairy Queen can be found within the [[Color Dungeon]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 148}}</ref> She is residing in the last room in a pool, and as thanks for visiting her, she will offer to grant [[Link]] the "power of color" by changing his green tunic into either a [[Red Clothes]] or a [[Blue Clothes]]. The Red Tunic will increase the offense of Link's weapons, whereas the Blue Tunic will increase his defense against attacks.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, Link . I admire you for coming this far. I will give you the power of color. If you want offense, choose red. If you want defense choose blue. Which power do you want?|Great Fairy|LADX}}</ref> Link can revisit the Fairy Queen to change between the two tunics, but he can never change back into his green tunic. However, in the Nintendo Switch version of Link's Awakening, Link can re-visit the Color Dungeon to have the Great Fairy change his tunic to either the tunic he didn't choose (after initially completing the Color Dungeon, then as long as he is wearing the red/blue tunic from that point onward), or he can change back into his original green tunic (if he's not already wearing it) as much as he desires. For example, after initially beating the Color Dungeon, Link has the choice of either the red tunic or the blue tunic. From the first re-visit onward, Link can choose the red tunic or green tunic (if he's wearing the blue tunic), the blue tunic or green tunic (if he's wearing the red tunic), or the red or blue tunic (if he's wearing the green tunic). The Fairy Queen's design in the Nintendo Switch version also has a red dress, and her color scheme is similar to that of her appearance in {{OoA|-}}, but with yellow antennae and yellow wings with orange tips.

==={{OoA|-}}===
The Fairy Queen in {{OoA}} is known as the guardian of the sea and lives near the [[Eyeglass Island Library]], protecting the waters of the [[Zora Seas]].<ref name="OoA"/> When Link first meets her, she has been transfigured by [[Veran]] into an [[Octorok]], and informs the young hero that only by sprinkling the [[Fairy Powder]] on her can she return to her normal appearance.<ref>{{Cite|The magic of Fairy Powder can turn me back to normal. Veran has poisoned the sea. Soon all living things in it will perish! Please go find some Fairy Powder!|Fairy Queen|OoA}}</ref> Link finds the powder and uses it on her, restoring her as the Fairy Queen and cleansing the sea of Veran's evil influence.<ref>{{Cite|You brought me Fairy Powder! Now I can return to normal! Sprinkle the powder on me! Thank you, young one! Now I can cleanse the seas of evil!|Fairy Queen|OoA}}</ref>

Curiously, the Fairy Queen from {{OoA|-}} has the same appearance as the Great Fairies from this and other portable ''Zelda'' games. The only difference between them is their color: the Fairy Queen is red while other Great Fairies are orange.
{{Clear|left}}

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Queen of Fairies}}
The Queen of the Fairies in {{TWW}} is located in the [[Mother and Child Isles]],<ref name="TWW"/> which can be accessed only by using the [[Ballad of Gales]] and after rescuing [[Aryll]] from the [[Forsaken Fortress]]. She rewards Link with the ability to wield [[Fire Arrow]]s and [[Ice Arrow]]s.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TWWHD Light Blue|I like you. And so, I shall give new power to the {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|bow}} that you wield.}}|Queen of the Fairies|TWWHD}}</ref>

Unlike previous Fairy Queens, she has the voice, appearance, and mannerisms of a child, although it's unknown if this is her true form. The mechanical sounds in her variation of the Great Fairy music, her puppetry of a Great Fairy doll, and a "cut puppet strings" motion when she disappears cast doubt on this. She also holds a doll resembling a Great Fairy, which is what she uses to grant the young hero the Fire and Ice Arrows. After the Queen of the Fairies hands over the powerful Arrows, she flirts with Link by saying that he is just her type,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TWWHD Light Blue|Child... I must tell you... You are just my type. Tee hee hee!}}|Queen of the Fairies|TWWHD}}</ref> which embarrasses him.

==={{FSA|-}}===
[[File:Fairyqueenfsa.png|thumb|right|The Queen of Fairies as seen in {{FSA|-}}]]
The Queen of Fairies also makes a small cameo in {{FSA}} during the [[Hyrule Castle (Four Swords Adventures)|Hyrule Castle]] level. She had heard the [[Maiden|Shrine Maidens]]' cries from afar and came to the castle in an attempt to help them, but found that the castle was overtaken by fiends who captured her and split her into two [[River Zora|Zora]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I heard the maidens' cries and came running to find the castle a nest of fiends. They captured me and split me in two. That's why I look so horrible right now. Please, I beg of you. Reunite my two halves! Make me whole again!|Fairy Queen|FSA}}</ref> She asks the four Links to find her other half, and when he does, they reunite and reveal themselves to be the Fairy Queen,<ref name="FSA">{{Cite|I didn't think I would ever be allowed to reclaim my true form as a Great Fairy again! Thank you so much, brave ones!|Fairy Queen|FSA}}</ref> who easily obliterates the barrier and soldiers guarding the entrance to the castle.<ref>{{Cite|Uh-oh! It looks like those soldiers have noticed us. I'll take care of this!|Fairy Queen|FSA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So this is the magical barrier that barred your way? Allow me to take care of this.|Fairy Queen|FSA}}</ref> As thanks for helping her, the Queen of Fairies promises the four Links to tell the other Great Fairies to help them whenever needed.<ref>{{Cite|I'll let all of my friends throughout Hyrule know... "If you run into four travelers with one face, tend to their weariness and ease the burden of their travels!"|Fairy Queen|FSA}}</ref>

Even though the Fairy Queen has a similar appearance as the one from {{TWW|-}}, she curiously refers to herself as a Great Fairy after she recovers her true form.<ref name="FSA"/> The power used by the Fairy Queen during the Hyrule Castle level is the first and only time thus far that allows the audience to catch a glimpse of the tremendous power that Fairy Queens possess.
