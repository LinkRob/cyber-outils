The {{Term/Store|Northern Icehouse||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Northern Icehouse'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}} is an underground ice depository north of [[Gerudo Town]].<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}} is an ice depository that was made by digging a hole in the ground.|Jules|BotW}}</ref> It is managed by a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} woman named {{Term|BotW|Anche|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Seeing as we live in a desert, it's pretty important stuff for us Gerudo... So I manage this place 24 hours a day.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}} is used to store the ice brought down from the [[Gerudo Highlands]].<ref>{{Cite|It sounds like they cut ice from the nearby {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Highlands}}, take it there, and then store it.|Jules|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must retrieve ice from it in the [[Shrine Quest]], "[[The Perfect Drink]]". All of the drinks served at [[The Noble Canteen]] are made with the ice from the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, a big part of the drink is the ice we use from the {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}}. It makes them especially refreshing.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|All of the drinks here are made using ice from the nearby {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}}.|Jules|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to melt a formation of ice inside the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse}}, or if she detects any fire inside the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|display=Icehouse}}, {{Term|BotW|Anche}} will scold him for damaging her supply of ice before kicking him out.<ref>{{Cite|You! What's the idea, bringing that fire into the icehouse?! Get out of here with that! You're gonna ruin our supply!|Anche|BotW}}</ref>
