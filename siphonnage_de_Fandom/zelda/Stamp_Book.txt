The {{Term/Store|Stamp Book||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Stamp Book}} is given to {{Term|ST|Link|link}} by {{Term|ST|Niko|link}} after taking {{Term|ST|Alfonzo|link}} back to {{Term|ST|Aboda Village|link}}. {{Term|ST|Niko}} wants {{Term|ST|Link}} to help him collect {{Plural|ST|Stamp (Item)|link}} from the 20 {{Plural|ST|Stamp Station|link}} scattered across {{Term|ST|New Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Since you can travel all over the world by train now... Would you mind doing a favor for ol' Niko here? Would you use this stamp book to collect those stamps for me?|Niko|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Niko}} reveals to {{Term|ST|Link}} that he wished he had it when he was traveling around the world, as he often found {{Plural|ST|Stamp (Item)}}, but had nothing to put them in.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, collecting these stamps was a way to record my travels. I loved those little stamps more than anything! But back then... I didn't have anything with me to stamp them in. It was a sad state of affairs.|Niko|ST}}</ref>

Whenever {{Term|ST|Link}} finds a {{Term|ST|Stamp Station}}, he can use it to mark the location's {{Term|ST|Stamp}} on one of the remaining blank pages in the {{Term|ST|Stamp Book}}. The date on which the {{Term|ST|Stamp}} was collected, according to the [[Nintendo DS]] internal clock, is also recorded on the page. {{Term|ST|Link}} can view the entire {{Term|ST|Stamp Book}} at any time through the {{Term|ST|Collection|link}} menu.
