==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Warm Darner}}
Warm Darners can be found in open areas of the [[Akkala Highlands]] and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. They're also sold by [[Maypin]] and by {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} at [[Gerudo Canyon Stable]], [[Rito Stable]], [[Serenne Stable]], and [[Snowfield Stable]]. Combining one with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a [[Cooking Pot]] will result in a [[Spicy Elixir]], which grants {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} temporary [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] accepts Warm Darners as an ingredient for Red [[Dye]].

The [[Side Quest]] "[[Little Sister's Big Request]]" requires Link to gather Warm Darners and other dragonflies for [[Jana]] as [[Gleema]]'s birthday present.<ref>{{Cite|When I grow up, I wanna travel the world and meet some real {{Color|BotW Blue|dragonflies}}!|Gleema|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well, this is her birthday wish... I didn't have a choice.|Jana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Bring me a {{Color|BotW Blue|warm darner}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cold darner}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|electric darner}}, please.|Jana|BotW}}</ref> However, Jana will become disgusted by the dragonflies and asks Link to deliver them in her stead.<ref>{{Cite|BAH! I CAN'T DO THIS! Please give them to her...just...give them to Gleema yourself!|Jana|BotW}}</ref> Jana will give Link one hundred {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} upon completing this quest.
