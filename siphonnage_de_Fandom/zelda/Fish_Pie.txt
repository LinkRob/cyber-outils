{{Term/Store|Fish Pie|Fish Pies|Series, BotW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fish Pie}}<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Fish Pie}}<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Spoils|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Fish Pies are made in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} by combining [[Tabantha Wheat]], [[Goat Butter]], [[Rock Salt]], and any type of Seafood. This includes {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Crab|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}. Either {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Bass|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp|link}} must be used to make regular Fish Pies. They can be eaten to restore four {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}, but they can also be sold for 40 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. They're considered a mainstay in the homes of fishermen, and their crisp crust is known to pair well with their fishy flavor.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Depending on what ingredients are used and whether additional ingredients are added, Fish Pies can be of the following variations:

* '''Hearty Fish Pie''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Fish Pie}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They can be made by using a {{Term|BotW|Hearty Bass|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hearty Blueshell Snail|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hearty Salmon|link}}, or other hearty ingredient. Eating one restores all of Link's Hearts and grants him temporary additional Hearts as well.<ref name="Hearty"/> They also sell for 70 Rupees.

* '''Energizing Fish Pie''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Fish Pie}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Staminoka Bass|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}}, or other energizing ingredients. When eaten, they'll replenish some of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They heal Link for four Hearts, and they sell for 50 or 70 Rupees, depending on the ingredient used.

* '''Enduring Fish Pie''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Fish Pie}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring-type ingredients to the recipe, such as {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. They restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel when eaten.<ref name="Enduring"/> They restore six Hearts to Link's Life Gauge and sell for 60 Rupees.

* '''Hasty Fish Pie''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Fish Pie}}<br/>Grants a mid-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding speed-type ingredients, such as {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}. If they're eaten, they'll temporarily increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/> They also restore two Hearts to his Life Gauge, but they can also be sold for 70 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Fish Pie''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Fish Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level cold resistance.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Sizzlefin Trout|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}}. Eating one grants Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Spicy"/> They'll heal four of Link's Hearts, and sell for 40 to 50 Rupees.

* '''Electro Fish Pie''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Fish Pie}}<br/>Grants mid-level electricity resistance.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Term|BotW|Voltfin Trout|link}} or other electricity-related ingredients. When consumed, these pies will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/> They heal four Hearts in Link's Life Gauge and sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Fish Pie''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Fish Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Razorclaw Crab|link}}, or other attack boosting ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They heal four Hearts, but they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Tough Fish Pie''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Fish Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Armored Carp|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ironshell Crab|link}}, or other defensive ingredients. Eating them temporarily increases Link's defensive abilities.<ref name="Tough"/> They also heal Link for four Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Fish Pie''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Fish Pie}}<br/>Grants a mid-level stealth boost.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stealthfin Trout|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sneaky River Snail|link}}, or other stealth ingredients. When eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities will be temporarily boosted.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They heal four Hearts in his Life Gauge, but they sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Fish Pie''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Fish Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level heat resistance.<br/>A mainstay in any fisherman's home, the crisp crust pairs well with the fishy flavor.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Chillfin Trout|link}} or other icy ingredients. Eating one temporarily increases Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/> They restore four of Link's Hearts, and they can be sold for 50 Rupees.
