{{Term/Store|Purple Chu Jelly|Purple Chu Jelly|Series, TP, TPHD}} is an item in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 135}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Purple Chu Jelly can be obtained in the [[Lakebed Temple]], [[Hyrule Field]], [[Snowpeak Ruins]], and [[Cave|Caves]]. After defeating a [[Purple Chu]], Link can collect Purple Chu Jelly in an [[Empty Bottle]]. Drinking it will have a random effect on Link’s [[Life Gauge]]. Usually it drains one [[Heart]], but it can occasionally restore one or all of Link's Hearts. Purple Chu Jelly can also drain Link's health down to a quarter of a Heart.
