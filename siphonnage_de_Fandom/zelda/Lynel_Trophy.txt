==Location and Uses==
Lynel Trophies are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's [[Scenario]] battle or a [[Challenge]], with a Trophy earned for every [[Lynel]] kill (be they regular, [[Blue-Maned Lynel|Blue-Maned]], [[White-Maned Lynel|White-Maned]], [[Silver Lynel|Silver]], [[Fire Lynel|Fire]], [[Ice Lynel|Ice]], and [[Electric Lynel|Electric]]) a Warrior obtains.

Lynel Trophies can be used to either complete [[Side Quest#Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|Quest]]s or be used in the Hyrule Blacksmith Guild workshop to de rust Rusted Weapons or remove Seals on a weapon. However, due to the rarity and work needed to kill a Lynel, it's not recommended to use it with the Blacksmiths.
