{{Term/Store|Meaty Rice Balls|Meaty Rice Balls|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Meaty Rice Balls}} are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} predominantly {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}} together with some form of  meat. If consumed by {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, they will restore his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} by at least four {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Meaty Rice Balls}} have a sweet and spicy taste.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add additional ingredients to the recipe for Meaty Rice Balls, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll heal all of [[Link|Link's]] Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. Eating them will refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. They can be eaten to completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Eating these rice balls will temporarily increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Consuming them will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. Link can eat them to gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. If eaten, Link gains a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They increase Link's defense when they're eaten.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Meaty Rice Balls''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Meaty Rice Balls}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The sweet and spicy meat stuffed into these rice balls will keep you full for some time.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. When eaten, they grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
