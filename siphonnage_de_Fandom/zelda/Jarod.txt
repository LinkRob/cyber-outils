==Biography==
In the past, {{Term|BotW|Jarod}} and {{Term|BotW|Lasli|link}} were a pair of lovers in a secret relationship that not even {{Term|BotW|Lasli}}'s {{Term|BotW|Claree|link|display=sister}} knew about.<ref name="Secret"/> {{Term|BotW|Jarod}} vowed to always protect her; this came at the cost of his life when a monster attacked.<ref name="Sleeps">{{Cite|This is...where the love of my life sleeps.
