The '''Royal Shield''' {{Exp Game|BotW, TotK}} is a recurring item in {{TLoZ|series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Shield}}<br/>A shield issued to the Hyrulean royal family's immediate guard detail. It boasts a high defense, but these days it's more a collector's item due to its ornamentation.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> It belongs to the [[Royal Gear]] equipment and is associated with the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].

Chief among metal {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} are the {{Plural|BotW|Royal Shield}}, whose {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}} and defensive power outshine many others. Before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, these {{Plural|BotW|Royal Shield|display=Shields}} were issued to the immediate guard detail of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear|link}} equipment archetype, a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} associated with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|display=Royal Family}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> Though they have an incredibly high defense, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Shield}} are considered collector's items due to their ornamentations.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Being rare, there are only nine {{Plural|BotW|Royal Shield}} scattered across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, with a tenth in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}. Of these, six are held by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}}. Some {{Plural|BotW|Royal Shield}} with consistent locations include:
* Within a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} under {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Faron Woods|link}}
* Beneath a spiral staircase in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Shield}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|Series|Toon Zelda|link}} ({{TWW}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

A {{Term|BotW|Royal Shield}} can be obtained as a reward from {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}} for completing the {{Term|BotW|Shield-Surfing (Mini-Game)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} with a time less than 1:30.<ref>{{Cite|A shiny new record deserves a shiny new shield. Here y'go!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Selmie}} with a {{Term|BotW|Royal Shield|link}} equipped, she will take note. She points out the one she has hanging up and claims it is the best {{Term|BotW|Shield}} she has seen for {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh, a royal shield! I have one hanging up in my cabin, too. It's hands down the best design for surfing I've ever seen!|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Shield}} are made of metal, and therefore conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} also works on them, allowing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pick up and manipulate them.
