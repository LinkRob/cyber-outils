{{Term/Store|Beedle's Airshop||Series, SS}},{{Exp Game|SS}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= SS|publisher= Prima|page= 8}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Beedle's Air Shop||ST}},{{Exp Game|ST}}<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh, valued customer! We have everything you need at Beedle's Air Shop!|Beedle|ST}}</ref> is a shop run by [[Beedle]].

==Features and Overview==
==={{ST|-}}===
{{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop}} is operated in a hot-air balloon and opens after [[Link]] completes the [[Snow Temple]] in {{ST}}, at which point the young hero will receive a [[Letter]] from Beedle inviting him to visit his store. Beedle's current location is marked in the overworld's [[Rail Map]].

As Link is traveling around the four realms, he can get Beedle's attention by blowing the [[Spirit Train]]'s whistle when his hot-air balloon is nearby. He will land by the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}}, allowing the young hero to stop by it to visit his shop. Similarly to the [[Beedle's Shop Ship]], Beedle offers Link a membership at his shop after he buys his first item. The membership allows Link to save up 1 point for every 10 {{Term|ST|Rupee|link}} he spends in the store, which lends itself to certain rewards (including membership upgrades) after Link passes point milestones. After the young hero obtains the [[Silver Membership]], he will be rewarded with a [[Freebie Card]], which will allow him to obtain any item from his shop for free, although no points will be gathered if this card is used.<ref>{{Cite|You got a Freebie Card! Choose any item you want, and you'll get it for free!|N/A|ST}}</ref> Accumulating 500 points will reward Link with the [[Gold Membership]] along with a [[Heart Container]] for being such a frequent shopper.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! You have 500 points! You're now an official Gold Member! That means you get 20% off all merchandise AND a lovely prize!|Beedle|ST}}</ref>

{{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop}} is the only place where Link can buy a [[Bomb Bag]], which is set at a price of 500 {{Plural|ST|Rupee}}. Other than this, Beedle also offers [[Red Potion|Red]] and [[Potion#Purple Potion|Purple Potions]] as well as [[Bomb]]s and a variety of [[Treasure]]s. Unlike other shops in [[Hyrule]], Treasures can only be bought once, meaning that Link will have to wait a day before Beedle restocks.

Link can take a boy from [[Aboda Village]] to {{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop}} to receive a [[Force Gem]], which will reveal a new set of {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} in the [[Forest Realm]].

When exiting {{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop}}, it will fly up when Link starts getting further. If Link shoots it with the [[Cannon#Spirit_Tracks|Cannon]], it will immediately fly up instead of waiting for Link to be far enough.

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Term|SS|Beedle's Airshop}} flies over [[Skyloft]] during the daytime in {{SS}}. Beedle has to pedal to keep his shop aloft. In order to climb into the shop, Link must ring the bell that hangs at the bottom of the aircraft either with his [[Slingshot]], [[Beetle (Item)|Beetle]], [[Clawshot]], or [[Arrow]]s in order for a rope to drop down and pull him up. Once inside, Link is able to purchase items or take a nap at the far end of the room. If Link fails to purchase anything after viewing one of the items, Beedle will release a trap door underneath him,<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh! Please wait a second! Do you know how heavy you are?! I have to pedal extra hard with you up here! Then you have the gall to leave without buying anything? Off with you!|Beedle|SS}}</ref> causing him to fall to the ground.

There is a sidequest in which Link retrieves Beedle's pet, a Horned Colossus Beetle, after winning in the minigame run by [[Strich]] in [[Bug Rock]]. By giving the insect back to Beedle, Link not only receives five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s, but is also discounted the price of a single purchase by 50%, useful to buy the most expensive items at a cheaper price.
