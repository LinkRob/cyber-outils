{{Term/Store|Phantom Rider|Phantom Riders|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 197}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Phantom Riders only appear during the horseback fight against [[Ganondorf]] in [[Hyrule Field]]. They can be summoned by Ganondorf after releasing a bolt of energy. Phantom Riders charge at [[Link]] five at a time, knocking him off [[Epona]] if hit. They disappear after charging until summoned again by Ganondorf. Link can safely avoid them by riding in between or by attacking with his [[Sword]] just before they ram him.
