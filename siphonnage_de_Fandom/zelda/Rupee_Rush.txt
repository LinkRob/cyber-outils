{{Term/Store|Rupee Rush||Series, ALBW}} is a {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{ALBW}}, appearing in both {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}} and {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}.

==Location and Rules==
During the {{Term|ALBW|Mini-Game|link}}, {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} must collect as many {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}} as possible before the time runs out.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give you 30 seconds to grab as many Rupees as you can.|Rupee Rush Guy|ALBW}}</ref> Based on how close to the time limit {{Term|ALBW|Link}} finishes the game, multipliers are added to the score. The catch of the game consists in not showing the timer on-screen, so that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must guess when the time runs out.<ref>{{Cite|But here's the twist... There's no timer!|Rupee Rush Gal|ALBW}}</ref> If {{Term|ALBW|Link}} finishes the game after the 30 second time limit, he will not receive any of the {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} that he collected.<ref>{{Cite|You have 30 seconds from the start to grab as many Rupees as you can! If your time exceeds 30 seconds, you forfeit all the Rupees you collected, so speak to me before then, OK?|Rupee Rush Gal|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Talk to me within 30 seconds, or I'll take back all the Rupees you pick up. Good luck rushing for Rupees!|Rupee Rush Gal|ALBW}}</ref>

In {{Term|ALBW|Hyrule|link}}, the game is run by the {{Term|ALBW|Rupee Rush Gal|link}} who charges {{Term|ALBW|Link}} 50 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} per game.<ref>{{Cite|Do you want to play Rupee Rush? It's 50 Rupees for one go!|Rupee Rush Gal|ALBW}}</ref> Some {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} can be found hidden beneath stone tiles or trapped inside {{Plural|ALBW|Boulder|link}}. These can be obtained by using the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|link}} or the {{Term|ALBW|Hammer|link}}. If {{Term|ALBW|Link}} manages to score at least 100 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, he will be rewarded with a {{Term|ALBW|Piece of Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That was so ultra out there! That's the first time I've seen someone cut it that close. It was great watching you at work. So I got you a super-special bonus!|Rupee Rush Gal|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wait a second... Isn't that a new record? Hey, you got such a high score! Take this piece of heart as a prize!|Rupee Rush Gal|ALBW}}</ref>

In {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}}, the game is run by the {{Term|ALBW|Rupee Rush Guy|link}} who charges {{Term|ALBW|Link}} 100 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} per game.<ref>{{Cite|Play some Rupee Rush? C'mon, it's only 100 Rupees for one go.|Rupee Rush Guy|ALBW}}</ref> The {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}} is required in order to access some of the most valuable {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}. If {{Term|ALBW|Link}} scores at least 150 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, he will receive another {{Term|ALBW|Piece of Heart}}.<ref>{{Cite|What?! Just in time?! Er, sorry. I don't usually get so, uh, excited. But thanks. Nice to see someone do really well here.|Rupee Rush Guy|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Lookit that! A new record! Looks like you figured out the secret to rushing! That's a towering achievement! Here's your reward!|Rupee Rush Guy|ALBW}}</ref>
