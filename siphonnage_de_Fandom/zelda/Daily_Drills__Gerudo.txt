{{Term/Store|Daily Drills: Gerudo||HWAoC}} is a Level 27 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daily Drills: Gerudo|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Daily Drills: Gerudo}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hydromelon|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat six {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within five minutes. Only one {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain|link}} is seen at first, surrounded by soldiers, but another spawns after she is killed. The second spawns once the first {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain}} is defeated, 30 seconds pass, and the second {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain}} loses about two thirds of their health. The last two {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captains}} spawn later, when two of the three previous {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captains}} are defeated. When all five are killed, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} defeats {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}.
