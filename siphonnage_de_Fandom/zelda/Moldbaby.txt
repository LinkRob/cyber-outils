{{Term/Store|Moldbaby|Moldbabies|Series, MM3D|plural}} are enemies in {{MM3D}}.<ref name= "Encyclopedia"/>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|MM3D|Moldbaby}} only appear during the second phase of the {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold}} fight in {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}}. Once the blue {{Term|MM3D|Twinmold}} is defeated and the {{Term|MM3D|Giant's Mask}} is obtained, three Moldbabies will appear from underground. {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} can defeat them by simply rolling into them while giant.
