The {{Term/Store|Demon Blade||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Ghirahim]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}}.

==Variations==
The default Demon Blade is the '''{{Term|HW|Demon Tribe Sword|link}}'''. The second form of the Demon Blade is the '''Demon Longsword''', followed by the '''True Demon Blade'''.

The Lv.★ form of the Demon Blade is the '''8-Bit Arrow''', which is modeled after the [[Arrow]] from {{TLoZ}}. It was added to {{HW|-}} with the Master Quest Pack DLC. In {{HW|DE}}, instead of appearing as a separate Weapon, the 8-Bit Arrow replaces the Darkfire Demon Blade when the "8-Bit Weapons" Setting is turned on in Game Settings within the Settings menu.


In {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE|-}}, there exists two Lv. 4 variations of the Demon Blade. They are the '''True Demon Blade +''' and the '''Darkfire Demon Blade''', the latter of which is associated with both the Elements of Darkness and {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Fire}}.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Ghirahim bows and chuckles before snapping his fingers. A sphere of his diamond magic forms half-embedded in the ground, before a number of swords plunge into it, followed by one larger sword, similar in appearance to [[Demise's Sword]]. The swords launch enemies away while Ghirahim laughs heartily. This attack damages a circular region in front of Ghirahim and carries no Element.{{verify}}
