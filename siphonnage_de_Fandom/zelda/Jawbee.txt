==Characteristics==
==={{TFoE|-}}===
{{Plural|TFoE|Jawbee}} commonly appear in caves and certain buildings. While they are normally black in color, they also appear in a rarer red version. Black {{Plural|TFoE|Jawbee}} attack from below, while red {{Plural|TFoE|Jawbee}} attack from above. They are both fairly simple for {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} to dispatch.

==={{TWoG|-}}===
{{Plural|TWoG|Jawbee}} commonly appear in caves and certain buildings. While they are normally black in color, they also appear in a rarer red version. Black {{Plural|TWoG|Jawbee}} attack from below, while red {{Plural|TWoG|Jawbee}} attack from above. They are both fairly simple for {{Term|TWoG|Princess Zelda|link}} to dispatch.
