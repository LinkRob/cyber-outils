The {{Term/Store|Korok Trial|Korok Trials|Series, BotW|plural}} are an event in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|So will you test your mettle in the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trials}}?|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arrives in the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest|link}}, he will learn from the elder {{Term|BotW|Chio|link}} that the {{Plural|BotW|Korok|link}} have prepared a series of {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}} for him.<ref>{{Cite|Us Koroks all got together and prepared some {{Color|BotW Red|trials}} just for you, Mr. Hero!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> They have become a tradition amongst the {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}, their purpose being that anyone who can visit all three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Great Hyrule Forest|link}} can be considered a true adult.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, where was I? Oh, yeah! The {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trials}}! Long story short—you need to visit all the shrines in the Korok Forest so that you can be considered {{Color|BotW Red|a real grown-up}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trials}} are challenges Koroks must complete in order to become esteemed members of Korok society! Or something like that... *cough*|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> These {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trials}} have been completed by many {{Plural|BotW|Korok}}, including {{Term|BotW|Chio}} himself, {{Term|BotW|Hestu|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|The Great Deku Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've done it...and so has Hestu...and him over there, and her too, and even the Great Deku Tree himself has done it!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Chio}} retracts his last claim.<ref>{{Cite|OK...that last part was a lie. He's pretty rooted.|Chio|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} proves himself in the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}}, the {{Plural|BotW|Korok}} have arranged prizes to give to him.<ref>{{Cite|We've also prepared some wonderful prizes for you!|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

Completing all of the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}} is necessary for "{{Term|BotW|The Korok Trials|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. Once they have all been finished, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Chio}} to inform him.<ref>{{Cite|What?! You've finished all of the {{Color|BotW Red|trials}}?|Chio|BotW}}</ref> He recognizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants a reward for visiting all of the {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}},<ref>{{Cite|Ah! I know that look. You want me to give you something since you visited all the places, don't you?|Chio|BotW}}</ref> so he gives him his three treasured {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...I guess I have no choice. I'll give you my treasured item.|Chio|BotW}}</ref>

==="Trial of Second Sight"===
{{Main|Trial of Second Sight}}

The "{{Term|BotW|Trial of Second Sight|link}}" is the first {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}} shared by {{Term|BotW|Chio}}, located in the southwest of {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial one!}} In the southwest forest: {{Color|BotW Red|Trial of Second Sight}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Zooki|link}} is at first uncertain if a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} is allowed to attempt the {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}}, they decide to ignore it.<ref>{{Cite|A Hylain seeks to attempt the {{Color|BotW Red|Korok Trial}}! Is that even allowed?<br/>Who cares!|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> They explain that the only objective for their {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}} is to not get lost in the mist of the {{Term|BotW|Lost Woods|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let me tell you about the {{Color|BotW Red|trial}}. All you need to do is keep going and not get lost in the mist! Easy, right?|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> To help {{Term|BotW|Link}} find his way, {{Term|BotW|Zooki}} offers a hint,<ref>{{Cite|But don't worry—I've got a cryptic little hint for you!|Zooki|BotW}}</ref> which instructs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to seek out the iron of the hungry {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|"The hungry trees will show you the way. Spy their {{Color|BotW Red|iron}} to win the day."|Zooki|BotW}}</ref>

The path to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} is lined by {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} that have iron boulders in their mouths. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can easily find them by using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Along the path, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield|link}} surrounded by {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}}. Nearby, a {{Term|BotW|Tree}} with a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}} in front of it will ask for the {{Term|BotW|Rusty Shield}} to be placed in its mouth, promising a reward from its brother.<ref>{{Cite|Feed me the aged shield guarded by monsters eternal. My brother will repay you in kind.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Doing so will cause a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}} to pop out of the mouth of another tree. Across {{Term|BotW|Lake Saria|link}}, another {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet}} in front of a {{Term|BotW|Tree}} will ask for the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, as it was stolen by the brother.<ref>{{Cite|Please place my chest in my mouth.<br/>{{Small|The shield eater's brother stole it from me.}}|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Kuhn Sidajj Shrine|link}} will rise from the ground, and {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have completed the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}.

==="The Lost Pilgrimage"===
{{Main|The Lost Pilgrimage}}

"{{Term|BotW|The Lost Pilgrimage|link}}" is the second {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}} shared by {{Term|BotW|Chio}}, located in the northwest of {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial two!}} In the northwest forest: {{Color|BotW Red|The Lost Pilgrimage}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> There, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will meet {{Term|BotW|Tasho|link}}, who will ask that he follow {{Term|BotW|Oaki|link}} on their {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|Could you please follow Oaki without being seen? I want to be sure nothing bad happens...|Tasho|BotW}}</ref> By successfully following {{Term|BotW|Oaki}} without being noticed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be lead to {{Term|BotW|Daag Chokah Shrine|link}}. Finding it will complete both the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}.

==="The Test of Wood"===
{{Main|The Test of Wood}}

"{{Term|BotW|The Test of Wood|link}}" is the third {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}} shared by {{Term|BotW|Chio}}, located in the eastern part of {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Trial three!}} In the eastern forest: {{Color|BotW Red|The Lost Pilgrimage}}!|Chio|BotW}}</ref> This {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}} is given by {{Term|BotW|Damia|link}}, who shares that it is believed to be the hardest of the three.<ref>{{Cite|But do you really think you're ready for the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}?! Some say it's the hardest of the Korok Trials.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s goal will be to reach the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} using only {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Korok Gear|link}} set.<ref>{{Cite|But it's not complicated—all you need to do is equip this {{Color|BotW Red|Korok weapon set}} and reach the shrine at the back of the area.|Damia|BotW}}</ref> However, if he unequips or breaks these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, he will fail the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial|display=Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have to keep the {{Color|BotW Red|sword}}, {{Color|BotW Red|bow}}, and {{Color|BotW Red|shield}} equipped the whole time. If they break or you unequip them, you're out!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>

At the end of the path through {{Term|BotW|Mido Swamp|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Maag Halan Shrine|link}}. This completes the {{Term|BotW|Korok Trial}}.<ref>{{Cite|CONGRATULATIONS!<br/>You've passed the {{Color|BotW Red|Test of Wood}}!|Damia|BotW}}</ref> Unlike other {{Plural|BotW|Korok Trial}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to replay the {{Term|BotW|Test of Wood|link}} as a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you ever want to do the trial again, just let me know.<br/>See ya!|Damia|BotW}}</ref>
