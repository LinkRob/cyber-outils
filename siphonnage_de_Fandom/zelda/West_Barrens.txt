The {{Term/Store|West Barrens||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''West Barrens'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The West Barrens are located in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. The West Barrens make up the entire northwest section of the Gerudo Desert. In the [[Shrine Quest]], "[[The Silent Swordswomen]]", [[Link]] must follow a series of the swordswomen statues through the sandstorm in the West Barrens. This will point Link to the hidden [[Kema Zoos Shrine]]. The Barrens contains the [[West Gerudo Ruins]]. [[Kilton]]'s shop, [[Fang and Bone]], can be found in the West Barrens at night on a small platform near [[Gerudo Town]].

A [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Gold Rupee]] sits on a platform in the center of the ruins. Several skeletons of large creatures are scattered around the area.
