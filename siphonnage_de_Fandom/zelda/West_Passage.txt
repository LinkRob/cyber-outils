The {{Term/Store|West Passage||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''West Passage'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|West Passage|link}} is a defunct tunnel system that is part of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Underground|link}}. The entrance of the {{Term|BotW|West Passage}} is marked by several {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}}, and it can be found near the {{Plural|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Moat|link}}, west of the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle Gate|link}}. A cracked tile wall separates the entrance of the {{Term|BotW|West Passage}} to the rail system located deeper inside, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can traverse on a {{Term|BotW|Mine Cart|link}}.

At the other end of the rail, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find another cavernous chamber. There is a {{Term|BotW|Hot Spring|link}} here, and a sloped ledge above this {{Term|BotW|Hot Spring|display=Spring}} leads further into a tunnel that ends at a cracked tile wall. If destroyed, it will open up a passage to {{Term|BotW|Lockup|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Water Pump Room|link}}. The second chamber of the {{Term|BotW|West Passage}} was a {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}} excavation site, as evidenced by the five half-buried, broken {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} found throughout.

Inside the {{Term|BotW|West Passage}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a number of {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}}, which can be destroyed for supplies. There is also a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|West Passage|display=Passage}}'s entrance, containing a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
By the {{Term|BotW|Hot Spring}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Offering Spot|link}}, marked by three {{Plural|BotW|Stone Statue|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Hard-Boiled Egg|link}} can be found on the flanking pools. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} places a {{Term|BotW|Bird Egg|link}} in the empty pool, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
