{{Term/Store|Zelda's Training: Bows||HWAoC}} is a level 46 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda's Training: Bows|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Zelda's Training: Bows}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can only be completed by {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}, who must use either the {{Term|HWAoC|Sacred Bow|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Sealing Bow|link}}, or {{Term|HWAoC|Bow of Light|link}}. After completing the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Topaz|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Princess|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Giant Ancient Core|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}'s objective is to defeat 1,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within ten minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}, except for three stationary {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} far from {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}'s location.  More {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}} appear when Zelda reaches 50, 200, 300, 400, and 500 KOs, while {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos}} spawn when she reaches 600, 700, 800, and 900. The three {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} start to move as well, with one of them approaching Zelda at 200, 500, and 800 KOs. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 1,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
