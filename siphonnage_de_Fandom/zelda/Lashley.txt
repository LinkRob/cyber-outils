{{Term/Store|Lashley||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lashley|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Lashley}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who guards the {{Term|BotW|Southeast Gate|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She wields a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} tries to enter {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} without a disguise, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} will stop him from entering and tell him that men are forbidden.<ref>{{Cite|Stop right there! {{Color|BotW Red|Voe are forbidden}} from entering this town. Turn back the way you came!|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, she will reiterate that men are not allowed inside and tell him to leave.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'otta! Hear this: {{Color|BotW Red|men are not allowed}} in Gerudo Town. So quit loitering, and get out of here!|Lashley|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} while wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}, she tells him that it is dangerous to wander around the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} because of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq! It's dangerous to wander around out here. Especially since we're so close to that {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}.|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> While the {{Term|BotW|Lookout Post|link}} is between them and the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}, she does not believe it is enough to protect them.<ref>{{Cite|We do have a {{Color|BotW Red|lookout post}}, but that doesn't mean it's perfectly safe around here...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}}, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} explains that it was said to be a god who protected the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, but it suddenly appeared and began to wander aimlessly.<ref>{{Cite|hat's the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}}. They say it used to be a god who protected this area, but... It suddenly appeared a little while ago and just started walking around aimlessly.|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> She claims that everyone is afraid that it will wander into {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, but the chief, {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, is doing everything she can do to avoid this.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone's afraid that it may wander into town eventually.|Lashley|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} is doing what she can about it, but...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} also shares a rumor that {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|display=Naboris}} shoots those who come near it with {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}}, and she hopes that it does not come their way.<ref>{{Cite|I heard a rumor that if you get too close to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}}, it will shoot you with lightning and burn you to a crisp. All we can do is hope that it doesn't come this way...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> Even though the {{Term|BotW|Lookout Post}} is safe from the {{Term|BotW|Lightning}}, there are many monsters in the area, so {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} against visiting.<ref name="Monsters">{{Cite|The lightning from the Divine Beast can't reach the {{Color|BotW Red|lookout post}}, but there are still plenty of monsters in the area... If I were you, I wouldn't even dream of going near it.|Lashley|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the {{Term|BotW|Lookout Post}}, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} points in its direction and explains that it is where {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} is monitored from.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Red|lookout post}} is over there. It's the building with the flag flying over it. They're monitoring the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} every moment of the day from that post.|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} then asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} is planning to go to the {{Term|BotW|Lookout Post}}.<ref>{{Cite|...You don't really intend to go to the {{Color|BotW Red|lookout post}}, do you?|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> Though it appears close, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} claims that it is actually far away, and she recommends that {{Term|BotW|Link}} should ride a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} to get there.<ref>{{Cite|It may look close, but it's actually pretty far away... At least take a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}, if you're truly set on going there.|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> She repeats her warning about the monsters surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Lookout Post}}.<ref name="Monsters"/> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} ends their conversation, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} tells him not to worry, as it is safe in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|Well, for now it is safe to be in town. Despite all I said, don't let the stomping worry you too much! Sav'orq!|Lashley|BotW}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} has been calmed, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} claims that she was worried about {{Term|BotW|Riju}} setting out on her own, but she now sees that she is capable.<ref>{{Cite|I was worried about what might happen when the chief set out on her own... But she's really capable, isn't she?|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> She mentions hearing that {{Term|BotW|Riju}} fought alongside a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} woman, remarking that she must have been strong.<ref>{{Cite|Which reminds me, apparently a Hylian vai, just like you, was fighting alongside the chief. She must be pretty strong...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Which reminds me, apparently a Hylian was fighting alongside the chief. A Hylian vai, no less! She must be pretty strong...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} claims not to have seen this {{Term|BotW|Hylian}} from her post, and she wonders where she could have gone.<ref>{{Cite|However, I've been here this whole time, and I haven't seen a vai like that around. I wonder where she went...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref>

When spoken to again, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} expresses her disbelief that the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast}} is actually gone.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe the Divine Beast is gone...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> While it only caused problems for the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}, {{Term|BotW|Lashley}} had been watching it for quite some time.<ref>{{Cite|All it did was cause problems for us, but I've been watching it for so long...|Lashley|BotW}}</ref> She admits that she feels somewhat lonely now.<ref>{{Cite|Now that it's gone, I actually feel a little lonely.|Lashley|BotW}}</ref>
