==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
White Chuchu Jelly can be obtained by killing {{Plural|BotW|Ice Chuchu|link}}, or by killing {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}} with ice weapons. It can also be made from regular {{Term|BotW|Chuchu Jelly|link}} by using an [[Ice Rod]] or [[Blizzard Rod]] to freeze it or by dropping it into icy water. They are cool to the touch and can be squeezed to relieve stress.<ref name="Inventory"/> If hit by a weapon, the jelly will burst and release a cloud of ice that can freeze nearby enemies.<ref name="Inventory"/>

[[Elixir]]s can be made with White Chuchu Jellies by being combined with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]]. The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] accepts White Chuchu Jellies to make White [[Dye]], while [[Kilton]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] can exchange them for [[Mon]]. 

White Chuchu Jelly is used to upgrade the [[Desert Voe Set]] of [[Armor]] to its first and second tiers at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Desert Voe Set''': 24 (3, 5, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
White Chuchu Jelly was used to make Vaporeon white in a very funny video.
