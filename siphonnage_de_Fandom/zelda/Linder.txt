{{Term/Store|Linder||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Linder}}

Linder is a [[Korok]] from the [[Forest Haven]]. He is a friend of [[Makar]]. When [[Link]] arrives at the Forest Haven, he informs the [[Great Deku Tree]] and Link that Makar fell into the [[Forbidden Woods]]. After Link is sent to rescue Makar, Linder can be found standing on the ramp up to [[Hollo]]'s [[Hollo's Forest Potion Shop|Potion Shop]]. Following the [[Forest Ceremony]], Linder receives a [[Guardian Seed]] and heads to [[Greatfish Isle]] to plant new [[Tree]]s.
