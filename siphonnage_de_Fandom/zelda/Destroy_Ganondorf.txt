"{{Term|TotK|{{Page Name}}|Series, TotK}}" is a {{Term|TotK|Main Quest|link}} in {{TotK}}.

==Overview==
After reawakening the five sages and regaining the {{Term|TotK|Master Sword|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Link|link}} has prepared everything for the confrontation against {{Term|TotK|Ganondorf|link}}, the {{Term|TotK|Demon King|link}}. The whereabouts of Ganondorf were still unknown, but {{Term|TotK|Purah|link}} suspected he could be in {{Term|TotK|The Depths|link}}. She suggested talking to {{Term|TotK|Josha|link}} to indicate his location.

After his investigation in The Depths with Josha's guidance,{{Note|This is closely related to the side quest, {{Term|TotK|Master Kohga of the Yiga Clan|link}}. As the prior quest progresses, Josha will update more locations of interests until the quest completes and Link obtains the location of Ganondorf.}} Link disclosed that Ganondorf is located deep within the depths under {{Term|TotK|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa... So the Demon King is in the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">deepest reaches of the Depths</font>... Far <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">beneath Hyrule Castle</font>.|Purah|TotK}}</ref> With the path clear, Link heads into the Depths of Hyrule Castle.

Regardless, or unless otherwise, Link entered the chasm of Hyrule Castle, descends into a dark ruin surrounded by {{Term|TotK|Gloom|link}} to reach Ganondorf. On the way, he faces treacherous depths and gloom-ridden monsters roaming the catacombs. As he delves deeper, the avatar of the {{Plural|TotK|Sage|link}} are unable to maintain their form with Link.<ref>{{Cite|The power of a sage cannot reach you.|N/A|TotK}}</ref> Eventually, Link reaches a {{Term|TotK|Forgotten Foundation|display=familiar place}} and eventually, the place where the {{Term|TotK|Imprisoning Chamber|link}} once was. From there is another {{Term|TotK|chasm|link}}; diving into it lead Link down into {{Term|TotK|Gloom's Lair|link|display=a red rooted lair covered in gloom}}. When he lands, Link is ambushed by {{Term|TotK|The Demon King's Army|display=a whole army of monsters}}. After he defeats the armada, Link is faced by large servants of Ganondorf — {{Term|TotK|Colgera|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Marbled Gohma|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Mucktorok|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Queen Gibdo|link}}, and {{Term|TotK|Seized Construct|link}}. Once he defeated them all, the path to the core of the lair opens.

If Link awakened all the five sages<ref>{{Cite|You don't have to fight all by yourself, you know!|N/A|TotK}}</ref> — {{Term|TotK|Tulin|link}},<ref>{{Cite|When we fight together... We're unstoppable!|Tulin|TotK}}</ref> {{Term|TotK|Yunobo|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I'm gonna make my ancestors proud today, Link! I'll fight by your side!|Yunobo|TotK}}</ref> {{Term|TotK|Sidon|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Link! We shall protect Hyrule together!|Sidon|TotK}}</ref> {{Term|TotK|Riju|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The Gerudo owe you for your help. You can count on me!|Riju|TotK}}</ref> and {{Term|TotK|Mineru|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I will atone for my past failure. It is time to stop the Demon King!|Mineru|TotK}}</ref> will all arrive as backup to face against the Demon King's Army. After defeating the army altogether, the Sages will face against the servants of the Demon King in Link's place,<ref>{{Cite|We are fated to fight these beasts! Don't worry about us!|Sidon|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We'll be fine! We already beat them once! This'll be a breeze for sure!|Tulin|TotK}}</ref> which allowed him to head into the core of the lair while the entire place crumbled under rocks.<ref>{{Cite|It is time, Link. The rest is up to you!|Mineru|TotK}}</ref>

Link finally reached Ganondorf, waiting inside his lair. Dismayed that Link — and the light — remains,<ref>{{Cite|How disappointing... This world should be shrouded in darkness, not bathed in insufferable light. All these weak, peace-loving cowards running rampant... It would have been more...satisfying to overcome a worthy foe.|Ganondorf|TotK}}</ref> Ganondorf regenerates to his normal form and affirms to rid Link himself.<ref>{{Cite|Regardless... I will reshape this world as it was meant to be. I will crush any opposition. I will rule. That is what a king must do. Do not look away. You witness a king's revival. And the birth of his new world!|Ganondorf|TotK}}</ref> Link prepares to fight Ganondorf — If Link has obtained the {{Term|TotK|Master Sword|link}}, he unsheathes it and points at Ganondorf, gleaming with purifying light. The two engaged, with Link gaining the upper hand.

Impressed by Link's fighting, Ganondorf empowers himself with his {{Term|TotK|Secret Stone|link}} to fight at full strength.<ref>{{Cite|Heh heh heh... What a feeling... I had almost forgotten the thrill of battle. That feeling as blood surges in my veins... And I am not even nearing the limits of my power!|Ganondorf|TotK}}</ref> He then summons dark aparations of himself to surround Link — If any of the Sages have awakened, Link's companions will catch up to him<ref>{{Cite|I'm with you, Link!|Tulin|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll flatten that Demon King!|Yunobo|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|All right! I'm here!|Sidon|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let's take him down together, Link!|Riju|TotK}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We settle this now, Ganondorf!|Mineru|TotK}}</ref> to distract the aparations for him. In the heat of battle, Ganon absorbed his aparations — unleashed a shockwave which knocked out Link's companions if present — and began to {{Term|TotK|Flurry Rush|link|display=riptose Link's attacks}}. In the end, Link defeats Ganondorf.

However, Ganondorf refuses to yield<ref>{{Cite|I will yet rule this land... You will not stop me! A mere mortal...will not stand in my way!|Ganondorf|TotK}}</ref> — he took his Secret Stone in his hands and consumed it,<ref>{{Cite|My body... My mind... Everything! I'll sacrifice it all to destroy you... and cast this world into eternal night!|Ganondorf|TotK}}</ref> infusing him with immeasurable power and transformed into the {{Term|TotK|Demon Dragon|link}}. It grabbed Link and escaped the lair — leaving the Sages, if present, behind.<ref>{{Cite|Link!|N/A|TotK}}</ref>

The Demon Dragon rose above Hyrule Castle and high into the {{Term|TotK|sky|link}}, while Link is trapped in the dragon's jaws. In the midst, a bright white dragon — the {{Term|TotK|Light Dragon|link}} — charged the Demon Dragon, flinching it and allowing Link to escape. He lands on top of the Light Dragon's head. Unsheathing the Master Sword for the second time, the duo prepare to battle the Demon Dragon. After weakening the Demon Dragon, Link dives onto the Demon Dragon's head at full speed and impaled its Secret Stone with the Master Sword — shattering it into pieces and vanquishing the Demon Dragon.

Link falls, but he is caught one last time by the Light Dragon. They flew past by the vanquished Demon Dragon, and watched its final moments as it explodes into a massive pillar of light, ending the calamity which threatened Hyrule for countless millenia.
