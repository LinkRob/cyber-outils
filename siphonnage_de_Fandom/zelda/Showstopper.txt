The {{Term/Store|Showstopper||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 138}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Showstopper attracts stares from everyone, including enemies. It's impossible to look away...}}

The {{Term|TFH|Showstopper}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes the {{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Grim Temple|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} dresses {{Term|TFH|Link}} in a golden suit and top hat, complete with large, golden wings and a continuous sparkling effect. The gaudiness of the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} prompts the {{Term|TFH|Stylish Woman|link}} to call {{Term|TFH|Link}} a "flashy little flan" when he dons it.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh la la! You flashy little flan!|Stylish Woman|TFH}}</ref>

The {{Term|TFH|Showstopper}} causes most [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] and [[Bosses in Tri Force Heroes|Bosses]] to target the wearer with their attacks and chases.<ref>{{Cite|So flashy, it draws extra attention from enemies. Looks GOOD though.|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref> The effect is mainly strategic, as it can lure Enemies toward traps and leave their weak spots vulnerable to the other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} has no effect in a single-player game, since Enemies normally only target the {{Term|TFH|Links}} which the player is controlling.
