"{{Term/Store|Sunken Treasure||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Sunken Treasure}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Lurelin Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will be welcomed by the head of the {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|display=Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Rozel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you a traveler? Welcome to Lurelin Village. I'm Rozel. I'm the head of this village.|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks him about the ocean, {{Term|BotW|Rozel}} reveals that there are tales of a treasure located in the water nearby.<ref>{{Cite|[About the ocean...] There are tales passed down of sunken {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}} in the ocean around here...|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> Though he is interested in finding it for himself, he is too old to do so.<ref>{{Cite|I would look for it myself if I were a bit younger, but I've grown old...|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> Instead, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to search, as he is younger and stronger.<ref>{{Cite|But you look like you have a young pair of legs and a strong back. Do you want to go look in my stead?|Rozel|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to help, {{Term|BotW|Rozel}} will share the hint to the treasure's location, which is at the center of a golden triangle.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm on it!] I couldn't be happier to hear it. Now, according to the old tales... "{{Color|BotW Red|The treasure sleeps in the center of the golden triangle}}."|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> He also suggests that {{Term|BotW|Link}} borrow his {{Term|BotW|Raft|link}} to aid him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, by the by, do you happen to have a boat? If not, I can lend you my raft. It's tied up at the wharf. Help yourself.|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> Once he is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will begin.

Just off the {{Term|BotW|Clarnet Coast|link}}, three rocks point out above the water. The treasure located at the center of the rocks, and it must be pulled up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. There are four {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in total, containing a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Thunderblade|link}}, and two {{Plural|BotW|Topaz|link}}. After finding the treasure, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must return to {{Term|BotW|Rozel}} to tell him.<ref>{{Cite|You pinpointed the location referred to by the legend and retrieved the treasure from the waters off Clarnet Coast.<br/><br/>You should tell Rozel the good news.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

Back in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Rozel}} will be pleasantly surprised to hear the news.<ref>{{Cite|You really found it?! Ho ho! Incredible! After all these years, people still manage to surprise me.|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> He informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that there might be other treasures in the ocean that have not been found yet.<ref>{{Cite|You know, there might be some {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}} that yet sleeps in this ocean. A diligent boy like you could get rich finding it...|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> With a wish of luck,<ref>{{Cite|May all your delving end in gold!|Rozel|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} ends.
