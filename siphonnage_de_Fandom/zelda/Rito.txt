The {{Term/Store|Rito|Rito|Series, TWW, TWWHD, BotW|plural}} are a recurring race in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Encyclopedia"/><ref>{{Cite|It's considered to be quite the masterpiece of aerial techniques, even among the Rito.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> They are people who possess avian features, including wings which give them the ability of flight. In {{TWW}}, they are primarily humanoid with few bird-like traits. In {{BotW}}, Rito have adopted completely avian forms.

In {{TWW|-}}, the Rito are an evolved form of the [[Zora]]s who have gained the ability to fly.<ref name="Evolved">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= ''[[Zelda Box]]''|publisher= Enterbrain|page= 29}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The Rito are primarily avian in appearance, possessing wings, a beak and talons. The range of their avian characteristics varies between {{TWW|-}} and {{BotW|-}}, with the two games featuring a stark difference in appearance. In the former, Rito are much more humanoid and possess fewer avian features, whereas in {{BotW|-}}, the Rito are more akin to anthropomorphic birds with almost no human traits, though it's possible the two races may be subspecies of one another, the humanoid and avian versions populating different parts of Hyrule.
In {{TWW|-}}, the Rito are the evolved form of the Zoras.<ref name="Evolved"/><ref name="Laruto">{{Cite|You must find the one who carries on my bloodline... The one who holds this sacred {{Color|TWW Red|instrument}}...|Laruto|TWW}}</ref> 

The Rito reside in the volcanic [[Dragon Roost Island]] in {{TWW|-}}. There they tend to the Sky Spirit [[Valoo]], who sits atop the peak of the island volcano. Rito in {{TWW|-}} appear as humanoids with pointed ears, wide red eyes, orangish-yellowish beaks in place of noses, and talon legs. Many members have dark skin and white hair that is often braided; the exception to this being [[Medli]], a female Rito who has light skin and red hair tied into a ponytail. Whether or not this is a difference of sex in the race is unknown. Rito are initially wingless; when they come of age, they must travel to Valoo and receive a scale that will enable them to grow their wings.<ref>{{Cite|We do so by the graces of the sky spirit, {{Color|TWW Red|Valoo}}. When a Rito reaches adulthood, he or she journeys to the top of {{Color|TWW Red|Dragon Roost}} to receive a {{Color|TWW Red|scale}} from the great dragon. It is this scale that enables the Rito to grow his or her wings.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref> Most Rito have their wings folded around their arms as "sleeves", the exception being Medli and the [[Rito Chieftain]]. When they take flight, their arms spontaneously transform into open wings. Their ability to fly makes them well suited as mail carriers across the [[Great Sea]]. The Rito were not always creatures of the sky, and the [[Grappling Hook]] is a remnant of those days.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|TWW Red|Grappling Hook}}! This was an item used by the Rito people before they had wings.|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's a {{Color|TWW Red|device}} we Rito used {{Color|TWW Red|before we evolved wings}}.|Medli|TWW}}</ref>

The Rito have adopted a much more bird-like appearance in {{BotW}}. They have almost no humanoid traits whatsoever. In addition, their children have wings even at a young age and there is no sign of a guardian deity to fill a similar role to Valoo's in {{TWW|-}}. Both Rito and Zoras exist in {{BotW|-}}; whether or not this means the Rito are still evolved from Zoras is unknown. These Rito live at [[Rito Village]] above [[Lake Totori]] in [[Hebra]]. Their males are urged from birth to emulate [[Revali]], the Rito Champion from a century prior.<ref>{{Cite|All Rito males are urged from birth to become honorable warriors just like Revali.|Huck|BotW}}</ref> While Rito can fly forward, they cannot ascend on their own and rely on the wind to lift them upwards.<ref>{{Cite|Link. As you know, us Rito use our wings to fly forward. The wind lifts us up into the glorious sky above. However, we cannot ascend on our own.|Teba|BotW}}</ref> The art of creating an updraft to soar high into the sky, which Revali could do, is considered to be a masterpiece of aerial techniques even among the Rito.<ref>{{Cite|Yet I have made an art of creating an updraft that allows me to soar. It's considered to be quite the masterpiece of aerial techniques, even among the Rito.|Revali|BotW}}</ref>

Rito have poor night vision and struggle to see in dimly-lit places.<ref>{{Cite|Us Rito... We haven't got the best night vision, to be honest.|Frita|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If Rito Village were brighter, we'd be able to go out at night, too...|Frita|BotW}}</ref> They prefer to eat meat or fish, like birds of prey. Their feathers retain heat, helpful for both the chilly climate they live in during {{BotW|-}} and for when they fly at high altitudes. A young Rito's feathers molt once per year, and the feathers have formed a thriving industry for the Rito who use them to craft [[Snowquill Set|clothes for Hylians]] or beds. Known for their musical abilities, the Rito pass down legends in the form of songs.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 131}}</ref> They are not particularly long-lived.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 400}}</ref>

==History==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Image|type=caption|image=TWW Medli Artwork.png|size=150px|align=right|caption=Medli from {{TWW|-}}, a notable Rito}}
The Zoras evolved over the span of a century into the Rito after the events of the [[Great Flood]] because of the unnatural water created by the gods, which was uninhabitable to Zora.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 69}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 65}}</ref> They live in [[Dragon Roost Island]], specifically within the [[Rito Aerie]], a small cavern to the side of the island mountain. Due to their wings enabling them to fly over the Great Sea with ease, they mainly work as post carriers, and as such their Aerie is mainly used as a [[Post Office]]. Their location is significantly important to their way of life; it is where [[Valoo]] the Sky Spirit roosts atop the [[Dragon Roost Cavern]] peak. When a Rito reaches adulthood, they must receive their wings by traveling to Valoo to receive a scale. A Rito is appointed among the tribe to serve as Valoo's attendant. As Valoo can only speak in [[Hylian Language#Third Hylian Script|ancient Hylian]], his attendant is tasked with translating his words.<ref>{{Cite|I may be just an apprentice attendant, but I can {{Color|TWW Red|understand}} some of the {{Color|TWW Red|great Valoo's language}}. I'm not exactly bursting with confidence, but...|Medli|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My grandma used to be Valoo's {{Color|TWW Red|attendant}}. She was the only person on the island who understood {{Color|TWW Red|Valoo's language}}.|Komali|TWW}}</ref> Performing music for Valoo is also an expected duty of the attendant.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I'd better continue practicing so that I can be useful around here, too! Performing music like this is an important duty of an attendant.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> The tribe is led by the [[Rito Chieftain]].<ref>{{Cite|As chieftain of the Rito, my first responsibility is to solve this problem.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref>

[[Golden Feather]]s are very popular among Rito women.<ref>{{Cite|Tell me, small fry, do you have any of them {{Color|TWW Red|Golden Feathers}}? I hear those things are all the rage with Rito ladies on {{Color|TWW Red|Dragon Roost Island}} these days. They just go ga-ga over them!|Fishman|TWW}}</ref> Before taking flight, the Rito customarily go to visit the [[Dragon Roost Island|Wind God's Shrine]] at the base of the island.<ref>{{Cite|We Rito cannot live without the wind. Before we take to the skies, we always go down and visit the {{Color|TWW Red|wind god's shrine}}. That's the way it's always been.|Rito|TWW}}</ref> The Rito also sponsor the [[Bird-Man Contest]] on the [[Flight Control Platform]] every year.<ref>{{Cite|Every year, the Rito sponsor a Bird-Man Contest on that island off to the east.|Rito|TWW}}</ref>

The first Rito Link encounters is [[Quill]], a postman who visits [[Outset Island]] around the time when the [[Helmaroc King]] abducted [[Aryll]] after mistaking her for [[Tetra]]. It is by Quill's urging, pointing out that Aryll would have never been taken had it not been for Tetra and her [[Tetra's Pirate Crew|Pirate Crew]]'s arrival, that convinces Tetra to ferry Link to the [[Forsaken Fortress]] where his sister is imprisoned. Link's failure to rescue her, and subsequent meeting with the [[King of Red Lions]], eventually brings him to the Rito's home of Dragon Roost Island.

There, Link learns that the Rito are having a crisis. Valoo has become violent and restless, preventing the Rito from receiving his scales needed to grow their wings, and is thus threatening their way of life.<ref>{{Cite|Recently, however, the once-gentle Valoo has grown violent and unpredictable. Sadly, we can no longer approach him. If this continues, the {{Color|TWW Red|fledglings}} who are of age will never be able to receive scales from {{Color|TWW Red|Valoo}} on {{Color|TWW Red|Dragon Roost}}. They will remain {{Color|TWW Red|wingless}}, and in time, our very way of life will be threatened.|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref> Furthermore, the Rito Chieftain is also concerned about his son, [[Komali]], and his lack of confidence in light of the situation.<ref>{{Cite|Let me be direct. My son, {{Color|TWW Red|Komali}}, is {{Color|TWW Red|of the age to earn his wings}}... Yet...he is weak, in some ways...and in light of the current situation, he may just {{Color|TWW Red|give up on ever getting them}}...|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref> Valoo's previous attendant and Komali's grandmother had recently passed away, dashing Komali's confidence and turning him into a recluse.<ref>{{Cite|However, his grandmother passed recently, and Komali seems lost. His confidence is...gone. His grandmother was always with him, you see.|Rito|TWW}}</ref> The young Prince now spends his days cooped up in his room with [[Din's Pearl]], a treasure of the Rito that his grandmother gave him.<ref>{{Cite|You received {{Color|TWW Red|Din's Pearl}}! This is the gem the Ritos claim was passed down from the great goddess!|N/A|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's so pretty, isn't it? It's called {{Color|TWW Red|Din's Pearl}}. My grandma gave it to me.|Komali|TWW}}</ref>

While trying to encourage Prince Komali, Link is asked by [[Medli]], the newly appointed attendant and former apprentice of Komali's grandmother,<ref>{{Cite|See, Prince Komali's grandmother was the great Valoo's former {{Color|TWW Red|attendant}}. She was an amazing woman. I was honored to have her as my teacher... She was kind and brave, and unsurpassed in her dealings with the great Valoo.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> to meet her outside at the [[Dragon Roost Pond]]. There she asks Link for help in reaching the entrance to Dragon Roost Cavern, which she cannot reach due to the wind's direction. Link helps her and Medli goes on ahead to help Valoo, and Link decides to follow. He later finds her captured by a [[Moblin]] and rescues her. Medli then informs him that she has discovered that something within the Cavern is harassing Valoo. Link discovers that the cause of Valoo's distress is in fact a [[Gohma]]. Link quells the dragon's discomfort by defeating the Gohma, finally ending the Rito's plight. Prince Komali also regains his courage, and leaves Link with Din's Pearl.

Later during his quest, Link eventually meets the spirit of [[Laruto]], the deceased Zoran [[Sage|Sage of Earth]] who was killed by [[Ganondorf]]'s forces, resulting in the [[Master Sword]] losing some of its former power. Laruto beseeches that Link finds her successor, the one who carries the same [[Rito Harp|Harp]] as she, who is destined to become the next Sage of Earth.<ref name="Laruto"/> Link locates Medli, and after playing the "[[Earth God's Lyric]]," awakens her as the Sage of Earth. Together they travel to the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]] where Link defeats the evil there, allowing Medli to pray so the Master Sword can regain some of its power.

==={{BotW|-}}===
The Rito also appear in {{BotW}}, with their main population center at [[Rito Village]] in [[Tabantha Frontier]]. It is unknown if these Rito are descendants of the [[Zora]] as they are in {{TWW|-}}.

==Nomenclature==
The name Rito is likely a play on the Japanese word "tori", which means "bird" ("Rito" is a reversal of the characters "to" and "ri"). It may also be derived from [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]], the Zora princess from {{OoT}}, referring to the Rito's ancestry.

In {{TWW|-}}, most of the Rito's Japanese names describe their role or behavior, while most of their German names are derived from real-life bird species and genera.
