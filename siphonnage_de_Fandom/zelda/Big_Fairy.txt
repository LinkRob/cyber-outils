{{Term/Store|Big Fairy|Big Fairies|Series, OoT, OoT3D|plural}} are a species in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E|publisher= |page= 116}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|OoT3D|Big Fairy}} resemble wingless glowing balls. In certain areas, such as near the portal to {{Term|OoT3D|Zora's River|link}} in the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods|link}}, or at {{Plural|OoT3D|Gossip Stone|link}}, if {{Term|OoT3D|Navi|link}} glows green, {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} can play the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina|link}} to make a {{Term|OoT3D|Big Fairy}} appear. It restores eight {{Plural|OoT3D|Heart|link}} and refills the {{Term|OoT3D|Magic Meter|link}}. It cannot be caught in a {{Term|OoT3D|Bottle|link}}, and therefore cannot revive {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} when he runs out of health.

In {{OoT3D}}, {{Plural|OoT3D|Big Fairy}} have changed to yellow to be more easily distinguishable from normal {{Plural|OoT3D|Fairy|link}}, have wings, and now fully heal {{Term|OoT3D|Link}}.
