'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time'' Pathways to Adventure''' is a book published by Sybex Inc. in November 1999.<ref name=amazon>{{Cite Web|retrieved=2013-03-27 |url=http://www.amazon.com/Legend-Zelda-Ocarina-Time/dp/078212478X |title=The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time: Jason Rich: 9780782124781: Amazon.com: Books |site=Amazon}}</ref> It basically retells the story of {{OoT}}, straight from the game, and even includes screen shots from the game. It is part of the "Pathways to Adventure" series, and seems to be aimed mainly at children.<ref>[http://www.zeldalegends.net/index.php?n=books ZeldaLegends.net]</ref> However, the story also provides some hints on how to defeat most of the bosses and solve some of the major puzzles as well. It contains 106 pages.
