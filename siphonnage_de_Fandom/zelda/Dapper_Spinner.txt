==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Dapper Spinner ensemble lets you Spin Attack much faster! And you'll look like... HIM. *le swoon*}}

The {{Term|TFH|Dapper Spinner}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}. It becomes available for purchase after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} opens a losing {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} in the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}} mini-game for the fifth time, at which point the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches Owner|link}} places an order for the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} in recognition of {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s repeated bad luck.<ref>{{Cite|You lost FIVE times... Impressive! I have a special gift to acknowledge such spectacularly bad luck.<br/>In anticipation of such an occasion, I've ordered a special outfit from the one and only {{Color|TFH Blue|Madame}}.<br/>She calls it...<br/>The {{Color|TFH Blue|Dapper Spinner}}!|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} costs ten {{Plural|TFH|Freebie|link}}, which can only be obtained from {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches}}, and 300 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}.

Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Dapper Spinner}} allows {{Term|TFH|Link}} to execute a {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack|link}} quickly by pressing the B button three times.<ref>{{Cite|Look like HIM!<br/>♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦<br/>Press {{Icon|TFH B}} 3 times to unleash the power. Look like that heroic knight with the cool Spin Attacks.|Catalog|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} resembles {{Term|TFH|The Dapper Spin Knight|link}}, a storybook hero popular among [[Hytopia]]ns, especially the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches Owner}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's the outfit of choice for my... I mean, Hytopia's favorite storybook hero, {{Color|TFH Blue|The Dapper Spin Knight}}!|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That swift Spin Attack of his... Fella, it's something else! I always looked up to him as a kid, heh.<br/>The story goes that he appears before the unlucky and slices away their bad luck with a single marvelous spin!|Daily Riches Owner|TFH}}</ref>

In reality, the {{Term|TFH|Dapper Spinner}} appears to be based on ''{{Wp|The Three Musketeers}}''. In addition to its cavalier hat and noble clothing, the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} gives {{Term|TFH|Link}} a twirled mustache and a {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} similar to a {{Wp|Rapier|rapier}}.

Any {{Plural|TFH|Links}} who join the game through [[Download Play]] will be unable to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Dapper Spinner}}, since they cannot leave the [[Multiplayer]] lobby to visit the {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches}}.
