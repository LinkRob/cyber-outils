'''Wasteland''' may refer to any of these areas specifically named as one in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series.

*'''[[Tabahl Wasteland]]'''- the gloomy wasteland on Koholint Island in {{LA}}.
*'''[[Haunted Wasteland]]''' - a massive desert between [[Gerudo's Fortress]] and the [[Desert Colossus]] in {{OoT}}. 
*'''[[Natzu|Natzu Wasteland]]'''- the small area in {{OoA}}, that only appears with [[Moosh]] as Link's pet.
*'''[[Wasteland Tower]]'''- a [[Sheikah Tower]] in {{BotW}} located in the east [[Gerudo Highlands]].
