{{Plural|SS|Remlit}} are well-mannered in the day and will follow {{Term|SS|Link|link}} around if he picks them up and then set them down again. They will flee, however, if he takes out a weapon. {{Plural|SS|Remlit}} will make sounds that are akin to a cat's meow or purr when picked up and carried around. If thrown off the side of {{Term|SS|Skyloft}}, they will use their ears as wings to fly right back up. During the day, {{Plural|SS|Remlit}} (except for {{Term|SS|Mia|link}}) reside in a small flower bed area to the right of the {{Term|SS|Statue of the Goddess|link}}, where they can be seen resting. This area can be accessed only after {{Term|SS|Link}} acquires the {{Plural|SS|Clawshot|link}} and before the {{Term|SS|Statue of the Goddess}} returns to the surface near the end of the game.

{{Plural|SS|Remlit}} exhibit aggressive behavior at night, prowling in the dark along with other creatures.<ref>{{Cite|Such an odd creature, the {{Color|SS Blue|Remlit}}. She may look cuddly now, but at night, it's another story entirely!|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|When night falls here in Skyloft, creatures like this Remlit become possessed by an evil force. And monsters that hide in caves during the day make their way out and prowl in the dark of night.|Horwell|SS}}</ref> These {{Plural|SS|Remlit}} attack {{Term|SS|Link}} by leaping at him, but will cower after being struck a few times. If {{Term|SS|Link}} picks them up while they are cowering, they will start to attack again. {{Plural|SS|Remlit}} cannot be defeated and will get stunned before running away if sprinkled with {{Term|SS|Glittering Spores|link}}. After {{Term|SS|Link}} turns {{Term|SS|Batreaux|link}} into a human, {{Plural|SS|Remlit}} cease their aggressive tendencies.<ref>{{Cite|These small animals dwell in {{Color|SS Blue|Skyloft}}. Due to {{Color|SS Blue|Batreaux}}'s transformation into a human, they no longer exhibit aggressive tendencies at night.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This gives me pause... The usual monsters that prowl Skyloft at night are nowhere to be seen, and the Remlits no longer show aggressive behavior. What could have happened?|N/A|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Look, Link! Though night has come, Mia exhibits none of the nocturnal aggressiveness we've come to expect in Remlits! It's just amazing!|Horwell|SS}}</ref>
