'''The 30th Anniversary ''The Legend of Zelda'' Game Music Collection''' is a collection of various songs from {{TLoZ|Series}} released to celebrate the 30th anniversary of the series. It is exclusive to Japan. The limited first print edition included a luxury box and 16 sleeves featuring the box art of several games in the series.

Pre-ordering the soundtrack included an IC card sticker on Amazon Japan or a clear file, both featuring the soundtrack's cover.
