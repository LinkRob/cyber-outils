{{Name|Japanese|Gasoringo}} is the first boss in {{RTBToL}}.{{Name Ref|RTBToL}}

==History==
At the beginning of {{Term|RTBToL|Page 6|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} and company stop in Vegetable Village, due to a malfunction with the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner|link}} fuel supply. Because the {{Term|RTBToL|Tinliner}} only works with a plant-based fuel, the group must find a gasoline apple-like fruit called {{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo}}. However, the tree will only grow under specific conditions.

After several events, including obtaining Seeds of {{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo}}, and finding proper soil to plant them, a gasoline apple tree finally grows. But, instead of a normal tree, it reveals to be a large three-eyed plant monster. 

==Characteristics==
In order to defeat it, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must use the aid of his three friends, {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi|link}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki|link}}, and {{Term|RTBToL|Lion (Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love)|link}}. They all have to use their various abilities at certain points in the battle. Firstly, Lion's strength is needed to topple the tree, revealing a hole where {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}} can crawl in. Secondly, {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}} will lower {{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo}}'s head, making it easy for {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} to shoot its eyes with the {{Term|RTBToL|Slingshot|link}}. After that, {{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo}} throws a grenade-like fruit to the group, and when the grenade is about to explode, {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki}} is required to kick it back to the creature. Once all the remaining eyes are damaged, {{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo}} will explode, leaving the apples behind for {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} and friends to use them.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|RTBToL|Gasoringo}}'s name may be a portmanteau of the Japanese pronunciation of gasoline, ガソリン (Gasorin), and the Japanese word for apple, りんご (Ringo).
