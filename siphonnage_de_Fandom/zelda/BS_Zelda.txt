'''''BS Zelda''''' may refer to any one of three ''BS Zelda'' games that were released for the [[Super Famicom]] via the [[BS-X Broadcasting System]].

*'''''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]''''' – The first BS Zelda game, which is essentially a varied remake of the original {{TLoZ}} with two different quests.
*'''''[[BS The Legend of Zelda: Ancient Stone Tablets]]''''' – The final BS Zelda game, which is a sequel to {{ALttP}}.
*The '''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past#BS-X Broadcasting_System|BS-X port of {{ALttP|-}}]]''', often known as ''{{Name|Fan|BS The Legend of Zelda: Triforce of the Gods}}''
