{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask Orchestrations''||Series, MM}} is one of two soundtracks for {{MM}} that was released in 2001.

==Overview==
Published by Enterbrain, the album contains 11 rearranged tracks from the game. However, unlike [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Hyrule Symphony|Hyrule Symphony]], ''Majora's Mask Orchestrations'' is not performed with acoustic instruments, but with synthesizers. The tracks are composed by [[Koji Kondo]] and are arranged and performed by Shoji Horikoshi, Masahide Yumi, Masaharu Toyoda, Keita Kikuchi, and Katsuya Takahashi.
