The '''Suthorn Ruins''' are the first dungeon in {{EoW}}.

==Overview==
===First visit===
In the prologue of the game, [[Link#Echoes of Wisdom|Link]] infiltrates the ruins after having learned of [[Princess Zelda]]'s capture by what has been assumed to be the culprit behind the [[Rift]]s opening across [[Hyrule]], "stealing away" many citizens, not all of whom are seen again. After battling the enemies with [[Sword of Might Lv. 2|the Sword of Might]], [[Bow of Might Lv. 2|the Bow of Might]], and [[Bombs of Might Lv. 2|some Bombs of Might]], Link eventually confronts [[Echo Ganon|the doppelganger of Ganon]].

After a fierce battle, Link triumphs, but the Ganon doppelganger laughs before fading into nothingness, leaving its [[trident]] behind. As Link goes to free Zelda, the Ganon doppelganger's trident vanishes into a new Rift, spreading from where the trident had been placed. Unable to escape it, Link fires an arrow from his Bow of Might before being consumed. This allows Zelda to break free and, taking Link's discarded cloak, flees the now crumbling ruins.

===Second visit===
Much later, Zelda returns, now accompanied by [[Tri]], in search of a means to mend the Rift. Diving into the [[Still World]], where the consumed ruin lies decaying, Zelda finds what may be Link, but is surprised when he reveals himself as [[Echo Link|the Dark Echo of Link]], like the [[Dark Echo]]es that had replaced [[King of Hyrule#Echoes of Wisdom|her father]] and [[General Wright]], as well as [[Minister Lefte]], whom the duo had seen outside.

Using her new [[Tri Rod]] and the [[Echo]]es recorded within, Zelda defeats the Echo Link and recovers the [[Sword of Might]], albeit in a de-powered state. Proceeding further into the ruins, Zelda finds the true boss of the dungeon: the [[Seismic Talus]]. Upon defeating it, the [[Tris]] are released, allowing them to mend the Rift and restore the consumed area, and the people trapped within, including Minister Lefte, a villager from [[Suthorn Village|Suthorn]], and [[Lueberry]], whom Zelda had been sent by [[Impa#Echoes of Wisdom|Impa]], Lueberry's older sister, to see about the Rift situation.

===Aftermath===
Following this, Zelda can both enter the restored Suthorn Ruins, though for no necessary purpose, and revisit the Still World area where the consumed version remains, if she missed any [[Treasure]] in her earlier visit.
