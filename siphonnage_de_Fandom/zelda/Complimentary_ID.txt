==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Term|TWWHD|Beedle|link}} will send the {{Term|TWWHD|Complimentary ID}} attached to a {{Term|TWWHD|Letter|link}} to any {{Term|TWWHD|Postbox|link}} once {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} has accumulated 30 points on his membership card for {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle's Shop Ship|link}}.<ref name="TWW Points"/> The {{Term|TWWHD|Complimentary ID}} can be given to {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle}} in exchange for a verbal compliment which fills {{Term|TWWHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Life Gauge|link}}.<ref name="E 120"/>

==={{PH|-}}===
Once Link has accumulated 50 points on his membership card for {{Term|PH|Beedle's Shop Ship}}, {{Term|PH|Beedle}} will send him the {{Term|PH|Complimentary ID}} attached with a {{Term|PH|Letter}}.<ref name="PH Points"/> It can be given to {{Term|PH|Beedle}} in exchange for a verbal compliment.<ref>{{Cite|You're the best!<br/><br/>You're the BEST!<br/><br/>YOU'RE THE BEST!<br/><br/>And one more for good luck!<br/><br/>YOU'RE THE BESSSST!|Beedle|PH}}</ref>
