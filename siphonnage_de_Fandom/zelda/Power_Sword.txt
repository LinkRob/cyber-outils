The {{Term/Store|Power Sword|Power Sword|Series, TWoG}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TFoE}} and {{TWoG}}.<ref>{{Cite|Power Sword|N/A|TWoG}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{TFoE|-}}===
The {{Term|TFoE|Power Sword}} goes unnamed in {{TFoE}}. It is a stronger {{Term|TFoE|Sword|link}} that allows {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} to fire {{Plural|TFoE|Sword Beam|display=Power Blast|link}} when he is at full health.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=It is rumored that hidden in Koridai are ways to make your sword fire deadly Power Blasts.|game=TFoE|page=3}}</ref> {{Term|TFoE|Link}} receives the {{Term|TFoE|Sword}} from {{Term|TFoE|Anutu|link}} in {{Term|TFoE|Crater Cove|link}} after correcting him that the crab he caught is actually a {{Term|TFoE|Gohma|link}}.

[[File:TFoE Fire Sword Sprite.png|left]]
Later in his quest, {{Term|TFoE|Link}} can have his {{Term|TFoE|Power Sword}} upgraded into a stronger form that allows him to fire Power Blasts at any time, even when he is at low health. {{Term|TFoE|Link}} has his {{Term|TFoE|Sword}} tempered into this form after freeing {{Term|TFoE|Kulvan|link}} in {{Term|TFoE|Shipwreck Cliff|link}} and giving him a {{Term|TFoE|Fire Diamond|link}}. In its in-game cutscene, the {{Term|TFoE|Power Sword}} is shown to have become very powerful, capable of shooting devastating, explosive Power Blasts. 

==={{TWoG|-}}===
In {{TWoG}}, the {{Term|TWoG|Power Sword}} appears identically as in {{TFoE|-}}, allowing {{Term|TWoG|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda|link}} to fire Power Blasts when she is at full health. Zelda finds the {{Term|TWoG|Power Sword}} inside of a dark house in {{Term|TWoG|Sakado|link}} inhabited by {{Plural|TWoG|Moblin Spearthrower|link}} and {{Term|TWoG|Keese|link}}.

The {{Term|TWoG|Power Sword}} can also be upgraded into a stronger form that allows Zelda to fire Power Blasts at any time. Her {{Term|TWoG|Sword|link}} is tempered into this form by {{Term|TWoG|Yokan|link}} in {{Term|TWoG|Sakado}} after bringing him a {{Term|TWoG|Heat Crystal|link}}.
