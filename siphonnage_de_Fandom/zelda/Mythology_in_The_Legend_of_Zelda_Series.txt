==Golden Goddesses==
{{Main|Golden Goddesses}}
Before time began, there existed three Golden Goddesses, themselves sisters, who descended upon chaos to create the world of [[Hyrule]], known as [[Din (Goddess)|Din]], [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru]], and [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]].<ref>{{Cite|Before time began, before spirits and life existed... Three golden goddesses descended upon the chaos that was Hyrule...|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> Din created the land,<ref>{{Cite|Din... With her strong flaming arms, she cultivated the land and created the red earth.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> Nayru blessed it with the law,<ref>{{Cite|Nayru... Poured her wisdom onto the earth and gave the spirit of law to the world.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> and Farore filled it with life.<ref>{{Cite|Farore... With her rich soul, produced all life forms who would uphold the law.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> When they departed, they left behind at the point where they left the world a fragment of their power in the Sacred Relic known as the [[Triforce]],<ref>{{Cite|The three great goddesses, their labors completed, departed for the heavens. And golden sacred triangles remained at the point where the goddesses left the world. 
Since then, the sacred triangles have become the basis of our world's providence.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref> and the place where it came to rest became known as the [[Sacred Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|And, the resting place of the triangles has become the Sacred Realm.|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref>

==The Triforce and the Sacred Realm==
{{Main|Triforce, Sacred Realm}}
[[File:TLoZ Series Triforce Artwork.png|thumb|248x248px|The complete Triforce]]
The Triforce is a sacred golden relic left within the sacred ground by the Golden goddesses, Din (power), Nayru (wisdom), and Farore (courage) after their completion of the creation of the land of Hyrule. The Triforce was formed at the exact point at which the goddesses left the physical world. The piece on top is the [[Triforce of Power|Triforce of Power,]] the lower-left is the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] and the lower-right piece is the [[Triforce of Courage]]. Whomever touches the Triforce will be granted one wish, regardless of their intentions.

The area around the Triforce became the [[Sacred Realm]]. The Sacred Realm is an exact parallel to Hyrule. The [[Temple of Light]] sits at the center of the realm. Should [[Ganon|someone with evil intentions]] touch the Triforce, it would split due to his unbalanced heart and a [[Link|hero]] would appear to defeat him to restore order.

==Four Giants==
{{Main|Four Giants}}
[[File:FourGiantStory.png|left|thumb|261x261px|The Four Giants and Skull Kis]]
The Four Giants are the guardian gods and creators of [[Termina]]. They are located in the four cardinal drections of Termina: [[Woodfall]], [[Snowhead]], [[Great Bay]] and [[Ikana Canyon]]. Always vigilant, the giants told the people to [[Oath to Order|summon them]] should they ever need help. However, they are friendly and will play with lesser beings, such as the [[Skull Kid]] in {{MM}}.

Once the evil of [[Majora's Mask (Item)|Majora's Mask]] corrupted the Skull Kid, he trapped the giants and threatened Termina out of revenge, believing that they hated him. The legendary Hero of Time, after his [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|previous adventure in Hyrule]], set out to free the Giants to stop the Skull Kid. Once they were free, the young hero summoned the giants and they aided him in saving Termina. After [[Link]] stopped the evil of Majora's Mask, the giants returned to their respective regions to continue watching over Termina.

==Legend of the Hero of Time==
{{Main|Hero of Time}}

If someone with an evil mind has his wish granted, the world will be consumed by evil...That is what has been told...|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> One day long ago, a man of great evil named [[Ganondorf]], king of the [[Gerudo]] tribe, schemed to enter this magical realm and lay claim to the Triforce in order to become the true master of the world.<ref>{{Cite|This evil man ceaselessly uses his vile, sorcerous powers in his search for the Sacred Realm that is connected to Hyrule...|Great Deku Tree|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What Ganondorf is after must be nothing less than the Triforce of the Sacred Realm. He must have come to Hyrule to obtain it! And, he wants to conquer Hyrule...no, the entire world!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> With the unwitting assistance of a young highly courageous boy named [[Link]] and Hyrule's [[Princess Zelda]], Ganondorf was able to enter the Sacred Realm through much cunning and deceit.<ref>{{Cite|Though you opened the Door of Time in the name of peace... Ganondorf, the Gerudo King of Thieves, used it to enter this forbidden Sacred Realm! He obtained the Triforce from the Temple of Light, and with its power, he became the King of Evil...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> However, because his heart was not in balance, the Triforce separated into three component parts upon being touched by Ganondorf's tainted hands, leaving him with only the [[Triforce of Power]] and transforming the Sacred Realm into a corrupt world of evil and despair, now known as the [[Dark World|Evil Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|If the heart of the one who holds the sacred triangle has all three forces in balance, that one will gain the True Force to govern all. But, if that one's heart is not in balance, the Triforce will separate into three parts: Power, Wisdom and Courage. Only one part will remain for the one who touched the Triforce...the part representing the force that one most believes in.|Sheik|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|He went on to invade the Sacred Realm... Ganondorf had become the Evil King, and the Sacred Realm became a world of evil.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> The [[Triforce of Wisdom]] was then bestowed upon Princess Zelda and the [[Triforce of Courage]] upon Link respectively.<ref name=quote1>{{Cite|The one who holds the Triforce of Courage is... You, Link! And the other, who holds the Triforce of Wisdom... is the seventh Sage, who is destined to be the leader of them all... It is I, the Princess of Hyrule, Zelda.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Link himself was put into an enchanted slumber for 7 years until he matured enough to properly handle the Blade of Evil's Bane - the [[Master Sword]], which he had removed from the [[Pedestal of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|The Master Sword is a sacred blade which evil ones may never touch... Only one worthy of the title of "Hero of Time" can pull it from the Pedestal of Time.... However, you were too young to be the Hero of Time... Therefore, your spirit was sealed here for seven years. And now that you are old enough, the time has come for you to awaken as the Hero of Time!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Meanwhile, Zelda went into hiding (disguised as a [[Sheikah]] warrior known as [[Sheik]]) for the seven dark years of Ganondorf's evil reign over the kingdom as the Usurper King of Hyrule, awaiting the eventual time when Link would reappear and they could overthrow the Gerudo warlock together.<ref>{{Cite|I apologize for meeting you in disguise, but it was necessary to hide from the King of Evil.[...]I passed myself off as a Sheikah and hoped that you would return. I waited for seven years... And...now you are back. The dark age ruled by Ganondorf the Evil King will end!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref>

Sure enough, Link returned seven years later from the Sacred Realm, now grown up as the Hero of Time, and came in search of the mythical [[Sage#Ocarina_of_Time|Seven Sages]] who were said to have the power to bind Ganondorf and return the light of peace to Hyrule. Through much toil and trouble, the Hero of Time braved the many evils Ganondorf had cast upon the realm and sought out each of the major [[Dungeons in Ocarina of Time|temples]] throughout Hyrule and, on the instructions of [[Rauru]] the Sage of Light,<ref>{{Cite|Find the other Sages and save Hyrule!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> awakened each of the Six Sages, who curiously turned out to be figures he himself had befriended in his childhood ([[Saria]] the Sage of Forest, [[Darunia]] the Sage of Fire, [[Princess Ruto]] the Sage of Water, [[Impa]] the Sage of Shadow, and [[Nabooru]] the Sage of Spirit). After each of the Sages had been awakened, Princess Zelda revealed herself to be the seventh and final Sage and their leader, as well as revealing that she and Link both contained the other two pieces of the Triforce Ganondorf himself had failed to capture.<ref name=quote1/> Ganondorf soon captured Zelda, however, and hoped to use her as bait to lure the Hero of Time to the top of his [[Ganon's Castle|castle]] in order to steal the Triforce of Courage back from him as well as Zelda's.<ref>{{Cite|But, with the Triforce of Wisdom that Zelda has... When I obtain these two Triforces... Then, I will become the true ruler of the world!! If you want to rescue Zelda, come to my castle!|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> 

Link soon found the fiend's castle and, with the assistance of the remaining Sages,<ref>{{Cite|It's Rauru, the Sage. We six will gather our power to create a bridge to the castle where Ganondorf dwells...|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> entered Ganon's Castle, and fought his way to Ganondorf's throne room atop the castle's tower. Here, the Hero of Time confronted the King of Evil in an epic battle that ended with Ganondorf collapsing to the ground seemingly dead and Zelda released from her prison. The Evil King, with his "last breath" then attempted to crush the two in the ruin of the tower, however, when he began to bring down his own castle around them.<ref>{{Cite|This tower will collapse soon! With his last breath, Ganondorf is trying to crush us in the ruins of the tower! We need to hurry and escape!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> The pair along escaped in time, and although their lives are spared for the moment, Ganondorf suddenly emerges from the rubble in a burst of rage, and transforms himself into the Dark Beast [[Ganon]] - his actual form, using the Triforce of Power. The Hero of Time then fought his archenemy a second time in hand to hand combat and, though the battle was fierce, ultimately defeated him again with the might of the Master Sword and Triforce of Courage, allowing the Sages the time they finally needed to bind him and throw him back into the void of the Evil Realm before sealing the portal between the worlds.<ref>{{Cite|I'm using my power to hold the Evil King! You use your sword and deliver the final blow!|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ancient Creators of Hyrule! Now, open the sealed door and send the Evil Incarnation of Darkness into the void of the Evil Realm!!|Rauru|OoT}}</ref> Reverted back to his Gerudo form once more, Ganondorf screamed in rage that as long as the Triforce of Power was in his hand, he would return one day to exterminate the descendants of the Hero of Time and the Seven Sages to make them pay for his defeat and imprisonment.<ref name=quote3>{{Cite|YOU... CURSE YOU...ZELDA! CURSE YOU...SAGES!! CURSE YOU...LINK! Someday... When this seal is broken.... That is when I will exterminate your descendants!! As long as the Triforce of Power is in my hand....|Ganondorf|OoT}}</ref> While saying goodbye, Princess Zelda then used her power as a Sage to send the Hero of Time back to his own age for good in order to regain his lost childhood.<ref>{{Cite|Link, give the Ocarina to me... As a Sage, I can return you to your original time with it.|Princess Zelda|OoT}}</ref> Upon his departure, the Triforce of Courage split apart into [[Triforce|eight shards]] and scattered across the land to be found again one day by another Hero that would have need of its power in the distant future.<ref>{{Cite|When the Hero of Time was called to embark on another journey and left the land of Hyrule, he was separated from the elements that made him a hero. It is said that at that time, the Triforce of Courage was split into eight shards and hidden throughout the land.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> The Master Sword was also left in the Hero's wake, being returned to its pedestal in turn. With this act by Zelda, the Hero of Time vanished from this branch of Hyrule's history, leaving only his legend behind.

==The Oocca==
{{Main|Oocca}}
In Hylian mythology revealed in {{TP}}, the [[Golden Goddesses]] created the Oocca even before the Hylians. Some say that it was actually the Oocca who created the Hylians and that they are their ancestors.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, according to legend, Hyrule was made by the Hylians, who, as we all know, are the closest race to the gods. Yes, it said that a mysterious rod was handed down from the people of the sky, and it was called the Dominion Rod... From generation to generation, my ancestors have guarded the book that, by royal decree, was to be given to the messenger to the heavens.|Impaz|TP}}</ref> The Oocca are also much more advanced than the Hylains, as shown by the complex machinery in their home.
