{{Term/Store|Lester||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|Lester|N/A|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Lester}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} man who works at {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Frontier|link}}. He spends his days sitting by the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lester}}, he admits that he enjoys spices.<ref>{{Cite|The thing about me is....I just can't get enough spice in my life.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> In particular, he likes the spiciness of {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Spicyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy... I need that burny tingle... I need {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref> While he has the {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice|link}} he needs in order to make it, he has run out of {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now, I have {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} on hand, which I need to make {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}, but I'm fresh out of {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref> However, he is unable to leave {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable}} to acquire it.<ref>{{Cite|I can't just run off and leave the stable.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} if he ever has some.<ref>{{Cite|If you happen to get your hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}, can you give it to me?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Lester}} will thank him, as {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice}} keeps him warm.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure thing!] Thank you kindly, sonny boy. {{Color|BotW Blue|Curry rice}} always warms me right up.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> The "{{Term|BotW|Curry for What Ails You|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} will then begin.

After obtaining {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Rito Stable}} to give it to {{Term|BotW|Lester}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's that? You got your hands on some {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}? Can you show it to me?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Lester}} has confirmed its authenticity, he offers to buy it from {{Term|BotW|Link}} for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} all right! Hey... Could I ask you to part with that {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} for, say, {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to this deal, {{Term|BotW|Lester}} will be relieved that he can make {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice}} to keep warm.<ref>{{Cite|[It's all yours!] Thank you! With {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}}, I'll be able to make some {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}} to warm my soul.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> He then gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here—your {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Lester}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him more {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} if he has it, offering him {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice}} in return.<ref>{{Cite|If you come across any more {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}}, please bring it to me. Next time, I'll trade you some {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} for it.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> He also shares that by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} other ingredients with {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} and {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can make a variety of curry dishes.<ref>{{Cite|If you add other ingredients to {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}}, you can make all kinds of different curry rice dishes. Try it next time you have some spare ingredients.|Lester|BotW}}</ref> When he finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Lester}} afterwards, he will recognize him and ask if he has any {{Term|BotW|Goron Spice}} to trade for {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heeeey. It's the {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} guy. Got any more for me? Will you trade me your {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} for some {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}}?|Lester|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to the exchange, {{Term|BotW|Lester}} is expresses his joy to be able to make {{Term|BotW|Curry Rice}}, at which point he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} his {{Term|BotW|Hylian Rice}}.<ref>{{Cite|[It's all yours!] Thanks! I can use this {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron spice}} and {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}} to make some more {{Color|BotW Blue|curry rice}}! Here—your {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian rice}}!|Lester|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Lester}} claims he will not force him to do anything, noting that he looks like he needs a good meal.<ref>{{Cite|[Sorry, I can't.] I won't force you to part with it. You look like you could use a good meal, young man...|Lester|BotW}}</ref>

In {{TotK}}, Lester works as a stablehand in [[Lookout Landing]], a newly-built village located south of Hyrule Castle. [[Karson]] plans to help build a small stable for Lester to house the horses he cares for.
