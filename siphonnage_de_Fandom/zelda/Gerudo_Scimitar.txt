{{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} are one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} made by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}}. They are commonly wielded by {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} women, especially travelers that journey through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref> People who carry {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} are often seen carrying {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Shield|link}} as well. They are common in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, particularly around {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}, as well as in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} can be found inside {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. They may also be wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} throughout the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}}. The {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} also has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} when it is scanned by the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} is required to remake the {{Term|BotW|Scimitar of the Seven|link}}, along with five pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|To make a {{Color|BotW Red|Scimitar of the Seven}}, I'll need {{Color|BotW Blue|one diamond}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|five flint}}, and a {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo scimitar}}.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

Due to being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they can also conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. In addition, {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo Scimitar}} are unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
