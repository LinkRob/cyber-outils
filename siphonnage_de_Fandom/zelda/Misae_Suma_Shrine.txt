The {{Term/Store|Misae Suma Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Misae Suma Shrine}}<br/>Misae Suma's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Misae Suma's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Misae Suma Shrine is found in the {{Term|BotW|East Barrens|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially finds {{Term|BotW|Pokki|link}} leaning on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate Terminal|link}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. She's dehydrated and convinced she's going to die without one last taste of a {{Term|BotW|Noble Pursuit|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh, my throat... It's so dry... I can't move a muscle... If this really is the end... I wish I could have one last taste... One sip... One sip of an ice-cold {{Color|BotW Red|Noble Pursuit}}...|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> This activates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Perfect Drink|link}}".

When Link asks {{Term|BotW|Furosa|link}} at {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}} for a Noble Pursuit for Pokki, she reveals that she can't retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Ice|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Northern Icehouse|link}} herself.<ref>{{Cite|Pokki's collapsed? Hmm. I was to help her out, but I can't make a you-know-what without ice. There's ice at the {{Color|BotW Red|icehouse}}, but my legs aren't in the best shape for hauling ice...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Link offers to get the Ice for her, which she'll accept.<ref>{{Cite|[Should I get it for you?] Are you serious? You'll get it? That'd be a huge help.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She tells Link that one he explains the situation to the manager, {{Term|BotW|Anche|link}}, she'll let him take some Ice.<ref>{{Cite|Anche manages it, but I think if you explain the situation, she'll let you have some ice.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Furosa then says Link only needs to bring the Ice to the entrance to the ruins, at which point she'll take it to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you bring the ice to the {{Color|BotW Red|entrance to the ruins}}—between town and the icehouse—I can take it from there.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

At the Northern Icehouse, Anche allows Link to take some Ice for Furosa.<ref>{{Cite|I see... Well, if Furosa wants ice, then you should bring her some. You're permitted to take sone ice.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> She reminds him to move quickly so that it doesn't melt.<ref>{{Cite|You've taken the heat into account, right? If you move too slowly, the ice will surely melt.|Anche|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Ice Delivery (Mini-Game)|link|display=Ice Delivery}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.

Once Link has completed the Mini-Game and brought the Ice to Furosa, she says she'll be able to make the ultimate Noble Pursuit.<ref>{{Cite|With this, I'll be able to make the ultimate {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She asks Link to let Pokki know that it's waiting for her, believing she'll come running once she hears about it.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but could you please let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Tell her that I made the best one ever and I've got it waiting for her... Knowing her, she'll probably come running when she hears that there's an amazing drink with her name on it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

Back at Misae Suma Shrine, Link can tell Pokki about the drink.<ref>{{Cite|Did you say "best {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}} ever"? Pfft, I am GONE!|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> Suddenly rejuvenated, Pokki runs through the desert, back towards Gerudo Town.

With the Sheikah Slate Terminal cleared, Link is able to enter Misae Suma Shrine. Once inside, he's greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Misae Suma|link}}, who says he's proven himself by entering the Shrine.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Misae Suma. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Misae Suma|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The Misae Suma Shrine is one large room with no puzzles or enemies, just a straight path leading to Misae Suma. There is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the center of the room containing a {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}}.

As a reward for arriving at the Shrine, Misae Suma grants Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Misae Suma|BotW}}</ref> He then wishes Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Misae Suma|BotW}}</ref>
