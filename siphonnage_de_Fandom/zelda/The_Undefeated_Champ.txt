"{{Term/Store|The Undefeated Champ||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Undefeated Champ}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Sand-Seal Rally}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will find a {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} displayed in front of a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. However, when he tries to take the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne|link}} will stop him and say only champions of the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|link}} can touch it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Hey, hey! Hey, hey, hey! Hey!!!}} That item is only for those who have proven themselves in the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal races}}! Hands off!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref>

After calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will discover {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} and {{Term|BotW|Tali|link}} speaking to one another at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally}}.<ref>{{Cite|Finally, Tali!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Tali}} does not say anything,<ref>{{Cite|...|Tali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} takes offense at being interrupted.<ref>{{Cite|What do you want? The great Tali doesn't have time to chat with just anyone!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> Now that the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} is gone, they are able to make use of their race course again.<ref>{{Cite|You see, now that the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} is gone, we can finally use the race course.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, {{Term|BotW|Tali}} must focus on preparing for the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Tali}} is busy focusing her energy for the {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal race}}!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> Sensing an opportunity, though, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to challenge {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s record.<ref>{{Cite|Actually... Did you want to try to beat Tali's record? I'll warn you—they don't call her Dame of the Dunes for nothing.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} several questions. If he asks about the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race}}, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} is shocked to hear that not everyone knows about it.<ref>{{Cite|[Sand-seal race?] You've never heard of Hyrule's pastime? The world-famous {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal racing}}? I thought everyone knew about it. Isn't that shocking, Tali?|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> She explains that the goal of {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} is to surf with a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} through all of the arches before reaching the finish line.<ref>{{Cite|You surf with a {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}} and race to the finishb line while also going through arches. That's {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal racing}}!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> In addition, he can be disqualified by getting off his shield or leaving the boundaries of the course.<ref>{{Cite|And if you get off your {{Color|BotW Red|shield or leave the course bounds}}, you're disqualified.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} can beat {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s record of 1 minute and 30 seconds, he will be able to claim the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Tali's record is {{Color|BotW Red|1}}:{{Color|BotW Red|30}}! If you can beat that record, that {{Color|BotW Blue|trophy}} is yours.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> which is the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} that has been passed down by {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} champions for 10,000 years.<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|grand trophy}} that's been passed down by ten thousand years of sand-seal-racing champions.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> She then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} once again if he wants to challenge {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s record.<ref>{{Cite|Tali here is the current champion. Will you try to challenge her record?|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to race, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

{{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that there is an entry fee of 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} per race.<ref>{{Cite|I forgot to mention...<br/>Since you're racing against the champ's record, it'll be {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}} per race.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} pays her, she will be excited about the money and begin the race.<ref>{{Cite|[OK.] All right. Payday! Enough talk! Let's begin!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} announces to the rest of the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}} that a new challenger has appeared to challenge {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s record.<ref>{{Cite|A challenger has appeared to take on the Gerudo sand-seal race! Apparently one who does not mind risking life and limb! But will our current record fall? The one set by our undefeated champion?!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> With his {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} ready,<ref>{{Cite|The incomparable beast of the desert... The sand seal is ready to ride!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} wonders whether {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to reach the goal in under 1 minute and 30 seconds.<ref>{{Cite|Can our new contestant reach the goal in {{Color|BotW Red|less than 1:30}} while still passing under every arch?!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> With this, she begins the countdown for the race.<ref>{{Cite|The suspense is killing me! I'm going to start...the...countdoooooown!!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref>

The race course will take {{Term|BotW|Link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally}} through the {{Term|BotW|Palu Wasteland|link}} and into the {{Term|BotW|East Barrens|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} successfully completes the course in less than 1 minute and 30 seconds, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne}} will be shocked that he has beaten the record.<ref>{{Cite|It's a... A new record?!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> The spectators will gather around the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} with {{Term|BotW|Link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tali}}, who is impressed by {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s accomplishment.<ref>{{Cite|...<br/>Perfect...|Tali|BotW}}</ref> She allows him to take the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}}, as it now belongs to him.<ref>{{Cite|I believe this belongs to you...|Tali|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Tali}} says that the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} is special, as a legend concerning it has been passed down by {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} champions for 10,000 years.<ref>{{Cite|This trophy is something special, you know. Our champions have passed down a legend about it for ten thousand years.|Tali|BotW}}</ref> It states that, when the chosen one offers the "holy jewel" to the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, they will be granted a new power.<ref>{{Cite|The legend goes... "When the holy jewel is offered unto the pedestal, the chosen one will be granted a new power."|Tali|BotW}}</ref> While no one has been able to obtain this power,<ref>{{Cite|But that power has never been granted to anyone. Not even to me, obviously...|Tali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Tali}} senses a great power from {{Term|BotW|Link}} and encourages him to try.<ref>{{Cite|And yet... I sense a power emanating from you... The likes of which I have never felt in anyone else. You should give it a shot.|Tali|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Tali}} declares {{Term|BotW|Link}} the new champion of the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race}},<ref>{{Cite|And so... A new champion is born!|Tali|BotW}}</ref> and the crowd cheers for him.

After the ceremony, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pick up the {{Term|BotW|Grand Trophy}} and place it in the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}'s {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot}}. It will disappear as the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} activates, causing {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo Shrine|link}} to emerge from the sand.<ref>{{Cite|When you placed the jewel on the pedestal, a shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
