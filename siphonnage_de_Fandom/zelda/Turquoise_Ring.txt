'''Turquoise Ring''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
Turquoise Ring fires a spread of blue sparkles, which spread out as they travel. It is a costly yet strong Spell that can defeat the [[Kelpi]]es of the [[Shrine of Water]]. It costs 26 [[Rupee]]s to use.

Turquoise Ring is sold by [[Madame Kriggle]] in her magic shop in [[Great Wimbich]] for 20 Rupees.
