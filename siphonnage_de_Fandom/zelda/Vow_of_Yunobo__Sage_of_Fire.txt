The {{Term/Store|Vow of Yunobo, Sage of Fire||TotK}} is a [[Key Items in Tears of the Kingdom|Key Item]] in {{TotK}}.

==Location and Uses==
The vow is used to summon or dismiss the [[Sage Avatar|avatar]] of [[Yunobo]]. It is obtained after completing the [[Yunobo of Goron City]] quest line.

[[Link]] may exchange four [[Sage's Will]]s at any [[Goddess Statue]] to upgrade the Vow into a [[Solemn Vow of Yunobo, Sage of Fire]], which grants the avatar increased attack power when assisting in battle and a slightly shorter ability recharge.

The avatar of Yunobo cannot be summoned in [[Beneath Hyrule Castle]], [[Town]]s, and [[Stable]]s.
