The {{Term/Store|Scepter||Series, HW}} is a class of Weapon used by [[Cia]] in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Darkness}}. The Weapon is a magical rod that allows Cia to conjure powerful magical attacks, including summon [[Dark Link]]s to aid her in battle. The Scepter also doubles as a whip. Cia's battle style with the Scepter primarily relies on area coverage and long range over brute force.

==Variations==
The default Scepter is the '''Scepter of Time'''. The second form of the Scepter is the '''Guardian’s Scepter''', followed by the '''Scepter of Souls'''.

===Special Attack===
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Cia rises into the air as several circles of black magic form around her. Spreading her arms wide, the circles explode into pillars of dark flames, blowing enemies back. This attack deals damage in a circular range around Cia, and carries no Element.{{verify}}
