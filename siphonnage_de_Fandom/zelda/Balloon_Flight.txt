"{{Term/Store|Balloon Flight||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Balloon Flight}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Woodland Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Shamae|link}} talking about {{Plural|BotW|Balloon|link}} to herself.<ref>{{Cite|Baloons. Floaty balloons.|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> Upon speaking to her, she shares her belief people used to live in the sky, which she sometimes sees in her dreams.<ref name="Dreams">{{Cite|I sometimes see it in my dreams. Do you think that a long time ago people used to live in the sky? I think so...|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Shamae}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that she would like to see balloons floating in the sky,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, mister! Just once in my life, I want to see {{Color|BotW Red|balloons}} float right up into the sky!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> promising a treasure for the sky if he helps her.<ref>{{Cite|If you'll help me realize my {{Color|BotW Red|balloon}} dream, I'll give you a treasure I got from the sky as thanks!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Shamae}} tells him to put {{Plural|BotW|Balloon}} on a {{Term|BotW|Barrel|link}} to make it float.<ref>{{Cite|[Leave it to me.] Thank you! Now put some {{Color|BotW Red|balloons}} onto a barrel and make it float up high!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can brings {{Term|BotW|Shamae}} {{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon|link}}, at which point she tells him to tie some to the barrel next to her.<ref>{{Cite|Yaaaaay! You brought me {{Color|BotW Red|balloons}}! What a guy! You should tie those {{Color|BotW Red|balloons}} on to that barrel and make it soar into the sky!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> At least two {{Plural|BotW|Octo Balloon}} are needed in order for the {{Term|BotW|Barrel}} to start floating. Once this is done, {{Term|BotW|Shamae}} becomes overjoyed.<ref>{{Cite|Floaty! {{Color|BotW Red|Balloons}} soaring high in the sky! Fly up and away to the land in the clouds!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> Shamae tells Link that her dream is to float up to the land in the sky with a balloon, and then to ride a big bird.<ref name="Bird">{{Cite|I want to visit the land in the sky by {{Color|BotW Red|balloon}}. Then, I'm going to ride a really big bird. Yep, that's my dream!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, she rewards {{Term|BotW|Link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Star Fragment|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you, mister! This is a {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}} from the sky! Here, take it!|Shamae|BotW}}</ref>

** {{Term|BotW|Shamae}} wants to ride a big bird in the sky, alluding to {{Plural|Series|Loftwing|link}}.<ref name="Bird"/>
