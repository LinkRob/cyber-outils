{{Term/Store|Lonni||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lonni|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Lonni}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who travels between {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}} and a small shelter southeast of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}, walking along the {{Term|BotW|Torin Wetland|link}} on her way. She carries a {{Term|BotW|Hunter's Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}.

Often, {{Term|BotW|Lonni}} is confused for a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} by other travelers, which confuses her.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, you mistook me for a Gerudo? No worries, for some reason it happens all the time. No idea why!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! You startled me. Were you hoping to buy something from me? Sorry, but I'm not a merchant.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> Like many {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo}}, {{Term|BotW|Lonni}} is traveling in search of love, but she feels that her love of {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} has been scaring away potential candidates.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe because I'm traveling in search of true love. I'm single and ready to mingle, but my love of monsters scares people...|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm out here traveling in search of my true love. I'm single and ready to mingle, but my love of monsters scares people away.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, hello! I'm a bit surprised you decided to talk to me. My love of monsters often scares people away. No idea why!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} talks to her, she will be surprised, as she believes she is not as interesting as a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} or a {{Term|BotW|Monster}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're talking to me? I'm just a plain ol' Hylian traveler is all, nothing quite as interesting as a Gerudo or a monster.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> As she walks, she will occasionally claim that she needs to find someone to date.<ref>{{Cite|I need to find a date.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Where to meet someone...|Lonni|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Lonni}} is familiar with a variety of {{Plural|BotW|Monster}}. For example, she informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that most {{Plural|BotW|Octorok|link}} will drown if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, the only exception being the {{Term|BotW|Water Octorok|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Speaking of... Did you know Octoroks can drown even though they're octopi? Only Water Octoroks survive the plunge!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did you know Octoroks can drown even thought they're octopi?<br/>Only Water Octoroks survive the plunge!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> She also shares directions to the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}, though she warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he would encounter strong {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} on his way there.<ref>{{Cite|If you go north from here, you'll find the {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stables}}. I heard there are some strong monsters along the way. Just thinking about such strong monsters gives me goosebumps!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref>

In particular, {{Term|BotW|Lonni}} appreciates {{Plural|BotW|Stalnox|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stalnoxes are pretty big and creepy, but the way they throw their own bones at you...is just so cool!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> She hopes that she will find someone who loves {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} as much as she does, so they can talk about {{Plural|BotW|Stalnox}} all night.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I'll find someone who likes monsters as much as I do. Then we can stay up all night talking about Stalnoxes!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> When she is near the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Plains|link}}, she claims to have heard about a large {{Term|BotW|Monster}} in the area, referring to the nearby {{Term|BotW|Stalnox}}.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there's a really big monster somewhere in this region. Like...REALLY big.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> However, she does not have enough information to track it down.<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, that's all the info I've got on it. It's not enough to track it down, but I would love to see such a big monster.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> She occasionally remarks on this {{Term|BotW|Stalnox}}, stating that she gets so excited when she thinks about it that she starts to shake.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there's a great big monster along the way to {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stable}}, and I've been shaking ever since. Shaking with excitement, that is!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Even just hearing about a giant monster along the way to {{Color|BotW Blue|East Akkala Stable}}... It got me so excited—I'm still shaking!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref>

Aside from {{Plural|BotW|Monster}}, {{Term|BotW|Lonni}} is also interested in the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} that can be found in {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}. She shares a rumor that the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} can bring someone happiness with a single glance.<ref>{{Cite|Right now, I'm interested in rumors about the {{Color|BotW Red|Great Fairy}}. They say just a single glance will bring you happiness...|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I heard there's a {{Color|BotW Red|Great Fairy}} in this region. They say if you find her, you'll be blessed with happiness. I hope it's true!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> She has also heard that she can enhance people's {{Term|BotW|Armor|link|display=clothes}}.<ref>{{Cite|And that she can make your clothes more luxurious!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> In addition to this, she also has learned that a lot of treasure was left behind in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, though she is more excited about the {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} that have taken up residence within it.<ref>{{Cite|Seeing Hyrule Castle can make for a nice view if you can get it, but all the monsters around make it even nicer. I hear there's a lot of treasure left in the castle, but I get more excited thinking of all the monsters that must be in there!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref>

While she travels, {{Term|BotW|Lonni}} will remark on the {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}} or the time. Her favorite time of day is the morning.<ref>{{Cite|I just love the morning.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> During the night, she remarks that it is a good time to encounter {{Plural|BotW|Monster}}.<ref>{{Cite|Monsters prowl in the dark...|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> If it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}}, she will run for shelter, commenting that the {{Term|BotW|Weather}} is bad.<ref>{{Cite|What lousy weather!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> Once she is safe, she claims that she does not mind having to hide out, since it gives her a chance to meet someone in a romantic setting.<ref>{{Cite|I don't mind taking shelter from the rain. It raises my chances of meeting my special someone in a romantic setting!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Lonni}} is attacked by {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} or {{Plural|BotW|Maraudo Wolf|link}}, she will run away in fear.<ref>{{Cite|Yikes!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ruuuuun!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Yikes! Help!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Somebody, please!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh nooooo!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Nooo!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|*pant...pant* Just...let me catch my breath...|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> During the attack, she states that while she loves {{Plural|BotW|Monster}}, she hopes they will be struck by {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} so she does not have to deal with them.<ref>{{Cite|I may love monsters, but this is a bit much even for me. Maybe I'll get lucky and lightning will strike them.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves her, she will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I'm saved!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't even want to think about what would've happened if you hadn't come along to help.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> She may also wonder if this could be a destined encounter between them.<ref>{{Cite|You saved my life! I'm glad we were able to meet like this.<br/>Is this our destined encounter?|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> In addition, she might claim that this was the last day of her journey.<ref>{{Cite|And to think, I was just one day from this journey being over.|Lonni|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives an {{Term|BotW|Energizing Honeyed Apple|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please, take this. As a thank-you!|Lonni|BotW}}</ref>
