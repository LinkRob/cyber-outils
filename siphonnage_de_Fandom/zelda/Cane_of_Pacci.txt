The {{Term/Store|Cane of Pacci||Series, TMC}} is an item in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci}} is acquired after defeating a group of {{Plural|TMC|Spiny Chuchu|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames|link}}, and can be used to fire an energy beam that flips any object in its path. It can flip {{Plural|TMC|Jar|link}}, platforms, mine carts, some enemies,<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= Press the item button to fire a bolt from the Cane of Pacci. The bolt flips whatever it hits.|game = TMC|page = 17}}</ref> and blue-white pots that reveal {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} where {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can become {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized, and vice versa.

When fired at a hole in the ground, it energizes the hole to act like a trampoline, launching {{Term|TMC|Link}} into the air after he leaps in. By obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} is able to traverse previously inaccessible areas through the energizing and trampoline-like nature of these charged holes.

The {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci}} is essential to defeating {{Term|TMC|Gleerok|link}} within the {{Term|TMC|Cave of Flames}}, and can also be used to flip {{Plural|TMC|Spiked Beetle|link}} as an alternative to the {{Term|TMC|Shield|link}}.
