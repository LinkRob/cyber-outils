{{Term/Store|Triforce Shard|Triforce Shards|Series, TLoZ, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142 {{Exp Game|TLoZ, TWW}}}}</ref>

==Location and uses==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
[[File:TLoZ Link Triforce Shard Sprite.png|thumb]]
In the original {{TLoZ}}, in an attempt to prevent [[Ganon]] from obtaining the remaining [[Triforce of Wisdom]] after stealing the [[Triforce of Power]], [[Princess Zelda]] divided her respective piece of the [[Triforce]] into eight fragments and scattered them throughout the land of [[Hyrule]].<ref name="TLoZ Manual 3" /> She then sent [[Impa]] to find a man courageous enough to defeat the evil monster<ref name="TLoZ Manual 3">{{Cite manual|quote= |game= TLoZ|page= 3}}</ref>; however, Impa is eventually attacked and cornered by Ganon's minions. Just as there seemed no hope, a young adventurer known as [[Link]] saves her from peril.<ref name="TLoZ Manual 3" /> Impa saw the great potential in this boy, and decided he would be the hero to end her search. She presented him with a mission: to find the eight shards of the Triforce of Wisdom hidden in each [[Dungeon]] and rescue Zelda. Link set out to end Ganon's reign of evil and recover both the Triforce of Wisdom and Power. During gameplay, it is required to assemble all of the eight shards of the Triforce of Wisdom to enter [[Level 9|Level-9]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
[[File:WW Treasure02.png|thumb|221x221px|Link finding a shard of the Triforce of Courage in ''The Wind Waker'']]
In the [[Great Flood|flooded]] land of Hyrule in {{TWW}}, Link must recover the eight shards of the [[Triforce of Courage]] that hid throughout Hyrule after the departure of the [[Hero of Time]] in the events of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', as this piece was split due to the past Link being "separated by the elements that made him a hero".<ref>{{Cite|Once, long ago, he defeated Ganon and brought peace to the Kingdom of Hyrule... A piece of the Triforce was given to the Hero of Time and he kept it safe, much as Zelda kept hers. That sacred piece is known as the Triforce of Courage. When the Hero of Time was called to embark on another journey and left the land of Hyrule, he was separated from the elements that made him a hero. It is said that at that time, the Triforce of Courage was split into eight shards and hidden throughout the land.|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> With help from [[Tingle|Tingle's]] cartography skills, he sets out to find the [[Triforce Chart]]s that would show him the location of each of the 8 fragments (or only three of them in {{TWWHD|-}}, where the rest of them can be found directly). It is only after Link fully assembles the Triforce of Courage that he sets off to defeat Ganondorf and save both the [[Great Sea]] and Hyrule. When the young hero restored the shattered Triforce of Courage to become its new bearer since his predecessor, the Hero of Time, he gained the title Hero of Winds. In the original release of {{TWW|-}}, all of the Triforce Shards were hidden beneath the sea and had charts leading to them, while in {{TWWHD}} there were only three of them beneath the sea and the rest could be found in the caves where the charts originally were, due to controversy by the ''Zelda'' series fanbase finding the search in the original [[Nintendo GameCube|GameCube]] version too tedious alongside requiring a huge total of 3184 [[Rupee|Rupees]] to decipher the eight Triforce Charts altogether (each costs 398), which is only possible after obtaining the second [[wallet]] upgrade to carry up to 5000 Rupees at a time.
[[File:WW Triforce of Wisdom Shard.png|thumb|213x213px|The larger broken piece of the Triforce of Wisdom in ''The Wind Waker'']]
The necklace worn by the pirate leader [[Tetra]] is actually a large shard of the Triforce of Wisdom. Later in the game, [[Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule]] assembled it with the other half to become whole and transforms her into Princess Zelda, revealing her true identity.

== Trivia ==

* The Triforce of Power is the only piece of the Triforce never to be broken into fragments in ''The Legend of Zelda'' series, unlike the Wisdom and Courage counterparts. In the planned but ultimately scrapped third entry of the ''Oracle'' games, however, [[Ganondorf]] ironically splits it apart to safeguard his piece of the Triforce from Link and Princess Zelda, similar to the reason why Zelda herself divided the Triforce of Wisdom in ''The Legend of Zelda''.
* Both the Triforce of Wisdom and Courage share the same number of shards to be eventually reassembled - 8.
* The Triforce of Wisdom is the only piece in both instances of the franchise where any individual Triforce part is split into smaller fragments.
