'''Real Action Heroes''' (RAH) is a series of high-end figures produced and distributed by Medicom Toy. These figures are highly detailed and feature interchangeable parts, accessories, and the use of realistic materials such as fabric. Several figures based on {{TLoZ|Series}} have been released since 2014.
