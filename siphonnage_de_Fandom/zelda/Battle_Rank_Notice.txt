"{{Term/Store|Battle Rank Notice||Series, ST}}" is the name of three {{Plural|Series|Letter|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Battle Rank Notice|Letters|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
"{{Term|ST|Battle Rank Notice}}" {{Plural|ST|Letter}} are obtained by playing the multiplayer {{Term|ST|Battle Mode|link}} and accumulating enough points to raise {{Term|ST|Link|link}}'s battle rank. After climbing four battle ranks from "'''H'''" to "'''D'''", {{Term|ST|Link}} can return to the main quest and visit any {{Term|ST|Postbox|link}}, where the {{Term|ST|Postman|link}} will deliver a congratulatory {{Term|ST|Letter}} from the {{Term|ST|Battle Association|link}}, along with a random {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a package and a letter from...the Battle Association? What's a "battle"? And why does it have its own association? There's so much in the world I don't know about!|Postman|ST}}</ref> Two further "{{Term|ST|Battle Rank Notice}}" {{Plural|ST|Letter}} will be delivered after {{Term|ST|Link}} climbs three more battle ranks to "'''A'''", then two more ranks to "'''&#9733;&#9733;'''". The included {{Term|ST|Treasure}} increases in rarity each time.

The first {{Term|ST|Letter}} reads,
<blockquote>''You've achieved a battle rank of {{Color|ST Dark Red|D}} and have been registered as an intermediate competitor. To celebrate, here is a prize for you. Enjoy!''

''To boost your competitor level, you'll need to attain a battle rank of A. Best of luck, and fight the good fight!''

''Battle Committee''</blockquote>

The second {{Term|ST|Letter}} reads,
<blockquote>''You've achieved a battle rank of {{Color|ST Dark Red|A}} and have been registered as an advanced competitor. To celebrate, here is a prize for you. Enjoy!''

''To boost your competitor level, you'll need to attain a battle rank of &#9733;&#9733;. Best of luck, and fight the good fight!''

''Battle Committee''</blockquote>

The third {{Term|ST|Letter}} reads,
<blockquote>''You've achieved a battle rank of {{Color|ST Dark Red|&#9733;&#9733;}} and have been registered as a master competitor. To celebrate, here is a prize for you. Enjoy!''

''Congratulations on reaching such a high level of competition. Please continue to show the rookies how it's done!''

''Battle Committee''</blockquote>

Though {{Term|ST|Link}}'s battle rank may increase once more to "'''&#9733;&#9733;&#9733;'''", no {{Term|ST|Letter}} is received for achieving this rank.
