The {{Term/Store|Nurturing Warmth||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 133}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The [[Nurturing Warmth]] is the reward for completing [[Unicorn's Cave]], and is guarded by [[Digdogger]]. It is the fifth of the eight [[Essence of Nature|Essences of Nature]] collected by Link. Its description text reads: "Balmy days build strong saplings with their [[Nurturing Warmth]]!"<ref>"Balmy days build strong saplings with their [[Nurturing Warmth]]!" – N/A ([[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Seasons|Oracle of Seasons]])</ref>
