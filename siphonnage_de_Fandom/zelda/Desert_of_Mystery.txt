The {{Term/Store|Desert of Mystery|Deserts of Mystery|Series, ALttP, ALBW}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=229 {{Exp Game|ALttP}} }}</ref>{{Name Ref|ALBW, ALttP (Ishinomori)}}

==Features and Overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The {{Term|ALttP|Desert of Mystery}} is a wide open area filled with cacti. It is the location of the second {{Term|ALttP|Dungeon}} in the game, the {{Term|ALttP|Desert Palace|link}}, which contains the {{Term|ALttP|Pendant of Wisdom|link}}. The only other features are a few cliffs and caves, one which is the home of {{Term|ALttP|Aginah|link}}.

On a cliff only reachable with the {{Term|ALttP|Flute|link}}, there is a {{Term|ALttP|Portal (Object)|link}} to the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} hidden under a {{Term|ALttP|Rock|link}}. This is the only way to enter the {{Term|ALttP|Dark World|link}} equivalent of the {{Term|ALttP|Desert of Mystery}}, the {{Term|ALttP|Swamp of Evil|link}}. On another cliff is a tablet where {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} can get the {{Term|ALttP|Bombos Medallion|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
In {{ALBW}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Desert of Mystery}} is sealed off during {{Term|ALBW|Link}}'s search for the {{Plural|ALBW|Pendant of Virtue|link}}, making him completely unable to reach it. It is not until {{Term|ALBW|Link}} has access to {{Term|ALBW|Lorule|link}} that he can enter the {{Term|ALBW|Desert of Mystery|display=Desert}} via a {{Term|ALBW|Fissure|link}} located in the {{Term|ALBW|Misery Mire|link}}. Traversing the {{Term|ALBW|Desert of Mystery}} requires the {{Term|ALBW|Sand Rod|link}} to raise platforms to walk across so that {{Term|ALBW|Link}} can access different {{Plural|ALBW|Fissure}} between the {{Term|ALBW|Misery Mire}} and the {{Term|ALBW|Desert of Mystery|display=Desert}}. The {{Term|ALBW|Desert Palace|link}} also appears in the area, though its boss is fought in {{Term|ALBW|Lorule}}.
