==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Omelets can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Bird Egg]] in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they will heal some of {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. These dishes are common in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}, as making them only requires one to fry an egg until it's plump.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add several ingredients to Omelets, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Omelet''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Omelet}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating them will heal all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Omelet''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Omelet}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. They can be eaten to refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Omelet''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Omelet}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Consuming them will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Omelet''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Link can eat them to receive a boost to his movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Omelet''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. When eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Omelet''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. By eating these, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Omelet''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. They grant Link a boost to his attack power when eaten.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Omelet''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. If eaten, Link will receive a boost to his defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Omelet''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Omelet}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. Eating them will boost Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Omelet''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Omelet}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This simple dish is common all over Hyrule. Simply fry egg until it's nice and plump.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. When consumed, Link will gain one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
