'''Battle H8''' begins with the chosen Warrior placed just north of the Central Keep, which is inaccessible. Two groups of enemies are located westward, with the initial goal set to defeating the [[Lizalfos]] located at the northwestern section of the map.<ref>{{Cite|Defeat {{Color|HWDE Red|Lizalfos}}!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> After two minutes and thirty seconds, the Lizalfos will inspire the enemy troops, causing them to grow stronger.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Red|Lizalfos}} has inspired the {{Color|HWDE Red|enemy troops}}! They have grown stronger!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref> Once 100 enemies have been slain, a King Dodongo will appear. After three and six minutes have passed, Magic Jar Transport Troops will spawn.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Green|Magic Jar Transport Troops}} appeared!|Battle Log|HWDE}}</ref>

Completing this Battle opens up squares [[#Battle H7|H7]] and [[#Battle G8|G8]] with a C-Rank Victory, and Square H9 with a B-Rank Victory.
