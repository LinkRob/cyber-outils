==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>This is {{Color|SS Blue|Pumm}}, the somewhat stubborn proprietor of the Lumpy Pumpkin, which he runs with his daughter.</p><p>Their famous pumpkin soup is popular in Skyloft for its nutrient content and revitalizing properties.</p>

Pumm is the owner of the [[Lumpy Pumpkin]] pub. His [[Pumpkin Soup]] is renowned in [[Skyloft]] for its ability to restore health.<ref>{{Cite|Their famous pumpkin soup is popular in Skyloft for its nutrient content and revitalizing properties.|Fi|SS}}</ref> His daughter, [[Kina]], serves as a waitress at the pub.<ref>{{Cite|The Lumpy Pumpkin is a little gathering spot on an oblong island to the southeast of Skyloft. The owner's daughter, {{Color|SS Blue|Kina}}, works there. She has the most lovely voice.|Owlan|SS}}</ref>

If [[Link]] breaks the chandelier in the Lumpy Pumpkin, Pumm will make him do various tasks to pay off the damages, such as delivering Pumpkin Soup to [[Eagus]],<ref>{{Cite|The Knight Commander is a regular here. He says he can't get his day started without a bowl of my soup!|Pumm|SS}}</ref> helping Kina harvest [[Pumpkin]]s,<ref>{{Cite|This should be an easy one for you. Kina spends her afternoons picking pumpkins in the patch out front.|Pumm|SS}}</ref> and performing a duet with Kina after the young hero has obtained the [[Goddess's Harp]].<ref>{{Cite|Ahh! You came! Let me see... What was it that I wanted you to do next...? Hey! What's that?! That's a strange-looking instrument you have there... Oh yes! You might just be surprised to hear that Kina is actually quite the singer! It wasn't that long ago that she would sing for our customers! But she had to stop because her partner quit. Think you'd be willing to do a quick duet with Kina?|Pumm|SS}}</ref> 

Later, Link must help Pumm deliver a basin of Pumpkin Soup to [[Levias]] to find the cause of his abnormal behavior.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|SS Blue|Levias}}... What about him? Sure, I know Levias well, but I wasn't aware anything was wrong... Every year I offer him an entire basin full of my {{Color|SS Red|pumpkin soup}}! But lately a huge thunderhead has been brewing there, and I haven't yet made this year's offering. I tell you, I feel horrible about it! You say you'll deliver a pot of my soup to Levias? Huh. I don't know how you plan to fly into that huge thunderhead brewing out there, but... If that's the case though, I'm going to do everything I can to make Levias the best batch of soup ever! You wait right here! I'm getting inspired to fire up some soup like I've never souped before!|Pumm|SS}}</ref> Link must summon [[Scrapper]] to help carry the soup to Levias.

==Nomenclature==
Both his and his daughter's names are a pun on "pumpkin".
