{{Term/Store|Blue Pesto|Blue Pestos|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 197}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TMC|Blue Pesto}} are found in {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized areas and {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. They attack by dropping dirt balls onto {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, covering him in earth; this can attract {{Plural|TMC|Moldworm|link}} to attack him. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} throws {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}} at a {{Term|TMC|Blue Pesto}}, it can pick them up and drop them back onto him. Using {{Plural|TMC|Remote Bomb|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can defeat a {{Term|TMC|Blue Pesto}} by detonating the {{Term|TMC|Remote Bomb|display=Bomb}} while the {{Term|TMC|Blue Pesto|display=Pesto}} is carrying it.
