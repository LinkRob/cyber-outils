==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Stealth Tights are a piece of [[Armor]] which can be purchased at [[Enchanted]] in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. According to their description, the noise-suppressing cloth used to make the Tights are a hallmark of ancient [[Sheikah]] technology.<ref name="Inventory"/> When worn, they provide one level of Stealth Up. The Stealth Tights can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Stealth Tights, alongside the [[Stealth Mask]] and [[Stealth Chest Guard]], compose the [[Stealth Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a [[Night Speed Up]] [[Set Bonus]].
