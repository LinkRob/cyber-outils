{{Term/Store|Washa's Bluff||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Washa's Bluff|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Washa's Bluff is located in [[Hyrule Ridge]] between [[Illumeni Plateau]] and [[Satori Mountain]]. It is a large oval-shaped plateau similar to the nearby Illumeni Plateau. The terrain is very grassy and rough, with many small ridges and cracks running through the earth. Along the bluff, many misshapen [[tree]]s sprout from the ground, which resemble ''Dracaena cinnabari'' trees.

In the southern area of the bluff, a gigantic misshapen tree sprouts out of the ground, and atop this tree [[Kass]] can be found. Behind Kass, a small structure holds a table which [[Kass's Journal]] rests. If [[Link]] speaks to Kass, he will state that he knows a song that speaks of Washa's Bluff.<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place. Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If Link asks to hear the song, Kass will oblige and sing it for him. This starts the [[Shrine Quest]] [[Under a Red Moon]]. Completing this quest reveals the [[Mijah Rokee Shrine]] to Link.
