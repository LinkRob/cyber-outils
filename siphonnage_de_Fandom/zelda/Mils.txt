{{Term/Store|Mils||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mils|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mils}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who hunts for treasure throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} alongside his sister, {{Term|BotW|Mina|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's where we come in. We're treasure hunters, searching for a one-in-a-million payday.|Mina|BotW}}</ref> The siblings will be under attack by a pair of {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} comes upon them. They can be encountered near the {{Term|BotW|Exchange Ruins|link}}, in the {{Term|BotW|Gatepost Town Ruins|link}}, and at the {{Term|BotW|Rauru Settlement Ruins|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Mils}} carries a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}}.

While {{Term|BotW|Mils}} is being accosted, he will draw his {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword}} to defend himself.<ref>{{Cite|I'll take you down!|Mils|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let's go, you filth!|Mils|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Have at you!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> During this time, he resolve to give the {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} a fight they will not forget.<ref>{{Cite|I'll give them a fight they won't forget!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> He may also wonder why this is happening to him.<ref>{{Cite|Gah! Why is this happening to me?!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Mina}} is knocked unconscious while {{Term|BotW|Mils}} is fighting, he will be concerned for her.<ref>{{Cite|Mina! They'll pay for this!|Mils|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, no... Not Mina...|Mils|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Mils}} is the one knocked out, he will note that he was useless against the {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}}, and he considers whether he should have played dead.<ref>{{Cite|I was totally useless against them. Maybe I should have played dead...|Mils|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves {{Term|BotW|Mils}}, he will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|Haah, haah...|Mils|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Phew...|Mils|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm saved!|Mils|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's fine...|Mils|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, he may thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his assistance.<ref>{{Cite|I was cutting it pretty fine there. Thanks for the save!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> He might also wonder what would have happened had {{Term|BotW|Link}} not helped them.<ref>{{Cite|Where would I be now if you hadn't come along? Nowhere good, that's for sure. You should have this.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That was nice of you. Here, something for your troubles.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} have been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Mils}} will thank {{Term|BotW|The Goddess Hylia|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Mina}} is safe, and he recalls that it was dangerous to go treasure hunting at their current location.<ref>{{Cite|Thank Hylia that Mina is safe. I knew it wasn't smart to go treasure hunting in these parts.|Mils|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Mils}} searches for treasure, {{Term|BotW|Mina}} will direct him to hurry,<ref>{{Cite|Hurry it up, Mils!|Mina|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Mils}} apologizes for.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry about that.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mils}} during this time, he states that any treasure at their current location already belongs to him and {{Term|BotW|Mina}}, and he discourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} from searching on his own.<ref>{{Cite|I want to be clear—whatever treasure is to be had here belongs to us. So don't go poking around.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> However, he then remarks that nobody in their right mind would be searching for treasure there to begin with.<ref>{{Cite|Not that anyone in their right mind would be poking around here for treasure to begin with.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mina}} is the one who decides where they search, but {{Term|BotW|Mils}} only wants to return home.<ref name="Sister"/> Hearing him speaking, {{Term|BotW|Mina}} orders {{Term|BotW|Mils}} to keep searching for treasure.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! We're not here for a chin wag! Hurry up and find some treasure!|Mina|BotW}}</ref> After this, {{Term|BotW|Mils}} grumbles to himself about family.<ref name="Family">{{Cite|Ugh... Family...|Mils|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mina}}, she warns him to stay away from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}, since it is surrounded by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm guessing you are too. So as a tip from one pro to another, stay clear of Hyrule Castle. It's protected by things called {{Color|BotW Red|Guardians}}. If they spot you, all bets are off on whether you survive.|Mina|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mils}} joins their conversation and claims that people who are spotted by {{Plural|BotW|Guardian}} do not live long afterwards, which makes him nervous.<ref>{{Cite|They say once you're in a Guardian's sights, you don't have long to live. I get nervous just thinking about 'em.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mina}} remarks that he should focus on finding treasure, or else she will give him something to worry about.<ref>{{Cite|All right, you, go find some treasure, or I'll give you something to be nervous about!|Mina|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mils}} then grumbles about family.<ref name="Family"/>

During "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Urbosa's Song|link}}," which is a {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack, {{Term|BotW|Mils}} and {{Term|BotW|Mina}} can be encountered at the back entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}. They came in search of the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb|link}}, which the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} stole from the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? Are you looking for treasure too? The {{Color|BotW Red|Yiga Clan}}... They took one of the Gerudo Tribe's treasures in there, a {{Color|BotW Red|sacred orb}} or something. It looked like it could sell for a high price, so I followed them. But... Well...|Mina|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Mils}} is surprised that so many members of of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga}} remain, as he had heard they were defeated by a traveling swordsman.<ref>{{Cite|I heard the Yiga Clan were taken out by a traveling swordsman. And yet... There are still an awful lot of 'em around! They seem to be hoarding bananas too. Maybe they're still up to no good?!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> He has been able to determine that the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}} is located on the second floor on the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|display=Hideout}}.<ref>{{Cite|I know one thing for sure... The {{Color|BotW Red|orb they stole from the Gerudo tribe}} is in their second floor storage area.|Mils|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Mils}} believes that infiltration would be too dangerous, as an important part of being a great treasure hunter is staying alive.<ref>{{Cite|But getting there is too dangerous. Staying alive is essential to being a great treasure hunter!|Mils|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} exits the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout}} with the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}}, {{Term|BotW|Mils}} will be shocked that he was able to steal it.<ref>{{Cite|Did you really go in there... and nab {{Color|BotW Red|that orb}}? You stole it, yeah? You really did?!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> He claims that when the {{Term|BotW|Yiga}} find out, they will chase {{Term|BotW|Link}} for the rest of time.<ref>{{Cite|Yikes. If they find out, they'll surely chase you down until the end of time!|Mils|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} throws the {{Term|BotW|Sacred Orb}} into the pit at the rear of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|display=Hideout}}, he and {{Term|BotW|Mina}} will run to the edge of the pit. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mils}}, he angrily asks what {{Term|BotW|Link}} was thinking.<ref>{{Cite|Oooh... You threw the precious treasure down below... What were you thinking?!|Mils|BotW}}</ref>
