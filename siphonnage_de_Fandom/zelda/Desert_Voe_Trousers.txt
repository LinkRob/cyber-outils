==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Desert Voe Trousers are pieces of [[Armor]] that can be purchased in the [[Gerudo Secret Club]] in [[Gerudo Town]] once "[[The Secret Club's Secret]]" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} has been completed. They can also be bought in the [[Rhondson Armor Boutique]] in [[Tarrey Town]] once the "[[From the Ground Up]]" Side Quest has been completed. When worn, they provide one level of Heat Resistance.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Desert Voe Trousers can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Desert Voe Trousers, alongside the [[Desert Voe Headband]] and [[Desert Voe Spaulder]], compose the [[Desert Voe Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Shock Resistance [[Set Bonus]].
