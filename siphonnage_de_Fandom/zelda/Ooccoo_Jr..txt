{{Term/Store|Ooccoo Jr.||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character and an {{Term|TPHD|Item|link}} in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 277"/>

==Biography==
==={{TP|-}}===
{{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo Jr.}} is {{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo|link}}'s only son. By {{Term|TPHD|Warping|link}} to his mother, {{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo Jr.}} allows {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} to return to a certain point inside a {{Term|TPHD|Dungeon|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo Jr.}} is a young member of the {{Term|TPHD|Oocca|link}} tribe. {{Term|TPHD|Ooccoo Jr.}} writes very little, and this is evident in his {{Term|TPHD|Letter|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Link}} after the completion of the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, kid! Awesome!|Ooccoo Jr.|TP}}</ref><ref name="letter"/>
