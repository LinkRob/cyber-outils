The {{Term/Store|Tingle Trophy||Series, TMC}} is an {{Term|TMC|Item|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 141}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
In {{TMC}}, the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Siblings?|link}} believe {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fusing}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} will help them meet {{Plural|TMC|Fairy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|They believe fusing Kinstones will help them meet fairies, so they are recording Kinstone data on their maps.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Link}} makes 100 {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}}, {{Term|TMC|Tingle}} will turn the {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Bag|link}} into the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Trophy}} in his {{Term|TMC|Inventory|link}}. After obtaining the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Trophy}}, {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} can no longer be found in the {{Term|TMC|Overworld|link}} from cutting {{Plural|TMC|Grass|link}} or defeating {{Plural|TMC|Enemy|link}}; however, they can still be found in {{Plural|TMC|Chest|link}} and bread from the {{Term|TMC|Bakery|link}}. Once the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Trophy}} is obtained, the {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} gathered by {{Term|TMC|Link}} can still be viewed by selecting the {{Term|TMC|Tingle Trophy|display=Trophy}}.
