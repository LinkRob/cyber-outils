==Location and Uses==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod}} can be obtained from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} after completing the {{Term|ALBW|Eastern Palace|link}}. It costs 50 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}} to rent, and 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}} to purchase. When used, the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod}} creates a whirlwind in place that lifts {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} into the air for several seconds. {{Term|ALBW|Link}} cannot move around while airborne, but he can use the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod|display=Rod}} to drop onto moving platforms that pass under him, or to enter the path of a {{Term|ALBW|Wind|link}} gust.

The large whirlwind that the {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod}} creates when he used can also throw other objects into the air, including {{Plural|ALBW|Jar|link}}, stone tiles, and some items in {{Plural|ALBW|Tree|link}}. {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} caught in the whirlwind will become dizzy for a few seconds. The {{Term|ALBW|Tornado Rod}} is required to entering the {{Term|ALBW|House of Gales|link}}.
[[File:ALBW Nice Tornado Rod Icon.png|left|50px|Nice Tornado Rod sprite]]

It can be upgraded to the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Tornado Rod|link}} by bringing it to {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}} after rescuing 10 baby {{Plural|ALBW|Maiamai|link}}. This upgraded version has a bigger gust radius and deals light damage to {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy}}.
