{{Term/Store|Karat Crab|Karat Crabs|Series, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 186 {{Exp Game|ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALBW|-}}===
{{Plural|ALBW|Karat Crab}} are the [[Lorule]]an counterparts of {{Plural|ALBW|Sand Crab}} in {{ALBW}}. They have darker coloring and deal four times more damage than {{Plural|ALBW|Sand Crab}}. {{Plural|ALBW|Karat Crab}} move slowly in vertical directions, but will dash horizontally at high speeds. One of their claws is much larger than the other. They are found primarily in the {{Term|ALBW|Southern Ruins|link}} surrounding the {{Term|ALBW|Swamp Palace|link}} and around {{Term|ALBW|Lorule Lake|link}}.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Karat Crab}} appear in [[The Dunes]] and the [[Desert Zone]] of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}} in {{TFH}}. They can be found hiding underneath moving {{Plural|TFH|Sand Mound|link}}, which can be blown away with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}.
