==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Her name is {{Color|SS Blue|Jellyf}}. She is the leader of the Parella race of aquatic creatures.</p><p>More intelligent than other Parella, they follow her lead. She serves the Water Dragon, {{Color|SS Blue|Faron}}.</p>
}}

Jellyf is a [[Parella]] who lives in [[Lake Floria]]. She serves as the leader of the Parella and acts as a servant to the [[Faron (Dragon)|Water Dragon Faron]].

When [[Link]] first encounters Jellyf while searching for the Water Dragon Faron, she is initially afraid of him.<ref>{{Cite|Ack! Another human! Ok, Ok... I just have to avoid eye contact and back away slowly...|Jellyf|SS}}</ref> As Link follows her through the water channel, she quickly becomes annoyed with him and swims between a wooden grate, believing that he cannot reach her now.<ref>{{Cite|Quit following me, pest! If you don't move along, I'll be forced to tell Her Excellence the Water Dragon about your little incursion!|Jellyf|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ha-HA! Didn't see that coming, did you? Yep, there's no way you're going to be able to follow me now! So, what now? Are you going to cry? If you think you can make it through, just go ahead and try!|Jellyf|SS}}</ref> However, Link manages to destroy the grate by spinning into it, to Jellyf's shock.<ref>{{Cite|GAH! How is that even possible? I've never seen a human pull off a spin like that!|Jellyf|SS}}</ref> However, after seeing Link's [[Water Dragon's Scale]], Jellyf realizes the he is the emissary of the [[Hylia|Goddess]].<ref>{{Cite|Wait... That's a {{Color|SS Blue|Water Dragon's Scale}}! Does that make you the emissary of the goddess, like the Water Dragon said?|Jellyf|SS}}</ref> Jellyf informs Link that [[Ghirahim]] had invaded the Lake and attacked Faron with his horde of monsters, wounding her in the process.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, about that... It happened really recently. This weirdo came in here with a gang of monsters and went right after Her Excellence. My lady the Water Dragon is a fierce fighter, and, boy, did she gobble up most of those monsters in the first few seconds. Thing is, there were just so many of them! While she was distracted, the weirdo leading all those creatures managed to wound her.|Jellyf|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Shortly before your arrival, I was attacked by a pompous creature calling himself {{Color|SS Blue|Ghirahim}}. I stood against this self-proclaimed Demon Lord, but I was wounded in the encounter.|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> However, she also explains that Faron is running low on [[Sacred Water]] needed for her recovery.<ref>{{Cite| She's healing up now, but she says we don't have enough special water for her to make a full recovery.|Jellyf|SS}}</ref> She then implores that Link help Faron and guides him through Lake Floria's submerged tunnels to her lair.

Along the way, Jellyf assists Link by informing him of the [[Froak]]s and the [[Cranioc]] that patrol the way. At the very end of the waterway, Jellyf opens the door to Faron's dwelling by inserting her head crest into its lock as a key.

==Trivia==
*Jellyf is the only Parella who is visibly different from her kind, being red instead of blue and having a larger head crest.
*Hitting Jellyf with an underwater dash while following her through Lake Floria will cause [[Rupee]]s to fall out of her.

==Nomenclature==
Jellyf's name is likely derived from or a shortening of {{Wp|jellyfish}}.
