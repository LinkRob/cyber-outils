The {{Term/Store|Royal Rapier||Series, CoH}} is an item in {{CoH}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Royal Rapier}} {{Icon|CoH Zelda}}<br/>Legendary weapon. Deals {{Color|CoH Red|3 damage}} ({{Color|CoH Red|2}} if adjacent to an enemy) and lunges you forward 1, knocking back foes at the cost of stamina.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|CoH|Royal Rapier}} is a special {{Term|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} after she completes the {{Term|CoH|King of Hyrule|link}}'s trial within {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My daughter... {{Color|CoH Blue|Zelda}}... What has happened while I slept? ... ... I see... For generations, our family has passed down a special weapon... If you can complete the trial in the vault, take it, and put a stop to {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}}!|King of Hyrule|CoH}}</ref> It is locked inside a blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} located at the end of the vault after {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} maneuver past the fireballs from {{Term|CoH|Green Gargoyle|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Purple Gargoyle|link}} and either cross the gap with the {{Term|CoH|Hookshot|link}} or {{Term|CoH|Longshot|link}}, or use the {{Term|CoH|Magic Boomerang|link}} to break open the {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest}} and retrieve the {{Term|CoH|Royal Rapier|display=Rapier}}.

It functions similarly to the {{Term|CoH|Rapier|link}} where it lunges {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}} forward after she knocks back an enemy, but deals an output of 2 or 3 damage instead of 1.<ref name="Inventory"/>
