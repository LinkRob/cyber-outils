{{Term/Store|Brown Skulltula|Brown Skulltulas|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= Brown Skulltula |game= CoH |system= Nintendo Switch |version= 1.0.0 |country= United States of America}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Brown Skulltula}} are stronger than {{Term|CoH|Skulltula|link}}, possessing more health then their weaker counterpart and can shoot their web in adjacent directions only. They move to the beat of the music, coming down from the ceiling for a total of five beats, firing a web on the third beat before retracting themselves back up to the ceiling on the sixth beat. Afterwards, they will disappear for another four beats before reappearing on the fifth beat in order to repeat the pattern.
