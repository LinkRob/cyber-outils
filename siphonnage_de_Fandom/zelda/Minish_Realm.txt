The {{Term/Store|Minish Realm||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Furthermore, the royal family kept the existence of the land of the Picori, the Minish Realm, hidden.|book=HH|page=78}}</ref> 

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm}} is the homeland of the thumb-sized {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} race that is briefly seen in a cutscene from {{TMC|-}}. As the homeland of the {{Term|TMC|Minish}}, it is also the homeland of {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}, an evil {{Term|TMC|Minish}} that would eventually become an infamous wind demon, and his former master {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}, one of the greatest sages of the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} race. This {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|display=Realm}} is connected to the land of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} via the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|link}} that only opens once very briefly every 100 years.<ref>{{Cite|The doorway to the sanctuary opens only once every hundred years!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> When this door opens, the two worlds are connected via the {{Term|TMC|Elemental Sanctuary|link}} that is located within the courtyard of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The sanctuary is a strange realm, trapped between two worlds. It is the bridge between the Minish world and the human world.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> Though {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} originally called this {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|display=Realm}} home, he became enchanted by the evil that could come from the hearts of men and longed to become a sorcerer in his own right. He seized his chance when he stole the {{Term|TMC|Mage's Cap|link}} from his master's workshop and fled through the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}}, which coincidentally was opening at the same time, hoping to acquire the legendary {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} wherever it was in the land of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}, having been cursed into the shape of a cap himself by {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}, followed his former apprentice through the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|display=Door}} in an attempt to stop him, and after {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s defeat and his subsequent restoration to his original form, {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}} returned to the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm}} through the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door}} just as it closed for another century.

==Hyrule Through the Eyes of the Minish==
The term "Minish World" is also used by {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} to refer to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} itself, only as seen and lived in larger scale, through the eyes of a tiny {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}. It is accessed through {{Plural|TMC|Minish Portal|link}} via the use of {{Term|TMC|Ezlo}}.<ref>{{Cite|You who desire to enter the realm of the Minish, seek out the hidden portal.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|So this perfectly normal-looking stone was another portal to the Minish world!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's right! Even some human objects are portals to the Minish world!|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref> In the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm}}, normal enemies appear giant, and some even function as {{Plural|TMC|Boss|link}}, like the guardians at the end of the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, two {{Plural|TMC|Dungeon|link}} entered and explored exclusively from the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm}}. 

The [[Overworld]] {{Term|TMC|Minish}} create homes in mushrooms, old boots, pots, holes in house drywall, and cracks in the ground, and adorn them with objects commonly found in the surrounding area, along with small tables, chairs, and beds.
