==Location and Uses==
The Snowquill Headdress can be purchased at the [[Brazen Beak]] in [[Rito Village]], alongside the [[Snowquill Trousers]] and [[Snowquill Tunic]]. All three of these clothing items are part of the [[Snowquill Set]], which makes {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} unfreezable when worn together. Wearing the Snowquill Headdress on its own will grant Link a level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/>

The Headdress can be both [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] and upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
