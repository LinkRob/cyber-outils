The {{Term/Store|Katah Chuki Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Katah Chuki Shrine}}<br/>A Minor Test of Strength}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Minor Test of Strength''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Katah Chuki Shrine}} is surrounded by rocky outcrops of the {{Term|BotW|Quarry Ruins|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Katah Chuki|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Katah Chuki. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this combat trial.}}|Katah Chuki|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Katah Chuki Shrine}} features a large, central chamber which acts as an arena. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the center of the arena, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II|link}} wielding a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Spear|link}} will rise from a pit to challenge Link.

In order to reach the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat the {{Term|BotW|Guardian Scout II}}. Doing so will cause the portcullis in the back of the chamber to rise. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} interacts with {{Term|BotW|Katah Chuki}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for overcoming the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your triumph over the test of strength subverts a prophecy of ruin. From the ashes of Hyrule, a hero rises. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Katah Chuki|BotW}}</ref> Following this, {{Term|BotW|Katah Chuki}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Katah Chuki|BotW}}</ref>

Behind the portcullis, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Royal Halberd|link}} can be recovered from inside.
