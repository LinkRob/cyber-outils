The {{Term/Store|Cap of the Hero||Series, BotW}} is an item in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Cap of the Hero}}<br/>According to legend, this cap was once treasured by an ancient hero. It's quite the simple cap, yet there's something about it that's just so appealing...|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Cap of the Hero is a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the 30th Anniversary 8-Bit Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Cap of the Hero. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Cap of the Hero, alongside the [[Tunic of the Hero]] and [[Trousers of the Hero]], comprise the [[Hero Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from the original [[Legend of Zelda]]. When it has been enhaced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
