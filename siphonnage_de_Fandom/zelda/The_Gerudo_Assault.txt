'''The Gerudo Assault''' is a memory in {{TotK}}.

== Overview ==
During twilight on a cliff near the then-ruling place of Hyrule, a group of [[Gerudo]], led by a man named [[Ganondorf]], observe Hyrule down below. Ganondorf then vows that Hyrule will bow down before him. Upon making a gesture with his right arm, a Gerudo plays a trumpet that proceeded to send [[Molduga]]s down to Hyrule as a means to decide whether they can withstand a full-scale Gerudo assault. The Hylian sentries notice their arrival, and realize it's an entire swarm. Rauru, Sonia, and Zelda then arrive to the front lines. Zelda is shocked that the Gerudo would send a swarm, although Sonia and Rauru prepare their magic to dispell the swarm. Zelda follows their lead, with Sonia also noticing that Zelda's Secret Stone is emitting significantly more power. The combined magic forms a beam that completely obliterated the swarm. The Gerudo observing this are shocked at the blast wiping out their Moldulga, with Ganondorf grudgingly acknowledging that he'll need far more than brute strength if he is to conquer Hyrule. However, he then notices Rauru's secret stone, and smiles, realizing he could use that to his advantage.
