{{Term/Store|Insect Cavern||Series, FPTRR}} is the fourth {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon}} in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Entrance to the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|display=Cavern}}==
The structure is located on a mound located within the {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp|link}}. The entrance is swarming with large bees, and it cannot be accessed until the {{Term|FPTRR|Queen Bee}} offers to take {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} to the top of the mound for a price. The actual doorway will not open until {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} has activated four {{Plural|FPTRR|Switch}} located throughout the swamp. To do this, he must blast the stone-covered switches with a {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle Bomb|link}}.

The inside of the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|display=Cavern}} is poorly lit. It will cost {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} 3,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} to enter through the main gate. Shortly after entering, {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}} will make an appearance, and disappear.

==Themes and Navigation==
Through the {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon|link}} are chambers filled with poison gas, large spikes, and various other traps. There are a total of three {{Plural|FPTRR|Insect Head|link}}, each of which must be destroyed to continue on. The {{Plural|FPTRR|Insect Head}} act as mini mini-bosses. Part of their bodies lie in the underground {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|display=Cavern}}, while their heads stick through various caves in the {{Term|FPTRR|Overworld|link}}. They are destroyed when {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} throws a total of three bombs at each and makes contact.

The {{Term|FPTRR|Boss|link}} of this level is the {{Term|FPTRR|Ultra Death Bug|link}}, which is very similar to the boss in {{Term|FPTRR|Hero's Shrine|link}}. After the boss has taken a lot of damage, and morphed a time or two, it will take on the form of {{Term|FPTRR|Gargantu-Bug|link}}, which is far superior to its previous forms. After it is destroyed, his body will yield 40,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got 40,000 rupees!|Pinkle|FPTRR}}</ref>
