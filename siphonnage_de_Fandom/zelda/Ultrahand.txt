}}{{Term/Store|Ultrahand||Series, TotK}} is an ability in {{TotK}}.
==Location and Uses==
The Ultrahand ability is obtained in the [[Ukouh Shrine]] at the [[Great Sky Island]].
With Ultrahand, Link can grab, move, and rotate objects. He can even attach things together to create bridges, and vehicles. It is similar to Magnesis, however it is not restricted to just magnetic objects. It also cannot maneuver objects at the same force that [[Magnesis]] can.

====Differences====
While Magnesis can only pick up metal objects, Ultrahand can pick up almost anything.
