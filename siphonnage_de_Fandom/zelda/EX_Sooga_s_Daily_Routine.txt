{{Term/Store|EX Sooga's Daily Routine||HWAoC}} is a level 64 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Sooga's Daily Routine|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the second wave of the game’s {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian of Remembrance|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Sooga's Daily Routine}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be only completed by {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} receives a reward of ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Silent Shroom|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Chillshroom|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hot-Footed Frog|link}}, 2,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}}'s objective is to reach a Targeted Position with {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga|link}}. Upon their arrival, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} appear. They must be defeated, and when they are, another Targeted Position is given not far from the first one. This time, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Thunder Wizzrobe|link}} appear when they arrive, and have to be defeated as well. The next Targeted Position is slightly farther away, in the eastern portion of the map. When {{Term|HWAoC|Sooga}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} reach the Targeted Position, a pair of {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}} appear, and then an {{Term|HWAoC|Ice Hinox|link}} after one of them takes a little bit of damage. Afterwards, an exhausted {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} suggests heading to a place without any {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}. The new Targeted Position is in the top right corner of the map, and it is also where the pair finds {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}} being attacked by {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}. They are a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Meteo Wizzrobe|link}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the two are defeated.
