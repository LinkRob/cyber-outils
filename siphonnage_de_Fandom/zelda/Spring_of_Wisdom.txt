{{Term/Store|Spring of Wisdom||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Spring of Wisdom'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom}} is located atop [[Mount Lanayru]]. The [[Dragon]], [[Naydra]], is first found here, plagued with [[Malice]]. By offering [[Naydra's Scale]] at the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|display=Spring}}, the entrance to the [[Jitan Sa'mi Shrine]] is revealed.

Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Princess Zelda]] visited the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Wisdom|display=Spring}}, accompanied by her [[Champion]], [[Link]], to pray to the Goddess [[Hylia]] in an attempt to awaken her sealing powers, to no avail.

== Trivia ==

* The layout of the Spring of Wisdom strongly resembles that of both [[Skyview Spring]] and the [[Earth Spring]] in {{SS}}, featuring identical geometry and architecture. It can be noted however that the Spring of Wisdom, like the [[Earth Spring]], is located at the top of a mountain, as seen in Skyward Sword. While the Earth Spring was located on top of Eldin Volcano in the times of Skyward Sword, it is not unheard of for a volcano to go dormant and accumulate snow at its peak. 
* The [[Goddess Crest]] can be seen chiseled into the platform in front of the Goddess Statue at both the [[Spring of Power]] and [[Spring of Wisdom]] in {{BotW}}. It is notably absent however at the [[Spring of Courage]], either too obscured to be seen underneath floating detritus or  having been worn away by the passage of time. 
*The Spring of Wisdom is the last spring that Princess Zelda must visit in ''Breath of the Wild''. Coincidentally, the Earth Spring is the last spring Zelda visits in ''Skyward Sword.''
