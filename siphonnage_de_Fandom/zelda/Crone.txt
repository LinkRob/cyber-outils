The {{Term/Store|Crone||Series}}<ref>{{Cite Web|quote=Crone's hut|author=John Szczepaniak|published=April 30, 2010|retrieved=January 29, 2021|url=http://blog.hardcoregaming101.net/2010/04/random-images-some-very-rare.html|title=Random images – some very rare|site=''Hardcore Gaming 101 - Blog''}}</ref> is a character in {{TFoE}}.

==Biography==
The Crone lives in [[Spearfish Falls]] in a cave near its peak. She claims to know a few magic tricks and asks [[Link]] to bring her some [[Grapple Berries]] for her to conjure one. Once Link brings her the Berries, she stirs them in her cauldron to make him a [[Power Glove]], allowing Link to destroy enemies and rocks that would normally require ten [[Bomb]]s.

==Trivia==
*Two of her demonstrated tricks are turning a smoke wisp into cloth and a Grapple Berry into a coin.
*According to the Spriters Resource website, her name is revealed to be Zorga, as she's only seen stirring what looks like to be a large ladle in a big pot over a fire. 

== Conversation ==
If Link speaks to her without the grapple berries: "I may be old and ugly, but I still know a few tricks. Bring some Grapple Berries; I'll show you a good one." Otherwise: "Stir the berries in tub, let the juices soak the glove. Let Link fight and never cower, for his glove's a Glove of Power."
