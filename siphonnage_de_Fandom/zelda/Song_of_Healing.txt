The "{{Term/Store|Song of Healing||Series, ST}}" is a recurring [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96 {{Exp Game|MM}} & 97 {{Exp Game|TP, ST}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the "Song of Healing" is taught to [[Link]] by the [[Happy Mask Salesman]] after Link, cursed in the shape of a [[Deku Scrub]], retrieves the stolen {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} from [[Skull Kid]]. The notes of the song heal Link's soul, and the young hero returns to his [[Hylian]] form. The [[Deku Mask]] then materializes and is kept as a memento of his time as a Deku.<ref>{{Cite|You got the Deku Mask to keep as a memento!|N/A|MM}}</ref> Link utilizes the song many times thereafter, as it has the capability of healing a soul and giving peace to those who cannot forget.<ref>{{Cite|This is a melody that heals evil magic and troubled spirits, turning them into masks.|Happy Mask Salesman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What a soothing melody... My sorrows are melting away into the song.|Darmani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Baby! If I die like this... Even if I die... It won't be in peace... That's for suuure! Somebody, please rescue her eggs before the pirates take their toll. Oh, somebody, somebody, please heal my soul.|Mikau|MM}}</ref>

Frequently, using the "Song of Healing" on tormented individuals yields a [[mask]]. The [[Goron]] warrior [[Darmani III]] and the [[Zora]] guitarist [[Mikau]] each grant Link the use of their mortal bodies through [[Transformation#Majora's Mask|transformation masks]] in return for his promise to aid their people.<ref>{{Cite|I leave my undying feelings with you...|Darmani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|For the Goron Village... I have asked for your assistance...|Darmani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm depending on you to help that singer girl...|Mikau|MM}}</ref> The spirits, finally at ease, depart to the afterlife as Link watches over their graves.<ref>{{Cite|The deeds I accomplished while living are carved on my grave. You should read it.|Darmani|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Carve my farewell song on my grave... |Mikau|MM}}</ref>

The "Song of Healing" is also used to heal [[Kamaro]]—a dancer's ghost found in [[Termina Field]]—<ref>{{Cite|I am no longer part of the living...My sadness to the moon... I haven't left my dance to the world...I am filled with regret.|Kamaro|MM}}</ref> and the [[Pamela's Father|researcher]] in [[Ikana Valley]].<ref>{{Cite|This mummy looks different... Sorta like it's here waiting for its human heart to be healed.|Tatl|MM}}</ref> If the song is played to the [[Poe Collector#Majora's Mask|Poe Collector]] in the [[Spirit House]], he will lower the price of his mini-game from 30 to 10 [[Rupee]]s, while commenting that not all spirits can be healed by that song.<ref>{{Cite|You cannot heal all souls with that song. The earthly cost to you will be 30 Rupees! I'd like to be able to take that much... But I shall take 10 Rupees...|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref>

Playing the "Song of Healing" for [[Sharp]] will have him remark that a song like that no longer has an effect on someone like him.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? Somehow, my heart is eased. This melody has a serenity to it... And yet... To one of the dead and darkness, like myself, a song like that no longer holds meaning for me.|Sharp|MM}}</ref>

Similar to [[Zelda's Lullaby]] in ''Ocarina of Time'', it will also repair broken signs upon being played near them.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, [[Wolf Link]] can howl the "Song of Healing" in [[Death Mountain#Twilight Princess|Death Mountain]] to summon the [[Golden Wolf]]. Once he is found, the Hero's Spirit then proceeds to teach Link the [[Shield Attack]].

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, the "Song of Healing" is learned at the entrance to the {{Term|ST|Forest Temple|link}}. If it is not learned there, there are other opportunities at the entrances to some, but not all of the other temples. However, the song itself sounds and functions entirely differently. Playing it with the [[Spirit Flute]] while in a dungeon causes the [[Spirit of Healing]] to appear and heal Link completely. It can only be used once per dungeon.<ref>{{Cite|The Spirit of Healing can't help you more than once...|N/A|ST}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got the Song of Healing! Play it in a dungeon to restore your hearts one time.|N/A|ST}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*When Link plays the "Song of Healing" in the European version of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|Spirit Tracks]]'', it says that he played the "Song of Recovery".{{Fact|ST}}
*Coincidentally, the notes for the Song of Healing in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' are [[Saria's Song]] from {{OoT}} mechanically in reverse. This is most likely due to the limitations of the [[Nintendo 64]] button inputs for [[Link]]'s [[Ocarina]].
*The "Song of Healing" is one of the most recognizable [[Song|songs]] in the [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]' fanbase.[[File:Song of Healing MM.png|thumb|229x229px|The comical scene of the Happy Mask Salesman playing the "Song of Healing" in ''Majora's Mask'']]
*The song itself is a popular meme among some fans in the ''Zelda'' community, as when it was taught to Link by the [[Happy Mask Salesman]] in ''Majora's Mask'', he himself is shown playing it on a humorously massive piano despite needing only three notes.
