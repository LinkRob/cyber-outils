The {{Term/Store|Beret Boy||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305 {{Exp Game|TFH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Beret Boy lives in [[Hytopia]]. He likes [[Mimi]] and can often be found playing with her. He mentions that she seems more preoccupied with finding blue birds and the [[Drablands Diary]].<ref>{{Cite|Mimi's always looking for blue birds. But for now, she's preoccupied. All she can talk about is a book called the Drablands Diary. Chicks are so unpredictable, amiright?|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref> She may sometimes lend volume 6 of the diary to him, which he will then read to a cat laying on a bench.<ref>{{Cite|Mimi lent me a book! I was so happy, I read it to a cat. The cat liked it! Do you want to hear it too?|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref> After [[Link]] lifts the curse off of [[Princess Styla]], he can be found talking with Mimi. The Beret Boy is disappointed that Mimi always ignores him, and says if she wants to find something blue, she should look at him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Mimi... If you want something blue, just look at me when you're ignoring me. *sigh*|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref> Mimi agrees to this and asks him never to leave her, which makes the Beret Boy very happy.<ref>{{Cite|Mimi! I'm so glad you finally know how I feel about you!|Beret Boy|TFH}}</ref>
