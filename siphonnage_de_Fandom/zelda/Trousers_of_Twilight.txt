==Location and Uses==
The '''Trousers of Twilight''' are a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the Super Smash Bros. Link Amiibo '''or''' Twilight Princess Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Trousers of Twilight. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Trousers of Twilight, alongside the [[Cap of Twilight]] and [[Tunic of Twilight]], comprise the [[Hero of Twilight Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from [[Twilight Princess]]. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
