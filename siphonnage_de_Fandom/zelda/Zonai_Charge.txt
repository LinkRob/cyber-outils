'''Zonai Charges''' are [[Material|Materials]] received from defeating [[Construct|Constructs]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom]]''. It is used to power [[Zonai Device|Zonai devices]] and constructs, and can be consumed to replenish Link's [[Energy Cell]].{{Cite Item|{{PAGENAME}}|TotK}}

== Location and Uses ==

=== Tears of the Kingdom ===
Zonai Charges are [[Monster Part|Monster Parts]] that are dropped by [[Soldier Construct|Soldier Constructs]] upon their defeat. Can also be traded for [[Zonaite]] at a [[Forge Construct]].
