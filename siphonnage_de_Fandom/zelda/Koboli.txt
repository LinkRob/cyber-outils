{{Term/Store|Koboli||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=|book=E|publisher=|page=259}}</ref> He is the [[Letter Sorting|mail sorter]] found on the upper floor of the [[Mail Center]] at [[Dragon Roost Island]].

Koboli is the third generation in a family of postmen. As Koboli's [[figurine]] suggests, he is based on the [[Postman]] from {{OoT}} and {{MM}},<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Koboli is a bit of fan service amongst the Rito tribe. Since the Rito tribe works distributing the post, I thought it would be funny to slip in the Postman from Ocarina of Time and Majora’s Mask, and stuck him in close to the end of the development.|author= Haruhana|published= 2002|retrieved= March 20, 2013|url= http://www.glitterberri.com/the-wind-waker/zelda-box/character-commentary/the-minor-characters/|title= The Developers Discuss Koboli & The Salvage Corp.|site= Zelda Box|type=}}</ref> which explains the facial resemblance that both of them have.

==Role==
When Link first meets Koboli, he is looking for an intern to help him with mail sorting, and offers Link one Rupee for each [[letter]] that he sorts. When Link sorts 25 letters within the 30-second time limit, Koboli gives him Link two Rupees per each letter.

About a day later, a man named [[Baito]] is hired by Koboli to help him sort mail.
