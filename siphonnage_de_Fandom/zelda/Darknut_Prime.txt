{{Term/Store|Darknut Prime||Series}} is an enemy character from {{TLoZ|Valiant Comics}} published by Valiant Comics.{{Fact}} He is the apparent leader of the [[Darknut]]s and does the bidding of [[Ganon]]. Throughout the comics, [[Link]] and [[Princess Zelda]] encounter Darknut Prime on various occassions. He wears a blue outfit which sets him apart from the other Darknuts who wear red. 

==Biography==
Darknut Prime first appears in ''[[The Power]]'', where Link and Zelda encounter him and three other [[Darknut]]s while they are in the [[Underworld]]. The young hero manages to bury Darknut Prime and the other foes under a pile of rubble thanks to a [[bomb|bomb's]] explosion,<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I just need a little DISTANCE to throw this bomb! Cave in!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=2 [The Power]}}</ref> but the mighty knights recover and start chasing Link again. Eventually, Link is able to easily defeat them with the use of the [[Triforce of Power]]. Later on, in ''[[The Price]]'', Zelda is taken into captivity by Darknut Prime when she goes to look for Link in one of Hyrule's palaces.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Perhaps there are scavengers as well--seeking easy prey!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=3 [The Price]}}</ref> When Darknut Prime brings her into the throne room where Link and Ganon are, Ganon threatens to kill her unless Link surrenders the Triforce of Power.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Surrender the Triforce or my Darknut Prime will destroy your precious princess!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=4}}</ref> Ganon orders the blue Darknut to kill her<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=You're BLUFFING! Darknut! Destroy her!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref>, but Darknut Prime refuses and states that he has only one master: Link.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There is only ONE true power here! What do YOU wish me to do...|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=5}}</ref>

Near the end of the comic series, Darknut Prime allows Zelda to enter Ganon's lair to defeat the Prince of Darkness, revealing his hidden hatred for his master.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=I've no quarrel with you, Darknut Prime! I'm here to destroy Ganon!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref> However, he lets her know that if she fails in slaying Ganon, then he will take his rage out upon her. <ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Then by all means enter, bold one, where none of us would dare-- though we hate our pig-nosed master even more than you do! But know this...if you fail to destroy Ganon...we will vent our disappointment by destroying you!|book= TLoZ (Valiant Comics)|publisher= |page=6 [The Day of the Triforce]}}</ref>
