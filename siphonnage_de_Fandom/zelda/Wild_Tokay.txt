The {{Term/Store|Wild Tokay||Series, OoA}} is a {{Term|OoA|Mini-Game|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Wild Tokay|Map Screen|OoA}}</ref>

==Location==
The Wild Tokay is located inside a cave on the eastern end of [[Crescent Island]] in the past.<ref>{{Cite|The Wild Tokay was a legendary ceremony here on Crescent Island.|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> The entrance to the cave is originally blocked by boulders, which can be blown away with [[Bomb]]s. The proprietor of the mini-game is a orange [[Tokay]], the only one of his kind. In the present, the mini-game is replaced by the Wild Tokay Museum.

==Rules==
In the Wild Tokay challenge, [[Link]] stands in a ring while Tokay pass by on either side. Link must throw meat falling down from above to each of the Tokay. If Link misses one Tokay, the game ends. If Link throws meat to every Tokay, including the final red Tokay, he wins the game.<ref>{{Cite|Pass meat to each Tokay that runs by on the left and right. If you give meat to each one, you win! If you skip even one, you fail.|Wild Tokay Proprietor|OoA}}</ref>

The main prize for winning the Wild Tokay is the [[Scent Seedling]], which can be planted in the "tree nursery" on Crescent Island.<ref>{{Cite|This is our precious tree nursery. They say a Scent Tree was planted here long ago. If I had a seedling, I'd plant it here. Hey! Is that a Scent Seedling? Let's plant it!|Tokay|OoA}}</ref> In the present, the seedling will grow into a [[Mystical Tree|Scent Tree]], allowing Link to gather his first [[Scent Seed]]s. With the seeds, Link is able to buy back some items that were stolen from him when he arrived at the island. As such, completing the Wild Tokay is necessary in order to progress further in the game.

Afterwards, Link can play the Wild Tokay for [[Rupee]]s, [[Gasha Seed]]s, and unappraised [[Magic Ring]]s. The game gets increasingly difficult every time Link successfully completes the challenge.

==Wild Tokay Museum==
The '''Wild Tokay Museum'''<ref>{{Cite|Wild Tokay Museum|Map|OoA}}</ref> is a museum in the present age that honors the game the Tokay once played long ago.<ref>{{Cite|This is the Wild Tokay Museum. We Tokay used to play here long ago, but not anymore.|Tokay (Museum Curator)|OoA}}</ref> It is found in the exact same location as the mini-game in the past, and it is also run by an orange-colored Tokay. It is implied by the museum curator that the exhibits found inside the museum are actually real Tokay, instead of statues.<ref>{{Cite|The Tokay on your left and right are real... I mean real-looking Tokay. They won't respond if you talk to them.|Tokay (Museum Curator)|OoA}}</ref>

In a [[Linked Game]], Link can tell a password he learned from a friendly [[Moblin]] (found in the ruined [[Moblin's Keep]] after Link has defeated the [[Great Moblin]] and talked to him in [[Sunken City]]) to the curator of the Wild Tokay Museum.<ref>{{Cite|Bwee! Link! I'm no enemy! I heard a rare secret. Do you want to hear it? This secret is
related to the Tokay people in Labrynna! Understand?|Friendly Moblin|OoA}}</ref> Doing so will inspire the curator to bring back the Wild Tokay mini-game, allowing Link to play it in the present.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! That's the legendary Wild Tokay secret! Now I want to bring back the Wild Tokay!|Tokay (Museum Curator)|OoA}}</ref> After successfully completing the mini-game, the curator will reward Link by allowing him to carry more [[Bomb]]s.
