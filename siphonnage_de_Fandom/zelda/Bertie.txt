==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>This is {{Color|SS Blue|Bertie}}, tireless househusband and businessman. He is in charge of infusing potions at the Potion Shop that he runs with his wife.</p><p>He is also responsible for taking care of their infant child.</p>
}}


Bertie is a timid, overworked father who works alongside his wife, [[Luv]], by infusing the [[Potion]]s bought at her [[Potion Shop#Skyward Sword|Potion Shop]] in his [[Infusion Shop]] in exchange for [[Bug]]s and [[Rupees]].<ref>{{Cite|I, uh...infuse the potions my wife sells with new ingredients to increase their power. It's nothing, really...just something I like to do. Well...ah, do you have any potions you would like me to infuse?|Bertie|SS}}</ref> He also cares for their young child, who he carries on his back.

Later in the game, Bertie is found to be tired and restless at his job. When spoken to at his house during the night, he will reveal that his {{Term|SSHD|Bertie's Baby|link|display=Baby}}'s [[Rattle]] had been taken by a bird some time ago, causing her to be unable to sleep and thus preventing him from sleeping as well.<ref>{{Cite|The baby just goes on and on like this all night. I haven't slept a wink in... Ahhh... I don't even know how long. The baby usually sleeps like a sack of rocks. But that was before we lost the you know what... The baby's {{Color|SS Gold|Rattle}}.|Bertie|SS}}</ref> Link locates the Rattle in [[Skyloft]] lying in a nest atop a house. Returning the Rattle to Bertie will reward Link with 5 [[Gratitude Crystal]]s, ending Bertie's sleepless nights.<ref>{{Cite|Whew. The baby seems to have settled down nicely. I bet the baby will sleep well tonight. Oh! That means I'll finally get a good night's sleep too! Thank you so much! I won't forget what you did for me.|Bertie|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Bertie's name, and that of his wife [[Luv]], is derived from {{Wp|lovebird}}, a small species of parrot.
