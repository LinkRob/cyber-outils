=== Open a Door without a Key===
{{Youtube|XXZw_R9d9E0|250|right|Saving a Key in Eagle with the glitch.}}
*'''Effect:''' Progress through the Eagle without using all the keys.
*'''How To:''' When first entering {{Term|TLoZ|Level 1|link}}, the door on the north side of the room will be locked and requires a [[Key#Small Keys|Key]] to open it. If Link exits the dungeon and then re-enters, the door will have mysteriously unlocked, allowing Link to progress through the level without using one of the Keys, which allows him to skip getting a Key in another dungeon.
