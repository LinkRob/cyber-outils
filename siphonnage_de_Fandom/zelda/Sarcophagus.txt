{{Term/Store|Sarcophagus|Sarcophagi|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite Internal|quote= Sarcophagus |game= CoH |system= Nintendo Switch |version= 1.0.0 |country= United States of America}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Sarcophagus}} are stationary objects that can summon undead enemies like the {{Term|CoH|Stalfos|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Stalfos Rider|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Gibdo|link}} in order to attack {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. Despite their capabilities, they can only summon one enemy at a time, only spawning a new one when either the previous enemy or the {{Term|CoH|Stalfos Rider|link}} it summoned loses its mount. They only appear in caves and dungeons. 

==Trivia==
They share their name and summoning ability with the Sarcophagus enemies from ''Crypt of the NecroDancer''.
