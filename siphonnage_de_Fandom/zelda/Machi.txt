==Biography==

Machi is a helpful member of the [[Kikwi]] tribe, encountered in [[Faron Woods#Skyward Sword|Faron Woods]]. Link first finds Machi when he saves him from being attacked by a group of [[Bokoblin]]s, but Machi mistakes Link for a green Bokoblin and runs away. Link then finds Machi hiding behind mushrooms and slices them away until the Kikwi hides behind a tree. When Link speaks to him, he tells the young hero that he saw a [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|blonde girl]] being chased by Bokoblins and that she was running with the Kikwi elder, [[Bucha]].<ref>{{Cite|That's weird, kee-koo. A little while ago I ran into another funny animal like you, but that one was a girl. She seemed to be in big trouble when I saw her. She a friend of yours, kwee?|Machi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I don't know who this girl was, koo-kwee, but she was in a bunch of trouble when I saw her. A pack of those mean red guys were after her, kwee, but she escaped with the Kikwi {{Color|SS Blue|elder}}.|Machi|SS}}</ref> Machi then asks Link to tell Bucha that he is safe.<ref>{{Cite|Kee-paleep... It sounds like you're set on finding this friend and the elder. If you're heading that way anyway, kwee, would you mind {{Color|SS Red|telling our elder that I'm safe}}?|Machi|SS}}</ref>

When Link returns to the [[Deep Woods]] after completing the [[Skyview Temple]], Machi asks if he had found his friend and thanks him for reuniting him with the other Kikwis.<ref>{{Cite|Heya! Remember me? You ever find that girl you were searching after? [...] Oh... I sure hope you two find each other soon. Let me tell you, there's nothing like reuniting with friends. I'm sure glad all those mean guys are gone. We can finally live in peace again!|Machi|SS}}</ref> If Link says he does not remember him, he tells Link that he can distinguish him from other Kikwis by looking at the icons on his map.<ref>{{Cite|Want to know the best way to tell us Kikwis apart? If you point to us on your map, you'll see our names appear.|Machi|SS}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Machi's name is derived from {{Wp|matcha}}, {{Wp|green tea}} that has been pounded into a powder.
