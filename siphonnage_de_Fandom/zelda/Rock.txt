==Location and Uses==
[[File:Lifting Small Rock OoT3D.jpg|thumb|left|190px|Link lifting a small Rock from {{OoT3D|-}}]]
Rocks usually yield items when destroyed, such as [[Heart]]s, [[Rupee]]s, [[Arrow]]s, and [[Bomb]]s. They also serve as obstacles that must be lifted and thrown, or destroyed with Bombs or a [[Hammer]]. When thrown, they can also damage some enemies.

To move or lift some Rocks, [[Link]] must wear certain items. In {{ALttP}}, Link must wear the [[Power Glove]]s to lift both small and large light gray Rocks, while the Titan's Mitt is required to lift dark gray ones. He can also use the [[Pegasus Boots#A Link to the Past|Pegasus Shoes]] to destroy piles of Rocks. In {{OoT}}, large gray Rocks are bomb-proof and can only be lifted by Adult Link with the Silver and Golden Gauntlets, whereas brown Rocks can be either bombed or destroyed with the [[Megaton Hammer]], and red ones can only be destroyed with the Hammer. In {{MM}}, brown Rocks can be bombed, destroyed with [[Goron Link]]'s [[Goron_Link#Goron_Punch|punching]] technique, or blown up with a [[Powder Keg]], whereas red ones can only be destroyed with the latter two methods. Large gray Rocks, which can be found underwater, cannot be interacted with in this episode, although Goron Link was originally supposed to be able to lift those when found on land. In {{TP}}, Link can also use [[Bomb Arrow]]s to destroy Rocks from afar.

Rocks also appear as visual hints, mostly pointing out hidden areas or covering secrets. In {{ALttP|-}}, [[Magical Warp Tile]]s can sometimes be found under Rocks. In {{OoT|-}}, Rock circles denote the location of [[Cave|secret grottoes]] that can be opened by using a Bomb, the Megaton Hammer, or by playing the "[[Song of Storms]]." Some large Rocks also hide [[Treasure Chest]]s underneath them. In {{ALBW}}, some Rocks hide lost [[Maiamai]]s underneath them.

In the [[Dark World]] and [[Lorule]], Rocks are replaced with [[Skull]]s, which may occasionally turn out to be [[Stal]]s.

==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Rock|An ordinary rock that might be found anywhere. Good for throwing.<ref name="EoW Rock">{{Cite|An ordinary rock that might be found anywhere. Good for throwing.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Rocks are common objects found throughout [[Hyrule]] that can be created after [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] learns their [[Echo]]. Once learned, she can summon them anywhere using the [[Tri Rod]]. Rocks can be thrown to damage enemies and sometimes drop items such as Rupees and Hearts.

==Variations==
===Pillars===
[[File:HW Link Power Gloves - Stone Pillar.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Link holding a pillar from {{HW|-}}]]
In {{OoT|-}}, huge pillars can be found blocking the entrance to the [[Great Fairy Fountain]] outside [[Ganon's Castle]] and within the castle itself. They can only be lifted by wearing the Golden Gauntlets. These pillars are automatically thrown by Link during a cutscene after being lifted. The pillars, although they were never used, were present on the Japanese 1.0 version of {{MM|-}}.<ref>[https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Majora%27s_Mask/Unused_Actors,_Objects,_%26_Variables#object_heavy_object TCRF - The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask]</ref>

{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Stub|HW}}
In {{HW}}, pillars appear as part of Link's Power Gloves moveset.
{{Noncanon|End}}

===Stone Watchers===
{{Main|Stone Watcher}}
In {{TWW}}, Stone Watchers appear in various locations hiding [[Cave|secret cave]]s beneath them, or covering secret passages and entrances. They can only be lifted by wearing the [[Power Bracelet]]s found inside the [[Fire Mountain]], where the first Stone Watcher is found blocking the exit.

===Rupee Rocks===
{{Main|Rupee Rock}}
[[File:ALBW Rupee Rock Model.png|left|100px]]
In the [[Game Boy Advance]] version of {{ALttP}} and in {{ALBW}}, some Rocks have glimmers of green light around them. If Link lifts the Rock, it will turn into a normal Rock. Though if struck by his [[Sword]], Rupees will pop out for a limited time every time the Rock is hit. If Link hits the Rock fast enough, higher value Rupees will also start appearing.
