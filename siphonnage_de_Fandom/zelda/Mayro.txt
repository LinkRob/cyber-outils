{{Term/Store|Mayro||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mayro|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mayro}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who resides at the {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. He can be found inside, where he admires a painting displayed on its wall. During the night, he sleeps in one of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}}.

When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Mayro}} claims that he wishes he had the skill to make such a painting.<ref>{{Cite|I wish I had this kind of artistic skill...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> Turning to face {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, he asks if he has also come to admire the painting, and he reveals that he could stare at it for hours.<ref>{{Cite|Have you come to look at this picture too? It's so beautiful... I could spend hours just looking at it.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> He has heard the the owner of {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Ozunda|link}}, was the one who created it.<ref>{{Cite|This painting was made by Ozunda, the owner of this stable, or so I hear.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> In particular, he appreciates {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}} in the background and how the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} blends into the background.<ref>{{Cite|That volcano in the background... It's so majestic. And the stable is subtle in the scenery.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> By looking at the painting, {{Term|BotW|Mayro}} is able to deduce that it depicts the {{Term|BotW|Stable}} from the south.<ref>{{Cite|I think the {{Color|BotW Red|stable was painted from a southern direction}}... The composition is just so impressive...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> Following this conversation, the "{{Term|BotW|A Landscape of a Stable|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Mayro}} after finding the {{Term|BotW|Tah Muhl Shrine|link}}, he realizes that there is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} on the painting's left-hand side.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... There's a shrine on the left-hand side of the picture...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> Despite how often he looks at the painting, he has never noticed it before.<ref>{{Cite|I never noticed that shrine before. I've looked at this painting so much, and I never noticed it...|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> He believes this is because it is an unusual place for a {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, and he wonders who would go out of their way to visit it.<ref>{{Cite|Must be because that's an odd place for a shrine to be located. Who would go to a shrine there, anyway?|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> He is then surprised to hear that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has already visited it, and he rationalizes that there are some people who simply want to travel the world.<ref>{{Cite|Wait, what? You went there? I guess for some people, there's nothing to stop them from traveling the world.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to again, he hopes he will one day be able to paint as well as {{Term|BotW|Ozunda}} does.<ref>{{Cite|I hope that someday I can capture the same majesty that Ozunda does in my own work.|Mayro|BotW}}</ref>
