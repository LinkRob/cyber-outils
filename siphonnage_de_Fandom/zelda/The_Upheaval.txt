== Overview ==
The Upheaval is an influential event in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]. It is caused when [[Link]] & [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] are exploring the catacombs of [[Hyrule Castle]] to find the origin of [[Gloom]]. They come across Ganondorf who attacks them. After shattering the [[Master Sword]], [[Ganondorf]] launches Gloom at an inverted pyramid. Almost simultaneously, Hyrule Castle appears to levitate into the air, however it is held up by a rocky pillar decorated with [[Zonai]] carvings. During this period, four places in Hyrule are affected by events known as “[[Regional Phenomena]]”. Many new monster species are also resurrected due to the surge in [[Ganondorf]]’s power. Earthquakes affect places all across [[Hyrule]], causing [[cave]] entrances to open and Chasms to form, leading to the [[The Depths|Depths]]. 

== [[Regional Phenomena]] ==
Across [[Hyrule]], places are affected by the Upheaval but none more than the following.

=== [[Gerudo Desert|The Gerudo Desert]] ===
After the Upheaval, the [[Gerudo Desert]] is plagued by [[Gibdo]], a kind of skeleton, resurrected in the Upheaval. With the Gibdo comes the Sand Shroud, a gigantic sandstorm covering almost the whole desert. After Link raises the [[Lightning Temple]] from the sand and kills [[Queen Gibdo]], the Sand Shroud dissipates and the Gibdo all but disappear.

=== [[Eldin Volcano]] ===
During the Upheaval, Chasms form across [[Hyrule]]. One such Chasm forms in [[Death Mountain Crater|Death Mountain’s crater]], causing it to fall dormant. The Chasm is inaccessible until Link defeats [[Moragia]], giving him access to the [[Fire Temple]]. Along with the newly formed caves came [[Marbled Rock Roast|marbled rock roast]], a delicacy to the [[Goron|Gorons]]. It is incredibly addictive and appears to have affects on personality. After clearing the [[Fire Temple]], the [[Marbled Rock Roast|marbled rock roast]] vanishes from [[Eldin]].

=== [[Zora's Domain|Zora’s Domain]] ===
[[Zora's Domain|Zora’s Domain]] is being affected by [[sludge]] falling from a sky island. [[Sludge]] is damaging to [[Zora]], as well with it mixing with water creates a very hazardous environment for their species. Link creates a [[whirlpool]] to the [[Ancient Zora Waterworks]], giving him a way to access the sky islands above [[Zora's Domain|Zora’s Domain]]. After Link defeats [[Mucktorok|Mucktorock]], the flow of the [[sludge]] is stopped and the water is clean once more.

=== [[Hebra]] ===
[[Hebra]] is being affected by a freezing cold blizzard causing gale force winds, making flight for the [[Rito]] incredibly dangerous. Link traverses flying boats to reach the [[Wind Temple]] where he finds the source of the blizzard, a monster called [[Colgera]]. After Link dispatches it, the Blizzard disappears and temperatures rise slightly, making travel easier.

== New Monster Appearances ==
Lots of new monsters form in the Upheaval due to [[Ganondorf]]’s influence. These include:

* [[Gleeok|Gleeoks]]
* [[Gibdo]]
* [[Boss Bokoblin|Boss Bokoblins]]
* [[Like Like|Like-Likes]] 
* [[Aerocuda]]
* [[Horriblin|Horroblin]]
* [[Gloom Spawn|Gloom spawn]]

Many monsters also gained new horns from [[Ganondorf]]’s power, such as [[Lizalfos]], [[Bokoblin|Bokoblins]], [[Lynel|Lynels]], etc.

== Trivia ==

* Some Chasms were formed thousands of years before Tears of the Kingdom and were sealed off over time.
* Some Chasms already existed but were inaccessible such as the Chasm in the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]], which led Master Kohga to finding [[the Depths]]
