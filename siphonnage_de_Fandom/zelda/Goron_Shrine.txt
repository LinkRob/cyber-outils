The {{Term/Store|Goron Shrine||Series, MM}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goron Shrine<br/>Please see the gatekeeper above.|Sign|MM}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
[[File:MM3D Goron Shrine Notebook Portrait.png|left]]
The entrance to the Goron Shrine is shut during winter, therefore, [[Link]] must talk to the gatekeeper outside in order for the door to open.<ref>{{Cite|You're Darmani! How? You're alive?! Do you want to enter the Goron Shrine? Then I'll open the door with a Goron Pound. I'm going to close it right away so it doesn't get cold inside, so hurry up and get in. Are you ready?|Goron|MM}}</ref> It is the home of many [[Goron]]s during the winter season. If [[Goron Link]] speaks to the Gorons within the Shrine, they will express their disbelief that he is [[Darmani III]].<ref>{{Cite|...Darmani? You're alive?! The Goron Elder said you had died... Was that a lie?|Goron|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Those sideburns... Aren't you Darmani? You're supposed to be dead...but you're alive? What have you been doing? Thinking you were dead, the Elder went to Snowhead on his own... But for now, please go see the Elder's son. Hurry!|Goron|MM}}</ref> 

The sharp cries of the [[Goron Elder's Son]] cause the rest of the Gorons to become uneasy, trying to cover their ears.<ref>{{Cite|Whatever the case may be, welcome back... So... does that incessant crying sound familiar to you? The Goron Elder's son won't stop crying.|Goron|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Elder went to Snowhead and hasn't come back, so now his son is so lonely he won't stop crying. If you don't find the Goron Elder and ask him to come back... I'll never be able to sleep. And it just keeps getting colder outside... Even in here it is so cold that we could freeze.|Goron|MM}}</ref> Once Goron Link plays the "[[Goron Lullaby]]" to the child, he will fall asleep, lighting the [[Torch]]es in his room and relieving the rest of the Gorons of his cries.

Torches can be found all around the Goron Shrine and, once they are all lit up, the chandelier above will light up and start spinning. By breaking one of the Goron heads on the chandelier with Goron Link's roll, he can find a [[Rock Sirloin]] for the hungry Goron in [[Mountain Village]]. The [[Goron Shop]] can also be found inside, selling items which value varies depending on the season.

==Trivia==
*In {{MM3D}}, a [[Wii]] Remote can be found next to some toys in the Goron Elder's Son room.
