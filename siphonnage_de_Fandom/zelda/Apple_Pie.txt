{{Term/Store|Apple Pie|Apple Pies|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Apple Pie}}<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Apple Pie}} can be made in {{Plural|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} by using {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, and an {{Term|BotW|Apple|link}}. Though they sell for 30 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, they'll sell for 50 Rupees if a second Apple or Tabantha Wheat is added. Eating one will restore three {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They'll also restore four Hearts if an additional Apple is added, as well as five Hearts if another Tabantha Wheat is added. Their flaky pie crusts and sweet apples are thought by many people throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}} to be a match made in heaven.<ref name="Inventory"/>

[[Misa's Recipe Book]] at the [[Slippery Falcon]] in [[Rito Village]] contains a recipe for Apple Pie. [[Misa]] first suggests buying Tabantha Wheat, Goat Butter, and Cane Sugar from the Slippery Falcon.<ref>{{Cite|First, you'll want to buy {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|cane sugar}} here at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Slippery Falcon}}.|Misa's Recipe Book|BotW}}</ref> She then tells the reader to cook them with an Apple until the crust is a flaky golden brown and the insides are gooey.<ref>{{Cite|Cooke those ingredients with an {{Color|BotW Blue|apple}} until the crust is flaky golden brown and the insides are gooey and delicious.|Misa's Recipe Book|BotW}}</ref> Finally, she tells the reader to substitute the Apple with another ingredient for an added kick.<ref>{{Cite|And for an added kick, try substituting the apple with something else!|Misa's Recipe Book|BotW}}</ref>

A fifth ingredient can be added to Apple Pie, which will result in it having different effects:

* '''Hearty Apple Pie''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Apple Pie}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's maximum Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> They also heal all of Link's Hearts and sell for 60 Rupees.

* '''Energizing Apple Pie''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Apple Pie}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They can heal seven of Link's Hearts and also be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Enduring Apple Pie''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Apple Pie}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/> They also heal Link for eight Hearts, and they're worth 50 Rupees if sold.

* '''Hasty Apple Pie''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Apple Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They'll grant Link a speed boost when they're eaten.<ref name="Hasty"/> In addition, they heal four of Link's Hearts and can be sold for 70 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Apple Pie''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Apple Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will increase Link's [[Cold Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Spicy"/> They heal for three Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Electro Apple Pie''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Apple Pie}}<br/>Grants mid-level electricity resistance.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will grant Link [[Shock Resistance]] for a short period of time.<ref name="Electro"/> They're also able to restore four Hearts in Link's Life Gauge and can be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Apple Pie''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Apple Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> When eaten, they'll grant Link a small boost to his attack-power.<ref name="Mighty"/> They're also able to heal five of Link's Hearts and are worth 50 Rupees when sold.

* '''Tough Apple Pie''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Apple Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> If they're eaten, they'll temporarily boost Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/> They also heal four Hearts and sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Apple Pie''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Apple Pie}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Eating one will increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They can also restore four of Link's Hearts and be sold for 50 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Apple Pie''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Apple Pie}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The crispy, flaky pie crust and sweet apples are a match made in heaven.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They can be eaten to temporarily increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Chilly"/> They're also worth four Hearts of healing, and they can be sold in exchange for 50 Rupees.
