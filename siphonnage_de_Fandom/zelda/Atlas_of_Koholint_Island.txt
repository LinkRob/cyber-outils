The {{Term/Store|''Atlas of Koholint Island''||Series, LA, LADX}} is an object in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|'Atlas Of Koholint Island' You can move the cursor and look up the name of a place...|Atlas of Koholint Island|LADX}}</ref>{{Note|This object is also referred to as the '''Island Map''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 12}}</ref> However, as the in-game term takes precedence, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
The Atlas of Koholint Island is located in the [[Village Library]]. It allows [[Link]] to see the map of [[Koholint Island]] in its entirety rather than seeing only places he has already discovered.
