{{Term/Store|Ore Chunk|Ore Chunks|Series, OoS|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{OoS}}.<ref name="currency">{{Cite|Having Rupees doesn't do you any good. You need Ore Chunks!|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> They are the {{Term|OoS|Currency|link}} of {{Term|OoS|Subrosia|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|OoS|Ore Chunk}} are obtained at random by digging up ground anywhere in {{Term|OoS|Subrosia|link}}. Some areas are richer in {{Plural|OoS|Ore Chunk}} than others.{{Which}}{{Verify|OoS}}<ref>{{Cite|If you dig up the ground near here, you can get Ore Chunks. Some spots produce lots of Ore Chunks!|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> {{Plural|OoS|Subrosian|link}} can sense nearby {{Plural|OoS|Ore Chunk}}.<ref>{{Cite|I smell Ore Chunks nearby...|Subrosian|OoS}}</ref> The {{Term|OoS|Green Joy Ring|link}} and {{Term|OoS|Gold Joy Ring|link}} double the value of found {{Plural|OoS|Ore Chunk}}.

As with {{Plural|Series|Rupee|link}}, there are different colors of {{Term|OoS|Ore Chunk}} which have different values.
