{{Term/Store|Stalfos Knight Head|Stalfos Knight Heads|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Stalfos Knight Head|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Stalfos Knight Head}} are a component of the {{Term|CoH|Stalfos Knight|link}} boss. They only pop off when the Stalfos Knight is badly damaged, flying around in every possible direction including diagonal. After being struck enough times, they will return to their body. Destroying the bones of a Stalfos Knight will also destroy the head as well.
