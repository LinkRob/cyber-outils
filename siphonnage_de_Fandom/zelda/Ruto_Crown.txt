==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
{{Plural|PH|Ruto Crown}} are an item of {{Term|PH|Treasure|link}}. They can be bought in certain shops, found in {{Plural|PH|Treasure Chest|link}}, or given to {{Term|PH|Link|link}} as a gift from {{Term|PH|Nyave|link}}. {{Plural|PH|Ruto Crown}} can be sold to {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} or to the {{Term|PH|Ho Ho Tribe|link}}, who will buy it for a higher sum on certain days.

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Plural|ST|Ruto Crown}} are an item of {{Term|ST|Treasure}}. They can be found inside {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest}}, bought from shops, or won with a {{Term|ST|Prize Postcard|link}}. {{Plural|ST|Ruto Crown}} can be sold for 150 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} each, or trade them with {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} in exchange for {{Term|ST|Train Car|link|display=Train Parts}}.

==Nomenclature==
The {{Term|ST|Ruto Crown}} gets its name from {{Term|OoT|Princess Ruto|link}}  the {{Term|OoT|Zora|link}} princess from {{OoT}}.{{Nomenclature}}
