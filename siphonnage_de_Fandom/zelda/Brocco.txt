{{Term/Store|Brocco||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Brocco}}
{{Term|TMC|Brocco}} is a vegetable vendor in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. He frequently quarrels with {{Term|TMC|Pina|link}}, the fruit vendor, over the superiority and health benefits of vegetables.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule's vegetable vendor. He sells fine produce. He also argues constantly with the fruit vendor, Pina, over the health benefits of vegetables.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> His crop is imported from the fields of {{Term|TMC|Eenie|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Meenie|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Hyrulean vegetables grown in their fields are exquisitely delicious. Apparently, Brocco sells the best of their crops.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Brocco}} first appears during the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, where he peddles his vegetables from the northwestern stall of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town Square|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Come on up! Come one, come all! Gather round and have a look-see! I'm having a huge sale on all manner of delicious and festive vegetables!|Brocco|TMC}}</ref> As the game progresses, he grows increasingly desperate to sell his wares,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! You buy, or I don’t let you leave! What is this! I haven’t sold anything all day! Hey, hey!|Brocco|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! The other vendor’s not doing so hot, but neither am I! My arm’s getting tired, so buy, buy, buy! Hey, hey!|Brocco|TMC}}</ref> claiming that his mother will be angry if he is unable to sell them and even offering to trade for fruit.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! My mom’ll be mad if I take all this stuff home, so shop up! Anyone want to trade for fruit!? Anybody? Hello? Hey, hey!|Brocco|TMC}}</ref>

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? I'd be glad to! Thank you!|Brocco|TMC}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Brocco}}'s name is a contraction of the word "{{Wp|broccoli}}", a leafy vegetable.
