'''Boss Bokoblin''' (ボスボコブリン) is an enemy in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom]]''.

==Characteristics==
==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Compendium|number=126|name=Boss Bokoblin|locs=Hyrule Field
Hyrule Ridge|loot=Boss Bokoblin Fang
Boss Bokoblin Horn|desc=These heavyweight monsters are leaders of the Bokoblins. They swagger about, whistling commands to the teams of minions nervously trailing them. Their heavy horns are so sharp, they can slice large trees clean in half.}}
Boss Bokoblins appear as {{Plural|TotK|Enemy}} in {{TotK}}, leading [[Bokoblin]]s or either one of their stronger variants in battle against [[Link]]. They wield various weapons, such as [[Sturdy Thick Stick]]s, and can throw rocks at Link from a distance, while blowing their horns to get the Bokoblins organized and even ramming Link with the horns on their heads.
