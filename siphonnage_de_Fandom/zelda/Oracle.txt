The {{Term/Store|Oracle|Oracles|Series, OoA|plural}}<ref>{{Cite|The powers of we Oracles will guide you to Princess Zelda! Cast down Twinrova and stop the Evil King Ganon's revival!|Din (Oracle)|OoA}}</ref> are a group of three powerful women. They are: [[Din (Oracle)|Din]], the Oracle of Seasons; [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]], the Oracle of Ages; and [[Farore (Oracle)|Farore]], the Oracle of Secrets. They all appear in {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, with the former two Oracles having prominent roles in those games, as well as {{TMC}}.

==Biography==
==={{OoS|-}}===
The Oracle of Seasons is fated with keeping the world's climatic order in balance. When Din is kidnapped and forced into a crystal prison by [[General Onox]], the seasons go awry and change at the slightest notice.

==={{OoA|-}}===
The Oracle of Ages has the ability to travel through time. When [[Veran]] takes of the body of Nayru, the Oracle of Ages, she goes back in time to change the course of history.

The Oracle of Secrets plays a very minor role in the ''Oracle'' games. She resides in the [[Hall of Secrets]], only talking about secrets from the [[Linked Game]] and giving special items if given certain passwords.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, the Oracles appear as traveling vagabonds in search for permanent residence in [[Hyrule Castle Town]]. Link can fuse Kinstones with all three, but only the first two fusions provide the travelers with permanent homes in the town. If the Kinstone Fusion grants Din, Farore, or Nayru a house, Link will be presented with [[Din's Charm]], [[Farore's Charm]], or [[Nayru's Charm]], respectively.

==Trivia==
*The Oracles are named after the famed Hylian goddesses: [[Nayru (Goddess)|Nayru, Goddess of Wisdom]], [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore, Goddess of Courage]], and [[Din (Goddess)|Din, Goddess of Power]].
*In the original Japanese text, the Oracles are referred to as "Shrine Maidens". This is the same title as the Maidens of {{FSA}}. It is not known if there is a connection between the two.
*Farore was originally planned to be the focus of the cancelled third game, ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Mystical Seed of Courage|Mystical Seed of Courage]]''.
