{{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}}, along with {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher|link}}, are the two variations of {{Term|SSHD|Watcher|link}} that protect the {{Term|SS|Silent Realm}}. Unlike the {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Guardian}}, the {{Plural|SS|Earth Watcher}} are not affected by the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear|link}} and will continue to roam about. The {{Plural|SS|Earth Watcher}} patrol the {{Term|SSHD|Silent Realm}} in a set path by shining a beam of light produced by the lanterns they carry. If {{Term|SSHD|Link|link}} passes through this light, the {{Term|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} will emit a loud shriek that will awaken and alert the {{Plural|SSHD|Guardian}} to {{Term|SSHD|Link}}s' location, canceling the effects of the {{Plural|SSHD|Sacred Tear}} no matter how much time {{Term|SSHD|Link}} has remaining. {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} will give chase to {{Term|SSHD|Link}} if he gets too close, but will stop once {{Term|SSHD|Link}} gets far enough away. {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} will also give away their position in ways that give {{Term|SSHD|Link}} warning so he is not caught off guard. The {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} have dimmer, larger light radii, and it is safe for {{Term|SSHD|Link}} to be caught in this light, as long as their brighter direct light does not touch him. They also will give off a jingling sound upon noticing {{Term|SSHD|Link}}.

{{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} are very similar to {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}}, but {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} float near the ground, while {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}} fly higher up, patrolling from the sky. Unlike the {{Plural|SSHD|Sky Watcher}}, {{Plural|SSHD|Earth Watcher}} will chase {{Term|SSHD|Link}} when he gets near.
