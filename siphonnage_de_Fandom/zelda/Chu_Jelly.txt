{{Term/Store|Chu Jelly|Chu Jelly|Series, TWW, TWWHD, TP, TPHD|plural}},{{Exp Game|TWW, TWWHD, TP, TPHD}}<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=105 }}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Chuchu Jelly|Chuchu Jellies|BotW, TotK, HWAoC|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW, HWAoC}}<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Chuchu Jelly}}<br/>A gelatinous substance that came from a Chuchu. It's unusable in this state, but applying a bit of elemental stimulation will change its form.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Chuchu Jelly}}<br/>A gelatinous substance that came from a Chuchu. It's unusable in this state, but applying a bit of elemental stimulation will change its form.|Spoils|HWAoC}}</ref> are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They are items retained from defeating {{Plural|Series|Chuchu|link}}, and have restorative properties. Depending on the game, they can either be used as an ingredient to make {{Plural|Series|Potion|link}} or can be scooped up and used in their current states in an {{Term|Series|Empty Bottle|link}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} can obtain {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}} either by defeating a {{Term|TWWHD|Chuchu}} or [[Thievery#Looting|stealing]] it from the {{Term|TWWHD|Chuchu}} with the [[Grappling Hook]] in {{TWW}}. The {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}} is then kept in the [[Spoils Bag]]. It cannot be used as an item but must be given to {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam|link}} on {{Term|TWWHD|Windfall Island|link}} to brew into {{Plural|TWWHD|Potion}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam}} already has the {{Term|TWWHD|Red Potion|link}} available, while {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Potion}} and {{Plural|TWWHD|Blue Potion}} must first be created by giving him fifteen {{Plural|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}} of the respective color. Once {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam}} has made one {{Term|TWWHD|Potion}} from the first batch of {{Plural|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}} and has given {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} a free sample, that {{Term|TWWHD|Potion}} will then be available for purchase from his store. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can also continue to give {{Term|TWWHD|Doc Bandam}} five additional {{Plural|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}} of any color to receive a free {{Term|TWWHD|Potion}}. The {{Plural|TWWHD|Chu Jelly|display=Jellies}} can also be sold to {{Term|TWWHD|Beedle|link}} for one {{Term|TWWHD|Rupee|link}} each.

There are three types of {{Term|TWWHD|Chu Jelly}} available:

*'''{{Term|TWWHD|Red Chu Jelly|link}}:''' dropped by {{Plural|TWWHD|Red Chuchu|link}} and makes {{Plural|TWWHD|Red Potion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a gelatinous substance in the red jelly. It's an effective cure for exhaustion, but it can't be used in its current state.|Inventory|TWW}}</ref>
*'''{{Term|TWWHD|Green Chu Jelly|link}}:''' dropped by {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Chuchu|link}} and makes {{Plural|TWWHD|Green Potion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a gelatinous substance in the green jelly. that is filled with magic power, but you can't use it in its current form.|Inventory|TWW}}</ref>
*'''{{Term|TWWHD|Blue Chu Jelly|link}}:''' dropped by {{Plural|TWWHD|Blue Chuchu|link}} and makes {{Plural|TWWHD|Blue Potion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a gelatinous substance in the blue jelly that is a cure-all, but you can't use it in its current state.|Inventory|TWW}}</ref> Only one {{Term|TWWHD|Blue Chu Jelly}} can be obtained per {{Term|TWWHD|Blue Chuchu}}, for a total of 23 {{Plural|TWWHD|Blue Chu Jelly|display=Jellies}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
{{Term|TPHD|Chu Jelly}} can be found by defeating {{Plural|TPHD|Chu|link}} in {{TP}}. Unlike in {{TWW|-}}, once a {{Term|TPHD|Chu}} is defeated, its dissolving remains can be scooped up into an {{Term|TPHD|Empty Bottle}} and be used as drinkable {{Term|TPHD|Chu Jelly}}.

There are several different types of {{Term|TPHD|Chu Jelly}} available:

*'''{{Term|TPHD|Purple Chu Jelly|link}}:''' obtained from {{Plural|TPHD|Purple Chu|link}}. It has a random effect when drunk. Usually it drains one {{Term|TPHD|Heart|link}}, but it can occasionally restore one or all of {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}}'s {{Plural|TPHD|Heart}}. {{Term|TPHD|Purple Chu Jelly}} can also drain {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s health down to a quarter of a {{Term|TPHD|Heart}}.
*'''{{Term|TPHD|Yellow Chu Jelly|link}}:''' obtained from {{Plural|TPHD|Yellow Chu|link}}. It has the same use as [[Lantern Oil]] and can be used to refuel the [[Lantern]]. It can also be scooped with the Lantern to instantly refill it as well. 
*'''{{Term|TPHD|Red Chu Jelly|link}}:''' obtained from {{Plural|TPHD|Red Chu|link}}. It has the same effect as {{Plural|TPHD|Red Potion}} and restores eight {{Plural|TPHD|Heart}}.
*'''{{Term|TPHD|Blue Chu Jelly|link}}:''' obtained from {{Plural|TPHD|Blue Chu|link}}. It has the same effect as {{Plural|TPHD|Blue Potion}} and restores all {{Plural|TPHD|Heart}}.
*'''{{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu Jelly|link}}:''' obtained from {{Plural|TPHD|Rare Chu|link}}. It has the same effect as [[Great Fairy's Tears]], which restores all {{Plural|TPHD|Heart}} and grants {{Term|TPHD|Link}} double attack power for 16 seconds or until harmed. This {{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu Jelly|display=Jelly}} has to be obtained quickly, as if the {{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu}} merges with another kind of {{Term|TPHD|Chu}}, it will become that {{Term|TPHD|Chu}}'s color. If {{Term|TPHD|Link}} has {{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu Jelly}} already, {{Term|TPHD|Yellow Chu}} will appear instead of the rare one.
*'''{{Term|TPHD|Green Chu Jelly|link}}:''' obtained from {{Plural|TPHD|Green Chu}}. It has no effect when drunk. {{Plural|TPHD|Green Chu}} only appear when a {{Term|TPHD|Blue Chu}} and {{Term|TPHD|Yellow Chu}} merge in the [[Wii]] and HD versions of the game (they create a {{Term|TPHD|Purple Chu}} in the [[GameCube]] version), which can only occur in the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals|link}}. In the Wii version, the {{Term|TP|Green Chu Jelly}} has no description on the item subscreen and no text appears when it is obtained; in {{TPHD}}, text was added in both cases, even referencing the fact that is has no effect when drunk.<ref>{{Cite|You put green Chu Jelly in your bottle! It doesn't look all that tasty...|N/A|TPHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Jelly from a green Chu.<br/>*Set it to [Y], [X] or [R] and drink it, though it doesn't look like it will do much...|Inventory|TPHD}}</ref>
*'''Black Chu Jelly:''' An unused item intended to appear from a hypothetical Black Chu, which does not appear in the final game. It is fully functional in the code, and depletes one of {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Life Gauge|link|display=hearts}} when consumed, similarly to {{Term|TPHD|Nasty Soup|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= This item is fully functional. Drinking it causes Link to lose one heart, similar to the Nasty Soup item you can receive from Coro in Faron Woods.|author= GlitterBerri|published= March 14, 2016|retrieved= October 29, 2020|url= https://tcrf.net/The_Legend_of_Zelda:_Twilight_Princess/Unused_Items#0x9F_-_Black_Chu_Jelly|title= The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess/Unused Items|site= The Cutting Room Floor|type= }}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} can be found by defeating {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} exhibit mutagenic properties when exposed to elemental attacks (e.g. setting them on fire changes them to {{Term|BotW|Red Chuchu Jelly}}),<ref name="Inventory"/> and will burst if struck. {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} comes in four colors:

*'''Chuchu Jelly''': Appears light blue in color. Drops from normal {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu}}. Harmless if struck.
*'''{{Term|BotW|Red Chuchu Jelly|link}}''': Drops from {{Plural|BotW|Red Chuchu|link}} or exposing other {{Term|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} to fire. Explodes if struck by physical attacks.
*'''{{Term|BotW|White Chuchu Jelly|link}}''': Drops from {{Plural|BotW|Ice Chuchu|link}} or exposing other forms of {{Term|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} to ice. Releases a freezing mist if struck by physical attacks.
*'''{{Term|BotW|Yellow Chuchu Jelly|link}}''': Drops from {{Plural|BotW|Yellow Chuchu|link}} or exposing other forms of {{Term|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} to electricity. Releases an electric burst if struck by physical attacks.

As a {{Term|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} with {{Plural|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} will result in {{Term|BotW|Dubious Food|link}}. However, if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking|display=Cooks}} them with critters, he can make a variety of {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. They can also be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} or used at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} to make {{Term|BotW|Light Blue Dye|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Chuchu Jelly}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}:
