{{Term/Store|White Pigeon|White Pigeons|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|White Pigeon}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|White Pigeon}}
White Pigeons evolved from {{Plural|Series|Wood Pigeon|link}}, turning white to blend in with the snow.<ref>{{Cite|It's said that a wood pigeon survived atop a snowy mountain by changing its feathers to blend in with the snow and soon after became a white pigeon.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> They're commonly found in the [[Hebra Mountains]], though they also live in the [[Gerudo Highlands]].<ref>{{Cite|This white-feathered breed of pigeon lives in the Hebra region.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Their extra layer of meat protects them from the cold, and as a result, they yield high quality meat.<ref>{{Cite|They have an extra layer of fat to guard against the cold, so they have been known to yield high-quality meat.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When they're killed, White Pigeons drop either a [[Raw Bird Thigh]] or a [[Raw Whole Bird]].
