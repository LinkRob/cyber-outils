'''Firestorm''' is a spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
Whenever [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] uses Firestorm, flames shoot around her in the four cardinal directions. It costs 1 Rupee to use.

The Spell is obtained from the [[Beggar]] outside of the [[Mobilin's Head Inn]], who begs for Rupees so she can feed her children. The Beggar gives Zelda the Spell once she gives the Beggar five Rupees.
