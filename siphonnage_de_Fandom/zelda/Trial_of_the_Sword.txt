The {{Term/Store|Trial of the Sword||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|It is called the Trial of the Sword. It will challenge you as you have never been challenged before.|The Great Deku Tree|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''Sword Monk's Shrine''',{{Name Ref|BotW}} is a challenge and mini-dungeon in {{BotW}} added in {{TMT}} DLC Pack.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} can be accessed from the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}} by placing the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} back in its {{Term|BotW|Pedestal of the Master Sword|display=Pedestal}}. If the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} is out of energy, the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}} cannot be accessed.<ref>{{Cite|You cannot participate in the Trial when the Master Sword is out of energy.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> During the challenge, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will go through a series of floors and must defeat every {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}} on a floor to proceed to the next one. At the start of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}}, he is stripped of all of his {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armor|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to use any of the {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}} during the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}}, but he is not able to use any {{Term|BotW|Champion|link}} abilities. {{Term|BotW|Link}} begins the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}} with the same amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} and temporary boosts that he had when entering, even with {{Plural|BotW|Heart}} lost from damage or temporary {{Plural|BotW|Heart}}. The {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} is divided into three sets of floors. Once a set is completed, it can be skipped during future attempts of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}}; however, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will begin empty handed when resumed and any {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} gathered during the challenge will be lost. After completing all 54 floors of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}} (with 45 being fighting rooms and the other 9 being rest stops), the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} awakens its true power, allowing it to stay in its powered-up state for much longer than usual.{{Clarify|reason=How much is it powered up?}}<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= When you get to a certain sacred location, you can take on the new "Trial of the Sword" challenge. Face an onslaught of enemies, one wave after another. Link starts the challenge without any equipment or weapons. When all the enemies in a room are defeated, Link proceeds to the next. Clear all the trials (about 45 rooms in total), and the true power of the Master Sword awakens.|author= |published= |retrieved= May 1, 2017|url= http://zelda.com/breath-of-the-wild/news/expansion-pass-dlc-pack-1-detailed/|title= Expansion Pass DLC Pack 1 Detailed|site= Zelda.com|type=}}</ref>

===Beginning Trials===
The Beginning Trials are the first 13 floors of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}}. The first 7 floors are forest themed, with each of them containing {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Rock|link}} and grass. The sixth floor is the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}} of the {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus|link}}. The seventh floor is a resting stop with a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Fairy|link}} and three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Shield of the Mind's Eye|link}}, an {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Blade|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Serpentine Spear|link}}. Floors 8 through 12 all contain water. The twelfth floor is the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword|display=Trial}} of the {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}}. On the thirteenth and final floor of the Beginning Trials, {{Term|BotW|Link}} finds himself in an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} themed room known as the Sword Monk's Shrine, and a mysterious voice tells him that he is now strong enough to wield more of the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}'s power.<ref>{{Cite|To the one chosen by the sacred blade...I commend your efforts in this trial. You are now strong enough to wield more of the Master Sword's power than ever before. Courageous hero...chosen by the blade... Take hold of the Master Sword.|N/A|BotW}}</ref> At the end of a long staircase rests the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} in a pedestal, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} pulls it and is transported back to the {{Term|BotW|Korok Forest}}.

===Middle Trials===
The Middle Trials are the middle 17 floors of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}}. The first 4 floors are shrine-themed and feature a constant upward draft over a bottomless pit and scattered platforms. The fifth floor is a resting stop with a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, a {{Term|BotW|Fairy}} and three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, one of which contains {{Term|BotW|Hylian Trousers|link}}. Floors 6 through 10 are in a dark forest setting, not unlike those of {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}. The tenth floor is the Trial of the {{Term|BotW|Hinox}}. Floor 11 is another resting stop with a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Fairy}}, and three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, one of which contains a {{Term|BotW|Hylian Tunic|link}}. Floors 12 through 16 are again shrine-themed, but exclusively feature {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout|link}}. On the seventeenth floor, {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to the Sword Monk's Shrine, where the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} rests on a different pedestal that is now connected to the previous pedestal by an added walkway.

===Final Trials===
The Final Trials are the final 24 floors of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}}. The first 5 floors are set in a jungle during a thunderstorm and feature Stal enemies. The fifth floor is the Trial of the {{Term|BotW|Stalnox|link}}. The sixth floor is a resting floor with a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, a {{Term|BotW|Fairy}} and three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, one of which contains {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Boots|link}}. Floors 6 through 11 are volcano-themed, complete with burning temperatures. The eleventh floor is the Trial of the {{Term|BotW|Igneo Talus|link}}. The twelfth floor is another resting floor, with a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Fairy}}, and three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, one of which containing a {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Stone Smasher|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Hylian Tunic}}. Floors 13 through 17 are ice-themed, with appropriate temperatures. The sixteenth floor is the Trial of the {{Term|BotW|Frost Talus|link}}. The eighteenth floor is another resting spot with a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, three {{Plural|BotW|Fairy}} and three {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}}. Inside the {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Royal Shield|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Royal Bow|link}}. Floors 19 through 23 are set in ruins and feature various {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}. On the twenty-fourth floor, {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to the Sword Monk's Shrine. This time, the pathways lead a final pedestal with the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}, surrounded by the seven unnamed {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} who prepared the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} and now deem {{Term|BotW|Link}} worthy of the fully awakened {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}. They then disappear, having fulfilled their duty, leaving {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pull out the now fully awakened {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}}.<ref>{{Cite|You have done well...The depth of your heroism is beyond question. We are the ones who prepared this trial, following a revelation from the Goddess Hylia. To the one who has overcome the Trial of the Sword...you have proven your hand worthy of the legendary Master Sword. Now that the hero has gained the power necessary to combat the Calamity, our sacred duty has been fulfilled.|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

Unlike the other floors, three of the floors contain {{Plural|BotW|Animal|link}} that do not need to be defeated before progressing to the next floor. Specifically, there are {{Plural|BotW|Stalhorse|link}} on the fourth floor and regular {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} on the twentieth, twenty-second, and twenty-third floors.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} was originally called the Cave of Trials during the Expansion Pass announcement.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo |published= February 14, 2017 |retrieved= May 2, 2017 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vbbZslUchyA |title= The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass|site= YouTube|type= Video}}</ref>
*Each of the seven {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} at the end of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} have posed their arms in a way that mimics the poses of the seven {{Plural|OoT3D|Sage|link}} when they were sealing {{Term|OoT3D|Ganon|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Okay, so I just finished the Trial of the Sword and immediately noticed the 7 monks are representing the 7 Sages.|author= kamuilyn |published= April 16, 2018 |retrieved= April 18, 2018 |url= https://www.reddit.com/r/Breath_of_the_Wild/comments/8cl9gl |title= Okay, so I just finished the Trial of the Sword and immediately noticed the 7 monks are representing the 7 Sages. : Breath_of_the_Wild |site= Reddit |type= }}</ref>
*Any {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} killed inside of the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} will not be added to the hidden point counter that determines {{Term|BotW|Monster}} difficulty scaling throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link|display=Hyrule}},{{Verify}} and all {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} in the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}} will not be changed based on this value. All {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} have a set difficulty status inside the {{Term|BotW|Trial of the Sword}}.
