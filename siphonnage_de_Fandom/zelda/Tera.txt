{{Term/Store|Tera||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tera|N/A|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Tera is a [[Great Fairy]] who can be found beneath the [[Gerudo Great Skeleton]] in the southwest corner of the [[Gerudo Desert]]. She is the oldest of the four fairy sisters in {{BotW|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|<small>*cough...cough*</small> Ah... Well, hello there... I am the great earth fairy, Tera. I am the oldest of the four sisters.|Tera|BotW}}</ref> Like the other Great Fairies, Tera will upgrade [[Link]]'s [[Armor]] in exchange for [[Material]]s.

Unlike her sisters, Tera has several odd mannerisms; she occasionally uses [[Gerudo]] forms of greeting when Link visits her, and will inform him about various goings-on beneath the water of her fountain. When she first meets Link, she attempts to convince him that she is all-knowing, even offering to read his thoughts to prove her omniscience.<ref>{{Cite|I have been watching your activities from inside my bud all this time! And haven't you been busy, swinging that sword around, adventuring... I'm not just making wild assumptions, you know! I truly do see all... I'll prove it right now! I'll...predict what you are thinking right at this moment!|Tera|BotW}}</ref> After a long pause, she assumes that he is wondering if they can cut to the point where she upgrades his clothing, and proceeds to chastise him for his impatience.<reF>{{Cite|You're probably thinking... "Can't we just skip to the part where she enhances my clothing..." Right? Looks like I hit the mark, hm? *ahem* What an impatient person...|Tera|BotW}}</ref>
