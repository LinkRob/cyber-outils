The {{Term/Store|Whimsical Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Whimsical Ring<br/>Sword damage {{Icon|OoS Up Arrow}}<br/>Sometimes deadly|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Whimsical Ring can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be found in a [[Treasure Chest]] on the [[Zora Seas]] during the present. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it decreases [[Sword]] damage while occasionally increasing it.<ref name="Description"/> The Whimsical Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
