== Overview ==
At some point after [[the Upheaval]], [[Phantom Ganon|"Zelda"]] gave [[Yunobo]] a mask, which radically altered his personality. This mask led to his discovery of Marbled Rock Roast, an extremely delicious yet dangerous variant of the Goron's favored cuisine. The [[Gloom]]-tainted [[Rock Roast]] caused nearly all of the [[Goron]]s to develop an addiction, drastically reducing their productivity as they spent all their time gorging on it and scamming travelers for money to buy more. However, as the children and elder Gorons weren't able to digest the Marbled Rock Roast, they remained unaffected but neither were able to talk any sense into Yunobo and the other victims.

[[Link]] eventually arrives in [[Goron City]], learning about the situation from [[Bludo]], [[Slergo]], and [[Offrak]]. Yunobo interrupts their conversation to voice his belief that the Marbled Rock Roast isn't a problem, before leading Slergo and Offrak back to [[YunoboCo HQ]]. Link visits YunoboCo HQ to try to talk Yunobo out of it, nearly getting through before "Zelda" amplifies the mask’s power to force him to attack Link. Fortunately, Link is able to destroy the mask, bringing Yunobo back to his senses. The two of them attempt to catch up with "Zelda" after she flees the situation, and they eventually find her walking into [[Death Mountain]]. It is then when [[Moragia]] appears out of the mountain and attacks them. With Yunobo's help, Link defeats Moragia and the two decide to jump into Death Mountain Chasm, where they make their way to the Fire Temple. After navigating the temple and opening five padlocks, the two defeat the [[Marbled Gohma]]. Yunobo then receives his ancestor's [[Secret Stone]] and becomes the new Sage of Fire. The Marbled Rock Roast vanishes from Goron City, returning the affected Gorons to normal.
