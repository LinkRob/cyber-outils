==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Amber Earrings can be purchased in the [[Starlight Memories]] shop at [[Gerudo Town]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s plus ten pieces of [[Amber]]. The Amber in the Earrings harnesses the land's power giving them increased defense compared to most other [[Armor]].<ref name="Inventory"/> The Amber Earrings can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], though they cannot be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The earrings have no other use than to increase Link's defense.
