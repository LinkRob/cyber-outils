{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Madame Scratchcard||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Madame Scratchcard|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|FPTRR|Madame Scratchcard}} lives in {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town|link}} with her husband, the {{Term|FPTRR|Jeweller|link}}, and has a daughter named {{Term|FPTRR|Missy (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|link}}. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} finds a {{Term|FPTRR|Triangle Chip|link}} during his quest, she will let him play a {{Term|FPTRR|Mini-Game|link}}. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} scratches off three of the same icons, he will win a hefty amount of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} come across an elusive {{Term|FPTRR|Super Chip|link}}, she will let him play a special version of the game which yields a far greater amount of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}}.
