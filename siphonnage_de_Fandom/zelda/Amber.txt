==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Amber}} are {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} that can be found in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Gem|link}} can also sometimes be found inside snowballs when they shatter, as well as within {{Plural|BotW|Ore Deposit|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stone Pebblit|link}} also occasionally drop these. They are sold by {{Term|BotW|Kairo|link}}, who travels between {{Term|BotW|Foothill Stable|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Maw of Death Mountain|link}}. Ten pieces of {{Term|BotW|Amber}} can be used at {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories|link}} to create the {{Term|BotW|Amber Earrings|link}}, which uses the {{Term|BotW|Amber}} to harness the power of the land and increase defense.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Amber Earrings}}<br/>One of the items sold by the Gerudo jeweler. These earrings are made with amber, a gem that harnesses the power of the land to increase defense when equipped.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} also accepts {{Plural|BotW|Amber}} as an ingredient to make {{Term|BotW|Orange Dye|link}}.

When speaking to {{Term|BotW|Ramella|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, she will explain that if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} brings her 10 Ambers, she will pay more for them than a store will.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, 10 pieces of amber... Sell them to the shop, and you'll get 300 rupees. Or sell them to me for {{Color|BotW Red|500 rupees}}!|Ramella|BotW}}</ref> This will begin the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Jewel Trade|link}}". Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} may sell batches of 10 {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} to {{Term|BotW|Ramella}} in exchange for an increased payment.<ref>{{Cite|I need to procure other {{Color|BotW Blue|jewels}} too, so keep bringing me what you find. Bring me {{Color|BotW Red|10}} at a time, got it? As for what kinds of jewels I want to buy, well... That's likely to change {{Color|BotW Red|every once in a while.}}|Ramella|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Amber}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
The Amber material is set to reappear in [[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|''Tears of the Kingdom'']], though it has a different design compared to {{BotW|-}} and ''Age of Calamity''.

==Other Appearances==
{{Ambig|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In ''Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity'', Ambers share their appearance with their {{BotW|-}} version. They can be bought at the store in the Gerudo Desert, or be found in gray treasure chests in main quests, as well as be obtained through first-clear rewards. They can be sold to most Merchants for 30 Rupees, but [[Dayfah]] will buy them for 75 Rupees.
{{Ambig|End}}
