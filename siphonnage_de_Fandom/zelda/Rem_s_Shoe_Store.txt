{{Term/Store|Rem's Shoe Store||Series, TMC}},<ref>{{Cite|The proprietor of {{Color|TMC Blue|Rem's Shoe Store}}.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> also known as '''Rem's Shoe Shop''',<ref>{{Cite|Whuzza?! Oh, welcome! Welcome to {{Color|TMC Green|Rem's Shoe Shop}}!|Rem|TMC}}</ref> is a location in {{TMC}}.

==Features and Overview==
Located in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store}} is owned by {{Term|TMC|Rem|link}}, a shoe-maker that is always falling asleep on his job.<ref>{{Cite|Rem / The proprietor of Rem's Shoe Store.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> Due to this, the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} are usually the ones that make shoes while {{Term|TMC|Rem}} is asleep, making him think that he works while he sleeps.<ref>{{Cite|Lately, whenever I take a nap, all my shoes are done when I wake up! You know, if I can make shoes in my sleep, I must be in the right job!|Rem|TMC}}</ref><ref name=minish>{{Cite|Ho, ho! You need Pegasus Boots? Why, you're just in time! We made a pair just now while Rem was sleeping. But only Rem can put the finishing touches on them...|Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Huh? What now?! There are more new shoes! You probably won't believe this, but... I have a strange habit of making shoes in my sleep!|Rem|TMC}}</ref>

Since {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} cannot get across {{Term|TMC|Castor Wilds|link}} without the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}, he must visit {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store}} and ask {{Term|TMC|Rem}} to make a pair for him. The {{Term|TMC|Minish}} mention that they just finished making a pair of {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots}}, but since {{Term|TMC|Rem}} is the only one who can put the finishing touches on the shoes, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must find a way to wake him up.<ref name=minish/><ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! Rem has some other urgent business he should be working on... So please, wake him up as soon as you can! Time's a-wastin'!|Minish|TMC}}</ref> As such, the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} tell him where to find the {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom|link}}, located in {{Term|TMC|Syrup the Witch's Hut|link}} deep in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ha, ha! That sleepyhead Rem is nearly impossible to wake up... If you want to wake him, you'll need to get an item from Syrup's hut. It's a little bit of a trek, but we'll mark it on your map for you.|Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ho, ho! We've done all we can with these shoes. You'll need to go to Syrup's hut so Rem can put on the finishing touches.|Minish|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ha, ha! Syrup's hut is north of the Minish Woods. Just check your map.|Minish|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom}} and uses it in front of {{Term|TMC|Rem}}, the shoe-maker thanks him for waking him by giving {{Term|TMC|Link}} the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots}} for free.<ref>{{Cite|Whaa--! What is that terrifyingly bad smell!? A wake-up mushroom?! I should have guessed! So you woke me up? Thanks, I just remembered I have something urgent to do! [...] So you must have come here because you want some of my shoes, right? Tell you what: because you were nice enough to wake me, you can have 'em! Hold on a sec, though. I just want to finish one last bit...|Rem|TMC}}</ref>

One of the three {{Term|TMC|Minish}} on {{Term|TMC|Rem}}'s desk will recognize {{Term|TMC|Link}} as the one who brought the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} to {{Term|TMC|Melari|link}} and states that he would like to go on a quest, but laments that he must make shoes for {{Term|TMC|Rem}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! You're Link, right? You're the one who brought the sword to Master Melari! You're on a quest to help the princess, aren't you? I wish I could go on a quest, but then who'd make Rem's shoes for him?|Minish|TMC}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The {{Plural|TMC|Minish}} living in {{Term|TMC|Rem}}'s shop are likely a reference to the famous Grimm's fairytale of ''{{wp|The Elves and the Shoemaker|The Elves and the Shoemaker}}''.
*The three {{Term|TMC|Minish}} in {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store}} have recurring verbal quirks whenever they speak. One begins by saying, "Hey, hey!" while the other two say, "Ho, ho!" and, "Ha, ha!" respectively.
