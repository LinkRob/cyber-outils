{{Term/Store|Mega Ice||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 131}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|ST|Noko|link}}, an entrepreneurial {{Term|ST|Anouki|link}} from {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}}, sets up a shop at {{Term|ST|Wellspring Station|link}} selling {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}}—the {{Term|ST|Anouki}}'s major export—after having trouble originally opening up for business in his home village.<ref>{{Cite|Ya know how we sell Mega Ice here? Yeah, it's our village's major export. Well, a little while ago, an Ice Chu Chu fell in the water... And ever since, there's been a gooey film floatin' on the surface... It's revoltin'!|Noko|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Noko}} will sell {{Term|ST|Link|link}} 20 pieces of {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}} for 100 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}} the first time, and 50 {{Plural|ST|Rupee}} any subsequent time, though on certain occasions, {{Term|ST|Link}} is able to barter him down to 25 {{Plural|ST|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|The ladies go wild for it, and I'll even cut ya a special deal. How does 25 Rupees sound to ya?|Noko|ST}}</ref> or even get the {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}} for free if he continues to badger the prices down.<ref>{{Cite|Sheesh, ya really know how to twist a guy's flipper. Tell you what--THIS time, the ice is free. Heck, I'll even load it on your train for ya. Yes, for free. Sheesh. Happy now?|Anouki|ST}}</ref>

{{Term|ST|Link}} must transport some {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}} from {{Term|ST|Wellspring Station}} to {{Term|ST|Goron Village|link}} so the {{Plural|ST|Goron|link}} can access sections of the village that had been blocked off due to volcanic eruptions. The {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}}, as long as it is transported inside of the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}, will not melt; however, once {{Term|ST|Link}} ventures out into any other Realm, it will begin to dwindle.<ref>{{Cite|The ice is fine until ya get to warmer areas. Then ya gotta look out! If ya putter around too long in the grass, all that sweet cargo's gonna melt.|Noko|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}} is also fragile, so {{Term|ST|Link}} must avoid crashing the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} and enemy attacks.<ref>{{Cite|First, don't hit anything or let the train sway when the ice is loaded on. If ya do, the ice will spill everywhere. Also, watch out for monster attacks.|Noko|ST}}</ref> 

{{Term|ST|Mega Ice}} can also be used to keep and preserve the fish stock in {{Term|ST|Papuchia Village|link}}, and to free up the last of the {{Term|ST|Goron}} homes in {{Term|ST|Goron Village}}, in exchange for {{Plural|ST|Force Gem|link}}. Since the {{Term|ST|Mega Ice}} deliveries require transporting it over long distances, the use of {{Plural|ST|Spacetime Gate|link}} is advised.
