==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Biggoron is a gigantic [[Goron]] living on [[Death Mountain]] next to the [[Great Fairy Fountain]]. He is a skilled blade maker,<ref name=":0">{{Cite|Hylian carpenterrrrrs praise me forrrrrr my skillssssss. I'm not lyinnnnng...|Biggoron|OoT}}</ref> and creator of the powerful [[Biggoron's Sword#Ocarina of Time|Biggoron's Sword]]. To obtain it, [[Link]] must first complete the {{Term|OoT3D|Trading Quest|link}}. During the {{Term|OoT3D|Trading Quest}}, the [[Hero of Time]] brings Biggoron the [[Broken Goron's Sword]] to fix, but finds that Biggoron is unable to because of his irritated eyes. He then sends Link to get some [[Eye Drops]], and gives him a [[Prescription]]. Eventually Link brings back the Eye Drops which clears Biggoron's eyes, which he reacts with delight. He then gives Link the [[Claim Check]] for the Biggoron's Sword, which takes three days to complete. Biggoron has a younger brother, [[Medigoron]], who is also in the sword-making business, although the [[Giant's Knife|blades]] he produces are far inferior.<ref name=":1">{{Cite|My Brotherrrr... Opened a new storrrre... It's Medigoron's Blade Storrrrrrrre... Howeverrrrr... I am betterrrrrr at making bladessssss...|Biggoron|OoT}}</ref> Biggoron can also be seen during the game's ending credits dancing alongside regular-sized Gorons at [[Lon Lon Ranch]].

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Biggoron appears as the guard of the [[Snowhead Temple]] and is unrelated to the one in {{OoT|-}}. He goes to [[Snowhead]] to investigate why spring has not yet come,<ref name="Darmani">{{Cite|Darmani! What in the world have I been doing this whole time? I can't remember a thing since I went out to look around Snowhead to figure out why spring wasn't coming... What am I doing sitting in a place like this???|Biggoron|MM}}</ref> but as his size bars him from entry he ends up simply sitting outside in the cold, his body blocking the path and the sheer force of his breath causing deadly wind gusts that prevent approach to the temple. [[Darmani III]] tried to investigate soon afterwards, only to get blown away by Biggoron's breath, leading to his death.<ref>{{Cite|I was fine until I marched off to Snowhead by myself, hoping that I could drive off a demon. It had been wreaking havoc on Goron Village...Then the blizzard at Snowhead blew me into the valley...And now...Here I am...|Darmani|MM}}</ref>  To enter the temple, Link in his [[Goron Link|Goron form]] must play the "[[Goron Lullaby]]", putting Biggoron to sleep and causing him to roll off the cliff, thereby clearing the entrance. After defeating [[Goht]] and bringing spring to Snowhead, Biggoron can be found sitting in the gorge unaware of why he was there and what he had been doing.<ref>{{Cite|Why am I sitting in a place like this?|Biggoron|MM}}</ref><ref name="Darmani"/>

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, Biggoron appears atop the peak of [[Goron Mountain]]. When the [[Temple of Seasons]] sank, the climate on the mountain became colder, causing Biggoron to catch a cold, similar to {{MM|-}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goron Mountain has always had winter year-round, but ever since the Temple of Seasons sank, it's gotten even colder. You can catch cold just going outside.|Goron|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Biggoron is up above, but he has a cold, so be quiet.|Goron|OoS}}</ref> Biggoron's ailment can be cured with the [[Lava Soup]] as part of the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest}}. In return, he gives Link the [[Goron Vase]].

In a [[Linked Game]] of {{OoA}}, a lone Goron on [[Rolling Ridge]] will provide Link with the [[Biggoron Secret]]. Telling the secret to Biggoron in {{OoS|-}} allows him to forge the [[Biggoron's Sword#Oracle Series|Biggoron's Sword]].<ref>{{Cite|I want to forge a great sword like those the Gorons of old are known for. But I'll never be able to forge it properly without Biggoron's secret.|Biggoron|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's it! The Goron's Great Sword is reforged! I'll call it Biggoron's Sword! You made this possible, so you get the first one!|Biggoron|OoS}}</ref>

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, Biggoron is the name of the chief of [[Goron Island]]. Unlike past appearances, Biggoron is closer to the size of an average Goron, although he is still the biggest Goron on the island. He is the father of [[Gongoron]], and gives Link permission to enter the [[Goron Temple]] after having tested him in a quiz.

==Trivia==
*In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'', wearing the [[Goron Mask]] while speaking to [[King Zora]] reveals that Biggoron and the leader of the [[Zora]] race are actually friends, and have known each other since childhood.<ref>{{Cite|Talk about a nostalgia trip! That reminds me of my childhood friend, Biggoron!|King Zora|OoT}}</ref>
*Biggoron in {{OoT|-}} has a verbal tic where he prolongs the "r", "n", and "s" sound, such as "Brotherrrr", "Rrrrrreally", "Lyinnnnng" or "Bladesssss".<ref name=":1" /><ref name=":0" />
*According to Biggoron in {{OoT|-}}, the [[Biggoron's Sword]] is his finest work.
*Considering most real-life swords in the medieval era take about 2 weeks to produce and Biggoron created the Biggoron's Sword in only 3 days, it is clear his professional skills as a blacksmith are great and a drastic contrast to [[Medigoron]], who took 5 or 6 years to make the [[Giant's Knife]], which shatters easily.
