==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Sanke Carp}}
{{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp}} are a type of {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} found exclusively in {{Term|BotW|Lantern Lake|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. They are a subspecies of {{Term|BotW|Armored Carp|link}} that do not provide any special effects when {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cooked}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> When consumed {{Plural|BotW|Sanke Carp}} restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} each to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. When exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}, Sanke Carp become [[Roasted Carp]], while they can be chilled by [[Ice]] to make [[Frozen Carp]].

Similar to other fish, Sanke Carp can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]]. They also count towards seafood when making [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].
