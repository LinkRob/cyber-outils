{{Term/Store|Karin||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Karin|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Karin}} lives in the {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} with her parents, {{Term|BotW|Reede|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Clavia|link}}. She spends her days walking back and forth throughout the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|display=Village}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Karin}} during the day, she will begin to argue with him, but she becomes embarrassed once she realizes she is addressing the wrong person.<ref>{{Cite|It's true! I really saw her! Oh...oops. Heheh..sorry!|Karin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if she was speaking to him, {{Term|BotW|Karin}} explains that she thought he was either {{Term|BotW|Azu|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Sefaro|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[You talking to me?] I thought you were one of the boys playing spies.|Karin|BotW}}</ref> However, she encourages him to listen to her anyway.<ref>{{Cite|But you've got to listen to this anyway!|Karin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Karin}} explains that she saw a {{Term|BotW|Purah|link|display=young girl}} outside of the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a {{Color|BotW Red|girl}} staying up at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}. I saw her come outside! She was right over there! It's true!|Karin|BotW}}</ref>

Unfortunately, when {{Term|BotW|Karin}} tried to tell an adult about what she saw, she was told that the only people who live there are an old man and woman.<ref>{{Cite|But when I tried to tell a grown-up about it, they said the only people who've lived there are an old man and old woman...|Karin|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Karin}} still asserts that she saw a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} girl.<ref>{{Cite|She was a {{Color|BotW Red|Sheikah girl}}, I swear!|Karin|BotW}}</ref> She laments that people never listen when kids have something to say.<ref>{{Cite|No one ever listens to kids...|Karin|BotW}}</ref> Due to {{Term|BotW|Azu}} and {{Term|BotW|Sefaro}} playing spies, {{Term|BotW|Karin}} has heard a lot about the girl, but they have yet to catch sight of her.<ref>{{Cite|I've heard so much about that {{Color|BotW Red|girl}} up at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}. The boys playing spies are looking out for her, but I don't think she has shown up here yet.|Karin|BotW}}</ref> In her sleep, {{Term|BotW|Karin}} will occasionally mention the girl.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|There's a {{Color|BotW Red|girl}} in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab}}...}}|Karin|BotW}}</ref>

During {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Karin}} will stay inside the {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House}}. She sits on the floor as she reads a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Karin}} will follow {{Term|BotW|Link}} around if he passes by while carrying the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}. Seeing it up close, she says that it looks like a star.<ref>{{Cite|Wow... Seeing it up close...it almost looks like you're holding a star.|Karin|BotW}}</ref> She also asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} where she can get one.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, hey! Where can I get that blue thing you got there?|Karin|BotW}}</ref>
