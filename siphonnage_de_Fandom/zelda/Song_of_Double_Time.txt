The "{{Term/Store|Song of Double Time||Series, MM, MM3D}}" is a [[Song]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 96}}</ref>

==Overview==
The "Song of Double Time" lets [[Link]] warp to the night of the same day if played during the day, or to the dawn of the next if played at night. The Song cannot be played during the night of the [[Final Day]]. In {{MM3D}}, playing the "Song of Double Time" lets Link jump to a specific hour of the current day. The Song is composed of the first three notes of the "Song of Time" played twice each.<ref>{{Cite|If you play that mysterious song backward, you can slow the flow of time. And if you play each note twice in a row, you can move a half day forward in time!|Scarecrow|MM}}</ref> Link can learn about the "Song of Double Time" by talking to a [[Scarecrow]]; however, it will not be saved in the Quest Status screen.
