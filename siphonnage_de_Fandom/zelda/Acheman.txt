The {{Term/Store|Acheman|Achemen|Series, TAoL|plraul}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 162 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TFoE}} 

==Characteristics==
==={{TAoL|-}}===
{{Term|TAoL|Acheman}} are {{Wp|vampire}}-like bat-[[demon]]s that are part of the monster horde that seek {{Term|TAoL|Ganon|link}}'s resurrection. Although they do not disguise themselves as {{Plural|TAoL|Hylian|link}} like {{Plural|TAoL|Ache|link}}, they have a far more dangerous transformation. Hanging from the ceilings of their abode, they may resemble {{Plural|TAoL|Ache|link}} at first glance, albeit deep red ones. When they sense an intruder nearby, they will swoop down like an {{Term|TAoL|Ache}}. However, when {{Plural|TAoL|Acheman}} touch the ground, they transform into winged devils that spit fire at intruders. After a few volleys of fire, {{Plural|TAoL|Acheman}} will retreat into bat form and return to the ceiling. {{Plural|TAoL|Acheman}} are significantly stronger than their {{Term|TAoL|Ache}} relatives, but can still be killed with {{Term|TAoL|Link|link}}'s blade.
