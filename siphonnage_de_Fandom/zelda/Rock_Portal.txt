==Location and uses==
Rock Portals are a type of [[Minish Portal]] that are exclusively found through [[Mount Crenel]]. They allow [[Link]], with the help of [[Ezlo]], to reduce his size to that of a [[Minish]], and are also used to return Link to normal size.

Two Rock Portals can be found on [[Mount Crenel's Base]] and four of them can be found on Mount Crenel itself.
