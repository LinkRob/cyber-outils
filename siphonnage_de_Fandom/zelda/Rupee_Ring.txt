The {{Term/Store|Rupee Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rupee Ring<br/>10,000 Rupees collected|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Rupee Ring is received from [[Vasu]] after collecting 10,000 [[Rupee]]s. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 Rupees. It has no effect when worn. The Rupee Ring can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
