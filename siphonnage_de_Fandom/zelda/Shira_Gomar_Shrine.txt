The {{Term/Store|Shira Gomar Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Shira Gomar Shrine}}<br/>Aim for Stillness}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Aim for Stillness''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar Shrine}} is found on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}, just above the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}} near {{Term|BotW|Upland Lindor|link}}. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} accessed as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Revali's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

To access this {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must wait at the top of {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon}} at night until the {{Term|BotW|Dragon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}, arrives, at which point he must shoot at it.<ref>{{Cite|♪Wind's ally soars the land of cloud.<br/>{{Color|BotW Blue|Medoh}}'s Champion, swift and proud.♪<br/>♪The hero's power shall grow.<br/>Seek trials {{Color|BotW Blue|monuments}} show.♪<br/>♪One, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot the flame dragon's horn}}.<br/>Two, {{Color|BotW Red|race down a peak rings adorn}}.♪<br/>♪Three, {{Color|BotW Red|shoot four targets to win}}.<br/>Champion, the trials begin!♪|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dinraal}}'s horns will begin to glow blue as it passes Upland Lindor, as noted by {{Term|BotW|Mazli|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Come to think of it...the {{Color|BotW Blue|dragon}} always flies through this valley at night. When it does, its {{Color|BotW Red|horns light up}}. There's something about that light that's truly captivating.|Mazli|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot its horns with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}, which causes {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar Shrine}} to rise up from beneath the ground on the south side of the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|display=Canyon}}. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Shira Gomar. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Shira Gomar|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar Shrine}} consists of several fans and walls of destructible blocks. Past the {{Term|BotW|Elevator Platform|link}} are three fans creating {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use to {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link|display=Paraglide}} across the large gap. On the second platform is another fan, but the path to the third platform is blocked by a bombable wall above it. By dropping a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} onto the fan, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can detonate it when it reaches the wall, destroying it in the process. The same result can be obtained by destroying the wall with {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. After the path is cleared, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paraglide}} onto the third platform.

On the third platform is a circular funnel, the end of which leads towards a fan. The {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} from this fan is directed towards a bombable wall. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} drops his spherical {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} into the funnel, it will be deposited onto the fan. When the {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} is near the bombable wall, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can detonate it. Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can destroy the bombable wall with {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow}}. Following this, he can {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paraglide}} on the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} to the fourth platform.

The {{Term|BotW|Wind}} from the fans on the fourth platform is capable of sending {{Term|BotW|Link}} to the fifth platform. Between the fifth and sixth platforms is a large spinning gear, which features two funnels that deposit their contents onto fans. The path to the sixth platform is blocked by a bombable wall. There is also a {{Term|Series|Wind Switch|link}} between the platforms that drops a portcullis in front of the sixth platform when it is activated. The wall can be detonated by using {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} on the {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}}, which prevents the portcullis from falling. {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} can also be used on the spinning gear while it is aimed at the sixth platform, which allows the portcullis to raise itself again before the fan moves away. Alternatively, the wall can be destroyed by using {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must then {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paraglide}} on the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} while the {{Term|Series|Wind Switch}} is under the effect of {{Term|BotW|Stasis}}, allowing him to reach the sixth platform.

From the sixth platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paraglide}} on a series of fans to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. Upon approaching {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for overcoming the Trial and rewards him with {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of the hero. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this gift... Medoh's Emblem.}}|Shira Gomar|BotW}}</ref> If this is the first or second {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} that has rewarded {{Term|BotW|Link}} with this {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem|display=Emblem}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} he must collect three and states how many remain.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Collect three emblems to open the path to a new challenge.}}|Shira Gomar|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Two remain...}}|Shira Gomar|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|One remains...}}|Shira Gomar|BotW}}</ref> If it is the third {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} to reward him with {{Term|BotW|Medoh's Emblem}}, {{Term|BotW|Shira Gomar}} will tell him to go to {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Now that you have collected three emblems, you must go to Divine Beast Vah Medoh...}}|Shira Gomar|BotW}}</ref>

Near the spinning gear is a platform with a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} on top of it. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can access this platform by {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|display=Paragliding}} from the {{Term|BotW|Wind}} of one of the fans. The {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chest}} contains 10 {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow}}.
