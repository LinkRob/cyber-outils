A Chu Worm can only be hurt by first dragging it out of the jelly or by blasting it with Bomb Arrows.|book= E |page= 170}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Chu Worms are insects that fight in synergy with [[Shell Jelly|Shell Jellies]]. Upon spotting [[Link]], they will hop into a nearby Shell Jelly to defend themselves, making them invulnerable to attacks. To defeat them, Link must either first drag them out using the [[Clawshot]], or blast their Shell Jellies with a Bomb Arrow. After getting separated, Chu Worms will attempt to hop back into their Shell Jellies.
