Assign it to {{Icon|CoH A}}, {{Icon|CoH B}}, {{Icon|CoH X}}, or {{Icon|CoH Y}} on the inventory screen. This will heal {{Color|CoH Green|5 hearts}} and some stamina if you use it!|N/A|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Big Fish}} can be caught as a random catch by using an {{Term|CoH|Bottle|link}} over a tile of bubbling water. These tiles can be found in any body of water, including the deep water found inside dungeons and {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle|link}}, and therefore act as an alternative method for {{Term|CoH|Heart|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Stamina|link}} recovery when {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} run out of {{Plural|CoH|Potion|link}}.<ref name="Obtention"/>

However, unlike {{Plural|CoH|Fairy|link}} and {{Plural|CoH|Potion}}, {{Plural|CoH|Big Fish}} are not automatically consumed when the user runs out of {{Plural|CoH|Heart|link}} or {{Term|CoH|Stamina|link}}, but they will stay in their {{Term|CoH|Inventory|link}} even after a {{Term|CoH|Game Over|link}}.
