The {{Term/Store|Phonogram Man||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Phonogram Man}}'s name is a misnomer, as he should instead be referred to as the ''Phonograph'' Man.}} He teaches {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} the "{{Term|OoT3D|Song of Storms|link}}".

==Biography==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Phonogram Man}} stands inside {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|link}} playing his {{Term|OoT3D|Phonograph|link}}. He is attempting to compose a musical theme inspired by the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|display=Windmill}}'s cyclicality.<ref>{{Cite|I'm trying to come up with a musical theme inspired by this windmill...going around and around and around!!!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref> He loves to "go around and around."<ref name="Happy">{{Cite|Go around! Go around and around and around! What fun! I'm so happy! I'm a music man who loves to go around and around!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref> Ironically, he is responsible for a time loop that ruins his enjoyment of the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|display=Windmill}} by making it spin faster.<ref>{{Cite|Go around, go around, go around... What? It's going way too fast!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref>

The {{Term|OoT3D|Phonogram Man}} is initially happy when young {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} speaks to him.<ref name="Happy"/> When adult {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} speaks to him, he is bitterly angry at "that {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|display=Ocarina}} kid" about an event seven years ago.<ref>{{Cite|Grrrrrrrrr! I'll never forget what happened on that day, seven years ago! Grrrrrrrrr! It's all that Ocarina kid's fault! Next time he comes around here, I'm gonna mess him up!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref> He fails to recognize adult {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} as that boy. When {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} pulls out his {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|link}} in front of the {{Term|OoT3D|Phonogram Man}}, he angrily recounts the day the boy tampered with the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|display=Windmill}} by playing the "{{Term|OoT3D|Song of Storms|link}}" on the {{Term|OoT3D|Ocarina of Time (Item)|display=Ocarina}}.<ref>{{Cite|What?! You've got an ocarina!! What the heck! That reminds me of that time, seven years ago! Back then a mean kid came here and played a strange song. It messed up this windmill!|Phonogram Man|OoT}}</ref> He plays the {{Term|OoT3D|Song}} on his {{Term|OoT3D|Phonograph|link}} in demonstration. In doing so, he unwittingly becomes the one who teaches it to {{Term|OoT3D|Link}}. The hero goes back in time and plays the "{{Term|OoT3D|Song of Storms|link}}" in the {{Term|OoT3D|Kakariko Windmill|display=Windmill}} as young {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} to drain the {{Term|OoT3D|Bottom of the Well}}. This upsets the {{Term|OoT3D|Phonogram Man}} and completes the time loop.
