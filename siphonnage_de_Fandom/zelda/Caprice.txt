{{Term/Store|Caprice||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Caprice}} is a member of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|link}}, and can be found at the second floor of their {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link|display=Home}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}. She watches over {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} while he is afflicted by the {{Term|TMC|Ghost|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe {{Color|TMC Green|old Gregal}} is sick. He was looking so healthy. I guess something just took hold of him...|Caprice|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} saves {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} by using the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Caprice}} will thank him upon his return later in the game;<ref name="Gramps"/> otherwise, she will simply explain the history of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} to him.<ref>{{Cite|Although we are of the Wind Tribe, we, too, once lived on the surface. Once, the place you call the {{Color|TMC Green|Wind Ruins}} was, to us, a home.|Caprice|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} arrives at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} via the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}}, he can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Caprice}},<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}, don't you? Well, OK! Let's give it a try.|Caprice|TMC}}</ref> resulting in a {{Term|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a blue {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} appearing at {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.
