{{Plural|BotW|Edge of Duality}} are double handed {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that were crafted using {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}} {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} for the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}}, who were unfamiliar with the single edged blades traditionally favored by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 170}}</ref> They have a brown blade, blue and gold handle, and a black cross guard. The swords themselves are carried in open black sheaths that, along with the cross guards, are engraved with the {{Term|BotW|Crest of the Sheikah|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Edge of Duality}} can be found at various locations within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. One may also randomly appear inside a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} if the {{SSB|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Sheik|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Edge of Duality}} are made of metal, and will therefore attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if they are equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they can be picked up again and manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. Unlike wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, they will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.
