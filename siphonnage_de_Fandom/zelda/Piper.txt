==Biography==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Her name is Piper, and she is a skilled cook. She is the mother of the mischievous Gully.</p><p>In addition to running the restaurant at the bazaar, she is also a food experimenter who is always trying to find new recipes.</p>
}}

Piper is a resident of [[Skyloft]] and the mother of [[Gully]], a mischievous boy who has an avid interest in all types of insects. She is a skilled cook that manages the restaurant in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}}. She has a habit of repeatedly tasting her food before settling on the right flavor,<ref>{{Cite|Next, a dash of pepper... Delicious! Another culinary delight ready to be savored and enjoyed!|Piper|SS}}</ref> and often experiments with new combinations of ingredients. During the day, she can be found in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar}}, preparing food for the patrons. However, she is known for taking a long time among the customers.<ref>{{Cite|You looking to get your grub on? The food here is sooooo good! You gotta be patient, though. It seems like you wait forever for anything around here.|Dovos|SS}}</ref> At night, she returns to her home and starts making dinner.<ref>{{Cite|Mmm-MMM! Another day, another tasty soup!|Piper|SS}}</ref> As with her food during the day, she takes a long time preparing it, apparently late into the hours of the night.<ref>{{Cite|Is that you, Link? What are you doing up so late? I'm hungry! My mom's still cooking, but it's taking forever. When will it be ready?|Gully|SS}}</ref> 

Because Piper is a cook, many spices and cooking supplies can be found throughout her house. Above her bed is a large photo of Skyloft and three of Gully's drawings.

==Trivia==
*Piper does not appear to carry a [[Sailcloth]] with her, though her apron bears her design, like other Sailcloths.

==Nomenclature==
Like all other Skyloftians, Piper's name is derived from a bird, specifically the {{Wp|Sandpiper}}.
