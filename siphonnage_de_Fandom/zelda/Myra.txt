{{Term/Store|Myra||Series, TWoG}} is a character in {{TWoG}}.<ref>{{Cite Magazine|quote={{Exp|You get this lamp in Nokani Forest, from Myra to be specific.|'Die lamp krijg je in Nokani-bos, en wel van Myra.}}|magazine= CD Interactief|volume= 4|issue= 4|date= April 1997|publisher=HUB Uitgevers|page=36}}</ref> She is a woman [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] encounters in the forests of [[Gamelon]].

==Biography==
Myra is first encountered in [[Hanyu Forest]]. She asks Zelda if she has an [[Arpagos Feather]] and uses the one Zelda found in [[Gobiyan Ship]] to turn her [[Shroud]] into a [[Magic Cape]],<ref>{{Cite|Greetings Princess Zelda. Do you have any Arpagos Feathers? [...] Good, then let us make a cape from your Shroud.|Myra|TWoG}}</ref> allowing her to jump farther. She is later encountered again in [[Nokani Forest]], where she gives Zelda the [[Fairy Lantern]], telling her it will "penetrate the dark around [[Ganon]]." However she also warns her that only the [[Wand of Gamelon (Item)|Wand of Gamelon]] can defeat him and bids her good luck.<ref>{{Cite|This light will penetrate the dark around Ganon, but only the wand can defeat him. Good luck!|Myra|TWoG}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Although Myra uses Zelda's Shroud to turn into the Magic Cape, in her cutscene, her green apron is used to combine with Zelda's Arpagos Feather instead.
