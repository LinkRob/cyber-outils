==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Thunderwing Butterfly}}
Thunderwing Butterflies can be found flying on [[Hyrule Ridge]] and in the [[Gerudo Highlands]] and can be caught be sneaking up on them. [[Beedle]] also sells Thunderwing Butterflies at [[Lakeside Stable]], [[Riverside Stable]], and [[Tabantha Bridge Stable]]. They can be combined with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} to make an [[Electro Elixir]], which grants Shock Resistance when used.<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] can also use Thunderwing Butterflies to create Yellow [[Dye]].
