{{Term|TWWHD|Northern Fairy Island}} is a small, heart-shaped island located on quadrant A3 of the Great Sea. Unlike the other Fairy Islands, the entrance to the shell is not blocked by any obstacle. Link can enter to find a Great Fairy inside, who will upgrade the holding capacity of his [[Wallet]] to either 1000 [[Rupee]]s or 5000 Rupees, depending whether or not he has received the wallet upgrade in [[Outset Island]].
