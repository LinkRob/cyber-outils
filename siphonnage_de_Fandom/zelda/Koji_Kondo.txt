'''Koji Kondo''' is a composer and audio supervisor at [[Nintendo]].

==Biography==
{{Lacking Sources}}
Kondo has been both praised and criticized for his use of familiarity in his compositions. He is recognized for his ability to create themes that are catchy when first heard, and still remains enjoyable while looped. However, he has also been criticized for not varying his style more during his two decades as Nintendo composer. However, when Kondo tried to create something different for the [[The Legend of Zelda: Original Sound Track|{{OoT|-}} soundtrack]], he was again criticized by some for abandoning many of the familiar themes from previous games of {{TLoZ|Series}}, such as {{TLoZ|-}} Main Theme (Overworld Theme from {{ALttP}}).

He has composed the music to {{TLoZ}}, {{ALttP}}, {{OoT}}, {{MM}} (with [[Toru Minegishi]]), {{TWW}} (with [[Kenta Nagata]], [[Hajime Wakai]], and [[Toru Minegishi]]), and {{TP}} (with [[Toru Minegishi]] and [[Asuka Hayazaki]]). His music has become very well-known and popular, with the ''[[mariowiki:Super Mario Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]'' theme becoming one of the best-selling ringtones.
