==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Mighty Thistle}}
{{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle}} can be found growing in the [[Faron Grasslands]] and [[West Necluda]]. Many Mighty Thistles grow around the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} near the [[Tabantha Tower]]. When Mighty Thistles are used while making a meal in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, the resulting dish will increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s attack damage when eaten. Mighty Thistles that make contact with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} turn into {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Mighty Thistle|link}}. At the [[Kochi Dye Shop]], Mighty Thistles can be used to create Orange [[Dye]].
