The {{Term/Store|Necklace||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 132"/>

==Location and Uses==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA}}, the Necklace is the twelfth item in the game's {{Term|LANS|Trading Quest|link}}. It is obtained from the [[Fisherman]] beneath the bridge in [[Martha's Bay]], after [[Link]] gives him the [[Fishing Hook]]. After giving him the Hook, the Fisherman promises to give Link his next catch, which happens to be the Necklace.<ref name="E 132"/> The Necklace actually belongs to [[Martha]] the [[Mermaid]], who had lost the item to the waves while swimming.<ref>{{Cite|When I was swimming in the bay, the waves took a very important necklace from around my neck! If you find it, I will let you take a scale from my tail!|Martha|LADX}}</ref> In return for her Necklace, she lets Link take one of her [[Scale]]s.<ref name="E 132"/>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{TFoE|-}}===
[[File:Necklace Icon CD-i.png|left]]
In {{TFoE}}, the Necklace is an item found in [[Spearfish Falls]], guarded by a [[Gleeok]] inside a cavern. It was stolen from [[Alora]] in [[Toyku Lighthouse]], who asks Link to retrieve it for her. After bringing her back the Necklace, Alora rewards him with a kiss and a [[Canteen]]. Alora explains that the Necklace was a gift from her husband.<ref>{{Cite|My husband gave me this.|Alora|TFoE}}</ref>
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*In the Japanese, French, and German versions of ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'' and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening DX|Link's Awakening DX]]'', the Necklace is actually Martha's '''Pink Bra'''.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Simply put, she originally lost her top in the Japanese version, which is why she looks and acts so flustered. This is also why she's so quick to flee when Link tries to take a peek while diving.|author= |published= |retrieved= |url= http://artemis251.fobby.net/zelda/unused.php|title= Unused Parts|site= Artemis251's Link's Awakening Cache|type=}}</ref> In the North American and European version of the games as well as every release of the [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|Nintendo Switch remake]], the Pink Bra was changed to the Necklace to avoid controversy.
