{{Term/Store|King Dorephan||Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="King"/> He is the ruler of the {{Term|Series|Zora|link}} and, by the time of {{BotW}}, has been for up to 200 years.<ref name="History">{{Cite|Around 100 years after King Dorephan ascended to the throne, a stray Guardian crossed Upland Zorana into our domain.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} is the reigning monarch of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. In addition to this, he is the father of {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Sidon|link}}.<ref name="Bazz"/> He presides over the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}} from the {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} greatly cherished his daughter {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}.<ref name="Mipha"/> As the threat of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} rose, he eventually gave her permission to pilot {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, though he made her promise to return to him safely.<ref>{{Cite|Father has finally given his blessing for me to pilot the Divine Beast. The threat of the Calamity can no longer be ignored. Father said the Zora must play their part. He seemed on the brink of tears when he made me promise to return unharmed.|Mipha's Diary|BotW}}</ref> Following the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, he grew to regret allowing her to join the fight against {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|display=Ganon}}.<ref>{{Cite|I thought nothing of it at the time, but given the events that followed, I now regret allowing this to happen.|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref> Since then, he has prayed every day that {{Term|BotW|Mipha}}'s soul will rest in peace.<ref>{{Cite|I have spent many long years consumed by guilt. My dearest wish is that her soul will know peace. I pray for it every day.|History of the Zora, Part Six|BotW}}</ref>

According to the {{Term|BotW|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}} approached {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} through {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}} approximately 100 years after {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} became its ruler.<ref name="History"/> When the {{Term|BotW|Zora|link}} soldiers were unable to stop the {{Term|BotW|Guardian|link}}, {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan}} decided to face it on his own.<ref>{{Cite|The Guardian seemed unstoppable. Our best soldiers' spears barely left a scratch on its metal hull. It was then that our King Dorephan, without a thought for his own safety, came out to face the Guardian himself.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref> He threw the {{Term|BotW|Guardian}} into a ravine and destroyed it, which increased the trust and respect his citizens had for him.<ref>{{Cite|With supernatural strength, he lifted the Guardian and hurled it into a ravine. The impact of the fall left the Guardian in pieces, and it was never to trouble another Zora ever again. The citizens celebrated the king's valor. From then on, their trust and respect for him grew beyond compare.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref> He still has a scar on his forehead from this encounter.<ref>{{Cite|You can still see the scar he earned that day on his forehead—a token of his triumph.|History of the Zora, Addendum 1|BotW}}</ref>
