{{Term/Store|Barnes||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 277 {{Exp Game|TP}}}}</ref> He is the owner of the [[Barnes Bomb Shop|Bomb Shop]] in [[Kakariko Village#Twilight Princess|Kakariko Village]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 13}}</ref>

==Biography==
Barnes appears at first inside [[Renado]]'s sanctuary, hidden along with the children of [[Ordon Village]], {{Term|TPHD|Luda|link}}, and Renado himself, while the entirety of the [[Eldin Province]] is covered by the [[Twilight]]. When [[Link]] purges the province from it, Barnes discovers that his storage house has been destroyed, so he begins making new [[Bomb]]s before reopening his shop.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD|publisher= Prima|page= 114}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TPHD|publisher= Prima|page= 120}}</ref> As the game progresses, he manages to recover his lineup of products, including regular Bombs, [[Water Bomb]]s, and [[Bombling]]s.

==Trivia==
*If Link tries to pull out his [[Lantern]] while inside Barnes' Bomb Shop, Barnes will slam his fist on the counter, causing Link to be doused with water. If Link does this while close to Barnes, Barnes will yell at him in protest.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Hey now! You insane, bud? That's dangerous! Don't go lightin' fires in the middle of my shop!|Barnes|TP}}</ref>
*If [[Wolf Link]] enters his Bomb Shop, he puts his welders mask down to cover his face.

==Nomenclature==
Barnes's name may be a pun on "burns". In the French version of the game, his name is Crahmé, a pun on the French slang "cramé", meaning "burned".
