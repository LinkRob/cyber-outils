==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Luminous Stones can be found by breaking [[Luminous Stone Deposit]]s, which can be identified by their glow during the night. They are also dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Stone Talus (Luminous)|link}} upon their defeat. The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] can use Luminous Stones to make Navy [[Dye]]. Three of them are also required to buy one piece of the [[Radiant Set]] at the [[Gerudo Secret Club]].

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to [[Ledo]] at {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, he will explain that he needs more Luminous Stones in order to continue his repairs.<ref>{{Cite|Yes... Repairs require a large number of {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He will ask Link to gather ten Luminous Stones for him, which will initiate the "[[Luminous Stone Gathering]]" [[Side Quest]].<ref>{{Cite|For instance, if you have {{Color|BotW Red|10 of them}}, that would be a big help.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> Upon delivery, Ledo will reward Link with two {{Plural|BotW|Diamond|link}}. Afterwards, Link can bring him ten Luminous Stones at any time in exchange for one Diamond.<ref>{{Cite|If you ever find yourself in possession of {{Color|BotW Red|10}} {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} in the future, I hope you will bring them to me.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But if that happens, know that I'll only be able to give you {{Color|BotW Red|one}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}} for them.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

The Radiant Set requires Luminous Stones the be upgraded to its first, second, third, and fourth tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Radiant Set''': 129 (5, 8, 10, 20) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity, Luminous Stones share the same design as their Breath of the Wild counterpart. They can be found in gray treasure chests in main quests, as well as be obtained through first-clear rewards. They can be sold to most Merchants for 70 Rupees, but [[Dayfah]] will buy them for 175 Rupees.
{{Noncanon|End}}
