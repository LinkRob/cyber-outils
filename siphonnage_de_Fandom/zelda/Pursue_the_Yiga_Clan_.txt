{{Term/Store|Pursue the Yiga Clan!||HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Pursue the Yiga Clan!|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Pursue the Yiga Clan!}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Naboris}} with {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} as its pilot. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Thigh|link}}, and 400 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.

Throughout the entire {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}'s objective is to defeat 3,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. Most of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}, although {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Blademaster|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Molduga|link}} can be found as well. A large amount of {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} also spawns twice during the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, to fill up empty portions of the map as Vah Naboris approaches them. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once 3,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated.
