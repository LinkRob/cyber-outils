'''''The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword — Prima Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{SS}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 271 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{SS}}, collectibles and secrets such as {{Plural|SS|Gratitude Crystal|link}}, and listings for equipment upgrades, {{Plural|SS|Treasure|link}}, {{Plural|SS|Insect|link}}, and {{Term|SS|Potion|link}} recipes.
