The {{Term/Store|Post Office||Series, MM, MM3D, OoA, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring location in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoA Map Past|Post Office}}|Map|OoA}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Post Office|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Post Office Notice|Letters|TPHD}}</ref>

==Appearances==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, the Post Office is located in [[West Clock Town]]. It is open from 3 PM to 12 AM on the [[First Day|First]] and [[Second Day]]. The [[Postman]] resides here, and can usually be seen mentally training on his bed.<ref>{{Cite|In my mind, I am running for exactly 10 seconds without looking at a clock. I was in the middle of mental training!|Postman|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] may receive a [[Piece of Heart]] as a reward by playing a game with the Postman. The game is won by stopping at exactly 10 seconds on the timer.<ref>{{Cite|Huh?!? 10 seconds?!? That is amazing! You have reflexes suitable for a postman! As thanks for showing me something impressive, I give you this.|Postman|MM}}</ref> Equipping the [[Bunny Hood]] during the challenge keeps the timer displayed, which normally becomes invisible after 3 seconds. A calendar, a map, and a clock can be seen on the wall. There is also a dressing room in the back where the Postman keeps his hat and backpack.

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, the Post Office is located in [[Lynna Village]] in the past. Inside the Office, Link will find the Postman, who plays a role in the {{Term|OoA|Trading Quest|link}}. Link can trade him the [[Poe Clock]] in return for the [[Stationery]], the second item in the Sequence.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Mail Center}}
In {{TWW}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center|link}} has an arch on the second floor that says "Post Office" in {{Term|TWWHD|Hylian (Language)|link}}. The area is the headquarters of the {{Term|TWWHD|Island Postal Service|link}}. Here {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can play the {{Term|TWWHD|Mail Center (Minigame)|display=Mail Center minigame}} in which he helps {{Term|TWWHD|Koboli|link}} or {{Term|TWWHD|Baito|link}} sort letters.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, the {{Term|TMC|Post Office}} is located in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. It is where {{Term|TMC|Stamp|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Marcy|link}} work, as well as the {{Term|TMC|Postman}}, who runs around town delivering letters. After [[Kinstone Fusion|fusing]] {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Postman}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can buy the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}} published by {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Post Office}}. They contain secrets and techniques for playing the game.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, the first {{Term|TP|Postman|link}}'s first {{Term|TPHD|Mail Delivery|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} is a {{Term|TPHD|Letter|link}} entitled "{{Term|TPHD|Post Office Notice|link}}". No actual {{Term|TPHD|Post Office}} appears in the game, however.
