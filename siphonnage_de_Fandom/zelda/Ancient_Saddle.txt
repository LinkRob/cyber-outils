The {{Term/Store|Ancient Saddle||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Saddle}}<br/>A mysterious saddle made with forgotten ancient technology. Equip a horse with this and use {{Icon|BotW D-Pad Down}} to call them even when they are far away.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Saddle}} is a {{Term|BotW|Saddle|link}} made from ancient {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} technology that is exclusive to {{TCB}} DLC Pack.<ref name="Inventory"/> When the DLC is active, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will receive the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, "{{Term|Series|EX Ancient Horse Rumors|link}}", which instructs him to read the copy of {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill EX|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|Highland Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare horse gear.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> There, Link can read {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V3|link}}, which explains that the saddle is found at a spring where a horse spirit resides.<ref>{{Cite|The saddle is {{Color|BotW Red|at a mysterious spring where a horse spirit resides}}.|Super Rumor Mill V3|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, Link can find the Saddle inside a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that is embedded in the ground at {{Term|BotW|Malanya Spring|link}}.

Link can equip the Ancient Saddle to his Horses by talking to workers at Highland Stable, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Change to {{Color|BotW Red|Ancient Saddle}}?|Canni|BotW}}</ref> When equipped, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is capable of {{Term|BotW|Whistle|link|display=Whistling}} to {{Term|BotW|Warping|link|display=Warp}} his {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} nearly anywhere that a {{Term|BotW|Horse}} can be present,<ref name="Inventory"/> even if the {{Term|BotW|Horse}} is out of earshot and would not be able to run to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s location. As with other {{Plural|BotW|Saddle}}, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Saddle}} cannot be equipped to {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}.
