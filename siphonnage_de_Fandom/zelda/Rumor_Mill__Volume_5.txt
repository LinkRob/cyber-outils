The {{Term/Store|''Rumor Mill: Volume 5''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor Mill: Volume 5|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill: Volume 5}} is located on the table at {{Term|BotW|Wetland Stable|link}}. It is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Rumor Mill|link}} series written by {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}}. In this edition, she recounts rumors of {{Plural|BotW|Dragon|link}} sighted in {{Term|BotW|Lanayru (Region)|link|display=Lanayru}}, {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Faron (Region)|link|display=Faron}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragons Actually Exist!}} "No way! That simply can't be true!" Is that what you're thinking right now? I used to think that too... But then I traveled to Lanayru, Akkala, and Faron, and I heard some real eyewitness accounts!|Rumor Mill: Volume 5|BotW}}</ref> These accounts line up with the appearances of the {{Plural|BotW|Dragon}} {{Term|BotW|Naydra|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Farosh|link}}. Despite all of her witnesses being children, {{Term|BotW|Traysi}} has faith in them, giving this rumor four out of five stars.<ref>{{Cite|Of course, these were scoops from children, but still... Children are pure of heart, meaning they would never lie! I have total confidence in my sources. Traysi's Recommendation: ★★★★☆|Rumor Mill: Volume 5|BotW}}</ref>
