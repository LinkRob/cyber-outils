{{Term/Store|Madame Couture||Series, TFH, SSBU}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 305"/>

==Biography==
==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} is the titular proprietor of {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}, the high-end clothing store in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, where {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} may order {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}} in exchange for {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}}. {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}}'s clothes are famous around the world because of the special powers they contain.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Exp|服屋「マダムテーラー」のオーナー兼トップデザイナー。彼女の作る服には特別な力が宿るというウワサで世界中から引っ張りだこ。|The owner and top designer of the "Madame Tailor" clothes shop. Because of rumors that special powers dwell within the clothes she makes, they're in high demand worldwide.}}|author= Nintendo|published= September 16, 2015|retrieved=September 24, 2015|url= http://www.nintendo.co.jp/3ds/ea3j/storychara/characters.html|title= ゼルダの伝説　トライフォース３銃士 <nowiki>|</nowiki> ニンテンドー3DS <nowiki>|</nowiki> 任天堂|site= Zelda.co.jp|type=}} <sup>(Translation: [http://zeldauniverse.net/forums/index.php/Thread/178050-The-Legend-of-Zelda-TriForce-Heroes-Gameplay-spoilers-beware/?postID=5657575#post5657575 kaialone])</sup></ref> She has an obsession with {{Plural|TFH|Cat|link}} and keeps a number of them in her shop. There, she is assisted by a {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's Apprentice|link|display=green-haired boy}}.

{{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} eventually reveals that she is also {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}}'s younger sister.<ref name="Older Sister"/> Together they are part of a tribe who specialize in style and powerful magic.<ref>{{Cite|People of our tribe are gifted, my pet. Not just with a keen sense of style, but with real magical abilities!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> However, while {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} uses her magic for good by creating {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}} infused with magic, {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} grew power-hungry, and eventually cursed {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's why the clothes I design grant the wearer special powers. The Lady must have gotten power-hungry.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> Following the incident, {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} helped the {{Term|TFH|Witch-Hunting Brigade|link}} in tracking her down, but feels guilty as {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} is still her sister.<ref>{{Cite|Uncouth or not, she is still my sister. I feel a bit guilty about helping to hunt her down. But what can be done?|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> She hopes that a worthy hero will one day come not just to save the princess, but also {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} from herself.<ref>{{Cite|Oui. That's why I was looking for a hero... to save the princess, but also to save The Lady from herself.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> She also suspects that {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud}} must have had some reason for cursing the princess.<ref>{{Cite|Still, in my heart of hearts, I can't help but believe The Lady had some reason for doing what she did to the princess...|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref>

When {{Term|TFH|Link}} arrives in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, he is spotted by {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's Apprentice}}, who believes him to be the {{Term|TFH|Tri Force Hero|link|display=chosen hero}}. {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} herself, however, is highly skeptical that he could be the hero, as she has seen many would-be heroes come and go who all failed to catch the witch. But she reconsiders when {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}} recruits him and sends him back to {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}}'s shop for her to tailor a {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic|link}} for {{Term|TFH|Link}}. When {{Term|TFH|Link}} returns with the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Glasses|link}} he obtains from the {{Term|TFH|Woodlands|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture}} immediately recognizes it as one of {{Plural|TFH|The Lady's Materials|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm?! My heavens! You have THAT material! One of The Lady's materials? |Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> She starts to regain some hope, as it, along with the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Collar|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Parasol|link}}, can be used to make the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble|link}} and lift the curse from {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|display=Styla}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that you have some of The Lady's materials, it's time I showed you this little piece of work. I call it... The Lady's Ensemble! If you wear this and visit the princess, you should be able to lift her curse! All without defeating The Lady.|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref> She implores {{Term|TFH|Link}} to find the rest of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} on his travels in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The materials for The Lady's Ensemble are so rare, I had started to lose hope. But now I have regained le faith. Perhaps you can find them all. That's what heroes do, right? They help people, dahling!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref>

== Physical Description ==
Madame Couture is a short, stout woman with slightly yellow skin, a small mouth, a triangular head, short light blue hair with a large pink bow, and wears thin black glasses.

She wears a long, bright red dress with a salmon-colored spiral print.

== Abilities ==
Madame Couture is a powerful human witch and is also skilled at sewing, being able to create beautiful dresses and costumes and imbue them with her [[magic]] to give whoever wears them unique powers. Despite being a very strong witch, she is weaker than her sister Maud.
