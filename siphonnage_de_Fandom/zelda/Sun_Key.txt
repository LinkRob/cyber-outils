The {{Term/Store|Sun Key||Series, PH}} is an item in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Use==
The Sun Key is found deep beneath the southwestern part of the [[World of the Ocean King]]. It is located by use of a chart obtained from within the [[Temple of the Ocean King]], and retrieved by [[Link]] using the [[SS Linebeck]]'s [[Salvage Arm]]. In the Temple of the Ocean King, Link was to transfer the symbol of the Courage Pendant from a statue onto his map, thus illuminating the location of the Sun Key, approximately at the center of the Southwestern sea chart. The key itself is used to open a door emblazoned with the symbol of the sun in the [[Old Wayfarer]]'s hideout on [[Molida Island]], granting Link access to the [[Temple of Courage]] at the rear of the isle.
