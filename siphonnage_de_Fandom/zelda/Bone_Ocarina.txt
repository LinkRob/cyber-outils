The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Bone Ocarina||Series, FPTRR}}}} is an item in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|FPTRR Yellow|Bone Ocarina!}}|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|FPTRR|Bone Ocarina}} is obtained shortly after clearing the second {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon|link}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Pirate Hideaway|link}} in {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure|link}}. It can be used to summon the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate|link}} while standing on a {{Term|FPTRR|Dock|link}}. These {{Plural|FPTRR|Dock}} can be found in {{Term|FPTRR|Cape Treasure}} in the {{Term|FPTRR|First Continent|link}}, the {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp|link}} in the {{Term|FPTRR|Second Continent|link}}, and {{Term|FPTRR|Mount Desma|link}} in the {{Term|FPTRR|Third Continent|link}}.

[[File:Bone Ocarina Upgrade.png‎|left]]
{{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} can upgrade the {{Term|FPTRR|Bone Ocarina}} after helping the {{Plural|FPTRR|Pirate}} for a second time in the {{Term|FPTRR|Gooey Swamp}}. It can then be used to open the ten hidden blue Pirate {{Plural|FPTRR|Treasure Chest|link}} once they are found.
