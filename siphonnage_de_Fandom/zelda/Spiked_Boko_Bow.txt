{{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|East Necluda|link}}. They are {{Plural|BotW|Boko Bow|link}} that have been upgraded with {{Term|BotW|Animal|link}} bone to increase their {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}} and strength.<ref name="Inventory"/> Though they are sloppily made, they are light and easy to use.<ref name="Inventory"/> These {{Plural|BotW|Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Bow}} will burn if they touch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or if they are equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They also float if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they are incompatible with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boko Bow}} do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} like metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} do during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}.

If scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}, the {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} {{Term|BotW|Amiibo|link}} has a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Spiked Boko Bow}}.
