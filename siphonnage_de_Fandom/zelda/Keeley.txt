{{Term/Store|Keeley||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Keeley}} is a woman in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. She remains north of {{Term|TMC|Romio|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|link|display=House}} alongside {{Term|TMC|Verona|link}} until {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}}, whereupon she disappears from {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} altogether, and does not appear for the remainder of the game. She has an unnamed husband about whom she complains.<ref>{{Cite|Yow! That sword-fighting tournament was so exciting! Oh, if only my husband were talented enough to compete...|Keeley|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Even I’m a better swordfighter than my husband is. He can’t even handle a butter knife!|Keeley|TMC}}</ref> After {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} possesses {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}, she notes how strange he is acting, as well as the conspicuous absence of {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I haven’t seen {{Color|TMC Green|Princess Zelda}} in ages, and the {{Color|TMC Green|king}}’s just plain loopy! What’s going on around here?|Keeley|TMC}}</ref>

She is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}, don't you? Well, OK! Let's give it a try.|Keeley|TMC}}</ref>
