The {{Term/Store|Gallery||Series, HW, HWL, HWDE, HWAoC}} is a recurring interface in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|HWDE Red|{{Big|Gallery}}}}|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Gallery|System|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{HW|-}}===
The {{Term|HWDE|Gallery}} is a submenu in the {{Term|HWDE|Select Mode|link}} menu. The {{Term|HWDE|Gallery}} is where the player can display and view their collectables.<ref>{{Cite|View collectables.|Select Mode|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Gallery}} consists of six submenus. The {{Term|HWDE|View Warriors|link}} menu, where the player can view their unlocked {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|View playable warriors.|Gallery|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|View Cutscenes|link}} menu, where the player can rewatch any already viewed cutscene from {{Term|HWDE|Legend Mode|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rewatch cutscenes from the game's story.|Gallery|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Music Player|link}}, where the player can listen to any unlocked background music.<ref>{{Cite|Listen to unlocked BGM.|Gallery|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Illustrations|link}}, where the player can view completed pictures obtained from {{Plural|HWDE|Gold Skulltula|link}} and see the uncollected piececs.<ref>{{Cite|View completed pictures and check pieces.|Gallery|HWDE}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Medal List|link}}, where the player can view every {{Term|HWDE|Medal|link}}, collected or not.<ref>{{Cite|Check your medal collection.|Gallery|HWDE}}</ref> And finally, the {{Term|HWDE|Tutorials|link}}, where the player can read any unlocked {{Plural|HWDE|Tutorial|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|View unlocked tutorials.|Gallery|HWDE}}</ref>
