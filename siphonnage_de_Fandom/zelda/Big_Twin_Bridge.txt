{{Term/Store|Big Twin Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Big Twin Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Big Twin Bridge is one of two bridges that cross [[Squabble River]] to the east of [[Dueling Peaks]]. The second bridge is the [[Little Twin Bridge]]. The Big Twin Bridge is mostly dilapidated and has pieces missing. Debris has also built upon the eastern side, including dead [[Tree]]s, stone wreckage, and a stone slab. A traveler named [[Mezer]] can be seen crossing the Bridge.

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing an [[Opal]] can be found under the rock slab off the eastern side of the Bridge. In [[Master Mode]], there is a platform held aloft by [[Sky Octorok]]s that holds a Treasure Chest containing a [[Soldier's Spear]].
