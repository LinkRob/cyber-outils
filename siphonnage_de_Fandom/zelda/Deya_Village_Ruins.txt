The {{Term/Store|Deya Village Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Deya Village Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}} are the half-sunken remains of a village that once existed in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. They are found in the northern portion of {{Term|BotW|Deya Lake|link}} at the southern base of the {{Term|BotW|Hills of Baumer|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}} are set mostly on marshland, with little harvestable vegetation remaining. A {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} can be found among the {{Plural|BotW|Ruined House|link}} along the hill to the south. A {{Term|BotW|Barrel|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Chuchu|link}} can be found inside a {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Stone Talus|link}} rests in the northern section of the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}}. In {{Term|BotW|The Champions' Ballad|link}} DLC Pack, {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2}} can be found under a collapsed roof in one of the easternmost {{Plural|BotW|Ruined House}}. The {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|display=''Journal''}} contains the locations for six priceless relics stolen from {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Misko|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The six priceless relics are linked to four {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}}: "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Merchant Hood|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Garb of Winds|link}}", "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Usurper King|link}}", and "{{Term|BotW|EX Treasure: Dark Armor|link}}". When set as {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s destination, all four {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}} will mark the {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|display=''Journal''}}'s location in the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Map}} until {{Term|BotW|Link}} reads the page of the {{Term|BotW|Misko's EX Journal 2|display=''Journal''}} relevant to that {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|display=Quest}}.

Several broken {{Term|BotW|Wagon|link}} litter the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}}, which can be searched for {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}}. A stone slab can be found in the northeastern portion of the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}}, which can either be held with the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} and struck, or moved with the {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} from underneath. Beneath the slab is a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}}. A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Blade|link}} can be found embedded in a well south of this slab. In order to open it, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}} to pull it from the ground. Southeast of this well is a second stone slab, which covers a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}, valued at 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}. A fourth stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} is located beneath a pile of {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}}. It contains a bundle of 10 {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}. A metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} buried underground sits in the {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}} on the largest island, and contains a {{Term|BotW|Phrenic Bow|link}}. North of this is another {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}}, which hides a stone {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing another {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee}} behind two {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}}. Another metallic {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal}} can be found buried in an old well behind a {{Term|BotW|Ruined House}} in the {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}} on the southern hill overlooking the {{Term|BotW|Deya Lake|display=Lake}}.

A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found underneath a {{Term|BotW|Rock|link}} inside a hollow {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} trunk on the southwestern portion of the {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}}. A second {{Term|BotW|Korok}} can be found by moving a metal ball attached to a chain into a nearby well north of the first {{Term|BotW|Korok}}.

==Nomenclature==
The name for {{Term|BotW|Deya Village Ruins}} is derived from {{Term|BotW|Deya Lake}}'s name, specifically its name in the Japanese version. In Japanese, {{Term|BotW|Deya Lake}}'s name is {{Romanize|アデヤ湖|Adeya Ko|Adeya Lake}}, which is a pun on the Japanese name for the {{Wp|Pomacanthus xanthometopon|blueface angelfish}}, {{Romanize|アデヤッコ|Adeyakko}}.
