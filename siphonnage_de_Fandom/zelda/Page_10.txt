==Features and Overview==
Page 10 is a shipyard with a series of railroad tracks, and is divided into an east and a west side. The west side contains high-tech equipment, and is a more upper-class area, whereas the east side is full of sailors. Both [[Buriki]] and [[Lion]] find temporary jobs here, which allows them to gain a passport so they can move to [[Page 11]] and continue their adventure.
