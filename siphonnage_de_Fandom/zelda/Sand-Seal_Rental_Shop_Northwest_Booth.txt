{{Term/Store|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sand-Seal Rental Shop<br/>Northwest Booth|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth}} is a {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} operated by {{Term|BotW|Kohm|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. It rents {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Creature|link}} native to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert Gateway|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Sand seals}} are animals native to the Gerudo Desert Gateway.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> Its sister shop, the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}} is operated by {{Term|BotW|Kohm}}'s daughter, {{Term|BotW|Frelly|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That look... Hmm. You must be the Hylian Frelly was talking about. My name is Kohm—I'm Frelly's mother.|Kohm|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|display=Northwest Booth}} is marked by a large statue of a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} with the word "rental" written beneath it in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Language)|link|display=Gerudo}}. It costs 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} or 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} if {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} does not have a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to traverse the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} very quickly.
