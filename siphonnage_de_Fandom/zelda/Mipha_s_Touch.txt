"{{Term/Store|Mipha's Touch||Series, BotW}}" is the tenth {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Mipha's Touch}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|Recovered Memory #10}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"Mipha's Touch" is unlocked as part of the "[[Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|Divine Beast Vah Ruta]]" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}. While speaking to [[Muzu]] and [[Sidon]] in front of [[Mipha]]'s statue, Muzu comments that {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} clearly has no memory of Mipha.<ref>{{Cite|The facts are clear. He remembers nothing. Even when he looks upon Princess Mipha's statue...|Muzu|BotW}}</ref> Sidon counters that Link was always in Mipha's heart.<ref>{{Cite|It is the truth, Muzu. Though you never knew it, he was ever in Mipha's heart.|Sidon|BotW}}</ref> After this, Link will look up at Mipha's statue, which triggers the memory.

Mipha and Link are sitting together on the trunk of [[Divine Beast Vah Ruta]], with Mipha using [[Mipha's Grace|her Grace]] to heal Link's arm. She tells Link that this reminds her of when they first met, remarking that he was a reckless child who would always get hurt.<ref>{{Cite|I was thinking... this reminds me of the time we first met. You were just a reckless child... always getting yourself hurt at every turn.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> Every time he did, however, Mipha would always be there to heal him, just as she is now.<ref>{{Cite|Every time, I would heal you. Just as I'm doing right now.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> Mipha goes on the state that she thought it was funny how Link looked grown-up faster than she did, due to being a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I thought it was funny how, being a Hylian, you looked grown-up so much faster than I did.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> She tells Link that she was always willing to heal his wounds, though, even back then.<ref>{{Cite|I was... I was always willing to heal your wounds. Even back then.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref>

If [[Calamity Ganon]] does return, Mipha wonders what they can really do.<ref>{{Cite|So if this Calamity Ganon does, in fact, return, what can we really do?|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> She's discouraged by the fact they don't know much about it.<ref>{{Cite|We just don't seem to know much about what we'll be up against.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> However, Mipha assures Link that if he is ever harmed, then she will be there to heal him, no matter the time or severity.<ref>{{Cite|But know this: that no matter how difficult this battle might get... if you—if anyone ever tries to do you harm... Then I will heal you. No matter when, or how bad the wound...|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> She then affirms that she will always be there to protect Link.<ref>{{Cite|I hope you know... that I will always protect you.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref>

Mipha expresses a desire for things to go back to the way they used to be, once Calamity Ganon has been defeated.<ref>{{Cite|Once this whole thing is over... maybe things can go back to how they used to be when we were young.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> The memory ends with Mipha suggesting the two of them spend some time together.<ref>{{Cite|You know... Perhaps we could spend some time together.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref>
