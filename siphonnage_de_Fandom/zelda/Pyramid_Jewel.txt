The '''Pyramid Jewel''' is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Pyramid Jewel is found at the end of an underwater cavern outside the [[Dancing Dragon Dungeon]]. In a [[Linked Game]], the original location of the Pyramid Jewel is filled with 30 [[Rupee]]s, and it is instead found on a ledge in [[Sunken Falls]] after being tossed there by [[Vire]]<ref>{{Cite|Nar har har! I am Vire, Onox's loyal henchman! Onox has ordered me to stop you! So, I've moved the Pyramid Stone high onto this cliff! Get it if you can! By the way, these falls flow from Sunken City. If you can't swim, you can't get down them! Gar har har!|Vire|OoS}}</ref>, who appears after [[Link]] opens a nearby [[Treasure Chest]] that in an unliked game holds a [[Gasha Seed]]. It is used in conjunction with the other three Jewels to open the door leading to [[Tarm Ruins]].
