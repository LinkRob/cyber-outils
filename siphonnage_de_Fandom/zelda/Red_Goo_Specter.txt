==Characteristics==
{{Plural|LANS|Red Goo Specter}} are only found in the {{Term|LANS|Color Dungeon|link}}. They hide in red colored tiles and pop up to attack {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} when he gets near them.<ref name="E 170"/> There are only four {{Plural|LANS|Red Goo Specter}} in the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon|link}}; one of them is in the room above the entrance, two are in the room in the bottom right corner of the {{Term|LANS|Dungeon}}, and one is in the room below the {{Term|LANS|Giant Buzz Blob|link}}
