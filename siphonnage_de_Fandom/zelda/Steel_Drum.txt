{{Term/Store|Steel Drum|Steel Drums|Series, PH|plural}} are objects in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|There are many {{Color|PH Red|steel drums}} in the storage locker on the bottom floor!|Cubus Sisters|PH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|PH|Steel Drum}} are found in the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship|link}} where they appear in groups throughout the {{Term|PH|Dungeon|link}}. Like {{Plural|PH|Barrel|link}}, they can be picked up and destroyed to reveal {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}} or other collectible items, though occasionally a {{Term|PH|Rat|link}} or a {{Term|PH|Rope|link}} will be hiding beneath them.

The bottom floor of the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} contains a {{Term|PH|Steel Drum}} storage room, wherein a {{Term|PH|Floor Switch|link}} is hidden among the {{Plural|PH|Steel Drum|display=Drums}}. {{Term|PH|Link|link}} must enter this room from the rear, as the front entrance is actually a trap floor. In the northwest corner of the same floor, the final {{Term|PH|Cubus Sisters|link|display=Cubus Sister}} can be found hiding beneath a {{Term|PH|Steel Drum}}.
