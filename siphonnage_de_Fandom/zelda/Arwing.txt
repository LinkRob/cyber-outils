==Characteristics==

=== {{OoT|-}} ===
[[File:OoT Arwing.png|thumb|273x273px|The Arwing swooping and shooting at Link with its Twin Lasers in [[Kokiri Forest]]]]
In {{OoT|-}}, an unused enemy Arwing can be found and triggered by modifying the game's data, either by hacking or using a cheat device. When [[Link]] enters an area with an Arwing, a small cutscene will play showing the ship appearing, but does not change the music. The Arwing will then proceed to fly around the area and shoots at the hero with its twin lasers which will cause just a quarter of a [[Heart]] in damage. The laser beams can be blocked with the use of a [[shield]]. To hit it, Link must use the [[Boomerang]], [[Slingshot|Fairy Slingshot]], [[Deku Nut]]s, [[Hookshot]], or any [[Arrows]] as it swoops down toward him. The Arwing itself is closely based on its appearance in the 1997 [[Nintendo 64]] title ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_64|Star Fox 64]]'' and is miniature in size compared to the actual aircraft used by Fox McCloud and his teammates. When defeated, it will fall and crash down to the ground with a fiery explosion, just like in its source material.

Although technically a [[Cameos in The Legend of Zelda|cameo]] from another [[Nintendo]] franchise, the Arwing was actually a simple placeholder, used by the game developers to test the flight patterns of [[Volvagia]], the [[Boss]] of the [[Fire Temple]], according to [[Satoru Takizawa]].<ref name="Volvagia"/> It was left in the coding of ports of the original game, but is absent from {{OoT3D}}.

== In the ''Star Fox'' series ==
[[File:Arwing Star Fox 64.webp|thumb|254x254px|The Arwing as seen in ''Star Fox 64'', on the game's first level]]
In its original source material, the Arwing is the trademark starfighter used by the [[starfox:Star_Fox_(team)|Star Fox team]] lead by [[starfox:Fox_McCloud|Fox McCloud]], the main protagonist, in the ''Star Fox'' franchise, making its first appearance in the then-revolutionary 1993 [[Super Nintendo Entertainment System|Super NES]] title ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_(game)|Star Fox]]''. An ultra-high performance combat spacecraft developed by Space Dynamics Co., Ltd., the Arwing is equipped with the G-Diffuser system, an anti-gravity device which allows the pilot to accelerate or decelerate instantaneously and perform a wide variety of high performance combat maneuvers (shared with the F-Zero Machines in the [[fzero:F-Zero_(series)|''F-Zero'' series]]). By executing an aileron or barrel roll, the Arwing can also generate an electromagnetic shield which will deflect most optically based weapons, such as laser beam cannons. The Arwings specifically used by the Star Fox team are unlike any production versions as they have been extensively modified with improved armor and advanced weapon systems, such as Hyper Lasers, Homing Bolts and Smart Bombs. Despite numerous revisions and redesigns over the games, every model of Arwings have maintained the balance of speed, power and performance that they are credited for. The Arwing starfighter is the primary battle vehicle of the Star Fox team to combat [[starfox:Andross|Andross]], the main antagonist, or other forces, and is one of the symbols of their team.

==Trivia==
* In Japan, the Arwing is known as '''Arwin'''.
* The Arwing was inspired by the [https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/X-wing_starfighter X-Wing starfighter] of the ''[https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Wars Star Wars]'' franchise.
* The Arwing's lasers use the same sound effect as [[Phantom Ganon]]'s energy ball being [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_PhantomGanon_Ball_Reflect.wav reflected]. When the Arwing is shot down, the sound of [[King Dodongo]] [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_KingDodongo_Inhale.wav inhaling] is heard until it crashes.
* In ''Star Fox 64'', the Arwing is one of three vehicles used to combat Andross' forces. The other two are the [[starfox:Landmaster|Landmaster]] and [[starfox:Blue-Marine|Blue-Marine]]. Each of them are invented by [[starfox:Slippy_Toad|Slippy Toad]], the team's mechanic. It also has an equivalent used by the Star Fox team's rival, [[starfox:Star_Wolf|Star Wolf]], called the [[starfox:Wolfen|Wolfen]].
* The Arwing is featured in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]], where Link and other ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|Zelda]]'' characters can encounter them alongside Fox McCloud and [[starfox:Falco_Lombardi|Falco Lombardi]]. Unlike in {{OoT|-}}, however, they cannot be attacked and destroyed, as the starfighters serve as stage hazards or platforms.
* Although not directly related to the Arwing itself, one ''Star Fox'' title released on the [[Nintendo GameCube]] in 2002 by Rareware, ''[[starfox:Star_Fox_Adventures|Star Fox Adventures]]'', shares similar gameplay as {{OoT|-}} and {{TWW}}.
