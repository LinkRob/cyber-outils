{{Term/Store|Lava|Lava|Series}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Cross the Lava|Vision|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW |publisher= Prima |page= 12}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|game=TMC |publisher=Nintendo |page=34}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= ALBW |publisher= Prima |page= 17}}</ref>{{Name Ref|OoT, OoT3D, TWWHD, TP, TPHD, ST, BotW}}

==Location and uses==
Lava is molten rock usually appearing in the form of thick, fiery liquid that ranges from red to orange to yellow. In most {{TLoZ|-}} games, touching Lava causes [[Link]] to lose health if he falls in, and in a few 3D installments of {{TLoZ|-}} series, touching Lava forces him to restart the current room he is in.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
Lava appears in some dungeons in {{TLoZ}}, being represented by red tiles. Like [[Water (Terrain)|Water]], Lava cannot be walked on, making it harmless, although the [[Stepladder]] can be used to walk over Water and Lava.

==={{TAoL|-}}===
Lava appears in some caves and palaces in {{TAoL}}. Falling into lava will automatically cause [[Link]] to lose a [[life]], regardless of his current health.

==={{ALttP|-}}===
Lava appears only as part of the scenery in a room of [[Turtle Rock]] in {{ALttP}}.

==={{LA|-}}===
Lava appears only in [[Turtle Rock]], where the mini-boss, [[Hydrosoar]], and the boss, [[Hot Head]], are each inside their own room with a large pool of Lava. Similarly to {{ALttP|-}}, Link cannot walk into the Lava, although it hinders his ability to freely move around.

==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:OoT Lava 2.png|thumb|Lava in {{OoT|-}}]]
{{OoT}} is the first game where Link can walk on Lava, although this constantly damages him in the process. Link can take significantly less damage from Lava if he is wearing the [[Goron Tunic]].

Lava appears throughout a few fire-themed and volcanic areas. It first appears in [[Dodongo's Cavern]], followed by [[Death Mountain Crater]], the [[Fire Temple]], and a certain room within [[Ganon's Castle]]. There is Lava in the [[Boss Room]]s of [[King Dodongo]] and [[Volvagia]] respectively, and when the boss is defeated, the Lava cools and hardens.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Lava appears in [[Snowhead Temple]] and [[Stone Tower Temple]], and function similarly as in {{OoT|-}}. Whether Link can walk across Lava and whether he takes damage from doing so depends on his current form. In his regular form, Link can walk on lava, but this inflicts constant damage on him in the process. [[Deku Link]] and [[Zora Link]] will restart a room if they fall into lava, whereas [[Goron Link]] is completely immune to lava.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, [[Subrosia]] has a large abundance of Lava, and the location's inhabitants, the [[Subrosian]]s, are unaffected by the Lava. The [[Sword & Shield Maze]], located in Subrosia, also has a lot of Lava. If [[Link]] falls into Lava, he takes damage.

There is a Lava-based item named [[Lava Soup]], which the [[Subrosian Chef]] gives to Link at one point.

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{stub|OoA}}
In {{OoA}}, a large abundance of Lava appears in [[Symmetry City]], as well as in the [[Skull Dungeon]].

A different Lava-based item appears, [[Lava Juice]], which can be obtained through the [[Goron Shooting Gallery]] mini-game.

==={{FS|-}}===
In {{FS}}, there is Lava in [[Death Mountain (Four Swords)|Death Mountain]], and if one of the [[Links]] touches it, he loses health and respawns outside of it.

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, Lava appears in a few dungeons, including [[Dragon Roost Cavern]], [[Fire Mountain]], and in [[Ganon's Tower]].

In most areas, if Link falls into lava, he bounces upward from burn damage and respawns at the start of the room. An exception to this is during the boss fight again [[Gohma]], where Link instead bounces out of the lava upon taking damage. Link can create temporary platforms in the Lava by throwing [[Water Pot]]s or shooting [[Ice Arrow]]s into it, and he sometimes has to do so in order to progress.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{stub|FSA}}
Lava appears in the [[Tower of Flames]] in {{FSA}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
Lava appears in the [[Cave of Flames]] in {{TMC}}. It will damage [[Link]] if he falls into it. Link can traverse Lava using [[Updraft]] as well as unstable rocky platforms that must occasionally be flipped over with the [[Cane of Pacci]]. Lava is featured prominently in the boss fight against [[Gleerok]].

==={{TP|-}}===
Lava appears in the [[Goron Mines]] in {{TP}}. If Link falls into Lava, he will lose two hearts simultaneously before returning to the start of the room. However, if Link is wearing the [[Zora Armor]], this damage is multiplied by 10, making Lava an instant-death hazard for Link.

==={{PH|-}}===
Lava appears in the [[Temple of Fire]] in {{PH}}. If Link falls into Lava, he suffers damage and respawns outside of it, similarly to {{FS}}.

==={{ST|-}}===
Lava appears in the [[Fire Temple]] and on several floors of the [[Tower of Spirits]]. Lava has the same damaging effects on Link as before.

==={{SS|-}}===
Lava appears in the [[Eldin Volcano]] in {{SS}}. If Link touches the lava, he catches on fire and respawns outside of the Lava. However, Link can create temporary platforms on the Lava by throwing [[Water Fruit]]s on it, similarly to the [[Water Pot]]s in {{TWW|-}}. If Link makes contact with Lava while having the [[Wooden Shield]] equipped, it burns up.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Lava appears in [[Turtle Rock]] and [[Lorule Castle]] in {{ALBW}}. Similarly to many other two-dimensional games in {{TLoZ|Series}}, if Link falls into a pool of lava, he takes damage and respawns outside of the Lava. Link can now freeze Lava using the [[Ice Rod]], including on Lava Geysers in certain areas.

==={{TFH|-}}===
Like in previous game, if Link falls into Lava, he takes damage and respawns outside of the Lava. There are two ways for him to safely traverse lava: by riding a [[Totem Armos]] or by wearing the [[Goron Garb]], which allows him to swim in Lava harmlessly.

==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Stub|BotW}}
Lava is an obstacle in the [[Eldin (Region)|Eldin]] region of [[Hyrule]]. Just as before, touching Lava inflicts damage upon Link, and most enemies are defeated merely from touching Lava.
