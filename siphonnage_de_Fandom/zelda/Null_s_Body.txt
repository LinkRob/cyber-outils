'''Null's Body''' is the eighth and final [[Dungeon]] of {{EoW}}.

==Entrance to the Dungeon==
Null's Body can only be accessed at the end of the "Rescuing the Hero Link" quest line, which itself can only be unlocked once the first seven dungeons are all completed.

After making her way through the Stilled Ancient Ruins and rescuing [[Link]] from [[Tekom]], Zelda and Link follow the [[Triforce|Prime Energy]] into a dark, cloudy sphere at the center of the Still World.

==Themes and Navigation==
As its title suggests, Null's Body takes place within the insides of [[Null]], a primordial being that has been trying to destroy Hyrule through a series of rifts, all so it can break free from its prison and consume all of existence. Null has been consuming [[Tri]]'s friends; as such, the purple walls of Null's Body are lined with digested Tris in its cells.

To get to Null's core, Zelda and Link must work together to solve puzzles and battle the various echoed enemies that Null summons to impede their progress. Trials faced in Null's body include: 

* A gauntlet of sorts where Link and Zelda must battle dark world echoes of various monsters, triggered when they touch Zelda ending with a Lynel Echo that could 1 shot all of Zelda's echoes including her own Lynels.
* Link and Zelda getting separated leading to a room where Zelda has to figure out how to trigger a switch which allows both Link and herself get to the next area,
* A room where Link and Zelda both separated by a soft wall must face 3 different assorted enemies each, Both can offer support to the other where the CPU Link will mostly use arrows to help Zelda clear off her foes if he has finished and Zelda can summon echoes bypassing the soft wall to help Link.
* Zelda must then get past a dungeon, Link's path isn't shown.
* A final room where Zelda must use her Tri Rod alongside it's abilities and echoes to get Link to where he can trigger the next area.

Eventually, Zelda and Link will reach a straight path leading into a dark abyss: Null's core. Link nods at the princess, and the two jump in to face Null together. It should be noted Zelda has no more access to Swordfighter Mode in this area increasing her and the player's dependance on Link for safety.
