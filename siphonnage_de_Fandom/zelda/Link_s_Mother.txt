{{Term/Store|Link's Mother||Series}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
Link's Mother is an unnamed woman who gave birth to and cared for [[Link]] before the events of {{OoT}}. She is credited with protecting and entrusting the [[Hero of Time]] as a baby with the [[Great Deku Tree]] in the [[Kokiri Forest]] during the time of the [[Hyrulean Civil War]].<ref>{{Cite|Some time ago, before the King of Hyrule unified this country, there was a fierce war in our world. One day, to escape from the fires of the war, a Hylian mother and her baby boy entered this forbidden forest.|Deku Tree Sprout|OoT}}</ref> Link, unfortunately, knows nothing of his parents, because his mother died shortly after giving him up to the Great Deku Tree, due to her being grave injuries.<ref>{{Cite|The mother was gravely injured... Her only choice was to entrust the child to the Deku Tree, the guardian spirit of the forest.|Deku Tree Sprout|OoT}}</ref> In presiding over the [[Kokiri]] race, The Great Deku Tree chose to raise Link as a Kokiri, despite his [[Hylian]] blood.<ref>{{Cite|After the mother passed away, the baby was raised as a Kokiri.|Deku Tree Sprout|OoT}}</ref> Because of this, Link awakens in the beginning of the game wearing Kokiri garb as his [[Green Clothes|green tunic]], and without the presence of a [[fairy]], a facet of Kokiri society that is prevalent in all of its members.<ref>{{Cite|You must be wondering why only you have grown up! Well, as you might have already guessed, you are not a Kokiri! You are actually a Hylian!|Deku Tree Sprout|OoT}}</ref>

A mother figure is not mentioned in any other canonical game since {{OoT|-}}, where the only mention of her existence is revealed to Link after he completes the {{Term|OoT3D|Forest Temple|link}}, by the freshly-grown [[Deku Tree Sprout]].

==={{ALttP|-}} (Ishinomori)===
In the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}, it is revealed that Link's parents were both [[Knights of Hyrule]]; however, they somehow stumbled into the [[Dark World]], leading Link to be adopted by [[Link's Uncle|his uncle]] instead.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Your parents were Knights of Hyrule... But they stumbled into the Dark World... You will never be able to enter the Dark World and find your parents! [...] They dwell now in the Dark World... After they disappeared, your uncle adopted you and shielded you from the intrigues of the Royal Family...|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=14}}</ref> When Link's Uncle is defeated by [[Agahnim]], he mentions that if the young hero manages to rescue [[Princess Zelda]] and the people in the Dark World, he might be able to meet his parents.

Link's parents are seen for the first time in the comic as Link is walking through [[Death Mountain]], where he activates one of the Dark World [[Portal (Object)|Portal]]s and sees a vision of his mother and father. Although Link tries to reach out for his mother's hand, his hand turns into a claw when it passes the portal's boundary.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link... Link... We are here with our brother...in the world of darkness....|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=6}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Uncle?! Are you my '''father''' and '''mother'''?! It '''can't''' be!!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=7}}</ref> Link's parents are not seen again until the final chapter after Link has defeated [[Ganon]] and peace returns to Hyrule. At [[Hyrule Castle]], Link sees a vision of his father, mother and uncle, who congratulate him on his difficult victory and tell him to never forget the efforts of the many generations of knights and Wise Men that preceded him.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link... You have done well, Link... In the Imprisoning War of years past, great armies risked all to seal Ganon in the Dark World. You, however, accomplished a much more lonely and difficult victory... It was not only by your own power, but also that of the many generations of knights and Wise Men who preceded you. Let the memories of their efforts humble you. Never forget!|book= ALttP (Ishinomori)|publisher= |page=11, 12}}</ref>

==={{TLoZ|-}} (Mishouzaki)===
{{Main|Princess Zelda}}
In {{TLoZ|Mishouzaki}} by Yuu Mishouzaki, Link's mother is [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda XVI]]. Zelda XVI had an affair with the elf warrior [[Rune (Mishouzaki)|Rune]] and gave birth to Link as a result. Link was raised by [[Impa]] without ever knowing his true heritage. Zelda XVI later gave birth to Link's half-sister Princess Zelda XVII with the [[King of Hyrule]]. After passing on the [[Triforce of Wisdom]] to Zelda XVII, Zelda XVI is attacked and murdered by [[Ganon]].

==={{ALttP|-}} (Himekawa)===
In the {{ALttP|Himekawa}}, it is revealed that Ganondorf subjected Link's parents to a period of cursing and smiting in the [[Dark Realm]]. Although Link finds traces of his mother throughout his journey, he never truly finds her.{{Fact}}

==={{OoT|-}} (Himekawa)===
The {{OoT|Himekawa}} has her appear in the beginning chapter for the manga. Her role was essentially the same as what was described in the main game, although it adds that she specifically fled a burning castle (heavily implied to be Hyrule Castle) during a thunderstorm during the war via horseback.
