The {{Term/Store|Jee Noh Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Jee Noh Shrine}}<br/>On the Move}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''On the Move''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Jee Noh Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Pass|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. It is unguarded and sits in plain sight. As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} enters, he will hear the voice of {{Term|BotW|Jee Noh|link}}, the Shrine's {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, greet him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Jee Noh. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Jee Noh|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Lacking Sources|BotW}}
The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}} consists of three different main rooms. The first room has a {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} moving across a set of conveyor belts. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must freeze the orb with {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} and shoot it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} to make it roll into the slot it belongs in. This opens the door to the next room.

In the second room, there is a similar puzzle as the first room, except the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} is accompanied by two {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Scout I|link}} on the conveyor belt. They will fire short lasers at {{Term|BotW|Link}} and must either be defeated with a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} or avoided as he once again puts the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into {{Term|BotW|Stasis}} and shoots it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow}}, opening yet another door.

In the third room, there are two sets of conveyor belts. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must carry an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} across one of them, which is moving against the direction that he needs to go and slows {{Term|BotW|Link}} down. This makes it more difficult for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to avoid the three tripwire lasers emitting from nearby statues. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must wait for {{Plural|BotW|Block|link}} to block the lasers before crossing the first and third lasers and must turn off the second laser with {{Term|BotW|Stasis}}. After making it across safely, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can then simply place the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} into the slot to open the door to the end of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}. There is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can jump to on the conveyor belt across from him.

In the final room of the {{Term|BotW|Jee Noh Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Jee Noh}} is found waiting for the hero and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Jee Noh|BotW}}</ref> He will then wish Link well and fade away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Jee Noh|BotW}}</ref>
