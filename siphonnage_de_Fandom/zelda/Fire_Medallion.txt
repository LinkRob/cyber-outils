The {{Term/Store|Fire Medallion||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an {{Term|OoT3D|Item|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 123}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|OoT3D}}
The {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Medallion}} is the {{Term|OoT3D|Sage Medallion|link}} that {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} obtains after defeating {{Term|OoT3D|Volvagia|link}} in the {{Term|OoT3D|Fire Temple|link}} and freeing all of the trapped {{Plural|OoT3D|Goron|link}} within.<ref>{{Cite|You received the {{Color|OoT3D Red|Fire Medallion}}! Darunia awakens as a Sage and adds his power to yours!|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> It is given to him by the newly awakened {{Term|OoT3D|Sage|link}}, {{Term|OoT3D|Darunia|link}}. According to Darunia, it contains the "power of the fire spirits" and that {{Term|OoT3D|Darunia}} considers it a token of his friendship with {{Term|OoT3D|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Brother, take this! This is a {{Color|OoT3D Red|Medallion}} that contains the power of the fire spirits—and my friendship.|Darunia|OoT3D}}</ref>
