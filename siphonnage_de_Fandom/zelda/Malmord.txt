For I am Malmord, who controls future and past! She resembles a {{wp|Death (personification)|Grim Reaper}} carrying a large scythe. She claims to have control over destiny and [[Princess Zelda]]'s fate.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda, I am your destiny! I weep because of your quest. I measure and I cut what you lose. I deem what is fate and what is best, and I leave you no power to choose! Ha!|Malmord|ZA}}</ref><ref name="Home"/>

==Characteristics==
Malmord attacks by melting into the shadows and moving around the boss room. She will briefly reappear for a few seconds before disappearing into the shadows again, and can only be attacked during this brief moment that she is solid. As Malmord has no real weaknesses, the [[Items in Zelda's Adventure#Wand|Wand]] is an option in fighting her.

Malmord is fought a second time in the [[Vision Henge]] when [[Ganon]] summons the Shrine Keepers to fight Zelda on her way to the final showdown. Unlike the other Shrine Keepers in the Vision Henge, Malmord is fought much differently than in the original fight. She will stand still in the center the room, and transform into a swarm of [[Dragonfly|Dragonflies]] the moment she is attacked. These Dragonflies must all be defeated to proceed. They are notably the only enemies inside the henge that will drop [[Heart]]s.
