{{Term/Store|Mighty Thunder of the Gerudo||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 26 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mighty Thunder of the Gerudo|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Mighty Thunder of the Gerudo}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}} while defending an Allied Stronghold. The first {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin|link}} and has a {{Term|HWAoC|Moblin|link}} as its boss. The path between that {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} and the Allied Stronghold is also filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Bokoblin}}. Shortly after the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}} spawn nearby the Allied Stronghold, and eventually two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} spawn in the same spot, both groups attempting to capture it. At around the same time as the {{Plural|HWAoC|Moblin}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos|link}} spawn in the same area.

The second {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}} and has two {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Moblin|link}} as bosses. There are {{Plural|HWAoC|Blue Bokoblin}} just outside the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}'s exit, and the path to the third {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Lizalfos}}, along with two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}.

The third {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} and has two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} as bosses. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when all three of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using only {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} along with any other {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
