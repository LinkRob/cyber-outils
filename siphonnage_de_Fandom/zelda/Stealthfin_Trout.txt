==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Stealthfin Trout}}
Stealthfin Trout can be found in bodies of water in both the [[Great Hyrule Forest]] and [[Eldin Mountains]], though they're plentiful within [[Lake Mekar]]. They're easy to find at night due to the fact that they glow in the dark.<ref name="Inventory"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} eats a Stealthfin Trout, he will regain one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They can also be burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} to become [[Roasted Trout]] or chilled by [[Ice]] to become [[Frozen Trout]]. [[Cooking]] with Stealthfin Trout results in a meal that will temporarily increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Stealthfin Trout, as well as other fish, can be used in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make a [[Fish Skewer]], a [[Fish and Mushroom Skewer]], and [[Salt-Grilled Fish]] in a [[Cooking Pot]]. Stealthfin Trout are also counted among the seafood that can make [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].

In order to upgrade the [[Stealth Set]] to its final tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, Link needs to gather ten Stealthfin Trout and five {{Plural|Series|Silent Princess|link}} for each piece.
