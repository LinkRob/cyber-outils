==Biography==
{{Manual|LA|Grandpa Ulrira|The old man is a walking encyclopedia of Koholint Island. He knows everything about the island! However, he is not very talkative and it can be difficult to get information out of him. His wife is the loud old woman who loves sweeping the front of her house.}}
{{Term|LADX|Grandpa Ulrira}} is a shy old man who lives in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It seems that old man Ulrira is a shy guy, in person...|N/A|LANS}}</ref> If {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} talks to him face to face in his house, he will tell {{Term|LADX|Link}} to speak to him through a telephone outside.<ref>{{Cite| Er...Uh...Hmm... How to say... Please call... Outside...|Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref> Wherever {{Term|LADX|Link}} calls {{Term|LADX|Grandpa Ulrira}} through a {{Term|LADX|Telephone Booth|link}}, he gives him helpful hints on how his current challenge. He is married to {{Term|LANS|Grandma Yahoo|link}},<ref name="E 233"/> a loud, cheerful old woman who spends her time sweeping {{Term|LADX|Koholint Island|link}} with her broom.

{{Term|LADX|Grandpa Ulrira}} is said to know everything about {{Term|LADX|Koholint Island}},<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= |game= LA|page=29}}</ref> and often tells {{Term|LADX|Link}} exactly what he needs to do to progress in his quest.<ref>{{Cite|'BRRING! BRRING! Hello! It's me, Ulrira! Ask me anything about the island! If you get lost, give me a call! Bye! CLICK!' |Grandpa Ulrira|LADX}}</ref>

In {{LA|DX|-}}, {{Term|LADX|Link}} can take a peek through the window of {{Term|LADX|Old Man Ulrira's House|link}}. If he does, the {{Term|LADX|Photographer|link}} appears and snaps a {{Term|LADX|Photo|link}} of {{Term|LADX|Grandpa Ulrira}} through the window, revealing his talkative nature.
