The {{Term/Store|Scrap Shop||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location featured in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite|Scrap Shop|Map|SSHD}}</ref> It is one of the many shops in the {{Term|SS|Skyloft Bazaar|link}} and is run by [[Gondo]]. 

==Overview==
[[Link]] can [[Upgrading|Upgrade]] his items and [[Shield]]s in the Scrap Shop by providing certain [[Treasure]]s to give them added advantages, like extra strength or durability.<ref>{{Cite|I can use the stuff you find to upgrade your gear and make it even stronger!|Gondo|SS}}</ref> All upgrades are permanent and take a few seconds to complete.<ref>{{Cite|All right. You want to upgrade this? There's no going back, y'hear?|Gondo|SS}}</ref> As Link progresses, he is able to upgrade more of his items as he collects more Treasures.<ref>{{Cite|You got it! These are all the items you have that I can upgrade right now.|Gondo|SS}}</ref> Each item also costs a certain amount of [[Rupee]]s to upgrade.
