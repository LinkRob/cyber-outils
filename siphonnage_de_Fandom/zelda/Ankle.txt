==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Ankle}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Ankle}} resides at the top of {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Tower|link}} in {{TWW}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Ankle}} considers his twin brother [[Knuckle]] to be lazy and good-for-nothing, and was taught how to decipher [[Map]]s by his older brother, [[Tingle]].<ref name="TWW Guide 23"/> He helps [[David Jr.]] to spin {{Term|TWWHD|Tingle Tower}}. He initially mistakes [[Link]] for Tingle, saying that he was very worried about him.<ref>{{Cite|Big Brother! I was worried about you! Huh?!? You are not my brother! My brother said he was going to Windfall Island, but it has been so long...and still he does not come back!|Ankle|TWW}}</ref> The only time he feels he can truly be himself is when he's tending to the flower garden on [[Tingle Island]]. His favorite proverb is <q>''One may as well hang for a stolen sheep as for a stolen lamb.''</q><ref>{{Cite|Birthplace: Unknown<br/>Favorite Proverb: "One may as well hang for a stolen sheep as for a stolen lamb."<br/>Since being taught how to decipher maps by his older brother Tingle, Ankle has been supporting his good-for-nothing twin brother Knuckle.<br/>The only time he feels he can truly be himself is when he's tending to the Tingle Island flower garden.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> Unlike Tingle, he wears pink and the mole on his face is above his mouth rather than below it. In addition, the rosy patches on his cheeks are completely filled in.
