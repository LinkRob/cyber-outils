The '''Jade Amulet''' is a Spell in {{ZA}}.

==Overview==
The Jade Amulet fires a spread of green sparkles, which spread out as they travel. It costs 24 [[Rupee]]s to use.

The Jade Amulet is sold by [[Madame Kriggle]] in her magic shop in [[Great Wimbich]] for 100 Rupees.
