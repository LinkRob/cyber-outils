==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} found in the overworld of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. They are used as part of {{Plural|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} to reveal {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} that have been buried beneath the ground. This is done by performing certain actions on top of the {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}}. Certain {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}} also bear a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}, into which an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} must be inserted.
