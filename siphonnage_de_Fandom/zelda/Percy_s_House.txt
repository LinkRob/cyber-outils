{{Term/Store|Percy's House||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Percy's House|Sign|TMC}}</ref> As its name suggests, it is home to {{Term|TMC|Percy|link}} the poet, located in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}. 

At first, the way to {{Term|TMC|Percy's House}} is blocked by a fallen tree; however, when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} [[Kinstone Fusion|fuses]] {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Percy}}, the tree will restore itself, opening the path.<ref>{{Cite|But here I am, returning after a long absence only to find the road blocked. It wouldn't feel like such a waste if I hadn't lost half of my Kinstone!|Percy|TMC}}</ref> Unfortunately, {{Term|TMC|Percy}} finds out that while he was away on a long journey, his house is being occupied by a stranger, and the poet is too shy to talk to her.<ref>{{Cite|When I finally returned to my home, sweet home, a stranger was there! I'm an extremely shy person, and I just can't talk to someone I don't know!|Percy|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} walks inside, he will find a {{Term|TMC|Monster Lady|link|display=strange woman}} inside who tells him to not turn on the lights since the darkness warms her soul.<ref>{{Cite|A dark room settles my soul...I say. Do not turn on the lights...I say.|Monster Lady|TMC}}</ref> Turning on both torches with the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}} reveals the woman to be a {{Term|TMC|Moblin|link}}, who tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} to not tell anyone and gives him 50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, dear. You found me out! Don't tell anyone!|Moblin|TMC}}</ref> 

With the {{Term|TMC|Moblin}} gone, {{Term|TMC|Percy}} thanks {{Term|TMC|Link}} for driving the stranger away.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! Link! Thanks to you, I can finally go back in my house. It's been totally rearranged, but I think it's a good look!|Percy|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Link}} exits the poet's house and returns, {{Term|TMC|Percy}} will reward him with 100 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}.

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TMC|Moblin}} inside {{Term|TMC|Percy}}'s house is a direct reference to the original {{TLoZ}}, in which {{Term|TLoZ|Link}} could find {{Plural|TLoZ|Moblin}} hidden in secret caves with two flames at their side, often rewarding him with {{Plural|TLoZ|Rupee}} and uttering the famous phrase: "IT'S A SECRET TO EVERYBODY."
