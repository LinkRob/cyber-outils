{{Term/Store|Nabooru Town||Series, TAoL}} is a location in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 108 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> It is located in [[Eastern Hyrule]]. Since Nabooru is across the sea, [[Link]] must obtain the [[Raft]] from the [[Island Palace]] in order to reach this town.

==Features and Overview==
Nabooru can be found in the very center of [[Eastern Hyrule]]. To receive a spell from the [[Old Man]] living here, Link takes water from a nearby fountain and gives it to a woman who is thirsty.<ref>{{Cite|I AM THIRSTY.|Woman|TAoL}}</ref> The woman thanks the young hero by letting him inside her house and allowing him to talk to the Old Man at the end of the basement, who teaches Link the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Fire|Fire]] magic as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|YOU HAVE WATER. COME TO MY HOUSE.|Woman|TAoL}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|THIS MAGIC WILL MAKE YOUR SWORD SHOOT FIRE|Old Man|TAoL}}</ref>

In the house next to the fountain, Link can awake and talk to a friendly sleeping red [[Ache]] (or potentially an [[Acheman]]), who will offer him advice if woken up.<ref>{{Cite|YOU ARE PERSISTENT FIND HEART OVER OCEAN|Ache|TAoL}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*This town is said to have been named after [[Nabooru]], the [[Gerudo]] [[Sage]] of Spirit from {{OoT}}.
