The {{Term/Store|Swiftblade Possession Technique||Series, TMC}} is an ability used by {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is the {{Color|TMC Blue|Swiftblade Possession technique}} of training!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Swiftblade|link}} uses the {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade Possession Technique}} to teach {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} various skills by taking control of his body and showing him how the move is performed.<ref>{{Cite|But one must FEEL the technique, not just hear about it! That's why I will now possess your body so as to demonstrate the technique!|Swiftblade|TMC}}</ref> After this, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must repeat the action he was shown in order to earn the {{Term|TMC|Tiger Scroll|link}} for the ability.

{{Term|TMC|Swiftblade}} will use this {{Term|TMC|Swiftblade Possession Technique|display=Technique}} to teach {{Term|TMC|Link}} four abilities: the {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Rock Breaker|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Dash Attack|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Down Thrust|link}}.

A similar technique is used by his brothers to teach Link their own skills.
