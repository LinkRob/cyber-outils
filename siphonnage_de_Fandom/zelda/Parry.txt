== Overview ==
=== {{TP|-}} ===
The {{Term|TPHD|Shield Attack}} is the second {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill|link}} that {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} can learn. This {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} lets {{Term|TPHD|Link}} bash the enemy with his {{Term|TPHD|Shield|link}} to open their defenses. The {{Term|TPHD|Shield Attack}} can be learned by first finding the {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone|link}} located on the trail leading to {{Term|TPHD|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Search for the statues that howl with the {{Color|TPHD Red|sound of the wind}}. Seek the sound that calls the spirit of the beast to awaken me again.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref> Howling at the {{Term|TPHD|Howling Stone}} awakens the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf|link}}, who joins in howling with {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}}. After howling with the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}}, the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} instructs {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to find him while in human form and marks where he will wait on {{Term|TPHD|Link}}'s {{Term|TPHD|Map|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let teachings of old pass to you... Take sword in hand and find me...|White Wolf|TPHD}}</ref> Once {{Term|TPHD|Link}} returns to human form, he can find the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} waiting at the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Spring|link}}. Approaching the {{Term|TPHD|White Wolf}} can return {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to the clouded area where he can find the {{Term|TPHD|Hero's Spirit}} and learn the {{Term|TPHD|Shield Attack}}.

To perform the {{Term|TPHD|Shield Attack}} on the GameCube version, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must get close enough to the enemy while targeting it and then press the R-Button, which will cause the enemy to lower its defense and thus allow {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to attack the foe with another skill, such as the {{Term|TPHD|Helm Splitter|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What ails you? You cannot shield-attack with [R] unless you lock on with [L] first! Get closer to your foe and shield-attack with [R]!|Hero's Spirit|TP}}</ref><ref name="Skill Info"/> In the Wii version of {{TP|-}}, this {{Term|TPHD|Hidden Skill}} can be executed by thrusting the [[Nunchuk]] forward.<ref name="Shield">{{Cite|While [Z]-targeting your enemy, thrust the Nunchuk forward to shove with your shield. You can also reflect your foe's projectiles!|N/A|TP}}</ref>

Other than bringing the enemy's defenses down, the {{Term|TPHD|Shield Attack}} can also be used to deflect certain enemy projectiles.<ref name="Shield"/><ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|TPHD Red|shield attack}} I just taught you can also be used to {{Color|TPHD Red|repel}} an enemy's projectile attacks.|Hero's Spirit|TPHD}}</ref>

=== {{SS|-}} ===
{{Term|SS|Link|link}} can learn the {{Term|SS|Shield Bash}} ability after first acquiring a {{Term|SS|Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... Get a {{Color|SS Red|shield}} and come back here! Then I'll be able to teach you the {{Color|SS Red|secrets of defense}}!...|Eagus|SS}}</ref> The {{Term|SS|Eagus|display=Knight Commander}}, {{Term|SS|Eagus|link}} teaches {{Term|SS|Link}} this ability at the {{Term|SS|Sparring Hall|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're not sure how to use a shield, {{Color|SS Blue|Knight Commander Eagus}} at the Sparring Hall will happily teach you.|Owlan|SS}}</ref> To perform the {{Term|SS|Shield Bash}}, {{Term|SS|Link}} must ready his {{Term|SS|Shield}} right before an enemy attacks him. The force from his {{Term|SS|Shield}} arm will repel the enemy's attack.<ref>{{Cite|If you ready your shield before an enemy attacks you, the force from your shield arm will {{Color|SS Green|repel}} the enemy's attack.|Eagus|SS}}</ref> The {{Term|SS|Shield Bash}} gives {{Term|SS|Link}} an opening to strike his opponent.<ref name="Technique"/> The technique is useful against enemies who throw weapons or refuse to give an opening.<ref>{{Cite|This technique is very useful against enemies who throw weapons at you or just refuse to give you an opening for attack.|Eagus|SS}}</ref> Using the {{Term|SS|Shield Bash}} to parry an attack requires perfect timing in battle.<ref>{{Cite|In a real battle, pulling off a defensive shield maneuver requires perfect timing. Believe me, you really don't want to miss.|Eagus|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Link}} can practice this ability on the {{Term|SS|Defense-Practice Log|link}} in the {{Term|SS|Sparring Hall}}.<ref>{{Cite|Why don't you put what you learned to use with that {{Color|SS Red|defense-practice log}}?|Eagus|SS}}</ref>

=== {{BotW|-}} ===
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can {{Term|BotW|Parry}} by readying his {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} with ZL and pressing the A button in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''. By {{Term|BotW|Parry|display=Parrying}} at the right time - just before an attack makes contact with his shield - {{Term|BotW|Link}} can execute a {{Term|BotW|Perfect Guard|link}}, which will unbalance {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} and redirect projectiles without causing damage to the shield. Nearly every form of attack in both Breath of the Wild can be parried, even things like explosions and Lynel fireballs, which will pass through Link without causing damage. This technique is particularly useful against [[Guardian]]s, as it will reflect their beam attack directly back at them and deal 500 damage. 

=== {{TotK|-}} ===
{{Stub|TotK}}
Parrying is largely the same in {{TotK|-}} as it was in {{BotW|-}}. One key difference, however, is that using [[Fuse]] to combine Link's shields with certain [[Material/Tears of the Kingdom|Material]]s can produce additional effects. For example, if Link is using a Lynel shield or a shield with a sharp object fused to it, the shield itself can deal damage if it makes contact with an enemy.
