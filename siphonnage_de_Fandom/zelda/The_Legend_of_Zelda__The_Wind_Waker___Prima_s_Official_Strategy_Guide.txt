'''''The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker — Prima's Official Strategy Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TWW}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 192 pages, and contains an overview of the {{TLoZ|Series}}, a complete walkthrough of {{TWW}}, a listing of {{Term|TWWHD|Locations in The Wind Waker|display=islands|link}}, and a checklist of collectibles, as well as a brief 20-page strategy guide for {{OoT}}.
