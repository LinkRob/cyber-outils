==Location and Rules==
{{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} run by {{Term|BotW|Modar|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course|link}}, which is located along the bottom of the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Modar}} invented the game recently after he rolled down a hill, making him the first and only one to play it.<ref>{{Cite|I invented it the other day rolling down the hill, so I'm the only one who's played it. And I love it ta smithereens!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he claims that it is famous and that none of the participants can get enough of it.<ref>{{Cite|Ya don't know {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}}?! It's famous! No one who's played it even once can get enough!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Modar}}, he recognizes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is tough and asks if he would like to play a round of {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}}.<ref name="Modar"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what it is, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} realizes that it is sensible that he has never heard of it before.<ref>{{Cite|I guess it makes sense that ya haven't heard about it, come ta that.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He explains that the objective of the game is to hit the {{Term|BotW|Ball|link}} into the hole at the end of {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course}}, which is marked by a {{Term|BotW|Big Flag|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It couldn't be simpler ta play! Whack the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} and try to knock it inta the hole at that flag.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s score will increase the fewer hits it takes to reach the goal, and he only has to pay 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to play.<ref>{{Cite|The fewer tries it takes, the higher yer score! The best part? It only costs {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} ta play!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Modar}} suggests using two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} to send the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} flying, he reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to overdo it.<ref>{{Cite|Two-handed weapons like hammers can really send the ball flyin', but ya gotta take care not ta overdo it.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> On the other hand, single-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} can be used to move the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} short distances, but they can also be easily broken.<ref>{{Cite|Swords and one-handed weapons are better for short distances, but be careful ya don't break 'em playin' the game!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} says he should go wild on the {{Term|BotW|Ball}}, as the more power he puts into his swings, the further it will go.<ref>{{Cite|Or ya can just go hog wild on the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}}! The more power ya put inta yer swing, the further it goes flying! FOOOOORE!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

Unfortunately, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} off of the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course}}, he will be forced to take a one-stroke penalty and continue from his previous location.<ref>{{Cite|If the ball rolls off the course, ya take a {{Color|BotW Red|one-stroke penalty}} and have ta keep going from where ya hit it.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Modar}} promises to keep track of the number of strokes {{Term|BotW|Link}} has taken, instructing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to reach the goal in less than 20 strokes.<ref>{{Cite|I'll keep track of how many {{Color|BotW Red|strokes}} it takes. Yer job is to get the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} in the hole in {{Color|BotW Red|20 strokes}} or less!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> There are no rules limiting how {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits the {{Term|BotW|Ball}}, but {{Term|BotW|Modar}} suggests sending it as far as possible with every stroke.<ref>{{Cite|How ya hit the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} is yer call. But the further ya can smash it with every smack, the better!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> No matter how far the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} goes, if it moves even a little bit, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} counts it as a stroke.<ref>{{Cite|If the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} moves even an inch, that counts as {{Color|BotW Red|a stroke}}. Huh, I guess those rules ain't so simple after all. Whoops!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to play, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will be excited,<ref>{{Cite|[It's time!] Say no more! It's time ta tee off! {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}} is go!}}|Modar|BotW}}</ref> before he then asks for the fee of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref name="Fee"/> After this fee is paid, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will then be free to play.<ref>{{Cite|Rock on! I'll start setting up!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> In order to play the game, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to keep the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} in place while he hits it. Otherwise, it will barely move. Each time the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} is launched counts as a single stroke. The {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} ends when {{Term|BotW|Link}} either runs out of strokes or reaches the goal.<ref>{{Cite|NICE shot! Way to take a whack at it! Give me a second to figger out yer {{Color|BotW Red|score}}.|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} takes all 20 hits allowed, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will stop the game and tell him to try again sometime.<ref>{{Cite|Whatta sorry showing... That's 20 strokes, pal. I'm cuttin' ya off there. Try again sometime, all right?|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He is saddened so see someone reach 20 strokes, informing {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he was rooting for him.<ref>{{Cite|Hate to see a guy go to 20 strokes. Just so's ya know, I was rootin' for ya.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> In addition, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} starts to stray from the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will call out to stop him.<ref>{{Cite|Where ya goin'? It ain't quittin' time already, is it?|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that if he goes any further, he will be forced to end the game.<ref>{{Cite|Go too far off course and I'll hafta call the game there. Ya won't get back yer entry fee, neither. So watch it!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} goes further, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will disqualify him.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, kid, but I hafta {{Color|BotW Red|disqualify}} ya. Them's the rules!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he should have said if he wanted to quit, but he says to come back and play when he is ready.<ref>{{Cite|If ya wanted ta quit, ya shoulda said so! Come back when yer feelin' serious. The {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}} course is always open! See ya around!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

After the game is over, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will look over {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s score. If it took {{Term|BotW|Link}} more than 11 shots to reach the goal, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will be disappointed and tell him that {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} is not that hard.<ref>{{Cite|Yer score was... {{Color|BotW Red|11}}?! Sheesh, kid. {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}} ain't easy, but it ain't THAT hard!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He encourages {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to give up and to keep practicing.<ref>{{Cite|Aw, buddy. Well...don't give up, OK? Ya just need ta practice, practice, and then practice some more.|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the goal between six and 10 shots, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} reveals that he thinks {{Term|BotW|Link}} can do better.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... Ya got a {{Color|BotW Red|6}} this time out.<br/>That ain't bad, but ya strike me as a guy who could do better.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> However, he assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the game takes time to figure out, saying "every journey starts with a single stroke".<ref>{{Cite|Don't worry, the game takes some practice ta figger out. They say {{Color|BotW Red|every journey starts with a single stroke}}!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.

Next, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the goal in four or five shots, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} remarks that he is becoming a heavyweight.<ref>{{Cite|Yer {{Color|BotW Red|score}} was...{{Color|BotW Red|5}}! Yer gettin' ta be a real heavyweight, kid.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> However, he reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he has yet to beat the record and encourages him to keep trying.<ref>{{Cite|Don't go thinkin' ya got it all figgered out, now! Keep at it, and set the highest record ever in {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}}!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He then gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} reached the goal in three or less shots, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} is shocked and says he could be a professional at {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} if there were a league.<ref>{{Cite|Ya got {{Color|BotW Red|3}}?! Well, don't that beat all! Ya could be a pro {{Big|Boom Bam Golfer!}} {{Small|If there was a league...or other players...}}|Modar|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|It is impossible to achieve a hole in one without moving the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} with a {{Term|BotW|Mine Cart|link}}.}} While he has forgotten his own score, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} recalls that it was somewhere around {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa...I lost track of my {{Color|BotW Red|stroke count}}. If I remember right, though, it was about the same as yers, give or take!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He believes {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to help make {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} popular, and he encourages him to keep playing and get higher scores.<ref>{{Cite|Yer the kinda player who's gonna make {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}} hit the big time! Keep it up, and smash those high scores!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> After this, he will give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Modar}} has given {{Term|BotW|Link}} his reward, he asks if he wants to play again.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Ya look like ya wanna go again! Heck, who am I ta stop ya?|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} is comforted that he at least got to see the beauty of {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} for himself.<ref>{{Cite|Whatta shame. But at least now ya've seen the majesty of {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Gold}}}} for yerself, right?|Modar|BotW}}</ref>
