{{Term/Store|Shrouded Stalfos|Shrouded Stalfos|Series, LA, LADX, OoS, OoA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 203 {{Exp Game|LADX, OoS, OoA}}}}</ref> They are {{Term|Series|Stalfos|link}} that wear robes. They have a significantly different fighting strategy compared to other {{Term|Series|Stalfos}}, as they throw spears or shoot {{Plural|Series|Arrow|link}}.

{{Term|Series|Shrouded Stalfos}} who wield {{Plural|Series|Sword|link}} are called {{Term|Series|Sword Stalfos|link}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
In {{LA|-}}, {{Term|LA|Shrouded Stalfos}} behave differently compared to ordinary {{Term|LA|Stalfos|link}}. They are much slower, and do not jump from {{Term|LA|Link|link}}'s attacks. Instead, the moment they spot him, they will either throw a spear or shoot an Arrow at him. {{Term|LA|Sword Stalfos}} are a stronger variant that wields a {{Term|LA|Sword}} and carries a {{Term|LA|Shield|link}}. They will charge at {{Term|LA|Link}} when they notice him, similarly to the {{Plural|LA|Sword Soldier|link}} of {{ALttP}}. Their {{Plural|LA|Sword}} are able to parry {{Term|LA|Link}}'s {{Term|LA|Sword}}, thus requiring that {{Term|LA|Link}} strike them from behind where they are vulnerable.

In {{LA|DX}}, they are depicted with blue robes. Like normal {{Term|LA|Stalfos}}, they are common to many of {{Term|LA|Koholint Island|link}}'s {{Plural|LA|dungeon|link}}.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS|-}} and {{OoA|-}}, {{Term|OoS|Shrouded Stalfos}} and {{Term|OoS|Sword Stalfos}} appear identical as in {{LA|-}}. They, however, wear green robes and the {{Term|OoS|Sword Stalfos}} no longer carry a shield with them. They are common in several dungeons in both {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}} and {{Term|OoS|Labrynna|link}} of {{OoS}} and {{OoA}}, respectively.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
A Shrouded Stalfos is seen alongside a [[Stalfos]] and a [[Spiked Beetle]] in {{LA|Cagiva}}, fighting Link as soon as he enters the [[Tail Cave]]. A Sword Stalfos is later seen attacking Link by the time he enters the [[Bottle Grotto]]. Another Shrouded Stalfos is seen fighting Link alongside a regular [[Stalfos]], while many more are seen among the monsters of the game attacking [[Animal Village]] and fight Link to the death.
{{Noncanon|End}}
