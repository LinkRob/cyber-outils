The {{Term/Store|Maku Tree||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 250 {{Exp Game|OoS}} & 254 {{Exp Game|OoA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Both versions of the Maku Tree are an important character in the plots of {{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}} and are the guardians of their respective lands and [[Oracle]]s. Although the Maku Tree plays an identical role in each game's plot, both versions differ in personality and appearance. The Maku Tree is one of the first characters [[Link]] meets in each game. When Link goes to meet the Maku Tree for the first time, he will find the way to the Maku Tree's grove barred by a closed gate; the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree Gate|link}} in {{OoS|-}}. In {{OoA|-}}, Link must use a detour through [[Maku Road]] to reach the tree. In {{OoS|-}}, the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree Gate}} can only be opened by "showing courage."<ref>{{Cite|Maku Tree Gate<br/>Show courage to gain passage!|N/A|OoS}}</ref> Link shows courage by obtaining the [[Wooden Sword]] from the [[Hero's Cave]] and holding it up before the {{Term|OoS|Maku Tree Gate}}. The Maku Tree is first found while in a weakened state due to the havoc wreaked by [[Veran]] in {{OoA|-}} or [[General Onox]] in {{OoS|-}}.<ref name="memory">{{Cite|With the flow of time disrupted, my memory is fading...|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="might">{{Cite|What's to be done, Link? With the seasons in chaos, the land is robbed of the very essence of nature, draining all of my might!|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> In order to defeat the villain, Link must restore the Maku Tree's state by gathering the eight [[Essences of Time]] in {{OoA|-}} or the eight [[Essences of Nature]] in {{OoS|-}}.<ref name="Essences of Time">{{Cite|I don't recall, but I think you need the eight Essences of Time hidden in Labrynna to beat Veran! The essences hold the power to see the truth, and they hold my memory! You won't beat Veran if you can't see the truth!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="Essences of Nature">{{Cite|You'll need the eight Essences of Nature that lie sleeping in Holodrum. Gathering all eight is the only option.|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> Throughout Link's quest, the Maku Tree offers Link helpful advice and information via telepathy.<ref name="ooa first essence">{{Cite|First, I want you to go to Yoll Graveyard in the east. I hear the first essence there. I can't do much, but take this.|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="oos first essence">{{Cite|Go to the Giant root near the lake up north! The first essence should be there, but I'd expect some resistance. Be careful!|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> After each dungeon, the Maku Tree will point Link to the direction of the next Essence.<ref name="ooa next essence">{{Cite|It's me, the Maku Tree! I hear an Essence of Time echoing in the Western Woods! Can you go there?|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="oos next essence">{{Cite|Hwaah... Thank you, Link. I, too, feel the Bright Sun! I dreamt of the waterfall on the mountain north of Holodrum. Perhaps the next essence is near there...|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> In both games, the Maku Tree is unable to sense the final Essence and Link must find it on his own.<ref name="ooa last essence">{{Cite|The last essence must be hidden somewhere in Labrynna... Good luck!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="oos last essence">{{Cite|Say, Link! Now for the last Essence of Nature! I must rely on you, Link! It lies hidden in Holodrum. Good luck!|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> By selecting the Maku Tree's area on the [[Overworld]] map, Link can review the current objective given by the Maku Tree. Link is be able to enter the hollow of the Maku Tree, where he will find the [[Hall of Secrets]]. After collecting each Essence, the Maku Tree will grow. Eventually, Link will be able to climb up to the very top of the Tree. Here Link will find many items, such as [[Gasha Seed]]s and [[Fairy|Fairies]]. A [[Great Fairy]] also resides here. Once Link obtains all eight Essences, the Maku Tree produces a [[Huge Maku Seed]], which is capable of dispelling the barrier that surrounds the antagonist's lair.<ref name="ooa seed">{{Cite|Link! You've collected all eight Essences of Time and know their power! Thanks to you, time flows true in me again, and I remember all I had forgotten! I'm the guardian of Nayru, Oracle of Ages, and my Maku Seed can be used only by a hero who knows the powers of the Essences of Time. The hero who knows the Essences of Time sees unwavering truth across time and space. If you can do this, Link you can stop Veran! Take this!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="oos seed">{{Cite|Link! You've finally gathered all eight Essences of Nature. You've become a true hero who understands the Essences of Nature. You are hope for all that live! Take this seed! Link! Surely now you can defeat Onox, the General of Darkness, and free Din, Oracle of Seasons!|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> In each [[Linked Game]], the Oracles create a portal next to the Maku Tree once they have been freed. The portal transports Link to the [[Room of Rites]]. After defeating [[Ganon]], the Maku Tree rescues Link and [[Princess Zelda]] by teleporting them out of the crumbling Room of Rites just as it collapses.

==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|The Maku Tree|This gigantic tree is the guardian spirit of al lHolodrum, and it now awaits a visit from a brave young man. It is said one can meet the Maku Tree only after showing great courage.}}
[[File:Male Maku Tree 1.png|left|120px]]
The Maku Tree in {{OoS|-}} is the guardian of [[Holodrum]] and the Oracle, [[Din (Oracle)|Din]].<ref name="Horon Village"/><ref name="din taken">{{Cite|Hwaaa...... Wh-who's that? Did you wake me? Who are you? Hmm... What?!? Din was taken while I slept? There's no sign of the Temple of Seasons?!? This is awful!!! Guarding the oracle is my duty... Alas, how I've withered!|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> The Maku Tree is male, with a long nose and a constant sleepy expression. He has a mature and serious personality. The Maku Tree falls asleep very often and must be woken by popping his nose bubble with a [[Sword]] if Link wishes to speak with him. Doing so startles the Maku Tree awake and he will fall back to sleep as soon as he is finished speaking.<ref name="din taken"/>

[[File:OoS Maku Tree.png|thumb|right|The Maku Tree sleeping in {{OoS|-}}]]
After regaining consciousness following the events of Din's capture by Onox, Link finds Impa standing over him. Impa sends Link to the Maku Tree for help.<ref name="Horon Village"/> The Maku Tree, who had slept through the events of Din's capture and the subsequent destruction of the [[Temple of Seasons]], finds his body withered and his might drained due to the chaos among the four seasons.<ref name="din taken"/><ref name="might"/> The Maku Tree then tells Link that in order to defeat Onox, he must first restore his might by collecting the eight Essences of Nature scattered across Holodrum.<ref name="Essences of Nature"/> The Maku Tree then directs Link to the [[Gnarled Root Dungeon]], the location of the first Essence of Nature.<ref name="oos first essence"/>

The Maku Tree points Link to the direction of the next Essence after every dungeon.<ref name="oos next essence"/> However, he cannot sense the location of the final Essence, the [[Changing Seasons]], leaving Link to find it on his own.<ref name="oos last essence"/> The Maku Tree also helps Link find the [[Temple of Seasons]], where the [[Rod of Seasons]] is located.<ref>{{Cite|I saw the Temple of Seasons in a strange land I do not know. There I saw a precious item that will aid you, Link.|Maku Tree|OoS}}</ref> After Link gathers all eight Essences and restores the Maku Tree's might, he gives Link a Huge Maku Seed, which allows him to penetrate the evil forces surrounding [[Onox's Castle]] and enter.<ref name="oos seed"/>

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Maku Tree|This giant tree is Labrynna's guardian. Link is able to earn her favor by helping her in the distant past—when she was but a young sprout.}}
[[File:MakuTreeSprout.png|left|60px]]
The Maku Tree in {{OoA|-}} is the guardian of [[Labrynna]] and the Oracle, [[Nayru (Oracle)|Nayru]].<ref>{{Cite|Pleased to meet you, young hero. I am the Maku Tree, guardian of Labrynna.|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="ooa seed"/> The Maku Tree is female, with rosy cheeks and a pink flower in her foliage. She has a bright and cheery personality. In the past, the Maku Tree is a small sprout named '''Maku Sprout'''.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 212}}</ref> Due to being rescued by Link as a sprout, the Maku Tree becomes emotionally attached to Link to the point of waiting to marry him for 400 years.<ref name="bride">{{Cite|Dear! ...Umm... ...ahh... When I grow up, I'm going to be your bride! Ahh! I said it!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="marry">{{Cite|When I grow up, I'll marry you! That's what you said, Link. Four hundred years I waited.|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref>

[[File:Oracle Of Ages - Maku Tree.png|thumb|right|The Maku Tree in {{OoA|-}}]]
After witnessing Nayru's capture by Veran, [[Impa]] sends Link to the Maku Tree for help.<ref name="Lynna Village"/> Soon after Link meets the Maku Tree, Veran's actions 400 years in the past take effect and cause the Maku Tree to vanish in the present.<ref>{{Cite|I feel so weird. I'm vanishing! Something must be happening to me in the past! Help!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref><ref name="the present">{{Cite|The present age and the past age are related. Events in the past affect events in the present.|N/A|OoA}}</ref> By travelling to the past, Link finds a group of Veran's forces attacking the Maku Tree sprout and defeats them. The Maku Tree sprout admires Link's brave rescue, saying that she will repay him when she grows up.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for rescuing me! I will never, ever forget it! And I promise I'll repay you when I grow up!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref> Just before Link leaves, the Maku Tree sprout exclaims that she wishes to be his bride.<ref name="bride"/>

Upon returning to the present, Link discovers that the Maku Tree's memory is fading due to the disruption of the flow of time.<ref name="memory"/> She is still able to remember Link's rescue centuries ago. However, she has confused the vow she made to marry Link as being Link's own words.<ref name="marry"/> The Maku Tree is dismayed upon realizing that Link has not come back for her love.<ref>{{Cite|I knew you wouldn't forget about me. Our hearts are bound across time by a thread of... What? That's not why you're here? Why won't you listen to me? Fine! You want to talk about Veran, right?|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref> The Maku Tree shifts her focus to defeating Veran and tells Link that he must first restore her memory by collecting the eight Essences of Time scattered across Labrynna.<ref name="Essences of Time"/> The Maku Tree then directs Link to [[Yoll Graveyard]], the location of the first Essence of Time, and gives him the [[Seed Satchel]] and [[Ember Seed]]s needed to access the area.<ref name="ooa first essence"/> 

The Maku Tree points Link to the direction of the next Essence after every dungeon.<ref name="ooa next essence"/> However, she cannot sense the location of the final Essence, the [[Falling Star]], leaving Link to find it on his own.<ref name="ooa last essence"/> After the sixth dungeon, [[Mermaid's Cave]], the Maku Tree informs Link that [[Queen Ambi]] has left her [[Ambi's Palace|palace]] and that it is time to rescue Nayru from Veran's clutches.<ref>{{Cite|Link! I have great news!!! Queen Ambi has just left the palace! Now is your chance! This may be your only opportunity to save Nayru!|Maku Tree|OoA}}</ref> After Link gathers all eight Essences and restores the Maku Tree's memory, she gives Link a Huge Maku Seed, which allows him to penetrate the evil forces surrounding the [[Black Tower]] and enter.<ref name="ooa seed"/>

==Trivia==
*The Maku Tree and the Huge Maku Seed it produces are referred to in the Japanese titles of both {{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}}, which translate as "''The Legend of Zelda: Nut of the Mysterious Tree: Chapter of Space-Time/Earth''".
*In the French translation, the name Maku was changed to Bojo. This is because "Maku" sounds too similar to a vulgar expression for one's bottom.{{Fact}}
