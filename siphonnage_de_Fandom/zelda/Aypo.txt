==Biography==
Aypo is a reader who lives in his library atop the mountain summit of Nortinka. He offers to read a secret verse in the [[Book of Koridai]] once [[Link]] retrieves it.<ref>{{Cite|If you bring me the Book of Koridai, I will gladly read the secret verse.|Aypo|TFoE}}</ref> When Link brings it to him, Aypo reveals that the Book is the only thing that can defeat [[Ganon]].<ref>{{Cite|Listen, such is the power of the prince of darkness that he can kill with a single look! Attacks against Ganon will prove fruitless unless Link attacks with the sacred book.|Aypo|TFoE}}</ref>
