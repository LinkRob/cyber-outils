{{Term/Store|Lake Saria||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Lake Saria'''|Map|BotW}}</ref> The name is based on the character, [[Saria]] from [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]].

==Features and Overview==
Lake Saria is a small body of water in the southwestern section of the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. It is the end section of the [[Korok Trial]] and [[Shrine Quest]] "[[Trial of Second Sight]]". Link must use a [[Raft]] provided to cross the Lake in order to transport a [[Treasure Chest]] to a small island in the center of the Lake and place it inside the [[Tree]] living on the island. Once Link does this, the [[Kuhn Sidajj Shrine]] appears on the far side of the Lake.
