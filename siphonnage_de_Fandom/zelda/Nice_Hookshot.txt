==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|ALBW}}
The Nice Hookshot is an item from The Legend of Zelda: A Link Between Worlds. It is a more powerful version of the Hookshot. If Link has purchased the Hookshot from Ravio, Mother Maiamai will upgrade it into the Nice Hookshot after he rescues ten Lost Maiamais. It has a longer length than the Hookshot and can deal damage.

==Trivia==
