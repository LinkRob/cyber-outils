{{Term/Store|Bow Moblin|Bow Moblins|Series, TMC|plural}},{{Exp Game|TMC}}<ref>{{Cite|Bow Moblin|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Moblin Archer|Moblin Archers|NL|plural}},{{Exp Game|NL}}{{Name Ref|NL}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. They are a variant of {{Plural|Series|Moblin|link}} that carry a {{Term|Series|Bow|link}} and shoot {{Plural|Series|Arrow|link}}. {{Plural|Series|Bow Moblin}} go unnamed in {{FS}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{FS|-}}===
In {{FS}}, {{Plural|FS|Bow Moblin}} carry {{Plural|FS|Bow|link}} with which they launch {{Plural|FS|Arrow|link}}. Whenever they see one of the {{Plural|FS|Link|link}}, they will launch one or two {{Plural|FS|Arrow}} in their direction. It is possible to block their {{Plural|FS|Arrow}} with the {{Term|FS|Shield|link}}. Since they do not guard themselves, they can be attacked by any means from any direction. The {{Term|FS|Boomerang|link}} can be used to stun them. {{Plural|FS|Bow Moblin}} tend to appear in groups, and appear in the {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees|link}} and {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace|link}}. In {{FSAE}}, they also appear in the {{Term|FS|Realm of Memories|link}} and the {{Term|FS|Hero's Trial|link}}.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Bow Moblin}}
In {{TMC}}, {{Plural|TMC|Bow Moblin}} appear identically to those in {{FS|-}}. They shoot {{Plural|TMC|Arrow}} at {{Term|TMC|Link}} whenever he is within sight. They appear in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}} and the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}. Blue-colored {{Plural|TMC|Bow Moblin}} also appear in {{Term|TMC|Dark Hyrule Castle|link}}. These {{Plural|TMC|Bow Moblin}} are functionally identical as the ordinary brown variety. According to the {{Term|TMC|Swordsman Newsletter|link}}, defeating them is a good source of income; however, this is not necessarily true.<ref>{{Cite|Those pig-faced Moblins... You see them around the Minish Woods, right? They're big and dumb? Well, they're also rich! Beat one up for some quick cash!|Swordsman Newsletter 5|TMC}}</ref>

A {{Term|TMC|Bow Moblin}} disguised as the {{Term|TMC|Monster Lady|link}} invades {{Term|TMC|Percy|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Percy's House|link|display=House}} while he is away. She tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} not to light the torches, and doing so reveals her true form. The {{Term|TMC|Moblin}} gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} 50 {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}} not to tell anyone.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, dear. You found me out! Don't tell anyone!|Moblin|TMC}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{NL|-}}===
{{Plural|NL|Moblin Archer}} are enemies that appear in the {{BQ}} attraction in {{NL}}, bearing the likeness of {{Plural|NL|Moblin}} and using {{Plural|NL|Bow}} to attack. They appear in many stages, but only if the Wii U Gamepad is used. They are weak enemies, and take only one {{Term|NL|Arrow|link}} to defeat.
{{Noncanon|End}}
