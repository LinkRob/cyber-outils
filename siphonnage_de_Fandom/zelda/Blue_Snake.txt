The {{Term/Store|Blue Snake||Series, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|I am Blue Snake. I give Ring fortunes. Would you like a ring fortune?|Blue Snake|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I am Blue Snake. I give Ring fortunes. Would you like a ring fortune?|Blue Snake|OoA}}</ref> The Blue Snake appears alongside the [[Red Snake]] at [[Vasu Jewelers]].

==Purpose==
===Transferring Rings===
Same as the [[Red Snake]], the Blue Snake's main purpose is to allow Link to transfer all his appraised [[Magic Ring]]s between [[Linked Game|linked]] {{OoA}} and {{OoS}} game files. With the Blue Snake, this is done with the use of two [[Game Boy Color]]s (not [[Game Boy Advance]]s) and a [[wikipedia:Game Link Cable|Game Link Cable]]. With both games inserted in either Game Boy Color, the Blue Snake must be spoken to in one of the games, while the other remains at the starting screen. If done correctly, when "Link" is selected upon speaking to the Blue Snake, all the rings will transfer to the opposing game.<ref>{{Cite|With a Game Link cable and two Game Boy Color systems, you can bring rings you found in an Oracle of Seasons game to Labrynna without saving your secret. Insert your Oracle of Seasons Game Pak into another Game Boy Color, insert a Game Link cable and turn the power ON, but don't press any buttons. Play Oracle of Ages, talk to Blue Snake and choose "Link" to bring Oracle of Seasons rings here. In the same way, you can take rings found here to your Oracle of Seasons game.|Ring Link Basics|OoA}}</ref> 

The link will not work if the two games inserted into the Game Boy Color are the same game (two ''Ages'' or two ''Seasons''), or if the two games are not [[Linked Game|linked]].<ref>{{Cite|If you haven't finished this quest before or come by secret to Labrynna, you cannot link.|Ring Link Basics|OoA}}</ref> 

If a Game Link Cable is not available, the Blue Snake cannot be used to transfer rings. The Red Snake can then be used instead as an alternative. 

===Telling Ring Fortunes===
Besides transferring rings, the Blue Snake has a second particular purpose that the Red Snake does not have: giving "Ring Fortunes".<ref>{{Cite|I am Blue Snake. I give Ring fortunes. Would you like a ring fortune?|Blue Snake|OoS}}</ref> By connecting any two game files of {{OoA|-}} and {{OoS|-}} with a Game Link Cable, then simultaneously speaking to the Blue Snake in both games, the snake will award both players a "Ring Fortune", which comes in the form of a non-appraised ring.<ref>{{Cite|Through a Ring Fortune you and a friend each get a ring as a sign of your friendship when you and a friend use a Game Link cable to link two Game Boy Color systems. Talk to Blue Snake after connecting your Game Boy systems to link.|Ring Link Basics|OoS}}</ref> The rings that are obtained from Ring Fortunes are usually fairly common. Ring Fortunes are not required to obtain a specific Magic Ring.
