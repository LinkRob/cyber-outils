{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Spear}} are {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} that were once used by the guards of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are known to be easy to use but difficult to master.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} commonly wield these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}.

When scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, the following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Spear}}.
* {{TLoZ|Series}} Series {{Term|LA|Link|link}} ({{LA}}) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

As metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Spear}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}, and they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. However, they are unable to be burned by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They will also sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Spear}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which was used in the defense of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
