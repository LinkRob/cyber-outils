{{Term/Store|Auros Ruins||Series, FPTRR}} is the last area in the Third Continent in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Plot Role==
To complete the area, [[Tingle]] must fight several boss battles against very powerful [[Oinker]]s. After defeating all Oinkers, including their leader, Tingle can enter the Rupee Shrine where the [[Master Rupee]] is found in a huge treasure chest.

In this area, Tingle can free Pinkle after collecting all the [[Rupee Goods]]. Only after this is done can the real ending be unlocked.

==Local Speciality==
This area's specialty is Auros Nampla. The ingredients for Auros Nampla are as follows:

*Electric Eel X 8
*Spicy Prawn X 4
