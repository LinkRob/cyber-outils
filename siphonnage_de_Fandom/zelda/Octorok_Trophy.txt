==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok Trophy}} are obtained as post-battle rewards after finishing any given Chapter's {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} battle or a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, with a Trophy earned for every {{Term|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} kill (be they {{Term|HWAoC|Forest Octorok|display=Forest|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Rock Octorok|display=Rock|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Snow Octorok|display=Snow|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Treasure Octorok|display=Treasure|link}}) a {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} obtains.

{{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok Trophy}} can be used to either complete {{Plural|HWAoC|Side Quest|section=Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity|display=Quest|link}} or be used in the {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Blacksmith Guild|link}} workshop to de-rust rusted {{Plural|HWAoC|Weapon|link}} or remove Seals on a {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
