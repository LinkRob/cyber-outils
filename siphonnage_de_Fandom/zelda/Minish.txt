The {{Term/Store|Minish|Minish|Series, TMC}},<ref name="Encyclopedia"/> also known as '''Picori''' by the {{Plural|Series|Hylian|link}},<ref name="Encyclopedia"/> are a small race of people no bigger than {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s thumb{{fact}} that appear in {{TMC}}. {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}} were once {{Term|TMC|Minish}} too.

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|TMC|Minish}} resemble anthropomorphic mice that, like {{Plural|TMC|Hylian|link}}, have long, pointed ears. They came from the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm|link}} long ago, when monsters threatened mankind, and gave the {{Term|TMC|Hero of Men|link}} a {{Term|TMC|Sword}} of unparalleled power: the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}}. They also left behind the {{Term|TMC|Light Force|link}} or Golden Light for the {{Term|TMC|Royal Family of Hyrule|display=Royal Family}}. Their help is celebrated annually in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} through the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}. Every one hundred years, the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|link}}, a doorway between their world and the world of men, appears in the courtyard of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}.

It is said that {{Term|TMC|Minish}} are invisible to adults; only good, well-behaved children can see them.<ref>{{Cite|No, soldiers will not do. The Picori do not show themselves to anyone but children. Our soldiers could search for days and still find no sign of them.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref> The {{Term|TMC|Minish}} are well-known for their {{Term|TMC|Kinstone|link}} hiding capabilities. Wanting their human counterparts to find them, they place them in various obvious and inconspicuous places, and even keep some for themselves to {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Link}} when he comes along.

Their language is slightly different from that of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm}} and they do not understand the {{Term|TMC|Hylian}} language.<ref name="Encyclopedia"/>

==Trivia==
*Other name ideas for the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} included "Chilorians" and "Tiny People".<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 273}}</ref>
*Whereas the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} are known as Picori to {{Plural|TMC|Human}} in the English version of the game, the other language versions do not actually feature such a distinction and have {{Term|TMC|Minish}} and {{Plural|TMC|Human}} using the same word.
*The Japanese, German, French and Italian versions of the game have the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} speak in reverse. This trait was kept in the English release of {{TMC|Himekawa}}, although with a different font than normal.
*The {{Term|TMC|Minish}} appear to be based on the {{wp|Koro-pok-guru}} of {{wp|Ainu people|Ainu}} folklore. Likewise, the {{Term|TMC|Town Minish|link}} are reminiscent of the {{wp|Brownie (folklore)|Brownies}} from Scottish folklore and the German {{wp|Heinzelmännchen}}.
*{{BotW}} was originally planned to feature small people as well, with {{Term|BotW|Link}} even shrinking down to interact with them. While they are not specifically identified as {{Term|TMC|Minish}}, the video where this is revealed does show footage from {{TMC|-}} during the discussion.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= For example, we had this idea of including tiny people. Since this is a 3D game, we could have all these tiny-sized towns, and Link himself could shrink.|author= |published= March 14, 2017|retrieved=March 14, 2017|url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T3CsGbtifZI|title= The Making of The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Video – Story and Characters|site= YouTube|type= video}}</ref>
