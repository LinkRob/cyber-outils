"{{Term/Store|The Eye of the Sandstorm||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Eye of the Sandstorm}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Kara Kara Bazaar}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} named {{Term|BotW|Nobiro|link}} can be found standing on a small outpost, watching the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm|link}} over the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs|link}}. He claims that the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} stops for a few hours a day, but in the time he has been watching, he has not seen this happen.<ref>{{Cite|That sandstorm... They say it stops for a few hours a day, but it hasn't let up even a little since I've been watching.|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> There is a rumor of an ancient treasure hidden inside the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it {{Color|BotW Red|an ancient civilization hid a treasure}} inside that sandstorm.|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> As he has been waiting for days, he has begun to suspect he was given a bad lead.<ref>{{Cite|I've been here for days waiting for the storm to settle down so I can nab it! I just hope it wasn't a bad lead...|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> Suddenly, {{Term|BotW|Nobiro}} looks down at {{Term|BotW|Link}} and asks who he is again.<ref>{{Cite|Wait, who did you say you are again?|Nobiro|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} talks to the {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} {{Term|BotW|Strade|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar}} before freeing the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, he will find out that the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} clears up during the night.<ref>{{Cite|But I hear that sandstorm over there will die down for a few hours each day, clearing a path.|Strade|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I watch that sandstorm around the clock! Every minute of the day! Except when I'm sleeping, of course.|Strade|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|They said it dies down occasionally, but I've never seen it happen. Did they lie to me?|Strade|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can search the {{Term|BotW|Great Cliffs}} while the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} is gone during the night between 9:00 PM and 4:00 AM. The {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}} begins to return at 3:00 AM, but is not fully blown until 4:00 AM. Eventually, he will come upon {{Term|BotW|Dako Tah Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The treasure sleeping in the sandstorm visible from the Kara Kara Bazaar turned out to be an ancient shrine.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> By activating the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
