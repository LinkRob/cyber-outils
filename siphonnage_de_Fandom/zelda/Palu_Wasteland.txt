The {{Term/Store|Palu Wasteland||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Palu Wasteland'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Palu Wasteland is located in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. The Palu Wasteland is a large, barren region which bears several skeletons of large creatures.

In ''Tears of the Kingdom'', a [[Molduga]] is found at the southern end of the region.
