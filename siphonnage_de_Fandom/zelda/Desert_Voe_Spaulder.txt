==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Desert Voe Spaulders are pieces of [[Armor]] that can be purchased in the [[Gerudo Secret Club]] at [[Gerudo Town]] once "[[The Secret Club's Secret]]" [[Side Quest]] has been completed. They can also be bought in the [[Rhondson Armor Boutique]] at [[Tarrey Town]] once the "[[From the Ground Up]]" Side Quest has been completed. When worn, they provide one level of Heat Resistance. Desert Voe Spaulders can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. Wearing it on its own will increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} [[Heat Resistance]] by one level.<ref name="Inventory"/> The Desert Voe Spaulder, alongside the [[Desert Voe Headband]] and [[Desert Voe Trousers]], compose the [[Desert Voe Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Shock Resistance [[Set Bonus]].
