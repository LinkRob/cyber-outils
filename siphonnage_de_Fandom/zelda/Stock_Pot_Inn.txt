The {{Term/Store|Stock Pot Inn||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in [[East Clock Town]] in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Stock Pot Inn is the only commercial "rent-a-room" facility in [[Clock Town]]. It is a small inn located on East Clock Town which is run by [[Anju]]<ref>{{Cite|Anju, Stock Pot Inn's innkeeper|Bombers' Notebook|MM}}</ref> and her [[Anju's Mother|mother]]. The inn used to be a cafeteria, but after [[Tortus|Anju's father]] passed away, the room rentals that were part of their service became their main focus.<ref>{{Cite|We used to be a cafeteria, but after my father died, the room rentals that were part of our service became our main focus.|Anju|MM}}</ref> Although the Stock Pot Inn only has two rooms, people from all over [[Termina]] go to stay at the inn as the [[Carnival of Time]] approaches.<ref>{{Cite|We're just a small inn with only 2 rooms, but people from all over come here at this time of year...|Anju|MM}}</ref>

The Stock Pot Inn is open from 8 AM<ref>{{Cite|Stock Pot Inn / Check in begins at 8 a.m.|N/A|MM}}</ref> to 8:30 PM;<ref>{{Cite|It is 8:30 p.m. and the Stock Pot Inn will now be locking the door. Thank you for joining us.|Anju|MM}}</ref> however, people staying at the inn receive a [[Room Key]] that allows them to go in and out of the building before and after the doors close.<ref>{{Cite|Room Key / With this, you can go in and out of the Stock Pot Inn at night.|N/A|MM}}</ref> While the inn's main door is only open for roughly twelve hours, there is another entrance on the second floor that is unlocked at any given hour.<ref>{{Cite|It seems the veranda door of the town's Stock Pot Inn has carelessly been left unlocked...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref> [[Link]] can reach this door as [[Deku Link]] by using the [[Deku Flower]] nearby.
 
The building is run by a friendly staff, and Anju is usually the one on the counter ready to serve customers. During the [[Second Day|Second]] and [[Final Day]], depending on the outcome thus far in the [[Reuniting Kafei and Anju]] sidequest, Anju's Mother will be the one at the counter instead.

As the Carnival of Time approaches, the Stock Pot Inn is known to get completely booked, usually having to turn people away as a result.<ref>{{Cite|We're always totally booked right before the carnival. We're sorry we have to turn people away...|Anju|MM}}</ref> During Link's stay in [[Termina]], he can only get a room if he talks to Anju during the afternoon before [[Link (Goron)|Link the Goron]] asks for his reservation at 3:45 PM. In {{MM3D}}, this can only be done while in [[Goron Link|Goron form]]. If Link claims the reservation before Link the Goron does, then the Goron will instead sleep outside in the streets. 

There are two rooms available for rent on the second floor of the Stock Pot Inn, one of which Link is capable of entering only if he has the proper Room Key; the other room, however, can be entered with or without a key. This room houses the members of the [[Gorman Troupe]], and it contains four bunk beds, a table, and four chairs, making it rather crowded. The second room, known as the Knife Chamber,<ref>{{Cite|Your room is our "Knife Chamber" on the second floor. Here is your key.|Anju|MM}}</ref> is more spacious, equipped with two beds, a fireplace, and a [[Treasure Chest]] containing 100 [[Rupee]]s. The third room is for employees only, and is the first room seen going up the stairs to the second floor. It is otherwise known as Anju's room, and it is where Anju and Kafei reunite on the Final Day at the end of the Reuniting Kafei and Anju sidequest. The [[Couple's Mask]] is received here at the end of the sidequest. Like the Knife Chamber, this room also contains a [[Treasure Chest]] with 100 [[Rupee]]s in it.

The restroom, the kitchen, and [[Granny|Anju's Grandmother]]'s room are all located on the first floor of the inn. The kitchen, which is just behind the counter, is a rather ample room where Anju can sometimes be seen cooking, and it is also where Anju hands over the [[Letter to Kafei]] to Link during their night meeting.<ref>{{Cite|Tonight at 11:30, please come to our kitchen. We'll talk then...|Anju|MM}}</ref> The restroom has no door leading to it, and after midnight, {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} pops out of the toilet asking for paper.<ref>{{Cite|Pa-Pa-Pa-Paper, please!|??? (Majora's Mask)|MM}}</ref> If Link gives ??? paper (either any of the [[Title Deed]]s, the Letter to Kafei, or the [[Special Delivery to Mama|Express Mail]]), he will receive a [[Piece of Heart]] in return. Link can also visit Anju's Grandmother's room to listen to her stories to earn two more Pieces of Heart.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Tortus. Did you finish helping father? Then let mother read you a story.|Anju's Grandmother|MM}}</ref> This requires wearing the [[All-Night Mask]] however, as Link will otherwise fall asleep to her stories.

In {{MM3D|-}}, Link can obtain a [[Bottle]] by bringing [[Mystery Milk]] to [[Gorman]] during the Second Day.
