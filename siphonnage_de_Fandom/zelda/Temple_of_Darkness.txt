The {{Term/Store|Temple of Darkness||Series, FSA}} is the third {{Term|FSA|Dungeon|link}} of {{Term|FSA|Dark World|link}} in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 267}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
After wandering through the various locations taken over by the darkness, the four {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} reach the final location of {{Term|FSA|Dark World|link}}. This {{Term|FSA|Temple of Darkness|display=Temple}} was said to house the {{Term|FSA|Dark Mirror|link}}. In {{Term|FSA|Kakariko Village|link}}, they were told to swear allegiance to a great king,<ref>{{Cite|Leave the village and follow a mighty river downstream and you'll find a temple. You should go there and swear your allegiance to the great king.|N/A|FSA}}</ref> who the loitering {{Plural|FSA|Deku Scrub|link}} reveal to be none other than {{Term|FSA|Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Why have you come to the Temple of Darkness? Are you here to see Lord Ganon?!|Deku Scrub|FSA}}</ref> At the end of the {{Term|FSA|Temple of Darkness}}, the {{Term|FSA|White Maiden|link}} and the four {{Plural|FSA|Links}} learn that someone has stolen the {{Term|FSA|Dark Mirror|display=Mirror}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! The Dark Mirror... Someone's stolen the Dark Mirror!|White Maiden|FSA}}</ref>

The {{Term|FSA|Boss}} is {{Term|FSA|Phantom Ganon|link}}, who is more powerful than before. After defeating him, the {{Term|FSA|Red Shrine Maiden|link}} is rescued.
