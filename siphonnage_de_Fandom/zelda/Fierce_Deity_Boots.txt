==Location and Uses==
Fierce Deity Boots are pieces of [[Armor]] in {{BotW}}. They are obtained by scanning the {{MM|-}}-themed {{TLoZ|-}} Series Link [[amiibo]] with the [[amiibo (Rune)|amiibo]] [[Rune]]. According to their description, the Fierce Deity Boots are the same boots as the ones worn by the Fierce Deity in the [[Era of the Hero of Time]].<ref name="Inventory"/>

When worn, the Fierce Deity Boots provide one level of Attack Up. The Fierce Deity Boots can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]], though they cannot be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Fierce Deity Boots, alongside the [[Fierce Deity Armor]] and [[Fierce Deity Mask]], compose the [[Fierce Deity Set]].
