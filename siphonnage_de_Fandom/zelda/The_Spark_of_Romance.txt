"{{Term/Store|The Spark of Romance||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Spark of Romance}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Rito Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter the {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Jogo|link}}, who asks if he is also staying in the {{Term|BotW|Rito Village|display=Village}}.<ref>{{Cite|So are you staying in Rito Village too?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> He is glad to meet another admirer,<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] I see. Nice to meet a fellow admirer. I always liked the simple nature of this village.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> though he regrets choosing it for his honeymoon.<ref>{{Cite|Although, I'm starting to regret choosing it as my honeymoon destination...|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> His wife, {{Term|BotW|Juney|link}}, has been complaining due to the cold and lack of activities, and now refuses to talk to him.<ref>{{Cite|It's really cold here and there's not much to do, so my {{Color|BotW Blue|wife}} is complaining nonstop. She won't even talk to me right now.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> Without any {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple|link}} to give to {{Term|BotW|Juney}}, he fears that the two of them will end up divorced.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man. If this doesn't work, she's going to divorce me for sure! I need to make some {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}} to keep my wife happy...|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, he does not have {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} for a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But I don't have any {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}} to start a fire...|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has a piece of {{Term|BotW|Flint}} on him, {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} will be able to smell it.<ref>{{Cite|That unmistakable, slightly acrid scent... You have some {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}} on you, don't you?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> He asks to buy on piece from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Can you sell me {{Color|BotW Blue|a piece}}?|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, he gives him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as payment.<ref>{{Cite|[OK, sure.] Th-thanks! As promised, here's your payment.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> Though {{Term|BotW|Jogo}} is happy to be able to make {{Plural|BotW|Baked Apple}},<ref>{{Cite|A-all right! Now I can make some {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}}.|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> he still needs to make more, and therefore asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring him some pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}}.<ref>{{Cite|I still need to make more {{Color|BotW Blue|baked apples}} to keep my wife happy, so please bring me more {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}!|Jogo|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be complete.
