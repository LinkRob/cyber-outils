The {{Term/Store|Korsh O'hu Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Korsh O'hu Shrine}}<br/>Korsh O'hu's Blessing}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Korsh O'hu's Blessing''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The Korsh O'hu Shrine is found in the {{Term|BotW|East Gerudo Ruins|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, though it's initially buried beneath the sand. By speaking to {{Term|BotW|Rotana|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can hear some of her archeological research.<ref>{{Cite|I can't explain why...but I trust you for some reason. I'll tell you the details of my research if you can keep a secret.|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> She'll tell Link about the East Gerudo Ruins, where she discovered an ancient scripture.<ref>{{Cite|There's a temple east of here near the entrance to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}}. It's dedicated to the {{Color|BotW Blue|seven giant statues of the heroines}}, said to be the divine protectors of the Gerudo. It was at this temple that I originally discovered the ancient scripture. It reads: "When the symbols of the seven find their place, the blessed path will be revealed."|Rotana|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Seven Heroines|link}}".

At the East Gerudo Ruins, Link will find several metallic spheres, each bearing its own symbol. These symbols correspond with the symbols on the statues of the {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines|link}}. Link must locate each sphere and place it in front of its corresponding statue.

Once the orbs and statues have been matched, Korsh O'hu Shrine will rise from the ground. As Link enters the Shrine, he is greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Korsh O'hu|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Korsh O'hu. By entering this place, you've already proven your worth.}}|Korsh O'hu|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The Korsh O'hu Shrine is one large room with no puzzles or enemies. There is a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in the center of the room containing a {{Term|BotW|Flamespear|link}}.

Monk Korsh O'hu sits waiting nearby. He tells Link that he's done well to arrive at the Shrine, granting him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|You have done well to arrive at this shrine. A hero rises to right the wrongs of Hyrule. In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Korsh O'hu|BotW}}</ref> He wishes Link well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Korsh O'hu|BotW}}</ref>
