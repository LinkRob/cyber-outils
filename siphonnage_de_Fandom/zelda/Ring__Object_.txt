==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} agrees to participate in the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider Course|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo|link}} will set up 70 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}} throughout the {{Term|BotW|Eventide Island|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That took longer than I expected. My apologies. But it is ready now for you to fly whenever you are ready.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Using both his {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Updraft|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must pass through as many of the {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}} as he can without touching the ground.

Depending on how many {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} clears, he will receive a reward from {{Term|BotW|Mimo}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes through 19 or less {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}}} will be disappointed in his abilities and encourages him to keep training.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm? How many rings did you achieve? {{Color|BotW Red|19}}? I appear to have overestimated your skill a bit, it seems. You may be unaccustomed to flying. But this is nothing a bit of practice on my course can't correct.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> No reward will be given to {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Next, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} managed to clear between 20 and 24 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} says he still has progress to make to become one with the {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}} and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref name="Mimo"/> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} passes 25 to 29 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} tells him he did well and gifts him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You achieved {{Color|BotW Red|25 rings}}, hm? Very good. You take to your strange wings quite well. Here, this is for you.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} pass through 30 to 49 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} tells him that not even {{Plural|BotW|Rito|link}} score so well, granting him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|You achieved {{Color|BotW Red|30 rings}}?! You have truly become one with the wind, and so I must insist that you accept this reward.|Mimo|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} totals at least 50 {{Plural|BotW|Ring (Object)}}, {{Term|BotW|Mimo}} asks if he is a {{Term|BotW|Rito}} in disguise,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|70}}?!
