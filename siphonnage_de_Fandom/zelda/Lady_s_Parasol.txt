The {{Term/Store|Lady's Parasol||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 129"/>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|TFH|Lady's Parasol}} is one of the three {{Plural|TFH|The Lady's Materials|link|display=Lady's Materials}} required to make the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Ensemble|link}}, which is used to lift the curse on {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}.<ref name="E 129"/>

After {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} defeats {{Term|TFH|The Lady|link}} and clears the {{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}} for the first time, he finds the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Parasol}} inside a {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}}, underneath the usual {{Term|TFH|Material|link}} reward.<ref>{{Cite|Hm? There's something else here!|N/A|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You got The Lady's Parasol!|N/A|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Lady's Parasol}} cannot be obtained by any other means, and the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} will not buy it from {{Term|TFH|Link}} due to their association with the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} witch.<ref>{{Cite|Bah! {{Color|TFH Blue|The Lady's Parasol}}! Witch materials gives me the creeps! You're on your own there.|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref>
