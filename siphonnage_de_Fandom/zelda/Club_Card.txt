The {{Term/Store|Club Card||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Club Card}} is an item that {{Term|ST|Link|link}} can purchase from {{Term|ST|Beedle|link}} at his {{Term|ST|Beedle's Air Shop|link|display=Air Shop}} for 100 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I see you haven't yet joined {{Color|ST Light Red|Beedle's Membership Club}}! There are lots of great benefits to joining {{Color|ST Light Red|Beedle Member's Club}}. And it's just 100 Rupees to join! How about it?|Beedle|ST}}</ref> It is a symbol of membership at the {{Term|ST|Beedle Member's Club|link}} that allows customers to accumulate points based on the number of {{Plural|ST|Rupee}} spent. These points accumulate toward various thresholds which offer greater benefits and tiers of membership that are represented by {{Plural|ST|Beedle's Membership Card|link}}.

For every 10 {{Plural|ST|Rupee}} spent, {{Term|ST|Link}} can accumulate 1 point. However, once {{Term|ST|Link}} has reached 2000 points and attained {{Term|ST|Diamond Membership|link}}, he will not be able to accumulate any more points.
