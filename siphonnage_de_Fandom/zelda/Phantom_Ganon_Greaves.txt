The {{Term/Store|Phantom Ganon Greaves||Series, BotW}} are an item in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Phantom Ganon Greaves}}<br/>Some believe these greaves are inspirited by a dark entity resembling the Great King of Evil himself. They're a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They are a piece of [[Armor]] exclusive to [[The Champions' Ballad]] DLC Pack.

==Location and Uses==
Obtaining the Phantom Ganon Greaves is one part of the objective of the "[[EX Treasure: Dark Armor]]" [[Side Quest]]. The Greaves were stolen from [[Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|Hyrule Castle]] by [[Misko]] and a clue to their location can be found in [[Misko's EX Journal 2]] at the [[Deya Village Ruins]].<ref>{{Cite|I hid the six priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests... I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The evil spirit's armor set is hidden within {{Color|BotW Red|Faron's sea of trees}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at the highest of three waterfalls north of Lake Floria}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at the bridge between small waterfalls at Floria River}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the broken stone bird of Ebara Forest}}.|Misko's EX Journal 2|BotW}}</ref> The metallic [[Treasure Chest]] that hides the Phantom Ganon Greaves can be found buried in the ground near a bird statue in [[Ebara Forest]]. Link must use the [[Magnesis]] [[Rune]] to dislodge the Chest from the ground to retrieve the Greaves.

When worn, the Greaves provide one level of Stealth Up. The Phantom Ganon Greaves cannot be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} nor can they be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Phantom Ganon Greaves, alongside the [[Phantom Ganon Skull]] and [[Phantom Ganon Armor]], compose the [[Phantom Ganon Set]].

According to their description, it is believed that the Phantom Ganon Greaves are infused with a [[Phantom Ganon|dark entity]] that resembles the [[Great King of Evil]].<ref name="Inventory"/> After obtaining the Phantom Ganon Greaves, [[Granté]] in [[Tarrey Town]] will begin selling them for 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.
