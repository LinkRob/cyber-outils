==Location and Uses==
Ancient Greaves are a piece of [[Armor]] that can be purchased from [[Cherry]] at the [[Akkala Ancient Tech Lab]]. When worn, they provide one level of Guardian Resist.<ref name="Inventory"/> Ancient Greaves can be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Ancient Greaves, alongside the [[Ancient Helm]] and [[Ancient Cuirass]], compose the [[Ancient Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides an Ancient Proficiency [[Set Bonus]].
