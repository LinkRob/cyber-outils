==Location and Uses ==
Roasted Bass is the result of [[Hyrule Bass]] or [[Staminoka Bass]] that has made contact with [[Fire]]. Blue {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} also have the potential to drop them after death. They're also sold by the [[Protein Palace]] in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}. Roasted Bass is said to have flaky flesh and smell savory.<ref name="Inventory" /> [[Link]] can restore one and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} by eating a Roasted Bass.
