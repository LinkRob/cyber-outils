"{{Term/Store|Under a Red Moon||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Under a Red Moon}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Hyrule Ridge}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} playing his bandoneon and singing on a {{Term|BotW|Tree|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff|link}}, which is located on {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Ridge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|When the moon bleeds...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him, he apologizes for the chance his song was rough on the ears.<ref>{{Cite|Oh? Excuse me.<br/>Was that a bit rough on the ears?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> However, when he recognizes {{Term|BotW|Link}}, he congratulates him for climbing all the way up the {{Term|BotW|Tree}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! You've done well climbing all the way up here!|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Kass}} informs {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he knows an "{{Term|BotW|Ancient Song|link}}" about the area,<ref>{{Cite|I know a song about this place.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> which he offers to share with him.<ref>{{Cite|Would you like to hear the ancient verse passed down in this region?|Kass|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to listen, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will then proceed to sing it for him.<ref>{{Cite|[Let's hear it.] Excellent! Without further ado...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> The lyrics describe the {{Plural|BotW|Monk|link}} inviting the hero when the moon bleeds,<ref>{{Cite|When the moon bleeds and the fiends are reborn / The monks will invite you as they have sworn.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> but only when he stands on the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} wearing nothing.<ref>{{Cite|But first you must stand on the pedestal bare / With nothing between you and the night air.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After his song, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} wonders what it means for the moon to bleed.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm. "{{Color|BotW Red|When the moon bleeds}}..." I wonder what that means.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> He wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well on his journey,<ref>{{Cite|May the light illuminate you path.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

During a {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must stand on the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} below the {{Term|BotW|Tree}} with no {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} equipped. Doing this will cause the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}} to activate, and {{Term|BotW|Mijah Rokee Shrine|link}} will emerge from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|After you took off all your clothing and stood on top of the pedestal on the night of the blood moon, {{Typo|an}} the ancient shrine appeared.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} has been revealed, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.

==Trivia==
* This {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} is hinted at in {{Term|BotW|Kass's Journal|link}},<ref>{{Cite|"The ancient song of {{Color|BotW Blue|Washa's Bluff}}."|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> which is found in the hut behind him at the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest's}} location. Within, {{Term|BotW|Kass}} recounts that he has heard rumors about an ancient song on {{Term|BotW|Washa's Bluff}} pertaining to the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've heard whispers of an ancient song about the "night of the red moon" traditionally sung in {{Color|BotW Blue|Washa's Bluff}}.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref> He remarks that little information is known about the cause and purpose of {{Plural|BotW|Blood Moon}}.<ref>{{Cite|No one quite knows why the red moon rises. Or what happens in the span of that night.|Kass's Journal|BotW}}</ref>
