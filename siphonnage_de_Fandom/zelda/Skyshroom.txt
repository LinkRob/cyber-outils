== Location And Uses ==
Skyshrooms are [[Mushroom|Mushrooms]] that grow near Trees on [[Sky Islands]] and near Fallen Debris on the [[Hyrule|Surface]]. When eaten, they restore one quarter of a Heart to Link's Life Gauge. They can be roasted in Fire or Scorching Climates to create [[Toasty Skyshroom|Toasty Skyshrooms]]. Skyshrooms have no special effects when [[Cooking]] with them, and [[Weapons]] with Skyshrooms Fused to them cause [[Enemies]] to fly back when struck.
