{{Term/Store|Green Bow Guard|Green Bow Guards|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Green Bow Guard|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Green Bow Guard}} are enemies that move to the beat of the music in a circular pattern, always crouching for one beat before hopping onto an adjacent tile on the second beat. If they run into an object or enemy on a beat, they will hop away in the opposite direction on the next beat without crouching, then resume their normal beat pattern for two beats before attempting to form another circle. The direction in which they move could be either clockwise or counter-clockwise, though the direction they pick is always random. 

When they see either {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, they will then become aggressive and abandon their posts in favor of chasing them. They can harm the heroes at both close range and at a distance, where if the player stands at least two tiles ahead of them, they will draw their bow on one beat and then release their arrow on the following beat before resuming the chase.
