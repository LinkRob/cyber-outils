The {{Term/Store|Split Gauge||Series, TMC}} is a mechanic in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|This is it! Your {{Color|TMC Red|Split Gauge}} has been {{Color|TMC Red|sped up}}!|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Term|TMC|Split Gauge}} is a gauge that appears whenever {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} charges a {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack|link}} when standing on special tiles. When the {{Term|TMC|Spin Attack}} is fully charged, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can channel the power of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} and create up to three copies of himself. He gains this ability when the reforged {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}} is infused with two of the {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link}}. When all {{Term|TMC|Four Elements}} are infused into the {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade}}, it becomes the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}, granting {{Term|TMC|Link}} the ability to break {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}'s curse on the denizens of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} by unleashing a wave of energy after fully charging the {{Term|TMC|Split Gauge}}.<ref>{{Cite|We must use the power of the sacred {{Color|TMC Red|Four Sword}} to {{Color|TMC Blue|undo this}}!|N/A|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Link}} can shorten the time it takes to charge his {{Term|TMC|Split Gauge}} by receiving training from {{Term|TMC|Splitblade|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.
