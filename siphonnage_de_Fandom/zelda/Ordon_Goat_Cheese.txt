{{Term/Store|Ordon Goat Cheese|Ordon Goat Cheese|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TP|-}}===
Ordon Goat Cheese is made from [[Ordon Goat]] [[Milk]]. It is found in the {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak Ruins|link}} inside a {{Term|TPHD|Treasure Chest|link}} while {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} searches for the ruins' {{Term|TPHD|Bedroom Key|link}}. {{Term|TPHD|Yeta|link}} mistakenly directs {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to its location as a result of her forgetting where the  {{Term|TPHD|Bedroom Key}} is. This follows the [[Ordon Pumpkin]], the first item whose location {{Term|TPHD|Yeta}} mistook for the {{Term|TPHD|Bedroom Key|display=Key}}.

When brought to {{Term|TPHD|Yeto|link}}, he will take the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Goat Cheese|display=Cheese}} to use in his Soup, just as he did with the Ordon Pumpkin. It will upgrade his Good Soup to Superb Soup. As a powerful ingredient in the Superb Soup, the Soup will now have the power to replenish 8 [[Heart]]s, similar to the effects of the [[Red Potion]], captured [[Fairy|Fairies]], and [[Red Chu Jelly]].

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWDE Ordon Goat Cheese Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Ordon Goat Cheese appears as a Feast-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. It is imbued with the Darkness {{Term|HWDE|Element|link}}. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Ordon Goat Cheese can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Goat Cheese}} is shaped into a ring and striped to resemble the curved horns of Ordon Goats.
*Both the {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Goat Cheese}} and the Ordon Pumpkin are stored on the {{Term|TPHD|Mansion Map|link}}, where the {{Term|TPHD|Big Key}} is normally kept.
