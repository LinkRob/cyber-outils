The {{Term/Store|Blue Ring||Series, TLoZ, OoS, OoA}} is a recurring [[Defense]] [[item]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117 {{Exp Game|TLoZ}}}}</ref><ref name="Description">{{Cite|Blue Ring<br/>Damage taken<nowiki>=</nowiki>1/2|Quest Status Screen|OoS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Blue Ring<br/>Damage taken<nowiki>=</nowiki>1/2|Quest Status Screen|OoA}}</ref>

==Mainline appearances==
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
{{Manual|TLoZ|Blue and red rings|The blue ring reduces the damage inflicted by the enemy by half, and the red ring reduces damage to one quarter.}}
In {{TLoZ}}, the Blue Ring can be purchased for a very pricey 250 [[Rupee]]s in a [[Shop]] within a [[cave]] accessed through a staircase hidden beneath an [[Armos]]. In the [[Second Quest]], it is in a different location in the northeastern portion of [[Hyrule]], costing the same price. Aside from giving [[Link]] a whitish blue tunic, having the Blue Ring equipped makes it so that the young hero takes half the amount of damage he normally would.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= The blue ring reduces the damage inflicted by the enemy by half, and the red ring reduces damage to one quarter.|game= TLoZ|page= 23}}</ref>

Due to the graphical limitations of the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]], certain [[Characters in The Legend of Zelda|characters]], [[Items in The Legend of Zelda|items]], and [[Enemies in The Legend of Zelda|enemies]] share the same palette with [[Green Clothes|Link's tunic]] and will also appear whitish blue when Link has the Blue Ring equipped, such as [[Princess Zelda]]'s dress, the whiskers of a [[Pols Voice]], and even the [[Sword]]. The Blue Ring can be replaced with the [[Red Ring]] found in [[Level-9]], which the more advanced Ring further reduces the amount of damage down to a quarter of what Link normally takes.

==={{OoS|-}}===
In {{OoS}}, the Blue Ring can be transferred from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Oracle of Ages|Oracle of Ages]]'' by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].

==={{OoA|-}}===
In ''Oracle of Ages'', the Blue Ring can be found under a statue on the [[Sea of No Return]], requiring the [[Power Glove]] to obtain it. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, like in {{TLoZ|-}}, it halves damage taken by Link.<ref name="Description"/>

==In other media==
==={{TSP|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
[[File:Blue Ring TSP.png|left|50px]]
In {{TSP}}, the Blue Ring makes another appearance, although its use is completely different from the one seen in the games and actually leads to a Game Over if Link receives this item. The young hero can obtain the Blue Ring from [[Charles of Moria]] if he decides to stay at [[North Castle]] and chooses to fight Charles after he hears him talking to some monsters. Link drops down from the tree and demands that Charles stop his evil magic, challenging him to a fight. Confused, the Morian knight tells the young hero that he just wants to be his friend, and gives him a Blue Ring as part of a [[Moria]]n custom of exchanging gifts.

Later on in the book, after Link finds out that Charles is actually [[Ganon]] in disguise, he follows the false Morian knight into the woods but is promptly surrounded by a gang of [[Moblin]]s. Link confidently reaches into his pocket and pulls out the blue ring that Charles gave him, which causes for the knight and the Moblins to take a step back. The young hero then reminds Charles that it was the magic ring that he gave Link as a token of friendship, and Charles begins to look nervous. Link guesses that from the way Charles is reacting that the ring must be powerful, but Charles just mocks him when suddenly blue smoke rises from the ring. This causes the young hero to begin to feel weak and eventually collapses. The page ends with a Game Over as Charles laughs at Link and pokes the young hero's weakened body.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
{{Main|Ring}}
In {{HW}}, the Blue Ring appears as the first tier weapon of [[Wizzro]]'s Ring weapon type. Like all of the Ring weapons, it is of the Darkness element and enables Wizzro to use and manifest his dark magic into an assortment of attacks.
{{Noncanon|End}}

== Trivia ==

* With the hefty price of 250 [[Rupee|Rupees]], the Blue Ring is by far the most expensive item in the original {{TLoZ|-}}, just shy of the 255 maximum count. Due to the limitations of getting many Rupees quickly and the currency itself only comes in such a small value of 1 to 5, some fans find acquiring the Blue Ring a tedious task and met with criticism.
* The [[Blue Mail]] in {{aLttP}}, although it makes [[Link]]'s tunic pure blue as opposed to a pale shade, is based on the effects of the Blue Ring.
* Link's eyes in {{TLoZ|-}}, like the aforementioned items and characters, also use the same color palette as his tunic, and will consequently appear very light blue as the Blue Ring is equipped.
* When the Blue Ring is equipped in {{TLoZ|-}}, [[Princess Zelda]] resembles how she would appear in later games of the franchise.
* The Blue Ring in the Japan-exclusive [[Satellaview]] title ''[[BS The Legend of Zelda]]'' makes the [[Player|BS-X avatar]]'s tunic become the Blue Mail rather than the whitish color from the original [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] game.
* In an early box art of {{TLoZ|-}}, Link can be seen wearing the Blue Ring tunic rather than his standard [[Green Clothes]].
* Although the Blue Ring itself does not appear in the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] or ''[[SoulCalibur II]]'', the tunic from this item based on {{TLoZ|-}} is available as an alternate costume for Link in these games.
