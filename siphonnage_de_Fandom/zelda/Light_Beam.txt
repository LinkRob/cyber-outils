{{Term/Store|Light Beam|Light Beams|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}},{{Exp Game|TWW}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWW |publisher= Prima |page= 12}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Sunlight||OoT, MM}},{{Exp Game|OoT, MM}}<ref>{{Cite Guide|game=CE|publisher=Nintendo|page=92 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|game=CE|publisher=Nintendo|page=146 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> are recurring physical manifestations of [[light]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|ALttP}} {{Plural|Series|Light Beam}} go unnamed in {{TMC}}.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, [[Link]] uses a light beam to reveal the true form of the [[Thieves' Town (Dungeon)|Thieves' Town]] boss, [[Blind the Thief]].

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, light beams are found in the [[Spirit Temple]] and behind the Spirit barrier door in [[Ganon's Castle]]. Link can use either movable [[Mirror (Object)|Mirror]]s or the [[Mirror Shield]] to reflect the light onto [[Sun Pattern]]s.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, light beams are manipulated using the Mirror Shield in various Mirror puzzles and to clear [[Sun Block]]s in the [[Ancient Castle of Ikana]] and the [[Stone Tower Temple]].

==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, light beams are only found in the [[Earth Temple]] and in the grotto within the [[Angular Isles]], shining through holes in the ceiling. Link can use the Mirror Shield to bend the light to multiple Mirrors, while [[Medli]] can use her [[Rito Harp]]. This light also cures Link when [[Jinx]]ed.

==={{TMC|-}}===
In {{TMC}}, light beams are found in the frozen [[Temple of Droplets]], shining through skylights which can be opened and closed. They are used to melt [[block]]s of ice to free their contents, such as [[Key]]s. After beating one of the temple's three mini-bosses, Link will find the [[Flame Lantern]] which allows him to melt blocks of ice without sunbeams.
