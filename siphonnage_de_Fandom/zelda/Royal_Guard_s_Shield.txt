{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield}} are found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, either abandoned or being used by the {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} inside. They were created using the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} for use of the {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Though they have an incredibly high defense, a structural weakness led to their low {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}}, making them impractical for combat.<ref name="Inventory"/> This is a common feature of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|link}}, the equipment archetype to which {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield}} belong.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>

One {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|King's Study|link}}, which is within {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}'s {{Term|BotW|Library|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} wears a {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield}} while talking to {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}}, she will be unimpressed to the point of yawning and calls them flimsy pieces of garbage that are worthless to serious {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|display=Shield Surfers}}, due to their poor {{Term|BotW|Durability}} making them unsuitable for {{Term|BotW|Shield Surfing|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A royal guard's shield... *yawn* Those flimsy pieces of garbage are worthless to serious surfers.|Selmie|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield}} conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. However, they will not burn like wooden {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} do when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also sink if they are dropped in water, but they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
