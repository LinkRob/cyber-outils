{{Term|Series|Level}} may refer to any of the following:

*'''{{Term|Series|Experience|link}}''', also referred to as Leveling Up, a game element in {{TAoL}} and {{HW}} where a level is gained after earning enough {{Term|Series|Experience}}, resulting in increased stats.
*'''{{Plural|Series|Stage|link}}''', locations in a few {{TLoZ|Series|-}} games that constitute a self-contained world or area selected from a menu as opposed to an open overworld.
*'''{{Term|Series|Level|link}}''', usually a grouping of {{Plural|Series|Stage}}, but is sometimes another name for {{Term|Series|Stage}}.
*'''[[Levels in Four Swords Adventures|Levels in {{FSA|-}}]]''', the name of chapters in {{FSA}} that consist of regions of [[Hyrule]] with three Stages each.
*'''[[Levels in Tri Force Heroes|Levels in {{TFH|-}}]]''', the name of self-contained areas in {{TFH}}.
