The {{Term/Store|Castle Dungeon||Series, ALttP}},<ref>{{Cite|CASTLE DUNGEON|Credits|ALttP}}</ref> also known as the '''Sewer Passageway''',<ref>Hyrule Castle and Sewer Passageway - [https://www.ign.com/wikis/the-legend-of-zelda-a-link-to-the-past/Hyrule_Castle_and_Sewer_Passageway IGN Walkthrough]</ref> is a dungeon in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and overview==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
====Entrance to the {{Term|ALttP|Castle Dungeon}}====
After [[Link]] frees [[Princess Zelda]] from imprisonment in the dungeon of [[Hyrule Castle]], which has been taken over by the betraying sorcerer, [[Agahnim]],<ref>{{Cite|Agahnim has seized control of the castle and is now trying to open the seven wise men's seal.|Princess Zelda|ALttP}}</ref> she tells him of a way to escape safely; a secret passage in the Throne Room that leads to a [[Sanctuary (A Link to the Past)|Sanctuary]].<ref>{{Cite|There is a secret passage in the throne room that leads to Sanctuary. I'm sure the old man there will help us.|Princess Zelda|ALttP}}</ref> Upon reaching the throne room, Link and Princess Zelda combine their efforts to push aside an ornamental shelf, revealing a pitch dark tunnel that was hidden behind it.<ref>{{Cite|That ornamental shelf should open. Do you have a light? It's pitch dark inside and you can't see without one. If you're ready, let's go! Help me push it from the left!|Princess Zelda|ALttP}}</ref> If Link doesn't possess a [[Lantern|Lamp]], the game will not allow the ornamental shelf to be pushed. 

====Themes and Navigation====
Entering the tunnel with Link's Lamp to light the way, it begins as a seemingly normal, yet unlit castle room, filled with [[Rat]]s. Link will need to light the [[Torch]]es to see the entirety of the rooms he progresses though. Running into looked [[Rope]]s, [[Keese]] and finding hidden keys for locked doors as they progress deeper until they find themselves trudging through a sewer area, located somewhere beneath the moat surrounding the castle. Navigating through this area eventually takes Link and Zelda to a well lit room with a pair of cracked walls that Link can break down later in the game with the [[Pegasus Boots#A Link to the Past|Pegasus Shoes]] or [[Bomb]]s to find a considerable amount of [[Rupee]]s. To get back to this room later, Link must find a secret headstone in the [[Graveyard#A Link to the Past|Cemetery]] east of the Sanctuary.

Climbing a staircase in the room leads to a pair of rooms with wooden floorboards, the second of which contains two {{Plural|ALttP|Lever|link}}; one is a trap, while the other opens the way to the Sanctuary.<ref>{{Cite|Sanctuary is just beyond that
door. Pull the switch over there.|Princess Zelda|ALttP}}</ref> If Link pulls the wrong one, Ropes fall from the ceiling. After pulling the correct switch, Link and Princess Zelda step through to be greeted by the [[Loyal Sage|Sage]] who is waiting for them in the Sanctuary.

==In other media==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{ALttP|-}} (Ishinomori)===
The sewer passageway is also briefly featured in the {{ALttP|Ishinomori}}. After rescuing Princess Zelda from two [[Soldier (Enemy)|Soldiers]] that were taking her away, the young hero and the princess wind up at a dead-end, but soon afterwards one of the castle walls mysteriously opens and reveals the path to the secret passage. Using a nearby Lantern, Link and Zelda advance through the passage to eventually reach the Sanctuary.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The [[Castle Town Sewers]] from {{TP}} are somewhat similar to the Sewer Passageway, although rather than using them to escape the castle, Link uses them to sneak inside.
