The {{Term/Store|Rubber Armor||Series, BotW}} is an [[Armor]] set in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 180}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Rubber Armor is an [[Armor]] set consisting of the [[Rubber Helm]], [[Rubber Armor]], and [[Rubber Tights]]. All three pieces are obtained in places connected to thunderstorms:
*The helm is obtained as a reward from completing the "[[Thunder Magnet]]" Side Quest at [[Lakeside Stable]].
*The armor is obtained from a treasure chest in [[Toh Yahsa Shrine]] at [[Thundra Plateau]], accessed by completing the "[[Trial of Thunder]]" Shrine Quest.
*The tights are obtained from a treasure chest in [[Qukah Nata Shrine]] at [[Calora Lake]], accessed by completing the "[[A Song of Storms]]" Shrine Quest.

Each piece increases Link's shock resistance by one level. If all three pieces are worn and have been upgraded at least twice by a [[Great Fairy]], they provide the Unshockable set bonus, preventing Link from being stunned from electrocution.
