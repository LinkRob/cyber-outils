{{Term/Store|Carlov||Series, TWW, TWWHD, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="E 259"/> He is a master {{Term|Series|Figurine|link}} carver.

==Biography==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Figurine|TWW|Carlov the Sculptor}}
{{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}} runs the {{Term|TWWHD|Nintendo Gallery|link}} near the {{Term|TWWHD|Forest Haven|link}} in {{TWW}}. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} has obtained the {{Term|TWWHD|Deluxe Picto Box|link}}, he may then take color {{Plural|TWWHD|Pictograph|link}} which he may present to {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}} for him to make a {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine}} from the {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph}}. For {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}} to make a {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine}} from a {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph}}, it must meet certain requisites:
*The subject is in the center of the {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph}}
*The entire body of the subject is taken
*The subject is facing {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} when the {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph}} is taken

If these requirements are met, {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}} will ask {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} to wait an entire day, in which that time he will spend making a {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine}} out of the subject in the {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph}}.

{{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}} will even carve a {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine}} of himself if a {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph}} is taken of him, although he does not quite recognize his own face; however, he does believe the person in the {{Term|TWWHD|Pictograph}} is exceptionally handsome.{{Fact}} According to {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}}'s {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine}}, it is rumored that the reason he does not let anyone watch him carve is because he does so in his underwear.{{Fact}}

In {{TWWHD}}, he will work on a maximum of 12 {{Plural|TWWHD|Figurine}} at once as opposed to the previous limit of a single {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine}} at a time. The {{Term|TWWHD|Picto Box}} is also equipped with a new feature that will place a golden {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}} icon on {{Plural|TWWHD|Pictograph}} that meet {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}}'s requirements to be turned into a {{Term|TWWHD|Figurine}}. Thus {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} does not have to return to the {{Term|TWWHD|Nintendo Gallery}} to receive {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}}'s approval.

Once the {{Term|TWWHD|Nintendo Gallery}} is completed, {{Term|TWWHD|Carlov}} leaves to find inspiration elsewhere, leaving {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} as the {{Term|TWWHD|Nintendo Gallery|display=Gallery}}'s new master.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Carlov}}
{{Term|TMC|Carlov}} operates the {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} in {{TMC}}, and has a younger brother named {{Term|TMC|Borlov|link}}.<ref name="Borlov"/> The {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|display=Gallery}} becomes accessible after {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|White Sword|link}} and infuses the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link|display=Earth}} and {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link|display=Fire Elements}} into it. {{Term|TMC|Carlov}} is a collector of {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}, and will trade {{Plural|TMC|Figurine|link}} for them.<ref>{{Cite|I collect those {{Color|TMC Red|Mysterious Shells}} you've seen scattered all over the place. If you bring those shells to me, I'll trade you these {{Color|TMC Red|figurines}} I have made.|Carlov|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} collects all 136 {{Plural|TMC|Figurine}}, {{Term|TMC|Carlov}} rewards him with the {{Term|TMC|Carlov Medal|link}}, a golden medal of his own face.<ref>{{Cite|No way! Congratulations! You've {{Color|TMC Blue|collected every single figure I've ever made}}! I'll keep a good eye on your collection on the shelf here. And here, I want you to have this!
