{{Term/Store|Monari||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Monari|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Monari}} is an elderly resident of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}, which is located on {{Term|BotW|Lake Akkala|link}}. She and her husband, {{Term|BotW|Moggs|link}}, move in after {{Term|BotW|Fyson|link}} opens the {{Term|BotW|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)|link}}. Their {{Term|BotW|House|link}} is located behind the {{Term|BotW|Ore and More|link}} {{Term|BotW|Jewelry Shop|link}}. During the day, she walks around the center of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}. At night, she sleeps in one of the {{Plural|BotW|Bed|link}} inside her {{Term|BotW|House}}.<ref>{{Cite|So...sleepy...|Monari|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Monari}} and {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} have a son together who has recently left to travel the world.<ref name="Family"/> Due to this, the pair moved to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} with their {{Plural|BotW|Hylian Retriever|link}} after hearing a rumor about it.<ref>{{Cite|My son is grown up and I'm retired, so when I heard a rumor about {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}}... I just had to move here. And I'm glad I did! My pets seem much happier here, too.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> Though their {{Term|BotW|House}} feels empty without their son, {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} believes his absence has brought him and {{Term|BotW|Monari}} closer together.<ref>{{Cite|The house feels a little empty without him around, but at least it's helped me and my wife get closer together.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref>

Upon first meeting {{Term|BotW|Monari}}, she greets {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} and asks who he is.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hello. And who might you be?|Monari|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} responds, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} questions if he came to {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}} to purchase a holiday home.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Is that so? By the way... I don't suppose you came to {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}} to purchase a holiday home, did you?|Monari|BotW}}</ref> Whatever {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s response is, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} warns him to be careful, as there seem to be many strange people living in {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Is that so? Anyway, please be careful. My husband is taking care of this area, but there seem to be a lot of strange folks just outside of town.|Monari|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Monari}} again, she asks if he needs something from her.<ref>{{Cite|Do you need me for something?|Monari|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the strange things she has seen, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} claims to have seen strange plants and guys with ugly faces outside of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Any strange things?] Strange plants and guys with ugly faces live just outside the village. There are a lot of them, right?|Monari|BotW}}</ref> She also mentions the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} southeast of {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town}}, but after speaking about it with {{Term|BotW|Moggs}}, she came to the conclusion that one should not disturb things that they do not understand.<ref>{{Cite|Especially the {{Color|BotW Red|big flower}} that's to the {{Color|BotW Red|southeast}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|Tarrey Town}}... I was chatting with my husband about how there's a huge flower out there. You really shouldn't mess with things you don't understand...|Monari|BotW}}</ref>

While speaking with {{Term|BotW|Moggs}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask him about {{Term|BotW|Monari}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Your wife?] My wife? She's probably wandering around town right now, I'd imagine.|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> He claims that she is great at {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}}, and he particularly enjoys her {{Term|BotW|Mushroom Skewer|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|She's a really gifted chef. Her mushroom skewer is out of this world!|Moggs|BotW}}</ref> After this conversation, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Monari}} about this, and she claims that {{Term|BotW|Moggs}} was exaggerating her skills.<ref>{{Cite|[You're a chef?] Cooking? Oh, my, where did you hear about that? It was from my husband, wasn't it? That man can be so misleading about things sometimes...|Monari|BotW}}</ref> While she knows the basics of {{Term|BotW|Cooking}}, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} believes her real talent lies in baking.<ref>{{Cite|Sure, I can keep from burning water, but my real gift is baking cakes and sweets and the like.|Monari|BotW}}</ref> She learned her skills from her grandmother, who ran a sweet shop for the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref name="Grandmother"/> However, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} claims that {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not appear to need sweets, instead saying he needs to eat a lot of {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} to put meat on his bones.<ref>{{Cite|But...you don't look like someone who needs sweets. You should eat a lot of food and put some meat on your bones first!|Monari|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks {{Term|BotW|Monari}} about her baking skills again, she claims that her grandmother taught her how to make {{Term|BotW|Carrot Cake|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Pumpkin Pie|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|For example, {{Color|BotW Blue|carrot cake}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|pumpkin pie}}... Also...|Monari|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s prompting, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} reveals that she also knows how to make {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|["Also" what?] Well, I was taught how to make something called a {{Color|BotW Blue|monster cake}} too.|Monari|BotW}}</ref> While {{Term|BotW|Monari}} considers it easy to make, needing only {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Cane Sugar|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Goat Butter|link}}, she has found that the final ingredient, {{Term|BotW|Monster Extract|link}}, is difficult to obtain as of late.<ref>{{Cite|It's easy to make. You just need {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cane sugar}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}... And you also have to add some {{Color|BotW Red|monster extract}}, but that's been tough to come by lately...|Monari|BotW}}</ref> Despite its disgusting appearance and smell, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} claims that {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}} is a very filling meal.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Monster cake}} may look a little gross and smell a little nauseating, but it'll fill you right up!|Monari|BotW}}</ref> After hearing this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ask {{Term|BotW|Monari}} to repeat the recipe for {{Term|BotW|Monster Cake}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Monster-cake recipe?] Sure... sure... I'll teach you as many times as you like. You need to add some {{Color|BotW Red|monster extract}} to some {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|cane sugar}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}.|Monari|BotW}}</ref>

At 9:00 PM, {{Term|BotW|Monari}} will return to her {{Term|BotW|House}} for the night. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her, she may tell him that she has to get ready to go to sleep, suggesting that he do the same.<ref>{{Cite|I have to get ready for bed. You should do the same.|Monari|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, she might say that she has to go to {{Term|BotW|Bed}}, since she struggles during the day if she does not sleep well.<ref>{{Cite|Now then, I've got to get to bed. It's rough on my body if I don't get a good night's rest.|Monari|BotW}}</ref>
