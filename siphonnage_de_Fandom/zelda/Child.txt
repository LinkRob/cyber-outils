The {{Term/Store|Child||Series, TAoL}} is a kid who gets kidnapped in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 223}}</ref>

==Biography==
His mother, an [[Old Woman]] of [[Darunia]], implores [[Link]] to rescue her Child who was taken to [[Maze Island]].<ref>{{Cite|RESCUE THE KIDNAPPED CHILD ON THE ISLAND|Old Woman|TAoL}}</ref> The Child is guarded by an orange [[Geru]] in a {{Term|TAoL|Cave|link}} on the southeast corner of the Island. Rescuing and taking back the Child to the Old Woman rewards Link with meeting an [[Old Man]] who teaches him the [[Reflect]] [[Magic]].<ref name="Rescued"/><ref>{{Cite|THIS MAGIC WILL STRENGTHEN A SHIELD.|Old Man|TAoL}}</ref>

In the Japanese version, he is bound in ropes due to the way Link picks up items (you have to stab to pick it up). Therefore, stabbing is more like cutting the rope off him
