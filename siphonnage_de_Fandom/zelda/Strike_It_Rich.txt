{{Term/Store|Strike It Rich||Series, HWAoC}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Strike It Rich|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Strike It Rich}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given eight minutes to defeat two {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Talus}}. Each {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} fights one {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus}}, as a gate prevents them from leaving their area and fighting the other one. Several {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Pebblit|link}} are also scattered across the map. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Sapphire|link}}.
