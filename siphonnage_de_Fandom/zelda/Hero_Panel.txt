{{Term/Store|Hero Panel|Hero Panels|Series, TFH|plural}} are a [[Multiplayer]] interface and feature in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Manual|quote= <u>'''6''' Hero Panels</u><br/>Each hero's color, energy gauge, and carried item will be displayed at the top. Below that, you can see each player's name and outfit.|game= TFH|page= 8}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Plural|TFH|Hero Panel}} appear on the bottom screen of the [[Nintendo 3DS]] once two or more {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} have gathered in a [[Multiplayer]] game. Each {{Term|TFH|Hero Panel}} represents one of the heroes and feature's an icon of that {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s face, his chosen name, and a shortened name for the {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} he wears (eg. the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic|link}} is called "Hero"). Once the group enters the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}, the {{Plural|TFH|Hero Panel}} also display the {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} that each {{Term|TFH|Links}} carries, as well as miniature copies of their {{Plural|TFH|Energy Gauge|link}}. During a {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}} battle, {{Plural|TFH|Hero Panel}} also display each {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s {{Term|TFH|Life Gauge|link}}.

By pressing and holding on a {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s {{Term|TFH|Hero Panel}}, the upper screen will shift to that {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s position, allowing any player to see what the other {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are doing at any time. Pressing the A or B button while holding down a {{Term|TFH|Hero Panel}} will bring up a dialog box with the option to [[Blacklist]] that {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s player.
