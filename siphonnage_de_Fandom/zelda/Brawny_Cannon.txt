The {{Term/Store|Brawny Cannon||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Brawny Cannon|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Brawny Cannon}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} that can be purchased from {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} at a cost of one {{Term|ST|Alchemy Stone|link}}, three {{Plural|ST|Palace Dish|link}} and two {{Plural|ST|Goron Amber|link}}. Its design features a gold statue of a human figure holding the {{Term|ST|Cannon|link}} on its shoulders, in a pose similar to common depictions of {{Wp|Atlas (mythology|Atlas}} in {{Wp|Greek mythology}}. Its description reads "This cannon might look fancy, but it's strong enough for heavy jobs!"

Outfitting the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Brawny Cannon}} increases its durability to five Hearts. When used alongside the three other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}} in the {{Term|ST|Golden Train|link}} set, the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}}'s durability is increased to eight Hearts.
