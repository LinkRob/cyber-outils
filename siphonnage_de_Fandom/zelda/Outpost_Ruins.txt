The {{Term/Store|Outpost Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Outpost Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Outpost Ruins are the site of a confrontation between [[Guardian (Breath of the Wild)|Guardian]]s and the citizens of [[Hyrule]], located just east of the [[Great Plateau]] in [[Hyrule Field#Breath of the Wild|Hyrule Field]]. The remains of three Guardians can be found scattered throughout the Ruins, which can be searched for [[Ancient Material]]s.

If [[The Master Trials]] DLC is installed, [[Misko's EX Journal]] will appear on a broken bookshelf in the southeasternmost corner of the southernmost ruin.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Misko's Treasure}}<br/>Eight items have been added, including Majora's Mask and other relics honoring the legacy of The Legend of Zelda series. There is apparently a journal at Outpost Ruins written by the fiend who stole these items—the great bandit Misko!|N/A|BotW}}</ref> Its pages provide hints as to the current locations of several pieces of [[Armor]] that [[Misko]] had stolen from [[Hyrule Castle]].<ref>{{Cite|I hid the eight priceless relics I stole from Hyrule Castle in treasure chests… I shall write down their locations. -{{Color|BotW Blue|Misko}}|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> The Journal is part of the [[Side Quest]]s "[[EX Treasure: Ancient Mask]]", "[[EX Treasure: Fairy Clothes]]", "[[EX Treasure: Twilight Relic]]", and "[[EX Treasure: Phantasma]]".

Littered throughout the Ruins, Link can find [[Rusty Claymore]]s, [[Rusty Halberd]]s, [[Rusty Shield]]s, and a [[Rusty Broadsword]]. A [[Wooden Bow]] can be found lying on the ground near the decrepit fountain in the center. Link can also find several [[Barrel]]s and wooden crates around the Ruins, which contain [[Arrow]]s and [[Apple]]s. 

A stone [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Traveler's Bow]] rests on the keep in the center of the Ruins. A second stone Treasure Chest can be found in the building to the far east of the Ruins, containing a [[Red Rupee]]. A metal Treasure Chest containing [[Amber]] can be found embedded in the ground in the southernmost building.
