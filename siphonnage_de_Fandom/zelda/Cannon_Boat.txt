{{Term/Store|Cannon Boat|Cannon Boats|Series, PH, ST|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 170 {{Exp Game|PH, ST}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{PH|-}}===
Cannon Boats are controlled by [[Miniblin]] pirates in {{PH}}. The larger [[Pirate Ship (Enemy)|Pirate Ship]] will attempt to board the [[SS Linebeck]], while the smaller metal ones provide covering fire. When destroyed, the larger ships often leave behind [[Ship Parts]] that can be salvaged with the [[Salvage Arm]]. 

==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Cannon Boats only appear while Link travels around the [[Ocean Realm]], usually attacking in groups of three. They are replaced by [[Tank]]s in other realms.
