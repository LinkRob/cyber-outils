==Overview==
{{Term|TotK|Link|link}} encountered {{Term|TotK|Master Kohga|link}} — an old foe — within {{Term|TotK|The Depths|link}}. He returned after falling down in a chasm since their last battle and has started utilizing {{Term|TotK|Zonai Devices|link}} that they've found as part of their arsenal. He swears that he'll defeat Link and gain the favor of the "Magnificent One," then flies off for the abandoned southwest mine.<ref>{{Cite|I'll grab that power you cheated me out of for MYSELF, off at the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Southwestern Abandoned Mine</font>! Then the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">Magnificent One</font> and I will guide this world to its miserable end. And there's nothing you can do to stop us!|Master Kohga|TotK}}</ref>

Link caught up with Kohga at the {{Term|TotK|Abandoned Gerudo Mine|link}}, who is attempting to activate a dormant {{Term|TotK|Steward Construct|link}} trying to use the same trick with Link's {{Term|TotK|King Rauru's Right Arm|display=right arm}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'm doing what you did! Exactly! But it refuses to work.|Master Kohga|TotK}}</ref> Upon finding Link, he engages him in a flying contraption, but was defeated. No longer interested in attaining Link's power for himself, he decided to go with collecting {{Plural|TotK|Crystallized Charge|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Our collection of <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">crystallized charges</font> is well underway, and the restoration of the <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">final weapon</font> is close at hand!|Master Kohga|TotK}}</ref> He flees for the {{Term|TotK|Abandoned Lanayru Mine|link|display=eastern abandoned mine}}.

Caught up to him, Kohga fights Link in the waters of the {{Term|TotK|Abandoned Lanayru Mine|link}} but is defeated again. Nonetheless, Kohga proclaimed that his underlings have apparently found the {{Term|TotK|Demon King|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|And there's more. I just got word from my underlings... That they've located the man himself!|Master Kohga|TotK}}</ref> He also declared that they have enough crystalized charges to activate their "ultimate weapon,"<ref>{{Cite|That's where I'll use those charges to activate our <font color{{=}}"OrangeRed">ultimate weapon</font>, offer it to the Demon King, and...|Master Kohga|TotK}}</ref> and Kohga heads off for the northwest abandoned mine to raid one more mine.

Link caught up to Kohga once more at the {{Term|TotK|Abandoned Hebra Mine|link}}. Awaiting for Link's arrival, he unleashes his "ultimate weapon" against him: A large Zonai Construct. He plans to offer it to the Demon King, by demonstrating it to defeat Link. Kohga commandeers the construct and pilots it to attack Link. In the end, Kohga was defeated once again.

Kohga deployed one more trick against Link: A rocket contraption.<ref>{{Cite|Ahahahaha! Tremble in fear before my unstoppable Kohga Rocket!|Kohga|TotK}}</ref> He unleashed the rocket, but it misfired and ended up slamming into Kohga. The rocket soared out of control and sent Kohga flying out the depths — never to be seen again.<ref>{{Cite|It is unlikely he will appear in another abandoned mine. His manner of departure attests to that.|Steward Construct|TotK}}</ref>
