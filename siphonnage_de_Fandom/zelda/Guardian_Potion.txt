{{Term/Store|Guardian Potion|Guardian Potions|Series, SS|plural}} are items in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 126}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Guardian Potions can be bought at the [[Potion Shop#Skyward Sword|Potion Shop]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s. They can be stored in empty [[Bottle]]s and halve any damage received when drank.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|SS Gold|Guardian Potion}}! This mysterious potion halves any damage you receive! The effect lasts for {{Color|SS Red|three minutes}}.|N/A|SS}}</ref>

Guardian Potions can be upgraded at the potion shop by infusing them with various [[Bug]]s, preventing any damage for three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|SS Gold|Guardian Potion +}}! This mighty potion prevents you from sustaining any damage for {{Color|SS Red|three minutes}}!|N/A|SS}}</ref>
