{{Compendium/Extract|Dragonbone Moblin Club}}
{{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Moblin Club}} are two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} such as {{Plural|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Golden Moblin|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Stalmoblin|link}}. They are {{Plural|BotW|Moblin Club|link}} that have had ancient bones attached to them the make them more damaging in battle.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are commonly found in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Moblin Club}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is a series of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} crafted by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Dragonbone Moblin Club}} are not made out of metal, therefore equipping one will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They are made of wood, however, and will consequently catch {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} when exposed to open flame or equipped in the {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}. They will also float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but the are unable to be picked up by the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
