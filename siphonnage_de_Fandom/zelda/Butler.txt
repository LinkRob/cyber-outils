The {{Term/Store|Butler||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is the devoted guardian of the Cabana of the [[Private Oasis]].<ref>{{Cite|There was a very dignified {{Color|TWW Vermilion|butler}} standing before the door, and I snapped his photo without even thinking... That aside, I am certain this is someone's private {{Color|TWW Vermilion|cabana}}.|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TWWHD}} He is quite elitist and concerned with cleanliness,<ref>{{Cite|...And just what do you want me to do with that bit of rubbish!? You disrespectful, unwashed imp!|Butler|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ewww! Unclean! Most foul! Don't lay those grungy hands...|Butler|TWW}}</ref> and is, above all, loyal to "the master."<ref name="Trespassing">{{Cite|Eh-HEM! This cabana belongs to the master, and the master alone! You grimy, trespassing little scoundrel! Away with you! AWAY, I say!|Butler|TWW}}</ref> As it turns out, he's not a character of flesh and blood at all, but is actually the Cabana's front door.<ref>{{Cite|I am sorry to have bothered you with such a trifling thing! Most terribly sorry! Ah! All of a sudden, I have become painfully aware of my existence as a door. Wah! Waaaaaah!|Butler|TWW}}</ref>

==Biography==
When [[Link]] first arrives at the Cabana, the Butler is exceedingly rude and accuses him of trespassing, but if Link presents him with the [[Cabana Deed]], the Butler's attitude turns reverent.<ref name="Trespassing"/><ref>{{Cite|Why that's the... Are you... Are you the new master?! ...Are you Master Link? Ah... I am so pleased to hear that name!|Butler|TWW}}</ref> When in the presence of his master, he can actually be quite self-effacing,<ref>{{Cite|Bzzzt! Of course! Bzzzt! Sorry to bother you with such trifling things, Master! Awfully sorry!|Butler|TWW}}</ref> and manages to imply insulting things with graceful politeness.<ref>{{Cite|My goodness gracious... Master has some difficulty circulating blood to his brain, does he not? Most unfortunate. I shall explain again, Master.|Butler|TWW}}</ref>

The Butler is well versed in the [[Sliding Puzzle|amusements of nobility]] and he will explain the basics of the puzzle if Link asks.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, Master! Do you have an interest in the amusements of nobility? [...] Well, in that case, would you care to hear an explanation of the game?|Butler|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please tilt [+] up, down, left, or right to move the panels and try to arrange the picture. I will place the final panel in the top-right space to complete the painting. Oh, and Master! Please bear in mind that this game is for amusement only. If you are successful, all I can offer as a reward is money! Do remember that!|Butler|TWW}}</ref> If Link manages to complete a puzzle, he says he is "among the most talented gamesmiths of the noble classes" and will reward him with 50 [[Rupee]]s as an expression of his "respect and admiration" for his skills.<ref>{{Cite|Well done, Master... Well done, indeed! You are among the most talented gamesmiths of the noble classes! I offer you this as an expression of my respect and admiration of your skills. Please accept it!|Butler|TWW}}</ref> He has 16 different puzzles for Link to try, but asks Link to vacate the Cabana while he sets up the next one, so exactly how he manages to replace the pieces and the completed picture across from it is unknown.<ref>{{Cite|I must apologize. It will take me just a moment to prepare the next picture. Please, if you could briefly wait outside...|Butler|TWW}}</ref>
