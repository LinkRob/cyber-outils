"'''Midna's Lament'''" is a [[Song]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}, originating in {{TP}}. It is a popular and iconic track for its depressing and melodramatic feel.

==Overview==
"Midna's Lament" is the song that plays in {{TP}} when {{Term|TPHD|Wolf Link|link}} carries a dying {{Term|TPHD|Midna|link}} to {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}}, after [[Zant]] severely weakens her with light from [[Lanayru (Spirit)|Lanayru]].

While "Midna's Lament" is the main song in this scenario, the song is sometimes interrupted by the "Enemy Battle" theme.

===''Super Smash Bros. series''===
{{Noncanon}}
"{{Term|SSBB|Midna's Lament}}" plays in the {{Term|SSBB|Bridge of Eldin|section=Super Smash Bros. Series}} stage in both {{SSBB}}, and {{SSB4|Wii U}}.

A very different arrangement of "Midna's Lament" appears as a medley in {{SSBU}}, and plays in any Stage related to {{TLoZ|-}} series. Unlike in ''Brawl'' and ''SSB4'', which both use the track from the original game, the track in ''Ultimate'' has been changed to a much more upbeat and 
loud tone, with some orchestral voices, not sounding anything like the original emotional song from {{TP}}.
