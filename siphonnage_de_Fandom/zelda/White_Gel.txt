{{Term/Store|White Gel|White Gels|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: White Gel|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|White Gel}} are slime-like enemies that appear after a {{Term|CoH|White Zol|link}} is defeated. While weak, their true power lies in large numbers as they tend to appear in groups of four unless the tiles that they spawn on is blocked by an object. Like other enemies, they move to the beat of the music, hopping to an adjacent tile on every other beat while trying to make their way towards {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}}, and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} in order to attack them.
