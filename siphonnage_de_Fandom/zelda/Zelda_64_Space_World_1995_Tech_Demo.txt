''The'' ''Legend of Zelda'' "'''Space World 1995 Tech Demo'''" was a 3D demo created by programmer Giles Goddard on the [[Nintendo 64]] hardware. It was meant to showcase what a [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'']] game on the 64-bit cartridge home console could potentially look like. As it was made one year before the N64's release in 1996, the 11-second demo was likely a very early prototype of what would eventually become the then-revolutionary ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' in 1998. It simply shows [[Link]] (possibly the [[Hero of Time]], in an appearance based on ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]''/''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'') fighting and defeating an enemy in what appears to be a knight, for a brief moment of time.

==Overview==

[[File: Zelda 64 Space World 1995 Tech Demo.mp4|436px|right|<center>The Zelda 64 tech demo</center>]]

In describing the intent of the tech demo, Goddard stated: 

"When they were just first developing the prototypes for [''Ocarina of Time''], I think they just wanted to show something. Because they announced the game and they wanted to show that they actually had a game there, even though they had nothing. So we knocked up a game with [Yoshiaki] Koizumi-san. We knocked up a little demo of him fighting a metallic character with all these effects going on to show off. All these demos were always showing off some bit of new tech and I think at that point it was floating realtime lights. We had three lights that were swinging around, particle effects, and environment mapping."<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= When they were just first developing the prototypes for Zelda, I think they just wanted to show something. We knocked up a little demo of him fighting a metallic character with all these effects going on to show off. == Trivia ==

* This is the earliest appearance of [[Link]] rendered in three-dimensional graphics, alongside his [[Cameos of The Legend of Zelda in other media|cameo]] in ''[https://donkeykong.fandom.com/wiki/Donkey_Kong_Country_2:_Diddy%27s_Kong_Quest Donkey Kong Country 2: Diddy's Kong Quest]''. However, this is the first case in true 3D.
* The brief duel in the demo shows Link delivering a kick onto the enemy, something which does not occur in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]'' or most other ''[[The Legend of Zelda (series)|Zelda]]'' games. However, the character can perform such attacks in spin-off appearances, prominently the [[Super Smash Bros. (series)|''Super Smash Bros.'' series]] and ''[[SoulCalibur II]]''.
** As ''Ocarina of Time'' and ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_64|Super Mario 64]]'' from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' series]] were developed alongside each other by the exact same staff (coincidentally like ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' and ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros.|Super Mario Bros.]]'' for the [[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]]), the kick ability was likely transferred to the latter [[Nintendo 64|N64]] game instead, as [[Mario]] can perform such an attack against foes.
* Link's [[Sword]] and [[Shield]] in this demo are the same as the knight enemy. The textures of the weapons and foe are notably a glossy metal, which would later resemble that of [[mariowiki:Metal_Mario_(form)|Metal Mario]] from the ''Mario'' franchise.
