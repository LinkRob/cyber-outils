{{Term/Store|White Lizalfos|White Lizalfos|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: White Lizalfos|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|CoH|White Lizalfos}} are the strongest variety of sword-wielding {{Term|CoH|Lizalfos|link}}, possessing more health and attack power than the {{Term|Series|Green Lizalfos|link}} and {{Term|Series|Orange Lizalfos|link}}. However, they still share the same beat pattern as they move to the beat of the music. While they spend the majority of their time standing still, they will charge at {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}} with their sword withdrawn, charging on every single beat within a straight line until they collide with something. Once contact is made, they will be stunned and open for attack for three beats, and won't regain consciousness until the fourth. After regaining consciousness, they will either resume standing idle or charging again if the heroes are still in front of them.
