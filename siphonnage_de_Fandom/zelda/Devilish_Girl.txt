The {{Term/Store|Devilish Girl||Series}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.<ref name="Devilish Girl"></ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|ALBW|Devilish Girl}} is a girl who resides at the entrance of the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower|link}}. She waits for contenders to come and challenge the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower}}'s various levels of difficulty for a fee,<ref>{{Cite|Boom boom in the room! Glad to see you back here, sword boy! C'mon and give your luck a try! What's 100 Rupees these days? [Boom boom!] That's the spirit, sword boy! The rules are super simple! A bunch of baddies are gonna attack you. Just beat 'em! You look just like you can take care of yourself, but since it's your first time, just stick to the Beginner course. Just five stages, so it'll be a piece of boom-boom cake for you! That'll be 100 Rupees. Please and thank you! All right! Have fun in there! And do come back in one piece.|Devilish Girl|ALBW}}</ref> though she does not expect challengers to return alive.<ref>{{Cite|I forgot to tell you... Returning alive is NOT guaranteed! Enjoy!|Devilish Girl|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wow! You made it back! that's, like...crazy! Your clear time was XX minutes, XX seconds! And since you cleared this course, you can try the Intermediate course next time if you want to! The Intermediate course is 15 levels! Lots of baddies AND a piece of heart is waiting for you at the end! It only costs 200 Rupees to try it out! Have I sold you on it yet? Just lemme know if you want another go at it! The {{Term|ALBW|Devilish Girl}} gives {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} various things for completing the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower}}. For beating the Intermediate level the first time, she will give {{Term|ALBW|Link}} a {{Term|ALBW|Piece of Heart|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can't believe it! Well, I CAN believe it, but...I CAN'T believe it! Amazing! Your clear time was XX minutes, XX seconds! If you can beat your time on this course, I'll give you some extra Rupees for your pockets! And since you cleared this course for the first time, I have something special for you...|Devilish Girl|ALBW}}</ref> Upon completing the advanced level of the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower}} for the first time, the {{Term|ALBW|Devilish Girl}} will upgrade {{Term|ALBW|Link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Lamp|link}} to the {{Term|ALBW|Super Lamp|link}}, and for completing the advanced level for a second time, she will upgrade {{Term|ALBW|Link}}'s {{Term|ALBW|Net|link}} to the {{Term|ALBW|Super Net|link}}. For completing any previously beaten level of the {{Term|ALBW|Treacherous Tower}} with better times, she will give {{Term|ALBW|Link}} {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|A new record! Boom boom all around! Here's an extra bonus for you!|Devilish Girl|ALBW}}</ref>
