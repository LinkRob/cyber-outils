{{BasePageLink|Sheikah Language}}
The [[Sheikah (Language)|Sheikah Language]] appears in {{BotW}} and {{HWAoC}}. Certain Sheikah text reappear in {{TotK}} such as the [[Purah Pad]]. As its name implies, the Sheikah Language appears exclusively on {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} architecture and technology.

The Sheikah Language is based on English, as its letters are a cipher of the Latin alphabet.
