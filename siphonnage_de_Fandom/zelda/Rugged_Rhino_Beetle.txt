==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Rugged Rhino Beetle}}
Rugged Rhino Beetles can be found resting on trees in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the [[Faron Grasslands]]. [[Cooking]] one with {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} in a [[Cooking Pot]] will create a [[Tough Elixir]], which will temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} defense. Alternatively, they can be traded with {{Term|BotW|Beedle|link}} for a random [[Food]] or [[Elixir]]. The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] accepts Rugged Rhino Beetles as an ingredient for Green [[Dye]].
