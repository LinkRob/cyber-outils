{{Term/Store|Sumati||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sumati|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Sumati}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} who can be found in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}'s bar, {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} goes into the Canteen, he will find {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} accompanied by two other {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} named {{Term|BotW|Pyra|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Yaido|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to one of them, they talk to each other about a password for a secret place.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I was going to tell you... I finally got my hands on the {{Color|BotW Blue|password}} for you-know-where.|Yaido|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Seriously? Wow... That place is a little crazy, isn't it?|Sumati|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Maybe, but I'm really interested...|Pyra|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anyway, the password is...|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> They stop talking about it as soon as they notice that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is listening and {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} tells him to go away.<ref>{{Cite|Wait a second! You!! Did  you really think we wouldn't see you standing there, listening to our conversation?!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Private">{{Cite|So rude! This is a private conversation! Go away!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can eavesdrop on them by going onto the opposite side of the nearby window in {{Term|BotW|Rotana|link}}'s house. 

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} eavesdrops on them here before ever speaking to them while in {{Term|BotW|The Noble Canteen}}, he will hear {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} asking the others if they have heard of a rumor about {{Term|BotW|Fashion Passion|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, have you heard the {{Color|BotW Red|rumor about Fashion Passion}}?|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} replies that she has heard the rumor and {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} responds by saying that she asked {{Term|BotW|Saula|link}} about it,<ref>{{Cite|I have!|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> but {{Term|BotW|Saula}} wouldn't tell her anything.<ref>{{Cite|I tried asking Saula about it indirectly, but she wouldn't tell me anything.|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pyra}} then mentions how suspicious it is that {{Term|BotW|Saula}} won't give away any information.<ref>{{Cite|Doesn't Saula work at Fashion Passion? Geez, that's extremely suspicious...|Pyra|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} is eavesdropping on the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} women after speaking to them directly, he will overhear {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} referring to him as a little {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} girl.<ref>{{Cite|What was up with that little Hylian vai earlier, anyway?|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Pyra}} then says to forget about the girl and urges {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} to tell them the password.<ref>{{Cite|Forget her! Just hurry up and tell us the password, Yaido!|Pyra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} reveals that she overheard {{Term|BotW|Saula}} say that the password is "GSC♦."<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's right, the password! It's {{Color|BotW Red|GSC♦}}. I overheard Saula talking about it, so I'm sure that's right!|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> Sumati quickly realizes that it must stand for "Gerudo Secret Club" before the conversation ends.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|GSC♦}}? What could that... Oh, I get it. Gerudo Secret Club—GSC. But what's the {{Color|BotW Red|♦}} at the end mean?|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> The password that they reveal allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} entry to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club|link}}.

After {{Term|BotW|Link}} calms {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link|display=Naboris}}, he gains entry to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Secret Club}} and speaks to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} women again, {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} will question where {{Term|BotW|Perda|link}} is.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder what {{Color|BotW Blue|Perda}}'s up to...|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} says that she left in search of {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Pyra}} comments that it is rumored to be the perfect place to find true love.<ref>{{Cite|Didn't she say she was going to look for {{Color|BotW Red|Lover's Pond}}?|Sumati|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The place that's supposed to be perfect for meeting your one true love or some nonsense?|Pyra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} then proceeds to say something,<ref>{{Cite|That's right! I've heard of that place before. Did you know...|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> but is interrupted when {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to go away.<ref name="Private"/> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can eavesdrop on them again from the window in {{Term|BotW|Rotana}}'s house to hear them talk about {{Term|BotW|Perda}} in more detail. While listening, {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} wonders how {{Term|BotW|Perda}} is doing and {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} comments about how enthusiastic she was to find {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond}}. <ref>{{Cite|I wonder how Perda's doing...|Yaido|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|She was so enthusiastic about searching for {{Color|BotW Red|Lover's Pond}}...|Sumati|BotW}}</ref> Yaido then talks about how quickly she left when she heard that {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond}} is in {{Term|BotW|Faron (Region)|link|display=Faron}}.<ref>{{Cite|And she took off so quickly when she heard it was somewhere in {{Color|BotW Red|Faron}}...|Yaido|BotW}}</ref> This surprises {{Term|BotW|Pyra}}, who thought it was only a rumor.<ref>{{Cite|What? It's definitely in {{Color|BotW Red|Faron}}? I was pretty sure that was just a rumor...|Pyra|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sumati}} says that she does not know for sure, but her and {{Term|BotW|Pyra}} conclude that {{Term|BotW|Perda}} must have found true love because she has not returned yet.<ref>{{Cite|Obviously I don't know for sure. But she hasn't come back yet, which can only mean one thing...|Sumati|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That she met her true love?|Pyra|BotW}}</ref> At the end of the conversation, {{Term|BotW|Yaido}} contemplates if she should also travel to {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond}}.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe I should go out there, too...|Yaido|BotW}}</ref>
