{{Term/Store|Modar||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Modar|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Modar}} is a {{Term|BotW|Goron|link}} who resides at the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}. He wears a {{Term|BotW|Helmet|link}} on his head, and he carries a {{Term|BotW|Mallet|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Modar}} operates the {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}. He invented the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} after rolling down a hill, making him the only person to play it.<ref name="Invented">{{Cite|I invented it the other day rolling down the hill, so I'm the only one who's played it. And I love it ta smithereens! I guess it makes sense that ya haven't heard about it, come ta that.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he claims that it is famous and anyone who plays it is unable to get enough.<ref name="Famous">{{Cite|[What is it?] Ya don't know {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}}?! It's famous! No one who's played it even once can get enough!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Modar}}, he is surprised to see a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} and reasons that he must be tough.<ref>{{Cite|Holy moly! A Hylian? Here?! Ya must be tough as iron ta survive the trip out this way!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Due to this, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is interested in trying out {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}}.<ref>{{Cite|Getting here must mean ya have the stones ta strut yer stuff in a round of... {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}}! Well? Are ya in?|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to play {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} at any time, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will be disappointed, but he welcomes {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back in the future.<ref>{{Cite|Aww, that ain't fun. The course is always open, though. Yer welcome anytime!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what it is, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} is shocked that he has never heard of it.<ref name="Famous"/> However, after explaining that he only invented it a few days ago, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} realizes that it makes sense for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be unfamiliar with it.<ref name="Invented"/> He explains that the goal is to hit the {{Term|BotW|Ball|link}} into the hole at the end of the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course}}, which is marked by a {{Term|BotW|Big Flag|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It couldn't be simpler ta play! Whack the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} and try to knock it inta the hole at that flag.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will score higher the fewer tries it takes him to achieve this, and it costs 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} to participate.<ref>{{Cite|The fewer tries it takes, the higher yer score! The best part? It only costs {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} ta play!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

To send the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} a great distance, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} suggests using two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, such as {{Plural|BotW|Hammer|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Two-handed weapons like hammers can really send the ball flyin', but ya gotta take care not ta overdo it.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> To move the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} a short distance, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} says to use one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, but he must be careful not to break them.<ref>{{Cite|Swords and one-handed weapons are better for short distances, but be careful ya don't break 'em playin' the game!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} is knocked off the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will inflict a one-stroke penalty from {{Term|BotW|Link}}, and it will be returned to its previous location.<ref>{{Cite|If the ball rolls off the course, ya take a {{Color|BotW Red|one-stroke penalty}} and have ta keep going from where ya hit it.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will keep track of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s strokes, but he must hit the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} into the hole in less than 20.<ref>{{Cite|I'll keep track of how many {{Color|BotW Red|strokes}} it takes. Yer job is to get the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} in the hole in {{Color|BotW Red|20 strokes}} or less!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that the further he can hit the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} along the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course|display=Course}}, the better it will be for his score.<ref>{{Cite|How ya hit the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} is yer call. But the further ya can smash it with every smack, the better!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Finally, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} explains that any movement from the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} counts as a stroke, at which point he realizes that the rules are not as simple as he thought.<ref>{{Cite|If the {{Color|BotW Red|ball}} moves even an inch, that counts as {{Color|BotW Red|a stroke}}. Huh, I guess those rules ain't so simple after all. Whoops!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he will play {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}}.<ref>{{Cite|So are ya in or are ya in?|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to play {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}}, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will be excited.<ref>{{Cite|[It's time!] Say no more! It's time ta tee off! {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}} is go!}}|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Before they can start, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to pay the fee of 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|At least, once I get that {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} for the greens fee. Browns fee? Ehh.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} does so, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will take a moment to set up the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rock on! I'll start setting up!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} will begin.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Modar}} during the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}}, he assumes that he wants to stop playing.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? Yer throwin' in the towel already? Say it ain't so!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses to give up, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} states that {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf|display=Boom Bam Golfers}} never say die.<ref>{{Cite|[Me? Never!] Attaboy! True {{Big|Boom Bam Golfers}} never say die!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Similarly, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} leaves the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} calls out and warns him that the game will end if he goes any further.<ref>{{Cite|Where ya goin'? It ain't quittin' time already, is it? Go too far off course and I'll hafta call the game there. Ya won't get back yer entry fee, neither. So watch it!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} ignore this and continue to leave the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course|display=Course}}, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will disqualify him, telling him that he should have said something if he wanted to stop playing.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, kid, but I hafta {{Color|BotW Red|disqualify}} ya. Them's the rules! If ya wanted to quit, ya should said so! Come back when yer feelin' serious. The {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}} course is always open! See ya around!|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not reach the goal in 20 strokes, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will cut him off.<ref>{{Cite|Whatta sorry showing... That's 20 strokes, pal. I'm cuttin' ya off there. Try again sometime, all right?|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He is saddened to see someone accumulate 20 strokes, especially since he was rooting for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hate to see a guy go to 20 strokes. Just so's ya know, I was rootin' for ya.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes a round, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will bring him back to the beginning of the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon Course}} to tally his score. If it took {{Term|BotW|Link}} 11 or more strokes to reach the goal, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will be disappointed, saying that {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} is not that hard.<ref>{{Cite|Yer score was... {{Color|BotW Red|XX}}?! Sheesh, kid. {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}} ain't easy, but it ain't THAT hard!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} not to give up and encourages him to keep practicing.<ref>{{Cite|Aw, buddy. Well...don't give up, OK? Ya just need ta practice, practice, and then practice some more.|Modar|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits the {{Term|BotW|Ball}} into the goal in six to 10 strokes, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} states his belief that he can do better.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... Ya got a {{Color|BotW Red|XX}} this time out.<br/>That ain't bad, but ya strike me as a guy who could do better.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that it takes time to become good at something, and he gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Don't worry, the game takes some practice ta figger out. They say {{Color|BotW Red|every journey starts with a single stroke}}!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the goal in four to five shots, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} remarks that he is becoming a heavyweight.<ref>{{Cite|Yer {{Color|BotW Red|score}} was...{{Color|BotW Red|X}}! Yer gettin' ta be a real heavyweight, kid.|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to keep trying to set the {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} record, giving him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Don't go thinkin' ya got it all figgered out, now! Keep at it, and set the highest record ever in {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}}!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} reaches the goal in three or less strokes, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will be impressed, saying he could be a professional if there were {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} leagues.<ref>{{Cite|Ya got {{Color|BotW Red|X}}?! Well, don't that beat all! Ya could be a pro {{Big|Boom Bam Golfer!}} {{Small|If there was a league...or other players...}}|Modar|BotW}}</ref> While he cannot recall his exact score, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} believes it was close to {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa...I lost track of my {{Color|BotW Red|stroke count}}. If I remember right, though, it was about the same as yers, give or take!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He thinks that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is the type of player who could make {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} popular, and he encourages him to keep playing.<ref>{{Cite|Yer the kinda player who's gonna make {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}} hit the big time! Keep it up, and smash those high scores!|Modar|BotW}}</ref> He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.

After giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} his reward, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} believes thinks that {{Term|BotW|Link}} looks like he wants to play {{Term|BotW|Boom Bam Golf}} again.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Ya look like ya wanna go again! Heck, who am I ta stop ya?|Modar|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} refuses, {{Term|BotW|Modar}} will be disappointed, but he is pleased that {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to see the majesty of the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game}} for himself.<ref>{{Cite|Whatta shame. But at least now ya've seen the majesty of {{Big|{{Color|BotW Blue|Boom Bam Golf}}}} for yerself, right?|Modar|BotW}}</ref>
