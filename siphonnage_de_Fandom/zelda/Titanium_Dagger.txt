{{Term/Store|Titanium Dagger|Titanium Daggers|Series, CoH|plural}} are items in {{CoH}}.<ref name>{{Cite|{{Color|CoH Yellow|Titanium Dagger}} {{Icon|CoH Zelda}}{{Icon|CoH Cadence}}<br/>Deals {{Color|CoH Red|2 damage}} to a single enemy in front of you.|Inventory|CoH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Titanium Daggers are a stronger variant of {{Term|CoH|Dagger|link}} that always deal 2 damage to a single enemy. They can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}, and thus can only be acquired either after they infuse the Dagger with {{Term|CoH|Titanium|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, or if either them or {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} opens the blue {{Term|CoH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing an already-infused one within {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Desert|link}}.

They function identically to the {{Term|CoH|Titanium Short Sword|link}}.
