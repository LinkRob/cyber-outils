The {{Term/Store|Den of Trials||Series, TFH}} is a location in {{TFH}},<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=161 }}</ref> added through the Version 2.0.0 update.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote='''Ver. 2.0.0 (Released December 2, 2015)'''<br/><br/>Den of Trials will be added. |url=https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/15571/~/how-to-update-the-legend-of-zelda%3A-tri-force-heroes |title=How to Update The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes |site=Nintendo Support}}</ref> It is designated as the 9th {{Term|TFH|Area|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} and contains 40 floors based on the previous {{Plural|TFH|Area}}. At the bottom of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials}} rests the {{Term|TFH|Fierce Deity Armor|link}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
To access the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials}}, {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} must complete nine {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and/or {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}}, then return to {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, where {{Term|TFH|The Great Tripini|link}} tells the young hero about the newly-discovered {{Term|TFH|Area|link}} and the powerful {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} said to be found within.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, uh, hi there, little hero dude! Hey, have you heard? They've finished surveying a new, uh, Drablands area. You can, ah, go on a trip there, too. I hear it's called, the, ah... Yeah. The {{Color|TFH Blue|Den of Trials}}.|The Great Tripini|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ol' Trip hears, uh, stuff. Like about a one-­of­-a­-kind outfit at the, um, bottom floor of the {{Color|TFH Blue|Den of Trials}}... That's mighty...ah...awesome, right?|The Great Tripini|TFH}}</ref> Though the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials}} cannot be unlocked or selected through [[Download Play]], the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can still access the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|display=Den}} if the "host" {{Term|TFH|Link}} selects it. However, the other {{Plural|TFH|Link}} will not be able to obtain the {{Term|TFH|Fierce Deity Armor}} in this mode.

The Den of Trials is divided into portions called Zones, with each portioned Zone being based on the [[Woodlands]], [[Riverside]], [[Volcano]], [[Ice Cavern]], [[Fortress]], [[The Dunes]], [[The Ruins]] and the [[Sky Realm]] respectively. Each Zone contains five Floors and is accessible from a central hub area with eight {{Plural|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}}. The Floors contain {{Plural|TFH|Enemy|link}} that must all be defeated in order to activate the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} to advance to the next Floor.<ref>{{Cite|­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­­ Hey, it's the little hero! Hey. Yeah. Looks like this is the {{Color|TFH Blue|Den of Trials}} we've been, ah, hearing about... And, uh, it looks like there's some special rules in this place that, um, set it apart from other places.<br/>[…]<br/>They say the {{Color|TFH Blue|Den of Trials}} has, ah, 40 underground floors. Like...whoa. Yeah. You grab the treasure in each of the zones, and that, um, that completes it. And then you get to go deeper down. From the topmost {{Color|TFH Blue|Forest Zone}} to the, uh, {{Color|TFH Blue|Baneful Zone}} at the bottom... Sounds like a long way to, ah, go. Oh, but, ah! If you can complete a zone, then you'll be able to restart from there. So don't, uh, stress, OK?|The Great Tripini|TFH}}</ref> According to {{Term|TFH|The Great Tripini}}, all of the {{Plural|TFH|Fairy|link}} that accompany the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} flee in fear of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials}}, leaving them with only one to start with.<ref>{{Cite|I hear all the, uh, fairies ran away in fear. Yeah. So you only start with, ah, just one! They say there are more, um, hidden away in...uh...oh yeah. In treasure chests in safe rooms.|The Great Tripini|TFH}}</ref> However, by reaching the "safe room" on the fifth Floor of a Zone, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can obtain another {{Term|TFH|Fairy}} trapped in a {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} after collecting the {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} from that Zone and [[Saving]] their progress. Each safe room also contains a {{Term|TFH|Squiddy|link}} from which supplementary {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} can be retrieved before descending further. When a Zone is completed, its corresponding {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}} in the hub area becomes activated. If the entire {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials}} is completed in a single session, beginning from the first Floor, then the final safe room will contain eight {{Plural|TFH|Treasure Chest|display=Chests}} with more valuable {{Plural|TFH|Material}} than the usual three.

A party cannot enter any Zones that has not been reached by the other participant {{Plural|TFH|Links}}, forcing them to begin from the lowest common Zone available.<ref>{{Cite|Where you can go depends on, ah, the allies you’ve brought with you, it seems. You can only take a trip to the zone just after the deepest one that all fellow heroes have completed. Oh Yeah. It looks like you haven’t completed any yet... That’s OK, dude. Everyone has to start, ah, somewhere. You’ll just have to start at the start. Yeah! That’ll be the, uh, Forest Zone. The Den of Trials is a confusing place. Is there, ah, anything else you wanna know about it?|The Great Tripini|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials}} features several "dark" variants of common {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} as well as new {{Plural|TFH|Enemy}} that were not present in the other {{Plural|TFH|Area}} of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. The final Floor features a triumvirate of {{Plural|TFH|Shadow Link|link}}, equipped with powered up versions of the weapons available.

==Trivia==
*For each consecutive safe room that the Links reach, a new congratulatory message will scroll across the screen. These messages will accumulate and reappear at each safe room thereafter, eventually covering most of the screen. The messages for each consecutive safe room are as follows:
#No message
#"'''Well THAT was unexpected!'''"
#"'''You're unstoppable, snickerdoodle! ♥'''"
#"'''Oh!!'''"
#"'''Again? How?! Tell me, tell me!'''"
#"'''Unbelievable, dahling!'''"
#"'''My awe has been inspired!'''"
#"'''You're legendary, chum! Really!'''"
*If the Links reach all eight safe rooms consecutively, they will also be greeted by a loud round of applause, followed by an audible cheer from [[Sir Combsly]] and the onscreen message "'''Good work, soldier!'''"
