==Characteristics==
{{Plural|ZA|Jackaroo}} are dinosaurian-like enemies that bare a slight resemblance to {{Wp|Velociraptor}}s. They are only encountered in the {{Term|ZA|Shrine of Illusion}}; first when {{Term|ZA|Princess Zelda|link}} slides down a slide into a cage containing a group of them at the beginning of the Shrine, and again in another cage just before the boss room where {{Term|ZA|Pasquinade|link}} is fought. Like most {{Plural|ZA|Enemy}} in the game, {{Plural|ZA|Jackaroo}} simply wander aimlessly around their enclosure while making roaring sounds. They can easily be defeated with three strikes of the {{Term|ZA|Wand}}.
