{{Compendium/Extract|Moblin Spear}}
{{Plural|BotW|Moblin Spear}} are wooden {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} carried by regular {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}, particularly in the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}. They are roughly fashioned and created from whittling {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}}, and they therefore have both a low {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}} and little strength.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

Being made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Moblin Spear}} will float if they are dropped in the {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. However, they will also burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or equipped in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they are unable to be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
