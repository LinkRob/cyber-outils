==Location and Uses==
==={{TMC|-}}===
The {{Term|TMC|Bow of Light}} is obtained alongside the {{Plural|TMC|Light Arrow|link}} in {{TMC}}. To obtain them, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} must {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}}, a man who lives in a house in western {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. This will open a portal in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}} that leads to the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Goodness! We rarely get visitors here. Tell me, how did you get here?|Hailey|TMC}}</ref> Here, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can use the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}} to suck in the ghost circling {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}}, saving his life; if {{Term|TMC|Link}} visits {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} again after reaching the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}} through {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} will thank him with the {{Plural|TMC|Light Arrow}} and the {{Term|TMC|Bow of Light}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, it's Link! Thank you for your help earlier! I've got something else good for you, so here, please take it!|Gregal|TMC}}</ref> If {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} is not saved before the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}} are reached via {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls}}, he will have passed away and the {{Term|TMC|Bow of Light}} will be impossible to obtain. Holding an {{Term|TMC|Arrow|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Bow of Light}} will charge up a {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow}}; if the {{Plural|TMC|Light Arrow}} are not charged, they will act as normal {{Plural|TMC|Arrow}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
:''See also: [[Zelda's Bow#Twilight Princess|Zelda's Bow]]''
The {{Term|TPHD|Bow of Light}} appears in the horseback phase of {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf|link}}'s battle in {{TP}}. After {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda|link}} asks the {{Plural|TPHD|Spirit of Light|link}} for a weapon against {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}}, she receives {{Plural|TPHD|Light Arrow}} for the {{Term|TPHD|Bow of Light}}. When riding on {{Term|TPHD|Epona|link}}, {{Term|TPHD|Link}} must lock on to {{Term|TPHD|Ganondorf}} so {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda}} can fire the {{Term|TPHD|Bow of Light}} at him, stunning him and allowing {{Term|TPHD|Link}} to attack him with the {{Term|TPHD|Master Sword|link}}.

This version reappears in Breath of the Wild as the [[Twilight Bow]]. It has 30 base Attack and 100 base Durability. It is one of only 2 bows that fire Light Arrows the other of course being the BotW Bow of Light received in the final battle. Neither can fire the other arrows, and Light Arrows are generated by the bow itself, so they are infinite. This version can only be acquired when scanning the Smash series Zelda amiibo. It's randomly in the chest that drops, and can only appear if you've defeated at least 1 Divine Beast.

==={{ST|-}}===
The {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}} is obtained after defeating {{Term|ST|Skeldritch|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Sand Temple|link}} in {{ST}}. The {{Term|ST|Bow of Light|display=Bow}} was once wielded by the {{Plural|ST|Spirit of Good|link}} during their war against the Demon King, {{Term|ST|Malladus|link}}.<ref name= "Battle">{{Cite|In the battle between the Demon King and the spirits, there was a weapon called the Bow of Light... Perhaps it can drive his spirit from your body.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> Following the war, the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}} was placed in the depths of the {{Term|ST|Sand Temple}} deep in the heart of the {{Term|ST|Sand Realm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Go to the final temple, the Sand Temple. The Bow of Light is located deep inside.|Anjean|ST}}</ref> If the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}} is held down long enough, [[Light]] will be infused into an {{Term|ST|Arrow}}, turning it into a {{Term|ST|Light Arrow}}. If released quickly, the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light|display=Bow}} will shoot a normal {{Term|ST|Arrow}}.

In the battle within the {{Term|ST|Dark Realm|link}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} must use the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}} to shoot {{Term|ST|Malladus}} while {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} holds him in place, banishing {{Term|ST|Malladus}}' presence from {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}}'s body. When {{Term|ST|Malladus}} takes over the body of {{Term|ST|Chancellor Cole|link}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} must distract the Demon King long enough for {{Term|ST|Princess Zelda}} to shoot a weak spot on his back with the {{Term|ST|Bow of Light}}, eventually creating an opening for {{Term|ST|Link}} to destroy {{Term|ST|Malladus}}' two horns protecting a crystal on his head.<ref>{{Cite|You distract Malladus, Link. When I see an opportunity, I'll shoot him in the back with the Bow of Light.|Princess Zelda|ST}}</ref> This leaves the crystal exposed, allowing {{Term|ST|Link}} to destroy it with the {{Term|ST|Lokomo Sword|link}}.

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Bow of Light}} is obtained during the fight against {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon|link}} in {{ALBW}}. After being successfully struck, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} [[Wall Merging|merges]] into a wall, thus becoming invulnerable. At this point, {{Term|ALBW|Princess Zelda}}, speaking through her {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}}, gives {{Term|ALBW|Link}} the {{Term|ALBW|Bow of Light}}. While wall merged, {{Term|ALBW|Link}} must shoot {{Plural|ALBW|Light Arrow}} into {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}}'s back to stun him, at which point he is forced to emerge from the wall and is left vulnerable to {{Term|ALBW|Sword|link}} strikes. Once this process is repeated twice more, {{Term|ALBW|Yuga Ganon}} is defeated.

The {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light}} is given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} before the final battle against {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="BotW Inventory"/> It has an attack power of 100, fires infinite {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow|link}}, and has infinite durability. Once obtained, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must wait for {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}} to weaken {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon|display=Ganon}}'s power, then shoot {{Plural|BotW|Light Arrow}} with the {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light}} in the weak spots she creates. Once {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon}} is sufficiently weakened, an {{Term|BotW|Eye of Malice|link}} representing his very core and essence will appear on his forehead. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} shoots {{Term|BotW|Dark Beast Ganon}}'s core with the {{Term|BotW|Bow of Light}}, it will destroy his physical form.

The {{Term|TPHD|Bow of Light}} used by {{Term|TPHD|Princess Zelda}} in {{TP|-}} can also be unlocked as a weapon by using the {{SSB|-}} series Zelda {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}}; however, it is referred to as the {{Term|BotW|Twilight Bow|link}}.
