The {{Term/Store|Southern Mine||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Southern Mine|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Southern Mine is a small quarry that sits on the road leading to [[Goron City]] in west [[Eldin Canyon]]. It is denoted as [[Death Mountain]] Marker #3.<ref>{{Cite|Death Mountain Marker #3<br/>Southern Mine|Sign|BotW}}</ref> The Mine is nestled in a crag where the [[Goron]]s mine for [[Gem]]s, which are sold and keeps Goron City prosperous.<ref>{{Cite|The reason Goron City is so prosperous is because of all the work we do.|Dorill|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm a proud Goron of Goron City. If we dig up {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}} like this, Goron City will prosper. Everyone wins!|Jengo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We sell the {{Color|BotW Blue|ore}} we pull from the ground. Our home, Goron City, profits from that.|Bohrin|BotW}}</ref> Originally, the Gorons used to mine at the [[Death Mountain Summit]] before relocating to the Southern Mine by [[Bludo]]'s advisory when the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}} appeared.<ref>{{Cite|We used to mine by {{Color|BotW Blue|the summit}}, but with that {{Color|BotW Blue|lizard monster}} launchin' those {{Color|BotW Blue|magma bombs}} at everything... We just decided to make the most outta diggin' here.|Bohrin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We're diggin' this site out under direct orders from the Boss of Goron City. No time to chat! Gotta dig!|Dorill|BotW}}</ref> Amenities for when the Gorons are not mining, including tarp tents and a lit [[Cooking Pot]], are set up for use between shifts. A number of [[Ore Deposit]]s can be found around the Mine, which can be broken to yield Gems. The Mine also has a high abundance of [[Fireproof Lizard]]s, some of which can be found hiding beneath rocks.

During the day, the Gorons [[Dorill]], [[Jengo]] and [[Greyson]] mine for Gems, while the Goron children [[Axyl]] and [[Pelison]] play. The elderly Goron [[Bohrin]] sleeps through the day beneath a tarp, before waking up at night to work the night shift while the other Gorons rest.<ref>{{Cite|I'm goin' to sleep now. I work the night shift, so if I don't rest now, it'll be tough to get through the night.|Bohrin|BotW}}</ref> A [[Hylian]] man named [[Kima]] can also be found at the Mine, wearing a [[Flamebreaker Helm]] to protect himself from the heat. Kima wants ten Fireproof Lizards as a souvenir, and promises to give Link a [[Flamebreaker Armor]] if he brings him enough, initiating the "[[Fireproof Lizard Roundup]]" [[Side Quest]].<ref>{{Cite|I'm on my way back from {{Color|BotW Blue|Goron City}} but forgot to buy souvenirs.  I wanted to capture some of those {{Color|BotW Blue|fireproof lizards}} that live here on Death Mountain, but those suckers are quick. Say you look pretty speedy. I bet you could catch a ton! If you bring me {{Color|BotW Red|10}}, I'll even give you some heat-resistant armor.|Kima|BotW}}</ref>

One of the Goron miners, Greyson, expresses that he is tired of mining and desires a job where he could use his strength to make an actual difference.<ref>{{Cite|I'm a digger here, as you can see. I dig and I dig... Day in and day out... To be honest, I'm sick of it. I feel like...maybe I'm not meant to toil away in obscurity like this. *sigh* Is there a job out there for me that'd let me use my {{Color|BotW Red|strength}} to make a difference?|Greyson|BotW}}</ref> As part of the "[[From the Ground Up]]" [[Side Quest]], he can be invited to move to [[Tarrey Town]] in [[Akkala]] to help [[Hudson]] demolish its boulders. Greyson leaves with his brother Pelison, leaving the other Gorons to work at the Mine.
