{{Term/Store|The Eighth Heroine||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Eighth Heroine}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Desert}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can begin this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Bozai|link}} while wearing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} can be found sometimes running around the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} in front of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. With the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit}} on, Bozai will think {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a woman and become interested in him. {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} will introduce himself to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, making note that he is single and loves jogging on sand.<ref>{{Cite|So yeah, hey, the name's Bozai. I'm 35, single, and I LOVE jogging. Especially on sand.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He claims that people say he is tenacious,<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, everyone says I'm tenacious as a tick! And I think some of them meant it in a nice way, too!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> and so he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants to spend some time with him.<ref>{{Cite|S-so...you out here on your own? Pretty rough and tumble place... Wanna hang out with me awhile?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

However, instead of answering, {{Term|BotW|Link}} notices how well he is running on the sand, which {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} attributes to his {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nice sand-jogging!] Ah... You noticed these old things? Yeah, they're my {{Color|BotW Blue|sand boots}}. They allow me to traverse the desert without being slowed down at all, so jogging is a breeze.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He once again tries to coerce {{Term|BotW|Link}} into spending some time alone with him.<ref>{{Cite|If you'd like to check themout, we could grab a quiet corner and...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> but he is thrown off by him asking for the {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Gimme those boots.] Huh?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Though he is initially hesitant due to their rarity,<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Well... Here's the thing. These {{Color|BotW Blue|sand boots}} are super rare, and I'd happily give them to you, but...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> he changes his mind when he realizes he could use this to woo {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|... {{Small|...Wait. This is a golden chance to woo...}}|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bozai}} agrees to give the {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}} in exchange for a favor.<ref>{{Cite|I mean, sure! I'd LOVE to give them to you, you hungry little boot monster! But first, a favor|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has ever heard of the {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ever heard the legend of the {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He explains that while everyone has heard of the {{Term|BotW|Seven Heroines|link}}, there is also a legend of an {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}} in the northern {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}} that has been wiped from history.<ref>{{Cite|Everyone's heard the legend in the Gerudo region about the seven heroines, but...some tell of an {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wiped clean from history, and no one knows why or how...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|<nowiki>*</nowiki>ahem!* According to those who idolize the {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}, she is enshrined somewhere in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Highlands}}.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} promises to give {{Term|BotW|Link}} his {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots}} if he shows him a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}}.<ref>{{Cite|Find her! Show me the {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}, and I'll give you these {{Color|BotW Blue|sand boots}}. What do you say?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anyway, draw a picture or something if you find her! And then show me!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} also suggests that there might be someone in Gerudo Town who knows more about the legend.<ref>{{Cite|Who knows, there might be someone in {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Town}} who knows more about the legend of the seven heroines.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Before he finishes speaking, {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} lends {{Term|BotW|Link}} his pair of {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots|link}} for faster traversal through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and here... If you'd like, you can use these. I'm sure they'll come in handy. Besides, you seem really into boots.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Once this is done, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} begins.

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} goes into the palace in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} and feeds a piece of fruit to {{Term|BotW|Riju|link|display=Riju's}} {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Patricia|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} may receive additional information about the {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}}. {{Term|BotW|Padda|link}} interprets {{Term|BotW|Patricia}}'s words, saying that {{Term|BotW|Risoka Snowfield|link}} rests upon the {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}}'s shoulders.<ref>{{Cite|"{{Color|BotW Blue|Risoka Snowfield}} rests upon the shoulders of the eighth heroine..." A rare, pun-free prophecy...|Padda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Risoka Snowfield}} is located in the northwest section of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands}}. On the northern edge of the {{Term|BotW|Risoka Snowfield|display=Snowflied}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|link}}.

After taking a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|display=Statue's}} torso with his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} to show him the picture. {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} is glad to see {{Term|BotW|Link}} is safe after starting to worry he would never return.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Oh, thank goodness! You made it back safely! You were gone for so long, I started to fear the worst...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He admits that the {{Term|BotW|Eighth Heroine}} does not exist and only used it as an excuse to get close to {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I have to come clean. The {{Color|BotW Blue|eighth heroine}} is just an urban legend—it doesn't actually exist... I... Well, I just wanted an excuse to get close to you, y'know?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Since his excuse put {{Term|BotW|Link}} in harm's way,<ref>{{Cite|I wanted to keep you engaged so I just thought up something impossible to hold your interest. And as a result, I put you in harm's way! I'm a cad! And I've been so, so worried...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} vows not to put him in danger anymore and to be his protector.<ref>{{Cite|I won't put you in danger anymore! From now on, I'll be your protect—|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} interrupts to say that he found the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|display=Statue}}, {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} will think that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is merely trying to console hi.,<ref>{{Cite|[Found it.] Are you trying to console me because I've been so heartsick over this?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} a kind soul, but he insists there is no need to coddle him.<ref>{{Cite|You're such a kind soul. But no need to coddle me. From now on I'll—|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} interrupts {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} again to show him the {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of the {{Term|BotW|Statue of the Eighth Heroine|display=Statue}},<ref>{{Cite|[Look at this!] That...certainly is...the way it was rumored to look...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} is shocked to actually exists.<ref>{{Cite|That— {{Big|THAT'S AMAZING!}} I thought it was just an old wives' tale! The {{Color|BotW Blue|eighth heroine}} actually exists?!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> He promises to keep it a secret as a way for the two of them to bond.<ref>{{Cite|...But I won't tell anyone... Yeah, that way, it'll just be our little secret, right? Sort of a bonding thing.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Bozai}} begins to ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} for romantic involvement,<ref>{{Cite|And yeah, i-if it's OK, I'd like to take this opportunity to ask you—|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> before {{Term|BotW|Link}} interrupts him, asking for the {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots}} he was promised.<ref>{{Cite|[Sand boots, please!] Oh... Oh, OK...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} takes the {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots}} off and gives them to {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Just a second. I'll take them off...|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> telling him to treat them tenderly.<ref>{{Cite|Treat them as tenderly as you would me.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} then requests his {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}} back, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} obliges.<ref>{{Cite|*ahem* And now, could you be a lamb and return my {{Color|BotW Blue|snow boots}}? Otherwise ol' Bozai's going to be barefoot!|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Before leaving, {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to join him later at the nearby tent.<ref>{{Cite|I think I'll just park myself under the tent in front of Gerudo Town and take five. Care to join me later?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} says bye without answering the question,<ref>{{Cite|[Good-bye.] Huh? Aw... Good... Good-bye.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> and the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed.
