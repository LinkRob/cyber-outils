The {{Term/Store|Kokiri Tunic||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 129}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|OoT3D}}
The {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Tunic}} is the set of clothes that the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri|link}} wear.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|OoT3D Green|Kokiri Tunic}}<br/>A humble but comfortable green garment. Everyone in the Kokiri Forest wears one.|N/A|OoT3D}}</ref> It is consequently {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}}'s default clothing. It is a simple leaf-green {{Term|OoT3D|Tunic}} that enables no special abilities, unlike the {{Term|OoT3D|Goron Tunic|link}} and {{Term|OoT3D|Zora Tunic|link}}. However, it cannot be stolen by {{Plural|OoT3D|Like Like|link}}. As such, if a {{Term|OoT3D|Like Like}} swallows {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} while he is wearing another {{Term|OoT3D|Tunic}}, he will wear the {{Term|OoT3D|Kokiri Tunic}} when spat out.
