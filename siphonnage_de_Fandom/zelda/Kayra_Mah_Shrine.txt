==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Kayra Mah Shrine}} features a steep upward incline. The incline is sectioned by gapped fences along its center, creating three paths upward. At the very top of the incline is the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}.

Upon approaching the incline, a series of traps are triggered in which {{Plural|BotW|Iron Ball|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Spiked Boulder|link}} come rolling down toward {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Among the rolling hazards are also {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} of various amounts. These {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} are meant to serve as bait, as they are shortly followed by the hazards. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can avoid the hazards simply by evading them, but can also use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} to freeze them in place. Eventually all of the traps will be released, leaving no more {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} left as they collect at the bottom and making for a safe climb the rest of the way. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the bottom of the incline to collect any {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} that he had missed.

At the very top of the incline is the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. When spoken to, {{Term|BotW|Kayra Mah}} will gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kayra Mah|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kayra Mah|BotW}}</ref>

Along the walls of {{Term|BotW|Kayra Mah Shrine}} are two {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}, one on a platform and another in an alcove in the wall. These {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|display=Chests}} can be reached by gliding to them using the {{Term|BotW|Paraglider|link}} from a higher elevation. From inside them, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain five {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}.
