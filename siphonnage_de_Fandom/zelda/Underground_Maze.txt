The '''Underground Maze''' is a stage in {{SSBM}}.

==Features and overview==
[[File:SSBM Underground Maze.png|thumb|left|200px|Sheik fighting ReDeads in the Underground Maze]]
The Underground Maze is a labyrinth themed after {{TLoZ|Series}}. It is the first portion of the third stage in [[Adventure (Super Smash Bros. Melee)|Adventure mode]]; completing it takes the player's [[fighter]] to the [[Temple (Stage)|Temple]], where they have to battle [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]].

The object is to find the [[Triforce]], which is hidden in one of five rooms, all sharing the same layout. The location of the Triforce is randomized each time the player plays the stage, and the other four areas each have a [[Master Sword]] in a [[Pedestal of the Master Sword|pedestal]]. If the player's fighter enters an area with a Master Sword, they are forced to fight [[Link]] and have to KO him before they can proceed. [[ReDead]]s, [[Octorok]]s, and [[Like Like]]s appear as enemy obstacles in the stage.

Like the [[Temple (Stage)|Temple]] stage, the music theme that plays in the Underground Maze is a rearrangement of the [[Palace]] theme from {{TAoL}}.
