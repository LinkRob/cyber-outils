The '''Sludge Like''' {{Exp Game|TotK}} is a [[Boss]] in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'' that ambushes Link and Sidon at [[Mipha Court]].

== Characteristics ==

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
{{Stub}}{{Compendium|name=Sludge Like|loot=None|locs=Lanayru Great Spring|desc=These sludge-loving Like Likes are nightmarishly mobile. They ooze sludge, and they use that sludge to move around, sometimes creeping into areas that other types of Like Likes tend to avoid. As if that weren't enough, they also spew sludge out of their maws to slow and capture prey.|numbermaster=000|number=Entry No. ---|file=TotK Hyrule Compondium Sludge Like.png}}
The Sludge Like is a [[Like Like]] covered in [[sludge]]. When the sludge is cleared off by having, it can be seen to have purple coloration around its mouth rather than green. It is capable of spewing out sludge at long ranges that deal about 4-6 hearts of damage. It is resistant to all attacks until you wash off the sludge. It will then burrow into the ground and resurface completely covered in sludge again.

<nowiki>==== DEFEAT ====</nowiki>

To defeat the Sludge Like, get [[Sidon|Prince Sidon]] to make you a bubble of water using your weapon will send it spraying in that direction. If you hit the boss with the water, it will then shoot some sludge at you and then show its purple tongue. Shooting the tongue will let you hit it with a melee weapon. Eventually the Like will hide and recover, covering its sludge once more. Repeat until it dies.
==== Attack on King Dorephan ====
Sometime after the [[Upheaval]] occurred and the sludge contaminated [[Zora's Domain]], sludge monster attacked King Dorephan, although he managed to fend it off, he was severely wounded from the sludge and leading him to retreat to the [[Pristine Sanctum]] to recover. It is believed that this monster was the Sludge Like.

==== Attack on Mipha Court ====
In Mipha Court, after [[Yona]] convinces [[Sidon]] to assist [[Link]] in investigating the sludge contaminating Zora's Domain and the [[Lanayru (Region)|surrounding region]], the Sludge Like emerges from the ground and ambushes the group. Link and Sidon fight it together. It is covered in sludge, which makes it invulnerable. It attacks by firing balls of sludge around the area, which create sludge on the ground that impedes Link's movement. The sludge balls can be blocked with a shield or parried, though parrying them will not reflect them. Using Sidon's Power or any other water-based attack such as [[Opal|opals]] or [[chuchu jelly]] thrown or [[Fuse|fused]] to an [[arrow]], the Sludge can be cleared from the Sludge Like's body. It will continue attacking, but it will now become vulnerable after attacking, revealing it's uvula-like weak point similar to other Like Likes. Attacking this weak point will stun the Sludge Like and you can continue attacking the weak spot like any other Like Like until the Sludge Like regenerates its sludge and moves to a new position. Repeat until you defeat it, at which point it will drop a chest containing a Royal Claymore, though the once-powerful weapon is decayed.
