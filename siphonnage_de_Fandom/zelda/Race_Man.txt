The '''Race Man''' is a supporting character from ''The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past''. He is at the finish line of a race at the [[Quarreling Brothers]]' house. He is shown with a piece of heart as a reward for Link if he manages to get through a maze in 15 seconds or less. If, afterward, Link plays again, the man pitifully says he has nothing more to give to him.

It is only a possibility that he is the boys' father.
