The {{Term/Store|Nice Ice Rod||Series, ALBW}} is an item in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 132}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Once Link acquires the three [[Pendants of Virtue]], he can buy the [[Ice Rod]] from Ravio for 1,200 Rupees. Once he owns the rod, Link can then find and bring 10 [[Lost Maiamai|Lost Maiamais]] to [[Mother Maiamai]] to get her to upgrade the [[Ice Rod]] into the '''[[Nice Ice Rod]]'''.

The '''[[Nice Ice Rod]]''' is much the same as the [[Ice Rod]], freezing objects as well as freezing or damaging enemies by dropping an icicle on them. The major enhancements are that it has a wider area of effect by dropping 4 icicles instead of 1, and if the player holds down the item button they can indicate where the icicles will land.{{Stub|ALBW}}
