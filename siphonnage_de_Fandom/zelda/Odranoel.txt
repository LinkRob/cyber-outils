==Biography==
{{Term|TFoE|Odranoel}} is an astronomer {{Term|TFoE|Link|link}} encounters at the end of the {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin Bazaar|link}}, where he keeps his observatory. He appears to have been imprisoned by {{Term|TFoE|Harlequin|link}}, who was carrying the {{Term|TFoE|Key|link}} to his room. He hypothesizes that the stars are made of ice, which according to him, makes the night cold. He asks {{Term|TFoE|Link}} to bring him the {{Term|TFoE|Ice Crystal|link}} from {{Term|TFoE|Serigon Caves|link}}, so that he may prove that ice produces a stronger light.<ref>{{Cite|The stars are made of ice, thus the night is cold. Bring the crystal from Serigon, and I will prove that the light of ice penetrates more than fire.|Odranoel|TFoE}}</ref> Once {{Term|TFoE|Link}} brings him the {{Term|TFoE|Ice Crystal}}, he combines it with {{Term|TFoE|Link}}'s {{Term|TFoE|Lantern|link}} by adding a vacuum to it and spinning the {{Term|TFoE|Ice Crystal}} inside, turning it into the enhanced {{Term|TFoE|Magic Lantern|link}}. He then leaves the vicinity afterwards.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TFoE|Odranoel}} is "Leonardo" backwards.
