Mineru's Construct is a mount and ally from {{TotK}}.

==Overview==
Mineru’s [[Construct]] is a [[Crafter Construct]] used by [[Mineru]], the Sage of Spirit, in Tears of the Kingdom. [[Link]] assembles it using the Zonai Relic from Dragonhead Island, brought to the Construct Factory in the Depths via Tobio’s Hollow Chasm. After installing the head and retrieving four limbs from nearby depots, the Construct is complete.

Link can ride the Construct, [[Fuse|Fusing]] objects to its arms (for combat) and back (to affect movement). It uses [[Energy Cell|Energy Cells]] only when Link pilots it and is immune to [[Gloom]]. While it can’t use bows, Link can still shoot his own while riding.

At the [[Spirit Temple]], Link and Mineru defeat the corrupted [[Seized Construct]], allowing Mineru to reclaim her [[Secret Stone]] and become the Sage of [[Spirit]]. Link can then summon the Construct using the [[Vow of Mineru]].
