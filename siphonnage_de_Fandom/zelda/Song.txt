==={{TLoZ|-}}===
Playing the Flute will summon a tornado, which can carry Link to dungeon entrances. It also dries up a pond, and will cause [[Digdogger]] to split into three fragments.

==={{TMC|-}}===
While music does not play a large role in the story or mechanics of {{TMC}}, when {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}}, he can play it (using the same tune as the {{Term|TLoZ|Flute|link}} from {{TLoZ}}) to summon {{Term|TMC|Zeffa|link}}, who will swoop down and transport him to any {{Term|TMC|Wind Crest|link}} he has activated across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}.

Additionally, after {{Term|TMC|Link}} collects the first 130 {{Plural|TMC|Figurine|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Figurine Gallery|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Herb|link}} will let him inside the {{Term|TMC|Music House|link}}, where a {{Term|TMC|Phonograph|link}} can be used to play songs from the game.

There are also two {{Plural|TMC|Song}} briefly sung in-game: "{{Term|TMC|Live Long, Love Long|link}}" by {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} and "{{Term|TMC|Ting Tong|link}}" by the {{Plural|TMC|Mountain Minish|link}}.
