==Characteristics==
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Baby Dodongo}}<br/>Watch out for its leaping attack! It will explode after it's defeated!
}}

Baby Dodongos are infantile versions of the [[Dodongo]]s. They are small, serpentine creatures that slither along the ground which can be found in [[Dodongo's Cavern]]. Upon detecting [[Link]]'s presence, Baby Dodongos will burrow up from underground, often in groups, and lunge at him when they are in range. Baby Dodongos are relatively weak and can be dispatched quickly. Upon their defeat, they will explode. This explosion can be used against Cracked Tile walls in lieu of [[Bomb]]s.
