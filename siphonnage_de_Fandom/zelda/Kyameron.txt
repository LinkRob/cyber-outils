{{Term/Store|Kyameron|Kyamerons|Series, ALttP, ALBW|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 188 {{Exp Game|ALttP, ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Kyamerons are bubble-like enemies that appear out of the shallow water when [[Link]] walks by. Once they have completely formed, they will bounce around the room until they hit Link. Otherwise, they will disappear into nothingness. To defeat a Kyameron and prevent its regeneration, Link can freeze it and then hit it with the [[Magic Hammer]].

In {{ALttP&FS}}, while fighting [[Arrghus]] in the [[Palace of the Four Sword]], the boss is able to summon swarms of Kyamerons after all the [[Bari]] surrounding it have been destroyed.
