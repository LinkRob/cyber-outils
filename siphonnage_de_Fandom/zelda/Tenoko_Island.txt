{{Term/Store|Tenoko Island||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Tenoko Island'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Tenoko Island is located in the [[Necluda Sea]]. Tenoko Island hosts [[Chaas Qeta Shrine]], where Link can find [[Climbing Gear]]. Tenoko Island is also home to a lone palm tree bearing [[Palm Fruit]], under which [[Link]] can find a [[Korok Leaf]]. A [[Raft]] can also be found on the eastern side of the Island. On the shores of Tenoko Island, Link may find [[Hearty Blueshell Snail]]s. [[Mighty Porgy]] and [[Armored Porgy]] can be found swimming the waters around Tenoko Island.

There is a small stump on the eastern shore of Tenoko Island that acts as a [[Korok]] puzzle. If Link steps on the stump, a timed puzzle will appear on a small mass of land due east of the Island, requiring Link to reach it within the time limit. If Link does so, a Korok will appear and reward Link with a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>

In the waters southeast of the Island, Link can find a wooden [[Treasure Chest]] which he can either push to shore or raise out of the water with [[Cryonis]]. This Treasure Chest contains 10 [[Arrow]]s.
