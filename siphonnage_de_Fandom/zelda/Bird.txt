{{Term/Store|Bird|Birds|Series|plural}} are recurring [[Animal]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 98 {{Exp Game|Series}}}}</ref>

==Species==
===Common Bird===
In {{OoA}} and {{OoS}}, the [[Know-It-All Birds]] provide Link with helpful tips about the gameplay.

In {{TMC}}, many birds live on the [[Fortress of Winds]] and on the [[Tower of Winds]]' roof. One of those birds, [[Zeffa]], is a blue bird who can, similarly to the [[Flute Boy's Bird|Flute Boy's pet]], [[Warping|warp]] Link to any one of the 8 [[Wind Crest]]s scattered across the overworld, when summoned by using the [[Ocarina of Wind]].

[[File:ST Whip Bird Model.png|120px|right|A large bird from {{ST|-}}]]
In {{TP}}, [[Hena]] and [[Coro]] each own a bird, named respectively [[Purdy]] and [[Trill (Twilight Princess)|Trill]]. Trill runs a [[Trill's Shop|shop]] near the [[Forest Temple]] that sells [[Lantern Oil]] and [[Red Potion]]. The bird [[Plumm]] might belong to their sister, [[Iza]], as Plumm can be found at the finish point of Iza's [[Rapids Ride]].

In {{ST}}, Link can play the "[[Song of Birds]]" to summon birds (Cuccos included). The young hero can use his [[Whip]] to latch on the trapeze that Whip Birds <ref>The Legend of Zelda Encyclopedia</ref> are holding, which allows him to be carried by the birds to certain areas. Also in this game, doves appear in [[Aboda Village]] and while riding the [[Spirit Train]] in the [[Forest Realm]].

[[File:ALBW Blue Bird Model.png|left|62px]]
In {{ALBW}}, birds are found along with [[squirrel]]s in the [[Sacred Grove]], and gathering around the [[Bird Lover]] under a bridge. These birds are found in the colors blue, yellow, and pink. They fly away when Link gets close. In the ending, several birds and squirrels are seen gathering around [[Gulley]] in the [[Haunted Grove]].

In {{TFH}}, pink, yellow, and a rarer blue bird appear in the town of [[Hytopia]]. They are slightly smaller than the variety found in {{ALBW|-}}. They tend to  flock in small groups and will fly away when approached. [[Mimi]] believes that if a picture of a blue bird is taken, everyone who sees the picture will be happy.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! Have you seen a blue bird? Mimi hears if you take a picture of a blue bird, everyone who sees it will be happy! Teehee!|Mimi|TFH}}</ref> However, there is no significance to this. The [[Street Merchant]] will pay Link more for [[Material]] if he has seen a blue bird.

===Cucco===
{{Main|Cucco}}
[[File:MM Cucco.png|left|160px|A Cucco]]
Cuccos made their first appearance in {{ALttP}} and are among the first animals introduced in the series. Cuccos make their return in {{LA}}, and in most games since. The [[Cucco#Flying Rooster|Flying Rooster]] introduced the concept of [[Cucco#Cucco Gliding|Cucco Gliding]].

Cuccos made their first 3D appearance in {{OoT}}. A Cucco is essentially the [[Hylian]] version of a chicken. This game also included the concept of Cucco gliding, actually requiring Link to make use of this mechanic to find all of the [[Cucco Lady]]'s Cuccos. Cuccos have had many uses throughout {{TLoZ|-}} series, sometimes comical, such as in {{MM|-}}, where they are used as a getaway escape by the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice|Bombers]].

[[File:Cucco chick.png|right|80px|Cucco chicks from Majora's Mask]]
In {{TMC|-}}, the chicks inside [[Anju]]'s house will bite at Link when he is [[Minish]]-sized. One, however, will not, and perches on top of [[Swiftblade]]'s dojo; Link can [[Kinstone Fusion|Fuse]] [[Kinstone]] with this Cucco chick if he is Minish-sized.

Cuccos have appeared in the following games: {{ALttP|-}}, {{LA|-}}, {{OoT|-}}, {{MM|-}}, {{OoA|-}}, {{OoS|-}}, {{FSA}}, {{TMC|-}}, {{TP|-}}, {{PH}}, {{ST|-}}, {{ALBW|-}}, {{TFH|-}}, and {{BotW|-}}.

===Dove===
{{Main|Dove}}
[[File:ST Dove on Link Model.png|50px||right|A dove on Link's head]]
Doves are common all throughout Hyrule in {{ST|-}}. They largely replace the [[#Seagull|Seagulls]] from {{PH|-}} and behave similarly, although they are more interactive than the seagulls were. Using the [[Song of Birds]] near one will cause it to fly and land on Link's head. It will stay on his head until Link rolls, spin attacks, or hits something hard with his sword.

===Duck===
{{Main|Duck}}
[[File:Duck.gif|left|30px|The Flute Boy's Duck]]
In {{ALttP|-}}, when Link plays the [[flute]] nearby the [[Weathercock]] in the center of [[Kakariko Village]], the [[Flute Boy's Bird|Flute Boy's duck]] will appear from inside the statue. If Link plays the flute again, the bird will appear and [[Warping|transport]] him to one of 8 different locations in the [[Light World]].

Ducks are found in [[Ordon Village]] and in the [[Fishing Hole]] in {{TP|-}}. They can either be found swimming in the pond or walking along the banks.

[[File:Hawk.jpg|thumb|190px|Link calls a Hawk in Ordon Village]]
===Hawk===
[[File:TP Hawk Model.png|left|140px]]
In {{TP|-}}, Link can blow into [[Hawk Grass]] in order to summon a hawk, which he can use to solve certain puzzles. The hawk can be aimed and released to retrieve faraway objects or attack enemies. After this, the hawk will fly away, but it can be called on again if need be. If Link calls on the hawk as a [[Wolf Link|Wolf]], the Hawk will be able to point out secrets in the area. At the beginning of Link's quest, it is necessary to summon a hawk to get [[Uli]]'s crib back from a [[monkey]].

===Owl===
[[File:OoT Kaepora Gaebora Artwork.png|left|100px]]{{Main|Owl}}
A mysterious [[Owl]] first appears in {{LA|-}} to share his words of wisdom with Link.

[[Kaepora Gaebora]] is the name given to the wise owl from {{OoT|-}}, {{MM|-}}, and {{FSA|-}}.{{clear}}

===Seagull===
[[File:TWW Seagull Figurine Model.png|right|120px]]{{Main|Seagull}}
Link can control seagulls in {{TWW|-}} using a [[Hyoi Pear]]. At the [[Forsaken Fortress]], several of them circle around the window of the room in which [[Aryll]] is being held captive. They sometimes fly alongside the [[King of Red Lions]], Link's boat.

In {{PH|-}}, Seagulls return mainly to add atmosphere while traveling by sea. They will follow [[SS Linebeck|Linebeck's Ship]] in groups of four. If Link shoots one of them with the cannon, they will all flee.

===Loftwing===
{{Main|Loftwing}}
In {{SS|-}}, all the inhabitants of [[Skyloft]] are paired up with a large, rideable bird known as a Loftwing. Link has a [[Crimson Loftwing]], a breed previously thought extinct, which he can use to fly around in between the various islands in the [[The Sky|Sky]]. Loftwings are a necessary part of life in Skyloft, seen when [[Groose]] and his henchmen, [[Cawlin]] and [[Strich]], steal and hide Link's Loftwing.

===Chirri===
{{Main|Chirri}}
Chirris are small Birds that can be found on the [[Surface]] in places such as {{Term|SS|Faron Woods|link}}. There are three varieties: yellow, orange and blue.
