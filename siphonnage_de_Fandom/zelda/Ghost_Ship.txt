The {{Term/Store|Ghost Ship||Series, TWW, TWWHD, PH}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Dungeon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|The '''Ghost Ship''' is here!<br/><br/>I bet there's treasure in it!|Tingle|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 156}}</ref>{{Name Ref|TWWHD}}

=={{TWW|-}}==
===Entrance===
The {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship}} haunts seven islands of the {{Term|TWWHD|Great Sea|link}} during the night. It appears at different locations depending on the current state of the moon. Only by obtaining the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship Chart|link}}, hidden on {{Term|TWWHD|Diamond Steppe Island|link}},<ref name= "Diamond Steppe Island">{{Cite|Oh, I've got some great information, small fry! ===Themes and Navigation===
The {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship}} is transparent with surrounding blue flames. It bears a tattered sail and flag, and, on its bow, is a carving of what appears to be a person praying. Whenever {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} is in the same area as the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship}}, the sky fills with dark clouds and thunders, and a haunting melody starts to play. The interior has broken wooden beams and, on both sides of the floor, are four skulls lined in a row. The cracked left side of the ceiling shows what appear to be spirits floating by. There is also a small beam of light on the right side, containing a lone flower. In {{TWWHD}}, the interior floor also bears a ghastly haze.
[[File:TWW Ghost Ship Interior.png|thumb|220x220px|The Ghost Ship's interior in {{TWW|-}}]]
Once inside the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship}}, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} must defeat two {{Plural|TWWHD|Poe|link}} and a {{Term|TWWHD|Wizzrobe|link}}, which is able to summon {{Plural|TWWHD|ReDead|link}} and {{Plural|TWWHD|Stalfos|link}}. After defeating all the enemies, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} will be able to climb up a ladder leading to a {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} which contains a {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce Chart|link}} in {{TWW}} or a {{Term|TWWHD|Triforce Shard|link}} in {{TWWHD|-}}. After collecting the contents of the {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest}}, a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/MC/MC_BoundChest_Spirits_Scream.wav demonic scream] sounds throughout the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship}}. {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} then awakens aboard the {{Term|TWWHD|King of Red Lions|link}}, with the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship}} disappearing forever.

=={{PH|-}}==
===Entrance to the Ghost Ship===
Prior to the events of {{PH|-}}, the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} had built up a reputation as a cursed ship that nobody has ever returned from.<ref>{{Cite|Is it the Ghost Ship people have been talking about? They say that those it takes are never seen again!|Ciela|PH}}</ref>

While {{Term|PH|Link|link}} and the {{Plural|PH|Pirate|link}} are sailing the seas, the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} appears within a ghostly fog. {{Term|PH|Tetra|link}} boards it and shrieks once she is out of sight of the others, prompting {{Term|PH|Link}} to follow her in an attempt to rescue her. However, he instead falls into the ocean and is taken to the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|So you were following after her... And you got separated from your own ship, huh?|Ciela|PH}}</ref> {{Term|PH|Link}} then searches for a ship captain who will give him a ship to use to chase the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}. As a result, he comes across {{Term|PH|Linebeck|link}}, who agrees to help him find the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}.

After {{Term|PH|Link}} has obtained a {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}} for the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}}, the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} will soon reappear. {{Term|PH|Link}} attempts to follow it, but soon becomes lost in the fog. After traveling to {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}} to find the path he must take, he is able to get away from it, although the fog does not dissipate until {{Term|PH|Link}} rescues {{Term|PH|Tetra}} from the ship.

After {{Term|PH|Link}} has freed {{Term|PH|Leaf|link}} and {{Term|PH|Neri|link}} as well as regaining {{Term|PH|Ciela|link}}'s memory, he once more sails into the fog. This time, he locates and boards the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}.

===Themes and Navigation===
Once inside the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}, {{Term|PH|Link}} must reunite the four {{Plural|PH|Cubus Sisters|link}}, who reveal themselves to be demons. They then challenge {{Term|PH|Link}} to a game of {{Term|PH|Dead Man's Volley|link}}, after which {{Term|PH|Link}} is given the the {{Term|PH|Ghost Key|link}} to open the door leading to {{Term|PH|Tetra}}'s whereabouts.

When {{Term|PH|Tetra}} is rescued, {{Term|PH|Oshus|link}} reveals himself to be the Ocean King, and explains that the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} is actually a way for {{Term|PH|Bellum|link}} to get victims. He spread rumors of a great treasure within the ship to lure people there.

After {{Term|PH|Bellum}} is defeated within the {{Term|PH|Temple of the Ocean King|link}}, he takes {{Term|PH|Tetra}} again and takes control of the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}. Here, {{Term|PH|Link}} sails alongside it, launching {{Plural|PH|Bomb|link}} at {{Term|PH|Bellum}}'s eyes. Eventually, it sinks, and the final boss battle takes place on its remains. After the game's credits, {{Term|PH|Link}} and {{Term|PH|Tetra}} awaken on the mysteriously repaired the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}}. They go back onto their own ship, and the {{Term|PH|Ghost Ship}} soon disappears without a trace.

==Trivia==
*The Ghost Ship uses the exact same model as {{Term|TWW|Tetra's Ship|link}}, except with tattered, non-descript sails, and a different figurehead.
*In the small room containing the {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{TWW}}, there is an engraved face on the wall that seems to have a wide, eerie grin from afar. However, when {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} approaches it, the face quickly changes to that of an expressionless, normal face. This was removed in {{TWWHD}}.
*Due to a programming bug, if Link performs a [[Jump Slash|Jump Attack]] on the Treasure Chest in the small room in {{TWW|-}}, the game will crash. This is fixed in ''The Wind Waker HD''.
*If Link doesn't collect the [[Heart Container]] after completing the Ghost Ship in {{PH}}, he'll eventually get a letter from an unknown sender informing him that they've picked it up for Link and attached it to the letter. If he again refuses to obtain the Heart Container, it will disappear permanently.
*The eerie scream that is heard after opening the {{Term|TWWHD|Treasure Chest}} is actually a sped-up version of an [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Jabun_Distress2.wav unused audio clip] of {{Term|TWWHD|Jabun|link}} screaming.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Beta64 |published= March 18, 2013 |retrieved= October 12, 2015 |url= http://youtu.be/EBToXMXJh1o?t=4m20s |title= Beta64 - Wind Waker |site= YouTube |type= video}}</ref>
**This same scream sound effect can be heard in {{TMC}}, when [[Vaati]] opens the [[Bound Chest]].
*At some point, the {{Term|TWWHD|Ghost Ship}} was encountered and photographed by {{Term|TWWHD|Lenzo|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This foul ship, veiled in mist and fading in and out of sight like something of another world... Yes, my young sir... 'Tis none other than the Ghost Ship!|Lenzo|TWW}}</ref>
*In the Japanese prerelease of {{TWW|-}}, there's a few differences with the Ghost Ship:
**When in the same vicinity as the ship itself, [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DeUnV9QzthE the sky does not change into dark clouds and lightning]. In the final version, it does.
**Blue Bubbles could be summoned by the Wizzrobes inside the ship, whereas they do not in the final version.
**A Stalfos appears in the ship's interior as well, but is removed in the final version.
**When Link performs a jump attack to get on top of the Treasure Chest in the third Ghost Ship, the game does not crash. In the final version, and as stated above, it happens in the original version.
***If using hacks, Link jumping into the face behind it will indeed cause the game to softlock.
**Originally, Link had to find [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q1V6actlwS4 3 Ghost Ships], as evidenced by looking in the Ghost Ship Chart. The first two will hold 50 Rupees while the third one will hold the Triforce Chart. In the final version, Link only has to find one.
