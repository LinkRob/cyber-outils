==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Moblin Guts are dropped by [[Blue Moblin]]s, [[Black Moblin]]s, [[Silver Moblin]]s, and, in [[Master Mode]], [[Golden Moblin]]s. They have a pungent odor, but can be used while [[Cooking]] with critters to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]] or used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Light Blue [[Dye]].

Moblin Guts can be used at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} to upgrade several pieces of [[Armor]]. [[Fireproof Armor]] requires three Moblin Guts to upgrade each piece to their third tier, while the [[Radiant Set]] and [[Soldier's Set]] require them for their second tiers.

* Total for '''Fireproof Armor''': 9 (0, 0, 3, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Radiant Set''': 9 (0, 3, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Soldier's Set''': 9 (0, 3, 0, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
