==Location and Uses==
==={{PH|-}}===
The Wood Heart is obtained by giving the [[Guard Notebook]] to [[Nyave]] in the Southwestern sea of the [[World of the Ocean King]]. Giving it to the [[Old Wayfarer]] on his ship in the Southeastern sea allows [[Link]] to return to [[Bannan Island]] and get the [[Swordsman's Scroll]], which allows him to use the [[Spin Attack]].

==={{ST|-}}===
The Wood Heart appears as a [[Treasure]] in {{ST}}. They can be found inside {{Plural|ST|Jar|link}}, {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest|link}}, or under piles of leaves, as well as being be bought from shops or won from a {{Term|ST|Prize Postcard|link}}. They can be sold for 50 [[Rupee]]s or traded with [[Linebeck III]] for [[Train Cars]].
