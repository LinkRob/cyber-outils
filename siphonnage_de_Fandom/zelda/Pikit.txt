{{Term/Store|Pikit|Pikits|Series, ALttP|plural}} are enemies in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 390 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Pikits can be found in the [[Dark World]] hopping around. When [[Link]] gets close to them, they extend their tongues to steal from him. Their tongue can even extend over short walls in places like the [[Skeleton Forest]]. Pikits can steal Green [[Rupee]]s, [[Bomb]]s, [[Arrow]]s, and [[Shield]]s, with the exception of the [[Mirror Shield]]. If Link defeats a Pikit, it will drop the last item it ate.
