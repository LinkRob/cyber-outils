The {{Name|Japanese|Usotami Village}} is a location in {{RTBToL}}.{{Name Ref|RTBToL}} It serves as home to the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami|link}}, striped people who have a strong tendency to lie. 

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village}} is located beneath a desert in {{Term|RTBToL|Page 11}}, the entrance to the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}} resembles a head of an {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami}} made out of rocks. Stone tablets with riddles and idols with smiling and angry faces can be seen on walls, as well as {{Plural|RTBToL|Torch|link}} and {{Plural|RTBToL|Lantern}} hanging from the ceiling. The {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}} is home to {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}}, one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s love interests. 

After completing the desert area, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Lion (Tingle's Balloon Trip of Love)|link}}, and {{Term|RTBToL|Buriki|link}} end up in the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}} while chasing {{Term|RTBToL|Segāre|link}}, who has kidnapped {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi|link}}. To reach {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi}}, who is being held in a cave behind the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}}, {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must solve several puzzles, all of them involving lies. 

At the end of the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}} resides the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Chief|link}} in a room with a large herd of colorful goats. One of them is the required for {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}} to milk it in order to obtain {{Term|RTBToL|Milk|link}}, which {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} must give as an offering to the boss {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar|link}} to calm him down.

==Trivia==
*Once the page is finished, a {{Term|RTBToL|Coin Collector|link}} will appear at the beginning of the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}}. Giving him 50 demon coins (gained from defeating {{Plural|RTBToL|Enemy|link}} in the desert) rewards {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} with an entry for {{Term|RTBToL|Secret Notes|link}}.
