'''Pieces of Power''' are [[items]] in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 134"/>{{Note|This item is also stylized as '''Piece o' Power''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 11}}</ref> However, because {{LA|NS}} takes precedence, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
[[File:LA - Piece of Power 2.gif|left|thumb|Link obtaining a Piece of Power in ''Link's Awakening DX'']]
Pieces of Power appear after defeating 40 to 45 [[Enemy|enemies]].<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 89}}</ref> When obtained, the music changes and [[Link]]'s [[Sword#Link's Awakening|Sword]] begins to flash, causing [[Weapon Strength|double damage]] and sending enemies flying when hit by the blade.<ref name="E 134" /> These items also increase Link's speed.<ref name="E 134" /> Their effect wears off after being hit three times or moving into different areas of the [[overworld]].<ref name="E 134" />

The [[Guardian Acorn]] is its [[defense]] counterpart, as it doubles the hero's durability against damage.

In {{LA|DX}}, when the [[Red Clothes]] are worn, obtaining a Piece of Power quadruples the original damage output, resulting in devastating blows against foes, especially with the [[Koholint Sword|Level 2 Sword]] which can inflict eight times the regular sword's strength.

==Trivia==
*Pieces of Power were the inspiration for the [[Force Gem]]s in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 267}}</ref>
*The Piece of Power resembles the [[Triforce of Power]] as it appears in ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]''.
*Pieces of Power have the same name in Japanese as [[Might Crystal]]s from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Echoes of Wisdom|Echoes of Wisdom]]''.
