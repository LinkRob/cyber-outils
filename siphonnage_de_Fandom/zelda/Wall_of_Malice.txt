==Location and Uses==
In {{Term|HWAoC|All Hyrule United|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}} creates the {{Term|HWAoC|Wall of Malice}} to stop the party from entering {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}. They eventually eliminate it by defeating certain strong {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}, and are able to progress inside.
