{{Term/Store|Mubs||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mubs|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mubs}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who resides in {{Term|BotW|Lurelin Village|link}}. She runs the {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat|link}} {{Term|BotW|General Store|link}} on a {{Term|BotW|Dock|link}}, where she sells {{Plural|BotW|Fish|link}} and other supplies.<ref>{{Cite|Fresh fish! Just recently caught! Take a look!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}} itself carries some of her storage, including extra {{Plural|BotW|Armored Porgy|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Porgy|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mubs}}, she welcomes him to the {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}} and remarks that she has never seen him before.<ref>{{Cite|Heeey! Welcome! You're a new face!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> During the day, she says that her fresh {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} are perfect for hot {{Term|BotW|Weather|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heeey! Welcome! Know what sounds good on such a hot day? Some fish! What do you think?|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> She also claims that she restocked her {{Plural|BotW|Fish}} that morning.<ref>{{Cite|Heeey! Welcome!<br/>I just got fresh stuff in this morning!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> In the evenings, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is shopping for his dinner.<ref>{{Cite|Heeey! Welcome!<br/>You here to buy something for dinner?|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> At night, she inquires if {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants a midnight snack.<ref>{{Cite|Heeey! Welcome!<br/>Got a case of the midnight munchies?|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> At the end of their conversations, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to return to the {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}}.<ref>{{Cite|Come again! I'll be waiting!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref>

If asked, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can make a purchase by standing in front of her stock and interacting with it.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll buy.] If you want something, just let me know by standing in front of it and selecting it!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> Whenever {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes a purchase from the {{Term|BotW|Merchant Boat}}, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} will thank him for his business.<ref name="Thanks">{{Cite|Thanks for your business!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys the entirety of one {{Term|BotW|Item|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} promises to restock soon.<ref>{{Cite|You need more? I'll be sure to get some more for next time!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} stands on {{Term|BotW|Mubs}}' products, she yells at him to stop.<ref>{{Cite|You stop that!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref>

Like other {{Term|BotW|General Store}} managers, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell his own {{Plural|BotW|Item}} to {{Term|BotW|Mubs}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll sell.] What will ya sell me?|Mubs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy a lot of different things from ya!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy anything!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Don't hesitate to sell me stuff!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Mubs}} buys an {{Term|BotW|Item}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}, she will be grateful and encourage him to sell more.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you!<br/>Are you gonna sell me anything else?|Mubs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you!<br/>I'll take anything you don't need off your hands!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'll buy anything off ya!|Mubs|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you very much! Did you need anything else?|Mubs|BotW}}</ref> After selling to {{Term|BotW|Mubs}}, she will thank him for his business.<ref name="Thanks"/>
