{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda 25<sup>th</sup> Anniversary Special Orchestra CD''||Series}} is a collection of live performances of the [[The Legend of Zelda 25th Anniversary Symphony]]. The CD was shipped with the Special Edition of {{SS}} to celebrate the [[The Legend of Zelda 25th Anniversary|25th anniversary]] of {{TLoZ|Series}}.
