'''Club Boarblin Lv. 2''' is an [[Enemy]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
{{Manual|EoW|Club Boarblin Lv. 2|This desert-dwelling warrior wields a club and shield. Fiercely territorial, it will pursue intruders doggedly.<ref name="EoW Boarclub Lv. 2">{{Cite|This desert-dwelling warrior wields a club and shield. Fiercely territorial, it will pursue intruders doggedly.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Club Boarblin Lv. 2 are stronger variants of the standard Club Boarblin, dealing heavier damage and withstanding more hits. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can use the [[Tri Rod]] to learn their [[Echo]], which costs four Echo energy to summon.
