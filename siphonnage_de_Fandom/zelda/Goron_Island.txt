{{Term/Store|Goron Island||Series, PH}} is a location in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |page= 113 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref> It is found in the southeastern quadrant.

==Features and Overview==
===Population===
[[File:Goron Island.png|thumb|170px|A map of Goron Island]]
The island serves as home to the {{Term|PH|Goron Temple|link}}, the fifth dungeon in the game. A population of {{Plural|PH|Goron|link}} inhabit this isle, which, out of context, may explain where most of them were in {{TWW}}. The {{Term|PH|Goron}}'s tribal leader on the island is named {{Term|PH|Biggoron}}, and his son who later becomes a friend of Link's is called {{Term|PH|Gongoron}}. There are a total of 14 {{Plural|PH|Goron}} who inhabit this island, all of which {{Term|PH|Link|link}} must talk to before he can even speak to the tribal leader.

The only other island containing Gorons in {{PH|-}} is [[Dee Ess Island]].

===Biggoron's Quiz===
[[File:PH Biggoron Model.png|100px|left]]
After {{Term|PH|Link}} speaks to every {{Term|PH|Goron}} on the island, {{Term|PH|Biggoron}} will let the hero try a quiz. By passing this quiz, {{Term|PH|Link}} will become a "sworn brother" of the {{Plural|PH|Goron}}, earning the right to enter {{Term|PH|Goron Temple}}. In this test, {{Term|PH|Link}} will be asked questions about the island and its inhabitants, some of which ask how many {{Plural|PH|Goron}} live on the island, what the specific {{Term|PH|Goron}} is doing, etc.. There are six questions in all, and {{Term|PH|Link}} can make unlimited attempts, but each try costs twenty {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}. Through the process of attempting this quiz, {{Term|PH|Biggoron}} will tease {{Term|PH|Link}} with the prospect of earning {{Plural|PH|Rupee}}, but take them all away if he misses a single question. The "fee" {{Term|PH|Biggoron}} asks {{Term|PH|Link}} to pay to become an official {{Term|PH|Goron}} is equal to the price he wins in passing the quiz.

After {{Term|PH|Link}} has become a sworn brother, {{Term|PH|Goron}} residents will add the prefix "Goro-" to whatever the player named the hero in their save file. For example, if the player named him {{Term|PH|Link}}, he would be called "Goro-Link".
{{clear}}

===Northern Goron Island===
On the north part of the island is a massive maze, which {{Term|PH|Link}} will need to use {{Plural|PH|Bomb|link}} in order to block up cracks in the walls. Blue {{Plural|PH|ChuChu|link}} and {{Plural|PH|Like Like|link}} live in the area and {{Term|PH|Link}} must kill them off with bows or bombs in order to proceed. There are a few treasures waiting for {{Term|PH|Link}} in the area that he can get. If {{Term|PH|Link}} runs out of {{Plural|PH|Bomb}}, he can use some of the {{Plural|PH|Bomb Flower|link}} in the area.

==Trivia==
*The background music on the southern half of {{Term|PH|Goron Island}} is a remix of the music first heard when visiting {{Term|PH|Goron City|link}} in {{OoT}}.
*The only resident of {{Term|PH|Goron Island}} who isn't a {{Term|PH|Goron}} is the girl running the isle's shop, although compared with most other shop girls of the game, her skin tone is much darker and thus more similar to a Goron.
