{{Term/Store|Darunia Town||Series, TAoL}},<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 109 {{Exp Game|TAoL}}}}</ref> also known as the '''Mountain Town of Darunia''',<ref>{{Cite|MOUNTAIN TOWN OF DARUNIA|Sign|TAoL}}</ref> is a location in {{TAoL}}. It is a town among the mountains of the northwest coast of [[Eastern Hyrule]].

==Features and Overview==
In this town, [[Link]] learns the [[Jump Thrust]] from a [[Swordsman]] living within. Since the door to the Swordsman's house is locked from the inside, the young hero uses the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Jump|Jump]] magic to jump from roof to roof and eventually enter the house by going down its chimney. At the end of the basement, the warrior teaches Link his new skill upon talking to him.<ref>{{Cite|WHEN YOU JUMP PRESS UP TO STAB.|Hylian|TAoL}}</ref>

One of the [[Old Woman|elderly women]] implores Link to rescue a [[Child]] who was taken to [[Maze Island]].<ref>{{Cite|RESCUE THE KIDNAPPED CHILD ON THE ISLAND|Old Woman|TAoL}}</ref> Rescuing the child and taking him back to the woman rewards Link with meeting an [[Old Man]] who teaches him the [[Magic of The Adventure of Link#Reflect|Reflect]] magic.<ref>{{Cite|THIS MAGIC WILL STRENGTHEN A SHIELD.|Old Man|TAoL}}</ref>

Like those in [[Saria Town]], many inhabitants transform into [[Ache]] (called "Eyes of [[Ganon]]" by the other townsfolk) after Link talks to them.

==Trivia==
*A message can be found on the wall of the first house, hinting at the location of the [[Palace on the Sea]].<ref>{{Cite|IT IS SAID THE EAST OF NABOORU HAS...|N/A|TAoL}}</ref>
**The Japanese text makes it clear that there is actually something written on the wall. It reads:"{{Romanize|ナボールノ　ズット　ヒガシ二　カミガスム　ト　カベニカイテアル|ナボールのずっと東に神が住むと壁に書いてある|'God lives all the way east of Nabooru,' is written on the wall|3}}". This is a hint for Link on where the Fifth Palace is located. {{Romanize|神|かみ|God|3}} is a reference to both {{Romanize|'''神'''殿|Shinden|Temple|3}} and {{Romanize|守護'''神'''|Shugojin|Guardian Deity|3}} (The Japanese term for the Dungeon Bosses).
*The town is named after [[Darunia]], one of the [[Sage|Seven Sages]] from {{OoT}}.<ref name=zeldacom>{{Cite Web|quote=Darunia is a small town in northern Hyrule named after Darunia, Sage to the Fire Temple. |retrieved=September 29, 2014|url=http://web.archive.org/web/20160309185730/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/d.jsp |title=Zelda Universe: The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site=Zelda.com}}</ref> With him being a [[Goron]] who lived on [[Death Mountain]], the location of the town is very apt.
