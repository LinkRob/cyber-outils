{{Term/Store|Water Pot|Water Pots|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Prima"/> {{Plural|Series|Water Pot}} go unnamed in {{FSA}}. They are primarily used to carry water.

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
In {{TWW}}, {{Plural|TWWHD|Water Pot}} are primarily used in {{Term|TWWHD|Dragon Roost Cavern|link}} to cool pools of magma, creating temporary platforms of solidified rock to either cross said pools or to ride up spouts of magma to higher ground. {{Plural|TWWHD|Water Pot}} also appear in the {{Term|TWWHD|Gohma|link}} segment of {{Term|TWWHD|Ganon's Tower|link}}, albeit with no special purpose.

{{Plural|TWWHD|Water Pot}}, like regular [[Pot]]s, can also contain items when broken with a [[Sword]], such as [[Heart]]s or [[Arrow]]s. {{Plural|TWWHD|Water Pot}} are also capable of extinguishing flaming [[Boko Stick]]s. When thrown onto [[Magtail]]s, they immediately curl into balls, temporarily pacifying them.

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Plural|FSA|Water Pot}} in {{FSA}} appear in a few Stages. In [[The Mountain Path]], they are used to extinguish swaths of flames. In the [[Lost Woods (Four Swords Adventures)|Lost Woods]], a [[Deku Scrub]] named Dekki asks for the [[Links]] to bring him three different items in order, one of which is a  pot with water in it.<ref>{{Cite|You ready? I'm gonna name three items, and I want you to remember each of 'em! I ain't repeating myself!! Then you go into the woods, nab 'em, and bring 'em back. And in order, you hear!?<br/>1. Bomb<br/>2. Grass<br/>3. Water jar|Dekki|FSA}}</ref> They can be found in houses and caves.
