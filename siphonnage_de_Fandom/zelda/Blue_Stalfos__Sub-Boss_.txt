The {{Term/Store|Blue Stalfos||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Sub-Boss}} of the {{Term|OoA|Ancient Tomb|link}}, the eighth {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 112}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|OoA|Blue Stalfos}} is a large variant of a {{Term|OoA|Stalfos|link}} that carries a scythe and resembles a {{Wp|Grim Reaper}}. It moves around in battle by phasing from one random spot to another, stopping each time to summon a ball of magic to throw toward {{Term|OoA|Link|link}}. Most of these magic attacks are harmful, asterisk-shaped attacks that split into six pieces when near {{Term|OoA|Link}}, but every third attack is a round ball with the ability to transmogrify its target. {{Term|OoA|Link}} can use his {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}} to deflect this attack back at the {{Term|OoA|Blue Stalfos}}; doing so will briefly turn it into a fluttering bat with a {{Term|OoA|Stalfos}}'s head, vulnerable to damage by {{Term|OoA|Link}}'s {{Term|OoA|Sword}}. However, if the magic ball hits {{Term|OoA|Link}}, he will be turned into a defenseless baby for about eight seconds, and can only move during this time by crawling around slowly. The {{Term|OoA|Blue Stalfos}} will also swipe at {{Term|OoA|Link}} with its scythe if he gets too close. After taking enough damage in its bat form, the {{Term|OoA|Blue Stalfos}} will be defeated and will leave behind a {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} and a portal to the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}}'s entrance.
