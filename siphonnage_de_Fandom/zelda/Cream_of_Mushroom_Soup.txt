{{Term/Store|Cream of Mushroom Soup|Cream of Mushroom Soups|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Cream of Mushroom Soups are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any {{Term|BotW|Mushroom|link}}, [[Fresh Milk]], [[Rock Salt]], and any vegetable, herb, or flower in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Regular Cream of Mushroom Soup is made by either using ingredients without any effects or using two ingredients with contradictory effects. Eating one restores four {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They're worth 30 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} when sold. These soups are known to be so chunky that they're as filling as a full meal.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Several different variations of Cream of Mushroom Soups exist, depending on the type of ingredients used:

* '''Hearty Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Truffle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Truffle|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. When eaten, they'll completely restore all of Link's Hearts and temporarily increase his maximum hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/> They also sell for 50 Rupees.

* '''Energizing Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Stamella Shroom|link}} or other stamina-increasing ingredients. Eating one will refill a portion of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/> They restore seven Hearts to his Life Gauge and they sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Enduring Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Shroom|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}}. Consuming this soup will completely restore and overfill Link's Stamina Wheel.<ref name="Enduring"/> They heal for five Hearts and sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Hasty Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Rushroom|link}} or other speed-increasing ingredients. These soups will temporarily increase Link's speed when eaten.<ref name="Hasty"/> They also restore four Hearts to Link's Life Gauge, and they'll sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Spicy Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Sunshroom|link}} or other heat-related ingredients. Eating one will temporarily grant [[Link]] one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/> They also heal him for four Hearts, but they're worth 30 Rupees when sold.

* '''Electro Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Zapshroom|link}} or other electricity-related ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]] for a short time.<ref name="Electro"/> They also heal four of his Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Mighty Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Razorshroom|link}} or other attack-increasing ingredients. These soups increase Link's attack power when consumed.<ref name="Mighty"/> They restore four Hearts to Link's Life Gauge and sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Tough Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Ironshroom|link}} or other defensive ingredients. Eating one will temporarily increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/> They replenish four Hearts to his Life Gauge, and they sell for 40 Rupees.

* '''Sneaky Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants a mid-level stealth boost.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Silent Shroom|link}} or other sneaky ingredients. Consuming this soup increases Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} for a short period.<ref name="Sneaky"/> They heal four of Link's Hearts and sell for 30 Rupees.

* '''Chilly Cream of Mushroom Soup''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Cream of Mushroom Soup}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The creamy mushroom and vegetable soup is so chunky it eats like a meal!|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made using {{Plural|BotW|Chillshroom|link}} or other cold ingredients. When eaten, these soups will increase Link's [[Heat Resistance]] by one level temporarily.<ref name="Chilly"/> They heal four of Link's Hearts, but they can also be sold for 30 Rupees.
