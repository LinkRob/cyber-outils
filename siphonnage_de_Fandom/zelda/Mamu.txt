'''Mamu''' is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}<br />{{Cite|Mamu|N/A|LANS}}</ref> He is [[mariowiki:Wart|Wart]] from the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]], who is the main antagonist of the 1987 [[Family Computer Disk System|Famicom Disk System]] title ''[[mariowiki:Yume_Kōjō:_Doki_Doki_Panic|Yume Kojo: Doki Doki Panic]]'', released outside of Japan as ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Bros._2|Super Mario Bros. 2]]'' on the [[Nintendo Entertainment System]]. However, in the case of the [[Game Boy]] [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of'' ''Zelda'']] game, he is not evil and serves as a supporting character for [[Link]].

Mamu himself is a large regal frog who wears a crown on his head and a robe that can scarcely conceal his big belly. He also wears a necklace. Although he is shown to sing in ''Link's Awakening'', he is known for spitting dangerous bubbles in ''Super Mario Bros. 2''.

==Biography==
Mamu resides in the pond beneath the [[Signpost Maze]].<ref name= "Ulrira"/> He teaches Link the "[[Frog's Song of Soul]]" after paying him 300 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Ribbit! Ribbit! I'm Mamu, on vocals! But I don't need to tell you that, do I? Everybody knows me! Want to hang out and listen to us jam? For 300 Rupees, we'll let you listen to a previously unreleased cut! What do you do?|Mamu|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You've learned The Frog's Song of Soul! It's a very moving tune... == In ''Super Mario Bros. 2'' ==
[[File:SMB2 Wart battle.webp|thumb|219x219px|Mamu as seen in ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', battling against Toad ([[Nintendo Entertainment System|NES]] version)]]
In his original source material, Mamu, or Wart, is the game's primary villain, rather than [[Bowser]]. His goal in the ''Mario'' game is to take over [[mariowiki:Subcon|Subcon]], a land of dreams (ironically like [[Koholint Island]]) where he overthrows the world's [[mariowiki:Ōsama|king]] and kidnapped its [[mariowiki:Subcon_(species)|namesake inhabits]], and it is up to [[Mario]], [[Luigi]], [[mariowiki:Princess_Peach|Princess Peach]], and [[mariowiki:Toad_(character)|Toad]] to stop him. As the leader of an army called [[mariowiki:8_bits|8 bits]], his own henchmen include [[mariowiki:Birdo_(species)|Birdos]], [[mariowiki:Mouser|Mouser]], [[mariowiki:Tryclyde|Tryclyde]], [[mariowiki:Fryguy|Fryguy]], and [[mariowiki:Clawgrip|Clawgrip]]. During the [[Final Boss]] battle against him, Wart is vulnerable to [[mariowiki:Vegetable|vegetables]] appearing from the machine thrown at him when his mouth is open shooting bubbles at the protagonists.

==Trivia==
*"Mamu" is actually the Japanese name for Wart. This was given an indirect reference in ''[[Hyrule Warriors]]'' with [[Ganondorf]]'s Mamu palette swap.
*Wart in ''Link's Awakening'' is one of several cases of characters from other [[Nintendo]] franchises, specifically ''Super Mario'', appearing in a [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] title as a [[Cameos in The Legend of Zelda series|cameo]].
*The official artwork of Mamu is completely different from how the character appears in his source material, looking like an [[Frog|actual frog]] wearing a robe.
*Strangely, all of Wart's in-game appearances have his eyes closed in a manner as if he is laughing, while official artwork has them open.
**Ironically, in ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', when he takes damage or is ultimately defeated during the final battle, Wart's eyes appear open.
*In [[Game Boy Advance]] remake of ''Super Mario Bros. 2'', ''[[mariowiki:Super_Mario_Advance|Super Mario Advance]]'', Mamu (Wart) as well as his underlings were voiced by the iconic [[mariowiki:Charles_Martinet|Charles Martinet]], who is the longtime former voice actor of Mario.
**In his case, when approached, he speaks "''I am the great Wart! Ha ha ha!''" before the final battle begins.
*Unlike Bowser and some other recurring ''Mario'' antagonists, Mamu never makes a reappearance in any core ''Super Mario'' games other than a few cameos since his defeat.
*He makes a surprising return for the ''Mario'' franchise in 2026 as one of the characters of ''[[mariowiki:The_Super_Mario_Galaxy_Movie|The Super Mario Galaxy Movie]]'' feature film.
