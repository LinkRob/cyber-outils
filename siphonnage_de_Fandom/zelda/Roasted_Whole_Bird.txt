==Location and Uses ==
A Roasted Whole Bird can be made by burning a [[Raw Whole Bird]] with a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Roasting it with fire has brought out its natural taste.<ref name="Inventory" /> They can also be dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Black Hinox|link}} when they're killed. Eating one restores four and a half {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}.
