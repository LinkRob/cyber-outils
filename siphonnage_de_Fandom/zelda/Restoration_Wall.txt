{{Term/Store|Restoration Wall||Series, OoA}} is a location in {{OoA}}.<ref name="Patch">{{Cite|Patch lives at the top of Restoration Wall. I think he can fix the broken Tuni Nut, but I could never climb that wall.|Hylian|OoA}}</ref> It is located in the past and connects the northeastern sector of [[Talus Peaks]] to the otherwise unreachable [[Symmetry Village]], and at its southwestern side even indirectly connects the [[Fairy Woods]] to these two other regions. This region, in specific, is known for its gargantuan rock wall, which can be climbed at one's risk, and its craggy, mountainous landscape, similar to [[Rolling Ridge]], uncommon to the lush, diverse terrain of Labrynna. 

Restoration Wall seems to derive its name from its only inhabitant, [[Patch]], known as the only man in [[Labrynna]] capable of not only item, but sword restoration, as well.<ref name="Patch"/> To reach Patch, Link must ascend the vertical maze of vines on the dangerous, rocky facade in this region, avoiding the falling boulders as he climbs higher, to reach his cave dwelling at the wall's summit. 

Reaching and completing Patch's {{Term|OoA|Patch's Crazy Cart|link|display= ceremony}} is a necessary step towards repairing the fractured [[Tuni Nut]] of Symmetry Village, the only item capable of balancing the city's foundations.<ref>{{Cite manual|quote= This nut is the symbol of Symmetry City-a city found in a distant corner of the world. If anything happens to this nut, the city will fall into a state of severe imbalance.|game= OoA|page= 33}}</ref>

At the present time in {{OoA|-}}, the Restoration Wall is completely replaced by the large river portion of the [[Nuun Highlands]].
