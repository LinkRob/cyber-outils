==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|CoH|Emerald Rapier}} are {{Plural|CoH|Weapon|link}} that can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}}. They can only be obtained after she infuses a {{Term|CoH|Rapier|link}} with an {{Term|CoH|Emerald|link}} at a {{Term|CoH|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. Like all {{Plural|CoH|Rapier}}, they can only be wielded by {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda}}.
