The {{Term/Store|Minish Village||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 112 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> It is located in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods|link}}, and is the capital of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}'s {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} world. {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}'s original population of {{Term|TMC|Minish}} founded this {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}}, which continues to thrive today, although many {{Term|TMC|Minish}} have relocated outside of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods}} since then. All {{Term|TMC|Minish}}, since their expansion, categorize themselves based on location: those still living within the confines of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village}} call themselves {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish|link}}, as do the many living in the outskirts and about the {{Term|TMC|Minish Woods}}. The {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish}} are supervised by their elder, {{Term|TMC|Gentari|link}}. The architecture of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village}} ranges from hollowed out {{Plural|TMC|Barrel|link}}, habitable shoes, and mushrooms &ndash; anything in the area big enough to support the sedentary, minimalist {{Term|TMC|Minish}} lifestyle. The {{Term|TMC|Minish Village}}'s existence is a secret to all but those with close ties to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Its very existence is a secret to all but those with close ties to the royal family.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=52}}</ref>

==Overview==
===Link's Reception in the Village===
When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} first arrives in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}}, the {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish}} act surprised to see him, as it has been ages since the last human visit to the village.<ref name="ezloentry">{{Cite|I gather it's been quite some time since they last saw a human. ...What's that? You didn't understand what they were saying just now? Ah yes. That was the language of the {{Color|TMC Green|Minish}}. It's a little different from the dialect I am most familiar with. I'm afraid I didn't catch most of what they said myself. But perhaps there is someone here who {{Color|TMC Blue|understands your language}}. We should look around.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref><ref name="festariintro">{{Cite|Hmm...I've never seen an outfit like that before. Are you a...human? Oh, my! It's been quite some time since any humans came here. My name is {{Color|TMC Green|Festari}}. I watch the abbey, as well as the shrine to the north. You...seem to be having some trouble with our language, don't you? You could use a {{Color|TMC Red|Jabber Nut}}. It will allow you to understand our tongue. You should be able to find one in the barrel house just south of here.|Festari|TMC}}</ref> Despite the relative shock shared by most {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish}} at {{Term|TMC|Link}}'s presence, a prominent {{Term|TMC|Minish}} named {{Term|TMC|Festari|link}} enthusiastically greets him at his home, revealing himself as an admirer and human enthusiast.

===Language===
The {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish}} that inhabit the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}} incorporate their own language into their societal culture. {{Term|TMC|Link}} and {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}} are unable to understand the language,<ref name="ezloentry" /> and therefore seek out the {{Term|TMC|Jabber Nut|link}}, hinted at by {{Term|TMC|Festari}} himself, in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}} so they may be able to understand the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} language.<ref name="festariintro" /> The {{Term|TMC|Minish}} of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village}} speak a language slightly different from that of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Realm}}, and do not understand the {{Term|Series|Hylian|link}} language.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The Minish Village. The Minish here speak a language slightly different from that of the Minish Realm, and they do not understand Hylian language.|book=E|publisher=Dark Horse Books|page=52}}</ref><ref name="ezloentry" />

===Dungeon===
North of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|display=Village}} lies the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}, the game's first {{Term|TMC|Dungeon|link}} and the location of the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}}. To access the {{Term|TMC|Dungeon}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} must get the approval of {{Term|TMC|Gentari}} and {{Term|TMC|Festari}}; the latter guards the passage to the {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|display=Shrine}} itself.

==Trivia==
*On a pier on the small lake east of {{Term|TMC|Festari}}'s home, a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}} can be obtained. After getting the {{Term|TMC|Flippers|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link}} can swim across the lake to reach a {{Term|TMC|Minish}}'s house, and [[Kinstone Fusion|fusing]] {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with him will result in a special {{Term|TMC|Heart Container|link}} becoming accessible.
*To the west of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village}}, a {{Term|TMC|Forest Minish}} named {{Term|TMC|Belari|link}} lives inside a small mushroom. He will give {{Term|TMC|Link}} his first {{Plural|TMC|Bomb|link}}, and will, after a certain {{Term|TMC|Kinstone}} fusion with {{Term|TMC|Gentari}}, upgrade them to {{Plural|TMC|Remote Bomb|link}}.
