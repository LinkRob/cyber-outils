{{Term/Store|Vintage Linen|Vintage Linens|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 142"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Vintage Linen}} are bronze-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of {{Term|TFH|The Dunes|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes|link}}''', ''"Adventure in the dark!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Vintage Linen}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Infinity Dunes}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Vintage Linen}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Vintage Linen}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Stone Corridors}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Vintage Linen}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Gibdo Mausoleum|link}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Vintage Linen}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Desert Temple|link}}''', no challenge: two {{Plural|TFH|Vintage Linen}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Vintage Linen}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Gust Garb|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Ninja Gi|link}} {{Plural|TFH|Outfit|link}}.<ref name="E 142"/>
