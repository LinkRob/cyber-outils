The {{Term/Store|Spring Water Cave||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Spring Water Cave}}<br/>Entry prohibited due to ghost sightings!|Sign|MM3D}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Spring Water Cave}} is referred to as the '''Ikana Cave''' in the {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 84}}</ref> However as this name contradicts the one given in {{MM}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Spring Water Cave}} was referred to as the '''Springwater Cave''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Springwater Cave}}<br/>Springwater Cave is located in the eastern region of Termina's Ikana Canyon.|retrieved= January 24, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160311084700/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/s.jsp#SpringwaterCave|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
The cave is found in the [[Ikana Canyon]] of [[Termina]]. It serves as the source of water which flows through the [[Ikana River]]. It houses several rocks and plenty of [[Bottled Water|Spring Water]] which can be [[Bottle]]d, but most importantly it is the resting place of the ghost of [[Composer Brothers#Sharp|Sharp]], one of the [[Composer Brothers]].<ref>{{Cite|Spring Water Cave<br/>Entry prohibited due to ghost sightings!|Sign|MM}}</ref>

According to Sharp's brother, [[Flat]], Sharp sold his soul to the devil,<ref>{{Cite|Oh...Sharp, my dear brother. He sold his soul to the devil and was the one who locked me in here...|Flat|MM}}</ref> and is the one responsible for the Spring Water Cave being dried up, subsequently resulting in Ikana River drying up as well. Due to the curse put on it, the [[Music Box House]] lacks the water to function properly, and as such, it is currently being encircled by [[Gibdo]]s roaming the outside of the house. Flat teaches [[Link]] the "[[Song of Storms]]", which is necessary in order to cleanse Sharp's dark soul, thus breaking the curse of the spring and restoring the natural flow of the River.<ref>{{Cite|Here is written the song that shall cleanse his cursed, black soul.|Flat|MM}}</ref>
