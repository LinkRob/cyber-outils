{{Term/Store|Set Bonus|Set Bonuses|Series, BotW|plural}} are a mechanic in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Main Quest|Set Bonus}}|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus}} are effects given to {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} when he equips certain complete {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set|link}}. Some {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set|link}} should be enhanced twice to give a {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus}}, like the {{Term|BotW|Radiant Set|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Armor Sets<br/>Wearing the top, bottom, and headwear from the same set can earn you some bonus effects.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus}} are related to and sometimes the superior forms of {{Plural|BotW|Effect|link}}, which are abilities granted to {{Term|BotW|Link}} upon wearing individual pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} or by consuming certain {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Set|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hylian Set|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Well-Worn Outfit|link}} are the only {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set}} that do not grant {{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus}}.

There exist unnamed {{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus}} that affect how various characters behave around {{Term|BotW|Link}}. For example, the {{Term|BotW|Dark Series|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Phantom Ganon Set|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tingle's Set|link}} cause people to react in fear of {{Term|BotW|Link}}. Similarly, the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to freely move about {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} without accusation of being a ''voe''. Despite this, {{Term|BotW|Greta|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I've never seen a voe disguise himself as a vai like you have, though...|Greta|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Jules|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Say, are you, uhh... It's really impressive—you getting in here without anyone else noticing!|Jules|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Paya|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A man?!}}|Paya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Quince|link}}<ref>{{Cite|Yo... Oh! Hey, girl... It's been a while since I've seen a vision as fine as you around he— Wait a sec... You're a guy, aren't you? Look, pal. Those clothes might fool someone from Gerudo, but they're not fooling this {{Color|BotW Blue|Hylian}}. *sigh* I should have known I wouldn't meet any women at this crummy stable.|Quince|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Muava|link}} will be able to see through {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s disguise.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'a... ... What's a voe doing here...|Muava|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Surprised? You might be able to fool all the other vai around here, but you won't fool me so easily. Don't worry, though. |Many enemies will not attack Link when you have this set bonus unless you attack them. If wearing a Bokoblin Mask, only Bokoblins will not attack you. If there is another enemy that notices you and starts to attack you, then any enemy that was not attacking you will start to. This is important to know, for if you put on a Bokoblin Mask and go into a group of enemies that is only Bokoblins, then they will not attack you. If the group that you walk into with the Bokoblin Mask includes any other enemy besides Bokoblins, then they will all attack you, including the Bokoblins. Majora's Mask is an exception because almost all enemies will not attack you when wearing it. Enemies that can be fooled with the disguise set bonus are Bokoblins, Moblins, Lizalfos, Stal enemies (except Stalnoxes) and Lynels (only temporarily, for they will start attacking after a certain amount of time has passed). Enemies that are not fooled are Octoroks, Hinoxes (including their Stal counterparts), any Yiga soldier, Keese, Wizzrobes, all Guardian variants and other bosses. It is also important to note that Lynels will start attacking if you pull out any of your runes.
