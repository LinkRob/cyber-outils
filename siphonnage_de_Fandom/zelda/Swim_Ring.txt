{{Term/Store|Swim Ring|Swim Rings|Series, PH|plural}} are items in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Swim Ring|Shipyard|PH}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|PH|Swim Ring}} are {{Plural|PH|Anchor|link}} belonging to the {{Term|PH|Vintage Ship|link}} set of {{Plural|PH|Ship Part|link}}. There is doubt that they actually work as {{Plural|PH|Anchor}}.<ref>{{Cite|It is what it is... No one knows if it works as an anchor.|Shipyard|PH}}</ref> Just like most {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}}, {{Term|PH|Link|link}} can collect multiple {{Plural|PH|Swim Ring}}. They can be obtained through various methods:
{{#section:Ship Parts|Methods}}

Attaching three, six, or all eight {{Term|PH|Vintage Ship}} parts to the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}} will increase its stamina to five, six, or seven {{Plural|PH|Heart}}, respectively. {{Plural|PH|Swim Ring}} can be sold to the {{Term|PH|Treasure Teller|link}} for either 50, 150, or 800 {{Plural|PH|Rupee|link}}. Their value is determined randomly when the [[Saving|Save]] file is created, and is the same for all {{Plural|PH|Ship Part}} from the {{Term|PH|Stone Ship}} set.
