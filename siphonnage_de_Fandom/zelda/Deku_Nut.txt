{{Term/Store|Deku Nut|Deku Nuts|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TMC|plural}} are recurring [[item]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 121 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM}}}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC|publisher= Nintendo|page= 112}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Deku Nut}}<br/>This little nut explodes on impact, dazzling fighters on the ground and launching those who are airborne.|Items|SSBU}}</ref> They are used to stun enemies with a bright flash when thrown to the ground.

==Location and uses==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In the item's first appearance in {{OoT}}, Deku Nuts are the dried buds of [[Deku Baba]]s. Although defeating Deku Babas is the most common source of these items, they can also be found on certain other enemies, in trees, and in [[jar]]s as well. They are usually for sale in shops at 15 [[Rupee|Rupees]] for five units.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoT3D|publisher= Prima|page= 42}}</ref> [[Business Scrub]]s will offer to sell some at a slightly higher price than shops, at five Deku Nuts for 20 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|I surrender! In return, I will sell you Deku Nuts! 5 pieces, 20 Rupees they are!|Business Scrub|OoT}}</ref> [[Deku Scrub|Deku Scrubs]], especially the [[Mad Scrub|Mad]] variant, also use Deku Nuts as their way of attacking, as they spit these at [[Link]] with their snouts to harm him.
[[File:OoT Deku Nut on Gohma.gif|thumb|288x288px|A Deku Nut in action, stunning [[Gohma|Queen Gohma]], the [[boss]] of [[Inside the Deku Tree]]]]
Throughout the game, Link can use Deku Nuts to temporarily stun enemies (which is indicated by a blue skin that filters over the enemy and a [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_DekuNut_Freeze.wav distinctive sound]). When a Deku Nut collides with the ground, it lets off a large flash which freezes enemies caught in the blast.<ref>{{Cite|Set it to C and try throwing it! It will flash and stun the enemy!|N/A|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Upon impact, it makes a blinding flash, freezing enemies in their tracks!|N/A|MM}}</ref> Oddly enough, even [[Dark Link]] or [[Ganon]] may be injured by Deku Nuts if timed correctly.

In {{OoT|-}}, it is possible to increase the maximum number of Deku Nuts the [[Hero of Time]] can carry in his inventory. The first upgrade increases the amount from 20 to 30.<ref>{{Cite|Now you can carry many Deku Nuts! You can hold up to 30 nuts!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> The second raises it from 30 to 40.<ref>{{Cite|You can now carry even more Deku Nuts! You can carry up to 40 nuts!|N/A|OoT}}</ref> These two upgrades can be obtained in any order.

The first upgrade is obtained as a child by entering the [[Forest Stage]] while wearing the [[Mask of Truth]] (additionally, the Deku Scrubs will refill the supply of Deku Nuts).<ref>{{Cite|All my young Deku Scrub brothers say... You have a horrible face! But don't worry! We will reward you with many Deku Nuts. Of course, we will also enable you to carry more of them! Abracadabra… Alakazaaaam!!|Deku Scrub|OoT}}</ref> In the original [[Nintendo 64]] version of {{OoT|-}}, due to a glitch, if the upgrade is not acquired before Link obtains the [[Poacher's Saw]] from the [[Trading Quest]], it becomes impossible to receive this upgrade. This was fixed in {{OoT3D}}.

The second upgrade can be purchased for 40 Rupees from a Business Scrub in the [[Lost Woods]], just outside the [[Sacred Forest Meadow]] entrance.<ref>{{Cite|I surrender! To make your quest easier, I can enable you to pick up more Deku Nuts! But, it'll cost you 40 Rupees!|Business Scrub|OoT}}</ref> The [[Bomb Bag]] is required. 

The two [[Sheikah]], [[Impa]] and [[Sheik]], appear to use Deku Nuts after speaking to Link to make themselves instantly vanish out of the young hero's sight. 

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, like in {{OoT|-}}, Deku Nuts are the weapons of Deku Scrubs, which spit them from their snouts. However, the Deku Nuts utilized by Deku Scrubs in this game do not explode on impact, and instead simply deal damage and crumble. That being said, [[Deku Link]] is able to drop Deku Nuts while flying using a [[Deku Flower]], which have the same effect as throwing them. Besides being used for battle, Deku Nuts also hold aesthetic value for the [[Deku Royal Family]] in particular, as the [[Deku King]]'s scepter is adorned with a large Deku Nut on top.

In the [[Pirates' Fortress]], when a [[Gerudo Pirate]] is defeated, similar to how Impa and Sheik use them in the game's prequel, they throw a Deku Nut to immediately escape from Link's view.

==={{TMC|-}}===
Deku Nuts appear in {{TMC}} as projectiles spit by [[Business Scrub]]s. [[Link]] can deflect them with his [[Shield]], and they will stun the Business Scrubs who fired them.

==={{TP|-}}===
In {{TP}}, Deku Nuts appear as objects and can be picked up from deceased Deku Babas and thrown like jars or rocks.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 9}}</ref> They will not explode or produce a flash, but instead will yield [[Slingshot Pellets|Deku Seeds]], which can be used with the [[Slingshot]].

==Spinoff appearances==
==={{HW|L|-}}===
[[File:HWDE Deku Nut Food Icon.png|64px|left]]
In {{HW|L}}, Deku Nuts appear as Plant-type [[Food#Hyrule Warriors|Food]]. They are imbued with the Lightning [[Element]]. When consumed by a [[Companion Fairy]] in the [[Dining Room]] of the [[My Fairy]] mode, Deku Nuts can increase the Fairy's Trust level and personality traits.

==Crossover appearances==

==={{SSBB|-}}===
Deku Nuts appear as an item in {{SSBB}}. When thrown, the Deku Nut explodes upon contact, stunning any nearby fighter for a few seconds. A Deku Nut also causes approximately 18% worth of damage. Deku Nuts also explode after a while if ignored while on the ground, so they can stun whoever is holding it. If a fighter gets caught in a Deku Nut explosion while in midair, they get blasted away. 

Deku Nuts also appear in the form of a [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|Trophy]] and a [[Sticker]].

==Trivia==
*As Deku Nuts are throwable items known to flash brightly with a "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_DekuNut_Snap.wav bang]" and stun targets when they explode, this would make them essentially [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]]' equivalent to a real-world [[wikipedia:Stun_grenade|stun grenade]].
*Ironically enough, [[Deku Scrub|Deku Scrubs]] in the Japanese versions of the main games are known as "Deku Nuts".
*The [[Great Deku Tree]]'s seeds in {{TWW}} resemble giant Deku Nuts.
*The way the [[Sheikah|Sheikahs]] and [[Gerudo Pirate|Gerudo Pirates]] use the Deku Nuts is an adaptation of the {{wp|Metsubushi|metsubushi techniques}}, used by real-life ninjas.
*When [[Impa]] and [[Sheik]] use a Deku Nut after speaking to [[Link]], a very small period of time passes before the impact's light vanishes and the Sheikah disappear, apparently implying that they go away instantly. It is possible, however, that Link stays stunned for longer, and only recovers consciousness after the item's effect fades away.
*In {{OoT}}, with the exception of the final blow with the [[Master Sword]], [[Ganon]] can actually be defeated using only Deku Nuts on his tail in the [[Final Boss]] battle. While more are required than Link can carry at once (even with upgrades), several of the pieces of rubble from [[Ganon's Castle]] on the battlefield contain Deku Nuts once Ganon smashes them open.
**If Deku Nuts were to hit Ganon while Link is in front of his archenemy, though it only temporally makes him stop and shake his head, the [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_Hookshot_Clang.wav sound effect] of the [[Hookshot]] being fired by a wall is oddly heard (likely due to the Demon King's sheer durability by the [[Triforce of Power]]). This also applies if the hero uses [[Din's Fire]].
*In {{OoT|-}}, if Link were to successfully hit [[Dark Link]] with a Deku Nut, a [[Glitches in Ocarina of Time|glitch]] occurs where the deadly shadow will begin to completely mimic his doppelganger's every move, including the [[Iron Boots]], where if Link were to wear them, Dark Link will similarly start moving as if he is also wearing the Iron Boots.
**It is possible for Dark Link to be stunned normally like regular enemies, but this is very rare.
*[[Deku Seeds]] in {{SS}} are incredibly similar to Deku Nuts in that they come from [[Deku Baba]]s or forest plants, produce a flash on impact, and stun enemies that come close to the flash. In context, it is possible that without full maturity, the seed is a weaker version of the Nut.
*In the [[Desert Colossus]] in {{OoT}} and the [[Great Bay Region]] in {{MM}}, Deku Nuts will occasionally drop from [[Tree|Coconut Tree]]s if Link [[Rolling|rolls]] into them. For the latter, interestingly, the coconuts themselves use the Deku Nut sprite but are darker.
