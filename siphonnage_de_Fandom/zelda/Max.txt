{{Term/Store|Max||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283{{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Max is the first-ranked [[Knight of the Cobble Kingdom|Knight]] and guardian of [[Mutoh's Temple]]. When he first meets [[Link]], he is surprised that a human was able to break the seal on the island, and tells him that he needs to draw the sacred crest on the corridor door, and hints that the answer can be found by reading the riddle written on a tablet in the corridor. When Link does this, he is allowed to meet [[King Mutoh]] himself.<ref>{{Cite|I am the first knight, Max. So a human can break this island's seal...To enter the king's temple, you must draw the secret crest on the corridor door. Discover the crest, and the great corridor door will open for you. You must solve the riddle written on the tablet in the corridor. I will open the entrance to the corridor. Now, be on your way...Ah. So you solved the riddle and opened the corridor door. Go forth and slay the monster that broke the king's rest. Meet the king.|Max|PH}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Max's name is a reference to [[Mack]], a [[Carpenter]] working for [[Mutoh]] in {{TMC}}.
