The {{Term/Store|Mountain Goddess||Series, ST}} is a character in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Path to the Altar of the Mountain Goddess|Sign|ST}}</ref>

==Biography==
She is an unseen deity mentioned by the [[Goron]] tribe that lives on the mountain in the [[Fire Realm]] of the re-established kingdom of [[Hyrule]]. A shrine, the [[Altar of the Mountain Goddess]], is built in her honor just beyond the boundaries of [[Goron Village]] and is a place of worship for the Gorons, namely their appointed ambassador to the shrine, [[Kagoron]]. Few others are permitted to enter her shrine of their own accord, though an exception is made for the young hero [[Link]] when he comes to Goron Village and is told to meet with Kagoron. 

Link goes to the shrine to meet with Kagoron to inquire about how to meet with the village elder and learn the location of the {{Term|ST|Fire Sanctuary|link}} so that he can restore the realm's lost [[Spirit Tracks (Object)|Spirit Tracks]] and reach the {{Term|ST|Fire Temple|link}} high atop the mountain. The Gorons send Kagoron to pray to the Mountain Goddess when the mountain becomes violent and unstable, hoping that their patron deity will help calm the mountain's rage (in reality the work of a monster named [[Cragma]]). Beyond this, not much is known of the Mountain Goddess's true nature or even her true identity. She is obviously well known amongst the Goron tribe, but no light is shed upon her origins or who she really is within the game, though she appears to be the one the Gorons turn to in times of crisis.
