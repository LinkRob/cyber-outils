{{Term/Store|Paralyzing Fog|Paralyzing Fog|Series, TWWHD}} is a trap in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD |publisher= Prima |page= 19}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog}} can be found in certain areas of the {{Term|TWWHD|Earth Temple|link}}. If {{Term|TWWHD|Link|link}} comes into contact with it, he will become {{Term|TWWHD|Curse|link|display=Cursed}}, meaning that none of his items work, including his {{Term|TWWHD|Sword}}. While affected, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} is surrounded by a concentrated smog, his body flashes purple, and he acts disoriented or weakened. The curse will not be lifted unless he gets out of the {{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog}} or finds a {{Term|TWWHD|Light Beam|link}} to take refuge under.

To get through the areas of {{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog}} safely, {{Term|TWWHD|Link}} can use the {{Term|TWWHD|Deku Leaf|link}} to blow the {{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog|display=Fog}} away from a small area at a time, and if he shines light at it with his {{Term|TWWHD|Mirror Shield|link}}, the part of the {{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog}} exposed to the light will dissipate. {{Term|TWWHD|Medli|link}} can also use the face of her {{Term|TWWHD|Rito Harp|link|display=Harp}} in the same fashion. The pair will often have to work together to travel through, or over, the {{Term|TWWHD|Paralyzing Fog}} unhindered.
