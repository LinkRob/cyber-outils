The {{Term/Store|Deku Butler's Son||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a mentioned character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 244}}</ref>

==Biography== 
The Deku Butler's Son is first mentioned by the [[Deku Butler]] after leading [[Link]] to the [[Mask of Scents]]. The butler apologizes to Link for traveling so fast, stating that Link reminds him of his son whom he used to race with through the [[Deku Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|Actually, when I see you, I am reminded of my son who left home long ago... Somehow, I feel as if I am once again racing with my son...|Deku Butler|MM}}</ref> During the [[New Day]], the Deku Butler is seen mourning the deceased Deku tree seen at the start of the game, which implies that the dead tree is the butler's lost son. 

== Trivia ==
*In the {{MM|Himekawa}}, the [[Deku Princess]] is said to have been in love with the Deku Butler's Son.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The princess was in love with him. How sad.|book= MM (Himekawa)|publisher= |page= 86}}</ref>
*It is widely believed by fans that the soul of [[Deku Link]] is the Deku Butler's Son. However, this is not confirmed by [[Nintendo]].
**If this is indeed the case, then the Deku statue provided by the [[Elegy of Emptiness]] may resemble how the son looked in life.
