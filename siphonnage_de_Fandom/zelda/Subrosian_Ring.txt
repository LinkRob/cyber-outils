The {{Term/Store|Subrosian Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Description">{{Cite|Subrosian Ring<br/>Become a Subrosian|Inventory|OoS}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Subrosian Ring can be found inside the cave where [[Talon]] sleeps at [[Mt. Cucco]] in {{OoS}}. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it transforms [[Link]] into a [[Subrosian]].<ref name="Description"/> While in this shape, Link cannot use items nor can he be eaten by [[Like Like]]s. The Subrosian Ring can be transferred to {{OoA}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
