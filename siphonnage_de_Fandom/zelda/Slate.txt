==Location and Uses==
The four {{Plural|OoA|Slate}} are found in the {{Term|OoA|Ancient Tomb|link}}. Within the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon|link}}, the {{Term|OoA|Slate Room|link}} contains a giant stone tablet sitting on a pedestal, with receptacles for the slates surrounding it. When all four are placed into their receptacles, the larger stone tablet will part, exposing the staircase that leads to the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon}}'s {{Term|OoA|Boss|link}}, {{Term|OoA|Ramrock|link}}.
