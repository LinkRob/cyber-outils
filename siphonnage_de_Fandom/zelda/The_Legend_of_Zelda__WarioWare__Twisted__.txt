'''The Legend of Zelda''' is a microgame in ''[[WarioWare|WarioWare: Twisted!]]'' and the second microgame in the ''WarioWare'' series based on the first {{TLoZ}}, after the ''WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!'' microgame of the [[The Legend of Zelda (WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!)|same name]].

==Gameplay==
Like most other microgames in ''WarioWare: Twisted!'', The Legend of Zelda microgame utilizes the cartridge's built-in sensor. The player must tilt the system so that [[Link]] can rotate his [[Shield]] and deflect the rocks spit out by [[Octorok]]s attacking him. If Link is hit by an Octorok, he takes damage, and the player loses.

The sprites in the background show unique birds-eye view sprites of Link and Octoroks that are not in {{TLoZ|-}}.

==Levels==
* '''Level 1:''' Two Octoroks will shoot.
* '''Level 2:''' Three Octoroks will shoot.
* '''Level 3:''' A large group of Octoroks will be present, but only a few will shoot.

==In-game text==
*'''Description:''' ''Defend yourself with your shield.''
