{{Term/Store|Octorok Sucker|Octorok Suckers|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 133"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Octorok Sucker}} are silver-rarity {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Riverside|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Secret Fortress|link}}''', ''"Evade the Wallmaster!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Octorok Sucker}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Abyss of Agony|link}}''', ''"Guard the orb!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Octorok Sucker}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Cove of Transition|link}}''', ''"Halved attack and defense!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Octorok Sucker}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Cove of Transition}}''', ''"Clear within the time limit!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Octorok Sucker}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Water Temple|link}}''', no challenge: one {{Term|TFH|Octorok Sucker}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Water Temple}}''', ''"Clear with halved energy!"'': two {{Plural|TFH|Octorok Sucker}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 500 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Octorok Sucker}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Jack of Hearts|link}}.<ref name="E 133"/>
