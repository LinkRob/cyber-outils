The {{Term/Store|Adult Wallet|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}},{{Exp Game|MM}}<ref name="E 115">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 115 {{Exp Game|OoT, MM}}}}</ref> also known as {{Term/Store|Adult's Wallet|OoT, OoT3D|plural}},{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref name="E 115"/> is an [[item]] in {{OoT}} and {{MM}}.

==Location and uses==
In all of its appearances, the Adult Wallet is the first upgrade to the standard [[Wallet]], capable of holding up to 200 [[Rupee]]s. The Adult Wallet is a light-brown sack decorated with a silver hexagon resembling a Silver Rupee. In {{OoT3D}} and {{MM3D}}, the hexagon is blue instead.

==Mainline appearances==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT|-}}, Link can obtain the Adult's Wallet after collecting ten [[Golden Skulltula Spirit|Gold Skulltula Tokens]] and then visiting the [[House of Skulltula]], where the first child freed from the curse gives Link the Adult's Wallet.

==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM|-}}, Link can obtain the Adult Wallet by depositing at least 200 Rupees in the [[Clock Town Bank]], as by doing so, the [[Banker]] gives Link the Adult Wallet.
