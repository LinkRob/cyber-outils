'''Stickers''' are collectible items in {{SSBB}}. Each sticker has artwork of various characters that have appeared in the games pertaining to the fighters appearing in {{SSBB|-}}. They can be found in several ways: by collecting them during Brawls, winning them through {{ssb|Spectator Mode}}, defeating enemies in stages of [[Adventure Mode: The Subspace Emissary]], won in the {{ssb|Coin Launcher}}, or by beating them out of the {{ssb|Sandbag}}. However, if the player gets a Game Over during the Subspace Emissary, they will lose the stickers that they have collected in that certain stage.

The main purpose of the stickers is to give characters certain advantages in the Subspace Emissary, such as increased attack power. They cannot be used in other modes such as Brawls or the {{ssb|Stadium}}. The stickers must be added to the [[Trophy (Super Smash Bros.)|trophy]] base of the character that the player wishes to use them on. 

There are a total of 700 stickers in {{SSBB|-}}, with each sticker having a different rarity level. Collecting all 700 stickers will unlock with the Stickers trophy.

==Sticker Effects==
Each sticker has a different effect, although some of these can only be used by certain characters. These effects only work in the Subspace Emissary mode.
