"{{Term/Store|The Temple of Time||Series, BotW}}",<ref>{{Cite Track|author= Yasuaki Iwata |track= The Temple of Time |discography= {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Sound Selection|link}} |publisher= Nintendo Co., Ltd. |year= 2017 |format= CD}}</ref> also known as "{{Term/Store|Temple of Time||OoT, OoT3D}}",{{Exp Game|OoT}}<ref>{{Cite Track|author= Nintendo |track= Temple of Time |discography= {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Sound Track CD|link}} |publisher= Nintendo Co., Ltd. |year= 1998 |format= CD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Track|author= Nintendo |track= Temple of Time |discography= {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D Official Soundtrack|link}} |publisher= Nintendo Co., Ltd. |year= 2011 |format= CD}}</ref> is a recurring {{Term|Series|Song|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}. "{{Term|Series|The Temple of Time}}" goes unnamed in {{TP}}.

==Overview==
==={{OoT|-}}===
"{{Term|OoT3D|The Temple of Time}}" is track 20 of {{Term|OoT|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Sound Track CD|link}}, track 44 of {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Original Sound Track|link}}, and track 24 of {{Term|OoT3D|The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D Official Soundtrack|link}}. {{Term|OoT3D|The Temple of Time}} plays inside the eponymous {{Term|OoT3D|Temple of Time|link}}.

==={{TP|-}}===
"{{Term|TPHD|The Temple of Time}}" is track 5 in Disc 3 of {{Term|TPHD|The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess HD Original Soundtrack|link}}.

==={{BotW|-}}===
"{{Term|BotW|The Temple of Time}}" is track 3 of {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Sound Selection|link}} and track 12 in Disc 1 of {{Term|Series|The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Original Soundtrack|link}}. It consists of the "{{Term|Series|Song of Time|link}}" being played in a very slow, drawn-out manner on a piano, appearing almost as individual notes.
