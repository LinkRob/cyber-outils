The {{Term/Store|Serpent's Toga||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 137}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Serpent's Toga makes you a chiseled, invincible statue. The effect fades upon moving... Such is fashion!}}

The {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}} after {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} completes {{Term|TFH|The Lady's Lair|link}}. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} features a snake-like headdress and turns {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s eyes bright red with slit pupils.

While wearing the {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga}}, {{Term|TFH|Link}} can turn into a stone statue by standing still for approximately one second, giving him temporary invincibility.<ref>{{Cite|Rock solid!<br/>♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;♦<br/>Stop moving and you'll turn to stone. Invincible stone! It's a curse, but it's not so bad...|Catalog|TFH}}</ref> In this state, {{Term|TFH|Link}} is unaffected by [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemy]] attacks, explosions of {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}}, and gusts of air, fire and ice. He remains vulnerable to falls, [[Quicksand]] and [[Lava]].

{{Term|TFH|Link}} can become a statue while carrying an object or other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} in a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}}, or while being carried as part of a {{Term|TFH|Totem}}, but not while readying a [[Items in Tri Force Heroes|Weapon]] or charging his {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} for a {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack|link}}. He will instantly revert to his normal state by performing any of the following actions:

*Walking or {{Term|TFH|Dash Attack|link|display=Dashing}}<ref>{{Cite|The Serpent's Toga makes you a chiseled, invincible statue. The effect fades upon moving... Such is fashion!|Madame Couture|TFH}}</ref>
*Using his {{Term|TFH|Sword}} or {{Term|TFH|Item}}
*Throwing or catching an object or other {{Plural|TFH|Links}}
*Being moved by another {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s {{Term|TFH|Boomerang|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Water Rod|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar|link}}, or {{Term|TFH|Gripshot|link}}
*Being thrown by another {{Term|TFH|Links}}; simply being lifted or caught in a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} will not cause him to revert.

The design of the {{Term|TFH|Serpent's Toga}} is likely inspired by {{Wp|Medusa}}, the {{Wp|Greek mythology|Greek mythological}} monster who could turn any who gazed upon her into stone, and who is often depicted with snakes in place of her hair.
