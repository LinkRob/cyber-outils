{{Term/Store|Joy Pendant|Joy Pendants|Series, TWW, TWWHD|plural}} are items in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 128}}</ref> They are a kind of [[Spoil]].

==Location and Uses==
Every [[Bokoblin]] stashes one Joy Pendant in its pockets.<ref name="Fishman">{{Cite|I hear the bird-people who live on {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Dragon Roost Island}} east of here have invented an incredibly useful device that they call a {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Grappling Hook}}... No lie, fry! I hear you can throw that thing at monsters and use it to grab {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|treasure they've hidden in their pockets}}. Talk about cool. Oh, and have you seen those {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|imp-like creatures}} called {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Bokoblins}} that hang out on the lookout platforms at sea and stare through their telescopes? I hear those guys keep {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Joy Pendants}} hidden in their pockets, so if you get one of those Grappling Hooks, you can use it to swipe their pendants!|Fishman|TWWHD}}</ref> Link may take their Joy Pendants by defeating them or by stealing them with the [[Grappling Hook]].<ref name="Fishman"/>

Each dungeon in the game has two to four [[Treasure Chest]]s containing Joy Pendants.{{verify|TWW}}

Once the [[Killer Bees]] lose to Link at [[Hide and Seek]], the delinquents hide a Joy Pendant atop the [[Tree]] next to the [[Bomb Shop]] on [[Windfall Island]].<ref>{{Cite|Actually, I gotta admit, we hid that thing up in the tree. We thought you might find it!|Ivan|TWWHD}}</ref> They do so in the hopes that Link will find it and give it to [[Mrs. Marie]] as a birthday gift, as they are too proud to make the peace offering directly.<ref>{{Cite|Listen up! It's gonna be {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|Mrs. Marie's}} birthday soon. We were thinkin' of givin' her a present, so it was good that we finally got our hands on that thing...|Ivan|TWWHD}}<br/>{{Cite|...But we can't be goin' around givin' presents to people! No way! That just don't fit the Killer Bees' style!|Jan|TWWHD}}<br/>{{Cite|Come on, bud! Can't you give it to Teach' for us? Can't you?|Ivan|TWWHD}}</ref> The Joy Pendant can be knocked from the Tree by rolling into it.

[[Mrs. Marie]] has made a hobby of collecting Joy Pendants due to her love of jewelry.<ref>{{Cite|To tell you the truth, I love jewelry above all else! And that... Why, that is extremely rare and hard to come by. It's the hottest thing around, dear!|Mrs. Marie|TWWHD}}</ref> Once Link gifts her one on behalf of the Killer Bees, she hints at wanting more.<ref>{{Cite|Oh my, yes! I will happily take a single one from you! No, no! I insist! Only one. To tell you the truth, {{Color|TWWHD Vermilion|I'd actually like about 20 of them}}. But that would probably only happen in my wildest of dreams.|Mrs. Marie|TWWHD}}</ref>

* In exchange for 20 or more Joy Pendants, she gives Link the [[Cabana Deed]] to the [[Private Oasis]].
* In exchange for 40 or more Joy Pendants, she gives Link the [[Hero's Charm]] in {{TWW|-}} and [[Treasure Chart|Treasure Chart #45]] in {{TWWHD}}.
* In exchange for additional{{How Many}} Joy Pendants, she gives Link [[Rupee]]s.{{How Many}}

[[Beedle]] buys Joy Pendants for 5 Rupees in {{TWW|-}} and 20 in {{TWWHD|-}}.
