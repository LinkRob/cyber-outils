==Characteristics==
{{Compendium/Extract|Wood Pigeon}}
{{Plural|BotW|Wood Pigeon}} are commonly found in forests, grasslands, and villages throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|These pigeons inhabit vast regions all throughout Hyrule, so they can often be found in forests, grasslands, or even villages.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Their bodies are gray in color, though their head has purple feathers. They're commonly hunted by amateur hunters due to their lack of awareness.<ref>{{Cite|They don't have a very strong sense of awareness, so even less-skilled hunters can nab them pretty easily.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When killed, they drop a {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Drumstick|link}}.
