{{Term/Store|Lynel Gear||Series, BotW}} is a recurring set of items in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC|publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}} is a type of weaponry made and exclusively carried by {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}}. It consists of three tiers of increasing strength and quality. The first tier is found in the possession of regular {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}}, while the stronger Mighty {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}} is used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}} and the strongest tier, Savage {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}}, is used by {{Plural|BotW|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Lynel|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Lynel|link}}. In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} carry {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}} that is a tier below what they typically wield. As a whole, {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}} is distinguished by its high strength and durability. As {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} are typically large and composed of heavy stone and metal, many {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}} have difficulty using {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}} as easily as a {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} does.<ref name="Lynel Sword">{{Cite|{{Big|Lynel Sword}}<br/>This Lynel-made sword was designed with smashing in mind rather than slicing. It's on the heavy side compared to what Hylians are used to, but it's very strong.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Lynel Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Lynel Spear}}<br/>The crescent-shaped spearhead of this Lynel-made weapon gives it poor balance, making it difficult to wield. Lynels, however, can swing it effectively with a single hand.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Mighty Lynel Spear">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Lynel Spear}}<br/>The weight and cutting edge of this Lynel-made spear have both been enhanced. It's immensely heavy for a Hylian, but a Lynel can cleave through rock with a single swing.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Mighty Lynel Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Lynel Bow}}<br/>This massive Lynel bow sports a bowstring made from a metal so tough, mere Hylians have trouble drawing it back.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Lynel Sword|link}} and its variations are sturdily built, and the most basic kinds rely on physical impact over their slicing edges for damage.<ref name="Lynel Sword"/> Higher-tier swords, however, possess keener edges enhanced by the presence of secondary blades branching off from the primary one, and they can cut foes in two with a passing blow.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Lynel Sword}}<br/>This Lynel-made sword boasts more blades and more attack power. A skilled Lynel can draw this sword simply in passing and still cut a foe in two.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Savage Lynel Sword}}<br/>A brutal sword carried by white-haired Lynels. The savage blades are strong enough to cut down any foe, no matter how strong.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Lynel}} two-handed weapons consist of large metal clubs named {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Crusher|link}}.<ref name="Lynel Crusher">{{Cite|{{Big|Lynel Crusher}}<br/>This two-handed weapon is favored by the Lynels. It may be more accurate to call it a lump of metal than a weapon, but if wielded by a Lynel, it can pound you into a fine dust.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> They are used primarily through powerful downward blows, which can crush armored foes into the dust.<ref name="Lynel Crusher"/><ref name="Mighty Lynel Crusher">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Lynel Crusher}}<br/>This Lynel-made two-handed weapon has been reinforced to increase its durability and striking power. Its overwhelming heft will crush your foe, shield and all.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Lower-tier {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Crusher}} are little more than lumps of metal, but higher-quality {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Crusher|display=Crushers}} are strengthened with more deliberately fashioned metal reinforcements, which additionally increase their weight and thus the power of their downward swing.<ref name="Mighty Lynel Crusher"/><ref name="Savage Lynel Crusher">{{Cite|{{Big|Savage Lynel Crusher}}<br/>This Lynel-made two-handed weapon is immensely heavy thanks to a rare metal from Death Mountain's peak. The power of its downward swing is in a class all its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Crusher|link}} in particular are fitted with a rare kind of metal obtained from the peak of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref name="Savage Lynel Crusher"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Spear|link}} are tipped with crescent-shaped blades designed for swinging blows, but the shape of their blades make them difficult to balance.<ref name="Lynel Spear"/> Higher-tier variants possess larger, axe-like blades, which greatly increase the weight of the weapon and consequently the force of its blows.<ref name="Mighty Lynel Spear"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Savage Lynel Spear}}<br/>White-haired Lynels favor this brutal spear. Its axe-like spearhead and exceptional weight give it absolute destructive power.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Lynel}} are known to be able to cleave through rocks with a single blow of these {{Plural|BotW|Lynel Spear|display=Spears}}.<ref name="Mighty Lynel Spear"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Bow|link}} are formed entirely from metal, as are their bowstrings.<ref name="Mighty Lynel Bow"/><ref name="Lynel Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Lynel Bow}}<br/>A Lynel-made bow crafted from rough metal. True to the vicious nature of Lynel weaponry. It fires a spread of multiple arrows at once. Ideal for taking down quick-moving targets.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Savage Lynel Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Savage Lynel Bow}}<br/>This Lynel bow is made from a special steel found at the peak of Death Mountain. It has tremendous stopping power and can pierce thick armor as easily as thin paper.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Bow|link}}, similarly to {{Plural|BotW|Savage Lynel Crusher}}, are made from a kind of steel obtained from the the peak of {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain}}.<ref name="Savage Lynel Bow"/> They fire multiple {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} at once, making them well-suited to taking down swift foes, and they propel them with enough force to pierce conventional {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}.<ref name="Lynel Bow"/><ref name="Savage Lynel Bow"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Lynel Shield|link}} possess blade-like cutting edges; higher-tier shields possess more such blades than lower-tier ones.<ref name="Lynel Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Lynel Shield}}<br/>A sturdy shield favored by Lynels for its defensive and offensive capabilities. First and foremost a shield, but the banded edges can deal slashing attacks when deflecting.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Mighty Lynel Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Lynel Shield}}<br/>This Lynel-made shield has been reinforced with armor and even more blades. Stronger in both defense and offense, it can tear through basic armor when deflecting.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> This trait allows them to deal increased damage to foes when parrying blows, although they are still intended primarily as defensive implements.<ref name="Lynel Shield"/><ref name="Mighty Lynel Shield"/><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Savage Lynel Shield}}<br/>This ultimate Lynel shield is used only by the white-haired Lynels. It excels at defending against even the most brutal of attacks and cutting down powerful foes when deflecting.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Lynel Gear}} is formed out of metal, all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} within this archetype sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, but they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, they and can be manipulated through use of the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
