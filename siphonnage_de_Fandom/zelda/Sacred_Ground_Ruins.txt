The {{Term/Store|Sacred Ground Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Sacred Ground Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Sacred Ground Ruins are an ancient site in [[Hyrule Field#Breath of the Wild|Hyrule Field]] south of the [[Hyrule Castle Town Ruins]] where [[Hyrule]]’s [[Royal Guard]]s were honored by the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]].<ref>{{Cite|Armor worn by fierce phantoms, feared even by heroes, is hidden {{Color|BotW Red|in the ruins of Hyrule Field}}. Find these pieces {{Color|BotW Red|at a battleground where brave souls tested their skill}}...{{Color|BotW Red|at ceremonial grounds where royal guards are honored}}...and {{Color|BotW Red|at the ruins where the soldiers of Hyrule gathered}}.|Misko's EX Journal|BotW}}</ref> At the center of the Sacred Ground Ruins, there is a symbol of a [[Triforce]] that bears the crests of [[Din]], [[Farore]], and [[Nayru]] in their respective Triforce positions. There is also a [[Wingcrest]] in the blank space of the Triforce.

Prior to the second [[Great Calamity]], [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] and the [[Champion]]s gathered here to honor [[Link]]’s appointment as Zelda’s personal knight.<ref>{{Cite|Link is chosen to be the appointed knight to Zelda, princess of the royal family of Hyrule. Zelda does her best to facilitate the fabled "ceremony of legend," as suggested by Daruk, but her own insecurities are laid bare...|Memory|plural= true|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hero of Hyrule, chosen by the sword that seals the darkness... You have shown unflinching bravery and skill in the face of darkness and adversity. And have proven yourself worthy of the blessings of the Goddess Hylia. Whether skyward bound, adrift in time, or steeped in the glowing embers of twilight... The sacred blade is forever bound to the soul of the Hero. We pray for your protection...and we hope that—that the two of you will grow stronger together, as one.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> Consequently, the Sacred Ground ruins are the site of the [[Recovered Memory]], "[[Subdued Ceremony]]", which Link can recall by visiting the Ruins after speaking to [[Impa]] and receiving the "[[Captured Memories]]" [[Main Quest]].

A [[Korok]] can be found by lifting a [[Rock]] atop the northeastern pillar of the Sacred Ground Ruins. He will give Link a [[Korok Seed]] in return for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> In the "[[EX Treasure: Phantasma]]" [[Side Quest]] exclusive to [[The Master Trials]] DLC Pack, a metallic [[Treasure Chest]] is added to the Sacred Ground Ruins. It holds the [[Phantom Armor]] and is lodged in the ground in the southeastern portion of the Ruins’ pool.
