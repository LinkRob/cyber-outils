{{Plural|Series|Wind Switch}} are pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}}, found within {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}. They can be activated using the wind of a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}}, which will cause them to spin. When active, a {{Term|BotW|Wind Switch}} can open portcullises. In several {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, multiple {{Plural|Series|Wind Switch}} need to be activated in order to create a change in the environment.
