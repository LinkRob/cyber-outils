"{{Term/Store|The Spring of Power||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Spring of Power}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|East Akkala Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can meet {{Term|BotW|Nobo|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry! I haven't introduced myself. I'm Nobo.|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> While she has traveled all over {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, she finds that she enjoys {{Term|BotW|Akkala|link}} the best.<ref>{{Cite|I've traveled everywhere, but at the end of the day, I like {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala}} best of all.|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> As a result, she is familiar with the terrain of the area,<ref>{{Cite|That's why I'm pretty familiar with stuff around here.|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> and so she asks if there is something {{Term|BotW|Link}} would like to know.<ref>{{Cite|Is there something you wanna know?|Nobo|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the surrounding area, {{Term|BotW|Nobo}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|link}} is west of the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[This area?] Let's see... If you go {{Color|BotW Red|west}} of the stable, there's a sacred place called the {{Color|BotW Red|Spring of Power}}!|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> She shares a legend that says that one should offer a "sacred article" to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|display=Spring}},<ref>{{Cite|"Offer the {{Color|BotW Red|sacred article}} to the Spring of Power." That's what the legend says.|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> which she thinks may have been part of the rituals {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} performed.<ref>{{Cite|Maybe it was part of a ritual the {{Color|BotW Red|princess}} of {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}} used to perform?|Nobo|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.

At the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pray to the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue|link}}, which congratulates him for finding the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power|display=Spring}}.<ref>{{Cite|... You have done well to find this spring.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> It commands him to offer {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale|link}} and says {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} will guide him.<ref>{{Cite|Offer {{Color|BotW Red|Dinraal's scale}}, received from the red spirit, to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Spring of Power}}. I, Goddess Hylia, will guide you.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Dinraal|link}} flies through {{Term|BotW|Eldin's Flank|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Tanagar Canyon|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain one of its {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale|display=Scales}} by shooting an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} at its body. Once he has obtained a {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale|display=Scale}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to offer it to the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power}}. When he does, the entryway behind the {{Term|BotW|Goddess Statue}} will open, allowing access to {{Term|BotW|Tutsuwa Nima Shrine|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Hylia}} encourages him to move forward.<ref>{{Cite|Your path has shown itself. Now go forth.|Goddess Statue|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.
