==Location and Uses==
Stable Saddles are the default {{Term|BotW|Saddle|link}} given to {{Plural|BotW|Horse|link}} when they're registered at a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}. The only Horses who don't receive these Saddles are {{Term|BotW|Epona|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Giant Horse|link}}, who will be equipped with their own special Saddles. While {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can replace them throughout his journey, he can always change his Horses back into them by speaking to workers at {{Term|BotW|Highland Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Outskirt Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Stable|link}}, or {{Term|BotW|Woodland Stable|link}}.<ref name="Canni"/>
