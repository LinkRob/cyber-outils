'''Cactoroks''' are [[enemy|enemies]] in {{ZA}}.

==Characteristics==
Cactoroks are cacti creatures appearing only in the [[South Gubasha Desert]]. Cactoroks can attack [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] by throwing [[rock]]s at her. If Zelda either touches a Cactorok or is hit by one of their rocks, she takes a [[Heart]] of damage. Cactoroks take three hits from the [[Wand]] to defeat, or two if Zelda has five or six [[Celestial Sign]]s.

==Nomenclature==
Their name is a portmanteau of "cactus" and "[[Octorok]]," a species in {{TLoZ|Series}} capable of spitting rocks.
