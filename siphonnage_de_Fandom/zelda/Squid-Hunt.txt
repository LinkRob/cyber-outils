{{Term/Store|Squid-Hunt||Series, TWW}} is a [[Mini-Game]] in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TWWHD|publisher= Prima|page= 293}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The Squid-Hunt minigame is found inside the lighthouse on [[Windfall Island]] and is run by [[Salvatore]], costing 10 [[Rupee|Rupees]] to play. During the game, [[Link]], the Hero of Winds, must find and destroy three randomly hidden squids in the 8×8 grid with only 24 cannonballs.<ref>{{Cite|Zere are three squid groups: a large one, a medium one, und a small one!|Salvatore|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But be varned...you haff only 24 cannonballs zat you can fire.|Salvatore|TWW}}</ref> The squids are two, three, and four squares in length and only reveal their locations upon bombing the squares in which they are hidden.<ref>{{Cite|To destroy zee marauding squid pods, you must score four direct hits on zee large, three on zee medium, und two on zee small!|Salvatore|TWW}}</ref>

The first time Link finds all three squids, he is rewarded with a [[Piece of Heart]].<ref>{{Cite|Hoorayyy! Yayyy! Yayyy! Oh, thank you, Mr. Sailor! Please take this Piece of Heart as a sign of our gratitude. You are soooooo GREAT!|Salvatore|TWW}}</ref> A second victory yields [[Treasure Chart]] 7.<ref>{{Cite|Hoorayyy! Yayyy! Yayyy! Oh, thank you so much, Mr. Sailor! This is our thanks to you! It's been passed down on our island for many years, so don't tell the island elder, OK? Here... Please accept this Treasure Chart!|Salvatore|TWW}}</ref> Every subsequent victory provides a Purple Rupee.<ref>{{Cite|Yayyy! Yayyy! Yayyy! Oh, thank you, Mr. Sailor! Please accept all of our combined allowances! Take these 50 Rupees as your reward! Hooray for money!|Salvatore|TWW}}</ref> If Link can locate all three squids using fewer than 20 shots, he will also be rewarded with Treasure Chart 23.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa. Wait a sec. 20 Cannonballs used in Zee Fleet?! Hey, that's a new record... So, uh, you get this, too.|Salvatore|TWW}}</ref> After Link has obtained both Treasure Charts, he will receive an Orange Rupee every time he beats his personal record.

==Trivia==
*The rules of Squid-Hunt are based on the famous [[wikipedia:Battleship_(game)|Battleship board game]].
*Three Yellow Rupees can be found behind the model ship seen in the background of the grid.[[File:TWW Squid-Hunt JP version.png|thumb|208x208px|The Mini-Game as seen in the Japanese version]]
*In the Japanese version, the goal of the game is to sink ships rather than squids, making it a direct reference to Battleship. It is unclear why this was changed for the international release.<ref>{{Cite Web|author=PyoroX  |published=January 15, 2011  |retrieved=June 27, 2021  |url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1yyZyy_cMaU  |title=Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker - Japanese Battleship Mini-game  |site=YouTube  |type=video }}</ref>
*In 2020, nearly two decades after {{TWW|-}}'s initial release, members of the [[speedrun]]ning community reverse-engineered the minigame and published an [http://peter.website/web-sploosh-kaboom/ open-source probability calculator] for it.<ref>{{Cite Web|author=Linkus7|published=May 19, 2020|retrieved=January 3, 2022|url=https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1hs451PfFzQ|title=How We Solved the Worst Minigame in Zelda's History|site=YouTube|type=video}}</ref>
*Salvatore's comical "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Salvatore_Sploosh.wav Sploosh!]" (miss) and "[https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_Salvatore_Kerboom.wav KERBOOM!]" (hit) quotes during the mini-game has become a popular meme among [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]' fanbase.
**However, in the Japanese release of the game, he does not speak in English for its [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_SalvatoreJP_Hit.wav hit] and [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/WW_New/WW_SalvatoreJP_Miss.wav miss] equivalents.
