{{Term/Store|Jeddo Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Jeddo Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Jeddo Bridge is a dilapidated wooden bridge that spans over the [[Tamio River]] between [[Hyrule Field]] and [[Hyrule Ridge]].

A [[Yiga Footsoldier]] may pass through the Jeddo Bridge disguised as a [[Traveler]].

Two [[Treasure Chest]]s can be found on Jeddo Bridge in [[Master Mode]]. The first is a metallic Treasure Chest containing a [[Royal Bow]], situated atop platform suspended by [[Sky Octorok]]s east of the Bridge. A second metallic Chest containing a [[Topaz]], can also be found atop a platform west of the Bridge.
