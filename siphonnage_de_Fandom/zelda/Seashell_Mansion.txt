The {{Term/Store|Seashell Mansion||Series, LA, LADX}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 234{{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>{{Note|This location is also referred to as '''Shell House''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= LA|publisher= Nintendo|page= }}</ref> However, as {{E}} supersedes the guide, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
The Seashell Mansion is a building where [[Link]] can redeem the [[Secret Seashell]]s he has collected for one of several different rewards. When Link has five or ten Seashells, the [[Spirit of the Mansion]] will give him another Seashell. When he has collected at least twenty Seashells, he will receive the [[Level 2 Sword]].
