{{Term/Store|Morpheel||Series, TP, TPHD}} is the {{Term|TP|Boss|link}} of the {{Term|TP|Lakebed Temple}} in [[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|''The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess'']]<ref name="E 194"/> and the third overall boss of the game. As Morpheel's name suggests, it is a massive and serpentine monster resembling an eel.

When {{Term|TP|Link|link}} enters {{Term|TP|Morpheel}}'s chamber, he only finds a single tentacle at the bottom. Once he sinks to the chamber's ground, the rest of Morpheel's tentacles and its face emerge. To fight Morpheel, {{Term|TP|Link}} must equip the {{Term|TP|Iron Boots|link}} and avoid the {{Term|TP|Bombfish|link}} it releases. He must grab the creature's eyeball with the {{Term|TP|Clawshot}} and then attack it immediately. After this is done twice, Morpheel's tentacles retract and its eyeball gets swallowed, ending up on its back.<ref>{{Cite|Did you see its back? It's got an eyeball there, too!|Midna|TP}}</ref> 

Afterwards, the creature's full body is revealed as it swims around the chamber. If {{Term|TP|Morpheel}} swims in front of {{Term|TP|Link}}, it will suck him in and damage him, spitting him out afterwards. {{Term|TP|Link}} must proceed to swim above it in order to use the {{Term|TP|Clawshot}} to latch onto its eye.<ref>{{Cite|So, it's eyeball really is the key! Good! Keep aiming for that eye!|Midna|TP}}</ref> After slashing it several times, {{Term|TP|Link}} is thrown off. He must continue to hook onto the creature until it is vanquished, causing it to crash into the wall, draining all the water in the room.

Upon defeating {{Term|TP|Morpheel}}, {{Term|TP|Link}} receives the third and final {{Term|TP|Fused Shadow|link}} and a {{Term|TP|Heart Container|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|TP|Morpheel}}'s name is a combination of ''morph-'' and ''eel'', sharing the prefix with {{Term|TP|Morpha}}, a similar boss from {{OoT}}.

==Trivia==
*There are several notable similarities between {{Term|TP|Morpheel}} and {{Term|TP|Morpha}} from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time]]''.
**Both are the bosses of the water-themed dungeon in their respective games.
**Both take the form of a serpent-like creature.
**In both games, {{Term|TP|Zora's Domain|link}} is frozen and the water level in {{Term|TP|Lake Hylia|link}} is reduced. However, {{Term|TP|Morpheel}} is not the cause of these phenomena.
**Both have a weak point in the form of an eye that travels along a tentacle.
**Both require the {{Term|TP|Hookshot|link}}/{{Term|TP|Clawshot}} to be defeated.
**Both of their names begin with "Morph”.
*Morpheel also has a multiple similarities to that of the [https://teamico.fandom.com/wiki/Colossus_VII#Original Seventh Colossus], another boss from the video game, ''[https://teamico.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_of_the_Colossus Shadow of the Colossus]''.
**Both are water-themed bosses.
**Both have a serpent-like design.
**Both are fought by its sword-wielding protagonist.
**Both are fought entirety underwater. 
**Both are fought very similarity, with the player having to grab onto a certain part of their head to deal damage.
