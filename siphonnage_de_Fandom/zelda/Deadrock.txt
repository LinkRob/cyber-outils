{{Term/Store|Deadrock|Deadrocks|Series, ALttP, FSA, ALBW, TFH|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Enemy|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 173 {{Exp Game|ALttP, FSA, ALBW, TFH}}}}</ref> They are bipedal, rock-based creatures that typically make their home on mountain ranges, such as [[Death Mountain]]. When hit by a weapon such as a [[Sword]], they will briefly morph into impenetrable rock statues and remain in that state for a few moments before transforming back into their normal, mobile form. Their statue form renders them invulnerable to nearly all attacks.

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
In {{ALttP}}, Deadrocks appear on the mountain ranges of Death Mountain in the [[Light World]]. They move in quick, erratic patterns and inflict heavy damage if they run into [[Link]], making them particularly dangerous enemies. When they are hit with weapons such as his [[Sword]], they transform into stone statues and remain that way for a few seconds before becoming mobile again. While the statue form is nearly invincible, they will not cause damage when stunned, allowing Link to pass them by unhindered. Although Deadrocks cannot be defeated by most weapons, if [[Magic Powder]] is sprinkled on a Deadrock or if Link uses the [[Quake Medallion]], they will turn into [[Slime]]s and can easily be dispatched.

==={{FSA|-}}===
Deadrocks in {{FSA}} are similar to their {{ALttP|-}} counterparts, though they may be destroyed by fire and can be crushed with the [[Magic Hammer]].

==={{ALBW|-}}===
Deadrocks in {{ALBW}} have the same behavior as those in {{ALttP|-}}. However, when turned into stone, they are vulnerable to the [[Hammer]] and [[Bomb]]s. They can be spawned from the falling boulders erupted from volcanoes.

==={{TFH|-}}===
{{Plural|TFH|Deadrock}} appear in {{Plural|TFH|Level}} of the {{Term|TFH|Ice Cavern|link}} in {{TFH}}, in contrast to the warmer climates that they seem to prefer. As with their previous appearances, they run around erratically and are often found in groups. They are normally found in places with slick, icy floors suspended above bottomless pits, rendering the fight much more difficult. Striking them with any weapon briefly turns them into stone statues, which can then be destroyed by quickly smashing them with the {{Term|TFH|Magic Hammer|link}}. As these {{Plural|TFH|Deadrock}} return to their regular forms much faster than in previous appearances, it is advisable that the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} try to team up against them, and to use the {{Term|TFH|Sword|link}} against an active {{Term|TFH|Deadrock}} rather than the {{Term|TFH|Magic Hammer}}, which cannot be swung quickly enough in succession. Wearing the {{Term|TFH|Cozy Parka|link}} to gain traction or {{Term|TFH|Hammerwear|link}} to increase the {{Term|TFH|Magic Hammer|display=Hammer}}'s speed and range will make the battle much easier. {{Plural|TFH|Deadrock}} appear in the {{Term|TFH|Silver Shrine|link}} and the {{Term|TFH|Ice Temple|link}}, as well as the {{Term|TFH|Scorching Zone|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Den of Trials|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
{{Plural|CoH|Deadrock}} appear as enemies in {{CoH}}, where they can be found within {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Desert|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Gerudo Ruins|link}}. They chase the player on every beat, and when attacked, they transform into rock. They must then be hit with an explosive (i.e. a [[Bomb]], a [[Bombchu]], or [[Din's Fire]]) to completely kill them. When in rock form, they can be picked up while wearing the {{Term|CoH|Power Glove|link}} and thrown at enemies. Tossing them into lava will not only kill the Deadrock, but also turn the lava into hot coal which could be walked on, making them useful for navigating areas with lots of lava.
{{Ambig|End}}

==Nomenclature==
The name Deadrock likely refers to their appearance and defensive abilities, as enemies with small, angelic wings who turn to stone.
{{Nomenclature}}

== Trivia ==

* The Deadrock has notable differences in its appearance in later games since ''[[The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past|A Link to the Past]]'', as it no longer has wings and horns are added on its head, making the enemy more beast-like.
