'''''Smashing...Live!''''' is the official soundtrack album of ''[[Super Smash Bros. Melee]]'', recorded live by the {{Wp|New Japan Philharmonic|New Japan Philharmonic Orchestra}}. The album includes three ''Zelda'' songs: the palace theme from {{TAoL}}, the main ''Zelda'' theme, and "[[Saria's Song]]" (featured in the "Smash Bros. Great Medley").
