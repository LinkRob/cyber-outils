The {{Term/Store|Tingle Times|Tingle Times|Series|plural}} are articles on signs posted throughout the Hylian landscape in {{FSA}}.{{Name Ref|FSA}} The writings offer insight into the childish nature and fairy obsession of [[Tingle]], in addition to providing a few hints to the relevant area.

==Volume 0==
Found at [[Tingle's Tower]], while playing the Monster Hunting/Monster Hunting Returns minigame.

     -Tingle Times-
                  Volume 0<br><br>
     Tingle's a tad upset about
     all the monsters appearing
     hereabouts!<br>
     If you're going to kick the
     beasts around a bit, you'd
     best have Force Fairies!<br>
     And no whining!
                  Tingle

==Volume 1==
Found outside of [[Hyrule Castle]].

     -Tingle Times-
                  Volume 1<br><br>
     Tingle had an encounter with
     a charming lady fairy! It
     was fate! Destiny!<br>
     I was sneaking along behind
     her when she went into a
     castle... and never came out!<br>
     Surely she's been captured,
     because she's too adorable
     for her own good!<br>
     If you happen to see
     Miss Fairy, gaze upon
     her lovingly for me. <br>
     Oh... and save her too!
                  Tingle

==Volume 2==
Found in the [[Village of the Blue Maiden]].

     -Tingle Times-
               Volume 2
     Strange days have arrived
     in the village as one child
     after another disappears.<br>
     Tingle bets that they're all
     ditching school, no doubt
     playing some charming game.<br>
     Ooh!
     Tingle wants to play, too!<br>
     If you discover the kids'
     whereabouts, play awhile
     for Tingle's sake!<br>
     Search 'til your eyes
     are bloodshot and weary!
                    Tingle

==Volume 3==
Found in [[The Field]].

     -Tingle Times-
                Volume 3<br><br>
     Tingle hears that little
     boys and girls who behave
     get to meet a fairy here.<br>
     Perhaps if Tingle does his
     best to be kind, he may meet
     this fairy face to face!<br>
     Here's a good deed! I'll put
     up road signs!<br>
     (up) Someone's Storeroom
     (left) Then (up)... Strange Cave
     (left)...Someone's House<br>
     Praise my kindness! <br>
                   Tingle

==Volume 4==
Found in [[The Swamp]].

     -Tingle Times-
                Volume 4<br><br>
     Tingle got stuck in icky
     sludge! It was so... slimy.<br>
     Tingle must write this all
     down so that the same thing
     doesn't happen next time.<br>
     I will gracefully flit
     from leaf to handy leaf,
     jumping with such joy. <br>
     Almost like skipping from
     Zora to Zora!<br>
                   Tingle

==Volume 5==
Found in [[Lost Woods]].

     -Tingle Times-
                Volume 5<br><br>
     Ooh, everywhere I go, woods,
     woods, and more woods!
     Lost Woods everywhere! <br>
     Here's a solitary road sign
     just for you!<br>
     (up) Woods
     (left) (right) Woods
     (down) Woods<br>
     Perfect!<br>
                   Tingle

==Volume 6==
Found in [[Kakariko Village]].

     -Tingle Times-
                Volume 6<br><br>
     WAAAAAAAAHHHHH!
     Tingle's been had! <br>
     Someone's stolen his
     precious Force Gems!<br>
     This village is filled to
     the brim with thieves!
     It's not fair...<br>
     If you see the culprit,
     please catch him for Tingle!<br>
     Show no mercy!<br>
                   Tingle
