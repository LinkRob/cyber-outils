The {{Term/Store|Akkala Parade Ground Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Akkala Parade Ground Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Parade Ground Ruins}} are located on the {{Term|BotW|South Akkala Plains|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}, south of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Parade Ground Ruins|display=Ruins}} consist of rows of planted {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} and an abandoned fountain.

A {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} sleeps in the center of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Parade Ground Ruins}}.<ref>{{Cite|South Akkala Plains<br/>{{Big|Hinox}}|N/A|BotW}}</ref> It has a single wooden brace around one of its ankles, which is capable of blocking attacks from {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}. However, this brace can be burned away with {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}}. Around the {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}}'s neck is a {{Term|BotW|Royal Broadsword|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Royal Bow|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Royal Halberd|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} can pick up upon its defeat.
