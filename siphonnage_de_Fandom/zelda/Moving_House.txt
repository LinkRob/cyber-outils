The {{Term/Store|Moving House||Series, FSA}} is a location in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 265}}</ref> It was made by the [[Old-Timer in Moving House|Old Timer]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh ho ho ho! At last, at last! I've done it! My lifelong dream... A moving house!!!|Old-Timer in Moving House|FSA}}</ref> The house itself is made of a special light, yet sturdy wood.<ref>{{Cite|It's made of a special light, yet sturdy, wood!|Old-Timer in Moving House|FSA}}</ref> The wood is also fireproof,<ref>{{Cite|. . . Fire poses no threat at all! It's absolutely, completely fireproof!|Old-Timer in Moving House|FSA}}</ref> which makes it able to have four blowtorches on each of the four sides on the roof of the house.<ref name="Guide 39">{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= FSA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 39}}</ref> As the name indicates, one is able to push or pull the house in different directions. If the [[Links|four Links]] do so, the blowtorch on the opposite side of the Links will spurt fire.

The purpose of the house is to defeat the [[Deadrock]]s that are roaming around in the area. After all of them have been defeated, four [[Hammer]]s will appear at the entrance of the area.<ref name="Guide 39"/>

The inside of the house is kept wholly unfurnished so that it remains light enough to move. This doesn't seem to bother its owner, however.<ref>{{Cite|Fill the house with furniture and it becomes too heavy to budge. That's why I've had to leave it entirely empty. It's a bit stark, but I like it!|Old-Timer in Moving House|FSA}}</ref>
