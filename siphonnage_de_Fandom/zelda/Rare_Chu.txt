{{Term/Store|Rare Chu|Rare Chus|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are enemies in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 199}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|TPHD|Rare Chu}} are a significantly rare variety that are pearl-colored and sparkle. They appear anywhere where {{Plural|TPHD|Chu|link}} are, but the chances of finding one are slim. The only certain places to find them are underneath a {{Term|TPHD|Tree|link}} south of the {{Term|TPHD|Great Hylian Bridge|link}} in {{Term|TPHD|Western Hyrule Field|link}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Cave of Ordeals|link}}, inside a small cavern in {{Term|TPHD|Death Mountain|link}}, and inside three hidden caves accessed via a dig spot: under a {{Term|TPHD|Tree}} at {{Term|TPHD|Snowpeak|link}}, in the {{Term|TPHD|Gerudo Desert|link}}, and beneath the barn in {{Term|TPHD|Ordon Ranch|link}}. {{Plural|TPHD|Rare Chu}} are highly valuable as they leave behind {{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu Jelly|link}}, which has the same effect as {{Plural|TPHD|Great Fairy's Tears|link}}. However, not only are they rare, but they are also the first to be targeted by {{Plural|TPHD|Purple Chu|link}} to merge. If a {{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu}} and any other {{Term|TPHD|Chu}} merge, the new {{Term|TPHD|Chu}} will become the other color, canceling out the {{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu}}. Additionally, if {{Term|TPHD|Link|link}} already has {{Term|TPHD|Rare Chu Jelly}}, any {{Plural|TPHD|Rare Chu}} set to appear will be replaced with {{Plural|TPHD|Yellow Chu|link}}.
