{{Term/Store|Stronger Yet||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 39 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stronger Yet|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Stronger Yet}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given six minutes to defeat a {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox|link}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Hinox|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel|link}}. Only the {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}} is aware of the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s presence at the beginning of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, but once both the {{Term|HWAoC|Hinox}} and the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Hinox}} are killed, the {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}} will begin an assault on the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon}}.
