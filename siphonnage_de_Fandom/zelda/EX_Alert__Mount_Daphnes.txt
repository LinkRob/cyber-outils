{{Term/Store|EX Alert: Mount Daphnes||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 78 {{Term|HWAoC|EX Vicious Monster Battle|link}} in {{HWAoC}} included in the second [[Downloadable Content]] pack, {{GoR}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Alert: Mount Daphnes|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} must capture four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}. Two of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} have a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}} as their boss, while the other two have an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}}. After the four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured, the vicious monsters spawn in the center of the map. While the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} fights the vicious monsters, an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}} will appear to fight as well.

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the vicious monsters are defeated, and can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}. When completed, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Tabantha Wheat|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hylian Rice|link}}, 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
