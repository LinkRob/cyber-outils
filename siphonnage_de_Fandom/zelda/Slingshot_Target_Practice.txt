{{Term/Store|Slingshot Target Practice||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a {{Term|OoT3D|Mini-Game|link}} in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= CE|publisher= Nintendo|page= 57}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
The {{Term|OoT3D|Mini-Game|link}} can be found deep within the {{Term|OoT3D|Lost Woods}}. {{Term|OoT3D|Link|link}} must fire a {{Term|OoT3D|Deku Seed|link}} at a round, wooden target using the {{Term|OoT3D|Fairy Slingshot|link}} to achieve points. The bullseye is worth 100 points, and if it is hit three times in a row, a {{Term|OoT3D|Deku Scrub|link}} will jump up and compliment {{Term|OoT3D|Link}} on his accurate firing and reward him with a {{Term|OoT3D|Bullet Bag}} upgrade.<ref>{{Cite|Cool! You're great! You scored three perfect bull's-eyes! I have to give a neat present to such a wonderful person! Please take it!|Deku Scrub|OoT}}</ref>
