Limited quantities of [[Technology in The Legend of Zelda Universe|uncommon technology]] recur in {{TLoZ|Series}}, {{Term/Store|Ancient Technology|Ancient Technologies|Series, BotW}}, also known as '''Sheikah technology''', particularly features prominently in {{BotW}}.<ref name="CaC 404">{{Cite Book|quote=Ancient Technology |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 404}}</ref><ref name="CaC 4042">{{Cite Book|quote=Ancient energy gives off a pale blue glow that can be seen in operational '''Sheikah technology'''. |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 404}}</ref><ref name="AoC">{{Cite|they made their way to the Ancient Tech Lab|Rhoam|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} was created by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link|display=Sheikah Tribe}} 10,000 years ago.<ref name="CaC 404"/> All forms of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} are powered by {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}}, a fluid found in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, on {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}, and underneath {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}.<ref name="CaC 404"/> When it is active, {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} glows a pale blue or orange color, but when {{Term|BotW|Malice|link}} is infecting it, it glows a hot pink.<ref name="CaC 404"/>

{{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} led to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} becoming considerably advanced under the guidance of the ancient Sheikah civilization’s [[Ancient Energy|blue aura]].<ref name="CaC 362">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 362}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Long ago, a highly advanced tribe known as the Sheikah inhabited these lands. The great power of their wisdom saved this kingdom time and time again.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hyrule was then blossoming as a highly advanced civilization. Even the most powerful monsters posed little threat to the denizens of the realm.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The kingdom of Hyrule was once a land of lasting peace / A culture of such strength and wit, that suffering did cease.|Kass|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Web|quote=The Sheikah Tribe's blue aura breathed through the land and this mutual prosperity continued until a cataclysmic disaster struck.|url=https://web.archive.org/web/20170118202859/http://www.ign.com/articles/2017/01/18/the-legend-of-zelda-breath-of-the-wild-backstory-revealed-by-special-edition-map|title=The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild Backstory Revealed by Special Edition Map|site=IGN}}</ref> With the state of their {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|display=Technology}}, the people of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}} decided to prepare for the return of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The people thought it wise to utilize their technological prowess to ensure the safety of the land, should Calamity Ganon ever return.|Impa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But Ganon lurked beneath the surface, strengthening its jaws / So the ancient people of Hyrule set out to help the cause.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> However, following {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}'s sealing, the {{Term|BotW|King of Hyrule|link}} and the rest of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} felt threatened by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah}}, and so they were forced to abandon their {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|display=Technology}}.<ref name="CaC 362"/><ref>{{Cite|But their ancient technology disappeared long ago... Or so it is said.|Old Man|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We of the Sheikah tribe have long been heralded as a people of great wisdom. Our technology became the key to sealing {{Color|BotW Red|Ganon}} away during the Great Calamity, some ten thousand years ago. At one point, our technology was praised as the power of the gods...but eventually the people turned on it. Turned on us. Our creations came to be viewed as a threat to the kingdom. The Sheikah became outcasts, forced into exile.|Cado|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 368}}</ref>

100 years ago, when a fortune-teller foretold the resurrection of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} ordered excavations in search of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}.<ref>{{Cite|Stories of Ganon were passed from generation to generation in the form of legends and fairy tales. But there was also...a prophecy. "The signs of a resurrection of Calamity Ganon are clear. And the power to oppose it lies dormant beneath the ground." We decided to heed the prophecy and began excavating large areas of land. It wasn't long before we discovered several ancient relics made by the hands of our distant ancestors.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> As part of this endeavor, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Research Institute|link}} was founded to study {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, led by {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}.<ref name="CaC 374">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 374}}</ref><ref name="CaC 396">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 396}}</ref> Since there were concerns about the use of the potentially dangerous {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy}} so close to {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}} and {{Term|BotW|Purah}}'s recklessness, {{Term|BotW|Impa|link}} became a royal advisor to oversee the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Research Institute|display=Institute}}'s work.<ref name="CaC 374"/> {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} herself often researched {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}} in her {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|link|display=Study}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 251}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}} did eventually return, he used his {{Term|BotW|Malice}} to corrupt {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}}'s defenses.<ref name="Control">{{Cite|He appeared from deep below Hyrule Castle, seized control of the Guardians and the Divine Beasts, and turned them against us.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> This led to the deaths of the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} and {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam}}, and {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} was mortally wounded while defending {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}.<ref name="King Rhoam">{{Cite|The Champions lost their lives. Those residing in the castle as well. The appointed knight, gravely wounded, collapsed while defending the princess...|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

During the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Research Institute}} was destroyed, eventually becoming the {{Term|BotW|Royal Ancient Lab Ruins|link}}.<ref name="CaC 396"/> Two new laboratories were established by {{Term|BotW|Purah}} and {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} to continue their research. At the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} has studied the {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}} of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.<ref name="CaC 398">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 398}}</ref> Meanwhile, {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} has focused his research on creating {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} that are effective against {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.<ref name="CaC 398"/> These locations require the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} from {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}}, a source of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy}}, to power their {{Plural|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}}.<ref name="Purah">{{Cite|You know the unlit {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}} on the wall just outside this laboratory? ====Structures====
{{Main|Ancient Shrine, Sheikah Tower, Shrine of Resurrection}}
A multitude of Ancient Sheikah structures in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom}} were been constructed by their ancient civilization with the use of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}. Following the Hylian King of the time banishing such technology, the structures remained dormant underground. Unlike other pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, they have remained untouched by {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon}}, though they are all initially in inactive states.<ref name="CaC 404"/>
