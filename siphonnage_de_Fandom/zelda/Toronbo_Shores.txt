{{Term/Store|Toronbo Shores||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a beach located in south-west [[Koholint Island]] in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Toronbo Shores}}|N/A|LANS}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LANS|Toronbo Shores}} is where [[Link]] washed up when his boat was capsized in a storm. After a local girl named [[Marin]] nursed him back to health, north in [[Mabe Village]], and her father [[Tarin]] gave him his shield,<ref>{{Cite|Well, Link, ya finally snapped out of it... Name's {{Color|LANS Pink|Tarin}}. Hope yer feelin' better... What? How did I know your name? You think it's weird, eh? Well, I saw it on back of this shield!|Tarin|LANS}}</ref> Link journeyed down and talked with a mysterious {{Term|LANS|Owl|link}} about the [[Wind Fish]], he found his [[sword]] in the wreckage.<ref name="Sword">{{Cite|Oh, yeah... Some other stuff like this washed up on the beach... If'n you go look, watch out for monsters! Ever since you showed up, Link, I've seen 'em all over the place!|Tarin|LANS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You found your Sword! It must be yours because it has your name engraved on it! |N/A|LADX}}</ref> He then turned northward to the [[Mysterious Woods]] the locale where the owl advised him to journey next. He later journeyed here to talk with Marin about removing a walrus from the entrance to [[Yarna Desert]].  This beach borders the region called [[South of the Village]] to the north and the [[Martha's Bay]] region to the east.

Toronbo Shores is a beautiful beach with tall palm trees for shade in the sun. The beach extends far and gives lots of space for relaxation. Marin has been known to constantly look out towards the ocean, wandering what is outside of the island. However, when Link arrived washed up on shore, he drew monsters toward him knowing that he could wake the [[Wind Fish]].<ref name="Sword"/> {{Term|LANS|Toronbo Shores}} contains {{Term|LANS|Sale's House o' Bananas|link}} as well as a cave on its southern portion that can be accessed after acquiring the {{Term|LANS|Magnifying Lens|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{LA|-}} (Cagiva)===
In the {{LA|Cagiva}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link|link}} arrives to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Toronbo Shores}} searching for his {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Sword|link}} in volume 1. He is then attacked by a group of {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Octorok|link}} and then a {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Sea Urchin|link}}. The {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl|link}} eventually arrives, scaring the {{Plural|LA (Cagiva)|Octorok}} away. After obtaining the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ocarina|link}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} visits {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Marin|link}} at {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Toronbo Shores}}, where they play together the "{{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Ballad of the Wind Fish|link}}"

In volume 2, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} goes to {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Toronbo Shores}} and begins making a boat to leave {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|link}}. The next day, he and {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Marin}} leave the island in {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}}'s boat; however, they kept coming back to the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|display= Island}}, so {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} gives up. Once they are back at {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Toronbo Shores}}, {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Marin}} tells {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}} about a dream she had where she left the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Koholint Island|display= Island}}. Shortly after, the {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Owl}} appears to question {{Term|LA (Cagiva)|Link}}'s intentions.
