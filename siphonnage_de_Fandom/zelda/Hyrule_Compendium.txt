The {{Term/Store|Hyrule Compendium||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, and [[Purah Pad]] function in both {{BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Hyrule Compendium}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref> and {{TotK}}.

==Location and Uses==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} obtains the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}, some of its data, including the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}}, is damaged.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|The Sheikah Slate has been damaged.}}|Sheikah Slate|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Strange... It doesn't seem like you have the {{Color|BotW Red|basic runes}}... I don't understand why they're missing. There must be some reason...|Symin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} is able to restore the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}} and other functions,<ref>{{Cite|And to help the one true hero, I, the one and only Purah, will restore the {{Color|BotW Red|basic functions}} missing from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> but only once the furnace outside the lab has been lit with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Could you please bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} in town? Oh, and also use it to light our {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}}? This sweet little {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} will start working once you do that, allowing us to restore your missing {{Color|BotW Red|runes}}!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> After this has been completed, {{Term|BotW|Purah}} will remove the lock on the {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} and allow {{Term|BotW|Link}} to use it.<ref>{{Cite|Hokay then, {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah Slate}} repair... underway! {{Color|BotW Blue|Guidance Stone}} lock removed! Now...let's do this thing!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone}} takes note of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate}}'s missing files and initiates a repair.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Camera, Hyrule Compendium, and album missing files confirmed. Starting repair.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref> This restores the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|display=Slate}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Repair complete.}}|Guidance Stone|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}} compiles pictures and information of every {{Term|BotW|Creature|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Material|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}}, and treasure.<ref>{{Cite|Pictures created are saved in the album. It has a useful feature that connects to the Hyrule Compendium and automatically registers pictures of new entries.|Camera|BotW}}</ref> In order to register an entry, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must take a picture of the subject with the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. Alternatively, once the "{{Term|BotW|Sunshroom Sensing|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can purchase any missing picture from {{Term|BotW|Symin|link}} for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} each. Once {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} is defeated, pictures of elite enemies and bosses can be bought for 500 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} each. Entries saved in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}} can be detected using the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Sensor +|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Sheikah Sensor + is a rune that expands the features of the normal Sheikah Sensor. It is able to detect any object that is listed in the Hyrule Compendium.|Sheikah Slate Manual|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}}, he can obtain the {{Term|BotW|Classified Envelope|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Symin}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link! My goodness! Have you really collected EVERY picture?! Incredible! I never dreamed that I might someday see a complete collection. This level of dedication to research deserves a reward, but I'm not sure the lab budget has room for one... {{Small|Although... If he's that passionate about pictures, maybe...}} Well, it will have to do. Here. I found this sandwiched in a book. I have no interest in it myself, so...|Symin|BotW}}</ref> Equipment and {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} exclusive to the Expansion Pass DLC are included in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}}, but some are only available in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}. Due to these extra equipment and {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} entries, some entries may have altered number listings from the base game dependent upon whether or not the Expansion Pass DLC has been purchased and these numbers could be further different depending upon whether or not the game is being played in {{Term|BotW|Master Mode}}. All number listings for entries are noted below in the following format: Normal number prior to DLC Pack 2 (after DLC Pack 2) / Master Mode number prior to DLC Pack 2 (after DLC Pack 2). Creatures and equipment exclusive to amiibo are not included in the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Compendium}}.

==Trivia==
*The Sunshroom entry is the only one in the Compendium that cannot be obtained by purchasing it from Symin, due to the "Sunshroom Sensing" Side Quest (which automatically registers the Sunshroom to the Compendium) being required to be able to purchase entries in the first place. A default image for the Sunshroom still exists in the game's files, however.
*The storage location in the game's data for Compendium images taken using the Camera Rune is the same for both Normal Mode and Master Mode. As a result, images taken in one mode get overwritten when an image of that same entry is taken in the other mode. Images purchased from Symin, however, do not get overwritten by this, as they are stored in a different location.
