==Location and Uses==
The Power Ring L-2 can be found randomly by opening [[Gasha Nut]]s or by stumbling into [[Maple]]. In {{OoA}}, it can also be found in a cave at [[Crescent Island]] during the past, requiring the [[Long Hook]] to reach it. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee]]s. When worn, it increases [[Sword]] damage while also increasing damage taken by [[Link]].<ref name="Description"/> The Power Ring L-2 can be transferred between games by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
