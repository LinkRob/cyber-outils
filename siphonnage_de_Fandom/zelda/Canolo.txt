{{Term/Store|Canolo||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Canolo|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
Canolo is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} traveler who can be found in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon|link}}. She was previously traveling with her friends {{Term|BotW|Sesami|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Oliff|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Canolo|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Palme|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, did you happen to see any of my friends? They're four travelers named {{Color|BotW Blue|Oliff}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|Flaxel}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|Canolo}}, and {{Color|BotW Blue|Palme}}....|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> However, the five of them were attacked by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} along the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path|link}} and ended up separated.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know... We were all walking together when some monsters suddenly jumped us near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Koukot Plateau path}}.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref>

At {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Sesami}}, who managed to escape.<ref>{{Cite|I took off running and didn't stop until I got here.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to let {{Term|BotW|Canolo}} and the rest of his friends know that he is waiting for them at the {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, if you happen to run into any of my friends on the road, please let them know Sesami is waiting for them here.|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the "{{Term|BotW|Missing in Action|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Canolo}} can be found on the northern part of the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau Path}}, which bridges the {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mount Nabooru|link}} together. There, she will be under attack by a {{Term|BotW|Blue Bokoblin|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link}} must defeat.<ref>{{Cite|I want to go home!|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> Initially, {{Term|BotW|Canolo}} is hesitant to believe that she has been saved.<ref>{{Cite|Wait...<br/>Am I...saved?|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> After being informed of {{Term|BotW|Sesami}}'s whereabouts, {{Term|BotW|Canolo}} expresses her anger towards him for abandoning her.<ref name="Girl"/> She agrees to go to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}, though she is still upset.<ref>{{Cite|He's at the Gerudo Canyon Stable? If he thinks I'm going to forgive him for this, he's got another thing coming!|Canolo|BotW}}</ref>

When she makes it to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Canolo}} calls {{Term|BotW|Sesami}} a {{Term|BotW|Cucco|link}} and thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for saving her.<ref>{{Cite|Sesami is such a Cucco. If you hadn't come along... *shudder* Thanks for saving me.|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> She admits that she did not know traveling could be so dangerous, as it was not mentioned in the guidebook she read.<ref>{{Cite|I had no idea traveling was so dangerous. There was nothing like this in the travel guidebook. I guess you can't assume to know anything until you experience it for yourself.|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> Having decided traveling is not for her, {{Term|BotW|Canolo}} plans on heading back home after she rests.<ref>{{Cite|But at least I've figured out that traveling isn't my thing. I'm going to rest here awhile and then head back home.|Canolo|BotW}}</ref> However, she has a feeling {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not have the option to go home, so she wishes him well.<ref>{{Cite|I get the feeling that going home isn't an option for you, so take care of yourself out there.|Canolo|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Canolo}} and the rest of her friends chip in to give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}} as thanks for saving them.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry it's so small. Everyone chipped in for it, but we're in the middle of traveling, y'know?|Sesami|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is completed, {{Term|BotW|Canolo}} and her friends, with the exception of {{Term|BotW|Sesami}}, disappear from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable}}.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|Series|Canolo}} and her friends are named after types of {{Wp|Vegetable oil}}. {{Term|Series|Canolo}} herself is named after {{Wp|Canola oil}}.
