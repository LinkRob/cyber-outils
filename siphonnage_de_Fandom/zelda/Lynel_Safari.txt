"{{Term/Store|Lynel Safari||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Lynel Safari}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Once {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} arrives in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} and completes the "{{Term|BotW|Reach Zora's Domain|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, he can speak to {{Term|BotW|Laflat|link}} to obtain this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. She explain to Link that she overheard Sidon exclaiming that he and Link were going to stop {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link|display=Divine Beast Vah Ruta's}} flow of water.<ref>{{Cite|Prince Sidon ran off through the eastern gate. He was shouting something like... "I'm off to stop the Divine Beast! Link the Hylian is coming with me!" I assume that means you're going up {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}} in search of {{Color|BotW Blue|shock arrows}}?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|When {{Term|BotW|Link}} obtains this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} after completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} tells him that there is still a threat to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link... The Divine Beast has been appeased, but I fear there is still something that threatens the domain...|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> Her explanation afterwards is the same.}} To capitalize on his venture up {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} asks Link to capture a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of a {{Term|BotW|Lynel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] If so, I have a request that is...somewhat related. There's a red-maned monster called a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} that lives on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. I REALLY need a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. It can be a picture of any red-maned {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}. Not just the one on the mountain. So what do you say? Can you get me a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} further explains that she requires a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} in order to scare off any would-be divers that are seeking to dive from {{Term|BotW|Shatterback Point|link}} after being goaded by {{Term|BotW|Gruve|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Understood!] I am glad that was settled so quickly. See, there's this fool in the domain who goads people into jumping off of {{Color|BotW Blue|Shatterback Point}} on {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}. It's an incredibly dangerous jump, and this guy pressures people into jumping off by questioning their courage. He's a real bottom-feeder. If this keeps up, someone is surely going to wind up horribly injured! I need a picture of a {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} to show everyone how scary they are. That way, maybe people will stop going up there. Does that answer your question, Link? I'll leave the details to you. Just bring me a {{Color|BotW Red|picture of a Lynel}} so I can describe them in terrible detail. Thank you!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref>

In order to fulfill {{Term|BotW|Laflat}}'s request, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must capture a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of any red-maned {{Term|BotW|Lynel}}. As the nearest {{Term|BotW|Lynel}} to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} is the one atop {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain}}, she directs {{Term|BotW|Link}} there.<ref>{{Cite|Laflat asked that you {{Color|BotW Red|show her}} a picture of a red-maned {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} if you see one. The nearest one she knows of is on Ploymus Mountain.<br/><br/>Use whatever means you have at your disposal to give her a look.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

Once Link has taken a suitable {{Term|BotW|Picture}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Laflat}} and show it to her.<ref>{{Cite|Link! How is that {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}} mission coming along?|Laflat|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[I have your picture.] Oh, wow! Please show me!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> Upon seeing it, she will exclaim in fright before excusing herself.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|WAAAH?!}} *ahem* My apologies... That sound was not very dignified, was it? P-pardon me... I'm all better now. That {{Color|BotW Red|Lynel}}... How terrifying! One look at this, and I doubt anyone would get any reckless ideas about visiting {{Color|BotW Blue|Ploymus Mountain}}!|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> She then thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for providing a suitably scary {{Term|BotW|Picture}} and offers him the {{Term|BotW|Zora Greaves|link}} in return.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! I'll tell the children of the domain about this. Maybe that will scare them away from the mountain. Oh, right! Now for your reward...|Laflat|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is marked as completed.
