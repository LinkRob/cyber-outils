The {{Term/Store|Rauru Settlement Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Rauru Settlement Ruins'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Rauru Settlement Ruins}} are a small collection of broken and dilapidated buildings south of {{Term|BotW|Rauru Hillside|link}} along the road to {{Term|BotW|Pico Pond|link}}. There is a small, unnamed body of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} that marks the western border of the {{Term|BotW|Rauru Settlement Ruins}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Rauru Settlement Ruins}} contain a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that holds a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm Rod|link}} that is guarded by an {{Term|BotW|Electric Wizzrobe|link}}. It is possible to locate the travelers Mina and Mils here, and after you rescue them from some Blue Bokoblins, they will begin searching through the ruins in search of valuables.
