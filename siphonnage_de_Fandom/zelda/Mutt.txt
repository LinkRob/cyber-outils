{{Term/Store|Mutt|Mutts|Series, LA, LADX, LANS|plural}} are animals in {{LA}}.<ref name= "Encyclopedia"/>

==Characteristics==
==={{LA|-}}===
{{Plural|LANS|Mutt}} are {{Plural|LANS|Dog|link}} found in {{Term|LANS|Mabe Village|link}}. Although not normally aggressive, they will attack {{Term|LANS|Link|link}} by pouncing on him a single time if provoked, and return to a docile state after damaging him. Unlike {{Plural|LANS|Cucco|link}}, {{Plural|LANS|Mutt}} will not swarm {{Term|LANS|Link}} en masse in response to multiple attacks. In all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}, {{Plural|LANS|Mutt}} can be killed by either throwing {{Term|LADX|Magic Powder|link}} on them or by being hit with the {{Term|LADX|Magic Rod|link}}.

If spoken to, {{Plural|LANS|Mutt}} usually will bark at {{Term|LANS|Link|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yip yiiip!|Mutt|LANS}}</ref> However, if they are standing near {{Term|LANS|Marin|link}} while she is singing, they will become too absorbed in her song to notice him.<ref>{{Cite|It seems to be totally absorbed in Marin's song!|N/A|LANS}}</ref>
