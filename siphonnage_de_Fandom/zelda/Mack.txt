{{Term/Store|Mack||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{MM}} and {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Mack takes part in constructing the fireworks-viewing tower for the [[Carnival of Time]]. During the first days, Mutoh is in the [[Mayor's Official Residence|Mayor's Office]], on the first day he can be heard yelling at his apprentice to focus on his work instead of the moon.
==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|The Carpenters}}
{{Term|TMC|Mack}} is a {{Term|TMC|Carpenter|link}} that first appears alongside the other {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} repairing the northern entrance to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} is turned to stone by {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} are later found repairing an area in the {{Term|TMC|Eastern Hills|link}} after {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}}. He tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that {{Term|TMC|Mutoh}} works the {{Plural|TMC|Carpenter}} hard, not even allowing breaks.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh! No time to talk, kid! The boss has us working our fingers to the bone! No time for a break, even!|Mack|TMC}}</ref>

After {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Mack}} can be found in the {{Term|TMC|Carpenters Shop|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}} for the remainder of the game. He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? I hope they fit!|Mack|TMC}}</ref>

== Notes ==
* Mack's unnamed apprentice is the man who buys the [[Oceanside Spider House]]. If Link clears the house on the first day, Mack will still be yelling to this apprentice even though he is no longer present on top of the tower.
