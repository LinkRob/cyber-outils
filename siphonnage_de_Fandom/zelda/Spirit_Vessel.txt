{{Term/Store|Spirit Vessel|Spirit Vessels|Series, SS}} is an item featured in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 139}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Spirit Vessel is a physical representation of [[Link]]'s spirit,<ref>{{Cite|You got the Spirit Vessel! This mysterious plant represents your spirit, Link.|N/A|SS}}</ref> it is similar to the [[Vessel of Light]] from {{TP}} in that it is used to obtain the [[Sacred Tears]] within the [[Silent Realm]].<ref>{{Cite|To fill the Spirit Vessel, you need to locate and collect the Sacred Tears scattered across this Silent Realm.|Fi|SS}}</ref> When all 15 of the Sacred Tears are put into the Spirit Vessel, the trial is complete, and [[Link]] receives another item to help him access a previously inaccessible area of [[Hyrule|The Surface]].<ref>{{Cite|When your Spirit Vessel is filled with 15 tears, your spirit will grow and you will be blessed by the goddess with a new power.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The sacred flames are three in number. To obtain them, you must also earn relics known as the three sacred gifts. For each trial you overcome, you shall be blessed with one of the gifts...|Fi|SS}}</ref>
