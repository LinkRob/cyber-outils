{{Term/Store|Karusa Valley||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Karusa Valley'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Karusa Valley is located in the [[Gerudo Desert]]. Karusa Valley is a long valley that leads to the [[Yiga Clan Hideout]]. [[Link]] must pass through here to reach the Yiga Clan Hideout during the "[[Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|Divine Beast Vah Naboris]]" [[Main Quest]].
