{{Term/Store|Olivio||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TWW|Olivio}}
Olivio is a [[Korok]] who lives in the [[Forest Haven]]. Before the Forest Ceremony, Olivio can be found standing on a column outside, on the way to the Forbidden Forest. After the Forest Ceremony, Olivio goes to the [[Eastern Fairy Island]] to plant new [[Tree]]s.
