The {{Term/Store|Street Merchant||Series, ALttP, ALBW, TFH}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 228,{{Exp Game|ALttP}} 300,{{Exp Game|ALBW}} 305 {{Exp Game|TFH}}}}</ref> In all of his appearances, he is a merchant who sells wares on the streets of a town, most commonly {{Term|Series|Kakariko Village|link}}.

==Biography==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
The Street [[Merchant]] sits on a carpet directly west of the {{Term|ALttP|Weather Vane|link}} in {{Term|ALttP|Kakariko Village|link}}. {{Term|ALttP|Link|link}} can buy a {{Term|ALttP|Bottle|link}} from him for 100 {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You, sir! Have you been going through life without one of my hold-anything bottles? Well step right up and make your life complete! I've got one on sale now for the low, low price of 100 Rupees! What do you say? Interested?|Street Merchant|ALttP}}</ref>

If {{Term|ALttP|Link}} brings him a {{Term|ALttP|Fish|link}} from the {{Term|ALttP|Great Swamp|link}}, the {{Term|ALttP|Street Merchant}} will give him 21 {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee}}, 10 {{Plural|ALttP|Arrow|link}}, 8 {{Plural|ALttP|Bomb|link}} and a {{Term|ALttP|Magic Jar|link}} in exchange for it.<ref>{{Cite|Hey! They say eating fish makes you smart. You have to give me this fish for this stuff, OK? Done!|Street Merchant|ALttP}}</ref> If {{Term|ALttP|Link}} brings him a {{Term|ALttP|Golden Bee|link}}, he will buy it for 100 {{Plural|ALttP|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! I've never seen such a rare bug! I'll buy it for 100 Rupees, OK? Done!|Street Merchant|ALttP}}</ref>

==={{ALBW|-}}===
The {{Term|ALBW|Street Merchant}} appears in {{Term|ALBW|Kakariko Village|link}} in {{ALBW}}, vending a {{Term|ALBW|Bottle|link}} and {{Plural|ALBW|Heart|link}} after {{Term|ALBW|Yuga|link}} seals {{Term|ALBW|Seres|link}} within a {{Term|ALBW|Painting|link}}. When {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} first meets him, the {{Term|ALBW|Street Merchant}} tells him that he should be prepared for the troubling times.<ref>{{Cite|I've heard talk of some awful events over at the castle. In times of trouble, it's important to be prepared. Oh, a customer! Don't be shy. Come on over.|Street Merchant|ALBW}}</ref> After the {{Term|ALBW|Shady Guy|link}} has stolen the {{Term|ALBW|Smooth Gem|link}} from {{Term|ALBW|Oren|link}} and pawns it to the {{Term|ALBW|Street Merchant}},<ref>{{Cite|Good news! I have some new stock that may interest you. Please come take a look. Have you seen this rare stone? Smoother than silk! Go on! Feel it! Just touching such a treasure will make your skin happy all over!|Street Merchant|ALBW}}</ref> he will sell it for 200 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, yes! A gemstone of remarkable quality. Smooth as silk! And for you? Only 200 Rupees! Interested, my friend?|Street Merchant|ALBW}}</ref> If {{Term|ALBW|Link}} tries to purchase a {{Term|ALBW|Heart}} from the {{Term|ALBW|Street Merchant}} with full health, he will refuse to sell it to the young hero in order to save on {{Plural|ALBW|Heart}}.<ref>{{Cite|But you don't have a scratch on you! I think I'll save these precious hearts for someone who needs them.|Street Merchant|ALBW}}</ref>

==={{TFH|-}}===
[[File:Street Merchant's Stall.jpg|right|thumb|200px|The {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}}'s stall with several available {{Plural|TFH|Material}}]]
In {{TFH}}, the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} appears in the town of {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}, selling {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} at a stall across from {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}. He opens up shop after the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} have completed three Levels of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Material}} he sells are typically very expensive, but his selection often includes rare {{Plural|TFH|Material}} that are otherwise hard to come by. One {{Term|TFH|Material}}, the {{Term|TFH|Frilly Fabric|link}}, is exclusive to his shop.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, the Frilly Fabric. You've got a good eye. That's a rare material that can only be found here.|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} will restock every day, in real-time. The {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} remarks that {{Term|TFH|Link}} seems familiar, implying that he may be the same {{Term|ALBW|Street Merchant}} from {{Term|TFH|Hyrule|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I feel like I've seen you somewhere before...|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref>

The {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}}'s stall ordinarily has five {{Plural|TFH|Material}} for sale, including the {{Term|TFH|Frilly Fabric}}. This selection is reduced to only three {{Plural|TFH|Material}} if {{Term|TFH|Link}} does not have SpotPass functionality enabled.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that shelf's only for our preferred {{Color|TFH Red|SpotPass}} customers. If you switch {{Color|TFH Red|SpotPass}} on, I'll send those materials your way too.|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref>

Aside from selling {{Plural|TFH|Material}}, the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} can also buy {{Plural|TFH|Material}} from {{Term|TFH|Link}} for one-fifth their cost. He will only buy {{Plural|TFH|Material}} if {{Term|TFH|Link}} speaks with him directly, by breaking the {{Term|TFH|Pot|link}} to the right side of him. If the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} sees either the blue bird or the {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link|display=black ghost}} around town, he will buy {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s {{Plural|TFH|Material}} at twice the price.<ref>{{Cite|I swear I just saw that black ghost everyone's talkin' about... I'm in a good mood today, so I'll buy your stuff at twice the price. Whacha wanna sell?|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} will not purchase the {{Term|TFH|Lady's Glasses|link}}, {{Term|TFH|Lady's Collar|link|display=Collar}}, and {{Term|TFH|Lady's Parasol|link|display=Parasol}} from {{Term|TFH|Link}}, as he finds items belonging to {{Term|TFH|Lady Maud|link}} creepy.<ref>{{Cite|Bah! The Lady's Glasses! Witch materials gives me the creeps! You're on your own there.|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref>

The {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} also has a blue tent next to his stall. It is too dark to see inside, requiring {{Term|TFH|Link}} to wear the {{Term|TFH|Light Armor|link}} to take a look. Volume 7 of the {{Term|TFH|Drablands Diary|link}} can be found inside the tent.

In the 2.0.0 update, the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} will begin to sell {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token|link}} after the curse is lifted from {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! You did it!! So you finally broke the princess's awful curse, did ya? Good one. This town's finally lively again, and I'm turning a fine profit. It's all thanks to you, pal. Oh, that reminds me. I've decided to finally put "those" up for sale, starting today. What are "those"? They're called... Friendly Tokens!! They're special treasures, ya know. Rare. Valuable. Ya can't find these things, even in the Drablands.|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref> He advises {{Term|TFH|Link}} not to tell the {{Term|TFH|Faux Hero|link}} of this, as the child gets on his nerves.<ref>{{Cite|Just...do me a favor and don't tell that loud kid who hangs out in front of the castle. He gets on my nerves.|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref>

In the 2.1.0 update, {{Term|TFH|Link}} can present {{Plural|TFH|Hero Point|link}} to the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} for free {{Plural|TFH|Material}},<ref>{{Cite|Hey, pal! The old man at the castle told me what's what. He wants me to encourage heroic folks like you. Know what I said? Sure! WHY NOT?! Could be fun. Helpin' heroes is like an act of heroism itself, doncha think? So tell ya what. Go out and collect those hero point things and I'll make it worth your while.|Street Merchant|TFH}}</ref> which are earned by completing Levels that {{Term|TFH|Link}} did not initially vote for over [[multiplayer]]. The kind of {{Plural|TFH|Material}} the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} will give will be the kind that {{Term|TFH|Link}} still needs to complete unfinished {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}}. Through this, it is possible to receive the exclusive, gold-ranked {{Plural|TFH|Material}} normally only received from {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}} or the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum (Mode)|link}}. {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}} are not earned through this way. Once {{Term|TFH|Link}} has obtained all {{Plural|TFH|Material}} necessary to craft the remaining {{Plural|TFH|Outfit}} (excluding those requiring {{Plural|TFH|Friendly Token}}), the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant}} will no longer give free {{Plural|TFH|Material}}.
