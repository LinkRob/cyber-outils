The {{Term/Store|Hero's Tunic||Series, TFH}} is an {{Term|TFH|Item}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 127}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|So you want to be a hero, do you? Then you simply can't go on without a Hero's Tunic in your sad little closet.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that is visually identical to the {{Term|TWW|Hero's Clothes|link}} of {{TWW}}, {{PH}} and {{ST}}, which are in turn based on the iconic green tunic worn by {{Term|Series|Link|link}} throughout the majority of his adventures. The {{Term|TFH|Outfit}} does not grant {{Term|TFH|Link}} any special abilities, though it provides better protection than his default {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, the {{Term|TFH|Bear Minimum|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|A tunic with no special abilities. May the way of the Hero lead to the Triforce!|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Lose a Heart Container and take double damage. But if a true hero wears it...|Pick an Outfit|TFH}}</ref>

When {{Term|TFH|Link}} enters {{Term|TFH|Hytopia Castle|link}} for the first time, he is met by {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly|link}}, who enlists him into the Witch-Hunting Brigade and supplies him with a {{Term|TFH|Hytopian Silk|link}}, which can be used to craft the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic}} at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Consider yourself enlisted. As a token of welcome, I will supply you with one firm handshake and one {{Color|TFH Blue|Hero's Tunic}}.<br/>Here are the necessary materials. Take them to {{Color|TFH Blue|Madame Couture}} at once, and she'll make you the outfit!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> If {{Term|TFH|Link}} proceeds to any of the lobbies to journey to the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} in his default {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} will warn him and insist that he purchase the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic}} for his own protection.<ref>{{Cite|Hold, [Link]! The {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands}} are too dangerous to visit without proper equipment.<br/>I strongly advise you to change into the {{Color|TFH Blue|Hero's Tunic}}. My conscience demands it...|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref> Once {{Term|TFH|Link}} has completed the [[Drablands#Woodlands|Woodlands]] {{Term|TFH|Area}} by defeating {{Term|TFH|Margoma|link}}, he will be summoned to {{Term|TFH|King Tuft|link}}'s throne room; at this point, {{Term|TFH|Sir Combsly}} will not allow {{Term|TFH|Link}} to proceed or continue his adventure without a {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, [Link]! I'm pleased you made it back in one piece.<br/>[...]<br/>I'd like nothing better than for you to head right back into the {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands}} to continue your mission. However...<br/>{{Color|TFH Blue|King Tuft}} has summoned you. A direct summons from the king is not something to take lightly, boy!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Now then. Explain to me, soldier, how it's possible that you have still not picked up your {{Color|TFH Blue|Hero's Tunic}}!<br/>You can't see the king dressed like that. You're a member of MY brigade, boy. Laziness will not be tolerated.<br/>Hurry over to {{Color|TFH Blue|Madame}}'s, and get your {{Color|TFH Blue|Hero's Tunic}} this instant, soldier. Don't keep {{Color|TFH Blue|King Tuft}} waiting!|Sir Combsly|TFH}}</ref>

Once the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} have gathered and each is assigned a different color, the cap of the {{Term|TFH|Hero's Tunic}} gains a yellow brim, which visually separates each {{Term|TFH|Links}}'s cap from his newly-colored hair.
