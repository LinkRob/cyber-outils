==Other Appearances==
==={{HWAoI|-}}===
Agraston, is the [[Goron]] Chief and an old friend of [[King Rauru|Rauru]], the King of Hyrule. He is an intellectual and calm leader. At some point following [[Princess Zelda|Zelda's]] arrival to Ancient Hyrule, Agraston is summoned to the Ancient Hyrule Castle's Audience Chamber. There, he receives a [[Vah Rudania Divine Helm|Divine Helm]] from Rauru.
