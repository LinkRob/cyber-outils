The {{Term/Store|Photo Bro||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=305 }}</ref> He runs the {{Term|TFH|Miiverse Gallery|link}} in {{Term|TFH|Hytopia|link}}.

==Biography==
The {{Term|TFH|Photo Bro}} is the owner of the {{Term|TFH|Miiverse Gallery|link}}, where {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} can view and post photos taken in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands|link}} to [[Miiverse]]. He tends to speak with excessive, outdated slang.<ref>{{Cite|Ya gotta get started making those memories, dudebro. Press {{Icon|TFH X}} in the {{Color|TFH Blue|Drablands}} anytime. Memories are like pies. We all like 'em, but someone's gotta make 'em happen. And don't forget about those groovy stamps you get every time you buy a new outfit. So rad.|Photo Bro|TFH}}</ref> The {{Term|TFH|Photo Bro}} will give {{Term|TFH|Link}} a {{Term|TFH|Camera|link}} the first time he visits him, which is needed to take photos while in the {{Term|TFH|Drablands}}. At the end of {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s adventure, following the ceremony in celebration of the curse being lifted off of {{Term|TFH|Princess Styla|link}}, it is revealed that the {{Term|TFH|Photo Bro}} had filmed the entire event and gave it as a gift to her.<ref name="ceremony"/>
