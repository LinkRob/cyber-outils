==Location and Uses==
==={{FS|-}}===
{{Plural|FS|Big Switch}} appear in various areas in {{FS}} and usually have different functions that occur when all {{Plural|FS|Links|link}} step on them. Sometimes, stepping on a {{Term|FS|Big Switch}} will block all exits in a room, forcing the {{Plural|FS|Link}} to battle {{Plural|FS|Enemy|link}} before they can proceed. {{Plural|FS|Big Switch}} can also cause several {{Plural|FS|Rupee|link}} to appear, occasionally showing the correct way to go. {{Plural|FS|Big Switch}} can also simply open paths forward.{{How}}

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Manual|FSA|Big Switches|Single players cannot activate these large switches alone. Try balancing a formation of characters on these switches to activate them.}}
{{Stub|FSA}}
{{Plural|FSA|Big Switch}} in {{FSA}} function similarly to how they do in {{FS}}.
