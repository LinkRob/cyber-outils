'''Oinkers''' are {{Term|FPTRR|Pig|link}}-like enemies that appear in {{FPTRR}}. They are fought like mini-{{Plural|FPTRR|Boss|link}} and their defeat often leads to finding an {{Term|FPTRR|Item|link}} of great worth. They are invulnerable to attacks as long as they are not stunned when running into obstacle like a wall or another {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}}.

==Areas==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}} can be found in all Continents. They are first encountered in {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow|link}}, and after that also in the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest|link}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plains|link}}, and in the {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins|link}}. They are always in one place, behind a gate that can only be opened with the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Oinker Snout|display=Oinker Snout|link}}.

==Story==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}} must often be defeated to obtain a precious item that someone has lost. Such an item will be bought back with many {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}. As the story progresses, increasingly powerful {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}} will be encountered. In the {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins|link}}, the powerful [[Oinker Chief]] and his followers must be defeated to obtain the {{Term|FPTRR|Master Rupee|link}}. Not all {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}} are hostile, though; the {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Couple}} of {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins}} try to break away from the customs of their people.

==Characteristics and Variations==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}} are the most varied enemies in the game. Besides the normal weapon wielding types, there are also {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard|link}} and larger armored {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}}.

===Oinker===
The {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}} from the {{Term|FPTRR|Lon Lon Meadow}} are the first {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker}} {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} will encounter. They've stolen the {{Term|FPTRR|Town Guard|link}}'s {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Golden Button|display=Golden Button|link}} and are supposedly responsible for the kidnapping of {{Term|FPTRR|Missy (Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland)|link}}, though they are later found innocent. They can drop the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Shoddy Lance|display=Shoddy Lance|link}} when defeated.

===Forest Oinker===
[[File:FPTRR Forest Oinker Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Forest Oinkers''' live in a camp in the {{Term|FPTRR|Deku Forest|link}}. They've stolen {{Term|FPTRR|Bella|link}}'s {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Dazzling Brooch|display=Dazzling Brooch|link}} and can drop the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Low-Budget Mace|display=Low-Budget Mace|link}} and the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Shoddy Shield|display=Shoddy Shield|link}} when defeated.{{clear}}

===Snow Oinker===
[[File:FPTRR Snow Oinker Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Snow Oinkers''' call a camp in {{Term|FPTRR|Icy Plain|link}} their home. Together with a pair of {{Plural|FPTRR|Oinker Wizard}}, they guard the stolen
{{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Gold Lamé Socks|display=Gold Lamé Socks|link}} of {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum|link}}. When they are defeated they can drop {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Shoulder Spikes|display=Shoulder Spikes|link}} or a {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Poker Lance|display=Poker Lance|link}}.{{clear}}

===Axe Oinker===
[[File:FPTRR Axe Oinker Sprite.png|left]]
{{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} first encounters this {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}} type in the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Garden|link}}, where a single '''Axe Oinker''', known as '''Stray Oinker''', guards the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Iron Herb|display=Iron Herb|link}}. Later, they can also be found in {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins}} where they guard they patrol the walls of the last {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}} camp. If defeated, they can drop the {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Destructor Axe|display=Destructor Axe|link}}.{{clear}}

===Lance Oinker===
[[File:FPTRR Lance Oinker Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Lance Oinkers''' accompany the Axe Oinkers in the {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}} camp of {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins}}. If defeated, they can drop a {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=Poker Lance|display=Poker Lance|link}}.{{clear}}

===Pubarian===
[[File:FPTRR Pubarian Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Pubarian''' is only encountered during the battle with the Blue and Red {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Captain|link}}. They are a lot faster than regular Oinkers when rolling on their broken {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} orbs but are nonetheless defeated the same way as the other Oinkers.{{clear}}

===Small Oinker Captain===
[[File:FPTRR Oinker Bodyguard Sprite.png|left]]
The '''Small Oinker Captains''' are the last guard of {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker}} camp's first room in {{Term|FPTRR|Auros Ruins}}. They have a lot more health than regular Oinkers, but they are a bit slower. If defeated they can drop a {{Term|FPTRR|Items in Freshly-Picked Tingle's Rosy Rupeeland|section=10-Man Slicer|display=10-Man Slicer|link}}.{{clear}}

===Oinker Bodyguard===
[[File:FPTRR Small Oinker Captain Sprite.png|left]]
The elite version of the Small {{Term|FPTRR|Oinker Captain}} is the '''Oinker Bodyguard'''. They are only encountered during the fight with the [[Oinker Chief]] who calls them regularly. They can also drop the 10-Man Slicer.{{clear}}
