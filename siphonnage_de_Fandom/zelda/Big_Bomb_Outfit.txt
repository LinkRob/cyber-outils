The {{Term/Store|Big Bomb Outfit||Series, TFH}} is an item in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 116}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Madame Couture|The Big Bomb Outfit turns normal Bombs into Big Bombs. The name says it all, really.}}

The {{Term|TFH|Big Bomb Outfit}} is an {{Term|TFH|Outfit|link}} that can be crafted at {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture's|link}}. When {{Term|TFH|Link|link}} wears the {{Term|TFH|Outfit}}, any {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}} he holds will turn into {{Plural|TFH|Big Bomb|link}}, doubling their blast radius and the amount of damage they deal. This applies not only to {{Term|TFH|Link}}'s own {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}}, but also any {{Plural|TFH|Bomb Flower|link}} or {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} thrown by [[Enemies in Tri Force Heroes|Enemies]] or other {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} that he manages to hold before detonation. These {{Plural|TFH|Big Bomb}} will not revert to regular {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} if held by a {{Term|TFH|Links}} who is not wearing the {{Term|TFH|Big Bomb Outfit}}.

==Trivia==
*When {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|link}} tailors the {{Term|TFH|Big Bomb Outfit}}, the sound of a burning fuse will be heard, followed by an explosion and {{Term|TFH|Madame Couture|display=Madame}} coughing, instead of her usual cheer.
