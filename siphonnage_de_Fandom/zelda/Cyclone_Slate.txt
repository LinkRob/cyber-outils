The {{Term/Store|Cyclone Slate||Series, PH}} is an {{Term|PH|Item|link}} in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|PH}}}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate}} is given to {{Term|PH|Link|link}} by {{Term|PH|Golden Chief Cylos|link}} on the {{Term|PH|Uncharted Island|link}}, after he solves the puzzle that gives him access to the island's cavernous interior. It is a stone tablet, bearing the image of {{Term|PH|Golden Chief Cylos}}, that serves as the [[Warping]] mechanism of the game.<ref>{{Cite|That is the Cyclone Slate! By drawing symbols on it, you can ride cyclones too! Want to leap elsewhere with your ship? Then spring into action with the slate!|Golden Chief Cylos|PH}}</ref>

The {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate}} can be accessed at sea by opening the Menu in the lower-left hand corner of the screen and then selecting the "Slate" icon. In order to use it, {{Term|PH|Link}} must first locate any of the six {{Plural|PH|Golden Frog|link}} scattered throughout the {{Term|PH|World of the Ocean King|link}} and shoot them down with the {{Term|PH|SS Linebeck|link}}'s {{Term|PH|Cannon|link}}. Once hit, the {{Term|PH|Golden Frog|display=Frog}} will hold up a wooden {{Term|PH|Sign|link}} displaying a symbol; drawing this symbol on the {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate}} will summon a cyclone that warps the young hero to the location of the {{Term|PH|Golden Frog}} who taught it.<ref>{{Cite|You can move your ship vast distances on the winds by using the Cyclone Slate. There is a different symbol for each spot to which you want to soar. Ask my gilt minions, found at myriad spots in the sea, what the symbols might be! Catch their attention with a good blast of your cannon, slow-toe! That will do it!|Golden Chief Cylos|PH}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*The {{Term|PH|Cyclone Slate}} is shaped much like a [[Game Boy Advance]] cartridge.
*Two of the warp symbols are based on Greek letters. The symbol drawn to warp to the {{Term|PH|Golden Frog}} near {{Term|PH|Molida Island|link}} is the lowercase letter {{Wp|Phi|phi}}, and the symbol drawn to warp to the {{Term|PH|Golden Frog}} near {{Term|PH|Dee Ess Island|link}} is similar to the uppercase letter {{Wp|Omega|omega}}.
