The {{Term/Store|Bird Key||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is an item in {{LA}}.<ref name="E 117"/>{{Note|The {{Term|LADX|Bird Key}} was also referred to as the '''Eagle's Key''' in {{Guide|LA|Nintendo}}.<ref name="LA Guide 71"/> However, because it contradicts the name given in {{E}}, this term is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
The Bird Key is located deep within a cave in [[Tal Tal Heights]].<ref name="LA Guide 71"/> Unfortunately for [[Link]], he cannot access it alone due to the huge pits in the cave. He must bring back the [[Flying Rooster]] to life by playing the [[Frog's Song of Soul]] then use its flying ability to fly over the pits.<ref name="LA Guide 71"/>

The Bird Key allows Link, by inserting it into the {{Term|LADX|Bird Keyhole|link}}, to unlock access to the [[Eagle's Tower]].<ref name="E 117"/>
