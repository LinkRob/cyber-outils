{{Term/Store|Urbosa's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 5 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Urbosa's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, an {{Term|HWAoC|Amber|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Hydromelon|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}}'s objective is to defeat 100 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within three minutes. All of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are Gerudo Soldiers, although a {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain}} spawns once {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} reaches 10,  30, and 70 KOs. More Gerudo Soldiers also spawn as {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa}} defeats them. Once the objective is reached, a fourth {{Term|HWAoC|Gerudo Captain}} spawns and the time limit is taken away. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once all of the Gerudo Captains are defeated.
