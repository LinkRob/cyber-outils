{{Term/Store|Mezer||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mezer|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Mezer}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}}. He works as a wandering butcher, selling meat during his journey.<ref>{{Cite|I'm Mezer. I'm a wandering butcher. Huh. That sounds a touch weird when I say it out loud like that.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey. Hey, buddy. You wanna buy some meat?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He travels between {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}} and the abandoned {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} to the west, passing along the {{Term|BotW|Squabble River|link}} and through the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}} on his way. {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} carries an {{Term|BotW|Emblazoned Shield|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}}.

Due to the meat he carries with him, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} smells like {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What? You smell it, don't you? Like raw meat, wafting through the air? Well, you guessed right! It's me!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Mezer}}, he will often notice that he appears to be hungry.<ref>{{Cite|What is it? That face... Is that...the face of hunger? You can't adventure on an empty stomach.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Huh? What's the matter? Why the grumbles?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He will then offer his wares to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, stating that it can rectify his problems.<ref>{{Cite|Grill some meat to get your strength back! In fact, who don't you give me a sec, and I'll sell you some meat.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What's the matter? Aren't you hungry? I'll sell ya some meat.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well, if it's your tummy that's grumblin', you're talkin' to the right guy. I'm a butcher, as luck would have it! I'd be happy to sell you whatever I've got in stock.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He also gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} advice about {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} with meat, such as grilling it with the {{Term|BotW|Rock Salt|link}} that he sells.<ref>{{Cite|How 'bout I whet your appetite with a little cooking tip? I've got just the thing to make your cuisine sizzle! All you gotta do...is add a dash of {{Color|BotW Blue|rock salt}} to double your dish's deliciousness!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The secret to delicious meat is to grill it well! And adding a dash of {{Color|BotW Blue|rock salt}} doubles down on the deliciousness. You feel that sensation? That's your mouth watering. That's what makes me such a good salesman right there.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Huh? What's your problem? You look real salty... Wait, is that what you're after? Well, you're in luck! I sell rock salt and meat, and I tell you what, they do go great together!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you're not getting enough salt in your diet, I'll sell you some rock salt. And why not some meat while I'm at it?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> This recipe will result in {{Term|BotW|Salt-Grilled Meat|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Salt-Grilled Prime Meat|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Salt-Grilled Gourmet Meat|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Mezer}} may also notice that {{Term|BotW|Link}} wants to speak to someone.<ref>{{Cite|What's up? You lookin' for someone to talk to?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> While he is willing to talk with him, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he only talks about meat.<ref>{{Cite|Fine by me, as long as we're talkin' meat, 'cuz that's all I really have that's worth talking about.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He then asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he is interested in buying.<ref>{{Cite|Finest meat in the land, a-yup. You, uh, fixin' to buy some?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Speaking of meat, {{Small|and I'm sure we were}}, I'm selling some, so if you want to buy, let's get down to business!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> In addition to this, if he believes that {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be lost, he offers directions to {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}} and {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What's goin' on with you? Are you lost or something? {{Color|BotW Blue|Kakariko Village}} is a ways north of here. If you're goin' to {{Color|BotW Blue|Dueling Peaks Stable}}, it's not much farther from here.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Kakariko Village}} is a bit farther north of here. If what you're looking for is a little fresh meat, I'm willing to trade with you.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} himself typically frequents {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You can usually find me at the stables, but if you can't wait, we can do a little business right here.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> Finally, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be tired, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} tells him to go to {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}, but he offers to sell him meat if he does not have the strength to make it there.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? What's the matter with you? You look right tuckered out. You know, {{Color|BotW Blue|Dueling Peaks Stable}} is real close. If you're feelin' a mite peckish, I've got just the thing to get your strength back. A nice hunk o' meat!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Dueling Peaks Stable}} is right over there. If you're feeling too tired to make it, how about a little meat to charge you up?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref>

While {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} is resting at the abandoned {{Term|BotW|Monster Stronghold}}, he will feel as though he is being judged by {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|What? Don't give me that look!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He claims that he finds being alone in the wilderness peaceful, even if it can be considered a wasteland.<ref>{{Cite|Yeah, maybe it's a bit of a wasteland here... But I find it peaceful, being all alone out in the wilderness.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He then states that if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has time to judge him, then he has time to buy meat from him as well, and doing so would cheer him up.<ref>{{Cite|If you've got time to judge, you've got time to buy some meat from me! Maybe I can even cut you a deal.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You know what would cheer me up right now? If you bought some of my meat. That's what.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> This continues at {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}, where he does not appreciate {{Term|BotW|Link}} judging where he is sleeping for the night.<ref>{{Cite|Huh> You got opinions about where I rest my head at night? Well, I can't see how it's any of your business.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> In reality, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} would like to sleep in a {{Term|BotW|Bed|link}}, but he does not make enough selling meat to afford one.<ref>{{Cite|Sure. I'd love to stay in a nice, comfy bed. Who wouldn't? But the meat trade ain't exactly rakin' in the dough. Why not help a fella out by stockin' up, as long as you're here?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} is attacked by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} while he travels, he will draw his {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword}} to defend himself.<ref>{{Cite|I'll take you down!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Let's go, you filth!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Come get some!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> During his fight, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} claims that one needs to be tough to fight {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}}, and he attempts to ward off his attackers.<ref>{{Cite|You gotta be real tough to go toe-to-toe with some of the monsters out here.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Go on, get! Dang ol' monsters! You wanna fight? Is that it?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> After the {{Plural|BotW|Enemy}} have been defeated, he will try to calm himself down.<ref>{{Cite|Phew...|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Haah, haah...|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm fine...|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} saves him, he will be relieved that both he and his wares are safe, and he may comment that his meats likely attracted the {{Plural|BotW|Monster}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh me, oh my... I coulda sworn I was gonna be somethin's dinner right there!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh me, oh my... At least my delicious selection of meats didn't get damaged!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Aw, dang! They must have been lured by the fine meats I've got here in my bag!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for saving me. Here, I want you to have this.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref>

When it {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=Rains}}, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} will take note.<ref>{{Cite|It's raining...|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He will take refuge under the cliffs of the southern {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|display=Dueling Peak}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, man! It's raining fierce out! Guess I'll have to take cover for a while.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he is still determined to keep selling meat.<ref>{{Cite|But even though I'm wet to the bone, meat waits for no one! So you better believe I'm open for business. I sell...meat.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> During {{Term|BotW|Rain}}, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} will add {{Term|BotW|Raw Gourmet Meat|link}} to his list of wares.<ref>{{Cite|Look at you, chasing adventure no matter the weather! You know what you need? Some raw gourmet meat!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} travels, he will advertise his wares to passerby.<ref>{{Cite|Tasty meats|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Farm fresh and ready!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Top-quality steak!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Juicy sirloin!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He claims to sell the best meats, offering his customers their {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} back if they are dissatisfied.<ref>{{Cite|I carry only the finest meats. I guarantee these are top-choice provisions, or your rupees back!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to browse his wares, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} will promote his {{Term|BotW|Raw Prime Meat|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Raw Bird Thigh|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll buy some meat.] Mm, you can't beat {{Color|BotW Blue|raw prime meat}}, unless you've got {{Color|BotW Blue|raw bird thigh}}! Nothing compares to rare!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes a purchase, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} will thank him and encourage him to return.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you're after some choice meat, you come find me, all right?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> He may also repeat his motto, which is "If you want meat, you want {{Term|BotW|Mezer}}!"<ref>{{Cite|Remember, if you want meat, you want Mezer! That's my motto.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} stops buying, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} asks if he wants anything else, since it would be a waste if his meat went bad.<ref>{{Cite|Anything else you need? Be a shame to let all this meat go to waste.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} buys all of his stock, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} informs him that he has nothing left to sell.<ref>{{Cite|That's the last of it. I'm afraid I've sold every last thing I've got.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> However, he still allows {{Term|BotW|Link}} to view the meats he will have when he restocks.<ref>{{Cite|I don't have anything left to sell today... Tell you what, though. You want a quick peak at what I'll be ordering next?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref>

Like other {{Plural|BotW|Merchant}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell his own {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Mezer}}.<ref>{{Cite|[I'd rather sell.] Watcha got?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you've got somethin' to sell, show me what it is!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Why don't we just take a little look-see?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Whatever you're sellin', I'm buyin'!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} makes a purchase, he will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} and ask if there is anything else he needs.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks! You're doin' me a real service here.|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thank you so much!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anything else I can do for you?|Mezer|BotW}}</ref> When their business is concluded, {{Term|BotW|Mezer}} thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} and wishes him well.<ref>{{Cite|[I'm good, thanks.] Thanks for stoppin' by. Good luck out there!|Mezer|BotW}}</ref>
