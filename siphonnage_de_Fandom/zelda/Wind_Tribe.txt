==History==
After living with the wind for a long time, the tribe gained mastery over it.<ref name="Tablet"/> They eventually abandoned the {{Term|TMC|Wind Ruins|link}} and used their wind magic to move their palace to the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} and keep it suspended there.<ref name="Figurine"/><ref>{{Cite|Our tribe abandoned the place you call the {{Color|TMC Green|Wind Ruins}}... With our magic, we moved our palace up into the skies. We are the {{Color|TMC Green|Wind Tribe}}.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref> They took the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element|link}} with them and placed it in the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}, guarding its entrance ever since.<ref>{{Cite|I do believe this is where the Tribe of the Winds once lived. Hm... So they've left for the heavens, and taken their element with them.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|In this tower dwell the people of the Wind Tribe. We left your surface world long ago to serve a greater duty. We now protect the gate to the Palace of Winds, where the Wind Element lies.|Strato|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The Wind Tribe lives here! They protect the path to the Palace of Winds, where the Wind Element lies!|Strato|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is the home of the Wind Tribe. We left the surface long ago. Now, we guard the entrance to the Palace of Winds... ...In this palace, the Wind Element resides.|N/A|TMC}}</ref> The {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|display=Tribe}} constructed {{Term|TMC|Mazaal|link}} to repel intruders and guard a stone tablet on top of the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Constructed by the Wind Tribe to repel intruders, this sturdy machine cannot be destroyed by any external force.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> The tablet tells those seeking the {{Term|TMC|Wind Element}} that the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe|display=Tribe}} moved to the sky and instructs adventurers to use the {{Term|TMC|Ocarina of Wind|link}} given to them by {{Term|TMC|Zeffa|link}}.<ref name="Tablet"/><ref>{{Cite|Those who come seeking our power must play the notes Zeffa teaches. May they lead you onward to the power that you seek.|Stone Tablet|TMC}}</ref>

Early in {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}'s adventure, he can {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}} to reach the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops|link}} via a [[Warping|warp]] portal in {{Term|TMC|South Hyrule Field|link}}. He is warped to a small section of {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}} where the ailing {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} resides; {{Term|TMC|Link}} cannot venture further until he eventually accesses the {{Term|TMC|Cloud Tops}} from {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}.

==Society==
Members of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} are very skilled at finding {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}}, storing what they can manage in {{Plural|TMC|Treasure Chest|link}} around their {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link|display=Home}}.<ref>{{Cite|We of the Wind Tribe are very skilled at finding Kinstone pieces. So skilled in fact, that we have more of them than we can manage! We can't even store them all. I wish someone would take a few from us.|Flurris|TMC}}</ref> This skill may be necessary, as they seem to depend on {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} to call winds.<ref>{{Cite|I bet if I could just fuse a Kinstone, I could call a good homeward wind...|Gale|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} may {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|display=Fuse}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone|link}} with some of their members, including {{Term|TMC|Gale|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Strato|link}} on the bottom floor, {{Term|TMC|Caprice|link}} on the second floor, {{Term|TMC|Flurris|link}} on the third floor, and {{Term|TMC|Hailey|link}} on the fourth floor.

The {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} seemed to be on good terms with many of the races in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} before leaving for the sky. They were a favored people of {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}},<ref>{{Cite|He was very fond of the people of the Wind Tribe.|Figurine|TMC}}</ref> an ancient king of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} built {{Plural|TMC|Armos|link}} statues for them long ago.<ref>{{Cite|Built by the Minish for the Wind Tribe long ago. They look like stone statues, but if you get too close, they move!|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

The {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}}'s leader, {{Term|TMC|Siroc|link}}, learns everything that happens on the surface by listening to the winds.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= The leader of the Wind Tribe knows everything happening on the surface by listening to rumors carried by the wind.|book= E |publisher= |page= 52}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We have been watching, and we know all that happens on your surface world. We know a small boy collects {{Color|TMC Red|elements}} to lift the curse from a princess. We know of {{Color|TMC Green|Vaati}} and his evil acts... The winds tell us these things.|Siroc|TMC}}</ref>

==Physiology==
Though they resemble typical {{Plural|TMC|Human|link}}, all members of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} have an innate ability to walk on clouds, though {{Term|TMC|Hailey|link}} implies this may be a result of having pure hearts.<ref name="Skywalker">{{Cite|I've never seen a surface dweller walk on clouds before! You must be quite pure of heart!|Hailey|TMC}}</ref> In addition, each of them has red-colored hair.

==Trivia==
*{{TP}}, {{SS}}, and {{TotK}} also feature races that live in the sky: the {{Plural|TPHD|Oocca|link}} in the {{Term|TPHD|City in the Sky|link}}, the {{Plural|SS|Hylian|link}} in {{Term|SS|Skyloft|link}}, and the [[Zonai]] in the [[Sky Islands]] respectively. Much like the people of {{Term|SS|Skyloft}}, the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} also sometimes refers to {{Term|Series|Hyrule|link}} as "The Surface".<ref name="Skywalker"/><ref>{{Cite|Some of Father's old texts talks about a place called the surface. The old tales describe a whole world under there, far more vast than Skyloft!|Zelda|SS}}</ref>
*The members of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} all have red hair, a trait they share with the {{Term|Series|Gerudo|link}}. {{Term|SS|Groose|link}} is another resident of the {{Term|SS|Sky|link}} with red hair. The [[Ancient Hero's Aspect]] -a Zonai- is also red-haired.
*Like many other races in {{TLoZ|Series}}, the names of many of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} members fit a theme. In this case, the members of the {{Term|TMC|Wind Tribe}} are related to weather:
**A ''{{Wp|Sirocco|siroc}}'' is a cold Mediterranean wind.
**{{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} comes from {{Wp|Gregale}}, a hot Mediterranean wind.
**A ''caprice'' is a sudden, unexpected change. A caprice can occur in weather.
**A ''gale'' is a very strong wind.
**{{Term|TMC|Hailey|link}} comes from ''hail'', which is precipitated lumps of ice.
**{{Term|TMC|Flurris|link}} comes from snow ''flurry'', which is a brief snow shower.
**{{Term|TMC|Strato|link}} is a shortened form of ''stratosphere'', the layer of the Earth's atmosphere just above the troposphere. The troposphere contains the majority of clouds, so {{Term|TMC|Strato}}'s name may literally mean "above the clouds."
