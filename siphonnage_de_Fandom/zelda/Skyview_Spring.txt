The {{Term/Store|Skyview Spring||Series, SS, SSHD}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 75}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Skyview Spring|Map|SSHD}}</ref> It is a sacred {{Term|SSHD|Spring (Location)|link}} located deep inside the [[Faron Woods]]. It is here where [[Link]] finds the [[Ruby Tablet]], which makes a rift in the clouds leading to [[Eldin Volcano]] once placed inside the [[Statue of the Goddess]].

== Appearance and Features==
The Skyview Spring is found deep inside the [[Skyview Temple]] and is filled with scenic waterfalls and [[Sacred Water]]. Link first visits Skyview Spring intending to find [[Princess Zelda#Skyward Sword|Zelda]] at the spring, but his fight with [[Ghirahim]] prevents that.<ref>{{Cite|I fear I spend far too long teasing and toying with you. The girl's presence has all but faded from this place, which means there's no reason to linger here.|Ghirahim|SS}}</ref> At the end of the spring, Link finds a crest that is similar in appearance to the crest found inside the Statue of the Goddess. Link must perform a [[Skyward Strike]] on the crest to activate it. 

[[Fi]] says she has a message from the gods of the old and begins to translate it for him.<ref>{{Cite|Master, I have a message written in the language of the gods of the old. Allow me to translate for you.|Fi|SS}}</ref> The text of the message states that the spirit maiden has set off for a second sacred place similar to Skyview Spring, known as the [[Earth Spring]].<ref>{{Cite|From the edge of time I guide you, the one chosen to carry out the goddess's mission. The spirit maiden who descended from the clouds must travel to two sacred places to purify her body. You stand in one of these places: Skyview Spring. The other is known as the Earth Spring. This second spring is hidden away deep within the scorched earth of Eldin. The spirit maiden, ever mindful of the heavy task entrusted to her, has set out to this second sacred place.|Fi|SS}}</ref> After the translation is complete, Link receives the Ruby Tablet.

Link must later return here so that he can receive some Sacred Water for the wounded [[Water Dragon|Faron]] from the spring's waters.

==Trivia==
*If Link tries to take water from the spring before meeting Faron, or after the quest with her is finished, it will be regular [[Bottled Water|water]].
*The Skyview Spring strongly resembles the [[Spring of Power]], [[Spring of Wisdom]], and [[Spring of Courage]] in {{BotW}} which feature similar geometry, landscape and architecture.
