{{Term/Store|''Selmie's Notes''||Series, BotW}},<ref>{{Cite|Selmie's Notes|N/A|BotW}}</ref> also known as the '''''Evacuation Hut Notes''''',<ref name="Cover">{{Cite|Evacuation Hut Notes<br/>By Selmie, The Duchess of Downhill|Selmie's Notes|BotW}}</ref> are an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|Series|Selmie's Notes}} are contained within a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} left at the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Trailhead Lodge|link}}. The first three pages are written by {{Term|BotW|Selmie|link}},<ref name="Cover"/> where she gives tips for surviving up in the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}}. She instructs readers to dress appropriately for the weather,<ref>{{Cite|And before anything else, you must dress appropriately for the weather here, not like you're heading to {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Field}}|Selmie's Notes|BotW}}</ref> and says they should look for {{Plural|BotW|Big Flag|link}} to find supplies that she has left behimd.<ref>{{Cite|I've left a few things behind on the mountain trails and at the base of the flags planted.|Selmie's Notes|BotW}}</ref>

After Selmie's part of the notes ends, several other inhabitants of the lodge have left their own messages in the book.<ref>{{Cite|Someone else has added notes...|Link|BotW}}</ref>
