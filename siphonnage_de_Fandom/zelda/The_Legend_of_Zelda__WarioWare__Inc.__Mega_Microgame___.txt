'''The Legend of Zelda''' is a microgame in the ''[[WarioWare]]'' series. It features a scenario from the first ''[[The Legend of Zelda]]'' game.  It first appears in ''[[mariowiki:WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!|WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Microgame$!]]'' and ''[[mariowiki:WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!|WarioWare, Inc.: Mega Party Game$!]]'', as part of [[mariowiki:9-Volt|9-Volt]]'s [[mariowiki:Nintendo Classics|Nintendo Classics]] stage, and the minigame later appears in ''[[WarioWare Gold]]'', as one of [[mariowiki:5-Volt|5-Volt]]'s microgames.

==Gameplay==
The player has to guide [[Link]] into a cave, as in the first screen of that game. The ''WarioWare Gold'' version features a different layout, as Link has to go across a bridge and pass some rocks placed in front of the entrance.

*'''Level 1:''' There are no obstacles.
*'''Level 2:''' There is a [[Peahat]] to avoid.
*'''Level 3:''' Besides the Peahat, there is a [[Zora (Enemy)|Zora]] that shoots energy balls at Link.
