==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Octorok Eyeballs are {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Forest Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Rock Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Snow Octorok|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Octorok|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Water Octorok|link}} when killed. They can be combined with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to make [[Elixir]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] accepts Octorok Eyeballs as an ingredient for Yellow [[Dye]], or they can be exchanged at the [[Fang and Bone]] for [[Mon]].

Five Octorok Eyeballs are needed to upgrade the [[Snow Boots]] to its third tier.

*Total for '''Snow Boots''': 5 (0, 0, 5, 0)
