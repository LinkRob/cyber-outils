{{Term/Store|Red Ice||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an object in {{OoT}}.<ref name="Blue Fire">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 117}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Red Ice is a type of [[ice]] found in [[Link]]'s adulthood in [[Zora's Domain]], the {{Term|OoT3D|Ice Cavern|link}}, and [[Ganon's Castle]]. 

When Link returns to Zora's Domain, he finds [[King Zora]] frozen in Red Ice as a product of the freezing of Zora's Domain. He also finds the entrance to the [[Zora Shop]] blocked by the ice. While venturing in the Ice Cavern, Link finds [[Blue Fire]], which can be scooped inside an empty [[Bottle]] and used to melt the Red Ice.<ref name="Blue Fire"/> Blue Fire can be used inside the cavern to gain access to [[Rupee]]s, [[Treasure Chest]]s, and a [[Piece of Heart]]. Link can also take some Blue Fire to free King Zora, which will then reward him with the [[Zora Tunic]]. Link finds one last time some Red Ice inside Ganon's Castle water barrier area.
