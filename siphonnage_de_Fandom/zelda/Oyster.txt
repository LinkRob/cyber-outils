'''Oysters''' are large [[wikipedia:Mollusca|mollusk-like]] creatures that appear on the {{Term|FPTRR|First Continent|link}} in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics==
Regular {{Plural|FPTRR|Oyster}} are found on {{Term|FPTRR|Sunshine Seashore}} and yield {{Term|FPTRR|Snatched Pearl|link}} (worth 100 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}}) which can be snatched from their open mouths. While stealing their {{Plural|FPTRR|Statched Pearl|Pearls}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} must be cautious not to have them clamp down on him, causing him to lose 15 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}}. {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} can be freed from the {{Term|FPTRR|Oyster}}'s grip by tapping it with the stylus.
