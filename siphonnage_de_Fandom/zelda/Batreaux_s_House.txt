'''Batreaux's House''' Is a location in [[The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword|Skyward Sword]]. It is found under [[Skyloft]]'s [[Residential Quarter]], with the entrance found in the [[Graveyard]]. This is where [[Link]] can start the [[Gratitude Crystal]] [[Side Quest|Side quest]].
