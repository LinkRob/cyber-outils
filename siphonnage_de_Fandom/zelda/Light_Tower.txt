The {{Term/Store|Light Tower||Series, SS}} is a location in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 296}}</ref> It is a tall, scalable pillar located near the southern tip of [[Skyloft]]. During [[Link]]'s quest to rescue [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], he must activate the tower in order to infiltrate the thick layer of storm clouds enveloping the [[Thunderhead]].

==Features and Overview==
Hidden in the lyrics of the [[Ballad of the Goddess]] are the instructions to activating the Light Tower.<ref>{{Cite|Oh youth, show the two whirling sails the way to the Light Tower...and before you a path shall open, and a heavenly song you shall hear.|Gaepora|SS}}</ref> Following the clues, Link uses the [[Gust Bellows]] to redirect the two windmills until they face the Light Tower. A section of the tower then raises from the center, revealing a gazebo-like structure at the top of the tower. Link climbs up to investigate, and pulls out the [[Goddess's Harp]], strumming in time to [[Fi]]'s beat. The platform rotates, and a beam of concentrated sunlight is projected through an ornate glass window and fired directly at the Thunderhead. This bright emanation successfully clears a small portion of the thick, swirling cloud cover of the Thunderhead, allowing Link to fly in on his [[Loftwing]].

A [[Sacred Tear]] is located atop the Light Tower in Skyloft's [[Silent Realm]], as well as a lone [[Guardian#Skyward Sword|Guardian]].
