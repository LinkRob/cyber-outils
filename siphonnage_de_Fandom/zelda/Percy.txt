{{Term/Store|Percy||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Percy}}
{{Term|TMC|Percy}} is a poet who first appears at the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}},<ref>{{Cite|I've been on the road for a while. I came back in time for the festival.|Percy|TMC}}</ref> and can later be found in a {{Term|TMC|Tree|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}. While he was away from his {{Term|TMC|Percy's House|link|display=House}} in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}, the way was blocked by a fallen {{Term|TMC|Tree}},<ref>{{Cite|I travel all over the realm, writing verse wherever I go. But here I am, returning after a long absence only to find the road blocked.|Percy|TMC}}</ref> and he lost a red {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Piece|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Happy Hearth Inn|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It wouldn't feel like such a waste if I hadn't lost half of my {{Color|TMC Red|Kinstone}}! When misfortune rains down upon my head, it pours in torrents! I think {{Color|TMC Blue|I may have dropped it in the inn}} I stayed at last night...|Percy|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link|display=Fuses}} {{Plural|TMC|Kinstone Piece}} with him,<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! That {{Color|TMC Red|fragment}} looks just like mine! They might even fit together!|Percy|TMC}}</ref> the fallen {{Term|TMC|Tree}} will rise, allowing access to {{Term|TMC|Percy's House}}.

If {{Term|TMC|Link}} goes to {{Term|TMC|Percy's House}} in the {{Term|TMC|Western Wood}}, he will find {{Term|TMC|Percy}} distraught outside, as the {{Term|TMC|Monster Lady|link}} has taken up residence while he was away.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, dear! {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! Won't you listen to my verse? When I finally returned to my home, sweet home, a stranger was there!|Percy|TMC}}</ref> Once {{Term|TMC|Link}} obtains the {{Term|TMC|Flame Lantern|link}} from the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, he can light the two {{Plural|TMC|Torchlight|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Percy's House}}, revealing the {{Term|TMC|Monster Lady}} to be a {{Term|TMC|Bow Moblin|link}}. After this, {{Term|TMC|Percy}} is able to return to his {{Term|TMC|Percy's House|display=House}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! Thanks to you, I can finally go back in my house. It's been totally rearranged, but I think it's a good look!|Percy|TMC}}</ref> thanking {{Term|TMC|Link}} and giving him 100 {{Plural|TMC|Mysterious Shell|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, just a moment... My dresser is entirely stuffed full of these icky blue seashells. Would you clean them out for me? They're so...smelly. Please?|Percy|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Percy}} is the author of {{Term|TMC|So Your Son Wants to Be a Hero... It Could Be Verse!|link}}, found at the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There seem to be many interesting books in here... {{Color|TMC Blue|Hyrule on 10 Rupees a Day}}, {{Color|TMC Blue|So Your Son Wants to Be a Hero}}... {{Color|TMC Blue|It Could Be Verse!}}, by Percy, and {{Color|TMC Blue|Rem's Anthology for Sleepless Nights}}.|Ezlo|TMC}}</ref>
