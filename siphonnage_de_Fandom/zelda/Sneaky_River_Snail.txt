==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Sneaky River Snail}}
Sneaky River Snails are found on the shores of bodies of water in [[West Necluda]] and the [[Lanayru Great Spring]]. They're easy to spot at night due to their glow.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be eaten raw to restore one {{Term|BotW|Heart|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. Roasting one by a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} will result in [[Sneaky River Escargot]], but they can also be chilled by [[Ice]] to make {{Plural|Series|Frozen River Snail|link}}. Meals made in a [[Cooking Pot]] using Sneaky River Snails will temporarily increase Link's [[Stealth]].<ref name="Inventory"/>

While [[Cooking]], either a Sneaky River Snail, a [[Hearty Blueshell Snail]], or any {{Term|BotW|Fish|link}} is required to make [[Salt-Grilled Fish]]. To make a [[Seafood Skewer]], either a snail, a [[Bright-Eyed Crab]], an [[Ironshell Crab]], or a [[Razorclaw Crab]] must be used. Sneaky River Snails also count as seafood when making [[Fish Pie]], [[Creamy Seafood Soup]], [[Copious Fish Skewers]], [[Glazed Seafood]], [[Gourmet Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Monster Stew]], [[Pepper Seafood]], [[Prime Meat and Seafood Fry]], [[Seafood Meunière]], [[Seafood Rice Balls]], and [[Steamed Fish]].

When upgrading the [[Stealth Set]] at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}, five Sneaky River Snails and eight {{Plural|Series|Silent Shroom|link}} are needed for each individual piece to reach its third tier.

* Total for '''Stealth Set''': 15 (0, 0, 5, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
