==Location and Uses==
Blast Blooms can be found in various locations in [[Holodrum]], usually under high ledges that [[Link]] cannot reach. During [[Spring]], they bloom and open their petals. If Link hops into a Blast Boom, it will close its petals around him and shoot him up. A Blast Bloom in [[Mt. Cucco]] leads to a [[Piece of Heart]].
