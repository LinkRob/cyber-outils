{{Term/Store|Floret Sandbar||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Floret Sandbar'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Floret Sandbar is a small island in the middle of the [[Hylia River]] in [[West Necluda]], located west of [[Eagus Bridge]]. It is the home of a flower maze, grown by [[Magda]].<ref>{{Cite|I planted flowers around the shrine. Please be careful where you step. You don't want to hurt them, do you?|Magda|BotW}}</ref> If [[Link]] speaks to Magda, or steps on or damages the flowers, she will warn him not to hurt them,<ref>{{Cite|Hey! What do you think you're doing?! {{Color|BotW Red|You must not harm the flowers}}!|Magda|BotW}}</ref> initiating the "[[Watch Out for the Flowers]]" [[Shrine Quest]]. At the end of the flower maze sits the [[Hila Rao Shrine]].

Along the western bank of the Sandbar, Link can find a [[Raft]]. Hidden in the water near the raft is a metallic [[Treasure Chest]], which contains a [[Purple Rupee]].
