"{{Term/Store|The Silent Swordswomen||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Silent Swordswomen}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Laine|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Northwest Gate|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, she warns him to avoid {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We caution all travelers to avoid sandstorms.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> It is easy for even the {{Plural|BotW|Gerudo|link}} to lose their way in a {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm}},<ref>{{Cite|If you're caught in a sandstorm, you'll lose you way. Even us Gerudo have trouble navigating them.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> which has made the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} a prime location for thieves and ancient cultures to hide their treasures.<ref>{{Cite|The danger of the sandstorms makes the desert a perfect hiding place for thieving bandits and priceless treasures.|Laine|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...Which is precisely why there are so many stories about ancient cultures hiding their treasure in the desert.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Laine}} after calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}}, she will be relieved that it is gone.<ref>{{Cite|I couldn't get a good look at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} from here anyway, but I'm glad it's gone.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> However, she wishes something could be done about the {{Plural|BotW|Monster}} and the {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}}, as she could then search for treasure.<ref>{{Cite|Now, if we could only do something about the monsters and sandstorm. Without those, I'd be able to find all of that treasure hidden out in the desert! If the legends are true, that is...|Laine|BotW}}</ref>}} Though {{Term|BotW|Link}} is curious about this treasure, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} admits that she does not know many of the details.<ref>{{Cite|[Treasure?] Having said that, I don't really know the details either.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Laine}} points out the nearby {{Term|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, explaining that there are more throughout the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|There are {{Color|BotW Red|statues of swordswomen}} scattered across Gerudo Desert. They're remnants of ages long past.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> There is a rumor that the {{Term|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|display=Statues}} lead to an ancient treasure, but {{Term|BotW|Laine}} dismisses this as superstition.<ref>{{Cite|People talk about the statues pointing to the hiding place of ancient treasure... but that's just a superstition.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> Due to the {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} that hide in the {{Plural|BotW|Sandstorm}}, however, {{Term|BotW|Laine}} sees no reason to risk such a journey.<ref>{{Cite|Even if it was true, there are dangerous monsters in the sandstorms.<br/>I wouldn't risk it.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.{{Note|It is possible to immediately complete this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} by locating {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos Shrine}} before speaking to {{Term|BotW|Laine}}.}}

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can follow the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue}} though the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}}. Along his path, he will encounter {{Plural|BotW|Monster Stronghold|link}} that he must either defeat or avoid. Eventually, he will come upon {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You followed the guidance of the swordswomen statues to find the treasure amidst the desert sandstorms. The prize turned out to be an ancient shrine!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> Upon activating the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
