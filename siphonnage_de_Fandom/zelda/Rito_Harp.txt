The {{Term/Store|Rito Harp||Series}} is an object in {{TWW}}.{{Name Ref|TWW}} It is [[Medli]]'s most valued possession.<ref>{{Cite|Her greatest treasure is her harp that she carries on her back.|Figurine|TWW}}</ref> The Rito Harp went unnamed prior to its appearance in the non-canonical spin-off title, {{HW}}.

==Location and Uses==
According to Medli, playing music on the Rito Harp is an important duty of being the Sky Spirit [[Valoo]]'s attendant, and as such, she spends most of her free time practicing in order to improve her Harp skills.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I'd better continue practicing so that I can be useful around here, too! Performing music like this is an important duty of an attendant.|Medli|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Master Link, Medli is busy with her attendant studies.|Akoot|TWW}}</ref> The Rito Harp plays an important role in the game due to it being required to awaken Medli as a [[Sage]] as well as restoring the [[Master Sword]]'s [[Power to Repel Evil|true power]].

It is unknown how Medli came in possession of the Rito Harp; however, the young [[Rito]] girl mentions that when [[Komali's Grandmother]] saw the Harp that Medli was carrying, the old woman sensed what destiny had in store for the young girl, but kept quiet about it, instead using her position to teach Medli all that she had to know until the day fate would call her.<ref>{{Cite|This instrument is called one of fate's tools. Prince Komali's grandmother saw me carrying it one day and called out to me to stop. She'd seen my fate in the curves of the instrument, you see. She eventually became my teacher, and that's how I became the attendant you see before you today. So, now you understand why I need to hurry and improve my skills, so that I may help the aerie.|Medli|TWW}}</ref>

After [[Link]] meets [[Laruto]], the former Sage of Earth before [[Ganondorf]] murdered her, she informs the young hero that he must look for the one carries the same sacred instrument as her, a Harp, and play the "[[Earth God's Lyric]]", the melody that will awaken the new Sage of Earth.<ref>{{Cite|The song you just conducted is one which will open the eyes of the new sage and awaken within that sage the melody that will carry our prayers to the gods.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref> The young hero then travels to [[Dragon Roost Island]], where he sees Medli practicing with the Rito Harp in one of the island's cliffs. As instructed by Laruto, Link conducts the "Earth God's Lyric" as Medli plays along with the Rito Harp, which causes the young girl to faint.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... What a mysterious song... It sounds so... familiar. It's almost as if something I've forgotten is trying to be remembered... Oh... I feel...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> She meets Laruto in her dreams, where the two of them play the sacred song with their Harps. Realizing her new duty, Medli wakes up as the new Sage of Earth, overwhelmed by the fact that she can actually do something to help the world.<ref>{{Cite|Just now, a sage spoke to me. She spoke so gently... There's something...something that I must do. Link... Thanks to you, I've been awakened to the knowledge that I'm a sage of the Earth Temple. There's actually something that I can do to help this world. It's incredible...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> The young Rito girl then urges Link to take her to the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]] to thus complete her duty as the Sage of Earth.<ref>{{Cite|Link, please... You must take me to the Earth Temple. We must hurry and wake the power to repel evil that sleeps within the Master Sword.|Medli|TWW}}</ref>

[[File:Halfpower.png|thumb|right|Link conducting "Earth God's Lyric" as Medli plays along with the Rito Harp]]
Inside the Earth Temple, the Rito Harp plays an important role; by using the "[[Command Melody]]", Link gains the ability to control Medli. By making her stand near a ray of light, Medli can take out her harp to change the direction of rays of light, which often is part of a more complex "light puzzle" and thus serving as a second [[Mirror Shield]]. This function of the harp is extremely useful to make [[Blue Fog]] disappear, complete the various light puzzles around the Earth Temple, and even shed light on [[Poe]]s to make them appear.

After defeating [[Jalhalla]], Link conducts the Earth God's Lyric for Medli as she plays the song on the Rito Harp. As they play it, Laruto temporarily appears next to Medli to play along. This restores some of the power of the Master Sword, to which Medli takes notice and urges Link to go to the Wind Temple. Medli remains in the Earth Temple to assure the power to repel evil stays in the Master Sword, which is done so by continuing to play the Rito Harp.

In {{HW}}, the Rito Harp is a class of Weapon used by Medli. It is associated with {{Term|HWDE|Element|link|display=Light}}. Along with Medli, it is free DLC released in version 1.9.0 for the [[Wii U]] version, and version 1.3.0 for {{HW|L}}. The [[Nintendo 3DS]] version requires the purchase of the Master ''Wind Waker'' Pack to unlock the higher levels.

====Variations====
The default Rito Harp is the '''Sacred Harp'''. The second form of the Rito Harp is the '''Earth God's Harp''', followed by '''Din's Harp'''.

=====Special Attack=====
{{Main|Special Attack}}
Medli slashes two gusts of wind at the group of enemies in front of her, forming a large typhoon ball that traps and damages her enemies. She then flies up into the air and then comes crashing down over it, sending her enemies flying at the typhoon's burst. Medli then stands up in a brief dizzy state.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*The Rito Harp is one of the few bump-mapped objects in the entire {{TLoZ|Series}}, along with the [[Mirror Shield]] from {{TWW|-}} and the [[Goddess Cube]]s from {{SS}}.
*As an Easter Egg, two pictures appear on the back wall of the [[Milk Bar]], one of a harp and one of a violin side-by-side in {{ALBW}}, seemingly a reference to the two instruments used by the [[Sage]]s in {{TWW|-}}.
