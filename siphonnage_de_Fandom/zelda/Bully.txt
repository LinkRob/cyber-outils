The {{Term/Store|Bully||Series, ALttP}} is a boy transformed into a monster due to the [[Dark World]]'s magic in {{ALttP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 388 {{Exp Game|ALttP}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Bully was transformed into a monster when he came in search of the [[Triforce]] with [[Bully's Friend|his friend]], who was transformed into a ball-like creature.

[[File:Bully.gif|25px|left]][[File:Bully(Cured).gif|25px|left]]
Link finds him on top of [[Death Mountain]] where the Bully chases after his ball-friend. The ball constantly runs away from him, but the Bully follows him and kicks him like a soccer ball. When Link wishes on the Triforce for Ganon's evil to be undone, all of Agahnim's wrongdoings are undone as well. As a result, Bully is transported back to the Light World. During the end credits, the Bully can be seen chasing after his friend just outside of the [[Tower of Hera (A Link to the Past)|Tower of Hera]], both in their normal forms.
