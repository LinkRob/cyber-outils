==Location and uses==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Whirlpool Waterway}}
Whirlpools are first seen in {{ALttP}}, known as [[Whirlpool Waterway]]s and are scattered throughout [[Hyrule]]. [[Link]] may use certain Whirlpools to [[Warping|Warp]] to different areas in Hyrule. Whirlpools also indicate the surfacing of [[River Zora|Zora]]s from bodies of water.

==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Vortexes appear in some rooms of the [[Water Temple]], and they can draw in Link and return him to the start of the room if he gets caught. The infamous "serpent-shaped" room contained a maze of these vortexes, although the {{OoT|MQ}} version adds [[Hookshot]] targets to make the room a lot easier to navigate.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Stub|MM}}

==={{OoA|-}}===
Whirlpools appear in the [[Sea of Storms]] in {{OoA}}. They are impassible barriers that will swallow Link whole if he attempts to cross them. The only means of passing them is through an underwater entrance in the past.

==={{FS|-}}===
Whirlpools appear in the [[Sea of Trees]] in {{FS}}. If Link falls into one, he will drown and receive two [[Heart]]s of damage before resurfacing on dry land.

==={{TWW|-}}===
Whirlpools are found all over the [[Great Sea]] in {{TWW}}. Whirlpools form around various obstacles, such as the barrier to [[Jabun]]'s cave on [[Outset Island]] or when water is displaced from [[Big Octo]]s surfacing. Whenever they appear, Link must complete the task at hand within a timely manner or he will be swallowed by the Whirlpool. Entering a Whirlpool in {{TWW|-}} causes the weather to become stormy until the Whirlpool disappears.

==={{FSA|-}}===
Whirlpools appear in {{FSA}}, where they suck the [[Links]] down into small, underwater caverns.

==={{PH|-}}===
In {{PH}}, there are only small Whirlpools, and they can damage the [[SS Linebeck]]. Once Link obtains the [[Cannon]], he can destroy the Whirlpools with the cannonballs.

==={{TotK|-}}===
In {{TotK}}, following the [[Upheaval]], a whirlpool is in {{Term|TotK|Lake Hylia|link}}, east of the {{Term|TotK|Bridge of Hylia|link}}. When Link is sucked in, it leads to the [[Lake Hylia Whirlpool Cave]], containing [[En-oma Shrine]].

During the quest [[Sidon of the Zora]], {{Term|TotK|Sidon|link}} creates a whirlpool in the {{Term|TotK|East Reservoir Lake|link}} that leads to the [[Ancient Zora Waterworks]].

During {{Term|TotK|Whirly Swirly Things|link}}, {{Term|TotK|Kula|link}} requests Link to find a phenomenon that matches a "whirly swirly thing,"{{Fact}} where Link must show Kula a picture of a whirlpool using the {{Term|TotK|camera|link}} function.
