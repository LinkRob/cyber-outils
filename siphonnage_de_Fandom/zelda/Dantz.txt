{{Term/Store|Dantz||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Dantz|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Dantz}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who works at {{Term|BotW|Hateno Pasture|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. He carries a {{Term|BotW|Farmer's Pitchfork|link}}, which he uses to move hay. When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first approaches him, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} asks if he is a wanderer.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Good day, stranger! Hrm? Are you some kinda wanderer?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> He mutters to himself that this could work,<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Hrm, interesting... This could work out...}}|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> noting {{Term|BotW|Link}} looks like he can use a {{Term|BotW|Bow|link}} and asking if he could help.<ref>{{Cite|You look handy with a bow. Mind helping a poor farmer out?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} remarks that he is the answer to his prayers.<ref>{{Cite|Woo-hoo! I had a lucky feeling you were the answer to my prayers!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he needs {{Term|BotW|Link}} to hunt the {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Buck|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest|link}}, since they are overpopulated and destroying the environment.<ref>{{Cite|So what I need is for you to hunt some {{Color|BotW Red|deer}} out in {{Color|BotW Blue|Retsam Forest}}. They're overpopulated these days, and it's destroying the forest.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The thing with these deer, though... We really have to get their numbers under control.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> He gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a time limit of one minute.<ref>{{Cite|That's why I was hoping you could hunt all the {{Color|BotW Red|deer}} you can...in {{Color|BotW Red|one minute}}.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} accept this, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} wants to know if he can start hunting right away.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! Can...can you go right away?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks if there is a trick, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} suggests using a strong {{Term|BotW|Bow}} so that he will only have to take one shot.<ref>{{Cite|[Is there a trick to this?] A trick? Well, I guess it helps to have a strong bow to take 'em down with one shot.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> He also advises sneaking up to {{Plural|BotW|Deer|link}} without them noticing.<ref>{{Cite|And of course, you want to get close enough to the deer to line up your shot without them noticing you.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} decides to head out, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} calls him a trooper and asserts that he is counting on him.<ref>{{Cite|What a trooper! I'm counting on you!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Before the {{Term|BotW|Deer Hunting|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} starts, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} says he will call for {{Term|BotW|Link}} once the minute is up.<ref>{{Cite|About that {{Color|BotW Red|deer hunting}}... No time like the present, right? Go get started, and I'll call you once your {{Color|BotW Red|minute}} is up, OK?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} runs out of time, he will be transported back to {{Term|BotW|Dantz}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's time! Come back, wanderer!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} fails to get a single {{Term|BotW|Deer}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} refuses to give him any reward.<ref>{{Cite|Huh? You didn't even get ONE? Sorry, but no results, no reward.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> He gives him one {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} for getting one to three {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} as thanks for doing his part.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks for doing your part! Here's a reward for you...|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> If he gets four to six deer, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} vicious and rewards him with a {{Term|BotW|Blue Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You're downright vicious with that bow, wanderer! Here's a reward for you...|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> For seven to nine {{Plural|BotW|Deer}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} admits it is more than he thought {{Term|BotW|Link}} would get and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I knew you were a go-getter, but this is more than I ever dreamed of! Here's a reward for you...|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} is shocked if {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets all 10 {{Plural|BotW|Deer}}, saying there is such a thing as being too good at hunting and giving him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got 10 deer?! Easy there, wanderer. I didn't think I'd be saying this, but there's such a thing as being TOO good a hunter. I won't skimp on it this time!|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

Like all residents of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz}} will notice if {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to him while carrying the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}. He recalls that {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} used to carry a similar flame,<ref>{{Cite|Old lady Purah used to carry a {{Color|BotW Blue|flame}} of that color... You think? Nah...|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> and while he does not know why a stranger has it, he sends {{Term|BotW|Link}} along anyway.<ref>{{Cite|I don't know what a stranger like you is doing with it, but you'd better hurry along now.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, he will encourage {{Term|BotW|Link}} to continue on his delivery for {{Term|BotW|Purah}}.<ref>{{Cite|Shouldn't you deliver that {{Color|BotW Blue|flame}} to old lady Purah up in her lab?|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>
