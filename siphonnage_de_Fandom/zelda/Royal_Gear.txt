The {{Term/Store|Royal Gear|Royal Gear|Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear}} was equipment issued to the immediate guard details of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Claymore}}<br/>A two-handed sword issued to the Hyrulean royal family's immediate guard detail. Its powerful strikes are said to crush an opponent's body and resolve alike.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Royal Halberd">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Halberd}}<br/>This spear was issued to the knights who guarded Hyrule Castle's throne room. Its ornate design was applied by a craftsman in service to the royal family.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Royal Shield">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Shield}}<br/>A shield issued to the Hyrulean royal family's immediate guard detail. It boasts a high defense, but these days it's more a collector's item due to its ornamentation.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> as well as in recognition of impressive martial feats.<ref name="Royal Broadsword">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Broadsword}}<br/>The Hyrulean royal family would award this sword to knights who achieved remarkable feats. A sword that balances strength and beauty as elegantly as this one is a rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref name="Royal Bow">{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Bow}}<br/>In the past, the king of Hyrule presented this bow to only the most talented archers in the land. Its combat capabilities are as impressive as its extravagant design.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Weaponry of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear}} is notable for its combination of power and elegant, elaborate design.<ref name="Royal Shield"/><ref name="Royal Broadsword"/><ref name="Royal Bow"/> The weapons' distinctive ornamentation was created by craftsmen in the royal family's service.<ref name="Royal Halberd"/>

Every piece of {{Term|BotW|Royal Gear}} is metallic. This means they will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, but they do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. While they will sink if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they can be picked up and manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
