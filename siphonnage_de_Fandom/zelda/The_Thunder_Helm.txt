"{{Term/Store|The Thunder Helm||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Thunder Helm}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Gerudo Town}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} regains control of the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, the legendary {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} can be found resting on a small pedestal beside {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} interacts with the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}} will stop him from touching it.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Halt! That is the treasure of the Gerudo, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}}!}} You may be held in high esteem, but this does not give you the freedom to lay your hands on the pride of our people.|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}} interjects and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he wants the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|display=Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|Becalm yourself, Buliara. You...desire this helm?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> While she is unable to gift the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} to {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|[I want it!] I see... And yet, despite the great debt we owe you, I cannot just give such a precious thing to you.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> but since he is fighting the enemy of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} offers to lend it to him.<ref>{{Cite|...But you ARE up against the enemy of Lady Urbosa... And if you truly desire it, then perhaps I can lend it to you...|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to only take the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} temporarily, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} says that he must gain the trust of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} in order to earn it.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes! Lend it to me!] Well then, let's see how serious you are. This helm is a cultural treasure. To earn it, you must gain the trust of our people.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She tasks him with solving the problems of the citizens of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}.<ref>{{Cite|I want you to address their concerns and bring a resolute peace to our town.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> thus beginning the quest. The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins at this point. While {{Term|BotW|Link}} is powerful, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} reminds him he will not be able to solve the people's problems with only his power.<ref>{{Cite|We already know that you are powerful. But you can't solve all of my people's problems with power alone.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

In order to satisfy {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s terms, {{Term|BotW|Link}} complete the following {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}}:{{Note|While "{{Term|BotW|The Secret Club's Secret|link}}" does take place in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} does not need to complete it for this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.}}
* "{{Term|BotW|Tools of the Trade|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|The Mystery Polluter|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|Medicinal Molduga|link}}"
* "{{Term|BotW|The Search for Barta|link}}"

After all four {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest}} are done, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}}, where {{Term|BotW|Riju}} shares that she has heard of his good deeds.<ref>{{Cite|I have heard of your recent actions. You have returned smiles to the faces of my people...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She offers her thanks on behalf of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} and declares him a true friend.<ref>{{Cite|On behalf of all the Gerudo, I must offer you my heartfelt thanks. Sarqso. You are, from this moment on, a true friend of the Gerudo!|Riju|BotW}}</ref> Keeping her promise from before, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} lends {{Term|BotW|Link}} the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}.<ref>{{Cite|And if it can help a true friend in need, we will gladly lend you this sacred treasure.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She assures {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he will be able to use the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} to its fullest potential and unlock the power of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}} that lies within.<ref>{{Cite|I know that you, more than any other, will be able to use this {{Color|BotW Blue|Thunder Helm}} to its fullest potential... I believe you can unlock the power of Urbosa that lies within.|Riju|BotW}}</ref> This completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
