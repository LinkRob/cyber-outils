The {{Term/Store|Sand-Seal Rally||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Sand-Seal Rally'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally}} is a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|link}} racing spot south of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. Two {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} women, {{Term|BotW|Shabonne|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tali|link}}, can be found here. By completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Undefeated Champ|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can reveal the {{Term|BotW|Raqa Zunzo Shrine|link}} that resides here. In order to do this, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must beat {{Term|BotW|Tali}}'s record of one minute and 30 seconds in the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|link}}.
