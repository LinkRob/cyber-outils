"'''Village of the Blue Maiden'''" is a [[Song]] in {{FSA}}. It has two different versions.

==Overview==
==={{FSA|-}}===
"Village of the Blue Maiden" serves as the background music for the location of the same name in {{FSA}}, [[Village of the Blue Maiden]]. It is a rendition of "[[Kakariko Village (Theme)|Kakariko Village]]", and it also contains a small part of the [[The Legend of Zelda (Theme)|overworld theme]] from the original {{TLoZ}}.

At first, when entering the Village, the theme is a dramatic and low-pitched sounding tune. After the four Links defeat Shadow Link, however, and return the village to normal, the theme changes to be more upbeat and cheery sounding.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{SSBB|-}}===
"Village of the Blue Maiden" appears as a track in {{SSBB}}. The version used here is the upbeat and cheery version of the Village theme. It is an unlockable track that plays in the [[Tetra's Ship#Super Smash Bros. Brawl|Pirate Ship]] Stage. It can be unlocked by picking up a random CD. "Village of the Blue Maiden" plays during the credits sequence after completing [[Classic Mode|Classic]] or All-Star Mode as [[Toon Link]].

==={{SSB4|Wii U|-}}===
"Village of the Blue Maiden" reappears as a default [[Song]] in {{SSB4|Wii U}}. It plays in the [[Skyloft#Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Skyloft]] and [[Tetra's Ship#Super Smash Bros. for Wii U|Pirate Ship]] Stages.

==={{SSBU|-}}===
"Village of the Blue Maiden" reappears as a track in {{SSBU}}. It is a default Song for any Stage related to [[The Legend of Zelda (Series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] and the Song plays while shopping in [[Beedle's Tent]].
{{Noncanon|End}}
