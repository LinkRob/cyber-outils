The {{Term/Store|Water Dragon's Scale||Series, SS}} is an {{Term|SSHD|Item|link}} in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 142}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[File:SS Water Dragon's Scale Icon.png|left]]
The Water Dragon's Scale is a relic from [[Faron (Skyward Sword)|Faron]], the dragon spirit and protector of the {{Term|SS|Faron|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I say, that's one of MY scales you have there!|Faron (Dragon)|SS}}</ref> It allows {{Term|SS|Link|link}} to dive and swim underwater.<ref>{{Cite|As you may have observed during your time as one, human beings are not a species that can stay underwater for long periods of time. Somewhere in the world is an item called a Water Dragon's Scale. Rumors suggest that this item allows a person to dive deep underwater.|Fi|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The great spirit of the {{Color|SS Blue|Water Dragon}} has provided you with the ability to swim freely underwater.|Fi|SS}}</ref> He is also capable of performing a spin maneuver underwater.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SS Gold|Water Dragon's Scale}}! This sacred gift left by the goddess grants you the power to explore underwater and even execute a spin maneuver!|N/A|SS}}</ref> If Link spins towards the top of the water, he will leap out of it.<ref>{{Cite|If you spin up {{Color|SS Red|toward the water's surface}}, you can do an amazing leap right out of the water!|Jellyf|SS}}</ref> The scale is obtained as a reward for completing [[Farore (Goddess)|Farore]]'s [[Silent Realm]]. It is required to access {{Term|SS|Lake Floria|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you use the {{Color|SS Blue|Water Dragon's Scale}} from the {{Color|SS Red|trial}} in {{Color|SS Blue|Faron Woods}}, there is a 90% chance you will be able to reach a new area in {{Color|SS Blue|Faron Province}}.|Fi|SS}}</ref>
