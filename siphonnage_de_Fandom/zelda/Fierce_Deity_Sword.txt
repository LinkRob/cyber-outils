The {{Term/Store|Fierce Deity Sword||Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="BotW Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Fierce Deity Sword}}<br/>A peculiar greatsword allegedly used by a hero from a world in which the moon threatened to fall. It slashes wildly in battle as if possessed by a fierce deity.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|MM3D}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
When {{Term|MM3D|Link}} equips the infamous {{Term|MM3D|Fierce Deity's Mask|link}} in {{MM}} to become [[Fierce Deity Link]], the [[Hero of Time]] manifests the Fierce Deity's Sword - a very powerful blade that is able to focus {{Term|MM3D|Link}}'s magical power into a [[Sword Beam|disk of light]] and release it during an attack while [[Targeting|Z-Targeting]]. As well as possessing this useful ability, the Fierce Deity's Sword inflicts great physical damage - equal to that of the {{Term|MM3D|Great Fairy's Sword|link}}. If {{Term|MM3D|Link}} strikes an enemy while targeting and has any [[Magic Meter|Magic Power]] remaining, the enemy will take damage both from the Sword itself and from the energy blast, for a grand total of eight times the destructive power of the {{Term|MM3D|Kokiri Sword|link}}. However, this doesn't work on normal enemies because the sword was only ever intended to be used against {{Plural|MM3D|Boss|link}}; These enemies will resist the secondary damage from the energy disk because its ability to break through the defenses were not programmed into regular foes. The energy beams can, however, be used against objects (i.e. {{Plural|MM3D|Gossip Stone|link}}, {{Plural|MM3D|Sign|link}}, etc.). Due to its double helix shape, the weapon is sometimes referred to as the "Double Helix Sword" or "Double Helix Blade." The Fierce Deity's Sword cannot be put away unless Link is inside a building. It is indeed one of the strongest [[Sword|Swords]] in {{TLoZ|-}} series, on-par with the [[Master Sword Lv3]] from {{aLttP}} and {{aLBW}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Sword}} appears in {{BotW}} as an exclusive {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} {{Term|BotW|Item|link}}. It has a rare chance of appearing in a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} after scanning the {{MM|-}} {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}} with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}, but only after {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed at least one of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}. It functions as a two-handed {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} and its description refers to it as a "peculiar greatsword" allegedly used by a {{Term|MM3D|Link|link|display=hero}} from a world in which the moon threatened to fall. The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Sword}} slashes wildly in battle as if possessed by a fierce deity, as suggested by its name.<ref name="BotW Inventory"/> It can be wielded by {{Term|BotW|Link}} even when he is not wearing the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Set|link}} acquired using the same {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can acquire replacements of the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Sword}} by using the {{Term|Series|Amiibo}} again. Like other two-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, it can be thrown and can also be displayed on the {{Term|BotW|Weapon Mount|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link's House|link}}. It has a base attack power of 60, making it equal to the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Power to Repel Evil|link|display=full Power}}, but its durability is at a 35, making it inferior to the {{Term|BotW|Master Sword}} in that aspect.

The {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Sword}} is made of metal, and it will therefore conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} if it is equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. It will sink if dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but due to its magnetic nature, it can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}. It also will not burn if exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
The Fierce Deity Sword appears in ''The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom''. It has an attack power of 38. Rock Octorocks cannot repair it.

The {{Term|TotK|Fierce Deity Sword}} can be bought from a Bargainer Statue for 160 Poes.<ref>https://www.looper.tube/?s=580&e=762&spd=1&v=Ak-6x9F-V8Q - 00:09:41 in this YouTube video</ref>
