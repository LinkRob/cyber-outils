The {{Term/Store|Item Seller|Item Sellers|Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.

==Biography==
===Hyrule===
The Item Seller in [[Kakariko Village]] runs the village's Item Shop and is married to the [[Runaway Item Seller]]. She does not approve of the [[Street Merchant]] and the wares he sells just outside her shop and advises that [[Link]] does not buy from him.<ref>{{Cite|Lend me your ear for a moment, won't you? You know the street merchant outside? He's selling hearts. Can you believe that? Hearts! I mean, you can just scoop them off the ground with a net. The nerve if that man! Might as well sell leaves off trees! Don't buy them, whatever you do.|Item Seller|ALBW}}</ref>
	
===Lorule===
The Item Seller in {{Term|ALBW|Thieves' Town|link}} runs the town's Item Shop. He shares physical characteristics with the Street Merchant.
