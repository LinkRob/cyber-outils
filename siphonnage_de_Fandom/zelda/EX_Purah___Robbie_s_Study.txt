{{Term/Store|EX Purah & Robbie's Study||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 52 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|EX Purah & Robbie's Study|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> It can only be played by purchasing the first wave of the game's {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Warriors: Age of Calamity Expansion Pass|display=Expansion Pass|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Pulse of the Ancients|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|EX Purah & Robbie's Study}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} must escort {{Term|HWAoC|Purah|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Robbie|link}} to a Targeted Position. Only {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} and a few {{Plural|HWAoC|Octorok|link}} are in the way, and when {{Term|HWAoC|Purah}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Robbie}} are close to the Targeted Position, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Blizzrobe|link}} spawn. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} then must defeat them. There are also a handful of {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin|link}} stationed on the path to the {{Plural|HWAoC|Blizzrobe}}. When the {{Plural|HWAoC|Blizzrobe}} are defeated, a gate opens to a new Targeted Position located in the center of the map, to which {{Term|HWAoC|Purah}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Robbie}} must be taken. The {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} will encounter {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Bokoblin}} on the way there as well, along with some {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Keese|link}} in the area of the Targeted Position. When they reach the Targeted Position, {{Term|HWAoC|Purah}} approaches an inactive {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, which comes to life when {{Term|HWAoC|Purah}} comes close to it. Soon, another {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} comes to life, which must be defeated in addition to the first one. When they are defeated, a gate opens again, leading to the final Targeted Position located not far east from the second position. There is a small group of {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} stationed on the path to the Targeted Position, along with a larger group located in the Targeted Position's area. {{Term|HWAoC|Robbie}} does not move towards the final position, but when {{Term|HWAoC|Purah}} reaches it, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}} spawn near the southeastern corner of the map and move towards her. Another {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}} spawns when one of the two is defeated, and another when one is defeated again. When all four of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Guardian}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} besides {{Term|HWAoC|Purah & Robbie}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of a {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian Claw|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Robbie's Curing Agent|link}}, and 500 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.
