==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Daruk's Training Journal}} is a {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} that was used by {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} to document his personal thoughts. The {{Term|BotW|Book}} can only accessed with the purchase of {{TCB}} DLC Pack. When downloaded, it will be in {{Term|BotW|Goron City|link}}, in the house behind the {{Term|BotW|Rollin' Inn|link}}. It tells of {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}'s brotherly bond with {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} as well as his inauguration as a {{Term|BotW|Champions|link}} and pilot of {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Rudania|link}}.

When asked about {{Term|BotW|Daruk}}, {{Term|BotW|Yunobo|link}} is reminded of the fact that he found the {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Training Journal|display=''Journal''}}.<ref name="Yunobo"/> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about it, {{Term|BotW|Yunobo}} reveals that he found it while he was cleaning the {{Term|BotW|Abandoned North Mine|link}}, and he is now keeping it at his home.<ref>{{Cite|[Training journal?] That's right! I found it when I was cleanin' out the {{Color|BotW Blue|Abandoned North Mine}} where we met. {{Color|BotW Blue|Lord Daruk's training journal}} is at {{Color|BotW Blue|my house}} now, goro.|Yunobo|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} refights {{Term|BotW|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Daruk}} may comment on his missing memories and suggest that he read his {{Term|BotW|Daruk's Training Journal|display=''Training Journal''}} to help, though he almost refers to it as his diary.<ref>{{Cite|Little guy! I'm guessing your memory's still scrambled, yeah? Well... Maybe it'd help if you read that thing about our memories. Ya know, the one from back in the good ol' days. What's that thing? Oh, yeah, my diary. I mean...my training journal. I bet it's still around.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> He notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s surprise, telling him to find it if he is curious.<ref>{{Cite|What? Surprised I kept a journal? If you're curious, try to find it!|Daruk|BotW}}</ref>
