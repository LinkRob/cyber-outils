{{Term/Store|Maypin||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Maypin|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Maypin}} is a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} who specializes in selling {{Plural|BotW|Insect|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I sell bugs. What? It's a living!|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> He travels from the gateway near {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau|link}} to and the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert Gateway|link}}, stopping at {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Canyon Stable|link}} along the way. {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} wields a {{Term|BotW|Traveler's Sword|link}} and a {{Term|BotW|Wooden Shield|link}}, and he is always seen with his {{Term|BotW|Donkey|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} reaches one of the endpoints of the path he takes, he will stop for a moment to investigate the road ahead. Near {{Term|BotW|Koukot Plateau}}, he states that the {{Plural|BotW|Monster|link}} ahead are too dangerous to risk.<ref>{{Cite|Careful... There's lots of monsters past this point. If they claim any more travelers, my business is sunk. After all, what's a monster need with my bugs? Nah... This is the end of the line for me.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm turning back. Too many monsters past this point for my tastes.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> At the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert Gateway}}, he claims that the {{Term|BotW|Sand|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}} is difficult for him and his {{Term|BotW|Donkey}} to traverse, so he has no choice but to turn back.<ref>{{Cite|I'm turning back. The {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}} sands are murder on my feet, and my donkey's too loaded down to ride.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> As he walks away from the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert Gateway|display=Gateway}}, he also reveals that there is too much competition for him at the {{Term|BotW|Kara Kara Bazaar|link}} for it to be worth the trip.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Kara Kara Bazaar}} just beyond the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Desert}} is an oasis packed with merchants. Too much competition for me. Plus I don't wanna drag my donkey out there. The stables are more my speed.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> Despite this, he is interested in making sales in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, but he has yet to find a pack {{Term|Series|Animal|link}} that will not get stuck in the {{Term|BotW|Sand}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'd like to travel the desert to sell my wares, but I haven't found a pack animal yet that won't get stuck in the sand.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Maypin}} is knowledgeable about {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}} that can be made with {{Plural|BotW|Insect}}, especially those that make journeying through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} easier.<ref>{{Cite|You'll need some of my bugs if you have a long trip planned. Why, you ask? Good question. They're important ingredients in the elixirs you need to survive harsh climates.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Bugs are important ingredients in the elixirs you need to survive the desert climate.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> He will inform {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} that these {{Plural|BotW|Elixir}} can be made with only {{Plural|BotW|Insect}} and {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you know you can make an elixir that keeps you warm or cool with just a few bugs and monster parts?|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Either one just takes a few bugs and some monster parts.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Got enough elixir for your trip into the desert? Need any monster parts to make more?|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s plans include a trip into the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}, {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} tells him to prepare {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Elixir|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Chilly Elixir|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Are you REALLY ready to cross the desert? If not, you should stock up on elixir. You can make some that warm you up or cool you down while taking a break right here at the stable. Try it.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You can make an elixir that warms you up or cools you down while taking a break right here at the stable. Try it.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo Canyon Stable}} is a nice place and all, but are you going further into the desert from here? Because if so, you should make some elixirs to keep you both warm and cool.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You should make some elixirs to keep you both warm and cool in the desert.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> Due to the {{Term|BotW|Heat|link}} during the day and the {{Term|BotW|Freezing Climate|link}} at night, {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} believes only {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} women would be prepared for both.<ref>{{Cite|The desert's scorching by day and freezing at night. Those Gerudo women are probably the only ones prepared for both.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The desert's scorching by day and freezing at night. You'd better be prepared for both!|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> He suggests using {{Plural|BotW|Chilly Elixir}} for traversing the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} during the day, but a {{Term|BotW|Spicy Elixir}} is the better choice for the nighttime.<ref>{{Cite|If you're planning a trip through the desert by day, you'll need an elixir to cool you off. At night, it's a different story. That's when you need some warming tonic.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> Should {{Term|BotW|Link}} only have the supplies to make one {{Term|BotW|Elixir}}, {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} states that it should be a {{Term|BotW|Chilly Elixir}}, since the {{Term|BotW|Heat}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} is worse than the cold {{Term|BotW|Temperature|link}} at night.<ref>{{Cite|The daytime heat is much worse than the chill at night. If you can only do one, I'd make some cooling elixir.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} encounters {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} while he is traveling, he will run away in fear.<ref>{{Cite|Help meee!|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Gyaah!|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} defeats the {{Term|BotW|Enemy}}, he will be relieved.<ref>{{Cite|That was close...|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Wow...|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} may thank compliment {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s fighting ability.<ref>{{Cite|Wow, look at you. Nice "moves."|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> He also has a chance to express concern for his {{Term|BotW|Donkey}} and the cargo it carries.<ref>{{Cite|Is my donkey OK? What about my cargo?|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> For saving him, {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} {{Term|BotW|Enduring Veggie Rice Balls|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|You saw me through alive. I don't know if you'd even want this, but it's yours.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref>

As {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} walks, he will advertise his {{Plural|BotW|Insect}} to passing travelers.<ref>{{Cite|Hot and cold bugs here!|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Anyone need some bugs?|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Bugs for sale! All kinds!|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I got both kinds here...|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks to see what {{Term|BotW|Maypin}} is selling, he reveals that his only wares are {{Plural|BotW|Warm Darner|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Cold Darner|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I've got some darners...and that's about it. See for yourself.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes a purchase from {{Term|BotW|Maypin}}, he will be grateful.<ref>{{Cite|Nice. Thanks.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's it. I'm out. Thanks for that.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> Like other {{Plural|BotW|Merchant}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also sell his own {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Maypin}}.<ref>{{Cite|Can't hurt to look.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What are you offering?|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you have something to sell, don't hold out on me.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref> After paying for {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Item}}, he will either thank him or ask if he needs something else.<ref>{{Cite|*nod* Thanks.|Maypin|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Did you need something else?|Maypin|BotW}}</ref>
