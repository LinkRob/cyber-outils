==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|Series|Torchlight}} are often found within [[cave]]s or [[dungeon]]s. They have a variety of uses from illuminating the surrounding area to being a key aspect in solving a puzzle or unlocking a hidden secret. Because of their nature of being lit or unlit, dungeons and other areas can be designed with the prospect of Link having to take advantage of this-lighting them to cause an event or sequence like unlocking a door or revealing a [[Treasure Chest]].

Outside of being decorative, Torches are primarily used in puzzles. They can be lit by using a fire source such as a burning [[Deku Stick]], a [[Fire Arrow]], the [[Fire Rod]], [[Din's Fire]], or the [[Lamp]]. Sometimes they can only be lit by pressing a [[switch]] or clearing a room of enemies. In dark areas, [[Link]] can light a Torch to light up the room.

Some torches known as [[Face Lamp]]s can appear as traps shooting fireballs at Link, similar to [[Medusa]]s. They are usually invulnerable to any form of attack. Additionally, In {{PH}}, some of them can be deactivated by hitting a nearby switch, or defeating all the enemies in the room.

Torches vary across the series from housing a simple group of sticks tied together as well as more elaborately designed metal ones. {{TP}} has the most variety of Torch designs in the series, with various wooden Torches, stone Torches, and other, more ancient-themed Torches.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Plural|TMC|Torchlight}} serve as simple decorative objects, sources of light, {{Plural|TMC|Switch|link}}, and {{Plural|TMC|Enemy|link}} in {{TMC}}. Some (such as four in {{Term|TMC|Deepwood Shrine|link}} and the four hidden in {{Plural|TMC|Tree|link}} in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}}) are directly connected to {{Plural|TMC|Floor Switch|link}}, which, when stepped on, illuminate the {{Plural|TMC|Torchlight}} and produce some kind of effect. Others (such as those in the {{Term|TMC|Fortress of Winds|link}}, the {{Term|TMC|Temple of Droplets|link}}, and the {{Term|TMC|Palace of Winds|link}}) shoot balls of energy at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, damaging him. These attacks can be blocked with the {{Term|TMC|Small Shield|link}}.

{{Manual|EoW|Brazier|Stand near this portable fire pit and bask in its warming glow to stave off the cold.<ref name="EoW Brazier">{{Cite|Stand near this portable fire pit and bask in its warming glow to stave off the cold.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Braziers are objects found throughout [[Hyrule]], commonly seen in caves and ruins. [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] can learn their [[Echo]] using the [[Tri Rod]], which costs two Echo energy to summon. Brazier Echoes can be used to ignite flammable objects, or to provide warmth in cold regions such as [[Hebra Mountains|Hebra Mountain]].
