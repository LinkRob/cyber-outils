==Biography==
==={{LA|-}}===
The {{Term|LADX|Ghost}} is a spirit who starts haunting {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} after the completion of the {{Term|LADX|Angler's Tunnel|link}}, wanting him to take it to its house.<ref>{{Cite|...the house... ...take me... ...the house... ...at the bay...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> As {{Term|LADX|Link}} cannot enter {{Plural|LADX|Dungeon|link}} while the {{Term|LADX|Ghost}} follows him, he must help it in order to enter {{Term|LADX|Catfish's Maw|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...N-N-No!... ...N-not there!|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> Once {{Term|LADX|Link}} brings it to the {{Term|LADX|House by the Bay|link}} north of {{Term|LADX|Toronbo Shores|link}},<ref>{{Cite|...Here!... ...enter... ...my house...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> the {{Term|LADX|Ghost}} examines it before saying it is essentially unchanged from what it was when it was alive.<ref>{{Cite|...Nostalgia... ...unchanged... ...boo hoo...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> The {{Term|LADX|Ghost}} then asks {{Term|LADX|Link}} to bring it to its grave,<ref>{{Cite|...Enough... ...cemetery... ...take me... ...my grave...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|...my grave... ...take me... ...my grave...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> which is located in the {{Term|LADX|Koholint Prairie|link}} near the {{Term|LADX|Cemetery|link}}. Once there, the {{Term|LADX|Ghost}} thanks {{Term|LADX|Link}} and tells him to look inside one of the {{Plural|LADX|Pot|link}} in its house to find a {{Term|LADX|Secret Seashell|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|...Thank you... ...a jar... ...in my home... ...look inside.. ...bye...bye...|Ghost|LADX}}</ref> The {{Term|LADX|Secret Seashell}} does not appear until after {{Term|LADX|Link}} has fulfilled the {{Term|LADX|Ghost}}'s requests.

In {{LA|DX}}, if {{Term|LADX|Link}} approaches the {{Term|LADX|Ghost}}'s grave after returning it there, the {{Term|LADX|Photographer|link}} will appear and take a {{Term|LADX|Photo|link}}.

In [[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)|''Link's Awakening (Nintendo Switch)'']], the [[Secret Seashell]] inside its house can be found at any time. Instead, the Ghost rewards Link with a [[Fairy Bottle]] after returning it to its grave.

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Main|Gustaf, Spekter, Spookter}}
The {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} is an evil spirit that haunts {{Term|TMC|Gregal|link}} at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Nobody believes me, but I saw a ghost that has taken hold of Granddad!|Flurris|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What? An evil spirit had taken hold of me? And you drove it away?|Gregal|TMC}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} can save {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} by sucking the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} up with the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}. If {{Term|TMC|Link}} does not save {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} from the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} before arriving at the {{Term|TMC|Home of the Wind Tribe}} via {{Term|TMC|Veil Falls|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} will die and both he and the {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} will disappear. This renders the {{Term|TMC|Light Arrow|link}} unobtainable, as it is a reward given to {{Term|TMC|Link}} by {{Term|TMC|Gregal}} if he is saved.

An ancient king of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Gustaf|link}}, appears as a {{Term|TMC|Ghost}} residing in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Crypt|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I hear you can fuse {{Color|TMC Blue|Kinstone pieces}} with the {{Color|TMC Green|ghost}} in the {{Color|TMC Green|Royal Crypt}}.|Hurdy-Gurdy Man|TMC}}</ref>

Additionally, there are two {{Plural|TMC|Ghost}} named {{Term|TMC|Spekter|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Spookter|link}}; {{Term|TMC|Spekter}} resides in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Valley|link}}, while his brother {{Term|TMC|Spookter}} can be found by {{Term|TMC|Anju's Henhouse|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}.
