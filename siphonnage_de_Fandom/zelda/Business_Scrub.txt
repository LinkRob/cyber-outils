{{Term/Store|Business Scrub|Business Scrubs|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D, TMC|plural}} are recurring [[Deku Scrub]]s in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 169 {{Exp Game|OoT, OoT3D, TMC}} & 244 {{Exp Game|MM, MM3D}}}}</ref>{{Note|{{Plural|Series|Business Scrub}} were referred to as '''Akindo Nuts''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 11}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Characteristics==
Business Scrubs appear all across the land including [[Dungeon|Dungeons]], wherever profit can be gained from selling [[Link]] items he may need during his journey. Often thinking that Link himself is an enemy, like normal Deku Scrubs, they fire a single [[Deku Nut]] at him to keep him away whenever he is within their range. By reflecting one of the Deku Nuts back at them, or pounding the ground with the [[Megaton Hammer]] to uproot them (which is especially effective with multiple present), they will surrender and offer Link a deal on useful merchandise, often items such as [[Deku Stick]]s, [[Arrow]]s, [[Bomb]]s, or [[Potion]]s, though they often charge more in price than local [[Shop]]s would to make profit. If the hero declines their offers or if he attempts to buy such an item but is currently full in the [[inventory]], the Business Scrubs often flee.

==={{OoT|-}}===

[[File:OoT3D Business Scrub Model.png|thumb|A Business Scrub in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D|Ocarina of Time 3D]]'']]
In {{OoT}}, Business Scrubs appear in a multitude of places. They can be found underground and in dungeons, selling items such as Bombs, Potions, Arrows, and [[Deku Nut]]s at larger prices than they cost at traditional shops. A few even offer important items such as a [[Piece of Heart]] or the ability to carry more Deku Nuts or Deku Sticks.

The [[Mirror Shield]] cannot deflect Business Scrubs' attacks, as it is too powerful. Only the [[Hylian Shield|Hylian]] and [[Deku Shield]] are able to ricochet their Deku Nuts.

Business Scrubs that sell general wares will respawn after Link purchases them, but there are Business scrubs that sell unique wares listed below that do not respawn after he purchases them.

Notably, unlike most later appearances, the Business Scrubs in this game have rather sad or worried eyes, similar to [[Deku Link]] in {{MM}}.

==={{MM|-}}===
{{Main|Business Scrub Association, Trading Quest}}
[[File:MM Business Scrub Model.png|thumb|211x211px|A Business Scrub in {{MM}}]]
There are seven Business Scrubs in {{MM}}. Two of them run the [[Deku Scrub Playground]] in [[North Clock Town]]. The other five are merchants spread across the five regions of [[Termina]]. They are members of the [[Business Scrub Association]] and are the participants in the [[Trading Quest]], of which the first part is required to complete the game. The Trading Quest consists of exchanging [[Title Deed]]s between Business Scrubs so they can fulfill their dreams of moving to different regions. Each of the five [[Deku Flower]]s vacated by one of the Scrubs can be used to reach a Piece of Heart. Unlike in {{OoT}}, they do not attack Link. 

The Scrub who owns the yellow Deku Flower in [[South Clock Town]] has already sold out his wares ahead of the[[Carnival of Time]]. He wishes to return to his home village, but not before buying a [[Moon's Tear]] as a souvenir for his wife.<ref>{{Cite|I've already sold out of my wares, and the carnival hasn't even begun. I'm thinking of closing up shop so I can buy a gift for my wife and return to her in my village!|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref> When Link gives him one, he gives the [[Town Title Deed]] in return, then leaves [[Clock Town]] with his belongings. [[Deku Link]] can then use the vacant Deku Flower to reach the platform in front of [[Clock Tower]].

If the young [[Hero of Time]] then returns to the [[Astral Observatory]] and looks through the telescope, he will see the Business Scrub from Clock Town leave the area and go into a [[Cave]] in [[Termina Field]] just outside the Observatory. If Link enters the cave and speaks to the Business Scrub, he will react in anger and explain that it is his secret storage. He will sell Link a Piece of Heart for 150 Rupees in exchange for keeping it a secret. Refusing the offer will prompt the Scrub to lower the price to 100 Rupees. If Link declines again, or cannot afford it,{{Verify|MM}} the Business Scrub loses his patience and leaves for the remainder of the three-day cycle. Even if Link accepts the offer and buys the Piece of Heart, the Scrub decides to move his storage to be safe.<ref>{{Cite|It's not that I don't believe you, but just to be safe, I'm moving my storage! You hurry up and get out, too!|Business Scrub|MM}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
Business Scrubs lay hidden beneath certain bushes that cannot be cut or destroyed, and will pop up when Link walks near them in {{OoS}}. Unlike most other incarnations of Business Scrubs, they will not attack the hero. The Business Scrubs often sell Shields, and their prices can vary from Scrub to Scrub. A few others sell Bombs and Mystical Seeds. A certain Business Scrub in [[Spool Swamp]] offers Link helpful information for his quest in order to entice him to buy a Shield.<ref>{{Cite|Let me share my wisdom! South of the floodgates is a strange vortex! It seems to be linked to another world! I offered my wisdom, so... Buy something!|Business Scrub|OoS}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Business Scrub}}
[[Link]] first encounters a Business Scrub in {{TMC}} when one blocks the route to [[Hyrule Castle]] during the [[Picori Festival]]. It hits [[Princess Zelda]] with a Deku Nut, and she asks Link to do something about it.<ref>{{Cite|Owwwwch! That must be the {{Color|TMC Green|Business Scrub}} I heard the soldiers talking about. They were saying a Deku Scrub had been hassling all the passersby. {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}, do something about it! Otherwise, we can't get to the castle. If only we had some way to defend ourselves against {{Color|TMC Blue|those nuts of his}}.|Princess Zelda|TMC}}</ref>

Once Link uses his newly acquired [[Small Shield]] to deflect the Deku Nut, the Business Scrub apologizes, stating that it had only come because it had hoped to sell its merchandise at the Picori Festival; however, as it unintentionally shoots Deku Nuts whenever it opens its mouth, its customers had been scared away without buying anything.<ref>{{Cite|I heard there was a {{Color|TMC Blue|festival}}, and I came to {{Color|TMC Green|town}} hoping to hawk my wares. Unfortunately, we scrubs have a tendency to spit nuts when we speak! Everyone got scared and ran away, and no one would buy anything! This is a terrible place to do business! I'm going back to my cave!|Business Scrub|TMC}}</ref> It returns home to its cave, clearing the path north to [[Hyrule Castle]].

Throughout his journey across [[Hyrule]], Link can fight and pacify Business Scrubs in order to buy items or gain information. In some cases this is necessary in order for him to proceed; one Business Scrub in [[Trilby Highlands]] will sell Link an [[Empty Bottle]] for 20 Rupees so that he can water [[Crenel Bean]]s and progress up [[Mount Crenel]].<ref>{{Cite|OK, ya got me! Let me make it up to ya! You can have this most deluxe of all {{Color|TMC Red|bottles}} for only 20 Rupees!|Business Scrub|TMC}}</ref> A second Business Scrub on Mount Crenel will sell Link the [[Grip Ring]] for 40 Rupees, allowing him to scale rocky walls.<ref>{{Cite|OK, ya got me! Let me make it up to ya! This fabulous Grip Ring is just perfect for a young mountaineer like yourself!|Business Scrub|TMC}}</ref>

Business Scrubs are often found behind walls that require the use of a [[Bomb]] to access, though others are found in [[Tree]]s. One Business Scrub in [[Castor Wilds]] will [[Kinstone Fusion|Fuse]] [[Kinstone Piece]] with Link after being pacified,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, do you want to fuse Kinstones with me? Well?|Business Scrub|TMC}}</ref> opening a Tree in the [[Minish Woods]] with a different Business Scrub inside. This particular Business Scrub will sell Link Kinstone Pieces (for 200 Rupees each in the Japanese and North American versions, and for 100 Rupees each in the PAL version), and also has two Kinstone Fusions available. The first Kinstone Fusion causes a crack to appear in Castor Wilds with a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a red Kinstone Piece inside. The second Fusion results in a [[Golden Octorok]] appearing in [[Western Wood]].

==={{EoW|-}}===
In {{EoW}}, many Business Scrubs populate the land of Hyrule. They have cornered the [[Smoothie]] market and will make smoothies for customers, including [[Princess Zelda]], who bring them various resources.

==Trivia==

*In the Japanese releases, Business Scrubs are known as '''Business Nut'''.
*Perhaps comically, in the French localization, these types of [[Deku Scrub]] are called '''Merchandising Nuisance'''.
*In {{MM}}, if [[Link]] were to return to the cave at [[Termina Field]] in subsequent cycle after obtaining the rather pricey [[Piece of Heart]], the Business Scrub will recognize him and immediately leaves, making it seemingly one of the very few characters in the game who retain their memories when the young hero returns to the [[First Day]] via [[Song of Time]].
