{{Term/Store|Mutoh||Series, MM, MM3D, TMC}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 245 {{Exp Game|MM}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> He is the stubborn and short-tempered leader of a group of {{Plural|Series|Carpenter|link}}. {{Term|Series|Mutoh}} is often found with his arms crossed and scoffing.

==Biography==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Mutoh is the head of the [[Clock Town]] Carnival Committee in charge of constructing the fireworks-viewing tower for the [[Carnival of Time]]. During the first days, Mutoh is in the [[Mayor's Official Residence|Mayor's Office]], arguing with [[Captain Viscen]] about whether or not to flee the town in the wake of the impending [[Moon (Object)|Moon]]. Link can use the [[Couple's Mask]] obtained from [[Anju]] and [[Kafei]] to stop the argument, earning him a [[Piece of Heart]].

Most of the time, Mutoh maintains watch over the building of the tower, while the other Carpenters are working on supplies and construction. At night of the [[Final Day]], he stands at the foot of the completed tower, after the other Carpenters have abandoned him and the town, yelling at the Moon, "If you're gonna fall, then fall already!"<ref>{{Cite|If you're gonna fall, then what's stoppin' ya, you monster?! Cowards! All of you! Not a one of you stayed! My apprentice will be disgusted with all of you! Hmph! I'll have a fine carnival without you... If you're gonna fall, then fall already!|Mutoh|MM}}</ref>

He indicates that he has some sons who have already fled the town.<ref name=sons>{{Cite|!!! The mayor's son? No good runnin' off before the ceremony! ...I wish I could get mad, but my own sons ran off in a hurry before the carnival... What a shame! I could cry!|Mutoh|MM}}</ref> He also wonders about the location of his wife (whether she has fled or gone missing is undetermined) and dislikes [[Kafei]] for going missing and compares him to his sons.<ref>{{Cite|I wonder...Did my wife flee?|Mutoh|MM}}</ref><ref name=sons/>

==={{OoA|-}}===
In {{OoA}}, Mutoh appears with his Carpenters in the [[Nuun Highlands]], where they are repairing a bridge that connects to [[Symmetry Village]]. However, all of his Carpenters have run off and are missing, and he asks Link to find them. Link must call for the aide of one of his [[Link's Partners|animal companion]]s to help locate them, as the landscape of Nuun Highlands is harsh and changes according to Link's companion.
