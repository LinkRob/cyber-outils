{{Term/Store|Duck|Ducks|Series|plural}} are recurring [[bird]]s in the {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref}}

==Characteristics==
==={{ALttP|-}}===
{{Main|Flute Boy's Bird}}
[[File:Duck.gif|left|30px|The Flute Boy's Duck]]
In {{ALttP}}, a bird resembling Duck, known as the Flute Boy's Bird, makes a vital appearance as a form of [[Warping|transportation]]. When [[Link]] plays the [[Flute]] near the [[Weathercock]] in the center of [[Kakariko Village]], the bird will appear from inside the statue. Link can play the Flute again to have the bird fly him to one of eight different locations in the [[Light World]].

==={{TP|-}}===
Ducks are found in [[Ordon Village]] and in the [[Fishing Hole]] in {{TP}}. They can either be found swimming in the pond or walking along the banks. As a [[Wolf Link|wolf]], Link can talk to them. One of the Ducks in the Fishing Hole tells him how to get the [[Fishing#Sinking Lure|Sinking Lure]].

==={{BotW|-}}===
[[Bright-Chested Duck]]s appear in {{BotW}}. These waterfowl can be found on the surface of ponds and rivers, often in flocks of three or more. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} approaches them, they will fly away.
