{{Term/Store|Poultry Curry|Poultry Curries|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Poultry Curry}}<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can make Poultry Curries by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Raw Bird Drumstick]], [[Hylian Rice]], and [[Goron Spice]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. When eaten, they will heal some of [[Link|Link's]] {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The savory meat in this common dish pairs well with the aroma of Goron Spice.<ref name="Inventory"/>

By adding other ingredients to Poultry Curry, several different variations can be made:

* '''Hearty Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Poultry Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. Eating these curries will heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Poultry Curry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. If eaten, they will refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Poultry Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be completely restored and overfilled by eating them.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. Consuming them will increase Link's movement speed.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. These curries can be eaten to grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Eating them will grant Link a boost to the power of his attacks.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. Link will gain a boost to his defense when he eats these.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} abilities.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Poultry Curry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Poultry Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>The savory meat pairs well with the aroma of spice in this common curry.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. They grant Link one level of [[Heat Resistance]] when eaten.<ref name="Chilly"/>
