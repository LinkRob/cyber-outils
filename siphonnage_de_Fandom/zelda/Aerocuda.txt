'''Aerocuda''' is an enemy in {{TotK}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Compendium|number=118|name=Aerocuda|desc=An airborne monster that can be recognized by its distinctive cries. They have strong, dexterous feet and can often be seen cruising through the sky with animals or other monsters in their clutches. Thankfully, the breed's lightweight, aerodynamic body makes it easy to shoot down.|locs=Greater Hyrule|loot=Aerocuda Wing, Aerocuda Eyeball}}
Aerocudas make their debut in {{TotK|-}}, being the second most common flying enemy in the game, after [[Keese]]. They fly towards Link and dive-bomb him, which can cause him to momentarily lose control if he is attacked while descending from the [[Sky]]. They can carry various monsters, such as [[Bokoblin]]s, in addition to various objects such as [[Bomb Barrel]]s and [[Snowball]]s to drop on Link. If they patrol a camp, they will alert the entire camp if they spot Link. One Aerocuda steals [[Tulin]]'s [[Swallow Bow]] during Link's trek to the Wind Temple, prompting the Hero of Hyrule to get it back for him.
