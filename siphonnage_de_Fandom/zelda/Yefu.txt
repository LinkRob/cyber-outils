{{Term/Store|Yefu||Series, ST}} is an {{Term|ST|Anouki|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 288}}</ref> He resides in {{Term|ST|Anouki Village|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}.

==Biography==
{{Term|ST|Yefu}} lives in a house in the southwest portion of {{Term|ST|Anouki Village}}. When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} visits the village for the first time, he is tasked by {{Term|ST|Honcho|link}}, the {{Term|ST|Anouki}} chief, to pair up all of the {{Plural|ST|Anouki}} in order to put together a village watch for all the monsters that have begun to appear after the {{Plural|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} vanished. {{Term|ST|Yefu}} comments to {{Term|ST|Link}} that his partner must be agile in the field, so he would prefer a pertner without big, flashy horns.<ref>{{Cite|My partner needs to move freely in the field, so flashy horns are a major no-no!|Yefu|ST}}</ref> As such, {{Term|ST|Link}} ends up pairing him with {{Term|ST|Honcho}}, the village chief himself.
