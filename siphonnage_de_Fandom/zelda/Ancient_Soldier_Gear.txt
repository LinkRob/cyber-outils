The {{Term/Store|Ancient Soldier Gear|Ancient Soldier Gear|Series, BotW, SSBU}} is a recurring set of {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|And on that note... Sorry for the long preamble, but I'd like to provide you with some {{Color|BotW Red|ancient soldier gear}}.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear}} was created as a result of extensive research done by {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} into the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}}. It is distinguished by glowing blue features that vanish when the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} are stored and appear when they are drawn, which possess much higher cutting and piercing power than metal blades.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Short Sword}}<br/>The blade of this sword was made using an ancient power lost to this modern age. Its blade appears only when drawn, and its cutting power surpasses metal swords.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Bladesaw}}<br/> This two-handed sword was forged using ancient Sheikah technology. Its unique rotating blades give it impressive cutting power that will slice enemies to shreds.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Ancient Spear}}<br/> This spear is the result of countless hours of research into the ancient technology used by Guardians. The glowing spearhead has high piercing potential.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> This composition leads them to deal extra damage when they are used against {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear}} is produced at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} by the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}}, which is a modified {{Term|BotW|Guidance Stone|link}} created by Robbie for this purpose.<ref>{{Cite|[Cherry?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}... that is, the ancient oven... is a machine I made to create ancient soldier gear.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> However, this function is not accessible at first due to the {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}} outside the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab}} becoming extinguished, requiring it to be lit again with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}... erm, the ancient oven... creates ancient soldier gear. But I fear she's out of sorts at the moment. Hm. I'm afraid someone will have to bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} again...|Robbie|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ah... The ancient oven... You see, it's a bit out of sorts right now, so it's impossible to make {{Color|BotW Red|ancient soldier gear}}. Hm... Yes, I suppose we'll have to carry the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} over from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} again.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> Once this is done, {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}} can be exchanged with the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven}} for {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}}, in addition to parts of the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Set|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|In addition to {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient arrows}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}} can make all kinds of ancient soldier gear. If you're looking to acquire some, give your {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient materials}}... {{Small|and rupees...}} to {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear}} is made out of non-metallic {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}. This means that that do not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, nor can they attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, but they cannot be picked up or manipulated with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
