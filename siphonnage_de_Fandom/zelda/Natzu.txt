{{Term/Store|Natzu||Series, OoS}} is a region in {{OoS}} whose landscape varies to suit the {{Term|OoS|Animal Companion|link}} {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} befriends.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 251}}</ref> It is the {{Term|OoS|Holodrum|link}} counterpart of the {{Term|OoA|Nuun Highlands|link}} in {{OoA}}.

==Features and Overview==
Unlike the {{Term|OoA|Nuun Highlands|link}}, the name of the region itself is variable:

* {{Term|OoS|Natzu Prairie|link}} appears when {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} has {{Term|OoS|Ricky's Flute|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Natzu Prairie}} has holes and cliffs that only {{Term|OoS|Ricky|link}} can jump.
* {{Term|OoS|Natzu River|link}} appears when {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} has {{Term|OoS|Dimitri's Flute|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Natzu River}} has strong currents and a waterfall that only {{Term|OoS|Dimitri|link}} can swim in.
* {{Term|OoS|Natzu Wasteland|link}} appears when {{Term|OoS|Link|link}} has {{Term|OoS|Moosh's Flute|link}}. {{Term|OoS|Natzu Wasteland}} has holes that only {{Term|OoS|Moosh|link}} can fly across.

Though the precise locations vary, Natzu always has the same three caves, which contain:
* A {{Term|OoS|Fairy Fountain|link}}
* A singing {{Term|OoS|Deku Scrub|link}} who gives Link a {{Term|OoS|Seed Satchel|link}} upgrade in exchange for the {{Term|OoS|Deku Secret|link}}
* A {{Term|OoS|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 50 {{Plural|OoS|Rupee|link}}, located at the opposite end of a large gap interspersed with moving platforms

{{Term|OoS|Link}} initially enters {{Term|OoS|Natzu}} via {{Term|OoS|Holodrum Plain|link}} to the west. {{Term|OoS|Link}} traverses {{Term|OoS|Natzu}} in order to reach {{Term|OoS|Sunken City|link}} to the east. The {{Term|OoS|Great Moblin's Keep|link}} is located in eastern {{Term|OoS|Natzu}} next to {{Term|OoS|Sunken City}}.

The region is in a perpetual {{Term|OoS|Spring|link}}. It has no {{Term|OoS|Tree Stump|link}} with which to change the {{Term|OoS|Season|link}}.

==Nomenclature==
Natzu is derived from {{Romanize|ナッツ|Nattsu}}, which is part of the Japanese name for Deku Scrubs, {{Romanize|デクナッツ|Deku Nattsu}}. This may be in reference to the lone Deku Scrub who resides in one of the caves.

Alternately, it can be a corruption of {{Romanize|夏|Natsu}}, the Japanese term for the season of summer, fitting the game's theme of seasons.
