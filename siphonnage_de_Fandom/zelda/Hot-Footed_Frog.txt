==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hot-Footed Frog}}
Hot-Footed Frogs can be located near bodies of water, swimming or hopping about on the bank.<ref name="Inventory"/> Link may crouch to sneak up and catch them, or quickly dash to snare them before they startle and disappear, otherwise if they spot him, they will quickly escape. They are primarily used in creating [[Food#Hasty Elixir|Hasty Elixir]]s, along with [[Monster Part]]s or [[Dragon]] [[Material]]s.<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] also accepts them as an ingredient for Green [[Dye]].

A Hot-Footed Frog appears in [[Recovered Memory #9: Silent Princess]], where [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] captures it to show Link,<ref>{{Cite|Is that what I think it is? Look at this! I don't believe it, but I actually caught one!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> excitedly saying that it is a delicacy known to have potent effects.<ref>{{Cite|This delicacy is known to have very, very, potent effects under the proper circumstances.|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She then states that [[Hyrule Castle]]'s research shows that eating one can enhance certain abilities, going on to add that they aren't in a controlled environment, and since Link has a high level of physical fitness, he would be perfect for the study.<ref>{{Cite|Research from the castle shows ingesting one of these can actually augment certain abilities. We wouldn't be in a controlled environment out here, and with your level of physical fitness...you'd be a perfect candidate for the study!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> She presses him to taste it,<ref>{{Cite|Go on! Taste it!|Zelda|BotW}}</ref> though it is unknown whether he actually did, as the Memory ends immediately afterward.

Ten Hot-Footed Frogs, as well as five {{Plural|Series|Ice Keese Wing|link}}, are needed to upgrade each piece of the [[Climbing Set]] to its third tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.

* Total for '''Climbing Set''': 30 (0, 0, 10, 0) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
