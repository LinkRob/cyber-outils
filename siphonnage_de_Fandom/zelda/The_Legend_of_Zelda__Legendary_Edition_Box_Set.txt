'''''The Legend of Zelda: Legendary Edition Box Set''''' is a compilation featuring all five volumes of the Legendary Editions series spanning 8 manga series by [[Akira Himekawa]] loosely based on various {{TLoZ|Series}} titles. The box set was released in English by VIZ Media and in Spanish by Panini.

==Overview==
The English version of the box set contains exclusive hardcover editions of all five Legendary Edition manga encased in a {{Term|Series|Treasure Chest|link}} holder.
