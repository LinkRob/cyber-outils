==Location and Uses==
==={{OoS|-}}===
{{Manual|OoS|Scent Seeds|Scent Seeds emit a distinct smell that attracts certain monsters and enemies. Not all enemies you meet will be affected, though.}}
In {{OoS}}, {{Plural|OoS|Scent Seed}} are {{Plural|OoS|Mystical Seed|link}} that grow on {{Plural|OoS|Scent Tree|link}} in the Spring. They emit a weird smell that attracts many enemies when cracked open. They can also be used as projectiles to damage many enemies and some bosses (this damage can also be dealt by dropping them directly on an enemy).

==={{OoA|-}}===
{{Manual|OoA|Scent Seeds|Scent Seeds emit a distinct smell that attracts certain monsters and enemies. Not all enemies you meet will be affected, though.}}
In {{OoA}}, {{Plural|OoA|Scent Seed}} are {{Plural|OoA|Mystical Seed|link}} that grow on {{Plural|OoA|Scent Tree|link}}. They emit a weird smell that attracts many enemies when cracked open. They can also be used as projectiles to damage many enemies and some bosses (this damage can also be dealt by dropping them directly on an enemy).
