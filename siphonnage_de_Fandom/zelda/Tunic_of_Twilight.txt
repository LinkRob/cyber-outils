==Location and Uses==
The '''Tunic of Twilight''' is a piece of [[Armor]] which can be obtained using the [[Amiibo (Rune)| amiibo Rune]] while scanning the ''Super Smash Bros.'' Link Amiibo '''or''' ''Twilight Princess'' Link Amiibo. Each time the figure is scanned, a chest will appear that has a small chance to contain the Tunic of Twilight. It can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] but cannot be [[Dye|dyed]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].

The Tunic of Twilight, alongside the [[Cap of Twilight]] and [[Trousers of Twilight]], comprise the [[Hero of Twilight Set]] which resembles Link's outfit from ''[[Twilight Princess]]''. When it has been enhanced by two levels, it provides the Master Sword Beam Up [[Set Bonus]] when worn with the full set.
