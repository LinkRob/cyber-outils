{{Term/Store|Phantom Ganon's Sword|Phantom Ganon's Swords|Series, HWL|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|TWW, HWL}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Stub}}
The Phantom Ganon's Sword is an [[Enemy Weapon]] in [[Ganon's Tower (The Wind Waker)|Ganon's Tower]] that [[Link]] can pick up and use after each time {{Term|TWW|Phantom Ganon|link}} is defeated. Upon entering a new room in the correct section of the {{Term|TWW|Dungeon|link}}, Link will reencounter Phantom Ganon. After being defeated by Link, Phantom Ganon will vanish, leaving his sword behind to drop on the floor. The correct way forward is designated by the direction the hilt of the sword is pointing. Once Link defeats Phantom Ganon for the final time with the {{Plural|TWW|Light Arrow|link}}, Link can pick up the sword and is able to proceed to {{Term|TWW|Ganondorf|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub}}
[[Phantom Ganon]] wields his Sword in battle in {{HW|L}} and {{HW|DE}}. After sustaining 30% damage to his health, Phantom Ganon will summon a second, identical Sword and dual-wield both. Phantom Ganon's Swords are also Gold [[Material]]s which are dropped by Phantom Ganon in {{HW|L|-}} and {{HW|DE|-}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}
