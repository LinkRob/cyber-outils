[[File:MM3D Anju Hugging Kafei.png|thumb|350px|Kafei and Anju hugging each other as they are reunited in at the end of the sidequest in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'']]

'''Reuniting [[Kafei]] and [[Anju]]''' is a task in the [[Bombers' Notebook]] in {{MM}}.{{Name Ref|MM, MM3D}} It includes various characters and spans [[First Day|all]] [[Second Day|three]] [[Final Day|days]]. In order to complete every entry in the [[Bombers' Notebook]], [[Link]] will have to go through most of the sequence twice, which means returning back to the First Day to fill in any remaining gaps in the Notebook he was unable to complete in the standard three-day-span. This is the longest [[Side Quest|sidequest]] in the game.

==Event Procedure==
===[[First Day]]===
As [[Link]] exits the [[Clock Tower]], he will see a boy in purple wearing the [[Keaton Mask]] put a letter in the mailbox and run away. After that, he returns back to the [[Laundry Pool]] to enter his hideout, where Link himself cannot enter.

On the First or [[Second Day]], Link can talk to [[Madame Aroma]] in the [[Mayor's Official Residence]] (which is open to the public from 10 am to 8 pm), who will send him to look for her missing son [[Kafei]], giving him [[Kafei's Mask]] to do so.<ref>{{Cite|The person I want you to locate is my son, Kafei. You know him, don't you?|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref>

[[File:Anjupostman.png|thumb|The Postman teases Anju while she begs him to tell her which Mailbox the Letter From Kafei was removed from, in ''Majora's Mask 3D''|254x254px|right]]
From 1:50 pm and 4:00 pm, if Link speaks with [[Anju]] she will tell him about his apparent reservation and give him the [[Room Key]]<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Link, I have you down for an afternoon arrival. Your room is our "Knife Chamber" on the second floor. Here is your key.|Anju|MM}}</ref> (in {{MM3D}}, she will only give Link the key if [[Goron Link|he is a Goron]]).

At 2 pm, the [[Postman]] will enter the [[Stock Pot Inn]]. He delivers a letter to Anju from a mysterious person. She insists that he tell her which postbox it came from, but he refuses.{{Ref Cluster|name="Anju & Postman"|<ref>{{Cite|This letter, wh-where did you?!?|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|From the postbox.|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Th-that's not what I mean! From the postbox where?!?|Anju|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|From the postbox somewhere.|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|That's not what I mean!|Anju|MM}}</ref>}} After this, the Postman leaves. From the moment the Postman leaves (2:20 pm) to 9:05 pm, if Link speaks to Anju wearing [[Kafei's Mask]], she asks the young hero to meet her in the kitchen at 11:30 pm.<ref>{{Cite|I have a request! Kafei...I have a clue...that will help you find him. Tonight at 11:30, please come to our kitchen. We'll talk then...|Anju|MM}}</ref> As Link got a reservation for a room in the second floor, he is able to stay in the inn even after it closes at 8:30pm. In the event that Link fails to receive the [[Room Key]], he can still enter the Inn through the unlocked door on the veranda.<ref>{{Cite|It seems the veranda door of the town's Stock Pot Inn has carelessly been left unlocked...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref>

If Link scheduled the meeting, Anju will exit the staff-only room at 11:30, arriving in the kitchen at 12 am. If he speaks to Anju before she leaves, she tells Link that she received a letter from Kafei himself,<ref>{{Cite|I am sorry to trouble you late at night. It's about him...Kafei. I received a letter from...him. Strange...Isn't it? Getting a letter from a missing person... But there's absolutely no mistaking it's from him!|Anju|MM}}</ref> then gives him a [[Letter to Kafei|letter addressed to Kafei]] and asks Link to put it in the postbox.<ref>{{Cite|Please! Here is my letter. Please put this in a postbox.|Anju|MM}}</ref> She then asks him to meet with [[Kafei]] in person, as she is scared to see how he had become.<ref>{{Cite|When this is delivered...When he receives my letter... You should be able to meet him! Please tell him that Anju is waiting for him. And please, after you've seen him... Tell me how he looked. I'm scared...I...I can't go.|Anju|MM}}</ref>

'''Quest-Ending Actions:'''
*If Link goes to the restroom and gives {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} the letter, he uses it as toilet paper, and the item will be lost.
*If he stops [[Sakon]] from stealing the [[Bomb Bag]] from the [[Old Lady from Bomb Shop]] in [[North Clock Town]] (or blows him up therefore killing Sakon), the thief never sells it to the [[Curiosity Shop]], halting the storyline by causing Kafei to never leave the Curiosity Shop.<ref name="Sakon appears">{{Cite|A customer came to my shop last night... Now Kafei sees him, and Kafei's color just changes and he goes runnin' after the guy. The guy's a regular... A greedy thief named Sakon. I think he's from Ikana Village...|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref>
In any of these cases, as they are not recommended to do, Link will have to restart the quest again.

===[[Second Day]]===
After Link deposits Anju's Letter into the mailbox, the Postman eventually takes it while examining every mailbox. Meanwhile, Link hangs around the [[Laundry Pool]] until 3 PM on the afternoon of the Second Day. The Postman delivers Link's letter to the young boy he saw on the First Day. Following the boy into the door at the Laundry Pool leads him to a hidden room of the [[Curiosity Shop]]. Once inside, Link speaks with the boy to learn that he is actually Kafei by removing his Keaton Mask to show his face, who himself has an uncanny resemblance to the young [[Hero of Time]].<ref>{{Cite|Anju trusted you. I shall also trust you. I am Kafei.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>

[[File:MM3D - Kafei and Link.png|thumb|Link and [[Tatl]] meeting Kafei behind the Curiosity Shop in ''Majora's Mask 3D''|242x242px|left]]
Kafei tells Link that he has been turned into a child matching his age by the [[Skull Kid]] using the [[Majora's Mask (item)|Majora's Mask]]'s powers and his ceremonial [[mask]] for the [[Carnival of Time]], the [[Sun Mask]], was stolen by [[Sakon]].<ref>{{Cite|I was turned into this by a strange imp wearing a mask... But I'm not hiding because I look this way. When I was turned into this, I went to see the Great Fairy in the shrine near the North Gate... But on the way, my precious mask--a wedding ceremony mask--was stolen from me by some prancing man with a grinning face!|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Kafei promised Anju that he would come with the mask, so he refuses to return until he has the mask back in his possession, as the two are engaged to be married.<ref>{{Cite|I know Anju is worried...but I can't go out yet. I made a promise to her that I would bring the wedding mask and greet her.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> He then gives Link the [[Pendant of Memories]] and asks him to deliver it to Anju.<ref>{{Cite|This pendant... Give it to Anju.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> If Link hurries, he will become able to give it to her on the Second Day (the [[Bunny Hood]] or [[Inverted Song of Time]] is helpful). If not, he can still wait until the [[Final Day]] to deliver it.

In any case, two optional events can be witnessed from this moment on. At 9:30pm, after entering the room he reserved in the First Day, he can hear a dialogue from Anju's room, where the aforementioned owner of the inn is arguing with her [[Anju's Mother|mother]], who suspects that Kafei fled to the [[Romani Ranch|ranch]] to join her friend [[Cremia]], but Anju refuses to believe that, and decides to patiently wait for him (either until he finally comes or until 6pm from the next day if Link hasn't given the pendant to her yet);<ref>{{Cite|She needs strength from a partner and business support from Madame Aroma. If Kafei really has run off with Cremia, she'll get both.|Anju's Mother|MM}}</ref> at 1am, Sakon goes to the Curiosity shop to sell the [[Man from Curiosity Shop|shopkeeper]] the Bomb Bag he stole the previous day (this is why Link shouldn't stop him at that moment). Kafei sees the thief from the back room, then plans to follow him.<ref name="Sakon appears"/>
===[[Final Day]]===
If Link has already given Anju the Pendant of Memories, then the hero can sit around the Laundry Pool until the afternoon. If not, he needs to deliver it first (in this case, Anju is found in the room where [[Gorman]], [[Guru-Guru]] and the two pairs of twins were), then head to the Laundry Pool.

At 1pm, the door is unlocked and Link can go back inside. Once there, he meets the Man from the Curiosity Shop; He tells Link that Kafei has left for [[Ikana Canyon]] and gives him both the Keaton Mask and [[Special Delivery to Mama|Priority Mail]] for [[Madame Aroma]].<ref name="Sakon appears"/><ref>{{Cite|Now Kafei... He says he wants you to take this to his mother.|Man from Curiosity Shop|MM}}</ref> If Link has saved the [[Old Lady from Bomb Shop|old lady of the bomb shop]] from Sakon on the night of the First Day, Kafei will still be there since Sakon wouldn't have come yet to the shop (not without something stolen to sell); therefore, Link cannot continue this sidequest, and must start the quest over from the beginning.<ref>{{Cite|From here, I check on the Curiosity Shop's customers. That guy will definitely appear. I'll be waiting when it happens.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>

[[File:Kafeisakon.png|thumb|Controlling Kafei while inside Sakon's Hideout in ''Majora's Mask''|266x266px|right]]
Link then goes to Ikana Canyon, and finds Kafei where the river leaves the area, behind a rock. Sakon's [[Sakon's Hideout|stronghold]] is here, and Kafei tells Link that it's only matter of time before the thief returns there.<ref>{{Cite|I found him, green hat boy... He's using this place as his safe house for keeping his stolen goods. Apparently, his name is Sakon. He came to the shop last night, and I followed him. His storage for the things he's stolen is on the other side of this rock door. Only Sakon can open it. The only way in is to wait for Sakon to arrive.|Kafei|MM}}</ref> At 6pm, Sakon indeed returns to his hideout and opens the door.

'''Quest-Ending Moment:''' As Sakon approaches, Link must not be seen; he must hide behind the nearby pile of rocks or use the [[Stone Mask]]. If Sakon suspects that someone is watching him enter the hideout, he flees and never comes back. The sidequest then ends at this moment. Nonetheless, Link may still take the priority mail to the [[Milk Bar]] and obtain the sixth bottle.

Once inside the hideout, Kafei accidentally sends his mask down a conveyor belt.<ref>{{Cite|Now I've done it! [...] Ohhh! The mask! He's trying to pull it out of our reach and make his escape... We've got to hurry!|Kafei|MM}}</ref> It is soon realized that the switches and the doors in the place are mixed up, meaning that at least two people are required to infiltrate in the hideout, one in each route.<ref>{{Cite|It's some setup where the door stays open only while the switch is pressed!|Kafei|MM}}</ref> For the next few minutes, the player will control both Link and Kafei as the two team up and work through Sakon's Hideout (this is arguably a first for [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]). 

*As Kafei, the player needs to solve some simple [[Block]] puzzles. The blue [[Switch|Switches]] open the doors from Link's route. If Kafei steps on a red Switch he and Link lose some time, as the conveyor belt temporarily moves faster; but if he steps on a yellow Switch he and Link gain some time because the belt temporarily moves slower. If Kafei pushes a block into one of those Switches, it will permanently be in effect until he removes it.

*As Link, the player fights off [[Deku Baba]]s and a [[Wolfos]]. The death of these enemies opens the doors from Kafei's route.

'''Quest-Ending Moment:''' If the two heroic characters take too long, the mask will fall into a hole leading to Sakon's whereabouts (where the thief is waiting for his acquisition to flee from [[Termina]] for good). If this happens, the sidequest is over.<ref>{{Cite|Yesss! My security system is impenetrable|Sakon|MM}}</ref>

At the end, Kafei gets the [[Sun's Mask]] back.<ref>{{Cite|I got the Sun's Mask back! There's still time! I must get back to town!|Kafei|MM}}</ref> Link then sees him start running back to town, and Link also needs to get back there himself, but the Eve of the Carnival of Time has already begun and therefore the player has a few real-time minutes left. It is recommended to warp to Clock Town using the [[Song of Soaring]].

It's over 12am when Link goes to town, but the anticipated reunion only occurs at 4:30am. He can, meanwhile, do one of these two things:

*'''Route 1:''' He can head to the [[Milk Bar]] and go inside; he then puts on Kafei's Mask, and speaks to Madame Aroma. She takes the Priority Mail Link received from the Curiosity shop's owner and then gives the Hero a [[Bottle]] full of [[Chateau Romani]] as a reward for his actions.<ref>{{Cite|Oh dear...Priority mail? This!!! It's from Kafei! Correct? Correct? Wonderful! You really are an expert!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref>

*'''Route 2:''' He can head to the [[Post Office]] and go inside, and show the aforementioned Priority Mail to the [[Postman]], who is deeply worried because he wants to flee, but he also wants to fulfill his scheduled activities.<ref>{{Cite|Ohhh...I want to flee! B-but, it's not written on the schedule. T-to me, the delivery schedule... It's the h-highest priority. Th-this is a priority mail seal! This is the highest of priorities. I shall deliver it!|Postman|MM}}</ref> He gets ready and takes the letter to Madame Aroma at the [[Milk Bar]], with Link following him.{{Ref Cluster|name="Postman & Madame Aroma"|<ref>{{Cite|I have a delivery for you, Postmistress!|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh my, it can't be! You're still here?|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|To...to...to...tomorrow's delivery is still scheduled!|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What are you saying?!? Did you see the sky? It's terrible!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|But, but...|Postman|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! Well!!! What shall we do?!? It's from Kafei! [...] Thank you. You flee now. That's an order!|Madame Aroma|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Understood.|Postman|MM}}</ref>}} After the Postman exits, Link can speak with him to receive the [[Postman's Hat]] as a reward for helping the postman to fulfill his last duty and enabling him to flee.<ref>{{Cite|I have decided to flee. It is an order from the Postmaster. I am now free! I can set my own schedule!|Postman|MM}}</ref>

'''Note:''' Any player who wants to accomplish a [[100% Completion|100% completion]] in the game will therefore need to guide Link through this long sidequest ''twice'' in order to get all rewards. Because the quest can be started early in the game, it can be played as soon as possible to help either Madame Aroma or the Postman, then once more after access to Ikana Canyon to help the other character ''and'' to further proceed in the task. It helps that the bar is open to the public between 6 and 9pm on the Final Day.

Finally, Link heads to the Employees Only room of the Stock Pot Inn, whose door has been unlocked by this point. Inside, Link sees Anju waiting for Kafei, but only if Link gave her the pendant. At 4:30am, the two receive Kafei, who finally comes in. The two engaged characters hug each other and then exchange masks, the Sun Mask and [[Moon's Mask]] (guarded by Anju), which magically merge to become the [[Couple's Mask]], and the couple gives it to Link for witnessing the act (and obviously for helping them reunite).<ref>{{Cite|We have exchanged our oaths and have become a couple. You are our witnesses. Please accept this mask. Please take refuge. We are fine here. We shall greet the morning... together.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>

[[File:MM3D Anju and Kafei Reunited.png|thumb|Reunited, Anju and Kafei wait for the morning together, in ''Majora's Mask 3D''|248x248px|right]]
As rewarding as it feels to reunite them, Link only gets to enjoy it for a brief moment; As the [[Moon]] is dangerously close to Clock Town at this point, about one Terminan hour after he has reunited them, it will [[Terminan Apocalypse|crash]] into Termina. So, to prevent a [[Game Over]], Link needs to play the [[Song of Time]] immediately to save his progress, or hurry to the Clock Tower if he plans to confront the Skull Kid and, if the [[Four Giants]] were freed, to complete the game's main objective by ultimately defeating Majora.

==Aftermath==
If returning to the [[First Day]] was the chosen option, there is one more action that can be performed, thanks to the availability of the Couple's Mask. At 10am of the First or [[Second Day]], Link heads to the [[Mayor's Residence|Mayor's office]] but this time he goes left. He enters the middle of a fierce meeting, and puts on the Couple's Mask and speaks with the [[Mayor Dotour|Mayor]] to end the meeting and receive a [[Piece of Heart]]. Likewise, if he got the Postman's Hat, he can view any mailbox to receive another Heart Piece.

Finally, if the Skull Kid was defeated after completion of the sidequest, Anju and Kafei's [[Anju and Kafei's Wedding|wedding]] will be shown during the [[New Day|ending credits]].
==Trivia==
*Due to its romantic story and unique gameplay during when [[Link]] and [[Kafei]] must team up inside [[Sakon's Hideout]], this sidequest is arguably the most popular among fans who played ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'' or its [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|remake]].
*If Link does not talk to [[Anju]] with the Kafei Mask during the [[First Day]], she will go outside to the [[Laundry Pool]].
*If Link forgets to give the [[Pendant of Memories]] to Anju, she will flee Clock Town with her family into [[Romani Ranch]]. If Link still helps Kafei, he will go to the [[Stock Pot Inn]] anyway and ultimately find that Anju is gone.<ref>{{Cite|...But my promise... I couldn't keep it.|Kafei|MM}}</ref>
*When Anju and Kafei are hugging each other up on completing the sidequest, [[Tatl]] can be heard saying they look just like a mother and child in spite of them being lovers, likely due to the fact that although both are technically adults, the latter's age was the same as Link by the [[Skull Kid]]'s curse.
*Interestingly, during Link and Kafei's teamwork in Sakon's Hideout, Kafei, when controlled by the player, can perform punches when pressing the [[Sword]] button. However, there is no legitimate use for it, as he himself does not face any enemies in his route, unlike Link.
**When close enough to Link's area, however, the [[Deku Baba|Deku Babas]] do attempt to attack Kafei but cannot reach him.
*This sidequest is also useful to test one of the [[Glitches in Majora's Mask|glitches]] involving the free use of the [[Fierce Deity's Mask]].
*Due to a developer oversight, Anju's expression is not seen reacting with happiness when she is reunited with Kafei. This is fixed in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]''.
*The [[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask (Himekawa)|manga adaptation of ''Majora's Mask'']] by [[Akira Himekawa]] references this sidequest as part of its story.[[File:Anju's Wedding 3.png|thumb|250x250px|The wedding of Anju and Kafei at the ending credits of ''Majora's Mask'', when debug codes are used to show the unseen angle]]
*The scene framing during the end credits' wedding sequence is done in a way that avoids directly showing Kafei. While this is most likely to avoid the need of developers to create a second character model for Kafei, some fans have joked this could imply Kafei hasn't (or won't) change back into an adult and is an attempt to make the sequence look less awkward.
**However, it is revealed that if debugging codes were used to freely move the camera in an attempt to see Kafei, he is not visible in the wedding at all. This because Kafei does not have an adult model in-game in spite of concept art suggesting this was planned.
