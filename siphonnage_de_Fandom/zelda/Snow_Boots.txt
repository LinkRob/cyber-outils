==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}} allow {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to travel more quickly on {{Term|BotW|Snow|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be worn in [[Gerudo Town]] along with the [[Gerudo Veil]] and [[Gerudo Top]]. 

To obtain them, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must wear the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} and talk to {{Term|BotW|Bozai|link}} outside {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. By accepting {{Term|BotW|The Eighth Heroine|link}} {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, he will temporarily be given the {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and here... If you'd like, you can use these. I'm sure they'll come in handy. Besides, you seem really into boots.|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has completed the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, he will return the {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}} in exchange for the {{Term|BotW|Sand Boots|link}}. Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can obtain the {{Term|BotW|Snow Boots}} permanently by talking to {{Term|BotW|Bozai}} again and completing {{Term|BotW|The Forgotten Sword|link}} {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}. Once they're acquired, [[Granté]] will begin to sell the Snow Boots in [[Tarrey Town]].

Snow Boots can be both upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} and [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
