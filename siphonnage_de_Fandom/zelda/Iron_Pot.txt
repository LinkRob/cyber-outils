The {{Term/Store|Iron Pot||Series, OoS}} is an item in {{OoS}}.<ref name="E 128"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|OoS|Link|link}} can obtain the {{Term|OoS|Iron Pot}} from {{Term|OoS|Mrs. Ruul|link}} in exchange for the {{Term|OoS|Ghastly Doll|link}}.<ref name="E 124">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|OoS}}}}</ref> {{Term|OoS|Link}} can then trade the {{Term|OoS|Iron Pot}} to the {{Term|OoS|Subrosian Chef|link}} to receive {{Term|OoS|Lava Soup|link}}.<ref name="E 128"/>
