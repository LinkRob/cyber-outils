{{Term/Store|Death Caldera||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Death Caldera|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Death Caldera is a large depression that forms part of the lava moat that surrounds [[Death Mountain]] in [[Eldin Canyon]]. The northern shore slopes further downward, leading toward the mountain range bordering [[Eldin's Flank]] and [[Gut Check Rock]]. The Caldera is fed by Death Mountain's constant eruptions and flows into [[Lake Darman]], [[Gorko Lake]], [[Darb Pond]], and continues to the [[Bridge of Eldin]].
