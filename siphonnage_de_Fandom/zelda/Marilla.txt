{{Term/Store|Marilla||Series, MM, MM3D}} '''Rosa''' is a character in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= '''''Marilla'''''|book= E |publisher= |page= 245{{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
Marilla is one part of the twin dancing group, the [[Rosa Sisters]], which itself is a part of the [[Gorman Troupe]]. Alongside her sister, [[Judo]], she dances as part of the [[Gorman Troupe]] for the annual [[Carnival of Time]]. She and Judo hail from an unspecified village in [[Termina]].<ref name="Village">{{Cite|Maybe I'll blame it on the troupe leader and go back to the village... But Judo wouldn't like it.|Marilla|MM3D}}</ref>

During the daytime, Marilla can be found pacing in the room booked by the Gorman Troupe in the [[Stock Pot Inn]]. There, she deliberates adding spins to extend the length of their performance,<ref>{{Cite|Judo's very good at it... Maybe we should add some spins...|Marilla|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If we do a poor job of extending it, the audience will get bored...|Marilla|MM3D}}</ref> whether to leave the Troupe and return home,<ref name="Village"/> and whether the song for their performance should be changed.<ref>{{Cite|We shouldn't change the song now... Guru-Guru- is scary when he gets mad...|Marilla|MM3D}}</ref> At night, Marilla can be seen practicing her dance with Judo in [[West Clock Town]].

==Trivia==
*In {{MM3D}}, if Gorman informs the Rosa Sisters of the concert's cancellation after Link teaches them Kamaro's Dance, they will still ask him to attend their performance. This may be due to a development oversight.
