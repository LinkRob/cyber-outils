==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} are sources of {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}} describes as singular places that gather huge amounts of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Energy|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|An {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} can be simply described as a singular place that gathers massive amounts of energy.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain their {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame}} using a {{Term|BotW|Torch|link}} or other flammable {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}. There are only two {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. One is located in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, on the {{Term|BotW|High Ground|link}} behind the {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You'll find the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} on the {{Color|BotW Red|high ground}} behind Reede's house.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> The second is found on top of {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Get the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from {{Color|BotW Blue|Tumlea Heights}}, put it in the {{Color|BotW Blue|furnace}}, and then {{Color|BotW Blue|Cherry}}... I mean...the ancient oven will be fixed.|Robbie|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} arrives at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Locked Mementos|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Furnace|link}} outside of it will be unlit. {{Term|BotW|Purah}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace}}, which he would then use to relight the {{Term|BotW|Furnace}}.<ref name="Purah"/> {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to be careful with the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}, as he will have to return to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} to reignite his {{Term|BotW|Torch}} if he loses it. Alternatively, he can light the {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|link}} throughout {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} to create a new {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} source closer to the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab}}.<ref>{{Cite|It'll help to light the village {{Color|BotW Blue|lanterns}} you'll see one your way back here and to {{Color|BotW Red|avoid steep inclines}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref>

During "{{Term|BotW|Robbie's Research|link}}", the {{Term|BotW|Furnace}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} will be similarly unlit. {{Term|BotW|Robbie}} wishes for someone to bring the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} from the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} at {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hm. I'm afraid someone will have to bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|ancient furnace}} again...|Robbie|BotW}}</ref> Once again, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will have to retrieve the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}}. The path between the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|display=Lab}} and the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace}} is lined not only by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}}, but {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}} as well.
