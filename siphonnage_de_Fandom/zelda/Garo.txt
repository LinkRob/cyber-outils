{{Term/Store|Garo|Garo|Series, MM, MM3D|plural}},<ref name="E 179">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 179}}</ref> also known as '''Garo Robes''',<ref name="E 179"/> are enemies in {{MM}}.

==History==
In life, the Garo were enemies of the [[Ikana Kingdom]]. Their nation sent several spies to investigate [[Ikana Canyon]] to gather intelligence, and even after they have died, they continue to haunt Ikana, ready to report to their [[Garo Master|superior]].<ref name= "Shells">{{Cite|They are merely shells that are empty on the inside. They're the shells of spies from an enemy nation sent to investigate Ikana. They have been unable to forget their living days. Even now their spirits--emptiness cloaked in darkness--continue to spy.|Pamela's Father|MM}}</ref> Their primary target in the Ikana region was the [[Ancient Castle of Ikana]].<ref name= "Mask">{{Cite|That is the mask of the leader of the ninjas who once spied on the hilltop castle with the blood-stained history. With that, you may be able to call out their spirits that even now are still wandering.|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref> Although they appear to be humans with cloaks, Pamela's Father claims Garo are empty shells, animated by willpower.<ref name= "Shells"/>

It can be inferred that they had an elaborate code of honor.<ref name= "Quote">{{Cite|R-regrettable... Although my rival, you  were spectacular. I shall take my bow by opening my heart and revealing  my wisdom... Belief or disbelief rests with you. To die without leaving a corpse... That is the way of us Garo.|Garo|MM}}</ref> Any spies captured or defeated on enemy ground were required by law to commit ritual suicide and die without leaving a corpse.<ref>{{Cite|Die I shall, leaving no corpse. That is the law of us Garo.|Garo Master|MM}}</ref> The leader of the Garo is the [[Garo Master]],<ref name= "Master">{{Cite|Master! You called! ...!!! What are you???|Garo|MM}}</ref> who Link battles in the [[Stone Tower Temple]]. Unlike his underlings, the Garo Master uses a [[Bomb]] to literally explode at his defeat. The Garo's Mask, which Link acquires in a race against the [[Gorman Brothers]], originally belonged to him.<ref name= "Mask"/>

==Characteristics==
{{Tatl
|quote1= It said that it's a {{Color|MM White|Garo}}...<br/> Just concentrate on blocking and dodging those sharp swords!
}}

The Garo use long, sharp Swords in battle that can be easily blocked with the [[Shield]]. Blocking their attacks will render them weaponless, as their hard Sword strikes will ricochet off of the Shield and out of the their grasp. During [[Link]]'s visit to Ikana Canyon, he can summon a single Garo at a time by wearing the [[Garo's Mask]] and wandering into an area where "a thirst for blood" looms.<ref name= "Mask"/><ref>{{Cite|I can't see it, but I sense there's a thirst for blood looming all around us... Don't you have a mask that can summon the unseen?|Tatl|MM}}</ref> When the Garo realizes it has mistaken Link for its master, it summons a ring of fire and attacks.<ref name= "Master"/> If defeated, Garo will "surrender their wisdom" and subsequently dissolve in green fire.<ref name= "Quote"/>

== Trivia ==
In the series [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC8LcA3grYZg0GNpxlXh8owg SMG4], a character named [https://supermarioglitchy4.fandom.com/wiki/Bob_Bobowski Bob Bobowski] formerly used a modified {{MM|-}} Garo model, but has since been redesigned to avoid copyright issues with Nintendo by making him an original character.

A theory exists that the Garo tribe come from the world the portal in the [[Stone Tower Temple]] takes you to fight the dungeon's boss, [[Twinmold]]. Some even suggest the portal goes to [[Hyrule]], based on [[Triforce]] imagery on some textures in or around [[Stone Tower]].
