{{Compendium/Extract|Lizal Boomerang}}
{{Plural|BotW|Lizal Boomerang}} are {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} favored by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos|link}} that can be used for either melee or thrown attacks.<ref name="Inventory"/> They belong to the {{Term|BotW|Lizalfos Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} created by {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> Though they are most commonly used by regular {{Plural|BotW|Lizalfos}}, {{Term|BotW|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ice-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Electric Lizalfos|link}} can also use these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} early on. When thrown, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must catch the {{Term|BotW|Boomerang|link}} before it returns to him, or else he will take damage. They can be found around {{Term|BotW|Lake Hylia|link}} and in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Wetlands|link}}.

As metal {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Plural|BotW|Lizal Boomerang}} will spark and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, and they are capable of conducting {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They will sink when dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, however, they can also be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}. Finally, they will not burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}.

=== ''Tears of the Kingdom'' ===
Lizal Boomerangs reappear in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]'', having the same properties as in ''Breath of the Wild'', but can be [[Fuse|Fused]] with various Materials to give it more power.
