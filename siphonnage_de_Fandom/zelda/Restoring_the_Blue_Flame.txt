{{Term/Store|Restoring the Blue Flame||Series, BotW}} is a {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= BotW|publisher= Piggyback|page= 340}}</ref>

==Location and Rules==
{{Term|BotW|Restoring the Blue Flame}} is operated by {{Term|BotW|Aya|link}}, a worker at {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}}. She is clearly saddened,<ref>{{Cite|*sigh...*|Aya|BotW}}</ref> but when {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} asks her why, she reveals that the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}} is the reason.<ref>{{Cite|[Is something wrong?] {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|The}} {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}}{{Color|BotW Gray|...}}}}|Aya|BotW}}</ref> With further prodding, she reveals that she her life has been dull as of late, and she used to pass her time by looking at the nearby {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Hmm?] Oh, sorry! Guess I was daydreaming. Life has been so dull since I married into this family. I used to pass the time by staring at the {{Color|BotW Blue|lantern}}'s {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}}...|Aya|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, the director of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Robbie|link}}, has not lit the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} in quite some time.<ref>{{Cite|But the {{Color|BotW Blue|director}} at the {{Color|BotW Blue|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} hasn't lit the fire in so long.|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can offer to light the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} for {{Term|BotW|Aya}}, which she realizes is a possible solution for her problem.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll light it for you.] You? Hmm... That's one possibility, I guess.|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She challenges {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring back a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} to light the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} in under three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|How fast can you do it? Do you think you can bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} to this {{Color|BotW Blue|lantern}} in under {{Color|BotW Red|three minutes}}?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees to this, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will then request 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} as a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}-handling fee.<ref name="Aya"/> At {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s confusion, she explains that she can not see how he could achieve such a time without riding a {{Term|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Horse-handling fee?!] Well, you're going to use a horse, right? I don't see any other way to do it that fast. So yeah, {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}.|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} pays, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} says that he will not regret it.<ref>{{Cite|You won't regret it!|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She reminds him that his goal is to return in under three minutes.<ref>{{Cite|Remember—{{Color|BotW Red|three minutes}} or less!|Aya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Aya}} begins her countdown,<ref>{{Cite|Alrighty... On your mark...get set... {{Big|Three... Two... One... Go!}}|Aya|BotW}}</ref> only to reveal that this is what she will say before {{Term|BotW|Link}} starts.<ref>{{Cite|See, I'll say that, and then you'll start.|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

During the {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must reach the nearest {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} source and return to the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} at {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} within three minutes. If no preparation has been done ahead of time, the nearest location to get a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} is the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Furnace|link}} on top of {{Term|BotW|Tumlea Heights|link}}. However, the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} just north of {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable}} is much closer, and it will remain lit throughout the duration of the game. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can retrieve a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} from this {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestal}} much quicker.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} relights the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}}, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will be elated that the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} has returned.<ref>{{Cite|Unbelievable! It's back! The {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} is really back!|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She realizes that she should give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a reward, and so she gives him a {{Term|BotW|Purple Rupee|link}}.<ref name="Thank">{{Cite|I really ought to do something to thank you, huh?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She says goodbye to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, telling him she will see him later.<ref>{{Cite|I'll see you later! Bye!|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

To continue playing {{Term|BotW|Restoring the Blue Flame}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must extinguish the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} within the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal|display=Pedestal}}. This can be done quickly by waving a {{Term|BotW|Korok Leaf|link}} or shooting it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}}. However, doing this will irritate {{Term|BotW|Aya}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|I told you not to put it out...}}}}|Aya|BotW}}</ref>

Since the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} went out, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he could relight it a second time.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} went out... Can you light it again?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> Now, she asks if he can complete this challenge in under one minute.<ref>{{Cite|How fast can you do it? Do you think you can bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} to this {{Color|BotW Blue|lantern}} in under {{Color|BotW Red|one minute}}?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, she will take another 20 {{Term|BotW|Rupee}} {{Term|BotW|Horse}}-handling fee.<ref name="Aya"/> Before he leaves, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} once again reminds him that he must complete the task in one minute or less.<ref>{{Cite|Rmemeber—{{Color|BotW Red|one minute}} or less!|Aya|BotW}}</ref> This time, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} gifts {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref name="Thank"/>

The next time the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame Pedestal}} is extinguished, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to bring a {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame}} back in under 20 seconds.<ref>{{Cite|How fast can you do it? Do you think you can bring the {{Color|BotW Blue|blue flame}} to this {{Color|BotW Blue|lantern}} in under {{Color|BotW Red|20 seconds}}?|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She reminds him of this time again before he sets out.<ref>{{Cite|Remember—{{Color|BotW Red|20 seconds}} or less!|Aya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s reward for completing this challenge is a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref name="Thank"/>

After accomplishing this, {{Term|BotW|Aya}}'s reward stays at 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}}, which she will warn {{Term|BotW|Link}} of if he tries again.<ref>{{Cite|You can try anyway, but the reward is still the same {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}}.|Aya|BotW}}</ref> She is appreciative if {{Term|BotW|Link}} still decides to help her.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you!|Aya|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} completes this task, {{Term|BotW|Aya}} gives him a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}},<ref name="Thank"/> which covers the cost of the fee.
