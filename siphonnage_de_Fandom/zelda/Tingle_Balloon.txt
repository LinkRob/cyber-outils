The {{Term/Store|Tingle Balloon|Tingle Balloons|Series}} is a balloon used by [[Tingle]] to float in the air.{{Name Ref|MM, OoA, FSA, FPTRR, HW, HWL}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{MM|-}}===
In {{MM}}, Tingle uses the balloon to float above the land and draw maps. If [[Link]] pops this balloon with his [[bow]], or any other ranged weapon, Tingle will fall to the ground and offer to sell him maps.

==={{OoA|-}}===
If Link strikes Tingle's balloon while using the [[Roc's Feather]] in {{OoA}}, he will fall and subsequently offer to be the young hero's friend. If Link accepts, Tingle will give the young hero an [[Sea Chart#Related Charts|Island Chart]].

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Tingle can be seen using this balloon to attempt to collect [[Force Gems]].
