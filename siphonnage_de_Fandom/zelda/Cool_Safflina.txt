==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Cool Safflina}}
{{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina}} can be found growing in the snow of the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Mountains|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They also sprout near the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina}} into {{Plural|BotW|Food|link}} {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} will result in a meal that grants {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}} when eaten.<ref name="Inventory"></ref> At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina}} can be used to create {{Term|BotW|Light Blue Dye|link}}.
