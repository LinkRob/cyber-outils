{{Plural|BotW|Windcleaver}} are the {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} of choice among high-ranking members of the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> Proficient fighters are able to use this {{Term|BotW|Weapon}} to cleave the very {{Term|BotW|Wind|link}}, creating a vacuum.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Blademaster|link}}, who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}} and throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. These swords belong to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} created by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 171}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Windcleaver}} have a unique property amongst melee {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} where swinging them causes a short range projectile to be emitted forwards from the blade. This projectile can be used a few times before the blade must recharge. Similar to the {{Term|BotW|Eightfold Longblade|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver}} also has a unique charged attack. Rather than spinning around with the {{Term|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} will draw the {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver}} back and then slash forwards at considerable speed, knocking large {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} over and sending smaller ones flying. This charged attack also releases a projectile which increases in size, duration and damage depending on how long the charge is held, dealing a minimum of 10 damage and a maximum of 40. The {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver}} also possesses a minor hidden property which causes it to fly in a straight line for a short time when it is thrown with the [[R Button]]. Even if the weapon does not strike something directly, it will lose [[Durability]] from releasing the projectile.

As part of "{{Term|BotW|The Weapon Connoisseur|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Nebb|link}} will ask {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to show him a {{Term|BotW|Windcleaver}}, which he has heard is not common around {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|windcleaver}}? Grandpa said you can't find those around here. If you get ahold of one, let me see it!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} brings him one, {{Term|BotW|Nebb}} will be excited and give him a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Isn't that the {{Color|BotW Blue|windcleaver}}? You remembered our promise! Thanks! Let me see!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Thanks! Here's a token of my thanks!|Nebb|BotW}}</ref>

Being made of metal, {{Plural|BotW|Windcleaver}} will conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} and attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}. They also are unable to be burned by {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, but they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. If they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they will sink.
