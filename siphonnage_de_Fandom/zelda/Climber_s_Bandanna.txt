==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Climber's Bandanna is a piece of [[Armor]] located in the [[Ree Dahee Shrine]] at [[Dueling Peaks]]. After obtaining it, [[Granté]] will begin selling the piece of Armor for 4,000 [[Rupee]]s. According to its description, it is infused with ancient technology. When worn, it provides one level of Climb Speed Up. The Climber's Bandanna can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Climber's Bandanna, alongside the [[Climbing Gear]] and [[Climbing Boots]], compose the [[Climbing Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides a Climbing Jump Stamina Up [[Set Bonus]].
