{{Term/Store|Pico Pond||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Pico Pond'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Pico Pond is a small body of water that sits beside the [[Woodland Stable]] in [[Eldin Canyon]]. It is notably the location of the [[Mirro Shaz Shrine]], which sits at its north shore.

An isle in the center of the Pond holds a block formation with a missing piece. Pushing its missing piece into the formation using [[Magnesis]] will cause a [[Korok]] to appear, who will then give Link a [[Korok Seed]].
