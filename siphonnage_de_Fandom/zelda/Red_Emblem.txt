==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|MM3D|Red Emblem}} are oval-shaped, red stones with golden designs around them. After {{Term|MM3D|Link|link}} defeats the {{Term|MM3D|Garo Master|link}} in {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|link}}, they reveal the secret of the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|display=Temple}}.<ref name="Heavens"/> As the {{Term|MM3D|Garo Master}} explains, {{Term|MM3D|Link}} must shoot a {{Term|MM3D|Light Arrow|link}} into the {{Term|MM3D|Red Emblem}} outside of the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple|display=Temple}} in the surrounding {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower|link}} to flip the landscape upside-down.

This change in landscape allows {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to explore the rest of the {{Term|MM3D|Dungeon|link}}. Two other {{Plural|MM3D|Red Emblem}} can be found inside the {{Term|MM3D|Stone Tower Temple}}, which rotate the rooms {{Term|MM3D|Link}} is standing in. {{Plural|MM3D|Red Emblem}} can also be found engraved on {{Plural|MM3D|Death Armos|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Death Armos, a temple guard... Look at that red mark on its chest! I know you've seen something like that before...|Tatl|MM}}</ref> Shooting the {{Plural|MM3D|Red Emblem}} on {{Plural|MM3D|Death Armos}} will cause them to flip, allowing {{Term|MM3D|Link}} to defeat them by tricking them into striking themselves into the ground.

==Trivia==
*In all versions of {{MM}} prior to {{MM3D}}, the {{Term|MM|Garo Master|link}} refers to the {{Term|MM|Red Emblem}} as being "blood-stained", which is a reflection on the violent history of the {{Term|MM|Ikana Kingdom|link}} and the themes of {{Term|MM|Stone Tower|link}}. This reference was removed in {{MM3D|-}}.<ref name="Heavens"/>
