{{Term/Store|Wraith|Wraiths|Series, CoH|plural}} are enemies in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Defeated By: Wraith|Game Over|CoH}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|CoH|Wraith}} are enemies that only appear in dungeons, spawning within two to eight beats when {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Link|link}}, {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} or {{Term|CoH|Princess Zelda|link}} has accidentally stepped on a {{Term|CoH|Wraith}} {{Term|CoH|Switch|link}}. Once summoned, they move on every beat to attack the heroes until slain.
