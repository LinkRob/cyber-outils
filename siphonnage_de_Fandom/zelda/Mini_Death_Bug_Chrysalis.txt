The '''Mini Death Bug Chrysalis''' is a dungeon item found in {{FPTRR}}. 

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|FPTRR|Mini Death Bug Chrysalis}} is found in the {{Term|FPTRR|Insect Cavern|link}} {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon|link}}. To complete the {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} must give the item to {{Term|FPTRR|Teddy Todo|link}}, who is entangled in some spider webs. After defeating the {{Term|FPTRR|Dungeon}}'s boss, {{Term|FPTRR|Ultra Death Bug|link}}, Todo consumes the Chrysalis, which greatly increases his agility, speed, and endurance. With his new-found power, he declares himself a Drifter {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}}, and joins the ranks of {{Term|FPTRR|Yamori|link}} and {{Term|FPTRR|Ronny|link}}.
