{{Term/Store|Yellow Picolyte|Yellow Picolyte|Series, TMC|plural}} are items in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 143}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Term|TMC|Yellow Picolyte}} is made from {{Plural|TMC|Pico Bloom|link}} by a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} in the {{Term|TMC|Minish Village|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're doing cutting-edge research on Pico Blooms here.|Minish|TMC}}</ref> In order to grow {{Plural|TMC|Yellow Pico Bloom|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} is asked to bring {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Milk|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I need {{Color|TMC Red|milk}} to continue my improvements on this Pico Bloom.|Minish|TMC}}</ref>

In {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}, if {{Term|TMC|Link}} cleans the dust on the carpet by using the {{Term|TMC|Gust Jar|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Beedle|link}} will eventually start selling {{Term|TMC|Picolyte|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Yellow Picolyte}} becomes available once the {{Term|TMC|Minish}} grows a {{Term|TMC|Yellow Pico Bloom}}.

Once bought, the {{Term|TMC|Yellow Picolyte}} is stored in an {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|link}}; however, only one {{Term|TMC|Empty Bottle|display=Bottle}} of it can be carried at a time.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, I'm sorry! You already have some of that kind of Picolyte!|Beedle|TMC}}</ref> When consumed, {{Term|TMC|Yellow Picolyte}} increases the rate of finding {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You got some {{Color|TMC Red|Yellow Picolyte}}! If you use it, you might find lots of Rupees!|N/A|TMC}}</ref> The effects of {{Term|TMC|Yellow Picolyte}} wear off after approximately 30 seconds.
