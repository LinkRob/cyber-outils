{{Term/Store|Old Man||Series, OoS}} is a character in {{OoS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= A&A |publisher= |page= 209}}</ref>

==Biography==
Old Man is an elderly man who can be found in the southern part of [[North Horon]], at the west side of [[Eyeglass Lake]]. He is busily trying to retrieve [[Mittens]], his pet [[Cat]], from a tree by using a [[Megaphone]] to call her down, but has no success. He laments that he wishes he had a [[Fish]] to lure her down with.<ref>{{Cite|Look! No matter how much I call her, my precious Mittens won't come down! If only I had her favorite treat--Fish...|Old Man|OoS}}</ref>

Old Man plays a role in the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}}. [[Link]] may give him the Fish to lure the Cat down from the tree. He then gives him the Megaphone in exchange for helping him. His Megaphone can later be used to wake [[Talon]] on [[Mt. Cucco]].
