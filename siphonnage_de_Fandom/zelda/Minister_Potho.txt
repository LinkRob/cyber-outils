{{Term/Store|Minister Potho||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 270}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Figurine|TMC|Minister Potho}}
{{Term|TMC|Minister Potho}} is the minister to the {{Term|TMC|Kingdom of Hyrule|link}}. He is a short man with white hair only on the sides with a bald top, a large white mustache, and large glasses. He functions as the caretaker and tutor of {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda|link}} by order of her father, {{Term|TMC|King Daltus|link}}. In his capacity as minister, {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho|display=Potho}} has great access to the {{Term|TMC|Royal Family of Hyrule|link}} and resides with them at {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|link}}, serving largely as the king's major-domo.

When the story begins, {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} sneaks out of {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}} without {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho}}'s knowledge or consent in order to find her childhood friend,<ref>{{Cite|Goodness me! Princess Zelda! Did you sneak out of the castle and come all this way alone? The minister is sure to be worried about you! You know how he gets!|Smith|TMC|}}</ref> {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, and attend the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}} with him. Upon their return to {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle}} soon after, {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho}} is shaken to see that {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} has been outside the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} without protection, though he brushes it aside and accepts {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh! {{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! You brought the sword, did you? Then, as minister to the kingdom of Hyrule, I, {{Color|TMC Green|Potho}}, accept this blade. You came just in time. The award ceremony will begin soon.|Minister Potho|TMC}}</ref> crafted by {{Term|TMC|Smith|link}} and intended for the winner of the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|link}} of the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival}}. When the winner, {{Term|TMC|Vaati|link}}, prepares to open the {{Term|TMC|Bound Chest|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho}} is among the many that are knocked aside before {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} is turned to stone by his {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse|link}}.

When {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} decides to send {{Term|TMC|Link}} on a quest to restore the shattered {{Term|TMC|Picori Blade|link}} in order to break {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}'s {{Term|TMC|Sorcerer's Curse|display=Curse}} and save {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}}, {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho}} gives {{Term|TMC|Link}} {{Term|TMC|Smith's Sword}}, which was meant to be given to {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} as the winner of the {{Term|TMC|Sword-Fighting Tournament|display=Tournament}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Picori should know how to create a new sacred sword.|King Daltus|TMC}}</ref>

When {{Term|TMC|Link}} returns from {{Term|TMC|Mount Crenel|link}} with the {{Term|TMC|Earth Element|link|display=Earth}} and {{Term|TMC|Fire Element|link|display=Fire}} {{Term|TMC|Four Elements|link|display=Elements}}, {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho}} tells him a story his grandmother told him about the location of the {{Term|TMC|Minish Door|link}}.<ref name="Sanctuary"/>

At some point after {{Term|TMC|King Daltus}} is impersonated by {{Term|TMC|Vaati}}, {{Term|TMC|Minister Potho}} is turned to stone along with the other residents of the {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}} while in the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard|link}}. After {{Term|TMC|Link}} restores him with the power of the {{Term|TMC|Four Sword|link}}, he tells {{Term|TMC|Link}} that {{Term|TMC|Vaati}} has taken {{Term|TMC|Princess Zelda}} to the roof.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TMC Green|Link}}! We need your help! {{Color|TMC Green|Vaati}} took our petrified princess to the roof of the castle... He must be planning something fiendish. You must stop him!|Minister Potho|TMC}}</ref> He leaves the {{Term|TMC|Palace Courtyard}} with the other {{Plural|TMC|Vassal|link}} before {{Term|TMC|Link}} fights {{Term|TMC|Vaati's Wrath|link}}, and does not reappear for the remainder of the game.

He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure, why not?|Minister Potho|TMC}}</ref>
