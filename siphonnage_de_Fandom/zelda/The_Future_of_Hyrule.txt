{{Term/Store|The Future of Hyrule||Series, HWAoC}} is the final {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|All Hyrule United|Chapters|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
The scenario starts with {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}} as well as two character of choice at the gate of {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Astor|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Harbinger Ganon|link}} arrives at Hyrule Castle and two Stalmoblins and a Stalnox are to be defeated initially inside the Great Hall.

After the three foes are defeated, Astor will summon the four Blight Ganons, in which the objective then change to defeating the four Blight Ganons. {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon|link}} is located in the Dining Hall, {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon|link}} is located in the Guard's Chamber, while {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}} are located on the surface of Hyrule Castle.

After the four Blight Ganons are defeated, {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} and Harbinger Ganon will appear at the first and second gatehouses. When their health bar drops to zero, they will not die, instead they will withdraw, meeting upstairs at a location close to a flight of stairs and near the Sanctum.

There, {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} and Harbinger Ganon will merge into {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}} and control {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako|link}}. The first player ({{Term|HWAoC|Link}}) will fight {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} and when the latter's health bar drops to zero, a cutscene about Zelda and {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} will play.

The final destination arrives at the Sanctum, where the squad will finally fight {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}} head-on. In the first phase, the player cannot hurt Ganon. The broken {{Term|HWAoC|Terrako}} will intervene when Zelda has difficulty sealing Ganon, and will weaken Ganon's strength, allowing the player to deal damage to Ganon.

When Ganon's health drops to half, he will attempt to break the floor and the scene switches to the Observatory. Ganon will be more powerful, having movesets belonging to the 4 Blight Ganons, but he will also have weaknesses to all 4 of the {{Term|HWAoC|Sheikah Slate|link}} runes at one point of his attacks.

The battle ends when Ganon's health and weak-point gauge are drained, in which then the character the player is currently on will perform a Weak-point smash. {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} will slash Ganon's body open and Zelda will seal Ganon's corpse.

A cutscene will play in which the remnants of Terrako, a [[Blessed Screw]], sends {{Term|HWAoC|Sidon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Teba|link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Riju|link}} back to their original timeline. The credit plays and all is well.
