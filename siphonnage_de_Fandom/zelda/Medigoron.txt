{{Term/Store|Medigoron|Medigorons|Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238,{{Exp Game|OoT}} 244 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
[[File:Medigoronsbladeshop.jpg|left|thumb|218x218px|Medigoron inside his shop in ''Ocarina of Time'']]
Medigoron is a large [[Goron]] who owns a store in [[Goron City]] and is also the younger brother of [[Biggoron]] - who is even larger - in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite|My Brotherrrr... Opened a new storrrre... It's Medigoron's Blade Storrrrrrrre... Howeverrrrr... I am betterrrrrr at making bladessssss.|Biggoron|OoT}}</ref> He sells [[Link]] the [[Giant's Knife]] for 200 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|How about buying this knife for 200 Rupees?|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> The large [[Sword]] closely resembling the [[Biggoron's Sword]], despite having taken Medigoron from five to six years to make and therefore can only be purchased when the main protagonist becomes the [[Hero of Time]],<ref>{{Cite|I'm working on something really cool right now! But I think it's going to take a while... If you can wait five or six years, it should be ready. OK?|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> breaks after using it just eight times or immediately after striking a hard object.<ref>{{Cite|It looks like there is a problem with its durability... But still...|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> Though Link must use two hands to wield the Giant's Knife, Medigoron considers it to be a small weapon.<ref>{{Cite|I just completed a small weapon. How about it...|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> The terribly slow rate and fragility of his construction of the blade clearly indicates Medigoron has very poor skills as a blacksmith, unlike Biggoron.

[[Medigoron's Blade Store]] is a tight fit for his enormous size,<ref>{{Cite|Tight fit...|Medigoron|OoT}}</ref> a side effect of his lack of foresight.<ref>{{Cite|They say that Medigoron didn't really think about his own size, so his store is really cramped.|Gossip Stone|OoT}}</ref> In the game's ending [[credits]] following the defeat of [[Ganon]], he comes down to [[Lon Lon Ranch]] for the celebration and watches the [[Cucco Lady]] juggle several [[Cucco]]s.

==={{MM|-}}===
[[File:Medigoron Keg MM.jpg|thumb|226x226px|Medigoron giving Link a Powder Keg as a test for Link in ''Majora's Mask'']]
Medigoron in {{MM}} appears inside the [[Goron Powder Keg Shop]] at [[Goron Village]], which is frozen over in the winter, so Link must shoot a [[Fire Arrow]] onto the ice or wait until spring to enter it. He must first pass a certification test before he can purchase a [[Powder Keg]] (he must be as [[Goron Link]] in order to do so). Medigoron will give the hero a lit Powder Keg with a very long fuse, and he must use it to destroy the boulder blocking the entrance to the [[Goron Racetrack]] before the powerful bomb explodes. Once this is successful, Link will be given a free Powder Keg, allowing him to buy and carry one at a time. Subsequent purchases require 100 Rupees (a Goron at [[Clock Town]]'s [[Bomb Shop]] sells for only 50 Rupees, however), but in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask 3D|Majora's Mask 3D]]'' it is reduced to just 20. He once again is the brother of Biggoron.

==Nomenclature==
Medigoron is a portmanteau of ''medium'' and ''Goron'', referencing his medium size compared to Biggoron and other normal-sized Gorons.

== Trivia ==

* In Japan, he is known as '''Mediumgoron'''.
* Both of Medigoron's shops in the [[Nintendo 64]] games share the same layout.[[File:OoT Giant's Knife Render.png|thumb|The Giant's Knife, Medigoron's creation]]
* Considering Medigoron's time it takes build the [[Giant's Knife]] in ''Ocarina of Time'' - 5 or 6 years, most real-life swords during the [[wikipedia:Middle_Ages|Middle Ages]] take about 2 weeks on average to make, while it's only 5 hours to a day, today.
* If [[Link]] was to ignore obtaining the Giant's Knife and instead gets the [[Biggoron's Sword]], and then speak to Medigoron, he will talk to him as if the hero has a non-broken Giant's Knife when it is in fact the latter [[Sword]]. This is likely due to both weapons sharing the same model.
* Unlike [[Biggoron]], Medigoron is never shown standing up.
