{{Term/Store|Trading Post|Trading Posts|Series, MM, MM3D, ST|plural}} are recurring locations in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Features and Overview==
==={{MM|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]'', the {{Term|MM3D|Trading Post}} is located in {{Term|MM3D|West Clock Town|link}} and is open almost 24 hours a day, with the exception of 9 PM to 10 PM.<ref>{{Cite|On break. Back in 1 hour.|N/A|MM}}</ref> During the day, it is operated by the {{Term|MM3D|Man from Trading Post|link}}. At night and on the {{Term|MM3D|Final Day|link}}, it is staffed by the {{Term|MM3D|Part-Timer|link}}. The shop offers various restock items, such as {{Plural|MM3D|Arrow|link}} and {{Plural|MM3D|Deku Nut|link}}, and it is one of the few places where [[Link]] can buy a {{Term|MM3D|Hero's Shield|link}} if he loses his prior one to a {{Term|MM3D|Like Like|link}}. The shop is also one of the two places where {{Term|MM3D|Link}} can encounter {{Term|MM3D|Pierre|link}} the {{Term|MM3D|Scarecrow|link}}.

Link's [[transformation]] [[Mask|Masks]] have different reactions by the Man from Trading Post and Part-Timer. However, if the hero attempts to buy items as his [[Deku Link|Deku form]], they will utterly refuse to let him purchase anything in the shop, as they see Link as a young child, like the [[Soldier|Soldiers]].

==={{ST|-}}===
In ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks|Spirit Tracks]]'', the {{Term|ST|Trading Post}} is situated in the southeast corner of the {{Term|ST|Forest Realm|link}}, serving as the home to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}} and the resting place of his grandfather, {{Term|ST|Linebeck|link}}. {{Term|ST|Link|link}} is forced to make a stop at the {{Term|ST|Trading Post}} after he sees that the bridge leading to the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm|link}} is broken. Here, he meets {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}, owner of the {{Term|ST|Linebeck Trading Company|link}} and the only resident of the whole station. {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}} explains that the reason for the bridge being broken is due to the storms that have ravaged the area, and while he was meaning to call {{Term|ST|Kenzo|link}}, a bridge worker, he is more worried about the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} disappearing since it is affecting his business.<ref>{{Cite|But I agree that the bridge is a sad mess. The huge storms we had really did a number on it. I was going to call a {{Color|ST Light Blue|bridge worker}} I know in the {{Color|ST Light Red|Snow Realm}} to fix the mess. But with the Spirit Tracks vanishing, I've got bigger fish to fry. See, I deal with merchants all over the world, and I can't get anywhere now!|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref> He then realizes that {{Term|ST|Link}} is a {{Term|ST|Royal Engineer|link}} and asks him to bring {{Term|ST|Kenzo}} so that they may both continue with their own business.<ref>{{Cite|Wait... You're an engineer, aren't you? Well, that's just perfect! Go fetch that bridge worker so we can both get on with our business.|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref> After the young hero brings {{Term|ST|Kenzo}} from the {{Term|ST|Snow Realm|link}}, he will offer to fix it for 5,000 {{Plural|ST|Rupee|link}}, which includes the fee for the work he did to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}'s house.<ref>{{Cite|Course, course. Just leave it to me! Shouldn't be a problem. Assumin', of course, that you've got my repair fee on hand. Ya do, right? Ya also still owe me for the work I did on your house. So along with the bridge... Hmm, I'd say 5,000 Rupees oughta cover the bill nicely!|Kenzo|ST}}</ref> The trading company owner refuses to pay the bill, so the burden of paying the repair fee befalls {{Term|ST|Link}}, whom {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}} is quick to blame it on.<ref>{{Cite|Lucky for both of us, this young fellow has kindly agreed to foot the bill. He's the one who wanted the bridge fixed in the first place, after all. All I did was send him your way, friend!|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref> {{Term|ST|Kenzo}} then mentions that he does not care who pays him as long as the money is ready by the time the bridge is done.<ref>{{Cite|Eh, I don't really care who pays me, so long as I get my 5,000 Rupees. I'll go fix that bridge, but that money better be ready for me when I'm done!|Kenzo|ST}}</ref>

When {{Term|ST|Link}} goes to talk to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}, he mentions that his grandfather, {{Term|ST|Linebeck}}, left him a note on how to find a high-valued {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}}, known as the {{Term|ST|Regal Ring|link}}, that he hid somewhere near the {{Term|ST|Trading Post}}. {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}} hasn't been able to figure out the riddle, so he hands over the note to {{Term|ST|Link}} in hopes that he may find the 8,000-{{Term|ST|Rupee}} {{Term|ST|Treasure}} and thus pay off the bridge fee.<ref>{{Cite|Excellent! Now listen closely, as I only want to say this once. I've heard whispers about some high-value loot that was hidden here long ago. What loot, you ask? A gem of a treasure known as the Regal Ring! From what I can gather, the thing's got to be worth a cool 8,000 Rupees, easy! My grandfather left a letter detailing the ring's location shortly before his passing. I've read the clues he gave and looked all over, but I haven't hit pay dirt... This is where you come in. What say you find the ring for me and bring it back? Do that, and we'll generate more than enough Rupees to pay off your debt!|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref>

To the left of {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}'s house is a small cave that is infested with {{Plural|ST|Like Like|link}}. After passing through the cave, the young hero will find himself in the back portion of the {{Term|ST|Trading Post}}, which is where the grave of {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}'s grandfather is located. Past the graveyard is an {{Term|ST|Air Stone|link}} where {{Term|ST|Link}} can learn the "{{Term|ST|Song of Light|link}}" and, after {{Term|ST|Link}} plays it, the {{Term|ST|Air Stone|display=Stone}} will blast off to reveal a light switch beneath it. {{Term|ST|Link}} must then hit another {{Term|ST|Switch|link}} with the {{Term|ST|Boomerang|link}} in the direction that the light is pointing to in order to make a small bridge appear that connects to a small island off the station. Once {{Term|ST|Link}} crosses the bridge and bombs the wall to reveal another cave, the young hero must play the "{{Term|ST|Song of Light}}" on the two light switches and play the "{{Term|ST|Song of Discovery|link}}" four stone tiles north and six stone tiles west to reveal a {{Term|ST|Treasure Chest|link}} containing the {{Term|ST|Regal Ring}}.

After {{Term|ST|Link}} shows the {{Term|ST|Regal Ring}} to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}, {{Term|ST|Kenzo}} will jump into the conversation and take the rare {{Term|ST|Treasure}} as payment.<ref>{{Cite|So this little doodad's worth 8,000 Rupees? Coulda fooled me. Still, it beats takin' another IOU! If you need anything else, just give me a holler!|Kenzo|ST}}</ref> From here on out, {{Term|ST|Link}} can bring and sell any {{Term|ST|Treasure}} to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}, and if he has enough {{Term|ST|Treasure}}, he can also trade it for new {{Plural|ST|Train Car|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Still, you've got a real talent for sniffing out treasure. Ooh! Tell you what--from here on out, bring any treasure you find to me! If they impress me, I'll trade you some lovely new train cars for those trinkets! Sound like a sweet deal? That's 'cause it IS!|Linebeck III|ST}}</ref>

If {{Term|ST|Link}} brings five units of {{Term|ST|Dark Ore|link}} from the {{Term|ST|Dark Ore Mine|link}} in the {{Term|ST|Fire Realm|link}} to {{Term|ST|Linebeck III}}, he will reward the young hero with a {{Term|ST|Force Gem|link}} that restores the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)}} in the {{Term|ST|Ocean Realm}}, thus opening the {{Term|ST|Lost at Sea Station|link}}.
