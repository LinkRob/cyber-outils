==Characteristics==
Magunesu are disk-like creatures with a blue face and a red face, each one side of the same disk. Each face has a different magnetic polarity than the other. The northern red face is attracted by southern polarity while the southern blue face is attracted by northern polarity. Magunesu will regularly change faces to confuse [[Link]]. When they change their polarity, they will spit a fireball at Link, often used when fleeing from his [[Sword]]. A conjunction of the [[Magnetic Gloves]] and the Sword is almost required to defeat these enemies.
