The {{Term/Store|Rented Bow||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Bow<br/>Arrows fly straight to take down enemies! You can also move while aiming!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Bow}} is the first {{Term|ALBW|Item}} {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} obtains in {{ALBW}}, being given to him by {{Term|ALBW|Ravio|link}}. It is necessary to open the way to the {{Term|ALBW|Eastern Palace|link}}. Just as the normal {{Term|ALBW|Bow|link}} does, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Bow}} fires an {{Term|ALBW|Arrow|link}} when activated, which can be used to damage {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy}} and hit {{Plural|ALBW|Shock Switch|link}}. {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} offers it for rent for only ten {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}} before the {{Term|ALBW|Eastern Palace}} is cleared, compared to its normal price of fifty.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Bow}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Bow}} for 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Bow|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
