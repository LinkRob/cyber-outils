{{Term/Store|Bertri||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Bertri|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Bertri}} is a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier who can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. She guards one of the entrances to {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom|link}} upstairs, and she carries a {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Spear|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} initially tries to go past her, {{Term|BotW|Bertri}} stops him and tells him that he better leave if he does not want to be skewered.<ref>{{Cite|Halt! This isn't a place someone like you can walk in and out of whenever you like!|Bertri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you don't want me to skewer you right here, you'd better leave at once!|Bertri|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has recovered the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Hideout|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bertri}} is shocked that {{Term|BotW|Link}} has permission to enter {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s bedroom and asks who he is.<ref>{{Cite|You've got permission to enter the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s bedroom?! Who ARE you?|Bertri|BotW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
