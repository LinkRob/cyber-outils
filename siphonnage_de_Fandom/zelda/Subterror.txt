{{Term/Store|Subterror||Series, OoA}} is the {{Term|OoA|Sub-Boss}} of the {{Term|OoA|Moonlit Grotto|link}} in {{OoA}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= OoA|publisher= Nintendo|page= 79}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Term|OoA|Subterror}} is a mole-like creature who sports sunglasses and a drill for a nose. It begins the fight by burrowing into the sand-covered ground and chasing after {{Term|OoA|Link|link}}; its path visible as a small bump in the sand. {{Term|OoA|Subterror}} cannot be harmed while underground, so {{Term|OoA|Link}} must use the {{Term|OoA|Shovel|link}} when it's within reach to dig it out of the ground, then strike it with the {{Term|OoA|Sword|link}} while it's temporarily dizzy. It will then saunter off and dive back underground. However, if {{Term|OoA|Subterror}} gets too close to {{Term|OoA|Link}}, it will harm him by spiking its drill nose upward through the sand. After taking enough damage, {{Term|OoA|Subterror}} will be defeated, leaving behind a {{Term|OoA|Fairy|link}} and a portal to the {{Term|OoA|Dungeon|link}}'s entrance.

==Nomenclature==
"Subterror" a portmanteu of ''subterranean'', referring to its underground habitat, and ''terror''.
