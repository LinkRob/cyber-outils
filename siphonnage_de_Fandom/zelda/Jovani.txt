{{Term/Store|Jovani||Series, TP, TPHD}} is a character in {{TP}}.<ref name="E 276"/>

==Biography==
Jovani is an overweight man who sold his soul to the [[Imp Poe]]s for untold riches. However, they also changed him into a golden statue with gems for eyes and his [[Cat]], [[Gengle]], fused to his head. He now values his humanity over his wealth and asks [[Link]] to retrieve his shattered soul.<ref name="Girlfriend 1"/> When [[Wolf Link]] visits him for the first time, he learns how to defeat the Imp Poes. Jovani then tells him that if he gets 20 [[Poe Soul]]s or more, he will get a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Once you've found and defeated the 20 ghosts that lurk in the darkness, come here by digging by the wall where the cats gather...|Jovani|TP}}</ref>

When Link gets 20 Poe Souls, Jovani gives him a [[Bottle]] filled with [[Great Fairy's Tears]].<ref>{{Cite|You did it! I can move again! This is all thanks to you, right? Oh, I have to ask you... Will this do? Ah ha ha ha! I hear you can get this stuff in the cave on the eastern edge of the Gerudo Desert, too!|Jovani|TP}}</ref> Jovani and Gengle also recover their mobility, however, Jovani stays golden.<ref>{{Cite|But...my body is still golden... Does this mean my soul is still not whole? That's strange... Maybe we need the souls of 60 ghosts... Can I ask another favor of you, doggie? Can you defeat more ghosts?|Jovani|TP}}</ref> In  {{TPHD}}, Jovani also gives Link the [[Ghost Lantern]].<ref>{{Cite|It must be though finding those creepy crawlies, right? Take this Lantern with you!|Jovani|TPHD}}</ref>

When Link gets 60 Poe Souls, Jovani's curse is lifted resulting in him becoming thin physically, and Gengle gives Link 200 [[Rupee]]s each time he visits him.<ref>{{Cite|You did it! Look at me! I'm back to my old self again! Thanks to you, everything in my life is coming up roses! Ha ha HA! Oh, of course, I need to thank you... Please accept this... All right! I need to let my girlfriend know that I'm back!|Jovani|TP}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, it's you. I see... So my master got dumped, huh? Well, I guess it goes to show that for humans, it's all about money. I guess that means we don't need any more money...so you can take it all.|Gengle|TP}}</ref>

Afterwards, Jovani can be found in [[Telma's Bar]] crying because his girlfriend had found another man.<ref name="Girlfriend 2"/> His girlfriend is seemingly now involved with a castle [[Guard]] she was questioning earlier,<ref>{{Cite|Do you have a second? Do you know a guy named Jovani? My girl here put out a missing- persons report on him. I think she's just trying to make me jealous. Strange things are happening...and some people are even saying it's the work of evil ghouls... It's kind of become an urban legend, in a way... If you ever meet a man called Jovani, tell him to get in touch with this girl here...|Guard|TP}}</ref> and believes Jovani was inside the bar the whole time he was missing.<ref>{{Cite|Word came in that he's at the bar... Ugh, what a nuisance...|Hylian|TP}}</ref>

According to {{Guide|TP|Prima}}, Jovani is around 40 years old.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TP|publisher= Prima|page= 14}}</ref>
