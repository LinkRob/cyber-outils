==Features==

{{T&T|-}} contains 103 pages, and includes an overview of the characters, {{Plural|TLoZ|Enemy|link}}, and {{Plural|TLoZ|Dungeon|link}} of {{TLoZ}}. It provides a full map of the first seven {{Plural|TLoZ|Dungeon}}.
