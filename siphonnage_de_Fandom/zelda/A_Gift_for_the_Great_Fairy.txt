"{{Term/Store|A Gift for the Great Fairy||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Gift for the Great Fairy}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Tabantha Bridge Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Toren|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable|link}}. He will be speaking to himself about a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Ahh... {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}...}}|Toren|BotW}}</ref> though he excuses himself when he notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, pardon me... I was lost in thought.|Toren|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he has heard of the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} from the old stories,<ref>{{Cite|You've heard of the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}, right? The one from all those old stories?|Toren|BotW}}</ref> admitting that he believes her to be real and is searching for her.<ref>{{Cite|Well, they're not just stories! I believe the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}} is real! And I'm on a journey to find her.|Toren|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Toren}} points out the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tower|link}} beyond the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Great Bridge|link}}, sharing a rumor that if one is on top of it during the afternoon, its shadow will point towards the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's a tower just beyond the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha Great Bridge}}. They say that if you go to the top and look at the ground sometime in the afternoon... {{Color|BotW Red|The tower's shadow will point}} to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}'s fountain!|Toren|BotW}}</ref>

Once he heard of this legend, {{Term|BotW|Toren}} went to investigate himself, but he has become stuck at the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable}} because he knows he can not climb the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Tower}}.<ref>{{Cite|After hearing that legend, I decided to find out for myself. But now I'm stuck here at this stable because... Well... No matter how hard I try, I just know I'll never be able to climb that tower.|Toren|BotW}}</ref> He even saved up for an offering for the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}}.<ref>{{Cite|And to think, I even saved up all my rupees as an offering for the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}.|Toren|BotW}}</ref> However, because {{Term|BotW|Link}} appears to be strong, {{Term|BotW|Toren}} asks him to make the offering for him.<ref>{{Cite|But you... You look like you could climb that tower without breaking a sweat! WOuld you be willing to make an offering of rupees to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}} on my behalf?|Toren|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts, {{Term|BotW|Toren}} gives him 500 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}, which are the entirety of his life savings.<ref>{{Cite|[I'll do it!] Oh, thank you! Here, take the {{Color|BotW Red|500 rupees}} I saved up. It's my life savings! {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|So please don't just go running off with it, OK?}}}}|Toren|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has given an offering to the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} {{Term|BotW|Kaysa|link}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Toren}} at the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Bridge Stable}}. He asks if {{Term|BotW|Link}} gave the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} an offering,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, it's you! Did you give the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}} an {{Color|BotW Red|offering}}?|Toren|BotW}}</ref> and he is grateful to hear that he has.<ref>{{Cite|[Of course.] All right! Thank you! Looks like asking you was a good call after all.|Toren|BotW}}</ref> Though he is jealous that {{Term|BotW|Link}} met {{Term|BotW|Kaysa}},<ref>{{Cite|So that means you met the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}, huh? Man, I'm so jealous...|Toren|BotW}}</ref> but he realizes that he technically made his offering.<ref>{{Cite|But hey, at least you got to meet her! And that means, technically, I got to make my offering to the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}!|Toren|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Toren}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} that he can not thank him enough.<ref>{{Cite|I can't thank you enough!|Toren|BotW}}</ref> However, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} hints at a reward,<ref>{{Cite|[Forgetting something?] Huh? Um, no, I don't think so... Wait, what? You aren't trying to tell me you want a reward, are you?|Toren|BotW}}</ref> Toren is confused, as he got to see the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} because of him.<ref>{{Cite|You got to see the {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}} thanks to my tip, didn't you? And now you're asking for more?!|Toren|BotW}}</ref> He wonders aloud why the {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy}} will not appear before him, even in his dreams.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh, {{Color|BotW Blue|Great Fairy}}... Even if it's just in my dreams, won't you appear before me?|Toren|BotW}}</ref> This complete the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
