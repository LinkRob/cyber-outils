Swordfighter form is an ability that Zelda can activate upon equipping the [[Sword of Might]].<ref name="Leaving Swordfighter Form">{{Cite|While in swordfighter form, the Energy gauge in the upper-left corner of the screen will decrease. You'll automatically exit swordfighter form after the Energy gauge is depleted. You can also exit this form by pressing [Directional Button Up].|Game Screen|EoW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After Zelda Defeats the [[Echo Link|Link Echo]] in Suthorn ruins, Zelda gains the ability to fight and use items exclusive to him, such as the [[Bow of Might]], and the [[Bombs of Might]]. Using Swordfighter form uses [[Energy]], which Zelda can obtain by collecting it in the still world, defeating Dark monsters, or just by drinking Smoothies. The weapons appear to do higher damage to Dark World monsters when wielded by Zelda.

Zelda can Upgrade her Swordfighter form equipment at [[Lueburry]] to Level 3, which Increases damage, and Energy duration. Energy duration extending items can also be earned at the [[Slumber Dojo]].

==Trivia==

* It appears that Link is already maxed out in the Might weapons at the start of the game, and Zelda herself upgrading Link's weapons or not upgrading has no effect on him when he reclaims his weapons.
* Maxed out Bombs of Might imitate [[Bombchu]] when charged.
* The maxed out Sword of Might gives Zelda access to the [[Sword Beam]] when her HP is full. Link's has this by default.
