The {{Term/Store|Rented Hookshot||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Hookshot<br/>Hooks on to anything made of wood. Can help you travel across treacherous gaps.|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Hookshot}} is used to lift and carry {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} across gaps and onto walls. It also will stun some {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} for a short time if shot. It is rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for fifty {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Hookshot}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Hookshot|link}} for 800 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Hookshot|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
