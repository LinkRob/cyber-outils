{{Term/Store|Hyrule's Greatest Warrior||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 63 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule's Greatest Warrior|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule's Greatest Warrior}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given ten minutes to defeat three {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. The first {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}} is {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}, and when he is defeated, {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}} appears. {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} appears after {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}}'s defeat, and when the objective is reach, {{Term|HWAoC|Harbinger Ganon|link}} spawns and the time limit is removed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends upon {{Term|HWAoC|Harbinger Ganon}}'s defeat, and can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Master Kohga}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Cap of the Wild|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Tunic of the Wild|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Trousers of the Wild|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
