{{Term/Store|Taafei Hill||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Taafei Hill'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Taafei Hill is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]].

A [[Stone Talus (Luminous)]] can be found along the southern edge of Taafei Hill.

There is an incomplete circle of rocks on the northern slope of Taafei Hill that acts as a [[Korok]] puzzle. If Link finishes the circle of rocks by placing a [[Rock]] in the gap, a Korok will appear and reward Link with a [[Korok Seed]] for finding him.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
