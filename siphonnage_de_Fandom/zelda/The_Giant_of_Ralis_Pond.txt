"{{Term/Store|The Giant of Ralis Pond||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Giant of Ralis Pond}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has calmed {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, he can encounter {{Term|BotW|Torfeau|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}. She calls him incredible for appeasing the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Hylian! To think that you were able to appease {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruta}}... You turned out to be a pretty incredible guy!|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> before revealing that she has a task for him.<ref>{{Cite|In fact, I wonder if you can do this, too...|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> She asks him to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Hinox|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Ralis Pond|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Exterminate the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hinox}} by {{Color|BotW Blue|Ralis Pond}}!|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Torfeau}} encourages him to try,<ref>{{Cite|OK... In the case, give it a try!|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> though she thinks it will be easy for him since he saved the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}}.<ref>{{Cite|For he who saved the domain, this should be a piece of fish cake. Go on, I'm counting on you!|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref>

Near {{Term|BotW|Ralis Pond}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Torfeau}} wants him to fight. Once it has been defeated, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must return to her to tell her.<ref>{{Cite|Hm... That stench... Did you perhaps... defeat that {{Color|BotW Blue|Hinox}} by {{Color|BotW Blue|Ralis Pond}}?|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> She is shocked to hear this news and thanks him.<ref>{{Cite|[Yes.] Whaaaat?! You really did?! I just... I can't... Wow! Th-thank you. Really! I knew it... The Champion who saved the domain is in a whole other league!|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, she gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Please, take this. Consider it a small token of my immense gratitude.|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} was able to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Hinox}} when she could not, {{Term|BotW|Torfeau}} resolves to get stronger.<ref>{{Cite|I guess you beat me to dealing with that {{Color|BotW Blue|Hinox}}. I...really wanted to get stronger.|Torfeau|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} ends.
