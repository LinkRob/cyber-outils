'''''The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time 3D — Prima Official Game Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{OoT3D}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 224 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{OoT3D}}, a listing of {{Plural|OoT3D|Item|link}} and collectibles, and detailed maps.
