The {{Term/Store|Common Cooking Area||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 259}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Common Cooking Area}} is a part of {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}} in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. It features a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Campfire|link}} under which can be lit in order to prepare {{Term|BotW|Food|link}}. Two {{Plural|BotW|Jar|link}} at the side of the structure can be broken for supplies.

In the evening, {{Term|BotW|Olkin|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mellie|link}} will head to the {{Term|BotW|Common Cooking Area}} to make dinner.<ref>{{Cite|Let's see here... I gotta sort the old man's supper... What should we have today?|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Their meals are made using many {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}},<ref>{{Cite|My wife's tough dishes are made from {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkins}}. They're out of this world.|Olkin|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Olkin}} finds to be better than {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|My wife's {{Color|BotW Blue|fortified pumpkin}} dishes are the best. Way better than a barrel o' carrots, that's for sure!|Olkin|BotW}}</ref>

During the "{{Term|BotW|Flown the Coop|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}, one of {{Term|BotW|Cado|link|display=Cado's}} {{Plural|BotW|Cucco|link}} can be found in and around the {{Term|BotW|Common Cooking Area}}. It can easily be picked up and returned to the {{Term|BotW|Cucco Coop|link}}. Afterwards, it will continue to appear in this location during the day.
