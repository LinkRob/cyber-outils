==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TMC|Pot Entrance}} are a type of {{Term|TMC|Minish Portal|link}}. There are eight of them in total, all of which are found within {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. Some of them are upside-down initially, and must be flipped over with the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci|link}} before being used. They allow {{Term|TMC|Link|link}}, with {{Term|TMC|Ezlo|link}}'s help, to reduce his size to that of a {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}, and are also used to return {{Term|TMC|Link}} to normal size.

The eight {{Plural|TMC|Pot Entrance}} are found in the following locations in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town}}:
*Inside {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}} (requires the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci}})
*Inside {{Term|TMC|Stockwell's Shop|link}}
*Inside {{Term|TMC|Rem's Shoe Store|link}}
*Inside the {{Term|TMC|Carpenters Shop|link}}
*Inside the {{Term|TMC|Guy's House|link}} (requires the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci}})
*On the roof of the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} (requires the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci}})
*Inside {{Term|TMC|Funday School|link}} (requires the {{Term|TMC|Cane of Pacci}})
*On the roof of {{Term|TMC|Funday School}}
