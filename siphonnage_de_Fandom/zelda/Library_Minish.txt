The {{Term/Store|Library Minish|Library Minish|Series, TMC|plural}} are a group of {{Term|Series|Town Minish|link}} in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Library Minish|Kinstone Fusion|TMC}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
The {{Term|TMC|Library Minish}} is the name given to a pair of {{Plural|TMC|Town Minish|link}} that live in the {{Term|TMC|Royal Hyrule Library|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. The one wearing yellow clothing, {{Term|TMC|Jotari|link}}, can be found on the bottom shelf of the {{Term|TMC|Bookshelf|link}} where they reside. The other, unnamed {{Term|TMC|Town Minish}} wearing blue clothing can be found on the second shelf. {{Term|TMC|Librari|link}} serves as the elder to both of them.<ref>{{Cite|I'd better get back to {{Color|TMC Green|Elder Librari}} now! He'll be wondering where I am.|Jotari|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We can't get up the shelf, because some human checked that book out! But at least we don't have to listen to {{Color|TMC Green|Elder Librari}}'s boring old stories!|Library Minish|TMC}}</ref>

Both {{Term|TMC|Jotari}} and the unnamed {{Term|TMC|Library Minish}} are potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to fuse pieces? Do you think you have one that matches mine?|Jotari|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Let's give it a try!|Library Minish|TMC}}</ref>
