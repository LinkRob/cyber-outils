The {{Name|Fan|{{Term/Store|Three Witches||Series}}}} are the third bosses in {{TWoG}}.{{Fact}} They are located at the [[Fairy Pool]].

== Biography==
The Three Witches are minions of [[Ganon]]. They are responsible for freezing the Fairy Pool and trapping the [[Fairy|Fairies]] that live there.<ref>{{Cite|We have frozen the fountain of life! All that is good we have made evil!|Three Witches|TWoG}}</ref> They are obsessed with spreading evil and use their combined powers to cover the forest in darkness.<ref>{{Cite|Let's bring the darkness down!|Three Witches|TWoG}}</ref> [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] defeats them and they are blown away by a powerful gust of wind, thus freeing the fountain's Fairies.

==Characteristics==
The Witches' combined powers makes the entire Pool pitch dark, requiring the use of a [[Lantern]] to see. Each of the Witches are scattered about the Pool. One is on ground level close to the entrance, another is at the fountain's Pool, while the third is on a branch above the Pool. They are all capable of throwing fireballs at Zelda that will kill her if it collides. The Witches are all weak to the [[Sword]], which will defeat one in a single strike.
