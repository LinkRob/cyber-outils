{{Term/Store|Reclaim the Land||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 54 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Reclaim the Land|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Reclaim the Land}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} are given fifteen minutes to capture four {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}. Each of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns next to one of them at the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}. A {{Term|HWAoC|Frost Talus|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox|link}}, and another {{Term|HWAoC|Guardian}} will each be the boss of an {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} and must be defeated to capture it. A {{Term|HWAoC|Silver Lynel|link}}, alongside two {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}, may also capture one of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} once they have already been taken. For the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} to recapture the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}}, he or she must defeat all three of the monsters. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any four {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}} and 1,000 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.
