{{Term/Store|Steel Mask|Steel Masks|Series, TFH|plural}} are items in {{TFH}}.<ref name="E 140"/>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|TFH|Steel Mask}} are rare {{Plural|TFH|Material|link}} that resemble the protective masks worn by {{Plural|TFH|Helmasaur|link}}. They are awarded for completing the following {{Plural|TFH|Level|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Drablands Challenge|link}} of the {{Term|TFH|Sky Realm|link}}:

*'''{{Term|TFH|Dragon Citadel|link}}''', ''"Don't fall at all!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Steel Mask}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Sky Temple|link}}''', ''"Clear without any items!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Steel Mask}}
*'''{{Term|TFH|Sky Temple}}''', ''"Fewer Heart Containers!"'': one {{Term|TFH|Steel Mask}}

They can also be won in the {{Term|TFH|Coliseum|link}} and {{Term|TFH|Daily Riches|link}}, or purchased from the {{Term|TFH|Street Merchant|link}} for 2,000 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} each.

{{Plural|TFH|Steel Mask}} are one of the {{Plural|TFH|Material}} used to make the {{Term|TFH|Spin Attack Attire|link}}.<ref name="E 140"/>
