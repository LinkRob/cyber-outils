{{Term/Store|Monster Curry|Monster Curries|Series, BotW, HWAoC|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Monster Curry}}<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Monster Curry}}|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Monster Curries are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} [[Hylian Rice]], [[Goron Spice]], and [[Monster Extract]] together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Since Monster Extract is used in their recipe, the amount of {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} that they'll heal to {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}} is random each time they're made. The strength and duration of any status effects will also be random. Monster Curries are considered an unusual take on curry, since their use of Monster Extract mean they don't rely heavily on spices.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Link can add more ingredients when making Monster Curry, which can result in the following variations:

* '''Hearty Monster Curry''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Monster Curry}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hearty ingredients. When eaten, all of Link's Hearts will be healed and he will gain temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Energizing Monster Curry''':<ref name="Energizing">{{Cite|{{Big|Energizing Monster Curry}}<br/>Instantly refills some of your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding energizing ingredients. Eating these curries will refill part of Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Energizing"/>

* '''Enduring Monster Curry''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Monster Curry}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding enduring ingredients. Link's Stamina Wheel will be restored and overfilled if he eats them.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Monster Curry''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Monster Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding hasty ingredients. They increase Link's movement speed when eaten.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Monster Curry''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Monster Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding spicy ingredients. Eating them will grant Link one level of [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Monster Curry''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Monster Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding electric ingredients. If eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Monster Curry''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Monster Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding mighty ingredients. Link's attack power can be increased by eating these curries.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Monster Curry''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Monster Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding tough ingredients. They can be eaten to increase Link's defense.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Monster Curry''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Monster Curry}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding sneaky ingredients. When eaten, Link's {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} will receive a boost.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Monster Curry''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Monster Curry}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>This unusual take on curry uses monster extract and doesn't rely on spices.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by adding chilly ingredients. If these curries are eaten, Link will gain one level of [[Heat Resistance]].<ref name="Chilly"/>
