{{Term/Store|Armor Set|Armor Sets|Series, BotW, TotK|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref>{{Cite|Armor Sets<br/>Wearing the top, bottom, and headwear from the same set can earn you some bonus effects.|Tips and Tricks|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|BotW|Armor Set}} are matching collections of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}. When worn, particular {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set}} may confer additional effects known as {{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus|link}} if every piece of {{Term|BotW|Armor}} in that {{Term|BotW|Armor Set|display=Set}} has been enhanced at least twice by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}}. As {{Plural|BotW|Armor}} introduced with {{TMT}} and {{TCB}} cannot be enhanced, the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Tingle's Set|link}} will provide their {{Plural|BotW|Set Bonus}} when worn regardless. The {{Term|BotW|Phantom Equipment|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Set|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Well-Worn Outfit|link}} lack any kind of {{Term|BotW|Set Bonus}}.
