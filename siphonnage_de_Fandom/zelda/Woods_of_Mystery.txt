The {{Term/Store|Woods of Mystery||Series, MM, MM3D}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Woods of Mystery}}<br/>Beware of ferocious turtles.|Sign|MM3D}}</ref>{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Woods of Mystery}} are referred to as the '''Lost Woods''' in {{Guide|MM|Versus}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= MM |publisher= Versus |page= 23}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
The forest is located past the [[Magic Hags' Potion Shop]] in [[Southern Swamp]]. It consists of several areas connected in a maze-like fashion, similar to the [[Lost Woods#Ocarina of Time|Lost Woods]] in {{OoT}}. [[Link]] must follow a certain path or he will get lost and find himself back at the entrance to the Woods.

[[Koume]], the person in charge of the Boat Cruise in the [[Swamp Tourist Center]], is immobilized in one of the areas within the Woods of Mystery, as the result of the [[Skull Kid#Majora's Mask|Skull Kid]] attacking her.<ref>{{Cite|I was just busy minding my own business, picking mushrooms, when... BAM! I got hit from behind! That pesky Skull Kid!|Koume|MM}}</ref> To find her, Link must follow a [[Monkey#Majora's Mask|Monkey]], who will lead the young hero to where Koume is. If Koume is not rescued by a certain point in the game, her sister [[Kotake]] sets out to find her, and Link can encounter Kotake in another area of the forest, which is also the location of a [[Mushroom]] that can be detected by the [[Mask of Scents]]. Once Link finds Koume, he must report back to Kotake about her sister's injuries. She will give him a [[Bottle]] filled with [[Red Potion]], which he must take back to Koume for her to consume.<ref>{{Cite|What's that?!? The Skull Kid got to Koume?!? That's ridiculous... If it's just the Skull Kid, then what harm could he possibly do? Oh!! Well, if that's true, then take this potion to her... This isn't good...|Kotake|MM}}</ref>

When Link gives Koume the Red Potion, she will instantly recover, and as a thanks to Link, she gives him a free Boat Cruise ride the next time he stops at the Swamp Tourist Center.<ref>{{Cite|Ooh! Feel the energy flow! Koume is revived!!! You saved me! I'm the swamp tour guide. I run the boat cruise, so come by if you want a free ride.|Koume|MM}}</ref>

===Navigation===
The location of Koume in the Woods of Mystery varies depending on the day; likewise, the path that the Monkey takes will be different. On the [[Second Day]], Link can enter a [[Cave#Majora's Mask|Secret Grotto]] with a [[Treasure Chest]] containing a [[Purple Rupee]], worth 50 [[Rupee]]s. The paths to Koume are as follows:

'''First Day:'''
Left, right, right, straight, left, left.

'''Second Day:'''
Right, left, straight, left, left, right.

'''Final Day:'''
Straight, left, right, right, straight, right.
