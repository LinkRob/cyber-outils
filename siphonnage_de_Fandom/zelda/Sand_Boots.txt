==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Sand Boots can be used to travel more quickly across sandy ground, such as the [[Gerudo Desert]] or beach areas.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be worn in [[Gerudo Town]] (in conjunction with the [[Gerudo Top]] and [[Gerudo Veil]]).

To get the Sand Boots, [[Link]] must speak to [[Bozai]], who can be found jogging around Gerudo Town. Link must wear the complete {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Outfit|link}} to initiate a Side Quest called [[The Eighth Heroine]]. Link will temporarily be given [[Snow Boots]] for this quest. Once Link has ventured to the [[Gerudo Highlands]] and taken a picture of the [[Statue of the Eighth Heroine]], he must show it to Bozai. Link will trade back the Snow Boots and be given the Sand Boots.<ref>{{Cite|Find her! Show me the {{Color|BotW Red|eighth heroine}}, and I'll give you these {{Color|BotW Blue|sand boots}}. What do you say?|Bozai|BotW}}</ref> After acquiring them, [[Granté]] will begin to sell the Sand Boots in [[Tarrey Town]].

The Sand Boots are not required to complete any quests in the game, but Link will be able to access [[The Forgotten Sword]] quest after obtaining them.
