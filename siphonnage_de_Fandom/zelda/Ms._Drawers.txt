{{Term/Store|Ms. Drawers||Series, TFH}} is a character in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 305}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Ms. Drawers}} is friends with {{Term|BotW|Mrs. Pompadour|link}} and can often be seen gossiping with her. She apparently has some spinach stuck in her teeth, which her friend regrets having not told her.<ref>{{Cite|I...wasn't able to tell her. About the spinach in her teeth. Did I fail her as a friend...?|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref> Ms. Drawers reports seeing a [[Princess Styla|black ghost]] around town to the castle entrance [[Mr. Guard|guard]], and asks him to help her find it and take a picture.<ref>{{Cite|I really saw it! The black ghost! I really did! It was tall and dark, and it ran off when I tried to get closer. You've GOT to believe me!|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> The same guard appears to be smitten with her, as she has seen him looking at her all day while working.<ref>{{Cite|I've seen you staring at me all the long day, you rascal! Could it be... Have I ensnared your senses... ?!|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> However, he may simply have been distracted by the spinach in her teeth.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, I'm more concerned about that bit of food stuck in her teeth than this alleged ghost...|Mr. Guard|TFH}}</ref>

After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} lifts the curse from {{Term|BotW|Princess Styla}}, both women come to realize that true fashion and beauty is what is on the inside.<ref>{{Cite|Ah, but you're not the only one who's learned something! From now on, I'm all about what REALLY matters...|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} wears the {{Term|BotW|Kinda Cursed Tights|link}} and speaks with them, they will believe him to be the black ghost haunting the town.<ref>{{Cite|Goodness gracious me! Is that...|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh! Aren't those...|Mrs. Pompadour|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|THE BLACK GHOST!!!|Ms. Drawers|TFH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|H-Hurry!
