==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Horn}} are {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} that are dropped by {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Stalkoblin|link}} upon defeat. They can be used with critters in a [[Cooking Pot]] to {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=Cook}} {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> The [[Kochi Dye Shop]] accepts Bokoblin Horns as an ingredient for Gray [[Dye]]. They can also be sold to {{Term|BotW|Kilton|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Plural|BotW|Mon|link}}.


{{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin Horn}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
