{{Term/Store|Zirco Mesa||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Zirco Mesa'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Zirco Mesa is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. The Mesa contains multiple levels of ice with a flat surface at the top. Multiple ice chunks containing random items such as [[Hyrule Bass]] can be found atop the Mesa. 

There is a [[Korok]] under a rock on the highest point on Zirco Mesa.
