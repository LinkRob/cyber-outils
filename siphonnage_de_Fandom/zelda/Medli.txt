{{Term/Store|Medli||Series, TWW, TWWHD}} is a character in {{TWW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 259{{Exp Game|TWW}}}}</ref> She is a young [[Rito]], and the apprentice attendant of [[Valoo]], the patron deity of the Rito.<ref>{{Cite|I'm an attendant to the great sky spirit, Valoo. My name is Medli.|Medli|TWW}}</ref><ref name= "Quote 1">{{Cite|I may be just an apprentice attendant, but I can understand some of the great Valoo's language. I'm not exactly bursting with confidence, but...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> She is close friends with [[Komali]] and is always watching and worrying over him. Medli is known to be kind-hearted and responsible and, when it comes to helping others, she is not afraid to take on challenges and other obstacles that might get in her way despite not being able to fly too well.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, please? I wouldn't ask if it wasn't important! It's just that...I'm not that good at flying yet...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Medli accompanies [[Link]] into two [[dungeon]]s, [[Dragon Roost Cavern]] and especially the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]], in order to fulfill her job as the attendant of Valoo and  [[Sage of Earth]], respectively. 

Even during her free time, Medli can be seen and heard trying to improve her skills as Valoo's attendant by practicing her most valued possession: the [[Rito Harp]].<ref name= "Quote 2">{{Cite|Well, I'd better continue practicing so that I can be useful around here, too! Performing music like this is an important duty of an attendant.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> She is also notable as one of the few characters in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]] that can be controlled by the player other than Link. 

==Biography==
===Valoo's Apprentice===
Some time before the events of {{TWW|-}}, Medli was chosen by [[Komali's Grandmother]] to become her apprentice and successor as [[Valoo]]'s attendant.<ref>{{Cite|See, Prince Komali's grandmother was the great Valoo's former attendant. She was an amazing woman. I was honored to have her as my teacher... She was kind and brave, and unsurpassed in her dealings with the great Valoo.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Komali's grandmother saw the harp that Medli was carrying and had sensed what destiny had in store for the young girl, but kept quiet about it; instead, she used her position to teach Medli all that she had to know until the day fate would call her.<ref>{{Cite|This instrument is called one of fate's tools. Prince Komali's grandmother saw me carrying it one day and called out to me to stop. She'd seen my fate in the curves of the instrument, you see. She eventually became my teacher, and that's how I became the attendant you see before you today. So, now you understand why I need to hurry and improve my skills, so that I may help the aerie.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> However, Komali's grandmother passed away, leading Medli to became the new attendant of Valoo,<ref>{{Cite|You're Link, right? Oh, me? I'm an attendant to the great sky spirit, Valoo. My name is Medli. Well, to tell the truth... I'm not an attendant quite yet. I'm actually still studying to be one.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> but unlike the former attendant of the Sky Spirit, Medli could not fully understand Valoo's language and thus was not too confident in her abilities.<ref name= "Quote 1"/> 

One day, [[Gohma]] appeared in the [[Dragon Roost Cavern]] and began tormenting Valoo's tail, causing the Sky Spirit to go into a fit of rage. Valoo's home became inaccessible and Medli could no longer fulfill her job as his attendant. Moreover, since Medli was not as knowledgeable with the Sky Spirit's language, she could not truly comprehend why the dragon had begun to go berserk. Due to this, Medli feels it is partly her fault that [[Komali|Prince Komali]] has shut himself in and lost his confidence to go and ask Valoo for his pair of wings: for if Medli had possessed some of Komali's grandmother's strength, the prince of the Rito would have more faith in himself.<ref>{{Cite|I'm not yet worthy of being mentioned in the same breath as her. If only I'd possessed some of her strength, I'm sure Prince Komali would have felt more secure.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> 

Eventually, [[Link]] arrives on [[Dragon Roost Island]] and is asked to meet with Medli so that the young hero can give Prince Komali a letter from [[Rito Chieftain|his father]].<ref>{{Cite|I have something I wish for you to give to my son. A young girl named Medli is holding it for me. Would you find her and take it?|Rito Chieftain|TWW}}</ref> After handing over the letter, Medli in turn asks Link to meet her at the entrance of Dragon Roost Cavern once he has spoken with Prince Komali.<ref>{{Cite|When you meet Prince Komali, please don't get offended by his manner. He has no bad intentions, I promise. By the way, ummmm... Listen, Link... I have a small favor I'd like to ask of you... Could you please come to the entrance of Dragon Roost Cavern later?... I'll explain everything then.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Link complies with Medli's favor and goes to meet her at the entrance of Dragon Roost Cavern, where she expresses her blame about Prince Komali's rude behavior towards Link.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you actually came! I'm really sorry for bringing you to such a dangerous place. I had to! [...] Tell me, how was Prince Komali? Oh... That doesn't sound right... Not at all... I may be partially to blame for the bad turn that Prince Komali's taken... See, Prince Komali's grandmother was the great Valoo's former attendant. She was an amazing woman. I was honored to have her as my teacher... She was kind and brave, and unsurpassed in her dealings with the great Valoo. I'm not worthy of being mentioned in the same breath as her. If only I'd possessed some of her strength, I'm sure Prince Komali would have felt more secure.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> She also asks that Link help her reach Valoo by giving her a lift to the entrance of the cavern in spite of the winds randomly changing direction from the bad atmospheric currents by Valoo's rage.<ref>{{Cite|Link, I'm sorry to ask this... but I need your help! I want to go to the small shrine that's near the peak of Dragon Roost, but that ledge over there is so high... If I could get some wind under my wings, I'm sure I could get up there. So... Will you help me?|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Medli successfully gets some wind under her wings and reaches the entrance, where she thanks Link and asks that she should look out after Prince Komali if anything is to happen to her as she climbs Dragon Roost to meet with Valoo.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, thank you! I think now I'll be able to climb Dragon Roost and meet with the great Valoo! Don't worry, I'll be fine! I may just be an apprentice attendant, but I can understand some of the great Valoo's language. I'm not exactly bursting with confidence, but... Look, if anything happens to me... Please look after Prince Komali!|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Just before she departs, she gives the young hero an empty [[Bottle]], and wishes good luck to the both of them.<ref>{{Cite|This is all I have to give you. I know it's not much, but please take it! Oh, and please don't tell anyone that I'm climbing Dragon Roost! It'll be our secret, OK? Well, good luck!... To both of us.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> 

Knowing she should not do this alone, Link promptly follows Medli into the cavern after he hears that several Rito saw her get kidnapped by monsters.<ref>{{Cite|Just now, some fellow told me that he saw some unfamiliar creatures near Dragon Roost Cavern... He said that they grabbed a girl that looked like Medli... ...Which is odd, because I know Medli can't fly well enough to get to the Dragon Roost Cavern entrance by herself.|Quill|TWW}}</ref> After ascending Dragon Roost Cavern himself, Link eventually finds her locked up in a cage high atop the mountain where she is being guarded by two [[Bokoblin|Shield Bokoblin]]s. Link gets rid of them alongside a [[Moblin]] and frees his friend, who informs him that a [[Gohma|large creature]] is tormenting Lord Valoo's tail. She asks him to save the Sky Spirit and gives him the [[Grappling Hook]] to help him reach Valoo, which is what she used to get to the top of Dragon Roost.<ref>{{Cite|Link! You came to rescue me! Oh, thank you! I have to tell you what I found out! This is terrible!... Some creature is doing awful things to the great Valoo's tail! That's why he's so angry! The great Valoo's tail hangs down into the room right below here. There's got to be something in there... I wonder if those mean monsters who captured me have something to do with it... We've got to do something! Now! Before it's too late! I'll go and tell everyone what's happening! Here, Link! Use this to get out of here! It's what I used to get this far. It's a device we Rito used before we evolved wings. Here, Link. Climb up on that little ledge over there, so I can show you something. Grab this branch with the Grappling Hook and you can swing over there to get out of this area! Don't you worry about me, Link. Get going!|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Medli then takes off to get help from the other Rito. Soon after, Link destroys the dungeon's [[boss]], known as Gohma, but not without a little help from Valoo himself.

After saving Valoo from Gohma, Link meets Medli and Komali on the beach of Dragon Roost Island, where she supports Komali in giving his most precious possession, [[Din's Pearl]], to Link. As Link himself does not understand ancient [[Hylian Language|Hylian]], Medli kindly translates Valoo's words of gratitude and also gives her interpretation of the Sky Spirit's hints of where Link should go next.<ref>{{Cite|Use the wind god's...wind? That's what he said, but I don't understand. I wonder what it means? The wind god's wind... I wonder if what he's talking about has anything to do with the Wind Shrine that's through that tunnel... Oh, I almost forgot! The great Valoo also names you, Link, a true hero. I agree with him. As far as we're concerned, Link, you really ARE a hero!|Medli|TWW}}</ref> She also tells him that Valoo acknowledges Link as a hero, which she agrees. They say goodbye to each other when Komali excitedly runs away to obtain his pair of wings from Valoo and Medli feels she needs to go with him.<ref>{{Cite|Well, I'm going to go and visit Valoo! I'll come and see you sometime with the best pair of wings ever!|Prince Komali|TWW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey! Prince Komali! Thanks so much, Link. See you again someday!|Medli|TWW}}</ref>

===Sage of Earth===
With the Sky Spirit now safe, Medli goes back to studying for her job as Valoo's attendant, practicing her harp on one of the cliffs of Dragon Roost Island.<ref>{{Cite|Master Link, Medli is busy with her attendant studies.|Akoot|TWW}}</ref><ref name= "Quote 2"/> Later, once Link meets [[Laruto]], the previous [[Sage of Earth]], she informs the hero that he must look for the one carries the same sacred instrument as her, a harp, and play the "[[Earth God's Lyric]]," the melody that will awaken the new Sage of Earth.<ref>{{Cite|The song you just conducted is one which will open the eyes of the new sage and awaken within that sage the melody that will carry our prayers to the gods.|Laruto|TWW}}</ref> Link returns to Dragon Roost Island where, upon finding Medli, she reveals that Prince Komali has been asking all about the young hero's adventures, expressing her pride in him yet also sadness that Komali is now doing things without her guidance.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, Link! You're OK! You know, Prince Komali's been asking me all about your adventures, Link. I can rest easy now that I've seen you safe and sound with my own eyes. Prince Komali's turned into a fine young adult, hasn't he? Recently, he's even begun doing things on his own, without my guidance.... Every conversation still ends up centered on you, though, Link. Watching Prince Komali grow up fills me with pride... but it makes me a little sad, as well... I wonder if this is how a mother feels... Oh, but just listen to me go on! How weird I must sound! Hee hee...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> As instructed by Laruto, Link conducts the "Earth God's Lyric" as Medli plays along with her harp, which suddenly causes the young girl to faint.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... What a mysterious song... It sounds so... familiar. It's almost as if something I've forgotten is trying to be remembered... Oh... I feel...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> She meets Laruto in her dreams, where the two of them play the sacred song together. Realizing her new duty, Medli wakes up as the new Sage of Earth, overwhelmed by the fact that she can actually do something to help the world, shedding a tear in her eyes.<ref>{{Cite|Just now, a sage spoke to me. She spoke so gently... There's something...something that I must do. Link... Thanks to you, I've been awakened to the knowledge that I'm a sage of the Earth Temple. There's actually something that I can do to help this world. It's incredible...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Link and Medli, however, notice Komali flying in the air above them. She then asks that Link take her to the [[Earth Temple (The Wind Waker)|Earth Temple]], quickly but quietly, for she wishes for Prince Komali to not take notice of her departure, and, importantly, to just remember her as a simple attendant.<ref>{{Cite|Link, please... You must take me to the Earth Temple. We must hurry and wake the power to repel evil that sleeps within the Master Sword. Let's go now, quickly and quietly. Oh, Komali... I just want him to remember me as a simple attendant...|Medli|TWW}}</ref> Once the young Rito arrives, the two are nowhere to be found.

Once at the Earth Temple, Medli plays a major role in gameplay, as she now serves as Link's [[Link's Partners|partner]]. The [[King of Red Lions]] advises Link and Medli how to progress through the temple, resulting in the player gaining control of another character.<ref>{{Cite|Medli, listen carefully to what I am about to tell you. The temple ahead is the nest of an evil creature... the same creature that stole the life of your predecessor, the sage. It is a dark and frightening place. That is why, if you do nothing else, then at least do this... If Link comes near you and presses R to call you, then be silent, and follow closely behind him. And if Link presses A, allow him to lift you, and if need be, fly and carry him through the air. And Link... If Medli gets stuck and cannot continue in the temple, use the Wind Waker to conduct the song you learned in the Tower of the Gods. Do you understand? Link, protect Medli. And Medli, protect Link. The power to repel evil shall surely be returned to the Master Sword!|King of Red Lions|TWW}}</ref> The basic setup in the dungeon is that Medli closely follows Link unless she cannot for whatever reason or because Link commands her to stay still. By pressing the [[Controller Buttons|R Button]], Link can call Medli to him. By using the "[[Command Melody]]," Link can take control of Medli and can use her ability of flight to reach places otherwise inaccessible, and can also use her harp to deflect rays of light like the [[Mirror Shield]] (which he soon obtains in the temple), which are often used as part of light [[Puzzle|puzzles]]. Medli can also be picked up and carried by Link to reach certain areas. Though Medli is not yet proficient at flying, she can carry Link across gaps for a short amount of time.[[File:Halfpower.png|thumb|right|Link and Medli performing the "Earth God's Lyric"|248x248px]]At the end of the dungeon, Link temporarily leaves Medli to fight its boss, [[Jalhalla]], but she joins him again after the defeat of the giant [[Poe]] to fulfill her duty as the Sage of Earth. Link conducts the "Earth God's Lyric" for Medli as she plays the song on her harp. As they play it, Laruto temporarily appears next to Medli to play along. This restores the first half of the [[Master Sword]]'s sacred power to which the young Rito takes notice and urges Link to go to the [[Wind Temple]] to completely restore the legendary blade's full power with the help of another friend, [[Makar]], to become the [[Sage of Wind]]. Medli stays inside the Earth Temple continuing to pray so that the [[Power to Repel Evil]] can return to the Master Sword.<ref>{{Cite|Link, the power to repel evil is not yet fully awakened. To complete the awakening of the Master Sword, you must go to the Wind Temple for one more prayer. Now, Link... Step into the light behind you and return to the surface. Your next duty is to find the sage of the Wind Temple.|Medli|TWW}}</ref> As Link departs back to the [[Great Sea]], Medli asks Link to watch over Prince Komali for her.<ref>{{Cite|I will remain here and continue to pray. You must hurry!... And Link! Um... About Prince Komali... Please watch over him for me.|Medli|TWW}}</ref>

Medli appears for the last time during the ending scene. Together with [[Tetra's Pirate Crew]], [[Aryll]], Prince Komali, and Makar, she picks up Link and [[Tetra]] after their final battle with Ganondorf. It is unknown what happened to Medli or the Hero of Winds' other friends, as they are not seen or mentioned in the game's direct sequel, ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass|Phantom Hourglass]]'', but it can be safely assumed they simply returned to their home islands.

===''Animal Crossing: New Leaf''===
Medli was added as a villager in the ''{{Animal Crossing|Welcome amiibo|Welcome amiibo}}'' update of ''{{Animal Crossing|Animal Crossing: New Leaf}}'', alongside [[Wolf Link]], [[Ganon]], and [[Epona]]. If the [[Toon Link]] [[amiibo]] is scanned into the game, Medli will show up at the campground and can be asked to move into the town. She is a {{Animal Crossing|Bird}} villager that has a {{Animal Crossing|Normal}} personality, and is thus kind, a little shy, and easy to befriend. Her birthday, December 13, references the Japanese release date of {{TWW|-}}.

==={{HW|-}}===
{{Stub|HW}}
[[File:HWDE Medli Mini Map Icon.png|left]]
Medli appears as a playable Warrior in {{HW}}. She is unlocked by downloading the Version 1.9.0 and Version 1.3.0 updates of {{HW|-}} and {{HW|L}}, respectively. In {{HW|DE}}, Medli is unlocked as a Reward for Battle Victory on [[Great Sea Map/Hyrule Field (Hyrule Warriors: Definitive Edition)#Battle B8|Battle B8]] of the [[Great Sea Map]] in [[Adventure Mode]].

Medli attacks by strumming her [[Rito Harp#Hyrule Warriors|Rito Harp]], similarly to [[Sheik]], and launching sphere-shaped gusts of wind at enemies. She can also summon a tornado to trap enemies, then reflect light off her harp to damage them, or by flying into the air before dive-bombing enemies. One of her more powerful combos allows her to attack a large group of enemies with light, gathering them into a small cluster then blasting them away as a symbol resembling [[Din's Pearl]] appears on the ground. Her [[Special Attack]] has her gather enemies with a gust of wind before headbutting them away, which leaves her stumbling dizzily for a few seconds, much like when Link throws her into a wall in {{TWW|-}}.

When defeated in battle, Medli may drop [[Ruto's Earrings]] as a silver [[Material]] or [[Ruto's Scale]] as a gold Material.

==Trivia==
*She is one of three characters in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker|The Wind Waker]]'' who can be controlled by the player other than [[Link]], alongside the statues in [[Tower of the Gods]] and [[Makar]], which is met with praise by fans. 
*The symbol on Medli's apron is similar to that of [[Nayru's Pearl]] and the [[Zora's Sapphire]]. This may be referencing how the [[Rito]] tribe evolved from the [[Zora]] race.[[File:TWW Medli reaction to Green Clothes.png|thumb|253x253px|Medli's excited reaction from Link's [[Green Clothes|Green Tunic]] in ''The Wind Waker'']]
*Upon first meeting Medli, she notably expresses interest in Link's [[Hero's Clothes]], due to its elf-like appearance. In the [[Second Quest]], where he wears the [[Hero's New Clothes]], she says a different quote but remains interested. This is likely because [[Quill]] had explained to the Rito tribe what the young hero looks like.
*If Medli is captured by a [[Floormaster]] in the [[Earth Temple]], she will be locked up in a cage above the room where the first statue requiring her and Link's teamwork to put light into its "eyes", but she can easily fly out with the [[Command Melody]].
*Makar is a mirror to Medli in which he is a friend of Link the Hero of Winds who at first needs to be [[Forbidden Woods|rescued]] and become an important ally in a [[Wind Temple|later dungeon]].
*If Medli crashes into a wall when Link throws her at the entrance of [[Dragon Roost Cavern]] or inside the Earth Temple, she can be comically seen slowly turning back toward the hero, dizzy for a few seconds.[[File:Laruto Medli.jpg|thumb|237x237px|Medli with the previous Sage of Earth, Laruto, in ''The Wind Waker'']]
*As ''The Wind Waker'' takes place in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Adult Timeline]], the Rito were evolved from the Zora, and [[Laruto]] is her predecessor as a [[Sage]] as well as helping Link in stopping [[Ganondorf]], it is likely Medli is a distant descendant of [[Princess Ruto]], the Sage of Water and fiancée of the [[Hero of Time]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time|Ocarina of Time]]''. 
*During the cutscene of when Medli becomes awakened as the new Sage of Earth and she and Link look up to see [[Komali]] flying above, a romantic guitar-themed version of the [[Dragon Roost Island]] theme plays. Alongside her dialogue, this has led some fans to believe that she and Prince Komali are in a relationship, or at the very least have crushes on each other. If Link were to meet him, the young hero will discover he is actually trying to give Medli a flower, which eventually withers, further suggesting the speculation.
*Humorously, due to AI programming limitations or a developer oversight, if Medli is actively following Link and the young hero gets paralyzed by a [[ReDead]] within the Earth Temple, she will continue to move forward in a straight line until Link regains control.

==Nomenclature==
Due to {{TWW|-}} being heavily associated with music, it is possible that Medli's name could be a pun on "Medley" or "Melody". Medli's name also sounds similar to the {{Wp|Sanskrit}} name "Medini", which means "earth".
