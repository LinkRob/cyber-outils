{{Term/Store|Apprentice Yunobo||HWAoC}} is a level 45 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Apprentice Yunobo|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Apprentice Yunobo}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Spice|link}}, and two {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}.

{{Term|HWAoC|Yunobo}}'s objective is to defeat three key {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within twelve minutes. One of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Blue Hinox|link}}, is found right in front of the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} and surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}}. The other two {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, a {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus|link}} and a {{Term|HWAoC|White-Maned Lynel|link}}, are found on the other side of the map. As the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} follow the path to the two {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, they will encounter three {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}, followed by scattered groups of {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos|link}} and three more {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu}}. Near the end of the path is a bridge leading to the {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}}, containing two small groups of five {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Keese|link}}. As usual, the {{Term|HWAoC|Stone Talus}} is surrounded by {{Plural|HWAoC|Stone Pebblit|link}}, and when all three of the key {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends.
