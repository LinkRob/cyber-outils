==Features and Overview== 
==={{TLoZ|-}}===
The map of {{TAoL}} showing the placement of {{TLoZ}}'s location on [[Western Hyrule]] retroactively depicts the water body on the eastern border of {{TLoZ|-}} as the sea between Western and [[Eastern Hyrule]], and shows the water flows into the sea south of Western Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=The map from {{TLoZ|-}} is only a small portion of a much greater land to the north, and a second continent to the northeast, where {{TAoL|-}} takes place. [...] Another difference is that the water in {{ALBW|-}} flows into a lake, but in {{TLoZ|-}} it flows into a sea. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=61 }}</ref>

==={{TAoL|-}}===
:''See also: [[Palace on the Sea]]''
The [[Palace on the Sea]] is the fifth dungeon in {{TAoL}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=146 }}</ref>
 
==={{LA|-}}===
At the start of {{LA}}, [[Link]] is sent overboard when lightning strikes his boat on his way back to Hyrule. He awakes on an island called [[Koholint Island]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=A storm rages in the seas not far from Hyrule. Link awakens to find himself marooned on Koholint Island. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=38 }}</ref><ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Link, having restored peace to the kingdom of Hyrule during his adventure in {{ALttP|-}}, had set out on a journey to distant lands in order to train. On his way back to his beloved Hyrule, his boat was beset by a storm. Lightning struck his vessel, splitting it in two, and sending Link overboard. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=231 }}</ref> The name of the ocean is not mentioned in the game.

==={{MM|-}}===
:''See also: [[Great Bay]]''
In {{MM|-}}, the western region in the land of [[Termina]], the [[Great Bay]], is a sandy coast bordering a large ocean to the west of Termina that stretches as far as the eye can see, but a mysterious fog prevents anyone from going too far off of the land. It is here where the [[Zora]] live and also the location of the Great Bay Temple, the third dungeon of the game.

==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Main|Great Sea}}
In the {{TWW|-}}, the ocean Link traverses across is the [[Great Sea]]. It is composed of 49 islands set up in a 7x7 grid. There are three main races of this ocean: the [[Korok]]s, the [[Rito]], and [[Human]]s.

==={{PH|-}}===
{{Main|World of the Ocean King}}
Link wanders into a parallel world similar to the Great Sea in {{PH}} known as the [[World of the Ocean King]], where time flows differently to Hyrule.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=It is a parallel world that the hero Link wanders into in {{PH|-}}. The world of the Ocean King, though in another realm, appears very much like the Great Sea of Link's world. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=39 }}</ref> It is a vast ocean composed of 16 islands in four quadrants.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=Its map depicts a vast ocean dotted with islands in four quadrants. In all, there are sixteen islands Link and Linebeck can visit. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=284 }}</ref> This ocean is inhabited by [[Goron]]s and [[Anouki]].<ref>{{Cite Book|quote=There are numerous islands, including some inhabited by Gorons and Anouki, races found in old and new Hyrule. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=39 }}</ref>

==={{ST|-}}===
{{Main|Ocean Realm, Ocean Floor}}
In {{ST}}, the {{Term||Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} run over and under the sea of the [[Ocean Realm]]. The tracks lead to the [[Ocean Temple]] on the [[Ocean Floor]].<ref name="ocean realm"/><ref>{{Cite Book|quote={{color|E Blue|Ocean Temple}} Off the coast of the Ocean Realm, deep beneath the sea, lies this watery temple. To reach it, Link must first restore Spirit Tracks that run to the ocean floor. |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=157 }}</ref>

==={{SS|-}}===
{{Main|Lanayru Sand Sea}}
The Sand Sea, a large desert sea, is home to the Sandship, the fifth dungeon of the game. The sea, however, has evaporated over time and turned into a large patch of quicksand. To navigate the Sea, Link must activate a Timeshift stone in a boat, which both powers the boat and returns a patch of quicksand around the boat to it's present state.
