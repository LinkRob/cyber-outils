The {{Term/Store|Noble Pursuit|Noble Pursuits|Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring [[item]] and [[object]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}. It appears in {{BotW}} and {{HWAoC}} as a cocktail, and in {{TotK}} as a cocktail and fruit juice.  <ref name="Furosa">{{Cite|Our featured cocktail is called {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}. Many of our customers come here specifically to order it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Noble Pursuit}}<br/>The Noble canteen's most famous drink... or an iced tropical-juice mixture that does it justice.|Cooking (Interface)|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Mainline appearances==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Noble Pursuit is a featured cocktail at [[The Noble Canteen]] that many customers seek out.<ref name="Furosa"/> [[Furosa]], the owner of The Noble Canteen, describes it as having a light, sweet taste and providing a cooling sensation.<ref>{{Cite|The light, sweet taste... The cool sensation from each sip... It's like drinking distilled motivation!|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> It is made using [[ice]] from the [[Northern Icehouse]], and so is refreshing.<ref>{{Cite|Well, a big part of the drink is the ice we use from the {{Color|BotW Blue|icehouse}}. It makes them especially refreshing.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> However, Furosa considers [[Link]] too young for her [[shop]] and refuses to sell to him.<ref>{{Cite|I can't tell how old you are, but you look a little young to be coming to my shop. You see, we mix special drinks here. Drinks that are definitely just for adults...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

Link can find [[Pokki]] collapsed outside the [[Misae Suma Shrine]].<ref>{{Cite|I'd just found the shrine too... Ugh, my throat... It's so dry... I can't move a muscle.|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> She laments that, if she is to die here, she was unable to have one more sip of a Noble Pursuit.<ref>{{Cite|If this really is the end... I wish I could have one last taste... One sip... One sip of an ice-cold {{Color|BotW Red|Noble Pursuit}}...|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> This begins the [[Shrine Quest]] "[[The Perfect Drink]]".

Back in [[Gerudo Town]], Link can ask Furosa for a Noble Pursuit.<ref>{{Cite|[Noble Pursuit?] You want to try our signature drink, {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Not only has The Noble Canteen run out of Ice,<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, I need a huge amount of ice to make that drink, and I'm fresh out right now.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Furosa also refuses to sell the drink to Link due to its strength.<ref>{{Cite|Besides, it's a really strong drink. Even if I could make it, I wouldn't sell it to a little vai like you.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> When Link explains that the Noble Pursuit is for Pokki,<ref>{{Cite|[Actually...] What'd you say? Pokki's collapsed?|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Furosa reveals that she can't retrieve the Ice herself since her legs are not in shape.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm. I want to help her out, but I can't make a you-know-what without ice. There's ice at the {{Color|BotW Red|icehouse}}, but my legs aren't in the best shape for hauling ice...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Link offers to get the Ice for her, which relieves Furosa.<ref>{{Cite|[Should I get it for you?] Are you serious? You'll get it? That'd be a huge help.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> Furosa offers to meet him at the entrance of the ruins between the Icehouse and Gerudo Town.<ref>{{Cite|Carrying ice all the way through the desert and then all the way to town? I can't let that stand. If you bring the ice to the {{Color|BotW Red|entrance to the ruins}}—between town and the icehouse—I can take it from there.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

Once Furosa receives her Ice, she proclaims that she will be able to make the ultimate Noble Pursuit.<ref>{{Cite|With this, I'll be able to make the ultimate {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}}.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She asks Link to pass a message along to Pokki that the best Noble Pursuit ever is waiting for her.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, but could you please let {{Color|BotW Blue|Pokki}} know? Tell her that I made the best one ever, and I've got it waiting for her...|Furosa|BotW}}</ref> She believes that when Pokki hears this, she will come running.<ref>{{Cite|Knowing her, she'll probably come running when she hears that there's an amazing drink with her name on it.|Furosa|BotW}}</ref>

At Misae Suma Shrine, Link can inform Pokki of the Noble Pursuit that Furosa has prepared for her.<ref>{{Cite|[Actually...] Huh?|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> When she finds out it is supposedly the "best Noble Pursuit ever", she immediately takes off for Gerudo Town.<ref>{{Cite|Did you say "best {{Color|BotW Blue|Noble Pursuit}} ever"? Pfft, I am GONE!|Pokki|BotW}}</ref> This completes the Shrine Quest.

==={{TotK|-}}===
The Noble Pursuit is an adult beverage at the bar in the Gerudo Shelter. Normally, Furosa would have considered Link too young to get his hands on a Noble Pursuit. But, Furosa mentions a special version that is actually a fruit juice instead, but she doesnt have the ingredients necessary to make it. When [[Palm Fruit]], [[Hydromelon]], [[Voltfruit]], and [[Rock Salt]] are cooked together, Link gets a Noble Pursuit, which if Link tries doing this in Breath of the Wild, he will get [[Simmered Fruit]] instead.
