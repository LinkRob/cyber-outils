{{Term/Store|Staltroop|Staltroops|Series, TP, TPHD|plural}} are specialized enemies that appear only during [[Link]]'s battle with [[Stallord]] at the [[Arbiter's Grounds (Dungeon)|Arbiter's Grounds]] in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 207}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
Staltroops materialize from the sand in waves, forming barricades with their own bodies to deflect Link's [[Spinner]] as he attempts to reach Stallord's vulnerable spine. Although a single hit reduces them to sand, these foes respawn at an infinite rate; their purpose is only to deter Link from defeating Stallord, not to attack.
