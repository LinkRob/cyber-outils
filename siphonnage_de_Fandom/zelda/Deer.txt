{{Term/Store|Deer|Deer|Series, BotW}} are recurring {{Plural|BotW|Animal|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref name="Dantz">{{Cite|So what I need is for you to hunt some {{Color|BotW Red|deer}} out in {{Color|BotW Blue|Retsam Forest}}. They're overpopulated these days, and it's destroying the forest.|Dantz|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Plural|BotW|Deer}} are brown ruminants with cloven hooves found in the forested regions of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}. They are most immediately recognized for the branching antlers that appear on males of the species.<ref>{{Cite|They're well known for their huge, branching antlers, which they'll swing in self- defense if threatened.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's easily distinguished by its immense antlers, which these moose shed and regrow yearly.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> Though they will use their antlers in self-defense, {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} are more likely to run away from danger as they are notoriously skittish and timid.

Three different {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} appear in {{BotW}}. The most populous individuals are the {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Buck|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mountain Doe|link}} that can be found roaming in wooded areas and grassy plains throughout {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}. The third and largest {{Term|BotW|Deer}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|display=Hyrule}} is the {{Term|BotW|Tabantha Moose|link}} found in the cold {{Plural|BotW|Region|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|The largest breed of deer in Hyrule...|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> It is a hardy animal that cannot be ridden, but serves as a source of rare meats.<ref>{{Cite|Their meat is tender and high quality, so it works well in a stew.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Deer}} can be hunted using {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} and drop {{Term|BotW|Raw Meat|link}} when killed. Due to overpopulation of the {{Term|BotW|Retsam Forest|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Dantz|link}} has begun to task travelers with the {{Term|BotW|Deer Hunting|link}} {{Term|BotW|Mini-Game|link}}.<ref name="Dantz"/> Using {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can mount and ride {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Buck}} and {{Plural|BotW|Mountain Doe}} like he would a {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Deer are cautious by nature so it can be tricky getting close, but if you can capture one, you may be able to take it for a ride.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> When mounted, the {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} will try to buck {{Term|BotW|Link}} off until it is soothed, at which point it will allow him to ride it. Unlike {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is unable to spur the {{Plural|BotW|Deer}} while riding to get a speed boost, and they cannot be registered at {{Plural|BotW|Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Looks like you've caught yourself a new steed! Do you want to register it? Not a problem. Let me just— Oh, for the love of Hylia... That's not a horse! We only board horses here, stranger. No exceptions.|Tasseren|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} dismounts from the {{Term|BotW|Deer}}, it will flee. Riding a {{Term|BotW|Deer}} in this way is necessary for the completion of "{{Term|BotW|The Crowned Beast|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} to gain access to {{Term|BotW|Mezza Lo Shrine|link}}.
