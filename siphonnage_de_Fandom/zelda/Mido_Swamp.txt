{{Term/Store|Mido Swamp||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Mido Swamp'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Mido Swamp is a small mire in the northeastern section of the [[Great Hyrule Forest]]. It is the end section of the [[Korok Trial]] and [[Shrine Quest]] "[[The Test of Wood]]". If Link falls into the Swamp, he will drown and be sent to the beginning of the Trial. The Swamp is punctuated by strips of land that form a makeshift pathway through the mud. On the far end of the Swamp is [[Maag Halan Shrine]], the end of the Trial.

Surrounding the swamp are multiple [[Stalkoblin]]s that shoot [[Fire Arrow]]s at Link as he attempts to cross the mire.

==Nomenclature==
Mido Swamp is named for [[Mido]], the self-proclaimed boss of the [[Kokiri]] from {{OoT}}. Its name is also similar to [[Midoro Swamp]] from ''[[Zelda II: The Adventure of Link|Adventure of Link]]''.
