{{Term/Store|Steamed Fruit|Steamed Fruits|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Steamed Fruit}}<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Steamed Fruits are made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a fruit with any vegetable, herb, or flower together in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}. Their default version can be made in two different ways. The first way requires {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to cook {{Plural|BotW|Hyrule Herb|link}} with either {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Palm Fruit|link}}, or {{Plural|BotW|Wildberry|link}}, as none of these ingredients provide a status effect. Link can also use two ingredients with differing effects, as these will cancel each other out. Eating these meals will heal some of his {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} to his {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. They're regional dishes, made by steaming near-ripe fruits in the leaves of plants.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Both the kind of fruit and the type of herb, vegetable, or flower used will effect the type of Steamed Fruit made:

* '''Hearty Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Radish|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Big Hearty Radish|link}}, or other hearty ingredients. By eating them, all of [[Link|Link's]] Hearts will be healed, and he will be granted temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>

* '''Enduring Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Enduring">{{Cite|{{Big|Enduring Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Restores and overfills your Stamina Wheel.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Endura Carrot|link}} or other enduring ingredients. They can be eaten to completely restore and overfill Link's {{Term|BotW|Stamina Wheel|link}}.<ref name="Enduring"/>

* '''Hasty Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Hasty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hasty Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level movement-speed boost.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Carrot|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Swift Violet|link}}, or other hasty ingredients. When eaten, Link's movement speed will be boosted.<ref name="Hasty"/>

* '''Spicy Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Spicy">{{Cite|{{Big|Spicy Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level cold resistance.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Spicy Pepper|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Warm Safflina|link}}, or other spicy ingredients. Link can eat them to gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Spicy"/>

* '''Electro Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Electro">{{Cite|{{Big|Electro Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level electricity resistance.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Voltfruit|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Electric Safflina|link}}, or other electric ingredients. If they're eaten, they'll grant Link one level of [[Shock Resistance]].<ref name="Electro"/>

* '''Mighty Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Mighty">{{Cite|{{Big|Mighty Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level attack-power boost.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Thistle|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Mighty Bananas|link}}, or other might ingredients. Eating them will provide Link with a boost to his attack power.<ref name="Mighty"/>

* '''Tough Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Tough">{{Cite|{{Big|Tough Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level defense boost.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Armoranth|link}}, or other tough ingredients. Link will receive a boost to his defense when he eats these fruits.<ref name="Tough"/>

* '''Sneaky Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Sneaky">{{Cite|{{Big|Sneaky Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Grants a low-level stealth boost.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silent Princess|link}}, or other sneaky ingredients. They will grant Link a boost to his {{Term|BotW|Stealth|link}} when he eats them.<ref name="Sneaky"/>

* '''Chilly Steamed Fruit''':<ref name="Chilly">{{Cite|{{Big|Chilly Steamed Fruit}}<br/>Grants low-level heat resistance.<br/>A regional dish made by steaming near-ripened fruits in the leaves of fragrant plants.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using {{Plural|BotW|Hydromelon|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Cool Safflina|link}}, or other chilly ingredients. When eaten, Link will gain one level of {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}.<ref name="Chilly"/>
