"{{Term/Store|Shrouded Shrine||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Shrouded Shrine}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Thyphlo Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} steps into the {{Term|BotW|Thyphlo Ruins|link}}, he will be transported to the darkened entrance. He hears the voice of the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai|link}}, who instructs him to find the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in the ruins.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Traveler from beyond these woods, you are now faced with a trial... Find the shrine hidden in these dark ruins...}}|Ketoh Wawai|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}.

Ahead of {{Term|BotW|Link}} is a lit {{Term|BotW|Torchlight|link}} in the shape of a bird. By following the {{Plural|BotW|Torchlight}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will eventually come across a {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} with a {{Term|BotW|Concave Slot|link}}. Past it is a sleeping {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox|link}} with an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} tied around its neck. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must take the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} either by defeating the {{Term|BotW|Blue Hinox}} or sneaking past it and insert it into the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}, which will cause {{Term|BotW|Ketoh Wawai Shrine|link}} to rise from the ground.<ref>{{Cite|When you stole the object from the Hinox hidden in the depths of the ruins and placed it on the pedestal, a shrine appeared!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> After this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
