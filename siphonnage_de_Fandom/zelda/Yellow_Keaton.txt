{{Term/Store|Yellow Keaton|Yellow Keatons|Series, TMC}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 187}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Keaton}}
{{Plural|TMC|Yellow Keaton}} can be found in {{Term|TMC|North Hyrule Field|link}}, {{Term|TMC|Trilby Highlands|link}}, and {{Term|TMC|Western Wood|link}}. They will charge forward at {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} with their daggers; if he is struck, he will lose {{Plural|TMC|Rupee|link}}, though they can be recovered. {{Plural|TMC|Yellow Keaton}} often drop {{Plural|TMC|Blue Rupee|link}} when defeated.

==Trivia==
*{{Plural|Series|Yellow Keaton}} are derived from the mythological Japanese fox creature, [[Wikipedia:Kitsune|kitsune]], notable for their trickery.{{Fact}}
