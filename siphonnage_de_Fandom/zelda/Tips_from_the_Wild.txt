{{Term/Store|Tips from the Wild||Series, BotW}} was a promotional service for the [[Nintendo Switch]] version of {{BotW}}.<ref name="Channel">{{Cite Web|quote= Director Hidemaro Fujibayashi shares some useful tips that may help you along your journey. |author= Nintendo |published= August 8, 2017 |retrieved= November 16, 2017 |url= |title= The Legend of Zelda™: Breath of the Wild - Tips from the Wild|site= |type=}}</ref>

==Overview==
Tips from the Wild was a weekly promotional service for the [[Nintendo Switch]] that distributed tips and items digitally via the eponymous News Channel.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Hello everyone.<br/><br/>I'm Hidemaro Fujibayashi, Director of The Legend of Zelda™: Breath of the Wild.<br/><br/>Welcome to our new channel for the game, where I hope to share some helpful game tips. People have already found all sorts of things to do in the game, but maybe you will learn something new.<br/><br/>And as a special thank you, if you own The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild (either as a physical game card or the digital version), you will be able to receive items directly from some articles*. For example, an article about a recipe may also gift you a few of the ingredients to get you started.<br/><br/>Thank you very much for your interest in the game. Please look forward to more articles!<br/><br/>{{Color|BotW Gray|*This feature is compatible with Version 1.3.1 and above of the game. You can update your game by highlighting the game icon from the Home Menu, pressing the + button, and selecting "Software Update." Internet connection required.}}|author= Hidemaro Fujibayashi |published= August 8, 2017 |retrieved=October 5, 2017|url= |title= Welcome to The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild tips channel.|site= |type=}}</ref> Tips from the Wild was presented by the director of {{BotW}}, [[Hidemaro Fujibayashi]].<ref name="Channel"/>

There were certain limitations on receiving tips and items from the Tips from the Wild Channel:
*Link must have obtained the [[Paraglider]] in order to begin receiving items.<ref>{{Cite|- In order to receive the items, you must already have the paraglider item.|Tips from the Wild|BotW}}</ref>
*Items could not have been received while in select locations, such as [[Ancient Shrine]]s, [[Divine Beast]]s and on "certain terrain."<ref>{{Cite|-There are some locations in the game where you cannot receive these items, such as shrines, dungeons, and certain terrain.|Tips from the Wild|BotW}}</ref>
*Tips from the Wild was only available from Version 1.3.1 and onwards.<ref>{{Cite|This feature is compatible with Version 1.3.1 and above. You can update your game by highlighting the game icon from the HOME Menu, pressing the + Button, and selecting Software Update. Internet connection required.|Tips from the Wild|BotW}}</ref>
Additionally, Tips from the Wild was regionally locked and therefore intended only for the regions listed in given posts.<ref>{{Cite|This news is distributed by Nintendo for our Nintendo Account holders in the Americas.|Tips from the Wild|BotW}}</ref></br> The final article was released on December 26, 2017.
