The {{Term/Store|Signpost Maze||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a location in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite|Signpost Maze|System|LANS}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|LANS|Signpost Maze}} is an area with numerous {{Plural|LANS|Signpost|link}}. Each {{Term|LANS|Signpost}} points {{Term|LANS|Link}} in the direction of the next one to read. After successfully following all of the {{Plural|LANS|Signpost}}, {{Term|LANS|Link}} enters {{Term|LANS|Mamu|link}}'s home. After being paid 300 {{Plural|LANS|Rupee|link}}, he teaches {{Term|LANS|Link}} the "{{Term|LANS|Frog's Song of Soul|link}}". The {{Term|LANS|Signpost Maze}} cannot be solved without the {{Term|LANS|Hookshot|link}}, which is required to reach the {{Term|LANS|Signpost}} in the southwest corner of the area.

The {{Term|LANS|Signpost Maze}} is fairly isolated from the rest of {{Term|LANS|Koholint Island|link}}, and can only be reached by crossing over a five-hole gap with the {{Term|LANS|Hookshot}} from {{Term|LANS|Ukuku Prairie|link}} in the north.{{Note|In all versions of {{LA}} prior to {{LA|NS}}, the gap was only three holes across and could be crossed by using the {{Term|LANS|Pegasus Boots|link}} in tandem with {{Term|LANS|Roc's Feather|link}}. This gap was increased by two pits in {{LA|NS|-}}, making it impossible to jump across naturally.}} Other areas which border it, but are inaccessible from it, include the {{Term|LANS|Pothole Field|link}} to the east, {{Term|LANS|Toronbo Shores|link}} to the south, and the {{Term|LANS|South of the Village|link}} to the west.
