The {{Term/Store|Buyer||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is a character in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 238 {{Exp Game|OoT}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Buyer can be found in [[Hyrule Castle Town]] when [[Link]] is a child, and in [[Kakariko Village]] seven years later. He will pay 50 [[Rupee]]s for a [[Bottle]] of [[Bug]]s, 100 Rupees for a [[Fish]], and 150 Rupees for [[Blue Fire]]. Link cannot sell him [[Poe Soul]]s, [[Potion]]s, [[Lon Lon Milk]], or bottled [[Fairies]].

==Trivia==
