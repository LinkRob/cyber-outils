The {{Term/Store|GBA Nature Ring||Series, OoS}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Location and Uses==
The GBA Nature Ring can be bought for 100 [[Rupee]]s at the [[Advance Shop]] in {{OoS}} only when playing on a [[Game Boy Advance]]. The [[Magic Ring]] can be appraised at [[Vasu Jewelers]] for 20 [[Rupee|Rupees]]. It has no effect when worn. The GBA Nature Ring can be transferred to {{OoA}} by talking to either the [[Blue Snake|Blue]] or [[Red Snake]].
