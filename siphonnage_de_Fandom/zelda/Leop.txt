{{Term/Store|Leop||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}} and ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|Leop|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Leop}} is an older {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} who lives in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. He diligently stands outside {{Term|BotW|The Great Ton Pu Inn|link}}, welcoming visitors and imploring them to stay.<ref>{{Cite|Come on in!|Leop|BotW}}</ref> He can be found at the base of the {{Term|BotW|Inn|link}}'s stairs at most hours of the day and night, greeting {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} if he approaches.<ref>{{Cite|Good day.|Leop|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Good to see ya, even at this hour.|Leop|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Leop}} will retreat inside if it begins to {{Term|BotW|Rain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Good to see ya, even in this bad weather.|Leop|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Starting to rain...|Leop|BotW}}</ref>

According to {{Term|BotW|Leop}}, {{Term|BotW|The Great Ton Pu Inn}} is the best place in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} to rest.<ref>{{Cite|Best place to rest up in all of Hateno Village is {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}}!|Leop|BotW}}</ref> He suggests travelers speak with {{Term|BotW|Prima|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Inn}}'s proprietor, if they wish to stay.<ref>{{Cite|If you plan to stay, just talk to the clerk {{Color|BotW Red|across the counter}}.|Leop|BotW}}</ref> When spoken to again, he will encourage {{Term|BotW|Link}} to spend some time at the {{Term|BotW|Inn}}.<ref>{{Cite|Mr. Traveler, don't give it a second thought—just take a load off at the inn.|Leop|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If ya need to rest those bones, go check out {{Color|BotW Blue|Ton Pu Inn}}.|Leop|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Leop}} while carrying the {{Term|BotW|Blue Flame|link}}, he asks if {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} is unable to get around on her own anymore.<ref>{{Cite|Can old-woman Purah not get around on her own anymore? Is she making the youth carry things around for her?|Leop|BotW}}</ref> Realizing he has not seen {{Term|BotW|Purah}} in some time, he wonders if the rumors he heard about her are true.<ref>{{Cite|I haven't seen Purah lately... I wonder if those rumors are true...|Leop|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that {{Term|BotW|Purah}} is doing well, {{Term|BotW|Leop}} will be relieved, as the people in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village}} tend to get worried easily.<ref>{{Cite|[She's doing great!] Well, that's a relief to hear. Us village folk get worried so easily, y'know?|Leop|BotW}}</ref> Alternatively, if {{Term|BotW|Link}} has difficulty answering him, {{Term|BotW|Leop}} shares the rumor that {{Term|BotW|Purah}}'s granddaughter has been visiting her, believing {{Term|BotW|Link}} to have given it away.<ref>{{Cite|[...] A kid... Ahh! Aha! You gave it away! So the rumor about Purah's grandkid visiting her is true after all?!|Leop|BotW}}</ref> He is unable to think of any other reason that a child would be staying at the {{Term|BotW|Hateno Ancient Tech Lab|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|I can't think of any other reason why {{Color|BotW Red|a young girl}} would be hanging around the {{Color|BotW Blue|research lab}}.|Leop|BotW}}</ref>
