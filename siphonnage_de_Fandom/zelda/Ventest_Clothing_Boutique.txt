The {{Term/Store|Ventest Clothing Boutique||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} {{Term|BotW|Shop|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|- Ventest Clothing Boutique -|Sign|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Ventest Clothing Boutique is a shop in {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} managed by [[Sophie]], a girl who stands quietly in the corner of the store.<ref>{{Cite|Did you need something? [You like this corner?] ... ... OH! Am I nestled too far in the corner? Is it hard to see me? I know it's a little weird, but I just feel more confident over here.|Sophie|BotW}}</ref> The Ventest Clothing Boutique notably sells two complete Sets of [[Armor]]: the [[Hylian Set]] and the [[Soldier's Set]].

The Ventest Clothing Boutique was formerly operated by Sophie's father, [[Seldon]], who now simply paces outside the shop offering tour guides to travelers.<ref>{{Cite|You've got the look of a traveler! Is this your first time in Hateno Village? Well, you're in luck, because you have a lifelong resident to show you around!|Seldon|BotW}}</ref> As such, it is the final destination of "Seldon's Village Tours" and Link is welcomed to visit the store whenever he has the time to do so.<ref>{{Cite|<small>*pant pant...*</small> This is the {{Color|BotW Blue|clothing store, Ventest Clothing}}! They sell...well, I'm sure you can guess what they sell. To be straight with you, this is actually my store! So come hang out when you've got some time. Stock up here first before heading over to {{Color|BotW Blue|Kochi Dye Shop}}. Whew... I'm pretty much beat! Let's call this the end of the tour, yah? <small>{{Color|BotW Gray|Just don't have the pep for tour guiding like I used to...}}</small>|Seldon|BotW}}</ref> If it is raining outside, Seldon will run inside and sit at the work station located at the back of the store. The Ventest Clothing Boutique also features a side room with functions as a joint kitchen and bedroom.

==Nomenclature==
Like its neighboring shop, the [[East Wind]], the Ventest Clothing Boutique is thematically named after wind. "Ventest" is a corruption of ''ventus'', Latin for "wind."

Another likely origin, given the association of France with high fashion, is the French phrase "Vent d'Est", which translates literally to "East Wind".
