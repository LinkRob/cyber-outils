==Locations and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The Snowquill Tunic is a piece of [[Armor]] that can be bought for 600 [[Rupee]]s at the [[Brazen Beak]]. When worn, it provides one level of Cold Resistance. The Snowquill Tunic can be enhanced by a [[Great Fairy]] and can be [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]]. The Snowquill Tunic, alongside the [[Snowquill Headdress]] and [[Snowquill Trousers]], compose the [[Snowquill Set]]. When each piece of the Set has been enhanced at least two times by a Great Fairy and the full Set is worn, it provides an Unfreezable [[Set Bonus]].
