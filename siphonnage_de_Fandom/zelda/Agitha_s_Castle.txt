{{Term/Store|Agitha's Castle||Series, TP}} is a location in {{TP}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|TPHD Purple|AGITHA'S CASTLE}}|Sign|TPHD}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Home to the self-proclaimed princess [[Agitha]], it is located on the south road of [[Hyrule Castle Town]]. When [[Link]] meets Agitha inside her Castle, she mistakes him for a bug and goes on to tell him that she sent invitations to 24 [[Golden Bug]]s around [[Hyrule]] for the ball. Unfortunately, it seems none have arrived, and she asks for the young hero's help in bringing all the bugs to the castle.<ref>{{Cite|I've invited the {{Color|TPHD Red|24 golden bugs}} of the world to the ball, but… I don't know if they got lost on the way or what...but they're not here, that's for sure. If you see them, would you please bring them here to {{Color|TPHD Red|Agitha's Castle}}? And tell them the princess awaits! Oh, boy, if you have a {{Color|TPHD Red|bug}} with you now, please give it to me. The ball must begin!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> If Link brings her a Golden Bug, she will reward Link with [[Rupee]]s for his troubles: 50 Rupees for bringing a bug, and 100 Rupees for bringing in both the male and female pairs of the same bug.<ref>{{Cite|If any bug becomes part of a {{Color|TPHD Red|pair}}, I think I'll be able to share {{Color|TPHD Red|GREAT happiness}} with you!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> The first time that he brings Agitha a bug, Link is given a [[Big Wallet]].<ref>{{Cite|…You actually went bug hunting for me, didn't you? To be honest, I doubted you would… I guess some might consider me a poor-mannered princess… From now on, if you bring me insects, I'll share my {{Color|TPHD Red|happiness}} with you. You can keep it in this!|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref> Bringing all 24 bugs to the Castle rewards Link with the [[Giant Wallet]]. If Link tries to walk out of the Castle even though he still has bugs on hand, she will mention it as he leaves.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I know you have bugs…}}|Agitha|TPHD}}</ref>

The inside of Agitha's Castle reflects her deep passion for bugs. Plastered all over the walls are posters of various insects, and there are many empty [[Bottle]]s as well. In the middle of the room is a giant tree where many of the bugs can be found once Link brings them in. However, extra bugs can be seen in Agitha's Castle later on.
