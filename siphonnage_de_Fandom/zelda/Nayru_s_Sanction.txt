'''Nayru's Sanction''' is a [[Key Item]] from {{EoW}}.
==Characteristics==
==={{EoW|-}}===
{{Manual|EoW|Nayru's Sanction|A mystical sanction entrusted to you by the goddess Nayru, given only to those who have proven their wisdom.<ref name="Nayru's Sanction">{{Cite|A mystical sanction entrusted to you by the goddess Nayru, given only to those who have proven their wisdom.|Notebook|EoW}}</ref>}}
Nayru's Sanction is a [[Key Item]] obtained after defeating [[Skorchill]] in the [[Lanayru Temple]]. Following the battle, the goddess [[Nayru]] appears to [[Princess Zelda]], granting her Nayru's Sanction. It is one of the three blessings required to open the path to the [[Triforce|Prime Energy]].
