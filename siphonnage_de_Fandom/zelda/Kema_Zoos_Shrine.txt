The {{Term/Store|Kema Zoos Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Kema Zoos Shrine}}<br/>A Delayed Puzzle}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''A Delayed Puzzle''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos Shrine}} is found by completing "{{Term|BotW|The Silent Swordswomen|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can receive the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|display=Quest}} by talking to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} soldier {{Term|BotW|Laine|link}}, who will tell him about the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There are {{Color|BotW Red|statues of swordswomen}} scattered across Gerudo Desert. They're remnants of ages long past.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> She will also share a superstition that the statues point to the location of a hidden treasure.<ref>{{Cite|People talk about the statues pointing to the hiding place of ancient treasure...but that's just superstition.|Laine|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} must follow the {{Plural|BotW|Swordswoman Statue|display=Statues}} through the {{Term|BotW|Sandstorm|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|West Barrens|link}}, eventually finding {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos Shrine}} and completing the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}}.<ref>{{Cite|You followed the guidance of the swordswomen statues to find the treasure amidst the desert sandstorms. The prize turned out to be an ancient shrine!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> Upon entering, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}} {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos|link}} can be heard offering the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine...I am Kema Zoos. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Kema Zoos|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos Shrine}} consists of one big room with an electrically charged metal sphere slowly bouncing back and forth between two {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}}. To get the sphere to stop, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must either use the {{Term|BotW|Stasis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on it while it is flying through the air, at which point he must shoot it with an {{Term|BotW|Arrow|link}} to throw off its trajectory, or use the Stasis Rune while it is resting in a launcher, such that it will drop instead of launching after the launcher extends. It will then drop to the floor below, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} can move it with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can place the sphere near a hidden switch on the left side of the room to open a portcullis, revealing a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} holding a {{Term|BotW|Moonlight Scimitar|link}}. To complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must place the sphere next to the switch directly to the right of portcullis containing the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}. This will open the door to this room, where the {{Term|BotW|Monk}} {{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos}} waits for {{Term|BotW|Link}}.

{{Term|BotW|Kema Zoos}} commends {{Term|BotW|Link}} for his resourcefulness and gives him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Kema Zoos|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} then wishes {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Kema Zoos|BotW}}</ref>Completing this Shrine will cause the surrounding Sandstorm to end.
