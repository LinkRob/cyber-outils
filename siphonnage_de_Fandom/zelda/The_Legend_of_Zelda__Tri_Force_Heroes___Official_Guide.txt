'''''The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes — Official Guide''''' is a strategy guide released by [[Prima Games]] for {{TFH}}.

==Features==
{{Stub}}
The books features 256 pages, and contains a complete walkthrough of {{TFH}}, strategies for {{Plural|TFH|Boss|link}}, maps, and a listing of collectibles.
