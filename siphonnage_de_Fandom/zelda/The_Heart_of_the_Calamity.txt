{{Term/Store|The Heart of the Calamity||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 77 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|The Heart of the Calamity|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|The Heart of the Calamity}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} is given 25 minutes to defeat six {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} one by one. They are {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Fireblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Waterblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Harbinger Ganon|link}}, and {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}}. They appear in that order, and only after the {{Term|HWAoC|Enemy}} before them is defeated. When {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon}} is defeated, the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends. 

The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and also without using any {{Plural|HWAoC|Item|link}}. After completing the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Ancient Core|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, 700 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
