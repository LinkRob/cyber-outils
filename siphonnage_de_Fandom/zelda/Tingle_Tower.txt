==Features and Overview==
==={{TWW|-}}===
{{Stub|TWW}}

==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Term|FSA|Tingle's Tower}} is an unlockable multiplayer {{Term|FSA|Mini-Game|link}} gallery in {{FSA}}. Simply progressing through the Hyrulean Adventure mode with multiple players will gain access to {{Term|FSA|Tingle's Tower}} and new {{Plural|FSA|Mini-Game}}. {{Term|FSA|Tingle|link}} will carry the {{Term|FSA|Tingle's Tower|display= Tower}} to a location in each section of the overworld map. 

{{Term|FSA|Tingle}} will reward the player obtaining the most {{Plural|FSA|Force Gem|link}} as well as the player who meets other goals in the {{Plural|FSA|Mini-Game}}.

==Games==
*'''Bucking Broncos''' – A horse racing course. Collect the most Force Gems or win the race for a Force Fairy.
*'''Cucco Wranglers''' – Much like an [[Anju]] cuccoo game. Round up the most and toss them into the pen.
*'''Monster Hunting''' – Slay the most enemies. Beat your friends up a little too.
*'''[[Hammer Tag]]''' – When "it," swing your hammer to tag other Links
*'''Volley by Golly''' – Slash the bouncing ball when it's your color
*'''Mole Melee''' – Colored moles pop up from the ground, Hammer your color in.
*'''Monster Hunting Returns''' – More difficult Monster Hunting
*'''Bonus Course''' – Tingle's own mini dungeon
