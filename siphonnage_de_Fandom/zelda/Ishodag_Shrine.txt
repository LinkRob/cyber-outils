The '''{{PAGENAME}}''', designated by the Trial name ''A Windy Device'', is a [[Shrine of Light]] in {{TotK}}. It corresponds to the [[Gadohsi Lightroot]] in [[The Depths]]. Just outside the shrine, there is a chest on top of a spiky hill. You can access it by airgliding to it using the two fans nearby.
