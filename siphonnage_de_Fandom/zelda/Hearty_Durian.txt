==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Hearty Durian}}
{{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian}} are found growing on trees in wooded areas of the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands}}. They're purchasable from the travelling merchant, {{Term|BotW|Chumin|link}}, as well as {{Term|BotW|Lorn's Fruit Stand|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. Red {{Plural|BotW|Hinox|link}} have the potential to drop {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian}} when killed.

{{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian}} can be eaten raw to restore three {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}, but they can also be roasted by direct flames to become {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Hearty Durian|link}}. If one or more are {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link|display=cooked}} into a dish in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}, they will temporarily increase {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}'s maximum {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}}. At the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian}} are one of the ingredients that can be used to make Light Yellow {{Term|BotW|Dye|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian}}, as well as other fruits, can be used in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make {{Term|BotW|Fruit Pie|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fruitcake|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Honeyed Fruits|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Simmered Fruit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Steamed Fruit|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Fruit and Mushroom Mix|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Copious Simmered Fruit|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
In {{HWAoC}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian}} can be bought from {{Term|HWAoC|Saezuri|link}} for 36 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}} and from {{Term|HWAoC|Dayfah|link}} for 60 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}} and sold at every service for 15 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee}}. They can also sometimes be obtained by destroying {{Plural|HWAoC|Wooden Box|link}}. {{Plural|HWAoC|Hearty Durian}} are a required ingredient in cooking {{Term|HWAoC|Honeyed Fruits}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Dubious Food|link}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
* The {{Term|BotW|Hearty Durian}}'s nickname, "king of fruits", is a reference to real-life {{wp|durians|Durian}}, which have the same nickname.
* Eating {{Plural|BotW|Hearty Durian}} whilst drinking alcoholic beverages is ill-advised, although reasons why are not provided in-game. This is another trend it shares with the real-life durian.
