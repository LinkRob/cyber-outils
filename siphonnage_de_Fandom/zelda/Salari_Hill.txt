{{Term/Store|Salari Hill||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Salari Hill'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Salari Hill is located in [[Hyrule Field]].

==Nomenclature==
Salari Hill derives its name from ''celery''. Salari Hill shares its Japanese name, {{Romanize|マリッタ|Maritta}}, with the [[Maritta Exchange Ruins]], [[Salari Plain]], and the [[Serenne Stable]].
