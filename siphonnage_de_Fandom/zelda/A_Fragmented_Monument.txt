"{{Term/Store|A Fragmented Monument||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Fragmented Monument}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Palmorae Ruins}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can obtain this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} by speaking to {{Term|BotW|Garini|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins|link}} after obtaining the {{Term|BotW|Camera|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}} attempts to initiate the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} without the {{Term|BotW|Camera}}, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} will simply wonder what was written on the stone monument without acknowledging his presence.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm... What was written on this stone monument?|Garini|BotW}}</ref>}} {{Term|BotW|Garini}} will be scared by {{Term|BotW|Link}} at first,<ref>{{Cite|Ahhh!|Garini|BotW}}</ref> but he apologizes and asks him if he needs something.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry about that. I was in the zone. Did you need something?|Garini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what he is doing, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} will explain that he was examining the monument.<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] |Garini|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Garini}} notes that the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins}} have a spiritual aura to them.<ref>{{Cite|This place has a very...spiritual aura to it. Do you feel it?|Garini|BotW}}</ref> Recently, he noticed that the stone monument has writing engraved on it,<ref>{{Cite|I recently realized that there is writing engraved on this stone monument...|Garini|BotW}}</ref> so now he is trying to decipher what is written.<ref>{{Cite|So now I'm trying to figure out what exactly is written here.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} offers to help, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} will note that he is also the curious type.<ref>{{Cite|[Can I help?] Ah, so you're the curious type, too.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> He explains that the stone monument was once whole, but an earthquake lead to it becoming shattered.<ref>{{Cite|This stone monument was once much larger, but it broke apart during an earthquake a while back.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to find the missing {{Plural|BotW|Monument Shard|link}} that have been scattered and return with a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of them.<ref>{{Cite|If you really want to help, help me find the missing fragments, will you? They'll be scattered all around. I think the fragments had the same kind of script carved into them... If you find a fragment, please draw a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}} of it and bring it to me. I'll be here finishing my examination of this stone monument!|Garini|BotW}}</ref> To aid with {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s search, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} informs him that the text on the {{Plural|BotW|Monument Shard}} glows at night, making them easier to see.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh, right. And the words on the stone monument glow with an eerie light at night... That should make them easier to find.|Garini|BotW}}</ref> Additionally, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} hints that one {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard|display=Shard}} is close by.<ref>{{Cite|One of them should be right around here. Please find that first!|Garini|BotW}}</ref>

The first {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins}}, near some {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} east of the monument. The second {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard}} is located among some rocks and flotsam to the west of the {{Term|BotW|Palmorae Ruins|display=Ruins}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you find any fragments of the stone monument? [I sure did.] Oh! Hmm... {{Small|Some of the fragments must still be missing.}} Well, keep looking. {{Small|I suppose the waves may have washed some away or pulled them underwater...}} |Garini|BotW}}</ref> The third and final {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard}} sits beneath a {{Term|BotW|Palm Tree|link}} at the tip of {{Term|BotW|Soka Point|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hrm...hrm... Hmm... {{Small|Some of the fragments must still be missing.}} Well, keep looking. {{Small|I wonder if the currents are strong enough to have carried a fragment all the way to Cape Cresia or Soka Point...}}|Garini|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to {{Term|BotW|Garini}}, he will ask if he found the {{Plural|BotW|Monument Shard}}.<ref>{{Cite|Did you find any fragments of the stone monument?|Garini|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} confirms that he has, he will show the {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} to him.<ref>{{Cite|[I sure did.] Oh! Amazing! Show me the next one! Hrm...hrm... Perfect! You have more?! That's amazing!|Garini|BotW}}</ref> Now that he has seen all of the {{Term|BotW|Monument Shard|display=Shards}}, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} believes he will be able to decipher the entire monument.<ref>{{Cite|......... Excellent. Thanks to you, I've got a good shot at deciphering the entire monument. Give me a second to see if I can put this all together...|Garini|BotW}}</ref> When he successfully figures it out, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} will share the text with {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wow! Thanks to you, I've deciphered the entire passage!|Garini|BotW}}</ref> It states, "When the two find their place and kneel in reverence, the shrine will reveal itself."<ref>{{Cite|"When the two find their place and kneel in reverence, the shrine will reveal itself."|Garini|BotW}}</ref> He has no idea what that means, though.<ref>{{Cite|...Ah! I...haven't the foggiest what that's supposed to mean.|Garini|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} kneels down on one of the nearby {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Garini}} will be shocked.<ref>{{Cite|Huh?! ...Is that it?!|Garini|BotW}}</ref> He will then kneel on the other {{Term|BotW|Pedestal}}. Both {{Plural|BotW|Pedestal}} will activate, which causes {{Term|BotW|Kah Yah Shrine|link}} to rise from the sand.<ref>{{Cite|You knelt on one platform as Garini took his place on the other, causing an ancient shrine to appear!|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
