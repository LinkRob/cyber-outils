{{Term/Store|Baris||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Baris}} is seated inside {{Term|TMC|Mama's Cafe|link}} and remains there throughout the entire game. He is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available. He describes himself as an easygoing person,<ref name="Easygoing"/> and espouses philosophical musings on silence, truth, and inevitability as the game progresses.<ref>{{Cite|Talking only to oneself... is an exercise in eloquent silence.|Baris|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What is truth? When we understand this, then we will conquer even time itself...|Baris|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Everything with a beginning also has an end. To put it another way, all things move toward their end... Even from the moment they begin. The end is near. For me, as well as for you.|Baris|TMC}}</ref>
