The {{Term/Store|Red Snake||Series}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.{{Name Ref|OoS, OoA}} The Red Snake appears alongside the [[Blue Snake]] at [[Vasu Jewelers]].

==Purpose==
As for the [[Blue Snake]], the Red Snake's sole purpose is to allow [[Link]] to transfer all his appraised [[Magic Ring]]s between [[Linked Game|linked]] {{OoA}} and {{OoS}} game files. The Red Snake allows Link to do so via Ring Secrets. By asking the Red Snake for a Ring Secret (a 15-character password) in the game where his rings are kept, he can then tell the secret to the Red Snake in the opposing game to have them transferred to that file.<ref>{{Cite|If you save the secret the Red Snake told you in an Oracle of Seasons game you played, and tell it to the Red Snake here, you can bring rings found in your Oracle of Seasons game here. In the same way, you can take rings from here to your Oracle of Seasons here.|All About Ring Secrets|OoA}}</ref> Rings can be transferred both ways. However, the Ring Secret will only work in a game that has been linked with the game where the Ring Secret was obtained, or else the Red Snake will not accept the secret.<ref>{{Cite|If you haven't finished your quest or used a secret to visit Labrynna, your ring secret will not work.|All About Ring Secrets|OoA}}</ref>

As an alternative, the Blue Snake can also be used to transfer rings without the use of secrets if two [[Game Boy Color]]s and a {{Wp|Game Link Cable}} are available.<ref>{{Cite|Good news! With a Game Link cable and two Game Boy Color systems, you can bring rings here without saving your secret!|All About Ring Secrets|OoA}}</ref>
