"{{Term/Store|Leviathan Bones||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Leviathan Bones}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Serenne Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Serenne Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Akrah|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Garshon|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Onya|link}} discussing the {{Plural|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Object)|link|display=remains}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Leviathan|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|How did they go extinct?|Akrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Bones...|Garshon|botW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Leviathan...|Onya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Onya}} theorizes that they went extinct because of an ice age,<ref>{{Cite|My theory is that the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathans}} of ancient times went extinct due to an extended ice age...|Onya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Akrah}} believes they went extinct because of a volcanic eruption,<ref>{{Cite|My theory is that the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathans}} of ancient times went extinct due to a violent volcanic eruption...|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Garshon}} thinks the extinction occurred due to a drought.<ref>{{Cite|My theory is that the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathans}} of ancient times went extinct due to a sudden, cataclysmic drought long ago!|Garshon|BotW}}</ref> When the three of them notice {{Term|BotW|Link}} listening, {{Term|BotW|Akrah}} explains that they are scientists trying to decipher the reason the {{Plural|BotW|Leviathan}} went extinct.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh? Pardon me. I've gotten ahead of myself. *ahem* Why did the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathans}} go extinct? That is the question we scientists are trying to answer.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref>

Unfortunately, the three have been unable to locate any {{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Object)}}, which means their claims have no evidence.<ref>{{Cite|However, we haven't yet located any leviathan remains, so we're stuck with theories for the time being.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> They do have an idea of where the remains can be found: the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Mountains|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Hebra|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Gerudo (Region)|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|But we do have a general idea of where such remains might be found... The {{Color|BotW Blue|Eldin Mountains}} in the northeast, the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra region}} in the northwest, and the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo region}} in the southwest.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> Since {{Term|BotW|Link}} looks like an adventurer,<ref>{{Cite|You've got the look of someone who's done a fair bit of adventuring.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Akrah}} asks him to find the {{Term|BotW|Leviathan Bones (Object)}} and bring them a {{Term|BotW|Picture|link}} of them.<ref>{{Cite|Would you be willing to find these {{Color|BotW Red|leviathan bones}} and draw a {{Color|BotW Red|picture}} of them?|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> Specifically, he wants to see the heads of the skeletons,<ref>{{Cite|What I especially want to see is the {{Color|BotW Red|head}} of the animal. If you can get just a bit of it in the picture, that'd be adequate.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> and he promises {{Term|BotW|Link}} 300 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} if he can do this for him.<ref>{{Cite|Do this for me, and I'll give you {{Color|BotW Red|300 rupees}} as a reward.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} accepts, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

The {{Term|BotW|Eldin Great Skeleton|link}} is located along the northern edge of {{Term|BotW|Eldin (Region)|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Hebra Great Skeleton|link}} is found inside a cave under the {{Term|BotW|Hebra North Summit|link}}. Finally, the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton|link}} is located in the southwest of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}, near {{Term|BotW|Dragon's Exile|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must take a {{Term|BotW|Picture}} of each of them for the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can show his {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} to their corresponding researchers. {{Term|BotW|Akrah}} wants to see the {{Term|BotW|Eldin Great Skeleton}},<ref>{{Cite|I really need to see the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathan bones}} from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Eldin region}}.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Garshon}} wants to see the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Great Skeleton}},<ref>{{Cite|I must see that {{Color|BotW Red|leviathan}} skeleton from the {{Color|BotW Blue|Gerudo region}}...|Garshon|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Onya}} wants to see the {{Term|BotW|Hebra Great Skeleton}}.<ref>{{Cite|I'd really like to see the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathan}} skeleton in the {{Color|BotW Blue|Hebra region}}...|Onya|BotW}}</ref> Each of them praise {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} and claim they will help with proving their theories.<ref>{{Cite|This is...wonderful! You drew this?! With this, I'll finally be able to prove that the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathans}} went extinct as a result of a massive volcanic eruption!|Akrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I cannot believe my eyes! You are REALLY good at drawing! With this, I'll finally be able to prove that the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathans}} went extinct due to a catastrophic drought!|Garshon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Amazing! It's as if I'm seeing the real thing! This is precisely the evidence I needed to prove that the {{Color|BotW Red|leviathans}} went extinct due to a harsh ice age!|Onya|BotW}}</ref> They also ask {{Term|BotW|Link}} to help the other scientists.<ref>{{Cite|Please assist my brothers with their research goals as well.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm sure my brothers would appreciate your help with their pictures too.|Garshon|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please help my other brothers out in the same way!|Onya|BotW}}</ref>

Once all three {{Plural|BotW|Picture}} have been shown, {{Term|BotW|Akrah}} will thank {{Term|BotW|Link}} and reward him with a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! You got {{Color|BotW Red|pictures}} of all three {{Color|BotW Red|leviathan}} skeletons! My thanks. Here's your reward.|Akrah|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will end here, and the three brothers continue their debates.<ref>{{Cite|I shall conduct further research on my hypothesis while making careful study of your picture!|Akrah|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Your pictures will do wonders for our research! Thanks again!|Garshon|BotW}}</ref>
