{{Term/Store|Heart's Escort||HWAoC}} is a level 64 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Heart's Escort|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Heart's Escort}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can only be completed by {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}}, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Diamond|link}}, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Zora Armor|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}}'s objective is to rescue two {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Captain|link}} by defeating their {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. The first is being attacked by three {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Moblin|link}}, and the second is attacked by {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin|link}}. Other, weaker {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} such as{{Plural|HWAoC|Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Ice Lizalfos|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Lizalfos|link}}, and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} can also be found in areas surrounding the two captains. Once the {{Plural|HWAoC|Zora Captain}} are rescued, they reveal {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}} to be fighting alone at the top of the map. The path to {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}'s location is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Lizalfos|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Chuchu}}, and to save him, {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} has to kill the {{Term|HWAoC|Malice Hinox|link}} and both {{Plural|HWAoC|Silver Moblin}} attacking him. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Mipha}} does so.
