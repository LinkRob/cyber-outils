==Location and Uses==
Naydra's Claws can be obtained by shooting the claws of [[Naydra]] as it flies by. If you hit another part of Naydra beforehand, you must wait 24 in game hours to obtain the claw. Naydra can be found on [[Mount Lanayru]], from which it will fly down to either the [[Lanayru Promenade]] or [[Lanayru Bay]]. When [[Cooking]] with Naydraw's Claws, the resulting meal will always be [[Dubious Food]]. Naydra's Claws can be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Light Blue [[Dye]].

Two Claws, as well as five {{Plural|Series|Courser Bee Honey|link}}, are needed to upgrade the [[Tunic of the Wild]] to its second tier at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. One can also be used to upgrade the {{Term|BotW|Fierce Deity Armor|link}} to its second tier.

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Armor''': 1 (0, 1, 0, 0)

* Total for '''Tunic of the Wild''': 2 (0, 2, 0, 0)
