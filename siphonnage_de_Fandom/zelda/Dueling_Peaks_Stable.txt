The {{Term/Store|Dueling Peaks Stable||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}} and ''[[Tears of the Kingdom]]''.<ref>{{Cite|'''Dueling Peaks Stable'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

According to [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]], this stable was only established after the [[Great Calamity]]<ref>''"Dueling Peaks Stable is one of the newly established stables. Considering this whole area was devastated by the Calamity and completely overrun by guardians, it's a good thing there was not a stable here before..."'' – [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] ([[The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom|Tears of the Kingdom]])</ref>.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}} is a {{Term|BotW|Stable|link}} located in [[West Necluda]], situated beside the road running north from the [[Big Twin Bridge]], east of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar Shrine|link}} is in a shallow pool of water between the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}} and the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks}}, providing a convenient [[Travel Gate]] to the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}} when activated. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Plural|BotW|Wooden Box|link}} surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}} that contain {{Plural|BotW|Apple|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}}. A {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} can be found outside of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}. The {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}'s uniqueness comes from the fact that most of the inhabitants are twins, including the workers.<ref>{{Cite|Oh... Uh... Have you noticed it's mostly twins here at this stable? It's kind of creeping me out.|Sagessa|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}} is run by [[Tasseren]], who mans both its {{Term|BotW|Horse|link}} services and lodging. The children [[Shibo]] and [[Darton]] can be found in front of the stabled {{Plural|BotW|Horse}} during the day, and will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} tips about catching and training {{Plural|BotW|Horse}}.<ref>{{Cite|Dad taught us a lot! If you wanna know about horses, just ask us!|Darton|BotW}}</ref> As with all other {{Plural|BotW|Stable}}, [[Beedle]] can occasionally be found here selling his wares. [[Hylian]] travelers [[Agus]], {{Term|BotW|Brokka|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Bugut|link}}, [[Joute]], [[Toma]], and [[Mezer]] can be seen traveling to and from the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}. {{Term|BotW|Brokka}} will occasionally stop at the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}} at night.

[[Hino]] can be found near the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}'s entrance, studying the mystery of the {{Term|BotW|Blood Moon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Whenever the clock strikes midnight on unlucky nights, the sky turns blood red. That's the {{Color|BotW Red|blood moon}}.|Hino|BotW}}</ref> [[Sagessa]] can be seen sitting next to the {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot}}, talking about [[Elixir]]s,<ref>{{Cite|If you're going to be traveling, you should know a thing or two about {{Color|BotW Blue|elixirs}}.|Sagessa|BotW}}</ref> or standing across from {{Term|BotW|Ha Dahamar Shrine}}, remarking on the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}'s new glowing feature.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|shrine}} only recently started glowing, but it's been there for something like 10,000 years.|Sagessa|BotW}}</ref> While running back to the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}} for shelter when it [[Rain]]s, she will warn {{Term|BotW|Link}} of the dangers of metal objects.<ref>{{Cite|Aww, dang, it started raining! The lightning in Hyrule tends to strike metal objects... You watch yourself if you go wandering around with a metal weapon.|Sagessa|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can receive the [[Side Quest]] "[[Misko, the Great Bandit]]" by talking to [[Domidak]] while he converses with [[Prissen]] inside the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}. The Side Quest "[[Wild Horses]]" can be gained by talking to [[Rensa]], who is next to Tasseren at the front of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}. Rensa will also give {{Term|BotW|Link}} information about the Wild Horses nearby, and directions to [[Kakariko Village]] and [[Hateno Village]].<ref>{{Cite|[Wild horses?] You're asking me now? There certainly are not as many as in years past, but there are still quite a few around...|Rensa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Kakariko Village?] Kakariko Village is the village where the Sheikah clan lives. It's said that, some time ago, they had advanced techniques that were more powerful than anything anyone had seen. But that power put them at odds with the king at the time, and they were scattered across the land... Anyway, if you leave here and follow the road straight to the north, you'll find a big bridge. Cross that and keep following the path, and you'll come to the Kakariko Village gate.|Rensa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|[Hateno Village?] Hateno Village is a village on the edge of Hyrule. It was largely unaffected by the events of the {{Color|BotW Red|Great Calamity}}, so the people there are pretty easygoing. I hear they even have a {{Color|BotW Blue|research center}}... But folks tend to avoid the weird scientist who lives there... Anyway, head north from here until you reach a fork in the road. Then go east and just follow the road. It's a little far if you walk it, but with a horse, you should get there in under a day!|Rensa|BotW}}</ref>

By befriending the [[Hylian Retriever]] behind the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can be led to a metal {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}} embedded in the ground beneath the [[Tree]] northeast of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}.

A [[Korok]] can be found hiding as [[Leaves with Fairy Dust]] at the very top of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|display=Stable}}.
