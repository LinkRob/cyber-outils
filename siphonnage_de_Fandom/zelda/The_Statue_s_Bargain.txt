"{{Term/Store|The Statue's Bargain||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Statue's Bargain}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Hateno Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
Along the path from {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Firly Pond|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue|link}}. Upon praying to him, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} will remark that there are still some who pay homage to him.<ref>{{Cite|Ahhhh... So there are some who still pay me homage.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> Realizing that {{Term|BotW|Link}} is able to hear him,<ref>{{Cite|[You can talk?!] You don't say... You can hear my voice?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} will explain that he was oncer a dealer in "life and power".<ref>{{Cite|A dealer in life and power... That was me, once.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} did not like what the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} was doing, so she trapped him in a stone statue.<ref>{{Cite|Alas, my life-for-money bargains didn't sit well with the {{Color|BotW Blue|Goddess Hylia}}. She trapped me in this stone statue long, long ago.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} was then discarded by the {{Plural|BotW|Hylian|link}}, and now very few pay him any mind.<ref>{{Cite|The villagers here call me the Horned Statue and don't even bother to wipe away the bird droppings.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref>

Despite the years of ignorance, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} is still willing to deal in life and money.<ref>{{Cite|One would think years upon years of such treatment would have made me mend my ways... One would think wrongly. I have only been biding my time for the moment someone who can hear me appears. Someone such as you. But I've said enough.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> He asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he would like to strike a bargain.<ref>{{Cite|Now then, shall we strike a bargain? Then stay that way. No further action on your part is required.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> Before {{Term|BotW|Link}} can answer, though, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} takes away a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Fa ha ha... My touch is as subtle as ever... Note how I took that {{Color|BotW Red|essence}} from you without leaving a mark.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} demands that he gives him the {{Term|BotW|Heart Container}} back, but he and asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he thought he was deceived.<ref>{{Cite|[Give it back.] Fa ha ha... Did you think I'd deceived you?|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} then explains that {{Term|BotW|Link}} can take the {{Term|BotW|Heart Container}} back or that he can trade it for a {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Perish the thought. I hope that ours will be a long and profitable relationship. So which will it be? As I may have mentioned once or twice, I'm a dealer in life and power. I can provide a valuable service to you. I take {{Color|BotW Red|Heart Containers}} or {{Color|BotW Red|Stamina Vessels}} from you...and then, for a price, return whichever of the two you like.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} chooses one to receive, the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} will tell him to remain still.<ref>{{Cite|A wise choice. Now, remain still.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> He grants {{Term|BotW|Link}} his {{Term|BotW|Heart Container}} or {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel}}.

The {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} notes {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s skepticism and tells him not to worry, since he sees money and life as the same thing.<ref>{{Cite|You look skeptical. Not to worry. Money and life are all as one to me.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> From this point on, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can sell a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container}} or {{Term|BotW|Stamina Vessel}} to the {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you're ready to begin, then first you must sell me either type of {{Color|BotW Red|essence}} for {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}}.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> However, buying it back will cost 120 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}},<ref>{{Cite|If you want to buy an essence back, the price rises to {{Color|BotW Red|120 rupees}}.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> though he can choose which one he receives.<ref>{{Cite|Before you cry foul, know that you may choose which one I return to you: a {{Color|BotW Red|Heart Container}} or a {{Color|BotW Red|Stamina Vessel}}.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Horned Statue}} invites {{Term|BotW|Link}} to come back to trade any time,<ref>{{Cite|I'm eager to help tamper with your essence anytime you want to return and pray here.|Horned Statue|BotW}}</ref> which completes the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
