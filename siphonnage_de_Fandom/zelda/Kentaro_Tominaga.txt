'''Kentaro Tominaga''' was formerly a game designer for [[Nintendo]]. 

==Biography==

{{stub}}

Kentaro Tominaga worked at Nintendo from 2002-2022, in which time he was credited for 10 games and 1 expansion, most of the work being directorial. Aside from Zelda games, he is credited for the following:<ref>https://www.mobygames.com/person/87741/kentaro-tominaga/credits/</ref>

*Assistant Director - ''Mario Kart: Double Dash!!'' (2003)
*Sequence Director - ''Super Mario 64 DS'' (2004)
*Planning - ''Mario Kart Wii'' (2008)
*Mini-game Director - ''Wii Fit Plus'' (2009)

Tominaga left the company in 2022, and joined Papergames.<ref>https://www.ign.com/articles/former-zelda-director-working-on-open-world-dress-up-rpg-infinity-nikki</ref>
