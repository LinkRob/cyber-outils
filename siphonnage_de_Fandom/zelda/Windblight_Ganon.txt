{{Term/Store|Windblight Ganon||Series, BotW}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Boss|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Windblight Ganon}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Windblight Ganon}}
{{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}} is one of the four {{Plural|BotW|Blight Ganon|link}}, phantoms of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}} that took control of the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|This phantom of Ganon attacked the Divine Beast Vah Medoh and was responsible for the demise of the Champion Revali.|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref> It is found either atop the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} or in the center of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} if it is not already defeated. {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}} is responsible for the death of {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It defeated me 100 years ago... but only because I was winging it.|Revali|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} requires protection from the cold to resist the cold damage during his fight against {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}}. The battle, taking place atop {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, has two main phases. During the first phase, {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}} will fly and teleport around the area, firing powerful blue energy blasts at {{Term|BotW|Link}}; {{Term|BotW|Revali}} warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} that these attacks are incredibly accurate.<ref>{{Cite|His aim is dead-on! Watch yourself!|Revali|BotW}}</ref> It will also fire large tornadoes at {{Term|BotW|Link}}.

In order to inflict damage against {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can fire {{Plural|BotW|Arrow|link}} while it is in the air. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use one of the many vents in the area to glide up and shoot {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}} from the air. It will occasionally land on the ground, where {{Term|BotW|Link}} can use melee {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} to damage it.

Once {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}}'s hit points have been depleted by half, the second phase of the battle begins. During this phase, {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}} sends four small objects into the air; these objects all charge one another with blue electricity and fire it at {{Term|BotW|Link}}. To temporarily disable {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shoot these objects while they float in the air. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} hits {{Term|BotW|Windblight Ganon}} with enough {{Plural|BotW|Arrow}} in quick succession, it will fall to the ground and become vulnerable to other attacks.

When Windblight Ganon is defeated, {{Term|BotW|Link}} is rewarded with a {{Term|BotW|Heart Container|link}}. After that, Revali's soul appears before Link to thank him for setting him and Vah Medoh free<ref>{{Cite|Well done. I suppose I should thank you now that my spirit is free. This returns Medoh back to its rightful owner!|Revali|BotW}}</ref> and will grant him [[Revali's Gale]] as a reward for his efforts.<ref>{{Cite|I do suppose you've proven your value as a warrior. A warrior worthy of my unique ability. The sacred skill that I have dubbed Revali's Gale!|Revali|BotW}}</ref>

==Other Appearances==
{{Noncanon|Start}}
==={{HWAoC|-}}===
{{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}} appears in {{HWAoC}}, filling roughly the same role it did in {{BotW}}. Unlike its appearance in {{BotW|-}}, it now bears a second, larger hand cannon replacing its left hand, and it now has four tubes of {{Term|HWAoC|Malice|link}} connecting its arms to its back. It also sports purple glyphs, signifying its elemental affinity with wind, and has a red eye instead of a blue one.

{{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}} first appears in the "{{Term|HWAoC|Air and Lightning|link}}" {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario|link}} after {{Term|HWAoC|Calamity Ganon|link}} is revived. {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}} fights with {{Term|HWAoC|Revali|link}} on top of {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Medoh|link}} for the control of the {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast|link}}. Before the fatal blow that should have killed {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} is fired, a portal opens, bringing the {{Term|HWAoC|Rito|link}} warrior {{Term|HWAoC|Teba|link}} from 100 years into the future. After {{Term|HWAoC|Link|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda|link}}, and their allies have saved {{Term|HWAoC|Urbosa|link}} from {{Term|HWAoC|Thunderblight Ganon|link}}, they arrive on {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}} to assist {{Term|HWAoC|Revali}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Teba}}, only for {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}} to retreat. It later reappears to try and stop both {{Term|HWAoC|Rito}} warriors while they are destroying enemy camps with {{Term|HWAoC|Divine Beast Vah Medoh}}, before it is finally vanquished.

{{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}}, along with the other {{Plural|HWAoC|Blight Ganon}}, is later revived by {{Term|HWAoC|Astor|link}} using the souls of a number of {{Plural|HWAoC|Yiga Footsoldier|link}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Astor}} then uses the {{Plural|HWAoC|Blight Ganon}} in the "{{Term|HWAoC|Each Step Like Thunder|link}}" {{Term|HWAoC|Scenario}}, aiding him in the {{Term|HWAoC|Siege of Fort Hateno|link}} when trying to end the lives of both {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}}, along with the other {{Plural|HWAoC|Blight Ganon}}, overpower {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} while he buys time for {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} and {{Term|HWAoC|Impa|link}} to escape, but {{Term|HWAoC|Zelda}} ends up awakening her powers, temporarily stunning all the {{Plural|HWAoC|Blight Ganon}}. {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}} appears again in an attempt to stop the heroes from activating a device made by {{Term|HWAoC|Purah|link}}.

Later, when the heroes lay siege to {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle|link}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Windblight Ganon}} is summoned one final time, along with the other {{Plural|HWAoC|Blight Ganon}}, to defend {{Term|HWAoC|Hyrule Castle}}; however, it is defeated by the heroic forces.
{{Noncanon|End}}
