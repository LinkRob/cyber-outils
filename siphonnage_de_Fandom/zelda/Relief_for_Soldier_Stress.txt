==Overview==
“{{Term|HWAoC|Relief for Soldier Stress}}” is a {{Term|HWAoC|Quest}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} must supply {{Plural|HWAoC|Soldier|link}} with three {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Herb|link}} to help with their meditation in the {{Term|HWAoC|Sage Temple|link}}. After burning the {{Plural|HWAoC|Hyrule Herb|display=Herbs}}, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Soldier}} are refreshed and access is gained to {{Term|HWAoC|Meditative Training|link}}.
