The {{Term/Store|Akkala Bridge Ruins||Series, BotW}} are a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Akkala Bridge Ruins|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bridge Ruins}} are located in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Highlands|link}}. They are the remains of a bridge that once led to the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins|link}}. Both ends are littered with the deactivated remains of {{Plural|BotW|Guardian Stalker|link}}, which {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can scavenge for {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Material|link}}.

On the western side of the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bridge Ruins}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find {{Term|BotW|Nell|link}} fighting off a {{Term|BotW|Blue Moblin|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Let's go, you filth!|Nell|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} helps him, {{Term|BotW|Nell}} will offer his thanks and gift him {{Term|BotW|Food|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|Th-thanks... That monster was more than I bargained for. I'm a dab hand with a blade, but you... Well, my hat is off to you, boy. Here, take this for your help.|Nell|BotW}}</ref> He explains that he wanted to go to the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins}} to pray for his ancestors who fell there,<ref>{{Cite|I found out my own kin died in that battle, so I came to at least pray at the spot where they fell.|Nell|BotW}}</ref> but since the {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} surrounding the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Citadel Ruins|display=Ruins}} are still active, he has decided not to progress past the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bridge Ruins}}.<ref>{{Cite|But it turns out that the old Guardians are still active, so this is as far as I go.|Nell|BotW}}</ref>

In {{Term|BotW|Master Mode|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} among the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Bridge Ruins}} that contains a {{Term|BotW|Royal Claymore|link}}.
