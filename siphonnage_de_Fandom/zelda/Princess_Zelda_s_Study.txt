{{Term/Store|Princess Zelda’s Study||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Princess Zelda's Study'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study}} is a small room located in a tower structure connected to {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Room|link}} by a bridge. The bridge and the air encircling the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|display=Study}} is patrolled by a lone {{Term|BotW|Guardian Skywatcher|link}}. The interior of the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|display=Study}} is filled with several {{Plural|BotW|Book|link}}, charts, and rotted plants.

Prior to the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} conducted her research on {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} and plants in this room.<ref name="CaC">{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 251}}</ref> The "{{Term|BotW|Father and Daughter|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}} can be accessed on the bridge connecting {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study}} to the rest of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. During this, {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} confronts {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda}} as she leaves her {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|display=Study}},<ref>{{Cite|What are you doing out here, Zelda?|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> eventually forbidding her from continuing her research.<ref>{{Cite|As the king, I forbid you to have anything to do with these machines from this moment on and command you to focus on your training.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Following the return of {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study|display=Study}} was overtaken by {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s plants.<ref name="CaC"/>

Placed open-faced on the desk at the far end of the study {{Term|BotW|Zelda's Research Notes|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Book}} detailing {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda}}'s findings on {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology}}, as well as the effort to pilot the {{Plural|BotW|Divine Beast|link}} with the {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}} at their helm.<ref>{{Cite|Today I met with Impa of the Sheikah tribe and began my research into the ancient technology in earnest. Impa introduced me to Purah and Robbie, other respected members of her tribe. Tomorrow I embark on an excavation with them. We hope to find ancient tech with which to operate the Guidance Stones.|Zelda's Research Notes|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We have started training the Champions who will pilot the Divine Beasts. It may sound rude that I found this unexpected, but Mipha mastered the controls with surprising ease. Daruk struggled at first but eventually got the hang of it. Urbosa and Revali both managed just fine as well. I can finally see the light of hope in our fight against the rising Calamity.|Zelda's Research Notes|BotW}}</ref> A singular {{Term|BotW|Silent Princess|link}} can be found growing in the mound of rubble near the center of the room, an allegory of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|display=Zelda's}} love of research over her regal duties.<ref>{{Cite|This one here is called the silent princess. It's a rare, endangered species. Despite our efforts, we can't get them to grow domestically yet. The princess can only thrive out here in the wild.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref>

[[File:BotW Korok Seed Icon.png|left|40px]]
On the spire on top of {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda's Study}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}}. Interacting with them will cause a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} to appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>
