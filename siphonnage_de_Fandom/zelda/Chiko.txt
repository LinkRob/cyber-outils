{{Term/Store|Chiko||Series}} is a young girl who appears in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}} She lives in {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town|link}} with {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum|display=her mother|link}}. When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} meets her, she tells him that she has lost her prized {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Doll|link}} which her mother had made for her. When {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} returns it, {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum}} will reward him. 

{{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}} has always wanted to meet a real {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy|link}}. By throwing 20,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} into the Fountain in {{Term|FPTRR|Port Town}}, the town's guardian {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy}} will appear. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} speaks to {{Term|FPTRR|Chiko's Mum}}, she will reward him with a maximum of 36,000 {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}}. 

{{Term|FPTRR|Chiko}} and her mother both have the gift of being able to see {{Plural|FPTRR|Ghost|link}}.
