{{Term/Store|Pairodd|Pairodds|Series, LA, LADX|plural}} are enemies in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 196}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
When approached at normal speed, Pairodds will teleport away from [[Link]], appearing at the opposite side of the room. They will then shoot projectiles at him. Pairodds can be defeated by using the [[Bow]] or [[Bomb]]s from afar before they can teleport away. If Link equips the [[Pegasus Boots]], he can [[Dash Attack]] into a Pairodd with his [[Sword]] before it teleports away.
