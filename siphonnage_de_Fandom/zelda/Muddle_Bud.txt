{{Term/Store|Muddle Bud|Muddle Buds|Series|TotK|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TotK}}.

==Locations and Uses==
==={{TotK|-}}===
{{Plural|TotK|Muddle Bud}} are common throughout [[The Depths]] during the events of {{TotK}}, often found at the base of some trees or bought for 16 Poes apiece at Bargainer Statues. They can be thrown by Link or [[Fuse|fused]] on an arrow to confuse enemies that are hit by them into attacking each other. Muddle Buds can also be used alongside [[Dazzlefruit|Dazzle Fruits]] for Scatter Traps
