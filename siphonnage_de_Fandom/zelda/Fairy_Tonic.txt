==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{hatnote|”This powerful recovery elixir harnesses the power of fairies. It has a sweet fragrance.” — [[Inventory]]}}
[[Link]] can make Fairy Tonics by using at least one [[Fairy]] on a [[Cooking Pot]]. These elixirs harness the power of Fairies, making them able to heal some of Link's [[Heart]]s to his [[Life Gauge]].<ref name="Inventory"/> Therefore, the more Fairies used, the stronger their effects will be. Their fragrance is known to be sweet.<ref name="Inventory"/>
