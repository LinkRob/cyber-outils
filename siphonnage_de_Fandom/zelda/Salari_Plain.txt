{{Term/Store|Salari Plain||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Salari Plain'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Salari Plain is located in [[Hyrule Field]].

==Nomenclature==
Salari Plain derives its name from ''celery''. Salari Plain shares its Japanese name, {{Romanize|マリッタ|Maritta}}, with the [[Maritta Exchange Ruins]], [[Salari Hill]], and the [[Serenne Stable]].
