{{Term/Store|King Rhoam's Training||HWAoC}} is a Level 49 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|King Rhoam's Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam's Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using only {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}. When completed for the first time, {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Raw Bird Thigh|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Palm Fruit|link}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Royal Guard Cap|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within six minutes. Only one {{Term|HWAoC|Hylian Captain|link}} is seen at first, but another one spawns when {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} reaches 50 and 150 KOs. When 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, {{Term|HWAoC|Link}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|King Rhoam}} defeats {{Term|HWAoC|Link}}.
