{{Term/Store|Isha||Series, BotW}} is a character in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Isha|N/A|BotW}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|BotW|Isha}} is a jeweler who owns and works at the {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories|link}} {{Term|BotW|Jewelry Shop|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}. According to {{Term|BotW|Cara|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Isha}}'s real name is Jewel, but she goes by an alias to avoid the being a jeweler named Jewel.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|The owner's name is actually an alias. Her real name is Jewel. It's a little too coincidental for a jeweler to be named Jewel, so she was embarrassed.}}|Cara|BotW}}</ref> In the past, she and {{Term|BotW|Ashai|link}} were involved in a love triangle with {{Term|BotW|Aurie Taamu|link}}, but they both have remained single since then.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Apparently she and the teacher of the relationship class, Ashai, were embroiled in a tragic love triangle... They're both still single, so I guess we know how that went...}}|Cara|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Cara}} has also heard that {{Term|BotW|Isha}} sleeps wearing nothing but accessories.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|I heard the owner... sleeps wearing nothing... but accessories. I wonder if she gets cold...}}|Cara|BotW}}</ref> Despite claiming to do nothing special with her looks, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} visits the {{Term|BotW|Hotel Oasis|link}} once a week.<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|The owner claims that she's not doing anything special with her looks, but she actually visits Hotel Oasis once a week.}}|Cara|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} first arrives in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Isha}} wandering around in front of {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to her, she initially greets him, but she then realizes that she is pressed for time and should not have done so.<ref>{{Cite|Sav'aaq... Great! You got the last of my free time with that greeting.|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Vasaaq... Not that I have any reason to be greeting people...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks what the problem is, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} explains that she has run out of the {{Term|BotW|Flint|link}} she needs to make her accessories.<ref>{{Cite|I manage a shop that specializes in handmade accessories, but I've run out of {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}. I need it to make my items.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She then says that if someone would give her 10 pieces of {{Plural|BotW|Flint}}, she would offer a nice reward.<ref>{{Cite|If only I had {{Color|BotW Red|10}}... Just {{Color|BotW Red|10}}... If some kind vai would give me {{Color|BotW Red|10 pieces of flint}}, I could offer a nice reward...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> However, she believes that no one would really do that for her.<ref>{{Cite|I can dream all I want, though... Nothing goes that smoothly in the real world.|Isha|BotW}}</ref>

If asked about her accessories, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} explains that she uses {{Plural|BotW|Ruby|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Sapphire|link}} and other {{Plural|BotW|Gem|link}} to make the accessories and that each {{Term|BotW|Gem}} has a unique power that transfers into the wearer.<ref>{{Cite|[Accessories?] I use {{Color|BotW Blue|rubies}}, {{Color|BotW Blue|sapphires}}, and other gems to make {{Color|BotW Blue|accessories}}. Each gem type has its own power that it can transfer to whoever wears it.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately, without {{Term|BotW|Flint}}, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} cannot make any accessories.<ref>{{Cite|There's nothing I can do about not having any {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, though...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> At this point, {{Term|BotW|Link}} receives the "{{Term|BotW|Tools of the Trade|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Isha}} then apologizes for having nothing to show him.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sorry that you came all the way to see me for nothing.|Isha|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has collected 10 pieces of {{Term|BotW|Flint}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to offer them to {{Term|BotW|Isha}}, much to her surprise.<ref>{{Cite|[Here's some flint.] Uh... What?! You're just...giving these to me?|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}}, but since he has been so kind to her, she offers to make an accessory for him.<ref>{{Cite|Sarqso! But...I can't take this all from you with just a sarqso. We hardly know each other, and you've been so nice... The shop's been closed so long, I hardly have any inventory... Oh, of course! Now that I have some {{Color|BotW Blue|flint}}, I could make a jeweled accessory for you if you're interested in something.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} is then able to choose if he wants the {{Term|BotW|Ruby Circlet|link}},<ref>{{Cite|[Ruby?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Rubies}} have the power to grant cold resistance. So you'd like a {{Color|BotW Blue|ruby circlet}}?|Isha|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Sapphire Circlet|link}},<ref>{{Cite|[Sapphire?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Sapphires}} have the power to grant heat resistance. So you think you'd like a {{Color|BotW Blue|sapphire circlet}}?|Isha|BotW}}</ref> or {{Term|BotW|Topaz Earrings|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Topaz?] {{Color|BotW Blue|Topaz}} has the power to grant electric resistance. So you think you'd like {{Color|BotW Blue|topaz earrings}}?|Isha|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} makes his selection, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} remarks that she had a feeling that would be the one he picked, before she takes a moment to prepare his reward.<ref>{{Cite|Teehee, I thought you might pick that one. Please give me a moment...|Isha|BotW}}</ref> After giving {{Term|BotW|Link}} his accessory, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} offers him a special discount at {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thanks to you, I got the shop running again, so if you ever need an accessory, please come by for a special discount.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> However, she asks him not to inform her other customers of this deal.<ref>{{Cite|Just...don't tell my other customers, {{Small|OK?}}|Isha|BotW}}|</ref> When she finishes speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} will be completed.

Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can purchase a variety of jewelry accessories from {{Term|BotW|Isha}} at {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories}}. If asked, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} will explainthat wearing {{Plural|BotW|Gem}} grants a form of protection.<ref>{{Cite|You see, each gem contains its own special power. Wearing one will grant you a form of protection.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She goes on to say that {{Term|BotW|Sapphire}} offer {{Term|BotW|Heat Resistance|link}}, while {{Plural|BotW|Ruby}} offer {{Term|BotW|Cold Resistance|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|For example, {{Color|BotW Blue|sapphires}} offer resistance to intense heat, and {{Color|BotW Blue|rubies}} have the power to resist chilling temperatures.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> By crafting circlets and earrings with these {{Plural|BotW|Gem}}, she is able to channel their powers to protect their wearers.<ref>{{Cite|I'm able to craft {{Color|BotW Blue|circlets}} or {{Color|BotW Blue|earrings}} that channel these powers to protect the wearer.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> Since the {{Plural|BotW|Material|link}} needed to make them are difficult to acquire, she needs her customers to bring them to her.<ref>{{Cite|Sadly, the materials to make such items are difficult to get ahold of... That's why you have to bring me the materials.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> While it will take time to craft, {{Term|BotW|Isha}} guarantees {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be satisfied with the result.<ref>{{Cite|It'll take some time, but there's nothing that'll satisfy you more than that.|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She also claims that her accessories can be enjoyed by both men and women, making them great gifts for others.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, and don't forget that voe and vai alike can enjoy these. They're great gifts if you want to impress someone, hehe.|Isha|BotW}}</ref>

Like most other shop owners, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also sell {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} to {{Term|BotW|Isha}}.<ref>{{Cite|Any self-respecting vai would have her inventory well-organized.|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|It's interesting how one vai's trash is another vai's treasure.|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Care to sell anything else? Hopefully something of great beauty...|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If it's beautiful, please show it to me!|Isha|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for everything he sells her, especially appreciating their beauty.<ref>{{Cite|What a great item. Sarqso.|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sarqso!|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I'm glad it's such a beautiful item.|Isha|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sarqso! Is it already time to say sav'orq?|Isha|BotW}}</ref>
