==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Gear}} can be obtained by defeating {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} or finding them in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}}. They are also sold by the {{Term|BotW|Slippery Falcon (Tarrey Town Branch)|link}} and the travelling {{Term|BotW|Merchant|link}} {{Term|BotW|Teli|link}}, who can be found on the road northwest of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. As is the case with all {{Plural|Series|Ancient Material|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Gear}} were made by the ancient {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}} and used in their machinery. In spite of their age, they are still more advanced than anything else that exists during {{BotW|-}}.<ref name="Inventory"/>

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Gear}} can be given to the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab|link}} to make various pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}. Since they count as {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}}, they can be combined with critters in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}} to make {{Plural|BotW|Elixir|link}}. They can also be exchanged at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} or used as an ingredient for {{Term|BotW|Gray Dye|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Gear}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
