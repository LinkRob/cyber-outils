The {{Term/Store|Mah Eliya Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Mah Eliya Shrine}}<br/>Secret Stairway}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Secret Stairway''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}. It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Mah Eliya Shrine}} is located on {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Lanayru Great Spring|link}}, though it is initially buried beneath the ground. It is one of three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} unlocked as part of the "{{Term|BotW|EX Champion Mipha's Song|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.

To access the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} must swim up through the {{Plural|BotW|Glowing Ring}} that appear on the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall|link}} that leads into {{Term|BotW|Mikau Lake|link}}. From there, he will proceed towards {{Term|BotW|Lulu Lake|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Waterfall}} above it, all the way up to {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain}}. Once he passes through the final {{Term|BotW|Glowing Ring}}, {{Term|BotW|Mah Eliya Shrine}} will emerge from the ground. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|display=Shrine}}, he will be greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Mah Eliya|link}}, who presents him with the trial.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Mah Eliya. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Mah Eliya|BotW}}</ref>
