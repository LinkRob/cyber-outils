{{Term/Store|Grass|Grass|Series}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=64 {{Exp Game|Series}} }}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Manual|ALttP|Bushes|You will find Rupees and Hearts hidden away in these bushes. Cut them down or pull them up to reveal hidden items.}}
Grass has appeared in the series as a type of terrain. In some cases, Link can cut down tall Grass to receive [[Rupee]]s, [[Bomb]]s, [[Arrow]]s, [[Heart]]s, [[Kinstone Piece]]s (only in {{TMC}}<ref>{{Cite manual|quote=Kinstone Pieces are scattered all over the world. There are lots of different ways to get your hand on them: • Cutting up grass; [...]. |game= TMC|page= 22}}</ref>) or other items.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= PH|publisher= Prima|page= 14}}</ref> Grass is commonly found near Bushes, though in later games, Grass is more prominent than Bushes. In 3D console games, Link may sometimes find Rupees or other items when walking through Grass. In {{BotW}}, cutting grass have a chance to spawn [[Restless Cricket]]s, [[Hot-Footed Frog]]s, [[Hylian Rice]], and rarely [[Fairy|Fairies]].
