==Location and Uses==
{{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can drink Tough Elixirs to receive a boost to his defense.<ref name="Inventory"/> His bones will be fortified and his defense strengthened, making them useful for facing off against dangerous enemies.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Tough Elixirs can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} any of the following critters with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} in a {{Term|BotW|Cooking Pot|link}}:

* {{Term|Series|Rugged Rhino Beetle|link}}

By adding any of these tough ingredients, Link can make a Tough Elixir that will also heal him:

* {{Term|Series|Ironshroom|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Fortified Pumpkin|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Armoranth|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Armored Carp|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Armored Porgy|link}}

* {{Term|Series|Ironshell Crab|link}}
