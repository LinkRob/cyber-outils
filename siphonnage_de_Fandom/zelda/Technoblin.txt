==Characteristics==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Target lock: {{Color|SS Blue|Technoblin}}</p><p>These Bokoblins wield sophisticated weaponry created by an ancient and highly evolved civilization.</p><p>If you are struck with the discharge from one of their electrical weapons, you may become paralyzed.</p><p>These creatures also wear the latest in Bokoblin fashion.</p>
}}

{{Plural|SS|Technoblin}} take on the appearance of regular {{Plural|SS|Bokoblin|link}}, but wear plated armor and wield {{Term|SS|Electricity|link|display=Electric}} baton-like weapons, both of which were created by an ancient and highly-advanced civilization.<ref>{{Cite|These Bokoblins wield sophisticated weaponry created by an ancient and highly evolved civilization.|Fi|SS}}</ref> They inhabited the {{Term|SS|Lanayru Desert|link}} region in large numbers before the events of {{SS|-}}. It is also implied that they captured and tortured the {{Plural|SS|Ancient Robot|link}} who worked in the region.<ref>{{Cite|If you hadn't come along, they would of sucked all of the electricity out me, vrrm!|Ancient Robot|SS}}</ref>

{{Plural|SS|Technoblin}} are fought using the same tactics used against {{Plural|SS|Bokoblin}}; they use their batons to block attacks from certain angles, forcing {{Term|SS|Link|link}} to strike at an opening in their defense. However, {{Term|SS|Link}} will be shocked from the discharge of the electric baton should his {{Term|SS|Goddess Sword|link|display= Sword}} make contact with it. {{Plural|SS|Technoblin}} can be quickly defeated with the use of a {{Term|SS|Spin Attack|link}} to knock them off their feet, then finished with a {{Term|SS|Fatal Blow|link}}.

Upon defeat, {{Plural|SS|Technoblin}} sometimes drop {{Plural|SS|Ornamental Skull|link}} or {{Plural|SS|Golden Skull|link}}.

After {{Term|SS|Tentalus|link}} is defeated, a group of {{Plural|SS|Technoblin}} can be found in the cell where {{Term|SS|Skipper|link}}'s crew used to be imprisoned.
