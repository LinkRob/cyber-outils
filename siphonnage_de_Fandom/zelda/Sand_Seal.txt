{{Term/Store|Sand Seal|Sand Seals|Series, BotW|plural}},{{Exp Game|BotW}}<ref>{{Cite|'''{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Sand Seal}}'''|Hyrule Compendium|BotW}}</ref>{{Name Ref|HWAoC}} also known as '''Sand-Seals''',{{Name Ref|BotW}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Creature|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.

==Characteristics==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Sand Seal}}
{{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} are naturally yellowish tan in color and have long tusks similar to a {{Wp|walrus}}. {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} are native to the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert Gateway|link}},<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Sand seals}} are animals native to the Gerudo Desert Gateway.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> but can be found throughout the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} swim through the sands of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}} as if it were water. {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} surf by catching a wild {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, but he must have a {{Term|BotW|Shield|link}} and must approach it very slowly due to its sensitivity to sound.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Sand seals}} are especially sensitive to the sounds around them. The trick is to approach very, very slowly...|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also approach them after temporarily stunning them using {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} surfing is the quickest way to travel through the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you are hoping to cross the vast Gerudo Desert, {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal}} surfing is the way to do it!|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> 

The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}} train {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} and rely on them for travel across the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Desert}}.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}} here were all trained by Frelly.|Essa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Obviously, {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seals}} are incredibly important to the Gerudo people.|Frelly|BotW}}</ref> Trained {{Plural|BotW|Sand Seal}} are red in color, faster and will wait for their rider to return instead of running off the moment they are dismounted.<ref>{{Cite|They're faster, and they'll wait for you to return to them.|Essa|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|By comparison, the ones outside who haven't been trained will just run off the moment you dismount.|Essa|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can rent a {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Northwest Booth|link}} or the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rental Shop Southeast Booth|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}} for 20 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}} or 50 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee}} if he does not have a {{Term|BotW|Shield}}.<ref>{{Cite|It's only {{Color|BotW Red|20 rupees}} to rent one! Need a shield, too? Just pay {{Color|BotW Red|50 rupees}}! What do you say?|Frelly|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}'s blue {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}}, {{Term|BotW|Patricia|link}}, can be found in the palace in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} being taken care of by the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} soldier, {{Term|BotW|Padda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, I make sure to care for Patricia so that she'll be ready at a moment's notice from the chief.|Padda|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sav'saaba. Are you a traveler? Sitting here is the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s favorite {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}, Patricia. But she's no ordinary {{Color|BotW Blue|sand seal}}...|Padda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Patricia}} is said to be a wise oracle.<ref>{{Cite|She's actually something of an oracle!|Padda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Padda}} will translate {{Term|BotW|Patricia}}'s words of wisdom if {{Term|BotW|Link}} feeds the {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal|display=Seal}} some fruit.<ref>{{Cite|If you offer fruit to Patricia by placing it before her, you'll receive some words of wisdom in return!|Padda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Patricia}} assists {{Term|BotW|Riju}} in getting near the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|link}} while disabling its legs during the {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Naboris}} has finally been calmed! It sounds like our {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} and Patricia played a big part in the whole adventure, too!|Lorn|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} participate in {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|link|display=Sand-Seal Racing}}, a traditional {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} competition that involves {{Term|BotW|Sand Seal}} surfing races.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|sand-seal race}} is a traditional Gerudo competition.|Laine|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} can participate in {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} at the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Rally|link}} after calming {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris|display=Naboris}}.<ref>{{Cite|Now that the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast}} is gone, we can finally use the race course!|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref> To complete the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}}, "{{Term|BotW|The Undefeated Champ|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Link}} must beat the current record of 1:30, held by the {{Term|BotW|Sand-Seal Race|display=Sand-Seal Racing}} champion, {{Term|BotW|Tali|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Tali's record is {{Color|BotW Red|1}}:{{Color|BotW Red|30}}! If you can beat that record, that {{Color|BotW Blue|trophy}} is yours.|Shabonne|BotW}}</ref>
