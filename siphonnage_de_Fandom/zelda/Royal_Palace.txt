The {{Term/Store|Royal Palace||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 320}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}} is a feature of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|link}}, located along the southwest edge of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town|display=Town}}. A large rock spire rests on top of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|display=Palace}}, which serves as the source {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}'s {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. {{Term|BotW|Riju|link}}, the chief of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo|link}}, inhabits the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|display=Palace}},<ref name="Ploka">{{Cite|This is the home of the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} of the Gerudo. It is my duty to make sure that no one suspicious enters.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> and her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom|link|display=Bedroom}} takes up its entire second floor. Initially, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is unable to access this part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|display=Palace}}.<ref>{{Cite|Halt!<br/>This isn't a place someone like you can walk in and out of whenever you like! If you don't want me to skewer you right here, you'd better leave at once!|Bertri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Halt!}} Unless you have permission, you'll stay right there! I don't care who you are!|Katta|BotW}}</ref> The left exit leads to a courtyard, where {{Term|BotW|Patricia|link}} can be found, and the right exit leads to the {{Term|BotW|Barracks|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Relationship Classroom|link}}.

Alongside {{Term|BotW|Riju}}, several {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}} guards patrol the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}}. {{Term|BotW|Ploka|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Reeza|link}} guard the front entrance,<ref name="Ploka"/> while {{Term|BotW|Bertri|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Katta|link}} protect the stairs to {{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom}}. {{Term|BotW|Buliara|link}} acts as {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s personal guard and protects her while she sits on her {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|display=Throne}}.

Before {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arrival in {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}}, the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link|display=Yiga Clan}} infiltrated the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}} and stole the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}, an heirloom of the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo}}.<ref>{{Cite|The chief's heirloom was stolen by thieves belonging to the Yiga Clan the other day, so everyone is on edge...|Teake|BotW}}</ref> This has caused the soldiers of {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Town}} to go on high alert.<ref>{{Cite|We're on high alert after the recent theft, so you should clear out.|Teake|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|We're on high alert right now. If you want to do some sightseeing, go check out the market instead!|Liana|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Can't you tell we're on high alert right now?! And no, you can't help! Outsiders must stay out of it!|Babi|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Babi|link}} has also inflicted rigorous training on her soldiers as punishment for allowing the thieves to get by them.<ref>{{Cite|*pant pant*<br/>Babi's training is really tough, but... It's our fault the thieves were successful. We have no room to complain.|Marta|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}}, he can ask {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} if he is able to go inside. While she wants to turn him away, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} has allowed visitors to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace|display=Palace}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Can I go in?] {{Big|We're busy! It's an emergency!! Authorized personnel only!!!}} ...That's what I would say if I had my way, but he {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} is a kind person. She's told me to allow visitors.|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> Though he is allowed inside, {{Term|BotW|Ploka}} threatens him should he say anything rude to the chief.<ref>{{Cite|If you want to go inside, be my guest. But if you say even one rude thing to the chief... You and my spear will have a very personal conversation!|Ploka|BotW}}</ref> Her fellow guard, {{Term|BotW|Reeza}}, thinks {{Term|BotW|Riju}} allowing visitors in these tough times portrays her as a kind chief who puts her people first.<ref>{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}} may be young, but she's a kind person who puts her people first. She told me to allow visitors through, even in these tough times...|Reeza|BotW}}</ref> However, she also warns {{Term|BotW|Link}} against disturbing {{Term|BotW|Riju}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, listen! Even though I'm letting you inside, that doesn't mean you can go getting in the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s way! Watch out!|Reeza|BotW}}</ref>

The first time {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}}, he will be greeted by {{Term|BotW|Riju}}, who wonders how he managed to enter.<ref>{{Cite|Yet another traveler... How did you get in here?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> However, she takes note of his {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}, calling it something interesting.<ref>{{Cite|It seems you have something rather interesting there...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} appraoches, but once he reaches the steps to {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} demands he stop and declare his business.<ref>{{Cite|You stand before {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Riju}}, chief of the Gerudo! Declare your business, but come no closer!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} returns to the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}} after recovering the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Buliara}} will direct him to {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s room on the second floor, which he has been granted special permission to enter.<ref>{{Cite|If you are looking for {{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Riju}}, she's on the {{Color|BotW Red|second floor}}. Normally people aren't allowed into her sleeping quarters, but she has given you permission it seems...|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> She threatens {{Term|BotW|Link}} to be on his best manners, or else he will meet a bad end.<ref>{{Cite|Be on your best manners. If she deems you to be a disturbance to her, it will not end well for you!|Buliara|BotW}}</ref> Both {{Term|BotW|Bertri}} and {{Term|BotW|Katta}} will be shocked by this.<ref>{{Cite|You've got permission to enter the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}'s bedroom?! Who ARE you?|Bertri|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|You must be pretty special to have gotten permission from the {{Color|BotW Blue|chief}}.|Katta|BotW}}</ref>

At the top of the stairs, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} she has been waiting for him and can sense the power of the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} from him.<ref>{{Cite|I've been waiting for you... Oh, you needn't say a word. As the chief of the Gerudo, I can sense the power of the heirloom flowing from you. I know that you've succeeded!|Riju|BotW}}</ref> When it was stolen, {{Term|BotW|Riju}} felt as though a shadow had fallen over her,<ref>{{Cite|When my family heirloom was stolen, I felt as though a shadow had fallen over me...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> but now she sees {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s arrival in such a dark time as the work of {{Term|BotW|Urbosa}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, your arrival in the midst of all of this must be the work of Lady Urbosa...|Riju|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Riju}} then puts the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}} on and asks how she looks,<ref>{{Cite|{{Small|Um. How do I look?}}|Riju|BotW}}</ref> which triggers the "{{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Hand|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}. Though {{Term|BotW|Riju}} notices {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s staring, she dismisses it and asks if she looks right.<ref>{{Cite|What's wrong? You're just staring... Anyway, what matters now is... How...how is it?! Do I look all right?|Riju|BotW}}</ref> She tells {{Term|BotW|Link}} to meet her at the {{Term|BotW|Lookout Post|link}} before leaving.<ref>{{Cite|I'll head out now. Let's meet up at the {{Color|BotW Red|lookout post}} {{Color|BotW Blue|south of town}}.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

On top of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Leaves with Fairy Dust|link}}. When he inspects them, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give him a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref> After, it will say goodbye to him.<ref>{{Cite|Buh-bye!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>

==={{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom}}===
{{Main|Riju's Bedroom}}
{{Term|BotW|Riju's Bedroom}} contains several {{Plural|BotW|Sand-Seal Toy|link}}, including a large one that rests on her bed. In front of the bed is the {{Term|BotW|Chief's Diary|link}}, which is {{Term|BotW|Riju}}'s personal diary.<ref>{{Cite|-Diary of Makeela Riju-<br/>Chief of the Gerudo|Chief's Diary|BotW}}</ref> When {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack is active, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa's Diary|link}} will be present alongside it after completing the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Lady Urbosa's diary}} is in {{Color|BotW Blue|my room}}. Since she trusted you, I'm certain she wouldn't mind you reading it.|Riju|BotW}}</ref>

==={{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room}}===
{{Main|Royal Palace's Throne Room}}
The first floor of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace}} is comprised of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Palace's Throne Room}}, containing {{Term|BotW|Riju's Throne|link}} and the pedestal for the {{Term|BotW|Thunder Helm}}. Several of {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s meeting with {{Term|BotW|Riju}} during the "{{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Naboris (Quest)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}} occur here.
