{{Term/Store|Proxim Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Proxim Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Proxim Bridge}} spans the {{Term|BotW|Hylia River|link}}. between {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. The Bridge is guarded by a {{Term|BotW|Hylian|link}} named {{Term|BotW|Brigo|link}}, who protects it from monsters for travelers. It is said to be an important route for travel.<ref>{{Cite|Just patrolling. This bridge is an important route. I keep really busy making sure monsters don’t nest here by chasing them off the bridge and stuff.|Brigo|BotW}}</ref> When asked, {{Term|BotW|Brigo}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} directions and insights into {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Ah, I see. Kakariko Village is that way. On the other side of Duelling Peaks, there’s a stable. Stop there and ask someone for further directions.|Brigo|BotW}}</ref> the {{Term|BotW|Great Plateau|link}},<ref>{{Cite|The Great Plateau?! Why would you possibly need to go to an abandoned place like that? How would you even begin to scale that cliff? Well, in any case, it’s over there. There are quite a few monsters about, though. So be careful.|Brigo|BotW}}</ref>and {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hyrule Castle?! I don’t know how many lives you’ve got, but in a place with that many Guardians, I guarantee you it’s not enough! See that? You can see it in the distance. ==Trivia==
*Unlike most Bridges, {{Term|BotW|Proxim Bridge}} has stone fire pits that line the walls of the Bridge. They can be rekindled as long as it is not {{Term|BotW|Rain|link|display=raining}} in the area.

==Nomenclature==
{{Term|BotW|Proxim Bridge}} derives its name from ''proximity''. "Proxim" is a mistranslation of its Japanese name, which shared its name with {{Term|BotW|Aboda Village}}. In Japanese, {{Term|BotW|Aboda Village}} was named after a pun which means "Neighboring Village". This connection was missed by the localization team.
