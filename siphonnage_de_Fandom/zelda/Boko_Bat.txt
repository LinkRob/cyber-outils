{{Plural|BotW|Boko Bat}} are clubs found across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, particularly in the hands of {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}}. They also have a chance of appearing in {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} that appear when the {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|Series|Bokoblin}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo|link}} is scanned using the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. {{Plural|BotW|Bokoblin}} create these {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, making them part of the {{Term|BotW|Bokoblin Gear|link}} equipment archetype.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref> They are clunky and able to knock the {{Plural|BotW|Shield|link}} out of an {{Term|BotW|Enemy|link}}'s hands.<ref name="Inventory"/>

Since they are made of wood, {{Plural|BotW|Boko Bat}} will burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They will also float if they dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. While this means they will not conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}} or attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}}, they also are not able to be picked up with the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.

==={{TotK|-}}===
Outside of the settings, there is a difference in performance with a Sturdy Thick Stick that only looks like a weapon.
