'''The Final Titans' Gathering''' is a dream in {{EoW}}.

==Location and Rules==
The Final Titans' Gathering can be attempted by Zelda in the [[Slumber Dojo]] after she has completed all 14 other dreams. Zelda will need to defeat some of the bosses she has faced in the past within 15 minutes and 50 seconds. If she completes the dream in less than 13 minutes and 30 seconds, she will achieve Astounding and receive a better reward.

Zelda will have to defeat [[Seismic Talus]], [[Vocavor]], [[Mogryph]], [[Volvagia]], [[Gohma]], [[Skorchill]], and [[Echo Ganon]] all back to back without dying to complete the dream.
