{{Term/Store|Crystal Ball|Crystal Balls|Series, FSA|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 120 {{Exp Game|FSA}}}}</ref>{{Fact}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{FSA|-}}===
{{Stub|FSA}}
In {{FSA}}, Crystal Balls can be thrown and broken to obtain random items such as [[Force Gem]]s and [[Carrot]]s.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, after Link defeats [[The Imprisoned]] for the second time, [[Sparrot]]'s Crystal Ball shatters, making him get depressed.<ref>{{Cite|It's almost as though these exquisite, all-seeing eyes of mine have begun to dull and cloud over with sadness now that my crystal ball is gone. If only... If only I had a new crystal ball to gaze into...|Sparrot|SS}}</ref> [[Link]] can use the [[Dowsing]] ability to locate a new Crystal Ball for the fortune-teller,<ref>{{Cite|Your {{Color|SS Red|dowsing}} ability can be calibrated to locate the crystal ball that this person is seeking.|Fi|SS}}</ref> which can be found atop the entrance to the [[Earth Temple (Skyward Sword)|Earth Temple]]. To reach it, Link must [[Clawshot]] to the top of the temple and call [[Scrapper]] to carry it to [[Skyloft]] for him. Once Link gives Sparrot the new Crystal Ball, the fortune-teller rewards him with five [[Gratitude Crystal]]s.<ref>{{Cite|...Oh! What do my eyes behold!? This...this is it! You found it! This is exactly the same as the crystal ball I used before! Fates and fortunes! My eyes are wide with the thrill of possibility and damp with tears of gratitude! I'll be able to start gazing into the future and making predictions at once! Come by the bazaar again, and I'll tell your fortune for a special price!|Sparrot|SS}}</ref>
