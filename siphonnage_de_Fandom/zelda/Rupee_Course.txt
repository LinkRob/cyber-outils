The {{Term/Store|Rupee Course||Series}} is a {{Term|TWWHD|Mini-Game|link}} in {{TWW}}.{{Name Ref|TWW, TWWHD}}

==Overview==
The Rupee Course is encountered at various points across the [[Great Sea]]. Two barrels with flags will rise from beneath the waves, and [[Link]] must sail between them to begin the course. Barrels will rise and [[Link]] must sail across them, collecting the [[Rupee]] floating above them, in order to make the next appear. First on the course come five green Rupees, then five blue, and finally five yellow.
