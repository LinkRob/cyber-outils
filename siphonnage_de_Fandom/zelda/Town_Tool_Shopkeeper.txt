The {{Term/Store|Town Tool Shopkeeper||Series, LA, LADX, LANS}} is a character in {{LA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 233 {{Exp Game|LA}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The {{Term|LADX|Town Tool Shopkeeper}} is the owner of the {{Term|LADX|Town Tool Shop|link}} in {{Term|LADX|Mabe Village|link}}. He sells items that are necessary for {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} to complete his quest such as the {{Term|LADX|Shovel|link}} and the {{Term|LADX|Bow|link}}. {{Term|LADX|Link}} can either pay for them or steal them. To steal an item, {{Term|LADX|Link}} must pick it up and walk out the shop's door while the {{Term|LADX|Town Tool Shopkeeper}} is looking away from the door instead of taking it to the counter.<ref>{{Cite|Guess what? You got it for free. Are you proud of yourself?|Town Tool Shopkeeper|LADX}}</ref> If {{Term|LADX|Link}} sets foot again in the {{Term|LADX|Town Tool Shop}}, the {{Term|LADX|Town Tool Shopkeeper}} blasts {{Term|LADX|Link}} with a magical electric beam that instantly kills him.<ref>{{Cite|I wasn't kidding when I said pay! Now, you'll pay the ultimate price!!|Town Tool Shopkeeper|LADX}}</ref> Upon saving, {{Term|LADX|Link}} will respawn in the shop and be able to visit there again.

==Trivia==
*If {{Term|LADX|Link|link}} steals something from the {{Term|LADX|Town Tool Shop|link}}, he would be called "[[Thievery|THIEF]]" instead of the player's chosen name by all characters for the rest of his journey.
*In the ''Switch'' version, the scene where the shopkeeper confronts Link over the latter's earlier theft is altered slightly, showing him with a furious demeanor just before doing so.
*When arriving back in the store after stealing an item, the boss BGM plays in the background as the shopkeeper scolds Link and proceeds to kill him.
