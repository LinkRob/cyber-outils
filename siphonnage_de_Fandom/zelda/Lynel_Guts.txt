==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Lynel Guts are {{Plural|Series|Monster Part|link}} that can be dropped by all types of {{Plural|BotW|Lynel|link}} upon their defeats. They pulse with the vitality of a Lynel, which makes them useful for making [[Elixir]]s in a [[Cooking Pot]].<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] to make Crimson [[Dye]] or exchanged for [[Mon]] at the [[Fang and Bone]].

Several pieces of [[Armor]] require Lynel Guts to be upgraded at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}. The [[Barbarian Set]] requires them for its third and fourth tiers, while the [[Radiant Set]], the [[Fierce Deity Set]], and the [[Soldier's Set]] only need them for their final tiers.

* Total for '''Barbarian Set''': 9 (0, 0, 1, 2) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Fierce Deity Set''': 6 (0, 0, 0, 2) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Radiant Set''': 3 (0, 0, 0, 1) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}

* Total for '''Soldier's Set''': 6 (0, 0, 0, 2) {{Exp|For Each Piece of Armor in the Set|x3}}
