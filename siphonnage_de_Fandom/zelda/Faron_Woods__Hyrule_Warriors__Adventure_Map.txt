'''Battle C1''' has Impa, wielding only the Giant Blade, tasked with defeating Agitha, who leads an army of Dinolfos, while also rescuing fellow captains of the Allied Forces. The Allied Forces itself does not govern any of the keeps on the map, instead all being owned by the Enemy Forces; the enemy base being in the Deku Tree, protected by a Water Barrier that can be dispelled with the Water Fairy in Eastern Tree. The Allied Forces are led by Zant who rests at the intersection west of the Fairy Fountain. Impa starts further west near the North Square. Midna and one of the Goron Captains start north and south of the West Square respectively. The first mission of the battle is to rendezvous with the stranded Midna and Goron Captain by breaching several of the enemy-owned keeps before either of them are defeated. At the start, Impa is walled off from reaching them by numerous closed gates. To open some of them, Impa will need to capture either the North Square or the Eastern Tree. Capturing Eastern Tree is preferred over North square to obtain the Water Fairy that hides within it. A Cucco will spawn near Impa and will help fight the Enemy Forces. As Impa racks up KOs, more and more Cuccos will appear to assist the Allied Forces with the battle. When the first of the two keeps are captured, both of their gates open as well as the gate north of the Deku Tree, granting access to the Western Tree and Southern Tree keeps. At this time, some Dinolfos will advance from the un-captured keep towards Zant, and more Dinolfos will close in on Midna. Impa will need to capture one of the the next couple of keeps to successfully reach Midna and the others. The Southern Tree is also protected by a Water Barrier, which would require the only Water Fairy on the map to dispel so Impa will either need to capture the Western Tree with no issue or brave the former keep's barrier and claim it as fast as possible. When either of these keeps are wrestled from the Enemy Forces' control, their gates will open along with the gate east of the Deku Tree. Now Impa can rendezvous with Midna and the Goron Captain. Impa will now need to defeat the Dinolfos that threaten the two. However, a trio of Manhandla Stalk will appear in some of the enemy keeps and start a seed attack on Impa. They can be dispatched with the Boomerang, but Impa will have to go out of her way to do so. The main focus is to deal with the three Dinolfos and complete the first objective. The rescuees will gain a boost to their morale with the mission now complete. The gates to the Enemy Base in the central Deku Tree will now be open and the remaining Dinolfos will march to Zant. Impa now needs to dispel the Water Barrier in the Enemy Base and defeat Agitha to win the battle.

If Impa completes the battle with an A-Rank, her Lv.3 Giant Blade, the Biggoron's Sword, will be unlocked.

'''Battle D2''' has the chosen Warrior fight a selection of enemies in various keeps. The Warrior starts outside the Southern Tree, where the first enemies will be fought. Inside, a Big Poe and an Icy Big Poe will appear and need to be defeated in order to proceed. Along with the two enemies will be a couple of Deku Babas that will damage the Warrior if they are within the toxic pool that surrounds them. They can be easily dispatched with the Bow & Arrow. Once the Poes have been felled, the Warrior can proceed to the Eastern Tree. Two Icy Big Poes will appear upon entering the keep alongside the two Deku Babas that reside there beforehand. When the second couple of Poes are defeated, the last keep can be accessed at North Square. Here, alongside another two Deku Babas, a Big Poe, an Icy Big Poe, and a Stalmaster will appear. When the tree captains are KO'd, the battle will be successfully completed.

Achieving an A-Rank while controlling Agitha will reward her with a Heart Container.

'''Battle D9''' is a battle against several groups of enemies led by Moblins, however every enemy will be defeated in one hit. In return the chosen Warrior will be instantly brought to 1/4 of a heart of health. The Warrior starts near the Deku Tree while a group of four Moblins start near the Southeast Tree. Defeating a certain number of Moblins will cause more Moblins to spawn. When two Moblins are KO'd, three more will appear near the Eastern Tree. When four Moblins are felled, a Transport Captain will appear south of the Eastern Tree with a large Triforce Shard. Defeating the Transport Captain does not count towards the ten necessary KOs for the mission. once five Moblins are defeated, the last three will appear near the Deku Tree's south entrance. When the ten Moblin captains are KO'd, the battle will end in victory.

Achieving an A-Rank while playing as Impa will reward her with a Piece of Heart.

'''Battle E7''' has the player control Link to defend the Allied Forces from the Enemy Forces' keep-destroying Bombchus. Link is not limited to using a specific weapons, but it is recommended to use his Magic Rod weapon as it matches the suggested element of Fire. The Allied Forces control the Deku Tree and Southern Tree. They also control the Southeast Tree that stands as their Base with their leader, Sheik, on guard. the player-controlled Link starts by the Deku Tree and Lana starts near the Eastern Tree. The Enemy Forces claims Dark Forest Keep as their base, along with the other remaining keeps. When the battle begins, Link is tasked with destroying two Bombchus that the Enemy Forces send to destroy the Allied Forces' keeps. The first Bombchu starts near the Western Keep and will advance to the Deku Tree. When the Bombchu reaches its destination, a one minute timer will countdown until the Bombchu self-destructs in the keep. Whether the Bombchu is destroyed or not, the battle is not lost. The second Bombchu will now tread towards the Allied Base from near the Enemy Base. If the Bombchu explodes the Allied Base, the battle will be lost. When both Bombchus have been destroyed, the Enemy Base's gate will open and both forces will clash with each other. Link only needs to defeat the Moblin that acts aas the Enemy Forces' leader to win the battle for the Allied Forces.

If Link completes the battle with an A-Rank, his Lv.2 Magic Rod, the Prism Rod, will be unlocked.

'''Battle E8''' is a timed battle that has the player limited to using Lana with any of her available weapons. Lana will be given 10 minutes to rack up a total of 400 KOs against the Eneny Forces. She will start outside the Deku Tree's south entrance with a squad led by a Lizalfos in front of her and a Darknut-led group starting near the Southeast Tree. A sub-mission will be given to defeat the Darknut who will rally his fellow captains and increase their morale after two minutes and thirty seconds from when the sub-mission is given unless it is defeated. As Lana defeats the starting enemies, more squads of enemies and even some Giant Bosses will start to appear. By reaching 101 KOs, two more Lizalfos will appear east of the Deku Tree and by the Eastern Tree's southern gate along with a Gohma. At 201 KOs, A fourth Lizalfos will appear within the Southern Tree and a Moblin will spawn north of Southern Tree. the Moblin will also rally his fellow troops if he is not defeated with two minutes and thirty seconds after the sub-mission to defeat it is given. When Lana reaches 301 total KOs, a second Gohma will appear nearby Southern Tree and two more Lizalfos will spawn north of Southern Tree and between the central lakes. To assist Lana in completing the mission, Transport Captains carrying Magic Jars will appear after three and six total minutes have elapsed. The first will spawn outside the Deku Tree and the other will spawn by Southeast Tree. Once Lana has achieved 400 total KOs before the ten minutes are up, the battle will be over.

Clearing the mission with an A-Rank will reward Lana with a Piece of Heart

'''Battle G11''' limits the player to controlling Impa. She is allowed to use any of her weapons, but its preferred to use her Naginata to match the recommended element of Fire. Impa is tasked with defeating the leaders of the Enemy Forces and the Rogue Forces. The Allied Forces stand their ground at the Deku Tree, the Southern Tree, and the Southeast Tree. Zelda takes the role as leader and guards the Southeast Tree that marks the Allied Base. Link will also fight for the Allied Forces near the Eastern Tree. The Enemy Forces' take hold of North Square, Eastern Tree, Fairy fountain, and Dark Forest Keep, which is where their base is stationed. The Rogue Forces claim West Square as their Base along with the remaining Keeps, South Square and Eastern Tree. When the battle starts, Impa is given the mission to defeat a specific captain from each of the two opposing factions: a Moblin from the Enemy Forces and a Darknut from the Rogue Forces. The Moblin will be near the North square and the Darknut will be east of the Western Tree. Both captains will start with high morale. As Impa starts outside the Deku Tree, she should defeat the Darknut first as it is the closer of the two. When one of the captains have been defeated, their respective base will open, leaving their leader available to be fought. The weakened faction will then send more groups of enemies, led by a soldier with boosted morale, to target the Allied Base until the leader is felled. As Impa defeats more captains from one faction, a captain in the opposing faction will gain an increase to their morale. When Impa enters the weakened faction's base, the opposing faction's remaining soldiers will gain boosted morale. When the first leader is defeated, a notice will appear regarding a chest hidden in one of the keeps, in this case it is hidden in Eastern Tree however it can be found in another Keep if Eastern Tree is captured before the notice is given. Capturing the Keep will have the chest spawn, but when Impa opens the chest, the Keep's gates close and two enemy squads led by Moblins will appear. Impa will need to defeat both Moblins in order to re-open the gates and leave the Keep. Once the second captain is defeated, the remaining Base will open and the last leader can be defeated. Doing so will claim victory for the Allied Forces and end the battle.

If Impa achieves an A-Rank in the battle, her Lv. 2 Naginata, the Scorching Naginata, will be unlocked.

'''Battle H9''' has the chosen Warrior fight against Volga and the Enemy Forces that he governs. The Warrior sides with the Allied Forces, led by Zelda who guards the Allied Base in Dark Forest Keep. Link also fights for the Allied Forces near the North Square. The Warrior starts outside the southern entrance to the Western Tree and is first tasked with capturing two, enemy-owned keeps: Deku Tree and South Square. Capturing the Deku Tree will release the Water Fairy that held inside, which can be used to dispel the Water Barrier that cloaks South Square. While the Warrior goes after the keeps, two enemy groups, each led by a Lizalfos, will advance to the Allied Base. One group will be south of the Western Tree in front of the Warrior and the other will be north of Eastern Tree. To ease on the impending assault to the Allied Base, the Warrior can take out the western Lizalfos before attempting to claim ownership of the Deku Tree. During the battle, a Mama Cucco and a Cucco Chick will appear on the field. The Mama Cucco will always appear near the North Square by Link and the Cucco Chick will appear in a random enemy-owned keep. A sub-mission is given to escort the Cucco Chick to the Mama Cucco before the Cucco Chick is forced to flee. The Warrior would need to pave the way for the Cucco Chick so it can reach the Mama Cucco and complete the sub-mission. If successful, the Mama Cucco will fight alongside the Allied Forces. If not, then the Mama Cucco will dart to to and attack the Warrior until either is defeated. When both keeps have wrestled from the Enemy Forces, the gate to the Allied Base in the Southwest Tree will open and Volga can then be defeated to win the battle.

Achieving an A-Rank while controlling Sheik will reward her with a Heart Container

'''Battle H13''' pits the chosen Warrior against a trio of King Dodongos within the time limit. The Warrior starts inside the Deku Tree while the three King Dodongos start by the closed Eastern Tree, south of the Deku Tree, and nearby the Southern Tree. To help the Warrior with completing the mission, Transport Captains carrying useful items will appear on the field. One east of the Deku Tree will have a large Magic Jar, another south of the Deku Tree will carry a large Triforce Fragment, and the last near the Southern Tree will drop a large Heart when defeated. Over time, more Transport Captains will spawn. after two minutes have elapsed, a Transport Captain carrying another large Triforce Fragment will appear north-west of the Southern Tree. After 4 minutes have passed, a fifth Transport Captain will appear right of the southeast Tree and will have another large Heart. The last will appear between ht etwo lakes in the middle after six minutes and will be carrying another large Magic Jar. Once all three King Dodongos have been felled before the ten minutes have passed, the battle is complete.

Achieving an A-Rank while playing as Ghirahim will award him with a Heart Container
