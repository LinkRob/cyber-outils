{{Term/Store|Passenger Car|Passenger Cars|Series, ST|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Solid Passenger Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|ST|Passenger Car}} allow {{Term|ST|Link|link}} to transport {{Plural|ST|Passenger|link}} on the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}. The {{Term|ST|Solid Passenger Car|link}} is the only {{Term|ST|Train Car}} that comes with the {{Term|ST|Spirit Engine|link}} when {{Term|ST|Link}} first obtains the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}} from {{Term|ST|Anjean|link}} at the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}}. As with other {{Plural|ST|Train Car}}, {{Term|ST|Link}} can trade {{Term|ST|Treasure|link}} for new {{Plural|ST|Passenger Car}} at {{Term|ST|Linebeck III|link}}'s {{Term|ST|Trading Post|link}}.

At first the {{Term|ST|Passenger Car}} is attached directly to the {{Term|ST|Engine}}. When {{Term|ST|Alfonzo|link}} outfits the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train}} with the {{Term|ST|Practical Cannon|link}}, he places it between the {{Term|ST|Engine}} and the {{Term|ST|Passenger Car}}. The {{Term|ST|Trusty Freight Car|link}} is later attached to the back of the {{Term|ST|Passenger Car}}.
