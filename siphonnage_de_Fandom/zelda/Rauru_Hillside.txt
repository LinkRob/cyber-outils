The {{Term/Store|Rauru Hillside||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Rauru Hillside'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
The Rauru Hillside is a hill overlooking [[Lake Mekar]], east of the [[Military Training Camp]]. The Hillside also overlooks the [[Rauru Settlement Ruins]]. The Rauru Hillside is empty except for [[Tree]]s spaced out along the lower sections of the Hillside.

==Nomenclature==
Rauru Hillside is named for [[Rauru]], the [[Sage of Light]] from {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= '''Rauru Settlement Ruins, Rauru Hillside''' Rauru is a recurring figure in the series – an ancient Hylian and the sage of Light in Ocarina of Time, memorialized in one of the stained glasses windows in Hyrule Castle's basement in The Wind Waker|game= BotW|publisher= Piggyback|page= 351}}</ref>
