{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Bow}} are {{Plural|BotW|Bow|link}} designed for armed conflict, and they will therefore deal more damage than their civilian counterparts.<ref name="Inventory"/> They are used by {{Plural|BotW|Enemy|link}} across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}}, especially in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Field|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Faron Grasslands|link}}.

The following {{Plural|Series|Amiibo|link}} have a chance to spawn a {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Bow}} when they are scanned with the {{Term|BotW|Amiibo (Rune)|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}:
* {{SSB}} Series {{Term|SSB|Princess Zelda|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW}} Series {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} (Archer) {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}
* {{BotW|-}} Series {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}} {{Term|Series|Amiibo}}

Despite their upgrades, {{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Bow}} will burn if they are exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they cannot be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune}}.<ref name="Inventory"/> They will float if they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and they will not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Soldier's Bow}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which is associated with the {{Plural|BotW|Knight of Hyrule|link}} from before the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 169}}</ref>
