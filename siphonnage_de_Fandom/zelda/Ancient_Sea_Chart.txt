The {{Term/Store|Ancient Sea Chart||Series, SS, SSHD}},<ref name="Obtention">{{Cite|You got the {{Color|SSHD Gold|Ancient Sea Chart}}! This old map details the area as it was long ago, before the sea succumbed to the desert sand.|N/A|SSHD}}</ref> also known as '''Skipper's Sea Chart''',<ref>{{Cite|Master, I have determined that this is {{Color|SSHD Gold|Skipper's sea chart}}. Let's take it back to him.|Fi|SSHD}}</ref> is an item in {{SS}}. It is an old sea chart that used to belong to [[Skipper]], and shows the [[Lanayru Sand Sea]]'s condition as a large body of water in its past state.<ref name="Obtention"/>

==Location and Uses==
After hearing of Link's search for [[Sacred Flames|Nayru's Flame]], [[Skipper]] requests his assistance in finding the [[Sandship]].<ref>{{Cite|You say you're searching for {{Color|SSHD Red|Nayru's Flame}}, bzzt? Oh... So you need {{Color|SSHD Red|Nayru's Flame}} to find someone important to you, vrrrm...? In that case, you should help me search for my ship and crew, phweep!|LD-301N Skipper|SSHD}}</ref> He says that Link's current map looks odd, and that he needs a proper sea chart, the Ancient Sea Chart, to help him navigate the dangerous waters of the [[Lanayru Sand Sea]] sucessfully.<ref>{{Cite|Hmm... There's something weird about that map of yours...vrrm. If you're going to navigate these treacherous waters, then you need a proper {{Color|SSHD Gold|sea chart}}, doo-weep!|LD-301N Skipper|SSHD}}</ref> He then helps him by telling him that the Ancient Sea Chart can be found within his cabin at [[Skipper's Retreat]], and instructs Link to travel there using his boat.<ref>{{Cite|There is a {{Color|SSHD Gold|sea chart}} in my shack at {{Color|SSHD Blue|Skipper's Retreat}}, bzzzt. So first you need to get on this boat and set a course for {{Color|SSHD Blue|Skipper's Retreat}}!|LD-301N Skipper|SSHD}}</ref> The Ancient Sea Chart can be found buried under some [[sand]] in Skipper's Cabin, which requires the [[Gust Bellows]] to blow away. Using the chart, Link is able to explore the rest of the Lanayru Sand Sea and reach his next destination, the [[Lanayru Shipyard]].
