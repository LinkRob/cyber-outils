{{Term/Store|Cursed Spume|Cursed Spumes|Series, SS|plural}} are enemies in {{SS}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 172}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Fi
|quote1= <p>Target lock: {{Color|SS Blue|Cursed Spume}}</p><p>This mysterious monster's natural habitat includes poisonous swamps and other dangerous environments. It spits evil curses that it stores up in its body.</p><p>Cowardly by nature, it sits with its eyes just above the surface in order to detect any danger and flee if necessary.</p>
}}

Cursed Spumes are found in the [[Volcano Summit]] and the [[Fire Sanctuary (Skyward Sword)|Fire Sanctuary]]. Oddly, Fi's comment states that Cursed Spumes live in poisonous swamps which is not true. These Spumes spit balls of dark energy that will [[Jinx|curse]] Link, rendering him unable to use his Sword or items until the curse fades away. The Spume's projectiles can only be reflected with the [[Sacred Shield]] and its upgrades, along with the [[Hylian Shield]].
