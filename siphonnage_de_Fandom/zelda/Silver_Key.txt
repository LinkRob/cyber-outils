==Location and Uses==
A {{Term|FS|Silver Key}} from the {{Plural|FS|Great Fairy|link}} of {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Forest|link|display=Forest}}, {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Ice|link|display=Ice}}, and {{Term|FS|Great Fairy of Flame|link|display=Flame}} are required to enter the silver door of {{Term|FS|Vaati's Palace|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If all of you collect the three {{Color|FS Orange|Silver Keys}} of the Great Fairies of Forest, Ice, and Flame, the path to Vaati's Palace shall open.|Great Fairy|FS}}</ref>

They are obtained by completing the {{Term|FS|Sea of Trees|link}}, {{Term|FS|Talus Cave|link}}, and {{Term|FS|Death Mountain|link}} {{Plural|FS|Stage|link}} each with a minimum of 1000 {{Plural|FS|Rupee|link}}.<ref name= "Silver Key"/>
