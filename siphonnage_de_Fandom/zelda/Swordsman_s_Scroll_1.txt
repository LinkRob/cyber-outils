The {{Term/Store|Swordsman's Scroll 1||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 140}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The Swordsman's Scroll 1 can be obtained after Link manages to capture all 50 [[Rabbit]]s and take them to [[Rabbitland Rescue]]. The scroll allows Link to shoot [[Sword Beam]]s out of the [[Lokomo Sword]] when he is at full health.
