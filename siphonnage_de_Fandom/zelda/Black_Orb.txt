The '''Black Orb''' is an item in {{ZA}}.

==Location and uses==
The Black Orb is a [[Treasure]] found in the [[Shrine of Illusion]]. The Orb appears in a room with a checkerboard wall pattern after [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] defeats the four [[Patra]]s guarding the room. The Orb can be placed into a pedestal in the middle of a room with a colorful floor pattern also guarded by four Patras. Upon placing the Orb, the bars blocking the doorway to the right will vanish, allowing Zelda to advance further into the Shrine.
