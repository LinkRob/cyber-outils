The {{Term/Store|''History of the Zora, Part Seven''||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|H---ory of the Zor-, Part S-ven<br/>The H-ro Who D-feat-ed the Ly--l<br/>As T--d b-y King Dorephan|History of the Zora, Part Seven|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|Series|History of the Zora, Part Seven}} is one of several {{Plural|BotW|Stone Monument|link}}. Two iterations of this {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument|display=Monument}} exist. One is located at the back of the {{Term|BotW|Sleeping Chamber|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}}, where {{Term|BotW|Jiahto|link}} can be seen looking at it. The other can be found along the west coast of {{Term|BotW|Ruto Lake|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Got it! It's in this area here... The west coast of {{Color|BotW Blue|Ruto Lake}}!|Jiahto|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} needs to read one of them for the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Zora Stone Monuments|link}}".

Time has lead to this {{Term|BotW|Stone Monument}} becoming worn, as noted by {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Time has taken its toll on this...|Link|BotW}}</ref> It tells of a hero who fought the {{Term|BotW|Lynel|link}} on {{Term|BotW|Ploymus Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|T--re was a time when the p--ple of the land were thre--ened by the dr--- beast Ly-e- who lived on Pl--mu- M--ntain. But on Hy---n drove th- beast back an- re--ored p--ce to the do--in.|History of the Zora, Part Seven|BotW}}</ref> There is also a hint for the location of the {{Term|BotW|Zora Helm|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Th- {{Color|BotW Blue|Zora helm}} w-n in th-s fight i- no- north of --- in the r--ns near ---- L-ke.|History of the Zora, Part Seven|BotW}}</ref>
