The {{Term/Store|Spring of Power||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.

==Features and Overview==
The {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power}} is a spring located in {{Term|BotW|Deep Akkala|link}}, to the west of the {{Term|BotW|East Akkala Stable|link}} and connects to the {{Term|BotW|Ordorac Quarry|link}} via tunnel. By offering {{Term|BotW|Dinraal's Scale|link}} at the spring, the entrance to the {{Term|BotW|Tutsuwa Nima Shrine|link}} is revealed. A {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} can be found hiding under a rock on outer ledge surrounding the spring.

At some point in the past, {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}} visited the Spring, accompanied by her {{Term|BotW|Champion|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}}, to pray to the Goddess {{Term|BotW|Hylia|link}} in an attempt to awaken her {{Term|BotW|Triforce|display=sealing powers|link}}.

== Trivia ==

* The layout of the {{Term|BotW|Spring of Power}} strongly resembles that of both [[Skyview Spring]] and the [[Earth Spring]] in {{SS}}, featuring identical geometry, waterfalls and architecture. It can be noted however that the Spring of Power has a particularly strong resemblance to the {{Term|SS|Skyview Spring|link}} in particular, featuring nearly identical geometry, surrounding terrain, placement of waterfalls and trees, as well as a long staircase, as seen in Skyward Sword.
* The [[Goddess Crest]] can be seen chiseled into the platform in front of the Goddess Statue at both the [[Spring of Power]] and [[Spring of Wisdom]] in {{BotW}}. It is notably absent however at the [[Spring of Courage]], either too obscured to be seen underneath floating detritus or  having been worn away by the passage of time.
