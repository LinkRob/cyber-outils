The {{Term/Store|Lottery Shop||Series}} is a location in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lottery Shop|Sign|MM3D}}</ref> It is manned by the '''Lottery Shop Attendant''', who is a person listed in the [[Bombers' Notebook]], but is never seen.

==Features and Overview==
The Lottery Shop is located in [[West Clock Town]]. Inside, [[Link]] can buy a ticket with three numbers he selects for 10 [[Rupee]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Would you like the chance to buy your dreams for 10 Rupees?|Lottery Shop Attendant|MM}}</ref> If bought, the storefront will turn and show the back of it, where Link can check the winning numbers during the night of the same day.<ref>{{Cite|We will announce the selected numbers here.|Lottery Shop Attendant|MM}}</ref> If his ticket matches with the winning numbers, Link will receive a prize of 50 Rupees.<ref>{{Cite|Pick any three numbers, and if those are picked, you'll win 50 Rupees.|Lottery Shop Attendant|MM}}</ref> Any ticket Link buys is only valid for one day.<ref>{{Cite|Your lottery ticket is good only through tonight.|Lottery Shop Attendant|MM}}</ref> The shop will only sell tickets from 6 a.m. to 5:59 p.m. and announce the winning numbers from 6 p.m. to 11 p.m.<ref>{{Cite|Closed for the day. Ticket Sales: 6 a.m. ~ 5:59 p.m. Exchanges: 6 p.m. ~ 11 p.m.|Lottery Shop|MM}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Please note that the winning numbers will be announced tonight from 6:00 to 11:00.|Lottery Shop Attendant|MM}}</ref>

The winning numbers are random for each file, but stay the same each time Link goes back in time using the "[[Song of Time]]." Thus, Link can cheat by learning the numbers of all three days and then going back in time to use those same numbers to win the lottery.
