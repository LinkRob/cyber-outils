==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Compendium/Extract|Summerwing Butterfly}}
Summerwing Butterflies can be found in the [[Great Hyrule Forest]] and less scorching areas of the [[Eldin Mountains]]. They can be caught by sneaking up on them or purchased from [[Beedle]] at the [[Kara Kara Bazaar]], [[Snowfield Stable]], and [[Tabantha Bridge Stable]]. Combining one in a [[Cooking Pot]] with {{Plural|BotW|Monster Part|link}} will create a [[Spicy Elixir]], which grants {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} temporary [[Cold Resistance]].<ref name="Inventory"/> They can also be used to make Red [[Dye]] at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]] in [[Hateno Village]].
