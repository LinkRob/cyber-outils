The "{{Term/Store|Tune of Currents||Series, OoA}},"<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 97}}</ref> also known as the "'''Currents Tune''',"<ref>{{Cite|You learned the {{Color|OoA Red|Currents Tune}}! Play it to move from the past to the present! Choose it when you use the {{Color|OoA Red|harp}}!|N/A|OoA}}</ref> is a song in {{OoA}}.

==Location and Uses==
It is the second tune that [[Link]] learns to play on the [[Harp of Ages]]. It is given to him by [[Tokkey]], a man who researches tunes and time traveling, when Link plays the "Tune of Echoes" for him.

The "Tune of Currents" causes time to flow quickly forward.<ref>{{Cite|Ye of many tunes, I grant you a new tune! Time flows on when you play the Tune of Currents! When you play this tune, you'll immediately drift back to your own time!|Tokkey|OoA}}</ref> When Link plays it in the past age, he immediately forwards to the present, no matter what his location. However, if he cannot land in that space in the present age, he will be sent back (just like the Magic Mirror in A Link To The Past).<ref>{{Cite|If you are in a place from which you cannot reach your own time, you'll return to the past.|Tokkey|OoA}}</ref>

A temporary portal will be left at Link's destination, which can be used to travel back to the past age. This temporary portal will disappear when Link uses it, or one of the time portals on the map.

==Trivia==
*The [[Symbols|symbol]] for this tune is the same symbol on [[Din's Pearl]] in {{TWW}}.
