{{Term/Store|Joelle||Series, OoT, OoT3D, MM, MM3D}} is a recurring character in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=186 }}</ref> She is the eldest of the four [[Poe Sisters]].

==Characteristics==
==={{OoT|-}}===
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Joelle - one of the Poe sisters}}<br/>Aim for her when she appears!
}}

Joelle appears along with her Sisters in the [[Forest Temple]] in {{OoT}}. Like her Sister, [[Beth (Poe)|Beth]], her room consists of a three-level staircase and three picture frames, one on each level. One will contain a picture of her and the other two with black canvases. When [[Link]] approaches the picture of her, she will cackle and disappear into another picture frame. If Link shoots the portrait of her with an [[Arrow]], the portrait will go up in flames and her likeness will disappear into another frame. When the third picture is burnt, Joelle will reveal herself and appear at the bottom of the stairs, awaiting Link. If Link targets the Poe, she will disappear and reappear, and spin towards him with flaming torch in hand. The [[Sword]] and [[Bow]] are effective weapons against her, but only when she is visible. After five hits, she will vanish in a puff of smoke, and a red-flamed torch in the main room of the Temple will ignite.
