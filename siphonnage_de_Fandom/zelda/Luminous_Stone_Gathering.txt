"{{Term/Store|Luminous Stone Gathering||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Luminous Stone Gathering}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Zora's Domain}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} has met with {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}, he can speak to {{Term|BotW|Ledo|link}}, who explains that he and {{Term|BotW|Fronk|link}} have been working on repairing the damage to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} caused by {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|That's why my senior apprentice, Fronk, and I are repairing the domain. However, our materials are a bit depleted...|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Link}} inquires further, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} explains that repairing the {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|display=Domain}} requires {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}}, which are plentiful in {{Term|BotW|Upland Zorana|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Materials?] Yes... Repairs require a large number of {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}}. You can find plenty of them around {{Color|BotW Blue|Upland Zorana}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} asks him if he has found any {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}} on his journey.<ref>{{Cite|Have you, by chance, already stumbled across some {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} during your travels?|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He asks for 10, which would help him greatly with repairs.<ref>{{Cite|For instance, if you have {{Color|BotW Red|10 of them}}, that would be a big help...|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} has retrieved 10 {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}}, he can return to {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain}} to give them to {{Term|BotW|Ledo}}.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you! You're really giving me {{Color|BotW Red|10}} {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}}?!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> He then counts out all 10 of them,<ref>{{Cite|In that case, please excuse me while I count them! {{Big|And a oooone! And a twooooo! And a threeeeee! Foooooour! Fiiiiiive! Siiiiiiix! Seveeeeeeen! Eeeeeeeeeeight! Niiiiiiiiine! Aaaand...TEN!}}|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> and he accepts them when he confirms the number is correct.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, my! You really brought all 10! Thank you! Yes, I will happily take those {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} from you!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> As a reward, he initially gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} one {{Term|BotW|Diamond|link}},<ref>{{Cite|Oh, that's right... I must reward you!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref> but he adds a second as a bonus.<ref>{{Cite|And what's more, I'll give you {{Color|BotW Red|one}} extra as a bonus!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>{{Note|If {{Term|BotW|Link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Diamond}} {{Term|BotW|Inventory|link}} is full, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} will hold onto his {{Plural|BotW|Diamond}} until he has room for them.<ref>{{Cite|Or...I'd like to, but you currently don't have room for {{Color|BotW Red|two more}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamonds}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Well... I'd like to, anyway, but you don't currently have room for {{Color|BotW Red|one more}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}}.|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>}}

After this {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} has ended, {{Term|BotW|Ledo}} will continue to accept groups of 10 {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone}} from him, though only in exchange for one {{Term|BotW|Diamond}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you ever find yourself in possession of {{Color|BotW Red|10}} {{Color|BotW Blue|luminous stones}} in the future, I hope you will bring them to me. But if that happens, know that I'll only be able to give you {{Color|BotW Red|one}} {{Color|BotW Blue|diamond}} for them. I would be honored if you would accept it when the time comes!|Ledo|BotW}}</ref>
