{{Term/Store|Puffstool|Puffstools|Series, TMC|plural}} are [[enemy|enemies]] in [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|''The Legend of Zelda'' series]]. They originate from the [[w:c:pikmin:Pikmin (series)|''Pikmin'' series]].

==Characteristics==
==={{TMC|-}}===
{{Figurine|TMC|Puffstool}}
Puffstools in {{TMC}} have red caps and are nearly invincible. If [[Link]] uses the [[Gust Jar]] on them, they turn gray, leaving them open to attacks. Puffstools appear in [[Deepwood Shrine]] and some [[cave]]s. When they shake, they release spores that slow down Link; these spores can be cleared away with the Gust Jar, and sometimes cover [[switch]]es.

==Crossover appearances==
==={{CoH|-}}===
{{Ambig|Start}}
[[File:CoH Puffstool Sprite.png|left]]
Puffstools appear as enemies in {{CoH}}. They behave like the Mushroom enemies from ''{{wp|Crypt of the NecroDancer}}'', remaining stationary and releasing spores every fourth beat. There are a total of four varieties of Puffstool, starting with the standard blue-capped variety, the stronger [[Purple Puffstool]]s, [[Black Puffstool]]s, and explosive [[Red Puffstool]]s, as well as the [[Green Puffstool]]s introduced in [[Symphony of the Mask]]. Each possesses different attack patterns or abilities.
{{Ambig|End}}
