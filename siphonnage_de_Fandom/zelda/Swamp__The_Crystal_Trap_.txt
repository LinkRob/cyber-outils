The '''Swamp''' is a location featured in {{TCT}}.  When {{Term|TCT|Princess Zelda|link}} reaches the {{Term|TCT|Pillar Crossroads|link}}, she will have a choice between taking the swamp, {{Term|TCT|Forest (The Crystal Trap)|display=forest|link}}, or {{Term|TCT|Desert (The Crystal Trap)|display=desert|link}} path.

As Zelda is traveling through the swamp, she will hear the sound of a creature far off in the distance. When she reaches the noise of the creature, the princess will meet {{Term|TCT|Geetchee|link}}, a blue-furred, ugly monster who is trying to dig out something out of the mud. Zelda, thinking that whatever the monster is looking for could be important, scares {{Term|TCT|Geetchee}} away with her {{Plural|TCT|Arrow|link}} and digs up in the place of the creature to find the {{Term|TCT|Large Brass Key|link}}. 

Eventually, Zelda will encounter a path of dry ground to her right that seems to go up a slope and out of the swamp. It is at this point that the young princess must decide whether she should exit the swamp or head deeper into it. If Zelda exits the swamp now, she will end up outside the {{Term|TCT|Cavern (The Crystal Trap)|link}}, just in front of the {{Term|TCT|Iron Door|link}}. However, if she decides to go deeper into the swamp, she will meet up with {{Term|TCT|Geetchee}} again, who is trapped in a pit of quicksand and begs Zelda to save him. The princess hesitates on saving the monster, but {{Term|TCT|Geetchee}} then throws a stick against her feet, causing Zelda to fall into the quicksand as well. The monster calls Zelda stupid and says he has a rope to free himself from the quicksand, which he uses to escape. Watching helplessly as {{Term|TCT|Geetchee}} walks away with the rope, she slowly starts stinking deeper into the quicksand.

If Zelda has the {{Term|TCT|Magic Rope|link}} from {{Term|TCT|Midoro Palace|link}}, the princess will be able to safely get out of the quicksand and exit the swamp. Not possessing the {{Term|TCT|Magic Rope}} will lead to Zelda's demise.
