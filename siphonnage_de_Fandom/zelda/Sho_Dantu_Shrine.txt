The {{Term/Store|Sho Dantu Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Sho Dantu Shrine}}<br/>Two Bombs}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Two Bombs''',<ref name="Trial"></ref> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
{{Term|BotW|Sho Dantu Shrine}} is located in the {{Term|BotW|Gerudo Highlands|link}}, along the path of {{Term|BotW|Karusa Valley|link}}, though it is initially buried beneath the ground. Nearby is a pedestal with a {{Term|BotW|Stone Tablet|link}} in front of it, which instructs {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} to offer a shining blue stone.<ref>{{Cite|If you seek power untold, offer a shining blue stone.|Stone Tablet|BotW}}</ref> The pedestal is surrounded by {{Plural|BotW|Luminous Stone Deposit|link}}, which can be broken for a {{Term|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}} to place on top of it.

Once this has been done, {{Term|BotW|Sho Dantu Shrine}} will rise from the ground. As {{Term|BotW|Link}} enters the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}, he's greeted by its {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Sho Dantu}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Sho Dantu. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Sho Dantu|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
{{Term|BotW|Sho Dantu Shrine}} features three primary chambers, two of which contain pillars with {{Plural|BotW|Shock Switch|link}} at the top. Thought {{Term|BotW|Link}} can ride the nearby {{Plural|BotW|Launcher|link}} to reach them, they are surrounded by a cage, making them inaccessible. In order to complete the Trial, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must place a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} on top of the {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} and blow them up once they reach their apex. This will activate the {{Plural|BotW|Shock Switch}}.

Once the two {{Plural|BotW|Shock Switch}} are active, a door will open, leading to a third chamber. This chamber features another {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}} in a cage, this time with two slanted {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} pointed towards it. Over the entrance to this room is the a doorway leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal|link}}, and two inactive {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} are embedded in the ground on either side.

When placed on a slanted {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}, the square {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} will land in the cage, but the spherical {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} will constantly move back and forth between the {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}}. By placing a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} on top of a {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to explode it once it approaches the {{Term|BotW|Shock Switch}}, activating it. This will raise the {{Plural|BotW|Launcher}} embedded in the ground, but it will also close the portcullis leading to the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. The square {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb}} must be left in the cage, while the spherical one must be moving back and forth. One {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|display=Bomb}} can be detonated while {{Term|BotW|Link}} is standing on a {{Term|BotW|Launcher}}, bringing him up to the same level as the {{Term|BotW|Monk's Pedestal}}. Then the second {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|display=Bomb}} can be detonated, reopening the portcullis.

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} does this, he will be able to access {{Term|BotW|Sho Dantu}}. {{Term|BotW|Sho Dantu}} will commend {{Term|BotW|Link}} for completing the Trial, and they give him a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} as a reward.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Sho Dantu|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Monk}} will then wish {{Term|BotW|Link}} well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Sho Dantu|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} behind the pillar in the left chamber contains a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}.
