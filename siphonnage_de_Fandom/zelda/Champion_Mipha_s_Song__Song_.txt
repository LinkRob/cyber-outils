"{{Term/Store|Champion Mipha's Song||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Song|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|So? What do you think of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha's Song}}?|Kass|BotW}}</ref>

==Learning Location and Uses==
"{{Term|BotW|Champion Mipha's Song (Song)}}" was written by {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} while trying to finish his teacher's {{Term|BotW|Unfinished Song|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ah! Wandering around here and listening to anecdotes about {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}} has truly invigorated me. In fact, it has inspired me to honor her in my own way, separate from my teacher's {{Color|BotW Blue|unfinished song}}.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} finishes his rematch against {{Term|BotW|Waterblight Ganon|link}} inside {{Term|BotW|Divine Beast Vah Ruta|link}}, he will find {{Term|BotW|Kass}} nearby.<ref>{{Cite|That is why I felt inclined to roost here, near the {{Color|BotW Blue|Divine Beast Vah Ruta}}, which {{Color|BotW Blue|Mipha}} herself mastered.|Kass|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Kass}} will then sing the {{Term|BotW|Song|link}} for {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|I've written a song...to try and capture the essence of {{Color|BotW Blue|Champion Mipha}}...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> Listening to it will trigger the "{{Term|BotW|Champion Mipha's Song (Memory)|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Recovered Memory|link}}.
