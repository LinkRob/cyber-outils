The {{Term/Store|Solid Passenger Car||Series, ST}} is an item in {{ST}}.<ref>{{Cite|Solid Passenger Car|Train (Interface)|ST}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ST|Solid Passenger Car}} is a {{Term|ST|Train Car|link}} and part of the {{Term|ST|Spirit Train|link}}, initially stationed at the {{Term|ST|Tower of Spirits|link}} along with the {{Term|ST|Spirit Engine|link}}. {{Term|ST|Link|link}} is granted use of the {{Term|ST|Solid Passenger Car}} by its guardian, {{Term|ST|Anjean|link}}, after the young hero restores the {{Term|ST|Spirit Tracks (Object)|link}} to the {{Term|ST|Forest Sanctuary|link}}, but cannot travel there due to {{Term|ST|Alfonzo|link}}'s train having crashed earlier.

The description of the {{Term|ST|Solid Passenger Car}} reads "Whoever crafted this well-made carriage must have been a true master."
