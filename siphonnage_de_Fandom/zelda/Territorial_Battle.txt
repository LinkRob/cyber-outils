{{Term/Store|Territorial Battle||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 39 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Territorial Battle|Map|HWAoC}}</ref> 

==Overview== 
{{Term|HWAoC|Territorial Battle}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} are given six minutes to capture three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost|link}}. One {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} can be found in the northwest, southwest, and northeast corner of the map. The southwest {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Chuchu|link}}, {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire-Breath Lizalfos|link}}, and a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}}, while the northwest {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} contains their ice variants. The last {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} in the northeast is filled with {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Lizalfos|link}}, and is captured by defeating the {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin|link}} found inside. After all three {{Plural|HWAoC|Outpost}} are captured, a {{Term|HWAoC|Black Moblin}}, {{Term|HWAoC|Blue-Maned Lynel}}, along with {{Plural|HWAoC|Black Bokoblin|link}} and {{Plural|HWAoC|Chuchu}}, capture the only {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} previously held by the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, located at the bottom of the map. The timer resets to three minutes, and the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when the {{Term|HWAoC|Outpost}} is captured. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}} receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}} and 800 {{Plural|HWAoC|Rupee|link}}.
