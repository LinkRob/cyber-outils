The {{Term/Store|Royal Guard Cap|Royal Guard Cap|Series, BotW, HWAoC}} is a recurring {{Term|Series|Item|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard Cap}}<br/>This spiffy cap is part of the uniform for Hyrule's Royal Guard. It's lighter than it looks and easy to move around in. It's a rather rare find.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Royal Guard Cap}}<br/>This spiffy cap is part of the uniform for Hyrule's Royal Guard. Its elegant ornamentation mirrors the crest. It's lighter than it appears and is easy to move about in.|Allies|HWAoC}}</ref> It is exclusive to {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack.

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap}} is obtained through the "{{Term|BotW|EX Royal Guard Rumors|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. During the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} is required to read the {{Term|BotW|Super Rumor Mill V4|link}}, which is located at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rumor has it a Royal Guard uniform can still be found at Hyrule Castle.<br/><br/>Apparently {{Color|BotW Red|Riverside Stable}} has a copy of Super Rumor Mill EX, which details some rumors about this rare outfit.|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref> Within, {{Term|BotW|Traysi|link}} claims that pieces of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set|link}} can be found in the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}}, near the {{Term|BotW|Guard's Chamber|link}}, and on the second floor.<ref>{{Cite|Amongst {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule's soldiers}}, only the best of the best had the honor of guarding the royal family. Apparently, the equipment they wore can still be found somewhere in {{Color|BotW Blue|Hyrule Castle}}. They say a former maid spoke of treasure near the {{Color|BotW Red|Dining Hall}}, in the {{Color|BotW Red|Guards' Chamber}}, and on {{Color|BotW Red|the second floor}}.|Super Rumor Mill V4|BotW}}</ref>

The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap}} can be found in an EX {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Champions' Hall|link}}, which is located on the second floor of the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. After it has been found, {{Term|BotW|Granté|link}} will begin to sell it in {{Term|BotW|Tarrey Town|link}}.{{Note|Prior to the [[The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild#Updates|Version 1.3.3 Update]], {{Term|BotW|Granté}} would not sell any {{Term|BotW|Armor}} that is exclusive to the {{Term|BotW|Breath of the Wild Expansion Pass|link}}. This meant that the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap}} could not be replaced if sold.}} The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap|display=Cap}} is part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Set}}, along with the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Boots|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Uniform|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 190}}</ref> Wearing all three pieces of {{Plural|BotW|Armor|link}} will decrease the amount of {{Term|BotW|Stamina|link}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} uses during a charge attack. The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard Cap}} cannot be enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} or {{Term|BotW|Dye|link|display=Dyed}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}.
