{{Term/Store|Anti-Flame & Lightning Training||Series, HWAoC}} is a level 45 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Anti-Flame & Lightning Training|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>

==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Anti-Flame & Lightning Training}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} in which the {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior|link}} are given five minutes to defeat 200 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}}. One {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} spawns in the northern part of the map and is surrounded by fire monsters, while the other spawns in the south and is surrounded by ice monsters. As each {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} moves further in a certain direction, they will encounter a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Moblin|link}} or an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Moblin|link}}, depending on their location. As they move even further, two {{Plural|HWAoC|Electric Wizzrobe|link}} or two {{Plural|HWAoC|Fire Wizzrobe|link}} spawn. When 200 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy}} are killed, a {{Term|HWAoC|Fire Hinox}} spawns in the upper-right hand corner of the map and an {{Term|HWAoC|Electric Hinox}} in the lower-right hand corner. The timer also resets back to five minutes. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends when they are both killed. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} can be cleared using any two {{Plural|HWAoC|Warrior}}, and when completed for the first time, they receive a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, five {{Plural|HWAoC|Ruby|link}}, fifteen {{Plural|HWAoC|Endura Carrot|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.
