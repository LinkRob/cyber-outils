{{Term/Store|Volga||Series, HW}} is an antagonist in {{HW}}.{{Name Ref|HW}} In the version 1.3 update, he is also a playable Warrior. He is a Dragon Knight whose magical powers allow him to breathe fire and turn into a dragon. He also can turn certain parts of his body into dragon limbs, such as his arm. He relies on powerful attacks to devastate his enemies, although he has a moderate attack speed.

==Abilities==
Volga, in contrast to his cohorts [[Cia]] and [[Wizzro]], favors swift, brutal power over finesse. His weapon is the [[Dragon Spear]] which is of the Fire element. His combos tend to be very quick but also strong with good crowd control. Many of his combos involve him changing parts of his body into his dragon form, such as turning his arm into a clawed limb that sends out a shockwave. His fourth combo has him turning into his dragon form and dive bombing the enemy, causing a huge explosion.

==Biography==
Volga is the general of Cia's army, with whom he wages war on the Kingdom of [[Hyrule]]. At one point, he traded his soul to Cia for great physical strength.<ref>{{Cite Web| url=http://www.gamecity.ne.jp/zelda/character_valga.html | site= Hyrule Warriors Japanese Webpage}}</ref> It is implied that he is not actually human,<ref name="Humans">{{Cite|Leave me be. I have no interest in humans or their wars.|Volga|HW}}</ref> and may possibly be a dragon who can take on human-form.

In "[[The Armies of Ruin]]", Volga first confronts [[Link]] and the [[Hyrulean Forces]] after the [[Dark Forces]] lay siege to [[Hyrule Field (Stage)|Hyrule Field]]. He descends upon the [[Keep|Abandoned Fort]] after the Hyrulean Forces attempt to secure it, initiating a fight.<ref>{{Cite|If you're ready to join your ancestors, then I accept your challenge!|Volga|HW}}</ref> Link brings his health down to half, before Volga ends the battle and decides to kill Link for his interference. He attacks him with his transformed draconian arm,<ref>{{Cite|Get out of my way, boy.|Volga|HW}}</ref> but is interceded by [[Impa]]. Volga attempts to destroy the two of them with a fire blast,<ref>{{Cite|How noble. Enjoy your shared grave.|Volga|HW}}</ref> but finds they had both survived by a magical barrier created from Link's [[Triforce of Courage]] that mysteriously appeared on his hand. Volga then retreats, allowing the Hyrulean Forces to resume their counterattack and to open the Mine Entrance, but swears that he has not been beaten.<ref>{{Cite|You haven't beaten me. You've merely hidden behind your shields, human and otherwise.|Volga|HW}}</ref>

Volga again appears in the [[Valley of Seers]] in "[[The Sorceress of the Valley]]", where he confronts and fights Impa en route to the valley's eastern Keep. She is outmatched by his strength and requests the assistance of either [[Lana]] or [[Sheik]]. He is defeated by either one and escapes from battle. 

After Cia opens the [[Gate of Souls]] to the different eras, Volga travels to {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft (Stage)|link}} in "[[Land in the Sky]]", where he leads the Dark Forces and terrorizes the inhabitants. He faces Link in combat again after [[Fi]] connects the islands of Skyloft with the help of [[Levias]]. Volga is eager to fight Link again,<ref>{{Cite|Ahh, this might just be interesting. Are you ready to fight a real warrior?|Volga|HW}}</ref> and just when he is about to be defeated, he mocks him as he regains his health and grows stronger in strength and speed.<ref>{{Cite|Don't make me laugh! I was expecting a battle worth my time, not this child's play!|Volga|HW}}</ref> He is however weakened after Link and Fi pray to the [[Great Fairy]], who sends [[Fairy|Fairies]] to bring Pumpkin Soup directly to [[Levias]]. Levias' empowered electrical blast weakens Volga, allowing Link to defeat him and he again retreats.<ref>{{Cite|I would have defeated you if not for the Sky Lord's interference...|Volga|HW}}</ref> Until Levias intervenes, Volga cannot be defeated normally. Otherwise he will kneel for a moment before mocking Link and restoring all of his health again.<ref>{{Cite|Disappointing. Come at me again!|Volga|HW}}</ref>

Volga again faces Link a fourth time at the Valley of Seers in the final battle against Cia in the Scenario "[[Valley of Seers#Shining Beacon|Shining Beacon]]". Cia summons him mid-way into the battle and fortifies him with her life energy. He demands to fight with the Allies' commander and charges after [[Princess Zelda]] (or [[Princess Ruto|Ruto]] if playing as Zelda).<ref>{{Cite|Hrah! The enemy commander is MINE!|Volga|HW}}</ref> She however attempts to reason with him instead of fighting, and questions him how he can find joy in using dark magic to fight his battles.<ref>{{Cite|Can you really take pleasure from resorting to dark magic to win your battles?|Princess Zelda|HW}}</ref> She urges him that she needs him to be a noble knight, and not a tool for the Dark Forces.<ref>{{Cite|Open your eyes! I believed you to be a proud dragon war, not a sellsword for darkness!|Princess Zelda|HW}}</ref> Zelda's words manage to convince and bring Volga back to his senses, and he denounces his alliance to the Dark Forces as the dark power is pushed away from him.<ref>{{Cite|No... NO! I won't be ruled by darkness!|Volga|HW}}</ref> He resolves to fight Link one last time in a fair fight and readily advances toward him.<ref>{{Cite|This battle is nearly at an end... Now let me show you how a true warrior finishes a fight!|Volga|HW}}</ref> Link defeats him again, and Volga admits his defeat but without regrets.<ref>{{Cite|I lose, but I do so without regrets...|Volga|HW}}</ref> It is implied he perishes as he falls over.

===Cia's Tale===
{{Stub|HW}}
In the ''Master Quest'' DLC Pack, Cia and her army's backstory and point of view were expanded by additional Scenarios as part of [[Cia's Tale]].

Volga is the dragon guardian of the [[Eldin Caves]], where Cia first encounters him in "[[Eldin Caves#The Dragon of the Caves|The Dragon of the Caves]]". When Cia first became corrupted by Ganondorf's influence, she entered the Eldin Caves to enlist him in her army as its general.<ref>{{Cite|However, controlling an army would require a commander, and Cia realized she would need a powerful warrior to serve as her general.|N/A|HW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|The perfect candidate came readily to mind: The Dragon Knight of the Eldin Caves.|N/A|HW}}</ref> Volga however resists and fights back,<ref name="Humans"/> before eventually retreating from his injuries as reinforcements from [[Forces#Rogue Forces|his Force]] arrive to combat Cia's. In the ensuing war between Cia's, Volga's and the [[Forces#Goron Forces|Goron Forces]], Cia gradually conquers Volga's claimed Keeps before eventually confronting him, fully healed, at the Entry Square Keep. During their second fight, the defeated [[Lizalfos]] and [[Dinolfos]] Chieftains of Volga's army call upon a pair of [[King Dodongo]]s to retaliate. Despite the large beasts threatening her progress, Cia manages to defeat Volga as well as a traitorous Wizzro, who had defected to Volga's Force after witnessing their advantage. She enhances them both with dark magic to ensure their loyalty.<ref>{{Cite|Cia is enhancing Volga and Wizzro with dark magic!|N/A|HW}}</ref> They swear eternal loyalty to her, with Volga's only desire to fight worthy opponents.<ref>{{Cite|My... only desire... is to fight worthy foes.|Volga|HW}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*Volga's name, physical appearance, and magical abilities are a reference to the boss [[Volvagia]] from {{OoT}}. When he turns himself into a dragon, his appearance also resembles Volvagia.
**In addition, the skull visor on his helm is highly reminiscent of Volvagia's skull when it is defeated in {{OoT|-}}.
*When Volga is fought as an enemy, he appears to have a hidden move where he shoots a fireball during his fourth combo. However, there is no way for this attack to be performed when played.
*Curiously, {{Term|HWDE|Volga}} is shown in official material wielding the second form of the {{Term|HWDE|Dragon Spear|link}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Stonecleaver Claw|link}}, instead of the first form, the {{Term|HWDE|Dragonbone Pike|link}}.
