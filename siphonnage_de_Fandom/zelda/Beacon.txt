{{Term/Store|Beacon|Beacons|Series, SS, SSHD|plural}} are recurring {{Plural|Series|Object|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|And so I will give you the power to create {{Color|SSHD Red|beacons}}.|Impa|SSHD}}</ref>{{Name Ref|BotW}}

==Location and Uses==
==={{ST|-}}===
In {{ST}}, Beacons are grey floor tiles with floating octahedral crystals above them. When {{Term|ST|Link|link}} plays the {{Term|ST|Song of Light|link}} near one, its crystal begins to rotate and glow, emitting a beam of light in a particular direction.<ref>{{Cite|You got the {{Color|ST Light Red|Song of Light}}! Play it to activate beacons.|N/A|ST}}</ref> These beams typically point toward the locations of hidden {{Plural|ST|Treasure Chest|link}}, {{Term|ST|Bomb|link|display=Bombable}} walls, or other secrets. While most Beacons lay dormant in plain sight, some Beacons are hidden in the ground and are both revealed and activated by the {{Term|ST|Song of Light}}.

==={{SS|-}}===
In {{SS}}, when [[Link]] places a Beacon on his [[Map]], either on [[The Surface]] or [[The Sky]], a blue column of light will appear, acting as a waypoint to reach the desired location. Beacons can only be placed on [[Overworld]] Maps by pressing the C Button.<ref>{{Cite|When a beacon is marked on your map, a column of light will stand at that location. It will act as your waypoint from afar.|Impa|SSHD}}</ref>

The power to place Beacons is only activated after Link first talks to the aged [[Impa]] in the [[Sealed Temple]], at which point only one Beacon can be placed at a time. Once Link reaches the northern portion of the [[Lanayru Desert]] where the entrance to the [[Lanayru Mining Facility]] is located, [[Fi]] will enable the ability to place up to five Beacons on the map.<ref>{{Cite|Simulations suggest that placing beacons to mark a safe route along these paths before proceeding would significantly reduce risk. I recommend placing {{Color|SSHD Blue|multiple}} beacons.|Fi|SSHD}}</ref>

The [[Sacred Tears]] also emit Beacons when Link collects a [[Light Fruit]] or when he is being chased by a [[Earth Guardian|Guardian]] in the [[Silent Realm]]s.<ref>{{Cite|You got a {{Color|SSHD Gold|Light Fruit}}! This mysterious fruit enables you to see beacons over the positions of Sacred Tears for {{Color|SSHD Red|30 seconds}}!|N/A|SSHD}}</ref>

==={{BotW|-}}===
In {{BotW}}, Beacons can be placed while on the Map or by using the Scope. Up to five of them can be placed at a time, appearing in red, blue, yellow, green, and purple. When a Beacon is placed, it will be shown on the minimap at all times. They can also be seen as columns of light while using the Scope. Beacons can be replaced with any stamp while on the Map.
