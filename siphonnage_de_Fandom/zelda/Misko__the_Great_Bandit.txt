"{{Term/Store|Misko, the Great Bandit||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Misko, the Great Bandit}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Dueling Peaks Stable}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter the twin brothers {{Term|BotW|Domidak|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Prissen|link}}, discussing the location of an unknown object.<ref>{{Cite|I bet it's over there!|Prissen|BotW}}</ref> When they are approached, {{Term|BotW|Prissen}} tells his brother that he has a lead on the location of {{Term|BotW|Misko|link|display=Misko's}} treasure.<ref>{{Cite|I've got the scoop on Misko's {{Color|BotW Red|treasure}}, and it's great! Listen up, Dak...|Prissen|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} notices {{Term|BotW|Link}} listening and halts him before he can reveal his information.<ref>{{Cite|Prissen! Hang on!|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> He calls {{Term|BotW|Link}} rude for eavesdropping on their conversation,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, buddy! What's your problem? You aren't being incredibly rude and listening to our conversationg, are you?|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> and {{Term|BotW|Prissen}} reveals that they are searching for the treasure of the bandit {{Term|BotW|Misko}}.<ref>{{Cite|We're treasure hunters, on the trail of our latest score... The {{Color|BotW Red|treasure of the great bandit Misko}}! It's as good as ours!|Prissen|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} berates his brother for almost giving away their lead,<ref>{{Cite|Prissen! You're going to give it all away!|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> which {{Term|BotW|Prissen}} apologizes for.<ref>{{Cite|Sorry, Dak... I got carried away.|Prissen|BotW}}</ref> As they are busy, they tell {{Term|BotW|Link}} to leave them alone.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, we're busy here, dude. If you need something, take it up with someone else.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref>

If {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to the brothers again, {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} tells him to pay no mind to {{Term|BotW|Prissen}}'s words.<ref>{{Cite|You again? Pay no mind to my little bro's chatter from before. He's got a penchant for running his mouth with crazy talk.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> However, when {{Term|BotW|Link}} asks about the treasure, {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} realizes that they have been figured out.<ref>{{Cite|So... Misko's treasure?] Ahhh shoot. Welp. Can't say I didn't try to throw him off...|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Prissen}} suggests telling {{Term|BotW|Link}} their lead so he will leave them alone,<ref>{{Cite|Hey, Dak... Why don't we just tell him? Maybe he'll leave us alone if we do.|Prissen|BotW}}</ref> as there is no way he could figure out the riddle.<ref>{{Cite|I mean, if we can't figure this riddle out, there's no way someone like him will be able to.|Prissen|BotW}}</ref> Agreeing with {{Term|BotW|Prissen}}, {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} shares that they are searching for {{Term|BotW|Misko}}'s treasure, which would make them rich.<ref>{{Cite|...That's a good point. We're after nothing less than the {{Color|BotW Red|treasure of Misko, the Great Bandit}}! Finding it would mean incredible riches...|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> However, he refuses to give their information to {{Term|BotW|Link}} for free,<ref>{{Cite|I'm not about to give this information away for free. I'm no fool!|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> instead asking for 100 {{Plural|BotW|Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|It'll be {{Color|BotW Red|100 rupees}} to learn what we know.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref>

Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} pays the fee,<ref>{{Cite|[Deal!] Heh. Good deal.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Domidak}} will share the riddle with him. The riddle states, "The little twin steps over the little river. My cave rests above that river's source."<ref>{{Cite|The little twin steps over the little river. My cave rests above that river's source. Misko hid the location of the treasure in some kind of strange secret code.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> He heard this riddle from the former dog walker of the neighbors of one of {{Term|BotW|Misko}}'s underling's descendants.<ref>{{Cite|I heard this from a former dog walker of one of Misko's underling's descendant's neighbors. As solid a lead as any.|Domidak|BotW}}</ref> After {{Term|BotW|Link}} has heard the riddle, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} begins.

The part of the riddle speaking about a "little twin" is referring to the {{Term|BotW|Little Twin Bridge|link}}, south of the {{Term|BotW|Dueling Peaks Stable}}. It crosses over a small branch of the {{Term|BotW|Squabble River|link}}, which is the "little river" in the riddle. {{Term|BotW|Link}} must follow the river further south to {{Term|BotW|Hickaly Woods|link}} where it begins as a small waterfall. Climbing the waterfall leads the young hero to a bombable pile of {{Plural|BotW|Rock|link}}, which hides the cave where {{Term|BotW|Misko}} stored their treasure.

Misko's treasure is made up of a number of {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} and a few {{Plural|BotW|Barrel|link}}. Inside the {{Plural|BotW|Treasure Chest}} are an {{Term|BotW|Opal|link}}, an {{Term|BotW|Amber|link}}, two {{Plural|BotW|Sapphire|link}}, and a {{Term|BotW|Luminous Stone|link}}. The {{Plural|BotW|Barrel}} contain {{Plural|BotW|Roasted Bird Drumstick|link}} or {{Plural|BotW|Seared Steak|link}}. Sitting off in a corner is a group of three {{Plural|BotW|Toasted Hearty Truffle|link}}, and laying on the floor is a {{Term|BotW|Soldier's Spear|link}}. Another bombable wall hides a final {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}, housing a {{Term|BotW|Flameblade|link}}. Opening any {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}} and taking its contents ends the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.
