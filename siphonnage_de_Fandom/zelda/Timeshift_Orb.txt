==Location and Uses==
{{Plural|SS|Timeshift Orb}} are portable {{Plural|SS|Timeshift Stone|link}} that can be found within the [[Pirate Stronghold]] and [[Sky Keep]]. They serve a similar function to {{Plural|SS|Timeshift Stone}}, but they can also be carried around and remain constantly active. Certain areas contain mounds of earth that appear in the past, which can serve as platforms or roadblocks.

If {{Term|SS|Link}} installs the {{Term|SS|Timeshift Orb}} in the Pirate Stronghold in the pedestal near the entrance, the "mouth" outside will raise, allowing {{Term|SS|Fi|link}} to receive data on the [[Sandship]], allowing {{Term|SS|Link|link}} to [[Dowsing|Dowse]] for it.<ref>{{Cite|There is a 60% probability that these masts and these sails are from the ship that protects {{Color|SS Red|Nayru's Flame}}. [...] You can now set your {{Color|SS Red|dowsing}} ability to search for the ship that holds {{Color|SS Red|Nayru's Flame}}.|Fi|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Link}} must use a {{Term|SS|Timeshift Orb}} within Sky Keep to activate conveyor belts in order to move it to separate sections of the same room.
