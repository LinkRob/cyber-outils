The '''Moth Gibdo''' is a flying variant of the [[Gibdo]] in {{TotK}}. Much like their standard variant, Moth Gibdos are vulnerable to fire and electric attacks. 

Upon defeat, the Moth Gibdo may drop Gibdo Bones, Guts, and its wings which can be attached to arrows and weapons to make them fly father when launched.

== Habitat ==
The '''Moth Gibdo''' is often located and found in the [[Gerudo Desert]] and the Gerudo Desert Depths.
