==Location and Uses==
{{Stub|AST}}
The {{Term|AST|LV4 Sword}} is found in the {{Term|AST|Lost Woods|link}}, and is the {{Term|AST|Master Sword|link}} after having been strengthened by {{Term|AST|Link|link}} into the {{Term|AST|Golden Sword|link}} during the events of {{ALttP}}.{{Fact|AST}} It is the only sword that can shoot {{Plural|AST|Sword Beam|link}} and hurt {{Term|AST|Ganon|link}} without using the {{Term|AST|Whirling Blade Technique|link}}.
