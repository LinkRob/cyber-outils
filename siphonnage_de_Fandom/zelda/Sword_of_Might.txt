The '''Sword of Might''', also known as '''Swordsman's Blade,''' and '''Mysterious Sword''', is a [[sword]] in {{EoW}}. It is a sword of mysterious power that is owned by the swordsman [[Link]]. The blade is created by [[Lueburry]] using a mysterious crystal called a [[Might Crystal]], which are created whenever a rift in the world is mended. 

==Overview==
The sword is wielded by Link during the prologue, where he wields it against [[Echo Ganon|a doppelganger]] of [[Ganon]]. 

The sword is then later obtained in [[Suthorn Ruins]], wielded by a [[Dark Echo|doppelganger]] of Link. After defeating [[Echo Link|the clone]], [[Princess Zelda]] acquires the sword, gaining the ability to turn into [[Swordfighter Form]].

The Sword of Might can be upgraded to [[Sword of Might Lv. 2|Level 2]] by giving 10 [[Might Crystal|Might Crystals]] to [[Lueburry]], significantly increasing its damage. It can be further upgraded to [[Sword of Might Lv. 3|Level 3]] with an additional 20 Might Crystals, enhancing its damage even more and unlocking the [[Sword Beam]] attack, which activates when the wielder is at full health.

The Sword of Might deals additional damage against enemies generated from the rifts of Hyrule.

==Trivia==
* The Sword of Might draws some similarities to the [[Master Sword]], being a weapon, normally wielded by Link, that is highly effective against the active threats in the game, more so than regular weaponry.
* Despite being shown with it in artwork, Link does not wield the Sword of Might's Lv. 1 form during his infiltration of Suthorn Ruins, as Lueburry had managed to upgrade the Weapons of Might to Lv. 2 before Link set forth.<ref>{{Cite|The Sword of Might has been forged as best as I am able. I gave him some other weapons, too.|Lueburry's Diary, pg. 5|EoW}}</ref> It is unknown how they downgraded back down to Lv. 1 while in possession of the Echo Links.
* The Sword of Might requiring Energy to wield was intended as a balance to incentivize players to also use their [[Echo]]es in combat, but due to the rarity of Energy drops outside the [[Still World]], it led many players to avoid using [[Swordfighter Form]] whenever it was not required. This problem also came up in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}} with the [[Durability]] System, that incentivized players to seek new weapons/shields/bows as the ones they had would not last forever, causing players to hoard sufficiently useful ones and minimize their use.
* While the Sword of Might is said to be stronger against Rift monsters it has a damage multiplier against even the optional bosses who have little or nothing to do with that aspect of the game.
