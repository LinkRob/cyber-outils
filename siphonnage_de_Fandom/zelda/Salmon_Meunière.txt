{{Term/Store|Salmon Meunière|Salmon Meunières|Series, BotW|plural}} are {{Plural|BotW|Item|link}} in {{BotW}} and {{TotK}}.<ref name="Inventory">{{Cite|{{Big|Salmon Meunière}}<br/>The crispy skin of this fried hearty salmon puts its texture in a class all its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
Salmon Meunières are meals that can be made by {{Term|BotW|Cooking|link}} a [[Hearty Salmon]] with [[Tabantha Wheat]] and [[Goat Butter]]. Regular Salmon Muenières can only be made by adding another ingredient with an effect other than hearty. Eating them will heal several {{Plural|BotW|Heart|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Link|link|display=Link's}} {{Term|BotW|Life Gauge|link}}. The crispy skin of Hearty Salmons puts the texture of this dish into a class of its own.<ref name="Inventory"/>

After calming [[Divine Beast Vah Medoh]], [[Link]] can find [[Kheel]] on her own at the [[Warbler's Nest]]. She'll ask him to retrieve her sisters for their rehearsal, but [[Genli]] may be difficult since she wants Salmon Meunière.<ref>{{Cite|Can you go tell my sisters in Rito Village to stop ditching rehearsal? They won't listen to me, but they might listen to a scary-looking Hylian like you. Although Genli might be a tough sell. She's probably in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}} yelling, {{Big|"I want some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!"}}|Kheel|BotW}}</ref> When Link speaks to Genli, she explains that she can't sing until she gets some Salmon Meunière.<ref>{{Cite|I know {{Color|BotW Blue|Kheel}} wants me to practice singing with her, but I just can't. Not until I get my {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}!|Genli|BotW}}</ref> The rest of their sisters left to retrieve the ingredients, but Genli wonders what's taking them so long.<ref>{{Cite|My sisters already left to get {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} ingredients.|Genli|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|What is taking my sisters so long? I thought they were just going to the {{Color|BotW Red|Slippery Falcon}}.|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

Link can locate and help the sisters put together Salmon Muenière. [[Cree]] is found in [[The Slippery Falcon]], where she'll give Link the Goat Butter she bought.<ref>{{Cite|If I give you this {{Color|BotW Blue|goat butter}}, will you make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli? She should be in the {{Color|BotW Red|kitchen}}.|Cree|BotW}}</ref> [[Kotts]] is found at one of the ponds leading into [[Rito Village]], fishing for Hearty Salmon.<ref>{{Cite|Hiyah! HIYAH! I'm fishing for {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}}!|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> When reminded of her rehearsal, she gives Link the Hearty Salmon she caught.<ref>{{Cite|Please take this {{Color|BotW Blue|hearty salmon}} and go make some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli.|Kotts|BotW}}</ref> Finally, [[Notts]] is found on one of the ridges above Rito Village, and she'll explain that she finished gathering her ingredients early and so began practicing.<ref>{{Cite|It's just that Genli really wanted some {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}, so my sisters and I have been looking for ingredients. I finished early, so I was practicing her to kill time.|Notts|BotW}}</ref> She gives Link her Tabantha Wheat.<ref>{{Cite|Well then...I guess I'll give the {{Color|BotW Blue|Tabantha wheat}} to you! Please make the best {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}} for Genli|Notts|BotW}}</ref>

Returning to Genli, Link can make Salmon Meunière for her. If he gives it to her, she'll eat the whole thing and decide to keep her promise to her sisters and attend the rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|...Huh? My promise to my sisters? I suppose I shouldn't break my promise...|Genli|BotW}}</ref> Genli thanks Link for the Salmon Meunière and asks him to attend their rehearsal.<ref>{{Cite|Anyway, thank you for the amazing {{Color|BotW Blue|salmon meunière}}! It was so soft and flaky and delicious! I'll give this rehearsal my all! ♪Come listen later!♪|Genli|BotW}}</ref>

Only one variation of Salmon Meunière can be made:

* '''Hearty Salmon Meunière''':<ref name="Hearty">{{Cite|{{Big|Hearty Salmon Muenière}}<br/>Restores your health and temporarily increases your maximum hearts.<br/>The crispy skin of this fried hearty salmon puts its texture in a class all its own.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> Made by using the default recipe. When eaten, they'll heal all of Link's Hearts and grant him temporary Hearts.<ref name="Hearty"/>
