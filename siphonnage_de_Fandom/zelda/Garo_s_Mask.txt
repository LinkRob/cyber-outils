The {{Term/Store|Garo's Mask||Series, MM, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 124 {{Exp Game|MM}}}}</ref> It a mask that resembles the head of a [[Garo]] in {{MM|-}} and the head of a [[Garo Master]] in {{MM3D}}.

==Location and Uses==
{{Happy Mask Salesman|<p>That's the {{Color|MM Red|Garo's Mask}}, isn't it?</p><p>If you have that mask, you must be one with quite a bit of courage.</p>}}

The Mask is given to [[Link]] by the [[Gorman Brothers]].<ref>{{Cite|Near the ranch are a pair of men with the mask of an Ikana ninja. Men of poor character who commit evil deeds.|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref> To obtain it, he must go to the [[Gorman Track]] once he has [[Epona]], and ask to race against them for 10 [[Rupees]].<ref>{{Cite|Is that pathetic thing your horse? [...] Why don't you give us 10 Rupees for the chance to race against us? If you win, we'll give you somethin' nice, kid.|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref> If Link wins, they will award him with the Garo's Mask.<ref>{{Cite|We gotta give him something. Well, he's a kid, so what could he do with it? It should be fine... In exchange, you can't tell anyone where you got this!|Gorman Brothers|MM}}</ref>

Wearing it allows entry into [[Ikana Canyon]],<ref>{{Cite|That is the mask of the leader of the ninjas who once spied on the hilltop castle with the blood-stained history. With that, you may be able to call out their spirits that even now are still wandering. Perhaps you may be able to save the souls that wander beyond here... Yee-hee-hee.|Poe Collector|MM}}</ref> as well as the ability to encounter and battle Garo Ninjas. If defeated, the Garo Ninja will reward Link with a tip that may help him on his quest.<ref>{{Cite|R-regrettable... Although my rival, you were spectacular. I shall take my bow by opening my heart and revealing my wisdom...|Garo|MM}}</ref>

If a [[ReDead]] sees the Mask, it will start dancing, allowing Link to walk past it without being attacked.<ref>{{Cite|It seems the ReDeads that appear in Ikana Castle start dancing when the Captain's Hat, Gibdo Mask or Garo's Mask are worn. But that does not change things much...|Gossip Stone|MM}}</ref>

==Trivia==
*This is one of the few duplicated masks in the game. The other masks with this characteristic are the [[Keaton Mask]], which each of the [[Bombers Secret Society of Justice|Bombers]] claim to have, [[Romani's Mask]], which is owned by all customers of [[Latte]], the [[Couple's Mask]], which is a symbol of marriage, and [[Kafei's Mask]], which [[Anju]] also has.
*The Garo's Mask is one of six masks that are necessary to complete the game, the others being the three transformation masks, the [[Gibdo Mask]], and the [[Captain's Hat]].
*The Gorman Brothers also appear to be wearing Garo's Masks when they sabotage [[Cremia]] on the Second Night.
