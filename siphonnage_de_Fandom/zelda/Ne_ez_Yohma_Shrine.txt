The {{Term/Store|Ne'ez Yohma Shrine||Series, BotW}},<ref name="Trial">{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|{{Big|Ne'ez Yohma Shrine}}<br/>Pushing Power}}|Map|BotW}}</ref> designated by the Trial name '''Pushing Power''',<ref name="Trial"/> is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} in {{BotW}}.

==Entrance to the Shrine==
The {{Term|BotW|Ne'ez Yohma Shrine}} is located in the middle of {{Term|BotW|Zora's Domain|link}} below {{Term|BotW|King Dorephan|link}}'s {{Term|BotW|Throne Room|link}}. It is surrounded by a pool of {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}} that {{Plural|BotW|Fleet-Lotus Seeds|link}} and {{Plural|BotW|Bright-Eyed Crab|link}} can be found in. Upon entering the {{Term|BotW|Ne'ez Yohma Shrine}}, the {{Term|BotW|Monk|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Ne'ez Yohma|link}}, greets him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|To you who sets foot in this shrine... I am Ne'ez Yohma. In the name of the Goddess Hylia, I offer this trial.}}|Ne'ez Yohma|BotW}}</ref>

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|BotW|Ne'ez Yohma Shrine}} consists of one large room that slopes upwards. The entrance starts at the bottom and leads down a platform to the right. Across the platform, a gate blocks {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} from the {{Term|BotW|Monk}}. In order to open the gate, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must place a large {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} in a pedestal, which the pedestal is located next to the gate.

Above the small platform, a {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link|display=Waterfall}}, sloped enough for {{Term|BotW|Link}} to walk up, carries {{Plural|BotW|Boulder|link}} that {{Term|BotW|Link}} must dodge. Along the waterfall, many large blocks create barriers that roll the {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} at different angles. A laser also creates an obstacle for {{Term|BotW|Link}} if he tries to climb the waterfall. The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} sits at the top of the waterfall and {{Term|BotW|Link}} must make his way to the top.

After reaching the top, the {{Plural|BotW|Boulder}} will stop rolling down the waterfall. Using {{Term|BotW|Cryonis|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} must shove the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} off of its platform and move it down the waterfall into the pedestal. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also knock the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} to the left or right and use the straight platform to roll the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} down to the pedestal.

Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb}} is in the pedestal, the gate will open and {{Term|BotW|Link}} will be able to access {{Term|BotW|Ne'ez Yohma}}. He grants Link a {{Term|BotW|Spirit Orb|link}} for completing the Trial,<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|Your resourcefulness in overcoming this trial speaks to the promise of a hero... In the name of Goddess Hylia, I bestow upon you this Spirit Orb.}}|Ne'ez Yohma|BotW}}</ref> wishing him well before fading away.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Monk Blue|May the Goddess smile upon you.}}|Ne'ez Yohma|BotW}}</ref>

A {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest|link}} containing a {{Term|BotW|Zora Spear|link}} can be found atop one of the blocks along the waterfall. After reaching the top of the waterfall, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can take a side path that leads back down the waterfall and stretches above the middle. Using this platform, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can drop down onto the block and open the {{Term|BotW|Treasure Chest}}. {{Term|BotW|Link}} can also use {{Term|BotW|Cryonis}} from below and jump to the platform.
