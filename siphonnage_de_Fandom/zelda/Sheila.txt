{{Term/Store|Sheila||Series, TMC}} is a character in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 271}}</ref>

==Biography==
{{Term|TMC|Sheila}} is a woman in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. During the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|link}}, she wonders if the {{Plural|TMC|Minish|link}} will bring her happiness.<ref>{{Cite|They say the Picori bring happiness to all the humans they meet... I wonder when they will bring happiness to me...|Sheila|TMC}}</ref>

After the {{Term|TMC|Picori Festival|display=Festival}} ends, she can be found near {{Term|TMC|Mayor Hagen's House|link}}, focused on the bell nearby.<ref>{{Cite|There's an old legend that something big will happen when Hyrule's bell rings. Everyone is afraid of what it might mean to ring the bell, so they avoid it.|Sheila|TMC}}</ref> When {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} rings the bell using the {{Term|TMC|Roc's Cape|link}}, revealing a {{Term|TMC|Piece of Heart|link}}, she is moved beyond words.<ref>{{Cite|I think the time has come. The time spoken of in legends. I’m...moved beyond words...|Sheila|TMC}}</ref>

{{Term|TMC|Sheila}} is one of the potential characters to have a random {{Term|TMC|Kinstone Fusion|link}} available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}, don't you? Well, OK! Let's give it a try.|Sheila|TMC}}</ref>
