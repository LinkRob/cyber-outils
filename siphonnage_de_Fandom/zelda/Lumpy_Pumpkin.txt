The {{Term/Store|Lumpy Pumpkin||Series, SS}} is a recurring location in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book=HH |page=36 {{Exp Game|SS}}}}</ref> The {{Term|HWDE|Lumpy Pumpkin}} goes unnamed in {{HW}}. 

==Features and Overview==
==={{SS|-}}===
{{Fi
|quote1= This is the {{Color|SS Blue|Lumpy Pumpkin}}, a refreshment-distribution hub run by {{Color|SS Blue|Pumm}}. It has established local renown for its pumpkin soup.
}}

In {{SS}}, the {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin}} is a pumpkin-shaped bar located on {{Term|SS|Pumpkin Landing|link}}. The {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin}} is owned by {{Term|SS|Pumm|link}} and his daughter, {{Term|SS|Kina|link}}. {{Term|SS|Kina}} works as a waitress, and {{Term|SS|Pumm}} works as a bartender.<ref>{{Cite|The Lumpy Pumpkin is a little gathering spot on an oblong island to the southeast of Skyloft. The owner's daughter, Kina, works there. She has the most lovely voice.|Owlan|SS}}</ref> The pub is famous for its {{Term|SS|Pumpkin Soup|link}}, known all over the world for its ability to restore health,<ref>{{Cite|Their famous pumpkin soup is popular in Skyloft for its nutrient content and revitalizing properties.|Fi|SS}}</ref> and many bird riders come here to rest.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to every bird rider's favorite rest stop, the Lumpy Pumpkin!|Pumm|SS}}</ref>

If {{Term|SS|Link|link}} talks to {{Term|SS|Kina}}, she will tell him to ignore the {{Plural|SS|Rupee|link}} and {{Term|SS|Piece of Heart|link}} atop the room's chandelier.<ref>{{Cite|Have the Rupees sitting on top of the chandelier caught your eye? Don't go thinking that you can get those down, you hear? You might think that you could knock the Rupees down by bumping something and making the chandelier shake, but... Don't even think about it! Are we clear?|Kina|SS}}</ref> Throughout the {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin}}, {{Term|SS|Link}} can read notes warning customers not to knock down the fancy chandelier.<ref>{{Cite|No roughhousing on the balcony! Big ruckuses cause the chandelier to shake.|N/A|SS}}</ref> If he walks up the staircase and rolls into the balcony twice in rapid succession, the chandelier will collapse; {{Term|SS|Link}} can now go back down to the first floor and collect the {{Plural|SS|Rupee}} and the {{Term|SS|Piece of Heart}}. However, {{Term|SS|Pumm}} will scold {{Term|SS|Link}} for his actions and makes him work at the {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin}} for free to pay off the cost of the chandelier by delivering {{Term|SS|Pumpkin Soup}},<ref>{{Cite|What is wrong with you?! Just look at what you've done! You broke my fancy, custom-ordered chandelier! You're going to be working for me for free until you pay off every single Rupee that chandelier cost me!|Pumm|SS}}</ref> storing {{Plural|SS|Pumpkin|link}} for harvest,<ref>{{Cite|Go on out and talk with Kina. I need you to help her with the pumpkin harvest!|Pumm|SS}}</ref> and playing his {{Term|SS|Goddess's Harp|link}} along with {{Term|SS|Kina}}'s singing to entertain the patrons.<ref>{{Cite|I got it! I've got the perfect job for you! But that job can only be done at night. Wait until nightfall and come back and see me, all right? Hey, it's you! Oh, I know that look. You're fixing to play that harp of yours, aren't you?|Pumm|SS}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|All right then! Hey! Kina! ... This kid is going to accompany you while you sing! Now how about letting your Pops hear that beautiful voice of yours, eh?!|Pumm|SS}}</ref> After accomplishing these tasks, {{Term|SS|Pumm}} will announce that Link is done paying his dues and rewards him a {{Term|SS|Piece of Heart}} for his all his hard work.<ref>{{Cite|With this, I say we're even! You don't have to work for me anymore. Great job! [...] Considering all the hard work you put in around here, I feel like I should give you a little something. Take this!|Pumm|SS}}</ref> {{Term|SS|Pumm}} then proceeds to buy a new chandelier to replace the old one.<ref>{{Cite|You know, I'm going to buy a new chandelier to replace that old one! It really fancied up the place.|Pumm|SS}}</ref> Alternatively, {{Term|SS|Link}} can choose to not complete the tasks {{Term|SS|Pumm}} assigned him and exit the pub after breaking the chandelier, but he will not be able to obtain the {{Term|SS|Piece of Heart}} from {{Term|SS|Pumm}} until he does.

When {{Term|SS|Link}} is searching for a means of communicating with {{Term|SS|Levias|link}} in the {{Term|SS|Thunderhead|link}}, {{Term|SS|Pumm}} gives him a large basin full of {{Term|SS|Pumpkin Soup}} as an offering,<ref>{{Cite|Levias... What about him? Sure, I know Levias well, but I wasn't aware anything was wrong... Every year I offer him an entire basin full of my pumpkin soup! But lately a huge thunderhead has been brewing there, and I haven't yet made this year's offering. I tell you, I feel horrible about it! You say you'll deliver a pot of my soup to Levias? Huh. I don't know how you plan to fly into that huge thunderhead brewing out there, but... If that's the case though, I'm going to do everything I can to make Levias the best batch of soup ever! You wait right here! I'm getting inspired to fire up some soup like I've never souped before!|Pumm|SS}}</ref> which he can transport there using {{Term|SS|LD-301S Scrapper|link}}.

==Other Appearances==
==={{HW|-}}===
{{Noncanon|Start}}
In {{HW}}, the {{Term|HWDE|Lumpy Pumpkin}} appears on the {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft (Stage)|link|display= Skyloft}} {{Term|HWDE|Stage|link}}, north of the West Town {{Term|HWDE|Keep|link}} and south of the S. Rockface {{Term|HWDE|Keep}}. The {{Term|HWDE|Lumpy Pumpkin}} and {{Term|HWDE|Pumpkin Landing|link}} appear crashed into {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft}} on their side, with the whole island tilted at an angle against {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft}}. Despite the {{Term|HWDE|Lumpy Pumpkin}} now touching {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft}}, the {{Plural|HWDE|Warrior|link}} still cannot walk on {{Term|HWDE|Pumpkin Landing}} or go inside the {{Term|HWDE|Lumpy Pumpkin}}, and only the exterior of the {{Term|HWDE|Lumpy Pumpkin}} can be seen. The {{Term|HWDE|Pumpkin Soup|link}} used to attract {{Term|HWDE|Levias|link}} in the "{{Term|HWDE|Land in the Sky|link}}" {{Term|HWDE|Scenario|link}} looks to be the same {{Term|HWDE|Pumpkin Soup}} made at the {{Term|HWDE|Lumpy Pumpkin}}, but the making of the soup is never seen. The {{Term|HWDE|Pumpkin Soup}} is instead found in various places throughout {{Term|HWDE|Skyloft}}.
{{Noncanon|End}}

==Trivia==
*Many of the characters in the {{Term|SS|Lumpy Pumpkin}} vaguely resemble the characters in {{Term|TPHD|Telma's Bar|link}} from {{TP}}. {{Term|SS|Keet|link}} resembles {{Term|TPHD|Shad|link}}, {{Term|SS|Rusta|link}} resembles {{Term|TPHD|Auru|link}}, {{Term|SS|Kina|link}} resembles {{Term|TPHD|Ashei|link}} and {{Term|SS|Pumm|link}} resembles {{Term|TPHD|Telma|link}}.
*{{Term|SS|Pumm}} and {{Term|SS|Kina}}'s names, when put together ({{Term|SS|Pumm}}-{{Term|SS|Kina}}), form a pun on the word "pumpkin".
