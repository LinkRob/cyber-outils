'''Roperi's Mother''' is a character in the {{OoA|Himekawa}}.

==Biography==
Roperi's Mother lived with her daughter [[Roperi]] and husband {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi|link}} before the [[manga]]'s events. When [[Queen Ambi]] commanded the black tower to be built, {{Term|OoA (Himekawa)|Jacobi}} was taken as a slave to construct it.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I can't tell you how many people are suffering because of that tower. Roperi's father went four months ago, and she hasn't seen him since.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 83}}</ref> Along with many other citizens of [[Labrynna]], Roperi's Mother and her daughter were brought to the [[Hidden Village#Oracle of Ages (Himekawa)|Hidden Village]] by [[Sir Raven]] for protection.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... But before they can be executed, I bring them here.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 82}}</ref>  

Roperi's Mother makes her first appearance as Roperi is dancing around, because the seed Sir Raven had given her sprouted.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= ... The seed Raven gave me sprouted!...|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 71}}</ref> As the girl announces this, her mother tells her that is nice.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= That's nice, Roperi.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 71}}</ref> She isn't seen again until later on while Roperi is staring at the plant. Roperi's Mother asks if she is still staring at it and notices there is a bud.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Roperi, are you still staring at that plant?... Oh, there's bud!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 118}}</ref> Roperi voices hope that her father will come home in time to see it bloom.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= I hope Dad comes home in time to see the flower bloom.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 118}}</ref> Her Mother says [[Link]] and Raven are sure to help him and to believe in them and wait.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Link and Raven are sure to help him. Believe in them, and wait.|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 118}}</ref> 

[[Veran]] discovers that Sir Raven is actually allied against her puppet, Queen Ambi, so she sends the monster [[Ramrock]] to destroy the village.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Rise, Ramrock... Veran, the Sorceress of Shadows, commands it!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 127}}</ref> Roperi's Mother screams her daughter's name as she runs onto the fields to keep Ramrock from crushing their crops.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Roperi!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 129}}</ref> Link successfully defeats the monster. Sir Raven stands in front of the villagers, Roperi and her Mother included, as Queen Ambi's forces lay siege to the hidden village. The army's commander accuses Raven of treason.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Raven, you are the worst scum of all...Pretending loyalty but working against the queen all the while!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 132}}</ref> Roperi's Mother hugs Roperi close as Raven asks if following orders truly makes one a knight, even if the orders come from a tyrant torturing her people.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= What is loyalty? Does simply following orders make you a knight? Even the orders of a tyrant who tortures her people?|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 133}}</ref> Sir Raven and the village's defenders fight back the army until archers surround them.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Give up! Your village is surrounded by archers!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 136}}</ref> Roperi's Mother is shown with her arms protectively around her daughter as they are threatened to be turned into pincushions.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Surrender or we'll turn you into pincushions!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 136}}</ref> Sir Raven surrenders himself for the sake of the villagers, causing Roperi to sob in her Mother's arms. Roperi's Mother and the remaining villagers who weren't taken to the tower hide in a cave.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= They captured everyone and took them to the tower!|book= {{OoA|Himekawa|-}}|publisher= VIZ Media|page= 153}}</ref> She shows happiness at seeing Link arrive with Sir Raven unharmed once he is rescued. 

At the manga's end, Roperi's Mother is reunited with her husband, their daughter between them as they embrace. She is present when [[Nayru]], [[Ralph]], and Link return to present day Labrynna.
