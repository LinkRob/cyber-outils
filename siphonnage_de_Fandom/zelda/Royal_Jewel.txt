The {{Term/Store|Royal Jewel|Royal Jewels|Series, FSA|plural}} are {{Plural|Series|Item|link}} in {{FSA}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH|publisher= Dark Horse Books|page= 120}}</ref>

==Overview==
The {{Plural|FSA|Royal Jewel}} are based on the [[Wikipedia:Classical element|classical elements]]: {{Term|Series|Earth|link}}, {{Term|Series|Fire|link}}, {{Term|Series|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and {{Term|Series|Wind|link}}. In {{FSA}}, the {{Term|FSA|Four Sword Sanctuary|link}} lies in ruins and only a few remains lay on the {{Plural|FSA|Royal Jewel}}' pedestals. The {{Plural|FSA|Links|link}} need them in order to gain entrance to the {{Term|FSA|Tower of Winds|link}} to save {{Term|FSA|Princess Zelda|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Link... You've returned to where you started, the Four Sword Sanctuary. Now, give us the four royal jewels... Hear me, heavens... Hear me, earth... Heed the light of the royal jewels enshrined here, and restore to us... the Tower of Winds!!|Maiden|FSA}}</ref> They are in the possession of the spirits of four {{Plural|FSA|Knight of Hyrule|link}}, who were cursed to roam as {{Term|FSA|Stalfos|link}} in the {{Term|FSA|Dark World|link}} until the {{Plural|FSA|Links}} free them. By doing so, the {{Plural|FSA|Royal Jewel}}, which were also cursed and turned into gates to the {{Term|FSA|Dark World}}, are cleansed.<ref>{{Cite|The jewel was also cursed, and it became a portal to the Dark World.|Blue Knight|FSA}}</ref>
