{{Term/Store|Bomb Storage||Series, TFH}} is a {{Term|TFH|Level|link}} in {{TFH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book |book=E |publisher=[[Dark Horse Books]] |page=160 }}</ref> It is the second {{Term|TFH|Level}} in the {{Term|TFH|Fortress|link}} {{Term|TFH|Area}}.

==Themes and Navigation==
The {{Term|Series|Level}} takes place within the walls of a giant castle towering high up in the air, filled with many traps and obstacles. As its name implies, it is a storage hold of {{Plural|TFH|Bomb|link}}, although this only becomes obvious once the {{Plural|TFH|Links|link}} reach the {{Term|TFH|Level}}'s {{Plural|TFH|Boss|link}}, the {{Plural|TFH|Hinox Brothers|link}}, who actively throw {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} during their fight. {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} are also one of the {{Term|TFH|Level}}'s usable {{Plural|TFH|Item}}, and are used to solve a variety of puzzles.

In '''Stage 1''', the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} appear in a corner section. {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} and a pair of {{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar|link}} are immediately provided for use. To move on from the area, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must cross over the large gap by propelling each other across using the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}}. Two {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are propelled over the first gap to a pair of {{Plural|TFH|Floor Switch|link}}, which creates a bridge for the third {{Term|TFH|Links}}. The heroes then notice a cracked window within a balcony. A {{Term|TFH|Bomb Flower|link}} growing nearby must be propelled with the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}} towards it, which will shatter the glass and reveal a {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch|link}}. Using the same method, the {{Term|TFH|Switch}} is activated and lowers the bridge to the next area. In this same area, the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can collect a string of green {{Plural|TFH|Rupee|link}} floating over some gaps by having one {{Term|TFH|Links}} propel another across, or simply swinging their {{Plural|TFH|Sword}} to collect them from the sides. Over the bridge, another balcony window conceals a {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch}}. As there is no {{Term|TFH|Bomb Flower}}, one {{Term|TFH|Links}} must place a {{Term|TFH|Bomb}} so that another can propel it across. Hitting the {{Term|TFH|Switch}} activates three platforms that form a bridge to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway|link}}. A nearby {{Term|TFH|Treasure Chest|link}} containing 20 {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} can be reached by throwing one of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} onto the ledge.

In '''Stage 2''', the area splits into three paths, each with a switch and a large gate barricading the way. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must move onto these paths individually, by again propelling themselves across using the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}}, preferably so that the {{Term|TFH|Links}} with the {{Term|TFH|Bomb}}s is on the furthest left, and the two {{Plural|TFH|Links}} with the {{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}} are on the middle and right paths. Pressing the three {{Plural|TFH|Floor Switch}} opens the gate, however the right and middle paths are blocked by cracked {{Plural|TFH|Block|link}}, and the leftmost path is blocked by flames. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} then have to work together to help each other cross their respective paths, using {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} to demolish the {{Plural|TFH|Block}}, and the {{Term|TFH|Gust Jar}} to blow out the flames.

The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} walk down their respective paths, while fighting {{Plural|TFH|Hardhat Beetle|link}} along the way. Eventually the middle and right paths combine, reuniting these two {{Plural|TFH|Links}}, and the paths become more narrow. One of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} can be propelled over to the left path, which leads to a raised platform with a {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch}} nearby. The {{Term|TFH|Links}} with {{Plural|TFH|Bomb}} should throw a {{Term|TFH|Bomb}} over the gap to hit it; this becomes easier to achieve by forming a {{Term|TFH|Totem|link}} with a second {{Term|TFH|Links}}. Hitting this {{Term|TFH|Switch}} activates a moving platform that allows the right path to continue forward. Two of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must ride this platform ahead, so that they can form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} to reach another {{Term|TFH|Switch}} that activates another moving platform for the other {{Term|TFH|Links}} on the left path. Once all three {{Plural|TFH|Links}} are reunited, they can form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} of three to hit a switch that lowers a bridge to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}.

'''Stage 3''' features an aerial lift attached to an overhead cable hanging over a long pit, which the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must operate by propelling it forward with the {{Plural|TFH|Gust Jar}}. {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}} can be found along the cable, which can be grabbed by forming a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} of three and carefully throwing the top {{Term|TFH|Links}} at them. {{Plural|TFH|Arrow Trap|link}} on the rear walls will fire {{Plural|TFH|Arrow|link}} as the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} move the lift forward. The lift brings them to a tower with an opening and closing {{Term|TFH|Shock Switch}}, which can be hit by throwing a {{Term|TFH|Bomb}} over. This activates a tower with spinning shields holding another {{Term|TFH|Switch}}. The {{Plural|TFH|Links}} must then form a {{Term|TFH|Totem}} of three and throw another {{Term|TFH|Bomb}} into this tower to activate its {{Term|TFH|Switch}}, lowering the bridge to the {{Term|TFH|Triforce Gateway}}. On the other side of the pit is a {{Term|TFH|Squiddy|link}} and several {{Plural|TFH|Jar|link}} with {{Plural|TFH|Heart|link}} and {{Plural|TFH|Rupee}}, plus lever {{Term|TFH|Switch}} which can be struck to immediately move the lift from one side of the long pit to the other, should any of the {{Plural|TFH|Links}} get stuck.

'''Stage 4''' is where the {{Plural|TFH|Hinox Brothers|link}} are fought, who throw {{Plural|TFH|Bombs}} in the {{Plural|TFH|Links}}' direction from gated balconies.
