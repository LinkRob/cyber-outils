"{{Term/Store|The Better to Hear You With||Series, SSBU}}" is a {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}} event for {{SSBU}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Event: The Better to Hear You With}}<br/>10/13/2020 |author= Nintendo |published= October 13, 2020 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/a0c1a218/index.html |title= Event: The Better to Hear You With |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref>

==Overview==
"{{Term|SSBU|The Better to Hear You With}}" is an event wherein a greater number of {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit|link}} with unusually large ears randomly appear on the {{Term|SSBU|Spirit Board|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= Spirits gifted with larger ears than most are coming to the Spirit Board en masse!|author= Nintendo |published= October 13, 2020 |retrieved= August 15, 2022 |url= https://www-aaaba-lp1-hac.cdn.nintendo.net/en-US/a0c1a218/index.html |title= Event: The Better to Hear You With |site= Nintendo.net |type= }}</ref> Additionally, certain Legendary {{Plural|SSBU|Spirit}} befitting this theme are set to appear at set times each day.
