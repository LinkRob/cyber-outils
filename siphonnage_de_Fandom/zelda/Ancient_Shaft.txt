==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Ancient Shafts can be found by defeating {{Plural|BotW|Guardian|link}} or within chests in {{Plural|Series|Ancient Shrine|link}}. They are also sold by the travelling merchant {{Term|BotW|Teli|link}}, who can be found on the path northwest of {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}. Like other {{Plural|Series|Ancient Material|link}}, they were made 10,000 years ago to be used in the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} of the {{Plural|BotW|Sheikah|link}}. They are made of a sturdy, yet unknown material.<ref name="Inventory"/> Ancient Shafts can also be obtained infinitely through the "[[Glitches in Breath of the Wild|Ancient Part Glitch]]".

During the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|Slated for Upgrades|link}}", {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can give {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}} three {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shaft}} to upgrade his {{Plural|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} {{Plural|BotW|Rune|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|You'll need {{Color|BotW Red|three ancient shafts}} to power up your {{Color|BotW Blue|Remote Bombs}}.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Ancient Oven|link}} in the {{Term|BotW|Akkala Ancient Tech Lab}} also requires {{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shaft}} to make certain pieces of {{Term|BotW|Ancient Soldier Gear|link}}. They can be used to make {{Term|BotW|Gray Dye|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}} or exchanged for {{Term|BotW|Mon|link}} at the {{Term|BotW|Fang and Bone|link}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Ancient Shaft}} are required to upgrade the following pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}} at a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy Fountain|link}}.
