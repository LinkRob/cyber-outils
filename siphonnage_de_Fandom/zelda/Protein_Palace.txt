{{Term/Store|Protein Palace||Series, BotW}} is a General Store in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome to {{Color|BotW Blue|Protein Palace}}, the hot spot for on-the-rocks grilling!|Aji|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Protein Palace is an outdoor barbecue shop in [[Goron City]] that sells [[Food]] that has been [[Cooking|grilled]]. It is managed by the [[Goron]] [[Aji]]. Aji states that, since Gorons normally eat rocks, the shop is mainly intended for tourists.<ref>{{Cite|I'm glad ya asked that... I'm happy to explain anything! Well, take a look around. This is a grilling joint! Normally Gorons only eat rocks. Mmm, the good stuff! Still, we sell this kinda thing for the tourists. They love it. Come on! Eat up, and get yourself a meaty muscle body like us Gorons!|Aji|BotW}}</ref>

[[Offrak]] can be found sitting in front of the grill during the night.
