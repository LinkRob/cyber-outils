'''Kamonamo Ducks''' are found in {{FPTRR}}, solely in the {{Term|FPTRR|Steamy Marsh}}. They will run away from {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} when he approaches. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} chases one for a sufficient period of time, it will simply fly into the air and burst into feathers, denying {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} any items in the process. The best method to battle these enemies is to use a {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}} or stun them with a {{Term|FPTRR|Bomb|link}}. They usually drop {{Term|FPTRR|Tough Meat|link}}.
