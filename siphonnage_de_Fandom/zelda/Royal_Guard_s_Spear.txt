{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear}} are {{Plural|Series|Weapon|link}} that are part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Gear|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> They were crafted by the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} using {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} to fight the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, and while they have a high attack power, a design flaw left them with low durability.<ref name="Inventory"/> They can be found in the interior of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}} as they are intended to be wielded by the {{Plural|BotW|Soldier|link}} who previously served as guards. In the {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|display=Castle}}, a {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear}} can be found hidden in the fireplace in the {{Term|BotW|Dining Hall|link}}, and another can be found wielded by a {{Term|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}.

If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Parcy|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}, she will reveal that she wants to find the gear that belonged to the {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} of {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle}}.<ref>{{Cite|Have you gone to the giant castle north of here? That's Hyrule Castle. It's said to have fallen to ruin over a hundred years ago. I used to sneak in there to salvage ancient treasures, but it's too dangerous to go in there now. These days it's surrounded by an eerie mist and savage machine-like creatures. Back when it was safer, I went in looking for this ultra-rare equipment that once belonged to the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard}}. It's legendary among collectors, but I was never able to find any...|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear}} is one of the {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} {{Term|BotW|Link}} can bring to her for a reward, which {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} immediately recognizes.<ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that spear you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> She will be grateful and give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}} in return.<ref>{{Cite|Thank you. You have shown me something truly spectacular. I need to go out and find one of these myself.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Here's a token of my appreciation.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> Following this exchange, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can continue to trade {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear}} to {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} for a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}}, a {{Term|BotW|Ruby|link}}, or a {{Term|BotW|Sapphire|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that spear... Will you trade it for a topaz?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, that spear... Will you trade it for a ruby?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Hey, that spear... Will you trade it for a sapphire?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear}} are made of metal, and they will therefore attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. If {{Term|BotW|Link}} drops them in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, they will sink, but they are incapable of burning when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}. They can also be manipulated using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}} on the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}}.
