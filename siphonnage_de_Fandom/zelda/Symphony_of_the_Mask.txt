{{Term/Store|''Symphony of the Mask''||Series, CoH}} is the third downloadable content pack for {{CoH}}.<ref name="Partner Showcase">{{Cite Web|quote= |author= Nintendo |published= July 20, 2020 |retrieved= July 20, 2020 |url= https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kNtacp8-GoI |title= Nintendo Direct Mini: Partner Showcase {{!}} July 2020 |site= YouTube |type= video}}</ref> It is available as part of the {{Term|CoH|Cadence of Hyrule Season Pass|link}}.

==Overview==
{{Term|CoH|Symphony of the Mask}} introduces the {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid|link}} as a playable character, as well as a new map, story, and {{Plural|CoH|Song|link}}.<ref name="Partner Showcase"/>

{{Term|CoH|Symphony of the Mask}} follows the story of the {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid}}, who has the ability to change {{Plural|CoH|Mask|link}} in order to make use of their respective powers, akin to the {{Plural|MM3D|Mask}} from {{Term|MM3D|Termina|link}} in {{MM}}. The {{Plural|CoH|Mask}} available to the {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid}} are the {{Term|CoH|Deku Mask|link}}, the {{Term|CoH|Skull Mask|link}}, the {{Term|CoH|Darknut Mask|link}}, the {{Term|CoH|Zora Mask|link}}, the {{Term|CoH|Goron Mask|link}} and the {{Term|CoH|Mask of Truth|link}}. It also shows the Gerudo and Deku civilizations are still surviving in the Future Hyrule after [[Ganon]] takes power and defeats [[Octavo]], as well as new enemies such as [[Rope]]s, [[Vire]]s, [[Goriya]]s, and [[Taros]]es, while enemies like [[Moblin]]s, [[Bokoblin]]s, and [[Tektite]]s wear Skull Masks allowing them to survive an additional hit.

{{Term|CoH|Future World|link}} is accessible either in the dedicated {{Term|CoH|Symphony of the Mask (Mode)|link}} mode or in standard {{Term|CoH|Story Mode|link}}.
