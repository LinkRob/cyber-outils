{{Plural|BotW|Demon Carver}} are one-handed {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{BotW}}. {{Plural|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|link}} begin to wield them when they ambush {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} after {{Term|BotW|Master Kohga|link}} has been defeated. The only way to obtain them is to defeat the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Footsoldier|display=Footsoldiers}} that carry them. They were forged by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} and designed to quickly defeat any of their opponents.<ref name="Inventory"/> {{Plural|BotW|Demon Carver}} belong to the {{Term|BotW|Yiga Clan Gear|link}} equipment archetype, which includes all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} made and used by the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|display=Yiga}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 171}}</ref>

As metallic {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}, {{Plural|BotW|Demon Carver}} will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They also are unable to burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, will sink in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}, and can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
