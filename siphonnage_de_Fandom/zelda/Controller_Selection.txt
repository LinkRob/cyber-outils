The {{Term/Store|Controller Selection||Series, TWWHD, TPHD}} menu is a recurring interface in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite|Controller Selection|N/A|TWWHD}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Controller Selection|N/A|TPHD}}</ref>

==Overview==
==={{TPHD|-}}===
In {{TPHD}}, the {{Term|TPHD|Controller Selection}} menu functions to decide whether the player will use the [[Wii U GamePad]] or a [[Wii U Pro Controller]] while playing {{TPHD|-}}. The {{Term|TPHD|Controller Selection}} menu does not appear in {{TP}} for the [[Nintendo GameCube]] or the [[Wii]].

==={{TWWHD|-}}===
In {{TWWHD}}, the {{Term|TWWHD|Controller Selection}} menu lets the player decide whether they will use the [[Wii U GamePad]] or a [[Wii U Pro Controller]] while playing {{TWWHD|-}}. The {{Term|TWWHD|Controller Selection}} menu does not appear in {{TWW}} for the [[Nintendo GameCube]].
