The {{Term/Store|Broken Goron's Sword||Series, OoT, OoT3D}} is an item in {{OoT}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 119}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
[[Link]] can obtain the Broken Goron's Sword by returning the [[Poacher's Saw]] to the [[Master Craftsman]] in [[Gerudo Valley]].

He must then visit the sword's maker, [[Biggoron]], on [[Death Mountain]], who needs [[World's Finest Eye Drops|Eye Drops]] after a recent eruption. Providing them to him by taking his [[Prescription]] to his childhood friend [[King Zora]], and subsequently an [[Eyeball Frog]] to the [[Lake Scientist]], will allow him to reforge the Broken Goron's Sword into [[Biggoron's Sword]], but it will take him three days to do it, so Link will have to hold onto a [[Claim Check]] in the meantime.

After the days elapse, Link must present the Claim Check to Biggoron to obtain Biggoron's Sword.

==Trivia==
* The Master Craftsman calls it his "Biggoron tool" but it isn't clear what kind of tool it was expected to be.
* The Broken Goron's Sword looks like the Broken [[Giant's Knife]] but was made by Biggoron instead of [[Medigoron]].
* This sword breaking, despite being made by Biggoron, implies it was made before Biggoron mastered his craft, as once he reforges it into Biggoron's Sword, there is no concern about it breaking ever again.
