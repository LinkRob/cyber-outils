{{Term/Store|Talon and Malon's House||Series, TMC}} is a location in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 272}}</ref>{{Note|{{Term|Series|Talon and Malon's House}} was referred to as '''Talon's House''' in {{Guide|TMC|Nintendo}}.<ref>{{Cite Guide|quote= |game= TMC |publisher= Nintendo |page= 19}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the name given in {{E}}, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House}} is located in the central area of {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Talon|link}} and {{Term|TMC|Malon|link}} live within it when {{Term|TMC|Malon}} is not selling {{Term|TMC|Lon Lon Milk|link}} in {{Term|TMC|Hyrule Town|link}}. It is locked until {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} needs to obtain a {{Term|TMC|Wake-Up Mushroom|link}} from {{Term|TMC|Syrup|link}} to wake up {{Term|TMC|Rem|link}} and obtain the {{Term|TMC|Pegasus Boots|link}}. {{Term|TMC|Link}} can shrink down to {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}} size and enter the {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|display=House}}, where he will find the {{Term|TMC|Spare Key|link}} inside one of the {{Plural|TMC|Jar|link}}. Giving the {{Term|TMC|Spare Key|display=Key}} to {{Term|TMC|Talon}} will unlock the {{Term|TMC|Talon and Malon's House|display=House}}, giving {{Term|TMC|Link}} access to a portion of {{Term|TMC|Lake Hylia|link}}.
