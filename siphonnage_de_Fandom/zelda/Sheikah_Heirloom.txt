The {{Term/Store|Sheikah Heirloom||Series, BotW}} is an {{Term|BotW|Object|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref name="Paya">{{Cite|The {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah heirloom}}... {{Big|The {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} has been stolen!}}|Paya|BotW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Orb|link}} in {{Term|BotW|Kakariko Village|link}}. It has been passed down through {{Term|BotW|Impa|link|display=Impa's}} family for nine generations.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|"The hero, as chosen by the Sheikah heirloom, will be gifted the blessing of antiquity."}} With these words as our guide, we have watched over this sacred artifact since my grandmother's grandmother's time... And even since HER grandmother's grandmother's time before that!|Paya|BotW}}</ref> Initially, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} may not take the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Heirloom}} freely.<ref>{{Cite|That {{Color|BotW Blue|Sheikah heirloom}} has been guarded by our tribe for generations. I must request that you not touch it... Even for an esteemed guest such as yourself, there are certain rules that must be respected.|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

In the last entry of the {{Term|BotW|Journal of Various Worries|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Paya|link}} writes that she's heard voices telling her of the Sheikah Heirloom's important destiny.<ref>{{Cite|There is a precious heirloom that has been passed down through my family for generations. Recently, I've begun to hear voices telling me that the heirloom has an important destiny.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref> Though she's nervous, she can tell that its destiny will happen soon.<ref>{{Cite|It's going to happen soon. It makes me nervous, but I can feel it in my bones.|Journal of Various Worries|BotW}}</ref> She's also been cleaning the Heirloom regularly.<ref>{{Cite|I had been cleaning the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} regularly every day from morning to dusk.|Paya|BotW}}</ref>

Once Link has completed the {{Term|BotW|Main Quest|link}}  "{{Term|BotW|Find the Fairy Fountain|link}}" and the {{Plural|BotW|Side Quest|link}} {{Term|BotW|Flown the Coop|link}}" and "{{Term|BotW|By Firefly's Light|link}}", he can enter {{Term|BotW|Impa's House|link}} to find that the Sheikah Heirloom has been stolen.<ref name="Paya"/> Just before that, Paya had finally discovered the Heirloom's secret.<ref>{{Cite|And just when I had finally discovered the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}}'s secret...|Paya|BotW}}</ref> She believes Link is the hero chosen by the Sheikah Heirloom and that the "blessing of antiquity" is an {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Paya thinks "{{Color|BotW Red|The hero, as chosen by the Sheikah Heirloom}}" is you, so she stayed up all night researching the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}}. She came to the conclusion that "{{Color|BotW Red|the blessing of antiquity}}" refers to one of those {{Color|BotW Red|ancient shrines}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> According to her research, the Heirloom is the key to open the Shrine.<ref>{{Cite|And that the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} is the {{Color|BotW Red|key that opens the shrine}}.|Impa|BotW}}</ref> However, Impa and Paya were not able to inform Link of this before it was stolen.<ref>{{Cite|And just before we were able to tell you this, the thing is stolen!|Impa|BotW}}</ref>

Paya thinks that whoever took the Sheikah Heirloom did so during the night.<ref>{{Cite|Someone must have stolen it during the deepest hour of night.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> Due to Impa's House being off-limits to outsiders, Paya wonders whether someone in the village stole the Heirloom.<ref>{{Cite|This place is off-limits to outsiders... And no one from the village would do such a thing! Would they?|Paya|BotW}}</ref> This initiates the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Stolen Heirloom|link}}".

After Link spends the day by Paya's side,<ref>{{Cite|Thank you for staying by my side for the whole day.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> she realizes that her fear will not help in retrieving the Sheikah Heirloom.<ref>{{Cite|I see now that crying and feeling scared won't help us get the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} back.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Dorian|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Cado|link}} only saw villagers around.<ref>{{Cite|I checked with Dorian and Cado, but they said they did not see anyone other than villagers around.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> Paya says she will try to remember everything about that night, but she warns Link that the thief may still be nearby and not to approach anyone acting suspiciously at night.<ref>{{Cite|I'll try again to remember all I can about the night the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} was stolen, but the thief may still be hiding nearby. I have a bad feeling. If you spot any suspicious movement at night, {{Color|BotW Red|don't approach}}...|Paya|BotW}}</ref>

When Link speaks to Dorian, he wonders who could have stolen the Sheikah Heirloom.<ref>{{Cite|But who could have stolen the {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}}?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that some villagers came by, but nobody he would consider suspicious.<ref>{{Cite|There were some people visiting the manor, but they were all known villagers. I don't remember seeing anyone suspicious.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> The only two people he saw were {{Term|BotW|Lasli|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Mellie|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Who came by?] The only people who came by yesterday were... {{Color|BotW Blue|Lasli}} from the clothing shop and {{Color|BotW Blue|Grandma Mellie}} from the plum garden. That's about it.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

Cado is also angry when Link speaks to him, wondering how the thief made it past himself and Dorian.<ref>{{Cite|That filthy thief! Me and {{Color|BotW Blue|Dorian}} were standing guard the entire time! How did they get past us?!|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that villagers are allowed to come and go from Impa's House as they please.<ref>{{Cite|Yes, known villagers are free to come and go as they please.|Cado|BotW}}</ref> He has the same information as Dorian, revealing that Lasli and Mellie came by during the night.<ref>{{Cite|I think the only people that stopped by around the time the heirloom went missing were Lasli and Mellie. They used to come around noonish, but lately they've been stopping by at night.|Cado|BotW}}</ref>

If Link follows Lasli to the graveyard at night, he can interrupt her story to ask where the Sheikah Heirloom is. She is initially confused, asking if he's talking about the thing in Impa's House.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Blue|Heirloom}}? You mean that thing in Impa's house?|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> When Link asks if she stole it, Lasli realizes that she is considered a suspect.<ref>{{Cite|[Did you steal it?] What?! Why would I steal something like that? Are you... Are you following me because you think I'm a suspect?!|Lasli|BotW}}</ref> She is offended by this judgement of her character.<ref>{{Cite|How awful of you...|Lasli|BotW}}</ref>

If Link confronts Mellie about the location of the heirloom, she asks if he is talking about the "garish thing" in Impa's House.<ref>{{Cite|[Where's the heirloom?] You mean that garish thing in Impa's house?|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> Mellie is offended when he asks if she stole it and almost chokes.<ref>{{Cite|[Did you steal it?] *cough cough* Is that any way to talk to an old woman? I almost choked on my carrot!|Mellie|BotW}}</ref> She thinks he is referring to her {{Term|BotW|Mellie's Plum Garden|link|display=Plum Garden}}, revealing that Impa gave it to her.<ref>{{Cite|Impa GAVE me the plum-tree orchard! And yes, the orchard may be an heirloom of this village, but it's technically mine! {{Small|{{Color|BotW Gray|You young people...}}}}|Mellie|BotW}}</ref>

At 10 pm, Dorian abandons his post and starts to head towards the woods behind {{Term|BotW|Ta'loh Naeg Shrine|link}}. If Link speaks to him during this time, he is startled by his appearance.<ref>{{Cite|Whoa! Well if it isn't Master Link. What are you doing wandering around in the dead of night?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Though he initially struggles to explain his own actions,<ref>{{Cite|[What are you doing?] Me? Um... I was just... The village...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> he eventually settles on the claim that he was patrolling for the thief.<ref>{{Cite|Yes! I was patrolling the village! The thief could be hiding anywhere, you know!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

In the woods, Dorian confronts a {{Term|BotW|Yiga Blademaster|link}}, accusing the {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} of stealing the Sheikah Heirloom.<ref>{{Cite|So you're the ones who stole the heirloom!|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> The Blademaster confirms this and notes how special the Heirloom is to the village.<ref>{{Cite|It seems this thing is truly special to the fools of this village.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> When Dorian asks why,<ref>{{Cite|Why would you steal it?|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> the Blademaster instead decides to scold him for leaving the organization.<ref>{{Cite|Don't play dumb, Dorian. You knew what you were getting yourself into when you decided to leave the organization.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that Dorian was the one who told the Yiga about the Heirloom,<ref>{{Cite|You did well to tell us about the heirloom.|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref> but his usefulness has come to an end.<ref>{{Cite|Unfortunately, your usefulness has come to an end, as must you. But first...|Yiga Blademaster|BotW}}</ref>

Once Link has defeated the Yiga Blademaster, Dorian admits that everything was his fault.<ref>{{Cite|I'm sure you've figured it out, but all of this is my fault... There's no point in trying to hide it.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he used to be a member of the Yiga Clan,<ref>{{Cite|I am...a member of the Yiga Clan. Or I used to be, anyway.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> but the Yiga killed his wife when he tried to leave.<ref>{{Cite|But then I met the most incredible woman who blessed me with two amazing children. And so I tried to change my ways. Tried to leave the clan. But the Yiga... They took my wife's life as punishment.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> With the lives of his children threatened, he used his position as a gatekeeper to feed information to the Yiga.<ref>{{Cite|I couldn't let them hurt my children, so I used my position as a gatekeeper to gather information for the Yiga. I told them about you and about the village in exchange for safety.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

While none of the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} could make the Sheikah Heirloom work, Dorian shares that Paya believes Link can figure it out.<ref>{{Cite|Paya figured out that this {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} is the {{Color|BotW Red|key to opening an ancient shrine}}. We weren't able to make it work, but Paya thinks that maybe you can.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> He instructs Link to place it on its pedestal.<ref>{{Cite|Now, please restore this {{Color|BotW Blue|heirloom}} {{Color|BotW Red|to its pedestal}}.|Dorian|BotW}}</ref> Once Link places the Sheikah Heirloom in the {{Term|BotW|Stone Sphere Socket|link}}, it disappears.<ref>{{Cite|So the heirloom WAS the key to opening the shrine. Incredible...|Dorian|BotW}}</ref>

Link can inform Paya of what happened to the Sheikah Heirloom.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|What?!}} Oh... So that means the heirloom... has finally fulfilled its purpose.|Paya|BotW}}</ref> She is relieved that the Heirloom was used to unlock the Shrine, so long as it was able to help Link.<ref>{{Cite|Thank goodness! It doesn't matter how it happened, so long as the object we have guarded preciously for generations... So long as it was able to help you out in some way, I couldn't be happier!|Paya|BotW}}</ref>
