==Characteristics==
{{Navi
|quote1= {{Color|OoT White|Dead Hand}}<br/>Watch out for its infinite hands! Aim for its head!
}}
[[File:OoT Dead Hand Attacking Link.png|thumb|249x249px|The Dead Hand restricting Link's movements before beginning to attack him in The Bottom of the Well]]
{{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} is a zombie-like monster with a large jaw. Red blotches, thought to be bloodstains, cover its body.<ref name="E 173" /> Multiple seemingly disembodied hands, usually four to six, circle the chamber in which {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} resides. The creature uses these hands to snag prey, at which point its main body emerges from the ground to consume the captured meal. In the case of [[Link]], if he could not break free from the monster's hands quickly enough, the Dead Hand itself will attempt to [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_DeadHand_Bite.wav bite him], causing half a [[Heart Container]] in damage. It can also be heard [https://noproblo.dayjo.org/zeldasounds/OOT/OOT_DeadHand_Laugh.wav laughing] as it slowly approaches the hero. The enemy comes toward Link with its neck pointing up to the air but goes down when in reach to attack him.

The {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} at the {{Term|OoT3D|Bottom of the Well|link}} can be overcome by intentionally letting oneself be grabbed by the hands, then breaking free and slashing the {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} in the face when it appears. The hands can also be destroyed, and although they regenerate after about 20 seconds, destroying enough of them inevitably forces the {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} to emerge, giving Link an opportunity to strike with his [[Sword]] without leaving himself vulnerable. Once the {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} has been defeated, it collapses and he is rewarded with the {{Term|OoT3D|Lens of Truth|link}}.

The same strategy can be used for the {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} in the [[Shadow Temple]] (multiple Dead Hands in {{MQ}}), but there is an easier method in which the [[Hero of Time]] can use the {{Term|OoT3D|Lens of Truth}}, acquired from the {{Term|OoT3D|Bottom of the Well}}, to look on the ground for the shadow of the {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} moving about underground, and place a {{Term|OoT3D|Bomb|link}} on top of it. Once the Bomb explodes, {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} proceeds to come out to attack Link. The wave of earth created by a {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}}'s emergence or burrowing can injure Link. If {{Term|OoT3D|Dead Hand}} is unsuccessful in retaining Link with one of its hands, it will bury itself again. Upon the monster's defeat, the hero acquires the sacred {{Term|OoT3D|Hover Boots|link}} as a reward.

== Trivia ==

* Due to its zombie-like appearance, red blotches resembling bloodstains in the original [[Nintendo 64|N64]] version and behavior like [[ReDead|ReDeads]], the Dead Hand is an infamous enemy among [[The Legend of Zelda (series)|{{TLoZ|-}} series]] community, with many fans calling it one of the most frightening creatures in the franchise and likening it to enemies from Mature-rated games such as ''[https://silenthill.fandom.com/wiki/Silent_Hill_(franchise) Silent Hill]''.
* The Dead Hand shares the same sound as the [[Armos]], but is slowed down.
* Oddly, if [[Link]] attempts to block the Dead Hand's bite attack with the [[Hylian Shield]], it produces the same effect as when defending with the [[Deku Shield]].
