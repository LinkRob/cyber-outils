{{Term/Store|Story Mode||Series, CoH}} is a {{Term|CoH|Mode|link}} in {{CoH}}.<ref>{{Cite|Story Mode|Select Mode|CoH}}</ref> It contains the main story of the game, with a total of five characters that can be unlocked.

==Overview==
The story starts with {{Term|CoH|Cadence|link}} finding herself in {{Term|CoH|Hyrule|link}}, after {{Term|CoH|Octavo|link}} has cursed both {{Term|CoH|Link|link}} and {{Term|CoH|Zelda|link}} to sleep with his {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute|link}}. {{Term|CoH|Trill|link}} finds her, and together they venture into a cave only to find one-way portals leading to {{Term|CoH|Link}} and {{Term|CoH|Zelda}}.<ref>{{Cite|What?! Look at these weird {{Color|CoH Blue|portals}}. You can see my friends inside of them! If anyone can figure out what's going on in Hyrule, it's {{Color|CoH Blue|Link}} and {{Color|CoH Blue|Zelda}}. But it looks like these portals are only {{Color|CoH Blue|one way}}!|Trill|CoH}}</ref> {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} chooses one, waking up the selected hero and leaving to find her own way out of this world. {{Term|CoH|Link}} or {{Term|CoH|Zelda}} is filled in on the situation by {{Term|CoH|Trill}} and sets off to restore peace to {{Term|CoH|Hyrule}} by defeating {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} and his champions.

Along the way, they can wake up the other hero in {{Term|CoH|Kakariko Village|link}} with a {{Term|CoH|Hibiscus Potion|link}}, give 20 {{Plural|CoH|Deku Seed|link}} to {{Term|CoH|Tingle|link}} and team up with {{Term|CoH|Yves|link}} the {{Term|CoH|Deku Scrub|link}},<ref>{{Cite|As thanks, Tingle's friend {{Color|CoH Blue|Yves}} will join you! He's always wanted to be an adventurer.|Tingle|CoH}}</ref> and encounter {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} in caves close to dungeon entrances to find upgrades and eventually have her join the party.<ref>{{Cite|I'm beginning to think that I'll never get home with {{Color|CoH Blue|Octavo}} around. He reminds me of someone from my world that I {{Color|CoH Blue|really}} don't like! Why don't we join forces to take him down?|Cadence|CoH}}</ref> They may even travel to the {{Term|CoH|Future World|link}} to meet a {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid|link}}, who joins the group after they retrieve the {{Term|CoH|Skull Mask|link}} from the {{Term|CoH|Deku Hollow|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Is that the {{Color|CoH Yellow|Skull Mask}}? You must be pretty brave to go into that cave. I've made up my mind! I'm gonna go with you! I've always wanted to see the world outside our village.|Skull Kid|CoH}}</ref>

One by one, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}}'s champions are defeated, and with all four instruments in hand, the heroes enter {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle}} to face the musician himself. Upon his defeat, the {{Term|CoH|Golden Lute}} vanishes in a portal to the {{Term|CoH|Future World}}, leading {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} and the heroes to go chasing after it. Once through the future {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle}}, {{Term|CoH|Octavo}} is seen lying on the ground, having failed in his mission to defeat {{Term|CoH|Ganon|link}}. {{Term|CoH|Link}}, {{Term|CoH|Zelda}}, and {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} challenge {{Term|CoH|Ganon}} as a trio, eventually defeating him and using the power of the {{Term|CoH|Triforce|link}} to send {{Term|CoH|Cadence}} to another world, hopefully sending her back home.

==Trivia==
*The final boss of [[Symphony of the Mask]], '''Skull Ganon''', can be challenged in {{Term|CoH|Story Mode}} if the final boss chamber is entered as {{Term|CoH|Skull Kid}}.
*The map of {{Term|CoH|Hyrule Castle}} differs slightly depending on if {{Term|CoH|Link}} or {{Term|CoH|Zelda}} is selected by {{Term|CoH|Cadence}}. Zelda's version of the castle includes a bridge to the east that allows her to escape, as well as a {{Term|CoH|Sheikah Stone|link}}. It is also one pixel wider than the usual version of the area.
