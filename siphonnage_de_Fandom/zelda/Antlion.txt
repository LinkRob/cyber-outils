{{Term/Store|Antlion|Antlions|Series|plural}} are {{Plural|FPTRR|Enemy|link}} in {{FPTRR}}.{{Name Ref|FPTRR}}

==Characteristics==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Antlion}} appear in the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Garden|link}} and randomly spawn in a corner of the map when {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} is walking on sand and will pursue him as long as he stays on sandy ground. {{Plural|FPTRR|Antlion}} are much faster than {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} and can only be evaded by stepping on more solid ground, leaving the screen, or driving the {{Term|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} vehicle. When {{Plural|FPTRR|Antlion}} reach {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}}, they swallow him whole, making it impossible to fight back. After losing a considerable amount of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee}}, {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} reawakens near the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Fountain|link}} in the center of the {{Term|FPTRR|Fairy Garden|display=Garden}}.
