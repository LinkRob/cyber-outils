The '''Hidden Ruins''' are a secret cave in {{EoW}}.

== Overview ==
=== {{EoW}} ===
The Hidden Ruins can be found in the southern part of the [[Faron Wetlands]]. To access it, Zelda has to warp to Blossu's House in the southeast corner of the region and then head west. Upon encountering torches, proceed north up the stairs to a pond with two unlit torches and a [[Drippitune]] enemy. Defeating the Drippitune stops the rain, allowing the torches to be lit, which reveals a hidden staircase leading to the Hidden Ruins. 

Within the Hidden Ruins, Zelda undertakes the [[Cotton-Candy Hunt]] side quest, which involves solving electricity-based puzzles and culminates in a boss fight against [[Smog]]. Successfully completing this quest rewards Zelda with the [[Curious Charm]] accessory.
