{{Term/Store|Scissors Beetle|Scissors Beetles|Series, TMC|plural}} are enemies in {{TMC}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 201}}</ref>

==Characteristics==
{{Figurine|TMC|Scissors Beetle}}
{{Plural|TMC|Scissors Beetle}}' large mandibles act as a shield, and are detached and thrown like a {{Term|Series|Boomerang|link}}. The {{Plural|TMC|Scissors Beetle}} stop moving when they throw their mandibles to attack, giving {{Term|TMC|Link|link}} an opportunity to strike them while they are vulnerable. They appear in areas across {{Term|TMC|Hyrule|link}} that {{Term|TMC|Link}} accesses while {{Term|TMC|Minish|link}}-sized.
