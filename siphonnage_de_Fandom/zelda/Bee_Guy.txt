{{Term/Store|Bee Guy||Series, ALBW}} is a character in {{ALBW}}.{{Fact}}

==Biography==
The Bee Guy is an inhabitant of [[Kakariko Village#A Link Between Worlds|Kakariko Village]] who lives in a house displaying a [[Bee#A Link Between Worlds|Bee]] sign, close to the center of the town. He is an avid fan of Bees,<ref>{{Cite|Spending my days surrounded by a buzzy swarm of bees has always been my fondest dream.|Bee Guy|ALBW}}</ref> so much, in fact, that he dresses up as one. He asks [[Link]] to catch any Bees that he may encounter during his travels and bring them to him. In order to help him with this task, the Bee Guy gives Link the [[Bug Net#A Link Between Worlds|Bug Net]]. If Link brings him a normal Bee, he will reward Link with a Purple [[Rupee]].<ref name=puns>{{Cite|Wasp's that? You found one? I couldn't bee more delighted! Wow, look at that bee's sheen! She's the bee's knees! Here's my way of saying thanks. Take this-buzz, buzz!|Bee Guy|ALBW}}</ref> If Link brings him a [[Golden Bee#A Link Between Worlds|Golden Bee]], he will give Link the [[Bee Badge]].<ref name=puns2>{{Cite|You're a real pro at catching bees, Link. But I wonder if you can find the most bee-coming of bees. The golden bee! I've never seen one before! If you can bring me one of those, I'd...! Well, I'd bee very generous.|Bee Guy|ALBW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Oh, bee still my beating heart! Is THIS--? Oh my, it IS! It's a golden bee! Thank you! She's very bee-witching, don't you think? That golden glow? That sweet note of honey that lingers in the air? And so regal--a queen of bees! She's perfect! You have outdone yourself. So please take this as thanks. Buzz.|Bee Guy|ALBW}}</ref> If Link brings him more Golden Bees after that, he will reward him with a Gold Rupee.

==Trivia==
*The Bee Guy is fond of bee or wasp-related puns and frequently utilizes them in his speech.<ref name=puns/><ref name=puns2/>
*If Link is carrying a [[Fairy]] in a [[Bottle]], he will think it is a new type of Bee, and become extremely excited. Once realizing it is a Fairy, he will lose all interest.<ref>{{Cite|Let's have a look inside that bottle of yours... WHAT? Is this some new breed of bee?
