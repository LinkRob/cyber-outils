{{Term/Store|Sarjon Bridge||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Sarjon Bridge'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge}} is located in {{Term|BotW|West Necluda|link}} northeast of {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Woods|link}}. The {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge}} extends over the {{Term|BotW|Floria River|link}} between two small waterfalls. Occasionaly, travelers like {{Term|BotW|Chumin|link}} walk along this bridge.

On the northern side of the {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge}}, a {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail|link}} can be found near a small pillar. If {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} walks close to the flower, it will disappear and reappear closer to the {{Term|BotW|Sarjon Bridge}}, eventually leading {{Term|BotW|Link}} across the bridge. Once {{Term|BotW|Link}} gets to the end of the {{Term|BotW|Flower Trail}}, a {{Term|BotW|Korok|link}} will appear and give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Korok Seed|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Yahaha! You found me!|Korok|BotW}}</ref>

The stylings of the bridge's stonework construction are typical of the {{Term|BotW|Zonai|link}}.
