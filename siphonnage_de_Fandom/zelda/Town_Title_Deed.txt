The {{Term/Store|Town Title Deed||Series, MM3D}} is an item in {{MM}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Red|Town Title Deed}}<br/>The title deed for the Deku Flower in Clock Town. You traded your Moon's Tear for this.|Gear Screen|MM3D}}</ref>{{Note|In all versions of {{MM|-}} prior to {{MM3D}}, the {{Term|Series|Town Title Deed}} was known as the {{Term/Store|Land Title Deed||MM}}. As this contradicts the name given in {{MM3D|-}}, this name is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}{{Note|The {{Term|Series|Town Title Deed}} was referred to as the '''Town Land Title Deed''' on [[Zelda.com]]'s encyclopedia.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= {{Big|Town Land Title Deed}}<br/>In Termina, Land Title Deeds were issued to Deku Scrubs so that they could keep track of which Deku Flowers were owned and which were still up for grabs.|retrieved= January 22, 2022|url= https://web.archive.org/web/20160309162654/http://zelda.com/universe/pedia/t.jsp#TownLandTitleDeed|title= The Great Hyrule Encyclopedia |site= Zelda.com |type= archive}}</ref> However, as this contradicts the names given in-game, it is not considered [[Guidelines:Canon|Canon]].}}

==Location and Uses==
<!-- {{Color|MM3D Red|Town Title Deed}}<br/>The title deed for the Deku Flower in Clock Town. You traded your Moon's Tear for this. -->
The Town Title Deed is a claim to the Deku Flower in [[South Clock Town]], which is owned by a [[Business Scrub]]. If Link attempts to approach the Deku Flower, he will be stopped by the Business Scrub who owns the Flower.<ref>{{Cite|Wait! Wait! Hang on!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|This is my private property. Don't try using it when I'm not around!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If Link speaks to the Business Scrub, he will tell Link about a precious stone known as a [[Moon's Tear]]. As he is hoping to give one to his wife as a souvenir, the Business Scrub will offer Link the Town Title Deed and his Deku Flower in exchange for a Moon's Tear.<ref>{{Cite|Sir! Sir! I keep hearing about a stone called a {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tear}}, and it's supposed to be the shiniest stone in the world! Do you have one? If you have one, I'll trade you this spot and I'll throw in the {{Color|MM3D Red|Deku Flower}}, too! This is a very popular spot among Deku Scrubs. Haven't you heard about this spot? I'm telling you, it's a real bargain!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|If you have a {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tear}}, please tell me! I want one as a souvenir for my wife!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sir, if you have a {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tear}}, I must get it from you. And I'm not asking to get it for free!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Ah! That stone! You must give it to me! In exchange, I'll give you my spot here, and I'll throw in this {{Color|MM3D Red|Deku Flower}}, too!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> If Link agrees to the exchange, the Business Scrub will thank him and inform him that he can resell the Town Title Deed to a Deku Scrub outside of [[Clock Town]] for a generous price due to its high demand.<ref>{{Cite|You traded the Moon's Tear for a {{Color|MM3D Red|Town Title Deed}}!|N/A|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Dark Blue|The town Deku Flower}}<br/>You gave the {{Color|MM3D Red|Moon's Tear}} to the Business Scrub in South Clock Town. In return, you got the {{Color|MM3D Red|Town Title Deed}}!<br/>South Clock Town|Deku Scrub Scramble|MM3D}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Sir, you've really helped me out! Now I have the perfect souvenir for my wife! She hasn't seen a jewel or strange, shining stone like this in quite a long time... Hee-hee! The {{Color|MM3D Red|Title Deed}} for this spot will be in big demand among Deku Scrubs outside of town. You should be able to sell it to a Deku Scrub for a good price!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>

With the Town Title Deed in Link's possession, he can freely use the yellow Deku Flower in South Clock Town to fly to ledges within range, such as the ledge at the entrance to the Clock Tower. Link can alternatively repurpose the Town Title Deed as toilet paper by giving it to {{Term|MM3D|??? (Majora's Mask)|link}} in the [[Stock Pot Inn]]'s bathroom after midnight each night.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|MM3D Light Blue|Crisis in the toilet!}}<br/>You helped the mysterious hand in the restroom. It's no good to be out of {{Color|MM3D Red|paper}} in the toilet!<br/>Stock Pot Inn - restroom|The Ghost of the Inn|MM3D}}</ref> Link can also trade the Town Title Deed to the Business Scrub outside the [[Swamp Tourist Center]] in the [[Southern Swamp]] in exchange for the [[Swamp Title Deed]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh! That's a {{Color|MM3D Red|Town Title Deed}}! I wish you'd let me have that. I'll give you this land in return! Deal?|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref> However, if he does so, the Business Scrub will move to the Deku Flower in South Clock Town and Link will not be able to access it.<ref>{{Cite|I'm going off to town right now! I'll leave the rest up to you!|Business Scrub|MM3D}}</ref>
