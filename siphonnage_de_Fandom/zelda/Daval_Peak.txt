{{Term/Store|Daval Peak||Series, BotW}} is a location in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|'''Daval Peak'''|Map|BotW}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
Daval Peak is located in the [[Gerudo Highlands]]. On the side of Daval Peak is the {{Term|BotW|Pedestal|link}} that must be activated in order to complete the "[[Secret of the Snowy Peaks]]" [[Shrine Quest]] and reveal the [[Suma Sahma Shrine]].
