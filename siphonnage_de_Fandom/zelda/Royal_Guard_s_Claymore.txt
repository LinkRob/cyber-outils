{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Claymore}} are part of the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Series|link}}, an equipment archetype that includes the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Sword|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Spear|link}}, the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Bow|link}}, and the {{Term|BotW|Royal Guard's Shield|link}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 168}}</ref> The {{Term|BotW|Sheikah|link}} used {{Term|BotW|Ancient Technology|link}} to forge them.<ref name="Inventory"/> They were initially designed to help defend against the {{Term|BotW|Great Calamity|link}}, but they found little use due to their low durability.<ref name="Inventory"/> All {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Claymore}} are found in {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)|link}}. They can be found in {{Term|BotW|Lockup|link}} and the {{Term|BotW|Guards' Chamber|link}}.

When {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Parcy|link}} at {{Term|BotW|Riverside Stable|link}}, she will tell him about the equipment belonging to the {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard|link}} inside {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle (Breath of the Wild)}}, and she laments that she could not find all of it.<ref>{{Cite|Have you gone to the giant castle north of here? That's Hyrule Castle. It's said to have fallen to ruin over a hundred years ago. I used to sneak in there to salvage ancient treasures, but it's too dangerous to go in there now. These days it's surrounded by an eerie mist and savage machine-like creatures. Back when it was safer, I went in looking for this ultra-rare equipment that once belonged to the {{Color|BotW Red|royal guard}}. It's legendary among collectors, but I was never able to find any...|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> This will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} "{{Term|BotW|The Royal Guard's Gear|link}}" {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}}. A Royal Guard's Claymore can be turned in for this quest in exchange for a {{Term|BotW|Gold Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Wait just a minute! Can you show me that big sword you've got there?!|Parcy|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Here's a token of my appreciation.|Parcy|BotW}}</ref> Afterwards, {{Term|BotW|Parcy}} will give {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Topaz|link}} if he keeps bringing her {{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Claymore}}.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that big sword... Will you trade it for a topaz?|Parcy|BotW}}</ref>

{{Plural|BotW|Royal Guard's Claymore}} are made of metal, which means they will attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} when equipped during a {{Term|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} and conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. They also can not burn near a {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or in a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, and they can be picked up using the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}. However, they will sink if they are dropped in{{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}.
