{{Term/Store|Daily Drills: Goron||HWAoC}} is a Level 21 {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge|link}} in {{HWAoC}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daily Drills: Goron|Map|HWAoC}}</ref>
==Overview==
{{Term|HWAoC|Daily Drills: Goron}} is a {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} that can be completed using any            {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} except for {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}}. When completed for the first time, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}} receives a reward of an {{Term|HWAoC|Ethereal Stone|link}}, ten {{Plural|HWAoC|Goron Spice|link}}, and a random {{Term|HWAoC|Weapon|link}}.

At the start of the {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}}, the {{Term|HWAoC|Warrior}}'s objective is to defeat 300 {{Plural|HWAoC|Enemy|link}} within four minutes. After reaching 300 kills, a gate opens and {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk|link}} spawns. The {{Term|HWAoC|Challenge}} ends once {{Term|HWAoC|Daruk}} is defeated.
