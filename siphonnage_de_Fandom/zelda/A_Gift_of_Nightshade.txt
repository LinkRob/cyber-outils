"{{Term/Store|A Gift of Nightshade||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Side Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|A Gift of Nightshade}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Side Quest|Faron}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
At the {{Term|BotW|Lover's Pond|link}} atop {{Term|BotW|Tuft Mountain|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can find {{Term|BotW|Wabbin|link}} and {{Term|BotW|Perda|link}}. The two of them are standing on opposite sides of the pond. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} speaks to {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}}, he reveals that he is working up the courage to speak to {{Term|BotW|Perda}}.<ref>{{Cite|H-hey! I'm trying to work up the courage to talk to her. Just... Let me, yeah?|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} allows him to have a chance, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} is relieved.<ref>{{Cite|[No problem.] ...Phew. I'm so glad! Oh, by the way, my name is Wabbin!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> He reveals that he became lost and closed his eyes, and when he opened them, the most beautiful woman he ever saw was standing there.<ref>{{Cite|To be honest, I lost my way earlier. I closed my eyes for a minute, and when I opened them, the most beautiful woman I've ever seen was standing before me.|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref>

At first, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} thought he was dreaming or that she was a ghost,<ref>{{Cite|At first I thought I was dreaming...or that she was a ghost or something.|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> as it is uncommon to run into a gorgeous woman in the middle of nowhere.<ref>{{Cite|I mean, it's not every day you run into a gorgeous woman in the middle of nowhere...|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> It was love at first sight, and so he is gathering his courage to confess to her.<ref>{{Cite|But figment of my imagination or not, it was love at first sight. And so I've decided to gather my courage and go confess my feelings to her!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} decided long ago that he would give the woman he loves a [[Blue Nightshade]] when he confesses to her,<ref>{{Cite|I decided long ago that when I meet the girl of my dreams, I'll hand her a {{Color|BotW Red|blue nightshade}} and confess my love!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> and he believes {{Term|BotW|Perda}} to be that woman.<ref>{{Cite|If I had a {{Color|BotW Red|blue nightshade}}, I'd head straight over to that woman.|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> He thinks that {{Term|BotW|Perda}} would look beautiful holding such a flower.<ref>{{Cite|I mean, imagine her and a {{Color|BotW Red|blue nightshade}}... That'd make a beautiful scene. Don't you think?|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> This begins the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}}.

{{Term|BotW|Link}} can return to {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} with a {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} for {{Term|BotW|Perda}}. He will lament the fact that he does not have anything to give her,<ref>{{Cite|I want to talk to her, but I don't have anything worthy of being a gift. If only I had {{Color|BotW Red|blue nightshade}}.|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> before recognizing the scent of {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} on {{Term|BotW|Link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ahh, the smell of it... I can almost imagine it right now. It's so clear, almost like...it's... Wait, do you have some {{Color|BotW Red|blue nightshade}} on you right now?|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> Frantically, he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for the flower, saying it could not be more important.<ref>{{Cite|Please! You must give me that {{Color|BotW Red|flower}}! It couldn't be more important!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref>

When {{Term|BotW|Link}} gives {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} the flower, he will be ecstatic and thank him.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Thank you!!!}}<br/>This is so perfect! It's exactly what I needed!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} becomes shy when he looks at {{Term|BotW|Perda}},<ref>{{Cite|Ah... I just... Ugh.<br/>When I look at her, my legs freeze up. My throat goes dry. I...I can't do it.|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> and so he asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to make the delivery in his stead.<ref>{{Cite|Please, could you take {{Color|BotW Red|my gift}} to her?|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} agrees, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} will thank him and return the {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}}.<ref>{{Cite|[Sure!] {{Big|Thank you!}} I'll give you back this {{Color|BotW Blue|blue nightshade}}.|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> He reminds {{Term|BotW|Link}} to tell {{Term|BotW|Perda}} that the present is from him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! And don't forget to mention it's a present from ME!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Perda}} is confused when {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her.<ref>{{Cite|What's this?|Perda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Link}} explains that the {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} is a gift from {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}}.<ref>{{Cite|[A gift from that guy.] I see... So...you're delivering these {{Color|BotW Red|flowers}} for that voe over there?|Perda|BotW}}</ref> Since she can not sense any deceit from {{Term|BotW|Link}},<ref>{{Cite|... I don't sense any deceit from you...|Perda|BotW}}</ref> she decides to approach {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}}.<ref>{{Cite|So you're the one who gave me the {{Color|BotW Red|flowers}}!|Perda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} is nervous because of her confrontation and immediately apologizes, believing she does not like {{Plural|BotW|Blue Nightshade}}.<ref>{{Cite|Eeek, I'm sorry!!! Could...could it be that you don't like {{Color|BotW Blue|blue nightshade}}?|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> However, {{Term|BotW|Perda}} reveals that {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} is her favorite flower and asks how he knew, accusing him of being a {{Term|BotW|Yiga|link}} spy.<ref>{{Cite|Why would you say that? This is my favorite kind of {{Color|BotW Red|flower}}! How did you know that anyway? Are you a Yiga spy?!|Perda|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} replies that {{Term|BotW|Blue Nightshade}} is also his favorite flower.<ref>{{Cite|{{Color|BotW Red|Blue nightshade}} just happens to be my favorite {{Color|BotW Red|flower}}! Long ago, I decided to give my true love this flower...|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Perda}} realizes that this must be destiny,<ref>{{Cite|Your true love...<br/>So this is what they call destiny...|Perda|BotW}}</ref> so she tells him to speak his piece.<ref>{{Cite|You're the voe that destiny has chosen for me, then? All right! Go ahead and speak your piece!|Perda|BotW}}</ref> At {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}}'s confusion,<ref>{{Cite|What do you mean?|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Perda}} says that he should say the things men say to women.<ref>{{Cite|No, that's not right at all! I'm talking about what a voe says to a vai!|Perda|BotW}}</ref> When {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} is still lost, {{Term|BotW|Perda}} gives him examples, such as "I like you" and "Please, go out with me!"<ref>{{Cite|You know, something like, "I like you," or, "Please go out with me!"|Perda|BotW}}</ref> Unfortunately for {{Term|BotW|Perda}}, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} mistakes this for her asking him out, which he accepts.<ref>{{Cite|Oh! Uh, sure! Asking so directly, how could I possibly say no?!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref>

Overjoyed, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} turns to {{Term|BotW|Link}}, asking if he heard what {{Term|BotW|Perda}} said.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, wow! Did you hear that?! She said she likes me, and she wants to go out!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> No one has ever said anything like that to him before, so he is happy to hear it from the woman of his dreams.<ref>{{Cite|This is the first time anyone has ever said they like me, and for it to come from the woman of my dreams...|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> He is happy to have been born into this world,<ref>{{Cite|This is such an amazing feeling... I'm so glad I was born into this world...|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> As a token of his thanks, he gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Red Rupee|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|There's no way I can truly thank you, but please take this as a token of my thanks.|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref>

{{Term|BotW|Perda}} is outraged that {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} so little after everything he has done.<ref>{{Cite|What? That's it?! That's all you're willing to offer him for everything he's done for you?!|Perda|BotW}}</ref> Covering for his mistake, {{Term|BotW|Wabbin}} gives {{Term|BotW|Link}} a {{Term|BotW|Silver Rupee|link}}, claiming it is what he originally intended to give him.<ref>{{Cite|I... No, of course not!<br/>Here, this is what I meant to give you!|Wabbin|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Perda}} approves of this reward.<ref>{{Cite|Much better.|Perda|BotW}}</ref> She thanks {{Term|BotW|Link}} for helping her and hopes he can be lucky enough to find someone like she did.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, you! I guess I owe you too... I hope you have as much luck finding someone as I did... thanks to you!|Perda|BotW}}</ref> With this, the {{Term|BotW|Side Quest}} is complete.
