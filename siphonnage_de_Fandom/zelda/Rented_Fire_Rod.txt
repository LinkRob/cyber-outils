The {{Term/Store|Rented Fire Rod||Series, ALBW}} is an {{Term|ALBW|Item|link}} in {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite|Rented Fire Rod<br/>Handle with care! Attack enemies with a burst of flames!|N/A|ALBW}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
The {{Term|ALBW|Rented Fire Rod}} is used by {{Term|ALBW|Link|link}} to create pillars of flame, which can damage {{Plural|ALBW|Enemy|link}} and melt frozen blocks of ice in the {{Term|ALBW|Ice Ruins|link}}. It is rentable from {{Term|ALBW|Ravio's Shop|link}} for 100 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee|link}}.<br/><br/>Being a rented {{Term|ALBW|Item}}, the {{Term|ALBW|Rented Fire Rod}} will be returned to {{Term|ALBW|Ravio}} by {{Term|ALBW|Sheerow|link}} if {{Term|ALBW|Link}} falls in battle. It may be permanently obtained as the {{Term|ALBW|Fire Rod|link}} for 1200 {{Plural|ALBW|Rupee}}, and further upgraded into the {{Term|ALBW|Nice Fire Rod|link}} by {{Term|ALBW|Mother Maiamai|link}}.
