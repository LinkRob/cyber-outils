"{{Term/Store|Secret of the Cedars||Series, BotW}}" is a {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} in {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Secret of the Cedars}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Shrine Quest|Hateno Village}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
In {{Term|BotW|Hateno Village|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} can encounter {{Term|BotW|Clavia|link}} standing on the {{Term|BotW|Dock|link}} behind the {{Term|BotW|Kochi Dye Shop|link}}. When {{Term|BotW|Link}} approaches her, she realizes he is a traveler and asks that he listen to her.<ref>{{Cite|You're a traveler, aren't you? Then listen up!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> If {{Term|BotW|Link}} stays, she points out a group of three {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} atop {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|[What is it?] You see those three trees up on top of that snowy mountain there?|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Clavia}} found a note between the pages of an old {{Term|BotW|Book|link}} in her {{Term|BotW|Village Chief's House|link|display=home}},<ref>{{Cite|I found a small letter stuck between the pages of this old book at my house...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> which instructs the reader to turn towards the {{Term|BotW|Necluda Sea|link}} when the {{Plural|BotW|Tree}} appear as one to find a trial "encased in stone".<ref>{{Cite|It said, "When the three summit trees become one, turn your back to them and head toward the sea. A trial encased in stone awaits you there." I don't know what that means!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> She believes it may be directing them to {{Term|BotW|Afromsia Coast|link}}, which is located east of the {{Plural|BotW|Tree}}.<ref>{{Cite|But maybe it means we have to head to {{Color|BotW Blue|Afromsia Coast}}, which is just east of those trees on the mountain!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> In addition, she thinks that the trial may be referring to a treasure.<ref>{{Cite|For the "trial {{Color|BotW Red|encased in stone}}..." but, I wonder if you get some kind of treasure for doing that...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref>

Though she is curious to find the treasure, {{Term|BotW|Clavia}} is unable to go on such a journey because of her role as a mother.<ref>{{Cite|But I have a child! I can't just go off on a journey across the mountains...|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> Therefore, she asks {{Term|BotW|Link}} to investigate on her behalf and retrieve any treasure he finds.<ref>{{Cite|But you can do that, right?! You'd be great at that! Go on! Head out there and bring us back that treasure!|Clavia|BotW}}</ref> When she is done speaking, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest|link}} will begin.{{Note|It is possible to immediately complete this {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} by finding {{Term|BotW|Tahno O'ah Shrine}} beforehand.}}

Through following {{Term|BotW|Clavia}}'s directions, {{Term|BotW|Link}} will find a pile of rocks blocking the entrance to a cave on {{Term|BotW|Mount Lanayru}}. Using a {{Term|BotW|Remote Bomb|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Bomb Arrow|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Link}} can destroy the rocks, which reveals {{Term|BotW|Tahno O'ah Shrine|link}}. Once the {{Term|BotW|Ancient Shrine|link}} has been activated, the {{Term|BotW|Shrine Quest}} will be completed.
