{{Term/Store|Malon||Series, OoT, OoT3D, OoS, FSA, TMC}} is a [[Wikipedia:Recurring character|recurring character]] in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 239,{{Exp Game|OoT}} 250,{{Exp Game|OoS}} 265 {{Exp Game|FSA}} & 270 {{Exp Game|TMC}}}}</ref> In the majority of games she has appeared in, Malon is the daughter of the rancher {{Term|Series|Talon|link}}, who owns {{Term|Series|Lon Lon Ranch|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Ohh... You're a fairy boy from the forest! My name is Malon! My dad owns Lon Lon Ranch!|Malon|OoT}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|My name's Malon! I'm from the Lon Lon Ranch right up the road.|Malon|FSA}}</ref> Portrayed as a kind-hearted and responsible girl, Malon is often seen taking care of [[Epona]], one of the [[horse]]s of [[Lon Lon Ranch]] She also enjoys singing, specifically "[[Epona's Song]]."

==Biography==
==={{OoT|-}}===
In {{OoT}}, Malon is a young cheerful girl who loves to sing outside [[Hyrule Castle]]'s walls at night. She, along with [[Ingo]], takes care of most of the duties at [[Lon Lon Ranch]] due to her father's laziness. [[Link]] first meets Malon, who calls him "Fairy Boy", due to the young hero's [[Kokiri]]-like clothing and having [[Navi]] by his side, in the [[Market]] where she is looking for her father, [[Talon]].<ref>{{Cite|The girl from the ranch asked us to find her father...I wonder where he is?|Navi|OoT}}</ref> Link finds Talon asleep outside Hyrule Castle and wakes him up using the [[Cucco]] that hatched from the [[Weird Egg]] Malon gave him.<ref>{{Cite|Oh yeah, if you'll look for him, I'll give this to you. I've been incubating this egg very carefully... Tee hee!|Malon|OoT}}</ref> After this, Talon returns to the Ranch and reunites with Malon.<ref>{{Cite|What?! Malon was looking for me? I'm gonna catch it from her now! I messed up bad, leaving Malon behind to wait for me! She's really gonna let me have it!|Talon|OoT}}</ref> When Link visits Malon at the Ranch, she introduces him to a certain young [[Horse]] named [[Epona]],<ref>{{Cite|Oh yeah, I have to introduce you to my friend, fairy boy! She's this horse. Her name is Epona. Isn't she cute?|Malon|OoT}}</ref> and teaches the young hero "[[Epona's Song]]", a song her mother composed.<ref name= "Mother">{{Cite|My mother composed this song. Isn't it nice? Let's sing together.|Malon|OoT}}</ref>

When Link returns to Lon Lon Ranch seven years later, he discovers that Ingo exiled Talon and took over the Ranch. Malon, now grown-up, has been working for Ingo out of fear of the Ranch's Horses facing abuse from him if she disobeys his orders.<ref>{{Cite|Oh...a visitor! It's been a long time since we've had a visitor here... Where did you come from? Since Ganondorf came, people in the Castle Town have gone, places have been ruined, and monsters are wandering everywhere. Mr. Ingo is just using the ranch to gain Ganondorf's favor... Everyone seems to be turning evil... But Dad... He was kicked out of the ranch by Mr. Ingo... If I disobey Mr. Ingo, he will treat the horses so badly... So... There's nothing I can do...|Malon|OoT}}</ref> Moreover, since Ingo was struggling to tame Epona, he was planning to give her as a present to [[Ganondorf]], though Malon was the only one who could tame her.<ref>{{Cite|Epona really liked that song... Only I could tame that horse... Even Mr. Ingo had a hard time... Hee hee hee!|Malon|OoT}}</ref> When she reunites with Link, Malon tells the [[Hero of Time]] that she recognized him but was not sure who he was until he told her his name, which she reacts with happiness, once again calling him "Fairy Boy".<ref>{{Cite|Thank you very much for the other day... I haven't even asked you your name yet... ........ Really? Link! That's what I thought! You're the fairy boy from the forest! That was years ago! Do you remember me? You do? I was sure it was you because Epona remembered you! Oh, I have to tell you about Mr. Ingo... He was afraid that the Evil King might find out that Epona had been taken away... It really upset him! But one day, all of a sudden, he went back to being a normal, nice person! Now my dad is coming back...I can't believe it, but peace is returning to this ranch! It's all because of you! I owe you so much! Thank you! Thank you, Link!|Malon|OoT}}</ref>

As a minigame, Malon lets him race around Lon Lon Ranch to compete against her best time.<ref>{{Cite|How about trying your skill with Epona on an obstacle course? I'll time you for two laps. It's pretty challenging. If you miss jumping a fence in the proper order, you'll fail. How about it? Do you want to try?|Malon|OoT}}</ref> When the hero is able to beat her time, she rewards him by sending a [[Cow]] to his [[Link's House|House]] in [[Kokiri Forest]], which may be used as a free source of [[Milk|Lon Lon Milk]]. Should Link best his own time afterwards, Malon will simply compliment his and Epona's teamwork.<ref>{{Cite|You and Epona make a great team!|Malon|OoT}}</ref>

With the [[Mask of Truth]], Link can learn from the [[Gossip Stone]]s that Malon dreams for a knight (likely [[Knight of Hyrule|Knights of Hyrule]]) to take her away.<ref name="knight"/> A Gossip Stone in front of the [[Temple of Time]] also says that Malon was the original record holder of the Lon Lon Ranch obstacle course,<ref>{{Cite|They say that Malon set the original record in the obstacle course of Lon Lon Ranch.|Gossip Stone|OoT}}</ref> with it being 50 seconds.

{{OoT|-}} is the only game in which Malon's mother is mentioned. She is not present by the time of the events of the game, but it is clear she had a great influence on Malon and the others on the Ranch. She was the one who composed "Epona's Song", which is said to be liked by everyone on the farm.<ref name= "Mother"/><ref>{{Cite|You... Do you know my mother's song? Everyone really liked that song... My dad... Even Mr. Ingo...|Malon|OoT}}</ref> If Link speaks to Talon while wearing the [[Gerudo Mask]] or the [[Goron Mask]], he says that it reminds him of his wife, but he quickly retracts that statement.<ref>{{Cite|Hey, that reminds me of my wife... On second thought, it doesn't... It doesn't look like her at all!|Talon|OoT}}</ref>

==={{OoS|-}}===
Malon plays a small role in the {{Term|OoS|Trading Quest|link}} for {{OoS}}. She lives with her father Talon north of [[Horon Village]], where the two raise Cuccos. When Link first meets her, Talon has gone to climb [[Mt. Cucco]] and has left Malon to raise the Cuccos by herself; however, she confesses to the young hero that she does not know much about the hens.<ref>{{Cite|My dad, Talon, went to climb Mt. Cucco, so I have to care for our hens. But there's so much I don't know...|Malon|OoS}}</ref> Link can help her by giving her the [[Cuccodex]], an information database of Cuccos, in exchange for a [[Lon Lon Egg]].<ref>{{Cite|Say! Is that a Cuccodex? That would be a big help... May I have it? Really? Thank you! Take this! It's a Lon Lon Egg. These eggs are all the rage with cute girls!|Malon|OoS}}</ref>

==={{FSA|-}}===
In {{FSA}}, Malon appears on [[The Field]] stage. Upon arriving, the [[Links]] encounter Malon standing by herself. She tells them that she was running errands, but just as she was about to head back to her home, it started raining; furthermore, the [[Soldier]]s were moving all the [[Cannon]]s.<ref>{{Cite|Like today! I was coming home after running errands but...I couldn't get back. I mean, it started raining, and the castle guards were moving all the cannons... The cannons are really old, and they're not meant to be used... It was really weird.|Malon|FSA}}</ref> Helpless, Malon asks the four Links if she can accompany them until she gets back to Lon Lon Ranch with her father, Talon.<ref>{{Cite|Say, if you're all going on ahead, take me with you! You're friends with the green fairy, so of course it's OK, right? Oh, great! Thanks! My name's Malon! I'm from the Lon Lon Ranch right up the road. It's not very far... Thanks, again!|Malon|FSA}}</ref> During their travel, Malon can be killed by the Castle guards, as she has only one [[Heart Container]]. Once the Links return her safely to the ranch, she admits that at first she was not sure she could count on them, but soon realized her mistake and acknowledges the Links as real heroes.<ref>{{Cite|I hate to admit it, but at first, I wasn't sure I could really count on you guys. But it turns out, you guys are the best! You're real heroes!|Malon|FSA}}</ref>

==={{TMC|-}}===
Malon and her father, [[Talon]], both operate [[Lon Lon Ranch]] east of [[Hyrule Town]] in {{TMC}}, similar to in ''Ocarina of Time''.<ref>{{Cite|This small ranch lies just outside Hyrule Town. Father-and-daughter team Talon and Malon run it, and they sell the finest milk in Hyrule!|Figurine|TMC}}</ref>

Link first encounters Malon after speaking with the [[Town Minish]] in [[Rem's Shoe Store]]. She and Talon are locked out of their [[Talon and Malon's House|House]].<ref>{{Cite|Honestly! Where did Dad go this time?!? I leave for just a few minutes, and he locks up the ranch and takes off!|Malon|TMC}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|I lost my key, and I can't get inside. {{Color|TMC Red|And the spare key}}'s inside the house!|Talon|TMC}}</ref> Link helps them by shrinking to [[Minish]] size and going into their House to find the [[Spare Key]], which is inside of a [[Jar]]. This gives him access to [[Lake Hylia]] and the northern portion of [[Minish Woods]], where [[Syrup the Witch's Hut]] is located.<ref>{{Cite|You really helped me out, sonny. If you ever want to go to {{Color|TMC Green|Lake Hylia}}, use the shortcut out behind our ranch.|Talon|TMC}}</ref>

Afterwards, Malon can be found in Hyrule Town selling [[Lon Lon Milk]] for 100 [[Rupee]]s alongside [[Epona]].<ref>{{Cite|Oh, hello there. I'm selling creamy, nutritious {{Color|TMC Red|Lon Lon Milk}} for 100 Rupees per {{Color|TMC Red|bottle}}. Would you like to buy some?|Malon|TMC}}</ref> She is one of the potential characters to have a random [[Kinstone Fusion]] available.<ref>{{Cite|You want to {{Color|TMC Blue|fuse Kinstones}}? Sure!|Malon|TMC}}</ref>

Whenever Link speaks to Malon, a brief portion of "Epona's Song" plays.

==Development==
In an {{OoT|-}} developer's interview posted on {{wp|Shigesato Itoi}}'s website, 1101.com, script writer Toru Osawa said that Talon and Malon from {{OoT|-}} are based on [[Tarin]] and [[Marin]] from {{LA}}, and it is intended that players who have played the previous game will recognize the reference.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= A father and daughter named Marin and Tarin that were introduced in Link's Awakening for GameBoy also appeared in this Zelda game. We're hoping that people who've played the Zelda series from the very beginning will recognize them. If you wonder "is this a reference to then?" we'll be happy.|name= Toru Osawa|url= http://www.glitterberri.com/ocarina-of-time/1101-interviews/playing-the-ocarina/|title= Playing the Ocarina}}</ref> 

In the same interview, [[Shigeru Miyamoto]] said that Malon being a songstress is inspired by his love of country music, including people like {{wp|Emmylou Harris}}.<ref>{{Cite Person|quote= I've always been a fan of country music, including people like Emmylou Harris so I insisted on having a songstress in the game. I changed the ranch girl Malon into a songstress, so the horses were attracted to her because of her song. However, this didn't really suit her dialogue at the time. Originally we had her singing alone in the middle of the ranch, but halfway through we changed it so she went to the castle, but then she didn't sing anymore. It was kind of fun to consider those kinds of things. So, that was mostly my part in creating the music.|name= Shigeru Miyamoto|url= http://www.glitterberri.com/ocarina-of-time/1101-interviews/playing-the-ocarina/|title= Playing the Ocarina}}</ref>

==Trivia==
* The symbols on the bottom of her dress spell "Lon" in [[Hylian Language|Hylian]].
* Malon has two counterparts in [[Termina]], [[Romani]] and [[Cremia]], look-alike sisters based on her respective child and adult age. Romani in particular would reteach [[Link]] "[[Epona's Song]]" in ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Majora's Mask|Majora's Mask]]''.[[File:Malonpendant.jpg|thumb|281x281px|Malon's Bowser brooch]]
* Both Malon and her father [[Talon]] wear a brooch of [[Bowser]]'s face, who is the main antagonist of the [[Mario (franchise)|''Mario'' franchise]]. Ironically, Talon resembles [[Mario]], that series' main protagonist, while [[Ingo]] resembles [[Luigi]]. This would be shared with Romani and Cremia, her Terminan counterparts.
* A symbol of the [[Triforce]] can be seen on adult Malon's belt. This would be shared with Cremia, although the golden relic is never worshiped in Termina.
* Malon is one of the six females implied to have a love interest in the [[Hero of Time]], alongside [[Princess Zelda]], [[Princess Ruto]], [[Saria]], and possibly [[Nabooru]] and [[Navi]], and is the only one among the first five that is not a [[Sage]].
** Interestingly, after Link completes [[Talon's Chicken Game]] in Lon Lon Ranch to earn a free [[Empty Bottle|Bottle]] as a child, Talon himself will ask him would he'd like to marry Malon. Regardless if the young hero says "Yes" or "No", Talon will then laugh and say he was joking before reminding Link that he is a bit too young. Ironically, when Link obtained the [[Spiritual Stone of Water]], he unintentionally vowed an engagement with Ruto.
** Cremia, Malon's adult Terminan counterpart, can be seen giving Link a hug after helping her deliver her [[Chateau Romani]] after already obtaining the [[Romani's Mask]] in ''Majora's Mask''.
** Despite Link the Hero of Time's canonical engagement with Princess Ruto, there is a wide speculation among the series' community that Malon may have ultimately won his affections among the aforementioned female characters in the events leading to becoming the [[Hero's Spirit]] well after ''Majora's Mask'', due to the fact that she is not a Sage and her wish was to have a knight in shining armor wiping her feet one day according to a [[Gossip Stone]] in ''Ocarina of Time'', as well as that Link himself could possibly be one according to ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Hyrule Historia|Hyrule Historia]].'' His descendant in the [[The Legend of Zelda timeline|Child Timeline]], the hero from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Twilight Princess|Twilight Princess]],'' initially a rancher like Malon, knows "Epona's Song", the song she herself teaches the Hero of Time, sparking the misconception by fans that Link the Hero of Twilight is also a descendant of Malon. This, however, has never been confirmed by [[Nintendo]] in any way and these connections are likely just pure coincidence.
* According to the {{HH}}, [[Tarin]] and [[Marin]] from ''[[The Legend of Zelda: Link's Awakening|Link's Awakening]]'' may be the product of Link's memories of Malon and Talon.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= Tarin and Marin may be the product of Link's memories of Malon and her father Talon, of Lon Lon Ranch|book=HH|page= 102}}</ref>
* [[Malo]] and [[Talo]] from ''Twilight Princess'' have names that reference Malon and Talon.
* Malon's famous "fairy boy" quote to Link is referenced in ''Majora's Mask'' with Romani calling him "Grasshopper", due his his [[Green Clothes|Green Tunic]].
* Malon is one of the three characters from Link's childhood to grow up into an adult when he becomes the Hero of Time, alongside Princess Zelda and Ruto.
*
*
* Whenever Link is near Malon in ''Ocarina of Time'', she can be heard singing Epona's Song, including when as an adult during daytime, where she does not appear to be singing.
* Malon in ''Ocarina of Time'' shares the same voice actress as Princess Zelda/[[Sheik]], [[Jun Mizusawa]].
* Malon's voice can be heard in the [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CqZXoRdQsGY Hyrule Field (Night)] theme from ''Twilight Princess'', a callback to ''Ocarina of Time''.
** Her singing is also heard in the [[Ikana Canyon]] theme from ''Majora's Mask''.
* [[Lulu]] in ''Majora's Mask'' shares Malon's singing voice.
* In the [[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Himekawa)|''Ocarina of Time'' (Himekawa)]] manga, Malon is referred to as '''Maron''', her Japanese name.
