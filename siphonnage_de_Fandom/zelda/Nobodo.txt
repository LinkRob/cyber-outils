{{Term/Store|Nobodo||Series, PH}} is a character in {{PH}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 283}}</ref>

==Biography==
Nobodo is a homeless [[Anouki]] who perches atop a cliff on the [[Isle of Frost]].<ref name="Intro">{{Cite|Yeah, I'm Nobodo. Yeah, I got no house. And yeah, they call me "No-abodo." Real clever. I got no "abode." Ha ha. Go on, laugh at my frozen tears.|Nobodo|PH}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|Go back to your stupid warm houses! I'm happy freezing out here.|Nobodo|PH}}</ref> It claims to enjoy being cold, lonely, and miserable.<ref>{{Cite|Actually, know what? I like being sad. But what do you care?|Nobodo|PH}}</ref> It tells [[Link]] to dig in front of all of the signs next to Anouki houses, in the [[Anouki Estates]] and elsewhere, because the Anouki have a habit of burying [[Rupee]]s in front of their houses.<ref>{{Cite|Here's a secret for ya. Anouki WITH HOUSES bury stuff in front of the signs outside their houses. We Anouki are tricky like that.|Nobodo|PH}}</ref>

==Nomenclature==
Nobodo's name is a play on the phrase "no abode."<ref name="Intro" />
