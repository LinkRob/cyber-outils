The {{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Waitress||Series, FPTRR}}}} is a character in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|Waitress|N/A|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Waitress lives in [[Port Town]] with her family, helping them operate their restaurant. She's the wife of the [[Chef]] and mother to both Big Bro and Dave. She gives Tingle a bigger pot every time he makes a new [[Recipe]] the Chef gives him.
