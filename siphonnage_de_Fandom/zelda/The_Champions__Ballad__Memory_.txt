"{{Term/Store|The Champions' Ballad||Series, BotW}}" is the fifth and final {{Term|BotW|EX Recovered Memory|link}} in {{TCB}} [[DLC]] Pack for {{BotW}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|The Champions' Ballad}}<br/>{{Color|BotW Memory|EX Recovered Memory #5}}|Adventure Log|BotW}}</ref>

==Overview==
"The Champions' Ballad" memory is sparked by listening to {{Term|BotW|Kass|link}} perform the {{Term|BotW|Champion's Ballad|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|If you would, please listen to this special song... The Champions' Ballad...|Kass|BotW}}</ref> As {{Term|BotW|Link|link}} looks upon {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Castle|link}}, he remembers an event that occurred one hundred years ago.

In the {{Term|BotW|Sanctum|link}} of Hyrule Castle, where {{Term|BotW|King Rhoam|link}} stands above {{Term|BotW|Princess Zelda|link}}, the five {{Plural|BotW|Champions|link}}, and representatives of their races. He welcomes them and thanks them for accepting their tasks.<ref>{{Cite|Welcome, warriors! I'd like to thank you for joining me here today, and for your bravery in accepting this...fateful task.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He officially appoints them as Champions of Hyrule, bestowing upon them sacred garbs.<ref>{{Cite|I officially appoint you Hyrule's Champions and bestow upon you this sacred garb.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> The blue is a symbol of the [[Royal Family of Hyrule]] passed down for generations.<ref>{{Cite|That blue is a symbol of the Royal Family, one that has been passed down for countless generations.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref>

Princess Zelda herself crafted the garments that the Champions wear.<ref>{{Cite|Those garments you now wear were all crafted by my daughter, Zelda.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> King Rhoam reminds Zelda of her duty, calling it a task only a daughter of the Royal Family could fulfill.<ref>{{Cite|Zelda. I trust you with the task only a daughter of the royal family can fulfill.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> He tells her to lead the Champions and protect {{Term|BotW|Hyrule Kingdom|link}} from {{Term|BotW|Calamity Ganon|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Lead our Champions, Princess. And together, protect our kingdom from the threat of Calamity Ganon.|King Rhoam|BotW}}</ref> Fireworks go off above the Castle in celebration.

The scene shifts to Zelda and the Champions in a gazebo on the castle grounds. {{Term|BotW|Daruk|link}} stretches and tells Link that formal events tend to make him tired.<ref>{{Cite|I tell ya, those formal shindigs really take it outta me.|Daruk|BotW}}</ref> Meanwhile, {{Term|BotW|Revali|link}}, {{Term|BotW|Urbosa|link}}, and {{Term|BotW|Mipha|link}} inspect the {{Term|BotW|Sheikah Slate|link}} that Zelda shows them.<ref>{{Cite|Hmph. This is the Sheikah Slate, eh?|Revali|BotW}}</ref> Zelda reveals that it has more uses than they initially thought, though they have yet to discover all of its secrets.<ref>{{Cite|It is. Apparently there are more uses for it than we originally thought. Sadly, we've yet to decipher all of its secrets.|Princess Zelda|BotW}}</ref> When Revali drops the Slate in Mipha's hand, Urbosa shares that Zelda showed her how it can take pictures.<ref>{{Cite|The princess showed me something strange recently. Somehow it can create true-to-life images.|Urbosa|BotW}}</ref> Mipha is interested and the concept and says she would love to see it.<ref>{{Cite|Oh, wow. I would love to see it.|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> Mipha then asks Zelda for a favor,<ref>{{Cite|Um, Princess... May I ask a special favor of you?|Mipha|BotW}}</ref> looking over her shoulder at Link.

From the view of the Sheikah Slate, Zelda and the Champions are seen gathered together. {{Term|BotW|Purah|link}}, who is holding the Slate, tells everyone to keep their eyes on it.<ref>{{Cite|All right! This spot should work nicely. Keep your eyes on the Sheikah Slate, everyone!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> She instructs Daruk to crouch down so he can fit in the frame, calling him as big as {{Term|BotW|Death Mountain|link}}.<ref>{{Cite|Daruk, can you crouch down a bit? You're as big as Death Mountain.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> She also encourages Zelda to smile,<ref>{{Cite|What's with the glum face, Princess? Gimme a big smile!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> to which Urbosa puts a comforting hand on her shoulder. Purah tells Revali to move closer to the group,<ref>{{Cite|Revali, move your tail closer to the group.|Purah|BotW}}</ref> and though he is annoyed by it, he does so anyway.<ref>{{Cite|Ugh, fine.|Revali|BotW}}</ref> Noticing how tense Mipha appears, Purah suggests she take deep breaths.<ref>{{Cite|Mipha, you look so tense. Deep breaths, OK?|Purah|BotW}}</ref> In response, Mipha attempts to control her breathing.<ref>{{Cite|Right!|Mipha|BotW}}</ref>

Once she is happy with their positioning, Purah tells them to stay where they are.<ref>{{Cite|Stay just like that! Here we go!|Purah|BotW}}</ref> She reminds them all to smile,<ref>{{Cite|Smiiiile...|Purah|BotW}}</ref> and just as she shouts her catchphrase of "Click, snap!",<ref>{{Cite|Click, snap!♪|Purah|BotW}}</ref> Daruk squishes all of the Champions together in a hug. The picture holds on screen before fading away as the memory ends.
