The {{Term/Store|Moblin Gear|Moblin Gear|BotW, HWAoC, Series}} is a recurring archetype of {{Term|BotW|Weapon|link}} in {{TLoZ|Series}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= CaC |publisher= |page= 174}}</ref>

==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
{{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear}} is a variety of equipment wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Moblin|link}}, which occurs in three tiers of increasing strength and durability. It includes only two types of {{Plural|BotW|Weapon|link}}, spears and two-handed clubs. Regular {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear}} is typically wielded by basic {{Plural|BotW|Moblin}} and is created by crudely carving {{Plural|BotW|Tree|link}} into shape. It has low {{Term|BotW|Durability|link}} due to the poor quality of its construction.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Moblin Club}}<br/>A crudely fashioned club favored by Moblins. It's carved from a sturdy tree but is sloppily made, so it breaks easily.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Moblin Spear}}<br/>This wooden spear is most often used by Moblins. It's made from a hastily whittled tree, so its stabbing power and durability are both pretty low.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear}} with the "Spiked" modifier is a stronger tier of weaponry typically used by {{Plural|BotW|Blue Moblin|link}}. It is strengthened through the use of {{Term|Series|Animal|link}} bones, giving it greater {{Term|BotW|Durability}} and damage output.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Spiked Moblin Club}}<br/>Animal bone has been affixed to this Moblin club to greatly improve its damage.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Spiked Moblin Spear}}<br/>This Moblin-made spear uses a horned animal bone as the spearhead. Like many Moblin weapons it's sloppily made, but this one packs some respectable piercing power.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref> The third and strongest variation, {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear}} with the "Dragonbone" modifier, is typically wielded by {{Plural|BotW|Black Moblin|link}}, {{Plural|BotW|Silver Moblin |link}}, and {{Plural|BotW|Golden Moblin |link}}. It's reinforced with fossilized bones, which further increases its attack power and {{Term|BotW|Durability}} beyond those of Spiked {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}}.<ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragonbone Moblin Club}}<br/>The bone of an ancient beast has been affixed to this Moblin club, further increasing its damage. Moblins carrying these in battle are particularly dangerous.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref><ref>{{Cite|{{Big|Dragonbone Moblin Spear}}<br/>This spear is a fan favorite among Moblins. The spearhead is made from fossilized bones adorned with spikes, which greatly increases its stabbing power.|Inventory|BotW}}</ref>

As all {{Term|BotW|Moblin Gear}} is formed around a wooden base, all {{Plural|BotW|Weapon}} within this archetype float when they are dropped in {{Term|BotW|Water (Terrain)|link}}. They also burn when exposed to {{Term|BotW|Fire|link}} or a {{Term|BotW|Scorching Climate|link}}, but they do not attract {{Term|BotW|Lightning|link}} during {{Plural|BotW|Thunderstorm|link}} or conduct {{Term|BotW|Electricity|link}}. Finally, they cannot be manipulated through use of the {{Term|BotW|Magnesis|link}} {{Term|BotW|Rune|link}}.
