{{Name|British|{{Term/Store|Bodyguard Salon|Bodyguard Salons|Series, FPTRR|plural}}}} are locations in {{FPTRR}}.<ref>{{Cite|This place is called the Bodyguard Salon.|Salona|FPTRR}}</ref>

==Features and Overview==
{{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard Salon}} are places where {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle|link}} can hire {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard|link}} to help him fight {{Plural|FPTRR|Enemy|link}}.

There is a {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard Salon}} on every island except {{Term|FPTRR|Hometown Prairie|link}}, run by the {{Term|FPTRR|Salona|link}}. Each salon will offer {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} three possible {{Plural|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} to hire. If {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} desires an alternative {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}} to those on display, he may ring the desk-bell and change the line-up for a fee. This fee increases from continent to continent.

When making a contract, the {{Term|FPTRR|Salona}} charge a commission. This fee allows {{Term|FPTRR|Tingle}} to talk to a {{Term|FPTRR|Bodyguard}}, and means that unlike other haggling opportunities in the game he will not lose large amounts of {{Plural|FPTRR|Rupee|link}} if he does not offer enough.

Although each salon appears identical, the doormat is unique to every island.
