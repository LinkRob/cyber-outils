==Characteristics==
Toadpolis are armored, two-legged [[Frog]]-like enemies that are found in watery areas, swimming in Water. They act very similarly to [[Octorok]]s. They spit projectiles at [[Link]] which he can slice with his [[Sword]] or deflect them back, defeating the Toadpolis. If Link comes too close to them, they dive back under the [[water]].

Toadpolis grow into [[Deku Toad]] after a thousand years.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= HH |publisher= |page= 192}}</ref>
