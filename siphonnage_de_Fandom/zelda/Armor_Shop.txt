==Features and Overview==
{{Plural|BotW|Armor Shop}} are {{Plural|BotW|Shop|link}} that sell pieces of {{Term|BotW|Armor|link}}. They can typically be found in major settlements across {{Term|BotW|Hyrule|link}}. Certain merchants and {{Plural|BotW|Jewelry Shop|link}} such as {{Term|BotW|Starlight Memories|link}} may also sell {{Term|BotW|Armor}}, but they do not qualify as {{Plural|BotW|Armor Shop}}.

{{Plural|BotW|Armor Shop}} usually sell specific {{Plural|BotW|Armor Set|link}}, such as {{Term|BotW|Ripped and Shredded|link}}, which sells the {{Term|BotW|Flamebreaker Set|link}}.
