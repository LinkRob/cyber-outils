The {{Term/Store|Derby Boy||Series, ALBW}} is a character from {{ALBW}}.<ref>{{Cite Book|quote= |book= E |publisher= |page= 300 {{Exp Game|ALBW}}}}</ref>

==Biography==
The Derby Boy can be found at the playing field in central [[Lorule]]. He runs the [[Octoball Derby]] mini-game with the [[Derby Girl]] and [[Octo]]. He invites [[Link]] to try their game at the cost of 50 [[Rupee]]s. If Link scores at least 100 points in the game, the Derby Boy will reward him with a [[Piece of Heart]].
