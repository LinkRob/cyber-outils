==Location and Uses==
==={{BotW|-}}===
Soldier's Greaves can be purchased at the [[Ventest Clothing Boutique]] in [[Hateno Village]]. They're a part of the [[Soldier's Set]], along with the [[Soldier's Helm]] and [[Soldier's Armor]]. Wearing them provides no bonus aside from an increased defense. Soldier's Greaves can be both enhanced by a {{Term|BotW|Great Fairy|link}} and [[Dye]]d at the [[Kochi Dye Shop]].
