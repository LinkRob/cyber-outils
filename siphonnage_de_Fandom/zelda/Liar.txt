==Biography==
{{Term|RTBToL|Liar}} is the daughter of the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Chief}} and one of {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}}'s love interests. She is first seen on {{Term|RTBToL|Page 11|link}}, in {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|link}}. Because she is an {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami|link}}, she only speaks with lies. At first, she is fighting with the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Chief}}, then runs off, pushing {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle|link}} into a herd of {{Plural|RTBToL|Goat|link}}. When she sees {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}, she runs away in tears. After that, the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Chief}} tells the group that their friend, {{Term|RTBToL|Kakashi|link}}, is being kept in a cave at the end of the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}}. However, to enter the cave, they need an offering first, and only {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}} can help them with that.

{{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} is sent to find {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}}, who will not speak with him until he gives her enough gifts in a {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push|link}}. When the {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push}} is completed, she will accept to help {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}. {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}} gives {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} a list of characteristics of a specific {{Term|RTBToL|Goat}} he needs to find from the herd. When selected, {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}} will milk the {{Term|RTBToL|Animal}} as part of a ritual, finally giving {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} the {{Term|RTBToL|Milk|link}}, allowing the group to finally go to {{Term|RTBToL|Big Liar|link}}'s cave. After the battle is over, {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}} and the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Chief}} will come to rescue {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}} and his friends. Once the {{Term|RTBToL|Page}} is completed, {{Term|RTBToL|Liar}} will stay in the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village}}. If her second heart is unlocked, she will move to the desert, where her final {{Term|RTBToL|Love Push}} takes place. She then returns to the {{Term|RTBToL|Usotami Village|display=Village}} after the date scene with {{Term|RTBToL|Tingle}}.
