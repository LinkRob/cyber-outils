{{Term/Store|''The Legend of Zelda: Skyward Sword Original Soundtrack''||Series, SS}} 「ゼルダの伝説 スカイウォードソード オリジナル・サウンドトラック」is the original soundtrack for {{SS}}. It includes five discs in a digipak. The limited edition also features a music box that plays "{{Term|SS|Ballad of the Goddess|link}}" and a slipcase.<ref>{{Cite Web|quote= |author= |published= |retrieved= September 17, 2021|url= https://columbia.jp/prod-info/COCX-41640-4/|title= ゼルダの伝説 スカイウォードソード オリジナルサウンドトラック【初回数量限定生産盤】|site= Nippon Columbia|type= }}</ref> It is exclusive to Japan.

The soundtrack was composed by [[Hajime Wakai]], [[Takeshi Hama]], [[Shiho Fujii]], [[Mahito Yokota]], [[Masato Mizuta]] and [[Koji Kondo]].
