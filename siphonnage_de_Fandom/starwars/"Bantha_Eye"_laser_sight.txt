The '''"Bantha's Eye" laser sight''' was a model of scope that sold for 500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The [[Scope|sight]] could be mounted upon a [[rifle]] and emitted a beam of light that could be used to assist the shooter in targeting.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
