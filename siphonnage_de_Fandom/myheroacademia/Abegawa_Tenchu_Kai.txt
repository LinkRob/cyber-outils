{{Nihongo|'''Abegawa Tenchu Kai'''|{{Ruby|阿|あ}}{{Ruby|辺|べ}}{{Ruby|川|がわ}}{{Ruby|天|てん}}{{Ruby|忠|ちゅう}}{{Ruby|會|かい}}|Abegawa Tenchū-kai}} was a former [[Yakuza]] organization appearing in ''[[My Hero Academia: Vigilantes]]''.

== Overview ==
The Abegawa Tenchu Kai was a violent designated [[Villains|villain]] organization consisting of yakuza, whose roots can be traced back long before the emergence of the [[Quirk|paranormal]]. They had their headquarters stationed in Tokyo's Danto Ward. 

The Abegawa’s gang was known because all of it’s executives wielded incredibly powerful quirks, but what was truly fearsome about them was not the strength of their quirks, but rather their "reflexes" for violence, allowing them to instantaneously transition into combat.{{Ref/Vigilantes|special=9a|ep=6}}

==Plot==
After the Tenchu Kai was categorized as a villainous organization by the police, their headquarters is invaded by the vigilante [[Chizome Akaguro|Stendhal]] and its high-ranking executives were all killed in combat.{{Ref/Vigilantes|special=9a|ep=6}}

==Trivia==
*The organization itself, as well as the Quirks and appearance of each member, are based on the {{Wikipedia|Avengers (comics)|Avengers}}, primarily their Marvel Cinematic Universe incarnations:
**The name "Abegawa" resembles the Japanese pronunciation of Avengers, "Abenjāzu".
**[[Rojiya Yonenaga]] resembles {{Wikipedia|Captain America}}.
**[[Tetsu]] resembles {{Wikipedia|Iron Man}}.
**[[Haruhisa]] resembles the {{Wikipedia|Hulk}}.
**[[Soji]] resembles {{Wikipedia|Thor (Marvel Comics)|Thor}}.
**The remaining three unnamed members resemble {{Wikipedia|Bucky Barnes|the Winter Soldier}}, {{Wikipedia|Black Widow (Natasha Romanova)|Black Widow}}, and {{Wikipedia|Hawkeye (comics)|Hawkeye}}.
