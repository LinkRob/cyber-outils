{{Nihongo|'''About a Voyage'''|航海の唄|Kōkai no Uta}} is the sixth ending theme of the [[My Hero Academia (Anime)|''My Hero Academia'']] anime adaptation and the first ending of [[My Hero Academia Season 4|Season 4]]. It was performed by {{wikipedia|Sayuri}}.

Nowhere to turn, you stand at a a crossroads by yourself

So that you can't backtrack, now

But even so, you'll go, won't you?
It's okay to keep lacking what you lack, just go search for it now

There is a light you want so now you stand alone at a crossroads with nowhere else to go
Making sure there’s no going back, now you stifle a fleeting sense of anxiety as you cut off all ways to go back home

And yet, you’ll still go

That will be the one thing that saves you, no matter what kind of pain awaits in the future

Breathing life into that each step

==Trivia ==
*The ending details [[Eri|Eri's]] past from living happily with her family, being abandoned because she accidentally [[Rewind|rewinded]] her father out of existence and ending up with [[Kai Chisaki|Overhaul]] until meeting with [[Izuku Midoriya|Deku]] and [[Mirio Togata|Lemillion]] and escaping from Overhaul.
