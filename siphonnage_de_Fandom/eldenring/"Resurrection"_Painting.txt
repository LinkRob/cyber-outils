'''"Resurrection"''' is an [[Info Item|info item]] and [[Painting|painting]] in {{ER}}. <br>Depicts the scene of a distant [[Academy of Raya Lucaria]] as seen from the perspective of [[Caria Manor]].

Leads to the [[Juvenile Scholar Cap]] and [[Juvenile Scholar Robe]].

==Acquisition==
[[File:ER Site of Grace Artist's Shack (Liurnia).png|thumb]]
'''Loot:''' [[Liurnia of the Lakes]]
*Acquired from interacting with the painting found inside the [[Artist's Shack (Liurnia)|Artist's Shack]] located near a cliff in east Liurnia.

==Effect==
Allows the [[Player]] to perceive the soul of a painter and retrieve a unique item from their location. <br>The location being the point of perspective depicted by the painting itself.

The soul in question can be found on a lying rock pillar on the eastern cliffs behind [[Caria Manor]].

The painter is initially invisible when viewed from a distance but will eventually reveal themselves as the player gets physically closer to them. Successfully locating the spirit will make it dissipate before leaving the [[Juvenile Scholar Set]] for the player to pick up from the ground.

==Notes==
*This painting's name, "Resurrection" is likely a reference to [[Queen Rennala|Queen Rennala's]] endless cycle of re-birthing her [[Juvenile Scholar|Juvenile Scholars]]. Due to its given item, its perspective being taken from Rennala's home and it's depiction of Raya Lucaria, Rennala's current prison.
