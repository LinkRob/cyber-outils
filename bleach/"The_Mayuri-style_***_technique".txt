{{translation|'''"The Mayuri-style *** technique"'''|マユリ式{{ruby|○○○|ビー}}術|Mayuri-shiki bī Jutsu}} is a unique ability utilized by [[Mayuri Kurotsuchi]].<ref>''[[Bleach: Official Character Book 3 UNMASKED]]'', page 155</ref>

==Description==
If [[Nemu Kurotsuchi]] is left on death's door by her lifeforce being drained, Mayuri can revive her to full health using this technique, which thoroughly embarrassed [[Renji Abarai]] and [[Uryū Ishida]], leaving them confused as to how it actually restored Nemu.<ref>''Bleach'' manga; Chapter 305, pages 9-10</ref>
