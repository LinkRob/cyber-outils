The '''.390-Caliber''' is a type of ammunition used by the [[United Nations Space Command|UNSC]].

==Description==
The .390 (9.9mm) is one of the calibers used by the [[MA2B Individual Combat Weapon System]], it is a larger round than the [[M118 7.62x51mm Full Metal Jacket Armor-Piercing|M118 7.62x51mm]] round used in the [[MA5 Series]].
