The ''''Ahtulai Workshop Attack Flyer''', commonly known as the '''Banshee''', was a variant of the [[Banshee]] utilized by [[The Banished]].{{Ref/Reuse|RefName=cfartofwars}}

==Design==
The Banished Banshee bears a heavy resemblance to the A and C variants of the [[Type-26 Ground Support Aircraft|Type-26 "Banshee"]], but appears to have been reinforced with additional metal plating on its cowling and wings. It also possesses a unique color scheme shared with other Banished vehicles: silver with red-colored panels on its cowling, wings and plasma cannons as opposed to the predominantly purple coloration of other Banshee models.{{Ref/Reuse|RefName=cfartofwars}}

This was done to more closely look like an aerial predator from the skies of [[Doisac]].{{Ref/Website|RefName=cfartofwars|URL=https://www.halowaypoint.com/en-us/news/the-art-of-war-s|SiteName=Halo Waypoint|PageName=Canon Fodder - THE ART OF WAR(S)}}

===Armament===
A lot of Banished Banshees used experimental weapons. These were to give the Banished large amounts of damage and assist in psychological warfare.{{Ref/Reuse|RefName=cfartofwars}}

==Gameplay==
The Banished Banshee is a core air unit available when a Banished leader is selected and is constructed in the Apex.
