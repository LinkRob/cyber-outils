''''Kasamee''' was a [[Covenant Empire|Covenant]] [[Sangheili]] who was part of the forces occupying the crash site of the {{UNSCShip|Pillar of Autumn}} during the [[Battle of Installation 04]]. As he was considered a veteran, it is probable that he held the rank of [[Sangheili Major|Major]].<ref name="Flood">'''[[Halo: The Flood]]''', pages 319-320</ref>

==Biography==
While present on [[Installation 04]] in [[2552#September|September 2552]], 'Kasamee was assigned to the Covenant force stationed at the crash site of the abandoned ''Pillar of Autumn'' under the command of [['Ontomee]].<ref name="Flood"/> When [[Zuka 'Zamamee]] arrived at the ship under the alias of "[[Huki 'Umamee]]," 'Kasamee looked upon him with respect for his vigorous enthusiasm for personally hunting down [[SPARTAN-II Program|Spartan]] [[John-117]]. However, the two never spoke and 'Zamamee quickly left the room to hunt the Spartan.<ref name="Flood"/>

'Kasamee might have been killed by the [[Flood]] or the [[Z-1500 Automated Systems Drone|Sentinel]]s, although he certainly would have been killed when the ''Pillar of Autumn'' and, by extension, Installation 04, were destroyed.
