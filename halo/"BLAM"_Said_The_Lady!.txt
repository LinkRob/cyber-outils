'''"BLAM" Said The Lady!''' is an achievement in ''[[Halo: The Master Chief Collection]]''. This achievement is obtained by swapping [[Veronica Dare|Dare]]'s [[M6C/SOCOM|pistol]] for a [[M41 Surface-to-Surface Rocket Medium Anti-Vehicle/Assault Weapon|rocket launcher]] on [[Coastal Highway]].
