ARTICLE : 'Mdama

{{References}}'''Mdama''' is a state on [[Sanghelios]]. It is considered by other states to be a backwater state.
==History==
When the [[Great Schism]] started, many [[Sangheili]] struggled merely to get food, and power shifted towards agriculture on Sanghelios. 'Mdama suddenly gained a great deal of power due its fertile farmland.

After the [[Human-Covenant war]], the [[Arbiter]] [[Thel 'Vadam]] visited the state to gather support for a peace treaty with humanity. Many supported the idea, including 'Mdama's [[Kaidon]], [[Levu 'Mdama]]. However, the elder of [[Bekan Keep]], [[Jul 'Mdama]] and his friend [[Forze]] did not, and pledged their keeps' support to the [[Servants of the Abiding Truth|Servants of Abiding Truth]], who opposed the Arbiter.
