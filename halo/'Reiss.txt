''''Reiss''' is a [[Sangheili]] Tankmaster who is very skilled in driving [[Type-58 Light Assault Gun Carriage|Wraith]]s.

==Gameplay==
'Reiss appears in certain [[Warzone Firefight]] simulations as a Legendary boss. He fights in a Temple Wraith. He, [['Ahtulai]] and [['Lcmutee]] are collectively known as the ''Wraith Trio''.
