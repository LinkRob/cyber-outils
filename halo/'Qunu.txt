'''{{'}}Qunu''' was a [[Covenant Empire|Covenant]] [[Sangheili]] and [[Shipmaster]] of the destroyer ''[[Far Sight Lost]]''.<ref name = "GoO">'''Halo: Ghosts of Onyx''' - ''page 278-280''</ref> His knowledge of the ritual responses from the ''[[Fire and Repentance Codices]]'' was said to be unmatched.<ref name = "GoO"/>

In [[2552]], his ship, which was part of the ''[[Combined Fleet of Righteous Purpose]]'', was sent with the ''[[Incorruptible]]'' to [[Trevelyan]]. 'Qunu was ordered ahead of the main fleet to try and make contact with the [[Onyx Sentinel]]s. The Sentinels, in turn, destroyed his ship and killed him along with the ship's entire crew.<ref name = "GoO"/>
