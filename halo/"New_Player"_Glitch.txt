ARTICLE : "New Player" Glitch
==============================

{{Era|HR}}
{{Orphan|date=April 2013}}
This [[:Category:Glitches|glitch]] occurs when, at any time during a single player match of any type in ''[[Halo: Reach]]'', a new player locally logs in and initiates split screen. If this new player has a [[Sangheili]] archetype selected and the game mode permits its use, then this glitch may activate.

==Effects==
This glitch causes an interesting cosmetic effect. To anyone else viewing, your character appears to be a completely default Spartan, but in first person the character's arms and legs appear to be that of a Sangheili Minor without the armor on the wrists. There do not seem to be any adverse effects on gameplay.
==Causes==
The only known cause is to sign in to a game already in session.
==Fixes/Prevention==
The only way to fix this glitch is to die or respawn, and there is no way to prevent this from happening.
