''''Ontomee''' was a high-ranking [[Sangheili]] stationed on [[Installation 04]] in [[2552]].

==Biography==
After {{UNSCShip|Pillar of Autumn}} was shot down and [[First Lieutenant]] [[Melissa McKay]] conducted a salvage operation of supplies in the ship, 'Ontomee was put in charge of the crash site to make sure the [[Human]]s could not access it anymore.

During the final days of the [[Battle of Installation 04]], 'Ontomee and his overworked, undermanned staff received fortunate relief in the form of [[Zuka 'Zamamee]] (under the alias of [[Huki 'Umamee]]) and [[Yayap]]. Without asking questions, 'Ontomee put them in charge of twenty [[Kig-Yar]], and assigned them to inspect and unload supplies.<ref>'''[[Halo: The Flood]]''', ''page 296''</ref>

Under attack from both [[Flood]] and [[Forerunner]] constructs, 'Ontomee and his staff were further troubled when [[John-117]] arrived aboard the ship with the intention of destroying [[Installation 04]]. 'Ontomee called a meeting of his staff where he, [['Kasamee]], and Zuka 'Zamamee discussed the Spartan and agreed to hunt him down.<ref>'''[[Halo: The Flood]]''', ''pages 319-320''</ref>
