'''...and I don't do bits and pieces''' is an achievement in the [[Avery Johnson|Sergeant Johnson]] DLC for ''[[Halo Wars 2]]''. This achievement is obtained when a player uses the Repair Beacon special ability 15 times in either [[Multiplayer]], [[Skirmish]] or [[Blitz]].

==Trivia==
*The title of the achievement could be a reference to Sergeant Johnson saying: "Crazy fool! Why do you always jump? One of these days, you're gonna land on somethin' as stubborn as you are! And I don't do bits and pieces!" during the level [[Arrival]] of ''[[Halo 3]]''.
