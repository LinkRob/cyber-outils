'''Alfred Matthew "Weird Al" Yankovic''' is an American actor, singer-songwriter, musician, record producer, satirist, and parodist. He is best known for his humorous songs that make light of popular culture and often parody specific songs by contemporary musical acts. He is also well-known for starring in his own meta-show called ''{{WikipediaLink|The Weird Al Show}}'', as well as voicing [[w:c:mlp:Cheese Sandwich|Cheese Sandwich]] in ''{{WikipediaLink|My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic}}''.

For [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]], he appeared as himself in the [[1998]] television film ''[[Safety Patrol]]'', as well as sang the introduction song of the [[1996]] [[Hollywood Pictures]] film ''[[Spy Hard]]''. He has also done several voice-over roles, such as guest-starring as the singing minstrel in the ''[[Lilo & Stitch: The Series]]'' episode "[[Tank (episode)|Tank]]", [[Probabilitor the Annoying]] in the ''[[Gravity Falls]]'' episode "[[Dungeons, Dungeons, & More Dungeons]]" (as well as himself in a hidden video promoting ''[[The Book of Bill]]''), [[Dr. Screwball Jones]] in ''[[Wander Over Yonder]]'', Preston Change-O in the ''[[Star vs. the Forces of Evil]]'' episode "[[Trickstar]]", and Shapeshifter in ''[[The 7D]]''. Beginning in [[2016]], Yankovic voiced [[Milo Murphy|the titular character]] of ''[[Milo Murphy's Law]]'' and made a cameo in ''[[Phineas and Ferb the Movie: Candace Against the Universe]]'' as Shirt Cannon Guy, as well as voiced Chip Dingle in ''[[Hailey's On It!]]''. Additionally, he appeared in shows on [[ABC]] as himself in ''{{WikipediaLink|The Goldbergs (2013 TV series)|The Goldbergs}}'' and the Head Monk in ''[[Galavant]]''.

Prior to all of Yankovic's above-listed roles, his song "{{WikipediaLink|Eat It}}" (a parody of [[Michael Jackson]]'s "{{WikipediaLink|Beat It}}") was the subject of a music video featured in the [[DTV]] TV special ''[[DTV#DTV Doggone Valentine|Doggone Valentine]]'', and "I Want a New Duck" (a parody of [[Huey Lewis]] and the News' "{{WikipediaLink|I Want a New Drug}}") was used as the opening and closing song for ''[[Down and Out with Donald Duck]]''. Yankovic himself also appeared in "Captain EE-I-EE-I-O", a one-shot comic featured in the January [[1993]] issue of ''[[Disney Adventures]]''.

His cover of the "[[George of the Jungle (song)|George of the Jungle]]" theme was used in the opening credits sequence of [[George of the Jungle|the movie adaptation of the TV show]], and he was also referenced in the ''[[Lizzie McGuire]]'' episode "[[Bunkies]]". Additionally, the ''[[Randy Cunningham: 9th Grade Ninja]]'' episode "[[M-M-M-my Bologna]]" is named after one of his songs.

Yankovic wrote a song about the [[Jungle Cruise]] attraction entitled "Skipper Dan", which was referenced back into the attraction via a canteen prop added to the queue during the [[2021]] refurbishment. In 2024, Yankovic would incorporate the ''[[Encanto]]'' song "[[We Don't Talk About Bruno]]" into his single "Polkamania!".

Yankovic was mentioned in the ''[[Runaways (TV series)|Runaways]]'' episode "[[Rewind (Runaways)|Rewind]]".
