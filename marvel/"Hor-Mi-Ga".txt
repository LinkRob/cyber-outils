| History                 = Located in a different galaxy, the planet "Hor-Mi-Ga" was inhabited by a man-sized humanoid ant species. They had very advanced technology, including inter-galactic flight, ray weapons and an electronic brain that allowed them to revert the pass of time, turning a target area into a past version of it. They were however unable to produce food in big enough amounts to feed all of its inhabitants. This led to the leadership of the species to try and invade another planet, having chosen [[Earth]] as their candidate. As Earth's technology was a match for their weaponry, the aliens tried to revert time to a point where primitive men inhabited (and shared their world with dinosaurs, either because Earth-TRN911's pre-history was like that, or because of a secondary effect of the "Hor-Mi-Ga" technology). However, this brought the "Hor-Mi-Ga" aliens into conflict with time-displaced superhero [[Peter Parker (Earth-TRN911)|el Hombre Araña]], who was not reverted to a primitive state, and who allied himself with another time-displaced traveller, [[Steves (Earth-TRN911)|Steves]], and with primitive [[Zor (Earth-TRN911)|Zor]]'s tribe.{{r|Amazing Spider-Man (MX) Vol 1 178}} With this help, el Hombre Araña caused a fire and a stampede of mammoths near the alien headquarters on Earth. The aliens panicked, especially fearing fire, and attempted to flee in their ships. When the alien [[Jefe Supremo from "Hor-Mi-Ga" (Earth-TRN911)|Jefe Supremo]] saw the inferno, he decided to cancel his invasion plans for Earth and activated the destruction of his facility there, not caring about the loss of life of his warriors. This returned el Hombre Araña and Steves to their era.{{r|Amazing Spider-Man (MX) Vol 1 179}}
