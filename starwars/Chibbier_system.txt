The '''Chibbier system''' was located in [[Stensen sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square I-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> contained the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Chibbier]].<ref name="ACOF" />

The [[bounty hunter]] [[Raslin Grace]] told a story to several individuals<ref name="Tall Tales" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The present-[[Standard day|day]] events of "[[Tall Tales]]" take place after the [[Skirmish aboard the Acquisitor|attempted assassination of Domina Tagge]] and before [[Boushh's armor]] is taken by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] in "[[Bounty Hunted]]," events that ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively.</ref> claiming they visited a seedy [[Unidentified dive|dive]] on Chibbier. According to Grace's account, while in the dive, the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] had sent out a bounty on a pair of [[Unidentified Cathar twin 1|Cathar]] [[Unidentified Cathar twin 2|twins]]. Grace claimed that<ref name="Tall Tales" /> she<ref name="Boba Fett 4">[[Boba Fett — Black, White & Red 4|''Boba Fett — Black, White & Red'' 4]]</ref> departed Chibbier to pursue the [[twin]]s and successfully completed the job, though several of the individuals listening did not believe her.<ref name="Tall Tales" /> Around [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. As the novel establishes that [[Delta Jeet]] left [[Chibbier]] two [[Standard month|months]] before its events, she must have departed around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> a number of workers were employed to cut down [[tree]]s on Chibbier, including the [[human]] [[Delta Jeet]] and her cousins. Eventually, the forests ran out, and all the workers were laid off.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chibbier system was introduced in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The first appearance of the planet Chibbier<ref name="Tall Tales" />&mdash;and therefore the Chibbier system, which contained that world<ref name="Appendix" />&mdash;was via flashback in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Tall Tales]]," written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and illustrated by [[David Baldeón]]. It was published by [[Marvel Comics]] in the one-shot comic [[Revelations (2023) 1|''Revelations'' 1]]<ref name="Tall Tales" /> on [[December 20]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112390/star_wars_revelations_2023_1|text=STAR WARS: REVELATIONS (2023) #1}}</ref>
