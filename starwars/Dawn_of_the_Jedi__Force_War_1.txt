'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force War'' 1''' is the first issue of the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force War|''Force War'']] miniseries, a story arc in the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series. Written by [[John Ostrander]] and [[Jan Duursema]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[November 23]], [[2013]], the issue begins in [[25,792 BBY]], one [[Standard year/Legends|year]] after the events of the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''Prisoner of Bogan'']] arc. ''Force War'' deals with the [[Rakata/Legends|Rakatan]] [[Infinite Empire/Legends|Infinite Empire's]] [[Invasion of the Tython system|invasion]] of the [[Tython system/Legends|Tython system]], and the first issue sees the [[Je'daii Order]] [[Battle of Shikaakwa|repel]] the Infinite Empire's [[Flesh Raider]] [[Soldier/Legends|soldiers]] from the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Shikaakwa]]. ''Force War 1'' also sets up the rest of the arc, revealing [[Predor]] [[Skal'nas]]'s plan to invade the planet [[Tython/Legends|Tython]] and locate the [[Infinity Gate]] rumored to exist there, as he hopes that the Gate will restore his [[Species/Legends|species]]' declining [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-sensitivity]].

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|The fearsome Rakata have invaded the Tython system, and war rages on every world. With Forcesabers in hand, the Je'daii have aligned themselves with unlikely generals: Daegen Lok, the mad Je'daii, and the stranger, the Force Hound Xesh—one the devil they know, the other the wild card they don't!|Publisher's summary for ''Force War 1''|<ref name="DarkHorse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/22-793/Star-Wars-Dawn-of-the-Jedi-Force-War-1|text=Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi—Force War #1}}</ref>}}
[[25,792 BBY|One year]] after the [[Infinite Empire/Legends|Infinite Empire]] of the [[Rakata/Legends|Rakata]] [[Species/Legends|species]] has [[Invasion of the Tython system|invaded]] the [[Tython system/Legends|Tython system]], the Empire has conquered the [[Planet/Legends|planets]] [[Furies Gate]], [[Obri]], [[Mawr]], and [[Ska Gora]]. They were [[First battle of Tython|repelled]] by the [[Je'daii Order]] at [[Tython/Legends|Tython]], though the Rakata have established a [[Rakatan Base Prime|command post]] on Ska Gora. The [[Je'daii]] have allied with the [[Settled Worlds]] and appointed [[Rajivari]] as the [[General/Legends|general]] of their joint forces, and also constructed [[Forcesaber]]s with the aid of the former [[Force Hound]]-turned-Je'daii [[Tau (individual)|Xesh]]&mdash;but the weapons forced the Je'daii to draw upon the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]] of [[The Force/Legends|the Force]]. The mad Je'daii [[Daegen Lok]] has been freed from his imprisonment on the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Bogan]] and allowed to lead the Je'daii into battle against the Flesh Raiders.<ref name="DOTJ 11" />

[[File:FleshRaiderFW.jpg|thumb|right|235px|[[Flesh Raider]]s search for the [[Je'daii Order|Je'daii]].]]
When the Je'daii [[Force vision/Legends|foresee]] the Infinite Empire's [[Battle of Shikaakwa|attack]] on the planet [[Shikaakwa]], they arrange for their forces to ambush the enemy in the swamps surrounding [[Ryo Fortress]], which serves as a command post for the [[Tythan]]s. As the [[Flesh Raider]] [[Soldier/Legends|soldiers]] of the Empire make their way through the swamps around Ryo Fortress, Xesh ambushes a group and engages them in battle. Xesh dispatches many of the Flesh Raiders, and when a [[Unidentified Force Hound|Force Hound]] tries to attack Xesh, the [[Je'daii Ranger]] [[Shae Koda]] intercepts the Force Hound atop her [[rancor-dragon]] [[Butch]]. Koda kills the enemy's mount, and Xesh defeats the Force Hound when his opponent survives. Xesh and Daegen Lok then flee from a trio of destructive [[Rakatan annihilator]]s, and Xesh convinces Lok to order an airstrike on their position in order to take out the enemy walkers. Despite the presence of other Je'daii in the area, Lok orders [[Hawk Ryo]] and his [[Hunter-class starfighter|''Hunter''-class starfighters]] to bomb the Annihilators. Amid the chaos, the Je'daii [[Sek'nos Rath]] saves his ally [[Trill (Force Hound)|Trill]] and ferries her out of danger. In Ryo Fortress, General Rajivari oversees the battle despite criticism from [[Baron/Legends|Baron]] [[Volnos Ryo]], and upon learning of the successful attack on the Annihilators, he orders [[Je'daii Master|Masters]] [[Tave]], [[Quan-Jang]], and [[Tem Madog]] to launch their primary assault against the Rakatan forces.<ref name="DOTJ 11" />

The Rakatan commander, [[Sub-Predor]] [[Ceh'let]], informs [[Predor]] [[Skal'nas]] of the heavy losses that her forces are taking, and Skal'nas allows Ceh'let to withdraw her forces to Ska Gora. Despite the Rakatan retreat, the Flesh Raiders continue to fight, and as a result Xesh orders the Je'daii to wipe them all out. Hours later, Lok gives a victory speech to the remaining Je'daii, though Sek'nos Rath storms off in disgust, as he dislikes how the Je'daii are embracing the dark side. Xesh struggles to recall where he remembers Trill from, and he and Shae Koda share a moment before Koda departs. On Ska Gora, Ceh'let openly challenges Skal'nas's authority in front of the other commanders, prompting Skal'nas to end the war conference and speak to her in private. Skal'nas explains that the Rakata are losing their [[Force-sensitive/Legends|connection]] to the Force, and that he seeks the [[Infinity Gate]] built by the [[Kwa/Legends|Kwa]] species that supposedly exists on Tython. With the Infinity Gate, Skal'nas hopes to reverse the decay of his species' Force potential, and by doing so become the ruler of the Empire. However, after explaining his plans, Skal'nas then executes Ceh'let with [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]] as punishment for challenging him.<ref name="DOTJ 11" />

==Development==
''Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force War 1'' is the first issue in [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force War|''Dawn of the Jedi: Force War'']], a five-part [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries produced by [[Dark Horse Comics]]. ''Force War'' is the third story arc miniseries in the series ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'', and follows the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''Prisoner of Bogan'']] arc. The story for ''Force War 1'' was written by [[John Ostrander]] and [[Jan Duursema]], the creative team behind ''Dawn of the Jedi''; in addition to the overall story, Ostrander handled the script and Duursema the pencils for the issue. [[Dan Parsons]] was the issue's inker, while [[Wes Dzioba]] handled coloring duties and [[Michael Heisler]] lettered the issue. The cover for ''Force War 1'' was drawn by [[David Michael Beck]], and [[Randy Stradley]] served as editor. [[Jimmy Presler]], [[Freddye Lins]], and [[Mike Richardson]] were also credited as the designer, assistant editor, and publisher for the issue respectively.<ref name="DOTJ 11" /> The ''Force War'' arc was first announced as "Force Wars" at Wondercon 2013 on [[April 1]],<ref name="Wondercon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/wc13-dark-horse-grows-its-star-wars-empire|text=WC13: Dark Horse Grows its "Star Wars" Empire|date=2013-04-01|archivedate=20200918111637}}</ref> and ''Force War 1'' was first officially announced on [[August 14]], [[2013]] in Dark Horse's [[November]] 2013 solicitations. The issue itself was released on [[November 20]].<ref name="Solicit">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/dark-horse-comics-solicitations-for-november-2013|text=Dark Horse Comics Solicitations for November, 2013|date=2013-08-14|archivedate=20200918111807}}</ref>

==Continuity==
{{Quote|Xesh has forgotten much of his life as a Force Hound of the Rakata. But he never forgot these creatures&mdash;mutated lower caste Rakata, bred in the thousands as soldiers. As weapons, hungering to kill, hungering to eat. ''Flesh Raiders''.|Xesh recalls the origins of the Flesh Raiders|<ref name="DOTJ 11" />}}
The Flesh Raiders that appear in ''Force War 1'' are a species which first originated in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', and the comic was the first ''[[Star Wars]]'' material outside of the game's metaseries to depict the species. ''Force War 1'' supplies backstory for the Flesh Raiders, establishing them as mutated lower-caste Rakata, and gives a precedent for their presence on Tython in ''The Old Republic'', twenty-four thousand years later. The issue is also the first to depict a female Rakata in Ceh'let, and introduces the fact that female Rakata have hair unlike their male counterparts. ''Force War 1'' depicts Rajivari, a character first introduced in ''The Old Republic'', as the general of the Je'daii and the Settled Worlds, and builds off of Rajivari's established in-game history as a Je'daii leader.<ref name="DOTJ 11" />
