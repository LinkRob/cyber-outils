The '''Apprentice Nihilator''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Sith Apprentice (Rieve)|standard costume]]. It was designed to resemble the types of [[armor]] commonly worn by the [[Nihil]], anarchistic [[Pirate|marauders]] who rose to prominence during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Apprentice Nihilator was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Rieve, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Nihilator Saber Pattern]].<ref name="Hunters" />
