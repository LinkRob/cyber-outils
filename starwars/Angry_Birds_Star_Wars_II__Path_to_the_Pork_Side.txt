'''''Angry Birds Star Wars II: Path to the Pork Side''''' is a children's book by [[Dorling Kindersley]]. It was released on [[November 18]], [[2013]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Angry Birds Star Wars II'' will be coming to an app store near you on September 19, 2013!

Can Redkin Skywalker resist the tempting power of the Pork Side? Find out all about the young Jedi Bird in this fun-filled Level 2 Reader from DK. Meet the brave Jedi Birds and their newest recruit, who may not be all he seems!
