'''Agaris''' was a [[planet]] with a damp, clammy climate located on the very edge of [[Wild Space]] and was the adopted [[homeworld]] of the [[Agarian]] [[species]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Tualon Yaluna's company]] used Agaris in an attempt to hide their approach to [[Olgothon 3]] during a [[Mission to Olgothon 3|mission]] to find [[Ginntho (Anzellan)|Ginntho]].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> Before the [[Reproduction|birth]] of their daughter [[Lina Graf|Lina]], the [[cartographer]]s [[Auric Graf|Auric]] and [[Rhyssa Graf]] visited the world. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], Agaris hosted an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[mining]] [[Imperial compound (Agaris)|compound]].<ref name="The Cold" /> The mining compound was established by the Empire in an effort to mine [[Quadanium steel|quadanium]] for the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a battle station armed with a [[Mk I Superlaser|weapon]] powerful enough to destroy an entire planet.<ref name="The Rescue" />
