'''Durasteel coils''' were a type of [[starship]] component.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[pirate]]s in the [[Khepi system]] modified these type of coils to constantly funnel low levels of energy to a ships [[Hull repair system|repair systems]] in order to help survive the corrosive effects of the [[Sayma Nebula]]. A set of coils were stashed in a container in a hangar in [[Hauler's Keep]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] discovered the reinforced coils and used them to upgrade the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
