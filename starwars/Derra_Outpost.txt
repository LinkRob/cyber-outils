The '''Derra Outpost''' was an [[Jedi outpost|outpost]] of the [[Jedi Order]] located on<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Derra system]]</ref> astronomical object [[Derra IV|Derra]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> By [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]] was the outpost's [[marshal]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> after the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[First Battle of Cyclor|attacked]] the [[Cyclor Shipyards]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Cyclor]], the Derra Outpost dispatched a [[drift]] of [[Jedi Vector]]s led by Jedi Master [[Porter Engle]] to assist.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

Upon learning of the attack, [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Stellan Gios]] questioned what the nearest outpost to Cyclor was, and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]] informed him it was the Derra Outpost. As Gios tried to recall who the outpost's marshal was,<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Nib Assek]] provided him with the name. Afterward, Gios contacted Sinube to inform him of the attack, but the marshal told him the outpost had been aware of the attack and had already sent the drift to help. Following this, Gios informed [[Grand Master]] [[Pra-Tre Veter]] of the Derra Outpost's reinforcements.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
