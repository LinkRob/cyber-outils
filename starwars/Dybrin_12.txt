'''Dybrin 12''' was a [[Deep Core]] [[moon]] in the [[Dybrin system]] with an extremely [[Temperature|hot]] climate and a [[poison]]ous, highly pressurized [[atmosphere]]. It was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Dybrinthe]] [[species]] and a type of [[Unidentified atmospheric glider|atmospheric glider]], the latter of which could be modified into [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[dybbuk]] silencers. The Dybrinthe [[bounty hunter]] [[Athgar Heece]] [[Homeworld|hailed]] from Dybrin 12.

==Description==
The [[moon]] Dybrin 12 was situated in the [[Deep Core]]<ref name="AOTG" /> and [[the Interior]]<ref name="Interior" /> and was located in the [[Dybrin system]]. It lay in grid square L-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Dybrin 12 shared its name with [[Dybrin]], a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> in the same [[star system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In spite of its blisteringly [[Temperature|hot]] climate and [[poison]]ous, highly pressurized [[atmosphere]], it was [[Homeworld|home]] to living organisms, including the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Dybrinthe]] [[species]]<ref name="AOTG" /> and a type of [[Unidentified atmospheric glider|atmospheric glider]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

==History==
[[File:Athgar_Heece_CT.png|thumb|180px|Athagar Heece hailed from Dybrin 12.]]
Using [[Mobile refinery-city|mobile refinery-cities]], the Dybrinthe clans exploited the natural [[fuel]] that bubbled up from the moon's extensive subterranean [[ocean]]s. Since most offworlders could not survive on Dybrin 12, visitors were usually confined to [[space station]]s [[orbit]]ing the moon.<ref name="AOTG" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], the company [[Chiewab Amalgamated Pharmaceuticals|Chiewab Pharmaceuticals]] modified Dybrin 12's atmospheric gliders into [[dybbuk]] silencers, a type of [[Parasite|parasitic]] life form.<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

The Dybrinthe [[bounty hunter]] [[Athgar Heece]],<ref name="AOTG" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[0 ABY]]<ref name="BirthDateAthgar">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]]. As {{GEAttr|Star Wars: Datapad}} states that [[Athgar Heece]] was thirty-four [[Standard year|years]] old during the themed land's events, he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[0 ABY]].</ref> and remained active by the time of the [[New Republic Era]], was a native of Dybrin 12.<ref name="AOTG" /> Dybrin 12 was mentioned in an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]], which was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dybrin 12 was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a [[2015]] reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The moon was first identified by name in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="AOTG" /> and released on [[August 23]], [[2016]].<ref name="Studio Fun">{{StudioFun|url=books/star-wars-aliens-of-the-galaxy-9780794437312|text=Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy}}</ref> In the English edition of [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 23|twenty-third issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine,<ref name="HC23">{{HelmetCollectionCite|23|Databank A-Z}}</ref> published on [[November 16]], 2016<ref name="Date">{{HCdates|23}}</ref> and written by [[Iain Lowson]],<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|creator=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> the homeworld of Athgar Heece was described as a planet instead of a moon.<ref name="HC23" /> This article assumes ''Aliens of the Galaxy'', which directly refers to Dybrin 12, is correct.
