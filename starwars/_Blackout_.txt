"'''Blackout'''" was the callsign<ref name="X-Wing" /> of a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> test [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] for the [[First Order]]'s [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter]]s during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. At some point, Blackout was stationed on a world with bodies of water and volcanic areas, proceeding to skim the water's surface while firing the craft's [[laser cannon]]s. At this time, they were considered ill-fated.<ref name="X-Wing" />
