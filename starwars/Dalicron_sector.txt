{{Quote|Nevarro will become the first truly independent trade anchor in this entire sector.|Greef Karga to Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 17" />}}
The '''Dalicron sector''' was a largely unexplored [[sector]]<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[planet]]s [[Mydasos]]<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> and [[Nevarro]].<ref name="Encyclopedia2024">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Though Nevarro had been a center for the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]] and its operations,<ref name="Chapter 3" /> [[Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] and [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] worked to "clean up" Nevarro to make it respectable. Karga hoped it could become an anchor for the sector's trading operations once an [[Nevarro Imperial base|Imperial base]] was [[Second skirmish on Nevarro|destroyed]]. This base, which had been created during the [[Imperial expansion]], was destroyed with the help of [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="Chapter 12" /> Nevarro later became an independent trade anchor and Outer Rim [[Hyperspace route|Hyperlane]] port of the [[Hydian Way]]. [[Belters]] were mining the [[Nevarro system asteroid fields|asteroid fields]] at the edge of [[Nevarro system|Nevarro's system]].<ref name="Chapter 17" />
