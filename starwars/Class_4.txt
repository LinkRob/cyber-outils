'''Class 4''' was a literature rating within the [[Galactic Empire]] that could be assigned to [[book]]s that contained objectionable content. The [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] [[agent]] [[Paramiah Shrove]] classified the book that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' as a Class 4, as it contained notes and journal entries from a variety of criminals.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

Shrove confiscated the book and the [[spacer]]'s strongbox it was contained within, but the box was ultimately returned to its owner on orders from [[Chief Imperial Inspector]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Hedge Devlin]], who was interested in the confiscation of [[spice]] rather than questionable literature.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Class 4 was first mentioned on a supplemental item included in the deluxe edition of the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
