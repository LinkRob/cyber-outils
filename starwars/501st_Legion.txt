The '''501st Legion''', initially created as the '''501st Clone Battalion''' under the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and later joining the [[Imperial Army]] as the '''501st Imperial Legion''', the '''501st Stormtrooper Legion'''&mdash;and nicknamed "'''Vader's Fist'''"&mdash;was an elite [[stormtrooper]] [[legion]] under the command of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. The legion predated the [[Galactic Empire]], with its origins dating back to the [[Clone Wars]] of the late [[Republic Era]], when the 501st was an elite [[Republic Military|military]] [[battalion]]-turned-legion of [[clone trooper]]s in the Grand [[Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Under the leadership of [[Jedi General]] Anakin Skywalker, [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], and [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]], the 501st fought against the [[Separatist Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Throughout the conflict, the [[soldier]]s of the 501st were known for their courage, unconventional tactics, and loyalty to the Republic.

The 501st saw action on multiple battlefronts across [[the galaxy]], becoming part of several [[Timeline of galactic history|historic]] engagements such as the [[Battle of Christophsis]], the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], and the [[Battle of Umbara]]. During the campaign on [[Umbara]], Skywalker was recalled to the Republic [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]], leaving [[General]] [[Pong Krell]] to assume command of the legion in the interim. Unbeknownst to the [[Cloning|clones]] who served him, Krell turned to the [[dark side of the Force]], and having foreseen the rise of a [[New Order]], he worked in secret to undermine the Republic. The 501st mutinied against Krell when his [[Treason|betrayal]] was revealed. They sustained multiple casualties while confronting their [[commanding officer]] but ultimately succeeded in [[Prisoner|capturing]] Krell, who was [[Death penalty|executed]] while in [[Prison|military custody]].

An incident within the 501st nearly exposed a [[Sith]] conspiracy against the [[Jedi Order]]. During the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]], 501st trooper [[Tup]] betrayed and killed General [[Tiplar]], resulting in an investigation that revealed a hidden [[behavioral modification biochip]] within the [[brain]] of every clone [[trooper]]. An [[Advanced Recon Commando]] attached to the 501st, [[Fives]], investigated further into the matter and learned that the chips were designed to force the clones to kill their [[Jedi]] allies. Alarmed by this discovery, he revealed his findings to Skywalker and Rex, but was unable to convince either of them before his [[death]] by the [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]] of the [[Coruscant Guard]].

In the last [[Standard day|days]] of the Clone Wars, Skywalker divided the 501st by creating the [[332nd Division]] and dispatching it, including the [[332nd Company]], to [[Mandalore]]. Led by [[Commander]] Rex and former Commander Tano, the [[division]] defeated the [[Mandalorian super commando]]s and captured their leader, the former [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul]]. During the journey to Coruscant, however, the [[Great Jedi Purge]] commenced when [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]&mdash;the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious&mdash;declared that [[Order 66]] was in effect, turning the clone troopers against their Jedi [[officer]]s. Although the 501st [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|failed]] to terminate Tano, the other half of the legion stormed the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] and killed its inhabitants, including [[Padawan]]s and [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]], under the leadership of the newly christened Sith [[Lord]] Darth Vader under [[Operation: Knightfall]]. Rex deserted the 501st after defending Tano against his own [[troop]]s, including [[Jesse]], all of whom died attempting to carry out Order 66. On Coruscant, the 501st was defeated by [[Jedi Master]]s [[Yoda]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] who infiltrated the [[Jedi temple|Temple]], while Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the Galactic Empire.

The 501st was absorbed into the [[Imperial Military]], becoming a legion of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]], which over time transitioned to the use of [[human]] recruits after the Empire suspended the cloning operation on [[Kamino]]. Although the [[1st Legion]] replaced the 501st as Vader's personal legion, the 501st also served under the Dark Lord's command, even being personally led by him during events like the [[Secret mission to Tatooine|capture]] of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. At the height of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the 501st participated in the [[Battle of Hoth]], where the Empire sought to destroy the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Ultimately, the legion's last action was at the [[Battle of Endor]], and it was fragmented together with the death of the [[Emperor]] and the defeat of the Empire.

==Description==
===Republic service===
====Command====
{{Quote|Master Krell, this is Rex, my first in command. You won't find a finer or more loyal trooper anywhere.|Anakin Skywalker, to Pong Krell|<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" />}}
[[File:Leaders-of-the-501st.png|thumb|left|300px|For most of the Clone Wars, the 501st Legion was led by Jedi General Anakin Skywalker, Jedi Commander Ahsoka Tano, and Clone Captain Rex, who served as their first in command.]]
The 501st [[Legion]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> originally operating<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> as the 501st [[Cloning|Clone]] [[Battalion]],<ref name="Lost Commanders" /> was an elite group<ref name="HC 16">{{HelmetCollectionCite|16|Helmets}}</ref> within the [[Republic Military]]. Structured as a battalion and later as a legion,<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> the 501st was composed of elite [[clone trooper]]s under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The legion was briefly and tentatively led by [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Pong Krell]] during the [[Battle of Umbara]], an instance where Skywalker was pulled away from the frontline by the [[Jedi High Council]] at the request of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] himself. [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] served as Skywalker's first-in-command.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> Despite only holding the title of [[captain]], Rex commanded the entirety of the 501st<ref name="Join the Battle" /> as its leader,<ref name="501st Legion DB">{{Databank|501st-legion|501st Legion}}</ref> with only Jedi [[officer]]s standing above the captain.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /><ref name="Duel of the Droids">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref>

At the time of the Battle of [[Umbara]], at least one [[Clone Commander]] was present within the legion<ref name="Umbara Legion">{{Legion|set=Republic Specialists Personnel Expansion|cardname=Clone Commander}}</ref> but did not take away from Rex's status.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> At the end of the Clone Wars, Rex was promoted to Clone Commander himself when the 501st Legion divided into two groups. Rex took command of the [[332nd Division]],<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> while the other half of the 501st was placed under [[Appo]],<ref name="Join the Battle" /> a [[Clone Sergeant]] within the legion who was promoted to Clone Commander upon the execution of [[Order 66]]<ref name="HC 16" /> with Rex offworld with the 332nd.<ref name="Victory and Death" /> Other officers in the 501st included Clone [[Captain]] [[Vaughn]],<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Jesse]],<ref name="Victory and Death" /> Clone [[Lieutenant]] [[Hawk]],<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" /><ref name="TCW film" /> Clone [[Sergeant]] [[Fox (501st Legion)|Fox]], and [[Unidentified clone sergeant|another clone sergeant]].<ref name="TCW film" />

====Organization====
{{Quote|I'll divide the 501st, make a new division under Ahsoka's command.''"<br />"''Unfortunately, Ahsoka is no longer a part of the Grand Army of the Republic.''"<br />"''We'll promote Rex to Commander and have him lead the new division. Ahsoka can go with him as an adviser.|Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi|audio=RexPromotion-OFNF.flac|<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" />}}
[[File:501st-Databank.png|thumb|right|250px|The 501st was an elite clone trooper legion of the Grand Army of the Republic.]]
Although originally a battalion, the 501st was reorganized as a legion.<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> As such, the 501st consisted of various subunits. When the unit was divided into two groups at the end of its service to the [[Galactic Republic]], one group was the 332nd Division.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> The 501st additionally contained at least three [[brigade]]s, to which [[Carnivore Battalion]] and [[Execute Battalion]] belonged to,<ref name="Cargo of Doom" /> as well as a [[Anakin's battalion|battalion]] on [[Umbara]],<ref name="The Clone Wars">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Clone Wars}}</ref> each containing a standard 576 [[trooper]]s.<ref name="Battalion Note">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that one [[company]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] consists of 144 [[clone trooper]]s. Since ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'' establishes that four companies made up one battalion, it can be deduced that a battalion contains a standard number of 576 [[trooper]]s.</ref> There was also an [[Unidentified tank brigade|armored brigade]] deployed under the 501st.<ref name="Battles" /> Many [[Company|companies]] made up the legion, including the [[332nd Company]]<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> and [[Torrent Company]].<ref name="Databank Rex" />

[[Platoon]]s in the legion included a [[Unidentified platoon (332nd Company)|platoon]] within the 332nd Company,<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> [[Rex's platoon|another platoon]] under the direct command of Captain Rex,<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> as well as [[Unidentified platoon (Galactic Republic)|another platoon]] that operated in cold-weather operations and included four [[squad]]s, including [[first squad]].<ref name="Trespass" /> Other squads within the legion included a [[Rex's squad|squad]] directly led by Rex,<ref name="Plan of Dissent" /> and a [[Skywalker's squad|squad]] led by Skywalker.<ref name="TCW film" /> Other units in the legion included [[Skywalker's team]],<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> [[Calm Tree]], [[Snow Wolves]], and [[Wildfire]].<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus" /> Additionally, the 501st had its own [[starship]] group.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" />

====Culture and training====
{{Quote|A few of General Skywalker's plans seemed reckless too, but they worked.''"<br />"''Yeah, but General Skywalker is usually leading his men up in the front, not bringing up the rear like General Krell.|Rex and Fives|<ref name="The General">{{TCW|The General (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Rex_Yerbana.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The 501st had a reputation for courage and unconventional warfare.]]
Renowned for their bravery, unconventional tactics,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and unwavering loyalty, the 501st Legion was a tight-knit [[squadron]]<ref name="501st Legion DB" /> that participated in [[Timeline of galactic history|historic]] conflicts from the [[Battle of Christophsis]] to the [[Siege of Mandalore]] and often served alongside Jedi General [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The 501st won numerous battles, usually against seemingly impossible odds. Many, including Kenobi, hypothesized that Skywalker's unorthodox approach influenced the members of his unit. They were unflinchingly fierce in combat, and did not demonstrate mercy. The 501st were initially trained with basic combat tactics, but were not trained for Separatist forces at the onset of their service.<ref name="HC 16" /> A regime of strict training improved their performance.<ref name="Imperial Troops" />

====Equipment and vehicles====
{{Quote|Generation one armor always holds up.|Rex, on his clone trooper armor|<ref name="The Last Battle">{{Rebels|The Last Battle}}</ref>}}
[[File:501stARFATRT-SS.png|thumb|right|200px|An ARF trooper on AT-RT painted in the blue markings of the 501st Legion]]
The 501st Legion used [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s,<ref name="TCW film" /> [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s, and other vehicles.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> During the [[Battle of Horain]], the 501st Legion stole a [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]].<ref name="Roger Roger">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=19|story=Roger Roger}}</ref> The 501st Legion utilized the [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] during the early period of the Clone Wars, from the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="HC 16" /> until the introduction of the [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II]] model by the time of the [[Battle of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> On certain occasions, Rex and other members of the 501st made use of [[jetpack]]s for aerial transportation and greater mobility.<ref name="TCW film" />

By the time of the [[Battle of Arantara]], Rex wore his [[armor]] with [[Color|blue]] markings, a command [[pauldron]], and a [[kama]].<ref name="501 Plus One" /> By the time of [[Christophsis]], Rex was not the only clone trooper in the legion with blue markings, as several others possessed them as well. A clone sergeant had blue markings around this time.<ref name="TCW film" /> Over time more troopers of the 501st came to customize their own kit, also using blue as a complementary color to the standard white.<ref name="Children of the Force" /> The distinctive blue color and markings clearly identified them as one of the army's most famous units.<ref name="HC 16" />

As the war continued, Phase II [[clone trooper armor]] became widespread within the ranks of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], supplanting the Phase I model. The 501st welcomed the new armor, and quickly added their distinct blue markings.<ref name="HC 16" /> Nevertheless, some clones such as Rex, the [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s and even common [[soldier]]s retained their own unique designs that differed from the rest of the 501st.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> As an elite unit, the 501st understood that communication was vital to coordinate actions during combat, and so used their Phase II helmet's built-in [[comlink]] on the battlefield.<ref name="HC 16" /> Ahsoka Tano's [[Togruta]] markings inspired the redesigned [[helmet]]s worn by the 501st clones who remained loyal to their former commander.<ref name="501st Legion DB" />

====Troopers====
{{Quote|You're exactly the kind of men I need in the 501st.|Rex, to clone troopers Echo and Fives|<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:501-battleforce-textless.png|thumb|left|300px|Officers, battlefield medics, and other specialists served alongside standard soldiers in the ranks of the 501st Legion.]]
As a legion of the Grand [[Army]] of the Republic, the 501st Legion consisted of various specializations of clone troopers. Beside the standard clone trooper, the 501st deployed [[clone trooper pilot]]s via its starship and ground vehicle groups, as well as [[clone gunner]]s, and various [[clone trooper officer]]s with a ranging degree of customized armor and equipment.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> The 501st had a range of specialized clone troopers that served in operational capacities, including [[Clone Comms Technician]]s who were responsible for proper communication, and [[Clone Engineer]]s who were tasked with confirming that Republic vehicles were operational.<ref name="Republic Specialists">{{Legion|set=Republic Specialists Personnel Expansion}}</ref>

Elite [[special forces]] clone trooper variants were also attached to the elite 501st Legion, including several Advanced Recon [[Commando]]s for special operations<ref name="Databank Fives" /> and general deployment,<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> many [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]] for reconnaissance and light vehicle support,<ref name="Plan of Dissent" /> and [[clone commando]]s.<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> The 501st was also outfitted with other types of specialized troopers for different combat scenarios, including many [[clone jetpack trooper]]s for aerial combat,<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> [[clone sharpshooter]]s for [[Sniper|sniping]] and marksman action, [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s for heavy infantry support,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[clone medic]]s for medical aid and assistance,<ref name="Plan of Dissent" /> and [[clone cold assault trooper]]s for cold weather operations.<ref name="Trespass" />

===Imperial service===
====Command and organization====
[[File:Stormswarm.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The 501st transitioned to using stormtrooper armor during their service to the Galactic Empire.]]
Under service to the [[Galactic Empire]], the 501st Legion was led by [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Vader, previously the [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker, as his personal legion of [[troop]]s.<ref name="HC 16" /> Officers who served the legion included General [[Maximilian Veers]],<ref name="HC 29" /> [[Brigadier General]] [[Nevar]], [[Colonel]] [[Starck]],<ref name="CL 2016" /> Commander [[Daine Jir]], Commander [[Praji]],<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" /> [[Stormtrooper Commander]] [[TD-4445]],<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> Stormtrooper Commander [[TK-9091]], [[commanding officer]] [[Tarvyn Lareka|TK-4601]],<ref name="The Bucket" /> Captain [[Wanten]],<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> Captain [[TS-4068]],<ref name="Truest Duty" /> [[Unidentified snowtrooper captain|another captain]],<ref name="Invasion of Echo Base">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=1|story=Tales of Villainy: Invasion of Echo Base}}</ref> and Sergeant [[Kreel]].<ref name="Star Wars 12" />

The legion could be divided down to squads, which could be further divided into fireteams.<ref name="The Witness" /> [[Blizzard Force]], a unit consisting of [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-ATs]] and [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] was part of the 501st Legion, with Blizzard Force taking their name for their aggressive tactics, striking their targets hard and fast. Blizzard Force used a variety of ground vehicles, including the AT-ATs and [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-STs]]. Blizzard Force troops were not subtly, and were infamous for not taking [[prisoner]]s unless specifically ordered to do so. Members of Blizzard Force were within their own right one of the most feared units in the Imperial Army.<ref name="HC 29" /> Blizzard Force's AT-AT [[squadron]] was the [[Thundering Herd]],<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and was additionally made up of other units such as [[Blizzard Force unit two-two-eight-seven|unit two-two-eight-seven]].<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> The 501st additionally maintained a standing [[Unidentified sandtrooper regiment|sandtrooper regiment]], which included multiple units such as [[Foot Patrol 7]].<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion" /> [[Task Force 99]], a [[Special Commando Advanced Recon Trooper]] squad, was attached to the elite 501st, which at the time continued answering to the highest levels of [[Imperial High Command]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

With the end of the [[Republic Era]], the 501st transitioned from clone troopers to [[stormtrooper]]s<ref name="Databank Stormtrooper">{{Databank|stormtroopers|Stormtroopers}}</ref> and continued to utilize several variant units, including the elite [[storm commando]]s<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> and snowtroopers.<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> With the Republic's transformation into the Empire,<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> the Kaminoan cloning production was shut down<ref name="Dark Lord 2">[[Darth Vader (2017) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 2]]</ref> and the [[Imperial Military]] replaced clones with [[human]] conscripts.<ref name="Databank Stormtrooper" /> Such changes applied to the 501st as well. By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> human<ref name="ECUC">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'' establishes that the [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] were all [[human]]s.</ref> soldiers such as [[TK-7624]], [[TK-8332]], and Commander TK-9091 served in the 501st.<ref name="The Bucket" />

====Equipment and vehicles====
After the 501st was absorbed by the Imperial Military, becoming a division of the [[Stormtrooper Corps]],<ref name="Databank Stormtrooper" /> its members were equipped with [[stormtrooper armor]] along with the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]] as their primary weapon.<ref name="The Bucket" /> The legion also made use of terrain-based equipment utilized by cold weather assault stormtroopers during the mission on [[Hoth]].<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" />

[[File:Republic_Assault.png|thumb|left|250px|The 501st Legion served in the Battle of Geonosis, the first battle of the Clone Wars.]]
At the beginning of the pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Episode II" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the Galactic Republic [[Military Creation Act|mobilized]] an army of clone troopers to fight the [[Separatist Droid Army|armies]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], ending a [[millennium]] of peace.<ref name="Episode II" /> These soldiers, who were cloned from the [[DNA|genetic source]] [[Blood|material]] of the human [[Clone template|template]] [[Jango Fett]], formed the backbone of the Grand Army of the Republic,<ref name="Databank Clone Troopers">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> which was divided into various [[corps]], battalions, [[regiment]]s, platoons, and other units.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

One such unit within the Grand Army of the Republic was the<ref name="HC 16" /> 501st Clone Battalion,<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> which began as an elite group of clone troopers and participated in the first action that sparked the Clone Wars, the First Battle of [[Geonosis]]. Although they were not yet assigned to the Jedi Anakin Skywalker, as he was in pursuit of Separatist [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the 501st was brought from its [[homeworld]] of [[Kamino]] to Geonosis by [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], and battled the Separatist forces. Despite being well trained in basic combat tactics, members of the 501st had no special training to combat Separatist forces, though fortunately for the Republic troops, the Separatists were fighting a rear-guard action in defense of their [[transport]]s which were trying to escape into [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="HC 16" />

[[File:MissionToBenglor-TCWBattleTales1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Command of the 501st was given to Jedi General Anakin Skywalker whose first officer was Clone Captain Rex.]]
Despite the circumstances, 501st Clone Battalion troopers fought against the latest [[droid]] forces, included [[B2-series super battle droid]]s that were so fresh from the production line that they were not painted.<ref name="HC 16" /> Led by Clone Captain Rex, he and his clone troopers fought well at Geonosis, which earned them veteran status within the Grand Army.<ref name="Captain Rex Biography Gallery Image 1">{{Databank|clone-captain-rex-biography-gallery|Clone Captain Rex Biography Gallery}} {{C|Image 1 caption}}</ref> Veterans of the battle earned critical frontline experience, which were passed down to rookie troopers meant to replace the casualties incurred by the unit. Additionally in the aftermath, the 501st was reorganized<ref name="HC 16" /> and sponsored by Republic Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine, eventually turning it into the 501st Legion,<ref name="Imperial Troops" /> and placed under the command of Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="HC 16" />

With Skywalker's promotion to Jedi Knight and receiving the military rank of Jedi General, Skywalker led the legion<ref name="TCW film" /> with Captain Rex serving as his first-in-command.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> The 501st Legion was deployed to numerous battlefronts as war swept across [[the galaxy]], and more Jedi began serving the Republic Military.<ref name="TCW film" /> During the war, Skywalker and the 501st attacked a number of droid factories.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

====Early missions and Hisseen====
One of the unit's earlier missions was the Battle of Arantara, during which 501st Clone Captain Rex was incapacitated. Once Rex had woken up, General Skywalker spoke to him and taught him that a true leader always led from the front.<ref name="501 Plus One">{{StoryCite|story=501 Plus One|book=Age of Republic Special 1}}</ref> Another early mission was a [[mission to Benglor]] in order to investigate Separatist activity on the [[planet]]. After discovering traces of Separatist [[battle droid]]s, members of the 501st were attacked by an [[Unidentified Benglor creature|indigenous creatuore]], resulting in the [[death]] of 501st clone trooper [[Bellow]].<ref name="Battle Tales 1">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 1|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 1]]</ref>

[[File:ClonesOnHisseen-TCWBattleTales1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Captain Rex's 501st often served alongside Clone Commander Cody and the 212th Attack Battalion.]]
Subsequently, the 501st Legion participated in the Separatist [[siege of Hisseen]] alongside the [[7th Sky Corps]] under the command of [[High Jedi General]] Obi-Wan Kenobi and [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]], and the [[104th Battalion]] led by Jedi General [[Plo Koon]] and Clone Commander [[Wolffe]]. After securing a foothold on the planet [[Hisseen]], clone troopers from the three units protected the [[Hisseenian]]s against waves of battle droids, until the three Jedi arrived and defeated the surviving droid forces. The Jedi were soon forced to leave their troops to defend the ground while they moved to engage Separatist leader Count Dooku and his apprentice [[Asajj Ventress]]. At that time, more droid forces bore down on the clone troopers,<ref name="Battle Tales 1" /> and members of the 104th and 7th were dispatched to rescue members of the [[Hisseen's parliament|Hisseenian parliament]] while the 501st held the frontline.<ref name="Battle Tales 2">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 2|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 2]]</ref>

Shortly after rescuing the parliamentary members, Rex contacted Cody requesting reinforcements,<ref name="Battle Tales 3">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 3|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 3]]</ref> as the droid attack intensified. As the 501st troopers sustained higher casualties and were being overrun, Commanders Cody and Wolffe returned with their units to support the troopers.<ref name="Battle Tales 4">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 4|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 4]]</ref> After several explosions, the clone units were disoriented and captured by the droid forces, who had overrun the Republic position, but were rescued when the Jedi Generals returned. With Separatist forces in retreat from Hisseen, Republic forces, including the 501st Legion, were recalled to the Republic [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Battle Tales 5">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 5|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 5]]</ref>

====Battle of Christophsis====
{{Quote|What's our plan of attack, sir?''"<br />"''Follow me.|Rex and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="TCW film" />}}
[[File:Anakin_and_Rex_on_Christophsis.png|thumb|right|250px|The 501st during the Republic campaign on Christophsis.]]
Skywalker and Rex led the 501st's Torrent Company in the campaign to retake the planet of Christophsis. During the battle, the 501st was joined by Skywalker's new apprentice, Ahsoka Tano, who claimed the authority of Jedi Commander through her status as a [[Padawan]] in the Order. Upon defeating the Separatists with the help of the 212th Attack Battalion, the 501st was redirected to the planet [[Teth]] with orders to rescue [[Rotta]], son of the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. They sustained heavy losses against the droid army but ultimately prevailed in the [[Battle of Teth]] with the support of General Obi-Wan Kenobi and the 212th.<ref name="TCW film" />

Afterwards, the 501st Legion participated in the [[Battle of Mimban]] alongside the [[224th Imperial Armored Division|224th Clone Division's]] [[Mud Jumpers]]. The 501st and 224th fought against a large force of Separatist droid forces, and following the death of Jedi General [[Laan Tik]] and after receiving heavy fire, Captain Rex ordered the Republic forces to retreat to their military outpost. There, Captain Rex and his subordinates reviewed and discussed the situation, with Captain Rex deciding to pull his forces out while he destroyed the Separatist [[deflector shield generator]] alone. [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]], who accompanied Republic troops, used General Tik's [[lightsaber]] to aid Captain Rex after Rex was injured and defeated. The two then fought ahead to the shield generator.<ref name="501 Plus One" />

[[File:RexPromotesRookies-R.png|thumb|left|250px|Rookies Echo and Fives transferred to the 501st.]]
Skywalker's legion continued to serve the Republic over the course of the war, completing a variety of assignments and gaining new troopers [[Echo]] and [[Fives]], who were deemed worthy of serving in the ranks of the elite 501st by Rex.<ref name="Rookies" />

As Skywalker often worked alongside his [[Jedi Master|mentor]] Kenobi, the 501st also served with the 212th on several missions, including the [[Skirmish aboard the Coronet|voyage]] of [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] from [[Mandalore]] to Coruscant.<ref name="Voyage of Temptation">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref> The [[Death Watch]], a [[terrorist]] organization opposed to Kryze's [[New Mandalorians|government]],<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref> failed to [[assassin]]ate the duchess before she could reach the galactic capital due to the combined efforts of the 501st and 212th.<ref name="Voyage of Temptation" /> Later, a detachment of the 501st was sent to [[Naboo]] along with a squad of [[clone ordnance specialist]]s where they ultimately [[Raid on Nuvo Vindi's laboratory|prevented]] [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]]'s plan to attack the population through [[Blue Shadow Virus|biological warfare]].<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus" /><ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons">{{TCW|Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref>

Units of the 501st under Skywalker, Tano, and Captain Rex participated in the [[First Battle of Felucia]] alongside Kenobi and Commander Cody. Outnumbered and outgunned, Republic forces were pinned down into a pocket with only their AT-TE walkers providing cover and support. While General Plo Koon and his forces attempted to break through the Separatist [[Felucia blockade]], Tano led a jungle patrol from a [[HAVw A6 Juggernaut]] escorted by a pair of AT-TE walkers. After Koon's forces broke through, Republic gunships evacuated Republic forces from the AT-TEs, and then moved to evacuate Tano and her patrol group.<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref>

Following the [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|theft]] of a [[Jedi holocron|holocron]] from the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on Coruscant, the 501st's Carnivore and Execute Battalions were sent on a [[Battle of Devaron|mission]] to [[Devaron]] in order to retrieve the [[Holocron|artifact]] from [[Cad Bane]].<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref> In the ensuing battle between the Republic and [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]], the 501st forces boarded a Separatist frigate but failed in securing the holocron. The bounty hunter was presumed to have died in the battle, but in fact, survived by disguising himself with the clone armor of the 501st trooper [[Denal]] and escaping with the holocron intact.<ref name="Children of the Force">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref>

====Battles of Kamino and Lola Sayu====
{{Quote|Echo, Fives, you're both officially being made ARC troopers.|Cody, to Echo and Fives|<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:EchoFivesSniperPosition-ARCT.png|thumb|right|250px|The 501st defended the clone hatcheries in the Battle of Kamino.]]
When the Separatists plotted to deal the Republic a crippling blow by attacking the cloning facilities of Kamino, the 501st and 212th rushed to the defense of their homeworld<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> and to reinforce the local [[Kamino garrison|garrison]].<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> The [[Battle of Kamino]] was a hard-fought victory for the Republic, due in part to the actions of the 501st. In particular, the troopers Echo and Fives were recognized for their service by Captain Rex, as well as Commander Cody of the 212th, and were both promoted to the elite status of ARC trooper in the aftermath of the battle.<ref name="ARC Troopers" /> Afterward, Rex and the new ARC troopers of the 501st were sent with Skywalker and Kenobi to [[Battle of Lola Sayu|infiltrate]] the [[Citadel]].<ref name="The Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref> Though the Republic succeeded in reacquiring the information pertaining to the [[Nexus Route]] from General [[Even Piell]] and his captain, [[Wilhuff Tarkin]],<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref> the group lost Echo, who survived but was presumed dead,<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> in addition to several other casualties.<ref name="Citadel Rescue" />

====Battles of Felucia and Horain====
[[File:501stCloneTrooper-SWCT.png|thumb|left|250px|The troopers of the 501st adopted the Grand Army's new Phase II armor.]]
Following the Battle of Lola Sayu, the 501st Legion was dispatched to [[Felucia]] once again alongside the 104th Battalion and several clone tank divisions, ultimately becoming involved in the [[Second Battle of Felucia]].<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> After this time, the implementation of Phase II clone trooper armor to replace the standard Phase I took place, with the 501st Legion adopting the armor throughout its ranks. The 501st's Torrent Company was then eventually dispatched to [[Horain]] to combat the Separatist [[Horain garrison|garrison]] stationed there and participated in the [[Battle of Horain]]. During the battle, Captain Rex and members of Torrent Company encountered a group of [[Horian]] children in a crater, and after helping them escape, Rex became separated from the rest of his men, and was pinned down by a large contingent of Separatist forces. After attempting to contact General Skywalker, Captain Rex was saved by General Kenobi. In the following skirmish, Separatist droid [[B1-0516]] received damage to its central processing unit and began aiding Rex and Kenobi. After the droid was captured, Rex and Kenobi rescued the droid from the Separatist headquarters, and managed to reunite with Skywalker and the rest of Torrent Company at the [[Republic Evacuation Point]].<ref name="Roger Roger">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=19|story=Roger Roger}}</ref>

====Battle of Umbara====
{{Quote|Remember, General Krell, the entire invasion depends on your battalion.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, to Pong Krell|<ref name="The General" />}}
[[File:Standoff-CM.png|thumb|right|250px|The 501st mutinied against Jedi General Pong Krell during the Battle of Umbara.]]
A [[Anakin's battalion|battalion]] of the 501st was among with the clone formations that the Republic deployed to reconquer Umbara from the Separatist Alliance. During the campaign, Skywalker was recalled to Coruscant by the Jedi High Council at the request of Chancellor Palpatine, leaving the 501st under the command of Jedi General Pong Krell.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" /> Although he was a recognized war hero with a distinguished record of service,<ref name="The General" /> Krell became a Separatist [[spy]] after turning to the [[dark side of the Force]]. He deliberately sabotaged his own strategies to undermine the clones, whom he regarded with contempt and disgust.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

Krell's [[Treason|betrayal]] was revealed after he tricked the 501st and the 212th into fighting each other, causing clones on both sides to kill each other by mistake. Under the leadership of Captain Rex, the battalions mutinied against Krell, but the fallen Jedi slaughtered many clones. However, he was ultimately captured while attempting to escape. In response to his actions, Krell was summarily executed by his most loyal soldier, [[Dogma]], who was subsequently taken into Republic custody for killing an officer, but Rex sent Dogma a reassuring nod when he was taken away. Afterward, Fives informed the Clone Captain that Umbara had finally been conquered, but Rex no longer knew what the point of the war was and wondered what would happen to them when it ended.<ref name="Carnage of Krell" />

====Conspiracy====
{{Quote|Well, there's a sinister plot in the works against the Jedi! I have proof of it, I can prove that everything I know is true beyond the shadow of a doubt!''"<br />"''Show me the evidence!''"<br />"''The evidence is in here, it's all in here, every one of us, every clone!|Fives and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:GoodbyeFives-O.png|thumb|left|250px|Fives was killed while trying to expose a Sith conspiracy against the Jedi Order and the Galactic Republic.]]
During the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]], the 501st was forced to retreat from the Separatists due to an unexpected development: [[Tup]] killed Jedi General [[Tiplar]]. Suffering from a malfunction in his [[Behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chip]], the trooper became hostile towards the Jedi, which in turn led to a premature execution of [[Order 66]]<ref name="The Unknown" />&mdash;a clone protocol designed to turn the clone army against their Jedi officers in the event of a Jedi rebellion. This turn of events led to an investigation on Kamino but later moved to Coruscant at Chancellor Palpatine's request.<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref>

By then, ARC trooper Fives had partially uncovered the nature of the inhibitor chips and, believing they were a threat to the security of the Grand Army, requested their immediate removal from the [[brain]]s of every clone trooper. However, the Chancellor revealed his knowledge of the chips and their true purpose to Fives, causing the clone to become a fugitive after attempting to assassinate Palpatine. The rogue clone made an effort to expose Palpatine's conspiracy to Rex and Skywalker, but Fives was killed by Commander [[Fox]] of the [[Coruscant Guard]] before he could prove his claims. He died in Rex's arms, telling his superior officer that he had only wanted to do his duty and that his nightmares were finally over.<ref name="Orders" />

====Final battles====
The 501st Legion participated in the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], including the [[Battle of Anaxes]].<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref> It also participated in the [[Fourth Battle of Mygeeto]]<ref name="Mustafar SWE">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> and the [[Battle of Yerbana]]. After that engagement, the 501st split into two forces in order to participate in the [[Battle of Coruscant]] and the [[Siege of Mandalore]].<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> Elements of the 501st Legion participated alongside the 41st Elite Corps and the [[Wookiee Army]]'s [[Kachirho Wookiee Militia]] in the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]] in order to defend [[Kachirho]] [[City]] and prevent further landings by Separatist forces in their attempt to capture the vital trade and communications hub.<ref name="HC 27">{{HelmetCollectionCite|27|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref>

====Great Jedi Purge====
{{Quote|There's been a rebellion, sir. Don't worry. The situation is under control. I'm sorry, sir. It's time for you to leave.|Appo, to Bail Organa|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:332ndCompanyTrooper-Sideshow.png|thumb|right|200px|A company of troopers' helmets were customized to resemble Ahsoka Tano's face.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the 501st and 212th served in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] Sieges, including the Battle of [[Yerbana]]. After this battle, Ahsoka Tano,<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> who had left the Jedi Order after being framed in a [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]],<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> reunited with Skywalker and the legion, as she and [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] of the [[Mandalore resistance]] had located the rogue [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] in the [[Mandalorian]] capital of [[Sundari]]. While Skywalker was ultimately willing to fulfill their wish for a joint Republic/resistance operation, he and General Kenobi learned that Separatist General [[Grievous]] had attacked Coruscant. Skywalker created a new division of the 501st, the [[332nd Division]], promoting Rex to be its commander. The division was to be sent to Mandalore with Tano and the Mandalorians, while the rest of the legion would go with him and Kenobi to Coruscant.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten" /> The Battle of Coruscant proved to be a Republic victory, with Skywalker even killing Separatist Head of State Count Dooku,<ref name="Episode III" /> and the Siege of Mandalore eventually resulted in the 501st and resistance's victory and Maul's capture.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref>

The conspiracy that Fives discovered<ref name="Orders" /> came to fruition with the mass execution of Order 66, forcing the Grand Army to betray their Jedi leaders across the galaxy. Palpatine, who had orchestrated the Clone Wars as Darth Sidious, Dark Lord of the [[Sith]], utilized the clone army as the instrument of both the fall of the Jedi Order and the Sith's rise to absolute power. Aiding the Dark Lord in the initial phase of the [[Great Jedi Purge]] was Anakin Skywalker, now anointed in the dark side as the Sith Lord Darth Vader.<ref name="Episode III" /> On the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tribunal]]'',<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Rex, his division of the 501st, and a force of Coruscant Guard [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]], received the order while traveling back to Coruscant with Maul in custody. While Tano was no longer part of the Jedi Order, she was targeted by the clones, but Rex managed to resist long enough to tell her to locate ARC trooper Fives. Rex then succumbed to his control chip and led the hunt for his friend. The Commander also ordered his troops to kill Maul, but Tano freed the rogue Sith to serve as a distraction.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref>

[[File:Grogus_Flashback.png|thumb|left|250px|The 501st Legion conducted an assault on the Jedi Grand Temple.]]
On Coruscant, Sidious' new [[Sith apprentice]] was tasked with leading the 501st Legion in a direct siege on the Jedi Temple, codenamed [[Operation: Knightfall]], resulting in the deaths of its inhabitants while thousands of Jedi were killed by their troops across the galaxy. The 501st served under Appo<ref name="Episode III" />&mdash;formerly a Clone Sergeant<ref name="The General" /> that was promoted to the rank of Clone Commander<ref name="Join the Battle" /> by the time of Order 66&mdash;in addition to Vader.<ref name="Episode III" /> A [[Appo's battalion|battalion]]<ref name="Skywalker family">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]''</ref> of 501st troopers marched in columns<ref name="Episode III" /> as a [[garrison]]<ref name="Darth Vader: Sith Lord">''[[Darth Vader: Sith Lord]]''</ref> to the entrance of the Jedi Temple behind Lord Vader<ref name="Episode III" /> in silence, with their inhibitor chip's programming ensuring their obedience. Once inside, the 501st's slaughter began. Lord Vader allowed his troopers to complete their objectives while he killed stragglers attempting to hide, including a class of younglings in the Jedi High Council chambers. During the siege, the 501st worked in squads to bring down their Jedi targets, using their superior weaponry and firepower to overwhelm them. The 501st killed anyone they encountered, demonstrating the same ruthlessness as their Sith leader.<ref name="HC 16" />

[[File:Zett_Jukasa_Order_66.png|thumb|right|250px|Among the Jedi that perished in the Temple was Zett Jukassa, a Padawan who was killed in battle by the 501st Legion.]]
As alarm across the Temple spread, fires began to ignite throughout the building. Witnessing the assault, [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] of [[Alderaan]]<ref name="HC 16" /> travelled to the Jedi Temple in his [[airspeeder]], landing in a platform outside. He was confronted by Commander Appo and a contingent of troops before they were attacked by Jedi Padawan [[Zett Jukassa]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Commander Appo was struck down,<ref name="HC 16" /> and Jukassa was eventually killed by [[Clone Lieutenant]] [[Bow (clone trooper)|Bow]].<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> Senator Organa fled the scene, with the 501st troopers allowing him to escape per the order of [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Fox (501st Legion)|Fox]].<ref name="Episode III" />

Elsewhere in the Temple, the outnumbered Jedi died defending themselves and each other against the clones. Jedi Master [[Minas Velti]] was killed while protecting a group of [[Jedi Initiate]]s, striking down several 501st soldiers including the clone who fatally wounded her. The [[youngling]]s in Velti's care attempted to escape as more Jedi fell against the 501st,<ref name="Part I">{{Kenobi|Part I}}</ref> but their route was blocked by a column of clone troopers led by Darth Vader, whom they recognized as Anakin Skywalker. The younglings had assumed that Skywalker had come to save them; instead, the fallen Jedi Knight proceeded to kill them with [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. The youngling [[Reva Sevander]] survived Vader's attack and hid among the bodies of her friends, feigning death until the 501st departed.<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> The youngling [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] survived as well,<ref name="Chapter 6">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref> after he was rescued by Master [[Kelleran Beq]] when Grogu's Jedi security were killed by 501st troopers. Beq dispatched a number of 501st clones in their escape, including trooper that arrived to the temple in [[BARC speeder]]s.<ref name="Chapter 20">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 20: The Foundling}}</ref>

[[File:JesseDemotesRex-VaD.png|thumb|left|250px|Through Order 66, the 501st was directed by Darth Sidious to execute the former Jedi Ahsoka Tano, turning their guns on Commander Rex as well when he was saved from Order 66.]]
While aboard the ''Tribunal'', Tano looked into Fives and discovered the control chip conspiracy, so she and a team of astromech droids&ndash;[[R7-A7]], [[RG-G1]], and [[CH-33P]]&ndash;knocked out Rex, allowing them to bring him to a medical bay and remove his chip. With Rex freed from Order 66, he, Tano, and the droids made their way to the hangar to look for an escape ship, but the [[Star Destroyer]] was pulled out of [[hyperspace]] when Maul destroyed the [[hyperdrive]], leading to the [[warship]] being caught in a [[Unidentified moon|moon's]] gravitational pull. When Rex, Tano, and the droids made it to [[bay 12]], they discovered the clone troopers, now under the command of ARC Clone Lieutenant Jesse, had predicted their actions. Pretending to have captured Tano, Rex tried to reason with Jesse, stating that she was no longer part of the Jedi Order and thus not a target. When the lieutenant recounted Rex's earlier command that they were under special order to kill Tano and all clones who refused to obey, Rex tried to get through to his friend one last time, but Jesse refused, instead stating Rex would be demoted and subject to execution alongside Tano.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

[[File:JesseMemorial-VaD.png|thumb|right|250px|The clones who tried to terminate Tano were killed in the destruction of their Star Destroyer.]]
However, Rex had managed to stall Jesse and the troopers, as the astromech droids were able to secretly release the hangar elevators. While this managed to separate most of the clones, Maul arrived in the hangar and stole the available [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class shuttle]], forcing Rex and Tano to look below decks, which was where the majority of the clones had been dropped. While their three astromech droid companions had been killed by the time they found a [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]], Tano and Rex escaped the doomed Star Destroyer aboard the [[bomber]]. Jesse, his 501st division, and any other clone troopers aboard the ''Venator'' were killed when it crashed into the [[moon]], but Rex and Tano honored the Clone Lieutenant and other clones by burying them outside the ship's remains.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

Following their siege of the Jedi Temple, the 501st patrolled the grounds around the site,<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=Clone Troopers|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/Clone-Trooper-Four-Pack-Black-Series-Entertainment-Earth.asp}}</ref> with some troopers even donning Jedi robes as a [[Disguised Clone Trooper|disguise]] for any Jedi survivors.<ref name="French Novel">''[[Revenge of the Sith - The Film Novel]]''</ref> On the eve of Chancellor Palpatine's [[Proclamation of the New Order|proclamation]] to the [[Galactic Senate]], the 501st was confronted by two Jedi Masters, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Grand Master Yoda, upon their brief return to Coruscant. Despite their efforts, the clones failed to eliminate the Jedi, both of whom killed many troopers during their infiltration of the fallen Jedi Temple.<ref name="Episode III" /> At some point, the 501st was sent to the world of [[Zondula]] to [[Massacre on Zondula|eliminate]] five Jedi who were stationed on the world.<ref name="Inquisitors 4">[[Inquisitors 4|''Inquisitors'' 4]]</ref>

===Imperial Era===
====Dissolution and reformation====
With the fall of the Jedi Order, Darth Sidious consolidated his power by declaring himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Episode III" /> The clone troopers who survived the war were retained by the [[New Order|new regime]], effectively becoming the first generation of Imperial stormtroopers at the start of the Emperor's reign.<ref name="Databank Stormtrooper" /> At one point, a trooper in the garb of the 501st joined a 7th Sky Corps paratrooper, a clone trooper in [[phase I clone trooper armor]], and an AT-TE adorned with the [[Imperial crest]] for a battle in a sandy location.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> In the first weeks after the rise of the Empire, the 501st was disbanded by Emperor Palpatine.<ref name="Master of Evil" />

When Darth Vader embarked on a personal mission to locate the dark side locus within the [[Temple of Diso]], he hand-picked twenty clone troopers from the 501st, though they did not know he was their former general. Among the group was Appo, who served as the formation's senior officer. The gathered troopers still retained the blue and white markings of the 501st despite the unit's formal dissolution. Vader referred to this task force as "[[Shadow Platoon]]," and he formed a strong connection with Appo on the mission.<ref name="Master of Evil" /> Eventually, the 501st was reformed<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> and remained the personal troops of [[Lord]] Vader, obeying his orders without question. As such, Vader had the full confidence of his troops and their deadly efficiency. Over the next few years, the 501st Legion earned the nickname "Vader's Fist."<ref name="HC 16" />

====Legacy of the clones====
{{Quote|Palpatine's clones did their part, but the rest&hellip; He killed. Even the younglings. The ''younglings''. By the light.|Ferren Barr, on Operation: Knightfall|<ref name="Dark Lord 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref>}}
During the [[invasion of Mon Cala]] in the [[18 BBY|first year]] of the Empire's reign, fugitive Jedi Padawan [[Ferren Barr]] showed a [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[hologram]] recording of Vader's 501st Legion marching on the Jedi Temple on Coruscant at the start of the Jedi Purge to his [[Ferren Barr's disciples|disciples]] after learning that Vader and his [[Inquisitor]]s were present on [[Mon Cala]]. Barr stated to his disciples that after the Purge, he studied how the Jedi were massacred so quickly, and continued to speak on Vader before evacuating his [[Ferren Barr's cave refuge|cave refuge]].<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

Around [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi date">{{SW|url=news/obi-wan-kenobi-premiere-date|text=''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' Will Debut May 27 on Disney+}} establishes that the story of [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|''Obi-Wan Kenobi'']] begins ten [[Standard year|years]] after the events of {{Film|III}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'', including the unidentified [[Cloning|clone]] veteran's appearance, must have taken place around [[9 BBY]].</ref> the fugitive Jedi Master Obi-Wan Kenobi [[Search for Princess Leia|encountered]] a veteran clone trooper, [[Nax]], whose armor bore the markings of the 501st Legion. The retired 501st trooper had become a homeless beggar on the streets of a [[Daiyu City|city]] on [[Daiyu]],<ref name="Part II">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref> the Empire having [[Attack on Kamino|decommissioned]] the [[Kaminoan]] production of clone troopers in favor of a [[Project War-Mantle|conscription and volunteer force]].<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref> Kenobi obliged the clone veteran's request by granting him a few [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="Part II" />

====Stormtrooper unit====
[[File:501st_Commander_AR.png|thumb|left|250px|Acquiring the moniker "Vader's Fist," The 501st Legion remained active during the reign of the Galactic Empire.]]
The 501st continued to exist<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> as an elite<ref name="The Bucket" /> formation in the Stormtrooper Corps<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> with recruited stormtrooper soldiers.<ref name="The Bucket" /> The 501st became infamous as Vader's personal enforcers.<ref name="Clone Tales">{{InsiderCite|187|Clone Tales}}</ref> However, the [[1st Legion]] also served as Darth Vader's personal stormtrooper legion. The 1st Legion recruited its stormtroopers from the ranks of the 501st.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> Nevertheless, 501st remained under Vader's command,<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> with [[Everi Chalis]] claiming that the 501st was Vader's personal legion.<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> Even the "Vader's Fist" moniker was applied to the 501st<ref name="Battles" /> as a symbol of its place as Vader's personal legion.<ref name="The Bucket" /> No matter the case, the 501st fought<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> under the command of Vader<ref name="The Bucket" /> against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" />

===Galactic Civil War===
====Raid on the ''Tantive IV''====
{{Quote|She'll be all right. Inform Lord Vader we have a prisoner.|TK-4601, on Leia Organa|<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>}}
[[File:Stormtrooper_ME.png|thumb|right|250px|The 501st stormed the ''Tantive IV'' and captured Princess Leia of Alderaan.]]
In 1 BBY,<ref name="IV Date" /> shortly before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'' followed the [[CR-series|Alderaanian diplomatic cruiser]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'' to the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> A contingent<ref name="The Bucket" /> of the 501st Legion<ref name="Battles" />&mdash;among them new member [[Tarvyn Lareka|TK-4601]], who was assigned to the ''Devastator'' and Vader's Fist right after graduating from the [[Imperial Academy]]<ref name="The Bucket" />&mdash;boarded the [[cruiser]], engaging [[Rebel trooper]]s while the princess downloaded the [[Death Star plans]] into the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]]. Despite sustaining several casualties in the initial assault, the 501st defeated the Rebels and swarmed through the corridors, searching for Organa.<ref name="Episode IV" />

With her [[Secret mission to Tatooine|secret mission]] compromised, Organa dispatched the [[R2-series astromech droid|R2 unit]] to Tatooine along with the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] via an [[escape pod]]. She remained on board her starship, hiding from the 501st until a squad of stormtroopers found her. Organa opened fire on the stormtroopers,<ref name="Episode IV" /> fatally shooting TK-9091 in the process, but was [[stun]]ned by TK-4601 as she attempted to flee.<ref name="The Bucket" /> The 501st escorted Organa to Darth Vader, but the princess refused to cooperate and objected to the attack on her ship, citing her status as a member of the [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

====Unleashing Task Force 99====
=====The Ghost Moon=====
{{Quote|Who's ready to kill some ''Rebels''?|Kreel|<ref name="Star Wars 19">[[Star Wars (2015) 19|''Star Wars'' (2015) 19]]</ref>}}
[[File:SCARSquad-2015StarWars59.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The SCAR troopers of Task Force 99 comprised the special forces wing of the 501st.]]
Following an [[Prison siege at Sunspot|assault on Sunspot Prison]], the 501st's Task Force 99 were dispatched to investigate the [[prison]] and search for [[Kolar Ludd]], an imprisoned member of [[Imperial Special Forces]]. After a brief search, Task Force 99 was given new orders<ref name="Star Wars 19" />to capture Rebel Alliance [[Admiral]] [[Verette]], who was on his way to board the hijacked [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Harbinger (Imperial I-class)|''Harbinger'']] which was to be deployed against Imperial forces at the [[siege on Tureen VII]].<ref name="Star Wars 22">[[Star Wars (2015) 22|''Star Wars'' (2015) 22]]</ref> Task Force 99 intercepted the admiral's [[Taylander shuttle]], which crashed onto the [[Ghost Moon]]. There in the abandoned city, Rebel forces managed to escape the shuttle and hide in a [[skyscraper]], though they were eventually found by Task Force 99. After engaging Rebel forces and bringing the building down, Task Force 99 continued to engage the Rebels among the wreckage, and following a brief [[speeder]] chase, were forced into the sewers to avoid a Rebel-controlled modified [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]]. Using explosives, Task Force 99 destroyed the road, causing the tank to fall into the sewer, where Admiral Verette was then captured.<ref name="Star Wars 21">[[Star Wars (2015) 21|''Star Wars'' (2015) 21]]</ref>

Following the [[skirmish on the Ghost Moon]], General [[Crix Madine]] forwarded an official document to General [[Pitt Onoran]] about the 501st's Task Force 99. In the communique, General Madine stated that he believed that the extermination of Rebel troopers were conducted by Imperial SCAR troopers, and attached a document detailing elite stormtrooper squads, and further information regarding the SCAR program and how Task Force 99 was attached to an elite legion who answered to the highest levels of Imperial High Command. The documents additionally recorded an analysis of the [[Primtara incident]] perpetrated by Task Force 99, as well as documents of its members. These documents were included in the rebel depository ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

=====The ''Harbinger''=====
{{Quote|You're gonna need more than a lightsaber and some other bucketheads to stop this ship, Kreel.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Star Wars 24">[[Star Wars (2015) 24|''Star Wars'' (2015) 24]]</ref>}}
[[File:LukeVsKreel-2015StarWars24.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Armed with a lightsaber, Sergeant Kreel fought against the Jedi apprentice Luke Skywalker.]]
Several [[Standard day|days]] after the Ghost Moon skirmish, Task Force 99 used the Taylander shuttle, with Verette's corpse tied to the front if its [[hull]], as a distraction to gain entry into the ISD ''Harbinger''.<ref name="Star Wars 23">[[Star Wars (2015) 23|''Star Wars'' (2015) 23]]</ref> After entering the ''Harbinger'', Task Force 99 sabotaged several systems, and jettisoned the bodies of a rebel maintenance crew after defeating them. Task Force 99 then split into three groups, with a three members neutralizing the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], another group of three, including Sergeant Kreel, securing the hallways to maintenance, and a lone member securing the engine room, where the Rebel droids R2-D2 and C-3PO were located. However, rebel forces fought back Task Force 99 at all locations, with Sergeant Kreel engaging [[Luke Skywalker]] in a [[lightsaber duel]], while Princess Leia Organa and Captain [[Han Solo]] maintained control of the helm and [[Chewbacca]] aided the rebel droids.<ref name="Star Wars 24" />

Within the [[Standard hour|hour]], the ''Harbinger'' arrived on [[Tureen VII]] and attacked the Imperial blockade, which was caught by surprise. During the duel with Skywalker, Kreel remarked that Task Force 99 would have destroyed the ''Harbinger'' if ordered to do so, but Darth Vader had other plans for the special forces group. With the battle intensifying, Vader arrived in his [[TIE Advanced x1]].<ref name="Star Wars 24" /> At that time, Task Force 99's members began losing control of the situation aboard the Star Destroyer, and were each summarily defeated. Kreel was then incapacitated by Skywalker while dueling and additionally reporting to Vader. With the Rebels destroying Imperial forces in orbit and on the surface, Task Force 99 proceeded to destroy the ''Harbinger'' as the Rebels escaped. Despite the failure of their mission, Task Force 99 managed to capture C-3PO, revealing it to Darth Vader who chastised them for their defeat.<ref name="Star Wars 25">[[Star Wars (2015) 25|''Star Wars'' (2015) 25]]</ref>

====Battle of Hoth====
{{Quote|My lord, the fleet has moved out of lightspeed. Com-scan has detected an energy field protecting an area of the sixth planet of the Hoth system. The field is strong enough to deflect any bombardment.''"<br />"''The Rebels are alerted to our presence. [[File:DarthVader2020-3-Sprouse-textless.png|thumb|left|250px|Vader led the 501st against the Rebel Alliance in the Battle of Hoth.]]
Both the First Legion<ref name="Altaya Hoth Death Star">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Battle of Hoth and the Second Death Star}}</ref> and the 501st Legion fought against the Alliance at the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Battlefront: Twilight Company" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> three years after the destruction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]] at [[Yavin Prime|Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> When Vader's starfleet, the Imperial force known as [[Death Squadron]], first arrived, they came out of hyperspace too close to Hoth to surprise the rebels. Afterwards, Vader sent General Maximilian Veers to lead<ref name="Episode V" /> the 501st<ref name="HC 29" /> [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] group [[Blizzard Force]] for a ground assault. Veers led the AT-ATs to destroy the rebel power generator, and the walkers decimated their lines of infantry. As he approached the generator, Veers ordered his troops to disembark for ground assault. The generator was soon destroyed, and Vader<ref name="Episode V" /> who had been leading the First Legion from the east,<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> was informed that he could begin his landing to [[Echo Base]].<ref name="Episode V" />

Vader and 501st cold weather assault stormtroopers stormed into Echo Base,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> with 1st Legion snowtroopers also backing up Vader.<ref name="Commanding 1st">{{SWU|set=Spark of Rebellion|cardname=Darth Vader|subtitle=Commanding the First Legion|link=8506660490|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20240215142429/https://swudb.com/card/sor/087/darth-vader}}</ref> The remaining rebel personnel rushed to evacuate and abandoned Echo Base, officially making the battle an Imperial tactical victory.<ref name="Episode V" /> 501st snowtroopers tried to help Vader in capturing the rebel [[freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', with some 501st troopers even setting up an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]], but the starship managed to escape Vader's grasp.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> After several [[Standard week|weeks]], Blizzard Force personnel remained at Echo Base to take possession of Alliance contraband and to continue searching for signs of rebel activity per [[Captain]] [[Ivies]]. [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], rogue archeologist and criminal, infiltrated Imperial forces with a repulsorlift transport and informed the snowtroopers to load up the contraband aboard the vehicle. After travelling for some time, the snowtroopers realized that they were being led elsewhere and came under attack by Aphra's companions, [[Krrsantan]] and [[Tubleek Ruz]]. Aphra revealed herself and the snowtroopers were eliminated, but were forced to escape once an AT-AT began firing on the crew.<ref name="Aphra 2020 1">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]]</ref>

===Battle of Endor and demise===
Hours before the [[Battle of Endor]], Darth Vader informed the Emperor that his [[Skywalker family|son]] Luke Skywalker had descended to the surface of [[Endor]]. Forbidding him from openly seeking out Skywalker, the Emperor instead sent Vader to the Imperial base in charge of guarding the [[Death Star's shield generator]], where his master predicted that his son would seek him out. Vader grew increasingly impatient as he waited at the base, and openly considered the idea of ordering the 501st Legion to lay waste to the [[forest]] in order to find his son.<ref name="Beware of the Power of the Dark Side!">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> The 501st Legion subsequently participated in the Battle of Endor, although the legion died there along with Emperor Palpatine, Darth Vader, and the second Death Star.<ref name="Imperial Troops" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|Do you remember back home? Would you like to remember? Let me help you remember.|Luke Skywalker, to Grogu|<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
[[File:DiamondJediCrest-BOBF6.png|thumb|right|250px|The 501st Legion's assault on the Jedi Temple was remembered by Grogu, a survivor of the Great Jedi Purge.]]
The 501st Legion's attack on the Jedi Temple resulted in the deaths of many Jedi, including the younglings.<ref name="Episode III" /> However, the youngling Grogu survived the siege. A member of [[Yoda's species]], Grogu lived through the [[Imperial Era]] and became a [[foundling]] in the care of the Mandalorian [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. With the help of the Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker, Grogu recovered his memories from his life in the Jedi Temple on Coruscant. As a result, Grogu remembered the 501st Legion's blue and white armored clone troopers that stormed the Temple, attacking and killing the Jedi<ref name="Chapter 6" /> who had been branded as traitors to the Galactic Republic under Order 66.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:Vader-walks-in.png|thumb|left|250px|Reva Sevander witnessed the deaths of her friends as the Jedi Temple fell to Darth Vader and the 501st Legion.]]
The 501st Legion's attack on the Jedi Temple also affected the life of Reva Sevander, the Jedi youngling who turned to the dark side of [[the Force]] and joined the [[Inquisitorius]] as a [[Jedi hunter]]. Her position as an [[Inquisitor]] was a ruse, though, as the former youngling had infiltrated the Empire's ranks in the hope of an opportunity to assassinate Darth Vader for the slaughter of her peers. During the [[attack on Jabiim]]<ref name="Part V" /> in [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">{{KenobiDate|V|the attack on Jabiim}}</ref> Sevander recalled the night of Order 66 to Obi-Wan Kenobi, confirming the Jedi Master's suspicion that she was a youngling who survived the Temple's fall to the 501st. Through Sevander, Kenobi learned that the young Inquisitor had personally witnessed the killing of her fellow younglings by Vader as he led the 501st into the Temple.<ref name="Part V" />

===Development===
"[[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st Legion]]" is also the name of a fan costuming organization formed in 1997 by Albin Johnson and Tom Crews. The group originally intended to re-create accurate stormtrooper costumes but has expanded to include Sith Lords, bounty hunters and other villains from the film series, encompassing EU source material as well as canonical ones. The group goes under the nickname, "Vader's Fist," and was the inspiration for George Lucas to reference the organization in official Star Wars canon. The group actively does charity and volunteer work for a number of organizations, and currently consists of over 10,000 members worldwide.

The 501st Legion appear in a flashback scene of Order 66 in the [[Part I|first]],<ref name="Part I" /> [[Part V|fifth]],<ref name="Part V" /> and [[Part VI|sixth episodes]] of the [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi|''Obi-Wan Kenobi'']] television series.<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref> Writer [[Joby Harold]] wanted the series to begin with Order 66 to remind the audience of where Obi-Wan Kenobi's journey began and to establish that the stakes are high for everyone.<ref name="Vader terrifying">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2022/06/obi-wan-kenobi-explained|text=''Obi-Wan Kenobi:'' Darth Vader Was Originally Even More Terrifying|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2022-06-03|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']]|archivedate=20220603154447}}</ref> The troopers were played by at least nine people wearing physical armor.<ref name="New Kenobi photos">{{WebCite|url=https://thedirect.com/article/obi-wan-kenobi-hayden-christensen-jedi-photo|text=New Obi-Wan Kenobi Photo Shows Hayden Christensen with Other Jedi on Set|author=Sanders, Savannah|date=2022-06-24|archivedate=20220624163146}}</ref> In the [[Part II|second episode]], [[Temuera Morrison]] played a veteran of the 501st Legion.<ref name="Part II" /> This was the first time that Morrison wore physical [[clone trooper armor]] while portraying a clone.<ref name="First time">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/videos/temuera-morrison-never-wore-physical-clone-trooper-armor-before-obi-wan-kenobi|text=Temuera Morrison Never Wore Physical Clone Trooper Armor Before Obi-Wan Kenobi|date=2022-06-01|work=[[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN]]|archivedate=20220601033750}}</ref>
