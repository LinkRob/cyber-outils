'''Blue sprouts''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] that was [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s and blue in [[color]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|DFS}}</ref> the [[human]]s [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and [[Sylvestri Yarrow]] invited [[Jedi Master]] [[Mirro Lox]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Amadeo Azzazzo]], to a meal at a [[Pop's canteen|canteen]] on the [[Republic Defense Coalition mobile launch and repair station]]. For the meal's first course, the [[Cantina|canteen's]] owner, the [[Iktotchi]] [[Pop Teeno]], served the group blue sprouts and a [[stewed sweet beet]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
