'''Currency''', also referred to as '''money''', or as '''dosh''',<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> was used as a principal medium of exchange within a particular territory, and introduced into circulation by the appropriate regional authority on economic policy. Two main currencies existed in [[the galaxy]]: the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]], which later became the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] credit before being replaced by the [[New Republic]] credit,<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> and the [[wupiupi]], the latter circulating almost exclusively in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

Several tertiary currencies also existed throughout the galaxy, such as the [[druggat]],<ref name="ADOU">{{TCW|A Death on Utapau}}</ref> the [[Calamari Flan]],<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> [[Zukkel]]s,<ref name="The High Republic 1">[[The High Republic (2022) 1|''The High Republic'' (2022) 1]]</ref> and the [[Batuuan Spira]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref>

Various commodities in certain [[sector]]s of [[Realspace|space]] were utilized as a makeshift currency. A rare substance, the [[nova crystal]], was bartered so frequently that it was considered a form of currency, especially considering its immense value.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>
