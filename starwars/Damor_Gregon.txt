'''Damor Gregon''' was a [[human]] prep cook with a shaved head and massive beard. He worked for Strono Tuggs in the kitchen of [[Maz Kanata|Maz Kanata's]] [[Takodana Castle|Castle]] on [[Takodana]] for many years, becoming his longest tenured staff member.<ref name="Recipe" /> 

In [[146 BBY]], Gregon entered into a cooking competition for the title of [[sous-chef]] after the kitchen's sous-chef, [[Robbs Ely]], was killed. He competed against his other prep cooks, the [[Devaronian]] [[Sama Macoy]], [[Relva Jace]] from [[Corellia]], and the [[Quarren]]/[[Mon Calamari]] hybrid [[Jom Jarusch]], preparing a flayed and grilled [[dianoga]] with lightly seasoned and basted pikobi eggs on the side. Gregon was promoted to sous-chef under Tuggs after he won the competition judged by his boss and two culinary droids. He oversaw the other line cooks in the kitchen for the rest of his tenure at Takodona Castle.<ref name="Recipe" />
