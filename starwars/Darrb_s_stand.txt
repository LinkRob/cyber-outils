A [[fruit]] and [[vegetable]] '''stand''' was owned by the [[Gabdorin]] [[Merchant|vendor]] [[Darrb]] on the [[planet]] [[Varkana]]. In [[34 ABY]], [[Resistance]] operative [[Kazuda Xiono]] spilled a pot of the stand's wares, causing Darrb to throw a piece of [[Foodstuff|produce]] at the former.

==Description==
The stand was located in [[Vargo Spaceport]] on<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[planet]] [[Varkana]],<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> owned by the [[Gabdorin]] [[Merchant|vendor]] [[Darrb]].<ref name="SWE" /> Its roof consisted of large [[Color|green]] material edged by gray frames, which kept it suspended in broad arches. The stand kept several containers of various shapes<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> filled with multicolored [[fruit]]s and [[vegetable]]s for sale to customers.<ref name="SWE" />

==History==
{{Quote|Ow! Hey! I'm taking this!|Kazuda Xiono, to Darrb, after the vendor hits the former with a piece of produce outside the stand|audio=DarrbsStand2-TheMissingAgent.flac|<ref name="The Missing Agent" />}}
[[File:DarrbThrowing-TheMissingAgent.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Darrb threw produce at Kazuda Xiono after he spilled a pot of the stand's produce.]]
By the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the stand operated on Varkana, staffed by its owner, Darrb.<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">{{ResistanceDate|2|12}}</ref> Resistance operatives [[Kazuda Xiono]] and [[Jarek Yeager]],<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> the [[pirate]]<ref name="SWE" /> [[Synara San]], and Xiono's [[BB-series astromech droid|astromech droid]], [[CB-23]], [[Rescue of Norath Kev|traveled]] to Varkana to track a Resistance [[distress signal]] initiated by the [[spy]] [[Norath Kev]]. Xiono looked backward to speak to Yeager while walking past the stand, causing<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> him<ref name="SWE" /> to trip over a pot carrying some of the stall's wares.<ref name="The Missing Agent" />

As Darrb shouted angrily at him, Xiono apologized and helped gather the spilled items. Darrb walked out from behind the stand and took the container from Xiono, throwing [[Foodstuff|produce]] at the Resistance operative in frustration. Xiono called out in surprise upon being hit in the head and quickly left the area, taking the piece of produce with him.<ref name="The Missing Agent" />
