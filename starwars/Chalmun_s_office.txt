The [[Wookiee]] [[Chalmun]] maintained a '''private office''' behind the main bar of [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], a [[cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] that Chalmun owned. It was decorated with the barest of necessities, including a [[shisha]], a steady supply of premium [[spice]], and a wall rack full of [[weapon]]s confiscated by patrons. A number of security features were hidden in the room, including an illumination panel which doubled as a secret observation window behind Chalmun's desk and a trapdoor under the rug.<ref name="Complete" />
