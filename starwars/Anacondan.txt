'''Anacondans''' were a snakelike [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:Morley_detail.png|thumb|left|120px|Extended view of an Anacondan]]
Anacondans were [[snake]]-like [[Sentience|sentients]]<ref name="Databank Ana" /> whose long, tapering, cylindrical bodies could measure up to 4.7 [[meter]]s in length.<ref name="Databank Morl">{{Databank|morley|Morley}}</ref> Because they lacked [[limb]]s, they moved along on the ground with a twisting movement,<ref name="Brothers" /> using their tails to grab hold of objects.<ref name="Deception" /> Their heads featured a large [[mouth]] filled with needle-like [[Tooth|teeth]] and two expressive [[eye]]s with lids, but lacked visible [[ear]]s.<ref name="Brothers" />

Their scaly skin could be evenly [[color]]ed<ref name="Brothers" /> or mottled.<ref name="Deception" /> Known patterns included rusty grey<ref name="Brothers" /> and red and yellow.<ref name="Deception" /> Their eye colors ranged from yellow<ref name="Brothers" /> to blue.<ref name="Deception" />

The Anacondans possessed speech organs that allowed them to speak [[Galactic Basic Standard]] without difficulty.<ref name="Brothers" />

==Society and culture==
Due to their serpentine nature, Anacondans typically wore no [[clothing]] at all.<ref name="Brothers" /><ref name="Deception" />

==Anacondans in the galaxy==
Due to their appearance, Anacondans were often mistaken for pests.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> During the [[High Republic Era]], the Anacondan [[doctor]] [[Gino'le]] served as Chief of Medical Operations aboard the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]], having outfitted himself with [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[limb]]s to better treat patients.<ref name="First Duty">{{InsiderCite|issue1=201|issue2=202|Starlight: First Duty}}</ref> By the time of the Clone Wars, some Anacondans had made their way to [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] world of the [[Galactic Republic]]. One of them worked as a [[bartender]] in [[Trueping's]], a dodgy [[cantina]] buried in the [[Coruscant Underworld|depths]] of Coruscant.<ref name="Deception" />

Another Anacondan, named [[Morley]], lived on the junk planet [[Lotho Minor]]. There, Morley made a deal with the exiled former [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] as a means of survival. Morley would lead unsuspecting individuals to one of the trap doors leading to [[Darth Maul's lair|Maul's underground lair]], in return he would get the "leftovers".<ref name="Databank Morl" />
