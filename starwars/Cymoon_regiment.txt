A full '''regiment''' of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]] was active on [[Cymoon 1]], as part of the Imperial defense of the [[moon]] and the vital [[Weapons Factory Alpha]]. The [[regiment]] was subdivided into [[battalion]]s and supported by a [[Cymoon system battle group|battle group]] that operated in the [[Cymoon system]]. Due to the various number of ad hoc groups, efficacy was reduced due to communications breakdowns between the units.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />
