'''Data pits''',<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> also known as '''command pits''',<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> '''control pits''',<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> a '''bridge pit''',<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> or '''data warehouses''',<ref name="SotEaR">{{EncyclopediaCite|Starships of the Empire and the Rebellion}}</ref> were sunken pits located on either side of an elevated platform that bissected the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of certain [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], later the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[capital ship]]s<ref name="CVD" /> like the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class]],<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I'']]<ref name="Part V">{{Kenobi|Part V}}</ref> and the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]]<ref name="Shattered Empire 2">[[Shattered Empire 2|''Shattered Empire'' 2]]</ref> and the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnoughts]]. They contained duty station consoles, and their walls were lined with control panels and monitors.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Those pits were designed so the [[flight data officer]]s, [[Tracking officer|tracking systems specialists]], and [[combat supervisor]]s that worked there could not see the bridge's [[viewport]]s, and thus focused on their consoles without unnecessary distraction.<ref name="CVD" />
