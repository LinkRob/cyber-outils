The '''Alkenzi Air Base'''<ref name="Garrison Databank">{{Databank|imperial-garrison-aldhani|Imperial Garrison (Aldhani)}}</ref> was an [[Airbase|air base]] on the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]] used by the [[Aldhani Garrison]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Description==
The Alkenzi Air Base was built inside a ridge between two [[mountain]]s<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref> 52 [[kilometer]]s away from the [[Nasma Klain]] river where the [[Aldhani dam]] fortress was located.<ref name="Aldhani" /> Among its features was a large [[hangar]] bay capable of housing at least one [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] as well as four [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/ln fighters]] in suspended racks. The hangar opened to a tunnel which led outside of the [[mountain]]s to the ridge. It was guarded by a detachment of [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="The Eye" />

==History==
===Imperialization===
Following the Imperial occupation of Aldhani around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the Empire took over the pre-existing airbase in Alkenzi and modified it to house TIE fighters and Imperial ships.<ref name="Aldhani" />

===Aldhani dam heist===
[[File:Alkenzi_Air_Base_Inside.png|thumb|An Imperial Pilot entering a TIE Fighter to enforce the skies during the Aldhani Dam Heist.|262x262px]]
In [[5 BBY]], a [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|rebel team]] led by [[Vel Sartha]], working under [[Luthen Rael]] and with assistance from disgruntled Imperial [[Lieutenant]] [[Gorn]], performed a [[Aldhani heist|heist]] on the Aldhani dam complex in order to steal the sector quarterly payroll stored in its vault. During the heist, the dam's communication array was disabled as part of the rebel team's plan, and the command tower was captured resulting in the base personnel failing to respond to the airbase's status checks.<ref name="The Eye" /> As per standard protocol,<ref name="Aldhani" /> three TIE fighters were immediately dispatched to the dam where they encountered the [[Max-7 Rono box freighter|Rono freighter]] stored there escaping through the dam launch tunnel. The fighters pursued it through the [[Eye of Aldhani]] which was passing over the area at the time, resulting in all three fighters being destroyed by the Eye's nanoparticles.<ref name="The Eye" />
