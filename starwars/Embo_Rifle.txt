The '''Embo Rifle''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This [[blaster]] [[rifle]] consisted of a [[Color|black]], gray, and silver frame, with yellow, orange, and red painted elements, and strips of brown cloth wrapped around the stock and grip, resembling the clothing commonly worn by the [[Kyuzo]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Embo Rifle was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Embo Bounty Hunter]].<ref name="Hunters" />
