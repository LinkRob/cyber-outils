'''Clem So''' is an actor who portrayed a [[Resistance]] fighter in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], a [[Canto Bight]] casino patron in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], and a [[Dryden Vos]] guest in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|Clem_So|status/1529885940314947584|Clem So|date=2022-05-26|quote=Queuing up at the cinema to watch Star Wars episode IV back in 77.
