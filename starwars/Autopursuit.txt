'''Autopursuit''' was a feature equipped on certain [[starfighter]]s, allowing such a [[Starship|vessel]] to pursue and fire upon targets without any further input from its [[pilot]].<ref name="Master & Apprentice" />

In approximately [[40 BBY]],<ref name="dating">The scenario described takes place in ''Master & Apprentice'', in which [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] is seventeen years old. The app {{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Obi-Wan Kenobi - Padawan}} states that Kenobi was born in [[57 BBY]]. Therefore, it can be deduced that the story takes place in either [[39 BBY]] or [[40 BBY]].</ref> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] took control of the [[Facet (starfighter)|''Facet'']] while engaged in operations aboard the ''[[Leverage]]'' as part of his and his [[Jedi Master|master's]] [[Mission to Pijal]]. On the vessel, he accidentally engaged this feature, resulting in a wild chase that eventually ended with it crashing into the ship's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. The experience deeply troubled Kenobi<ref name="Master & Apprentice" /> and resulted in a dislike of flying such vessels which would last until at least the end of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>
