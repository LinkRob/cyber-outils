"'''Dossier BXLH: Les mutants dans Star Wars'''" (English: '''''File BXLH: The mutants in Star Wars''''') is a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] source article published in the [[Graal 23|twenty-third issue]] of the French magazine [[Graal (magazine)|''Graal'']] in [[January]] [[1990]]. It was written by [[Olivier Frot]], with illustrations by [[Pascal Gayrard]].
