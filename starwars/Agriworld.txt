'''Agriworlds''',<ref name="Food">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&#x2014;Crew Run: "Value of Food"</ref> also known as an '''agricultural planet''', or as an '''agrarian world''',<ref name="Jedi Knights 6">[[Jedi Knights 6|''Jedi Knights'' 6]]</ref> were planets where [[crops]] such as [[grain]] were grown and exported to produce [[foodstuff]] across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Food" />

[[Farmer]]s would often use [[crop silo]]s to store crops, and in some cases like on [[Mina-Rau]], live inside them.<ref name="One Year Later">{{Andor|One Year Later}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Salliche is the breadbasket of the Core Worlds. If we fell, how would planets such as Coruscant survive?''"<br />"''There are plenty of agricultural worlds in the Outer Rim who could supply crops. Perhaps Salliche is afraid of the competition?|[[Senator]] [[Bulgeski]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Dooku]]|<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>}}
The agriworld of [[Ukio]], which was also part of the Outer Rim, served as a major producer of [[grain]] sent to the [[Core Worlds]] until it [[Separatist Crisis|seceded]] from the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

By the time of the [[Galactic Empire]], it became increasingly common for undocumented migrants to help with labor on agriworlds, and the Empire would often turn a blind eye due to the necessity of foodstuff across the galaxy.<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref>
