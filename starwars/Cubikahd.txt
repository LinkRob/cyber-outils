'''Cubikahd'''&mdash;or '''cu'bikad''', depending on the [[Mando'a|dialect]]&mdash;was a [[Mandalorian]] strategy [[hologame]]. A strategic and tactical [[board game]], players would control blades and move them around a cube-shaped board and grid. In [[2 BBY]], the Mandalorian [[Spectres|rebel]] [[Sabine Wren]] played cubikahd against the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protector]] [[Fenn Rau]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Dejarik requires half the tactical thinking of something like Cubikahd. The simplicity of getting those blades where they need to be? Makes me feel like a genius.|A patron at Takodana Castle describes cubikahd|<ref name="SWBFII">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Cubikahd-Board.png|thumb|right|250px|A cubikahd board.]]
Cubikahd was a two-player [[hologame]] and [[board game]]. Also known as cu'bikad in some [[Mando'a|dialects]], the game involved participants moving four [[Knife|knives]], which could occupy various positions on a three-dimensional, cube-shaped board and gridlines, with the intention of capturing their opponent's pieces.<ref name="Cubikahd" /> The knives were also referred to as blades. Players stated their moves when making them. Legal moves included blade to cube face four and blade to cube face two. The game was played by [[Mandalorian]]s of<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Comprehensive">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos" />

Some compared cubikahd to other strategy games such as [[dejarik]].<ref name="SWBFII" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="TakoDate">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates [[Han Solo]] meeting with an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Ralsius Paldora|defector]] at [[Takodana Castle|Maz's castle]] to get information that would help in the [[liberation of Kashyyyk]] to twenty-nine [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the meeting and the resulting skirmish took place in [[5 ABY]].</ref> as the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] searched for the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Desertion|defector]] [[Ralsius Paldora]] on<ref name="SWBFII" /> the planet<ref name="Comprehensive" /> [[Takodana]], he overheard a patron of [[Takodana Castle]] state that cubikahd was twice as tactical as dejarik and elaborate on how smart they felt when succeeding at the game.<ref name="SWBFII" />

==History==
{{Quote|I'll bet your game has slipped since you left Mandalore.|Fenn Rau, to Sabine Wren|<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos" />}}
[[File:Fenn_Rau_no_armor.png|thumb|left|160px|Fenn Rau faced Sabine Wren in a game of cubikahd.]]
In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{RebelsDate|307}}</ref> the Mandalorian [[Spectres|rebel]] [[Sabine Wren]] played a game of cubikahd with [[Fenn Rau]], a rebel [[prisoner]] and leader of the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]], aboard the [[CR90 corvette]]<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos" /> [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']].<ref name="AD">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> The [[Hologram|holographic]] board and pieces were displayed via the [[C1-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper's]] [[holoprojector]]. The two played with a [[Color|yellow]] holographic board and blades that were colored blue for one player and orange for the other. As they played, Rau remarked that he imagined Wren's skill with the game had slipped since leaving Mandalore, criticizing her style as "showy" and her strategy of having too much bravado. Although Wren disagreed, Rau was the victor in their game. After the game, Chopper reset the holographic board, but the three were called away for a mission briefing.<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Star_Wars_Rebels_Season_Three_poster.jpg|thumb|right|160px|Cubikahd appeared in ''Star Wars Rebels'' Season Three.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], cubikahd first appeared in "[[Imperial Supercommandos]],"<ref name="Imperial Super Commandos" /> the seventh episode in the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Three|third season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', which aired on [[November 5]], [[2016]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|174|Star Wars Rebels Season Three Episode Guide|''Star Wars Rebels'' Season Three Episode Guide}}</ref> Cubikahd was first identified in a [[Wikipedia:Twitter|tweet]] posted by the official Star Wars account on the same day,<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|starwars|status/795062658638565376|Star Wars|quote=Cubikahd is a serious game.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/uYkfx}}</ref> while the game's [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry on [[StarWars.com]] identified it with the alternate name cu'bikad on the [[November 6|following day]].<ref name="Cubikahd" />

''[[Cu'bikad]]'' originally appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the [[2007]] novel ''[[Legacy of the Force: Sacrifice]]'', written by [[Karen Traviss]],<ref name="Sacrifice">''[[Legacy of the Force: Sacrifice]]''</ref> before appearing and being identified in Traviss' [[2008]] novel ''[[Legacy of the Force: Revelation]]'', where some considered the game an activity non-Mandalorians were not meant to participate in.<ref name="Revelation">''[[Legacy of the Force: Revelation]]''</ref>

During the course of production for "Imperial Supercommandos," original scripts called for Wren and Rau to play a game of dejarik. Ultimately, the production crew elected to adapt the pre-existing game of ''cu'bikad'' for the episode, in order to have the two Mandalorian characters engage in a game more unique to the Mandalorian culture. Members of the ''Star Wars Rebels'' production team jokingly referred to cubikahd as "stabble" behind the scenes.<ref name="ImperialSuperCommandosEpGuide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/imperial-super-commandos-trivia-gallery|text="Imperial Super Commandos" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref>
