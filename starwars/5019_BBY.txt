==Events==
*With the leadership of the [[Stybla family]] the [[Chiss]] [[Chiss Ascendancy|Ascendancy]] enter a period of exploration.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /><ref name="why 5019">The events dated by this article to 5019 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] were described by [[Patriarch]] [[Stybla'mi'ovodo]] "Lamiov" as having taken place five-thousand [[Standard year|year]]s prior. Lamiov described these events as five [[Millennium|thousand years]] ago shortly before the [[Vagaari pirate operations]] began. The [[Vagaari]] [[pirate]] operations took place prior to the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', whose main events took place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, these events can be dated to circa 5019 BBY.</ref>
