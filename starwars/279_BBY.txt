'''279 BBY''' or '''279<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Around that year,<ref name="Date" /> the [[Galactic Republic]] assisted with a full-scale [[Evacuation of Hintis IV|evacuation]] of the [[planet]] [[Hintis IV]] due to the impending death of its [[star]], [[Hintis]].<ref name="EoD">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
279 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the [[2023]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'', written by [[George Mann]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="EoD" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
