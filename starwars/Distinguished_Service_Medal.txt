The '''Distinguished Service Medal (DSM)''' was a decoration that was awarded to both [[Alliance Military]] personnel and civilians who distinguished themselves by exceptionally meritorious service to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in a position of great responsibility, and was the [[Alliance Army]] equivalent of the [[Distinguished Flying Medal]]. It was principally a non-combat award that was issued to civilians who provided aid and material support to the Rebel Alliance at great risk to themselves, or to non-combatant military personnel such as clerks, analysts, cargo handlers, supply personnel, and others who provided vital support to Alliance operations despite not participating in the front lines of combat.<ref name="Lead" />

The Distinguished Service Medal was characterized by a small, round, [[silver]] medallion that featured the [[Rebel Alliance starbird]] that was surrounded by [[Aurebesh]] text on three sides that said "For Meritorious Service" and the date "0 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]" at the bottom. The medallion was suspended from a silver rectangular [[ribbon]] with a lone broad [[Color|blue]] stripe that ran vertically down the center. The award was usually issued once to an individual.<ref name="Lead" />
