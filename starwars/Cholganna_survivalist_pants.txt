'''Cholganna survivalist pants''' were a type of highly resistant [[Clothing|pants]] manufactured<ref name="Outlaws" /> on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Nexu GoC">{{GoC|Nexu}}</ref> [[Cholganna]] and they could protect their wearer from Cholganna's dangerous creatures.<ref name="Outlaws" /> The pants were [[color|dark green]] and included a black leg brace below the left knee as well as a pair of black [[boot]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Quarren]] [[merchant]] [[Shuma (merchant)|Shuma]] had at least one pair Cholganna survivalist pants as well as a [[Cholganna survivalist jacket]] for sale form their [[Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport]] as they traveled around the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] outlaw [[Kay Vess]] purchased a pair of Cholganna survivalist pants from Shuma for 2150 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A Cholganna survivalist pants appeared in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', a [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. When equipped by the player, the pants greatly reduced the damage taken from explosions while the player is crouched, rolling, or sliding. If the player is wearing a full set of Cholganna survivalist gear&mdash;the pants, the [[Cholganna survivalist belt|belt]], and the jacket, the player will slowly heal fully depleted health segments over time.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Iuliia Misiul]] created concept art of the Cholganna survivalist pants and other outfits used by Vess.<ref name="Art Station">{{ArtStation|username=Iuliia Misiul|url=/artwork/4N9Ve4|name=[[Iuliia Misiul]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws &mdash; Kay's Gearsets}}</ref>
