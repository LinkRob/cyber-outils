The '''curved-hilt lightsaber''' was a type of [[lightsaber]] utilized by the [[Jedi]] and the [[Sith]]. Its curved hilt design allowed the [[lightsaber duel]]ist to [[Lightsaber combat|slash and lunge]] at their opponent with greater precision. [[Count]] [[Dooku]] wielded a [[Dooku's lightsaber|curved-hilt lightsaber]] during his [[time]] as a [[Jedi Master]] and as the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Tyranus. His choice of weapon was emulated by his [[Sith apprentice]], [[Asajj Ventress]], who possessed [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|two curved-hilt lightsabers]] which doubled as a [[Double-bladed lightsaber|saberstaff]] when [[Split saber|joined]] together. After the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Imperial Era|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], former [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] fashioned a pair of [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|curved-hilt sabers]] with a more rectangular profile. As with her [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|previous Jedi weapons]], she crafted one standard and one [[shoto lightsaber]].

==Description==
[[File:AhsokaTanoWhiteLightsabers2-TLC.png|thumb|left|300px|Lightsabers with a curved-hilt design provided for increased precision in combat.]]
The curved-hilt [[lightsaber]] was a type of [[melee weapon]]; elegant in design, its gracefully curved hilt enabled the wielder to utilize greater finesse when slashing and lunging at an opponent.<ref name="Dooku DB" /> [[Count]] [[Dooku]], an accomplished [[Lightsaber duel|duelist]]<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> who favored the [[Form II|second form]] of [[lightsaber combat]],<ref name="AEYNK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> wielded a [[Color|red]]-bladed [[Dooku's lightsaber|curved-hilt lightsaber]].<ref name="Dooku DB" /> [[Asajj Ventress]], a [[Force-sensitive]] [[assassin]] trained by Dooku in the ways of the [[dark side of the Force]],<ref name="Ventress DB">{{Databank|asajj-ventress|Asajj Ventress}}</ref> wielded [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|two lightsabers]] that featured a curve-shaped hilt similar to her [[Sith Master|master's]] weapon. When [[Split saber|joined together]], Ventress's lightsabers formed a [[double-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Ventress sabers DB" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
Curved-hilt lightsabers were used by several [[Jedi Knight]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] since the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="THR Show #3" /><ref name="HRA 4" /> Examples of [[Jedi]] with curved-hilt lightsabers included [[Cohmac Vitus]]' [[Cohmac Vitus' lightsaber|lightsaber]]<ref name="THR Show #3" /> and [[Torban Buck]]'s [[Torban Buck's blue-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="HRA 4" /> The Jedi [[Obratuk Glii]] owned [[Obratuk Glii's lightsabers|several lightsabers]], one of which had a curved-hilt design.<ref name="THRA7">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 7|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 7]]</ref>

===Fall of the Republic===
====Dooku/Darth Tyranus====
{{Quote|It is obvious that this contest cannot be decided by our knowledge of the Force&hellip; but by our skills with a lightsaber.|Darth Tyranus, to Yoda|<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>}}
[[File:Justice-Force-choke.png|thumb|right|300px|Dooku wielded a curved-hilt lightsaber since his days as a Jedi.]]
When Dooku was a [[Jedi Initiate]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Lene Kostana]] made up the story of a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] named [[Darth Sakia]] to test him. Part of the story was that she wielded a curved-hilt lightsaber.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> Later, once Dooku himself was a Jedi Master, he replaced [[Dooku's first lightsaber|the lightsaber]] he had been using since his [[Standard day|days]] as a young [[Padawan]] with a<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> handcrafted curved-hilt lightsaber<ref name="Ultimate" /> that emitted a blue blade. Dooku used this lightsaber on multiple assignments that he undertook during his time in the [[Jedi Order]], including the [[Mission to rescue Dagonet's son|mission to rescue]] [[Senator]] [[Dagonet]]'s [[Uriel Dagonet|son]]<ref name="Justice">{{TOTJ|Justice (episode)}}</ref> and the [[Incident on Raxus Secundus|mission]] to [[Raxus Secundus]].<ref name="Choices">{{TOTJ|Choices}}</ref>

When his new allegiance to the [[Sith]] was discovered by Jedi Master [[Yaddle]], Dooku [[Duel in the Works|attacked]] his former ally at the behest of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]]. Rejecting Yaddle's entreaty of [[Light side of the Force|atonement]], Dooku [[Death|killed]] her with his own lightsaber and thereby became Sidious' new [[Sith apprentice]],<ref name="The Sith Lord">{{TOTJ|The Sith Lord}}</ref> taking the name of [[Darth]] Tyranus<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> in addition to his public title, the [[Count of Serenno]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

[[File:Dooku_salute.png|thumb|left|300px|Dooku's curved-hilt lightsaber emitted a red blade after he became the Sith Lord Darth Tyranus.]]
[[Count]] Dooku's lightsaber blade was changed from blue to red after his conversion to the dark side.<ref name="AoR Dooku" /> During the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], Dooku engaged in a duel with Jedi Knight [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and Padawan [[Anakin Skywalker]]. He succeeded in wounding Kenobi and severing Skywalker's right [[Limb|arm]],<ref name="Episode II" /> but was bested<ref name="Dooku DB" /> by his former master, [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], and retreated from [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Episode II" />

Dooku dueled with Kenobi and Skywalker on several occasions during the [[Clone Wars]], as well as his prospective apprentices Ventress and [[Savage Opress]].<ref name="Dooku DB" /> In his final bout which took place during the [[Battle of Coruscant]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> Dooku was disarmed and beheaded by his nemesis Skywalker,<ref name="Dooku DB" /> who used [[Skywalker lightsaber|his own lightsaber]] and the Count's weapon to carry out the execution.<ref name="Episode III" />

====Asajj Ventress and Barriss Offee====
{{Quote|The forbidden disciple of Darth Tyranus carried two lightsabers with curved hilts, each similar in style to the lightsaber used by her master.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SOTS">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>}}
[[File:AsajjVentress-SWTLC.png|thumb|220px|Asajj Ventress owned dual curved-hilt lightsabers that could be joined together to form a double-bladed lightsaber.]]
Though initially [[Jedi training|trained]] as a Jedi Padawan, Asajj Ventress switched her green-bladed lightsaber for a pair of red-bladed curved-hilt lightsabers during her [[Standard year|years]] as Darth Tyranus's apprentice.<ref name="Ventress sabers DB" /> Both hilts could be joined together to form a unique [[double-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Throughout her time in service to the Sith cause, Ventress utilized her lightsabers in several capacities&mdash;as her master's [[emissary]]<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> and assassin,<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> as well as a [[Confederacy military|military]] [[commander]] of the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="Ambush" /> She fought a number of Jedi on several occasions with varying degrees of success, such as Kenobi, Skywalker,<ref name="The Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> [[Luminara Unduli]], and [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Cloak of Darkness" />

Ventress retained her Sith lightsabers after Tyranus discarded her on the orders of his master, Darth Sidious, and continued to use them as a [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]]<ref name="Nightsisters">{{Databank|nightsisters|Nightsisters}}</ref> before pursuing a career as a [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref> She allowed Obi-Wan Kenobi to use one of her lightsabers during their [[Skirmish above Raydonia|fight]] against the [[Dathomirian]] [[Maul's family|brothers]] [[Maul]] and Savage Opress.<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref>

Both lightsabers were lost when the fallen Jedi [[Barriss Offee]] ambushed Ventress in the [[Coruscant Underworld]] and took possession of her weapons,<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" /> consequently forcing the [[bounty]] hunter to acquire a [[Asajj Ventress's lightsaber|replacement]].<ref name="Ventress sabers DB" /> The [[Mirialan]] used the weapons when she ambushed Ahsoka Tano at an [[Abandoned munitions warehouse|abandoned warehouse]], leading the exhausted Tano, on the run in an attempt to clear her name after being framed by Offee for her crimes, to assume her attacker was Ventress.<ref name="TCAJ">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> Offee was later forced to use Ventress's lightsabers when Skywalker [[Duel at the Jedi Temple|confronted]] her in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], and thereby revealed herself as the culprit responsible for framing Tano.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" /> 

[[File:AsajjVentress-TBBs3trailer.png|thumb|left|208px|Ventress new final saber had a curved design and a yellow blade.]]
Although Ventress manage to replace it a [[Asajj Ventress's lightsaber|yellow lightsaber]] she bought from the [[black market]] during the Clone Wars. But, that lightsaber had a straight hilt, which helped distance herself from her previous life as Dooku's [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]]<ref name="FactFile 2014 112">{{FactFile2014|112|LIG 33-34|Asajj Ventress' Lightsabers}}</ref> and was laid to rest with her after her apparent [[death|death.]]<ref name="TBB article">{{SW|url=news/bad-batch-interview-nika-futterman-asajj-ventress|text=Ventress Strikes Back}}</ref> During the early [[Imperial Era]] had a curved hilt similar to her old twin red lightsabers.<ref name="Harbinger">{{TBB|The Harbinger}}</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
====Ahsoka Tano====
[[File:Ahsoka_Tano_SWDL_Darren_Tan.png|thumb|250px|Ahsoka Tano built a pair of curved-hilt lightsabers during the reign of the Galactic Empire.]]
Ahsoka Tano, once a Jedi Padawan who departed the Order late in the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars after being framed for [[treason]] by Barriss Offee,<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" /> abandoned her [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|first pair]] of lightsabers on a [[Unidentified moon|moon]] in the aftermath of [[Order 66]]<ref name="VAD">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> to fake her death to the nascent [[Galactic Empire]]. Over a year later, she finally built herself a pair of [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|white-bladed lightsabers]] after becoming involved in an [[Raada Uprising|uprising]] against the Empire on the remote moon [[Raada]]. The parts were made from the [[Sixth Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of the Inquisitor known as the [[Sixth Brother]]. Although the weapons' hilts were originally rough due to being constructed in the middle of battle,<ref name="Ahsoka" /> when she finished them, they were curved with a rectangular profile. Like her original lightsabers, Tano's new blades consisted of a standard single-bladed lightsaber and a [[Shoto lightsaber|shoto]].<ref name="TFOTF">{{Rebels|The Future of the Force}}</ref>

Tano used the weapons throughout the [[Imperial Era]], including when she encountered the members of the [[Spectres]], a rebel crew that included Jedi [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]].<ref name="FATG">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> She used her blades in at least one sparring match with Bridger.<ref name="ADL">{{FOD|A Disarming Lesson}}</ref> Over the course of her association with the Spectres and [[Phoenix Cell]], Tano [[Duel in Hammertown|crossed blades]] with Imperial [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] [[Seventh Sister]] and [[Fifth Brother]] on [[Takobo]].<ref name="TFOTF" /> Eventually, Tano accompanied Jarrus and Bridger on a [[Mission to Malachor|mission]] to the dark world of [[Malachor]], where they encountered the Inquisitors. Tano was eventually forced to duel her former master Darth Vader as Jarrus and Bridger escaped, after which she was presumed dead.<ref name="TOTA">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> However, Tano survived the ordeal when Bridger rescued her via the [[World Between Worlds]], from his perspective several years later. Tano used her weapons to defend herself from [[Galactic Emperor]] Sheev Palpatine's [[Sith alchemy]] before they both escaped.<ref name="AWBW">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref>

====Kirak Infil'a and Darth Vader====
{{Quote|You came here to get something. I can sense it. You want my lightsaber.|Kirak Infil'a, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 3">[[Darth Vader (2017) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 3]]</ref>}}
[[File:VadersSuite-2017DarthVader8.png|thumb|left|250px|The lightsaber of Jedi Master Kirak Infil'a was claimed by the Sith Lord Darth Vader.]]
A [[Kirak Infil'a's lightsaber|curved-hilt lightsaber]] served as the weapon of Jedi Master [[Kirak Infil'a]] and Sith [[Lord]] Darth Vader in turn.<ref name="Dark Lord 4" /> It first belonged to Infil'a and was therefore a Jedi lightsaber that emitted a green blade.<ref name="Dark Lord 3" /> During the early days of the [[Emperor]]'s reign, Vader sought to claim Infil'a's lightsaber,<ref name="Dark Lord 4" /> having [[Duel on Mustafar|lost]] the blue lightsaber that he wielded as Anakin Skywalker, in accordance with the ways of the Sith.<ref name="Dark Lord 1">[[Darth Vader (2017) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 1]]</ref> After [[Mission to Al'doleem|killing]] Infil'a on [[Al'doleem]],<ref name="Dark Lord 4" /> Vader traveled to the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] to change the blade's color to red by subjecting its [[kyber crystal]] to a dark-side process known as [[bleeding]]. His first attempt failed, resulting in a [[Force vision]] that showed Vader returning to the light as Anakin Skywalker, and using Infil'a's green-bladed lightsaber to kill Darth Sidious.<ref name="Dark Lord 5">[[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]]</ref>

However, Vader rejected the vision and made a second attempt to bleed the crystal. The process succeeded and Vader returned to [[Coruscant]] where he presented his new curved-hilt [[Sith lightsaber]] to Sidious.<ref name="Dark Lord 5" /> Vader used the lightsaber when he fought [[the Grand Inquisitor]] and later when he [[Hunt for Jocasta Nu|hunted]] the [[Jedi]] Master and [[Chief Librarian]] of the [[Jedi Archives]], [[Jocasta Nu]].<ref name="Dark Lord 9">[[Darth Vader (2017) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 9]]</ref> The hilt was subsequently destroyed during an [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|attempt]] on Vader's life by a [[Chas|family]] of [[bounty hunter]]s on [[Cabarria]], during which the hilt was shattered by the combined strain of a [[tractor rifle]] and Vader's pulling it back with the Force.<ref name="Dark Lord 11">[[Darth Vader (2017) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 11]]</ref> Salvaging the crystal, Vader soon crafted his own, [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|new hilt]] for it.<ref name="Dark Lord 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref>

===Chelli Lona Aphra===
During the [[hunt for the Spark Eternal]], the [[Spark Eternal]] [[artificial intelligence]] took control of [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]]'s body, forcing the doctor to live out the Spark Eternal's "memories" as it piloted her body. Inside one memory, after helping [[Miril]], the leader of the [[Ascendant]] cult, defeat the [[Darkseekers]] group of Sith, Aphra picked up a curved-hilt Sith lightsaber.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 24">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 24|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 24]]</ref> In another memory, as part of a test of the [[Null Blade]], Aphra cut the [[sword]]s blade, using the saber but the saber was neutralized as the Null Blade had a [[cortosis]] alloy.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 25]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Oppo_Rancisis_lightsaber_design.jpg|thumb|Designs for Oppo Rancisis's lightsaber|150px]]
Curved-hilt lightsabers first appeared with Count Dooku's lightsaber in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" /> It was first formally identified in the short story "[[Dooku Captured (short story)|Dooku Captured]]."<ref name="DCSOLAD">{{StoryCite|story=Dooku Captured (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref>

The [[Oppo Rancisis's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of [[Oppo Rancisis]] was set to appear in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', but the show was cancelled before the blade made an appearance. Designs for the lightsaber that were based on Count Dooku's lightsaber and included the addition of a hilt-guard were revealed by [[Dave Filoni]] and [[Pablo Hidalgo]] in their ''The Untold Clone Wars'' panel at [[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]] in 2015.<ref name="Untold Clone Wars">{{SW|url=news/swca-the-untold-clone-wars-panel-liveblog|text=SWCA: The Untold ''Clone Wars'' Panel Liveblog}}</ref>
