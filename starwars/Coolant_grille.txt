A '''coolant grille''' was a device that could flash-freeze any object or lifeform that was placed on top of it. One coolant grille was used on the [[Galactic Empire]] [[wreckage-prison]] [[Accresker Jail]] to freeze [[Bor Ifriem|the]] interrogation [[Mairan|Bor]]. [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] used the coolant grille to freeze the [[Changeling|shapeshifter]] "Lopset Yas" in the form of [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]], in order to trick [[Tam Posla]] into trading him for an escape [[shuttle]].<ref name="Aphra 24" /> However, both Aphra and Posla were unaware that "Yas" was actually Evazan in disguise, and not a shapeshifter.<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 25]]</ref> Aphra and Tolvan later used the coolant grille to freeze the [[gundravian hookspore]]s that had infected Accresker Jail as they tried to possess Posla.<ref name="Aphra 24" />
