The '''Collections and Security Division''' was the military branch of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] and consisted of the [[Iotran Guard]] and the [[IG-series battle droid army]], which was led by the [[Muun officer corps]].<ref name="Rise" /> A [[Cybloc Transfer Station|transfer station]] maintained by the Banking Clan in the [[Cybloc system]] made use of [[spaceport police droid]]s.<ref name="Revival">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref> [[San Hill]] promised the Banking Clan's [[droid]] forces to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] at the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="AotC" /> as part of his non-exclusive contract with the Separatists.<ref name="HC 23">{{HelmetCollectionCite|23|Databank A-Z}}</ref> Although the Security Division continued to exist in an independent form, such as through the Cybloc system's defense droids,<ref name="Revival" /> its forces also joined the ranks of the Separatist army. Banking Clan droids took part in the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]] as part of the CIS army.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3">[[Obi-Wan 3|''Obi-Wan'' 3]]</ref> During the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], Separatist-Banking Clan forces were [[Fourth Battle of Mygeeto|entrenched]] on the [[planet]] [[Mygeeto]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
