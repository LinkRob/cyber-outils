The [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] managed a '''factory''' on the [[Pijal's moon|moon]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]] that employed workers to produce [[nutritional supplement]]s during the [[Republic Era]]. In [[40 BBY]], the facility was [[Blackguard campaign|destroyed]] by [[blackguard]]s acting under the orders of the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]]. The demolition of the [[factory]] was ultimately blamed on the [[Opposition]] performance troupe under the assumption that they were [[Opposition protests|protesting]] the [[Pijali monarchy]]'s [[Governance Treaty]] initiative. As a result of the factory attack, [[Lord]] [[Regent]] [[Rael Averross]] requested assistance from the [[Jedi High Council]] to help protect Fanry until her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] took place.

==Description==
{{Quote|That factory had few defenses to speak of. Perhaps we should've expected this.|Captain Deren about the factory's destruction|<ref name="MA" />}}
During the [[Republic Era]], the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] operated a [[nutritional supplement]] [[factory]] on the [[Pijal's moon|moon]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pijal]]. The factory had minimal defenses set up to protect the facility from attacks.<ref name="MA" />

==History==
{{Quote|What is it?"''<br />''"The moon. Halin Azuca. The Opposition. Again. And it's worse this time.|Lord Regent Rael Averross and a guard, about an explosion at the factory|<ref name="MA" />}}
For some [[time]], the factory produced nutritional supplements for the Czerka Corporation and was operated by workers in the [[Standard day|daytime]]. One night<ref name="MA" /> in [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the factory was [[Blackguard campaign|destroyed]] in an explosion secretly orchestrated by the [[Pijali]] [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] and carried out by the [[blackguard]] [[soldier]]s working for her. The factory's demolition was recorded by one of Pijal's [[satellite]]s and broadcasted to a [[viewscreen]] in the [[Pijali palace]]'s [[Grand Hall (Pijal)|Grand Hall]], where it was seen by a [[Unidentified guard (Pijal)|royal guard]] stationed there. The [[Royal guard (Pijal)|guard]] assumed that the explosion was initiated by members of the [[Opposition]] performance troupe, as the group was known for engaging in several [[Opposition protests|protests]] against the upcoming signing of the [[Pijali monarchy]]'s [[Governance Treaty]]. He reported the incident to [[Lord]] [[Regent]] [[Rael Averross]] and Fanry, who rushed to the Grand Hall and viewed [[Hologram|holographic]] recordings of the factory's destruction.<ref name="MA" />

[[File:JediMasterAndPadawan-TPM.png|thumb|left|300px|Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi (pictured) investigated the factory's destruction.]]
Seeing that the incident infuriated Averross, Fanry outwardly acted horrified as well, despite having helped plan the attack. The regent's ire calmed after royal guard [[captain]] [[Deren]] confirmed that no one had [[Death|died]] in the explosion. The event prompted Averross to request assistance in protecting Fanry from the [[Jedi High Council]] on the planet [[Coruscant]], and eventually, [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], were [[Mission to Pijal|dispatched]] to aid Pijal. The [[Jedi]] pair began looking into the origins of several attacks in the [[Pijal system]] and acquired the recording of the factory's destruction from Deren to assist their efforts.<ref name="MA" />

During the Jedi pair's investigation of the Opposition, both were told by their leader, [[Halin Azucca]], that the attacks on Czerka facilities had been carried out by another group and then framed on her performance artists. Days later, the blackguards' involvement in the factory's destruction became publicly known on Pijal after Fanry publicly decried Czerka in her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]] speech and claimed responsibility for the actions of her blackguards.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Czerka Corporation factory appeared in a hologram in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
