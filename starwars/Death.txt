{{Quote|Death is a natural part of life. Rejoice for those around you who transform into the Force.|Yoda|<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>}}
[[File:OwenBeruDead-MOSW.png|thumb|right|250px|Death was a stage in life that saw the deceased become one with the Force.]]
'''Death''' was the permanent (temporary if you count the Poincaré recurrence theorem) cessation of all vital signs and functions of a living thing. When a life form died, their energy, their life passed from the [[Living Force]] to the [[Cosmic Force]] and became one with it, thus, the dead were part of [[the Force]], usually losing their individuality.<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref><ref name="Destiny" /> Death was a cause of dread and despair for most living beings, with some even vowing to defeat it and to keep people from dying, whereas others saw it as passing to the Force, a natural part of life.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref><ref name="Episode III" /> Some, like the [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]], were able to delay their spirit to be reversed into the Cosmic Force by shackling their spirit to the physical realm through [[magick]], while others, like the [[Sith Lord]]s, dreamed of [[immortality]]. [[Darth Sidious]] was able to use the [[dark side of the Force]] to [[Transference|preserve]] his essence in a [[Cloning|clone]] body. However, this technique couldn't grant him true deathlessness, as opposed to those beings who were able to maintain their consciousness after death by becoming [[Force spirit|manifestations]] of the Force.<ref name="TROS EE" />

==Causes of death==
{{Quote|Death is death, no matter the mechanism that is employed to beget it.|[[Harter Kalonia]]|<ref name="Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>}}
[[File:YodasDeath-ROTJ.png|thumb|left|250px|After nine-hundred years, Grand Master Yoda of the Jedi Order died from illness and old age.]]
Death was an inevitability and a natural part of life - although some [[species]] experienced longer lifespans than others, as was the case for [[Yoda's species]],<ref name="Chapter 6">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref><ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref> all life forms lived only for a finite period of [[time]], before they died and became one with the [[Cosmic Force]], the wellspring from which the [[Living Force]] was generated.<ref name="HRA 7">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 7|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 7]]</ref> As they grew old, beings gradually grew weaker and more vulnerable for sickness, until their bodies eventually surrender to change, resulting in natural death.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> It was also possible to physically damage a living thing so severely that, when injuries were untreated or untreatable their body was no longer able to function and it shut down, causing them to die. Living beings could be killed by each other in wars and other conflicts,<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> and by themselves as well by committing suicide, which was the act of intentionally causing one's own death.<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> [[Torture]] could also result in death.<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref> A [[Death penalty]] was a form of punishment that lead to death.<ref name="Bloodline novel">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> Living things could succumb to [[radiation]],<ref name="AEYNtK ">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> diseases,<ref name="MM">{{StoryCite|story=Mercy Mission (short story)|book=The Rise of the Empire}}</ref> starvation,<ref name="Survival Guide">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref> dehydration, or exposure to certain chemical substances.<ref name="Senate Murders">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref> However, in some cases, there was no [[medical]] explanation for death: the [[GH-7 series medical assistance droid]] at [[Polis Massa]] was unable to explain the reasons why [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] was dying during [[Reproduction|childbirth]],<ref name="Episode III" /> stating, "She's lost the will to live."<ref name="Padmé Amidala DB">{{Databank|padme-amidala|Padmé Amidala}}</ref>

==Views on Death==
===Jedi Order===
{{Quote|There is no death, there is the Force.|Jedi Code|<ref name="SotJ" />}}
[[File:YodaCouncilsAnakin-ROTS.png|thumb|right|250px|The Jedi were trained to accept death as a natural part of life and to let go of everything they feared to lose.]]
The [[Jedi Code]] stated that "there was no death," only the Force. The Jedi taught that life did not cease at death, but merely changes form in the Force.<ref name="Legacy (episode)">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> As such, the Jedi understood that life and death formed a cycle that ensured the continuation of the Force itself. Knowing that one day, all things that ever lived, must be transformed into the [[Cosmic Force]], the wellspring from which the Living Force emanated, becoming one with it, the Jedi saw death as a natural part of life, a return to the Cosmic Force.<ref name="Convergence" /> A Jedi Knight had to learn to let go of everything they fear to lose - the fear of losing the living to inevitable death was attachment, the shadow of greed, a dangerous path to the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Despite being saddened by it, they were advised to remember that one day they would all pass on, and rejoice and celebrate those around them who passed away and became one with the Force, instead of grieving and missing them.<ref name="Episode III" /><ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref>

Although everything that the Jedi knew about the Force told them that an individual retaining their identity after passing to the Cosmic Force was impossible,<ref name="Episode III" /> with the Jedi indeed not believing in the concepts of ghosts or reincarnation,<ref name="Convergence" /> there were actually ways to [[Force spirit|manifest one's consciousness]] after death as a Force spirit. [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] began discovering the secrets to manifesting his consciousness. He traveled to a [[Wellspring of Life|planet]] strong with the Force, a [[Planet|world]] that was the birthplace of life and the origin of [[midi-chlorian]]s. He learned from five [[Force Priestesses]] who had retained their consciousness after death. That knowledge was passed down to a select few: at the time when the [[Clone Wars]] was nearing to its end, knowing that was [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]'s destiny to complete the training he himself did not, Jinn was able to reach out to the old Jedi Master. Yoda completed the trials and was deemed worthy of learning the secret of eternal consciousness.<ref name="Episode III" />

After the end of the Clone Wars and the [[Order 66|fall]] of the Jedi Order, Yoda revealed [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] that Jinn had returned from the [[netherworld of the Force]] and could guide his former [[Padawan]] in learning the secret.<ref name="Episode III" /> Although [[Anakin Skywalker]]— a fallen [[Jedi Knight]] and the [[Chosen One]] of the Force—never received the training that allowed his mentors to become Force spirits, his [[Battle of Endor|redemption and sacrifice]] made it possible for his consciousness to be preserved after death by Kenobi and Yoda, much like how his grandson became a spirit in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> In [[34 ABY]] and 35 ABY respectively, [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]—Anakin's biological children—attained immortality like their [[Parent|father]].<ref name="TROS EE" />

===Sith Order===
{{Quote|There is no life after death. Only nothingness awaits you, Jedi.|Sith specters, to Yoda|<ref name="Sacrifice" />}}
[[File:Sith_specters.png|thumb|left|250px|The Sith were unable to see themselves as parts of a continuum, asserting that to die was to become nothing.]]
Although the Sith shared the Jedi's knowledge that life merely changes form in the Force,<ref name="IG-88" /> they viewed mortality as one of the limitations of the physical realm, and feared it, unable to see themselves as parts of a continuum, asserting that to die is to become nothing.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> For this reason, they sought to prevent their life from passing from the Living Force to the Cosmic Force, wishing to live forever and to hold on to the people they cared about through stopping them from dying. Thus, the Sith Order's devotion to the dark side was motivated by their desire for [[immortality]].<ref name="SotS" />

Sidious described the dark side of the Force as a pathway to abilities that others considered unnatural, in particular the power to "cheat death." According to a "[[Tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise|Sith legend]]", the Sith Lord [[Darth Plagueis]] acquired unique abilities such as the power to create life through the manipulation of [[midi-chlorian]]s, managing to keep those who cared about from dying.<ref name="Episode III" /> In his efforts to convert Anakin Skywalker to the dark side, Sidious offered to reveal this secrets of immortality to Skywalker, knowing that the Jedi Knight was fearful of losing his secret wife, Padmé Amidala.<ref name="SotS" /> Unsettled by [[Force vision|premonitions of the future]] concerning the nearing death of his pregnant wife, Skywalker came to believe through Sidious' influence that the dark side could ensure the survival of Amidala.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:SithTeachings-Convergence.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious turned Anakin Skywalker to the dark side by convincing him that the Sith could conquer death.]]
Although Sidious claimed to Skywalker that only one Sith had achieved such power, he stated, if they work together, they can unlock the secret. Skywalker ultimately joined the Sith, gaining new powers through the dark side as Darth Vader, but Amidala died nevertheless.<ref name="Episode III" /> However, despite claiming to be able to save her, Sidious later informed Vader that the dark side could not be used to restore Amidala to life. Through his master's teachings, Sidious managed to transfer his essence to an imperfect [[Cloning|clone]] vessel that had been [[Clone template|modeled]] on his original body. However His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when his granddaughter, Rey called upon the strength of past Jedi to vanquish him by turning his own [[Force lightning|power]] against him, destroying Sidious and his [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cultist|cultists]].<ref name="TROS EE" />

The Sith honed their powers by undergoing trials designed to enhance their connection to the dark side of the Force. One such trial required the sacrificial death of a loved one. This tradition was not limited to members of the Sith Order;<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Kylo Ren was not a Sith, but was trained to be just as powerful nonetheless.<ref name="SotS" /> During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]],<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> unbeknowst to him, Ren completed his Sith [[trial of ascendancy]] when he killed his father, Han Solo.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Committing patricide, however, did not lead to greater strength with the dark side, as Ren had believed. The death of his father contributed to his internal conflict, leaving Ren in a weakened state.<ref name="Kylo Ren DB">{{Databank|kylo-ren|Supreme Leader Kylo Ren}}</ref>

===Knights of Ren===
{{Quote|The price to join their fabled ranks is simple: Choose a worthy target, and give them {{'}}''a good death.''{{'}} This cost was one young Ben Solo paid in full when he struck down the group's master and assumed leadership of the Knights of Ren himself.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SotS" />}}
[[File:KarrstFilinMeetRen-TheRiseOfKyloRen1.png|thumb|left|250px|Potential recruits were required to kill a worthy target as part of their initiation in the ranks of the Knights of Ren.]]
According to legend, the [[Knights of Ren]] were a nomadic group of dark side practitioners that originated in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="SotS" /> In addition to Force-sensitivity,<ref name="Kylo Ren 1">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref> prospective recruits had to willingly kill a person in order to join the Knights' ranks.<ref name="Kylo Ren 3">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 3|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 3]]</ref> Darth Sidious described the Knights' initiation process as selecting a "worthy target" and giving them "a good death."<ref name="SotS" /> It was a requirement that applied to all potential initiates, regardless of whether they already had killed.<ref name="Kylo Ren 1" />

At one point, the Knights attempted to recruit [[Karrst]] who was hunted by [[Sector Security]] for killing at least a hundred people. He was killed by his younger brother, [[Filin]], when [[Ren (human)|Ren]]&mdash;leader of the Knights&mdash;instructed Karrst to commit fratricide in order to join the group. However, Filin lacked his brother's connection to the Force and was consequently executed by Ren.<ref name="Kylo Ren 1" />

[[File:BenKillsRen-TheRiseOfKyloRen4.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Ben Solo joined the Knights of Ren as their new master, Kylo Ren, after killing their original leader.]]
While the Knights' connection to the dark side was hindered by their lack of training, their philosophy as a group appealed to the Jedi apprentice [[Ben Solo]]<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> who, despite his initial devotion to the Jedi way, struggled to fulfill his expectations as a scion of the legendary [[Skywalker family]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> Following his role in the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker]], Solo left the Jedi Order<ref name="Kylo Ren 1" /> and tracked the Knights to [[Varnak]]. On his journey, however, he was confronted by the Jedi apprentices [[Hennix]], [[Tai]], and [[Voe]] in an abandoned [[Elphrona Outpost|Jedi outpost]] on [[Elphrona]]. Although Hennix was [[Battle of Elphrona|killed]] by Solo, the Knights' deemed his death insufficient and suspected that Solo had not wanted his former ally to die.<ref name="Kylo Ren 3" />

After the death of Hennix, the Jedi tracked Solo and the Knights while they were on a [[Skirmish on the Minemoon|mission]] to the [[Minemoon]]. Tai was killed by Ren, who was killed in turn by Solo.<ref name="Kylo Ren 4">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]]</ref> The death of Ren secured not only Solo's place among the Knights' ranks, but also his status as Ren's successor.<ref name="SotS" /> In addition to adopting the name [[Ren|name]] Kylo Ren, the former Jedi assumed the title of [[Ren|master of the Knights of Ren]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Death and culture==
===Disposal of remains===
====Funerary customs====
{{Quote|I remember every time it happened, every time the dead lifted me&hellip; With their truth. And now I'm dead. And I yearn to lift you.|Maarva Carassi Andor's funeral speech|<ref name="RR">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:MaarvaAddressesProcession-Andor112.png|thumb|left|250px|The loss of a loved one was generally observed by a period of grieving.]]
When a person died, it was common for people affected by the loss to shed tears. [[Funeral|Ceremonies]] to mark the end of the person's life were also common.<ref name="IG-88">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – IG-88 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – IG-88'' 1]]</ref> [[Burial]] and [[cremation]] were examples of funeral customs.<ref name="Convergence" /> On [[Ferrix]], it was customary to create [[funerary stone]]s out of the remains of prominent Ferrixians, such as members of the [[Daughters of Ferrix]].<ref name="RR" /> A funerary [[stone]] was made by mixing the ashes of the deceased with mortar and local dust,<ref name="DF">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> creating a solid brick with the citizen's name as well as their birth and death&mdash;dated according to the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar]]&mdash;inscribed on it. Another custom of Ferrix's death ritual was for the deceased to create a prerecorded [[Hologram|holographic]] speech to be played at their funeral. [[Maarva Carassi Andor]], a former [[president]] of the Daughters of Ferrix, used her funeral to instigate a [[Ferrix Riot|local uprising]] against the [[Ferrix garrison|Imperial]] [[Ferrix occupation|occupation of Ferrix]].<ref name="RR" />

On the planet [[E'ronoh]], the dead were not buried nor cremated, but instead were placed within the tombs of [[the Rook]] to showcase all were equal through death. Even members of the [[E'ronoh royal family]] were placed in the Rook upon death. The family or friends of the deceased wrote a byline for the coffin, although visitor [[Axel Greylark]] pondered who would get to write the line if no friends or family could be found. A robed [[priest]] gave a final blessing before the coffin was inserted into its resting place. Welcoming a crowd of mourners, a grand funeral was held for [[Captain]] [[Jerrod Segaru]] to honor the man as a war hero. However, another dead soldier, [[Braxen]], did not receive the same level of honor. At Segaru's funeral, [[Axel Greylark]] asked [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] if she found the display morbid, to which she answered that death was morbid for most people. Nattai also shocked at how glib Greylark was about the funeral before him.<ref name="Convergence" />

Burial<ref name="Episode II" /> and cremation<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> were practiced on the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]]. [[Shmi Skywalker Lars]] was buried in a grave on the [[Lars homestead|homestead]] of the [[Lars family]]. Her [[Funeral of Shmi Skywalker Lars|funeral]] was attended by her son, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] Anakin Skywalker, her husband, [[Cliegg Lars]], and her stepson, [[Owen Lars]], as well as Amidala and [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru Whitesun]].<ref name="Episode II" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the [[droid]] [[C-3PO]] used a [[Fire|pyre]] to dispose the bodies of [[Jawa]] [[scavenger]]s who were killed by [[Stormtrooper|Imperial stormtroopers]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

[[File:PadmeFuneral-RotS.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Senator Padmé Amidala received a state funeral on her homeworld of Naboo.]]
A [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|state funeral]]<ref name="Padmé Amidala BG29">{{Databank|padm-amidala-biography-gallery|Padmé Amidala Biography Gallery}} {{C|slide 29}}</ref> was held for [[Senator]] Padmé Amidala, a former [[monarch of Naboo]], on her [[homeworld]] of [[Naboo]]<ref name="Episode III" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Regarded as a "beloved [[representative]]" by [[Naboo (people)|her people]], thousands of Naboo citizens&mdash;humans and [[Gungan]]s alike&mdash;attended the ceremony to honor the late senator.<ref name="Padmé Amidala BG29" /> After her death on [[Polis Massa]], Amidala's funeral procession included [[Naberrie family|family members]] and other significant people, such as [[Queen]] [[Apailana]], [[Rugor Nass]], and [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The [[Tomb of Padmé Amidala]] served as her final resting place.<ref name="Darth Vader 4">[[Darth Vader (2020) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 4]]</ref> During the Imperial Era, Amidala's remains were nearly exhumed by her [[Marriage|widower]], [[Darth]] Vader,<ref name="Darth Vader 5">[[Darth Vader (2020) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 5]]</ref> despite the [[Amidalans]]' efforts to [[Battle at Padmé's tomb|prevent]] what they saw as the desecration of her grave.<ref name="Darth Vader 4" /> While he nearly opened her casket, Vader found himself unable to carry through with the act, with his droid companion [[ZED-6-7]] assuming he did not want to damage any evidence. Despite only slightly opening her casket, Zed-6-7 was able to trace a [[med implant]] present on her body to Polis Massa.<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />

====Jedi funerary practices====
{{Main|Jedi funeral}}
{{Quote|One with the Force, they are. And our job it is to remember that we will in time, also pass on. Luminous beings are we but temporary vessels, our bodies are. And we shall all find ourselves here in time. A moment of silence, I ask to remember and to move on.|Yoda|<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref>}}
[[File:FuneralofKatri-Choices.png|thumb|left|250px|The Jedi Order knew that all life-forms became one with the Force after death.]]
The Jedi Order practiced cremation in its funerary rituals<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref> for all Jedi.<ref name="Convergence" /> During the [[Republic Era]], in which the Order reached the zenith of its power, the remains of dead Jedi&mdash;either placed in a sarcophagus<ref name="Choices">{{TOTJ|Choices}}</ref> or on an altar and covered by a burial shroud<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />&mdash;were lowered into a chamber beneath the<ref name="Choices" /> consecrated ground of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on Coruscant. After being lowered, a beam of light arose from the ground,<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" /> and the Jedi was cremated. Upon death, a Jedi's [[kyber crystal]] was also taken from their [[lightsaber]] and placed within the [[Kyber Arch]].<ref name="Convergence" />

Although the Jedi Order usually retrieved the remains of their fallen brethren for funeral services to be held in the Coruscant temple,<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" /> as they did with the body of Jedi Master [[Katri]] who was killed on [[Raxus Secundus]],<ref name="Choices" /> sometimes the ceremony was conducted off-world. In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="GA" /> Jedi Master [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] was cremated in [[Theed]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Naboo]], following his death during the [[Battle of Naboo|battle]] that ended the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo]]. The body of [[Jedi General]] [[Even Piell]], who was killed during the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]], was wrapped in a shroud, and, with insufficient time and a priority to leave from [[Lola Sayu]], was lowered into a river of [[Magma|lava]] by Jedi [[General]]s Kenobi and Skywalker.

[[File:FuneralOfQuiGonJinn-TPM.png|thumb|right|250px|Following the Invasion of Naboo, Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn's body was cremated on a funeral pyre.]]
Jedi funeral ceremonies were open to those who were not members of the Jedi Order. [[Politics|Government]] officials of the [[Galactic Republic]], such as the [[Supreme Chancellor]] and members of the [[Galactic Senate]], and during the Clone Wars, [[Military rank|high-ranking]] [[Republic officer|officers]] of the [[Republic Military]]<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" /> might attend to pay their respects to the fallen Jedi. The attendees of Jinn's funeral included his former apprentice, Jedi Knight Obi-Wan Kenobi and his Padawan, Anakin Skywalker, the Jedi High Council, the [[Supreme Chancellor election, 32 BBY|newly elected]] Supreme Chancellor [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], Queen Padmé Amidala and [[Governor]] [[Sio Bibble]] of the [[Royal House of Naboo]], and [[Boss]] Rugor Nass of the [[Gungan High Council]].<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> When Obi-Wan Kenobi faked his death so he could uncover a looming [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] plot to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine, his funeral service was observed by various members of the Order, including the [[Jedi High Council]], Senator Amidala and [[Satine Kryze|Duchess Satine]].<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref>

Carried out by Skywalker and Kenobi, Even Piell's cremation was observed by Jedi Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Captain]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], and their clone [[trooper]]s, including [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], the [[Advanced Recon Commando]] [[Fives]], and [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]].<ref name="Citadel Rescue" /> Later in the war, one of the Jedi Temple's [[starship]] [[hangar]]s was [[bomb]]ed in a [[terrorist]] [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|attack]] on the Jedi Order, killing several Jedi as well as clone troopers and Temple workers.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> A [[Funeral of the Jedi Temple bombing victims|service]] was held for the Jedi victims who were killed in the attack, with Yoda delivering their eulogy before an assembly of Jedi and Republic officials.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" /> The [[Funeral of Tu-Anh]] was held on the planet [[Utapau]], where she was found deceased. The funeral was witnessed by Skywalker, Kenobi, and several [[Pau'an]]s.<ref name="A Death on Utapau">{{TCW|A Death on Utapau}}</ref>

[[File:Anakinfuneral.png|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker received a Jedi funeral after he died renouncing the persona of Darth Vader.]]
Anakin Skywalker was given a [[Funeral of Anakin Skywalker|Jedi funeral]] by his son, Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker, in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]], during the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Episode VI" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="GA" /> The ceremony occurred on the [[forest]]ed [[moon]] of [[Endor]] with Luke as the only attendee. The newly anointed Jedi Knight had built a funeral pyre to burn [[Darth Vader's armor]], which his [[Parent|father]] had worn during his time as a [[Sith]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> In spite of Anakin's actions as Vader,<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> including his role in the [[Great Jedi Purge|near-extinction]] of the Jedi Order,<ref name="Episode IV" /> Luke chose to remember his father for the Jedi he once was,<ref name="Bloodline" /> and the [[Chosen One]] who restored balance to the Force.<ref name="SotJ">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>

====Sith burial====
{{Quote|Why do you come to my tomb, Jedi?|A specter of Darth Bane, to Yoda|<ref name="Sacrifice">{{TCW|Sacrifice (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Darth_Banes_tomb.png|thumb|right|250px|The ancient Sith Lord Darth Bane was buried on the Sith homeworld of Moraband.]]
Deceased Sith [[Lord]]s were buried on their ancient homeworld of [[Moraband]], in the [[Valley of the Dark Lords]] that contained monuments and crypts dedicated to the ancient Sith "of [[Millennium|millennia]] past."<ref name="Moraband DB">{{Databank|moraband|Moraband}}</ref> The [[Sith temple (Valley of the Dark Lords)|largest structure]] in the Valley contained<ref name="SWE Moraband">{{SWE|locations|moraband|Moraband}}</ref> the [[Tomb of Darth Bane|tomb]] of [[Darth Bane]],<ref name="Sacrifice Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sacrifice-episode-gallery|text="Sacrifice" Episode Gallery}} {{C|slide 4}}</ref> an ancient Sith Lord<ref name="Sacrifice" /> who founded the modern Sith Order through the [[Rule of Two]].<ref name="Moraband DB" /> The stone sarcophagus that held his remains was located at the base of a large statue of Bane.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> He was the last [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] to be buried on Moraband prior to its abandonment by the Sith.<ref name="SWEBane">{{SWE|characters|darth-bane|Darth Bane}}</ref>

The spirits of the Sith who were entombed on Moraband remained connected to their Order's homeworld. They lived eternally after their death, according to their successor, Darth Sidious, whom they communed with. In his [[book]], ''[[The Secrets of the Sith]]'', Sidious stated his intention to visit Moraband and discover the "dark secrets" of his Sith predecessors.<ref name="SotS">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>

===Remembrance of the deceased===
Although it was harder to maintain the tradition with the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|Forever War]] taking up so much, the [[E'roni]] of the planet E'ronoh practiced the concept of a [[E'ronoh Remembrance|remembrance]] for the recently deceased, where the deceased individual's family would gather together to celebrate their fallen loved one. In rural areas, such Remembrances were held by the entire town. Stories of the deceased were spread as their favorite songs and meals filled the celebration. On E'ronoh's rival world of Eiram, the Eirami, while they also believed in reincarnation, had a similar celebration centered on one day of the year, where the entire planet came together in remembrance of the deceased.<ref name="Convergence" />

===Presumption of death===
{{Quote|Why is he talking about you like you're dead?!"<br />"Because I was on Starlight Beacon when it fell and then went missing, so I was legally declared dead. My death was politically convenient for uncle Tragkul. He used it to push his anti-Republic agenda. And falling in battle is not an honorable way to die, according to Tragkul anyway, because it means we lost. So not only am I technically dead, I'm also out of favor.|Zeen Mrala and Sevran Tarkin, commenting on Tragkul Tarkin|<ref name="High Republic Adventures 2023 9">[[The High Republic Adventures 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]]</ref>}}
A presumption of death occured when an individual was presumed to be deceased, even in the absence of direct evidence confirming the person's demise.  In the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Galactic Republic]] maintained a longstanding automated protocol that stipulated individuals who were missing and presumed dead would be officially declared deceased. Consequently, [[Padawan|Jedi Padawan]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] was officially declared dead [[229 BBY|one year]] after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="High Republic Adventures 2023 1">[[The High Republic Adventures 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 1]]</ref> 

[[Sevran Tarkin]] was on [[Starlight Beacon]] when it fell and subsequently went missing, which led to her being legally pronounced dead. Her uncle, [[Tragkul Tarkin]], exploited her demise to advance his anti-Republic agenda. After Sevran turned up alive and well, she found that establishing her status as alive was considerably more intricate than one might assume. The bureaucratic hurdles became a significant obstacle, leaving her still regarded as "technically dead." She fell out of favor with her uncle, who held the belief that dying in battle was not a noble death, as it signified their defeat.<ref name="High Republic Adventures 2023 9" />

==Death and the Force==
{{Quote|Twilight is upon me, and soon night must fall. That is the way of things&hellip; the way of the Force.|Yoda|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
[[File:Alderaanexplosion.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The loss of life on a large scale could cause a disturbance in the Force, the energy field that was generated by life.]]
The Force was a mystical energy field generated by all life-forms.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> It consisted of two aspects, the [[Living Force]] and the [[Cosmic Force]] that co-existed in a state of [[symbiosis]] and balance.<ref name="TSWB" /> The Living Force was generated by life-forms, according to Qui-Gon Jinn, which powered the wellspring, the Cosmic Force in turn.<ref name="Voices" /> Once a life ended, its [[Soul|spirit]] would ascend to a different [[Netherworld of the Force|plane of existence]] and become one with the Cosmic Force.<ref name="Destiny">{{TCW|Destiny (The Clone Wars)}}</ref>

Mass death created [[Disturbance in the Force|disturbances in the Force]], perceivable [[galaxy]]-wide by Force-sensitives.<ref name="Episode IV" /> During the last [[Standard day|days]] of the Clone Wars, Yoda sensed the deaths of Jedi throughout the galaxy, a result of the Jedi Order's systematic extermination under [[Order 66]].<ref name="Episode III" /> When Kenobi sensed the planet [[Alderaan]]'s [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]], he described it as a terrible feeling in which "millions of voices cried out in terror and were then silenced."<ref name="Episode IV" />

==Extinction==
{{Quote|They're dead. All of them.|Ezra Bridger, on the Geonosians|<ref name="The Honorable Ones">{{Rebels|The Honorable Ones}}</ref>}}
[[File:AphraGeonosis-2015DarthVader4.png|thumb|left|250px|The Geonosian species was exterminated by the Galactic Empire.]]
Extinction was the mass death of an entire species. Despite their technological advancement, the [[Zeffonian]]s became an endangered species as a result of the dark side's increasing influence over the [[Zeffo Sage]]s. With their race on the brink of extinction, the surviving Zeffo retreated toward unknown [[Realspace|space]], abandoning [[Zeffo|their homeworld]] and its [[Zeffo system|system]].<ref name="SWJ:FO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> The deadly [[Blue Shadow Virus]] was purposely driven to extinction, though it was reintroduced to the galaxy by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]].<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref> The [[Lasat]] species was driven to near-extinction by the forces of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Legends of the Lasat">{{Rebels|Legends of the Lasat}}</ref> The Empire drove the [[Geonosian]] species to [[Sterilization of Geonosis|extinction]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref>

Genocide was the intentional mass-killing of a species or culture that led to that group's extinction. The Separatist [[Atrocity on Mahranee|attack]] on the planet [[Mahranee]]<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> of [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Mahranee date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[A Death on Utapau]]" and [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] in [[19 BBY]]. As ''Dark Disciple'' takes place between these two pieces of media according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, the events of the novel, which include the attack on [[Mahranee]], must also take place in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> was considered a genocide of the indigenous [[Mahran]] species.<ref name="The Dark Side">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref>

==Attempts to cheat death==
===Eternal life===
{{Quote|For centuries, our Order has seen the dark side of the Force as a means of achieving immortality.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SotS" />}}
[[File:ALifeImmortal.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sanguis and Darth Noctyss became abominations in their pursuit of deathlessness according to legend.]]
According to the legend of the ancient Sith Lord [[Darth Noctyss]], there was a long-forgotten Sith ritual, throughly recorded in an abandoned citadel at the heart of [[Exegol]], that could make one immortal. The ritual required three days of incantations and slaying a willing sacrifice, so its essence could be absorbed and used to remold its performer's soul. However, even though the ritual gave everlasting life, it left the one who performed it as a twisted, grotesque version of their former self, cursed to vegetate as a feral creature, so broken that it barely cling to existence: to live a life undying and yet unable to truly live.<ref name="A Life Immortal">{{StoryCite|story=A Life Immortal|book=Dark Legends}}</ref>

Oblivious to the price, the Sith [[Darth Sanguis]], yearning for eternal life, researched and completed the ritual. After ages, Darth Noctyss, searching for a way to make herself immortal, traveled to Exegol and discovered the [[Darth Sanguis' laboratory|laboratory]] and research of Sanguis and her forebear himself, although she believed, the miserable creature was a [[Slavery|slave]] of the Sith. Sanguis helped Noctyss to recreate the ritual, allowing himself to be sacrificially killed by her, so he could be freed from his tortured existence. The ritual ultimately caused Noctyss to become a deformed, broken creature, like Sanguis before her, haunting the bowels of Exegol.<ref name="A Life Immortal" />

===Possessed masks===
{{Quote|Upon his death, Momin's very essence transferred through the Force into the mask he had worn. Now, when any living being wears this ancient helm, the sinister spirit trapped within takes control.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SotS" />}}
[[File:MominHelmetActivated-Lando3.png|thumb|left|180px|The mask of Darth Momin was possessed by the spirit of its creator.]]
[[Sith mask]]s were designed to induce fear in their wearers' opponents, but could also serve as vessels for the spirits of the Sith Lords who created them. One such Sith [[Helmet|mask]] was the [[Mask of Lord Momin]], an ancient [[Sith artifact]] created by the unorthodox Sith Lord [[Darth Momin]]. After his [[Darth Momin's destruction of a city|death]], Momin's spirit transferred to his mask and was trapped within it. As a result, however, he had the ability to control any living being who wore it.<ref name="SotS" /> The Jedi stored Momin's mask in the vaults of the [[Jedi temple|Temple]] on Coruscant; it was reclaimed by the Sith following the [[Operation: Knightfall|siege of the Jedi Temple]] and Sidious' ascent to power as [[Galactic Emperor]].<ref name="Dark Lord 12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref>

Momin initially tried to possess Sidious' [[Sith apprentice]], Darth Vader, before agreeing to serve as the [[architect]] of [[Fortress Vader]] on [[Mustafar]]. Throughout its time in Vader's possession, the mask possessed several hosts in turn,<ref name="Dark Lord 22">[[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]]</ref> beginning with [[Lieutenant]] [[Roggo]] who killed [[Colonel]] [[Alva Brenne]] while under Momin's influence.<ref name="Dark Lord 21">[[Darth Vader (2017) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 21]]</ref> Upon the Fortress' completion, Momin opened a [[portal]] through which he regained his original body.<ref name="Dark Lord 23">[[Darth Vader (2017) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 23]]</ref> His resurrection was short-lived, as he was killed by Vader during the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]].<ref name="Dark Lord 24">[[Darth Vader (2017) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 24]]</ref>

[[File:ShadowofSith-Kizamasked.png|thumb|right|230px|Kiza was possessed by the spirit of Exim Panshard, whose Sith mask served as his link to the realm of the living.]]
The [[mask of Viceroy Exim Panshard]] was a Sith artifact composed of [[Meteorite|meteoritic]] metal and strongly attuned to the dark side of the Force. The screams of a hundred people were imprinted on the mask after they had been killed for the pleasure of [[Viceroy]] [[Exim Panshard]], a Sith Lord who ruled over a [[Unidentified planet (Exim Panshard)|forgotten planet]]. Panshard's spirit survived through the mask and its connection to the dark side. The mask was given to [[Kiza]], a member of the [[Sith cult]] known as the [[Acolytes of the Beyond]], by the [[Emperor's adviser]] [[Yupe Tashu]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> Possessing the mask enabled Kiza to hear the voice of Panshard in her mind, which, in turn, caused the [[Pantoran]] acolyte to succumb to the dark side's influence. The dark side enhanced Kiza's [[blood]]lust to the point where she killed her fellow acolyte, [[Remi]], in a fit of rage when he tried to take [[Kiza's lightsaber]] during their [[Attack on a New Republic outpost|attack]] on the [[New Republic]]'s [[Devaron]] [[Devaron New Republic outpost|outpost]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

Through the mask, Kiza continued to commune with the spirit of Panshard who promised her greater power if they could locate the Sith world of Exegol. At Panshard's direction, Kiza established her [[Kiza's world|domain]] on a large [[asteroid]] near the [[Red Honeycomb Zone]] and formed an [[Kiza's droid army|army]] of [[battle droid]]s that she acquired from an [[Unidentified Separatist core ship|old]] [[Core ship|Separatist core ship]]. The mask was destroyed<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> in [[21 ABY]]<ref name="21 ABY">The events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'', which include the destruction of the mask of [[Viceroy]] [[Exim Panshard]] on [[Taw Provode's solitary moon]], takes place in [[21 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> when Kiza fought against Jedi Master Luke Skywalker on [[Taw Provode]]'s [[Taw Provode's solitary moon|solitary moon]]. Without the mask, Panshard lost his connection to the physical plane.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

===Transference===
{{Quote|I have died before. The dark side of the Force is a pathway&hellip; to many abilities some consider to be&hellip; unnatural.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>}}
According to a "[[Tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise|Sith legend]]", the Sith Lord [[Darth Plagueis]] acquired unique abilities such as the power to create life through the manipulation of [[midi-chlorian]]s.<ref name="Episode III" /> Having trained as Plagueis' Sith apprentice, Darth Sidious attested to the achievements of his [[Sith Master]], the first in their Order's history to discover the Force's ability to prevent the deaths of living beings.<ref name="SotS" /> Sidious credited the discovery of this power<ref name="Episode III" />&mdash;a technique known as [[transference]]<ref name="SotS" />&mdash;to his mentor's extensive knowledge of the dark side of the Force,<ref name="Episode III" /> despite which he failed to survive his apprentice's betrayal.<ref name="SotS" />

[[File:DarthSidiousExegol-TROS.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Darth Sidious returned from death by transferring his consciousness to a clone of his original body.]]
After the death of Plagueis, Sidious studied the methods of his late Master until he learned to transfer his essence to a new body.<ref name="SotS" /> As a result, Sidious was resurrected within a clone of his original body after his first demise. However, not only was the process imperfect,<ref name="TROS EE">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> the cloning methods of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cultist|cultists]] failed to produce a viable specimen from his [[DNA|genetic]] [[Clone template|template]].<ref name="SotS" /> The resulting clone was [[gene]]tically unstable and vulnerable to the effects of the dark side,<ref name="TROS EE" /> consequently preventing Sidious from leaving the [[Sith Citadel]] on Exegol due to the deformities of his cloned body.<ref name="SotS" />

The transference ability allowed the Force-user to send their consciousness "from one mortal vessel to another," according to Sidious,<ref name="SotS" /> who was at risk of dying again because his new vessel was compromised at the genetic level.<ref name="TROS EE" /> As such, the immortality he coveted remained elusive.<ref name="TSWB" />

===Witchcraft===
{{Quote|The Army of the Dead. Our reinforcements.|Asajj Ventress|<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref>}}
[[File:Undead_Nightsister.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Nightsisters used witchcraft to raise an undead army on Dathomir.]]
The Nightsisters of Dathomir kept their dead close by, suspended in pods near their [[Nightsister lair|village]], so that no sister ever truly left the clan. They believed that the dead could share in the emotions of the living, and with a magical [[Chant of Resurrection|chant]] could temporarily be reanimated in the form of [[Nightsister Zombie|undead warriors]]. The Nightsisters resurrected [[Army of the Dead|their dead]] to fight the [[Battle of Dathomir]], but they were ultimately destroyed by [[General]] [[Grievous]] and the [[Separatist Droid Army]].<ref name="Massacre" />

When the body of the Nightsister [[Asajj Ventress]] was laid to rest in the pools of the village, a green mist arose as the voices of her sisters welcomed her home.<ref name="DarkDisciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> After death, the spirits of the Nightsisters were tethered to the [[Nightsisters' altar|altar]] in the Nightsister lair. Several years later during the Rise of the Empire, the Nightsister [[Merrin]], the sole survivor of Grievous's attack, used the undead Nightsisters to [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|attack]] the Jedi [[Cal Kestis]]. She believed that the Jedi had orchestrated the attack because of the lies of the former Jedi Master [[Taron Malicos]].<ref name="SWJ:FO" />

The Nightsisters' spirits were awoken by [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Maul]]. Two Nightsister spirits possessed two of Bridger's friends, [[Sabine Wren]] and [[Kanan Jarrus]], in an effort to return to the physical world and rebuild their clan and as price for their aid to Maul and Bridger. However, Bridger destroyed the altar, and consequently, the spirits dissipated.<ref name="Visions and Voices">{{Rebels|Visions and Voices}}</ref>

===Experimentation===
{{Quote|You're telling me those Sith scientists, this Project Blackwing, created Death Troopers? On purpose?|[[Saponza]], on Project Blackwing|<ref name="SWC" />}}
[[File:Death_Troopers_mash.png|thumb|left|250px|The reanimation of corpses into the undead was the result of Project Blackwing.]]
Project Blackwing was an experiment conducted by [[Sith scientist]]s<ref name="SWC" /> working for the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]]. The project's ultimate goal was to achieve immortality through science and [[Sith alchemy]]. In their efforts to revive necrotic tissue,<ref name="Rogue One VG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> the [[scientist]]s inadvertently created a virus capable of [[Zombie|reanimating]] infected hosts. In addition to reanimation, other side effects of the virus included aggression and cannibalism. An [[Undead Troopers invasion|outbreak]] in the [[Research facility (Blackwing)|Blackwing research facility]] on [[Dandoran]] resulted in the infection of multiple [[stormtrooper]]s who succumbed to a painful death before reanimating as "Undead Troopers."<ref name="SWC" />

==Transcending death==
===Immortality===
{{Quote|You will learn to preserve your Life Force, and so manifest a consciousness which will allow you to commune with the living after death.|Qui-Gon Jinn, to Yoda|<ref name="Voices" />}}
{{Main|Force spirit}}
[[File:LeiaLukeForceGhost-TSWB.png|thumb|right|250px|Force-sensitives could preserve their consciousness by becoming Force spirits after death.]]
Through special training, it was possible to preserve their conscious individuality beyond death.<ref name="TSWB" /> The Jedi who preserved their consciousness became manifestations of the Force,<ref name="Voices" /> allowing them to achieve a state of immortality<ref name="Episode III" /> as Force spirits<ref name="Episode V" /> after their physical bodies disappeared upon death.<ref name="TSWB" />

Qui-Gon Jinn was the first to learn the ability to retain his consciousness after death,<ref name="TSWB" /> but was incapable of appearing to the living in consequence of his incomplete training. As a result, he could only commune with Yoda as a disembodied spirit.<ref name="Voices" /> In time, however, Jinn could appear as a spirit which he did once his former Padawan Obi-Wan Kenobi was "ready" to see him.<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref> Jinn shared his knowledge with both Yoda and Kenobi.<ref name="TSWB" /> After his [[Rescue of Princess Leia|death]] in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> Kenobi continued to offer guidance to Luke Skywalker during his [[Jedi training]].<ref name="Episode V" /> Skywalker and his [[twin]] sister, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo]], became Force spirits after their deaths as well.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Anakin Skywalker achieved the state of a Force spirit as a result of his [[Light side of the Force|redemption]]; by sacrificing his life to save his son, Luke Skywalker, the spirits of Yoda and Kenobi were able to preserve Anakin's consciousness after his death.<ref name="Anakin Skywalker BG22">{{Databank|anakin-skywalker-biography-gallery|Anakin Skywalker Biography Gallery}} {{C|slide 22}}</ref> The physical body of Ben Solo, Anakin's grandson and the last of the Skywalker bloodline,<ref name="TSWB" /> disappeared<ref name="Episode IX" /> following his conversion to the light and subsequent [[Battle of Exegol|death]] on Exegol.<ref name="TSWB" />

[[File:BenSoloHealsRey-TROS.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Ben Solo resurrected Rey by sacrificing his life for her.]]
Some Jedi discovered the ability to revive a being from death.<ref name="Altar of Mortis">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref><ref name="Episode IX" /> During the [[Mission to Mortis|mission]] on [[Mortis]], Anakin Skywalker resuscitated his Padawan Ahsoka Tano by transferring the life energy of the [[Daughter]] to Tano after the [[Force wielder]] was fatally injured by the [[Son]].<ref name="Altar of Mortis" /> After [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] sacrificed her life in the destruction of Darth Sidious, Ben Solo [[Force healing|healed]] her by transferring his energy through their [[Force-bond|bond]] as a [[Force dyad]]. This resulted in Rey's resurrection at the cost of Solo's life.<ref name="TSWB" />

==Scientific ways to sustain life==
{{Quote|Luke. Help me take this mask off.''"<br />"''But you'll die.''"<br />"''Nothing can stop that now.|Anakin Skywalker and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
[[File:DarthVader-Strata.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Vader relied on his armor's life support systems in order to remain alive.]]
A dying person could be kept alive with the use of [[life support]] machinery.<ref name="Star Wars 48">[[Star Wars (2015) 48|''Star Wars'' (2015) 48]]</ref> Darth Vader was forced to survive on life support after his near [[Fire|immolation]] on the [[Volcano|volcanic]] world of Mustafar, where he was [[Duel on Mustafar|defeated]] by Obi-Wan Kenobi. In addition to the dismemberment of his [[limb]]s, Vader's body was severely burned and his [[lung]]s irreparably damaged.<ref name="Darth Vader DB">{{Databank|darth-vader|Darth Vader}}</ref> Darth Sidious' [[Imperial shock trooper]]s used a [[medical capsule]] to safely deliver the [[Emperor]]'s injured apprentice to the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility]] on Coruscant. The facility's [[medical droid]]s surgically reconstructed Vader as an [[armor]]ed [[cyborg]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[Cybernetics]] not only compensated the loss of his organic arms and legs,<ref name="Episode III" /> but also assisted his breathing of [[oxygen]] through mechanical lungs.<ref name="Darth Vader DB" /> A suit of armor was specially designed for the Dark Lord by [[Doctor]] [[Cylo]] and other Imperial scientists.<ref name="Darth Vader 20">[[Darth Vader (2015) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 20]]</ref> The suit included a masked helmet that functioned in conjunction with the rest of the armor to regulate Vader's artificial respiratory system. Because of the armor's fear-inducing visage, only a few beings in the galaxy were aware of its actual function<ref name="SotS" /> and the fact that Vader was unable to breathe without his mask.<ref name="AoR-General Hux">[[Age of Resistance - General Hux 1|''Age of Resistance - General Hux'' 1]]</ref>

The [[King of Mon Cala]] [[Lee-Char]] was placed on life support sometime after the Galactic Empire [[Invasion of Mon Cala|invaded]] and [[Occupation of Mon Cala|occupied]] the planet [[Mon Cala]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] [[king]] was discovered in an [[Unidentified Imperial base (Strokill Prime)|Imperial base]] on [[Strokill Prime]] by [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Organa noted the king's condition, stating that machines were all that prevented him from dying.<ref name="Star Wars 48" />
