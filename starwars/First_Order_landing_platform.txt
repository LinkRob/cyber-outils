{{Quote|We need that platform, Karé. There's nowhere to land on this planet&mdash;It's all mountains and ravines. If we stay here too long, we'll run out of fuel. But if we can get it we'll have a landing spot for our ships, a chance to refuel and control over the only way in and out of that cave.|Black Squadron pilot Temmin Wexley, to fellow pilot Karé Kun|<ref name="PD2" />}}
During the [[Mission to Ovanis]], the [[First Order]] used a model of '''landing platform''' as a [[staging area]] to launch both [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s and [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s as well as [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|atmospheric assault landers]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:MaximaACruiser-PoeDameron2.png|thumb|right|250px|First Order landing platforms could be deployed from Maxima-A-class heavy cruisers]]
The [[First Order]] landing platform was a type of flatbed [[starship]] used for the transportation of [[starfighter]]s and troops.<ref name="PD2" /> The platform was a simple rectangular flat starship, distinguished by a pair of frontal fins and a single off-center [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. The platform was armed with 3 ventral twin [[laser cannon]]s and one twin laser cannon mounted next to the bridge.<ref name="PD1" /> The First Order landing platform could be deployed by a larger [[cruiser]], such as a [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|Maxima-A class heavy cruiser]].<ref name="PD2" />

==Role==
Similar to<ref name="Dark Lord 15">[[Darth Vader (2017) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 15]]</ref> their [[Galactic Empire|predecessor's]]<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> [[aerial landing platform]],<ref name="Dark Lord 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref> the First Order's landing platform could be utilized to transport troops and various starcraft and to act as a [[staging area]].<ref name="PD1" /> It provided [[fuel]] and acted as a landing platform for [[starfighter]]s on [[mountain]]ous [[planet]]s where it would otherwise be difficult.<ref name="PD2" />

The platform could carry an assortment of 8 [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE/fo]] or [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter|TIE/sf]] space superiority fighters, along with an additional two [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport]]<ref name="PD1" /> and at least 1 [[First Order speeder bike]].<ref name="PD2" />

The First Order landing platform carried a crew of at least 14.<ref name="PD1" /><ref name="PD2" />

During the [[Cold War]], the military junta<ref name="Abrams Luke Skywalker">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ew.com/article/2015/08/12/star-wars-luke-skywalker-hooked-jj-abrams/2|text=''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'': J.J. Abrams explores 'Who IS Luke Skywalker &hellip;?'|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2015-08-12|archivedate=20200607192519}}</ref> known as the First Order employed at least one of these landing platforms. Around [[33 ABY]],<ref name="Twitter note">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1046802728788226048|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=Off the top of my head, I'd say issue 1 is at least a year off from TFA.|archivefile=File:TFADATE_TWITTER.png}} [[Matt Martin]] states on Twitter that [[Poe Dameron 1|''Poe Dameron'' 1]] takes place at least one [[Standard year|year]] before [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that the events of Episode VII take place in [[34 ABY]], then the events of ''Poe Dameron 1'' must take place around [[33 ABY]].</ref> during the [[Resistance]]'s [[Mission to Ovanis|mission]] to [[Ovanis]], the First Order deployed this platform from the Maxima-A-class [[heavy cruiser]], ''[[Ravenous]]'',<ref name="PD3">[[Poe Dameron 3|''Poe Dameron'' 3]]</ref> to the surface of Ovanis to reinforce [[Agent]] [[Terex]] during his hunt for the Resistance [[pilot]] [[Poe Dameron]]. The platform brought with it 2 TIE/fo space superiority fighters, 6 TIE/sf space superiority fighters, 2 AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transports, 2 [[Unidentified flametrooper transport|single-person transports]], a First Order speeder bike, and a squadron of First Order [[First Order stormtrooper|Stormtroopers]] consisting of at least 2 [[First Order flametrooper|Flametroopers]], 8 [[First Order jet trooper]]s, and 2 [[First Order Special Forces]].<ref name="PD1" /><ref name="PD2" />

The landing platform floated above Ovanis' [[rock]]y surface, blocking the entrance to a [[Cave of the Crèche|cave]], cutting off Resistance starfighter group [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] from their [[commander]], Poe Dameron, who at the time was inside the cave negotiating with the native [[Crèche]].<ref name="PD1" /> Dameron was unaware of the First Order's arrival, or that Agent Terex was in puruit with a squadron of troopers. In a bid to take the landing platform for themselves and gain the upper hand, Black Squadron attacked the platform, destroying the First Order's [[TIE series|TIE]] support. However, during the assault the landing platform was destroyed. The debris from the destruction blocked the opening to the Crèche cave, trapping Poe Dameron with the First Order.<ref name="PD2" />
