The '''Dug Council''',<ref name="AotG" /> also known as the '''Malastarian council''',<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-zillo-beast-episode-guide|text="The Zillo Beast" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> was the [[planet]]ary governing body of the [[Dug]] [[species]] on [[Malastare]]. A certain number of delegates represented their respective tribal nations on the council, which was ruled by a [[doge]].<ref name="AotG" /> The Dug Council's seat of power was the [[Imperial palace (Malastare)|Imperial palace]], a conical building that overlooked a cleared area of land home to [[fuel]] refineries. At the time of the [[Clone Wars]], the Council was chaired by Doge [[Nakha Urus]].<ref name="ZB">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref>
