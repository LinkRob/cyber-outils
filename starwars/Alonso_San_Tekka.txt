'''Alonso San Tekka''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Republic Pathfinder]] official and member of the [[San Tekka clan]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was involved in the plotting of a [[hyperspace route]] to a [[Sophros|planet]] on the edge of [[Wild Space]]. News of the [[planet]], which was said to have valuable [[Gemstone|jewels]], drew the attention of [[Radicaz Dobbs]], a [[smuggler]] and [[information broker]] who had been working with the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] to steal [[Force artifact]]s. Dobbs persuaded San Tekka to sell him a copy of the route coordinates on [[Hon-Tallos]], as well as a cut of the recovered jewels. Although the first part of the deal was carried out, Dobbs [[Death|killed]] San Tekka after he failed to deliver upon the second part.<ref name="Path of Deceit" />
