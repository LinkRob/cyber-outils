The '''''Darkhawk''''' was a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[freighter]] that had been refitted to be used by the [[1st Legion]], an elite [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[Legion|unit]] that served as the personal forces of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="T:A" />

It was used during the Empire's [[Imperial–Grysk conflict|conflict]] with the warlike [[Grysk]] to board their [[starship]]s.<ref name="T:A" />

==History==
The ''Darkhawk'' was a [[freighter]] belonging to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. After the [[Republic Era|fall]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|of the]] [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], and the [[Clone Wars]] ended, the freighter was refitted and modified by the [[1st Legion|First Legion]], the personal [[legion]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="T:A" />

When [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] sensed a [[disturbance in the Force]], he dispatched Vader and [[Chiss]] [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] to [[Batuu]]. He personally ordered Vader to bring the ''Darkhawk'' and the First Legion with him as well.<ref name="T:A" />

Along the way, Thrawn's personal assassin [[Rukh]] snuck aboard the ''Darkhawk'' several times using his [[Sinrich optical dephaser|cloaking device]], as he was curious about the ship. [[Commander]] [[Kimmund]] warned Rukh that if he did this one more time, he would be shot and [[Death|killed]].<ref name="T:A" />
