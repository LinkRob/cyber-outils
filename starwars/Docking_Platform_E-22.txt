'''Docking Platform E-22''' was a docking platform in [[Hanna City]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Chandrila]]. After freeing [[advisor]] [[Yupe Tashu]] from [[prison]], [[Windom Traducier]], an undercover [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[agent]] posing as a [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senate]] [[Senate Guard (New Republic)|guardsman]], informed Tashu that a [[Starship|ship]] was waiting for him on Docking Platform E-22.<ref name="Life Debt" />
