'''Elkew-Nine''' was an [[IG-86 sentinel droid]] who worked alongside the [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]]. Together with other IG-86 droids, Elkew-Nine was tasked with ensuring her security while she copied a list of [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[agent]]s from [[Asajj Ventress]] at the [[Doric Prime power plant]]. The droid encountered [[Clone Force 99]], who were attempting to recover the list, and attacked them before being destroyed by [[Wrecker]].<ref name="Ghost Agents 3" />
