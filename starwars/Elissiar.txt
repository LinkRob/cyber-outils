'''Elissiar''' was a [[Politics|politician]] who was affiliated with the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]], [[Kirames Kaj]], chose not to run for reelection. Two of the candidates that the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] knew of who wished to succeed her were Elissiar and the politician [[Finis Valorum]].<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elissiar was mentioned in ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="MA" />
