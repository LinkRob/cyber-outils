'''Calliope Station''' was a former [[Con Star Mining Corporation]] station on the planet [[Parnassos]] that survived a [[Devastation of Parnassos|nuclear cataclysm]] that reduced the planet to a barbaric, post apocalyptic state. During the [[New Republic Era]], the [[Scyre]] warrior [[Siv]] sought refuge there after being abandoned by her former leader [[Phasma]], who accompanied [[General]] [[Brendol Hux]] into the [[First Order]]. Siv lived there for the next ten years, giving birth to a child named [[Torbi]].<ref name="Phasma" />
