'''Chief''', sometimes known as '''chieftain''', was a title used in various organizations and cultures to denote a position of authority. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Thi-Sen]] was the chieftain of [[Talz]] on [[Orto Plutonia]];<ref name="Trespass" /> [[Nossor Ri]] was the chieftain of the [[Quarren]] on [[Mon Cala]];<ref name="Water War" /> [[Pieter]] was the chieftain of the [[Ming Po]] village on [[Carlac]];<ref name="Pieter">{{Databank|chieftain-pieter|Chieftain Pieter}}</ref> [[Tarfful]] was the chieftain of [[Kachirho]] on [[Kashyyyk]];<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and [[Qin Yazal]]<ref name="TCW CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> was the chieftain of a [[Poletec]] [[Poletec village|village]].<ref name="On the Wings of Keeradaks" />

In the [[Galactic Empire]], chief was a [[Imperial Military|military]] rank, and was used by such individuals as [[Moradmin Bast]] and [[Siward Cass]].<ref name="AIR">{{StoryCite|story=An Incident Report|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> In [[Bright Tree Village]], an [[Ewok]] village on the forest [[moon]] of [[Endor]], Chief [[Chirpa]] was the leader of the tribe.<ref name="Databank Chirps" />
