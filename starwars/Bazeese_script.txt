'''Bazeese script''' was a [[writing]] system used on [[Ferrix]],<ref name="Rix Road Trivia">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e12-trivia-gallery|text="Rix Road" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor|quote=The brick that Andor touches while sneaking around Ferrix is Clem Andor's funerary stone. It says, in Bazeese script: Clem Andor, and the dates 7896.39.5 - 7959.318.3. / The funerary stone that Brasso carries is Maarva's stone, and says on it, in Bazeese script: Maarva Andor and the dates 7895.330.9 - 7972.216.4. / The language of Ferrix, Bazeese, is named after graphic designer Barry “Baz” Gingell, its creator.}} {{C|Slides 3, 6 and 8}}</ref> a [[planet]] located in the [[Free Trade sector]].<ref name="Kassa" /> It was used alongside [[Aurebesh]] and appeared in places such as the signage for businesses like [[Greyble Service]], [[Gavalox Communications]], [[Zorby's Western Shiplot]], a [[Unidentified droid parts store|droid parts store]] and [[Nicola's]]; on a [[HFY-3920 shuttle ferry|local shuttle]], and [[funerary stone]]s.

==Usage==
Bazeese characters were painted at the top of a wall on the outside of the [[Ferrix Depot]], but it merely produces gibberish when translated.<ref name="Kassa" /><ref name="Rix" />

A sign outside [[Greyble Service]]<ref name="Narkina">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> said "PARTS SOLD HERE" in this script.<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref>

A device in [[Timm Karlo]]'s kitchen carried a vertical label written in this alphabet, with blue letters spelling out "MADE IN FERRIX".<ref name="Me" /><ref name="MadeAurek">{{Twitter|AurekFonts|status/1574957808285806592|AurekFonts|date=2022-09-28|quote=MADE IN FERRIX|archivedate=20230416145052}} {{C|Note that the picture in the tweet has been rotated 90 degrees}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I drew it, called it Bazeese, kept saying the name in the office, on set, in meetings, on uploaded files, until it was just "real" and couldn't be called anything else.|Graphic designer Barry Gingell|<ref name="BazeeseBarry">{{Twitter|BazatronPrime|status/1901762916946149573|[[Barry Gingell]]|date=2025-03-17|quote=I drew it, called it Bazeese, kept saying the name in the office, on set,  in meetings, on uploaded files, until it was just "real" and couldn't be called anything else.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/STq8M}}</ref>}}
[[File:Bazeese_mapping.png|thumb|right|200px|A fan mapping of the Bazeese letters and numbers]]
Bazeese script was created for the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Kassa" /> The script was named after graphic designer [[Barry Gingell|Barry "Baz" Gingell]], who created the alphabet.<ref name="Rix Road Trivia" /> Gingell himself named the script Bazeese and continued using the name consistently, until it became "real" and couldn't be called anything else.<ref name="BazeeseBarry" /> While [[Andor Season 1|Season 1]] was airing, Gingell confirmed on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that this was a new alphabet created for the TV series, but he was not in a position to reveals its name.<ref name="GavaBarry">{{Twitter|BazatronPrime|status/1574883352242429952|[[Barry Gingell]]|date=2022-09-22|quote=You are correct. It is the same new script. It does have a name. I'm just not sure I have permission to reveal it haha. That's a @pabl0hidalgo or @PhilSzostak level thing. Above my paygrade.|archivedate=20220928135536}}</ref> The name of the script was eventually revealed to be Bazeese script, in the episode guide for the season finale "[[Rix Road (episode)|Rix Road]]."<ref name="Rix Road Trivia" />

According to Gingell, symbols representing the digraphs OO and AA were also created, because the symbols were designed to flow as a block of text, and double letters messed up the flow and interaction between the characters. Although [[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor's]] name appears to be misspelled as "Marva" on the [[funerary stone]] in "Rix Road," Gingell explained that the first A is actually a symbol representing a double A, with the size of the circle in the A determining whether it is single or double.<ref name="StoneBarry" />
