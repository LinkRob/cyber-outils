'''Darktroopers''' were a group of elite [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]]<ref name="Rebellion">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref> empowered with the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]] used by the [[Dark Empire]] during [[Operation Shadow Hand]]. The best [[Trooper/Legends|troopers]] in the [[Legion/Legends|legions]]<ref name="World of the Ancient Sith" /> received limited [[The Force/Legends|Force]] training that made them far deadlier than the average stormtrooper.<ref name="Rebellion" /> In the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[10 ABY/Legends|10 ABY]],<ref name="NEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref> they served under [[Zasm Katth]] and [[Baddon Fass]] on [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]]<ref name="Duel on Nar Shaddaa" /> and later on [[Ossus/Legends|Ossus]] under the command of [[Sedriss QL]].<ref name="World of the Ancient Sith" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[1998]] [[Wikipedia:Real-time strategy|real-time strategy game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']] mistakenly conflated the game's [[Dark Trooper/Legends|dark trooper]] [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] with darktroopers, suggesting the droids were force-sensitive stormtroopers using "[[Phase III Dark Trooper|enhanced exo-armor]] and [[Assault cannon/Legends|weaponry]]."<ref name="Rebellion" />
