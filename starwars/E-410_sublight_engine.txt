The '''E-410 sublight engine''' was a [[Color|gray]] model of [[sublight engine]] manufactured by [[Girodyne]]. Upgrading an [[EML-850 light freighter]] previously equipped with the slower [[E-310 sublight engine]]s to E-410s cost two thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and required a [[X-FP-C28 ship computer]], twenty units of [[Quadanium steel|quadanium]], and eight enhanced [[durasteel]] [[hull]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[Huttese]] advert for the [[engine]] was played over the speakers of the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]'', claiming that they provided speed that you could count on.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] paid for the services of a [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] to upgrade her EML-850 [[light freighter]] ''[[Trailblazer]]'s'' E-310 engines to E-410 sublight engines, providing the necessary parts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
E-410 sublight engines appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Upgrading the ''Trailblazer'' with E-410 engines is required for the "Might want to buckle up" achievement.<ref name="Outlaws" />
