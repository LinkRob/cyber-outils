'''Engineering''' was a location within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Star Destroyer]] [[Implacable (Star Destroyer)|''Implacable'']]. It was worked by a team of [[engineer]]s including [[Lieutenant]] [[Naylyn Bashan]] and led by the [[Starship|vessel's]] [[chief engineer]], [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Del Meeko]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />

==Behind the scenes==
