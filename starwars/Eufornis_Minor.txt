'''Eufornis Minor''', sometimes referred to as simply '''Eufornis''', was a [[planet]] located in the [[Shadola sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. A remote colony, it was sometimes confused with [[Eufornis Major]], a prestigious city-planet located within the [[Core Worlds]].

==History==
{{Quote|So the Empire gives the 'Glom total control of transport on Eufornis Minor. Can't get on a tram without chaincodes, let alone a shuttle, and I'm stuck way out in the middle of muck all.|Chass na Chadic recalls her experience on Eufornis Minor|<ref name="Shadow Fall" />}}
Eufornis Minor was the [[homeworld]] of [[Nazhros Oleg]], a [[cadet]] in the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" />

At some point before [[4 ABY]],<ref name="4 ABY">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' states that [[Chass na Chadic]] escaped Eufornis Minor before she joined [[Hound Squadron]]; Chadic was with Hound [[Squadron]] by the events of [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], which takes place several [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates Endor, and thus the events of ''Alphabet Squadron'', to [[4 ABY]], meaning the escape must have happened before then.</ref> the Empire gave the [[Slipglass Conglomerate]] total control of [[transport]] on Eufornis Minor, enforcing [[Chain code|chaincode]] regulations on public transportation. The [[Theelin]] [[Chass na Chadic]] was able to escape offworld despite complications.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> she recounted her time on the planet, with embellished details, to [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]] while the pair [[Cerberon system campaign|served]] with the [[New Republic]] in the [[Cerberon system]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eufornis Minor was first mentioned in the [[2015]] junior novel ''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]'', the second installment in the [[Star Wars Rebels: Servants of the Empire|''Servants of the Empire'']] series written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" />
