The '''Coruscant Rescue Ops''' was a fire department that existed only to serve the greater good of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], [[Coruscant]], by providing disaster relief whenever necessary. They notably put out [[fire]]s using [[F-143 emergency speeder|emergency fire suppression speeders]].<ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters" />

==History==
[[File:Emergency_firespeeder_pilot.png|thumb|250px|left|Coruscant Rescue Ops used firespeeders to put out fires.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Coruscant Rescue Ops help put out flames from the immensely broken ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' as it was being guided down by [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|General]] and [[Jedi Knight|Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], who was accompanied by his [[Jedi Master|master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. The pair had just rescued [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] from [[General]] [[Grievous]] and [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. Due to complications when the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] ship had entered Coruscant's atmosphere, it was ablaze. Using emergency fire suppression speeders, the Coruscant Rescue Ops was able to help ensure the safety of the Chancellor as well his Jedi saviors.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>
