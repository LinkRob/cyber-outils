'''Clone medical officers''',<ref name="BMF 89">{{BuildFalconCite|89|Starship Fact File}}</ref> also known as '''medical officer clones''',<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> were [[Galactic Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s specialized in [[medic]]al treatments. They served aboard [[Haven-class medical station|''Haven''-class medical stations]] and Republic [[starship]]s during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="SoM" />

They wore a similar [[Republic military uniforms|uniform]] to the [[clone navigation officer]]. During the early stages of the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, the [[Clothing|uniform]] was [[Color|white]] with an insignia on their left [[Limb|arm]],<ref name="SoM" /> later adding red stripes to the sides of the uniform and moving the insignia to the chest.<ref name="Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The clone medical officers first appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]] episode [[Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence'']],<ref name="SoM" /> but were not identified in [[canon]] until the book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
