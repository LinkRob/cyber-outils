'''Barricade destruction''' was a type of move in the [[Unidentified sport|ball sport]] played by [[Manac-Nebut]]. The athlete was mainly used in the game for [[trench crossing]]s and barricade destructions.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Barricade destruction was mentioned in a segment called "Prospects", written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
