The '''city hall''' was located in [[Lonisa City]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. During the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Occupation of Valo|occupation]] of the planet, Nihil [[Commander]] [[Vark Tarpalin]] kept an [[Vark Tarpalin's office|office]] in the hall.<ref name="EFV" />
