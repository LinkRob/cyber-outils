'''BO-N1''' was a [[service droid]] who worked at the [[droid]] [[Droid Spa|spa]] on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Evil Plans" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
BO-N1 was voiced by [[Angelique Perrin]], who also voiced fellow spa droid [[SN-D1]] in the eighth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', "[[Evil Plans]]." Though not named in the episode itself, BO-N1's name is given in the episode's credits.
