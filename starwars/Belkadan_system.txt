The '''Belkadan system''' was a [[star system]] in the the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]]'<ref name="New Territories" /> [[Dalonbian sector]]. It was situated within grid square L-1 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Belkadan]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Beyond Victory Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> a job listing for a deep [[Realspace|space]] signal surveyor based at Belkadan was featured on a [[viewscreen]] in [[Sornah's Garage]] on<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Malastare DB">{{Databank|malastare|Malastare}}</ref> [[Malastare]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Belkadan system was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> The system was later identified as the "Belkadan system" in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],"<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix accompanying the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]], an article on [[StarWars.com]] featuring a map of the galaxy.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]] and posted on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Fry">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map...|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Belkadan system/Legends|Belkadan system]] first appeared in the [[1998]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']], which was developed by [[Coolhand Interactive]].<ref name="Rebellion">[[Star Wars: Rebellion (video game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']]</ref>
