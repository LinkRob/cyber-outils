The '''cold-weather issue command parka'''<ref name="Bust Announcement">{{WebCite|url=http://www.the3dstudio.com/specialty-items/show-exclusives/general-mcquarrie-celebration-vii-exclusive-mini-bust|text=General McQuarrie Celebration VII Exclusive Mini Bust|archivedate=20150920224318}}</ref> was a type of insulated [[jacket]] worn as [[Alliance military uniform|uniform]]<ref name="CEUE" /> by [[Alliance officer|officers]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] while they were stationed in [[Echo Base]] on the icy [[planet]] [[Hoth]]. The parka was [[Color|brown]] in color with two breast pockets and a high collar. [[Officer]]s who wore the parka included the base's commander, [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]], and his second-in-command General [[Pharl McQuarrie]], as well as [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]]. Each of them wore their [[Rebel command insignia]] on the breast of the parka.<ref name="ESB" />
