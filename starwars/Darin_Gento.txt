'''Darin Gento''' was an [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[agent]] who investigated a crime scene alongside fellow [[agent]] [[Jessen Pol]]. During the investigation, Gento opened a door attached to a [[Thermal Detonator|thermal detonator]] via a string. The open door caused the [[bomb]] to explode, killing both agents.<ref name="GoD" />
