{{Quote|The lesser leaf viper of Baran-Bai is significantly more deadly than its larger cousin, the elder viper, though it appears to be no more interesting than a rock worm.|Jecki Lon, to her opponent|<ref name="Crown" />}}
The '''elder viper''' was a type of [[Snake|viper]]. It was larger and significantly less deadly than its cousin, the [[lesser leaf viper]], which was native to the location [[Baran-Bai]].<ref name="Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> during the [[dueling tournament]] of the [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|Crowning Convocation]] coming-of-age ritual on the [[planet]] [[Siline]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]]'s [[Jecki Lon's opponent|opponent]] commented on her small size. In response, Lon compared herself to a lesser leaf viper, noting that although it was smaller than an elder viper, it was also deadlier.<ref name="Crown" />

==Behind the scenes==
Elder vipers were mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]'', a [[2025]] young-adult novel by [[Tessa Gratton]].<ref name="Crown" />
