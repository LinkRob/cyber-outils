'''Bill Burr''' is an American stand-up comedian and actor who portrayed [[Migs Mayfeld]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="IGN" /> In his comedy, Burr had a habit of making fun of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, which made ''The Mandalorian'' producer [[Jon Favreau]] think it would be funny to cast him into the series. After taking part in ''The Mandalorian'', Burr has reflected it was one of the highlights of his career.<ref name="Bill Burr Answers" />
