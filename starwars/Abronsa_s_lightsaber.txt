'''Abronsa's lightsaber'''<ref name="Lightsaber Collection" /> was a [[Color|purple]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] wielded by the [[Jedi]] [[Ravna Abronsa]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Precedent" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Abronsa's lightsaber first appeared in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance: Precedent]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]] and [[Tomio Ogata]] and illustrated by Ogata.<ref name="Precedent" />
