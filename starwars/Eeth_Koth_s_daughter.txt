After the fall of the [[Galactic Republic]], the former [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth]] [[Reproduction|fathered]] a [[Force-sensitive]] '''daughter''' during the [[Imperial Era]]. The [[Zabrak]] [[Gender|female]] was born to Koth and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Mira (Zabrak)|Mira]]. Immediately following the girl's birth, Koth was [[Hunt for Eeth Koth|discovered]] by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Death|killed]] in a duel. With her father dead, the child was taken by Vader to the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and placed into the care of [[nursemaids]].

==Biography==
[[File:Nursemaids-2017DarthVader19.png|thumb|left|200px|The baby being given to the nursemaids]]
This infant was [[Reproduction|born]] to [[Eeth Koth]] and his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Mira (Zabrak)|Mira]], on a [[Unidentified planet (Eeth Koth)|planet]]. As soon as the baby was born, Koth was eager to hold her, but before he could, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who had been searching for Koth with the assistance of the [[Inquisitorius]], appeared. Koth ordered Mira to take their baby and run. Mira almost made it to [[Mira's father|her father's]] transport when an Inquisitor named<ref name="Dark Lord 19" /> [[Thirteenth Sister]]<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> appeared. Mira begged her not to take her baby. Akaris pretended to let them go. Mira and her child boarded her father's [[Starship|ship]], but as they ascended the Inquisitor used [[the Force]] to take the baby out of Mira's hands.<ref name="Dark Lord 19" />

Akaris brought the baby to Vader, who was in a [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber combat|duel]] with her father. Koth saw the baby in the Inquisitor's hands and was [[Death|killed]] from a slash in the back as Vader used the window of opportunity to force his advantage. The baby was given to Vader and continued crying in his hands before he used the Force to silence it. The group then took their [[Zeta-class shuttle|''Zeta''-class shuttle]] back to [[Coruscant]] where the baby was given to two [[nursemaids]] to be put into [[Project Harvester]]. As the nursemaids took her, they exclaimed its prettiness in a manner that [[The Grand Inquisitor]] found worrying.<ref name="Dark Lord 19" />

==Personality and traits==
She had black [[eye]]s and light skin.<ref name="Dark Lord 19" />

==Skills and abilities==
Like her father, Eeth Koth's daughter was [[Force-sensitive]] and had the potential to connect to [[the Force]].<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
