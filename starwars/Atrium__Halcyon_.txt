The '''Atrium''' was a central location on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. In it, both crew and passengers mingled. It featured [[Hologram|holographic]] displays and [[viewscreen]]s, as well as seating for multiple individuals.<ref name="SWGS page">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref> [[Galaxy Cake Bar]] was also sold there.<ref name="SW GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref> It was located on Deck 6.<ref name="SWGS Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)|''Star Wars'': Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref>
