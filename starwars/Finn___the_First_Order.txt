'''''Finn & the First Order''''' is a young readers book in the [[World of Reading]] series written by [[Elizabeth Schaefer]] and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] that adapts scenes from [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. The book, a level two title, was published on [[December 18]], [[2015]], comprises 32 pages, and is intended for children ages six through eight.<ref name="Hachette" /><ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/a-galaxy-of-star-wars-the-force-awakens-books-coming-december-18-first-look|text=A Galaxy of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Books Coming December 18 - UPDATED!}}</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''Read along with ''Star Wars''! Desperate to escape his past, Finn is plunged into adventure as he journeys down a heroic, but dangerous, path.''<ref name="b&n">{{BN|url=w/world-of-reading-star-wars-lucasfilm-press/1121865259?ean=9781484720066|text=Star Wars: Finn & the First Order: A Lucasfilm Read-Along (Level 2)}}</ref>

==Continuity==
The book features a scene not present in the film; Finn presents his blaster to Captain Phasma and states it was broken as the reason for not firing down on Jakku.
