The [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Advancement]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] and the [[Jedi Master]] [[Sskeer]] were '''captured''' in a skirmish on an [[asteroid]] [[Unidentified Nihil base|base]] by a [[Muglan's crew|crew]] of [[pirate]]s led by the [[Gloovan]] [[Muglan Tarantyne]]. [[Sskeer]] had previously been [[Rescue of Sskeer|rescued]] by his former [[Padawan]], [[Jedi]] Master [[Keeve Trennis]] and [[Keeve's crew|her crew]] from [[Boolan]]'s experimentations. The Jedi crew had captured Boolan, seized his [[Nameless control|Nameless control unit]], and took control of a pair of [[cybernetics|cybernetically]]-enhanced [[Shrikarai|Nameless]]. As the [[Kotabi]] [[bond-twins]] and [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Terec]] and [[Ceret]] worked with Jedi Master [[OrbaLin]] to infiltrate Boolan's heavily encrypted research files, Sskeer and former Nihil [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] worked to move the Nameless as far from the [[Force-sensitive]] members of their party as possible. When they tried to place the creatures in an [[airlock]], however, Dee realized that the systems were unresponsive. She quickly recognized Nihil tactics, and the base was soon attacked by a cadre of [[security droid|enforcer droids]] led by the pirate [[captain]] Muglan Tarantyne, a former member of [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Dee's Tempest]]. As the pressure of the attack grew, Sskeer used the Nameless control to guide the creatures in their fight against the enforcer droids, but they were [[stun]]ned, leaving Sskeer to eliminate many of the other droids. Meanwhile, the other members of the Jedi crew downloaded Boolan's encrypted files onto the [[survey droid|scout droid]] [[SK-0T]] and fought their way to their [[shuttle]], Boolan as their [[prisoner]] in tow. The pirates destroyed their shuttle, and in the explosion's aftermath, apprehended Boolan. Two of the pirates, [[Renga]] and [[Gabb]] realized that the Nameless control made Sskeer vulnerable to the creatures' pain, and exploited that to capture the large Jedi.<ref name="Script" />  

The Jedi crew chased after Tarantyne's crew in Boolan's [[Cloudship]], the ''[[Dalnan Dawn]]'', hoping to rendezvous with the ''[[Ataraxia]]''. In their way, however, all three ships were attacked by a swarm of Nihil [[scav droid]]s. Though Tarantyne managed to jump to [[hyperspace]], the Jedi had to crash land into the ''Ataraxia''{{'s}} [[hangar]] bay.<ref name="Script" />
