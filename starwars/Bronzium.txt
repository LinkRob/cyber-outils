'''Bronzium'''<ref name="Trkn">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> was a [[Color|bronze]]-colored<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> alloy<ref name="Hunters" /> which could be used to make statues. The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] kept bronzium statues of the [[Four Sages of Dwartii]] in his [[Emperor's office|office]] on [[Coruscant]] during his time as [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Complete Locations" /> After becoming [[Galactic Emperor]], Sidious had the statues moved to his new quarters in the [[Imperial Palace]].<ref name="Trkn" /> The ancient [[droid]] [[ME-8D9]] had a bronzium-enriched finish.<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Bronzium could also be used in lightsaber construction.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> It could also be used as [[armor]], as worn by [[Zorii Bliss]], and weapons, as Bliss used for [[barrel]]s in her [[Zorii Bliss's blasters|blasters]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] was forced to wear a [[dancing-girl costume]] that was partially made of bronzium during her enslavement to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]].<ref name="Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
