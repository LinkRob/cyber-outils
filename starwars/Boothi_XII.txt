'''Boothi XII''' was a [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]. During her [[time]] at the [[University of Bar'leth]], [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] explored a [[Cave|subterranean]] [[Boothi XII vault|vault]] on Boothi XII, hoping to discover artifacts that would further her career as an [[archaeologist]]. However, when exploring the vault, she found nothing of value.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />
