'''Dokar Venas''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served the criminal gang the [[Strus clan]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] around the [[time]] of the outbreak of the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. After word reached the Strus clan that a group of nomads had discovered a secret treasure within the wreck of the ''[[Starlight Wanderer]]'', a [[New Republic]] [[CR90 corvette]] that had crashed on Jakku many [[Standard year|years]] earlier, Venas led a group of Strus clan enforcers that attacked the nomads in an attempt to access the ''Starlight Wanderer'' crash site.<ref name="TFA Beginner game" />
