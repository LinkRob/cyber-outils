'''Bossk's Relby-v10 Mortar Gun''' was a [[Relby-v10 micro grenade launcher]] used by the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] as his primary weapon. It held the ability to fire [[Color|red]]<ref name="SWQ">{{Asmodee|subdomain=cdn.svc|domain=net|url=production-amgcom/uploads/2025/04/DOC13_Mercenary_Units.pdf|text=''Star Wars: Legion'' Mercenary Unit Cards (PDF)}} {{C|Card: Bossk &ndash; Trandoshan Terror}}</ref> heavy-duty [[blaster]] bolts, [[micro-grenade]]s, and came with a [[mortar]] attachment.
