'''Fascism''' was a [[Politics|political]] philosophy based on militant totalitarianism.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> The [[Galactic Empire]] and its successor state, the [[First Order]],<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> were both considered fascist governments.<ref name="Follow and Lead" /><ref name="Ion Control">{{StoryCite|story=Ion Control|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] hid his policies behind the veneer of political populism, painting himself as a man of the people who had ended the [[Clone Wars]] and who would usher in a new era of prosperity for the average worker. [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]], one of Palpatine's political opponents, saw through Palpatine's image and knew he was simply restructuring galaxy power into the hands of himself and his followers.<ref name="MaskFear">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> Indeed, when making enough [[money]] to get by became difficult for average Imperial citizens, Imperial [[propaganda]] was quick to try and cover up the realities of day-to-day life, painting the image of a far more prosperous society than that which actually existed.<ref name="Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="9BBY">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> in a recorded speech, Senator [[Daho Sejan]] blamed the economic and security issues facing the Empire on enemies from within, "terrorists," whom he claimed lived among the everyday people in secret.<ref name="Jedi: Survivor" />

The [[Bith]] [[artist]] [[Janyor]] referred to the Galactic Empire as fascist, expressing his disgust with the [[propaganda]] [[Painting|piece]] ''[[Imperius Unitada ober Totallex]]''{{'s}} use of fascist imagery.<ref name="Propaganda" /> The rise of the First Order was regarded as a return of militarism and fascism to [[the galaxy]]. During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] decreed the elimination of all [[Resistance]] collaborators and other subversives who opposed the First Order.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> During Ren's rule, some civilians unaffiliated with the larger movement begun by Ren's [[Parent|mother]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], resisted what they described as the First Order's fascist takeover of the [[galaxy]].<ref name="Follow and Lead" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{SeeAlso|Democracy#Behind the scenes}}
{{Quote|We are at a turning point: fascism or revolution.|George Lucas' 1974 rough draft of ''Star Wars'', describing the Empire as "America ten years from now"|<ref name="Making">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref>}}
Fascism was first mentioned in the current [[canon]] continuity in the [[2014]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'' and is used to designate the [[Galactic Empire]] regime,<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> which first appeared in [[George Lucas]]' [[1977]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] was first referred to as being [[Fascism/Legends|fascist]] in [[2008]] through the ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]'' video game.<ref name="Force Unleashed">''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''</ref> [[George Lucas]]' original idea for the Empire was both politically and aesthetically inspired by fascist regimes like [[Wikipedia:Nazi Germany|Nazi Germany]] in addition to the rise of [[Wikipedia:Consumerism|consumerist]] and strong [[Wikipedia:Capitalism|capitalist]] culture in the "[[Wikipedia:American imperialism|American empire]]."<ref name="Behind the Magic">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> Fascism is an authoritarian political ideology most prominent in [[Wikipedia:Fascist Italy (1922–1943)|Italy]] and Germany between the [[Wikipedia:World War I|First]] and [[Wikipedia:World War II|Second World Wars]]. Fascist regimes entailed the dominance of a [[Wikipedia:Cult of personality|cult of personality]] and the term originated from the the [[Wikipedia:Latin|Latin]] "[[Wikipedia:Fasces|fasces]]."<ref name="Fascism: a Warning">{{BookCite|author=[[Wikipedia:Madeleine Albright|Albright, Madeleine]]|title=[[Wikipedia:Fascism: A Warning|Fascism: a Warning]]|publisher=[[Wikipedia:HarperCollins|HarperCollins]]|date=2018|isbn=9780062802187}}</ref>

Lucas' [[The Star Wars rough draft|rough draft]] of ''Star Wars'' in [[1974]] describes the Empire as "America ten years from now," at a "turning point" of "fascism or revolution." "Nixonian gangsters"&mdash;referring to [[Wikipedia:Richard Nixon|Richard Nixon]], [[Wikipedia:President of the United States|President of the United States]] from 1969 to August 1974&mdash;would have assassinated the Emperor and rigged an election to take power, starting "race riots" and helping rebel groups before imposing a "[[Wikipedia:Totalitarianism|'total control']] [[Wikipedia:Police state|police state]]," firstly welcomed by the people who are frustrated with rampant criminality, then exploiting them with high taxes and utility and transport costs. Lucas's first theatrical film, [[1971]]'s [[Wikipedia:THX 1138|''THX 1138'']], is set in a dystopian consumerist society tightly controlled by robotic police.<ref name="Making" />

The [[original trilogy]] concept artist [[Ralph McQuarrie]] drew on Nazi imagery in designing Imperial stormtroopers.<ref name="EW Rogue One">''[[Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One]]''</ref> The closing scene of ''A New Hope'', in which the Rebel Alliance assembles to celebrate its victory over the Empire at the [[Battle of Yavin]], was inspired by the imagery of the 1935 Nazi propaganda film [[Wikipedia:Triumph of the Will|''Triumph of the Will'']].<ref name="Behind the Magic" />
