'''Dhandu''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Dhandu system]], within [[the Interior]] and the [[Expansion Region]], in grid square I-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. It was [[orbit]]ed by at least three [[moon]]s, including [[Dhandu 3]], whose low-[[gravity]] [[grass]]land was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Dhanduese]], a [[species]] of [[Bird|avian]] [[Sentience|sentients]].

==Description==
Dhandu was a [[planet]] [[orbit]]ed by at least three [[moon]]s, including [[Dhandu 3]], which was the [[homeworld]] to the [[Bird|avian]] species [[Dhanduese]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> The planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>  was located in the [[Dhandu system]], in grid square I-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[the Interior]]<ref name="Location" /> and the [[Expansion Region]], of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
[[File:StronoCookieTuggs.png|thumb|right|200px|Strono Tuggs mentioned Dhandu in his cookbook.]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the first part of ''[[Pirate's Price]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> [[Mahjo Reeloo]], a [[Cloning|clone]] created by [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]] [[Kolac Pru]], hired the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] to take her to Dhandu's third moon, where she recovered a security key for a deposit box in the [[Undervaults of Gwongdeen]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> [[Eloc Throno]], a [[historian]] and author, included a [[map]] with the location of Dhandu in his ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', a [[book]] that detailed the planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> between 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Dhandu 3. In Tuggs' third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 ABY,<ref name="UltimateDate" /> he mentioned Dhandu while expounding on his travels to Dhandu 3 when documenting his recipe for the [[Dhanduese Drum]]s [[Foodstuff|dish]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dhandu was first mentioned in ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', a novel written by [[Lou Anders]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
