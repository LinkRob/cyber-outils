The '''First Battle of Geonosis''', also referred to as the '''Battle of Geonosis''' or the '''Battle on Geonosis''', was the first major battle fought in [[22 BBY]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] on [[Geonosis]], marking the beginning of the three-[[Standard year|year]] [[Clone Wars]]. It would be the first major combat of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], as well as the first major battle the [[Jedi]] had fought in years. 

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I've got a bad feeling about this.|Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
Prior to the outbreak of the battle on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], the [[Galactic Republic]] had endured for several [[Standard century|centuries]] as it expanded and prospered. However, despite its foundation as a democratic governmental body, with a [[Galactic Senate]] of hundreds of [[senator]]s representing thousands of [[star system]]s, the Republic had begun to develop indications of corruption and neglect for worlds beyond the [[Core Worlds]] within its democratic foundation. As a result of its corruption and ineffectiveness, several star systems had developed a dissatisfaction for the Republic, which led to [[Politics|political]] tensions, establishment of coalitions and alliances, and the accumulation of resources, as well as the inception of a [[Separatist Crisis|secessionist movement]] under the direction of the fallen [[Jedi Master]] and [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who founded the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] as a new government supported by thousands of seceded star systems and trade and commercial groups, such as the [[Trade Federation]], [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]], [[Techno Union]], [[Commerce Guild]] and [[Corporate Alliance]], following his [[Raxus Address|address]] on the planet [[Raxus Secundus]].<ref name="Rise" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA" /> following the Confederacy's establishment, with Dooku as its [[Head of State]], as [[terrorist]] strikes were conducted on the Republic's [[capital]] world of [[Coruscant]], the democratic government's senate and [[Supreme Chancellor]], [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]&mdash;secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] Darth Sidious&mdash;had prepared to discuss the consideration of the [[Military Creation Act]]. However, on the [[Standard day|day]] of the vote, Senator [[Padmé Amidala]] of the planet [[Naboo]] had traveled to Coruscant in an attempt to oppose the proposed bill, was prevented from denouncing the Military Creation Act as a result of a series of [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|assassination attempts]] orchestrated by the Federation's [[Viceroy]], [[Nute Gunray]], which led to the assignment of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], as her security detail at the [[Chancellor]]'s request. While Amidala was under protection by the [[Jedi Order]], the two Jedi managed to discover the identity of the Senator's assailant as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]], whose [[death]] by [[Kamino saberdart|saberdart]] by her employer, the [[bounty]] hunter [[Jango Fett]], resulted in Amidala's return to Naboo accompanied by Skywalker and a wider investigation by Kenobi.<ref name="Episode II" />

As Kenobi investigated the unidentified bounty hunter, the Jedi Knight, who determined the saberdart to be of [[Kaminoan]] origins, [[Mission to Kamino (Separatist Crisis)|traveled]] to the planet [[Kamino]], which had unknowingly been removed from the [[Jedi Archives|archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] by an anonymous force, in an attempt to locate the assailant. While on Kamino, Kenobi had discovered the foundation of an [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]] of [[clone trooper]]s for the Republic, whose creation by the Kaminoans had been arranged by deceased Jedi Master [[Sifo-Dyas]] [[32 BBY|ten years]] prior to Kenobi's arrival. After his discovery of the [[Cloning|clone]] [[army]]'s benefactor as the bounty hunter Jango Fett, Kenobi requested to meet with the [[clone template]], where he began to question Fett about his travels and the army, who explained that he was recruited by a man known as Tyranus to be the army's template.<ref name="Episode II" />

Following their meeting, the bounty hunter, after a brief skirmish with Kenobi, made his departure from Kamino to the planet Geonosis, but was pursued by the Jedi Knight, who landed on the surface, where he managed to intercept a meeting of the Separatist Council members and discover that Viceroy Gunray was responsible for Amidala's assassination attempts, as well as the Confederacy's creation of a massive [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] on the planet. However, as the Jedi Knight contacted Coruscant, whose transmission was transferred to the Republic and Jedi by Amidala and Skywalker on the planet [[Tatooine]], Kenobi explained that the Federation was to produce a [[droid]] army and the conspirator of Amidala's attempted assassinations but was instead taken captive by the Confederacy.<ref name="Episode II" />

In response to Kenobi's capture, the Republic Senate, at the proposal of [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] of Naboo, granted Chancellor Palpatine the authority to form a Grand Army of the Republic in order to combat the rising Separatist Droid Army as decreed by the government's [[Emergency Powers Act]]. As a result, Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] proclaimed that they would be departing for Kamino to investigate the clone army, while Jedi Master [[Mace Windu]] would lead a [[Jedi assault team|strike team]] to Geonosis in an attempt to rescue Kenobi and prevent Dooku from proceeding with his plans. Although ordered by Windu to remain on Tatooine, Amidala and Skywalker had departed for Geonosis in order to rescue the Jedi Knight, where they, following a prolonged struggle in the droid manufacturing facilities, were also taken prisoner by the [[Geonosian]]s and sentenced to [[Death penalty|execution]] in the [[Petranaki Arena]] along with Kenobi. However, the three managed to narrowly escape their executions as they outmaneuvered [[Unidentified acklay (Petranaki Arena)|their]] [[Unidentified nexu|animal]] [[Unidentified reek|executioners]], while Windu and his strike team arrived on the planet, prompting the chaotic First Battle of Geonosis.<ref name="Episode II" />

==The battle==
===Beginning===
{{Quote|My Master will not let the Republic get away with this treachery.|Count Dooku|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:AggressiveNegotiations-SWL66.png|thumb|left|250px|Skywalker and Amidala at the start of the battle]]
A group of 212 Jedi,<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> which was the largest deployment of Jedi since the [[High Republic Era]],<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> including [[Coleman Trebor]], [[Eeth Koth]], [[Kit Fisto]], [[Plo Koon]], [[Even Piell]], [[Shaak Ti]], [[Luminara Unduli]], [[Aayla Secura]], [[Barriss Offee]], and [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] were tasked with rescuing Obi-Wan Kenobi and investigating the reports of Separatist warmongering on Geonosis by the [[Jedi High Council]].<ref name="Episode II" /> Despite this large number of experienced Jedi, among the group were many new Jedi Knights who lacked the experience of their masters. Among these newer knights was [[Bultar Swan]], who later stated that "The battle wasn't supposed to happen." She also said that she knew it possibly could, but she expected the enemy to surrender as soon as the Jedi ignited their [[lightsaber]]s.<ref name="Front Lines" />

The [[Zilkin]] [[Meebur Gascon]] participated in the battle, as part of [[Republic High Command|Republic Strategic Command]], by analyzing Geonosis's hostile terrain and recommending an effective attack plan.<ref name="enc">{{SWE|characters|colonel-meebur-gascon|Colonel Meebur Gascon}}</ref> A first wave of Jedi were deployed by clones on shuttles to the planet. One group, consisting of Jedi Masters and Knights including [[Klefan Opus]], [[Sember Vey]], [[Bavoc Ansho]], [[Gobi]], and [[Theca]], as well as Padawans such as [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]], [[Charlin Plaka|Charlin]], [[Onielle]], [[Fvorn]], [[Zeeth]], and [[Tualon Yaluna]] were ordered by Mace Windu to take out any nearby long-ranged weapons and cannons that could be used against the Jedi in the arena. Once the shuttle landed, Klefan led the group underground, guided by the light of their lightsabers.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

As the team moved underground, a Geonosian caught the group by surprise and electrocuted Fvorn. Klefan stuck down the Geonosian, but was too late, with Fvorn becoming the first casualty. Zeeth cried out in pain, having been close friends with Fvorn, only for more Geonosians to ambush the group. Iskat’s instincts kicked in and she immediately began to cut the Geonosians down. As the Jedi became surrounded by Geonosians in the tunnel, they sprang into action. Soon after, Onielle became injured by a [[sonic blaster]], causing the Jedi to retreat, except Zeeth, who still clung to his fallen friend. While Zeeth mourned, Klefan defended Zeeth but became overwhelmed, causing Iskat to return and help defend the grieving Zeeth. During combat, Iskat became enchanted and began to lose composure, to the point where Klefan interfered to stop her from cutting down the Geonosians who were already killed. The two took Zeeth away from his friend's body, and Klefan convinced Zeeth to return to the mission. They rejoined the rest of the group and moved around the arena, having split up to disarm the area.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

===Arena battle===
{{Quote|They ''should'' have surrendered, but instead they fought back. Suddenly all the relatively new Knights like me, who mistook our overconfidence for foresight, had to figure out what to do next.|Bultar Swan, on the battle|<ref name="Front Lines" />}}
[[File:GeonosisJediDieDeflectingShots.jpg|thumb|300px|Members of the strike team slowly perishing]]
Skywalker, Amidala, and Kenobi were able to break free of their shackles and fight or avoid the creatures that had been set upon them long enough for Master Windu's Jedi strike team to show up. As the Jedi came into view throughout the arena, many Geonosians chose to flee rather than face them in combat. However, Count Dooku deployed the Separatists' new [[battle droid]] army, beginning a violent battle took place on the arena floor<ref name="Episode II" /> as the droid [[legion]]s were unleashed.<ref name="The Engines of War">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/11/14/the-engines-of-war|text=The Engines of War}}</ref> The first batch of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s were part of the army.<ref name="SWDF">[[Star Wars: Droid Factory (book)|''Star Wars: Droid Factory'']]</ref> Though outnumbered, the Jedi fought bravely even as their ranks were slowly diminished in the face of an unending number of battle droids.<ref name="Episode II" /> Jedi [[Stass Allie]] and Bultar Swan would fight back-to-back during the battle against the droid army.<ref name="Front Lines" />

With the distraction of the Jedi assault, Iskat and Sember rush at a cannon. A Geonosian soldier notices them and fires at them with his Sonic cannon, but Iskat throws her lightsaber at him, killing the Geonosian, while Sember destroys the cannon by slicing the machine with her lightsaber. Other members of the team take out their targets as well, preventing the Geonosians from using their cannons against Republic forces. The group then hides, waiting for the Jedi to win the arena fight and give the all clear but instead watch as the endless battle droids annihilate the Jedi.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> 

[[File:JourneysBeginnings-ShaakTiGeonosis.png|thumb|left|233x233px|Shaak Ti and Pablo-Jill fight battle droids during the battle.]]
It was during the fierce battle<ref name="Episode II" /> that Padawan [[Tan Yuster]] was overwhelmed and killed by super battle droids,<ref name="Lightsabers">''[[Star Wars Lightsabers: A Guide to Weapons of the Force]]''</ref> and bounty hunter Jango Fett attempted to kill the famed Jedi Master, Mace Windu. However, during their confrontation, the [[Reek]] charged Fett and nearly crushed him; although Fett slayed the beast, its attack had broken his [[jetpack]]. When the duel resumed, Mace Windu charged straight towards Fett, deflecting his [[blaster]] shots. At the last [[second]], the [[Mandalorian]] attempted to activate his jetpack, but it backfired, allowing Mace to close in, quickly bisect Fett's blaster pistol and [[Dismemberment|decapitate]] the notorious bounty hunter. Still standing on a balcony where he was first confronted, Dooku appeared to be disturbed by Fett's death. Fett's damaged [[helmet]] was soon found by his "son" [[Boba Fett]] after the conclusion of the arena battle.<ref name="Episode II" />

An unexpected participant in the battle was the [[3PO-series protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], whose head had been detached and welded onto a battle droid's body while a battle droid's head had been attached to his body. Both incarnations walked into the battle alongside the battle droids and were knocked out by Kit Fisto and another Jedi. By the end of the battle, the [[R2-series astromech droid|R2-series]] [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] was able to locate C-3PO's head and reattach it to his original body.<ref name="Episode II" />

Seeing the battle going south, Master Klefan orders his hidden group to retreat from the shadows and back to the shuttle. As the group begins to retreat, Sember is killed by a Geonosian in front of her Padawan, Iskat. Iskat decapitates the guard and moves to her master, as she says her last words, and Sember dies in her Padawan’s hands. Klefan comes across the situation and pulls Iskat away and they retreat from the arena and back to their much emptier shuttle.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

[[File:JediCircle.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The strike team surrounded.]]
Despite the bravery of the Jedi, Dooku's droids held the upper hand however, due to the [[Unidentified battle droid legion|legion]] of battle droids significantly outnumbering the Jedi. Many Jedi were slain by the droids' blaster [[fire]], and when the last of the surviving Jedi became surrounded, Dooku called a halt to the fighting. The Separatist Head of State offered the Jedi the chance to surrender, but Windu declined, saying the Jedi would not become hostages with which Dooku could barter. Dooku then apologetically ordered the surrounding droids to slaughter the Jedi, only for Yoda to arrive overhead with<ref name="Episode II" /> a [[Yoda's regiment|regiment]]<ref name="Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> of clone [[trooper]]s.<ref name="Episode II" />

Launching a somewhat desperate assault into the arena in waves<ref name="Queen's Hope" /> aboard [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]], the sudden arrival of the clone army saved the survivors and created a perimeter for long enough that they could board and escape.<ref name="Episode II" /> [[Yoda's squad]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and the other troopers landed, and, during this arena battle, enormous [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship]]s, including the ''[[Implacable (Acclamator-class)|Implacable]]'', had penetrated the upper [[atmosphere]] of Geonosis, knocking out the Geonosian [[launch hive]]s with [[aerial bombardment]]s and [[clone commando]] strike teams and clearing the way to deploy the Grand Army of the Republic.<ref name="Episode II" /><ref name="SWCC2016" /> The clones used their sudden,<ref name="Episode II" /> overwhelming force to their advantage instead of relying on precision in the arena.<ref name="Queen's Hope" /> As [[Jedi General]] Yoda and the survivors evacuated the vacant arena cluttered with droid parts, the first battle of the [[Clone Wars]] began.<ref name="Episode II" /><ref name="SWCC2016" />

General [[Grievous]] was in the Stalgasin Hive around the time of the arena battle, though entirely by accident. He retreated<ref name="HC 8" /> through the hive tunnels beneath Geonosis<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> and he encountered several Jedi<ref name="HC 8" /> and clone troopers. Preventing them from reaching the Separatist Council<ref name="Separatists">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Separatists}}</ref> and keeping his existence a secret,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> Grievous butchered all those who came across him,<ref name="HC 8" /> leaving none to speak of his existence<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> until much later.<ref name="HC 8" />

===War on Geonosis===
{{Quote|If Dooku escapes, rally more systems to his cause, he will.|Yoda|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:Battle-of-Geonosis-LAAT.png|thumb|left|270px|The newly formed Republic Military fought the Droid Army across a plain on Geonosis.]]
Following the Grand Army's arrival, the [[Neimoidian]]s on Geonosis fled to their Federation [[core ship]]s,<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> while Dooku and the other CIS leaders left to a [[Geonosian War Room|Geonosian Command Center]] to oversee the battle.<ref name="Episode II" /> Jedi [[Sidn-ee]] and [[Till-ee]] were the first to reach the outside reaches of the [[Trade Federation landing zones]].<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> After the rescue of the Jedi, the droid and clone armies battled one another in an intricate series of confrontations on a number of different fronts. Much of the Separatists' heavy equipment had already been loaded onto their transports, but with their communications being jammed the rest was hastily thrown at the clones in order to buy the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]] enough [[time]] to escape. Meanwhile, the Republic army's goal was to capture the Separatists leadership and as much of the Geonosian droid foundries intact as possible in order to uncover the depths of the Separatist conspiracy, precluding the indiscriminate use of [[starship]] [[weapon]]s.<ref name="Episode II" /><ref name="SWCC2016" />

The resulting battle would mostly consist of ground combat then, but, although the clone troopers were greatly outnumbered by battle droids, the Republic enjoyed air dominance thanks to their preemptive strikes on the launch hives.<ref name="Episode II" /><ref name="SWCC2016" /> Nevertheless, at some point during the battle, the Geonosians did launch thousands of standby [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class territorial defense starfighters]] to break the Republic's [[orbit]]al cordon, which was preventing Separatist ground reinforcements from the Corporate Alliance.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> Corporate Alliance [[NR-N99 Persuader-class droid enforcer|NR-N99 ''Persuader''-class droid enforcers]] did, nonetheless, see active use in the battle.<ref name="The Separatists" /> Meanwhile, Obi-Wan, Anakin, and Senator Amidala took a [[gunship]] to attack the lightly armored Techno Union transports while the gunship carrying Yoda and Mace Windu proceeded on to a nearby assembly area.<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:Homing_Spider_Droid.png|thumb|right|275px|The forces of the Separatists engaged the Republic during the Battle of Geonosis]]
After arriving, Mace took command of a number of special [[commando]] units while Yoda proceeded to the [[Forward Command Center|forward command center]], where he would direct the overall battle. On all fronts, the two sides grappled with each other in fierce combat: heavily armored [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE]] [[walker]]s facing down hordes of battle droids. However, when the Trade Federation core ships started to take off, a lone AT-TE fired a shot on a core ship. As [[OG-9 homing spider droid]]s swept their [[laser cannon]]s across clone trooper [[battalion]]s, Republic gunships strafed the Separatist front lines and lost a number to intense [[laser]] flak. As the Trade Federation core ships attempted to take off, Yoda commanded the army's [[Self-Propelled Heavy Artillery]] to bring a number of them crashing back to the ground.<ref name="Episode II" /><ref name="SWCC2016" /> Because the core ships on Geonosis were ringed with air and land defenses, many of them were able to escape to the skies.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" />

===Confronting the Count===
{{Quote|I can't take Dooku alone! I need you! If we catch him, we can end this war right now! We have a job to do!|Obi-Wan Kenobi to Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
====Kenobi vs. Dooku====
{{Quote|As you see, my Jedi powers are far beyond yours. Now&hellip;back down.''"<br />"''I don't think so.|Count Dooku and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:Dooku-bio-5_b3c3c559.jpg|thumb|left|281x281px|Dooku, escaping from the battle with the plans for the "Ultimate Weapon"]]
As the clone troopers pushed back the battle droids,<ref name="Dooku biography gallery image 5">{{Databank|url=count-dooku-biography-gallery|title=Count Dooku Biography Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> Dooku attempted to retreat to a [[Dooku's hangar|hangar]] where his [[Count Dooku's solar sailer|starship]] was docked using a [[Flitknot Speeder Bike|Flitknot speeder]].<ref name="Episode II" /> His escape was covered by the [[Dark Acolyte]] [[Sev'rance Tann]]<ref name="English Prelude to the Clone Wars" /> and another [[Dark Acolyte (Geonosis)|dark acolyte]].<ref name="Prelude to the Clone Wars" /> [[Magistrate]] [[Passel Argente]], who was paying special interest to the service of his Corporate Alliance tank droids, was so absorbed watching the developing battle, which he saw as a spectacle, that he failed to notice it was becoming a defeat for the Separatist Army.<ref name="The Separatists" />

Dooku's escape was spotted by Obi-Wan Kenobi, who directed the gunship carrying them to chase after the count. Unfortunately, the gunship was unable to shoot down the Count's [[speeder]], and Dooku's escorting ''Nantex''-class starfighters slipped in behind it and opened fire. One of their shots knocked Padmé and a clone trooper out of the gunship, but despite Anakin's protestations, they did not go back for her. The gunship continued to pursue Dooku, dropping Kenobi and Skywalker into the Count's [[hangar]] shortly before being blasted out of the sky by laser fire.<ref name="Episode II" />

Upon arriving at Dooku's hangar, Kenobi and Skywalker confronted the Count. As Kenobi began to strategize, Skywalker impatiently charged the Count. Before he could reach Dooku however, the Count intercepted Skywalker with a blast of [[Force lightning]], hurling the young Jedi against a wall and incapacitating him. Dooku and Kenobi then began to circle one another, and Dooku remarked that his powers were far beyond those of his opponent. Ordering Kenobi to back down, he unleashed a blast of [[The Force|Force]] lightning at the Jedi Knight, but Kenobi simply used his lightsaber to absorb the incoming energy and calmly rebuffed Dooku's demand. Dooku then ignited his [[Dooku's lightsaber|own lightsaber]] and awaited Kenobi.

Kenobi initiated the [[lightsaber duel]] by aggressively charging the Count, but Dooku deflected Kenobi's opening attack and his subsequent jab. After a quick flurry of bladework, Kenobi sliced at Dooku's [[Limb|legs]], but the Count leapt over the strike and flourished his blade to drive Kenobi back as he landed. As Kenobi retreated, Dooku remarked upon his disappointment, noting that he had expected a better performance from someone who was held in such high esteem by Yoda.<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:Sai.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Kenobi and Dooku engage one another.]]
A short while later, Kenobi was pulled into a bladelock by the Count. Dooku, a full grin on his face, parried Kenobi's weapon and jabbed at his arm, slicing it open and causing Kenobi to drop his lightsaber. He immediately followed the attack up with a slash across one of Kenobi's legs, and Kenobi collapsed in agony.<ref name="Episode II" />

As Dooku lifted his lightsaber to finish Kenobi, Skywalker managed to recover from the Count's blast of Force lightning. Seeing Kenobi's plight, Skywalker ignited his lightsaber and used the Force to leap across the hanger. As he landed, he managed to intercept what would have been a killing stroke on Kenobi by Dooku. Dooku turned to regard Skywalker and mocked him for his earlier foolishness, and as the two opponents disengaged, Kenobi used the Force to reclaim his fallen lightsaber and threw it to Skywalker.<ref name="Episode II" />

====Skywalker vs. Dooku====
{{Quote|Brave of you, boy, but I would have thought you had learned your lesson.''"<br />"''I'm a slow learner.|Count Dooku and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:FearSurpriseIntimidation-2025SeparatistAllianceCommandCards.png|thumb|left|275px|Skywalker loses his arm to Dooku in battle.]]
Skywalker ignited the second blade and unleashed a flurry of [[Jar'Kai]] bladework upon the Count. Initially taken off his guard by Skywalker's aggressive series of attacks, Dooku briefly retreated but managed after a short time to regain his composure. The Count quickly reestablished his footing and pushed Skywalker back, taking the advantage when he cut apart the hilt of Skywalker's [[Color|green]] blade. After losing his extra weapon, Skywalker launched two quick strikes at the Count and retreated.<ref name="Episode II" />

As he regrouped, Skywalker flicked his remaining weapon through a power cable on the floor, damaging it and causing the hangar lights to flicker out for a brief while. Dooku then slowly advanced on Skywalker and both angled their blades into a high guard position. They spent a short time regarding each other in silence, then engaged in a relatively long and continual bout of swordplay. Though Skywalker managed in that time to hold his own against the Count, Dooku unceremoniously ended the duel by slicing off Anakin's right arm above the elbow. As Anakin shouted in agony, Dooku blasted him aside with a [[Force push]]. The young Jedi skidded over to where Kenobi was lying and lost consciousness.<ref name="Episode II" /> Padme had only just found a gunship to transport her to the hangar, while Mace Windu engaged in combat with two of Dooku's dark acolytes on the valley below.<ref name="Prelude to the Clone Wars" />

====Yoda vs. Dooku====
{{Quote|Fought well you have, my old Padawan.''"<br />"''This is just the beginning.|Yoda and Count Dooku, former master and apprentice|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:YodaVsDooku-CGSWG.png|thumb|right|300px|Yoda duels Dooku.]]
As a visibly winded Dooku caught his breath, Yoda entered the hangar and confronted his former student. Dooku asserted that his diminutive former master would not interfere with his affairs again, and immediately attempted to kill Yoda by [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] flinging a pair of wall fixtures at him. Yoda repelled the fixtures with his own powers but launched no counteroffensive. Dooku then used the Force to break apart a small portion of the hangar ceiling, which he dropped onto Yoda. Again however, Yoda was able to toss the improvised [[missile]]s aside. Yoda noted that Dooku had become powerful but lamented that he could sense the [[dark side of the Force]] in his former student. In response, Dooku boasted that his power was now greater than that of any Jedi, even Yoda's own. He then unleashed a storm of Force lightning at the Grand Master, but Yoda used the Force to catch the bolt with his bare hand. Without any apparent strain, he [[Force deflection|redirected]] the blast back at Dooku, who deflected it into a nearby wall. Dooku proceeded to unleash a second volley against Yoda, but as a demonstration of his superior mastery, Yoda absorbed the bolt completely and remarked that the Count still had much to learn.<ref name="Episode II" />

Recognizing the futility of continuing his Force-based assault, Dooku challenged Yoda to [[lightsaber combat]] and ignited his blade. Yoda silently accepted and ignited his [[Yoda's lightsaber|own lightsaber]], and the two combatants leapt at each other. A brief but very intense duel then ensued in which Yoda was able to gain a slight but visible advantage over the Count. Recognizing that he could not defeat Yoda, and forced to act due to incoming Republic reinforcements, Dooku diverted Yoda's focus by using the Force to collapse one of the hangar's power columns onto Kenobi and Skywalker. Yoda broke off the duel in order to telekinetically catch the power column, preventing it from crushing the two Jedi and hurling it safely aside. Seizing the opportunity, Dooku quickly boarded [[Count Dooku's solar sailer|his ship]] and fled.<ref name="Episode II" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|I have good news for you, my Lord. The war has begun."''<br />''"Excellent. Everything is going as planned.|Darth Tyranus and Darth Sidious|<ref name="Episode II" />|audio=TheWarHasBegun-AOTC.flac}}
[[File:Grand_army_formation.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Grand Army of the Republic amassed in the aftermath of the battle.]]
Count Dooku, who was secretly a Sith Lord called [[Darth]] Tyranus, traveled to Coruscant, to convene with his [[Sith Master|master]] Darth Sidious, who was settled in a [[Inquisitorius Headquarters|deserted building]] in [[The Works]]. Pleased by the news of war, Sidious commanded his apprentice to arrange a second battlefront against the Republic. Meanwhile, the Grand Army of the Republic was assembled throughout [[the galaxy]] as the Clone Wars began.<ref name="Episode II" /> 1,000 more [[Acclamator I-class Assault Ship|''Acclamator I''-class Assault Ships]] were ordered following the battle.<ref name="Encyclopedia RS">{{EncyclopediaCite|Republic and Separatist Ships (1)}}</ref> Jedi injured in the battle were treated at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant, with Jedi Master [[Depa Billaba]] later thanking Padawan [[Barriss Offee]]'s special attention to the injured as the reason for their quick recovery. Offee tried to avoid the war for as long as possible, continuing to work as a healer at the Temple.<ref name="Queen's Hope" /> However, Offee would eventually return to the battlefield and even return to the planet where the conflict began when war returned to Geonosis.<ref>''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' - "[[Weapons Factory]]"</ref>

At the Jedi Temple, Kenobi and Windu both declared their shared belief that the battle would not have been won without the clones. However, Master Yoda was quick to point out the irony in such a pyrrhic victory, as the Republic had only fought their first of many such battles, in what became known as the Clone Wars. The Council also agreed to investigate the Sith Lord that Dooku had mentioned to Obi-Wan Kenobi during the latter's capture, Darth Sidious, in case Dooku was telling the truth. It would not be until three [[Standard year|years]] later, nearing the end of the Clone Wars, that they would finally identify Sidious as being none other than Supreme Chancellor Palpatine. Skywalker, meanwhile, accompanied by Senator Amidala, headed back to Naboo, where they [[Wedding of Anakin Skywalker and Padmé Amidala|married]] in a secret ceremony.<ref name="Episode II" />

With the Clone Wars being declared, members of the Jedi Order became [[General]]s and [[Jedi Commander|Commanders]] in the Grand Army of the Republic.<ref name="The Clone Wars (film)">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> However, the loss of so many Jedi greatly depleted their ranks, with Iskat noting how much emptier the temple felt following the battle. To reinforce their ranks, many Padawans had their trials skipped and were rushed into knighthood, including Iskat, Anakin, Onielle, Zeeth, Tualon, and Charlin.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> Clone trooper "[[Slick]]" participated in the Battle of Geonosis, but he came to the conclusion that the Jedi were not competent [[commander]]s. Slick began to feel anger at the Jedi Order and felt that their defeat would end the war faster, and that this would free his fellow clone troopers from the service that he saw as [[slavery]].<ref name="Slick Front Lines" />

[[File:2nd_Battle_of_Geonosis_2.png|thumb|right|250px|Geonosis would be host to another key battle of the Clone Wars]]
When Republic [[engineer]]s announced that they were unable to reconfigure Geonosian [[Factory|factories]] for Republic needs, its strategic value fell.<ref name="Front Lines" /> This eventually led to the [[Geonosis garrison|Republic occupation force]] stationed on the planet being overrun by Separatist and Geonosian forces, ending the [[occupation of Geonosis]] and leading to the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>

The First Battle of Geonosis was remembered during the [[Imperial Era]] by some as Jedi Master Mace Windu and a criminal gang interfering with legal executions, which sparked the Clone Wars.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Climax of ''Attack of the Clones''===
The First Battle of Geonosis first appeared in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode II" />

[[Samuel L. Jackson]], the actor who played [[Mace Windu]], requested to<ref name="Graham Norton Show">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@BBC|name=BBC|video=ubyo5eZz_Jc|text=Why Samuel L. Jackson has a purple light sabre &#124; The Graham Norton Show - BBC}}</ref> director<ref name="Episode II" /> [[George Lucas]] that his character should have a purple lightsaber because he wanted to be able to find himself in the Geonosis battle scene in ''Attack of the Clones''. He reasoned that, since Windu is the second most powerful Jedi after Yoda, Windu should have a lightsaber of a different color from the customary red, blue, and green.<ref name="Graham Norton Show" />

===Yoda and Count Dooku's duel===
{{Quote|Finally, I got the script, sat down, and my memory of the first draft was, 'In a fight that defies description, Yoda and Count Dooku battle.' That was it. That was just one line.|Rob Coleman|<ref name="Clones at 20">{{SW|url=news/clones-at-20-rob-coleman-interview|text=''Clones'' at 20 &#124; Rob Coleman on Bringing Yoda, Dexter Jettster, and More to Digital Life}}</ref>}}
From the start of the creation of Yoda and Count Dooku's lightsaber duel, most of the work would fall to animation director [[Rob Coleman]]'s team. He didn't get the script until he had been in [[Wikipedia:Sydney|Sydney]], [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]] for one or two [[Standard week|weeks]]. The first draft described the fight as "'In a fight that defies description, Yoda and Count Dooku battle." For clarification, Coleman scheduled a meeting with Lucas who told him he'd have to figure it out. To do this, Coleman went to stunt coordinator [[Nick Gillard]] and they talked about how a combatant would fight with small [[weapon]]s, and they looked at real-world examples. Also, [[Ahmed Best]], the actor who played Jar Jar, introduced Coleman to some [[Wikipedia:Anime|anime]]<ref name="Clones at 20" /> and [[Unarmed combat|martial arts]]<ref name="Ahmed Best help">{{Twitter|ArfKeldo|status/1411905860196921344|[[Rob Coleman]]|quote=We used a lot of references while developing the Yoda/Dooku battle. I consulted with @ahmedbest about martial arts & anime, I studied the action in "Swordsman II" (1992) in particular, I referred to Glen's dynamic drawings and I supervised the gifted animator @timanimator|archivedate=20220207192122}}</ref> that he thought could be helpful. However, Coleman still wasn't sure how to depict Yoda's speed and style in a fight.<ref name="Clones at 20" />

He attended a screening of the [[1992]] film [[Wikipedia:Swordsman II|Swordsman II]] and one of the scenes showed [[Wikipedia:Ninja|ninjas]] leaping between [[tree]]s and bouncing around.<ref name="Clones at 20" /> Coleman studied the action in the film,<ref name="Ahmed Best help" /> and this inspired him to give Yoda the ability to jump around.<ref name="Clones at 20" /> While working on the duel, Coleman referred to [[Glen McIntosh]]'s dynamic drawings.<ref name="Ahmed Best help" /> In the duel, Lucas wanted a shot of Yoda pulling back his duster to reveal his lightsaber as a reference to the iconic shot of the [[Wikipedia:Gunfight at the O.K. Corral|gunfighter at the O.K Corral]]. After finishing the duel, Lucas and Coleman felt happy with it. Despite Coleman's concerns, Lucas wanted Yoda to jump, leap, and flip during the duel. The duel is now considered to be a highlight of the prequel trilogy.<ref name="Clones at 20" />

===Discrepancies===
The [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that the Republic was outnumbered by the Separatist Army by giving the number of [[troop]]s each side used.<ref name="SWCC2016" /> A later reference book, [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', claimed that the Republic held the numerical advantage.<ref name="Front Lines" /> "[[Prologue to the Clone Wars and the First Battle of Geonosis|Prelude to the Clone Wars and the First Battle of Geonosis]]" later affirmed the numbers given by<ref name="Prelude to the Clone Wars" /> ''Complete Locations''.<ref name="SWCC2016" /> This article assumes ''Complete Locations'' and "Prelude to the Clone Wars and the First Battle of Geonosis" are correct.

In the [[2022]] ''Visions'' reference book ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]'' claims that Shad-Rah Varcanella was killed during the events of Order 66<ref name="Art of Visions" /> as opposed to the First Battle of Geonosis, as was envisioned by "The Village Bridge" crew.<ref name="Backstory" /> "The Lost Ones," however, went onto affirm the Battle of Geonosis background for F and Varcanella.<ref name="Lost Ones" />
