The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''31 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3246 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''12 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7946''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*Disappearance of [[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]].<ref name="Vindi">In [[Blue Shadow Virus (episode)|"Blue Shadow Virus"]], which involves the [[raid on Nuvo Vindi's laboratory]], [[Gregar Typho]] states that Vindi disappeared "ten years ago". As the raid is dated to [[21 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', Vindi must have disappeared in 31 BBY.</ref>
*[[Denetrius Vidian|Lemuel Tharsa]], an inspector for the [[Interstellar Thorilide Guild]], contracts [[Shilmer's syndrome]] while inspecting the [[Introsphere]] [[Moonglow Polychemical refinery|refinery]] on [[Gorse]].<ref name="Tharsa">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'' states that [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Hera Syndulla]] met on [[Gorse]], as depicted in the novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], six [[Standard year|years]] prior to the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states that ''Star Wars Rebels'' begins in the [[Standard year|year]] [[5 BBY]], thereby dating the events of ''A New Dawn'' to [[11 BBY]]. During the [[Gorse Conflict]], Jarrus, Syndulla, [[Zaluna Myder]] and [[Skelly]], investigating [[Denetrius Vidian]]'s identity, discover that he was once Lemuel Tharsa via hologram footage taken twenty years before during his visit to the [[Introsphere]] facility. Thus, math indicates the visit took place in 31 BBY.</ref>
*[[Hetto]] begins working at [[World Window Plaza]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="A New Dawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref>
*[[Kaeden Larte|Kaeden]] and [[Miara Larte]] and their parents move to [[Raada]].<ref name="Lartes">In [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']], Kaeden Larte says that her family moved to Raada when she was four and Miara was one. In the novel, set one year after the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], Kaeden and Miara are respectively 17 and 14 years old. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the rise of the Empire to [[19 BBY]], it can be calculated that the novel is set in [[18 BBY]] and the Lartes moved to Raada in 31 BBY.</ref>

==Births==
*[[Jaybo Hood]] on [[Iego]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="JayboAge">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/mystery-of-a-thousand-moons-episode-gallery|text="Mystery of a Thousand Moons" Episode Gallery}} states that Jaybo Hood is 10 [[Standard year|years]] old in {{TCW|Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of this episode to [[21 BBY]], meaning that Jaybo Hood was born in 31 BBY.</ref>

Therefore, Sevander must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around 31 BBY.</ref>
*[[Chanath Cha]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Chanath">[[Hidden Empire 3|''Hidden Empire'' 3]] states that [[Chanath Cha]] was twelve [[Standard year|years]] old when her [[parent]]s were [[Death|killed]] in [[Darth Vader (2017) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 11]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore Cha was [[Reproduction|born]] around 31 BBY.</ref>
*[[Rebolt]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Rebolt">"[[Tobias Beckett and Other Characters of the Underworld]]" establishes that [[Qi'ra]] was born in 31 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. As ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' states that [[Rebolt]] was approximately the same age as Qi'ra, he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around 31 BBY.</ref>
*[[Jagleo]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Jagleo">"[[Tobias Beckett and Other Characters of the Underworld]]" establishes that [[Qi'ra]] was born in 31 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. As ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' states that [[Syke]] was approximately the same age as Qi'ra, he must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around 31 BBY</ref>
*[[Salbee]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Salbee">"[[Tobias Beckett and Other Characters of the Underworld]]" establishes that [[Qi'ra]] was [[Reproduction|born]] in 31 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. As ''[[Crimson Climb]]'' states that [[Salbee]] was approximately the same age as Qi'ra, she must have been born around that 31 BBY.</ref>
*[[Hawk (Advanced Recon Commando)|Hawk]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Hawk">Per his character folio, Hawk was born 10 years before ''[[Begun, The Clone Wars Have!]]'', which takes place in [[21 BBY]].</ref>
*[[Unidentified man (Five Points Station)|Unidentified man]] {{Circa}}<ref name="man">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Jyn Erso]]'s [[Reproduction|birth]] to [[21 BBY]]. According to ''[[Rebel Rising]]'', Jyn Erso was 18 years old when she met [[Unidentified man (Five Points Station)|the man]] aboard [[Five Points]] [[Space station|station]], so they met in [[3 BBY]]. Since ''Rebel Rising'' states that the man was about ten years older than Jyn, he must have been born around 31 BBY.</ref>
*[[Thela]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Thela">In "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]", Thela mentions that he was 12 years old when the [[Tarkin Massacre]] occurred. According to "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]", the Tarkin Massacre occurred 16 years before the events of the episode. As the events of the episode are established to occur in [[3 BBY]], the Tarkin Massacre must have occurred in [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, Thela must have been [[Reproduction|born]] around [[31 BBY]].</ref>
