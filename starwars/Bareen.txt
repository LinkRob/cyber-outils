'''Bareen''' was a terrestrial astronomical object that housed a [[Unidentified distillery|distillery]] during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Hunt for the Falcon 1" /> Between around [[31 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Han Solo - Hunt for the Falcon 1|''Han Solo - Hunt for the Falcon'' 1]] take place between the theft of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' by [[Gannis Ducain]], as depicted in "[[Flight of the Falcon, Part 5: Grand Theft Falcon]]," and the events of {{Film|VII}}. The events of "Grand Theft Falcon" take place no earlier than around [[31 ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:SequelEraContentDate|here]], while ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> as the [[human]] [[Racer|racing]] team owners<ref name="Hunt for the Falcon 1" /> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> and [[Han Solo]] shared a [[glass]] of [[ale]] on<ref name="Hunt for the Falcon 1" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[planet]] [[Malastare]], Calrissian praised the quality of the [[Alcohol|drink]], wondering if it was from the distillery on Bareen.<ref name="Hunt for the Falcon 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bareen was mentioned in the [[Han Solo - Hunt for the Falcon 1|first issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo - Hunt for the Falcon]]''. The issue was written by [[Rodney Barnes]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Hunt for the Falcon 1" /> on [[September 3]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122242/star_wars_han_solo_-_hunt_for_the_falcon_2025_1|text=Star Wars: Han Solo - Hunt for the Falcon (2025) #1}}</ref>
