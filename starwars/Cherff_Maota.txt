'''Cherff Maota''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Master]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]], and trained [[Avar Kriss]] as their [[Padawan]].<ref name="Rising Storm" /> Maota served as a [[Jedi seeker|seeker]] in the [[Jedi Order]] and encountered the [[Force-sensitive]] infant [[Aloxian]] [[Zint]] in [[230 BBY]], when he brought her into the Order. He also encountered a young [[Sifo-Dyas]] on [[Minashee]], and brought him to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] so that he could become a [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> [[Maz Kanata]] owned a bust in Maota's image, which she kept in her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bust of Cherff Maota was one of the props created for the castle of Maz Kanata in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] ([[December 18]], [[2015]]).<ref name="TFAVD" /> In the movie's [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|novelization]], the private collection of Kanata notably includes what is described as "a bust of some unknown bearded [[human]]."<ref name="Novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> Maota's bust, however, clearly has [[Alien|non-human]] features.<ref name="TFAVD" />
