'''Actlyon''' was a terrestrial [[planet]] located in the [[Actlyon system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Jjannex sector]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Unidentified cantina (Actlyon City)|cantina]] operated in its [[Actlyon City]]. The [[Gender|girl]] [[Haze Delik]]'s [[parent]]s worked in the [[Cantina|establishment]] but were [[Death|killed]] by stray blasts in a cantina shootout. By [[34 ABY]], another [[Unidentified cantina (Actlyon)|cantina]] was operating in the [[Actlyon City lower district|lower district]] of Actlyon City. That [[Standard year|year]], the [[smuggler]] [[Ana Tolla]]'s [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]] docked their [[Ana Tolla's ship|starship]] and the [[Meridian (light freighter)|''Meridian'']]&mdash;the personal [[Light freighter|vessel]] of one of the group's members, [[Izal Garsea]]&mdash;in the world's [[Actlyon City port|port]] in order to restock before their next [[Skirmish at Kat Saka's farm|mission]].

The smugglers visited the Actlyon City cantina on their last [[Local day|night]] on the world, deciding to leave Garsea behind on the planet instead of including her in their next endeavor. Shortly afterward, the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Pall Gopal]] met with Garsea in the establishment and hired her to deliver a package for him to the [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]] on Batuu. The smuggler departed Actlyon shortly afterward, setting a [[hyperspace]] course for her new destination.

==Description==
{{Quote|I need someone to deliver a parcel to the Outer Rim.''"<br />"''We are in the Outer Rim.|Pall Gopal and Izal Garsea|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
The terrestrial [[planet]] Actlyon was located<ref name="ACOF" /> in the [[Actlyon system]] of the [[Jjannex sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> within the [[Western Reaches]] portion<ref name="Western Reaches" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The world was situated in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="GalaxyMap" /> The [[Lipsec Run]]<ref name="LipsecRun" /> [[hyperspace route]] linked the world to both the [[Rycep asteroid belt]] and the Outer Rim<ref name="GalaxyMap" /> planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Taul]],<ref name="GalaxyMap" /> while another route connected it to the Outer Rim planet [[Batuu]]. Actlyon had [[mountain]]ous terrain, [[Color|rusty]] skies,<ref name="ACOF" /> and a Type I [[atmosphere]]<ref name="Atmosphere" /> that was breathable to multiple [[species]].<ref name="ACOF" /> By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the world's atmosphere was polluted and foul-smelling.<ref name="ACOF" />

==History==
===Shootouts===
{{Quote|What happened to your parents? Did you get separated?''"<br />"''We left Helikoprion. They're dead. I came here alone.|The [[ensign]] [[Welga]] and Haze Delik, after the latter's parents' death on Actlyon|<ref name="BTN" />}}
[[File:NihilEye-PhaseIII.svg|thumb|right|220px|After her parents' deaths, Haze Delik joined the Nihil (emblem pictured), hoping to never feel as helpless as she did on Actlyon.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], a [[Unidentified cantina (Actlyon City)|cantina]] operated within the planet's [[Actlyon City]] and employed the [[parent]]s of the [[Heliko]] [[Gender|girl]] [[Haze Delik]] in its kitchens. One [[Local day|night]], a brawl broke out in the [[Cantina|establishment]] and both Delik's [[Haze Delik's mother|mother]] and [[Haze Delik's father|father]] were [[Death|killed]] by stray blasts. Meanwhile, the girl hid in a storage closet, traumatized by the sound of screams coming from the kitchen.<ref name="BTN" />

Afterward, Delik departed Actlyon and joined the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], promising to herself she would never be as scared or helpless as she was in the cantina again.<ref name="BTN" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="BTNDate">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the girl reflected on the deaths of her parents on Actlyon. While on the planet [[Palagosal]]<ref name="BTN" /> that year,<ref name="BTNDate" /> Delik visited the [[Restaurant|diner]] [[Marl's Good Enough Eats]], and it reminded her of her parents' prior workplace.<ref name="BTN" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Actlyon was part of the [[New Republic]]'s territories.<ref name="MandoVG" />

===Spy operations===
{{Quote|How did you know I'd be here?''"<br />"''Sad when a generation loses belief, isn't it? But to answer your question, I recognized the ship. I'd heard about what happened&mdash;years ago. Then I saw your name on the ship manifest.|Izal Garsea and Pall Gopal, in an Actlyon City cantina|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
[[File:BuyerBeware-DL.png|thumb|left|220px|While on Actlyon, Pall Gopal searched for a way to transport a package to Dok-Ondar (pictured) on Batuu for the Resistance.]]
By the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the [[musician]] [[Pall Gopal]] settled on Actlyon and began playing with the [[band]] [[Sentient 7 and the Clankers]] in an Actlyon City [[Unidentified cantina (Actlyon)|cantina]]. At some point, Gopal started operating as a Resistance [[spy]] on the world. He eventually acquired a [[Pall Gopal's package|package]] that the Resistance needed to send to the [[Ithorian]] [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]] on Batuu and began searching for a way to [[transport]] it to its destination safely.<ref name="ACOF" /> In 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] and [[author]] [[Eloc Throno]] included Actlyon on a [[map]] in his Batuu [[Book|guidebook]], ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Ana Tolla's crew|crew]] of the [[smuggler]] [[Ana Tolla]] visited Actlyon after concluding their last mission, parking their [[Ana Tolla's ship|ship]]&mdash;and the [[light freighter]] [[Meridian (light freighter)|''Meridian'']], the personal [[Starship|vessel]] of the crew member [[Izal Garsea]]&mdash;in the planet's [[Actlyon City port|port]]. After its arrival, Gopal recognized the ''Meridian'', having originally [[Currency|sold]] the light [[freighter]] to Garsea's mother, [[Ixel Garsea]]. He later looked at the ship's manifest, learning that Izal was the ''Meridian''{{'s}} current owner, and resolved to contact the woman to transport his package offworld.<ref name="ACOF" />

While on Actlyon, Tolla's crew prepared for their next endeavor by resupplying their ship and completing repairs. Izal begrudgingly completed several tasks for her boss, finding the errands redundant and a waste of time when she could instead be upgrading the ''Meridian''. However, Tolla informed the rest of her colleagues that she intended not to bring Izal on their next [[Skirmish at Kat Saka's farm|engagement]] to Batuu, despite the smuggler's boyfriend, [[Damar Olin]], remaining part of the group.<ref name="ACOF" />

===Cantina abandonment===
{{Quote|We have to go, ''now''! Now, Olin!''"<br />"''Izzy. I'm sorry. The job&mdash;Ana&mdash;What I mean is&mdash;You're not coming. Don't hate me, please.|Ana Tolla and Damar Olin, to the latter and Izal Garsea|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
On the crew's last [[Standard day|day]] on Actlyon&mdash;which was also Izal's eighteenth [[birthday]]&mdash;Tolla led her companions to an Actlyon City cantina for a celebratory night out before they departed on their next mission. Though most of the smugglers enjoyed their [[Foodstuff|meal]] and drinks, Izal felt disgruntled by Olin's dismissive behavior and Tolla's disparaging treatment of her. She then fetched drinks for the rest of Tolla's crew, overhearing the cantina's patrons discuss the recent activities of the Resistance fighting the First Order.<ref name="ACOF" />

[[File:GalaxysEdge-Slider.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Ana Tolla's crew abandoned Izal Garsea on Actlyon, leaving on their next mission to Batuu (pictured) without her.]]
Meanwhile, while playing with his band, Gopal surveyed Garsea from across the cantina and recognized the woman. Though he attempted to catch her attention by winking at her from his place on the stage, Izal ignored him. Shortly after she returned to her table, the smuggler confronted Olin for ruining her birthday, as he had not kept his promise of making the day special. However, their conversation was interrupted when a gang of [[bounty hunter]]s raided the establishment, kidnapping a [[Unidentified Trandoshan waitress|Trandoshan waitress]] for her debts. Tolla departed the cantina with her crew as the skirmish began and left Garsea behind, not intending to take the woman with them on the Batuu mission. After making their way to the world's [[Spaceport|port]], the smugglers left Actlyon.<ref name="ACOF" />

Left alone in the cantina, Izal was approached by Gopal, who then explained his connection with her parents. Following some negotiation, she agreed to transport his package to Dok-Ondar on Batuu, with half the payment upfront and half upon delivery. The smuggler then [[Sleep|slept]] on the ''Meridian'' for a few [[Standard hour|hours]] before departing Actlyon to deliver the package. As she exited the world's atmosphere, Izal calculated her route to Batuu and then jumped into [[hyperspace]]. Later, while on Batuu, the smuggler thought about her time on Actlyon. When she and Olin encountered each other on Batuu, the latter assured her he would not have left with Tolla's crew in Actlyon City if he had not been sure she would be fine on her own. Not long thereafter, however, Izal considered that the group's abandonment of her on Actlyon had been for the best as she helped save the [[Batuuan]] [[farmer]] [[Kat Saka]]'s [[Saka Homestead|farm]], which Tolla's crew had been trying to [[Saboteur|sabotage]].<ref name="ACOF" />

==Inhabitants==
During the High Republic Era, the Heliko girl Haze Delik and her parents lived on Actlyon.<ref name="BTN" /> By the time of the First Order&ndash;Resistance War, the [[Rodian]] Pall Gopal resided on the world. The planet was also home to hundreds of smugglers and pirates.<ref name="ACOF" /> In 34 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> individuals of several species were employed in an Actlyon City cantina, including Gopal, a [[Trandoshan]], and two [[Twi'lek]]s.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Locations==
{{Quote|If you're mad about Actlyon City, I know you're a good shot. I wouldn't have left if I thought you couldn't take care of yourself.|Damar Olin, to Izal Garsea|<ref name="ACOF" />}}
Actlyon had a [[city]] of the same name with a cantina by the time of the High Republic Era.<ref name="BTN" /> By 34 ABY,<ref name="Date" /> a cantina operated in Actlyon City's [[Actlyon City lower district|lower district]], featuring a patio and a stage area for musicians to perform. Actlyon also had a port, which smugglers and pirates used to land their ships.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|That was intentional, because I&hellip;you know sometimes when you create these planets you never know if you're gonna be able to return to them.|Zoraida Córdova|<ref name="Interview">{{WookYouTube|bJUdOoC6kTw|Wookieepedia Interviews - Zoraida Córdova}}</ref>}}
Actlyon first appeared in ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', a [[2019]] young-adult novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="ACOF" /> A mention of the planet was later included in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', also authored by Córdova and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="BTN" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> In an interview, Córdova confirmed the reference to Actlyon had been an intentional callback to her previous ''[[Star Wars]]'' work.<ref name="Interview" />
