By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] fought a '''battle''' on [[Cartao]], a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Battlefront" /> in the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> While on the [[planet]] [[Sullust]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Hazram Namir]], the acting [[captain]] of the 61st, asked the [[chief of engineering]] [[Vifra]] if she had been present for the battle on Cartao. When the [[engineer]] told him she had not, Namir promised to tell her about the battle another [[time]].<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
