'''Durasteel struts''' were a high quality [[speeder]] part. They were the only things strong enough to secure an aftermarket [[hydro-repulsor]] to a [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> members of [[Surat Nuat's gang]] took [[Temmin Wexley]]'s best parts, including durasteel struts, while he was at a [[Unidentified junkyard (Great Basin)|junkyard]] near [[Mount Selasa]] on [[Akiva]]. He was later saved from members of the gang by a [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] who was hoping Temmin would install a hydro-repulsor on her [[S-57 Cardinal]] speeder bike. After explaining that members of the gang stole his struts, Vess had him install the repulsor anyway so she could make to the [[Surat Nuat's island|gang's island]] in [[Lake Amari]] where the gang took the stolen struts and recover them. A [[Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport]] left the island with the parts as Vess arrived. She then proceeded to chase the transport to [[Surat Nuat's jungle base|another base]] in the [[Stormhollow]] region of the planet where she ultimately recovered the struts. She later returned them to Temmin in [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Akiva, where he used them to secure the repulsor for Vess.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Durasteel struts appeared as a speeder upgrade in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was part of the game's quest "The Scavenger".<ref name="Outlaws" />
