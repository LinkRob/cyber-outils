The '''Arkanian polar drake''' was a type of creature from [[Arkania]]. It was considered sacred by the [[Arkanian]]s and was a protected species by law.<ref name="UC">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>

The [[Transport|cargo vessel]] ''[[Sketter's Quarrel]]'' was carrying an Arkanian polar drake on board while en route to deliver exotic creatures, many of which were illegal to personally own, to [[Regent]] [[Solculvis]] of the [[Mol'leaj system]]. During the journey, the crew of the [[Starship|vessel]] were [[Death|killed]] by [[kheilwar wasp]]s that had escaped from a damaged cargo container, leaving the vessel dead in space.<ref name="S&V" />

Around [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' dates the discovery of the ''[[Sketter's Quarrel]]'' to 8011 [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] and gives 7980 C.R.C. as the [[Standard year|year]] that the [[carbon-freezing]] of [[Han Solo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]]. Therefore, the discovery of the ''Sketter's Quarrel'' must therefore take place around [[34 ABY]].</ref> the ship was discovered and members of the [[Sector Ranger]]s catalogued its contents, including the polar drake. Information from the Rangers' file on the case was later published in the [[book]] ''[[Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was authored by [[Law enforcement agency|Police]] [[Commissioner]] [[Exantor Divo]].<ref name="S&V" />

==Behind the scenes==
An Arkanian polar drake was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="S&V" />
