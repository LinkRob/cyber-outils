{{Quote|I think after all my wanderings, I can recognize the description of a Jedi outpost when I hear it, Luke.''"<br />"''I'm not questioning your expertise&mdash;I just didn't realize the Jedi ever had an official presence so far from the Core.|Lor San Tekka and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />}}
'''Elphrona''' was a rocky [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was home to an ancient [[High Republic Era]] [[Jedi]] [[Elphrona Outpost|outpost]] used as a treasure trove for a number of artifacts. While attempting to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] [[Mission to Elphrona|visited]] the outpost with the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Lor San Tekka]] and his own student, [[Ben Solo]]. After escaping the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of Skywalker's [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi temple]] and intending to join the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] enclave known as the [[Knights of Ren]], Solo was tracked to Elphrona by [[Hennix]], [[Tai]], and [[Voe]], three of Skywalker's students who survived. In the resulting [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], Hennix was [[Death|killed]], and Solo escaped, leaving Tai and Voe trapped in the ruins of the Jedi outpost.

==Description==
{{Quote|You're sure about this, Lor?''"<br />"''As sure as I can be. My contact told me a great deal about the location.|Luke Skywalker and Lor San Tekka|<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />}}
Located on the edge of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], near the [[Unknown Regions]], Elphrona was a terrestrial [[planet]] with rocky terrain.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" /> An [[ore]] world, Elphrona's terrain consisted of [[iron]] hills,<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> as well as cliffs and [[mountain]]s. It had an [[atmosphere]] that was breathable to several [[species]], including [[human]]s,<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 3">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 3|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 3]]</ref> but had harsh conditions. Some regions of the planet were magnetized in such a way as to make air travel impossible in those areas, and the powerful magnetic field lines underlying this phenomena were a major factor in the formation of the planet's distinct topography. [[Rust storm]]s were a known weather phenomenon. Locals considered any natural precipitation to be an exceedingly rare event, with as long as a decade between rains. However Elphrona had at least some natural bodies of water located in underground reservoirs and lakes far below its mineral rich surface.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

Native fauna included [[chromant]]s, [[steelee]]s, and the [[Dog|canine]] [[Species|creatures]] known as [[charhound]]s.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==History==
===Jedi activity===
{{Quote|It's probably High Republic Era. It was a time of greatly expanded Jedi activity throughout the galaxy.|Lor San Tekka|<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />}}
During the [[High Republic Era]], in a [[time]] of expanded [[Jedi]] activity throughout the [[galaxy]], Elphrona was visited by the [[Jedi Order]], who constructed an [[Elphrona Outpost|outpost]] within the side of a cliff. The Jedi used the outpost as a treasure trove, storing a number of [[Jedi artifact|artifacts]] within.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" /> The outpost was in operation<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> by [[382 BBY]],<ref name="382 Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' and ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> during which time it was under the [[marshal]]ship of [[Jedi Master]] [[Rinn (Duros)|Rinn]].<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> After discovering that Jedi Master [[Zallah Macri]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] had [[Mission to Dalna (Macri and Zink)|met their ends]] on the planet [[Dalna]] at the hands of an [[Great Leveler|unknown weapon]],<ref name="Path of Dec">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Azlin Rell]] ventured to Elphrona with their remains to meet with Rinn&mdash;whom Rell had known since he was a Padawan&mdash;and share his discovery. In response, Rinn took Rell with him to the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Coruscant]], where they shared the news with the [[Jedi High Council]] themselves. Unnerved by the entire ordeal, Rell himself had wanted Rinn to fully take over the investigation, yet he remained involved as matters spun out of control into the [[Night of Sorrow|Battle of Dalna]] two months after his discovery.<ref name="Path of Vengeance" />

Later in the High Republic Era, the Jedi Master [[Loden Greatstorm]] was stationed at the outpost with his Padawan, [[Bell Zettifar]], working to keep the peace. Zettifar owned a pet charhound named [[Ember]] while on Elphrona,<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> and often enjoyed wandering the planet's hills with the creature.<ref name="Padawans">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-the-high-republic-meet-the-padawans|text=Inside ''Star Wars: The High Republic'': Meet the Padawans}}</ref> Also stationed at the outpost were Master [[Porter Engle]] and Jedi Knight [[Indeera Stokes]]. Aware of the outpost and figuring that the [[Nihil]] might succeed in capturing a Jedi, [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] approved a [[raid on Elphrona]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|It was on the planet where we first met. Where you fought Luke Skywalker.''"<br />"''How can I forget?''"<br />"''I went back there to see if I could find a path to you and the other Knights, but I was followed somehow. By three ''Jedi''. Students of Luke Skywalker.|Ben Solo and Ren|<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 3" />}}
[[File:KnightsOfRenCircleLorLukeBen-TheRiseOfKyloRen2.jpg|thumb|left|280px|Luke Skywalker confronts the Knights of Ren in the outpost on Elphrona.]]
Many [[Standard year|years]] later in [[19 ABY]],<ref name="Elphrona Date">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]'' states that the mission to [[Elphrona]] took place the [[Standard year|year]] [[Ben Solo]] turned fourteen. ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that Solo was born twenty-nine years prior to the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the mission took place in [[19 ABY]].</ref> after the [[Great Jedi Purge|fall]] of the Jedi Order and during Jedi Master [[Luke Skywalker]]'s attempt at restoring it, he visited Elphrona alongside his student, [[Ben Solo]], and the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Lor San Tekka]], who had learned of the planet and its outpost. At the same time, the [[Knights of Ren]], a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] organization led by the mysterious "[[Ren (human)|Ren]]," also entered the Jedi outpost, hoping to [[Thief|steal]] the artifacts for themselves. Skywalker, intending to retrieve the artifacts for his new Jedi Order, encountered the Knights within the outpost. Skywalker confronted the Knights, refusing to allow them to take the artifacts. The Jedi Master managed to defeat the Knights of Ren, whose leader agreed to leave, but not before attempting to tempt Solo to turn to the dark side and join them.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />

[[File:ElphronaOutpostDestruction-TheRiseOfKyloRen3.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Ben Solo destroys the Elphrona Outpost.]]
In [[28 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> after witnessing the [[Destruction of the Jedi Temple|destruction]] of Skywalker's [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi temple]] and believing he had killed Skywalker,<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref> Solo, in search of the Knights of Ren, returned to the Jedi outpost on Elphrona. He put on the mask of Ren, who had left it in the outpost for Solo to eventually find. Communicating with Ren through the mask, Solo was instructed to go to the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world [[Varnak]]. However, before he could leave, he was confronted by [[Hennix]], [[Tai]], and [[Voe]], three of Skywalker's former students who had survived the destruction of the [[Jedi temple|temple]].<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />

Defending himself from his attackers, who believed he was responsible for the temple's destruction, Solo began to [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] his former fellow students. Solo [[Force jump|leapt]] to the top of a cliff, where he was followed by Voe. Driven by anger, Solo attempted to [[Force push]] Voe off of the cliff, and was attacked by Hennix, who threw his [[lightsaber]] at Solo. In attempting to deflect the lightsaber, Solo [[Death|killed]] Hennix, and subsequently chose to leave the other two students alive, trapping them inside the Jedi outpost and collapsing it on top of them. Solo departed Elphrona, heading for Varnak, where he met up with Ren and the Knights of Ren.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 3" />

===A research mission===
{{Quote|Today, the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts has sent use to Elphrona to answer a burning question--do charhounds play fetch?|Aree|<ref name="Charhound" />}}
The [[Safari droid]] [[SF-R3]] of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] ventured to Elphrona in [[34 ABY]] to discover if [[charhound]]s played fetch. Aree travelled to a cave on the planet, in which he found a charhound. However, when he tried to play catch with the charhound, the hot temperatures of the charhound burned the rod. Aree then tried a reinforced rod, which also burned. Aree kept trying different rods, provided by CAM who took them from his ship. The rods all burned. About to give up, Aree noticed some heat resistant [[crystal]]s. Trying to play fetch one last time, the heat-resistant crystals worked, and Aree played fetch with the charhound.<ref name="Charhound">{{GoC|Charhound}}</ref>
