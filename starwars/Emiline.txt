'''Emiline''' was a [[goat]] present at the [[Death Star's shield generator]] during the [[Battle of Endor]]. The [[Sacred Order of Ramulus]], who sought to archive all of [[The galaxy|galactic]] history, stored a [[Hologram|holographic]] simulation of the location, including the goat, at the [[Observatory HoloSimulator]] attached to the archive known as the [[Observatory (The Island)|Observatory]]. Long after the battle, the [[Shozer]] [[Wett Zantu]] found the goat in the simulation and named her "Emiline." Despite only being the hologram of a goat, Wett Zantu became attached and worried about Emiline when the Observatory and HoloSimulator became plagued by a [[Computer virus|virus]] called the [[Corruption (virus)|corruption]].<ref name="Castaways" />
