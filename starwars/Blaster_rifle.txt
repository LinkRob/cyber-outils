{{Quote|Sometimes a blaster rifle is all you need to get the answers you need.|[[Imara Vex]]|<ref name="SWHunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>}}
'''Blaster rifles''' were weapons more powerful and larger than other types of [[blaster]]s, such as [[blaster pistol]]s. It was used in various factions.<ref name="Databank" /> [[Stormtrooper]]s of the [[Imperial Army]] were armed with [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]]s. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used many different types of blasters, as they could not afford to mass produce weapons. However, the [[A280 blaster rifle]] was the standard-issue Alliance rifle.<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

[[File:E-11_blaster_rifle_DICE.png|thumb|250px|left|The E-11 medium blaster rifle was the most commonly used weapon by Imperial stormtroopers.]]
[[Pulse cannon]]s were powerful blaster rifles.<ref name="BF">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> [[Assault rifle]]s were modular versions of the blaster rifle.<ref name="UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Longblaster]]s had high precision and power over long ranges.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> [[Boiler rifle]]s were illegal weapons.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Stun rifle]]s were a type of blaster rifle and stun weaponry.<ref name="Savage Spirits">''[[Savage Spirits]]''</ref> The [[Relby-v10 micro grenade launcher]],<ref name="GD">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]''</ref> [[blaster spear]],<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> [[bo-rifle]],<ref name="Rebels VD">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> and the [[SP-B50 blaster rifle]] were blaster/hybrids.<ref name="ROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

Blaster rifle length ranged from the short E-11 and [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] to the longer A280 and [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]. Rifles almost always had a [[scope]], a fully automatic firing mode, and some kind of [[stock]]. Other forms of blaster [[rifle]]s<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> included [[blaster carbine]]s,<ref name="Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[heavy blaster rifle]]s,<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> [[light repeating blaster]]s, and [[sniper rifle]]s.<ref name="Databank" />
