'''Ainlee Teem''' was a [[Gran]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> [[senator]] who [[Malastare delegation|represented]] the [[planet]] of [[Malastare]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the last [[Standard decade|decades]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. When the [[Trade Federation]] [[Invasion of Naboo|invaded]] the planet [[Naboo]], the then-[[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]] was voted out of his [[Office of the Chancellor|office]]. Along with [[Bail Antilles]] of [[Alderaan]] and [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of Naboo, Teem was nominated as Valorum's potential successor.<ref name="TPM" /> However, Teem's reputation was tainted with suspicions of corruption, and his candidacy was met with general disdain.<ref name="Encyclopedia Naboo Crisis">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Crisis on Naboo}}</ref> Ultimately, it was Palpatine who won the election.<ref name="TPM" /> Teem continued to serve the Senate during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Senate Murders" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ainlee Teem was first mentioned in the [[1999]] film {{Film|I}}<ref name="TPM" /> and later made his first appearance in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Senate Murders]],"<ref name="Senate Murders" /> later returning in the episode "[[Crisis at the Heart]]."<ref name="CrisisHeart">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref> However, Teem was not identified by name in either episode;<ref name="Senate Murders" /><ref name="CrisisHeart" /> instead, the Gran was only confirmed to be Teem in the [[Guide to the Galaxy]] section of [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 64|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 64]].<ref name="GuidetoGalaxy">{{BuildFalconCite|64|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref>
