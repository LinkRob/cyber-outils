'''Aaston Lok''', designated '''Prisoner Five-Nine-One''', was a [[prisoner]] located on [[Starlag XIX]]. 

== Biography ==
At some point, Aaston Lok became a prisoner of [[Tolteck]], who imprisoned him in his private prison, [[Starlag XIX]].<ref name="Final Round" /> In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"Final Round" features the ''[[Phantom II]]'', which first appeared in "[[The Last Battle]]," and it also mentions [[Jun Sato]], who died in "[[Zero Hour]]." ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'' places "The Last Battle" seventeen [[Standard year|years]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]], meaning that "The Last Battle" takes place in [[2 BBY]]. ''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]'' places the events of "Zero Hour" 17 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|formation]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], which occurred in [[19 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', meaning that "Zero Hour" takes place in 2 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], and "Final Round" takes place in the same year.</ref> Lok was set to fight the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebels]]' champion, [[Garazeb Orrelios]], for whether [[Ketsu Onyo]] would be released or remain prisoner on Starlag XIX. However, upon entering the arena, he identified Orrelios as [[Lasat]] and refused to fight him. Toltreck, embarrassed by the prisoner's refusal, activated Lok's [[shock collar]] and ordered him to fight. Enraged after being electrocuted, he knocked Orrelios off the arena platform when the Lasat tried to calm him down. However, he caught the falling Orrelios by his leg, believing that Orrelios was the last of his kind.<ref name="Final Round" />

After pulling him back into the arena, he told Orrelios to fake fight so that he did not get shocked again. Two other rebels, [[Hera Syndulla]] and [[Rex]], were awaiting the result, but had enough of the situation. They attempted to leave but learned that Toltreck had sold them to [[Drisk]]. Rex then told a second team of rebels, [[Tonk's crew]] to set off an explosive that damaged the facility, disabling the prisoners' shock collars. This caused general mayhem as the prisoners all attempted to escape  Starlag XIX simultaneously. As the base began to fall apart, Lok offered to guide Orrelios and his fellow rebels back to their [[shuttle]] in order to escape. Orrelios in turn offered Lok a ticket out of the [[prison]], but Lok refused and held up the doors to the complex to allow the rebels to escape.<ref name="Final Round" />

== Traits and personality ==
Lok was a tall, large, [[Gender|male]] of an unidentified species with blue skin and yellow eyes. He was strong and powerful.<ref name="Final Round" />
