The '''Blacklip Pirates''' were a [[pirate]] organization whose members arrived in the [[Anoat sector]] seeking for plunder exactly as the [[Galactic Empire]] decided to [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] the whole [[sector]]. The pirates managed to scrap three Imperial [[cruiser]]s before fleeing toward [[Ozu]].<ref name="Black Lip Pirate" /> The [[Gender|female]] [[Zabrak]] [[Daen Nark]] was the organization's matriarch.<ref name="Daen Nark" />
