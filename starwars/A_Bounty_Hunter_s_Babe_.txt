"'''A Bounty Hunter's Babe!'''" is an interview of [[Mercedes Ngoh]] conducted by [[Pat Jankiewicz|Patrick Jankiewicz]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 111|''Star Wars Insider'' 111]]. Ngoh discusses her experience playing [[Rystáll Sant/Legends|Rystáll]], as well as meeting [[Femi Taylor]] and [[George Lucas]].
