'''Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine''' was a location in [[Realspace|space]] used for the regrouping of the scattered [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] forces during the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> following the [[Galactic Empire]]'s devastating [[Battle of Hoth|attack]] on [[Echo Base]], the Rebellion's base of operations constructed on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]].<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>

After the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} [[escape from Cloud City]], the [[starship]] joined the Alliance's [[Fourth Division]], which gathered at the rendezvous point after being nearly destroyed by an Imperial [[Battle at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three|attack]] at [[Rendezvous Point Delta-Three]]. At the [[time]], [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] speculated that they could be what remained of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]].<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> Afterwards, [[Luke Skywalker]] had his lost [[Limb|hand]] replaced by a [[Mechno-arm|robotic one]] on<ref name="ESB" /> the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Redemption]]'' at the rendezvous point.<ref name="EOFSAOV">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref>

Gathered at the rendezvous point were several [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s, [[BTL Y-wing starfighter]]s, and [[GR-75 medium transport]]s,<ref name="ESB" /> alongside two EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigates, including the ''Redemption'', the ''Millennium Falcon'', as well as at least three [[CR90 corvette]]s.<ref name="Star Wars 1" /> Several other starships were also present.

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Backup_Rendezvous_Point_Gamma-Nine.png|thumb|right|260px|The rendezvous point, as depicted in ''Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures'']]
The rendezvous point first appeared in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], the second installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="ESB" /> The location remained unidentified in [[canon]] until the release of [[Star Wars (2020) 1|''Star Wars'' (2020) 1]] in [[2020]], which gave the name "Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine." However, in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the rendezvous point was codenamed and identified in [[1996]] as "[[Haven (rendezvous point)|Haven]]."<ref name="SoL">''[[Shield of Lies]]''</ref> In the ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]'' short "[[Clash on Cloud City]]," the rebel ships gathered at the rendezvous point are depicted to be leaving it through [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Clash on Cloud City">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures|text=Clash on Cloud City|video=h6dPVI_AKzA}}</ref>
