The '''Battle of Ab Dalis'''<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> was a battle that occurred on [[Ab Dalis]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] during the Alliance's [[Operation Starlight]]. The [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|ISD]] ''[[Ultima II]]'' under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Kalaxo]] trapped the Alliance [[Eleventh Division]] on the surface of Ab Dalis. The Rebel [[Ground Defense Command]] under [[Colonel]] [[Chouch]] organized the surface defense against Imperial [[walker]]s and [[tank]]s. Following Ground Defense Command's destruction, [[Starlight Squadron]] arrived and destroyed ''Ultima II'' by causing an eruption of the [[volcano]] directly below it.<ref name="SW 15" />
