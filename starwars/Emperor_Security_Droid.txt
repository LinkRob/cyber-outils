The '''Emperor Security Droid''' was a body configuration owned by the [[KX-series security droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[K-A0S]] that she could swap for her standard [[KX-Series Security Droid (K-A0S)|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This frame featured glowing shoulders and exposed tubing on the back,<ref name="Hunters" /> with a [[cloak]] resembling [[Zeyd-cloth|the one]] worn by [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> draped over top.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Emperor Security Droid was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter K-A0S, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 2.0.0 alongside Season 2 on [[July 30]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Imperial Grafted Shields]]. This costume could be unlocked by reaching Tier 70 on the Season 2 Arena Pass' premium track.<ref name="Hunters" />
