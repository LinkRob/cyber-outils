'''"Bight Size"''' was a song that played on the radio station [[BSO 401.72]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="GE" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> [[Palob Godalhi]], the station's host, declared that "Bight Size" was one of his favorite songs ever after it played.<ref name="GE" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Bight Size" was created for the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GE" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> It can be heard playing during the land's background radio loop.<ref name="GE" />
