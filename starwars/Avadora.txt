The '''''Avadora''''' was a small [[First Order]] [[yacht]] owned by [[Vroc Raynshi]], the [[Parent|father]] of [[Maize Raynshi]]. When Vroc was reassigned, Maize, along with the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Karr Nuq Sin]], and the [[droid]] [[RZ-7]], [[Thief|stole]] the [[Starship|ship]] for their journey to travel around [[the galaxy]] and uncover [[Jedi artifact]]s. Eventually, Sin and RZ-7 returned the ship when they traveled back to [[Merokia]].<ref name="Force Collector" />
