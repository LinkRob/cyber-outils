'''''Adventures in Wild Space: The Steal''''' is a book by [[Cavan Scott]]. It is the third of six entries in the ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' young-readers novel series, published by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd. The book was released on [[June 30]], [[2016]] and came to 160 pages.

The title was issued in the United States by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[April 11]], [[2017]] as '''''Adventures in Wild Space: The Heist'''''.

==Publisher's summary==
''Still on the hunt for their kidnapped parents, Milo and Lina Graf head to Lothal in search of an ally. But when something precious is stolen from them, they have to embark on their most dangerous mission yet. Will they succeed in THE STEAL?''<ref name="Steal">''Adventures in Wild Space: The Steal''</ref>
