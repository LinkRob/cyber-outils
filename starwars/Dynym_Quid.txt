'''Dynym Quid''' was an [[Dynym Quid's species|alien]]<ref name="Official companion">[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi The Official Movie Companion|''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' The Official Movie Companion]]</ref> that spent [[time]] in the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]<ref name="Episode VIII" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|VIII}}</ref> While the [[Resistance]] agents [[Finn]] and [[Rose Tico]] [[Mission to Canto Bight|searched]] the [[casino]] for the [[Master Codebreaker]], Quid stood at one of the establishment's bars with a [[human]] and caressed their hand. The building then briefly shook around them as several [[fathier]]s raced past on the nearby Canto Bight [[racetrack]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Quid had [[Color|gray]] skin and two blue [[eye]]s, with a series of brown-tipped chin tentacles, and two [[Limb|arms]] ending in five-fingered hands. The [[alien]] wore a black [[Clothing|robe]] at the casino.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dynym Quid first appeared in the [[2017]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and was first identified in the [[2018]] magazine ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi The Official Movie Companion]]''.<ref name="Official companion" />
