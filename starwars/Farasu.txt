'''Farasu''' was a [[Gas giant/Legends|gas giant]] located in the [[Felucia system/Legends|Felucia system]], which in turn was found in the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Thanium sector/Legends|Thanium sector]]. During the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], this area of space was contested over by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]].
