'''Company Cresh''' was an [[infantry]] [[company]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]]. After being rotated off active duty for training, Company Cresh and other [[stormtrooper]] units were placed under [[Red Battalion]], an [[Imperial cadet]] training formation for several [[Standard day|days]] of training exercises on [[Sirpar]]. There, the young [[cadet]]s would command the stormtrooper units to demonstrate the value of discipline and training. The Imperial cadet [[Anya Razar]] served as the company's [[commanding officer]], while [[Zare Leonis]] was placed as a [[lieutenant]] and the [[platoon leader]] of [[Cresh-3 Platoon]], a sub-unit of the company.<ref name="Secrets" />
