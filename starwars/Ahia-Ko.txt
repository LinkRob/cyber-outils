The '''Ahia-Ko''' were an ancient [[species]] and civilization which lived on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. Constructing great temples in the planet's [[jungle]] wilderness, they believed that water from the planet's special canyons was so pure, it could take one's sins away and leave them a better person. As a result, one of the oldest feats of architectural engineering was located near the [[water]]fall of the [[Witch's Finger]], named after an odd-looking cliff on the world.<ref name="Aftermath" />
