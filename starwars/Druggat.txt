'''Druggats''' were a unit of [[currency]]. During the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], an [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]] paid the [[Toydarian]] [[Garri]] twenty druggats for the use of his offices on the [[planet]] [[Utapau]] with no questions asked. The Toydarian later told a pair of [[Jedi]] investigators about the transaction when [[Anakin Skywalker|one]] threatened him with a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="ADOU" />

==Behind the scenes==
Druggats were first created by author [[Terry Brooks]] in the [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'' Episode I: ''The Phantom Menace'']] released in [[1999]]. As the currency appeared in the novel but not the film itself, it was considered part of the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' continuity. Druggats were made [[canon]] when they received mention in "[[A Death on Utapau]]," an episode from the canceled sixth season of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' which was released as an unfinished story reel on [[StarWars.com]] in [[2014]] as part of [[The Clone Wars Legacy|''The Clone Wars'' Legacy]].
