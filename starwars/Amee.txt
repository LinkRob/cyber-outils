'''Amee''' was a [[human]]<ref name="TPM" /> [[Gender|girl]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> who was born on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Geektionary French" /> in around [[38 BBY]].<ref name="Birth" /> She was a close friend of the [[Slavery|slave]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and spent [[time]] socialising with him and his friends [[Wald]] and [[Kitster Banai]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which include Amee's appearance, to [[32 BBY]].</ref> when Amee was six,<ref name="Birth" /> the trio visited Skywalker in [[Slave Quarters Row]] with the children [[Seek]] and [[Melee]]. Skywalker, who was working on his [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|podracer]] in a courtyard, informed them all that he would be taking part in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] the next [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="TPM" />

The other children were dubious about Skywalker's chances in the race, and Amee<ref name="TPM" /> doubtfully<ref name="Geektionary" /> pointed out that he had been working on the vehicle for years. Seek then suggested they all go play a ball game, and Amee left the courtyard with him, Wald and Melee, giggling as they went. Skywalker went on to win the podrace and leave Tatooine.<ref name="TPM" /> Amee was the only member of her friend group to wear [[braces]],<ref name="Geektionary" /> and also wore a [[Color|brown]] [[Clothing|dress]]. She had blonde [[hair]], and fair skin.<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amee was played by [[Katie Lucas]], daughter of director [[George Lucas]],<ref name="Family">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-a-family-affair|text=''Star Wars'', A Family Affair|nolive=1}}</ref> in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. Katie was credited under the pseudonym "Jenna Green".<ref name="TPM" /> In the script, Amee also says Melee's only line of dialogue<ref name="Insiders">[[Star Wars: Episode I Insider's Guide|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Insider's Guide'']]</ref> which is also incorrectly attributed to her in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''.<ref name="Geektionary" />
