The '''Combat Utility Vibroblade''' ('''C.U.V''') was a [[vibroblade]] created by [[Shistavanen]] survivalists from the [[planet]] [[Uvena Prime]]. It was created to contain every conceivable tool necessary to survive in battle or in the wilderness. Each C.U.V was a custom configuration, and could contain a [[fire]] starter, a [[hydrospanner]], or a [[water]] contaminant detector.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />
