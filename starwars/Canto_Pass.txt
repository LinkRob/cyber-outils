'''Canto Pass''' was a [[board game]] played across [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" />

The game involved multiple players with the goal of gaining the most points through playing several shaped pieces, each with their own abilities and uses in the game. Canto Pass pieces came in four varieties: Boons (which earned points), duds (which were worth negative points), tricks (which allowed special moves), and threats (which allowed other special moves). Game pieces could be found on [[planet]]s like [[Tatooine]], [[Akiva]], [[Kijimi]], and the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] by 3 ABY.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Canto Pass was first seen in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]],''<ref name="Outlaws" /> where pieces used in the game appeared as collectible vehicle cosmetics across the game.
