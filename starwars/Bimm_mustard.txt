'''Bimm mustard''' was a type of [[Mustard/Legends|mustard]]<ref name="Menu" /> that shared its name with two [[Species/Legends|species]], one [[Bimm (furred species)/Legends|furred]] and the other [[Bimm/Legends|Near-Human]].<ref name="P&M">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref> The [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]] [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]] enjoyed the taste of the mustard and used it in [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]], his [[Restaurant/Legends|restaurant]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], before<ref name="Menu" /> the business was destroyed at some point in the first two [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] rule.<ref name="DexEra">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]'' established that [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' later stated that the novel took place around the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]]. ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' states that the Empire was formed in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], so Dex's Diner was destroyed either during 19 BBY or 18 BBY.</ref> It would be served upon request alongside any [[slider]] on the menu.<ref name="Menu" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bimm mustard is mentioned in a [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive release, "[[Dining at Dex's]]" by [[Gregory Walker]], which represents the [[in-universe]] menu of Dex's Diner.<ref name="Menu" />
