The '''Cademimu sector''' was a [[sector]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Among others it contained the [[gas giant]] [[Ajara]] and its [[moon]], [[Ajan Kloss]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> as well as the [[planet]] [[Aldhani]] and the moon [[Frezno]].<ref name="SWECGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
