In [[231 BBY]], the '''Defense of the ''Halcyon'''''<ref name="Timelines" /> was carried out by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Nib Assek]] and her [[Padawan]] [[Burryaga Agaburry]] when the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Zeetar]] attempted to raid the ''[[Halcyon]]'' [[cruiser]] in order to gain information from the passengers onboard. The two [[Jedi]] were able to repeal the attack with the help of the late arrival of [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] and several [[Jedi Vector]]s.<ref name="HL1" />
