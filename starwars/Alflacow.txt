An '''alflacow''' was a type of [[cow]]. The [[Nihil]] [[Minister of Protection]] [[Abediah Viess]] once had an [[Abediah Viess' alflacow ranch|alflacow ranch]] at her [[Abediah Viess' manor|manor]] on the [[planet]] [[Sarumo]], but,<ref name="TOTF" /> between [[229 BBY]] and [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}} [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 4|''The High Republic: Shadows of Starlight'' 4]] establishes that [[Abediah Viess]] joined the [[Nihil]] four [[Standard month|months]] after Starlight Beacon's fall, meaning she must have joined the Nihil in 229 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. As Viess' [[Abediah Viess' manor|manor]] became a Nihil base after she joined the Nihil and before the events of ''Temptation of the Force'', it became a Nihil base between 229 BBY and 228 BBY.</ref> the manor, including the alflacow [[Farm|ranch]], had become an operating base for her [[Ministry of Protection]].<ref name="TOTF" /> In 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Master]] [[Porter Engle]] infiltrated the manor where the former alflacow ranch was located while [[hunting]] Viess.<ref name="TOTF" />
