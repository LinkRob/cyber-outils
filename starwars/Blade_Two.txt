'''Blade Two '''was the [[callsign]] attributed to the second member of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] [[Blade Squadron]]. During the [[Battle of Endor]], The [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Braylen Stramm]] served as Blade Two until the [[Blade Leader|leader]] of Blade Squadron, [[Wing Commander]] [[Adon Fox]] was killed during the battle.<ref name="BS" /> After the battle, Stramm was promoted to Wing commander and was given command of the squadron.<ref name="BS: Zero Hour">{{InsiderCite|160|Blade Squadron: Zero Hour}}</ref>
