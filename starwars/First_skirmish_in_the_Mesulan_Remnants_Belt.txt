A '''skirmish''' occurred in the [[Mesulan Remnants Belt]] between [[Fyzen Gor]] and several [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', piloted by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[L3-37]], became involved after arriving in the Mesulan Remnants to follow a [[distress signal]] sent by the [[droid]] [[D9]]. Gor and his droids destroyed most of the [[Starfighter|fighters]], but the ''Millennium Falcon'' escaped.<ref name="Last Shot" />
