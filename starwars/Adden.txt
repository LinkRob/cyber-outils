{{Quote|It's no good, Adden. Forget about the stash. I tried to get the keycard from Scrapper but I couldn't get its panels open. And now that they cut the lights, I can't even find the doshing droid anymore!|A Rokana Raider's data message to Adden|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
'''Adden''' was a member of the [[Rokana Raiders]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> around [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate|APF=1}}</ref> while the [[pirate]] group was based at the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Sayma Nebula]]. During their time serving with the raiders, they became attached to an [[Astromech droid|astromech]] named [[Scrapper (astromech)|Scrapper]]. This attachment saved the [[droid]] from being [[Blaster|blasted]] by [[Unidentified Rokana Raider (Scrapper)|another raider]] after it [[Thief|stole]] a [[keycard]] to an area at the base's forge where a number of items had been stashed. The raider eventually wrote two data messages about the theft, one of which was intended for Adden and told them to forget about the stash since the raider failed to get the keycard back and could no longer find the droid after the lights were cut in the forge area.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Around 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> after infiltrating the tomb while disguised as a Rokana Raider, the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] read two data messages on two separate [[datapad]]s, found and shot Scrapper, retrieved the keycard, and recovered the stash for herself.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}
