'''Desalination plants''', or '''desalination chambers''', were machines constructed to the bottom of some of [[Tipoca City]]'s domes with the purpose of [[desalination]]&mdash;the act of removing [[salt]] from [[ocean]] [[water]]&mdash;in order for it to become distilled water usable in the [[city]]. These plants were fitted with [[compressor unit]]s as well as tanks for condensation and evaporation. Water was pumped from the ocean via city support pylons into tanks of untreated water.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

With funding from the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] from before the time of [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] a permanent camp had been established at [[Alpinn]]'s archeological station which included prefab dwellings, [[wind turbine]]s and a desalinization plant.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
