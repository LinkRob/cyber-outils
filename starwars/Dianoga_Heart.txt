The '''dianoga heart''' was an item and presumably the heart of a [[Dianoga/Legends|Dianoga]]. Yoda asked [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] to retrieve this for him, and to do so, Luke must defeat a strange creature, much unlike that of a Dianoga. When retrieved, [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] would trade the heart for a [[Spirit Heart]].
