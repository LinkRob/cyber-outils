'''Barry Lowin''' is an actor<ref name="Instagram" /> who served as a double for [[Din Djarin]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', alongside [[Brendan Wayne]] and [[Lateef Crowder]],<ref name="Chapter 4" /> and played a [[Coastal defender stormtrooper|shoretrooper]] in "[[Chapter 15: The Believer]],"<ref name="Chapter 15">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 15: The Believer}}</ref> and [[Garfalaquox]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]''.<ref name="Stranger">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref>
