'''Cyclo-dioxis''' was a type of [[gas]] that could be used in [[gas canister]]s. It was invisible and nonlethal, but could hinder individuals' capability to move. The [[New Republic pilot]] used a canister of cyclo-dioxis when infiltrating an [[Imperial hideout]] on the [[moon]] [[Narthex]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
