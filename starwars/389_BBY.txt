'''389 BBY''', or '''389<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Around that year,<ref name="Date" /> the [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="QFTHC">''[[The High Republic: Quest for the Hidden City]]''</ref> [[Rooper Nitani]] became the [[Padawan]] of<ref name="Shield">{{StoryCite|story=Shield of the Jedi|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> the human<ref name="QFTHC" /> [[Jedi]] [[Silandra Sho]].<ref name="Shield" />

==Behind the scenes==
389 BBY was indirectly mentioned in the short story "[[Shield of the Jedi]]," written by [[George Mann]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Shield" />
