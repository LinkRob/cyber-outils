'''Don Post Studios''' was a premier American mask and costume manufacturer, creating masks for the [[Wikipedia:Hellraiser (franchise)|''Hellraiser'']] film franchise and a [[Wikipedia:William Shatner|William Shatner]] mask that was eventually altered for the character [[Wikipedia:Michael Myers (character)|Michael Myers]] in the horror film [[Wikipedia:Halloween (1978 film)|''Halloween'']]. Don Post Studios created a mask of [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]], which artists [[Greg Hildebrandt|Greg]] and [[Tim Hildebrandt]] used as an illustration model for artwork featured in the [[1996]] [[1996 Topps Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire|''Shadows of the Empire'' trading card]] set.
