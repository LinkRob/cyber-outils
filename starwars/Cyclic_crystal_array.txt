A '''cyclic crystal array''' was a [[lightsaber]] attachment that allowed for the saber to hold multiple [[kyber crystal]]s at the same time and switch between them at the press of a button. The attachment cost 1,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], and was considered unorthodox.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cyclic crystal arrays were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
