'''Ben Morris''' is the Creative Director at [[Industrial Light & Magic]] and a founding member of the company's London studio. He served as Visual Effects Supervisor on the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] and had a cameo as the pilot [[Ben Teene]].
