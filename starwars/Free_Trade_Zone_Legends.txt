The '''Free Trade Zones''' were areas of [[Realspace/Legends|space]] located in the [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim]] and [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]] where trading could be conducted without taxation by the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] .

==History==
===Creation===
The Free Trade Zones were created by the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Galactic Senate]] in [[124 BBY]] to jump-start legitimate commerce in the region in the hopes of ending the political isolation, stagnation and crime that had gripped the area since the [[Ruusan Reformations/Legends|Ruusan Reformations]]. The acts benefited various mega-corporations, including the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|InterGalactic Banking Clan]] and the [[Corporate Alliance/Legends|Corporate Alliance]], but none more so than the [[Trade Federation/Legends|Trade Federation]], a [[Neimoidian/Legends|Neimoidian]] trade cartel. The Trade Federation rapidly expanded into the Outer Rim, stifled competition, and enforced deals with its powerful [[Trade Defense Force]]. It soon dominated hundreds of star systems and commanded tremendous influence on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].

===Reform===
The Free Trade Zones were reformed in [[67 BBY/Legends|67 BBY]] after an intense debate on the issue. However, the [[Trade Federation/Legends|Trade Federation]] and several other major commerce corporations opposed this act as it would give startup companies in the Outer and Mid Rim, as well as small planetary business, greater control over trade in the outlying systems. Predominantly in the opposition camp was the financial lobbying company [[Damask Holdings/Legends|Damask Holdings]], while the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] campaigned strongly in favor of reform.

Secretly however, Damask Holdings Magister, [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Hego Damask II]], knew the trade zones would be reformed regardless so he instead negotiated a compromise to strengthen the Federation. In exchange for Damask's support, the pro-creation lobby granted the Federation full [[Senator/Legends|representation]] in the Senate, while Damask advised the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|InterGalactic Banking Clan]] to invest in startup shipping operations in the Outer Rim.

===Legacy===
By the waning [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the Galactic Republic, it was felt that the Free Trade Zones had made the Trade Federation far too powerful. In [[33 BBY/Legends|33 BBY]], [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum/Legends|Finis Valorum]] advocated restoring taxation to the Free Trade Zones in an effort to curb the Trade Federation. [[Senate Resolution BR-0371]] would have effectively abolished the zones. Regarding this as an attack on their interests, and urged on by [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]], the Trade Federation [[Invasion of Naboo/Legends|blockaded]] the planet [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] in protest.
