'''ET-2T''', pronounced '''Eetee Tootee,''' was a [[droid]], or colony of four interconnected droids,<ref name="FF" /> active sometime between [[8 ABY]] and [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|204}}</ref> They were present in [[Hanna City]],<ref name="FF" /> located on the [[planet]] [[Chandrila]],<ref name="Chandrila">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> specifically around the time of the [[Standard year|annual]] [[Hanna City Flight Academy]] [[end-of-year family race]].<ref name="FF" />

ET-2T consisted of four bipedal droid units connected via a series of [[Color|gray]], ribbed tubes. The three rear units possessed nearly identical bodies, only varying in the placement of red, gray, and white buttons, rectangular paneling, and colored squares and stripes spread across their otherwise white-plated frames.<ref name="FF" />

The forwardmost unit bore a slightly different appearance, possessing long [[Limb|arms]] consisting of a shoulder component and blocky hand-like appendage, connected via a thin rod. The lead droid also possessed a small, circular, yellow [[sensor]], as well as a cylindrical protrusion extending from the center of their torso. They were also able expel black smoke from a pipe on the top of their head.<ref name="FF" />

Each unit possessed a dome-shaped head, as well as stubby legs that resembled the tubes that connected them. These tubes were capable of compressing when the units grew close together, as well as retracting when disconnected. As such, the individual units could detach from the leader and move independently, though they would quickly grow confused when doing so.<ref name="FF" />

The leader droid was capable of issuing simple verbal commands such as "Go!" or "Stop!" to direct the others, though the three follower units sometimes experienced delayed reactions, clumsily bumping their bodies together.<ref name="FF" />

==Behind the scenes==
ET-2T was originally designed for "[[I Am Your Mother]]," a non-[[canon]] stop motion animated short film created by [[Aardman Animations]] as part of [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 2|''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2]],<ref name="FF" /> which was released on [[May 4]], [[2023]],<ref name="Release">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-2|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 2 Release Date, Studios Revealed}}</ref> though they would not make it into the short's final cut.<ref name="I Am Your Mother">{{Visions|I Am Your Mother}}</ref> Instead, concept art of ET-2T would first be showcased in the ''[[Star Wars: Visions Filmmaker Focus]]'' featurette that accompanied the episode on [[Disney+]]. This art was revealed to have been created by concept artist [[Andy Janes]] at some point during [[2022]], and though many fully realized clay models were shown during this featurette, one depicting ET-2T was not among them, indicating that the idea may not have made it that far into the short's production.<ref name="FF" />

Two different variations of the concept were displayed among the art, each possessing a similar appearance but slightly differing external traits. The first featured flatter bodies and blue markings across all four units, with the back droids specifically shown to have small shoulder and hand-like attachments that may have been extendable like the leader's, while said leader possessed a second spout on their torso that also emitted soot. The second variant, instead, had rounder bodies and red markings as opposed to blue, with the three follower units lacking any sort of arm or hand, and the front substituting the torso smoke emitter for a much smaller but still cylindrical stub.<ref name="FF" />

Due to the unique nature of their design, it is unclear if ET-2T would be classified as one singular droid or four individual droids that form a collective unit.<ref name="FF" />
