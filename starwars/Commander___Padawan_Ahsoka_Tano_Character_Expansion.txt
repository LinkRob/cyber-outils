The '''Commander & Padawan Ahsoka Tano Character Expansion''' is an expansion to the asymmetrical two player infantry-base wargame ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It includes 2 miniatures of [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. It was released on [[May 16]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''SWQ22 brings the long awaited Commander Ahsoka Tano and Padawan Ahsoka Tano to the battlefield of Star Wars: Legion. From the Clone Wars Animated Series, Ahsoka grew to become a fan-favorite and battle hardened leader of the 501st Legion.''<ref name="Commander & Padawan />

'' This Character Expansion is an exciting release which brings a variety of new rules, army options, and strategy to Star Wars: Legion. For Galactic Republic players, this will work well with their existing armies and miniature options.''<ref name="Commander & Padawan />

==Contents==
*2 [[Ahsoka Tano]] Miniatures 
*2 Bases
*1 Unit Card
*3 Command Cards
*1 Upgrade Card
*1 Punch Sheet
*1 Insert Sheet
