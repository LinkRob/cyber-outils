The '''Ascetic Bow''' was a [[composite bow]] utilized by the [[Zabrak]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Nox]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Wild Bow|standard bow]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ascetic Bow was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Nox's Wild Bow, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Ascetic Discipline]]. This wrap could be unlocked by reaching Nox Hunter Fame level 22.<ref name="Hunters" />
