'''Chickens''' were a type of domesticated [[bird]]s that could be found on [[Takodana]],<ref name="Battlefront 2" /> [[Endor]],<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> and [[Batuu]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s, a type of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[walker]], were derisively nicknamed "chicken walkers."<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> A [[Gender|female]] chicken was known as a '''hen'''.<ref name="SW AENK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> A [[cuckoo hen]] was a type of hen.<ref name="After">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> [[Tip-yip]]s were a subspecies of chicken native to Endor.<ref name="SW AENK" /><ref name="CYD">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Han & Chewie Adventure]]''</ref>
