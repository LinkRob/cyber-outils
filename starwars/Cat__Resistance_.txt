'''Cat''' was a [[Nefrian]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the [[Resistance engineer|flight engineer]] of the [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber]] ''[[Cobalt Treasure]]'' during the [[Atterra campaign]]. He later took over the duties as flight [[engineer]] of the ''[[Cobalt Wasp]]'' after the [[bomber]]'s previous [[Unidentified flight engineer (Cobalt Wasp)|flight engineer]] had returned sick after struggling to adjust to the climate on [[Refnu]]. On the second run of the Atterra campaign, Cobalt and Crimson Squadron were under attack by the First Order. Cat was killed when the ''Cobalt Wasp'' was destroyed by [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
