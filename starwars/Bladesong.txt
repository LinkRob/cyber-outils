'''Bladesong''' was a sculpture created by [[Darth Momin]].<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee">{{StoryCite|story=The Grand Imperial Jubilee|book=A New Legacy 1}}</ref>

==Description==
The sculpture used [[aurodium]], [[cortosis]], and [[alumabronze]] in its construction. It was estimated by [[Sana Starros]] to be nine [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" />

==History==
[[Darth Momin]] created Bladesong at some point before [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" />

In 2 BBY, the sculpture was put on display at the [[Third Decennial Imperial Jubilee]] on [[Alderaan]]. [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and [[Sana Starros]] were hired to infiltrate the Jubilee and [[Thief|steal]] the sculpture. Aphra brought a small [[holoprojector]] to replace it with, under the impression that the sculpture was around nine [[centimeter]]s tall—however, upon finding it on display, she realized her mistake.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" />

Meanwhile, Momin was also in attendance at the Jubilee, having possessed the body of a [[stormtrooper]] via his [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]]. While appraising the displayed artwork, Momin was outraged to find that his piece was credited to "Artist Unknown." Momin used [[the Force]] to lift the sculpture, breaking it into shards, which he flung at attendees.<ref name="Grand Imperial Jubilee" />
