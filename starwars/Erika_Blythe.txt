'''Erika Blythe''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] [[miner]] who was the mother of [[Bailen Blythe|Bailen]] and [[Ronn Blythe|Ronn]] and the wife of [[Ottoh Blythe]]. She and her husband had a [[Blythe homestead|homestead]] on the [[planet]] [[Elphrona]]. Shortly after the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], the family was kidnapped from their home by [[Nihil]] raiders, who planned to ransom them to their wealthy relatives that lived in the [[Core Worlds]]. Erika and her children were eventually rescued by [[Jedi]] from the [[Elphrona Outpost]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
