'''Blue Leader''', also known as '''Blue One''' was the [[callsign]] used by the leader of various units called Blue Squadron.

==History==
===Galactic Republic===
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] used this [[callsign]] in both the [[Battle of Ryloth]]<ref name="Storm over Ryloth" /> and [[Battle of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref>

===Rebel Alliance===
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[General]] [[Antoc Merrick]] flew as [[Color|Blue]] Leader in the [[battle of Scarif]] but was [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

Later, one [[Blue Leader (Vrogas Vas)|pilot]] flying under this callsign was killed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|battle]] of [[Vrogas Vas]].<ref name="VD 1" />

===New Republic===
During the [[Battle of Jakku]], the leader of [[77 Squadron]] (which flew as Blue Squadron in that battle) flew under the callsign.<ref name="Hyperspace Mountain" />

===Resistance===
During the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], the [[Resistance pilot]] [[Captain]] [[Temmin Wexley]] flew as Blue One.<ref name="Flight Log" />

During the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] of [[D'Qar]], [[Tallissan Lintra]] served as Blue Leader.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Batuu|Mission to Batuu]], a male individual using the callsign of Blue Leader communicated with the Resistance operatives on the planet to confirm that the [[Datacard]] they had sought had been retrieved and was en route to General Organa as planned. The individual then thanked [[Vi Moradi]] for her efforts.<ref name="Kylo vs Rey Galaxy's Edge">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@attractionsmagazine|name=Attractions Magazine|video=UGUsYWmVV2I|text=Star Wars Lightsaber Battle and Stunt Show in Galaxy's Edge during media event &#124; Attractions Magazine|date=2019-12-04}}</ref>
