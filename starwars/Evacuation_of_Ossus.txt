The '''evacuation''' of the [[planet]] [[Ossus]] took place at some point before the construction of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]]. The [[Jedi]] managed to save a few of the [[holobook]]s and [[Force artifact|artifacts]] from the [[Great Jedi Library]] of Ossus which made it to the [[Jedi Archives]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Nexus" />

In the legendary [[Qel-Droma Epics]], Ossus was devastated during the [[Cron Supernova]], when the [[Sith Lord]] [[Naga Sadow]] triggered [[star]]s in the [[Cron Cluster]] to become [[supernova]]e. The Jedi had little warning of the coming cataclysm and prioritized the artifacts from the [[Chamber of Antiquities]]. Despite the efforts, they only managed to save a fraction of the artifacts housed on the planet which made it to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the planet Coruscant via nearby [[Exis Station]], and the Great Library was left in ruins. Several crates of ancient scrolls, books, and electronic storage media that never made it to an [[Evac ship|evacuation ship]] were left behind in the Chamber of Antiquities.<ref name="Nexus" />
