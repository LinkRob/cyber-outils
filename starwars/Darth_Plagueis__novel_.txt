Fortunately, the Star Wars universe is so large that I was able to research the book almost in the same way I would have a more traditional historical novel. As it progressed, I was forced to hew more closely to established lore, but even then it proved possible to shed light on what was going on in the wings behind the scenes.|[[James Luceno]]|<ref name="SWI130">{{InsiderCite|130|Blaster}}</ref>}}
'''''Darth Plagueis''''' is a novel by [[James Luceno]] that tells the story of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Darth Plagueis]]. It was originally scheduled for release in [[2008]], but its release was canceled. However, [[Sue Rostoni]] later stated that the book had been reinstated, and the novel was to be released on [[January 10]], [[2012]].<ref name="SWBooks" /> Narration for the [[Darth Plagueis (audiobook)|audio version]] was performed by [[Daniel Davis]].

==Plot summary==
[[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Plagueis]] has just [[Death/Legends|murdered]] his [[Darth Tenebrous/Legends|master]] in cold blood to become the dominant [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Sith]], so he begins to look for an [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprentice]] of his own to advance his goals of becoming an [[Immortality/Legends|immortal]] [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]]. Soon enough, he encounters his [[Darth Venamis|Master's secret apprentice]], who he swiftly defeats and then uses to locate someone powerful in the [[The Force/Legends|Force]], but each person he finds is not good enough for his plans. One [[Standard day/Legends|day]], Plagueis finds himself on [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] and takes an interest in a young [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]], who he eventually finds out controls the powers of the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|Dark Side]] on such a level that he was able to hide his abilities until Plagueis drew them out. Soon after, Plagueis takes his new apprentice, now named Sidious, to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Mygeeto/Legends|Mygeeto]] to begin his training, and once he is ready teaches him how to become even more powerful than he currently is. Plagueis helps Sidious rise to the [[Military rank/Legends|rank]] of [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] of Naboo. Meanwhile, Sidious trains a secret apprentice behind Plagueis' back and begins to create the perfect [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] as a [[Youngling/Legends|young]] [[Gender/Legends|boy]] from [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] is brought before the [[Jedi Council/Legends|Jedi Council]] to be trained in their ways. However, Plagueis has his own plans for the boy.

===Prologue===
The novel begins on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] in the [[Kaldani Spires Residential Apartments|Kaldani Spires Residential Apartment building]] owned by Hego Damask, a [[Muun/Legends|Muun]] who is secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Darth Plagueis]]. The Muun has just been [[Death/Legends|killed]] by his [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprentice]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Darth Sidious]]. Sidious wonders for a moment if he made a horrible mistake and his mentor has tricked him. His fear passes as the now [[Sith Master/Legends|Sith Master]] experiences a moment where he feels a dramatic shift in the [[The Force/Legends|Force]], which promises him that the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|Dark Side]] will control him as much as he controls it. Sidious notices that Plagueis looks almost exactly as he did decades ago when he first encountered the Muun on his homeworld of [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]].

===Main story===
====Part One: Enlistment, 67&ndash;65 years before the Battle of Yavin====
[[67 BBY/Legends|Thirty-five years earlier]], [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Darth Plagueis]] and his [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] [[Sith Master/Legends|Sith Master]], [[Darth Tenebrous/Legends|Darth Tenebrous]], are in a [[Cave/Legends|cave]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bal'demnic/Legends|Bal'demnic]] to examine its natural [[Cortosis/Legends|cortosis]] deposits, which are being [[Mining/Legends|mined]] without the knowledge of either the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] or the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]]. Tenebrous plans to use the cortosis' natural resistance to [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] blades in his future attempts to overthrow the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] and replace the Republic with a [[Sith Empire/Legends|Sith-controlled Empire]], in accordance with [[Darth Bane/Legends|Darth Bane's]] [[Grand Plan/Legends|Grand Plan]]. During the investigation, the mining [[EM-Two probe droid]], against Tenebrous' orders, begins digging for an [[Explosive charge/Legends|explosive]] [[lethane]] pocket next to a cortosis vein, prompting the two Sith to rush back to their [[Darth Tenebrous's personal starship|ship]] and escape the resulting explosion. Ultimately, the explosion occurs before they make it back to the ship. Tenebrous is able to use the [[The Force/Legends|Force]] to contain the blast so that it does not [[Death/Legends|kill]] the two [[Sith/Legends|Sith]], while Plagueis makes sure that their ship is not destroyed by the impact of the blast.

In the process, Tenebrous is severely weakened and Plagueis has the opportunity to end his Master's life and take over the role of Sith Master. He betrays him by using the Force to hurl falling debris on top of Tenebrous; then, attempting to deceive him into believing it looks as it was a natural event, he rushes to his Master's side. The Bith informs his [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprentice]] that the mining [[Droid/Legends|droids]] were supplied to him by [[Subtext Mining]]. Plagueis, however, cannot resist revealing to Tenebrous what his true plans are and what they mean for the future of the Sith. The [[Muun/Legends|Muun]] tells his Bith Master that he will put an end to the old order of the [[Rule of Two/Legends|Rule of Two]] before breaking Tenebrous' neck and observing his dying body through the Force in an attempt to [[Midi-chlorian manipulation|manipulate]] [[Midi-chlorian/Legends|midi-chlorians]]. However, Tenebrous dies before Plagueis is even able to at least prolong his Master's life for the sake of his experimentation. The ship designed by Tenebrous has been destroyed by the debris, and the wounded Muun sets off for the nearest [[City/Legends|city]] on the planet.

Hiding aboard the LS-447-3 ship ''[[Woebegone]]'', the Muun make it off the planet. The ship's crew, all [[Non-huMan|non-humans]], discover Plagueis in a cooling container and confront him. They do not trust him and want to return to Bal'demnic to give him to the local authorities. After [[Ellin Lah]], the [[Togruta/Legends|Togruta]] [[Captain/Legends|captain]] of the ''Woebegone'', tells him this, he goes on a [[Massacre on the Woebegone|killing spree]], killing every member of the crew except the four-armed [[11-4D's model|medical]] droid [[11-4D/Legends|11-4D]], who tends to the Muun's wounded back. The two travel to the [[Deep Space Demolition and Removal]] [[Space station/Legends|space station]], where the ''Woebegone'' is slagged and 11-4D's memory core is [[Memory wipe/Legends|erased]] without erasing the droid's medical protocols. The Muun and his new droid then return to his homeworld of [[Muunilinst/Legends|Muunilinst]], where Plagueis appears in his public persona as Hego Damask, CEO of [[Damask Holdings/Legends|Damask Holdings]].

The two are greeted by [[Larsh Hill]], Chief Operating Officer of [[Damask Holdings/Legends|Damask Holdings]]. After a brief confrontation between Damask and the Chairman of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|InterGalactic Banking Clan]], [[Tonith]], the two travel to [[Aborah]], where the Muun instructs the droid to study Sith history and shows him his [[Laboratory/Legends|laboratory]]. On [[Sojourn (moon)|Sojourn]], also known as the Hunter's Moon in the [[Carrion sector/Legends|Carrion sector]], Damask Holdings holds an [[Gathering (Sojourn)|annual secret meeting]] with several leaders of various corporations and cartels. One of these guests is [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder/Legends|Gardulla the Hutt]], who seeks Damask's [[Currency/Legends|financial]] support to take control of [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] and escape the influence of her enemies, particularly [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] and the [[Desilijic/Legends|Desilijic clan]]. Gardulla offers to develop a new [[Mos Espa Grand Arena/Legends|Podrace track]] on Tatooine and take Damask on as a silent partner. In return, Damask demands that Gardulla allow [[Cabra|Boss Cabra]], a [[Black Sun/Legends|Black Sun]] [[Vigo/Legends|Vigo]] and partner of the Muun, to operate freely on [[Nar Shaddaa/Legends|Nar Shaddaa]]. After some hesitation, Gardulla agrees to the terms, promising Damask that he will not be disappointed. [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Pax Teem]], representing the [[Gran Protectorate/Legends|Gran Protectorate]], expresses anger and concern at the rumors of Gardulla's plans. Teem is upset about the potential competition for [[Malastare/Legends|Malastare's]] own [[Podracing/Legends|Podracing]] events, which could negatively affect his interests. Damask and Hill assure Teem that their dealings with the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] will remain confidential. They suggest offsetting any losses from Podracing by raising the cost of Malastare's fuel exports, which Teem reluctantly accepts as a starting point for discussion. The [[Gran/Legends|Gran]] also accuses Damask of favoring the [[Trade Federation/Legends|Trade Federation]], complaining that the Federation's full representation in the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Senate]] benefits Damask Holdings more than Malastare, and leaves in anger. After the Gran leaves, Plagueis confronts representatives of the Subtext Mining Corporation, who have been invited by Hill on Damask's instructions. Damask threatens their lives, and in response they tell the Muuns of a [[Plasma/Legends|plasma]] vein on the [[Mid Rim/Legends|Mid Rim]] planet [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]]. Plagueis spares their lives and has them relocated to the most remote world on the [[Tingel Arm/Legends|Tingel Arm]] so they would be in reserve in case he needs them again&mdash;he has them killed later on when it becomes clear that he cannot have anymore dealings with Pax Teem, who is a supporter of Subtext.

After the meeting ends and the guests leave, a member of the [[Sunguard]]s informs Plagueis that the intrusion alarm has been activated. The Muun heads into the forest surrounding the [[Castle/Legends|castle]], knowing that only Tenebrous had the access codes, indicating that something is not right. He is confronted by [[Darth Venamis]], a Bith Sith whom Tenebrous had secretly trained as a contingency plan in case Plagueis failed to live up to Tenebrous's expectations. Plagueis defeats Venamis in [[Lightsaber combat/Legends|battle]], forcing him to swear allegiance before [[Poison/Legends|poisoning]] the lesser Sith. Plagueis then uses Venamis in his experiments of manipulating midi-chlorians in an attempt to achieve immortality and conquer death.

The Sunguard discovers a list of [[Force-sensitive/Legends|Force-users]] on the ship's [[Computer/Legends|computer]]. Like Plagueis, Tenebrous has ordered Venamis to look out for potential Force-sensitive beings to use in the Sith's schemes. Plagueis sets out to locate these individuals to determine if they pose a threat to his plans. Accompanied by 11-4D, he travels to [[Lianna City]]. At a [[Casino/Legends|casino]], they identify a [[Shi'ido]], a [[Changeling/Legends|shapeshifter]], who is using his [[Force power/Legends|Force abilities]] to cheat. They follow the Shi'ido and discover that he is transferring money to [[Kerred Santhe the Second]]. Perplexed, Plagueis confronts the shapeshifter and learns that Santhe is heavily in debt and threatened by Vigos of the Black Sun. According to the shapeshifter, Santhe has lost all his money since the murder of his [[Kerred Santhe|father]], an act committed by Plagueis approximately twenty-five [[Standard year/Legends|years]] into his apprenticeship. Plagueis gives the shapeshifter one last chance to make money before reporting him to the [[Galactic Gaming Commission]], allowing him to leave.

On [[Saleucami/Legends|Saleucami]], Plagueis observes a gathering led by the next person on Venamis' list, an [[Iktotchi/Legends|Iktotchi]] [[Unidentified Iktotchi prophet|prophet]]. He witnesses her predictions of [[Clone Wars/Legends|impending galactic turmoil]] and the rise of a [[Palpatine/Legends|ruthless dictator]]. Realizing the potential danger her message poses to his plans, Plagueis decides to intervene. Approaching her, he subtly channels [[Force lightning/Legends|Force lightning]] into her body as he touches her arm. The Prophet dies in front of her followers, and Plagueis leaves in the chaos that ensues.

Plagueis and 11-4D travel to [[Bedlam/Legends|Bedlam]] to locate [[Naat Lare]], a [[Nautolan/Legends|Nautolan]] and last on Venamis' list. Upon arrival, they discover that Lare has disappeared from the [[Bedlam Institution for the Criminally Demented]] where he was confined, possibly with the help of Venamis, who has been posing as a [[Doctor/Legends|physician]]. Learning of Lare's troubled past and potential danger, Plagueis searches for clues to his whereabouts. They discover that Lare may have fled to [[Felucia/Legends|Felucia]], [[Caluula/Legends|Caluula]], or [[Abraxin]]. After hearing of recent murders on Abraxin that resemble Lare's work, they head there. Plagueis encounters [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Ni-Cada]] and his [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Lo Bukk]] investigating the murders and senses Lare's presence. He urges Lare to prove himself by attacking the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] before slipping away as the conflict unfolds. Plagueis briefly considers taking Naat Lare as an apprentice, as Venamis had promised the Nautolan. This thought is abandoned when Naat Lare is killed by the Jedi and his Padawan.

In exchange for the plasma located beneath the surface of Naboo, Damask Holdings and the Trade Federation begin a bid to support [[Bon Tapalo/Legends|Bon Tapalo]] as [[King/Legends|king]] of the planet. They are invited by [[Ars Veruna/Legends|Ars Veruna]], one of Tapalo's advisors, to Naboo. During this visit, Damask Holdings presents an economic development plan to Bon Tapalo's faction, proposing to exploit the planet's plasma resources in exchange for support in Tapalo's bid for the throne. Tapalo's faction, initially wary of foreign involvement, ultimately agrees to the partnership. Through Veruna, Plagueis learns of a young [[Gender/Legends|man's]] pivotal role in leaking information that benefited Bon Tapalo's faction. This man's name is [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]], whose motivations and actions go against those of his [[Parent/Legends|father]], [[Cosinga Palpatine|Cosinga]], who wants Naboo to remain an isolationist world. Seeing potential in Palpatine in terms of ambitions and even actual power in the Force, Plagueis begins using him as a source of information regarding the campaign of Tapalo and his opponents. Palpatine, meanwhile, sees in Hego Damask the father figure he never had, even as he knows that he is being manipulated. Naturally, this attracts Cosinga's attention, who tries to separate his son from Plagueis. After a meeting between the Muun and the young Naboo royal on [[Chandrila/Legends|Chandrila]], Palpatine is forced to meet with his father on the [[Palpatine family starship|family starship]]. Cosinga wants to send his son to [[Chommell Minor/Legends|Chommell Minor]] to live with the [[Greejatus family]]. In a burst of rage, Palpatine kills his entire [[Palpatine family|family]] and their honor guard by using his full, unbridled power in the dark side of the Force. He later informs Plagueis of the deed, who then congratulates Palpatine on "emancipating" himself. Soon, Plagueis takes Palpatine under his wing as his Sith apprentice after revealing to the young man the nature of himself and of the Sith, promptly naming him Darth Sidious.

====Part Two: Apprenticed to power, 54&ndash;52 years before the Battle of Yavin====
In the eleven [[Standard year/Legends|years]] that follow of [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Plagueis]] teaching [[Palpatine/Legends|Sidious]] the nature of the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]] and the ways of the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]], Palpatine, as the last of [[House Palpatine/Legends|House Palpatine]], climbs his way up the [[Politics/Legends|political]] ladder of [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] to become its [[Ambassador/Legends|ambassador]] throughout the [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]]. And with the help of new friends like [[Kinman Doriana/Legends|Kinman Doriana]] and [[Sate Pestage/Legends|Sate Pestage]], Palpatine arranges for the successful [[Death/Legends|assassination]] of his former mentor and Naboo's representative [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] to the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]], [[Vidar Kim]]. As a result, Palpatine becomes the new Senator of Naboo, taking a seat in the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Republic]] Senate as Plagueis continues his plans of [[Midi-chlorian manipulation|manipulating midi-chlorians]] to [[Immortality/Legends|prolong his own life indefinitely]]. One such plan involves the [[Cloning/Legends|cloning]] industry on the backwater world of [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]], inadvertently setting the stage for the eventual [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]. Meanwhile, amidst his own political machinations, Palpatine, in his urge to know more about the dark side than what Plagueis teaches him, manages to make a visit to the world of [[Dathomir/Legends|Dathomir]], where he encounters a [[Dathomirian/Legends|Dathomirian]] [[Kycina|woman]] who gives him a [[Zabrak/Legends|Zabrak]] [[Youngling/Legends|infant]] simply known as [[Darth Maul/Legends|Maul]]. Palpatine later has Maul sent to [[Mustafar/Legends|Mustafar]], where he could be nurtured at his young age in the dark side of the Force.

As Sidious and Plagueis promptly proceed to eliminate their political opponents, Plagueis is attacked during an [[Order of the Canted Circle]] ceremony for his assistant [[Larsh Hill]] by [[Maladian]] [[Assassin/Legends|assassins]]. Hill and the other members of [[Damask Holdings/Legends|Damask Holdings]] are killed, but Plagueis, albeit severely wounded, is forced to unleash his [[Force power/Legends|powers]] and is able to fight off and kill several of the assassins long enough for Palpatine and Sate Pestage (who is inducted into the conspiracy as an ally) arrive to assist the superior [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]], barely delivering him from the assassin's [[Dagger/Legends|daggers]]. The Sith and the members of the Order cover up the battle. Palpatine had previously discerned that a powerful political opponent of his, [[Pax Teem]], had outwitted Plagueis and had sent the assassins to kill him and his partners during the Canted Circle ceremony. In retaliation, Palpatine personally attacks Teem during a celebratory feast, killing him and all of his guests, leaving no survivors or evidence. In the aftermath of the attack at the botched ceremony, as Plagueis is forced to wear a [[Transpirator/Legends|transpirator]] to survive, he becomes more alert than ever about his surroundings, even going so far as forever renouncing [[Sleep/Legends|sleep]], and is even more obsessed with finding a way to live forever. As Damask, he trains Hill's son, [[San Hill/Legends|San]], to become the new head of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|InterGalactic Banking Clan]]. Meanwhile, Plagueis manages to kill and then resurrect [[Darth Venamis]] in one of his experiments; he successfully does this numerous times until the [[Bith/Legends|Bith’s]] organs have given out and Plagueis grants him everlasting death.

====Part Three: Mastery, 34&ndash;32 years before the Battle of Yavin====
[[34 BBY/Legends|More than two decades pass]] as [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] gains more power in the [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Senate]] as the reign of [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum/Legends|Finis Valorum]] comes to an end. The rest of the novel tells the events depicted in other, preexisting ''[[Star Wars]]'' stories, such as ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Council: Acts of War|Jedi Council: Acts of War]], [[Cloak of Deception]], [[Star Wars: Darth Maul (2000)|''Star Wars: Darth Maul'']], and ''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]], until the novel takes place during the entirety of ''[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace]]''. Throughout these events, it is learned that [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Plagueis]] had [[Death/Legends|killed]] former [[King/Legends|King]] [[Ars Veruna/Legends|Ars Veruna]] of [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] (thus, allowing Palpatine to have a teenaged [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]] elected as the [[Planet/Legends|planet's]] [[Queen/Legends|Queen]]) and that Plagueis also suggested to [[Sifo-Dyas/Legends|Sifo-Dyas]] to sanction a [[Human/Legends|human]] [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] [[Army/Legends|army]] on [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]]. Palpatine, who had also named [[Darth Maul/Legends|Maul]] [[Darth/Legends|Darth]] behind Plagueis's back, also sees a potential ally in the former [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]]. As the novel comes to a close during the events of ''The Phantom Menace'', Sidious and Plagueis learn that a [[Youngling/Legends|young]] [[Gender/Legends|boy]] named [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]], a former [[Slavery/Legends|slave]] found by [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], has been [[Reproduction/Legends|born]] of the [[The Force/Legends|Force]]. They discern that this is most likely due to Plagueis's experiment of creating life through the Force, which he had commenced about a [[Standard decade/Legends|decade]] earlier.

The novel climaxes where it began&mdash; as the [[Battle of Naboo/Legends|Battle of Naboo]] occurs, Palpatine coerces Plagueis into sleep with the promise of making him [[Co-Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]], just as Plagueis demanded decades earlier, now that Palpatine appears to be winning in the election to replace Finis Valorum as Chancellor. With that, Sidious attacks Plagueis and, as he kills him, he explains that he is the one who has really been manipulating Plagueis the whole time, with deals such as the clone army for example. In the end, Sidious ends Plagueis' life with the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side of the Force]], although his feeling of triumph is ruined as he feels uneasy for some reason. He later attributes this to the supposed death of Darth Maul, though, as he initially suspected, it may very well have been Plagueis himself reaching out to him from beyond the Force.

As Palpatine is officially made Supreme Chancellor of the Republic, he begins to commence his solo plans of taking over the [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]] with the upcoming [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], such as seeking Dooku as a placeholder [[Sith apprentice/Legends|Sith apprentice]] and ally in the war. But the biggest part in Darth Sidious's plan is young Anakin Skywalker, now a [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] sees will be [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|easily manipulated]] into becoming his true apprentice, and a harbinger of Palpatine's own power.

Plagueis, who denigrated him in private for hiring an inept assassin to carry out the murder of Senator Kim—and yet who allowed himself to be tricked by the Gran and nearly killed by mercenaries. Plagueis, who turned away from the Grand Plan to focus entirely on himself, in an egotistical quest for immortality.|Sidious on Plagueis.|<ref name="Novel">[[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']]</ref>}}
[[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Plagueis]] gave the [[Sun Guard|Sun Guards]] the night off, leaving him, [[Palpatine/Legends|Sidious]], and [[11-4D/Legends|11-4D]] alone. As the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lords]] removed their [[Cloak/Legends|cloaks]], 11-4D served them [[Sullustan/Legends|Sullustan]] [[Wine/Legends|wine]] in expensive [[Glass/Legends|glassware]]. Plagueis remarked on the age of the wine and proposed a toast in honor of the culmination of [[Standard decade/Legends|decades]] of planning and execution, and to the new meaning they would "give" the [[Rule of Two/Legends|Rule of Two]]. Both beings drained their glasses, which were immediately refilled by 11-4D. Sidious complemented Plagueis on his part of the plan and told him he would endeavor to live up to Plagueis' expectations and fulfill his responsibility. Sidious then began rehearsing the speech he would give tomorrow─at Damask's request, he began by saying that the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] had been intact for a [[Millennium/Legends|millennia]] thanks to largely invisible beings whose accomplishments needed to be brought to the light of [[Standard day/Legends|day]]. Plagueis stated that he was pleased and Sidious continued.

He stated that Hego Damask was one of those beings, the being who had overseen the development of the [[Republic Reserve Administration]] and who provided [[Currency/Legends|financial]] support for the [[Resettlement Acts]] that enabled beings to colonize distant worlds. He continued by saying that Hego Damask was who transformed the [[Trade Federation/Legends|Trade Federation]]; however, Damask interrupted him and told him that it was not the right time to mention a controversial organization. Plagueis told him to take a different approach, and so he did.

As the night wore on, Sidious continued to amend and improvise, touching on Damask's [[Youngling/Legends|childhood]] on [[Mygeeto/Legends|Mygeeto]] and on the elder Damask's contributions to the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|InterGalactic Banking Clan]] during his term as co-chair. As he continued, the [[Droid/Legends|droid]] brought a third, and then a fourth bottle of wine. Plagueis, now pleasantly [[Alcohol/Legends|intoxicated]], only wanted to [[Sleep/Legends|sleep]] and dream of his march into the [[Grand Convocation Chamber|Senate Rotunda]], the looks of surprise and astonishment on the faces of the gathered [[Senator/Legends|senators]]. Plagueis tried to end the drinking, however, Sidious did not let him, instead turning Plagueis' own words against him. Their glasses were filled again, and Plagueis' [[Eye/Legends|eyelids]] began to flicker and close&hellip; Plagueis fell asleep, and Sidious was about to leave &mdash; but the [[Dark side of the Force/Legends|dark side]] would not let him &mdash; the dark side told Sidious that his victory was assured and that the Sun Guards were absent&hellip; Plagueis unsuspecting and asleep. Sidious turned back and betrayed his [[Sith Master/Legends|master]] by throwing a web of [[Force lightning/Legends|Force-lightning]] into Plagueis' [[Transpirator/Legends|breathing device]].

Plagueis woke up, alarmed, his breath going away. The [[The Force/Legends|Force]] began to gather in Plagueis like a storm. Plagueis had survived [[Death/Legends|assassinations]], and killed countless opponents. He did not fight back, but merely gazed at his [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprentice]], until Sidious noticed that he was being challenged! Plagueis was confident that he had achieved [[Immortality/Legends|immortality]] and sat absolutely still, denying that he was slowly suffocating. Sidious stopped, and stood absolutely still, Plagueis gazing back at him. The question lingered for only a moment, then Sidious unleashed another tangle of lightning, drawing more deeply on the dark side than he ever had. Sidious then repeated the "second part of the speech" calling Plagueis a fool. He then described everything Plagueis had done since his arrival at [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] - draining the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] dry of [[Plasma/Legends|plasma]], setting the Trade Federation as overseers; the one who set his sights on a seemingly confused young [[Gender/Legends|man]] and, with meticulous skill, manipulated the man into committing patricide, matricide, and fratricide. Plagueis had taken him as apprentice, sharing some of his knowledge but withholding his most powerful secrets, denying the apprentice his wishes, as [[Darth Tenebrous/Legends|Tenebrous]] had once done. Plagueis had made his apprentice hire an inept [[Assassin/Legends|assassin]] to murder Senator [[Vidar Kim]] &mdash; and yet allowed himself to be tricked by a [[Gran/Legends|Gran]] and nearly killed. Plagueis had turned away from the [[Grand Plan/Legends|Grand Plan]] to focus entirely on himself, in a quest for immortality. At this point, Plagueis tried to stand up, but fell onto the couch. The lightning resumed, and shortly after, Plagueis attempted to [[Midi-chlorian manipulation|gather his midi-chlorians]]. Sidious told him that his [[Midi-chlorian/Legends|midi-chlorians]] could not do anything if he was being denied breathing, that his studies would be useless to save him, and that Sidious could save him; however, he stated that he would do no such thing. Just as Plagueis fell onto the [[Couch/Legends|couch]], dead, the Force departing from him, Sidious sensed a great [[Disturbance in the Force/Legends|disturbance in the Force]]. He asked himself if Plagueis had achieved immortality, or that the disturbance was Plagueis' death. However, he realized it was neither of those; Sidious later realized that [[Darth Maul/Legends|Maul had died]]. Although he really did not.

Sidious felt a tremor taking hold of the planet, and he also sensed himself staying in the middle of it, a chorus of voices praising his assassination and the birth of a new [[Order of the Sith Lords|order]]. He quickly dismissed the voices and focused on himself. Sidious breathed heavily, and looked around the room, witnessing [[Painting/Legends|paintings]], [[Tapestry/Legends|tapestries]], [[Carpet/Legends|carpets]], and a lot of broken furniture, as well as Plagueis' corpse, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith/Legends|dark lord]] lying on the floor, dead. However, Sidious thought that perhaps his master had achieved immortality, which he later dismissed. He also thought about Damask's egotism, asking himself if Plagueis had centered so much on his goal that he had forgotten about his own safety.

Sidious rolled Plagueis' corpse over to showcase the [[Muun/Legends|Muun's]] face; at that angle, Plagueis looked like he did when he met Palpatine for the first time. His fears were dismissed as Sidious witnessed the Muun's features relaxing, a sign that he was truly dead. Sidious decided that the will of the dark side had been done, and therefore, turned to the [[Window/Legends|window]] of [[Darth Plagueis' apartment/Legends|Plagueis' suite]]. Sidious thought that the two of them had changed the [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]] for trillions of beings, and that with the birth of Darth Sidious, it had already changed. Sidious observed the planets and [[Star/Legends|stars]], aligned in different configurations long before both of them had been born; however, they had changed. Palpatine sensed something rising from him, confident that the dark side was his, he decided that something inside him &mdash; a monster &mdash; would soon be liberated. He later thought about Plagueis' master's death; Plagueis had never talked about it. Sidious asked himself if the same thing that had happened to Plagueis as it had happened to Tenebrous. He then once again looked out the window and left.

==Development==
{{Quote|In dealing with Plagueis' quest for immortality, I originally structured part of the book as a kind of race between him and Qui-Gon Jinn, they inadvertently sabotaged one another without either of them realizing they were pursuing the same goal. In the end, Qui-Gon succeeded by making contact with the Whills- the mysterious species of immortals George Lucas mentioned in an epigraph for the first Star Wars novel. As a result, the first version of the Plagueis novel was all over the place, way too long, and trying too hard to resolve every loose end.|James Luceno|<ref name="SWI130" />}}
On [[June 8]], [[2006]], [[StarWars.com]] announced that [[James Luceno]] was scheduled to write a novel about Darth Plagueis that would be published by [[Del Rey]] and released in [[2008]].<ref name="announcement">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/novel/news20060608.html|text=Luceno to Pen Plagueis Page-turner}}</ref> However, in a talk show interview in [[February 5]], [[2007]], Luceno indicated that the novel had been put on hold.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://recordings.talkshoe.com/TS-8518.mp3|text=James Luceno Interview, Part Two|author=Meadows, Chris|date=2007-02-05|format=mp3|work=[http://terrania.us/liberty Space Station Liberty]|nobackup=1}}</ref>

===Cancellation===
{{Quote|I'm sure you've noticed that the Plagueis novel is not listed. It's true. The novel has been cancelled. We decided that this was not the right time to delve into Palpatine's backstory and Plagueis's beginnings. You'll still see Jim Luceno around, though, as he's an author we are not willing to lose.|Sue Rostoni|<ref name="Cancel">{{Blog|eeusuestornii|9|Ballantine/Del Rey Fiction Schedule}}</ref>}}
On [[March 27]], [[2007]], [[Sue Rostoni]] revealed in her blog that the novel had been canceled, in an entry revealing an updated publishing schedule.<ref name="Cancel" />

The announcements for the novel stated that [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Plagueis]] was a [[Muun/Legends|Muun]].<ref name="announcement" /> [[Leland Chee]] confirmed that, since Plagueis's species did not originate in the novel, it was still considered his canonical species by [[Lucas Licensing]].<ref>{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=1290#16446203|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Tasty Taste]]|date=2007-03-30|time=11:52 AM}}</ref>

The novel was replaced with ''[[Darth Bane: Rule of Two]]'';<ref>{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=222689&start=04980#16712103|thread=Books, Comics, & Television VIPs|user=[[Sue Rostoni|Eeusu Estornii]]|date=2007-06-18|time=9:57 AM}}</ref> however, Luceno had said he still hoped he could convince the powers that be at LucasFilm to let him write the novel, and cited sales of [[Millennium Falcon (novel)|''Millennium Falcon'']] could help influence that.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.swactionnews.com/archives/ep166.htm|text=James Luceno interview on Star Wars Action News Episode 166|work=SW Action News|archiveurl=https://archive.is/OQeAD}}</ref>

===Rejuvenation===
{{Quote|Plagueis was born into an historical setting that allowed him to bring many important beings under his influence, and to bring the re-emergent dark side fully to the attention of the Jedi Order.|James Luceno|<ref name="SWI130" />}}
On [[July 27]], [[2010]], Sue Rostoni stated that the book was back on schedule, with a release date of [[February 28]], [[2012]]. This announcement came following a sales database leak, which the fan site [[New Jedi Order Encyclopedia]] published.<ref name="revival">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?messageID=17921027|thread=Darth Plagueis Novel by James Luceno *Speculation*|user=[[Sue Rostoni|Eeusu Estornii]]|date=2010-07-27|time=12:01 PM|nobackup=1}}</ref> Believing it to be a replacement for the canceled ''[[Imperial Commando 2]]'', fans asked for clarification. Rostoni confirmed that the book was going to be published in 2012, but that it is not intended to replace ''Imperial Commando 2''. The release date was later changed to [[December 27]], [[2011]], and then again to [[January 10]], [[2012]]. The story underwent careful refinement with direct input from [[Howard Roffman]], president of [[Lucas Licensing]].

On [[August 22]], 2011, it was announced on the Star Wars Books Facebook page that [[Matthew Stover]] would be writing a short story, entitled ''[[The Tenebrous Way]]'', that ties into the book. It was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 130|''Star Wars Insider'' 130]] on [[December 13]], 2011.

===Original outline and inspiration===
''Darth Plagueis'' was first set to be a first-person narrative. [[Palpatine/Legends|Sidious]] would have found [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Plagueis's]] holocron shortly after he [[Death/Legends|murdered]] his [[Sith Master/Legends|master]] and it would have retold Plagueis's recorded life story. [[wikipedia:Anne Rice|Anne Rice's]] ''[[Wikipedia:The Vampire Chronicles|The Vampire Chronicles]]'' served as inspiration for the storyline. This direction evolved as the story grew to tell of Palpatine's beginnings as much as the origin of Plagueis.<ref name="TERC" />

==Continuity==
{{Quote|Prince Xixor and Sise Fromm will inherit the dregs.|Darth Plagueis|<ref name="Novel" />}}
''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''  says that [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']] was the first source to identify [[Darth Plagueis/Legends|Darth Plagueis]] as Sidious’s [[Sith Master/Legends|Master]],<ref name="TERC" /> but in fact ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'',<ref name="Labyrinth">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref> which was released before<ref name="Amazon">{{RandomHouseOld|url=author/results.pperl?authorid=18222|text=James Luceno}}</ref> ''Revenge of the Sith'',<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> identified Sidious's master as Darth Plagueis<ref name="Labyrinth" /> while ''Revenge of the Sith'' doesn't reveal the identity of Plagueis's apprentice.<ref name="ROTS">[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']]</ref>

[[James Luceno|Luceno]] gathered story elements from across the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] as part of this narrative. ''Darth Plagueis'' built upon fragments of Palpatine’s history revealed in [[The Clone Wars: Wild Space|''Wild Space'']] ([[2008]]) – that he was a speed demon as a young [[Gender/Legends|man]] – and the short story ''[[The Monster]]'' ([[2000]]), which featured Palpatine’s co-conspirator [[Sate Pestage/Legends|Sate Pestage]] during [[King/Legends|King]] [[Ars Veruna/Legends|Veruna’s]] reign. Also referenced was the [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|Episode I]] lead-in story ''[[Cloak of Deception]]'' ([[2001]]) and [[Darth Maul/Legends|Maul’s]] early assignments from ''[[Darth Maul: Shadow Hunter]]'' ([[2001]]) and ''[[Darth Maul: Saboteur]]'' (2001).<ref name="TERC" />

From [[Dark Horse Comics]] stories, the [[Yinchorri Uprising]] was originally depicted in [[Star Wars: Jedi Council: Acts of War|''Jedi Council: Acts of War'']] (2000) miniseries, while [[Vidar Kim]] – Palpatine’s [[Galactic Senate/Legends|Senatorial]] predecessor – first appeared in ''[[Star Wars Republic]]'' [[Republic 64|#64]] ([[2004]]). Darth Maul’s mission to behead [[Black Sun/Legends|Black Sun]] was from the [[Star Wars: Darth Maul (2000)|''Darth Maul'']] comic series (2000). The novel also featured [[Romeo Treblanc]], a [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] mover and shaker who dabbled in the criminal world, and the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bal'demnic/Legends|Bal'demnic]] – elements created by fans and officially sanctioned as ''[[Star Wars]]'' lore by the [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Star Wars Fan Club]] in [[2008]]. The book also weaved elements from concurrently developed projects, like Luceno’s short story ''[[Restraint]]'' and ''[[End Game]]'', both created in anticipation of the [[2012]] 3-D release of ''[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace]]''. ''Darth Plagueis'' also included elements from [[Ryder Windham]]'s ''[[The Wrath of Darth Maul]]'' (2012) novel, and the developing story of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|forth]] and [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth]] seasons of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']] animated series ([[2011]]-[[2013]]).<ref name="TERC" />

In Part II and Part III of the book, where the [[Standard year/Legends|years]] the parts take place in were established, the years were mistakenly said to take place in "BBC" rather than "BBY". Part I, however, was "BBY". On page 390 of the paperback edition of ''Darth Plagueis'', [[Naberrie family/Legends|Naberrie]] was misspelled as "Naberri." Also in the paperback edition of the novel, [[Xizor/Legends|Xizor]] was misspelled as "Xixor" on page 407. The novel's back cover was later used in the [[Canon]] [[Emperor Palpatine (reference book)|Emperor Palpatine]] issue of the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars'' Encyclopedia]] by [[De Agostini]].<ref name="Emperor Palpatine">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'' booklet series]]</ref>
