'''Futhork''' was the most recent of the two [[writing]] systems used by the [[human]] [[Naboo (people)|inhabitants]] of [[Naboo]], the other being [[Futhark]]. It was used by the common populace in informal contexts.<ref name="VE" /> Futhork abundantly appeared on [[starship]] [[computer]] monitors and status displays (as exemplified by [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Amidala's]] [[Naboo Royal Starship|Royal Starship]] and the [[N-1 starfighter]]s), and on the [[Armor|helmets]] of [[engineer]]s working for the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

Futhork also appeared on a commemorative plaque presented to a [[mortician]] on Naboo. It read:<ref name="DV10">[[Darth Vader (2015) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 10]]</ref>

<center><poem>PRESENTED TO
[[Commodex Tahn|COMMODEX<br />TAHN]]
FOR SERVICES
TO NABOO

BY
PADMÉ
AMIDALA.</poem></center>

In [[5 BBY]], hand painted signs written in this script were displayed outside a [[Unidentified screen store|store selling screens]] on [[Ferrix]], with a [[Color|pink]] sign saying "MADE TO <small>''[obscured]''</small>" and a blue one saying "WHILE YOU WAIT".<ref name="Kassa" />

[[File:Djarin_console.png|thumb|left|300px|Futhork on the console of Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter]]During the [[New Republic Era]], the console on [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter]] used Futhork: A blue circular monitor on the console,<ref name="Fett5">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> which functioned as a [[hyperspace]] [[map]],<ref name="Mando17">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref> said "SYSTEM READY", "M", "FLIGHT,"<ref name="Fett5" /> "PT", "STB" and "ON."<ref name="Mando18" /> The greenish-blue navigation monitor in the middle, showed indicators for "P" (the port side), "D" (directly ahead) and "S" (the starboard side).<ref name="Fett5" /> The blue [[Proximity sensor|enemy proximity warning indicator]] on the right side of the console said "SYSTEMS ONLINE • TURBO BOOST ENACTED<ref name="Mando17" /> • LIFE SUPPORT ACTIVE."<ref name="Mando18">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 18: The Mines of Mandalore}}</ref> However, when [[Din Djarin]] left [[Nevarro]], the entire image (which started flashing red when [[Gorian Shard's pirate crew|Gorian Shard's gang]] showed up) was mirror-inverted.<ref name="Mando17" />

During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the population of the only [[Ashas Ree outpost|outpost]] on [[Ashas Ree]] also used Futhork.<ref name="The Relic Raiders" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Naboo_mccaig.jpg|thumb|280px|A mapping of Futhork, from Legends continuity]]
Futhork first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="TPM" /> and was first [[canon]]ically identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Futhork/Legends|Futhork]] was first identified in the 1999 reference book [[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary|''Star Wars: Episode I The Visual Dictionary'']].<ref name="E1 VD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Both "futhark" and "futhork" are alternate words for the [[Wikipedia:Runes|runic alphabet]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/futhark|text=Futhark|quote=variants: or ''less commonly'' '''futhorc''' or '''futhork''' (...) '''Definition of''' '''''futhark''''': the runic alphabet|work=[[Wikipedia:Merriam-Webster|Merriam Webster]]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/Bf9NL|archivedate=January 30, 2022}}</ref>
