==History==
{{Quote|Hey, you Belo? Danka sent me.''"<br/>"''Ah, yes, I heard you were coming.|Kay Vess to Belo Roovin|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Belo Roovin was a [[Pantoran]] [[armorer]] and [[merchant]] who resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> He owned and operated [[Belo Pawn Spot]] in the [[pyke market]] section of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on Toshara. He was pictured on advertisements for the pawn shop that were placed around the city including at the [[Mirogana Spaceport]] and near [[Makal's Gambling Parlor]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> That same year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the pawn shops inventory included [[actuating module]]s, [[ray accelerator]]s, [[cryo-coil]]s, [[MG-2 power exceeder]]s and at least one [[ion casing]]. He offered discounts and exclusive stock which included at least one [[blaster recirculator]] to members of the [[Crimson Dawn]] crime syndicate and its allies. He was also a contract for the [[Mon Calamari]] [[information broker]] [[Danka Orsato]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the Orsato contacted Roovin and informed him that [[Kay Vess]], a [[thief]] Orsato was hiring for a job, would arrived at the shop to acquire the parts to modify her [[Kay's blaster|blaster]]. When Vess arrived at the pawn shop and informed Roovin of who she was, Roovin allowed her to take a [[ion casing]] for free. After which Vess ask Roovin if he had [[ion energy converter]]s for her [[blaster]], to which Roovin informed her that they were illegal on Toshara and that only criminal syndicates had access to them.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Later that same year<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> Vess purchased a blaster recirculator from Roovin for 1350 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] which she used it to further upgrade her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Personality and traits==
Roovin had [[color|blue]] skin, blue [[eye]]s, brown [[hair]] and had a short beard in 3 ABY. He had yellow facial [[tattoo]]s consisting of three stacked horizontal lines on each check, two angled lines between their eyes and two angled lines below their [[mouth]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Skills and abilities==
He spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]] with an accent.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Equipment==
While working in Mirogana, Roovin wore a black [[jacket]] over a dark red [[Clothing|shirt]], brown pants, a black [[scarf]] and a brown waist [[sash]] with an attached pouch.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Belo Roovin appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Prior to the game's release on [[August 30]], he was pictured in the game's "Behind The Scenes &ndash; Navigating the Underworld" trailer that was released on [[June 25]], 2024. In the game, Roovin was part of the "New Trick" main quest which is required to complete the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
