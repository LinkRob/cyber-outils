The '''Bandit Troopers'''<ref name="Art of Visions">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Visions]]''</ref> were a group of bandits led by the [[Sith]] [[Gender|female]] [[Kouru]] that was active on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Genbara]] twenty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Sith rebellion]]. A [[Unidentified teahouse shopkeep|teahouse shopkeep]] believed the bandits were either [[soldier]]s or ex-soldiers, particularly theorizing they were former [[Sith troops]] leftover from the rebellion. They eventually began to stage raids on other settlements from a [[Unidentified deserted village|village]] on Genbara.<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|I'm proud of my innovative design for the Bandit Troopers. Have you ever seen a such a surly batch of stormtroopers? I think we were able to do something cinematic.|Takashi Okazaki|<ref name="Art of Visions" />}}
The troopers were designed by [[Takashi Okazaki]] and were inspired by the bandits in the film ''[[Wikipedia:Seven Samurai|Seven Samurai]]'', by [[Wikipedia:Akira Kurosawa|Akira Kurosawa]]. Okazaki has referred to them<ref name="MakingOfEn">{{WebCite|url=https://cgworld.jp/special-feature/202202-duel1-en.html|text=The Making of Star Wars: Visions: The Duel: Part 1: A Novel Look Combining Kurosawa and Star Wars|date=2022-04-25|work=CGWORLD|archivedate=20250803165953}}</ref> as "落ちトルーパー"<ref name="MakingOfJp">{{WebCite|url=https://cgworld.jp/special-feature/202202-duel2.html|text=スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ『The Duel』メイキング［後篇］黒澤映画と『スター・ウォーズ』への尊敬と共に、全力で遊んだ|date=2022-04-12|work=CGWORLD|language=Japanese|archivedate=20250219130559}}</ref> (fallen troopers).<ref name="MakingOfEn" />
