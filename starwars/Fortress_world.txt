The term '''fortress world''' gained prominence during the [[Clone Wars]], when the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] concentrated self-replicating [[Techno Union]] factories, refineries, and assembly lines around [[planet]]s in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The [[Galactic Republic]] also had fortress worlds, protection of which was among the duties of the [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighters]] during the war.<ref name="SWCV, NE">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref>

If they were left undisturbed, [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s facilities built during the Separatist [[invasion of Utapau]] could have turned [[Utapau]] into another Outer Rim fortress world.<ref name="SWCV, NE" />
