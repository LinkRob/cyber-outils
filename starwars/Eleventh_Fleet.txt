The '''Eleventh Fleet''' was a numbered [[fleet]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was active at the [[time]] of the [[Lothal campaign]]. In [[1 BBY]], [[Commodore]] [[Karyn Faro]] was approved to assume [[Commanding officer|command]] of the fleet by [[Imperial High Command]] after being recommended for the role by her commander, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]].

==Description==
The Eleventh Fleet was a numbered [[fleet]] within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==History==
{{Quote|Your reassignment was approved earlier this morning. You'll be leaving the ''Chimaera'' at Lothal for Coruscant and orientation on your new command.|Grand Admiral Thrawn, to Commodore Faro|audio=11th-Fleet-Approved.flac|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
[[File:Grand_Admiral_Thrawn_canon.png|250px|thumb|right|Faro was recommended to command the Eleventh Fleet by her superior, Grand Admiral Thrawn (pictured).]]
The Eleventh Fleet was active at the [[time]] of the [[Lothal campaign]]<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> [[Commodore]] [[Karyn Faro]], [[captain]] of the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'', was originally set to assume [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Task Force 231]], although [[Imperial High Command]] failed to meet their deadlines for her pre-command orientation. In truth, Faro's commander, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] of the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]], had requested to Imperial High Command that Faro be considered to command the Eleventh Fleet. A few [[Standard week|weeks]] later, Thrawn's request had been approved by High Command.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

On the [[Standard day|day]] of the approval, the Grand Admiral told his subordinate that he had intentionally been preventing her move to Task Force 231. Thrawn, who had taken Faro as his protégé, believed that she was better than the leader of a [[task force]] and revealed that he had requested Faro to be considered for command of the Eleventh Fleet, which he believed could lead to a promotion from her [[Military rank|rank]] of commodore. He added that Imperial High Command had approved his request earlier that morning, with Faro due to leave the ''Chimaera'' upon its return to the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] and head to the planet [[Coruscant]] for orientation on her new command.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|Congratulations, Commodore Faro. May you serve the Eleventh Fleet as well as you've served the Seventh.|Grand Admiral Thrawn|audio=Faro-Congratulations.flac|<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />}}
The Eleventh Fleet was set to be commanded by Karyn Faro, who was cited by her commanding [[officer]], Grand Admiral Thrawn, as having superior skills as an [[administrator]], tactician, officer, commander, and [[Politics|political]] agent.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Eleventh Fleet was mentioned in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2019]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'', the final installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
