The '''Anaxes shipyards''' were [[shipyard]]s vital to the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] located on the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]]. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] sought to claim these shipyards during the [[Battle of Anaxes]]. As a backup plan, the Separatist army had also planted a [[bomb]] in the facility near the [[Fusion generator|fusion reactor]], the detonation of which could potentially destroy the entire planet. After [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC Trooper]] [[Echo]] sent out a feedback pulse that shut down all ground forces on Anaxes, the Harch [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] enacted the backup plan, activating the bomb. [[Jedi Master]]s [[Mace Windu]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] ordered their troops to evacuate the assembly complex before Windu tracked down the bomb and deactivated it with the help of Echo and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] retrieving the code to do so.<ref name="UB" />
