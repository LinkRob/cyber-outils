The '''Executioner''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Devaronian]] [[bounty hunter]] active during the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He had [[Color|red]] skin and a scarred face.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

Having once [[Death|killed]] the [[Phydolon]] [[Alk ol Nojj]], he was later confronted by [[Ark ol Loor]], but she was rescued by a [[Force-sensitive]] old man, who [[Force push]]ed the Executioner.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Executioner appears in the opening story in [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[2018]] [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Knights of Fate]]''.
