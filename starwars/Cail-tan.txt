'''Cail-tan''' was a [[human]] [[X-wing starfighter]] [[pilot]] for the [[New Republic]]. Around [[9 ABY]] their squadron was [[Invasion of At Attin|deployed]] to the [[planet]] [[At Attin]] in order to repel a [[Silvo's pirates|group]] of [[pirate]]s that had invaded.

==Biography==
Cail-tan was a [[human]] who served in the [[New Republic]] as an [[X-wing starfighter]] [[pilot]]. During the [[New Republic Era]], the [[astronomer]] [[Kh'ymm]] contacted the New Republic on behalf of a [[Skeleton Crew|group]] of [[youngling]]s who where requesting help from a [[Silvo's pirates|group]] of [[pirate]]s that had [[Invasion of At Attin|invaded]] their [[Homeworld|home]] [[planet]] [[At Attin]]. Their squadron of eight [[T-65 X-wing starfighter]]s was deployed along with two [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]]s and a [[CR90 corvette]] to repeal the pirates.<ref name="Good Guys" />

As they approached the planet and the [[Silvo's pirate frigate|pirate frigate]], [[Commander]] [[Kent]] instructed the squadron to lock their [[S-foils]] in attack positions. Cail-tan further set their [[Starfighter|fighter's]] [[deflector shield]] to double forward. Afterward the squadron began their attack on the pirate [[frigate]]. The squadron eventually destroyed the frigate which crashed down into the [[Unidentified city (At Attin)|city]] below.<ref name="Good Guys" />

==Personality and traits==
Cail-tan had tan skin, long brown [[hair]] and brown [[eye]]s. During their attack on the pirates on At Attin they had their hair braided and draped to their right side.<ref name="Good Guys" />

==Equipment==
Cail-tan wore an blue [[flight suit]] with and a white [[K-22995 light flight helmet]]. The helmet had a yellow strip between two blue stripes down the center of the helmet with a yellow New Republic [[Starbird (symbol)|starbird]] [[Sigil of the New Republic|symbol]] on each side of it. They had a red and blue [[victory decal]] on each side of their helmet.<ref name="Good Guys" />
