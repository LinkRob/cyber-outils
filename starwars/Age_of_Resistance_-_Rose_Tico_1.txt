'''''Age of Resistance - Rose Tico'' 1''' is the seventh one-shot in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Age of Resistance]]''. It was written by [[Tom Taylor]], illustrated by [[Ramon Rosanas]], and published on [[September 18]], [[2019]] by [[Marvel Comics]]. The issue features the story "'''My Hero'''," and it also includes an essay written by [[Bria LaVorgna]].

==Publisher's summary==
''"MY HERO" Sisters. Friends. Co-pilots. Growing up, Rose and Paige Tico were everything to each other. Until the First Order tore their world apart. See the bond between Rose and Paige before it was forever broken.''
