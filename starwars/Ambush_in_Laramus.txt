"'''Ambush in Laramus'''" was a short vignette included in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', published in [[1987]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]] undertake a successful ambush of an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] convoy in the [[Laramus system/Legends|Laramus system]]. It was reprinted in the [[The Star Wars Sourcebook (Second Edition)|second edition]] of the book in [[1994]].

==Plot summary==
The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance]] [[Cruiser/Legends|cruiser]] ''[[Visseon II]]'' ambushes an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] convoy in the [[Laramus system/Legends|Laramus system]]. As the Imperial convoy split into groups in an attempt to isolate the ''Visseon II'', a [[Flight group/Legends|flight group]] of [[T-65 X-wing starfighter/Legends|T-65 X-wing starfighters]] emerges from [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]] and overwhelms the [[Transport/Legends|transports]]. The result of the ambush was fourteen Imperial transports captured without a single loss for the Rebellion.
