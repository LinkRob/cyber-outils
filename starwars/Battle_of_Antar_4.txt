The '''Battle of Antar 4''' was an engagement fought at<ref name="Survivor" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Antar 4]]<ref name="Survivor" /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> One [[BX-series droid commando]] served with distinction in the battle and, during the unit's service to the [[Bedlam Raiders]], repeatedly bragged of his success.<ref name="Survivor" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the BX began telling the story again while on guard duty with two [[B1-series battle droid]]s, only for one of the B1s to object at needing to hear the story for the thirty-eighth time. The [[commando]] threatened his fellow unit for interrupting him.<ref name="Survivor" />
