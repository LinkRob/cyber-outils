'''First Order snowtroopers''' were a variant of the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] used by the [[First Order]] thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. They wore insulated [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]] and [[helmet]]s designed for cold weather. Snowtroopers saw action during the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] and the [[Battle of Crait]] in [[34 ABY]], and by [[35 ABY]] they took part in the First Order's [[Occupation of Kijimi|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="TROS" />

==Equipment==
[[File:FOSnowtrooperGear-SWBC50.png|thumb|left|300px|Snowtrooper gear: backpack, chestplate, gloves and helmet]]
The successor to the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Cold weather assault trooper|Snowtroopers]], First Order snowtroopers wore advanced insulated [[armor]] and [[helmet]]s made from [[betaplast]] with flared neck shroud and a polarized slit visor to minimize ice glare, two breather tank inlets. They wore insulated [[glove]]s with adjustable heating units. Their body armor also had heating controls,<ref name="VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and they wore an insulated belt [[kama]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> They had modified [[F-11D blaster rifle]]s with antifogging optics and heat shunting devices.<ref name="Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Some also wore large capes.<ref name="TROS" />
[[File:SnowtrooperOfficerTROSVD.png|thumb|right|145px|A Snowtrooper wearing a cape]]

Some [[First Order Walker Driver]]s wore armor similar to that of both standard walker pilots and snowtroopers.<ref name="TLJ ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref><ref name="Hasbro">[[:File:Hasbro First Order snowtrooper walker pilot.jpg]]</ref>

==History==
[[File:FOSnowtrooperOfficer-SWBC50.png|thumb|left|250px|A snowtrooper officer in Starkiller Base]]
Snowtroopers were deployed to [[Hays Minor]] during the First Order's occupation of the planet.<ref name="Age of Resistance" /> Many snowtroopers were stationed on the artic world<ref name="Episode VII" /> of [[Ilum]]<ref name="Episode IX VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> after it was transformed into [[Starkiller Base]]. However, it was [[Battle of Starkiller Base|attacked and destroyed]] by the [[Resistance]]<ref name="Episode VII" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

Snowtroopers were also involved in the [[Battle of Crait]] shortly thereafter.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Although [[Crait]] was only superficially a snowy planet due to the planet's white [[salt]] flats obscuring [[Rhodochrosite|red]] [[crystal]]s on the ground, it nonetheless had similar enough properties to necessitate their deployment in the battle.<ref name="TLJ:TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Under the order of [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]], snowtroopers assisted in the [[Destruction of Tah'Nuhna|destruction]] of the peaceful planet [[Tah'Nuhna]] for their aid toward the Resistance.<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="35 ABY Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the "[[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]]." As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Starkiller Incident to [[34 ABY]], it can be deduced the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> snowtroopers [[Occupation of Kijimi|occupied]] [[Kijimi City]], enforcing the [[Supreme Leader]]'s will. However, under [[Protocol 13]], all forces were withdrawn before the planet was [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroyed]] by the [[Sith Eternal]].<ref name="Episode IX VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The latest generation of cold weather assault Stormtroopers, Snowtroopers wear advanced insulated armor and helmets that reduce the glare of icy terrain.|[[Star Wars: The Black Series]]|<ref name="Hasbo">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=Snowtrooper|altlink=https://galacticfigures.com/figureDetails.aspx?id=3659}}</ref>}}
First Order Snowtroopers first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. For the costumes used in the film, the snowtroopers' armor, helmet, and backpack were made of molded [[Wikipedia:polyurethane|polyurethane]], while their [[glove]]s were made of nylon with additional molded polyurethane elements. Their quilted tunics and collars were made from jersey-backed neoprene, with the tunics also incorporating polyester and cotton.<ref name="SW7N">{{WebCite|url=https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com/2015/04/closer-look-at-the-force-awakens-flametrooper-snowtrooper-and-more.html|text=Closer look at ''The Force Awakens'' flametrooper, snowtrooper and more!|work=[https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com Star Wars News Network]|archivedate=20170607072928}}</ref> The nylon boots they wear are "Kamik White Men's Canuck Cold Weather Boots."<ref name="fb">{{Facebook|noprofile=1|author=The Parts of Star Wars|date=2015-11-20|url=media/set/?set=a.774461679343115.1073741853.193037424152213|archivedate=20200820185952|nolive=1}}</ref>
