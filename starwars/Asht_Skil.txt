'''Asht Skil''' was the [[author]] of ''[[Left Beneath the Snow: Rebel Grief on Hoth]]'', a work that focused on the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Battle of Hoth|defeat]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] recommended Skil's work in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Asht Skil was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
