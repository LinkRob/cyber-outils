The '''Crakull system''' was located in the [[Unknown Regions]] in grid square G-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crakull [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Star system/Legends|star system]] that was home to the [[Croke]] [[Species/Legends|species]] was first indirectly mentioned in the [[1983]] novel ''[[Lando Calrissian and the Starcave of ThonBoka]]'', the final volume in [[Star Wars: The Lando Calrissian Adventures|''The Lando Calrissian Adventures'']] trilogy, which was written by [[L. Neil Smith]].<ref name="ThonBoka">''[[Lando Calrissian and the Starcave of ThonBoka]]''</ref> The [[2002]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]'' then specified that the Croke were native to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Crakull]].<ref name="NEGtC">''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''</ref> Finally, in [[2009]], the reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' introduced the [[Crakull system/Legends|Crakull system]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
