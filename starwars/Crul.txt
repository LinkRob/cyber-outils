'''Crul''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="True Love" /> located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] that was covered in [[Temperature|warm]], shallow,<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> and [[salt]]y<ref name="True Love" /> [[Ocean|seas]] and lagoons.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of two [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]: the [[Crolute]]s, who were exclusively [[Gender|male]], and the [[Gilliand]]s, who were exclusively female.<ref name="True Love" />
