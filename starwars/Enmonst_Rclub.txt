'''Enmonst Rclub''' was an establishment located in a seedy part of the [[Coruscant Underworld]]. It was situated right between [[Sleeping Rose]] and [[The Hole]]. A run-down sign spelled out the name in luminous [[Aurebesh]] characters. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] stayed in an apartment on the same block as Enmonst Rclub, while planning a [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|mission]] to break into the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="HH" />
