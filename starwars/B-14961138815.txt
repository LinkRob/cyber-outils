'''B-14961138815''' was a [[B1-series battle droid]] who at one point broke a door on [[Fortress Vader]] with an [[Axe|ax]] which scared [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]], [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]].<ref name="Tales Trailer" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
B-14961138815 first appeared in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] [[Disney+]] Halloween special, ''[[LEGO Star Wars Terrifying Tales]]''.<ref name="Tales Trailer" />

B-14961138815 breaking down a door with an ax and saying his name is a direct reference to an iconic scene in the [[1980]] film [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Shining_(film) The Shining], in which the character [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Torrance Jack Torrance] breaks down a door with an ax and yells "Here's Johnny!" The droid's name includes the numbers [[1138 (easter egg)|1138]], a reference to [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/THX_1138 THX-1138], [[George Lucas]]' first film.<ref name="20 Easter Eggs">{{SW|url=news/lego-star-wars-terrifying-tales-easter-eggs|text=20 Eerie Easter Eggs from ''LEGO Star Wars Terrifying Tales''}}</ref>
