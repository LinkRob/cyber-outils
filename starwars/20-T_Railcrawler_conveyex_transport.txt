The '''20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport''', known simply as the '''conveyex''',<ref name="Databank" /> and also known under its manufacturing code '''ATD-C45''',<ref name="Walkers and Ground Vehicles">{{EncyclopediaCite|Walkers and Ground Vehicles of the Empire and First Order}}</ref> was a model of heavily [[armor]]ed cargo [[train]] manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]].<ref name="Custom Nissan" /> It saw action during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="TOG" />

==Description==
[[File:ConveyexCar-SWBC45.png|thumb|left|300px|An armored car from a conveyex train]]
20-T Railcrawler conveyex transports were used for rapid [[transport]] of special cargo across surface distances on frontier [[Planet|worlds]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] used heavily armored conveyex vehicles that could travel along rails winding through treacherous terrains.<ref name="Databank" /> Unlike other train systems, however, conveyex systems used narrow, seemingly endless link belts similar to [[tank]] treads, rather than steel wheels and tracks.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

It had several high-security containers that were only accessible with quantum-switch code keys, which contained shape-shifting data lattices and contact terminals, and signatures of their wearers. For example, the container [[ICC-5537]] once carried 400 [[kilogram]]s of the [[Fuel|hyperfuel]] [[coaxium]]. They were sometimes guarded by [[range trooper]]s with magnetic boots to protect valuable freight.<ref name="TOG" /> Conveyex transports were a large target for criminals and train robbers, though an assault on one typically resulted in the criminals' destruction.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==History==
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' establishes that the events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' begin nine [[Standard year|years]] after the founding of the [[Galactic Empire]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the Empire's founding to [[19 BBY]], meaning the events of ''Solo'', and the 20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport's appearance in it, can be placed in [[10 BBY]].</ref> a conveyex on the planet [[Vandor]] was carrying freight of [[coaxium]] that [[Beckett's gang]] [[Heist on Vandor|tried to steal]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Due to the Empire being focused on security over scenery, the train lacked windows save for narrow, grilled viewing points atop the drive chain at the front. In addition, the train was split into three portions: The powerful main drive engine, a series of standard intermodal cargo containers, and a caboose with stabilizer bars and cannon turrets at the rear. Due to Vandor's deliberate inaccessibility for normal supply deliveries for security purposes, the conveyex acted as the only way to deliver various supplies between Imperial installations.<ref name="TOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport, simply referred as "Imperial train" in the film's script, was designed by the [[Lucasfilm]] design supervisor [[James Clyne]] for the 2018 film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Who Designed This">{{SW|url=news/designing-solo-a-star-wars-story-part-3-conveyex-train-heist|text=Designing ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'', Part 3: Pulling Off the Train Heist}}</ref> Two early influences for the transport were the hard-angled and armor-plated [[Wikipedia:World War II|World War II]]&ndash;era [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] army trains known as [[Wikipedia:Panzerzug|Panzerzugs]] and the [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|British]] [[Wikipedia:British heavy tanks of World War I|Mark I and V Tanks]], while the train engine car's design was inspired by the [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] and [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] walker "heads" featured in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]] and the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> 

A specialized [[Imperial weapons technician]]s wearing a black [[sheep]]skin [[Jacket|coat]] with a fur collar due to adjustments to the planet's cold environment was conceived for the conveyex, with concept art created by [[Glyn Dillon]] and physical helmets having been created,<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> but ultimately didn't end up in the final film.<ref name="Solo" /> A minifigure with the design however appears in the 2018 [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set 75257 "Imperial Conveyex Transport" operating a gun.<ref name="LEGO">{{LEGOCite|num=75217|name=Imperial Conveyex Transport}}</ref>
