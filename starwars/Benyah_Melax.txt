'''Benyah Melax''' was a [[human]] [[starship]] [[Technician|mechanic]] who resided on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which operated a [[Benyah Melax's ship parts|shop]] at the [[Kijimi Spaceport]] and had a no refund policy. Melax had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and spoke [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]]. While operating the shop they wore a dark [[jacket]], brown [[glove]]s, a brown [[hat]] and [[goggles]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|option=1}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] utilized Melax's services to upgrade the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Benyah Melax appeared in the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, they were one of several mechanics that the player could interact with to upgrade their starship.<ref name="Outlaws" /><ref name="Outlaws" />
