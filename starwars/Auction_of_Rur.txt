The '''auction of Rur ''' was held by rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] around [[1 ABY]] on the [[Sorca Retreat]] [[space station]] in which she hoped to sell off the [[Rur Crystal]], an ancient [[Ordu artifact]] which contained the corrupted [[artificial intelligence]] of the former [[Jedi]] [[Rur]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9" />

==Prelude==
After acquiring the [[Rur Crystal]], the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] decided to sell the artifact for a profit.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9" /> She managed to organize an auction at the [[space station]] [[Sorca Retreat]] by infiltrating two [[Undervine]] [[journalist]]s' [[starship]] and using their [[transmitter]] to advertise her auction. To distract the journalists [[Dixnet Dat]] and [[Domthro Rus]], Dr Aphra hired the [[Wookiee]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]] to entertain them with stories about his life as a bounty hunter in the service of the [[Xonti Brothers]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra Annual 1">[[Doctor Aphra Annual 1|''Doctor Aphra Annual'' 1]]</ref>

==The auction==
===The bidding war===
The Auction of Rura attracted several guests including [[Yonak]] of the [[Son-tuul Pride]], the [[Cyban Front]], the [[Shadow University]], the [[Dominion of the Ezaraa]], [[Thomas Toov]], Papa [[Toren]] and [[Sutha]] of the [[Hutt Clan]]. Addressing the guests, Dr Aphra claims that Eternal Rur can provide invaluable data, hold the secrets to eternal life, and can take control of artificial intelligence through [[technopathy]]. The [[Cyban Front]] is skeptical of her claims until [[Unidentified blue Cyban Front droid|one]] of their droids is possessed by Eternal Rur before being struck down. Dr Aphra then leaves so that the bidders can consider their bids.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9" />

Seeking [[Droid rights|freedom]] from their master, Dr Aphra's "killer [[droid]]s" [[0-0-0|Triple Zero]] and [[BT-1|Beetee]] decide to sabotage her plans by informing [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] about the auction.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9" /> While the blue Cyban Front droid was being repaired, Triple Zero and Beetee also secretly installed the Rur crystal inside the droid's chassis in an effort to turn the situation to their favor and due to their love for carnage and violence.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />

Dr Aphra then met with the bidders. Papa Toren, who was representing the [[Xonti Brothers]], said that his clients would like to examine the artifacts before making an offer. The first bidder was Thomas Toov, who desired immortality. Despite their mutual animosity, the Son-tuul Pride and Hutt Clan combined their bids in an attempt to bleed each other. By contrast, the [[Unidentified red Cyban Front droid|red droid]] representing the Cyban Front offered to pay Dr Aphra to destroy the crystal, fearing the danger of technopathy.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />

Meanwhile, the [[Xenophobia|xenophobic]] Ezaraa placed a bid 0.000001% of their Imperial revenue for the Rur crystal. They threatened to torture Aphra and her descendants should she refuse to accept their offer. Meanwhile, the Shadow University's representatives offer Dr Aphra unique artifacts and full access to the [[Shadow University's archives]]. They also threatened to expose the fraudulent methods she had used to obtain her doctorate should she refuse. Finally, the Xoxti Brothers wanted to use the Rur crystal to power [[android]] gladiators. Their presence created friction with their former slave gladiator Black Krrsantan, who wanted to settle scores with them for their brutal training.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />

===A disastrous auction===
After Triple Zero informed Dr Aphra that the Shadow University had increased its bid to a little over a third of Toov's bid, Dr Aphra decided to accept the university's offer. Summoning the bidders to the main hall, Dr Aphra announced that the Shadow University had won the bid. Before she could arrange shipping however, Eternal Rur broke out of the containment cylinder. Housed inside the chassis of the blue Cyban droid and armed with two blue lightsabers, the artificial intelligence sought revenge against those who had tried to buy him.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />

==Aftermath==
Eternal Rur proceeded to [[Skirmish aboard the Sorca Retreat|slaughter]] many of the bidders. Triple Zero and Beetee, who had taken over the gun turrets, also unleashed blaster fire on the guests. Amidst the mayhem, Dr Aphra struck a deal with Black Krrsantan to help him access weapons he needed to honor his "[[life debt]]" to the Xonti Brothers. The surviving bidders armed themselves with weapons while Dr Aphra made her way back to her room in order to renegotiate a deal with her killer droids. Lord Vader, who had been alerted to the auction, arrived with an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] and [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 11]]</ref>

Dr Aphra managed to convince Triple Zero and Beetee to spare her life in return for removing the inhibitor program that allowed her to control them. Aphra subsequently helped Black Krrsantan to capture the Xonti Brothers, allowing him to settle scores with them.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 12|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 12]]</ref> Aphra managed to escape with the Shadow University's ship, which was carrying rare artifacts. Meanwhile, Darth Vader defeated Eternal Rur and captured the Rur crystal. The deaths of many underworld leaders at the Sorca auction crippled the underworld, allowing the [[Galactic Empire]] to focus on the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 13">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 13]]</ref>
