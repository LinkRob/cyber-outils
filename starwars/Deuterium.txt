'''Deuterium''' was a type of [[fuel]]. It was used to power the [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]].<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In real life, deuterium is a heavy isotope of [[hydrogen]]. It is a light, [[color]]less, odorless, flammable gas, and can be used as a fuel, both chemical and fusion.
