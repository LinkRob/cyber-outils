'''Borja Pindado''' is a [[Wikipedia:Spaniard|Spanish]] illustrator for [[Fantasy Flight Games]], providing illustrations for several of their products including ''[[Allies and Adversaries]]'' for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' game, [[LAAT/le Patrol Transport Unit Expansion]] for ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' miniature game.<ref name="Artstation" />
