'''Agon''' was an individual who lived on the [[planet]] [[Ridlay]] during the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. In one of the planet's total [[eclipse]]s&mdash;which occurred every fifty [[Standard year|years]], bringing with it the appearance of beings that Ridlay's [[Unidentified Ridlay species|inhabitants]] believed to be [[Ghost (folklore)|ghosts]]&mdash;the beings instead attacked the inhabitants, driving them from their homes. After [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] and a group of [[Galactic Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s were dispatched to investigate the subsequent lack of communications on Ridlay, Agon was sent to warn them. Shortly after Agon's arrival, the supposed ghosts arrived, and Agon led Windu and the [[Cloning|clones]] to the shelter where the displaced residents were hiding.<ref name="Separation Anxiety Part One" />

After speaking with the group's [[Unidentified Ridlay leader|leader]], Windu wished to investigate the [[Ascension Well]], where the spirits emerged from, and the leader assigned Agon to guide him. Once Agon, Windu, and the clones arrived at the Ascension Well, they were once again confronted by the beings, though Windu determined through [[the Force]] that they were alive and that their minds were conflicted. Before Windu could investigate further, [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] leader [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] subsequently revealed his presence to the group atop a [[Multi-Troop Transport]].<ref name="Separation Anxiety Part One" />
