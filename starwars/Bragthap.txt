'''Bragthap''' was a [[bartender]] who worked at the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]].<ref name="Last Shot" /> Around [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of ''[[Last Shot]]'' that feature [[Han Solo]] to around [[3 BBY]].</ref> he put a bubbly concoction in front of Kanata as she sat next to the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] when he visited the castle as he waited for [[Sana Starros]] to show up with the cargo she needed his help smuggling.<ref name="Last Shot" /> He was friends with [[Strono Tuggs]], the long time [[Chef|cook]] at Max's castle, who praised Bragthrap for the patience he had to work with the bar's "ne'er do well" clientele.<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref>
