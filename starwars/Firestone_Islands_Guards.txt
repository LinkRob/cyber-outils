The '''Firestone Islands Guards''' was a [[Law enforcement agency|security force]] on the [[planet]] [[Atterra Bravo]] that [[Rendal Panzoro]] was a [[pilot]] for. They policed [[Pirate|piracy]] and [[Smuggler|smuggling]]. After the [[First Order]] [[blockade]]d the [[Atterra system]], they impounded the [[Starship|ships]] used by the planets security forces including some of the Firestone Islands Guards, though the [[Bravo Rising]] were able to hide some before they were impounded. The Firestone Island Guards were later brutally and ruthlessly targeted by the First Order.<ref name="Cobalt" />
