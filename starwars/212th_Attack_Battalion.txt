The '''212th Attack Battalion''', also known as the '''212th Republic Battalion''' or simply the '''212th Battalion''', was an elite [[clone trooper]] [[battalion]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Serving under the [[7th Sky Corps]], the battalion was led by [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]]. The [[212th Recon Division]] was a special detachment of the battalion, which also consisted of various units including [[Foxtrot Group]] and [[Ghost Company]]. A full [[Mekedrix's company|company]] of [[Roon's military defense forces]] was transferred to the 212th during the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, expanding the battalion to include [[Roonian]] [[soldier]]s for the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]].

While serving under [[General]] Kenobi and [[Clone Commander]] Cody, the 212th became known for its successful tactics and involvement in several major engagements, such as the [[Battle of Christophsis]], the [[Battle of Teth]], and the [[Battle of Ryloth]]. The 212th also played a role in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], where it assisted in the destruction of a newly-constructed [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|droid factory]] on [[Geonosis]], as well as the capture of [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]]. During the [[Battle of Umbara]], the 212th and the [[501st Legion]] arrested the [[Dark side of the Force|fallen]] [[Jedi]] General [[Pong Krell]] for [[treason]]. In an effort to bring an end to the war, the battalion was deployed against the [[Separatist Droid Army]] on [[Utapau]], where Kenobi ultimately [[Death|killed]] General [[Grievous]].

During the [[Battle of Utapau]], however, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] issued [[Order 66]] to the 212th and the rest of the clone [[army]], ordering them to [[Great Jedi Purge|purge]] the [[Jedi Order]] for treason against the Republic. [[Behavioral modification biochip|Programmed]] for obedience, [[Commander]] Cody and his [[trooper]]s complied with the chancellor's order, but failed in their efforts to kill Kenobi. In the aftermath of the Clone Wars, the 212th was absorbed into the [[Imperial Military]] under the [[Proclamation of the New Order|newly-established]] [[Galactic Empire]]. As an Imperial [[stormtrooper]] battalion, the 212th [[Wookiee revolt|subjugated]] the [[Wookiee]] [[homeworld]] of [[Kashyyyk]] under the command of General [[Kahdah]].

===Culture and training===
{{Quote|That bunker's gonna be a problem, General.''"<br />"''Leave the bunker to me. Bring in your troopers on my signal.|Cody and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" />}}
<div style="float:right">[[File:ROTS_Utapau_surprise.jpg|thumb|250px|As a battalion, the 212th specialized in frontal assaults and siege warfare.]]</div>
Under the leadership of General Kenobi and [[Clone Commander]] Cody, the 212th gained a distinguished career in the [[Republic Military]], achieving victories on several [[planet]]s across [[the galaxy]] from [[Ryloth]] to [[Umbara]] and [[Utapau]]. The battalion was renowned particularly for its use of full-frontal assaults and siege breaking tactics, as well as for often serving alongside the elite [[501st Legion]]. The 212th Recon [[Division]] was specifically trained to conduct covert missions behind enemy lines.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[Advanced Recon Force trooper]]s within the 212th led clandestine reconnaissance missions.<ref name="MinistravCard">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/star-wars-legion-ministrav-card-update|text=Star Wars: Legion Ministrav Card Update|archivedate=20251115231930}}</ref>

===Equipment and vehicles===
{{Quote|Minimal destruction with blasters and droid poppers only. No rockets or detonators. Check your aim.|Cody|<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" />}}
[[File:212th-Trooper-P2.png|thumb|left|200px|Members of the 212th Attack Battalion wore clone trooper armor with orange markings.]]
The members of the 212th wore the [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I]] and [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II]] versions of [[clone trooper armor]] in turn.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Although Cody originally wore armor with yellow markings<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> like other commanders active during the early war,<ref name="Episode II" /> the 212th eventually, like other battalions and units, decorated their standard white [[armor]] with unique markings, using [[Color|orange]] patterns to denote their affiliation with the 212th Battalion.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Although there were still some clones within the unit who retained white armor,<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> Cody and many others adopted the orange markings<ref name="Legacy">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref> that helped single out 212th members among the otherwise identical clone ranks.<ref name="SWE Boil">{{SWE|characters|boil|Boil}}</ref> The number of clones taking on the color only grew as the war ground on.<ref name="Episode III" /> Its use special unit markings began with the Phase I armor and carried over to Phase II. The 212th Recon Division used a camouflaged version of the Phase I armor to fulfill its role as a reconnaissance unit.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

For weapons, the 212th had access to an array of [[blaster]]s, including the standard-issue [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] and the [[DC-15A blaster rifle]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The battalion's variant units were outfitted with weapons suited to their roles, such as the [[DC-15LE]] [[blaster rifle]] or the [[Valken-38 blaster rifle|Valken-38x]] [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Although the battalion possessed [[missile launcher]]s, [[thermal detonator]]s and other destructive firepower, its troops also carried [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade|Electro Magnetic Pulse grenades]] to minimize destruction to property and loss of life.<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" />

The 212th had a particular focus on vehicles, exemplifying the [[combined arms]] tactics of the GAR.<ref name="MinistravCard" /> [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s were used by the 212th Battalion's splinter group, the 212th Recon Division, which was trained to operate fast and agile [[walker]]s behind enemy lines.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> A variety of other vehicles were also used by the 212th, including [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s, [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]]s,<ref name="Episode III" /> and [[TX-130 Saber-class fighter tank|TX-130 ''Saber''-class fighter tactics]], which were deployed as the spearhead of the battalion's armored assaults.<ref name="MinistravCard" />

===Troopers===
{{Quote|Go, go, go, go! Move it! Move it! Everybody out. Let's go, let's go.''"<br />"''Cover yourself. Come on.|Clone troopers of the 212th Attack Battalion|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:CloneTales-SWI187.png|thumb|right|300px|The 212th, under General Kenobi, regularly worked with the 501st Legion under General Skywalker.]]
As a battalion of the Grand [[Army]], the 212th's ranks consisted of [[clone trooper]]s who were [[Cloning|bred]] from the [[Clone template|template]] of [[Jango Fett]] and regarded as the "latest evolution in [[Galaxy|galactic]] warfare." In addition to standard [[trooper]]s,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> the 212th included [[clone flametrooper]]s,<ref name="HC 35" /> [[clone trooper officer]]s,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> clone paratroopers,<ref name="Episode III" /> [[clone sharpshooter]]s, and [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers served in the 212th<ref name="MinistravCard" /> and its 212th Recon Division subunit.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> The 212th Battalion's Foxtrot Group was a [[squad]] of elite [[clone commando]]s including Captain Gregor.<ref name="Missing in Action" />

The 212th expanded its ranks to include [[Roonian]] [[soldier]]s, who served alongside the battalion's clones as supplemental units during the attack on [[Abrion Bridge]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> Following the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganization]] as the [[Galactic Empire]], the 212th became an Imperial [[stormtrooper]] battalion of the overall [[Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="HoloNet" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
====Service on Christophsis and Teth====
{{Quote|Cody, battle positions!''"<br />"''Up to the front!|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Cody|<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>}}
[[File:Cody_Christophsis.png|thumb|left|250px|The 212th Battalion successfully defended Christophsis against the Separatist Droid Army.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Cat & Mouse Date">{{TCWcanondates|216}}</ref> the 212th Attack Battalion was sent to [[Christophsis]],<ref name="Cat & Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> where they fought alongside the 501st [[Legion]]'s [[Torrent Company]] led by Jedi General [[Anakin Skywalker]] and Clone Captain [[Rex]].<ref name="TCW film" />&mdash; During the initial phase of the [[Battle of Christophsis]], the 212th and the 501st attempted to ambush the [[Separatist Droid Army]]. The ambush was foiled by [[Clone Sergeant]] "[[Slick]]," who had secretly betrayed the Republic by becoming a Separatist [[spy]] for [[Asajj Ventress]]. Slick's actions allowed the Separatists to ambush their would-be ambushers, thereby forcing the Jedi and their clones to retreat to their [[Christophsis Republic base|base]]. Although Slick was subsequently arrested by Commander Cody and Captain Rex, the renegade clone managed to sabotage the base's [[gunship]]s and AT-TEs. However, the [[AV-7 Anti-vehicle Artillery Cannon]]s were saved.<ref name="Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> Using the [[cannon]]s, the 212th and the 501st were able to hold out until Jedi General [[Yoda]] arrived with reinforcements.<ref name="TCW film" />

Following General Kenobi's negotiations with [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], the 212th went to [[Teth]] to assist General Skywalker, [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and Torrent [[Company]] in [[Battle of Teth|rescuing]] Jabba's son, [[Rotta]]. The 212th were able to rescue the survivors on Teth and picked up General Skywalker and Commander Tano on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="TCW film" />

====Battle of Abrion Bridge====
{{Quote|Excuse me, General Kenobi. I'm ''Commander Mekedrix''. This is ''Lieutenant Oron''. We just transferred over from the ''Roon system''.''"<br />"''Ah yes. Welcome to the 212th.|Mekedrix and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />}}
[[File:RoonianSoldiers-ObiWan3.png|thumb|right|250px|Led by Commander Mekedrix, a company of Roonian soldiers was transferred to the 212th Attack Battalion.]]
The [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]] was the result of information recovered by General Kenobi and Cody prior to the [[Defense of Rishi Station|destruction]] of [[Rishi Station]]. Having learned that the Confederacy's [[War Faction]] created a [[mega-ion cannon]] that was capable of disabling a whole [[fleet]] with one blast, the Republic ordered the 212th and [[442nd Siege Battalion]] to acquire the Separatist weapon's schematics on [[Abrion Major]]. The [[Separatist weapons factory (Abrion Major)|Separatist weapons factory]] was located on the center island of an archipelago chain in the [[western sea]]; a single supply bridge served as the factory's only access point. Therefore, the battalion was divided into two groups to capture both ends of the bridge before converging on the Separatist complex. Due to the scale of the operation, the Republic expanded the 212th Battalion to include a full company of Roonian soldiers as well as their commanding officers, Commander Mekedrix and Lieutenant Oron.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

[[File:CutOffByIGs-ObiWan3.png|thumb|left|250px|Though reinforced by Roonian soldiers, the 212th suffered high casualties during the Battle of Abrion Bridge.]]
Despite Mekedrix's optimism for victory, Kenobi&mdash;still adjusting to his new roles as a Jedi Master and a Jedi General&mdash;considered Abrion Bridge a "death trap." The 212th was separated into groups [[Attack-1]] and [[Attack-2]]; the former was led by Kenobi and Cody, while the latter served under Mekedrix and Oron. Kenobi ordered Attack-2 to secure the north side of the bridge while Attack-1 concentrated on the south. Both groups sustained heavy losses while attempting to gain control of the bridge; although Kenobi and Attack-1 achieved the objective of acquiring the plans to the [[ion cannon]], Attack-2 failed to take the north end of Abrion Bridge. As a consequence, hundreds of Separatist forces, including [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank|IG-227 ''Hailfire''-class droid tanks]], [[IG-series]] droids, and [[Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter|''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighters]] closed in on Kenobi's group from the north.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

Kenobi, Mekedrix, and Cody ultimately survived, but the 212th's losses were "countless" according to Mekedrix. By the battle's end, their casualties included Lieutenant Oron and the clone trooper [[Airo]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

====The liberation of Ryloth====
{{Quote|We need to remember why we're here. We came to aid the Twi'leks, not destroy their home. Cody?''"<br />"''That means we'll be taking it back the hard way.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Cody|<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" />}}
[[File:Ghost_Company2.png|thumb|right|250px|The 212th Battalion's Ghost Company served on Ryloth during the Separatist occupation of the Twi'lek homeworld.]]
During the [[Battle of Ryloth]], the 212th's Ghost Company was sent to [[Nabat]] to secure the landing area. Upon their landing, Ghost Company moved into Nabat to take out a battery of five [[J-1 proton cannon]]s. Despite an attack by a pack of starving [[gutkurr]]s, Ghost Company was able to distract the [[battle droid]]s while General Kenobi destroyed the [[proton cannon]]s. After General [[Mace Windu]] and the rest of the Republic's forces landed,<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> Kenobi and the 212th were able to take the [[Jixuan desert]] and secure Ryloth's southern hemisphere.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

====Losses on Felucia and Sarrish====
{{Quote|Prepare the troops to evacuate.|Obi-Wan Kenobi, to Cody|<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref>}}
[[File:BattleOfFelucia-HH.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The 212th and the 501st were routed by Separatist forces in the First Battle of Felucia.]]
During the [[First Battle of Felucia]] in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="21 BBY">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' conclusion of the [[Battle of Ryloth]] and the [[second mission to Rodia]] to [[21 BBY]]. As the episode this event takes place within takes place between the events of the episodes the other events are attached to according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, this event must take place in 21 BBY.</ref> the 212th and the 501st were surrounded by battle [[droid]]s. They were able to hold out until General [[Plo Koon]] and the [[104th Battalion]] evacuated them from [[Felucia]].<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

The second year of the war<ref name="Helmet Collection 21">{{HelmetCollectionCite|21|Databank A-Z}}</ref> also saw the 212th and its Foxtrot Group serving on [[Sarrish]]. The [[Battle of Sarrish]] became one of the Republic's most devastating defeats, during which the 212th sustained heavy casualties. The battalion also lost the members of Foxtrot Group who were reported as missing in action. Captain Gregor managed to survive the battle, but suffered from [[amnesia]] while stranded on [[Abafar]].<ref name="Missing in Action" />

====The second invasion of Geonosis====
{{Quote|No, sir. I wasn't involved in the first assault on Geonosis.''"<br />"''Well, you didn't miss much. Last time, I was chained to a pole and attacked by several humongous monsters.''"<br />"''That sounds&hellip; entertaining.''"<br />"''It was&hellip; for the Geonosians.|Cody and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Point Rain" />}}
[[File:212thBattalionPilot-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|The 212th, along with other units, participated in the effort to retake Geonosis for the Galactic Republic.]]
The 212th was sent, along with the 501st, [[Jet's battalion]], the [[41st Elite Corps]], and General [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] to [[Second Battle of Geonosis|retake]] [[Geonosis]] from the Separatists. Kenobi and Cody's force of the 212th were the first to land on Geonosis, while the troops of Skywalker and Mundi were forced to crash-land before reaching their landing zone. The 212th were able to use their [[gunship]]s and AT-TEs to create a circle to protect them from the [[Geonosian]] forces until Skywalker's and Mundi's forces arrived.<ref name="Point Rain" />

[[File:CodyLoT.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The 212th fought a swarm of Geonosian zombie drones in the catacombs of the Prograte Temple.]]
After the destruction of the [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]], a [[platoon]] of the 212th accompanied Cody, Skywalker and Kenobi as they searched for General [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]] in the catacombs beneath the [[Progate Temple]]. They came under attack by Geonosian [[zombie]]s under [[Queen]] [[Karina the Great]]. Together, they were able to rescue Unduli, capture Poggle, and bury the queen alive during their escape.<ref name="Legacy" />

====The hunt for General Grievous====
{{Quote|Can you pinpoint the landing zone for the other pod?''"<br />"''I could put us within two to three klicks of it, sir.''"<br />"''Alert the men. We've picked up the scent.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Cody|<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref>}}
[[File:Grievous_Intrigue_212th_Firing.png|thumb|right|250px|The 212th assisted in the rescue of Jedi General Eeth Koth.]]
The 212th later assisted Kenobi aboard the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] ''[[Surrogator]]'' on the rescue of General [[Eeth Koth]], who was [[Capture of Eeth Koth|captured]] by the Separatists prior to the [[Battle of Saleucami]]. Kenobi created a plan to lure the general into his [[starship]] while Skywalker and General [[Adi Gallia]] rescued Koth. When they arrived near Grievous' [[fleet]], all remaining ships started firing with the exception of the ''Surrogator'', baiting Grievous into a trap. Cody and his troopers were able to take Grievous' [[commando]] droids, albeit with a few losses, while Kenobi engaged the general himself. However, Grievous managed to escape the fight and ordered the destruction of the ''Surrogator''. Cody and a few troopers engaged Grievous, attempting to keep him in place, but the ship's destruction forced them to let him escape to [[Saleucami]]'s surface.<ref name="Grievous Intrigue">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref>

[[File:Crys-Examiner.png|thumb|left|250px|The 212th pursued General Grievous to Saleucami, intent on capturing the Separatist general.]]
Kenobi, now commanding both the 212th and the 501st, landed on the planet and ordered his troops to head to the wreckage of the Separatist ship Grievous used to escape. The 212th inspected the crash site and realized the incident hadn't happened long ago. While the 212th would remain inspecting the area, a few 501st troopers, including Rex, went to patrol a nearby wetland and to warn the rest of the forces if they spotted Grievous. The 212th found a suitable droid for inspection on one of the ship's [[escape pod]]s and were able to pinpoint the locations of the other pods, one of which was found by nightfall, albeit deserted. The 501st troopers, with the exception of an injured Rex, were summoned to help engage the droids at the last escape pod. They were able to find Grievous and battle his forces. When a [[shuttle]] came to rescue the general, Kenobi ordered his AT-TEs to shoot it down, but they were unsuccessful in preventing Grievous' escape.<ref name="The Deserter" />

====The mission on Lola Sayu====
{{Quote|We're flying right into the heart of Separatist territory.|Cody|<ref name="The Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:MissiontotheCitadel-The_Citadel.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Members of the 212th infiltrated the Citadel in order to rescue Jedi General Even Piell and Captain Wilhuff Tarkin.]]
Several members of the 212th went with Cody, Kenobi, Skywalker and Rex to [[Lola Sayu]]. Their [[Battle of Lola Sayu|mission]] was to rescue General [[Even Piell]] and Captain [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] from the [[Citadel]]. To enter the planet, the entire rescue team was put under [[Carbon-freezing|carbon freezing]] to avoid the life scans of the facility. Once inside, the 501st trooper "[[Charger]]" accidentally alerted the enemy forces of the Republic presence through his [[death]]. On the way to find Piell, the 212th trooper "[[Longshot]]" was killed by an electric field. Upon finding the [[prisoner]]s, Piell joined Kenobi's group while Tarkin stayed with Skywalker's group.<ref name="The Citadel" />

They were successful in escaping the Citadel<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref> and the planet despite the death of General Piell.<ref name="CR">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref>

====Action on Umbara and Kiros====
{{Quote|Remember, Anakin, Cody and I will be 12 klicks to your south. We're counting on you to take out those local fighters or, I'm afraid, the capital will never surrender.''"<br />"''Does my battalion have to do everything?''"<br />"''You seem to always volunteer.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Darkness" />}}
[[File:TCW_Waxer_platoon_stops_firing.png|thumb|left|250px|During the Battle of Umbara, the 212th Battalion and the 501st Legion were tricked into fighting each other by the fallen Jedi General Pong Krell.]]
The 212th served in the [[Battle of Umbara]] along with the 501st, which was temporarily assigned to General [[Pong Krell]] when Skywalker was recalled to [[Coruscant]]. Its mission was capture the [[Umbaran capital city]] while the 501st dealt with the [[Umbaran Militia]].<ref name="Darkness" /> During the battle, Waxer's platoon was informed by General Krell that the [[Umbaran]]s were disguised as clones, resulting in an accidental skirmish between the 212th and the 501st until Rex stopped the fighting. After Waxer died, the remaining members of his platoon assisted the 501st in arresting [[Dogma]] and capturing Krell. Despite Krell's betrayal, the 212th were able to take the [[capital]].<ref name="Carnage of Krell" /> The 212th then [[Battle on Kiros|went]] to [[Kiros]], where they surrounded the [[governor's tower]] and received a message from the Separatist commander, [[Darts D'Nar]].<ref name="Kidnapped">{{TCW|Kidnapped}}</ref>

====The final days====
{{Quote|I'll keep them distracted until you get there. Just don't take too long.''"<br />"''Come on. When have I ever let you down?''"<br />"''Very well. The burden is on me not to destroy all the droids until you arrive.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Cody|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:BattleOfYerbana-OFNF.png|thumb|right|250px|The 212th continued to engage the Separatist Droid Army in the final days of the Clone Wars.]]
Near the end of the war, during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], the 212th were joined by the 501st in [[Battle of Yerbana|attacking]] a [[Yerbana city|Separatist stronghold]] on [[Yerbana]]. Each battalion attacked one side of a bridge. Neither group could find the [[Unidentified super tactical droid (Yerbana)|tactical droid]], and the 212th was suffering heavy casualties. General Kenobi saved Cody from an explosive, and he along with the surviving troops took cover. At that time, the 501st arrived, having finished their side of the battle. With a successful distraction, Anakin made the tactical droid show up and destroyed it, allowing both the 501st and 212th to advance towards the enemy troops, winning the battle.<ref name="Old Friends Not Forgotten">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>

[[File:Battle_of_Utapau.png|thumb|left|250px|The 212th fought in the Battle of Utapau, where General Kenobi sought to end the war by destroying General Grievous.]]
Later, the 212th took part in the [[Battle of Utapau]], in order to try to defeat General Grievous once and for all. General Kenobi went to Utapau in advance to confront Grievous and allow his battalion to surprise attack the enemy forces. The plan was successful, and Kenobi was able to kill Grievous. However, it was not long before the [[Great Jedi Purge]] began and Commander Cody received [[Order 66]] from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], who declared that every Jedi was guilty of treason. Cody obeyed the chancellor by ordering an AT-TE to fire upon their Jedi General, Obi-Wan Kenobi, though he survived falling a great distance, and evaded probes sent by the clones for confirmation of death.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Imperial Era===
====The occupation of Utapau====
{{Quote|Start loading your men on the ships. Move it.|Cody, to a subordinate|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:Occupation-of-Utapau.png|thumb|right|250px|The 212th seized control of Utapau, subjugating its population while the Republic was reformed into the Galactic Empire.]]
With [[High-Altitude Entry Transport-221]]s and gunships supporting Cody's clone troopers as they arrested the Pau'ans they suddenly deemed to be enemies,<ref name="Episode III" /> the inhabitants of Utapau experienced one of the most bitter reversals of the Clone Wars; the clone troopers turned against them in the wake of Order 66, their liberation from [[Invasion of Utapau|Separatist control]] replaced by another [[Occupation of Utapau|military occupation]] that began during the transitional period between the Galactic Republic and the Galactic Empire. After securing control over the planet, the 212th proceeded to arrest members of the [[Utapaun Security Forces|Utapaun resistance]] along with [[Port Administrator]] [[Tion Medon]].<ref name="SWE Tion Medon">{{SWE|characters|tionmedon|Tion Medon}}</ref>

====The subjugation of Kashyyyk====
{{Quote|The Imperial 212th Attack Battalion led by the brave General Kahdah has successfully put down a Wookiee revolt on the planet of Kashyyyk.|Alton Kastle|<ref name="HoloNet" />}}
[[File:Mantis_above_Kashyyyk.png|thumb|left|250px|The 212th was involved in the Empire's efforts to pacify and enslave the Wookiee homeworld of Kashyyyk.]]
The 212th remained active in the aftermath of the Clone Wars, serving as an Imperial battalion in the Stormtrooper [[Corps]] under General Kahdah.<ref name="HoloNet" /> Between [[19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Kashyyyk Date">{{TBBDate|203|[[Cody]]'s escape from the [[Galactic Empire]]}} The pacification of Kashyyyk takes place when [[Cody]] is still with the Galactic Empire, which ''Timelines'' dates the foundation of the Empire to 19 BBY. Therefore, the pacification of Kashyyyk must occured between 19 BBY and 18 BBY.</ref> Commander Cody and the 212th oversaw the [[pacification of Kashyyyk]] and the [[Slavery|enslavement]] of the planet's [[Wookiee]] population.<ref name="Helmet Collection 35">{{HelmetCollectionCite|35|Helmets}}</ref> Shortly after the subsequent occupation, Cody [[Desertion|deserted]] the Empire after questioning if the Empire was truly on the side of peace.<ref name="Solitary">{{TBB|The Solitary Clone}}</ref>

The battalion saw further action on [[Kashyyyk]] when the Wookiees attempted to overthrow the [[Imperial Military]]'s [[Occupation of Kashyyyk|occupation]] of their [[homeworld]]. Following the suppression of the [[Wookiee revolt]], the 212th's actions were reported in the [[HoloNet News]] by the [[journalist]] [[Alton Kastle]], who [[Propaganda|claimed]] that Kahdah bravely led his battalion to victory over the Wookiees of Kashyyyk.<ref name="HoloNet" />

===Legacy===
[[File:KenobiWanted-OWKpII.png|thumb|right|250px|Kenobi became a hunted fugitive after surviving the 212th Attack Battalion's unsuccessful execution of Order 66.]]
{{Quote|Did you find Kenobi?''"<br />"''Sir, no one could have survived that fall.|Cody and a clone paratrooper|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
Despite Commander Cody's efforts to find Kenobi after his attempt to kill him, the Jedi General was presumed dead by his clone troopers after they failed to locate him on Utapau. The fall of the [[Jedi Order]] and the Empire's rise to power forced the remaining Jedi into [[Exile (punishment)|exile]], including Kenobi<ref name="Episode III" /> who was [[Hunt for Obi-Wan|hunted]] by his fallen apprentice, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth]] Vader and a group of [[Jedi hunter]]s known as the [[Inquisitorius]].<ref name="Teaser Trailer">{{SWYouTube|TWTfhyvzTx0|Obi-Wan Kenobi &#124; Teaser Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/teaser-trailer-obi-wan-kenobi}}</ref>
