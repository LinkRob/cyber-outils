The '''Baktoid Armor Workshop''', also known simply as '''Baktoid Workshop''' was a design company based on [[Geonosis]] that was responsible for the production of a portion of the weapons, vehicles, and [[droid]]s used by the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], and later the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]].

==History==
Baktoid Armor Workshop was originally part of the [[Trade Federation]]'s portfolio. It was a subsidiary of [[Baktoid Industries]]<ref name="HC4" /> and [[Techno Union]].<ref name="CVD" /> Baktoid Armor was the sister company of [[Baktoid Combat Automata]].<ref name="Build R2 45">{{BuildR2Cite|45|Droid Directory}}</ref> Baktoid Armor manufactured weapons and droids as well as terrestrial vehicles and starships. Vehicles they built included the [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]], the [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tank]], the [[Multi-Troop Transport]], and the [[Separatist super-tank prototype|super tank]].<ref name="USW" />

[[File:Baktoid_Prototype_SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Baktoid technicians working on a prototype of the ''Hyena''-class Droid Bomber]]
Droids they designed included the [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid]] and [[OG-9 homing spider droid]] deployed alongside the [[battle droid]]s of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederacy]] forces. They also made the [[E-5 blaster rifle]]s carried by the Confederacy's [[B1-series battle droid]]s and [[BX-series droid commando]]s. The company also created the [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bomber]] based on the design of the [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droid starfighter]]. Both were used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]].<ref name="USW" /> ''[[Xanadu Blood]]'', the personal [[Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter|''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighter]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] was also manufactured by the Workshop.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> Baktoid also manufactured the [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighter]], which they licensed from the [[Haor Chall Engineering Corporation]] and then upgraded.<ref name="OSWM 121" />
