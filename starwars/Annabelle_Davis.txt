'''Annabelle Davis '''(born March 28, 1997, Peterborough, Cambridgeshire, England) is an English actress who worked as a creature and droid performer for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'' Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and portrayed [[Thromba]] in the film. Davis also appeared in the [[2017]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] as [[Dodibin]], one of the [[Suerton]] brothers known as the [[Lucky Three]].<ref name="10Aliens">{{SW|url=news/much-to-learn-you-still-have-10-aliens-you-should-know-from-star-wars-the-last-jedis-canto-bight|text=Much to Learn You Still Have: 10 Aliens You Should Know from ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi''{{'s}} Canto Bight}}</ref><ref name="Looper">{{WebCite|url=https://www.looper.com/1097259/where-youve-seen-the-cast-of-disney-s-willow-before|text=Where You've Seen The Cast Of Disney+'s Willow Before|author=Gibson, Nathan|date=2023-02-17|archivedate=20231207225340}}</ref> Annabelle also portrayed at least one character in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', helping her father, [[Warwick Davis]], who was cast as too many characters in some scenes of the film.<ref name="Solo"> {{YouTube|video=CpKMrrsH_RU|text=Warwick Davis & Annabelle Davis Panel &ndash; Wales Comic Con November 2023|channel=@geektown|name=Geektown|date=2023-11-19|quote=On Solo they cast me as so many different roles. In one scene they cast me as three characters in the same scene without realizing it. Annabell had to step in and don some of my suits and do the job for me. We tag-teamed.|archivedate=20240227150849}} </ref> As Dodibin, she performed alongside her father, who portrayed [[Wodibin]], brother to Annabelle's character.<ref name="TLJ">{{SW|url=news/much-to-learn-you-still-have-10-aliens-you-should-know-from-star-wars-the-last-jedis-canto-bight|text=Much to Learn You Still Have: 10 Aliens You Should Know from ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi''{{'s}} Canto Bight}}</ref>
