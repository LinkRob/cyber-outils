'''0 ABY''' was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Beginning immediately after the Battle of Yavin and followed by the year [[1 ABY]].<ref name="GA" />

==Events==
*Immediately following the [[Battle of Yavin]] the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] celebrate with a [[Royal Award Ceremony]] on the [[moon]] [[Yavin 4]]<ref name="ANH" /> before [[Evacuation of Yavin 4|evacuating]] the world.<ref name="PL1">[[Princess Leia 1|''Princess Leia'' 1]]</ref>
*[[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] embarks on a [[Mission to rescue the survivors of Alderaan|mission]] to rescue the survivors of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="PL1" />
*[[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] are sent to rescue [[Caluan Ematt]] from the [[planet]] [[Cyrkon]].<ref name="SR">''[[Smuggler's Run: A Han Solo & Chewbacca Adventure]]''</ref>
*[[Anandra Milon]] and her brother [[Santigo Milon]] find [[Unidentified fugitive camp|refuge]] from the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] on the planet [[Coruscant]].<ref name="OTLD">{{InsiderCite|151|One Thousand Levels Down}}</ref>
*Chewbacca aids [[Zarro]] in stopping [[Jaum]]'s [[mining]] operations on the planet [[Andelm IV]].<ref name="ChewieV">[[Chewbacca (2015) 5|''Chewbacca'' (2015) 5]]</ref>
*A scouting group explore the planet [[Fex]] for [[Kelen Biolabs]] but are almost all [[Death|killed]] by [[Fexian skullborer]]s.<ref name="HTTJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
*[[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] rescue [[Drusil Bephorin]] from Imperial custody and return her to her family.<ref name="HTTJ" />
*Luke Skywalker trains in the [[Temple of Eedit]] on the planet [[Devaron]] before [[Skirmish in the Temple of Eedit|dueling]] the [[scavenger]] [[Sarco Plank]].<ref name="WoaJ">''[[The Weapon of a Jedi: A Luke Skywalker Adventure]]''</ref>
*The Rebel Alliance commence an [[Assault on Cymoon]].<ref name="SW1">[[Star Wars (2015) 1|''Star Wars'' (2015) 1]]</ref>
*The Alliance [[Bombing of the Imdaar supply base|bomb]] an [[Imdaar supply base|Imperial supply base]] on [[Imdaar]].<ref name="SW4" />
*The Alliance [[Bombing of the Kuat shipyards|bomb]] the shipyards on the planet [[Kuat]].<ref name="SW4">[[Star Wars (2015) 4|''Star Wars'' (2015) 4]]</ref>
*After [[pirate]]s [[Skirmish over an unidentified green planet|destroy]] Imperial [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|shuttle]] [[CZ-246]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Attack on an unidentified pirate base|seeks out and kills them]] at their [[Unidentified pirate space station|base]].<ref name="DV2">[[Darth Vader (2015) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 2]]</ref>
*Vader recruits [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]], [[BT-1]] and [[0-0-0]],<ref name="DV3">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref> and together they travel to [[Geonosis]] to procure a [[droid]] army.<ref name="DV4">[[Darth Vader (2015) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 4]]</ref>
*[[Boba Fett]] [[Duel in Ben Kenobi's home|tracks down]] Luke Skywalker on [[Tatooine]], but Skywalker escapes with the help of [[R2-D2]].<ref name="SW5">[[Star Wars (2015) 5|''Star Wars'' (2015) 5]]</ref>
*Vader confronts [[Cylo]] on his research base.<ref name="DV6">[[Darth Vader (2015) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 6]]</ref>
*[[Sana Starros]] tracks down her "husband" Solo and his current target of affection, Organa.<ref name="SW6">[[Star Wars (2015) 6|''Star Wars'' (2015) 6]]</ref>
*Skywalker travels to the moon [[Nar Shaddaa]]<ref name="SW8">[[Star Wars (2015) 8|''Star Wars'' (2015) 8]]</ref> but is capture by [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]], eventually being [[Rescue of Luke Skywalker|freed]] by Solo, Organa, Chewbacca and Starros.<ref name="SW12">[[Star Wars (2015) 12|''Star Wars'' (2015) 12]]</ref>
*Vader uses Aphra and a group of [[bounty hunter]]s to [[Son-tuul Pride robbery|steal a small fortune]] from the Empire for his own uses.<ref name="DV8">[[Darth Vader (2015) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 8]]</ref>
*Vader [[Mission to the Anthan system|destroys evidence]] of his involvement in the theft while under the scrutiny of [[Thanoth]].<ref name="DV9">[[Darth Vader (2015) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 9]]</ref>
*Aphra, BT-1 and 0-0-0 are tasked by Vader with learning what they can about Luke Skywalker.<ref name="DV10">[[Darth Vader (2015) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 10]]</ref>
*An [[Arrth-Eno Mission|attempt]] by Rebel agent [[Eneb Ray]] to free imprisoned [[senator]]s and [[assassin]]ate [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] on Coruscant fails.<ref name="SWA">[[Star Wars Annual (2015) 1|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 1]]</ref>
*Vader [[Mission to Shu-Torun|travels]] to the planet [[Shu-Torun]] and kills all of the Royal family bar Princess [[Trios]].<ref name="DVA">[[Darth Vader Annual 1|''Darth Vader Annual'' 1]]</ref>
*The Alliance is [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|forced to retreat]] by the Empire from its base on [[Vrogas Vas]], but manages to take Aphra captive.<ref name="DV15">[[Darth Vader (2015) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 15]]</ref>
*Eneb Ray [[Prison siege at Sunspot|attempts to execute]] all of the prisoners in the Rebel [[Sunspot Prison]], but is stopped by Aphra, Organa and Starros.<ref name="SW19">[[Star Wars (2015) 19|''Star Wars'' (2015) 19]]</ref>
*Vader returns to Shu-Torun to put down a [[Shu-Torun War|civil war]] and reveals Cylo-V as a traitor to the Empire.<ref name="DVA" />
*Cylo [[Cylo Hijacking|temporarily captures]] the ''[[Executor]]'', but is defeated and killed by Vader.<ref name="DV25">[[Darth Vader (2015) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 25]]</ref>
*Imperial [[Task Force 99]] [[Skirmish on the Ghost Moon|capture]] Alliance [[Admiral]] Verette on the [[Ghost Moon]].<ref name="SW21">[[Star Wars (2015) 21|''Star Wars'' (2015) 21]]</ref>
*The Rebel Alliance manage to [[Hijacking of the Harbinger|capture]] the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Harbinger (Imperial I-class)|''Harbinger'']].<ref name="SW23">[[Star Wars (2015) 23|''Star Wars'' (2015) 23]]</ref>
*[[Thane Kyrell]] deserts from the Empire and ends up working as a pilot and navigator onboard the ''[[Mighty Oak Apocalypse]]'' under the command of the Wookiee [[Lohgarra]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
