A '''bomb''' was an explosive device designed to explode under specified conditions.<ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> The [[blazer bomb]],<ref name="hyena bombers">{{SWE|technology|hyenabombers|hyena bombers}}</ref> [[electro-proton bomb]],<ref name="TZB">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref> [[Electro-Proton Bomb/Defoliator]],<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> [[gas bomb]],<ref name="TZBSB">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast Strikes Back}}</ref> [[molecular purge bomb]],<ref name="DV 7">[[Darth Vader (2015) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 7]]</ref> [[plasma bomb]],<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[proximity bomb]],<ref name="Battlefront" /> [[viral bomb]],<ref name="BSV guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/blue-shadow-virus-episode-guide|text="Blue Shadow Virus" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> [[VL-61/79 proton bomb]], and [[ion bomb]]<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref> were bomb types that saw use in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Bomber]]s were [[starship]]s designed to strike heavily [[armor]]ed targets and armed with projectile weapons such as [[proton torpedo]]es,<ref name="Battlefront" /> [[proton bomb]]s,<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> and [[concussion missile]]s.<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> [[Kyber crystal]]s were normally used to power [[lightsaber]]s, but could also be used to power other weapons. People had tried to make kyber bombs and kyber [[blaster]]s, and there were a few weapons of that kind in the [[Jedi Archives]].<ref name="Lesson">{{StoryCite|story=The Lesson (Obi-Wan & Anakin)|book=Star Wars (2020) 25|sformatted=The Lesson (Obi-Wan & Anakin)}}</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[infiltrator demolition droid]]s of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] have explosive charges that allowed them to [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct]] in [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|blowing up]] the [[central power distribution grid]] on [[Coruscant]]. The action of these [[demolition droid]]s caused a [[Coruscant mandatory blackouts|mandatory blackout]], thereby stopping the vote for the [[Republic financial reform bill|bill]] to deregulate the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>
