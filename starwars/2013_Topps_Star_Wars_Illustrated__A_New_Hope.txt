'''2013 Topps ''Star Wars'' Illustrated: ''A New Hope''''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[November 6]], [[2013]].<ref name="SWI145">[[Star Wars Insider 145|''Star Wars Insider'' 145]]</ref> The 100-card set depicts scenes not seen in the [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|movie]], such as those heard in the [[Star Wars (radio)|original ''Star Wars'' radio drama]].<ref name="Preview">{{Blogspot|subdomain=packwar|url=2013/09/preview-star-wars-illustrated-new-hope.html|name=Pack War|text=Preview: Star Wars Illustrated: A New Hope|date=2013-09-16}}</ref>

The card set has three base card parallels: Black parallels, Bronze parallels, and Gold parallels, which amount to 1:12 packs, 1:24 packs, and #/10. The Gold parallels are strictly for hobby usage only.<ref name="Preview" /> Besides the parallels, there are also several different sets within each pack, including:

*''One Year Earlier'' (1:2 Packs), detailing events that appeared a year prior to the events of ''A New Hope'' ([[1 BBY/Legends|1 BBY]]). Also included a 18-card checklist;<ref name="Preview" />

*''Movie Poster Artist Reinterpretations'' (1:3), a set of nine cards each depicting an artist's interpretation of the ''A New Hope'' movie poster;<ref name="Preview" />

*''Etched Foil Radio Drama Puzzle'' (1:8), six foil cards that recreate a scene from the radio drama;<ref name="Preview" />

*''The Mission: Destroy the Death Star'' (1:12), a set of twelve cards, plus a checklist, detailing the specific steps to the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]]. Also included a bonus [[Death Star/Legends|Death Star]] card issued to any player who registers all twelve cards.<ref name="Preview" /> These cards also had codes on the back that could be redeemed online to listen to a clip from the radio drama that corresponded with that particular card. When collectors entered the twelve codes on "Topps Star Wars" Facebook page, they were rewarded with an exclusive card depicting an exploding Death Star.<ref name="SWI145" />

In addition, the main box also includes additional features, such as a panorama sketch card, 35mm film cells taken from a print of the original film, called "Film Cell Relics," printing plates for a base card, and autographed cards from various voice actors who contributed to the radio drama.<ref name="Preview" />

==Release and reception==
To coincide with the release of this set, [[HighBridge Audio]] re-released the [[Star Wars (radio)|original ''Star Wars'' radio drama]] in two limited-edition CDs: ''Star Wars: A New Hope &ndash; The Original Radio Drama'', ''"Light Side"'' and ''"Dark Side."'' Each volume comes with an exclusive Topps card drawn either by [[Matt Busch]] (''"Light Side"'' edition), or [[Randy Martinez]] (''"Dark Side"'' edition).<ref>{{Blogspot|subdomain=packwar|url=2013/09/a-limited-edition-topps-star-wars.html|name=Pack War|text=A Limited Edition Topps Star Wars Illustrated Card|date=2013-09-24}}</ref>

Two sets of two exclusive promo cards were also offered with [[Star Wars Insider 145|''Star Wars Insider'' 145]]. These cards are "[http://images.wikia.com/starwars/images/1/1b/SearchingMosEisley-SWI145.jpg Searching Mos Eisley]," "[http://images.wikia.com/starwars/images/3/3a/ImperialsLastMoments-SWI145.jpg The Rebellion's Imminent Victory]," "Creating the Radio Play," and "The Cast in Action."
