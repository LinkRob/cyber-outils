'''Banshees'''<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-general-episode-gallery|text="The General" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 13}}</ref> were a [[Sentience|non-sentient]] [[species]] of airborne [[Diet|predators]] native to the [[planet]] [[Umbara]]. During the [[Battle of Umbara]] in the [[Clone Wars]], wild banshees attacked the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s on several occasions. [[Jedi General]] [[Pong Krell]] killed a pair of banshees after they attacked the [[501st Legion]].<ref name="DOU" />
