'''Brox Anthar''' was a [[Color|light]]-skinned [[Snivvian]] [[Gender|male]] criminal who lived during the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> he was wanted for multiple accounts of grand larceny with the [[SoroSuub Corporation]]. As a result, SoroSuub reached out to the [[Benelex Marshal Service]], which sent [[Marshal]] [[Buck Vancto]] to apprehend the Snivvian fugitive. The marshal tracked Anthar to the<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />

After Vancto gained information on his target from a [[Unidentified Pa'lowick bartender|Pa'lowick bartender]], the marshal caught Anthar running down a street, making pursuit from atop the nearby buildings. Anthar's pursuer caught him with an electrified [[bola]], bringing the Snivvian to the ground. As Vancto then jumped down and informed Anthar of his charges, the marshal was contacted by a [[Unidentified Twi'lek (Buck Vancto)|Twi'lek]] who informed Vancto they had another job for him. Anthar wore a yellow and black top with brown trousers and a black [[belt]], [[glove]]s, and [[boot]]s.<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />
