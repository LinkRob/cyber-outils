The '''Altar of Mortis''' was an altar located in a cave on the ethereal [[planet]] [[Mortis]] in [[Wild Space]]. It held the [[Dagger of Mortis]]. It was located in a deep cave at the bottom of a staircase, and it was surrounded by a green aura of fire, which one had to cross in order to retrieve the mystical weapon. It was designed so that certain members of the family of [[Force wielder]]s resident on the planet, called the "Overlords" or "[[The Ones]]," could not retrieve the dagger.<ref name="Altar" />

After luring the [[Chosen One]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], to Mortis,<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref> the [[Force wielder]] known as the [[Father (Mortis)|Father]] was betrayed by one of his children, [[Son|the Son]]. In an effort to stop the Son, the [[Daughter]] took [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] to the Altar and informed him that, while she could go no further, he could retrieve the Dagger in order to stop the Son.<ref name="Altar" />

After the weapon materialized before the [[Jedi Master]], Kenobi took it to Skywalker, but the dagger was seized by his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], who was under the Son's influence. The Son then gained control of the dagger and accidentally [[Death|killed]] the Daughter,<ref name="Altar" /> before the Father turned the dagger against himself, rendering the Son vulnerable enough for Skywalker to slay him. With this, balance in [[the Force]] was restored to Mortis, but a blinding [[Color|white]] light from the Father's [[Mortis monastery|monastery]] made the entire realm disappear.<ref name="Ghosts" />
