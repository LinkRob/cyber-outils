'''Cham Detta''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Mandalorian/Legends|Mandalorian]], the brother of [[Suvar Detta]], and a member of the [[Mandalorian Protectors (Spar)|Mandalorian Protectors]]. Along with his brother, he was trained by [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] as the new generation of [[Mandalorian Supercommando]]s. During the time of the [[Second Galactic Civil War]], Cham was the [[Bartender/Legends|bartender]] of the ''[[Oyu'baat]]'' [[Tapcafe/Legends|tapcaf]] in [[Keldabe/Legends|Keldabe]]. He was present when [[Jaina Solo Fel|Jaina Solo]] confronted [[Bardan Jusik|Gotab]] about his past as a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] in the tapcaf and later he served drinks at the celebration of [[Mirta Gev]]'s [[Marriage/Legends|marriage]] to [[Ghes Orade]].
