'''Bendoh''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] that [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Luke Skywalker]] came across when leading [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] drones away from a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Fuel|refueling]] station.<ref name="Amazon" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bendoh was originally mentioned in the listing on Amazon.com for the [[Star Wars Vol. 12: Rebels and Rogues|twelfth volume]] of the [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]].<ref name="Amazon" /> Bendoh was not included in the volume itself, with a similar role being filled by the character [[Warba Calip]].<ref name="Star Wars 69">[[Star Wars (2015) 69|''Star Wars'' (2015) 69]]</ref>
