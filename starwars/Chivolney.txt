{{Quote|Maybe he tosses away a few mid-level bureaucrats, maybe Isard and Chivolney resign in disgrace. Maybe it all ends there, the Jedi are exonerated but remain quite dead, and everyone is happy&hellip;except you, I think?|Haki Zeophrine, to Bail Organa|<ref name="MOF" />}}
'''Chivolney''' was a [[bureaucrat]] in service of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="MOF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> shortly after the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and the ensuing [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the Empire, Chivolney was considered a mid-level bureaucrat by [[Imperial Intelligence]] operative [[Haki Zeophrine]]. When Zeophrine's temporary ally [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] reiterated his desire to prove the innocence of the [[Jedi Order]] to [[the galaxy]] after they were [[Great Jedi Purge|purged]] by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], Zeophrine told Organa that the most change he could hope for was the [[Emperor]] blaming bureaucrats like [[Armand Isard]] or Chivolney and forcing them to resign.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chivolney was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
