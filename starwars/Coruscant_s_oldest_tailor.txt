'''Coruscant's oldest tailor''' was a store on [[Republic Avenue]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. The [[tailor]]'s was built next to an alley that separated it from a luxury [[airspeeder]] showroom.<ref name="ACS" /> Around [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Reveal">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]" to around [[232 BBY]].</ref> the [[thief]] [[Nittel Karkson]] fled down the alley past the tailor while being pursued by the [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]].<ref name="ACS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Coruscant's oldest tailor appeared in "[[A Coruscant Solstice]]," a short story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in the [[2021]] ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'' anthology.<ref name="ACS" />
