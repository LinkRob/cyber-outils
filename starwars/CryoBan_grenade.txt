'''CryoBan grenades''' were a type of [[grenade]] that used [[CryoBan]] chemicals to absorb most of the heat within a given area. The [[WW-41 CryoBan grenade]] was a model of CryoBan grenade created by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] for [[fire]] supression of [[Starship|shipboard]] fires, although several [[bounty hunter]]s and [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] added it to their inventory as it could encase a targeted being in [[Water|icy]] frost.<ref name="FO" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], CryoBan grenades were mentioned in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] supplement [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="FO" /> The [[CryoBan grenade/Legends|grenades]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[2003]] [[BioWare]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>

In ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'', [[Han Solo]] is able to throw a CryoBan grenade as Finisher move in ''Star Wars'' Playsets, while in Toybox areas Han throws a [[Carbon-freeze bomb]].
