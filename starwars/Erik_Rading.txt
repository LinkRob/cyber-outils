'''Erik Rading''' is a 3D artist who worked on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] DLC for the video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]'' developed by [[Electronic Arts|EA]] [[DICE]]. He was responsible for the designs of [[CJ-9 electrified bo-rifle|CJ-9 bo-rifle]], [[TL-50 Heavy Repeater]], [[K-16 Bryar Pistol]], and [[laser trip mine]].<ref name="Rading" />
