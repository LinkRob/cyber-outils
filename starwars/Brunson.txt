'''Brunson''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]]. She commanded an [[Brunson's Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]] dispatched to [[Geonosis]], the former site of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star's]] [[Project Stardust|construction]], in [[2 BBY]], in response to a triggered proximity sensor. There, she discovered the [[Spectres|rebel]] [[starship]] known as the ''[[Ghost]]'' on the [[planet]]. Hoping to earn herself and her crew promotions, she attempted to destroy the rebels using [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]], [[Jumptrooper|rocket troopers]], and finally by trapping the rebels in an underground [[cave]]rn in an attempt to bury them alive. When she used her [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|cruiser]] to block their escape, the ''Ghost'' [[fire]]d two [[proton torpedo]]es, resulting in the destruction of her ship.

==Biography==
===Geonosis===
[[File:Brunson.png|thumb|left|240px|Captain Brunson was in command of an ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser.]]
Brunson was a young [[captain]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] who commanded an [[Brunson's Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]].<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" /> About [[2 BBY|two years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis date">{{InsiderCite|204|A Certain Point of View}} dates the events of "Ghosts of Geonosis" to two [[Standard year|years]] before [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which corresponds to [[2 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> she responded to a proximity alert on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]] which had been triggered by a visit by the rebel cell [[Spectres]], who were on a [[Mission to investigate Geonosis|mission]] to find [[Saw Gerrera]] and investigate the [[Sterilization of Geonosis|disappearance]] of the [[Geonosian]] [[species]]. After one of her crew informed her that they had picked up live-readings on Geonosis, Captain Brunson deployed two [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]] to [[scout]] for intruders.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" />

The [[TIE series|TIE]] [[bomber]]s soon discovered the rebel [[Starship|ship]] ''[[Ghost]]'' and strafed it. After the bomber crew transmitted a [[Hologram|holographic]] image of the ''Ghost'', Brunson recognized the ship as belonging to the [[Phoenix Cell]], which was part of the [[Galactic Civil War|rebellion]]. The ''Ghost'' managed to destroy the bombers. In response, Captain Brunson ordered her crew to pinpoint the rebel ship's last known location. She believed that they had fled into the depths of Geonosis. Brunson was proven right when they discovered that the ''Ghost'' had descended into a deep air shaft to rendezvous with their comrades, Saw, and the Geonosian [[Klik-Klak]].<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" />

Brunson's [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|light cruiser]] managed to trap the ''Ghost'' inside the air shaft. She then dispatched a squad of [[Jumptrooper|rocket troopers]] to board the rebel ship. However, the rebels managed to repel the boarders and destroy the entire [[squad]]. In response, Brunson ordered her crew to open [[fire]] on the rebel ship and to collapse the air shaft on top of them. Despite her efforts, the Spectres outwitted Brunson by using their [[laser cannon]]s and [[proton torpedo]]es to blast through the Imperial [[light cruiser]], setting it ablaze before fleeing into space.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" /> Captain Brunson survived the explosion but her light [[cruiser]] was destroyed.<ref name="Databank" />

[[File:BrunsonCruiser-GhostofGeonosis.png|thumb|right|250px|Brunson's light cruiser destroyed.]]
As a result, the Spectres and Saw escaped offworld with imagery evidence of the [[poison]] [[Imperial gas canister|canisters]] which the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] had used to exterminate the Geonosians. In addition, they obtained a [[deflector core]] and helped Klik-Klak to escape with the [[Karina|last Geonosian queen egg]] into the deepest levels of Geonosis.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" />

===''Chimaera''===
Brunson was later seen being escorted away from [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] [[Thrawn's Office|office]] on board his flagship, the ''[[Chimaera]]'', by a pair of [[stormtrooper]]s, along with [[Commander]] [[Brom Titus]] and Captain [[Slavin]]. The three had been interviewed by the Grand Admiral as part of his investigation into the identity of a rebel spy in the local Imperial ranks. The group was passed by [[Lieutenant]] [[Yogar Lyste]] and [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]]. The former referred to the trio as officers of the [[Imperial Sector Command|Sector Command]].<ref name="TIE" /> Her fate following this is unknown.

==Personality and traits==
Brunson earned the high [[Military rank|rank]] of captain at a young age,<ref name="Databank" /> proving herself to be a gifted but ultimately inexperienced commanding [[officer]]. She was an ambitious climber, regarding her duties on Geonosis with disdain and eager to destroy the ''Ghost'' in hopes of being promoted. This proved to be a naive decision on her part as she didn't fully appreciate the ''Ghost''{{'s}} feared reputation. Her attempt to trap the ''Ghost'' using her ship to block their escape resulted in her own ship being split in two by a pair of proton torpedoes. The ''Ghost''{{'s}} [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]] remarked upon the captain's inexperience.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" />

Unlike other Imperial officers, she did show some degree of camaraderie towards her subordinates, expressing her desire to see them all promoted. She could also be officious and petty towards them, as demonstrated when she responded to an officer's report that they had lost their rocket troopers by slapping him on the back of his head.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" />
