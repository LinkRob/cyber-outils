"'''Convor'''" is the fifteenth episode of the webseries ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]''. The episode was released on [[StarWarsKids.com]] on [[January 24]], [[2023]], alongside the series' sixteenth episode, "[[Gergilla (episode)|Gergilla]]."<ref name="News" />

==Official description==
''Aree has the perfect plan to draw out the elusive convor - dress up Cam as the creature!''

==Plot summary==
Aree and CAM travel to [[Atollon]] to search for an elusive [[convor]]. Aree dresses CAM in a convor costume. This attracts a [[Gender|male]] convor which dances with CAM. Amidst the excitement, Aree causes the coral formation he is standing on to collapse. Aree sinks into the surrounding quick sand and calls for help.

CAM comes to his rescue and tries to drag him out but Aree is too heavy. The two are plucked to safety by three convors. They are joined by three more convors which befriend the droids. Aree remarks that there is no better way to befriend a convor than to be yourself.
