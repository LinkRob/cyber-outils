'''Chromants''' were a type of [[ant]] that lived on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Elphrona]], residing in nests that could contain up to a hundred individuals. During the [[High Republic Era]], residents of the planet typically called in [[extermination unit]]s when they were having trouble with the creatures.<ref name="LOTJ" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> a [[Unidentified claim|claim]] on Elphrona experienced difficulties with a nest of chromants. [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]]&mdash;who was stationed at the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Elphrona Outpost]] south of the claim&mdash;intended to help them with the issue after finishing his meal and suggested that he could defeat one hundred of them. When [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indeera Stokes]] questioned whether an extermination unit could assist, he indicated he wished to fight the chromants. Greatstorm's [[Padawan]], [[Bell Zettifar]], wanted to help as well but had other [[Controlled descent|training]] to do. However, the [[Jedi]] ultimately did not help the claim with their chromant issue because they dispatched on a [[Raid on Elphrona|rescue endeavor]] elsewhere on Elphrona instead.<ref name="LOTJ" />
