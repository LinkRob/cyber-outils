An '''analyst''' was an occupation. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Chellwinark Frethylrin]] worked as an analyst for the [[Galactic Empire]]&mdash;which she characterized as a "people-watcher"&mdash;until she [[Desertion|deserted]] from the Empire.<ref name="Battle Scars">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref> Before she also deserted from the Empire, [[Kidi Aleri]] served as a [[signals analyst]].<ref name="Moving Target">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> [[Ria Clarr]] was an analyst who worked for the [[Imperial Mining Institute]].<ref name="Rebel Bluff">{{InsiderCite|158|Rebel Bluff}}</ref> Analyst was one of multiple bureaucratic occupations held by citizens of [[At Attin]]; the [[Gender|female]] [[Myykian]] [[Nooma]] worked as an analyst, and her son [[Neel]] aspired to be one like her when he grew up.<ref name="Can't Say I Remember No At Attin">{{SkeletonCrew|Can't Say I Remember No At Attin}}</ref>
