'''Enraged Ewok''' was an [[Ewok]] of the [[Bright Tree tribe]] on the forest [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. She was worried about her [[Endangered Ewok|husband]], who had taken the key to her treasure chest as a lucky charm and got stuck after crossing a broken bridge. When he returned and revealed that he had given the key to his savior, she chased him across the [[Bright Tree Village|village]] in rage.<ref name="LEGO" />

==Behind the scenes==
Enraged Ewok first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the puzzle "Ewok Lock".<ref name="LEGO" />
