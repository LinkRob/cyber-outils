The '''Cronian battlebird''' was a [[warship]] model that was deemed ancient by the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> a restored battlebird joined the [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] assembled in response to [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] call to action against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]], and then partook in the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="TROS Junior Novel" />
