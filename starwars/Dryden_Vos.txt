'''Dryden Vos''' was a [[near-human]] [[Dryden Vos' species|alien]] [[Gender|male]] who served as the public face of the crime syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. He owned the ''[[First Light]]'', and possessed a large quantity of wealth as Crimson Dawn's figurehead leader. He recruited the [[Corellia]]n [[Qi'ra]] to be his [[Lieutenant (criminal)|Lieutenant]], and hired [[Beckett's gang]] to steal [[coaxium]] for him. When he tried to kill [[Han Solo]], he was betrayed by Qi'ra, and the two engaged in a quick duel which resulted in Dryden Vos being stabbed and killed.

==Biography==
====Clone Wars====
{{Quote|Maul just told the syndicate leaders to go into hiding.|[[Rook Kast]], on Maul's instructions to Vos and the other syndicate heads|<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" />}}
Subordinate to [[Boss]] [[Rintero]],<ref name="MSL C9">{{MSL|Chapter 9: Strange Allies}}</ref> Dryden Vos was a member of the crime syndicate [[Crimson Dawn]], one of the most ruthless criminal syndicates in [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Solo" /> during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" /> and the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. However, he secretly answered to the [[Zabrak]] [[crime lord]] known as [[Maul]],<ref name="Solo" /> leader of the [[Shadow Collective]] alliance, which Crimson Dawn had joined.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" /> During the Clone Wars, he was a rising star in the criminal underworld of the galaxy, becoming a key leader for Crimson Dawn and a [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] in the Shadow Collective.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

Into the Imperial Era,<ref name="MSL C9" /> Vos replaced Rintero as leader of Crimson Dawn<ref name="Climb">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref> and worked as its public face, but he remained subordinate to Maul, the true leader of Crimson Dawn. To communicate with Maul, Vos used a [[Dryden Vos' signet ring|ring]] to activate the [[hologram]] panel.<ref name="Solo" /> Vos owned a [[Kalevala]]n [[yacht]] known as the ''[[First Light]]'', where he had a study filled with several relics, including a slab of an [[obsidian]] stone he plundered from a [[Unidentified Sith temple|Sith temple]].<ref name="TOG" />

[[File:SyndicateLeadersGoHide-TPA.png|thumb|right|250px|Maul orders his syndicate's leaders into hiding.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Clone Wars date">{{TCWcanondates|710}}</ref> during the final [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, Vos was contacted by Maul via hologram, alongside [[Marg Krim]] of the [[Pyke Syndicate]] and [[Ziton Moj]] of the [[Black Sun]]. All leaders in the Shadow Collective, they were instructed by their master to go into hiding, as Maul had foreseen the [[Mission to Mustafar|imminent end]] of the war, and the [[Great Jedi Purge|chaos]] that would follow, leading to the [[Proclamation of the New Order|creation]] of the Galactic Empire.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" />

===Imperial Era===
Sometime between [[17 BBY]] and c. [[13 BBY]], on the planet [[Janix]], Maul noted to [[Rook Kast]] that Crimson Dawn, alongside the Pyke Syndicate and Black Sun, had betrayed him following the [[Siege of Mandalore]], and vowed to take revenge against them.<ref name="MSL Ch1">{{MSL|Chapter 1: The Dark Revenge}}</ref> Later, following the [[Assassination of Marg Krim]], Dryden Vos, still a representative of Crimson Dawn, contacted [[Looti Vario]], through an encoded [[comlink]] channel, and requested an audience with the former [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="MSL Ch8">{{MSL|Chapter 8: The Creeping Fear}}</ref>

[[File:DrydenVosContactsMaul-MSL-Chapter9.png|left|thumb|225px|Dryden Vos contacts Maul to propose his rescue from Janix]]
During his meeting with Maul via [[hologram]], Vos revealed he knew of Maul's situation and that he was operating behind Rintero's back, offered safe passage off of Janix in exchange for Maul killing Rintero and installing Vos as the new leader of Crimson Dawn. Although Maul had questioned Crimson Dawn's sudden offer of sanctuary, he was impressed by Vos' deviousness, accepted the offer, and promised to be at the rendezvous point, which Vos provided the encoded coordinates, for the extraction window.<ref name="MSL Ch9">{{MSL|Chapter 9: Strange Allies}}</ref> En route to Janix, Vos again contacted Vario, and though the [[Aleena]] tried to warn him that they had encountered [[Anakin Skywalker|complications]], which Vos angry remind him to have no complications before a pilot told him they need cut communications to avoid detection as he approached. Although [[Unidentified Crimson Dawn pilot|a Crimson Dawn pilot]] warned of the risk, Vos remained determined that, with Maul's help, he would reap the rewards of taking over the syndicate. [[Dryden Vos's shuttle]] shot down a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i]] [[gunship]] with Vos and a pilot came out near the [[entry ramp]] covering fire at the [[TK stormtrooper]]s. After Vario arrived, Vos asked where is Maul, which Vario replied he will be here and headed into the shuttle as Vos continuing to fight off the [[stormtrooper]]s. Despite the heavy fire, Maul arrived after [[2B0T|Two-Boots]], [[Rylee Lawson]], and [[Devon Izara]] came into the [[shuttle]]. After jumping into [[hyperspace]], Vos told him he'd upheld his end of the bargain, and Maul replied promising to do the same.<ref name="MSL Ch10">{{MSL|Chapter 10: The Dark Lord}}</ref>

[[File:FirstLight-SWOR.png|thumb|right|225px|Dryden Vos's yacht, the ''First Light'']]
During his tenure as crime lord, sometime after [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Solo Prologue">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the prologue events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref> Dryden Vos bought the [[Corellian]] [[Qi'ra]] from [[Sarkin Enneb]], who in turn bought her from [[Lady]] [[Proxima]], the ruthless [[Grindalid]] crime boss of the [[White Worms]] gang, who sold Qi'ra after her attempted escape from [[Corellia]].<ref name="Solo Expanded Edition">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> As such, Vos demanded in return her lifelong loyalty to Crimson Dawn, branding her forearm and her neck with his organization's symbol. Qi'ra became his top lieutenant. He even trained her in [[Unarmed combat|martial arts]], including [[Teräs Käsi]].<ref name="Solo" /> After Qi'ra became skilled in such arts,<ref name="Solo 3">[[Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation 3|''Solo: A Star Wars Story Adaptation'' 3]]</ref> she killed Enneb.<ref name="Solo Expanded Edition" />

On one occasion too, Vos had [[Razzi warehouse raid|raided]] a warehouse of the [[Razzi Syndicate]] and seized low-grade [[andris]] [[spice]], leading him to consider "squeezing" the [[Hutt]] [[Zorba]] to find what he knew about the spice's provenance.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>

===Coaxium heist===
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Death date" /> the scoundrel [[Tobias Beckett]] became indebted to Vos, promising him a good amount of [[coaxium]] as payment.<ref name="Solo" /> Prior to his indebtedness, Beckett killed the [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]] under Vos' orders, something which pleased Vos enough to give Beckett a bonus.<ref name="Solo Expanded Edition" /> Beckett and [[Beckett's gang|his crew]] attempted to [[Heist on Vandor|steal]] a container full of coaxium that belonged to the Empire from a [[ICC-5537|conveyex transport]]. Unfortunately for Beckett, the heist ultimately failed due to the intervention of the [[pirate]] [[Enfys Nest]] and her [[swoop]] gang, the [[Cloud-Riders]], costing him the cargo and two of his teammates, his lover [[Val]] and the [[Ardennian]] pilot [[Rio Durant]]. However, his recruit, the young [[Han Solo]], insisted that he meet Vos to talk with him about what had happened.<ref name="Solo" />

[[File:SoloBeckettAndVos-Solo.jpg|thumb|left|260px|Vos speaks with Beckett and Solo.]]
Upon arriving at Vos' yacht, as Han reunited with Qi'ra, Vos received Beckett and expressed his condolences for the loss of Val, revealing that he knew what happened with the conveyex heist. In his office, Vos told Beckett that he needed to face his wrath for having failed him once more, but Solo attempted to convince Vos to give Beckett another opportunity by sending them to the [[Spice mines of Kessel|spice mines]] of [[Kessel]] to steal the coaxium stored there. At first, Vos rejected the plan because he didn't want to anger the [[Pyke]]s, and risk an all out war with the syndicates due to their uneasy alliance but Qi'ra ultimately convinced him that it was a good idea.<ref name="Solo" />

In order to attack the spice mines of Kessel, Beckett's crew enlisted the help of the [[smuggler]] and [[sabacc]] player [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and his [[droid]] [[L3-37]], who brought them to Kessel aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. There, Beckett's crew [[Raid on Kessel|infiltrated]] Kessel's spice mines, took down [[Quay Tolsite]] and his [[Pyke sentinel|guards]] and managed to steal the coaxium, at the cost of L3-37's destruction. After escaping from the Empire's clutches once more in the [[Maw Cluster]],<ref name="Solo" /> making the [[Kessel Run]] in just twelve [[parsec]]s,<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> the crew arrived at the planet [[Savareen]] to refine the coaxium. After Lando departed with the ''Falcon'' and the crew encountered Nest, learning of her intentions to help the rebellion with the coaxium, Solo decided to deceive Vos and give the coaxium to the Cloud-Riders. He and Qi'ra took the coaxium to Vos, telling him that Beckett did not survive the mission.<ref name="Solo" />

===Final plot===
[[File:DrydenVos-2024Base.png|thumb|right|210px|Vos engaged Solo and Qi'ra with his petars.]]
To their surprise, Vos revealed that he knew the coaxium aboard his yacht was fake, and that he was warned by his "inside man" about Han's betrayal. Though Han initially believed that Qi'ra betrayed them, Vos revealed that his "inside man" was in fact Beckett, who decided to betray his crew so that Vos spared his life. However, as they anticipated Beckett's betrayal, Solo had presented the true shipment of coaxium to Vos, and had only told Beckett it was fake. Nevertheless, Beckett resolved to take Chewbacca hostage and escape with the real coaxium, [[Showdown on Savareen|leaving]] Vos to face Qi'ra and Han.<ref name="Solo" />

The encounter led to a fight in which Vos accidentally destroyed several artifacts in his study, including the [[masthead of Xim the Despot]].<ref name="Solo" /> In the midst of Solo and Vos' attempts to kill each other, Qi'ra came to Han's defense by attacking Vos with a [[vibrocutlass]] retrieved from the corpse of [[Toht Ra]], one of the [[Hylobon Enforcers]].<ref name="Altaya">{{EncyclopediaCite|Melee and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> In the fierce scuffle between the two, Vos cut the blade of his opponent's [[sword]] in half, which resulted in [[Dryden Vos's petars|his own weapon]] getting stuck. Unarmed and defenseless, Vos was finally impaled by Qi'ra and dropped to the floor, [[Death|dead]]. After promising Han that she would take valuables from the yacht and meet him outside, Qi'ra instead sealed the room after Han's departure and used Vos' ring to falsely tell Maul that Tobias Beckett had been responsible for Vos' death, and was ordered to take the yacht to [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Test me! Test me one more time and you'll see what happens.|Dryden Vos|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:Dryden_Vos_victim.png|thumb|left|250px|Vos stands over the corpse of [[Governor]] [[Diles Anevi]].]]
A [[near-human]] [[Gender|male]] crime lord, Dryden Vos had [[Color|Blond]] hair, blue [[eye]]s, light skin<ref name="Solo" /> and was 1.92 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]], 4 inches) tall.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Vos was a pitiless enforcer and known as a [[gangster]] of taste and wealth. Vos socialized with the galaxy's elite aboard his yacht the ''First Light'', surrounding himself with opulence. In addition, Vos enjoyed showing off his superb collection of antiquities. His wealth and status was also displayed in the food he enjoyed, including delicacies such as [[colo claw fish]] eggs. Being near-human, Dryden had a number of alien attributes such as a claw that extended from his thumb and many intricate striations ran along his face that were connected to the activity in his circulatory system and adrenaline levels.<ref name="TOG" /> In moments of stress and rage, Dryden's scar-like marks would become more pronounced and bright and his eyes would appear bloodshot.<ref name="Solo" />

Though Vos was known for being ruthless, albeit not a cutthroat,<ref name="Databank" /> he displayed good manners with his guests, as he showed when he met Han Solo.<ref name="Solo" /> Nevertheless, his manners weren't to be mistaken for weakness, as he could change from a generous host to merciless killer in a moment.<ref name="Databank" /> Beyond that, however, Vos was simply one more lackey of the crime lord Maul, the true leader of Crimson Dawn behind the shadows.<ref name="Solo" />

==Skills and abilities==
Dryden Vos was an expert martial artist. He was specifically trained by Maul in Teräs Käsi, a form designed specifically to combat Force-wielders. To compliment this, Vos favored a pair of twin petar blades, which left him at a disadvantage from a distance, but gave him frightening versatility up close. As a Teräs Käsi practitioner, Vos excelled in close-quarters combat, as showcased when he easily fought and disarmed Han Solo.<ref name="TOG" /> Thanks to these abilities, Vos was able to easily outfight Han Solo, who was armed with a [[blaster]], as well as hold his own against Qi'ra; albeit eventually losing, he managed to force her to a defensive position with his attacks. Vos also excelled in leadership,<ref name="Solo"/> negotiations, persuasion and intimidation.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Dyden Vos often wore a black suit with a [[cape]]. He carried [[Dryden Vos's petars|a pair of]] custom made [[Kyuzo]] [[petar]]s, carefully designed to account for his fingers and claws, and perfectly weighted to fit his reach and fighting style. He also [[Dryden Vos' signet ring|wore a]] [[signet ring]] with the crest of Crimson Dawn.<ref name="TOG" /> During the early Imperial Era, he carried a [[blaster pistol]] of an unknown model, but shared among one of the pilots, that had a long [[barrel]].<ref name="MSL Ch10" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Milestones===
Dryden Vos was portrayed by [[Paul Bettany]] in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="EW cover">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2018/02/07/solo-star-wars-story-ew-cover|text=Solo: A Star Wars Story soars on this week's EW cover|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2018-02-07|work=[[Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|Entertainment Weekly]]|archivedate=20200713082155}}</ref> The character was originally going to be played by [[Michael Kenneth Williams]] as a motion-captured alien character resembling a [[Wikipedia:Cougar|mountain lion]],<ref name="Michael K Williams">{{WebCite|url=https://www.avclub.com/michael-k-williams-shares-details-on-his-excised-solo-1823621496|text=Michael K. Williams shares details on his excised Solo: A Star Wars Story character|author=Barasanti, Sam|date=2018-03-08|work=A.V. Club|archivedate=20191121122443}}</ref> but when the original directors [[Phil Lord]] and [[Christopher Miller]] were fired from the project and replaced by [[Ron Howard]], Williams wasn't available for extended reshoots due to scheduling conflicts with [[Wikipedia:Gideon Raff|Gideon Raff's]] [[2019]] film [[Wikipedia:The Red Sea Diving Resort|''The Red Sea Diving Resort'']], so the part was recast with Bettany.<ref name="Solo inspirations">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2018/02/12/solo-a-star-wars-story-influences|text=''Star Wars: Solo'' inspirations include Big Treasure, Big Lebowski, and more|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2018-02-12|work=[[Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|Entertainment Weekly]]|archivedate=20200316213644}}</ref> Though he has the same last name as [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]], [[Lucasfilm]] has confirmed that this is merely a coincidence.<ref name="Solo rogues gallery">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2018/02/09/solo-a-star-wars-story-woody-harrelson-thandie-newton-paul-bettany|text=''Star Wars'': Woody Harrelson, Thandie Newton, Paul Bettany character details|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2018-02-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Entertainment Weekly|Entertainment Weekly]]|archivedate=20200527050119}}</ref>

Vos would make his animated debut in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|Season Seven]] episode "[[The Phantom Apprentice]],"<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" /> which was first released on [[Disney+]] in [[April 24]], [[2020]].<ref name="Disney Press Release">{{DisneyPlus|subdomain=press|url=disney-plus/star-wars-the-clone-wars/710|text=Episode 710 "The Phantom Apprentice"}}</ref> Vos's appearance, along with that of his fellow syndicate leaders Marg Krim and Ziton Moj, was added late into development, during the episode's animation phase. In order to create his animation model, the team used a background Mandalorian citizen, a Mandalorian [[minister]], and the hair of<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=the-phantom-apprentice-trivia-gallery|text="The Phantom Apprentice" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" /> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Trivia" /> In [[2021]], Bettany expressed an interest in returning to the role of Vos in a television series.<ref name="Return">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/paul-bettany-would-reprise-star-wars-role-for-a-series|text=Paul Bettany Would Reprise Star Wars Role for a Series|author=Liu, Narayan|date=2021-01-10|work=Comic Book Resources|archivedate=20210112000647}}</ref> Although The Phantom Apprentice Episode Guide refers to Vos as a syndicate leader,<ref name="Trivia" /> ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' later established the leader of Crimson Dawn at the time was [[Rintero]],<ref name="Strange Allies">{{MSL|Chapter 9: Strange Allies}}</ref> who Vos would end up replacing.<ref name="Climb" />

===Development===
[[File:Dryden_Vos_Manzella.png|thumb|right|210px|Concept art of Dryden Vos' original appearance by Ivan Manzella]]
One of the inspirations for Dryden Vos was the character Leo from [[Wikipedia:Michael Mann|Michael Mann's]] [[1981]] film [[Wikipedia:Thief (film)|''Thief'']]. Coincidentally, Leo's portrayer [[Wikipedia:Robert Prosky|Robert Prosky]] collaborated along ''Solo'' director Ron Howard in the [[1992]] film [[Wikipedia:Far and Away|''Far and Away'']]. Another of the character's influences was [[Wikipedia:Don Draper|Don Draper]], the protagonist of [[Wikipedia:AMC (TV channel)|AMC's]] drama series [[Wikipedia:Mad Men|''Mad Men'']].<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The first draft of the film's script described Vos like a sort of [[Wikipedia:Tom Hagen|Tom Hagen]], one of the main characters of [[Wikipedia:The Godfather (film series)|''The Godfather'']] film series.<ref name="Dryden Hagen">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1064689594376093701|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=Not exactly true. In the 1st draft, Dryden was second-in-command, a sort of Tom Hagen, to a character called The Kazier, a genial but ruthless Hume Cronyn-type who'd replaced his right hand w/ a teched-out rococo key. When that character went out, Dryden's superior became Maul.|archivedate=20190907052615}}</ref> Interestingly, the original ''[[Star Wars]]'' films inspired Bettany to become an actor in the first place.<ref name="Solo DVD Commentary">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' DVD commentary</ref> The film's crew wanted Vos to be, physically, the antithesis of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], the Hutt crime lord of [[1983]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Jon's Twitter">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1040529505670946817|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=continued|archivefile=File:Jon-Kasdan-SoloFacts-September-14-2018.png}}</ref>

Early concept art for Vos depicted him as an avian [[alien]], then a reptilian alien, and finally a [[Lasat]], which was possibly the version depicted originally by Williams. In the original draft of the film's script, Vos was going to be based on [[Taanab]].<ref name="Art of Solo" /> According to Paul Bettany, Vos' facial marks were done by [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|CGI]] and that he just discovered it when Howard sent him a photograph.<ref name="Solo DVD Commentary" /> [[Jake Lunt Davies]] recalls that maybe [[Dava Cassamam]]'s design started off as one possible for Vos.<ref name="LDava Cassamam's Design">{{SW|url=news/solo-a-star-wars-story-designs|text=Inside the Designs of ''Solo: A Star Wars Story''}}</ref> The facial marks, which turn red when Dryden gets angry, are similar to those possessed by [[Naare]], a character from ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', a ''Star Wars'' TV series which began in [[2016]], two years prior to ''Solo: A Star Wars Story''. The similarity was noted by [[Leland Chee]], a member of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]],<ref name="Naare1">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/1000122619096526848|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=Dryden Vos isn't the only one who has facial markings that show up when they're angry. Just sayin'. #Naare|archivedate=20190722102318}}</ref> but he made sure to mention this did not make the two canonically related.<ref name="Naare2">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/1000127931392315392|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=Just to be clear, when I say "just sayin'" I mean "just sayin'." I'm not making any statements about causality.|archivedate=20200328174118}}</ref>
