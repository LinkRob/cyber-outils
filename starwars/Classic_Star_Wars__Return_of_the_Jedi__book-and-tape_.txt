'''''Classic Star Wars: Return of the Jedi''''' is a 24-page book and audio cassette tape adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], released in [[February]] [[1995]]. It uses the same audio and text from the original [[1983]] [[Return of the Jedi (book-and-record)|book-and-record adaptation]].
