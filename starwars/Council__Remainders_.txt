The '''Council''' was the governing body within the [[Remainders]], a group of former [[bounty hunter]]s who managed [[Moonbender Colony]]. Its members included [[General]] [[Zandloss]], the [[sheriff]] [[Barva-Zul]], the [[doctor]] [[Korvik]], [[Jeq]], and [[Zheemo]]. When the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] [[Battle for Moonbender|arrived]] to [[Death|kill]] the Remainders, the Council met and opted to defend the colony.<ref name="DM-BW&R 2" />
