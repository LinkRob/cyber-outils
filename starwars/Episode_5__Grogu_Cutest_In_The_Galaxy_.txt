The '''fifth episode''' of the animated web series ''[[Grogu Cutest In The Galaxy]]'' released on [[January 21]], [[2025]]. It was posted by ''[[Star Wars]]'' Japan on [[Wikipedia:Instagram Reels|Instagram Reels]].<ref name="Instagram" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
As [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] readies for a meal sitting near a table, a tube extends itself, letting out some [[Trask Chowder]] onto a bowl. As Grogu prepares to eat it with a [[spoon]], a [[Unidentified squid|type of squid]] leaps out from the soup, onto Grogu's face. [[Din Djarin]] uses a knife to remove the squid from Grogu's face, and the creature jumps back into the chowder. Grogu inspects the soup again, and lets out a smile.
