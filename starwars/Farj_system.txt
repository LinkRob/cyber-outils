The '''Farj system''' was an uninhabited system<ref name="QfPX" /> in the [[Varada sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It had three [[planet]]s. The second planet was a gas giant that had a [[moon]] that had no [[water]] on it. [[Sky Graf]] set a course for it while piloting the ''[[Brightbird]]'' so they could land the ship and fix the navicomputer.<ref name="QfPX" />
