The '''Destro-6 demolition charge''' was a type of [[detonite charge]] that was favored on covert operations that required little to no close-quarters fighting. Preparation for these charges were known to take [[time]] and precision, although significantly confident demolition specialists could quicken the procedure by disabling certain safeties. Once in place, the charges could be detonated via a [[Timer/Canon|timer]], with a delay of up to six [[Standard hour|hours]], or by a remote signal. If properly placed, they were capable of incapacitating [[armor]]ed vehicles or blasting major breaches within fortifications. The cost of the Destro-6 demolition charge was 750 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Destro-6 demolition charge was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
