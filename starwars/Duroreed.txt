'''Duroreed''' was a type of [[Color|brown]] [[Reed (plant)|reed]] used in the construction of [[duroreed internode armor]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Sith assassin]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] wore a set of duroreed internode armor with two bands of duroreed over her torso.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> she wore the [[armor]] during the [[Indara's murder|murder]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Indara]] on<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> the [[planet]] [[Ueda]],<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> the [[Skirmish on Olega|murder]] of Jedi Master [[Torbin]] on<ref name="RevengeJustice">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> the planet [[Olega]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and subsequent fight against Jedi Master [[Sol]] there,<ref name="RevengeJustice" /> and then on<ref name="Day">{{Acolyte|Day (episode)}}</ref> the planet<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> [[Khofar]], where she initially intended to fight Jedi Master [[Kelnacca]]<ref name="Day" /> but ultimately abandoned the armor to disguised herself as her twin [[Gender|sister]], [[Verosha Aniseya]].<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Duroreed first appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LostFound" /> the first episode of the live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired on [[Disney+]] on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> It was first identified in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
