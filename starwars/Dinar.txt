'''Dinar''' was a settlement close to the equator of the [[planet]] [[Lothal]].<ref name="DRVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> A few [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], a rogue [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] named [[Jovan]] stopped in Dinar to find a discreet way off Lothal and escape his pursuer, [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]. At the time of that event, the population of Dinar was comprised of [[human]]s and at least one [[Gotal]]. A number of [[Loth-cat]]s roamed on the edges of the city.<ref name="KH" />
