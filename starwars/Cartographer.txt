A '''cartographer''' was an individual who made [[map]]s. Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] at the end of the [[Clone Wars]], exploration of [[Wild Space]] resumed, which allowed cartographers to make a decent living selling new maps of the mysterious region. The [[Marriage|spouses]] [[Auric Graf|Auric]] and [[Rhyssa Graf]] were both cartographers,<ref name="The Escape" /> as was their [[Parent|descendant]] [[Emil Graf]].<ref name="Stop, Thief">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=1|story=Tales from Wild Space: Stop, Thief!}}</ref>
