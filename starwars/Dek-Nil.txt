'''Dek-Nil''' was a first generation [[droideka]] who was reprogrammed by [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]]. He joined Aphra on a [[Mission to Skako Minor (Son-tuul Pride)|mission]] to retrieve the memories of [[0-0-0|Triple-Zero]], and was later [[Prison|imprisoned]] in [[Accresker Jail]] along with her, being used as a [[hubdroid]]. As Aphra was [[Attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|attempting]] to escape the prison with the help of Dek-Nil, his [[restraining bolt]] was moved by a mysterious [[Force spirit]], allowing the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] programming to control him. Aphra managed to override his restraining bolt in [[Attack on a pirate station|another]] escape attempt, but Dek-Nil was disassembled by the Force spirit. The apparent [[Changeling|shape-shifter]] "[[Cornelius Evazan|Lopset Yas]]" retrieved Dek-Nil's head, along with his [[central processor]], and carried it with him.

==Biography==
===Recovering memories===
====Skako Minor====
{{Quote|Such viridescent vapors the heart oh the heart sings let us dance in the mist.|Dek-Nil, on Skako Minor|<ref name="Aphra 15" />}}
[[File:AphrasCrew-2016DoctorAphra15.png|thumb|left|220px|Dek-Nil was part of Chelli Aphra's crew of mercenaries]]
The [[droideka]] that became Dek-Nil was reprogrammed by [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]], who fitted him with probability tech, also tinkering with his [[existential circuit]]s.<ref name="Aphra 15" /> Aphra made a decoy control circuit to prevent restraining bolts taking over the droid, hiding the real one behind [[beskar]] plating.<ref name="Aphra 20" /> Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{Aphra2016Date|15}}</ref> Dek-Nil was chosen by the [[assassin droid]] [[0-0-0]], leader of the [[Son-tuul Pride]] syndicate, as part of a crew put together in order to carry out a [[Mission to Skako Minor (Son-tuul Pride)|mission]] to [[Skako Minor]] in hopes of locating his memories. Once the crew arrived on Skako, the [[Skakoan]] [[Hallio Bas]] revealed that he had betrayed them, leading them into an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] trap. Aphra killed Bas and the Imperials with [[Flufto]], an explosive [[tooka]] that she had [[Cloning|cloned]].<ref name="Aphra 15" />

Able to continue their mission, the crew broke into the [[Wat Tambor's workshop|workshop]] of [[Wat Tambor]]. Dek-Nil was able to sense [[Magna Tolvan]]'s [[Delta-class T-3c shuttle|''Delta''-class T-3c shuttle]] moments before it crashed into the workshop, awakening a [[chthonic worm god]]. During the resulting fight that occurred, Dek-Nil fired a shot that caused a [[stormtrooper]] to misfire and collapse the ceiling on the worm god, killing it. The crew looted what was left of the workshop before leaving Skako Minor. However, Aphra discovered that 0-0-0's memories were stored on the [[Tarkin Initiative]] [[space station]] [[Hivebase-1]].<ref name="Aphra 16">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 16]]</ref>

====Hivebase-1====
{{Quote|Behold in awe for I live I give I am the best at playing dead yes.|Dek-Nil|<ref name="Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 19]]</ref>}}
With the help of jump signatures located in Tambor's files, the crew managed to track down and locate the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] flight school ''[[Lucrehulk Prime]]''. The Rebel [[officer]] [[Bini]] assumed the crew were recruits, showing them around the school. When [[Sister Six]] questioned where the [[Defel]] [[Glahst Ombra]] had gone, Dek-Nil cryptically predicted that Magna Tolvan was wearing Ombra's fur as a disguise, breaking a pipe that leaked onto the floor. The crew were then discovered by [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]], who realized they were [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[Prison|imprisoned]] them. As Dek-Nil had predicted, Tolvan entered his and Aphra's cell wearing the fur of Ombra. As Tolvan questioned Aphra, threatening her, the pipe that Dek-Nil had broken leaked onto several wires, causing an electrical surge that knocked Tolvan unconscious and unlocked the prison's cells. The crew escaped the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]], managing to capture General Syndulla as their prisoner.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

[[File:DekNilShot-2016DoctorAphra18.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Dek-Nil is shot by the Hivebase-1 rejects]]
Arriving at Hivebase-1, Aphra used Syndulla to both trick the Imperials into allowing them to board and to allow the ''Lucrehulk Prime'' to pursue them, becoming trapped in a [[Infiltration of Hivebase-1|skirmish]] with the Imperial forces. Before they could complete their mission, Tolvan informed [[Commander]] [[Yewl]] of their presence, leading Yewl to defrost the prototypes stored aboard the station. One of the prototypes shot Dek-Nil, seemingly destroying him.<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 18]]</ref>

However, Dek-Nil was not destroyed, and had been merely playing dead. He sabotaged the station's registry, renaming its firing patterns, before shooting at the [[scaraphyte]] swarm that surrounded the station, causing it to behave erratically. Dek-Nil's sabotage of the registry tricked the Imperial gunners into saying the codeword that activated Aphra's exploding tookas, who had been let free around the station. The crew managed to complete their mission and steal the data from the station, but Dek-Nil was later found and captured by Tolvan, who sent him to [[Accresker Jail]] along with Aphra.<ref name="Aphra 19" />

===Accresker Jail===
====Failed escape attempt====
{{Quote|Welcome to Squad Dek-Nil can you taste the stars listen they surge they surge ohhhh we are the crescendo of a flea's dream let us not advance no but dance yes daaaance.|Dek-Nil, to Chelli Aphra and "Lopset Yas"|<ref name="Aphra 20" />}}
[[File:SquadDekNil-2016DoctorAphra20.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Dek-Nil welcomes Chelli Aphra and Lopset Yas to his squad]]
Once he arrived at the [[wreckage-prison]], Dek-Nil was taken and put to use as one of the many [[hubdroid]]s. The Galactic Empire attempted to reprogram Dek-Nil by placing a [[restraining bolt]] on him, but Aphra had his real control circuit hidden behind a solid [[beskar]] casing. After being assigned a [[Squad Dek-Nil|squad]], Dek-Nil arranged for the deaths of his squad members in what appeared to be improbable accidents. During an [[Attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|attack]] on a Rebel Alliance [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]], Dek-Nil encountered Aphra and [[Chelli Lona Aphra's squad|her squad]], who had sabotaged and destroyed their [[Unidentified hubdroid|hubdroid]] moments earlier. Aphra, along with the apparent [[Changeling|shape-shifter]] "Lopset Yas", the only survivor from their squad<ref name="Aphra 20" /> who was truly the rogue surgeon [[Cornelius Evazan]] in disguise,<ref name="Aphra 25">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 25|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 25]]</ref> was saved by Dek-Nil, who transferred their [[proximity bomb]]s to himself, preventing their detonation. Despite detecting a [[Pluripleq|third]] lifeform, Dek-Nil transferred the two convicts into his squad.<ref name="Aphra 20" />

Aphra led Dek-Nil and Yas to an [[escape pod]], which had become stuck on the edge of the prison's [[airdome]]. Before they could board the escape pod, however, Dek-Nil's restraining bolt was mysteriously moved into place by a [[Force spirit]]. With the restraining bolt in place, the Imperial programming was able to take over his actions. Dek-Nil destroyed the escape pod, forcing Aphra and Yas to return to Accresker Jail. Aphra was later taken in for interrogation, where Dek-Nil suggested several ideas to stop Aphra resisting the [[mind probe]]. On their way back to the prison, Aphra pleaded with Dek-Nil, hoping he was pretending to be under Imperial control, but the droideka silenced her, ordering her back to her quarters.<ref name="Aphra 20" />

====Destruction====
{{Quote|Well&mdash;let's ''see''&mdash;it ''tore'' up my crazy droid, so my soul cries out for revenge.|Aphra following Dek-Nil's destruction|<ref name="Aphra 20" />}}
Shortly after the battle, Dek-Nil and another hubdroid detected a transmission being broadcasted from the prisoners' quarters, and went out searching for it. The two hubdroids were unsuccessful in finding it and soon after, the prisoners were assigned to attack another target. The prison arrived at the scene of three [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]], which had disabled a [[Unidentified pirate station|pirate station]] as a part of the Empire's [[bandit purges]]. The prisoners were released to [[Attack on a pirate station|lay siege]] on the stranded [[pirate]]s, with Dek-Nil leading Aphra and Yas into the engagement. As they charged into battle, Aphra used [[Imperial authority code six-six-nine-zero-beta-one|Imperial authority code 6690B1]] on Dek-Nil, overriding his restraining bolt, and forced him to take them around to a meeting point where Magna Tolvan had arrived to pick up Aphra and Yas.<ref name="Aphra 21">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 21|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 21]]</ref>

[[File:DekNilDestroyed-2016DoctorAphra22.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Force spirit disassembles Dek-Nil]]
Aphra and Yas were about to escape aboard Tolvan's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] when their plan was foiled by the Force spirit, who destroyed the [[sensor disruptor]]s before leading Aphra to the abandoned wreckage of a [[Penumbra-1 shattersprite]]. Aphra, distracted by the ancient [[Jedi]] craft, went to investigate, and Dek-Nil followed.<ref name="Aphra 21" /> However, as they reached the ship, the spirit disassembled Dek-Nil with [[the Force]], leaving his components scattered. Aphra discovered that the spirit's existence was due to [[gundravian hookspore]]s mutated with the Force, but Aphra and Yas were forced to retreat due to the receding airdome. Yas took Dek-Nil's [[central processor]] along with them to keep their [[proximity bomb]]s safe, and continued to carry the piece of Dek-Nil as they were forced to return to the prison.<ref name="Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 22]]</ref>

After Aphra was forced to reveal the infestation of hookspores while being interrogated by the [[Mairan]] [[Bor Ifriem]], the [[Accresker command]] [[Skirmish aboard Accresker Jail|abandoned]] the prison, setting its course to crash into the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]-affiliated [[planet]]<ref name="Aphra 22" /> [[Tiferep Major]]. Aphra, Tolvan, Yas and their associate [[Sana Starros]] broke into the wreckage of the [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class]] tug [[cruiser]], and Yas carried Dek-Nil's central processor on his back. Once inside the cruiser, Aphra asked Yas to give her the piece of Dek-Nil, which she used to slice into the generator that controlled the [[gravity]] in the prison. Yas took Dek-Nil's head back after she had done so, and carried it with him once again.<ref name="Aphra 23">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 23|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 23]]</ref>

Yas was still carrying the piece of Dek-Nil when Aphra froze him in his natural appearance as Evazan, so that she could trick the vigilante [[Tam Posla]] into trading what she thought was a [[Changeling|shape-shifter]] for an [[Unidentified shuttle (Tam Posla)|escape shuttle]].<ref name="Aphra 24">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 24|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 24]]</ref> Posla later discovered he had been tricked, returning to Accresker. Yas went with him, tracking Aphra's proximity bomb using Dek-Nil. Yas continued to carry Dek-Nil on his back even as he [[stun]]ned Aphra, taking her captive aboard [[Posla's ship]]. By the time that he had revealed his true identity as Evazan to her, he was no longer carrying the droid.<ref name="Aphra 25" />

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|S-so&mdash;you know this droid?''"<br />"''If you mean did I create and program this singularly amazing probability machine?|Lopset Yas and Chelli Aphra, on Dek-Nil|<ref name="Aphra 20" />}}
As a probability droid, Dek-Nil could predict events before they even happened, and could cause chain reactions that he knew the result of.<ref name="Aphra 16" /> His existential circuits had also been modified,<ref name="Aphra 15" /> causing him to incessantly make philosophical comments on the wonders of existence that others considered to be strange.<ref name="Aphra 17" />

[[File:DekNilShoots-2016DoctorAphra20.jpg|thumb|left|260px|Dek-Nil fires upon an escape pod]]
Despite being a droideka, Dek-Nil was heavily modified, only possessing one [[Limb|arm]], which was armed with a [[blaster cannon]], powerful enough to destroy an entire escape pod within seconds. He was altered to have a decoy control circuit, with the real circuit hidden behind beskar plating, preventing restraining bolts from affecting him.<ref name="Aphra 20" /> He was made of several mismatched parts, including the head of an [[astromech droid]].<ref name="Aphra 15" /> When taken to Accresker Jail, he was given a restraining bolt and became one of the prison's hubdroids, but his decoy control circuit protected him. He was later taken control of by the Imperial programming,<ref name="Aphra 20" /> but Aphra freed him from it.<ref name="Aphra 21" />
