{{Quote|War does not come with a guarantee. No soldier gets the promise of safety, survival or victory. But men, I guarantee you this: every member of this clone youth brigade will have his moment.|[[Crasher]] to the [[Cloning|clone]] cadets abroad the ''[[Endurance]]''|<ref name="Death Trap" />}}
The '''Clone Youth Brigade''' was a [[brigade]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that was made up of young [[clone cadet]]s training to become [[clone trooper]]s. At one point, an outfit of the Youth Brigade was sent to tour the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Endurance]]'', when it was infiltrated by the [[bounty hunter]] and [[Cloning|clone]] [[Boba Fett]]. Fett sought to exact revenge on [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], who had killed his father [[Jango Fett]], the genetic template for all clones. Joining a squad of clones including [[Whiplash (Clone Youth Brigade)|Whiplash]], [[Jax (clone cadet)|Jax]], [[Hotshot]], [[1151]], and others, Boba Fett succeeded in sabotaging the ship and caused the ''Endurance'' to crash on [[Vanqor]].<ref name="Death Trap" />
