'''Aguth Omak''' was a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate]] who represented [[Sluis Van]],<ref name="MoF" /> an astronomical object in the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> The [[Pau'an]] [[Tychon Nulvolio]] served as Omak's [[Chief of Staff]]. During the [[Separatist Crisis]], Omak was a secessionist and left the Republic in order to serve in the [[Separatist Senate]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in a role of middling importance in the Separatist's civilian government.<ref name="MoF" />

After the [[Clone Wars]] broke out between the Republic and the Confederacy, Nulvolio faded from public view until Omak [[Death|perished]] during the war, at which point Nulvolio ran for the senator's seat and won. In the first [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|reign]] following the end of the Clone Wars<ref name="MoF" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Senator [[Mon Mothma]] of the [[Imperial Senate]] thought of Omak when she met Nulvolio for the first time.<ref name="MoF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aguth Omak was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', which was written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first instalment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MoF" />
