The '''Bondsman Guild Protocol''' was a protocol which dictated the procedure and policies to be followed in providing an individual to a [[bounty hunter]] or other authorities. Subparagraph 16 of this protocol compelled individuals holding an asset to immediately produce it to a bounty hunter under certain conditions.<ref name="Mandalorian1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> A degree of respectability was gained with the conferral of the protocols.<ref name="SWI">{{InsiderCite|201|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[IG-series]] droid bounty hunter [[IG-11]] cited the Bondsman Guild Protocol, and subparagraph 16 specifically, while attempting to retrieve "[[Din Grogu|the asset]]" during a [[Mission to Arvala-7|mission]] on [[Arvala-7]].<ref name="Mandalorian1" />
