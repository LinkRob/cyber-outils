'''Dawn Dininger''' is an actress who portrayed the [[Unidentified Rodian prisoner]] in "[[Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land]]," the first episode of the [[2021]] live-action television series, ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', which was released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Stranger" /> on [[December 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Disney+">{{DisneyPlusYouTube|t9Ca4ARSfqA|Next On Disney+ &ndash; December}}</ref> She also was a puppeteer for episodes of the live action series, ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]''.
