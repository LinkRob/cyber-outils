An '''aurora snowflake pin''' was a type of pin that resembled a snowflake and represented the vibrant [[aurora]]s of the snowy [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Quarren]] [[merchant]] [[Sol D'lorak]] had at least one such pin for sale at their [[Sol D'lorak's store|store]] in the [[Thieves' Quarter]] of [[Kijimi City]] on Kijimi. The pin was worth 1000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and was part of an exclusive inventory which D'lorak offered to members of the local [[Ashiga Clan]] and their allies at a discount.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] gained a good reputation with the Ashiga Clan and purchased the pin from D'lorak.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An aurora snowflake pin appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The item is used as a minor charm in the game and gives the player a chance to not consume [[bacta vial]]s when they are used.<ref name="Outlaws" />
