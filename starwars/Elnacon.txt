'''Elnacons''' were a large peaceful [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> that hailed from [[Nacon]],<ref name="USW" /> a [[Planet|world]] that had an [[ammonia]]-rich [[atmosphere]]. When on other worlds, they had to wear special [[Breath mask|breathing apparatus]]. They had [[nose]]less faces and four-fingered hands. They were often mistaken as thugs because of their alarming height and strength, but were actually a very peaceful, family-oriented species. [[Dava Cassamam]], a deep cloud-[[miner]] on [[gas giant]] planets, was a keen [[sabacc]] player.<ref name="Alien Archive" />
