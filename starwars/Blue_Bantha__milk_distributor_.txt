'''Blue Bantha''' was a [[milk]] distribution company that sold both [[Blue milk|blue]] and [[green milk]], owning and operating multiple locations across the various battlefields of the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]], utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition. With the help of specially equipped, [[Color|blue]] and white&ndash;clad [[R5-series astromech droid]]s, they ran both the [[Blue Bantha Refreshments]] milk bar and smaller [[Blue Bantha Milk Kiosk]], found within the [[Mos Espa Waystation]] and [[Dune Sea Outpost]] arenas respectively. Their branding included trapezoidal signage featuring a blue coloration, and both white and yellow text.<ref name="Hunters" />
