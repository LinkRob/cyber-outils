The '''''Boco''''' was a [[patrol ship]] in service to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. It served under the command of [[Senior Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Mitth'raw'nuru]] during a rescue of a [[Unidentified excursion liner|luxury passenger liner]] that was descending into a [[Unidentified triple-ringed gas giant|gas giant]] [[planet]], during which time it was Thrawn's first command.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==History==
While commanding the ''Boco'', Thrawn saved the lives of 8,000 [[Chiss]] aboard a derelict [[Unidentified excursion liner|luxury liner]], aided by his commanding officer [[Senior Captain]] [[Ar'alani|Irizi'ar'alani]]. The passengers and crew aboard the liner had been evacuated from the liner's luxury wings to the vacuum-sealed central compartment. Thrawn proceeded to shoot off the liner's luxury wings with the ''Boco''{{'s}} laser weapons, reducing the liner's mass enough for the ''Boco''{{'s}} [[tractor beam]]s to rescue the passengers and crew from the central compartment. In the process, Thrawn saved the lives of [[Aristocra]] of at least five of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The luxury liner's owners in the [[Boadil family]] were livid at the loss of property. The liner's captain, a member of the [[Ufsa family]], was responsible for the vessel succumbing to the upper [[atmosphere]] of the [[Unidentified triple-ringed gas giant|gas giant]]. The Boadil were allied with the Ufsa, as well as Senior Captain Ziara's [[Irizi family]]; so [[Chiss family#Family politics|family politics]] concerns led the Boadil to scapegoat Thrawn for the loss of property. The Boadil family demanded Thrawn be demoted or even expelled from the fleet for property damage, but the grateful Aristocra of other Ruling Families whose lives Thrawn saved balanced out the Boadil's fury. Those families insisted Thrawn be commended and even promoted. Ultimately, Thrawn was neither promoted nor demoted; punished nor commended, with one crucial exception: command of the ''Boco'' was taken away from him.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Having lost his first command, Senior Captain Ziara requested that Thrawn be transferred to her command to serve as an officer on her ''[[Parala]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
