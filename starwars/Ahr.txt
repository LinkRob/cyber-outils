'''Ahr''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Moff]] and [[governor]] of the [[Esuain sector]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] who [[Commanding officer|commanded]] a [[Ahr's Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|navy]]. Along with the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], he was assigned to [[Attack on Namzor|neutralize]] a threat on [[Namzor]] around [[2 BBY]]. However, during the Namzor counter-insurgency, Vader committed insubordination, resulting in Ahr contacting [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], who ordered Vader to abide by all the governor's directives.

Ahr and Vader went on to [[Pacification of the Esuain sector|liquidate]] threats on [[Ahn Krantarium]] and [[Phelzepham]], both of which resulted in successes. After dealing with the three problems, the Moff wanted Vader to [[Death|kill]] a [[Unidentified creature (Kankalo Belt Containment Zone)|creature]] as a test, but the Sith Lord had other plans. After seeing the creature from his [[Vader's starship|starship]], Vader turned around and made it rupture the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], grasping and killing Ahr in [[Realspace|space]].

==Biography==
===Namzor and contacting the Emperor===
{{Quote|Vader! This is Governor Ahr! Pull back, do you hear me? Vader!|Ahr angrily commands Darth Vader|<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />}}
[[File:PalpatineAfterNamzorAttack-AORDarthVader1.png|thumb|left|300px|Ahr contacted the Emperor to discuss Darth Vader's insubordination at Namzor.]]
Ahr served as the [[Moff]] and [[governor]] of<ref name="Age of Rebellion" /> the [[Esuain sector]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] as well as the [[commanding officer]] of a [[Ahr's Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="Age of Rebellion" /> Around [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> as part of a [[Pacification of the Esuain sector|pacification operation]], he was sent to [[Attack on Namzor|deal]] with [[Namzor insurgents|insurgents]] on the Mid Rim astronomical object [[Namzor]] with the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. However, the insurgents had set up their base over a [[coaxium]] [[hypermatter]] deposit. Ahr, who had been instructed to obtain the coaxium, ordered all [[starfighter]]s to return to the [[Star Destroyer]] to avoid damaging the coaxium. The [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter]] withdrew, but Vader, in his [[Vader's starship|starship]], did not. Despite the Moff ordering the [[Sith Lord]] to pull back, Vader continued attacking the base until he destroyed both the insurgents and the coaxium deposit.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

A very displeased Ahr contacted [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] via [[hologram]] to report Vader's failure to follow orders, his arriving unannounced, and his destruction of the valuable coaxium. However, Vader disagreed, stating that he had destroyed the insurgents so that they could not escape. Palpatine questioned Vader about his awareness of the governor's orders and then commanded Vader to follow all of Ahr's orders as if they were from Palpatine himself until he had learned his lesson. Vader was angry about the outcome, seeing himself as nothing more than a [[Slavery|slave]] to others.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

===Ahn Krantarium, Phelzepham, and death===
{{Quote|Vader! The&mdash;the Emperor told you to obey my commands&mdash;to the letter! So kneel! Do you hear me?! To the letter!|Ahr, to Vader before his death|<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />}}
Afterward, the Moff traveled to the Mid Rim astronomical object [[Ahn Krantarium]] to [[Attack on Ahn Krantarium|eliminate]] a dangerous group of self-rebuilding [[droid]]s. He sent out a [[stormtrooper]] patrol, but all of them perished, so instead of deploying more of them, Ahr sent out Vader. The Sith Lord destroyed all the droids in the vicinity, surprising Ahr, who ate some [[Foodstuff|food]] after the completion of Vader's mission. Next, the governor went to the Mid Rim [[moon]] [[Phelzepham]] to [[Attack on Phelzepham|liquidate]] more [[Phelzepham insurgents|insurgents]]. Ahr deployed Vader and multiple stormtroopers, who captured the moon within an [[Standard hour|hour]]. The stormtroopers were withdrawn by Ahr beforehand while he left Vader to finish the work.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

[[File:AhrDeath-AORDarthVader1.png|thumb|right|300px|Ahr was killed by the Kankalo Belt creature.]]
The governor decided to head to the [[Kankalo Belt Containment Zone]] despite the protests of a [[Ahr's subordinate|subordinate]] of his. He sent out Vader in his [[starship]] to destroy the greatest threat there&mdash;the [[Unidentified creature (Kankalo Belt Containment Zone)|Kankalo Belt creature]]. Since Vader saw Ahr as his biggest threat, when he saw the creature, he turned back toward the Star Destroyer&mdash;with the creature following him&mdash;to eliminate the Moff. When Vader returned, Ahr was angry with him and ordered that he kneel before him as the Emperor commanded. When Vader kneeled, the creature clawed into the Star Destroyer's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] to grab Ahr away, killing him and multiple other personnel in the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]]. The Emperor then [[Force sense|sensed]] the governor's death and saw that Vader had learned his lesson.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Hm. Tell all troops to retreat... except Vader.|Ahr, to a subordinate|<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />}}
Ahr was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] with [[Color|gray]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and light skin. He tested Vader for pleasure by sending him on dangerous missions to see if the Sith Lord would be killed.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />

==Equipment==
Ahr wore a gray-green [[Imperial military uniforms|Imperial officer's uniform]] with the [[Rank insignia plaque|rank insignia]] of Moff and four [[code cylinder]]s.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
