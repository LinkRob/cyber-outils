'''Droid rights''' was the belief that [[droid]]s should be granted the [[Manumission|rights]] of other [[Sentience|sentient]] beings. [[L3-37]] cared deeply about droid rights<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|l3-37|L3-37}}</ref> and decried the systematic oppression of mechanical intelligence throughout the [[galaxy]]. She longed for a droid revolution where her fellow mechanicals would overthrow [[Species|organic]] rule and be free to determine their own fates.<ref name="Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>

Proponents of droid rights pointed to the [[Galactic Constitution]], which declared all sentients equal. They decried [[memory wipe]]s maintained to eliminate personality quirks and questioned why they were recommended if droids truly lacked personalities. The movement also considered the use of [[restraining bolt]]s a form of [[slavery]], a practice outlawed by the Galactic Constitution. The brutal work conditions experienced by droids and daily threat of being thrown away following an injury on the job were another issue the droid rights movement protested.<ref name="Tales">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
According to the collectible art book accompanying the Blu-ray edition of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', the concept of droid rights originated with ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]] in the early 2000s.<ref name="George and the Droids Rights">{{WebCite|url=https://comicbook.com/starwars/2018/10/10/star-wars-solo-movie-l3-37-george-lucas|text=Star Wars: How George Lucas Influenced L3-37 in 'Solo'|author=Cavanaugh, Patrick|date=2018-10-09|archivedate=20190621031743}}</ref> On the other hand, in the featurette "[[The Characters of Star Wars]]" released in [[2004]] and an interview in [[2005]] Lucas stated that, droids, while some of them like R2-D2 and C3PO are very human in nature, were only programmed to act like human beings and had no soul.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-news/george-lucas-and-the-cult-of-darth-vader-247142|text=George Lucas and the Cult of Darth Vader|author=Gavin Edwards|work=[[Wikipedia:Rolling Stone|''Rolling Stone'']]|date=2005-06-02|archivedate=20230216004224}}</ref><ref name="The Characters of Star Wars">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars Trilogy (DVD)|featurette=The Characters of Star Wars|ftext=The Characters of ''Star Wars''}}</ref>
