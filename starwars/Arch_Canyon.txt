'''Arch Canyon''' was a location on the [[Mos Espa podrace circuit]]. Most races opted to travel over the [[Dune Sea]] instead of through Arch Canyon, though the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] did not.<ref name="SWCL" />
{{Nature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Just like racing through Arch Canyon!|Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>}}
Arch Canyon appears as part of the [[Mos Espa]] [[Podracing|circuit]] in the non-canon [[2022]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''. During the [[Battle of Naboo]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] then compares flying a [[N-1 starfighter]] through the interior of the [[Lucrehulk-class battleship/Legends|''Lucrehulk''-class Droid Control Ship]] ''[[Vuutun Palaa]]'' to podracing through the canyon.<ref name="LEGO" />
