'''Eriadu Spaceport Control''' was a security organization located in [[Eriadu City/Legends|Eriadu City]] on the planet of [[Eriadu/Legends|Eriadu]] circa [[32 BBY/Legends|32 BBY]]. Spaceport Control was involved in the safety of the [[Eriadu Trade Summit]] and aftermath, specifically around the area of the spaceport. They helped manage those going in and out of the area.
