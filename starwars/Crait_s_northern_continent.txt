The '''northern continent''' of the [[planet]] [[Crait]] contained a base that was used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in its [[Galactic Civil War|struggle]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. The inhabitants of the base were the planet's sole residents.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" />
