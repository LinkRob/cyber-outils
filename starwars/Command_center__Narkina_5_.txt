A '''command center''' was the command center and prison-wide control room for a [[Imperial factory facility]] part of the [[Narkina 5]] [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|Prison Complex]].<ref name="One Way Out" />

==Description==
{{Quote|I am speaking to you from the command center on Level Eight.|Kino Loy|<ref name="One Way Out" />}}
The command center was staffed by the [[Unidentified Narkina 5 prison commander|commander]] in charge of the particular prison cylinder and two [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[technician]]s and was equipped with three white colored duty posts which controlled key prison-wide functions such as the activation or deactivation of the [[Tunqstoid steel]] floors and the isolation of specific levels. The commander's duty post was accessible only to him and controlled the most sensitive prison functions, including the hydroelectric generator floodgates which provided power to the entire cylinder and the prison-wide PA system. The duty posts were surrounded by large screens which displayed the prisoners' location within the complex as yellow dots superimposed on detailed black and white blueprints of the facility.<ref name="One Way Out" />

==History==
In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|110}}</ref> a command center was attacked and saw one of its command techs killed during the [[Narkina 5 prison escape|prisoner uprising]] in one of the cylinders. [[Kino Loy]] and [[Cassian Jeron Andor]], two of the prisoners incarcerated in the facility used the controls located in the room to deactivate the floors and open all seals and doors in the prison to liberate their fellow inmates.<ref name="One Way Out" />
