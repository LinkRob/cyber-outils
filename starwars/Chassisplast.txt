A '''chassisplast'''<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> or '''sticker''' was a slip of thin material with a picture or [[writing]] on it and an adhesive substance on its back that was used to fasten it to a surface. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[artist]] and rebel [[Sabine Wren]] used chassisplasts as a quick and easy means to propagate her art and deface [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]].<ref name="sketch">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref>
