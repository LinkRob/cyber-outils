'''Brad Schoeneberg''' is a Product Developer for toys at [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Parks and Resorts|Disney Parks]]. With his team, Schoeneberg notably worked on the ''[[Star Wars: Star Tours]]'' toy line, which includes the [[Mickey Mouse|Jedi Mickey]] action figures and Mickey's official [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]].
