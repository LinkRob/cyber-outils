'''Aloxl''' was a [[Temperature|hot]] [[planet]] located in the [[Deep Core]] within [[the galaxy]]. The planet had an unusual ellipse around its gigantic [[star]] causing a 125-[[Standard year|year]] hot season, and a 25-year cool season. Uninhabitable during its hot cycle, [[species]] that could withstand high temperatures could sustain themselves below ground in subterranean [[cave]]rns.<ref name="Savage Spirits" />
