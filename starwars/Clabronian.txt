The '''Clabronians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the world of [[Clabron]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], they were led by [[Grand Minister]] [[Stin]], who considered siding with the [[Galactic Republic]], despite the fact that many Clabronians wished to stay out of the conflict. When Stin organized a [[Mission to Clabron|meeting]] with Republic [[senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] [[Death|assassinated]] Stin. He was replaced by [[Second Minister]] [[Tarmin]], who, while not wanting to fight in the war, promised to provide aid to the Republic in other ways.<ref name="Age of Republic" />
