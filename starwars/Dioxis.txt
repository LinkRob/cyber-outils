'''Dioxis''' was a toxic [[gas]] used by the [[Trade Federation]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. [[Dioxis grenade]]s were grenades that released the poisonous gas, and were used during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==History==
{{Quote|They must be dead by now. Destroy what's left of them.|[[Nute Gunray]] regarding Obi-Wan Kenobi and Qui-Gon Jinn|<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref>}}
[[File:TournamentOnSerenno-TB.png|thumb|left|250px|Dioxis fills the Box during the tournament on Serenno]]
During the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], the [[Neimoidian]]s attempted to [[Death|kill]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] by flooding a conference room of their [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleship]] with the [[poison]]ous [[gas]]. However, both Jinn and Kenobi found their way out of the room and [[Escape from the Saak'ak|fled]] to Naboo.<ref name="Episode I" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] used dioxis as part of a [[Tournament on Serenno|contest]] on [[Serenno]]. Within an area known as the [[Box]], eleven [[bounty hunter]]s competed in a series of tests to prove whether they should be chosen as part of a team that would kidnap [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. The first task saw the competitors, which included a disguised Kenobi, having to escape a room that was being flooded with the gas.<ref name="The Box" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dioxis first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode I" />

For the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[The Box]]," it was originally intended to fill the chamber of the first challenge at the Box with [[water]] instead of dioxis. According to supervising director [[Dave Filoni]], however, when they wrote the story, they had yet to solve water, leading the production crew to drop water in favor of dioxis. Though they eventually solved for water, the constrained FX budget for the episode necessitated sticking with dioxis.<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-box-trivia-gallery|text="The Box" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref>
