'''Dazhda''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Rodian]] who served as a [[Sergeant]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Uprising]]. After the [[Clone Wars]], Dazhda swore to never use a [[blaster]] again, but he found himself unable to ignore the [[Galactic Empire]]'s increasingly despotic rule. After being trapped in the [[Anoat sector]] by [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Iron Blockade]], he developed a reputation as one of the Alliance's best [[scout]]s operating in the [[sector]].<ref name="Uprising" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Eventually, Dazhda joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], who dispatched the Rodian on a variety of missions throughout the Anoat sector.<ref name="Uprising" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dazhda was created by [[Kabam]] for ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', a [[canon]] mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] released for iOS and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] operating systems in 2015. The player could send Dazhda on numerous missions for useful items.<ref name="Uprising" />
