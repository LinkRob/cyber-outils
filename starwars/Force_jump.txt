'''Force jump''',<ref name="USC" /> or '''Force Leap''',<ref name="Force Leap">{{Topps|set=2015 Topps Star Wars Chrome Perspectives|subset=Jedi Training|cardname=Force Leap|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/117723/cid/8023328/2015-Topps-Chrome-Star-Wars-Perspectives-Jedi-vs.-Sith---Jedi-Training-6-Force-Leap}}</ref> was an [[Force power|ability]] of [[the Force]] that allowed a [[Force-sensitive]] to perform an '''extended leap''' across extensive distances.<ref name="USC" /> Some untrained Force-sensitives, including [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], instinctively demonstrated this skill despite their lack of training or knowledge of their Force powers.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref><ref name="Leia, Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> Moreover, Force-users could use this '''Force-falling''' power to endure great falls and decent.<ref name=":0">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref><ref name="OFNF">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>

The feat was helpful for Jedi to emply since they may frequently fall off surfaces. During the High Republic one form of Jedi training was flinging a student off a cliff to impel the Padawan to learn the technique.<ref name=":0" />

The [[Jedi]] [[Tarados Gon]], [[Nicanas Tassu]], and [[Kanan Jarrus]] were all noted users of the Force jump.<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> When [[Luke Skywalker]] [[Duel on Cloud City|dueled]] his estranged [[Parent|father]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on [[Cloud City]], he made use of the ability to escape from the [[Carbon-freezing chamber|carbon-freeze chamber]].<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> The action made him feel like the air itself was moving him. Skywalker's jump was as high and fast as those of Gon, Tassu, and Jarrus.<ref name="Odyssey" />
