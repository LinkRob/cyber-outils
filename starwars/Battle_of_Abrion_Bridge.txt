The '''Battle of Abrion Bridge''' was a battle that occurred between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> around [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]]. It was considered to be painful and full of [[blood]]shed.<ref name="Reflects">{{SW|url=news/marvel-july-2022-star-wars-comics-exclusive-preview|text=General Obi-Wan Kenobi Reflects on the Clone Wars and More from Marvel's July 2022 ''Star Wars'' Comics &ndash; Exclusive Preview}}</ref> [[Standard year|Years]] later, [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] reflected on his experiences during the battle when waiting out a [[sandstorm]] on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

==Prelude==
Before the destruction of [[Rishi Station]], Master Kenobi and Commander Cody recovered intel regarding a hidden Separatist weapons factory under a granary complex on [[Abrion Major]]. The factory would used build the new [[Mega-ion cannon]] for [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]]. According to Admiral Yularen, the only way was through the bridge. Kenobi volunteered for himself and the 212th battalion to lead the assault. The 212th were then joined by Commander [[Mekedrix]], Lieutenant [[Oron (lieutenant)|Oron]] and his [[Mekedrix's company|company.]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

==The Battle==
Kenobi, Cody and the 212th took several gunships to Abrion Major. En route to the weapon complex, the gunships came under fire by the complex's shelling. Kenobi, Cody, Mekedrix, and their forces were able to land. The Separatist forces attempted to blow the bridge. Kenobi accompanied Airo as he carried a banner to let the republic navy see how far they could go. Kenobi and his troops were find the ion cannon plans. Mekedrix and were forces than came under attack by several IG-units and ''Rogue''-class fighters from the Banking Clan. The 212th were trapped.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" />

Despite the heavy casualties, Kenobi refused to leave his men on the bridge. Cody and his troops followed him. Kenobi was able to find Airo, but he died. Kenobi was then held at gunpoint by an IG unit until Mekedrix destroyed it. They then both left the bridge leaving countless casualties.<ref name="Obi-Wan 3" /> [[Clone Commander]] [[Verd]] also fought the battle.<ref name="OSWM 121" />

==Aftermath==
Following the battle, Mekedrix continued to serve the Republic until he disappeared after the [[Devaron massacre|massacre]] at the [[Temple of Eedit]] on [[Devaron]]. He went rogue in the [[Death Wind Corridor]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4">[[Obi-Wan 4|''Obi-Wan'' 4]]</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="DateNeyo">{{GermanFanCite|121|Database}} establishes that "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]" was written during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]].</ref> Commander Verd mentioned the participation in the Battle of Abrion Bridge and how they fought against the [[IG-series battle droid army]] in a report describing [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank|IG-227 ''Hailfire''-class droid tanks]], which was included in the survival compendium "[[Neyo's Almanac of Clankers]]."<ref name="OSWM 121" />
