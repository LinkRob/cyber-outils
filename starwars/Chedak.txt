'''Chedak''' was a company that produced [[Subspace transceiver|subspace radios]]. The modified [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was equipped with an upgraded Chedak subspace radio around thirty [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="ICS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chedak were first mentioned in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book which was written by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]] for publication in [[1987]]. The company were then confirmed to be [[canon]] when they were mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]] and released in [[2015]].
