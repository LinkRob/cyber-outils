An '''individual''' was a [[programmer]]<ref name="Powered Down 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=10|story=Powered Down, Part 1}}</ref> who worked as an employee of the company [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="DV43" /> They reprogrammed<ref name="Powered Down 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=10|story=Powered Down, Part 1}}</ref> the [[LOM-series protocol droid]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[4-LOM]].<ref name="Powered Down 1" /> At some point, 4-LOM was assigned to a [[luxury liner]],<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> but a flaw in his programming allowed for his [[Personality matrix|personality software]] to be corrupted,<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> leading to the [[protocol droid]] overriding it.<ref name="Card Trader">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=4-LOM - Bounty Hunter}}</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date1">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Powered Down, Part 1]]" to [[9 BBY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] thought about the programmer, considering them a lunatic for reprogramming 4-LOM, as the [[droid]] by then worked as a [[bounty hunter]] chasing individuals such as himself.<ref name="Powered Down 1" />

The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="HC 78">{{HelmetCollectionCite|78|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref> 4-LOM recalled that his programmer once<ref name="Powered Down 2" /> naively<ref name="DV43" /> spoke to him of legends about a [[Unidentified planet (droid uprising)|world]] where droids had risen up against their masters. 4-LOM believed the legend to be true as he fought off [[scorpion-droid]]s alongside his [[Gand]] [[bounty]] hunter partner, [[Zuckuss]], Solo, and the smuggler [[Chewbacca]]<ref name="Powered Down 2" /> while on the [[Planet|world]], finding the situation fascinating.<ref name="HC 78" />

==Behind the scenes==
The programmer was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story "[[Powered Down, Part 1]]," published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 10|tenth issue]] of the [[2017]] anthology comic series [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]. The comic was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="Powered Down 2" /> on [[May 9]], [[2018]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2017-2020-10/digital-comic/652799|text=Star Wars Adventures #10|nolive=1}}</ref>
