'''Binkberries''' were a type of [[berry]] found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] grew a type of [[hybrid binkberry]] in fields at their [[Path compound|compound]] on Dalna.<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="POD Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] passed by the fields and observed the hybrid binkberries. Later, the [[Evereni]] Path member [[Marda Ro]] noticed [[cloud]]s forming and felt that overnight [[Water|rain]] would be good for the binkberries and [[layer lettuce]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" />

No earlier than [[18 BBY]],<ref name="San Date">{{SanctuaryDate}}</ref> on the planet [[Tryth]], the rogue [[Cloning|clone]] [[Wrecker]] utilized a homemade [[thermal detonator]] with a shell made from a binkberry preserves can. [[Ponder]], another former [[clone trooper]], recognized the famous logo from the can on the detonator's shell.<ref name="Sanctuary">''[[Sanctuary: A Bad Batch Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
