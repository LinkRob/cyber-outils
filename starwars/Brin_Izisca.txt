'''Brin Izisca''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[pastor]] and philanthropist, as well as a leading member of the [[Church of the Force]]. He sent five [[pilgrim]]s to the [[planet]] [[Christophsis]] to return a crate of [[kyber crystal]]s back to where they belonged. After twelve arduous [[Standard day|days]], the pilgrims finally reached a broken [[crystal]] wall where they returned the excavated crystals to their home. Only [[Addar]] survived that quest.<ref name="AEE" />
