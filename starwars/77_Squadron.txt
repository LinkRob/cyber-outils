'''77 Squadron''', also known as '''Squadron 77''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] that served as part of the [[New Republic]]'s starfighter corps during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Mostly consisting of [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighters]], the squadron participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]] under the designation '''"Blue Squadron"'''.

==Description==
77 Squadron,<ref name="HM" /> alternatively Squadron 77<ref name="Past is Prologue">{{StoryCite|story=Past is Prologue|book=Free Comic Book Day 2025: Star Wars 1|bformatted=Free Comic Book Day 2025: ''Star Wars'' 1}}</ref> and in at least one instance designated "Blue Squadron,"<ref name="HM" /> was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] within the [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps]] that was equipped with [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="HM" />

==History==
===Battle of Jakku===
After receiving reports of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] near [[Jakku]], Admiral [[Gial Ackbar]] sent a reconnaissance team to investigate with 77 Squadron accompanying them as a security escort. Upon emerging from [[hyperspace]], the group quickly ran into a group of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] guarding a [[Star Destroyer]] orbiting the planet. In the skirmish that ensued, the Star Destroyer's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] was taken out and the recon team quickly returned to base.<ref name="HM" /> Eventually, the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] and [[New Republic army|army]] travelled to Jakku and engaged the remaining loyalist [[Imperial Military]] forces in the climactic [[Battle of Jakku]], the final battle of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. During the large conflict, 77 Squadron communicated with other New Republic forces that they were arriving on the left flank just as the ISD ''[[Punishment]]'' rammed the Republic [[Starhawk-class Battleship Mark I|''Starhawk''-class Battleship]] ''[[Amity]]''.<ref name="Last Stand 4">[[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 4|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 4]]</ref>

===New Republic Era===
After the Galactic Civil War and the collapse of the Empire, 77 Squadron travelled through the edge of the [[Fenril sector]], travelling around a [[gas giant]]. Afterwards, a squadron led by [[Luke Skywalker]] passed through the same area, and informed his squadron that they would take a shortcut rather than the route 77 Squadron took.<ref name="SW 1">[[Star Wars (2025) 1|''Star Wars'' (2025) 1]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
77 Squadron (pronounced "seven-seven" by Admiral Ackbar<ref name="YouTube HM" />) was created for ''[[Hyperspace Mountain]]'', a ''Star Wars''-themed overlay of the [[Wikipedia:Space Mountain|Space Mountain]] attraction for [[Disneyland]]'s [[Season of the Force]] event and [[Hong Kong Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Tomorrowland Takeover|''Star Wars'': Tomorrowland Takeover]]. The Squadron takes its number from [[1977]], the year both [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] and the Disneyland incarnation of Space Mountain first premiered.<ref name="OhMyDisney">{{Disney|subdomain=ohmy|url=insider/2016/03/15/how-hyperspace-mountain-fits-into-the-star-wars-story|text=How Hyperspace Mountain Fits Into the Star Wars Story|nolive=1}}</ref> Images in the ride queue before and after the overlay depict the similarly-named "Space Station 77".
