'''Elocin''' was an individual who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] who operated a [[Elocin's crew|crew]] and ran a [[Elocin's club|club]]. She also came to know the [[smuggler]]s [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Han Solo]].<ref name="Lando 1" /> In [[6 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Return of the Jedi – Lando 1|''Return of the Jedi – Lando'' 1]] take place after [[Star Wars (2020) 34|''Star Wars'' (2020) 34]], which dates itself to four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[destruction of Jedha City]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]]. The issue also takes place before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which ''Timelines'' dates to [[4 ABY]]. As ''Return of the Jedi &ndash; Lando'' 1 establishes that [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] cheated Elocin ten [[Standard year|years]] prior, it must have taken place in [[6 BBY]].</ref> Calrissian cheated Elocin out of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and she told him she never wanted to see him again. Sometime later, she also ended on bad terms with Solo.<ref name="Lando 1" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> in need of ten thousand credits to acquire the plans to the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] to rescue Solo, Calrissian visited Elocin's club and asked for her to loan him the [[Currency|money]]. After she refused, Calrissian offered to [[Gambling|win]] the credits in a game of [[sabacc]]. Once the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] agreed to her terms of joining her if she won, Elocin accepted Calrissian's offer of playing for the credits. During the match, the [[Galactic Empire]] arrived, having been led to the club by the [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[Narin]] who knew of the Empire's [[bounty]] on Calirssian. She wielded both a [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blaster]] and a [[DH-17 blaster pistol]], during the ensuing [[Skirmish at Elocin's club|skirmish]]. Elocin was taken captive by an [[Unidentified Imperial officer (Narin)|Imperial officer]], though Calrissian was able to save her from the [[Imperial officer|officer]]. Afterwards, Elocin captured Narin and took his information regarding the plans to Jabba's Palace and sent it to the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and informed Calrissian and Chewbacca.<ref name="Lando 1" />
