'''Darrus Jeht''', a [[Jedi|member]] of the [[Jedi Order]], was a [[Jedi General]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At the [[Battle of Kromus]] and the [[Battle of Cularin]],<ref name="Lead by Example" /> events occurring at some point between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Lead by Example]]'' indicates that the [[Battle of Kromus]] and the [[Battle of Cularin]] occurred during the same period of [[time]] as the [[Clone Wars]], which lasted from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''.</ref> Jeht was the [[commanding officer]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Maelstrom-class battle cruiser|''Maelstrom''-class battle cruiser]] [[Maelstrom (Maelstrom-class)|''Maelstrom'']], which served with distinction in both battles.<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Darrus Jeht was introduced in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead by Example" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Darrus Jeht/Legends|Darrus Jeht]] first appeared in "[[Conflict and Mastery]]," a short story written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a supplement to [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']], a [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game|roleplaying]] campaign created by [[Wizards of the Coast]]. The story was published on [[Wizards.com]] on [[March 11]], [[2004]].<ref name="Conflict and Mastery">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/livingforce/swlf20040311|text=Conflict and Mastery|int=Conflict and Mastery}}</ref>
