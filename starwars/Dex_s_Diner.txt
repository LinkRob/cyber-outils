'''Dex's Diner''' was a small [[Restaurant|eatery]] in a dilapidated industrial area of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] called [[CoCo Town]]. The diner was owned and operated by [[Dexter Jettster]], a [[Besalisk]] with a colorful background.

==Description==
[[File:Dexs_Diner_WotF.png|thumb|left|250px|Dexter Jettster serving popular cuisine from the diner kitchen]]
Serving mostly hard workers from the area, the diner offered home-style cooked [[Foodstuff|meals]] and freshly brewed [[ardees]] ("Jawa Juice"). Jettster worked as the [[Chef|cook]] and employed three [[Waiter|wait staff]], the [[human]]s [[Wanda (human)|Wanda]] and [[Hermione Bagwa]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> and their [[WA-7 waitress droid]] partner, [[FLO]].<ref name="Diner" /> Other offerings at Dex's Diner included the [[Shawda club sandwich]] for 6.7 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], served on toasted or therm-zapped [[bread]]; the [[Sic-Six-layer cake]],<ref name="whatwookiee" /> which was one of his most popular desserts,<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> served by the slice at 2.5 credits; and the [[Photon Fizzle]] at 1.7 credits. [[Credit chip]]s were accepted at Dex's Diner.<ref name="whatwookiee" />

As a result of Dex's welcoming nature and belief that he shouldn't intrude into the private affairs of his customers, Dex's Diner was a frequent meeting spot for criminals.<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

==History==
Shortly before the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] came to Dex's Diner to see if his old friend, Jettster, could identify a [[poison]]ed [[Kamino saberdart|dart]]. Jettster identified it as originating from the planet [[Kamino]], which he knew of from his [[Standard day|days]] as a [[prospector]].<ref name="AotC" />

[[File:DexsDinerAd-SWH.png|250px|thumb|right|Dex's Diner advertisement at the Grand Arena]]
Around [[16 BBY]],<ref name="Veteran Date">"[[The Veteran]]" establishes that Dex's Diner shut down shortly after [[FLO]] broke down, three [[Standard year|years]] after [[Order 66]], which corresponds to [[16 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Jettster lost the diner. At the time of its closure, Jettster presumed that he would only have to close it temporarily, but he was unable to reopen it afterwards, with the diner being left abandoned for the next two and a half decades.<ref name="Veteran">{{StoryCite|story=The Veteran|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> By the [[time]] after the [[Galactic Empire]]'s defeat in the [[Battle of Endor]], Dex's Diner was still defunct and abandoned.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

During the [[New Republic Era]], an [[author]] noted fond memories of Dex's Diner in the survival compendium "[[Species From A to Z]]."<ref name="OSWM">{{GermanFanCite|112|Database}}</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> during the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]], a [[hologram]] advertisement for Dex's Diner was displayed within [[Coruscant Underworld (battlefield)|Coruscant Underworld]],<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> a battlefield inspired by the dark [[Coruscant Underworld|underbelly]]<ref name="Explore the Coruscant Underworld">{{Hunters|url=news/explore-the-coruscant-underworld|text=Explore the Coruscant Underworld!}}</ref> of the [[city]] [[planet]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Explore the Coruscant Underworld" />

==Behind the scenes==
In ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', Dex's Diner is located in the [[Uscru District]], as CoCo Town is not accessible.

In ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'', Dex's Diner is located near the [[Jedi Temple]], as the game created the visitable areas to be small and feature certain locations.
