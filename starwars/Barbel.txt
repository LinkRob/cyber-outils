'''Barbels''',<ref name="AotG">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref> informally known as "'''chin tentacles''',"<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> were the tendril-like appendages that fringed the [[mouth]] and chin of a [[Mon Calamari]] regardless of their [[Gender|sex]].<ref name="AotG" /> Some, like [[Prince]] [[Lee-Char]], adorned their barbels with ornaments.<ref name="Prisoners">{{TCW|Prisoners}}</ref> The barbels were an indicator of youth when only one was present on the chin,<ref name="LoG Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lair-of-grievous-episode-guide|text="Lair of Grievous" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> and this made elder Mon Calamari look bearded.<ref name="AotG" /> Similarly, the [[Gungan]]s of [[Naboo]] grew fleshy chin whiskers as they aged.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>
