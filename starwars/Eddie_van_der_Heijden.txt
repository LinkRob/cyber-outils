'''Eddie van der Heijden''' is one of the contributors to the [[2009]] ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', in which he worked with [[Nathan Butler]] to formulate timelines for the guide. In the guide, his name was misspelled Hejiden.<ref name="Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> Nathan P. Butler's ''Star Wars Timeline Gold'' attributes the [[Great ReSynchronization]] dates for the three [[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order|The New Jedi Order]] maps printed in the Atlas to van der Heijden.
