'''Cordota''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="TRS" /> located in [[Lantillian sector]]'s [[Cordota system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and [[the Slice]],<ref name="TheSlice" /> situated in grid square P-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. A [[hyperspace route]] connected it to<ref name="AcolyteVG" />the [[Planet|worlds]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|nix-jerd|Nix Jerd}}</ref><ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Lantillies]] and [[Bracca]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> During the start of [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]]'s career, she at one point worked as a cub reporter covering [[micro-g basket]] weaving on Cordota at the behest of her [[Rhil Dairo's producer|producer]], among contributing to other stories.<ref name="TRS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Dairo reflected on how far she had progressed in her profession while working with the [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]], thinking about how the significance of her work had increased since her [[time]] on the astronomical object.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cordota was first mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref>
