The '''Cylo Chemist''' was a costume owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Chemist|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|black]], gray, dark blue, light blue, and gold color scheme, with golden patterns lining the apron.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cylo Chemist was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Skora, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.18.0 within the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cylo Applicator]]. Unlike most cosmetic sets in the game which are added at the same time, the Cylo Applicator was not added in 0.18.0.<ref name="Hunters" />
