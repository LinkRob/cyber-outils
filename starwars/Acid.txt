'''Acid''' was a tough corrosive substance used to break down objects. One notable type of acid was [[xenoboric acid]], which was used by refineries to break down [[thorilide]] during the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]]. Xenoboric acid was capable of killing sentient beings.<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> [[Luthen Rael]] poured caustic acid on the switchboard of his [[fractal radio]] before [[Raid on the Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest|his arrest]], erasing any links to his contacts.<ref name="Make It Stop">{{Andor|Make It Stop}}</ref>
