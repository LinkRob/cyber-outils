The '''''Albedo Brave'''''<ref name="Art" /> was a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] in the [[Republic Navy|navy]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> It served as the [[command ship]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Jaro Tapal]] and his [[13th Battalion]] of [[clone trooper]]s,<ref name="Art" /> who led a [[Bracca Invasion|campaign]] to defend the [[Bracca system]] from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Shortly after the campaign, [[Order 66]] was executed, forcing Tapal and his [[Padawan]], [[Cal Kestis]], to [[Skirmish aboard the Albedo Brave|escape]] from their clone [[trooper]]s. Tapal sabotaged the [[reactor]] of the ''Albedo Brave'' to conceal their escape, but was fatally wounded before he could board an [[escape pod]]. Kestis escaped the ''Albedo Brave'' before its destruction, landing on [[Bracca]], where he would remain for the next five years.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
