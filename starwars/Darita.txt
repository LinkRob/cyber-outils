'''Darita''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] as the [[Squad leader|squadron leader]] of [[Squadron Two (Shadow Wing)|Squadron Two]]. During her time with Shadow Wing, she and [[Yrica Quell]] provided mutual support for each other, despite hardly knowing each other. In<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY" /> she was killed during the [[New Republic]]'s [[Battle of Troithe|attack on Troithe]], while trying to defend Shadow Wing as they made their escape from [[Troithe]]. Her squadron was to be the last aboard the [[Yadeez|bulk freighter]] seized by Shadow Wing, and she fell while escorting it.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Her sister was [[Lieutenant]] [[Darita (lieutenant)|Darita]], and one of them used the first name Shay. Darita was married to a [[Darita's husband|man]] who resided on [[Jakku]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
