A '''company''' of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[327th Star Corps]] was led by [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] and [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bly]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During a [[mission to Escander]], the [[company]] was ambushed by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[battle droid]]s on [[speeder]]s. The [[clone trooper]]s fought back and repulsed the ambush, but many [[trooper]]s were injured. As a result, [[General]] Secura ordered them to retreat to their [[starship]] while she and others pressed forward. Since [[Commander]] Bly was also injured, [[Unidentified clone trooper (327th Star Corps)|another trooper]] led the rest of the company.<ref name="Destiny" />

After Secura attacked the [[Escander Separatist base|Separatist installation]], the trooper ordered the rest of the company to hold their position. After some time, Securra had not returned, so the trooper ordered the others to remain at their posts while the [[Cloning|clone]] snuck into the base and rescued their Jedi General. The company then retreated when [[Asajj Ventress]] fought against Secura. The clone trooper [[Flash]] was also part of the company.<ref name="Destiny" />
