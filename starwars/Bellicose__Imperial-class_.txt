The '''''Bellicose''''' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] in the [[Galactic Empire]] that was [[captain]]ed by [[Commander]] [[Tylux]]. It was destroyed while chasing a [[Unidentified rebel spy (Bellicose)|spy]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The ''Bellicose'' was part of a [[fleet]] that consisted of at least five ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers.

==Characteristics==
[[File:BellicoseBridgeCrew-VaderDarkVisions2.png|thumb|left|200px|The bridge crew of the ''Bellicose'']]
The ''Bellicose'' was an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Visions 2" /> and, as such, was manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]]<ref name="HtH" /> and was 1,600 [[meter]]s in length.<ref name="DB ISD" /> It was armed with sixty [[turbolaser]]s, sixty heavy [[ion cannon]] emplacements, and ten [[tractor beam projector]]s.<ref name="HtH" /> The ''Bellicose'' was equipped with three KDY [[Destroyer-I ion engine]]s and four emergency use [[Cygnus Spaceworks]] [[Gemon-4 ion engine]]s. It was also equipped with a [[solar ionization reactor]], two [[deflector shield generator]] domes, and a [[communications tower]].<ref name="SWCT" /> It could reach speeds of 975 [[Kilometer per hour|kph]]<ref name="HtH" /> and 60 [[Megalight per hour|MGLT]].<ref name="Graphics" />

As an ''Imperial''-class, the ''Bellicose'' maintained a complement of twenty [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Galactic Civil War)|AT-ATs]], thirty [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-STs]], eight [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttles]],<ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters">The ''Bellicose'' was of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class line]], which carries 20 AT-ATs, 30 AT-STs, 8 ''Lambda''-class shuttles, 9,200 officers and 27,850 enlisted crew according to ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''. Since the ''Bellicose'' is of this class, it must also share these characteristics.</ref> two [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class crusiers]],<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and seventy-two [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] [[starfighter]]s,<ref name="TIE models set" /> which could be deployed from its two [[hangar]] bays.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' demonstrates that ''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers have two [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] as part of their complement and two [[hangar]] bays. Since the ''Bellicose'' is of this class, it must share these characteristics.</ref> The ''Bellicose'' also utilized a [[hyperdrive]]<ref name="Visions 2" /> and had consumables for two [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="AbEverything">The ''Bellicose'' was of the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class line]], which carries 9,700 [[stormtrooper]]s and has two [[Standard year|years]] of consumables, according to ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.</ref> It was maintained by a crew consisting of 9,200 [[officer]]s, 27,850 enlisted, and 9,700 [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Encyclopedia Starfighters" />

==Role==
The ''Bellicose'' served as a [[warship]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], being part of a [[fleet]] that consisted of four other [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="Visions 2" />

==History==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Bellicose'' served under the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]] as part of a [[fleet]]. It was [[captain]]ed by [[Commander]] [[Tylux]] when it participated in a skirmish against [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] forces. At the occasion, the Star Destroyer stopped in the mouth of an [[Exogorth]] in a moment.<ref name="Visions 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Bellicose'' debuted in the [[2019]] [[canon]] comic book [[Vader - Dark Visions 2|''Vader - Dark Visions'' 2]], which was written by [[Dennis Hallum]] and pencilled by [[Brian Level]].<ref name="Visions 2" />
