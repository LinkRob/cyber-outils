"'''Frog Lady'''" was the nickname of an [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[Gender|female]] [[Querm Rybet]] who was escorted by [[Din Djarin]].

==Biography==
[[File:FrogLady-TMCh5.png|thumb|left|270px|Frog Lady conversing with another patron in Chalmun's Cantina]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> a [[Querm Rybet]]<ref name="SWE" /> nicknamed "Frog Lady" was on [[Tatooine]] in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] when [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] entered the [[cantina]] in search of work.<ref name="Chapter 5">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref> Some time later she talked to [[Peli Motto]] about getting an escort from the desert [[planet]] to the [[Water|estuary]] [[moon]] of [[Trask]] because she had [[egg]]s that needed to be fertilized by [[Frog Man|her husband]]. Peli enlisted Djarin to escort her. Djarin helped but asked for information regarding other Mandalorians seen on Trask. During their [[Mission to Trask|journey]], Frog Lady slept in the cockpit, unaware that [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] took an interest in her eggs and ate some of them.<ref name="Chapter 10" />

After crashing on [[Maldo Kreis]], Frog Lady and Djarin clashed regarding what to do next, in part due to their [[language]] barrier. Frog Lady seemed able to speak only her native tongue. Frog Lady did prove to be technically adept, however, as she repaired the remnants of [[Q9-0]] well enough to use his [[vocabulator]] as a translation device, enabling her to persuade Din Djarin to keep his word.<ref name="Chapter 10" />

[[File:SpacefarerMealtime-TM10.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Frog Lady needed to safely transport her eggs, but Djarin's ship was stranded on Maldo Kreis.]]
With their resolve renewed, Frog Lady later ventured out into the surrounding ice caves. There, she located and bathed in a natural hot spring, which allowed her to incubate her remaining eggs. The peace did not last, however, as their presence eventually roused the many ice spiders in the den, including the menacingly large parents of the hatchlings. In fleeing the spiders, Frog Lady revealed a prehensile [[tongue]], that could be used to grasp objects. She also moved more speedily by leaping using all four legs, so as to better flee [[knobby white ice spider]]s.<ref name="Chapter 10" />

After escaping Maldo Kreis, Frog Lady assisted Din Djarin in a landing attempt on Trask by pulling a lever to stabilize the thrusters, but it didn't work and they ended up in the water. Once they got pulled out, Frog Lady left the [[Razor Crest|ship]]. Shortly after she heard her husband's voice at the docks, the couple finally reunited, with both husband and wife thanking Din for his aid.<ref name="Chp11" />

After Din Djarin agreed to help [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] hijack a [[Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|''Gozanti''-class Assault Carrier]], Frog Lady looked after Grogu for a time while her eggs began to hatch. Frog Lady and her husband were later playing with Grogu and one of their hatched offspring when Din returned to fetch Grogu. He thanked her for looking after the Foundling. Frog Lady and her husband continued to bond over their offspring as Djarin and Grogu departed.<ref name="Chp11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref>

==Powers and abilities==
Frog Lady was an excellent shot and a skilled [[droidsmith]].<ref name="TMH">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref>

==Equipment==
Frog Lady wielded a [[Shard-3A holdout cluster blaster]].<ref name="TMH" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Frog Lady" first appeared in "[[Chapter 5: The Gunslinger]]," the fifth episode of [[The Mandalorian Season One|season one]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Chapter 5" /> and she played a larger role in "[[Chapter 10: The Passenger]]," the second episode of [[The Mandalorian Season Two|season two]]. She was portrayed in costume by [[Misty Rosas]]. Voice performance was provided by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Chapter 10" /> During the scene in which Frog Lady hopped away from the attacking ice spiders, a [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|CGI]] version of the character was instead used to give a feeling of sloppiness instead of athleticism.<ref name="Disney Gallery">{{DisneyGallery|Disney Gallery: The Mandalorian: Making of Season Two|Making of Season Two}}</ref>
