The '''Eleutherian Plaza''' was a large plaza in [[Hanna City]] that housed the [[Chandrila Senate house]] following the [[Battle of Endor]]. The Chandrila Senate house housed the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] between [[4 ABY]] and [[5 ABY]]. It was near the [[Old Gather-House]] and the [[Senate Plaza (Chandrila)|Senate Plaza]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
