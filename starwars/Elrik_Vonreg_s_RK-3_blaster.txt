A customized [[Color|red]]-accented '''RK-3 blaster''' was utilized by [[First Order]] [[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]]. The [[RK-3 blaster|pistol]] was manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] and [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]]. In [[34 ABY]], after the First Order hired [[pirate]] [[Kragan Gorr]] and his [[Warbird gang]] to [[Torra Doza's abduction|abduct]] the [[pilot]] [[Torra Doza]], Vonreg employed the [[weapon]] to betray the pirates aboard their [[sail barge]], the ''[[Galleon]]''. The [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] shot the pirates [[Drell]] and [[Skreek]] during the [[blaster fight]] and retrieved Doza from her captors.

==Description==
[[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]]'s [[Imperial Era|Imperial-era]] [[RK-3 blaster]] was customized<ref name="Xwing" /> and featured a [[Color|red]] finish to match his [[Vonreg's red armour|crimson armor]]. The one-[[Limb|handed]] [[weapon]] featured white indicator lights at the back of the [[Blaster pistol|pistol]] to indicate the weapon's power levels. Vonreg's [[pistol]] had pinpoint precision and discharged red [[blaster]] bolts.<ref name="USWNE" />

==History==
{{Quote|Good evening, Kragan. Commander Pyre sends his regards.|Elrik Vonreg, to the Warbird Gang|audio=VonregKraganRegards-TheDozaDilemma.flac|<ref name="DD" />}}
The blaster was manufactured by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] and [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]] and was utilized by the [[First Order Special Forces]] [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] Elrik Vonreg.<ref name="USWNE" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">{{ResistanceDate|1|14}}</ref> the [[First Order]] secretly hired the [[pirate]] [[Kragan Gorr]] and his [[Warbird gang]] to [[Torra Doza's abduction|abduct]] the [[pilot]] [[Torra Doza]] from the ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]] on the [[planet]] [[Castilon]] to entice [[Imanuel Doza]]&mdash;the [[Supertanker fuel depot|station's]] [[captain]] and Torra's [[Doza family|father]]&mdash;into hiring the First Order to protect the [[Space station|station]] from raids from the very same pirates. After the Warbirds successfully captured Torra, the pirate leader contacted [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] of the First Order to inform him of the mission's success. Pyre then arranged for Vonreg to deliver the pirates' [[Galactic Credit Standard|payment]] to their [[sail barge]],<ref name="DD" /> the ''[[Galleon]]''.<ref name="USWNE" />

[[File:BetrayingPirates-TheDozaDilemma.jpg|thumb|left|265px|Elrik Vonreg shoots Drell with his RK-3 blaster.]]
When the major arrived and disembarked from a [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|shuttle]], he was welcomed by the pirates [[Drell]], [[Skreek]], and [[Valik]] with a captured Doza. Vonreg greeted Gorr before revealing his pistol and opening [[Blaster fight|fire]] on the Warbirds. The major successfully shot Drell, knocking<ref name="DD" /> him<ref name="DB">{{Databank|drell|Drell}}</ref> unconscious, and Skreek, knocking<ref name="DD" /> him<ref name="Tobias">{{EncyclopediaCite|Tobias Beckett and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref> into the [[ocean]] of Castilon<ref name="DD" />&mdash;though both survived the attack.<ref name="Tobias" /> Vonreg then escorted Doza to the [[shuttle]] while continuing to fire at Gorr's pirates. After leaving the skirmish, the [[Starship|shuttle]] returned to the ''Colossus'' station, where Vonreg returned Torra to her [[Parent|father]].<ref name="DD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The blaster first appeared in "[[The Doza Dilemma]],"<ref name="DD" /> the fourteenth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' animated television series<ref name="EpNum">{{InsiderCite|190|Resistance Revisited|''Resistance'' Revisited}}</ref> which premiered on [[January 27]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> Prior to the episode's release, the pistol was first pictured in the article "NYCC 2018: Hasbro Joins the ''Resistance''" that was posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 6]], [[2018]]. The article featured images of the [[Hasbro]] action figure for Elrik Vonreg from the [[Star Wars Resistance (toy line)|''Star Wars Resistance'']] line,<ref name="Hasbro">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2018-hasbro-star-wars-resistance|text=NYCC 2018: Hasbro Joins the ''Resistance''|author=[[Bria LaVorgna]]|date=2018-10-06}}</ref> which released in [[December]] later that year.<ref name="HasbroRelease">{{Rebelscum|url=Major-Vonreg-Star-Wars-Universe-Resistance-Hasbro.asp|text=Major Vonreg ''Star Wars Resistance'' 3.75-inch action figure}}</ref> The blaster included with Vonreg's figure omitted its red-colored accents.<ref name="Hasbro" /> Concept art for the pistol was created by [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Aaron Pham]] on [[July 27]], [[2017]].<ref name="EpGuide">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance-the-doza-dilemma-concept-art-gallery|text="The Doza Dilemma" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> The model for the blaster was used as the source for another RK-3 blaster, titled "blasterFoA."<ref name="Disappeared">{{SW|url=star-wars-resistance-the-disappeared-concept-art-gallery|text="The Disappeared" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref>
