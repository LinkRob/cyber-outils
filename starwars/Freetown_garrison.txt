The '''Freetown garrison''' was a force of volunteers raised from the town of [[Freetown]] during the [[War against the Pykes|gang war]] between the [[Pyke Syndicate]] and the [[Fett gotra]].

==History==
{{Quote|Where'd all these sand scurriers come from?''"<br />"''We're here to save the tails of some city rats.|Skad and Jo|<ref name="Chapter 7">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref>}}
[[File:BattleForMosEspa-BoBFCh7.png|thumb|left|300px|The garrison fights the Pykes alongside Fett's gotra.]]
Following Marshall [[Cobb Vanth]]'s run-in with several [[Pyke]] couriers, Vanth and his deputy [[Scott]] were approached by the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]]. Djarin, remembering how Freetown's citizens were good fighters against the [[Krayt dragon]],<ref name="TM Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref> asked Vanth to organize the citizens into a garrison to help [[Daimyo]] [[Boba Fett]] and his Gotra in fighting the Pykes. Vanth was unsure if the citizens would help the Gotra in fighting the Pykes at [[Mos Espa]]. After Djarin left, Vanth considered his offer. However, Vanth and Scott were gunned down by [[Cad Bane]] under the orders of the Pykes. Bane informed them that Tatooine belonged to the Pykes.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

Despite Vanth being out of action, Freetown's citizens formed a small garrison. Using a landspeeder, the garrison assisted Fett, Djarin, [[Krrsantan]], and the [[Mods]] in pushing the Pykes back in Mos Espa.<ref name="Chapter 7" />
