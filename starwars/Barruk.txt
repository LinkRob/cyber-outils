'''Barruk''' was a member of the [[Guardians of the Whills]] [[Religion|religious order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Barruk was stationed at the [[Second Spire]] in the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] of the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] while it held a [[Summit on Jedha|peace summit]] between the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War|warring]] [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]s [[Eiram]] and [[E'ronoh]]. When an [[Assassination attempt on the ambassadors|explosion]] rocked the Second Spire, Barruk and other Guardians, including [[Selik]] and [[Jance]], ran to the source and discovered that [[Eirami]] [[Ambassador]] [[Lian Cerox]] had been injured. Selik then sent Barruk and Jance to guard and escort [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] as he brought Ambassador Cerox to the Spire's [[Second Spire medical station|medical station]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Barruk appeared in the audio drama ''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]'', written by [[George Mann]]<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> and released on [[January 3]], [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2023}}</ref> The exact spelling of the character's name was unconfirmed until the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha (script)|script]]<ref name="Battle of Jedha script" /> on [[February 14]] that year.<ref name="Reader's Guide" />
