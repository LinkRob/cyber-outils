'''Dagobah Slug Syrup''' was a [[syrup]] that imitated the [[Slime|sticky secretions]] of [[swamp slug]]s,<ref name="Cookbook" /> a [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Dagobah]].<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The slime of the [[Slug (creature)|slugs]] was [[sugar]]y with hints of the fragrant [[Plant|herbs]] that the slugs had [[Diet|digested]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> It was prized by [[Bartender|mixologists]] and cooks,<ref name="Traveler's Guide to Batuu">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> including the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], who described it as "unusually saccharine" and poured it over [[Foodstuff|desserts]] or mixed it into drinks.<ref name="Cookbook" />

Tuggs developed an imitation of the slime that used [[Standard day|everyday]] herbs, sugar, and [[water]] to make a clear liquid infused with sweetness and herbaceous flavor, suitable for use as a substitute syrup when the slugs were in short supply.<ref name="Cookbook" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs authored ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', which included his recipes for Dagobah Slug Syrup and two [[cocktail]]s that were sweetened with the syrup, [[Photon Fizzle]] and [[Dagobah Slug Slinger]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dagobah Slug Syrup was introduced in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe uses the ingredients [[Wikipedia:Sucrose|sugar]], water, fresh [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]], and a sprig of [[Wikipedia:Rosemary|rosemary]]. The rosemary and ginger are strained out of the syrup after it is infused with their flavors.<ref name="Cookbook" />

For the Dagobah Slug Slinger, the syrup is used to sweeten other ingredients to make a cocktail that is garnished with another sprig of rosemary. In the Photon Fizzle, the Dagobah Slug Syrup is used with [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|food coloring gel]] to steep [[Wikipedia:Tapioca balls|boba pearls]] and turn them green.<ref name="Cookbook" />
