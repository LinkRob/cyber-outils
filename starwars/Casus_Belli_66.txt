'''''Casus Belli'' 66''' was a French roleplaying magazine from November of [[1991]]. It contained many roleplaying articles, including three adventures for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|D6]] system. The articles were called "[[Le dieu mort]]" (in English "The dead God"), "[[Dans la nuit des temps]]" ("In the mists of time") and "[[Le trésor oublié]]" ("The forgotten Treasure"), all written by [[Jean Balczesak]].
