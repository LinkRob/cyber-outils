'''Fe Remine''' was a [[Kessarine]] [[Gender|male]] who was the son of [[Ferize]], [[Er Dal]], and [[Fel Ix]]. When he was born<ref name="POD" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he was put through a [[Naming ceremony]] that inducted him into the [[Path of the Open Hand]], a [[Religion|cult]] that his parents were members of. That same year, the [[Path compound]] on [[Dalna]] where they all lived was struck by a severe flood, and Fe Remine was stuck behind a rock with Er Dal underground, with Ferize trying to get to them. However, after Fel Ix pointed them in the right direction, their friend and fellow Path member [[Marda Ro]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] saved Fe Remine and Er Dal and got them to the surface.<ref name="POD" />
