'''Droid ball fighting''' was a game in which [[Fighting droid|spheroid droids]] bashed against each other in a square arena table. Droids of the same [[color]] were able to clump together to form fiercer fighters. The outcome of a droid ball fighting match could be the subject of [[Gambling|bets]]. At the time of the [[Cold War]], a droid ball fighting table was installed in the [[Takodana Castle|castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]] on [[Takodana]].<ref name="The Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
