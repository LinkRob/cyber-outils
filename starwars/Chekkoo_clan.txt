The '''Chekkoo clan''' was a [[Rodian]] clan on the [[planet]] [[Rodia]]. The clan was based in the [[Chekkoo Enclave]] on the continent of [[Betu]]. Members of the Chekkoo clan were known to be skilled in manufacturing weapons, [[armor]], and other related munitions. [[Laneet Chekkoo]] was a member of this clan.<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Chekkoo clan opposed Rodia's ruling [[Chattza clan]]. [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] believed that the clan would be willing to rebel against the Empire just to spite the Chattza clan. Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Luke Skywalker]] went on a [[Mission to Rodia (Luke Skywalker)|mission]] to Betu to recruit the Chekkoo clan into the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi" />
