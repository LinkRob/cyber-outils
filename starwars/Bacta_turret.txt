'''Bacta turrets''' were a type of custom automated defensive emplacement designed by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] for use in the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition hosted on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. These [[turret]]s could expel [[bacta]], healing Sprocket's allies within its vicinity while active.<ref name="Kay" />

{{Tech-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The bacta turret was designed by concept artist [[Anna Kay]]<ref name="Kay" /> for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> as a part of the playable character Sprocket's potential ability kit, though this particular turret never made it into the final release of the game.<ref name="Kay" />
