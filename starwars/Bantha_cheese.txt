==Behind the scenes==
Bantha cheese was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2019]] young readers reference book ''[[Star Wars: Creatures Big & Small]]'' written by [[Calliope Glass]] and [[Caitlin Kennedy]].<ref name="Creatures Big Small" />

Bantha cheese first appeared in the non-[[canon]] crossover special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'',<ref name="Phineas">''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''</ref> which premiered on [[July 26]], [[2014]].<ref name="Reporter">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/live-feed/phineas-ferbs-star-wars-special-711433|text='Phineas and Ferb's' 'Star Wars' Special Lands Premiere Date|author=Ng, Philiana|date=2014-06-12|work=The Hollywood Reporter|archivedate=20201205100409}}</ref> It was used as one of the primary ingredients of a [[bantha cheese hoagie]].<ref name="Phineas" />
