The '''Fial system''' was a [[star system]] situated within the [[Auril sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was coreward of the [[Murkhana system]]; however it was still considered far removed from major [[Hyperspace route|hyperlanes]] such as the [[Perlemian Trade Route]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] had no [[Imperial Military|military]] presence in the system, although the nearby [[Belderone system]] was [[Belderone Imperial naval base|heavily fortified]].<ref name="Trkn" />
