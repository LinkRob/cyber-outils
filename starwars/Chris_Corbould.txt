'''Chris Corbould''' is a veteran [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] [[Wikipedia:Special effects supervisor|special effects coordinator]] who has worked on many blockbuster films, including eleven [[Wikipedia:James Bond (film series)|''James Bond'']] films. Corbould was nominated for the [[Wikipedia:Academy Award for Best Visual Effects|Academy Award for Best Visual Effects]] for his work on [[Wikipedia:The Dark Knight (film)|''The Dark Knight'']] ([[2008]]) and won the award for his work on [[Wikipedia:Inception|''Inception'']] ([[2010]]).

Corbould served as special effects supervisor for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. He was nominated for an [[Wikipedia:Academy Awards|Oscar]] for his work.
