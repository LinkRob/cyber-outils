The '''''Fast Friend''''' was a [[starship]] owned and [[captain]]ed by the [[Devaronian]] [[smuggler]], [[Kurjak]]. In [[0 ABY]], Kurjak was hired by [[Del Meeko]] and [[Gideon Hask]], members of [[Inferno Squad]] who were posing as brothers, to deliver them and a container to [[Eriadu]]. En route to their destination, the ''Fast Friend'' was attacked and boarded by the ''[[Opportunity]]'', a [[pirate]] ship belonging to the [[Blood Bone Order]] and their "[[Pirate Queen]]", [[Lassa Rhayme]].<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
