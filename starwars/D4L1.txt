'''D4L1''' was a [[data processing droid]] owned by [[Xira]], a member of the [[Free Ryloth Movement]]. During the movement's [[Mission to Ryloth|attempt]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], he aided Xira in coordinating an attack on the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Perilous]]''. He was topped with a silver dome.<ref name="LOTS" />
