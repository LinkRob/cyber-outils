The '''Flame-Wielders''' were a group who fought against the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Battle of Nar Shaddaa]],<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> which took place by [[68 BBY]] on<ref name="68 BBY" /> the [[moon]]<ref name="BtMF">{{BuildFalconCite|81|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Nar Shaddaa]]. After being [[Fire|immolated]] by the Flame-Wielders, [[Jedi Master]] [[Crucitorn]] managed to [[endure]] the pain and continued to fight back.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around 68 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="68 BBY" /> Jedi Master [[Dooku]] mentioned the Flame-Wielders when demonstrating pain endurance to his [[Padawan]], [[Qui-Gon Jinn]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

Dooku later told his [[House Serenno|sister]], [[Jenza]], about his conversation with Jinn in a [[holo-letter]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave several [[Datacard|data disks]] containing his messages to Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his [[Gender|sister's]] location, and she listened to the holo-disk that mentioned the Flame-Wielders.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Flame-Wielders were mentioned in a flashback in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The spelling of the Flame-Wielders was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="DJL script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
