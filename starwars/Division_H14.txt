'''Division H14''' was a division of the [[Tagge Corporation]] that was part of a branch on [[Korfo II]].<ref name="Aphra 16" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 16]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[Gender|female]] individual [[Sira Folsomn]] served as [[Assistant Packaging and Fulfillment Manager]] of the division until her concurrent employment by [[Crimson Dawn]] was discovered by the company and she was brought before [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]'s<ref name="Aphra 16" /> [[secretary]]<ref name="WOTBH Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> [[Lapin Tagge]]<ref name="Aphra 28">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 28|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 28]]</ref> for a performance review. Folsomn attempted to [[Death|assassinate]] Tagge during the review, though she was unsuccessful and ultimately killed by Tagge.<ref name="Aphra 16" />
