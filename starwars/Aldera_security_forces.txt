The [[city]] of [[Aldera]] on the [[planet]] of [[Alderaan]] maintained highly skilled '''security forces''' that served as a [[law enforcement agency]]. It was notable for its deployment of non-lethal policing techniques and added to Alderaan's reputation of being a peaceful world. Visitors who proved to be troublesome were often not tolerated for long, and difficult criminals were quickly detained or escorted off world.<ref name="Dawn" />

Although the techniques used added to the pacifist demonstrations of Alderaan, it also allowed for a good ruse for covertly escorting [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|rebel]] craft without attracting [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] attention.<ref name="Dawn" />
