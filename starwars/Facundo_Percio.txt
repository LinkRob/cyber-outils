'''Facundo Percio''' is an Argentine artist who provided artwork for two issues of [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse's]] [[Star Wars (Dark Horse Comics 2013)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series.

Percio also pencilled [[Wikipedia:Warren Ellis|Warren Ellis's]] [[Wikipedia:Anna Mercury|''Anna Mercury'']], and the ten-issue comic series [[Wikipedia:Fashion Beast|''Fashion Beast'']] written by [[Wikipedia:Alan Moore|Alan Moore]], [[Wikipedia:Malcolm McLaren|Malcolm McLaren]], and [[Wikipedia:Antony Johnston|Antony Johnston]]. His studio is located in [[Wikipedia:Villa Devoto|Villa Devoto]], a district of [[Wikipedia:Buenos Aires|Buenos Aires]], [[Wikipedia:Argentina|Argentina]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.lanacion.com.ar/lifestyle/facundo-percio-las-mujeres-me-dieron-mucho-trabajo-en-la-vida-nid1542999|text=Facundo Percio: "Las mujeres me dieron mucho trabajo en la vida"|author=Varise, Franco|date=2013-01-05|archiveurl=https://archive.is/wKLFM}}</ref>
