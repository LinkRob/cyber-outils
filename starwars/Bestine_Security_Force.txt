The '''Bestine Security Force''', or simply '''Bestine Security''', was the [[law enforcement agency]] on the [[planet]] [[Bestine IV]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Sergeant]] [[Mey Kilrow]] was a corrupted member of the security force, as she had a protection deal with the [[Nova Razors]], allowing them to operate and continue to be the planet's most ruthless gang.<ref name="Knights of Fate" />
