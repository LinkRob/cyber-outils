The '''''Arquitens''-class command cruiser''', also known as the '''Imperial light cruiser''', was a multipurpose light cruiser used by the [[Imperial Navy]], in which it filled roles from detached [[Patrol ship|patrol vessel]] to communications ship to [[capital ship]] support. It was a reworking of [[Kuat Drive Yards]]' [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]], though they shared only structural similarities.

==Characteristics==
The ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser<ref name="Imperial Light Cruiser Expansion Pack" /> was manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] for the [[Imperial Navy]], initially entering service<ref name="Dawn" /> as a reworking of the [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruiser]] which had served with the [[Republic Navy]].<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/rebel-resolve-trivia-gallery|text="Rebel Resolve" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> The multipurpose [[light cruiser]] design served in a variety on roles, protecting remote [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] assets, performing detached [[Patrol ship|patrol]] duties in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], acting as support to [[Capital ship|larger]] [[battle group]]s, serving as an armed [[transport]],<ref name="Dawn" /> and as a communications ship.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

[[File:Heavy-Arquitens-SWS.png|thumb|left|200px|A heavily armed ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser with additional weapons emplacements]]
Measuring 230 [[meter]]s in length,<ref name="Ships of Empire and Rebellion" /> the command cruiser had several subtle updates in its design despite appearing to be a simple repaint of its predecessor;<ref name="Trivia Gallery" /> in reality they shared few characteristics besides the basic hull shape—kite-shaped hulls with two forward spars—and the class name.<ref name="Dawn" /> Structural changes included an enclosed horizontal housing on the three powerful main [[engine]]s, as well as the shape of its [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] tower.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" /><ref name="Dawn" /> The Republic variant's side-mounted [[turbolaser]] batteries were removed and replaced with eight [[escape pod]]s,<ref name="Rebel Resolve Concept Art Gallery" /> and lateral entry ways near the bow allowed the deployment of Imperial [[Jumptrooper]]s.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" /> The vessels also featured a detention block.<ref name="TIE">{{Rebels|Through Imperial Eyes}}</ref>

The ''Arquitens''-class was armed with four turbolaser batteries, eight [[Quad laser cannon|quad laser batteries]] mounted on four [[turret]]s&mdash;two dorsal and two ventral&mdash;four forward-mounted [[concussion missile launcher]]s on the port and starboard<ref name="Dawn" /> and a [[tractor beam projector]].<ref name="Blood Sisters">{{Rebels|Blood Sisters}}</ref> However, an ''Arquitens''-class could also be armed with three dorsal quad laser battery turrets, two ventral turrets, and six twin laser-cannon turrets: two near the engines at the dorsal and ventral hull, and the other four above the port and starboard docking tubes on the dorsal and ventral sides. It additionally had a dorsal missile launcher and four concussion-missile launchers.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

Under the [[Class 546 program]], an ''Arquitens'' could be adapted for greater speed by becoming a [[Class 546 Cruiser]].<ref name="Battles that Changed" /> The ''Arquitens''-class was protected by [[deflector shield]]s<ref name="Rebel Resolve" /> and equipped with a class 2 [[hyperdrive]] with a class 12 backup.<ref name="Dawn" /> The cruiser's small craft launch and recovery systems, positioned between the two spars,<ref name="Dawn" /><ref name="Rebel Resolve" /> could dock either a [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]],<ref name="Rebel Resolve" /> two [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s,<ref name="Legends of the Lasat" /> [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]],<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" /> [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter]]s,<ref name="ITNOTR" /> or three [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" />

==History==
Several [[Standard year|years]] prior to [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Date-4BBY">"[[Rebel Resolve]]," the first appearance of the ''Arquitens''-class command cruisers, directly follows the events of "[[Call to Action]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 BBY]]. ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'' states the conversion to the command cruiser variant happened "several years ago". As the sourcebook's in-universe perspective is apparently around the time of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', it is assumed here that the conversion took "several years" before the cruisers first appearance.</ref> the Imperial Navy recalled all of the ''Arquitens''-class light cruisers still in service to Kuat Drive Yards, where they would be refitted as part of a service life extension program. The result, the ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser, featured radically improved speed, agility, combat ability, power output, and other systems, in line with the navy's order of battle. Though this generation of refits would eventually be retired due to age, they would be replaced by production-line command cruiser variants that served well into the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Dawn" />

[[File:Shuttle_and_Cruiser.png|thumb|right|250px|A ''Sentinel''-class shuttle docking with an ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]]
About four [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], after [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] ordered the [[Raid on the communications tower|destruction]] of the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]'s [[Imperial Communications Center]], the local [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial Military|forces]] used an ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser<ref name="Rebel Resolve" /> known as the ''[[Tellstar]]''<ref name="BMF 60">{{BuildFalconCite|60|Starship Fact File}}</ref> to capture and relay information from surface [[R4 courier droid|courier droids]] brought aboard.<ref name="Imperial Light Cruiser" /> It was [[Action at Mustafar|infiltrated]] by a team of Lothal-based rebels known as the [[Spectres]] to discover the location of [[Kanan Jarrus]], a [[Jedi]] who survived the [[Order 66|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]] at the end of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

Several command cruisers responded to an [[Mission to steal Imperial shield generators|attack]] by [[Phoenix Cell]] on an Imperial [[convoy]].<ref name="Siege of Lothal">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref> Later, during the [[Blockade of Ibaar|blockade]] of [[Ibaar]], at least three of them worked alongside a pair of [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] under the command of [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] to prevent Phoenix Squadron from delivering relief supplies to the starving Ibaarans. The Imperial blockade drove off the first run of Phoenix Squadron, but was broken in the second run when the rebel fleet was joined by the [[Prototype B6|prototype B-wing fighter]], which possessed a beam cannon strong enough to pierce the shielding and armored plating of one of the blockades' three ''Arquitens''-classes. The command cruiser's destruction allowed the rebel cell to make their run and drop the relief supplies before jumping to [[hyperspace]] once again.<ref name="Wings of the Master" />

Above the planet [[Garel]], a command cruiser was on patrol in the system until they received word of a [[Skirmish in the Garel City Spaceport|theft]] of a [[GX1 short hauler|shuttle]] from [[Garel City Spaceport]] and was deployed to investigate. Arriving in orbit above Garel the Imperials found the aforementioned [[transport]] docked with the ''[[Shadow Caster]]''; opening a channel with the ships, the Imperials demanded them to surrender and prepare for boarding. Unknown to the Imperials, the shuttle was taken by Lothal rebel operative [[Sabine Wren]] who specialized in explosives and was working alongside her old friend, the bounty hunter [[Ketsu Onyo]], Wren devised a plan to escape by overloading the shuttles' hyperdrive while escaping aboard the ''Shadow Caster''. Wren alerted the ''Arquitens''-class that their ships were damaged and their hyperdrive was in danger of self-destructing; buying the rebels time to plan for an escape on the ''Shadow Caster''. Wren then planted explosive charges on the shuttles' control panel and re-activated the [[Unidentified RX-series pilot droid (Garel)|shuttles' droid pilot]], telling him that his ship was going to come under attack by the command cruiser, and suggesting for it to enact emergency protocols to escape destruction. Wren evacuated aboard the ''Shadow Caster'' with Onyo, [[C1-10P|Chopper]], and [[EG-86]] while the GX1 shuttle was caught in a [[tractor beam]] and dragged towards the command cruiser before exploding. The resulting explosion damaged the command cruiser's tractor beam projector, allowing the ''Shadow Caster'' to break free and escape to hyperspace.<ref name="Blood Sisters" />

Two ''Arquitens''-class command cruisers were assigned as an escort detail for the prototype [[Interdictor (Interdictor-class)|Imperial Interdictor]] under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Brom Titus]] while it was undergoing trial runs in the [[Del Zennis system]]. During the trial runs, the Interdictor captured the flagship of the Phoenix rebel cell: the [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']], along with Ezra Bridger and the cells' commanding officer [[Jun Sato]]. The victory was [[Rescue on the Imperial Interdictor|short-lived]], however, as the rebels escaped capture and sabotaged the Interdictor's gravity wells before escaping back aboard the ''Liberator''. Admiral Titus, in a last-ditch effort, activated the ship's gravity well to pull the rebel flagship back, but due to Chopper's sabotage to the gravity well generators, the command cruisers were also dragged in towards the Interdictor, crashing into the ship and allowing the ''Liberator'' to jump to hyperspace before the Interdictor collapsed in on itself and imploded. Agent Kallus arrived shortly afterwards to find Titus in an escape pod, and chastised the admiral for not heeding his advice earlier regarding Ezra.<ref name="Stealth Strike">{{Rebels|Stealth Strike}}</ref>

Following the revelation of the rebel forces being stationed on Garel, the entirety of the Imperial fleet stationed over Lothal [[Battle of Garel|departed]],<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> leaving behind a minimal garrison, including at least one command cruiser under the command of Lieutenant [[Yogar Lyste]] to oversee the planet in case of another insurgency. Lystes' Imperial cruiser later encountered Princess [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] as she flew in with relief supplies for Lothal on her [[Sphyrna-class Hammerhead corvette|''Sphyrna''-class Hammerhead corvettes]], which she intended to [[Mission on Lothal|deliver]] to Phoenix Squadron; after the Imperials checked her codes, they allowed the Princess to land on Lothal.<ref name="A Princess on Lothal">{{Rebels|A Princess on Lothal}}</ref>

[[File:Imperial_jumptroopers.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Imperial jumptroopers inside an ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]]
Later, Agent Kallus received word of the Spectres' activity on the planet [[Nixus]] and departed with Admiral [[Kassius Konstantine]] aboard Kallus' personal command cruiser to intercept the rebels, arriving in the system mere moments before the ''Ghost'' jumped to hyperspace past them. Using the hidden tracker built into [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s communicator he gave to Ezra, Agent Kallus pursued the ''Ghost'' through hyperspace until reaching [[Wild Space]], where he re-encountered the ''Ghost'' heading towards an imploded [[star cluster]]. Kallus hailed the ''Ghost'' offering a chance for the rebels to turn themselves in or be pulled in by the star cluster, only for the ''Ghost'' to fly ahead towards the cluster. Kallus ordered the launch of a pair of TIE fighters to try and slow the ''Ghost'' down long enough to get them in range of a tractor beam. Due to the ''Ghost'' being placed under the influence of [[Garazeb Orrelios]]'s [[bo-rifle]], however, the ship remained intact while the TIE fighters were both torn to pieces. Seeing the freighter navigating the cluster prompted Kallus to bombard with the command cruisers' quad laser turrets, only to have the blasts pulled into the gravity wells of the stars ahead of them. Kallus ordered the command cruiser to pull back when he saw dorsal sections of the hull get torn off and pulled into the stars.<ref name="Legends of the Lasat" />

In [[2 BBY]], an ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser [[Massacre near Teralov|intercepted]] a rebel convoy near [[Teralov]]. The cruiser launched three TIE Interceptors which wiped out the convoy.<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" /> Later, [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] observed [[Cham Syndulla]] and the Spectres [[Second mission to Ryloth|escape]] from a trap set by [[Captain]] [[Slavin]] from the bridge of a command cruiser. He allowed the ''Ghost'' to escape because he believed the rebels deserved their victory.<ref name="Hera's Heroes">{{Rebels|Hera's Heroes}}</ref> Later, Admiral Konstatine commanded a single command cruiser, two ''Gozanti''-class cruisers, and several TIE fighters and bombers during a [[Skirmish over Mykapo|skirmish]] over [[Mykapo]]. It was badly damaged when the Spectres and [[Iron Squadron]] dropped a [[magno-mine]] over the cruiser's forward compartment.<ref name="Iron Squadron">{{Rebels|Iron Squadron (episode)}}</ref>

Captain [[Brunson]] later commanded a command cruiser which attempted to [[Mission to investigate Geonosis|hunt down and destroy]] the ''Ghost'' on [[Geonosis]], sending many jumptroopers to attack. However, the Spectres defeated the jumptroopers and used [[proton torpedo]]es to damage the ship and flee into hyperspace.<ref name="Ghosts of Geonosis" /> [[Lieutenant]] Yogar Lyste later commanded an ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser stationed above Lothal which [[Rescue of Alexsandr Kallus|captured]] the rebel operative [[Ezra Bridger]]. However, Ezra's capture was a ruse to rescue the rogue ISB Agent Kallus, who had become a rebel agent known as [[Fulcrum]].<ref name="TIE" /> One command cruiser [[Operation Handoff|attempted]] to capture the rogue [[senator]] and rebel leader [[Mon Mothma]] but was badly damaged by [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]].<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref> Several command cruisers participated in Thrawn's [[Battle of Atollon|assault]] on the planet [[Atollon]], in which they attempted to cut the rebels off.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref>

[[File:ArquitensCommandCruiser-SWS.png|thumb|right|250px|An ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser, with no equipped turrets.]]
Soon after the rebel defeat on Atollon, the Rebel Alliance launched a [[Mission to Jalindi|mission]] to [[Jalindi]] to hijack the [[Jalindi Station|outpost]] there in order to spy on Imperial fleet movements in [[1 BBY]]. This attempt was thwarted by the ''Arquitens''-class cruiser [[Marauder (Arquitens-class command cruiser)|''Marauder'']], commanded by Titus. After a skirmish, the [[Partisans]] arrived and destroyed the outpost, resulting in a massive explosion that also claimed the ''Marauder''.<ref name="ITNOTR" />

A [[prison-tug]], a modified variant of the ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser, pulled [[Accresker Jail]], a [[wreckage-prison]], into battle in order for it to deploy its [[Accresker Penal Legion|convict army]]. After some deadly spores were discovered in the prison, it was decided that it would be shot into a rebel planet, and the cruiser was sent hurtling into the wreckage.<ref name="Aphra 23">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 23|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 23]]</ref> Following the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], ''Arquitens''-class cruisers were uncommon, largely due to an [[Attack on Fondor|assault]] on the [[Fondor Shipyards]] in the same year that caused the shortage of available cruisers. One cruiser was present on the Imperial world [[Vardos]] during a [[Skirmish on Vardos|skirmish]] there soon after the Imperial defeat on [[Endor]], and another participated and was destroyed in a [[Skirmish on Takodana|skirmish]] on [[Takodana]] in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> One [[Class 546 Cruiser|Class 546 model]] of this cruiser was under the command of [[Moff]] [[Gideon]] and [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|his forces]] sometime after the Battle of Endor.<ref name="Chapter 12">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser was created for the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. It first appeared in "[[Rebel Resolve]]," the fourteenth episode of the show's [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]],<ref name="Rebel Resolve" /> which originally aired on [[February 23]], [[2015]].<ref name="Insider 156">{{InsiderCite|156|Star Wars Rebels Recounted|''Star Wars Rebels'' Recounted}}</ref> The command cruiser was reworked from the ''Arquitens''-class light cruiser that appeared in the earlier ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series, featuring subtle updates to its design, and was inspired by the transition of [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] into Imperial vessels seen at the end of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars:'' Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Trivia Gallery" /> Various elements of the ''Arquitens''-class asset were reused to create the [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]]<ref name="Homecoming Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/homecoming-concept-art-gallery|text="Homecoming" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> and [[class four container transport]].<ref name="The Forgotten Droid Concept Art Gallert">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/the-forgotten-droid-concept-art-gallery|text="The Forgotten Droid" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref>

Although initially said to be the same as the ''Arquitens''-class light cruiser,<ref name="Imperial Light Cruiser" /> it was later identified as the ''Arquitens''-class command cruiser by [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada's]] ''[[Imperial Light Cruiser Expansion Pack]]'', clarifying the two to be separate models.<ref name="Imperial Light Cruiser Expansion Pack" />
