'''Boga noga''' was a type of [[ale]] that could be found on<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13" /> the [[Smuggler]]'s [[Moon]]<ref name="Star Wars 13">[[Star Wars (2020) 13|''Star Wars'' (2020) 13]]</ref> of [[Nar Shaddaa]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 13|''Bounty Hunters'' 13]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Dengar]] was served a boga noga, which he thought looked refreshing. Before he could drink it, however, fellow [[bounty]] hunter [[Beilert Valance]] was thrown by the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] onto Dengar, which almost caused him to spill the drink.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13" />
