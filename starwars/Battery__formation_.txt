A '''battery''' was a type of organizational formation within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> and later within [[Imperial Army]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="S&S">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> In the [[Galactic Republic]], a battery could contain multiple [[RX-200 Falchion-class assault tank|RX-200 ''Falchion''-class assault tanks]] along with their [[clone trooper pilot]]s, with one such [[Unidentified tank battery|tank battery]] present in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Malastare]] and subdued the [[Zillo Beast]].<ref name="The Zillo Beast">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref> A battery within the [[army]] of the Empire could consist of several [[All Terrain Defense Turret]]s, their [[Imperial combat driver]]s, support [[trooper]]s, and be equipped with a stockpile of ammunition.<ref name="S&S" /> Additionally, an Imperial battery could also be organized as an [[infantry]] formation, with a [[Mapuzo battery|full battery]] stationed on the [[mining]] [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]].<ref name="Part III" />
