'''Byl''', designated '''0037''', was a [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="OtherName" /> As a [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning/Legends|clone]] of [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]], 0037 was created on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]],<ref name="db" /> had [[Color/Legends|tan]] skin,<ref name="Skin" /> and stood at 1.83 [[Meter/Legends|meters]] tall.<ref name="db" /> 0037 became acquainted with fellow [[Trooper/Legends|troopers]] [[1137]] and [[1139]], and would eventually take on the name Byl. When 1139 wanted a name rather than being referred to by their designation number, 1139 thought up the names "Fil" and Byl to be used by them and by fellow trooper 1137. Upon telling 1137 the name suggestions, 1137 informed 1139 that those names were already taken by the troopers who they knew as [[Fil (2720)|2720]] and 0037 the prior [[Standard week/Legends|week]].<ref name="OtherName" />

==Behind the scenes==
Byl was first mentioned in the animated comic "[[A clone by any other name]]" on the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website, released on [[August 21]], [[2009]].<ref name="OtherName" />
