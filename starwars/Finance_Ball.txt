The '''Finance Ball''' was an [[Standard year|annual]] gala held in [[Coronet City]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]] to mark the beginning of open-market trading. Typically a highly extravagant event, it was sponsored by Coronet's [[City Father]]s, particularly the [[Father of Finances]]. In [[230 BBY]], the Finance Ball was [[Battle of the Corellian Shipyards|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
