'''Coruscant Hotel''' was a [[Hotel/Legends|hotel]] located on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. Parents of [[Padawan/Legends|Padawans]] attending parent-teacher conferences at the [[Jedi academy]] in the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on Coruscant were hosted at this hotel while they visited.<ref name="return">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''</ref>
