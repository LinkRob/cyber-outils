'''Darren Abysina''' was a [[Twi'lek]] who was an [[ambassador]] for the [[Galactic Republic]] during the late [[High Republic Era]]. Around the year<ref name="TCC" /> [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> Abysina served as a Republic ambassador to the [[planet]] [[Siline]] who were in negotiations to join the Republic after been affiliated with it for almost two hundred years. The [[Jedi Master]] [[Brand (Jedi Master)|Brand]] also served as delegate or the [[Jedi Order]] to the negotiation, but accidentally created a diplomatic incident when he repeatedly refused the challenges to a friendly duel by the [[Silinese War Council]] member [[Rap Arcon]]. In response, Brand was ordered away from Siline and Masters [[Sol]] and [[Elishe]] and their [[Padawan]]s [[Jecki Lon]] and [[Yord Fandar]] [[Mission to Siline|were sent]] to the planet to to clear up the misunderstanding. During their stay, the Jedi managed to uncover a conspiracy during [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|in that year's]] [[Crowning Convocation]]. Afterwards the Jedi successfully returned to [[Coruscant]] and Abysina believed that the negotiations would go better now, though they remained delayed while the [[Silinese]] worked themselves out internally.<ref name="TCC" />
