The '''Adikaria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It was situated at the coordinates K-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Adikaria/Legends|Adikaria]], a terrestrial<ref name="JoJ" /> astronomical object<ref name="Appendix" /> where a ball was held at some point prior<ref name="JoJ" /> to between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]'' take place between the events of {{Film|VIII}} and {{Film|IX}}, which corresponds to between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Adikaria system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
