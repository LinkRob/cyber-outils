'''Dark Acolyte''' was a title held during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|59|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> by certain disciples of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]], the [[Sith]] persona of [[Count]] Dooku. [[Asajj Ventress]],<ref name="Ultimate" /> [[Sev'rance Tann]],<ref name="Prelude English">{{EncyclopediaCite|Prologue to the Clone Wars and the First Battle of Geonosis}} {{C|English version}}</ref> and [[Savage Opress]] held the title during their [[time]] under Dooku.<ref name="Ultimate" />

As an [[agent]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Head of State|leader]], a Dark Acolyte had commanding authority over forces in the [[Confederacy military]].<ref name="Ambush" /> In at least the case of Ventress, she was further identified as a [[Sith acolyte]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> Tann and [[Dark Acolyte (Geonosis)|other Acolyte members]] participated in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]].<ref name="Prelude English" /> After losing Opress,<ref name="Witches">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> Tyranus would go onto take on another apprentice, the fallen [[Jedi]] [[Quinlan Vos]]. In conversation with Ventress, Tyranus claimed Vos was a full [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] instead of a mere acolyte, yet Vos would ultimately betray Tyranus.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> In this text ''[[The Secrets of the Sith]]'', Tyranus' [[Sith Master]] [[Darth Sidious]] identified Ventress, Oppress, and Vos as Dooku's three '''dark disciples'''.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref>
