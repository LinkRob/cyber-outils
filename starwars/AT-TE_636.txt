'''AT-TE 636''' was an [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] that served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]]. During the battle, it's crew was ordered by [[Cody|Commander Cody]] to lay down covering fire at point 35 while [[clone trooper]]s [[Boil]] and [[Waxer]] pulled [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and clone trooper [[Trapper]] from their downed [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" />
