The '''''Fortune'''''<ref name="Volume 1" /> was a [[Dynamic-class freighter|''Dynamic''-class freighter]]<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> that served as a mobile headquarters for the [[Zendiat Collective]], a [[Thief|thieving]] guild led by the [[human]] treasure hunter [[Zool Zendiat]]. Because one of its passengers, the [[Patrolian]] [[Kanna]], belonged to an [[Water|aquatic]] [[species]], the ''Fortune'' notably housed a water tank.<ref name="Volume 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Fortune'' first appeared in "[[Better the Devil You Know, Part I]],"<ref name="BTDYK 1" /> a comic story written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Derek Charm]] which was published in the [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 1|first issue]] of the [[2017]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars Adventures (2017)|''Star Wars Adventures'']]. The comic was released by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="IDW">{{IDW|url=product/star-wars-adventures-1|text=Star Wars Adventures #1}}</ref> on [[September 6]], [[2017]].<ref name="CBR">{{WebCite|url=https://www.cbr.com/star-wars-adventures-storylines-creative-teams-reveal-exclusive|text=Star Wars Adventures: First Storylines, Creative Teams Revealed|author=Albert Ching|date=2017-06-14|archivedate=20190110214816}}</ref> It was first identified in the trade paperback collection ''[[Star Wars Adventures, Volume 1: Heroes of the Galaxy]]'', which was published by IDW<ref name="Volume 1" /> on [[October 31]] of that year.<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/564636/star-wars-adventures-vol-1-heroes-of-the-galaxy-by-landry-walker-derek-charm|text=Star Wars Adventures Vol. 1: Heroes of the Galaxy}}</ref>
