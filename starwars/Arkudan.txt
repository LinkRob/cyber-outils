{{Quote|Those are Arkudan gaming cubes. They're supposed to bring luck, but I just keep them because they're centuries old, and I like the way they look.|Tionne Solusar|<ref name="JJK">''[[Junior Jedi Knights: Kenobi's Blade]]''</ref>}}
The '''Arkudans''' were a [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]], native to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Arkuda]]. They were a squat species, with vaguely ursine characteristics.<ref name="Atlas" /> Their planet was located in the [[Gordian Reach/Legends|Gordian Reach]] [[Sector/Legends|sector]], at the junction of the [[Junction-Tierell Loop/Legends|Junction-Tierell Loop]] and the [[Korphir Trace]].<ref name="Atlas" /> For hundreds of [[Standard year/Legends|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="JJK" /> the Arkudans produced [[Arkudan gaming cube|gaming cubes]] that were jewelled and highly ornamental.<ref name="Atlas" /> These cubes were believed by some to bring good [[luck]],<ref name="JJK" /> and it was primarily for these cubes that the Arkudans were known.<ref name="Atlas" /> [[Tionne Solusar]], a member of the [[New Jedi Order]], owned a pair fluffy Arkudan gaming cubes, which she hung from the ceiling of her [[Starship/Legends|starship]], the ''[[Lore Seeker]]''.<ref name="JJK" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Jason Fry]] created the Arkudans for ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', published by [[Del Rey]] in [[2009]].<ref name="Atlas" /> This provided a backstory for the Arkudan game cubes mentioned in ''[[Junior Jedi Knights: Kenobi's Blade]]'', written by [[Rebecca Moesta]] and published by [[Berkley Boulevard]] in [[1997]].<ref name="JJK" />
