A '''contamination pod''', also called a '''contamination cell''' was a type of cell made with [[transparisteel]]. Multiple contamination pods were located on the [[Elite-class vessel|''Elite''-class vessel]] ''[[Innovator]]''.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> after a [[Mission on Palagosal|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Palagosal]], members of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] and [[Jedi Order]] restrained the [[Nihil]] [[Haze Delik]] and two [[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|consuming]] [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creatures in the ''Innovator''{{'s}} contamination pods.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />
