The '''C-B7 Interceptor''' was an opulent [[landspeeder]] manufactured by [[Arlen-Dempler Luxury Speeders]].

==Characteristics==
The C-B7 Interceptor was a two-door model of [[speeder]] able to accommodate four passengers in addition to a single [[Pilot|driver]]. It came equipped with a sophisticated entertainment system, as well as a top-of-the-line [[Engine|drive system]], comfort and safety features. In spite of its luxury accoutrements, the Interceptor was simple to maintain and highly modifiable. This made it a popular choice for intelligence agencies and their operatives working on affluent [[Planet|worlds]].<ref name="CAM" />
