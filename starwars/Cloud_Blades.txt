The '''Cloud Blades''' were a set of unique [[Dual Vibroblades]] owned by the [[Kubaz]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Glikkin]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Vibroblades (Glikkin)|standard vibroblades]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cloud Blades was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Glikkin's Dual Vibroblades, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cloud Thief]]. This wrap could be unlocked as one of two grand prizes within the "Cloud Rogue Vault."<ref name="Hunters" />
