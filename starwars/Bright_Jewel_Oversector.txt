The '''Bright Jewel Oversector''' was an [[oversector]] of [[the galaxy]] which contained the [[moon]] [[Axxila III]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Bright Jewel Oversector Flight Base]] was located on Axxila III.<ref name="LCATZA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bright Jewel Oversector was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' novel ''[[Tatooine Ghost]]'', which was written by [[Troy Denning]] and released in [[2003]]. The region was confirmed to be [[canon]] when it appeared in the short story ''[[Last Call at the Zero Angle]]'' written by [[Jason Fry]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 156|156]] in [[2015]].
