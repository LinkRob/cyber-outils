The '''Blade of Ochi of Bestoon''',<ref name="BOB" /> or simply '''Ochi's blade''' or '''Ochi's dagger''', was a [[dagger]],<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> an ornate [[knife]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> and [[Sith dagger]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> owned by the [[Sith cultist]] and [[assassin]], [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]]. The [[Sith assassin]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> and relic hunter<ref name="Ochi DB">{{Databank|ochi-of-bestoon|Ochi of Bestoon}}</ref> Ochi used the [[melee weapon]] to [[Death|kill]] many. The blade itself was capable of withstanding [[lightsaber]] blows, making it a formidable weapon. The dagger's crossguards have concealed measurements arcs for use in pinpointing the missing wayfinder's location.<ref name="TROS VD" /> Additionally, it was inscribed with runes<ref name="BOB" /> in the [[Ur-Kittât|Sith language]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> which recorded coordinates leading to the location of the [[Emperor's wayfinder|wayfinder]] belonging to [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="BOB" /> 

==History==
[[File:OchiofBestoon-Timelines.png|thumb|250px|left|Ochi has an almost psychic connection with his blade.]]
[[Ochi]] was given the blade by the [[Sith Eternal]] for its use during the [[quest for Exegol]]. During the quest, he found the blade almost speaking to him in his mind and longing for new murders, with Ochi experiencing a pleasurable white noise in his mind from the dagger whenever he killed with it. He first found this out when he killed [[Zargo Anaximander]], during which the blade sucked Anaximander's blood from his body and imbued itself within the runes of the blade itself. This was also the case when he killed [[Stiper]]. The relic seemed to have a want of its own; the want to consume blood, a bloodlust which would be passed on to whomever wielded it.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

With the blade, he killed the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] [[Parent|father]], [[Dathan]], and mother, [[Miramir]] in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] before leaving for the world of [[Pasaana]] on the false belief that was where they had hidden Rey. When Ochi was sucked under the quicksands of the [[Forbidden Valley]], the dagger fell below with him<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> and was later found to be left next to where he would eventually die and decay into a [[Skeleton|skeletal]] state. As a result of the murders it was used to carry out, the [[Sith]] [[dagger]] resonated in [[the Force]] with the evil imbued within, thus shocking Rey<ref name="BOB" /> upon the [[human]] [[Jedi]]-[[Padawan|aspirant's]] touch.<ref name="Episode IX" /> The dark deeds carried out by the dagger could be glimpsed by Force-sensitive individuals.<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>

The [[droid]] [[C-3PO]] had his systems altered to decipher this inscription, having been prevented from translating the forbidden [[language]] by a programming block. After arriving on [[Kef Bir]], Rey uses the dagger to locate the wayfinder by aligning a plate hidden in the hilt with a specific part of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star's]] [[Death Star ruins|wreckage]], and discovering the two shapes match perfectly.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Isaiwinokka<br />Hoyakuts<br />Itharii Exegol<br />Eternal One|translated=1|Example of the Sith language, transliterated from ur-Kittât as per the Jedi Journal|<ref name="TROS VD" />}}
An example of the Sith language inscription was provided in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''. It could be transliterated to the lines quoted above, using the real-world [[Jedi Journal]] product of unconfirmed [[canon]]icity from [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]] at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], which provides a translation between the alphabet of [[High Galactic]] and the [[Sith alphabet]] that does not visibly match the inscription on Ochi's dagger.<ref name="SW:GE">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref>

[[Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1]], set in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="CR Date">{{OutlawsComicDate|Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1}}</ref> depicts [[Ochi]] owning the blade while in service to the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate.<ref name="Crimson Reign 1">[[Crimson Reign 1|''Crimson Reign'' 1]]</ref> However ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' established that he didn't receive the blade until the [[quest for Exegol]]<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> in [[21 ABY]].<ref name="SotS Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> The book describes the dagger as "freshly machined," and the author [[Adam Christopher]] confirmed the intention was to show that the dagger was new, created shortly before the events of the book.<ref name="Tweet">{{Twitter|ghostfinder|status/1655982069221031948|[[Adam Christopher]]|quote=The dagger is new. In Shadow, Ochi can see how the edges are freshly machined. The inscription writes itself after the blade drinks his blood.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/8M043}}</ref> Also, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] established that the blade was inscribed with information about the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]'s wreckage meaning that the blade must've been created after [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
