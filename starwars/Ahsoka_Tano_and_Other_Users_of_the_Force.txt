"'''Ahsoka Tano and Other Users of the Force'''" is a volume in the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series of reference books published by [[De Agostini]]. It was published on [[January 20]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya" />

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''The apprenticeship of a padawan was a long and hard road, and not all students completed it. One of the best examples of this was Ahsoka Tano, Anakin Skywalker's apprentice, who fought in the Clone Wars as did other Jedi Generals such as Aayla Secura or Adi Gallia, but who ended up leaving the Jedi Order after being accused of a crime she had not committed and losing faith in the institution. However, she would never stop fighting for freedom and justice, and even helped the fledgling Rebellion in its fight against the Empire. Years after the fall of the regime, her voice would join many other Jedi in aiding Rey in her final confrontation against Emperor Palpatine on Exegol.''<ref name="De Agostini" />
