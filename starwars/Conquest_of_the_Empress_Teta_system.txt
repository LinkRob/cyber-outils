At some point, the [[Krath]] '''conquered''' the [[Empress Teta system]]. After their conquest, the Krath [[Carbon-freezing|carbon-freezed]] the ruling [[Tetan monarchy]].<ref name="A&S" /> This event was featured in the quasi-historical [[Qel-Droma Epics]] as the '''military coup''' taking place a [[millennium]] after the [[Great Hyperspace War]], during the [[Crusade of Krath]], the epics claim the coup was instigated by corrupt [[Tetan nobility|noble descendants]] of [[Empress]] [[Teta]], empowered by [[Sith artifact]]s, who were also the leaders of a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Religion|cult]] known as the Krath. At some point following the coup, the [[Iron Citadel]] was conquered by the Krath and used as a command center from which they directed their crusade.<ref name="NoP" />

Anderson]], illustrated by [[Chris Gossett]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Tales of the Jedi - Dark Lords of the Sith 1">[[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 1|''Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith'' 1]]</ref> on [[October 4]], [[1994]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54571/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_-_dark_lords_of_the_sith_1994_1|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi - Dark Lords of the Sith (1994) #1}}</ref>
