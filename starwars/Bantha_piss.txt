'''Bantha piss''' was a phrase meaning "nonsense." According to [[Sardis Ramsin]], 99% of the things [[Gender|his]] fellow [[Stormtrooper]] [[Crag]] said was "unaltered bantha piss."<ref name="In the Storm">{{StoryCite|story=Born in the Storm|book=From a Certain Point of View}} </ref>  [[Clone Force 99]] member [[Hunter]] frustratedly shouted "you bantha-piss rust bucket!" to the controls of the [[laser cannon]]s of the ''[[Marauder (Omicron-class)|Marauder]]'' at one point.<ref name="Sanctuary" /> The term literally referred to the [[urine]] of [[bantha]]s; when [[Sylvestri Yarrow]] was served a glass of [[toniray]], she thought to herself that "it could have been bantha piss and she wouldn't have cared," as long as it fixed the "bit of [[headache|pounding]]" building in her head.<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The phrase "bantha piss" is similar to the rude, real-world noun "bullshit," which also means "nonsense" and is a compound formed from the name of an animal, combined with a word for bodily waste.
