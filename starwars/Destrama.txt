The '''''Destrama''''' was a [[Chiss heavy cruiser|Chiss warship]] that served as the [[flagship]] of [[Commodore]] [[Ar'alani]] after her promotion to Commodore, and prior to her being given command of the [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']]. The ''Destrama'' was part of [[Picket Force Six]] and present for the [[skirmish above Solitair]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
