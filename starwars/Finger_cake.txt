'''Finger cakes''' were a small type of [[cake]] [[Diet|edible]] by the [[Evereni]] [[species]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="FaerDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. The feast occurred after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, it must have taken place no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> a feast was held on the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', a [[starship]] belonging to [[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the Evereni<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a group of [[Pirate|marauders]] who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;for which a [[Unidentified team member|being]] serving Ro baked finger cakes frosted with ground [[palm sugar]]. During the feast, [[Vika Faer]]&mdash;an Evereni who was staying with Ro's raiders&mdash;came over to Ro to sit with<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her,<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> bringing Ro a small plate of the finger cakes. Ro took one of the finger cakes and nibbled on it throughout their exchange.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Ro later mentioned the finger cakes in a [[Marda Ro's journal|recording]] she made for her [[Ro family|descendants]].<ref name="Closed Fist" /> By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during his youth, Ro's [[Gender|great-great-grandson]] [[Marchion Ro]] had listened to her memoirs, including the message where finger cakes were mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
Finger cakes appeared in a flashback in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Closed Fist" />
