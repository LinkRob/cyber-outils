'''Cirilian Noodle Salad''' was a type of [[salad]] made up of [[noodle]]s and [[vegetable]]s created by the [[Gilliand]] [[Z'Vk'Thkrkza]], known as "Aunt Z."<ref name="Cookbook" /> The dish shared its name with the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Cirilia system]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> Cirilian Noodle Salad was inspired by her [[Cirilian Sour Paste]] drink.<ref name="Cookbook" />

At some point, after leading a group rebelling against the [[First Order]] to the [[planet]] [[Takodana]], she encountered the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]], with whom she traded recipes. Later, after Tuggs published his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the chef included a recipe for Cirilian Noodle Salad in his [[Life Day|holiday]] [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', where he mentioned that the salad was enjoyable but not the same without Aunt Z's presence.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|This recipe gets its name from the drink Cirilian Sour Paste, served in Aunt Z's tavern on the Colossus in Star Wars Resistance. When it comes to serving, that's when the magic happens.|Jenn Fujikawa|<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/the-life-day-cookbook|text=Whip, Whip, Stir: Creating ''The Life Day Cookbook''}}</ref>}}
[[File:Star_Wars_The_Life_Day_Cookbook_cover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Cirilian Noodle Salad debuted in ''Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook''.]]
Cirilian Noodle Salad was introduced in ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a recipe book authored by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> and published on [[November 9]], [[2021]]. Prior to the book's release, the salad was revealed in a [[November 1]] [[StarWars.com]] preview that same year. When Fujikawa created the recipe for ''The Life Day Cookbook'', the author chose to name it after the beverage Cirilian Sour Paste from the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.<ref name="Preview" />

The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dressing includes [[Wikipedia:Chili sauce|chili sauce]], [[Wikipedia:Rice vinegar|rice vinegar]], [[Wikipedia:Soy sauce|soy sauce]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame oil|sesame oil]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic]], and [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]]. The noodles are [[Wikipedia:Rice noodles|rice noodles]] cooked with [[Wikipedia:Water|water]] and [[Wikipedia:Red cabbage|red cabbage]], and the [[Wikipedia:Salad|salad]] calls for [[Wikipedia:Cabbage|cabbage]], [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]], [[Wikipedia:Snap pea|snap peas]], a [[Wikipedia:Scallion|scallion]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame seed|sesame seeds]], and a [[Wikipedia:Lime|lime]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
