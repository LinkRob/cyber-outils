'''Battle stimulants''' were a type of [[stimulant]] that enhanced a user's [[combat]] abilities and reduced pain for a short [[time]]. Taking multiple battle stimulants in short succession could greatly enhance one's physical prowess at the cost of causing one's memories of events to blur.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

During the [[Battle of Galov]]<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> forces of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] boarded the [[Nihil]] [[cruiser]] [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|''Lourna Dee'']] and engaged the marauders aboard. Nihil [[Strike]] [[Andrik Keller]] gave a battle stimulant to his commanding [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] [[Tasia]] after she sustained an injury, and then took one himself. Upon meeting [[Jedi Master]]s [[Sskeer]] and [[Avar Kriss]] aboard the cruiser, Keller took a second battle stimulant and aggressively engaged the [[Jedi]] while trying to steal their Jedi [[shuttle]]. Tasia called Keller an "idiot" for his bravado.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

==Behind the scenes==
Battle stimulants first appeared in ''Star Wars'' canon in a flashback in the [[2021]] [[List of canon audio productions|audio drama]] ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and performed by a full voice cast.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Battle stimulant/Legends|battle stimulants]] first appeared in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]'' by [[BioWare]].<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>
