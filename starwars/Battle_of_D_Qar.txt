The '''Battle of D'Qar''', also known as the '''defense of D'Qar''', the '''evacuation of D'Qar''', the '''siege of D'Qar''', or the '''attack on D'Qar''', took place in [[34 ABY]], shortly after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], as part of the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]]. In destroying [[Starkiller Base]], the First Order's newest [[superweapon]], the Resistance had revealed the location of their [[Resistance base|base]]. Fearing reprisal attacks, they were forced to abandon their base on [[D'Qar]] in order to find a new base of operations.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|&hellip;the Resistance must be destroyed before they get to Skywalker.''"<br />"''We have their location. We tracked their reconnaissance ship to the Ileenium system.''"<br />"''Good. Then we will crush them once and for all. Prepare the weapon.|Supreme Leader Snoke and General Armitage Hux|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[First Order]] [[superweapon]] known as [[Starkiller Base]] destroyed [[Hosnian Prime]], [[capital]] [[planet]] of the [[New Republic]], and the other worlds of the [[Hosnian system]]. The [[Resistance]], led by [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], sent a reconnaissance team to discover the location of the superweapon and report back to the Resistance at their [[Resistance base|base]] on [[D'Qar]]. The First Order tracked the reconnaissance [[Starship|ships]] back to D'Qar, thus discovering the location of their enemy forces. On the orders of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]], who wanted to prevent the Resistance from finding [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and enlisting him in their fight against the First Order, General [[Armitage Hux]] prepared the weapon to destroy the Resistance. Without the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]], however, the Resistance seemed doomed to destruction.<ref name="Episode VII" />

Before the First Order could unleash the weapon, the Resistance [[Battle of Starkiller Base|sent]] a small strike force&mdash;ex-General [[Han Solo]], his first mate [[Chewbacca]], and former [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] and Starkiller Base worker [[Finn]]&mdash;to Starkiller Base to disable the shields preventing the destruction of the base. Meanwhile, [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] led a [[starfighter]] force to Starkiller Base to destroy it. The ground forces succeeded, though Solo was [[Death|killed]] in battle, and the Resistance fighters destroyed the base.<ref name="Episode VII" />

The Resistance fighters returned to D'Qar, celebrated their victory, and prepared a mission to discover Skywalker's whereabouts. The First Order, while reeling from the destruction of their weapon, still nonetheless had the location of the Resistance base.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==The evacuation==
{{Quote|This is where we snuff out the Resistance once and for all.|[[General]] [[Armitage Hux]]|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
<div style="float: right">[[File:Final_lifeboats_are_away.png|thumb|250px|The final Resistance lifeboats depart D'Qar.]]</div>
Shortly after Starkiller Base was destroyed, the Resistance began evacuating their base on D'Qar, and in the early hours of the evacuation, Organa held a [[Funeral of Han Solo|funeral]] for her fallen husband Han Solo.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> In orbit around the planet, General Organa commanded the Resistance fleet from the cruiser [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

At the same time as the battle of Starkiller Base, [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|Inferno Squad]] members [[Zay Versio]] and [[Shriv Suurgav]] recovered schematics for the First Order Dreadnought and intel on [[Project Resurrection]] from the ''[[Retribution]]''. As they arrived amid the evacuation, General Organa sent Inferno to rally their allies from the [[Mission to the Outer Rim|Outer Rim]], while passing on the schematics to Commander Dameron. From this, Poe was able to formulate an attack plan.<ref name="BF2 Resurrection" />

The escape was interrupted by the arrival of a First Order fleet consisting of three ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers and one ''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought, the ''[[Fulminatrix]]''. The dreadnought opened fire on the Resistance base almost immediately, but the base had been fully evacuated just in time. To buy time for the Resistance transports to escape, Dameron attacked the dreadnought in his X-wing despite Organa ordering him not to attack the dreadnought, eliminating its surface cannons and clearing the way for a squadron of Resistance bombers to attack despite counterattacks from the dreadnought and its TIE squadrons.

[[File:ScorchZetaLeader-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|First Order TIE/fo fighters engage the Resistance bombers to defend the ''Fulminatrix''.]]
As the ''Fulminatrix'' began to aim its [[orbital autocannon]]s at the ''Raddus'', Resistance bombers began to approach the dreadnought, defended by starfighters to block TIE fighter attacks. The resultant assault successfully obliterated the ''Fulminatrix'', but at a high cost. All bombers were lost along with four of their escorting starfighters, and after the first attack on the fleet, the Resistance was reduced to just 400 personnel aboard four ships. However, Dameron's attack bought enough time for their fleet to jump to hyperspace.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> At the same time they made their escape, the ''[[Supremacy]]'' and thirty more Star Destroyers arrived at the [[Oetchi system]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="TLJ Graphic Novel">[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi Graphic Novel Adaptation|''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Graphic Novel Adaptation]]</ref>

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|They can't get away, Supreme Leader. We have them tied on the end of a string.|General Armitage Hux to Supreme Leader Snoke|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}
The incident, identified as the Battle of D'Qar,<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> ended with a high cost for the Resistance, which led to General Organa demoting Commander Dameron to Captain, as he had disobeyed her direct order not to press the dreadnought assault after neutralizing its defenses. The Resistance was unable to fully escape the First Order due to the latter's new technology that could [[Hyperspace tracker|track]] ships through [[hyperspace]]. Eventually, after a nearly 18-hour long [[Battle of Oetchi|pursuit]] and with fuel supplies critically low, the Resistance slipped away and headquartered themselves in an [[Crait outpost|old Rebel outpost]] on the [[mineral]] planet [[Crait]]. This escape was detected by the First Order, however, and led to the subsequent [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Following the departure of both the First Order and the Resistance, [[Team Colossus|Team ''Colossus'']] aboard the ''[[Colossus]]'' arrived and saw debris from the battle. Captain [[Imanuel Doza]] authorized a [[Skirmish over D'Qar|salvage mission]] to retrieve fuel from ''Fulminatrix''{{'s}} wreckage.<ref name="Quick Salvage Run" />

Even though the Resistance managed to escape, [[First Order officer]]s who were not present for the battle were told that the Resistance had been destroyed.<ref name="Poe 31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The first footage of the battle of was shown in the [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] teaser trailer on [[April 14]], [[2017]].<ref name="TLJ teaser">{{SWYouTube|zB4I68XVPzQ|Star Wars: The Last Jedi Official Teaser|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-last-jedi-official-teaser}}</ref> The evacuation of D'Qar was first referenced as part of the story of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], which was released on [[December 15]], 2017, at San Diego Comic-Con 2017.<ref name="TLJ SDCC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-last-jedi-props-and-costumes-on-display-at-sdcc|text=''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Props and Costumes on Display at SDCC}}</ref> The opening bombing-run sequence of the Evacuation of D'Qar was inspired by the [[Wikipedia:World War II|World War II]] book ''Vertical Warfare'', which director [[Rian Johnson]] found while researching the library of [[Skywalker Ranch]].<ref name="Art TLJ">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]''</ref>

The comic issue [[Poe Dameron 28|''Poe Dameron'' 28]] establishes that Dameron suggested his plan to distract the First Order by flying ''Black One'' during the battle before the evacuation had even begun.<ref name="Poe Dameron 28" /> Previously, the "[[Poe's Plan]]" short story<ref name="Poe's Plan">{{StoryCite|story=Poe's Plan|book=5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back}}</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]'' had depicted Dameron as suggesting the plan after the First Order arrived over D'Qar.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> It is the comic's version of events that is reflected in ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.<ref name="Timelines" /> Furthermore, ''Poe Dameron'' 28 sets up the remaining issues of ''[[Star Wars: Poe Dameron]]'',<ref name="Poe Dameron 28" /> with those final issues themselves setting up the novel ''[[Resistance Reborn]]''.<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref> As there is more [[canon]]ical weight behind ''Poe Dameron'' 28, this article follows its version of events.
