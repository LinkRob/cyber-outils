The '''G-1A starfighter''',<ref name="Outer Rim" /> also known as the '''G-1A transport''', was a model of [[starfighter]]<ref name="Allies">''[[Allies and Adversaries]]''</ref> manufactured by [[Byblos Drive Yards]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> One such [[Starship|vessel]] was the ''[[Mist Hunter]]'' owned by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zuckuss]].<ref name="Allies" /> The starship was small enough that it could be equipped with a [[cloaking device]] and, during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], at least one G-1A starfighter was equipped with cloaking ability.<ref name="SWZ">{{FFGXW2|set=Rebel Alliance Conversion Kit|cardname=Cloaking Device}} {{C|Reissued in ''[[Scum and Villainy Conversion Kit]]''}}</ref>
