'''Dazur Dar''', also known as the '''Data Broker''', was a [[Gender|male]] [[Abednedo]]<ref name="SW:U" /> and a member of the mysterious [[Data Seekers]]. Like all his comrades, Dar bartered secrets like [[currency]], but he also traded a vast number of rare items discovered in the Seekers' travels.<ref name="Forums" /> Several [[Standard month|months]] into the [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] of the [[Anoat sector]], Dar maintained a presence at the [[Carbon Score Cantina]], on the [[Mining Worlds|mining world]] of [[Burnin Konn]].<ref name="SW:U" />
