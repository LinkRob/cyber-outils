'''Barma Battle Group''' was a [[New Republic]] [[battle group]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]. The battle group's [[flagship]] was the ''[[Lodestar]]''. [[Hail Squadron]] and [[Meteor Squadron]] were part of the battle group. Sometime after the [[Battle of Endor]], Syndulla made a deal with [[New Republic Intelligence]] [[Agent]] [[Caern Adan]] to station the newly formed [[Alphabet Squadron]] onboard the ''Lodestar''.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

Barma Battle Group fought in a series of battles, including sending Meteor Squadron on a scouting mission to [[Chazwa]] and a [[Skirmish on Argai Minor|fight]] at [[Argai Minor]], where they took the [[Treinhaus Citadel]], an Imperial installation on the planet. After the heavy fighting at Argai Minor, Barma Battle Group traveled to the [[Borleias system]] with a few damaged vessels. The group eventually made its way to [[Pandem Nai]], a [[planet]] fortified by the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
