'''Anvil Leader''' was the [[callsign]] assigned to the leader of the [[New Republic]]'s [[Anvil Squadron]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a [[Anvil Leader (pilot)|pilot]] using this callsign led defense forces against an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Scouring of Mon Cala|assault]] above [[Mon Cala]] and then later participated in a [[Battle of Galitan|battle]] in the [[Ringali Nebula]], to protect the heavily damaged ''[[Starhawk]]'' from a final strike by [[Titan Squadron]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
