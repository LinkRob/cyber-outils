'''Flurri Wiss''' was a [[human]] member of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> they held the rank of [[Jedi Knight]] and helped administer visitors to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]], interacting with the delegation of the [[Tynnan]] [[species]], as they were an expert in their culture.<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Wiss entered a [[Jedi training|training]] veranda at the [[Jedi temple|temple]] as [[Jedi Master]] [[Sol]] was finishing a session with a group of [[Jedi Initiate]]s.<ref name="LostFound">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref>

Sol then dismissed the [[youngling]]s for lunch, and Wiss and another [[Jedi]] stood smiling as the [[Youngling|initiates]] filed out of the room. The Knight then followed the last youngling out as Sol spoke with Master [[Vernestra Rwoh]].<ref name="LostFound" /> While at the temple, Wiss wore [[Color|brown]] and yellow [[mission robes]] with a brown [[belt]] with a seal of the late High Republic on the buckle. The human had light skin, gray [[eye]]s, and blonde [[hair]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
