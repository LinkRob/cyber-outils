The '''DC-15A blaster rifle''', known simply as the '''DC-15 blaster rifle''', was a [[heavy blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]], as part of the [[DC-15 family]]. It was one of the standard-issue weapons of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s, an [[army]] of [[Cloning|cloned]] [[soldier]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The [[blaster]] was a larger, heavier, and more powerful long-range alternative to the [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]. It was used throughout the Clone Wars by the Republic and its allies, and saw limited use during the early-stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

==Description==
===Physical description===
The DC-15A [[blaster rifle]]<ref name="HC16" /> was a long [[heavy blaster rifle]] that was capable of firing blue-[[color]]ed [[blaster]] bolts.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> It weighed 4.15 [[kilogram]]s<ref name="AEYNTK" /> and was almost fully black in color. It had a [[Gription|magnatomic adhesion]] grip and a nonconducting [[stock]], which was weighted to improve the weapon's overall balance and to help reduce recoil.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> An indicator for the blaster's [[gas]] pressure was located above the trigger, right next to its [[blaster power pack]]. Circuitry for the power amplifier was located behind the rifle's protruding iron sights, which were located towards the center of the blaster. A power setting adjustor was located on the right side of the iron sights, which could be turned to change the weapon's power setting. A [[sniper]] [[scope]] was stowed underneath the [[rifle]], which could be detached and re-attached to the weapon's iron sights, located directly above it.<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

[[File:DC-15_Phase_I_Trooper_SWL.png|thumb|left|250px|Two clone troopers fire the DC-15A blaster rifle]]
In addition, a flip-up optical sight was located at the end of the weapon's [[barrel]], which itself was surrounded by heat-radiator fins.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> The DC-15A blaster rifle utilized tibanna gas cartridges and blaster power packs to fire. When its user pulled the weapon's trigger, a gas release valve released a small amount of [[tibanna]] into the rifle's ignition chamber, where it was transformed into [[plasma]] by the ignitor, with energy from the blaster's power pack. The plasma was then transferred to the expansion chamber, where it became a [[particle beam]]. The beam was then intensified through a [[prismatic crystal]] and pushed through a [[galven circuit]]. [[Electromagnet]]s accelerated the blaster bolt out of the weapon's barrel, where it was stabilized by magnetic pulse stabilizers and a collimating tube.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /><ref name="Visual Dictionary" />

The firing process generated a lot of heat, which was evacuated through the blaster's radiator fins and a heat-exchange element located in the blaster's muzzle.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /><ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> To reload the blaster's power pack, the user would have to press a button to extract the pack, and insert a new one. Replacing the tibanna cartridge required the user to open the weapon, which left them vulnerable during combat situations. Due to this, most [[soldier]]s used high capacity cartridges to counter-act their vulnerability while reloading.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

===Characteristics and variants===
The DC-15A blaster rifle was heavy, poorly balanced, difficult to use in non-wide open environments, and also suffered from beam cohesion issues after sustained fire, which affected its short-to medium-range accuracy.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> It was capable of automatic fire,<ref name="HC22">{{HelmetCollectionCite|22|Helmets}}</ref> but slower, long-range fire was preferable,<ref name="Ultimate" /> as it made the weapon effective at any range.<ref name="HC15">{{HelmetCollectionCite|15|Helmets}}</ref> Its power output could be controlled by its user, and included a [[stun]],<ref name="Ultimate" /> low, and maximum power setting. The rifle's replaceable tibanna gas cartridges could yield 500 shots of charged plasma bolts when set on low power, and its blaster power pack could yield 50 shots when fully charged, which gave the rifle a total magazine capacity of 550 shots.<ref name="HC25">{{HelmetCollectionCite|25|Helmets}}</ref>

[[File:Senateguard_detail.png|thumb|right|200px|The elite Senate Commandos were known to use a special version of the DC-15A with a wooden stock]]
If set on its maximum power, the DC-15A could hit a target ten [[kilometer]]s away when equipped with a [[Tripod (weapon attachment)|tripod]],<ref name="HC25" /> fire 300 shots, and leave a 0.5 [[meter]]-wide hole in [[ferrocrete]].<ref name="AEYNTK" /> It could also punch a hole through laminate armor from one thousand meters away, and destroy lightly armored vehicles at closer ranges.<ref name="Rise" /> The weapon's built-in [[Targeting computer|targeting systems]] could be connected to the [[heads-up display]] of a [[clone trooper]]'s [[helmet]], making the blaster even more deadly.<ref name="Ultimate" /> In addition, many [[Clone sharpshooter|clone marksmen]] adopted the DC-15A blaster rifle as their primary weapon, equipping it with a [[Tripod (weapon attachment)|tripod]], [[gyroscopic stabilizer]]s, and long-range optical systems,<ref name="Rise" /> which worked in conjunction with the [[Hologram|holographic]] data readouts inside of their helmets. The blaster could also be equipped with an [[ascension cable]].<ref name="Ultimate" />

A special version of the DC-15A blaster rifle with a [[wood]]en stock was created for the elite [[Coruscant]] [[Senate Commando]]s.<ref name="HC22" /> The blaster rifle also had other variants, including the [[DC-15LE]] used by [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s,<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> and the [[DC-15x sniper rifle]] used by [[Advanced Recon Commando]]s.<ref name="DC15X">{{Legion|set=ARC Troopers Unit Expansion|cardname=DC-15x ARC Trooper}}</ref> The [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Purge Trooper]] [[Purge Trooper Commander|Commanders]]<ref name="Vader 13" /> and [[Elite Squad Trooper]]s used modified DC-15A blaster rifles that fired red-colored blaster bolts and had a scope.<ref name="Replacements">{{TBB|Replacements}}</ref>

==History==
===Creation===
[[File:FivesBlaster-CC.jpg|thumb|left|250px|All clone cadets received extensive training with the DC-15A blaster rifle on [[Kamino]]]]
Prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas]], a member of the [[Jedi High Council]], came to believe that [[the galaxy]] would be plunged into war. Fearing the worst for the [[Galactic Republic]], he advocated for the creation of an [[army]] to defend it. Dismissed and cast out of the [[Jedi Council|council]], Sifo-Dyas secretly contacted the [[extragalactic]] [[Kaminoan]]s and commissioned them to create an army of [[Cloning|clone]] [[soldier]]s, which he led them to believe was for the Republic.<ref name="SifoDyas">{{Databank|sifo-dyas|Sifo-Dyas}}</ref> Using the [[DNA]] of the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] as a [[Clone template|template]], the Kaminoans began work on the creation of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="CT DB">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref>

Physically identical to each other, clone troopers didn't need their weapons and [[Clone trooper armor|armor]] to be varied in height or form to fit individual [[trooper]]s. Every piece of equipment manufactured for the Grand Army of the Republic was precisely constructed to fit every clone trooper perfectly, and to maximize their combat effectiveness. Manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]] exclusively for the clone army, the DC-15A blaster rifle was one of the standard-issue weapons of the Grand Army,<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> and every [[clone cadet]] had to go through extensive training with it to fully qualify as Republic clone troopers.<ref name="HC22" />

===The Clone Wars===
[[File:Rifle-Clone.png|thumb|right|250px|A clone trooper wields a DC-15A]]
The DC-15A blaster rifle was favored by many clones over the smaller [[DC-15A blaster carbine]], including members of the elite [[501st Legion]], who considered the blaster easier to wield.<ref name="HC16" /> [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|Advanced Recon Force Scout Troopers]] also preferred the weapon, as it provided them with excellent long range capabilities.<ref name="HC37">{{HelmetCollectionCite|37|Helmets}}</ref> Furthermore, certain [[Clone Commander]]s also had a preference for the DC-15A blaster rifle. The [[commander]] of the [[Galactic Marines]], [[Bacara]] fully trusted the DC-15A's reliability in combat.<ref name="HC22" /> [[Cody]]<ref name="HC25">{{HelmetCollectionCite|25|Helmets}}</ref> and [[Ponds]] also preferred the weapon.<ref name="HC">{{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Helmets}}</ref>

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Galactic Republic [[First Battle of Geonosis|engaged]] Separatist forces on the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]], beginning the galaxy-wide conflict known as the Clone Wars. During the battle, Republic clone troopers used the DC-15A blaster rifle for the first time against the [[Separatist Droid Army]], charging towards the enemy under the command of their [[Jedi General]]s. Using the rifle, the Galactic Republic successfully defeated the Separatist forces on the planet, and claimed their first victory of the war.<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Throughout the rest of the Clone Wars, the DC-15A blaster rifle continued to be used by Republic forces on many worlds, including during the [[Battle of Teth]], where its ascension cable attachment was first used.<ref name="Ultimate" />

===Imperial Era===
Following the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the Galactic Empire, and the [[Great Jedi Purge|execution]] of the [[Jedi Order]],<ref name="ROTS" /> the DC-15A blaster rifle continued to see use within the new Imperial regime.<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref> The Empire's clone [[stormtrooper]]s continued to use the rifle<ref name="Vader 10" /> until it was phased out by the introduction of the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]]. Many Imperial clone troopers objected to the introduction of the E-11, claiming that it was less accurate than the DC-15A blaster rifle.<ref name="HC22" /> The DC-15A blaster rifle design was in-part inspiration of the [[T-21 light repeating blaster]].<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" />

[[File:Second Sister Purge Troopers JFO.png|thumb|right|300px|Purge troopers utilized DC-15A blaster rifles.]]
Purge Troopers of the [[Inquisitorius]] used DC-15A blaster rifles<ref name="Vader 13" /> and the modified DC-15LE variant.<ref name="SWFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> The [[Elite Squad]] Troopers created by the [[Project War-Mantle|War-Mantle]] project also used similar modified DC-15A blaster rifles.<ref name="Replacements" />

The surviving clones [[Wolffe]] and [[Gregor]] wielded these weapons when they encountered [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[Garazeb Orrelios]]. Upon seeing [[Kanan Jarrus' lightsaber]], Wolffe panicked and fired a few bolts which almost resulted in a gun fight. [[Rex]] convinced him to stand down and Rex apologized to Jarrus for the "weapon malfunction". Rex later used one to snipe a [[Dwarf probe droid]].<ref name="Lost Commanders">{{Rebels|The Lost Commanders}}</ref> When they were attacked by an [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]], Sabine Wren used a DC-15A to shoot it down.<ref name="ROR">{{Rebels|Relics of the Old Republic}}</ref> 

Just after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] led a group of savaged [[BX-series droid commando]] to attack former Naboo officer [[Commodex Tahn]]. He had used the blaster to defend himself.<ref name="V10">[[Darth Vader (2015) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 10]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The DC-15A blaster rifle first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Canon]] in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="AOTC" /> which was released on [[May 16]], [[2002]].<ref name="Season 2 Directors">{{ThisWeek|u0Z7i0LnXOg|Mandalorian Season 2 Directors, We Celebrate a Birthday, and More!|sw_url=video/mandalorian-season-2-directors-we-celebrate-a-birthday-and-more}}</ref> The blaster rifle was later identified in the ''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]'',<ref name="Factivity">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> a young-readers activity book from [[Dorling Kindersley]], released on [[June 30]], [[2014]].<ref name="DK">{{DK|url=uk/book/9781409352600-star-wars-ultimate-factivity-collection|text=Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars}}</ref> The DC-15A blaster rifle's name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first identified in the ''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]'',<ref name="AoTC Visual">''[[Star Wars: Attack of the Clones: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> the third book in the ''[[Star Wars Visual Dictionaries]]'' series, published by Doring Kindersley on [[April 23]], [[2002]]<ref name="VDRelease">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/news20020227.html|text=Book Preview: DK Episode II Books}}</ref> The DC-15A blaster rifle's design was based on the real-world [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] [[Wikipedia:MG 34|MG 34 machine gun]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />
