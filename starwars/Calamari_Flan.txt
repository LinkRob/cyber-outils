'''Calamari Flan''' was a form of [[currency]] that had a disc-shaped [[Color|white]] and blue coinage. While Flan could maintain its shape, it would squish under pressure unlike the rigid [[New Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]].<ref name="Mando1" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1|the appearance of the [[Mudhorn]]'s cave}}</ref> due to the recent collapse of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] refused to accept Imperial [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] as payment from [[Greef Karga]], an agent of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]], for capturing four [[Bounty|bounties]]. Despite Karga claiming that the Imperial credits could still be spent, Djarin got up to leave. The guild agent instead offered Calamari Flan, although he only possessed enough to pay half. Djarin accepted the payment.<ref name="Mando1" />

Djarin later used Calamari Flan on the [[Water|estuary]] [[moon]] of [[Trask]] to pay a [[Unidentified Mon Calamari server|Mon Calamari server]] for information.<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Calamari Flan first appeared in "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]]," the first episode of the [[Disney+]] live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Mando1" /> which aired on [[November 12]], [[2019]].<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|work=[https://dtcimedia.disney.com Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]|archivedate=20230315011507}}</ref> For the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> the currency was developed to be a collectible item as part of the "Nix Treasure" collection, though it was scrapped from the final product.<ref name="Outlaws Unused">{{ArtStation|subdomain=roachiedespawned|url=projects/YGGG6d|name=[[Leo Fulford]]|text=Nix Treasures|date=2024-10-03|quote=Here is a gallery of the collectible Nix Treasures I created start to finish for the recent release of Star Wars Outlaws.|archivefile=File:LeoFulford-NixTreasures-ArtStation.png}}</ref>
