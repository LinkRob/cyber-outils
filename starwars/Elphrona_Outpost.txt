The '''Elphrona Outpost''' was a [[Jedi]] [[Jedi outpost|outpost]] during the [[High Republic Era]] that was located on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Elphrona]]. The [[Jedi Order]] used the outpost as storage for treasure and artifacts.

==Complement==
The Elphrona Outpost housed treasures such as dozens of [[Jedi holocron]]s and a [[lightsaber rifle]].<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />

==History==
{{Quote|Well, hello there. Everything here is ours. We're going to take it&hellip;but ''how'' we take it is up to you. Your call, friends.''"<br />"''You're wrong. So, so wrong. This is a ''Jedi outpost''. Everything in it belongs to the Jedi Order, which, at the moment, means me. I am the last Jedi.|Ren and Jedi Master Luke Skywalker|<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />}}
[[File:BenLukeAndRen-TheRiseOfKyloRen2.jpg|thumb|right|300px|During the New Republic Era, Jedi Master Luke Skywalker and his Padawan Ben Solo would face the Knights of Ren in the Elphrona Outpost.]]
In [[382 BBY]]<ref name="382 Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Rinn (Duros)|Rinn]] was [[marshal]] of the Elphrona Outpost.<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> Later in the era, Jedi Masters [[Porter Engle]], [[Loden Greatstorm]], Greatstorm's [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]], and [[Indeera Stokes]] were stationed at the outpost to keep the peace<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> in [[232 BBY]].<ref name="232 Date note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> In [[19 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Skywalker: A Family at War]]'' states that the mission to [[Elphrona]] took place the [[Standard year|year]] [[Ben Solo]] turned fourteen. ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that Solo was born twenty-nine years prior to the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the mission took place in [[19 ABY]].</ref> Jedi Master [[Luke Skywalker]], along with his Padawan [[Ben Solo]] and the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Lor San Tekka]] [[Mission to Elphrona|visited]] the outpost as Skywalker was attempting to rebuild the [[Jedi Order]]. The trio encountered [[Ren (human)|Ren]] and the [[Knights of Ren]], who also wished to claim the artifacts inside the outpost.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2" />
