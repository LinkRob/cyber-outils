'''DUM-4''' was a [[DUM-series pit droid]] that was allocated to pit crew of [[podracer]] [[Ody Mandrell]] during the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] at the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] to [[32 BBY]].</ref> During the race, Mandrell pulled over in the arena for maintenance and shouted orders to the pit droids in his crew causing one to race round the vehicle and give orders to DUM-4.<ref name="TPM" />

DUM-4 then responded by marching toward the other pit droid in front of the podracer's [[engine]]s, which immediately sucked in the [[droid]]. The engine was completely destroyed, knocking Mandrell out of the race, but DUM-4 survived and was ejected out of the other side of the engine where it wailed in surprise.<ref name="TPM" /> Like all DUM-series pit droids, DUM-4 was a [[Maintenance droid|repair droid]] manufactured by [[Serv-O-Droid, Inc.]]<ref name="USW" /> and stood 1.19 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="Height" /> It had [[Color|brown]] plating and a black [[sensor]].<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
DUM-4 first appeared in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> The character was first named in the current [[canon]] in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''.<ref name="Geektionary" />
