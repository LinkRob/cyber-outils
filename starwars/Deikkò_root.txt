'''Deikkò root''' was a [[plant]] cultivated on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[mashed deikkò root]] was served with [[Che Mosskà]] at the [[Irio]] [[foodstuff|street food]] stall in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] of [[Mirogana]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Toshara.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics}}
During that same [[Standard year|year]], the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] and her [[Merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] share a meal of Che Mosskà which included mashed deikkò root.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}} <ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Deikkò root was mentioned as an ingredent in the "Che Mosskà" galactic street food perks in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
