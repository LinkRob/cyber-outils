'''Combat engineers''' were exceptional [[Skakoan]] [[engineer]]s deployed by the [[Techno Union]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Equipped with modified [[pressure suit]]s, these Skakoans were proficient fighters and, like the Techno Union's [[Commando (Techno Union)|commandos]], could be packed into [[Shekelesh-class freight gunship|''Shekelesh''-class freight gunships]].<ref name="Rise" />
