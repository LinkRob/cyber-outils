'''Cyclor''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> located in the [[Cyclor system]] in the [[Alui sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] that was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[insectoid]] [[Cyclorrian]] [[species]]. A relatively small planet, Cyclor appeared [[Color|green]] and brown from [[Realspace|space]] and was [[orbit]]ed by vast [[Cyclor Shipyards|shipyards]].<ref name="The Rising Storm" />
