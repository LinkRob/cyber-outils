Around the [[Standard year|year]] [[24 BBY]],<ref name="FlashbackDate" /> the [[Alpha (Kenari)|Alpha]] tribes people on the [[planet]] [[Kenari]]<ref name="Kassa" /> launched an '''exploratory mission to investigate a downed craft'''.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|kerri|Kerri}}</ref> It was their hope to secure supplies from the crashed [[Galactic Republic]] [[transpo corsair]].<ref name="Season One Companion" /> The Republic ship had the [[Separatist hex icon|logo]] of<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/andor/andor-season-1-episode-3-episode-guide|text="Reckoning" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}}</ref> the recently [[Raxus Address|formed]]<ref name="Founding note">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' dates the [[Raxus Address|formation]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to twenty-four years before [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which corresponds to [[24 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Episode Guide" /> and the crew had succumbed to a poison.<ref name="Reckoning" /> A surviving [[Republic officer]] [[Death|killed]] the [[Unidentified Alpha leader|Alpha leader]], leading to the children killing the [[officer]] but leaving with their leader's body. However, one boy, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Kassa]], was left angered<ref name="That Would" /> and entered the crashed [[starship]], where he found the remains of the poisoned crew and began destroying his surroundings. There, he was found by the [[Andor family]] of [[scavenger]]s, who took him off-world to keep him save from an incoming Republic reprisal.<ref name="Reckoning" />

==Behind the scenes==
The exact faction to which the crashed ship belonged is left unclear. While the vessel and its crew don the markings of the Separatist Alliance,<ref name="That Would" /> the Andor family identify the crew as Republic officers and notice a Republic frigate is incoming.<ref name="Reckoning" /> The trivia gallery for "Reckoning" only vaguely touches on the matter, claiming the symbol worn by the crew is that of "the eventual Separatist Alliance."<ref name="Episode Guide" /> The articles "[[Kassa (article)|Kassa]]"<ref name="Kassaarticle">{{InsiderCite|217|Kassa (article)}}</ref> and [[Star Wars: Andor Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Andor'' Season One Companion]] in [[Star Wars Insider 217|''Star Wars Insider'' 217]] firmly identify the vessel as a Republic ship.<ref name="Season One Companion" /> While the [[Star Wars Insider 219|''Star Wars Insider'' 219]] article "[[Galactic Britain II: Cassian's Travels]]" claims the ship was a Separatist cruiser,<ref name="Galactic Britain">{{InsiderCite|219|Galactic Britain II: Cassian's Travels}}</ref> [[Star Wars Insider 220|''Star Wars Insider'' 220]] repeated that it was a Republic transport in the article "[[On Program]]."<ref name="OnProgram">{{InsiderCite|220|On Program}}</ref> As such, this article follows the majority of sources<ref name="Season One Companion" /><ref name="Kassaarticle" /><ref name="OnProgram" /> and the Andor family's comments<ref name="Reckoning" /> by assuming the ship is a Republic vessel that&mdash;for reasons not yet revealed&mdash;was adorned with a Separatist logo and had a crew wearing the same.
