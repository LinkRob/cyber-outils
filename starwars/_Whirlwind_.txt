"'''Whirlwind'''" was the callsign of a [[First Order]] [[TIE/wi modified interceptor]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]]. "Whirlwind" once flew into conflict in their [[starfighter]] against three [[BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="Whirlwind Card" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Whirlwind" was created for the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' in the card of the same name released in the [[2021]] [[Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack]].<ref name="Whirlwind Card" />
