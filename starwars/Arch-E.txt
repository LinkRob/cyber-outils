'''Arch-E''' was a [[navigator]] [[droid]] who worked with the [[smuggler]] [[Jayko]], and was also able to play the [[drum]]s. Arch-E and Jayko were present in a [[cantina]] when the smuggler's former crewmate [[Tan]] entered, thanking Jayko for writing about him in his book, ''[[A Smuggler's Life]]''.<ref name="JTC-E10" />
