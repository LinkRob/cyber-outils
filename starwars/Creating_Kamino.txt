'''Creating Kamino''' is a featurette included in the Blu-ray and DVD release of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Three]]. It covers the development of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episodes "[[Clone Cadets]]" and "[[ARC Troopers (episode)|ARC Troopers]]."

The featurette is divided into nine thematics:
*[[Kamino]] Design
*[[Water]]
*[[AQ-series battle droid|Aqua Droids]]
*[[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC Troopers]]
*[[Trident-class assault ship|Trident Drill Ship]]
*[[Battle of Kamino|Space Battle]]
*Twinkly Bits
*[[Shaak Ti]]
*[[Domino Squad]]
