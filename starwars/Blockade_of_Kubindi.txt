By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date note" /> the [[Galactic Empire]] had '''blockaded''' the [[Planet|world]] of [[Kubindi]] and [[Slavery|enslaved]] its [[Homeworld|native]] [[Kubaz]] [[species]]<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot" /> at some point after the [[declaration of the Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="Rebel Declaration note" /> Imperial Kubaz [[spy]] [[Garindan ezz Zavor]] visited Kubindi while Imperial [[Imperial Navy|forces]] [[orbit]]ed the planet, and fled after seeing the Empire's firepower over the world. Determined to liberate his homeland, Garindan [[Currency|purchased]] codes to get through the [[blockade]] and obtained enough [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to hire a [[starship]] to take him to Kubindi.<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot" />

==Behind the scenes==
The blockade was first mentioned in the short story ''[[The Secrets of Long Snoot]]'' written by [[Delilah S. Dawson]]<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot" /> and included in the [[2017]]<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-from-a-certain-point-of-view-anthology-book-celebrates-40-years-of-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text=''Star Wars: From a Certain Point of View'' Anthology Book Celebrates 40 Years of A Galaxy Far, Far Away}}</ref> novel ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="The Secrets of Long Snoot" />
