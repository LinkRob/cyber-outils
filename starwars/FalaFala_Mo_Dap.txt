'''FalaFala Mo Dap''' was a [[hyperspace]] [[prospector]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He owned a [[Nav droid|navidroid]] [[N9 (droid)|N9]], and had a rivalry with the prospector [[Trenwick Mashwick]].<ref name="Art of THR" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
FalaFala Mo Dap was a character designed for ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing program, but ended up being cut. Concept art was drawn by [[Daniel José Older]].<ref name="Art of THR" />
