'''Ashsap trees''' were a type of [[tree]]. During the [[Rebellion on Akiva|rebellion]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[Zabrak]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Jas Emari]] smashed the head of the [[Ithorian]] bouncer [[Herf (Ithorian)|Herf]] into a wall at [[the Alcazar]] [[cantina]]. Emari watched him topple, and thought it appeared similar to the felling of an ashsap tree.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ashsap trees were mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
