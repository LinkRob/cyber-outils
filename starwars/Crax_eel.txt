'''Crax eels''' were a type of [[eel]]. At one point, the [[Sabi Sabo Acidy's species|alien]] [[crime lord]] [[Sabi Sabo Acidy]] [[Diet|fed]] his [[Sabi Sabo Acidy's brother|brother]] to a pack of feral crax eels for a bottle of [[Squig (beverage)|squig]]. Eventually the incident became known to the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and [[New Republic]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 3" /> [[Director]] [[Meteora Gray]].<ref name="Chaos Agent 1">[[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 1|''Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent'' 1]]</ref>

No earlier than [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{Marvel|url=articles/comics/star-wars-doctor-aphra-chaos-agent-1-cover-the-new-republic|text=Star Wars' Doctor Aphra Navigates the New Republic in New Ongoing Comic Series}} establishes the events of ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]'' take place after the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]].</ref> after being informed Acidy was to [[Mission to Felucia (New Republic)|return]] a precious [[Totem of Marthxi|totem]] to the [[Campi]] tribe in exchange for remaining in the [[New Republic]]'s good graces, Aphra&mdash;who worked for the Republic&mdash;asked if it was referring to the same Acidy who had fed his [[Gender|brother]] to the crax eels. In response, Gray confirmed it was.<ref name="Chaos Agent 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crax eels were mentioned in the [[Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent 3|third issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]''. The issue was written by [[Cherish Chen]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Chaos Agent 1" /> on [[August 27]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122234/star_wars_doctor_aphra_-_chaos_agent_2025_3|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra - Chaos Agent (2025) #3}}</ref>
