'''Darth Vader's armor''', also known as '''Darth Vader's suit''', was a suit of [[armor]] worn by the [[cyborg]] [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Designed with [[life support]] technology, the armor ensured Vader's survival following the severe [[lightsaber]] and [[Fire|burn]] injuries that he sustained in a [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] with [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on [[Mustafar]]. In addition to its practical use, Vader's armor inspired fear and terror across [[the galaxy]] during the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. With Vader's [[death]] in the [[Battle of Endor]], the armor was [[Funeral of Anakin Skywalker|destroyed]]. However, the [[helmet]] remained and eventually fell into the possession of Vader's [[Skywalker family|grandson]], the [[First Order]] [[warlord]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].

==Description==
{{Quote|The mask operated in tandem with his armor to regulate his breathing, facilitate his speech, and protect his body from harmful contaminants. Few realized its true function, as they were too fearful to see beyond its grim visage.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SotS" />}}
[[File:VadersArmorSchematic-2020DarthVader14.png|thumb|left|250px|The schematic of Vader's armor]]
[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader's]] [[armor]]<ref name="Learn to Draw">''[[Learn to Draw Star Wars: Villains]]''</ref> was made of materials including [[obsidian]], [[plasteel]], [[durasteel]],<ref name="Bust Collection 1" /> and [[plastoid]];<ref name="Inside Intel" /> although its design inspired fear and symbolized the strength of the [[Galactic Empire]], it was also critical to Vader's survival after his [[Duel on Mustafar|defeat]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="SotS" />

The armor's [[belt]] and chest plate held the controls for the [[life support]] system, ensuring that Vader's body received enough [[oxygen]], nutrients, and medicine; through this system, Vader could endure nearly any environment, including the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="LotS" /> and the depths of the sea, although the suit's on board oxygen reserves wouldn't last for long and the suit was prone to rupturing if exposed to a strong enough external pressure. Vader could however use the Force to manipulate external conditions around him to form an environment he could comfortably operate in<ref name="DV15">[[Darth Vader (2017) 15|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 15]]</ref> and even deactivate his [[Breath mask|breathing apparatus]] to approach a target stealthily, although the process greatly weakened him if prolonged.<ref name="DV18">[[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]]</ref> The suit could also withstand [[technopathy]] from [[Eternal Rur]]. In addition to its life-support functions, the [[Sith Lord]]'s [[helmet]] contributed to giving him a frightening appearance, and could be removed to use a feeding tube in his [[Darth Vader's meditation chamber|private chamber]] due to his inability to [[Diet|eat]] normally.<ref name="UFC">''[[Ultimate Factivity Collection: Star Wars]]''</ref> The high pressure and hyper-[[oxygen]] supplied<ref name="ManWarriorCommander">''[[Star Wars Master Models: Darth Vader]]''</ref> while the private chamber was closed around him<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref> gave Vader a chance to safely live without his armor.<ref name="ManWarriorCommander" />

The helmet, which was created using an alloy of durasteel, plasteel, and obsidian,<ref name="Bust Collection 1" /> had a sloped design for its sides and back<ref name="Episode IV" /> similar to that of the helmets on ancient [[Sith armor]].<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> The helmet created Vader's well-known menacing growl, as his natural voice was extremely weak from vocal-cord damage. It contained neural needles that painfully connected with the top of Vader's [[Bone|skull]] and spine to form one interconnected unit. Without these, Vader could not use his [[cybernetics]].<ref name="LotS" /> The suit's functions were linked to doctor Cylo's personal controller which he kept in his person at all times as a last-ditch contingency; with the click of a button, all of the armor's cybernetics could be deactivated, bringing Vader to his knees under their sheer weight. Vader was, however, able to overcome this restriction through the Force.<ref name="DV23">[[Darth Vader (2015) 23|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 23]]</ref> The armor had ten protective layers, each made of very strong alloy that could protect Vader from some explosions and weapons. Vader had four artificial [[limb]]s as a result of his injuries on Mustafar and at the hand of [[Dooku]] [[First Battle of Geonosis|on]] [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

[[File:DarthVaderHelmetParts-SW100Objects.png|thumb|right|300px|The apparatus of Vader's mask was designed to support his breathing.]]
The armor's [[boot]]s featured [[magnetic clamp]]s, which could [[magnet]]ically adhere the suit to a metallic surface,<ref name="DV5">[[Darth Vader (2015) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 5]]</ref> while the legs were heavily armored and able to withstand at least glancing blows from a [[lightsaber]].<ref name="DV3">[[Darth Vader (2017) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 3]]</ref> He weighed 120 [[kilogram]]s in his armor,<ref name="AbEverything" /> leaving deep, tank-like tracks in the mud wherever he walked,<ref name="DV18">[[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]]</ref> and was 2.03 [[meter]]s tall in it.<ref name="AbEverything" /> The armor also featured a remote to prep Vader's [[Darth Vader's black Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|interceptor]] for combat.<ref name="LotS" /> In the event of damage to one set of armor, Vader had a backup set which he kept in his quarters on-board whatever vessel he happened to be traveling in at the time. This proved useful when he was attacked by unusual [[insect]]s on [[Batuu]], which encased much of his armor in stone.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

Although Vader at first considered his armor acceptable,<ref name="Dark Lord 1">[[Darth Vader (2017) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 1]]</ref> he later modified it to his liking after its first bout of damage.<ref name="DV6">[[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]]</ref> The suit was described as "junk" by the [[Ought-Six|leader]] of the [[Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon]], who had been promised it contained advanced technology by [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]]. Despite Vader's reputation as the [[Galaxy|Galactic]] Empire's greatest weapon, a scan of his armor revealed to the droid captain that Vader was made of standard components, such as life support, a fusion furnace and a respirator. A further scan revealed the suit's components were no more technologically-advanced than the droids' own parts.<ref name="Vader 9">[[Darth Vader (2020) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 9]]</ref> The suit was also vulnerable to hacking, as a skilled operator could interface with Vader's central suit computer to send a [[holomessage]] on his behalf, manipulating his vocabulator to speak and his mechanic limbs to move.<ref name="Aphra 40">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 40|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 40]]</ref>

==History==
===Anakin Skywalker's vision===
{{Quote|Know yourself. Know what you will become!|The Son, to Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Ghosts">{{TCW|Ghosts of Mortis}}</ref>}}
[[File:AnakinVaderVision-GOM.png|thumb|left|250px|During the Clone Wars, Anakin Skywalker beheld a vision of his future as Darth Vader.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], Anakin Skywalker, [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Ahsoka Tano]] accidentally [[Mission to Mortis|discovered]] the [[Mortis monolith]], a gateway to another [[planet]], while answering an ancient [[Jedi]] [[Distress signal|distress code]]. The trio then entered the world of [[Mortis]], where they met a family of [[Force wielder]]s:<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> the [[Father (Mortis)|Father]], the [[Daughter]], and the [[Son]]. The [[Parent|Father]] had called Skywalker to the planet, believing him to be the prophesied [[Chosen One]]. The family then put Skywalker through a series of trials, to test whether or not he was the Chosen One.<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref>

During a confrontation between Skywalker and the Son, the Son offered to show Skywalker a [[Force vision|vision]] of the future. Through [[the Force]] Skywalker saw his [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|wife]] in distress, the [[Great Jedi Purge|deaths of many Jedi]], the evasive [[Darth Sidious]] unleashing his full power, his duel with Kenobi, and the [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] of [[Alderaan]]. The vision concluded with a ghostly image of Skywalker's future [[Cyborg|cybernetic]] helmet. While the Father would soon wipe Skywalker's memory of the extrapolations of his future,<ref name="Ghosts" /> each of these apparitions would prove to come true.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Darth Vader's reconstruction===
{{Quote|Upon my command, Vader's broken frame was remade, fitted with cybernetic parts and sheathed in obsidian armor. He reemerged as a towering symbol of the Galactic Empire's might.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SotS" />}}
[[File:VaderOperation-ROTS.png|thumb|right|250px|Defeated on Mustafar, Vader's body was taken to Coruscant for surgical reconstruction.]]
After his fall to the [[dark side of the Force]] and new beginning as [[Darth]] Vader, Skywalker was sent to the planet Mustafar, where he [[Mission to Mustafar|assassinated]] the entire [[Executive Separatist Council]], ending the Clone Wars. In the aftermath of the slaughter, Vader [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] his former [[Jedi Master]], Obi-Wan Kenobi. Vader lost the fight after having both legs and his left arm severed by Kenobi. Vader then tumbled down the [[Volcano|volcanic]] planet's surface, near a river of [[Magma|lava]], where his robes burst into [[Fire|flames]], swiftly consuming his entire body and impairing several of his biological processes. Kenobi left his former apprentice for [[Death|dead]].<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:VaderSidious-ROTS.jpg|thumb|left|250px|In addition to his cybernetics, Vader received a suit of armor that was vital to his survival.]]
Unbeknownst to Kenobi, Vader's new [[Sith Master]], Darth Sidious, would soon arrive to his [[Sith apprentice]]'s aid. Sidious ordered the [[scientist]] [[Cylo]] to create Vader's armor, which he did obediently in a long night of [[reconstructive surgery]].<ref name="Darth Vader 20" /> Vader was [[shuttle]]d to the [[Grand Imperial Medical Facility|Grand Republic Medical Facility]] on the planet [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> [[capital]] of the newly [[Proclamation of the New Order|formed]] Galactic Empire. A [[DD-13 medical assistant droid]] fitted him with cybernetic implants<ref name="JT">[[Star Wars: Journey Through Space (2015)|''Star Wars: Journey Through Space'']]</ref> while a [[2-1B surgical droid]] picked away any remaining chars of [[clothing]] from the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Dark Lord's]] body. An [[FX-9 surgical assistant|FX-9]] [[medical droid]] supplied Vader with [[blood]] transfusions during the surgery.<ref name="UltimateSW" /> Vader required a permanent breathing apparatus, a result of his encounter with the flames, with the vocabulator altering his voice into a deep growl.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Life in the armor===
{{Quote|Your armor, Lord Vader. How do you find it?''"<br />"''It is&hellip;''acceptable''.|Darth Sidious and Darth Vader|<ref name="Dark Lord 1" />}}
After being equipped with the suit, Vader described the armor as acceptable when asked about it by his master.<ref name="Dark Lord 1" /> Darth Vader wore the armor for over two [[Standard decade|decades]], until his redemption and death. He initially hated the armor, but after living with it for five [[Standard year|years]], he embraced how it isolated him from the rest of [[the galaxy]], allowing him to concentrate on becoming a pure instrument of the dark side.<ref name="LotS" /> Almost immediately after recovering from his operation, Vader was sent by Sidious to collect a lightsaber for himself; to do so, Vader searched for takers of the [[Barash Vow]] who, having willingly isolated themselves from the Jedi Order, were more likely to have survived the [[Great Jedi Purge]].<ref name="DV2">[[Darth Vader (2017) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 2]]</ref> Eventually, Vader located and attacked Master [[Kirak Infil'a]], a Jedi solely dedicated to combat training, on the moon of [[Al'doleem]]. During their fight, Vader's armor sustained severe damage, leaving him crippled without his left leg and without his central computer.<ref name="DV3" />

[[File:DarthVader-Chrome2023.png|thumb|right|200px|Vader addressed Kenobi from beneath his damaged helmet.]]
To repair himself, Vader manipulated parts from Infil'a's droid [[Arex]] into usable parts to cover the gaps in his armor, successfully upgrading it to defeat the Jedi by endangering Al'doleem civilians.<ref name="DV4">[[Darth Vader (2017) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 4]]</ref> After killing Infil'a and bleeding his lightsaber's [[kyber crystal]] on Mustafar,<ref name="DV5 2017">[[Darth Vader (2017) 5|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 5]]</ref> Vader returned to Coruscant where he was allowed by Sidious to repair his armor as he saw fit, upgrading the unit to his liking. As a test of his new armor systems, Vader engaged the [[Grand Inquisitor]] in a [[Duel in the former Jedi Temple|duel Sidious manipulated into being]], temporarily allowing himself to be challenged until he was certain all the suit's mechanic components worked as expected; he then swiftly disarmed the Inquisitor.<ref name="DV6" /> At least one of Arex's parts, a knee piece, would continue to be part of Vader's armor for years to come.<ref name="Vader (2020) 36">[[Darth Vader (2020) 36|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 36]]</ref>

Nine years prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], Vader engaged in a duel against his former [[Jedi Master|master]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on a small rocky moon. At the end of the duel, Obi-Wan damaged Vader's chest plate and cut open half his helmet, revealing the left side of his scarred face. Kenobi recognized it as he had last seen him after their duel on Mustafar,<ref name="Episode III" /> and addressed him as "Anakin," but Vader told him that he killed Anakin and he was all who remained. Kenobi accepted him no longer taking that name, and referred to him as "Darth" before leaving him.<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref> They would not see each other again until their final rematch on the [[Death Star]], right before the Battle of [[Yavin 4|Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Vader later returned to [[Fortress Vader|his castle]] on Mustafar. After his armor was repaired, he spoke to his master, Sidious, whom he vowed to remain as his Sith apprentice, and never Kenobi's Jedi apprentice again.<ref name="Part VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref>

[[File:Then_you_will_die.png|thumb|left|250px|On Malachor, Ahsoka Tano damaged Vader's helmet and thereby learned the truth of her former mentor, Anakin Skywalker.]]
Three years prior to the Battle of Yavin, Vader engaged in a [[Mission to Malachor|duel]] against his former [[Padawan|apprentice]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], inside the [[Malachor]] [[Malachor Sith Temple|Sith temple]]. During the duel, Vader's helmet suffered considerable damage, disabling his vocal modulator and exposing his right [[eye]]. Vader eventually stumbled out of the temple alive, albeit exhausted and short of breath.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref>

Vader had at least one backup suit of armor, which was onboard the ''Chimaera'' when Vader and Grand Admiral Thrawn went on a mission to [[Batuu]].<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

Prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]], he spent some time in a [[bacta tank]] in his [[Fortress Vader|castle]] on Mustafar before donning the suit again to meet [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

Shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth]], Vader [[Duel on Cloud City|dueled]] his son, [[Luke Skywalker]], aboard [[Cloud City]], a [[mining]] [[city]] floating within the clouds of [[Bespin]]. Skywalker caused minor damage to his father's suit after slashing him on the shoulder. Vader retaliated by severing his son's right hand.<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>

===Legend versus reality===
{{Quote|I got close enough for a ''scan''. And the rest of him looks like standard life support, fusion furnace, respirator&mdash;''junk''!|A captain of the Droid Crush Pirates|<ref name="Vader 9" />}}
[[File:DarthVaderOnExegol-2020DarthVader11.png|thumb|right|220px|The damage to his armor and body forced Vader to utilize scavenged battle droid parts.]]
Vader's encounter with his son motivated his investigation into Luke Skywalker's childhood,<ref name="Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2020) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 1]]</ref> which in turn led him to uncover details about the death of Padmé Amidala.<ref name="Vader 2">[[Darth Vader (2020) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 2]]</ref> Aware of Vader's actions and the grief that he experienced due to the memory of his late wife, Darth Sidious re-trained his apprentice in the ways of the Sith by inflicting pain on him. Through the Force the Emperor destroyed Vader's cybernetics and caused extensive damage to his armor, returning Vader to the limbless state that he was in after his duel with Obi-Wan Kenobi on Mustafar. Sidious then left Vader on Mustafar and ordered him to survive on his own, but without the use of the Force.<ref name="Vader 6">[[Darth Vader (2020) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 6]]</ref>

Hunted by the Emperor's assassin, Ochi and a group of [[droid]] [[pirate]]s,<ref name="Vader 9" /> Vader crawled to the abandoned facility where he executed the members of the Separatist Council. There he scavenged various [[battle droid]] parts to use as replacements for the cybernetic limbs that his master destroyed.<ref name="Vader 7">[[Darth Vader (2020) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 7]]</ref> The droids that aided Ochi did so for the opportunity to scavenge Vader's artificial components, believing that the cyborg Sith possessed advanced technology by virtue of his reputation as the Emperor's enforcer. However, a scan of the Sith Lord revealed to the droid [[captain]] that Vader's cybernetics were obsolete and not state of the art as he had been led to believe.<ref name="Vader 9" />

[[File:DarthVaderRebuilt-2020DarthVader12.png|thumb|left|250px|Vader's armor was repaired during his reconstruction on Coruscant.]]
Following his journey to [[Exegol]], Vader regained his place at Sidious' side and the two Sith Lords returned to Coruscant. Vader was then rebuilt at<ref name="Vader 12">[[Darth Vader (2020) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 12]]</ref> the same facility where he was first reconstructed as an armored cyborg.<ref name="Episode III" /> As a result, Vader gained new cybernetics and his armor was fully repaired. He allowed himself to experience the physical pain of undergoing surgical reconstruction, having elected to remain conscious throughout the procedure. After his restoration, Vader undertook a mission to hunt and kill his son in order to keep his place at Sidious' side.<ref name="Vader 12" />

After an [[Battle against Jul's droid army|encounter with the Handmaidens]], Vader was unable to control the Force as the [[Force Wave]] returned, resulting in his out of control powers beginning to tear apart the ''Executor'', thereby presenting another threat to the Empire: himself. Sabé, who quickly understood something had gone wrong with Vader's Force powers, took him to an [[Unidentified astronomical object (Sabé)|astronomical object]] as it would give the Sith Lord a place to practice and rediscover control over his powers. However Vader threw her out into the sea in his next blast of power. Vader then lifted the wreckage of his [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] and made repairs to his armor. Shortly after, Vader was attacked by a group of rebels. Using his modified suit Vader used a staff and [[personal energy shield]] to defeat them.<ref name="Vader 34">[[Darth Vader (2020) 34|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 34]]</ref>

===Death and funeral pyre===
{{Quote|Luke, help me take this mask off.''"<br />"''But you'll die.''"<br />"''Nothing can stop that now. Just for once. Let me look on you with my own eyes.|Anakin Skywalker and Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
[[File:Dvbones1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Vader was fatally injured while protecting his son, Luke Skywalker from his Sith Master, Darth Sidious.]]
Aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], Vader dueled Skywalker for the last time while in the presence of the [[Galactic Emperor]], Darth Sidious. Towards the end of the duel, Skywalker retreated to a hiding spot and refused to fight anymore. To bring Skywalker out of hiding, Vader taunted him by threatening to corrupt Skywalker's sister, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. Succumbing to his rage, Skywalker burst from his hiding place and viciously attacked Vader, beating him down and, in the final blow of the duel, cutting off Vader's right hand. Satisfied, the Emperor exhorted Luke to complete his acceptance of the dark side by killing the defenseless Darth Vader and taking his place, thus betraying his apprentice. Despite his fury, Skywalker had an epiphany when he saw the damaged stump of Vader's right forearm, reminding him of his own mechanical right hand. Realizing he had already begun his fall to the dark side, Skywalker chose to ignore his rage and relented, showing Darth Vader mercy.<ref name="Episode VI" />

[[File:Anakinredeemed.png|thumb|left|312x312px|Luke sees his father's face for the first and last time.]]
Angered at the loss of a potential Sith, the Emperor [[torture]]d Skywalker with [[Force lightning]], before eventually deciding to kill him. Unable to bear the sight of his son suffering, Vader returned to the light side and subsequently turned on his master, by throwing him down a [[reactor]] shaft, effectively killing Sidious. During the conflict, the suit was critically damaged by the lightning. No longer Darth Vader, the redeemed Jedi re-embraced his former identity, Anakin Skywalker. Realizing that his life was coming to an end, Anakin requested Luke to remove his helmet so he could see his face once, to which the young Jedi Knight obliged. A content Anakin acknowledged to his son that he was right about him, before peacefully dying<ref name="Episode VI" /> and his body disappeared into the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="The Story of Darth Vader">[[Star Wars: The Story of Darth Vader (2015)|''Star Wars: The Story of Darth Vader'']]</ref>

[[File:Anakinfuneral.png|thumb|right|250px|Anakin Skywalker passed into the Force, and the armor of Darth Vader was burned on Endor.]]
Luke escaped the destruction of the second Death Star with his father's empty armor. Upon landing on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]], Luke burned the armor in a [[Funeral of Anakin Skywalker|funeral pyre]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> Shortly before her wedding ceremony on Endor, Leia Organa came across Vader's funeral pyre. Realizing what it was, Leia started to become emotional but resolved not to cry for him. After gently tapping his helmet with her toe, Leia picked up the helmet, recalling the torture she had been forced to face at his hands on the Death Star. Leia then made her feelings known, angered at Vader's pride and letting him know that she was glad he was dead. She then hurled the helmet back into the ash of his funeral pyre, screaming that Vader would never be her father.<ref name="Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

===Post-destruction===
{{Quote|After Vader's death, the remains of his helmet were recovered and eventually obtained by his grandson, Kylo Ren. Its charred shell now serves as a constant reminder of the boy's dark legacy.|Darth Sidious|<ref name="SotS" />}}
[[File:DarthVaderHelmet-SW100Objects.png|thumb|left|200px|The burnt remnant of Vader's mask came into the possession of his grandson, the dark warrior known as Kylo Ren.]]
The disfigured helmet of the infamous suit was scavenged from the [[funeral]] pyre,<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> and eventually found its way into the possession of Vader's grandson, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], a [[Force-sensitive]] dark side practitioner of the [[First Order]].<ref name="Episode VII" /> Ren kept the helmet in his private quarters aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]'',<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> where he spoke with it whenever he felt the "pull to the [[Light side of the Force|light]]."<ref name="Episode VII" /> Ren was unable to hear anything from the helmet itself, though he did hear a voice when meditating near it. The entity with whom he communicated was not Vader, but rather the revived [[Sith]] Master Darth Sidious.<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> Following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="SW:GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the mask remained on the ''Finalizer'', although Ren was reluctant to visit the mask until after he was certain he matched up to Vader upon Snoke's reprimand of him shortly after [[Starkiller Base]]'s destruction.<ref name="TLJ: Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> the helmet was transferred to the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]. It was found by the [[Jedi]] Rey while she [[Mission to Kijimi|searched]] for the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]]'s belongings, which had been [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|confiscated]] by [[First Order stormtrooper|First Order stormtroopers]]. Vader's charred helmet rested upon an obsidian pillar<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> within Ren's suite on board the [[Starship|vessel]] in [[orbit]] of [[Kijimi]], and was disturbed when the pillar was shattered by [[Skywalker lightsaber|Rey's lightsaber]] during her duel with Ren. It was transported from the ''Steadfast'' to Ren's location in [[Kijimi City]] due to their [[Force-bond|bond]] as a [[Force dyad|dyad in the Force]], revealing Rey's physical location to Ren.<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
===The original trilogy===
[[File:DarthVadersArmorIVVI-SWCOT.png|thumb|right|300px|Darth Vader's armor from ''A New Hope'' (left) and updated version for ''Return of the Jedi'' (right)]]
With Vader lacking a mask in [[George Lucas]]'s early drafts for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']],<ref name="EW Rogue One">''[[Entertainment Weekly's Ultimate Guide to Rogue One]]''</ref> Darth Vader's armor was designed by [[Ralph McQuarrie]], who deemed it necessary for Vader to wear a breathing mask after reading the portion of the ''Star Wars'' script stating he would cross through space between ships. Lucas subsequently created the backstory that Vader needed the suit and mask after falling into a volcanic pit during his duel with Kenobi.<ref name="bringingvadertolife">{{SW|url=news/from-concept-to-screen-bringing-darth-vader-to-life|text=From Concept to Screen: Bringing Darth Vader to Life|nolive=1}}</ref> The resemblance between Vader's mask and a human skull was an intentional part of McQuarrie's design.<ref name="EW Rogue One" />

Costume designer [[John Mollo]] made one suit for actor [[David Prowse]] to wear, which was produced on a budget of $1,173. [[Brian Muir]] sculpted the mask. Mollo was inspired by McQuarrie's artwork, but was also influenced by [[Wikipedia:samurai|samurai]] armor, as well as the World War I&ndash;era trench armor and [[Wikipedia:Stahlhelm|Stahlhelm]] helmet. The suit's voice was provided by [[James Earl Jones]], while [[Ben Burtt]] provided its infamous breathing sound by recording himself using a scuba-diving apparatus. Burtt also worked to make sure Jones's voice sounded like it was reverberating out of a helmet.<ref name="bringingvadertolife" />

For [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']], the suit was redesigned to be more comfortable, with spares for looser parts of the costumes. The helmet had minor changes to fix the domed top more securely, and the triangular chin vent was made larger so Prowse could breathe and see more easily. A helmet made of tinted acrylic was made for Prowse's stunt double [[Bob Anderson]] to see through more than just the eyepieces. The costume was barely altered for [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']], although [[Fred Hole]] and [[Brian Archer]] designed and built a new helmet for the unmasking scene.<ref name="bringingvadertolife" /> ''Return of the Jedi'' did feature a new costume for Luke Skywalker, whose new black outfit was designed to invoke Vader's design.<ref name="Bantha 18">[[Bantha Tracks 18|''Bantha Tracks'' 18]]</ref>

===The prequel trilogy and ''Rogue One''===
[[File:HaydenDonsArmorFirstTime-LucasfilmFb.png|thumb|left|250px|On the set of ''Revenge of the Sith'', Hayden Christensen dons the armor for the first time on September 1, 2003.]]
Anakin Skywalker's portrayer [[Hayden Christensen]] wore the suit for the conclusion of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']]: molds of him were used to create the latest version of the armor by [[Don Bies]] and his team. [[Ryan Church]] designed the interior of Vader's helmet, which was shown in the film for the first time.<ref name="bringingvadertolife" /> The mask was digitally printed and so became the first symmetrical version.<ref name="SWYouTube-Revenge of the Sith Featurette: The Return of Darth Vader">{{SWYouTube|gq_2nK5KUAo|Revenge of the Sith Featurette: The Return of Darth Vader}}</ref>

For Vader's appearance in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', the production design team under [[Doug Chiang]] studied each version of Vader's costume in the original trilogy, ensuring the appearance in ''Rogue One'' was a hybrid but still familiar to what was seen before. Costume designers visited the Lucasfilm Archives to study the cape of the original Vader suit. Their hope was to ensure, for his appearance in ''Rogue One'', Vader's struts caused the cape to move in the exact same way as the original films.<ref name="EW Rogue One" />

===''Star Wars Rebels''===
''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' art director [[Kilian Plunkett]] said they could not experiment too much with Darth Vader's appearance, and that even the helmet hearkened back to McQuarrie's version.<ref name="SWYouTube-Rebels Recon #1.14: Inside Fire Across the Galaxy &#124; Star Wars Rebels">{{RebelsRecon|xggYEpemcck|#1.14: Inside "Fire Across the Galaxy" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-fire-across-the-galaxy}}</ref> [[Dave Filoni]] said Vader "has to have the cape," a costly stipulation that meant other characters had to be redesigned with form-fitting outfits.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/gallery/star-wars-rebels-gallery/2446143_gallery-star-wars-rebels-25|text='Star Wars: Rebels' — A deep dive into the shocking new trailer|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2016-01-15|archivedate=20160316135200}}</ref>
