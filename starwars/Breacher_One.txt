'''Breacher One''' was a [[breacher]] [[missile launcher]] used by the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] [[Nightdragon man-of-war]] ''[[Vigilant]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' takes place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[warship]]'s [[technician]]s had trouble fixing it after it was damaged, but they were ultimately successful. [[Senior Captain]] [[Kiwu'tro'owmis]], [[core name]] "Wutroow," reported Breacher One's return to status to the [[Starship|ship's]] [[commanding officer]], [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]], who commended the repair team on their work. Shortly after, during a [[Battle at listening post KR21|battle]], Ar'alani ordered [[Junior Captain]] [[Evroes'ky'mormi]] "Oeskym" to disable the thrusters in Breacher One so they could drop the missiles into [[Realspace|space]] without firing them.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Breacher One was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
