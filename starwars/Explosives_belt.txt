'''Explosives belts''' were small [[belt]]s that acted as concealed [[explosives kit]]s. They were normally made from broad hides or synthetic materials, and were produced in a wide variety of styles. Explosives belts typically contained [[detonite]] tape, a remote [[detonator]] cap, and a length of wire, all concealed within the belt buckle. They were produced by various intelligence agencies across [[the galaxy]], and cost 450 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market. They could be detonated from a distance, which made them common among [[saboteur]]s and [[infiltrator]]s who used them for sabotage.<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" />

==Behind the scenes==
Explosives belts were first mentioned in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Accept Your Mission">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/16/accept-your-mission|text=Accept Your Mission}}</ref>
