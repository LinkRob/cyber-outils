The '''Chancellor's "working office"''' was located directly below the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|Senate Chamber]] in the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]]. A part of the [[Chancellery Secretariat]],<ref name="CL2016" /> in its center housed the [[Chancellor's Podium|Chancellor's podium]], which was able to rise 30 [[meter]]s high to the chamber above. [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] was officed here along with [[Mas Amedda]] when [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] entered the chamber, who immediately knocked out two [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guards]] by the entrance.<ref name="ROTS" />
