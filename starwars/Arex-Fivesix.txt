'''Arex-Fivesix''' was an [[RX-series pilot droid]] that served as a [[Cam droid|camera droid]] for [[HoloNet News]] during the [[Republic Era]], working at a [[HoloNet News relay substation]] on [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="Jango Fett 4" /> Around [[33 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JangoFettSeriesDate}}</ref> when the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Jango Fett]] and [[Aurra Sing]] visited the substation while investigating the truth behind the [[Heist of the Hope of Glee Anselm|theft]] of the [[Hope of Glee Anselm]] relic, Fett requested a camera [[droid]], and Arex-Fivesix was dispatched to accompany the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunters]] to the [[Regional Republic Headquarters]] on [[Jaloria]]. Once the pair [[Skirmish on Jaloria|fought]] a number of other hunters led by [[Vigor Struk]], Fett took Arex-Fivesix to [[Governor]] [[Tok]], where the droid played a [[hologram]] of one of the statue's [[thief|thieves]], [[Nakano Lash]], revealing<ref name="Jango Fett 4" /> she<ref name="Jango Fett 2">[[Jango Fett 2|''Jango Fett'' 2]]</ref> was recruited by [[Ambassador]] [[Fiarok]]. After Fiarok's arrest, Arex-Fivesix reported on the incident's aftermath for HoloNet News.<ref name="Jango Fett 4" />
