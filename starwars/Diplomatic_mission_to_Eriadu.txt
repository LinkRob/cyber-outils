Around [[163 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a [[Unidentified Jedi Master (shishi tea)|Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] was to undergo a '''diplomatic mission''' to the [[Kessarine]] population of <ref name="Wayseeker" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Eriadu]]. Shortly before the [[Jedi Master]] embarked on the mission, they requested that [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Indara]] research and document a detailed history of the cultural relevance of [[shishi tea]] to Eriadu's Kessarine population.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

Around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Indara and [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] were [[Mission to Thelj|sent]] to the planet [[Thelj]] to protect [[Senator]] [[Jakob Garn]]. While traveling to Thelj, Indara read about the [[Jedi Annexation Act]] sponsored by Garn, which she did not know much of due to performing research for the Jedi Master's mission at the time of its proposal, and reflected that the research she had done for the mission may not have been the best use of her time.<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
The mission was mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]'', a [[2025]] novel written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
