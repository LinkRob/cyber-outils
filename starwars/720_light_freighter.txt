The '''720 light freighter''', also known as the '''Ghtroc 720''', was a model of [[light freighter]] manufactured by [[Ghtroc Industries]] and characterized by its [[Turtle|tortoise]]-like appearance. A newer and larger model than the [[690 light freighter]], it was meant to compete with the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]'s popular [[YT-series]].

==Description==
The 720 was a heavily [[armor]]ed, but lightly armed [[light freighter]] characterized by its unconventional configuration. Instead of occupying the rear mounting, its two banks of [[GI/80.b Overthruster ion engine|GI/80.b Overthruster]] [[ion engine]]s were housed in heavily armored nacelles at the bow, on either sides of the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. The greater part of the ship's mid section housed its two main cargo holds,<ref name="Starships and Speeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> with a total capacity of 135 [[metric ton]]s,<ref name="Before the Awakening" /> and the aft section bore two angled [[escape pod]] tubes. Because of that arrangement, the Ghtroc 720 had a rather [[Turtle|tortoise]]-like appearance.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> It was designed for a maximum crew of two, with room for another eight passengers.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

Although a solid and well-constructed ship by most performance metrics, the 720 had certain design flaws that turned off certain buyers. The most glaring deficiency was its weak armament, which consisted of a single turret-mounted twin light [[laser cannon]]. Among its other flaws were its display markings, which were adapted to [[Bith]] [[eye]]s but small and difficult to read for other species, its uncomfortable crew berths, and the system alerts that each had their unique alarm, which some buyers found irritating.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

As was the case with most light freighter models, the 720 was designed with customizability in mind.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==History==
The 720 light freighter was created by [[Ghtroc Industries]], a respected shipwright headquartered on the Bith [[homeworld]] of [[Bith (planet)|Clak'dor]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> A scaled-up version of the Ghtroc [[690 light freighter]],<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> it was meant to corner the light freighter market in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], in a bid to compete with the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]'s [[YT-series]]. Although the 720 never became as popular as Ghtroc had hoped, the ship managed to garner a small but devout following among [[smuggler]]s and free traders in the Outer Rim, and its durability earned it the nickname of "Pocket [[Gozanti-class cruiser|Gozanti]]."<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

At some point during the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] operatives [[Sabine Wren]], [[Rex]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] went on a mission to retrieve an illegal modification [[Programmer|program]] contained in the drive of the 720 light freighter ''[[Treadwater]]''.<ref name="ATW">{{RebelsAnimationCite|1|A Trooper's Worth}}</ref>

During her youth on [[Jakku]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] frequently practiced [[pilot]]ing the Ghtroc 720 via [[flight simulator]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' as taking place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> at least one 720 joined the [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] assembled in response to [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] call to action against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]].<ref name="TROS Junior Novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Ghtroc 720 light freighter was first introduced in [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition|the second edition rulebook]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] in 1992.<ref name="Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition">[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition|''Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game'', Second Edition]]</ref> The Ghtroc 720 was later [[canon]]ized in ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', a junior novel written by [[Greg Rucka]] and published in [[2015]].<ref name="Before the Awakening" />
