'''Chancellor''',<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> also known as '''Alliance Chief of State''',<ref name="Lead By Example">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> or '''Chief of State''',<ref name="RO Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> was the [[Military rank|rank]] given to the civilian leader of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the title was held by [[Mon Mothma]],<ref name="Star Wars 4" /> a former [[Senator]] of [[Chandrila]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> and one of the founders of the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Rebel DB">{{Databank|rebel-alliance|Rebel Alliance}}</ref> After the [[Battle of Endor]], Mothma became [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Aftermath" />
