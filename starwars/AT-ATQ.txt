The '''AT-ATQ''' was a type of [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]<ref name="Twitter" /> used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[walker]]s had hatches that concealed arms with turrets, and they were also armed with [[seismic charge]]s.<ref name="Four Wings" />

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
At-ATQs first appeared in "[[The Song of Four Wings]]", a [[Canon|non-canon]] short film produced by [[Project Studio Q]] as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume ]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="Four Wings" /> on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Special Preview">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-volume-three|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 Key Art Revealed with Special Preview of "BLACK"}}</ref> They were identified in a piece of concept art posted to the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] account on [[November 3]] of that year.<ref name="Twitter" />
