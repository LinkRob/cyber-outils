'''Compressed baryon canisters''' were a type of canister. In [[20 BBY|7957]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]], a compressed baryon canister was used to [[Smuggler|smuggle]] the [[NM-K reconstitutor]] used by [[terrorist]] [[Letta Turmond]] during the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] hangar on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Following the completion of the investigation into the bombing, [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tanivos Divo]] was given access to the canister used by Turmond.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
Compressed baryon canisters were first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
