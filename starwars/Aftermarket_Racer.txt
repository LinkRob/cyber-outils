The '''Aftermarket Racer''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|brown]], tan, white, light gray, dark gray, and black color scheme, including [[goggles]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aftermarket Racer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aftermarket Swoop]]. Unlike most Costumes in the game, Aftermarket Racer did not share the same rarity as its cosmetic set counterpart, with Aftermarket Swoop instead being a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap."<ref name="Hunters" /> It was initially planned to be called "Aftermarket Suit."<ref name="Hunters Panel">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-star-wars-hunters-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Panel}}</ref>
