'''Foot Patrol 7''', also known as '''Unit 7''', was a [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtrooper]] patrol [[squad]] that was part of a [[Unidentified sandtrooper regiment|regiment]] tasked by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]<ref name="Born in the Storm" /> and [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to search for a pair of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[droid]]s that carried the stolen [[Death Star plans]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The [[regiment]] searched the [[Dune Sea]], and eventually made its way to [[Mos Eisley]].<ref name="Rise of the Rebellion">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Rise of the Rebellion and the Battle of Yavin}}</ref>

They were patrolling the [[city]] when their leader, [[Stormtrooper Commander]] [[Wanten|TD-110]], stopped an [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|old man]], a [[Luke Skywalker|teenager]], a [[C-3PO|protocol droid]], and an [[R2-D2|astromech droid]], riding a [[X-34 landspeeder|speeder]], and asked for identification, as well as how long they had owned the [[droid]]s. TD-110 eventually let the group pass after the old man spoke with TD-110. Foot Patrol 7 was later called to reinforce other units by [[Commander]] [[TD-4445]] and [[TD-4445's squad|their squad]] during a skirmish to prevent the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from leaving a [[Docking Bay 94|nearby]] [[Hangar|landing bay]]. Members of Foot Patrol 7 included [[Sergeant]] [[TD-787]], [[Sardis Ramsin|TD-7556]], [[Crag]], and [[Tintop]].<ref name="Born in the Storm" />
