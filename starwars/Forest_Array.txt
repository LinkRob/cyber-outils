The '''Forest Array''' was a research array used jointly by the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Galactic Republic]] located deep inside the [[Basalt Forest]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. It was here that mysterious [[Koboh Matter|Koboh matter]] and the [[Koboh Abyss]] were studied by the [[Jedi]]. 

==History==
The Forest Array once served as a research array for [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic]] [[scientist]]s studying the mysterious [[Koboh Matter|Koboh matter]] and the [[Koboh Abyss]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. They were drawn to the area due to their fascination with the Basalt Forest. After the fall of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Dagan Gera]] to the [[dark side of the Force]], [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Santari Khri]] placed her friend in a [[bacta tank]] in the rehabilitation wing of the Forest Array, where he stayed in stasis until discovered by Jedi Knight [[Cal Kestis]]<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="JSDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref>

The Forest Array had a central rotational apparatus in which an [[orb amplifier]] was situated. The complex subterranean mechanisms which rotated the above ground ionic beam to dispel [[Koboh Matter|Koboh dust]] could be activated by use of [[the Force]] on an above-ground lever. In addition, there were four auxiliary [[telescope]]s around the array which served as generators of highly-excited energy currents that mimicked interstellar [[Ion storm|ionic storms]].<ref name="Survivor" />

By 9 BBY, the Forest Array was mostly in ruin, and was occupied by the [[Galactic Empire]], who knew little about the site. The mechanisms had not been moved in decades, and indigenous fauna had moved in, including the dangerous [[mogu]]. Errant Koboh dust had filled many of the surfaces, making travel difficult, and accumulations of Koboh matter into nearly impenetrable Koboh barriers had formed over several walkways.<ref name="Survivor" />
