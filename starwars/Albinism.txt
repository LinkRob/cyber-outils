'''Albinism''',<ref name="Frisk DB" /> also known as '''albino''',<ref name="Solo Junior Novel" /> was a deformity that caused the [[Color|pigmentation]] of a [[Species|creature's]] skin to appear as a pale white. The mutation could occur in members of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Trandoshan]] species, including the [[New Republic pilot]] [[Feresk Tssat]]. Known as Frisk to his companions, Tssat was ostracized by the more barbaric members of his species on [[Trandosha|Dosh]], later finding his place with the [[pilot]]s of the Republic's [[Vanguard Squadron]].<ref name="Frisk DB" />

Non-sentient species were also susceptible to albinism. [[Standard year|Years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], an albino [[Eriadu veermok]] stalked the [[Carrion Plateau]] on the<ref name="SW 7" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Eriadu]]. Since the creature's condition prevented it from utilizing the dense greenery as camouflage, the [[veermok]]'s life was a constant struggle. But the beast managed to survive, becoming the top predator of the plateau. It was later [[Death|killed]] by [[Ellian Zahra]], an [[officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] sent to hunt the creature by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].<ref name="SW 7" /> On [[Kashyyyk]] during the Imperial Era, albinism occurred in [[Wyyyschokk]]. They posed a threat to [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Cal Kestis]] alike.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> [[Norel Quo]], a [[Koorivar]] who served as [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s primary [[aide]] during the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster|Great Disaster]] in the [[High Republic Era|High Republic]], had unpigmented skin.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
Albinism first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'', a [[2020]] [[starfighter combat]] video game.<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref> The condition was specifically identified as albinism in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] for Feresk Tssat,<ref name="Frisk DB" /> a non-player character in the game.<ref name="Squadrons" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Albino/Legends|albino]] creatures first appeared in "[[Children of the Force (comic story)|Children of the Force]]," a short story in the [[Star Wars Tales 13|thirteenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book series]] ''[[Star Wars Tales]]''.<ref name="Children">{{Tales|13|Children of the Force (comic story)}}</ref>
