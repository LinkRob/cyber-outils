'''Fish stew''' was a type of [[stew]] containing [[fish]]. It was eaten on the [[waterworld]] [[planet]] of [[Pamarthe]],<ref name="Bloodline" /> as well as the terrestrial planet [[Tiikae]]. The version served in a [[Cantina|tavern]] on Tiikae was [[Color|red]] and had tiny, boiled [[seed]]s as an additional ingredient.<ref name="POD" />
