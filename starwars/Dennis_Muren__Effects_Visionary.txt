"'''Dennis Muren: Effects Visionary'''" is an interview with [[Dennis Muren]] about his career as a special effects artist and the making of the [[original trilogy]]. It was conducted by [[Thomas G. Smith]] for his book, ''Industrial Light & Magic: The Art of Special Effects'', released in [[1986]]. This article was published in [[Star Wars Insider 144|''Star Wars Insider'' 144]].
