'''Anthony Heald''' (born [[1940s|1944]], [[Wikipedia:New Rochelle, New York|New Rochelle, New York]])<ref name="1000 voices" /> is an [[Wikipedia:American|American]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] and [[Wikipedia:Voice actor|voice artist]] known for portraying [[Wikipedia:Frederick Chilton|Dr. Frederick Chilton]] in [[Wikipedia:The Silence of the Lambs|''The Silence of the Lambs'']] as well as narrating numberous audiobooks.<ref name="Oregonlive" /> From [[1993]] to [[2000]], Heald narrated thirty four abridged ''[[Star Wars]]'' audiobooks, including [[The Truce at Bakura (abridged audiobook)|''The Truce at Bakura'']],<ref name="Truce at Bakura">[[The Truce at Bakura (abridged audiobook)|''The Truce at Bakura'' abridged audiobook]]</ref> [[The Courtship of Princess Leia (abridged audiobook)|''The Courtship of Princess Leia'']],<ref name="Courtship">[[The Courtship of Princess Leia (abridged audiobook)|''The Courtship of Princess Leia'' abridged audiobook]]</ref> and [[I, Jedi (abridged audiobook)|''I, Jedi'']].<ref name="I, Jedi">[[I, Jedi (abridged audiobook)|''I, Jedi'' abridged audiobook]]</ref>
