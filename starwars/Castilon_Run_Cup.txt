The '''Castilon Run Cup''' was a [[sky race]] hosted on the<ref name="Racer" /> [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Castilon]]. The race was made up of several one-on-one rounds that involved racing through [[sky ring]]s while avoiding obstacles.<ref name="Racer" />

{{GameMechanics|char=1}}
While [[Mission on the Colossus platform|spying]] on [[First Order]] activity aboard the [[Supertanker fuel depot|refueling station]] ''[[Colossus]]'', [[Resistance]] member<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Kazuda Xiono]] participated in the Castilon Run Cup<ref name="Racer" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Xiono [[pilot]]ed the ''[[Fireball]]'',<ref name="Racer" /> which he borrowed from [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Jarek Yeager]].<ref name="USWNE" /> Xiono raced against memebrs of [[Ace Squadron]],<ref name="Racer" /> a group of elite racers that defended the ''Colossus'' as well as raced for entertainment,<ref name="USWNE" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Castilon Run Cup appeared in the non-[[canon]]ical [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Resistance Racer]]'',<ref name="Racer" /> which released [[June 20]], [[2019]].<ref name="Play Store">{{GooglePlay|url=apps/details?id=com.disney.datg.videoplatforms.android.watchdc&hl=en_US|text=DisneyNOW &ndash; Episodes & Live TV - Apps on Google Play}} {{C|[[:File:DisneyNow_Resistance_Racer_Update.png|Screenshot]]}}</ref> The game has the option to race as Xiono or [[Team Fireball|Team ''Fireball'']] mechanic [[Tamara Ryvora]]. This article assumes Xiono, who is the default choice, is correct. The game also allows you to customize the [[starship]], this article assumes Xiono used the ''Fireball'' with no customizations.<ref name="Racer" />
