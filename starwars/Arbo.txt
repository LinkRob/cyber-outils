{{Quote|Have you no faith in the soldiers of the New Republic, Senator Sindian? Senator Organa will travel with an entire squadron of guards to protect her at all times. Do not doubt their ability or their valor!|Arbo, to Carise Sindian|<ref name="BL" />}}
'''Arbo''' was a [[senator]] who represented the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the [[New Republic]]<ref name="BL" /> in [[28 ABY]].<ref name="Timeframe">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> A member of the [[Centrists|Centrist]] faction, Arbo was a notorious advocate of warlike policies. The [[Coruscanti]] senator supported the [[New Republic soldier|soldiers]] of the [[New Republic Defense Force]] and&mdash;at the [[time]] of the [[Amaxine warrior crisis]]&mdash;was confident that a [[squadron]] of [[Bodyguard|guards]] could ensure the safety of Senator [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] during her [[Mission to Bastatha|mission]] to [[Bastatha]], criticising his fellow Centrist, [[Lady]] [[Carise Sindian]], for her apparent lack of faith in the capabilities of their [[soldier]]s.<ref name="BL" />
