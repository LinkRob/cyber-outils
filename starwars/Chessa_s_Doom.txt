"'''Chessa's Doom'''" is a short story written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] and illustrated by [[John Paul Lona]]. It was originally published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 1|''Star Wars Adventure Journal 1'']] in [[February]] [[1994]] by [[West End Games]] before being made available online at [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club]] in [[January]] [[2008]]

"Chessa's Doom" is the first of five collaborations by Schweighofer and Lona, which span the first five issues of the ''Adventure Journal.'' The stories all intersperse narrative text by Schweighofer with wordless comics by Lona. Their [[wikipedia:frame story|frame story]] follows the [[Black Curs]] mercenaries on the planet [[Wroona/Legends|Wroona]], while much of "Chessa's Doom" takes the form of a flashback from the point of view of leader [[Dirk Harkness]].

==Plot summary==
Dirk Harkness, [[Platt Okeefe/Legends|Platt Okeefe]], [[Tru'eb Cholakk]], [[Jai Raventhorn]], and [[Starter]] have gathered in the [[Gorkin's Rest]] [[Cantina/Legends|cantina]] on Wroona. They have been swapping stories. After Starter tells a particularly unsatisfying and ridiculous tale, the others beg Harkness to share one about how he joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebellion]].

Harkness eventually gives in to the group's demands. He claims that he was about 20 years old and it was around the time of the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|destruction of the Death Star]], though his companions insist he must be older than that. During this time, Harkness is working as a [[Technician/Legends|mechanic]] on a [[Freighter/Legends|freighter]]. He and the [[First Mate/Legends|first mate]], [[Chessa Dohhenty|Chessa]], are in love and have dreams of marrying someday. 

One day, the couple are working to unload the cargo bay after landing in [[Kelada starport]]. Chessa has set aside a few crates for a private deal she made with someone in Kelada. Eight Imperial [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtroopers]] approach demanding to inspect the cargo. Chessa tries to fight them off with her [[Blaster/Legends|blaster]], and she is quickly killed. Harkness, stricken, picks up the blaster and begins shooting wildly while running out of the [[Hangar|docking bay]]. 

He runs in a daze through the city and enters a bar. The bartender takes pity on his desperate, ragged appearance and gives him a free drink. The sight of his own reflection only adds to the young man's feelings of loss and hopelessness. Just then, two men approach him, claiming to be friends of Chessa's. One of them reveals himself to be [[General/Legends|General]] [[Halomar Corros|Corros]] and offers to take him to safety. Through Corros, Harkness becomes involved in the Rebellion.

After Harkness concludes his story, Starter is disappointed at its lack of heroics, but the others are appreciative. They have a new understanding of why Harkness seems so downcast and brooding, and why he joined the fight against the Empire.
