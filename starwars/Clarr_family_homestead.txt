The '''Clarr family homestead''' was the [[homestead]] of the [[Chiss]] [[Clarr family]], one of the [[Ruling Families|Nine Ruling Families]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. It was located on the [[planet]] [[Rhigar]] in the Ascendancy's [[Rhigal system]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Clarr family homestead appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the final novel of ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
