The '''''Almanac of the Unknown''''',<ref name="OOTS">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> also known as the '''Schmireland almanac''', was a gazetteer of [[planet]]s and other astronomical objects created by the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Leric Schmireland]]. Commissioned by the [[Galactic Republic]] to catalogue worlds<ref name="AToC" /> around [[532 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]]. In ''A Test of Courage'', it is stated that [[Leric Schmireland]]'s mission occurred around three hundred years prior, which corresponds to around [[532 BBY]].</ref> Schmireland set out with a team and never returned to [[Coruscant]], employing [[Courier droid|messenger droids]] to relay his discoveries. [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] considered the almanac mostly useless as it gave information about worlds no one would want to visit, but uploaded a copy to her personal [[droid]] [[J-6]] out of boredom. The almanac later came in handy after Starros was stranded on a [[jungle]] [[moon]] with several others, as it allowed her and J-6 to identify the moon as [[Wevo]].<ref name="AToC" />
