"'''Blackburn'''" was the codename for a new [[starship]] design that was developed for the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="CC" /> at some point between [[5 BBY]] and [[4 BBY]].<ref name="Date Note">{{ChopperChaseDate}}</ref> The [[Gand]] [[Unidentified Gand engineer|engineer]] who had created it under Imperial duress managed to warn the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Consequently, the [[Spectres|rebel crew]] of the starship ''[[Ghost]]'', led by [[Hera Syndulla]] and [[Kanan Jarrus]], were [[Mission to rescue a Gand engineer|sent]] to both rescue the [[engineer]] from the Imperial base in which she worked, and sabotage the core plan for the Blackburn. The mission was successful, and all that was left of the ship's blueprints was a crudely drawn [[bantha]], courtesy of the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]].<ref name="CC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Blackburn was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars video games|desktop game]] ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Chopper Chase]]'', which was released on [[November 11]], [[2016]].<ref name="CC" />
