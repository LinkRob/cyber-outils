The '''Corporate Sector Authority''', also known simply as '''CSA''' or '''CorpSec''', was a corporate [[Politics|authority]] that administered the [[Corporate Sector]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].

Expanding the [[Corporate Sector Authority Security Police]], the CSA made use of [[Army|militarized]] [[CSA trooper|forces]] to enforce its jurisdiction during the [[New Republic Era]]. The CSA and its forces allied with the [[First Order]] and put down a [[Mission on Basta Core|revolt]] on [[Basta Core]]. By the time of the [[Cold War]], the Corporate Sector Authority [[Currency|monetarily]] supported the First Order.

==History==
===Early foundations===
Some form of the Corporate Sector Authority existed by the year [[132 BBY]]<ref name="AcolyteVGDate" /> during the [[High Republic Era]] as the governing body and security enforcement of the [[Corporate Sector]], which existed as a region independent from the [[Galactic Republic]]. As such, Republic law did not exist within the Corporate Sector, necessitating the creation of the region's own security force and governing organization.<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> Nonetheless, the Corporate Sector was only made up of a series of loosely-aligned autonomous [[star system]]s. Even if it was not yet a unified, singular entity, the Corporate Sector became home to numerous corporations as well as&mdash;within the [[Salient system]] specifically&mdash;tax evaders, arms [[merchant]]s, and [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> Complex extradition treaties existed between the Corporate Sector Authority and the Republic's [[Judicial Department]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

While still not a single, unified body,<ref name="Catalyst" /> a majority of the Corporate Sector supported the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="BMF 18">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> The star systems of the Corporate Sector were considered to be slowly coalescing into a unified entity<ref name="Catalyst" /> as of [[17 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]]. That was one of the main reasons the [[Galactic Empire]] decided to send a message to the systems defying it by [[Salient Campaign|occupying]] the Salient system on the edge of the [[sector]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

===Age of the Empire===
The Corporate Sector Authority existed during the Imperial Era. Despite the Corporate Sector's support for the CIS during the war, [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] decided against bringing its territory into his Empire. Instead, the CorpSec was allowed to spread into tens of thousands of systems, placing them under the CSA's control.<ref name="BMF 18" />

The CSA and Empire did create a cooperative relationship with each other, with the authority selling services and goods to the Imperials, while the Empire helped with the Corporate Sector's security when needed; when a group of [[pirate]]s were active in the sector and launching raids, [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] was dispatched to deal with them in a [[Campaign in the Corporate Sector|campaign]].<ref name="BMF 18" /> The criminal [[Big Bunji]] once worked with the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] on a scam that involved running counterfeit [[Authority cash voucher]]s to the poor people on the far side of the [[Cron Drift]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> Like it did with the Empire, the CSA sold goods and services to the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="BMF 18" />

===New Republic Era===
{{Quote|Corporate Sector Authority. I'd heard they were back in play, building themselves up, but I haven't seen that many together, ever. Looks like the rumors were true, they've formed some kind of army.|[[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], to [[Komat]]|<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />}}
[[File:GeneralPryde-TROSTVD.png|thumb|right|250px|Enric Pryde used the code name Steadfast to conceal the First Order partnership with the CSA.]]
The [[New Republic Era]] saw the expansion of the [[Corporate Sector Authority Security Police]] as well as a covert alliance between the CSA and the forming [[First Order]] in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> By [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> the CSA controlled an [[army]] of [[CSA trooper|Espos]] with more [[CSA trooper armor|armor and equipment]] than their [[Law enforcement agency|security police]] predecessors. At the same [[time]], the CSA secretly funded the Imperial remnant. An [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the remnant, [[High Colonel]] [[Enric Pryde]], worked with the CSA as a [[supervisor]] and was given authority over its forces. [[Viceprex]] [[Coromun]], an official of the CSA, served as Pryde's second-in-command. In order to maintain the secrecy of the remnant's connection to the CSA, Pryde was assigned the [[Callsign|code name]] Steadfast.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

During a CSA [[Mission on Basta Core|mission]] on [[Basta Core]], Steadfast was sought out by the [[Jedi hunter]] [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]]. An operative of the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult]] on [[Exegol]], Ochi requested Steadfast's assistance with the search for [[Dathan]], [[Miramir]], and their daughter [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. Steadfast reassigned a [[company]] of CSA [[trooper]]s to Ochi's command, overruling the objections of Coromun. Ochi initially failed to capture Rey and her family, despite the CSA's support, and returned to Steadfast to request more detachments of CSA [[troop]]s. Instead, Steadfast denied the hunter's request, having terminated their arrangement and further warned Ochi to avoid the Corporate Sector.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />

During the [[Cold War]], the [[Resistance]] leader [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] received intelligence that the Corporate Sector Authority was giving [[Currency|monetary]] support to the First Order who was channeling large sums of money to [[Senator]] [[Erudo Ro-Kiintor]]'s accounts through shells and third-party corporations via the CSA.<ref name="BTA" />

A former [[Councillor]], [[Sturg Ganna]], was untouchable by the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] and the Corporate Sector Authority Security Police.<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

==Organization==
===Military===
The Corporate Sector Authority was a tyrannical power that controlled the [[Corporate Sector]]. The CSA possessed a force of [[capital ship]]s it organized into a [[fleet]],<ref name="BMF 18" /> and its Security Police served as the main law enforcement agency in the Sector. Those police were made up of at least three branches, the [[Ground Forces]], [[Locational Enforcement]], and [[Situational Enforcement]]. The [[Picket Fleet]] served as the [[Navy|naval force]] of the Corporate Sector Authority. Viceprex was a position within the CSA.<ref name="A&A" />

===Outside affairs===
The Corporate Sector Authority was loyal to itself<ref name="BMF 18" /> and profits.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> As such, the CSA established economic relations with both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], with the CSA and the Empire enjoying a cooperative relationship with each other, which sold services and goods to it and was assisted with security matters in the sector by the Empire.<ref name="BMF 18" /> 

The CSA later lent its support to the First Order, a successor of the [[The galaxy|Galactic]] Empire, during the height of the ongoing tensions with the [[New Republic]], the then-galactic government, where shells and third-party corporations on behalf of the Order sent large sums of money via the Corporate Sector Authority into a Republic senator's personal accounts.<ref name="BTA" />
