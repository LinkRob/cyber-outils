A '''fern chick''' was a type of [[bird]] that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. They could be kept in a coop.<ref name="Path of Deceit" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> while on Dalna, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] helped [[Telekinesis|elevate]] a fern chick coop so it could be repaired after a flood in the [[City|town]] [[Ferdan]]. Afterward, Zink [[Brain|mentally]] noted that he felt he should be spending more [[time]] with the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] to carry out his [[Mission to Dalna (Macri and Zink)|mission]]&mdash;to find the location of the [[Rod of Seasons]], a [[Force artifact]] that had been [[Theft of the Rod of Seasons|stolen]] from the [[Hynestian royal family]] by the Path&mdash;and that elevating a fern chick coop so it could be repaired was not necessarily helping him find the [[Nameless control rods|rod]].<ref name="Path of Deceit" />
