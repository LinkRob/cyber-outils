'''Aludia''' was a [[forest]] [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]. During [[232 BBY]], a [[starship]] thief named [[Sty Trune]] crash-landed a starship on Aludia's surface after getting into a fight with [[bounty hunter]] [[Rhugo Tar]].<ref name="Best Bounty Buddies" /> Aludia was also the [[homeworld]] of the [[Mardorsh]], [[Skriffle]],<ref name="Creature Comforts" /> and [[Aludian trumble pig]] [[species]].<ref name="Best Bounty Buddies" />
