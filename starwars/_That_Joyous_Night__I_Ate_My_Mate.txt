"'''(That Joyous Night) I Ate My Mate'''" was a [[Music|song]] written and performed by the [[Max Rebo Band]]. At one point, it was considered number four on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]'s top five greatest hits.<ref name="AEYNTKUE" />

==Behind the scenes==
"(That Joyous Night) I Ate My Mate" was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The [[(That Joyous Night) I Ate My Mate/Legends|song]] was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] article "[[Who's Who in the Max Rebo Band]]," which was written by [[Rich Handley]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]''{{'s}} [[Star Wars Insider 67|sixty-seventh issue]]<ref name="Legends">{{InsiderCite|67|Who's Who in the Max Rebo Band}}</ref> on [[April 15]], [[2003]].<ref name="SW.com">{{SWArchive|url=community/news/2003/03/news20030317.html|text=Celebrate <i>Jedi</i>'s 20th in ''Insider'' #67}}</ref>
