The '''Ayn Rand School for Tots''' was a school for [[Youngling|toddlers]] located in the [[City|town]] [[Springfield]] on the astronomical object [[Earth]]. The school was painted [[Color|orange]] and had many [[Viewport|windows]] around it. The front [[Grass|lawn]] had a sign with the school's name on it and a pathway that led to a ramp connected to the entrance of the building. It was surrounded by various [[Plant|greeneries]], such as [[tree]]s and [[bush]]es with pink [[flower]]s scattered around. At some point, the [[human]] [[Maggie Simpson]] was offered to enroll in the school by<ref name="The Force Awakens From Its Nap" /> her [[Parent|mother]], [[Marge Simpson]],<ref name="The Force Awakens From Its Nap AD">"[[The Force Awakens From Its Nap]]" {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> though she rejected, enrolling in [[Jabba's Hut Jedi Preschool]] instead.<ref name="The Force Awakens From Its Nap" />
