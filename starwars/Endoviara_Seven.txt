'''Endoviara Seven''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Vader 49" /> in the [[Endoviara system]] in the [[Dantus sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> near the border of the Rim and [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> After an [[Assault on Exegol|assault]] on [[Exegol]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the forces of [[Sly Moore]]'s [[Schism Imperial]] visited Endoviara Seven. While on Endoviara Seven, Moore turned against Vader, revealing her plan to replace him as the second [[Sith]] beside [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], leaving a weakened Vader at the mercy of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[M.A.R. Corps]].<ref name="Vader 48" />
