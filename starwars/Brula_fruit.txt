The '''Brula fruit''' was a [[fruit]] found on the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. The fruit's [[nectar]] was a common reagent in [[Nightsisters]] potions and was used as an antivenom against [[snake]] bites. However, overconsumption of the [[nectar]] resulted in symptoms of "[[swamp madness]]" – convulsions, blurred vision and hallucinations.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brula fruit first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', published by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />
