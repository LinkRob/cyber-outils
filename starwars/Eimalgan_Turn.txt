The '''Eimalgan Turn''' was a [[starship]] maneuver that was named after the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[starfighter]] ace [[Cargin Eimalgan]]. It involved taking a [[starship]] from level to a half loop, then a hard roll, and turning the ship so that it could go the other direction entirely. The [[New Republic]] [[pilot]] [[Norra Wexley]] learned the maneuver while doing combat cargo runs for the Alliance. In [[5 ABY]], Norra used this maneuver during a [[Skirmish near Wild Space|skirmish]] near [[Wild Space]] with the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Scythe]]''.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>
