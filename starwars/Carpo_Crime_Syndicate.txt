The '''Carpo Crime Syndicate''', also known as the '''Carpo Organization''',<ref name="Star Wars 69" /> was a criminal syndicate led by [[Boss]] [[Carpo]], which held control over the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Lanz Carpo]]. Carpo and his syndicate often worked alongside the [[Galactic Empire]], who allowed Lanz Carpo to remain free of Imperial forces.<ref name="Star Wars 68" />
