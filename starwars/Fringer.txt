'''Fringers''' were a class of individuals. For a long [[time]], they remained indifferent to [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide [[politics]] along with [[pirate]]s, [[smuggler]]s, and scoundrels, believing that any kind of authority could be dangerous to their freedom. During the [[New Republic Era]], fringers assumed that the [[New Republic]] had limited reach in enforcing [[Planet|intraplanetary]] law&mdash;a view they drastically changed when the [[First Order]] rose to power.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> several fringers, such as [[Lazu Lirona]], participated in the [[Battle of Exegol]],<ref name="TLJVD" /> a battle that resulted in [[Darth Sidious]]'s [[death]] and brought an end to his [[First Order]] and [[Final Order]] regimes.<ref name="IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fringers made their [[canon]]ical debut in {{Film|IX}}, a [[J.J. Abrams]] movie that was the ninth episode of the [[Star Wars saga|''Star Wars'' saga]],<ref name="IX" /> which premiered on [[December 20]], [[2019]].<ref name="Insider 196">{{InsiderCite|196|The Jedi Redeemed: Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker|The Jedi Redeemed: ''Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker''}}</ref> The term was first used in ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]], released in the same [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="TLJVD" />
