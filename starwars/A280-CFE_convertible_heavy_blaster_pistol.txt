The '''A280-CFE''' ('''covert field edition''') '''convertible heavy blaster pistol''', also known as an '''A280-CFE blaster rifle''' or simply a '''A280-CFE blaster''', was a [[Reconfigurable blaster|modular version]] of the [[A280 blaster rifle]], as part of the [[A-series]]. It featured a core [[Blaster pistol|pistol]] that could be [[Reconfigurable blaster|reconfigured]] into an [[assault rifle]] or [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="UVG" /> [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] used an [[Cassian Andor's blaster|A280-CFE]] during the [[Battle on Jedha]], the [[mission to Eadu]], and the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Description==
[[File:CassianAndorFull-Fathead.png|thumb|left|200px|Cassian Andor's A280-CFE blaster converted to sniper rifle configuration]]
The A280-CFE convertible [[heavy blaster pistol]] featured an over-sized frame sporting the familiar, boxy design known by [[BlasTech Industries|BlasTech]] enthusiasts. The CFE could quickly reconfigure into a [[blaster rifle]] through two attachments. Initially designed during the [[Clone Wars]], the CFE was popular with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Fulcrum]] agents during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="GaG" />

A forward-facing [[barrel]] and housing unit with a [[Multi-optic sight|multi-optic]] [[scope]] clipped on over the pistol barrel, and a [[Stock|buttstock]] slid into the back to convert the pistol into rifle mode. The added length and stability improved accuracy, while enhanced [[Galven-pattern]]s increased penetration and cohesion. Unfortunately, the CFE had to use low-powered [[eleton]] gas, as the nature of the clip-on barrel precluded using higher-powered gases.<ref name="GaG" />
