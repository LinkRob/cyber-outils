'''EQ-005''' was the designation of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]].<ref name="ObiWan5" /> Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> they were stationed on<ref name="ObiWan5" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="SWAEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> [[Tatooine]] as as part of a [[JM-909's squad|squad]] led by [[JM-909]]. During a [[Skirmish on Tatooine|mission]], EQ-005 was assigned two other stormtroopers to flank suspected [[Tusken Raider]]s from the south. However, the Tusken Raiders had staged the area for an ambush, and attacked the squad, with JM-909 the only survivor.<ref name="ObiWan5" />
