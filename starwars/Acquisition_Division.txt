The '''Acquisition Division''' was an organization within the [[Jedi Order]] that was active by the [[time]] around the [[Jedi-Sith War]]. According to old records, the ancient [[Jedi Master]] [[Morrit Ch'gally]] created the discipline of [[Jedi Sentinel]] while a member of the Acquisition Division.<ref name="EV" />
