'''Blue Bantha with Guava and Dragonberry Rum''', also referred to as '''Blue Milk with Rum''' was an [[alcohol]]ic [[Foodstuff|beverage]] served at [[Oga's Cantina]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The drink was an alcoholic take on the [[Blue Bantha]] drink served there. It was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Bantha with Guava and Dragonberry Rum appeared as a beverage available to guests at the [[In-universe|real-world]] Oga's Cantina in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]], exclusively for [[Star Wars Nite|''Star Wars'' Nites]] in [[2023]].<ref name="2023 brochure">{{Disney|subdomain=cdn1.parksmedia.wdprapps|url=vision-dam/digital/parks-platform/parks-standard-assets/disneyland/events/star-wars-nite/Disneyland-After_Dark-Star_Wars-Nite-2023-Event_Guide.pdf|text=Disneyland After Dark: Star Wars Nite 2023}}</ref> The menu described it as "[[Blue milk|Blue Milk]], [[Wikipedia:Guava|Guava]] [[Rum]], [[Wikipedia:Bacardi|Dragonberry Rum]], topped with a [[Wikipedia:Falernum|Falernum]] Whipped [[Cream]], Rum-soaked [[Wikipedia:Passion fruit (fruit)|Passion Fruit]] Jellies."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/lunch|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Lunch Menu}}</ref> The beverage returned for ''Star Wars'' Nites in [[2024]], where it was renamed Blue Milk with Rum, but with the same recipe.<ref name="Ordinary Adventures">{{YouTube|video=RBiqQmTGmrs|text=Star Wars Nite 2024 Food Tour!|channel=@OrdinaryAdventures|name=Ordinary Adventures|date=2024-04-18}}</ref>
