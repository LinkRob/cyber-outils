{{Quote|And where were the Republic one year ago when our beloved Sevran, on a diplomatic mission to Starlight Beacon, vanished without trace amidst the attack?|Tragul Tarkin|<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />}}
In [[230 BBY]], the [[Tarkin family]] member [[Sevran Tarkin]] undertook a '''diplomatic mission to Starlight Beacon''' at the time the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destroyed]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]. During the attack on Starlight Beacon, Tarkin went missing and became a [[prisoner]] of the Nihil on the [[prison ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. As she had gone missing, Tarkin was declared legally [[Death|dead]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Eriadu]].

After being [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|rescued]] from the Nihil as part of a [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] mission in [[228 BBY]], Sevran returned to Eriadu where she attempted to prove that she was alive. Despite her efforts, however, she had difficulties because of meddling from her [[Gender|uncle]] [[Tragkul Tarkin]], though she was able to have her status restored and took his place as the family's head.

==The mission==
{{Quote|Why is he talking about you like you're dead?!''"<br />"''Because I was on Starlight when it fell and then went missing, so I was declared legally dead.|Zeen Mrala and Sevran Tarkin|<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />}}
In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Tarkin family]] member [[Sevran Tarkin]] was on<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[Starlight Beacon]] for a [[diplomat]]ic mission<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> while the station was in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Eiram system]].<ref name="Locations">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]] establishes that the diplomatic mission to Starlight Beacon took place concurrently to the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]'' states occurred while [[Starlight Beacon]] was in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Eiram system]].</ref> As Sevran was on Starlight Beacon,<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> the station was [[Saboteur|sabotaged]] and [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destroyed]] by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]], with it being sent crashing into the [[ocean]] of the [[planet]] [[Eiram]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|But you're ''not'' dead! You're right here?!''"<br />"''Proving that has been more complicated than you'd think. The bureaucracy has been a nightmare. I'm sure that's not a mistake.|Zeen Mrala and Sevran Tarkin|<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />}}
[[File:SevranTarkin-2023THRA9.png|thumb|right|250px|Sevran Tarkin (pictured) struggled to prove she was actually alive after having been declared legally dead after going missing during her diplomatic mission to Starlight Beacon.]]
In the aftermath of Starlight's destruction, Sevran was [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] on the Nihil [[prison ship]] ''[[Korvix Vorn]]''. While her family on<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> the Outer Rim<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 10|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 10]]</ref> planet [[Eriadu]] was unaware of what had happened to her, since she was missing, she was declared legally [[Death|dead]]. Her [[Gender|uncle]], [[Tragkul Tarkin]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> who led the family,<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10" /> used her supposed death to push his anti-Republic [[propaganda]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />

Eventually, in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 5 date">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and two weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} Since the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]] are set immediately after the fourth issue's conclusion, it must also take place the same year.</ref> Sevran, along with other prisoners of the ''Korvix Vorn'', was [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|rescued]] during a joint Republic and [[Jedi Order]] operation. Afterward, Sevran returned to Eriadu where she attempted to prove that she was in fact alive, but she encountered troubles<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> because Tragkul had tied bureaucratic knots around her reinstatement into official record, using it as a power play.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10" /> As well as being officially declared dead, Sevran was out of favor within her family as falling in battle was not considered honorable by her uncle.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" />

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9 date">{{StarlightDate|[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]]|one [[Standard year|year]] and a few [[Standard month|months]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> while at a meeting of the [[Quintad]]&mdash;the five ruling families of Eriadu&mdash;Tragkul again pushed his anti-Republic propaganda, asking where the Republic was when his niece had vanished during her diplomatic mission to Starlight Beacon. When the Republic representative<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10" /> asked Sevran why her uncle spoke about her like she was dead, Sevran explained about the effects of her disappearance when Starlight fell. Mrala pointed out Sevran was not dead, which Sevran noted had been more complicated to prove than Mrala could think. When Sevran attempted to endorse [[Jedi Knight]] [[Farzala Tarabal]] to address the Quintad, it was rejected, as her status had yet to be restored.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> [[Standard day|Days]] later, Sevran discredited her uncle and took his place as the family's head after he reinstalled her into the records.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 10" />

==Behind the scenes==
Sevran Tarkin's diplomatic mission to Starlight Beacon was mentioned in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|ninth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9" /> on [[August 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-802/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-9|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #9}}</ref>
