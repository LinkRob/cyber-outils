'''Daniel Naprous''' is an English stunt performer and actor who physically portrays [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] alongside [[Spencer Wilding]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Credits">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' credits</ref> Naprous plays the character in the fight scene at the end of the film, while Wilding portrays him in all the other scenes.<ref name="SWStory" />
