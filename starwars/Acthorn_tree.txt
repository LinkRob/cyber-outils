'''Acthorn trees''' were a type of [[tree]] that grew in the [[forest]]s of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. In [[231 BBY]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] noticed some of the trees while traveling through the world's [[Farodin Woods]]. After [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Occupation of Valo|occupied]] Valo the [[230 BBY|following year]], the [[commander]] [[Vark Tarpalin]] kept a desk made of acthorn [[wood]] in his [[Vark Tarpalin's office|office]]. In [[229 BBY]], the aspiring pirate [[Zyle Keem]] climbed an acthorn tree after crashing their [[Zyle's shuttle|shuttle]] on the planet.

==Description==
Acthorn trees were a type of large, tall [[tree]] found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. The [[Plant|trees]] featured crusty, swirled bark that carried a spicy scent. Acthorn trees often grew with several branches wound up its trunk that formed leafy canopies in areas where several of the trees were present. Its [[wood]] could be used to make furniture, and the patterns of its leaves could be used as [[clothing]] patterns.<ref name="EFV" />

==History==
During the [[High Republic Era]], acthorn trees grew on Valo&mdash;including in the world's [[Farodin Woods]] forest.<ref name="RTCT">''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]''</ref> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] and a pair of [[Bonbrak]] garage workers rode the [[droid]] [[V-18]]&mdash;who had been upgraded with [[speeder]] parts to be able to fly&mdash;through Valo's Farodin Woods, noticing the world's acthorn trees as they traveled.<ref name="RTCT" />

[[File:ZyleKeem-EscapeFromValo.jpg|thumb|right|250px|After crashing on Valo, Zyle Keem scaled an acthorn tree to get a better vantage point.]]
When [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Occupation of Valo|occupied]] Valo<ref name="EFV" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'' take place one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which equates to [[229 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''.</ref> the Nihil [[commander]] [[Vark Tarpalin]] kept a desk of the tree's wood in his [[Vark Tarpalin's office|office]] in [[Lonisa City]]'s [[city hall]]. By around that [[time]], [[Valon]]s living on the world used the patterns of acthorn trees' leaves on clothing. After crashing their [[Zyle's shuttle|shuttle]] on Valo<ref name="EFV" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date2" /> the aspiring pirate [[Zyle Keem]] scaled an acthorn tree to gain a better vantage point of their surroundings. They felt climbing the tree was easier than ascending a [[Coronet City comms tower|comms tower]] in [[Coronet City]]<ref name="EFV" /> on the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Corellia]],<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> using its many branches and crusty bark as a staircase to go upward. At the top of the forest's canopy, Keem surveyed Lonisa City to search for where the ''[[Innovator]]'' research [[Starship|vessel]] had sunk&mdash;hoping to raid it for its [[bacta]] medicine&mdash;but could not discern which [[Water|lake]] it had gone down in. Disappointed, they carefully descended the tree and traveled to Lonisa City, reflecting that the crisp [[Atmosphere|air]] smelled faintly of acthorn bark.<ref name="EFV" />

Later, as part of the [[Scarlet Skulls]] crew, Keem and the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]] [[Infiltration of city hall|infiltrated]] Tarpalin's office and noticed a collection of [[Knife|knives]] stabbed deep into its acthorn desk. Not long afterward, the crew visited the [[Parse home]] and noticed [[Gender|Missus]] [[Parse]] wore a [[Color|blue]] dress bearing a faint pattern of acthorn leaves. After the crew successfully [[Raid of the Innovator|expelled]] the Nihil from Valo, Gavi bid his friends goodbye as their group stood on the shore of [[Lake Lonisa]], the breeze smelling of [[salt]] and acthorn leaves.<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Acthorn trees first appeared in [[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower|''Race to Crashpoint Tower'']], a junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="RTCT" /> Phase I that was published on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to its release, the trees' appearance was included in a preview for the work printed in the junior novel [[The High Republic: A Test of Courage|''A Test of Courage'']],<ref name="ATOC">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> published on [[January 5]] of the same year.<ref name="Phase I" />
