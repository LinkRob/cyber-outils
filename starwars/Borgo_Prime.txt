'''Borgo Prime''',<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> also known as '''Starport Borgo''' or '''Port Borgo''', was an [[asteroid]]<ref name="Skeleton">{{Databank|jod-na-nawood|Jod Na Nawood}}</ref> that served as a [[shadowport]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> Located on the [[Braxant Run]],<ref name="Braxant" /> the former [[pirate]] [[Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] marked it on a [[map]] that she included in the [[book]] that would eventually become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

A group called the [[Dark Wing]] operated at the port during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During this time, a [[Gender|woman]] tried to join the group while at the port, but later told a sibling in the [[Rokana Raiders]] [[pirate]] group that it was basically impossible to join.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' {{C|''A Pirate's Fortune'' DLC}}</ref>

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SkelCrewDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> the shadowport was under the control of the [[Shistavanen]] pirate [[Brutus]] and their [[Silvo's pirates|crew]]. Several children&mdash;[[KB]], [[Wim]], [[Neel]], and [[Fern]]&mdash;arrived at the asteroid aboard the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'' after accidentally taking off from their home world of [[At Attin]].<ref name="Barrier" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:StarportBorgo.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Starport Borgo]]
In the current [[canon]], Borgo Prime was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> The location originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared as a [[Spaceport/Legends|spaceport]] built into an [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroid]] in the [[1995]] novel ''[[Young Jedi Knights: Shadow Academy]]'', which was written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]] and [[Rebecca Moesta]].<ref name="Shadow Academy">''[[Young Jedi Knights: Shadow Academy]]''</ref> ''Smuggler's Guide'' incorrectly places Borgo along the Hydian Way,<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> while later sources such as the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] place it on the Braxant Run.<ref name="Braxant" />

For Port Borgo's live action debut in ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', sound designer [[David W. Collins]] included an homage to the ''[[w:c:pirates:Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl|Pirates of the Caribbean]]'' franchise after being reminded of [[w:c:pirates:Tortuga|Tortuga]].<ref name="Skeleton Crew Strange Familiar Sounds">{{SW|url=news/skeleton-crew-skywalker-sound-interview|text=The Strange and Familiar Sounds of ''Star Wars: Skeleton Crew''}}</ref><ref name="Skeleton Crew David Collins">{{SWYouTube|oLYNXaKDRGs|Star Wars: Skeleton Crew &#124; Sound Designer David W. Collins - Pirates &#124; Disney+}}</ref>
