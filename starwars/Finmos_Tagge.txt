'''Finmos Tagge''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as [[Father of Chemicals]] in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Tagge was short and burly with a [[Color|red]] beard.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Tagge, along with the other [[City Father]]s, attended a gathering hosted by [[Alys Ongwa]] to honor recent losses in the [[city]]. The man shared their last name<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> with the [[House of Tagge]],<ref name="WOTBH Boushh">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> a human [[noble]] family<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=General Tagge - Galactic Empire}}</ref> connected to [[Tepasi]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>
