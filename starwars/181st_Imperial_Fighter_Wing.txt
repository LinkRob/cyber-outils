The '''181st Imperial Fighter Wing'''<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|33|Starship Fact File}}</ref> was a [[fighter wing]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]]. The group was known for the [[Color|red]] stripes that adorned their [[starfighter]]s, a menacing display to their victims that inspired fellow Imperial [[squadron]]s to adopt similar designs.<ref name="SW:S" />

At some point before the [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate]]<ref name="SW:S" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Raid date">{{FilmDate|IV|1|the [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate]]}}</ref> a [[Senator|member]] of the [[Imperial Senate|senatorial body]] advocated for the 181st to execute a [[Raid on the Alliance to Restore the Republic|raid]] on the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The mission proved to be disastrous, with the wing losing at least two men,<ref name="SW:S" /> including brothers of fellow [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] [[Havina Vonreg]], to a volley of Alliance [[torpedo]]es. [[Hedrian]], the youngest of the brothers, survived the initial attack and returned, injured, to his [[hangar]] before [[Death|dying]].<ref name="CtT">{{EA|url=games/starwars/squadrons/news/count-to-three-short-story|text=Count to Three|int=Count to Three}}</ref> She held the senator accountable for her brothers' deaths just as much she did the rebels.<ref name="SW:S" />

During the [[Battle of Endor]], the 181st Imperial Fighter Wing alongside the [[Moddell sector fleet|Imperial Sector Fleet]] and [[Scythe Squadron]], and other Imperial units fought against the forces of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Scythe Squadron and members of the 181st pursued [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] and [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] into the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]].<ref name="BMF" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Main story date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the main events of ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[pilot]] later mentioned the doomed mission to [[Titan Three (Flight Baron)|another member]] of her own starfighter unit, [[Titan Squadron]].<ref name="SW:S" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:X-wing_24_07.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The 181st Imperial Fighter Group as portrayed in ''Star Wars'' Legends.]]
The 181st Fighter Wing was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] continuity in the [[2020]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'', developed by [[Motive Studios]]. The "Ashen Cardinal," a paint job based on the wing's signature red stripes, is available as a "Rare" skin for the Imperial faction's starfighters in multiplayer modes. It can be unlocked by spending 400 Glory, one of the game's currencies.<ref name="SW:S" />

The [[181st Imperial Fighter Wing/Legends|181st Imperial Fighter Wing]] first debuted in the [[Star Wars Legends]] continuity through the [[1997]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 21|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 21]], written by [[Michael A. Stackpole]]. In ''Legends'', they were notably portrayed as an elite [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[TIE/IN interceptor/Legends|TIE/IN interceptor]] unit known as the 181st Imperial Fighter Group,<ref name="ItESP1">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 21|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 21]]</ref> [[Commanding officer/Legends|commanded]] by [[Baron of the Empire/Legends|Baron of the Empire]] [[Colonel/Legends|Colonel]] [[Soontir Fel/Legends|Soontir Fel]]. The group was also led at different points by Colonel [[Evir Derricote]]<ref name="TMoBF">[[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 25|''X-Wing Rogue Squadron'' 25]]</ref> and [[General/Legends|General]] [[Turr Phennir]].<ref name="XW:SC">''[[X-Wing: Solo Command]]''</ref> The trademark [[Color/Legends|bloodstripes]] that donned the unit's TIE Interceptors were awarded to [[TIE pilot|pilots]] with over ten kills.<ref name="DS2L">{{CCG|set=Death Star II Limited|cardname=Major Turr Phennir}}</ref>
