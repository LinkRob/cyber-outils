'''Brigadier General''' was a high commissioned [[military rank]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="SW" /> and [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="CL" /> that was senior to a [[colonel]]. Republic Colonel [[Meebur Gascon]] had dreams of being promoted to Brigadier General,<ref name="SW" /> which he eventually obtained.<ref name="HC 79">{{HelmetCollectionCite|79|Databank A-Z}}</ref> [[Nevar]] of the [[Imperial Army]] held the rank during the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="CL" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> and served as the [[vehicle commander]] of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] ''[[Blizzard 2]]''.<ref name="CL" />
