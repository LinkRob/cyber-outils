'''Delving droids''' were a class of [[mining droid]]. The [[droid]]s had a number of different functions, including sorting, digging, carrying debris, and detonating explosives. When the [[Nihil]] [[Raid on Elphrona|attacked]] the [[Blythe homestead]] on [[Elphrona]], [[Erika Blythe]] used the family's delving droids to resist the attackers.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
