'''''Enhanced Battle of Naboo''''' is an expansion card set of the ''[[Young Jedi Collectible Card Game]]'', produced by [[Decipher]]. The set is based on the original [[Battle of Naboo (YJCCG)|Battle of Naboo]] set. Contains cards from various scenes in the movie. As with [[Enhanced Menace of Darth Maul]], this set comes with booster packs of [[Battle of Naboo (YJCCG)]].

This set has 12 cards, six light side and six dark side.
