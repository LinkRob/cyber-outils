'''Blue Squadron''', also referred to as '''Blue Group''' or '''Blue Wing''',<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> was an [[airspeeder]] and [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] that served as part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|starfighter corps]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The squadron most notably participated in battles such as the [[Battle of Scarif]], [[Siege on Tureen VII]], the [[Battle of Hoth]], and the [[Battle of Endor]].

==Description==
Blue Squadron was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[starfighter]] squadron made up starfighter pilots and Astromech droids, that was led by various commanders during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. These included [[General]] [[Antoc Merrick]],<ref name="Rogue One" /> "[[Blue Leader (Vrogas Vas)|Blue Leader]],"<ref name="Vader Down 1" /> and [[Lieutenant]] [[Valeria]].<ref name="Squadrons" /> Merrick's second-in-command was [[Barion Raner]].<ref name="BuildXwing10" /> Staff officers included Lieutenant [[Taslet Colb]], [[Warrant Officer]] [[Laren Joma]], and Lieutenant [[Heff Tobber]]. Blue Squadron spent some of their time based at the [[Great Temple]] on [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />

[[File:BlueSquadronPilotsWalking.jpg|thumb|left|220px|A group of Blue Squadron pilots at the Rebel Headquarters on Yavin]]
Blue Squadron was a mixed squadron made up of various [[airspeeder]]s<ref name="Battlefront" /> and starfighters. It was expanded into a unit well beyond the usual 12 ships of the same design.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> For airspeeders, this included [[T-47 airspeeder]]s.<ref name="Battlefront" /> For starfighters, this included [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]]s,<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s,<ref name="Visual Guide" /> [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s<ref name="HelmetCollection67" /> [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s,<ref name="Rogue One" /><ref name="SW 24" /> of which General Merrick's own starfighter was named ''[[Blue One]]'',<ref name="Visual Guide" /> and [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> The squadron's starfighters were painted with [[Color|blue]] markings or stripes.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==History==
===Early rebellion===
During the early rebellion, Blue Squadron was a mix of T-65B X-wing starfighters, assigned to escort UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft into combat zones and a small number of BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber reallocated from squadrons in neighboring sectors.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

===Battle of Atollon===
Blue squadron was present at the [[Battle of Atollon]] where they flew at least three BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bombers, they lost at least one Y-wing after it was shot down by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref><ref name="Zero Hour Guide">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/zero-hour-behind-the-scenes-gallery|text="Zero Hour" Behind-the-Scenes Gallery}}</ref>

===Mission to Eadu===
[[File:BlueSquadronEscort-SWZ01.png|thumb|right|300px|A Blue Squadron X-wing deployed to Eadu]]
{{Quote|Squadron up. Target: Eadu.|General Draven ordering a strike|audio=SquadronUp.flac|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
By order of General [[Davits Draven]], Blue Squadron,<ref name="Rogue One Novel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref> consisting of eleven X-wings<ref name="Grounded">{{StoryCite|story=Grounded|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> and five Y-wings, was deployed to [[Mission to Eadu|target]] the [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory]] in order to eliminate [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]]. The callsign of Blue five in this battle was held by [[Farns Monsbee]]. During the strike, two of the X-wings were lost.<ref name="Rogue One" />

===Battle on Scarif===
{{Quote|Blue Squadron, get to the surface before they close that gate.|Admiral Raddus giving Blue Squadron their orders during the Battle of Scarif|audio=Raddus-BlueSquadron.flac|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
[[File:Blue_Squadron_forms_up.png|thumb|left|250px|Fighters of Blue Squadron offer air support to Rogue One]]
Blue Squadron, under the command of General Antoc Merrick ([[Blue Leader]]), took part in the [[Battle of Scarif]] along with [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]], and [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]]. Blue Squadron [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] accompanied the rest of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] to the aid of [[Rogue One]] on [[Scarif]] below. Blue Squadron was tasked with penetrating the shield gate and providing air support and infantry reinforcements to Rogue One.<ref name="Rogue One" /> Only Joma's U-wing and twelve X-wings,<ref name="SWGeek">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> including Merrick's, managed to bypass the Imperial defenses and travel down toward the surface.<ref name="Rogue One" />

[[Farns Monsbee]] [[Blue Five|(Blue Five)]] observed that the Empire would be quick to close the gate, which turned out to be correct as the gate was shut, forcing [[Vangos Grek]] ([[Blue Six]]) and another X-wing pilot to pull up. Both were destroyed on impact with the planetary shield and another X-wing crashed into the Shield Gate Orbital Command, but Barion Raner ([[Blue Four]]), [[Jaldine Gerams]] ([[Blue Three]]), [[Heff Tobber]] ([[Blue Eight]]) and at least one other X-wing managed to avoid impact and went to assist the Rebel fleet in space above.<ref name="Rogue One" />

[[File:DesigningLightspeed-TandemAssault.png|thumb|right|300px|Blue Squadron X-wings work in concert with rebel ground forces.]]
On the surface around the Citadel, [[Blue Eleven]], the U-wing crewed by Warrant Officer Laren Joma, with Lieutenant Taslet Colb as copilot and [[Bistan]] as gunner,<ref name="Visual Guide" /> reinforced the [[troop]]s on the ground, however they were shot down and [[Death|killed]]. The X-wings in the air were able to provide air support, managing to destroy the [[All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport|AT-ACTs]] and engage Imperial [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]]s. During the battle, the upper starboard [[engine]] of Merrick's T-65B X-wing was damaged by a TIE striker, causing his ship to spiral toward the ground, killing him on impact.<ref name="Rogue One" /> None of the pilots who made it through the shield gate survived the battle.<ref name="BuildXwing10" /> Most, if not all, of Blue Squadron's craft were destroyed at Scarif. The squadron's decimation at Scarif and the loss of its commanding officer meant that Blue Squadron would not be present at the following climactic battle at Yavin, leaving Red, Gold, and Green Squadrons to take on that engagement.

===Battle of Vrogas Vas===
{{Quote|Blue Five is down, this is Blue Three, He's right on my tail... Torpedoes Locked... I can't AARRRGG--|Blue Three, at Vrogas Vas|<ref name="Vader Down 1" />}}
[[File:VrogasVas-VaderDown1.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Blue Squadron flying at Vrogas Vas]]
Some time during the Galactic Civil War, the Rebel Alliance established a [[Rebel Refueling Base|refueling base]] on the [[planet]] [[Vrogas Vas]]. In 0 ABY,<ref name="VrogasVasDate">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' places the Battle of Vrogas Vas in the year immediately following the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' states concluded in 0 ABY. Therefore, the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas]] must have taken place in 0 ABY.</ref> Blue Squadron was one of three X-wing squadrons present, carrying out training drills above the planet, when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] arrived there in the search for his son [[Luke Skywalker]]. Blue squadron, along with [[Yellow Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Yellow Squadron]], were entirely wiped out in the resulting [[Battle of Vrogas Vas|battle]] with the [[Sith Lord]] in his [[TIE Advanced x1]]. Luke Skywalker (Red Five) purposely rammed into him with his X-wing. Father and son both fell to the planet's surface.<ref name="Vader Down 1" /> In a report to General [[Jan Dodonna]], [[Commander]] [[Arhul Narra]] from Red Squadron, described the event as one of the worst culling of starfighter pilots he'd ever seen, with the loss of Blue, [[Gray Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gray]] and Yellow Squadrons, as well as abandoning their starfighter outpost following the arrival of the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial fleet]]. Darth Vader escaped capture, however all Alliance command staff members escaped and all sensitive information stored at the outpost was recovered or intentionally destroyed. The Alliance were able to capture Dr. [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], a high-value prisoner, who was remanded to a [[Sunspot Prison|max security penitentiary]] at [[Sunspot]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

===Tureen VII===
{{Quote|Blue Group, keep those TIEs busy. The rest of you, you have your assignments. We have to take down those moon bases.|Unidentified pilot, at Tureen VII|<ref name="SW 24" />}}
[[File:BlueGroupHarbinger-2015StarWars24.png|thumb|right|200px|Blue Group deploy from the ''Harbinger'']]
After the incident on Vrogas Vas, Blue Squadron (Blue Group) were deployed by the stolen [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Harbinger (Imperial I-class)|''Harbinger'']], to break the Imperial [[Siege on Tureen VII|blockage]] on [[Tureen VII]]. They were tasked with protecting the ''Harbinger'' from Imperial TIE fighters, led by Darth Vader in his TIE Advanced x1, while Red Squadron (Red Team) attacked the moon bases. Leia Organa ordered Red Team to provide more room so the supplies could reach the surface but Vader was not making it easy. Luke Skywalker (Red Five) successfully destroyed the moon bases and tried to go after Vader. However, as the Alliance forces had suffered heavy losses, including the ''Harbinger'' which had to be abandoned, he was urged to stay on mission as there were wounded to evacuate. Several of the squadron's X-wings were destroyed.<ref name="SW 24" />

===Battle of Hoth===
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Blue Squadron was part of the Rebel force stationed on [[Hoth]] when the Empire discovered the base, launching an invasion that signaled the commencement of the [[Battle of Hoth]]. Using T-47 airspeeders, the pilots of Blue Squadron helped to defend [[Echo Base]] from [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s (AT-ATs) and [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s (AT-STs). They destroyed the AT-STs with the [[speeder]]'s primary blasters and used their harpoon and tow cables to defeat the AT-ATs. This allowed three transports to escape the base.<ref name="Battlefront" />

===Battle on Bakura===
At some point during the Galactic Civil War, Blue Squadron were instrumental in a [[Battle on Bakura|battle]] that took place on the planet [[Bakura]], which ended in a victory for the Alliance.<ref name="Squadrons" />

===Battle of Endor===
[[File:Blue_Squadron_Support_BotF.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Blue Squadron B-wing flown by Ten Numb]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Blue Squadron participated in the [[Battle of Endor]], composed of a mix of A/SF-01 B-wing starfighters and RZ-1 A-wing interceptors and included a [[Sullustan]] bounty hunter-turned-rebel [[Ten Numb]]. With the A-wings flying fighter cover&mdash;a "luxury" other B-Wing squadrons such as [[Blade Squadron]] did not have&mdash;and managed to bring down [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|two]] [[Star Destroyer]]s.<ref name="HelmetCollection67" /> Ten Numb flew a B-wing starfighter under the [[callsign]] [[Blue Five]].<ref name="SWCT Ten Numb">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Ten Numb - B-Wing Pilot}}</ref>

===Subsequent Blue Squadrons===
Under the [[New Republic]], [[77 Squadron]] flew as Blue Squadron at the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="HM">''[[Hyperspace Mountain]]''</ref> A [[Blue Squadron (Resistance)|Blue Squadron]] was also active as part of the [[Resistance Starfighter Corps]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Squadron appears in the [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|second season]] of the non-[[canon]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' animated series. The squadron appeared in four of the five promotional short films designed to promote the series. Blue Squadron is led by Lieutenant [[Colvett Valeria]], who leads the squadron into combat against the Galactic Empire in several skirmishes. In the series, Blue Squadron act as supporting characters to the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]], a family of scavengers who join the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="Thrown Into Battle">{{FreemakerAdventures|Thrown Into Battle}}</ref><ref name="Rowan's Secret Adventure">{{FreemakerAdventures|Rowan's Secret Adventure}}</ref><ref name="ZFSPG">{{FreemakerAdventures|Zander Freemaker, Superstar Pilot Guy!}}</ref> Under Valeria's leadership, Blue Squadron attacks an Imperial repair depot near [[Alzoc III]] and also participates in the Battle of Endor.<ref name="A Perilous Rescue">{{FreemakerAdventures|A Perilous Rescue}}</ref><ref name="RROTJ">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Return of the Jedi}}</ref> Blue Squadron appeared as a starfighter cosmetic option in the [[2020]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]'', that also mentioned their first connection to Valeria and Bakura in Star Wars canon.<ref name="Squadrons" />
