'''Bagara'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was an individual who worked for the [[crime lord]] [[Cenevax]] on [[Coruscant]]. When [[Sartori]], a corrupt [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[inspector]], attempted to flee [[Jedi]] pursuit, he contacted Bagara, demanding to see Cenevax.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bagara was mentioned in the audiobook ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
