'''B-2 series mercenary sentry droids''' were a variant of [[B2-series super battle droid]] that existed in [[the galaxy]] by the early [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Era|Era]].<ref name="Official Trailer swcom">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-trailer-key-art-2|text=Pedro Pascal Stars in New Trailer for ''The Mandalorian and Grogu''}}</ref> Several of these droids were members of the [[Droid Gotra]] who served the [[Hutt]] [[Unidentified Hutt brother|brother]] and [[Unidentified Hutt sister|sister]] duo known as [[the Twins]] on [[Nal Hutta]], with one such [[mercenary]] [[droid]] standing sentry behind the [[Hutt]] [[Unidentified Hutt brother|brother]] and [[Unidentified Hutt sister|sister]] duo known as [[the Twins]] when the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] entered their throne room with his young [[Mandalorian apprentice|apprentice]] [[Din Grogu]].<ref name="Official Trailer" />

B-2 series mercenary sentry droids' plating was a dull and rusted silver [[color]]. One B-2 wore a tattered [[cape]] and a [[bandolier]] over its chest, and wielded a [[machete]].<ref name="Cardboard Cutout" />

==Behind the scenes==
A B-2 series mercenary sentry droid was first pictured in the official trailer for [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]] released on [[February 22]], [[2026]].<ref name="Official Trailer" /> The droid model was first identified by the product name of a life-sized cardboard cutout sold by Advanced Graphics.<ref name="Cardboard Cutout" />
