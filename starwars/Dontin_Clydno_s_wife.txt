[[Dontin Clydno]], a [[diplomat]] in the [[Anoat sector]], had a '''wife''' and at least two [[Youngling|children]]. During the [[Iron Blockade]] of the [[sector]], shortly after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="SWU" /> between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> he joined the [[Uprising]] resistance movement against [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant]] with the goal of freeing the sector and ensuring the safety of his family.<ref name="SWU" />

==Behind the scenes==
