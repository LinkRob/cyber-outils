'''Eda'Ni''' was a [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] who lived during the [[Republic Era]]. At one point during the [[Clone Wars]], Eda'Ni was one of a number of Padawans who were present at the [[Sason Temple]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Aphra 33" /> [[gas giant]]<ref name="Aphra 32">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 32|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 32]]</ref> [[Sason]]. During their [[time]] at the [[Jedi temple]], Eda'Ni [[Lightsaber duel|sparred]] with [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]], who defeated the Padawan.<ref name="Aphra 33" />
