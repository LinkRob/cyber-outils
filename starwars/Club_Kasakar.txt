'''Club Kasakar''' was a nightclub located on [[Level 2685]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld|undercity]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Three [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] brought [[Anakin Skywalker]], then a [[Padawan]] to [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to that club.<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin 2" />
