"'''Fist of the Empire'''" is a four-page magazine article written by [[Megan Crouse]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 177|''Star Wars Insider'' 177]]<ref name="SWI 177" /> on [[November 28]], [[2018]].<ref name="SWI 177 release" /> It features an interview with [[Janina Gavankar]], who portrayed [[Iden Versio]] in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref name="SWI 177" /> The article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2019|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2019]].<ref name="SWI SE 2019" />
