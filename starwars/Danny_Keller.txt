'''Danny Keller''' is a [[Wikipedia:Storyboard|storyboard]] and [[Wikipedia:Previsualization|previsualization]] artist, and an episode director (starting in Season 4) for ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', a [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|CGI]] television series that debuted [[October 3]], [[2008]] on [[Cartoon Network]] as a continuation of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|film with the same name]], released two months prior. Keller was also head of animation for the mobile game ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''.<ref name="wired">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wired.com/2015/06/star-wars-uprising|text=There's an Epic-Sounding ''Star Wars'' RPG Coming to Mobile|author=Kohler, Chris|date=2015-06-04|work=[[Wikipedia:Wired (magazine)|''Wired'']]|archivedate=20200410015111}}</ref>
