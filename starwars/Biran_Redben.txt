'''Biran Redben''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] criminal<ref name="Mando14" /> who worked in a gang as a [[thief]]<ref name="Mandalorian Mayhem">{{InsiderCite|210|Mandalorian Mayhem}}</ref> during the [[New Republic Era]]. At some point, he was arrested on charges of [[Doctor|medical malpractice]] and was sentenced to serve fifty [[Standard year|years]] on [[Tamazall]] for his crimes.<ref name="Mando14" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|14|the appearance of Biran Redben}}</ref> he and a group of compatriots [[Hunt for Grogu|ambushed]] the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] on [[Tatooine]]. Their goal was to capture Djarin's [[Youngling|infant]] companion [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], a powerful [[Force-sensitive]] child. Although Redben's crew were successfully able to surprise the Mandalorian, they were ultimately defeated in combat.<ref name="Mando10">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 10: The Passenger}}</ref>

Sometime later, Redben's profile in the [[New Republic]] [[prison]] registry was one of several that [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] looked at from her office on the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]] while searching for the profile of [[prisoner]] [[Migs Mayfeld]].<ref name="Mando14" />

==Behind the scenes==
Biran Redben first appeared in "[[Chapter 10: The Passenger]]," the second episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',"<ref name="Mando10" /> which was released on [[November 6]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23 November">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref> The character went unnamed in the episode"<ref name="Mando10" /> and was only identified later as Biran Redben through [[Aurebesh]] text in the sixth episode of the season, "[[Chapter 14: The Tragedy]]," which was released on [[December 4]], 2020.<ref name="D23 December">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-december-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in December 2020}}</ref>
