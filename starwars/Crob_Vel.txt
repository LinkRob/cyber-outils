'''Crob Vel''' was a [[Gamorrean]] criminal who dealt in illicit merchandise. Vel hired the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]], who owed him a debt, to delay the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] in bringing him a delivery so that he could then force Solo to continue working for him to make up for it. Fett's intervention was successful, and the [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunter]] declared to Vel that his debt had been paid off.<ref name="Adventures 9" />
