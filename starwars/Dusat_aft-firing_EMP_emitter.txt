The '''Dusat aft-firing EMP emitter''' was a model of [[electromagnetic pulse]] weapon which was mounted on the [[Joben T-85 speeder bike]]s made by [[Zebulon Dak Speeder Corporation]]. The [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] disabled the EMP emitter on his T-85 bike when he used it on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]].<ref name="RVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dusat aft-firing EMP emitter was first identified in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Adam Bray]] and released in [[2014]]. The name of the Joben T-85 speeder bike and all its components are references to ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' characters from the ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]'', with the bike being named after [[Thall Joben/Legends|Thall Joben]], the EMP emitter being named for [[Jord Dusat]], and the bike's [[Moll K-19 power generator]] sharing its name with [[Kea Moll]].
