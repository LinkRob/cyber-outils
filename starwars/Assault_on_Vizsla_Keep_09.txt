During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] carried out an '''assault''' on [[Vizsla Keep 09]] in an effort to capture [[Maul]] and [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. The assault followed a [[Battle on Ord Mantell|battle]] on [[Ord Mantell]], where Maul captured Count Dooku and [[General]] [[Grievous]] after setting a trap for the two [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leaders. A team of [[Jedi]], led by [[Jedi Master]]s [[Mace Windu]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], tracked Maul to the supply outpost, where the former [[Sith apprentice]] convinced Dooku to join him and fight [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />

Once the assault began, the Jedi team, along with a group of [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], fought their way through an [[asteroid field]] and numerous [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|Gauntlet fighters]] towards the base, which they boarded. The Jedi engaged Maul and Dooku in a [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber duel|duel]], in which [[Jedi General]] [[Tiplee]] was [[Death|killed]]. After [[Death Watch]] forces incapacitated the Jedi, Maul and Dooku were able to escape. The alliance between Dooku and Maul led the Jedi to suspect that Dooku was the [[Sith Master]] they had been searching for.<ref name="Son of Dathomir 3" />
