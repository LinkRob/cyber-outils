'''Enak''' was one of the four [[moon]]s of the [[planet]] [[Rodia]], located in the [[Tyrius system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Savareen sector]].<ref name="BMF 58" /> The moon was situated at grid square R-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Grid" />

==Behind the scenes==
Enak was first mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 58|fifty-eighth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF 58" /> published around [[February 10]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BMFdates|58}}</ref> The [[Enak/Legends|moon]] was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'', a sourcebook for [[West End Games]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published in [[September]] [[1996]].<ref name="Shadows">''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]''</ref>
