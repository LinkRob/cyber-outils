'''Blaster muskets'''<ref name="So Much Trouble AD">{{SkeletonCrew|We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> were a type of [[blaster pistol]] that was used by the [[Shistavanen]] [[pirate]] [[Brutus]] during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="So Much Trouble" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SCDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> [[Captain]] Brutus was captured while welding a blaster musket by the ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'s'' [[Cargo-lifting arm]] while approached its landing ramp after it was [[tractor beam|tractored]] into the hanger of [[Silvo's pirate frigate|his frigate]]. After being grabbed by the lift arm he fired a single blast before dropping the blaster. The former pirate captain [[Jod Na Nawood|Silvo]] picked up Brutus' blaster musket after approaching the restrained Brutus. Avenging his previous mutiny, Silvo killed Brutus with a single shot from the blaster musket and retook his pirate crew.<ref name="So Much Trouble" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blaster muskets were first identified in the [[Disney+]] audio descriptions of "[[We're Gonna Be In So Much Trouble]]," the seventh episode of the live&ndash;action series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' which was released on [[January 7]], [[2025]].<ref name="So Much Trouble" />
