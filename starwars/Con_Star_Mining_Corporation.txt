The '''Con Star Mining Corporation''' was a key [[The galaxy|galactic]]-wide [[mining]] company active in the [[time]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]]. A hundred years before the [[New Republic Era]], Con Star abandoned the formerly lush mining world of [[Parnassos]] following a nuclear disaster, leaving the population to eke out a primeval existence. By the time of the [[New Republic]], they were still operating.<ref name="Phasma" />

==History==
Around [[162 BBY]],<ref name="Phasma date 1">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']] to [[34 ABY]]. As ''Phasma'' establishes that the Con Star Mining Corporation arrived on [[Parnassos]] one hundred and eighty-six years before chapters set ten years before the present day events, the company must have arrived on Paranassos in [[162 BBY]].</ref> the Con Star Mining Corporation established colonies and [[Mining|mines]] on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]] protected by a [[Parnassos' automated defense system|defense grid]]. The colonies were abandoned after a [[Devastation of Parnassos|nuclear accident]] at one of the facilities, forcing the natives into destitution.<ref name="Phasma" />

In [[24 ABY]],<ref name="Phasma date 2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']] to [[34 ABY]]. As the [[mission to Parnassos]] takes place in chapters set ten years before the present day events, the mission must have taken place in [[24 ABY]].</ref> [[First Order]] [[troop]]s led by [[General]] [[Brendol Hux]] crashed on the planet. They escaped with the help of the warrior [[Phasma]], and Hux implied the First Order would punish the company for their abandonment.<ref name="Phasma" />
