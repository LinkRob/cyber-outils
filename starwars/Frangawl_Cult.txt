{{Quote|The Frangawl ruled Bardotta with an iron hand for most of its early history. They were warriors. Some would call them barbarians, but in fact they were men and women of spirit.|[[Savatte]]|<ref name="Disappeared, Part I" />}}
The '''Frangawl Cult''', also known as the '''Frangawl clan''', was a<ref name="Disappeared, Part I" /> [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]<ref name="TSWB" /> cult from [[Bardotta]] dedicated to the worship of [[Malmourral]], the Bardottan [[demon]] of war. Once banished by the [[Dagoyan Order]], they foresaw a time when those masters would disappear. During the [[Clone Wars]], they captured the [[planet]]'s [[Queen]] [[Julia]] in attempt to fulfill their prophecy.<ref name="Disappeared, Part I" /> They revered the [[witch]] [[Talzin]] as the [[Mother (title)|Great Mother]].<ref name="Disappeared, Part II" />
