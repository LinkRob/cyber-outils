A [[species]] of [[Cyborg|cybernetic]] '''spiders''' that [[Diet|fed]] on power sources was created by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[scientist]] [[Cylo]] in the early [[Standard month|months]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Imperial Era|reign]]. At some point, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Shalla Mondatha]], better known as "the Shade," was tasked with transporting crates containing such [[spider]]s, but the creatures took over [[Moveable Feast|her ship]].<ref name="Dark" />
