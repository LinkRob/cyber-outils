'''Domabesh''' was an alphabetic script used throughout [[the galaxy]] for thousands of years. The script dated to [[Millennium|millennia]] prior to the [[Galactic Civil War]], having been used in carved inscriptions on a [[Unidentified Sith temple|temple]] honoring the ancient [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Exar Kun]]. Still in use by the beginning of the [[Imperial Era]], the script was seen on worlds such as [[Saleucami]], [[Jedha]] and [[Daiyu]].

==Description==
Domabesh letters were roughly square in proportion, with strokes of slightly varying widths, and some filled regions. The letters shared some visual similarities with the common [[Aurebesh]] writing system.<ref name="R1" />

==History==
===Republic era===
[[File:Offworld_Travel.png|thumb|left|180px|"Offworld Travel"]]
Domabesh<ref name="Art of Survivor">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> came into use thousands of years prior to [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date 1">"[[Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment]]" states that [[Exar Kun]] lived thousands of [[Standard year|years]] before [[Darth Sidious]] and [[Maul|Darth Maul]]. Maul's apprenticeship to Sidious ended during the [[Battle of Naboo]], dated to [[32 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, by 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] the script must have been in use for thousands of years.</ref> as inscriptions on a [[Unidentified Sith temple|temple]] honoring the ancient<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]<ref name="Lightsabers">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment}}</ref> [[Exar Kun]] were carved in it. The inscription read "{{Uc:Our temple honouring Sith Lord Exar Kun}}".<ref name="Solo Guide" /><ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' {{C|Note that, as shown in {{Reddit|url=r/aurebesh/comments/kf66n5/on_translating_the_runes_on_dryden_voss_desk|thread=On translating the runes on Dryden Vos's desk|user=Ender_HappyLeviathan|date=2020-12-17|archivedate=20220617040134}}, half the inscription on the [[obsidian]] slab can be seen in the film, while the rest can be seen in the guide book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''. Also note that the inscription on [[Tobias Beckett|Becket's]] box is seen upside-down.}}</ref> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{FilmDate|Solo}}</ref> the [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] of [[Crimson Dawn]] had plundered Kun's temple and looted an [[obsidian]] slab engraved with this script, which was set into a [[Dryden's desk|custom-made desk]] that Vos used on his [[Nau'ur-class yacht|yacht]], ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Solo Guide" />

An ancient [[Dataplaque|navigational dataplaque]] presumed stolen by Vos from the [[Bureau of Ships and Services Heritage Museum]] on [[Coruscant]], said to be from an old [[Jedi]] survey craft, was inscribed with "[[Light side of the Force|LIGHT]]" at the top, "[[Dark side of the Force|DARK]]" at the bottom, and "THE BALANCE" in the middle.<ref name="Scum">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

A page in a [[holobook]] that [[Kai Brightstar]] consulted to find a cure for [[sniffles]] read "TABLE OF CONTENTS" in this script.<ref name="Nubs">{{YJA|Get Well Nubs}}</ref> 

When Brightstar's fellow [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Lys Solay]] looked up information on the [[kibbin]] in the [[Galactic Creature Guide]], a picture of the creature appeared on her [[datapad]] along with a caption in Domabesh, although the name of the animal was misspelled as "KIBGIN".<ref name="The Missing Kibbin">{{YJA|The Missing Kibbin}}</ref>

After [[SF-R3]] added Lys Solay and [[Myra|Myra's]] research on the [[leepur]] to the Galactic Creature Guide, he showed them the new entry on a datapad, where the headline was written in Domabesh. It read "JUANBRAVO".<ref name="A New Discovery">{{YJA|A New Discovery}}</ref> 

When Solay later looked up the [[blue aklyrr]] in the guide, the captions were all written in this script: The first entry shows a picture of a Kibbin, although the name of the animal was once again misspelled as "KIBGIN". The second entry featured a [[tooka]], although the caption merely said "BHRISTINA"<small>''[sic]''</small>. The third entry contained a picture of the [[Color|blue]] aklyrr, although the caption made no sense: It seemed the intended word was "AKLYRR", as it consisted of six letters, with 'K' as the third letter and the last two letters being identical to each other. However, aside from the 'K', none of the symbols matched any known Domabesh characters.<ref name="The Wild Aklyrr">{{YJA|The Wild Aklyrr}}</ref>

While stuck inside because of the pouring [[Water|rain]], Solay took an interest in a dusty [[library]] holobook called ''[[Legends of the Jedi Temple on Tenoo]]''. When she opened the book, a [[Hologram|holographic]] projection of a furry creature known as a [[tazi sloth]] popped up, while the adjacent page displayed a meaningless headline written in Domabesh, which said "HNR KTIB".<ref name="The Rainy Day Beast" />

During a [[Standard day|day]] off, Brightstar chose to relax and read, opening a holobook to find both pages displaying the Domabesh headline "TABLE OF CONTENTS".<ref name="The Spaceport Setback">{{YJA|The Spaceport Setback}}</ref>

As [[0G-LC]] briefed the Jedi Initiates about the [[Raka (creature)|raka]], he referred to a datapad displaying an image of the creature along with the headline "OPAN IS UAJ" written in Domabesh.<ref name="Fossil Hunt">{{YJA|Fossil Hunt}}</ref>

When Solay looked up information on [[lakeetah]]s in the guide at a later point, the caption, written in Domabesh, did not does not correlate with the image of a lakeetah. It said "SBJH AJ".<ref name="Speeder Surprise">{{YJA|Speeder Surprise}}</ref>

===Imperial era===
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date 3">''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'' begins with [[Order 66]], the execution of which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. As the episodes "[[Cut and Run]]" and "[[Decommissioned]]" take place shortly thereafter, it must also be set in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> a sign for "[[Offworld Travel]]" in a [[Saleucami town|town]] on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]] was written in the script.<ref name="Cut and Run" /> The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date 3" /> [[decommissioning facility]] workers on [[Corellia]], including infiltrators [[Rafa Martez|Rafa]] and [[Trace Martez]], wore [[helmet]]s decorated with<ref name="Decommissioned" /> the letters "KVH" written in this script.<ref name="Decommissioned Concept Art">{{SW|url=decommissioned-concept-art-gallery|text="Decommissioned" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slides 1, 8}}</ref>

[[File:TamPosla-ROShadowPortraits2.png|thumb|250px|right|Tam Posla's helmet was marked with the letters "MA" in Domabesh script.]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Star Wars: Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> when the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' became trapped in a [[gravity well]], the crew was forced to inject a drop of [[coaxium]] into the [[Fusion generator|fusion reactor]] to give the [[YT-1000 light freighter|freighter]] the kick needed to escape. During the process, [[Tobias Beckett]] opened up a box with a sticky [[Color|grey]] substance that he used to attach a little pump to a hose. The word "DOM" was written on the lid, using this script.<ref name="Solo" />

In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="KenobiDate">{{KenobiDate|II}}</ref> two signs in [[Daiyu spaceport]], reading "[[Shipcyr|SHIPCYR]]" and "IS''<small>[obscured character]''</small>REW" respectively, were written in Domabesh, although every letter in the latter's name was mirrored. Some of the signs in [[Daiyu City]] also used this script. One such sign advertised "[[Fresh Brew|FRESH BREW]]," although the entire sign was upside-down. Another sign read "FRESH" and a third sign read "[[Unidentified disco|DISCO]]".<ref name="Kenobi">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref> 

In [[5 BBY]], a wall next to a set of stairs near a [[Unidentified screen store|screen store]] on [[Ferrix]] carried an inscription using this script, ending in the letters "TSO". White industrial container on a [[salvage yard]] on Ferrix carried a warning in this script,<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> reading "CAUTION": "FLAMMABLE".<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> [[Computer]] monitors inside the transport business [[Xanwan's freight kiosk]] contained some lettering in this font, although none of it made any sense.<ref name="That Would Be Me">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref> An ad painted at a wall said "[[Owauwaq Freight|OWAUWAC FREIGHT]]" (although the first letter in the ''second'' word had faded away and was obscured by a pipe), with the first word written in this font.<ref name="Reckoning" />

[[File:Domabesh.png|thumb|250px|left|"THEFORCESURROUNDS" written over an archway in Jedha City (note that the first letter is obscured)]]

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="RO Date">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> a [[Unidentified Rebel MP|Rebel MP]] wore a helmet with the initials "MP" written in this font, and a shirt with the letter G written in the same script. The [[Holy Quarter]] of [[Jedha City]] was decorated with writing in the script prior to the [[city]]'s [[Destruction of Jedha City|destruction]], on walls, archways and railings. For example, "BO MEATH THE<small>''[two characters obscured by burn marks]''</small> SZ T E" was written on one archway, while another archway read "THEFORCESURROUNDS" ("[[The Force]] surrounds") was painted on a different archway. Other inscriptions produced gibberish when translated. During the [[Battle of Scarif]], the [[Rebel pilot]] [[Red Twelve (Rebel Alliance)|Red Twelve]] wore a [[flight helmet]] with yellow markings in the script, with the inscription on the right side of the helmet ending with "OSS", while the one on the left began with "BOO".<ref name="R1" /> The [[bounty hunter]] [[Tam Posla]] wore a helmet adorned with "MA", short for "[[Milvayne Authority]]," in the script.<ref name="R1 VG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

===New Republic era===
By [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|2|8}}</ref> [[Resistance pilot]] [[Venisa Doza]] wore a flight helmet with red markings that spelled out<ref name="Rendezvous Point">{{Resistance|Rendezvous Point (episode)}}</ref> "DOZA"<ref name="RP Concept Art">{{SW|url=rendezvous-point-concept-art-gallery|text=Rendezvous Point Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref><ref name="Bucket">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-rendezvous-point-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 7 Fun Facts from "Rendezvous Point" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=November 25, 2019}}</ref> in the script.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It's called domabesh, and was made for Rogue One.|Pablo Hidalgo|<ref name="Pablo Tweet">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1399068407740321792|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2021-05-30|quote=It's called domabesh, and was made for Rogue One.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/qGyVn}}</ref>}}
[[File:Domabesh_font.png|thumb|left|300px|A representation of the Domabesh font.]]
Domabesh first appeared in the [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released in [[2016]].<ref name="R1" /> In [[2019]], when [[Glyn Dillon]], a costume designer of ''Rogue One'', was asked about the font on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], he revealed that it was called Domabesh and had been developed at the request of<ref name="Dillon Tweet">{{Twitter|glyn_dillon|status/1110995215219855363|[[Glyn Dillon]]|date=2019-03-27|quote=The fonts were made at Gareth's request for R.O. he didn't want a universe with just one text (and mostly one font). They were designed by the art dept graphics team. I was able to use them in any designs that needed text. Dishabesh just seemed to suit Enfys more than the others.|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/z5GaL|nolive=1}}<br />{{Twitter|LelalMekha|status/1111001692626210818|Alban Leloup|quote=Dishabesh, eh? That's the actual name they gave it? And the one in this picture is "Domabesh," right?|date=2019-03-27|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/z5GaL}}<br />{{Twitter|glyn_dillon|status/1111005548034646022|[[Glyn Dillon]]|quote=Correct|date=2019-03-27|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/z5GaL|nolive=1}}</ref> director<ref name="R1" /> [[Gareth Edwards]].<ref name="Dillon Tweet" /> According to [[Barry Gingell]], who worked as a graphic designer on the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'',<ref name="Kassa" /> the alphabet was created by [[Dominic Sikking]],<ref>{{Twitter|BazatronPrime|status/1668534758261809154|[[Barry Gingell]]|date=2023-06-13|quote=Domabesh was done by Dom Sikking.|archivedate=20230625005852}}</ref> a graphic designer on ''Rogue One''.<ref name="R1" />

In [[2021]], when asked on Twitter, [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] called it "Domabesh,"<ref name="Pablo Tweet" /> but the name did not appear in [[canon]] until the [[2024]] refererence book ''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Art Survivor">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> According to Hidalgo, Domabesh is thought to be an archaic script, not as ancient as [[Ur-Kittât]], but older than [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Pablo Age Tweet">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1401747106033913856|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=I don't know how that was decided; but I do know Domabesh is thought to be archaic, with its usage on Jedha. Not as ancient as the McQuarrie script, but much older than run-of-the-mill Aurebesh at least.|date=2021-06-21|archiveurl=https://archive.is/oqZDg}}</ref>

According to concept art by concept designer [[Will Htay]], the full Domabesh inscription on one of the archways in [[Jedha City]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' is "THEFORCESURROUNDSUSPENETRATESANDBINDSTHEGALAXYTOGETHER", or "[[The Force]] surrounds us, penetrates and binds [[the galaxy]] together";<ref name="Htaydesign">{{WebCite|url=https://www.htaydesign.com/work/rogue-one-a-star-wars-story|text=Rogue One: A Star Wars Story|author=[[Will Htay|Htay, Will]]|work=HTAYDESIGN|archiveurl=https://archive.is/UFjHR|archivedate=2023-05-17}} {{C|[https://archive.ph/n6Knm/05cd9ac78efd7a264c1469243b2ae8fa540d915f.jpg concept art]}}</ref> paraphrasing [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] in the film {{Film|4}}, where the old [[Jedi Master]] says, "The Force is what gives a [[Jedi]] his power. It's an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds us, penetrates us, it binds the galaxy together."<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> In the actual movie, only "THEFORCESURROUNDS" is seen.<ref name="R1" /> According to Gingell, font designer Sikking originally planned to use spaces, but the concept artist removed them in the production art of Jedha and this idea stuck.<ref>{{Twitter|BazatronPrime|status/1659109194186780672|[[Barry Gingell]]|date=2023-06-18|quote=UPDATE: Dom says hi and he's flattered. He says spaces are allowed and originally planned, but the concept artist removed them in his Jedha art and it stuck.|archivedate=20230625010841}}</ref>

A piece of concept art by [[Chris Caldow]] in the behind the scenes book ''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' shows a [[Jedha]] market cooking stall that says "FRY" in Domabesh.<ref name="Art Rogue">''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

In the teaser trailer for the film, there is a shot of [[Stormtrooper|Imperial stormtroopers]] driving a [[TX-225 Occupier]] assault [[tank]] through the streets of Jedha City, with a Domabesh inscription set in stone on the building behind them, reading "[[Gesh's Tapcafe|BAR BELLOQ]]";<ref name="Teaser">{{SWYouTube|Wji-BZ0oCwg|ROGUE ONE: A STAR WARS STORY Official Teaser Trailer}}</ref> seemingly a nod to [[w:c:indianajones:René Emile Belloq|René Belloq]], the main antagonist from the [[Lucasfilm]] production ''[[Wikipedia:Raiders of the Lost Ark|Raiders of the Lost Ark]]'',<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.giantbomb.com/rene-belloq/3005-4124|text=Rene Belloq|quote=Rene Belloq is one of Indiana Jone's <small>''[sic]''</small> villains in the classic film series. Belloq has been known to steal Indy's archeological finds before he can bring them to a museum or wherever they may be heading. Rene appears in Indiana Jones and the Raiders of the Lost Ark as well as games based off of the Indiana Jones movies.|work=''[[Wikipedia:Giant Bomb|Giant Bomb]]''|archivedate=20220222155023}}</ref> that the [[w:c:indianajones:Indiana Jones|titular character]] meets in an [[w:c:indianajones:Marhala Bar|Egyptian bar]] at one point.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Film/RaidersOfTheLostArk|text= Film / Raiders of the Lost Ark|quote=During Indy and Belloq's Nonviolent Initial Confrontation at a Cairo bar, Indy reaches for his gun only to discover that the entire place is filled with armed Arabs.|work=[[Wikipedia:TV Tropes|TV Tropes]]|archivedate=20230515001857}}</ref> This shot does not appear in the film.<ref name="R1" />

''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'' later confirmed the name Domabesh to be [[canon]].<ref name="Art of Survivor" />

In the ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' episode "[[A New Discovery]]," the headline of the newly written entry on the [[leepur]] in the [[Galactic Creature Guide]] reads "JUANBRAVO" in Domabesh. This appears to be a reference to [[Juan Luis Bravo]], who worked as a storyboard artist on this episode.<ref name="A New Discovery" />
