A nameless [[Stone|rocky]] '''barren moon''' not far from [[Jabiim]] was the site of a [[Fight on a barren moon|duel]] between [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[9 BBY]].

==Description==
A [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Jabiim]], located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> its barren surface<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|barren-moon|barren moon}}</ref> featured [[Unidentified barren moon's dry lands|dry lands]] and a [[labyrinth of rock pinnacles]].<ref name="Sideshow" /> The latter geography featured great crevasses among the [[Stone|rock]] spires.<ref name="Databank" />

==History==
{{Quote|I have to face him, Master. Whether he dies or I do, this ends today.|Obi-Wan Kenobi reaches out to [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] before his duel with Darth Vader|<ref name="Part VI" />}}
[[File:Acme-IWillDestroyYou.png|thumb|left|300px|The border between the rocky pillars and the dry lands becomes a historic site as Kenobi and Vader face off.]]
The moon lacked any kind of name,<ref name="Databank" /> and was simply known as a barren moon.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith: The Chronicles of Emperor Palpatine]]''</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">{{KenobiDate|VI|the duel on the barren moon}}</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], having just [[Attack on Jabiim|escaped]]<ref name="Part VI" /> the nearby<ref name="Databank" /> Jabiim with members of the [[Hidden Path]] network, decided to make his way to the moon, having realized that the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]&mdash;who, in truth, was Kenobi's former [[Padawan]] Anakin Skywalker&mdash;was more interested in hunting him than the greater Path network. Joined only by the [[toy droid]] [[L0-LA59]], whom Kenobi did not even realize was aboard his ship until landing, Kenobi departed from the Path's [[JT-731 Broadhorn transport]] aboard a smaller craft and landed within<ref name="Part VI" /> the labyrinth of rocky pinnacles.<ref name="Sideshow" /> Indeed allowing the Path to escape in favor of hunting Kenobi, Vader followed Kenobi aboard a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] and landed within<ref name="Part VI" /> the dry lands<ref name="Sideshow" /> on the border of the rocky network.<ref name="Part VI" />

Spotting his former master outside the rock pillars, the two exchanged greetings&mdash;Vader asking if Kenobi intended to kill him, with Kenobi only countering that he would do what was necessary, which was a sentiment that Vader returned&mdash;before beginning<ref name="Part VI" /> a [[Fight on a barren moon|historic lightsaber duel]]<ref name="Databank" /> that crossed from the dry lands into the rocky pillars. Vader initially bested Kenobi before his former master, drawing on his hope for the future, regained his strength and overcame the Sith Lord, damaging [[Darth Vader's armor|Vader's armor]] in the process. In speaking face-to-face with his former disciple, Kenobi was forced to admit that Anakin Skywalker was "dead,"<ref name="Part VI" /> cementing the nameless moon's place in history.<ref name="Databank" /> Kenobi then departed as Vader called after him, making his way to the planet [[Tatooine]] to [[Assault on the Lars Homestead|protect]] the young [[Luke Skywalker]] and then returning L0-LA59 to [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] on the planet [[Alderaan]]. Vader himself would leave and report to his new master, the Sith Lord [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Part VI" />
