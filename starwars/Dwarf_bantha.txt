{{Quote|In addition to the common bantha, two other species are known to exist: the diminutive dwarf bantha, which dwells in the canyons and cliffs of Tatooine’s outermost deserts, and the less furry dune bantha, which is capable of surviving in the heat (and severe drought!) of the equatorial zone.|From the journal of Ardis San Tekka|<ref name="Bestiary" />}}
The '''dwarf bantha''' was a diminutive [[species]] of [[bantha]], a type of [[Diet|omnivorous]] [[mammal]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the<ref name="Bestiary" /> [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="CGttSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Dwarf banthas lived in the canyons and cliffs of the world's outermost deserts. [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] [[Director of Creature Cartography]] [[Ardis San Tekka]] mentioned dwarf banthas in an entry on banthas in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]]<ref name="Bestiary" /> no earlier than [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], dwarf banthas were mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[S.T. Bende]].<ref name="Bestiary" /> The [[Dwarf bantha/Legends|species]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the ''[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide]]'' written by [[Ben Burtt]]<ref name="TravelGuide">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref> and published on [[August 7]], [[2001]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/21926/star-wars-galactic-phrase-book-and-travel-guide-by-ben-burtt|text=Star Wars: Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide}}</ref> Dwarf banthas were then pictured for the first time in ''[[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]]'', a reference book written by [[Terryl Whitlatch]] and [[Bob Carrau]], with illustrations by Whitlatch,<ref name="Wildlife">''[[The Wildlife of Star Wars: A Field Guide]]''</ref> that was published in [[November]] 2001.<ref name="WildlifeDate">{{SWArchive|url=eu/lit/ref/news20010702.html|text=Explore the Wildlife of ''Star Wars''}}</ref> The species then appeared for the first time in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] ''[[Star Wars Galaxies]]'', which was developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment.<ref name="SWG">{{SWGcite|exp=AED|type=location|Deserts|Tatooine}}</ref>
