'''Emorson ''' was a [[Gen'Dai]] [[doctor]] and [[cyberneticist]] active on [[Valtos]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was adept at [[cybernetics|cybernetic]] surgery and was an associate of the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]]. Soon after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], Emorson performed cybernetic surgery on the [[Gloovan]] Nihil [[Muglan Tarantyne]] after she was transected<ref name="Script" /> by the [[Avar Kriss' lightsaber|lightsaber]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]].<ref name="The High Republic (2021) 13">[[The High Republic (2021) 13|''The High Republic'' (2021) 13]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Emorson was mentioned in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'', a [[2024]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of the doctor's name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|script]] in [[2025]].<ref name="Script" />
