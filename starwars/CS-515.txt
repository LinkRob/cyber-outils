{{Quote|Yeah, this is, uh, CS-515, over. Having, uh, comm&mdash; uh, comm problems.''"<br />"''Affirmative, CS-515, head back to command.|Kazuda Xiono, as CS-515, and a First Order stormtrooper|<ref name="The New Trooper" />}}
'''CS-515''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] sent to the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform when the [[First Order]] began to [[Occupation of the Colossus|occupy]] it. He was knocked unconscious by the [[Youngling|child]] refugees [[Eila]] and [[Kel]]. Eila and Kel's friend [[Kazuda Xiono]], who was a [[Resistance]] [[spy]], then stole his [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]] in order obtain a First Order [[data rod]]. Due to Xiono's "erratic" actions, CS-515 was sent for [[reconditioning]] by [[Sergeant]] [[CS-812]] and [[Commander]] [[Pyre]].

==Biography==
===Knocked unconscious===
{{Quote|Uh, he's out cold. Did you kids do this?''"<br />"''He tried to take us away.|Tamara Ryvora and Eila, on CS-515|<ref name="The New Trooper" />}}
[[File:KazSleepingTrooper-TheNewTrooper.jpg|thumb|left|250px|During the First Order's occupation of the ''Colossus'', CS-515 was captured by residents of the refueling station.]]
CS-515 was a [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served with [[CS-Squad]], which was part of the [[Colossus garrison|First Order garrison]] assigned to the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform on the [[planet]] [[Castilon]]. His [[squad]] was assigned with verifying the identities of all residents living aboard the ''Colossus''.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

While searching the platform, he overheard [[Youngling|child]] fugitives [[Eila]] and [[Kel]] laughing while [[fishing]] on the underside of the platform. CS-515 confronted them and demanded that they produce their identification. When the children were unable to produce their identification, he demanded that they come with him and grabbed Eila. Kel sprang to the defense of his sister and threw a [[snarlfish]] at him, causing CS-515 to hit his head on a railing and fall unconscious.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

===Resistance entanglements===
{{Quote|You're not seriously considering&hellip;''"<br />"''We need to find out what info they have. I'll go topside as this trooper and see if he reported the kids.|Tamara Ryvora and Kazuda Xiono|<ref name="The New Trooper" />}}
After locking CS-515 inside a room, Kel and Eila asked the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] for help. The children led Xiono and his friends [[Neeku Vozo]], [[BB-8]], and [[Tamara Ryvora]] inside the room. After narrowly avoiding a stormtrooper patrol, Xiono and his friends hid the unconscious trooper in a room in the engineering deck, which was the domain of the [[Chelidae]] [[engineer]]s and [[Technician|mechanics]] who operated the ''Colossus''{{'}} machinery.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

[[File:TheNewTrooper-EpisodeGuide.jpg|thumb|right|250px|CS-515's armor was taken by a Resistance spy, Kazuda Xiono, who intended to impersonate the stormtrooper.]]
Since CS-515's comrades were searching for him, Xiono came up with a plan to steal the stormtrooper's [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]] and impersonate him while attempting to [[Theft of First Order data rod|discover]] if the unconscious [[trooper]] had reported the children's presence on the platform. While posing as CS-515, Xiono reported to the squad's [[commanding officer]], [[Sergeant]] [[CS-812]]. When CS-Squad was accosted by angry locals, Xiono took the opportunity to infiltrate an [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttle]] and steal a First Order [[data rod]].<ref name="The New Trooper" />

Meanwhile, a disoriented CS-515 awoke in unfamiliar surroundings. Neeku took the opportunity to "befriend" the stormtrooper before knocking him unconscious with an [[electro-shock prod]]. Meanwhile, Xiono's "erratic behavior" led Sergeant CS-812 and the garrison's leader [[Commander]] [[Pyre]] to assume there was something wrong with CS-515's mental conditioning. Before they could send him for [[Memory wipe|reconditioning]], Xiono fled into the ''Colossus''{{'}} corridors and led the stormtroopers on a chase.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

===Framed===
{{Quote|How'd I get here? My head hurts.''"<br />"''Take him to Reconditioning.|CS-515 and CS-812|<ref name="The New Trooper" />}}
[[File:CSSquad-TheNewTrooper.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Xiono's behavior was considered unusual for a stormtrooper, leading the other troopers to take the real CS-515 in for reconditioning.]]
Xiono managed to find his friends and change back into his [[Clothing|clothes]]. After Xiono fled, the stormtroopers tracked down the real CS-515, who had been dressed up in his [[armor]] again, and found him in a state of disorientation from repeatedly being knocked and shocked unconscious. This led them to take the dazed trooper off for [[reconditioning]], unaware of what had really transpired.<ref name="The New Trooper" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Let's see some identification. Now.|CS-515, to Eila and Kel|<ref name="The New Trooper" />}}
CS-515 was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] with [[Color|black]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and tan skin. As a member of the ''Colossus'' garrison, CS-515 sought to comply with superior orders to register all inhabitants living aboard the ''Colossus''. He was trained to wield a [[blaster]] and to detain people. CS-515 was not skilled at physical combat and was knocked unconscious by Kel, who was protecting his sister. CS-515 appeared to be trusting, regarding Neeku as his "green friend."<ref name="The New Trooper" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Ugh. This suit takes some getting used to.|Kazuda Xiono, on First Order stormtrooper armor|<ref name="The New Trooper" />}}
[[File:UndercoverKazFightsMB13A-TheNewTrooper.jpg|thumb|right|250px|CS-515 wore the standard armor of a First Order stormtrooper, which Xiono appropriated for the purpose of spycraft.]]
As a First Order stormtrooper, CS-515 wore the standard white stormtrooper armor over a black [[body glove]]. His [[helmet]] was equipped with a built-in [[comlink]]. He also carried an [[F-11D blaster rifle]].<ref name="The New Trooper" />

==Behind the scenes==
CS-515 first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|Season One]] episode "[[The New Trooper]]," which premiered on [[February 10]], [[2019]]. He was voiced by [[Steve Blum]], who voiced [[Garazeb Orrelios|Zeb]] and a majority of the stormtroopers in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="The New Trooper" />
