'''David Rache''' voiced [[Firmus Piett/Legends|Firmus Piett]] in [[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|''The Empire Strikes Back'' radio drama]].

Outside of the franchise he starred in the popular Disney/Warner television series ''Nurses'' (1992-1994) alongside famous ACE nominee comedian Stephanie Hodge and Golden Globes winner Loni Anderson, the television series ''Sledge Hammer'', the movie ''Amira and Sam'', among other titles.
