A '''comm tower'''<ref name="justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref> or '''Transmitter tower'''<ref name="DB">{{Databank|lothal-transmitter-tower|Lothal Transmitter Tower}}</ref> was a tower used to aid communications. [[Lothal]] had several of them, including [[Comm Tower E-234]]<ref name="justice" /> and [[LothalNet]] [[LothalNet comm tower E-272|comm tower E-272]], which [[Ezra Bridger]] lived in after it was abandoned.<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> The [[Galactic Empire]]'s main [[Imperial Communications Center|comm tower]] on the planet was later [[Raid on the communications tower|destroyed]] on the orders of [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] after the [[Spectres]] hijacked it.<ref name="Call to Action">{{Rebels|Call to Action}}</ref> Comm towers could also be found on [[Lah'mu]] and [[Wrea]].<ref name="rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> At some point by [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Rogue One}} states the [[demolitions job on Foerost]] occurred prior to the [[Battle of Scarif]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> a [[Foerost communications tower|communications tower]] was destroyed by the [[Drabatan]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Paodok'Draba'Takat]] during a [[Demolitions job on Foerost|demolitions job]] on the astronomical object of [[Foerost]].<ref name="RogueNovel">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (novelization)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' novelization]]</ref>
