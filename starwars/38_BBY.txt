The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''38 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3239 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''19 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7939''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Naberrie]] joins the [[Apprentice Legislature]] at the age of eight.<ref name="TCVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>
*The [[Naboo Royal Starship]] is completed.<ref name="BMF 47" />
*[[Gardulla Besadii the Elder]] loses the [[Slavery|slaves]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]] and [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi Skywalker]] in a [[Gambling|bet]] to [[Watto]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Gardulla">According to ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'', [[Watto]] won [[Anakin Skywalker]] from [[Gardulla Besadii the Elder|Gardulla]] when he was 3 [[Standard year|years]] old, around six years before he was discovered by the [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. Since the latter event took place in [[32 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the former event was around 38 BBY.</ref>

==Births==
*[[Wald]] on [[Tatooine]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Wald Databank">According to the script of {{Databank|wald|Wald}}, [[Wald]] is 6 [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of ''[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace]]''. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[32 BBY]], She must have been born in around 38 BBY.</ref>
*[[Amee]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Amee">According to the script of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], as seen in ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Insider's Guide]]'', [[Amee]] is 6 [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of the film. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the film to [[32 BBY]], she must have been born in around 38 BBY.</ref>
*[[Zeeth]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Zeeth">According to ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' (p. 25), [[Zeeth]] was 16 years old as of [[22 BBY]], he must have been born in around 38 BBY.</ref>

==Deaths==
*[[Feyra Akaris]] on [[Pkori]] {{Circa}}<ref name="Feyra">According to ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'', [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] was 18 [[Standard year|years]] old at the [[time]] of the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]]. Therefore, Akaris was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[40 BBY]]. As the novel also establishes that [[Feyra Akaris]] [[Death|died]] 2 years after Iskat's birth, she must have passed away around 38 BBY.</ref>
