The '''''Crumb Bomber''''' was a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]] used by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The [[Starship|ship's]] [[nose]] was decorated with art of a [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] holding a [[missile]] and the [[Aurebesh]] text "laugh this off". [[Standard year|Years]] later, during the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Lothal]] [[cantina]] [[Old Jho's Pit Stop]] had on display the nose of a LAAT/i gunship with the ''Crumb Bomber''{{'s}} nose art, which proprietor [[Jho]] claimed was the authentic gunship itself. Decades after that during the [[Cold War]], [[Aunt Z's Tavern]] on the [[Colossus]] platform displayed the Kowakian artwork as part of its décor.

==Description==
[[File:Kowakian_mascot.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The ''Crumb Bomber''{{'s}} nose art]]
The ''Crumb Bomber''<ref name="SOM Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=shadow-of-malevolence-trivia-gallery|text="Shadow of Malevolence" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> was a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] [[gunship]].<ref name="USW" /> Therefore, like all vehicles of the class, it had a length of 17.69 [[meter]]s,<ref name="ESOV">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> a height of 6.54 meters,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|republic-attack-gunship|Republic Attack Gunship}}</ref> and could carry up to thirty passengers.<ref name="USW" /> The ''Crumb Bomber'' was armed with the standard chin-mounted dual [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="Databank" /> It was normally flown by one [[clone trooper pilot]], who was accompanied by a co-pilot.<ref name="TCW film" /> Large spotlights were occasionally mounted on the front for increased visibility at night.<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> The ''Crumb Bomber'', in addition to its standard red-and-white paintjob, sported nose art depicting a [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] holding a [[missile]], with "laugh this off" written in [[Aurebesh]] above the creature. Orange kill markings were painted below the artwork.<ref name="EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/shadow-of-malevolence-episode-guide|text="Shadow of Malevolence" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], the ''Crumb Bomber'' saw action during the [[Battle of Teth|battle]] of [[Teth]]<ref name="TCW film" /> and was later stationed on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Resolute]]'' during the hunt for the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]''.<ref name="SoM">{{TCW|Shadow of Malevolence|Shadow of ''Malevolence''}}</ref> The ''Crumb Bomber'' also rescued the [[Clone trooper|clone]] survivors of the [[Defense of Rishi Station|battle]] of the [[Rishi Moon]]<ref name="Rookies" /> and participated in the [[Battle of Ryloth]].<ref name="IoR">{{TCW|Innocents of Ryloth}}</ref>

During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a [[cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] known as [[Old Jho's Pit Stop]] displayed the remains of a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport]] with the same nose art as the ''Crumb Bomber'', which cantina owner [[Jho]] claimed was the authentic remains of the Clone Wars-era ship.<ref name="ED Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/empire-day-trivia-gallery|text="Empire Day" Trivia Gallery - Star Wars Rebels}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> Several decades later, the Kowakian monkey-lizard art logo could still be found, on a wing panel used to decorate the wall of [[Aunt Z's Tavern]] on the ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="High Tower BL">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-high-tower-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 9 Fun Facts from "The High Tower" - ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2018-10-29}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Crumb Bomber'' was created for the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', and first appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]], in [[2008]].<ref name="TCW film" /> It was reintroduced in [[2014]] in the series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', as the remains of the gunship decorated the front of Old Jho's Pit Shop.<ref name="ED Trivia Gallery" /> In [[2018]], ten years after the ship appeared on screen for the first time, attentive viewers would be rewarded with a second easter egg: the logo art could be spotted on the wall of Aunt Z's tavern in the series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.<ref name="High Tower BL" /> The gunship's nose art and name resemble [[Salacious B. Crumb]], [[jester]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].
