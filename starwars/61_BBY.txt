==Births==
*"[[The Client]]" {{C|between 70 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 61 BBY}}<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Chapter 1: The Mandalorian]]," "[[Chapter 3: The Sin]]," and "[[Chapter 7: The Reckoning]]," including [[the Client]]'s [[death]] on [[Nevarro]], took place in [[9 ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:MandoDate|here]]. In the [[The Mandalorian Junior Novel|junior novelization]] covering the episodes' events, the Client is described as being in his seventies, meaning that he was between seventy and seventy-nine in 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]. He must have therefore been born between 70 BBY and 61 BBY.</ref>
*[[Gial Ackbar]] {{C|between 61 BBY and [[52 BBY]]}}<ref name="Bloodline date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place in [[28 ABY]]. As ''Bloodline'' states [[Gial Ackbar]] was in his eighties, it can be concluded that Ackbar was [[Reproduction|born]] between 61 BBY and [[52 BBY]].</ref> on [[Mon Cala]]<ref name="JM6">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 6|''Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine'' 6]]</ref>
*[[Cohbo'rik'ardok]]<ref name="birth">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' establishes that [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] was born in [[59 BBY]]. ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' establishes that [[Cohbo'rik'ardok]] was two [[Standard year|years]] older than Thrawn, placing her year of birth in 61 BBY.</ref> on [[Rentor]]<ref name="Chaos Rising">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]''</ref>
