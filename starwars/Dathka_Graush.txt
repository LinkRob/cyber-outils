'''Dathka Graush''' was a [[Sith alchemist]] that lived long before the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Scrolls belong to Graush and other [[Sith]], including [[Naga Sadow]] and [[Darth Betrius]] had been collected and stored at [[Fortress Vader]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], a conflict between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Terror" /> 

At some point between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Terror Date">{{TerrorOnMustafarDate}}</ref> [[Vaneé]], an [[aide]] who served [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] at Fortress Vader, used the works of Dathka Graush and the other Sith to build upon a procedure developed by Imperial [[scientist]] [[Harper]]. Vaneé's version involved implanting parts of a [[droid brain]] into an organic being forcing them become an obedient [[spy]] for the Empire.<ref name="Terror" />
