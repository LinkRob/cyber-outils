'''Burbank''' was a destination on the [[Coruscant train]] and [[subtram]]. It appeared on a list of stops on a data screen at the [[train station]] where [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Tera Sinube]] tracked [[Ione Marcy]] and [[Cassilyda Cryar|Cassie Cryar]]. Also listed was [[Arabia]], [[Ulendale]] and [[Alderaan]].<ref name="LL">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> For reasons unknown, the same data screens were found in [[Tipoca City]] on [[Kamino]].<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref> The name also appeared on subtram maps on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="TriviaGallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/to-catch-a-jedi-trivia-gallery|text="To Catch a Jedi" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Subtram_map.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Location of Burbank on a subtram map.]][[Wikipedia:Burbank|Burbank]] is a city in [[Wikipedia:California|California]] that is strongly associated with the entertainment industry. Nicknamed the "Media Capital of the World", many entertainment companies, including the [[The Walt Disney Company|Walt Disney Company]], have their headquarters or significant production facilities in Burbank.<ref name="Burbank">{{WebCite|url=http://www.usacitydirectories.com/travelamerica/index.php?entry=entry070420-130359|text=Burbank, Ca.- Media Capital of the World.|work=[http://www.usacitydirectories.com/ USACityDirectories.com]|archivedate=20150910002324|nolive=1}}</ref>

The name was inserted onto a [[subtram]] map as an in-joke by the production team of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', along with the other destinations.<ref name="TriviaGallery" />
