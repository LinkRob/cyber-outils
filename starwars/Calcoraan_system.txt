The '''Calcoraan system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> which contained the desolate [[planet]] [[Calcoraan]]. During the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s reign, the [[space station]] [[Calcoraan Depot]] was also located in the system and was owned by the industrialist [[Denetrius Vidian]]. When Vidian was sent to the [[Gorse system]] to increase efficiency of [[thorilide]] [[mining]] in the system he sent a convoy of [[starship]]s to Calcoraan system to collect explosives from Calcoraan Depot so that he could destroy the [[moon]] [[Cynda]].<ref name="AND" />
