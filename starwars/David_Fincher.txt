'''David Fincher''' (born [[August 28]], [[1960s|1962]]) is an US-American film director, writer and producer. He is considered to be one of the most preeminent directors of his generation and highly successful. Around 1981, when he was 19,<ref name="Independent 2009">{{WebCite|url=https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/films/features/david-fincher-awards-are-just-icing-on-the-cake-1520062.html|text=David Fincher: "Awards are just icing on the cake"|author=James Mottram|date=2009-02-01|work=''[[Wikipedia:The Independent|The Independent]]''|archivedate=20250808174146}}</ref> instead of going to film school like most of his contemporaries,<ref name="Guardian 2009">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2009/feb/03/david-fincher-interview-transcript|text=David Fincher|author=Mark Salisbury|date=2009-01-19|work=''[[Wikipedia:The Guardian|The Guardian]]''|archivedate=20250930232316}}</ref> Fincher instead worked at [[George Lucas]]' company [[Industrial Light & Magic]]. At ILM, Fincher worked<ref name="Independent 2009" /> as an assistant cameraman<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> for the last film in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]], [[1983]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi]]'', including on the scenes with the [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]]s during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Guardian 2009" /> Fincher also worked as a matte photographer for the [[1984]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] film ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''.<ref name="Caravan">''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]''</ref>

In [[2012]],<ref name="Playlist 2012">{{WebCite|url=https://theplaylist.net/david-fincher-latest-name-in-the-mix-to-direct-star-wars-episode-7-jon-favreau-reportedly-chasing-the-job-20121130/|text=David Fincher Latest Name In The Mix To Direct ‘Star Wars: Episode 7’; Jon Favreau Reportedly Chasing The Job|date=2012-11-30|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/lJE6D}}</ref> David Fincher was one of the many directors considered by [[Lucasfilm]] president [[Kathleen Kennedy]] for [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|the first film]] of the [[sequel trilogy]], but didn't take the job.<ref name="Empire 2017">{{WebCite|url=https://theplaylist.net/david-fincher-star-wars-20171019/|text=David Fincher Discusses The High Expectations Of Directing A ‘Star Wars’ Movie|date=2017-10-19|author=Kevin Jagernauth|archivedate=20240510031708}}</ref> At some point, Fincher also pitched Kathleen Kennedy [[Untitled Star Wars film (David Fincher)|a story]] set between ''[[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|Episode VIII The Last Jedi]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker]]'', but the idea didn't progress past a few phone calls.<ref name="Playlist 2025">{{WebCite|url=https://theplaylist.net/the-hunt-for-ben-solo-steven-soderbergh-adam-drivers-secret-star-wars-movie-was-greenlit-before-disney-pulled-the-plug-exclusive-20251024/|author=Rodrigo Perez|date=2025-10-24|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/yGcUG|text=‘The Hunt For Ben Solo’: Steven Soderbergh & Adam Driver’s Secret ‘Star Wars’ Movie Was Greenlit Before Disney Pulled The Plug [Exclusive]}}</ref>
