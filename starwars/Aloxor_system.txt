The '''Aloxor system''' was a [[star system]] located<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> in the [[Kontahr sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Aloxor]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
While ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'' originally placed the Aloxor system in the [[Esaga sector]] as it was in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]],<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> the [[2025]] appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" moved it and the [[Cygnus system]] to the Kontahr sector.<ref name="Appendix" /> The appendix's author [[Jason Fry]] later confirmed that the move was intentional, as both the Aloxor system and the [[Cygnus system]] were moved as part of the Esaga sector being split into two to accommodate the existence of the canon-only Kontahr sector.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|author=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref>
