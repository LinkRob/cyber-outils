'''Force lightning''', also known as '''Sith lightning''' or '''dark lightning''', was an offensive [[Force power|power]] that [[Force-sensitive|Force users]] could conjure through the [[dark side of the Force]].

==Description==
===Manifestation of death===
{{Quote|The ultimate physical manifestation of the Force is also one of its most malicious forms and is most commonly generated by those heavily versed in the ways of the dark side, such as Sith Lords. This aggressive energy attack is usually released from the fingertips in the direction of the intended target, leading to severe injury, disfigurement, and death.|Luke Skywalker|<ref name="SotJ">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
[[File:ShockerOnSerenno-Nightsisters.png|thumb|left|300px|Force lightning was a highly powerful dark side ability that could kill, maim, or just generally hurt those attacked by it.]]
Force lightning was a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]<ref name="DB" /> combinative technique which involved a discharge of electrical energy (as ''''lightning'''') from the practitioner's hands<ref name="Episode VI" /> and was capable of [[Death|killing]] or [[Torture|inflicting severe pain]] on the target, as well as possible disfigurement<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> or even disintegration. It could also be used to destroy physical objects, such as [[starship]]s. Moreover, sometimes the user's hand would emanate faint amounts of smoke just after employing this power.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> Sometimes this power was used to parry another's usage of the Force.<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Four" /> Using this power to stun an opponent was possible, as was using the lightning to move a target.<ref name="Episode II" />

[[Luke Skywalker]] considered it to be the ultimate manifestation of the Force in a physical sense.<ref name="SotJ" /> The [[Baran Do Sages]] understood a means of using Force lightning without drawing upon the dark side.<ref name="DoH" /> In some cases, the intensity of Force lightning was so high that the victim's skeletal system glowed and could be seen through their skin&mdash;as demonstrated with Windu<ref name="Episode III" />&mdash;or even through [[Darth Vader's armor|heavy armor]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> During the [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|mission to Pasaana]], while battling [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], the Jedi [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] released a burst of Force lightning so powerful it was able to obliterate a [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|First Order troop transport ship]]. Shortly after, during the [[Battle of Exegol]], Darth Sidious, having absorbed the power of a dyad, unleashed a tempest of Force lightning of such power and precision that it completely disabled an [[Citizens' Fleet|entire fleet]] of 14,000 ships, while leaving his own fleet of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] untouched.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Variations===
[[File:Talzin-vs-Sidious.png|thumb|right|300px|Mother Talzin uses Nightsister magick to bring forth her version of Force lightning against Darth Sidious's Sith Force lightning.]]
A variant of the ability, known as chain lightning, involved a burst of lightning that would spread from the first target to those around them. Sometimes this power was unleashed in a wave of stunning sparks. Another variant used by Sidious was the power [[Dark Aura]], allowing the user to charge the air around themselves.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

Force lightning occurred in at least five [[color]]s; blue,<ref name="Episode VI" /> green,<ref name="Massacre" /> yellow,<ref name="Dark Lord 22">[[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]]</ref> white,<ref name="Unlimited Power" /> and red.<ref name="Darth Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref> The typical form was a bluish-purple or bluish-white version as demonstrated by [[Darth Sidious]],<ref name="Episode III" /> [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]],<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[Snoke]],<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> [[Yoda]],<ref name="Unlimited Power" /> and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]];<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[magick]] equivalents were green like [[magical ichor]] as demonstrated by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]].<ref name="Massacre" /> [[Darth Momin]]'s lightning glowed yellow<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> and red lightning was used by the [[Son]]&mdash;a representation of the dark side<ref name="Altar of Mortis" />&mdash;and the disembodied spirit of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] when he entered a [[portal]] in his [[Fortress Vader|castle]] on [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Dark Lord 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref>

===Counteractions and effects===
{{Quote|As you can see, my Jedi powers are far beyond yours. Now, back down.''"<br />"''I don't think so.|Count Dooku and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:YodaFingerSpark-UP.png|thumb|left|200px|Yoda bending lightning with the Force on Dagobah]]
[[Jedi]] and [[Sith]] could deflect the lightning with a [[lightsaber]], such as when Obi-Wan Kenobi blocked Dooku's lightning with his saber; a powerful [[Jedi Master]] such as [[Yoda]] was capable of deflecting the attack using [[Force deflection]]<ref name="Episode II" /> but only for a short while and with immense effort.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[Gungan personal energy shield]]s were also capable of repelling Force lightning,<ref name="Weapons Poll">{{SW|url=news/poll-which-star-wars-weapon-would-you-use|text=Poll: Which ''Star Wars'' Weapon Would You Use?}}</ref> as were [[Squad shield]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[Mace Windu]]<ref name="Episode III" /> and Rey were both able to deflect Sidious' lightning back onto him using lightsabers. In Rey's case, the force of his own reflected lightning was what ultimately destroyed Sidious.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Anakin and Obi-Wan were able to use their lightsabers to absorb Dooku's Force Lightning.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref><ref name="Star Wars: The Clone Wars film">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref> [[Quinlan Vos]] was able to deflect the Force lightning of Tyranus back onto him with his lightsaber.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Despite the power of a saber as a weapon of protection, a bolt of lightning shot fast enough might be able to disarm a lightsaber wielder.<ref name="Episode III" />

Quinlan Vos described the feeling of being struck by Dooku's lightning as both burning and freezing his limbs. Those who were harmed with Force lightning often writhed in pain with uncontrollable muscle spasms. After [[Asajj Ventress]] was struck with Dooku's lightning, her skin became tattooed with dark lines where the lightning bolts had hit her.<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> One [[Unidentified dark sculpture|dark sculpture]] owned by Sidious had the ability to ground Force lightning, which was useful for Sidious since it prevented damage to his public office whenever he lashed out in rage.<ref name="Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

==Usage==
{{Quote|Now you will experience the full power of the dark side.|Darth Sidious, to Yoda|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
Aside from the obvious uses to stun or kill an enemy, this power was used for other tasks. Momin planned to pour the Force into his engine via his version of Force lightning, at the moment of ignition to use it to slow down time, trapping the expressions of horror and pain on the city's inhabitants forever.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" /> Count Dooku used this power to [[Torture|torment]] [[Savage Opress]], in the hope of teaching him to embrace his hate more fully and so deepen his connection to the Force.<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> While traveling through an illusion-filled plain of reality, the soul of [[Darth]] Vader was able to make use of a red Force lightning; he blasted an illusion of his [[Sith Master]], Darth Sidious, with red Force lightning before confronting the visage of his late [[Marriage|wife]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Darth Vader 25">[[Darth Vader (2017) 25|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 25]]</ref>

[[File:Snoke_Lightning.png|thumb|right|250px|Snoke using a short burst of Force lightning to subdue Kylo Ren.]]
Other dark side wielders besides the Sith were able to harness the ability. Mother Talzin conjured her own version of Force lightning that was powerful enough to block Sidious' lightning.<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Four">[[Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir 4|''Darth Maul — Son of Dathomir'' 4]]</ref> The Son used red lightning to attack his sister.<ref name="Altar of Mortis3">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref> [[Supreme Leader]] Snoke used it to subdue his apprentice, [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], when the young [[Knights of Ren|knight]] lost control of his anger. Snoke's use of Force lightning varied from previous practitioners, as he projected energy against the [[Supremacy|ship's]] floor, causing it to bounce upward and knock the target off his feet.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> [[Bendu]] created and joined with a massive storm, raining lightning upon the Imperial forces as well as those of the Rebellion. Due to Bendu's own claims about being of both the light side and dark side, it is unclear which side of the Force he used for this battle.<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref> [[Ben Solo]],<ref name=":0">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref> similarly, after a confrontation with his uncle, called down a bolt of lightning upon Luke Skywalker's Jedi temple, annihilating the structure, and those within.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 1">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 1|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 1]]</ref> During his fall to the dark side, Solo also conjured lightning around himself as he fought with Ren.<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 4">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 4|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 4]]</ref> [[Yoda]], after his death, [[Force spirit|appeared]] to Luke Skywalker and caused a lightning strike to [[Fire|burn]] a [[tree]] as a demonstration to Skywalker.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

[[File:Sidious_Force_storm.png|thumb|left|200px|With his powers amplified by a Force dyad, Sidious unleashed a storm of Force lightning upon his enemies above Exegol.]]
Although its use was officially forbidden by the [[Jedi Order]] for most of its history,<ref name="Expert Guide">''[[Star Wars Expert Guide]]''</ref> light-siders like the Jedi could also use this power and indeed some ancient Jedi used it against the Sith.<ref name=":0" /> Dooku, during his time as a member of the Jedi Order, utilized it spontaneously during a painful Force vision to kill some [[Presagers of Hakotei]], without any known prior training<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> and also used it on [[Shenda Mol]] to save his [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] while on [[Numidian Prime]], although Qui-Gon was unaware of it.<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> Plo Koon sometimes would emit a blast of blue sparks against his enemies.<ref name="Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> Yoda himself bended lightning while in exile on [[Dagobah]].<ref name="Unlimited Power">''[[Unlimited Power]]''</ref> Yoda was also able harvest the power of absorbed lightning to temporarily increase his telekinetic powers.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Force lightning was one of the dark side powers that Luke Skywalker admitted might be correct to use if the need of a Jedi was dire, though he still felt that under most circumstances using the dark side carried a cost beyond whatever benefits such power might grant.<ref name="SotJ" />

In a skirmish with the First Order during a [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|mission to Pasaana]], the Jedi Rey was trying to bring down a [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|First Order transport]] she believed was carrying [[Chewbacca]]. She and Kylo Ren were both trying to bring the transport to themselves with the Force, and in her anger, she accidentally unleashed lightning upon it, destroying the ship immediately.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Conception and development===
[[File:Adventures_of_Luke_-_Force_Lightning.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Darth Sidious blasting Luke Skywalker with lightning in [[Ralph McQuarrie]] artwork seen in ''[[The Adventures of Luke Skywalker, Jedi Knight]]'']]
Force lightning first appeared the [[canon]] continuity in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]], conversely, the [[1978]] novel ''[[Splinter of the Mind's Eye]]'' was the first work this power is introduced in.<ref name="Splinter">''[[Splinter of the Mind's Eye]]''</ref>

As sound designer [[Ben Burtt]] wanted sounds for sparks and artificial lightning akin to those in [[Wikipedia:Victor Frankenstein|Frankenstein]] films during the production of the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], Burtt tracked down [[Wikipedia:Kenneth Strickfaden|Ken Strickfaden]], the person who built and kept the props of [[Wikipedia:Frankenstein (1931 film)|''Frankenstein'']], and asked if he could record sounds from the still-functional ''Frankenstein'' props. Strickfaden initially denied the request, but, during the production of the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], was stunned after watching the 1977 ''[[Star Wars]]'' film and drove Burtt to his house in [[Wikipedia:Santa Monica, California|Santa Monica]], switching on all his props and allowing Burtt to record the sounds. Much of the Emperor's Force lightning attack on Luke Skywalker in ''Return of the Jedi'' made use of sounds recorded from Strickfaden's ''Frankenstein'' props, with the sound effects being used in subsequent ''Star Wars'' media.<ref name="Frankenstein">{{SWYouTube|gEBBuCSoC_E|Conversations: Sounds In Space - Frankenstein Sounds|sw_url=video/frankenstein-sounds-conversations-sounds-in-space}}</ref>

[[George Lucas]] originally conceived of Force lightning as being just another Force power and wanted Luke Skywalker and Darth Vader to use it against each other in ''Empire Strikes Back'', with Luke seeming to turn close to the dark side to increase his power at Vader's urging, but ultimately being defeated as in the final film, though ultimately this idea of Luke using lightning so early in his life was not implemented in ''The Empire Strikes Back''.<ref name="The Making of The Empire Strikes Back">''[[The Making of The Empire Strikes Back]]''</ref><ref name="Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back">{{Film|V}}</ref> The ''Return of the Jedi'' draft script saw Obi-Wan Kenobi and Yoda appear amid the Emperor's use of Force lightning against Luke: the two Force ghosts appeared in defense of the young Jedi and deflected the Sith's attack.<ref name="Rewind of the Jedi">{{InsiderCite|218|Rewind of the Jedi}}</ref>

===Disfigurement===
There has been a fan debate about the consequences of Force lightning in an individual.<ref name="Matt's Explanation">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1034554469596319746|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=This is not an official answer but I just always assumed there was more going on with Palps and it wasn't just a result of the lightning itself. Maybe he overexerted himself and that caused it? Maybe he had the voltage turned up higher? IDK, but I never saw it as an inconsistency|archiveurl=https://archive.is/790hW}}</ref> In ''Revenge of the Sith'', after having been bested by Mace Windu, Darth Sidious uses Force lightning against the [[Jedi Master]], who repels it with his lightsaber, deflecting it onto Sidious and disfiguring and scarring the [[Sith Lord]]'s body.<ref name="Episode III" /> However, previous to the Force lightning's appearance in ''Sith'', Sidious blasted [[Luke Skywalker]] with Force lightning in ''Return of the Jedi'' several times and Skywalker was not disfigured or scarred.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Likewise, Dooku used this power on Anakin Skywalker without permanently damaging him.<ref name="Episode II" />

Though an official explanation of this inconsistency has yet to be given, [[Lucasfilm Story Group]]'s [[Matt Martin]] has shared his thinking for what the explanation could be, as he never saw it as an inconsistency. In his mind, it was Sidious' own fault that his disfigurement took place, assuming that he overexerted himself, causing the Force lightning's voltage to be higher, unlike when he later attacked Skywalker.<ref name="Matt's Explanation" /> In Legends, it was suggested that Palpatine used a Sith power known as "[[Mask (Sith Alchemy)|Mask]]" to hide his true face, suggesting the lightning did not cause his disfigurement. Instead, the lightning only broke the [[Sith alchemy/Legends|alchemical disguise]] and revealed the Sith Lord's true appearance. Any further damage was because of the mask suddenly dropping from a blast of dark side energy.<ref name="Jedi Counseling 71">[[Jedi Counseling]] 71, [[Wizards of the Coast]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]] website [https://web.archive.org/web/20090602013752/http://www.wizards.com/default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20050804jc71 (link)]''<nowiki/>''</ref><ref name="Star Wars Insider 83">[[Star Wars Insider 83|''Star Wars Insider'' 83]]</ref>
