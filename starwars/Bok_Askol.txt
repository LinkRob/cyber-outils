'''Bok Askol''' was a [[Pacithhip]] [[Gender|female]] from [[Shimia]] who worked as a [[podracer]] [[engineer]]. In [[32 BBY]], Askol was present in [[Mos Espa]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] to attend the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]]. The Pacithhip spent [[time]] in the [[hangar]] of the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]] before watching the race from the [[Watto's box|private box]] of the [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]].

==Biography==
Bok Askol was a [[Pacithhip]] [[Gender|female]] [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Shimia]]<ref name="Datapad" /> around [[56 BBY]].<ref name="GEDates" /> Askol worked as a [[podracer]] [[engineer]] and had followed the dream of becoming an expert [[Technician|mechanic]] in the hopes of joining a [[podracing]] crew.<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Askol was in the [[spaceport]]<ref name="TPM" /> of [[Mos Espa]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|mos-espa|Mos Espa}}</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] to attend the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] podrace<ref name="TPM" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}} to [[32 BBY]].</ref>

Prior to the race, the Pacithhip spent [[time]] in the podracer [[hangar]]<ref name="TPM" /> of the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]],<ref name="Databank" /> where Askol spoke with a [[Quarren]] next to<ref name="TPM" /> the [[9T9-B Wasp|podracer]] of the [[Nosaurian]] [[Clegg Holdfast]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Askol then viewed the race itself from the [[Watto's box|private box]] of the [[Toydarian]] [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]] in the arena.<ref name="TPM" />

During the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Datapad" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDates" /> Askol was 90 years old. At this time, the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] sent several [[Thief|stolen]] identity profiles, including a profile of Askol, to the [[datapad]]s of several individuals in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu. The individuals were preparing to carry out a [[Heist on a First Order train|heist]] for [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]], and Ohnaka wished to test if they could remember information concerning a potential cover identity, quizzing them on details from each of the profiles.<ref name="Datapad" />

==Personality and traits==
Askol had [[Color|gray]] skin, blue [[eye]]s,<ref name="TPM" /> and no [[hair]]. The Pacithhip stood 6 [[Foot|feet]], 10 inches tall and weighed 269 [[pound]]s.<ref name="Datapad" />

==Equipment==
The Pacithhip wore a set of green [[Clothing|overalls]] during their time on Tatooine.<ref name="TPM" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bok Askol first appeared, albeit unnamed, in the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> The character was first named in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE" /> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used on a card in the [[2001]] ''[[Theed Palace Limited]]'' expansion set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]].<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Theed Palace Limited|cardname=Bok Askol}}</ref>
