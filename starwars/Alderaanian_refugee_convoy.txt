Following [[Destruction of Alderaan|the Disaster]], a '''convoy''' of [[Alderaanian]] survivors aboard three [[GR-75 medium transport]]s traveled to [[Fostar Haven]] in the hopes of evading the [[Galactic Empire]], which was under orders from [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to destroy all remainders of [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Squadrons" />

The convoy was discovered, and [[Commodore]] [[Rae Sloane]] sent [[Captain]] [[Lindon Javes]] of [[Helix Squadron]] alongside a blockade to Fostar Haven to eliminate the refugees. Javes, however, refused to kill innocent lives when he learnt about the Disaster and resolved to aid their escape.<ref name="Squadrons" /> During the mission, Captain [[Canonhaus]] was ordered to guard the outside perimeter to prevent the refugees from escaping.<ref name="Final Order">{{StoryCite|story=The Final Order|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

The convoy was evacuated by Javes and [[Echo Squadron]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Squadrons" />
