'''Fryoto''' was a [[Zabrak]] [[Gender|female]] who was [[Prison|imprisoned]] on [[Burnin Konn]] by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[purge trooper]]s. The Empire had suspected that she and [[Zepasi]] were affiliated with the [[Trade Spine League]] and knew of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[death]]. She and Zepasi were both rescued by a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] in an [[uprising]] against [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Iron Blockade]].<ref name="Uprising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fryoto is a non-player character created for ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', a [[canon]] mobile game developed by [[Kabam]] for iOS and [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]] operating systems. The player must liberate her and Zepasi from Imperial captivity in order to complete Story Mission 65, entitled "Even Families".<ref name="Uprising" />
