'''Docking Bay Nine''' was a [[Hangar|docking bay]] located in<ref name="Han Chewie 8" /> the [[City|town]]<ref name="Jabba the Hutt">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt'' 1]]</ref> [[Mos Entha]] on<ref name="Han Chewie 8" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Han Chewie 8" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> after he won<ref name="Han Chewie 8" /> the [[YT-1300 light freighter]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' in a game of [[sabacc]], the [[Rodian]]<ref name="Han Chewie 8" /> [[bounty hunter]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Greedo]] traveled to Tatooine, where he left the ''Falcon'' in Docking Bay Nine before heading to<ref name="Han Chewie 8" /> the city<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Mos Eisley]]. Sometime afterward, the criminal [[Phaedra]], and the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]], who owned the ''Millennium Falcon'', made their way to Tatooine to recover the [[YT-series|ship]]. After confronting Greedo, the Rodian told the three in [[Huttese]] that the ''Falcon'' was in Mos Entha. They then retrieved it and departed Tatooine.<ref name="Han Chewie 8" />
