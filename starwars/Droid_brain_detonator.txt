'''Droid brain detonators''' were some of the most sophisticated and uncommon [[detonator]]s in [[the galaxy]]. While common detonators were effective within narrow parameters, droid brain detonators could adapt to nearly any situation by using a [[droid brain]]. They abided to very specific trigger conditions and could follow complex instructions. A user could instruct their [[detonite charge]] to wait thirty [[Standard minute|minutes]] after multiple specific individuals have entered a facility before igniting itself, or tell their [[fragmentation grenade]] to explode after [[death]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

Droid brain detonators were fiercely loyal. They tended to develop unique personality quirks over their short existences. Some were annoyingly chipper or enthusiastic, and some could even exhibit [[Youngling|childlike]] naivete. Droid brain detonators cost 1,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the market and could be attached to [[grenade]]s and other explosive devices. Notable droid brain detonator models include [[CryonCorp]]'s [[D9 Demolitions Droid]] and [[SoroSuub Corporation|SoroSuub's]] [[XS-3 Intelligent Detonation System]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />
