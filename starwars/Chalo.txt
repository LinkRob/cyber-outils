'''Chalo''' was a [[human]] [[gambler]] with light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and brown [[hair]] that resided on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During which they played [[Sabacc]] at a [[Unidentified cantina (Sashin)|cantina]] in the [[city|town]] of [[Sashin]] on Akiva. While playing at the [[cantina]] Chalo wore a green [[clothing|vest]] over a black shirt, green pants and green [[glove]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] played a game of [[Kessel Sabacc]] against Chalo in which she won fifty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chalo appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They were part of several sabacc tables that players could choose to play at to win credits or valuables.<ref name="Outlaws" />
