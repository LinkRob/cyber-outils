The '''Air Taxi Racer''' was a costume owned by [[Dizzy (Ugnaught)|Dizzy]], the [[Ugnaught]] member of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] team [[Slingshot (team)|Slingshot]], that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Wild Racer|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|yellow]], greenish-gray, black, and brown color scheme, with a brown [[belt]] and silver belt buckle, yellow, silver, and brown [[goggles]], and [[Armor|shoulder pads]] that featured a black and white checkered pattern with red edges.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Air Taxi Racer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Slingshot, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 4.0.0 alongside Season 4 on [[November 25]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Air Taxi Paintwork]].<ref name="Hunters" />
