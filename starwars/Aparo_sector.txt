The '''Aparo sector''' was a [[sector]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that the [[Hydian Way]] passed through before ending in the neighboring [[Corporate Sector]], which also bordered the [[Wyl sector]] and [[Wild Space]].<ref name="BMF18" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aparo sector was mentioned in the [[2015]] magazine [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 18|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon'' 18]].<ref name="BMF18" /> The [[Wyl sector/Legends|sector]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]'', a supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] and published by [[West End Games]] in [[1993]].<ref name="HS&CS">''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]''</ref>
