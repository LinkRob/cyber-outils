'''C'ai Threnalli''' was an [[Abednedo]] [[Gender|male]] pilot who flew in the [[Resistance Starfighter Corps]] of the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[First Order]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Poe, the command ship!|C'ai to Poe Dameron, after noticing that the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] had gone down|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
Having previously flown for the [[New Republic]], Threnalli was one of several Abednedo who joined Leia's Resistance movement, as his people had been early supporters of the General. He was known to be an X-wing pilot by choice, but also received cross-training in other types such as the B-wing and Y-wing models.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Following the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Threnalli was amongst the personnel in the [[Resistance base]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]] who waved off the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', piloted by [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Chewbacca|her]] [[R2-D2|passengers]], as it set off to find the missing [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]] on the planet [[Ahch-To]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

Shortly after the Resistance [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuated]] the base on D'Qar, Threnalli flew as [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]]'s wingman, under the call sign [[Red Three]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /><ref name="TLJ Junior Novel" /> He remained loyal to Dameron, joining the mutiny against [[Vice Admiral]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] along with at [[Pammich Nerro Goode|least]] [[Nodin Chavdri|several]] [[Kaydel Ko Connix|others]]. However, he was stunned, along with Dameron's other allies, by Holdo after she caused a distraction.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:CaiThrenalli-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Threnalli flew his T-70 X-wing during the First Order-Resistance War]]
Threnalli was aboard the Resistance [[U-55 orbital loadlifter|transports]] that escaped the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] and later participated in the [[Battle of Crait]], managing to survive with only a small number of other pilots. He escaped aboard the ''Millennium Falcon'' along with the few Resistance survivors, and hugged Dameron.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] to one [[Standard year|year]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must occur in [[35 ABY]].</ref> Threnalli flew his T-70 in the [[Battle of Exegol]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]]. After the battle, he returned to Ajan Kloss and celebrated with the rest of the Resistance.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:CaiThrenalli-half-SWCEUAEE.png|thumb|right|200px|Threnalli in his flightsuit and flight helmet]]
At 1.88 [[meter]]s tall,<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> C'ai Threnalli was an [[Abednedo]] male<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> who had [[Color|bluish-brown]] skin,<ref name="TLJVD" /> brown [[hair]], black [[eye]]s,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> and dangling mouth tentacles,<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> which were heat and motion-sensitive.<ref name="TROS VD" /> His feet had three toes. He was considered a hotshot pilot, and was as comfortable flying an X-wing starfighter as much as a regular airspeeder, as he could turn his hand to any controls.<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> One maneuver Threnalli was particularly skilled in was the [[backwards tailslide]].<ref name="FightToFinish">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/9/23/fight-to-the-finish|text=Fight to the Finish}}</ref> He was loyal to fellow Resistance pilot Poe Dameron.<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> He learned to speak [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]], but often lapsed back into [[Abednedish|Abednedo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
C'ai Threnalli wore the standard Resistance pilot [[Flight suit|flightsuit]], with flight vest, [[Guidenhauser]] ejection harness, [[boot]]s, and [[life support]] equipment.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> His specially-shaped white [[flight helmet]]<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> sported a yellow [[Rebel Alliance starbird|Resistance symbol]], with blue and yellow stripes. The phrase "AUBREY" was written on the side in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="SWCT Tools & Props">{{SWCT|set=Star Wars: The Last Jedi - Tools & Props|cardname=C'ai Threnalli's Helmet}}</ref> He also carried a [[Glie-44 blaster pistol]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

==Behind the scenes==
C'ai Threnalli first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="Episode VII" /> where actor [[Paul Kasey]] is seen wearing the costume of [[Ello Asty]] in one of the final scenes of the movie.<ref name="Creatures">{{Film|VII}} &mdash; "Crafting Creatures" BluRay featurette</ref> As Asty's death had been shown earlier in the film,<ref name="Episode VII" /> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] stated that the Abednedo seen waving off the ''Falcon'' in that scene must be a different character.<ref name="Fellow pilot">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/703128144656478208|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=That's another Abednedo (well, it's the same costume, but we did see Ello die, so therefore, it's another one).|date=2016-02-28|archivefile=File:Other costume tweet.png|nolive=1}}</ref>

The design for Threnalli was created by creature concept designer [[Jake Lunt Davies]], while his costume was made by costume designner [[Michael Kaplan]].<ref name="TLJ SDCC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-last-jedi-props-and-costumes-on-display-at-sdcc|text=''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Props and Costumes on Display at SDCC}}</ref> Threnalli was performed by Paul Kasey, who was assisted by 'Animatronic Facial Performer' Phill Woodfine.

This character went unnamed until appearing in the "Escape from Starkiller Base" DLC expansion for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', which starred Threnalli, voiced by [[Christian Simpson]], crashing in a [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] during the Assault on Starkiller and then fighting through [[First Order]] forces and escaping the planet's surface alongside fellow Resistance members [[Lieutenant]] [[Wright]] and [[R3-Z3]].<ref name="Lego">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref> Story Group member [[Leland Chee]] confirmed on Twitter that the idea was for Threnalli to be the unnamed pilot from the film.<ref name="C'ai Threnalli Twitter">{{Twitter|holocronkeeper|status/856883334948847616|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2017-04-25|quote=That's the idea, yes. Could have been Ello Asty but then Ello died.|archivedate=20190904233341|nolive=1}}</ref>
