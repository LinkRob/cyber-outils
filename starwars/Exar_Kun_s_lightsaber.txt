A '''double-bladed lightsaber''' was the weapon of choice of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Exar Kun]] [[Millennium|thousands]] of [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[time]] that [[Darth]] [[Maul]] would construct his [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|own]] [[double-bladed lightsaber]].<ref name="Altaya" /> According to the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], double-bladed [[lightsaber]]s dated back to Exar Kun's own use of the weapon.<ref name="GaG">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Exar Kun's lightsaber was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="GaG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Exar Kun's lightsaber/Legends|Exar Kun's lightsaber]] first appeared as a [[Double-bladed lightsaber/Legends|double-bladed lightsaber]] in the [[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 3|third issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series, written by [[Kevin J. Anderson]], illustrated by [[Dario Carrasco, Jr.]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="TSW3">[[Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War 3|''Tales of the Jedi – The Sith War'' 3]]</ref> on [[October 17]], [[1995]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54597/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_-_the_sith_war_1995_3|text=Star Wars: Tales Of The Jedi - The Sith War (1995) #3}} {{C|First printing}}</ref>
