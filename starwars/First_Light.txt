{{Quote|The ''First Light'' is my home. I needed a place that I could take with me, a place I could keep secure. That means I have given up many things, like sunlight and fresh air. I know you must miss them, too, cooped up here, but I also know that you are aware of the honor you have been accorded.|Dryden Vos, to mercenaries aboard the ''First Light''|<ref name="Crimson Climb">''[[Crimson Climb]]''</ref>}}
'''''First Light''''' was a [[Nau'ur-class yacht|''Nau'ur''-class yacht]]<ref name="S&S" /> owned by the [[near-human]] [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]] of [[Crimson Dawn]] who used it as a personal residence and mobile base of operations for the crime syndicate.<ref name="Official Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> It fell into the possession of [[Qi'ra]] after Vos's [[death]].<ref name="POV 213">{{InsiderCite|213|A Certain Point of View}}</ref>

The [[yacht]] had a crew complement consisting of both organics and [[droid]]s and had its own onboard security force, in the form of the [[Hylobon Enforcers]].<ref name="Solo" />

==Design==
[[File:Qira_Women_of_the_Galaxy.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Qi'ra aboard the ''First Light'']]
Following in a similar vein to other [[Starship|spaceships]] made by [[Kalevala Spaceworks]] like the ''[[Coronet]]'',<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> the ''First Light'' was of a distinctive vertical design, being a very narrow, tall spaceship with the interior divided up into several floors and decks located on top of one another, with [[Dryden Vos]]' [[Dryden's study|office]] and personal quarters located at the very top. All floors were connected and accessible via an [[Turbolift|elevator]] running through the height of the ship.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
The interior of ''First Light'' was inspired by the Modernist interior of the [[Wikipedia:Marin County Civic Center|Marin County Civic Center]], designed by the architect [[Wikipedia:Frank Lloyd Wright|Frank Lloyd Wright]] and located at 20 minutes from [[Skywalker Ranch]] and where ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]] filmed in part the [[1971]] cult film [[1138 (easter egg)|''THX-1138'']], his directorial debut. A preliminary version of the yacht was inspired by the high collar of [[Wikipedia:Bela Lugosi|Bela Lugosi's]] vampire costume of the [[1930s|1931]] film [[Wikipedia:Dracula (1931 English-language film)|''Dracula'']].<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> In earlier versions of the script, however, Dryden Vos' operations base was an island fortress with a canal system like [[Wikipedia:Mont-Saint-Michel|Mont-Saint-Michel]], rather than a ship.<ref name="Jon's Twitter">{{Twitter|JonKasdan|status/1040529501870944256|[[Jon Kasdan]]|quote=In honor of the digital release of #SoloAStarWarsStory & in lieu of a commentary, here are 53 random factoids & notes about the making & writing for anybody who's interested. If you haven't seen the movie yet... maybe watch it before reading? Anyway, enjoy the movie & #MTFBWY|archivefile=File:Jon-Kasdan-SoloFacts-September-14-2018.png}}</ref>

Dyden Vos arrives on [[Janix]] in a newly designed Crimson Dawn shuttle rather than his yacht, the ''First Light'' seen in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''. This smaller shuttle allows Dryden to get to the [[Janix]] jungle rendezvous point much easier than the larger, more conspicuous ''First Light''.<ref name="MakingShadowLord">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-maul-shadow-lord-trivia|text=Inside the Making of ''Star Wars: Maul – Shadow Lord''}}</ref>
