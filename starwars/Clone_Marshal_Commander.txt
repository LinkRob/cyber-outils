A '''Clone Marshal Commander''',<ref name="HC 25">{{HelmetCollectionCite|25|Helmets}}</ref> or simply known as a '''Marshal Commander'''<ref name="Bacara FC">{{ForceCollection|Bacara|star=4}}</ref> or '''Clone Marshal''',<ref name="BC 31">{{BustCollectionCite|31|Character}}</ref> was a high-[[Military rank|ranking]] [[clone trooper]] [[commander]], usually in command of a [[corps]]-level formation alongside a [[Jedi General]] counterpart, in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] was assigned to be the clone marshal commander of the [[7th Sky Corps]] almost immediately after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="HC 25" /> Clone Commander [[Bacara]] served as the marshal commander of the [[4th Sector Army]]'s [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]].<ref name="Bacara FC" /> [[Bly]] served as a Clone Marshal Commander, leading the [[327th Star Corps]].<ref name="BFM 21">{{BuildFalconCite|21|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[Neyo]] was assigned command of the [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] following his completion of his ARC training and promotion to Clone Marshal Commander.<ref name="GAR">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref>
