'''Albrekh''' was a [[Symeong]] [[Gender|male]] [[metalsmith]] and [[Sith alchemist]] who served [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and the [[Knights of Ren]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. He worked on board the Knights' [[Oubliette-class transport|transport]], the ''[[Night Buzzard]]'', and was responsible for repairing their [[armor]].

This included the armor of Kylo Ren, and in [[35 ABY]] he used a [[Sith forge]] to repair the [[Kylo Ren's helmet|Supreme Leader's]] [[helmet]], reattaching the different pieces using [[Sarrassian iron]].

==Biography==
[[File:Albrekh-Forging.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Albrekh repairing Kylo Ren's helmet aboard the ''Night Buzzard'']]
Albrekh was a [[Symeong]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Databank" /> [[Sith alchemist]] and [[metalsmith]] who served [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], and his [[Knights of Ren]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. Albrekh served on board the Knights' personal [[Oubliette-class transport|''Oubliette''-class transport]], the ''[[Night Buzzard]]'', where he repaired their [[armor]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IX|Albrekh's appearance}}</ref> Ren brought Albrekh the many shards of his mask, some of which were warped beyond recognition after being carefully scavenged<ref name="TROSEE" /> from the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'' following the [[Battle of Crait]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

After assuring Ren the mask would be stronger, Albrekh lay out the pieces on his stone table and moved them into the correct places.<ref name="TROSEE" /> As Ren and his knights watched,<ref name="Episode IX" /> he then hammered them into shape with a special mallet, propped them together using magnetic forceps, and used molten [[Sarrassian iron]] to solder them together. Once the helmet was completely restored, he poured [[water]] over it repeatedly to cool it.<ref name="TROSEE" /> Once finished, he turned the completed helmet toward Kylo Ren, who silently took it and donned it before attending a meeting of his [[First Order Supreme Council]] on board the<ref name="Episode IX" /> [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']].<ref name="TROS VD" /> Ren and all his knights [[Death|perished]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]] not long after the helmet was repaired.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Personality and traits==
An eccentric Symeong, Albrekh was trained in classical metallurgy and was capable of smithing feats unheard of by the [[time]] he repaired Ren's mask. When beginning work on the object, he rubbed his hands together in anticipation then solved the puzzle of the fitting the pieces together with uncanny perception and speed. When soldering the pieces, Albrekh painstakingly applied the iron and worked with severe patience, focus, and precision.<ref name="TROSEE" />

Albrekh had dexterous, steady [[Limb|hands]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> and long, pointed, wide-spaced [[ear]]s that twitched at every sound or movement of air<ref name="TROSEE" /> and were sensitive enough to tell the temperature of metal based on the sound it made when struck.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The alchemist was small and thin, with a jutting [[Mouth|jaw]],<ref name="TROSEE" /> fair skin, and [[Color|brown]] [[hair]].<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
[[File:AlbrekhTools-SW100Objects.png|thumb|right|300px|Albrekh's workbench]]
When repairing Kylo Ren's helmet, Albrekh made use of a [[Sith forge]] and a series of four poseable arm mechanisms with magnetic forceps and reverse-[[repulsorlift]] [[Gravity|grav]]-focused bases in order to hold the pieces in place. The metalsmith also used a [[Hammer (tool)|hammer]], then a tool that could apply molten sarrassian [[iron]] and a compressed air cooling nozzle during the process. The Symeong wore a dark brown [[Clothing|robe]] with a tool kit attached to the chest, a brown [[belt]] and [[boot]]s, and a pair of magnification [[electro-goggles]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> He wore [[Sith alchemist gloves|gloves]] that Ren assumed must be made from a secret [[Sith]] fabric that stopped the Symeong's hands burning, but removed them at the end of the process.<ref name="TROSEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Wizard_52.png|thumb|left|200px|Wizard 52]]
{{Quote|It's safe to wear.|Albrekh has dialogue in the novelization of Episode IX that is not present in the film|<ref name="TROSEE" />}}
Albrekh first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], in which he was portrayed by [[Lynn Robertson Bruce]].<ref name="Episode IX" /> The character was first identified in the film's accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The [[2020]] [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition|novelization]] of the film written by [[Rae Carson]] has Albrekh speak when handing Ren the helmet,<ref name="TROSEE" /> but as Albrekh is silent in the film this article assumes the latter to be correct.<ref name="Episode IX" /> Chief costume concept artist [[Glyn Dillon]] created concept art of Albrekh for the film titled "Blacksmith Monkey Version 4A" and creature concept designer and senior sculptor [[Louis Wiltshire]] created a piece titled "Wizard 52."<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>
