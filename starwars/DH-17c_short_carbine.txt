The '''DH-17c short carbine''' was a model of [[blaster carbine]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]. Blurring the line between carbine and [[Blaster pistol|pistol]], its main selling points included its small size and range of firing modes. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] some [[Alliance Army|army]] [[engineer]]ing units within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] adopted the DH-17c as their official weapon.

==Description==
The DH-17c short carbine was a model of [[blaster carbine]] in the venerable [[DH-17 product line]]. Blurring the line between blaster carbine and [[blaster pistol]], the weapon was a shortened version of the [[DH-17 blaster rifle]]. The weapon's receiver and snub-nosed [[barrel]] came cast in a silvery [[color]].<ref name="FO" />

The main selling point of the DH-17c, aside from its small size, was its range of firing modes, which included the standard semi-automatic and [[stun]] settings along with an automatic setting, which gave users of the weapon an extra an unexpected edge in combat.<ref name="FO" />

==History==
The DH-17c short carbine was produced by [[BlasTech Industries]], who sold it from the factory without any accessories for 950 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]; however, many users chose to customize the weapon with optics and folding [[stock]]s, increasing both the performance and usability.<ref name="FO" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], several [[engineer]]ing units with the [[Alliance Army|army]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] adopted the DH-17c short carbine as their official weapon. This was due to the weapon's size and versatility allowing the engineers to swap between working and fighting with great ease than they would have using a heavier weapon.<ref name="FO" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DH-17c short carbine was created for the [[2018]] roleplaying supplement [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], which was released as part of the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] series produced by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="FO" />
