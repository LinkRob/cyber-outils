A '''fleet''', or '''armada''', was a conglomeration of [[warship]]s or other [[starship]]s that were organized into a single unit under a [[commanding officer]], though the term could be broadly used for any grouping of ships. Throughout [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]], numerous factions and forces made use of fleets and overall [[Navy|navies]].

==Description==
===Overview===
A fleet, or armada, was a formation of [[warship]]s or other [[starship]]s that was organized into a single unit and could consist of other sub-units and groups.<ref name="SWCL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> Fleets had both military and civilian purposes, and some [[Navy|navies]] were referred to as a fleet, such as the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]], often referred to as the Alliance Fleet,<ref name="Lead by Example">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> or the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]], which served as the [[New Republic]]'s naval branch.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>

===Military usage===
In military organization, fleets were typically a large formation of warships or military craft and part of a military's naval branch. Ranging from only a handful of [[capital ship]]s,<ref name="Shadow Warrior">{{Ahsoka|Part Five: Shadow Warrior}}</ref> to hundreds,<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> fleets were adaptable to various needs and responsibilities, such as rescue fleets,<ref name="HRA 2">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 2|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 2]]</ref> [[reserve fleet]]s,<ref name="Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> [[attack fleet]]s,<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> [[sector fleet]]s,<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> supply fleets, and more.<ref name="BMF 34">{{BuildFalconCite|34|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref>

==History==
Fleets were in use by the [[time]] the [[Sith armada]] was active in [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]], when it fought against the smaller yet ultimately triumphant [[Ghostfinder fleet]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> The Galactic Republic maintained a [[Republic rescue fleet|rescue fleet]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="HRA 2" /> The Republic maintained a fleet that was attached to each of its [[Sector Army|Sector Armies]], totaling twenty, during the [[Clone Wars]]. One of these twenty fleets was the [[2nd Fleet]].<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] possessed fleets in its [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|navy]] when it fought the Republic, and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] managed a [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] with which it fought against the Empire.

In the [[Imperial Navy]], a fleet could be commanded by an admiral,<ref name="TESB" /> a [[Fleet Admiral]], or a [[Grand Admiral]]. Some Imperial fleets were mobile and travelled from conflict to conflict,<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> such as the [[Third Fleet]],<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref> the [[Seventh Fleet (Galactic Empire)|Seventh Fleet]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> and [[Death Squadron]].<ref name="TESB" /> On the other hand, [[sector fleet]]s were set to a specific [[sector]] of [[the galaxy]], such as the [[Lothal sector fleet]]<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> and the [[Adelhard's fleet|Anoat sector fleet]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Task force]]s, such as the [[96th Task Force|Ninety-Sixth Task Force]], were units of ships smaller than fleets that were under the command of an admiral<ref name="Thrawn" /> or [[commodore]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

The [[Sith Eternal]] labored for years to create the [[Final Order]], a massive fleet of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]], which was set to launch as the largest fleet in [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> However, the [[Citizens' Fleet]], which saw action at the [[Battle of Exegol]] and overcame the [[Sith]] fleet, became the largest fleet ever created in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="SWBtCtG">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>
