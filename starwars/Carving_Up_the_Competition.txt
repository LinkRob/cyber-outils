"'''Carving Up the Competition'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short that aired online on [[September 15]], [[2021]] as part of the ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]'' webseries.

==Official description==
''Ahsoka Tano and Darth Maul have an epic pumpkin carving duel in the Mandalore throne room. Bo Katan cheers Ahsoka on while Gar Saxon and troops root for Darth Maul. Who's going to create the winning Jack-O'-Lantern? Tune in & find out!<ref name="YouTube" />''

==Plot summary==
[[Ahsoka Tano]] lands her starfighter outside the [[Sundari Royal Palace]] on [[Mandalore]]. [[Maul]] waits in the throne room and ignites his double-bladed [[lightsaber]]. The two engage in a competition to carve pumpkins with their lightsabers. [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and a pair of [[Clone trooper]]s from the [[501st Legion]] cheer Tano on. Maul is supported by [[Gar Saxon]] and his [[Mandalorian super commando]]s.

Tano presents her handiwork which is a carved pumpkin that has a figurine of a [[Togruta]]. Kryze and her team cheer and hold placards indicating she has won. Maul presents his handiwork which is a pumpkin carved into a likeness of himself. However, one of the horns drops off. Saxon and his team disapprove but change their minds when Maul flashes his lightsaber at them.

Tano and Maul continue the competition, carving up pumpkins with the [[Alliance Starbird]], [[X-wing starfighter]]s, [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]], and [[Yoda]]. For the final round, Tano and Maul carve a figure and sad face respectively onto their pumpkins. Both teams cheer Tano. Maul is upset and uses the Force to hurl pumpkins at Saxon and his super commandos. A food fight breaks out with Tano knocking Maul out with a pumpkin. Kryze awards a frowny face score to Tano but Maul awards a smiley face score to himself.
