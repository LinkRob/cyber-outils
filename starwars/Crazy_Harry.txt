'''Crazy Harry''' was a [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] veteran [[Droid/Legends|droid]] with a strong personality. He was a [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] for the [[Star Tours/Legends|Star Tours]] spaceline.<ref name="SWI104" />

==Behind the scenes==
The character of Crazy Harry was the original concept for the [[Pilot droid/Legends|pilot droid]] in [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] [[Star Tours (real-world)|''Star Tours'']] simulator ride. The idea of the "gung-ho veteran" pilot was later softened to become the nervous, rookie pilot [[RX-24/Legends|RX-24]], that guests could more empathize with.<ref name="SWI104" />
