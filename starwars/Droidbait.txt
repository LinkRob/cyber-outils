'''Droidbait''', identified by the number '''CT-00-2010''', was a [[clone trooper]] who served in [[Domino Squad]], a [[clone cadet]] [[squad]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Droidbait was stationed at a [[Republic Military]] [[Rishi Station|outpost]] on the [[Rishi]] [[Rishi Moon|moon]] where he was [[Death|killed]] by [[BX-series droid commando]]s.

==Biography==
Like all [[clone trooper]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], CT-00-2010, who adopted the name Droidbait, was grown on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] as an exact [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], and was part of the [[Clone cadet|cadet]] unit [[Domino Squad]]. In the group's final test, Droidbait, injured in the test, was left behind by his comrades on the ground, breaking the first rule of combat, which caused [[Clone Commander|Clone ARC Commander]] [[Colt]] to effectively end the test and give the Domino Squad an automatic failure. However, [[El-Les]], a [[bounty hunter]] who was hired by the [[Galactic Republic]] to be an instructor for clone cadets, made arrangements with [[Jedi Master]] [[Shaak Ti]] that the unit be given a final chance to pass the test, and through leadership and cooperation with one another, Droidbait and his unit successfully passed the test. After passing the test, Droidbait and his brothers&mdash;[[Fives]], [[Echo]], [[Hevy]], and [[Cutup]]&mdash;[[Graduation medal|graduated]] and were sent to fight the [[Separatist Droid Army|armies]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and restore peace to the Republic.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

===Battle on the Rishi Moon===
[[File:NubDroidbaitDead-Rookies.png|thumb|left|260px|Droidbait and Nub were killed by the commando droids, followed by Sergeant O'Niner.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Droidbait and his comrades were [[Rishi Station unit|stationed]] at the [[Rishi Station]], which was vital in the protection of Kamino from possible Separatist invasions. After the station's [[deck officer]] [[CT-327]] turned up missing, [[Clone Sergeant|Sergeant]] [[O'Niner]] sent Droidbait and [[Nub]] to investigate. Opening the [[blast door]], [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]] attacked and killed them both and O'Niner, forcing the rest of the clones to escape through the ventilation systems, allowing the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]], [[General]] [[Grievous]], to make pass through the [[Rishi system|system]] directly to Kamino unopposed. The general's victory was short-lived, however, as [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] "Cody" and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]] "Rex" arrived on the [[Rishi Moon|moon]] and helped the remaining [[trooper]]s regain control of the outpost and warn the [[Republic Navy|Republic fleet]] of the incoming attack.<ref name="Rookies" />

Later on, after losing contact with the commando droids, Grievous, hoping to keep the base's all-clear signal active, dispatched a [[C-9979 landing craft]] carrying a large force of [[battle droid]]s to reinforce the commando droids and keep the base under their control. However, the droids, unaware that a trap had been set for them, entered the control room and were suddenly destroyed by Hevy who ultimately blew up the base, therefore allowing the Republic to discover and stop Grievous's invasion [[fleet]].<ref name="Rookies" />

==Personality and traits==
Droidbait earned his nickname due to grumbling about being used as a way to draw out enemies during Domino Squad's exercises.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> When squad morale was low as they failed important exercises, Droidbait grew frustrated, but the group would eventually band together.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a clone trooper, Droidbait was skilled in both armed and unarmed combat as well as military tactics.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
Droidbait was armed with a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />
