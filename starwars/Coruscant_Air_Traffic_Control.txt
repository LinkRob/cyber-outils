The '''Coruscant Air Traffic Control''' was the authority that monitored the [[skylane]]s of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], [[Coruscant]], at the time of the [[Galactic Republic]]. It was so efficient that crashed [[airspeeder]]s were an uncommon sight on the low-level pedestrian walkways.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> The receivers of [[EasyRide passenger airspeeder|EasyRide]]<ref name="2020Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and [[ComfortRide passenger airspeeder]] [[air taxi]]s<ref name="2018Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> monitored the many channels of the Coruscant Air Traffic Control to allow the taxi drivers increased flexibility.<ref name="2020Vehicles" /> During the [[New Republic Era]], the [[Skylanes Agency]] held consultations about deliberations over proposed changes Coruscant's skylanes network.<ref name="GT:AFitC">{{InsiderCite|237|Galactic Tales: Another Face in the Crowd}}</ref>
