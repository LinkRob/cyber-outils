'''Alun''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] of [[the galaxy]]. Because no useful [[hyperspace route]]s connected it to other [[Star system|systems]], Alun was relatively isolated, and escaped [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] rule. The planet was ruled by a [[Royal family of Alun|royal family]].<ref name="Unlimited" />
