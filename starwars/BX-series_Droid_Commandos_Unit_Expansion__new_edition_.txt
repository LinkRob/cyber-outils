The '''BX-series Droid Commandos Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ52''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[BX-series Droid Commandos Unit Expansion]], it was released on [[March 13]], [[2026]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''This expansion allows players to add a unit of BX-Series Droid Commandos and an optional BX-Series Droid Commando Strike Team as a Special Forces option to their Separatist Alliance army.''

''This product contains the previously released SWL72 BX-Series Droid Commandos miniatures with updated rules in the new multilingual format. Updates SWL72 BX-Series Droid Commando unit with current game rules and in multilingual format.''

*''Ideal choice for Separatist Alliance armies looking for a disruptive and agile special forces option.''
*''Features a number of build options including blasters, vibroswords, and deflector shields.''
*''Includes two units, the standard Commando Droid unit and a two-droid Strike Team unit that can accompany the parent unit.''

==Contents==
*7 Miniatures
*3 Unit Cards
*3 Upgrade Cards
