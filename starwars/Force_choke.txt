'''Force choke''', a variant of and, at times, synonymous with '''Force grip''', was a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force power]] where one would use the [[the Force]] to '''choke''' or strangle a victim, oftentimes with a grip-like gesture. It was a technique of [[telekinesis]] that was frequently employed by the [[Sith]] and other users of the dark side of the Force, although there were some cases where [[Jedi]] or other users of the [[Light side of the Force|light side]] would use the technique.

==Description==
{{Quote|This ability perfectly exemplifies how acolytes of the dark side can twist the Force's powers to their sinister whims. Often employing a simple clawlike hand gesture to focus the attack, a practitioner of the dark side is able to use this ability to remotely strangle anyone who opposes them. Some use this corrupt form of telekinesis merely as a method of punishment and control, while others take the power much further and crush the life out of their foes. Although quite effective, this ability is rooted in fear and designed for pain, making it a power that a Jedi rarely calls upon.|[[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>}}
The Force choke was a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Force power]], meaning that the power was fueled from dark emotions such as fear, anger, and hatred. As the name suggested, the Force choke was used to [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] grip, squeeze or crush a victim's [[throat]] with [[the Force]] and restrict blood circulation or airflow. Some who used the ability demonstrated being able to lift their victim off the ground, holding the victim up by their necks. The technique could also be aimed to break the neck of a victim, either by twisting, bending or crushing it.<ref name="DVDLotSTCOPI">[[Darth Vader (2017) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 1]]</ref><ref name="DVDLotSTCOPIII">[[Darth Vader (2017) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 3]]</ref>

[[File:Force_choking_darksider_UP.png|thumb|left|250px|A dark side user Force chokes her victim.]]
The power was generally used at close range, but in rare instances the target could be choked at greater distances if they were seen through a [[viewscreen]].<ref name="Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back">{{Film|V}}</ref> However, this power could be applied even if the user did not have a direct line of sight to the target, or even if the target was [[light-year]]s away, viewed through a [[Hologram|holocomm]].<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref><ref name="The Lost One" /> Upon becoming a victim of the Force ability, a very distinctive sound could be heard resembling a person taking a deep gasp, usually followed by a low pitched bass-sound that ended when the user of the Force choke released their grip.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> However, there were very many instances of the technique being used without the sound occurring.<ref name="Episode III: Revenge of the Sith" /><ref name="Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back" />

Depending on the degree of power applied, the effects of a Force choke on a person would result in either [[death]], unconsciousness, or simply leaving the person gasping for air or unable to speak, with the ability sometimes being employed to incapacitate or subdue rather than to kill. However, the ability could be used to leave a victim mortally wounded even after releasing them, resulting in their death without the user having to do so directly.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref>

The Force choke was generally used against one target at a [[time]], while in some instances, perhaps involving an increase of dark side power fueled by intense emotions, such as rage, two targets could be grabbed in a Force choke at the same time; [[Asajj Ventress]],<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref> and [[Savage Opress]],<ref name="Witches of the Mist">{{TCW|Witches of the Mist}}</ref> demonstrated this on different occasions. However, both [[Darth Sidious]]<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] demonstrated the ability to choke at least two separate individuals simultaneously in relatively casual situations. Vader in particular was able to use the ability on numerous people simultaneously, and on one occasion used the ability to crush the throats of five Imperial officers to make a point.<ref name="DVDLotS12">[[Darth Vader (2017) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 12]]</ref>

==Applications==
[[File:NewWaysToMotivateThem-SWQ156.png|thumb|300px|Darth Vader used Force choke to "motivate" his subordinates.]]
{{Quote|Whatever conclusions you ultimately draw about the incident taking place between myself and Lord Vader during yesterday morning's briefing, he was wrong, and trying to crush someone else's windpipe doesn't make you any less wrong, if you're wrong to begin with. Which he was. I do not concede the argument.|An unrepentant Motti after being telekinetically choked by Darth Vader|<ref name="An Incident Report">{{StoryCite|story=An Incident Report|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>}}
The Force choke was used to inflict harm or injury for various purposes, such as [[Torture|forcing]] a person to obey the user's commands.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Sometimes it was used simply as an expression of anger or displeasure.<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref><ref name="Episode III: Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref> Other times, it was used to kill outright: the power could choke a target for long enough that they ran out of air<ref name="Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back" /> or be used to simply snap their neck.<ref name="Crimson Reign 4">[[Crimson Reign 4|''Crimson Reign'' 4]]</ref> The Force choke was an effective tactic when engaged in [[lightsaber]] [[Lightsaber combat|combat]].<ref name="Episode III: Revenge of the Sith" /> Related to this, a Force choke could also be used as a display of power.<ref name="Eminence">{{TCW|Eminence (episode)}}</ref>

==Users==
===Overview===
{{Quote|And before she could reveal the Jedi's name, Letta Turmond was strangled to death by way of the Force.|Padmé Amidala, during the trial of Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />}}
Generally, Force choke was used by the [[Sith]] and other dark side users like [[Sixth Brother]],<ref name="Ahsoka">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref> [[Snoke]],<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> or [[Quinlan Vos]] after he became apprenticed to Dooku.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> However, despite their strict adherence to the [[light side of the Force]], several [[Jedi]] were known to use this power, as although it was considered a dark side ability, it was also a technique of telekinesis. Its use, however, was forbidden by the Jedi.<ref name="Expert Guide">''[[Star Wars Expert Guide]]''</ref>

===Azlin Rell===
During the [[Night of Sorrow]] in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the Jedi engaged the [[Path of the Closed Fist]] cult in battle, only to discover the Path had unleashed unknown weapons that could overwhelm them. Those weapons were in fact the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]], creatures who feasted upon<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref> the [[Living Force]]<ref name="Insider 221">{{InsiderCite|221|Star Wars: The High Republic: Phase II Companion|''Star Wars: The High Republic'': Phase II Companion}}</ref> and, thus, hunted the Jedi. One Jedi Knight involved in the battle was [[Azlin Rell]], who became distraught and overwhelmed during the conflict. Upon meeting Path [[Herald (title)|Herald]] [[Werth Plouth]], Rell gave into his desperation by [[Mind probe (Force power)|probing into the man's mind]], with Rell's actions further creating a Force choke-like effect. Padawan [[Matthea Cathley]] urged Rell to stop and ultimately pushed him back with [[telekinesis]], but Rell defended his choice as desperate times necessitating desperate measures. Consumed by fear of the Nameless after the battle,<ref name="Path of Vengeance" /> Rell would go onto fall to the dark side of the Force.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 1">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 1]]</ref>

===Verosha Aniseya===
During a [[Duel on Brendok|skirmish]] on her [[homeworld]] of [[Brendok]], after hearing [[Sol]]'s confession about killing her [[Aniseya|mother]] and his reasons for not telling her the truth, an angered [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha]] choked and killed him, falling to the dark side as she did so.<ref name="The Acolyte">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''</ref>

===Anakin Skywalker/Darth Vader===
====As Anakin Skywalker====
{{Quote|Mind tricks? I don't need mind tricks to get you to talk.''"<br />&laquo;''I will never talk, Jedi.''&raquo;<br />"''We'll see about that, Poggle!|Anakin Skywalker, as he Force chokes information out of Poggle the Lesser|<ref name="Brain Invaders" />}}
[[File:Anakin_choke_Poggle.png|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker interrogating Poggle the Lesser with a Force choke.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[TB-73|ship]] [[Padawan]]s [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Barriss Offee]] were aboard was [[Skirmish aboard TB-73|infected]] with the [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[Geonosis]] [[brain worm]]s. [[Jedi Master]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Mace Windu]] attempted to gain intelligence from captured [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leader [[Poggle the Lesser]], who had vital knowledge that could save Tano and Offee. However, the Jedi Masters' attempts to extract that information were unsuccessful. An angry [[Anakin Skywalker]] decided to take matters into his own hands, and entered Poggle's cell alone. Skywalker attempted to use a [[mind trick]] to get Poggle to talk, but it was unsuccessful. Poggle informed Skywalker that mind tricks wouldn't work on [[Geonosian]]s, as the other Jedi had already discovered. Skywalker angrily knocked him down, telling him that he wouldn't need mind tricks to get him to cooperate. Skywalker then gave in to the dark side and grabbed Poggle using a Force choke, lifting him off the ground and throttling him until he finally explained that the [[brain]] [[worm]]s were vulnerable to cold [[temperature]]s. The Jedi Masters were curious as to how Skywalker had managed to get Poggle to cooperate, but he refused to elaborate, keeping his dark actions secret.<ref name="Brain Invaders" />

While [[Mission to Zygerria|working]] undercover on [[Zygerria]], Skywalker was captured by [[Queen]] [[Miraj Scintel]], and awoke from a thrashing of [[electro-whip]]s to find himself alone in her chambers. Realizing his apprentice Tano and former master Kenobi were missing, Skywalker angrily grabbed Scintel in a Force choke, demanding to know where his friends were. While choking in his grip, Scintel responded by telling him that his friends would die if he continued to misbehave. At this, Anakin reluctantly released her from the Force choke.<ref name="Slaves of the Republic">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref>

[[File:Anakin-vs-Bartender.png|thumb|right|270px|Anakin Skywalker chokes an Ithorian in anger, adapting to the alien's special physiology by forcing the bartender to cover their mouths.]]
While searching for Kenobi's "killer" [[Rako Hardeen]], who was in fact Kenobi himself, impersonating the [[bounty hunter]], Anakin employed a Force choke against the [[Unidentified Ithorian bartender (Nal Hutta)|Ithorian bartender]] in the saloon where they were last seen.<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref>

During the [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] of his Padawan Ahsoka, Skywalker went looking for [[Asajj Ventress]] in the dark [[Coruscant Underworld|underlevels]] of [[Coruscant]] in an attempt to gain information that would clear his Padawan's name. Anakin stalked Ventress and overpowered her, then Force choked her, demanding answers about her involvement with Ahsoka Tano and the rogue Jedi who framed Ahsoka for murder. Anakin was able to use the information he extracted from Ventress to expose Barriss Offee's involvement in the conspiracy.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

Upon seeing former Senator [[Rush Clovis]] attempting to kiss his secret wife [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], Skywalker lost control of his emotions and attacked the man, beginning with a Force choke and throwing him to a wall. The display was too much for Amidala, who was disturbed by her husband's actions.<ref name="The Rise of Clovis">{{TCW|The Rise of Clovis}}</ref> The two would later reconcile during the [[Invasion of Scipio|Battle of Scipio]].<ref name="CH">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref>

====As Darth Vader====
{{Quote|I find your lack of faith disturbing.|Darth Vader|audio=LackOfFaith.flac|<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>}}
[[File:PregnantDyingPadme.png|thumb|left|250px|The heavily-pregnant Amidala lies unconscious after being choked by Vader.]]
In the [[Imperial Era|aftermath]] of the Clone Wars, Amidala [[Duel on Mustafar|journeyed]] to [[Mustafar]] to confront her husband, unaware that Anakin Skywalker was now [[Darth]] Vader. Vader, who was bidden to [[Mission to Mustafar|destroy]] the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] by the [[Emperor]], contemplated his chances of overthrowing Sidious. Vader was convinced that the dark side had made him more powerful than the Emperor; he rejected Amidala's pleas to run away together, and declared that they would rule [[the galaxy]] in Sidious' place. Amidala refused to join him moments before Kenobi appeared, causing Vader to give into his anger under the belief that his wife had betrayed him. Vader seized Amidala by the neck using the Force to strangle her in spite of her pleas and Kenobi's demand to release her. Although Vader eventually relented, Amidala lost consciousness and had to be taken to recover.<ref name="Episode III: Revenge of the Sith" />

Following Vader's defeat and reconstruction as an [[Darth Vader's armor|armored]] [[cyborg]], he inquired about his wife's condition. Sidious told his apprentice that he killed Amidala in anger, leaving Vader confused and distraught as his rage boiled over, crushing the droids around him.<ref name="Episode III: Revenge of the Sith" /> Vader attacked Sidious with the Force, telekinetically slamming the Emperor into the wall and attempting to strangle him, though to no avail as Sidious shielded himself with his own powers. After reasoning with, threatening, and disciplining his apprentice, Sidious warned Vader that he would finish what Kenobi did not if Vader ever tried to use the Force on him again, only to then adopt a kind approach by claiming they could work well together.<ref name="DVDLotSTCOPI" />

[[File:DarthVader-Platinum2022.png|thumb|right|250px|Darth Vader favored Force choking his victims.]]
Shortly thereafter, Vader [[Attack on Brighthome|attacked]] the former Jedi outpost ''[[Brighthome]]'', deciding to attack its [[clone trooper]] occupiers rather than transmit his clearance codes. When [[trooper]]s "[[Ding]]" and "[[Kicker]]" threw a [[grenade]] at him to subdue the figure they thought was a Jedi, Vader, [[Force Stasis|holding back]] the explosion with the Force, informed both troops he was more than a Jedi, choking them both and breaking their necks.<ref name="Dark Lord 2">[[Darth Vader (2017) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 2]]</ref> When Vader arrived on the river moon of [[Al'doleem]] in pursuit of the Jedi Master [[Kirak Infil'a]] to claim his [[Kirak Infil'a's lightsaber|lightsaber]], several [[raptoran]] [[bird]]s were sent against Vader, who crushed several with the Force. After Infil'a called off the birds, Vader still crushed at least two more.<ref name="DVDLotSTCOPIII" /> At the end of their duel, Vader distracted Infil'a by damaging the dam over a nearby city, forcing the Jedi to focus on stopping a flood while he could claim his price. After that, Vader Force choked the Jedi Master and made him watch as the city flooded before snapping his neck and throwing him into the rushing water below.<ref name="Dark Lord 3">[[Darth Vader (2017) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 3]]</ref>

When [[Colonel]]<ref name="Dark Lord 8">[[Darth Vader (2017) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 8]]</ref> [[Barokki]]<ref name="DVDLotS12" /> presented a report on his [[Barokki's division|division]], Vader Force choked the officer for its failings to teach the colonel a lesson.<ref name="Dark Lord 8" /> After uncovering a [[Assassination attempts on Darth Vader|conspiracy]] against him from within the [[Imperial Officer Corps]], Vader was formally introduced to the [[Imperial Military]] hierarchy and, in retaliation for the [[assassin]]ation attempts, Force choked five officers he claimed were picked at random: [[Tomas Azoras]], [[Corin Ferro]], [[Joon Strephi]], [[Zorta Bingan]], and Barokki, who actually had been involved in the conspiracy. Vader promised to kill five officers for each attempt on his life and then left.<ref name="DVDLotS12" /> While [[Hunt for Maul|hunting Maul]] on [[Janix]], Vader used this ability to kill two [[Mandalorian super commando]]s, killing the last one [[Rook Kast]] as he dragged her back with the Force.<ref name="Strange Allies">{{MSL|Chapter 9: Strange Allies}}</ref> During his duel with Maul and Jedi Master [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] in the ruins on Janix, Vader froze [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|Maul's lightsaber]] with his his left arm and then choke the [[Zabrak]], while his right lightsaber arm dueled Daki after it was briefly [[Force Stasis|frozen]] by Maul.<ref name="The Dark Lord">{{MSL|Chapter 10: The Dark Lord}}</ref>
[[File:Vader-defeats-Cal.png|left|thumb|250x250px|Vader uses Force choke against Jedi Knight Cal Kestis at Fortress Inquisitorius.]]
When [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] was invaded by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] and former [[Jedi]] [[Cere Junda]], Vader appeared to confront the two after Kestis defeated the [[Inquisitor]] known as [[Second Sister]]. After killing the Inquisitor for her failure and temporarily getting rid of Junda, Vader used the Force choke on Kestis. Kestis tried to free himself by using the Force to try to hit Vader with a piece of machinery, but Vader stopped the attack and threw him, giving rise to a chase through the Fortress.<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

[[File:Choke_on_your_aspirations.png|thumb|250px|Vader chokes Director Krennic as a reminder to avoid "[choking] on aspirations"]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Vader met with [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] in his [[Fortress Vader|personal retreat]] on Mustafar to discuss the secrecy of [[Project Stardust]]. When Krennic had the audacity to make a direct request of Vader, who was not surprised, for an audience with the Emperor and asked if he was still in charge of the project, an irritated Vader responded by Force choking him, bringing the Director to his knees. Rather than kill him, however, Vader sardonically warned Krennic not to "choke on (his) aspirations" before releasing him.<ref name="Rogue One" />

[[File:Force_Choke_SWDA.png|thumb|180px|Darth Vader often used Force choke on officers who displeased him.|left]]
Not long after, in a meeting of Imperial [[officer]]s aboard the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]], [[Admiral]] [[Conan Antonio Motti]] boasted of the [[battle station]], claiming that the [[Death Star]] was the preeminent power in the galaxy. However, Vader rebutted this claim, warning Motti not to underestimate the power of the Force. Motti mocked Vader, insulting his devotion to the Force, contending that it had not helped him recover the [[Battle of Scarif|stolen]] [[Death Star plans]] or locate the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels']] [[Great Temple|hidden base]]. In response, and in a similar manner to how he had dealt with Krennic, Vader Force choked him and said, "I find your lack of faith disturbing." At [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s command, Vader released Motti from the Force choke, though only at the last moment.<ref name="Episode IV" />

Vader was able to choke to death the [[officer]] [[Kronn]] from afar, without seeing him, simply via comlink.<ref name="Star Wars (2015) 3">[[Star Wars (2015) 3|''Star Wars'' (2015) 3]]</ref> After learning he had a son in Luke Skywalker, Vader moved in secret to find the boy and turn him to the dark side, intending to supplant Sidious. When Imperial [[Inspector]] [[Thanoth]] was close to finding one of Vader's secret agents, [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]], the Sith Lord cornered her first and began to Force choke her, intending to eliminate her as a loose end to keep his conspiracy a secret. However, Aphra bought herself time by revealing she knew Skywalker's location, promising to only reveal it if she lived. Agreeing, Vader allowed her to escape and made it seem as though she had trapped him under rubble.<ref name="Vader 2015 11">[[Darth Vader (2015) 11|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 11]]</ref> Meeting with Aphra later and learning his son was on the planet [[Vrogas Vas]], Vader decided he would allow her to live, believing she had proven herself as a useful agent.<ref name="Vader 2015 12">[[Darth Vader (2015) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 12]]</ref>

[[File:YouAreInCommandNow-SoH.png|thumb|200px|Piett receives his promotion, and Ozzel his execution.]]

However, his plan backfired and the tactical blunder only alerted the Alliance to the Empire's arrival, prompting them to raise their [[V-150 Planet Defender|base's energy shield]] and evacuate the [[planet]]. Realizing that they would now have to waste resources in fighting a costly ground battle rather than simply destroying the [[Echo Base|base]] from [[orbit]] as a result of Ozzel's actions, Vader contacted the admiral from his [[Darth Vader's meditation chamber|meditation chamber]] aboard the ''[[Executor]]''. Via a viewscreen, Ozzel announced that the [[Death Squadron|fleet]] had arrived in the Hoth [[Star system|system]], but his report was cut short when Vader began to Force choke the admiral as punishment for his blunder. Deciding that the man had made his final mistake, the Dark Lord executed Ozzel by Force choking him to death and immediately promoted captain [[Firmus Piett]], who witnessed the gruesome execution, to fill Ozzel's position.<ref name="Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back" />

[[File:WorstSoreThroatEver-ESB.jpg|thumb|250px|Needa was telekinetically strangled to death by Darth Vader.|left]]
While in pursuit of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', [[Captain]] [[Lorth Needa]] was in command of the ''[[Avenger]]'', one of the Imperial [[Star Destroyer]]s. After the Falcon emerged from the [[Hoth asteroid field|asteroid field]], the ''Avenger'' resumed its pursuit, but Captain [[Han Solo]] was able to outwit Needa, making it seem as though the ''Millennium Falcon'' had escaped. When Vader demanded an update on the ''Avenger's'' progress, Needa decided to travel to the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] ''Executor'' to personally apologize to the Sith Lord for losing track of his quarry. Needa did just that, whereupon the Sith [[Lord]] Force choked the captain to death as penalty for his failure. Vader mockingly accepted Needa's apology before a pair of Imperial [[soldier]]s dragged his lifeless body away.<ref name="Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back" />

[[File:ForceChokeAttack-2020DarthVader6.png|thumb|250px|Darth Vader chokes Mas Amedda and the Emperor's Royal Guards before him.]]
[[Duel on Cloud City|Confronting]] Luke Skywalker created conflict within Vader, as did his encounter with [[Sabé]], a former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] who resembled the late Amidala. The Emperor sensed as much from his apprentice, and decided to punish Vader in order to keep him strong with the dark side. Despite having [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] taken away, Vader continued to fight back against the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]]s. [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] pulled a [[blaster]] on Vader, to which the Sith Lord called him a fool before telekinetically choking Amedda and the Royal Guards. Sidious approved of Vader's display of power, though it was not enough to stop the Emperor from overpowering Vader through his own command of the Force.<ref name="Vader 6" />

[[File:ForceChoke-2020DarthVader24.png|thumb|250px|Believing the assassin let a target escape, Darth Vader Force chokes Ochi of Bestoon.|left]]

As they fought with the governor's [[battle droid]]s and [[Unidentified technology|specialized technology]], [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]] arrived into the battle via [[speeder bike]] before presenting himself to Vader.<ref name="Vader 24">[[Darth Vader (2020) 24|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 24]]</ref> However, Vader was well aware of the fact that Ochi was working for both the Empire and the Dawn, although Ochi had remarked that he was a double agent for the Empire's benefit.<ref name="Vader 21">[[Darth Vader (2020) 21|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 21]]</ref> After Ochi presented himself, Vader began to Force choke the assassin, accusing Ochi of giving the governor a chance to escape. Ochi claimed that he had been trying to save Vader's life, which Vader found dubious by asking if he really thought their machines could hurt him. Sabé, however, noted the technology actually could pose a chance against Vader, to which Vader dropped Ochi to see what she meant.<ref name="Vader 24" />

Intending to punish those who had broken into his personal castle, Vader started his attack with a Force choke, aiming it at Knight [[Fyodor]] and snapping the Knight's neck. The Knights' leader, [[Ren (human)|Ren]], quickly began to duel Vader to allow his fellow Knights to escape, escaping with his comrades as Vader began to overpower him.<ref name="Crimson Reign 4" />

====As Darth Tyranus====
{{Quote|Punishment for a slave who disobeys her master.|Dooku, to Miraj Scintel, as he Force chokes the defiant queen|<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />}}
On the planet [[Florrum]], Darth Tyranus was captured by a [[Ohnaka Gang|gang]] of [[Weequay]] [[pirate]]s for ransom. One of the pirates, [[Turk Falso]], decided to go behind [[Hondo Ohnaka|his leader]]'s back and get the ransom for himself. However, his plans failed. Knowing that his leader would figure out his betrayal, Falso attempted to flee in a [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|shuttle]] when he and his accomplice encountered [[Count]] Dooku, who had escaped from the [[Hondo's complex|pirate]] [[prison]]. Falso's escape was suddenly halted as Tyranus grabbed him in a lethal Force choke and manipulated his body to force him to shoot his accomplice with his [[blaster pistol]]. Dooku then escaped Florrum in the waiting [[shuttle]].<ref name="The Gungan General">{{TCW|The Gungan General}}</ref>

On Naboo, while [[Skirmish on Naboo|luring]] Skywalker into a trap in the order of his master Darth Sidious after [[General]] [[Grievous]] was captured by the [[Gungan]]s, Tyranus Force choked Anakin and lifted him in the air after the latter Forced pushed his [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s in anger.<ref name="Shadow Warrior">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref>

[[File:MirajScintelChoked-EFK.png|thumb|250px|Dooku Force choking Queen Miraj Scintel for her threats.|left]]
On Zygerria, [[Queen of Zygerria|Queen]] Miraj Scintel received Dooku in her [[throne]] room and detailed her plot to [[Slavery|enslave]] the Jedi as she did Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker. She stated that an army of Jedi slaves would make Zygerria invincible. However, Dooku deemed her plan too ambitious and made it plain he was interested in eradicating the [[Jedi Order]], not enslaving it. Dooku sensed her emotions for Skywalker and demanded that he be disposed of. Infuriated by Dooku and [[Atai Molec]]s plan to replace her, Scintel drew an electro-whip and threatened the Count. Without even turning around to face her, Dooku throttled her with the Force. As she struggled to breathe against the Count's hold, Skywalker burst into the chamber and demanded that Dooku release her. Dooku did release Scintel from the Force choke, and dropped her to the floor. Before losing consciousness, she reached out to Skywalker and spoke his name softly.<ref name="Escape from Kadavo" />

Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker traveled to the planet [[Oba Diah]] to [[Mission to Oba Diah (Clone Wars)|investigate]] a new lead on the [[Mission to Oba Diah (Sifo-Dyas)|mysterious death]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Sifo-Dyas]], the Jedi who supposedly ordered the creation of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|clone army]]. There, Skywalker and Kenobi found a man named [[Silman]], who had been held captive by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] for many [[Standard year|years]]. Unfortunately for them, years of captivity had taken its toll on Silman's mental health, making it difficult to get any coherent information from him. However, Silman did reveal that someone powerful was behind the death of Sifo-Dyas, someone who wanted to steal his identity. Outside the cell, Dooku, who had arrived on the planet, Force choked two of the [[Pyke Syndicate Foot Soldier|Pyke guards]]. Then, just as Silman was about to explain more about what happened, he was lifted off the ground in front of the astonished Jedi and Force choked to death by Dooku. Despite this, the Jedi were able to learn that Dooku and Darth Tyranus were the same person, and that he was actually behind the creation of the [[Cloning|clone]] [[army]].<ref name="The Lost One" />

Aboard the Separatist [[flagship]], the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'', Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker found [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] Sheev Palpatine, who had been [[Battle of Coruscant|kidnapped]] by Count Dooku. Just then, Dooku entered the room, and after verbal jousting, a [[lightsaber duel]] ensued between the three. Dooku held off both Jedi with his swordsmanship, then he grabbed Kenobi in a Force choke and hurled him away, knocking him out and effectively taking him out of the fight, leaving Dooku to face Skywalker alone.<ref name="Episode III: Revenge of the Sith" />

===Asajj Ventress===
[[File:VentressChokesKenobiSkywalker-Nightsisters.png|thumb|250px|Asajj Ventress Force choking both Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker.]]
During the [[Battle of Teth|battle]] of [[Teth]], Ventress Force choked [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] in order to coerce him to give Skywalker's location in the [[B'omarr Order Monastery]]. However, Rex refused until she used a [[mind trick]] on him.<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> During the [[Battle of Kamino|Battle]] of [[Kamino]], she Force choked [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] commander [[Colt]] before she Force pulled him onto her lightsaber and kissed him.<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> In a [[Battle of Sullust|space battle]] in the [[Sullust system]], Asajj Ventress's [[starfighter]] crashed on a Separatist flagship. Injured, Ventress was confronted by Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi. A heated lightsaber battle ensued. After battling both Jedi, Ventress was brought to her knees and seemingly defeated. Refusing to surrender, she reached out in a final desperate fit of rage and Force choked both Jedi at the same time, momentarily halting their attack. Ventress was barely able to escape and seek refuge on her [[Homeworld|home planet]], [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Nightsisters" />

After being betrayed by the Sith, Ventress got involved with some bounty hunters, including a young [[Boba Fett]]. Towards the end of a bounty [[hunting]] [[Safe cargo objective|mission]], Ventress and Boba began to argue about their part in the mission and their share of the [[bounty]]. Tempers quickly flared, and Boba brashly told Ventress that she did not know who she was dealing with. At that, Ventress lifted Boba in a Force choke and informed him that he did not know who he was dealing with. After this encounter, Boba found himself bound, gagged, and locked inside a box.<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref>

===Savage Opress===
[[File:SavageChokesMasters-WOTM.jpg|thumb|250px|Savage Opress Force chokes both of his former masters.|left]]
After completing the first stages of his Sith training, new apprentice Savage Opress was sent by his master Count Dooku to kidnap [[Katuunko]], [[king]] of [[Toydaria]]. [[Jedi General]]s Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, who had been hunting for the [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], found Opress on Toydaria with the unconscious king slung over his back. While they [[Showdown at Toydaria|engaged in combat]], King Katuunko awoke and attempted to flee. Despite being nearly pinned by both Jedi, Opress caught Katunko in a Force choke, eventually snapping his neck and killing him.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />

After escaping the Jedi, Opress brought the dead king before Dooku, who became enraged that his orders were not followed and that the king was killed. Just then, Ventress entered Dooku's cabin. She reasserted her control over Opress, and they attacked the Count in concert. During the fight, Opress was repeatedly hit and weakened by Dooku's [[Force lightning]]. After Ventress and Dooku each criticized him for being weak, Opress snapped and turned against them both. In a fit of rage, he reached and grabbed both of them in a Force choke at the same time, then hurled them into the wall. This forced Ventress and Dooku to team up to repel his furious onslaught. They eventually left him to face Skywalker and Kenobi. Opress fought off the Jedi and the [[battle droid]]s who turned against him by Dooku's order and managed to escape, although badly injured.<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />

===Maul===
{{Quote|Our combined strength will be rewarded. Mandalore will be yours, and Kenobi, this Sith pretender Dooku, and all our enemies... will fall.|Maul making a point as he holds Bo-Katan Kryze in the air with a Force choke in front of the Death Watch|<ref name="Eminence" />}}
[[File:MaulTorturesBoKatan-Eminence.png|thumb|250px|Maul Force chokes Bo-Katan Kryze in a display of power.]]
At the [[Zanbar Death Watch camp|Death Watch camp]] on [[Zanbar]], [[Maul]] proposed an [[Shadow Collective|alliance]] to their leader [[Pre Vizsla]], telling him and his assembled soldiers that he can help [[Death Watch]] reclaim [[Mandalore]] and kill Kenobi. Death Watch member [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] spoke out against this possible alliance, stating that they had once allied with a Sith, Dooku, who ended up betraying them. She expressed her mistrust to the Sith and stated that they were no better than the Jedi. She stated that the Sith claimed to be powerful, but pointed out that Maul and Opress were put back together only after they were "gutted" by the Jedi. In response, Maul grabbed her in a Force choke. As Bo-Katan gasped for breath, the Death Watch soldiers in the tent raised their weapons toward Maul. Pre Vizsla ordered his solders to hold their position.<ref name="Eminence" />

While choking Bo-Katan and holding her in the air with the Force, Maul declared that together, all their enemies would fall under their combined strength. At this, he released Bo-Katan, who fell to her knees. Despite this ordeal, when Maul left, Bo-Katan and Vizsla smiled at each other, as if all was going according to plan. Maul later Force choked a [[Twi'lek]] enforcer on [[Nal Hutta]] before throwing him to [[Latts Razzi]].<ref name="Eminence" /> Maul later left [[Satine Kryze]] suspended in the air by the neck while taunting Obi-Wan Kenobi and attempting to turn him to the Dark Side. He caused her to glide towards him so he could impale her with the [[Darksaber]].<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> Much later, while [[Mission to Malachor|on]] [[Malachor]], Maul choked the [[Seventh Sister]] and urged [[Ezra Bridger]] to kill her, but when the orphan refused, Maul threw [[Maul's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] at her, killing her instantly.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> While fighting the [[Spectres]], Maul Force choked [[Sabine Wren]], before he [[Force push|pushed]] her into [[Hera Syndulla]].<ref name="The Holocrons of Fate">{{Rebels|The Holocrons of Fate}}</ref>

===Ahsoka Tano===
[[File:Ahsoka_Force_choke_Trandoshan.png|thumb|250px|Ahsoka Tano momentarily Force chokes one of her captors.|left]]
After Ahsoka was kidnapped and trapped in the harsh environment of [[Island Four]] to be hunted by [[Trandoshan]] hunters, she resolved to help her fellow prisoners. ===Barriss Offee===
[[File:PoggleIncarcerated-TJWKTM.png|thumb|209x209px|Barriss choking Letta from a distance]]
While Ahsoka Tano was investigating the [[bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar]], the suspected bomber, [[Letta Turmond]], was in custody and requested to speak with Tano alone. When Tano arrived, Turmond asked her for protection in exchange for the name of a Jedi involved in the temple bombing. However, before Turmond could reveal the Jedi's name, she was Force choked to death. [[Clone Commander]] [[Fox]], who had seen the exchange on the security [[camera]]s, believed Tano was Force choking the [[prisoner]], when in fact Tano was reaching out to Letta in an attempt to save her. Fox rushed into the cell, but Turmond was already dead and Tano was arrested for her murder. Tarkin came to visit Tano and showed her the security footage that made it looked like Tano was Force choking the prisoner. However, the sound had conveniently been disabled so no one could hear that Tano was trying to help.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref> It was later revealed that the person who Force choked Turmond to death and framed Tano was disgruntled Jedi Barriss Offee.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

===Darth Sidious===
{{Quote|This is not the first time you have proven to be ''clumsy'', Lord Tyranus. You know the price of failure.|Sidious to Dooku as he Force chokes a lesson into him|<ref name="The Lost One" />}}
Upon feeling a [[disturbance in the Force]] regarding the situation on [[Mandalore]], Darth Sidious as Sheev Palpatine called [[Mas Amedda]] to prepare [[Darth Sidious' Eta-class shuttle|his ship]]. Upon arriving in [[Sundari]], Sidious casually choked to death two [[Mandalorian super commando]]s who attempted to stop him as he did not have a landing permit, before heading to the [[Sundari Royal Palace]]. There, he announced his presence to his former apprentice Maul by Force choking two [[Mandalorian]] guards in the throne room from outside, levitating them up the side of the wall.<ref name="The Lawless" />

[[File:Sid_puts_the_squeeze_on_Tyranus.png|thumb|250px|Darth Sidious strangled Darth Tyranus to remind his apprentice of the penalty for failure.|left]]
A furious Darth Sidious contacted Dooku via a holocomm regarding the Jedi, who seemed to be investigating a new lead on the mysterious death of Sifo-Dyas. Dooku expressed that he did not know what trail the Jedi were following. Sidious ordered Dooku to tie up these loose ends lest their plans be exposed, then he grabbed Dooku in a Force choke and lifted him off the ground, reminding him of the dangerous consequences of clumsiness and failure. After the communication ended, Dooku was released from the Force choke and picked himself up while coughing from the punishment and went to fulfill his assignment. At the time, Dooku was on [[Serenno]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] and Sidious was on Coruscant in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="The Lost One" />

[[File:VaderChoked-2020DarthVader6.png|thumb|250px|Sidious used Vader's own method of punishment against him.]]
During his reign as Emperor, Sidious applied the Force choke technique on Vader to demonstrate how weak the apprentice was compared to the master. Vader struggled helplessly as his master taunted him about his past as the Jedi Order's prophesied Chosen One. The Emperor asked Vader how it felt to be powerless, hurt and afraid as he relentlessly strangled him with the Force, reminding Vader of the various individuals that he had used this technique on throughout his time as a Sith Lord, from Amidala and Sabé, to the many Imperial officers who suffered his wrath. Though Sidious spared Vader from death, he considered him to be no different than a tool that was discarded once its usefulness ended.<ref name="Vader 6" />

===Kylo Ren===
{{Quote|The Supreme Leader is dead.''"<br />"''Long live the Supreme Leader.|Kylo Ren and Armitage Hux|<ref name="Episode VIII" />}}

Ren ordered Hux to deploy the [[First Order Army]] to [[Crait]], determined to end the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]]. When Hux refused to comply, Ren started to strangle the general with the Force, using the dark side ability as a tool of intimidation. Unable to resist Ren's powers, Hux capitulated to Ren, whom he acknowledged as the new Supreme Leader of the [[First Order]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />

At the climax of the [[Hunt for the Colossus|hunt for the ''Colossus'']], Ren grew annoyed with [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] and [[Commander]] [[Pyre]]'s repeated failures to capture the ''[[Colossus]]'' refueling station. When General [[Domaric Quinn]] expressed his doubts on the Supreme Leader's faith on the [[Sith Eternal]], dismissing them as [[Sith cultist|cultists]] and soothsayers despite [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]]'s opinion that their resources could benefit them, Ren cut Quinn off by choking and slamming him into the ceiling of the conference room.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Force choking first appeared in the very first ''[[Star Wars]]'' movie, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']] and is the first example of telekinetic abilities being shown in the Canon timeline.<ref name="Episode IV" />

During the episode "[[Brain Invaders]]," while Anakin Skywalker was Force choking Poggle the Lesser, the background music played a variant of "[[The Imperial March]]," a somber musical piece that is frequently associated with Darth Vader, perhaps hinting at the darkness that resided in Anakin and his eventual fall to the dark side.<ref name="Brain Invaders" />

In ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', the distinctive Force choke sound effect introduced in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''The Clone Wars'' film]] was used on the big screen in live-action for the first time, heard in the section of the film where Vader chokes Director Krennic, warning him not to "choke on [his] aspirations."<ref name="Rogue One" />
