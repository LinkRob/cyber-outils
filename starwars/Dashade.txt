'''Dashades''' were a hulking [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. Two Dashades, [[Maliss]] and [[Sorath]], worked as [[pirate]]s. They were hired to run a [[Unidentified mobile way station|mobile way station]] as part of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]]'s scheme to capture [[freighter]]s carrying vital components for [[Project Stardust]]. When the [[Grysk]]s attacked the [[Space station|station]], both Maliss and Sorath charged in to attack, and Maliss was [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], a Dashade was present on the [[Wayward Comet]] [[Fuel|refueling]] station, and got into a fight with the Resistance member [[Finn]].<ref name="Allegiance 1" />

They were known to be one of the several species that enjoyed [[blosphi extract]] as a delicacy.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Dashades first appeared in the novel ''[[Thrawn: Treason]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> The species was first pictured in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]], written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]],<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> and published on [[October 9]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/76701/journey_to_star_wars_the_rise_of_skywalker_-_allegiance_2019_1|text=Journey to Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker - Allegiance (2019) #1}}</ref> The Dashade's species was not identified as such in the comic itself, but was mentioned in the comic's behind-the-scenes section.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />
