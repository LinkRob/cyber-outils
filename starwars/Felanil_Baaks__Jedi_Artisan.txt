'''Felanil Baaks, Jedi Artisan''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[March 13]] [[2003]] and took the form of an excerpt from a speech by Jedi artisan [[Felanil Baaks]] to the [[Almas Academy]] covering the meaning of [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsaber]] creation.
