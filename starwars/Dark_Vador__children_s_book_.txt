'''''Dark Vador''''' is a [[Wikipedia:French language|French]] young-readers book written by [[Sabine Boccador]] and published on [[April 15]], [[2016]] by [[Fleurus]] as part of the series ''[[La grande imagerie Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Amazon" />

==Publisher's summary==
''The 3rd title in the Star Wars saga. Children will discover Anakin Skywalker's journey to becoming a Jedi Knight, then a Sith Lord under the name of Darth Vader. For a better understanding of this famous saga.<ref name="Amazon" />''
