'''G2-1B7''' was a [[2-1B surgical droid]] that belonged to [[Saw Gerrera]], leader of the rebel [[Partisans]]. The [[Medical droid|droid]] was modified so that it could dispense drugs to Gerrera at what would normally be considered dangerous intervals and was baffled that he was still alive after inhaling near-[[Death|lethal]] levels of [[Geonosian insecticide]]. Exhausting all possibilities in keeping its master alive, G2-1B7 replaced much of Gerrera's body with [[cybernetics]] and showed ingenuity in recycling equipment.

In [[1 BBY]], G2-1B7 worked out of the Partisans' [[Catacombs of Cadera|headquarters]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] and was present when Gerrera was reunited with former ally [[Jyn Erso]], shortly before their base was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]. Both G2-1B7 and its patient perished in the blast.

==Biography==
===Partisan practioner===
During the ongoing [[Galactic Civil War|conflict]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[2-1B surgical droid]] G2-1B7<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> was the personal [[medical droid]] of the leader of the [[Partisans]] rebel movement, [[Saw Gerrera]], who kept it close at all [[time]]s.<ref name="HC">{{HelmetCollectionCite|55|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> The [[droid]] was tasked with the difficult primary objective of keeping Gerrera alive, as he had previously inhaled near-[[Death|lethal]] levels of [[Geonosian insecticide]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|g2-1b7|G2-1B7}}</ref> G2-1B7 exhausted all possibilities while treating his master, and replaced much of Gerrera's body with [[cybernetics]] to treat his more serious injuries.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

[[File:G2-1B7_asleep.jpg|thumb|left|300px|G2-1B7 served rebel Saw Gerrera on Jedha, working from a rudimentary examination room in the Catacombs of Cadera.]]
In the [[Standard year|years]] leading up to the [[Battle of Yavin]], G2-1B7 worked out of a small medical examination room with rudimentary conditions<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the Partisans' base in the [[Catacombs of Cadera]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]], where it was regularly deactivated in order to prevent it from building up an ethical case to stop<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> or alter Gerrera's treatment in its free time, and also to preserve energy.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Gerrera's disability was so severe that droid also had to be heavily modified in order to bypass programming that would stop it dispensing the dangerous amount of drugs that he needed to function.<ref name="HC" />

===Death in the catacombs===
G2-1B7 treated Gerrera and the other Partisans from a diagnostic station<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> that included a flight chair that it had refitted into an examination table, an improvised [[plethysmograph]] to assess [[lung]] capacity, and an [[Computer|analysis computer]] that tracked the purging of the [[insecticide]] from Gerrera's lungs.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> The droid also used more sophisticated tools like its [[microscope]] and cybernetic repair tools, which it stored in recycled ceramic glasses.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

[[File:G2-1B7Deactivated-RO.png|thumb|right|300px|G2-1B7 was present when Jyn Erso watched a hologram of her father, Galen Erso.]]
In the year [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> G2-1B7 was present in Gerrera's quarters when he was reunited with his former ward, [[Jyn Erso]]. The droid stood stationary in a corner of the room while Gerrera showed Erso a [[Hologram|holographic]] message from her [[Parent|father]], [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]]. When the message ended, Erso and many of the Partisans were forced to flee the catacombs as they were [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the shockwave of a blast from the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]], although Gerrera remained behind and perished.<ref name="Rogue One" /> Although no reliable source recorded G2-1B7's death, the droid certainly perished alongside its master.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Characteristics==
G2-1B7 was a [[medical droid]] equipped with a [[55VH Vocabulator]] and swappable [[servogrip pincer]]s<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> to handle medical equipment in specific circumstances.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> G2-1B7 was modified so that it could dispense drugs at intervals that were too dangerous for a standard 2-1B droid's intrinsic [[Programmer|programming]] to allow. It was also capable of formulating ethical cases based on the condition and treatment of its patients.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />

[[File:Gerrera-MedicalStation-ROUVG.png|thumb|left|300px|G2-1B7 showed ingenuity in keeping its master, Saw Gerrera, alive, using recycled medical equipment and replacing much of his body with cybernetics.]]
The droid did what it could to help its master,<ref name="BuildR2">{{BuildR2Cite|72|Droid Directory}}</ref> and showed ingenuity in its treatments and regular recycling of equipment. Despite the precautions taken to prevent G2-1B7 ceasing Gerrera's treatment, it could still question the treatment and condition of its patient, and within the limits of what a droid could express,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> it was baffled as to how Saw Gerrera was still alive by the time of his reunion with Jyn Erso,<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> and was usually amazed at his patient's will to survive. The droid noted Gerrera's grim determination as they prepared to face the deadly onslaught of the Death Star.<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> Like all 2-1B surgical droids, G2-1B7 was manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] and stood 1.77 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It had [[Color|white]], yellow, and silver plating.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
G2-1B7 first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film, ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The droid was not identified in the film<ref name="Rogue One" /> but received a designation in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" /> The English edition of the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 55|fifty-fifth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine,<ref name="HC" /> which was released on [[February 7]], [[2018]],<ref name="Date2">{{HCdates|55}}</ref> and written by [[Iain Lowson]],<ref name="HC-Credits">{{EmailCite|email=File:HCSV-Email-2025-07-26.png|author=[[Iain Lowson]]|date=2025-07-26}}</ref> erroneously refers to G2-1B7 as a "G2 unit"<ref name="HC" /> rather than a 2-1B medical droid.<ref name="Rogue One Visual Guide" />
