'''Arkoff''' was a [[Wookiee]] [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. The Wookiee [[Jedi]] served on the remote [[planet]] [[Banchii]], working with the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]] and several [[Padawan]]s. In the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], Arkoff and the other Jedi on Banchii assisted people who had been displaced by the catastrophe.

==Biography==
The [[Wookiee]] Arkoff served the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]], attaining the rank of [[Jedi Master]]. Arkoff was stationed at the [[Jedi temple (Banchii)|Jedi temple]] on the remote [[planet]] [[Banchii]], alongside the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]] and a number of [[Padawan]]s. In the time following the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]<ref name="The Edge of Balance V1" /> in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Arkoff and the [[Jedi]] of Banchii assisted those who had been displaced by the disaster, including settlers from [[Ta'klah]]. The Jedi also had to respond to a surprise attack on Banchii by the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s known as the [[Drengir]] in the time leading up to the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]].<ref name="The Edge of Balance V1" />

Arkoff was chose to be part of the [[Luminous Nine]]. He was present in the [[Jedi Temple's primary hangar]] during the loading of the [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] onboard the ''[[Ataraxia]]''. When one of the Nameless broke out of containment and made a break for the rest of the Jedi Temple, the Wookiee threw his body at the creature, knocking it down. He then wrapped his arms around its neck in an attempt to subdue it. During the fight, the Nameless bit down on Arkoff's leg which began to calcify. [[Torban Buck]] quickly amputated the leg with his [[Torban Buck's blue-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Arkoff-THREOB2.png|thumb|right|250px|Arkoff, Jedi Master]]
A Wookiee, Arkoff had braided face [[Hair|fur]]<ref name="The Edge of Balance V1" /> and was considered to be old by the Padawan [[Bell Zettifar]] around the time of the Great Disaster.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> When Jedi Padawans on Banchii were excited by news of the [[Republic Fair]], Arkoff reminded them that as Jedi, there was an order to their responsibilities, of which helping the people on Banchii was the most important.<ref name="The Edge of Balance V1" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a Jedi Knight Arkoff was more reliant on his Wookiee strength and size rather than actual skill in battle, which limited his effectiveness when fighting against multiple opponents or foes of similar size but by the time he had become a Jedi Master he had adopted his [[Ravna Abronsa|master]]'s fighting style, which consisted in striking only when his attacks could have the greatest effect on his enemy, making him a much more dangerous opponent.<ref name="Precedent" />
Padawan Zettifar considered Arkoff to be skilled in [[lightsaber combat]]; when he witnessed Jedi Master [[Porter Engle]] fighting off [[mole mine]]s during a [[Nihil]] [[Raid on Elphrona|raid]] on the planet [[Elphrona]], he judged Engle's [[lightsaber]] skills to be beyond anything he had seen from Arkoff.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> Arkoff was also proficient in telekinesis, being able to repel and move multiple [[Scav droid]] on the hull of a ship on his own.<ref name="The Edge of Balance V4">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 4|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 4]]</ref> As a Wookiee, Arkoff spoke the [[Shyriiwook]] [[language]].<ref name="The Edge of Balance V1" />

==Equipment==
Arkoff wore a set of [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]] with a [[belt]] and [[gauntlet]]s as well as a darker outer [[robe]].<ref name="The Edge of Balance V1" /> His belt held emergency rescue and medical tools.<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> Arkoff also utilized a<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> [[Color|green]]<ref name="Secrets">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Wookiees]]''</ref> [[Arkoff's lightsaber|lightsaber]] in combat.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
