"'''Dark Horse: Phase Two'''" is an article written by [[Daniel Wallace]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 115|''Star Wars Insider'' 115]]. The article is a continuation of Wallace's previous article, ''[[Dark Horse Rising]]''. ''Phase Two'' discusses how [[Dark Horse Comics]] launched multiple titles during the time when the [[Prequel trilogy|prequel]] movies were released. [[Randy Stradley]] from Dark Horse explained that their sales stayed at a lower level until after [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] came out, attributing it to too many ''[[Star Wars]]'' merchandise being offered, thus forcing the fans to make choices due to limited funds.<ref name="sw115" />

The article was republished in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2013|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2013]].<ref name="sw2013" />
