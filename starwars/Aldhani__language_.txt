'''Aldhani''', also known as '''Dhani''', was a [[language]] spoken by the native [[Dhani]] people on the [[Planet|world]] of the [[Aldhani|same name]]. It was [[Writing|written]] in its own [[Aldhani alphabet|alphabet]] of at least twenty-one letters, which had a one-to-one correspondence with [[Aurebesh]] letters.<ref name="Aldhani" />

==History==
On the eve of the [[Aldhani heist|mission]] to raid the local [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] garrison, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] was urged to learn a few Aldhani phrases from a [[datapad]] supplied by [[Taramyn Barcona]].<ref name="Aldhani" />
