'''Fern''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="This Could Be a Real Adventure" /> [[youngling]] who lived in [[the galaxy]] in the early [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="SkelCrewDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> Fern lived together with her [[Parent|mother]] [[Fara]] on [[At Attin]], where Fara was a [[teacher]] and part of the [[At Attin's government|government]], and was best friends with another child called [[KB]].<ref name="This Could Be a Real Adventure" /> Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="SkelCrewDate" /> Fern met the boy [[Wim]] who found an [[Onyx Cinder|old starship]] buried in the [[Forest|woods]] outside [[Unidentified city (At Attin)|their city]]. After the two, KB and Wim's best friend [[Neel]] investigated the ship, they accidentally activated it and became lost in space.<ref name="This Could Be a Real Adventure" /> The group of kids then forged an alliance with the ship's [[droid]] [[SM-33]] and a [[Force-sensitive]] man called [[Jod Na Nawood]] to find a way back home and avoid a [[Silvo's pirates|crew of pirates]].<ref name="Past the Barrier" />

==Personality and traits==
Fern was smart and could at times be quite tough and rebellious.<ref name="This Could Be a Real Adventure" /> Fern had a mostly positive outlook on life, believing that even in the worst parts of the Galaxy there were good people willing to help a [[Skeleton Crew|group of children]].
