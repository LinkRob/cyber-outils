'''Ak trees''' were a type of [[tree]] found on the [[Deep Core]] [[planet]] [[Tython]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> old ak tree [[forest]]s existed on the world's surface.<ref name="MandoVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], ak trees were mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'', a [[2026]] reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> The [[Ak tree/Legends|trees]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they appeared in flashbacks in [[Tim Lebbon]]'s [[2013]] novel ''[[Dawn of the Jedi: Into the Void]]''.<ref name="ITV">''[[Dawn of the Jedi: Into the Void]]''</ref>
