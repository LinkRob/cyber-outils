'''C03''' was a small [[droid]] living in the residence of [[Professor]] [[Mitaka]], one of several droids in his possession. C03 often tagged along with [[T0-B1]], such as in his quest for [[kyber crystal]]s. Along with T0-B1, C03 was hidden and thus spared from the destruction that ensued when the Professor's location was leaked to the [[Galactic Empire]]. Both surviving droids repaired the others and carried on Mitaka's dream of generating life on the planet they lived in.<ref name="T0-B1" />
