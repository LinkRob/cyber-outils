{{Quote|This flavorful fungus is usually processed to extract a powerful chemical called ixetal cilona, which is used to create an addictive narcotic known commonly as 'death sticks.'|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Balo mushrooms''', or simply '''balos''', were a type of [[mushroom]] found only on the heavily polluted [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Balosar]]. There, they were grown on [[farm]]s run by the massive corporations that controlled the world and refined into the chemical [[ixetal cilona]].

By the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], this was the most famous industrial operation on Balosar. The [[Fungus|mushrooms]] were also [[Foodstuff|edible]], and the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe using balo mushrooms in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', when it was published in [[35 ABY]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Honestly, I figured it'd be a minor miracle if anythin' could grow on Balosar, yet somehow the balo mushroom found a way to thrive.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
Balo mushrooms, also referred to simply as balos, were a type of [[mushroom]] that thrived on the heavily polluted [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Balosar]],<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> the only world upon which they could be found.<ref name="CandM">''[[Cyphers and Masks]]''</ref> They could be refined into the chemical<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> narcotic [[ixetal cilona]].<ref name="CandM" /> They were also [[Foodstuff|edible]] to members of the [[Artiodac]] [[species]] when sautéed.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|I've never been one for puttin' those types of toxins into my big ol' body, though. I'd much rather sauté a batch of balos in butter and herbs instead. That way, I get to enjoy all the terrific taste with none of the nasty side effects!|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
[[File:Deathsticks-DB.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A transparent cuboid container holds thin vials of red and yellow liquid.|The chemical ixetal cilona was refined from balo mushrooms and used to make death sticks (pictured).]]
By the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the refinement of balo mushrooms into ixetal cilona was the most famous industrial operation on Balosar. What little of the polluted world was not covered in [[Factory|factories]] or urban sprawl was used for balo mushroom [[farm]]s that could extend far underground. These farms were run by the massive corporations that controlled Balosar, such as [[BioTech Industries]] and [[Fergriss Pharmaceuticals]], and were protected by heavy security including [[Security droid|enforcement droids]], [[Patrol ship|patrol craft]], and large [[squad]]s of armed guards. Once harvested, the coveted mushrooms would be refined in towering factories within Balosar's [[City|cities]], with ixetal cilona then being used to produce everything from interrogation drugs<ref name="CandM" /> to addictive narcotics like [[death stick]]s.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

[[File:Strono_Tuggs_VD.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Strono Tuggs, a gray-skinned Artiodac with a disfigured face, stands with a cleaver in his left hand while wearing a brown apron and a green shirt.|Strono Tuggs was surprised to find balo mushrooms thriving on Balosar.]]
Competing corporations would regularly hire teams of deniable assests to target their competition's balo mushroom growing fields, [[Thief|stealing]] the produce, [[Saboteur|sabotaging]] the operation, or even destroying the farm all together. Many members of the [[Balosar (species)|Balosar]] species&mdash;who were native to the eponymous planet and often caught in its web of corporate corruption&mdash;were tempted to take part in these quick and dirty corporate [[Spy|espionage]] jobs in order to earn fast [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] while hindering the machinations of one of the corporations that controlled their [[homeworld]].<ref name="CandM" />

During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] did not expect to find anything growing on Balosar during a visit but was surprised to learn of the balo mushroom. He later included a recipe that made use of balo mushrooms in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he described the mushrooms as flavorful with a terrific taste, stating that he would rather sauté the [[fungus]] with [[butter]] and [[Plant|herbs]] than consume it as a narcotic, as it allowed him to experience the taste without negative side effects.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], balo mushrooms were first mentioned in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'', a [[2018]] supplement for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] game published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="CandM" /> The [[Balo mushroom/Legends|mushrooms]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[2006]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]'', which was written by [[Ann Margaret Lewis (real person)|Ann Margaret Lewis]] and [[Helen Keier]].<ref name="NEGTAS">''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]''</ref>

[[File:CreminiMushrooms.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=A pile of cremini mushrooms with gray heads and white stalks.|Cremini mushrooms were used to represent balo mushrooms in an out-of-universe recipe.]]
The [[2023]] canon cookbook ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]], included a recipe for a balo mushroom dish that used [[Wikipedia:Agaricus bisporus|cremini mushrooms]] to represent the fungus in the [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe. The recipe also used [[Wikipedia:Butter|unsalted butter]], [[Wikipedia:Shallot|shallots]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], fresh [[Wikipedia:Thyme|thyme]], [[Wikipedia:Basil|basil]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], grated [[Wikipedia:Parmesan|parmesan cheese]], and chopped, fresh [[Wikipedia:Parsley|parsley]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
