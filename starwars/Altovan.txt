'''Altovan''' was an employee of [[Canto Casino Hotel]]. When [[Kedpin Shoklop]] found his way to the [[Canto Casino Hotel]], it was Altovan who checked him in. Upon doing so however, Kedpin had realized his luggage had been stolen. Despite this, Altovan made Shoklop feel very welcome and showed him to his room, where Kedpin slept for an hour. Altovan later arranged for Shoklop to visit [[Zord's Spa and Bathhouse]], where he relaxed for the rest of the day.<ref name="Rules of the Game" />
