'''Chesna Braker''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who was a landlady on [[Coruscant]]. [[Arihnda Pryce]] confronted her for neglecting to repair a leaking pipe in one of her tenant [[Daisie]]'s apartment blocks. Following a tense argument, Braker reluctantly agreed to fix Daisie's leaking pipe after Pryce reminded her to comply with Coruscant laws and regulations.<ref name="Thrawn" />
