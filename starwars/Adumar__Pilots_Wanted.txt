'''Adumar: Pilots Wanted''' was a five-part ''[[Planet Hoppers]]'' article series from [[April]] [[2004]], covering the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Adumar/Legends|Adumar]]. The articles were written by [[Cory J. Herndon]] and published on [[Wizards.com]] by [[Wizards of the Coast]] as a supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''.

==Contents==
===Part 1: The Art of the Duel===
''&hellip;an anonymous [[New Republic Intelligence]] agent offers a field report on the culture of Adumar.''<ref name="Adumar" />

===Part 2: Four Is Green-Lighted===
''&hellip;[[Derek Klivian/Legends|Derek Klivian]] provides an [[X-wing starfighter/Legends|X-wing]] [[Pilot/Legends|pilot's]] perspective on Adumar's [[Blade series|Blade fighters]].''<ref name="Adumar" />

===Part 3: Three, Ready For a Furball===
''&hellip;[[Wes Janson/Legends|Wes Janson]] offers a brawler's look at ubiquitous Adumari honor duels and [[blastsword]]s.''<ref name="Adumar" />

===Part 4: Two Standing By, One Hundred Percent===
''&hellip;[[Tycho Celchu/Legends|Tycho Celchu]] describes Adumari native [[Cheriss ke Hanadi]]'s contributions to the [[Planet/Legends|planet's]] history.''<ref name="Adumar" />

===Part 5: Leader Has Two Lit and In the Green===
''&hellip;[[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]] offers a few words of wisdom for Adumar and recalls the turning point in his diplomatic mission.''<ref name="Adumar" />
