The '''DL-18 blaster pistol''' was a model of [[blaster pistol]] commonly used throughout [[the galaxy]], particularly by [[bounty hunter]]s and [[smuggler]]s. The blaster, manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]], was inexpensive and allowed for easy customization; because of this, the blaster pistol became very popular in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. Former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] and [[Order 66]] survivor [[Kanan Jarrus]] used a [[Kanan's blaster|modified DL-18]] with a [[dewback]] skin handle after the fall of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[BX-series droid commando]], [[ND-5]] used one while working together with [[Kay Vess]] .<ref name="Outlaws" />

The DL-18 [[blaster pistol]] featured a long [[barrel]] and over-concentrated beam that supplied extra firepower.<ref name="Bridger's Journal">''[[Rebel Journal by Ezra Bridger]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:KananBlasterSaber-Kanan1.png|thumb|left|250px|Kanan Jarrus wielded his DL-18 and lightsaber together with considerable skill.]]
During the [[Republic Era]], [[Boba Fett]] carried a DL-18 pistol while training under his father, [[Jango Fett]]. During a mission on [[Ord Mantell]], the bounty hunters [[Tiver]] and [[Rinn (Gand)|Rinn]], betrayed him and his father, Rinn picked up Fett’s blaster and held Boba at gunpoint, demanding Jango to give the [[Twi'lek]] [[Unidentified Twi'lek (Ord Mantell)|girl]] to them. After Boba broke free and shot both Tiver and Rinn with a hidden blaster. The third hunter [[Neelda]] handed Fett his DL-18 before leaving.<ref name="Age of Republic">[[Age of Republic - Jango Fett 1|''Age of Republic - Jango Fett'' 1]]</ref>

In the [[Standard year|years]] following the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the DL-18 became widespread across [[the galaxy]] among its [[smuggler]]s and [[bounty hunter]]s.<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide" /> The blaster was so popular among [[Tatooine]]'s inhabitants, it became known as the "[[Mos Eisley]] special."<ref name="USWNE" /> 

During the time of the Galactic Empire, former Jedi [[Cere Junda]] possessed a DL-18 after abandoning her [[Cere Junda's lightsaber|green-bladed lightsaber]]. She was able to use her blaster in conjunction with [[Second Sister's lightsaber|Trilla's Inquisitor lightsaber]] during her incursion on Nur.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" />

After surviving [[Order 66]], former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]]<ref name="Kanan 3">[[Kanan 3|''Kanan'' 3]]</ref>&mdash;who later renamed himself to Kanan Jarrus<ref name="Kanan 5">[[Kanan 5|''Kanan'' 5]]</ref>&mdash;took a [[Kanan's blaster|DL-18]], despite it feeling wrong to carry it at first.<ref name="Kanan 3">[[Kanan 3|''Kanan'' 3]]</ref> He preferred this model over other [[blaster pistol]]s due to its low cost, how easy it was to repair, and its low profile. He brought a couple of modifications to the [[blaster]] during his time with it, such as wrapping the grip in [[dewback]] [[leather]] and modifying the trigger's sensitivity. In addition, Kanan let [[Sabine Wren]] increase the weapon's power output.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> Jarrus once used the blaster alongside his lightsaber to fight [[the Grand Inquisitor]].<ref name="Gathering Forces">{{Rebels|Gathering Forces}}</ref> After being blinded by [[Maul]] on [[Malachor]],<ref name="Twilight">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref> Jarrus continued to carry his DL-18 but rarely using the weapon until his [[death]] on [[Lothal]] but he manage to kill several [[Death trooper]]s of [[Thrawn's guard]] before dying at the [[Lothal City fuel depot]].<ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref>

Various outlaws like [[ND-5]], [[Waka]], and former [[pirate]]-turned-[[sheriff]] [[Quint Cresadde]] wielded a DL-18. [[Hondo Ohnaka]] also wielded a DL-18 while working with the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] against the [[Rokana Raiders]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

[[File:DL18BlasterPistol-SWBF.png|thumb|right|250px|The DL-18 was a customizable blaster.]]
During the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], [[Luke Skywalker]] used [[the Force]] to grab [[Nizuc Bek]]'s DL-18 blaster pistol and attempt to shoot [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], but the Hutt activated a trapdoor to protect himself, dropping the Jedi and one of his own guards, [[Jubnuk]], into the [[Rancor Pit]]. While fighting above a [[Sarlacc]] pit, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando]] and a guard fought over a DL-18 before being knocked into the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. [[Chewbacca]] handed [[Han Solo]] a DL-18 so he can shoot the creature's tentacle and forced the Sarlacc to let go of Lando's leg so he can climb out. Several of Jabba's goons like [[Wooof]], used their DL-18s to fire at Skywalker, however, the Jedi deflected the shot rebounding the bolt back, killing them.<ref name="RotJ" />

Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|1}}</ref> a gang of [[Nikto]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] wieled a few DL-18s on the planet [[Arvala-7]], when they had a captive [[Din Grogu|child]]. When [[IG-11]] showed up, a Nikto attempted to shoot the [[assassin droid]], but the droid was much faster and killed the mercenary. Several more Nitko wielded DL-18s when battling IG-11 and [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref>
