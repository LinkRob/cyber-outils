'''Devshi''' was an [[ocean]] [[moon]]<ref name="Mace Windu 1" /> in the [[Devshi system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Devshi prison complex]], run by [[Warden]] [[Mattis Coto]],<ref name="Showdown at Ocean's Deep" /> was situated on the ocean floor of Devshi. During the [[Republic Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Jedi]] [[Mace Windu]] visited the moon<ref name="Mace Windu 1" /> and infiltrated the [[prison]] in order to confront Coto, who had been selling skeleton keys.<ref name="Showdown at Ocean's Deep" />
