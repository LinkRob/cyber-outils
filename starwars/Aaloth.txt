'''Aaloth''' was a [[Twi'lek]] colony [[planet]] in the [[Gaulus sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> During its history, Aaloth was once [[Occupation of Aaloth|occupied]] by the [[Sith Empire]].<ref name="Kindling" /> By the [[High Republic Era]], Aaloth was nominally a member of the [[Galactic Republic]], as a part of the [[Clan Assembly]] on [[Ryloth]] and was ruled by a [[Keeper of Aaloth|Keeper]]. It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]] and her [[Lourna Dee's family|family]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aaloth first appeared in the [[2021]] audio drama ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]. Due to ''Tempest Runner'' being an audio-only release,<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> the name's spelling was only confirmed by the short story "[[The Kindling]]" from the collection ''[[Life Day Treasury]]'', also published in 2021.<ref name="Kindling" />
