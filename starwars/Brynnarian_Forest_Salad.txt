'''Brynnarian Forest Salad''' was a type of [[salad]]<ref name="Mace">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref> that originated in the [[Brynnaria system]]<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=Brynnarian Forest Salad|system=[[Brynnaria system]]}}</ref> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> It could be served with [[Hallowaysian tree leaves]]<ref name="Mace" /> which originated from the nearby [[Halloways system]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Not long after the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]] attended a banquet in [[New Xaxxis]], the [[capital]] city of the planet [[Metagos]], where Brynnarian Forest Salad was among the dishes served.<ref name="Mace" />
