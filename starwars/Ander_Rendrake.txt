'''Ander Rendrake''' was an individual who served as a [[lieutenant]] in the [[Imperial Military|military]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] around the time of the [[Mid Rim Retreat]]. Rendrake, however, was actually an active operative of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service|Intelligence]] branch, providing information to the Empire's enemies.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ander Rendrake was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', a [[canon]] reference book released in [[2017]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
