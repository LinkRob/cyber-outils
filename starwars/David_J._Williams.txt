'''David J. Williams''' co-wrote the short stories ''[[Maze Run]]'' and [[Blade Squadron (short story)|''Blade Squadron'']] with [[Mark S. Williams]]. The stories appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 131|''Star Wars Insider'' 131]], [[Star Wars Insider 149|''Star Wars Insider'' 149]], and [[Star Wars Insider 150|''Star Wars Insider'' 150]].
