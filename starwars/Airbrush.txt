'''Airbrushes''' were devices used for painting. [[Sabine Wren]] used them to paint an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] during the ''[[Ghost]]'s'' mission to [[Lothal]]. She also regularly used these for her [[graffiti]].<ref name="ArtAttack">{{Rebels|Art Attack}}</ref>
