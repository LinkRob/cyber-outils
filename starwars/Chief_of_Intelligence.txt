The '''Chief of Intelligence''' was the head of [[New Republic Intelligence]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] and [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> Previously, under the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the title was referred to as the '''Chief of Rebel Intelligence''' and was also the head of the [[Rebel Alliance Intelligence Service]]. During the war, [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] served as the Chief under the Alliance<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> and New Republic.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> [[Tolo Mandah]] later served as the leader of New Republic Intelligence.<ref name="Free Fall">''[[Poe Dameron: Free Fall]]''</ref>
