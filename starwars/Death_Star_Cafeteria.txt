The '''Death Star Cafeteria''' was a cafeteria on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] that fed [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s. The cafeteria had a menu consisting of [[Boba Feta Cheese]], [[Lando Calzone]], [[Darth Wafer]], [[Yoda Yogurt]], [[blue milkshake]], [[Han Salad]], and more.<ref name="Training" />
