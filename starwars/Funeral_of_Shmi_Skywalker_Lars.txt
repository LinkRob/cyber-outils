In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> a '''funeral''' was held for [[Shmi Skywalker Lars]] at the [[Lars homestead|Lars moisture farm]] on [[Tatooine]]. The funeral followed her [[death]] one [[Standard month|month]] after being kidnapped and held hostage by [[Tusken Raider]]s. She was [[Mission to rescue Shmi Skywalker Lars|recovered]] from the [[Unidentified Tusken village|Tusken Raider camp]] by her son, [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], who returned her body to the Lars farm after slaughtering the Tuskens within the camp. Shmi was buried at the farm with a gravestone marking her grave.<ref name="Episode II" /> After her husband [[Cliegg Lars]] died, he was buried next to her. Both of their headstones were later presumed to have been removed by her stepson, [[Owen Lars]] and stepdaughter [[Beru Whitesun Lars|Beru Lars]] supposedly to protect Shmi's grandson Luke from being discovered.<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2020) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 1]]</ref>
