The '''electromagnetic plasma cannon''' was a [[cannon]] utilized by the [[Umbaran Militia]] on its [[Umbaran mobile heavy cannon|mobile heavy cannon]], [[Umbaran hover tank|hover tank]], and [[Zenuas 33 Umbaran starfighter]]. They were noted for their power and destructive ability and were a unique technology that the [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] had never encountered before.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" />
