A '''cathedral''' was located in [[Aldera]],<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> In [[1 BBY]], Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|annihilated]]<ref name="Destruction" /> by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> During her [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|wedding ceremony]] within a [[Bright Tree temple|temple]] in [[Bright Tree Village]] on<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[moon]] [[Endor]]<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] mentally noted that there was every chance that had things turned out differently, she would have been [[Marriage|married]] to [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[General]] [[Han Solo]] at the cathedral.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
