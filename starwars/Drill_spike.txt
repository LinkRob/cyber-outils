'''Drill spikes''', also called '''Drill Beaks''', were a type of weaponized [[drill]] that was used to drill through the hulls of [[starship]]s or buildings. The [[Trident-class assault ship|''Trident''-class assault ship]] manufactured by the [[Colicoid Creation Nest]] were armed with a drill spike and the weapons were used in the [[Battle of Kamino]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="EoSaOV" />
