The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''203 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3074 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''184 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7774''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*Jedi Master [[Mostima]] takes Sean as her Padawan learner. {{Circa}}<ref name="Sean Padawan">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since [[Sean]] was seventeen [[Standard year|years]] old in ''The Vow of Silver Dawn'', and the webnovel states that Sean was taken by [[Jedi Master]] [[Mostima]] as her [[Padawan]] learner when Sean was fourteen years old, Sean's apprenticeship began around 203 BBY.</ref>
*The Devaronian [[Begamor underworld]]&ndash;serial killer [[Bellon]] is offered a shelter by Jamie Brasson, who hires Bellon as one of his chefs.<ref name="Bellon">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Bellon]] had worked as a [[chef]] under [[Jamie Brasson]] for over three [[Standard year|years]], Bellon must have been employed in that capacity in 203 BBY.</ref>
