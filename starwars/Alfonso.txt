'''Alfonso''' was an [[Ithorian/Legends|Ithorian]] [[Spanish]]-[[Language/Legends|language]] [[Instructor/Legends|instructor]] and barber. They were responsible for [[Teacher/Legends|teaching]] the [[Protocol droid/Legends|protocol droid]] [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] Spanish&mdash;an experience C-3PO recalled when he was purchased by the [[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]] [[Owen Lars/Legends|Owen Lars]] in [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]]. In [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]], Alfonso gave [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Galactic Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] the idea to hire [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] to track down the remnants of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|I am C-3PO, human cyborg relations, and I'm fluent in over 6 million forms of&mdash;''"<br />"''Uh-oh! En este clase, nosotros hablamos en español. Bueno? Bueno.|C-3PO and Alfonso|<ref name="RobotChickenIII">''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]''</ref>}}
[[File:AlfonsoSi3PO.png|thumb|left|300px|Alfonso teaches C-3PO Spanish.]]
By [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]],<ref name="RC3Date">The skit featuring Alfonso in ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]'' takes place during and prior to the early events of {{Film|IV}}, which ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' dates to [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]].</ref> Alfonso served as an [[Instructor/Legends|instructor]] for a [[Spanish]]-[[Language/Legends|language]] course in which [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], [[Lobot/Legends|Lobot]], [[Ponda Baba/Legends|Ponda Baba]], and [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] were students. While C-3PO was introducing himself to the class via [[Galactic Basic Standard/Legends|Galactic Basic Standard]], Alfonso interjected, requesting that the [[Protocol droid/Legends|protocol droid]] do so in Spanish. They then asked C-3PO what his name was in Spanish and misunderstood the letter "C" in the [[Droid/Legends|droid's]] response as the Spanish word "sí." When C-3PO desperately attempted to correct Alfonso, the [[Teacher/Legends|instructor]] grew impatient and ordered C-3PO to take his seat. R2-D2 then approached Alfonso, who angrily demanded that the [[Astromech droid/Legends|astromech droid]] utilize Spanish as well. C-3PO recalled his class with Alfonso<ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> in 0 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]];<ref name="RC3Date" /> when the [[Moisture farmer/Legends|moisture farmer]] [[Owen Lars/Legends|Owen Lars]] asked the droid if he spoke Spanish while purchasing him, he dejectedly responded "Sí&hellip; 3PO."<ref name="RobotChickenIII" />

In [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]],<ref name="RC2Date">The skit featuring Alfonso in ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode II]]'' takes place during the events of {{Film|V}}, which ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' dates to [[3 ABY/Legends|3 ABY]].</ref> Alfonso was employed as a barber in service to [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Galactic Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]]. During an appointment between the two, Palpatine informed Alfonso that the former's [[Hair/Legends|hair]] was a mess as he had not had [[Time/Legends|time]] to cut it prior. The barber reassured their client that they would have him looking perfect and got started on performing the haircut. As Alfonso worked, they asked Palpatine how the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] was doing, reminding Palpatine that he had been excited about the [[Superweapon/Legends|superweapon]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]]. The [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] sullenly responded that they were already building a [[Death Star II/Legends|second one]] and became aggravated upon mentioning his [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]]. Vader then called the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]], and Alfonso interjected with the exclamation "Uh-oh!" throughout various points of the tense conversation.<ref name="RobotChickenII" />

[[File:AlfonsoPalpatine.png|thumb|right|300px|Alfonso works on Palpatine's hair.]]
After Palpatine hung up, Alfonso gave their client the idea to use [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] to track down members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]], stating that their [[Alfonso's sister|sister]] was dating one. Palpatine agreed with the idea and jokingly asked Alfonso what their [[Midi-chlorian/Legends|midi-chlorian]] count was before inquiring how they would go about hiring a [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] [[Hunting/Legends|hunter]]. The barber responded that Palpatine should simply put an ad in the newspaper, and he would find the Rebels in no time. Palpatine exclaimed that he liked the plan and then complimented Alfonso on their work with his hair.<ref name="RobotChickenII" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Oh, no! You're looking for some guys? What about a bounty hunter?''"<br />"''A what, now?''"<br />"''You know, a guy who looks for a guy for money. My sister's dating one.|Alfonso and Palpatine|<ref name="RobotChickenII" />}}
Alfonso was an [[Ithorian/Legends|Ithorian]] with [[Color/Legends|brown]] skin and black [[Eye/Legends|eyes]] with yellow pupils. Alfonso was a cheerful individual who was capable of giving advice to and even calming Emperor Palpatine during moments of stress.<ref name="RobotChickenII" /> However, Alfonso could become aggravated, having grown frustrated during a lengthy miscommunication between themself and C-3PO.<ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> They were capable of speaking both Galactic Basic Standard<ref name="RobotChickenII" /> and Spanish, the latter of which they were fluent enough in to teach.<ref name="RobotChickenIII" />

==Equipment==
While teaching the Spanish class<ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> by 0 BBY,<ref name="RC3Date" /> Alfonso wore a black [[Jumpsuit/Legends|jumpsuit]] and a yellow [[Jacket/Legends|jacket]] with white trimming over the top of it.<ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> While working as a barber<ref name="RobotChickenII" /> in 3 ABY,<ref name="RC2Date" /> Alfonso wore black [[Clothing/Legends|pants]] and a white-and-blue striped [[shirt]] with a white overcoat, as well as a black [[bow-tie]]. They utilized tools such as a spray bottle, a comb, and [[scissors]].<ref name="RobotChickenII" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alfonso first appeared in a sketch from the [[2008]] sketch comedy special ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode II]]''. The character was voiced by [[Hugh Davidson]].<ref name="RobotChickenII" /> Davidson reprised his role as the character in the [[2010]] sequel ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]''.<ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> Alfonso's speech is represented by a paper-craft mouth on the front of their head in both specials,<ref name="RobotChickenII" /><ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> as with all characters in [[Wikipedia:Robot Chicken|''Robot Chicken'']];<ref name="AdultSwim">{{WebCite|url=https://www.adultswim.com/videos/robot-chicken|text=''Robot Chicken''|work=[[Wikipedia:Adult Swim|Adult Swim]]|archivedate=20231021192522}}</ref> however, Ithorians [[canon]]ically have two [[Mouth/Legends|mouths]] located on either side of their necks.<ref name="EssentialGuide">''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]''</ref> Due to the parodic nature of ''Robot Chicken'', this article assumes Alfonso's paper-craft mouth is not a literal depiction of their anatomy.
