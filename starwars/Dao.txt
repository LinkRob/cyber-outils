'''Dao''' was a [[human]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[admiral]] within the [[Republic Navy|naval branch]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|We're in trouble down here. We need those supplies immediately.''"<br />"''I understand, General, but I am in no position to help. We are critically low on fuel and ammunition.''"<br />"''Contact the Jedi Council again. Tell them reinforcements must be dispatched without delay.|Ima-Gun Di and Admiral Dao, during the Battle of Ryloth|<ref name="Supply Lines" />}}
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Dao participated in a [[planet]]ary [[Battle of Ryloth|engagement]] of the world of [[Ryloth]]<ref name="Supply Lines" />, where he<ref name="SL Gallery" /> [[Commanding officer|commanded]] a relief supply [[Dao's fleet|fleet]] of Republic [[Star Destroyer]]s. During the battle, the [[Republic officer]] and his [[fleet]] attempted to repel a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Ryloth blockade|blockade]] of [[warship]]s led by [[Neimoidian]] [[Captain]] [[Mar Tuuk]] in Ryloth's [[orbit]].<ref name="Supply Lines" />

During the battle, the Republic [[officer]]'s forces had been rendered critically low on resources and were in no position to provide aid to the forces of [[Jedi General]] [[Ima-Gun Di]] on the surface. Soon thereafter, Dao was [[Death|killed]] amid a conversation with the [[Jedi High Council]], when an explosion claimed his [[Dao's flagship|Star Destroyer]]. As a result of Dao's death, the Separatist [[blockade]] was able to establish a planetary defense in orbit, while Di's forces continued to battle the [[Separatist Droid Army]] below.<ref name="Supply Lines" />

==Personality and traits==
Dao was a [[human]] with [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], blue [[eye]]s, and light skin.<ref name="Supply Lines" />

==Equipment==
Dao wore the standard [[Republic military uniforms|military uniforms]] of the Republic's military branch. The uniform was also accompanied by a [[Rank insignia plaque|insignia plaque]] showing three red and two blue squares in order to indicate Dao's rank of [[admiral]].<ref name="Supply Lines" />
