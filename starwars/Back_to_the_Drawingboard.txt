"'''Back to the Drawingboard'''" was a feature article in [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 10|''Star Wars Galaxy Magazine'' 10]], written by [[Don Charles]]. It discussed the development of [[Dark Horse Comics]]' [[Star Wars: A New Hope - The Special Edition|''Special Edition'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].
