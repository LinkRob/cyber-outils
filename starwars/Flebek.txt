'''Flebek''' was a [[Meftian]] who served as a [[general]] in the [[Confederacy military|military]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Around [[20 BBY]], she was sent by the Confederacy's [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to the [[planet]] [[Retta]] in order to [[Mission to Retta|discover]] the secret to the [[Segredo]] [[Kreeda]]'s invisibility, but the [[Jedi General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Yoda]] intervened in her plans. The Jedi deceived Flebek into believing that the secret was just a Jedi trick and forcing her to flee the planet.

==Biography==

Flebek was a [[Meftian]] [[general]] in the [[Confederacy military|military]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. She was sent to the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] of [[Retta]]<ref name="Hide and Seek" /> around [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Hide and Seek]]" to around [[20 BBY]].</ref> by the [[Head of State]] of the Confederacy and [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to find a hidden secret located on the planet. She deployed [[B1-series battle droid]]s to the surface of the planet to locate this secret. A [[OOM command battle droid]] contacted Flebek via a handheld [[holoprojector]] and reported to her that the [[jungle]] they were investigating was uninhabited with no signs of [[Sentience|sentient]] life forms. Flebek ordered the [[commander]] to continue the search and reminded the [[battle droid]] that the secret the Confederacy sought must be found and ended the call. The commander noted that the general was being snappy and ordered the battle droids to continue the search.<ref name="Hide and Seek" />

Shortly after, the Jedi overheard a battle droid reporting [[lightsaber]] damage to Flebek. Flebek then came to the conclusion that there were on the planet. Flebek then overheard Skywalker who was wearing a [[cloaking cap]]-which was a fake [[cloaking device]] that was a [[Kyuzo war shield]]- and claimed that it was making him invisible. Flebek fell for the trick and ran after Skywalker but was surprised when he was not invisible. Yoda then secretly eliminated Flebek's battle droids but Skywalker made the Separatist general believe that the [[droid]]s had vanished. Skywalker then activated [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]] and suggested to Flebek that he make her disappear too. She was then contacted over a [[comlink]] to report her situation when she aborted the mission, believing that the secret was just a Jedi trick and fled the planet.<ref name="Hide and Seek" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Wait a minute- you're not invisible.|Flebek to Skywalker|<ref name="Hide and Seek" />}}
Flebek was a Meftian with [[Color|white]] skin and grey [[eye]]s. Flebek gave orders to the battle droids that served under her and was known for being snappy. After being tricked by the Jedi she feared for her safety and fled the planet, being noted as a coward by Skywalker.<ref name="Hide and Seek" />

==Equipment==
Flebek wore a black and red [[Clothing|outfit]] with black [[boot]]s along with grey [[belt]] and pants. She also wore a [[cape]] of red and black. She used a [[blaster pistol]] and a comlink.<ref name="Hide and Seek" />
