'''Bioluminescent bacteria''' were a type of [[Bioluminescence|light-emitting]] [[bacteria]] that formed colonies. They could be found in the [[Seikara Caverns]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]]. Two [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Empire (Rei'izu)|Imperial]] [[Imperial unification|unification]], the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] noticed the bacteria in stalagtites and phytokarst crevices formed by previous generations of [[lichen]] in the Seikara Caverns while [[Catastrophe in the Seikara Caverns|searching]] the [[cave]]s for a shard of the [[kyber mirror of Shinsui Temple]].<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bioluminescent bacteria appeared in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" />
