'''Clawfish chowder''' was a type of [[chowder]] that the [[Jedi Master]] and [[chef]] [[Porter Engle]] cooked during the [[High Republic Era]]. During the [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Engle made a sharp remark to [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] about his presumed-[[Death|dead]] Master, [[Loden Greatstorm]]. [[Jedi Knight]] [[Nib Assek]] was displeased with the comment, indicating that she hoped his clawfish chowder was better than his empathy skills.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Clawfish chowder was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="TRS" /> for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
