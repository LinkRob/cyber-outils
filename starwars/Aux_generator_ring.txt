An '''aux generator ring''' was a component used to repair [[starship]]s. In [[34 ABY]], the [[racer]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] was informed by his boss, [[Jarek Yeager]], that repairing the [[racing starfighter]] ''[[Fireball]]'' would require ten aux generator rings. After, Xiono went with the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Neeku Vozo]] to the [[Office of Acquisitions]] to acquire the needed materials. Though the shop's proprietors [[Flix]] and [[Orka]] refused to give him the parts if he could not [[Currency|pay]], they eventually gave him some second-hand materials in exchange for a [[Bitey|gorg]] for [[Diet|lunch]]. Aux generator rings were also listed on materials lists stored on [[datapad]]s kept by the mechanic [[Tamara Ryvora]] and the Office of Acquisitions.

==Description==
An aux generator ring was a [[starship]] component that could be used to repair [[racing starfighter]]s.<ref name="The Recruit" />

==History==
{{Quote|We are here to obtain parts for one of Yeager's ships.''"<br />"''Uh, its not the "''Fireball''," is it?''"<br />"''Yes, yes, it is.''"<br />[Flix and Orka burst out laughing]|quoteend=0|Neeku Vozo and Orka in the Office of Acquisitions|audio=FireballParts3-TheRecruit.flac|<ref name="The Recruit" />}}
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ResistanceDate|1|2}}</ref> while in [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station]] on the [[Trantor-class supertanker fuel depot|supertanker fuel depot]] ''[[Colossus]]'', the [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Jarek Yeager]] informed his employee, the [[racer]] [[Kazuda Xiono]], that he would need ten aux generator rings in order to repair the [[Sky race|racing]] [[starfighter]] ''[[Fireball]]'' so it could be flown. As Yeager continued expounding on the other parts needed from the list on his [[datapad]], Xiono's excitement at the prospect dampened. The racer [[Torra Doza]], who was visiting the repair garage, expressed her surprise at the amount of parts needed to mend the ''Fireball''.<ref name="The Recruit" />

[[File:XionoNeekuParts-TheRecruitPart2.png|thumb|right|300px|Kazuda Xiono and Neeku Vozo visited the Office of Acquisitions to acquire parts for the ''Fireball''{{'s}} repair, which included ten aux generator rings.]]
Following this, Xiono and the mechanic [[Neeku Vozo]] visited the ''Colossus''{{'s}} [[Office of Acquisitions]] to obtain the needed parts, including the ten aux generator rings. Upon request, the shop's owners&mdash;[[Flix]] and [[Orka]]&mdash;began collecting them from around the store. However, the pair abruptly ceased when Xiono told Vozo he could not [[Currency|pay]] for the items. Ultimately, as a favor, the proprietors gave Xiono and Vozo an assortment of second-hand parts in exchange for bringing them a [[Bitey|gorg]] for lunch. Afterward, the pair kept the gorg as a pet because they had already eaten. Xiono and Vozo then used the parts to repair the ''Fireball''.<ref name="The Recruit" />

Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date2">{{ResistanceDate|1|13}}</ref> the mechanic [[Tamara Ryvora]] worked to repair the ''Fireball''.<ref name="Dangerous Business">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}}</ref> She<ref name="The Recruit" /> kept a list of parts on her datapad recorded in the [[Aurebesh]] [[writing]] system, which called for sixteen aux generator rings. Another datapad used to document the Office of Acquisitions' inventory also listed fifteen aux generator rings.<ref name="Dangerous Business" />
