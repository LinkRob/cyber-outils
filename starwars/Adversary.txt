The '''Adversary''', also known as the '''Darkness''', was a [[deity]] worshiped by the [[Ewok]] [[species]].<ref name="Divine Intervention" /> He represented darkness and [[death]] and was the rival of the [[Golden One]], though he was not evil, according to the [[shaman]] [[Logray]], who believed that the Adversary simply had a role, being part of the natural cycle of death and rebirth.<ref name="Ewoks 2" /> According to Ewok mythology, the Adversary made a deal with the Golden One to share the [[moon]] [[Endor]] equally, a deal that Logray thought was broken during the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Occupation of Endor|invasion and occupation of Endor]].<ref name="Divine Intervention" />

Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the Imperial [[Tern Koyatta]] led a team to retrieve an Imperial weapons depot on Endor for [[Ubrik Adelhard]]. Upon landing, the team was attacked by [[Dulok]]s. After they defeat and kill most of the attackers, one clings to life long enough to identify one of their members - the [[droid]] [[bounty hunter]] [[4-LOM]] - as the Adversary.<ref name="Ewoks 1">[[Ewoks (2024) 1|''Ewoks'' (2024) 1]]</ref>

Later, the group arrived at [[Bright Tree Village]], and 4-LOM presented himself as an [[Emissary]] of the Adversary and the rest of the group as his traveling court. He demanded the Ewoks assist them with locating the depot, which he called "an ancient holy site," and threatened that to deny him was to deny the Adversary.<ref name="Ewoks 2" />
