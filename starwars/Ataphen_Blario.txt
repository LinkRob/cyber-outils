'''Ataphen Blario''' was an individual who served as a [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] in the [[Resistance]]'s [[Resistance Starfighter Corps|Starfighter Corps]]. They wore a [[Color|yellow]] and gray [[flight helmet]], with a black [[Rebel Alliance starbird]] on top, and the [[Aurebesh]] inscription {{AurText|roaehal}} on the right-hand side that translated to "ROÆHAL";<ref name="TROS VD" /> the same inscription as fellow Resistance pilot [[Poe Dameron]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker">{{Film|IX}}</ref> They shared the last name with another [[pilot]] named [[Ronith Blario]].<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
"ROÆHAL", the inscription on Poe Dameron's flight helmet, and thereby Blario's as well, is a reference to "Ro & Hal", the sons of costume designer [[Glyn Dillon]].<ref name="Dillon">{{Instagram|username=glyn__dillon|url=p/BYtRMGjBZap|name=[[Glyn Dillon]]|date=September 6, 2017|quote=This is a new promotional poster for 'The Last Jedi' - I like it because they usually have Poe facing the other way... This way you get to see my boys names in Aurebesh ("Ro & Hal").|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/frsEk}}</ref>''
