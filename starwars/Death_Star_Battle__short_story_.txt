"'''Death Star Battle'''" is a [[2017]] short story that was published in the anthology ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back]]''. The story was written by [[Rebecca L. Schmidt]].<ref name="5MSWSSB">''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories Strike Back]]''</ref>

==Continuity==
*Destpite the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' losing its [[rectenna]] during the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> "Death Star Battle" depicts the ''Falcon'' fully intact after the battle.
*"Death Star Battle" states that [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] hadn't seen a [[Death Star]] before, however the ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]'' short story "[[The Angle]]" previously established that Calrissian had seen a [[holovid]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]].<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=The Angle|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
