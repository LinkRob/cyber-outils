'''Andrés Genolet''' is an [[Wikipedia:Artist|artist]] who pencilled and inked for the [[2020]] [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic books]] series [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']], including the [[Star Wars (2020) 26|twenty-sixth]],<ref name="SW26 Marvel" /> [[Star Wars (2020) 27|twenty-seventh]],<ref name="SW27 Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/95847/star_wars_2020_27|text=Star Wars (2020) #27}}</ref> [[Star Wars (2020) 28|twenty-eighth]],<ref name="SW28 Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/95848/star_wars_2020_28|text=Star Wars (2020) #28}}</ref> [[Star Wars (2020) 30|thirtieth]],<ref name="SW30 Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103037/star_wars_2020_30|text=Star Wars (2020) #30}}</ref> and [[Star Wars (2020) 31|thirty-first issue]].<ref name="SW31 Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103038/star_wars_2020_31|text=Star Wars (2020) #31}}</ref>
