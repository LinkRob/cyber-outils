'''Balada's Bacta''' was a type of [[bacta]]<ref name="SWH" />&mdash;a liquid healing agent<ref name="Bacta Tank DB">{{Databank|bacta-tank|Bacta Tank}}</ref>&mdash;that shared its name with<ref name="SWH" /> the [[Grand Arena]]<ref name="Secrets">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref> co-owner [[Balada]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="SWH" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> the bacta was advertised by the [[Dowutin]] announcer [[Boz Vega]] at the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] during its matches.<ref name="SWH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Balada's Bacta was first introduced in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]]<ref name="SWH" /> and initially published by [[Zynga]] on [[November 16]], [[2021]], as a [[Wikipedia:Soft launch|soft launch]]<ref name="Soft">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> The game was then taken offline on [[October 1]], [[2025]].<ref name="Offline">{{Hunters|url=news/sunset-on-vespaara|text=Sunset on Vespaara}}</ref> It was mentioned in one of the interchangeable audio lines Vega says during gameplay, though the capitalization was never specified.<ref name="SWH" />
