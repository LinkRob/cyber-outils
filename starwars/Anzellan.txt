'''Anzellans''' were a diminutive [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> Their size was comparable to infant members of other species.<ref name="LOTJ" /> The [[eye]]s of Anzellans had floating corneal micro-lenses that could see microscopic details<ref name="TROS VD" /> and they  also had [[Color|purple]] [[blood]].<ref name="LOTJ" />

==Anzellans in the galaxy==
[[File:AnzellanDroidsmith-TMUSC.png|thumb|right|225px|Minch, the Anzellan droidsmith]]
The tiny beings often took up tech jobs such as the [[droidsmith]] [[Babu Frik]] during the [[New Republic Era]] and [[Cold War]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> and the [[slicer]] [[Ginntho (Anzellan)|Ginntho]].<ref name="Inquisitor">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> One also worked on a [[Solar Array 22-X]] station as a crewmember in the [[Hetzal system]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="LOTJ" /> The Anzellan [[engineer]] [[Shug Drabor]] designed the ''[[Halcyon]]'' during that same era.<ref name="Midnight Horizon">''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]''</ref> A crew of [[Anzellan Droidsmiths|Anzellan droidsmiths]] operated on [[Nevarro]] during the earlier [[New Republic]] Era.<ref name="Chapter 17">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the original idea for Babu Frik, his species had been used to repair [[Star Destroyer]]s for generations due to their small size. Because of this, they had been able to discover a weakness in the [[First Order]]'s technology, and built a "sabotage brick" that could disable the entire First Order across the galaxy.<ref name="Art of TROS">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]''</ref>
