The '''Alpha Droid''' was a type of [[viper probe droid]] created for the [[Galactic Empire]] by the [[Rodian]] [[droidsmith]] [[Gedeek Obaz]] on [[Akiva]]. Gedeek designed the droid after he was arrested on [[Canto Bight]] and forced to work for the Empire. Due to his lack of a [[Core Worlds|Core World]] education, Gedeek felt he had to prove himself to his colleagues in the Empire; as a result, the [[droid]] he designed was faster and smarter that other viper droids. It could also hunt in packs, making it a dangerous new weapon for the Empire. However, the Alpha Droids caused problems for the Empire, because although improved, they were difficult to control. The droids attacked crime syndicates in Akiva without receiving orders to do so, which made the [[Hutt Clan]] want Obaz [[Death|dead]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Gedeek had begun to regret designing the droid, and teamed up with a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel group]]. He later recruited his former partner [[Kay Vess]] to help infiltrate [[Kassina Outpost]] and help him reprogram the Alpha Droid and hand it over to the rebellion. After Gedeek reprogrammed the droid, Vess escorted it to the base's hangar to be handed over to the rebels of [[Typhoon Squadron]]. Along the way to the hangar, the Alpha Droid took control of its fellow viper droids. When the group reached the hangar, a large firefight broke out with the rebels and the droids being victorious.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Alpha Droid appeared in the main quest "The Viper" in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
