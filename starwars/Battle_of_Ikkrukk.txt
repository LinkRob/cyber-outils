The '''Battle of Ikkrukk''', also known as the '''Battle of Grail City'''<ref name="Poe Dameron 31" /> or '''Mission to Ikkrukk''',<ref name="Timelines" /> was a battle fought<ref name="Poe Dameron 31" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] on the [[planet]] [[Ikkrukk]] when the First Order declared to the [[Ikkrukkian|inhabitants]] of the planet that they now fell under the control of the First Order.<ref name="Poe Dameron 30" /> Thanks to the help of [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] and the return of its [[commander]], [[Poe Dameron]], who arrived shortly after the Battle of Crait<ref name="Poe Dameron 31" /> that had been waged concurrent to the Battle of Grail City,<ref name="Timelines" /> the city's defenses were able to destroy the attacking [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|''Maxima-A'' class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Fortitude]]''. Overjoyed, [[Prime Minister]] [[Grist]] promised to help the Resistance in any way she could.<ref name="Poe Dameron 31" />

Only a few hours after the victory over the First Order, Grist went back on her word, alleging there was too much damage to Grail City to afford supporting the Resistance and that such aid would only give the First Order more cause to return. According to her, Grail City needed to rebuild as quickly as possible as a sign that the First Order would not overwhelm her people. To Dameron, however, her dismissal was a case of someone forgetting their promises as soon as they were out of immediate danger. He further suspected that First Order sympathizers existed within her administrative cabinet and would need to be removed.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
