'''Bogaranths''' were large, quadrupedal carnivorous creatures. They produced noxious [[slime]] when they were hungry, which was frequently. The [[Nihil]] used bogaranths in a [[battle rink]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'', wherein one contestant would ride the creature while a second served as a [[slime runner]] who guided the creature while attempting to avoid being eaten.<ref name="HRA 3" />
