'''Amber''' was an [[Color|orange]] material that could be found at locations such as [[Trammis III]].<ref name="TLJVD" /> Various [[Force-sensitive]] individuals were encased in amber beneath [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the oceanic [[moon]] [[Nur]]<ref name="Kenobi">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}</ref> in [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Kenobi Date">{{KenobiDate|IV}}</ref> A similar material known as [[Amberine]], also sometimes referred to as "Amber Stones," could be found within the caves of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] moon of [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Amber shared its name with a color featured in the names of various [[Clothing|costumes]] and [[weapon]]s used by [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]]s of the [[Grand Arena]] on [[Vespaara]] including [[Charr]], [[Zaina]], and [[Imara Vex]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> [[DJ]] owned a [[DJ's ring|ring]] that contained amber<ref name="TLJVD" /> by [[34 ABY]].<ref name="TLJ Date">{{FilmDate|VIII}}</ref>

Various other things found across the galaxy seemed to share some relationship with amber, including [[ambercryst]], a fossilized [[bone]] marrow substance known found within [[Ossein Plains]] on the [[Boneworld]],<ref name="Blade 1">[[The High Republic — The Blade 1|''The High Republic — The Blade'' 1]]</ref> [[Armored amberfly|amberflies]], a [[species]] of [[Fly|flies]] found within the [[Gemstone|crystalline]] caverns of [[Ootoo Prime]],<ref name="Mine">{{YJA|Mine and Ours}}</ref> and [[amberwise]], one of two colors used to describe the appearance of the upper levels of the vibrant spires and platforms on the planet [[Ylagia]], the other being [[melancholigo]].<ref name="MoF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>
