The '''mother''' of [[Breha Organa]] was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] from [[Alderaan]]. As was customary among [[Alderaanian]]s, she introduced her daughter to pacifist values.<ref name="Leia" /> Her [[Breha Organa's father|husband]] remained onworld during her entire pregnancy and for several [[Standard month|months]] afterwards. She later told Breha that after she was [[Reproduction|born]], she had a shift in her thinking where she thought about the future in a way she usually didn't.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

At some point before the [[mission to Pijal]]<ref name="MA">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> in around [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> Breha accompanied her mother to help negotiate a treaty.<ref name="MA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The mother was first mentioned in ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', a [[2017]] young-adult novel written by [[Claudia Gray]].<ref name="Leia" />
