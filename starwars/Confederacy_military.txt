The '''Confederacy military''', also known as the '''Separatist military''' or '''CIS military''', was the armed forces of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], comprised by various branches including the [[Separatist Droid Army]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]]. The [[Droid]] [[Army]] was composed of the [[Techno Union Droid Army]], the [[Trade Federation military]], the [[Geonosian droid army]], the [[IG-series battle droid army]], the [[Retail Caucus droid army]], and the forces of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]], [[Corporate Alliance]], and the [[Commerce Guild]], while other [[Planet|worlds]], [[Star system|systems]], and [[sector]]s also provided local military units.

==History==
===Origins===
{{Quote|The Separatist Alliance congealed into the Confederacy of Independent Systems, a coalition of loosely aligned worlds united for war. It pooled its resources to purchase huge quantities of battle droids, creating a ready-to-deploy army.|''A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy''|<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Battle_Droid_SWD_2018_Season_2_OP_Elite_Prize_Card.png|left|thumb|180x180px|B1 battle droids on Naboo ten years before the Clone Wars]]
[[32 BBY|Thirteen years]] before the [[Republic Era|Fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]],<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> the [[Trade Federation]] was allowed to maintain an [[Trade Federation Droid Army|army]] of [[B1-series battle droid]]s<ref name="Trade Federation landing ship">{{SWE|technology|tradefederationlandingcraft|Trade Federation landing ship}}</ref> to protect valuable shipments<ref name="STAP">{{SWE|technology|stap|STAP}}</ref> from piracy.<ref name="Trade Federation landing ship" /> The first major deployment of the Trade Federation's army was during the [[Invasion of Naboo|blockade and subsequent invasion]] of [[Naboo]]. The army fought against both the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces|Royal Security Forces]] and the [[Gungan]] [[Gungan Grand Army|Grand Army]]. The [[Trade Federation fleet]] was used to [[blockade]] the [[planet]]. However, the [[Trade Federation military|Federation military]] was eventually defeated, as the young [[Force-sensitive]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] destroyed the ''[[Vuutun Palaa]]'', resulting in the [[battle droid]]s shutting down.<ref name="Episode I" />

Other conglomerates, such as the [[Techno Union]], had their own [[Techno Union Droid Army|armies]] to protect their interests. During the [[Separatist Crisis]], the Techno Union, Trade Federation and the other conglomerates then pledged their armies to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and the emerging [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Together they formed the basis for the military<ref name="Episode II" /> The [[Retail Caucus]] also pledged their [[Retail Caucus droid army|droid army]] before the [[Battle of Christophsis]].<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> For [[Standard year|years]] before the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]], the Confederacy had been secretly building and training [[commander]]s.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

===Clone Wars===
{{Main|Clone Wars}}
Following a [[Geonosian accord|meeting]] on [[Geonosis]] between Dooku and his allies, the Republic learned that the Confederacy would take the shipment and use their army to force the Republic to their demands. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] was captured, the Republic's [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] was given emergency powers by the [[Galactic Senate]] to create a [[Grand Army of the Republic]],<ref name="Episode II" /> using an army ordered by [[Jedi]] Master [[Sifo-Dyas]].<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref> Both the [[Separatist Droid Army]] and the Republic's Grand Army fought against each other on Geonosis in the [[First Battle of Geonosis|opening battle]] of the Clone Wars.<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:2nd_Battle_of_Geonosis_2.png|thumb|right|238px|Battle droids combat Republic forces at the Second Battle of Geonosis.]]
Despite the defeat, the Confederacy was able to seize control of the major [[Hyperspace route|hyperspace lanes]], thus separating the Republic from the majority of its [[troop]]s.<ref name="TCWf">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> The military would continue to be a effective fighting force against the Republic.<ref name="Episode III" />

While the Republic had more victories than losses, the droid armies under [[Grievous]]'s command would win multiple victories against the Republic, such as his [[Capture of Eeth Koth|capture]] of [[Eeth Koth]]<ref name="Grievous Intrigue">{{TCW|Grievous Intrigue}}</ref> and his [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|bombing]] of [[Coruscant]]'s [[central power distribution grid]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> Among Grievous's most daring assaults was at the war's end when he launched an [[Battle of Coruscant|assault]] on Coruscant and attempted to escape with the Supreme Chancellor. However, it failed when Kenobi and Skywalker killed Dooku and rescued the Chancellor.<ref name="Episode III" /> As well as Dooku being killed, the Separatists lost a third of the fleet they had sent into the Battle of Coruscant.<ref name="Bust Collection 37">{{BustCollectionCite|37|Star Wars Universe}}</ref>

Following Dooku's death, Grievous became the new [[Head of State]] of the Confederacy.<ref name="Front Lines" /> However, his reign was short-lived as it came to a swift end when Kenobi found and [[Battle of Utapau|destroyed]] him on [[Utapau]]. The military then fell under the command of the Council. However, their time ended when Lord Sidious's new [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Darth]] Vader [[Mission to Mustafar|eliminated]] them on [[Mustafar]]. Vader also transmitted a master control signal that deactivated much of the Separatist military's droid forces. The elimination of the Separatists and the [[Great Jedi Purge]] enabled Palpatine to implement his [[Galactic Empire]] with himself as [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]].<ref name="Episode III" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|The Separatist war machines were shut down. Their droid warships were confiscated or destroyed.''"<br />"''Most were. Clearly, some escaped our notice or were made available by insiders to a host of new enemies.|[[Ars Dangor]] and [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
Despite the dissolution of the Confederacy, its legacy still remained in the form of [[Separatist holdouts]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> [[Stormtrooper]]s of the Empire were tasked with destroying surviving battle droids on sight.<ref name="The Last Battle">{{Rebels|The Last Battle}}</ref> During the [[Imperial Era]], at least three anti-Imperial insurgent groups, [[Cham Syndulla]]'s [[Free Ryloth Movement]],<ref name="LOTS">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref> [[Berch Teller]]'s [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> and [[Nevil Cygni]] made use of Separatist equipment.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>

[[File:Rebels_and_Rex_part_as_friends_with_Kalani.jpg|thumb|left|223x223px|During the Imperial Era, Captain Rex and General Kalani made their peace.]]
In [[2 BBY]], the former [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and members of the [[Spectres]] rebel cell encountered the [[ST-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|super tactical droid]] general [[Kalani]] and his [[Agamar garrison|garrison]] of battle droids on [[Agamar]] during a [[Mission to Agamar (2 BBY)|salvage mission]] to obtain [[proton bomb]]s from a derelict Separatist [[Unidentified DH-Omni Support Vessel (Agamar)|supply ship]]. Kalani had overrode the command signal on Agamar, believing it to be a Republic trick. Determined to end the Clone Wars on his terms, Kalani forced Rex and the Spectres to take part in a war game. The Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] convinced Kalani and Rex to put aside differences and make peace. The two sides then joined forces to escape an Imperial attack on Agamar.<ref name="The Last Battle" />

The future [[New Republic]] and [[Resistance]] starfighter pilot [[Temmin Wexley]] also rebuilt and befriended a heavily-modified B1 battle droid named [[Mister Bones]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> Mister Bones fought during the [[Galactic Civil War]] and met his end at the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

==Organization==
===Leadership===
{{Quote|The duke that Oenti mentioned. Is he the leader of the Separatists?''"<br />"''No, that's Count Dooku. The other real driving force of their military is General Grievous.|Commander [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] and Jedi General Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="Thrawn Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Rising_Malevolence.png|thumb|right|270px|Grievous and Dooku were major leaders of the CIS military.]]
As [[Head of State]] of the Confederacy and leader of the [[Separatist Senate]],<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> Dooku held the most authority over<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> Confederacy military<ref name="SWE supply ship" /> operations and its strategies<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth" /> and sat as a member of [[Separatist high command]].<ref name="Heroes and Legends">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/8/1/heroes-and-legends-1|text=Heroes and Legends}}</ref> The Confederacy's greatest general was the [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]] Grievous,<ref name="Lair of Grievous">{{TCW|Lair of Grievous}}</ref> who served as [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> Together, Dooku and Grievous shaped the Confederacy military and pushed it forward.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" />

Under Grievous and Dooku served a number of high-ranking officers including [[Supreme Leader (Confederacy of Independent Systems)|Supreme Leader]] [[Asajj Ventress]],<ref name="TCWf" /> General [[Kleeve]],<ref name="Kanan 1">[[Kanan 1|''Kanan'' 1]]</ref> [[Duke]] [[Solha]],<ref name="Thrawn Alliances" /> and [[Colonel]] [[Coburn Sear]].<ref name="Kanan 9">[[Kanan 9|''Kanan'' 9]]</ref> Members of the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] such as [[Viceroy of the Trade Federation]] [[Nute Gunray]],<ref name="Bombad Jedi">{{TCW|Bombad Jedi}}</ref> [[Emir]] [[Wat Tambor]],<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref> High Admiral Reaves, and [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]] were also known to take command of their military's various operations personally.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref>

Although primarily composed of droid forces, the Confederacy military also consisted of the local armed forces of the worlds under its control. The [[Umbaran Militia]],<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> [[Zygerrian Royal Guard|Zygerrian royal guards]],<ref name="Slaves of the Republic">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref> and [[Warrior caste (Geonosian)|Geonosian warriors]] were several that provided leaders and additional support.<ref name="Episode II" /> Other organic personnel who would join its ranks included several [[Kage Warriors]]<ref name="Kanan 7">[[Kanan 7|''Kanan'' 7]]</ref> and the mercenary-turned-true believer [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]].<ref name="MaskofFear" />

===Army===
{{Main|Separatist Droid Army}}
{{Quote|Separatist droid armies stomped through our cities, blasting and bombing. They seemed to have no purpose. Just to destroy things. Everything they could. [&hellip;] We had an army of our own, of course, but they were beaten quickly. There seemed to be an endless supply of those robots, but not of us. Even when we stopped fighting back, the droids just kept marching and destroying.|[[Mayvlin Trillick]] describes the Separatist [[Skirmish on Oktaro|invasion]] of [[Oktaro]]|<ref name="Mighty Chewie">''[[The Mighty Chewbacca in the Forest of Fear!]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Separatists_on_Onderon.png|thumb|left|250px|Droid forces on [[Onderon]]]]
The droid army was the largest army assembled during its era,<ref name="Episode II" /> reportedly outnumbering the Republic clone army by a hundred to one.<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> The Separatist Droid Army was formed by the armies of several large corporations, including the Trade Federation Droid Army, the Techno Union Droid Army,<ref name="Episode II" /> the Retail Caucus's private droid army,<ref name="Retail Caucus Droid" /> the [[Commerce Guild]]'s army, and the army of the [[Corporate Alliance]].<ref name="Episode II" /> The ranks of the Separatists also included a limited number of flesh and [[blood]] soldiers, however, officials such as Count Dooku touted that these were volunteers rather than [[Cloning|cloned]] soldiers which had been pressed into service.<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>

===Navy===
{{Main|Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy}}
[[File:TCW_Separatist_fleet_SW.png|thumb|right|250px|A Separatist fleet during the Clone Wars]]
The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy]] saw early action in the Clone Wars during the Battle of Christophsis where [[Admiral]] [[Trench]] blockaded the planet and held off Republic forces until the destruction of his [[flagship]], the ''[[Invincible]]''.<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> One ship in the navy, the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'', terrorized Republic convoys early in the war, and was initially perceived as a "mystery weapon." It was eventually discovered, however, that the ''Malevolence'' was equipped with two [[mega-ion cannon]]s that allowed it to leave enemy vessels defenseless with a single shot.<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> The ''Malevolence'' was destroyed during the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula]].<ref name="Destroy Malevolence">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

The Separatist navy was primarily composed of [[starship]]s utilized by groups like the Trade Federation<ref name="Episode II" /> and the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]]. The capital ships were also augmented by forces of starfighters, primarily composed of [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid star fighters]] and [[droid tri-fighter]]s.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Special operations===

[[CIS Special Operations]] was the [[special forces]] branch of the CIS military.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Intelligence===
{{Main|Separatist Intelligence}}
{{Quote|I was a man of some influence in Separatist Intelligence, Bail Organa. My propaganda swayed ''worlds''. I turned brother against brother and showed the true ugliness of the Republic to its most loyal troops. It was rare for my superiors to deny me information, or to poach my agents for their own operations.|[[Bana Rhuus]], to Bail Organa|<ref name="MaskofFear" />}}
[[Separatist Intelligence]] served as the intelligence gathering arm of the CIS. It also played a role in creating and spreading Separatist [[propaganda]].<ref name="MaskofFear" />
