'''Asa Kalama''' is the Vice President and Executive for Creative and Interactive Experiences at [[Walt Disney Imagineering]].<ref name="Darth Vader" /> In his previous role as executive creative director, he led development of [[Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run|''Millennium Falcon'': Smuggler's Run]]<ref name="Smugglers">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-millennium-falcon-smugglers-run|text=Learning to Fly the ''Millennium Falcon'' at ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> and [[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]] at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="Datapad">{{WebCite|url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/herocomplex/la-et-hc-galaxys-edge-game-20190708-story.html|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge becomes a live-in video game with the Disney Parks app|author=Martens, Todd|date=2019-07-08|work=L.A. Times|archivedate=20260211065049}}</ref> Kalama also worked on [[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|''Star Wars'': Rise of the Resistance]].<ref name="Wish">{{DPB|url=disney-experiences/imagineering-a-wish-disneys-170k-wish-comes-true|text=Imagineering a Wish: Disney’s 170,000th Wish Comes True}}</ref> In [[2026]], Kalama oversaw the extensive updates to [[Disneyland]]'s version of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge,<ref name="Darth Vader" /> including a new mission for Smuggler's Run.<ref name="MandoGroguSmugglers">{{SW|url=news/millennium-falcon-smugglers-run-update-mandalorian-and-grogu|text=Join the Mandalorian and Grogu on a New Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run Mission}}</ref>
