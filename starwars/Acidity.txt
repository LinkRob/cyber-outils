The '''''Acidity''''' was a [[Star Destroyer]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It served as the personal Star [[Destroyer]] and [[flagship]] of [[Grand Moff]] [[Randd]], who [[Commanding officer|commanded]] the Imperial [[fleet]] from the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of the ''Acidity'' during the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Acidity'' was first mentioned in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

In ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', it is established that Randd commanded Imperial forces from the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Ravager]]''.<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> However, the newer book ''Star Wars: On the Front Lines'' conversely states that Randd commanded Imperial forces from the ''Acidity''.<ref name="On the Front Lines" />
