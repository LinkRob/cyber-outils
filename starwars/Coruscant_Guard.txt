The '''Coruscant Guard''' was a [[corps]] of elite [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], organized to serve as a military police force and protect the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]]. The Coruscant Guard served under the leadership of [[Clone Commander]] [[Fox]], a [[clone trooper]] known for his fierce loyalty to the Republic as well as his command style of leading from the front. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Commander]] Fox became one of the most decorated [[soldier]]s in the [[Republic Military]].

The Coruscant Guard performed various duties in their service to the Republic, such as patrolling the [[Galaxy|galactic]] capital as [[riot clone trooper]]s to assist the [[Coruscant Security Force]], conducting hostage rescue operations, and serving as guards at the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]]. They gradually supplanted the [[Senate Guard]] by accompanying [[Jedi]], [[senator]]s, and other diplomats on offworld assignments. As [[the galaxy]]-wide conflict with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] progressed, the Coruscant Guard served as [[bodyguard]]s for [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], a status they shared with the [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Chancellor's Red Guard]].

After the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Coruscant Guard was retained by the [[New Order]], and thus continued to serve as a security force on Coruscant, albeit with the new mission of enforcing the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the self-[[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] Palpatine. Fox initially continued to serve as head of the Coruscant Guard until his [[Death|execution]] at the hands of the [[Sith apprentice|Emperor's enforcer]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Should we arrest the Hutt, Senator?|Fox, to Padmé Amidala, after apprehending Ziro Desilijic Tiure|audio=ShouldWeArrestTheHutt-TCWf.flac|<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>}}
[[File:RotS_Coruscant_Guard.png|thumb|left|250px|The Coruscant Guard patrols the streets of Coruscant]]
The Coruscant Guard was a [[corps]]<ref name="BC 37" /> of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] composed of the elite and deadly [[Imperial shock trooper|clone shock troopers]].<ref name="Databank Coruscant Guard" /> Led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Fox]], the Coruscant Guards served as a Security Force on [[Coruscant]], the [[Galactic Republic]] [[capital]] [[Planet|world]] where their main focus was protecting government buildings and vital infrastructure.<ref name="Databank Fox" /> However, as the war progressed and the [[Senate Guard]] became increasingly ineffective and corrupt, the Coruscant Guard found itself often supplementing the Senate Guard and eventually fully taking over their function by serving as [[bodyguard]]s for members of the [[Galactic Senate]] and the [[Supreme Chancellor]]. Shock troopers would also patrol the streets of Coruscant alongside the civilian [[police officer]]s and [[GU-series Guardian police droid]]s of the [[Coruscant Security Force]], assisting them in pursuing suspects and in providing urban [[Riot clone trooper|riot control]].<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" /> Coruscant Guards would also be responsible for guarding the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]],<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref> conducting hostage rescue missions,<ref name="Hostage">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref> serve as escorts for [[Politics|diplomatic]] missions,<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> and perform military police functions.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />

==History==
===The Clone Wars===
====Rescuing a Senator====
{{Quote|Is anybody home?|C-3PO, after Coruscant Guards blast open the door|audio=IsAnybodyHome-TCWf.flac|<ref name="TCW film" />}}
In the waning days of the Galactic Republic, the [[Separatist Crisis|Secessionist Movement]], under the secret guidance of the [[Sith]], caused the Galactic Senate to debate [[Military Creation Act|establishing]] a military to defend the Republic. When the [[Jedi]] discovered that the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] had a [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]], the Senate granted Supreme Chancellor [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] emergency powers. Palpatine, secretly the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] Darth Sidious and engineer of the crisis, used the powers to establish the Grand Army of the Republic of [[clone trooper]]s which had been [[Cloning|grown]] on the planet [[Kamino]] under Palpatine's orders using the guise of a long dead [[Jedi Master]], [[Sifo-Dyas]]. The clone troopers landed on [[Geonosis]] and the [[First Battle of Geonosis|battle]] was the start of the Clone Wars. As the Grand Army deployed across [[the galaxy]],<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> a division was established to protect the Republic capital of Coruscant. Called the Coruscant Guard, it was composed of the deadly and powerful clone shock troopers who were trained as peacekeepers and in urban pacification.<ref name="Databank Coruscant Guard" />

[[File:CoruscantGuard-TCWfilm.png|thumb|right|300px|Coruscant Guard troopers storm Ziro's night club.]]
When the son of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] was kidnapped, [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] went to Jabba's uncle [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] at his [[Ziro's Palace|night club]] on Coruscant in the hopes that Ziro would convince Jabba to allow the Jedi to rescue his son. However, Amidala discovered that Ziro was involved with the kidnapping which was perpetrated by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], leader of the Separatists and a [[Sith Lord]]. Dooku promised Ziro a large reward for the senator and the Hutt had Amidala escorted to his dungeon to be held. While in her cell, Amidala managed to contact her [[droid]], [[C-3PO]], to send help. Amidala was discovered and led to Ziro who instructed that she be executed. C-3PO meanwhile contacted the Coruscant Guard and requested their help in rescuing the senator. Commander Fox and several shock troopers accompanied C-3PO to the night club and [[Raid on Ziro's Palace|blasted]] their way in. They engaged the droids in a fight, destroying them, and Amidala was able to free herself in the commotion. Together with the shock troopers, they then cornered Ziro who was attempting to escape, and Commander Fox placed the Hutt under arrest.<ref name="TCW film" />

====Escorting Master Yoda====
{{Quote|Hm, learn something today have you, Lieutenant, hm?''"<br />"''I think we all did, General.|Yoda and Thire|<ref name="Ambush" />}}
[[File:ThiresBrightIdea-Ambush.png|thumb|left|300px|Troopers Jek and Rys with Lieutenant Thire on Rugosa]]
With both the Republic and the Separatists vying for suitable planets to establish bases, Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] was [[Battle of Rugosa|sent]] to the coral [[moon]] of [[Rugosa]] to secretly meet with [[Katuunko]], the [[King]] of [[Toydaria]]. The Coruscant Guard sent an escort with the Jedi led by [[Lieutenant]] [[Thire]] and including shock troopers [[Jek]] and [[Rys]]. However, [[Asajj Ventress]], apprentice to Count Dooku, arrived first to negotiate with Katuunko on behalf of the Separatists. The [[Consular-class light cruiser|Republic cruiser]] was ambushed by several Separatist warships, but Master Yoda and the shock troopers were able to land on the planet though not where the negotiations were to take place.<ref name="Ambush" />

Ventress challenged Master Yoda that she would send her best battle droids against him. If the Jedi Master could defeat her army, then Toydaria would enter the Republic. However, should the Jedi fail, then Toydaria would join the Separatists. Yoda agreed to this challenge. The Jedi and the Coruscant Guards began to make their way towards the negotiation's location. As they made their way through the coral forest, the Jedi and shock troopers encountered heavy fighting during which Thire was injured. Yet, with the encouragement of the aged Jedi, the small force was able to prevail. Dooku, seeing that his plot had failed, ordered Ventress to assassinate King Katuunko. Yoda and the Guards arrived in time to prevent the king's death though Ventress was able to escape.<ref name="Ambush" />

====A Gungan's orders====
{{Quote|I suggest we stay here and wait for the Republic to send a rescue party.|Stone|<ref name="The Gungan General" />}}
[[File:Clones_jar_jar.png|thumb|300px|A squad of the Coruscant Guard led by Commander Stone escorts Representative Binks]]
Commander [[Stone (clone trooper)|Stone]] and a squad of shock troopers accompanied Senator [[Kharrus]] to [[Mission to Florrum|deliver]] a [[spice]] ransom to a band of [[pirate]]s based on [[Florrum]] that had captured Count Dooku. However, they were ambushed by rogue pirates who wanted the spice for themselves. The pirates shot down the Republic shuttle and caused it to crash in the fields on Florrum's surface. Both the shuttle's [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]] and Senator Kharrus were killed in the crash, with only Commander Stone, a sergeant, three shock troopers and [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] who became the highest ranking individual, left alive.<ref name="The Gungan General" />

After Kharrus had been buried and given an impromptu funeral, the rogue pirates ambushed the clones and stole the spice. While Commander Stone favored waiting for reinforcement, Representative Binks encouraged them to move forward. As Binks was technically the highest ranking person in the Republic mission, Stone recognized they had to follow his orders, though the shock troopers were hesitant. The group counterattacked the pirates and they were able to retrieve the ransom spice. The pirates then attempted to wipe out the clones, but the Republic forces managed to defeat the pirates and commandeer their craft, taking them to the pirate outpost. They arrived at the facility and departed Florrum with Jedi Generals [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], who escaped a brief captivity at the hands of the pirates.<ref name="The Gungan General" />

A time later, Coruscant Guards would take custody of prisoners who were captured by members of the [[Jedi Order]] and escort them to the Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center. Coruscant Guards stood by at the Republic Detention Center to take custody of [[Boba Fett]] and [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] after they were captured by General [[Plo Koon]] and Skywalker’s apprentice Commander [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Lethal Trackdown">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref> They were also present at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] to take custody of [[Aurra Sing]] who was captured after her attempted assassination of Senator Amidala.<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref>

====Pursuing the Duchess====
{{Quote|She's escaping in a speeder. Heading for Section IG44.|A Coruscant Guard riot trooper|<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />}}
[[File:RiotCloneTroopers-DoM.png|thumb|left|270px|Coruscant Guards search for Duchess Kryze]]
When [[Duchess]] of [[Mandalore]], [[Satine Kryze]], held a secret meeting with [[Republic Ministry of Intelligence]] [[agent]], [[Davu Golec]] in the lower levels, a [[Death Watch]] assassin killed Golec. The shot alerted a Coruscant [[police probe]] which arrived on the scene as a crowd gathered. The police probe identified a shooting had taken place, but seeing Kryze with a blaster over the deceased Golec, identified her as the suspect. Police droids arrived on the scene seconds later and informed Kryze she was under arrest. Shocked but maintaining her innocence, Kryze fled the scene with the police in pursuit. The police probe informed nearby Coruscant Guard shock troopers who joined the police in the foot pursuit. Running into a crowed, Kryze fired a stun bolt with her blaster, disabling the the police probe tracking her and sending it to the ground. The police droids tripped over the disabled probe and fell to the ground, with the riot troopers also stumbling over them. The moment of confusion allowed Kryze to blend into the onlookers and ducking into an alley.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />

The troopers, however, spotted Kryze turning the corner and it was at that moment an individual in a speeder with a similar cloak as Kryze left the alley in his speeder. Believing the individual to by Kryze, the riot troopers communicated to the police units that she was fleeing the scene heading towards [[Section IG-44]]. Two Coruscant Police droids on BARC speeders picked up the chase, with more following, but allowing Kryze to avoid capture. Both Coruscant Police and Coruscant Guards continued the search for Kryze, questioning the locals if they had seen her, and [[Vice Chair]] [[Mas Amedda]] made a public statement demanding Kryze turn herself in to Republic law enforcement. Kryze requested help from an old friend, Master Kenobi and agreed to meet him in [[Monument Plaza]]. Avoiding the Coruscant Police and troopers searching the park for her, Kryze handed the holodisc to Kenobi and, after escaping a brief fight with the Death Watch assassin, the two made their way to the Senate Building. There, Kryze turned herself into the Senate Guards on duty and Kenobi gave the holodisc to Senator Amidala who then played the full recording of Jerec to the Senate. In it, Jerec affirmed Mandalore's ability to deal with Death Watch and with this, the Senate voted to not occupy the planet, Kryze was vindicated, and the police murder investigation into her was closed.<ref name="Duchess of Mandalore" />

====Senate hostage crisis====
{{Quote|Mission accomplished, sir. The Chancellor is safe.|A Coruscant Guard trooper after storming the Chancellor's Office|<ref name="Hostage" />}}
[[File:CC_stop.png|thumb|right|300px|Coruscant Guards confront the bounty hunters]]
When the Hutts hired the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] to [[Senate hostage crisis|seize]] the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]] and force Supreme Chancellor Palpatine to issue a pardon for the Hutt crime lord Ziro the Hutt, who was imprisoned in the Detention Center. Bane formed a team of bounty hunters and infiltrated the executive building, disposing of multiple [[Senate Commando]] guards along the way, the team took a small group of senators hostage. Bane trapped Palpatine and Senator [[Orn Free Taa]] in the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's office]] by shutting down the building's power. Bane then demanded Ziro was to be released or the senators would be executed. To secure the Hutt's release in exchange for the senators' lives, Bane ordered Taa to take a pardon disc to the Detention Center to release Ziro.<ref name="Hostage" />

Taa reluctantly complied and was taken to the Detention Center by Bane's accomplices where the shock troopers on guard released Ziro. As final leverage against Palpatine, the bounty hunters set up a system of lasers around the senators that would trigger explosives with the slightest touch before leaving the building. Clone troopers of the Coruscant Guard led by Clone Commander CC-1010 arrived and [[Unidentified Coruscant Guard (Chancellor's Office)|stormed]] Palpatine's office then moved to apprehend Bane's team on the landing platform. However, with the still-trapped senators Palpatine ordered the bounty hunters to be allowed to escape with Ziro.<ref name="Hostage" />

Coruscant Guards were present later at the Jedi Temple along with Jedi Masters Yoda and Mace Windu for the arrival of Padawan Ahsoka Tano and [[Captain]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] who had returned from a [[Battle of Lola Sayu|mission]] on [[Lola Sayu]]. They escorted Tarkin to the Senate Office Building to meet Supreme Chancellor Palpatine.<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref>

====Terrorism and prison riot====
{{Quote|Turn your weapons off stun. Kill the prisoners if you have to.|A Coruscant Guard trooper during a prison riot|<ref name="Deception" />}}
As the War progressed, the Confederacy of Independent Systems engaged in initiative towards a [[Confederate–Republic peace initiative|peace treaty]] with the Republic proposed by Separatist Senator [[Mina Bonteri]]. However, under the direction of Count Dooku, Coruscant was infiltrated by [[Infiltrator demolition droid|demolition droids]] who planned a [[Bombing of Coruscant's central power distribution grid|bombing]] of the [[central power distribution grid]]. The droids arrived at the docks where the shock troopers were on patrol. They then showed their identification to Commander Fox who waved them through. Upon arriving at the generator they killed several engineers and planted themselves on the wall as bombs. The droids then exploded, crippling all electrical sources on Coruscant, which resulted in power outages and fires as well as civilian deaths.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>

[[File:PrisonRiot-Deception.png|thumb|left|300px|Coruscant Guards during a prison riot]]
A [[Riot at the Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center|riot]] was instigated at the Detention Center by bounty hunter Cad Bane to provide a cover for his and [[Moralo Eval]]'s escape. The riot began when Bane had a young Boba Fett confront [[Rako Hardeen]] demanding Hardeen apologize to him for killing Master Kenobi and depriving Fett of the bounty. Unknown to everyone, Hardeen was Kenobi in an undercover disguise. Hardeen and Fett fought each other before they were stopped by two shock troopers. However, the guards were knocked out by the [[Trandoshan]] Bossk who attacked Hardeen for fighting with Fett. At that point more Coruscant Guards arrived to stop Bossk. However, as they began to fight Bossk, the other prisoners joined the riot. A shock trooper on the balcony called for reinforcements while stunning the prisoners.<ref name="Deception" />

As the riot grew, the Coruscant Guard troopers turned off their stun settings and began killing the prisoners that attacked them. During the confusion Eval and Bane began to make their escape. They were joined by Hardeen who convinced them to take him with them. The troopers were able to restore order though many prisoners were killed necessitating that they be cremated. Arriving at the detention center's morgue, the three were able to enter the chute cases before two clones arrived. The clones sent them down to the cremation center and there they attacked and killed the three clone officers on duty. Moving the loading bay, the three prisoners were able to overwhelm the troopers stationed there and escape in a [[Panther police interceptor]].<ref name="Deception" />

Shortly thereafter, members of the Coruscant Guard escorted Chancellor Palpatine along with members of the Senate Guard and Jedi Generals [[Mace Windu]] and Skywalker to his homeworld [[Naboo]] for the upcoming [[Festival of Light]]. During the ceremony, Cade Bane and Moralo Eval put their plan into action under the direction of Count Dooku to kidnap Palpatine. Together with their group of bounty hunters which included Kenobi as Hardeen, they [[Plan to kidnap Chancellor Palpatine|infiltrated]] the security detail using [[Holographic disguise matrix|holographic disguise matrices]]. Kenobi was able to pursue Eval and Bane who took Palpatine. He was able to capture them shortly before the arrival of Windu, Skywalker, and a shock trooper who arrested the two kidnappers.<ref name="Crisis on Naboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref>

====Arresting Tano====
{{Quote|Can't say I blame you Commander Tano, but all the same, you're under arrest.|Fox, to Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />}}
When a hangar in the Jedi Temple was the site of a [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|explosion]] that killed six Jedi and several clone troopers and Temple employees. After the explosion, the hanger was secured by troopers of the Coruscant Guard while Jedi Knight Anakin Skywalker and Padawan Ahsoka Tano investigated the incident with the assistance of the [[SP-4 ISC crime scene analysis droid|crime scene analysis droid]] [[Russo-ISC]]. With anger and confusion spreading, family members of the employees killed gathered outside the Temple to protest the Jedi. The Coruscant Guard was sent to the Temple with several riot troopers and a LAAT ship to assist the [[Jedi Temple Guard]] in controlling the crowd. The investigation found that [[Letta Turmond]], the [[Marriage|wife]] of Temple employee [[Jackar Bowmani]], had fed him volatile [[NM-K reconstitutor|Nano-droids]] which then exploded killing Bowmani and causing the bombing at the Temple. Turmond was arrested by Skywalker and Tano and placed in a holding cell in the Jedi Temple.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:AhsokaUnderArrest-TJWKTM.png|thumb|right|300px|Commander Fox arrests Ahsoka Tano]]
However, with clone troopers killed in the explosion, the Republic military claimed jurisdiction of Turmond who was taken into their custody under the direction of [[Admiral]] Wilhuff Tarkin. Turmond was moved to the prison complex at the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Republic military base]] which was under the control of the Coruscant Guard. However, Turmond refused to talk with anyone except Tano and so Admiral Tarkin requested Tano come speak to Turmond. Arriving at the base, Tano found Commander Fox personally monitoring the guard station. After having her leave her lightsabers and comlink at the guard post, Fox and two other troopers escorted Tano to Turmond's cell.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />

Leaving Tano with Turmond, Fox returned to the guard post. Meanwhile Turmond revealed that a Jedi had showed her how to make the bomb in an effort to make a statement against the Jedi being involved in the war. Before Turmond could give Tano the name, Turmond was [[Force choke]]d, unknown to all, by Jedi [[Barriss Offee]]. However, the view from the security cameras made it appear as if Tano was the one choking Turmond and a shock trooper notified Fox of Turmond's distress. Quickly Fox and two other troopers rushed into the cell, only to find Tano, apparently distraught, bending over Turmond's dead body. Fox, while not blaming Tano for wanting revenge on a traitor, placed her under arrest.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />

====The Padawan's escape====
{{Quote|Alert, alert! The Jedi prisoner has escaped!|Fox|<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />}}
Admiral Tarkin, upon being notified of the situation, arrived to interrogate Tano. He was escorted to her cell by two Coruscant Guards. Showing Tano the recording, Tarkin pointed out that the video appeared to show Tano telekinetic-ally choking Turmond though Tano maintained her innocence. Tarkin ordered that no one else was to be allowed to see Tano. Shortly thereafter Anakin Skywalker arrived and began arguing with Commander Fox, demanding to let him in. Fox had two shock troopers activate their [[electrostaff]]s, threatening to enforce the Admiral's orders with force, at which point Skywalker left.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />

[[File:AhsokaEscape.png|thumb|left|300px|Coruscant Guards briefly corner Ahsoka Tano]]
After that, Tano discovered a key card outside her cell. Believing it had been left by Skywalker, in actually it had been Offee, Tano used the key to leave her cell. Arriving at the guard post, Tano found three wounded shock troopers and next to them her lightsabers and comlink. At that moment Commander Fox entered the guard post and seeing Tano, issued the alert. Three Coruscant Guards chased her north into the corridor and sealed the area before a Coruscant Guard trooper notified Fox. Pursued by Fox, Tano came upon three troopers who had been killed by a lightsaber. Tano continued to run when Fox arrived. Assuming Tano as the killer, Fox issued a shoot-to-kill order.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />

However, Anakin Skywalker arrived at that point with [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Rex]] who argued with Fox that Tano would never kill troopers like that. Skywalker, failing to convince Tano to surrender, ordered the base locked down and searched. Tano was able to avoid a squad of Coruscant Guards, including Trooper [[Hound]] and his [[massiff]] [[Grizzer (massiff)|Grizzer]], who were patrolling the memorial area. However she was spotted by Commander Fox and his squad and was chased to the pipelines outside the base. The shock troopers attempted to stun blast her, but Tano was able to block them. She was briefly surrounded by several Coruscant Guards but managed to escape into the sewer system with Skywalker, Rex, Fox, and several squads of Coruscant Guards following her. Skywalker was able to chase her down one length of pipe which ended at the [[underworld portal]]. Tano, restating her innocence jumped into the portal onto a transport and escaped capture as Commander Fox and Captain Rex arrived.<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />

====Hunting a Jedi====
{{Quote|We're taking you in now, Commander.''"<br />"''That's not gonna happen. Trust me.|Wolffe and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="To Catch a Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref>}}
A short time later, two squads of Coruscant Guards were sent to find Tano in the [[Coruscant Underworld]]. One squad was under the command of Anakin Skywalker and Captain Rex and the other squad was commanded by Jedi Master Plo Koon and Commander [[Wolffe]]. Utilizing two police gunships, the squads followed a lead from the Coruscant Security Force's [[Coruscant underworld police|Underworld Police division]] that Tano had been spotted on [[Level 1312]]. Descending into the underworld, the squads were able to spot Tano along with with Separatist agent&ndash;turned&ndash;bounty hunter Asajj Ventress. Tano and Ventress activated the terminal gate's shield, preventing pursuit after which Skywalker ordered a group of probe droids to find the two fugitives. Wolffe and his team of Coruscant Guards then disembarked from the police gunship to monitor the probe droids' findings.<ref name="To Catch a Jedi" />

[[File:Epguide519.png|thumb|right|300px|Coruscant Guards hold Tano at blaster point]]
Before long, the squad found that Tano had been spotted and he immediately took his troopers to apprehend them. Upon being cornered and held at blaster-point by Wolffe's men, Tano attempted to get them to back down, but Wolffe refused to let her escape. A [[Capture of Ahsoka Tano|fight]] ensued, with Ventress activating her lightsabers and cutting through the shock troopers' blasters. Tano also joined the fight, making sure that she and Ventress did not harm the troopers beyond rendering them unconscious. Disabled and winded, Wolffe and the Coruscant Guard troops were unable to prevent Tano and Ventress from escaping. Notifying Master Koon that Ventress had been involved with Tano's getaway, Wolffe and his shock trooper squad had to recover before picking up the chase.<ref name="To Catch a Jedi" />

Meanwhile, Ventress left Tano who arrived at an abandoned munitions warehouse on [[Level 1315]] and was attacked by Barriss Offee disguised as Ventress. Their ensuing fight caught the attention of two Coruscant Police officers who notified Skywalker's squad of Coruscant Guards. The two squads arrived and found Tano in a lower storage room. Although Tano pleaded with Wolffe to let her explain, he stunned her unconscious before she could utter another word. The Coruscant Guard took her into custody and she was taken back to the Jedi Temple.<ref name="To Catch a Jedi" /> With the evidence stacked against her, and at the demand of the Senate, Tano was expelled from the Jedi Order and turned over to the Republic military to be put on [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] for her alleged part in the bombing of the Temple. Escorted to the courtroom by Coruscant Guards, she was defended by Padmé Amidala and persecuted by Tarkin. Skywalker discovered that fellow Jedi Barriss Offee was the true culprit, having worked with Turmond and then framed Tano for her crimes. Offee was then taken into custody while Tano was acquitted. However, Tano chose not to rejoin the Order when offered re-admittance.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref>

====Clone conspiracy====
{{Quote|Well, there's a sinister plot in the works against the Jedi! I have proof of it! I can prove that everything I know is true beyond the shadow of a doubt!|Fives|<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:CoruscantGuardssearching.png|thumb|left|300px|Coruscant Guards searching for Fives]]
During the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]], Clone trooper [[Tup]] shot and killed Jedi General [[Tiplar]]. Tup was brought to Kamino under [[Fives]]' watch, but died from complications involving a tumor and the rotting of an implanted [[behavioral modification biochip]]. To cover up the [[Order 66|Sith plot]], [[Kaminoan]] [[Doctor]] [[Nala Se]] injected Fives with a drug that would slow his reaction.<ref name="Orders" />

On Coruscant, Fives was framed by Chancellor Palpatine of attacking him and [[Escape from the Grand Republic Medical Facility|escaped]] from the [[Grand Republic Medical Facility|medical facility]]. Fives took a cab to [[79's]], a clone bar where he ran into his friend [[Kix]] and asked him to pass a message onto the commanding officers of the [[501st Legion]], Anakin Skywalker and Captain [[Rex]] to meet him so he could tell them what he learned in an attempt to clear his name. Unknown to Fives, he had been spotted and followed down to the meeting area by and the Coruscant Guard had been sent to capture him.<ref name="Orders" />

Skywalker and Rex arrived at the location Kix had given them and were ordered by a hiding Fives to throw down their weapons. Tossing away their weapons, Fives enclosed them in a ray shield. Both Skywalker and Rex wanted to help Fives, but neither seemed to believe his story, his drug-hazed state not helping his cause. Commander Fox and a squad of shock troopers ambushed the rogue clone, pointing their weapons at him and demanding he stand down. Out of desperation, Fives grabbed a blaster pistol and attempted to hold them back, but Fox shot Fives through the chest in retaliation. Skywalker and Rex were freed from the ray-shield and Rex went directly to Fives. Fives explained he was just trying to do his duty before reiterating Tup's final words before he too, died.<ref name="Orders" />

====Commander Thorn's final stand====
{{Quote|What is it, Commander Thorn?''"<br />"''We're under attack by the Separatist garrison. Looks to be a full invasion!''"<br />"''Invasion?''"<br />"''We can't get to you! I suggest you get to a ship as soon as you can!|Padmé Amidala and Thorn|<ref name="Crisis at the Heart" />}}
As the war continued, Republic and Separatist representatives [[Mission to Scipio|met]] on the planet [[Scipio]] to jointly preside over the installation of [[Rush Clovis]] as leader of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]]. Senator Padmé Amidala went to Scipio as the Republic representative. Accompanying her was a [[garrison]] of the Coruscant Guard led by Commander [[Thorn]].<ref name="Crisis at the Heart" />

[[File:Thornattacked.png|thumb|right|300px|Commander Thorn and his men are attacked.]]
Shortly after Clovis became the head of the InterGalactic Banking Clan, he found himself being blackmailed by Separatist leader, Count Dooku, to raise interest rates towards the Republic and not the Separatists. Dooku also pressured Clovis to allow the Separatists to take full control of the bank. After Clovis agreed, Commander Thorn and his shock troopers detected Separatist fighters closing in on the landing platform they were stationed at. Thorn ordered the troops and gunships around them to get off the landing platform and regroup beyond the city gates. He then warned Senator Amidala of the incoming Separatist [[Invasion of Scipio|invasion]]. However, Thorn informed her that they would not be able to get to her in time and she needed to flee the planet. The attacking droid fighters shot the gunships and ''Consular''-class cruiser down, leaving the remaining shock troopers with no reinforcements.<ref name="Crisis at the Heart" />

As Thorn and his men rushed toward the entrance of the neutral zone, droid gunships entered the firefight and blocked their path in the front and the landing pad behind them. The droids began to cut down the shock troopers and deployed commando and super battle droids to attack. Thorn was the last to remain standing but kept fighting, using his rotary blaster cannon as a melee and long range weapon. However, he was greatly outnumbered, and was shot multiple times in the chest until he was finally killed by a commando droid. These actions prompted the Galactic Senate to send forces of the Grand Army of the Republic under the command of General Anakin Skywalker to free the planet. In the aftermath of the battle, the Senate turned the banks over to the direct control of Supreme Chancellor Palpatine.<ref name="Crisis at the Heart" />

===End of the Clone Wars and Order 66===
{{Quote|I want you to go to the detention level. Execute Maul.''"<br />"''Yes, sir. All right, men.|Rex and a Coruscant Guard trooper|<ref name="Shattered" />}}
[[File:CloneShockTroopers-Shattered.png|thumb|left|300px|Ahsoka Tano prevented the Coruscant Guards from executing Maul.]]
Following the [[Siege of Mandalore]] and the capture of renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] by the [[332nd Division]], a contingent of shock troopers from the Coruscant Guard were dispatched in a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class transport]] to escort him and provide additional security. When the ''Nu''-class arrived<ref name="Shattered" /> on the ''[[Tribunal]]'',<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> in orbit, additional shock troopers helped transport him to a holding cell, and kept him under guard. When Chancellor Palpatine, secretly Sith Lord [[Darth Sidious]], issued [[Order 66]] that branded the Jedi as [[Treason|traitors]] to the Republic, the shock troopers on the [[Star Destroyer]] helped members of the [[division]] attack Tano on the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]].<ref name="Shattered" />

After Tano escaped the bridge, a clone shock trooper aided [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]] recuperate. Rex then ordered the trio of shock troopers on the bridge to kill Maul, and then began creating a strategy to kill Tano. Tano, however, freed Maul after incapacitating the two shock troopers guarding him so that he could distract Republic forces away from her. Maul then went on a [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|rampage]] throughout the ship as he made his way towards the main [[hangar]], and encountered additional shock troopers from the Coruscant Guard, some of which he knocked unconscious and others in which he killed. All remaining clones aboard the ''Tribunal'' were subsequently killed when the vessel crashed on the surface of a [[Unidentified moon|remote moon]].<ref name="Shattered" />

[[File:LAATi-troopers.png|thumb|right|270px|The Coruscant Guard chases escaping Jedi to a landing pad.]]
With Order 66 commencing the [[Great Jedi Purge]], the Coruscant Guard under Commander Fox aided with the initiative's implementation on Coruscant itself.<ref name="Dark Lord 9" /> When Jedi Master [[Kelleran Beq]] escaped from the [[501st Legion]]'s [[Operation: Knightfall]] with the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], the Coruscant Guard pursued them aboard Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry gunships. After one of the LAAT/i was destroyed, as Beq tricked the shock trooper pilot into flying the path into a [[train]], the other arrived behind him to a landing pad, where Beq met up with a detachment of the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]] for help in escaping aboard a [[H-type Nubian yacht]]. While Beq and Grogu escaped aboard the [[yacht]], clone troopers, some in blank-white armor and others in red-Coruscant Guard shock trooper armor, stepped off the gunship and traded fire with the Security Forces. Several [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|V-wing starfighters]] chased the Nubian but failed to destroy it before escaping into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Chapter 20">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 20: The Foundling}}</ref>

===Service to the Empire===
====Early days of the Empire====
{{Quote|Double your search!"<br />"Yes, sir! Right away!|Darth Sidious and Thire|<ref name="Episode III" />}}
[[File:Thiresenate.png|thumb|left|300px|Coruscant Guard shock troopers secure the Senate Building]]
Master Yoda later confronted Sidious in a [[Duel in the Galactic Senate|duel]] in the Senate chamber which reached a stalemate. Several Coruscant Guards arrived, one of them being Clone Commander Thire, and unsuccessfully searched for Yoda's body, however, the Jedi Master had escaped with the aid of Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]]. Sidious, suspecting that his apprentice Darth Vader was in danger, left Coruscant for the planet [[Mustafar]] with a detachment of Coruscant Guards. The shock troopers soon found the mangled and burned body of Vader, [[Duel on Mustafar|defeated]] by Kenobi, and alerted Sidious. As soon as he saw Vader, Sidious ordered the shock troopers to get a medical transport. Returning to Coruscant, Sidious and Vader were taken to the Grand Republic Medical Facility, all under the watchful eye of the Coruscant Guards. There, Vader underwent surgery that encased him in a life supporting suit.<ref name="Episode III" />

During the [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral of Senator Amidala]] in the city of [[Theed]], [[Naboo]], a detachment of the Coruscant Guard provided security for the occasion. When two of the troopers found Senator Organa, who was speaking to Tano in a restricted area, they checked his identification and informed him he needed to be escorted back to the ceremony, to which Bail agreed.<ref name="Resolve">{{TOTJ|Resolve (episode)}}</ref>

====Hunt for Jocasta Nu====
{{Quote|Stop there, citizen. Identify yourself.|Fox, to Jocasta Nu|<ref name="Dark Lord 7" />}}
[[File:JocastaNuAndCoruscantGuard-2017DarthVader7.jpg|thumb|right|300px|After returning to Coruscant, Jocasta Nu utilized the Jedi mind trick on Commander Fox and his guards.]]
In the early days of the Galactic Empire, the Coruscant Guard retained the duties of patrolling the levels of Coruscant, constantly checking the identifications of citizens. When Jedi Master [[Jocasta Nu]] returned to Coruscant after the initial purge there, she ran into Fox and several other shock troopers on patrol, and used a Jedi [[mind trick]] to get past them, in order for her to reach the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]].<ref name="Dark Lord 7" /> As Nu was able to infiltrate the Temple, her [[starfighter]] was discovered by members of the Coruscant underworld police, she ordered her droid, [[Beetoo]] to self detonate, resulting in a large explosion that killed the police officers as well as Beetoo. After the bombing, Coruscant Guard members were involved in securing the bomb site and aided the newly established [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and the underworld police in the investigation.<ref name="Dark Lord 8">[[Darth Vader (2017) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 8]]</ref>

Once Nu was detected in the Temple, Vader ordered the Coruscant Guard and the underworld police to secure the entire perimeter. As a result, the Coruscant Guard deployed two [[Company|companies]] while the underworld police deployed an entire contingent.<ref name="Dark Lord 9" /> The shock troopers fired on Nu when she attempted to flee out of a hole she'd blasted in the Temple wall, prompting her to fight them off with a lightsaber. When Vader appeared at the breach as well, the shock troopers mistook him for a Jedi as well (Commander Fox having neglected to provide a description of the Sith Lord) and opened fire on him as well before Fox arrived in a police gunship and ordered them to cease fire. Fox attempted to apologize for his blunder but Vader, not in the mood for apologies, snapped the commander's neck with the Force before ordering the remaining shock troopers to capture Nu alive.<ref name="Dark Lord 10">[[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]]</ref>

The archivist attempted to jump to her death, but Vader held her in place with the Force and she was bound and loaded into the gunship. When Nu taunted Vader over the knowledge she possessed that he now knew, one of the shock troopers offered to gag her in order to cut off her Jedi lies. Nu, still darkly amused, fired back that they themselves were taking orders from a Jedi in the form of Vader, who was Skywalker. Vader then killed all shock troopers in the gunship by blasting them out of it, sending them plummeting to their deaths.<ref name="Dark Lord 10" />

====Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill====
{{Quote|Guards, arrest and detain the Admiral."<br />"Stay back! I was following orders. I was follow--|Grand Vizier Mas Ammeda, ordering the Coruscant Guard to arrest Vice Admiral Rampart|<ref name="Truth" />}}
[[File:TruthAndConsequences-Rampart.png|thumb|left|300px|Coruscant Guards arrested Edmon Rampart for destroying Tipoca City for his personal agenda.]]
[[Standard month|Months]] after<ref name="BB S2 Date">{{Databank|hunter|Hunter}}</ref> the [[Attack on Kamino|destruction]] of [[Kamino]]'s cloning facilities, the [[Imperial Senate]] begun a vote on the [[Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill]], which would formalize the [[Imperial Military]]'s transition from an army comprised entirely of clone troopers, to a recruitment-based force of non-clone soldiers. Several squads of shock troopers were in attendance and provided security for the occasion. When [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] was exposed before the Senate for having personally ordered and taken part of the destruction of Kamino via his [[fleet]] of [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], a squad of shock troopers forcefully arrested Rampart under orders from [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], who appeared with Emperor Palpatine to address the Senate.<ref name="Truth">{{TBB|Truth and Consequences}}</ref>

====Continued service====
{{Quote|This has been explained. The Empire requires this location for a relay facility. It is ideally situated. You will all be compensated for the inconvenience.''"<br />"''Inconvenience? These are the only homes we've ever known. Where are we supposed to go?''"<br />"''Somewhere else.|A citizen protests to the Coruscant Guard as they enforce Imperial orders.|<ref name="Dark Lord 16" />}}
[[File:OrvekEvicted-2017DarthVader16.png|thumb|right|300px|Coruscant Guards forcing civilians out of their homes for the construction of an Imperial relay station.]]
During this early period under the Empire, a contingent of shock troopers bulldozed several homes, including the home of [[Orvek]], in a forested area to construct a relay station. Although the individuals were to be compensated, Orvek and others protested that they only knew one home and did not know where to go. Unflinching, a shock trooper grudgingly informed Orvek to go somewhere else. After the encounter, Orvek joined a [[Ferren Barr's disciples|group of disciples]] led by Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ferren Barr]], who was posing as a [[Jedi Master]].<ref name="Dark Lord 16">[[Darth Vader (2017) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 16]]</ref>

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Vader 18 Chronology Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]]. As [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] knows about [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s past on [[Eriadu]] in [[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]], which he only learned about in ''Tarkin'', issue 18 must take place after the novel. The later issue [[Darth Vader (2017) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 19]] featured the [[Second Sister]], who was [[Death|killed]] in ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''. ''Fallen Order'' takes place five years after the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which is dated to 19 BBY by ''Star Wars: Galactic Atlas''. Therefore, ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' is also set in 14 BBY. As ''Darth Vader: Dark Lord of the Sith 18'' is between both ''Tarkin'' and ''Darth Vader: Dark Lord of the Sith 19'', the events of the issue must take place in 14 BBY. Since [[Darth Vader (2017) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 20]] takes places immediately after the 19th issue, the events of this issue must also take place in 14 BBY.</ref> two shock troopers became involved in a [[Coruscant Incident|hunt]] for two rogue Imperial [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitors]] when Lord Vader required a [[speeder]] to chase them down. After the Inquisitors destroyed the [[Engine|thruster]]s of the speeder, Vader leaped across other speeders while the speeder with the two members crashed and exploded in a park, killing an important Gran Senator and her companion.<ref name="Dark Lord 20">[[Darth Vader (2017) 20|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 20]]</ref>
