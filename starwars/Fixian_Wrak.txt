'''Fixian Wrak''' was a [[Pirate|marauder]] who led a [[Fixian Wrak's crew|crew]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. They and their crew raided settlements on [[Wadi Raffa]], leading [[Jedi Master]]s [[Simmix]] and [[Laret Soveral]] of the [[Jedi Order]], alongside their [[Padawan]]s [[Cohmac Vitus]] and [[Orla Jareni]], to undertake a [[Mission to Wadi Raffa|mission]] to deter them.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
