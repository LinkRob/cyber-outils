"'''Finn and the Busted Droids – Chapter 3'''" is the thirteenth episode of the animated shorts series ''[[Star Wars Roll Out]]''. It was first released [[November 1]], [[2019]] on YouTube.

==Official description==
''After escaping (with help from the broken droids), Finn repays the favor and gets a second chance to prove himself to Captain Phasma''.<ref name="YT" />

==Plot summary==
The episode opens with [[Finn|FN-2187]] still stuck to the magnet as the compressors close in on him. When all seems lost, the machine senses that he is not a [[droid]]. A giant mechanical claw lifts him up and a circular shaft opens. The two busted droids raise their hands to the light in vain and despair. The mechanical claw brings FN-2187 back to the surface, leaving the two busted droids behind in the [[garbage compactor]].

More rubbish including a decrepit green droid are flushed down the garbage chute. The silver and orange droids are about to give up hope when FN-2187, who takes pity on the droids, opens a shaft and abseils down a rope. He then uses a rope pulley to lift the droids out of the garbage compactor. The droids thank Finn.

Shortly later, [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] and the rest of the [[FN Trooper Corps]] flee the green fire&ndash;breathing monster, which has broken free again. They huddle behind FN-2187 and the droids. The monster approaches a [[hoversled]] carrying trash. Finn has an idea and the droids use wire to restrain the monster long enough for FN-2187 to shove dirt down its maw. Finn and the busted droids then restrain the monster, earning the respect of Captain Phasma and the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]].

Later, Finn watches as the grateful droids are repaired on a factory conveyor belt. The episode ends with Finn laughing happily.
