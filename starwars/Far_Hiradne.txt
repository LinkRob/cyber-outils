'''Far Hiradne''' was an area on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. It was known in the days before the [[Galactic Empire]] for its forests with [[tree]]s that could be tapped for [[Greel wood|greel-wood]] [[Greel-wood syrup|syrup]]. The elderly [[poet]] named [[Holshef]]'s first job was as a [[sap collector]] in Far Hiradne. Both he and [[Jho|Old Jho]] still remembered the place fondly.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>
