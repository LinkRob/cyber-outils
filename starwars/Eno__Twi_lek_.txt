'''Eno''' was the leader of a resistance group on the [[planet]] [[Genya]] who desperately hoped to reunite with her missing [[Karin (Twi'lek)|sister]].<ref name="Bounty" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
En O appeared in "[[The Bounty Hunters]]", a non-[[canon]] short film directed by [[Junichi Yamamoto]] that is part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[WIT Studio]],<ref name="Bounty" /> released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="First Look">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-star-wars-visions-volume-3-spinoff-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Volume 3 First Look and Spinoff Announced – ''Star Wars'' Celebration Japan 2025}}</ref> She was first mentioned in a production brief for the series. En O is voiced by [[Yume Miyamoto]] in the original [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]] version and by [[Jodie Turner-Smith]] in the English dub.<ref name="Bounty" />
