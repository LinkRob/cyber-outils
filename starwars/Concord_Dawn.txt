'''Concord Dawn''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Concord Dawn system]], within the [[Mandalore sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]. The planet was devastated during one of the hundreds of wars it suffered, with roughly a third of its mass blasted out as asteroid-sized debris in its planetary orbit. During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], Concord Dawn was ruled by the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]], under the leadership of [[Fenn Rau]]. Concord Dawn was also famous as the homeworld of many elite warriors, including the [[bounty hunter]] [[Rako Hardeen]] and the [[Mandalorian warrior]] and [[foundling]] [[Jango Fett]].

==History==
===Early history===
Concord Dawn, a [[planet]] with three [[moon]]s located in the [[Concord Dawn system]]<ref name="Protector" /> within the [[Mandalore sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> was famous for its elite [[Mandalorian]] warriors, and during one of its many wars, much of the planet's southern hemisphere was wiped out, turning into much smaller [[asteroid]] chunks.<ref name="Protector" />

[[File:RakoHardeenRifle-SWE.png|thumb|right|250px|Hardeen became the "Marksman of Concord Dawn".]]
At some point, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Rako Hardeen]] was [[Reproduction|born]] on the shattered planet.<ref name="Encyclopedia">{{SWE|characters|rakohardeen|Rako Hardeen}}</ref> Due to his excellent skills as a [[Sniper|marksman]], Hardeen earned the nickname the "Marksman of Concord Dawn."<ref name="The Box" /> The famed [[Mandalorian]] bounty hunter and [[Cloning|clone]] [[Clone template|template]] [[Jango Fett]] was born there,<ref name="Helmet Collection 17">{{HelmetCollectionCite|17|Databank A-Z}}</ref> though his exact history was unknown, much to his amusement.<ref name="CVDNE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

Due to their reputation of training elite warriors, several Mandalorian warriors from Concord Dawn were hired by the [[Galactic Republic]] to train [[clone trooper]]s during the Clone Wars, with [[Fenn Rau]] serving as a [[Starfighter|fighter]] [[pilot]] [[instructor]] of [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]].<ref name="Protector" />

===Age of the Empire===
====Arrival of the rebels====
[[File:Mandalorian_Gauntlet_Fighter_SWA.png|thumb|left|250px|A modified [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transport]] patrols Concord Dawn.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]],<ref name="Protector" /> a group of Mandalorian warriors who once served the [[Mandalore (title)|ruler]] of [[Mandalore]] before its [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] occupation,<ref name="ProtectorsDB">{{Databank|the-protectors|The Protectors}}</ref> ruled Concord Dawn along with its [[Third moon of Concord Dawn|third]] [[moon]], where they set up a [[Protectors Camp|camp]]. Led by [[Fenn Rau]], the Protectors aligned themselves with the Empire. Three [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]], an [[Dogfight over Concord Dawn|aerial engagement]] occurred against members of [[Phoenix Squadron]] over free passage through the [[Star system|system]] and to the [[Lothal sector]]. The members of the cell escaped, however the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and the Mandalorian [[Sabine Wren]] [[Mission to the third moon of Concord Dawn|assaulted]] the Protectors' base.<ref name="Protector" />

Rau and Jarrus ended up in conversation, when they realized that both had been involved in the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]], where Jarrus and his [[Jedi Master|master]] [[Depa Billaba]] were saved by Rau and [[Skull Squadron]] from a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] attack. After some more encounters with the hiding Wren, who had planted several bombs around the base and had invoked the [[Mandalorian code]], Rau's [[starfighter]]s were destroyed, however, he escaped. In fear of the Empire arriving in their system, Rau allowed the members of the rebellion to pass through.<ref name="Protector" />

====Destruction of the Protectors====
[[File:Imperial-Supercommandos.png|thumb|right|250px|Saxon and his men wiped out the Protectors on Concord Dawn.]]
Some [[time]] later, the Galactic Empire arrived in the system, led by Imperial [[viceroy]] [[Gar Saxon]] and a squad of [[Imperial Super Commando]]s. The Imperial forces later [[Second mission to the third moon of Concord Dawn|engaged]] the returning Sabine Wren, Fenn Rau, and the young Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] when they arrived on Concord Dawn's moon, and eventually all parties fled the area.<ref name="ISC">{{Rebels|Imperial Supercommandos}}</ref>
