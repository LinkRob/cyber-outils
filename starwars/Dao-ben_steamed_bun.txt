'''Dao-ben steamed buns''' were a type of [[bread]] that could be found on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. In the [[Imperial Era]], buns of the bread were sold in a [[Master Hyor-ka's dao-ben steamed bun shop|shop]] owned by the businessowner Master [[Hyor-ka]] in Akiva's [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Myrra]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[junk dealer]] [[Temmin Wexley]] hid under the tarp of Hyor-ka's dao-ben steamed bun shop while [[spy]]ing on [[Surat Nuat's gang|thugs]] working for the [[crime lord]] [[Surat Nuat]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dao-ben steamed buns were mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
