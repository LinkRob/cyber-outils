==Behind the scenes==
The [[Wikipedia:French language|French language]] utilizes a [[Wikipedia:grammatical gender|grammatical gender system]] and thus both ''[[Les Barons d'Acier : trois capitaines pirates]]'' and ''[[La guerre des loups]]'' articles use the [[Gender/Legends|masculine]] ''Crucificateur'' as opposed to the [[Gender/Legends|feminine]] ''Crucificatrice'' in naming this vessel.<ref name="D7">{{DescartesCite|7|Les Barons d'Acier : trois capitaines pirates}} {{Un}}</ref><ref name="D9" />
