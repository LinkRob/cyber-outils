'''Baba-stone''' was a type of [[Color|tan]]-colored [[stone]] that could be used for sculpting. One such sculpture depicted a mischievous [[merqaal]] wrapped into a round ball. The sculpture had black [[eye]]s and was hung from a red string.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Baba-stone appeared in the form of the cosmetic "Merqaal Carving Trinket" item, which was part of the Cartel Ronin Bundle of items added to the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' as downloadable content<ref name="Outlaws" /> on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="DLCDate">{{Ubisoft|subdomain=store|url=us/star-wars-outlaws---cartel-ronin-bundle/66aa65c6046cab4bfdeab1c0.html|text=Star Wars Outlaws - Cartel Ronin Bundle|date=2024-11-21}}</ref> If purchased, the trinket trophy can be hung from the side of the player character [[Kay Vess]]'s [[S-57 Cardinal]] speeder bike or within the [[cockpit]] of her [[EML-850 light freighter]] ''[[Trailblazer]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
