'''Fzbol''' was a [[Wookiee]]. They carried the [[Anzellan]] [[Que-Dama]] in a [[MSE series]] [[droid]] strapped to their chest.<ref name="Day 3" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fzbol appeared in "[[The Duel: Payback]]", a non-[[canon]] short film directed by [[Takanobu Mizuno]] that is part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Kamikaze Douga]] and [[ANIMA (company)|ANIMA]], was released on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The character was first introduced at the ''Star Wars: Visions'' panel at [[Celebration Japan (2025)|Celebration Japan]] on [[April 19]], 2025.<ref name="Day 3" />
