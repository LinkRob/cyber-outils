'''Burra fish''' were a [[species]] of [[fish]] native to the [[planet]] of [[Dathomir]], who's flesh was [[Diet|edible]] by the [[Kiffar]], [[Dathomirian]]s, and [[Latero]]. After [[fishing]] families on the planet [[Batuu]] cultivated the foreign Burra fish for consumption, the Burra fish was sold as a part of several notable dishes at the [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu and aboard the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''.

==Description==
Burra fish,<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> referred to plurally as burra,<ref name="CYD: ACTM">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Clone Trooper Mission]]''</ref> were a [[species]] of [[fish]] from the<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="GalaxyMap">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Dathomir]]. The fish were notably known as a food item, consumable by several [[Sentience|sentient]] species such as the [[Dathomirian]]s, [[Kiffar]],<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> and [[Latero]].<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> Canned burra fish were often tightly packed together,<ref name="Hunters">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref> and cooked burra fish was often [[Color|brown]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">{{GEAttr|Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo}}</ref> or beige in color. They [[Reproduction|reproduced]] by laying small, black [[egg]]s.<ref name="GS">{{GSAttr|Crown of Corellia Dining Room}}</ref>

There were also several slang terms centered around burra fish. To [[List of phrases and slang#H|have bigger burra fish to fry]] was slang that meant to have more important or more interesting things to do or attend to.<ref name="PP">''[[Pirate's Price]]''</ref><ref name="Battle Scars">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref> To be [[List of phrases and slang#S|snared like burra in a trap]] was slang that could be said when someone was stuck, especially when in mud.<ref name="CYD: ACTM" />

==History==
[[File:Darkdisciple.png|thumb|right|250px|Asajj Ventress and Quinlan Vos (both pictured) ate a burra fish stew together on Dathomir.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="DD-Date">{{TCWcanondates|DD}}</ref> the Dathomirian [[bounty hunter]] and former [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] brought the Kiffar [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]] to Dathomir, in order to learn about her [[homeworld]] and her history as a [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]]. During this time, Vos went out and killed a burra fish, and the two ate [[stew]] with burra fish later that [[Standard day|day]].<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> The [[Latero]] [[captain]] [[Greez Dritus]] often made burra fish in his [[starship]], the ''[[Stinger Mantis]]'', which he described as poorly cooked.<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

[[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] used the slang term "bigger fish to fry"<ref name="Battle Scars" /> sometime between [[12 BBY]] and [[11 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{BattleScarsDate}}</ref> to describe his frustration with having to constantly deflect attacks from the bounty hunters of the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate.<ref name="Battle Scars" /> The [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] also used the term,<ref name="PP" /> in [[10 BBY]],<ref name="PP-Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of Part One of ''[[Pirate's Price]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> after noticing that a [[Narquois]] in the [[Undervaults of Gwongdeen]] storage facility tried to conceal her treasure from him. At this time, Ohnaka was preoccupied helping the [[Cloning|clone]] [[Mahjo Reeloo]] place a [[bomb]] in [[Jayyar Lu-wehs]]' storage box.<ref name="PP" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Hunters-Date">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'' takes place after [[Aran Tal]] joined the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]], dated to [[9 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As ''Battle for the Arena'' features [[Rieve]] join the Hunters, it takes place before ''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]'', and the [[The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters|in-universe]] book is set before [[The Mandalorian Season Three|''The Mandalorian'' Season Three]]. Season Three takes place in 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] per the reasoning [[Template:MandoDate|here]], therefore ''Battle for the Arena'' must be set around the same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> as [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] performer and contestant [[Rieve]] visited the [[Oasis Cantina]] in the [[Backwater District]] of the planet [[Vespaara]] and heard the music of the performing [[Unidentified gonkrock band|gonkrock band]], she expressed that she missed the positive and nostalgic vibe of people being packed together like canned burra fish to listen to music.<ref name="Hunters" />

[[Fishing]] families on the [[planet]] Batuu cultivated the foreign Burra fish for consumption.<ref name="GEStaff">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] has official backstory elements that, though not directly published for the public, are officially canon details that can be obtained verbally from cast members working in the land.</ref> [[Oven-roasted Burra Fish]] was a dish available for purchase at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu<ref name="Nerdist">{{WebCite|url=https://nerdist.com/article/star-wars-galaxys-edge-food-drinks-cocktails-menu-disney-parks|text=What You Can Eat and Drink in STAR WARS: Galaxy's Edge|author=[[Amy Ratcliffe]]|date=2019-02-27|work=Nerdist|archivedate=20190228074254}}</ref><ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GEDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Around this same time,<ref name="GSDate">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}} states that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser]]'' takes place between the events of {{Film|VIII}} and {{Film|IX}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of {{Film|VII}} to [[34 ABY]]. As ''The Last Jedi'' takes place immediately after the end of ''The Force Awakens'', it must also take place in 34 ABY. ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates ''The Rise of Skywalker'' to a [[Standard year|year]] after the events of ''The Force Awakens''. Thus, ''The Rise of Skywalker'' takes place in [[35 ABY]] and ''Galactic Starcrusier'' takes place between 34 ABY and 35 ABY.</ref> [[Burra Fish Caviar]] and [[Tamarind-Glazed Burra Fish]] were sold on [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser]], the ''[[Halcyon]]''.<ref name="GS" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BurraFish-BrickLife.png|250px|thumb|right|A depiction of the burra fish in ''LEGO Fortnite Brick Life'']]
Burra fish first appeared in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]'', which was written by [[Christie Golden]].<ref name="DarkDisciple" /> The species originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[1994]] novel ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]'', which was written by [[Dave Wolverton]].<ref name="Courtship">''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''</ref> Closed captioning in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'' erroneously misspells "burra" as "burrah."<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
