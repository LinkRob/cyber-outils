This [[Color|blue]]-bladed '''lightsaber''' was owned by [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] when he was [[Jedi training|apprenticed]] to [[Jedi General]] [[Jaro Tapal]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />

Kestis had been practicing his skills in [[lightsaber combat]] under his [[Jedi Master|Master's]] supervision when [[Order 66]] was issued to the [[clone trooper]]s, forcing the young [[Jedi]] to defend himself against his own [[soldier]]s. He lost his [[lightsaber]] in a [[turbolift]] shaft while trying to [[Skirmish aboard the Albedo Brave|escape]] from the [[13th Battalion]] onboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Albedo Brave]]''.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

The weapon was shot out of his hands and fell to the bottom of the turbolift shaft, subsequently being lost for good when the warship was destroyed. However, Tapal gave his [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|damaged lightsaber]] to Kestis, who escaped to the [[planet]] [[Bracca]], before [[Death|dying]] of his wounds. Later, the young Jedi created a [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|new lightsaber]] by combining the one his master gave him and [[Cere Junda's lightsaber|the one]] from [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cere Junda]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />
