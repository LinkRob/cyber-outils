The '''Baldamiro Family''' was one of one five crime families&mdash;alongside the [[Rang Clan]], the [[Wandering Star]], the [[Droid Gotra]], and [[envoy]]s of the [[Hutt Clan]]&mdash;that made up the [[Crymorah Syndicate]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Baldamiro Family was first mentioned in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" />
