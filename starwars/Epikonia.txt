'''Epikonia''' was a terrestrial tech-focused [[planet]]<ref name="Star Wars 40" /> located in the [[Ojoster sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was a communications hub that relayed and boosted signals from its [[sector]] to other parts of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Star Wars 40" /> 

==History==
A facility on the planet produced data-discs for [[droid]] code updates.<ref name="Dark Droids 4" /> These were produced in large numbers and shipped throughout the sector by an automated fleet of [[TR-286 transport]]s.<ref name="Dark Droids 4" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020) 40|''Star Wars'' (2020) 40]] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[cyborg]] [[Lobot]] was taken to Epikonia by the [[Scourge]] after he had been [[Scourge crisis|infected]] during a [[Mission to Jabba's Palace (Lando Calrissian)|mission]] to<ref name="Star Wars 40" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Star Wars 40" />

The cyborgs [[Beilert Valance]] and [[Magna Tolvan]] were also taken over by the Scourge and accompanied Lobot to the planet, where the Scourge planned to use the facility to spread it's consciousness further. The inhabitants of the surrounding city were also captured by Scourge-infected droids and experimented upon.<ref name="Dark Droids 4" />

After his arrival on the world, Lobot was tracked to Epikonia by his friend [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], who, along with [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]], and the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]], undertook a [[Battle on Epikonia|mission]] to the world to rescue Lobot.<ref name="Star Wars 40" /> After the Scourge was [[Second attack on Scourge 01|defeated]], it's influence on the droids, cyborgs and organic inhabitants of the planet ceased.<ref name="Star Wars 41">[[Star Wars (2020) 41|''Star Wars'' (2020) 41]]</ref>
