'''Bondu''' was an ancient [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] hero. The Twi'lek surname Bonduna meant "descended from Bondu."<ref name="GCG">''[[Galactic Campaign Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bondu was first mentioned in the [[2003]] supplement book ''[[Galactic Campaign Guide]]'', written by [[J.D. Wiker]] and [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="GCG" />
