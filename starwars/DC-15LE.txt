The '''DC-15LE''' was a [[blaster rifle]] and variant of the standard [[DC-15A blaster rifle]], which could be modified for long-range engagements or to fire explosive [[blaster]] bolts. This variant was utilized by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[heavy weapons clone trooper]]s<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Purge Trooper]] [[Purge Trooper Commander|commanders]].<ref name="Fallen Order" /> The weapons were also used by Imperial [[clone trooper]]s and [[TK stormtrooper]]s,<ref name="Extraction" /> as well as members of the [[Clone Underground]].<ref name="Infiltration" />
