'''1977 Topps ''Star Wars'' Series 3''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[1977]].<ref name="Volume One" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], and featured stills from the film, promotional photos and behind the scenes photos, all within a yellow border. Backs of the cards featured an "Official Description" theme describing characters or vehicles, or included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle. A sub-set of sticker cards was included.<ref name="Volume One" />

Card numbering in the set continued from the [[1977 Topps Star Wars Series 2|previous series]]. Two more series of the set followed.<ref name="Volume One" />

==Continuity==
Cards 189 and 191 feature images from the deleted scene of Luke repairing a [[Moisture vaporator/Legends|moisture vaporator]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]].<ref name="blu-ray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref> [[Grand Moff/Legends|Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin/Legends|Tarkin]] is referred to as Governor of the Imperial Outlands (mostly known later as the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]]), and [[Lightsaber/Legends|lightsabers]] are called "light sabers."
