'''Bogano''' was a [[grass]]y [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was largely unexplored during the time of the [[Imperial Era]] and could not be found on any maps. Its surface was covered in mesas and wetlands. The planet was home to a great [[Bogano Vault|Vault]] built by the ancient [[Zeffonian]] civilization. Bogano was important to the Zeffo due the presence of the [[Binog]], a large creature important to Zeffo art and culture. Another reason for the Vault's location was theorized by [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] to be because of the light electromagnetic winds, although in much smaller conditions than [[Zeffo]]. At least one Zeffo Colony was located on Bogano likely as a pilgrimage site. The Vault hid away a [[holocron]] containing a list of [[Force-sensitive]] children in the [[galaxy]], which was placed there by Cordova.

==Description==
Bogano was a planet in the Outer Rim region of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Databank" /> It was a generally uninhabited world, and its nature was in turn relatively untouched. The world was primarily comprised of flat, [[grass]]y plains, broken up by massive ravines and [[sinkhole]]s<ref name="Fallen Order" /> made of [[limestone]] and [[clay]].<ref name="Art Book" /> There were also water-filled [[cave]]s beneath the thin surface layer.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==History==
===Ancient history===
Long before the Imperial Era, Bogano was an important world to the [[Zeffonian]] [[species]], due to the revered [[Binog]], an [[amphi-mammalian]] creature native to the planet. The Zeffo built a [[Bogano Vault|Vault]] on Bogano that was protected by [[the Force]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />

===Imperial Era===
Before the [[Great Jedi Purge]], Jedi Master Eno Cordova discovered the planet, building a [[Abandoned Workshop|workshop]] there.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
[[File:CereCalBoganoVault.png|thumb|250x250px|Jedi Padawan Cal Kestis and former Jedi Cere Junda arrive at the Bogano Vault in search of knowledge to rebuild the Jedi Order.]]
In [[14 BBY]], [[Cal Kestis]] was [[Mission to Bogano|brought]] to the planet by [[Cere Junda]] to unlock the secrets of the [[Bogano Vault]] in hopes of [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|rebuilding]] the [[Jedi Order]]. Kestis would return to the world in search of supplies hidden by Cordova.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

Kestis eventually returned to the planet and used the [[astrium]], an ancient Zeffo artifact that he had acquired from [[Dathomir]], to unlock the Vault. He was [[Second mission to Bogano|followed]] by the [[Second Sister]] who stole the holocron and escaped. Several squads of [[stormtrooper]]s were defeated by Kestis on the planet, meaning that the [[Galactic Empire]] had found Bogano.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

===Galactic Civil War===
[[File:StarWarsAdventuresBoganoVault.png|left|thumb|250x250px|Princess Leia Organa and aspiring Jedi Luke Skywalker checked to see if Bogano could be used as a rebel base.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] [[Mission to Bogano (Galactic Civil War)|visited]] the world to find out if Bogano could host a base for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. Immediately upon her arrival, she found her friend and fellow rebel [[Luke Skywalker]]. Leia asked why he was there and not checking out other planets. Luke said the Force took him there, and the two analyzed the planet together. The two found a [[bogling]] that led them to the Bogano Vault. Luke felt the Force was strong in the place, and although Leia thought the world might be a good base, Luke disagreed. Luke was proven correct soon thereafter, as the small animal led the two to a group of stormtroopers who were dealing with several animals of the same species.<ref name="Princess and the Bog 1">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=7|story=The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 1}}</ref> The stormtroopers intended to sell the animals, which could be considered valuable on the black market, but Luke and Leia defeated the Imperial troops in a confrontation and saved the animals before leaving the planet.<ref name="Princess and the Bog 2">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=8|story=The Princess and the Bog, A Twin Tale, Part 2}}</ref>

===New Republic Era===
During the [[New Republic Era]], a [[New Republic reconnaissance squad]] visited planets such as Bogano to collect [[Biologist|biological]] information of various [[species]], this information was compiled and published in the survival compendium "[[Species From A to Z]]."<ref name="OSWM">{{GermanFanCite|114|Database}}</ref>

During or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Creatures Date">The [[Galaxy of Creatures (book)|''Galaxy of Creatures'' book]] depicts all episodes of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]'' taking place a relatively short [[time]] apart. {{GoC|Porgs}} features [[porg]]s on [[Batuu]], a [[species]] that ''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]'' establishes were not present on the [[planet]] Batuu until the [[Resistance]] brought them there between the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] and [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. Therefore, porgs must have been introduced to Batuu between those stories, which corresponds to [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', meaning that the events of ''Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures'' take place during or after 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> Bogano was visited by by [[SF-R3]] and [[CAM]], who were in search of a [[Bogling]].<ref name="Bogling (episode)">{{GoC|Bogling}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Bogano_concept_art.png|thumb|250x250px|Concept art of Bogano from ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'']]
Bogano is a location that was created for the game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Databank" /> During development, the surface of Bogano was always imagined as a vast marsh. Respawn Entertainment faced the challenge of integrating wall running and platforming into a flat landscape. To solve this, Respawn Entertainment developed a layered geology for the planet consisting of constantly fracturing cratered limestone and clay, which in turn helped them introduce the visual themes of exploration and the continuous decay of time. Concept art for Bogano was done by [[Liam MacDonald]] and [[Gabe Yeganyan]].<ref name="Art Book" />
