'''Chief justice administrator''' was a title used during the early [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. By that [[time]], an [[Unidentified chief justice administrator|individual]] who held the titled was fired after eight [[Standard day|days]] on the job and was listed as a recent entry on Imperial [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[datapad]]. The [[chief justice]] was a position that also existed in the Galactic Empire during that time, with the title being held by [[Ayelowa Kefarra]].<ref name="Mask of Fear">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The title of chief justice administrator was first mentioned in ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', a [[2025]] novel written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Mask of Fear" />
