'''''Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead'' 4''' is the fourth issue of the miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Blood Ties: Boba Fett is Dead]]'' from [[Dark Horse Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The man behind the murder of Boba Fett has sent his army of mercenaries to kill Fett's ex-wife and child, and Boba's half-brother Connor Freeman''.

''But what if Boba Fett isn't quite as dead as everyone thinks? Then there might be . . . Hell. To. Pay.''
