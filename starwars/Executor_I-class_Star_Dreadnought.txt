The '''''Executor I''-class Star Dreadnought''' was a model [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] used by the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At one point, one such [[Star Dreadnought]] flew over a cloudy, [[Color|white]] celestial body, while flanked by multiple [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]]. The model was<ref name="Armada" /> 19,000 [[meter]]s long,<ref name="Tech" /> propelled by red-hued<ref name="Armada" /> [[Executor-50.x engine]]s<ref name="Tech" /> and protected by two<ref name="Armada" /> [[Kuat Drive Yards|Kuat Drive Yard]]-model [[deflector shield generator dome]]s,<ref name="Tech" /> and was succeeded by the [[Executor II-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor II''-class Star Dreadnought]].<ref name="Executor II">{{Armada|set=Super Star Destroyer Expansion Pack|cardname=''Executor II''-class Star Dreadnought}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Executor I''-class Star Dreadnought was mentioned on a card of the same name in the [[2019]] ''[[Super Star Destroyer Expansion Pack]]'' by [[Fantasy Flight Games]], for the miniatures game [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]].<ref name="Armada" />
