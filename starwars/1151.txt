'''1151''' was a [[clone cadet]] who was a part of the [[Clone Youth Brigade]] during the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
Like all [[clone cadet]]s, 1151 was created by the [[Kaminoan]]s for the sole purpose of military service. By [[21 BBY]], 1151 was training as a [[cadet]] aboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Endurance]]'', which was commanded at the [[time]] by [[Jedi General]] [[Mace Windu]] and [[Admiral]] [[Shoan Kilian|Kilian]]. Unbeknownst to the cadets, their unit had been infiltrated by budding [[bounty hunter]] and [[mercenary]] [[Boba Fett]], who [[Sabotage of the Endurance|sabotaged]] the [[Star Destroyer]] in an attempt to [[Death|kill]] Windu. After a [[bomb]] planted in Windu's quarters failed to kill him, Fett then destroyed the [[reactor]]. Seeing that the [[Starship|ship]] was beyond saving, Kilian ordered the cadets to abandon [[Starship|ship]]. [[Crasher]], a [[Clone Sergeant]] who was conducting the tour, ordered the eight cadets to split up into two groups of four. 1151 and three other cadets got into the [[escape pod]] occupied by Crasher, while Fett and 3 other cadets got into another pod.<ref name="Death Trap" />
