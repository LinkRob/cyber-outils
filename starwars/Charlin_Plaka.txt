'''Charlin Plaka''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] [[Jedi Knight]] who had a [[lightsaber duel]] with [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] the [[Standard day|day]] prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. Akaris remembered her duel with Plaka and [[Onielle]] shortly before the battle and newly doubted her abilities in combat. She enjoyed frequently bullying Akaris. She later survived [[Order 66]] alongside [[Klefan Opus]] and a [[Unidentified Zeltron Jedi|Zeltron Jedi]], but was captured by pirates on the Sith planet of [[Firrhana]]. She was later found by Akaris, who was now an [[Inquisitor]] known as the Thirteenth Sister, and who killed her alongside her fellow survivors.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Personality and traits==
As a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]], Charlin Plaka had [[Color|pink]] skin and green eyes. Plaka was an egotistical [[Jedi]] who believed herself to be superior to those who faltered or struggled, such as [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]], and who enjoyed giving subtle jabs towards and against her bullying victims. Eventually, her bullying would catch up to her, and Akaris, now a member of the [[Inquisitorius]], would be sent on a mission that involved finding and killing the [[Twi'lek]], during which Akaris taunted her for how she was treated in the past.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />
