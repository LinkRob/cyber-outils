'''B9-DT''' was a [[Color|black]] and red [[astromech droid]] owned and used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The droid contained a series of maps and Imperial transmissions. Shortly after the [[Battle of Endor|Battle]] of [[Endor]], which saw the destruction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] and the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], B9-DT was located on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]], in the [[Anoat sector]]. In their efforts to escape a [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] placed on the Anoat sector following the Battle of Endor, [[gangster]] [[Dapp|"Happy" Dapp]] and his [[Chadra-Fan]] ally [[Shortpaw]], leader of the [[Merchant|trade]] organization known as the [[Trade Spine League]], dispatched a [[The Smuggler|scoundrel]] to capture the droid on Hoth. After several firefights with [[Stormtrooper|Imperial forces]], the scoundrel succeeded and returned to [[Burnin Konn]] with B9-DT.<ref name="SW:U" />
