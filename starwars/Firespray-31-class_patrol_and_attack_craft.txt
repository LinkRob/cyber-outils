The '''''Firespray-31''-class patrol and attack craft''',<ref name="USW" /> also known simply as a '''''Firespray''-class patrol and attack craft''',<ref name="The Glass Abyss">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref> '''''Firespray''-class interceptor'''<ref name="SW:AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> or '''Firespray gunship''',<ref name="BOBF 4" /> was a [[Patrol ship|patrol]] [[Starship|ship]] manufactured by [[Kuat Systems Engineering]].<ref name="USW" /> ''Firespray''s were used to guard and patrol the [[prison]] [[moon]] [[Oovo 4]],<ref name="SW:AEYNTK" /> while [[bounty hunter]]s [[Jango Fett]] and [[Boba Fett]], throughout their careers, piloted a customized ''Firespray'' named ''[[Slave I]]'',<ref name="USW" /> which Jango had stolen from Oovo IV.<ref name="SW:AEYNTK" /> Another bounty hunter,<ref name="NoDisintegrations">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref> [[Koshka Frost]], also flew [[Koshka Frost's Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|her own]] ''Firespray-31''.<ref name="S&V">{{FFGXW2|set=Slave I Expansion Pack|cardname=Koshka Frost}}</ref> Amid a stalemate during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] of the [[Clone Wars]], a ''Firespray-31'' was flown by a pro-[[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] racketeer.<ref name="Separatist Racketeer">{{FFGXW2|set=Jango Fett's Slave I Expansion Pack|cardname=Separatist Racketeer}}</ref>
