A '''language''' was used by the [[Nightsisters]] of [[Dathomir]], primarily in their [[magick]] spells. [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] used it in her transformation of [[Savage Opress]] into a warrior of the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]],<ref name="Monster" /> and Old [[Daka]] would later use it in the [[Chant of Resurrection]] in order to resurrect the [[Zombie|undead]].<ref name="Massacre" /> Symbols of the language, were carved into the sides of the holder of [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s lock of hair.<ref name="Massacre" /><ref name="Massacre Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/massacre-episode-guide|text="Massacre" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Talzin again spoke the language after [[Maul|Darth Maul]] returned to his [[homeworld]], and Talzin exorcised him.<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref> Later around [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Fallen Order date">The events of ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' take place about five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. Therefore, it can be deduced that the events of ''Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order'' take place around [[14 BBY]].</ref> during an [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|encounter]] with the [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]], the Nightsister [[Merrin]] repeated the Chant of Resurrection, raising the undead Nightsisters. When hair-braiding together cloth for [[burial pod]]s, Nightisters recited a [[spell of protection]].<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Ahsoka Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> the [[Great Mothers]] used the Chant of Resurrection on [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]'s [[Night Trooper]]s to aid him against [[Ahsoka Tano]], while they were escaping [[Peridea]].<ref name="Part Eight" />

===Spell of Protection===
A [[spell of protection]] was recited while hair-braiding together cloth for burial pods.<ref name="Fallen Order" />
