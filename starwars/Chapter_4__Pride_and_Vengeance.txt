"'''Chapter 4: Pride and Vengeance'''" is the fourth episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 13]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 3: Whispers in the Unknown]]". Its title was revealed on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Official description==
''A confrontation between Lawson and Maul threatens everyone.''

==Plot summary==
===Settling scores===
[[Crime lord]] [[Looti Vario]] travels in a [[starship]] to the [[Pyke]] homeworld of [[Oba Diah]]. Speaking over the [[intercom]], Vario identifies himself and is given permission to land by the air traffic controller. Vario clutches a [[blaster pistol]] for protection. The starship lands on a docking ramp. Several droids exit the landing ramp and deliver crates of [[Currency|credits]]. The Pyke officer orders his men to count the money. Vario nervously warns them that [[Maul]] is here. A hooded [[Zabrak]] suddenly exits the the starship and cuts down two Pyke enforcers who were trying to board the [[cargo ship]]. "Maul" cuts down several other Pyke enforcers before being [[stun]]ned by Vario. 

Vario tells the Pyke officer to tell his master [[Marg Krim]] that he was the one who captured "Maul." The Pykes deliver Vario and a bound "Maul" into Krim's stronghold. Krim is aware about Maul's return and demands that Vario explain himself. Vario explains that he had been taken prisoner by Maul, who forced him to do his bidding. He asserts that he took care of Maul before he could get to Krim. Krim inspects the Zabrak prisoner and realizes that it is not Maul but [[Icarus]], one of his Zabrak underlings. 

Maul himself enters the throne room and uses the Force to summon the captive Zabrak's lightsaber and cut down the crime lord. Maul then assembles his two lightsabers into a [[Darth Maul's double-bladed lightsaber|double-bladed lightsaber]], which he uses to deflect the Pyke guards' blaster bolts. He cuts down several guards but leaves three men alive. Maul kneels over the fallen Krim, chastising the crime lord for denying him shelter following his escape from [[Mandalore]] and attempting to kill him. Addressing the three remaining Pykes, he warns them about the consequences of betrayal. After the Pykes kneel to Maul, he speaks with the [[Captain of the Guard]], [[Kalt]], and places him in charge of the [[Pyke Syndicate]]. He warns Kalt that he now answers to him.

===Master and apprentice===
Meanwhile on [[Janix]], [[Devon Izara]] runs through the streets and reaches a balcony platform. She finds her [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] in a narrow alleyway. The two embrace. Daki notices her injured left arm but she reassures him that she is alright. He leads her to a shelter where he passes Izara [[Devon Izara's lightsaber|her lightsaber]]. When Daki asks how she escaped Maul, Izara says that it was not easy. Daki thinks they should leave the planet since Maul's presence will attract the attention of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Izara wants to finish Maul off but Daki counsels against letting vengeance cloud her judgment. 

When Izara says that she wants to bring Maul to justice, Daki warns that [[the galaxy]] no longer sees the [[Jedi]] as the defenders of justice. Izara says there are good people on Janix and they cannot let him terrorize the people of the planet. Daki warns that Maul has a lot of mercenaries. When Izara suggests working with detective [[Brander Lawson]], Daki replies that he is familiar with the man. 

===A new lead===
At the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]], a police officer with dark skin and curly hair named [[Wade]] informs [[Captain]] Lawson that he has received a tip from an "Uncle Daki." When Lawson asks the location, Wade says that the transmission came from [[Sector 14-Q]]. Lawson says this is abandoned area. He seeks permission from [[Chief]] [[Klyce]] to send a strike team to the area. Klyce approves the mission but warns Lawson that he must succeed this time. 

As Lawson musters his squad, [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots") leaves his desk and tells his master that he must follow his protocol and contact the Empire for assistance. The droid enters a communication room and Lawson follows him. When Lawson asks him to give him and his squad a chance, Two Boots expresses concern that Lawson is putting himself and his squad in danger. Before Two Boots can transmit a message, Lawson deactivates Two Boots and apologizes.

===Maul's proposal===
In Sector 14-Q, Izara and Master Daki wait for Lawson to arrive. Izara is worried that their plan won't work but Daki expresses confidence that the lawman would respond to the information he had sent him. Daki senses movement and tells Izara that the [[Tactical Defense Force]] has arrived. Several [[speeder]]s arrive and deploy police officers and [[Tactical Defense Droid]]s. The arrival of the police also rouses [[Spybot]], who is itching for a fight. Lawson and his officers deploy a [[probe droid]] as they enter the dilapidated former factory. 

As the police converge in the center of the building, they encounter Vario, who tells Lawson that Maul has a message for them. He activates a [[holoprojector]]. Addressing Lawson, he describes the detective as a man of honor and intelligence. He warns Lawson that he is greatly outmatched. Lawson disagrees but Maul motions with his hand. Several of Maul's Mandalorian and Zabrak followers including [[Icarus]] and [[Scorn]] emerge from the shadows and converge around the police officers. Izara and Daki watch the situation from a glass window in the ceiling. Maul offers to make a deal with Lawson by appealing to his interest in wanting to avoid any Imperial involvement on Janix. 

When Lawson asks how he can be certain they are not on their way, Maul replies that the Empire would have been here by now had Lawson abided by Imperial protocol. Getting to business, Maul proposes a truce. He promises not to bother [[Janix city]] if Lawson will allow him to operate unimpeded. Maul emphasizes their shared interest in avoiding an Imperial takeover of the city. Daki senses that Maul is in the building and leads Izara. Meanwhile, the camera droid flies through the factory's corridors. 

Unwilling to compromise with criminals, Lawson declines Maul's offer of a truce. Maul regards Lawson's decision as disappointing but honorable. Lawson issues an ultimatum and demands that Maul and his mercenaries surrender to the Tactical Defense Force. He says that if they comply, they will be held in custody humanely and tried under Janix rather than Imperial law. Vario scowls and slinks away. Maul admires his spirit and wishes Lawson a "good death." Maul's mercenaries open fire on the police, who are kettled in the middle of the factory building.

===Light versus darkness===
The camera droid closes in Maul on but the Force wielder uses [[the Force]] to shatter the droid from the inside. Daki and Izara approach Maul together. Maul says he was expecting her and her master. Daki issues an ultimatum to Maul, telling him that they will not harm him if he surrenders. Maul mocks Daki for subscribing to what he regards as the passive ideology of the Jedi. Daki counters that the Jedi have yet survived and promises to deliver justice in dark times. Maul counters that the Jedi survive but do not lead, adding that the Jedi should lead and be respected by all.  

When Maul disparages the fugitive Jedi Master for eking out a harsh living, Daki counters that the two of them have different definitions of what it means to live. He says that the Jedi's powers are meant to be used only to serve others. Maul says that attitude has gotten the Jedi crushed by their inferiors and says that the Jedi unwillingness to use their power makes them weak. Izara listens to their conversation. When Daki responds that Maul is being delusional, Maul activates his double-bladed lightsaber and challenges them to a fight. 

The two Jedi draw their blue lightsabers and clash with Maul. Maul uses his double-bladed lightsaber to fend off their attacks. He corners Daki against a metal wall until Izara comes to her master's aid. Maul then retreats into the dark depths of the abandoned factory. Back on the factory floor, Captain Lawson decides to pursue Maul based on information provided by the camera droid prior to its destruction. When [[Rook Kast]] attacks his officers with a [[flamethrower]] attached to her vambrace, he hurls a police shield at her. Lawson then uses his blaster to collapse a rig. The Mandalorian manages to dive out of the way before the rig cashes. 

Elsewhere, Maul and the Jedi fight near a large tubular tank. Maul uses the Force to hurl Izara against a wall but she quickly recovers. Daki manages to gut a gash on one of Maul's mechanical legs. Maul retaliates by cornering Daki, who climbs a scaffolding platform. He pushes the Jedi Master into an unused conveyer belt system. Before Maul can strike him, Izara jumps in the way of her master and uses her lightsaber to force Maul back. Maul remarks that Daki is fortunate to have her by his side. 

===Retreat and flight===
Spybot spots more police speeders reaching the factory site and alerts Kast, who orders Maul's mercenaries to retreat. Spybot aids their retreat by spraying a foam that disorientates the police. Instead of finishing off the Jedi, Maul decides to spare them both and retreats with his forces. 

Shortly later, the two Jedi are cornered by Captain Lawson, who asks Daki why he didn't tell him that that they were Jedi. Daki alludes to the repressive political climate. When Lawson asks where is Maul, Izara says that he is gone. He hands Daki his lightsaber and tells the Jedi to go before reinforcements arrive. Outside the abandoned factory, Chief Klyce arrives and uses her [[macrobinoculars]] to scan the smoky building. Lawson exits the building and waves to her and the other police. 

As police reinforcements pour into the building, Maul struggles to keep up with Kast and the others due to his damaged right leg. To slow down the police, he uses the Force to collapse part of the tunnel, creating a barrier. Spybot catches up and says they are in big trouble. Kast senses something is happening. 

On the streets of Janix city, the older Daki stops to rest. When Izara asks if he is alright, he apologizes for failing her. Inside the factory, droids evacuate wounded and fallen police officers. Captain Lawson speaks with Two-Boots and apologizes for deactivating him. The droid officer accepts the apology and says that he hopes he can forgive him as well. Just then, the police officers and droids' comlinks begin beeping ominously. The episode ends with a silhouette of an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] flying over Janix City.
