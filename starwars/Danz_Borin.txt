'''Danz Borin'''<ref name="This Week">{{ThisWeek|zW4kLP9hlwQ|Galaxy of Creatures, Bring Home the Bounty, and More!|sw_url=video/galaxy-of-creatures-bring-home-the-bounty-and-more}}</ref> was a light-skinned, [[Color|brown]]-[[hair]]ed [[human]] who spent [[time]] in<ref name="ANH" /> [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> While in the [[cantina]], Borin wore a white [[flight suit]] with a sealed white [[helmet]] and stood by a wall speaking to another individual in a [[Environment suit|space suit]] when the local [[moisture farmer]] [[Luke Skywalker]] entered the cantina with the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]].<ref name="ANH" />

Borin then moved toward the bar, cup in [[Limb|hand]], before sitting down in one of the booths with a different space suited-individual behind a [[Morseerian]] and the [[Rodian]]<ref name="ANH" /> [[Neesh]].<ref name="Neesh">{{ForceCollection|Neesh|star=1}}</ref> When Skywalker and Kenobi later got into an altercation with the criminals [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] and [[Ponda Baba]] at the bar, Borin and the other suited individual stood and looked in their direction.<ref name="CL2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
Danz Borin was portrayed by [[Chris Bunn]]<ref name="FanthaTracks">{{WebCite|url=https://www.fanthatracks.com/news/conventions-events/echo-live-bespin-bazaar-saturday-1st-october-2022-new-guest-announcements|text=Echo Base Live Bespin Bazaar: Saturday 1st October 2022: New guest announcements|author=Bayliss, Carl|date=2022-08-24|archivedate=20220824121706}}</ref> in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> The shots that Borin appears in were filmed on Stage 6 at [[Shepperton Studios]] between [[April 12]] and [[April 22]], [[1976]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified when their name appeared as an incorrect answer to the trivia question "who introduced Obi-Wan Kenobi and Luke Skywalker to [[Han Solo]]" in an episode of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' that aired on [[October 14]], [[2021]].<ref name="This Week" /> The [[Danz Borin/Legends|character's]] name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used on a card in the [[1996]] "[[A New Hope Limited]]" set of cards for the ''[[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]]'' produced by [[Decipher]]<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=A New Hope Limited|cardname=Danz Borin}}</ref>
