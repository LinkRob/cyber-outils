'''Elsbeth's regime'''<ref name="Journey">{{InsiderCite|216|The Journey of Din Djarin}}</ref> was a [[dictatorship]] led by the industrialist [[Lady]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]] on the [[planet]] of [[Corvus]] who reigned as [[magistrate]] of [[Calodan]].

==History==
{{Quote|You said the Empire would help to change things. We trusted you. Put our lives in your hands.''"<br />"''When I found your village, you were nothing. Just one of a thousand backwater worlds rotting away. I built your fortifications. I gave you strength to control the local systems.''"<br />"''And we let you run our lives. We worked for that success.''"<br />"''You promised us wealth. We sacrificed for you.|[[Wing]], Morgan Elsbeth, and Calodan villagers|<ref name="Anger" />}}
After she had lost her people, the [[Nightsisters]],<ref name="Anger" /> to the [[Battle of Dathomir|horrors]] of the [[Clone Wars]], [[Morgan Elsbeth]] obtained the position of [[magistrate]] of the [[city]] of [[Calodan]], a walled-in city located on the [[forest]] planet of [[Corvus]],<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> her new homeworld.<ref name="SWCEUAE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> She then established a ruthless regime,<ref name="Journey">{{InsiderCite|216|The Journey of Din Djarin}}</ref> and employed the ex-[[Military rank|military]] [[mercenary]] [[Lang]], to act as both her personal enforcer and the [[Captain of the Guard|leader]] of her [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces|army]].<ref name="db-Lang">{{Databank|captain-lang|Captain Lang}}</ref> Her reign of terror lasted until her rule was [[Liberation of Calodan|brought down]] by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] and former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]<ref name="Chapter 13" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date" />
