'''Duradan Soup''' was a type of [[soup]] [[Foodstuff|dish]] that was sold in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Kijimi]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was made using [[Fisherfolk's broth]] with sweet [[algae]] and [[larva|larvae]] simmered in [[Buyan-Ki mineral water]] and served with [[water|frozen]] [[chakino eel]]. In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Foodstuff|dish]] was sold for 200 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] at the [[Koori]] stall in [[Kijimiko Square]] in Kijimi City.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics}}
During that same [[Standard year|year]], the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] and her [[Merqaal]] companion [[Nix]] share a meal of Duradan Soup in Kijimiko Square.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Duradan Soup was created for the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. It was one of the galactic street foods featured in the game which gave equippable perks to Nix. The Duradan Soup perk in ''Star Wars Outlaws'' allowed Nix to distract multiple targets but reduced the distraction range.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Continuity==
[[FileKooriMenuKijimi-Outlaws.jpg|thumb|right|100px|Koori's menu listing Duradan Soup in Aurebesh]]
The menu shown in the game lists the price of Duradan Soup as 125 credits, however when the player pays for the meal it cost 200 credits.
