'''Drewen Qweebjillan''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Segredo]] [[Gender|male]] who resided during the [[High Republic Era]]. While at [[Kul Kenada Spaceport]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] astronomical object [[Blarrum]], Drewen overheard about the [[Species|monster]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Ty Yorrick]], who had been hired to capture an escaped [[gundark]]. Drewen ventured out into the [[forest]] to watch Yorrick in action, surprising the hunter as she fought the beast. Despite almost being attacked by the gundark before being saved by Yorrick, Drewen did not mind, amazed by her skill. Yorrick scolded the Segredo for putting himself at risk, leaving on her [[speeder]], but Drewen followed her back to her [[Rancor Rider|starship]] at the [[spaceport]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Thank you. You saved my life. You're ''amazing!"<br />"''I know a droid that might disagree with you. A ''client'' too for that matter.''"<br />"''Are you ''kidding''? The way you brought it down with your flashy sword thing.|Drewen and Ty Yorrick|<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />}}
[[File:TyDrewenGundark-THRATemplePeak1.jpg|thumb|left|260px|The Segredo was caught in Yorrick's skirmish with a gundark.]]
The [[Segredo]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 2" /> Drewen Qweebjillan<ref name="Saber for Hire 1" /> lived during the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> Between [[232 BBY]] and [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]]. ''The Rising Storm'' establishes that the events on [[Blarrum]], depicted in [[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 1|''The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak'' 1]], take place three [[Standard month|months]] prior. The events of ''The Monster of Temple Peak'' 1 must therefore be set between 232 BBY and 231 BBY.</ref> Drewen was present at the [[Kul Kenada Spaceport]] on [[Blarrum]], an astronomical object in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. While at the [[spaceport]], he heard about the [[Mercenary|saber-for-hire]] and [[Species|monster]] [[Hunting|hunter]] [[Ty Yorrick]], who had been hired by the [[Merchant|beast-trader]] [[Triv Blago]] to capture an escaped prize [[gundark]]. Hearing of Yorrick's skill, the Segredo wished to see her in action, following her out into the [[forest]]. As Drewen watched Yorrick attempt to wrangle the gundark, the monster hunter was thrown to the ground in front of him. Surprised, Drewen greeted Yorrick, who in turn questioned who he was.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

With the gundark approaching, however, Yorrick disregarded her question, instructing Drewen to avoid being caught by the creature. The Segredo ran from the gundark, screaming as the beast pursued him. With Drewen in danger, Yorrick&mdash;a former [[Jedi]]&mdash;brought out her [[Ty Yorrick's purple-bladed lightsaber|lightsaber]], cutting off the gundark's head and [[Death|killing]] the creature as it closed in on the Segredo. Drewen thanked Yorrick for saving his life, in awe at both her skill and her [[lightsaber]]. When Yorrick asked him why he had been there, Drewen told her that he had wanted to watch her at work. Yorrick scolded the Segredo for putting himself at risk as she left on her [[speeder]], but Drewen did not mind, believing his near-death experience was worth it to see Yorrick's skills.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

After Yorrick returned to Kul Kenada Spaceport, Drewen followed her. When the monster hunter arrived back at her [[Rancor Rider|starship]] after a brief altercation with Blago, Drewen was hiding behind a stack of crates near the [[entry ramp]]. Yorrick subsequently departed aboard her [[starship]], heading for a new job on [[Loreth]].<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Yeah, well, seeing me in action almost got you ''killed''. Nice work.''"<br />"''I don't mind&mdash;it was worth it.|Ty Yorrick and Drewen|<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />}}
Drewen was a Segredo male<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 2" /> who was referred to by Yorrick as a "kid." He possessed [[Color|purple]] [[hair]] and fur, light gray skin, and blue [[eye]]s.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> The Segredo stood at a height of 1.57 [[meter]]s (5 [[Foot|feet]] 2 inches) tall.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> Drewen was willing to put his life at risk to watch Yorrick capture a gundark, unconcerned by the danger and not minding that the creature nearly killed him. After being saved by Yorrick, Drewen was amazed at her abilities and the use of her lightsaber.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

==Powers and abilities==
Aside from his latent [[Force-sensitive|Force sensitivity]] Drewen was trained in the use of blasters and possessed some knowledge of close quarters combat.<ref name="Saber for Hire 4">[[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 4|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 4]]</ref> He was capable of piloting starships and like all Segredo he could make himself invisible with a [[mind trick]].<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 2" />

==Equipment==
Drewen wore a light tan [[Clothing|shirt]] with a wide black collar and a plate on the left shoulder, as well as brown pants and a dark gray [[belt]].<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Drewen Qweebjillan was introduced, albeit unnamed, in the [[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]]. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Rachael Stott]], and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" /> as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia initiative's Phase I<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> on [[August 11]], [[2021]].<ref name="Comixology #1">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-The-Monster-of-Temple-Peak-1-of-4/digital-comic/946350|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures&mdash;The Monster of Temple Peak #1 (of 4)}}</ref> He was identified as Drewen in the [[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 2|second issue]],<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 2" /> published on [[September 15]] of that year.<ref name="Comixology #2">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-The-Monster-of-Temple-Peak-2-of-4/digital-comic/951951|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures—The Monster of Temple Peak #2 (of 4)}}</ref> His surname of Qweebjillan was given in the [[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 1|first issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire]]'' comic series, written by Scott, illustrated by Stott, and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Saber for Hire 1">[[The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire 1|''The High Republic Adventures – Saber for Hire'' 1]]</ref> as part of the initiative's Phase III<ref name="Phase" /> on [[April 17]], [[2024]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3014-604/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures--Saber-for-Hire-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic--Saber for Hire #1}}</ref>

Prior to the release of the comic, Qweebjillan was pictured in a live-stream launch event for ''The High Republic'' on [[January 4]], 2021.<ref name="Livestream">{{SWYouTube|uILPgvzj708|Star Wars: The High Republic &#124; Live Stream Launch Event}}</ref> The depiction of Qweebjillan on the cover of the first issue of ''The Monster of Temple Peak'' differs from his actual appearance in the comic; the cover portrays him with green skin, purple hair, yellow eyes, and no antennae.<ref name="The Monster of Temple Peak 1" />
