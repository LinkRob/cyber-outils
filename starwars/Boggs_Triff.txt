'''Boggs Triff''' was an [[Arcona]] [[Gender|male]] [[Crime lord|crime boss]] who led a [[Boggs Triff's mob|mob]] on [[Nar Shaddaa]].<ref name="Tales" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]] was hired to [[Death|kill]] Triff.<ref name="Tales" /> [[Standard year|Years]] later, this incident was noted by [[Hondo Ohnaka]] in ''[[The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''.<ref name="SOTBH">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Boggs Triff appeared in the [[2021]] video game ''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]'', and was voiced by [[Darin De Paul]].<ref name="Tales" />
