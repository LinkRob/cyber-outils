'''''Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent'' 5''' is the fifth and final issue of the [[2025]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra – Chaos Agent]]''. The issue was written by [[Cherish Chen]], illustrated by [[Steven Cummings]] and [[Michael Atiyeh]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[October 29]], 2025.

==Publisher's summary==
''WHO SLICED THE SLICER? To avoid prison, DOCTOR APHRA became an agent of the NEW REPUBLIC! But to keep her in line during her parole, they had her electro-tattoos locked. But who could do that to the galaxy's greatest slicer? The answer WILL SHOCK YOU!''
