A '''blanket''' was a type of fabric used for covering, protection, warmth, and bedding.

When [[Zare Leonis]] attended the [[Arkanis Academy]] on [[Arkanis]], his [[Strategy and Tactics]] instructor [[Colonel]] [[Julyan]] sometimes kept his lower body wrapped in a [[Color|black]] blanket. [[Holshef]], a [[poet]] in hiding from the [[Galactic Empire]] for dissident ridings, made a nest of ratty [[pillow]]s and blankets when hiding out at [[1044 Chapel]].<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] kept a baby blanket, made of [[gillendown]], inside her [[Leia Organa's keepsake chest|keepsake chest]].<ref name="BL">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>
