The title of '''Chief Inquisitor''' was one of the three highest ranks in the [[Imperial Intelligence/Legends|Imperial Intelligence]] division of the [[Inquisitorius/Legends|Inquisitorius]]. The only two higher ranks were [[High Inquisitor]] and the [[Grand Inquisitor/Legends|Grand Inquisitor]], the latter of which was the head of the Inquisitorius. A known Chief Inquisitor was [[Loam Redge]].
