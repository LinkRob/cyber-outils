{{Quote|Indeed, most administrative recruits who entered the bureaucracy of the Empire were provided with a copy of Chief Administrator Svete Naemon's gargantuan work ''A Thorough Guide to the Bureaucratic Operation and Diligent Administration of the Galactic Empire'' which, from my own research, was not an easy read.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
'''''A Thorough Guide to the Bureaucratic Operation and Diligent Administration of the Galactic Empire''''' was a large text [[author]]ed by [[Chief Administrator]] [[Svete Naemon]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It detailed the Empire's complicated organizational structure and was given to most of its administrative recruits. The [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] read the text as part of the research for ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', a [[book]]<ref name="Rise" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> published<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> Kin did not find Naemon's work an easy read.<ref name="Rise" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
''A Thorough Guide to the Bureaucratic Operation and Diligent Administration of the Galactic Empire'' was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
