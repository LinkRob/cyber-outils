"'''Celebrating 10 Years of Kamino's Finest'''" is an article written by [[Mark Newbold]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 133|''Star Wars Insider'' 133]] in [[2012]]. The article serves to celebrate a decade of [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] in the [[501st Legion/Legends|501st Legion]].

Newbold interviewed [[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st]] founder Albin Johnson (USA, 501st Legion, TK 210), Dave G Hildreth (UK, UK Garrison, SL 8511), Nick Bishop (Tasmania, Thylacine Garrison, TK6273) and Adam Santorski (Poland, Polish Garrison, ID 8098).
