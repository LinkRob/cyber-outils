'''Colin Trevorrow''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] filmmaker best known for directing the [[2015]] film [[Wikipedia:Jurassic World|''Jurassic World'']] and the [[2012]] film [[Wikipedia:Safety Not Guaranteed|''Safety Not Guaranteed'']].

Trevorrow was originally set to direct ''Star Wars'': Episode IX, the third and final film in the [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Trevorrow Announced" /> His other contribution to the sequel trilogy was suggesting [[Rian Johnson]] to include a shot of [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] meeting for the first time in [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']].<ref name="Trevorrow Rise of Skywalker">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/movies/news/colin-trevorrow-star-wars-rise-skywalker-last-jedi|text=Star Wars: Colin Trevorrow On His Rise Of Skywalker Writing Credit And His Last Jedi Contribution &ndash; Exclusive|author=De Semlyen, Nick|date=2019-11-22|archivedate=20200701053935}}</ref> However, on [[September 5]], [[2017]], it was announced that Trevorrow had left Episode IX,<ref name="Colin Out">{{SW|url=news/a-statement-regarding-star-wars-episode-ix|text=A Statement Regarding ''Star Wars: Episode IX''}}</ref> which he co-written with [[Derek Connolly]] and was titled [[Star Wars: Episode IX Duel of the Fates|''Duel of the Fates'']],<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/colin-trevorrow-star-wars-script|title=Colin Trevorrow 'Star Wars' Script Leaks, Reveals a Very Different Movie Called 'Star Wars: Duel of the Fates'|author=Chris Evangelista|date=2020-01-15|archivedate=20200116045953}}</ref> although he retained story credit when the project was re-envisioned as [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="Trevorrow Rise of Skywalker" /> ''The Rise of Skywalker'' credits Trevorrow as co-writing the story with Connolly, [[J.J. Abrams]] (who also directed) and [[Chris Terrio]]. He told [[Wikipedia:Empire (film magazine)|''Empire'']] magazine that he would donate his residuals from the film to the Alexander Devine Children's Hospice in Berkshire. (A representative from the organization described it as "the equivalent of paying for a nurse for a year."<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2019/11/star-wars-rise-of-skywalker-colin-trevorrow-donating-star-wars-rise-of-skywalker-salary-alexander-devine-childrens-hospital-1202795416|text=Colin Trevorrow Donating 'Star Wars: Rise Of Skywalker' Residuals To Alexander Devine Children's Hospice|author=Fleming Jr., Mike|date=2019-11-26|archivedate=20191224065517}}</ref>)
