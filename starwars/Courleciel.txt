'''Courleciel S.A.R.L.''' (or simply '''Courleciel''') was the publisher of the [[Wikipedia:French|French]] official [[Star Wars Magazine (French)|''Star Wars Magazine'']]. The company, under the direction of [[Patrice Girod]], was based in [[Wikipedia:Paris|Paris]], France. Its name stemmed from "Luc Courleciel", the name under which [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] was credited in the original French VHS of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. In fall [[2009]], just before the release of the ''[[Star Wars Magazine 80]]'', Courleciel closed its business<ref name="last">{{SWArchive|url=fans/media/news20100122swm/index.html|text=Download the Last Issue of the French ''Star Wars Magazine''}}</ref> because of the impact of the worldwide economic crisis.<ref name="MIB">{{WebCite|url=http://www.mintinbox.net/Actus/09-12/1204courleciel|text=Courleciel&mdash;Le communiqué officiel de Lucasfilm|work=Mintinbox.com|archivedate=20121106165155}}</ref>
