'''Coruscant's Training Room''' was a training room in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the planet [[Coruscant]]. It was used by [[Jedi]] to train their [[lightsaber]] skills.<ref name="Training" /> At some point around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|101A}}</ref> [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Kai Brightstar]] used the room for lightsaber training.<ref name="Training" />
