'''Denn Maskar''' was a [[human]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. Holding the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[First Lieutenant|first lieutenant]], Maskar was one of twelve [[TIE series|TIE]] [[pilot]]s who were loaned to the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sanction]]'' for an anticipated [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Battle at Trenchenovu|attack]]. Maskar's [[squadron]] ultimately ambushed and defeated the rebel [[Nath Tensent's squadron|attackers]]. However, the [[Twin ion engine|TIE]] pilot was [[Death|killed]] by an explosion from sabotaged [[fuel]] piping almost a [[Standard month|month]] later.

==Biography==

During the [[Galactic Civil War]],<ref name="Alphabet" /> the [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> Denn Maskar served as a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilot]] in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]], holding the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[First Lieutenant|first lieutenant]].<ref name="Alphabet" /> Six [[Standard month|months]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Maskar was among a [[squadron]] of twelve [[TIE series|TIE]] [[pilot]]s loaned to the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Sanction]]'' by the 204th's [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]]. The lending of the pilots was in preparation to defend the [[Trenchenovu shipyards]] against an anticipated [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Battle at Trenchenovu|attack]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

[[File:Nath_Tensent.png|thumb|left|220px|Nath Tensent (pictured) wanted revenge on Denn Maskar's squadron for the loss of his own squadron, only to find out the members of Maskar's squadron were all dead.]]
When the attack came, Maskar's squadron ambushed the [[Nath Tensent's squadron|squadron]] of rebel [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]]s which attempted a bombing run on the [[Trenchenovu]] [[shipyard]]s. Losing multiple [[rebel pilot]]s, the [[bomber]]s were cut from their first escape route by the ''Sanction'' and chose to fly to the other side of the shipyards. Maskar and the other TIE pilots made chase, whittling down the rebels until only the Y-wing squadron's [[Squadron commander|commander]], [[Nath Tensent]], remained; he was able to escape into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Alphabet" />

Almost a month later, Maskar was [[Death|killed]] by an explosion on a flight deck caused by sabotaged [[fuel]] piping supplied by Alliance sympathizers. Around six months after that, Tensent confronted Nuress, wishing to find the names of the pilots who had killed his squadron so that he could get his revenge. After learning from the colonel that Maskar and the rest of the TIE pilots that served at Trenchenovu had died since the battle, he instead murdered Nuress when she confirmed that she had approved Maskar's squadron's deployment to Trenchenovu.<ref name="Alphabet" />
