'''Bon Yoth''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] before he argued with [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]] and left the Alliance. He was the contact of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] on the planet [[Basteel]] in the mission known as [[Operation Yellow Moon]]. He helped to place a [[hyper-transceiver beacon]] in the [[Corva sector]] in an attempt to distract the [[Galactic Empire]] from the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] gathering over [[Sullust]] in order to launch its assault on the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] at [[Endor]]. However, he wasn't interested in rejoining the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="MT" />
