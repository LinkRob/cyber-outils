'''FE-B3''', often referred to as '''Fee-Bee''', was a [[protocol droid]] designed to work as a translator for the [[Haddrex Gang]].

==Biography==
While searching for information on the [[crime lord]] [[Xev Xrexus]], [[Cad Bane]] and [[Cad Bane's group|his crew]] came across a [[freighter]] belonging to the Haddrex Gang. The [[bounty hunter]]s [[Altercation on Nar Shaddaa|killed]] all the members on board except for their protocol droid, FE-B3. FE-B3 was [[torture]]d and interrogated by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]] and the [[Chadra-Fan]] bounty hunter [[Troo-tril-tek]]. FE-B3 eventually provided his assailants with the location of Xrexus' [[Auction of Eldra Kaitis|auction]] as well as the verification codes for the event. FE-B3 was brought along to the auction in the [[Drazkel system]], where his pleas to be jettisoned into [[Realspace|space]] went unanswered.<ref name="Darth Maul 2" /> FE-B3 was taken with Maul and Bane's group after they killed [[Jee Kra]] and his crew. After escaping [[Xev's hideout]] with [[Eldra Kaitis]] and crashing onto the [[moon of Drazkel]],<ref name="Darth Maul 3">[[Darth Maul (2017) 3|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 3]]</ref> FE-B3 encountered Maul, and asked for help. However, Maul destroyed him with his [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Maul 5" />
