The '''All Terrain Tactical Enforcer''' ('''AT-TE'''), nicknamed "'''six legs'''" by the [[Separatist Droid Army]], was a multipurpose military ground [[walker]] and [[tank]] used by the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It had six legs, six [[laser cannon]] turrets along the body and a heavy projectile [[mass-driver cannon]] on the top of the walker. It was one of the main ground vehicles for the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] throughout the [[Clone Wars]]. 

The Republic's successor, the [[Galactic Empire]], retained and made extensive use of the AT-TE during the early years of its [[Imperial Era|reign]], before largely being replaced by other walker models by the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], while some fell into the hands of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:AT-TE-front.png|thumb|left|250px|The front view of an AT-TE walker.]]
The All Terrain Tactical Enforcer was a low-built vehicle that featured six articulated legs for propulsion. Its body consisted of two armored halves that were connected by a flexible concertina section (also housing the walker's power generators), which increased the walker's mobility on the field; it was essentially a mechanical version of the [[arctic horny whelmer]], a hardy animal native to [[Rothana]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> Because its legs were low to the ground, the walker had a relatively low top speed, but it also enjoyed greater stability.<ref name="Databank AT-TE" /> The AT-TE's low center of gravity, paired with its widely spread six legs made them have more stable footing than the taller, four-legged [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s.<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> Each of the AT-TE's legs had triple-jointed hydraulic [[limb]]s.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia Updated & Expanded" />

As its name suggested, the AT-TE was suitable for traversing all types of planetary surfaces, as its footpads were equipped with terrain sensors and [[Tractor beam|tractor-field generators]].<ref name="Databank AT-TE" /><ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> Not only was it able to climb sheer surfaces, it could also scale certain metal surfaces thanks to its ability to magnetize its feet.<ref name="Databank AT-TE" /><ref name="EOFSAOV">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref><ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> Furthermore, the walker was capable of surviving in the [[vacuum]] of [[realspace]] as it was able to be pressurized.<ref name="Databank AT-TE" /><ref name="Cargo of Doom" />

The AT-TE's most powerful weapon was a single [[mass-driver cannon]] located on the vehicle's dorsal surface, which gave the walker the purpose as a mobile artillery vehicle as well as a tank. In addition to bombarding fixed positions, the mass driver could be used to shoot down slow-moving aircraft<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> as well as intercept incoming [[Missile|rocket]] fire.<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref> The mass-driver cannon had a slow rate of fire,<ref name="SWEncyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> however, four smaller ball-turret laser cannons were mounted at the front, and two others at the rear, to defend the AT-TE from infantry.<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> The AT-TE was excellent in conjunction with the infantry, as the AT-TE could lay down covering fire from an elevated angle and the forward ball-turrets cannons were effective against battle droids.<ref name="SWEncyclopedia" /> The hexapod's armor could easily deflect small arms fire, and its conductive material spread the heat from weapons fire to minimize penetration.<ref name="Databank AT-TE" /><ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> The AT-TE was also well-shielded against [[electromagnetic pulse]] and [[Ion cannon|ion weaponry]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /> However the armor could be pierced by [[tanksmasher]] rockets, which could result in the destruction of the vehicle's complex propulsion mechanisms.<ref name="Databank AT-TE" />

[[File:ATTEpilot-FFp61.png|thumb|right|250px|An AT-TE clone trooper pilot in Phase I armor]]
The walker's crew consisting of a [[pilot]] and a spotter seated in a forward compartment; four gunners/support crew, two each seated in the forward and rear hull sections; and a turret gunner who sat exposed atop the walker to operate the mass-driver cannon. The spotter used a [[rangefinder]] periscope to select targets and coordinate the fire of the gunners, who operated their weapons via targeting controls.<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /><ref name="SWE AT-TE" /> The pilot sat in the wheelhouse, which served as both the driver and gunner cabin.<ref name="Epic Battles" />

The standard version of the AT-TE could seat two squads or 20 [[clone trooper]]s split between the forward and rear sections. [[Acceleration compensator]]s beneath their seats helped keep the troopers in place with seat restraints as backup in case of system failure.<ref name="Complete Vehicles, New Edition" /><ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Some variants also carried smaller [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s in their rear hold and some variants had a command center in the rear<ref name="SWE AT-TE" /> instead which was usually used as a mobile command center. [[Turbolift|Elevators]] could also be installed at the walker's rear to unload cargo.<ref name="Epic Battles" /> The crew and passengers were assisted by an onboard [[IM-6 Battlefield Medical Droid|IM-6 medic droid]], who attended to any injured troopers.<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
[[File:Acme-TheClonesAdvance.png|thumb|left|400px|AT-TEs made their debut at the First Battle of Geonosis.]]
Built by [[Rothana Heavy Engineering]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> the All Terrain Tactical Enforcer became the principal battle tank of the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Databank AT-TE" /> The [[B1-series battle droid]]s of the [[Separatist Droid Army]] took to calling the AT-TEs "six legs."<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> It was used in many large scale open battles<ref name="Supply Lines">{{TCW|Supply Lines}}</ref><ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref><ref name="TZB">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref> and was also used in operations smaller in scale.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" /> As the long war carried on, the AT-TE would regularly prove its worth through its versatility and mettle.<ref name="MicroGalaxy">{{Amazon|url=STAR-WARS-Micro-Galaxy-Squadron/dp/B0CMYSN5ZG|text=Star Wars Micro Galaxy Squadron at-TE - 9-inch Six-Legged Vehicle with 2.5-Inch Speeder and Five 1-Inch Micro Figure Accessories}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Teth]], AT-TEs charged up a mountain face to rescue [[Rotta]] the [[Hutt]]let while incurring fire from Separatist forces.<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> During the [[Battle of Devaron]], [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], knowing that the walkers were pressurized and had magnetized feet, deployed two AT-TEs, [[ATAT300]] and [[773]], onto [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]]'s [[Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] while it was in the vacuum of space. This strategy was condemned by [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] when Skywalker proposed it, but [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] understood his logic and commended his thinking. Ultimately, it was successful, though Skywalker and his team were unable to retrieve the [[Jedi]] [[holocron]] they were sent to retrieve.<ref name="Cargo of Doom" />

[[File:Verticle_Battle_RotS_Vlad_Ricean.png|thumb|right|300px|All Terrain Tactical Enforcers charge up a [[skyscraper]] during the Clone Wars.]]
AT-TEs continued to be used throughout the war.<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref> The success of the AT-TE also led to the creation of the [[All Terrain Heavy Enforcer]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> During the [[Battle of Kaller|Conquest of Kaller]], at least one AT-TE of General [[Depa Billaba]]'s [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|clone force]] fought against<ref name="Aftermath episode">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> a [[battalion]]<ref name="Aftermath Episode Guide">{{SW|url=aftermath-episode-gallery|text="Aftermath" Episode Gallery}}</ref> of [[battle droid]]s, but the walker was taken out by fire from [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]]s.<ref name="Aftermath episode" /> A blast from an AT-TE was [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]]'s chosen method in [[Death|executing]] [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] when he received [[Order 66]], since it allowed him to fire on Kenobi in a surprise attack, as Kenobi was not yet aware of the clones' betrayal. However, the blast only succeeded in knocking Kenobi into [[water]], and he later escaped to safety.<ref name="RotS" />

===Age of the Empire===
====Imperial service====
Upon the establishment of the [[Galactic Empire]], and in keeping with the doctrine of Imperial uniformity, Republic AT-TEs were stripped of their burgundy-[[color]]ed accents and thrust into Imperial Service. Several walkers were deployed on the Separatist capital of [[Raxus Secundus]], where they helped enforce the [[Occupation of Raxus Secundus|occupation effort]] headed by [[Captain]] [[Bragg]]. One day during the occupation, AT-TEs were called in to disperse protestors after the remaining senator of the [[Separatist Senate]], [[Avi Singh]], spoke out against the Imperial occupation. One AT-TE was later hijacked by [[Clone Force 99]] to rescue Singh and his droid [[GS-8]]. Several more AT-TEs were destroyed by the squad as they fled the city.<ref name="Common Ground" />

During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="18 Date">{{TBBDate|212}}</ref> numerous AT-TEs were positioned outside of the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ]] on [[Coruscant]] as a trio of clone troopers were forcibly retired and escorted out of the facility by Captain Bragg. [[Clone commando]] [[Crosshair]] watched on nearby before departing for a [[Mission to recover cargo|mission]] off-world on [[Barton IV]].<ref name="The Outpost">{{TBB|The Outpost}}</ref>

[[File:ImperialArmy-BountyHunters18.png|thumb|left|300px|An Imperial AT-TE takes part in a parade after the Liberation of Chorin City.]]
Following the [[Liberation of Chorin City|liberation]] of [[Chorin City]]<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18">[[Bounty Hunters 18|''Bounty Hunters'' 18]]</ref> fought a number of years before c. [[13 BBY]],<ref name="13 BBY Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Beilert Valance]] leaving [[Chorin]] to join the [[Galactic Empire]], an event that occurred after Chorin City's liberation according to [[Bounty Hunters 18|''Bounty Hunters'' 18]], to around [[13 BBY]]. Valance is visibly a number of [[Standard year|years]] older in the flashback of [[Target Vader 5|''Target Vader'' 5]], in which he leaves Chorin, than the flashback in ''Bounty Hunters'' 18.</ref> an AT-TE was part of an Imperial parade through the streets of the [[city]]. The AT-TE carried [[Captain]] [[Tarl Sokoli]], who had helped lead the [[liberation of Chorin]], and halted at Sokoli's order to meet a watching boy, [[Beilert Valance]].<ref name="Bounter Hunters 18" /> Five years after the end of the Clone Wars, AT-TEs could be found on the junkyard planet [[Bracca]] along with ships and other Republic war machines used during the conflict, where they were dismantled and their parts reused.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

====Days of Rebellion====
[[File:AT-TE-SW_Commander.png|thumb|right|250px|An All Terrain Tactical Enforcer used by the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.]]
Fifteen years after the end of the Clone Wars, [[Captain]] Rex, along with Commanders [[Wolffe]] and [[Gregor]], were living in a [[Rex's AT-TE|modified AT-TE]] on [[Seelos]].<ref name="TLC" /> They, along with the [[Spectres|Lothal Rebels]], later used the tank to evade [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces, which consisted of [[All Terrain Armored Transport (Prototype)|three]] All Terrain Armored Transport walkers. The tank's mass driver cannon was able to destroy one AT-AT in the sandstorm before Rex ordered a direct assault on the other two while the Rebels escaped. The mass-driver cannon was not strong enough to penetrate the AT-AT's armor, with the exception of the walkers' necks.<ref name="Relics">{{Rebels|Relics of the Old Republic}}</ref>

The AT-TE was able to sustain multiple shots from both AT-ATs before Wolffe rammed it into one of the walkers. The cannon was temporarily knocked out, but Gregor was able to restore power when the rebels returned and hijacked one of the AT-ATs, giving Rex the opening to shoot the last walker in the neck. The AT-TE was destroyed when the AT-AT collapsed on it.<ref name="Relics" /> Several AT-TEs were later modified by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Engineer|mechanics]] in order to be used as an attack vehicle during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. A number were modified with thicker armor and more powerful armament, becoming known as [[Elite AT-TE]]s.<ref name="Commander" /> At least one AT-TE was used in the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Defense of Kuat|defense]] of the [[Kuat Drive Yards]].<ref name="BS: Kuat">{{InsiderCite|168|Blade Squadron: Kuat}}</ref>
