'''D8-40''' was a [[droid]] with a [[Gender|male]] programming who resided in the [[Anoat sector]] at the time of the [[Iron Blockade]]. A lover of romantic literature and tragic holo stories, he was also one of the best (and most unusual) safecrackers in [[the galaxy]]. Although he claimed to be many [[Standard century|centuries]] old, his love of storytelling put everything he said in doubt.<ref name="D8-40" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the [[blockade]], D8-40 joined the crew of [[the Smuggler]], and was dispatched on a variety of missions throughout the sector.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref>
{{EndGame}}
