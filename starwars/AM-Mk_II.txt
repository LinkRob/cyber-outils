The '''AM-Mk II''' was a model of [[construction droid]] utilized during the mid-[[Republic Era]]. During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Veril Line Systems]] [[GT-series construction droid]] was the latest in a line of construction droids that dated back to the ancient AM-Mk II.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The AM-Mk II was mentioned in in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']], a [[2018]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />
