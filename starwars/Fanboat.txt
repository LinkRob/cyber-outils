A '''fanboat''' was a flat-bottomed vehicle used in [[swamp]]s that was powered by propeller fans projecting above its stern. The [[Pongeeta-class speeder|''Pongeeta''-class]] [[speeder]] retained the basic shape of a traditional [[Hutt]] fanboat, with [[Repulsorlift|repulsor]] technology and fan-shaped [[ion engine]]s instead of actual fans.<ref name="EOFSAOV">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref>
