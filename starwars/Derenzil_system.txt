The '''Derenzil system''' was a [[star system]] in of [[Wild Space]] that fell within grid square S-18 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Derenzil]], where a short-lived [[Derenzil civil war|civil war]] occurred<ref name="Complete Locations" /> by [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]'', to [[22 BBY]].</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Derenzil system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
