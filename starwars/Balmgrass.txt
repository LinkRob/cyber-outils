'''Balmgrass''' was a type of soft [[grass]] found on<ref name="Life Debt" /> the [[Core Worlds|Core World]] [[planet]]<ref name="MT">''[[Moving Target: A Princess Leia Adventure]]''</ref> [[Chandrila]] in the vicinity of [[Landing Platform OB-99]]. In [[spring]]time, the [[color]] of balmgrass changed from red to green.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Behind the scenes==
Balmgrass first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'', a [[2016]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]].<ref name="Life Debt" /> In the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' continuity, balmgrass was introduced in [[Greg Keyes]]'s [[2001]] novel ''[[The New Jedi Order: Edge of Victory II: Rebirth]]'', the eighth volume of the ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'' series.<ref name="NJOEVIIIR">''[[The New Jedi Order: Edge of Victory II: Rebirth]]''</ref> In the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', a green and white skin for the [[droid]] [[BD-1]] shares its name with balmgrass. The skin can be earned in the [[Tomb of Eilram]] on the planet [[Zeffo]].<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
