During the [[Galactic Civil War]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]] and her [[New Republic]] forces aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Deliverance]]'', continued their [[Hunt for Shadow Wing|hunt]] for the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. Days after their [[Third encounter with the Yadeez|previous encounter]] with the [[bulk freighter]] ''[[Yadeez]]'', another comm burst, that they were previously trailing, came through. With no tractor beam, the ''Deliverance'' emerged from [[hyperspace]] deep in-system in the hope of surprising the 204th. They arrived in an unmapped system with an alphanumeric designation, that was cluttered with planetoids and debris. The ''Yadeez'' and its escorts were forced toward one of the planetoids, to prevent them from jumping to [[lightspeed]]. The New Republic [[starfighter]] squadrons engaged in a skirmish with the 204th [[TIE series|TIEs]] that lasted six [[Standard minute|minutes]] before they escaped. Two TIEs were destroyed with no New Republic casualties, other than a single minor injury to [[Genni Avremif]] ([[Hail Six]]) after his ejector seat had accidentally triggered on landing.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
