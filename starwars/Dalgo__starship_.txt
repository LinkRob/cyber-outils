The '''''Dalgo''''' was a civilian [[Patrol ship|patrol craft]] used by [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]]. The [[Starship|ship]] had the capability of multiple propulsion methods, including sustained [[Engine|thruster]] bursts and also antigravity repulsors.<ref name="MOF" />

The ''Dalgo'' shared its name with [[dalgo]]s,<ref name="MOF" /> a [[Reptile|repitilian]] [[species]] native to Gerrera's [[homeworld]] [[Onderon]].<ref name="AWOTF">{{TCW|A War on Two Fronts}}</ref>
