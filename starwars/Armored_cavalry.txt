'''Armored cavalry''' were specialist [[army]] units that moved on the [[Ground warfare|battlefield]] using [[armor]]ed [[Ground assault vehicle|assault vehicles]] such as [[walker]]s and [[tank]]s.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Examples included the [[Imperial combat driver|combat drivers]],<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s, and [[All Terrain Armored Transport]]s of the [[Imperial Army]].<ref name="Scene to Stat">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/scene-to-stat-tempest-force-battle-force|text=Scene to Stat: Tempest Force Battle Force}}</ref> In the [[Imperial Military]] armored cavalry units were used in [[heavy battalion]]s.<ref name="Zero Hour" />
