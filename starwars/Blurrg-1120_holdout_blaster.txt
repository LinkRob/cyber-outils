The '''Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster''' was a [[Hold-out blaster|hold-out]] [[blaster pistol]]<ref name="Battlefront II" /> manufactured by [[Eirriss Ryloth Defense Tech]].<ref name="facebook" /> The weapon was named after the [[blurrg]], a creature native to [[Ryloth]], the home of its manufacturer. The Blurrg-1120 had a primary blaster barrel, a secondary blaster barrel, a small sight, a "lekku" trigger, an ergonomic grip handle, and a gas and power cell cartridge.<ref name="Rebels: The Visual Guide" /> The Blurrg-1120 was customizable, as it could be modified to fire explosive bolts.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> In addition, it sported nine unique firing modes,<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> including a standard single-shot mode and a two-blast burst mode.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Its final firing mode consumed a lot of energy, and didn't register how many shots were fired, forcing its user to check the indicator located under the pistol to know how many shots were left.<ref name="Blasters and Firearms" /> The pistol [[Hera's blaster|was favored]] by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Spectres|rebel]] [[Hera Syndulla]], who kept it in a [[holster]] on her right [[boot]].<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Blurrg-1120 holdout blaster was created for the character Hera Syndulla of the animated series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''<ref name="Spark of Rebellion" /> and first appeared in the [[2014]] novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']].<ref name="NewDawn">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref>

The weapon made it's live-action debut in [[2023]] in the second episode of the [[Ahsoka Season 1|the first season]] of the series ''[[Star Wars: Ahsoka]]''.<ref name="PartTwo">{{Ahsoka|Part Two: Toil and Trouble}}</ref> For the series, it was designed by [[Patrick Beals]] for property master [[Josh Roth]] and was built by the series' in-house props department. Beals said that the design was a "fun challenge" because it had to be adapted from the animated series. Beals sort of imagined it as a simplified version of the "real thing" and then worked backwards from it.<ref name="Beals">{{Instagram|username=patrick_beals_conceptart|name=[[Patrick Beals]]|url=p/CwhHCKwuTye|date=2023-08-29|quote=Seems like everyone is enjoying the first couple eps of Ahsoka so far!  I’m not cleared to release the artwork so here are some images of some prop designs I did for the incredible Property Master Josh Roth @fatdragonfilms All this stuff was built in-house by the amazing Ahsoka Props Department! [...] 4- Hera’s Blurrg sidearm- this was a fun challenge because it featured in the animated show as well.  You sort of have to imagine the animated version is a simplified version of a real thing (even though that design came first) and work backwards from there.|archivefile=File:AhsokaProps-PatrickBeals-Instagram.png}}</ref>
