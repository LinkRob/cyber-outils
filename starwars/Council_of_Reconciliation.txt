The '''Council of Reconciliation''' was one of the four [[Jedi Council|councils]] of the [[Jedi Order]] in operation during the rule of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Its role was to try and settle [[Planet|interplanetary]] disputes. To this end, it worked with the [[Galactic Senate]] and the [[Republic Diplomatic Corps]].<ref name="Graphics">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref>

Its councilmembers met in the [[Tower of Reconciliation]].<ref name="CL 2016" />
