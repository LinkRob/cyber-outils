'''Echo Three''' was a [[callsign]] used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], an [[Echo Squadron]] [[Echo Three (Fostar Haven)|pilot]] using this callsign participated in the [[battle at Fostar Haven]] against [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces.<ref name="Squadrons" />

During the Rebel Alliance's [[time]] in [[Echo Base]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]], [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] used this callsign while scouting for lifeforms in the frozen wastes.<ref name="Episode V" />

==Behind the scenes==
Echo Three was the callsign of a Rebel Alliance non-player character in the [[2020]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" />
