A '''compact charric''' was a compact [[charric]] ranged [[weapon]] which was silent when it fired used by members of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] government.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> [[Chiss]] [[Mitth family]] [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]] "Thalias" obtained [[Mitth family]] [[Senior Aide]] [[Thivik's compact charric]] after the aide to his [[Patriarch]], [[Mitth'urf'ianico]] "Thurfian," gave it to Thalias. Both Chiss entered a [[tube car]] with Thurfian's armed guards, however, Thalias and Thurfian were in a separated compartment, where the Chiss [[Caregiver]] threatened Thurfian with [[death]] if he did not listen to her request about [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]]. After the Chiss woman's request was heard, she gave the charric to Thurfian.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
