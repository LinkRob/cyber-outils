The '''Alderaan sector''' was a [[sector]] located in the [[Core Worlds]] region of [[the galaxy]].

==History==
It notably contained the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]],<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> a peaceful and influential world until its [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="DB Ald" /> It was represented in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] by such [[Alderaanian]] figures as [[Bail Antilles]]<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> and [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]].<ref name="BMF">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> Organa continued to represent the sector in the [[Imperial Senate]] before he was succeeded by his daughter, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]],<ref name="PSFB" /> who later went on to represent the sector in the reconstituted [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Ultimate SW" />
