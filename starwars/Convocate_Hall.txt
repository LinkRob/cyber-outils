The '''Convocate Hall''' was a structure located in [[Csaplar]], the [[capital]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] that was used for meetings of the [[Chiss Syndicure]]. The [[March of Silence]] was located in the Convocate Hall, and was so named because the Hall's design made it difficult for more than five people to hear a conversation at one time.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
