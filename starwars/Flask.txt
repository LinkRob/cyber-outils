A '''flask''' was a portable container for liquids. [[Rio Durant]] owned a metal flask which held some kind of [[alcohol]]ic beverage. On the night prior to [[Beckett's gang|their]] [[Heist on Vandor|attempted theft]] of [[coaxium]] on [[Vandor]], Durant shared the contents of his flask with [[Val]] before pouring some of the beverage into the dish he was cooking over a campfire.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
