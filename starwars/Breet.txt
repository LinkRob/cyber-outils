'''Breet''' was a [[Rybet]] who was a member of [[Pan Eyta]]'s [[Pan Eyta's Tempest|Tempest]] of the [[Nihil]], serving aboard Eyta's flagship ''[[Elegencia]]''.<ref name="TRS" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> during the Nihil's [[Valo Atrocity|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Valo]], Breet reported a call from the [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] to Eyta. After Eyta abruptly ended the call with Ro, Breet questioned Eyta's behavior toward Ro; in response, the [[Dowutin]] [[Tempest Runner]] fired his blaster on Breet, killing the Rybet.<ref name="TRS" />
