'''Eho''' was a [[senator]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Senate]].<ref name="MOF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> when Senator [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] brought Senator [[Mon Mothma]]'s [[Imperial Rebirth Act]], which would reduce [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] authority, to the floor in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|Senate chamber]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], Eho voted against the act. While the [[Crymorah Syndicate]] agent [[Hasalia Prederiko]] was watching the proceedings, she received notifications on her screens reporting how Eho and other senators had voted. After seeing seeing several votes against the bill, Prederiko became upset and poured her [[tea]] on the floor. The Senate ultimately passed the Imperial Rebirth Act, but it was later partially vetoed by Palpatine.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eho was mentioned on a screen in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
