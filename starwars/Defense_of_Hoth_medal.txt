The '''Defense of Hoth medal''' was a campaign [[medal]] that was given to all [[Alliance Military]] personnel who were assigned to the [[Echo Base]] on the [[planet]] [[Hoth]], and participated in the [[Battle of Hoth]] to evacuate the base after it was discovered by the [[Imperial Navy]]. The medal consisted of a simple round medallion that was likened to Hoth and was suspended from a tapering [[Color|white]] [[ribbon]] with a thin blue line and silver stripes along its edges.<ref name="Lead" />

The rack ribbon was a horizontal white ribbon with silver and blue stripes on its edges, and a black [[Starbird (symbol)|starbird]] in the center, which signified the loss of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] base and the personnel that were [[Death|killed]] in its defense.<ref name="Lead" />
