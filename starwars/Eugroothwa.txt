'''Eugroothwa'''<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was a [[Wookiee]] [[Gender|male]] who fought alongside the [[Galactic Republic]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the latter's attempt to [[Battle of Kashyyyk|invade]] the Wookiee [[species]]' [[homeworld]] of [[Kashyyyk]]<ref name="ROTS" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> He had [[Color|brown]] [[hair]] and wore an orange [[bandolier]] of [[blaster gas]] canisters clasped with a bejewelled tribal crest over a brown tunic.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eugroothwa first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="ROTS" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="SWYBY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> The Wookiee did not receive a name in the film,<ref name="ROTS" /> but was identified in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]'', which was written by [[Trisha Biggar]] and released on [[October 1]], [[2005]].<ref name="Dressing a Galaxy">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref> The name was confirmed to be [[canon]] in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', which was written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Tricia Barr]] and [[Cole Horton]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> and released on [[April 4]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-galaxy-guides-get-a-first-look-at-dks-star-wars-visual-encyclopedia-and-the-amazing-book-of-lego-star-wars|text=New Galaxy Guides: Get a First Look at DK's ''Star Wars Visual Encyclopedia'' and ''The Amazing Book of LEGO Star Wars''}}</ref>
