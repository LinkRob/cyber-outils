A '''pack''' of [[charhound]]s was [[Homeworld|from]]<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Planet|world]] [[Elphrona]] and lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'' establishes the [[Bad Hurt Time]] took place before [[Ember]] came to be owned by [[Bell Zettifar]]. As Zettifar owns Ember during the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''&mdash;which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of to [[232 BBY]]&mdash;the Bad Hurt Time must have occurred by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the pack was made up of two big [[Dog|charhounds]] and a number of small charhounds including the [[Gender|female]] puppy [[Ember]]. At one point, a [[spike-sparrow]] [[Bad Hurt Time|killed]] all of the pack except for Ember, who fought the spike-[[sparrow]] afterward. Ember crawled away from the fight and eventually became part of a new pack after coming to be owned by the [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Bell Zettifar]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> Around [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|26}}</ref> Ember mentally recalled her first pack and the event in which they were [[Death|killed]]&mdash;which she called the Bad Hurt Time&mdash;when she reflected on her life while on<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> planet [[Sophros]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
