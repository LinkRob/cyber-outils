The '''Aduba system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Bheriz sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the astronomical objects [[Aduba]]<ref name="Bomber Command" /> and [[Aduba-3]].<ref name="Adventures" /> By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Savareen]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', during which [[Benthic]] is depicted wearing the [[Jacket|duster jacket]], to [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[Tognath]] [[Benthic]] favored a [[Color|black]] [[Jacket|duster jacket]] that he had picked up in the system.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Aduba system was introduced on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map included in the [[2016]] [[roleplaying game]] [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> It was identified in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Aduba system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in which it first appeared in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Star Wars (1977) 7|''Star Wars'' (1977) 7]], which was written by [[Roy Thomas]] and [[Howard Chaykin]], with Chaykin also illustrating,<ref name="SW16">[[Star Wars (1977) 16|''Star Wars'' (1977) 16]]</ref> and published on [[October 11]], [[1977]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53797/star_wars_1977_7|text=Star Wars (1977) #7}} {{C|On-sale date is noted in backup link.}}</ref>
