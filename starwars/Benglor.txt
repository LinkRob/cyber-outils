'''Benglor''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Onatos sector/Legends|Onatos sector]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It had rocky terrain and a [[Temperature|hot]] climate. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] [[Mission to Benglor|investigated]] supposed [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] activity on Benglor with a force of [[clone trooper]]s of the [[501st Legion]]. On Benglor, Skywalker and his [[trooper]]s were attacked by a [[Unidentified quadruped (Benglor)|creature]], which Skywalker managed to defeat with the help of [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]] "Rex."<ref name="Battle Tales 1" />
