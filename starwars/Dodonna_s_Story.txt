"'''Dodonna's Story'''" is a short story written by [[Grant S. Boucher]], originally published in ''[[Galaxy Guide 1: A New Hope]]''. It was also included in ''[[The Movie Trilogy Sourcebook]]''.

{{Shortstory-stub}}

==Plot summary==
In an excerpt from [[General/Legends|General]] [[Jan Dodonna/Legends|Jan Dodonna's]] personal memoirs, the general discusses the night before the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]] and his attempts to formulate a plan to fight the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]]. He analyzes the [[Death Star plans/Legends|station's plans]] all night before discovering its unprotected exhaust port.
