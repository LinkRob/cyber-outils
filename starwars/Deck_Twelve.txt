'''Deck Twelve''' was a chamber inside the [[Megalox space station]] that contained the primary security field generator that projected the [[gravity]] shield above [[Megalox]] [[prison]] on the [[planet]] [[Megalox Beta]]. During the [[Mission to Megalox Beta]], several [[Resistance]] [[astromech droid]]s including [[BB-8]], [[O-R10N]], [[R2-HA]], and [[R4 (Black Squadron)|R4]] breached the chamber in order to deactivate the gravity shield and help [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] rescue [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="PD5" />
