'''Diego Luna''' (born [[December 29]], [[1979]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Mexicans|Mexican]] actor who portrayed [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. Luna reprised the role in ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. His performance in ''Rogue One'' received a nomination for the [[Wikipedia:Saturn Award for Best Supporting Actor|Saturn Award for Best Supporting Actor]] and his performance in ''Andor'' received a nomination for the [[Wikipedia:Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Television Series Drama|Golden Globe Award for Best Television Actor - Drama Series]].

Outside of his ''[[Star Wars]]'' filmography, Luna is known for his roles in the films [[Wikipedia:Y Tu Mamá También|''Y Tu Mamá También'']] and [[Wikipedia:If Beale Street Could Talk (film)|''If Beale Street Could Talk'']], and the television series [[Wikipedia:Narcos: Mexico|''Narcos: Mexico'']].

==Biography==
===''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story''===
{{Quote|Rogue One changed my life. I've lived with Cassian Andor for 10 years now.|Diego Luna|<ref name="IGN D23">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ign.com/articles/andor-season-2-details-revealed-at-d23-2024-with-teaser-krennic-and-k-2so-return-confirmed|text=Andor Season 2 Details Revealed at D23 2024 With Teaser, Krennic and K-2SO Return Confirmed|author=Weber, Rachel|date=2024-09-09|archivedate=20250203165505}}</ref>}}
[[File:Diego_Luna.jpg|250px|thumb|right|Luna's cast photo reveal for ''Rogue One: A Star wars Story''.]]
Diego Luna is from [[Wikipedia:Mexico|Mexico]]. When he started watching films, ''[[Star Wars]]'' was one of the first references in his life.<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> Diego Luna played the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] in the [[2016]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Rogue One" /> a film that he has reflected to be life changing for himself.<ref name="IGN D23" /> Luna's casting in the film was first announced on [[August 15]], [[2015]].<ref name="Rogue One Cast">{{SW|url=news/rogue-one-the-daring-mission-has-begun-cast-and-crew-announced|text=''Rogue One''&mdash;The Daring Mission Has Begun: Cast and Crew Announced}}</ref> Playing the character was an immense thrill for Luna.<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> The actor spoke with his natural [[Wikipedia:Mexicans|Mexican]] accent while playing Andor.<ref name="Diego Luna Accent">{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/movies/2017/01/04/rogue-one-diego-luna-fan-story-diversity|text=Diego Luna 'got emotional' over fan story about diversity in ''Rogue One''|author=Jessica Derschowitz|date=2017-01-04|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|''Entertainment Weekly'']]|archivedate=20191109032330}}</ref> Luna thought about the possibility of coming back and joked about it while on set. He figured that a prequel would be an obvious choice, but he never thought of it as a real chance and found it unproductive to think of the possibilities.<ref name="End cassian">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/andor-star-wars-diego-luna-rogue-one|text=Diego Luna says Andor will be the end of him playing Cassian|author=Dalton Ross|date=2022-09-20|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|''Entertainment Weekly'']]|archivedate=20220922183309}}</ref>

When Luna jumped to the tower<ref name="Tricky lines">{{YouTube|video=0q25e6uuFwA|text="Star Wars means so much!" Diego Luna and the Andor cast on auditions, tricky lines and falling over|channel=@bbcradio1|name=BBC Radio 1}}</ref> in the [[Scarif vault]],<ref name="Rogue One" /> he broke his rib but did not notice it. He did not tell anyone about his injury.<ref name="Tricky lines" /> After the film's release, Luna shared a story from a fan who took her Mexican father to see the film because of his accent, and he said that made him emotional.<ref name="Diego Luna Accent" /> Luna was fascinated by his character and wanted to know how six-year-old Andor got wrapped up in a rebellion. Luna assumed that ''Rogue One'' would be his only appearance in ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="This Story Matters">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/diego-luna-andor-star-wars-rogue-one|text=Diego Luna on returning to Star Wars with Andor: 'This story matters'|author=Devan Coggan|date=2022-08-25|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|''Entertainment Weekly'']]|archivedate=20220825150430}}</ref>

===''Andor'' television series===
{{Quote|There's so much that this series answers, that Rogue One just questions. For me, ''Rogue One'' is very much about an event, and now we're going to get the chance to actually understand and get to know [Cassian Andor] &mdash; who he is, what pain he carries. What are his fears? What is his real motivation? It's all going to be answered here.|Diego Luna|<ref name="This Story Matters" />}}
In mid-[[2019]],<ref name="Rebellion televised">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2022/05/star-wars-cover-the-rebellion-will-be-televised|text=''Star Wars:'' The Rebellion Will Be Televised|author=Anthony Breznican|date=2022-05-17|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']]|archivedate=20220517110251}}</ref> executive producer<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> [[Tony Gilroy]] called Luna and told him the full plan for an Andor-focused story. During the call, Luna spoke in code words so his driver would not understand the conversation. The actor was glad that the proposal included details that resonated personally with him,<ref name="Rebellion televised" /> and he was convinced by Gilroy's ground-level approach.<ref name="Real People">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2022/tv/news/star-wars-andor-tony-gilroy-diego-luna-1235348148|text=How ‘Andor' Became the First ‘Star Wars' TV Series for Grown-Ups: ‘I Wanted to Do It About Real People'|author=Adam B Vary|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|''Variety'']]|archivedate=20220824140042}}</ref> At the end of the call, Luna agreed to reprise his role in the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series.<ref name="Rebellion televised" /> After Gilroy developed the show's basic concept, he and Luna met in [[Wikipedia:New York City|New York City]], and they discussed what the show would be. Luna was very excited almost from the beginning, and Gilroy enjoyed how the actor was rational, imaginative, energized, and passionate.<ref name="Breaks down">{{SW|url=news/tony-gilroy-andor-season-1-interview|text=Tony Gilroy Breaks Down ''Andor'' Season 1}}</ref> Luna then talked about returning for two or three years before shooting started.<ref name="Challenge Rogue">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-diego-luna-talks-star-wars-return-season-2-1235209806|text=Diego Luna Says ‘Andor' Is Going to Challenge What You Think You Know About ‘Rogue One'|author=Brian Davids|date=2022-09-01|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20220922232520}}</ref> He was told that ''Andor'' will be a twelve-episode series that will be similar to a film. Luna was excited to explore the darker side of Andor's psyche, as well as to show the nuance and complexity of a younger incarnation.<ref name="ProductionBrief" />

To prepare for reprising his role, Luna imagined aspects of Andor's history that never made it into ''Rogue One'', which he used as background to bring the character to life. He and Gilroy discussed this as the scripts came together, which helped personalize Andor for the actor. Luna thought about people who are forced to run due to outside pressures, but decide to stop and make a stand.<ref name="All wrong">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2022/05/andor-star-wars-series-diego-luna|text='Andor' Star Wars Series: "What You Know Is Really All Wrong"|author=Anthony Breznican|date=2022-05-23|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']]|archiveurl=https://archive.is/5PVTM}}</ref> Gilroy thought that Luna was a great collaborator and actor.<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> Luna aimed to let audiences understand his character and explain how six-year-old Andor got wrapped up in a rebellion. He said that the trick is in telling a story that people can connect with and explaining how Andor got to where he was in ''Rogue One''.<ref name="This Story Matters" /> Luna found it very important to understand that the show depicts a childish version of Andor who is unaware of the consequences of his actions. He liked that the show showed what oppression looks and feels like. He also liked how the show's characters are in the gray areas and have a chance for redemption. Luna found it very important for young Andor to be played by a Mexican actor to show that the two actors shared something.<ref name="Murderous">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/andor-diego-luna-premiere-interview|text=Diego Luna weighs in on Cassian's murderous moment on ''Andor''|author=Dalton Ross|date=2022-09-22|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|''Entertainment Weekly'']]|archivedate=20220922134718}}</ref>

Production began in late [[November]] of [[2020]].<ref name="New Lucasfilm">{{SW|url=news/future-lucasfilm-projects-revealed|text=Future Lucasfilm Projects Revealed}}</ref> Scenes on Ferrix were filmed in an intricate set in [[Wikipedia:Marlow, Buckinghamshire|Little Marlow]], [[Wikipedia:Buckinghamshire|Buckinghamshire]].<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> When shooting started, Luna thought the first days on set felt very weird, and production was then delayed by the [[Wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref name="This Story Matters" /> During the pandemic, Luna first met [[Fiona Shaw]], the actress who played [[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor]].<ref name="Daughter">{{SW|url=news/fiona-shaw-andor-interview|text=Daughter of Ferrix: <i>Andor</i>'s Fiona Shaw on the Role of Cassian's Mother, Maarva}}</ref> Luna felt that the costume was either too big or too small.<ref name="Challenge Rogue" /> As he spent more time on set, he started to enjoy the process more and fall in love with his character,<ref name="This Story Matters" /> and he enjoyed it by the third or fourth day.<ref name="Challenge Rogue" /> Luna felt like it was the right time to show the origins of a revolution.<ref name="This Story Matters" /> He liked how the show's diverse cast reflects the real world.<ref name="Potent Moment">{{SW|url=news/andor-launch-interview-gilroy-and-cast|text="A Potent Moment in History": Insights from Tony Gilroy and the ''Andor'' Cast}}</ref> He managed to embody a rash and more impulsive Andor with a younger mindset.<ref name="ProductionBrief" />

Per [[Wikipedia:COVID-19|COVID]] production protocols, Luna and Shaw wore a mask during rehearsals. One of the first scenes they filmed together was an argument between their characters in their [[Andor household|house]].<ref name="Daughter" /> When [[Antonio Viña]], the actor who played young Andor,<ref name="Kassa" /> flew to [[Wikipedia:London|London]] for his last session, Luna made sure he was the right actor for the role, and they spent time together. The two actors were shooting at the same time, so Luna was able to welcome Viña to the show and make him feel part of the team. Luna thought that Viña was special because he does a lot with his [[eye]]s and has a "spark" in them.<ref name="Murderous" /> Luna, Viña, and Shaw worked together to shape Maarva's journey as an adoptive mother.<ref name="Daughter" /> Luna found the sets to be beautiful and liked how he could interact with real things.<ref name="Old School">{{WebCite|url=https://www.empireonline.com/tv/news/star-wars-andor-doesnt-use-volume-says-tony-gilroy-exclusive|text=Andor Doesn't Use Star Wars' Volume Video Wall: ‘We're Old-School,' Says Tony Gilroy – Exclusive Image|author=Ben Travis|date=2022-08-01|work=[[Wikipedia:Empire (magazine)|''Empire'']]|archivedate=20220801153835}}</ref> He thought that working with [[B2EMO]] was wonderful because he is practical, allowing him and other actors to film scenes with the droid and also improvise with him.<ref name="Meet B2EMO">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/tv/star-wars-andor-b2emo-droid|text=Meet B2EMO, the adorable new salvage droid in Andor|author=Devan Coggan|date=2022-09-14|work=[[Entertainment Weekly Books|''Entertainment Weekly'']]|archivedate=20220914154638}}</ref>

[[File:Narkina_5_bts_filming.png|thumb|left|280px|Diego Luna and Andy Serkis on the Narkina 5 prison set]]
Scenes on [[Aldhani]] were filmed at the [[Wikipedia:Cruachan Power Station|Cruachan Dam]] and the surrounding mountains in the [[Wikipedia:Scottish Highlands|Scottish Highlands]].<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> According to [[Faye Marsay]], Vel Sartha's actress, the scenes were filmed during the middle of the COVID-19 pandemic.<ref name="Real rebellions">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/1082395/andors-faye-marsay-talks-preparing-to-play-vel-sartha-the-real-rebellions-that-inspired-her-more-exclusive-interview|text=Andor's Faye Marsay Talks Preparing To Play Vel Sartha, The Real Rebellions That Inspired Her & More [Exclusive Interview]|author=Ethan Anderton|date=2022-11-02|work=[[Wikipedia:/Film|''/Film'']]|archivedate=20221102170524}}</ref> For the scenes filmed in [[Wikipedia:Pitlochry|Pitlochry]], [[Wikipedia:Scotland|Scotland]], Luna and the other actors walked for hours up a [[mountain]] to set up one shot.<ref name="Old School" /> According to Sanne Wohlenberg, the Narkina 5 prison scenes were filmed last before the wrap of Season One.<ref name="SWE Roundtable">{{YouTube|video=CZq4wKUM410|text=Were the Prison Episodes TOO Dark for Star Wars?|channel=@StarWarsExplained|name=Star Wars Explained|quote=The prison episodes of Star Wars Andor were very bleak for a Star Wars story. I got the chance to ask the writer of the arc about the balance between darkness and hope. Here is what he had to say.}}</ref> [[Andy Serkis]], the actor that played Kino Loy, said that all of the actors would walk on metal plates for weeks with bare feet, which drained their energy.<ref name="Messed head">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/andy-serkis-andor-prison-set-psychology-comments|text='Andor': Andy Serkis on How the Prison Set Messed With His Head|author=Arezou Amin|date=2022-11-04|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider (website)|''Collider'']]|archivedate=20221104174549}}</ref> When Andor and other prisoners arrived at the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|Narkina 5 prison]], the actors choreographed their convulsions to occur when the lights changed.<ref name="Career Highlight">{{WebCite|url=https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-features/andor-episode-8-toby-haynes-forest-whitaker-stellan-skarsgard-1235250954|text=‘Andor' Director Toby Haynes Talks That Forest Whitaker-Stellan Skarsgard Scene: “It Was a Career Highlight”|author=Brian Davids|date=2022-10-28|work=[[Wikipedia:The Hollywood Reporter|''The Hollywood Reporter'']]|archivedate=20221029050743}}</ref>

Luna was interviewed for ''[[Andor: A Disney+ Day Special Look]]'', a documentary about the making of ''Andor''.<ref name="Special Look">''[[Andor: A Disney+ Day Special Look]]''</ref> In [[September]], [[2022]], Luna attended the ''Andor'' launch event in [[Wikipedia:Hollywood, Los Angeles|Hollywood]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]].<ref name="Launch event">{{SWYouTube|Fv6hHyIToC0|Diego Luna and the Cast and Crew of Andor &#124; This Week! in Star Wars|sw_url=video/diego-luna-and-the-cast-and-crew-of-andor-this-week-in-star-wars}}</ref> On [[December 9]], Luna announced on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that he was currently filming the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of ''Andor''.<ref name="Luna Season 2">{{Twitter|diegoluna_|status/1601217806883659776|Diego Luna|date=2022-12-09|quote=Vamos por la segunda temporada de #Andor !!!! Muchas gracias por todo el cariño y apoyo.|archivedate=20221213002341}}</ref> He will not play Andor after filming the two seasons.<ref name="End cassian" /> For his performance in ''Andor'', Luna was nominated for the [[Wikipedia:Golden Globe Award for Best Actor – Television Series Drama|Golden Globe Award for Best Television Actor - Drama Series]].<ref name="Golden Globe Award nomination">{{WebCite|url=https://www.goldenglobes.com/person/diego-luna|text=Diego Luna Golden Globes|author=Golden Globes|date=2022-12-12|work=[[Wikipedia:Golden Globe Awards|Golden Globe Awards]]|archivedate=20221212141652}}</ref>
