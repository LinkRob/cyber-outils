'''Ferrol''' was a [[human]]<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Gender|male]] who was the [[Viceroy]] of the planet [[E'ronoh]] and one of [[Monarchy|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]]'s closest advisors.<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> after E'roni [[Princess]] [[Xiri A'lbaran]] brought an end to the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]] by deciding to [[Marriage|marry]] Prince [[Phan-tu Zenn]] of [[Eiram]], Ferrol and his son [[Rev Ferrol]] helped form the [[terrorist]] organization called the [[Children of E'ronoh]] that wished to take down the A'lbarans so that the [[Ferrols]] could take over the government and continue the war to destroy the [[Eirami]].<ref name="Convergence" />

Ferrol used his connections to get access to the path of Xiri A'lbaran's relief campaign across E'ronoh, and gave that information to his son so that he and the Children could intercept her. After his son's attack failed, he was questioned by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Enya Keen]] at the behest of both Monarch A'lbaran and [[Queen]] [[Adrialla]] of Eiram. After Ferrol was suspected for the murder of a [[Kage]] [[assassin]] named [[Abda]] who had attempted to kill Xiri A'lbaran, he confronted [[Axel Greylark]], the son of [[Chancellor]] [[Kyong Greylark]], as he had seen him enter the tower before the assassin was killed. Ferrol destroyed Axel Greylark's droid [[QN-1]] and told him what he knew, but Greylark saw that Ferrol hadn't ever killed anyone and was able to get the upper hand, stabbing Ferrol with his own bane blade and shooting and killing him with his own blaster. Ferrol's suspicion had been correct, as Greylark had actually killed Abda.<ref name="Convergence" />
