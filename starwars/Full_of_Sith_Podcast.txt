'''Full of Sith''' is a ''[[Star Wars]]'' podcast hosted by [[Bryan Young]] and [[Amy Ratcliffe]], both contributors to [[StarWars.com]] and ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'', as well as [[Wikipedia:Mike Pilot|Mike Pilot]], with semi-regular appearances from Bobby Roberts, who hosts the spoiler-focused spinoff "Rumor Control". The show features stories, interviews, and segments that revolve around all aspects of ''Star Wars'' and its fandom.
