'''''Darth Vader: Together We Can Rule the Galaxy''''', also marketed as '''Darth Vader in a Box''', is a collectible box set that includes a miniature [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] painted figurine with display stand and a forty-eight-page illustrated paperback book featuring Vader's greatest quotes, with an introduction by [[Pete Vilmur]]. It was published by [[Chronicle Books]] in [[July 25]] [[2012]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''This one-of-a-kind, hand-painted Darth Vader statue in a classic force-choke pose, complete with a customizable display stand and a booklet of the Sith Lord's most chilling and inspirational quotes, is an essential totem for Star Wars fans or anyone wishing to master the dark side of the Force.''
