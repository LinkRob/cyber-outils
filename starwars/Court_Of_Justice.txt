The '''Court Of Justice''' was the court of the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Allegiance 3" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Resistance]] [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo]] led a [[Mission to Mon Cala|mission]] to Mon Cala to get [[starship]]s needed for their [[Resistance navy|navy]]. But [[Chewbacca]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] were attacked by a [[Quarren]] mob but were accused of the crime. The group was brought before [[King]] [[Ech-Char]] in the [[Royal Throne Chamber]], who was the judge in the trial. The king ordered that Rey must fight [[RK-9|Arkay-Nine]] in the [[Ancient Rite of Challenge]].<ref name="Allegiance 3" />
