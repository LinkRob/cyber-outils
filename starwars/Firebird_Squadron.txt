'''Firebird Squadron'''<ref name="Past Mistakes">{{InsiderCite|issue1=205|issue2=206|Starlight: Past Mistakes}}</ref> was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Operating out of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]], the squadron flew [[Z-29 Skyhawk]]s. Firebird Squadron was deployed from the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Aurora IX]]'' in the [[Battle of Galov]] against the [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|forces]] of [[Nihil]] [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]], led by [[Firebird Leader]] [[Errington]]. Several of Firebird Squadron's [[pilot]]s, including Firebird Leader as well as [[Firebird Four]] [[Jano Brayson]] and [[Firebird Two]] [[Teff]] along with "[[Unidentified pilot (Firebird Three)|Firebird Three]]" boarded Dee's [[starship]], the [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|''Lourna Dee'']], but only Jano survived.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
