'''Amani''' was one of the four primary [[language]]s spoken on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Utapau]], along with [[Pau'an]], [[Utai]], and [[Galactic Basic Standard|Basic]]. The language shared its name with the [[Amani]] [[species]], some of whom settled on Utapau.<ref name="CotR" />
