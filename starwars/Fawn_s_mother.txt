The '''mother''' of the [[human]] [[musician]] [[Fawn]] was a [[Gender|female]] individual that settled on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="ACOF" /> at some point after [[21 ABY]]. In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. According to ''A Crash of Fate'', [[Izal Garsea]] left [[Batuu]] when she was five [[Standard year|years]] old, and as the novel takes place on Garsea's eighteenth [[birthday]], it can be deduced that she left the [[planet]] in [[21 ABY]]. The novel also establishes that [[Fawn]]'s family came to Batuu after Garsea left; therefore, they must have settled sometime after 21 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> they grew to dislike their son's [[band]], the [[Frozen Wampas]], practicing [[music]] in their family's house. Fawn related his [[Parent|mother's]] feelings to the [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]], and explained that they had rehearsed elsewhere since.<ref name="ACOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fawn's mother was mentioned in ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', a [[2019]] young-adult novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="ACOF" />
