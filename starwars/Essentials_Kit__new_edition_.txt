The '''Essentials Kit''' ('''SWQ03''') is an expansion to ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', an asymmetrical infantry-based wargame published by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. It was released on [[August 15]] [[2025]] as part of an overhaul of the game.<ref name="Release" />

==Publisher's summary==
The Star WarsTM: Legion Essentials Kit contains the Range Tools, Movement Tools, and generic Order tokens that can be used by any faction. This is the perfect accessory for players building their first army from scratch or for players who want an extra set of tokens and tools.

*ESSENTIAL TOOLS FOR LEGION PLAYERS: The Essentials Pack includes Range Tools, Movement Tools, and generic Order tokens, compatible with any faction in Star Wars: Legion.
*PERFECT FOR STARTING YOUR ARMY: Ideal for players building their first army from scratch or those looking to expand their collection of tools and tokens.
*COMPLETE SET FOR STRATEGIC GAMEPLAY: Provides critical components for movement and range measurement, ensuring smooth, strategic gameplay on the battlefield.
*OFFICIAL STAR WARS ACCESSORY: Officially licensed for Star Wars: Legion, this pack is designed to enhance your experience and keep you fully equipped for every battle.
*REUSABLE AND DURABLE: Built to last through countless battles, these high-quality tools and tokens are designed for regular use in tactical tabletop gaming.
