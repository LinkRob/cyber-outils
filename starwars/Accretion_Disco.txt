The '''Accretion Disco''' was a [[space station]] that [[orbit]]ed the [[Crseih Black Hole]]. Its [[Engine|thrusters]] kept the station outside of the event horizon of the [[black hole]], though it was still close enough to feel an exhilarating tingle when dancing.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date note" /> the Accretion Disco was hired out by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[gangster]] [[Omel Sampak]] so he could celebrate a [[Bombing of a Nar Kanji fight school|victory]] over a rival. To keep the [[gangster]] and his entourage alive during the celebrations, the [[T'onga's crew|crew]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[T'onga]] were hired. When a group of [[assassin]]s attempted to kill Sampak, [[Havoc at the Accretion Disco|chaos]] ensued,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 27" /> which resulted in Sampak's [[death]] and also the destruction of the Disco, which fell into the black hole.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 28" />
