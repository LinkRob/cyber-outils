During the [[New Republic Era]], a group of TIE [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|bombers]] '''bombed''' [[Kryze Fortress|Kryze Castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Kalevala]]. The fleet was encountered by [[Din Djarin]] and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] who were returning from a [[Mission to the Living Waters|mission]] to [[Mandalore]] when a group of [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptors]] attacked them. Though the pair were able to defend themselves from their initial attackers, destroying several interceptors, they were not able to prevent<ref name="Chapter 19" /> a [[Gideon's TIE/sa bomber squadron|squadron]]<ref name="Season Three Companion" /> three [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]] from destroying [[Kryze Fortress|Kryze Castle]]. Kryze was able to take out one of the bombers before she and Djarin were forced to retreat when the bombers were reinforced by additional interceptors.<ref name="Chapter 19" />
