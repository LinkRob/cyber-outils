'''Crescent mushrooms''' were a type of [[mushroom]].<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the anti-[[Nihil]] operative [[Cair San Tekka]] returned to his [[San Tekka clan|family's]] [[San Tekka compound|compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], opting to land on its ground-level landing pads. However, as he maneuvered his [[San Tekka family yacht|yacht]] into position, he reflected that he usually preferred the compound's shelf landing pads, which clung to the manor's structure like crescent [[Fungus|mushrooms]].<ref name="TOTF" />
