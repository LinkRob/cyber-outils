'''''Forces of Destiny'': Season Two''' is the second season of the animated micro-series ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]''. It was announced on [[March 16]], [[2018]], and debuted on [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] on [[March 19]].
