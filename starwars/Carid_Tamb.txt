'''Carid Tamb''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[junior officer]] in the [[First Order]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[Resistance]]. Recruited from a conquered [[planet]], Tamb gained experience through combat simulations and became a [[technician]]. Tamb served on the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] in the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]].

==Biography==
Carid Tamb was a [[human]] brought to the [[First Order]] from a conquered [[Planet|world]] and, like many such recruits, became a [[junior officer]] and was assigned to [[First Order Navy|fleet]] operations. [[Star Destroyer]] control stations were crewed by Tamb and other young and impressionable [[technician]]s, often under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] veterans. During the First Order's [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[Resistance]], junior [[officer]]s such as Tamb gained experience through vivid combat simulations.<ref name="TROS VD" />

[[File:CaridTamb-TROS.png|thumb|right|300px|Carid Tamb served on the ''Steadfast''.]]
In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> Tamb served on<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]<ref name="TROS VD" /> and was present on the [[Starship|ship]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]] against the Resistance, during which the officer interacted with the buttons of a [[Computer monitor|monitor]] as the [[Citizens' Fleet]] arrived. The battle ended with the ''Steadfast''{{'s}} destruction and the defeat of the First Order.<ref name="TROS" />

==Personality and traits==
Carid Tamb had [[Color|light]] skin and brown [[hair]] and [[eye]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Skills and abilities==
Tamb gained experience through combat simulations.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
The human donned a black [[First Order military uniforms|First Order military uniform]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> along with black [[boot]]s<ref name="GG">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Galactic Guide]]''</ref> and wore an [[ear]] device that routed internal communications through a shielded physical network.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carid Tamb first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS" /> and was identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
