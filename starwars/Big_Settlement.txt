'''Big Settlement''' was a major town located in [[Atterra Bravo]]'s [[Firestone Islands]]. The settlement was composed of a number of [[saltstone]] homes and a large [[Big Settlement hydrofarm|hydrofarm]], which helped to provide its [[water]]. When the [[First Order]] occupied Atterra Bravo, the settlement became a ghost town.<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
