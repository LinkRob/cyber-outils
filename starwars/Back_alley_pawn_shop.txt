The '''back alley pawn shop''' was a dodgy shop found within the [[Coruscant Underworld (battlefield)|Coruscant Underworld]] battlefield in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. It was covered in [[graffiti]] created by the [[Kubaz]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Glikkin]], including the words "Lawless Glikk" in [[Aurebesh]] tagged on the building alongside a depiction of what appeared to be a [[dianoga]]. Above the entrance was a sign that read "[[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] 4 [[beskar]]" in bold lettering.<ref name="Kay" />

==Behind the scenes==
The back alley pawn shop was first designed for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' by concept artist [[Anna Kay]]. It was proposed as one of four main points of interest on the Coruscant Underworld map, then still referred to as the "Locklands,"<ref name="Kay" /> though it did not make it into the final version of the game.<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>
