'''Cogon''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] until he deserted a few [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Before he could defect to the rebels, he was captured by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]].

== Biography ==
At some point Cogon joined the [[Galactic Empire]] as an [[Imperial officer|officer]] and was assigned to the planet [[Lothal]]. In [[4 BBY]], Agent [[Alexsandr Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] investigated Imperial forces on Lothal, searching for those who sympathized with the growing [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]] at the Empire. He discovered two candidates, one being Cogon. However, Cogon was alerted to the threat of capture by the Imperials.<ref name="NoSymp" />

Along with another defector, [[Swain]], he stole a [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]] from Lothal before going into hiding on the planet [[Thrad]]. There they met [[Beneda]], an agent of [[Senator]] [[Nadea Tural]], who organized a meeting between the Senator and the two defectors, who offered them passage offworld to join a [[Rebel cell (Klonoid)|rebel cell]] on the planet [[Klonoid]] in exchange for data that Swain had brought with her. Cogon and Swain accepted the offer, however, shortly after the meeting's end, they were [[Skirmish on Thrad|discovered]] by the Imperials when a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]] blew a hole through the wall of the room they were in and demanded their surrender. Cogon refused and using his [[blaster rifle]], shot and killed the [[TIE pilot]], causing the starfighter to crash into a nearby building.<ref name="NoSymp" /> 

With Kallus and his [[stormtrooper]] escort closing in on the room, Cogon and Swain escaped via the new hole in the wall. However, Kallus followed them, which Swain anticipated. Using herself as a diversion, Cogon tackled Kallus, disarming the ISB officer. Standing over Kallus, he offered to end Kallus but was stunned in the back by the arriving stormtroopers. He was arrested and taken back to the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Relentless]]''.<ref name="NoSymp" />

== Personality and traits ==
Cogon was a tall human male, with short blond hair, brown eyes, and light skin color. He wore a standard [[Imperial officer's uniform]] and carried a blaster rifle.<ref name="NoSymp" />
