{{Quote|Coruscant has not been attacked in over one thousand years!''"<br />"''Then it has a false sense of security.|Lott Dod and Nix Card|<ref name="HOBS">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>}}
A '''battle''' was fought on [[Coruscant]] during the [[Jedi-Sith War]] that ultimately ended in a [[Sith]] victory.<ref name="SWP" />

Over a [[millennium]] after the battle, the attack was significant enough to prompt [[Trade Federation]] [[Senator]] [[Lott Dod]] to recall it during a debate with fellow conspirators on how to get the [[Galactic Senate]] to vote for the deregulation of banks. Dod found the idea of staging an attack so significant, he brought up that the last time Coruscant was attacked was over 1,000 [[Standard year|years]] in the past. [[Nix Card]] replied that this long era of peace made the Coruscanti complacent and would make the attack more effective.<ref name="HOBS" />
