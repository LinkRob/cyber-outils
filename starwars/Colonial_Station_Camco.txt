'''Colonial Station Camco''',<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> also known as '''Colonial Station Cam'co''',<ref name="Lesser Evil map">{{LesserEvilMap}}</ref> was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> located within the [[Colonial Station Camco system]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Chiss Space]] in the [[Unknown Regions]]. It was situated in grid square [[Standard Galactic Grid|F-8]] on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Chiss]] [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]], known by the [[core name]] "Thalias," was [[Reproduction|born]] there before she became a [[sky-walker]] [[navigator]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Trials date">[[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]]'s [[Trials]] on [[Csilla]] were concurrent to [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]]'s [[Mission to Batuu|mission]] to [[Batuu]], which took place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Thalias undertook her [[Trials]] to become a [[Trial-born]] of the [[Mitth family]] at the [[Mitth family homestead|family's homestead]] on the Chiss [[capital]] and [[homeworld]], [[Csilla]]. At the [[homestead]], Thalias' first Trial was to trace the path of her life on a mosaic that was a stylized map of the Ascendancy. As such, the [[Gender|Caregiver]] first set foot on the icon depicting Colonial Station Camco.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Colonial Station Camco first appeared as a mosaic depiction in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the first novel of ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> It was first pictured&mdash;labeled as "Colonial Station Cam'co"&mdash;on a [[:File:Chiss_Ascendancy_map.jpg|map]] of the Chiss Ascendancy<ref name="Lesser Evil map" /> provided by publisher [[Del Rey]] as a bonus for purchasing<ref name="Preorder tweet">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/1451184292176924678|[[Del Rey]]|quote=Calling all #Thrawn fans - submit your preorder receipt for Thrawn Ascendancy III: Lesser Evil and receive a printable 2022 calendar and digital wallpaper featuring exclusive Thrawn art!<br />Offer open until November 16, 2021.|archivedate=20211021135313}}</ref> the physical, e-book, and [[Wikipedia:Audible (service)|Audible]] [[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil (audiobook)|audiobook]] versions of<ref name="Indeed tweet">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/1451189176385241097|[[Del Rey]]|quote=Indeed it does {{C|in response to "I'm planning on listening on audible [sic], does that preorder work too?"}}|archivedate=20211021141418}}</ref> the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', written by Zahn as the final novel of the ''Ascendancy Trilogy''.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>

The [[Colonial Station Cam'co system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
