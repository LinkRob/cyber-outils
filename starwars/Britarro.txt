The '''Britarro''' were a mysterious [[Sentience|sentient]] [[humanoid]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Britaxis Minor]]. [[Nik Hepho]] was a Britarro who worked as a [[bounty hunter]].

==Biology and appearance==
The Britarro were distinguished by their [[Color|blue]]<ref name="Aphra 20" /> or green skin, flat noses and hairless heads.<ref name="UVG" />

==History==
The Britarros [[Homeworld|hailed]] from [[Britaxis Minor]]. Besides this, they were very mysterious.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Britarros in the galaxy==
[[File:AccreskerPenalLegion-2016DoctorAphra20.jpg|thumb|left|180px|One Britarro was seen fighting with the Accresker Penal Legion]]
A Britarro named [[Nik Hepho]] worked as a [[bounty hunter]], specializing in tracking down adherents of the [[Church of the Force]], an illegal religion within the [[Galactic Empire]]. He was seen on the [[moon]] of [[Jedha]] in [[Jedha City]], shortly before the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed the city]].<ref name="UVG" />

One Britarro was also seen in [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra's]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra's squad|squad]] in the [[Accresker Penal Legion]]. The Britarro was killed in an [[Attack on an EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|attack]] on an [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] where it's [[Unidentified hubdroid|hubdroid]] malfunctioned and exploded, killing all of the squad except Aphra and [[Cornelius Evazan|Lopset Yas]].<ref name="Aphra 20" /> Another was present on the planet of [[Tatooine]] as a patron of [[Garsa Fwip]]'s [[Garsa Fwip's Sanctuary|Sanctuary]] when [[Daimyo]] [[Boba Fett]] confronted her about [[Mayor of Mos Espa|Mayor]] [[Mok Shaiz]]'s claims.<ref name="TribesofTatooine">{{BOBF|Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine}}</ref> Later on, they were present when the [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan]] attacked a group of [[gambling]] [[Trandoshan]]s.<ref name="Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Britarro species first appeared in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', directed by [[Gareth Edwards]] and released on [[December 15]] [[2016]].<ref name="RO" /> They were identified in ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released on [[December 16]], 2016.<ref name="UVG" />
