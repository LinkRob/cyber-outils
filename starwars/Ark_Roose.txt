'''Ark Roose''', known by the nickname "'''Bumpy'''" due to his aggressive nature during races, was a dim-witted [[Gender|male]] [[Nuknog]] [[podracer]] from the [[planet]] [[Sump]]. Using a [[Plug-8G 927 Cluster Array Podracer]], he participated in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] in [[32 BBY]]. Prior to the start of the race a band played in his honor, and during his first lap he was well placed in the lead group. After stopping for maintenance at the start of his second lap, Roose was overtaken by the [[human]] [[pilot]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], and during his third lap, the Nuknog was knocked out of the race when the [[Vulptereen]] racer [[Dud Bolt]] crashed into him.

==Biography==
Ark Roose was a [[Nuknog]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="SWFC" /> [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] from the [[planet]] [[Sump]]<ref name="CVD" /> who was also known as Bumpy due to his reputation for becoming aggressive during races.<ref name="Geektionary" /> He [[pilot]]ed a massive [[Vokoff-Strood]] [[Plug-8G 927 Cluster Array Podracer]]<ref name="SWFC" /> when competing in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]] on the planet [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> He was undeterred by his position in the outside of the fourth row<ref name="Geektionary" /> on the race's starting grid at the [[Mos Espa Grand Arena]]<ref name="CL 2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> and when the [[Troig]] announcer [[Fodesinbeed Annodue]] introduced Roose to the crowd, the Nuknog saluted a band in the audience as they played a piece in the racer's honor.<ref name="Geektionary" /> Prior to the race, Roose attempted to sabotage the [[Anakin Skywalker's Podracer|podracer]] belonging to the [[human]] competitor [[Anakin Skywalker]], but instead damaged the [[podracer]] of<ref name="Inside Intel">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-inside-intel-podracing|text=''Star Wars'' Inside Intel: Podracing|int=Star Wars Inside Intel}}</ref> the [[Toong]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Ben Quadinaros]].<ref name="Inside Intel" />

[[File:Roose-Pitstop-CVNE.png|thumb|left|250px|Ark Roose made a pit stop during his second lap of the Boonta Eve Classic.]]
Roose then strapped into his vehicle,<ref name="Geektionary" /> and when the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] signalled for the race to begin,<ref name="TPM" /> he took off and became well placed in the lead group during his first lap.<ref name="Geektionary" /> Roose's sabotage of Quadinaros, however, meant the Toong was the first competitor to be knocked out of the race, as his podracer refused to start.<ref name="TPM" /> He skilfully dodged fire from [[Tusken Raider]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> who were camped out on the [[Canyon Dune Turn]],<ref name="TPM" /> but then had to stop at the beginning of his second lap so that [[maintenance droid]]s could conduct repairs on his podracer. He returned to the race at full speed, but at the end of his second lap he was overtaken by Skywalker.<ref name="Geektionary" />

During the third and final lap of the race, Roose dangerously gained on Skywalker,<ref name="Geektionary" /> but while in [[the Coil]] portion of the [[Mos Espa podrace circuit|track]],<ref name="CL 2016" /> the [[Vulptereen]] podracer [[Dud Bolt]] crashed into Roose and caused the Nuknog to enter a tailspin and crash, knocking him out of the race. Skywalker ultimately won the race and Bolt ended up in the [[Mos Espa medcenter]] after his collision with Roose.<ref name="Geektionary" /> Roose was later mentioned in an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]], which was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AA" /> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Ark Roose was particularly dim-witted for a Nuknog, a [[species]] known for their diminished [[brain]] capacity. When racing, his strategy consisted of crashing his vehicle into other racers, which often led to his own hospitalisation.<ref name="AA" /> Roose was also willing to sabotage other competitors' racers, but was not always able to discern whether he had sabotaged the correct vehicle.<ref name="Inside Intel" /> He had [[Color|yellow]] [[eye]]s and purple skin.<ref name="CVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|He's not good-looking, but he's not shy. Ark "Bumpy" Roose!|Ark Roose's introduction in the extended version of the podrace|<ref name="Saga">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Ark_Roose_Concept_Crop.png|thumb|right|250px|Costume concept of Ark Roose by Iain McCaig]]
Ark Roose was created for the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM" /> The character was not included in the list of podracers given in the revised rough draft,<ref name="EPIG">[[Star Wars: Episode I Insider's Guide|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Insider's Guide'']]</ref> but his design was finalized in a series of sketches by concept artist [[Terryl Whitlatch]]<ref name="DB">{{DB|character|arkbumpyroose|Roose, Ark "Bumpy"}}</ref> including an anatomy sketch on [[August 26]], [[1996]] and a profile of the Nuknog's head on [[September 16]], 1996. Costume designer [[Iain McCaig]] also created a costume sketch for the character on September 16.<ref name="EPIG" />

A detailed maquette of the racer was then made to establish Roose's color scheme and three-dimensional details. A computer-generated version of the character was then made for the film,<ref name="DB" /> with animators patterning Roose's big, bulky, friendly persona after actor [[Wikipedia:John Goodman|John Goodman]].<ref name="Chronicle">''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]''</ref> A modified version of the model was then reused for the character [[Slyther Bushforb/Legends|Slyther Bushforb]] in the<ref name="DB" /> [[2002]] sequel to the ''The Phantom Menace'', [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Roose appears in two deleted scenes created for ''The Phantom Menace''. In "Complete Podrace Grid Sequence" his introduction by Fodesinbeed Annodue is shown along with the band that play in the Nuknog's honor, and in "Extended Podrace Lap Two" he is shown making a pit stop during the second lap of the race.<ref name="Saga" /> His actions in both these scenes were later made canon by the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''.<ref name="Geektionary" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], Roose appeared prior to the release of the film in its [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]] written by [[Terry Brooks]].<ref name="Novel">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'' novelization]]</ref>
