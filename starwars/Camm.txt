'''Camm''' was a<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> [[human]]<ref name="USW" /> [[Gender|male]] [[Imperial cadet|cadet]] who attended a flight [[Imperial Academy|academy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] during it's [[Imperial Era|reign]]. He wished to run away after repeatedly failing his simulator test, and asked fellow [[cadet]] [[Yrica Quell]] to run with him, who he had feelings for. Quell refused, fearing arrest and [[Death|execution]] and also not liking Camm as much as he liked her.<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-author-interview|text=In ''Shadow Fall'', the Dark Secrets of ''Alphabet Squadron'' Revealed}} establishes that ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' takes place approximately half a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], while ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' states [[Kes Dameron]] and [[Shara Bey]] moved to [[Yavin 4]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of [[Endor]]. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates their move to [[5 ABY]], the events of ''Shadow Fall'' must also take place around that [[time]].</ref> Quell saw a vision of Camm while being subjected to [[The Force|force energies]] from an [[Unidentified observatory|observatory]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
