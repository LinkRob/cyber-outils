A pair of '''red-bladed Sith lightsabers''' were used as personal weapons by [[Darth Shaa]] during her career as a Sith. At least a [[millennium]] before the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Lady Shaa used them to free the [[Sculptor]] [[Darth Momin|Momin]] from his [[prison]], [[Death|killing]] the guards and melting the lock on the cell door. She later used them against Momin during his lightsaber combat training, until her death at the hands of her apprentice during one such training.<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Darth Shaa's lightsabers debuted in the [[2018]] [[canon]] comic [[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]] in a flashback. The comic was written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Dark Lord 22" />
