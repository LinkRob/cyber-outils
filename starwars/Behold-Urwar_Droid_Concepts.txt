'''Behold-Urwar Droid Concepts''' was a manufacturer of [[BD explorer droid|BD unit]] [[explorer droid]]s, small [[Limb|bipedal]] [[droid]]s designed for navigation and repair. One BD unit manufactured by the company, [[BD-1]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was active by [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFO-Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> while another, [[TA-418]],<ref name="EncyclopediaNote">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' &mdash; Based on corresponding data for [[BD explorer droid|BD units]]</ref> operated by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="DA-Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 1]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> Another BD unit created by Behold-Urwar Droid Concepts, [[BD-72]], worked on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> by [[9 ABY]].<ref name="BOBF-Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Behold-Urwar Droid Concepts was mentioned in the description plate for the non-[[canon]] [[2022]] buildable [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set depicting [[BD-1]], a droid from<ref name="Lego">{{LEGOCite|num=75335|name=BD-1}}</ref> the canon [[2019]] [[Respawn Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''.<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> The manufacturer was first canonically mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
