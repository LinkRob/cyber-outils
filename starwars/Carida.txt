'''Carida''' was a [[mountain]]ous [[planet]] located in the [[Carida system]] within the [[Colonies]] region of [[the galaxy]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Valor (space station)|''Valor'']] [[space station]] [[orbit]]ed the planet, while the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] saw Carida as home to an [[Carida Academy|Imperial academy]] and a [[Imperial Navy Deepdock Facility Two|naval docking facility]].

==Description==
[[File:CaridaNavalAcademy-HanImperialCadet1.png|thumb|left|216px|Carida's barren landscape filled with mountains.]]
Carida was a [[planet]]<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> located in the [[Carida system]] within [[the galaxy]]'s [[Colonies]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Carida's two suns<ref name="Kendal" /> shone down on mostly barren landscapes filled with [[mountain]]s. The planet had an [[atmosphere]] breathable to [[oxygen]]-breathers, such as [[human]]s. The planet was also heavily populated with humans.<ref name="Imperial Cadet 1" /> Small villages populated the rocky, mountainous areas.<ref name="Kendal" />

==History==
===Age of the Republic===
[[File:Venator-I-FFG.png|thumb|right|300px|A ''Venator I''-class Star Destroyer patrols near Carida]]
Carida supported the [[Military Creation Act]] before the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> Sometime later, a [[Anti-military protest at the Carida Academy|protest]] against the increasing [[Republic Military|militarization]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] was held at the [[Carida Academy]]. [[Cassian Jeron Andor's father]] was [[Death|killed]] during the protest<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> according to the possibly false information in [[Cassian Jeron Andor]]'s [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] file.<ref name="Cassian Andor 101">{{SW|url=news/cassian-andor-101-the-story-of-a-rebel|text=Cassian Andor 101: The Story of a Rebel|nolive=1}}</ref>

During the Clone Wars, Galactic Republic strategists held a [[Republic strategy conference|conference]] aboard the [[Valor (space station)|''Valor'']] [[space station]] [[orbit]]ing Carida. Through the efforts of [[D-Squad]], the [[droid]] task force led by [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]], ''Valor'' narrowly avoided [[Carida incident|destruction]] by an unmanned, [[rhydonium]]-filled [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|cruiser]] which the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] intended to use as an unassuming bomb.<ref name="Point of No Return" />

===Imperial Era===
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Carida Academy]] was maintained on the planet. Former Republic naval officer [[Tabor Seitaron]] was among the staff there.<ref name="Twilight Company">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> The academy was notorious for producing many superb officers, including [[Kendal Ozzel]], [[Tian Karmiya]], and [[Canonhaus]].<ref name="Kendal" /> [[Ryleel Imperial Base]] was also located on Carida.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 25" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the academy became a target for an attack by rebels. After stealing [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s personal [[corvette]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', [[Berch Teller's rebel cell]], secretly aided by Imperial [[Vice Admiral]] [[Dodd Rancit]], planned to attack the Imperial facility. Meaning to betray his rebel allies, so as to claim glory among his Imperial peers, Rancit gathered a [[fleet]] above the planet and laid in wait for the ''Spike''{{'s}} arrival. Suspecting the vice admiral's treachery, the cell sent the corvette to Carida on auto-pilot and escaped. Upon his arrival in Carida space, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] boarded Rancit's [[Secutor-class Star Destroyer|''Secutor''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Conquest (Secutor-class)|''Conquest'']] and, revealing that Vice Admiral's plot had come to light, ordered that Rancit be ejected in an [[escape pod]] and shot down.<ref name="Tarkin" />

An [[Carida incident (Stinger Mantis)|incident]] involving the [[Stinger Mantis crew]], led by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]], occurred on Carida. The incident occurred when [[Klatooinian]] [[twin]]s [[Koob]] and [[Lizz]] became hungry. During the incident, Kestis' life was saved by [[Gabs]], a crew member. Gabs believed that Kestis owed her a [[starship]] for this.<ref name="Star Wars Jedi: Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

Shortly before the [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]] of the Carida Academy was pulled back into active duty by [[Prelate]] [[Verge]] and his [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Herald]]'' in an effort to hunt down Imperial defector [[Everi Chalis]].<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Behind the scenes==
Carida first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Point of No Return (The Clone Wars)|Point of No Return]]." The subtitles for that episode on the show's [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] Blu-ray incorrectly spell the planet's name as "Corrida."<ref name="Point of No Return" />
