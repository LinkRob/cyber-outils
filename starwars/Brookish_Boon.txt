'''Brookish Boon''' was a [[Sy Myrthian]] [[Gender|female]] from [[Sy Myrth]] who spent [[time]] outside the [[Galaxies Opera House]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in [[19 BBY]]. Many [[Standard year|years]] later, their [[Thief|stolen]] identity profile was used by the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] as part of a test for potential new employees of [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]].

==Biography==
Brookish Boon was a [[Sy Myrthian]] [[Gender|female]] who was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Sy Myrth]]<ref name="Datapad" /> around [[57 BBY]].<ref name="GEDates" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of {{Film|III}} to [[19 BBY]].</ref> Boon was on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and stood with a hooded individual at the foot of the steps leading into the<ref name="ROTS" /> [[Galaxies Opera House]] while it was showing a performance of the [[Mon Calamari Aquatic Ballet|ballet]] ''[[Squid Lake]]''.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> As the pair stood together, the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] rushed past while en route to meet with [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] inside the building.<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref>

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDates" /> when Boon was 91 years old, their [[Thief|stolen]] identity profile came into the possession of the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], owner of [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]]. Ohnaka used the profile to test several individuals who were looking to join a flight crew that the pirate was putting together for an upcoming [[Heist on a First Order train|heist]]. He sent Boon's profile, along with several others, to the prospective crew members' [[datapad]]s, then quizzed them on details from the files.<ref name="Datapad" />

==Personality and traits==
Brookish Boon had [[Color|brown]] [[hair]], red [[eye]]s, and pink skin.<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> They stood 6 [[Foot|feet]], 9 inches tall and weighed 316 [[pound]]s.<ref name="Datapad" />

==Equipment==
Boon wore a soft, purple Sy Myrthian [[Clothing|fur coat]] over a brown dress and white-and-brown sleeves<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> while at the [[opera]] house.<ref name="ROTS" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BrookishBoon-ROTS.png|thumb|right|250px|Brookish Boon is briefly visible in ''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith''.]]
Brookish Boon first appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="ROTS" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[2005]].<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> The opera house scenes were filmed on R Stage at [[Shepperton Studios]] as part of additional photography on Monday, [[August 23]], [[2004]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> Boon was also filmed as part of the opera house corridor shot, where the character was stood talking to a [[Swokes Swokes]]; however, the pair were removed during the editing of the entirely blue-screen shot, as they were replaced with one of the corridor's walls.<ref name="SkywalkerSage">{{HomeVideoCite|set=Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga|featurette=''Revenge of the Sith'': ILM Episode III Siggraph Reel|nolink=1}}</ref>

The character of Brookish Boon originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity from the [[in-universe]] news article "Ando, Sy Myrth Secede" in [[HoloNet News Vol. 531 45|''HoloNet News Vol. 531 #45'']],<ref name="HoloNet News">{{HoloNetNewsWeb|45|news/13228_1.html}}</ref>  which was written by Hidalgo and [[Paul Ens]]<ref name="Credits">{{HoloNetNewsWeb||credits.html|Credits}}</ref> and released on the [[HoloNet News (original website)|HoloNet News website]] in [[2002]].<ref name="HoloNet News" /> Boon was identified as the Sy Myrthian that would later appear in ''Revenge of the Sith'' in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[James Luceno]]<ref name="ROTS VD">''[[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and released on [[April 2]], 2005.<ref name="Planning">{{SWArchive|url=episode-iii/release/publishing/news20041124.html|text=Planning Your Episode III Bookshelf}}</ref> The book also misspelled the character's name as "Brookish Boone."<ref name="ROTS VD" />
