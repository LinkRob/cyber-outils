'''Bilgefrogs''' were a [[species]] of [[frog]] [[Homeworld|native]]<ref name="Most Wanted" /> to the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Corellia">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. Around dawn, they tended to make croaking sounds.<ref name="Most Wanted" /> In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Wanted Date">"[[Tobias Beckett and Other Characters of the Underworld|Tobias Beckett and Other Characters From the Underworld]]" dates the events of ''Most Wanted'' to thirteen [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]].</ref> [[Corellian]] [[scrumrat]] of the [[White Worms]] [[Qi'ra]] reminisced about the croaking sounds of the bilgefrogs which she normally heard in the [[Freighter boneyard]].<ref name="Most Wanted" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bilgefrogs appeared in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Most Wanted]]'', written by [[Rae Carson]].<ref name="Most Wanted" />
