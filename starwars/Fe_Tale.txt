'''Fe Tale''' was a [[Kessarine]] [[Gender|male]] who was the child of [[Ferize]], [[Er Dal]], and [[Fel Ix]]. When he was born<ref name="POD" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a [[Naming ceremony]] was held that inducted him into the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Religion|cult]] that his parents were a part of. That same year, the [[Path compound]] on [[Dalna]] where they lived flooded, and Fe Tale was stuck with Er Dal and two of his other siblings underground and behind a rock, with Ferize trying to get to them. Er Dal was able to pass Fe Tale to Ferize through a gap in the rock, and Ferize left to bring him to safety while their friend and fellow Path member [[Marda Ro]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] rescued Er Dal and the other two trapped children.<ref name="POD" />
