'''''Classic Star Wars: A New Hope''''' is a 24-page book and audio cassette tape adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], released in [[November]] [[1994]].<ref name="Amazon" /> It uses the same audio and text from the original [[1979]] [[Star Wars (book-and-record)|book-and-record adaptation]]. It is unknown who narrated the cassette tape as credits were not provided with the book-and-tape.<ref name="Book and tape" />

==Publisher's summary==
''The action-packed adventure begins when young Luke Skywalker is swept away from his quiet home into battle among the stars! With the help of beautiful Princess Leia, the smuggler Han Solo and the mysterious Obi-Wan Kenobi, Luke comes face-to-face with the awesome Death Star in this explosive Star Wars Adventure.''<ref name="Amazon" />
