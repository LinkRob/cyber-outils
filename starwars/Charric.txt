A '''charric''' was a handheld weapon used by the [[Chiss Defense Force]]. It had at least two settings.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Charric discharges were brighter and made a higher-pitched sound than [[Galactic Republic]] [[blaster]]s.<ref name="Thrawn Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> [[Mitth'ali'astov]] and [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] were both trained in the use of charrics.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
