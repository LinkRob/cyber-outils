The '''Endor Forest Ranger''' was a type of armored [[repulsorcraft]] employed by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Endor Forest Rangers were notably used by the [[Alliance Army]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. Focused on terrain adaptability, Endor Forest Rangers were designed to deal with many different combat situations, and they were equipped with two [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="Galactic Defense" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Forest Rangers first appeared in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Defense]]'', which was released in [[2014]]<ref name="Galactic Defense release date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-defense-arrives-on-mobile-devices|text=''Star Wars: Galactic Defense'' Arrives on Mobile Devices}}</ref> and shut down in [[2016]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" />

The vehicle was originally created for the [[Kenner Products]] [[Mini-Rigs]] toy line in [[1984]].<ref name="Kenner">{{KennerCite|set=Mini-Rigs|pack=Endor Forest Ranger|link=vintminiEndor.asp}}</ref>
