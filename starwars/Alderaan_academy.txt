An '''academy''' was located in a [[Unidentified Alderaan city (academy)|city]] on<ref name="Life Day" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. The [[Alderaanian]] [[Rel (Alderaanian)|Rel]] was a student at the academy for three [[Standard year|years]] before graduating<ref name="Life Day" /> before [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> While at the academy, Rel's perspective of the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]] broadened, leading to him joining a [[Unidentified charitable organization|charitable organization]] after graduating.<ref name="Life Day" /> In 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Alderaan was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
