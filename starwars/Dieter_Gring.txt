'''Dieter Gring''' is a [[Wikipedia:German|German]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]]. Gring's first work for the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise was providing the German voice over for the [[Gender|male]] [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]] player character for ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Knights of the Eternal Throne]]''.<ref name="TOR Eternal Throne" /> Gring would later  narrate ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' German audio drama adaptations.<ref name="Mando Drama 1">''[[The Mandalorian Episode 1 - The Mandalorian / The Child]]''</ref>
