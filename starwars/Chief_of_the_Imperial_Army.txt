The '''Chief of the Imperial Army''', also known as the '''chief of military operations''' was one of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military|Joint Chiefs]] in the [[Imperial Army]]. [[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]] held the position during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="AEYNTK" />
