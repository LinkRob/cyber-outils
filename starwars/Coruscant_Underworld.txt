The '''Coruscant Underworld''', also called the '''Lower Coruscant''', '''lower levels''' or '''undercity''', were the levels underneath the surface structures of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. These very first levels of Coruscant were constructed in [[100,000 BBY]] and eventually rose from the soil into a [[Ecumenopolis|world-spanning city]].<ref name="BMF 26">{{BuildFalconCite|26|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> The underworld was a haven for [[bounty hunter]]s, [[pirate]]s, and criminal gangs, but also harbored war refugees and poor immigrants trying to make a pitiful living. Influential crime families operated in the lower levels of Coruscant,<ref name="Pyke Syndicate">{{Databank|pyke-syndicate|Pyke Syndicate}}</ref> which were patrolled by the [[Coruscant underworld police|underworld police]] division of the [[Coruscant Security Force]].<ref name="CSFunderpolice">{{SWE|groups|coruscant-underworld-police|Coruscant underworld police}}</ref> Devoid of all sunlight, the lower levels were left in a perpetual state of darkness illuminated only by artificial lights for thousands of years.<ref name="Catch a Jedi" /> The underworld was polluted and infested with pests and dangerous escaped pets. The very lowest levels of the city were rumored to hold ancient secrets.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

[[Skyscraper]]s towered above the highest level on Coruscant, [[Level 5127]],<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> but under the surface structures the levels of Coruscant descended down all the way to [[Level 1]], which was deemed uninhabitable.<ref name="Catch a Jedi EP guide" /> Undercity dwellers often had to breathe toxic fumes from [[Millennium|millennia]] of urbanization, while wealthy citizens in the upper levels had access to rich and filtered air.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> The various levels of Coruscant were accessible by huge [[Underworld portal|portals]] that also served as ventilation shafts.<ref name="LethalTrackEpGuide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lethal-trackdown-episode-guide|text="Lethal Trackdown" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
