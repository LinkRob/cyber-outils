A '''Breathing sac''' was a type of anatomy that some species had in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="PastTheBarrier">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref>

In the [[New Republic Era]], a [[Unidentified greasy creature|greasy pirate]] on [[Borgo Prime]] threaten to pop the [[pirate]] [[Vane]]'s breathing sac for trying to take an [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] that they stole.<ref name="PastTheBarrier" />

==Behind the scenes==
Breathing sacs were first mentioned in "[[Way, Way Out Past the Barrier]]" the second episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' which aired on [[December 2]] [[2024]].<ref name="PastTheBarrier" />
