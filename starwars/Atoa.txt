'''Atoa''' was an [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] with [[Temperature|frigid]] temperature. It was located in the [[Atoa system]] within the [[Expansion Region]]'s [[Ghost Nebula sector]] in grid P-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], between [[22 BBY|22]] to [[19 BBY]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] set a [[Separatist prison camp|prison camp]] on Atoa. [[Karama]], a former reporter of the [[Galactic Republic]], was one of the prisoners before eventually joining the [[Partisans]] after getting rescued by the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]].

In [[9 ABY]], during the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]], the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]] did an interview where he reminisced on his time as an [[Stormtrooper|Imperial stormtrooper]] when he was deployed on an ice planet, though he could not recall if it was Atoa or other ice planets [[Hoth]] and [[Gigor]]. On that ice planet, Sentinel claimed to have fought victorious against a [[bonzami]] or a [[wampa]] and also found a [[Permafrost crystal]], which he later used to supposedly defeat a [[Fireworm]] during his next deployment.

==History==
===Prisoners of war===
Between [[22 BBY|22]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> during the Clone Wars&mdash;a war between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]&mdash;the Separatist established a prison camp on Atoa. Among its prisoners was [[Karama]]&mdash;a former reporter for the Republic's military news feeds&mdash;who later joined the [[Partisans]] after being found and rescued by the resistance fighter [[Saw Gerrera]] from the prison camp.<ref name="MoF" />

===Dubious tale===
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Hunters Date">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sentinel]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> did an interview<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> for the competitive sporting show [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> and revealed stories about his past as a [[stormtrooper]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. In one of his stories, Sentinel explained that at some point during his Imperial service, he was deployed to an ice planet&mdash;though he could not remember if it was Atoa or other ice planets, [[Hoth]] and [[Gigor]]. On that same mission, he claimed to have gotten separated from other troopers due to low visibility from the heavy pouring snow, forcing Sentinel to continue his deployment alone. Sentinel proceeded to tell the fans how he later tripped over another trooper&mdash;who was completely frozen solid&mdash;and found an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon|E-Web heavy repeater]] half-buried in the snow next to him, which was supposedly hinted to be the same [[weapon]] Sentinel was using for the sporting show,<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> the [[Prototype E-Web]].<ref name="Prototype E-Web">{{HuntersCite|character=Sentinel|costume=Prototype E-Web}}</ref>

Continuing his reminisce, the frozen trooper then woke up and stared right at Sentinel as a creature&mdash;which he thought was either a [[bonzami]] or a [[wampa]]&mdash;approached him, though he managed to defeat it with the heavy repeater he had found and used the body of the fallen creature as a "blanket" to survive for the night.<ref name="Sentinel M2" /> Another competitor of the show,<ref name="SWH" /> [[Imara Vex]], later came in to the interview while Sentinel was on a bio-break to debunk his story of how he got the Prototype E-Web, adding that Sentinel might have bought the heavy repeater from [[Brinna's Blasters and Bolts|Brinna's Blasters]]. Returning to the interview, Sentinel claimed to also have found a [[Permafrost crystal]] on the ice planet, which he supposedly used to defeat a [[Fireworm]] by putting the [[crystal]] in its maw while on his next deployment on a [[Magma|lava]] planet, though he was again unsure of it was either the planets [[Senna]] or [[Mustafar]]. Vex then returned once more to debunk Sentinel's dubious claims.<ref name="Sentinel M2" />
