'''Durilliam''' was a type of [[gas]] that was [[Poison|toxic]] to a number of [[species]], including [[Arcona]], [[Delphidian]]s, [[Gabdorin]]s, [[Gamorrean]]s, [[Ishi Tib]]s, and [[Twi'lek]]s. Under certain circumstances, the core of a crashed [[starship]] would leak durilliam gases.<ref name="Lost Treasure" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> two shipments of durilliam were sent to [[Fuel Station Visavi]] while it was stationed in orbit of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
