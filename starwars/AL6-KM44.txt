'''AL6-KM44''' was an [[Allanar N3 light freighter]] in the [[Imperial Navy|navy]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was used to transport components for the secret [[Project Stardust]] from the [[Kurost transfer point]]. As part of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Balanhai Savit]]'s scheme to steal components from Project Stardust for his own use, the [[light freighter]] was stolen under the pretense of a [[gralloc]] attack, and taken to a nearby system where a [[Unidentified mobile way station|mobile way station]] was to collect it. However, the [[Space station|station]] came under attack by the [[Grysk]]s, and AL6-KM44 was heavily damaged, with its crew [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" />
