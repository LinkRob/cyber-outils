The '''Chis Ascendancy's east-zenith sector''' was a [[sector]] of the [[Unknown Regions]]. It housed a portion of the [[Aristocra (organization)|aristocratic]] [[oligarchy]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. It also housed the [[Paataatus Hiveborn]]. It was located on the far side of the Ascendancy from the [[Lioaoin Regime]] and the [[Garwian Unity]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Chiss Ascendancy's east-zenith sector was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first volume in the [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]] written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
