'''Beris Ford''' was a minor [[gangster]] who was active during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Hunter vs Hunted" /> By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> he was captured by the [[Bounty hunter|bounty hunting]] duo [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]], and was kept [[prisoner]] aboard their [[starship]], the ''[[Mist Hunter]]'', for [[Standard week|weeks]]. Eventually, Ford's family hired [[Han Solo]] and his co-[[pilot]] [[Chewbacca]] to rescue him. When Zuckuss and 4-LOM kidnapped him, hoping to collect the [[bounty]] placed on his head by [[Rekias Nodo]], Solo managed to free himself and Ford thanks to Chewbacca, who had been smuggled aboard the ''Mist Hunter'' in a large barrel. Ford was, in turn, placed in the barrel, which was launched into [[Realspace|space]] with a [[homing beacon]] so his family could find him.<ref name="Hunter vs Hunted" />
