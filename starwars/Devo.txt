'''Devo''' was a [[Valon]] [[Jedi Master]] who served the [[Jedi Order]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]'s [[Valo Temple|Jedi temple]]. In [[231 BBY]], they and other [[Jedi]] welcomed [[Law enforcement agency|security forces]] sent by the [[Galactic Republic]] arriving for the world's [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]]. The [[230 BBY|following year]], Devo was [[Death|killed]] by [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] during an [[Attack on Valo|attack]] on Valo while trying to flee into the planet's [[mountain]]s with the [[Jedi temple|temple's]] [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]]. In [[229 BBY]], the Jedi [[youngling]]s [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]], [[Kildo]], and [[Tep Tep]]&mdash;who had survived the attack&mdash;returned to [[Lonisa City]] and informed [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] of Devo and the other Masters' fates.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Go back, stay with the adults, and they'll be able to protect you from the Nihil.''"<br />"''They won't. They can't. We don't have anyone to return to.|Ram Jomaram and Gavi, about Devo and Valo's other Jedi Masters|<ref name="EFV">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref>}}
Devo was a [[Valon]] [[Jedi Master]] who served the [[Jedi Order]] at the [[Valo Temple]] on<ref name="RTCT" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="EFV" /> [[planet]] [[Valo]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="RTCT" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date-RTCT">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> they and Master [[Shonnatrucks]] helped receive [[Law enforcement agency|security forces]] sent by the [[Galactic Republic]] for Valo's upcoming [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] scheduled to be hosted in the world's<ref name="RTCT" /> [[capital]],<ref name="EFV" /> [[Lonisa City]]. While there, the two Masters were accompanied by [[Padawan]]s from the [[Jedi temple|temple]]. Not long after, the [[droid]] [[V-18]] learned of a perimeter security breach of Valo's [[Crashpoint Tower]] [[Comm tower|communications tower]], followed by the planet's comms becoming faulty. After failing to locate Devo and the Valo Temple's other Jedi Masters or raise them on [[Comm unit|comm]], the droid informed [[Jedi]] Padawan [[Ram Jomaram]] of the issues and also recounted the locations of the rest of the Valon Jedi. Ultimately, the tower's functionality was restored by Jomaram, allowing the Jedi to work with the Republic to repel a [[Valo Atrocity|strike]] on the [[Republic Fair]] by [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]].<ref name="RTCT" />

[[File:EscapefromValo.png|thumb|right|280px|Three younglings informed Ram Jomaram (center left) of Devo's death to the Nihil.]]
Following the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]]<ref name="EFV" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="THRCE">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Valo's Jedi Masters prepared to flee to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="EFV" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Coruscant]]. However, their efforts were stalled when Valo was [[Attack on Valo|attacked]] by the Nihil not long after<ref name="EFV" /> in the same [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Date-EFV" /> All of the Valo Temple's Masters<ref name="EFV" />&mdash;including Devo<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1752785438899593614|[[Daniel José Older]]|date=2024-01-31|quote=yes all dead|archivefile=File:ValoMasters-OlderTweet.png}} {{C|In response to: "There's some Valo Jedi Masters from ''Race to Crashpoint Tower'' (Lege, Shonnatrucks, Devo) then also a smattering of Valo Jedi Masters mentioned throughout ''Escape from Valo''. The younglings in the novel and Driggit see many of them perish in the attack on Valo &mdash; is it safe to say all of them passed away save for Kunpar, or were there ones that survived and the younglings only assumed they all got dusted?"}}</ref>&mdash;hurriedly attempted to flee into the world's [[mountain]]s to escape Lonisa City. However, the Nihil caught the Jedi and [[Death|killed]] all of them despite their attempts to fight back, with several being [[Diet|fed]] to a [[Living Force|Force]]-eating [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] creature&mdash;resulting in only the [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] [[Gavi Takitaken|Gavi]], [[Kildo]], and [[Tep Tep]] surviving. After living in Valo's mountains alone for a [[229 BBY|year]], the three [[youngling]]s returned to the [[city]] and introduced themselves to Jomaram&mdash;who had been acting as an anti-Nihil [[saboteur]] nicknamed the "[[Scarlet Skull]]"&mdash;telling him of the deaths of the Valo Temple's Jedi Masters. The Padawan&mdash;who had hoped Devo and the others were gathering strength for a strike against the Nihil&mdash;grieved their passing.<ref name="EFV" />

==Powers and abilities==
As a member of the Jedi Order, Devo was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual [[Jedi training|trained]] to [[Force power|wield]] [[the Force]].<ref name="EFV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Devo was first mentioned in [[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower|''Race to Crashpoint Tower'']], a junior novel written by [[Daniel José Older]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="RTCT" /> Phase I that was published on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to its release, the Jedi's mention was included in a preview for the work printed in the junior novel [[The High Republic: A Test of Courage|''A Test of Courage'']],<ref name="ATOC">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> published on [[January 5]] of the same year.<ref name="Phase" /> On [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], Older confirmed that all of the Valo Temple's Jedi Masters&mdash;including Devo&mdash;perished in the Nihil attack mentioned in ''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]'',<ref name="Twitter" /> a [[2024]] junior novel written by himself and [[Alyssa Wong]] as part of the ''Star Wars: The High Republic''{{'s}}<ref name="EFV" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase" />
