The '''Elochar sector''' was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and [[the Slice]]. In the [[sector]]'s [[Leyoye system]], a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]-contracted team [[Mission on Leyoye Prime|deployed]] on the astronomical object [[Leyoye Prime]] in the early [[Standard month|months]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. The team of six was decimated by [[Galactic Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|forces]] in their attempts to exfiltrate.

In [[3 ABY]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Elochar sector flotilla|flotilla]] rendezvoused in [[Realspace|deep space]] in the Elochar sector. During the rendezvous, an infiltration team sent by the [[Galactic Empire]] successfully [[Infiltration of the Thunderstrike|infiltrated]] the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]''. Efforts from the [[61st Mobile Infantry|rebels]] onboard the [[corvette]] allowed the [[flotilla]] to scatter before Imperial [[Herald|reinforcements]] could pin them there.

==Description==
The Elochar sector was located within the intersection of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Slice" /> It contained the [[Ardroxia system|Ardroxia]], [[Celen system|Celen]], [[Leyoye system|Leyoye]], and [[Ord Thabl system]]s at the coordinates R-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]. The Leyoye system<ref name="Systems" /> contained the astronomical object [[Leyoye Prime]].<ref name="MOF" />

==History==
{{Quote|You have training to offer, experience to impart&mdash;''"<br />"''There's a whole flotilla here. More than enough soldiers to coach them better than me.''"<br />"''Perhaps. Then join me and Roach awhile? Captain So-Hem of the ''Sixmoon'' has invited members of Twilight Company to visit his ship.|Rebel soldiers [[Gadren]] and [[Brand]]|<ref name="BTC" />}}
[[File:ThunderstrikeAndApailanasPromise-XWSE.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Thunderstrike'' and ''Apailana's Promise'' (pictured) rendezvoused with other rebel ships in the Elochar sector.]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="3 ABY">{{BTCdate}}</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], four rebel [[battle group]]s rendezvoused in [[Realspace|deep space]] in the Elochar sector to form a [[Elochar sector flotilla|flotilla]] of over a dozen [[starship]]s. Among the [[flotilla]] were the ships of the [[61st Mobile Infantry]]&mdash;Twilight Company&mdash;which travelled in from the<ref name="BTC" /> nearby<ref name="Systems" /> [[Metatessu sector]]. The [[company]] had allocated a [[Standard month|month]] to repair its two vessels, the [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Thunderstrike]]'' and the [[Braha'tok-class gunship|''Braha'tok''-class gunship]] ''[[Apailana's Promise]]'', and allow [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] to heal up and train. Orders were expected from the [[Alliance High Command]] for the whole flotilla by the end of the month.<ref name="BTC" />

In the flotilla, repairs assisted by flotilla cargo haulers were undertaken on the ''Thunderstrike'' and Twilight Company underwent training exercises, with [[squad]]s also visiting other flotilla ships on shore leave. [[Captain]] [[So-Hem]] of the starship [[Sixmoon (starship)|''Sixmoon'']] invited Twilight Company personnel to visit his ship as well. Ten [[Standard day|days]] into Twilight Company's respite, however, the damaged rebel [[light freighter]] ''[[Trumpet's Call]]'' arrived at the flotilla without life support. The [[freighter]] had been [[Capture of the Trumpet's Call|attacked]] by the Empire, and an infiltration team was posing as survivors in order to reach the ''Thunderstrike''.<ref name="BTC" />

[[File:Herald-RebelFiles.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The ''Herald'' (pictured) arrived in the Elochar sector to hunt down the ''Thunderstrike''.]]
Taking the bait, the ''Thunderstrike'' took aboard the survivors, who were soon able to turn on their helpers and [[Infiltration of the Thunderstrike|capture]] the [[corvette]]'s [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. [[Lieutenant]] [[Sairgon]], the highest [[Military rank|ranking]] member of Twilight Company at the flotilla, sent a warning message to the rest of the flotilla before he was [[Death|killed]]. The rest of the flotilla thus scattered, leaving just the ''Thunderstrike'' and ''Apailana's Promise''. With the infiltration team holding the ''Thunderstrike''{{'s}} bridge,<ref name="BTC" /> the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]'' arrived to oversee Twilight Company's surrender. However, the ''Apailana's Promise'' was able to shield the ''Thunderstrike'' temporarily, and some members of the company were able to retake the bridge, allowing the remaining rebels to escape under fire from the [[Star Destroyer]]. Twilight Company fled the [[sector]] to the<ref name="BTC" /> [[Hutt Space]]<ref name="Systems" /> [[planet]] [[Ankhural]].<ref name="BTC" />

==Locations==
Leyoye Prime was a terrestrial astronomical object with fields. In the first few months of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]],<ref name="MOF" /> which began in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a Separatist contracted team of six [[Mission on Leyoye Prime|planted]] a [[bomb]] in a [[Unidentified city (Leyoye Prime)|city]] on Leyoye Prime but was unable to exfiltrate the astronomical object afterward. [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]], including [[clone trooper]]s and a [[Unidentified Jedi (Leyoye Prime)|Jedi]], pursued the team and killed all but two of them, [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] and [[Poan of Riek]].<ref name="MOF" />
