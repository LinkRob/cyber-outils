'''Darren Tan''' (born [[August 21]]) is an artist from [[Wikipedia:Malaysia|Malaysia]] who provided artwork for the ''[[Star Wars Galaxies Trading Card Game]]'' sets [[Champions of the Force (SWGTCG)|''Champions of the Force'']], ''[[Squadrons Over Corellia]]'', ''[[Galactic Hunters]]'', ''[[The Shadow Syndicate]]'', and ''[[Threat of the Conqueror]]''. He also designed the official poster art for [[Celebration Chicago|''Star Wars'' Celebration Chicago]].
