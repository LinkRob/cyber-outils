'''G4-4M''' was an [[astromech droid]] who served the [[Resistance]] on the [[planet]] [[Ajan Kloss]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War]]. The [[droid]] was one of five astromechs who worked under [[R5-2JE]] and who were all absent from their jobs at the landing pad one day. G4-4M was busy making a fanfic instead of doing R5-2JE's work.<ref name="LEGO saga" />

==Behind the scenes==
G4-4M first appeared in the [[2022]] non-canon video game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', published by [[TT Games]].<ref name="LEGO saga" />
