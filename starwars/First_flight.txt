'''First flight''' was a unit of [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s within [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] that was part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. They took part in the [[Battle of Yavin]] and was led by [[Commander]] [[Garven Dreis]], [[Red Leader|leader]] of [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]], with [[Puck Naeco]] and [[Theron Nett]] as his wingmen.<ref name="Duty Roster" />
