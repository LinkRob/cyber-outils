The '''Billaron system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Chitarghar sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square K-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Billaron]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Billaron system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
