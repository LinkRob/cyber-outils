'''''Doctor Aphra'' 40''' is the fortieth and final issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]], illustrated by [[Minkyu Jung]] and [[Rachelle Rosenberg]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 31]], [[2024]].

==Publisher's summary==
''NIGHT AS DAY! The TAGGE FLEET burns. The GALAXY reels. DOCTOR APHRA is alone. But as she explores an ancient ruin on a desolate planet, Aphra may find the answers she's been hunting for her entire life. Is this the end for DOCTOR APHRA?!''
