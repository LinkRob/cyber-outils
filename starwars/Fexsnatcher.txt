'''Fexsnatcher''' was an insult used in [[the galaxy]], referring to a form of body orifice found in certain [[species]].<ref name="After the Fall">{{StoryCite|story=After the Fall|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref> [[Sian Holt]] referred to [[Keefar Branto]] as a "[[kark]]ing fexsnatcher,"<ref name="Trail 1" /> and when [[Affie Hollow]] was describing [[Marchion Ro]] and the [[Nihil]] to the [[Kaleesh]] [[Dumas Mar'Ti]], she was unsure if the Kaleesh species had the specific orifice she was thinking of, but nevertheless settled on "fexsnatchers."<ref name="After the Fall" />
