'''''Dark Times'' 15''' is the fifteenth issue in the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]'' comic series and the third in the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times: Blue Harvest]]'' story arc. Originally solicited for [[June 24]], [[2009]], it was instead delayed until [[January 20]], [[2010]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Caught between two rival factions, former Jedi Dass Jennir has only one option left - play both sides against one another and hope that neither side tumbles to his plan!''
