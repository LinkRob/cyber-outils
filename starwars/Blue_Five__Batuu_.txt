"'''Blue Five'''" was a woman and a [[Mirialan]]. As a pilot for the [[Resistance]], she flew a [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] as a part of [[Blue Squadron]]. On her final mission, she flew as part of a wing guard for a Resistance recruitment transport leaving [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Rise" />

==History==
Blue Five joined the Resistance before [[34 ABY]] and flew as a starfighter pilot as a part of Blue Squadron under [[Captain]] [[Temmin Wexley]].
When the [[First Order]] attacked the Resistance base on [[D'Qar]] in 34 ABY, Blue Five was away on a mission and one of the few surviving members of Blue Squadron; [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]'s bombing run killed most of the squadron before they could deploy.<ref name="TLJ">''[[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi]]''</ref>

Under orders from [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa Solo]] to grow the Resistance, Blue Five joined other revolutionaries on Batuu to regroup and rebuild. There, she acted as air support should any First Order [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] attack their hidden base.<ref name="Rise" />
Later that year, [[Vi Moradi]] drummed up enough new recruits, that local command decided to fly the group to General Organa's secret base on [[Pacara]]. Blue Five joined the mission with [[Red Two (Batuu)|Red Two]] to escort an [[Intersystem Transport Ship]] leaving carrying the new volunteers for the cause. The three ships along with [[Commander]] [[Poe Dameron]] in the lead took off from the [[Black Spire Ruins]] and flew into orbit.<ref name="Rise" />

{{Quote|Red Two, Blue Five, keep it tight now. Let's get ready to make that jump to lightspeed on my mark.|Poe Dameron|<ref name="Rise" />}}
Before they could escape through [[Hyperspace]] their group was attacked by a flurry of TIE fighters launched from the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer]] hidden in orbit, the [[Finalizer]].<ref name="Rise" /> She leapt into action, gunning down a few TIEs; however, they were quickly overwhelmed, and the TIEs shot down her wingmate, Red Two. Blue Five took evasive action, but there were too many of them. She [[Death|died]] defending the transport when her fighter was destroyed. While she and Red Two perished in the skirmish, they bought Poe Dameron time to jump to hyperspace to scramble reinforcements. 
He brought back a small fleet of Resistance capital ships to engage the Star Destroyers while the new  recruits successfully met up with [[Finn]] aboard the Destroyer and escaped with valuable intelligence.<ref name="Rise" />
