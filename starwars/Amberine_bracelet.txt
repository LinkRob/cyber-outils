'''Amberine bracelets''' were decorative [[bracelet]]s often warn by [[Tosharan]]s on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an amberine bracelet could be sold for forty [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Amberine bracelets appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the bracelet could only be sold for credits, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaws" />
