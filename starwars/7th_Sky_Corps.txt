The '''7th Sky Corps''' was a [[Republic Military|military]] [[corps]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Like other units in the Grand [[Army]], the ranks of the 7th consisted of [[clone trooper]]s that had been [[Cloning|engineered]] to fight for the [[Galactic Republic]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], the 7th was led by [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Clone Marshal Commander|Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]].<ref name="Episode III" /> As a corps within the Grand Army of the Republic, the 7th Sky Corps consisted of 36,864 [[trooper]]s divided into sixteen [[regiment]]s.

==Description==
===Organization===
[[File:ObiWan3-textless.png|thumb|left|200px|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Cody were the Jedi General and Marshal Commander of the 7th Sky Corps, respectively.]]
As a [[corps]] within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the 7th Sky Corps consisted of 36,864 [[trooper]]s divided into sixteen [[regiment]]s.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that a [[corps]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] contains 36,864 [[clone trooper]]s divided into sixteen [[regiment]]s. It also establishes that each regiment contains sixteen [[Company|companies]], and that each company contains four [[platoon]]s. It further states that each platoon has four squads. Since the 7th Sky Corps is a corps within the Grand Army, it must also have 36,864 troopers divided into sixteen regiments, 256 companies, 1,024 platoons, and 4,096 squads.</ref> The corps also contained multiple [[battalion]]s,<ref name="Carnage of Krell" /> including the [[212th Attack Battalion]].<ref name="Ultimate" /> The [[212th Recon Division]] was a unit under the 212th Attack Battalion.<ref name="BFII Info" /> 256 [[Company|companies]] were part of the 7th Sky Corps,<ref name="Complete Locations" /> including the 212th's [[Ghost Company]]<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> and [[2nd Airborne Company]].<ref name="Clone Paratrooper" /> [[Waxer's platoon]] in the 212th<ref name="Carnage of Krell" /> was one of 1,024 [[platoon]]s that made up the 7th Sky Corps, and a total of 4,096 squads made up the corps.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> The 7th Sky Corps also contained [[Foxtrot Group]], a unit of [[special forces]] led by [[Clone Captain]] [[Gregor]].<ref name="Missing in Action" /> The 7th Sky Corps was under the overall command of [[High Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[clone trooper officer]], [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Cody]], with the duo personally commanding the 212th Attack Battalion.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Equipment===
The [[clone trooper]]s of the 212th Attack Battalion were identified by their [[color]] scheme of orange<ref name="SWE Boil">{{SWE|characters|boil|Boil}}</ref> on white [[clone trooper armor]] or retained their blank white armor.<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> [[Advanced Recon Force trooper|ARF troopers]] of the 212th Recon Divison used orange and brown camouflage.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> [[Crys]] customized his armor with yellow on his white armor.<ref name="The Deserter">{{TCW|The Deserter}}</ref>

===Vehicles===
The 7th Sky Corps utilized various vehicles from its arsenal. Its ground components utilized [[All Terrain Reconnaissance Transport]]s,<ref name="Kidnapped" /> [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s, and [[BARC speeder]]s. Aerial components used [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|LAAT carriers]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> Members of the 7th Sky Corps were at times stationed on [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] such as the ''[[Negotiator]]'',<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> as well as [[Arquitens-class light cruiser|''Arquitens''-class light cruisers]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

==History==
===Early conflicts===
After the [[Clone Wars]] began at the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the 7th Sky Corps<ref name="Ultimate" /> became part of the Grand Army of the Republic and was assigned to High Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi and [[Clone Commander|Clone Marshal Commander]]<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Cody.<ref name="The Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> Early during the [[Cloning|Clone Wars]], members of the 7th Sky Corps were dispatched to a series of [[battles on a rocky planet]],<ref name="QH">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref> the [[siege of Hisseen]],<ref name="Battle Tales 1">[[Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales 1|''Star Wars Adventures: The Clone Wars – Battle Tales'' 1]]</ref> and [[Christophsis]] alongside [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]'s [[501st Legion]]. As such, the corps fought in the [[Battle of Christophsis]] against [[Confederacy military|Separatist forces]], eventually emerging victorious and subsequently participating in the [[Battle of Teth]].<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>

===Further action and Ryloth===
During the [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' Campaign]], Marshal Commander Cody reported that the ships under Kenobi's command were in their defensive formations.<ref name="Rising Malevolence">{{TCW|Rising Malevolence|Rising ''Malevolence''}}</ref> Later, Commander Cody alongside Clone Captain [[Rex]] conducted reviews of [[Republic Military]] bases when they, alongside the clone trooper survivors of [[Rishi Station]], participated in the [[Defense of Rishi Station]].<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:Waxer_armor.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Clone Lieutenant Waxer during the Battle of Ryloth]]
The 7th Sky Corps participated in the [[Battle of Ryloth]], and were the first to secure a landing zone on the [[planet]] for the rest of Republic forces after Ghost Company<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> of the 212th Attack Battalion, a battalion of the 7th Sky Corps,<ref name="Ultimate" /> were deployed to liberate the [[city]] of [[Nabat]]. Led by Kenobi, the troops advanced through the city without use of heavy weaponry as not to decimate the town they were fighting to save. When Cody and [[Wooley]] discovered that the [[tactical droid]] commander [[TX-20]] was holding the Twi'lek's hostage in the middle of the city, the group planned a surprise attack and eventually liberated the Nabat.<ref name="Innocents of Ryloth" /> After the rest of Republic forces landed, the 7th Sky Corps moved and eventually secured the [[Jixuan desert]], bringing [[Ryloth]]'s southern hemisphere under Republic control. Eventually, Ryloth was liberated from Separatist control.<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

===Felucia and Black Stall Station===
During the [[First Battle of Felucia]], at least some members of the 7th Sky Corps, including Commander Cody and Kenobi, participated in the battle alongside the 501st Legion and the [[104th Battalion]], although all Republic units were forced to escape offworld.<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> After the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]] was captured by the [[Jedi Order]] for stealing a valuable [[holocron]], Commander Cody accompanied Kenobi and [[Mace Windu]] to [[Black Stall Station]] and [[pilot]]ed a [[T-6 shuttle]] there. Although Bane managed to escape, Kenobi and Windu managed to retrieve the holocron.<ref name="Children of the Force">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref>

===Second Battle of Geonosis===
====Landing at Point Rain====
After [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] uncovered that the [[Trade Federation]] and the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] were building [[droid]] factories on the planet [[Geonosis]], the Republic dispatched a [[task force]] made up of the 7th Sky Corps, 501st Legion, 41st Elite Corps, special operations, and more units that transported and supported by ten ''Acclamator''-class [[assault ship]]s and six ''Venator''-class Star Destroyers. The planet-wide invasion culminated in the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], with units<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> of the 212th Recon Division,<ref name="BFII Info" /> 501st Legion, and [[special ops clone trooper]]s launching an assault on the [[Geonosis primary droid foundry|primary droid foundry]]. Commander Cody devised a plan for a three-pronged attack, with General [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]]'s special ops troopers flanking from the north, the 7th Sky Corps' units, consisting almost entirely of ARF troopers and AT-TEs, approaching head-on, and Skywalker's 501st Legion coming from the south, all landing at [[Point Rain]] before moving to destroy the foundry's [[deflector shield generator]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" />

The 7th Sky Corps' 212th Recon [[Division]] was the first unit to launch from their assault ship, and the force consisted of LAAT/i gunships and LAAT carriers transporting AT-TEs. [[Skywalker's team]] was the next to follow, and then Mundi's special ops forces. Engaged by [[J-1 proton cannon]]s, [[LR1K sonic cannon]]s, and [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class territorial defense starfighters]], the Republic forces suffered heavy casualties, with only the 212th Recon Division reaching the rendezvous at Point Rain. Commander Cody established a perimeter with LAAT/i gunships and AT-TEs to hold their position against overwhelming [[Geonosian]] forces. During the battle, Kenobi's gunship was shot down, with Kenobi and clone trooper [[Trapper]] being the only survivors. They were eventually rescued by troopers [[Boil]] and [[Waxer]]. As the battle progressed, the 212th became more and more overwhelmed until [[Red Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Red Squadron]]&mdash;a [[squadron]] of [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s&mdash;arrived for air support and Skywalker and Mundi's units arrived at Point Rain. With surviving Republic forces now linked up, another assault was launched, this time to destroy the [[deflector shield]] generator. Skywalker and a small group of his men penetrated through the shield and jammed Geonosian scanners, allowing a team of AT-TEs to move in and destroy the generator. In the aftermath, Mundi, Kenobi, and other wounded troopers were evacuated while Skywalker and his forces dealt with the destruction of the generator.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" />

====Assault on the factory====
Units of the 41st Elite Corps were dispatched with General [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Barriss Offee]] to serve as reinforcements for Skywalker's assault on the primary foundry. The Republic staging area was secured by the 501st Legion, the 41st Elite Corps, and special ops troopers. During Skywalker and Unduli's attack on the factory, several members of the 212th Attack Battalion also participated, and Republic forces ultimately prevailed in destroying the factory.<ref name="Weapons Factory">{{TCW|Weapons Factory}}</ref>

====Progate Temple====
In the aftermath of the main assault, the Republic undertook cleanup operations throughout the planet as well as undertaking the mission of locating Geonosian leader [[Archduke]] [[Poggle the Lesser]]. After Unduli found evidence of Poggle at the [[Progate Temple]] only to lose contact, Kenobi and Skywalker, along with members of the 212th Attack Battalion, including [[Gearshift]] and Commander Cody, traveled into the temple's catacombs. There, they encountered [[Queen]] [[Karina the Great]] and her [[Zombie|reanimated]] [[Warrior caste (Geonosian)|Geonosian warriors]]. Eventually, Republic forces managed to rescue Unduli, capture Poggle, and [[Death|kill]] the Geonosian queen.<ref name="Legacy of Terror">{{TCW|Legacy of Terror}}</ref>
