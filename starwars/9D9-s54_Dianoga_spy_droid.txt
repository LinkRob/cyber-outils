The '''9D9-s54 Dianoga spy droid'''<ref name="Epic Battles" /> was a model of [[spy droid]] developed by the [[Imperial Department of Military Research]] for [[Spy|espionage]] purposes.<ref name="Spy Droid" /> Intelligent, they had a deceptively simple design<ref name="Epic Battles" /> and featured tracking and identification capabilities<ref name="Spy Droid" /> that allowed them to follow their targets and record every move they make with a high-definition [[holorecorder]], after which they would transmit their data back to the [[Galactic Empire]] over secure channels.<ref name="Epic Battles" /> [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] used a spy [[droid]] to track the movements of [[Minister]] [[Maketh Tua]], as well as to record footage of her [[assassin]]ation in order to falsely implicate the [[Spectres|rebels]] of [[Lothal]] in her [[Death|murder]].<ref name="Siege">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Siege of Lothal]]''</ref>
