'''Aurora''' was an individual who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" /> By [[229 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The section of [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 4|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 4]] that mentions Aurora takes place five [[Standard month|months]] after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]]. Per the reasoning [[Template:StarlightDate|here]], the section must therefore occur in [[229 BBY]].</ref> Aurora was [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] in a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Unidentified Galactic Republic prison|prison]] near<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Ryloth]] that was also located within the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s' [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the Nihil captured the [[prison]] and a [[Unidentified Nihil team|team]] led by a [[Unidentified Zabrak Nihil|Zabrak]] evaluated the prison's inmates&mdash;including Aurora&mdash;to see if any were Nihil, or if any might be useful enough to be recovered. Afterward, at a meeting held at a [[Nihil main base|Nihil fortress]] on<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" /> the Outer Rim planet [[Hetzal Prime]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> the [[Zabrak]] mentioned the captured prison, and that their team had assessed the inmates. As the Zabrak spoke, a [[hologram]] was displayed showing Aurora's name [[Writing|written]] in<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" /> the [[Aurebesh]] script<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> alongside fellow prisoners [[Boolan]] and [[Xolein]]. Recognizing the name Boolan, [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] personally visited the prison and recruited Boolan into the Nihil.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aurora was mentioned in a hologram in the [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight]]'', released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 4" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 3]], [[2024]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112955/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_shadows_of_starlight_2023_4|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - Shadows of Starlight (2023) #4}}</ref>
