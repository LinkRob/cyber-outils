On the [[planet]] [[Ylagia]], a '''fasting season''' was practiced by members of the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Alvadorjian]]s living there as a deeply ingrained part of their culture. By the time of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the Alvadorjian [[Soujen Vak-Nhalis]] and his [[Nahasta clan|family]] participated in at least one fasting season.<ref name="MOF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|9}}</ref> he briefly spoke with the [[Partisans|freedom fighter]] [[Karama]] about his origins on Ylagia. Though he considered informing her of the Alvadorjian's fasting season and other culturally significant occurrences, he decided not to, considering his culture too sacred to share.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The fasting season was mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
