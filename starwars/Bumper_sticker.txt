A '''bumper sticker''' was a type of [[Chassisplast|sticker]] that could be added to the bumper of a vehicle. The [[74-Z speeder bike]] came with a bumper sticker denoting that the owner's child was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] honor student.<ref name="Training" /> Several bumper stickers were collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles">''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''</ref>
