[[Major]] '''Carnok Yeteri''' was a [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military]]. He was known for his brutality. At some point during the [[Iron Blockade]], Yeteri was captured by the exiled [[Noble Court|aristocrats]] of the [[Anoat sector]], who hoped to obtain valuable Imperial secrets from him.<ref name="Int" />
