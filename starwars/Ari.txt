'''Ari''' was a fair-skinned [[Sephi/Legends|Sephi]]. At one point in history, they fought with dual [[Long knife|long knives]]. Sephi had dark [[Eye/Legends|eyes]], sported long [[Color/Legends|red]] [[Hair/Legends|hair]], and favored light [[Clothing/Legends|clothing]] that left them unhampered in their movements.<ref name="Ari" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ari was created by the ''[[Star Wars]]'' artist [[HOON]] during the conceptual research<ref name="Ari" /> for the [[2004]] [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] comic ''[[Jedi: Yoda]]'', written by [[Jeremy Barlow]].<ref name="JY">''[[Jedi: Yoda]]''</ref> A piece of concept art of the character used to appear on Hoon's website,<ref name="Ari" /> but Ari themselves was absent from the final release of ''Jedi: Yoda''.<ref name="JY" />
