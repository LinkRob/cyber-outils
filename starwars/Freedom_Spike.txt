The '''Freedom Spike''', previously called the '''Jackpot Spike''', was customized [[Computer spike|data spike]] created by [[Gedeek Obaz]]. Gedeek originally used the spike to slice [[slot machine]]s at the [[Canto Grand]] [[casino]] with [[Kay Vess]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> sometime before [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> During this time, the spike could also be used to neutralize [[restraining bolt]] while they were attached to [[droid]]s. Gedeek was later arrested and forced to work with the [[Galactic Empire]]. While Gedeek was working with the Empire,<ref name="Outlaws" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date" /> Vess began working with [[ND-5]], a [[BX-series droid commando]], with a restraining bolt forcing him to remain loyal to [[Jaylen Vrax]]. Vess suggested several times to ND-5 that she could use the spike to free him from Vrax's control but ND refused to offer. Later that same year, Vess reunited with Gedeek on [[Akiva]] to recurit him for a [[Heist on Canto Bight|heist]] but before Gedeek could leave he had Vess help him use the spike, which he had renamed to the Freedom Spike, to reprogram the [[Alpha Droid]], a new type of [[Viper probe droid]] he had designed for the Empire. [[Kay Vess' crew]] later completed the heist, only to be betrayed by Jaylon Vrax and taken aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] [[Revelator]]. Unwhiling to leave ND-5 behind, Vess had Gedeek give her the Freedom Spike and went after ND-5 while disquised as a [[stormtrooper]]. After initially failing to use the spike on ND while on the ship's bridge. Vess lured ND to an emitter between the primary and secondary [[power core]]s for the destroyer's deflector shields which briefly stunned him. However ND reactivated before Vess could use the spike. He grabbed the spike and destroyed it however Vess managed to use her blaster to destroy the restraining bolt, ultimately freeing ND.<ref name="Outlaws" />
