'''Force Illusion''' was a [[Force power|Force ability]] that allowed the user to generate illusions.<ref name="SWDSoR">{{Destiny|set=Spirit of Rebellion|cardname=Force Illusion}}</ref> This power was useful in combat, allowing a Force-sensitive individual to make copies of themselves to make it harder to find them or cause their opponent to lose focus by making them see things they feared. When [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] faced the fallen Jedi [[Dagan Gera]] at the Koboh Observatory, both used this ability, with which Kestis destabilized Gera by making him see something he feared: his former friend and colleague, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Santari Khri]], judging him and disapproving of his actions. This allowed Kestis and his [[Bode Akuna|ally]] to kill him.<ref name="Survivor" />
