The '''drop-seat bay''' was the special bay on a [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transport]] for deploying troops.<ref name="Gauntlet Fighter">{{FFGXW2|set=Gauntlet Fighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Drop-Seat Bay}}</ref> The bay contained a rack that seated up to twenty [[Mandalorian]] warriors who would deploy from the bay doors that opened on the ventral surface of the ''Kom'rk''-class fighter.<ref name="The Pirate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 21: The Pirate}}</ref>
