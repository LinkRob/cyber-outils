The '''colo claw fish''', also spelled '''colo clawfish''', was a large and nightmarish [[Eel|creature]] found in the depths of [[Naboo]]'s seas, as well as the [[Cordaxian Sea]]. The colo had a flat eel-like body, a bio-luminescent tail, a [[crocodile]]-like head, a row of sharp teeth, and a set of mandibles. They had a set of venomous fangs that they used to stun their prey. Their digestive system worked slowly, so they were at risk of being consumed from the inside out if they didn't kill their prey before eating it.<ref name="AA">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> A pair of large claws were located just behind the fish's head and were used to force [[Foodstuff|food]] into the creature's large mouth. The bioluminescent skin of the colo claw fish lured in prey.<ref name="VE" />

While trying to reach [[Theed]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Jar Jar Binks]] were attacked by a colo fish. Before this colo could even touch the group's [[Tribubble bongo|bongo]], it was attacked and devoured by an even larger [[sando aqua monster]].

==Biology and appearance==
[[File:BabyColoClawFish-SWCT.png|thumb|left|250px|An infant colo claw fish]]
The colo claw fish had a flat [[eel]]-like body,<ref name="AA" />, [[Bioluminescence|bioluminescent]] skin,<ref name="VE" />, a bioluminescent tail, a [[crocodile]]-like head, a row of sharp teeth, a set of mandibles, and a set of venomous [[Tooth|fangs]]. They possessed a digestive system that worked slowly.<ref name="AA" /> They had a serpentine and spine-studded body. They were able to emit a hydrosonic shriek that could disorient its prey.<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> They could swallow prey larger than its own head by unhinging its [[Mouth|jaw]], and it could expand its [[stomach]].<ref name="Absolutely">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

Colo claw fish were considered to taste good, and they were edible by [[Sullustan]]s and [[human]]s.<ref name="The Snare">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Snare]]''</ref> Their [[Colo claw roe|eggs]] were also a delicacy enjoyed by wealthy individuals such as [[Dryden Vos]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behavior==
[[File:ColoClawFish-SWE.png|thumb|right|300px|An adult colo claw fish]]
Colo claw fish hid in tunnels along [[Naboo]]'s [[ocean]]ic floor where they can lay still for [[Standard hour|hours]] waiting for prey,<ref name="Ultimate" /> such as young [[opee sea killer]]s, [[scalefish]], and large [[fish]].<ref name="Absolutely" /> Before attacking, it disorients its prey with a hydrosonic shriek. The colo then stuns the prey with its venomous fangs and distends its jaw to swallow the victim. If the victim is not rendered unconscious prior to swallowing, it could attempt to chew its way out of the colo's stomach.<ref name="Ultimate" /> Colo claw fish were eaten by [[sando aqua monster]]s.<ref name="Absolutely" />

==History==
Colo claw fish were native to the [[planet]]s Naboo<ref name="Episode I" /> and [[Odona]].<ref name="Annual 3">[[Star Wars Annual (2015) 3|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 3]]</ref> In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Star Wars: Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Jar Jar Binks]] used a [[Tribubble bongo|Gungan bongo submarine]] to travel through [[Naboo Abyss|Naboo's core]] to get to [[Theed]]. Their bongo was attacked by a colo claw fish, which was then attacked and devoured by a sando aqua monster.<ref name="Episode I" />

==Behind the scenes==
Colo claw fish first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''Star Wars'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Episode I" />

Early designs for the colo claw fish were based on an [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earwig earwig]. Features from a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crocodile crocodile] and a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moray_eel moray eel] were later added to the design.<ref name="Absolutely" />
