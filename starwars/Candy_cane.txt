A '''candy cane''' was a type of red and white striped [[candy]]. [[R2-H25]] was a [[R2-series astromech droid]] with plating resembling a candy cane and had a candy cane on top of it's dome.<ref name="SW Collector">{{WebCite|url=https://starwarscollector.com/2025/09/29/disney-press-release-2025-droid-factory-r2-h25-holiday-droid-at-disney-store-10-6-25/|title=Disney Press Release – 2025 Droid Factory R2-H25 Holiday Droid At Disney Store 10/6/25|date=2025-09-29|archivedate=20250930193326}}</ref> A [[gingerbread]] version of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] once also owned a large candy cane and two candy canes were also part of his [[Gingerbread AT-AT Walker]], where they were located in the place of it's<ref name="Gingerbread AT-AT">{{LEGOCite|num=40806|name=Gingerbread AT-AT Walker}}</ref> [[R-90C medium blaster]]s.<ref name="ATACT">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: AT-ACT Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref>
