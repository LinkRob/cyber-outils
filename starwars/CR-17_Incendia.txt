The '''CR-17 Incendia''' was a [[podracer]] produced by [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[CR-17 Incendia III|third model]] of the vehicle was released. The [[Zalpertine Cup]] was a [[Podracing]] series on [[Korrilan IV]] that strictly catered to the Incendia model racer.<ref name="EV" />

==Description==
The CR-17 Incendia Podracers were extremely fast, while incorporating safety features and cosmetic modifications. While other Podracers were built to demonstrate advanced technology or trick fabrication, the CR-17s were better used to demonstrate piloting skill.<ref name="EV" />
