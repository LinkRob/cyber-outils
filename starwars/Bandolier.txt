[[File:CustomBandolier-Convergence.png|thumb|right|300px|A custom Wookiee bandolier]]
A '''bandolier''' was a strap of [[clothing]] worn across the torso from the waist to the shoulder. It was often used for carrying equipment or ammunition which could be attached onto the strap for easy access. Many [[Wookiee]]s, including the [[smuggler]] [[Chewbacca]], wore nothing but a bandolier.<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
