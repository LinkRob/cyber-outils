The '''F3-Vara snubfighter''', also called '''''Cumulus''-class snub fighter,''' was a type of [[snub-fighter]] that was used by [[pirate]]s during the [[New Republic Era]]. A group of pirates, including [[Vane]], attempted to shoot down the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] using these fighters at the [[Nevarro system asteroid fields|asteroid fields]] at the edge of [[Nevarro system|Nevarro's system]]. Djarin destroyed several of their fighters before escaping.<ref name="Chapter 17" />

After [[Pirate King]] [[Gorian Shard]] attacked [[Nevarro]], a [[Children of the Watch|group]] of Mandalorians came to aid the victims of the [[Pirate siege on Nevarro|pirate attack]]. Shard ordered his snub-fighters to be dispatched to pursue and destroy Djarin, who was participating in the fight. By the end of the battle, all the snub-fighters had been destroyed, besides the one piloted by Vane, who fled the scene in his ship.<ref name="Chapter 21">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 21: The Pirate}}</ref>

The [[Quarren]] pirate [[Glerb]] flew a Pirate Snub fighter into the [[barrier]] but the barrier's lightning destroyed the snub-fighter.<ref name="So Much Trouble" />

==Behind the scenes==
The [[2025]] [[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'']] incorrectly identified the snubfighter as the "''Cumulus''-class," a designation that was already used for the [[Cumulus-class corsair|''Cumulus''-class Corsair]].<ref name="EOSAOV2" /> The [[2026]] ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]'' provided the corrected name of F3-Vara snubfighter for the ship.<ref name="MandoVG" />
