'''Bolt''' was the nickname given to the [[human]] because he could quickly deflect the slaps. He was a citizen of [[Bespin]]. When the [[Galactic Empire]] took over the [[planet]], he escaped with [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] as a [[technician]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
