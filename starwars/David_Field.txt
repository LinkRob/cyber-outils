'''David Field''' is an actor who portrayed a [[stormtrooper]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]<ref name="London Film and Comic Con">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-at-london-film-and-comic-con|text=''Star Wars'' at London Film and Comic Con}}</ref> and a [[Imperial Navy trooper|bunker control room operator]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="Making of" /> He was one of the stormtrooper performers who was a guest at the [[2016]] London Film and Comic Con.<ref name="London Film and Comic Con" />
