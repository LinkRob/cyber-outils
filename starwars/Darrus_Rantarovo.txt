'''Darrus Rantarovo''' was an [[Atterran]] [[Gender|male]] who served as [[foreman]] of the [[Big Settlement hydro farm]] on the [[planet]] [[Atterra Bravo]] until the planet's [[hydro farm]]s were taken over by the [[First Order]]. He was also a member of the [[Bravo Rising]] resistance team against the First Order<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[The Last Jedi: Cobalt Squadron]]'' takes place simultaneous with [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], culminating with the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates these events to [[34 ABY]].</ref> When [[Reeve Panzoro]] brought [[Rose Tico|Rose]] and [[Paige Tico]] of the [[Resistance]] to meet the members of Bravo Rising, Rantarovo shook the [[Tico family|Tico]] sisters' hands, but was too overcome by emotion to speak.<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
Darrus Rantarovo first appeared in the [[2017]] novel ''[[The Last Jedi: Cobalt Squadron]]'' written by [[Elizabeth Wein]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" />
