'''Eunice Ino''' was an [[Ugnaught]] who used to run a [[Eunice Ino's chop shop|chop shop]] in a asteroid hangar and was paid by [[Draiven Bosh]] to break open a stolen [[XD-7 speedster]].<ref name="Starship Show" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Eunice Ino first appeared in "[[The Starship Show]]," the first part of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]]'s [[The Starship Show / Nash's Super Busy Day|twenty-fourth episode]] of the [[Disney+]] and [[Disney Junior]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'',<ref name="Starship Show" /> which aired on [[February 14]], [[2024]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/young-jedi-adventures-new-episodes-feb-2024|text=''Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures'' Returns with New Episodes}}</ref> The character was initially designed by [[India Boeckh]] before getting redesigns by [[Chris Blackstock]].<ref name="Blackstock Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:ChrisBlackstock-EuniceInoDesigner-Email.png|creator=Chris Blackstock|date=2025-06-11}}</ref> In the animated series, Ino was voiced by [[Shelby Young]].<ref name="Starship Show" />
