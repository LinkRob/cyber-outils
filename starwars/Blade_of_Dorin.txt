The '''''Blade of Dorin''''' was a [[Color|blue]] [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Delta-7B ''Aethersprite''-class light interceptor]] flown by [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]] during the [[Clone Wars]] in service of the [[Galactic Republic]], alongside his [[astromech droid]] [[R7-D4]].

==History==
The ''Blade of Dorin'' was gifted to [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]] by the [[Jedi]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Malevolence Campaign|''Malevolence'' crisis]].<ref name="EpGuide201">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode201.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Holocron Heist}}</ref> Skywalker's [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] suspected Koon would be joining their [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula|mission]] to destroy the ''[[Malevolence]]'' and prepped his [[starfighter]] preemptively. As [[Shadow Squadron]] performed their call ins, Koon joined the [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber|BTL-B Y-wing]] formation aboard the ''Blade of Dorin'', with a [[Syliure-31 hyperspace docking ring|hyperspace docking ring]] attached. The two Jedi flew as [[Starfighter|fighter]] escort alongside [[Shadow Squadron]] to reach the [[Kaliida Shoals Medical Center]] before the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[warship]] ''[[Malevolence]]''.<ref name="Shadow" />

[[File:PloKoonDelta7B-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Plo Koon and his forces broke the blockade of Felucia.]]
In the [[First Battle of Felucia]], Koon led a group of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|gunships]] that were deployed from a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] to evacuate the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] troops on [[Felucia]]. He ordered his wingman [[Warthog]] to protect the [[gunship]]s while he destroyed the Separatist [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]]. They made it past the blockade and evacuated the ground troops.<ref name="Holocron">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref>

Prior to the [[Battle of Lola Sayu|mission to the Citadel]], Koon worked on his starfighter in a hanger in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]]. There he was approached by Tano, who was upset at being left behind by her Master who insisted the forthcoming mission was too dangerous. As Koon reached behind his fighter's seat, he told her that it was Skywalker's choice, looking up when Tano replied that when and how she put her life in danger should be her own decision. When the team, which was frozen in [[carbonite]] to fool the Separatist's [[life-form scanner]]s, was thawed on [[Lola Sayu]], they found Tano with them, who then deliberately misconstrued her conversation with Koon as orders to join the mission.<ref name="The Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel}}</ref>

[[File:PloKoonLeadingHisBros-EscapeFromKadavo.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Plo Koon lead his forces as Wolf Leader at Kadavo.]]
During the [[Battle of Kadavo]], Koon and several [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s arrived as reinforcements on [[Kadavo]] to assist the Jedi fighting against the [[Zygerrian]]s. Koon flew the ''Blade of Dorin'', leading the forces under the callsign [[Wolf Leader]]. When two [[HH-87 Starhopper]]s were pursuing him, Koon told his [[astromech droid]] [[R7-D4]] to cut the [[engine]]s on the starfighter, after which he fell back behind the enemy fighters and shot them down. He then tried to destroy the Zygerrians' [[turret]]s, so the Republic [[cruiser]] could move in for rescue. Their [[Deflector shield|shields]] prevented this, however, so Anakin Skywalker had to destroy them from the ground.<ref name="Kadavo">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref>
