'''Chandrila''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Core Worlds]] of [[the galaxy]]. The [[Gender|female]] [[human]] [[Mon Mothma]] hailed from Chandrila, and [[Senator|represented]] her [[homeworld]] in the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was also the homeworld and birthplace of [[Ben Solo]].

Chandrila was one of the planets targeted for destruction by [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], who planned to use the [[Battle station|station's]] planet-shattering [[superweapon]] to end the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] through sheer terror.

Following the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], Chandrila became the [[capital]] of the [[New Republic]] and the seat of the newly formed [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] as a tribute to the Republic of old. It was not permanent, however, as the Republic chose to rotate its capital world based on elections.

==Description==
Chandrila was a small [[Color|blue]]-green [[planet]] with calm [[Ocean|seas]] and rolling hills. It had mild seasonal weather and rarely experienced dangerous weather conditions.<ref name="Life Debt" />

The planet's [[capital]] [[city]] was [[Hanna City]] which hosted the [[Chandrila Senate house]]; the site of the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] in the [[Standard month|months]] following the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> Other known locations on Chandrila included the [[Old Gather-House]], the [[Senate Plaza (Chandrila)|Senate Plaza]],<ref name="Life Debt" /> the [[Mothma Estate]],<ref name="OYL" /> and the [[Silver Sea]]. The settlement [[Junari Point]] was located a few [[Kilometer|klicks]] outside Hanna City and hosted a [[Unidentified bar (Junari Point)|bar]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

==History==
===Pre-Republic===
[[25,000 BBY|Twenty-five thousand]] years before the [[Battle of Yavin]], the [[Rakatan]] species [[Sacking of Chandrila|attacked]] the planet, plundering a set of twelve [[Chandi Merle]] temple figures from Chandrila.<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref>

===During the Republic===
{{Quote|Even the Trade Federation steps carefully around Chandrila.|[[Bail Prestor Organa]]|<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>}}
In ancient [[time]]s, Chandrila was settled by settlers who built the Old Gather House.<ref name="Life Debt" /> Chandrila was a member of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and was one of the [[Core Founder]]s.<ref name="HC9">{{HelmetCollectionCite|9|Databank A-Z}}</ref> During the late [[Republic Era]], [[Mon Mothma]] served as Chandrila's [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> The [[Trade Federation]] [[megacorporation]] made a point to not anger Chandrila, which granted Mothma a degree of leverage over the corporation.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

Early into Mothma's career, a [[Succession crisis on Chandrila|succession crisis]] occurred on Chandrila, which forced Mothma to leave a goodwill historical tour of the fossilized [[Ecumenopolis|city planet]] [[Eyo-Dajuritz]] early, much to her upset.<ref name="MaskofFear">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> The [[Jedi Order]] was also involved in a [[diplomatic mission to Chandrila]]<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> in the years before [[22 BBY]].<ref name="DateMission">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that [[Josk Nivar]]'s [[diplomatic mission to Chandrila]] took place over several [[Standard year|years]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> An [[attack on Hanna City]] occurred early in the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="MaskofFear" />

===Age of the Empire===
During the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]], Chandrila was listed as an [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] [[safe world]]. A map of [[the galaxy]] with a legend listing the various Alliance safe worlds, [[starfighter]] hubs at level five or higher, rebel operations sectors and regional headquarters, and shadow planets with deep space caches, included Chandrila. The map was later included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Chandrila supported the Alliance.<ref name="Moving Target" />

By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Chronology Note">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text="Narkina 5" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> Chandrila had become one of the thousands of worlds that suffered under Imperial rule. Mothma, by that [[time]] the [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|chancellor]] of the Alliance, recognized her homeworld's suffering but believed she could not let her personal biases get in the way of the Rebellion's decision making; with thousands of worlds suffering under Imperial rule, she could not simply decide to point their resources at helping the one planet.<ref name="INR">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref> Due to Chandrila's support for the Rebellion, the planet was a target of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="Moving Target" /> [[Olia Choko]] regarded Chandrila as a small planet that was the origin point of big ideas.<ref name="Aftermath" />

===New Republic Era===
[[File:ChandrilaCoastSenate-GhostsOfVadersCastle2.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Hanna City was the capital of Chandrila and at one point hosted the New Republic Senate.]]
Following the Battle of [[Endor]] in [[4 ABY]], Chandrila became the capital of the restored [[Galactic Republic]], the New Republic, the successor government to the Rebel Alliance. The reestablished Galactic Senate was based in Hanna City. [[Queen of the Core Network]] began broadcasting on Chandrila. Imperial [[prisoner]]s from war zones like [[Coruscant]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> and [[Akiva]] were [[Prison|imprisoned]] on Chandrila. [[New Republic Defense Fleet Starfighter Corps|New Republic Starfighter Corps]] [[pilot]]s were trained at [[Landing Platform OB-99]] near the Silver Sea.<ref name="Life Debt" /> At one point, the New Republic's [[Vanguard Squadron]] was tasked with assisting in the defense of key worlds, including Chandrila.<ref name="Vanguard Databank">{{Databank|vanguard-squadron|Vanguard Squadron}}</ref>

After [[Han Solo]] and [[Norra Wexley]]'s [[Norra Wexley's team|team]] freed a hundred Rebel prisoners from [[Ashmead's Lock]] on [[Kashyyyk]], the former prisoners were flown to Chandrila where they were accommodated in temporary housing and received medical treatment. Later, the Senate accepted [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]]'s offer to conduct [[Imperial–Republic peace talks|peace talks]] near Hanna City. In secret, this was a pretext for [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] to launch his attack on the New Republic leadership using the former prisoners as unwilling [[assassin]]s controlled with [[Behavioral modification biochip|inorganic bio-chips]] installed in their [[brain]]s. The resulting [[Liberation Day (New Republic)|Liberation Day]] [[Attack on Chandrila|attacks]] were broadcast live on [[HoloNet]] to audiences across the [[galaxy]]. This had the result of undermining morale in the New Republic and setting the stage for the [[Battle of Jakku]]. The attack also led to calls to relocate the New Republic Senate to a more fortified world.<ref name="Life Debt" /> Chandrila was also [[Attack on Chandrila (Operation: Cinder)|attacked]] during [[Operation: Cinder]].<ref name="Palpatine">{{EncyclopediaCite|Emperor Palpatine}}</ref>

In the final months of the Galactic Civil War<ref name="Empire's End" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Life Debt Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the Republic began the process of temporarily relocating the Senate to the [[Expansion Region]] world [[Nakadia]] after an Imperial [[Attack on Chandrila|attack]] on the capital. However, [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] Mothma and several senators, including [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Tolwar Wartol]] still maintained offices, on Chandrila. During the Battle of [[Jakku]], Wartol tried to [[Assassination attempt on Mon Mothma|assassinate]] Mothma by bombing her office. However, Mothma was out of her office but her [[Advisor|Adviser]] [[Auxi Kray Korbin]] was [[Death|killed]]. Shortly later, Mothma, Organa, [[Sondiv Sella]], and [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] met with [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] to sign the [[Galactic Concordance]]; which facilitated the Empire's [[Galactic Concordance|surrender]] to the New Republic. The signing ceremony took place at the [[crystal]] cliffs north of Hanna City. On the same [[Standard day|day]], Organa gave [[Reproduction|birth]] to her son, [[Ben Solo]]. The Imperial capitulation was followed by a [[Standard week|week]]&ndash;long [[Festival of Liberation]].<ref name="Empire's End" />

By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mando Date">{{MandoDate|12}}</ref> Chandrila retained it's role as the New Republic's capital.<ref name="Chapter 12">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref> However, the capital would relocate to [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Insider 228" />

In [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[dream]]<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="TLJ EE Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> on [[Ahch-To]], in which he imagined how things were if he had not joined the Rebel Alliance and instead stayed on [[Tatooine]] and [[Marriage|married]] [[Camie Marstrap]], the Empire used the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] to not only destroy [[Alderaan]] but also [[Mon Cala]] and Chandrila.<ref name="Expanded Edition" />

==Inhabitants==
[[Chandrilan]]s were regarded as peaceful but slightly haughty, pedantic, and interested in [[politics]]. Following the [[Imperial Era]], some Chandrilans disliked former Imperials.<ref name="Life Debt" />

==Behind the scenes==
In [[canon]], Chandrila first appeared as the New Republic capital in [[Chuck Wendig]]'s [[2015]] novel [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]. In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' legends]] Chandrila was first mentioned in the [[1987]] roleplaying supplement ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''.
