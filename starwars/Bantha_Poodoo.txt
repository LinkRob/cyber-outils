'''''Bantha Poodoo''''' was a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Low Altitude Assault Transport]] which was stationed in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] at the time [[Jedi Master]] [[Plo Koon]] watched an infiltration party depart for [[Battle of Lola Sayu|their mission]] to rescue Master [[Even Piell]] from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[prison]] known as the [[Citadel]]. The [[gunship]] had customized nose art of a winged [[bantha]] dropping a pair of bombs.<ref name="Citadel" />
