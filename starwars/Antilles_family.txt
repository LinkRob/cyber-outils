The '''Antilles family''' was a family of [[human]]s that served prominently in the military of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], [[New Republic]] and the [[Resistance]]. The family consisted of [[Wedge Antilles]], his [[Wedge Antilles' aunt|aunt]], his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Norra Wexley]], and his step-son [[Temmin Wexley]].

{{Family-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
When asked by [[Dan Madsen]] of ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' in [[1994]] if there could be a part for him in the then-fortchoming ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]], [[Wedge Antilles]] actor [[Denis Lawson]] jokingly suggested he could play his character's grandfather.<ref name="SWI23">{{InsiderCite|23|Rebel Leader... Denis Lawson}}</ref>

Concept art produced by [[Sang Jun Lee]] during production of the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] featured Wedge Antilles' father.<ref name="TAoRotS">{{InsiderCite|85|The Art of Revenge of the Sith|The Art of ''Revenge of the Sith'', Part Three}}</ref> However, the character's inclusion was dropped for the finished film.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>
