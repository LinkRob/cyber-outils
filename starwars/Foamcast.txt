'''Foamcast''' was the term commonly used to describe a type of quick-setting aerosol foam that was used in construction and building maintenance. Designed to be sprayed into cracks, dents, holes or other spaces, the foam expanded and quickly hardened upon application, becoming indistinguishable from its surroundings once dry and possessing the strength and wearability of [[plastcrete]]. For 25 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the foam could be purchased in a tall can equipped with a trigger activated applicator and a long narrow tube that allowed for precision application.<ref name="FO" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], foamcast was mentioned in the [[2016]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was released by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] as part of [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']].<ref name="Lead by Example">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> The spray originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[2004]] novel ''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]'', which was written by [[Michael Reaves]] and [[Steve Perry]].<ref name="MI:BS">''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]''</ref>
