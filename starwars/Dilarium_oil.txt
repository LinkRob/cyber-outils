'''Dilarium oil''' was a type of [[Color|black]] [[oil]] that was one of the main exports of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Outlaw" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> It had a low viscosity and was valued at 280 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] per unit.<ref name="Outlaw" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'' to [[5 ABY]].</ref> the [[crime lord]] [[Lorgan Movellan]] told the [[sheriff]] [[Cobb Vanth]] about the [[Hutt Clan]]'s varying interests in Tatooine. Movellan noted that despite the resources he considered valuable on the planet, such as dilarium oil and [[silicax oxalate]], the Hutts' intrigue was primarily focused on the world's inhabitants.<ref name="AEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dilarium oil was mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', a [[2017]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="AEE" /> The oil first appeared as an item in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. In the game, the oil could only be sold for credits to vendors, and did not have another function.<ref name="Outlaw" />
