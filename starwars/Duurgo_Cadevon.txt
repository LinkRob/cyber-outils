'''Duurgo Cadevon''' was a [[Rodian]] [[Gender|male]] and a member of the criminal [[Cadevon Family]]. He and his brother [[Boorgo Cadevon|Boorgo]] crushed the [[Limb|leg]] of [[Riley]], a young orphan turned [[smuggler]] from [[Burnin Konn]], after she disagreed over payment while working for them. His preferred weapon of choice was a [[Blaster rifle]]. The Cadevon Brothers were both [[Death|killed]] during the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Iron Blockade]] after a confrontation with Riley's [[The Smuggler|sibling]].<ref name="SWU" />
