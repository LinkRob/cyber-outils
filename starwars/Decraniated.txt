The '''Decraniated''' were an order of servants produced by a fugitive surgeon [[Cornelius Evazan]], initially for [[Dryden Vos]]. Several Decraniated served aboard Vos's yacht ''[[First Light]]''. Members of the Decraniated were also seen in [[Jedha City]] when the [[Partisans]] attacked an Imperial convoy, before the [[city]] was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].

==Description==
The Decraniated were originally created by [[Cornelius Evazan]] as a money making scheme for his superior at the time, [[Dryden Vos]].<ref name="Scum and Villany" /> Made to be as subservient as droids, Evazan transformed his victims with [[Neuro-box interface|cybernetic technology]] to strip them of their identities. He then sold them unwillingly as servants,<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> putting them on catalogs.<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 18]]</ref> On [[Jedha]], Evazan took advantage of the local [[Jedha insurgency|insurgency strikes]] to abduct the casualties and convert them as part of the Decraniated.<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" />

==History==
{{Quote|Evazan built a host of mutilated cyberslaves. The first of the Decraniated.|[[Tam Posla]]|<ref name="Aphra 18" />}}
[[File:Evazan_Decraniation_Operation.png|thumb|left|250px|Evazan working on one of the Decraniated]]
By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Created date" /> [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] began creating the Decraniated under the employment of Dryden Vos, the second in command of [[Crimson Dawn]]. Evazan saw it as a money making scheme for his superior.<ref name="Scum and Villany" /> Some of these Decraniated came to serve on Vos' personal [[yacht]], the ''[[First Light]]'', as servants.<ref name="Solo" /> [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] once saw them in a catalog.<ref name="Aphra 18" />

Some time after, Evazan was on [[Jedha]], where he pieced together [[Caysin Bog]], a victim of an insurgency strike,<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> as a prototype for the Decraniated.<ref name="Aphra 18" /> Evazan transformed injured victims of the [[Jedha insurgency|local insurgency]] with [[Neuro-box interface|cybernetic technology]] to strip them of their identities before selling them into unwilling servitude.<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> Two members of the Decraniated were briefly seen in a café in [[Jedha City]], standing by group of Imperial officers, at the moment the [[Partisans]] attacked an Imperial convoy, before the [[city]] was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by a single-[[reactor]] [[superlaser]] blast from the newly-operational [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

Following the destruction of Jedha city, some Decraniated remained on Jedha, despite Evazan leaving, and served what remained of the Partisans during their [[Battle for Jedha|campaign]] to thwart the Imperial mining operations there.<ref name="Star Wars 38">[[Star Wars (2015) 38|''Star Wars'' (2015) 38]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
A character with the cybernetic modifications of the Decraniated first appeared in concept art of the bar in [[Takodana Castle]] created by [[Glyn Dillon]] during pre-production of [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] in [[November]] [[2013]], although the idea did not end up being used for that film.<ref name="Art">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
