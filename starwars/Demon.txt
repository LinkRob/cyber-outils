A '''demon''',<ref name="Disappeared, Part I">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part I}}</ref> '''demoness''',<ref name="Star Wars 7">[[Star Wars (2025) 7|''Star Wars'' (2025) 7]]</ref> or '''devil''',<ref name="Master of Evil">''[[Master of Evil]]''</ref> was, in mythology, an evil supernatural being. In the [[Bardottan]] faith, there was a demon called [[Malmourral]], whom the [[Frangawl Cult]] worshiped as a [[Deity|god]].<ref name="Disappeared, Part I">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part I}}</ref> In certain mythologies, demons were said to have [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s. When someone wrestled with a temptation, it was said metaphorically that he or she had an [[Diathim|angel]] and a demon on his or her shoulders.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> [[Gender|Male]] [[Devaronian]]s were thought to look like devils.<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> '''The devil''' was an individual demon used in the phrase "speak of the devil," which referred to something happening right after one considered it. [[Commodore]] [[Karyn Faro]] thought the phrase when, after pondering if she had unwittingly done something to annoy the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] appeared behind her.<ref name="Thrawn: Treason">''[[Thrawn: Treason]]''</ref>

By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">The [[Battle of Exegol]] took place in 35 ABY according to {{StoryCite|story=A Eulogy for Snap|book=Star Wars (2020) 25}}. The project began shortly thereafter.</ref> [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] compared [[Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]'s malevolence to that of a demon in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', stating that Palpatine being a mere [[human]] despite said malice was more frightening than otherwise.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
