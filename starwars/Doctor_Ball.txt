"'''Doctor Ball'''" was an [[IT-O interrogation droid|IT-O Interrogation Unit]] in service to the [[Galactic Empire]] before the start of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] used Doctor Ball to inject [[truth serum]] into the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] aboard Tarkin's [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Sovereign]]''. The Imperials hoped to procure information from Jarrus, but the Jedi was able to resist giving away any information under Ball's injection. Having grown tired of Ball's inability to break Jarrus, Tarkin had [[the Grand Inquisitor]] take over the interrogation.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />
