The ''Bantha song''' was a song sung by the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Lys Solay]] and [[Kai Brightstar]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Solay came up with the song prior to a mission to rescue lost [[bantha]] cubs belonging to the rancher [[Roon (individual)|Roon]]. Following a mission mix-up which ended with [[Kai Brightstar]] and [[Nubs]] being sent to retrieve the banthas, Brightstar used the song to convince a lost bantha cub to return with them to its owner Roon.<ref name="The Mission Mixup">{{YJA|The Mission Mixup}}</ref>
