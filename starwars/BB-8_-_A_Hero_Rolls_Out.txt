"'''BB-8 - A Hero Rolls Out'''" is the forty-first episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com|''Star Wars Kids'']] YouTube channel on [[March 27]], [[2020]].<ref name="BB-8" />

{{Media-stub}}

==Plot summary==
The video opens with [[BB-8]] dodging laser fire at [[Niima Outpost]] on [[Jakku]]. The narrator explains that in a "[[The galaxy|galaxy of adventures]]," BB-8 rolls into battle. BB-8 knocks down two [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] before escaping with [[Finn]] and [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. After Rey fires off the ship's engines, BB-8 clings to safety by using his grappling cable.

The video then jumps to BB-8 flying with [[Poe Dameron]] in his [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] ''[[Black One]]'' during the [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] on the [[Resistance navy]]. After the fighter is hit, Poe tasks BB-8 with repairing the ship's weapon system. The dutiful BB-8 descends down the [[astromech socket]], repairing the targeting system just in time for Poe to shoot down a [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]].

Later, BB-8 fires coins at officers from the [[Canto Bight Police Department]]. He then comes to Finn and [[Rose Tico]]'s rescue aboard the blazing hangar of the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''. BB-8 hijacks a [[First Order All Terrain Scout Transport]] and shoots down several stormtroopers. Finn and Rose ride with BB-8 in the walker. Finn gives BB-8 a thumbs up and the droid returns the favor with his [[spark projector]].
