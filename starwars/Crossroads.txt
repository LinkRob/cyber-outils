"'''Crossroads'''" is a short story written by [[Christopher Cerasi]], that was originally published on [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]. It was released on [[August 22]], [[2008]]. On October 1, [[2012]], it was republished on [[Unbound Worlds]], a subsidiary website of [[Random House]] publishing. The story takes place three [[Standard month/Legends|months]] before ''[[The Courtship of Princess Leia]]''.

==Plot summary==
In [[8 ABY/Legends|8 ABY]], [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]] visited [[Ta'a Chume]] on [[Hapes/Legends|Hapes]] in a bid to forge an alliance between the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] and the [[Hapan]]s. As Leia returns from an extensive [[Diplomat/Legends|diplomatic]] mission to the normally reclusive [[Hapes Cluster/Legends|Hapes Cluster]], she reflects on how busy her life is and how she has had to continually set aside personal concerns for the good of the New Republic.
