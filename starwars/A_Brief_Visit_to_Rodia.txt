"'''A Brief Visit to Rodia'''" is the introductory narrative to the [[September]] [[1996]] ''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'', published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The short story tells of how [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]] [[Shella Rinou]] and [[Kelric (Herglic)|Kelric]] sneak onto the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Rodia/Legends|Rodia]] in their [[Starship/Legends|starship]], the ''[[Listing Dewback]]''.
