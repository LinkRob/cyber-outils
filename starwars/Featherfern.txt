The '''featherfern''' was a [[Fern (plant)|fern]] [[Homeworld|native]] to several [[mesa]]s of the [[grass]]y<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Bogano DB">{{Databank|bogano|Bogano}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Bogano]]. The fern had leaves that were bigger at the base and decreased in size up the fern's single stem. It also had [[Color|purple]] [[flower]]s on the uppermost tip of the stem.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] traveled to the [[Fractured Plain]] on Bogano, where he harvested a featherfern [[seed]]. He grew the seed in a [[terrarium]] owned by his companion [[Greez Dritus]] aboard the [[luxury yacht]] ''[[Stinger Mantis]]''. Kestis' collection of various seeds from various planets was done as a form of payment for Dritus' help during the [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Mantis'' crew's]] [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /><ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /><ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="SurvivorDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> after the crew split up, Dritus settled in a [[Pyloon's Saloon|cantina]] on the planet [[Koboh]], and replanted the Featherfern in a pot within the cantina's basement.<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
