'''Ergaugh''' was a [[Tholothian]] [[technician]] who served the [[Nihil]] on the [[space station]] [[Lightning Crash]] which was situated in the [[Occlusion Zone]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Ergaugh was one of the many workers who helped the Nihil with the [[stormseed]]s, devices that implemented the [[Stormwall]] which, when inside, made up the Occlusion Zone. After the [[human]]s [[Xylan Graf]] and [[Avon Sunvale]] landed on the Lightning Crash, Graf conversed with Ergaugh before making his way to his [[Xylan's quarters|quarters]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
