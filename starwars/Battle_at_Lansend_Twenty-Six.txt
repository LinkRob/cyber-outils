After an [[Attack on the Sempre|attack]] on the [[transport]] ''[[Sempre]]'', insurgents and freed [[Wookiee]] [[Slavery|slaves]] attacked the [[space station]] [[Lansend Twenty-Six]] in an attempt to liberate their companions. With their communications jammed, Lansend Twenty-Six could not call on the nearby [[Baklek Base]] for support, but were aided by the ''[[Chimaera]]'' under the command of [[Commodore]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], who had anticipated the attack. The forces were defeated soon after.

==Prelude==
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Xenophobia|xenophobic]] [[Galactic Empire]] exploited the [[Wookiee]] species as an expendable [[Slavery|slave]] labor force; regarding them as beasts.<ref name="SWRTVG">''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> The Empire exploited the Wookiees because their large, robust physiology allowed them to work in harsh and dangerous conditions.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Enslaved Wookiees were used to harvest lumber, work in mines, build Imperial weapons such as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], and as test subjects in medical and weapons experiments.<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> In addition, the Empire turned the Wookiee homeworld of [[Kashyyyk]] into a giant slave labor camp and renamed it [[Imperial territory G5-623]].<ref name="Life Debt" />

The [[pirate]] and insurgent leader [[Nevil Cygni|Nightswan]] waged an [[Nightswan's campaign|insurgent campaign]] against the Empire. He and his followers engaged in various activities including smuggling [[doonium]], [[tibanna]], and [[Iridium (element)|iridium]] to raise funds for people hurt by the Empire. Eight [[Standard week|weeks]] before the [[Attack on the Sempre|attack]] on the [[troop carrier]] ''[[Sempre]]'', Nightswan's followers stole the [[frigate]] ''[[Castilus]]'' and two squadrons of [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s. They ambushed the ''Sempre'', freed the enslaved Wookiees, and killed the Imperial crew and [[stormtrooper]] contingent aboard.<ref name="Thrawn" />

The [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] ''[[Chimaera]]'' under the command of [[Commodore]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] responded to the ''Sempre''{{'s}} distress transmission. While [[stormtrooper]] [[Major]] [[Ayer]] tried to keep the ship's cargo a secret, Thrawn and [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] insisted on inspecting the ''Sempre''. They discovered that the troop carrier was transporting Wookiee slaves. Due to the tribal nature of Wookiee culture, Thrawn realized that the liberated Wookiees would seek vengeance against those who participated in the slaving: the [[space station]] [[Lansend Twenty-Six]], which processed Wookiee slaves exported offworld.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==The battle==
As Thrawn had predicted, the insurgents and liberated Wookiees traveled to Lansend Twenty-Six. Since a saboteur had managed to sabotage the long-range comm and starboard&ndash;side defense system, the insurgents were able to penetrate the space station. The attackers attempted to flank the Imperial defenders using a [[pincer maneuver]]. However, the station's [[Commanding officer|commander]] [[Colonel]] [[Zenoc]] managed to capture the saboteur and fire the station's portside weapons. After contacting [[Baklek Base]] for help, the ''Chimaera'' arrived at Lansend Twenty-Six to find the processing station under attack. After establishing contact with Colonel Zenoc, Commodore Thrawn requested the schematics of her base.<ref name="Thrawn" />

By then, the insurgents had breached the starboard docking hatch and were fighting their way inside. The insurgents' frigate and starfighters also tried to take out the portside defenses so that they could land another boarding party in a pincer movement. Thrawn directed his [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] to engage the enemy V-19s. Commander Vanto then reported that the portside V-19 force had split up with half engaging the TIEs while the other half lay siege to the station. Though the insurgents dispatch starboard V-19s to defend the ''Castilus'', [[Commander]] [[Karyn Faro]] took out the ship's [[hyperdrive]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

After ordering the TIEs to focus on the V-19s, Thrawn asked Colonel Zenoc if there were any slaves in sections A-four, A-five or B-five. Zenoc reluctantly confirmed the presence of non-military personnel in B-five. Thrawn ordered her to evacuate all personnel to B-eight so that Commander Faro's gunners could use the ''Chimaera''{{'s}} turbolasers to target seven points on the station's starboard side. Once Zenoc had complied, the ''Chimaera'' bombarded the sections of the station where the insurgents had boarded, trapping them. Thrawn also took care not to target B-five, which was now isolated from the rest of the station.<ref name="Thrawn" />

With the boarders neutralized, Thrawn then turned his forces' attention to battling the frigate and V-19s. Thrawn then informed Colonel Zenoc that his forces would continue operations until the Baklek reinforcements arrived. By that stage, enemy forces were down to the crippled frigate and three functioning V-19s. Thrawn gave orders for Commander Faro to capture prisoners for interrogation.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:Lansend26Aftermath-Thrawn4.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Thrawn's forces capture the insurgents and Wookiee slaves]]
Following the battle at Lansend Twenty-Six, Imperial forces captured the insurgents and secured the Wookiee slaves. Thrawn's forces also retrieved the ''Sempre'' and returned it to Imperial hands. Thrawn also left the Wookiee slaves on Lansend Twenty-Six, regarding them as Imperial assets who were being returned to their proper owners. When Lieutenant Commander Vanto expressed unease that the Empire was using slaves, Thrawn tried to rationalize slavery and insisted that the Wookiees would be treated like Imperial assets.<ref name="Thrawn" />

Despite the defeat of Nightswan's forces at Lansend Twenty-Six,<ref name="Thrawn" /> other rebel groups such as the [[Spectres]] took an interest in [[Mission to free Wookiee prisoners|liberating]] Wookiee slaves.<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> Later, the former smuggler and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[General]] [[Han Solo]] sought to honor his [[life debt]] to [[Chewbacca]] by freeing Wookiee slaves on Kashyyyk.<ref name="Aftermath" /> After months of trial and failure, Solo and Chewbacca mustered a motley force of former slaves and allies that succeeded in [[Liberation of Kashyyyk|liberating]] Kashyyyk from Imperial rule and exploitation in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Life Debt" />
