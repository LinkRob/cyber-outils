During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Imperial Navy]] [[Captain]] [[Conro]] commanded an '''[[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]]'''. This [[Star Destroyer]] was part of a [[flotilla]] including [[Commodore]] [[Idel]]'s [[Idel's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] and [[Captain]] [[Shan]]'s [[Shan's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] which attempted to recover [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] [[Theft of the Imperialis|stolen]] [[yacht]] ''[[Imperialis]]''. During the pursuit, both Conro and Shan's [[Starship|ships]] locked their [[tractor beam]]s onto the ''Imperialis''. However, [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] drew Conro's tractor beam onto Shan's ship, causing the two vessels to collide. This allowed Calrissian and his [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian's crew|team]] to escape with the ''Imperialis'' into deep space.<ref name="Lando 2" />
