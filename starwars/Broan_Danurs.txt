'''Broan Danurs''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel pilot]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Formerly a [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] [[pilot]] while [[Dantooine base|stationed]] on [[Dantooine]], Danurs was transferred to fly a [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] to fill a vacancy in [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] under the callsign [[Green Ten]]. [[Davish Krail]], another Gold Squadron Y-wing pilot, was a close friend and mentor to Danurs.<ref name="SWROUVG" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BroanDanursHelmet-SW100Objects.png|thumb|right|200px|Broan Danurs's flight helmet]]
''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]'' says he flew an A-wing during his time on Dantooine, which contradicts ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' that says he flew a Y-wing.

Danurs' helmet design resembles that of the helmet worn by [[Davish Krail]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''A New Hope'']].
