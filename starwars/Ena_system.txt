The '''Ena system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Steniplis sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square L-19 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the planet [[Ena]].<ref name="Light in the Darkness" />
