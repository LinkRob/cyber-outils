'''Bac eggs''' were a [[Foodstuff|food]] available to the [[Cloud City]] [[chef]] [[Torro Sbazzle]]. When [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] arrived on [[Bespin]] in [[3 ABY]], Sbazzle simmered a dozen bac eggs with [[muja fruit]], which she planned to make into a [[Moon-shaped dessert tart|dessert tart]].<ref name="But What Does He Eat?" />
