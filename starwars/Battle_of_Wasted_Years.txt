The '''Battle of Wasted Years'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> occurred by [[1032 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Krall]] participated in the battle and emerged victorious. The story of Krall winning the battle was included in the [[Teradine's holojournal|holojournal]] of [[Klias Teradine]], a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] during the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> who was [[Death|killed]]<ref name="MoTP 4">[[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 4|''The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak'' 4]]</ref> in [[241 BBY]]<ref name="Date 241">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]'', including [[Klias Teradine]]'s [[death]], to [[241 BBY]].</ref> by fellow Padawan [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera Yorrick]] after<ref name="MoTP 4" /> he<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> had become possessed by a [[vitalicron]],<ref name="MoTP 3">[[The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak 3|''The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak'' 3]]</ref> though he was later theorized by many Jedi to have been expelled from the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

In [[88 BBY]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the scene in which [[Sifo-Dyas]] and [[Dooku]] sneak into the [[Archive of Forbidden Artifacts]]&mdash;which mentions the Battle of Wasted Years&mdash;to [[88 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Sifo-Dyas]] told fellow Initiate [[Dooku]] about Teradine's [[holojournal]] and its information on [[Darth]] Krall's deeds, including the Battle of Wasted Years. Dooku later told his [[House Serenno|sister]] [[Jenza]] about their conversation in a [[holo-letter]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="Date 23">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave [[Datacard|data disks]] containing his messages to Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]], [[Asajj Ventress]], for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into his [[Gender|sister's]] location, and she listened to the message that mentioned the battle.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Wasted Years was first mentioned in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-script-by-cavan-scott/9780593157664|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The capitalization of its name was later confirmed with the release of the [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script]]<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin" />
