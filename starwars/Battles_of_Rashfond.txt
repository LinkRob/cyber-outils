The '''Battles of Rashfond''' were a series of battles that were fought by the [[Jedi Order]] at [[Rashfond system|Rashfond]], sometime prior to the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="ATOS" /> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">{{TCWcanondates|507}}</ref> during the Clone Wars, the [[architect droid]] [[Teacher|professor]] [[Huyang]] recalled the battles while listing conflicts the [[Jedi]] had been involved in to a [[Unidentified Clan (The Gathering)|clan]] of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] he was instructing on [[lightsaber]] construction, aboard the [[Paladin-class corvette|Jedi training vessel]] ''[[Crucible]]''.<ref name="ATOS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Battles of Rashfond were mentioned in "[[A Test of Strength]],"<ref name="ATOS" /> the seventh episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which released on [[November 10]], [[2012]].<ref name="EpGuide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref>
