'''Coonee''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Brevost sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> According to a theory from [[smuggler]] [[Tregga]], the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] raised an orphaned [[Han Solo]] after the end of the [[Clone Wars]], and before being captured by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the planet [[Mimban]] they made their living on planets such as Coonee and [[Saberhing]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref>

Tregga told the [[bartender]] [[Tivoche Bilure]] his theory who wrote it his [[Midnight's book|journal]]. Bilure did not believe the theory since Tregga's implants were known to malfunction.<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" /> Despite Tregga's theory, Han and Chewbacca first met on Mimban after they were captured by the Empire.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Coonee was first mentioned in the [[2018]] [[canon]] replica journal ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Solo Tales from Vandor" />

[[Coonee/Legends|Coonee]] was originally created by [[George Lucas]] as part of [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo's]] backstory, but it was never incorporated into Solo's biography. According to Lucas, Han Solo went to the planet Coonee, and spent three years herding and transporting [[coldppeda]]s throughout the universe. Lucas' notes on Han Solo's backstory were included in the [[2007]] reference book ''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]'' by [[Jonathan W. Rinzler]].<ref name="Making of SW">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref> Coonee was then introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
