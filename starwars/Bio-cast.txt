A '''bio-cast'''<ref name="The Children from Tehar" /> or '''biocast''' was a type of medical technology used to heal broken [[bone]]s.<ref name="Bacta Basis">{{InsiderCite|190|Bacta Basis: Medical Care in the Star Wars Galaxy|Bacta Basis: Medical Care in the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy}}</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">{{ResistanceDate|1|6}}</ref> the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]] insisted on treating the [[Youngling|child]] [[Eila]]'s broken [[Limb|leg]] with a bio-cast. However, her [[Kel and Eila's family|brother]], [[Kel]], instead pursued a [[Plant|herbal]] remedy.<ref name="The Children from Tehar" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bio-cast was first mentioned in "[[The Children from Tehar]],"<ref name="The Children from Tehar" /> the sixth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' animated television series which premiered on [[November 4]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> It was later identified as "biocast" in the article "[[Bacta Basis: Medical Care in the Star Wars Galaxy|Bacta Basis: Medical Care in the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy]]," which was written by [[Amy Ratcliffe]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 190|190th issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Bacta Basis" /> on [[June 11]], [[2019]].<ref name="Insider 190">{{TitanComics|url=m/2-star-wars-insider-190|text=Star Wars Insider #190}}</ref>
