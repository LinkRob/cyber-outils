'''Dogs''', also known as '''hounds''', were a classification of [[Sentience|non-sentient]], [[Diet|omnivorous]] [[Limb|quadrupedal]] or sometimes hexapedal<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> [[species]] of [[canine]]s in [[the galaxy]]. One type was the [[akk dog]], which was found on [[Anoat]].<ref name="Uprising" /> The [[Begamor two-headed dog]] was found on the [[planet]] [[Begamor]].<ref name="Silver Dawn">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref>

The [[List of phrases and slang|term]] "dog" could also be used as a word for contempt. While [[C-3PO]]'s head was temporarily attached to a [[B1-series battle droid]]'s body during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], he was heard shouting, "Die, [[Jedi]] dogs!"<ref name="Star Wars Episode II: Attack of the Clones">{{Film|II}}</ref> Additionally, the phrase "wet dog" was used by [[Luke Skywalker]] to describe an odor he smelled while on [[Kupoh]].<ref name="HTTJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
