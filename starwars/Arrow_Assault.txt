'''Arrow Assault''' was a [[hologame]] that simulated the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. The game was accessible via the [[Holo-Arcade]] and was created by the [[Mon Calamari]] child prodigy [[Sprocket]], who was one of the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] in the competition.<ref name="S4 Patch" /> It was introduced to the [[Grand Arena|Arena]] by [[Balada]] during the [[Feast of Prosperity]].<ref name="Feast of Prosperity">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] [[Star Wars]]|quote=Good Tidings - the FEAST OF PROSPERITY is here! Compete in Scrap Salvage for amazing rewards! Balada's generosity seemingly has no limits as she bestows upon the Arena new Holo-Arcade mode - Arrow Assault! #StarWarsHunters|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/WWxyK}}</ref> Although Arrow Assault included faithful recreations of the various battlegrounds of the Grand Arena, the game diverted from reality in that all eight Hunters were the [[Zabrak]] archer [[Nox]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arrow Assault is a limited-time game mode in the [[2024]] competitive arena combat [[Star Wars video games|game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' by [[Zynga]] and [[Lucasfilm Games]]. In the [[In-universe|real-world]] game, all eight players battle as Nox in a four-versus-four Squad Brawl to thirty points.<ref name="Hunters" />
