A '''medical center''' was located in [[Echo Base]], the secret headquarters of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. The medical center was equipped with [[bacta tank]]s, one of which was used to heal [[Luke Skywalker]] after he was injured by a [[wampa]], as well as a recovery room, an Intensive Care Unit, and surgery rooms. The center was led by a [[medical command officer]] and had a staff of 350 [[doctor]]s and 120 [[medical droid]]s.<ref name="SWCL" />
