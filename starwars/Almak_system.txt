The '''Almak system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Almak sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]],<ref name="Western" /> where it was situated in grid square J-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It contained [[Almak]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Leffingite]] [[species]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], many Leffingites came under attack from the government, forcing them to flee Almak.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Almak system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Almak system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
