The '''Frinn Mak Traveling Circus''' was a [[circus]] that traveled throughout [[the galaxy]]. At some point after the [[Garsea family]] left the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> in [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]]. The novel establishes that [[Izal Garsea]] left [[Batuu]] when she was five [[Standard year|years]] old, and as the novel takes place on the [[Standard day|day]] before and day of Izal Garsea's eighteenth [[birthday]], she must have left the [[planet]] in [[21 ABY]].</ref> the circus was heading for<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Cuyacan]] when it rescued the stranded [[smuggler]] [[Izal Garsea]] aboard her [[starship]], the [[Meridian (light freighter)|''Meridian'']], after it ran out of [[fuel]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Frinn Mak Traveling Circus was first mentioned in the [[2019]] novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />
