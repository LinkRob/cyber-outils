==Behind the scenes==
The duel on Vjun was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2020]] reference book "[[Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment]]," the ninth issue of the ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'' book series.<ref name="LJE" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the duel first appeared in ''[[Yoda: Dark Rendezvous]]'', a novel written by [[Sean Stewart]] and published by [[Del Rey]] as a part of the Clone Wars multimedia project on [[November 23]], [[2004]].<ref name="Dark Rendezvous">''[[Yoda: Dark Rendezvous]]''</ref>
