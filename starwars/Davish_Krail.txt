'''Davish "Pops" Krail''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] from the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]] who served as a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]] [[pilot]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Krail flew in [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] under the [[callsign]] [[Gold Five]] during the battle. Along with Captain [[Jon Vander|Jon "Dutch" Vander]] and [[Dex Tiree]], Krail undertook the first trench run on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. He aborted his unsuccessful run after Vander and Tiree were both [[Death|killed]], but his fighter was also destroyed by [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader's]] [[TIE Advanced x1]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|They came from behind - !|Davish Krail's last words at the Battle of Yavin|<ref name="ANH" />}}
[[File:PopsKrail-BYSP.png|thumb|left|205px|Krail flying his Y-wing during the Battle of Yavin]]
Davish Krail, also referred to by his nickname, "Pops," was a [[human]] male who [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], he was a [[pilot]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. As part of the Alliance's [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]], he flew a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]].<ref name="USW" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> Krail was already a veteran pilot<ref name="Grounded">{{StoryCite|story=Grounded|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> and was selected to join Gold Squadron in the attack on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Flying under the [[callsign]] [[Gold Five]], he was instructed to to cover Captain [[Jon Vander]] in the first bombing run on the [[thermal exhaust port]], located at the end of the [[meridian trench]]. Krail also estimated that there were at least twenty [[XX-9 heavy turbolaser battery|turbolaser batteries]] along the trench and alerted both Vander and [[Dex Tiree]] to keep a lookout for any [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Shortly afterwards, they were attacked by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] himself and a pair of escort fighters. Despite Vander's warnings that they couldn't outmaneuver Vader and his escort, Krail encouraged him to stick to their target, but when Vander was [[Death|shot down]] by Vader's [[TIE Advanced x1]], Krail was forced to abandon his trench run. In his last words, he attempted to warn [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] of the dangers that lay within the trench before Darth Vader destroyed his engine. The starfighter blazed out of control, and exploded, which killed him.<ref name="ANH" />

==Personality and traits==
Davish Krail was a [[human]] male who was a skilled pilot. By the time of the Battle of Yavin, "Pops" was an ace with 7,603 flight hours and seven confirmed kills.<ref name="Grounded" />

==Behind the scenes==
Davish Krail first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] as "Gold Five", played by [[Graham Ashley]].<ref name="ANH" /> After informing Red Leader [[Garven Dreis]] of the deaths of Vander and Tiree, Dreis refers to him as "Gold Leader." It is unknown if this is a movie mistake, an error made by the in-universe character, or Dreis simply acknowledging that Krail was now the acting commander of Gold Squadron.

The character was first given the name of "Davish Krail" in the [[1995]] "[[Premiere Limited]]" set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]]. After the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] was reclassified as "Legends," the name "Davish Krail" was reintroduced to [[canon]] in the [[2015]] reference title ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW" />
