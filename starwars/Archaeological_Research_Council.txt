The '''Archaeological Research Council''' was a committee of [[archaeologist]]s based on [[Kallidah]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> the [[homeworld]] of the [[Kallidahin]] [[species]].<ref name="Kallidahin">{{Databank|kallidahin|Kallidahin}}</ref> The council settled a [[Polis Massa Base|research base]] in [[Polis Massa]], an [[asteroid field]] that was created when a cataclysm destroyed the [[planet]] of the same name, to study the mysteries behind its destruction,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and after generations of working on the project, the Kallidahin came to be mistakenly known as Polis Massans.<ref name="Kallidahin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Archaeological Research Council was first mentioned in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], a [[2005]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book that compiled ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', and ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars Trilogy]]'', and included new content written by [[Kerrie Dougherty]] that covered locations from [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> It became [[canon]] when it was mentioned in ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', a reference book co written by [[Tricia Barr|Patricia Barr]], [[Adam Bray]], [[Daniel Wallace]], and [[Ryder Windham]] that was released in [[2015]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />
