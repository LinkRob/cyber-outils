'''Enarc''' was an astronomical object<ref name="TFABG" /> located in the [[Enarc system]] in the [[Alui sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing" /> Situated in grid square O-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was located at the confluence of a number of [[hyperspace route]]s;<ref name="TFABG" /> it was the southern endpoint of the [[Triellus Trade Route]]<ref name="Triellus" /> and the eastern endpoint of the [[Enarc Run]],<ref name="Enarc" /> and was situated along another route that ran from [[Darkknell]] on the [[Hydian Way]] to the [[New Cov system]] on the [[Corellian Run]].<ref name="TFABG" />

In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{Film|I}} ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' is set prior to and concurrently with ''The Phantom Menace'', meaning it must be set in the same year.</ref> the [[Trade Federation]] [[blockade]]d the [[planet]] [[Naboo]] in preparation for a planned [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]], resulting in [[Foodstuff|food]] shortages. [[Governor]] [[Kelma]] of the planet [[Karlinus]], located elsewhere in Naboo's [[Chommell sector]], sent an agricultural [[transport]] full of [[grain]] to Naboo as a sign of good faith after the recent [[Chommell sector summit|summit]]. However, the [[starship]] was refused passage and forced to divert to Enarc<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref> just south of Naboo along the Enarc Run,<ref name="TFABG" /> where the grain was placed in cold storage.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />
