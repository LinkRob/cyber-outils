"'''Added Muscle'''" is a short story in the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''. The story, written by [[Paul Dini]], is from the point of view of [[Boba Fett]].

==Plot summary==
Under [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]'s instructions, the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] meets Jabba and his entourage at [[Docking Bay 94]]. Jabba has told Fett that it is a collection job and he needs some insurance. Fett thinks the meeting can unfold in two ways. First, Jabba gets his money from [[Han Solo]], and they can all leave happy. Second, Solo and [[Chewbacca]] resist, and Fett gets to add a new scalp to his collection. Fett had initially been planning to leave [[Tatooine]] yesterday but had delayed his departure after learning that [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was looking for two renegade [[droid]]s. Fett also wanted to curry favor with Lord Vader after killing three rebel [[Spy|spies]] on [[Coruscant]].

Fett had joined the hunt for the two droids, but the trail had dried up at the site where [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]s had massacred a clan of [[Jawa]]s in their [[sandcrawler]]. Fett had answered Jabba's call with the hope of settling scores with Solo and hunting Chewbacca down. While with Jabba, the Hutt [[crime lord]] might consider the bounty hunter the closest thing he has got to a friend. Fett is present when Solo convinces Jabba to give him an extension to pay back the money. While Fett is disappointed, he vows to hunt down Solo and to skin Chewbacca for his auburn scalp.
