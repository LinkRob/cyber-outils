The '''Corvax Sentinel''' was a [[droid]] that guarded the [[Lord Corvax's tomb|tomb]] of [[Dorwin Corvax|Lord Corvax]] on [[Mustafar]]. When [[Corvax descendant|Corvax's descendant]] entered the tomb in hopes of retrieving the [[Bright Star]] artifact, the off-worlder had to fight several such droids.<ref name="Vader VR 2">{{VaderImmortal|II}}</ref>
