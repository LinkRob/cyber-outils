==Behind the scenes==
The barracks first appeared in "[[Into the Unknown]],"<ref name="Into the Unknown" /> the first episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'', which aired on [[October 6]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> It was first identified as First Order Barracks in the episode's "Bucket's List Extra," which was posted on [[StarWars.com]] the [[October 7|following day]].<ref name="BLE" /> [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Aaron Pham]] created concept artwork featuring the barracks dated to [[December 5]], [[2017]].<ref name="ConceptArt">{{SW|url=into-the-unknown-episode-concept-art-gallery|text="Into the Unknown" Episode Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 11}}</ref>
