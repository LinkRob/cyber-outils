'''Bolander''' was a [[Gender|female]] who served as an [[First Order officer|officer]] in the [[First Order]]. When [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] came aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Absolution]]'' for a meeting, he and [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]] spoke in his [[Brendol Hux|father's]] old suite about Captain [[Phasma]] and her [[Death|killing]] of Brendol. After Cardinal left the suite, Hux contacted Bolander and requested her to send over the list of guests who would be attending the meeting so that he could take Cardinal's name off the list.<ref name="Phasma" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Bolander" is the last name of [[Ron Howard]]'s character in [[George Lucas]]'s 1973 film [[Wikipedia:American Graffiti|''American Graffiti'']]. It is unknown if the character in [[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']] was meant to be a reference to this.
