The '''''Bright Hope''''' was a [[GR-75 medium transport]] that served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. There were twenty-nine other transports of the same model used by the rebels to escape Hoth. During the [[Battle of Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]], the [[transport]] was a part of the [[Code K-one-zero|evacuation plan]]. Rebel personnel like [[Lieutenant]] [[Harlize Dana]], [[Rainn Poras]] and [[communications officer]] [[Toryn Farr]] boarded the ''Bright Hope'' while the kitchen worker [[Chase Wilsorr]] helped [[Doctor]] [[Tristan Melthabi]], [[Officer]] [[Sendak]] and the [[Tauntaun]] handler [[Jordan Smythe]] make it to the transport. The ''Bright Hope'' then took off, escorted by three [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s [[pilot]]ed by [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Wes Janson]] and [[Tarrin Datch|Tarrin]]. It was the last transport to leave Hoth.

==Characteristics==
The ''Bright Hope'' was a [[GR-75 medium transport]] built by [[Gallofree Yards, Inc.]].<ref name="USW" /> Standing at 90 [[meter]]s,<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> the [[transport]] could travel at 650 [[Kilometer per hour|kph]] and was equipped with a class 4 [[hyperdrive]]. The ''Bright Hope'' also had a capacity of 19,000 [[metric ton]]s<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighters">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> as well as shield generators. The transport was built for the roles of transportation<ref name="USW" /> as well as the specialized roles of communications, medical ship, fireship, resupply, rescue and fire support.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==History==
{{Quote|The ''Bright Hope'' is leaving in ten minutes. I repeat, ten minutes. This is the last transport for all evacuees.|Toryn Farr, over Echo Base's intercom|<ref name="Good Kiss">{{StoryCite|story=A Good Kiss|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the ''Bright Hope'' served under the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] at [[Echo Base]],<ref name="ESB" /> the [[Alliance High Command]]'s secret base on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Hoth]].<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Project Swarm|discovered]] the base, prompting the Alliance to begin its [[Code K-one-zero|evacuation]] from the planet. The Empire arrived and [[Battle of Hoth|attacked]] Echo Base in the meantime, and the ''Bright Hope'' was the last scheduled transport to be departing Hoth,<ref name="ESB" /> with the help of [[rebel pilot]]s [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Wes Janson]],<ref name="USW" /> and [[Tarrin Datch|Tarrin]]<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> being assigned as escort.<ref name="ESB" /> [[Deck officer]]s, including [[Serenity Meeks]] directed evacuees to the ''Bright Hope'' and other GR-75s, which left one by one.<ref name="Good Kiss" />

When the kitchen worker [[Chase Wilsorr]] and [[Lieutenant]] [[Harlize Dana]] reached the transport, Meeks reported to [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]] that [[Doctor]] [[Tristan Melthabi]] and three medtechs were trapped at the medical facility. Wilsorr went to rescue the medical staff and directed them towards the ''Bright Hope'' while he searched for more stragglers. The [[communications officer]] [[Toryn Farr]] soon announced that the transport would be leaving in ten [[Standard minute|minutes]].<ref name="Good Kiss" /> Farr eventually fled her post at the command center for the ''Bright Hope''.<ref name="Ion Control">{{StoryCite|story=Ion Control|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] also left the center for the transport with [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]] and the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]].<ref name="ESB" /> Injured rebel pilots were taken to the ''Bright Hope''. Farr and found her injured sister, [[Samoc Farr]], among the injured pilots.<ref name="Ion Control" />

[[File:SouthSlopeStagingArea.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The ''Bright Hope'' during takeoff]]
Wilsorr also returned to the ''Bright Hope'' with other personnel he had found, including communications [[officer]] [[Sendak]]. With three minutes until take off, the kitchen worker rushed to the [[Tauntaun]] pens and evacuated the handler [[Jordan Smythe]].<ref name="Good Kiss" /> After Organa, Solo and C-3PO were cut off by a tunnel collapse, Solo contacted ''Bright Hope'', giving the [[starship]] clearance to take off without them, taking his two comrades aboard his [[light freighter]] the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' instead.<ref name="ESB" /> Meeks stalled the ''Bright Hope'' from taking off to allow Wilsorr and Smythe to board the vessel. After the pair arrived on the transport, riding the tauntaun [[Sunshine]], other rebels like [[Rainn Poras]] congratulated Wilsorr for his help saving personnel.<ref name="Good Kiss" /> The ''Bright Hope'' then took off from Hoth with its X-wing escort.<ref name="ESB" /> The ''Bright Hope'' then became part of the [[Fourth Division]] of the Alliance Navy alongside the [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] ''[[Redemption]]'' and multiple other [[corvette]]s and [[transport]]s, and was present at [[Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine]].<ref name="Fourth Division" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Bright Hope'' first appeared in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], directed by .<ref name="ESB" /> The transport was identified by [[Dorling Kindersley|DK publishing's]] [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which stated that [[Derek Klivian|Derek "Hobbie" Klivian]] was part of the starfighter escort for ''Bright Hope''.<ref name="USW" /> However the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]], later contradicted that by saying Hobbie died crashing his snowspeeder during the Battle of Hoth.<ref name="Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> Furthermore, another reference book by DK publishing named [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], published on [[2016]], replaced Hobbie with Tarrin as one of starfighter escort pilots.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the ''Bright Hope'' was first featured in the [[M. Shayne Bell]]'s short story ''[[Of Possible Futures: The Tale of Zuckuss and 4-LOM]]'', part of the [[1996]] anthology book ''[[Tales of the Bounty Hunters]]'', which revealed the transport to be the one which Han Solo attempts to bring Leia Organa to during the Battle of Hoth in ''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back''. The name ''Bright Hope'' came from one of C-3PO's spoken film lines, when he suggested to Organa that the final transport was their "only hope" for escape.<ref name="OPF">{{StoryCite|story=Of Possible Futures: The Tale of Zuckuss and 4-LOM|book=Tales of the Bounty Hunters}}</ref><ref name="ESB" /> Later, the [[1998]] [[Wikipedia:Personal computer|PC]] program ''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]'' [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively]] revealed that the ''Bright Hope'' was the GR-75 medium transport taking off in the background scene during which Luke Skywalker gets into his X-wing to evacuate Hoth in ''The Empire Strikes Back'', establishing ship's first in-universe appearance.<ref name="BTM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref>
