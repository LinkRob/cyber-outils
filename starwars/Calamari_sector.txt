The '''Calamari sector''',<ref name="USW" /> also known as the '''Mon Calamari sector'''<ref name="Star Wars 46">[[Star Wars (2015) 46|''Star Wars'' (2015) 46]]</ref> or '''Mon Calamari Space''',<ref name="Gadgets">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref> was a [[sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] region of [[the galaxy]] and contained the [[Mon Calamari system]].<ref name="USW" /> During the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="WW" /> much surrounding [[Realspace|space]] in the sector around the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]] was under the Separatists control,<ref name="English The Clone Wars">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Clone Wars}}</ref> and later, a [[Battle of Mon Cala|battle]] took place on Mon Cala.<ref name="WW" /> It bordered the [[Lothal sector]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>
