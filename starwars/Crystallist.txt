A '''crystallist''' was a title given to an individual with knowledge of [[crystal]]s and [[lightsaber]] technology. [[Var-Whill]] was a noted crystallist of the [[Jedi Order]] prior to the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In the aftermath of the [[Corsair Wars]], Var-Whill was recruited by [[Jedi Master]] [[Oo'ob]] to create the [[Farkiller]], a modified [[sniper rifle]] using lightsaber technology. As a result, both Oo'ob and Var-Whill were pronounced apostate and [[Death|killed]] within a [[Standard decade|decade]] of the Farkiller's creation.<ref name="Aphra 33" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of crystallist was first mentioned in [[Doctor Aphra (2016) 33|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 33]], a [[canon]] comic written by [[Simon Spurrier]]<ref name="Aphra 33" /> and released on [[June 19]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/71452/star_wars_doctor_aphra_2016_33|text=Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016) #33}}</ref>
