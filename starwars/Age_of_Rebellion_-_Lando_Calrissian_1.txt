'''''Age of Rebellion - Lando Calrissian'' 1''' is the sixth one-shot in the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] anthology series [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (comic series)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']]. It was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Matteo Buffagni]], and published on [[May 15]], [[2019]] by [[Marvel Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''CLOUD CITY BLUES! Former smuggler LANDO CALRISSIAN has a beautiful dream: a life of leisure and luxury as the totally legitimate BARON ADMINISTRATOR of CLOUD CITY. All he needs to do is take care of his money troubles with one last, big scam. But what will get in his way the most &mdash; his bad luck or this annoying sense of ethics he seems to be developing? Co-starring LOBOT!''
