The '''Arkorayal Cabaret Cantina''',<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 3" /> also known as the '''Arkorayal Cantina''',<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" /> the '''Republic base camp''',<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref> the '''Bri Phrang Outpost''',<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 7">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 7|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 7]]</ref> or nicknamed the "'''Arko''',"<ref name="THRA 18">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 18|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 18]]</ref> was a renowned [[cantina]] located in [[Bri-Phrang City|Bri Phrang City]] on the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]]. It was claimed by local elders that the cantina sat atop a [[sleep]]ing [[Dragon (creature)|dragon's]] lair.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 9">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 9|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 9]]</ref> During the [[High Republic Era]], the cantina served as a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Defense Coalition|Defense Coalition]] outpost<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 3" /> and was visited by [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" /> The [[Mrala-Talisola Wedding]] between [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] and [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]] took place in the cantina.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 14">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 14|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 14]]</ref>
