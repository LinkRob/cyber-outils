'''''Bounty Hunters'' 38''' is the thirty-eighth issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Davide Tinto]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 20]], [[2023]].

==Publisher's summary==
''GRIEVOUS ATTACKS! &ndash; A ''DARK DROIDS'' TIE-IN! ZUCKUSS, 4-LOM and their fellow bounty hunters face a deadly droid ambush! VALANCE's life hangs in the balance! What role has GENERAL GRIEVOUS played in the chaos roiling the galaxy?''

a mysterious fixer aboard a secret droid colony in a last-ditch
attempt to save their cyborg friend's failing neural processor.

But unbeknownst to them, their supposed savior, Kligson, has
already wiped Valance's memories&mdash;the last vestiges of what
made him human.

Other dangers are circling the crew. Even now, a deadly droid
army is advancing on Zuckuss and 4-LOM. All of Kligson's plans
tie into a long-simmering crusade&mdash;one that dates back to the
days of the Clone Wars.

A crusade that has been reignited by a major scourge
threatening all droids!}}
