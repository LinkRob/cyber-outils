Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], two [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Confederate]] [[gunship]]s '''ambushed''' a [[Galactic Republic]] [[troop]] [[transport]], causing it to crash and [[Death|killing]] or injuring the majority of the [[clone trooper]]s on board. The Separatist [[battle droid]]s pursued the downed craft and began killing off the wounded [[trooper]]s, leading one of the survivors, [[Cut Lawquane]], to flee from the battlefield and [[Desertion|abandon]] his military service in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="The Deserter" />
