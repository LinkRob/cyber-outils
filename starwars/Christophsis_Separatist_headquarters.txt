The '''Separatist headquarters''' on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Christophsis]] was the base of operations for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Battle of Christophsis]], one of the early engagements with the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. During the battle, a [[Lightsaber combat|lightsaber duel]] took place when [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] encountered the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] while investigating the base.<ref name="The Hidden Enemy" />
