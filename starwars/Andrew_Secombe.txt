'''Andrew Secombe''' (born [[April 26]], [[1950s|1953]])<ref name="New Cassian Andor Series" /> is a voice-actor who portrayed [[Watto]] in both [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] and reprised the role in various video games.
