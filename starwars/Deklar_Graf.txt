'''Deklar Graf''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served as [[Father of Metals]] in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] during the [[High Republic Era]] and had an elaborate mustache.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> Graf was invited by [[Alys Ongwa]] to a gathering to honor recent losses in the [[city]]. Graf attended the event with the other [[City Father]]s and wore a tall, shimmery [[hat]] in order to attract attention to themself.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> Graf shared their last name with the [[Graf family]], a human [[noble]] family.<ref name="Out of the Shadows">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref>
