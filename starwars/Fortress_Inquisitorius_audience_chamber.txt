An '''audience chamber''' was located in [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[moon]] [[Nur]]. In [[19 BBY]], [[the Grand Inquisitor]] hosted potential recruits for the [[Inquisitorius]] in the audience chamber. When the former [[Jedi]] [[Barriss Offee]] proved herself worthy of becoming an [[Inquisitor]], a group of Inquisitors gathered in the chamber and bowed their heads as [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] entered.

==Description==
The audience chamber<ref name="Devoted AD" /> was a room within the [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] structure on the [[moon]] [[Nur]]. The [[fortress]] was controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Devoted" /> specifically the regime's [[Inquisitorius]], an organization of [[Force-sensitive]] [[Jedi hunter]]s that served both [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]] and the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The fortress was made out of black [[stone]]s from<ref name="DoR:TVG">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]], the twin planet of [[Jestefad]],<ref name="CL">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> which Nur [[orbit]]ed.<ref name="Devoted" />

While the fortress was itself unfinished<ref name="Devoted" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TalesDate|E4}}</ref> the chamber was located in the section of the structure that lay beneath the [[ocean]]'s surface, with triangular [[viewport]]s that gazed into the [[water]]. The room had a single entrance and included a main floor, with the end of the room opposite the doors being an elevated space where a [[throne]] sat above a set of short steps. The throne belonged to Darth Vader, leader of the [[Inquisitor]]s.<ref name="Devoted" />

==History==
{{Quote|Now that you've all arrived, we may begin.|The Grand Inquisitor addresses a group of former Jedi in the audience chamber|<ref name="Devoted" />}}
[[File:RecruitmentSpeech-Devoted.png|thumb|right|350px|Three potential Imperial Inquisitors were briefed in the audience chamber.]]
Immediately following the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the [[Clone Wars]] and the ensuing [[Proclamation of the New Order|fall]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Devoted" /> in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the new Galactic Empire established the Inquisitorius and headquartered their operations at Fortress Inquisitorius. While the order already included several members, multiple candidates for the organization were gathered and brought to Nur, where they were assembled in the audience chamber. It was there that [[the Grand Inquisitor]] addressed [[Barriss Offee]], [[Dante]], and [[Ahmar]], all members of the [[Jedi Order]] who had survived the [[Great Jedi Purge]], telling the potential inductees that they would be tested to see if they were worthy of joining the order and helping to bring security to the new Empire.<ref name="Devoted" />

When Offee proved<ref name="Devoted" /> her<ref name="Devoted AD" />  merit and became a [[Sister (Inquisitorius)|Sister]] of the Inquisitorius, she joined the fellow Inquisitors [[Fourth Sister]], [[Marrok|First Brother]], and [[Eleventh Brother]] in the audience chamber. As the [[Grand Inquisitor]] stood next to the room's throne, he introduced them to their new master. All five individuals bowed as Vader entered the chamber and took<ref name="Devoted" /> his<ref name="DoR:TVG" /> seat upon the throne.<ref name="Devoted" />
