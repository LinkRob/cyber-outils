A '''battle''' took place between [[Phoenix Cell]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="A Day's Duty" /> between [[4 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Timeline" /> The Phoenix Squadron was able to retreat relatively unscathed but lost [[Phoenix Three]] to a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]. The Imperials suffered the loss of two [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/ln space superiority starfighters]] and one heavily damaged [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]].<ref name="A Day's Duty" />

== Prelude ==
The Phoenix Cell, comprising three [[CR90 corvette|CR90 corvettes]] and six [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]<nowiki/>s of [[Phoenix Squadron]], were awaiting the return of the [[VCX-100 light freighter]] ''[[Ghost]]'' which had departed on a supply run to [[Crandel]]. One of the A-wings returned from its patrol near [[Rinn]] in a damaged state, having been attacked by Imperials. After docking with the command ship, the ''[[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|Liberator]],'' the pilot, who was injured in the encounter, informed the force's commanding officer, [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]], he was unsure if he was followed.<ref name="A Day's Duty" /> 

== Battle ==
Sato immediately ordered the fleet to prepare to jump to [[hyperspace]] but was interrupted by the arrival of an Imperial fleet consisting of two [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]]<nowiki/>s, two [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]], and their accompanying [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE/ln space superiority starfighters]]. Sato then ordered the fleet to raise their [[Deflector shield|deflector shields]] and for Phoenix Squadron to intercept the Imperials.<ref name="A Day's Duty" />

As the A-wings engaged with the TIE fighters, one of the TIEs went after the ''Liberator,'' scoring hits that caused an explosion internally and disabled the ship's main [[Laser Battery|battery]]. As repairs were made, the ''Liberator'' attacked one of the ''Arquitens''-class cruisers. [[Phoenix Three]] was killed when his A-wing was destroyed by one of the TIE fighters. The ''Liberator'' scored hits on the ''Arquitens'' while another of the A-wings eliminated one of the TIEs. The ''Arquitens'' fought back and further damaged the ''Liberator.''<ref name="A Day's Duty" />

Once the ''Liberator''<nowiki/>'s main battery was repaired, the ship delivered a devastating blow to the ''Arquitens'', severely damaging the Imperial cruiser and knocking out its [[hyperdrive]]. Nearly simultaneously, the ''Ghost'' returned and provided cover as the rebel fleet jumped to hyperspace, destroying another TIE fighter.<ref name="A Day's Duty" />

== Aftermath ==
The Imperials failed to destroy the rebel fleet. Phoenix Cell moved its base to [[Garel]], where they were [[Battle of Garel|eventually discovered]] by the Empire.<ref>{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref>
