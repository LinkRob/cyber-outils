The '''"Dumb" Energy Quarrel''' was a type of explosive energy [[Quarrel/Legends|quarrel]] produced by [[Kelvarek Consolidated Arms/Legends|Kelvarek Consolidated Arms]] that was used in the company's [[MM9 mini concussion rocket launcher/Legends|MM9 rocket system]]. Produced at some point before [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]], the quarrels were much less effective than a [[Blaster pistol/Legends|blaster pistol]] and had a range of sixty [[Meter/Legends|meters]] when fired from the [[Wrist rocket launcher|rocket system]].

==Description==
A "Dumb" Energy [[Quarrel/Legends|Quarrel]] was a type of explosive ammunition that was designed to cause energy damage upon impact.<ref name="AEG" /> However, the quarrels were significantly less powerful than the [[Blaster/Legends|energy]] bolts fired from a [[Blaster pistol/Legends|blaster pistol]].<ref name="AEGdamage">The ''[[Arms & Equipment Guide]]'' describes the typical [[Blaster pistol/Legends|blaster pistol]] as dealing 3D6 of damage, while the "Dumb" Energy Quarrel only deals 2D6 of damage.</ref> "Dumb" Energy Quarrels had a maximum range of sixty [[Meter/Legends|meters]]<ref name="AEGrange" /> and were usable in the [[MM9 mini concussion rocket launcher/Legends|MM9 rocket system]].<ref name="AEG" /> "Dumb" Energy Quarrels lacked a targeting system, and in the MM9 system, they were used in tandem alongside other types of rockets that used homing systems.<ref name="weapons">''[[The Essential Guide to Weapons and Technology]]''</ref>

==History==
At some point before the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]],<ref name="AEGera" /> "Dumb" Energy Quarrels were produced by [[Kelvarek Consolidated Arms/Legends|Kelvarek Consolidated Arms]] as a type of ammunition that could be used in the MM9 rocket system, a model of [[wrist rocket launcher]] that was also produced by Kelvarek. The quarrels were widely available in [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] for the price of fifty [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] each, although the sale and ownership of the ammunition was restricted to individuals with the appropriate license and government approval.<ref name="AEG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "Dumb" Energy Quarrel was briefly mentioned in the ''[[Arms & Equipment Guide]]'', a [[2002]] sourcebook written by [[Jeff Grubb]] and [[Owen K.C. Stephens]] for [[Wizards of the Coast]]'s ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]''. The ''Arms and Equipment Guide'' did not specify the era when the quarrels were used beyond the fact that they were used with the MM9 rocket system, which was stated to only be available for purchase during the [[Rise of the Empire era]].<ref name="AEG" />
