"'''Cracken's Rebel Field Guide: Slicing Aids'''" is an article written by [[Craig Robert Carey|C. Robert Carey]] and [[J. P. Pietrzak|J.P. Pietrzak]] with illustrations by [[Matt Busch]]. It was published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 10|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' ''10'']] in [[May]] [[1996]] by [[West End Games]].

Following roughly the same format as the [[Cracken's Rebel Field Guide (sourcebook)|book of the same name]], it profiled [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] tools used for [[Slicer/Legends|slicing]] under the command of [[Alliance Intelligence/Legends|Alliance Intelligence]] chief [[Airen Cracken/Legends|Airen Cracken]].

==Contents==
This article serves as a supplementary piece for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', introducing several slicing equipment designed to help Rebel agents. Thses tools include the:

*[[Diagnostic Systems Diverter|UniTech "Patch"]]
*[[DataSearch Engine 9C|TerexComm DataSearch 9C]]
*[[DataSearch Engine 12C-A|TerexComm DataSearch 12C-A]]
*[[Encryption Package 1a.44.87|AccuTronics Encryption Package]]
*[[A/E computer|Analysis/Encoding Computer]]
*Modified [[TranLang III communications module/Legends|TranLang III Module]]
*[[Subspace tight-beam transmission core|Subspace Tight-Beam Transmission Core]]
*[[Intellex IV/Legends|Intellex IV]]
*[[TC-40 Padlink|Datapad Remote Link Unit]]
