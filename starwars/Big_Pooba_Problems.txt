"'''Big Pooba Problems'''" is the first part of the [[Big Pooba Problems / Best Bounty Buddies|seventeenth episode]] of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|second season]] the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It was released on [[Disney+]] on [[March 19]], [[2025]].<ref name="Announcement" />

==Official description==
''Nubs meets an adult Pooba.''

==Plot summary==
===The crash landing===
[[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Nubs]] and [[Lys Solay]] ride on their [[speeder bike]]s into the middle of a [[Tenoo tree forest|forest]] clearing on [[Tenoo]], hoping to find [[waalax tree]], which has been spotted growing around the area. Consulting her [[datapad]], Solay reads that waalax trees have thick layers of wax and huge leaves that can be used as sleds. Nubs spots a waalax leaf. He attempts to get it, but Solay volunteers to get it when Nubs cannot reach. She [[Force jump]]s up the tree and manages to retrieve the leaf. Nubs is unhappy and tells his friend that he wishes he was a grownup. Solay reassures him that he is great the way he is because he is kind and knows a lot about nature. 

Nubs and Lys soon witness a [[starship]] crash landing nearby and rush to the site to help. At the crash site, they find a [[droid]] named [[NP-87]] evaluating the damage. The droid explains that they ran out of [[fuel]] but declines the younglings' offer of help. When Solay asks who else is here, she spots an adult [[Pooba]]. Nubs and the adult Pooba are delighted to see another of their [[species]]. Nubs helps the adult Pooba carry a large cylindrical object before introducing each other in [[Poobian]]. The adult Pooba introduces himself as [[Zeepohz]]. Solay introduces herself while Zeepohz pats Nubs on the head. NP-87 tells the Jedi younglings that their ship got damaged and that they need to find a way to transport a cargo pod to [[Kublop Springs]] by nightfall.  Nubs and Solay offer to help, with Solay saying that Nubs knows the forest the best. Seeing that the cargo pod has a [[Repulsorlift|repulsors]], she proposes hitching it to their speeders. NP-87 agrees to the plan and tasks Zeepohz with helping the younglings. 

===Transporting the cargo pod===
Nubs tows the cargo pod with Zeepohz and NP-87 riding on top. Solay rides her speeder bike from behind. NP-87 praises the younger Pooba. Just then, a pair of [[gangul]]s ram into the cargo pod. Solay realizes that they must have intruded into their territory. Nubs confronts the quadrupedal creatures with [[Nubs's lightsaber|his lightsaber]]. He attempts growling at them, but they are not intimidated by his youthful noise. Zeepohz, with a resonant roar, grabs a trunk and drives the creatures back to the forest. 

When NP-87 expresses concern about encountering more creatures, Solay recommends Nubs to take them on another route, which involves riding through a [[canyon]]. Nubs and Solay ride their speeder bikes at the front, towing the cargo pod. Nubs again tells Solay that he longs to be a grownup. Just then, NP-87 spots a large boulder ahead, blocking their path. They bring their speeders to a stop. NP-87 grumbles about the obstacle, but Zeepohz volunteers to remove it given his immense strength. NP-87, however, thinks that it will be too much for Zeepohz to handle. 

At Solay's suggestion, Nubs joins forces with Zeepohz to try and remove the rock. However, the rock is too heavy for Nubs, and the uneven distribution of force leads to them flinging the boulder into the wall of the canyon. This dislodges a large amount of debris, causing stone to fall from above. Zeepohz shelters Nubs from falling debris while Solay uses [[the Force]] to shield herself, NP-87 and the cargo pod. Though the group and their cargo survive unhurt, they find their path blocked on both sides. NP-87 blames Nubs, but Zeepohz and Solay cheer him up and reassure him they will find a way out. 

===Up and down===
Unable to go forward or backward, Solay and Nubs come up with a plan that involves going upwards. Using the roots of a giant [[Tenoo tree]], they form an improvised crane and use ropes to pull themselves and their equipment to the surface. Continuing the journey from above the canyon, Solay spots starships approaching Kublop Springs. NP-87 thinks that they can make the trip in time. 

A thirsty Zeepohz goes to a nearby [[Water|river]] to drink. While mounting the cargo pod on a rock, Nubs accidentally dislodges it, causing it to slide down a hill and crash. Inspecting the damage, they find that the repulsors are damaged. NP-87 grumbles about missing their delivery. Nubs speaks with Zeepohz before storming off into the forest. Solay comforts Nubs, telling him that she can understand that he wants to be a grownup like Zeepohz. She reminds him of [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]]'s teaching that "size matters not" but that what matters is inside you. Zeepohz also comforts the younger Pooba.

Solay devises a plan to use waalax leaves like a sled to pull the cargo pod. Agreeing with the plan, Zeepohz lifts Nubs onto a waalax tree, allowing him to retrieve several waalax leaves. They place the leaves at the bottom of the cargo pod and use Nubs' speeder bike to pull it. 

===Mission accomplished===
At the [[Kublop Springs marketplace]], a [[Talpiddian]] [[spacer]] thinks that Zeepohz and NP-87 are not going to make it and prepares to leave on their [[freighter]]. Just then, the group arrive with the cargo pod on waalax leaves. [[Unidentified Talpiddian|The merchant]] is impressed with the amount of hard work that the crew went to delivering the cargo, and says they will hire them for future jobs. NP-87 and Zeepohz celebrate, while Solay reassures Nubs that they managed to complete the mission because of his big heart and ingenuity. 

Nubs agrees. She tells Nubs that one day he will be an adult, but to make the best of his childhood. NP-87 apologizes for doubting the children. Solay is forgiving and says that they are glad they could help. Speaking in Poobian, Zeepohz tells Nubs that he is a fantastic kid and will make a fantastic grown up today. NP-87 translates and tells Nubs not to grow up too fast. Zeepohz lifts Nubs up in celebration.
