'''Amnesty Officer M34''' was the designation of a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was part of the [[New Republic Amnesty Program]], a program for former members of the [[Galactic Empire]] who were given amnesty following re-integration initiatives so that they could loyally serve the New Republic. M34 previously served as an [[Imperial officer]]. Officer M34 chatted with [[Amnesty Officer M40]] and [[Amnesty Officer G27]] about what they missed from the Empire, such as the light of [[hyperspace]], the uniforms, and the food, though M34 was glad the Empire was gone like the others.<ref name="Chapter 19" />
