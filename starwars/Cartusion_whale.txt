The '''Cartusion whale''' was a [[species]] of [[whale]] whose [[bone]]s were used to inlay [[lightsaber]] hilts. During the [[Clone Wars]], a series of conflicts fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the breakaway [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[architect droid]] [[Huyang]] told a group of [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] aboard the ''[[Crucible]]'' that a Cartusion whalebone-infused hilt was an option as they began the process of building their lightsabers.<ref name="A Test of Strength" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cartusion whale was first mentioned in "[[A Test of Strength]]," the seventh episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''{{'}} [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]],<ref name="A Test of Strength" /> which originally aired on [[November 10]], [[2012]].<ref name="A Test of Strength air date">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep507|text="A Test of Strength" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref>
