The '''destruction''' of an [[abandoned weapons depot]] was undertaken in [[5 ABY]] by [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Shriv Suurgav]] on the [[planet]] [[Sullust]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I know my way around Sullust. After all, I did help liberate this place.''"<br />"''That would explain why the Empire is still here.''"<br />"''Okay. That was a good one.|Shriv Suurgav and Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Battlefront II" />}}
By [[5 ABY]], the demands of the [[Galactic Empire]] had outgrown the [[Abandoned weapons depot|depot's]] supply, and [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] ordered the depot to double production of weapons and vehicles immediately, as the depot provided vital armaments to the Empire's operations in the [[Western Reaches]]. The [[New Republic]] discovered this and planned to steal it first, sending [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and also [[Shriv Suurgav]] of [[Inferno Squad (Resistance)|Inferno Squad]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==The mission==
The original intent of the mission was to recover the weapons in the depot for the [[New Republic]] before the Galactic Empire could. However, the Republic discovered the Empire had secretly restarted the weapons factory, at which point Calrissian and Suurgav felt it was better to destroy the depot to prevent the Empire from producing more weapons.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
