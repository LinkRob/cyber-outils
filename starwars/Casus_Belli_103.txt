'''''Casus Belli'' 103''' was a French roleplaying magazine from March of [[1997]]. It contained many roleplaying articles, including one adventures for the [[West End Games]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|D6]] system. The article was called "[[Prélude à un nouvel espoir]]" (in English "Prelude to a New Hope") written by [[Philippe Rat]].
