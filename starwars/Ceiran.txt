'''Ceiran''' was a location<ref name="MA " /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]' [[Ceiran system]], situated in grid square N-14 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Gemstone|jewel]] [[thief]] [[Pax Maripher]] felt that Ceiran that was unlike the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="MA" /> In [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]].</ref> Maripher mentioned the difference between Coruscant and Ceiran when comparing [[kyber crystal]]s with [[kohlen crystal]]s to [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] while on the [[Pijal's moon|moon]] of the Inner Rim planet [[Pijal]].<ref name="MA" />
