'''Astrogation''' was the science of safely and reliably traversing [[Realspace|space]] in a [[starship]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> Throughout [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Timeline of galactic history|history]],<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[cartographer]]s<ref name="Wild Space">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Escape]]''</ref> and [[scout]]s<ref name="Wollivan DB">{{Databank|wollivan|Wollivan}}</ref> traveled between [[star system]]s in search of more reliable and safer [[hyperspace route]]s free of dangerous obstacles, advancing the science of astrogation. Traveling through [[hyperspace]] required a [[navigation computer]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> to make precise calculations in order to follow these lanes and avoid crashing into "[[mass shadow]]s," the [[Gravity|gravitational]] wells surrounding massive celestial bodies such as [[planet]]s.<ref name="Hyperdrive DB">{{Databank|hyperdrive|Hyperdrive}}</ref> Often, ships had to navigate by way of [[Navigation buoy|hyperspace buoys]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> or stop at [[Hyperspace waypoint|waypoints]] to calculate another hyperspace vector.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>
