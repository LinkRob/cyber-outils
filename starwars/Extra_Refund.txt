'''Extra Refund''' was a type of [[Shift Token]] that was used in the card game [[Kessel Sabacc]]. It allowed the players to retrieve three of their taxed chips.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] stole an Extra Refund token from the [[Hutt Cartel District]]'s vault in [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Shift tokens were created for Kessel Sabacc which first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They are one of several Shift Tokens that could be collected and used in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
