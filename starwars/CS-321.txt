'''CS-321''' was a [[Color|tan]]-colored [[RA-7 protocol droid]] that was owned by [[Weequay]] [[pirate]]s during the [[Clone Wars]], a war between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the breakaway [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. He was destroyed during a [[Rescue of Adi Gallia|battle]] between the two parties.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Soon it will be us.|CS-321 to C-3PO about fighting K0-5D|<ref name="Nomad" />}}
At some point during the [[Clone Wars]], CS-321 was captured by a band of [[Weequay]] [[pirate]]s.<ref name="Nomad" /> Over 1 [[Standard year|year]] into the war,<ref name="Date" /> CS-321 was present on the pirate [[Starship|ship]] [[Marauder (Weequay pirate ship)|''Marauder'']] watching a [[droid]] [[gladiator]]al fight when two droids, [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]], who had been captured on [[Balnab]], were sent aboard the pirate ship. CS-321, R2-D2 and C-3PO observed the fight where the warrior [[K0-5D]] was competing against other droids. Artoo and Threepio were about to be sent into the ring when the ''Marauder'' was targeted by [[General]] [[Grievous]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] for target practice. A laser blast ripped open the ''Marauder'' causing all aboard the to be sucked into [[Realspace|space]], the droids were captured by the Separatists and sent to be incinerated, CS-321 refused to be incinerated and was shot down by [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="Nomad" />
