The '''color of war''' was a specific shade of [[Color|red]] that was only to be worn by military combatants in the highly codified society of the [[Chagrian]] [[species]]. Any individuals wearing the color were considered valid military targets during [[time]]s of conflict. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], many of the Chagrians who joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] chose to work the color of war into their [[Clothing|uniform]], generally in the form of epaulettes, piping, combat webbing, or a combination of all three. This promoted solidarity between the Chagrian [[soldier]]s, and helped to identify individual Chagrians' dedication to the cause.<ref name="LBE">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The color of war was first mentioned in ''[[Lead by Example]]'', a [[2016]] supplement for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] system.<ref name="LBE" />
