The '''Farfax system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Marzoon sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square I-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Whilst they were on [[Weigh Station Karambola]], [[L3-37]] informed [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] that it was imperative that they depart the station immediately and head to the Farfax system before stating that she wouldn't have asked if it wasn't an urgent matter of life or [[death]]. They left on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', arriving in the midst of the [[Mesulan Remnants Belt]].<ref name="Last Shot" />
