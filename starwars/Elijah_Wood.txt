'''Elijah Jordan Wood''' (born [[January 28]], [[1981]])<ref name="This Week! Ep1" /> is an American actor, voice actor, DJ and producer known for his work in the [[Wikipedia:The Lord of the Rings (film series)|''Lord of the Rings'' trilogy]]. Wood voiced the character [[Jace Rucklin]] as a guest role in the [[2018]] animated series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.<ref name="Fuel for the Fire">{{DisneyPress|abc=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes/fuel-for-the-fire|text=Fuel for the Fire|archivedate=20180925041050}}</ref>
