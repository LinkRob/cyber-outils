'''Chorgad''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> located in the [[Chorgad system]] within the [[Fei Hu sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square Q-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]] to [[3 ABY]]. As ''Bounty Hunters'' 3 establishes the [[bombing on Chorgad]] took place prior to the [[Star Wars comic books|comic's]] events, it must have occurred by 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].</ref> a crew of the [[Mourner's Wail Syndicate]] carried out a [[Bombing on Chorgad|bombing]] on Chorgad, [[Death|killing]] [[soldier]]s of their rival, the [[Unbroken Clan]] criminal syndicate.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[bounty hunter]] [[Nakano Lash]] was informed of the [[bomb]]ing by a [[Unidentified human (Ruusan)|friend]] of hers when she asked them for news.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> Also that year,<ref name="Date" /> in order to avenge those who had been lost at Chorgad, the Unbroken Clan's [[General]] [[Vukorah]] carried out an [[Attack on a spice run|unapproved attack]] on a Mourner's Wail [[spice]] run at the [[Smugglers' Refueling Station]] in<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Kessel sector]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chorgad was mentioned in the [[Bounty Hunters 3|third issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 3" /> on [[June 24]], 2020.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/80122/star_wars_bounty_hunters_2020_3|text=Star Wars: Bounty Hunters (2020) #3}}</ref>
