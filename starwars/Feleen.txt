'''Feleen''' was a [[Politics|politician]] who served in the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senate]]<ref name="BL" /> in [[28 ABY]].<ref name="Timeframe">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} establishes that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|VII}}. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], so ''Bloodline'' must take place in [[28 ABY]].</ref> Feleen was a friend of the [[Loneran]] [[senator]] [[Varish Vicly]].<ref name="BL" />
