{{Quote|Bounty #239 is a paranoid scum. No surprise there. After making enemies in over a dozen systems &mdash; making deals, breaking them, selling tainted product, even letting some so-called friends take the rap for one of his own schemes on Corellia &mdash; that Bith slimo deserves everything that's coming for him.|[[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]]|<ref name="Mythos" />}}
"'''Bounty #239'''" was the designation used by the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] for a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] criminal who prospered by scamming and conning others throughout [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. The Bith's activities angered many individuals, including former friends, and it led to the placement of a [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] on his head, to be collected by Fett.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Anyone who tells you that cheaters never prosper clearly never talked to this guy. The small army he's hired would bankrupt a Hutt.|Boba Fett|<ref name="Mythos" />}}
[[File:BobaFett_after_Bounty_239.jpg|thumb|right|180px|Boba Fett, ready to break into [[Bounty 239's compound|Bounty #239's compound]]]]
The [[Bith/Legends|Bith]] [[Gender/Legends|male]] criminal known to the [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] simply as "Bounty #239" made enemies in more than twelve [[Star system/Legends|star systems]] for cheating, breaking deals, and selling defective merchandise. At one point, he let his associates take the fall for at least one fraudulent scheme he created while on<ref name="Mythos" /> the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core World]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Essential Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> [[Corellia/Legends|Corellia]]. For these reasons, a [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] was placed on his head by his detractors, which was to be collected by Fett.<ref name="Mythos" />

The Bith, however, had amassed a small [[Army/Legends|army]] throughout the [[Standard year/Legends|years]], which was composed of around twenty ordinary [[Mercenary/Legends|mercenaries]] as well as two [[Trandoshan/Legends|Trandoshan]] [[Unidentified Trandoshan twin brothers|twin brothers]]&mdash;who had [[Mission to Ryloth (Boba Fett)|caused problems]] for Fett previously on [[Ryloth/Legends|Ryloth]]&mdash;and three [[IG-88 assassin droid/Legends|IG-88 assassin droids]] shielded against [[Ion cannon/Legends|ion weaponry]], which effectively made the [[Droid/Legends|droids]] resistant to [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]]-era [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade/Legends|electromagnetic pulse grenades]].<ref name="Mythos" />

Fett, having accepted the job, planned a blitz attack on [[Bounty 239's compound|Bounty #239's compound]] and entourage. Fett planned to [[Death/Legends|kill]] Bounty #239 and destroy his stronghold by carrying copious amounts of armaments&mdash;not just an [[EE-3 carbine rifle/Legends|EE-3 carbine rifle]] and a [[Jetpack/Legends|jetpack]]&mdash;and deploying a "shock and awe" strategy.<ref name="Mythos" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Taking this guy down won't be the usual walk in the park. He's taken that paranoia and used it to his benefit.|Boba Fett|<ref name="Mythos" />}}
Bounty #239, [[Color/Legends|black]]-[[Eye/Legends|eyed]] and bald, was an accomplished scammer who prospered by tricking other individuals throughout [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]]. He was somewhat paranoid, however, and this trait motivated him to use his [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]] to buy himself a considerably sized posse of mercenaries and droids as well as a compound for his own use.<ref name="Mythos" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bounty #239 was created for a story in the [[Canon|non-canon]] ''[[Star Wars Mythos]]'' project as Boba Fett's bounty. The story's general style and aesthetics were inspired by the [[Wikipedia:Spaghetti Western|Spaghetti Western]] film genre, and Fett's outfit was particularly drawn from actor [[Wikipedia:Clint Eastwood|Clint Eastwood's]] [[Wikipedia:Man with No Name|"Man with No Name"]] character.<ref name="Mythos" /> The authors deliberately left some aspects of the story unclear, such as when Fett planned to raid Bounty #239's compound and whether the bounty hunter actually went ahead with his plans.<ref name="Mythos" />
