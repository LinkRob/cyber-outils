The '''''Aerie''''' was a [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. It [[Attack on the Hellion's Dare|pursued]] and destroyed the [[New Republic]] [[frigate]] ''[[Hellion's Dare]]''.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Aerie'' first appeared in the [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> The [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' utilizes an [[:File:Aerie-Timelines.png|image]] of the ''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> ''[[Phoenix Nest]]'' from the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Homecoming]]"<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref> in a section regarding the ''Aerie''.<ref name="Timelines" />
