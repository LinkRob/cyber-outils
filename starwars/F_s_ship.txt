'''F's ship'''<ref name="Meet the Ships">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-ships-and-vehicles|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Ships and Vehicles}}</ref> was a [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Clone Z-95 Headhunter]] flown by the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[F]] in the [[time]] between [[19 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|104}}</ref> F used the [[starfighter]] to travel to the [[planet]]<ref name="The Village Bride" /> [[Keelia]]<ref name="Heroes">{{SW|url=news/inside-star-wars-visions-meet-the-heroes|text=Inside ''Star Wars: Visions'': Meet the Heroes}}</ref> after being summoned there by an old ally, [[Valco]]. After F helped a local [[Meevai Village|village]] fight back against [[Pirate|raiders]], F departed the planet aboard her [[starship]].<ref name="The Village Bride" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It was a blast to bring this ''Clone Wars''-era favorite into the world of ''Star Wars: Visions''.|[[Josh Rimes]]|<ref name="Meet the Ships" />}}
F's ship appeared in "[[The Village Bride]]," a short film in the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series written by [[Takahito Oonishi]] and [[Hitoshi Haga]], directed by Haga, produced by [[Kinema Citrus Co.|Kinema Citrus]], and released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="The Village Bride" /> on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="AirDate">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> The ship was first revealed in an article on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 16]], prior to the release of ''Visions''.<ref name="Meet the Ships" />

In the early stages of development for the short, Haga was interested in providing F with a classic send-off, flying into the sunset aboard a heroic ship, such as an [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]]. The timeline of the short, however, prevented the X-wing from being a viable option, and [[Matt Martin]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] instead suggested that F's ship be a Z-95.<ref name="Meet the Ships" />
