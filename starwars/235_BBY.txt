The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''235 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3042 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''216 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7742''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Senator]] [[Ghirra Starros]] gives the [[droid]] [[J-6]] to her daughter [[Avon Sunvale|Avon]].<ref name="J-6" />
*[[Bell Zettifar]] becomes [[Loden Greatstorm]]'s [[Padawan]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Bell">''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' dates the [[Dedication of the Starlight Beacon|launch]] of the [[Starlight Beacon]] to 232 [[Standard year|years]] before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], which corresponds to [[232 BBY]] in the [[Galactic standard calendar|dating system]] used by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' depicts the launch, so its events must occur around that year. ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' says that [[Bell Zettifar]] was 15 years old when he became [[Loden Greatstorm]]'s [[Padawan]]. As Zettifar is 18 years old in ''Light of the Jedi'', he must have become Greatstorm's apprentice around 235 BBY.</ref>
*[[Affie Hollow]] joins the [[Byne Guild]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
