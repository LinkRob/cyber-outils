The '''Annoo system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Tharin sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]]<ref name="Slice" /> in [[Standard Galactic Grid]] square S-8.<ref name="Appendix" /> It was connected by a [[Zeltros–Junkfort Station hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to the [[Boonta system]] and<ref name="CLNE" /> the [[Junkfort Station system]].<ref name="Junkfort">Per {{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-03.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-03}}, [[Junkfort Station]]'s absence on the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] is due to spacing limitations with the neighboring [[Marleyvane]] label, and Junkfort Station is located at the junction of the two [[hyperspace route]]s.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Annoo system was mentioned as just "Annoo" on a map of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="CLNE" /> designed by [[Christopher Ibbitt]]<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-31.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-31}}</ref> and written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="TTS">{{WebCite|url=https://tarkinstopshelf.libsyn.com/381-talking-star-wars-complete-locations-with-emily-shkoukani-and-jason-fry|text=381: Talking Star Wars: Complete Locations with Emily Shkoukani and Jason Fry!|date=2025-05-09|work=[[Tarkin's Top Shelf]]|archivedate=20250511074859}}</ref> in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''.<ref name="CLNE" /> The [[Annoo system/Legends|system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in "[[Escape Into Terror]],"<ref name="EIT">{{DroidsCite|Escape Into Terror}}</ref> the second episode of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]'', which aired on [[September 14]], [[1985]].<ref name="Insider27">{{InsiderCite|27|A Star Wars CELibration|A ''Star Wars'' CELibration}}</ref> It was then identified as the Annoo system in the similarly titled article "[[Star Wars Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]," which was written by [[Rich Handley]] and [[Abel G. Peña]] and published in the [[Polyhedron 170|170th issue]] of the magazine ''[[Polyhedron]]'' in [[July]] [[2004]].<ref name="Polyhedron">{{PolyhedronCite|170|Star Wars Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO}}</ref>
