'''Escort Carriers'''<ref name="ArtStation">{{ArtStation|username=clintyoung|url=artwork/BkQqll|name=[[Clint Young]]|text=Star Wars: Hunters VisDev|quote=Previs for Star Wars: Hunters-I was Principal Concept Artist on the Natural Motion / Zynga project. Designed and concepted all environment biomes / arenas also characters / weapons and various call outs and POIs.|date=2025-05-18|archivedate=20260303231236}}</ref> were a type of [[repulsorcraft]] utilized within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] during the [[Escort]] game played by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]. These rectangular craft hovered just above the ground, [[Pilot|piloted]] by an [[Color|orange]] and white [[R4 astromech droid]], though it would only move when members of a specific team of [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] within its vicinity, attempting to move it from one side of the battlefield to the other within a time limit while defending it from the enemy team. Two variants of Escort Carriers existed, the [[Gorax Hunt Payload]] deployed within the [[Gorax Hunt]] arena, which sported a large [[harpoon]], and the [[Survivor Salvage Payload]] deployed within the [[Aftermath|Survivor Salvage]] arena, which instead carried the remains of a defunct [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]].<ref name="Hunters" />
