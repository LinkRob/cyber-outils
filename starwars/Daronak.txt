'''Daronak''' was a terrestrial [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="Valkyrie" /> located in the [[Daronak system/Legends|Daronak system]], a part of the [[Phelleem sector/Legends|Phelleem sector]]<ref name="EAOC" /> in [[The Slice/Legends|the Slice]] portion of<ref name="TEA" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories/Legends|Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="EAOC" /> It was the site of [[Channoga Boulevard]].<ref name="Valkyrie" />

At one point during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]], a team of Alliance agents were making contact with a Rebel cell on Daronak. However, an [[Unidentified informer (Daronak)|individual]] they had dealt with was in reality an informer who reported them to the Empire, leading to Imperial [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] pursuing the agents who in turn ran down Channoga Boulevard. Eventually, several [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunters]] employing [[Thermal detonator/Legends|thermal detonators]] also engaged the team, which ultimately made its way off-planet.<ref name="Valkyrie" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daronak was mentioned in "[[Scorched Earth]]," a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure written by [[Liz Holliday]] and published in the [[1997]] [[Valkyrie 14|fourteenth issue]] of the ''[[Valkyrie]]'' magazine for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. Since that article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process,<ref name="Valkyrie" /> it was considered non-[[canon]]ical with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="UnlicensedStatus">{{UnlicensedStatus}}</ref> The [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'' placed the Daronak [[Star system/Legends|system]], and therefore the planet itself, in grid square S-7.<ref name="TEA" />
