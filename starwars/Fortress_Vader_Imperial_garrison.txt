During the [[Imperial Era]], a '''garrison''' was stationed on [[Mustafar]] as part of the security for [[Fortress Vader]]. During the castle's construction, the [[garrison]] was [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Captain]] [[Junus]], and participated in the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]], where it was annihilated. Shortly afterwards, the garrison was supplemented to [[battalion]]-level, where it continued to provide security for Fortress Vader and a nearby [[Imperial base (Mustafar)|Imperial base]].

==Description==
{{Quote|There's an Imperial garrison over on the far side, but the planet's mag field messes with their sensors. We'll come at it from the south, use the jammers on the bikes. They won't even know we were here.''"<br />"''And if you're wrong?''"<br />"''Then a hell of a lot of stormtroopers are going to die.|[[Ren (human)|Ren]] and [[Marinda]]|<ref name="Crimson Reign 4" />}}
The [[garrison]] that was ordered to protect [[Fortress Vader]]'s construction on the [[Gahenn Plains]] on [[Mustafar]] first consisted of many resources of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Imperial construction crews built the many designs of the fortress, while they were defended by Imperial [[magma trooper]]s. [[Imperial officer]]s were part of the garrison as well, and the garrison deployed [[All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport|AT-ACTs]], [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-STs]], and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] for vehicle reinforcement. An [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]] was also stationed above the fortress in Mustafar's [[atmosphere]] at one point during the construction. At this time during the castle's construction, the garrison's [[commander]] was [[Captain]] [[Junus]].<ref name="Dark Lord 23" />

After the garrison was wiped out during the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]], it was rebuilt to continue to provide security for the fortress.<ref name="Tales from Vader's Castle 5" /> Based on the far side of the plains,<ref name="Crimson Reign 4" /> the castle's security consisted of [[Volcanic environment stormtrooper|lava troopers]] and [[Unidentified Fortress Vader droid model|security droids]], as well as [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Imperial Royal Guards]]. [[All Terrain Defense Pod|AT-DPs]] were added to the security's arsenal.<ref name="Tales from Vader's Castle 5" />

By the time of the [[Mission to Mustafar (Rebel Alliance)|mission to Mustafar]]<ref name="Secrets of the Empire" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Chronology Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Secrets of the Empire]]'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> an [[Imperial base (Mustafar)|Imperial base]] with additional security was built near the castle on the lava lake, and supplemented by standard [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Secrets of the Empire" /> Around this time, [[Admiral]] [[Gable Karius]] was the leader of the Imperial<ref name="Vader Immortal 1" /> garrison,<ref name="Vader Immortal 2">{{VaderImmortal|II}}</ref> and were considered a [[battalion]].<ref name="Vader Immortal 3" /> In addition to this growth, Karius had under his command a [[Star Destroyer]]-sized [[Interdictor-class Star Destroyer|''Interdictor''-class]], and standard stormtroopers provided security for Fortress Vader as well. Imperial forces were also on [[Corvax Fortress|Lady Corvax's sanctum]] located beneath Fortress Vader on occasion.<ref name="Vader Immortal 1" />

==History==
===Construction of the Fortress===
During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] traveled to [[Mustafar]] to create his own stronghold there. After the [[Mask of Lord Momin]] activated and revealed itself to Vader, construction began on the castle. During construction, a [[garrison]] was created to provide security for [[Fortress Vader]] and construction crews. During this [[time]], the garrison defended the construction site from [[lava flea]]s, which attacked more frequently as each design of the fortress was activated and subsequently destroyed. It wasn't until the ninth design of Fortress Vader the the structure maintained its integrity when activating the locus of [[the Force]] found in the [[Sith cave]], located beneath the fortress. While Vader activated the fortress, the garrison and castle came under attack by a [[Mustafarian]] [[army]] led by [[Father (title)|Father]] [[Kkkt]],<ref name="Dark Lord 23" /> culminating in the [[Battle of Fortress Vader]].<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

Although Captain Junus recommended that the garrison evacuate, Vader joined the frontlines and belayed his orders, instead leading the garrison against the attackers while [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]] was resurrected from the past by his mask when he activated the fortress.<ref name="Dark Lord 23" /> The entire garrison's ground forces were wiped out in a wave of lava created by Father Kkkt, although Vader managed to do the same to the Mustafarians when he re-entered his fortress and activated it once more.<ref name="Dark Lord 24" />

===Providing security===
After the Battle of Fortress Vader, the garrison was reformed to continue to provide security for the fortress. Soon after the completion of construction, the castle's security was involved in a [[Raid on Fortress Vader|raid]] on the castle, when a group of [[Southern Mustafarian]]s led by [[Criakan]] managed to break into the castle, although they were all defeated by Vader. The fortress continued to succumb to raids by Mustafarians during its time.<ref name="Tales from Vader's Castle 5" />

Later on, Mustafarians once again [[Attack on Fortress Vader|attacked]] Fortress Vader, this time albeit by [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Sssl]] and a mind-controlling plague. Mother Sssl managed to attack [[Volcanic environment stormtrooper|lava troopers]] on patrol and have them succumb to the plague. Once again, however, [[Darth]] Vader defeated the Mustafarians and slaughtered them all.<ref name="Return to Vader's Castle 5">[[Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle 5|''Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle'' 5]]</ref>
