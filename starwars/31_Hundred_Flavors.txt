'''31 Hundred Flavors''' was an [[ice cream]] stand on [[Disney Playground Planet]]. It served several different ice cream flavors, including [[Berry Poppins]], [[Bluey Berry]], [[Carbs]], [[Chocolate Chip and Dale]], [[Copyright Crunch]], [[Creamboat Willie]], [[Disney on Ice Cream]], [[Eggnogs and Spoonsticks]], [[Finding Fro-yo]], [[Frozen Eeyogurt]], [[Frutti and the Beast]], [[Fudge Marble Universe]], [[Huey, Chewy, and Gooey]], [[I am Groot Beer]], [[The Lime King]], [[Mango-lorian]], [[Obi-Rum Kenobi]], [[Thor Ragnarocky Road]], [[Turning Red Velvet]], [[Who Fudged Roger Rabbit]], [[Winnie the Peach]], [[Wookies and Cream]], and [[Wreck It Then Ralph]].<ref name="May12th" />
