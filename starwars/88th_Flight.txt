The '''88th Flight''' was a [[starfighter]] [[flight]] that served within the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. The flight consisted of at least three [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]] and was stationed aboard [[Skytop Station]]<ref name="BMF 56" /> on the [[Ruusan 2|second moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Ruusan]], where they engaged Republic forces that [[Raid on Skytop Station|boarded]] the station to rescue the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]]<ref name="Duel">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

The three Vulture droids were destroyed during the Republic mission; [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] mounted one in a station's [[hangar]], causing a second to fire upon it before Tano mounted the second and decapitated it. The third was destroyed when a [[fuel]] canister was thrown by Tano's [[Jedi Master]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], with [[the Force]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] firing on it, exploding and eliminating the remaining Separatist forces.<ref name="Duel" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 88th Flight made its first appearance, although only in the episode's opening newsreel, in "[[Ambush]],"<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> the first episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[October 3]], [[2008]].<ref name="Ambush Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode001.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Ambush}}</ref> It later made its full appearance in "[[Duel of the Droids]],"<ref name="Duel" /> the first season's seventh episode, which aired on [[November 14]], 2008.<ref name="Duel Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/duel-of-the-droids-episode-guide|text="Duel of the Droids" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

The flight was first identified in the fifty-sixth issue of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine, published around [[January 27]], [[2016]].<ref name="BMFdates">{{BuildFalconDates|56}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[88th Flight/Legends|88th Flight]] was originally identified in the 88th Flight Droid fighter card of the [[Clone Wars (PocketModels)|''Clone Wars'']] expansion pack released as part of [[Topps]]' ''[[Star Wars PocketModel TCG]]''<ref name="SWPM">{{SWPM|set=Clone Wars|cardname=88th Flight Droid fighter|link=http://www.swpocketmodels.com/images/shipcw11a.jpg}}</ref> trading card game on [[July 26]], 2008.<ref name="SWPMNews">{{WebCite|url=http://swpocketmodels.com/forum/index.php?topic=1788.0|text=SWPocketModels.com Discussions - The Clone Wars are Coming!
