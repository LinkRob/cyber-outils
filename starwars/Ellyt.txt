'''Ellyt''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who [[Coruscanti|lived]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and had a [[twin]] brother, [[Sym]]. They were both [[Historian|experts]] on the history of Coruscant.<ref name="TCOC" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[The Call of Coruscant]]" takes place concurrently with the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the twins and their friends [[Alees June]] and [[Loran]] went out to clubs in the streets of Coruscant and ran into a [[Jedi]], [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]]. June invited Azzazzo to join them, and they went to a club called [[the Uzzu]], where Sym put his arm over his brother's shoulder. Soon after, they left and went to an apartment with more of their friends, where Azzazzo showed off his [[Azzazzo's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. The Jedi and June then left.<ref name="TCOC" />

Both Ellyt and Sym were wiry and tall, and they each had dark, spiky [[hair]], and [[Color|black]] [[eye]]s. Ellyt wore [[clothing]] with slick, black, blue, and metallic colors unlike Azzazzo's [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]].<ref name="TCOC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ellyt appeared in the short story "[[The Call of Coruscant]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]'' as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="TCOC" />
