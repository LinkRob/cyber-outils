{{Quote|If it isn't the ''young wannabe Jedi'' and his ''so-called Master.'' I'm so ''glad'' we could have this ''small reunion.'' I ''hope'' it will be ''our last.|The Seventh Sister, to Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus|<ref name="Ice" />}}
A '''duel''' occurred on the [[Wild Space]] [[planet]] [[Bilzen]] in [[3 BBY]] between two [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Jedi]] and two [[Inquisitor]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Jedi hunter|Jedi-hunting]] [[Inquisitorius]]. [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], Jedi members of the rebel cell [[Spectres]], traveled to the planet to [[Jedi training|train]] Bridger. They were interrupted by the Inquisitors [[Seventh Sister]] and [[Fifth Brother]], having previously [[Duel in Hammertown|clashed]] with them on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Takobo]]. The rebels [[Lightsaber duel|fought]] with the Imperial agents, with Bridger employing the training he received on the planet. The Jedi eventually made their escape in their [[VCX-series auxiliary starfighter]], the ''[[Phantom]]'', though, due to damage to the [[engine]]s from the Fifth [[Brother (Inquisitorius)|Brother's]] [[Fifth Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]], they [[Comm unit|called]] fellow Spectre [[Hera Syndulla]] for assistance in the ''[[Ghost]]'', a modified [[VCX-100 light freighter]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|An ID9 droid!''"<br />"''I can guess who won't be far behind.|Ezra Bridger and Kanan Jarrus|<ref name="Ice" />}}
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel cell]] the ''[[Spectres]]'', traveled aboard a [[VCX-series auxiliary starfighter]], the ''[[Phantom]]'', to<ref name="Ice" /> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Wild">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Bilzen system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Bilzen]] to continue Bridger's [[Jedi training]]. Bridger attempted to reach out with [[the Force]] and pull an icicle down while Jarrus lectured him that a Jedi must be able to act in all environments, including the [[Temperature|cold]], while, unbeknownst to the duo, an [[ID9 seeker droid]] observed them.<ref name="Ice" /> 

[[File:ID9Discovered-IceBreaking.png|thumb|left|250px|Kanan Jarrus destroys an ID9 seeker droid before the Inquisitors arrive.]]
The ID9 [[Seeker (droid)|seeker]] droid attempted to sneak up on Bridger as the two focused, but Jarrus discovered the [[droid]] and destroyed it with his [[Kanan's blaster|blaster]]. The older Jedi then theorized the [[Inquisitor]]s would not be far behind. The [[Fifth Brother]] and [[Seventh Sister]]&mdash;members of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Jedi hunter|Jedi hunting]] [[Inquisitorius]]&mdash;then revealed themselves, with the Seventh [[Sister (Inquisitorius)|Sister]] referring to the [[Great Jedi Purge|meeting]] as a reunion,<ref name="Ice" /> having previously [[Duel in Hammertown|confronted]] the Jedi in [[Hammertown]] on<ref name="Future">{{Rebels|The Future of the Force}}</ref> the Outer Rim<ref name="Outer">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Takobo system]]</ref> planet<ref name="PlanetTakobo">{{Databank|takobo|Takobo}}</ref> [[Takobo]].<ref name="Future" /> The Seventh Sister and Jarrus exchanged taunts and activated their [[lightsaber]]s while the Fifth [[Brother (Inquisitorius)|Brother]] damaged the ''Phantom''{{'s}} [[engine]]s with<ref name="Ice" /> his<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> own [[Fifth Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Ice" />

==The duel==
===Splitting up===
{{Quote|The ''man'' is ''mine''! Do what you wish with the child.''"<br />"''That won't be a problem.|The Fifth Brother and Seventh Sister|<ref name="Ice" />}}
[[File:BridgerSeventhSisterClash-IceBreaking.png|thumb|right|250px|Ezra Bridger and the Seventh Sister lock blades.]]
The Seventh Sister and Fifth Brother [[Lightsaber duel|attacked]] the rebels, agreeing on the Fifth Brother fighting Jarrus while the Seventh Sister engaged Bridger. Before the Spectres clashed their [[Weapon|lightsabers]] with the Inquisitors, Jarrus reminded his Padawan to recall his training. Both Jarrus and Bridger parried their initial attacks, after which Bridger used his [[Ezra Bridger's first lightsaber|lightsaber-blaster hybrid]] to fire out an ID9 droid assisting the Seventh Sister, impressing the Sister. Meanwhile, the Fifth Brother [[Force push|Force pushed]] Jarrus into the cave wall. Bridger expressed concern for his master while clashing blades with the Seventh Sister, but Jarrus insisted he flee. The Seventh Sister encouraged him to run, excited to chase him.<ref name="Ice" />

The Fifth Brother accused Jarrus of trying to hide his fear while locking blades. The Jedi assured him he lost all his fears when he [[Action at Mustafar|battled]] the Inquisitors' [[Grand Inquisitor|leader]], [[the Grand Inquisitor]], while [[Telekinesis|pushing]] the Fifth Brother away with the Force. The Brother caught him and expressed satisfaction at the fight. Deeper into the cave, the Seventh Sister followed Bridger by [[Force sense|sensing]] his presence and taunted him as she pursued him, encouraging him to surrender. The Padawan encouraged himself to ignore her and use his training from before the Imperial agents arrived.<ref name="Ice" />

As he focused on the icicles overhead, the Seventh Sister offered Bridger the choice of being trained or [[Death|killed]] by her. After she found him, she ignited her [[Seventh Sister's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and demanded an answer. In that moment, the icicles above her began to fall, and while she destroyed them with her lightsaber, Bridger answered her question with a Force push off the edge.<ref name="Ice" />

===Regrouping===
{{Quote|''Still struggling'' to take down the Jedi I see.''"<br />"You''{{'}}ve ''not fared'' any ''better!|The Seventh Sister and Fifth Brother|<ref name="Ice" />}}
[[File:KanaShovesFifth-IceBreaking.png|thumb|left|300px|Kanan Jarrus pushes the Fifth Brother away with the Force.]]
Bridger reiterated he had nothing to learn from the Inquisitor and left her in the cavern below to return to his master. Jarrus and the Fifth Brother continued crossing blades until the Jedi threw [[Stone|rocks]] at the Inquisitor with the Force, pushing him away. As the Fifth Brother recovered, Bridger arrived and, together with Jarrus' instruction, shoved the Brother once more into the wall with the Force. The Padawan informed his master that he had finally adapted to the cold and progressed in his learning. While still validating his Padawan's achievement, Jarrus encouraged the pair to focus on fleeing. Bridger expressed concern over the ''Phantom''{{'s}} engines, which Jarrus assuaged.<ref name="Ice" />

[[File:InquisitorsTrySpinning-IceBreaking.png|thumb|right|350px|The Inquisitor spin their lightsabers to deflect laser fire.]]
The Seventh Sister returned to the group to find the Fifth Brother letting the rebels escape. They questioned each other's ability to kill Jedi. The rebels [[pilot]]ed the ''Phantom'' in reverse towards the cave's exit. As the Inquisitors charged at the [[starfighter]], they deflected the ''Phantom''{{'s}} [[laser]]s with their lightsabers. Bridger warned that<ref name="Ice" /> fellow Spectre<ref name="SWE" /> [[Garazeb Orrelios]] had no luck with that tactic during a previous [[Skirmish aboard a Republic medical station|encounter]], but Jarrus asked for trust. As the [[Starship|ship]] exited the cave, Jarrus [[Comm unit|signaled]] to [[Hera Syndulla]],<ref name="Ice" /> another Spectre,<ref name="Freedom">{{EncyclopediaCite|Freedom Fighters From Lothal and Other Characters}}</ref> to help them. Syndulla arrived in the ''[[Ghost]]'',<ref name="Ice" /> a modified [[VCX-100 light freighter]] that acted as a home for the Spectres,<ref name="SWE" /> and blasted the cave's entrance with the ''Ghost''{{'s}} [[laser cannon]]s, causing a cave-in and capturing the Inquisitors.<ref name="Ice" /> She<ref name="SWE" /> then engaged the [[freighter]]'s [[magnetic lock]] to attach the ''Phantom'' and fled the planet.<ref name="SWE" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|I just ''wish'' we could have ''finished what we started. How'' did you manage to ''lose'' that ''inquisitor?''"<br />"''I'' loosened some ''icicles'' in the ceiling and used them as a ''distraction'' against her. A little falling-ice distraction. See? I ''did finish'' what you ''started''.|Kanan Jarrus and Ezra Bridger|<ref name="Ice" />}}
[[File:InquisitorsEscape-IceBreaking.png|thumb|left|300px|The Inquisitor escape the cave-in after the Jedi flee the planet.]]
Aboard the ''Ghost'', Syndulla remarked that the two Jedi had quite a session of training, which Jarrus agreed with and added that the encounter had better prepared the two for their next fight with the Inquisitors. Bridger went on to compliment Jarrus' training, which his master felt was undeserved, thinking that they had not completed their session. When Jarrus inquired how his Padawan eluded the Seventh Sister, Bridger insisted they did finish their lesson as he had loosened an icicle from the ceiling onto the Inquisitor. Syndulla complimented Bridger as a student and asked if Jarrus had learned anything, to which Jarrus joked that he should not stand under icicles around Bridger.<ref name="Ice" />

Meanwhile, on Bilzen, the Inquisitors freed themselves from the enclosed cave. The Fifth Brother commented that the Jedi were strong in the Force but the Seventh Sister insisted they would be broken once their weaknesses were discovered. The Fifth Brother and Seventh Sister<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> later<ref name="Date" /> [[Battle of Garel|hunted]] the Jedi to<ref name="Legacy" /> the Outer Rim planet<ref name="SWE" /> [[Garel]], though they evaded the Inquisitors' grasp again.<ref name="Legacy" />
