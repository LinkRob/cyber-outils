'''Calamari pizza''' was a variety of [[Pizza/Legends|pizza]] that some [[Merchant/Legends|vendors]] refused to sell due to the existence of the [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Mon Calamari/Legends|Mon Calamari]] [[Species/Legends|species]].<ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> In [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]],<ref name="RobotChickenDate">The second skit in ''[[Robot Chicken: Star Wars Episode III]]'' involving Yarael Poof takes place during the events of {{Film|III}}, which ''The New Essential Chronology'' dates to [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Yarael Poof/Legends|Yarael Poof]] attempted to acquire calamari pizza for the [[Jedi High Council/Legends|Jedi High Council]], only to be refused due to Mon Calamari being people. When Poof returned to the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="RobotChickenIII" /> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] during the [[Great Jedi Purge/Legends|Great Jedi Purge]] with a stack of pizzas, he entered the empty [[Jedi Council Chamber/Legends|High Council Chamber]] while explaining why he was unable to get the calamari pizza.<ref name="RobotChickenIII" />
