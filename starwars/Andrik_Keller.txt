'''Andrik Keller'''<ref name="Script" /> was an [[Er'Kit (species)|Er'Kit]] [[Gender|male]] [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]].<ref name="Tempest Runner" /> He<ref name="Script" /> held the rank of [[Strike]] in the [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]] of [[Tempest Runner]] [[Lourna Dee]], serving aboard the [[starship]] [[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|''Lourna Dee'']]. He was promoted to [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] by Dee during the [[Battle of Galov]] after the Nihil, outgunned and outnumbered by the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] forces, were forced to evacuate the [[starship]]. Keller managed to escape, while numerous other Nihil were captured; Dee herself was among those taken to [[Starlight Beacon]] by the [[Jedi]], who, believing that she had [[Death|died]] in the battle, were unaware of her identity.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />

After learning from [[Unidentified Frizznoth Nihil|another]] escaped Nihil that the survivors had been taken, Keller went to [[Sran]] in the [[Ash Worlds]], hoping to recruit a crew in order to stage a rescue. On Sran, Keller unknowingly encountered former Tempest Runner [[Pan Eyta]], who sought revenge on Dee for betraying him. Having consumed several [[Alcohol|drinks]] on Sran, Keller recounted the events of the battle, revealing Dee's status. Eyta subsequently killed Keller, departing in search of Dee.<ref name="Tempest Runner" />
