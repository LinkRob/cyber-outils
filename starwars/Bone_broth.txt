'''Bone broth''' was a type of [[broth]] [[soup]]. On the [[planet]] [[Sorgan]], it was made using parts of a [[Species|creature]] known as a [[grinjer]].<ref name="Chapter 4" /> [[Hunting|Hunters]] and [[Merchant|traders]] often came to the [[Sorgan Common House]] to sip [[bone]] broth.<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|sorgan-common-house|Sorgan Common House}}</ref>
