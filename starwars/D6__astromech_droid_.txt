'''D6''' was an [[astromech droid]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the lead-up to the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="VR" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|RO}}</ref> D6 was assigned to [[Jory (Rebel Alliance)|Jory]] of [[Red Flight (Rebel Alliance)|Red Flight]] who was deployed to [[Liberation of Erso|assist]] [[Extraction Team Bravo]] in the [[Wobani system]]. During the extraction, D6 was tasked with repairing Jory's top port-side engine so that it would no longer expel smoke after Jory collided with a small asteroid while talking to [[Red Four (Red Flight)|Red Four]].<ref name="VR" />
