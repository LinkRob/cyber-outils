The '''B-series battle droid''' was a mass-produced line of [[battle droid]]s<ref name="Cyphers" /> prominently utilized by the [[Trade Federation]], its allies, and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], most notably during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

The B-series were the primary fighting force for the Separatist Alliance during the Clone Wars, and the counter to the Grand Army of the Republic's [[Clone trooper|clone]] [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]]. Prior to the Clone Wars, the B-series units had most notably been utilized in the [[Battle of Naboo]],<ref name="Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace" /> though they had been used elsewhere.

Most B-series models of battle droids of the Separatist Alliance were shut down during the [[mission to Mustafar]],<ref name="Revenge of the Sith">{{Film|III}}</ref> though some remained in operation, either through reprogramming<ref name="Aftermath" /> or other methods.<ref name="The Last Battle" />
