'''Dhoona''' was a [[human]] individual who was present on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Golak]]<ref name="Bandits" /> at some point between [[18 BBY]] and [[3 BBY]].<ref name="Bandits Date">{{VisionsDate|207}}</ref> At some point during those years, they were present outside [[Golak Mahal]] where they conversed with the [[Species|alien]] [[Maghadi]], before noticing a young girl nearby named [[Rani]] demonstrate [[Force-sensitive|Force-sensitivity]]. After Dhoona went to confront her, they chimed in as well, though his encounter was cut with the arrival of an [[Unidentified Inquisitor (Golak)|unidentified Inquisitor]] who inadvertently instigated a massive brawl between those dwelling outside of the motel. Maghadi and Dhoona began to tussle, before a surprise sandstorm swept through the area, knocking them all to the ground while the Inquisitor and a [[Jedi]]-in-hiding, [[Rugal]], engaged in a duel to the death for the fate of Rani.<ref name="Bandits" />
