'''First squad''' was a [[squad]] of [[clone cold assault trooper]]s that was part of a [[Unidentified platoon (Galactic Republic)|platoon]] of the [[501st Legion]] and under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]]. First squad was among one of the units that fought in the [[One-Day War]] against [[Thi-Sen]] and his [[Talz]] warriors during the [[Clone Wars]] after [[Chairman]] [[Chi Cho]] of [[Pantora]] instigated a war between their forces and the Talz.<ref name="Trespass" />

==History==
After failed negotiations between the two parties, the Chairman ordered Republic forces to follow him and mounted [[speeder]]s. After being surrounded by the Talz and sustaining casualties, including the loss of the [[Pantoran guard]]s and the Chairman sustaining a fatal injury, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] ordered first squad to cover the retreat of Republic forces.<ref name="Trespass" />

By the end of the battle, eleven [[clone trooper]]s from the [[platoon]] were [[Death|dead]] and the rest wounded, including Chairman Cho. After reinforcements arrived and a cease fire was in effect, [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] was given permission by the [[Speaker of the Assembly]] to broker peace with the Talz after Chairman Cho's actions were called out of order, of which Chuchi was successful in accomplishing.<ref name="Trespass" />

==Behind the scenes==
First squad first appeared in the fifteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which was released in [[2009]] and directed by [[Brian Kalin O'Connell]].<ref name="Trespass" />
