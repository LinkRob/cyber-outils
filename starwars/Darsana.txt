'''Darsana''' was a member of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] from the [[planet]] [[Glee Anselm]] who was a strong supporter of the [[Military Creation Act]]. In [[22 BBY]], Darsana attended a Senate session on the bill in the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]] on the Republic [[capital]], [[Coruscant]], bringing their [[repulsorpod]] toward the central podium during the debate.

==Biography==
[[File:GleeAnselm.jpg|thumb|right|250px||Darsana came from Glee Anselm.]]
Darsana was a member of the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] from [[Glee Anselm]],<ref name="Film Novel" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[homeworld]] of the [[Nautolan]] and [[Anselmi]] [[species]].<ref name="GleeAnselm">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure]]''</ref> During the [[Separatist Crisis]] in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Attack of the Clones - The Film Novel]]'' to [[22 BBY]].</ref> Darsana was present in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|main chamber]] of the [[Imperial Senate Building|Galactic Senate Building]] on the Republic [[capital]], [[Coruscant]], during a session debating the creation of a Republic [[army]] to face the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] through the [[Military Creation Act]]. Darsana was strong supporter of the bill to create an army, and their [[repulsorpod]] was one of several that had disengaged from the chamber's walls and surrounded the central podium where [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] sat by the [[time]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] arrived in the chamber following an [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempt]] on her life.<ref name="Film Novel" />

Amidala then [[pilot]]ed her own repulsorpod around those of Darsana and other supporters of the bill, such as Senators [[Orn Free Taa]] and [[Ask Aak]], in order to voice support in Palpatine's statements that the Republic's aims were peace rather than war. After a stunned silence, much of the Senate cheered and applauded Amidala, including some of the bills supporters, but some began to boo when the she then continued to speak out against the bill. Palpatine eventually delayed voting on the bill and ended the session for the [[Standard day|day]]. Not long after the session, the Senate granted Palpatine [[Emergency Powers Act|Emergency Powers]] that allowed him to order creation of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] to fight against the Separatists in the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Film Novel" />
