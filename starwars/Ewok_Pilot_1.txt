'''''Ewok Pilot #1''''' was a comic in the [[Ewok Pilot (comic)|''Ewok Pilot'']] series by [[Roan Novachez]]. It was the premiere issue of the comic, introducing the character of [[Ewok Pilot]] and explaining how he came to fly [[Starfighter/Legends|starfighters]] for the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]].<ref name="exclusive">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]'', Barnes & Noble exclusive edition</ref>

==Origins==
By the time Novachez began attending [[Jedi academy]] at the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], he had already drawn numerous comics in the series. ''Ewok Pilot #1'' went on sale as a collector's item in his second year at the academy, advertised in ''[[The Padawan Observer]]'', Vol. MXIII #8. It was available for one [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credit]] from the [[Padawan Observer Office]] and would be delivered within six to eight weeks of payment being received.<ref name="return">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''</ref>

==Story==
The story opens with two [[Human/Legends|Human]] starfighter [[Pilot/Legends|pilots]], one of whom has just crashed on a remote [[Planet/Legends|planet]]. The second pilot saw the other's starfighter crash and offers to help him back in his starfighter. However, before they can go anywhere, the starfighter is stolen by a mysterious individual that is later identified as an [[Ewok/Legends|Ewok]], who flies away with a happy "Ee chee wa maa!!" ("Wow, gee whiz!")<ref name="galactic">[[Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide (real-life book)|''Galactic Phrase Book & Travel Guide'']]</ref><ref name="exclusive" />

Back at base, the two pilots speak with their [[Commander/Legends|commander]], who seems to have taken a shine to the young Ewok. They express their doubts, but the commander offers the Ewok a starfighter of his very own. "Chaaa" ("this one") indicates the Ewok.<ref name="galactic" /> The Ewok, dubbed simply "Ewok Pilot," soon begins flight lessons, as the two pilots try to teach him how to stay in formation and avoid asteroids. Ewok Pilot has a rather unconventional flight style that leads one of the two pilots to wonder if he will be more dangerous to himself or to them.<ref name="exclusive" />

Later, an [[Distress beacon/Legends|emergency distress beacon]] is received from [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. Ewok Pilot is the first to arrive, saving a woman and her baby. The commander congratulates Ewok Pilot on saving everyone. Ewok Pilot shouts a happy "Allayloo!" (Celebrate!)<ref name="galactic" /> while one of the two pilots facepalms.<ref name="exclusive" />
