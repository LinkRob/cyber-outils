'''''Dark Horse Comics'' 8''' was a preview [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] issue, published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] in [[March]] of [[1993]].

==Contents==
Dark Horse Comics 8 previewed several comic issues which were yet to be released in 1993. Included in this issue were excerpts from:
*''Who is X, Part 1 of 3''
*''Robocop: Invasions - Part 3 of 4''
*''James Bond Light of My Death - Part 1 of 4''
*[[Tales of the Jedi 3|''The Saga of Nomi Sunrider, Part 2 of 3'']]

Even though the content of the except from ''The Saga of Nomi Sunrider, Part 2 of 3'' is the same as what would eventually be released, a main difference can be seen in the colors of the excerpt in ''Dark Horse Comics 8''. Compared with the colors in the final release of ''The Saga of Nomi Sunrider'', the colors in ''Dark Horse Comics 8'' are much more vibrant and bright, as compared with the final released version which have more of an earth-tone, less vibrant touch to it.
