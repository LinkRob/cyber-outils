A '''ceiling fan''' was a motorized fan attached to a ceiling for cooling those inside the room. [[Canto Bight]] private [[detective]] [[Neepers Panpick]] once used reward money he received from a [[Alissyndrex delga Cantonica Provincion|countess]] to purchase a ceiling fan for his office.<ref name="CB">{{StoryCite|story=Hear Nothing, See Nothing, Say Nothing|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>
