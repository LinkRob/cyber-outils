{{Quote|We are lords. ''Crime'' lords.|Maul|<ref name="Revival (episode)">{{TCW|Revival (episode)}}</ref>}}
A '''crime lord''',<ref name="Jabba's Palace DB">{{Databank|jabba-the-hutts-palace|Jabba the Hutt's Palace}}</ref> also known as a '''crime lady''',<ref name="Han & Chewie">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Han & Chewie Adventure]]''</ref> '''crime kingpin''',<ref name="Absolutely Everything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> or '''crime boss''',<ref name="Star Wars 71">[[Star Wars (2015) 71|''Star Wars'' (2015) 71]]</ref> was someone who ruled over a criminal organization.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

The [[Sith Lord]]s [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]] became crime [[lord]]s in their plot to gather multiple criminal syndicates as a [[Shadow Collective|base of power]] for their planned conquest during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Maul DB">{{Databank|darth-maul-biography-gallery|Darth Maul Biography Gallery}}</ref> Maul continued to command [[Crimson Dawn]] into the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>

[[Luke Skywalker]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and [[Han Solo]]&mdash;heroes of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]]<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>&mdash;had to escape greedy crime lords such as [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] in their adventures. The [[desert]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Tatooine]],<ref name="ROTJ" /> the [[swamp]]y world of [[Nal Hutta]], and the [[jungle]] world of [[Teth]] were amongst the many worlds ruled by [[Hutt Clan]] crime lords such as Jabba.<ref name="Hunt for Ziro" />

During the reign of the Empire, [[Cikatro Vizago]] was a [[Devaronian]] crime lord who ran the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]],<ref name="Rebellion" /> whilst the [[Quarren]] [[Lallani]] was a crime lady who boasted [[Mandalorian]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]], and was a rival of Jabba's.<ref name="Han & Chewie" />
