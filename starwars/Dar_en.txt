'''Dar'en''' was a [[Lupr'or]] who lived on the [[planet]] [[Luprora]] around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]. They served as a guide for the [[First Order]] visitors [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] and [[TN-3465]] during the [[Hunt for Rivas|hunt]] for [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]]. Dar'en helped them to salvage technology from an ancient Lupr'or starship. Dar'en later participated in Phasma's assault on the [[R'ora]] [[R'ora stronghold|stronghold]].

==Biography==
Dar'en was an adult [[Lupr'or]] who lived during the [[New Republic Era]]. They lived in the [[Lupr'or Village|Lupr'or village]], which was the home of remaining Lupr'or who had survived the rising sea levels and the threat of the aquatic [[Tsw'ells]] and the [[R'ora]]. Dar'en brought [[Captain]] [[Phasma]], [[TN-3465]], and the [[droid]] [[BB-K8]] before the village leader [[Jair'i]]. After recounting the bleak history of the Lupr'or, Jair'i told them that their "friend" [[Lieutenant]] [[Sol Rivas]] and that he would be [[Death|dead]] soon. After Phasma refused to accept the assumption that Rivas would be killed, she was told that Dar'en would take her and TN-3465 to where the creatures would keep Rivas so they could see how futile it was.<ref name="Captain Phasma 2" />

Dar'en brought Captain Phasma and TN-3465 to the R'ora stronghold, which lay in a lake surrounded by tsw'ell monsters. As Phasma bent down at the water's edge to study the site, Dar'en warned her that they should depart because it was dangerous to be close to the water's edge. When Phasma asked if the creatures had a sleep cycle, Dar'en told her that the creatures slept in the early hours of the morning but for long.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3">[[Captain Phasma 3|''Captain Phasma'' 3]]</ref>

Dar'en accompanied Captain Phasma, TN-3465, and [[Ali'ane's great granddaughter]] to the home of her great grandmother [[Ali'ane]]. While Captain Phasma questioned Ali'ane about the source of her great granddaughter's necklace, Dar'en waited with TN-3465. Later, Dar'en accompanied Phasma, TN-3465, and Ali'ane's great granddaughter to an abandoned Lupr'or starship, the only one that had not been claimed by the sea.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />

Returning to the village, Dar'en misunderstood TN-3465's message to bring everyone, thinking that she only needed to bring the men. Dar'en was present when TN-3465 argued with BB-K8 and told the droid to correct its mistake at the expense of looking stupid. Shortly later, Captain Phasma delivered a rousing speech urging the Lupr'or to fight for their survival against the R'ora. Captain Phasma came up with a plan to neutralize the tsw'ells surrounding the island stronghold by electrocuting them with generators recovered from the ship.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" />

After rowing across the lake, Dar'en climbed with Phasma and TN-3465 up the cliff overlooking the battlements of the R'ora stronghold. While climbing the cliff, Phasma experienced flashback of climbing with [[Torben]], [[UV-8855|Frey]], and [[Siv]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" /> While Dar'en and her people fought the R'ora, Phasma snuck away and killed Rivas, concealing her role in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destruction]] of [[Starkiller Base]]. Deeming the Lupr'or unfit to survive the planet's evolution, Phasma abandoned D'aren and their people to the R'ora and Luprora's brutal environment.<ref name="Captain Phasma 4">[[Captain Phasma 4|''Captain Phasma'' 4]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Dar'en was a Lupr'or with green skin, black hair, and green eyes. They were a head taller than Jair'i, the leader of the Lupr'or. They spoke [[Lupre'en]].<ref name="Captain Phasma 2" /> Dar'en was a dutiful and loyal guide who served Captain Phasma and TN-3465 well. Dar'en was also a good climber.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3" /> Dar'en was also capable of combat and took part in the assault on the R'ora stronghold.<ref name="Captain Phasma 4" /> They had blue overalls with a white collar and red straps.<ref name="Captain Phasma 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dar'en first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Captain Phasma]]'', a [[2017]] four-issue comic published by [[Marvel Comics]] as part of the ''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi]]'' promotional material. She was created by [[Kelly Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marco Checchetto]], and colored by [[Andres Mossa]].
