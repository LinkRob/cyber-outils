It is the moldering core of our overripe fruit and I wish to cut away such rot so as to preserve our sweet remains and of course the seeds within.|Fleet Admiral Gallius Rax, to Grand Admiral Rae Sloane, arguing against re-securing the throneworld|<ref name="LifeDebt" />}}
After the death of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] at the [[Battle of Endor]], a '''civil war''' broke out on [[Coruscant]], the [[capital]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], when celebrating crowds turned into [[Riot in Monument Plaza|riots]] when confronted with brutal repression. In the ensuing [[Standard month|months]], whole sectors of Coruscant freed themselves from [[New Order|Imperial]] rule supported by rebel fighters as the [[Imperial Military]], mostly [[stormtrooper]]s and the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] led by [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]], remained entrenched in government districts. 

The [[New Republic]] imposed a [[blockade]] over the planet, and eventually the fighting reached the [[Federal District]] as Mas Amedda was placed under house arrest inside his Imperial Palace quarters. Once he was freed by rebel youths, he officially signed the surrender of the Empire with the [[Galactic Concordance]], ending the civil war. Amedda was installed as a figure head of a [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] with New Republic overseers.

==Prelude==
{{Main|Riot in Monument Plaza}}
{{Quote|But the battle station was destroyed, Dad! The battle is over!''"<br />"''Jak&mdash;the battle isn't over. The battle is just starting.|Jak and his father Rorak, two citizens at the riot|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
The [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in the [[Core Worlds]] was the historical [[capital]] of [[the galaxy]], sitting as the seat of governance for both the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] that replaced it. As such, Coruscant reaped the benefits of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine's]] rule, receiving the claimed resources and wealth of outlying planets to make life on it more pleasurable. As such, the rulers of the Empire<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> and their wealthy supporters<ref name="Festive">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref> enjoyed the benefits of the oppressive regime,<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> and other [[Coruscanti]]&mdash;even those who were not rich&mdash;took pride in the pleasures offered by life on Coruscant.<ref name="Harvest">{{Andor|Harvest}}</ref> Nonetheless, unrest grew on the part of the Coruscant populace, particularly among the [[alien]] [[species]] of the [[Coruscant Underworld]], as Imperial control grew stronger.<ref name="1000 Levels">{{InsiderCite|151|One Thousand Levels Down}}</ref> For all those who openly walked about Coruscant<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> and took pride in living on the world,<ref name="Harvest" /> there were those&mdash;such as the citizens of [[Level 2046]]&mdash;who were too scared to openly walk the streets.<ref name="JS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

[[File:Corsectrooper.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Coruscant celebrates the defeat of the Empire.]]
During a large [[Battle of Endor|battle]] on the [[Endor|forest moon of Endor]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] defeated the Empire, destroying the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] where Palpatine and his [[Sith]] [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Death|died]]. With both the Emperor and Vader dead, the citizens of [[Coruscant]] celebrated, pulling down statues of Palpatine, overwhelming the [[stormtrooper]]s on the streets, and setting off chimes and [[fireworks]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> In response, [[Coruscant Security Force|Coruscant police forces]] on [[speeder]]s entered [[Monument Plaza]] and opened fire on the crowds without warning, killing many who were present. The [[Coruscanti]] erupted into [[Riot in Monument Plaza|rioting]] and some climbed edifices to jump onto the police speeders. One officer was flung from his speeder to the plaza's ground while another was struck in the head with thrown debris. The other police speeders retreated as blaster fire and fighting erupted across the capital between Coruscanti and the Imperial security forces.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==The fighting==
{{Quote|We were the Anklebiter Brigade from Coco-Town. That means something.''"<br />"''It means two things: zip and squat. People don't even know what we did. You think people care about a buncha orphans who hid in the sewers and messed with the bucketheads and other Imperials? I dunno if you noticed, but we aren't on Coruscant anymore. And even if we were&ndash;so what?''"<br />"''They care. We did more than that. We passed messages. Told them about troop movements. We gave them intel, Iggs. How do you think the rebels retook Coco-Town? That was us.|Streaks and Iggs|<ref name="Aftermath" />}}
During the fighting that erupted, [[Rorak (individual)|Rorak]] was killed by Imperial [[Commander]] [[Orkin Kaw]]. Rorak's son [[Jak]] determined to seek vengeance and joined the [[Anklebiter Brigade]], a group of children familiar with the inner workings of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] and who passed on information to Rebel fighters. He then staged a rescue from police custody of [[Lazula]], the women of [[Level 1313]] [[crime lord]] [[Talvee Chawin]], in order to gain the crime lord's favor. Using the favor as leverage Jak negotiated the procurement of an [[Electromagnetic pulse|EMP]] device that he planned to place at the abandoned [[Dex's Diner]] to disable the power grid underneath the Imperial forces fighting the Rebels for control of [[CoCo Town|Coco-Town]]. Jak hoped to use the loss of power and the subsequent Rebel attack as a distraction to kill Kaw and take revenge for his father's death. The Rebels were eventually able to capture CoCo Town from [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]].<ref name="Aftermath" />

In the ensuing months the [[New Republic]] was formed and re-established the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]] on [[Chandrila]].<ref name="Aftermath" /> Meanwhile Coruscant dissolved into a true state of civil war with some sectors remaining free from Imperial control and its citizens entrenching themselves with Rebel resistance fighters while other sectors actively fought against their neighbors and supported the Imperial forces. As the Empire fractured all over the galaxy, [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] took control of Imperial forces on Coruscant. However, the [[Imperial Navy]] withdrew support from the throne world as [[Fleet Admiral]] [[Gallius Rax]] refused to allow [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], the figurehead of the Navy, to send reinforcements seeing the planet as a part of the decay Rax wished to separate from to make room for his [[First Order|new vision]] of the Empire. In this vacuum, the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] was left to coordinate Coruscant's Imperial forces and so Amedda remained an administrator with little true power in the overall Empire beyond Coruscant.<ref name="LifeDebt" />

The ISB established a nominal blockade around the planet utilizing ISB spaceships and coordinated the efforts of the security forces and stormtroopers. Areas with vital government buildings such as the [[Verity District]] and the neighbouring [[Federal District]] were heavily fortified with checkpoints and barricades manned by stormtroopers and the area skylanes were diverted with ISB craft flying patrols. While the Federal District was relatively secure the other Imperial sectors and fortifications fell prey to continual attacks with the rebel soldiers and Coruscanti fighting a guerrilla war against Imperial authorities. Amedda, in his despair and desperation, sought to turn himself over to New Republic [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. However they refused to take him prisoner instead insisting that he find a way to capitulate the Empire and sign a full surrender.<ref name="LifeDebt" />

As the fighting intensified, Imperial forces steadily lost ground as they had no continual support from outside Imperial forces. Eventually the ISB placed Amedda under house arrest in quarters of the Imperial Palace as the fighting reached the Federal District. A group of youths from the Anklebiter Brigade managed to sneak their way into the palace and freed Amedda. Having lost all hope of Imperial victory in the greater war, Amedda contacted Mon Mothma.<ref name="EmpiresEnd" />

==Aftermath==
{{Main|Coruscant's provisional government}}
{{Quote|Mas Amedda has come out of hiding and wants to sign a cease-fire. He wants to end it. All of it.|Mon Mothma relaying the news of the Imperial surrender of Coruscant|<ref name="EmpiresEnd" />}}
At the [[Battle of Jakku]], the New Republic engaged and defeated vast majority of the Empire's armed forces, though mop-up fighting lasted for several months.<ref name="EmpiresEnd" /> The [[Galactic Concordance]] was signed and saw the surrender of the Empire to the New Republic, with one of the conditions involving the handover of the throneworld of Coruscant to the fledgling galactic government. However, it was decided by the Senate that all worlds would have an equal say in their government, in which member worlds such as Chandrila and [[Hosnian Prime]] would host the Republic's capital on a rotating basis rather than Coruscant alone.<ref name="TFAVisual">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Mas Amedda was installed as a figurehead of a [[Coruscant's provisional government|provisional government]] for Coruscant which was also given membership in the Galactic Senate.<ref name="EmpiresEnd" />
