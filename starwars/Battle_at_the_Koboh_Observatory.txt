In [[9 BBY]], a '''battle''' occurred at the [[Koboh Observatory|Mountain Observatory]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]] between the forces of the [[Bedlam Raiders]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. The raiders quickly gained the upper hand and the Imperials were no match for their forces, their leader the fallen [[High Republic Era]] [[Jedi]] [[Dagan Gera]] taunting the Empire using a [[Stormtrooper]]'s [[comlink]]. Amidst the battle, members of the [[Stinger Mantis crew|''Stinger Mantis'' crew]], including [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]], traveled to the Observatory to confront Gera, having recently [[Second mission to the Shattered Moon|learned]] from Gera's [[Lieutenant]] [[Rayvis]] that Gera was planning to use an [[Abyss compass]] at the Observatory to navigate the [[Koboh Abyss]] and reach the planet [[Tanalorr]] hidden within. After taking down an entire Imperial garrison, Kestis witnessed dogfights between the raider's [[Vulture-class starfighter|vuture droids]] and the Imperial's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] as his companion [[Bode Akuna]] attacked both in a [[Z-95 Headhunter]].<ref name="Survivor" /> 

Kestis fought his way to the top of the Observatory, facing warring stormtroopers and raiders along the way. He and Akuna eventually noticed that the skies were clearing after Kestis defeated [[Urgost]], a raider known as the "Fist of Rayvis," and began to worry that Gera had found the compass. By the time Kestis and Akuna met up, the raiders had defeated almost all of the Imperial forces. After fighting their way to the top of the Observatory, Kestis and Akuna came face-to-face with Gera. Their meeting resulted in a [[lightsaber duel]] in which Gera used [[the Force]] to [[Force vision|mess]] with Kestis's mind and confuse him by exploiting his fear. However, Kestis used this against Gera, making himself appear as [[Jedi Master]] [[Santari Khri]], Gera's old partner, in the shared [[hallucination]] and [[Death|killing]] Gera. As a result, the Abyss compass&mdash;though somewhat damaged&mdash;fell into Kestis and Akuna's hands, and the Jedi decided that they should use Tanalorr to host the [[Hidden Path]] network and train its operatives to fight the Empire. The two then left the Observatory, bound for the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="Survivor" />

The confrontation had an outcome that shifted the balance of power on Koboh. Although the Galactic Empire was already on Koboh, it avoided the planet because it was deemed not to be of sufficient value to justify the cost of confronting a group as powerful as the Bedlam Raiders. With so many casualties, the Raiders were unable to compete with the Empire for control of the planet, resulting in an increase in Imperial troops. Although weakened, the Raiders joined forces with [[Haxion Brood]] to try to eliminate the Jedi who killed their leaders and caused their downfall, Cal Kestis. The attempt failed, ending in more casualties and the final blow to the Bedlam Raiders.<ref name="Survivor" />
