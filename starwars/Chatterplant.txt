'''Chatterplants''' were a type of [[carnivorous plant]] that grew on [[Arbiflux]]. The plants had the ability to produce a chattering sound when disturbed. While living on Arbiflux, [[Lona Aphra]] encouraged her daughter [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli]] to use chatterplants to guard their [[farm]]'s [[nuna]]s, though the younger Aphra had difficulty doing so, due to the [[plant]]s biting her.<ref name="Aphra 32" /> Chelli often spent [[time]] counting dust motes on the chatterplants.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref>
