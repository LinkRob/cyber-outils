The '''DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle''' was a model of [[heavy blaster rifle]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. They were used by regular [[stormtrooper]]s and [[Heavy Weapons Stormtrooper]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]], but they also saw use by other parties, including the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and certain [[bounty hunter]]s.

==Description==
[[File:DLT19RiflePintle-SWL.png|thumb|left|300px|A DLT-19 rifle mounted on an Imperial combat tank]]
The DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle was manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It featured a high rate of fire and could deal heavy damage at long ranges, and was most commonly used for pinning down troops and eliminating large groups of enemies. Two variations of the DLT-19 existed, the [[DLT-19x targeting blaster]]<ref name="Battlefront" /> and the [[DLT-19D heavy blaster rifle]].<ref name="Bandai">{{WebCite|url=http://bandai-hobby.net/sw/jp/products/deathtrooperspecialist.html|text=STAR WARS PLASTIC MODEL|archivedate=20180627005250}}</ref> Both variations were [[longblaster]]s.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> The DLT-19 could also serve as a [[sniper rifle]].<ref name="Helmet Collection 61">{{HelmetCollectionCite|61|Helmets}}</ref>

The [[4-LOM's DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle|DLT-19]] wielded by the [[LOM-series protocol droid|LOM-series]] [[bounty hunter]] [[4-LOM]] was capable of firing [[electromagnetic pulse]]s as well as standard blaster shots.<ref name="Force Arena">''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]''</ref> Via a pindle, a DLT-19 could be mounted atop a vehicle, such as a [[TX-225 GAVw "Occupier" combat assault tank]], transforming the DLT-19 into a turret for a vehicle operator.<ref name="TX-225">{{Legion|set=TX-225 GAVw Occupier Combat Assault Tank Unit Expansion|cardname=DLT-19 Rifle Pintle}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:HanSolo_DLT-19_blaster_rifle.jpg|thumb|right|250px|[[Han Solo]] undercover as a stormtrooper aboard the [[Death Star]], completing the look with a DLT-19.]]
During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the DLT-19 was commonly used by the [[Imperial Military]]. The DLT-19 was used by both regular [[stormtrooper]]s and their more specialized variants, including [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtroopers]],<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Heavy Weapons Stormtrooper]]s,<ref name="Battlefront II" /> [[magma trooper]]s, and [[shadow trooper]]s.<ref name="Battlefront" /> Despite its common usage amongst Imperial ranks,<ref name="A New Hope" /> some [[Heavy Soldier]]s of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] made use of the blaster as well.<ref name="Battlefront" />

The bounty hunter [[Dengar]], who had used the similarly named [[DLT-18 laser rifle]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="IG-RM Thug Droids">{{Databank|ig-rm-thug-droids|IG-RM Thug Droids}}</ref> carried a DLT-19 as his preferred weapon during the Imperial Era.<ref name="Dengar fact file">[[:File:StarWars2020-18-Handbook.png]]</ref> Bounty hunters 4-LOM and [[Zuckuss]], who worked as partners during the Imperial Era,<ref name="WOTBH 4&Z">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss'' 1]]</ref> both made use of DLT-19s.<ref name="Helmet Collection 61" /><ref name="Force Arena" /> Zuckuss occasionally used his DLT-19 as a sniper rifle.<ref name="Helmet Collection 61" />

One [[Unidentified monster droid model|B1-KX monster droid]] who worked as part of the [[Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon]] wielded a DLT-19 during an [[Mission to Brentaal IV (Sabé)|assassination attempt]] against [[Jul Tambor]], a [[Skakoan]] revolutionary. After Tambor dodged a [[missile]] fired from the monster droid's partner, a fellow B1-KX unit wielding a [[missile launcher]], he shot that unit, causing the droid's head to go flying off. Still alive but reduced to its head, that B1-KX demanded their partner avenge them, which the DLT-19-equipped unit promised was coming. However, the DLT-19 unit was beheaded by the Imperial agent [[Sabé]]. Both droids survived, albeit just as heads, and promised the Droid Crush would one day kill Tambor.<ref name="Vader 29" />

After the [[Battle of Jakku|fall of the Empire]], some stormtroopers in [[Gideon's Imperial remnant]] continued to make use of the DLT-19. Two were wielded on [[Trask]] in [[Unidentified Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|a]] [[Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|''Gozanti''-class Assault Carrier]].<ref name="Chapter 11: The Heiress" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DLT-19 heavy blaster rifle first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars:'' Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]<ref name="A New Hope" /> and first identified in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Its original design was based on the German [[Wikipedia:MG 34|MG 34]].<ref name="BeyondArmor">''[[Star Wars: Stormtroopers: Beyond the Armor]]''</ref>
