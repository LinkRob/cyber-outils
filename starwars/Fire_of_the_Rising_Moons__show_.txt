'''''Fire of the Rising Moon''''' was a guest experience at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in Anaheim, California. The nighttime spectacular provided guests with an alternate view of the park's fireworks display set to iconic ''[[Star Wars]]'' music.<ref name="Disneyland" /> It debuted on [[April 6]], [[2024]] as part of that year's [[Season of the Force]] festivities. The show was hosted by original characters [[Tohan Lege]], the "Lore Weaver" (via posthumous [[hologram]]), and his [[astromech droid]] companion [[Bard]].

==Development==
As part of the [[2024]] [[Season of the Force]], ''Fire of the Rising moons'' was first set to debut on [[April 5]], 2024, but due to weather, the official premiere was pushed back to [[April 6]].<ref name="MouseInfo" /> The show was introduced by two new characters, the [[Miraluka]] [[historian]] [[Tohan Lege]], known as the "Lore Weaver", and his modified [[R1-series astromech droid]] [[Bard]]. In the first iteration of the show, a recording of Lege's voice was played in viewing areas across the land by the [[droid]],<ref name="Disneyland" /> who, beginning on [[May 24]], 2024, could be seen overlooking [[Docking Bay 5]], the area containing the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="WDWNT1">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/05/new-bard-droid-arrives-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-in-disneyland-for-fire-of-the-rising-moons-fireworks|text=New Bard Droid Arrives at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in Disneyland for 'Fire of the Rising Moons' Fireworks|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20240524210828}}</ref> Lege was voiced by prolific ''[[Star Wars]]'' voice actor [[Stephen Stanton]].<ref name="SWCJ Panel">{{YouTube|video=gmXgGvJusoU|channel=@NickTierce|name=Nick Tierce|text=Building the Galaxy with Walt Disney Imagineering &#124; Star Wars Celebration Japan 2025 Panel Audio}}</ref>

The show was ideated by the team of [[Michael Serna]] of [[Wikipedia:Disney Live Entertainment|Disney Live Entertainment]], who observed park guests watching Disneyland's nighttime fireworks displays from near the ''Millennium Falcon'' without any musical accompaniment; due to the immersion of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], the land did not play the out-of-universe music. They figured that instead of guests just hearing explosions, they could make a show out of it. Working closely with [[Lucasfilm]] [[Lucasfilm Story Group|Story Group's]] [[Matt Martin]], Serna convinced Disneyland Operations of the vision for ''Fire of the Rising Moons''.<ref name="SWCJ Panel" />

Serna's team quickly learned that reverse engineering music to fit an existing fireworks display is challenging.<ref name="SWCJ Panel" /> Furthermore, they had to create seven separate musical profiles to fit the seven different variations of the fireworks which played throughout the year.<ref name="SWCJ Panel" /><ref name="LeroyCycle">{{WebCite|url=https://www.jeromeleroy.com/latestnews/2025/1/8/full-cycle-for-fire-of-the-rising-moon-at-disneyland|text=Full cycle for "Fire of the Rising Moon" at Disneyland|work=[https://www.jeromeleroy.com/ jeromeleroy.com]|archivedate=20260221215122}}</ref> As such, the team used a wide berth of [[John Williams]]'s music from various ''Star Wars'' projects, including the [[Star Wars saga|Skywalker Saga]] films, [[Tokyo Disneyland]], and Galaxy's Edge. Arrangement was done by composer and long-time Imagineering collaborator [[Jérôme Leroy]].<ref name="LeroyCycle" /> Each program of the musical display was catered to not only match the fireworks, but also tell within it a musical story. For the creation of the show, additional music cues needed to be recorded, which were conducted by frequent collaborator of John Williams, [[William Ross]].<ref name="SWCJ Panel" />

While no official names have been given to the seven variants, names of several were shared by Jérôme Leroy such as "Dark Moons", which corresponded with Disneyland's "Halloween Screams" display. This highlighted more villainous themes such as "[[The Emperor's Theme]]", "[[The Imperial March]]", "[[Ben Solo's theme|Kylo Ren's Theme]]", and "[[Duel of the Fates]]" before transitioning to more heroic themes such as "[[Star Wars Main Title]]" and ending with "[[The Throne Room]]". Another variant was called "Festival of the Stars" and coincided with "Mickey's Mix Magic", in the early months of the calendar year. This opened with the "[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Symphonic Suite|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge Symphonic Suite]]" and included themes from "[[Here They Come!]]", "[[The Asteroid Field]]", "[[March of the Resistance]]" and also ended with "Star Wars Main Title" and "The Throne Room". A third variant was called "Festival of Life", which corresponded with the "Believe... in Holiday Magic" show during Disneyland's winter holiday celebrations. This also opened with the Galaxy's Edge symphonic suite and followed a similar song structure, but included "[[Princess Leia's Theme]]", "[[Han Solo and the Princess]]", and "[[Victory Celebration]]" before ending with the "Star Wars Main Title" and "The Throne Room".<ref name="LeroyCycle" />

In the development of ''Fire of the Rising Moons'', the development team wanted to have some other nighttime spectacular to run on nights when there would not be fireworks. Serna had the idea of creating a projection show using the [[petrified tree|spires]] in the same area. This eventually led to the creation of ''[[Shadows of Memory: A Skywalker Saga]]''. The characters Tohan Lege and Bard were created by Matt Martin to fill a story need for a narrator role.<ref name="SWCJ Panel" />

Starting on [[November 8]], 2024, Disneyland offered a special reservation dining package for ''Fire of the Rising Moons'' called Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo Nighttime Gathering, in which guests would receive an assortment of snack foods along with a souvenir specialty drink and unlimited fountain drinks before attending the fireworks show from an exclusive vantage point overlooking the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/11/reservations-open-new-docking-bay-7-gathering|text=Reservations Open for New Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo Nighttime Gathering|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|author=Ace, Shannen|archivedate=20241208004738}}</ref> The typical menu items included cheeseburger croquettes, assorted [[cheese]]s and [[fruit]], a wonton citrus-ginger chicken salad taco, pastries, [[chocolate]], a specialty-themed non[[alcohol]]ic beverage, unlimited [[Coca-Cola|Coke]], [[Diet Coke]], [[Sprite]], and bottled [[water]], and [[wine]] or [[Ale|beer]] for purchase.<ref name="NighttimeGathering">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=/dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-nighttime-gathering|text=Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo Nighttime Gathering}}</ref> 

In late [[May]], [[2025]], the show was updated to include some projection effects depicting a skirmish between [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] and [[TIE series|TIE fighters]], as well as a [[hologram]] of Tohan Lege, which was previously used in ''[[Shadows of Memory: A Skywalker Saga]]''.<ref name="WDWNT2">{{YouTube|video=jucfV3-uLiU|text=Fire of the Rising Moons with new projections in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disneyland|channel=@WDWNT|name=WDW News Today}}</ref>

In early [[February]], [[2026]], Disneyland quietly discontinued the Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo Nighttime Gathering dining package. The webpage was deleted, and the event page was removed from the Disneyland app.<ref name="Gathering discontinued">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2026/02/disneyland-quietly-removes-fireworks-dining-package-for-galaxys-edge|text=Disneyland Quietly Removes Docking Bay 7 Nighttime Gathering Fireworks Dining Package|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|author=Haughton, Austin|archivedate=20260208205316}}</ref>

==Continuity==
Two original characters were created for the show, Tohan "The Loreweaver" Lege and Bard. These characters were later incorporated into the ''Shadows of Memory: A Skywalker Saga'' show. 

According to Matt Martin, although guests hear out-of-universe music from the ''Star Wars'' films, the music is not considered diegetic. Similar to the film music heard during other Galaxy's Edge attractions such as ''[[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance]]'', the cinematic heights of the show are separate from the story-telling goal of in-universe personal experiences. He explains that the music can be thought of as playing in the guests' heads, rather than playing aloud in the universe of ''Star Wars''.<ref name="Skywalking">{{YouTube|video=1HwqK9UXnZk|text=Season of the Force FIREWORKS Behind-the-Scenes w/ Lucasfilm's Matt Martin & Disney's Michael Serna|channel=@SkywalkingThroughNeverland|name=Skywalking Through Neverland|date=2024-04-10}}</ref>
