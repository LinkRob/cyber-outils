'''Dave Chapman''' is an English puppeteer and actor who worked as one of the two performers for [[BB-8]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], and reprised his role for [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII]].<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://slate.com/culture/2015/12/bb-8-vocal-consultant-ben-schwartz-on-voicing-the-force-awakens-droid.html|text=BB-8 "Vocal Consultant" Ben Schwartz on Getting Into Character and BB-8's Gender|author=Wickman, Forrest|date=2015-12-18|work=[http://www.slate.com Slate]|archivedate=20191019110724}}</ref> He served as the principle performer for [[Proxima]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', and also portrayed a [[Mimban]] guard.<ref name="Solo">{{SW|url=news/secrets-of-the-solo-a-star-wars-story-creature-shop|text=Secrets of the ''Solo: A Star Wars Story'' Creature Shop}}</ref>

During production of the ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' television series, the [[droid]] [[B2EMO]]'s dialogue was originally going to be rerecorded by an actor.<ref name="Breaks down">{{SW|url=news/tony-gilroy-andor-season-1-interview|text=Tony Gilroy Breaks Down ''Andor'' Season 1}}</ref> Writer and executive producer<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> [[Tony Gilroy]] watched a long list of audition pieces for the role until his brother, [[John Gilroy]], decided he likes Chapman's voice the most. The two then called Chapman and told him he would voice the droid, which left him overwhelmed. Chapman also operated B2EMO in the show.<ref name="Breaks down" />
