The '''Blue Coral Divers Clan''' was a [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] of [[witch]]es from the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Bug" /> Like all Dathomirian witch clans, the clan was led by a [[Mother (title)|Mother]].<ref name="CollapseMother" /> They collected [[color]]ful shells and shared their name with [[blue coral]], a species of [[coral]] native to Dathomir. The witch of no coven [[Falta]] once traded with a traveling [[Unidentified Blue Coral Divers Clan witch|member]] of the clan for shells, which she used to create her [[Gender|daughter]] [[Yenna]].<ref name="Bug" /> Sometime between [[20 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"[[Bug (short story)|Bug]]" is set during the [[Clone Wars]] and several [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Dathomir]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the battle to [[20 BBY]], and the end of the Clone Wars to [[19 BBY]]. Thus, the short story must take place between those two [[Standard year|years]].</ref> Falta had traveled to the [[planetoid]] [[Sídi]], where she recounted her encounter with the witch to the [[Youngling|young]] girl known as "[[Bug (Sídi)|Bug]]."<ref name="Bug" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current [[canon]] continuity, the Blue Coral Divers Clan was mentioned in "[[Bug (short story)|Bug]]," a short story written by [[E. Anne Convery]] and published in the [[2020]] anthology book, ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]''.<ref name="Bug" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the clan was mentioned in [[Fate of the Jedi: Backlash|''Backlash'']], the fourth installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi]]'' book series published in [[2010]].<ref name="FOTJ:B">''[[Fate of the Jedi: Backlash]]''</ref>
