The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''9 ABY''', also known as '''3286 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7986''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[galactic recession]] is underway.<ref name="Chapter 1" />
*[[Pirate|Piracy]] becomes an increasing issue along remote [[hyperspace route]]s.<ref name="Real Adventure">{{SkeletonCrew|This Could Be a Real Adventure}}</ref> {{Circa}}<ref name="SkelCrewDate">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref>
*[[Din Djarin]] finds [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] on [[Arvala-7]]<ref name="Chapter 1" /> and [[Incident on Nevarro|escapes]] [[Nevarro]] with the child, beginning a life-changing journey for both.<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref>
*Lured to the [[space station]] by Djarin, [[New Republic]] [[X-wing starfighter]]s<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref> destroy the [[Roost]] station<ref name="Jib Dodger card">{{Topps|set=2023 Topps Star Wars|cardname=Jib Dodger|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/370777/cid/22170344}}</ref> used as a base by [[Ranzar Malk]] and his [[Mercenary|mercenaries]].<ref name="Chapter 6" />
*[[Ahsoka Tano]] corners [[Morgan Elsbeth]] on [[Corvus]] and demands the location of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn."<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref>
*[[Luke Skywalker]] builds a [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Jedi Temple]] on [[Ossus]] and trains Grogu in the ways of the Force before Grogu returns to Din Djarin.<ref name="BOBF Chapter 6" />
*[[Boba Fett]] goes to [[War against the Pykes|war]] with the [[Pyke Syndicate]] and triumphs.<ref name="BOBF Chapter 6" />
*[[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] makes its official [[HoloNet]] debut. [[Aran Tal]] joins the competition.<ref name="Timelines" />
*[[Rieve]] joins the Hunters of the Outer Rim.<ref name="BftA">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]''</ref> {{Circa}}<ref name="Hunters Battle">''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'' states that Rieve is 21 [[Standard year|years]] old during its events. ''[[Hunters: Battle for the Arena]]'' takes place after the fall of the [[Galactic Empire]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]]. Rieve is 21 years old; in a flashback set during the [[Imperial Era]], she is 16 years old. So the novel cannot take place more than four or five years after the Fall of the Empire. As it also takes place after [[Aran Tal]] joined the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to 9 ABY, the book must be set around that year.</ref>
*The [[Mandalorian]]s [[Conflict on Mandalore|reclaim]] their ancient [[homeworld]] of [[Mandalore]] and reignite the [[Great Forge]].<ref name="Chapter 24">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 24: The Return}}</ref><ref name="MandoDate" />
*Din Djarin officially adopts Grogu as his son and apprentice.<ref name="Chapter 24" /><ref name="MandoDate" />
*[[T'onga's crew]] is hired by the [[Black Sun]] syndicate to [[Capture of Stolak Vandello|capture]] [[Stolak Vandello]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 5">[[Halcyon Legacy 5|''Halcyon Legacy'' 5]]</ref><ref name="HL5 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Halcyon Legacy 5|''Halcyon Legacy'' 5]] to [[34 ABY]]. As the flashback events of ''Halcyon Legacy'' 5 take place around 25 [[Standard year|years]] before the main story, they must take place around [[9 ABY]].</ref>
*[[Sabine Wren]] and Ahsoka Tano resume their [[Quest to find Ezra Bridger|search]] for [[Ezra Bridger]]<ref name="Ahsoka Part Two" /> and find him on the [[extragalactic]] planet [[Peridea]]. Called to Peridea by Thrawn's allies the [[Great Mothers]], [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces]] find Thrawn and his [[Thrawn's forces|Imperial remnant]].<ref name="Ahsoka Part Six">{{Ahsoka|Part Six: Far, Far Away}}</ref><ref name="Ahsoka Timeline" />

[[File:Thrawn-AhsokaPart8.png|thumb|right|300px|Grand Admiral Thrawn's return from exile to the galaxy culiminated in a campaign against the New Republic.]]
*Thrawn successfully escapes Peridea and returns with his Imperial remnant to the main galaxy. He leaves Sabine and Ahsoka trapped on the planet; [[Baylan Skoll]] and [[Shin Hati]] also remain on Peridea.<ref name="Ahsoka Part Eight" /><ref name="Ahsoka Timeline" />
**Following his return to [[the galaxy]], Thrawn begins a [[Campaign against Thrawn's forces|campaign]] against the New Republic.<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
*Ezra Bridger returns home and reunites with [[Hera Syndulla]].<ref name="Ahsoka Part Eight" /><ref name="Ahsoka Timeline" />
*[[Silvo's pirates]] attempt to raid and plunder but find slim pickings over the course of several cycles. Upon discovering a single [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] as their reward after a [[Silvo's pirate raid|bloody raid]], Silvo's crew mutiny and overthrow their captain,<ref name="Real Adventure" /> installing [[Brutus]] as their new leader.<ref name="Barrier">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref> {{Circa}}<ref name="SkelCrewDate" />
*Children [[Wim]], [[Neel]], [[KB]], and [[Fern]] from<ref name="Real Adventure" /> the lost planet<ref name="Barrier" /> [[At Attin]] find themselves thrust into adventure after Wim discovers and activates the buried starship ''[[Onyx Cinder]]'',<ref name="Real Adventure" /> aboard which Fern installs herself as captain after meeting its droid crewmember [[SM-33]].<ref name="Barrier" /> {{Circa}}<ref name="SkelCrewDate" />
**On SM-33's advice, the children venture to the dangerous [[shadowport]] of [[Borgo Prime]], where they are captured by Brutus's pirates, discover the nature of their homeworld as a lost planet, and meet the human [[Jod Na Nawood]], who claims himself to be a [[Jedi]].<ref name="Barrier" /> {{Circa}}<ref name="SkelCrewDate" />
