'''Death Star City''' was the nickname given to a stretch of personnel housing that was located in the southern hemisphere of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. [[Death Star]] [[City]] was spread over hundreds of square [[kilometer]]s, housing the Death Star's enlisted members as well as civilian contractors finishing the final construction of the Death Star. Residents of Death Star City were housed in small, strict apartments. The berths of [[Star Destroyer]]s were considered luxurious compared to the apartments.<ref name="Dawn" />

Death Star City had multiple sectors, with each sector containing a commercial district with various markets, repair shops, [[cantina]]s, theaters, arcades, and more establishments that served the needs and wants of residents. A [[black market]] had also taken root in Death Star City, with many types of contraband and illegal entertainment being purchased, despite the attempts by [[agent]]s of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and [[Death Star internal security]] personnel to regularly end the underground market.<ref name="Dawn" /> Death Star City was destroyed, along with the rest of the Death Star, during the [[Battle of Yavin]] when [[Luke Skywalker]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], managed to launch his [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]'s [[proton torpedo]]es into a 2 [[meter]] shaft that led to the main [[reactor]], destroying the [[battle station]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
