'''Altadan Igar''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Army]] as a [[commander]] of an [[Endor shield generator bunker|shield bunker]] on the [[moon]] [[Endor]]. In [[4 ABY]], the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] surrendered himself to Igar shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]]. Igar presented the [[prisoner]] to the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who then ordered the commander to search the surrounding area for Skywalker's allies.

==Biography==
===Early life===
{{Quote|I was young when I went to war, and like you, I was a fool. I fought for my Republic and my home. It seemed romantic at the time, to be caught up in the battles of great houses, of wizards, counts, and queens.|Altadan Igar, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="TMWCLS" />}}
Altadan Igar<ref name="TMWCLS" /> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Geektionary" /> who was born on the [[planet]] [[Kuat]].<ref name="Geektionary French" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], when the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] invaded Kuat, a young Igar joined his local militia to fight against them. As the war wore on, Igar would serve the [[Galactic Republic]], including alongside a [[Unidentified Jedi (Altadan Igar)|member]] of the [[Jedi Order]] in a [[Battle of Celes IV|battle]] on the planet [[Celes IV]]. In the battle, the [[Jedi]] led Igar and the Republic troops in a risky charge to take a [[Separatist fortress]]. They were successful, but many of Igar's comrades died, causing him to [[Anti-Jedi sentiment|resent]] the Jedi and their ways.<ref name="TMWCLS" />

===Bunker commander===
{{Quote|A Jedi's weapon. Or, it was. Their fire is gone from the universe. Their altars are cold. Their temples warp no more children. Since the Clone Wars, I have seen lightsabers in the hands of collectors, cultists, and fools. Which one, I wonder, are you?|Igar, to Luke Skywalker, on Skywalker's lightsaber|<ref name="TMWCLS" />}}
[[File:Tempest1.png|thumb|left|250px|Igar rode aboard an All Terrain Armored Transport.]]
Igar would come to serve the [[Galactic Empire]] as an [[Imperial Army]] [[commander]] during its [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. He was stationed on the [[moon]] [[Endor]] as one of the commanding [[Imperial officer|officers]] of a [[garrison]] assigned to protect an [[Death Star's shield generator|Imperial command bunker]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> The bunker housed an [[SLD-26 planetary shield generator]] that was used to defend the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]] [[superweapon]] while it was under construction in [[orbit]] over the moon.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Alliance sent a [[Endor strike team|strike team]] to Endor to try and destroy the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] in preparation for an all out assault on the [[Death Star]]. Before the attack on the bunker began, the rebel [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]] purposefully surrendered himself to Igar<ref name="RotJ" /> while the officer was on patrol,<ref name="Beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> in hope of being brought to his own [[Parent|father]], the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], whom he hoped to redeem. Igar questioned Skywalker as to whether there were other rebels nearby, but the Jedi lied and told the commander he was alone.<ref name="RotJ" />

When he took Skywalker's lightsaber from him, Igar immediately recognized that Skywalker was a Jedi and made a disparaging comment. Realizing Igar had a history with the Jedi, Skywalker asked Igar about his beliefs, prompting the commander to reveal what he had experienced on Celes IV. He argued that the Jedi way and following the [[Cosmic Force|will of the Force]] was madness, but Skywalker insisted that it was destiny and the will of the Force that he had survived such risky charge made by the Jedi in the battle. They eventually came upon the body of stormtrooper [[Rell (Imperial)|Rell]]. Skywalker explained that he had taken the trooper and many others down, angering one of Igar's troopers named [[Dooze]]. As Dooze brutally hit Skywalker with the butt of his rifle in retaliation, Igar remembered the Jedi on Celes IV and how she had hesitated before leading the charge. Igar then ordered Dooze to stop his attack.<ref name="TMWCLS" />

===Reuniting father and son===
{{Quote|Good work, commander. Leave us. Conduct your search and bring his companions to me.|Darth Vader praises Igar|audio=Vader-Igar.flac|<ref name="RotJ" />}}
[[File:Igar_and_Vader.png|thumb|right|250px|Igar handed Luke Skywalker's lightsaber over to Darth Vader.]]
Igar then rode with Skywalker in a [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] to the bunker's landing pad, where Vader landed in order to meet them. The commander exited the [[walker]] with his [[prisoner]] and three [[stormtrooper]]s onto one of the pad's walkways and then handed Skywalker and the Jedi's [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|lightsaber]] over to the [[Sith]]. He then requested permission to search the surrounding area for Skywalker's allies, having not believed his prisoner's lie.<ref name="RotJ" />

Vader congratulated the commander then ordered him to search as he had requested, so Igar re-boarded the walker with the stormtroopers, leaving father and son alone on the walkway. Shortly after Igar's capture of Skywalker, the rebels attacked the shield bunker and managed to get inside, only to discover that Empire has prepared for their arrival and set up a trap. Despite this, the rebels ultimately managed to destroy the command bunker and shield generator, allowing the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] above the planet to destroy the [[Death]] Star.<ref name="RotJ" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|The Jedi way is madness. If the Force wants you to kill a chancellor, you kill a chancellor. If it wants you to hurt a child, you hurt the child. If it wants you to die, you die. It is destiny as a devouring mouth. We needed a new world. Clear and orderly and free, shaped by words and deeds and will and choice, not destiny and ancient magic. That is the galaxy we have built.|Igar, to Luke Skywalker|<ref name="TMWCLS" />}}
Igar had fair skin, [[Color|brown]] [[hair]] and blue [[eye]]s. When Luke Skywalker lied about not having any allies with him on Endor, Igar saw through the lie and requested permission to hunt the rebels from Vader.<ref name="RotJ" /> He was initially smug when presenting Skywalker to Vader, but became less smug by the time he returned to the walker.<ref name="Beware" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|He was armed only with this.|Igar hands over Skywalker's lightsaber|audio=Igar-Armed.flac|<ref name="RotJ" />}}
Igar wore a gray [[Imperial military uniforms|officer's uniform]] including a [[Hat|cap]], [[belt]] and [[boot]]s. He rode in an All Terrain Armored Transport while on Endor.<ref name="RotJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Igar first appeared in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. He was portrayed uncredited<ref name="RotJ" /> by actor [[William Hoyland]]<ref name="Making of">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref> in the film. The character was first named in the current [[canon]] in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'',<ref name="USW" /> with the name originating in the [[1999]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] ''[[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]]'' in the [[Endor Limited]] set.<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Endor Limited|cardname=Commander Igar}}</ref>
