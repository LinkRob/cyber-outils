The '''''FireStar''-class Orbital Defense Platform''' was a [[battle station]] manufactured by [[Rendili StarDrive]]. It was the predecessor of the [[FireStar II-class Orbital Defense Platform|''FireStar II''-class Orbital Defense Platform]] which was designed to provide a higher concentration of firepower from a smaller, cheaper, and more easily produced [[space station]] than the original.<ref name="Fully Operational" />
