'''Drelomon''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Politics|statesman]] who served as the [[chancellor]] of the [[Ryloth government]] during [[First Order-Resistance War|the war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]]. When the [[Ryloth Defense Authority]] provided assistance to the [[Resistance]], [[Emissary]] [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]] believed that neither Chancellor Drelomon nor [[General]] [[Ishel (Ryloth)|Ishel]] would support the move. When the [[First Order]] became drawn to [[Ryloth]] following the Resistance presence, Drelomon worried that the Resistance had been the cause.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
