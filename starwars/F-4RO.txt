'''F-4RO''' was a [[labor droid]] who worked at [[Kublop Springs spaceport]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Seeing him struggle to load a crate onto a [[starship]], the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]] and [[Nubs]] helped him but accidentally loaded the crate onto a [[pirate]] [[starship]] bound for [[Yarrum Tower]]. The Jedi younglings undertook a mission to recover the crate, bringing them into conflict with the [[Ganguls]]. Following the mission, F-4RO thanked the younglings for retrieving the crate.<ref name="Spaceport Setback" />

==Behind the scenes==
F-4RO first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]'' [[Young Jedi Adventures Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[The Spaceport Setback]]", which premiered on [[March 19]], [[2025]]. He was voiced by [[Taran Killam]].<ref name="Spaceport Setback" />
