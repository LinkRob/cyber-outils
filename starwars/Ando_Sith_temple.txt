A ziggurat-shaped [[stone]] '''Sith temple''' was constructed in a [[swamp]]y area of the [[Planet|world]] [[Ando]] during the [[Jedi-Sith War]]. A [[millennium]] later, during the [[Clone Wars]], the ruined [[Sith temple|temple]] was repurposed as an outpost of the [[Death Wind]] [[terrorist]] group. At some point between [[20 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]], the [[Galactic Republic]] sent [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] on a [[Mission to Ando (Mekedrix)|mission]] to apprehend the Death Wind leader, the former Republic [[commander]] [[Mekedrix]]. Traveling to the temple on a [[boat]], the two [[Jedi]] were led by a [[Unidentified Death Wind member|Death Wind guard]] into the building's interior. There, Kenobi and Skywalker met Mekedrix in the temple's [[throne]] room. After Kenobi attempted to persuade Mekedrix to return with them to the Republic, the rogue commander attacked him, at which point he was [[Death|killed]] by Skywalker.

==Description==
{{Quote|Into the night I go, sent there by the ''winds of death'', and all who feed its currents.|A Death Wind sign hung at the entrance to the Sith temple|<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />}}
[[File:AndoSithTempleEntrance-ObiWan4.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The entrance doorway of the Sith temple]]
A [[Sith temple]] was built on<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Timelines">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]</ref> [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> [[Ando]]. The structure was situated at at overgrown terminus of a [[Water|river]] in a remote area of [[swamp]]lands. It was constructed out of [[stone]] and had a ziggurat-like shape that narrowed toward its top. The temple featured a tall central entrance doorway as well as a long stairway that led up to it from the ground level. The bottom of the stairs connected to a cracked stone walkway that ultimately broke up into separate stone slabs suspended above the surface of the surrounding water. The temple's exterior displayed several sets of runic [[Writing|markings]] that related the building's affiliation to the [[Sith]], an ancient tradition of adherents of the [[dark side of the Force]]. Inside the Sith temple, a large, bare room with a slightly raised wide dais housed a large and ornate stone [[throne]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

A [[millennium]] after its construction, the Sith temple had entered a state of ruin, sporting cracks and missing parts of masonry. The markings on the temple's exterior had deteriorated, and thick, creeping [[Plant|vegetation]] had penetrated from above into the structure's throne room. After the [[Death Wind]] [[terrorist]] group adopted the Sith temple's ruins as an outpost, the water on both sides of the entrance stairway was filled with haphazardly-placed pieces of military technology of the ongoing [[Clone Wars]], such as the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[B1-series battle droid|B1-series]] and [[B2-series super battle droid|B2-series]] [[B-series battle droid|battle droids]] and [[Phase I clone trooper armor|armor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s as well as different-scale models of the Republic's [[Low Altitude Assault Transport|gunship]] and [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|frigate]]. The walkway leading up to the stairs was also lined by torches, and a sign was hung on a set of ropes extending across the base of the stairway. On the sign, a message written in the [[Aurebesh]] writing system espoused the Death Wind's philosophy of worshipping [[death]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

==History==
{{Quote|We may have finally ''found'' him. He and the Death Wind may have taken up a covert position on ''Ando'', among its swamplands.|[[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]], on Mekedrix|<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />}}
[[File:MekedrixThrone-ObiWan4.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Mekedrix met Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker in the throne room of the Sith temple.]]
The Sith temple was built during the [[Jedi-Sith War]], which was fought a thousand [[Standard year|years]] prior to the Clone Wars<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> and saw the eventual [[defeat of the Sith]].<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> During the former conflict, the structure served as a [[Fortress|stronghold]] of dark side&ndash;following [[Force-sensitive|Force-users]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> At some point between [[20 BBY]]<ref name="20 BBY" /> and the end of the Clone Wars<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the terrorist group known as the Death Wind, which was led by former Republic [[commander]] [[Mekedrix]], converted the ruined temple into a covert outpost. The Republic eventually tracked down Mekedrix and dispatched [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] on a [[Mission to Ando (Mekedrix)|secret mission]] to apprehend the rogue commander. The two [[Jedi]] traveled to Ando and then used a [[boat]] to traverse the river leading to the Sith temple. There, they encountered a pair of Death Wind sentries, [[Unidentified Death Wind member|one]] of which confiscated the Jedi's [[lightsaber]]s and then led them up the temple stairs and into the building's interior. Along the way, Kenobi noticed the Sith markings of the temple, realizing that the lingering presence of the dark side of [[the Force]] could encumber his and Skywalker's efforts to persuade Mekedrix to return to the Republic in order to answer for his crimes.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

The two Jedi were then ushered into the temple's throne room where they met the former commander himself. Kenobi pleaded Mekedrix to return with them to the Republic, to which the Death Wind leader replied by explaining his nihilistic philosophical views. After Kenobi in turn encouraged Mekedrix to recall the roles certain individuals from their shared past played in the latter's life, the rogue commander, no longer valuing his own life, leapt to attack his former Jedi compatriot with a [[Mekedrix's sword|sword]]. Skywalker defended his former master by [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] retrieving [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]] from the Death Wind guard and mortally wounding Mekedrix. After the former commander subsequently perished, the two Jedi returned to the Republic and the ongoing war.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> Around [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{Obi-WanFrameDate}}</ref> Kenobi recorded his and Skywalker's mission to the Sith temple in his [[The Journals of Ben Kenobi|journals]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />
