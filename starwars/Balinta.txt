'''Balinta'''<ref name="DftOZ 1" /> was an [[Eriadu veermok]] befriended by [[Sevran Tarkin]], a member of the [[Tarkin family]] of [[Eriadu]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. Tarkin initially set out to [[Hunting|hunt]] one of the creatures to restore her name, though instead realized she could make an ally of the [[veermok]] by showing her own vulnerability. After being reinstated and taking leadership over the family,<ref name="THRA 10" /> Tarkin was rarely apart from Balinta, who would follow her wherever she went. When attempting to learn the meaning behind Tarkin's secretive disappearances, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Qort]] and Tarkin family member [[Emelsine Tarkin]] used Balinta to locate Tarkin.<ref name="DftOZ 1" />
