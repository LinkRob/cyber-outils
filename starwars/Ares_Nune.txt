'''Ares Nune''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual who served as a [[Jedi Master]] within the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="BMF 43" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> following the destruction of Republic [[fleet]]s in a [[Malevolence Campaign|series of attacks]] from the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]'', [[Jedi General]] [[Plo Koon]] ordered Master Nune and a [[Ares Nune's task force|task force]] of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]] to locate and destroy the [[warship]] to prevent further losses.<ref name="BMF 43" />

However, while traveling through<ref name="BMF 43" /> the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Phu system]], Nune and the [[task force]] were intercepted by the ''Malevolence''. From there, Nune's transmissions were jammed by Grievous's [[dreadnought]], who proceeded to bombard the task force, resulting in the Jedi Master's [[death]] alongside the Republic forces.<ref name="BMF 43" />
