'''Doza's office''' was the office of [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]] in [[Doza Tower]] on the ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="The High Tower" /> It was located at the top of the station, with only the long-disused [[Colossus command bridge|command bridge]] above it, and accessible from the hallway below via a short [[turbolift]].<ref name="Secrets and Holograms">{{Resistance|Secrets and Holograms}}</ref><ref name="Into the Unknown">{{Resistance|Into the Unknown}}</ref> When the [[supertanker fuel depot]] was airborne, it could serve as the station's bridge.<ref name="BLE NE2">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-no-escape-part-2-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 7 Fun Facts from "No Escape: Part 2" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> Doza kept his old [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial military uniforms|uniform]] hidden in the office in a closet next to his desk.<ref name="Secrets and Holograms" />
