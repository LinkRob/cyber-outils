"'''Almas, Sweet Almas: A Dark Fortress'''" is an adventure from the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign that was written by [[Adam Burns]] and released in [[May]] [[2002]]. It was the third adventure in the [[Almas, Sweet Almas trilogy]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes must rescue the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] [[Misha Quor-Din]], who has been captured by the [[Believers]] and taken to the [[Almas Sith fortress]].
