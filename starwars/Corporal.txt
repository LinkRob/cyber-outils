'''Corporal''' was a [[military rank]] in [[army]] units.

==History==
Corporal was a [[RDC crewmember|crewmember]] rank<ref name="Pathogen">{{InsiderCite|225|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Pathogen}}</ref> in the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Survivors" /> Serving as a crewmember and [[scientific officer]],<ref name="Pathogen" /> [[Elping Tsoo]], an [[ecologist]] by trade who joined the RDC with hopes of helping [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Survivors" /> after the [[destruction of Starlight Beacon]],<ref name="Pathogen" /> secured the rank of corporal and served under the strict [[Lieutenant]] [[Visma Tarko]]. As he became involved in the [[Nihil conflict]], Tsoo became disturbed by the realities of conflict and believed it was not what he had signed up for.<ref name="Survivors" /> With both he and Tarko trapped behind within [[Occlusion Zone|Nihil territory]], Tsoo suspected that Tarko would have regarded him as someone in the way had he not been a corporal.<ref name="Pathogen" />

[[Unidentified OOM-series security droid corporal|One]] [[OOM-series security droid]] destroyed by [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] held the rank of corporal within the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]].<ref name="tpm" /> [[Comet (clone trooper)|Comet]]<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref> and [[Echo]] were both [[Clone Corporal|clone corporals]] of the [[Republic Military]].<ref name="unfinishedbusiness" /> Though [[Meebur Gascon]]'s rank in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] was [[colonel]], [[WAC-47]] repeatedly referred to him as "corporal." Later, however, as the two came to respect each other, Gascon gave WAC-47 a promotion to the rank of corporal.<ref name="sunny" /> During his time as an [[infantry]]man, [[Han Solo]] served as a corporal in the [[224th Imperial Armored Division]].<ref name="TOG" /> [[Ansin Thobel]] was a [[Death Star Trooper]] with the rank of corporal.<ref name="SWROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
