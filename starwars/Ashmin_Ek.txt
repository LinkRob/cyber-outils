'''Ashmin Ek''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who served as a [[New Republic]] [[senator]] for [[Anthan Spire]] in [[5 ABY]]. He worked on several councils and caucuses including the [[Committee for Imperial Reallocation]]. That year, Ashmin Ek received a bribe in the form of a trade deal from the [[Black Sun]] and [[Red Key Raiders]] to oppose [[Chancellor (New Republic)|Chancellor]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s resolution to send the [[New Republic Defense Force]] to [[Jakku]] to combat the [[Galactic Empire]]. He and four other senators, namely [[Dor Wieedo]], [[Rethalow]], [[Grelka Sorka]], and [[Nim Tar]], voted against the resolution. Ashmin Ek and his fellow senators later changed their votes after a team led by [[Han Solo]] and [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] [[Mission to Nakadia|exposed]] the criminal conspiracy. Posing as an [[Advisor|adviser]] to the Chancellor, Sinjir also offered Ashmin and his colleagues pardons and political favors. The plan succeeded and the New Republic sent its military to [[Jakku]], leading to the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Empire's End" />
