In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''dogfight''' occurred between [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces]] and former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] and [[Fulcrum]] [[agent]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] in the [[Denab system]], where Tano and her apprentice [[Sabine Wren]] discovered the ''[[Eye of Sion]]''. At [[Morgan Elsbeth]]'s command, the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] [[Shin Hati]] and [[Marrok]] led a starfighter squadron against Tano and Wren, and attempted to destroy the ''[[T-6 1974]]'' [[shuttle]] carrying the Jedi and her student. During the dogfight, Tano's [[droid]] [[Huyang]] scanned the ''Eye of Sion'' which was a large [[hyperspace transport ring]], but her [[T-6 shuttle]] was damaged in the process. Hati's [[squadron]] pursued the Jedi to the [[planet]] [[Seatos]] where both parties encountered and maneuvered around several [[purrgil]]. Ultimately the Jedi escaped from Hati and Marrok, whose [[Scout guard|squadmates]] were [[Death|killed]] during the battle.<ref name="Part Three" />
