The '''Angry Bloody Rancor''', also known as '''Bloody Rancor with Candied Meat''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE Date">The events of ''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]'' and those within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> It was a derivative of the [[Bloody Rancor]], and was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" />

==Behind the scenes==
Angry Bloody Rancor is available in the [[In-universe|real world]] to customers at the Oga's Cantina bar in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] at both [[Disneyland]], in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]], and at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]], where it is referred to as Bloody Rancor with Candied Meat.<ref name="Oga's Cantina" /> This version replaced the classic Bloody Rancor cocktail, which is based on the real-world cocktail [[Wikipedia:Bloody Mary (cocktail)|Bloody Mary]].
