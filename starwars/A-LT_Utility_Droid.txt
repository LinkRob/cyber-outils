The '''A-LT Utility Droid''' was a model of domed and tread-wheeled [[droid]]. Although they were designed as [[astromech droid]]s, A-LTs were often adapted for more menial tasks. For example, some had their domes removed, which exposed their cranial circuitry but allowed them to serve [[Foodstuff|food]] and drinks on a tray.<ref name="droid" /> One such unit, [[PA-LT4]], had the said modifications to serve the guests aboard [[Dryden Vos]]' yacht, ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], both the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] used modified A-LTs to bypass walls and overload the circuitry of enemy turrets on the battlefield.<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'' Version 6.1.0''</ref>

The dome of an A-LT was compatible with the body of an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]].<ref name="The Marshal">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|On Jyn’s homestead, Gareth [Edwards] wanted to see some agricultural droids, so we came up with an astromech—something Artoo-esque, but a bit wider, a bit stumpier. He’s dirty and dusty, his dome fogged and yellow from the sun.|Jake Lunt Davies|<ref name="Art of RO">''[[The Art of Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
The A-LT Utility Droid first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Concept art of the droid titled "AGRICULTURAL DROID VERSION 99X" was done by [[Jake Lunt Davies]] and was designed on the wishes of the film's director [[Gareth Edwards]] as a agricultural droid for the [[Erso homestead]] on [[Lah'mu]].<ref name="Art of RO" /> However the droid was ultimately not used in the scene in favor of the<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[SE-2 worker droid]],<ref name="UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> also designed by Lunt Davies.<ref name="Art of RO" /> The A-LT Utility Droid was subsequently first identified in the mobile [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.<ref name="SWC" />
