An '''engineer''' was an individual involved in the design,<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> maintenance,<ref name="TFA">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> or construction of infrastructure,<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> [[starship]]s,<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> or technology. Some ships had a dedicated engineering area.<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> Several companies specialized in engineering, such as the [[Quarren]] [[Free Dac Volunteers Engineering Corps]], [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]], [[Theed Engineering]], and [[Cygnus Spaceworks]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> [[Nenavakasa Nalor]] was a [[Nikto]] engineer during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].<ref name="The Engineer" />
