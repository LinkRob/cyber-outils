A '''checkpoint gate'''<ref name="Kenobi AD">{{Kenobi|Part III}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> was a type of [[laser gate]] used to prevent transit of vehicles and beings. It consisted on two projectors that when active run three powerful laser beams that were powerful enough to cut clean through [[stormtrooper armor]].<ref name="Kenobi" />

The [[Galactic Empire]] had started using the gates<ref name="Kenobi" /> by [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Kenobi date">{{KenobiDate|III}}</ref> with one in use at a [[Mapuzo checkpoint|checkpoint]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]].<ref name="Kenobi" /> The Empire continued to use them at imperial checkpoints through the [[Imperial Era]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planets [[Akiva]] and [[Tatooine]] as well as the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
A checkpoint gate first appeared in the [[Part III|third episode]] of ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' miniseries which released in [[2022]].<ref name="Kenobi" /> The gates later appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The gates appeared as part of the game's wanted system; however in the game, the gates only prevent the player from moving through them instead of cutting through the player<ref name="Outlaws" /> as they are shown to do in the ''Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi'' miniseries.<ref name="Kenobi" />
