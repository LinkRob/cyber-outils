The '''Boosodians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] with [[Color|gray]] skin and twenty [[eye]]s that lived in [[the galaxy]]. [[Amuncie Tidian]] was a Boosodian who worked as a [[Forger (profession)|forger]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In [[9 BBY]], a [[Unidentified Boosodian vendor|Boosodian vendor]] on [[Daiyu]] sold [[Clothing|clothes]] to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] whilst they were [[Search for Princess Leia|escaping the planet]].

Boosodian eyes were able to see a huge portion of the electromagnetic spectrum, which extended from x-rays to radio waves (though the latter was difficult to focus on). Additionally, Boosodians could focus on a specific part of the spectrum that they wished to watch closely.<ref name="TROS VD" />
