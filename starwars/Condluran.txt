'''Condlurans'''<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|freck|Freck}}</ref> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed within [[the galaxy]]. The Condluran [[Briff]] worked as a [[smuggler]] on [[Jedha]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The High Republic 2">[[The High Republic (2022) 2|''The High Republic'' (2022) 2]]</ref> During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Freck]] was a Condluran [[Gender|male]] who worked as a [[transport]] driver for the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[mining]] [[planet]] [[Mapuzo]].<ref name="PartIII" />
