'''Canto Bight Cab Sauv''' was a type of [[wine]] that could be consumed by [[human]]s. The [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' had facilities that served the beverage on tap by the glass or by the carafe<ref name="Sublight Lounge" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> The facilites included the [[starship]]'s [[Sublight Lounge]]<ref name="Sublight Lounge" /> and its [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]] where it could be ordered during breakfast, lunch, and dinner. The wine shared its name<ref name="Crown" /> with [[Canto Bight]], a [[casino]]-filled [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] in the [[Corporate Sector]].<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Canto Bight Cab Sauv was a [[Wikipedia:Wine|wine]] available at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Sublight Lounge" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] beverage is a [[Wikipedia:Cabernet Sauvignon|Cabernet Sauvignon]] made by [https://web.archive.org/web/20220403065208/https://goldschmidtvineyards.com/ Goldschmidt Vineyards] in [[Wikipedia:Sonoma County wine|Sonoma]].<ref name="Sublight Lounge" />
