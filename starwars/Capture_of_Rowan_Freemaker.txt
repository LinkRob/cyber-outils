In [[4 ABY]], [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] was '''captured''' by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. After the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] completed the [[Quest to build the Arrowhead|quest]] to build the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] [[starship]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] used the ship to inflict damage on [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces.

In response, the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] tasked Darth Vader and the Imperial [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]] with hunting down and capturing Rowan. M-OC managed to lure Rowan into a trap on the planet [[Qalydon]]. However, M-OC's victory was stolen by Darth Vader, who disintegrated the hunter droid and took Rowan to the Emperor.

==Prelude==
After the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] received a [[Force vision]] to build a powerful ship called the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']] which [[Quarrie]] believed could turn the tide of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="TTOAN">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Tower of Alistan Nor}}</ref> After building the ''Arrowhead'', the Freemakers used the ship to [[Mission to save the Rebel Fleet|save]] the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]] from the [[Imperial Navy]]. The grateful rebel leadership promoted the Freemakers and treated them as heroes.<ref name="FOTA">{{FreemakerAdventures|Flight of the Arrowhead}}</ref>

Working with the Alliance, the Freemakers used the ''Arrowhead'' to [[Skirmish on Kashyyyk (Freemakers)|destroy]] several [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] and [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] walkers on the planet [[Kashyyyk]]. Gaining favor with the Alliance leadership, Rowan's older brother [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]] was invited to join [[Lieutenant]] [[Colvett Valeria]]'s [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]] while his older sister [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] was promoted to the Alliance's Strategy Council. Kordi and Zander would play an important role in a [[Assault near Alzoc III|devastating raid]] on an Imperial fleet near [[Alzoc III]].<ref name="APR" />

Despite the Freemakers' and Rebel Alliance's good fortunes, Rowan Freemaker grew bored by the lack of action. Meanwhile, the Emperor tasked his second in command [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and the Imperial [[Hunter droid/LEGO|hunter droid]] [[M-OC/LEGO|M-OC]] with capturing Rowan and the ''Arrowhead'', and recovering the [[Kyber Saber]]. Knowing about Rowan's friendship with the [[Lurmen]] [[Gender|girl]] [[Maynar]], M-OC came up with a plan to lure Rowan to the planet [[Qalydon]] by impersonating her and claiming that she was in trouble. Falling for the ruse, Rowan traveled to Qalydon with the reluctant [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] without alerting his family.<ref name="APR" />

==The trap==
Rowan and Roger traveled to Qalydon aboard the Freemakers' ship ''[[StarScavenger]]''. Despite Roger's warnings, Rowan decided to proceed with the mission. Rowan tracked "Maynar's" signal to an Imperial base. While Roger disarmed the two [[stormtrooper]]s guarding the base, Rowan ventured inside only to discover that "Maynar" was a [[hologram]] projection created by the "invisible M-OC.<ref name="APR" />

M-OC and Rowan engaged in a [[lightsaber duel]] but M-OC quickly gained the upper hand since he was able to learn Rowan's moves from previous encounters. Rowan managed to break free by using [[the Force]] to hurl objects at M-OC. Rowan fled through the base's corridors but found the [[blast door]]s shut. Meanwhile, M-OC's rival Darth Vader arrived and sliced Roger across the legs, disabling the droid.<ref name="APR" />

M-OC cornered Rowan in a store room full of crates but Rowan fought back and tried to ambush him. However, he was outmatched by the hunter droid. Before M-OC could take Rowan into custody, he was interrupted by Darth Vader who used the Force to hurl M-OC around and to disintegrate the droid. After beheading M-OC, Lord Vader took Rowan into custody and departed aboard his [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]].<ref name="APR" />

==Aftermath==
Despite being separated from his legs, Roger managed to fly the ''StarScavenger'' back to the rebel flagship ''[[Home One]]'' and warn Zander and Kordi that their brother had been kidnapped.<ref name="APR" />

Meanwhile, Lord Vader brought Rowan back to the Emperor. Though Rowan revealed that he had seemingly destroyed the Kyber Saber, Vader managed to convince the Emperor to use Rowan to help them find more [[kyber crystal]]s for the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]].<ref name="EFC">{{FreemakerAdventures|Escape from Coruscant}}</ref>

Zander, Kordi and Roger embarked on a [[Rescue of Rowan Freemaker|mission]] to rescue Rowan from the Empire. They used the ''Arrowhead'' to fight their way to the [[Imperial Palace]] on [[Coruscant]] where Roger was being held. Despite rescuing their brother, the Freemakers lost the ''Arrowhead'' to M-OC, who had managed to rebuild himself.<ref name="EFC" />
