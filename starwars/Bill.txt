'''Bill''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Sullustan/Legends|Sullustan]] [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan]] who trained at the [[Jedi academy]] at the [[Jedi Temple/Legends|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. Like many other Bith, one of Bill's favorite hobbies was [[Music/Legends|music]]. He was friends with [[Roan Novachez|Roan]], [[Pasha]], and [[Egon Reich|Egon]].

In his first year, Bill ran for student council president against [[Ronald Rinzler]], and Roan made some posters to promote his election upon Bill's request. However, Ronald still won, and Bill was infuriated for a time, before finding a career in music and becoming a D.J. Bill still was mad at Roan before he became a D.J. because he knew Roan helped Ronald as well. Roan did talk with Cronah and Cyrus and he said if Roan was voting for Ronald, so Roan half-nodded because he was going to vote for Bill. Later, Bill was okay and Roan apologized that made Bill happy. He flew in the final flight exam during Roan's second year, but [[Cronah]] made him crash and ditched [[Roan]] during the flight exam, causing them to get a "C+" although Roan got a "C-". He had a talent for understanding [[Droid/Legends|droid]] [[Language/Legends|language]] and was able to explain to Roan at the beginning of the second school year that the reason the [[Astromech droid/Legends|astromech droid]] [[RW-22]] had been following him around and bleeping at him was because his [[shoelace]]s were untied.<ref name="return">''[[Jedi Academy: Return of the Padawan]]''</ref>
