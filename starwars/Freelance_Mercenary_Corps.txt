The '''Freelance Mercenary Corps''' were a new group of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] at the [[time]] of the three-[[Standard year|year]] [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Emerging specifically to serve the Confederacy,<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> the Freelance Corps was hired by [[Magistrate]] [[Passel Argente]] of the [[Corporate Alliance]] to fight for the Confederacy as part of the [[Corporate Alliance Policy Administration Directorate]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Freelance Mercenary Corps was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" /> It was originally created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in the [[2008]] short story [[MedStar: Intermezzo]], which was written by [[Michael Reaves]] and [[Steve Perry]] and published in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' [[Star Wars Insider 83|83]].<ref name="Intermezzo">{{InsiderCite|83|MedStar: Intermezzo}}</ref>
