{{Quote|Well, fellow enthusiasts, it turns out that a droid crew piloting a ship should simply stay away from energy-hungry mynocks.|SF-R3|<ref name="Mynock">{{GoC|Mynocks}}</ref>}}
'''Aree's ship''' was a [[starship]] used by the [[Safari droid]] [[SF-R3]] "Aree" and the [[cam droid]] [[CAM]] as they traveled [[the galaxy]] to study and document the various [[species]] of wildlife for the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]].The Society's logo was featured on the ship. The ship was equipped with a [[hyperdrive]] and [[missile launcher]]s.

==Characteristics==
[[SF-R3|Aree's]] ship<ref name="Mynock" /> was large in size and featured the logo of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]. The outside was primarily [[Color|white]] and orange. The ship was equipped with two [[engine]]s,<ref name="Tauntaun">{{GoC|Tauntaun}}</ref> a [[hyperdrive]],<ref name="Mynocks" />[[missile launcher]]s,<ref name="Tooka" /> a landing platform,<ref name="Nexu" />and a [[spotlight]].<ref name="Mynock" /> [[Missile]]s launched from the ship were able to cause a large explosion.<ref name="Tooka" /> The ship had two landing gear legs that were used for landing on flat surfaces.<ref name="Tauntaun" />The ship had a reinforced rod, a condenser coil, and other cylindrical equipment that were important in keeping the ship operational.<ref name="Charhound">{{GoC|Charhound}}</ref> The ship was capable of quickly turning around by rotating its engines.<ref name="Nexu">{{GoC|Nexu}}</ref> The ship could operate in extremely cold<ref name="Tauntaun" /> or hot conditions.<ref name="Bantha" />

Aree [[pilot]]ed his ship from a chair in the [[cockpit]] at the top of the ship. The cockpit had numerous buttons and a [[holoprojector]] that could display [[hologram]]s.<ref name="Tauntaun" />One of the screens in the cockpit showed a holographic image of the ship to assist in landings<ref name="Bantha">{{GoC|Bantha}}</ref> and to show areas of the ship that were damaged.<ref name="KML">{{GoC|Kowakian Monkey-Lizards}}</ref> A purple button turned on the cockpit's wipers to wipe away any creatures that flew into the ship.<ref name="Mynock" />

The interior of Aree's ship was mostly gray and it was very spacious. The ship had a room for storing a variety of [[foodstuff]]. Also, Aree had a bedroom on the ship that had a bed and [[plant]]s.<ref name="Tooka" />The ship had a room with a large [[power converter]]. There was a lever that opened and closed the ship's [[airlock]]. Aree kept a battery that could be used to restore his energy.<ref name="Mynock" />

==Role==
Aree and [[CAM]] used their ship to travel throughout the galaxy<ref name="Mynock" /> so they could learn about various [[species]] of creatures.<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxy-of-creatures|text=Meet Galactic Wildlife Big and Small in ''Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures''}}</ref> The ship was mainly used for for travel,<ref name="Mynock" /> though it possessed missile launchers capable of causing large explosions. The ship also doubled as a home for Aree.<ref name="Tooka" />

==History==
During the [[High Republic Era|High Republic era]], the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] was founded by a group of [[scientist]]s and adventurers that were interested in researching and cataloging the newly discovered species of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Frontier|frontier worlds]]. For the Society's dangerous assignments, they commissioned [[Serv-O-Droid, Inc.]] to build [[Safari droid]]s to autonomously document and interact with creatures in the wild and then report their findings to the society.<ref name="Activity Sheet">[[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts Activity Journal]]</ref> One of these Safari droids was Aree who traveled [[the galaxy]] in his ship to learn about various creatures.<ref name="Announcement" />

===Hoth===
{{Quote|Now, tauntauns can be used as mounts in extreme cold conditions, but how are we going to tame one of these noble steeds? Well, the Society has sent me to Hoth to find out.|SF-R3|<ref name="Tauntaun" />}}
During or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GOC Date">{{GOCDate}}</ref> the Society sent Aree and the [[cam droid]] CAM to the [[planet]] [[Hoth]]<ref name="Tauntaun" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Hoth DB">{{Databank|echo-base|Echo Base}}</ref> to learn how to tame a [[tauntaun]]. Aree traveled to Hoth in his ship and he parked it outside of a [[cave]] that was inhabited with tauntauns. After two failed attempts at riding a tauntaun, Aree managed to befriend a tauntaun with [[jogan fruit pie]]. The creature allowed Aree to ride it, and it then ran around in circles around Aree's ship. Aree and the tauntaun then rested near the ship, and the tauntaun continued to eat the [[pie]].

===Dathomir===
{{Quote|Greetings from Dathomir, home of the rancor. Speaking of which, our friend over there could use a good old teeth-cleaning, wouldn't you say.|SF-R3|<ref name="Rancor">{{GoC|Rancor}}</ref>}}
After visiting Hoth,<ref name="Tauntaun" /> the Society sent Aree and CAM to the planet [[Dathomir]]<ref name="Rancor" /> in the Outer Rim<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> to learn how to help a [[rancor]] improve its hygiene. After many failed attempts at trying to clean a rancor's [[Tooth|teeth]], Aree discovered that rancors allow their teeth to be cleaned by [[Unidentified blue Dathomir bird|blue birds]].<ref name="Rancor" />

===Tatooine===
{{Quote|Today I've just arrived on the desert world of Tatooine.|SF-R3|<ref name="Bantha" />}}
Afterwards, Aree and CAM flew their ship to the [[desert]] planet of [[Tatooine]]<ref name="Bantha" /> in the Outer Rim<ref name="Tatooine DB">{{Databank|tatooine|Tatooine}}</ref> to learn how [[Tusken Raider]]s were able to keep their [[bantha]] mounts clean. He landed his ship near [[moisture vaporator]]s and two [[bath]]s. After Aree cleaned and groomed a bantha using his various tools, he departed Tatooine in his ship.<ref name="Bantha" />

===Endor===
{{Quote|Greetings, Enthusiasts, from the Forest Moon of Endor.|SF-R3|<ref name="Blurrg">{{GoC|Blurrg}}</ref>}}
Aree and CAM then traveled to the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]]<ref name="Blurrg" /> in the Outer Rim.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Aree herded a group of [[blurrg]]s into a pen by luring them with [[music]].<ref name="Blurrg" />

===Cholganna===
{{Quote|We have to be quiet because we are venturing into the deadly jungles of Cholganna to study a nexu in its natural habitat.|SF-R3 to CAM|<ref name="Nexu" />}}
Aree and CAM then flew to the planet [[Cholganna]]<ref name="Nexu" /> in the Outer Rim<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> to study a [[nexu]] in its natural habitat. After landing his ship in the [[jungle]], Aree saw a nexu become trapped in an electric net by two poachers. The poachers placed the [[feline]] in a [[repulsorcraft]] attached to their [[speeder bike]]s and then drove away. Aree decided that the nexu needed his and CAM's help, so he had CAM fly the ship closely behind the speeder bikes. Aree stood on the ship's landing platform at the back of the ship, and CAM flipped the ship around and flew it closer to the nexu. Aree was unsuccessful in grabbing the nexu, so he jumped on the creature. He was electrocuted by the net, causing him and the nexu to fall off. The nexu then scared the two poachers away. While back on the ship, Aree told CAM,<ref name="Nexu" /> who was recording for the [[HoloNet|Holonet]],<ref name="Activity Sheet" /> that befriending a nexu can keep an individual safe. Aree and CAM then departed Cholganna in their ship.<ref name="Nexu" />

===Naboo===
{{Quote|Voorpaks are the pet choice for the Naboo. But besides being great lap warmers, they don't really do much around the house. The society wants to know if we can train these guys to be guard animals, and I'm here to find out!|SF-R3|<ref name="Voorpak">{{GoC|Voorpak}}</ref>}}
The Society sent Aree and CAM to the planet [[Naboo]]<ref name="Voorpak" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> on a mission to learn if [[voorpak]]s could be trained to be guards. Aree initially failed at training a voorpak, but he discovered that voorpaks were good at guaring and goofing off.<ref name="Voorpak" />

===Lothal===
{{Quote|Greetings, enthusiasts! Aree, here, on the planet of Lothal.|SF-R3|<ref name="Tooka (episode)">{{GoC|Tooka}}</ref>}}
Aree and CAM then traveled to the planet [[Lothal]]<ref name="Tooka" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Lothal">{{Databank|lothal|Lothal}}</ref>, and Aree tried to foster an orphaned [[loth-cat]] so it could be adopted into a permanent home, though Aree fist needed to catch the feline. He brought the loth-cat into his ship and he poured [[foodstuff]] in a bowl, though the lothcat was revolted by the smell. Believing that the presentation was the problem, Aree poured food in a bowl, topped it with a piece of a plant, and placed the bowl on a platter. He presented the food to the lothcat who then smacked the bowl at CAM. Aree then prepared the bowl again and a very large meal for the loth-cat who responded by throwing up. Aree smacked the bowl onto the ground in frustration, causing many [[rat]]s to start eating the food, and then the loth-cat ate the creatures. The loth-cat proceeded to run and jump around in the cockpit. After the loth-cat was tired out, Aree gave it a pillow to sleep on. It instead tried to sleep on the ship's control panel, which activated the warning alarm, caused compartments to open, turned on the cockpit wipers and the spotlights, and launched two [[missile]]s that resulted in a large explosion far from the ship. Aree allowed the loth-cat to sleep in a bed, though it decided to rest on his shoulder.<ref name="Tooka" />

===Elphrona===
{{Quote|Today, the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts has sent use to Elphrona to answer a burning question: do charhounds play fetch?|Aree|<ref name="Charhound" />}}
The Society then sent Aree and CAM to the planet [[Elphrona]]<ref name="Charhound" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="The Rise of Kylo Ren 2">[[The Rise of Kylo Ren 2|''The Rise of Kylo Ren'' 2]]</ref> to learn if wild [[charhound]]s play fetch. Aree used a cylindrical rod to play fetch with the charhound, but the creature's mouth burned the rod. CAM gave Aree a reinforced rod, a condenser coil, and other cylindrical equipment from the ship, though the charhound burned all of them. Aree asked CAM where it got the rods from, and CAM pointed to the ship collapsing from its weight after the rods were removed. Later, there was smoke coming out of the ship.<ref name="Charhound" />

===Asteroid field===
Aree and CAM landed their ship on an [[asteroid field]] in search of mynocks. Aree used a battery to lure a mynock out of a hole in the [[asteroid]], causing many more mynocks to appear. Aree realized that he and CAM were powered by energy, and the mynocks then chased them back to their ship. They then flew away from the asteroid field. The lights then turned off, and Aree discovered that several mynocks managed to sneak on their ship. He then noticed that the mynocks were draining energy from the ship's power converter. The mynocks then drained a lot of Aree's energy, putting him in low power mode. Aree struggled to pull down the lever the release the [[airlock]], and Aree, CAM, the mynocks, and everything else in the ship was blown into [[Realspace|space]]. CAM pulled Aree back into the ship and closed the airlock. CAM managed to get the ship's power back up and plugged Aree into a large battery. While CAM was recording, Aree said that pilots should avoid mynocks, and he turned on the wipers after a mynock flew into his ship. The ship then launched into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Mynocks" />

===Kowak===
{{Quote|Our mission today brings us in search of planet Kowak's most notorious pranksters, the Kowakian monkey-lizards. There's no shortage of laughter between these creatures, but how do you become their friend?|Aree|<ref name="KML" />}}
Aree and CAM flew their ship to the planet [[Kowak]]<ref name="KML" /> in the Outer Rim<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> to learn how to become friends with [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]]s. While he was telling this to CAM, the ship's alarm went off due to the monkey-lizards stealing equipment from the ship. Aree exited his ship and saw one of the engines fall off. Aree offered the monkey-lizards [[fruit]] in exchange for the ship's parts. Instead, the monkey-lizards threw fruit at Aree until he was hit, causing him to enter defensive mode. He threw a fruit at a monkey-lizard, so all of them laughed. This convinced the monkey-lizards to repair Aree's ship, though one of them kept a piece of equipment. Aree and CAM then departed Kowak in their ship.<ref name="KML" />

===Batuu===
{{Quote|Today, we are in the Batuu Wilds to see if we can teach porgs a trick or two. Let's fly on over there, and see if it's possible.|SF-R3|<ref name="Porgs">{{GoC|Porgs}}</ref>}}
Aree and CAM traveled to the [[Batuu Wilds]] on the planet [[Batuu]]<ref name="Porgs" /> in the Outer Rim<ref name="Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref> to learn if was possible to teach porgs a few tricks. Aree wasn't able to teach porgs any tricks, and he was chased by a [[Batuuan braga bear]].<ref name="Porgs" />
