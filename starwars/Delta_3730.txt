'''Delta 3730'''<ref name="BF:ES" /> was a [[Gender/Legends|male]]<ref name="db" /> [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic/Legends|Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="BF:ES" /> They were created on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Kamino/Legends|Kamino]] and [[Cloning/Legends|cloned]] from the [[Clone template/Legends|genetic template]] of [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett/Legends|Jango Fett]]. Like all clones of Jango Fett, Delta 3730 was 1.83 [[Meter/Legends|meters]] tall and had [[Color/Legends|tan]] skin.<ref name="db" /> Delta 3730 wore [[Clone trooper armor/Legends|clone trooper armor]] adorned with colored markings and served in a [[Battalion/Legends|battalion]] under the command of [[Jedi General/Legends|Jedi General]] [[Ferroda]]. While stationed on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], Delta 3730 participated in melee combat practice with [[X2]] as part of a training exercise presented to the new batch of clones in the battalion. The two sparred with [[Electrostaff/Legends|electrostaffs]] and X2 was able to knock out Delta 3730, finishing the fight.<ref name="BF:ES" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Delta3730.png|thumb|right|230px|One of Delta 3730's possible appearances.]]
Delta 3730 appeared in the [[Wikipedia:PlayStation Portable|PlayStation Portable]] version of ''[[Star Wars Battlefront: Elite Squadron]]'', during the tutorial. Their [[Clone trooper armor/Legends|armor]] type is randomized and can be either [[Clone trooper pilot/Legends|clone pilot]] [[Phase II pilot armor|armor]], [[AT-RT driver]] [[Armor/Legends|armor]], or [[Phase II clone trooper armor/Legends|Phase II clone trooper armor]]. The color of the armor's markings are also randomized, and can be either yellow, blue, or dark red. This article does not assume any specific armor type or color to be the correct one.<ref name="BF:ES" />

Within ''Star Wars Battlefront: Elite Squadron'', the name "Delta 3730" can also be randomly assigned to [[List of computer-controlled character names in Battlefront games|computer-controlled clone troopers]] in the various game modes. However, within the game's story-driven campaign mode, the name is always assigned to the clone on Tatooine. As it is the only time the name is seen throughout the entire story, it gives Delta 3730 a consistent and clear history within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, even if the trooper's armor is randomized.<ref name="BF:ES" />
