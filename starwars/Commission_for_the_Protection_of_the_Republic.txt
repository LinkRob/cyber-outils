The '''Commission for the Protection of the Republic''' ('''COMPOR''') was a populist movement and lobbyist group that existed during the [[Republic Era|late era]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Formed in a [[time]] of conflict across [[the galaxy]], COMPOR was responsible for promoting wartime [[propaganda]] to support the Republic against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The organization was overseen by a [[Unidentified director of COMPOR|director]] who, in addition to serving as [[Director|head]] of COMPOR, was also part of the [[Strategic Advisory Cell]]. Its membership included several [[artist]]s such as [[Venthan Chassu]], [[Byno Doubton]], [[Hamma Elad]], and [[Donclode Onstruss]]. They created various pro-Republic propaganda [[Painting|posters]] that COMPOR produced and circulated throughout the [[galaxy]].

As the conflict continued, COMPOR [[Do Not Bind His Hands|lobbied]] the [[Galactic Senate]] to give more authority to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], believing that a more powerful [[Office of the Chancellor|executive office]] would lead to the Republic's decisive victory over the Separatists. By the [[Mission to Mustafar|war's end]], Palpatine [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] himself [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]]. The [[New Order]] absorbed COMPOR by reorganizing the group as a government agency, the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] (COMPNOR), that served as the Empire's propaganda wing throughout the [[Imperial Era]].

==History==
===Establishment===
{{Quote|Yes, Count Dooku succeeded in using the HoloNet for Separatist propaganda purposes, but Republic forces were quick to shut down those Shadowfeeds. If memory serves, COMPOR itself was established as a result of the navy's actions at the time.|[[Dodd Rancit]]|<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>}}
[[File:Boys_in_white.jpg|thumb|left|200px|COMPOR used propaganda to support the Galactic Republic in the Clone Wars.]]
The [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Era|final years]] were marked by the [[Separatist Crisis]] that escalated into a pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] war known as the [[Clone Wars]]&mdash;the first full-scale conflict in a [[millennium]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> Rallying to the Republic's defense, the Commission for the Protection of the Republic (COMPOR) was formed as a populist movement consisting of [[loyalist]]s<ref name="Propaganda" /> who supported the Republic against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="RiseFall" /> In specific, COMPOR was founded in response to the [[Republic Navy]]'s discovery of Separatist [[Shadowfeed]]s broadcasting [[propaganda]] over the [[HoloNet|Holonet]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Its establishment fulfilled the wishes of certain political actors who wanted a way to sure up the citizenry's support for the Republic.<ref name="RiseFall" />

Over [[time]], COMPOR grew in membership and influence as the civil war spread throughout [[the galaxy]], becoming associated with high-ranking [[officer]]s and [[Politics|politicians]] such as [[Admiral]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and [[Senator]] [[Orn Free Taa]], respectively, as well as [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

===Not a republic preserved, but transformed===
During the Clone Wars, the [[Unidentified director of COMPOR|Director of COMPOR]] was one of several Republic officials, such as [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]], who attended a meeting held by the [[Strategic Advisory Cell]] in the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Republic Center for Military Operations]]&mdash;a [[Republic Military|military]] complex located on the galactic [[capital]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Coruscant]]&mdash;prior to the first anniversary of the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. Together, they discussed the [[Project Stardust|clandestine plan]] to create a [[Death Star|mobile battle station]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

Although the war ended with the Confederacy's defeat, the Republic was also brought to an end by Chancellor Palpatine, who [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] himself [[Galactic Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire|First Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> As the [[New Order|new government]] rose in place of the old [[Democracy|democratic]] institution, COMPOR was subsequently reformed into the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] (COMPNOR).<ref name="Tarkin" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], COMPNOR heavily relied on propaganda to support the Empire and its [[Emperor]], much as its predecessor had done for the Republic and its chancellor during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Ideology==
[[File:Do_Not_Bind_His_Hands.png|thumb|right|200px|COMPOR believed that the Republic should unite behind Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine.]]
The mission of COMPOR was to strengthen public morale and support the war effort by presenting and fostering a positive image of the Republic. To that end, COMPOR engaged in the creation and dissemination of propaganda throughout the Galactic Republic. The group was especially supportive of Sheev Palpatine as a wartime chancellor, and produced the propaganda [[Painting|piece]] "[[Do Not Bind His Hands]]" which asserted the position of granting all necessary powers to the leader of the Republic in order to secure total victory. COMPOR also considered emphasizing the Republic's relationship with the [[Jedi Order]] in its propaganda, but ultimately decided against this due to the [[Jedi]]'s reluctance as [[Jedi General|military leaders]]. As such, the poster "[[Jedi Unite for Peace]]" never saw distribution.<ref name="Propaganda" />

In supporting the war effort, COMPOR not only promoted patriotism but also pride in the newly-established armed forces of the Republic.<ref name="Propaganda" /> The [[clone trooper]]s, elite [[soldier]]s of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] for which the Clone Wars was named after,<ref name="ClonesDB">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> became tools for COMPOR's propaganda, leading to pieces such as "[[Unite]]" and "[[Support the Boys in White]]."<ref name="Propaganda" />

COMPOR fostered anti-Separatist sentiment in the general population while promoting civic pride in the Republic. Its propaganda machine churned out various pieces designed to mock the Confederacy leadership and generally portrayed Separatists as evil. The poster "[[Evil Has Its Eyes On You]]" featured two prominent Separatist leaders, [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]], as ghoulish caricatures.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Organization==
COMPOR was organized as a political movement of Republic loyalists from diverse backgrounds, such as [[artist]]s, communications specialists, and writers. Through its membership, COMPOR's influence extended to various levels of the Republic's infrastructure, including media, military, [[Galactic Senate|government]], and even the [[Office of the Chancellor]]. Its recruitment efforts were also successful with younger generations, leading to the creation of [[SAGroup (COMPOR)|SAGroup]], a youth program and sub-branch of COMPOR.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Commission for the Protection of the Republic (COMPOR) was first mentioned in the [[2014]] [[canon]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]].

The [[2016]] canon reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', written by [[Lucasfilm Story Group]]'s [[Pablo Hidalgo]], erroneously refers to COMPOR as the "Commission for the Preservation of the Republic."<ref name="Propaganda" />
