'''Bizu''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|male]] who was the son of [[Yendor Brethen]], a [[pilot]] in both the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[New Republic]]. Yendor was forced to leave Bizu behind on their [[homeworld]] of [[Ryloth]] when he joined the Alliance during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />
