'''''Empire'' 3''' is the third issue in the ''[[Star Wars: Empire]]'' series of [[Star Wars comic books|comics]]. It contains the story "'''Betrayal, Part 3'''," and it was published on [[November 13]], [[2002]] by [[Dark Horse Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''In part three of "''Betrayal''," Darth Vader has landed on a distant world to track down a mysterious Jedi. But, instead of a Jedi Knight, he is confronted by a cadre of bounty hunters who have come to the conclusion that ganging up on&mdash;and defeating&mdash;the Sith Lord will bring them fame and fortune. Boy, are they in for a wake-up call! Meanwhile, on Coruscant, the ranks of those plotting to kill Emperor Palpatine are thinned as the co-conspirators begin targeting each other.''
