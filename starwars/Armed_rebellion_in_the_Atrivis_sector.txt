In [[5 BBY]], an '''armed rebellion in the Atrivis sector''' was fought by the [[Liberators]] in the [[Atrivis sector]], who managed to [[Battle on Mantooine|seize]] the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial garrison (Mantooine)|Mantooine garrison]]. Shortly afterward, the [[Imperial Navy]] counterattacked, swiftly defeating the Liberators on [[Mantooine]]. The [[Atrivis Resistance Group]] was also active, and after due deliberation, soon joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
