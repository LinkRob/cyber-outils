The '''Baltizaar system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Corthenia sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square K-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the celestial body [[Baltizaar]]. As [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and her travel companion [[Amilyn Holdo]] departed the [[planet]] [[Paucris Major]]<ref name="LPOA" /> in [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> they decided to plot a [[hyperspace]] course to Baltizaar and travel onward to the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Coruscant]] from there. However, the pair ultimately abandoned the plan shortly afterward.<ref name="LPOA" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Baltizaar system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Baltizaar system/Legends|system]] was first mentioned in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
