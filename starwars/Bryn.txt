'''Bryn''' was an operative of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] who,<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> attended a meeting of rebel personnel in [[Bright Tree Village]] on the [[moon]] [[Endor]] to discuss the Alliance's next steps after the [[Battle of Endor]]. Once the meeting had finished, Bryn, alongside fellow operatives [[Maxx (rebel)|Maxx]] and [[Kelan]], was instructed by rebel [[Chancellor (Rebel Alliance)|leader]] [[Mon Mothma]] to stay behind for additional briefing. The three peeled away from a group of rebels and moved closer to Mothma and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], before the latter provided each with [[datapad]]s.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bryn appeared in ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'', a [[2022]] novel written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />
