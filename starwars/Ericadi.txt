'''Ericadi''' were creatures native to [[Siline]]. They were considered the [[planet]]'s most dangerous animal and apex predator, and they held deep cultural significance to the [[Silinese]]. Ericadi were ceremonially [[Hunting|hunted]] as part of the [[great hunt]] portion of the planet's [[Crowning Convocation]], which coincided with the eruption of geothermal [[red diamond geyser]]s every seven to ten planetary years and the creatures' migration. Although the traditional great hunt involved [[death|slaying]] the creatures and dissecting out their precious [[liver stone]],<ref name="TCC" /> by the decades leading up to [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> the rules of the great hunt were amended to only tag the creatures with trackers due to ecological concerns.<ref name="TCC" />

==Description==
Ericadi were [[Siline]]'s apex predator. At over three [[meter]]s tall at the shoulder, they were considered the planet's most dangerous species. Ericadi had [[Color|iridescent]] scales as thick as a [[humanoid]] hand, with diamond-sharp edges that covered the creature's body and shifted with motion, wind, and breathing. Ericadi had six spindly three-jointed [[limb]]s that moved slowly, as well as two sets of fast-beating and humming wings, which were not used for flight. They had five deep garnet red [[eye]]s, each the size of a humanoid head, and spiraling rows of needle-like [[tooth|teeth]] strong enough to chew [[crystal]]s, [[mineral]]s and [[Stone|rock]]s in a mouth that was large enough to fit a whole humanoid inside. Surrounding the head was a mane of flexible spines visually similar to [[Silinese]] [[hair]] filaments. These swirled slowly around the head. When an ericadi was angered, it raised the scales on the posterior neck in a display of aggression while making a loud, echoing, gnashing sound.<ref name="TCC" />

A smaller [[Ericadi's freshwater counterpart|freshwater counterpart]] of the ericadi also existed. When first seeing a image of the ericadi, they reminded [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] of giant [[Cicada (creature)|cicada]]s of [[Thies IV]].<ref name="TCC" />

==Behavior==
Ericadi consumed crystals, rocks, and minerals, which included the crystalline [[bone]]s of Silinese individuals. They digested this inorganic material with a specialized organ referred to as a [[liver stone]]. This shimmering black [[diamond]]-looking crystal pummeled minerals while they passed through the creature's digestive tract. The ericadi had multiple distinct lifecycles: They spent the majority of their time in burrows, but when the geothermal [[red diamond geyser]]s erupted every seven to ten planetary years, they made their way to the surface to migrate to [[ocean]] for [[Reproduction|mating]] and nesting.<ref name="TCC" />

==Ericadi in the galaxy==
The ericadi held a deep cultural significance to the Silinese. Historically, the scales and liver stones of the ericadi were harvested due to their financial value. They were also hunted as part of the [[great hunt]], the third and often last stage of the [[Crowning Convocation]], which also coincided with the eruption. In the historical rules, combatants had to kill as many ericadi as possible and collect their liver stones.<ref name="TCC" />

As Siline became [[Silinese goverment|unified]] and the Crowning Convocations became international and more civilized, killing ericadi was outlawed, largely due to ecological concerns of over-hunting. In the contemporary rules, the ericadi, were abducted from their natural migrating paths to the sea and brought to the [[hunting arena]]. There, they were tagged with trackers for [[Scientist|scientific]] studies. In case of emergencies, the Convocation participants were also given [[Hummer (Siline)|hummer]]s that could ward ericadi off, though this would result in their immediate disqualification. Before the rule change, the great hunt was the most deadly part of the Convocation, but the amended rules improved safety and allowed participants to interact with many more ericadi than before. Nevertheless, the great hunt still was a difficult challenge. To tag the ericadi, the combatants would provoke the creature into lifting up their neck scales, allowing for the tag to be being secured in the bone loops underneath the scales. The changes were controversial among the Silinese population due to the lucrative trade of the scales.<ref name="TCC" />

During [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|the Crowning Convocation]]<ref name="TCC" /> in [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the great hunt was secretly sabotaged by the Siline-native [[human]] [[Lionine Graf]]. Graf hired [[Orisent's mercenaries|a group]] of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] led by the off-worlder [[Orisent]] and the Silinese [[Parnix]], in hopes that the sabotage would help facilitate Siline joining the [[Galactic Republic]] by damaging the people's hold on tradition. The conspiracy involved the mercenaries infiltrating the hunting arena, [[slicer|slicing]] the [[cam droid]]s, and releasing a pair of [[lylek]]s as a distraction while they killed and harvested ericadi. A few [[standard hour|hours]] into the great hunt, the conspiracy led to its premature conclusion. In the aftermath, the illegal hunting of ericadi was thwarted by Jedi Padawans Jecki Lon and [[Yord Fandar]], who were able to stymie the poaching and call for help from Silinese authorities and their [[Jedi Master|Masters]].<ref name="TCC" />
