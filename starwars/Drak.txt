'''Drak''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]. He served under [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Leh'ahnyo Vas]] and her [[Leh'ahnyo Vas' master|Master]]. While deployed at a [[farm]]ing village under attack by [[Confederacy military|separatist forces]], Drak saved a [[Gender|girl]] named [[Taya (Tam)|Taya]] and her [[Parent|father]], [[Tam (Taya)|Tam]]. He brought them to cover and defended them from approaching [[battle droid]]s until help arrived.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Sorry I'm late, Drak!''"<br />"''I just expect you to show up after I've done all the heavy lifting.''"<br />"''Well, you are really good at it.|Leh'ahnyo Vas and Drak|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Drop_Zone_EaW.png|thumb|right|280px|Drak served during the Clone Wars as one of the many clone trooper of the Grand Army of the Republic.]]
A [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning|clone]]<ref name="Rise" /> of [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="Clones" /> Drak<ref name="Rise" /> was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] and trained to be a [[clone trooper]].<ref name="Clones" /> Drak served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Rise" />&mdash;fought between [[22 BBY]] and [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>&mdash;in a [[Leh'ahnyo Vas' unit|unit]] led by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Leh'ahnyo Vas]] and her [[Leh'ahnyo Vas' master|Master]]. When [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Confederacy military|forces]] attacked a [[farm]]ing [[City|village]] in a valley, Drak's unit was deployed to the valley to help protect the villagers. While on the ground, he spotted a young girl named [[Taya (Tam)|Taya]] trying to drag her unconscious [[Parent|father]], [[Tam (Taya)|Tam]], to safety. Drak rushed over and picked up the unconscious man, which took Taya off-balance, causing her to fall before being caught by Drak. Drak then placed Tam behind a [[Stone|boulder]] for cover before gently pushing Taya behind the boulder as well.<ref name="Rise" />

Drak told the girl that they were there to help before unslinging his [[blaster rifle]] and opening fire on the unsuspecting [[battle droid]]s. He blew apart three [[droid]]s and swept the formation as the droids sprayed the area with [[blaster]] bolts. Drak continued to calmly mow down the droids, even as they marched closer and closer to the boulder. Just as they were about to be overwhelmed by the droids that were mere [[meter]]s away, Jedi Padawan Leh'ahnyo Vas leaped over Drak's head and deflected a volley of blaster bolts towards the sky. Vas apologized for her late arrival as she cut down battle droids, and Drak replied that he expected her to show up after he had done all the heavy lifting. Together, Drak and Vas defeated the remaining droids, with Drak exploding the last droid's head with a pinpoint shot. Drak then proceeded to cover Vas as she checked on Taya and gave Tam medical attention as they held out until the [[Clone medic|medics]] arrived.<ref name="Rise" />

==Personality and traits==
As a clone of the bounty hunter Jango Fett, Drak shared Fett's [[Color|tan]] skin and height of 1.83 meters (6 [[Foot|ft]]),<ref name="Clones" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" /> He was calm under pressure and did not flinch while fighting.<ref name="Rise" />

==Equipment==
Like the other clone troopers, Drak was implanted with a [[behavioral modification biochip]] by the [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s to ensure that he would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clones" /> Drak wore chipped, white [[clone trooper armor]] and sported bold, red lines on his [[helmet]] which gave it a fearsome, snarling aspect.<ref name="Rise" /> While most clone [[armor]] was made of [[plastoid]],<ref name="Clone Armor DB">{{Databank|clone-trooper-armor|Clone Trooper Armor}}</ref> Drak's armor was made of [[plasteel]]. He was armed with a [[heavy blaster rifle]].<ref name="Rise" />
