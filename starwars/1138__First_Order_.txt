'''1138'''<ref name="TFA" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[First Order military|military forces]] of the [[First Order]] [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The trooper and members of the [[FN Trooper Corps]] participated in the [[Attack on Tuanul|attack]] on [[Tuanul]], a [[Sacred Villages|village]] located on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. After the attack, 1138 checked areas of the village with a fellow stormtrooper and ordered their colleague to fan out during the search, to which the second complied.<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[StarWars.com]] revealed the trooper's identification number and their inclusion in the [[attack on Tuanul]] in its article "From "Blast That X-wing!" to "Traitor!": The Voices of ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens''". According to the article, 1138 and their fellow stormtrooper were voiced by [[James Arnold Taylor]] and [[Sam Witwer]] in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']],<ref name="SW" /> although it is unknown which actor voiced which trooper. However, the dialogue cannot be heard in the actual film.<ref name="TFA" />
