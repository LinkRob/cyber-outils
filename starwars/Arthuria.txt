'''Arthuria''' was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Arthurian]] [[species]],<ref name="FoD Ahsoka & Padmé" /> located in the [[Arthuria system]] in the [[Merthian sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">The events of ''Star Wars: Forces of Destiny&mdash;Ahsoka & Padmé'' take place between the events of "[[ARC Troopers (episode)|ARC Troopers]]" and "[[Heroes on Both Sides]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' and ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' date to [[21 BBY]], respectively. Therefore, it can be deduced that the events of ''Star Wars: Forces of Destiny&mdash;Ahsoka & Padmé'' also take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> during the [[Clone Wars]], the Arthurians were considering allying Arthuria with the [[Galactic Republic]], after they felt that the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] was rude and disrespectful towards their homeworld.<ref name="FoD Ahsoka & Padmé" />
