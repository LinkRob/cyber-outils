'''Achra Station'''<ref name="Showcase" /> was a former [[Trade Federation]] supply [[Space station|station]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[orbit]] around the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. It was used in the [[Clone Wars]] as a [[Fuel|refueling]] station, but was turned into an independent [[Merchant|trade]] hub afterward. Illegal trades and activities ran rampant in the station in the aftermath of the Clone Wars,<ref name="Showcase" /> with black market merchants making money outside of the purview of the [[Satrapy of Myrra]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{Station-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Achra Station appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', which was developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
