The '''Bonadan Embassy''' was the embassy<ref name="SWCL2016" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Bonadan]]<ref name="Scarif">{{EncyclopediaCite|Scarif and Other Planets in the Outer Rim}}</ref> on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], [[capital]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. The embassy was located at the top of a tower in the upscale [[Federal District]] of Coruscant's [[Imperial City|Galactic City]].<ref name="SWCL2016" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[22 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] passed near the building and nearly collided with the joyrider [[Magaloof]] while holding on to the [[assassin droid]] [[ASN-121]] after the [[droid]] had just unsuccessfully [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|attempted]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="SWCL2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Bonadan Embassy was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="SWCL2016" /> from the [[Bonadan Embassy/Legends|embassy's]] first mention in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity: the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]'', which was written by [[Curtis Saxton]] and [[Simon Beecroft]], and illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]].<ref name="InsideAOTC">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Attack of the Clones]]''</ref>
