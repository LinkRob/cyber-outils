'''Five-blossom bread''' was a kind of [[bread]] from the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]] that could be consumed by [[human]]s<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> and [[Artiodac]]s.<ref name="Cookbook" /> During her childhood, [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] used to make the bread, and felt it a speciality of hers. During the [[Clone Wars]], the senator was given some of this bread by a Naboo [[ambassador]]. She saved it for a special occasion and offered it when her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] visited her in her [[Naboo Queen (restaurant)|apartment]].<ref name="Senate Spy" /> [[Saché]], one of Amidala's [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]], considered five-blossom bread to be one of her favorites.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

At some point after the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for five-blossom bread in his cookbook, ''[[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', and began serving it in his [[restaurant]], [[Tuggs' Grub Sienar-Chall Utilipede Transport|Tuggs' Grub]] while it was on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="Cookbook" /> On Batuu, it was served at [[Oga's Cantina]] in the [[city|settlement]] [[Black Spire Outpost]]. At the [[cantina]] it was served with [[Mustard Cream]], or Spicy [[Cheese]] [[Sauce]].<ref name="Oga" />

==Behind the scenes==
Five-blossom bread was first mentioned in [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars television series'']] episode "[[Senate Spy]]," the fourth installment of its [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|second season]]<ref name="Senate Spy" /> that aired on [[October 16]], [[2009]].<ref name="Senate Spy Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode204.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Senate Spy}}</ref> The bread first appeared in ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'', a [[2019]] novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> Five-blossom bread was first pictured in ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', a recipe book released the same year that was authored by [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

In [[2022]], five-blossom bread began being served at the [[In-universe|real-world]] restaurant Oga's Cantina in the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge]] at [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]]. There, the bread was first described as a "Warm [[Wikipedia:Pretzel|Pretzel]] Knot with [[Wikipedia:Hawaii|Hawaiian]] Black [[Wikipedia:Sea salt|Sea Salt]] served with [[Wikipedia:Honey mustard|Honey-Mustard]] Cream Foam and [[Wikipedia:Calabria|Calabrian]] Cheese Sauce."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/breakfast|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Breakfast Menu}}</ref><ref name="WDWNT2022">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/05/review-ogas-cantina-celebrates-may-the-4th-with-new-drinks-and-delicious-five-blossom-bread-at-disneyland|text=REVIEW: Oga's Cantina Celebrates May the 4th With New Drinks and Delicious Five-Blossom Bread at Disneyland|author=Katie Francis|work=WDWNT|archivedate=20221021103929}}</ref> Some time in early [[2023]], the recipe and name of the bread changed at Disneyland. Its new name was [[Garlic]]-Cheese Five Blossom Bread with Spicy Cheese Sauce, and its new description read "Warm Garlic [[Wikipedia:Parmesan|Parmesan]] Pretzel Knot and [[Wikipedia:Chile de árbol|Chile de Arbol]] Cheese Sauce."<ref name="Menu" />
