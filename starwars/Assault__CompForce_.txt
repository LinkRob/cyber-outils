'''Assault''' was one of two [[Division|subdivisions]] comprising the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[CompForce]], the other being [[Observation]]. It was an elite unit that was dispatched on special missions that often appeared to be settling personal vendettas rather than missions crucial to the Empire's security. The training regimen of the Assault subdivision was harsh enough that members who completed it were fortunate.<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
CompForce's Assault group was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2018]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Assault (CompForce)/Legends|Assault]] [[Division/Legends|subdivision]] originated from the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in [[West End Games]]' sourcebook ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', which was released in [[1989]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Sourcebook">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> Assault's first appearance was in "[[Operation: Shadowstrike (adventure)|Operation: Shadowstrike]]," a three-part adventure within West End Games' [[1996]] ''[[Instant Adventures]]'' roleplaying book.<ref name="Shadowstrike">{{WEGCite|story=Operation: Shadowstrike (adventure)|book=Instant Adventures}}</ref>
