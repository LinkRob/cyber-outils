'''Akul''' were an entity known to the [[Togruta]]<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" /> [[species]].<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 5|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 5]] features the [[Battle of Eriadu]], which takes place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Royal Togruta Fleet]] led by the [[Regasa Elarec's throneship|throneship]] of [[Regasa]]<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" /> [[Elarec Yovet]]<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> arrived to reinforce the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Stormwall Defense Fleet]] that was participating in the [[Battle of Eriadu]] against the [[Nihil]]<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" /> [[pirate]]s.<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> After their arrival, the Togruta on the regasa's [[throneship]], began seeing a pack of [[Ghost (folklore)|phantom]] akul, which unbeknownst to them were [[Force vision|illusions]] created by the Nihil [[Children of the Storm|Child of the Storm]] [[H'Tar]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" />

The [[Starship|throneship]] then reported about the akul to Republic [[Commander]] [[Velko Jahen]] on the [[Longbeam-class corvette|Longbeam cruiser]] ''[[Gios]]''. When [[Jedi Master]] [[Keeve Trennis]] asked Jahen to provide<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" /> her<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 3">[[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 3|''The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi'' 3]]</ref> a report, the commander informed Trennis about the phantom akul. Shortly after, H'Tar was [[Death|killed]].<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" />

==Behind the scenes==
Akul were mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi 5|fifth issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic – Fear of the Jedi]]'', released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Fear of the Jedi 5" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[June 4]], 2025.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/122251/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_fear_of_the_jedi_2025_5|text=Star Wars: The High Republic - Fear of the Jedi (2025) #5}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Tooth/Legends|teeth]] of [[Akul/Legends|akul]] were [[Retroactive continuity|retroactively]] established as first appearing in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] [[Star Wars saga|film]] [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> by the [[2006]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]'', written by [[Ann Margaret Lewis (real person)|Ann Margaret Lewis]] and [[Helen Keier]].<ref name="New Essential Guide to Alien Species">''[[The New Essential Guide to Alien Species]]''</ref>
