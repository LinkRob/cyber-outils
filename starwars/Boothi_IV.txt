'''Boothi IV''' was a location<ref name="Visual Guide" /> in the [[Boothi system]], which was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was part of the [[Abrion sector]] and lay in grid square S-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[nori-inkal]], a rare<ref name="Visual Guide" /> [[coral]]-like creature<ref name="Revenge/Justice AD">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> whose fins secreted an [[oil]]y serum that could revitalize aged skin, was from Boothi IV.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> a nori-inkal was kept in a custom tank filled with [[water]] in the [[Olega Apothecary]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Olega]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />
