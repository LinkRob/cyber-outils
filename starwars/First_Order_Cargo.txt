'''First Order Cargo''', previously known as '''Black Spire Surplus''',<ref name="OT Update">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-disneyland-update|text=Darth Vader and Other Original Trilogy Updates Coming to ''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' at Disneyland}}</ref> was a [[First Order]] storage [[hangar]] located within [[Docking Bay 9]],<ref name="What You Need to Know" /> which was on [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|Merchant Row]]<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|formatted=''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge|themed land}}</ref> in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]]. Marked by a nearby [[TIE/es assault shuttle|TIE Echelon]], the establishment, operated by the First Order, sold [[propaganda]] items to residents and visitors.<ref name="What You Need to Know" /> During the [[First Order-Resistance War|conflict]] between the First Order and the [[Resistance]], [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] landed at Black Spire Outpost, intending to find the Resistance operative [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] in [[34 ABY]]. Upon arrival, he [[Force choke|strangled]] one of his officers for incompetence via [[the Force]] in front of a crowd of onlookers.<ref name="VIDEO: Kylo Ren and the First Order Interactive Show at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2019/06/video-kylo-ren-and-the-first-order-interactive-show-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=VIDEO: Kylo Ren and the First Order Interactive Show at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|author=Figueroa, Jessica|date=2019-06-05|work=[https://wdwnt.com Walt Disney World News Today]|archivedate=20200823150531}}</ref>

The business was originally founded as Black Spire Surplus, a military supply shop, by two former [[clone trooper]]s who became close during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Stacks]] and [[Farmer (clone trooper)|Farmer]]. Though one [[cloning|clone]] served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[stormtrooper]], and the other joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the two eventually reconciled and entered business together on Batuu.<ref name="OT Update" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:First_Order_Cargo_SWGE.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Concept art of the store]]
First Order Cargo is one of the stores available in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], where visitors to the park can buy First Order merchandise.<ref name="What You Need to Know" /> Aside from this, it also periodically acts as a live action show area, which depicts Kylo Ren arriving on Batuu in order to track down Rey.<ref name="VIDEO: Kylo Ren and the First Order Interactive Show at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge" />

A refresh to [[Disneyland]]'s version of Galaxy's Edge was announced on [[January 14]], [[2026]] in which much of the [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|Merchant Row]] area would be updated on [[April 29]] of that year to change the timeline to the [[Galactic Civil War]] as featured in the [[original trilogy]]. As such, land theming featuring the [[First Order-Resistance War]], most overtly First Order Cargo, would need updating. The shop received the new name of Black Spire Surplus, and retained its military supply theming, just timeline-appropriate.<ref name="OT Update" />
