The '''Agricultural Corps''', also known as the '''AgriCorps''', was one of the four branches of the [[Jedi Service Corps|Service Corps]] of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="BMF 47" />

The [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] contained the [[Jedi Agricultural Corps research laboratory]], in which flora from other [[Planet|worlds]] was researched.<ref name="completelocations" /> The Agricultural Corps built greenhouses in the [[Gardens of T'alla]] on [[Ossus]] where they grew and experimented with plants.<ref name="NOP" /> Before the [[Great Jedi Purge|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]], a small [[Jedi chapter house (Taanab)|Jedi chapterhouse]] was established in the the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Pandath]] on [[Taanab]] to house initiates in the Agricultural Corps.<ref name="BMF 47" />

Prior to becoming the [[Padawan]] of [[Depa Billaba]], [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] believed he would end up in the Agricultural Corps.<ref name="Force Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force}}</ref>
