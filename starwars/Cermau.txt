'''Cermau''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> located in the [[Cermau system]] in the [[Bakura sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It sat along the [[Bakura Trace]]<ref name="Bakura" /> [[hyperspace route]], and was an endpoint of another route that linked it to [[Batuu]] northward.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>

A [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[freighter]] set to travel from the planet [[Batuu]] to Mokivj listed Cermau as its destination in its navigation systems. When investigating the freighter, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] discovered its false destination, and Skywalker commented that he had not heard of the planet before. Mitth'raw'nuruodo deduced that the freighter's true destination was Mokivj, situated on the route between Batuu and Cermau.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> The author and [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] included Cermau on a map in his book ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''.<ref name="TGTB">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>
