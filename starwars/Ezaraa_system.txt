The '''Ezaraa system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Spinward sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square M-2 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Ezaraa (planet)|Ezaraa]].<ref name="Origin" />
