The '''Daimyo''', also known as the ''' Daimyo of Tatooine''', was the ruler over the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]&mdash;that was maintained through the allegiance and tribute of vassals, such as government officials and proprietors. The title of Daimyo was held successively by the [[crime lord]]s [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], [[Bib Fortuna]], and [[Boba Fett]].

==Powers==
{{Quote|I'm the Daimyo of this district and I will bring order.''"<br />"''You're a crime boss. Just like the rest of them.|Boba Fett and Drash|<ref name="Chapter 3" />}}
[[File:Boba_Fett_and_Guards.png|thumb|left|300px|Daimyo of Tatooine overall was a position of powerful status on the planet.]]
The title of Daimyo was self-appointed; the holder of this title was generally responsible for enforcing laws, maintaining order, and protecting property. Additionally, the Daimyo was a [[crime lord]] who controlled businesses<ref name="Chapter 3" /> and government officials,<ref name="Chapter 2" /> and received tribute from vassals.<ref name="Chapter 1" />

The Daimyo could also grant audiences to their vassals. A less powerful Daimyo relied on alliances with other crime families in order to preserve their status.<ref name="Chapter 3" />

==History==
====Reign of Jabba Desilijic Tiure====
{{Quote|Jabba ruled with fear. I intend to rule with respect.|Boba Fett|<ref name="Chapter 1" />}}
[[File:Jabba_Boonta_Eve.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Jabba Desilijic Tiure was a Daimyo for centuries; his majordomo Bib Fortuna succeeded him briefly after his death.]]
The [[Hutt]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] ruled a [[Jabba's criminal empire|criminal empire]] during the [[Republic Era]]<ref name="TCWFilm">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> and the [[Imperial Era]]<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> as a daimyo<ref name="Jabba Cunning">{{SWU|set=Spark of Rebellion|cardname=Jabba the Hutt|subtitle=Cunning Daimyo|link=5035052619|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20240215160542/https://swudb.com/card/SOR/181/jabba-the-hutt}}</ref> and [[crime lord]]. Having spread his operations and influence across the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and earning great wealth and riches out of various criminal activities such as smuggling and [[Slavery|slave]] trading.<ref name="RotJ junior novel">[[Star Wars: Return of the Jedi (paperback novel)|''Star Wars: Return of the Jedi'' junior novelization]]</ref>

Jabba was harsh, and would demand endless tributes from various local factions and governments on Tatooine in order to keep them in line. These vassals, out of fear, would not dare challenge the ruthless crime lord.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> Many of Jabba's [[captain]]s accrued wealth and riches under the Hutt.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

====Bib Fortuna rule====
{{Quote|He was a terrible leader with no right to the throne.|Fennec Shand, referring to Bib Fortuna|<ref name="Chapter 4">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref>}}
Following Jabba's [[Rescue of Han Solo|demise]], his [[majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]] seized power and amassed servants and slaves of his own.<ref name="Mando Chapter 16">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref> Jabba's former captains quickly left when Fortuna claimed to be the heir. [[Fennec Shand]] claimed that Fortuna was a "terrible leader" with no legitimate claim on Jabba's throne. This resulted in the captains attempting to take his place, but were thwarted by Fortuna's guile and treachery.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

Fortuna's reign ended when he was [[Attack on Jabba's Palace (Boba Fett)|killed]] by [[Boba Fett]]; a former [[bounty hunter]] who worked with Jabba's syndicate<ref name="Chapter 2" /> as well as the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Chapter 6">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref> Fett swore to kill Fortuna for betraying him.<ref name="Chapter 4" />

====Rise of Boba Fett====
{{Quote|You should remember, you serve as long as the Daimyo of Tatooine deem it so.|Boba Fett, to Mok Shaiz|<ref name="Chapter 2" />}}
[[File:Boba_and_fennec_on_the_throne.png|thumb|left|250px|Boba Fett killed Fortuna in order to become the new Daimyo of Mos Espa.]]
In the aftermath of Fortuna's [[death]], Fett founded [[Fett gotra|his own criminal empire]] as the self-proclaimed Daimyo of [[Mos Espa]] and Tatooine. Unlike Jabba and Fortuna, Fett intended to rule through respect, though the people initially did not like the change of power and did not respect him.<ref name="Chapter 2" /> Fett accepted payments of tribute<ref name="Chapter 1" /> and vowed to bring order to the [[Worker's District]] of Mos Espa. The office of [[Mayor of Mos Espa]] lacked real power<ref name="Chapter 3" /> and was therefore subordinate to the Daimyo.<ref name="Chapter 2" /> Unlike Fortuna who enriched [[Mayor]] [[Mok Shaiz]] in an effort to hold onto power,<ref name="Chapter 3" /> Fett attempted to assert authority over the mayor by exacting tribute from Shaiz.<ref name="Chapter 2" /> The [[Water monger|water-monger]] [[Lortha Peel]] offered to double his tribute to the Daimyo in exchange for protection against a [[Mods|street gang]] that had been stealing his [[water]] inventory. Instead, Fett hired the gang after [[Drash]] and [[Skad]] informed him about the unemployment and profiteering in his district. Fett also instructed Peel to lower his prices or relocate his business to [[Mos Eisley]].<ref name="Chapter 3" />

Fett's rule was initially challenged by the arrival of [[The Twins]], who wanted to claim Jabba's territory through kinship. Fett initially refused to cede his lands, so they sent [[Krrsantan]] to kill him. Krrsantan failed in his task, so the Twins apologized by giving Fett a [[Boba's rancor|rancor]]. They stated that the mayor had promised the planet to the [[Pyke Syndicate]], not wanting to start a war over a worthless "rock". Initial attempts of negotiation failed, with Fett agreeing to [[Cobb Vanth|Vanth's]] reinforcements in return for eliminating the [[spice]] trade. This caused Jabba's captains [[betrayal|betraying]] Fett, as they did not approve of his rule in the first place similar to Fortuna. The Pykes attempted to make their presence known on the planet by bombing Sanctuary and hiring [[Cad Bane]] to intimidate the people of Freetown.

This resulted in a [[Battle of Mos Espa|battle in Mos Espa]] that ultimately resulted a victory for Fett. As Fett stood up for his people against the Pykes, they were grateful for his protection and began to respect him.

==Residence==
{{Quote|It was their monastery before Jabba moved in. He's adapted much of their architecture since taking over.|[[Canno Wix]]|<ref name="Escape">''[[Choose Your Destiny: An Escape Mission]]''</ref>}}
[[File:BobaFettsPalace-TMCh16.png|thumb|right|250px|The palace was the fortress of the Daimyo.]]
Daimyos resided in a [[Boba Fett's Palace|large palace]]<ref name="Chapter 1" /> on the fringes of the [[Northern Dune Sea]]<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> in Mos Espa.<ref name="Chapter 1" /> The structure was a fortress, with a defensive design that focused primarily on protection from attack.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> It was once a [[monastery]] that belonged to the [[B'omarr Order]], a [[Religion|religious]] community who believed in isolating themselves from all physical stimuli to enhance their mental powers. The earliest parts of the monastery were underground, and included a mine.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
