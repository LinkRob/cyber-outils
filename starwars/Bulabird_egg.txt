{{Quote|This cheese board showcases the flavors of its host planet&ndash;with deviled bulabird eggs and tintolvies plucked fresh from the trees linin' the crystalline cliffs just north of Hanna City&ndash;as well as favorite snacks from a number of worlds across the galaxy (all of which are included in this collection).|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Bulabird eggs''' were the [[egg]] of the [[bulabird]],<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> a type of [[bird]] that could be found on<ref name="LifeDebt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Chandrila]]. They could be used to make [[deviled bulabird egg]]s, a [[Foodstuff|dish]] in which the solidified egg [[Color|whites]] were filled with a yellow filling with a black garnish on top. The deviled eggs in turn were one of the components of a [[Chandrilan Cheese Board]], a dish which showcased the best [[Chandrilan]] flavors.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the eggs were mentioned in the Chandrilan Cheese Board recipe that the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which he published following a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> taken between [[34 ABY]] and 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:BulabirdEgg-BrickLife.png|thumb|right|150px|alt=A white LEGO egg.|Bulabird eggs appeared in ''LEGO Fortnite Brick Life''.]]
Bulabird eggs were mentioned in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe in which they are mentioned uses large [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|eggs]] as the real-world equivalent.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> They first appeared in the non-[[canon]] [[Epic Games]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Fortnite Brick Life]]'' as part of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' Festival update<ref name="BrickLife">''[[LEGO Fortnite Brick Life]]''</ref> released on [[May 2]], [[2025]].<ref name="LEGODate">{{FortniteWeb|url=news/star-wars-hits-lego-fortnite-brick-life-and-odyssey|text=Star Wars Hits LEGO® Fortnite Brick Life and Odyssey!}}</ref> In the game, the eggs can be collected from a fridge in the [[Durrr Burger Cantina]] in the ''Star Wars'' Festival area and either eaten or used in place of a normal egg in any recipe.<ref name="BrickLife" />
