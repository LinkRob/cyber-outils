'''Degno''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Gran/Legends|Gran]] who lived during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="SWG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Degno appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online role-playing game|massively multiplayer online roleplaying game]] developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG" /> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref> According to these files, introduced with the [[2005]] "[[W:c:swg:New Game Enhancements|New Game Enhancements]]," Degno would have been present on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]] and in some way involved with the Mos Eisley part of the "[[W:c:swg:Legacy Quest|Legacy Quest]]." However, he is not known to have appeared on the game's live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what he would have looked like if he were added to the game. Although the Interchange File lists his name as Degno, the game's creature database lists his name as [[Durr Roddack]], which was actually the name used for a [[Human/Legends|Human]] male NPC which made it onto the live servers.<ref name="SWG" />
