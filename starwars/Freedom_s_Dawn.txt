The '''''Freedom's Dawn''''' was an [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]]<ref name="CL" /> manufactured by [[Mon Calamari Shipyards]]<ref name="HC 39" /> that served in the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="CL" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Note" /> the ''Freedom's Dawn'' was present on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] during the [[Battle of Jakku]],<ref name="CL" /> the final battle of the war fought between the [[New Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|forces]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> The ''Freedom's Dawn'' was wrecked near [[Namenthe's Crater]] and [[Pilgrim's Road]] on Jakku's surface. The [[Star Cruiser]] had a bulbous, winged, and light [[Color|brown]] [[hull]].<ref name="CL" />
