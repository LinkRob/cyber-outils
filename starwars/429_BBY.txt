'''429 BBY''' or '''429<ref name="Date" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Around that year,<ref name="Date" /> [[Hintis]]&mdash;the [[Color|red]] [[star]] of the [[planet]] [[Hintis IV]]&mdash;began to inflate and die, leading to Hintis IV's [[Hintian|inhabitants]] starting to leave the [[Hintis system|system]].<ref name="EoD">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
