'''Bomarmo''' was a [[Chiss]] [[Gender|male]] member of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Cohbo family]] and [[Marriage|husband]] of [[Cohbo'rik'ardok]], [[core name]] "Borika."<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' take place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the pair owned [[Ardok Ranch]] on the [[planet]] [[Ool]]. The [[Farm|ranch]] was home to a training site for the [[Seekers' Shadehouse]], a facility which wiped the memories of the Chiss [[sky-walker]]s, [[Youngling|young girls]] with [[The Force|abilities]] that helped the Chiss [[Navigator|navigate]] the [[Unknown Regions|space]] outside of the Ascendancy's borders.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> Around that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Borika introduced herself and mentioned Bomarmo when [[Caregiver]] [[Mitth'ali'astov]] "Thalias" came to Ardok Ranch looking to speak with Borika about a [[Che'ri|sky-walker's]] interactions with an [[alien]], [[The Magys]], who could [[Precognition|see]] into the future.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bomarmo was mentioned in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the third and final volume of the [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|The Ascendancy Trilogy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
