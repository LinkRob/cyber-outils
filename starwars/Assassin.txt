{{Quote|Put quite plainly, an assassin is one who kills for money—or, in some cases, just for the fun of it.|Hondo Ohnaka|<ref name="TSotBH">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref>}}
'''Assassins''' were individuals, sometimes professionally trained or experienced, who [[Death|killed]] individuals for personal cause, in service of an organization,<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> in return for [[Currency|payment]], or even out of enjoyment.<ref name="TSotBH" /> A paid assassin was also called a '''hitman''', who might be hired as a [[mercenary]] by powerful people such as [[crime lord]]s, businessmen, [[Politics|politicans]], or [[Noble Court|aristocrats]] to assassinate their rivals.<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> Some hitmen worked indefinitely for criminal organizations as '''enforcers''' to facilitate their operations.<ref name="SWO">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> Often times, assassins also worked as [[bounty hunter]]s, being hired by employers who would pay a substantial reward for the death of a certain target or targets.<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> 

==History==
[[File:Fennec_Shand_MK_Sniper_Rifle.png|thumb|left|300px|Master Assassin Fennec Shand]]
[[Sith assassin]]s were assassins who served the [[Sith]] Order.<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref> [[Clone X trooper]]s were specialized [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="Truth Slide 2">{{SW|url=truth-and-consequences-story-gallery|text="Truth and Consequences" Story Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> in service to the [[Galactic Empire]] who were programmed to serve as an assassins.<ref name="Echo">{{Databank|echo|Echo}}</ref> Some assassins throughout [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] included [[Mae-ho Aniseya]],<ref name="Acolyte">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="AotC" /> [[Cad Bane]],<ref name="CrisisNaboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref> [[Zam Wesell]],<ref name="AotC" /> [[Aurra Sing]],<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref> [[Maul|Darth Maul]],<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Savage Opress]],<ref name="Monster">{{TCW|Monster}}</ref> [[Rukh]],<ref name="Kindred">{{Rebels|Kindred}}</ref> [[Deathstick]],<ref name="SWU">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Ochi]],<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> and [[Fennec Shand]].<ref name="Chapter 5">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref> The position of "[[Master Assassin]]" was claimed by some skilled assassins, such as Ochi<ref name="Vader 22">[[Darth Vader (2020) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 22]]</ref> and Shand.<ref name="Boba 1">{{BOBF|Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land}}</ref>
