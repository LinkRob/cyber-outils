'''DN-20''' was a [[hubdroid]] who served within the [[Vardos Star Scouts]] on the [[planet]] [[Vardos]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Terror" /> Between [[1 BBY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[droid]] was reprogrammed by [[spy]] [[Deeta Kardon]] and served within the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] until his destruction.<ref name="Terror" />

==Behind the scenes==
DN-20 appeared in the [[2026]] audiobook ''[[Terror on Mustafar]]'', which was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and narrated by [[Marc Thompson]]. As the story is only available in audio format, no official spelling for the character's name is available.<ref name="Terror" />
