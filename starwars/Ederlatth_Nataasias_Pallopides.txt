'''Ederlatth Nataasias Pallopides''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who claimed to be the daughter of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] after his apparent [[death]] at the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Despite there being no evidence to link Pallopides to Palpatine, she gave herself the honorific "[[Lady]]" and claimed legitimacy as an heir to the [[throne]].<ref name="Rise" />

Following the [[Battle of Jakku|end]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> she maintained a presence within the social elite of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] for [[Standard year|years]] and was notably featured in the "Profile" section of issue [[Coruscanti Society 4117|#4117]] of the periodical ''[[Coruscanti Society]]''.<ref name="Rise" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned her in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', considering Pallopides to be the most famous of the seven humans who claimed to be children of Palpatine that he found traces of in his research.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Ederlatth Nataasias Pallopides was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> The character originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity as [[Ederlathh Pallopides/Legends|Ederlathh Pallopides]], who was mentioned in the [[1993]] ''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]'', a supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' written by [[Michael Allen Horne]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="DES">''[[Dark Empire Sourcebook]]''</ref>
