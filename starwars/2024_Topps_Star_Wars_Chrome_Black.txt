'''2024 Topps ''Star Wars'' Chrome Black''' is a trading card set released by [[Topps]] on [[March 19]], [[2025]]. It will include 100 base cards, along with the following insert sets:
*25 Years of Darth Maul
*Embrace the Darkside
*Depth of the Darkside
*Animated Short Prints
*Galactic Factions
*Lightside Autographs
*Darkside Autographs
*Duality of the Galaxy Autographs
