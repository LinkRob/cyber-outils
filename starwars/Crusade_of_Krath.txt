The '''Crusade of Krath''' was a conflict featured in the quasi-historical [[Qel-Droma Epics]]. Taking place a [[millennium]] after the [[Great Hyperspace War]], the epics said the crusade was led by the [[Krath]], a [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] [[Religion|cult]], against the [[Jedi Order]] from the Krath's command center in the [[Iron Citadel]] following a prior [[Conquest of the Empress Teta system|military coup]] by corrupt [[Tetan nobility|noble descendants]] in the [[Empress Teta system]].<ref name="NoP" />

At some point during the conflict, they slaughtered thousands of [[Jedi]], including [[Ulic Qel-Droma]]'s [[Ulic Qel-Droma's Jedi Master|Jedi Master]]. Following his Master's death, Qel-Droma volunteered to infiltrate the Krath, but was corrupted by the dark side, taking over the cult alongside the fallen Jedi [[Exar Kun]], forming a [[Sith Empire (Exar Kun)|new Sith Empire]] with [[Empress Teta]] as it's stronghold. Qel-Droma was eventually redeemed after being cut off from [[the Force]]. While the inhabitants of Empress Teta presented the events of the Qel-Droma Epics, including the Krath's religious crusade, as historical fact, [[Archaeology|archaeological]] expeditions failed to verify the legends.<ref name="NoP" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Crusade of Krath was first [[canon]]ically mentioned in the [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Nexus of Power]]''.<ref name="NoP" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, this conflict was known as the [[Krath Holy Crusade]] where it was fought from [[3997 BBY|3997]] to [[3996 BBY]] between a [[Krath/Legends|Krath]] headed by [[Satal Keto/Legends|Satal]] and [[Aleema Keto]] and an alliance of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]].<ref name="DLS 1">[[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 1|''Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith'' 1]]</ref>
