'''Everi Chalis''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] from a remote and impoverished [[Unidentified planet (Everi Chalis)|planet]] in [[the galaxy]]. Enrolling in the [[Colonial Academy]] to become an [[artist]], she took an apprenticeship under [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. She later became his successor and served as the [[governor]] of [[Haidoral Prime]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], before temporarily defecting to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
{{Quote|We had ''nothing''. My mother tried to sell me to a Trade Federation exploratory vessel when I was six. I was too small. Out of pity, the captain gave me a packet of nectrose crystals.|Everi Chalis, revealing her childhood to Hazram Namir|<ref name="BF" />}}
Everi Chalis was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] who grew up on an impoverished [[Unidentified planet (Everi Chalis)|planet]] under the care of [[Everi Chalis's mother|her mother]], living in the ruins of a [[bomb]]ed out [[Unidentified paper mill|paper mill]] where they shared a stained mattress. When Chalis reached the age of six, her [[Parent|mother]] tried selling her to a [[Trade Federation]] exploratory [[Starship|vessel]]. The vessel's [[Unidentified captain (Everi Chalis)|captain]] rejected the offer because Chalis was too small, and the [[captain]] gave her a packet of [[nectrose crystal]]s out of pity. She used her [[Limb|fingers]] to eat the [[crystal]]s, not knowing that it was intended to be sprinkled in [[water]] to add [[fruit]]iness, although Chalis didn't have access to fresh [[water]] anyway.

Chalis rationed the crystals and had them only once a [[Standard week|week]] for [[Standard month|months]]. She soon realized she was living in filth, eating garbage, and drinking [[poison]], while others in [[the galaxy]] were rich enough to share treats such as nectrose packets. Desperate to leave her [[Planet|world]], she enrolled into the [[Colonial Academy]] and made it off-world. Eventually, she made it offworld, but found she had nowhere to go under the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="BF" />

===Imperial career===
{{Quote|All parts of the armor-production chain will be brought under the Imperial Department of Military Research. It will be a lengthy transition; I am placing Everi Chalis directly in charge.|Count Denetrius Vidian|<ref name="Bottleneck" />}}
[[File:Stormtrooper-SWBC3.png|thumb|left|250px|An issue with stormtrooper armor (pictured) on Gilvaanen led to Everi Chalis's promotion.]]
After the [[Galactic Empire]] was [[Proclamation of the New Order|born]] from the Republic<ref name="BF" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Imperial [[Count]] [[Denetrius Vidian]] saw quality in Chalis's sculptures and admired her ability to visualize concepts, so he offered her an apprenticeship. Chalis accepted the offer, and artistry drifted out of her life.<ref name="BF" /> Rising to be a [[lieutenant]] under the tutelage of Vidian on [[Calcoraan]],<ref name="Bottleneck" /> Chalis found herself committing horrible acts, such as proposing the [[mining]] of an inhabited planet's [[atmosphere]] and deceiving and [[Death|killing]] an Imperial [[moff]].<ref name="BF" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Bottleneck date">"[[Bottleneck (short story)|Bottleneck]]" ends with [[Rae Sloane]]'s promotion to [[captain]], and [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']] refers to her as "the new captain," which means that "Bottleneck" is set shortly before ''A New Dawn''. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''A New Dawn'' to [[11 BBY]], "Bottleneck" takes place around that year.</ref> Chalis was Vidian's most trusted [[aide]], and he constantly had her traveling on assignment. After Vidian and [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] uncovered a plot on [[Gilvaanen]], a planet responsible for producing [[stormtrooper]] [[Stormtrooper armor|armor]], Vidian placed Chalis in charge of the [[Imperial Department of Military Research]].<ref name="Bottleneck" /> After Vidian's death during the [[Gorse Conflict]]<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> in [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Chalis took over the roles of her mentor, becoming an [[emissary]] to the [[Imperial Ruling Council]] and the honorary [[Grand Architect of the New Order]].<ref name="BF" />

===Defecting to the Rebellion===
{{Quote|I've been appointed a task, by our beneficent Emperor: the capture of Everi Chalis, former emissary to the Imperial Ruling Council and honorary Grand Architect of the New Order&mdash;now defector to the Rebel Alliance.|Prelate Verge|<ref name="BF" />}}
During the [[Battle of Yavin]], the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]] was destroyed by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. Bolstered by their victory, the Alliance then began a [[Mid Rim Retreat|campaign]] into the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="BF" /> around [[1 ABY]].<ref name="Mid Rim start">{{BTCdate}} The novel also states that the beginning of the [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim campaign]] took place eighteen [[Standard month|months]] prior to the main events of the novel. ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]'' establishes that a standard year was 365 [[Standard day|days]] long, while the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 61|sixty-first]] issue of [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon|''Star Wars: Build the Millenium Falcon'']] sets the length of a standard month at thirty-five days, with eighteen months therefore being approximately 1.7 years. Thus the Mid Rim offensive must have begun around [[1 ABY]].</ref> At that time, [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] went and blamed the higher echelons of the Empire for the loss of the Death Star, arranging the [[death]]s of a number of high status individuals such as Moff [[Coovern]] and [[Minister]] [[Khemt]]. Chalis herself was appointed as the [[governor]] of the Mid Rim planet [[Haidoral Prime]], which she considered a punishment.<ref name="BF" />

The Alliance's campaign into the Mid Rim went awry nine months in, the Empire slowly chasing the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] out of the region. Nine months into the retreat, the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]], better known as Twilight Company, [[Attack on Haidoral Prime|attacked]] Haidoral Prime. Convinced she would be punished by her superiors regardless of winning or losing the fight, she defected to Twilight [[Company]], and was brought aboard the ''[[Thunderstrike]]'', their [[CR90 corvette]]. She was placed in the [[airlock]] as a make-shift cell for her safety. She stayed there for [[Standard day|days]], until the ''Thunderstrike'' was attacked by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]]. The section of the [[corvette]] containing Chalis's airlock caught [[fire]], so [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] attempted to rescue her. Namir reached the airlock, but the heat's intensity prevented him from making a return trip, so he locked himself in the airlock with her. Eventually the flames were put out, and their burns were treated.<ref name="BF" />

===Battle of Hoth===
[[File:Acme-AttackonEchoBase.png|thumb|right|300px|Darth Vader breached Echo Base (Vader in Echo Base pictured) and encountered Chalis, who wrongly assumed he was hunting her.]]
Recalling the [[Rescue of Tseebo|Tseebo incident]], Chalis believed that Vader would come looking for her. In actuality, Vader was searching for [[Luke Skywalker]], the [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] responsible for destroying the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] two years earlier, while [[Verge]], a young [[prelate]], led the search for Chalis. The company continued moving from planet to planet fighting in numerous battles, before being summoned to the [[Echo Base|new Rebel base]] on the ice planet [[Hoth]]. While on Hoth, Chalis gained the trust of Rebel [[general]] [[Philap Bygar]], and she provided the [[Alliance High Command]] with statistical data of the Empire. In the following days, the Empire discovered the location of the Rebel base, and within eleven days of Chalis's arrival the Rebellion began evacuating. In the subsequent [[Battle of Hoth]], [[Imperial Army|Imperial ground forces]] led by [[Darth]] Vader breached the base. Before Chalis was able to evacuate, she and her Rebel escort encountered Vader and the [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] of [[Blizzard Force]]. After a short-lived battle, Chalis, believing Vader was there for her, approached the [[Sith Lord]] and submitted herself for execution. Vader, uninterested in Chalis, [[Force choke]]d her and demanded to know the location of Skywalker. As Chalis struggled, a stormtrooper informed Vader that the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' had been found. Vader then released Chalis and resumed his chase for the young rebel.<ref name="BF" />

Other than Chalis, only Namir survived Vader's attack. The two limped to a [[shuttle]] and managed to slip past the Imperial [[blockade]] using Chalis's old clearance codes. Following the jump to [[hyperspace]], they rendezvoused with the rest of Twilight Company on the planet [[Ankhural]]. Chalis, now aware of her triviality to the Empire, wanted to get revenge. Together with Namir, who unofficially replaced the deceased [[Micha Evon]] as captain of Twilight, Chalis plotted out seemingly random targets for the company to hit, finishing with an Imperial [[Star Destroyer]] construction yard on the planet [[Kuat]]. Understanding the workings of the Imperial system, Chalis knew that destroying the listed facilities would be a devastating setback for the Empire. Namir, not seeing any alternatives, complied with Chalis's plan. Twilight began its campaign against the locations determined by Chalis, and after several victories the company reached their second-to-last target: the [[Inyusu Tor]] [[Inyusu Tor mineral processing facility|mineral processing facility]] on [[Sullust]]. However, Prelate Verge had determined Chalis was assisting the Rebels, and he was able to predict their attack. Twilight Company was ambushed, and they suffered heavy losses, including the ''Thunderstrike''.<ref name="BF" />

===Siege of Inyusu Tor===
During the ensuing [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|battle]], Chalis fled to the nearby [[city]] of [[Pinyumb]], killed an [[Imperial officer]], and attempted to escape the planet in a shuttle. Before she was able to leave the atmosphere, Verge's Star Destroyer, the ''[[Herald]]'', locked onto the shuttle with its [[tractor beam]]. Verge, accompanied by [[Tabor Seitaron]], approached the captive shuttle. Chalis exited the vessel, and after exchanging a few words with the prelate, revealed the shuttle had been rigged with [[ion charge]]s. Chalis activated the charges, dealing major power loss to the Star Destroyer. Unable to power its weapons and maintain altitude, the ship began to drop towards the surface. Verge, adamant about continuing the attack, ordered the ship's crew to continue firing and began scuffling with Chalis. However, Seitaron, who was not willing to die unnecessarily, shot and killed the young prelate, ordering the crew to cease the attack and move the ship into [[orbit]]. As Seitaron prepared to execute Chalis, she took advantage of his fear of punishment for killing Verge, and she convinced him to let her go.<ref name="BF" />

===Starting over===
{{Quote|I'm very far from Sullust and I won't be returning to Twilight Company or the Rebellion. We never really were a good match, even when our goals looked similar. You made that clear to me in the end. I thought I owed it to you to say in person. Or as close as I can get, under the circumstances. Where I'm going next shouldn't and won't concern you. It's best that our paths don't cross again.|Chalis, in a message to Namir|<ref name="BF" />}}
No longer a captive of the Rebellion, Chalis traded the shuttle for a civilian [[starcutter]] and drained her offworld [[Currency|financial]] accounts. In the following days she sent a recorded message to Namir, telling him that he should have trusted her and that she would not return to the Rebellion. Thinking of plans for her future, she decided to reside on a remote planet and continue her artwork. Recalling Namir speak of his [[homeworld]], [[Crucival]], she decided to start there.<ref name="BF" />

==Personality and traits==
Everi Chalis was a female human with [[Color|light]] skin and white-streaked, graying, black [[hair]]. She spoke with an exaggerated [[Coruscanti]] accent, enjoyed reading classical fiction, and was a skilled sculptor.<ref name="BF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Everi Chalis was first mentioned in the [[canon]] short story "[[Bottleneck (short story)|Bottleneck]]," which was published in ''[[The Rise of the Empire]]'' in [[2015]]. Written by [[John Jackson Miller]], "Bottleneck" bridges the novels [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] and [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']].<ref name="ROTE">{{SW|url=news/details-revealed-for-upcoming-paperback-rise-of-the-empire|text=Details Revealed for Upcoming Paperback, ''Rise of the Empire''}}</ref> Chalis made her first full debut in the novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', which was written by [[Alexander Freed]] and released [[November 3]], 2015.<ref name="BF" />

''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' mistakenly lists her homeworld as [[Haidoral Prime]].<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
