The '''''Bantha's Charge''''' was a [[starship]] which went missing while carrying a shipment along the [[Rimma Trade Route]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Merchant]]s blamed the disappearance on [[TIE series|TIE]] attacks, and<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> the [[New Republic Intelligence]] marked it as a possible activity of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]]. The [[New Republic]] Intelligence [[agent]] [[Caern Adan]] and his colleague [[Yrica Quell]] reviewed the ''Bantha's Charge''{{'s}} disappearance in their search for the 204th but marked the event as unremarkable.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Bantha's Charge'' was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the second volume in his ''[[Star Wars: Alphabet Squadron]]'' trilogy.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
