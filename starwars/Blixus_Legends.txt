'''Blixus''' were large [[Sentience/Legends|non-sentient]] [[Diet/Legends|carnivorous]] [[Cephalopod/Legends|cephalopods]] from an unknown [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of origin. During the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], one such creature was kept on the [[Zygerrian/Legends|Zygerrian]] [[Slavery/Legends|slave]] [[Starship/Legends|vessel]] owned by Captain [[Onyx (individual)|Onyx]]. When the vessel was attacked by a group of [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] and [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]], Onyx released the blixus to kill them. The creature managed to kill several clones, but was defeated by the Jedi. Separatist commander [[Darts D'Nar/Legends|Darts D'Nar]] also had a blixus on his ship, the [[Tecora/Legends|''Tecora'']]. Blixus could also withstand the [[Vacuum/Legends|vacuum]] of [[Realspace/Legends|space]].
