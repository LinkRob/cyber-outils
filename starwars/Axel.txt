'''Axel''' was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]] that was purchased by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Pyya Bocc]] on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]] while broken. Bocc's friend [[Volo Bolus]] was not pleased to see the Twi'lek bring [[Sornah's Garage|home]] the [[droid]] and suggested he be returned, but Bocc was keen not to hurt Axel's feelings. Bocc proved overconfident in his droid repair abilities and never fixed Axel prior to the Twi'lek's [[death]], after which Bolus mused he might one [[Local day|day]] repair the droid around [[10 BBY]].

Axel was an [[R-series]] [[astromech droid]]<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="IndustrialAutomaton" /> The [[Twi'lek]] [[podracing]] [[crew chief]] [[Pyya Bocc]] purchased the [[droid]] while in [[City|town]] on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]], believing that he got a good deal despite the fact Axel was broken down and non-functional. When Bocc brought the droid back to the [[Sornah's Garage|garage]] where he worked and lived, his friend and [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]], [[Volo Bolus]], questioned why he had Axel, as the [[racer]] had sent Bocc to get [[lifter fluid]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

The Twi'lek introduced the droid and tried to stop Bolus from hurting his feelings, but the racer ignored the droid's name and continued referring to him as an "it" that should be returned to the seller, even asking if their mutual friend [[Deland Tyerell]] had put Bocc up to buying the droid as a joke. When Bocc admitted that the droid was non-functional, Bolus became even more exasperated and told their friend to turn the droid off, as Axel had recorded the entire conversation. Axel was not the first broken stray that Bocc had brought home, but as with the others the Twi'lek proved overconfident in his droid repair abilities and never returned to the project of fixing Axel after opening the droid up.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

{{GameMechanics}}
Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> Bolus removed a [[hydrospanner]] from an open panel in Axel's head while the droid sat stationary by a window beside Bolus's bed in the garage. The action caused the droid to play the recording of the conversation between Bolus and Bocc, the latter of who had since [[Death|died]]. Bolus mused that he would perhaps one day get Axel working.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
Axel appeared in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]. Interacting with Axel and hearing the recording during the game's story is optional but required to complete the challenge "Letting Go." As such, this article assumes the interaction occurred.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />
