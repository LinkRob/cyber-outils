The '''Fillithar system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> located in the [[Colonies]] It was located in grid square M-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Fillithar (planet)|Fillithar]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Fillithar system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In the first iteration of the appendix, Fillithar was placed in the Inner Rim in grid square N-10;<ref name="Appendix" /> however, the ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' map instead placed it in the Colonies in M-9.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> The second version of the appendix updated the system's listing to match the map.<ref name="Appendix" />
