'''David Wahlberg''' is a compositor. David was nominated for a [https://www.visualeffectssociety.com Visual Effects Society's] 2021 award for Outstanding Compositing in an Episode for his work on "[[Chapter 15: The Believer]]" of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''<ref name="Nomination">{{WebCite|url=https://www.visualeffectssociety.com/press-releases/19th-annual-ves-awards-nominees|text=Visual Effects Society Announces Nominees for 19th Annual VES Awards|date=2021-03-01|archivedate=20210406011208}}</ref> Notably, David also worked on [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
