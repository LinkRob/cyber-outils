The '''Central Wideroad''' was a road that passed through [[grass]]land on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> winds made the road hazardous to travel on using a [[landspeeder]] and during the [[Holonet News Toshara]]'s wind report, [[Journalist|reporter]] [[Botto Fna]] issued a warning about the conditions, discouraging any non-essential travel on the road. Fna also suggested the use of [[Deflector shield|deflectors]] during any long-distance crossing of the grasslands on the route.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Central Wideroad was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
