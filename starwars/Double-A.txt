The '''Double-A''' was a modified [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]] that was created by [[Novaldex]] twenty-four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. It had modded fins, and a metal coating that made its hull shiny and reflective like a mirror. [[Han Solo]] flew the Double-A for a test run during a [[Corellia]]n racing event known as [[The Evil Eye]].<ref name="Scorched" />
