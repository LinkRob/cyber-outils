'''Eye-Oh-Ninety-Eight-Dee''' was the designation of a [[Droid brain|droid processor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Vader 43" /> The [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] utilized the [[droid]] brain and several others to take control of the [[M.A.R. Corps]], a team of [[cyborg]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]], with Eye-Oh-Ninety-Eight-Dee being implanted into [[Masakint]].<ref name="Vader 42">[[Darth Vader (2020) 42|''Darth Vader'' (2020) 42]]</ref> During a skirmish with former [[governor]] [[Tauntaza]], the droid brain was disconnected from the cyborg, allowing them a moment of freedom in which they turned against Vader. The [[Sith]] used [[the Force]] to reconnect the droid brain, which thanked him.<ref name="Vader 43" />
