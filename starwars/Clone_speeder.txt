A '''model''' of [[speeder]]<ref name="ShopPage" /> was utilized by [[clone trooper]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. A pair of clone troopers once utilized a speeder of this model on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] alongside [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and [[Padawan]] [[Barriss Offee]].<ref name="75206Clones&Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
The speeder was first depicted in the [[2018]] [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] set [[w:c:brickipedia:75206 Jedi and Clone Troopers Battle Pack|75206 Jedi and Clone Troopers Battle Pack]].<ref name="75206Clones&Jedi" /> It was identified as a speeder on the set's LEGO website shop page.<ref name="ShopPage" />
