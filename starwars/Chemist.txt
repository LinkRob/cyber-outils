'''Chemists''' were a type of [[scientist]] who specalized in working with chemicals. The chemists employed by [[Ayelixe/Krongbing Textiles]] created the synthetic [[shadowsilk]] after studying the properties of [[Defel]] [[Hair|fur]].<ref name="G&G">''[[Gadgets and Gear]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chemist was first mentioned in the sourcebook ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', which was written by [[Alexis Dykema]], [[Keith Kappel]], and [[Phil Maiewski]],<ref name="G&G" /> and published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] on [[September 13]], [[2019]].<ref name="G&G Release">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/9/13/available-now-september-13|text=Available Now – September 13}}</ref> The scientific occupation later made its proper debut through the addition of Skora to the mobile [[Star Wars video games|game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'' in [[2022]].<ref name="Skora Debut">{{Hunters|url=news/new-star-wars-hunters-tm-skora-and-sprocket|text=New Support Hunters are entering the Arena}}</ref>
