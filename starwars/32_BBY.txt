The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''32 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3245 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''13 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7945''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie]] is [[Coronation of Queen Amidala|elected]] [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="SWP">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>
*Amidala hosts the [[Chommell sector summit]] for the leaders of other [[planet]]s in the [[Chommell sector]], which, unbeknownst to her, leads [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth Sidious]] to accelerate his plans regarding Naboo.<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref>
*The [[Trade Federation]] [[Invasion of Naboo|invades]] Naboo.<ref name="TPM" />
*[[Anakin Skywalker]] wins the [[Boonta Eve Classic]], thus securing his freedom.<ref name="TPM" />
*After 1,000 [[Standard year|years]] operating in secret, the [[Sith]] make their presence known to the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="TPM" />
*[[Senator]] Sheev Palpatine is [[Supreme Chancellor election, 32 BBY|elected]] [[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], replacing [[Finis Valorum]],<ref name="TPM" /> thus beginning the [[Republic Era|downfall]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="SWP" />
[[File:AvengeMyMaster.png|thumb|right|300px|Obi-Wan Kenobi and Darth Maul face each other on Naboo in 32 BBY.]]
*The [[Battle of Naboo]] occurs:<ref name="TPM" />
**The Trade Federation's occupation of Naboo is overthrown.<ref name="TPM" />
**[[Maul|Darth Maul]] kills [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], but Maul himself is then bested by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="TPM" /> Maul survives but is presumed dead.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref>
*Kenobi is promoted to the rank of [[Jedi Knight]]. Anakin Skywalker is assigned as Kenobi's [[Padawan]] learner.<ref name="TPM" />
*[[Doctor]] [[Nuvo Vindi]] disappears and does not resurface for another [[22 BBY|ten years]].<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref>
*[[Yaddle]] and [[Dooku]] have a [[Duel in the Works|duel]] on [[Coruscant]], with Dooku being victorious and completely falling to the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="The Sith Lord">{{TOTJ|The Sith Lord}}</ref> Dooku replaces Maul as Sidious's [[Sith apprentice]].<ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> Dooku is given the name of Darth Tyranus.<ref name="Episode II" />
*[[Funeral of Qui-Gon Jinn]]<ref name="TPM" />
*Dooku hires the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to serve as the [[clone template]], the [[Kaminoan]]s began creating [[clone trooper]]s on [[Kamino]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref><ref name="AORJF">[[Age of Republic - Jango Fett 1|''Age of Republic - Jango Fett'' 1]]</ref>
*The first generation of [[clone trooper]]s are created on [[Kamino]].<ref name="Rex birth">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'' states that [[Rex]] was [[Reproduction|born]] thirty-two [[Standard year|years]] before the events of {{Film|IV}}, which corresponds to 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, Rex must have been born on [[Kamino]] in 32 BBY. "[[Battle Scars]]" established that Rex was part of the first generation of [[clone trooper]]s.</ref>
