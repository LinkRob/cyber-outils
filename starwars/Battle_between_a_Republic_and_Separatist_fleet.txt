During the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] of the [[Clone Wars]], a '''battle''' broke out in [[Realspace|space]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Old Friends" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Time" /> The battle occurred following the outbreak of a massive offense across the [[Outer Rim Territories]] by the [[Kaleesh]] Separatist [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander]], [[General]] [[Grievous]], which led to the sieges.<ref name="Old Friends" />

During the battle, a Separatist [[fleet]] of at least six [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnoughts]] under Grievous's [[Commanding officer|command]] engaged a Republic fleet of at least six [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]]. As Grievous watched from one of the Providence's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], which was crewed by several [[B1-series battle droid]]s, Separatist [[droid tri-fighter]]s and [[Clone Z-95 starfighter|Clone Z-95 Headhunters]] engaged one another in [[Starfighter combat|dogfights]] around the [[capital ship]]s, while at least two of the [[dreadnought]]s sustained extensive damage to their [[hull]]s.<ref name="Old Friends" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle between a Republic and Separatist fleet appeared in the opening newsreel of "[[Old Friends Not Forgotten]],"<ref name="Old Friends" /> the ninth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series,<ref name="Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/old-friends-not-forgotten-episode-guide|text="Old Friends Not Forgotten" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> which aired on [[April 17]], [[2020]].<ref name="dtcimedia">{{DisneyPlus|subdomain=press|url=disney-plus/star-wars-the-clone-wars/709|text=Episode 709 "Old Friends Not Forgotten"}}</ref>
