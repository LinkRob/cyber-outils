'''Engi Golba''' was a [[Didynon]] from the [[Planet|world]] [[Didyma V]]. He originally worked as a [[miner]], setting explosives in the [[lommite]] [[Mining|mines]], but escaped his [[Homeworld|home]] when it was [[Annexation of Didyma V|annexed]] by the [[First Order]]. Golba joined the [[Resistance]], utilizing his talents with explosives. In [[35 ABY]], he was part of the Resistance's [[Exegol landing team|landing team]] that fought [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] on the surface of the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] [[command ship]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]].

==Biography==
Engi Golba [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the terrestrial [[Planet|world]] [[Didyma V]]. He worked as a [[miner]] there, planting explosives in [[lommite]] [[Mining|mines]] in order to open new tunnels. When the [[First Order]] [[Annexation of Didyma V|annexed]] the world and took over production, he fled the mines and eventually joined the [[Resistance]], bringing his skills in utilizing explosives with him.<ref name="TROS VD" />

[[File:Golba-Briefing-TROS.png|thumb|left|300px|Golba joined the Resistance after escaping Didyma V's mines.]]
In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}}, which include Engi Golba's appearance, to [[35 ABY]].</ref> he was stationed at the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance's base]] on<ref name="Episode IX" /> the [[jungle]] [[moon]]<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[Ajan Kloss]] when [[General]]s [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Finn]] briefed the Resistance on their [[Battle of Exegol|mission]] to the planet [[Exegol]], aiming to eliminate the [[Final Order|fleet]] of the [[Sith Eternal]] and First Order. Golba was a member of the Resistance's [[Exegol landing team|landing team]] whose main objective was to sabotage a [[navigation tower]] that would allow the [[fleet]] to depart the planet.<ref name="Episode IX" />

The team traveled to Exegol via a [[YC-123B transport hauler]]<ref name="Episode IX" /> named the [[Fortitude (transport)|''Fortitude'']].<ref name="TROS VD" /> Upon arriving, however, the [[Sith]] fleet switched the navigation signal to<ref name="Episode IX" /> the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']]<ref name="TROS VD" /> [[command ship]]. General Finn then ordered the [[transport]] to land on the ''Steadfast'' instead. The landing team was subsequently deployed on the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|starship]], and they began advancing toward the tower. Sprinting behind a [[Talpiddian]], Golba assisted in engaging the [[Sith trooper]]s and [[Sith Jetpack Trooper|jet troopers]] that had arrived to defend the tower. Eventually, Finn and former [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Jannah]] took out the signal, and Golba and the landing team retreated to the ''Fortitude''. Ultimately, the Resistance won the battle and returned to Ajan Kloss for celebrations.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Personality and traits==
Golba was a [[Didynon]] with [[Color|white]] skin. With his skills in utilizing explosives, he had a zeal for destroying First Order property.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Skills and abilities==
Golba was talented with explosives, using them in lommite mines and later in the destruction of First Order equipment.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
Golba wore a brown [[robe]] over a gray [[Clothing|cowl]], brown dress, and brown [[belt]]. He utilized explosives in Didyma V's lommite mines, and he carried an [[EL-16HFE blaster rifle]] as a member of the Resistance.<ref name="TROS VD" />
