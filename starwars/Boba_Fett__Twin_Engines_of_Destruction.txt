'''''Boba Fett: Twin Engines of Destruction''''' is a [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] of the [[Star Wars: Boba Fett (comic series)|Boba Fett]] series that collects "Boba Fett: Twin Engines of Destruction," a comic story originally serialized in the [[Topps]] publication ''[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine]]'', issues [[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 5|5]]–[[Star Wars Galaxy Magazine 8|8]]. The comic book was first published on [[January 15]], [[1997]] by [[Dark Horse Comics]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Boba Fett is the most feared, most respected bounty hunter in the galaxy. He only cares about two things: his money and his reputation. So when he discovers someone has been impersonating him, he decides to teach his inferior doppelgänger exactly why he's the most feared, most respected bounty hunter in the galaxy! It's Boba Fett vs. Boba Fett in this 32-page one-shot—a fight you won't want to miss!'' Twin Engines of Destruction ''was originally serialized in the pages of Topps' ''Star Wars Galaxy'' magazine.''

==Plot summary==
[[Dengar/Legends|Dengar]] has a troubled meeting with [[Jodo Kast]], a young bounty hunter who's becoming famous for his wearing a set of [[Mandalorian armor/Legends|Mandalorian armor]], very similar to [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett's]]. The Corellian bounty hunter contacts Fett, informing him that his new "alter ego" is becoming a highly requested mercenary, thanks to his habit of pretending to be better than Fett himself. Fett, disguised as Sava Brec Madak, hires Kast himself, in order to trap him on [[Nal Hutta/Legends|Nal Hutta]] and punish the impostor. Kast (still impersonating Fett) goes to collect the bounty, but finds himself with Fett's gun to his head. A violent fight ensues, with Fett finally killing the impostor.
