'''Chris Hohl''' is an [[Wikipedia:Illustrator|illustrator]]<ref name="About" /> who worked as a designer for the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''.<ref name="The Young Jedi">{{YJA|The Young Jedi / Yoda's Mission}}</ref> Hohl was responsible for many exterior and interior sets seen in the animated series, including [[Hap's Sap Tap]], [[Hazun]], and the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]].<ref name="YJA Design">{{WebCite|url=https://chrishohl.com/young-jedi-adventures|text=Young Jedi Adventures|work=Chris Hohl's [https://chrishohl.com official website]|archivedate=20241216163403}}</ref>
