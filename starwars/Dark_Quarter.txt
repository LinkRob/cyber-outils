The '''Dark Quarter''' was at [[Yuw]],<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> a location in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim's]] [[Herdessa sector]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Yuw system]]</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Target Vader 1|''Target Vader'' 1]] to [[1 BBY]].</ref> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] learned of a [[Hunt for Vader|plot]] to [[Death|assassinate]] him, and subsequently traveled to the Dark Quarter, where he interrogated and killed several [[bounty hunter]]s in order to gain information on the organization behind the plot, the [[Hidden Hand]] criminal syndicate.<ref name="Target Vader 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Dark Quarter appeared in the [[Target Vader 1|first issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book series]] ''[[Star Wars: Target Vader]]'', written by [[Robbie Thompson]], illustrated by [[Marc Laming]] and [[Cris Bolson]], and released by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Target Vader 1" /> on [[July 3]], [[2019]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/77664/star_wars_target_vader_2019_1|text=Star Wars: Target Vader (2019) #1}}</ref>
