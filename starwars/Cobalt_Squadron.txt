'''Cobalt Squadron''' was a [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] [[Squadron|unit]] within the [[Resistance]].

==History==
[[File:CobaltSquadronBomber-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|A bomber of Cobalt Squadron loaded with relief supplies.]]
Cobalt Squadron was a [[Squadron|unit]] within the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Ello DB" /> [[Ello Asty]] was a [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] escort [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] in the squadron before transferring to [[Red Squadron (Resistance)|Red Squadron]].<ref name="HC74" /> Asty wore the unit's insignia on his [[Flight helmet|helmet]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of D'Qar|Evacuation of D'Qar]], the squadron consisted of [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17s]].<ref name="Cobalt Squadron" /> During that battle, the squadron was wiped out except for [[Fossil]] and [[Rose Tico|Rose]] during a bombing run against a [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]], though they accomplished their objective of destroying the ship.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

Prior to the Battle of D'Qar, Cobalt Squadron pilot [[Wrobie Tyce]] was reassigned to Snap Wexley's command, leading to her avoiding getting killed with her fellow squadron teammates during the evacuation.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cobalt Squadron was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] reference books. It was later expanded upon ''[[The Last Jedi: Cobalt Squadron]]''.
