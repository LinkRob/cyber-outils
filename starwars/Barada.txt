'''Barada''' was a [[Klatooinian]] who served the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] before [[1 BBY]], and would continue to serve Jabba up to four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2">[[Galaxy's Edge 2|''Galaxy's Edge'' 2]]</ref> When Jabba attempted to [[Death|execute]] a group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], Barada was one of the crime lord's minions who traveled with the prisoners to the pit. At the execution, however, the Rebels fought to free themselves, and in the struggle the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] slashed Barada with his [[lightsaber]] and killed the Klatooinian.

==Biography==
The [[Klatooinian]] [[Gender|male]] Barada was born on the [[planet]] [[Klatooine]] but became [[Slavery|enslaved]] to the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] four [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. He worked as a [[Technician|mechanic]]<ref name="USW" /> and [[skiff]] [[Security guard|guard]]<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]], and was responsible for the gangster's entire pool of [[repulsorlift]] vehicles,<ref name="USW" /> which included his [[sail barge]] ''[[Khetanna]]'',<ref name="ROTJ" /> in addition to serving as muscle.<ref name="Databank" />

Barada was in the Hutt's employ when Jabba was in possession of the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] frozen in [[carbonite]]. Solo's companions in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] hoped to rescue their comrade, planting some of their number in Jabba's court: [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], and then [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]], both attempted to free Solo, but ended up Jabba's captives along with the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]]. After Jabba's [[rancor]] failed to [[Death|kill]] Skywalker, the crime lord demanded that Solo, Skywalker and Chewbacca all be executed at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], where they would be fed to the [[sarlacc]]. Barada was among the henchmen Jabba assigned to ride in a secondary skiff, to travel alongside the skiff carrying the prisoners to the pit. Once at the pit, Skywalker's [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]], who was serving in Jabba's court, armed the [[Jedi]] with his [[lightsaber]], which he used to quickly slay all of the henchmen on the skiff with him. The Jedi then leaped across to the vehicle carrying Barada, and killed a number of the men on it before the Klatooinian attacked him. Skywalker dodged the blow and slashed at Barada, knocking him into the pit of the sarlacc. The Rebels went on to slay Jabba and destroy his sail barge, killing many other members of his court in the process and escaping.<ref name="ROTJ" />

Barada was killed by Skywalker's [[Luke Skywalker's green lightsaber|green lightsaber]].<ref name="Databank" /> Languishing in the Pit of Carkoon, Barada would be slowly digested by the sarlacc over the next thousand years.<ref name="USW" /> Barada was mentioned as having famously maintained Jabba's skiffs in an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]], which was restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="Alien Archives" /> at some point after [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Barada had [[Color|green]] skin<ref name="ROTJ" /> and blue [[eye]]s. Although a slave, Barada found reasonable contentment with his lot, and helped maintain Jabba Desilijic Tiure's repulsorlift vehicles.<ref name="USW" /> He was experienced<ref name="HothEncyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Battle of Hoth and the Second Death Star}}</ref> and knew how to mind the Hutt's vile tempers.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

==Equipment==
Barada wore a white [[Clothing|shirt]], blue pants and an orange bandana while at the Great Pit of Carkoon. The Klatooinian also wore a backpack and carried a battle-staff weapon.<ref name="ROTJ" />
