'''Davin Felth''' was a [[human]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtrooper]] [[sergeant]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Following the escape of two [[droid]]s from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[cruiser]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'' after it was [[Secret mission to Tatooine|captured]] by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] above the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], Felth was amongst a [[squad]] of stormtroopers sent to search for the droids in the [[desert]]. While searching, Felth managed to locate evidence of the droids' presence.

==Biography==
[[File:Sideshow-LookSirDroids.png|thumb|left|250px|Davin Felth finds evidence of the escaped droids amongst the sands.]]
Davin Felth<ref name="DtD">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/4/8/dominate-the-desert|text=Dominate the Desert}}</ref> was a [[human]]<ref name="Species" /> who served as a [[Stormtrooper|member]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]]<ref name="ANH" /> as a [[Stormtrooper Commander|stormtrooper sergeant]]<ref name="Sergeant">{{HotToysCite|set=Movie Masterpiece Series|packtype=Sixth Scale Figure|pack=#722: Sandtrooper Sergeant and Dewback|link=collectibles/star-wars-sandtrooper-sergeant-and-dewback-hot-toys-912535}} {{C|Sandtrooper Sergeant tab}}</ref> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="ANH" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1|Felth's appearances}}</ref> the Rebel [[CR90 corvette]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'' was [[Secret mission to Tatooine|captured]] by the Empire over the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] while carrying [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the Empire's [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]. The [[droid]]s [[R2-D2]] and [[C-3PO]] managed to slip through the Empire's clutches and leave the [[Starship|vessel]] with the plans in an [[escape pod]] that carried them to the surface of the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ANH" />

Keen to recover the plans, the Empire dispatched [[Desert stormtrooper|sandtroopers]], including Felth, into the [[desert]]s of Tatooine to search for the droids. The [[trooper]]s managed to locate the droid's escape pod where Felth discovered<ref name="ANH" /> a metal O-ring that had jarred loose from C-3PO's left knee joint.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Felth picked up the evidence and alerted the [[Commanding officer|commander]]. Ultimately, the droids successfully escaped Tatooine and delivered the plans to the Alliance.<ref name="ANH" />

==Equipment==
Felth wore [[stormtrooper armor]] with a [[Color|white]] [[pauldron]]<ref name="ANH" /> to designate the rank of [[sergeant]]<ref name="Sergeant" /> while in the desert.<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Davin Felth first appeared in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[original trilogy]].<ref name="ANH" /> [[Wikipedia:Disc jockey|Disc jockeys]] [[Terence McGovern]], [[Jerry Walter]], [[Scott Beach]], and [[Morgan Upton]] provided the voices for all of the stormtroopers in the film while speaking over a [[Wikipedia:Walkie-talkie|walkie-talkie]]. The voices were then recorded by [[Lucasfilm]] sound designer [[Ben Burtt]].<ref name="Sounds of Star Wars">''[[The Sounds of Star Wars]]''</ref> McGovern maintains a personal [http://www.terrymcgovern.com/starwars.html Web site] that lists all of the dialogue he was responsible for in the film, which does not include Felth's one line of dialogue.<ref name="McGovern">{{WebCite|url=http://www.terrymcgovern.com/starwars.html|text=Star Wars|author=Terence McGovern|work=terrymcgovern.com|archivedate=20110717073022}}</ref>

In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the character was first identified in "Dominate the Desert", an article published on [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' official site in [[2019]].<ref name="DtD" /> The name originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used ''[[When the Desert Wind Turns: The Stormtrooper's Tale]]'', a short story written by [[Doug Beason]] and published in the [[1995]] anthology book ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]''. In the book Felth is also depicted as being the stormtrooper with an orange pauldron seen in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]];<ref name="WtDWT">{{StoryCite|story=When the Desert Wind Turns: The Stormtrooper's Tale|book=Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina}}</ref> however, in the new ''Star Wars'' canon this trooper was identified as [[Wanten]] in the short story "[[Born in the Storm]]," which was written by [[Daniel José Older]] and published in the [[2018]] anthology, ''[[From a Certain Point of View]]''.<ref name="BitS">{{StoryCite|story=Born in the Storm|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>
