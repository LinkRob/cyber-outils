The '''destruction of Nacronis''',<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> also referred to as the '''cleansing of Nacronis''',<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> was a massacre carried out by the [[Galactic Empire]] as part of [[Operation: Cinder]], following the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] in the [[Battle of Endor]]. The [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] provided assistance in the attack after [[Colonel]] [[Shakara Nuress]] was given orders by one of the Emperor's [[Sentinel (Contingency)|Messenger]] [[droid]]s. The [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] used [[vortex detonator]]s fired from [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bombers]] to stoke the [[planet]]'s [[Siltstorm|storms]], but the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] attempted to defend Nacronis. By the time that Nacronis was covered entirely in one singular storm, the Rebel Alliance had stopped attacking, and the Empire was successful in wiping out the population of the planet. After the storms cleared, one [[pilot]], [[Yrica Quell]], defected from the Empire, and would later be found by the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
