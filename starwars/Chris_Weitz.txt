'''Chris Weitz''' is an American screenwriter, director, producer, and actor who has worked on the films [[Wikipedia:American Pie (film)|''American Pie'']], [[Wikipedia:About a Boy|''About a Boy'']], [[Wikipedia:The Golden Compass (film)|''The Golden Compass'']], [[Wikipedia:The Twilight Saga: New Moon|''The Twilight Saga: New Moon'']], and [[Wikipedia:Cinderella (2015)|''Cinderella'']]. On [[March 12]], [[2015]], it was announced that Weitz had taken over screenwriting duties from [[Gary Whitta]] for the ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'' film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', which was released on [[December 16]], [[2016]].<ref name="Weitz Announced">{{SW|url=news/rogue-one-is-the-first-star-wars-stand-alone-film-rian-johnson-to-write-and-direct-star-wars-episode-viii|text=''Rogue One'' is the First ''Star Wars'' Stand-Alone Film, Rian Johnson to Write and Direct ''Star Wars'': Episode VIII}}</ref>
