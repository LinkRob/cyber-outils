'''Docking tickets''' were notices issues to [[Pilot/Legends|pilots]], requiring them to pay a given amount of money. A [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Sanyassan/Legends|Sanyassan]] known as the "[[Marauder of Endor]]" was known to have at least 79 convictions and counts for a plethora of felonies, as well as numerous unpaid docking tickets.<ref name="MAGL">''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Docking ticket was mentioned in a one-page [[Wikipedia:wanted poster|wanted poster]] for the [[Marauder of Endor]], written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that combines creature designs and photographs from various projects of [[George Lucas]], creator of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, with original text by Carrau.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]].<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
