'''Blockroll''' was an individual that, during the [[High Republic Era]], served under the [[Gloovan]] [[crime lord|crime boss]] [[Straan Valgar]], who ran a small [[Straan Valgar's mob|mob]] on the [[Port Hunavon]] [[space station]].<ref name="Script" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TB}}</ref> when one of Valgar's sport [[unarmed combat|fights]] deviated from its planned outcome, a masked [[bounty hunter]], [[Quin Carree]], confronted Valgar. Hearing [[blaster]] bolts ringing out from Valgar's torturous interrogation, Blockroll [[comlink|commed]] their boss to check his status.<ref name="Script" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blockroll's voice could be heard in flashbacks in ''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]'', a [[2024]] audio drama written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published as a part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Tempest Breaker">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The spelling of their name was later confirmed with the release of the audio drama's [[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker (script)|script]] in [[2025]].<ref name="Script" />
