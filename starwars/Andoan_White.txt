'''Andoan White''', also simply called '''Andoan''', was a type of [[Color|white]] [[wine]] from the [[planet]] [[Ando]]. By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Oga's Cantina]] establishment on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]] imported some of the wine from Ando to serve to its patrons. In [[19 BBY]], the [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] visited the Batuu [[cantina]] and ordered two mugs of Andoan White.

After the end of the Clone Wars, the beverage was served in the establishments [[79's]] and [[Cid's Parlor]] on the planets [[Coruscant]] and [[Ord Mantell]], respectively, and also kept in a wine cabinet on the planet [[Raxus Secundus]]. By [[34 ABY]], the wine was still available in Oga's Cantina as an offering, and the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] wrote about Andoan White's traits in his [[Traveler's Guide to Batuu|Batuu guidebook]].

==Description==
Andoan White, also referred to simply as Andoan,<ref name="THA" /> was a type of [[Color|white]] [[wine]] originating from the [[planet]] [[Ando]], the [[Homeworld|home]] of the [[Aqualish]] [[species]]. The [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] felt that the wine tasted thirst-quenching, light, and refreshing and had a crisp, balanced flavor.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> Andoan White was [[Diet|consumable]] by [[human]]s and could be served in mugs.<ref name="THA" /> It came in a brown bottle with black and yellow labels, with the drink's name [[Writing|written]] in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Rampage EG">{{SW|url=rampage-concept-art-gallery|text="Rampage" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|We've got Batuu Brew, Black Spire Brew, Blurrgfire, Toniray White, Andoan White, Moogan Tea&mdash;with or without alco&mdash;|Janott to Padmé Amidala, in Oga's Cantina|<ref name="THA" />}}
[[File:OgasCantina_DisneyParksBlog.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Andoan White was served in Oga's Cantina (pictured).]]
By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], a [[Oga's Cantina|cantina]] in the [[Black Spire Outpost]] settlement on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]] imported some of the [[alcohol]]ic beverage from Ando to serve to its patrons.<ref name="THA" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="THA Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[Mission to Batuu|mission]] to [[Batuu]], which is depicted in ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', to [[19 BBY]].</ref> the [[cantina]]'s [[bartender]], [[Janott]], offered Andoan White and other beverage selections to the human [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] upon her visit to the establishment. When Amidala chose Andoan White, he poured a few [[centimeter]]s of the wine into an [[obsidian]] mug for her in exchange for five [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. As she sipped her drink, Janott inquired if she liked the wine, and the senator confirmed that she enjoyed it. Afterward, Amidala ordered a second serving of Andoan White while writing a [[Funeral|funerary]] [[Music|song]] for her [[Death|deceased]] former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]], [[Duja]], on her [[datapad]]. After half an [[Standard hour|hour]] of nursing the wine, she eventually finished it and left the cantina.<ref name="THA" />

[[File:CidsParlor-Rampage.png|thumb|right|235px|Andoan was served at Cid's Parlor on Ord Mantell after the end of the Clone Wars.]]
Shortly after the Clone Wars<ref name="Rampage" /> in 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of [[The Bad Batch Season 1|''The Bad Batch'' Season 1]] in [[19 BBY]].</ref> Andoan was among the beverages available at the [[Cid's Parlor]] establishment in [[Ord Mantell City]] on the planet [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="Rampage" /> By the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> it was also stored in a wine cabinet within the [[Avi Singh's residence|residence]] of former senator [[Avi Singh]] on the planet [[Raxus Secundus]]<ref name="Common Ground" /> and was available in the [[79's]] cantina on<ref name="Clone" /> the planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Clone" /> Andoan White remained on offer at the Batuu cantina,<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> which was renamed Oga's Cantina after its new proprietor&mdash;the [[Blutopian]] [[Oga Garra]]&mdash;around [[14 ABY]].<ref name="GEStaff">{{GEStaff|Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|formatted=''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge|land}} One such backstory element dates [[Oga Garra]]'s takeover of [[Oga's Cantina]] to around twenty [[Standard year|years]] prior to the park's events. As ''Star Wars'' Galaxy's Edge takes place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', she must have started operating there around [[14 ABY]].</ref> In his Batuu guidebook [[author]]ed<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', Throno briefly described the wine's qualities in comparison to other beverages currently offered at the establishment, reccomending Oga's Cantina as a place to visit for Batuu newcomers.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CidsParlorDrinks-RampageConceptArt.jpg|thumb|right|320px|Chris Madden's concept artwork for Cid's Parlor's bottle designs]]
Andoan White first appeared in the [[2018]] novel ''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]'', written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the second installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy]]''.<ref name="THA" /> On [[February 28]], [[2019]], the wine was reported to be part of the upcoming dining offerings of the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in its [[In-universe|real-world]] Oga's Cantina bar.<ref name="77 Things">{{WebCite|url=https://io9.gizmodo.com/star-wars-galaxys-edge-the-77-most-fascinating-things-1832883014|text=''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge''&mdash;The 77 Most Fascinating Things We Learned on Our Trip|author=Germain Lussier|date=2019-02-28|work=[[Wikipedia:io9|io9]]|archivedate=20190228213029}}</ref> However, the drink did not appear on the menu after the land opened<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/ogas-cantina/menus/dinner|text=Disneyland: Oga's Cantina: Dinner Menu}}</ref> on [[May 31]] of that year.<ref name="GE OpeningDate">{{DPB|url=2019/05/foodie-guide-to-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Foodie Guide to ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref>

Andoan White was first visually depicted in "[[Rampage (episode)|Rampage]],"<ref name="Rampage" /> the fifth episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', which aired on [[May 28]], [[2021]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-may-2021|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in May 2021|nolive=1}}</ref> [[Lucasfilm Animation]] artist [[Chris Madden]] created a concept-art illustration of the beverage's bottle design for the episode on [[August 16]], 2019, which labeled it as "Bottle E."<ref name="Rampage EG" />
