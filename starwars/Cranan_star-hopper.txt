The '''Cranan star-hopper''' was a type of [[starship]]. The [[Barbadelan]] [[pilot]] [[Karamu]] owned one such ship with his [[L-1g general purpose droid]] [[SV-3]], but it was damaged when it crashed onto [[Rendel]].<ref name="TFVC 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cranan star-hopper first appeared in a flashback section of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 3|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 3]], written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Corin Howell]], and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="TFVC 3" /> on [[October 17]], [[2018]].<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Tales-From-Vaders-Castle-3-of-5/digital-comic/705076|text=Star Wars Adventures: Tales From Vader's Castle #3|nolive=1}}</ref> On [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]], Scott confirmed that the starship's name was a reference to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Cranan 12]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity,<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|cavanscott|status/1226769124522647553|[[Cavan Scott]]|quote=Absolutely a reference.|archivefile=File:CrananName.png}}</ref> first mentioned in "[[The Greel Wood Haven]]," an article written by [[Craig Robert Carey]] and published in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 6|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 6]] in [[1995]].<ref name="Adventure Journal 6">[[Star Wars Adventure Journal 6|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 6]]</ref>
