'''Betaplast''', also known as '''Composite Betaplast''' or '''betaplastoid''', was an [[armor]]ed material that comprised the [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]] of the [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]]. Offering improved protection over [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[plastoid]], betaplast was known for its ballistic qualities<ref name="Visual">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and high-impact resistance.<ref name="TLJV">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] claimed it to be durable enough that not even a [[Wookiee]] could crush it&mdash;though when threatened she relented rather than allow [[Chewbacca]] to test her claim.<ref name="TFAn" />

Betaplast was also used in the neck guards and armored collars of the members of [[Canto Bight]]'s [[Uniformed Branch]].<ref name="TLJV" /> It was also used in the armor of [[Gideon's Praetorian Guard]].<ref name="TMVD">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref>
