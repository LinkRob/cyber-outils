'''Ephemera''' was a remote resort [[planet]]<ref name="Resistance Reborn" /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> with many [[spa]]s. During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Tibanna]] [[gas]] was mined to extinction by the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] before they abandoned the planet. After the empire left, the native inhabitants discovered [[tuusah]], a residue from mine runoff. Tuusah had medical properties that led to the establishment of a new industry, turning the planet into one of the largest spas in the galaxy. The capital city, [[Wish]], was once known as Outpost 665. Despite having no solid ground, Ephemera was home to a [[Unidentified feline species|species]] of [[feline]]s, which had been tamed by a [[Unidentified Rothkahar philosopher|Rothkahar philosopher]] who later discovered that their [[Tuusah|excrement]] had healing properties due to high concentrations of [[tibanna]] gas being naturally processed into tussah.<ref name="Resistance Reborn">''[[Resistance Reborn]]''</ref>

During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], [[Poe Dameron]] visited Ephemera seeking to recruit the aid of [[Maz Kanata]], who was relaxing at one of the planet's spas.<ref name="Resistance Reborn" />
