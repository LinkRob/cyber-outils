'''Chief manager''' was a title provided to [[Imperial Army]] [[General]] [[Moradmin Bast]] as part of his role on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]]. General Bast also served as an [[aide]]<ref name="SWCT" /> to the supreme commander of [[Battle Station Command]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>
