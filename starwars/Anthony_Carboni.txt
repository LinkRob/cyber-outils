'''Anthony Carboni''' is an actor who hosted the ''[[Celebration Anaheim (2015)|Celebration Anaheim]]'' and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]] - World Premiere''. In [[2018]], Carboni is also the co-host of ''[[The Star Wars Show]]'' alongside [[Andi Gutierrez]],<ref name="SW Show 34" /> and in [[September]] 2018, he became the host of the webseries ''[[Science and Star Wars]]''.<ref name="SWS">{{StarWarsShow|jb2dMryFHJA|Astronaut Cady Coleman, New 'Science and Star Wars' Sneak Peek, and Your Star Wars Show Fan Art!|sw_url=video/astronaut-cady-coleman-new-science-and-star-wars-sneak-peek-and-your-star-wars-show-fan-art}}</ref> He has also worked on ''Indie Game: The Movie'' ([[2012]]) and the ''Doctor Who 50th Anniversary Live Pre-Show'' ([[2013]]).

Along with [[Andi Gutierrez]] and [[Corey Vidal]], he was a host for the [[Star Wars]] [[Celebration Anaheim (2022)|Celebration Anaheim]].<ref name="SWCE2022">{{SW|url=news/watch-star-wars-celebration-anaheim-live-on-starwars-com-powered-by-verizon|text=Watch ''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim Live on StarWars.com, Powered by Verizon!}}</ref>
