The '''fall of Yordain Core''' was an event which took place at the [[Yordain Core]] where the individual [[Zab]], who was eventually a [[Sergeant]] in the [[61st Mobile Infantry]], experienced a struggle for survival and was present for a blessing from a [[Priest-king/Canon|priest-king]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> Around [[5 ABY]],<ref name="5 ABY">{{SW|url=news/shadow-fall-author-interview|text=In ''Shadow Fall'', the Dark Secrets of ''Alphabet Squadron'' Revealed}} establishes that ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'' takes place approximately half a [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Battle of Endor]], while ''[[Before the Awakening]]'' states [[Kes Dameron]] and [[Shara Bey]] moved to [[Yavin 4]] around six [[Standard month|months]] after the Battle of [[Endor]]. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates their move to [[5 ABY]], the events of ''Shadow Fall'' must also take place around that [[time]].</ref> he was reminded of the event during a [[scout]]ing mission to the [[planet]] [[Catadra]], and the sergeant told this to his comrades in the 61st on the planet [[Troithe]] after. Zab told the story of his survival and the blessing to the other [[New Republic soldier|soldiers]] as well as some [[New Republic pilot|pilots]] from the New Republic's [[Alphabet Squadron]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
The fall of Yordain Core was mentioned in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" />
