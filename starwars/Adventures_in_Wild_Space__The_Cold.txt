'''''Adventures in Wild Space: The Cold''''' is a book by [[Cavan Scott]] and the fifth entry in the ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' young-readers novel series. The book is published by [[Egmont]] UK Ltd on [[March 9]], [[2017]] and comprises 160 pages. [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] published a U.S. edition of the title on [[October 24]], [[2017]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Milo and Lina Graf have picked up the trail of their kidnapped parents&mdash;but an ambush in the depths of Wild Space leaves them stranded on a desolate ice planet. With an old enemy out for revenge, can they survive THE COLD?''
