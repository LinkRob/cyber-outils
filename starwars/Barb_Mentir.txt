'''Barb Mentir''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] that was part of [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang|pirate cartel]]. During a [[Galactic Republic]] [[mission to Florrum]], Mentir and [[Turk Falso]] conspired against Ohnaka to take the [[spice]] meant for ransom for themselves. Mentir utilized a [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttle]] to shoot down the [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]] carrying the Republic envoy. Eventually, when Falso's plan fell apart, Mentir was [[Death|killed]] when [[Count]] [[Dooku]] used [[the Force]] to manipulate Falso into shooting Mentir during his escape.

==Biography==
Barb Mentir was a [[Gender|male]] [[Weequay]] member of [[Hondo Ohnaka]]'s [[Ohnaka Gang]]. When the opportunistic Ohnaka captured the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and soon after [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], the pirates demanded a ransom from the Republic in the from of spice. When a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]] traveled to the pirates' [[Hondo's complex|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Florrum]], Mentir and Ohanka's second in command [[Turk Falso]] decided to shoot down the ship. Mentir piloted a [[Unidentified Flarestar-class attack shuttle (Barb Mentir)|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttle]] and used missiles to shoot down the Republic ship. The pirates then claimed the spice and traveled back to their base.<ref name="Gungan General" />

However, the survivors of the crash would advance across the [[Doshar fields]] to the base, eventually assisting in freeing the three captives. As the Sith Lord escaped, he advanced on Mentir and Falso, who were outside with their ship. [[Force choke|Choking]] Falso, he used he Force to manipulate him into shooting and killing Mentir with his own [[Weequay blaster pistol|blaster]].<ref name="Gungan General" />
