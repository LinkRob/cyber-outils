'''Brother Cyst''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Duros]] and a member of the [[Kouhun (order)|Kouhun]], a secretive and mystical order of [[assassin]]s operating in the [[Anoat sector]]. A [[Color|turquoise]]-skinned Duro, he had a gouged-out right [[eye]] and a humped back.<ref name="Uprising" /> He developed dozens of [[poison]]s for the Kouhun.<ref name="Beacons">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Run: "The Antidote"</ref>
