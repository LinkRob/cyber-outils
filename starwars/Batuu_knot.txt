'''Batuu knots''' were a type of knot&mdash;considered complex by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]]&mdash;that could be used to tie things<ref name="A Test of Courage">''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]''</ref> and shared their name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> the [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] had become familiar with Batuu knots and taught her [[protocol droid]] [[J-6]] how to tie using them after they were sent to the outpost [[Port Haileap]] on the Outer Rim planet [[Haileap]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> J-6 was part of a group stranded on the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Wevo]]. After [[Wevo crisis|capturing]] the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s [[Klinith Da]] and [[Gwishi]] while on Wevo, J-6 used a series of Batuu knots to tie up the pirates and put them in the cargo deck of their [[Starship|ship]]. After the [[droid]] had finished tying up the two, Rwoh mentally noted her use of the Batuu knots.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
