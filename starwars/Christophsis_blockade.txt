A '''blockade fleet''' of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] was active early in the [[Clone Wars]] and participated in the [[Battle of Christophsis]]. The [[fleet]] was led by CIS [[Admiral]] [[Trench]]

==Description==
The [[blockade]] [[fleet]] was under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Trench]], who was supported by [[Commander]] [[TI-99]]. Admiral Trench led the fleet from his [[flagship]], the ''[[Invincible]]'', a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]]. The [[dreadnought]] was supported by six [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]], and at least twenty-three [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]].<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> Furthermore, the fleet's arsenal included at least one [[Hardcell-class Battle Refit|''Hardcell''-class Battle Refit]]<ref name="Armada">{{Armada|set=Separatist Alliance Fleet Starter|cardname=''Hardcell''-class Battle Refit}}</ref> and at least eight [[C-9979 landing craft]].<ref name="
The Hidden Enemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighters]] and [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Fighter/Bombers]] were also part of the fleet.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />

==History==
{{Quote|I hate to admit it, but... good job, Anakin. We are beginning our attack on the separatist fleet.|Obi-Wan Kenobi to Anakin Skywalker on the Christophsis blockade|<ref name="Cat and Mouse" />}}
Early into the [[Clone Wars]], and soon after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] took the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Christophsis]]. Taking the planet gave the CIS access to the [[Corellian Run]], as well as a world full of minerals. The CIS were expecting the Republic to strike back however, and as such created a thirty [[Starship|ship]] blockade of the planet under the command of [[Admiral]] [[Trench]].<ref name="FL">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> The fleet also served the purpose of trapping [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]], his relief mission, and cutting off supplies from the outside.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> The blockade engaged the [[Republic Navy|Republic naval]] [[Open Circle Fleet|fleet]] and [[Skywalker's fleet|taskforce]] under [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|Generals]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] several times throughout the battle. The blockade was finally defeated when [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] reinforcements led by [[Jedi General]] [[Yoda]] and [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]] arrived.<ref name="TCW film" />
