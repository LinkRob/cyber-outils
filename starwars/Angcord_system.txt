The '''Angcord system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square I-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the planet [[Angcord]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Angcord system was first mentioned in the second iteration of the [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]],<ref name="Appendix" /> an appendix of star systems written by [[Jason Fry]] and released on [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 13]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' originally placed Angcord in the Inner Rim Territories,<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> and the appendix repeated that placement;<ref name="Appendix" /> however, the update to the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on the same day instead erroneously moved Angcord to the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>
