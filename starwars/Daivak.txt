'''Daivak''' was a rocky dry [[planet]]<ref name="THR 6" /> in the [[Daivak system]] in the [[Karthakk sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], it was considered part of the [[Galactic Frontier]] and was inhabited by settlers. The planet became overrun by the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s known as the [[Drengir]] several [[Standard month|months]] into their [[Drengir crisis|infestation]] across the frontier, and a combined [[Jedi Order]] and [[Hutt Clan]] force was sent to fight back the Drengir on Daivak.<ref name="THR 6" />
