'''1980 Topps ''Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back'' Series 1''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[1980]].<ref name="Volume Two" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], and featured stills from the film and promotional photos, all within a red and silver border. The first 10 cards were "Star File" character profile cards. The "Story Digest" themed cards told the story of the movie on the back, and "Space Paintings" cards featured [[Ralph McQuarrie]] concept art on the front and "Movie Facts" on the back.<ref name="Volume Two" />

A sub-set of sticker cards were included, the backs of which included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle.<ref name="Volume Two" />

Two more series of the set would follow.

==Continuity==
Most of the profile cards gave characters ages at the time of ''The Empire Strikes Back'' that conflicted with later sources. Here, [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] was 34 years old, [[Lando Calrissian/Legends|Lando Calrissian]] 36, [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] 973, [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] 50, [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett]] 43, and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Princess Leia]] 20 (two years younger than her later-revealed twin brother, Luke Skywalker). Chewbacca's home planet of [[Kashyyyk/Legends|Kashyyyk]] used the alternate spelling seen in early sources, "Kazhyyyk." Solo's planet of birth was listed as "Unknown."

Card 21 featured an image of [[2-1B/Legends|2-1B]] examining a dead [[Tauntaun/Legends|tauntaun]], as shown briefly in the movie. The card text suggests the tauntaun is the one Skywalker had been riding in previous scenes.
