'''Cova Nell''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[transport]] and [[starfighter]] [[pilot]] in the [[Resistance navy|naval branch]] of the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]].

==Biography==
[[File:Cova_Nell_Resistance_Transport_SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|Cova Nell piloted the Resistance transport that delivered General Leia Organa to Takodana.]]
Cova Nell piloted the [[Resistance transport]] that delivered [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and her team to [[Takodana]]. She landed her transport outside the ruins of [[Takodana Castle]] after [[Poe Dameron]]'s squadron [[Battle of Takodana|drove off]] the forces of the First Order following their temporary capture of [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], [[Finn]], and the [[droid]] [[BB-8]].<ref name="SWZ">{{FFGXW2|set=Resistance Transport Expansion Pack|cardname=Cova Nell}}</ref>

Nell was a co-pilot<ref name="Complete Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> for [[Pammich Nerro Goode]] in one of the thirty [[U-55 orbital loadlifter]] that were used to evacuate the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] after the First Order [[Battle of Oetchi|attacked]] the Resistance [[fleet]].<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Her [[Starship|vessel's]] passengers included General Organa and [[Captain]] [[Poe Dameron]]; it was one of the transports that made it to the surface of the [[planet]] [[Crait]].<ref name="TLJ" /> During planetfall they were comforted by General Organa, who reflected that the pilots would soon feel survivors guilt.<ref name="TLJ novel">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> On Crait, the Resistance made a [[Battle of Crait|last stand]] against the First Order in an old [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Crait outpost|outpost]], with those who survived escaping in the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="TLJ" />

==Personality and traits==
Nell had fair skin and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="TLJVD" />

==Equipment==
She wore a [[Color|white]] [[Armor|helmet]] with a yellow [[Clothing|flight suit]] made of [[Fire|flame]]-resistant material with a white harness and brown [[boot]]s.<ref name="TLJVD" /> An [[Aurebesh]] inscription on her pilot helmet read "Hello nasty."<ref name="TLJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cova Nell was portrayed by Daniela Tlumacova in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> The character was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TLJVD" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref> The [[Aurebesh]] inscription on Nell's [[pilot]] helmet, "Hello nasty,"<ref name="TLJ" /> is a reference to the [[Wikipedia:Beastie Boys|Beastie Boys]] [[Wikipedia:Hello Nasty|album]] of the same name.
