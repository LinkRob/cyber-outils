The '''Corporate Alliance Policy Administration Directorate''', also known as the '''Corporate Alliance security forces''', were the security forces of the [[Corporate Alliance]]. They were pledged to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] by Corporate Alliance [[Magistrate]] [[Passel Argente]] with his signing of the [[Geonosian accord]] and integrated into the [[Confederacy military]] shortly before the [[Clone Wars]].
