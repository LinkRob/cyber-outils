In [[3 BBY]], the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' was '''ambushed''' by the [[Galactic Empire]] in an [[ICM-092792|Imperial construction module]] that [[orbit]]ed [[Geonosis]]. The rebels boarded the construction module while investigation intelligence reports from the rebellion that the Empire had been constructing something over Geonosis. Unknown to the rebels, this was the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], an [[armor]]ed [[battle station]] capable of destroying entire planets, which had already been moved. When the rebels boarded one of the construction modules, they were quickly attacked by [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and several [[stormtrooper]]s. The rebels fought their way back to the ''Ghost'', but one of the rebels, [[Garazeb Orrelios]], remained locked in single combat with Agent Kallus. The ''Ghost'' departed while Orrelios headed for an [[escape pod]], but he was followed by Kallus, who also boarded the departing pod.

The ''Ghost'' and Imperial forces engaged in a firefight outside of the module, which damaged the escape pod and sent it crashing onto [[Bahryn]], an [[Water|icy]] [[moon]] of Geonosis. Orrelios and Kallus were forced to work together in order to survive. In the process, they learned more about one another's pasts and motivations, and Kallus began to recognize the cold realities of serving in the Empire.

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the growing rebellion, intelligence gathered by the rebellion suggested that the Empire was building something in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]],<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> the world on which the [[Clone Wars]] had [[First Battle of Geonosis|begun]] many years earlier.<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> Several [[ICM-092792|Imperial construction modules]] were in orbit of Geonosis, which at one point&mdash;unknown to the rebels at the time&mdash;<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> were used to support the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]],<ref name="Rebels Recon">{{RebelsRecon|9E3T5LhlV_c|#2.16: Inside "The Honorable Ones" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-the-honorable-ones}}</ref> an [[armor]]ed [[battle station]] capable of destroying entire planets.<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The station was moved following the rise of the Empire,<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> and the [[Geonosian|population]] of Geonosis was [[Sterilization of Geonosis|destroyed]].<ref name="Continuity Note">"[[The Honorable Ones]]" suggests that there is no life remaining on Geonosis. In [[Darth Vader (2015) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 4]], it was revealed that most of the world's population was killed by the Empire.</ref>

The [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'', who were part of the rebellion, traveled to Geonosis to investigate the reports from rebel intelligence. They found the construction modules and deduced that whatever was being built, it had to have been massive to justify building it in space rather than on the surface of the planet. The rebels also learned, through [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]]'s connection to [[the Force]], that there was no sign of life on the planet. To investigate further, the rebels boarded one of the construction modules.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

==The ambush==
{{Quote|Keep it running in case things&mdash;''"<br />"''In case things go like they usually do?''"<br />"''Pretty much, yeah.|Kanan Jarrus and Hera Syndulla|<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />}}
After docking aboard the construction module, four of the rebel crew members&mdash;Bridger, the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], [[Lasat]] warrior [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and [[Mandalorian]] warrior [[Sabine Wren]]&mdash;departed the [[Starship|ship]]. The crew's leader, [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]], remained behind in order to keep the ''Ghost'' ready to depart in the event they needed a quick exit. Captain [[Rex]] also remained behind. As the rebels began making their way through the module, where they intended to check the primary [[databank]]s to see if they could uncover what the Empire was building, they were spotted by an Imperial astromech droid. The droid was there as part of a trap set by [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], who had lured the rebels to the station and intended to ambush them.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

[[File:Kallus_ambushes_the_rebels.png|thumb|right|300px|Kallus ambushes the rebels.]]
Just as the rebels began to suspect that they were walking into a trap, the droid closed the blast doors behind them and separated the rebels from the ''Ghost''. Kallus then revealed himself, flanked by many [[stormtrooper]]s. Most of the rebels contended with the stormtroopers, while Orrelios engaged Kallus in single-combat; the two had a long-standing rivalry, as Kallus was involved in the [[Siege of Lasan|destruction]] of Orrelios' people and [[Lasan|planet]]. Jarrus contacted the ''Ghost'' to let them know what was happening, so Rex ordered the rebel [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]] ("Chopper") to leave the ship and assist the rebels. Chopper found the Imperial astromech droid, pushed it out of the way, and began opening the doors. The Imperial droid fought back, but it was ultimately defeated when Chopper electrocuted it. This allowed Chopper to open the doors, letting most of the rebels return to the ''Ghost''. Orrelios remained locked in combat with Kallus, however, and told the others to leave while he headed for an [[escape pod]]. Syndulla agreed and the ''Ghost'' flew out of the module, flying past three [[All Terrain Defense Pod]]s that were stationed on the outside of the facility.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

Orrelios boarded one of the pods, but so too did Agent Kallus, and the two continued their struggle as the escape pod blasted away from the station. As it made its way towards Geonosis, three [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s attacked the ''Ghost''. The ''Ghost'' was able to fend them off, while Orrelios and Kallus scuffled aboard the escape pod. Their fight caused damage to the craft, knocking it off course and sending it towards [[Bahryn]], an [[Water|icy]] [[moon]] of Geonosis. The pod crashed beneath the surface of the moon, while the ''Ghost'' successfully destroyed the Imperial forces.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The only thing I know about Geonosis is that the population is gone. I never asked questions.''"<br />"''Well, maybe you should start. Or are you afraid of the answers you'll get? Afraid you'll learn the Geonosians were wiped out by your precious Empire?''"<br />"''And why would we do that? What could possibly be the point?''"<br />"''Ah, good questions. Chase the answers, and maybe you'll learn the truth.|Agent Kallus and Garazeb Orrelios|<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />}}
[[File:Zeb_and_Kallus_fight_a_bonzami.png|thumb|left|300px|Orrelios and Kallus fight a bonzami.]]
The escape pod's crash onto Bahryn broke Kallus' [[Limb|leg]]. Orrelios and the injured agent found the craft's [[Escape pod transponder|transponder]], but the signal was not strong enough to make it through the ice. They had to reach the surface in order to transmit a distress signal, though it was unclear who would arrive first: the rebels or the Empire. The two also faced dangers in the form of the rapidly dropping temperature and ice creatures known as [[bonzami]]. As they worked to survive, Orrelios and Kallus began to learn more about one another and how they were both warriors fighting for causes that they believed in. Kallus also revealed that his role in the fall of Lasan was exaggerated, and that his actions there were based on the horrors that a [[Unidentified Lasat mercenary|Lasat mercenary]] once inflicted on his unit on the planet [[Onderon]]&mdash;forging a belief that Lasats were dangerous. The two were finally able to escape when they climbed up a pillar that held the ice up, escaping from two bonzamis as they did so. Once on the surface, they transmitted their distress signal.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

The ''Ghost'' found Orrelios the next morning. Orrelios told Kallus that the rebels would treat the agent fairly if he chose to leave with them, but Kallus decided to take his chances in the cold and wait for the Empire to find him. The two bade one another farewell, having come to an understanding, and Kallus witnessed the warm reception that Orrelios received when the Lasat returned to the ''Ghost''. Once Kallus was rescued and returned to a [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]], Orrelios' reception was starkly contrasted when Kallus received a cold reception. [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] did not acknowledge his rescue and safe return, and Kallus retreated to his quarters alone.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" />

[[File:SWR_S4_Kallus_1.png|thumb|right|250px|Kallus became a member of the Rebel Alliance.]]
Kallus's newfound sympathies towards the rebels soon developed into the initiative to provide assistance for their cause. He became a [[Fulcrum]] agent and provided Phoenix Squadron with Imperial intelligence,<ref name="An Inside Man">{{Rebels|An Inside Man}}</ref> including that of would-be defectors [[Wedge Antilles]], [[Derek Klivian]], and [[Rake Gahree]] at the [[Skystrike Academy]]<ref name="The Antilles Extraction">{{Rebels|The Antilles Extraction}}</ref> and reports regarding [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn's]] search for Phoenix Squadron's [[Chopper Base|base]].<ref name="Through Imperial Eyes">{{Rebels|Through Imperial Eyes}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Atollon]], after his rebel allegiances were discovered, Kallus defected from the Empire<ref name="Zero Hour">{{Rebels|Zero Hour}}</ref> and joined the Rebel Alliance at their [[Great Temple|base]] on [[Yavin 4]].<ref name="In the Name of the Rebellion">{{Rebels|In the Name of the Rebellion}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ambush on the Imperial construction module appeared in "[[The Honorable Ones]]," an episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|second season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="The Honorable Ones" /> The event formed the basis for the story between Garazeb Orrelios and Agent Kallus, which was designed to show different aspects of the conflict between the rebellion and the Empire and how perceptions of one's enemies are not always accurate.<ref name="Rebels Recon" />
