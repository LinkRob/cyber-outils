The '''8-T gardening model''', known as '''8-T droids''' or '''Aytees''', were a model of [[droid]] utilized for [[garden]]ing. The droids had a similar appearance to that of an [[astromech droid]] dome, with [[magnet]]ic treads and pincer claws that the droids used for pruning [[plant]]s. The droids were programmed to attack any being that posed a threat to their garden. A number of 8-T droids were left aboard the [[Amaxine Space Station|Amaxine space station]] long after it was abandoned with several [[Drengir]] imprisoned within.<ref name="Into the Dark" />
