A '''transmitter tower''' was used by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[planet]] [[Crucival]] to communicate with other worlds. Protected by [[stormtrooper]]s, [[probe droid]]s, and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s, the [[Comm tower|tower]] could activate its own [[deflector shield]] dome as well. While the Imperials rarely left their base, [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] patrolled the surrounding area. In [[1 BBY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] went to [[Battle of the Tower|attack]] the transmitter tower, with the [[Imperial Military|Empire's forces]] there enlisting Crucival residents to help protect the structure. Many of the new recruits were [[Death|killed]], and most of the Imperial forces hid behind the tower's shield dome. After five [[Standard day|days]] of fighting, the tower was destroyed.

==Description==
The transmitter tower was located on<ref name="BTC" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Timelines Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]] &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Crucival system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Crucival]] and used by the [[Galactic Empire]] to communicate with other worlds. The [[Comm tower|tower]] was protected by a number of Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s, [[probe droid]]s, and [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s, as well as a large [[deflector shield]] dome which could be used upon activation.<ref name="BTC" />

==History==

[[File:Twilight_Company_patch.png|thumb|left|250px|The 61st Mobile Infantry (logo pictured) attacked and destroyed the transmitter tower.]]
The Empire used the transmitter tower on Crucival<ref name="BTC" /> by [[6 BBY]].<ref name="6 BBY">The section of ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'' where the [[Comm tower|transmitter tower]] first appears is set thirteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]], which [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] in [[19 BBY]], according to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, the transmitter tower must have been used by the [[Galactic Empire]] by [[6 BBY]].</ref> While [[TIE series|TIE]] [[Starfighter|fighters]] made frequent patrols around the surrounding area, Imperial personnel rarely left the tower's grounds. [[Hazram Namir]], a young [[soldier]] who served the [[Malkhanis]] clan under the name Donin, was startled by a TIE fighter heading back to the transmitter tower as it flew over many Malkhani warriors preparing for a battle against another faction. [[Unidentified Malkhani soldier|Another soldier]] warned Namir not to mess with the Imperials or go near the tower as it would likely get him [[Death|killed]].<ref name="BTC" />

In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="1 BBY" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] targeted Crucival's transmitter tower. In preparation for the attack, the Imperials there offered [[blaster]]s to Crucival residents who were willing to help defend the tower. Namir and a [[Hazram Namir's band|band]] of unaligned soldiers he had recruited took up the Empire's offer. However, when the 61st [[Battle of the Tower|attacked]], Namir's allies were massacred and the [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] hid under the tower's shield dome, save for some probe [[droid]]s and TIE fighters. The rebels continued to rain fire on the shield dome with its [[artillery]] and the 61st's [[CR90 corvette]], the ''[[Thunderstrike]]''. The tower fell on the fifth [[Standard day|day]] of the battle, and [[scavenger]]s went to pick at the battlefield for [[weapon]]s and other resources.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The transmitter tower appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
