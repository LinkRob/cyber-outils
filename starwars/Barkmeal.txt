'''Barkmeal''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|food]].<ref name="LSW" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|II}}&mdash;which ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'' adapts&mdash;to [[22 BBY]].</ref> a [[merchant]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] sold fresh cups of Barkmeal, advertising it as a good start to one's [[Standard day|day]]. The merchant was overheard by the [[Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] while they searched for the [[bounty hunter]] [[Zam Wesell]].<ref name="LSW" />

==Behind the scenes==
Barkmeal was introduced to the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the non-canon [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', which was released in [[2022]]. In the game, the player can hear a merchant in the background advertising his Barkmeal, though it cannot actually be purchased in-game. The player first hears the merchant while playing the story campaign for the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], though the dialogue can also be heard while playing free-play mode on Coruscant at any other point.<ref name="LSW" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Barkmeal/Legends|barkmeal]] was introduced in the [[2008]] novel ''[[Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor]]'', authored by [[Matthew Stover]].<ref name="Mindor">''[[Luke Skywalker and the Shadows of Mindor]]''</ref>
