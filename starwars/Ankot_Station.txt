'''Ankot Station''' was a [[space station]]<ref name="Free Fall" /> located in the [[Brema sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]],<ref name="Appendix" /> near the edge of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. The station was under the control of [[Galactic Empire]] during its [[Imperial Era|reign]], becoming a highly trafficked base. After the [[Battle of Jakku]], the station was controlled by various [[spicerunner]] gangs before the [[New Republic]] formally took it over. However, the New Republic's control was in name only, and Ankot Station was abandoned.<ref name="Free Fall" />

It had a viewing platform, which in earlier days was likely a popular leisure destination, judging by the stores and vending machines all around it.<ref name="Free Fall" />

[[Zorii Bliss]], [[Poe Dameron]], and the [[droid]] [[EV-6B6]], members of the [[Spice Runners of Kijimi]], visited the station to make a deal with the [[smuggler]] [[Alfris Sotin]]. However, Sotin betrayed the Spice Runners to the [[Guavian Death Gang]], leading to a [[Skirmish at Ankot Station|skirmish]] in which Bliss and Dameron escaped.<ref name="Free Fall" />
