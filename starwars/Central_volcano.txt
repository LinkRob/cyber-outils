The '''central volcano''' of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Mustafar]] was one of the [[Magma|lava]] world's few constant features. Lava poured out of the spot at nearly 100,000 [[liter]]s per [[second]] and pooled at the giant [[volcano]]'s base. Toward the north, heavy volcanic activity took place in the [[Burning Plains]].<ref name="BMF17" />
