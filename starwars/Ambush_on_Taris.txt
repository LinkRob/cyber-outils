The '''ambush on Taris''' took place<ref name="Aphra 6" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> when the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] was ambushed on<ref name="Aphra 6" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Taris">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Taris system]]</ref> [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Tynnra Pamlo">{{Databank|senator-tynnra-pamlo|Senator Tynnra Pamlo}}</ref> [[Taris]] by [[bounty hunter]]s [[Bounty|hired]] by [[Lady]] [[Domina Tagge]]&mdash;[[Executive officer|head]] of the [[Tagge Corporation]]&mdash;after Aphra had blown up her [[House of Tagge|nephew]] [[Ronen Tagge]]. The [[Hunting|hunters]] failed to capture the archaeologist on Taris and after they later [[Ambush on Burnin Konn|failed]] to apprehend her on<ref name="Aphra 6" /> the Outer Rim world<ref name="Art of Uprising">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> [[Burnin Konn]], Aphra traveled to [[Arveesh Station]]. While Aphra was [[Skirmish at Arveesh Station|attacked]] by bounty hunters at the station, she mentioned the ambush on Taris and asked why she was being hunted.<ref name="Aphra 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ambush on Taris was mentioned in flashbacks in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 6|sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. The issue was written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> on [[November 25]], 2020. Prior to the release of the comic, the ambush was mentioned in a preview article of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 18]] of the same year.<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-doctor-aphra-6-exclusive-preview|text=There's a New Bounty in Marvel's ''Doctor Aphra'' #6 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
