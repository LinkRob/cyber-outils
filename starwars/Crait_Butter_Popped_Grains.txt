'''Crait Butter Popped Grains''' was a form of popped [[Surabat grain]] served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. The kernels were mixed with [[butter]] and sprinkled with [[Color|red]] [[salt]] from [[Crait]], an uninhabited mineral world.<ref name="KSK" />

==Behind the scenes==
This popcorn is available exclusively at Kat Saka's Kettle in [[Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. The menu describes the popcorn as being served, "with Crait Red Salt."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/kat-sakas-kettle/menus|text=Disneyland: Kat Saka's Kettle Snack Menu}}</ref>
