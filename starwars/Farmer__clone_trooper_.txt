'''Farmer''' was a [[clone trooper]]<ref name="Black Spire Surplus" /> who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="Clone troopers" /> during the [[Clone Wars]] and fought alongside fellow [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]] [[Stacks]].<ref name="Black Spire Surplus" /> After the Clone Wars, the two joined opposite sides of the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]. The two clones eventually decided to retire together<ref name="Andor Props" /> and reunited on [[Batuu]] to open up the [[First Order Cargo|Black Spire Surplus]] military supply shop.<ref name="Black Spire Surplus" />
