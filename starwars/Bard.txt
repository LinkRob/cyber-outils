'''Bard''' was a modified [[R1-series astromech droid]] that belonged to the [[Batuu]] [[historian]] and "Lore-weaver" [[Tohan Lege]]. Lege found the discarded remains of an old [[droid]] behind the [[Droid Depot]] in the [[Land Port]] of [[Black Spire Outpost]] and recruited local [[Technician|mechanics]] to help create a new one. Assembled into its current form from assorted junkyard parts, Bard helped Lege in his mission to assemble and record an oral history of [[Black Spire Outpost]] and ensure that it could be carried on into the future. In the [[Standard year|years]] after his [[death]], Bard continued the Lore-weaver's legacy by replaying his stories for the Batuu festival known as the [[Fire of the Rising Moons]].<ref name="Disneyland" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bard was created for "Fire of the Rising Moons," an alternate audio presentation of [[Disneyland]]'s fireworks shows for guests within the boundaries of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]. A physical figure of the droid debuted on [[May 24]], [[2024]], placed on a platform near [[Docking Bay 5]], near the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/05/new-bard-droid-arrives-at-star-wars-galaxys-edge-in-disneyland-for-fire-of-the-rising-moons-fireworks|text=New Bard Droid Arrives at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in Disneyland for 'Fire of the Rising Moons' Fireworks|work=WDW News Today|archivedate=20240524210828}}</ref>
