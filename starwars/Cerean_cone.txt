'''Cerean cones''' were a type of [[Ice cream/Legends|ice cream]] cone. Scoops of soft ice cream, such as [[Denta bean ice cream]] and [[Trammistan chocolate ice cream]], was served in Cerean cones in [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] for 1.7 [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]].
