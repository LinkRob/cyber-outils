'''Akko''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[smuggler]] who led a small gang. He worked with [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] in a scam on [[Garel]], and later encountered them on [[Balnab]] shortly after Solo assisted the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] with the [[Cyrkon Extraction]]. Akko convinced Solo to help him smuggle [[spice]] to [[Troiken]], but they were discovered by the [[Galactic Empire]], who forced them to hand over the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] they had received.<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
