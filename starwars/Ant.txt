'''Ants''' were a [[species]] of [[insect]]s that could be found on the [[planet]] [[Salient I]].<ref name="Catalyst" /> It was felt among the people of [[Skuhl]] that they were ants and the [[Galactic Empire]] was a giant that would take no notice of an [[Outer Rim Territories]] planet like them, though this was proven false as the Empire established a presence there and brought destruction.<ref name="rebelrising" />
