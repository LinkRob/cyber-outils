'''Corrections officer''',<ref name="Junior novel" /> also called the '''correctional officer''',<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> was the title of an [[New Republic officer|officer]] in the<ref name="Junior novel" /> [[New Republic Correctional Corps]]. [[Lieutenant]] [[Lant Davan]] held the position aboard the [[New Republic Correctional Transport]] ''[[Bothan-5]]''.<ref name="Chapter 10" /> When the [[prison ship]] was [[Bothan-Five incident|infiltrated]] near the [[Dilestri system]]<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|6}}</ref> Davan faced the infiltrators, only to be [[Death|killed]] by a [[knife]] throw from the [[Twi'lek]] [[mercenary]] [[Xi'an]].<ref name="Chapter 6">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 6: The Prisoner}}</ref>

A corrections [[officer]] donned a blue [[Clothing|uniform]] with a [[New Republic provisional-era crest]] on the left shoulder. Their white shoes<ref name="Chapter 6" /> that resembled those of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]]<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> and their [[belt]] displayed an [[officer's disk]] upon the buckle. The white [[helmet]] of the officer, which featured a black tinted visor,<ref name="Chapter 6" /> was the same type of helmet as the ones used by the [[Alderaanian consular security]]<ref name="Rogue One UVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> and could include the officer's designation printed in [[Aurebesh]] on the side. Corrections officers could possess a [[A-180 blaster]], [[holster]]ed on their belt, and a [[Homing beacon|tracking beacon]].<ref name="Chapter 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
A corrections officer first appeared in "[[Chapter 6: The Prisoner]]," the sixth episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]] of [[Jon Favreau]]'s live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', which was directed by [[Rick Famuyiwa]]<ref name="Chapter 6" /> and released on [[December 13]], [[2019]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|date=2019-09-24|format=[[Wikipedia:PDF|PDF]]|work=[[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International|Walt Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]]|pages=[https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf#page=1]|archivedate=20210623202717}}</ref> The position was later identified in [[Joe Schreiber]]'s [[The Mandalorian Junior Novel|junior novelization]] of ''The Mandalorian'' Season One, released in [[2021]].<ref name="Junior novel" />
