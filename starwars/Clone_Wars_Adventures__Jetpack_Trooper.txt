'''Clone Wars Adventures: Jetpack Trooper''' is a single-player online flash player game found on [[StarWars.com]]. It is a tie-in to the MMORPG [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures (video game)|''Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures'']].

{{Game-stub}}

==Gameplay==
The player controls a [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] equipped with a [[Jetpack/Legends|jetpack]]. The objective is to reach the top of the communication tower. Upon doing so, the player is rewarded with a secret code that unlocks a bonus speeder bike for use in ''Clone Wars Adventures''.
