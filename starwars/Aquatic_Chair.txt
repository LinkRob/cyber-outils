The '''Aquatic Chair''' was the collective name of a custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]] and a pair of [[Blaster Remote]]s owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Command Chair (Sprocket)|standard chair and remote set]]. The chair featured a [[Color|purple]] base with swirling pink patterns, and black, silver, and gray components, while the [[remote]]s were pink, orange, gold, and varying shades of purple, with black [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aquatic Chair was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sprocket's Command Chair, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aquatic Programmer]].<ref name="Hunters" />
