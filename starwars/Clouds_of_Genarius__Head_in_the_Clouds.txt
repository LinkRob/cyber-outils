"'''Clouds of Genarius: Head in the Clouds'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the first episode of the [[Clouds of Genarius trilogy]] and was written by [[Jason Nichols]].

==Plot summary==
Recently hired scientist [[Shilaea Motacc]] was sent to the resort city of [[Rorkee]], where she was kidnapped. The group of heroes are hired by the [[Usable Resource Location, Recovery, and Development Corporation]] to travel to Rorkee undercover as archaeology students to discover what happened to her and to rescue her. Ultimately, Motacc is discovered under the heroes' hotel and returned to [[Cularin/Legends|Cularin]].
