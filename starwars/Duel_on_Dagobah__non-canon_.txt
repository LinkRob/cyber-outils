This '''duel''' took place on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Dagobah/Legends|Dagobah]] some months after the [[Battle of Hoth/Legends|Battle of Hoth]], between [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] and the combined force of the [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] and his student [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]]. During the confrontation, Vader learned that Organa was in fact his daughter and that his son, [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], had died months before on [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]]. Vader was eventually [[Blaster/Legends|blasted]] and killed by [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]].
[[File:JediLeia-SWITESB3.jpg|thumb|right|200px|[[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] in her [[Jedi apparel/Legends|Jedi apparel]] while fighting her [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|father.]]]]

==Behind the scenes==
This duel appears in the [[Infinities]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] ''[[Star Wars Infinities: The Empire Strikes Back]]'', and thus is [[Canon|non-canon]]. The story represents an alternative scenario for [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] in the event that Luke Skywalker dies from his [[Wampa/Legends|wampa]] encounter on Hoth.
