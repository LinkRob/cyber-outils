'''Cygnats''' were small biting pests [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Thisspias]]<ref name="UP" /> in the [[Expansion Region]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> They could be deterred by dense [[hair]], such as the beard of [[Thisspiasian]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Oppo Rancisis]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> Almost two hundred [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Galactic Civil War]], a group of [[terrorist]]s who [[assassin]]ated the [[Blood Monarch]] of Thisspias wore something similar to a cygnat symbol. Because of that, when anti-monarchist abolitionist factions of Thisspiasians began wearing cygnat pins during the Galactic Civil War, it concerned even some moderate members of the [[species]].<ref name="UP" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], cygnats were first mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Character Encyclopedia" /> The [[Cygnat/Legends|creatures]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[1999]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]].<ref name="TPMVD">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
