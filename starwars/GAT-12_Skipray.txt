The '''GAT-12 Skipray''' was a [[starship]] that was on the military market during the [[Clone Wars]]. It was comparable in role to its contemporaries, the [[Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|''Firespray-31''-class patrol and attack craft]] and the [[SS-54 assault ship|SS-54 light assault gunship]].<ref name="KoF">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref>
