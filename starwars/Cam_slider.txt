A '''cam slider'''<ref name="PClassic" />&mdash;also referred to as a '''sensor slide'''<ref name="Yeager">''[[Star Wars Resistance: Le Secret de Yeager]]''</ref>&mdash;was a type of mechanical component utilized on [[starship]]s. A cam slider was installed on the [[racer]] [[Marcus Speedstar]]'s [[Racing starfighter|starship]],<ref name="PClassic" /> the ''[[Galaxy's Glory]]''.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="PClassicDate">{{ResistanceDate|1|9}}</ref> as part of Speedstar's preparation for the race on<ref name="PClassic" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Castilon]] known as the [[Platform Classic]], the racer instructed<ref name="PClassic" /> the [[Resistance]] [[spy]]<ref name="USWNE" /> [[Kazuda Xiono]]&mdash;who was helping Speedstar as a [[Technician|mechanic]]&mdash;to reorient his ship's cam slider. When Xiono struggled to find the correct tool for the job<ref name="PClassic" /> as well as which slider to adjust,<ref name="Yeager" /> Speedstar questioned the sarcastic claim of his brother, the mechanic [[Jarek Yeager]], that Xiono was the best mechanic under his employ. Xiono replied by stating that he was in the "top three" of such rankings.<ref name="PClassic" />

==Behind the scenes==
The cam slider was mentioned in "[[The Platform Classic]],"<ref name="PClassic" /> the ninth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' animated television series which premiered on [[November 25]], [[2018]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> It was first identified as a "sensor slide" in the [[2019]] French junior novel [[Star Wars Resistance: Le Secret de Yeager|''Yeager's Secret'']], written by [[Vanessa Rubio-Barreau]].<ref name="Yeager" />
