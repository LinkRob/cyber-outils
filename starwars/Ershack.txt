'''Ershack''' was a [[Colonel/Legends|colonel]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. He was responsible of the operations in the [[Kubilaï system]] and its vicinity.

==History==
Sometime after the [[Battle of Yavin/Legends|Battle of Yavin]], Ershack sent a team of Rebel operatives investigating on the ''[[Solemnis]]'' disappearance. The ship conveyed an important loading of [[Ytterbium]], stolen during an attack against an Imperial base on [[Abelard]] one month sooner.<ref name="L'écume de l'espace" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ershack was created by [[Jean Balczesak]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] scenario ''[[L'écume de l'espace]]'' which appeared in the French magazine ''[[Casus Belli 44]]''.<ref name="L'écume de l'espace" />
