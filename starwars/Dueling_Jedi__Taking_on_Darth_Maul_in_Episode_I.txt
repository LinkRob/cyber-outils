"'''Dueling Jedi: Taking on Darth Maul in Episode I'''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider 73|''Star Wars Insider'' 73]] and written by [[Ron Magid]].<ref name="Insider" /> It covers the making of the fight between [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]] and the two [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. It includes behind-the-scenes photos of the set, cast, and filming.<ref name="Insider" />
