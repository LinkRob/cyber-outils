The '''Fear and Dead Men Squad Pack''' ('''SWP21''') is an expansion for [[Atomic Mass Games]]' miniatures game [[Star Wars: Shatterpoint|''Star Wars'': Shatterpoint]]. It was released on [[January 26]], [[2024]], [[2023]], and contains miniatures of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[TK-289]], and two stormtroopers.

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''The iconic Darth Vader helps usher in a whole new era in'' Star Wars™: ''Shatterpoint! Wielding the full power of the dark side of the Force, Vader enters the game as one of the first Primary Units from the Galactic Civil War era, opening even more squad-building possibilities. He is flanked by a trio of fearsome Imperial Stormtroopers, including Lt. TK-289 as a Secondary Unit and two standard Stormtroopers as a Supporting Unit ready to bring the weight of the Empire to bear against their opponents. Rounding out this pack are all the Stat, Order, and Stance cards players need to add these characters to their squads.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

===Back blurb===
{{Quote|All I am surrounded by is fear. And dead men.|Darth Vader|<ref name="Asmodee" />}}
''One of the most terrifying commanders of the Galactic Empire, Darth Vader strikes down any foe at the behest of his dark master the Emperor. Known by many titles, Lord Vader's authority is all but absolute among the forces of the Galactic Empire. Wielding the full power of the dark side of the Force, Vader is capable of dispatching even the most skilled opponents swiftly and efficiently.''<ref name="Asmodee" />

''When "Darth Vader, the Emperor's Servant" takes to the field of battle, he commands the most experienced and competent of Imperial stormtroopers drawn from the ranks of the Empire's army. The mere sight of Darth Vader flanked by his stormtroopers strikes fear into those who would oppose the might of the Empire...''<ref name="Asmodee" />
