'''Arli''' was a [[Color|red]] [[ocean]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. During the [[Great Hyperspace Rush]], the planet was settled and had a [[hyperspace route]] mapped by the [[San Tekka clan]], and a hardy [[Red moss|moss]] was discovered on its islands that could be utilized in various materials. However, a more optimal location was soon mapped one [[star system]] over, and many of the settlers moved on, leaving the San Tekkas, who had made a home on the planet's [[Arli's moon|moon]]. When the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Deployment of the stormseeds|established]] the [[Occlusion Zone]], Arli was trapped behind the [[Stormwall]]. The Nihil prevented the moon's inhabitants from leaving, but they were eventually [[Mission to Arli's moon|rescued]] by [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] and [[Cair San Tekka]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />
