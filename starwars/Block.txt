'''Block''' was an [[admiral]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]{{'s}} [[Republic Navy|navy]], who served aboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Vigilance (Venator-class)|''Vigilance'']] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="DD" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Dark Disciple]]'' to [[19 BBY]].</ref> Block [[Commanding officer|commanded]] Republic naval forces as [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Quinlan Vos]] led an [[Destruction of the Vanqor listening station|attack]] on the Confederacy's [[Vanqor listening station|listening post]] on the<ref name="DD" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="DC">{{TCW|Dooku Captured}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Vanqor]]. As the crew prepared to descend to the planet's surface, the admiral asked Kenobi whether he wanted to launch a second wave of [[Starfighter|fighters]], to which the general accepted. Vos, who was secretly working with the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]], warned his [[Sith Master|master]] about the attack, and the Separatists were able to evacuate the facility before [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]] arrived.<ref name="DD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Block appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Dark Disciple]]'', written by [[Christie Golden]] and adapted from an unfinished eight-episode story arc for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series that was written by [[Katie Lucas]], [[Matt Michnovetz]], and [[Dave Filoni]].<ref name="DD" /> Block was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2008]] reference title ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'', written by [[Stephen J. Sansweet]], [[Pablo Hidalgo]], and several others.<ref name="CSWE">{{CSWECite|II|368|''Negotiator''}}</ref>
