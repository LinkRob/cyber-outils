The '''Battle of Xorrn''' was a battle on the [[planet]] [[Xorrn]] and in orbit during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between an allied group that was composed of [[Old Mandalorians]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], and the [[Clan Assembly (Xorrn)|Clan Assembly]] against a [[Ralchio Nervi's task force|naval task force]] and a [[Erle Hovlinn's invasion force|ground invasion force]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Friends" />
