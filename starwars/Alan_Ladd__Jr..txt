'''Alan Ladd, Jr.''' (died on [[March 2]], [[2022]])<ref name="SW.com" /> was a film industry executive and producer. He was famous for giving [[George Lucas]] the go-ahead to make ''[[Star Wars]]'' while working for [[20th Century Studios|20th Century Fox]].
