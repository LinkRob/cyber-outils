'''Crocodiles''' were a scaly [[species]] that shared physical similarities with the sentient [[Saurin]]s.<ref name="Join the Resistance 1">''[[Join the Resistance]]''</ref> The criminal and [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Rekias Nodo]] was described by [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] as having crocodilian features.<ref name="Choose Your Destiny">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Han & Chewie Adventure]]''</ref> [[Happabore]]s resembled a cross between a crocodile and a [[pig]].<ref name="AmazingBook">''[[The Amazing Book of Star Wars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Crocodiles were first mentioned in the [[2016]] young reader's reference book ''[[The Amazing Book of Star Wars]]'', written by [[Elizabeth Dowsett]].<ref name="AmazingBook" /> In real-life, [[Wikipedia:Crocodile|crocodiles]] are a type of large [[Wikipedia:Reptile|reptile]].<ref name="Animal Facts">{{WebCite|url=https://a-z-animals.com/animals/crocodile|text=Crocodile|date=2023-01-12|work=[https://a-z-animals.com/ a-z-animals.com]|archivedate=20230120002104}}</ref>
