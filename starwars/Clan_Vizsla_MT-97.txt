The '''Clan Vizsla MT-97''' was a pair of [[MT-97]] [[blaster pistol]]s owned by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal MT-97|standard blaster pistols]]. Though part of [[Clan Tal]] himself, who had a complicated history with [[Clan Vizsla]], Aran brandished these [[weapon]]s, bearing the rival [[Mandalorian clan|clan]]'s traditional colors and displaying their branch sigil on the grips of each [[pistol]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Clan Vizsla MT-97 was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Aran Tal's Clan Tal MT-97, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 1.0.0 alongside Season 1 and the game's global launch on [[June 4]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Clan Vizsla Warrior]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing the Vizsla Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
