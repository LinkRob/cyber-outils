The '''Akar's Gift''' was an area of near [[Mount Selasa]] on [[Akiva]], a [[jungle]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Galactic Empire]] had established [[Research Station 57]] and [[Imperial Outpost 79]] within the area.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Akar's Gift first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It is only identified in the game by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] communications about the player's last seen location while they have a wanted level.<ref name="Outlaws" />
