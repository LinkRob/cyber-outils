The '''"My First Blaster" Safety Kit''' was a model of insulated firing mechanism produced by the [[BlasTech Industries]] manufacturing company. It was utilized at some point around the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The "My First Blaster" Safety Kit was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />
