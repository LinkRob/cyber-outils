'''Alderaanian wine''' was a luxury [[alcohol]]ic beverage from the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. Various types of Alderaanian [[wine]] existed, including the [[Toniray]]<ref name="BL">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> and the [[Alderaanian white]].<ref name="The Rescue">''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Rescue]]''</ref> There also existed at least one red Alderaanian wine.<ref name="Storms of Crait">[[The Last Jedi – The Storms of Crait 1|''The Last Jedi – The Storms of Crait'' 1]]</ref>

In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> by which point Alderaanian wine was hard to get one's hands on, [[Count]] [[Dooku]] drank a glass of floral-scented Alderaanian wine on [[Raxus Secundus]], during a party [[Raxian Humanitarian Award|honoring]] him.<ref name="DD" />

After Alderaan was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], Alderaanian wine became even more rare and expensive. By the time of the [[First Order-Resistance War]], [[Derla Pidys]] had sold over one hundred bottles of what she claimed to be the "last bottle" of wine from [[Alderaan]] to various individuals, even those who knew this was an obvious fib and only really cared about the story.<ref name="The Wine in Dreams">{{StoryCite|story=The Wine in Dreams|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>
