'''Aviay''' was a [[Jedi Knight]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the [[Guardian Protocols]] were implemented<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> in [[229 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> he partnered with the [[Cathar]] Jedi Knight [[Far Linghe]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> Aviay and Linghe were part of the Jedi team under [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] who took part in an [[Assault on Naboo|assault]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]] which was under [[Occupation of Naboo|occupation]] by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s in their [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]] that was behind the [[Stormwall]] barrier which prevented [[hyperspace]] travel.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

During the assault, Aviay [[pilot]]ed a [[Jedi Vector]] but was forced to [[Ejector seat|eject]] from his [[Starfighter|Vector]] after his [[Starship|ship]] was shot down. After landing safely in the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Theed]], his partner went to find him. The two later again took part in the aerial battle. After Naboo was freed from Nihil rule and the majority of the Jedi and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] forces left the planet, Aviay and Linghe remained on Naboo, believing the world needed a few Jedi to stay and that there was plenty for them to do within the Occlusion Zone.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
