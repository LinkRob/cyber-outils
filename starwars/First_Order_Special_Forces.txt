The '''First Order Special Forces'''<ref name="Encyclopedia of Starfighter">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> were an elite unit of the [[First Order]] [[First Order military|military]]. They used [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]]s<ref name="DB" /> and reported directly to the upper ranks of First Order military hierarchy.<ref name="Foirst Order TIE pilot DB">{{Databank|first-order-tie-fighter-pilots|First Order TIE Fighter Pilots}}</ref>

Visual difference to normal TIE fighter pilots was that the Special Forces fighter pilots wore [[Color|red]] flashes on their [[Flight helmet|helmets]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> like the [[Baron of the Empire|flight barons]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Old Empire]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> Some members of this group were composed of [[elite stormtrooper]]s, who piloted [[elite speeder bike]]s. They were equipped with [[Color|black]] [[First Order stormtrooper armor|stormtrooper armor]], featuring two red stripes on the left side of the chest-plate, that matched up with the color scheme of their speeder bikes.<ref name="Elite Bike" />

A notable member of this force was [[Major]] [[Elrik Vonreg]], who differed from other [[First Order Special Forces TIE fighter pilot|Special Forces pilots]], as he wore all red armor, and flew a [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor]] instead of a TIE/sf space superiority fighter.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" />
