'''Elise''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Batuuan]] from the village of [[Skirl]]. She fell in love with and married [[Menir]], a [[farmer]] who lived on the outskirts of town. Over time, Menir became harsh and unwilling to allow any change, which meant that when Elise invited the traveler [[Iren]] into their home, Menir became furious and attacked him. Iren left a bag containing three seeds behind, which Menir mocked and threw outside. Before long, the seeds had grown into monsters called [[Drengir]], which destroyed Menir's house, killing Elise.<ref name="ABH" />
