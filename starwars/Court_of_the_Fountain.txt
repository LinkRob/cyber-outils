'''Court of the Fountain''' was a [[Hutt]]-owned exotic [[restaurant]] in [[Mos Eisley]]. It was situated near [[Outer Curved Street]].<ref name="Complete Locations" />

The restaurant was in a stone and stucco palace and featured fountains and exotic [[plant]]s. [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] personal [[chef]], [[Porcellus]], worked as head chef there on the rare occasions that Jabba didn't need him.<ref name="Complete Locations" />
