'''Ai Fairouz''', ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: ファイルーズあい, ''Fairūzu Ai''; born [[July 6]], [[1993]], in [[Wikipedia:Tokyo|Tokyo]], [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]]) also known as '''Fairouz Ai''', is a [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]] affiliated with [[Wikipedia:Mausu Promotion|Mausu Promotion]]. She is known for voicing [[Wikipedia:Jolyne Cujoh|Jolyne Cujoh]] in the [[2021]]&ndash;[[2022]] anime [[Wikipedia:JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Stone Ocean|''JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Stone Ocean'']] and [[Wikipedia:Power (Chainsaw Man)|Power]] in the 2022 [[Wikipedia:Chainsaw Man (TV series)|anime adaptation]] of [[Wikipedia:Chainsaw Man|''Chainsaw Man'']]. Fairouz voiced the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Aniseya family|twins]] [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha]] and [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae Aniseya]] in the Japanese [[Wikipedia:Dubbing|dub]] of the [[2024]] live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''. She also voiced the [[bounty hunter]] [[Sevn]] in the original Japanese version of "[[The Bounty Hunters]]," a [[2025]] short film that is part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series.

On [[June 4]], 2024, the day before the Japanese release of the first two episodes of ''The Acolyte'', Fairouz attended the premiere event of ''The Acolyte'' in Tokyo.<ref name="Acolyte event tweet">{{Twitter|username=starwarsjapan|url=status/1797982188379718120|name=スター・ウォーズ公式|date=2024-06-04|quote=『#スターウォーズ：#アコライト』 最速上映会 開催 諏訪部順一さん（ソル） ファイルーズあいさん（メイ） 武内駿輔さん（ヨード） 吹替声優が登壇 {{C|translation: ''Star Wars: The Acolyte'' early screening event with Junichi Suwabe (Sol), Ai Fairouz (Mae), and Shunsuke Takeuchi (Yord). The voice actors attended.}}|archivedate=20240604132213}}</ref> During the event, she said that her work on ''The Acolyte'' introduced her to the ''Star Wars'' franchise. She also said that voicing in ''The Acolyte'' was twice as exhausting as usual, suggesting that she voiced not only Mae but also Osha.<ref name="Acolyte event">{{YouTube|video=Vci50avHaZw|text=【トークノーカット】諏訪部順一、ファイルーズあい、武内駿輔が登壇「スター・ウォーズ：アコライト」日本最速特別上映会イベント|channel=cinematoday|name=シネマトゥデイ|date=2024-06-04}}</ref> Fairouz voiced the Aniseya siblings in the first episode, "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="LF" /> and the second episode, "[[Revenge / Justice]],"<ref name="RJ">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> which both aired on [[June]] 4, 2024.<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|229|Star Wars: The Acolyte Companion|''Star Wars: The Acolyte'' Companion}}</ref> She also voiced Mae and Osha in the fourth episode "[[Day (episode)|Day]],"<ref name="Day">{{Acolyte|Day (episode)}}</ref> the fifth episode "[[Night]],"<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref> and the sixth episode "[[Teach / Corrupt]],"<ref name="TC">{{Acolyte|Teach / Corrupt}}</ref> which premiered on [[June 18]], [[June 25]], and [[July 2]] of 2024, respectively.<ref name="Insider" /> Finally, she voiced the Aniseya siblings in the eighth episode, "[[The Acolyte (episode)|The Acolyte]],"<ref name="TA">{{Acolyte|The Acolyte (episode)}}</ref> which aired on [[July 16]] of that year.<ref name="Insider" /> In addition to her main role as Stenberg's character in these six episodes, Fairouz also provided additional voices for the third and seventh episodes, "[[Destiny (The Acolyte)|Destiny]]"<ref name="Destiny" /> and "[[Choice]],"<ref name="Choice">{{Acolyte|Choice}}</ref> which aired on [[June 11]] and [[July 9]], 2024, respectively.<ref name="Insider" />

[[File:Sevn-TheBountyHunters-episodeguide.png|thumb|right|175px|alt=A female bounty hunter, Sevn, with red and black hair tied back in a ponytail, one blue eye and one brown eye, and light skin|Ai Fairouz voiced the bounty hunter Sevn in the original Japanese version of "The Bounty Hunters."]]
Fairouz also voiced the [[bounty hunter]] [[Sevn]] in the original Japanese version of "[[The Bounty Hunters]]," a short film that is part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series.<ref name="The Bounty Hunters">{{Visions|The Bounty Hunters}}</ref> Her involvement in the short film was first announced on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 26]], [[2025]],<ref name="Vol3 cast" /> and on the same day, the official Japanese [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]] website revealed that she voiced Sevn.<ref name="Sevn cast">{{Disney|subdomain=starwars|domain=co.jp|url=news/20250926_01|text=日本語オリジナル版＜本予告＞が全世界解禁！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3}}</ref> On [[October 28]], 2025, Fairouz appeared at the premiere event of the third volume of ''Star Wars: Visions'' in Japan. At the event, she said that she auditioned for "The Bounty Hunters" immediately after finishing the recording of the Japanese dub of ''The Acolyte''. Fairouz described Sevn as an independent character with a rough tone but a strong sense of justice, and she stated that Sevn's act of standing up for oppressed children reminded her of the poverty she witnessed in [[Wikipedia:Egypt|Egypt]] during her childhood. She said that Sevn is the character who gives her the courage to help someone without hesitation when she finds herself in a position to do so.<ref name="Vol3 event">{{YouTube|video=FUYb52fpIFg|text=【トークノーカット】浪川大輔、ファイルーズあい、黒沢ともよ、監督9名が登壇！『スター・ウォーズ：ビジョンズ』Volume3 配信直前イベント|channel=@cinematoday|name=シネマトゥデイ|date=2025-10-28}}</ref> The entire volume was released on Disney+ on [[October 29]], 2025.<ref name="Vol3 date">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref> In an interview article for "The Bounty Hunters," [[Wikipedia:Animation director|director]] [[Junichi Yamamoto]] stated that Fairouz accurately portrayed Sevn's immature and underdeveloped aspects.<ref name="TBH interview">{{WebCite|url=https://eiga.com/news/20251031/26|text=【インタビュー】「スター・ウォーズ　ビジョンズ」は、視聴者の“ビジョン”で完成する　山元隼一監督が語る|date=2025-10-31|work=[https://eiga.com 映画.com]|language=[[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]|quote=ファイルーズさんは、セブンの未熟で成長しきれていない部分を的確に表現してくださいましたし&hellip; {{C|translation: Fairouz perfectly voiced the immature and underdeveloped aspects of Sevn, and&hellip;}}|archivedate=20251101094235}}</ref>
