"'''Breathe to the Light Side'''" is the second episode of the [[Canon|non-canon]] animated series ''[[Find your Force with Star Wars]]''. It was released by [[Wikipedia:Headspace (company)|Headspace]] on [[August 15]], [[2022]].<ref name="Breathe" />

==Official description==
''No one wants to see an angry Wookiee™. ''

''In Chapter II of Find your Force, a new family and kids mindfulness series from Headspace and ''Star Wars''™, Chewbacca™ and BB-8™ are off&hellip; very slowly&hellip; to Tatooine™ in search of a part that'll fix the ''Falcon''™ hyperdrive. As they play a game of Dejarik™ to pass the time, Headspace expert Samantha Snowden helps Chewbacca™ practice being a good sport, and learn that having fun with our friends is the best prize of all.''<ref name="Breathe" />
