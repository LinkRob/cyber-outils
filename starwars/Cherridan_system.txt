The '''Cherridan system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Belnar sector]] of the [[Colonies]]. It was located in grid square M-9 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Cherridan]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Cherridan system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
