'''ES-04''', also known as '''Firebrand''', was the designation of a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[Elite Squad Trooper]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] during the early [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Imperial Era]]. She was recruited through [[Project War-Mantle]] along with her [[squad]]mates, and was described by [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] as one of the best [[soldier]]s in [[the galaxy]]. As a member of [[Clone Commander]] [[Crosshair|CT-9904]]'s [[Elite Squad]], ES-04 participated in the [[Mission to Onderon (Elite Squad)|execution of civilians]] after [[ES-01]] was [[Death|killed]] for refusing to comply with the [[commander]]'s orders.

==Biography==
===Recruit of the Empire===
{{Quote|May I present our first Elite Squad. Top soldiers from across the galaxy.|Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart introduces ES-04 and her squad to Governor Tarkin|<ref name="Replacements" />}}
[[File:EliteSquadNoHelmets-Replacements.png|thumb|left|300px|ES-04 was one of four recruits led by the clone commando Crosshair.]]
After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|dissolution]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] and the formation of a new [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Replacements" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the burgeoning [[Imperial Military]] sought to reorder its ranks. Under an initiative known as [[Project War-Mantle]], [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]] began enlisting conscripted [[human]] recruits to potentially replace [[clone trooper]]s, the standard infantry of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. The individual who would take on the designation ES-04,<ref name="Replacements" /> also known by the nickname Firebrand,<ref name="AMG Elite" /> was one of these recruits, becoming one of the first [[Elite Squad Trooper]]s of the Empire. She was regarded as one of the top [[soldier]]s in [[the galaxy]] by the Vice Admiral, who took great pride in his troops.<ref name="Replacements" />

Along with fellow recruits [[ES-01]], [[ES-02]], and [[ES-03]], ES-04 was brought by Rampart to the [[Tipoca City Military Complex]] on [[Kamino]], where the [[Elite Squad|squad]] was assigned to the [[clone commando]] [[Crosshair|CT-9904]] "Crosshair," a modified [[Cloning|clone]] regarded for his skills as a [[Sniper|sharpshooter]]. After undergoing a medical examination by [[Imperial nanny droid|Kaminoan droids]], the group was tasked by [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to [[Mission to Onderon (Elite Squad)|eliminate]] [[Saw Gerrera]], an insurgent located on the [[planet]] [[Onderon]]. Hoping to test the squad's effectiveness, Tarkin ordered the unit to destroy [[Saw Gerrera's camp|Gerrera's camp]] and wipe out any resistance.<ref name="Replacements" />

===A tangible test===
{{Quote|Good soldiers follow orders. Finish the mission.|Crosshair orders ES-04 to execute civilians|<ref name="Replacements" />}}
[[File:EliteSquadConfrontRefugees-Replacements.png|thumb|right|300px|ES-04 aims her flamethrower at the refugees.]]
Arriving at Onderon aboard a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]], ES-04 and her squadmates entered the planets [[jungle]]s and located the camp of fighters, though Gerrera himself was absent. As Crosshair led the main unit in a frontal assault on the insurgents, ES-04 flanked the enemy with a [[BT X-42 heavy flame projector]] and took out several fighters as they retreated towards an [[LAAT/le patrol gunship]]. Her next target, the occupants of the [[gunship]]'s cabin, were spared from her stream of [[fire]] when the [[Unidentified male Onderonian fighter|ship's pilot]] sealed the [[Starship|vessel's]] hatch doors. A well placed shot from an Elite, however, took the [[pilot]] out and left the ship defenseless.<ref name="Replacements" />

With only [[refugee]]s left aboard, ES-01 encouraged his team to take them as prisoners, but Crosshair disagreed, shooting the [[trooper]] for refusing to wipe out all members of the camp. Under orders from their [[Clone Commander|clone commander]], ES-04 and the rest of her squad turned their weapons on the huddled refugees, killing them all. Upon returning to Kamino, Tarkin and Rampart commended ES-04 and the rest of the squad for their diligence, anticipating the victories they would accomplish in the name of the Empire. The squad then took their leave to their [[Bad Batch barracks|barracks]], where Crosshair's old team, [[Clone Force 99]], had previously resided.<ref name="Replacements" />

===Searching for Clone Force 99===
After being notified by scavengers on [[Bracca]] that [[Clone Force 99]] had been spotted on the planet, they were taken [[Echo|to]] search for them along with the rest of their squad and three Empire ships worth of [[stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="Replacements" />

===Death===
ES-04 and her squad were tasked with capturing Clone Force 99 on Kamino. Crosshair argued with [[Hunter]] before attempting a truce with them by telling the Elite Squad to stand down. When they refused and turned on Crosshair, he fired a shot that ricocheted around the room, killing them all.<ref name="Return to Kamino" />

==Personality and traits==
ES-04 was a human female with [[Color|dark]] skin, brown [[eye]]s, and auburn [[hair]]. She lamented the fact that the end of the [[Clone Wars]] signified that there were fewer enemies in the [[galaxy]] to fight, and so willingly enlisted in the Imperial Military. She displayed levels of loyalty to the Empire that Vice Admiral Rampart deeply appreciated, and she was willing to follow her orders despite any moral underpinnings to the contrary.<ref name="Replacements" />

==Skills and abilities==
Vice Admiral Rampart considered ES-04 and her squad to be among the greatest soldiers in the galaxy and spoke highly of their skills to others within the Empire. Although the [[Kaminoan]] [[Prime Minister]] [[Lama Su]], who oversaw the creation of clone troopers for the Republic, believed that conscripted soldiers could never reach the same levels of proficiency as fighters [[Reproduction|bred]] for war, Rampart saw otherwise; he was of the opinion that troops like ES-04 would learn any necessary skills over [[time]].<ref name="Replacements" />

==Equipment==
[[File:ES04FlamethrowerOnderon-Replacements.png|thumb|right|300px|ES-04 using her flamethrower on Onderon.]]
Like the other Elites, ES-04 wore modified [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], specially fitted to match her individual body type. The black [[armor]] sported a [[helmet]] with green lenses.<ref name="Replacements Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=replacements-trivia-gallery|text=Tech's Notes: "Replacements" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> ES-04 carried a BT X-42 heavy flame projector into combat, a unique weapon among the others found in her Elite Squad.<ref name="Replacements" />

==Behind the scenes==
ES-04 made her first appearance in "[[Replacements]]," the third episode of the [[The Bad Batch Season 1|first season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', released on [[May 14]], [[2021]]. Her designation is given in the episode's credits, which also point out that the character is voiced by actress [[Daheli Hall|Dahéli Hall]].<ref name="Replacements" /> The descriptive audio for the episode available on [[Disney+]] incorrectly identifies the trooper wielding a flamethrower on Onderon as Crosshair, when it is in fact ES-04.<ref name="Replacements Audio Description">Per the Audio Description for "[[Replacements]]" available on [[Disney+]].</ref>
