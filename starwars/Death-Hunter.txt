"'''Death-Hunter'''" is an adventure for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' which appeared in [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 9|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 9]]. It was written by [[Tony Russo]] and illustrated by [[Matt Busch|Doug Shuler]].

==Plot summary==
[[General/Legends|General]] [[Crix Madine/Legends|Crix Madine]] receives a troubling report from Rebel operative "[[Tigress]]" about an Imperial project known as [[Operation Death-Hunter|Death-Hunter]]. Despite concerns that Tigress may have been compromised, a team of Rebel agents is dispatched to investigate. Their first lead takes them to the Imperial shipyards on [[Jaemus/Legends|Jaemus]] where [[Courier droid/Legends|courier droid]] are stored, before they investigate a series of kidnappings on the planet [[Bescane/Legends|Bescane]].

They soon discover that Death-Hunter is a secret project developed by [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] [[Jesco Comark]] to forcefully modify individuals with elaborate [[Cybernetics/Legends|cybernetics]], transforming them into [[Cyborg/Legends|cyborgs]] with completely loyalty to the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. Comark's ultimate goal is to replace the Empire's reliance on bounty hunters with [[Death-Hunter Cyborg|these enhanced soldiers]].

The Rebel operatives, joined by [[Amber Comark]]—Moff Comark's daughter who assumed the identity of Tigress after the original spy was caught—embark on a mission to [[Zeta Zero Nine]], the planet where the Death-Hunter project is headquartered. They manage to successfully free the imprisoned subjects and sabotage the project before retreating to their Rebel base.
