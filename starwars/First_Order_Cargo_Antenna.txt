The '''First Order Cargo Antenna''' was a communications antenna on a tower connected to [[First Order Cargo]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]]'s [[Docking Bay 9]]. Some time after the [[Battle of Crait]], [[Hondo Ohnaka]] paid a visitor to the outpost to access encrypted transmissions from the antenna and retrieve [[First Order]] access codes.<ref name="Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]]</ref>
