'''Chewbacca Returns''' is a featurette included in the Blu-ray and DVD release of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars The Complete Season Three|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' The Complete Season Three]]. It covers the development of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episodes "[[Padawan Lost]]" and "[[Wookiee Hunt]]."

The featurette explores the introduction of [[Chewbacca]] in the series, the involvement of [[Peter Mayhew]], the development of the jungle and environment of [[Wasskah]], the [[Garnac's hunting guild/Legends|Trandoshans]], and the [[Battle on Wasskah]].
