'''Amanaman''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Amani]] headhunter from the [[planet]] [[Maridun]] who worked as a [[bounty hunter]] for the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. After the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] was frozen in [[carbonite]] in [[3 ABY]], Solo's friend [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] met with Amanaman in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[desert]] world [[Tatooine]] and asked that the Amani alert him if the bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]] arrived at the palace, as Fett was tasked with transporting Solo to Jabba. After Solo was delivered and displayed on Jabba's wall, Amanaman stood near the smuggler in [[4 ABY]] and laughed when Jabba declared he did not want to lose his favorite decoration after the Hutt listened to a message from the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] asking to bargain for Solo's freedom.

The headhunter then sat in one of the palace's booths during a performance by the [[Max Rebo Band]] which ended in the [[Slavery|enslaved]] dancer [[Oola]] being dropped into Jabba's [[rancor]] pit and [[Death|eaten]]. [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], another of Solo's allies, then arrived in the [[throne]] room disguised as the [[bounty hunter]] [[Boushh]] and negotiated with Jabba over the [[bounty]] on the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]]. Later that [[Standard day|night]], Amanaman was part of a trap set for Organa, and hid behind a curtain while she freed Solo from carbonite. Jabba then revealed the trap and Organa and Solo were captured as Amanaman and others blocked their escape route.

==Biography==
===Staying flat at the palace===
{{Quote|And like I said, you hear anything about what we talked about, you be in touch. I'll make it worth your while''"<br />"''Scree!|Lando Calrissian and Amanaman agrees on an information exchange regarding Boba Fett|<ref name="SW20202" />}}
[[File:AmanamanLando-2020StarWars2.png|thumb|left|300px|Amanaman agreed to let Lando Calrissian know when Boba Fett arrived at Jabba's Palace.]]
Amanaman was a mysterious [[Amani]]<ref name="AEYNTK" /> [[Gender|male]] headhunter<ref name="Geektionary" /> from the [[planet]] [[Maridun]].<ref name="Geektionary French" /> Eventually, Amanaman gave up headhunting.<ref name="BountyStickers">''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters Ultimate Sticker Collection]]''</ref> Instead, he would go onto [[Jabba's criminal empire|work]] for the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] and lived in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="HC2" /> There, he would wait for Jabba to approach him with odd jobs.<ref name="BountyStickers" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="ESBDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] was frozen in [[carbonite]] and given to the bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]] to bring to Jabba. Solo's friend [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] approached Amanaman at the palace and sat with him at a table while asking if the Amani had seen Fett arrive. When Amanaman responded in the negative, Calrissian asked that the headhunter contact him when Fett did arrived before being interrupted by Jabba's [[majordomo]], [[Bib Fortuna]]. Before Calrissian left with Fortuna, Amanaman fist bumped him and agreed to contact Calrissian if Fett was spotted.<ref name="SW20202" />

[[File:LaughingHermi-ROTJ.png|thumb|right|300px|Amanaman laughed when Jabba declined the offer of the droids.]]
Fett eventually did successfully deliver Solo to Jabba, who displayed the smuggler on the wall of his [[throne]] room frozen in [[carbonite]].<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="ROTJDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|VI}} to [[4 ABY]].</ref> Amanaman was stood near to the frozen Solo when Jabba declared that he would not be bargaining for the smuggler's freedom after watching a [[Hologram|holographic]] message from the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]], a friend of Solo's who wished to negotiate his release. Amanaman and others laughed when Jabba referred to the smuggler as "his favorite decoration." The Amani later sat beside the [[Quarren]] [[Tessek]] in one of the throne room's booths while the [[Max Rebo Band]] performed for the court. After they finished a song,<ref name="ROTJ" /> Jabba demanded the [[Slavery|enslaved]] dancer [[Oola]] repeat her performance, but thinking that the Hutt was speaking to them, the band began playing the [[Music|song]]<ref name="Darkside">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> "[[Jedi Rocks]]."<ref name="AEYNTK" />

===Trapping Organa===
[[File:Gargan-Laughing-ROTJ.png|thumb|left|300px|Amanaman sat in a booth while Oola was eaten.]]
The Hutt then interrupted the second performance when he dropped Oola into a pit beneath the throne room. Amanaman remained seated while much of the court rushed forward to watch the dancer be devoured by Jabba's pet [[rancor]],<ref name="ROTJ" /> [[Pateesa]].<ref name="LD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> Once the ordeal was over, the court's attention was by drawn to the throne room's entry way by the sound of [[blaster]] fire, which marked the arrival of [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] with the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]]. Organa, who was disguised as the bounty hunter [[Boushh]], used a [[thermal detonator]] to negotiate the price of the [[bounty]] on Chewbacca with Jabba, scaring much of the court, but causing the Hutt to laugh and offer her a increased price.<ref name="ROTJ" />

Jabba, who had been warned by Fett about Organa's disguise,<ref name="Darkside" /> then set up a trap for her, and had Amanaman and many other court members hide behind a curtain on the stairs<ref name="ROTJ" /> leading to the throne room's exit.<ref name="Darkside" /> Later that night, Organa snuck back into the throne room and [[Rescue of Han Solo|freed]] Solo from the carbonite, after which Jabba pulled back the curtains and revealed his trap, with Amanaman, who was stood behind a [[Gamorrean]] guard and next to the<ref name="ROTJ" /> [[arms dealer]] [[Ephant Mon]],<ref name="Geektionary" /> and the others blocking Organa and Solo's escape. The pair were captured, but Skywalker arrived the next day to rescue them.<ref name="Darkside" />

[[File:Amanaman-Prisoners-ROTJ.png|thumb|right|300px|Amanaman was present at Jabba's Palace during the rescue of Han Solo.]]
After the Jedi was dropped into the rancor pit and killed the rancor, he was dragged back up to the throne room, where Amanaman was stood at the top of the stairs with Mon. Jabba then sentenced Skywalker, Solo, and Chewbacca to death and left with them aboard his [[sail barge]], the ''[[Khetanna]]'', in order to execute them at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. However, the prisoners managed to turn the tables at the pit, killing Jabba and destroying his barge before escaping.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Stay flat, pal.|Lando Calrissian, to Amanaman|<ref name="SW20202" />}}
Amanaman was a gangly Amani<ref name="Geektionary" />&mdash;which gave him a flat-appearing body<ref name="SW20202" />&mdash;with cruel [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s and enormous [[Limb|hands]].<ref name="Geektionary" /> The front of his body was yellow in color with some green streaks, while the rear was entirely green.<ref name="ROTJ" /> As a headhunter, Amanaman never showed mercy to his victims.<ref name="Geektionary" /> He did not communicate in [[Galactic Basic Standard]] but could understand it.<ref name="SW20202" />

==Skills and abilities==
Amanaman was skilled with a variety of traditional weapons<ref name="Geektionary" /> and preferred traditional hunting weapons over [[blaster]]s.<ref name="AEYNTK" />

==Equipment==
Amanaman carried a [[staff]] with three [[humanoid]] skulls attached to the top.<ref name="ROTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Amanaman-Maquette-Chronicles.png|thumb|right|300px|The Amanaman maquette]]
Amanaman first appeared in the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].<ref name="ROTJ" /> The character was portrayed by puppeteer [[Ailsa Berk]],<ref name="Berk">{{OfficialSite|url=http://ailsaberk.co.uk/website/GALLERY.html|text=Ailsa Berk's official website|author=[[Ailsa Berk|Berk, Ailsa]]|archivedate=20190906022637}}</ref> who was credited as a mime artist.<ref name="ROTJ" /> A prototype model of the character was made for the film and measured 43 centimeters by 20 centimeters by 64 centimeters.<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles]]''</ref> The production crew used the name Amanaman for the character<ref name="BTM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> but also nicknamed him "Banana Slug." The puppet included two circular flaps in the neck that could be opened to ventilate the costume.<ref name="Archeology">{{YouTube|video=RWw8-s_a0vM|text=Star Wars: Return of the Jedi - Cantina Archeology Part 3: Behind The Scenes of Jabba's Palace|channel=@CredibleNerds|name=The Credible Nerds}}</ref>

The palace throne room scenes were filmed between [[January 25]] and [[February 8]], [[1982]] on Stage 8 at [[Elstree Studios]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref> The character was first identified as Amanaman [[in-universe]] in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="AEYNTK" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the first in-universe use was on an action figure produced in [[1985]] by [[Kenner Products]] as part of the [[Star Wars: The Power of the Force (1985 toy line)|''Star Wars: The Power of the Force'']] toy line.<ref name="Kenner">{{KennerCite|set=Star Wars: The Power of the Force (1985 toy line)|pack=Amanaman|link=VINtAmanaman.asp}}</ref>
