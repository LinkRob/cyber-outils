The '''Ahch-To system''' was a [[Binary star system|binary]] [[star system]] located in an uncharted sector of the [[Unknown Regions]], containing twin [[Star|suns]], around which at great enough distance<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[orbit]]ed the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Ahch-To]], home of the [[First Jedi Temple|first]] [[Jedi]] [[Jedi temple|temple]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that the Ahch-To system is a [[Binary star system|binary]] [[star system]],<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> as [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] features the planet with twin [[Star|suns]].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> Despite this, "[[Forces of Nature: The Wild Spaces and Wilder Creatures of the Star Wars Galaxy|Forces of Nature: The Wild Spaces and Wilder Creatures of the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy]]" in [[Star Wars Insider 216|''Star Wars Insider'' 216]] states that the appearance of twin suns over Ahch-To was a vision of the [[Tatoo system]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|216|Forces of Nature: The Wild Spaces and Wilder Creatures of the Star Wars Galaxy|Forces of Nature: The Wild Spaces and Wilder Creatures of the ''Star Wars'' Galaxy}}</ref>
