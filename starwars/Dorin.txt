'''Dorin''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> located in the [[Dorin system]] in the [[Deadalis sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Kel Dor]] [[species]]. Its [[Dorin gas]] and [[helium]]-rich environment meant that they were forced to rely on [[rebreather]]s,<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> such as [[antiox breath mask]]s,<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> to survive off-world, as [[oxygen]] was deadly to them.<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi]] [[Jedi General|General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] [[Engagement near Dorin|routed]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] forces near Dorin.<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" />

While the atmosphere was not breathable for most off-world species, the native plants were edible. [[Zand nut]]s and the fruit of [[kand tree]]s were two such foods.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook" />
