The '''biffleflies''' were native flying [[insect]]s found on the [[forest]] [[planet]]s [[Tenoo]] and [[Yamradi]].<ref name="Get Well Nubs" /> They were similar to [[Butterfly|butterflies]].<ref name="Galactic Training">{{DisneyNOW|url=games/star-wars-young-jedi-adventures-galactic-training/GM5533269029|text=Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures: Galactic Training|int=Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures: Galactic Training}}</ref>

== Biology ==
Biffleflies has these markings that looks like a wave with purple-colored raindrops, while the main body is a greenish red with light blue antennae.{{Creature-stub}}
