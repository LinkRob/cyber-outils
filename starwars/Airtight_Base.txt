'''Airtight Base''' was one of the notable bodies within the [[Hoth asteroid field|Hoth asteroid belt]], which overlapped the boundries of the [[Hoth system]] and [[Anoat system]] in the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Falcon" />

==Behind the scenes==
Airtight Base was mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 39|issue 39]] of [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon|''Star Wars: Build the Millennium'']],<ref name="Falcon" /> which was released around [[September 30]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|39}}</ref> The [[Airtight Base/Legends|base]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it appeared in "[[Rough and Tundra]]," a [[2004]] adventure for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]]'' released by [[Wizards of the Coast]].<ref name="R&T">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20040325adv|text=Rough and Tundra|int=Rough and Tundra}}</ref>
