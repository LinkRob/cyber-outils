'''09''' was a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[BX-series droid commando]] that participated in the [[defense of Rishi Station]], where they were subsequently destroyed along with [[07]] and [[08]].

==Biography==
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> 09<ref name="Old EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode005.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rookies}}</ref> and other [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[BX-series droid commando]]s were tasked with [[Defense of Rishi Station|infiltrating]] the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Rishi Station]] on the [[Rishi Moon]] and activate the "all-clear" signal, allowing for a surprise attack on the key Republic [[Planet|world]] of [[Kamino]]. 09 and the other [[droid]]s used [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ships]], camouflaged by a [[meteor shower]], to land near the base. After sneaking in and killing several [[clone trooper]]s, the droids hard-wired the "all-clear" signal. The Republic survivors of the attack, [[Hevy]], [[Echo]], and [[Fives]] managed to escape to the outside of the base.<ref name="Rookies" />

[[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]] "Rex" and [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody|CC-2224]] "Cody" arrived for a standard inspection, a droid disguised as a clone approached them in an effort to cancel the inspection. When Hevy alerted the clone officers with a [[droid attack flare]], Rex shot and destroyed the disguised commando droid. They were subsequently ambushed and driven off the platform, with the Separatist droids believing that they were pulverized. Afterwards, 09, [[07|Unit 07]], and [[08]], guarded the entrance to the base when Rex tricked them into opening the doors for [[Unit 26]], a droid that was previously destroyed. When the doors opened, 09 and the other droids were destroyed, and the clones recaptured the base. A [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|fleet]] of Separatist [[warship]]s arrived overhead and deployed reinforcements, however.<ref name="Rookies" />

==Behind the scenes==
09 first appeared in "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]," a [[2008]] episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the TV-series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Rookies" /> 09 was identified in the episode guide released at that time.<ref name="Old EG" />
