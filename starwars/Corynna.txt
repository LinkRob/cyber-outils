'''Corynna''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who served as concierge in [[Crimson Dawn]] to [[Dryden Vos]]. She was [[Death penalty|executed]] by Vos after she tried to poison guards meals who worked for Vos.<ref name="CC" />

==Biography==
Corynna met with [[Sarkin Enneb]] to collect a debt that was owed to Crimson Dawn. She took interest in [[Qi'ra]] who delivered the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and decided to purchase her from Enneb. They boarded a [[shuttle]] and Corynna observed Qi'ra throughout the trip. She informed the girl that she had purchased her due the concierge's knowledge of Vos' preferences. When they arrived at the ''[[First Light]]'', she ordered [[Margo]] to get Qi'ra cleaned up while she went to meet Vos.<ref name="CC" />

After several months, Corynna grew to resent Qi'ra and would often punish her for infractions during her training.<ref name="CC" /> In [[12 BBY]],<ref name="CCDate" /> She was called to Vos's office to witness Qi'ra receiving the [[tattoo]] of the Crimson Dawn symbol on her wrist which indicated she was a member of the criminal organization.<ref name="CC" />

She continued to train Qi'ra after her return from Thorum. She attended a meeting on [[Nightsend]] with Vos and other Crimson Dawn members. While there, Corynna arranged future meetings with other members of the [[Five Crime Syndicates]].<ref name="CC" />

During this time, she was plotting to overthrow Vos and seize control on Crimson Dawn. She anonymously gave information to [[Cerveteri Slane]] to use to setup independent operations and undermine Vos's leadership. Qi'ra's investigation caused Corynna to become scared and she attempted to poison Vos's guards. She was caught by [[Aemon Gremm]] and brought before Vos. He used his [[petar]] to slash her throat and she slowly choked to death in agony.<ref name="CC" />
