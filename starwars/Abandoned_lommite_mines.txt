The '''abandoned lommite mines''' were a type of mine that was located on the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]], formerly used to mine<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> the [[ore]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[lommite]].<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">According to ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'', the [[Guardian Protocols]] were implemented in [[229 BBY]]. According to [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|''Shadows of Starlight'' 1]], this happened one week after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places in [[230 BBY]]. The events of ''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]'', including the [[Expansion of the Stormwall]], take place exactly one year after the Fall of [[Starlight Beacon]]. The events of "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Survivors]]" take place one [[Standard month|month]] after the expansion, and "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Family Ties]]" takes place immediately after "Survivors." "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: The Fate of Eriadu]]" takes place after "Family Ties," so its events must take place in [[228 BBY]].</ref> several [[starship]]s of the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] were hidden within the mines, awaiting a possible battle that could break out on Eriadu.<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" />

==Behind the scenes==
The abandoned lommite mines were first mentioned in the short story "[[Tales from the Occlusion Zone: The Fate of Eriadu]]," written by [[Lydia Kang]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Fate of Eriadu" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-star-wars-the-high-republic-panel|text=SWCE 2023: 9 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: The High Republic'' Panel}}</ref> The story was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 229|229th issue]] of the magazine ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' as the sixth installment in the [[The High Republic: Tales from the Occlusion Zone|''Tales from the Occlusion Zone'']] serialized fiction on [[December 10]], [[2024]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/171-star-wars-insider-229|text=Star Wars Insider #229}}</ref>
