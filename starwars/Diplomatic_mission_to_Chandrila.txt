A '''diplomatic mission''' to<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|chandrila|Chandrila}}</ref> [[Chandrila]] was conducted by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Josk Nivar]] and his [[Unidentified Padawan (Josk Nivar)|Sullustan Padawan]]<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> prior to [[22 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> and took place over several [[Standard year|years]].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> like many other members of the [[Jedi Order]], Nivar and his [[Padawan]] were called back to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] by the [[Jedi High Council]] and deployed to the planet [[Geonosis]] as part of a [[Jedi]] [[Jedi assault team|assault team]] tasked with rescuing<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> Jedi Knight<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Padawan [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] from their [[Geonosian]] captors. During the ensuing [[First Battle of Geonosis|battle]], Nivar's Padawan was [[Death|killed]]. A few [[Standard week|weeks]] later, Nivar was assigned to lead a [[Mission to Thule|mission]] to the planet [[Thule]]; one of his subordinates, the [[Knighting Ceremony|newly-Knighted]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]], who had also fought at Geonosis, noted to herself the few details she knew about Nivar.<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

==Behind the Scenes==
The diplomatic mission to Chandrila was mentioned in [[Delilah S. Dawson]]'s [[2023]] novel [[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade|''Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade'']].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />
