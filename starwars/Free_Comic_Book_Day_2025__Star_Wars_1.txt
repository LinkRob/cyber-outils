'''Free Comic Book Day 2025: ''Star Wars'' 1''' is a [[canon]] one-shot [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] featuring stories written by [[Marc Guggenheim]], [[Alex Segura]], and [[Charles Soule]], and illustrated by [[Madibek Musabekov]], [[Phil Noto]], [[Stefano Raffaele]], [[Luis Guerrero]], and [[Nolan Woodard]]. The issue was published as a [[Free Comic Book Day]] title by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[May 3]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''CELEBRATING THE NEW ERA OF ''STAR WARS'' COMICS! LUKE SKYWALKER finds himself in a wretched hive of scum and villainy facing off against pirates! JEDI KNIGHTS QUI-GON JINN & TENSU RUN are on the hunt for the villainous CORLIS RATH! Who is the mysterious VANEE ''[''sic'']'' and what is his connection to DARTH VADER & KYLO REN?''
