The '''''Ark Angel III''''' was a [[starship]] used by the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] in the [[time]] following the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="Aphra 1" /> Aphra utilized the vessel after the destruction of her previous starship, the ''[[Ark Angel II]]''.<ref name="Aphra 2">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 2|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 2]]</ref> The ''Ark Angel III'' was later destroyed by the [[Tagge's fleet|fleet]] of the [[House of Tagge]] scion [[Ronen Tagge]] on the [[planet]] [[Dianth]]. Aphra and her associates subsequently escaped Dianth on a vessel from the [[High Republic Era]]<ref name="Aphra 4">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 4|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 4]]</ref> that she later named ''[[Ark Angel IV]]''.<ref name="Aphra 5">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 5|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 5]]</ref>
