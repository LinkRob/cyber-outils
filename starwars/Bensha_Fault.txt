The '''Bensha Fault''' was a major tectonic feature on the geologically-young and unstable [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Dalna]]. One tip of the fault was located at the [[Maawat Juncture]].<ref name="MTD" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the [[Nihil]] weapons expert [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Zadina Mkampa]] placed powerful explosives within the Bensha Fault in order to trigger a planet-wide catastrophe. Dalna was devastated by [[Volcano|volcanic]] eruptions and seismic activity, but the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Jedi Order]] were able to [[Dalnan evacuation|evacuate]] its population by towing the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] to the [[Dalnan system]].<ref name="MTD" />
