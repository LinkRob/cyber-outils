The '''Camp Forward airfield''' was an [[Airbase|airfield]] that was housed at [[Imperial Forward Field Operations|Camp Forward]], the temporary headquarters for the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[224th Imperial Armored Division|forces]] located deep in the [[disputed wilderness zone]] on the [[swamp]] [[planet]] [[Mimban]], where combat against the [[Mimbanese Liberation Army]] was the strongest during the [[Mimban Campaign]].<ref name="TOG" />
