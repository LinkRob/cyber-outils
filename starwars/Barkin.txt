{{Quote|Barkin was going to keep trying for bail. If he gets it, you don't want to be all the way across Capital City when Mother's ready for you to come get her.|Arihnda Pryce tells her father Talmoor about Barkin|<ref name="Thrawn" />}}
'''Barkin''' was an individual who was associated with the [[Pryce family]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. When [[Elainye Pryce]] was arrested on embezzlement charges, he attempted to have her released on bail while Elainye's daughter [[Arihnda Pryce]] went to meet with [[Senator]] [[Domus Renking]]. Elainye's [[Marriage|husband]] [[Talmoor Pryce]] planned to visit with his daughter, but she convinced him to return home in case Barkin's attempts were successful.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Barkin was first mentioned in the [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], which was written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Thrawn" />
