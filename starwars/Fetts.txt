{{Quote|I want the whole galaxy to know what happens when they mess with Jango Fett. I figured&hellip; it's what you would do.''"<br />"''When they mess with either of us, you mean. The '''Fetts''' were a family consisting of the [[bounty hunter]]s [[Jango Fett]] and [[Boba Fett]]. Jango's [[homeworld]] was the [[Mandalorian]] [[planet]] [[Concord Dawn]]. Raised by a [[Jaste|mentor]] during his [[time]] as a [[foundling]] after losing his parents, Jango fought in the [[Mandalorian Civil Wars]]. Ultimately, however, he pursued the career of a [[bounty]] hunter, utilizing his combat skills to acquire wealth in [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. As a [[mercenary]], Jango wore a [[Mandalorian armor|suit]] of [[Mandalorian armor]] made from [[beskar]].

The [[Sith]] hired Jango to serve as the [[clone template]] for the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], an [[army]] secretly created on [[Kamino]] during the final [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Republic Era]]. As a result, the [[gene]]tically identical [[clone trooper]]s were modeled on Jango's [[DNA]]. In addition, the [[Kaminoan]] [[Cloning|cloners]] created Alpha and [[Omega]], both of whom possessed pure first generation Fett [[blood]]. Jango raised Alpha as his son, giving him the name of "Boba Fett," while the [[Gender|female]] clone known as Omega was raised for a [[time]] by [[Doctor]] [[Nala Se]].

Discovered by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], the Fetts retreated to [[Geonosis]] where Jango was [[Death|killed]] in [[First Battle of Geonosis|battle]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]]. At first, Boba sought to avenge his father by [[assassin]]ating Windu, but later shifted his attention to becoming a bounty hunter. The rest of Jango's clones formed the backbone of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Republic Military|new military]], and the conflict that engulfed [[the galaxy]]&mdash;the [[Clone Wars]]&mdash;was named after them. Although they were loyal to the Republic and the [[Jedi General]]s who served alongside them, the clones were [[Behavioral modification biochip|programmed]] to comply with [[Order 66]], a secret directive to [[Great Jedi Purge|exterminate]] the members of the [[Jedi Order]]. With the fall of the [[Jedi]] and the support of the clone army, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] claimed the title of [[Galactic Emperor]], [[Proclamation of the New Order|reorganizing]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]].

While the clones were [[Project War-Mantle|supplanted]] by [[Reproduction|natural-born]] [[stormtrooper]]s, Boba became a renowned bounty hunter whose clients included the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] and the Sith [[Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] inadvertently caused Boba to fall into the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. Although Boba survived the [[sarlacc]] that devoured him, his [[armor]] fell into the possession of [[Jawa]] [[scavenger]]s who later sold it to [[Cobb Vanth]]. The armor was taken from Vanth by the [[Mandalorian warrior]] [[Din Djarin]], leading to a confrontation on [[Tython]] with Boba who sought to regain his father's armor. Djarin acknowledged Boba's claim on the armor, although [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] denounced Boba as a clone rather than a Mandalorian or a true son of Jango. After aiding Djarin in his efforts to [[Second rescue of Grogu|rescue]] the [[Force-sensitive]] foundling [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], Boba and [[Fennec Shand]] [[Attack on Jabba's Palace (Boba Fett)|seized control]] of [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], killing [[Bib Fortuna]] in the process. Boba then claimed Jabba's [[throne]], with Shand by his side. They went on to form the [[Fett gotra]], a criminal organization centered in the port city of [[Mos Espa]] where they would enter into a conflict with the [[spice]] cartel known as the [[Pyke Syndicate]] over control of the city.

==History==
===Origins===
{{Quote|Your father was a foundling.''"<br />"''Yes. He even fought in the Mandalorian Civil Wars.|Din Djarin and Boba Fett|<ref name="Chapter 14" />}}
[[File:Jango_Fett_Armed_to_the_Teeth_SoH.png|thumb|left|250px|Jango Fett was a foundling who wore Mandalorian armor as a bounty hunter.]]
The Fetts traced their origins to the [[Mandalorian]]s,<ref name="Chapter 14" /> a [[Mandalorian clan|clan]]-based cultural group<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> that originated from the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Mandalore DB">{{Databank|mandalore|Mandalore}}</ref> [[Jango Fett]]'s [[homeworld]] of [[Concord Dawn]]<ref name="Helmet Collection 17" /> was located in the [[Mandalore sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="USW NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Because his parents were [[Death|murdered]],<ref name="2023 Calendar">[[Star Wars Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2023|''Star Wars'' Day-at-a-Time Calendar 2023]]</ref> Fett was [[Jaste|raised]] as a [[foundling]] by the Mandalorians, and he was given a [[Boba Fett's armor|suit]] of [[Mandalorian armor]] and ultimately fought in the [[Mandalorian Civil Wars]].<ref name="Chapter 14" />

As a [[bounty hunter]], Jango's Mandalorian [[armor]] gave him an advantage over his opponents, particularly in aerial combat due to his [[jetpack]]. By the [[time]] of the late [[Republic Era]], he had gained notoriety as the best [[mercenary]] in the [[galaxy]].<ref name="Jango Fett DB" /> Although Jango had been a foundling,<ref name="Chapter 14" /> the [[New Mandalorians]] disavowed any connection between Jango and Mandalorian society. [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]], the head of [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]'s government on Mandalore, dismissed Jango as a common bounty hunter who simply wore the armor of a [[Mandalorian warrior]].<ref name="TMP">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref>

===Republic Era===
====Clone template====
{{Quote|I do believe that this army will be our finest creation yet. Not a single clone unit has fallen below combat parameters. You must be very proud.''"<br />"''What do I have to be proud of? Livestock bred as cannon fodder? Like you said, they're ''your'' creations.|Taun We and Jango Fett|<ref name="AoR JF" />}}
[[File:JangoFettMeetsWithDooku-AORJangoFett1.png|thumb|right|250px|During the reign of the Galactic Republic, Jango was chosen by the Sith to become the template of a clone army.]]
In the aftermath of the [[Invasion of Naboo]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="GA" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Dooku|Darth Tyranus]] approached Jango Fett on the [[Moons of Bogden]]. Acting on the [[Ruling Council (Kamino)|Kaminoan government's]] behalf, Tyranus offered Jango a considerable amount of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] in exchange for regular access to his [[gene]]tic material, having chosen a [[human]] [[mercenary]] with a reputation for skill and discretion as the [[clone template]] for the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. Jango ultimately accepted Tyranus' proposition and was [[Cloning|cloned]] on the [[extragalactic]] world of [[Kamino]].<ref name="AoR JF" /> Using Jango's [[DNA]] as a blueprint,<ref name="Jango Fett DB" /> [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s created the first generation of [[clone trooper]]s.<ref name="AoR JF" />

[[File:CloneCadets-AORJangoFett1.png|thumb|left|250px|The clones of Jango Fett were grown in facilities throughout Kamino, including Tipoca City.]]
Due to their genetic modifications, the clone [[trooper]]s were engineered to grow at twice the rate of normal human aging, which allowed the Kaminoans to grow [[battalion]]s of battle-ready [[troop]]s within a [[standard decade]] instead of the lifetime that it would have taken without the use of growth acceleration. Furthermore, Jango's clones were mentally conditioned to be more docile than their progenitor, making them compliant to following orders. Nevertheless, they were still capable of creative thinking, which, according to Lama Su, made clones far superior to [[droid]]s.<ref name="Episode II" /> However, some clones were less susceptible to psychological conditioning, and therefore, more independent than most of their brethren.<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref> [[Cut Lawquane]] was one such clone; disillusioned by the purpose of his creation, he became a deserter and started a [[Lawquane family|family]] of his own by [[Marriage|marrying]] the [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]] [[Suu Lawquane]].<ref name="Cut Lawquane DB">{{Databank|cut-lawquane|Cut Lawquane}}</ref>

[[File:Order66-SoH.png|thumb|right|250px|The Sith guided the creation of the clone army, intending to use it as a secret weapon against the Jedi Order.]]
The clones were bred to serve as [[soldier]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]];<ref name="Episode II" /> unknown to the clones, however,<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref> they were purpose-built for the [[Great Jedi Purge|systematic destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]], the [[Sith]] having programmed the clones to terminate the [[Jedi]] for [[treason]] in accordance with [[Order 66]].<ref name="Clone troopers DB">{{Databank|clone-troopers|Clone Troopers}}</ref> To that end, the Sith designed [[behavioral modification biochip]]s to ensure the clones' compliance. Believing the biochips acted as a contingency against betrayal from rogue Jedi,<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> the Kaminoans implanted the device in the [[brain]] of every clone trooper at the earliest stage of their development.<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref>

Jango remained on Kamino for several [[Standard year|years]], residing in [[Tipoca City]] where he observed the [[clone cadet]]s who trained to become soldiers. While the Kaminoans such as [[Prime Minister]] [[Lama Su]]<ref name="Episode II" /> and [[Taun We]] reveled in the success of their creations, regarding the Fett clones as a testament to their skills in cloning, Jango dismissed his duplicates as "livestock" bred to be "[[cannon]] fodder."<ref name="AoR JF" />

[[File:Clonesmarching-fullshotATOC.png|thumb|left|300px|Utilizing growth acceleration, the Kaminoans completed the first generation of clone troopers in a decade's time.]]
Within the cloning facility of Tipoca [[City]] alone, over a million clones of Jango Fett comprised the first generation of the clone army,<ref name="Episode II" /> while millions more were bred in other facilities located throughout Kamino.<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]], who adopted the name Rex and served as [[commanding officer]] of the [[501st Legion]]'s [[Torrent Company]],<ref name="CT-7567">{{Databank|clone-captain-rex|Clone Captain Rex}}</ref> was a first-generation clone.<ref name="Battle Scars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref> Unlike the clone troopers who were genetically modified,<ref name="Episode II" /> two clones of Jango Fett&mdash;designated as [[Boba Fett|Alpha]] and [[Omega]]<ref name="Bounty Lost">{{TBB|Bounty Lost}}</ref>&mdash;were created through pure genetic replication,<ref name="Episode II" /> and therefore possessed Jango's DNA in its purest form.<ref name="Bounty Lost" /> Whereas Alpha<ref name="Boba Fett DB">{{Databank|boba-fett|Boba Fett}}</ref> and the clone troopers were male<ref name="SWECloneTroopers" /> like their template,<ref name="Jango Fett DB" /> Omega was the first female clone,<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> with [[Emerie Karr]] being the second.<ref name="Plan 99" />

====Jango Fett's heir====
{{Quote|You shot well. You trusted your judgment. And you've started building your own reputation. A father couldn't ask for a better start for his son's legacy.|Jango Fett, to Boba Fett|<ref name="AoR JF" />}}
[[File:DoctorAphra13-50years-textless.png|thumb|right|250px|Boba Fett was an unmodified clone that Jango raised as his son.]]
Taking no pride in the clones<ref name="AoR JF" /> that would form the backbone of the [[Republic Military]],<ref name="Clone troopers DB" /> Jango became invested in Alpha,<ref name="AoR JF" /> the unaltered clone that he specifically requested as part of his payment.<ref name="Episode II" /> Adopting the name "Boba Fett,"<ref name="Bounty Lost" /> he was regarded as a son by Jango.<ref name="R2">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref> Boba, in turn, considered Jango as his [[Parent|father]].<ref name="Chapter 14" /> The Fetts were able to communicate with each other in their own [[language]],<ref name="Episode II" /> which was known as the [[Fett Code]].<ref name="YouTube">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Language in Star Wars Movies|video=CZp8Civk2co|nolive=1}}</ref>

Jango's training shaped Boba into a lethal combatant; as a [[youngling]], Boba was more skilled in marksmanship than most beings in the galaxy. He learned the art of combat<ref name="Boba Fett DB">{{Databank|boba-fett|Boba Fett}}</ref> while accompanying Jango on his assignments as a [[bounty]] hunter. One such [[Mission to Ord Mantell (Jango Fett)|mission]] occurred on [[Ord Mantell]], where the Jango and Boba worked with the mercenaries [[Neelda]], [[Rinn (Gand)|Rinn]], and [[Tiver]]. The Fetts were ultimately betrayed by Rinn and Tiver, taking Boba as a hostage. However, they were both killed by the clone after Jango declined to interfere, having chosen to use the situation to test Boba. Although Boba based his actions according to what he believed Jango would have done in his position, Jango saw potential in his son and expressed pride as a father.<ref name="AoR JF" />

====Death of Jango Fett====
{{Quote|Do you like your army?''"<br />"''I look forward to seeing them in action.''"<br />"''They'll do their job well. I'll guarantee that.|Jango Fett and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Episode II" />}}
[[File:FettKenobiMeeting.png|thumb|left|250px|The Fetts encountered Obi-Wan Kenobi, a Jedi Knight, who traveled to Kamino in pursuit of the assassin who tried to kill the senator of Naboo.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="GA" /> during the last [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Separatist Crisis]], Jango Fett was hired by [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]]. The [[Plot to assassinate Senator Amidala|plot]] was foiled by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], resulting in the capture of Jango's partner [[Zam Wesell]]. Jango attempted to hide his involvement in the attempt on Amidala's life by killing Wesell with a [[Kamino saberdart]]. However, Kenobi successfully tracked the bounty hunter by investigating the saberdart that killed Jango's [[Clawdite]] partner. He subsequently discovered both the clone army and its template during his [[Mission to Kamino (Separatist Crisis)|visit to Kamino]], and was instructed by the [[Jedi High Council]] to bring Jango to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] for interrogation.<ref name="Episode II" />

[[File:Jangodied.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Jango Fett was killed in battle by Jedi Master Mace Windu.]]
The Fetts were in the process of leaving Kamino by the time Kenobi confronted them after reporting to the High [[Jedi Council|Council]]. Jango fought Kenobi while Boba prepared their personal [[starship]], ''[[Slave I]]'', for departure. Although they ultimately escaped<ref name="Episode II" /> after Boba almost killed Kenobi with ''Slave I''{{'s}} heavy weapons,<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> Kenobi pursued the Fetts from Kamino to [[Geonosis]] where he discovered the [[Separatist Droid Army]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The Separatists captured Kenobi, who was [[Death penalty|sentenced to die]] alongside Skywalker and Amidala in the [[Petranaki Arena]]. The [[First Battle of Geonosis|first battle]] of the [[Clone Wars]] commenced between [[battle droid]]s and the [[Jedi assault team]] that infiltrated the Separatist stronghold to rescue Kenobi. Jango confronted [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], an expert in [[lightsaber combat]], while defending his benefactor [[Count]] Dooku. Windu ultimately prevailed in the contest, using his [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|amethyst-bladed lightsaber]] to decapitate the bounty hunter<ref name="Jango Fett DB" /> shortly before the arrival of the clone troopers.<ref name="Episode II" />

====Clone Wars====
=====The orphan=====
{{Quote|Boba was on Geonosis when his father died. He watched as I killed him.''"<br />"''Ah&hellip; That would complicate things.|Mace Windu and Anakin Skywalker|<ref name="R2" />}}
[[File:Young_Fett.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Orphaned by Jango's death, Boba sought revenge against Windu.]]
Boba Fett, orphaned as a child, spent his adolescence in the galaxy's criminal underworld. He joined a group of mercenaries led by the bounty hunter [[Aurra Sing]],<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> who became a mentor to the orphaned clone of Jango Fett.<ref name="Aurra Sing DB">{{Databank|aurra-sing|Aurra Sing}}</ref> Sing raised Boba to become a bounty hunter as well, teaching him the skills of her trade<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> while also trying to impart her ruthlessness to her ward.<ref name="Aurra Sing DB" />

Shocked by Jango's swift death on Geonosis, Boba became obsessed with seeking revenge against the Jedi who killed his father.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> By then, Windu was a [[Jedi General]] in the Grand Army of the Republic, giving him the authority to command the clone troopers alongside the other Jedi.<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref> Intent on collecting the bounties placed on the Jedi by the Separatists, Sing became Boba's accomplice in his plan to assassinate Windu.<ref name="Aurra Sing DB" />

=====Attempted assassination=====
{{Quote|I just wanted to kill the Jedi that murdered my father.''"<br />"''Well, that will have to wait. Grow up. You'll get your revenge in time.|Boba Fett and Aurra Sing|<ref name="Death Trap" />}}
[[File:Epguide220.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Boba pretended to be a clone cadet while attempting to assassinate Windu.]]
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="21 BBY Date">{{TCWcanondates|220}}</ref> Boba joined a [[brigade]] of [[clone cadet]]s, the [[Clone Youth Brigade]], in order to infiltrate Windu's [[command ship]], the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Endurance]]'' where he bombed the [[general]]'s quarters. The assassination attempt failed, however, causing Boba to [[Sabotage of the Endurance|sabotage the ''Endurance'']].<ref name="Death Trap" /> He used an [[escape pod]] to leave the [[Star Destroyer]] as it crashed on [[Vanqor]], and was rescued by his accomplices Sing, [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], and [[Castas]].<ref name="Death Trap" /> Sing, having stressed the necessity for ruthlessness in Boba's training as a bounty hunter,<ref name="Aurra Sing DB" /> urged him to kill the [[cadet]]s who fled the ''Endurance'' with him.<ref name="Death Trap" /> Although Boba was a bounty hunter, he was not as ruthless as his mentor. As such, he refused to kill the cadets who looked like his [[twin]]s.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> While he did not consider himself a clone like the troopers who served the Jedi and the Republic,<ref name="Death Trap" /> they were nonetheless his genetic duplicates.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" />

[[File:BobaBosskCastasSing-TCWS2E20.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Joined by the bounty hunters Bossk and Castas, Boba and his mentor Aurra Sing attempted to assassinate Windu.]]
The bounty hunters [[Mission to Vanqor|traveled to Vanqor]] where Boba planted a bomb in the ''Endurance''{{'s}} [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]]. The bomb was planted in Jango's [[helmet]], which Anakin Skywalker inadvertently activated. Windu saved Skywalker's life using [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|pull]] the other Jedi away from the trap, although the explosion caused greater damage to the ''Endurance'', trapping Windu and Skywalker beneath the wreckage of the [[starship]]. Boba and his accomplices searched the ''Endurance'' in order to confirm Windu's death, but were distracted by Skywalker's [[astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], who deceived the bounty hunters into believing that Windu escaped from Vanqor. The mercenaries regrouped elsewhere, unaware that Windu was still on the ''Endurance'', taking several hostages with them.<ref name="R2">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref>

[[File:WinduSkywalkerEndurance.png|thumb|right|250px|Windu deduced that Boba was the assassin who tried to kill him, knowing the orphaned clone witnessed Jango's death.]]
Windu inspected the damaged Mandalorian helmet while waiting for Skywalker's [[droid]] to rescue them, and realized that the culprit was Boba. They had learned of Boba through Kenobi's report from Kamino, and knew that he was a clone whom Jango regarded as a son. Windu revealed to Skywalker that Boba witnessed his killing of Jango on Geonosis. Both Jedi were ultimately rescued by the [[Wolfpack]], a clone trooper [[squad]] led by [[Plo Koon]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]], R2-D2 having reached the [[Jedi temple]] on Coruscant.<ref name="R2" /> Despite Skywalker's insistence on capturing Boba, Windu chose to remain focused on the war effort, not wishing to be further distracted by Boba's personal vendetta.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lethal-trackdown-episode-guide|text="Lethal Trackdown" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

=====Showdown on Florrum=====
{{Quote|I see now I've done terrible things, but you started it when you murdered my father. I'll never forgive you.''"<br />"''Well, you're going to have to.|Boba Fett and Mace Windu|<ref name="Lethal Trackdown">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref>}}
Boba and Sing sent a transmission to Windu while he recovered at the Jedi Temple, revealing the [[prisoner]]s that they captured on Vanqor. The [[Hologram|holographic]] recording showed the execution of [[Clone Commander]] [[Ponds]], whom Sing personally killed<ref name="Lethal Trackdown" /> after Boba hesitated.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Ep Guide" /> The bounty hunter warned the Jedi that she would kill the other of the captives, including [[Admiral]] [[Shoan Kilian]], if Windu did not confront Boba. Ponds' body was then ejected from ''Slave I'' and left to drift in [[Realspace|space]]; Sing chastised Boba for not killing the [[clone trooper officer]], warning him not to hesitate.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown" />

[[File:Boba_Confronts_Mace_Windu.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Boba was unrepentant despite his incarceration, refusing to forgive Windu for Jango's death.]]
With Windu and Skywalker still incapacitated due to their injuries, Ahsoka Tano and Plo Koon [[Second mission to Florrum|tracked]] the bounty hunters to a [[priate]] [[Hondo's complex|lair]] on the planet [[Florrum]]. Sing fled from the Jedi, leaving Boba to be captured.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Ep Guide" /> In spite of Sing's efforts to instill ruthlessness in him,<ref name="Aurra Sing DB" /> Boba was shocked by his mentor's willingness to leave him behind.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> Defeated and captured,<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Ep Guide" /> he was initially uncooperative with the Jedi until the leader of the [[Ohnaka Gang]], [[Hondo Ohnaka]], convinced Boba that saving the hostages was an honorable choice that Jango would have supported.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown" /> Charged with the destruction of the ''Endurance'', attempted assassination, and taking hostages, Boba was imprisoned in the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]] on Coruscant. He did not regret his actions and continued to hold a vendetta against Windu.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" />

=====An aspiring bounty hunter=====
{{Quote|No one of true importance would lower themselves to trade with a mere boy.''"<br />"''Boy? You have no idea who you're talking to. I have been in charge of this ''whole'' operation&mdash;''"<br />"You'' have no idea who you are talking to.|Asajj Ventress and Boba Fett|<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:Boba_Fett_Tied_Up.png|thumb|right|250px|Boba was betrayed by Asajj Ventress, who rejected the authority of a teenaged bounty hunter.]]

In addition to Boba, the group consisted of Bossk, [[Dengar]], [[C-21 Highsinger]], [[Latts Razzi]], and [[Asajj Ventress]].<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> Unlike the other hunters who deferred to Boba's leadership, Ventress was unenthusiastic about following a child's orders.<ref name="Bounty Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/bounty-episode-guide|text="Bounty" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> Boba led the syndicate during an [[Safe cargo objective|operation]] on [[Quarzite]]. They were hired by the [[Belugan]]s to deliver [[Pluma Sodi]] to [[Otua Blank]], who intended to make Sodi his [[Marriage|bride]]. The [[Kage Warriors]] fought against the members of Krayt's Claw while attempting to rescue Sodi before she could be taken to the Belugan [[Dictatorship|dictator]], leaving only Boba and Ventress to complete the mission.<ref name="Bounty" /> However, Ventress sympathized with Sodi and allowed her to rejoin the [[Kage]], betraying Boba in the process.<ref name="Asajj Ventress DB">{{Databank|asajj-ventress|Asajj Ventress}}</ref>

===Imperial Era===
====End of the clone troopers====
{{Quote|If Rampart has his way, conscripted soldiers will make clones obsolete. I fear for the future of our operation. We must ensure our clones remain essential.''"<br />"''The original genetic material from Jango Fett continues to degrade.|Lama Su and Nala Se|<ref name="Replacements">{{TBB|Replacements}}</ref>}}
[[File:ImperialCloneArmy.png|thumb|left|300px|Jango Fett's clones composed the the first generation of Imperial stormtroopers.]]
By the end of the Clone Wars, the clone troopers had turned against the Jedi who led them throughout the conflict, destroying most of the Order while under the influence of the inhibitor chip that compelled them to comply with Order 66.<ref name="Clone troopers DB" /> In the wake of the Great Jedi Purge and the [[Mission to Mustafar|collapse]] of the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist leadership]], [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]&mdash;the Sith [[Lord]] [[Darth]] Sidious&mdash;was [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared]] the [[Galactic Emperor]], transforming the Republic into the first [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="GA" />

[[File:OmegaGalaxyOfHeroes.png|thumb|right|200px|The Kaminoans intended to use the clone Omega as a fresh source of first-generation Fett DNA.]]
Following the [[Emperor]]'s rise to power, [[Admiral]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] informed the [[Ruling Council (Kamino)|Kaminoan government]] of the [[New Order]]'s [[Project War-Mantle|plan]] to supplant clones with conscripted soldiers.<ref name="Aftermath episode">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> Prime Minister Lama Su argued that [[Reproduction|natural-born]] soldiers were inherently inferior to his clones, who were trained from their creation. The death of Jango Fett, however, left the Kaminoans with a limited supply of his genetic material, which had deteriorated over the course of the Clone Wars. Fearing for the future of Kamino and its operation, Lama Su sought to create a new version of clone troopers<ref name="Replacements" /> by using a fresh source of the Jango Fett genotype.<ref name="Bounty Lost">{{TBB|Bounty Lost}}</ref> The Prime Minister's contingency relied on two clones,<ref name="Replacements" /> Alpha and Omega, who possessed unmodified first-generation Fett DNA. As neither clone was in their possession by this time, the Kaminoans employed mercenaries to hunt Omega<ref name="Bounty Lost" /> after she escaped from Kamino with the renegade members of [[Clone Force 99]].<ref name="Aftermath episode" />

In spite of Lama Su's efforts to ensure the continuation of the clone trooper program, the Empire [[Attack on Kamino|decommissioned]] the cloning facilities across Kamino after canceling its contracts with the Kaminoan government.<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref> The final batches of Jango Fett's clones would be activated,<ref name="Dark Lord 2">[[Darth Vader (2017) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 2]]</ref> but future generations of [[stormtrooper]]s would consist of human volunteers<ref name="Stormtroopers DB">{{Databank|stormtroopers|Stormtroopers}}</ref> and conscripts.<ref name="Veteran Databank">{{Databank|clone-veteran|Clone Veteran}}</ref> The first wave of non-clone soldiers were trained by experienced Fett clones, such as Clone [[Commander]] [[Crosshair]], who trained a [[Elite Squad|unit]] of [[Elite Squad Trooper]]s,<ref name="Replacements" /> as well as the [[clone commando]]s who served as instructors at the [[Imperial Base on Daro|Imperial Base]] on [[Daro]].<ref name="War-Mantle" />

====Rise and fall of Boba Fett====
=====The deadliest hunter in the galaxy=====
{{Quote|The only time I fail is if you hired me to fail.|Boba Fett|<ref name="Darth Vader 1" />}}
[[File:Boba_Fett_SaV.png|thumb|left|250px|Boba Fett was a legendary bounty hunter, like his father before him, by the time of the Imperial Era.]]
Using the combat and martial skills he learned at a young age, Boba Fett cemented his career as a bounty hunter and was known throughout the galaxy as one of its most lethal mercenaries.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> The Mandalorian armor that Jango wore during his career as a bounty hunter was inherited by Boba.<ref name="Chapter 14">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 14: The Tragedy}}</ref> The armor was customized and damaged over the course of Boba's career, yet remained reminiscent of the design utilized by Jango.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> ''Slave I'', the modified [[Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft|''Firespray-31''-class patrol and attack craft]]<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> that once served as Jango's starship, was also left in Boba's possession.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" />

As a member of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]],<ref name="Age of Rebellion" /> Boba was often employed as a hired enforcer for the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. In addition to working for [[Jabba's criminal empire|Jabba Desilijic Tiure's criminal empire]], Boba accepted contracts from Darth Vader,<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] who served as the Emperor's chief enforcer in the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Darth Vader DB">{{Databank|darth-vader|Darth Vader}}</ref> Following the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Boba reported the identity of the [[Rebel pilot]]<ref name="Darth Vader 6">[[Darth Vader (2015) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 6]]</ref> who destroyed the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]],<ref name="Episode IV" /> [[Luke Skywalker]], to Vader. The Sith Lord who was once Anakin Skywalker realized that Luke was his [[Skywalker family|son]].<ref name="Darth Vader 6" />

=====The bounty on Han Solo=====
{{Quote|You may take Captain Solo to Jabba the Hutt after I have Skywalker.''"<br />"''He's no good to me dead.''"<br />"''He will not be permanently damaged.|Darth Vader and Boba Fett|<ref name="Episode V" />}}
[[File:BobaVaderLando.jpg|thumb|right|250px|In order to find the ''Millennium Falcon'', Darth Vader hired Boba Fett who sought to claim the bounty on Han Solo.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Vader hired Boba as well as several mercenaries to hunt down a Rebel ship, the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', that escaped from [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] during the [[Battle of Hoth]]<ref name="Episode V" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="GA" /> The ship's [[pilot]], [[Captain]] [[Han Solo]], was a [[smuggler]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> whom Boba encountered during a meeting with Jabba<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> on Tatooine, shortly before the Battle of [[Yavin system|Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" /> At the time Vader was determined to capture Luke Skywalker, his secret son who aspired to become a Jedi Knight. Aware of Skywalker's friendship with the occupants of the ''Millennium Falcon'', the Sith Lord intended to use them to lure Skywalker out of hiding. Vader therefore informed the bounty hunters that the Rebels they would hunt were to be captured instead of killed, and specifically warned Boba to not disintegrate his targets.<ref name="Episode V" />

Boba successfully tracked the ''Millennium Falcon'' to the [[Bespin system]], where Solo and his companions sought refuge in [[Cloud City]]. As a reward for his role in the capture of Skywalker's friends, Boba was allowed to take Solo to Jabba in order to claim the Hutt's bounty on the smuggler. Although [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] attempted to rescue Solo, who was frozen in [[carbonite]], she was unable to prevent Boba's departure from [[Bespin]].<ref name="Episode V" />

=====The sarlacc=====
{{Quote|Boba Fett? Boba Fett? Where?|Han Solo|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
[[File:Bobawhere.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Solo inadvertently ignited the jetpack worn by Boba, causing the bounty hunter to fall into a sarlacc pit.]]
Boba transported Solo to Tatooine, where he collected the carbon-frozen smuggler's bounty from Jabba, and resumed his place at the Hutt [[gangster]]'s side as one of Jabba's most trusted enforcers.<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> Remaining at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], Boba was present when Organa infiltrated the Hutt stronghold while [[Boushh's armor|disguised]] as the bounty hunter [[Boushh]], determined to [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] Solo. She was captured, however, and Jabba sentenced her friends to die in the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. As the Rebels fought with Jabba's [[Bodyguard|guards]] in a final attempt at escape, Boba personally confronted Luke Skywalker.<ref name="Episode VI" /> He was quickly taken out of the fight by Solo, who accidentally hit Boba's [[jetpack]]<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> while still blinded from the side effects of the carbon freezing.<ref name="Episode VI" /> The jetpack ignited, causing the bounty hunter to crash against the side of Jabba's [[sail barge]], the ''[[Khetanna]]'', before falling into the pit of a "man-eating"<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> [[sarlacc]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

===New Republic Era===
====Left for dead====
{{Quote|But fate sometimes steps in to rescue the wretched.|Boba Fett|<ref name="Chapter 14" />}}
[[File:Boba_Fett_Escapes_Sarlacc.png|thumb|right|250px|Although Boba survived the sarlacc, his armor was taken by a group of Jawas.]]
Although Boba Fett was rumored to have died,<ref name="Chapter 16">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 16: The Rescue}}</ref> he managed to escape from the sarlacc by burning it from the inside with his armor's [[flamethrower]]. A group of [[Jawa]]s found Boba after he dug his way back to the surface. The [[scavenger]]s removed Boba's armor while he was injured and unconscious, and left him in the desert where he was discovered by [[Tusken Raider]]s. Though initially treated as a [[prisoner]],<ref name="Stranger" /> Boba gradually gained the trust of his captors' [[Tusken Chieftain|chieftain]]<ref name="Tribes" /> after saving the life of a [[Unidentified Tusken Raider youngling|youngling]].<ref name="Stranger" />

Over time he learned to communicate in [[Tusken]], as well as how to fight with a [[gaderffii stick]]. Boba in turn taught the Tuskens how to operate [[repulsorcraft]]s in order to better protect their territory. Using several [[speeder bike]]s that Boba confiscated from a [[Unidentified Nikto biker gang|gang]] of [[Nikto]] bikers at [[Tosche Station]], the Tuskens [[Raid on a Pyke repulsortrain|raided]] a [[Pyke Syndicate]] [[Tatooine repulsor train|repulsortrain]] as it crossed through the [[Dune Sea]]. The [[hovertrain]] was derailed during the attack, allowing the Tuskens to seize the [[train]]'s supplies.<ref name="Tribes" />

[[File:BobaReturnsFromVisionQuest-BoBFCh2.png|thumb|left|250px|Boba was taken in by a tribe of Tusken Raiders, who taught him their ways.]]
In the aftermath of the raid, the [[chief]]tain gifted Boba with a [[Horned gekko|gekko]] that was meant to serve as his guide. The [[lizard]] entered Boba's [[brain]] through his [[Nose|nostril]], causing the clone to hallucinate as he wandered through the Dune Sea in a [[dream]] induced state. He saw visions of his childhood on Kamino, as well as his near-death experience in the sarlacc pit, and eventually found a [[tree]] in the desert. He used one of its [[Wood|branches]] to [[Gaderffii stick workshop|build]] his own gaderffii stick, which he presented to the members of the [[Unidentified Tusken Raider tribe|Tusken tribe]] that had taken him in.<ref name="Tribes" /> While Fett was away,<ref name="The Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> the Pyke Syndicate<ref name="Name of Honor">{{BOBF|Chapter 7: In the Name of Honor}}</ref> [[Massacre of a Tusken Raider tribe|butchered the tribe]].<ref name="The Gathering Storm" />

With his new family lost, Fett decided to return to the criminal underworld with a new perspective. After dealing with "idiot" crime lords who led people like him to their deaths,<ref name="The Gathering Storm" /> a fate he had only narrowly escaped in the sarlacc pit,<ref name="Stranger" /> and then seeing how successful one could be with a "tribe" supporting them, Fett believed he could forge a new, successful criminal family<ref name="The Gathering Storm" /> based upon respect.<ref name="Stranger" />

====The armor's custodian====
{{Quote|Where did you get the armor?''"<br />"''Bought it off some Jawas.|Din Djarin and Cobb Vanth|<ref name="Chapter 9">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 9: The Marshal}}</ref>}}
[[File:CobbVanthArmor-TMCh9.png|thumb|right|250px|The Jawas who took Boba's armor sold it to Cobb Vanth, who became the marshal of Mos Pelgo.]]
Following the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Chapter 9" /> in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="GA" /> the Mandalorian armor that the Jawas took from Boba Fett<ref name="Stranger" /> was in turn sold to [[Cobb Vanth]].<ref name="Chapter 9" /> During his time as the armor's custodian,<ref name="Cobb Vanth DB">{{Databank|cobb-vanth|Cobb Vanth}}</ref> Vanth liberated [[Freetown|Mos Pelgo]] and became a [[marshal]]. Although the armor had passed into Vanth's possession,<ref name="Cobb Vanth DB" /> Boba was determined to reclaim it. The armor was made from [[beskar]], which the Mandalorians claimed as their property. While Boba did not consider himself a Mandalorian, the armor was gifted to his father, Jango Fett, by the Mandalorians who raised him as a foundling. Therefore, as Jango's son, Boba believed that the armor was his inheritance.<ref name="Chapter 14" />

Boba's armor remained in Vanth's possession for several years during the [[New Republic Era]],<ref name="Chapter 9" /> which saw the [[Battle of Jakku|fall of the Galactic Empire]] and the rise of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="9 ABY">{{MandoDate|2}}</ref> Vanth encountered the Mandalorian warrior [[Din Djarin]] who demanded that the marshal relinquish the armor due to the fact that he was not a Mandalorian. Ultimately, they agreed to trade the armor for Djarin's help in killing the [[Leviathan krayt dragon|krayt dragon]] that threatened the population of Mos Pelgo. They accomplished their [[Mission to Tatooine (Din Djarin)|objective]] with help from the citizens of Mos Pelgo and the [[Tusken Raider]]s. With the dragon slain, Boba's armor passed from Vanth to Djarin.<ref name="Chapter 9" /> As a result though, Djarin was tracked by Boba who was still intent on regaining his armor.<ref name="Chapter 14" />

====Armor reclaimed====
{{Quote|I want my armor back.''"<br />"''It goes against the Mandalorian Creed.''"<br />"''The armor was given to my father, Jango, by your forebears.|Boba Fett and Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 14" />}}
Boba confronted Djarin on the planet [[Tython]], where the Mandalorian had traveled as part of his quest to reunite the foundling [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] with the Jedi Order. Aided by [[Fennec Shand]],<ref name="Chapter 14" /> a bounty hunter whom Boba saved after her [[Hunt for Fennec Shand|near-death experience]] on Tatooine,<ref name="Chapter 5">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 5: The Gunslinger}}</ref> Boba attempted to reclaim his armor from Djarin in exchange for the safety of Grogu.<ref name="Chapter 14" />

[[File:TheMandalorianSeason2-6-textless-variant-Maleev.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Boba reclaimed his armor on Tython, an outcome that Din Djarin accepted after learning that its original owner was a foundling.]]
Djarin rejected Boba's offer, remaining committed to the [[Mandalorian Creed]] which prevented him from surrendering the armor and its beskar to a non-Mandalorian. As Boba started to reveal the origins of his father in an effort to persuade Djarin, [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]'s [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] arrived on Tython with the goal of [[Hunt for Grogu|capturing Grogu]]. The ensuing [[Battle of Tython (9 ABY)|battle]] forced Djarin to fight alongside the bounty hunters in order to protect Grogu, giving Boba an opportunity to take his armor from the Mandalorian's ship, the ''[[Razor Crest]]''.<ref name="Chapter 14" />

Redonning his armor, Boba killed several stormtroopers on his own while the rest of the troops retreated to the [[V-82 dropship]]s. As the Imperials retreated, Boba launched the [[missile]] on his [[Z-6 jetpack]] at one of the vessels, resulting in a mid-air collision that destroyed both [[transport]]s. In the aftermath of the skirmish, Boba used the armor's [[chain code]] to support his claim on the armor, revealing that it once belonged to his father. Jango's past as a foundling convinced Djarin to allow Boba to keep the armor. Having guaranteed Grogu's safety in exchange for the armor's return, Boba pledged his service to the Mandalorian's cause until the Child was rescued from Gideon.<ref name="Chapter 14" />

====Working with Mandalorians====
{{Quote|You are not a Mandalorian.''"<br />"''Never said I was.|Bo-Katan Kryze and Boba Fett|<ref name="Chapter 16" />}}
[[File:BobaFettRepaintedArmor-SS.png|thumb|right|250px|Boba Fett in his cleaned up armor]]
With his armor back in his possession, Boba Fett's gear was cleaned up. With his newly repainted armor, Fett and company traveled to pick up former Imperial sniper [[Migs Mayfeld]]. During the ensuing [[Mission to Morak]], Fett did not join Djarin in entering an [[Morak Imperial refinery|Imperial refinery]], telling Djarin the Imperials would recognize his face, which forced Djarin to partner with Mayfeld. Fett flew him and Mayfield to safety in the ''Slave'' I once they had information to track down [[Gideon's light cruiser]].<ref name="Chapter 15">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 15: The Believer}}</ref>

Boba and Djarin traveled to the [[desert]] world [[Lafete]] seeking the help of [[Bo-Katan Kryze]],<ref name="Chapter 16" /> the leader of the [[Mandalorian resistance]] whom Djarin [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|worked with]] on the [[moon]] [[Trask]].<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> While Kryze acknowledged Djarin&mdash;a member of the [[Children of the Watch]]&mdash;as a fellow Mandalorian, she did not view Boba as such, knowing that he was a clone. She also considered his use of Mandalorian armor disgraceful. Although Boba did not claim to be a Mandalorian, he based his claim on Jango's armor<ref name="Chapter 16" /> as the son of a foundling.<ref name="Chapter 14" /> Kryze dismissed Boba's claim due to his origins as a clone and Jango's relation to him as a genetic donor. Nevertheless, Kryze agreed to join their quest in order to confront Gideon and reclaim the [[Darksaber]], an ancient [[lightsaber]] that served as a symbol of unity and leadership on Mandalore.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

With the Mandalorians' support, Djarin's allies executed a [[Second rescue of Grogu|plan to rescue]] Grogu. Using a stolen [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class T-4a shuttle]], the group infiltrated Gideon's light cruiser. Boba feigned an attack, firing the weapons on ''Slave I'' at the Imperial [[shuttle]] to deceive the [[light cruiser]]'s crew into believing that the [[shuttle]] was piloted by members of their remnant. With Djarin's team onboard the [[Class 546 Cruiser]], Boba drew away three [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s before jumping into [[hyperspace]], leaving his allies to complete the mission.<ref name="Chapter 16" />

====The new crime lord====
{{Quote|Jabba ruled with fear. I intend to rule with respect.|Boba Fett|<ref name="Stranger" />}}
[[File:BookofBoba1-EMGistCover.png|thumb|left|250px|Boba Fett became the new Daimyo of the criminal empire that was once ruled by Jabba the Hutt.]]
Having paid their debt to Djarin, Boba and Shand returned to Tatooine where they [[Attack on Jabba's Palace (Boba Fett)|confronted]] [[Bib Fortuna]],<ref name="Chapter 16" /> a [[Twi'lek]] crime lord who once served as the [[majordomo]] of Jabba Desilijic Tiure. Following Jabba's death,<ref name="Tribes" /> as well as the rumored demise of Boba,<ref name="Chapter 16" /> Fortuna had seized his late master's [[throne]] in the ensuing power vacuum on Tatooine. Boba ended Fortuna's reign by killing and replacing him as the Daimyo of [[Mos Espa]]. The newly styled Daiymo Fett began building his own [[Fett gotra|criminal empire]] on Tatooine<ref name="Tribes" /> inspired by what he had learned with the Tuskens.<ref name="The Gathering Storm" />

He established his headquarters in Jabba's [[Boba Fett's Palace|old palace]], keeping Shand as an ally and utilizing the droid [[8D8]] as an [[advisor]]. He also spared the lives of two [[Gamorrean Warrior]]s, who once served Fortuna, after they agreed to become his [[bodyguard]]s. In order to maintain Jabba's vast empire, Boba began vassalizing several prominent citizens of Mos Espa, such as Don [[Garfalaquox]] and [[Dokk Strassi]] of the [[Strassi family]]. In addition, the [[Garsa Fwip's Sanctuary|Sanctuary]]&mdash;a [[cantina]] owned by Madam [[Garsa Fwip]]&mdash;was brought under Boba's control in the wake of Fortuna's death. Ultimately, his goal was to preserve Jabba's empire by controlling the vassals of the deceased Hutt crime lord. Unlike his predecessors who ruled through fear, Boba sought to build his reign on the respect of his vassals. However, Shand was convinced that fear was a more reliable form of control in unstable times.<ref name="Stranger" />

[[File:BobaFettTwinsFaceoff-BoBFCh2.png|thumb|right|250px|Boba's legitimacy as the Daimyo of Mos Espa was disputed by Jabba's cousins, the Twins from Nal Hutta.]]
Following an [[Ambush outside of the Sanctuary|attempted assassination]] on Boba by the [[Order of the Night Wind]], Boba confronted [[Mok Shaiz]], the [[Mayor of Mos Espa]], although the [[mayor]] denied hiring the [[assassin]]s. Boba then learned that [[the Twins]]&mdash;a [[Unidentified Hutt brother|brother]] and [[Unidentified Hutt sister|sister]]&mdash;arrived from [[Nal Hutta]] to claim the bequest of their late cousin, Jabba Desilijic Tiure. Boba disputed the [[Twin|siblings']] claim by stating that Jabba's territory had been usurped by Fortuna, who was killed by Boba in turn. As such, Boba asserted that he was the legitimate Daimyo of Mos Espa.<ref name="Tribes" /> In the [[Battle of Mos Espa]], Fett asserted his claim by defeating the Pyke Syndicate's enforcers, including his former mentor [[Cad Bane]].<ref name="Name of Honor" />

===Legacy===
====An army of clones====
{{Quote|Think of it. A Grand Army of the Republic. A show of strength and power like the galaxy has never seen. All stemming from you. How many men can claim such a legacy?''"<br />"''The credits are good. But there's something else I'm going to need as compensation&hellip;|Darth Tyranus and Jango Fett|<ref name="AoR JF" />}}
[[File:ClonesInFormation.png|thumb|left|250px|Part of Jango Fett's legacy was an army of clone troopers, which the Sith utilized to destroy the Jedi Order and establish the Galactic Empire.]]
The millions of clone troopers who served in the Grand Army of the Republic deployed throughout the galaxy, fighting against Separatist forces on numerous contested worlds in the name of the Galactic Republic. However, neither the Republic nor the Jedi suspected that the clones whom they relied on were part of a [[Grand Plan|Sith plot]] to take control of the galaxy. Programmed for absolute loyalty to the Supreme Chancellor, they executed their Jedi [[officer]]s<ref name="Jango Fett DB" /> under Order 66,<ref name="Episode III" /> effectively purging the Jedi Order from the galaxy.<ref name="Jango Fett DB" /> In spite of their personal loyalty to the Jedi who fought alongside them, the clones were the instrument<ref name="Clone troopers DB" /> of revenge for the Sith<ref name="Episode III" /> and Jango Fett.<ref name="Jango Fett DB" />

====A clone for a son====
{{Quote|You are a disgrace to your armor.''"<br />"''This armor belonged to my father.''"<br />"''Don't you mean your donor?|Bo-Katan Kryze and Boba Fett|<ref name="Chapter 16" />}}
[[File:Vow_of_Vengeance_AtG.png|thumb|right|220px|The memory of Jango's death remained with Boba throughout his life.]]
Aside from the clone troopers, the legacy of Jango Fett&mdash;reputedly the "best bounty hunter in the galaxy"&mdash;survived through Boba Fett, the clone whom he raised as his son. Boba was a precocious child who grew up in the criminal underworld after Jango's death. Aspiring to become a legendary bounty hunter, Boba's reputation would ultimately surpass that of Jango, who taught him the family trade,<ref name="Jango Fett DB" /> as Boba went on to become "one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy."<ref name="Boba Fett DB" /> Boba carried the memory of his father into adulthood; he once used the alias "Beskar Brawler Jango" during his brief stint in [[Fighting pit|pit]] fighting.<ref name="WotBHA1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters Alpha'' 1]]</ref> After becoming a Daimyo on Tatooine, Boba experienced dreams of his past memories, including his childhood on Kamino and Jango's death on Geonosis.<ref name="Stranger" />
