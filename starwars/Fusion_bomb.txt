'''Fusion bombs''' were a type of powerful [[bomb]] device utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]], the [[Imperial Navy]] deployed a massive fleet of [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE/sa bombers]] armed with fusion bombs, devastating the domed cities of the planet and crystalizing much of its surface, converting it into [[trinitite]]. The bombs were also capable of disrupting the magnetic field of a planet and causing communication interference.<ref name="Chapter 18">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 18: The Mines of Mandalore}}</ref>
