'''Chaf'pri'uhme''', [[core name]] '''Afpriuh''', was a [[Chiss]] [[Gender|male]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[Senior Commander]] in the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. He served aboard the [[heavy cruiser]] ''[[Springhawk]]'' under [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] as [[weapons officer]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

Afpriuh played a pivotal role in destroying the fake [[Unidentified settlement (Agbui)|Agbui nyix mine]] on [[Hoxim]] during the [[Hoxim incident]], thus preventing a [[Jixtus's plan|scheme to cause a civil war]] among the Chiss from coming to fruition. Guided by [[sky-walker]] [[Che'ri]]'s powers of [[Precognition|Third Sight]], Afpriuh aimed the weapons of the ''Springhawk'' in such a way to destroy shoot the ''[[Saltbarrel]]'' at the "[[Mining|mine]]" and destroy it and make it look accidental. As a result, the rival [[Great Families]] that sought to seize the supposed [[nyix]] mine could not fight over it.<ref name="Greater Good" />
