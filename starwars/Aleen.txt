'''Aleen''' was a rocky [[planet]] located within the [[Bright Jewel sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. It was home to the diminutive, terrestrial [[Aleena]] [[species]], and the [[Kindalo]], a bioluminescent, [[tree]]-like species. At one point in history, the two species [[Aleena–Kindalo war|clashed]] and went to war, causing the extinction of most [[Sentience|sentient]] life on Aleen. After that war, however, the two species [[Aleena–Kindalo treaty|agreed]] to live separately and had a [[Great Seal|seal]] created to keep the surface-dwelling Aleena apart from the subterranean Kindalo, as well as separate the two [[atmosphere]]s of the planet.

During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Mission to Aleen|arrived]] to help the Aleena rebuild after a series of devastating [[groundquake]]s, during which the [[droid]]s [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] encountered both sides of the Aleen conflict.

==Description==
[[File:Aleen_underworld_seal.png|thumb|right|250px|The Jedi chapter house]]
From [[Realspace|space]], Aleen appeared as a rocky, [[Color|brown]], [[desert]] planet,<ref name="BMF 56" /> and possessed a variety of terrains such as mesas, deserts, shallow seas, and isolated [[forest]]s.<ref name="NoP" /> Its surface was dominated by [[arbozoic tree]]s, found close to settlements with their roots hanging over the edges of the mesas.<ref name="BMF 56" /> Dead arbozoic trees were at one point in the planet's history hollowed out to create the "Aleen colossi," which remain important in Aleena culture.<ref name="NoP" />

Aleen's uniqueness stemmed from the series of complex caverns located in its upper crust, considered to be part of a distinct subterranean biome with its own [[atmosphere]] separate from that of the surface and not breathable to most [[Sentience|sentient]] species. Formed by the growth of the arbozoic [[tree]]s, the [[cave]]rns were built-up of tunnels carved by [[water]], [[Magma|lava]], and tectonic shearing. Fostering a separate climate, and an atmosphere that was toxic to the surface-dwelling Aleena, the planet's two sentient species consciously decided to recognize the divide between the two biomes and remained separated. <ref name="NoP" />

==History==
===Early history===
The [[homeworld]] of both the Aleena and the Kindalo species, Aleen was a world of two distinct environments that had a shared yet separate history. The [[High Human|human-centric]] [[scholar]]ship of the [[Galactic Empire]] knew that the Aleena, and possibly the Kindalo, were client species of the [[Infinite Empire|Rakatan Infinite Empire]], though there was little evidence of any occupation. Establishing a strong culture and a fierce army, the Aleena built towering monoliths that were vital to the religious orders that formed on the planet in the earliest days of the [[Galactic Republic]], and long before the galactic community had discovered the world. Undergoing an expansionist era, the Aleena settled other worlds and spread into the [[The galaxy|greater]] [[galaxy]], yet many remained on Aleen. Many of the Aleena found throughout the galaxy traced their ancestry to [[Aleen Minor]] rather than Aleen.<ref name="NoP" />

===Conflict then peace===
Some years later, the Aleena came into conflict with the Kindalo that lived beneath the surface of the world, and the two cultures waged a [[Aleena–Kindalo war|terrible war]] that greatly damaged both societies through the use of technological weapons. When the casualties grew to threaten the existence of life on Aleen, a [[Aleena–Kindalo treaty|compact]] was formed that secured the notion that neither society would interfere with the other again. To this end, the [[Great Seal]] was forged to serve as a reminder of the compact, and was placed within a primary tunnel opening that allowed access to the [[Aleen Underworld|Underworld]], where the Kindalo resided.<ref name="NoP" />

While the Great Seal was more symbolic then any actual barrier to the Underworld, as multiple tunnels and caverns were spread throughout the upper crust of the planet, it was a revered site among the Aleena. Vowing to leave behind the society that caused the near apocalypse on Aleen, the Aleena entered an era of self-imposed seclusion and scaled back development considerably. Aleena families formed together into kinship clades known as [[tahiko]]s. The individual settlements across the planet maintained contact with a [[Aleena monarchy|planetary government]] that advised the [[King of Aleen]], a ceremonial role usually held by distinguished [[priest]]s. The Kindalo in turn kept to a sort of anarchic consensus form of government.<ref name="NoP" />

===Rediscovery===
Over the course of the [[Millennium|millennia]], [[Duros]] [[spacer]]s eventually rediscovered Aleen and introduced the [[Jedi Order]] to the world. Establishing a [[Jedi chapter house (Aleen)|chapter house]] over top of the Great Seal, the [[Jedi]] protected the world for a [[time]], before abandoning their chapter house and allowing it to fall into disrepair.<ref name="NoP" />

===Attack by the Nihil===
In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Aleen was [[Attack on Aleen|attacked]] by the [[Nihil]] as a distraction for the Jedi and Republic.<ref name="FallenStar">''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]''</ref>

===Clone Wars===
During the [[Clone Wars]], Aleen was represented in the [[Galactic Senate]] by [[Bogg Tyrell]],<ref name="BMF 56" /> and the [[King of Aleen]] was [[Manchucho]]. During the war, a series of [[groundquake]]s led the Republic to send a [[Mission to Aleen|humanitarian mission]], when the Great Seal became dislodged and the two atmospheres began to intermix.<ref name="MM" /> Though King Manchucho knew some details of the "gods below,"<ref name="BMF 56" /> knowledge of the underworld was scant during this time and the two droids [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] ended up traveling into the underworld and recorded a mysterious being called [[Orphne]] and the presence of the Kindalo. Despite their experiences meeting these species, the identity of the Kindalo was not known and their mission was fulfilled when the Great Seal was returned to its original state.<ref name="MM" />

When the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|came to power]] at the end of the war, the Empire established an [[Trohlu observation post|observation post]] and [[Trohlu listening station|listening station]] on Aleen's lone [[moon]], [[Trohlu]].<ref name="NoP" /> The [[Stormtrooper Corps]] once carried out a [[mission on Aleen]], in which a [[Aleena village|village]] of Aleena was attacked. The attack led to three [[stormtrooper]]s deserting and fleeing to [[Nothoiin]] with an orphaned [[Unidentified Aleena child (Nothoiin)|Aleena child]].<ref name="SWA 22" /> The Imperial General [[Octavion Sorin]] once carried out weapons tests at a compound on Aleen.<ref name="Legends">''[[Legends of the Alliance]]''</ref>
