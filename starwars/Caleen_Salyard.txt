The '''Caleen Salyard''',<ref name="Kassa Trivia Guide">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e1-trivia-gallery|text="Kassa" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> also known as '''Caleen Scrappers''',<ref name="Lando">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lando Calrissian and Other Characters From the Galaxy}}</ref> was a [[salvage yard]] run by the family of [[Bix Caleen]] on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]]. Following the [[death]] of her [[parent]]s, the salvage yard was run by Caleen.<ref name="ProductionBrief" /> Her lover, [[Timm Karlo]], worked alongside her<ref name="Kassa Trivia Guide" /> as a floor manager.<ref name="Timm DB">{{Databank|timm-karlo|Timm Karlo}}</ref> The [[Aurebesh]] lettering on the main building read "SALYARD. ION CONVERSION NODES AND COMPONENTS OF ALL VARIETY."<ref name="Kassa Trivia Guide" /> Another building said "CALEEN." [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] came there to sell an untraceable [[Imperial N-S9 Starpath Unit]] to Caleen, who was also a [[black market]] dealer.<ref name="Andor 1" />
