'''Albin Johnson''' is the founder of the [[501st Legion (fan organization)|501st Legion]], a ''[[Star Wars]]'' costuming group. After his daughter [[Katie Johnson|Katie]] was diagnosed with cancer in [[2005]], Johnson and the [[R2-D2 Builders Club]] created [[R2-KT]] to spread awareness of pediatric illness.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/contributor/ajohnson|text=Albin Johnson}}</ref>
