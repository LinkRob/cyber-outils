The '''Dulok Rifle''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This ornate [[blaster]] [[rifle]] featured a [[Color|black]], weathered frame covered in green, yellow, and red markings. Attached to the rifle were also a spiky, [[club]]-like nob for a stock, what appeared to be part of a [[Bone|spine]] over the scope, a small creature skull over the barrel, and green cloth bound around the grip.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dulok Rifle was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.18.0 within the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dulok Warfare]].<ref name="Hunters" />
