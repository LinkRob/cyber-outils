The '''Dupei''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. A group of [[Dupei monks]] resided on [[Nameel]], and stole [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from a nearby [[Trandoshan]] [[Vurrsk's tribe|tribe]] who tried to buy outlawed weapons from them. The [[Jedi]] [[Eno Cordova]] and [[Cere Junda]] were sent to Nameel to restore peace, and Cordova spoke with the Dupei, learning of their [[Thief|theft]].<ref name="Dark Temple 1" />
