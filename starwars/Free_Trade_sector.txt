The '''Free Trade sector''',<ref name="Andor1" /> also known as the '''Morlana sector''',<ref name="Aldhani" /> '''Preox-Morlana Corporate Sector''',<ref name="Star Wars Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> or the '''Morlana One Sector''',<ref name="Narkina 5">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}}</ref> itself a [[Sector|subsector]] of the [[Xappyh sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], was a region of space under the control of [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]]. It contained the [[Morlani system]], which was itself home to the [[desert]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Andor1" /> The [[Galactic Empire]] used the [[ambush on Ferrix]] as a justification to [[Takeover of the Preox-Morlana Corporate Sector|take control]] of Preox's Morlana's territory.<ref name="Aldhani" /> After initially falling as part of [[Lieutenant]] [[Blevin]]'s territory, the sector was placed under Lieutenant [[Dedra Meero]]'s supervision by [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]] as a reward for her due diligence and its importance to her investigation into the [[early rebellion against the Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Announcement">{{Andor|Announcement}}</ref>
