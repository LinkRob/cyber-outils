"'''Chewie and Ewoks - Hijacking a Walker'''" is the thirty-first episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com]] website and YouTube on [[June 7]], [[2019]].<ref name="Chewie and Ewoks" /><ref name="Star Wars Kids">{{Disney|url=star-wars-kids|subdomain=lol|text=Take your first step into a larger world. Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures arrives here at Star Wars Kids on November 30!|date=2020-02-25}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
During the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST walkers]] drive the [[Ewok]]s into [[Endor]]'s forest, destroying trees and causing a forest fire. The narrator explains that in a [[The galaxy|"galaxy of adventures"]], [[Chewbacca]] finds friends to take out a common enemy. Together with the [[Ewok]]s [[Widdle Warrick]] and [[Wunka]], they swing on a rope and land on top of an AT-ST walker.

The Ewoks distract the [[AT-ST driver]]s [[Major]] [[Newland]] and [[Lieutenant]] [[Blanaid]] by blocking the walker's viewports. When Lieutenant Blanaid climbs out of the hatch to investigate, Chewie grabs him and throws him down into the forest. Wicket and Wunka subdue Major Newland before hurling him out of a viewport. Chewbacca and the Ewoks then take over the walker. Wunka struggles with the controls, prompting Chewbacca to roar and take over the walker.

The three approach another AT-ST walker that is chasing Ewoks from behind and blast its head off. The three then attack several [[stormtrooper]]s and [[scout trooper]]s, turning the tables on the Imperial pursuers. Chewbacca brings the walker to the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star's]] [[Death Star's shield generator|shield generator]], where [[Han Solo]], [[Prince]]ss [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], [[C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2]] are holed up. Han initially raises his hands but is relieved to see Chewbacca and his Ewok allies.
