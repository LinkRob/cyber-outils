'''Dana Friedrich''' is a [[Wikipedia:German|German]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]]. Friedrich played [[Minister]] [[Maketh Tua]] in the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] which adapted a number of episodes of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated television series.<ref name="German Rebels" />
