'''Ekiya''', occasionally nicknamed '''Kiya''', was a [[Gender|female]] individual from the [[planet]] [[Rei'izu]]. When she was fourteen [[Standard year|years]] old, the [[Sith]] conquered her [[homeworld]] and she was conscripted to serve as a trooper in the [[Sith rebellion]]; she would later say she must have looked strong enough to fight and smart enough not to fight the Sith. She was part of a group of ten conscripts that included [[Haba]], [[Kabeji]], [[Sae]], [[Shogo]], and [[Unsuke]]. One [[Standard week|week]] after they had been shipped offworld, the leader of the rebellion&mdash;the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] who would later be known as [[the Ronin]]&mdash;betrayed his fellow Sith. As the rebellion collapsed, Rei'izu was somehow taken with it, leaving empty [[Realspace|space]] where the world had once been. The [[Unidentified Sith commander (Sith rebellion)|Sith commander]] giving orders to Ekiya and her group wanted them to make a last stand against the [[Jedi]] and the [[Empire (Rei'izu)|Empire]]. Instead, they conspired to [[Death|kill]] the commander to free themselves. They were successful, but only six survived the uprising.<ref name="Ronin" />

With Rei'izu missing, Ekiya and other beings from that world became refugees. Some [[time]] after the rebellion, Ekiya became the [[pilot]] of a [[starship]] named the ''[[Poor Crow]]''. Over the years, she collected [[relic]]s from fellow Rei'izu refugees. Within the relics were [[kyber crystal]]s that were believed to contain the [[Soul|ghosts]] of the dead, and they were meant to be brought to a temple on Rei'izu; with their world missing, it became impossible to send the ghosts home. Ekiya was entrusted with delivering the relics someday if Rei'izu could be found.<ref name="Ronin" />

As an adult, Ekiya had a round face, thick [[Color|black]] [[hair]], and many colorful [[Flower|floral]] [[tattoo]]s on her muscular [[Limb|arms]].<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Ekiya is someone who is very much in that frame of 'I need to eat breakfast, and so do you,' and that's how she copes with being alive in this world that can't really be trusted all the time.|Emma Mieko Candon|<ref name="RebelLegion">{{OfficialYouTube|video=Rg3fw6vMfcs|text=08 Emma Candon Interview Trimmed with Sub mkv|channel=@RebelLegionOfficial|name=Rebel Legion|date=2022-05-11}}</ref>}}
Ekiya appeared in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" /> Like all names in the novel, Ekiya's name is comprised of [[Wikipedia:Phoneme|phonemes]] used in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]].<ref name="Phonemes">{{Twitter|username=@EmmaCandon|url=/status/1442659690144354306|name=[[Emma Mieko Candon]]|date=2021-09-27|quote=Yup! Every name in Ronin uses Japanese phonemes.|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/51Gsj}}</ref> According to Candon, Ekiya was partially inspired by their own childhood experience as a gender-nonconforming ''[[Star Wars]]'' fan, drawn to pilot characters and ordinary individuals who held groups together through compassion. As an adult, Candon associated queerness with this archetype and further drew inspiration for Ekiya from queer friends who sought to provide safety, comfort, and happiness for their chosen communities. Candon described Ekiya as a "butch-of-center force for good" who prioritizes action over words and often forgets that she herself deserves care and happiness.<ref name="Newsletter2">{{WebCite|url=https://buttondown.com/emcandon/archive/entry-2-star-wars-but-make-it-gay|text=Entry 2: Star Wars, but Make It Gay|author=[[Emma Mieko Candon]]|date=2021-11-09|work=E.M. Candon Writes Good|archivedate=20250922130404}}</ref> Candon sees Ekiya having an "anachronistic aesthetic," thematically [[Wikipedia:cyberpunk|cyberpunk]], with short hair and colorful tattoos. Inspiration for the character came from [[Wikipedia:Edo period|Edo period]] firefighters, popularized in the film [[Wikipedia:Promare|''Promare'']] sometime before the book was written. The name "Ekiya" was created as a recombined version of a name<ref name="InspirationsThread">{{Twitter|username=EmmaCandon|url=status/1455604994845990912|name=[[Emma Mieko Candon]]|date=2021-11-03|quote=Ekiya has the most anachronistic aesthetic, an almost cyberpunk air with short hair and colorful tattoos—though for her I was thinking of the Edo firefighters, recently popularized in Promare. These days, tattoos carry certain social stigma in Japan for their association with yakuza, but for Ekiya the resonance is her membership with a tight-knit lower class group fiercely protecting their communities. [...] For a few characters, I took [[Tom Hoeler|@darthinternous]]'s advice and ran part of a friend or loved one's name through the wash a few times to make: Kouru, Ekiya, Idzuna…|archivedate=20220628114639}}</ref>  of a friend Candon's, who she believes has a compassionate, [[Han Solo]]-like personality.<ref name="Podralorian">{{WebCite|url=https://notsafforwork.com/podcasts/the-podralorian-playing-gay-chicken-with-lucasfilm-with-emma-mieko-candon|text=The Podralorian: Playing Gay Chicken with Lucasfilm with Emma Mieko Candon|date=2021-11-13|work=Not Saf For Work|archivedate=20250929211952}}</ref>

Candon also stated that Ekiya was intended from the beginning to serve as Kouru's primary point of connection to the other characters in the novel. She noted that while she did not initially anticipate the chemistry that developed between the two characters, Ekiya's grounded nature complemented Kouru's intense personality, and Kouru's willingness to devote herself to Ekiya provided a form of connection that Ekiya deserved at that point in her life.<ref name="Newsletter2" /> Their relationship has been described by the author as "pre-romantic," as she didn't feel it was a justified romantic arc by the end of the book.<ref name="RebelLegion" />

Ekiya's emotional return to Rei'izu were based on the author's painful feeling of disconnect when visiting [[Wikipedia:Kyoto|Kyoto]] in [[Wikipedia:Japan|Japan]] for the first time with her mother,<ref name="RuPalp">{{YouTube|video=m5hk1-6u5LY|text=What Does the Fox Say (feat. Emma Mieko Candon): Ep. 22|channel=@RuPalpsPodrace|name=RuPalp's Podrace: A Queer Star Wars Podcast|date=2024-01-11}}</ref> the author being a [[Wikipedia:Yonsei (Japanese diaspora)|fourth-generation]] [[Wikipedia:Japanese Americans|Japanese American]],<ref name="Ronin" /> and her mother being the daughter of a Japanese teacher with a Japanese translator, but who wasn't able to learn the language due to the [[Wikipedia:Internment of Japanese Americans|internment of Japanese Americans]] during [[Wikipedia:World War II|World War II]].<ref name="RuPalp" />
