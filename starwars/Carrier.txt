A '''carrier''' was a type of military [[starship]] specifically designed to focus on transporting smaller starships. The [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|''Quasar Fire''-class cruiser-carrier]] was one type of carrier used by both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Phoenix Cell]],<ref name="Homecoming">{{Rebels|Homecoming}}</ref> as was the [[Imperial escort carrier]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> This carrier featured two light [[turbolaser]] batteries at the forward part of the ship.
