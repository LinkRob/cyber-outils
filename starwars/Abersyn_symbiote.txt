'''Abersyn symbiotes''' were a [[species]] which fed on the [[brain]] stem of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. They had to be fed into a [[plasma]] furnace in order to keep them in a quiescent state, though a regular exposure to a plasma vent was proven to have the same effect on them. They were known for being responsible for the [[death]] of many historical empires; therefore, having a specimen was banned by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] decree.

==History==
[[File:NewKtathatnQueen-2016DoctorAphra8.jpg|thumb|left|200x200px|Unidentified Queen of Ktath'atn]]
During her [[time]] as a [[doctor]]al student in the [[University of Bar'leth]], [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] was studying and [[Archaeology|exploring]] the [[planet]] of [[Boothi XII]] looking for an incredible discovery. After finding nothing on Boothi XII, [[Toob-Nix]], Aphra's doctoral supervisor, showed her a full nest of Abersyn symbiotes in his personal chamber, telling her why he hated her so much and that he would never give her his approval to get the doctorate. As revenge, Aphra stole the whole nest with the help of [[Sana Starros]] and made it appear as if she had finally found the Abersyn symbiotes on Boothi XII. As a part of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] protocol, the symbiotes were disintegrated right away.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

On [[Ktath'atn]], the [[Unidentified Queen of Ktath'atn|Queen of the planet]] was an entity of Abersyn symbiote who has possessed a [[humanoid]] through [[Force drain]] and kept several members of her species that she used to control her collection. She attempted to use them on Aphra and [[Luke Skywalker]] as a feigned "dessert", but proved unsuccessful when Aphra realized the trap and escaped with the [[Padawan]].<ref name="Star Wars 31">[[Star Wars (2015) 31|''Star Wars'' (2015) 31]]</ref> Aphra later found a large colony of the symbiotes and [[0-0-0]] directly implanted one in [[Krrsantan]] so he would go berserk and attack the Queen's followers.<ref name="Doc7">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 7|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 7]]</ref>
