'''Cere Junda's lightsaber'''<ref name="Cal's ligthsaber Databank">{{Databank|cals-lightsaber|Cal's lightsaber}}</ref> was a [[Color|green]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] originally owned by [[Cere Junda]]. Sometime after escaping [[torture]], she removed the [[kyber crystal]] and sold it to help pay off [[Greez Dritus]]' debts with the [[Haxion Brood]], but kept the hilt. She later [[Mission to Ilum|gave]] it to [[Cal Kestis]] on [[Ilum]] for him to create a [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|new]] [[lightsaber]] with its parts.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> During the five years separated from Cal, Cere created a [[Cere Junda's second lightsaber|new lightsaber]] which produced a blue blade.<ref name="Jedi Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>
