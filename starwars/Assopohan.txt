'''Assopohans''' were one of the two variants of the [[Assorhian]]s, the others being the [[Ossapohan]]s. The race of each Assorhian determined their position in society: the black-skinned Ossapohans were the ruling caste, while the white-skinned Asoppohans were the [[Slavery/Legends|slave]] caste.<ref name="Casus86" />
