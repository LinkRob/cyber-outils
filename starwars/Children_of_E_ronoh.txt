The '''Children of E'ronoh''' were an extremist group that believed that their [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]] should not make peace with [[Eiram]] in the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], and instead continue to try and destroy the [[Eirami]].<ref name="Convergence" /> Active in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> they believed that [[Prince]]ess [[Xiri A'lbaran]] and subsequently her father [[Monarchy|Monarch]] [[A'lbaran]] of E'ronoh were traitors who had betrayed E'ronoh to Eiram and had been corrupted by them. They were viciously hateful of the Eirami, and even attacked Eiram's [[Queen]] [[Adrialla]] in their series of attacks that they attempted to use to pursue their aims.<ref name="Convergence" />
