'''4E''', nicknamed '''Foree''', was a conical [[astromech droid]] with the emblem of [[Alphabet Squadron]] on its chassis that served the [[New Republic]] toward the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The astromech droid was assigned to the [[T-70 X-wing starfighter]] flown by [[Yrica Quell]], former [[New Republic pilot]] of Alphabet Squadron, when she returned to the New Republic from within the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Concurrent with the [[Battle of Jakku]]<ref name="Victory's Price" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> 4E flew with Quell when she and [[Kairos]] undertook a [[Attack on Coruscant's Verity District|mission]] to the [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] in order to prevent [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] of the 204th from destroying the secret [[databank]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] that was utilized to select participants for [[Operation: Cinder]]. After the end of the war following the Battle of Jakku, Quell kept 4E while residing on [[Spirana]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
