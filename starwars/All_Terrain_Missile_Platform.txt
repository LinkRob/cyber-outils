The '''All Terrain Missile Platform'''('''AT-MP'''), was a bipedal [[walker]] model used by the [[Imperial Army|ground forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:ATMP.png|thumb|left|150px|An AT-MP]]
The All Terrain Missile Platform was a [[walker]] used by the [[Imperial Army]]. Its general appearance was similar to that of the [[All Terrain Scout Transport]], but it was fitted with "arms" acting as [[missile launcher]]s and a chin-mounted rotary [[laser cannon]]. While the AT-MP's salvos of missiles could take down almost any target, the walker itself was nowhere as agile as its AT-ST cousin. Because of that, it could be easily defeated by infantry and volley of fire tactics. It had a more heavily-armored, more powerful variant with a darker color scheme called the [[AT-MP Mark III]].<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" /> Another version of the AT-MP had dark armor plates, a rotating [[laser cannon]], and four missile launchers on each arm rather than six.<ref name="Rebels" />

==History==
The walker was used about five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]].<ref name="Rebels" /> One was stolen by the [[Spectres|young rebel]] [[Ezra Bridger]] at an Imperial installation while he was rescuing an [[Ithorian]] [[Lothalite]] named [[Dorthu Santim]] who was forced to work there by [[stormtrooper]]s. Later, shortly after the Battle of Yavin, the Empire deployed several AT-MPs to the [[desert]] planet [[Tatooine]] for use during their [[Battle for Tatooine|campaign]] there.<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />

==Behind the scenes==
The All Terrain Missile Platform was first introduced in the now-[[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] game ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]''.<ref name="TFUIIvg">''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]''</ref> Although it appears in [[canon]], its appearances differ in the media it appears in: in ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', though the basic design is the same as the original, the walker has twelve rocket tubes (six on each side) and no chin-mounted cannon,<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" /> whereas in ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Recon Missions]]'', it has eight missile tubes and has the chin-mounted cannon<ref name="Rebels" /> as in ''Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II''.<ref name="TFUIIvg" />
