A '''bhillen wrap''' was a type of wrap that could be found on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]].<ref name="Vengeance" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The bhillen wrap's mention in ''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]'' takes place concurrently to the [[Battle of Jedha]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> when visiting a [[Werth Plouth|prisoner]] in the [[Temple of the Kyber]] on Jedha, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Matthea Cathley]] offered to bring the guarding [[Guardians of the Whills]] a bhillen wrap or [[salad]] if they were interested. Her offer fell on deaf [[ear]]s.<ref name="Vengeance" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bhillen wraps were mentioned in [[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance|''Path of Vengeance'']], a novel written by [[Cavan Scott]] in [[2023]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia series'<ref name="Vengeance" /> [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Phase II">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2023}}</ref> [[Bhillen]] creatures were also associated with [[Foodstuff/Legends|food]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="SD">{{JournalCite|9|Slaying Dragons}}</ref>
