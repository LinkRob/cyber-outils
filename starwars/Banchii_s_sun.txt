A '''sun''' was part of the [[Inugg system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and was [[orbit]]ed by the [[planet]]s [[Banchii]] and [[Hon-Tallos]]. In [[228 BBY]], after the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Mission to Banchii|attacked]] Banchii, [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] took the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Nima Allets]] and [[Viv'Nia Nia'Viv]] to safety and explained why they were not ready to help by comparing the nutrients the [[Star|sun]] provided to [[wheat]] with the experience the [[youngling]]s needed. Later, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Arkoff]] watched the sunrise together after another Nihil [[Battle on Banchii|attack]].

==Description==
A [[Star|sun]]<ref name="Premonition" /> was part of the [[Jjannex sector]]'s [[Inugg system]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> and was [[orbit]]ed by the [[planet]]s<ref name="Encyclopedia" /><ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[Banchii]]<ref name="Premonition" /> and [[Hon-Tallos]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The sun was located in an intersecting portion of the [[Western Reaches]]<ref name="Western Reaches" /> and the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It was situated in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Map" /> The sun provided nutrients to [[wheat]] [[Farm|cultivated]] on Banchii.<ref name="Premonition" /> 

==History==
[[File:LilySunrise-THREOB4.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Lily Tora-Asi watches the sunrise.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Galactic Republic]] designated a section of the Outer Rim<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref>&mdash;which included Banchii, and therefore its sun<ref name="Lightsaber collection" />&mdash;as the [[Galactic Frontier]], locations considered far removed enough from the galaxy's center to send [[Scout|explorers]] to.<ref name="Chronicles" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|EOBP}}</ref> after the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] [[Mission to Banchii|attacked]] Banchii, [[Jedi]] [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] took the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Nima Allets]] and [[Viv'Nia Nia'Viv]] to safety. When the [[youngling]]s said they wanted to help, Yoda asked them what happened if the wheat they cultivated was harvested too soon. Allets responded it was useless because it did not have enough nutrients from the sun and soil, and Yoda explained that just as the wheat needed nutrients, the younglings needed experience.<ref name="Premonition" /> 

After repelling another Nihil [[Battle on Banchii|attack]] on Banchii soon after, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Lily Tora-Asi]] watched the sunrise. She was joined by [[Jedi Master]] [[Arkoff]], and the two reflected on recent events and their plans for the future.<ref name="Edge of Balance 4">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 4|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 4]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Banchii's sun first appeared in the [[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 1|first volume]] of the [[Wikipedia:Manga|manga]] series ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: The Edge of Balance]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and [[Shima Shinya]], illustrated by [[Mizuki Sakakibara]], and published by [[Viz Media]]<ref name="Edge of Balance 1">[[The High Republic: The Edge of Balance, Vol. 1|''The High Republic: The Edge of Balance'', Vol. 1]]</ref> on [[September 7]], [[2021]], as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
