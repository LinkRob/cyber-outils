'''Eric Johnston''' worked as lead programmer on [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s.

==Biography==
Johnston majored in electrical engineering and computer science at [[Wikipedia:University of California, Berkeley|University of California, Berkeley]]; upon graduation he worked at the NASA [[Wikipedia:Ames Research Center|Ames Research Center]] on graphics computers and robotic systems, designing and testing uses for robotics like [[Wikipedia:Teleoperation|teleoperation]] through helmet and gloves.<ref name="shadows">''[[The Secrets of Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]''</ref>

He worked for [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (video game)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'' video game]], released for [[Wikipedia:Nintendo 64|Nintendo 64]] and [[Wikipedia:Personal computer|PC]] in [[1996]] and [[1997]], respectively.

Later, he was one of the senior engineers of the development team for the [[Ronin (engine)|Ronin Engine]].<ref>''[[The Art and Making of Star Wars: The Force Unleashed]]''</ref>
