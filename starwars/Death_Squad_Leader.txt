The '''Death Squad Leader''' was the [[commander]] of the [[Death Squad (squad)|Death Squad]] of elite [[death trooper]]s. He was equipped with a modified [[E-11D blaster carbine]] which shot explosive bolts. He was also equipped with multiple [[C-25 fragmentation grenade]]s.<ref name="FA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Death Squad Leader is a character that appears in ''[[Star Wars: Force Arena]]'', a mobile PvP game developed by [[NetMarble]].
