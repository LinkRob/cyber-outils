'''Exantor Divo''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] that served as a [[Law enforcement agency|police]] [[commissioner]] on [[Hosnian Prime]].

==Biography==
Exantor Divo was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] and the son of [[Andressa Divo]], and the grandchild of [[Tanivos Exantor Divo]] and came from a long [[Divo family|line]] of law enforcement personnel. Upon his graduation from the [[Hosnian Prime Academy of Law Enforcement]] in [[19 ABY]], he intended to become a [[Sector Ranger]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" />

By [[34 ABY]], he was the [[New Republic]] [[Law enforcement agency|police]] [[commissioner]] on [[Hosnian Prime]] and lived on the [[planet]] with his [[Exantor Divo's partner|partner]] and two children. At that time, he compiled case files from his [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|grandfather]], mother, and his own career into a volume.<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" />
