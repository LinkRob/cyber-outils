'''Ar'Ven Vence''' was an [[Alderaanian]] [[architect]] who lived long before the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]]. She created many of the most iconic buildings in her native [[city]] of [[Aldera]], designing them as spears to emulate the shape of the [[Aldera Alps]]. Even after her death, many of Aldera's buildings would mimic her style.<ref name="Dawn" />
