'''Flitterpods''' were striped, [[Color|purple]] [[fish]] with [[Limb|fins]] that could be found in the [[Unidentified river (Tenoo)|river]] of the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Tenoo]]. Around [[233 BBY]], the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]] and [[Lys Solay]] traveled down the [[Water|river]] and noticed the creatures, with the latter becoming distracted by them and almost crashing her [[speeder]]. In other instances, a flitterpod [[Thief|took]] the [[Jedi]] [[youngling]] [[Nubs]]' [[Nubs's lightsaber|lightsaber]], and the [[droid]] [[RJ-83]] encountered a pair of the fish while in Tenoo's river. In [[232 BBY]], the Jedi younglings [[Linh]], Nubs, and Brightstar saw several flitterpods jumping out of the water.

==Description==
{{Listen3|Flitterpod-FWN.flac|a flitterpod chirping}}
Flitterpods were [[fish]]<ref name="Audio" /> found in the [[Unidentified river (Tenoo)|river]] of the [[forest]]<ref name="Get Well Nubs" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Tenoo]]. They had [[Color|black]] [[eye]]s, purple skin with stripes, a large pink-purple dorsal fin on their backs, and a thrice-forked tail. Flitterpods also had barbels, two pink-purple pectoral fins, and narrow snouts with nostrils at their base.<ref name="Get Well Nubs" /> The fish could make chittering sounds.<ref name="NubsLightsaber">{{FunWithNubs|Nubs Loses his Lightsaber}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Lys! Watch out!''"<br />"''Huh?|Kai Brightstar and Lys Solay, after the latter was distracted by flitterpods|audio=Kai-Lys-WatchOut-GWN.flac|<ref name="Get Well Nubs" />}}
Around [[233 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{YJADate|1}}</ref> the [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]] [[Kai Brightstar]] and [[Lys Solay]] traveled down Tenoo's [[Water|river]] on [[speeder]]s alongside a group of flitterpods. Solay, who had heard of the creatures but was unaware they lived in the area, became distracted while observing them flit in and out of the water, and<ref name="Get Well Nubs" /> she<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> narrowly avoided crashing into a [[tree]] root thanks to a warning from Brightstar.<ref name="Get Well Nubs" /> Later around the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Brightstar and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Bell Zettifar]] passed by some flitterpods swimming in a stream within one of Tenoo's forests.<ref name="Charhound Chase">{{YJA|Charhound Chase}}</ref>

[[File:FlitterpodStealingLightsaber-FWN.png|thumb|right|300px|A flitterpod took Nubs's lightsaber and swam down a waterfall.]]
Also around that time,<ref name="Nubs Date">{{YJADate|FWN}}</ref> the Jedi [[youngling]] [[Nubs]] tripped and flung his [[Nubs's lightsaber|lightsaber]] onto a rock within Tenoo's river. When he tried to retrieve it [[Telekinesis|using]] [[the Force]], a flitterpod leaped out of the water and [[Thief|took]] the [[Lightsaber|weapon]] in its [[mouth]], chittering happily. Nubs called out in alarm and chased the flitterpod as it swam down the river and over a waterfall before eventually swinging over to the creature using a [[vine]] to take his [[Weapon|lightsaber]] back.<ref name="NubsLightsaber" /> On another occasion, the [[RJ unit]] [[droid]] [[RJ-83]] dropped a [[starship]] tool into the river. When<ref name="Swim">{{FunWithNubs|RJ Goes For A Swim}}</ref> he<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> dove into the river to retrieve it, a pair of flitterpods bounced him between them as the tool sunk deeper. Eventually, RJ-83 managed to retrieve the tool and bring it to the surface, only to drop it in the water again almost immediately afterward.<ref name="Swim" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date2">{{YJADate|206B}}</ref> the Jedi younglings [[Linh]], Nubs, and Brightstar traversed through the [[Tenoo tree forest]]. On their way to the site where the [[barelia flower]] on Tenoo was expected to bloom, they passed by a waterfall where several flitterpods were chittering happily as they leaped out of the water.<ref name="Uprooted">{{YJA|Uprooted}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Flitterpods first appeared, and were identified, in "[[Get Well Nubs]]," the first part of the [[Get Well Nubs / The Junk Giant|third episode]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''{{'s}} [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]].<ref name="Get Well Nubs" /> The episode was released on [[May 4]], [[2023]].<ref name="D23 May">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-may-2023|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in May 2023}}</ref> Prior to the episode's release, the creatures were included, albeit unnamed, in a trailer for the series released on [[April 10]].<ref name="Trailer">{{SWKidsYouTube|otoDnun24zM|Young Jedi Adventures &#124; Official Trailer}}</ref>
