'''''A More Wretched Hive: The Mos Eisley Cantina''''' is a children's "scratch and sniff" book published by [[Golden Books]] in [[August]] [[1997]] and written by [[Margaret Snyder]].<ref name="Copyright" /> It retells [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker's]] visit to the [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina/Legends|Mos Eisley Cantina]].

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''Everyone knows that the Mos Eisley Cantina is a "wretched hive of scum and villainy." And as kids read about Luke Skywalker's and Obi-Wan Kenobi's adventures within its dingy walls, they can get a real whiff of the danger that lurks there.''<ref name="Amazon" />
