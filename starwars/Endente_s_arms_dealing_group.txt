A '''Sugi arms dealing group''' operated on [[Utapau]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="ISotC" /> a [[The galaxy|galaxy-wide]] conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

The group composed entirely of [[Sugi]]s, all of whom [[Clothing|donned]] [[cybernetics]] in the form of a<ref name="ISotC" /> "[[Power armor|powersuit]]"<ref name="Sugi DB">{{Databank|sugi-species|Sugi (Species)}}</ref> with the exception of the arms dealers' leader, [[Endente]].<ref name="Endente DB">{{Databank|endente|Endente}}</ref> The leader and his ranking henchman, [[Chong]], were the only Sugi arms dealers who spoke and understood<ref name="ISotC" /> [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="CE UE">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>

The [[arms dealer]]s possessed a giant [[kyber crystal]] which they stored in their [[Sugi arms dealers' ship|ship]] around the [[time]] of [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s departure from the [[Jedi Order]]. The [[crystal]] was to be purchased by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the Confederacy's [[Head of State]], when the [[Jedi]] investigating the [[death]] of [[Tu-Anh]] also expressed their wishes for the crystal.<ref name="ISotC" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Sugi_species_DB.jpg|thumb|left|224px|Concept art for a member of the arms dealing group in their ship's interior]]
The arms dealing group was created for the sixth season of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', first appearing in the episode "[[A Death on Utapau]]".<ref name="ADOU" /> Although the show was canceled before the episodes aired, they were released as unfinished story reels on [[StarWars.com]] in [[2014]] as part of [[The Clone Wars Legacy|''The Clone Wars'' Legacy]] and are still considered canon.<ref name="YouTube">{{SWYouTube|vnH1JKr4o_8|Star Wars: The Clone Wars Legacy|sw_url=video/the-clone-wars-legacy}}</ref> Character design concept art of the model a male Sugi warrior was done by [[Tara Rueping]] and finished on [[March 23]], [[2012]].<ref name="Concept Gallery">{{SW|url=tv-shows/crystal-crisis-on-utapau-concept-art-gallery|text=Crystal Crisis on Utapau Concept Art Gallery|nolive=1}}</ref>
