'''Council''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Anoat sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="SWU" /> A world of rocky [[mountain]]s, it was the heart of the [[Noble Court]], the original [[Monarchy|rulers]] of the [[Nothoiin (species)|Nothoiin]] [[species]] that once controlled the whole [[sector]].<ref name="Art of SWU" /> After the [[Galactic Empire]]'s attempt to wipe out the Anoat [[Noble|nobility]],<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> the surface of Council was dotted with ruins of ancient Nothoiin [[noble]] estates.<ref name="Art of SWU" />

Council was one of the many planets cut off from the rest of the galaxy by the [[Iron Blockade]], created by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]]. When [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] operative [[Tam Bastion]] and a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] sent out a sector-wide broadcast revealing that [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] was dead, riots broke out on planets such as Council and [[Delphon]].<ref name="SWU" />
