The '''assassin droid speeder''' was a type of [[Speeder bike/Legends|Speeder bike]] used by [[IG-86 sentinel droid/Legends|IG-86 sentinel droids]] during the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].

{{Veh-stub}}

==Characteristics==
The [[Speeder bike/Legends|speeder bike]] had two seats, one for a gunner and one for a pilot. It had two long fins coming from the back and two [[Missile launcher/Legends|missile launchers]]. The droids would attach their [[Blaster/Legends|blasters]] to the bike.

==History==
One such speeder was used by a group of [[IG-86 sentinel droid/Legends|IG-86 sentinel droids]] hired by [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] to hunt down [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]] and acquire the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Republic]] secrets hidden within him.

==Behind the scenes==
In December 2008, this [[Speeder/Legends|speeder]] was released by [[The LEGO Group]] in 8015 Assassin Droids Battle Pack, and it included five [[IG-86 sentinel droid/Legends|IG-86 sentinel droids]], three silver and two black (elite). They were added to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe in the [[Canon|non-canon]] LEGO comic, "[[A clone by any other name]]."
