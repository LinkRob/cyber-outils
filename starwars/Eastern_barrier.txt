The '''eastern barrier''' was a massive wall that served as a defensive fortress on [[Geonosis]]. During the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]], [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Jedi Commander]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], and members of the [[501st Legion]] including [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] encountered the barrier while fighting their way to the primary staging area at [[Point Rain]] after their gunships had been shot down. Tano had pointed the wall out to Skywalker during a pre-battle briefing, but the [[Jedi Knight]] had dismissed the possibility that they would be going anywhere near it. When they got to the supposed "barrier" instantly two clones were gunned down.<ref name="L@PR" />

Skywalker and Tano ascended to the top of the wall carrying explosives to demolish it. After destroying all the [[battle droid]]s defending it, with cover fire from the [[clone trooper]]s below and after being joined by Rex, the two [[Jedi]] put the explosives inside the wall via a trapdoor opened by a battle droid, before using [[telekinesis]] to drop Rex off the wall and jumping off just before it exploded. Skywalker and Tano, after landing, telekinetically caught Rex before he hit the ground, and the group headed through the ruins of the eastern barrier en route to Point Rain.<ref name="L@PR" />

==Behind the scenes==
The eastern barrier first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|season 2]] episode "[[Landing at Point Rain]]," which aired in [[2009]].<ref name="L@PR" />
