The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''49 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3251 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''30 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7928''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Nim Pianna]] becomes the [[Padawan]] of [[Rael Averross]].<ref name="Pianna">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' in [[40 BBY]]. Nim Pianna's [[death]] is established to have taken place eight years prior to ''Master & Apprentice'', meaning it must have occurred in [[48 BBY]]. Pianna began training under [[Rael Averross]] one year prior to her death, which equates to 49 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
*[[Gehren Rand]] leaves the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] in search of [[Gehren's father]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 1">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-obi-wan-1-exclusive-preview|text=See a Legend's Early Days in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'' #1 – Exclusive Preview}}</ref>

==Births==
*[[Zodu Onglo]]. {{Circa}}<ref name="Zodu">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' establishes that [[Zodu Onglo]] was 23 [[Standard year|years]] old in the year [[26 BBY|7951]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] and therefore [[Reproduction|born]] around the year 7928 C.R.C.</ref>
