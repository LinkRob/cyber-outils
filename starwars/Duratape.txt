'''Duratape''' was a type of tape used to hold objects together.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 10]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the wiring of the replicated [[Nihil]] [[Path engine]] [[hyperdrive]] that [[Beol De'Rruyet]] planned to unveil the device at a<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> [[De'Rruyet Industries confluence|confluence]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 9|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 9]]</ref> on the<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 8">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 8|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 8]]</ref> [[Midarr]] was held together by duratape. After the planned event was crashed by forces of the [[Unbroken Clan]], rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] attempted to stall the leader of the Unbroken Clan forces, [[General]] [[Vukorah]], by pretending to fix the device. As Vukorah grew impatient, she asked Aphra what was taking her so long. In response, Aphra informed the general that genuis couldn't be rushed, before adding that the wiring of the hyperdrive was a mess as it was held together with duratape and wishes.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10" />
