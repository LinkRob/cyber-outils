The '''''Finalizer''''' was a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[First Order Navy|naval forces]] of the [[First Order]]. It served as the [[flagship]] of [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].

==Description==
The ''Finalizer'' was a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] and was 2,915.81 [[meter]]s, the standard length for an ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer. As such it was armed with over 1,500 [[turbolaser]]s and [[ion cannon]]s, point-defense turrets, point-defense missile emplacements and [[tractor beam projector]]s<ref name="Cross" />

==History==
===Early years===
The ''Finalizer'' was secretly built by the [[First Order]] in their "distant [[orbit]]al [[Factory|factories]]"<ref name="TFAN">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref> in the [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor|Battle]] of [[Endor]],<ref name="Episode VII" /> which was in violation of the [[Galactic Concordance]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

It was the site of the advanced training for older stormtrooper [[cadet]]s. After the [[Parnassos]] warrior [[Phasma]] was brought into the First Order inner circle by [[General]] [[Brendol Hux]], [[Captain]] [[Archex|Cardinal]] was marginalized and the training was turned over to Phasma.<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref>

===Cold War===
====Attack on Jakku====
The [[Starship|ship]] was under command of General [[Armitage Hux]] during [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren's]] [[Attack on Tuanul|assault]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] in an attempt to obtain [[Luke Skywalker]]'s hiding position from a [[Lor San Tekka]], an ally of the [[Resistance]]. When the Resistance fighter, [[Poe Dameron]], was captured during the assault, the ''Finalizer'' remained in orbit during his interrogation.<ref name="Episode VII" />

[[File:Hux_on_the_bridge.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Hux on the ''Finalizer'']]
During Dameron's [[Escape from the Finalizer|escape]], he and the deserting stormtrooper, [[Finn|FN-2187]], stole a [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]] from one of the vessel's [[hangar]]s and managed to disable the ''Finalizer''{{'s}} turbolasers. During their escape, the [[Battlecruiser]] was only able to seriously damage the [[TIE series|TIE]], without fully destroying it. It was later present when [[Starkiller Base]] [[Hosnian Cataclysm|fired]] on the [[Hosnian system]], with Ren watching the blast from the ship's [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

===First Order-Resistance War===
====Attack on the Resistance base====
The ''Finalizer'' later participated in the hunt for the Resistance fleet after D'Qar was [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuated]] shortly after Starkiller Base's [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destruction]]. It was under the command of Captain [[Edrison Peavey]] during this time due to General Hux being transferred to Supreme Leader Snoke's flagship, the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''.<ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary" /><ref name="Episode VIII" />

====Pursuing the Resistance fleet====
It later participated in the [[First Order Navy]]'s [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] on the fleeing Resistance fleet.<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="TLJ Visual Dictionary" /> With the damage sustained to the ''Supremacy'' in the battle,<ref name="Episode VIII" /> the ''Finalizer'' took over as the First Order flagship.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 1">[[Legacy of Vader 1|''Legacy of Vader'' 1]]</ref>

====Kylo Ren’s ceremony====
[[File:WheresHeGoing-LegacyOfVader1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Snoke’s remains float into space from the hangar]]
After the escape of the remaining Resistance forces from the [[Battle of Crait]], Kylo Ren held a ceremony aboard the ship officially becoming the new [[Supreme Leader]] of the First Order, using the force to lift the remains of the bisected previous leader [[Snoke]] above the heads of attending stormtroopers and blaming the Resistance for his death, finally pushing his body out of the hangar to float into space.<ref name="Legacy of Vader 1" />

====Destruction of Tah'Nuhna====
The ''Finalizer'', along with 17 other ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers and a ''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought, participated in the [[Destruction of Tah'Nuhna|destruction]] of the planet [[Tah'Nuhna]] as they had contact with Resistance members.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />

====Final action at Batuu====
Eventually, the ''Finalizer'' was [[Battle of Batuu|present at Batuu]], although after an attack left it crippled, it was retired as the main flagship of the First Order in favor of the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']].<ref name="TROS Visual Dictionary" />

==Commanders and crew==
General Hux and Captain Peavey presided over bridge operations on the ''Finalizer''. However, the ship was, in a formal sense, the personal flagship of Kylo Ren,<ref name="POV 220">{{InsiderCite|220|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> who also exerted control over operations.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Finalizer'' first appeared in the second teaser trailer for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] on [[April 16]], [[2015]].<ref name="TFA second teaser">{{SWYouTube|ngElkyQ6Rhs|Star Wars: The Force Awakens Official Teaser #2|sw_url=video/star-wars-the-force-awakens-official-teaser-2}}</ref> It was first named in a [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry posted on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 4]].<ref name="FinalizerDB" />

The [[The Rise of Skywalker Adaptation 3|third issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker Adaptation|adaptation]] of [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the ''Steadfast'' is erroneously named the "Finalizer".<ref name="TROS 3">[[The Rise of Skywalker Adaptation 3|''The Rise of Skywalker Adaptation'' 3]]</ref>

In the second episode of the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Resistance Rises]]'' television series, entitled "[[The Trouble with Rathtars]]," the ''Finalizer'' intercepted the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] who was transporting [[Rathtar]]s onboard the [[freighter]] ''[[Eravana]]''.<ref name="Trouble">{{ResistanceRises|The Trouble with Rathtars}}</ref>
