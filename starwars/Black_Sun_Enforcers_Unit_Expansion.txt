The '''Black Sun Enforcers Unit Expansion''' is a [[soldier]] expansion to ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' that adds [[Black Sun Guard]]s into the game as [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] for the [[Shadow Collective]], [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], or [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Asmodee" />

==Publisher's summary==
===Online summary===
''Reinforcements arrive to help the notorious Black Sun syndicate spread its influence across the galaxy in this expansion for Star Wars: Legion! Operating out of its fortress on Mustafar, the Black Sun makes ample use of criminal elements willing to take on any task for the right price. This expansion invites players to hire an additional six Black Sun Enforcers that they can add to their Imperial, Separatist or Shadow Collective forces as a corps unit. Additionally, they will also find a Black Sun Vigo miniature that can lead this unit or act as a commander for the entire force. Finally, three command cards give these mercenaries their own playstyle while eight upgrade cards give players the chance to customize their units with additional weapons, gear, and more!''<ref name="Asmodee" />

===Back blurb summary===
''The Black Sun is a galaxy-spanning criminal empire whose influence reaches across the sparsely patrolled Outer Rim Territories. Led by a cabal of Fallen nobles from their fortress on Mustafar, the Black Sun prefer to do most of its business through intermediary groups who have no idea of the organization's true nature. Behind the fronts, the Black Sun are an imposing force more than capable of protecting its interests. The mainstay of their forces are their Enforcers, well-trained and well-armed these foot soldiers will stop at nothing to enact the will of the Vigos that lead them.''<ref name="Black Sun Enforcers" />

''This expansion for STAR WARS: LEGION contains 6 finely sculpted Black Sun Enforcer miniatures and 1 Black Sun Vigo miniature, enough for 1 corps unit and 1 commander unit, along with new unit and upgrade cards to field in battle.''<ref name="Black Sun Enforcers" />
