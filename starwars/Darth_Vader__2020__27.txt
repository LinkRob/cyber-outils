'''''Darth Vader'' 27''' is the twenty-seventh issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series [[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2020)|''Star Wars: Darth Vader'']]. The issue was written by [[Greg Pak]], illustrated by [[Raffaele Ienco]] and [[Alex Sinclair]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 21]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''The alliance between Darth Vader and Sabé has always seemed destined for disaster. After all, how long can the Dark Lord of the Sith and the idealistic handmaiden of Padmé Amidala find common ground? So as Vader and Sabé enter the final battle against a corrupt Imperial governor, something's going to break. Will it be Vader's cold resolve or Sabé's true heart? And how long until Ochi of Bestoon finally makes his move?''

==Plot summary==
===The Death Machine approaches===
On [[Gabredor III]], [[Ochi]] and several others including [[ZED-6-7]], [[Wald]], the [[Unidentified human colony member|balding villager]] and [[stormtrooper]]s watch the [[Super Star Destroyer]] ''[[Executor]]''s{{'s}} orbital bombardment scattering the sandstorm generated by [[Governor]] [[Tauntaza]]'s [[Tauntaza's fortress|"Death Machine"]]. When Zed-6-7, Wald and the balding villager ask about [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Sabé]], [[Kitster Banai]] and the Death Machine, Ochi says that he can only see dust and haze. [[Em-Sevenbee]] and ZED noticed that they are able to pick up comm signals again. ZED thinks that the blast disabled whatever the Governor was using to jam communications. 

Before Ochi can order the droids to contact Lord Vader, the [[Sith Lord]] arrives with Sabé and Kitster. Kitster tells Wald that they tried to destroy the Governor's Death Machine but were caught in a storm. Kitster credits Vader with saving him and Sabé from death. Em-Sevenbee and ZED [[Slicer|slice]] into the death machine's [[databank]]s and discover that it is predicted to reduce the planet to desert within 48 hours. Under Vader's orders, [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] orders the ''Executor'' to fire another blast at Tauntaza's "death machine." However, the mobile fortress is protected by powerful [[deflector shield]]s.

ZED-6-7 realizes that the Death Machine is draining the planet's energy to create a deflectors shield. When Sabé asks about the machine's weakness, Ochi thinks that the fortress is impregnable. Lord Vader agrees, prompting Ochi to submit. Based on information derived from Em-Sevenbee's hack into the local colonial databank, ZED discovers that the deflector shields protector the facility from above but that its underside is unshielded while it is draining Gabredor III.

===Vader versus Tauntaza's "technological terror"===
As the Death Machine approaches, Lord Vader enlists the services of Em-Sevenbee. As the [[R-series]] [[droid]] leaves with Vader, ZED explains that Em-Sevenbee is not in danger because the machine only absorbs organic life energy. When Sabé asks about Vader, ZED says that Vader is special but warns that the villagers are in danger. Sabé orders that the [[Gabredor III colony]] be evacuated. Kitster tells Sabé to come along since she cannot help Vader. As Vader and Sevenbee approach the Death Machine, it drains the life energies of several [[Unidentified six-legged horned creature|six-legged horned creatures]].

As the Death Machine approaches, Vader and Sevenbee stand below its tracks. They draw the attention of Governor Tauntaza and her crew. Though Tauntaza thinks that Vader is being protected by some protective shielding, an officer thinks that Vader is gone once the machine gets on top of him since he is flesh and blood. At Vader's signal, Sevenbee fires up [[Darth Vader's lightsaber]] from one of his head sockets. The machine slices through the Death Machine's motors and extractor. Vader uses [[the Force]] to levitate his lightsaber through the juggernaut, causing more damage. 

Sabé and several stormtroopers prepare to storm the Death Machine. In the command bridge, an officer reports that Vader has disabled the extractor and carved his way inside the fortress. Upon learning that Vader is in Sector B Hallway Twelve, the Governor realizes that he is heading for the energy center and decides to face the Sith Lord in a prototype armored suit. She convinces one of her technicians to plug her into the energy tanks.

Tauntaza attacks Lord Vader with her armored power suit's [[laser cannon]] but Lord Vader is able to deflect her blasts with his lightsaber. He taunts her that she has taken too much energy and that she has overplayed her hand. Tauntaza counters that Vader has too much power and too little control. After Vader uses his lightsaber to knock her laser cannon out of her hand, Tauntaza says that Vader has proved her point and says that he only destroys himself. As she escapes in her powered armor suit, Vader counters that he has destroyed all that she has built. 

===Aftermath===
Meanwhile, the Death Machine begins exploding from within. Sabé survives the explosions but two stormtroopers are caught amongst the explosions and falling debris. The explosions also short-circuit [[Darth Vader's armor]]. Upon discovering him, Sabé debates whether to kill Lord Vader. After experiencing a flashback of a young [[Anakin Skywalker]] telling her that he had come to farewell [[Padmé Amidala]] in case he did not see her again, she decides to trust Amidala's judgement and uses an energy tank to power up Vader's armor. 

As the Death Machine explodes, Em-Sevenbee meets with ZED-6-7 with new data that he had sliced before the fortress' destruction. Based on the data, ZED deduces that the Governor had been receiving instructions directly from [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] on [[Coruscant]]. Meanwhile, Vader and Sabé walk together. When Vader asks why she smiles, Sabé responds that she knows why she saved him.
