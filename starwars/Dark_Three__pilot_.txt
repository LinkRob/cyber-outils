A [[human]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]] used the callsign "'''Dark Three'''" and served the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Dark Squadron]] between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]. They and their [[squadron]] [[Assassination of Tarl Sokoli|attacked]] the Imperial [[light cruiser]] ''[[Forerunner II]]'' as part of their mission to eliminate the alleged [[Crimson Dawn]] mole [[Tarl Sokoli]]. During the battle, Dark Three was saved from an enemy [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] by [[Jeela Brebtin]] "[[Dark Two]]," but fell to the [[cruiser]]'s [[cannon]]s shortly after.

==History==
{{Quote|Stop whining, Dark Three. You're fine.''"<br />"''Thank you, Jeela!''"<br />"''Call me Dark Two on this channel or I'll shoot you myself.|Jeela Brebtin and Dark Three|<ref name="BH19" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="BH19" /> the [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> attended an [[Imperial Academy]] before becoming a [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE pilot]].<ref name="BH19" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> they were eventually assigned to Empire's [[Dark Squadron]], using the callsign "[[Dark Three]]" and flying a [[TIE/d "Defender" Multi-Role Starfighter|TIE defender]]. The squadron reported to the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]], and was stationed aboard the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]'', the [[flagship]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Dark Squadron was placed under the [[Squadron commander|command]] of the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]],<ref name="BH18">[[Bounty Hunters 18|''Bounty Hunters'' 18]]</ref> "[[Dark One]],"<ref name="BH19" /> who had been captured by Vader and forced into service by the [[Sith]] [[Lord]].<ref name="BH18" /> The squadron was then tasked with the [[Death|elimination]] of the Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Tarl Sokoli]], who was alleged to have been recruited by the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate to be one of their moles.<ref name="BH19" />

"Dark Three" and the rest of their squadron went outside the [[Eriadu system]], where Sokoli, aboard the [[light cruiser]] ''[[Forerunner II]]'', had been redirected under Vader's orders. Valance had the squadron immediately [[Assassination of Tarl Sokoli|attack]] as the [[cruiser]] deployed its [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. Dark Three felt uneasy about fighting other Imperial [[pilot]]s, adding that it was possible that they could have gone to the Imperial Academy with some of them. "[[Dark Four (pilot)|Dark Four]]" chimed in that the mission had come from Vader, questioning whether Three wanted to raise the issue with the Sith Lord himself.<ref name="BH19" />

[[File:DarkThreeDestroyed-BountyHunters19.jpg|thumb|left|300px|"Dark Three" fell to the ''Forerunner II''{{'s}} cannonfire.]]
As the battle progressed, Dark Three was pursued by one TIE fighter, asking his squadronmates for help. [[Jeela Brebtin]] "[[Dark Two]]" destroyed the fighter and told Three to stop whining. Dark Three thanked her, only to be scorned for using her real name over her callsign. After "[[Dark Five (pilot)|Dark Five]]" took out the ''Forerunner II''{{'s}} [[hyperdrive]], the cruiser begun firing its [[cannon]]s at its attackers. The ''Forerunner II''{{'s}} rear cannons nudged Dark Three towards its front cannons, which destroyed the pilot with their fighter. Despite Dark Three's loss, and eventually [[Dark Four]]'s, Valance was able to kill Sokoli and complete his squadron's mission.<ref name="BH19" />

==Equipment==
Dark Three wore a [[Color|black]] [[flight suit]] with a [[life support]] unit attached to their black [[helmet]], which bore gray markings.<ref name="BH19" />
