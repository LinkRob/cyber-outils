'''Dawn of Defiance: Gamemaster Primer''' or '''Dawn of Defiance Campaign Outline''' was an article on [[Wizards.com]]. It briefly summarised the plot of the [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game]] campaign, ''[[Dawn of Defiance]]'', which it was released in advance of, so as to allow gamemasters to have an idea of what was going to happen in the 10 adventures that would make up the campaign.
