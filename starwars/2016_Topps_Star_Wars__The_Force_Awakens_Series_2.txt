'''2016 Topps ''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Series 2''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[2016]] to coincide with the release of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. The set featured 102 base cards, autograph cards, and a limited set of concept art cards.
