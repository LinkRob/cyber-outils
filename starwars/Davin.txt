'''Davin''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Lasat]] who worked with [[Ephraim Bridger|Ephraim]] and [[Mira Bridger]] during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early stages of the rebellion]].<ref name="The Dark" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|They only caught him last week. He's been on the run for years, outwitting them at every turn. But they ran him down in the end.|Stel lies about Davin's past|<ref name="The Dark" />}}
Davin was a [[Gender|male]] [[Lasat]] operative. At one point, he was hit by a [[blaster]] shot and the [[medic]]s fitted him with a [[cyborg]] lung.<ref name="The Dark" />
In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelnes">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the events of ''Adventures in Wild Space: The Dark'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> he was working with [[Ephraim Bridger]] and [[Mira Bridger]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Dark" />

He agreed to be captured by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Shalla Mondatha|the Shade]] in order to help free the children [[Milo Graf]] and [[Lina Graf]] aboard the [[starship]] ''[[Moveable Feast]]''.<ref name="The Dark" />

With the help of Milo, they were able to escape when a cargo of [[Cylo]]'s [[Cylo's cyborg spider|cyborg spiders]] broke loose. They then rendezvoused with the Bridgers' vessel ''[[Sunburst]]''.<ref name="The Dark" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Name's Stel, by the way. And that lump of meat over there, that's Davin. But you don't really need to know that, because he won't speak to you, and if you've got any sense you won't speak to him, neither.|Stel|<ref name="The Dark" />}}
His [[Davin's mother|mother]] often called him brave. He was scared of [[spider]]s, but agreed to let the spiders swarm them in order to escape their cell. He then sheepishly admitted that he had a cyborg lung. After being hit with a [[stun stick]] again, he declared that he would never work with the Bridgers again. He also believed a [[Hydrospanner]] could be a good tool to hit people with.<ref name="The Dark" />
