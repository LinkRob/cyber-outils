'''Crothy''' was a location in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Crothy system]] in the [[Seitia sector]]. It was dealing with a fever outbreak by [[232 BBY]], prompting an [[Unidentified individual (Crothy)|individual]] and their colleagues to attempt to bring fever vaccines there. However, they were unsuccessful due to the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] disrupting [[hyperspace]] traffic [[The galaxy|galaxy]]-wide.

==Description==
Crothy was a location<ref name="ITD" /> in the [[Crothy system]] of the [[Seitia sector]], located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]] regions of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="WesternReaches" /> It was situated in grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
[[File:Reath-Silas.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Reath Silas (pictured) spoke to an individual who had been trying to deliver vaccines to Crothy.]]
By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="ITD Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], Crothy was dealing with an outbreak of [[Disease|fever]].<ref name="ITD" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="ITD Date" /> an [[Unidentified individual (Crothy)|individual]] and their colleagues attempted to bring fever vaccines to the location to help via a [[transport]]. However, they were prevented from delivering them to Crothy when the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] disrupted [[hyperspace]] traffic [[galaxy]]-wide.<ref name="ITD" />

The individual was injured during the event and arrived at a crowded [[Unidentified spacedock infirmary|spacedock infirmary]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] for medical treatment, alongside many other patients. In the disaster's aftermath, they told the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] about their attempted journey to Crothy, inquiring whether anyone else had been able to reach the location or if they had been able to contain the fever. However, Silas was unable to answer their query and silently reflected on how lucky he was to have survived the ordeal.<ref name="ITD" />
