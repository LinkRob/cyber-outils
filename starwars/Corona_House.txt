The '''Corona House''' was a location on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Explorer's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Corona House was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]'', a [[2019]] reference book written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Explorer's Guide" /> Within the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, it first appeared in ''[[Ambush at Corellia]]'', a [[1995]] novel by [[Roger MacBride Allen]].<ref name="Ambush">''[[Ambush at Corellia]]''</ref>
