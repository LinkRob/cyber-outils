Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] launched an '''assault''' on an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] and its escort of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]]. A scavenging family known as the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] picked up wreckage resulting from the skirmish in their starship, the ''[[StarScavenger]]''. During the skirmish, [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] accidentally destroyed the ''Gozanti'' cruiser. To avoid drawing attention, the Freemakers retreated into [[hyperspace]].

==Prelude==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] were a family of [[scavenger]]s who operated a ship salvage and repair business called [[Freemaker Salvage and Repair]].<ref name="AHD" /> They also owned a [[CT-900 freighter]]<ref name="TOT">{{FreemakerAdventures|Trouble on Tibalt}}</ref> called the ''[[StarScavenger]]'', which they used on numerous salvage runs. The Freemakers preferred not to get involved in the conflict between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]].<ref name="AHD" />

==The skirmish==
Unable to pay their rent, [[Kordi Freemaker/LEGO|Kordi Freemaker]] convinced her older brother [[Zander Freemaker]] to undertake a risky salvage run during a battle between the Galactic Empire and the Rebel Alliance. Their younger brother [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] was tasked with operating the ship's gun turret but was given orders not to open fire. During the battle, Rowan accidentally fired the ''StarScavenger''{{'s}} gun turret at the Imperial [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]], destroying ship. This alarmed both the Imperial and rebel pilots. To avoid drawing further attention, Zander took the ''StarScavenger'' back to the Freemakers' garage in [[The Wheel#Behind the scenes|the Wheel]] space station.<ref name="AHD" />

==Aftermath==
Returning to the Wheel, the Freemakers found their entrance to their garage blocked by one of their [[Aqualish]] landlord [[Furlac]]'s [[flood droid]]s. When Furlac demanded their rent, Kordi convinced him to defer it by one day. The Freemakers then attempted to build an [[Ugly|Z-wing]] out of the wreckage they had obtained. However, Rowan accidentally blew it up with the head of an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST walker]]. As a result, the Freemakers embarked on another [[Mission to Nal Kapok|salvage run]] to the planet [[Nal Kapok/LEGO|Nal Kapok]] with their [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR/LEGO|Roger]] to obtain more scrap metal. Rowan encountered the false "[[Jedi]]" [[Naare]] and became involved in a [[Quest for the Kyber Saber Crystals|quest]] to find the [[Kyber Saber]].<ref name="AHD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Assault on an Imperial Assault Carrier first appeared in the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season One|Season One]] episode "[[A Hero Discovered]]," which premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[June 20]], [[2016]].
