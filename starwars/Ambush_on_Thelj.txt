A subgroup of the [[Religion|cult]] The [[Path of the Open Hand]] called the [[Children of the Open Hand|Children]] were '''ambushed''' by the [[Graf family]]'s forces on the [[planet]] [[Thelj]] when they attempted to steal an artifact called [[Jedi Tears]] from the [[Graf family compound]] on the planet. The leader of the Path, [[Elecia Zeveron]], who used the alias "The Mother", had alerted the Grafs of the Children's arrival with the intent for the clan to kill them. [[Yana Ro]] was the only member of the Children who survived, and [[Radicaz Dobbs|Radicaz "Sunshine" Dobbs]], who had brought the Children to Thelj in the first place, agreed to take Ro back to the [[Path compound]] on [[Dalna]] so that she could meet with The Mother about the incident.<ref name="PoD" />
