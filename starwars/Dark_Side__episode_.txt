"'''Dark Side'''" is the third episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Sounds]]'', an inside look at the sound design of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' films and series.<ref name="D+" /> It premiered on [[September 29]], [[2021]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Announcement" />
