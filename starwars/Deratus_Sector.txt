The '''Deratus Sector''' was a region of the [[Galaxy/Legends|galaxy]].

During the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] era, it was ruled by [[Moff/Legends|Moff]] [[Tricus Phenge]].

==Behind the scenes==
The Deratus Sector was mentioned in the short story ''[[A Servant of the Empire]]'' in [[West End Games]]' canceled magazine [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 16|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 16]].
