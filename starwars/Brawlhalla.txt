'''''Brawlhalla''''' is a free-to-play online multiplayer 2D platform fighting [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed and initially published by [[Blue Mammoth Games]], which officially released on [[October 17]], [[2017]].<ref name="Steam release" />

On [[February 23]], [[2024]], it was announced that the game was having a special collaboration with ''[[Star Wars]]'' beginning on [[March 20]] of that year. A debut trailer revealed that [[Prequel trilogy|prequel era]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] would be joining the game as playable characters. A [[StarWars.com]] article announcing the collaboration teased at the introduction of more ''Star Wars'' content into the game throughout 2024.<ref name="High Ground">{{SW|url=news/brawlhalla-star-wars|text=Take the High Ground in the Brawlhalla ''Star Wars'' Event}}</ref> On [[March 9]], 2024, Brawlhalla released an announcement trailer revealing [[Ahsoka Tano]] would be joining as a third playable ''Star Wars'' character in the crossover event.<ref name="AhsokaReveal">{{BrawlhallaYouTube|Wan6yzL7lFA|Brawlhalla STAR WARS Event &ndash; Ahsoka Tano Reveal Trailer|date=2024-03-09}}</ref>

On March 20, 2024, the ''Brawlhalla'' ''Star Wars'' Event debuted with the Patch 8.06 update to the game.<ref name="PatchNotes">{{Brawlhalla|url=news/brawlhalla-star-wars-event-patch-8-06|text=Brawlhalla Star Wars Event – Feel the Power of the Force! – Patch 8.06}}</ref> A launch trailer for the event revealed that the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] would be a playable character, and it announced several themed cosmetic items along with a new [[Mustafar]]-based map.<ref name="LaunchTrailer">{{BrawlhallaYouTube|OwjXaxs4woo|Brawlhalla STAR WARS Event Launch Trailer|date=2024-03-20}}</ref>

On [[April 20]], 2024, it was announced that on [[May 1]], Darth [[Maul]] would join the game as Mythic Crossover.<ref name="Maul Announcement">{{BrawlhallaYouTube|qLmJUIOkAqw|Brawlhalla STAR WARS Event &ndash; Darth Maul Reveal Trailer|date=2024-03-20}}</ref>

During Brawlhalla World Championship 2024, on [[November 17]]; the second wave of ''Star Wars'' collaborations titled "Outlaws and Scoundrels" was announced for release on [[December 4]] of the same year. The new collaboration added [[Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca]], [[Kay Vess]], [[Nix]], [[Din Djarin]], and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] as skins for preexisting characters.<ref name="BCX 2024">{{YouTube|video=ui-kZgcTKhI|text=Brawlhalla World Championship 2024 &#124; Championship Sunday &#124; #BCX2024|channel=@Brawlhalla|name=Brawlhalla|date=2024-11-17}}</ref>

On [[April 26]], [[2026]] another ''Star Wars'' crossover, in honor of [[May 4|May the 4th]], was announced to begin on [[April 29]] of that year. This crossover brought many of the previous ''Star Wars'' cosmetics in new bundles, as well as introducing the new Mythic Crossover of [[Luke Skywalker]] as depicted in {{Film|VI}}. It also introduced three new emoji skins featuring [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]], [[Darth Sidious]], and [[Red Five (T-65B X-wing starfighter)|Luke's X-wing starfighter]].<ref name="2026 May 4">{{Brawlhalla|url=news/may-the-4th-be-with-you-new-content-gameplay-changes-patch-10-06|text=May the 4th Be With You! New Content & Gameplay Changes &ndash; Patch 10.06}}
}}</ref> 

==''Star Wars'' content==
The crossover event included new characters, a new map, and new cosmetics, as well as a limited-time game mode and a refreshed user interface. When a player logged into the game during the event, they would automatically receive the free title, [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="PatchNotes" />

===Mythic Crossovers===
In ''Brawlhalla'', playable characters from crossover events are referred to as Mythic Crossovers. At the launch of the event, [[Ahsoka Tano]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] (as depicted in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''), and Darth Vader were Mythical Crossovers. Each character mirrored the abilities of a pre-existing character, known in ''Brawlhalla'' as Legends. The purchase of each ''Star Wars'' character also came with cosmetic items related to the character and unique [[lightsaber]] sound effects.<ref name="PatchNotes" />

[[File:Brawlhalla_MythicCrossovers.png|left|thumb|285px|Ahsoka Tano, Anakin Skywalker, and Obi-Wan Kenobi as portrayed in ''Brawlhalla''.]]
Ahsoka Tano, described in ''Brawlhalla'' as "once a [[padawan]], now an outcast who helps those in need no matter what," is depicted with [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|two blue lightsabers]]. The animation in her Mythic Crossover panel in-game shows her cutting a hole in the floor beneath a [[B1-series battle droid]] before jumping up and posing with her lightsabers.<ref name="Patch Video">{{BrawlhallaYouTube|e3TinUJQHdc|Brawlhalla STAR WARS Event &ndash; Feel the Power of the Force! &ndash; Patch Notes 8.06|date=2024-03-20}}</ref> Ahsoka mirrored the abilities of the Legend Asuri, but with a custom katars light attack for Ahsoka's reverse grip. She had signature effects for swinging her blades, and purchase of Ahsoka came with the additional items of her lightsaber and [[Shoto lightsaber|shoto]], and her lightsaber alone.<ref name="PatchNotes" />

Obi-Wan Kenobi, who was described in-game as "a legendary [[Jedi Master]], noble and gifted in the ways of [[the Force]]," posed in his signature pre-duel stance, his second and third fingers pointed towards an unseen foe.<ref name="Patch Video" /> He mirrored the abilities of the Legend Val, with custom light attack effects. His signature effects were hoisting [[Stone|rocks]] up with the Force before launching them at enemies, and slashing low to the ground with his lightsaber. Purchase of Obi-Wan came with the additional items of his [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[General]]'s [[gauntlet]]s and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|his lightsaber]].<ref name="PatchNotes" />

Anakin Skywalker was described in ''Brawlhalla'' as "a compassionate yet reckless Jedi Knight believed to be the prophesied [[Chosen One]]." He posed with [[Skywalker lightsaber|his lightsaber]] aimed down in a defensive position.<ref name="Patch Video" /> He mirrored the abilities of Ezio, using a [[Jedi]] [[Marksman-H training remote|training remote]] as an orb. His signature effects involved lifting rocks from the ground to be thrust into enemies, and a wide slash of his lightsaber. The additional items that came with purchase of Anakin included his lightsaber and the Jedi training remote.<ref name="PatchNotes" />

Darth Vader was described in the game as "a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] tasked with hunting down the Jedi." He posed with [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] down, his left fist in the air as his [[cape]] billowed in the wind.<ref name="Patch Video" /> Like Anakin, he mirrored the abilities of Ezio, using [[durasteel]] debris as the orb. His signature effects included throwing large rocks and swinging widely at enemies. The items that came with the purchase of Vader were his lightsaber and the durasteel debris.<ref name="PatchNotes" />

===Cosmetics===
[[File:WeapSkinSW-Brawlhalla.png|right|thumb|275px|The Asgardian Lightsabers]]
Available in the in-game shop, Mallhalla, with the launch of the crossover were several cosmetic items, which could be applied beyond the ''Star Wars'' characters. One such cosmetic item was the emote, "Force [[Levitation]]," which allowed any playable character equipped with the emote to use the Force and lift up some small rocks.<ref name="Patch Video" /> As a purely cosmetic emote, the animation did not deal any damage. Another cosmetic was a set of Asgardian lightsabers, combining the Norse influences of the game with the iconic ''Star Wars'' weapon. The cosmetic could be applied to [[sword]]s, [[spear]]s, or katars, and included special animations and sound effects.<ref name="PatchNotes" />

[[File:SWEmojis-Brawlhalla.png|left|thumb|250px|The ''Star Wars'' emojis in ''Brawlhalla'']]
Lastly, Mallhalla sold four ''Star Wars'' avatars and five emojis. The avatars included the [[Jedi crest]] (called "[[Jedi Order]]" in the game), the [[Republic crest]] (called "Galactic Republic"), a clash between Obi-Wan's blue lightsaber and Darth Vader's red lightsaber (called "Jedi vs Sith"), and a smiling [[tooka]] (simply titled "Tooka"). The first emoji, called "Ahsoka GG," featured the [[Togruta]] hero next to the letters "GG," written in a ''Star Wars''-esque font, as if to say "good game, Skyguy." One emoji called "[[C-3PO]] Sweat" featured the [[protocol droid]] with dark gray beads of [[oil]]-like sweat on his forehead, holding a red rag to it. The "Vader Thumbs Down" emoji showed the floating head of the [[Sith]] with a thumbs down, also floating. The last two emojis, "Obi-Wan Wave" and "Anakin Think," showed the two Jedi doing just that.<ref name="PatchNotes" />

===Mustafar stage===
[[File:MustafarStageBG-Brawlhalla.png|250px|thumb|right|The "Mustafar" stage.]]
The launch of the event coincided with the release of a new free-for-all map in the game, "Mustafar." The map was reminiscent of the [[Duel on Mustafar|duel between Obi-Wan and Anakin]] during ''[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith]]'', with [[Repulsorcraft|repulsor platforms]] hovering over rising [[Magma|lava]].<ref name="LaunchTrailer" /> Similarly, the Brawl of the Week Game Mode, "Brawl of the Heroes," echoes the title of the song, "[[Battle of the Heroes]]" from that fateful duel.<ref name="PatchNotes" />
