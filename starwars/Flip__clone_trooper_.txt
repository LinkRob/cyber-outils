'''Flip''' was a [[clone trooper]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] who served in a [[Tualon Yaluna's company|company]] led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Tualon Yaluna]], [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Spider (Clone Captain)|Spider]]. He typically piloted the group's ship and was named Flip after the noise made by the electronic sound of him changing pages in his [[holobook]]s. Flip was a bibliophile and lent Akaris several holobooks during the group's journey to [[Olgothon 3]].<ref name="Inquisitor" />
