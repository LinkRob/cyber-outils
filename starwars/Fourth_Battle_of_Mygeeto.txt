The '''Fourth Battle of Mygeeto''', also known simply as the '''Battle of Mygeeto''', was a battle fought during the final [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Clone Wars]] on the [[planet]] [[Mygeeto]]. It was part of a three [[Standard year|year]]-long [[Mygeeto Campaign|campaign]] by the [[Galactic Republic]] to oust the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] from the strategically important world. The battle was mere [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]].

After the [[death]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] during [[Order 66]], which was enacted during the battle, [[Clone Marshal Commander|Marshal Commander]] [[Bacara]] and the [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]] continued fighting to destroy the last of the Separatist positions on the world.

==Prelude==
A strategically important<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> yet frigid [[Planet|world]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], [[Mygeeto]] was controlled by the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Mygeeto" /> but it was also sought by both the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]]. A number of battles were waged<ref name="First Blood 4">[[Kanan 10|''Kanan'' 10]]</ref> by the Republic to remove the Confederacy's presence on Mygeeto,<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> making the struggle into a three [[Standard year|year]] long campaign.<ref name="Force Collection1">{{ForceCollection|Bacara|star=4}} {{C|light side card}}</ref> While the overall Banking Clan was nationalized into the [[Office of the Chancellor|Office of the Republic's Supreme Chancellor]] after the [[Invasion of Scipio]],<ref name="CH">{{TCW|Crisis at the Heart}}</ref> [[Chairman]] [[San Hill]]'s [[Muunilinst Banking Clan|faction]] continued to support the Separatists.<ref name="ROTS" /> Further, the Confederacy refused to turn over Mygeeto to the Republic, resulting in continued conflict for the valuable planet<ref name="Planets51">{{DarthVaderCite|51|Planets}}</ref> while Confederate-aligned Banking Clan forces found safe haven on world.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

[[File:FennRau-Kanan10.jpg|thumb|left|180px|Skull Squadron and Republic forces fought in the Third Battle of Mygeeto.]]
The [[Third Battle of Mygeeto]] involved [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]]'s [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|battalion]] of [[clone trooper]]s and the Republic's [[Mandalorian]] allies in [[Skull Squadron]], yet even then the Republic was outnumbered by the [[battle droid]] forces on planet. [[General]] [[Grievous]] [[Gender|himself]] oversaw the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the battle,<ref name="First Blood 4" /> but he was forced into a retreat, turning the Third Battle of Mygeeto into a Republic victory.<ref name="First Blood 5">[[Kanan 11|''Kanan'' 11]]</ref>

Nevertheless, only [[Standard month|months]] later, the war returned to the world<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> when the Republic sought to take out entrenched [[Collections and Security Division|Banking Clan forces]] on Mygeeto<ref name="Complete Locations" /> and claim the planet from the Confederacy,<ref name="USW NE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> throwing it into the Fourth Battle of Mygeeto. The Fourth Battle<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> was also part of the [[Outer Rim Sieges]] campaign.<ref name="Mygeeto" /> [[Jedi General]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] and [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bacara]], who had served under Mundi throughout the Mygeeto campaign<ref name="Force Collection1" /> and often at other points during the war,<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> were assigned to lead their [[Galactic Marines|21st Nova Corps]] into the battle<ref name="ROTS" /> with the mission to claim the world for the Republic.<ref name="USW NE" /> Previously in the sieges, Bacara had been leading [[Republic Military|Republic forces]] against the Confederacy, leading to the Republic attack on Mygeeto.<ref name="Clone Commander Bacara">{{Databank|clone-commander-bacara|Clone Commander Bacara}}</ref> At one point, Mundi and Bacara appeared via [[hologram]] at a meeting about the sieges.<ref name="Old Friends">{{TCW|Old Friends Not Forgotten}}</ref>

==The battle==
{{Quote|Come on, we must take the bridge!|General Ki-Adi-Mundi, to his soldiers|<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />}}
[[File:FourthBattleOfMygeetoOrder66-GalaxysEdge2.png|thumb|right|300px|Fighting alongside the clone troops against the Droid Army, Ki-Adi-Mundi remained unaware of Order 66 until it was too late.]]
Members of the [[501st Legion]] participated in the battle, undertaking a secret mission to seize a huge [[crystal]].<ref name="Mustafar SWE" /> During the fighting, Mundi led his forces<ref name="ROTS" /> against Separatist positions on Mygeeto,<ref name="HC 19">{{HelmetCollectionCite|19|Databank A-Z}}</ref> the entrenched positions of the Banking Clan's forces.<ref name="Complete Locations" /> Thus, he fought alongside the Galactic Marines and [[Unstable Terrain Artillery Transport]]s on a bridge against the Droid Army.<ref name="ROTS" /> While fighting several [[B1-series battle droid|B1 units]] and a [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid]] by a crashed [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|Republic gunship]], Mundi yelled to his [[troop]]s that they needed to take control of the bridge, unaware that Bacara then received [[Order 66]]<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" /> from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], branding the entire [[Jedi Order]] as [[Treason|traitors]] to the Republic and ordering their instant [[Death penalty|execution]].<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref>

Although the [[Clone Commander]] hesitated briefly from disbelief,<ref name="Force Collection Bacara">{{ForceCollection|Bacara|star=4}} {{C|dark side card}}</ref> his obedience, like all [[Cloning|clones]], was enforced by his [[behavioral modification biochip]],<ref name="Aftermath" /> turning Bacara and the rest of the 21st against their own leader. As Mundi and the clones fought the droids, losing one UT-AT to a blast from a [[Octuptarra tri-droid]], the [[Jedi]] General yelled for his troops to follow him<ref name="ROTS" /> into battle<ref name="Mundi Base Series 1">{{SWCT|set=Base Series 1|cardname=Ki-Adi-Mundi - Galactic Republic}}</ref> against [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, only to notice that<ref name="ROTS" /> his [[Ki-Adi-Mundi's squad|squad]]<ref name="SWTCVDNE">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> had instead stopped when he heard them priming their [[blaster]]s. Bacara and his marines suddenly opened fire, and, though he managed to block some of their blaster bolts, Mundi was killed by their attack,<ref name="ROTS" /> dropping his [[Ki-Adi-Mundi's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to the ground.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />

After achieving victory in the [[Bracca Invasion]], Jedi General [[Jaro Tapal]]'s [[13th Battalion]] was ordered into the Fourth Battle of Mygeeto, but the execution of Order 66 ended with the [[Skirmish aboard the Albedo Brave|destruction]] of his [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]], the ''[[Albedo Brave]]'', before they could leave.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order" /> Following Mundi's execution, Bacara and the 21st continued the battle against the droids, fighting with the intention of wiping out the Confederacy's remaining positions on Mygeeto.<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> When the newly anointed [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] wiped out the [[Executive Separatist Council]], including San Hill, on the Outer Rim planet [[Mustafar]] and sent out a shutdown order for the [[Separatist Droid Army]] across the galaxy,<ref name="ROTS" /> the fighting on Mygeeto came to a quick end as the remaining battle droids on world deactivated.<ref name="Planets51" />

==Aftermath==
The Clone Wars ended with the [[mission to Mustafar]], during which [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] murdered the [[Executive Separatist Council]] and shut down the Droid Army, and Palpatine [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="ROTS" /> Mygeeto became a part of the Empire,<ref name="Shadows and Secrets, Part II">[[Darth Vader (2015) 8|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 8]]</ref> but the Banking Clan was able to continue to work from the planet. [[Vice Chairman]] [[Anolo]] came to own Mundi's [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" /> Despite the Imperial domination of the planet, Mygeeto found peace. The crystal found on Mygeeto by the 501st was later used for the [[superlaser]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]].<ref name="Mustafar SWE" />

While speaking with a [[Unidentified Givin codebreaker|Givin codebreaker]], Anolo compared its wielder to the Banking Clan, as the Banks were involved in a series of [[trade wars]] with the [[Hutt Clan]]. Shortly afterward, Anolo was killed during the [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]]'s [[mission to Mygeeto]], and the [[Rodian]] escaped with Mundi's lightsaber. After Greedo returned to [[Tatooine]], it was purchased by collector [[Dok-Ondar]], who kept it in his [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|Den of Antiquities]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />
