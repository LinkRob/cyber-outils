"'''Crisis on Cona'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. A group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] attempt to stop unscrupulous [[Smuggler/Legends|smugglers]] from importing a large amount of [[Salt/Legends|salt]] to the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Cona/Legends|Cona]], homeworld of the [[Arcona/Legends|Arcona]]. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
