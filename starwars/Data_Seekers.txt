The '''Data Seekers''' were an underground group whose members sought to unravel the mysteries of the [[Anoat sector]]'s ancient history. Their research interests included the origins of the [[Kouhun (order)|Kouhun]], the survival and exile of the [[Noble Court]], the hierarchy of the [[Ivax Syndicate]], and the existence of [[the Force]]. The Seekers were [[Spy|espionage]] experts who posed as [[merchant]]s, [[pirate]]s, and traders, offering work and minor intelligence to any individual likely to advance their cause. One of them, [[Dazur Dar]], known as the Data Broker, traded not only secrets, but also a whole range of items the Seekers had picked up in their travels.<ref name="Forums" />

By the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]], the Seekers had [[agent]]s planted within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s forces for many [[Standard year|years]], which had allowed them to learn the truth about the [[gassing of Anoat]], the [[prison]]s on [[Deyer]], and more.<ref name="Imp Plant">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Imperial Plant"</ref> The Seekers also placed spies as [[archivist]]s within the Noble Court, which allowed them to discover what the [[noble]]s were secretly doing on [[Council (planet)|Council]].<ref name="Archivist">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Archivist"</ref>
