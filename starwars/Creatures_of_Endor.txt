"'''Creatures of Endor'''" is an article published through the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[November 23]], [[2004]]. It provides information on several of the [[Species/Legends|species]] found on [[Endor/Legends|the Forest Moon of Endor]]. The article was a tie-in to the DVD releases of [[The Haunted Village (film)|''The Haunted Village'']] and ''[[Tales from the Endor Woods]]'', feature-length recuts of eight episodes of [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] animated television series. Accordingly, the species featured in the article all appear in ''Star Wars: Ewoks''.
