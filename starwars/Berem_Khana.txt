'''Berem Khana''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] who served as a [[Jedi]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[Republic Era]]. Not long after [[Knighting Ceremony|becoming]] a [[Jedi Knight]], they accompanied [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] [[Vekura]] in an attempt to negotiate an end to the [[Vekuran]]s [[Invasion of Syrinx Prime|invasion]] of the world [[Syrinx Prime]] due to their familiarity with culture in the two world's shared [[Unidentified Outer Rim Territories sector|sector]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> Khana, alongside Jedi Knight [[Seera Longa]], was [[Death|killed]] during a [[Skirmish on a starship|skirmish]] with [[Unidentified pirate group (Soona Taj)|pirates]]. Following Khana's death, the [[Lightsaber hilt|hilt]] of both [[Berem Khana's lightsaber|Khana's]] and [[Seera Longa's lightsaber|Longa's lightsabers]] were used in the construction of a [[Soona Taj's lightsaber|lightsaber]] built for [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Soona Taj]]. It was given to Taj by Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and [[Kelleran Beq]] as a tribute to the two Jedi's bravery.<ref name="Jedi Knights 8">[[Jedi Knights 8|''Jedi Knights'' 8]]</ref>
