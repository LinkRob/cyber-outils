The '''CS-12 Stun Master''' was a non-lethal weapon that was produced by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]] and popular with many [[Sullustan]]s. The weapons, which were fifteen [[centimeter]]s in length and cost 575 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], were cylinders with thirteen contact studs that could deliver an electric shock.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
