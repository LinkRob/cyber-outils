'''Burta''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[engineer]] and a member of [[Blue (Devaronian)|Blue's]] [[Blue's partisans|rebel team]] in [[Five Points system]]. Blue and her group, with [[Jyn Erso]], who was an undercover [[agent]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]], brought [[Foodstuff|food]] to the [[planet]] [[Watassay]]. After they arrived the planet, the rebels were captured and arrested by [[Admiral]] [[Bethava Rocwyn]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

Burta possessed a bias against [[human]]s.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
