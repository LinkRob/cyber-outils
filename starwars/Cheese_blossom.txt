'''Cheese blossoms''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] made with [[cheese]] that was [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="MOF" /> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> the [[spy]] [[Chemish Runazha]] spent time waiting in the [[Haki Zeophrine's safe house|safe house]] they and their mentor, the [[Imperial Intelligence]] spy [[Haki Zeophrine]], shared, listlessly trying to decide what to do with their life after several tumultuous events. One [[Local day|night]], they decided to venture out of the house and bought some cheese blossoms from a [[Unidentified street cart|street cart]] before returning to their lodgings.<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cheese blossoms were mentioned in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
