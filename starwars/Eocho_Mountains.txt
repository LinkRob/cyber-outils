The '''Eocho Mountains''' were a [[mountain]] range located on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Ossus]]. The mountains were allegedly riddled with [[Crystal Caves (Ossus)|crystal caves]] that contained [[kyber crystal]]s. In the western part of the mountains, the [[city]] of [[Knossa]] was located, as well as the [[Knossa Spaceport]] which was constructed around [[Agorn Mountain]]. Nearby to both of these locations was the [[Eye of Ashlanae]], a [[vergence]] in [[the Force]] which was frequently visited by pilgrims.<ref name="NoP" />

According to the legendary [[Qel-Droma Epics]], [[Jedi]] survivors of the [[Cron Supernova]] hid in the caves of the Eocho Mountains, and their descendants became the [[Ysanna]] people. The Ysanna would continue to live nearby to these caves, farming in the [[nitrogen]]-rich valleys near the mountains.<ref name="NoP" />
