'''Combined Officer's Curriculum 27''' was a file that detailed the combined curriculum of [[Commandant]]s [[Angor]] and [[Bedgar]] at the [[Corulag Military Academy]] on<ref name="Rise" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Servants">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Corulag]]. The two were paired due to their complementary skills, with Bedgar's lessons covering the intricacies of [[Imperial Military|military]] administration and Angor's lessons covering robust combat strategy.<ref name="Rise" />

This resulted in the curriculum turning [[officer]] [[Imperial cadet|cadets]] into well-rounded [[Imperial officer|officers]] who were both combat ready and able to deal with the bureaucracies of leadership. The file was stored in the [[Imperial Archives]] and cited by the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] when<ref name="Rise" /> he<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> discussed the commandants in his [[book]], ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'',<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Combined Officer's Curriculum 27 was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
