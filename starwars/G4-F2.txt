'''G4-F2'''<ref name="GEBSO">{{GEAttr|BSO 401.72}}</ref> was an [[R-series]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> [[astromech droid]] manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]].<ref name="Stats" /> A [[Standard day|day]] after arriving on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="GEStaff" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> G4-F2 called into the radio show [[Droid Station Frequency 3-7-6-4-3-7]] on station [[BSO 401.72]] to ask in [[binary]] where it could find an [[oil bath]]<ref name="GEBSO" /> as its joints were aching.<ref name="GEStaff" />

The show's host, [[B6-24]], informed the [[droid]] that if it was near [[Black Spire Outpost]] then the best place for an oil bath was the [[Droid Depot]]. She told G4-F2 that if it told the depot's owner, [[Mubo]], that she had sent it then Mubo would give a twenty percent discount.<ref name="GEBSO" /> G4-F2 thanked B6-24 for the advice<ref name="GEStaff" /> and ended the call.<ref name="GEBSO" />

==Behind the scenes==
G4-F2 was created for the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="GEBSO" /> which opened to guests at [[Disneyland]] Park and [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=creatures-droids-and-aliens-meet-the-fantastical-figures-of-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Creatures, Droids, and Aliens: Meet the Fantastical Figures of ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The droid can be heard speaking in binary on the land's background radio loop,<ref name="GEBSO" /> with a translation of what it is saying available from staff.<ref name="GEStaff" /> The radio loop was replaced with different audio at Disneyland Park as part of the park redesign<ref name="GEBSO" /> that launched on [[April 29]], [[2026]].<ref name="OT updates">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galaxys-edge-disneyland-update|text=Darth Vader and Other Original Trilogy Updates Coming to ''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' at Disneyland}}</ref>
