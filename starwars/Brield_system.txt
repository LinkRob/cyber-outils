The '''Brield system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]],<ref name="Interior" /> situated in grid square M-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> At some point during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] undertook a [[Rescue mission to the Brield system|rescue mission]] to the system to clear passengers from a badly malfunctioning [[transport]].<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="ITD Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Silas thought about his [[time]] in the [[star system]], considering the rescue mission a departure from his usual [[Jedi training|training]] within the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] [[Jedi Archives]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="ITD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Brield system was first mentioned in the [[2021]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="ITD" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
