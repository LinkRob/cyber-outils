The '''Asation system''' was located in the [[Gree Enclave]] within the [[Veragi sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[Star system|system]] was located in grid square K-3 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Asation system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the appendix [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]], published on the [[StarWars.com]] website on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> The [[Asation system/Legends|system]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
