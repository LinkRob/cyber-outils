The '''''Altor''-class supply ship''' was a class of [[fuel]] replenishment [[supply ship]] developed by the [[Galactic Empire]]. During its [[Galactic Civil War|war]] with the Empire, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] utilized the ''Altor''-class supply [[Starship|ship]] ''[[Pikka's Pride]]''. Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], ''Pikka's Pride'' was used to carry four rebel ships to the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] so the Alliance could carry out an [[Assault on Coruscant|aerial assault]].

==Characteristics==
The ''Altor''-class supply ship was a huge class of [[supply ship]], measuring 4,600 [[meter]]s in length. The supply [[Starship|ships]] were propelled by seven powerful [[Engine|thrusters]], with each individual thruster circled by a cluster of eight auxiliary engines, totaling the number of auxiliary engines to fifty-six. It carried six spheroidal [[fuel]] containers.<ref name="BMF 34" />

The ship was able to carry out [[Realspace|deep-space]] repairs as a result of its stern housing a cluster of [[hangar]]/dry dock complexes, meaning it could accommodate [[frigate]]-sized [[capital ship]]s. ''Altor''-class supply ships had multiple articulated arms with refueling nozzles, allowing them to handle two capital ships simultaneously&mdash;one on each side.<ref name="BMF 34" />

==Role==
''Altor''-class supply ships were used by the [[Galactic Empire]] for fuel replenishing.<ref name="BMF 34" />

==History==
The ''Altor''-class supply ship was produced by the Galactic Empire,<ref name="BMF 34" /> which was [[Proclamation of the New Order|founded]] in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], an ''Altor''-class supply ship was based at [[Naval Station Validusia]], an immense Imperial [[space station]] in the [[Validusia system]].<ref name="BMF 34" />

[[File:PikkasPrideCoruscantSystem-2020StarWars36.png|thumb|left|300px|''Pikka's Pride'' was inspected by the Galactic Empire when it entered the Coruscant system.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], the Alliance utilized at least one ''Altor''-class supply ship&mdash;''[[Pikka's Pride]]''.<ref name="Star Wars 36" /> Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-marvel|text=SWCE 2023: Marvel Reveals ''Star Wars: Dark Droids'' Horror Event and More}} states that the events of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, including [[Star Wars (2020) 36|''Star Wars'' (2020) 36]], take place between the events of {{Film|V}} and {{Film|VI}}, which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Unidentified rebel (Pikka's Pride)|owner]] of ''Pikka's Pride'' was sent to deliver [[fertilizer]]s for [[algae]] [[farm]]s within a [[Unidentified farming platform|farming platform]] in<ref name="Star Wars 36" /> the [[Core Worlds]]'<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[Coruscant system]]. At the same [[time]], the ship was secretly carrying four rebel ships&mdash;the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and three [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s&mdash;so they could undertake an [[Assault on Coruscant|aerial assault]] on the Imperial<ref name="Star Wars 36" /> [[capital]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Once ''Pikka's Pride'' arrived in the [[Star system|system]], the ship was boarded and inspected by the Empire. After passing the inspection, ''Pikka's Pride'' proceeded on its journey. As the ship reached as close to Coruscant as possible before it could draw the attention of nearby [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyers]], the owner of ''Pikka's Pride'' unloaded the four rebel ships and continued onward to the platform.<ref name="Star Wars 36" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:StarWars2020-36-Cover.jpg|thumb|right|215px|''Star Wars'' (2020) 36 marked the first appearance of the ''Altor''-class supply ship in the current ''Star Wars'' canon.]]
The ''Altor''-class supply ship was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 34|thirty-fourth issue]] of the magazine series ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'', published by [[De Agostini]]<ref name="BMF 34" /> around [[August 26]], [[2015]].<ref name="Date 2">{{BMFdates|34}}</ref> The ship first appeared in the [[Star Wars (2020) 36|thirty-sixth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Andrea Di Vito]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 36" /> on [[July 5]], [[2023]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/103043/star_wars_2020_36|text=Star Wars (2020) #36}}</ref>

The ''Altor''-class tanker originated as a [[2010]] [[fanon]] digital model by [[Ansel Hsiao]].<ref name="Fanon">{{WebCite|url=https://bbs.stardestroyer.net/viewtopic.php?p=3445959#p3445959|text=Something big|author=[[Ansel Hsiao|fractalsponge1]]|date=2010-12-15|work=[https://bbs.stardestroyer.net StarDestroyer.Net BBS]|archivedate=20231213230319}} &mdash; According to {{WebCite|url=https://fractalsponge.net|text=fractalsponge.net|work=https://fractalsponge.net/ Fractalsponge]|archivedate=20200412235819}}, [[Ansel Hsiao]] is "fractalsponge1."</ref> It then [[List of fanon elements in continuity|entered continuity]] both in name and design when it was introduced as the [[Altor-class replenishment ship|''Altor''-class replenishment ship]] in the [[2012]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]]. Hsiao was also an illustrator for the book.<ref name="The Essential Guide to Warfare">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref> The [[Wikipedia:Latin|Latin]] word "[[Wiktionary:altor#Latin|altor]]" translates to "nourisher" or "sustainer,"<ref name="A Latin Dictionary">{{WebCite|url=http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.04.0059:entry=altor|text=altor|author=[[Wikipedia:Charlton Thomas Lewis|Charlton T. Lewis]]; Charles Short|work=[[Wikipedia:A Latin Dictionary|''A Latin Dictionary'']]|archivedate=20220110111211}}</ref> meanings which Hsiao echoed when he created the ship.<ref name="Fanon" />
