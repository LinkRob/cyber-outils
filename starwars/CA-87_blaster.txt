'''CA-87 blasters''' were [[shock blaster]]s created by [[Jawa]]s and retrofitted to create a short-ranged weapon. After they were modified to shoot [[blaster]] cartridges, however, these blasters, despite their inaccuracy at range, could be deadly in close quarter combat. A single shot could easily slice through most body [[armor]]. The weapons were in use during the [[Galactic Civil War]] on both sides.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CA-87 shock blaster first appears in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars:'' Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> but also appears in the [[2015]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''.<ref name="Battlefront" /> It also appears in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as a Repulsor Cannon ability card for the specialist class.<ref name="https://battlefront.fandom.com/wiki/Repulsor_Cannon">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
