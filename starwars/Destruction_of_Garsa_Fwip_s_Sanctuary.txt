The '''destruction of Garsa Fwip's Sanctuary'''<ref name="Hit It">{{InsiderCite|224|Hit it, Max!}}</ref> was a terrorist [[bomb]]ing conducted by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] within [[Garsa Fwip's Sanctuary]] in [[Mos Espa]]. The Sanctuary was operating at the [[time]], with many patrons and staff inside. The bomb was hidden within a [[camtono]] which the [[Pyke]]s left inside the Sanctuary.<ref name="Chapter 6" /> Musician [[Maximilian Rebo]] survived the attack.<ref name="Hit It" /> Although [[Mayor]] [[Mok Shaiz]] approved of the Pykes operating in Mos Espa to combat [[Boba Fett]] and his [[Fett gotra|gotra]], he did not approve of the bombing. The incident marked the beginning of open warfare between the Fett Gotra and Pykes.<ref name="Chapter 7" />
