'''Alain Mauricet''' (known professionally as simply '''Mauricet''') is a [[Wikipedia:Belgium|Belgian]] comic book writer and artist born in [[1960s|1967]]. He notably worked for [[Wikipedia:Spirou (magazine)|''Le Journal de Spirou'']] for about nine years, and currently works for [[Wikipedia:DC Comics|DC Comics]].<ref name="IconOClash" /> He illustrated the short comic story "[[The Lost Eggs of Livorno]]," which was published as the secondary story of [[Star Wars Adventures Annual 2018|''Star Wars Adventures Annual'' 2018]] on [[April 18]], [[2018]].
