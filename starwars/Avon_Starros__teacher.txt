An '''individual''' who lived during the [[High Republic Era]] was a [[teacher]] of the [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] while she lived on<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> By [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> they had taught Starros about the interrelationship between energy and matter in a mathematics proof known as [[Hyderson's proof]], and at some point after they stopped teaching Starros, as she was sent to the outpost [[Port Haileap]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Haileap]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while Starros was [[Wevo crisis|stranded]] on the Outer Rim [[moon]] [[Wevo]], she told the [[Dalnan]] boy [[Honesty Weft]] about Hyderson's poof and that her teacher had taught her about it. A short time later, Weft questioned the girl how she had ended up at Port Haileap, noting that she had mentioned what her teacher had taught her on Hosnian Prime, which was a long was from Port Haileap.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />
