'''Christopher M. Zucker''', also known as '''Chris Zucker''', is an artist who contributed the book designs for multiple ''[[Star Wars]]'' novels in the former Expanded Universe (now known as [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]]) as well as novels in the current canon. Zucker notably created the [[Sigil of the New Republic|sigil]] of the [[New Republic]] and the patch of [[61st Mobile Infantry|Twilight Company]].<ref name="ZuckerFB">{{Facebook|username=starwarsbooks|url=starwarsbooks/photos/a.414408798712.177165.67519418712/10153636964513713/?type=1&comment_id=10153637037308713&reply_comment_id=10153637099513713&total_comments=4|name=[[Del Rey]]|date=Sept 21, 2015|archivedate=20200819183039}}</ref>
