The '''anti-intrusion club''' was a supposed club in the [[Vocational School for Institutional Security]] in [[Capital City]], [[Lothal]] attended by [[Merei Spanjaf]]. Spanjaf's parents, [[Gandr Spanjaf|Gandr]] and [[Jessa Spanjaf|Jessa]], approved of her [[time]] with this club, feeling it was a better use of her time than playing [[grav-ball]] her riding her [[Speeder bike|jumpspeeder]] with [[Gender|boys]]. Both were unaware that their daughter was, in fact, actually engaged in illicit intrusive activities of her own. Participation in the club was, in fact, a cover by Spanjaf for her illicit activities in aiding her boyfriend [[Zare Leonis]] to discover the truth beyond the disappearance of his sister, [[Dhara Leonis|Dhara]], who had been training to serve the [[Galactic Empire]] at Lothal's [[Academy for Young Imperials]]. Whether any such club actually existed was unknown.<ref name="ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>
