'''Chew-blubber''' was a [[Foodstuff|food]] item served at the [[Canto Casino Hotel]] in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/4-reasons-canto-bight-is-an-essential-read|text=4 Reasons ''Canto Bight'' Is an Essential Read}} states that the events of [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] take place a few [[Standard day|days]] before the events of {{Film|VIII}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the salesbeing [[Kedpin Shoklop]] noticed chew-blubbers among the food served during [[midmeal]] at the Canto Casino Hotel.<ref name="Rules of the Game" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chew-blubber first appeared in the [[2017]] short story "[[Rules of the Game]]," written by [[Saladin Ahmed]] for the novella collection [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']], released as part of the [[Journey to Star Wars: The Last Jedi|Journey to ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'']] multimedia project.<ref name="Rules of the Game" />
