'''Claw''' was a nickname that a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]], son of [[Dariana Frayus]] of [[House Frayus]] of [[Alderaan/Legends|Alderaan]],<ref name="Kingpin" /> took after he turned to crime and became leader of his own gang. He started as a prankster, but then turned to kidnappings, demolitions and armed robberies, which caused his mother to put a [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] on his head.<ref name="TOR" />
