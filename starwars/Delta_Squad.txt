'''Delta Squad''', with its members being known as the '''Deltas''', was a [[Republic Military|military]] [[special forces]] [[squad]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[The galaxy|galactic]] civil war between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The squad consisted of four [[clone commando]]s&mdash; [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Boss (clone commando)|Boss]], second-in-command [[Fixer (clone commando)|Fixer]], [[Scorch]], and [[Sev]]&mdash; who, on one occasion retrieved the bodies of [[Jedi Master]] [[Halsey]] and [[Padawan]] [[Knox]] following the [[Devaron massacre|massacre]] on [[Devaron]] and participated in other dangerous missions during the war.

==History==
===Frontline commandos===
[[File:DeltaSquad-GalacticRepublicUnitCards.png|thumb|left|250px|Delta Squad experienced the some of the toughest battles the Clone Wars had to offer.]]
Entering the [[Clone Wars]] with unparalleled combat training,<ref name="DeltaSquad" /> Delta [[Squad]] consisted of a team of four [[clone commando]]s<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />&mdash;unit leader [[Clone Sergeant]] [[Boss (clone commando)|Boss]], second-in-command [[Fixer (clone commando)|Fixer]], [[Sev]], and [[Scorch]]<ref name="Join the Battle" />&mdash;who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> as part of the [[Special Operations Brigade]].<ref name="HC21" /> They were also overseen by a [[Unidentified Clone Advisor|Clone Advisor]].<ref name="DeltaSquad" /> Experiencing some of the most dangerous missions the war had to offer<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and seeing deployment into its frontlines, with their operations putting each of their skills to the test, Delta Squad served the Republic with absolute loyalty, deadly precession, and overwhelming firepower. Over their service, each member of Delta Squad entered into each and every mission with those goals in mind.<ref name="DeltaSquad" />

Over their service, Delta Squad displayed a tendency to leap into action, rather than waiting for it to come to them.<ref name="DeltaSquad" /> Refusing to pull their punches, with Sev and Scorch going out of their way to ensure the squad did not,<ref name="ScorchSev">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/databank-download-sev-scorch|text=Databank Download – Delta Dossier: Sev & Scorch}}</ref> their tactics included the removal of any and all obstacles present on a battlefield, ensuring the completion of their goals. It was believed that, ultimately, they could conquer any objective no matter what opponent stood against them.<ref name="DeltaSquad" /> At one point, Delta Squad was deployed from a [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunship]] into a battle zone in order to take control of territory.<ref name="Seizing Territory" /> At another point, they were ordered to return to combat by a superior [[officer]], which was repeated by their apologetic advisor.<ref name="DeltaSquad" /> Boss once led Delta Squad into battle in a [[forest]]ed enviroment.<ref name="UnitCard">{{NewLegion|set=Galactic Republic Unit Card Pack|cardname=Clone Commandos &ndash; Delta Squad}}</ref>

===Mission to Devaron===
{{Quote|There were no survivors on Devaron. Everyone was killed, including Master Halsey and his Padawan.|Boss, reporting on the events on Devaron|<ref name="Witches of the Mist" />}}
In the aftermath of the [[Devaron massacre|massacre]] on the [[planet]] [[Devaron]], Delta Squad recovered the bodies of [[Jedi General]] [[Halsey]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Knox]] and brought them back to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> the [[capital]] world of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Coruscant">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref>

[[File:MasterHalseyReturned-WOTM.png|right|thumb|245x245px|The squad speaking with Kenobi, Gallia, Koon, and Tiin]]
Boss reported directly to several members of the [[Jedi High Council]]&mdash;[[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Plo Koon]], [[Adi Gallia]], and [[Saesee Tiin]]&mdash;and informed them that the entire [[Republic Military|military]] force on Devaron had [[Death|perished]] along with Halsey and his apprentice, leading the Council to suspect the emergence of a new threat to the [[Jedi Order]]. Their suspicion was confirmed upon recovering security footage of the battle; a [[hologram]] of Halsey's death revealed his killer to be a [[Nightbrothers|Nightbrother]] of [[Dathomir]],<ref name="Witches of the Mist" /> whom the [[Jedi]] would come to know as [[Savage Opress]].<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref>

===After the war===
Following the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the Clone Wars and the Republic's transformation into the [[Galactic Empire]], Scorch was one of many clone commandos who were tasked with training the new [[soldier]]s of the [[Imperial Army]].<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref> He was also eventually assigned to the [[Advanced Science Division]] with the [[military rank]] of [[Clone Commander]].<ref name="Scorch">{{Databank|clone-commander-scorch|Clone Commander Scorch}}</ref> In [[time]], the new [[troop]]s&mdash;recruited [[stormtrooper]]s&mdash;replaced the clone troopers.<ref name="Relics of the Old Republic">{{Rebels|Relics of the Old Republic}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Delta Squad first appeared in the [[2005]] ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''. During development of "[[Witches of the Mist]]," a [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season-three]] episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] requested that the creative team incorporate Delta Squad into the plot.<ref name="Episode Guide" /> All four commandos' names (but not their identification numbers) were brought into [[canon]] in the episode guide for "Witches of the Mist."<ref name="Original Episode Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode314.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Witches of the Mist}}</ref><ref name="Episode Guide" />

Within ''Republic Commando'', the game's final mission concerns a [[Mission to Kashyyyk (Clone Wars)|mission to Kashyyyk]], during which [[Delta Squad/Legends|Delta Squad]] rescues the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[Chief/Legends|Chieftain]] [[Tarfful/Legends|Tarfful]].<ref name="SWRC">''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''</ref> Within canon, there is a documented [[Rescue of Tarfful|rescue]] of [[Chief]]tain [[Tarfful]] from [[Trandoshan]]s,<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> with ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' establishing that a group of clone commandos carried out the rescue.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> Although this matches the depiction of the Legends rescue, no canon source has confirmed if the commando unit dispatched in canon was Delta Squad.
