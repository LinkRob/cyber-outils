{{Quote|I'm honored to be a Daughter of Ferrix, and honored to be worthy of the stone. Strange, I&hellip; feel as if I can see it. I was six, I think, first time I touched a funerary stone.|Maarva Andor, in a holorecorded speech|<ref name="RR" />}}
A '''funerary stone''' was a symbolic artifact of great importance in the culture of [[Ferrix]]. It was made of the ashes of an upstanding [[Death|deceased]] citizen, such as members of the [[Daughters of Ferrix]], which were then mixed with mortar and local dust to form a solid brick.<ref name="DF" /> The stone was then inscribed with the deceased's name in [[Bazeese script]]<ref name="Script">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e12-trivia-gallery|text="Rix Road" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> as well as the [[time]]s of [[Reproduction|birth]] and death in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] dating system, and it was placed in a wall somewhere in the city after the [[funeral]]. Notable individuals who were "bricked," as locals called it, were [[Marriage|spouses]] [[Clem Andor|Clem]] and [[Maarva Carassi Andor]].<ref name="RR" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="AndorDate">{{AndorDate|112}}</ref> Maarva Andor's funerary stone was used as an impromptu weapon by the [[Grappler (profession)|Grappler]] [[Brasso]] in a [[Ferrix Riot|riot]] that broke out following her [[Maarva's funeral procession|funeral]].<ref name="RR" />
