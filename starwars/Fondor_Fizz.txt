'''Fondor Fizz''' was an [[alcohol]]ic [[cocktail]] served at [[Oga's Cantina]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Foodie" />

==Behind the scenes==
This cocktail was first made available at [[Oga's Cantina]] at [[Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] on [[May 4]]th [[2022]]. It was described as "Toniray Teal Chardonnay, Tito's Vodka, and Joffrey's White Tea with flavors of lemongrass and pea flower citrus."<ref name="Foodie" />
