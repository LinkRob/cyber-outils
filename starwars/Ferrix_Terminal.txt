The '''Ferrix Terminal''' was located on the [[Free Trade sector]] planet [[Ferrix]], next to the [[Ferrix City|town]] and serviced [[HFY-3920 shuttle ferry|shuttle ferry]] arrivals and departures to and from around the [[planet]]. It was staffed by [[Imperial ground crew|ground crew]] and [[staircase droid]]s which assisted [[shuttle]]s with landing and passengers disembarking respectively. It could serivce at least three ferries at a time.<ref name="Andor 3">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>
