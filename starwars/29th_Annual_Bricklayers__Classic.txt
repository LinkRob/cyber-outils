The '''29th Annual Bricklayers' Classic''' was a [[Podracing|podrace]] event that took place on [[Tatooine]]. Following the [[Battle of Hoth]], the [[Freemakers/LEGO|Freemakers]] took part in the race while trying to evade [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]] and his henchmen including [[Dengar]], [[Baash]], and [[Raam]].
