The '''Andurkoss system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Nouane sector]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]], situated in grid square N-8 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Jason Fry]], author of the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix that introduced the Andurkoss system, stated that the system was added as a homeworld for one of the new [[species]] introduced in the [[2023]] [[video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-09.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-09}}</ref>
