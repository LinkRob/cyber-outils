The '''Eiloroseint system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Tokamac sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square N-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Eiloroseint]].<ref name="Origin" />

{{System-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Eiloroseint system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
