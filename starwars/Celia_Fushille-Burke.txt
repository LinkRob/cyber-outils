'''Celia Fushille-Burke''' was the dancer and actress who portrayed [[Greeata Jendowanian]] in the [[The Star Wars Trilogy Special Edition|Special Edition]] of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].

In a [[2024]] interview with "Tales From The Collection," Fushille-Burke shared details about her experience portraying Greeata, highlighting the physical challenges of performing in heavy makeup and prosthetics. She also discussed her background in dance, which influenced her work on ''Star Wars.'' Fushille-Burke has had an extensive career in the performing arts, including directing a dance company in San Francisco and collaborating with prominent choreographers.<ref name="TFTC">{{WebCite|url=https://talesfromthecollection.com/interview-with-celia-fushille-insights-from-a-versatile-performer|text=Interview with Celia Fushille: Insights from a Versatile Performer|date=2024|work=Tales from the Collection|archivedate=20240922040537}}</ref>
