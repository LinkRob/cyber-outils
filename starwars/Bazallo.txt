'''Bazallo''' was a [[Trandoshan]] who was a member of the [[pirate]] gang known as the [[Vile]]. After the previous leader, [[Bobo]], was [[Death|killed]] by [[Riftwalkers]] leader [[Zilastra]], Bazallo claimed leadership of the gang, although his rule was contested by [[Nanna Frouj]], who had claimed a chunk of the Vile.<ref name="The Living Force" />
