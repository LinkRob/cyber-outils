'''Coca-Cola x ''Star Wars'': Refresh Your Galaxy''' was a [[2025]] marketing campaign by [[Coca-Cola]]. The campaign featured 30 limited edition collectible cans and bottles featuring characters from throughout the ''Star Wars'' series. The packaging also features a QR code that when scanned accesses an Augmented Reality experience called the [[Holocreator]]<ref name="Holocreator">{{WebCite|url=https://www.coca-cola.com/us/en/offerings/star-wars/holocreator|text=Create Your Star Wars Hologram|work=[[Wikipedia:Coca-Cola|Coca-Cola]]'s [https://www.coca-cola.com official website]|archivefile=File:CocaCola-Holocreator-Web.png|nolive=1}}</ref> that allows the user to record a "hologram transmission" to share with friends and family.<ref name="SW Insider 233">[[Star Wars Insider 233|''Star Wars Insider'' 233]]</ref>
