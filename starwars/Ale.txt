An '''ale''', also referred to as '''beer''', was any type of [[alcohol]]ic beverage brewed from grains.<ref name="Wine in Dreams">{{StoryCite|story=The Wine in Dreams|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref> There were a number of different types of ale including [[Trandoshan ale]].<ref name="AND" /> During the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Mon Mothma]] drank ale with [[Chief of Intelligence|Chief of Rebel Intelligence]] [[Airen Cracken]] while discussing the scope of the [[Tarkin Initiative]].<ref name="Rebel Files" /> Following the [[Battle of Endor]], the [[cantina]] [[The Alcazar|Alcazar]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] served at least four types of ale along with [[grog]] and [[starfire 'skee]] according to the [[Unidentified bartender (Alcazar)|bartender]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>
