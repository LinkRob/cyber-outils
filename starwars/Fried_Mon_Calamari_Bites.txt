'''Fried Mon Calamari Bites''' were a [[Color|tan-colored]], light and crispy, ring-shaped snack [[Foodstuff|food]]. They came packaged inside a cylindrical container that featured the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] with a yellow speech bubble stating "IT'S A SNACK!" The container also had the descriptor "LIGHT AND CRISPY" and the snack's name on the front. The wording was written in the [[High Galactic]] alphabet.<ref name="Calamari" />
