'''''Baradium Dynasty''''' was a [[holodrama]] that had aired at least sixty episodes by [[3 ABY]].

==Description==
The holodrama had at least two characters&mdash;[[Talesh Makeric]] and [[Diara]]&mdash;as well as an announcer that would catch viewers up on the events of the prior episodes, and its logo depicted the word "[[Baradium]]" in [[Aurebesh]] beneath four geometric spires. The series aired at least sixty episodes<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> 

==Episodes==
===Fifty Eight===
In the recap before the fifty&mdash;eighth episode, Diara professed their love to Talesh, despite the awareness the latter's [[Talesh Makeric's father|father]] had of their relationship. Talesh hesitated, prompting Diara to ask if their [[Diara's mother|mother]] had given Talesh their job promotion. However, Talesh instead revealed that Diara's mother had [[Marriage|proposed]] to them&mdash;and they had accepted.<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Fifty Nine===
In the recap before the fifty&mdash;ninth episode, Talesh informed Diara's mother that he told Diara about their engagement. Diara's mother was excited to hear saying that the wedding can move ahead. Talesh hesitated, worrying that it was moving too fast and didn't want it to be about the [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Diara's mother told Talesh not to worry since it was all in the prenuptial which just needed his [[holo-seal]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

===Sixty===
In the recap before the sixtieth episode, Diara confronted their mother about taking their lover Talesh. Diara's mother told them that they were just marrying him for the credits and that after a [[cycle]] they would divorce Talesh and Diara could get back together with him. Talesh joined the conversation and told them that there had been an accident at the [[refinery]] and his father had been [[death|killed]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
