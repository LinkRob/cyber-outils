A '''field projector''' was a piece of technology used in [[Deflector shield generator|shield generators]] on board [[starship]]s.<ref name="Lost Found" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the [[meknek]] [[Fillik]] discovered that the [[emitter duct]] was not connected to the field projector when carrying out repairs on the shield generators of<ref name="Lost Found" /> the ''[[Fallon]]'', a [[Trade Federation]] [[WD-2550 Whydah-class Yield Conveyer|WD-2550 ''Whydah''-class yield conveyer]].<ref name="Fallon">{{Databank|fallon-freighter|Fallon freighter}}</ref> The meknek [[Verosha Aniseya]] then used the [[PIP droid]] [[Pip]] to begin welding on the ships exterior, but this led to a spike in the pressure gauge and caused a small explosion nearby on the ship's exterior.<ref name="Lost Found" /> A stabilizing field projector was also equipped to [[Sheathipede-class transport shuttle|''Sheathipede''-class transport shuttles]], personal [[transport]] [[shuttle]]s that were also employed by the Trade Federation.<ref name="Ships">{{EncyclopediaCite|Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The field projector was first mentioned in "[[Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)]],"<ref name="Ships" /> a booklet in a series of reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']], published by [[De Agostini]] on [[July 29]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The field projector later appeared in "[[Lost / Found]],"<ref name="Lost Found" /> the first episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', which aired [[June 4]], [[2024]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref>
