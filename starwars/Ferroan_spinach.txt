'''Ferroan spinach''' was a type of [[spinach]]<ref name="FOTJ" /> that shared its name with the [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Ferroan]] [[Species/Legends|species]].<ref name="CSWE">{{CSWECite|I|265|Ferroan}}</ref> Prior to its destruction between [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]] and [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]],<ref name="DexEra">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]'' states that [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]], and ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' places the events of the novel around [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]]. Since ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' states that the Empire was [[Declaration of a New Order|formed]] in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], Dex's Diner was destroyed in either 19 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]] or 18 BBY.</ref> the [[Restaurant/Legends|eatery]] [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] included Ferroan spinach as an optional addition for its [[Gartro egg omelet]] for an additional five [[Decicred/Legends|decicreds]].<ref name="Dex" /> In [[44 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' dates ''[[Fate of the Jedi: Ascension]]'' to [[44 ABY]].</ref> the [[Human/Legends|Humans]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa Solo]], [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], [[Jagged Fel]], [[Jaina Solo Fel|Jaina Solo]], and [[Tahiri Veila]] [[Diet/Legends|ate]] a meal in the Solos' [[Solo safe house|safe house]] on Coruscant that included steamed Ferroan spinach.<ref name="FOTJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ferroan spinach was first mentioned in "[[Dining at Dex's]]," a [[2009]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive article which was written by [[Gregory Walker]] and formatted as an [[in-universe]] menu.<ref name="Dex" /> It later appeared in the novel [[Fate of the Jedi: Ascension|''Ascension'']], written by [[Christie Golden]] and published in [[2011]] as the ninth installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Fate of the Jedi]]'' series.<ref name="FOTJ" />
