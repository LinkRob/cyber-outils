'''Barbara Affonso''' was a model maker and costume accessory specialist who began working at [[Industrial Light & Magic]] in [[1982]].<ref name="MM" /> She worked with the costume department on [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], creating custom headgear for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] dancers, [[Yarna d'al' Gargan]] and [[Oola]].<ref name="SWI 158" /> She continued to work at ILM into the 1990s, working as model project supervisor.<ref name="MM" />
