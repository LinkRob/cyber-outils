The '''Dawnriser Applicator'''<ref name="Hunters" /> was a long-range [[dart gun]]<ref name="Reveal" /> owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Applicator|standard applicator]]. It featured a [[Color|gold]], silver, and dark gray exterior, with white cloth wrapped around part of the stock, as well as a glowing pink core.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dawnriser Applicator was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Skora's Underworld Applicator, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.17.0 update on [[July 18]], [[2023]], during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Crimson Dawnriser]]. Unlike most cosmetic sets in the game which are added at the same time, the Crimson Dawnriser was not added in 0.17.0.<ref name="Hunters" />
