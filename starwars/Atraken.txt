'''Atraken''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> in the [[Atraken system]] in the [[Rolion sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Rich with natural mineral resources, both the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]] wanted control over Atraken during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="OF84">{{GermanFanCite|84|Database}}</ref> which was waged between [[22 BBY|22]] and [[19 BBY]].<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> During the conflict, there was an [[attack on Atraken]] at some point after the [[Battle of Abrion Bridge]]. Although the battle was ultimately a Republic victory, the planet was rendered inhospitable and millions [[Death|died]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />
