'''Avon's journals''' were a series of journals<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> written by the [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] that contained her research on [[crystal]] theory.<ref name="Sana Starros 2" /> At some point after she was [[Attack on Port Haileap|captured]] by the [[Nihil]],<ref name="Mission to Disaster">''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]''</ref> Avon began to realize that there were individuals who would use her and her research for terrible things and became reluctant to share her discoveries. In response, she locked the journals away in a [[data cube]] with a message of how to use it if it was found. Avon eventually went missing and her research untouched. When she was young, [[Thea Starros]], a [[Starros Clan|descendant]] of Avon, discovered the cube and unlocked it, discovering what Avon had left. The data cube was then kept safe for a long [[time]],<ref name="Sana Starros 2" /> until they were taken by the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Phel Starros]], a member of the Starros Clan, when he visited the [[Starros family homestead]] at one point.<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> At some point between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> Thea, [[Aryssha Starros|Aryssha]], and [[Mevera Starros]] planned to steal the journals back from the Empire. After Aryssha was [[Attack on the Starros family homestead|taken]] by the Empire by her [[Marriage|husband]], the [[Imperial officer]] [[Captain]] [[Cerasus Ehllo]], [[Sana Starros]] was informed of the plan by Mevera as they escaped their [[Starros family homestead|ancestral home]].<ref name="Sana Starros 1">[[Sana Starros 1|''Sana Starros'' 1]]</ref>

After they [[Capture of the King's Ransom|captured]] Ehllo's [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[King's Ransom]]'', and retrieved the data cube, Sana viewed the journals. Following this, she destroyed the data cube, to the dismay of [[Deva Lompop]].<ref name="Sana Starros 5">[[Sana Starros 5|''Sana Starros'' 5]]</ref>
