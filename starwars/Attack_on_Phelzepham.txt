As part of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Pacification of the Esuain sector|pacification operations]] in the [[Esuain sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]] around [[2 BBY]], [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Moff]] [[Ahr]] carried out an '''attack''' against the [[Phelzepham insurgents|insurgents]] based on a [[moon]] in the [[sector]], [[Phelzepham]]. Among the [[governor]]'s forces was the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who had been ordered to follow Ahr's orders to the letter by [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] to teach him a lesson. As Vader and the [[stormtrooper]] detachment he led pushed their numerical advantage and were on the verge of victory, Ahr decided to test his hold over Vader by ordering the stormtroopers to withdraw. Although he was suddenly left alone, Vader completed the mission and defeated the insurgents.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|But this bureaucrat ''knows'' his sector, Lord Vader. With his ''knowledge'', we will ''subdue'' it. So in ''my'' service&hellip; you will obey the governor's ''every order''&hellip; to the ''letter''&hellip; until you have ''learned'' your ''lesson''.|Emperor Sheev Palpatine orders Darth Vader to follow Governor Ahr's orders|<ref name="Darth Vader" />}}
During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] developed a reputation as being the [[Emperor's Fist|fist]] of his [[Sith Master]], [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. However, when the [[Galactic Empire]] launched an [[Attack on Namzor|attack]] on the astronomical object of [[Namzor]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Darth Vader" /> around [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Vader disobeyed a direct order from [[Moff]] [[Ahr]] to cease his assault, resulting in both the [[death]]s of the [[Namzor insurgents|insurgents]] he had been targeting and the destruction of the [[coaxium]] stores the Empire was after. As Ahr was the [[Politics|political]] official in charge of<ref name="Darth Vader" /> the [[Esuain sector]],<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Phelzepham system]]</ref> which he and Vader were charged with [[Pacification of the Esuain sector|pacifying]], Palpatine agreed that his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] should not have destroyed the insurgent fortification. As such, Palpatine ordered his apprentice to obey the [[governor]]'s every order to the letter until he had learned a valuable lesson.<ref name="Darth Vader" />

The politician quickly began to test his hold over Vader by throwing him into missions that could have resulted in the [[Sith Lord]] being killed in action. The first instance came when [[Ahr's Venator-class Star Destroyer|Ahr's ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] arrived at another astronomical object in his [[sector]], [[Ahn Krantarium]]. Once there, the governor [[Attack on Ahn Krantarium|deployed]] a force of [[stormtrooper]]s to the surface, only for the fearsome [[droid]]s that had long since overrun the terrain to wipe out the entire patrol. Informed that attacking the droids would be a [[Death|suicidal]] endeavor, Ahr ordered that Vader be sent alone. The Sith Lord ultimately triumphed over the droids and, per his master's orders, continued to show absolute loyalty towards the governor. Afterward, Ahr, Vader, and their forces traveled to the [[moon]] of [[Phelzepham]].<ref name="Darth Vader" />

==The attack==
{{Quote|Hmp. Very good, then.|Governor Ahr greets Darth Vader after his victory|<ref name="Darth Vader" />}}
[[File:AttackOnPhelzepham-AORDarthVader1.png|thumb|left|280px|Darth Vader leads Imperial stormtroopers from the front against the Phelzepham insurgents.]]
Another astronomical object in Ahr's sector, Phelzepham had become home to another group of [[Phelzepham insurgents|anti-Imperial insurgents]]. As part of the pacification operations in the sector, Vader and a force of stormtroopers were dispatched onto the moon's surface from the governor's Venator to engage the group, who had set up at least one anti-[[infantry]] [[turret]] in a rocky area. The attack went well for the [[Imperial Military]]; its forces outnumbered those of the insurgents, and Vader, using [[the Force]] and his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]], led the stormtroopers from the front. As many of the insurgents took cover behind the terrain of the rocky area, Vader and his [[soldier]]s fought on. At least one insurgent and two stormtroopers fell in combat, and the Sith Lord [[Force push|pushed]] back two other insurgents with a [[telekinesis]] as both factions exchanged fire.<ref name="Darth Vader" />

Aboard the [[Star Destroyer]], meanwhile, Ahr was informed by an [[Ahr's subordinate|Imperial officer]] the moon would likely fall to the Empire within the [[Standard hour|hour]]. However, instead of maintaining his numerical advantage, the politician sought to test Vader again and thus ordered all stormtroopers to fall back, leaving the Sith Lord to handle the insurgents on his own. Standing alone, Vader continued the attack despite being suddenly outnumbered and [[Blaster|outgunned]]. Nevertheless, Vader triumphed in the end, although his [[Darth Vader's armor|armor]] was left [[Fire|aflame]]. Ignoring that, Vader returned to the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] of Ahr's [[destroyer]], where the governor accepted the knowledge of the Sith Lord's survival and victory as good news, only to look for another test that was likely to result in the death of the Emperor's Fist.<ref name="Darth Vader" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Ha ha ha! Yesss, Lord Vader&hellip; lesson ''learned''.|Emperor Palpatine senses the death of Governor Ahr|<ref name="Darth Vader" />}}
Ultimately, Ahr ordered for his Star Destroyer to travel into the [[Kankalo Belt Containment Zone]] and ordered for Vader, flying out of the Venator in his [[Vader's starship|personal starship]], to destroy the biggest threat he found within the [[asteroid field]]. Following the governor's order to the letter once again, Vader turned back to the Venator upon finding a [[Unidentified creature (Kankalo Belt Containment Zone)|crustaceous creature]]; as Vader reasoned that it was the governor who had become the biggest threat to him, he returned to the bridge and watched as the creature ripped through the [[viewport]], taking the governor out into the vaccuum of [[realspace]]. Ahr and several other Imperials who were standing close by were killed. Palpatine sensed the murder and judged Vader, who stood tall on the bridge as stormtroopers and [[Imperial officer]]s bowed to him, to have learned his lesson.<ref name="Darth Vader" />
