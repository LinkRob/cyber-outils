The '''Battle of Haruun Kal''' was a battle that took place on the [[planet]] [[Haruun Kal]] in [[19 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]] led her [[Depa Billaba's battalion|battalion]] of [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[clone trooper]]s against enemy forces from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The Separatist [[commander]], [[General]] [[Grievous]], engineered an onslaught that [[Death|claimed the lives]] of ninety percent of Billaba's [[troop]]s, resulting in a Confederate victory. Grievous personally bested Billaba in single [[Lightsaber combat|combat]], and she remained in a coma for the next six [[Standard month|months]] as she healed from her extensive injuries. Eventually, she recovered and received command of a [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|new battalion]] of [[soldier]]s, though her failure at Haruun Kal haunted her for some [[time]].

==The battle==

In [[19 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> during the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="First Blood III" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Force Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> [[Haruun Kal]]<ref name="First Blood III" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> became a [[Ground warfare|battlefield]] when detachments from the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and the [[Confederacy military|military]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] clashed there.<ref name="First Blood III" /> Overseeing the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[infantry]] was [[Jedi General]] [[Depa Billaba]], a member of the [[Jedi High Council]]. Her [[Depa Billaba's battalion|battalion]] of [[clone trooper]]s encountered resistance in the form of the Confederate [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Grievous's forces decimated Billaba's [[troop]]s, causing her [[Battalion|unit]] to suffer ninety percent [[Death|casualties]].<ref name="First Blood I" /> Only four [[soldier]]s survived the carnage: [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Grey (clone trooper)|Grey]]; [[Clone Captain|Captain]] [[Styles]]; [[Sergeant]] [[Soot]]; and [[Corporal]] [[Big-Mouth]].<ref name="First Blood II" />

Grievous, meanwhile, [[Lightsaber duel|engaged]] Billaba in single [[Lightsaber combat|combat]] and defeated her,<ref name="First Blood 5">[[Kanan 11|''Kanan'' 11]]</ref> severely injuring her and rendering her comatose.<ref name="First Blood I" /> Though Grievous failed to confirm her death, the Confederacy eventually learned of her survival and included it in its intelligence files.<ref name="First Blood III" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Am I&hellip;'damaged goods'? I imagine the answer is yes.''"<br />"''But you seem fully recovered.''"<br />"''From physical injury, yes. Nevertheless, the inescapable truth is&hellip;many good men ''died'' under ''my command''. Those deaths are ''my'' responsibility. And I ''fear'' causing more&hellip;which makes my ''usefulness'' as a commander in the field&hellip;''suspect''.|Depa Billaba and Caleb Dume|<ref name="First Blood I" />}}
[[File:DepaBillabaBactaTank-Kanan7.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Billaba remained comatose for six months after her defeat on Haruun Kal.]]
On account of the Republic's catastrophic losses, the Confederate forces claimed victory on Haruun Kal. Billaba was brought back to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the Republic [[capital]] of [[Coruscant]], where she entered medical treatment.<ref name="First Blood I" /> Some of her surviving men, including Styles and Grey, believed that they had failed her at Haruun Kal and swore to never do so again.<ref name="First Blood 5" /> Other troopers, however, came to regard her as "damaged goods" for her failure at Haruun Kal and the losses her command sustained.<ref name="First Blood I" />

Billaba did not emerge from her coma until six [[Standard month|months]] later, at which point her fellow [[Jedi Master]]s evaluated her and allowed her to return to her seat on the [[Jedi]] High Council. Her defeat at Haruun Kal not only tarnished her reputation as a military [[commander]], but it also hindered her confidence as a general and forced her to live with the guilt of losing so many men.<ref name="First Blood I" /> Nevertheless, she helped to thwart a [[Bombing at the Jedi Temple|Separatist attack]] on the Jedi Temple and resumed her military service with a new [[Padawan]], [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]]. Grey, Styles, and the other survivors of Billaba's previous unit became the [[commanding officer]]s of her [[Depa Billaba's second battalion|new battalion]] within the Grand [[Army]].<ref name="First Blood II" /> Despite the fear that had entered many troopers' hearts after Haruun Kal that she was "damaged goods,"<ref name="First Blood I" /> Billaba's command, by the time of the [[Battle of Kaller]] at the war's end, included many soldiers who had served with her for years.<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> Conflict leftover from the Clone Wars on Haruun Kal would culminate in the [[bombardment of Haruun Kal]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Final Order">{{StoryCite|story=The Final Order|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Battle of Haruun Kal was created as part of the backstory for ''[[Star Wars: Kanan]]'', a [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series published by [[Marvel Comics]]. Writer [[Greg Weisman]] developed the details of the battle for the series' second story arc, [[Star Wars: Kanan Book II: First Blood|''First Blood'']]. The battle was initially alluded to in the story arc's [[Kanan 7|first issue]].<ref name="First Blood I" />

The plot point of Depa Billaba entering a coma after a [[Battle of Haruun Kal/Legends|battle on Haruun Kal]] originated in [[Shatterpoint (novel)|''Shatterpoint'']], a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel that was written by author [[Matthew Stover]] and published in [[June]] [[2003]].<ref name="Shatterpoint">[[Shatterpoint (novel)|''Shatterpoint'']]</ref>
