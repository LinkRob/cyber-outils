The '''''Aspiration''''' was an [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]]. During its [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance]] service, the ''Aspiration'' participated in a battle against a [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class Star Destroyer]].

==Characteristics==
The ''Aspiration'', an [[MC75 Star Cruiser]] of the [[MC-series star cruiser|MC series]], had a length of 1,204.44 [[meter]]s (3,951 [[Foot|ft]] 7 in) and a height of 435.56 meters (1,429 ft). At amidships, the [[Star Cruiser]]'s hull was augmented by an [[armor]]ed collar. The collar's dorsal side supported a mast containing a suite for transmission of communications and a [[sensor]] cluster. An outrigger fin holding a [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] pod on its extremity was connected to the collar's ventral portion. At the stern, the ''Aspiration'' was equipped with four [[Gemon-15 sublight ion drive]]s.<ref name="Rogue One The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> The [[starship]] was also armed with at least six starboard [[laser cannon]]s and at least two [[proton torpedo launcher]]s.<ref name="Aspiration" />

==History==
During the ''Aspiration''{{'s}} Alliance career, the ship fought a battle against a [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class Star Destroyer]]. While the [[Star Destroyer]] was off the ''Aspiration''{{'s}} starboard side, the rebel [[cruiser]] engaged it with [[laser]]fire and [[proton torpedo]]es. The ''Aspiration'' scored multiple hits on the Star Destroyer's dorsal starboard hull between amidships and the aft, resulting in several fiery explosions.<ref name="Aspiration" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Aspiration'' was first pictured, albeit unnamed, in "[https://www.fantasyflightgames.com/en/news/2018/1/3/profundity Profundity]", an article published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] on their website on [[January 3]], [[2018]]. The article advertised the [[Profundity Expansion Pack|Profundity ''Expansion Pack'']],<ref name="Profundity">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/1/3/profundity|text=Profundity}}</ref> released on [[February 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Launch the Fleet">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/2/1/launch-the-fleet|text=Launch the Fleet}}</ref> In the expansion pack, the ''Aspiration'' was identified as such on a card of the same name.<ref name="Aspiration" />
