[[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor]] suspected there would be at least six fuel nodules onboard a crashed [[transpo corsair]] that she and her husband [[Clem Andor|Clem]] salvaged on the [[planet]] [[Kenari]] with their droid [[B2EMO]].<ref name="Reckoning" />
