The '''Bogdo Sinkholes''' was a region of the<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bogano|Bogano}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Bogano]], which consisted of a cluster of [[sinkhole]]s. The [[Oggdo Bogdo]], a rare subspecies of the [[oggdo]] [[Species|creature]] with which the region shared its name, inhabited the Bogdo Sinkholes. Also inhabiting the area, was the [[Hair|furry]] [[bogling]] species. During his stay on the planet, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] built a small offshoot of his [[Abandoned Workshop|workshop]] within the Bogdo Sinkholes.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bogdo Sinkholes appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. After the "[[Ordo Eris]]" chapter of the story, the location is one of many where the player may encounter one of the members of the [[Haxion Brood]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
