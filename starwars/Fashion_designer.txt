'''Fashion designer''' was an occupation of which an individual would design or create outfits. During the [[Imperial Era]], traveling to the [[planet]] [[Ghorman]], known for its [[Ghorman twill]], was seen as a rite of passage for fashion designers.<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> 

In [[3 BBY]]<ref name="GhormanDate">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" dates the events of "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]" to [[3 BBY]].</ref> [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] traveled to Ghorman while posing as a [[Coruscanti]] fashion designer named Varian Skye. <ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Fashion designer was first mentioned in "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]]," the fifth episode of the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'' released on [[Disney+]]<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> on [[April 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Trailer News">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-teaser-trailer-key-art-reveal|text=First ''Andor A Star Wars Story'' Teaser Trailer and Poster Revealed}}</ref>
