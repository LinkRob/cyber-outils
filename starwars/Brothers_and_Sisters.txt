"'''Brothers and Sisters'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story featuring [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] that was published in ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual]]''. The story was written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Nick Brokenshire]] and [[Vladamir Popov]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[January 29]], [[2025]].

==Plot summary==
In the [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center]] on [[Coruscant]], [[Baron]] [[Boolan]] spends his days as a [[prisoner]] paying for his crimes against [[the galaxy]] following his attempt to unleash a [[Boolan's virus|virus]] to strip [[the Force]] from individuals. He scrawls at his cell's walls with his overgrown fingernails and thinks about how everyone underestimates him and his genius. 

Some time before, over [[Chenley Prime]], a [[starship]] approaches [[Shingcu Station]]. Within, the [[Fallanassi]] [[bounty hunter]] [[H'Tar]] joins her benefactor, the [[Nazzar]] [[Krrl]], to infiltrate the station and rescue his sister [[Akray]] from Boolan's experimentation on [[Force-sensitive]]s. H'Tar is skeptical of the job's chances of success, but Krrl is set on saving his sister.

The two enter the base, H'Tar wishing the Nazzar would have stayed behind on the ship. Krrl insists upon actively helping to save Akray. Soon the pair encounter a number of Boolan's failed experiments, individuals butchered and maimed with crude [[cybernetics]]. H'Tar reasons that these people were Force-sensitive like herself and Akray. 

H'Tar soon senses Akray nearby, and despite a bad feeling, she rushes ahead to find her. Akray is tied to a crude examination table, Boolan supervising as his assistant, [[Reek (Nihil)|Reek]] prepares to initiate the experimentation. H'Tar and Krrl scope out the situation. H'Tar uses her [[Force power|powers]] as a Fallanassi to infiltrate Reek's mind, though she cannot access Boolan's given his [[psychic dampener]]s. 

In sifting through Reek's mind, H'Tar comes to the grave realization that there are [[Shrikarai|Nameless]] on the station. Recognizing the danger she is in, she informs Krrl that she cannot save his sister. Krrl thanks her for already doing so, and [[stun]]s.

H'Tar wakes up strapped to the table, Boolan and Reek looming over her ready to begin their experiments. Reek informs H'Tar that Krrl had made an offer that if he brought Boolan a bounty hunter strong in the Force that they would trade his sister for them. H'Tar tries to resist, not wanting to become another failed experiments, but Boolan assures her that he has much bigger plans for her. He begins his experiments, shocking her with electricity, monologuing about her becoming the first member of his [[Children of the Storm]], who will help him make the Force free. 

Back in the present, Boolan reflects on the sounds of her screams, along with those of his other Children of the Storm. He has confidence that with them still active in the galaxy, that they will strike at the [[Jedi]] soon and turn the tides of the [[Nihil conflict|war]]. He is ready for the Force to be free and the Jedi to die.
