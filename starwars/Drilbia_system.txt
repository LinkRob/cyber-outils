The '''Drilbia system''' was located in the [[Sullust sector]], a part of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> It was situated in grid square M-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Drilbia [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the "[[Drilbia system/Legends|Drilb system]],"<ref name="Twinkle">{{VoyagesCite|13|Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are}}</ref> in reference to the [[Drilbian wine]] [[Foodstuff/Legends|beverage]],<ref name="Campaign Pack">''[[Star Wars Campaign Pack]]''</ref> was first mentioned in "[[Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star, How I Wonder Where We Are]]," a [[1990]] [[roleplaying game]] source article written by [[Glen Allison]] as a supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and published in the [[Voyages SF 13|thirteenth issue]] of the ''[[Voyages SF]]'' magazine. Since the article was released outside of the [[Lucas Licensing]] process, its canonicity with respect to ''Star Wars'' Legends was never confirmed.<ref name="Twinkle" /> The system was eventually named "Drilbia" in the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and Jason Fry.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
