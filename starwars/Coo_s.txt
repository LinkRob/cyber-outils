'''Coo's''' was a [[Cantina|bar]] and [[restaurant]] in the [[peacekeeping zone]] on the [[planet]] [[Plazir-15]]. An [[Aurebesh]] sign over the entrance stated the business name, next to the restaurant's slogan, "house of [[teppanyaki]]"; while another sign read "BAR". Additional signage was put up in the streets used to attract potential customers. The [[Foodstuff|food]] there was eaten with [[chopstick]]s. When [[Din Djarin]] was chasing a rogue [[B2-series super battle droid|super battle droid]], he ran through the restaurant and tackled the battle droid by jumping through a closed window, making shards of shattered [[glass]] fly everywhere.<ref name="Chapter 22" />
