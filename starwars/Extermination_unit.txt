'''Extermination units''' operated as pest controllers on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Elphrona]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="LOTJ" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> a [[Unidentified claim|claim]] on Elphrona experienced difficulties with a nest of [[chromant]]s. [[Jedi Master]] [[Loden Greatstorm]] of Elphrona's [[Elphrona Outpost|Jedi outpost]] sought to help the claim instead of having them call for an extermination unit, though [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indeera Stokes]] questioned the necessity of his involvement. However, the [[Jedi]] ultimately did not help the claim with their chromant issue due to embarking on a [[Raid on Elphrona|rescue mission]] elsewhere on Elphrona.<ref name="LOTJ" />
