The '''Anselmi–Nautolan treaty''' was a treaty that was supposed to be signed by the [[Anselmi]] and the [[Nautolan]]s on the [[planet]] [[Jaloria]] to end the [[Anselmi–Nautolan war|war]] between the two sides on their [[Glee Anselm|home planet]]. The treaty was interrupted by [[Unidentified thief group|thieves]] who [[Heist of the Hope of Glee Anselm|stole]] the [[Hope of Glee Anselm]].<ref name="Jango Fett 1" />
