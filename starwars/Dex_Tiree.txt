'''Dex Tiree''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[ace]] [[pilot]] from the [[planet]] [[Onderon]] who flew for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. During the battle, Tiree flew a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]] in [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] as [[Gold Two]] and the wingman of [[Jon Vander]].<ref name="USW" /> Tiree's fighter was destroyed after being hit by [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader's]] [[TIE Advanced x1]].<ref name="ANH" />

==Biography==
{{Quote|The guns - they've stopped.|Tiree's last words during the Battle of Yavin|<ref name="ANH" />}}
Dex Tiree was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Onderon]], sometime before [[19 BBY]]. He<ref name="Databank" /> visited [[Alderaan]] with [[Dex Tiree's father|his]] [[Dex Tiree's mother|parents]] as a child, and in [[12 BBY]], his [[parent]]s had a [[Dex Tiree's sister|daughter]]. Some [[time]] later, prior to [[1 BBY]],<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> Tiree joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]], and flew a [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|Y-Wing Starfighter]] as a part of [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]]. He also found and took possession of an [[R5 series astromech droid|R5 series]] [[astromech droid]] that he named "[[R5-T3|Sparks]]," both for a saying he remembered his mother using, and for a [[fire]] the [[droid]] started on a [[moon]] of the [[planet]] [[Utapau]].<ref name="Sparks">{{StoryCite|story=Sparks (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

[[File:DexTiree-BYSP.png|thumb|left|250px|Tiree flying at the Battle of Yavin]]
During the [[Battle of Yavin]], Tiree participated in the Alliance's counterattack on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], flying under the [[callsign]] [[Gold Two]], although his fellow pilots of Gold Squadron attacking the Death Star's [[turbolaser]]s. Dex manages to take out one turbolaser<ref name="Sparks" />. Alongside [[Davish Krail]], Tiree provided cover to [[Captain]] [[Jon Vander|Jon "Dutch" Vander]] when [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]] began the first bombing run on the [[Death Star]]'s [[thermal exhaust port]], located at the end of the [[meridian trench]].<ref name="ANH" />

When Vander ordered his [[squadron]] to activate their [[targeting computer]]s, Tiree confirmed that he'd gotten a lock on the exhaust port. Shortly afterwards, the [[XX-9 heavy turbolaser battery|turbolasers]] stopped firing at them, much to Tiree's confusion. [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] soon caught up to [[Color|Gold]] Squadron in [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|his]] [[TIE Advanced x1]], and both Tiree and Sparks were [[Death|killed]] when their starfighters were shot down from behind by Vader.<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dex Tiree first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. He was played by [[Jeremy Sinden]],<ref name="ANH" /> but was overdubbed by [[Harry Shearer]].<ref name="Cinetropolis">{{WebCite|url=http://cinetropolis.net/interview-with-harry-shearer|text=Exclusive Interview with Harry Shearer|work=Cinetropolis|archivedate=20121009114422}}</ref> He was given the full name of Dex Tiree in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''.<ref name="USW" />

Tiree's helmet prop — or one of a matching design — was later worn by an unnamed pilot<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref> (named [[Habeer Zignean]] in the ''[[Star Wars Legends]]'' continuity)<ref name="Star Wars Insider">{{InsiderCite|180|Rogues Gallery|Rogues Gallery: ''Solo''}}</ref> in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="ESB" />
