The '''Endor Set''' was the name given to a set of two weapons, an [[Arc Caster]] and a [[Pulse Blaster]], utilized by the team of [[Jawa]] brothers known collectively as the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Utooni]] that they could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to their [[Suspicious Set|standard set]]. These bulky weathered [[weapon]]s featured a [[Color|green]], black, blue, gold, and silver color scheme, with various glowing orangish-yellow elements, yellow and red decal designs, and small green leaves protruding from different areas. The Arc Caster also featured a brown handle and a pin on the stock, while the Pulse Blaster possessed rusted components on the barrel.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Set was a "Legendary" rarity "Endor Reclaimers" for the Hunter Utooni's Suspicious Set, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Bantha Brothers]].<ref name="Hunters" />
