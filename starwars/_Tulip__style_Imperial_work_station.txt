A '''"tulip" style Imperial work station''' was a circular post able to monitor and remotely control instrument functions. They were primarily manned by members of a military and were used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Galactic Empire]], and the [[First Order]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:ChristophsisDutyPost.png|thumb|left|300px|Two semi-circled work stations on Christophsis]]
Work stations were raised platforms in a circular or semi-circular shape<ref name="Episode IV" /> surrounded by control boards within [[space station]]s,<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[starship]]s,<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> and ground installations.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> They could remotely monitor and control instruments, such as the [[superlaser]] on the second [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

Sometimes, work stations were wider and lower to the floor, while the number of control boards could vary from four<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> to eight. Typically, the amount of these boards dictated how much of the circle shape was filled out.<ref name="Episode IV" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
Work stations were in use throughout the [[Clone Wars]] and were used by both the Galactic Republic and the Confederacy of Independent Systems. They were in both the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer's]] hyperdrive room<ref name="Victory and Death" /> and brig.<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> The Republic also had a work station inside of the [[Imperial Inspectorate HQ|Republic Center for Military Operations']] detention block control room<ref name="TJWKTM">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref> and two inside of the court chamber.<ref name="TWJ">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> Two were located in a Republic-controlled sector of [[Chaleydonia]] on [[Christophsis]], one of which was used for cover by [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Captain]] [[Rex]] during the [[Battle of Christophsis]] in [[22 BBY]].<ref name="TCW movie">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Additionally, a Republic-controlled [[Ringo Vinda space station|space station]] above [[Ringo Vinda]] contained a work station. In [[19 BBY]], the Separatists took control of the station and the work station was manned by two [[B1-series battle droid]]s until they were destroyed during the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]]<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

[[File:InvisibleHandDutyPost.png|thumb|right|300px|A work station onboard the ''Invisible Hand''.]]
Work stations were located in the [[observation deck]]s of [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnoughts]]. Two were present inside the [[Wizard's Tower]]'s [[Unidentified observation chamber|deck]] on the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' in [[19 BBY]]. They were destroyed when the ship broke in half during the [[Battle of Coruscant]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

===Use by the Empire===
Throughout the [[Imperial Era]], the Empire continued to use work stations. Various Imperial installations contained them, such as the [[Citadel Tower]] of the [[Imperial Center of Military Research]] on [[Scarif]], which had one inside of its [[Scarif vault|vault]],<ref name="Rogue One" /> the [[Imperial Complex (Lothal)|Imperial Complex]] on [[Lothal]], which had four inside of its generator room,<ref name="Family Reunion - and Farewell">{{Rebels|Family Reunion – and Farewell}}</ref> the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex]], which had three inside its [[Command center (Narkina 5)|command center]],<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref> and [[Research Station 9]] on [[Endor]], which had two inside of it.

Additionally, they were present on many of the Empire's ships. Several were located inside the engine rooms of [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]],<ref name="FATG">{{Rebels|Fire Across the Galaxy}}</ref> and at least four were in the reactor rooms of [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruisers]],<ref name="Stealth Strike">{{Rebels|Stealth Strike}}</ref> while two were inside the briefing rooms of [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]]. The latter were manned by [[Imperial officer]]s.<ref name="Star Wars Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>

[[File:DS1_Overbridge-RO.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Two work stations onboard the Overbridge of the first Death Star.]]
Work stations were present on the [[Overbridge (Death Star I)|Overbridge]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]] and were manned by [[Death Star Trooper|Death Star troopers]]. A work station was also located in [[Detention Block AA-23]]<ref name="Episode IV" /> and in [[1 BBY]]<ref name="IV Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> was manned by [[Lieutenant]] [[Shann Childsen]]. These work stations were destroyed when the Death Star exploded during the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

When the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star]] was rebuilt, work stations were located throughout [[Command Sector (North)|Command Center North]].<ref name="Battlefront II" /> Additionally, a work station was also included in its [[Overbridge (Death Star II)|Overbridge]]. In [[4 ABY]], it was manned by [[Jad Bean]]. During the [[Battle of Endor]], Bean was ordered by [[Moff]] [[Tiaan Jerjerrod]] to fire the superlaser at the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Rebel Fleet]], which he did using the work station. Three<ref name="Cutout">[http://www.dorlingkindersley-uk.co.uk/static/html/features/starwars/locations_gallery/index.html Dorling Kindersley: ''Star Wars'' cross-section locations gallery]</ref> work stations were also located in the [[Emperor's Throne Room]] in the [[Emperor's Tower]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The work stations in Command Center North<ref name="Battlefront II" /> and the one in the overbridge were destroyed when the Rebels managed to make the second Death Star explode,<ref name="Episode VI" /> however, the Emperor's Tower was not completely destroyed, with [[Death Star ruins|its wreckage landing]] on [[Kef Bir]], and as such, these work stations survived.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

===The First Order===
The First Order, like its predecessor the Galactic Empire, also utilized work stations, notably aboard the [[space station]] [[Station Theta Black]],<ref name="STB">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref> the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'',<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>, the [[supertanker fuel depot]] the ''[[Titan]]'' in 34 ABY.<ref name="StS">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref> These work stations were manned by [[First Order fleet gunner|First Order fleet engineers]].<ref name="Episode VIII" /><ref name="StS" /> [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]] also contained two inside their hyperdrive generator rooms.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==Behind the scenes==
Work stations first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. In the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity, they were known as "Command sector duty posts", however, in [[canon]], the episode guide to [[Star Wars: Andor|Andor]] Episode 10, [[One Way Out]], names them "Tulip" style Imperial work stations.<ref name="Episode guide">{{SW|url=series/andor/andor-season-1-episode-10-episode-guide|text="One Way Out" Episode Guide &#124; Andor}} {{1stID|"tulip" style Imperial work station}}</ref>
