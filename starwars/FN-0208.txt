'''FN-0208'''<ref name="BF2" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served in the [[First Order]]'s<ref name="BF2" /> [[FN Trooper Corps|FN Corps]]<ref name="FN Corps" /> as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]]. They wore [[First Order stormtrooper armor]]. At some point during the [[First Order-Resistance War]],<ref name="BF2" /> which spanned from [[34 ABY]] to [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> FN-0208 participated in a training program. Due to their adequate performance in the program, FN-0208 was named [[Worker Of The Month]] by the First Order to recognize the [[trooper]]'s achievement. FN-0208 was then rewarded with one [[Standard week|week]] of [[holovid]] access. A [[Hologram|holographic]] announcement of FN-0208's accomplishment, including a picture of them in their [[armor]], was present on multiple tables in a mess hall aboard a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="BF2" />

==Behind the scenes==
FN-0208 appeared in a hologram in the [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' by [[DICE]].
