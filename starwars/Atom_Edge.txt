'''''Atom Edge''''' was a [[freighter]] affiliated with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] in use after the [[Battle of Yavin]]. It was carrying supplies to the [[Tatooine rebel cell|Rebels]] on [[Tatooine]] when it was shot down. The [[Galactic Empire]] arrived at the crashsite first, and set up a trap for the Rebels. When [[Saponza's partner]]'s forces arrived to salvage, the Imperials attacked, although they were defeated. The Rebels then recovered supplies.<ref name="SWC" /><ref name="Starwars.com">In [[Kekit]]'s Databank entry, it is stated that Saponza and his partner played an integral part in the campaign that liberated a Jawa clan from the Tusken Raiders. It also states that the plan was orchestrated by Jennica Pierce of the Rebel Alliance. This scenario does not occur if the player aligns with the Empire, so it can be deduced that Saponza's gang joined the Rebellion.</ref>
