'''Applause''' was a gift and toy company that produced licensed collector's figurines and toys sold in greeting card and comics shops.<ref name="shadows">''[[The Secrets of Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]''</ref>

==History==
Applause produced figures and toys for franchises such as ''[[Sesame Street]]'' and [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]].<ref name="shadows" />

The creative team included senior product manager Marc Bruderer and creative manager Robert Curet.<ref name="shadows">''[[The Secrets of Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]''</ref>

===''Star Wars Collector's Series''===
In the mid-90s Applause had a ''[[Star Wars]]'' license for toys. Seeing the precedent of the previous decades, and the [[Kenner Products|Kenner]] figures, Applause decided to focus on collector dolls with the protagonists in unusual appearances that reminisced scenes of the [[original trilogy]]. In the fall of [[1995]] they produced the ''Star Wars Collector's Series'' which included around 10-inch tall figures of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] with [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] on his back, and [[Han Solo/Legends|Han]] in [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtrooper]] [[Stormtrooper armor/Legends|armor]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station/Legends|Death Star]].<ref name="shadows" />

===''Shadows of the Empire''===
In the mid-90s Applause was one of the licensees of the ''[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'' multimedia project. [[Dave Williams]] was concept artist for these figures and proposed scenes.<ref name="shadows" /> 

For othe project Applause planned to produce two vinyl dolls, for [[Dash Rendar/Legends|Dash Rendar]],<ref>{{Rebelscum|url=soteapccsdash.asp|text=Dash Rendar Vinyl Doll}}</ref> and for [[Xizor/Legends|Xizor]].<ref>{{Rebelscum|url=soteapccsxizor.asp|text=Xizor Vinyl Doll}}</ref><ref name="shadows" /> 

There was a Limited Edition Statuette titled "Villains", featuring a bust of [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] with figures of Xizor and [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], with an embossed figure of a [[TIE/IN_interceptor/Legends|TIE Interceptor]] and Stormtroopers.<ref>{{Rebelscum|url=soteAPstatuette.asp|text=Limited Edition Statuette}}</ref>

Following the premise of the Collector line, there were plans for statuettes with specific scenes from the story, pairing classic with [[Star Wars Legends|original]] characters. Before the design of the statuettes, Dave Williams made storyboards of the relevant scene in order to capture the drama and fine-tune a desired snapshot.<ref name="shadows" /> 

Proposed scenes (but probably never released) included:<ref name="shadows" /> 
*Statue of Dash Rendar
*Statue of Xizor
*"Spirit of Villainy" with Vader and Xizor
*[[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke]] confronting [[Dash Rendar/Legends|Dash]] on [[Kile]] about [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia]] (in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Gall]]).
*The meeting of Darth Vader with Xizor.
*Luke's and Dash's stand against [[Gizz's Gang]] in the [[Beggar's Canyon Incident]].
