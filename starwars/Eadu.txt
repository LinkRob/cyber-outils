'''Eadu''' (pronounced /ˈiːduː/) was a storm-stricken [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It hosted the [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory]] prior to the outbreak of the [[Galactic Civil War]], where [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] and his team of [[scientist]]s attempted to unlock the secrets of the [[kyber crystal]]s as part of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] project. The refinery was [[Mission to Eadu|targeted]] by agents of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] following the [[destruction of Jedha City]] and resulted in the ruination of the facility and the [[death]] of Erso.

==Description==
{{Quote|We know Andor made it to the Eadu star system. Could've been shot down. Could've been shot ''at'', though the beacon's rugged enough to survive a lot of damage. We don't have much intel on Eadu, though, so for all we know the signal's blocked by a high-energy thermosphere&mdash;''"<br />"''How likely is that?''"<br />"''Not likely.|[[Nioma]] and [[Davits Draven]] on Cassian Jeron Andor|<ref name="Rogue One novel" />}}
[[File:Eadu_U-wing.png|thumb|left|250px|Cassian's [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft|U-wing]] flying over Eadu.]]
Eadu was a remote,<ref name="UVG" /> rugged,<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|eadu|Eadu}}</ref> [[mountain]]ous [[planet]] was located in the [[Eadu system]]<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]'<ref name="UVG" /> [[Bheriz sector]].<ref name="RO World Locations" /> It was solidly within [[Hutt Space]] along the [[Triellus Trade Route]] and lay on the edge of the known [[The galaxy|galaxy]].<ref name="Where in the Galaxy">{{SW|url=news/where-in-the-galaxy-are-the-worlds-of-rogue-one|text=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of ''Rogue One''?|int=Where in the Galaxy Are the Worlds of Rogue One?}}</ref> The [[Color|blue]]-hued world was shrouded in gloomy rainstorms, wind,<ref name="UVG" /> and lightning strikes.<ref name="Rogue One" /> The planet's cloud cover was so thick that it appeared to be a night world even in daytime, and clouds mired visibility while gales battered any [[starship]]s attempting to reach the surface.<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> It comprised difficult, rainswept terrain which afforded the planet natural defenses, although the southern hemisphere also contained an [[Eaduan village cluster]] populated by [[nerf herder]]s.<ref name="UVG" /> The ground was uneven and was made up of jagged rock formations, broad mesas, black stone ridges, and narrow spires. The rocky canyons bottomed out in dozens of writhing streambeds. The planet's rain had the smell of fecund soil with an undertone of acrid stink.<ref name="Rogue One novel" />

==History==
{{Quote|You think this pilot acted alone? He was dispatched from the installation on Eadu. Galen Erso's facility.|Wilhuff Tarkin to Orson Callan Krennic|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
[[File:Eadu_Scientific_research_base.png|thumb|right|250px|The Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory.]]
Eadu was little-known save for its rainfall and population of native [[nerf]] herders.<ref name="Databank" /> During the [[Imperial Era|Age of the Empire]], Eadu was owned by [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], and ceded to his eponymous [[Tarkin Initiative]] as part of the ongoing [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] project begun prior to the onset of the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] established a secret [[Eadu Energy Conversion Laboratory|high energy conversion laboratory]] within the remote mountaintops of Eadu's northern hemisphere. This refinery, defended by the [[Eadu Flight Station]], was administered by [[Sirro Argonne]] and guarded by [[stormtrooper]]s of the [[975th Battalion]].<ref name="UVG" /> [[Captain]] [[Magna Tolvan]] was responsible for security on Eadu.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 4">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 4|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 4]]</ref> [[Director]] [[Orson Callan Krennic]] put [[Galen Walton Erso]] to work on unlocking the secrets of the [[kyber crystal]]s within the secret installation. To this end, Erso led a team of scientists who conducted dynamic kyber field experiments south of their facility.<ref name="UVG" /> The world was considered strategically insignificant due to communications blackouts, which made an analysis of the world difficult and was officially designated by the Empire for research and chemical processing. Individuals such as [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] considered it to be a sodden lump of a planet.<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> However, the Empire created its final, functional [[superlaser]] on Eadu, and transported it to the Death Star to make the [[battle station]] fully operational.<ref name="UVG" />

[[File:Mission_to_Eadu_X_Wings.png|thumb|left|250px|[[T-65B X-wing starfighter]]s flying through Eadu's canyons.]]
In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Erso sent out [[pilot]] [[Bodhi Rook]] with a message for [[Saw Gerrera]], informing him of a structural weakness in the Death Star. Krennic learned of the security leak and ventured to Eadu to deal with the Imperial traitor. At the same time, a team of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] agents led by [[Jyn Erso]] and Captain Cassian Jeron Andor arrived on Eadu with the intention of locating Erso. The [[Mission to Eadu|mission]] became complicated after Krennic had Erso's fellow scientists shot in order to draw out a confession, and Rebel [[starfighter]]s bombed the Eadu complex landing platform. This resulted in Galen's death and led Jyn's team on a further [[Battle of Scarif|mission]] to steal the [[Death Star plans]] from [[Scarif]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> Because of the security breach, Tolvan was later sent to [[Yavin 4]] following the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the Death Star, as a punishment duty for the Eadu debacle.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 4" />

==Inhabitants==
[[File:Eadu.png|thumb|right|250px|Eadu's surface]]
Eadu had a relatively small planetary population<ref name="Rogue One novel" /> estimated at two-point-five million, which included native Eaduans such as the nerf herders living in a village cluster in the southern hemisphere.<ref name="UVG" /> These individuals were among the only features of Eadu known to the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]].<ref name="Databank" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of [[Kris Elioup]].<ref name="Dying Light 10">[[Darth Vader (2017) 10|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 10]]</ref>
