'''6L-CH''' was an overstressed<ref name="droid">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> [[Color|green]]-plated [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] [[astromech droid]] in the service of the [[Resistance]]. It was stationed on the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] above the planet [[D'Qar]] shortly after the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]], where it was repaired alongside other BB units.<ref name="Repair Bay" />

==Equipment==
6L-CH had an [[internal orbiculate motivator]] powered by a [[Selenium]] drive. On its body, it featured multiple cicular [[tool-bay disk]]s, surface [[sensor]]s and cooling vents for the motivator. Its head stayed on its body with the use of [[magnet]]ic balancing. On its head it had a receiver [[antenna]].<ref name="Repair Bay" />
