'''Crushgaunts''', also called '''''shuk'orok''''' in [[Mando'a]], were a type of strength-enhancing [[gauntlet]]s created by the [[Mandalorian]]s [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Clone Wars]]. They were made of a reactive, polymer-enhanced material reinforced by [[beskar]] plates. They could increase their wearer's grip strength to the point that they could shatter [[bone]]s, crush [[durasteel]], and deflect weapons, even [[lightsaber]] blades.<ref name="CoR">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref> [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] wore a pair of crushgaunts as part of her [[Phasma's armor|armor]].<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>
