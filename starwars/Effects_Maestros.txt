'''Effects Maestros''' was a recurring series of articles in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'', starting with [[Star Wars Insider 101|issue 101]], that focused on the people who worked on special effects and practical effects for the original trilogy.

*[[Star Wars Insider 101|''Star Wars Insider'' 101]] &mdash; [[Richard Edlund]]: Making the ''Falcon'' Fly
*[[Star Wars Insider 102|''Star Wars Insider'' 102]] &mdash; [[Phil Tippett]]: Creature Creator
*[[Star Wars Insider 103|''Star Wars Insider'' 103]] &mdash; [[John Dykstra]]: Star Warrior
*[[Star Wars Insider 104|''Star Wars Insider'' 104]] &mdash; [[Dennis Muren]]: The Magic of Muren
*[[Star Wars Insider 105|''Star Wars Insider'' 105]] &mdash; [[Ken Ralston]]: Breaking the Mold
