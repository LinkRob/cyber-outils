BT7 “Binder Wing” glider- with variant skins.. Some vehicle design and biome visual development for unreleased projects. [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] would utilize the gliders to fly into arenas from launch areas high above. They were highly customizable, with some variants coming equipped with [[astromech droid]] and potentially even [[laser cannon]]s like the [[KX9 laser cannon|KX9]].<ref name="Young" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Binder Wing Transport was designed for the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', which was developed by [[NaturalMotion]]'s [[BossAlien]]<ref name="SWH" /> and initially published by [[Zynga]] on [[November 16]], [[2021]] as a [[Wikipedia:Soft launch|soft launch]]<ref name="Soft">{{Twitter|ZyngaStarWars|status/1460656552734605317|[[Zynga]] Star Wars|date=2021-11-16|quote=We are excited to announce Star Wars: Hunters is now entering soft launch and is available on Android for players in India, Philippines, Malaysia & Indonesia.|archivedate=20220625084625|archivefile=File:ZyngaStarWars-SoftLaunchDate-Twitter.png}}</ref> before the global release on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Global">{{SWYouTube|4dZcq6m3y40|Star Wars: Hunters Launch Date Reveal Trailer}}</ref> They were designed by concept artist [[Clint Young]] early into the game's production as a part of a planned "battle royale" game mode<ref name="Young" /> similar to the popular video game [[Wikipedia:Fortnite|''Fortnite'']],<ref name="Fortnite">''[[Fortnite]]''</ref> though the concept was eventually scrapped and the BWTs went unused. They were designed to have multiple cosmetic changes, with two initially planned, one based off of an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE Fighter]], and the other was a "Legendary" rarity variant based off of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|X-wing starfighter]], with an "Outer Rim" variant meant to have been determined at a later date.<ref name="Young" />
