[[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] commanded a '''task force''' in the [[Republic Navy]]'s [[Open Circle Fleet|Open Circle Armada]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]].

==Characteristics==
The [[task force]]<ref name="Databank" /> was led by [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]], and trasnported the [[327th Star Corps]], Secura's ground unit that was led by [[Clone Marshal Commander]] [[Bly]].<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> Aayla Secura's task force<ref name="Databank" /> was comprised of three [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], all of which were destroyed during the Battle of Quell, and carried [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s.<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> The [[Liberty (Venator-class)|''Liberty'']] served as the task force's [[flagship]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==History==
Early during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Jedi General]] [[Aayla Secura]] was in command of a [[task force]]<ref name="Databank" /> of [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyers]], including her [[flagship]]<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> [[Liberty (Venator-class)|''Liberty'']].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> While located in the [[Quell system]], the task force participated in the [[Battle of Quell]] against a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval]] contingent of four [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]] led by [[Commander]] [[TF-1726]]. Engaged in [[Quell]]'s [[atmosphere]], the task force lost two ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]]s while Secura's [[flagship]] was critically damaged. As [[B2-RP battle droid|B2-RP super battle droids]] were sent to board the remaining cruiser, [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] was dispatched to rescue Secura and her forces.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

Skywalker's fleet, including the ''[[Resolute]]'', arrived in the midst of the battle. After using [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]] to board Secura's flagship, the surviving [[Galactic Republic]] forces made their way to a [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular''-class cruiser]] that was docked in the ventral [[hangar]]. After a rough escape which resulted in Skywalker being injured and the total destruction of the flagship, the [[cruiser]] was engaged by [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture starfighters]] and accidentally had its [[hyperdrive]] activated, resulting in the group leaving the battle.<ref name="Jedi Crash" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aayla Secura's task force<ref name="Databank" /> first appeared, albeit unidentified, in the [[2009]] ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|season one]] episode "[[Jedi Crash]],"<ref name="Jedi Crash" /> and was identified as a task force in Aayla Secura's [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Databank" /> "[[The Grand Army of the Republic (reference book)|The Grand Army of the Republic]]" encyclopedia incorrectly says Skywalker managed to save much of Secura's task force during the Battle of Quell.<ref name="Grand Army Encyclopedia">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Grand Army of the Republic}}</ref>
