'''Elizabeth Dulau''' is an actor who portrayed [[Kleya Marki]] in the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. ''Andor'' was her first role after she graduated drama school in [[2020]].<ref name="Dulau Vogue" />
