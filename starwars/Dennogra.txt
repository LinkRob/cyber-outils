'''Dennogra''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Thrawn" /> in the [[Tharin sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], connected<ref name="Appendix" /> via the [[Triellus Trade Route]]<ref name="Triellus" /> to [[Junkfort Station]] and [[Taskeed system|Taskeed]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] a regional [[planet hopper]] traveled from Dennogra to the [[planet]] [[Batonn]] landing in the [[Paeragosto City]]. 

During the [[Batonn insurgency]], [[Governor]] [[Arihnda Pryce]] and [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]] [[Gudry]] were transported to Dennogra from Batonn by [[Colonel]] [[Wullf Yularen]] and later took the planet hopper while undercover back to Batonn as part of a plan to scout rebel activity at the [[Creekpath Mining and Refining complex]] on Batonn. The round trip time of their travel to Dennogra and back took less than twelve hours.<ref name="Thrawn" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[shuttle]] to Dennogra was scheduled to depart from [[Hauler's Keep]] in [[Wild Space]] with a number of passengers including [[CV-4X]] and [[Magret Windtester]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Dennogra_web.jpg|thumb|right|Image of Dennogra from the ''Star Wars'' legends comic "The Rebel Thief"]]
Dennogra was first mentioned in the [[canon]] continuity on a galaxy map included in the [[roleplaying game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game]]'' published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] on [[August 4]], [[2016]].<ref name="Force Awakens Beginner">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> Dennogra was first introduced in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity in the [[1997]] comic "[[The Rebel Thief]]" by [[Ryder Windham]]. <ref name="Rebel Thief">{{StoryCite|series=Star Wars Kids (1997)|srtext=''Star Wars Kids'' (1997)|issue1=1|issue2=5|story=The Rebel Thief}}</ref>
