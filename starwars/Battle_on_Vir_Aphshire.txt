During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a '''battle''' was fought in the honey fields of [[Vir Aphshire]] between the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]]. The news of [[Alderaan]]'s [[Destruction of Alderaan|destruction]] was given without hesitation by the 61st's [[Captain]], [[Micha Evon]].<ref name="BTC" />

Near the end of the battle, the 61st got news of the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], and it boosted morale. The 61st defeated the Imperial forces that day, but the [[stormtrooper]]s destroyed the hives to minimize looting from the rebels.<ref name="BTC" />

Afterwards, [[Sergeant]] [[Fektrin]] had a relatively new [[soldier]], [[Private]] [[Hazram Namir]], give the report to the Captain saying everyone would need to meet him eventually. Namir met Evon and was moved by his speech on the 61st.<ref name="BTC" />
