'''Fausto De Martini''' was a senior illustrator for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> De Martini's primary responsibility was updating the design of the [[TIE series|TIE fighter]] from its [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|original]] iconic appearance into a [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|next generation]] version as well as the [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter|two-seated]] fight commandeered by [[Poe Dameron]] and [[Finn]]. He contributed further fine-tuning to the [[X-wing starfighter]] design for the film, developing illustrations by<ref name="TFA Web" /> concept artist<ref name="TFA" /> [[Ryan Church]] to fit<ref name="TFA Web" /> production designer<ref name="TFA" /> [[Darren Gilford]]'s vision. De Martini also explored concept ideas for [[Han Solo]]'s [[Eravana|freighter]], an unused [[starship]] that would have penetrated [[Starkiller Base]]'s [[force field]], and [[snowspeeder]] concepts for an ultimately deleted scene.<ref name="TFA Web" />
