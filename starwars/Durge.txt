'''Durge''' was a fearsome [[Gender|male]] [[Gen'Dai]] [[bounty hunter]] who was active during the [[Clone Wars]] and [[Imperial Era]]. Durge was involved in the war by working for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. He got the title of being unkillable after battling many [[Jedi]] and [[clone trooper]]s. He was a feared legend that even the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] did not want any part of after hearing stories of the Gen'Dai bounty hunter, fearing his own safety.

In [[3 ABY]], during the [[Proclamation of the New Order|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], he was hired to capture [[Ebann Drake]] for weapons smuggling which he later tracked to the abandoned luxury [[starship]] ''[[Opal Empress]]''. Aboard the ship he encountered [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and [[Sana Starros]]. The two parties made a truce after learning that the abandoned ship was infested by the small [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[cymote]]s, though the truce did not last long and Aphra betrayed Durge, ejecting the Gen'Dai into the [[vacuum]] of [[Realspace|space]].

Sometime later after the incident, while lounging at the [[Bounty Hunters Guild Social Club]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Assem]], Durge was hired by the scoundrel [[Khel Tanna]] and the bounty hunter [[T'onga]] to join a [[T'onga's crew|crew]] of lethal individuals to save the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] before losing his memory and what was left of his humanity. The crew later made a deal with the [[Cloning|clone]] bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]] after a [[Skirmish on the Rishi Moon|battle]] that broke out due to a misunderstanding between the two parties. They helped Fett deal with the criminal syndicate [[Black Sun]], in exchange for a lead to help fix Valance. Durge then went to the [[moon]] [[Quantxi]] and fought off the Black Sun forces, along with the rest of the crew. Completing their end of the bargain, Fett fulfilled his and handed T'onga the coordinates of the cyborg fixer [[Tarr Kligson]].

Fett's lead led the crew to [[Haven (droid outpost)|the Haven]], a [[droid]] outpost orbiting [[Kligson's Moon]]. Upon landing on the outpost, they were greeted by the droid [[Telemak]]. T'onga asked for Telemak to help Valance. The droid obliged to her request, sending Valance to meet Kligson for his repairs.

==Biography==
===Clone Wars===
{{Quote|You see, long ago, during the Clone Wars, there was talk of an enormous armored bounty hunter working for the Separatists.|Hondo Ohnaka, on the rumors of Durge's involvement during the Clone Wars|<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters" />}}
[[File:Durge-SWZ97.png|thumb|left|300px|Durge piloting his ''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighter.]]
Durge was a notorious<ref name="Aphra 11" /> [[Gen'Dai]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> [[bounty hunter]] who was known for being difficult to kill.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> Durge was active during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Launchpad 203">{{InsiderCite|203|Launchpad}}</ref> between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="The Clone Wars (film)">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Durge worked with the Confederacy, and at one point, he piloted a [[Rogue-class Porax-38 starfighter|''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighter]] over a [[Color|green]] astronomical object.<ref name="Transmissions">{{AtomicMassGames|url=transmission/hot-shots-aces-ii-separatist-overview|text=Hot Shots and Aces II Separatist Overview|date=2022-11-22}}</ref>

Durge's reputation grew over the course of the war; there was talk of the bounty hunter as if he was virtually unkillable despite the efforts of [[Jedi]] and [[clone trooper]]s. The [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] encountered individuals who had heard the tales but had never seen the hunter with their own eyes. The tales spread and Durge's name became feared on countless worlds.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters" />

===Imperial Era===
====Double-crossed====
{{Quote|Bad news though. They're supposed to be ''extremely extinct''.''"<br />"''Don't worry. I can ''fix'' that.''"<br />"''Not sure you can, actually. They're notoriously hard to kill.''"<br />"''So am ''I''.|Chelli Lona Aphra and Durge, on cymotes|<ref name="Aphra 11" />}}
[[File:AphraSanaDurge-2020DoctorAphra11.png|thumb|right|270px|Durge encounters Doctor Chelli Lona Aphra and Sana Starros during his search for Ebann Drake.]]
Durge's next known action came<ref name="Launchpad 203" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 11|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 11]], and therefore Durge's appearance within, to [[3 ABY]].</ref> by which point the Clone Wars had been over for over two [[Standard decade|decades]]. With the defeat of the CIS and [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformation of the Republic]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]], the galaxy had entered into the [[Imperial Era]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> during which time Durge continued his work as a bounty hunter.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> Durge's 3 ABY mission<ref name="Doctor Aphra 11 Date" /> began when he was hired to capture [[Ebann Drake]], who was wanted in two different systems for weapons smuggling and murder.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> Traveling aboard his [[starship]]<ref name="Aphra 10">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 10]]</ref> in pursuit of his target, Durge tracked Drake to the abandoned<ref name="Aphra 11" /> luxury [[starship]]<ref name="Aphra 10" /> ''[[Opal Empress]]''. However, the ship was filled with corpses, including Drake's. While exploring, he also met the rogue archeologist [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] and smuggler [[Sana Starros]]. When Durge threatened them both, Aphra explained that she and Starros had not killed any of the people aboard.<ref name="Aphra 11" />

The three of them were then attacked by a swarm of [[Parasite|parasitic]] [[cymote]]s, which chased them throughout the ship. As Durge fought the parasites, Aphra remembered that cymotes were sensitive to atmospheric pressure changes, which gave them the idea to lure the creatures to an [[airlock]] and jettison them from the ship. Durge aided in their plan by leading the cymotes to one of the ship's airlocks. Once he entered the airlock with the parasites, Aphra closed the airlock on him and jettisoned him into space along with the cymotes, which exploded in the [[vacuum]] of space. Though Durge did not pursue Aphra or Starros, he remained alive.<ref name="Aphra 11" />

====Saving Valance====
=====Hired muscle=====
{{Quote|My&hellip; beautiful&hellip; club&hellip;''"<br />"''Sorry.|A club owner and Durge|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />}}
[[File:DurgeAndDeathstick-BountyHunters35.png|thumb|left|250px|Durge and Deathstick forming a truce]]
Sometime later between 3 ABY and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35 Date">The events of ''Bounty Hunters'' 35 take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> Durge spectated two [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]]s fight in a small arena along with the [[assassin]] [[Deathstick]] at the [[Bounty Hunters Guild Social Club]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Assem]]. As they watch the fight, a brawl started within the club and Durge formed a truce with Deathstick while they fight other bounty hunters. After the brawl concluded, Durge apologized to the [[Unidentified Boosodian club owner|club owner]] for the mess. Seeing a potential, the scoundrel [[Khel Tanna]] hired Durge and Deathstick to join the bounty hunter [[T'onga]] and her [[T'onga's crew|crew]] in an effort to save the [[cyborg]] [[Beilert Valance]] from losing his memory and what was left of his humanity after<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> the [[Imperial Special Forces]]<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> [[Inferno Squad]] did a [[memory wipe]] on him under the order of [[Lieutenant]] [[Jyala Haydenn]] to protect classified Imperial information.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 34">[[Bounty Hunters 34|''Bounty Hunters'' 34]]</ref>

Their first job was to track down the lethal bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]]. Fett's head was recently put on a big bounty by the criminal syndicate [[Black Sun]], making him an even more dangerous target to confront than usual. Other crew members joining in for the job were the [[Trandoshan]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk]], the [[Changeling|shapeshifting]] [[Clawdite]] [[Catak]], and the [[Gand]] [[Findsman]] [[Zuckuss]], along with his [[droid]] friend [[4-LOM]]. Durge and the rest of the crew then went aboard<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> Tanna's [[light freighter]]<ref name="Han Solo & Chewbacca 5">[[Han Solo & Chewbacca 5|''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' 5]]</ref> ''[[Enigma Catalyst]]'' and start their journey to track down Fett. While on the ship, Durge saw Bossk and Catak getting into an argument after the latter shapshifted into one of Valance's past lover [[Yuralla Vega]] to mock the cyborg, though the giant Gen'Dai knocked both of them down after getting annoyed by the loud noise they made. The commotion prompted a sarcastic remark by Deathstick about the crew.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />

[[File:DurgeThrows-BountyHunters35.png|thumb|right|275px|Durge throws a piece of scrap at Boba Fett]]
Soon after, they arrived on the [[Rishi Moon]] to [[Skirmish on the Rishi Moon|ambush]] Fett. He was resting in the wreckage of the<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> Republic outpost<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> [[Rishi Station]], preparing to fight whoever came to hunt him down. T'onga carefully approached Fett and gave him his first and final warning to stand down, which he denied. The crew of bounty hunters and assassins started to open fire at Fett to take him down, though Fett was able to hold himself up. Durge threw a big piece of scrap which hit Fett's [[Z-6 jetpack|jetpack]] and caused him to fall. Durge continued running toward the fallen bounty hunter and kicked him aside, though Fett recovered and shot a [[missile]] at Durge. He dodged the missle initially, but had not realize that the projectile turned, hitting him on the back and knocking him down. Fett handled other bounty hunters and assassins quiet efficiently, although he was later caught a knifepoint by T'onga. Rather than [[Death|killing]] Fett, she threw a small pouch of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to hire the wanted bounty hunter. Fett accepted the offer, though he also offered a [[Mission to Quantxi|job]] for the crew regarding the bounty still currently on his head.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />

=====Black Sun kerfuffle=====
{{Quote|Taking too long. My turn.|Durge, shooting his way through with an E-Web|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36">[[Bounty Hunters 36|''Bounty Hunters'' 36]]</ref>}}
[[File:TongasCrewBoba-BountyHunters36.png|thumb|left|300px|Durge and the rest of the crew as they land on the junk moon Quantxi.]]
Soon after, Durge and the rest of the crew went aboard Fett's starship ''[[Slave I]]'', while Catak stayed in the ''Enigma Catalyst'' for extraction. While on the way to the junk [[moon]] [[Quantxi]], Fett asked Valance if he remembered him, though the cyborg struggled to remember. Valance then asked if they were friends and Fett corrected him that they were acquaintances. Deathstick disrupted the conversation to tell them about their arrival on the moon. As they land on the junk moon, a [[regiment]] of Black Sun [[gangster]]s surrounded ''Slave I'' and awaited their arrival to collect Fett. Before going out to meet with the Black Sun, Fett reminded T'onga of their agreements and to uphold her part of the bargain. Bossk taunted Fett about double-crossing the wanted bounty hunter, to which Fett responded by headbutting the Trandosan aside.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

Durge and the rest of the crew walked out with Fett locked in [[stuncuffs]] to be handed to the Black Sun. Delighted to see Fett apprehended, the [[Falleen]] [[Lord]] [[Xanak Grunseit]] electrocuted him with a small [[stunner]] which made Deathstick unsure of who to root for. Before Lord Grunseit tortured Fett any further, T'onga stopped him and reminded the terms of their agreement by completing the payment before doing anything else to Fett. Lord Grunseit apologized and asked for the bounty hunters to disarm all of their weapons due to the highly explosive gas leaks from the junk piles throughout the area. 4-LOM and Zuckuss shared some concerns of it being a trap with 4-LOM not scanning any such gases. T'onga agreed with their concerns too, though she still complied with the order nonetheless. Grunseit then proceeded to walk and led the others. As they walk, the Falleen admitted that he put Fett's head on the bounty to protect himself from the feared bounty hunter after a [[death mark]] was placed by the [[Hutt Clan]]. Grunseit added that he was the great nephew of the most revered Black Sun leader [[Xomit Grunseit]] and the cousin to the [[Xanak Grunseit's cousin|current leader]] of the criminal syndicate.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

They were led to a facility by Grunseit, expected to be paid for the completed bounty per their agreement. Unfortunately, the bounty hunters were surrounded by Grunseit's forces shortly after entering the facility. The Falleen told them that he needed the credits to back his claim in the criminal syndicate, betraying the deal initially agreed upon with T'onga. While Durge and others were held at gunpoint by Grunseit's forces, he attempted to remove Fett's [[helmet]], wanting to stab him in the [[eye]] with a [[dagger]]. Fett evaded the attempt and admitted that he was not working for the Hutts but the leader of Black Sun itself before throwing a small ball of deadly fast-acting [[neurotoxin]] [[Syntox]] at the Falleen's face; immediately killing Grunseit in the process.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

[[File:DurgeRampage-BountyHunters36.jpg|thumb|right|210px|Durge opens fire with an E-Web]]
Grunseit's death caused a massive shoot-out between the crew and the Black Sun. Valance immediately opened fire by shooting his [[palm blaster]], incinerating some of the enemy forces. Durge, annoyed by the loud noise; rampaged his way through and stole an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon]]. As they kept fighting, the crew was slowly getting overwhelmed by the enemy forces. The Gen'Dai started to become impatient and shot his way through with the [[heavy repeating blaster]], almost immediately finishing the battle. With the battle over, Durge and the crew fulfilled their end of the bargain. Fett then fulfilled his end of the bargain by giving the coordinates to the cyborg fixer [[Tarr Kligson]]. Fett told T'onga that he got the information from the only [[Jango Fett|person]] he trusted and promptly left with the ''Slave I''. Tanna then called Catak for extraction of the crew.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

=====A dubious lead=====
{{Dialogue|Bossk|It'sssss been ''hours''. How much ''longer'' do we have to ssssit and wait?|Durge|Boring.|Deathstick|Bossk, if you keep whining, I'll cut off the parts that Boba Fett left intact.|Bossk|Hey!|attr=Bossk, Durge, and Deathstick, waiting on Beilert Valance|src=<ref name="Boutny Hunters 37">[[Bounty Hunters 37|''Bounty Hunters'' 37]]</ref>}}
Shortly after, Durge and the rest of the crew went back to the ''Enigma Catslyst'' to follow a lead provided by Fett. Before the ship departed, Valance's condition worsened as his memory continued to fade away. Lost in his memory, Valance was awaken by a concerned 4-LOM, though the cyborg told him that he is fine. Durge waited at the back of the ship as Tanna and T'onga figure out the lead. Unsure about Fett's promise, Tanna questioned where he got the lead about Kligson to begin with. Unbeknownst to Tanna and the rest of the crew, Kligson was an old friend of Fett's own [[Parent|father]], [[Jango Fett]]. Following the lead, they were led to an empty [[Kligson's Moon|remote moon]]. With his ability, Zuckuss sensed a presence out there, with 4-LOM scanning an orbital body hidden by a [[distortion field]]. Tanna realized that the lead was not only coordinates, but also a code. She then sent the code, which revealed a [[space station]].<ref name="Boutny Hunters 37" />

[[File:TongasCrewHaven-BountyHunters37.png|275px|thumb|left|The crew arrives on the Haven.]]
Landing on the mysterious space station, Durge and the crew proceeded to walk out and was greeted by the droid [[Telemak]], who claimed to be the assistant of the guardian of [[Haven (droid outpost)|the Haven]]. The droid asked how they managed to arrive at the station. In response, T'onga told them about Fett. Fascinated by the cyborg, Telemak brought Valance into the station for treatment while the rest of the crew waited outside. As hours went by, Bossk and Durge grew impatient and complained, which prompted a threat from Deathstick toward the Trandoshan. While Durge continues to wait, T'onga sent Zuckuss and 4-LOM to inspect the station further.<ref name="Boutny Hunters 37" />

Later, while T'onga argued with Telemak about how Valance still had not returned, Deathstick commented to Durge that they would be blasting their way out of the Haven soon, which he agreed with. Unbeknownst to them, Telemak was being remotely controlled by Tarr Kligson, who had also wiped Valance's memory. The droid soon deactivated, and Bossk complained that he was losing his patience. Deathstick recommended that they turn Telemak back on and find out what was in their memory bank, and Durge slammed a finger into the droid's head, knocking it off. He loomed menacingly over Catak when the Clawdite reprimanded him for his impulsive actions.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 38">[[Bounty Hunters 38|''Bounty Hunters'' 38]]</ref>

====Valance lost====
{{Quote|Step aside&hellip;It's'' my ''turn.|Durge, to T'onga|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39">[[Bounty Hunters 39|''Bounty Hunters'' 39]]</ref>}}
[[File:ValanceKillsCatak-BountyHunters38.png|275px|thumb|right|Beilert Valance kills Catak]]
Eventually, Valance walked into the hangar, accompanied by Kligson himself. His face had a blank expression, as the presumed cyborg fixer had taken control of him. In response, Catak attempted to calm him down by impersonating Yuralla Vega, though Valance disintegrated their head with his palm blaster, killing the Clawdite in an instant.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 38" /> Shocked by Valance's action, T'onga stepped in front of the cyborg, convincing him of his humanity. Similarly, Valance gave only a blank emotion, about to strike T'onga, which Zuckuss disrupted. The Gand then tried to take the cyborg down, soon followed by Bossk, though their attempt failed. With his last bit of humanity gone, Kligson pronounced that Valance was a perfect killing machine, preparing the cyborg as an empty vessel for a [[Scourge|higher consciousness]]. Deathstick also attempted to strike Valance down; to her surprise, the cyborg moved faster than he ever was before, striking the assassin down instead.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" />

T'onga once again stood against the lost cyborg, trying to convince him to stop, though she was interrupted by Durge, who wanted to take a shot at taking Valance down too. The Gen'Dai fought the cyborg, though similarly lost that fight, getting pierced and embedded in a wall by a long metal rod. Durge claimed that the fight was not over, though Valance ignored him, leaving the hangar with Kligson. Droid forces soon came, trying to take the crew down. Annoyed, Durge took down a droid and pulled the piercing rod from his abdomen. As Kligson and the others were about to leave the outpost, he was betrayed and left behind, getting caught by Durge and the rest of the crew. When asked by Deathstick if they failed, T'onga stated otherwise, to which Tanna objected and asked for her to come to terms with the loss of Valance. In anger, T'onga clarified that their goal was no longer about saving Valance, but killing him instead.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" />

[[File:DurgeHoldsZuckuss-BountyHunters40.png|235px|thumb|left|Durge betrays T'onga]]
Following their failure to apprehend Valance and his departure, Durge and the rest of the crew received the cyborg's last known whereabouts from Telemak. They continued to go after the cyborg and headed to the tech-based world [[Epikonia]], though the uncertainty and risk of the mission caused some arguments between them. As they approached the planet, T'onga told the rest that they needed to move swiftly to keep the element of surprise. However, looking from above, they realized that the city they were about to approach had been taken over by droids. Shortly after they landed, T'onga stated that the mission remained unchanged.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 40" />

With his ability, Zuckuss sensed that something was wrong, before getting struck from behind by Durge. Shocked by the Gen'Dai's betrayal, T'onga tried to pull out a blaster, though she was stopped by Deathstick. Durge and the others had secretly joined [[Khel Tanna's crew|forces]] with Tanna, overthrowing T'onga's leadership. Staying loyal to T'onga, Zuckuss was then thrown out of the ''Enigma Catalyst'', with T'onga being thrown right after. Durge and the rest of the crew left the two for dead on the overtaken planet.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 40" /> In the end, however, the pair would be rescued by T'onga's wife [[Losha Tarkon]] and her former enemy [[Vukorah]], with Valance's memories up to the [[Attack on the Executor|Attack on the ''Executor'']] even being restored thanks to data supplied by former [[Imperial officer]] [[Jyala Haydenn]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 41">[[Bounty Hunters 41|''Bounty Hunters'' 41]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Too loud.|Durge, as he knocks down both Bossk and Catak|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />}}
Infamous<ref name="Launchpad 203" /> and fearsome in nature, Durge worked as a bounty hunter<ref name="Aphra 11" /> on his own time.<ref name="Transmissions" /> Aphra deduced he was expensive to hire. He enjoyed his reputation of being hard to kill, openly mentioning it to Aphra and Starros shortly after meeting them, and was more than willing to strike down his enemies, even if it meant bringing about the extinction of a [[species]]. All the same, based on her experience with expensive-to-hire hunters, Aphra reasoned that he was not the type of person to senselessly slaughter an entire ship if he was only in pursuit of a single target, noting how such slaughter would waste the hunter's time.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> He had a calm demeanor and only spoke when he needed to, or was provoked.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> Despite his massive armament,<ref name="Aphra 11" /> Durge disliked loud noises.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" /> He was also significantly taller than Bossk's height of 1.90 [[meter]]s.<ref name="Height" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|You two are clearly the most competent ones here. You're hired.|Khel Tanna, hiring Durge and Deathstick|<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" />}}
Similar to others of his species, Durge had regenerative properties that allowed him to take damage that other species would consider fatal, such as being pierced through the [[Stomach|abdomen]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 39" /> As his profession suggested, Durge was skilled in using a variety of weapons.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> As a Gen'Dai [[Soldier|warrior]], Durge had a large stature and incredible strength. Being able to handle a plethora of enemies surrounding him with no issue<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> and carried large [[cannon]]s like the E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon as if it was a [[Z-6 rotary blaster cannon]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" />

==Equipment==

Durge wore a suit of powerful [[Color|silver]] [[armor]] with blue and red markings,<ref name="Aphra 11" /> being able to withstand blaster fires without leaving a mark.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" /> He also wore a helmet fully covering his face. He was armed with a large, multi-[[barrel]]ed [[rifle]], which alone convinced Aphra that his rate was expensive, a [[bola]], and other variety of armament on his armor.<ref name="Aphra 11" /> In addition to his large armament, the Gen'Dai was very resourceful with his surroundings,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35" /> with Durge once even picking up and wielding an E-Web heavy repeating blaster cannon as a hand-held blaster.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 36" /> During the Clone Wars, he flew a ''Rogue''-class Porax-38 starfighter.<ref name="Transmissions" /> He also piloted a starship during the Imperial Era.<ref name="Aphra 10" />

==Behind the scenes==
===Conception and development===
Durge first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[Doctor Aphra (2020) 10|tenth issue]] of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2020)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']] written by [[Alyssa Wong]] and pencilled by [[Ray-Anthony Height]].<ref name="Aphra 10" />

[[Durge/Legends|Durge]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[Republic 51|fifty-first issue]] of the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'', written by [[W. Haden Blackman]], illustrated by [[Tomás Giorello]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[March 19]], [[2003]].<ref name="Republic 51">[[Republic 51|''Republic'' 51]]</ref> He was created by [[Lucasfilm]] as part of the [[Clone Wars multimedia project|''Clone Wars'' multimedia project]] to be a new and memorable [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] antagonist who could challenge the [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]], designed by the [[Skywalker Ranch]] Art Department and named by Lucasfilm employees, whereas Blackman wrote his backstory and personality.<ref name="Blackman and Ching">{{WebCite|url=http://www.silverbulletcomicbooks.com/features/105137402377985.htm|text=A Conversation with Haden Blackman and Brian Ching|author=Jozic, Mike|work=[http://www.silverbulletcomicbooks.com SBC]|archivedate=20030507020701}}</ref>

===''The Clone Wars'' concept===
[[File:Durge-TCW.jpg|thumb|right|150px|An unused Durge design developed for ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']]
Early in the development of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated series, ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]] mandated that there would be no bounty hunters, as the series was to focus solely on the conflict and those swept up in it.<ref name="The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''</ref> However, as work on the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] progressed, there came a role that needed to be filled by a bounty hunter. Initially, Supervising Director [[Dave Filoni]] and writer [[Henry Gilroy]] explored incorporating Durge into the series. He was reimagined as a [[Human/Legends|Human]] with elaborate body armor and gadgets, with his more outlandish [[Alien/Legends|alien]] features being excised<ref name="DB Cad Bane">{{DB|character|cadbane|Bane, Cad}}</ref> due to budget constraints.<ref name="Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates">{{SWYouTube|85Jw-63YNYI|Star Wars Celebration Orlando 2017: Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates}}</ref>

This was short-lived, however,<ref name="DB Cad Bane" /> as Filoni preferred creating new characters for the series<ref name="The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars" /> and believed that the bounty hunter character should not be called Durge if they were unable to replicate the [[Changeling/Legends|shapeshifting]] abilities he had displayed in the earlier ''Clone Wars'' multimedia project.<ref name="Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates" /> The next iteration of the bounty hunter character,<ref name="DB Cad Bane" /> named Nom Kahbah by Gilroy,<ref name="Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates" /> retained Durge's all-concealing armor. However, the lines were smoothed out to resemble something from [[Wikipedia:World War II|World War II–era]] [[Wikipedia:Nazi Germany|Germany]].<ref name="DB Cad Bane" />

After seeing concept art of Kahbah,<ref name="Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates" /> George Lucas suggested the character instead draw from iconic characters from the [[Wikipedia:Spaghetti Western|Spaghetti Westerns]] that inspired the [[Original trilogy|original]] ''Star Wars'' films, which ultimately led to the creation of the [[Duros]] bounty hunter [[Cad Bane]].<ref name="DB Cad Bane" />
