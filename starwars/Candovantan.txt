'''Candovantans,''' or '''Candovants,''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] from the [[planet]] [[Candovant]] recognized by their bulbous, [[Hair|hairless]] heads and [[Color|brown]], tan, and red skin. The [[New Republic]] [[senator]] [[Zygli Bruss]] was a member of the species, as was the [[Resistance]] [[pilot]] [[Zizi Tlo]]. They were considered by some to be a powerful force within the political landscape of the galaxy at one point in time. In Candovantan culture it was considered a grave offense to refuse a [[Foodstuff|drink]] when offered.

==Biology and appearance==
Candovantans were a bipedal [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] distinguished by skin that was [[Color|brown]], tan, and red in coloration, with all three colors being present around their face. Being humanoids, Candovantans possessed two [[limb|legs]] and two arms, each ending in five-fingered hands. Their heads were large and round, with flattened faces featuring small [[mouth]]s and dark [[eye]]s capable of perceiving the [[ultraviolet]] spectrum, though they lacked visible [[Nose|nostrils]] or [[ear]]s.<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Society and culture==
Candovantans,<ref name="TLJ Junior novel" /> also known as Candovants, were known and present within the wider galaxy<ref name="Dooku" /> by at least [[23 BBY]].<ref name="Dragonfire Date" /> Some members of the species held prestigious political roles such as [[ambassador]]s<ref name="Dooku" /> or [[senator]]s.<ref name="TFAVD" /> According to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] of [[Serenno]], refusing a [[Foodstuff|drink]] from a Candovantan was seen as a grave dishonor within Candovantan society.<ref name="Dooku" />

==History==
The Candovantans<ref name="TLJ Junior novel" /> hailed from the [[planet]] [[Candovant]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> located within the [[Candovant system]], which itself was situated within the [[Colonies]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> According to [[Jedi Master]] [[Gretz Droom]]<ref name="Dooku" /> around [[58 BBY]],<ref name="Dragonfire Date">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'' establishes that the forty-eighth annual [[Dragonfire Air Rally]] took place shortly before [[Dooku]] accepted a seat on the [[Jedi High Council]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to around [[58 BBY]].</ref> the Candovantans were a powerful force in galactic [[politics]].<ref name="Dooku" />

==Candovantans in the galaxy==
Around [[58 BBY]],<ref name="Dragonfire Date" /> a [[Unidentified Candovant ambassador|Candovantan ambassador]] invited the [[Jedi]] [[Jor Aerith]] to the [[Forty-eighth annual Dragonfire Air Rally|forty-eighth]] [[Standard year|annual]] [[Dragonfire Air Rally]] hosted on the planet [[Coruscant]], an event that drew attention from across [[the galaxy]], and at which it was considered advantageous for Jedi to be seen. Alongside Jor, the ambassador also requested the presence of [[Jedi Order]] members Dooku, [[Qui-Gon Jinn]], and Gretz Droom, specifically to his personal [[Hoversled|hoverpod]], which supposedly offered the best view of the race.<ref name="Dooku" />

Speaking in his [[Candovantan (language)|native language]], the ambassador offered a drink to Qui-Gon, who refused. Dooku instead accepted on Qui-Gon’s behalf, revealing that it was a grave dishonor to refuse a drink from a Candovantan, and that wars had been started over less. Upon learning Dooku's origins as a [[Serennian]], a heritage he shared with the Dragon Air Rally [[pilot|racer]] [[Ramil]] who was, in fact, the Jedi's brother, the ambassador remarked that he knew who Dooku would be rooting for during the race, though Dooku assured him that he supported all of the racers. During the competition, Ramil would crash and be injured, only saved via intervention from his brother. When Dooku left the group to aid his brother, Qui-Gon asked the ambassador if they could borrow a [[speeder]]. The Candovantan, in turn, inquired where the Jedi were going, and Qui-Gon, apparently not understanding the language, interpreted the question instead as consent to go ahead and take one.<ref name="Dooku" />

In [[5 ABY]]<ref name="AndorKassaDate">{{AndorDate|101}}</ref>, a Candovantan was present on the planet [[Ferrix]] when [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] returned to [[Ferrix City]] speak with his friend [[Brasso]] at his [[Grappler (profession)|job]] after fleeing [[Morlana One]].<ref name="Kassa">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> Not long after, a Candovantan was present in the streets around the same location, riding on a [[Repulsorcraft]] past [[Luthen Rael]] and [[Bix Caleen]], who discussed Cassian's [[Search for Kerri|killing]] of two [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Preox-Morlana officers]].<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> Later that year<ref name="AndorAldhaniDate">{{AndorDate|104}}</ref>, a Candovantan was present within the [[Coruscant Eastern Spaceport]] on Coruscant when [[Syril Karn]] walked through the terminal after being fired from the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation]] on  Morlana One.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> During [[Senate Investiture]] [[Standard week|week]]<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere" /> in [[3 BBY]]<ref name="Episode Date">"[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]" establishes that the events of "[[I Have Friends Everywhere]] occur in [[3 BBY]].</ref>, a Candovantan was present on the planet [[Ghorman]], entering a shop that Cassian Andor, who was then undercover as "Varian Skye," was leaving while protesters demonstrated outside.<ref name="I Have Friends Everywhere">{{Andor|I Have Friends Everywhere}}</ref>

[[File:ZiziTlo-StarfighterMissions.png|thumb|right|200px|The Candovantan pilot Zizi Tlo]]
[[Zizi Tlo]] was a [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Starfighter Missions">''[[Star Wars: Starfighter Missions]]''</ref> Candovantan [[Resistance]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] who was killed during the [[Battle of D'Qar]] when [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIEs]] blew her [[RZ-2 A-wing interceptor|A-wing]] to pieces, alongside fellow pilot [[Jaycris Tubbs]].<ref name="TLJ Junior novel" /> During the [[New Republic era]], Senator [[Zygli Bruss]] represented Candovant within the [[New Republic Senate]] while on [[Hosnian Prime]]. He was injured during the [[Napkin Bombing]] incident, in which [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]] bombed a [[New Republic Senate conference building|conference building]] of the Galactic Senate.<ref name="BL">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> Bruss later perished on Hosnian Prime during the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] in which the First Order fired the [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]] at the [[New Republic]] capital and the other planets that shared its [[Hosnian system|system]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> in 34 ABY.<ref name="EP7Date">{{FilmDate|VII}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Candovantans were first developed for the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], directed by [[J.J. Abrams]], though they were ultimately cut from the final release.<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> The species was named and pictured, however,  within the ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', authored by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="TFAVD" /> which released the same day as the film.<ref name="DK">{{DK|url=us/book/9781465438164-star-wars-the-force-awakens-the-visual-dictionary|text=Star Wars: The Force Awakens The Visual Dictionary}}</ref>
