The '''Coyerti''' [[species]] was one of many life forms native to the [[planet]] [[Coyerti]].

==History==
The Coyerti were extremely resistant to [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] occupation, and held their own against the stronger force for a long [[time]]. However, due to their complex biology they were rendered extremely vulnerable during their [[Reproduction|reproductive]] season, a weakness the [[Imperial Garrison (Coyerti)|Imperials]] were sure to exploit. In preparation for this, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Alliance High Command|high command]] was contacted, and the Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]] was sent. The 61st, also known as Twilight Company, succeeded in destroying the Imperial [[biotoxin]] facilities and defending the Coyerti through their reproductive season.<ref name="Twilight Company" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coyerti species appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].
