{{Quote|My old friends on Jedha once told me that kyber crystals were the only power source for a lightsaber's blade, but Etai's citizens insist that their own etaan crystals are a perfectly suitable substitute.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>}}
'''Etaan crystals''' were a rare and unusual form of [[kyber crystal]] from the deep [[cave]]rns of the [[planet]] [[Etai]]. Raw etaan [[crystal]]s echoed sound with a humming resonance and this property was retained when they were used to build [[lightsaber]]s, creating a blade that thrummed in harmony with motion. The [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] was inspired to create the [[Spiced Jybbuk Juice]] beverage by the idea of using etaan crystals in lightsabers.

==Description==
Etaan crystals were an unusual type of [[kyber crystal]] that, in their raw form, echoed nearly any sound with a humming resonance. They formed in the deep [[cave]]rns of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Etai]], rarely appearing at less than five hundred [[meter]]s below surface level and usually found at twice those depths.<ref name="EV" />

==History==
===Lightsaber crystals===
[[File:LightsaberConstruction-SWE.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=The components of a deconstructed lightsaber, including a blue kyber crystal, are laid out roughly in the order they would be once combined.|Etaan crystals could be used during lightsaber construction.]]
Due to the depth at which they formed, etaan crystals were extremely difficult to locate, although searchers could use the [[crystal]]s' echoing quality to guide them to the formations. Once harvested, the crystals could be used in place of a normal kyber crystal during the construction of a [[lightsaber]]. Crystals used in such a way retained their resonant properties and created a lightsaber blade that chimed in response to nearby sounds and thrummed in harmony with motion.<ref name="EV" />

When coming into [[Lightsaber combat|contact]] with another lightsaber blade or a [[blaster]] bolt, blades generated by an etaan crystal created an especially spectacular response, producing a near-deafening cracking sound and a bright energy flare. This pushed apart lightsaber blades meeting in this fashion, but blaster bolts reflected by an etaan crystal blade would draw in some of its resonant energies and intensify while on their new trajectory.<ref name="EV" />

===Recipe inspiration===
{{Quote|Maybe it's true, maybe not. (Doesn't really matter, since there ain't any Jedi left anyhow.) But it got me thinkin' about what I could exchange in a recipe and still make it work.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
[[File:StronoTuggsCooksFish-UltimateCookbook.jpg|thumb|left|300px|alt=The Artiodac Strono Tuggs, wearing a brown apron, slices a large red tee fish on a chopping board with a knife. A lee romay shell sits beside the board and a bubbling pot with a red crustacean claw emerging from it sits behind Tuggs.|Strono Tuggs was inspired by etaan crystals to make Spiced Jybbuk Juice.]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], etaan crystals were rare to find and cost twelve thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to purchase.<ref name="EV" /> The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] was informed by the people of Etai that etaan crystals could be used in place of regular kyber crystals in a lightsaber, although he did not know<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> that etaan crystals were themselves a type of kyber.<ref name="EV" />

The Artiodac was uncertain if the claim was accurate or not and believed it mattered little as there were no [[Jedi]] left to use lightsabers, but he was prompted to think about similar exchanges he could make in his cooking recipes. The idea inspired him to swap the traditional [[fruit]] garnish that the people of Etai used on [[jybbuk slush]] topped with [[condensed nerf milk]] for [[candied hot pepper]]s, creating the beverage known as [[Spiced Jybbuk Juice]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included the recipe for Spiced Jybbuk Juice in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', following a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> He mentioned the crystals in the book but admitted he was still uncertain about their use in lightsabers.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Etaan crystals were first mentioned in ''[[Endless Vigil]]'', a [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement written by [[Tim Huckelberry]] and [[James M. Spahn]] for the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' tabletop [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="EV" />
