'''392 BBY''', or '''392<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> Before the Battle of Yavin''', was a [[Standard year|year]] in the [['ABY-BBY' dating system]]<ref name="GalacticAtlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> that was a part of the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> That year, the [[Galactic Republic]] began sponsoring missions by [[Outer Rim Exploration Team|Pathfinder teams]] made up of [[Jedi]] and [[Republic Pathfinder]]s to [[Scout|chart]] new safe [[hyperspace route]]s<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], helping the Jedi expand their knowledge and spreading the influence of the Republic.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Also around that year,<ref name="Date" /> the [[Quarren]] Republic leader [[Orlen Mollo]] negotiated a [[Territory dispute on Mon Cala|territory dispute]] on the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]].<ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
392 BBY was introduced via indirect mention of an event that had occurred a [[Standard decade|decade]] prior to the events of the [[2022]] novel [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" /> The year was directly referred to as "392 BBY" in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'',<ref name="Timelines" /> first published during [[Celebration Europe (2023)|Celebration Europe]],<ref name="Celebration">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-europe-2023-exclusives|text=Check Out ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Exclusives from Hasbro, the LEGO Group, and More}}</ref> which took place [[April 7]] through [[April 10|10]], [[2023]].<ref name="CelebrationSchedule">{{SW|url=news/swce-2023-panel-schedule|text=Plan Ahead with the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Europe 2023 Panel Schedule}}</ref> Prior to the book's release, the page mentioning 392 BBY was included in a [[March 8]] installment of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Show]]''.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/high-republic-show-march-2023|text=New Concept Art and More Revealed on ''Star Wars: The High Republic Show''}}</ref>

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[392 BBY/Legends|392 BBY]] was indirectly referenced in ''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]'',<ref name="DC">''[[The DarkStryder Campaign]]''</ref> which takes place in [[8 ABY/Legends|8 ABY]]<ref name="TERC">''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]''</ref> and mentions an event that took place four [[Standard century/Legends|centuries]] prior.<ref name="DC" />
