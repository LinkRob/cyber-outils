{{Quote|Alright. The good-lookin' fellow there with the horns, that's Burg. This may surprise you, but he's our muscle.|Migs Mayfeld, introducing Burg to Din Djarin|audio=MayfeldBurg-TMS01CH06.flac|<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
'''Burg''' was a [[Devaronian]] [[Gender|male]] who operated as a [[mercenary]] during the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]]. Burg acted as the muscle of a [[Ranzar Malk's crew|team]] recruited by [[Ranzar Malk]] to [[Bothan-Five incident|rescue]] the [[prisoner]] [[Qin]] from the ''[[Bothan-5]]'', a [[New Republic Correctional Transport]], which also included [[Migs Mayfeld]], [[Xi'an]], [[Q9-0]], and the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]], who had been recruited for the use of his [[gunship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. After Qin was rescued, Burg and the team betrayed Djarin and trapped him inside a cell. However, the Mandalorian managed to escape and took them down, imprisoning them in a cell aboard the transport and leaving them behind to deliver Qin to Malk alone.

==Biography==
===Assembling the team===
{{Quote|So, this is a Mandalorian. I thought they'd be bigger.|Burg, mocking Din Djarin|audio=BurgMando-TMS01CH06.flac|<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
[[File:Burg_The_Mandalorian.png|thumb|left|300px|Burg is introduced to Din Djarin.]]
A [[Gender|male]] [[Devaronian]] from [[Devaron]],<ref name="SWCEUAE" /> Burg was a [[mercenary]] during the [[New Republic era]].<ref name="Chapter 6" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|6}}</ref> Burg was recruited by the criminal [[Ranzar Malk]] as part of a [[Ranzar Malk's crew|team]] led by fellow mercenary [[Migs Mayfeld]], which also included the [[droid]] [[Q9-0|Zero]], the [[Twi'lek]] [[Xi'an]], and the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]], with the objective of rescuing [[Qin]] from aboard the ''[[Bothan-5]]'', a [[New Republic Correctional Transport]]. While the Devaronian carried a crate, Mayfeld introduced Burg to Djarin, describing him as the muscle of the group. Burg then dropped the crate and approached the Mandalorian, sneering at him and stating that he thought Mandalorians would be bigger. As the team boarded Djarin's [[starship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', Burg scoffed at his size.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

During the journey, Burg strolled around inside the ship, upsetting Xi'an, who told the Devaronian to be seated. He grunted at the Twi'lek, with Xi'an snarling back in response. Burg then tinkered with the controls, opening a weapons cabinet in the process. As he inspected the weapons with glee, Djarin intervened and closed the cabinet, angering the Devaronian. Tensions rose between the two, with Burg questioning why they needed a Mandalorian on the team. Mayfeld replied stating that they were considered to be the greatest warriors in [[the galaxy]]. Burg then asked why were they all dead, causing Mayfeld and Xi'an to laugh in response. The team then questioned why Djarin never removed his [[helmet]], with them guffawing when Mayfeld speculated if he was a [[Gungan]]. When Mayfeld suggested that he remove his helmet, Burg attempted to do so, but the Mandalorian retaliated, causing a fight to break out between the two. During the fight, the Devaronian accidentally opened the chamber where [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] resided, revealing his existence to the team. Zero dropped the ship out of [[hyperspace]] immediately afterwards and maneuvered around the prison ship, causing everyone to stumble.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

===The mission===
{{Dialogue|Burg|What is that thing?|Din Djarin|It's a tracking beacon.|Migs Mayfeld|He presses that thing we're all done.|attr=Burg, Din Djarin, and Migs Mayfeld, on Davan's distress beacon|audio=BurgBeacon-TMS01CH06.flac|src=<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
[[File:Mayfelds_Team_The_Mandalorian.png|thumb|right|300px|Burg and his team infiltrate the prison ship.]]
Upon landing, the team entered the prison ship and began their search for Qin, with Burg entering after Mayfeld and Xi'an. As they entered the hallway to the control room, they encountered a [[MSE-6 series repair droid|mouse droid]]. Burg toyed with the droid before shooting it, alerting a group of [[N5 sentry droid]]s to their position. The team was cornered in the hallway, but watched as Djarin sneaked behind the droids and destroyed them. They then mocked his efforts, with Burg intentionally bumping into him.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

The team entered the control room, discovering a [[human]] [[soldier]] named [[Lant Davan]] at the controls. As they surrounded the soldier, Davan revealed a [[Homing beacon|tracking beacon]]. While Djarin attempted to reason with him, a tense standoff ensued, with Burg siding with Mayfeld, who wished to kill Davan. The standoff ended when Xi'an killed the soldier by throwing a [[dagger]] at his abdomen, but not before he activated the beacon. Now under a strict time limit, Burg and the team rushed their way to Qin's cell. As they encountered an [[R1 security droid|R1 Security Droid]], Burg ran towards the droid and picked it up, proceeding to smash it on the ground. He then tossed the droid towards another oncoming droid, destroying them both.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

===Betrayal===
{{Quote|Let's see your face, Mandalorian.|Burg, while fighting Djarin|audio=BurgFace-TMS01CH06.flac|<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
Upon arriving at Qin's cell and opening it, Burg knocked Djarin into the cell as the team proceeded to betray him. While Burg and the team made their back to the ship, they were stopped in their tracks when Djarin escaped and returned to the control room, switching off the lights and locking the doors. As they continued rushing back to the ship, a door suddenly closed between them, separating the team and trapping Burg with Xi'an. The two then decided to split up and find Djarin. The Devaronian returned to the control room and searched for the Mandalorian. The latter then attempted to strangle Burg with his [[fibercord whip]] from the vents, but he pulled him down to the ground.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

[[File:Burg_Fight_The_Mandalorian.png|thumb|left|300px|Burg fights Djarin.]]
Burg kept Djarin on the ground as the Mandalorian attempted to use his weapons. Countering all of his attempts, Burg thrashed Djarin around the control room. However, when in front of the doors, Djarin knocked him into the entrance and destroyed the controls, causing one of them to close in on Burg. He lifted the door with his strength, but the Mandalorian activated another door, which closed around his face, severing his horns. The Mandalorian later incapacitated Xi'an and Mayfeld and imprisoned them on the ship along with Burg, leaving them behind as Djarin delivered Qin back to Malk alone and destroyed Zero in the process.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Tiny.|Burg, belittling Djarin|audio=BurgTiny-TMS01CH06.flac|<ref name="Chapter 6" />}}
[[File:Mandalorian_New_Republic_Droid.png|thumb|right|270px|Burg demonstrates his strength against a New Republic security droid.]]
Burg was a Devaronian male with [[Color|red]] skin and blue [[eye]]s, as well as multiple scars on his face. He used his size and strength to intimidate other individuals, most notably towards Djarin on several occasions during their rescue mission.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

==Skills and abilities==
[[File:Burg-CEUEEd.png|thumb|left|200px|While he relied on brute strength, Burg carried dual PCC-64 blasters.]]
In addition to utilizing [[blaster]]s, Burg possessed immunity to fire, as when Djarin sprayed him with a flamethrower, Burg simply shook it off. Burg also possessed immense strength, demonstrated at various points during Qin's rescue. He was able to lift an entire security droid and throw it with ease, as well as hold up a closing security door. During his fight with Djarin, Burg maintained the upper hand in combat until he was positioned in front of the door, where the Mandalorian knocked him into the entrance and incapacitated him by shutting both doors.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

==Equipment==
Burg wore a long, brown [[Jacket|coat]] with brown pants and [[boot]]s. He had two<ref name="Chapter 6" /> [[PCC-64 blaster pistol]]s<ref name="VG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[holster]]ed on his legs. However, he relied on his physical strength for the majority of the mission, only utilizing the blasters to take out a mouse droid and take aim at Davan during the standoff at the control room.<ref name="Chapter 6" />

==Behind the scenes==
Burg appeared in the [[Chapter 6: The Prisoner|sixth episode]] of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'',<ref name="Chapter 6" /> which aired on [[December 13]], 2019.<ref name="DTCI">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|work=[https://dtcimedia.disney.com Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]|archivedate=20230315011507}}</ref> He was portrayed by [[Clancy Brown]], with [[Justin Anthony Williams]] serving as a stunt double.<ref name="Chapter 6" />
