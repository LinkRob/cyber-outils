A '''birthday''',<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> also known as a '''name day''',<ref name="LivingForce">''[[The Living Force]]''</ref> was a [[Standard day|day]] that marked the anniversary of an individual's [[Reproduction|birth]]. [[Ezra Bridger]] was born on the day that the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Proclamation of the New Order|rose to power]], making his birthday the same day as [[Empire Day]].<ref name="Empire Day" /> It was customary to give gifts to others on their birthdays<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> and to prepare them decorated [[cake]]s.<ref name="Edge">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref> For example, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Wishan]] once [[Force-sculpture|created]] a set of [[clay]] figures as a 72nd birthday present for the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple's]] [[librarian]].<ref name="JTC-E1">{{JTC|1}}</ref>

[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birthday was three or four days before her [[Name Day]]. Organa didn't know her birthday for sure and didn't care as she'd become a [[princess]] of [[Alderaan]] on her Name Day and that was the anniversary she celebrated, most notably<ref name="Leia Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref> in [[3 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' to [[3 BBY]].</ref> with her [[Day of Demand]].<ref name="Leia Princess of Alderaan" />
