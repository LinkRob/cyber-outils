'''Bravais''' was an [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] astronomical object that featured [[Temperature|hot]] [[desert]]s. The celestial body's [[Plant|flora]] and fauna had adapted to live underground during the [[Local day|day]]. Bravais was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Bravaisian]] [[species]].

==Description==
Bravais was a terrestrial celestial body<ref name="AotG" /> located in the [[Bravais system]], a part of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="SWGalaxy" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated in grid square O-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and lay on a [[hyperspace route]] that connected it to the [[Japrael system|Japrael]] and [[Rajtiri system]]s.<ref name="SWGalaxy" />

Bravais [[orbit]]ed an intensely [[Temperature|hot]] [[star]],<ref name="LightsaberCollection" /> and its surface featured hot dirt and sand [[desert]]s. They were inhabited by [[plant]]s with [[tuber]]s as well as a variety of creatures living in subterranean dens and nests, such as [[insect]]s. The plants only extended their [[Diet|feeding]] tendrils aboveground at [[Local day|night]], and the creatures similarly only emerged during the periods of darkness, which led Bravais's deserts to appear lifeless during the day.<ref name="AotG" />

==History==
Bravais was mentioned in an entry of an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] dedicated to the celestial body's native [[Bravaisian]] [[species]]. The journal was eventually restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Inhabitants==
Bravais was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] Bravaisians. They evolved from a [[Bravaisian precursor species|type]] of pack [[Hunting|hunter]] creatures, who used their snouts and [[tongue]]s to search for tubers and insects. In order to avoid Bravais's heat and protect their small, sensitive [[eye]]s from light, the Bravaisians led a nocturnal lifestyle. Offworld, most Bravaisians wore protective [[goggles]], with some travelers even surgically removing their eyes altogether.<ref name="AotG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bravais was introduced in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'' by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="AotG" />
