'''Argus "Six Eyes" Panox''' was a six-[[eye]]d [[Azumel]] [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Official Guide" /> who lived in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. He was present during [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Han Solo]]'s first [[sabacc]] game.<ref name="Solo" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The character of Argus Panox<ref name="Topps">{{Topps|set=2018 Topps Denny's Solo: A Star Wars Story|cardname=Argus "Six Eyes" Panox}}</ref> was first revealed in an [[Wikipedia:Omaze|Omaze]] [[Wikipedia:Star Wars: Force for Change|Force For Change]] charity program video for the ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'' film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', released on [[April 18]], [[2017]].<ref name="Warwick Davis">{{SWYouTube|qCLQlf_opvU|Warwick Davis' Secret Han Solo Set Tour}}</ref> The character was later identified by name in a [[Wikipedia:Denny's|Denny's]]-exclusive [[Topps]] trading card.<ref name="Topps" /> A ''Solo'' commercial from Denny's featured Panox watching several individuals playing a game of [[sabacc]], including [[Han Solo]] and [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]].<ref name="Denny's">{{OfficialYouTube|channel=@dennys|name=Denny's|video=s8q-y2NkIK0|text=Solo: A Star Wars Story exclusive trading cards, available only at Denny's!|date=2018-04-02|nolive=1}}</ref>
