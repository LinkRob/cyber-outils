'''Bo-Katan's theme''' in the television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' was originally composed by [[Ludwig Göransson]] as a fast and heavy industrial sound; the composer describes it as a "heavily distorted synthesizer sound running through a gate pattern effect."<ref name="Season Two interview" /> The version is used throughout the series' [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second]] and [[The Mandalorian Season Three|third]] seasons. In the third season, composer [[Joseph Shirley]] introduces the use of French horns for Bo-Katan's theme.<ref name="Season Three interview" />
