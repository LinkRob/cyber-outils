'''Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers''' were a pair of [[lightsaber]]s owned by [[Ahsoka Tano]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The weapons were a single-bladed lightsaber with a [[Color|green]] blade, which was later accompanied by a [[shoto lightsaber]] with a yellow-green blade that she built upon taking up [[Jar'Kai]] during the latter half of the war. She often wielded her weapons in an unorthodox reverse grip. She used the weapons in many battles, although she was separated from them on a few occasions, such as when her lightsaber was stolen by [[Bannamu]] and wound up in the hands of the thief [[Cassilyda Cryar]], or upon her abduction by [[Trandoshan]] hunter [[Lo-Taren]] during the [[Second Battle of Felucia]].

After Tano's departure from the [[Jedi Order]] after being framed for treason, the weapons remained with the Order. Her former master, [[Anakin Skywalker]], returned the weapons to her upon her return to the [[Galactic Republic]]'s military to lead forces in the [[Siege of Mandalore]] near the end of the war. Tano abandoned the lightsabers for good after [[Order 66]], the means by which the [[Sith]] engineered the fall of the [[Jedi]], in order to fake her death to the new [[Galactic Empire]], before eventually replacing them with a pair of [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|white lightsabers]] upon joining the [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|nascent rebellion]].

==Description==
[[File:AhsokasLightsabers-OldFriendsNotForgotten.png|thumb|left|250px|Ahsoka's lightsabers]]
The primary [[lightsaber]] was a single-bladed weapon with a cylindrical silver hilt with black ornamentation.<ref name="TCWfilm" /> The [[Shoto lightsaber|shoto]] looked like a smaller, less-detailed version of the other lightsaber.<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides" /> Both sabers contained [[Kyber crystal|kyber]] [[Ilum crystal|crystals]] from the sacred [[planet]] of [[Ilum]], with the primary lightsaber having a [[Color|green]] blade and the shoto's blade being yellow-green.<ref name="Ahsoka" /><ref name="TCWfilm" /><ref name="The Citadel" /> Both sabers were capable of being wielded underwater, as demonstrated during the [[Battle of Mon Cala]].<ref name="Water War">{{TCW|Water War}}</ref> When [[Anakin Skywalker]] returned the lightsabers to [[Ahsoka Tano]] prior to the [[Siege of Mandalore]], the blades had become blue due to his tinkering with the weapons.<ref name="Old Friends" />

==History==
===The first lightsaber===
Ahsoka Tano, a [[Togruta]] [[Jedi]] [[youngling]], built the first of her lightsabers after receiving a kyber crystal on a [[Gathering]] to the remote world of Ilum.<ref name="Ahsoka" /> She brought it with her when she traveled to the planet [[Christophsis]] in the first year of the [[Clone Wars]] upon being appointed the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Knight]] and [[Jedi General|General]] Anakin Skywalker, who was [[Battle of Christophsis|fighting]] to take the planet back from the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] with his former Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="TCWfilm" />
[[File:AhsokaTanoTotJEpisode5-Displate.png|left|thumb|364x364px|Tano wielded her single blade against very formidable adversaries]]
Tano wielded the lightsaber as she fought alongside her master in many battles, including crossing blades with the feared [[General]] [[Grievous]] on a [[Raid on Skytop Station|mission]] to [[Skytop Station]],<ref name="Duel of the Droids">{{TCW|Duel of the Droids}}</ref> fighting [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]] on [[Rescue on the Tranquility|several occasions]],<ref name="Cloak of Darkness">{{TCW|Cloak of Darkness}}</ref> and using them in battles against Separatist forces on worlds like [[Defense of Maridun|Maridun]],<ref name="Defenders of Peace">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref> [[Raid on Nuvo Vindi's laboratory|Naboo]],<ref name="Blue Shadow Virus">{{TCW|Blue Shadow Virus (episode)}}</ref> [[First Battle of Felucia|Felucia]],<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> and [[Second Battle of Geonosis|Geonosis]].<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> She also duelled with disguised [[Clawdite]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Cato Parasitti]] after the shapeshifter had disguised herself as [[Chief Librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] during an [[Raid on the Jedi Temple|infiltration]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], noting that Parasitti lacked the skill of the woman she was impersonating and defeating her easily.<ref name="Holocron Heist" />

Her master Anakin Skywalker temporarily [[Jar'Kai|dual-wielded]] both her lightsaber with [[Skywalker lightsaber|his own]] against two [[B2-series super battle droid|super battle droids]] led by [[Cad Bane]] on a [[Cad Bane's Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigate]] in the [[Devaron system|Devaron System]] before rescuing her.<ref name="Cargo of Doom">{{TCW|Cargo of Doom}}</ref> Tano later fought her fellow Padawan [[Barriss Offee]] after the [[Mirialan]] had been [[Skirmish aboard TB-73|infected]] by a Geonosian [[brain worm]] while travelling to the [[Ord Cestus medical station]] on the [[frigate]] ''[[TB-73]]''.<ref name="Brain Invaders">{{TCW|Brain Invaders}}</ref>

One mission on [[Coruscant]] had Tano waiting on watch outside a [[cantina]] in [[slum district G17]] while Skywalker went inside to arrest arms dealer [[Car Affa]]. Tano was jostled by a crowd of people running outside as Skywalker took Affa into custody, so she didn't notice that the [[Patrolian]] thief [[Bannamu]] had taken the opportunity to swipe her lightsaber until after he had vanished into the masses. Desperate for her master not to find out about the loss, Tano teamed up with semi-retired [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]] to track the weapon down. Bannamu sold the lightsaber to a [[Trandoshan]] assassin named [[Nack Movers]]. However, Movers was shortly murdered by his girlfriend [[Ione Marcy]] and her associate [[Cassilyda Cryar]], so when Tano and Sinube tracked the weapon to [[Nack Movers's residence|Movers' apartment]], Tano was ambushed by the [[Terrelian Jango Jumper]] when she searched the place. Cryar fled through the window after realizing Tano was a Jedi, as she had not yet figured out how to activate the weapon. Tano chased Cryar over the rooftops, but Sinube took the long way around. Eventually, Tano and Sinube followed Marcy and Cryar to a [[Coruscant elevated maglev train|hovertrain]] station with the aid of a [[homing beacon]], where Marcy was arrested as Cryar fled onto the train. Tano followed her, but was forced to hold off when Cryar held some civilians hostage with the stolen weapon. However, Sinube met the train at the next station and disarmed Cryar, returning Tano's weapon to her.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />

Tano was later sent to [[Mandalore]] by the [[Jedi High Council]] to teach cadets at the [[Imperial Academy of Mandalore|Royal Academy of Government]] about the dangers of corruption, following a request by [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]. However, due to what had transpired on Master Kenobi's [[Fight on Concordia|earlier visit]], Tano was required to leave her lightsaber with her master, thus leaving her only her [[unarmed combat]] skills and [[the Force]] to help expose the [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|corruption]] of Satine's [[Prime Minister]], [[Almec]].<ref name="The Academy">{{TCW|The Academy}}</ref>

===Jar'Kai===
{{Quote|Don't forget your second blade, Ahsoka.''"<br />"''I'll try, master.''"<br />"''You're doing great. It takes time to learn how to wield two lightsabers.|Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Teach You">{{FOD|Teach You, I Will}}</ref>}}
[[File:AhsokaLightsabers-Overlords.png|thumb|right|250px|Ahsoka Tano wielding both of her lightsabers on Mortis.]]
At some point after her assistance in [[Rescue on the Pantora Droid Control Ship|rescuing]] [[Chi Eekway Papanoida]] following her [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|abduction]] by Separatist forces, Tano decided to take up [[Jar'Kai]], and journeyed to Ilum for a second kyber crystal.<ref name="SOI date note">"[[Sphere of Influence]]" is the last episode of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|''The Clone Wars'']], going by the {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, that Ahsoka Tano appears in without her [[shoto lightsaber]]. Therefore, it must have been constructed after the events of that episode.</ref><ref name="Ahsoka" /> She built a shoto lightsaber with a yellow-green blade to accompany her primary weapon. Tano received some training from [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] on the dual-wielding style of combat.<ref name="Teach You" />

Tano subsequently wielded the pair of lightsabers on occasions such as the [[Mission to Mortis|mission]] to [[Mortis]], where the blades had no effect against the family of [[Force wielder]]s that dwelt there.<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref> On Mortis, Tano dueled her master after being brainwashed to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] by the [[Son]].<ref name="Altar of Mortis">{{TCW|Altar of Mortis (episode)}}</ref> She later fought in the [[Battle of Lola Sayu|battle]] of [[Lola Sayu]] with the sabers, even though she had not been intended to go on the mission.<ref name="The Citadel" /> During the [[Second Battle of Felucia]], Tano was involved in an attack on a Separatist fortification along with Skywalker and Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]]. Tano's job was to go over the fort's back wall, along with [[clone trooper]]s from Plo's [[104th Battalion]], including [[Sinker]], [[Boost]] and [[Comet (clone trooper)|Comet]]. However, after all of the troopers had ascended the wall, Tano was ambushed by the Trandoshan hunter [[Lo-Taren]] with an [[electrified net gun]], searching for sentient prey for a sadistic bloodsport. Tano was dragged away, with her lightsabers left behind, and brought to the [[moon]] of [[Wasskah]], where she was forced to [[Battle on Wasskah|fight for her life]] unarmed with several newfound allies before she could return to the Republic.<ref name="Padawan Lost">{{TCW|Padawan Lost}}</ref><ref name="Wookiee Hunt">{{TCW|Wookiee Hunt}}</ref>

Tano was again separated from her weapons during the [[Mission to Zygerria|mission]] to the [[enslaver]] world of [[Zygerria]]. Along with Skywalker, Kenobi, and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], Tano had gone on the mission in disguise, in her case as a slave girl, much to her disgust. The Jedi's lightsabers had all been hidden inside of the [[astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]]. When the Jedi revealed themselves at the [[Royal Slave Auction]] and attempted to capture [[Queen]] [[Miraj Scintel]], they were overpowered, with Tano knocked unconscious by a [[shock collar]] she had been placed in as part of her disguise. The Queen subsequently kept the weapons as trophies.<ref name="Slaves of the Republic">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref> Eventually, Skywalker took the opportunity to free himself and Tano, reclaiming her weapons, before they left to [[Battle of Kadavo|rescue]] Kenobi, Rex and a large number of abducted Togruta colonists from the planet [[Kadavo]].<ref name="Escape from Kadavo">{{TCW|Escape from Kadavo}}</ref>

While attending the [[Confederate–Republic peace conference]] on Mandalore alongside [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], Tano was unexpectedly reunited with acquaintance [[Lux Bonteri]] of [[Onderon]], who had come to denounce Count Dooku for murdering his [[Mina Bonteri|mother]]. Sent to rescue him, Tano took him offworld, but he shocked her unconscious and turned their ship towards [[Carlac]], hiding her lightsabers onboard while telling R2-D2 that she had fallen asleep. Upon [[Battle on Carlac|arriving]] and discovering that Bonteri was working with the [[Mandalorian]] extremists [[Death Watch]], Tano concealed her status as a Jedi, signaling R2 to continue to conceal the weapons after he had retrieved them from where they had been hidden. Tano's initial [[Skirmish on Carlac|confrontation]] with the Death Watch ended in her defeat due to her lack of weapons, but after the droid returned them to her back at the warriors' camp, she fought her way out, crossing blades with Death Watch leader [[Pre Vizsla]], who wielded the ancient [[Darksaber]]. Tano, Bonteri and R2-D2 were able to escape, but Bonteri chose not to return to the Republic.<ref name="A Friend in Need">{{TCW|A Friend in Need}}</ref>

[[File:AhsokaDefendsAnakin-FaE.png|thumb|left|300px|Tano defends Skywalker's unconscious form from Cad Bane.]]
Later, Tano wielded her sabers when she, Skywalker and Kenobi were attacked by [[bounty hunter]] [[Rako Hardeen]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld]], where Kenobi was apparently killed in the confrontation. His death was actually a ruse intended to allow him to impersonate Hardeen and infiltrate a plot to kidnap [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref> from the [[Festival of Light (847th)|Festival of Light]] on [[Naboo]].<ref name="Crisis on Naboo">{{TCW|Crisis on Naboo}}</ref> However, Tano and Skywalker were kept in the dark as part of the plan, and Palpatine, knowing that Skywalker was upset after "Hardeen" [[Riot at the Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center|escaped]] from [[Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center|jail]], sent him and Tano on the path of their disguised friend. During their [[Showdown on Orondia|confrontation]] on [[Orondia]], Tano jumped into action with her weapons after [[Cad Bane]] nearly killed Skywalker, who had been briefly stunned by the disguised Kenobi. As a result, the disguised Jedi Master and the bounty hunters were able to escape, allowing Kenobi to continue his mission.<ref name="Friends and Enemies">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref>

Tano, Skywalker, Kenobi and Rex were sent to covertly assist in the [[Onderonian Civil War]], where she was reunited with Bonteri.<ref name="A War on Two Fronts">{{TCW|A War on Two Fronts}}</ref> She stayed behind on Onderon after the others departed to monitor the [[Onderon rebels]].<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref> Tano eventually publicly intervened at the attempted execution of rightful Onderonian king [[Ramsis Dendup]] to rescue<ref name="The Soft War">{{TCW|The Soft War}}</ref> General [[Akenathen Tandin]],<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> who had finally had enough of the usurper [[Sanjay Rash]] and turned on him.<ref name="The Soft War" /> However, due to the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council's]] orders that the rebels not become dependent on Jedi assistance, Tano did little fighting for the remainder of the war, despite her desire to help.<ref name="Tipping Points">{{TCW|Tipping Points}}</ref>

Later, Tano led a group of younglings on a [[Gathering (20 BBY)|Gathering]] to Ilum so they could gain their own lightsaber crystals.<ref name="The Gathering">{{TCW|The Gathering (episode)}}</ref> However, the return journey was fraught with complications, starting with an [[Raid on the Crucible|attack]] by the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] that led to Tano's capture and yet another separation from her weapons.<ref name="A Test of Strength">{{TCW|A Test of Strength}}</ref> The younglings set out to [[Rescue on Florrum|rescue]] Tano,<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> but an attack by General Grievous led to everyone being forced to work together. Tano once again crossed blades with the [[Kaleesh]] [[cyborg]] briefly while covering the younglings' escape.<ref name="A Necessary Bond">{{TCW|A Necessary Bond}}</ref>

===Parting and reuniting with the blades===
{{Quote|They're good as new... maybe a little better.|Anakin Skywalker upon returning Ahsoka's lightsabers|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
[[File:DeflectingStunBlasts-TJWKTM.png|thumb|right|300px|Ahsoka escapes Republic Military headquarters after being framed for murder.]]
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Cato Neimoidia Date">{{TCWcanondates|517}}</ref> Tano and Skywalker were called away from [[Defense of Cato Neimoidia|fighting]] on [[Cato Neimoidia]] to investigate a [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|bombing]] at the Jedi Temple [[hangar]], since their absence from Coruscant at the time would make them impartial. The pair identified [[Letta Turmond]], the wife of Jedi Temple employee [[Jackar Bowmani]], as the bomber, having used [[Nanotechnology|nano-explosives]] to unwittingly turn him into a walking bomb to protest the Jedi's involvement in the Clone Wars.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> Unbeknownst to anyone, however, Turmond was actually a proxy of Tano's friend Barriss Offee, who had secretly fallen to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] in her unease over the war.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi">{{TCW|The Wrong Jedi}}</ref> In an attempt to cover her tracks<ref name="TJWKTM">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref> and win Tano's sympathies, possibly hoping Tano would outright join her in the process,<ref name="Ahsoka" /> Offee framed Tano for the bombing, starting by [[Force choke|Force choking]] Turmond while her cell's surveillance systems had been sabotaged so no sound was recorded, making Tano's attempts to save Turmond's life look like murder.<ref name="TJWKTM" />

Tano was subsequently arrested, but Offee then assisted in her [[Escape from the Republic military base|escape]] in a way that made her look guilty, including Tano finding her lightsabers on the floor and then later finding several clone troopers who had been killed with such a weapon. During her flight, Tano lost her shoto before escaping into an [[underworld portal]].<ref name="TJWKTM" /> While on the run on [[Level 1313]], Tano encountered Ventress,<ref name="To Catch a Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> who had become a bounty hunter after being disowned by Dooku<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref><ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref> and wanted to try and collect the sizeable [[bounty]] on Tano's head. After Tano talked her into an alliance by pointing out the similarities in their positions and that the Jedi and Republic would be unlikely to reward her since she was wanted for crimes committed during her time with the Separatists, Ventress helped Tano non-lethally fight off the clone troopers sent after her. Tano also contacted Offee, unaware of her duplicity, and was thus lured into a trap at an [[abandoned munitions warehouse]].<ref name="To Catch a Jedi" />

After parting ways with the fugitive Padawan, Ventress was ambushed by Offee, who stole her mask and [[Asajj Ventress's lightsabers|lightsabers]] after knocking her unconscious. At the warehouse, Tano was attacked by the disguised Offee, who wore a cloak in addition to her stolen mask and weapons. Exhausted, Tano assumed that her attacker was Ventress, who had apparently changed her mind about taking her in. Offee was able to overpower Tano and disarm her before knocking her into a room full of boxes of the nanodroids used in the Temple attack, leading to her [[Capture of Ahsoka Tano|capture]] by the Coruscant Guard.<ref name="To Catch a Jedi" /> Tano was subsequently put on [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|trial]] for her alleged crimes after being expelled from the Jedi Order. Skywalker, investigating, tracked down Ventress and subsequently identified Offee as the true culprit. Tano was released after Offee confessed, but chose not to return to the Jedi, leaving her lightsabers in their possession.<ref name="The Wrong Jedi" />

[[File:AhsokaNewSabers-OFNF.png|thumb|left|300px|Skywalker presents Tano with her lightsabers, which now shine blue.]]
While the lightsabers were in the custody of the Jedi, Skywalker took care of them, taking the opportunity to, as he saw it, "improve" them. The blades changed color to blue as a result.<ref name="Old Friends" /> Tano, during her time away from the temple, eventually allied herself with Mandalorian warrior [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and her [[Nite Owls]],<ref name="Together Again">{{TCW|Together Again}}</ref> who welcomed a Republic invasion of Mandalore as it would unseat the [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]], who had taken over the planet, installed a puppet government and murdered Duchess Satine.<ref name="Old Friends" /><ref name="Shades of Reason">{{TCW|Shades of Reason}}</ref><ref name="The Lawless">{{TCW|The Lawless}}</ref> Tano made the decision to call on her former Master for help,<ref name="Ahsoka" /> and she reunited with him, Obi-Wan Kenobi, and Captain Rex. However, when the two Jedi were abruptly called back to Coruscant to [[Battle of Coruscant|help]] the [[Supreme Chancellor]], Skywalker left Rex and [[332nd Company|part]] of the [[501st Legion]] to help her. He also returned the lightsabers to Tano.<ref name="Old Friends" />

In the subsequent siege, Tano used the blades in battle once again, fighting [[Mandalorian super commando]]s on the descent to [[Sundari]] in midair. Seeking Maul, Tano was lured into a trap in the [[Undercity]] that the renegade Sith Lord had intended for his old enemy Kenobi<ref name="Old Friends" /> as well as Skywalker. Tano briefly fought Maul there, but the arrival of backup in the form of Rex and a squad of clones led Maul to choose to retreat then. As the [[Shadow Collective]]'s forces were beaten back from Sundari, the hunt for Maul continued after he took a hostage, [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Jesse]]. Maul eventually made his presence known by sneaking into the [[Sundari Royal Palace]] and waiting in the throne room to surprise Tano, Rex and Kryze, returning his hostage as a show of so-called good faith. With Tano left alone as Maul's super commandos attacked again, Maul made Tano an offer to team up and destroy the mysterious [[Darth Sidious]], an offer she initially accepted, but ultimately turned down when Maul revealed he had foreseen Skywalker becoming Sidious' new [[Sith apprentice]], thus orchestrating the siege in an attempt to lure him and Kenobi to the planet so he could kill both of them. In the ensuing duel, Maul briefly disarmed Tano of her primary blade, leaving her with only her shoto, but she was able to retrieve the weapon before kicking Maul, sending him flying through glass and out of the throne room.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref>

[[File:AhsokaTanoSeason7-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|200px|Tano used her blue-bladed lightsabers on Mandalore during the capture of Maul and into Order 66.]]
As the battle continued in the city, Tano pursued Maul to the top of Sundari's bio-dome, where he intended to meet an evacuation ship and abandon his forces. They dueled again atop the narrow support beams, and Tano was eventually disarmed of both of her lightsabers, with Maul threatening to drop her to a near-certain death if she refused to join him. However, Tano was able to knock him off of the beam and hold him in midair with the Force, leading to his capture when Rex arrived with reinforcements in several gunships.<ref name="The Phantom Apprentice" /> With Tano, Rex, and their allies having won the siege, Kryze retrieved Tano's lightsabers and returned them to her. Maul was imprisoned in the last existing [[Mandalorian vault]], a containment unit designed to imprison Force-users, and Tano and Rex set out for Coruscant<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> aboard the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Tribunal]]''<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia" /> to deliver him to the Jedi Council. However, [[Supreme Chancellor|Chancellor]] Palpatine's abrupt execution of [[Order 66]]—his plan as the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] to destroy the Jedi—[[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|disrupted]] the journey with staggering violence.<ref name="Shattered" />

After escaping the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]], Tano released Maul from the vault to serve as a distraction for the clones, refusing to loan him one of her lightsabers as she was not rooting for him to survive. With the help of three [[astromech droid]]s, Tano ambushed Rex and had his [[Behavioral modification biochip|control chip]] removed, thus removing his compulsion to follow the order like the other clones.<ref name="Shattered" /> Tano refused to kill any more clones, so the lightsabers were only used for deflecting blaster bolts when she and Rex subsequently fought their way to the [[hangar]]. Shortly after, Maul dismantled the ship's [[hyperdrive]], causing it to crash onto an [[unidentified moon]]; Maul, Tano, and Rex managed to escape, but all the other clones on the ship were killed in the crash. Tano and Rex built a memorial to the fallen.<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> In her anguish and defeat, Tano deposited both of her lightsabers at the site as well,<ref name="DB:Ahsoka">{{Databank|ahsoka-tano|Ahsoka Tano}}</ref> an action that helped to fake her death.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

[[File:VaderFindsTanoSaber-VaD.png|thumb|left|300px|Darth Vader recovers Ahsoka's lightsaber.]]
Over a year after the end of the Clone Wars, Tano built herself a [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|new pair]] of lightsabers, these ones with [[Curved-hilt lightsaber|curved hilts]] and white blades, from components she'd picked up on her travels and kyber crystals reclaimed from the [[Sixth Brother's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of the Imperial [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]] known as the [[Sixth Brother]]. She finished the weapons in time to assist in the [[Raada Uprising|evacuation]] of the population of the moon [[Raada]] from the Empire's wrath,<ref name="Ahsoka" /> and used them for many [[Standard year|years]] afterwards.<ref name="The Jedi">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], [[Darth]] Vader visited the ''Tribunal'' crash site, now buried in snow. In the wreckage, he found his former apprentice's lightsaber, heavily corroded but still working, which he took with him as he left.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AhsokasBlueLightsabers-CGSWG.png|thumb|right|300px|The blue blades of Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers, as they appeared in season seven]]
Ahsoka's lightsaber first appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'']] movie.<ref name="TCWfilm" /> The accompanying shoto first appeared in "[[Heroes on Both Sides]]."<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides">{{TCW|Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref> In the flashback in the novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']] (2017) where she abandons the lightsabers after the Siege of Mandalore, the blades are described as green.<ref name="Ahsoka" /> However, the episode "[[Old Friends Not Forgotten]]" depicts the lightsabers as blue-bladed upon being returned to her by Anakin prior to the siege.<ref name="Old Friends" /> Later editions of the novel changed the description of the lightsabers to match the television show.<ref name="Ahsoka" />

"[[Victory and Death]]" goes on to retcon the novel's claim that Tano abandoned the lightsabers on Mandalore on a fake grave she and Rex created for him. Instead, she abandons them at the graves that she and Rex made for their fallen clone comrades, on the moon that their ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer, the ''Tribunal'', crashed into shortly after the issuing of Order 66. She is also only seen abandoning her primary lightsaber,<ref name="Victory and Death" /> although her official [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry establishes she abandoned both of them there.<ref name="DB:Ahsoka" />

The scene in which Anakin Skywalker returns Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers to her before the Siege of Mandalore wasn't originally included back when the Siege of Mandalore story arc was in development before the cancellation of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''. When the series was [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|revived]] for [[Disney+]], the scene was conceived and added to the episodes.<ref name="OFNF Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=old-friends-not-forgotten-trivia-gallery|text="Old Friends Not Forgotten" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref>
