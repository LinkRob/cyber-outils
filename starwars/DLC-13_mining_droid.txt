The '''DLC-13 mining droid''', also known as the '''Mustafar panning droid''', '''droid lava collector''' or '''DLC-13 panning droid''', was a [[mining]] [[droid]]<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> used to extract valuable [[ore]]s from inhospitable environments, like [[Mustafar]] and the [[Belgaroth asteroid field]].<ref name="Droidography" /> The droids were made from heat-resistant [[carbonite]].<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know" />

Following the culmination of the [[Clone Wars]], several DLC-13 droids worked in Mustafar. During a [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] between [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Vader leapt onto one droid and took control of the droid's steering controls, causing it to drop its collection bucket into the lava below.<ref name="ROTS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DLC-13 mining droid first appeared in the [[2005]] feature film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']], the third film in the [[Prequel trilogy]]. The droid's backstory was explored in [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] literature like ''[[The New Essential Guide to Droids]]'' and [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. A mining droid nicknamed [[DLC-13/LEGO|DLC-13]] also briefly appeared in the non-[[Canon]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' episode "[[The Mines of Graballa]]," which first premiered on [[June 21]], [[2016]].
