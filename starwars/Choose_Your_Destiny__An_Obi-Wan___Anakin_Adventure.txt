'''''Choose Your Destiny: An Obi-Wan & Anakin Adventure''''' is a [[canon]] adventure book written by [[Cavan Scott]] and illustrated by [[Elsa Charretier]], and it is the third novel in the ''[[Star Wars: Choose Your Destiny]]'' series. The story is set before the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']], and it has over twenty possible outcomes, one of which is considered canonical. The book was first published on [[March 19]], [[2019]] by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Join Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker on a mission to rescue the captured Jedi Master''.

''With over twenty possible outcomes, you'll have to use the Force to keep Obi-Wan and Anakin safe from rogue battle droids, masked marauders, and so much more!''
