A '''blood spite''' was a predatory creature that made nests. A wounded [[bird]] in a blood spite nest would have little chance of survival. When the [[Chiss]] [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] first joined the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]], his interpreter [[Eli N. Vanto|Eli Vanto]] felt he had as much chance of surviving at the [[Royal Imperial Academy]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] as a wounded bird would have in a nest of blood spites.<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blood spites were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> They were originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[2013]] novel ''[[Dawn of the Jedi: Into the Void]]'', written by [[Tim Lebbon]].<ref name="Into the Void">''[[Dawn of the Jedi: Into the Void]]''</ref>
