'''Dune Sea Delicacy''' was a type of sweet meat that existed within [[the galaxy]]. In [[4 ABY]], the [[Waiter|waitress]] [[Valnir Nai]] offered the [[Foodstuff|food]] on a tray to the [[human]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] while located in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. Calrissian accepted the offer and consumed a single meat which made Nai's [[heart]] palpitate.<ref name="GT VaL" />
