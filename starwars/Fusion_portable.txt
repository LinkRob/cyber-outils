A '''fusion portable''' was a piece of technology that [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windy]] and [[Chuiee Two Thorpe]] would transport to [[Yavin Prime/Legends|Yavin]].<ref name="JOTW 1">''[[Journal of the Whills, Part I]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fusion portables were first mentioned in the ''[[Journal of the Whills, Part I]]'', the earliest draft for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. [[George Lucas]] scrapped the term in later drafts.
