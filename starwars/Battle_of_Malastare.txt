The '''Battle of Malastare''', one of the fiercest battles of the [[Clone Wars]], took place on the [[planet]] [[Malastare]]. During the battle, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] attempted to deplete the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[fuel]] supply by sending a massive [[Malastare droid army|army]] to conquer the world. The [[army]] was deactivated by a prototype [[electro-proton bomb]] that was designed by Republic [[scientist]] [[Sionver Boll]]. This opened up a large [[sinkhole]] that caused the [[Unidentified Zillo Beast|Zillo Beast]] to [[Sleep|awaken]].

==Prelude==
The [[Galactic Republic]] required access to [[Malastarian fuel]] in order to maintain its [[Republic Military]] and an ongoing offensive. As the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] were about to take over the [[planet]] and seize the [[Imperial palace (Malastare)|royal palace]], [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] ordered the use of an [[electro-proton bomb]] to deactivate the Separatists' [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]]. [[Jedi]] [[General]]s [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Mace Windu]] oversaw the battle.<ref name="TZB" />

==The battle==
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> after an extended [[Time|period]] of battle, the Confederacy was on the verge of capturing [[Malastare]], which would subsequently grant the Confederacy access to the [[fuel]] reserves on the planet. In response to the Separatist attack, [[Chancellor]] Palpatine authorized the Republic forces to use the electro-proton [[bomb]], a [[superweapon]] designed by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[scientist]] [[Sionver Boll]] that was capable of disabling [[Army|armies]] of [[droid]]s and vehicles within its [[Electromagnetic field|electromagnetic]] blast radius. However, Windu had initially feared that the [[weapon]] could potentially affect the planet's ecosystem, but the pair eventually came to an agreement at the Chancellor's urging.<ref name="TZB" />

As the massive [[Malastare droid army|droid army]], under the command of the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]] [[TN-123]], approached the plain near the [[Monarchy|royal]] palace, the droid gave the command to open fire and thousands of [[battle droid]]s started to fire on the outnumbered Republic forces led by [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]],<ref name="TZB" /> as he told his [[platoon]]<ref name="TZB AD" /> to hold the droid forces off until the bomb dropped. As Rex and the [[501st Legion]] took out a few battle droids, the Separatists launched several [[squadron]]s of [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]] and [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class droid starfighters]] to bomb the Republic's forces. [[RX-200 Falchion-class assault tank]]s, [[Dug rail gun]]s, and [[Dug debris loader]]s shot down many of them, but this led to several [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]]s being destroyed by crashing [[starfighter]]s.<ref name="TZB" />

The Republic then sent out its [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s escorted by [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 Starfighters]] and [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]s. The hundreds of [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]] attempted to shoot them down, though they were unsuccessful. A group of [[Dug cavalier|Dug warriors]] riding [[Insectomorph]]s attempted to charge the enemy lines, but were quickly [[Death|killed]] by several [[DSD1 dwarf spider droid]]s. Meanwhile, the [[clone trooper]]s managed to hold the line, despite heavy casualties.<ref name="TZB" />

The Y-wings finally reached the drop site and the bomb was released. The bomb initially created a large explosion that further grew into an even larger electromagnetic field that obliterated the whole army and caused all electronics to shut down.<ref name="TZB" />

==Aftermath==
After the bomb was initially detonated, a massive [[sinkhole]] formed and swallowed up several Republic vehicles and [[trooper]]s. This also caused the [[Unidentified Zillo Beast|Zillo Beast]] to [[Sleep|awaken]], which Windu, [[Clone Commander]] [[Ponds]], [[Trapper (clone trooper)|Trapper]], and [[Hawkeye]] soon discovered.<ref name="TZB" />
