'''David Espinoza''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developer that is credited as a member of the Art team for ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords]]''. He also served as a Senior Artist in the generalist department at Lucasfilm for ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker]]'', and season 2 of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.
