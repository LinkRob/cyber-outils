'''''Bastion Princess''''' was a cruise liner that journeyed from [[Bastion/Legends|Bastion]] to [[Gagrew Station]] in [[Hutt Space/Legends|Hutt Space]] as of [[44 ABY]]. It was almost [[Kilometer/Legends|kilometer]] long in a shape of diamond and painted in deep blue. It was boarded by [[Wraith Squadron]] during this time, who sabotaged its [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]] and fled in an [[Escape pod/Legends|escape pod]] as part of a ruse to seize an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] warship. It served [[Butter pastry|butter pastries]] as part of its breakfast.
