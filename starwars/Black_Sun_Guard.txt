The '''Black Sun Guard''',<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> also known as '''Black Sun foot soldiers''',<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/eminence-trivia-gallery|text="Eminence" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> were [[Falleen]] [[soldier]]s employed by the [[Black Sun]] criminal syndicate during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="Eminence" /> and the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Second Chance" /> They were led by [[Captain of the Guard]] [[Ziton Moj]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> who later became leader of Black Sun.
