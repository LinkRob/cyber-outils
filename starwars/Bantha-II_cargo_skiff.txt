The '''Bantha-II cargo skiff''', also known as simply as the '''Bantha class skiff''', '''desert skiff''', or '''sand skiff''', was a model of [[skiff]] manufactured by [[Ubrikkian Industries]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Bantha-II-CargoSkiff-SWOutlaws.png|thumb|left|250px|A Bantha-II skiff modified with a bench for passengers to sit.]]
Measuring 9.5 [[meter]]s in length, the [[Bantha]]-II cargo [[skiff]]<ref name="Desert Skiff" /> was a fast-moving<ref name="Commander" /> model of [[repulsorlift]] skiff.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Despite being lightly [[armor]]ed,<ref name="Commander" /> they were extremely durable making them an austere choice for [[desert]] climes and perfect [[transport]]s for passengers, cargo, or [[Slavery|slaves]].<ref name="Desert Skiff" /> Featuring an elongated deck,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Bantha-IIs were little more than floating platforms and were easily identified by their rust coloring, reinforced bows<ref name="Desert Skiff" /> build to withstand head-on collisions,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and intermittent<ref name="Desert Skiff" /> safety railings designed to protect passengers or cargo<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> around the perimeter.<ref name="Desert Skiff" /> They were operated from a rear control station designed for a single [[pilot]] and could exceed 250 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]] at their maximum speed.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Bantha-IIs also came equipped with planks.<ref name="Desert Skiff" />

==History==
Manufactured by [[Ubrikkian Industries]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> the Bantha-II cargo skiff was common outside the [[Galactic Republic]], and later the [[Galactic Empire]], and were used by the [[Hutt Clan]] and the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]].<ref name="Desert Skiff" /> The [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] used a number of the rugged skiffs equipped to survive the heat and [[sandstorm]]s of the [[Tatooine]] deserts.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

[[File:Han_rescues_Lando.png|thumb|right|300px|[[Han Solo]] saves [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] from falling into the [[sarlacc]].]]
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] outfitted Bantha-IIs with weapons specifically designed to defeat more heavily armed [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] vehicles. These modified skiffs were known as [[Anti-Vehicle Skiff]]s.<ref name="Commander" /> Accompanied by Jabba on his [[sail barge]], the ''[[Khetanna]]'', two Bantha-IIs were used by him when he attempted to [[Death|execute]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and his companions at the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. However, the prisoners managed to kill Jabba and used one of the skiffs to escape the destruction of the ''Khetanna''.<ref name="Return of the Jedi" />
