'''''Disney Dreamlight Valley''''' is an [[Wikipedia:Adventure game|adventure]] [[Wikipedia:Life simulation game|life simulation]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]]<ref name="About" /> developed by [[Gameloft]] Montreal<ref name="Developer" /> and published by Gameloft.<ref name="About" /> The game was first announced on [[April 27]], [[2022]].<ref name="Announcement">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gameloft.com/newsroom/live-magically-alonfside-disney-and-pixar-friends-in-disney-dreamlight-valley|text=Live Magically Alongside Disney and Pixar Friends in Disney Dreamlight Valley, a New Life-Simulation Adventure Game on PC, Mac and Consoles|work=[[Gameloft]]{{'s}} [https://www.gameloft.com official website]|date=2022-04-27|archivedate=20241212172047}}</ref> On [[April 14]], [[2025]], it was announced that the game would receive its first ''[[Star Wars]]'' collaboration through the addition of fashion and furniture items inspired by the franchise to the Premium Shop on [[April 23]] of the same year.<ref name="Whimsy">{{DDVYouTube|video=ZMMvpK6vLM4|text=Disney Dreamlight Valley Developer Update &#124; Wonderland Whimsy Update}}</ref> A second ''Star Wars'' collaboration was announced and released on [[April 15]], [[2026]], which was called the "2026 Collection".<ref name="2026 Trailer">{{DDVYouTube|aYNt5a1LNyM|2026 Disney Dreamlight Valley &#124; STAR WARS Collection Trailer}}</ref>

==''Star Wars'' content==
===2025 collection===
Cosmetic ''Star Wars'' items in the 2025 collection were centered around the [[Naboo]] [[Lake Country]], including [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie]] as featured in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. The Anakin Skywalker costume bundle included his [[Jedi apparel]] with a [[cloak]], [[Padawan braid]], and [[lightsaber]].<ref name="Trailer" /> The lightsaber introduced a series of four new photo mode poses players could assume representing different lightsaber [[Lightsaber combat|stances]].<ref name="ProbablySenpai">{{YouTube|video=sFJMinWsnvc|text=Star Wars FULL Showcase & Review! &#124; Your Weekly DLV Breakdown! &#124; Disney Dreamlight Valley|channel=@Probably_Senpai|name=Probably Senpai|date=2025-04-30}}</ref> Padmé's costume bundle featured her [[lakeside gown]] and hairstyle from the film.<ref name="Trailer">{{DDVYouTube|pIlAtflF3Lw|Disney Dreamlight Valley &#124; STAR WARS Collection Trailer}}</ref>

A new exterior home design based on the [[Varykino Villa]] was added, as were various interior decorations, such as a fireplace, sofa, bed, [[holotable]], dining table, and chairs. Floating around the villa was a roughly spherical [[gardener]] [[droid]] wielding a spray bottle to water [[plant]]s.<ref name="Trailer" />

[[R2-D2]] was introduced as a companion to the game, allowing players to have the [[astromech droid]] follow them around. Players could even have R2-D2 display a [[hologram]] of a heart.<ref name="ProbablySenpai" />

===2026 Collection===
Cosmetic ''Star Wars'' items in the 2026 collection included the items from the 2025 release, with the addition of new cosmetics centered around [[Tatooine]]. The update also introduced Padmé's [[summer meadow dress]]. Tatooine-based designs included a [[merchant]] kiosk, [[moisture vaporator]]s, an [[X-34 landspeeder]], a traditional Tatooine-inspired building, and a sandy wall.<ref name="2026 Trailer" />

[[BB-8]] was introduced as a companion similar to R2-D2. A BB-8 headband costume piece was also available.<ref name="2026 Trailer" />
