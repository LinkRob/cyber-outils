A '''Artillery Tower''' was a type of tower used by the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The tower had four models called tiers, each with a distinct design and crescent firepower and range.<ref name="GD" />
