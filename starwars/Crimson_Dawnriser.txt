The '''Crimson Dawnriser''' was a costume owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Chemist|standard costume]]. It featured various elements reflective of the criminal organization [[Crimson Dawn]]'s branding, including [[Color|black]] and gold accents, as well as Crimson Dawn's insignia attached to the chestplate.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Dawnriser was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Skora, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Dawnriser Applicator]]. Unlike most cosmetic sets in the game which are added at the same time, the Dawnriser Applicator was added within the 0.17.0 update on [[July 18]], [[2023]], while this Costume was not.<ref name="Hunters" />
