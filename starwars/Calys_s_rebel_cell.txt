{{Quote|Calys organized a local insurgency, and she managed to recruit Asara after making contact with a field agent from the Rebel Alliance.|ISB report on Asara Deyn|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
A '''rebel cell''' organized on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[jungle]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]] by a local named [[Calys]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. As the cell grew, Calys's [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|brother]] [[Asara Deyn]] began helping the cell by spying on Imperial patrols. After making contact with a representative from the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] Calys decided that she wanted to leave Akiva and join the Rebel Alliance. [[Standard month|Months]] after leaving, Calys [[death|died]] during the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Death date">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' establishes Calys [[Death|died]] at the [[Battle of Scarif]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[1 BBY]].</ref> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] had compiled a report on Asara in which it noted Calys's organization of the rebel cell and her later death.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[thief]] and acquaintance of Asara named [[Kay Vess]] found a copy of the ISB's report on Asara at the [[Hutt Poaching Station]] in the [[Hunter's Canopy]] region of Akiva..<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Calys's rebel cell were mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The cell was mentioned in Asara's ISB report collectible as well as the game's character databank. entry on Asara. The fate of the rebel cell after Calys joined the Rebel Alliance was not specified within the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
