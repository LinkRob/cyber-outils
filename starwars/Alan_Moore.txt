'''Alan Moore''' is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic-book]] writer who authored a number of stories which appear in ''[[Classic Star Wars: Devilworlds]]'' including ''[[Blind Fury!]]'', ''[[Rust Never Sleeps]]'', ''[[Dark Lord's Conscience]]'' and ''[[Tilotny Throws a Shape]]''.
