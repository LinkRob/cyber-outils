'''Crystal resonators''' were devices which could be used to detect unnaturally high concentrations of gems or [[crystal]]s. Following the [[Destruction of Jedha City|devastation]] of the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]]<ref name="SW39" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|RO|the [[destruction of Jedha City]]}}</ref> Imperial forces dropped crystal resonators onto the moon's surface to look for [[kyber crystal]]s. The resonators detected an unnaturally high concentration in what had once been the [[Catacombs of Cadera]]. When [[Queen]] [[Trios]] of the [[planet]] [[Shu-Torun]]&mdash;who was advising the Empire on mining kyber crystals and supplying them with miners and equipment&mdash;saw the reports, she confronted Imperial [[Commander]] [[Kanchar]] and demanded to know what he knew about the crystals in the catacombs. Kanchar revealed that they were the crystals that had previously been stolen by [[Saw Gerrera]], leader of the rebellious [[Partisans]].<ref name="SW39" />
