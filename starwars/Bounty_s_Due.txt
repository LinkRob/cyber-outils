"'''Bounty's Due'''" is a short story by [[Peter M. Schweighofer|Peter Schweighofer]] that was published in the ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Guri]], a [[Black Sun/Legends|Black Sun]] lieutenant, was to meet with the [[Ubese/Legends|Ubese]] [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] [[Boushh/Legends|Boushh]] on [[Gelgelar/Legends|Gelgelar]]. Boushh had captured alive a [[Nimbanel/Legends|Nimbanel]] who was the target of a [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] issued by [[Dark Prince|Prince]] [[Xizor/Legends|Xizor]], but was holding out for extra [[Galactic Credit Standard/Legends|credits]]. While meeting with Guri, Boushh became distracted when he was bitten by a [[Dinko (creature)|dinko]]. Guri took advantage of the situation, [[Death/Legends|killed]] the bounty hunter and left with the restrained Nimbanel.
