This [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] '''base''' was located on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Altyr V]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]], it came under [[Battle of Altyr V (Galactic Civil War)|attack]] by two artificial [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Asteroid/Legends|asteroids]]. Thanks to the efforts of the [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] [[Kyle Katarn]], the base [[Unidentified New Republic commander|commander]] managed to evacuate all personnel before the main Imperial force emerged from [[Hyperspace/Legends|hyperspace]].
