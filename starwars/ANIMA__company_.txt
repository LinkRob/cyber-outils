'''ANIMA''' is an animation studio which will produce a short with in the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 3|third volume]] of the [[Canon|non-canon]] animated anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'', which will release in [[2025]]. The studio was first revealed to working on the series on [[November 20]], [[2024]].<ref name="APAC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-season-3-announce|text=''Star Wars: Visions'' Returns with Volume 3 in 2025}}</ref>
