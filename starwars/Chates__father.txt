The '''father''' of [[Chates]] was a [[Gender|male]] individual.<ref name="TOG" /> He<ref name="Junior Novel" /> worked as a [[farmer]] and at some point traveled to [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]] with his son. When the pair visited a bank in the [[city]], Chates' father was [[Death|killed]] by muggers. After his father's death, Chates quickly fell in with the [[White Worms]] criminal gang led by [[Lady]] [[Proxima]].<ref name="TOG" />
