'''Bloomstone''' was a type of [[stone]] found in the [[Seikara Caverns]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dekien]]. While [[Catastrophe in the Seikara Caverns|onboard]] a [[Eternity Enterprises barge|pleasure barge]] operated by the company [[Eternity Enterprises]] on Dekien, the former [[Sith]] known as [[the Ronin]] watched a [[Hologram|holo]] of an [[Unidentified individual (Eternity Enterprises barge)|individual]] welcoming guests to the planet. As the [[Sail barge|barge]] approached the Seikara Caverns, a segment of the holo began playing in which the individual spoke about the [[cave]] system and described it as stretching through cathedrals of solemn bloomstone.<ref name="Ronin" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Bloomstone was mentioned in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', written by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" />
