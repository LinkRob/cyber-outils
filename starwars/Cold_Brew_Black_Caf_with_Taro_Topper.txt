'''Cold Brew Black Caf with Taro Topper''' also referred to as '''Nitro Cold Brew Black Caf with Taro Topper'''<ref name="Menu board pic">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2022/08/review-new-cold-brew-with-taro-topper-from-docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo-in-disneyland|text=REVIEW: New Cold Brew with Taro Topper from Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo in Disneyland|author=Matthew Soberman|date=2022-08-20|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|archivedate=20220830023916}}</ref> was a cold, sweet [[caf]] beverage available at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The drink was [[Color|brown]] in color and served with a light purple topper. It was consumable by [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cold Brew Black Caf with Taro Topper was added to the menu of Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo at [[Disneyland]]'s [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] on [[August 17]] of [[2022]] as a seasonal offering.<ref name="Release">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2022/08/15/new-menu-additions-coming-soon-to-docking-bay-7-in-disneyland/#more-707775|text=NEW Menu Additions Coming SOON to Docking Bay 7 in Disneyland|author=Cassie Agundes|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com Disney Food Blog]|archivedate=20220815192125}} displays a screenshot of the [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] menu showing exact release date and that it was seasonal.</ref> The menu description was "Nitro Cold Brew topped with a Sweet Taro Cream."<ref name="DL Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref> The drink is a spin-off of the [[Cold Brew Black Caf]], which is chocolate-based, rather than taro.<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=top-coffee-drinks-to-try-at-disneyland-resort-for-national-coffee-day|text=Top Coffee Drinks to Try at Disneyland Resort for National Coffee Day|author=Sarah Sterling|date=2022-09-29}}</ref>
