'''Brian George''' (born [[July 1]], [[1950s#1952|1952]]) is a voice actor who voiced additional characters in ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''. In ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' he voiced [[King]] [[Katuunko]], [[Chairman]] [[Chi Cho]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], and [[Silman]]. He portrayed [[Devotek]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''.
