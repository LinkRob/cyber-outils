The following events took place in or around the year '''228 BBY''', also known as '''3049 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]], '''209 BFE''' according to the [[Imperial calendar|Imperial Calendar]], and '''Year 7749''' according to the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.

==Events==
*The [[Occlusion Zone]] is ruled by the [[Nihil]] and [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]].<ref name="Friend or Foe">{{InsiderCite|223|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Friend or Foe?}}</ref>
*[[Visma Tarko]], [[Elping Tsoo]] and the [[Adrens]] form an alliance.<ref name="Survivors">{{InsiderCite|224|Tales from the Occlusion Zone: Survivors}}</ref>
*Numerous [[prisoner]]s on the ''[[Korvix Vorn]]'' are freed in a [[Raid on the Korvix Vorn|raid]] conducted by the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="THRA 5">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 5|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 5]]</ref>
*[[Naboo]] is liberated from the [[Nihil]] rule by the [[Jedi]] Order.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
*The Jedi Order carry out a rescue [[Mission on Palagosal|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Palagosal]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless">''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''</ref>
*The [[moon]] [[Inad Komesh]] is [[Evacuation of Inad Komesh|evacuated]] after being infected with the [[Blight]].<ref name="Into the Light">''[[The High Republic: Into the Light]]''</ref>
*The [[Drengir]] threat is ended.<ref name="Into the Light" />
*The [[Stormwall]] Falls.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>
*[[Azlin Rell]] escapes Jedi custody and disappears.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
*The Nihil surrender following their defeat at [[Eriadu]]<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
*[[Marchion Ro]] is captured, [[Trial of Marchion Ro|tried]], and [[Marchion Ro's prison|sentenced]] by the Republic.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
*The Blight is held back on [[Sophros]] by [[Avar Kriss]] and [[Elzar Mann]], ending its threat to the galaxy.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />
