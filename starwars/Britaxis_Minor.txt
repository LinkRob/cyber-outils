'''Britaxis Minor'''<ref name="AlienArchive" /> was a terrestrial<ref name="Unlock" /> celestial body<ref name="AlienArchive" /> located in the [[Britaxis Minor system]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It was situated in grid square O-11 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Timelines" /> and was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Britarro]] [[species]]. Britaxis Minor was eventually mentioned in an entry of an [[Unidentified artist|artist's]] [[Unidentified artist's journal|journal]] dedicated to the Britarro. The journal was eventually restored, expanded, and displayed at the [[Graf Archive]]<ref name="AlienArchive" /> at some point no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Britaxis Minor was introduced in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]'' by [[Natalie Clubb]] and [[Katrina Pallant]].<ref name="AlienArchive" />
