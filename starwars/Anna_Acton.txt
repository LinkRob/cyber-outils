'''Anna Acton''' is an English actress who provides additional voices in ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref name="Credits">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> She is best known for her role as Rochelle Barratt in the British police procedural television series [[Wikipedia:The Bill|''The Bill'']] and Geri Evans in the soap opera series [[Wikipedia:Family Affairs|''Family Affairs'']].
