'''Chalan Talosi''' was a [[Twi'lek]] [[Technician|mechanic]] in [[the galaxy]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] who served aboard a ship with [[Javlin]] and [[WAC-77]]. At one point they were pursued by [[Black Sun]] [[starfighter]]s and Talosi had to scramble to fix the ship. She once spoke about the tools available to the profession.<ref name="Special Modifications" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chalan Talosi appears in the opening story in [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' [[2016]] [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Special Modifications]]''.<ref name="Special Modifications" />
