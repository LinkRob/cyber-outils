A '''dolphin''' was a type of creature. The [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Duor]] [[Species/Legends|species]] of the [[Waterworld/Legends|waterworld]] [[Arrakan]], members of which possessed streamlined bodies with a [[Mouth/Legends|snout]], fins, and [[Limb/Legends|flippers]], physically resembled dolphins.<ref name="Valkyrie20">{{Valkyrie|issue=20|story=Flight of the Jedi|bypass=1}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DolphinRealWorld.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A real-world dolphin]]
Dolphins were introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise in the second part of [[Phil Sidebotham]]'s [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure "[[Flight of the Jedi]]," which was published in the Spring [[2000]] [[Valkyrie 20|twentieth issue]] of the ''[[Valkyrie]]'' magazine for use with [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Valkyrie20" /> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Dolphin|dolphins]] are [[Wikipedia:Mammal|mammals]] which are classified as [[Wikipedia:Toothed whale|toothed whales]] and which have [[Wikipedia:Spindle (textiles)|spindle]]-shaped bodies&mdash;mostly less than three meters in length&mdash;and toothed snouts. Dolphins exhibit remarkable intelligence, playfulness, and friendliness to humans.<ref name="Dolphin Britannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/animal/dolphin-mammal|text=Dolphin &#124; Facts & Pictures|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2024-12-09|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20250117181155}}</ref>

Recordings of sounds made by dolphins were used to create the voice of the [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] in<ref name="Kids1">[[Star Wars Kids (1997) 1|''Star Wars Kids'' (1997) 1]]</ref> the [[1977]]&ndash;[[1983]] [[original trilogy]] of ''Star Wars'' films<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> as well as the sounds made by the [[Acklay/Legends|acklay]] creature in<ref name="Drawing">{{SW|url=news/drawing-from-the-present-familiar-creatures-in-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text=Drawing from the Present: Familiar Creatures in a Galaxy Far, Far Away|int=Back from the Drawing Board|nolive=1}}</ref> the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="YearByYear" /> In addition, during the development of<ref name="ArtOfROTS">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> the [[2005]] prequel trilogy installment [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']],<ref name="YearByYear" /> the skin texture of the sentient [[Kallidahin/Legends|Kallidahin]] species was inspired by that of dolphins.<ref name="ArtOfROTS" /> Dolphins also went on to have an indirect role in the visual design of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="ArtOfSolo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> a [[2018]] film of the current ''Star Wars'' [[canon]]'s ''[[Star Wars Anthology Series]]'',<ref name="YearByYear" /> where a reference image of a painted and lacquered dolphin skull served as inspiration for the [[Enfys Nest's helmet|helmet]] worn by the character [[Enfys Nest]].<ref name="ArtOfSolo" />
