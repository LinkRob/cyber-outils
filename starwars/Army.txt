An '''army''' was a faction of [[troop]]s and [[soldier]]s who were dedicated to a central cause. Major armies throughout [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[Timeline of galactic history|history]] included the [[Gungan Grand Army]], the [[Trade Federation Droid Army]],<ref name="SWE1">{{Film|I}}</ref> the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the [[Separatist Droid Army]],<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> the [[Imperial Army]], the [[Alliance Army]], the [[New Republic army]],<ref name="BF">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> the [[First Order Army]], the [[Resistance Army]],<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> and the [[Sith Eternal army]].<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

In order to invade a [[planet]], a group could organize an '''invasion army''' and use [[landing craft]] to move them to the surface.<ref name="SWE1" /> The [[ecumenopolis]] of [[Coruscant]] effectively had a small army of [[Maintenance droid|repair droids]], [[INS-444]]s and [[CLE-004 window cleaning droid]]s among them, to maintain the [[skyscraper]]s.<ref name="BuildR2 38">{{BuildR2Cite|38|Droid Directory}}</ref>
