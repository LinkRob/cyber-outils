"'''Crossword'''" was a regular feature of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' for several issues. Crossword puzzles appear in issues [[Star Wars Insider 58|58]], [[Star Wars Insider 60|60]], [[Star Wars Insider 61|61]], [[Star Wars Insider 62|62]], [[Star Wars Insider 63|63]], and [[Star Wars Insider 73|73]].
