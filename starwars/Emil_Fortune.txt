'''Emil Fortune''' is an editor at [[Egmont UK Ltd]]. He notably edits the ''[[Star Wars: Adventures in Wild Space]]'' book series, co-authored by [[Cavan Scott]] and [[Tom Huddleston]], and wrote the [[2016]] book ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.
