The '''Banshee of Bestine''' was a [[First Order]] [[ace]] [[pilot]] who was active during the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Codebreaker" />

==Biography==
The Banshee of Bestine was a notorious ace pilot with at least twenty-six confirmed [[Death|kills]]. He patrolled [[Hutt Space]] and engaged [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] over the [[planet]] [[Nar Kaaga]] during their [[Mission to Nar Kaaga|mission]] to destroy a code-breaking device located on the surface.<ref name="Codebreaker" />

During the [[Starfighter combat|dogfight]], the Banshee destroyed two [[Resistance]] [[T-70 X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] and damaged [[Black Leader]] [[Poe Dameron]]'s X-wing. The Banshee followed Dameron into Nar Kaaga's [[atmosphere]] in order to ensure his death, but abandoned his pursuit once Dameron jettisoned most of his [[fuel]] to fake a crash landing.<ref name="Codebreaker" />

==Personality and traits==
According to Resistance [[Admiral]] [[Ushos O. Statura]], the Banshee of Bestine was a dangerous and deadly pilot, a sentiment echoed by the members of Black Squadron during their dogfight over Nar Kaaga. The Banshee was a confident pilot, but was disappointed that none of the members of Black Squadron could match their skill.<ref name="Codebreaker" />

==Equipment==
The Banshee of Bestine flew a [[Color|black]] [[TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor|TIE Interceptor]]. Their [[First Order stormtrooper armor|armor]] was gray rather than the black armor typically worn by [[First Order TIE fighter pilot]]s and had red accents, including red tally marks on the [[helmet]].<ref name="Codebreaker" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Banshee of Bestine first appeared in the [[Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker 1|first issue]] of the [[2025]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] mini-series ''[[Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]] and illustrated by [[Marc Yarza]], and was released on [[April 30]], 2025.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3012-763/Star-Wars-Hyperspace-Stories--Codebreaker-1|text=Star Wars: Hyperspace Stories--Codebreaker #1}}</ref>
