The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''5 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref><ref name="FF 72">{{FactFile2014|72|STY 1-2|The Spire}}</ref> also known as '''BBY 5''',<ref name="Episode 1">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> overlapping with '''3272 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7972''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*The [[Galactic Empire]] cuts off [[Ghorman]] shipping lanes.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref><ref name="AndorDate">{{AndorDate|1}}</ref>
*The [[Arvala-6 conflict|Arvala-Six conflict]] diminishes and mining resumes on the occupied lands.<ref name="Aldhani" /><ref name="AndorDate" />
*[[Cassian Jeron Andor]] is introduced to [[Luthen Rael]], flees his adopted homeworld of [[Ferrix]] after an [[Ambush on Ferrix|ambush]] performed by the [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team|security Inspection team]],<ref name="Episode 3">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref> and joins the [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> They are able to [[Aldhani heist|steal]] roughly eighty million [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] from the [[Aldhani Garrison]].<ref name="The Eye">{{Andor|The Eye}}</ref><ref name="AndorDate" />
*The Empire [[Takeover of the Preox-Morlana Corporate Sector|takes direct control]] of the [[Morlani system]] and [[Free Trade sector|Morlana Corporate Sector]].<ref name="Aldhani" /><ref name="AndorDate" />
*The [[Eye of Aldhani]] occurs.<ref name="The Eye" /><ref name="AndorDate" />
*The [[Imperial Emergency Act]] and [[Public Order Resentencing Directive]] are passed in the aftermath of the Aldhani heist.<ref name="Announcement" /><ref name="AndorDate" />
*[[Ezra Bridger]] joins the [[Spectres]].<ref name="Spark" />
*A [[Narkina 5 prison escape|prisoner uprising]] occurs in the [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|imperial prison complex]] on [[Narkina 5]]. Multiple prisoners, including [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] and [[Ruescott Melshi]], escape.<ref name="One Way Out">{{Andor|One Way Out}}</ref><ref name="AndorDate" />
*[[Anto Kreegyr]] and his [[Anto Kreegyr's group|rebel group]] [[Spellhaus attack|raid]] the [[Spellhaus Imperial power station]]. It ended in complete failure and all forces, including Kreegyr, were killed.<ref name="Rix Road">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref><ref name="AndorDate" />
*A [[Ferrix Riot|citizen uprising]] occurs on [[Ferrix]] during [[Maarva Carassi Andor]]'s [[funeral]] resulting in the deaths of several Imperials, including [[ISB Attendant]] [[Corv]]<ref name="Rix Road" /><ref name="AndorDate" />
*[[Jyn Erso]] is [[Mission to Tamsye Prime|abandoned]] by [[Saw Gerrera]].<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
*''[[The Rebel Files]]'' are created.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
*A [[Armed rebellion in the Atrivis sector|rebellion]] breaks out in the [[Atrivis sector]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' clarified the timeline of 5 BBY, noting that the events of "[[Announcement]]" had passed before the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''. As stated in its timeline, the Public Order Resentencing Directive had been prompted by the Aldhani heist at some point before Bridger joined the Spectres.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>
