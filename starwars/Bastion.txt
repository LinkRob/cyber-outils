'''Bastion''' was an industrialized [[planet]]<ref name="Battles" /> located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Also part of the [[New Territories]],<ref name="New Territories" /> it was connected via [[hyperspace route]] to [[Anthan Prime]].<ref name="Galaxy Map">{{GalaxyMapPoster}}</ref>

During the [[Clone Wars]], Bastion fell within [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] territory during the [[Outer Rim Sieges]].<ref name="New Territories" /><ref name="Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref>

Sometime after the [[Galactic Civil War]]'s [[Battle of Jakku|end]] with the [[Galactic Concordance|collapse]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] by the [[New Republic]], the successor to the Imperial state began to formulate within the Outer Rim. The successor, known as the [[First Order]], was formed around a string of industrialized worlds, which stretched from Bastion to the planet [[Yaga Minor]].<ref name="Battles" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bastion was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[The galaxy|galactic]] map in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] boxed set for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="TFABG" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Bastion/Legends|Bastion]], also referred to as Sartinaynian, was introduced in ''[[Specter of the Past]]'', a [[1997]] novel authored by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: The Hand of Thrawn Duology]]'' novel series.<ref name="Specter">''[[Specter of the Past]]''</ref>
