'''Dima''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who [[Monarchy|ruled]] as the [[queen]] of the [[planet]] [[Eiram]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. She was [[Marriage|married]] to Queen [[Thandeka]], her consort. The [[Directorate]], intending to destabilize the governments of Eiram and its twin planet, [[E'ronoh]], mistakenly [[Eiram–E'ronoh crisis|kidnapped]] Thandeka when attempting to take Queen Dima as a hostage.<ref name="Into the Dark" />

Although Dima was an absolute monarch, she listened carefully to her advisers, most of all to her wife of 30 years [[Thandeka]].<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> when Dima was presented with the choice to shoot down the lower half of [[Starlight Beacon]], which was on its way to crash into the city of [[Barraza City|Barraza]] or trust the Jedi onboard Starlight Beacon to find a solution, she chose trusting the Jedi as she did not want the [[Nihil]] to pressure them into justifying the killing of other people under the euphemism of "preventive action".<ref name="The Fallen Star" />
