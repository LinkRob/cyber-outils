A '''coma''' was a state of deep, prolonged unconsciousness resulting from disease or injury, from which a person could not be aroused. During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]] fell into a coma after sustaining grave injuries at the [[Battle of Haruun Kal]].<ref name="Kanan 7">[[Kanan 7|''Kanan'' 7]]</ref> During the [[rebellion on Akiva]], [[Norra Wexley]] also fell into a coma after crashing in her [[starfighter]].<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref> [[Finn]] entered a coma after sustaining [[lightsaber]] injuries from [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] during the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]].<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> He later awoke surrounded by a [[flexpoly bacta suit]].<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
