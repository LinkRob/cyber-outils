'''Dubat Krom''' was a [[Kakala]] from [[Corellia]] who served as the leader of the [[Broken Chain Clan]]. During the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the planet [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GEDates">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], including ''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]'', to [[34 ABY]].</ref> there was a 1,550 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credit]] [[bounty]] on Krom for property damage, piracy, and theft of personal property belonging to [[Oga Garra]]. Krom hid in one of the buildings at [[Black Spire Outpost]] and was found by an individual who had been assigned Krom's bounty by the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]].<ref name="BBH" />
