'''Alcohol''', also known as '''booze''' and '''hooch''', was a type of drink that came in several forms and had mind-altering effects on certain organic [[species]].

==Types of alcohol==
Various type of beverages consumed by [[Sentience|sentients]] such as [[ale]]s,<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref> [[liquor]]s, and [[wine]]s contained alcohol.<ref name="DD">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> Alcoholic drinks could be divided into several categories: wines were made by fermenting [[fruit]] or other [[Plant|vegetation]] with a rising agent, [[mead]]s were made by fermenting biological [[sugar]]s, [[Ale|beers]] were brewed from grains, and liquors were made from various pressed, fermented, and blended ingredients.<ref name="Wine in Dreams">{{StoryCite|story=The Wine in Dreams|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref>

Other words for alcohol were "booze"<ref name="Aphra 16">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 16|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 16]]</ref> and "hooch".<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

==Side-effects==
{{Quote|You may be surprised what people reveal when they have been drinking.|Plo Koon to Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Trackdown">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref>}}
[[File:Drunk_clones.png|thumb|left|250px|Drunk [[clone trooper]]s socializing with [[Fives]] outside the [[79's]]]]
Excessive alcohol consumption caused temporary intoxication (or "drunkenness"), typically followed by unpleasant physical aftereffects in the form of a [[hangover]].<ref name="LS" /> According to [[Sinjir Rath Velus]], [[Kowakian rum]] caused the worst cases of hangover, making people "plead for [[death]] while hiding in the darkness under [their] bedcovers or even under the bed itself."<ref name="AEE">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref>

One danger of intoxication was impaired judgement: While [[gambling]] in the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Casino]], the [[Khamarill]] [[Dobbu Scay]] became inebriated<ref name="TLJ Expanded" /> and mistook the [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]] for a [[slot machine]]. The [[droid]] ended up accumulating many of Scay's [[Cantocoin]]s.<ref name="EpVIII" /> During a mission to the [[Coruscant Underworld]], [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Plo Koon]] went into a [[Moshi Bar|cantina]] to find information on [[bounty hunter]] [[Aurra Sing]], due to people being more likely to reveal information while intoxicated.<ref name="Trackdown" />

Drunkenness could also lead to unsteadiness and a staggering gait. This happened to a [[Quarren]] member of [[Gorian Shard's pirate crew|Gorian Shard's pirate gang]] as they were drinking with their [[Trandoshan]] friend on [[Nevarro]].<ref name="Pirate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 21: The Pirate}}</ref>

Alcohol could also lead to sleepiness: Part of a fake cover story once concocted by [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] was that his friend [[Brasso]] had a bottle of [[Nog (beverage)|nog]] at home and they went there and drank themselves to sleep.<ref name="Andor 1">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:Drunk_Quarren.png|thumb|right|250px|A drunk Quarren and a Trandoshan]]

Although tolerance for alcohol varied between individuals, it was possible to train one's body to strengthen its resistance.<ref name="DD" />

Alcohol could be addictive: [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] claimed that [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] [[Miramir|mother]] and [[Dathan|father]] had sold off their own daughter for drinking [[Currency|money]],<ref name="EpVIII" /> although it later turned out that they actually sold Rey to protect her from her grandfather, the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

Depending on its strength, an alcoholic drink could impart a burning impression in the [[mouth]] and digestive system.<ref name="Bloodline" />
