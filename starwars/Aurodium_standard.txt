The '''aurodium standard''' was a [[currency]] standard that existed in [[the galaxy]]. The standard shared its name with [[aurodium]],<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> a rare and valuable metal<ref name="Kaz's trophy">{{Databank|kaz-s-lucky-trophy|Kaz's Lucky Trophy}}</ref> used in currency.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] had long abandoned the standard and was instead partnering with major [[bank]]s, such as the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] and [[Bank of the Core]], to hold a reserve of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to ensure the Republic's currency remained stable.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The aurodium standard was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[roleplaying game]] sourcebook ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', which was released in [[2019]] by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Rise" /> The [[Aurodium standard/Legends|standard]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via the article "[[Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Praji]]," which was written by [[Nathan O'Keefe]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[February 5]], [[2009]].<ref name="Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Praji">{{Hyperspace|url=hyperspace/source/praji/20090205.html|text=Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Praji|int=Tinker, Tailor, Soldier, Praji}}</ref> In the real world, the [[Wikipedia:Gold standard|gold standard]] is an obsolete monetary system where the value of a nation's currency is directly tied to [[Wikipedia:Gold|gold]],<ref name="Investopedia gold standard">{{WebCite|url=https://www.investopedia.com/ask/answers/09/gold-standard.asp|text=What is the Gold Standard?|author=Lioudis, Nick K.|date=2019-02-03|work=[[Wikipedia:Investopedia|Investopedia]]|archivedate=20191128153034}}</ref> the [[Wikipedia:Latin|Latin]] word for which is ''aurum''.<ref name="Aurum">{{WebCite|url=http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.04.0059:entry=aurum|text=aurum|author=Charlton T. Lewis|work=[[Wikipedia:A Latin Dictionary|A Latin Dictionary]]|archivedate=20151113192130}}</ref>
