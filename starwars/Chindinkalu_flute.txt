The '''chindinkalu flute''' or '''chidinkalu flute''' was a [[music]]al instrument. One of which was played by [[Droopy McCool]],<ref name="Databank" />, a member of the [[Max Rebo Band]], who used a hollow [[chindinkalu plant]] to construct it.<ref name="SWCT" />
