Between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[assassin]] [[Corlis Rath]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] engaged each other in<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> a challenging<ref name="Jedi Knights 7" /> '''duel''' on the [[agriworld]] of [[Mina-Rau]]. Having [[poison]]ed and [[Death|killed]] the [[Jedi]] Master [[Fondar Etzis]] with a fast acting toxin, Rath drew Jinn to Mina-Rau to surprise and kill the Jedi,<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> whom he had been hunting because of an incident that occurred when Jinn was a [[Padawan]].<ref name="MoonofDeath">{{StoryCite|story=The Moon of Death|book=Free Comic Book Day 2025: Star Wars 1|bformatted=Free Comic Book Day 2025: ''Star Wars'' 1}}</ref> Arriving on Mina-Rau as part of an honor guard with fellow Master [[Mace Windu]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Aayla Secura]], Jinn put together that Etzis had been murdered and sensed the assassin's approach when he finally revealed himself, whereas Windu and Secura were caught off guard and knocked out via poison darts. Rath engaged [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|Jinn's lightsaber]] with<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> his energy-tipped blades<ref name="MoonofDeath" /> and revealed his vendetta against the Jedi had something to do with<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> the dangerous world<ref name="Jedi Knights 7" /> of [[Sinsara]], something that shocked Jinn and gave Rath and opening to stab him through the chest.<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> Poisoned, Jinn's condition was nearly fatal. In order to save Jinn's life, Secura and Windu needed to journey to Sinsara itself to find an antidote.<ref name="Jedi Knights 7" />
