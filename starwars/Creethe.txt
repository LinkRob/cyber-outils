'''Creethe'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> was an [[Abyssin]] [[smuggler]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> Alongside [[Lyo]] and a third smuggler,<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> Creethe smuggled illegal [[nerve disruptor]]s to [[Asusto]] to wait for their buyer. When the [[Jedi]] [[Lene Kostana]], [[Sifo-Dyas]], and [[Dooku]] went on a [[Mission to Asusto|mission]] to Asusto, they encountered the Abyssin smugglers. Blinded by anger, Dooku [[Death|killed]] the smugglers and destroyed their cargo.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
