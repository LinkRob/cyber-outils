'''Ara''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[pilot]] who accompanied [[Toul]] on a mission. In the past, she was raised in a hidden temple and was part of a group with five other young people who could see prophetic images on rocks when it rained within [[Dolgarak]]. Ara was determined to cut off the head from the Dark Side in order to turn the tide of the war against the Sith. In the present, she petitioned this idea to the [[Jedi Council]], explaining to them that one large statue represents [[Light side of the Force|light]] and the other [[Dark side of the Force|dark]]. After asking them to approve a mission there, one of the members says to trust the [[the Force]] and agrees with her, sending Toul along with her.
