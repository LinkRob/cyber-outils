'''Advanced Marksman-H Training Remotes''' were remote droids used for practice or to help in battle. They were equipped with shields, unlike other previous models. They are sold at [[Mubo]]'s [[Droid Depot]] on [[Batuu]] by [[34 ABY]]. They could also be found in many various containers all around Batuu.<ref name="LastCall">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge – Last Call]]''</ref>
