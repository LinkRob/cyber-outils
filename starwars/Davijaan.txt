'''Davijaan''', designated '''CC-2237''' and also known by the [[callsign]] "'''Odd Ball'''," was a [[Clone Commander]] and [[Clone trooper pilot|pilot]] during the [[Clone Wars]] who participated in campaigns such as the [[Battle of Teth]], the [[Battle of Umbara]], the [[Battle of Coruscant]], and the [[Battle of Utapau]]. A skilled [[pilot]], Odd Ball flew an assortment of various [[starfighter]]s including a [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]], a [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]] and an [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170]]. During the Clone Wars, Odd Ball often served under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
The [[clone trooper]] CC-2237,<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> who eventually took on the name Davijaan,<ref name="Build Falcon 36">{{BuildFalconCite|36|Starship Fact File}}</ref> was a [[human]] [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] created on [[Kamino]].<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> He also received the code name "Odd Ball,"<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> which further served as his [[callsign]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> Used as if it was his name, "Odd Ball" became a common way to refer to the [[clone trooper pilot]].<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> While on Kamino, Odd Ball received early training as part of the [[pilot]] program.<ref name="HC 45" />

===The Clone Wars===
====The early days====
{{Quote|Thank you, sir. Sorry I panicked.''"<br />"''That's all right, Odd Ball. It happens to everybody.|Odd Ball and Obi-Wan Kenobi, during the Battle of Teth|audio=OddBallPanicked-TCWf.flac|<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>}}
With the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]] following the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], Odd Ball, now a [[Clone Commander]] and leader of [[Clone Flight Squad Seven]], was sent to a [[Coruscant flight academy|flight academy]] on the Republic capital [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]] for additional training. There, Odd Ball learned new tactics and tricks, which he passed down to the pilots under his command. As the [[squadron commander]] of Squad Seven, Odd Ball fell under the leadership of [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and under the structure of his [[Open Circle Fleet|Open Circle Armada]].<ref name="HC 45" />

[[File:OddBallV19Torrent-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Odd Ball during the Battle of Teth]]
During the Republic's attempt to rescue [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] kidnapped [[Rotta|son]], Odd Ball was called in to assist the [[troop]]s who were fighting [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Confederacy military|forces]] during the [[Battle of Teth]]. Flying in a [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]],<ref name="TCW film" /> he and Squad Seven<ref name="HC 45" /> fought oncoming [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] alongside Kenobi. During the battle, Odd Ball was tailed by vulture droids and remarked that he was unable to shake them. [[General]] Kenobi came to the pilot's aid and destroyed the droids, saving Odd Ball. The clone pilot apologized for panicking, and Kenobi assured him that it happened to everybody.<ref name="TCW film" />

====Battle of Umbara====
[[File:OddballCC2237-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|CC-2237 and a wingman conduct a bombing run during the Battle of Umbara.]]
{{Quote|Good old Odd Ball. Always on target.|[[Jesse]]|<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" />}}
At the start of the [[Battle of Umbara]], Odd Ball participated alongside the rest of Squad Seven,<ref name="HC 45" /> and he and another bomber were called in to provide reinforcements. They bombed a vulnerable enemy position moments after the last of the clone forces evacuated the area.<ref name="Darkness on Umbara" />

====Battle of Cato Neimoidia====
[[File:OddBall-CC2237-SWZ.png|thumb|left|220px|"Odd Ball" on Cato Neimoidia with Plo Koon]]
Odd Ball was present on [[Cato Neimoidia]], escorting [[Jedi]] General [[Plo Koon]] in the [[atmosphere]].<ref name="Odd Ball (ARC-170)">{{FFGXW2|set=ARC-170 Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname="Odd Ball"}}</ref> His unit,<ref name="Episode III" /> Clone Flight Squad Seven, participated in the [[Battle of Cato Neimoidia]].<ref name="Squad Seven Veteran">{{FFGXW2|set=ARC-170 Starfighter Expansion Pack|cardname=Squad Seven Veteran}}</ref>

====Battle of Coruscant====
{{Quote|Clone Flight Squad Seven launched from the carrier ''Ro-Ti-Mundi''. Our mission was to escort Jedi Generals Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker to the Separatist flagship ''Invisible Hand'' and cover them while they executed a boarding action.|Odd Ball's account of the Battle of Coruscant|<ref name="On the Front Lines" />}}
[[File:OddBallARC170-SWM.png|thumb|right|250px|Odd Ball during the Battle of Coruscant]]
Odd Ball participated in the [[Battle of Coruscant]], leading Clone Flight Squad Seven to the ''[[Invisible Hand]]'' for Kenobi and [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Episode III" /> He ordered the squad's V-19 Torrent [[starfighter]]s to take point. This group of fighters punched a hole through the [[droid tri-fighter]]s. This allowed the Jedi and the [[squad]]'s [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]] to move towards the ''Invisible Hand''. When the Jedi landed in the ship's [[hangar]], the squad eliminated priority targets and stayed close in case they were needed for aid. He did not expect the Jedi to survive.<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

====Battle of Utapau====
When it was discovered that the leader of the [[Separatist Droid Army]], General [[Grievous]], was hiding on the planet of [[Utapau]], Kenobi and the [[212th Attack Battalion]] were sent. Clone troopers engaged [[battle droid]]s in a heated confrontation, whilst Kenobi had his final confrontation with Grievous, resulting in the [[cyborg]]'s death.

Later, however, [[Order 66]] was issued by [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Episode III" /> As the contingency was issued, Odd Ball was in battle above Utapau<ref name="Databank" /> as part of [[Obi-Wan's fleet]].<ref name="HC 41">{{HelmetCollectionCite|41|Databank A-Z}}</ref> Odd Ball and the rest of Squad Seven on Utapau turned on the [[Utapau Skyforce]] and subjugated the planet. He then coordinated the rest of Squad Seven in an effort to hunt down Kenobi, as his body was not found after his reported death, coordinating ships to search the area around [[Pau City]].

Odd Ball and his unit later served the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="HC 45" />

==Personality and traits==
A human male clone of the bounty hunter Jango Fett,<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" /> CC-2237 was 1.83 [[meter]]s tall<ref name="Databank" /> and had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="Episode III" /> He proved to be a skilled pilot during the Clone Wars and was apologetic towards Obi-Wan Kenobi when the Jedi provided assistance at the Battle of Teth.<ref name="TCW film" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a veteran clone pilot, Odd Ball was an expert at starfighter,<ref name="Episode III" /> and starship piloting, as well as military tactics.<ref name="TCW Encyclopedia" />

==Equipment==
Initially during the Clone Wars, Odd Ball donned standard [[Phase I clone trooper armor|Phase I armor]] for all clone trooper pilots. Later in the war, he switched to the [[Phase II clone trooper armor|Phase II]] variant which featured a fully enclosed helmet and breathing apparatus. Still later in the war, he adopted the newer open-helmet design, which he wore during the Battle of Coruscant.<ref name="Episode III" />

[[File:OddBall.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Davijaan's helmet during the Battle of Coruscant]]
During the Battle of Coruscant, Davijaan wore the newer design of helmet that was primarily white in color with a black stripe down its center and three curved yellow markings crossed by a black symbol directly above the helmet's visor on the right side of Odd Ball's head. A yellow pattern also ran along the top of the two circular sides of the helmet.<ref name="Episode III" /> On a separate occasion, Odd Ball wore a helmet of the same make with differing coloring, still being primarily white but having a red stripe with a smaller yellow stripe inside it down the center with two thinner lines curving down the back of the helmet and then around under each cheek. The circular sides of the helmet each had three red stripes on top and a red symbol on the side.<ref name="HC 45" /> Another clone wore a helmet with this patterning during the Battle of Coruscant but perished.<ref name="Episode III" />

==Behind the scenes==
CC-2237 was portrayed by [[Temuera Morrison]] in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. He also appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]] and [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|television series]], in which he was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="TCW film" />

In [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 45|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 45]], predominantly in the first half of the magazine, Odd Ball is first referred to as a Clone Commander. In the second half of the magazine, the rank changes to [[Clone Captain]]. Because [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']] also refers to him as a Clone Commander, this article assumes that the rank of Clone Captain is erroneous.
