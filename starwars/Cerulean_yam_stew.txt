'''Cerulean yam stew''' was a type of [[stew]] made using [[yam]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was listed as costing six [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on both the menu at the [[Hot Pot]] [[Restaurant|eatery]] in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|market]] of the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] and at the [[Daruda Diner]] in the town of [[Jaunta's Hope]], both located on the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cerulean yam stew was created for the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Prior to the game's release, the menu mentioning the stew was visible during a gameplay preview released on [[June 13]], [[2023]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
