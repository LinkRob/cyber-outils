'''B-wing heavy assault starfighters''' were a type of heavy assault [[starfighter]] used primarily as an attack [[Starship|vessel]] against [[capital ship]]s and escorting other fighter [[squadron]]s and convoys.<ref name="RSOWM" />

==History==
The B-wing [[starfighter]] was originally designed by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Quarrie]], who was convinced by [[Hera Syndulla]] to let the rebel group [[Phoenix Squadron]] use his creation to fight against the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Wings">{{Rebels|Wings of the Master}}</ref> The B-wing was often the most heavily armed starfighter in the skies and it took inspiration from the [[T-6 shuttle]] and the [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]. The B-wings were known as the "blade wing" line.<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] used [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]]s in many battles, including the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Episode VI" /> The [[New Republic]] used them at the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="BS: Jakku">{{InsiderCite|172|Blade Squadron: Jakku}}</ref> During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[Resistance]] used a newer [[B-wing Mark III|variant]] at the [[Battle of Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX" />
