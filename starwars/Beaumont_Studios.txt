'''Beaumont Studios''' was an art studio that illustrated a number of [[Wikipedia:Spanish language|Spanish]] pop-up books featuring characters from [[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|''Star Wars Droids'']] and [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] for Editorial Roma in the 1980s. Around the same time, Beaumont Studios also provided artwork for a series of ''[[Star Wars]]''-themed short comics published under the ''[[MyComyc]]'' imprint.
