A '''bayonet''' was a sharp blade that could be mounted on the end of a [[blaster rifle]] or other long-barreled [[rifle]]. Bayonets could be purpose-made to attach to a weapon,<ref name="COTR" /> be regular [[Knife|knives]] attached to rifles by improvised means,<ref name="Bounty Hunters">{{TCW|Bounty Hunters (episode)}}</ref> or be built into the weapon itself, as with certain models of [[bo-rifle]].<ref name="AB-75">{{Databank|lasan-honor-guard-ab-75-bo-rifle|Lasan Honor Guard AB-75 Bo-Rifle}}</ref>
