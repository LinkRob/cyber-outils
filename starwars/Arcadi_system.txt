The '''Arcadi system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]. It was located in grid square N-10 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Arcadian Jazz]]|system=Arcadia [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> [[Arcadian Jazz]].<ref name="THR 1">[[The High Republic (2021) 1|''The High Republic'' (2021) 1]]</ref>
