"'''Boba Begins'''" is an article appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 99|''Star Wars Insider'' 99]]. It was written by [[Pete Vilmur]]. The article proves that [[Boba Fett/Legends|Boba Fett's]] first appearance was in the San Anselmo's Community Fair and Parade in [[September 24]], [[1978]], nearly two months before ''[[The Star Wars Holiday Special]]'' was shown. It was republished in [[Star Wars: Rogues, Scoundrels, and Bounty Hunters|''Star Wars Insider: Rogues, Scoundrels, and Bounty Hunters'']].
