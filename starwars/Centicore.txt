The '''''Centicore''''' was an [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]] in service to the [[Imperial Navy]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. At some point, the [[light cruiser]] fired shots at a target from behind an [[asteroid]].<ref name="Armada" /> It was 230 [[meter]]s in length<ref name="Note" /> and equipped with [[engine]]s and two<ref name="Armada" /> twin light [[turbolaser]] batteries.<ref name="Note" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Centicore'' was first mentioned in the [[2016]] [[Imperial Light Cruiser Expansion Pack]] for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' miniatures game [[Star Wars: Armada|''Star Wars'': Armada]], on a card of the same name.<ref name="Armada" />
