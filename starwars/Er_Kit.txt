'''Er'Kit''' was a [[desert]] [[planet]]<ref name="SWC">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> located in the [[Noonian sector]] of<ref name="SWSystems" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Er'Kit (species)|Er'Kit]] [[species]].<ref name="SWC" /> Before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Er'Kit [[senator]] [[Danry Ledwellow]] took bribes from [[Thalassian]] [[enslaver]]s, allowing them to use his planet for their illicit activities in exchange for a private army of [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank|IG-227 ''Hailfire''-class droid tanks]].<ref name="Commander" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Saponza]] and his [[Saponza's partner|partner]] traveled to Er'Kit to quash the [[Slavery|slaver]] group [[Blood Dawn]].<ref name="Commander" /> They were helped by the Er'Kit [[Dyro Felldor]]; however, Felldor had plans of his own and eventually betrayed them.<ref name="Commander2">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', ''Secret Alliances'' campaign</ref>
