'''''Droids\Ewoks''''' is a card game released by the Spanish company [[Heraclio Fournier]] in [[1986]]. The set consists of 32 game cards plus one instruction card. Each card depicts characters and scenarios from the [[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|''Star Wars Droids'']] cartoon series, the first season of the [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] cartoon series, and the toy packaging for the [[Star Wars: Droids (toy line)|''Star Wars: Droids'']] and [[Star Wars: Ewoks (toy line)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'']] toy lines. Each card also includes a short synopsis of plot events from those TV series.
