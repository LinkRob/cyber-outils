'''Elite speeder bikes''' were upgraded [[speeder bike]]s employed by the [[First Order]]. Piloted by experienced [[Color|black]]-[[armor]]ed [[Elite stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[scout]]s, the [[speeder]]s featured heavy [[blaster]]s and powerful [[engine]]s. The speeder bikes were black in color and featured red stripes that matched the red stripes featured on their pilots' breastplates. [[Laser cannon]]s were placed on either side of the speeder's nose, and two more cannons that pivot up and down were placed between the 'fins' on the nose. The engine could be accessed by lifting the paneling in front of the speeder's control sticks. The bikes were generally used for high-risk missions.<ref name="Elite Bike" />

They were carried aboard [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The elite speeder bike and its uniquely colored stormtrooper rider were first introduced in the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (toy line)|marketing campaign]] that led up the release of the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'']]. However, neither the bike nor the pilot appeared in the film itself.<ref name="Elite Bike" />

It later made appearances in the [[2020]] episode "[[The Escape (episode)|The Escape]]" in the animated series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''.<ref name="The Escape" />
