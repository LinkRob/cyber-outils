'''BL-R4''' was an [[astromech droid]] affiliated with the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]. A friend of fellow GSCR member [[Quinn (Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts)|Quinn]], he had [[Gender|male programming]] and a shy personality. While on a [[Flight to Batuu (Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts)|flight]] to [[Batuu]]<ref name="CargoBay" /> sometime after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">In the Creature Challenge activity of [[Star Wars: Cargo Bay|''Star Wars'': Cargo Bay]], the host mentions that [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] and [[Chewbacca]] work for the Resistance, while Rey later mentions that she and Chewbacca ran into rathtars previously, an event depicted in {{Film|VII}}. {{FilmDate|VII}}</ref> BL-R4 was encharged with [[Foodstuff|feeding]] some of the more dangerous creatures kept in the [[Unidentified ship (Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts)|starship]]. During said flight, BL fed meat to a [[wampa]], and while trying to feed a [[rathtar]], it was attacked and sent flying out of the enclosure, while the rathtar was inadvertently set loose.<ref name="CargoBay" />
