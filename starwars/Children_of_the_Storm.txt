The '''Children of the Storm''' were a group within the [[Nihil]] that were led by [[scientist]] [[Baron]] [[Boolan]]. They were former [[Force-sensitive]]s whom the baron had experimented on.<ref name="Eye of Darkness" /> They were [[Jedi hunter]]s who captured [[Force-sensitive|Force users]] for Boolan to perform experiments on.<ref name="Boolan Databank" />
