'''Felicita Victoria Prokešová''', or '''Felicita Victorie Prokešová''', (born [[February 11]], [[1997]], in [[Wikipedia:Třebíč|Třebíč]], [[Wikipedia:Vysočina Region|Vysočina]], [[Wikipedia:Czech Republic|Czech Republic]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Czechs|Czech]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]] who voiced the [[Force-sensitive]] [[Aniseya family|twins]] [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha]] and [[Mae-ho Aniseya|Mae Aniseya]] in the [[Wikipedia:Czech language|Czech]] [[Wikipedia:Dubbing|dub]] of the [[2024]] live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''. The voice actor also performed additional voices in [[Destiny (The Acolyte)|two]] [[Choice|episodes]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|I'm dubbing Mae. She is sharp, so I think we're similar in that, and it's probably also why the role is easier for me.|translated=1|Felicita Victoria Prokešová|<ref name="Promo" />}}
[[File:AcolytePost-Poster.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Felicita Victoria Prokešová voiced both Mae and Osha Aniseya.]]
Felicita Victoria Prokešová,<ref name="Felicita" /> also spelled as Felicita Victorie Prokešová,<ref name="Facebook">{{Facebook|username=1699021574|url=profile.php?id=1699021574&sk=about|name=Felicita Victorie Prokešová|archivefile=File:FelicitaVictoriaProkesova-Facebook.png}}</ref> is a [[Wikipedia:Czechs|Czech]]<ref name="Felicita" /> [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]]<ref name="LF" /> born on [[February 11]], [[1997]], in [[Wikipedia:Třebíč|Třebíč]],<ref name="Felicita" /> [[Wikipedia:Vysočina Region|Vysočina]], [[Wikipedia:Czech Republic|Czech Republic]].<ref name="Location" /> The voice actor worked on the [[Studio Virtual]]-produced [[Wikipedia:Czech language|Czech]] [[Wikipedia:Dubbing|dub]] of the live-action [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'', voicing [[Amandla Stenberg]]'s characters&mdash;the [[Force-sensitive]] [[twin]]s<ref name="LF">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> [[Verosha Aniseya|Verosha]] and [[Mae-ho Aniseya]].<ref name="Destiny">{{Acolyte|Destiny}}</ref> The first and second episodes aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|229|Star Wars: The Acolyte Companion|''Star Wars: The Acolyte'' Companion}}</ref> Prokešová voiced the [[Aniseya family|Aniseya]] siblings in both of the episodes,<ref name="LF" /><ref name="RJ">{{Acolyte|Revenge / Justice}}</ref> then again in the series'<ref name="Day">{{Acolyte|Day (episode)}}</ref> fourth episode "[[Day (episode)|Day]]," which aired two weeks later on [[June 18]].<ref name="Insider" /> The voice actor further provided the voice for the Aniseya siblings in<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref><ref name="TC">{{Acolyte|Teach / Corrupt}}</ref> the fifth episode "[[Night]]" and the sixth episode "[[Teach / Corrupt]]," which debuted on [[June 25]] and [[July 2]] of 2024 respectively.<ref name="Insider" /> Finally, Prokešová voiced Osha and Mae in<ref name="TA">{{Acolyte|The Acolyte (episode)}}</ref> the eighth episode, "[[The Acolyte (episode)|The Acolyte]]," which aired on [[July 16]] of that year.<ref name="Insider" />

In addition to the main role of Stenberg's characters in those six episodes, Prokešová also provided additional voices in "[[Destiny]]"<ref name="Destiny" /> and "[[Choice]],"<ref name="Choice">{{Acolyte|Choice}}</ref> the third and seventh episodes, which released on [[June 11]] and [[July 9]], 2024, respectively.<ref name="Insider" /> In a promotional video posted on [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] the [[June 5|day after]] ''The Acolyte''{{'s}} two-episode premiere, Prokešová discussed the role of Mae and how their similar personalities made the job easier, but due to the difficulty of dubbing the voice actor still needed guidance. Prokešová also spoke about enjoying working on ''The Acolyte'', and feeling honored to have gotten to work on the series. Despite discussing Mae in the video, the voice actor did not acknowledge the role of Osha.<ref name="Promo" />
