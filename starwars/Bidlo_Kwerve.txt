'''Bidlo Kwerve''' was a [[human]] or [[near-human]] employee of the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] who competed against the [[Twi'lek]] [[Bib Fortuna]] for the position of [[majordomo]] to the [[Hutt]] and as a result ended up as an early [[Death|victim]] of the crime lord's pet [[rancor]],<ref name="SWCL" /> [[Pateesa]],<ref name="ALD">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. 

==Biography==
By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Bidlo Kwerve had begun to work for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] and competed with the [[Twi'lek]] [[Bib Fortuna]] for the position of the [[Hutt]]'s [[majordomo]]. Kwerve would lose Jabba's favor and his life from this competition. Fortuna gifted their boss for his [[birthday]] a pet [[rancor]],<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> which Jabba would name "[[Pateesa]]" and become enamored with.<ref name="ALD" /> Not long after Jabba began using Pateesa as a form of [[Death penalty|execution]], Kwerve would be dropped in a pit and fed to the [[Dathomir]]ian predator.<ref name="SWCL" />

[[File:Acme-RancorDemise.png|thumb|left|300px|Posthumously, Bidlo Kwerve would be an instrument of his own killer's death.]]
Kwerve's head was left largely intact following his killing by Pateesa, and his [[Bone|skull]] remained in the rancor's pit below the throne room,<ref name="SWCL" /> overlooked until one morning in [[4 ABY]] when the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] was dropped into the pit after threatening Jabba. Without a weapon, the [[Jedi]] picked up the skull and threw it across the pit at a door control button as the rancor attempted to eat him. The button's activation caused the door to the pit to lower onto Pateesa's neck and kill the creature. This, coupled with Jabba's subsequent death during the [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue]] of [[Han Solo]], left Kwerve and countless others inadvertently avenged.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bidlo Kwerve's skull first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] in [[1983]].<ref name="ROTJ" /> The [[Bidlo Kwerve/Legends|character]] was then introduced without being linked to the skull in ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'', released by [[West End Games]] in [[1987]].<ref name="TSWS">''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''</ref> It was not until the release of the "[[Jabba's Palace Limited]]" set of [[Decipher]]'s [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]] in [[1998]] that the skull from ''Return of the Jedi'' was identified as being Kwerve's.<ref name="ccg">{{CCG|set=Jabba's Palace Limited|cardname=Skull}}</ref> This identification was confirmed to be [[canon]] by [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], a reference book released in [[2016]].<ref name="SWCL" />
