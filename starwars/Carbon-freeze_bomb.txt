'''Carbon-freeze bombs''' are a type of [[grenade]] that use [[Carbon]] chemicals to freeze an nearby area.

==Behind the scenes==
In ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'', [[Han Solo]] is able to throw a Carbon-freeze grenade as Finisher move in Toyboxes, while in ''Star Wars'' Playsets Han throws a [[CryoBan grenade]]. Enemies or characters hit by the explosion will be frozen in a raw block of carbonite in a pose mimicing Han when he was frozen in Carbonite. The Carbon-freeze bomb uses the same design as CryoBan grenades.
