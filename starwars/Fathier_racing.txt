'''Fathier racing''' was the practice of racing [[fathier]]s on [[racetrack]]s for sport. The fathier races were a popular event and a source of heavy [[gambling]] revenue at the [[Canto Bight]] racetracks on the planet [[Cantonica]], though the fathiers used in the races were often abused.<ref name="tlj" /> Historically, there had been attempts to regulate or outright ban the sport, but all failed due to the level of greed involved in it.<ref name="Complete Visual New">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

Betting on fathier races for high stakes was a popular practice in Canto Bight, using the the gambling [[currency]] [[Cantocoin]]s. Each fathier participating in a race was given odds of winning, and those with lower odds carried higher risk, but a correspondingly higher payout for a winning bet on that fathier.<ref name="The Ride">{{StoryCite|story=The Ride|book=Canto Bight (novella collection)}}</ref> Sometimes races were so close that the length of two competing fathiers' [[nose]]s could be the deciding factor.<ref name="Complete Visual New" />

[[Paige Tico]] dreamed of someday watching one of these races.<ref name="Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref>

[[Racer]]s who rode in the fathier races were known as [[jockey]]s.<ref name="TVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

[[Rael Averross]] mused in [[40 BBY]] that fathier racing would become obsolete if [[the galaxy]] as a whole discovered the joys of [[Pijal varactyl]]s,<ref name="Master & Apprentice">''[[Master & Apprentice]]''</ref> however as of [[34 ABY]], this had not happened.<ref name="tlj" />

As early as [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''The High Republic: Path of Vengeance'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> having "a fathier in the race" was a [[List of phrases and slang|slang term]] referring to having a stake in something.<ref name="Path of Vengeance">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>
