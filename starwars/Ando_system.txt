The '''Ando system''' was located in the [[Lambda sector]], a part of the regions known as the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Rise" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="Slice" /> It lay in grid square Q-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Location note" /> and contained the [[planet]] [[Ando]],<ref name="Rise" /> the [[Waterworld|aquatic]] [[homeworld]] of the [[Aqualish]] [[species]],<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> And [[Ando Prime]],<ref name="Appendix" /> an ice-covered planet.<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> The Ando system was connected via [[Manda–Mon Gazza hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to the astronomical objects [[Mon Gazza]] and [[Leritor system|Leritor]].<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref>

During the [[Separatist Crisis]], a period of turmoil occurred on Ando that staggered the rest of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> As a result, the Ando system seceded from the [[Galactic Republic]] to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] through the influence of Aqualish [[Senator]] [[Po Nudo]], who had represented Ando in the [[Galactic Senate]] as its [[Junior Delegate]] during the secessionist movement and the subsequent [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and Confederacy.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Ando system was introduced in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] on a [[Galaxy|galactic]] map included with the [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[roleplaying game]] produced by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] and released in [[2016]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The system later received its name in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to Fantasy Flight Games' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Rise" />

The [[Ando system/Legends|system]] was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in ''[[The Last Command]]'', a [[1993]] novel written by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the final volume of ''[[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Last Command">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref> In [[1994]], the Ando system made its first appearance in "[[Courier's Deal]]," a short companion narrative published in [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' sourcebook ''[[Creatures of the Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Courier's Deal">{{WEGCite|story=Courier's Deal|book=Creatures of the Galaxy}}</ref>
