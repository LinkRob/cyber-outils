"'''316'''" was an individual who served the [[Galactic Empire]] aboard the first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]], a [[moon]]-sized [[superweapon]]. Even as [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo]] were infiltrating the Death Star to try and [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescue]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] from [[Detention Block AA-23]], "316" was told to report to the station's control.<ref name="Episode IV" />
