The '''E-11P blaster''' was a [[blaster pistol]] used by [[coastal defender stormtrooper]]s, a specialized variant of [[stormtrooper]]<ref name="Dawn of Rebels" /> active under the [[Coastal Defense Division]]<ref name="Imperial Troops">{{EncyclopediaCite|Imperial Troops}} {{C|French edition only}}</ref> and deployed to beachside locations. The pistol was [[Color|tan]] in color, matching the [[Shoretrooper armor|armor]] of the shoretroopers themselves, and tipped with [[water]]-sealed cooling vanes. Those vanes also had built-in filters to keep out sand.<ref name="Dawn of Rebels" /> A black variant of the E-11P blaster was seen carried by a member of the [[Ghorman Front]] prior to the [[Ghorman Massacre]].<ref name="Who Are You?">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref>
