"'''A Change of Command'''" is a short story written by [[Paul Sudlow]], published in ''[[The Jedi Academy Sourcebook]]''. It depicts [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles's]] promotion to the rank of [[General/Legends|general]] prior to ''[[Star Wars: Dark Empire]]''.

==Plot summary==
Shortly after the [[Second Battle of Coruscant|evacuation of Coruscant]] in [[10 ABY/Legends|10 ABY]], [[Wedge Antilles/Legends|Wedge Antilles]] is brought to [[Admiral/Legends|Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar/Legends|Gial Ackbar's]] office at Headquarters [[Frigate]]. Ackbar informs him that the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire's]] surprise attack on the [[Capital/Legends|capital]] wiped out over 60% of the [[New Republic Army]]'s command staff. As a result, many lower ranking members of the army are being promoted to fill in the gaps. Ackbar offers to promote Antilles to the rank of [[General/Legends|general]] and put him on his command staff. Antilles refuses at first, arguing that he would contribute more by continuing to lead [[Rogue Squadron/Legends|Rogue Squadron]]. The two then compromise by having Antilles be promoted but still be kept in charge of Rogue Squadron.
