The '''Defoliator Deployment Tank (DDT)''', also known simply as the '''Defoliator Tank''', was a modified [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tank]] designed by [[Neimoidian]] weapons developer [[General]] [[Lok Durd]] for his [[defoliator]] weapon during the [[Clone Wars]]. The DDT was first deployed<ref name="Defenders of Peace" /> in [[21 BBY]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> on [[Maridun]]. There, Durd demonstrated its effects on two [[B1-series battle droid]]s near his [[Separatist outpost (Maridun)|outpost]] for [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. After that DDT's destruction during the [[defense of Maridun]],<ref name="Defenders of Peace" /> another DDT, in truth the second and last prototype,<ref name="HC 13">{{HelmetCollectionCite|13|Databank A-Z}}</ref> later saw use in the [[Battle of Dathomir]], only to be destroyed by the [[Nightsisters]].<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref>
