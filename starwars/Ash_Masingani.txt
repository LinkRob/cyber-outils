'''Ash Masingani''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] individual who served as a [[Jedi Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The High Republic: Seeds of Starlight]]'' take place in [[228 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:THRContent228BBY|here]]. ''Seeds of Starlight'' establishes [[Cam Lindon]]'s accident on [[Coruscant]] occurred four [[Standard year|years]] prior, therefore placing it in [[232 BBY]].</ref> she was present on<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" /> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Coruscant]] and was traveling in the world's [[skylane]]s alongside the [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Cam Lindon]] in a [[Landspeeder|hovercar]] when the [[Speeder|vehicle]] was struck by an out-of-control [[jet bike]]. The collision resulted in the [[death]] of the [[Unidentified hovercar pilot|hovercar pilot]] and caused Lindon to fall several levels onto a landing platform. Masingani, while badly wounded herself, commandeered another vehicle, which she used to [[pilot]] both herself and the [[youngling]] to the [[Jedi Temple medical bay|Jedi Temple's infirmary]]. Lindon's larynx was destroyed as a result of his fall and required a [[Vocabulator|vocoder]] to be installed in his throat.<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" />

In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> while [[Mission to investigate the blight|traveling]] on the [[Elite-class vessel|science ship]] ''[[Innovator]]'', Lindon&mdash;by then a [[Padawan]]&mdash;mentally recalled the accident that had destroyed his larynx. He considered his life had only been saved because of Masingani's quick thinking.<ref name="Seeds of Starlight" />
