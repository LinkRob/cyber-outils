'''Blue macarons''', or '''Nevarro Nummies''', were a type of [[cookie]] that could be found on [[Nevarro]] and were [[Diet|edible]] by [[human]]s and members of [[Din Grogu|Grogu's]] [[Yoda's species|species]]. In [[9 ABY]], a [[Unidentified student|young boy]] was eating the cookies while in a [[Nevarro school|class]] but had them stolen by the [[foundling]] Grogu, who also ate some. Grogu continued to eat the macarons as he was picked up by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], and eventually regurgitated them when Djarin [[Pilot|flew]] his [[gunship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', in a nauseating manner.

==Description==
Blue macarons,<ref name="SWI">{{InsiderCite|202|Star Wars: The Mandalorian Season Two Companion|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season Two Companion}}</ref> also known as Nevaro Nummies,<ref name="NummiesIU">{{SWCT|set=Galactic Cuisine - The Mandalorian|cardname=Grogu Treats himself to Nevarro Nummies|nolink=1}}</ref> were circular in shape and light teal in [[color]]. Producing a crunching noise when bitten, the [[cookie]]s could be [[Diet|eaten]] by both [[human]]s and members of a small tridactyl [[Yoda's species|species]]. They came in a silver metallic sleeve.<ref name="Chapter 12" />

==History==
{{Quote|Can I at least buy you a drink?''"<br />"''Sorry. I have some onboard maintenance I gotta take care of.|Greef Karga and Din Djarin, the latter referring to Grogu's puking of blue macarons|<ref name="Chapter 12" />}}
[[File:BlueYodaCookies.png|thumb|left|300px|Grogu consumes a blue macaron.]]
In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> a [[Gender|male]] [[Unidentified student|student]] learning from the [[protocol droid]]<ref name="Chapter 12" /> [[CB-3DU]]<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> on<ref name="Chapter 12" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]]<ref name="MandoVG" /> [[Nevarro]] ate from a package of the cookies during class. The [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]], who needed a place to keep his [[foundling]], [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], while he himself went on a [[Second skirmish on Nevarro|mission]], dropped the foundling off in the [[Nevarro school|classroom]] where the student was being taught. Grogu, seated next to the student, held out his [[Limb|hand]] and babbled as though to ask for a cookie<ref name="Chapter 12" />&mdash;the [[Youngling|young]] foundling was much more interested in the treat than the [[droid]]'s lesson.<ref name="Handbook">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Handbook]]''</ref> While the student refused to share and continued to enjoy his snack, Grogu, much to the student's surprise, used [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|move]] the entire sleeve of macarons to his own desk.<ref name="Chapter 12" /> He then proceeded to happily eat the macarons,<ref name="JN">[[The Mandalorian Season 2 Junior Novel|''The Mandalorian Season 2'' Junior Novel]]</ref> which he discovered a taste for.<ref name="SWI" />

When Djarin returned to depart Nevarro with Grogu, the [[youngling]] took the remaining cookies with him and continued to snack on them while the Mandalorian engaged a trio of [[Outland TIE fighter]]s aboard his [[gunship]], the ''[[Razor Crest]]''. During the fight, Grogu held the macarons over his head. He later vomited some of the cookies onto his [[robe]] following the [[TIE series|TIE fighters']] destruction, having been nauseated by Djarin's [[pilot]]ing. Djarin hastily&mdash;albeit ineffectively&mdash;tried to clean up the blue vomit with a cloth, referring to the incident as "onboard maintenance" via [[comlink]] to [[Marshal]] [[Carasynthia Dune]] and [[Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]]. Djarin and Karga had been discussing what they owed each other, and while Karga offered to repay the former with a drink, the Mandalorian declined, citing his need to clean up Grogu's mess.<ref name="Chapter 12" /> As he piloted away from Nevarro, Djarin took the last macaron from Grogu, though the youngling, no longer hungry, did not mind.<ref name="JN" /> Some time later, Grogu ate a blue macaron, as well as a bowl of [[soup]] and an [[arges frog]].<ref name="Cutest in the Galaxy">{{GroguCutest|3}}</ref>

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GS Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> blue macarons were available onboard the ''[[Halcyon]]'', a [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]], where they were served as dessert at dinner in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]]'s [[captain's table]]. A different dessert eventually replaced them.<ref name="Crown of Corellia" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Josh, our prop master, came to us and was asking, 'What do these cookies look like?' ''[&hellip;]'' We wanted it to be blue, 'cause it was like blue milk, and the prop master ended up baking macarons for it.|Jon Favreau|<ref name="Babish">{{YouTube|video=IGS0JouUgjM|text=Binging with Babish: Macarons from The Mandalorian (ft. Jon Favreau!)|channel=bgfilms|name=Babish Culinary Universe}}</ref>}}
[[File:CookieConcept.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Concept art of Grogu reaching for the cookies, by Christian Alzmann]]
Blue macarons first appeared in "[[Chapter 12: The Siege]],"<ref name="Chapter 12" /> the fourth episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|second season]] of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' that aired on [[November 19]], [[2020]].<ref name="SWI" /> Concept art for the cookies was revealed in the episode's credits,<ref name="Chapter 12" /> and a high-quality version was featured in the episode guide the following [[November 22|Monday]]. Done by [[Christian Alzmann]], the concept image showed a beige cookie,<ref name="Mando D">{{SW|url=news/mando-download-chapter-12-the-siege|text=Mando Download: "Chapter 12: The Siege"}}</ref> while the final product was blue.<ref name="Chapter 12" /> The cookies were first identified in "[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Season Two Companion|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian'' Season Two Companion]]," an article written by [[Kristin Baver]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 202|202nd]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="SWI" /> on [[April 27]], [[2021]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/72-star-wars-insider-202|text=Star Wars Insider #202}}</ref> The [[2023]] non-[[canon]] children's book ''[[The Mandalorian and Child]]'' features blue macarons in a scene depicting Djarin and Grogu aboard the ''Razor's Crest''.<ref name="Mando and Child">''[[The Mandalorian and Child]]''</ref>

Show-creator [[Jon Favreau]] later appeared on the [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] cooking show [[Wikipedia:Babish Culinary Universe#Binging with Babish|Binging with Babish]], where host Andrew Rea made the cookies from the show. Favreau explained that property master [[Josh Roth]] discussed how the cookies would look for the episode. Favreau wanted the cookies to be blue as a homage to the [[blue milk]] from the<ref name="Babish" /> [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="epIV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> Roth ended up baking [[Wikipedia:Macaron#France|macarons]] for the show, but instead of a traditional sandwiched macaron, he used half of the full cookie. Favreau described the flavor as that of [[Wikipedia:Blue raspberry flavor|blue raspberry]].<ref name="Babish" /> An officially licensed product from [[Wikipedia:Williams-Sonoma, Inc.|Williams-Sonoma]] titled "Nevarro Nummies" was later sold online.<ref name="Nummies">{{WebCite|url=https://www.williams-sonoma.com/products/nevarro-nummies-macarons|text=Nevarro Nummies™ Macarons|work=[[Wikipedia:Williams-Sonoma, Inc.|Williams-Sonoma, Inc.]]|archivedate=20201123221243|nolive=1}}</ref> and the name Nevarro Nummies was used to identify the cookies in a [[2021]] card set on the mobile application ''[[Star Wars: Card Trader]]''.<ref name="NummiesIU" />

Blue macarons' [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe as revealed in a [[StarWars.com]] article called for the ingredients [[Wikipedia:Almond meal|almond flour]], [[Wikipedia:Confectioners' sugar|confectioners' sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Egg white|egg whites]], [[Wikipedia:Sugar|sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla|vanilla]], and [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|blue food dye]] for the cookie. The filling calls for [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], [[Wikipedia:Confectioners' cream|confectioners' cream]], [[Wikipedia:Heavy cream|heavy cream]], vanilla, food dye, and [[Wikipedia:Raspberry jam|raspberry jam]].<ref name="OOU">{{SW|url=news/blue-milk-mando-macarons|text=Make Blue Milk Mando Macarons to Share With Everyone}}</ref> The cookies were also available for guests in the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida,<ref name="Crown of Corellia" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]],<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> and had its final voyage from [[September 28]] to [[September 30|30]], [[2023]].<ref name="StarcruiserClosure">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref>
