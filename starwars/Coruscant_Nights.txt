'''Coruscant Nights''' was a source of entertainment on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[musician]] known as [[Maximilian Rebo|Max Rebo]] was part of it. [[Painting|Posters]] were put up around [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] in order to promote Coruscant Nights.<ref name="Lethal">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref>

There were at least two two different poster designs: [[Max Reebo – Coruscant Nights|The first one]] showed [[Color|white]] text on a dark background and read "Max Reebo <small>''[sic]''"</small> – "Coruscant Nights". [[Coruscant Nights (poster)|The other poster]] showed the silhouette of two people against a blue background, and said "Coruscant Nights" at the top<ref name="Lethal" /> and "[[Coruscant Ballroom]]" at the bottom.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown">In "[[Lethal Trackdown]]," the [[Aurebesh]] text "[[Coruscant Ballroom]]" can be read at the bottom of a copy of the ''second'' [[Painting|poster]], glimpsed over the entrance to [[Moshi Bar]] as [[Plo Koon]] says, "You may be surprised what people reveal when they have been drinking." If you compare the design at bottom of this poster to the full poster seen ''inside'' the [[cantina]] (where only the "Coruscant Nights" parts can be deciphered), you can see that it is the same poster, meaning that it reads "Coruscant Nights" at the top and "Coruscant Ballroom" at the bottom. The full version of the poster ''over the cantina entrance'' can also be seen in the wide shot of the [[Jedi]] leaving the bar, but only from a distance.</ref> Fifteen [[Standard year|years]] after the Clone Wars ended, the first poster was used to decorate the walls of the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Spark">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref>
