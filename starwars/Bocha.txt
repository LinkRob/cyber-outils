'''Bocha''' was a flaky [[foodstuff]]. Typically served [[Temperature|hot]], the [[human]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] considered bocha delicious.<ref name="MtD" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> when Starros was kidnapped by the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s of [[Kara Xoo]]'s [[Kara Xoo's Tempest|Tempest]], she compared the pirates' expressions after she had been caught making an unsuccessful attempt to call for help to what they might make if they were looking at a plate of bocha.<ref name="MtD" />
