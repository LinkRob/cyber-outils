The '''Aleena''' were a [[species]] of short, [[Sentience|sentient]] [[reptile]]s<ref name="Dark Disciple" /> from the [[planet]] of [[Aleen]].<ref name="DB Aleena" /> The Aleena lived on the surface of their [[homeworld]], while the [[Kindalo]] lived in the [[Aleen Underworld|underground]], as was outlined in the [[Aleena–Kindalo treaty]] that ended [[Aleena–Kindalo war|their war]].<ref name="NoP" /> The Aleena were part of the [[Galactic Republic]], and their homeworld was home to a Republic relay station.<ref name="MM" /> Their [[Politics|government]] consisted of a [[king]], and during the [[Clone Wars]], they were ruled by an individual named [[Manchucho]].<ref name="DB Manchucho" /> They had a rocky natural habitat.<ref name="VE" /> It was known to the [[scholar]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]] that the Aleena were client species of the [[Infinite Empire|Rakatan Infinite Empire]], though there was little evidence of any occupation of their planet.<ref name="NoP" />

==Aleena in the galaxy==
One notable Aleena was [[Ratts Tyerell]], a [[podracer]] [[pilot]] who participated in the [[Boonta Eve Classic]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="SWGA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Tyerell [[death|perished]] during the race when he crashed his podracer in the [[Laguna Caves]].<ref name="TPM" />

During the [[Clone Wars]], [[King]] [[Manchucho]] was ruler of the Aleena people. He welcomed [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] relief forces when they came to assist the Aleena after groundquakes ravaged their planet. [[C-3PO]] translated the King's words for [[clone trooper]]s when they arrived.<ref name="MM" /> [[Tsui Choi]] was an Aleena Jedi<ref name="Ratts jedi">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1533099623178416130|Pablo Hidalgo|quote=Kashyyyk! Finally on-screen (and not part of a prime time musical variety show special!) Tsui Choi, the Aleena Jedi, is riding the can-cell that leads the Wookiee catamaran into frame here. I think production just called him "Ratts Jedi".|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/CfwTy}}</ref> who was active during the Clone Wars and traveled to the planet [[Kashyyyk]] when it was [[Battle of Kashyyyk|besieged]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], riding a [[can-cell]] ahead of the battle.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

Shortly after the formation of the [[Galactic Empire]], an Aleena named [[Fauja]] resided on [[Saleucami]]. Fauja informed [[cloning|clones]] [[Cut Lawquane]] and [[Hunter]] about the implementation of [[chain code]]s by the Empire.<ref name="Cut">{{TBB|Cut and Run}}</ref>

Early in the era of the Galactic Empire an Aleena [[crime lord]] named [[Looti Vario]] operated on the planet [[Janix]], where he formed an alliance with the former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] and police [[detective]] [[Captain]] [[Brander Lawson]].

In [[3 BBY]], an Aleena was a member of [[Skraik's gang]] stealing ship parts in [[Garel City]] on [[Garel]].<ref>{{RebelsMagCite|29|The Size of the Fight}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Aleena were first developed for the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], directed by [[George Lucas]]. Aleena was the name given to the species that [[Ratts Tyerell]], a character from ''Menace'', belonged to. Detailed conceptual art was designed by artist [[Terryl Whitlatch]]. The art was used to create a maquette for the character, and later, the digital model that would be used in the film. Several other Aleena appeared in the film, and the species reappeared in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. They also appeared in several episodes of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', and in numerous [[canon]] and [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] publications.
