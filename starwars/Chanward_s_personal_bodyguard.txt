The '''personal bodyguard''' of [[Duke]] [[Chanward]], an [[Noble|aristocrat]] of the [[Noble Court]] of the [[Anoat sector]], was [[assassin]]ated during the [[Iron Blockade]],<ref name="DD" /> an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] lockdown of the [[sector]] to prevent its occupants from learning of the [[death]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] during the Empire's crushing defeat at the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref> Suspicion for the murder fell on the duke's own staff.<ref name="DD" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
A replacement [[bodyguard]] was provided for the duke by the individual known as [[the Smuggler]], an ally of the Noble Court.<ref name="DD" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Chanward's personal bodyguard was created for the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]'', which was developed by [[Kabam]]<ref name="Uprising" /> and shut down in [[2016]].<ref name="ENDSWU">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?675751-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|date=2016-09-22}}</ref> The character was mentioned in an optional Crew Run mission, which this article assumes was successfully completed as part of 100% game completion.<ref name="DD" />
