'''Anaxes ruffian belts''' were a type of [[belt]] worn by common outlaws<ref name="Outlaws" />  on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|fort-anaxes|Fort Anaxes}}</ref> [[Anaxes]].<ref name="Outlaws" />  The [[Color|brown]] belt connected in the front with a gold colored circular buckle. On its back, it had space to store [[bacta vial]]s and an attached pouch. The belt also included an attached [[holster]] and space to store [[blaster module]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> one of the belts had been stored in the backroom of a building at the [[Pyke Syndicate]]'s [[Pyke poaching station|poaching station]] in the [[Lost Steppe]] region of [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] also located in the Outer Rim.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] snuck into the building and stole the belt from a container in the backroom.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Anaxes ruffian belt appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, the belt increased the number of [[grenade]]s that the player can carry.<ref name="Outlaws" />
