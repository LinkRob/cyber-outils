A '''zoo''' was located in [[Coronet City]]&mdash;the [[capital]] [[city]] of the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Corellia]]&mdash;and had creatures such as [[happabore]]s within.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> after she took the [[Shani]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]] into custody for attempting to [[Diet|eat]] a [[Bonbrak]]&mdash;a protected [[species]]&mdash;[[Chief Inspector]] [[Deemus Abrus]] of the [[Coronet City Chapter of the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] speculated that local authorities would just give Crufeela community service picking up happabore dung at the [[zoo]]. As the Shani was dragged into a [[turbolift]], Abrus informed Crufeela to not eat the happabores.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Coronet City zoo was mentioned in the [[2022]] young-adult novel ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'', written by [[Daniel José Older]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> [[Coronet Zoo]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' developed by [[BioWare]].<ref name="The Old Republic">''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''</ref>
