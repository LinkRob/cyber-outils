'''Friedrich Georg Beckhaus''' as credited as '''Friedrich G. Beckhaus''' is a [[Wikipedia:German|German]] [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice artist]] who voiced [[Bioto]] in the audio drama [[Dark Lord (German audio drama)|''Dark Lord'']].<ref name="Dark Lord" /> Later Beckhaus would voice [[Tera Sinube/Legends|Tera Sinube]] in ''[[The Clone Wars Episode 17 - Lightsaber Lost / The Mandalore Plot]]'' as part of the [[German Clone Wars audio dramas]].<ref name="TCW Drama 17">''[[The Clone Wars Episode 17 - Lightsaber Lost / The Mandalore Plot]]''</ref>
