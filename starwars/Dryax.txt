'''Dryaxes''' were a type of huge, [[humanoid]]-shaped [[Diet|predator]] found in the [[jungle]]s of the [[planet]] [[Wayland]]. They had glowing [[Color|red]] [[eye]]s and [[Wolf|lupine]] features. During or after [[18 BBY]], the [[Cloning|clones]] [[Omega]] and [[Crosshair]] encountered a dryax on Wayland while [[Escape from Tantiss|fleeing]] from [[Tantiss Base]]. The creature roared at them, but then fought a pack of [[lurca hound]]s sent from the [[mountain]], allowing the clones to escape.

When [[Clone Force 99]] later traveled to Wayland to [[Skirmish on Wayland|rescue]] Omega from Tantiss Base, the former [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]], who was aiding the clones, rested against a [[sleep]]ing dryax in the jungle not realizing it was a creature. The dryax awoke and then chased Clone Force 99 into the jungle before attacking a group of [[TK stormtrooper]]s who were searching for the clones.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Listen3|Dryax-Roar-FlashStrike.flac|A dryax roaring|A dryax roars at Clone Force 99 on Wayland}}
[[File:Dryax-SoTa.png|thumb|right|250px|Dryax could rear up to stand on their hind legs.]]
Dryaxes<ref name="DB" /> were a type of huge, [[humanoid]]-shaped,<ref name="ShadowsAD" /> [[Reptile|reptilian]]-headed<ref name="Flash Strike AD" /> [[Diet|predator]]<ref name="DB" /> with [[Wolf|lupine]] features<ref name="ShadowsAD" /> that dwelled in the deepest<ref name="GoH" /> [[jungle]]s of the [[planet]] [[Wayland]]. They possessed four<ref name="Shadows" /> long<ref name="ShadowsAD" /> [[limb]]s each ending in a paw with five<ref name="Shadows" /> long,<ref name="ShadowsAD" /> razor-sharp [[claw]]s.<ref name="DB" /> They moved on all fours, although they could rear up on their hind limbs,<ref name="Shadows" /> standing 3.2 [[meter]]s tall and measuring 4.4 meters in length.<ref name="DB" />

The creatures had smooth [[Color|gray]] skin with thick, greenish-brown<ref name="Shadows" /> or orange<ref name="GoH" /> feathers<ref name="Flash Strike AD" /> covering their backs and the top of their<ref name="Shadows" /> reptilian head. These feathers provided camouflage for the dryaxes<ref name="Flash Strike AD" /> in the jungle. A dryax's face consisted of two<ref name="Shadows" /> glowing<ref name="ShadowsAD" /> red [[eye]]s, a broad [[Nose|snout]], and a [[Mouth|maw]]<ref name="Shadows" /> of serrated [[Tooth|teeth]]<ref name="Flash Strike AD" /> with a lower jaw that protruded in front of the upper. The creature vocalized through grumbles, growls, and roars.<ref name="Shadows" /> Even a single dryax was a formidable foe due to their claws and massive stature.<ref name="DB" /> Their claws were even capable of damaging [[Katarn-class commando armor|''Katarn''-class commando armor]].<ref name="Flash Strike" />

==Behavior==
Dryaxes were among the deadliest alpha predators in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], using roars to display dominance and communicate with other dryaxes.<ref name="GoH" /> They could hunt alone<ref name="Flash Strike" /> or in packs. When attacking, dryaxes leapt on their prey and clawed at their underbelly.<ref name="GoH" />

==History==
===Escape enabler===
{{Quote|No, it was the creatures that roam the jungle. Even our strongest lurca hounds struggle against what's beyond these walls.|Doctor Royce Hemlock|audio=Hemlock-TheCreatures-Confined.flac|<ref name="Confined">{{TBB|Confined}}</ref>}}
[[File:DryaxVsClones-SoTa.png|thumb|left|300px|Omega and Crosshair encountered a dryax on Wayland while fleeing Mount Tantiss.]]
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|S3}}</ref> dryaxes roamed the wilds<ref name="DB" /> beyond the perimeter<ref name="Shadows" /> of [[Tantiss Base]], a secret [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] facility in [[Mount Tantiss]] led by [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] on Wayland. The Imperials trained a pack of [[lurca hound]]s to keep dryaxes and other predators at bay, although not beyond the perimeter, where even the toughest [[Dog|hounds]] struggled to survive. Such was the threat of the dryax-inhabited jungles that Hemlock considered anyone beyond the perimeter as good as [[Death|dead]]<ref name="Confined" /> and even trained [[Imperial commando]]s dreaded crossing paths with dryaxes while on patrol.<ref name="GoH" />

After the fugitive [[Cloning|clones]] [[Crosshair]] and [[Omega]] [[Escape from Tantiss|escaped]] from the [[mountain]], they encountered a dryax as they fled through the jungle to reach the area designated [[Sector four (Wayland)|sector four]]. The creature approached the pair after Omega stumbled and fell, clambering over a nearby fallen [[tree]] while growling. The dryax then reared up on its hind legs and roared at the pair, prompting Crosshair to level his [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] at it, but as the dryax dropped to all fours, it was distracted by the sound of approaching lurca hounds that had been dispatched from Tantiss Base to [[Hunting|hunt]] the escaped clones.<ref name="Shadows" />

[[File:DryaxVsLurcas-SoTa.png|thumb|right|300px|The lurca hounds chasing Crosshair and Omega attacked the dryax instead.]]
Turning to face the oncoming pack, the dryax stood as the first hound leapt at it, swatting it and the next leaping attacker to the floor with one of its paws. Three more hounds then leapt onto the dryax and held onto its back and head, causing the creature to tumble to the ground. Crosshair and Omega then fled as the lurca hounds were knocked from the tumbling dryax, which tossed the last hound away with a paw and then continued to fight the pack.<ref name="Shadows" />

===Wrong place to rest===
{{Quote|Slowly step away.|Hunter orders Rampart to move away from the awakened dryax|audio=Hunter-SlowlyStepAway-FlashStrike.flac|<ref name="Flash Strike" />}}
[[File:RampartDryax-TBB-FlashStrike.png|thumb|left|300px|Rampart rested against a camouflaged dryax.]] 
Not long after she escaped, Omega was [[Raid on Pabu|re-captured]] and returned to Tantiss Base,<ref name="Point of No Return">{{TBB|Point of No Return (The Bad Batch)}}</ref> causing her brothers in [[Clone Force 99]] to launch a [[Skirmish on Wayland|rescue mission]] that included recruiting the former Imperial [[Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart]].<ref name="Juggernaut">{{TBB|Juggernaut (episode)}}</ref> Crosshair, Rampart, and the clones [[Wrecker]] and [[Hunter]] managed to reach Wayland, crashing in the jungle after being intercepted by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]] from Tantiss Base, and so began to make their way toward the base on foot.<ref name="Flash Strike" />

While moving through Wayland's jungles to reach the base, they reached a cliff face and stopped to discuss how they might overcome the obstacle. As the clones spoke, Rampart sat down to rest against what he thought was part of the scenery but was in fact a [[sleep]]ing dryax. Rampart realized the dryax was beginning to move as it awoke, and he slowly backed away from it toward the clones as it turned to them and stood before advancing and growling at them. Rampart ordered the clones to shoot the creature, but Hunter refused, as they did not want to give away their location to Imperials searching the area for them.<ref name="Flash Strike" />

===Another Imperial distraction===
{{Quote|Did you think wrestling that creature was a good idea?''"<br />"''At the time, yes.|Crosshair and Wrecker|audio=Crosshair-Wrecker-Wrestling-TheCavalryHasArrived.flac|<ref name="Cavalry">{{TBB|The Cavalry Has Arrived}}</ref>}}
Rampart then fled alone as the dryax reared up on its hind legs and roared at the clones before charging at them, forcing them to scatter into the trees. Pursuing Hunter, who rolled out of its way, it swiped at the clone with its claws. Crosshair then drove the [[Stock|butt]] of his [[773 Firepuncher sniper rifle|sniper rifle]] into the dryax's head, distracting it so Hunter could get away. Wrecker leapt at the creature as it reared up to attack Crosshair, but the dryax threw Wrecker off its back and onto the ground, where it slashed his chest as he stood and threw him back into a nearby tree. The other two clones then helped Wrecker up and began to flee, with the dryax becoming impeded by a pair of trees as it tried to give chase.<ref name="Flash Strike" />

[[File:DryaxBadBatch-TBB-FlashStrike.png|thumb|right|300px|A dryax attacked Clone Force 99 on Wayland.]]
A group of [[TK stormtrooper]]s from Tantiss Base then spotted the trio of clones and opened fire; however, Clone Force 99 raced past the [[stormtrooper]]s while returning fire as they fled. The stormtroopers remained focused on the clones until hearing the dryax's roar behind them, turning as the creature charged into them and attacked. The [[trooper]]s opened fire on the beast as it began swiping at them, allowing Clone Force 99 to escape.<ref name="Flash Strike" /> Wrecker was injured in the attack, later stating that attacking the dryax had seemed like a good idea at the time.<ref name="Cavalry" /> The [[Twi'lek]] [[Fey Zara]] had a dryax headed mounted on the wall of her [[Fey's Cantina|cantina]].<ref name="GoH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dryaxes were first indirectly mentioned in "[[Confined]],"<ref name="Confined" /> the first episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'s'' [[The Bad Batch Season 3|third season]], which premiered on [[February 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Watch">{{SW|url=news/the-bad-batch-season-3-trailer|text=Watch the ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch'' Season 3 Trailer}}</ref> A dryax then first appeared in "[[Shadows of Tantiss]],"<ref name="Shadows" /> the third episode of the season that aired on the same date.<ref name="Watch" /> The name was provided in the episode's subtitles.<ref name="Shadows" /> Concept art of the dryax was created for the episode by [[Clinton Felker]], with the original description being "a massive bear/panther-like monster."<ref name="Gallery">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/shadows-of-tantiss-episode-guide|text="Shadows of Tantiss" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> A pair of dryaxes appeared as an enemy boss fight in the Coliseum [[holotable]] game mode as part of the "Era of Anniversary" event in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'', with the event starting on [[November 18]], [[2025]].<ref name="Anniversary">{{EA|url=games/starwars/galaxy-of-heroes/news/title-update-and-10-year-anniversary-announcement|text=Title Update and 10-Year Anniversary Announcement Post}}</ref>
