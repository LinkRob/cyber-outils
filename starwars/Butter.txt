'''Butter''' was a substance used in cooking which could be made from [[milk]],<ref name="AEYNTK" /> including [[bantha butter]]<ref name="BWDHE">{{StoryCite|story=But What Does He Eat?|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref> made from the [[Blue milk|one]] produced by [[bantha]]s,<ref name="AEYNTK" /> or from [[nut]]s to make [[nut butter]].<ref name="Heir to the Jedi">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref> [[Westhills butter]] was a type of butter made in the [[Westhills]] of the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. While at the [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on Lothal, [[Imperial cadet|cadet]] [[Jai Kell]] wished for some Westhills butter with [[maize roll]]s instead of the [[ration-square]]s the [[Imperial Academy|academy]] served.<ref name="RitR" /> [[Nerf butter]] was another kind.<ref name="Rose & Paige">''[[Forces of Destiny—Rose & Paige]]''</ref> When making first-place treats for the [[Great Jedi Rumble Race]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Torban Buck]] made sure to use extra butter.<ref name="THRA 11">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 11|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 11]]</ref>
