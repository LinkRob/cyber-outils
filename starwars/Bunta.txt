'''Bunta''' was a [[Color|light]], green, glowy, [[poison]]ous<ref name="RJ" /> elixir made from the<ref name="Highlight">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-highlights-lost-found-revenge-justice|text=''The Acolyte'' Explained &#124; Highlights from "Lost / Found" and "Revenge / Justice"}}</ref> yellow<ref name="Destiny">{{Acolyte|Destiny (The Acolyte)}}</ref> [[Tree|leaves]] of a [[bunta tree]], which grew on the [[planet]] [[Brendok]], and other ingredients to create a deadly [[cocktail]]. When distilled, the poison would only last for a short period of [[time]]. The [[Brendok coven]] [[Aniseya family|twins]] [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] and [[Verosha Aniseya]] were taught to use it for [[hunting]]<ref name="RJ" /> during [[winter]] and use [[bow]] and [[arrow]]s for the [[summer]].<ref name="Destiny" />

In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the [[Sith]] [[Sith acolyte|acolyte]] Mae used the poison created by her supplier [[The Stranger|Qimir]] to [[Death|kill]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Torbin]] at the [[Olega Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on<ref name="RJ" /> the world<ref name="Highlight" /> [[Olega]]. After infiltrating the temple and confronting Torbin again, Mae successfully convinced Torbin to drink the poison to seek "forgiveness" from her for the [[Mission to Brendok|incident]] that occured on Brendok [[148 BBY|sixteen]] [[Standard year|years]] prior which led to the Brendok coven being destroyed.<ref name="Destiny" /> Later, Osha and the rest of the [[Jedi]] discovered that Torbin was dead with Osha identifying that Torbin was poisoned by bunta leading to them finding Qimir at the local [[Olega Apothecary|apothecary]] nearby.<ref name="RJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bunta appeared in "[[Revenge / Justice]]," the second episode of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'',<ref name="RJ" /> which aired on [[June 4]], [[2024]] on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-teaser-poster|text=''The Acolyte'' Teaser Poster, Premiere Date Revealed}}</ref> The episode was first screened in select theatres in the United States on [[June 3]], 2024.<ref name="First Screen">{{SW|url=news/the-acolyte-in-theaters|text=Watch the Mystery of ''The Acolyte'' Unfold in Select Theaters June 3}}</ref>
