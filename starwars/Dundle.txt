'''Dundles''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] available for purchase in the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]], located in the [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an announcer speaking [[Huttese]] advertised dundles alongside fresh [[Gorra berry|gorra berries]] and [[fishfruit]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dundles were mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
