'''Capso''' was a [[human]] who was a member of the [[Ghorman Front]]. He was a close friend of [[Dreena]] and [[Wilmon Paak]].<ref name="Who Are You" /> Around [[3 BBY]], he was one of the participants in a heist planned by [[Carro Rylanz]] with the rebels [[Vel Sartha]] and [[Cinta Kaz]].<ref name="What a Festive Evening">{{Andor|What a Festive Evening}}</ref> In [[2 BBY]], he survived the [[Ghorman Massacre]] alongside Dreena.<ref name="Who Are You" />
