The '''''Anodyne Two''''' was a [[starship]] operational during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].<ref name="TROS Expanded" /> It shared its name with the [[Nebulon-C escort frigate]] [[Anodyne (Nebulon-C)|''Anodyne'']], a Resistance starship<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> destroyed by the First Order in an [[Battle of Oetchi|attack]] on its [[Resistance navy|fleet]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> When [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] called for allies during the Resistance's [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] above the [[planet]] [[Exegol]], the ''Anodyne Two'' joined a [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] to assist in the battle.<ref name="TROS Expanded" />
