'''Alison Brie''' (born [[December 29]],<ref name="Birthday" /> [[1982]]<ref name="Guardian" />) is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actress, director, writer,<ref name="Interview" /> and voice actress, known for her television roles as [[W:c:madmen:Trudy Campbell|Trudy Campbell]] in [[Wikipedia:Mad Men|''Mad Men'']] and [[W:c:community-sitcom:Annie Edison|Annie Edison]] in [[Wikipedia:Community (TV series)|''Community'']].<ref name="Guardian" /> Brie provided the [[Wikipedia:English language|English]] voice of the character [[Am]] in the "[[THE TWINS]]," a short film in the anthology series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''.<ref name="The Twins" /> Her casting was first announced on [[StarWars.com]] on [[August 17]], [[2021]], and the entire series was released on [[Disney+]] later that year on [[September 22]].<ref name="Visions promo">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref>
