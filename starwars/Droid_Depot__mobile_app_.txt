'''''Droid Depot''''' is a mobile app that allows users to operate [[droid]]s purchased from the [[Droid Depot]] store at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], as well as create digital droids. It was released on [[May 4]], [[2020]] both on Apple's App Store and Google Play.<ref name="DPB DD App">{{DPB|url=2020/05/all-new-droid-depot-app-brings-star-wars-galaxys-edge-to-life-at-home|text=All-New Droid Depot App Brings Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge to Life at Home}}</ref>
