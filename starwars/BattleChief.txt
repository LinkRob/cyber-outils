A '''BattleChief''' was a [[capital ship]] classification used by the [[Grysk]] warlike [[species]]. The Grysk [[Prism-class BattleChief|''Prism''-class BattleChiefs]] were equipped with [[engine]]s and a [[hyperdrive]] and [[Weapon|armed]] with [[spectrum laser]]s and [[missile]]s, and they were slightly smaller than a [[Chiss heavy cruiser]] and larger than a [[Light cruiser|Chiss light cruiser]] or [[patrol cruiser]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

In [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> eleven ''Prism''-class BattleChiefs served as part of the [[Jixtus's fleet|fleet]] of the Grysk operative known as "[[Jixtus]]." They were all [[Senior Captain Thrawn's last stand|destroyed]] during a battle against a [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Thrawn's fleet|fleet]] over the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] "[[Sunrise]]."<ref name="Lesser Evil" />

==Behind the scenes==
BattleChiefs appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', the final installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
