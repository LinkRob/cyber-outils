'''Asar''' was an exceedingly bright [[blue dwarf]] [[star]]<ref name="NOP" /> in the [[Ilum system]], in the [[7G sector]] of the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="USW" /> Asar was [[orbit]]ed by [[Ilum]],<ref name="NOP" /> an [[Atmosphere|airless]] [[Unidentified planet (Ilum system)|planet]] further away in the [[Star system|system]],<ref name="Ahsoka" /> at least four other [[planet]]s, and a [[Ilum system cometary cloud|cloud]] of [[comet]]s which was located roughly the same distance away from Ilum as Ilum was from Asar.<ref name="NOP" /> On Ilum, Asar only rose every nineteen [[Standard day|days]]. The sunlight from Asar was used on Ilum to open a [[Water|ice]]-covered entrance in the [[Jedi Temple (Ilum)|Jedi Temple]] on the planet.<ref name="TG" />

At some point prior to [[34 ABY]],<ref name="date note" /> Ilum was fitted with the [[Starkiller Base]] [[superweapon]] by the [[First Order]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and the planet eventually moved into the [[Starkiller Base system]], where it was [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destroyed]] and collapsed into a [[Solo (star)|star]].<ref name="awakensnovel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Asar was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in "[[The Gathering (episode)|The Gathering]]," the sixth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'',<ref name="TG" /> which first aired on [[November 3]], [[2012]].<ref name="Episode Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> Asar originally first appeared in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[2001]] book ''[[Jedi Quest: Path to Truth]]'', written by [[Jude Watson]].<ref name="Path to Truth">''[[Jedi Quest: Path to Truth]]''</ref> It was first identified in the new [[canon]] continuity by the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Nexus of Power]]'',<ref name="NOP" /> and Asar's first appearance in canon came with the release of the video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' in [[2019]].<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>
