{{Quote|Indeed, most administrative recruits who entered the bureaucracy of the Empire were provided with a copy of Chief Administrator Svete Naemon's gargantuan work ''A Thorough Guide to the Bureaucratic Operation and Diligent Administration of the Galactic Empire'' which, from my own research, was not an easy read.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
'''Chief Administrator''' was an [[Administrator|administrative]] title within the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Svete Naemon]], [[author]] of ''[[A Thorough Guide to the Bureaucratic Operation and Diligent Administration of the Galactic Empire]]'' held the title and it was included by the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] when he mentioned Naemon in his [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the title of Chief Administrator was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity the titled first appeared in ''[[The Abduction]]'', a [[1992]] adventure for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' that was written by [[Chuck Truett]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Abduction">''[[The Abduction]]''</ref>
