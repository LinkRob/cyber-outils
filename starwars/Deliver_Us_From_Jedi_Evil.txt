'''''Deliver Us From Jedi Evil''''' was a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Anti-Jedi sentiment|anti-Jedi]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by [[Moshenu Phobi]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The image depicts a bearded [[Jedi]] with full [[Jedi apparel|hood]] up and ominous glowing [[eye]]s with several fists raised in protest against a [[Color|blue]] background, plus what was then the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] being in the foreground. The [[artist]], [[Moshenu Phobi]], was drawing upon the [[Jedi Order]]'s reputation as warmongers.<ref name="Propaganda" />
