'''Frigates''' were a military class of [[starship]] measuring around 200-400 [[meter]]s in length,<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> and served various functions, such as combat, medical or supply purposes.<ref name="SW EOSAOV (SE)">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)]]''</ref> The [[pirate]] [[Q'anah (pirate)|Q'anah's]] [[fleet]] included several frigates.<ref name="Trkn">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> The [[Chiss Ascendancy]] utilized [[Chiss frigate]]s.<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], both the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] used frigates, with the former deploying [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|Republic frigates]]<ref name="RFDB">{{Databank|republic-frigate|Republic Frigate}}</ref> and [[Pelta-class frigate|''Pelta''-class frigates.]] The latter made use of [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]].<ref name="CWM">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> The [[Galactic Empire]] also used frigates including the [[Salliche (frigate)|''Salliche'']],<ref name="Trkn" /> as did the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] who fielded the frigate ''[[Promise]]'' and the ''[[Redemption]]'', a [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate|Nebulon-B]] escort frigate which served as a medical ship.<ref name="HttJ">''[[Heir to the Jedi]]''</ref>
