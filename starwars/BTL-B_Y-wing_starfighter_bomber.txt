{{Quote|The Republic commissioned the Y-wing for clone pilots to take on the Confederacy's droid starfighters, and last I checked, the droids didn't win.|[[Jon Vander]]|<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" />}}
The '''BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber''',<ref name="HC 76">{{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Databank A-Z}}</ref> also known as the '''BTL-B Y-wing starfighter''' and '''BTL-B Y-wing fighter-bomber'''<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" /> was a model of [[starfighter]]-[[bomber]] produced by [[Koensayr Manufacturing]], the first of the [[BTL Y-wing starfighter|BTL-series Y-wing]] line. A mainstay of the [[Republic Navy]] during the [[Clone Wars]], BTL-Bs were adopted by [[Clone trooper pilot|clones]] and [[Jedi]] [[officer]]s alike and were instrumental in the fight against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Though most were scrapped by the [[Galactic Empire]] after the conflict, the BTL-Bs were largely remembered positively and their successors, the [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber]], would see service with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] decades later in the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Characteristics==
{{Main|BTL Y-wing starfighter}}
[[File:Bombardment_Doctrine_XWZ.png|thumb|left|200px|A BTL-B Y-wing flying in the thick of battle]]
Designed by [[Koensayr Manufacturing]], the BTL-B Y-wing was intended to serve as a combination [[starfighter]] and long-range [[bomber]]. The two large Koensayr<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" /> [[ion jet engine]]s,<ref name="USW" /> along with their characteristic nacelles, were carried by a cross wing, which connected to the [[cockpit]] module by a central spar. BTL-Bs were equipped with [[hyperdrive]]s,<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" /> unlike many other starfighters.<ref name="Ships of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Ships of the Galaxy]]''</ref> This, along with the fuel recycling system present in all BTLs, made Y-wings durable long-range craft, though their tendency to run hot necessitated a complex cooling system extending throughout the ship.<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" />

The wedge-shape cockpit module carried one pilot and one tailgunner in a rotating bubble turret.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> These positions were built with [[Clone trooper pilot|clone pilots]] in mind, though they could be adjusted by mechanics for other operators, including [[Jedi]] pilots.<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" /> The module also featured an [[Astromech droid|astromech]] socket located directly behind the turret.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

==History==
===Clone Wars service===
[[File:Btlbywing.png|thumb|right|250px|Y-wings bombers depart on their attack on the ''Malevolence''.]]
The BTL-B Y-wing was used by the [[Republic Navy]] in the early stages of the [[Clone Wars]]. [[Anakin Skywalker]] led Y-wings in the [[Battle of Corvair]].<ref name="AOR">[[Age of Republic - Anakin Skywalker 1|''Age of Republic - Anakin Skywalker'' 1]]</ref> Y-wings were also used as part of the [[Battle of the Kaliida Nebula|attack]] on the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Separatist]] [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]''.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" />

Koensayr Manufacturing were still in the process of testing their new BTL-B Y-wings when [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] asked for a [[squadron]] of them to be used against the ''Malevolence''. The [[Galactic Republic]] considered the [[Y-wing project]], led by [[Senator]] [[Ask Aak]], to be essential in winning the war. However, Ask Aak still held resent for the [[Jedi]] who couldn't do anything to save [[Aks Moe|his predecessor]] from being [[assassin]]ated, and refused to give Skywalker the ships he needed. Instead of reporting this decision to his superiors, Anakin Skywalker headed directly to the Koensayr workshops in orbit of [[Bormus]] and took the [[bomber]]s he needed for himself.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> Under the command of Skywalker, [[Shadow Squadron]] successfully destroyed the ''Malevolence''{{'s}} [[ion cannon]] despite losing six out of their initial force of twelve bombers.<ref name="Shadow of Malevolence" /> Their early success prompted Koensayr to develop other variant Y-wings in the BTL-series for the Republic war effort, including the [[BTL-S3 Y-wing starfighter|BTL-S3]] and [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|BTL-A4]] models.<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" />

[[File:Y-wings_ryloth.png|thumb|left|250px|Y-wings proved invaluable in breaking the Separatist blockade around Ryloth.]]
Y-wings saw extensive service over the course of the war. Used effectively against enemy [[capital ship]]s, Y-wing bombing runs contributed to Republic victories such as [[Battle of Ryloth|breaking]] the blockade over [[Ryloth]],<ref name="Storm Over Ryloth">{{TCW|Storm Over Ryloth}}</ref> [[Battle of Kamino|destroying]] Separatist [[transport]]s at [[Kamino]],<ref name="ARC Troopers">{{TCW|ARC Troopers (episode)}}</ref> and [[Battle of Sullust|engaging]] [[Asajj Ventress]]'s fleet at [[Sullust]].<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

Despite their bugginess in atmospheric operations,<ref name="Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref> they were often employed as air support for the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during ground assaults. They prevented the destruction of the [[capital]] of Ryloth, [[Lessu City|Lessu]], by Separatist [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]],<ref name="Liberty on Ryloth">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref> and were selected to drop an experimental [[electro-proton bomb]] on Separatist forces near the end of the [[Battle of Malastare]], completely annihilating the droid army.<ref name="Zillo Beast">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref> BTL-Bs dropped ordnance on Confederate ground emplacements at the [[Second Battle of Geonosis]],<ref name="Landing at Point Rain">{{TCW|Landing at Point Rain}}</ref> [[Umbaran Militia|insurgent]] formations on [[Umbara]],<ref name="Darkness on Umbara">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref> and, [[19 BBY|near the end of the war]], on Separatist anti-aircraft cannons around the [[Anaxes assembly complex|assembly complex]] during the [[Battle of Anaxes]].<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:ShadowSquadronVeteran-SWZ.png|thumb|right|250px|Y-wings of Shadow Squadron were on Kamino.]]
Republic Y-wings were also engaged in more standard starfighter tactics as the war went on. Though Y-wings sometimes failed in defensive applications, such as the [[Battle of Patitite Pattuna|losses]] of Jedi General [[Adi Gallia]]'s [[flagship]] at [[Patitite Pattuna]]<ref name="ND" /> [[Attack on Obi-Wan Kenobi's fleet|and of]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'s [[flagship]], the ''[[Negotiator]]'',<ref name="Bound for Rescue">{{TCW|Bound for Rescue}}</ref> they displayed dogfighting capabilities at [[Geonosis]], engaging [[Geonosian]] [[Nantex-class territorial defense starfighter|''Nantex''-class starfighters]],<ref name="Landing at Point Rain" /> and at [[Battle of Umbara|Umbara]], engaging [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]] and [[droid tri-fighter]]s.<ref name="Plan of Dissent">{{TCW|Plan of Dissent}}</ref>

After the issuing of [[Order 66]], former [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]], who had had his [[Behavioral modification biochip|control chip]] removed,<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> escaped a [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|skirmish]] against mind-controlled [[clone trooper]]s aboard the crashing ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> ''[[Tribunal]]''<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> in a [[Rex's BTL-B Y-wing fighter-bomber|Y-wing]] they located in the repair bay.<ref name="Victory and Death" /> Rex kept the starfighter in the aftermath, using it while working against the [[Proclamation of the New Order|newly-formed]] [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Battle Scars">{{TBB|Battle Scars}}</ref>

===Legacy===
Y-wings earned a reputation for durability and firepower,<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" /> and veterans such as [[Captain]] Rex would remember them as solid in combat even years later.<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref> However, the end of the war saw the new [[Imperial Navy]] shift away from hyperdrive-equipped snub-fighters<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> in favor of a space superiority doctrine based on [[capital ship]]s,<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> causing Koensayr to lose its government contract. Though some Y-wings were sold to local planetary defense market, Imperial intervention caused many unsold, repossessed and surplus Y-wings to be allocated for [[Salvage yard|reclamation]].<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> By [[2 BBY]], the Empire had scrapped most BTL-Bs, though several decommissioned BTL-A4s remained awaiting reclamation.<ref name="Rebel Starfighters" />
