==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|One of the things I love to do when designing aliens and creatures for Star Wars is try and find weird ways for the costume to break the human form.|Jake Lunt Davies|<ref name="Facebook 2">{{Facebook|username=Jake-Lunt-Davies-work-100056465597821|url=permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid0XjxxgA2h24htCZnKbXNbtnRhxmWtrVcR6qU7dkPPpnUMDf73qXrvV6JP2ADxqZuhl&id=100056465597821|name=[[Jake Lunt Davies]]|date=2023-02-05|quote=One of the things I love to do&hellip;|archiveurl=https://archive.is/dPUOG}}</ref>}}
The Da-Vlunnian first appeared in the [[The High Republic (2023) 1|first issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series, written by [[Cavan Scott]], illustrated by [[Ario Anindito]] and [[Jim Towe]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="THR 1" /> on [[November 8]] of that year.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110346/star_wars_the_high_republic_phase_iii_2023_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic &lsqb;Phase III&rsqb; (2023) #1}}</ref> The design for the species<ref name="Facebook 1">{{Facebook|username=Jake-Lunt-Davies-work-100056465597821|url=permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid02G39gsRDyRTPXdoYMv1voBfPUeX7VuevnBbKcq37TPeJaQZ561UJmtUaPmC4Tjg8Gl&id=100056465597821|name=[[Jake Lunt Davies]]|date=2023-11-10|quote=Old concepts never die|archiveurl=https://archive.is/vIOKV}}</ref> originated from concept art created by [[Jake Lunt Davies]] for [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], intended to appear in the [[Spice Runners' Den|bar]] scene on [[Kijimi]]. The alien was designed such that its mouth was at the human performer's neck level, with the performer's head entirely above the costume's upper jaw.<ref name="Facebook 2" />
