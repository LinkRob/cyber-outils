|date=Between [[50 BBY]] and [[48 BBY]]<ref name="Date">"Choices" depicts [[Mace Windu]] gaining a seat on the [[Jedi High Council]]. {{Film|III}} established that [[Anakin Skywalker]] was the youngest to ever serve on the Council. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the film to [[19 BBY]] and Skywalker's birth to [[41 BBY]]. Therefore, Windu must have been at least twenty-two years old. According to the ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'' booklet "[[Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force|Mace Windu and Other Force Users]]," Mace Windu was born forty years before the [[Battle of Naboo]], which ''Star Wars: Timelines'' places in [[32 BBY]]. Therefore, Windu gained a seat on the Council no earlier than [[50 BBY]]. ''[[Master & Apprentice]]'' further establishes that Windu is part of the Jedi Council at the [[time]] of [[Nim Pianna]]'s [[Mutiny on the Advent|death]], which ''Timelines'' dates to [[48 BBY]]. Therefore, the events of "Choices" must take place between these two years.</ref>

Following [[Jedi Master]]s [[Dooku]] and [[Mace Windu]]'s [[Incident on Raxus Secundus|mission]] to [[Raxus Secundus]], a '''funeral''' was held for [[Jedi]] Master Katri. In addition to Dooku and Windu, the funeral was attended by many other venerable Jedi, including [[Yoda]], [[Plo Koon]], [[Saesee Tiin]], [[Depa Billaba]] and [[Tera Sinube]]. The service was officiated by [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]].<ref name="Choices" />
