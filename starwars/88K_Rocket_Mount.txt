The '''88K Rocket Mount''' was a model of [[Micro-Rocket Armor-Mounting]], a type of [[armor]] attachment that could fire [[Wrist rocket|micro-rockets]], manufactured by the [[SoroSuub Corporation]] and sold for 800 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Collapse" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 88K Rocket Mount was first mentioned in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'',<ref name="Collapse" /> a [[2019]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars Roleplaying|''Star Wars Roleplaying Game'']] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Available Now - June 28">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2019/6/28/available-now-june-28|text=Available Now – June 28}}</ref>
