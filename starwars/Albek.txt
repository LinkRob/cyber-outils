'''Albeks''' were a highly aggressive and territorial [[Force-sensitive]] species of [[reptile]] found on many worlds throughout [[the galaxy]].

==Description==
Albeks were small reptilian predators averaging under 1 [[meter]] in length. They walked on four [[claw]]ed feet and had sharp spikes protruding out their backs from their necks to the end of their tail. Despite their small stature, albeks were intrepid and highly territorial creatures who would stow away on board any [[Starship|vessel]] that landed within their territory, viewing the intrusion as a challenge. Once established, albeks were very difficult to remove due to their aggressive nature, fearless stubbornness and their ability to use [[the Force]] to anticipate attacks and [[Telekinesis|Force-pull]] a limb within reach of their sharp-toothed mouths.<ref name="FDSS" />
