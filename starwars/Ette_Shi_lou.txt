'''Ette Shi'lou''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Alliance officer|officer]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Alliance Army]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Shi'lou held the [[military rank]] of [[captain]] and commanded a [[Ette Shi'lou's platoon|platoon]] of the [[Marine Corps]] for several [[Standard year|years]]. Captain Shi'lou participated in the [[liberation of New Valis]], where she and her forces defeated the [[Galactic Empire]]'s forces led by [[Lieutenant]] [[Fhaen]].<ref name="Lead" />
