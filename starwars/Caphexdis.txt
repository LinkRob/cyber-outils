'''Caphexdis''' was a celestial body<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> located in the [[Caphexdis system]] of the [[Homon sector]],<ref name="Appendix" /> a part of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It was situated in grid square M-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> and was the [[homeworld]] of<ref name="TROS VD" /> the [[Caphex]]<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Seossra Thwisp]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The latter fact was noted in a [[bounty]] posting for Thwisp issued by the [[time]] of the [[First Order]] [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Datapad">{{DatapadCite|''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]''|BatuuBountyHunters-SeossraThwisp.png}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Caphexdis was introduced in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
