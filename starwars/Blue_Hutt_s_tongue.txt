'''Blue Hutt's tongue''' was a [[species]] of highland scrub [[Bush|shrub]] native to the [[planet]] [[Onderon]]. It consisted of bunches of long, thin [[Color|blue]] [[Plant|leaves]] and was named for the similarity in appearance its leaves bore to the [[tongue]]s of the [[Hutt]] species, despite Hutt tongues being pink. The plant collected [[water]] in the conical fronds of its leaves, which formed protected pools that breeding [[amphibian]]s would then use.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> During the [[Onderonian Civil War]]<ref name="Two Fronts" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> it could be found growing in the [[forest]]s surrounding the world's [[capital]] [[city]] [[Iziz]] as well as those close to the [[Saw Gerrera's camp|camp]] maintained by the [[Onderon rebels|resistance movement]] fighting in the war.<ref name="Two Fronts" /> The shrub also grew in the [[mountain]] region where the insurgent fort known as [[the nest]] was built.<ref name="Tipping Points" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Hutt's tongue first appeared in "[[A War on Two Fronts]]" the second episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television show's [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]],<ref name="Two Fronts" /> which aired on [[October 6]], [[2012]].<ref name="Explore: A War on Two Fronts">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/a-war-on-two-fronts-episode-guide|text="A War on Two Fronts" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> The plant then went on to appear in the following three episodes of the show, but was not named in any of them,<ref name="Front Runners">{{TCW|Front Runners}}</ref><ref name="Soft War">{{TCW|The Soft War}}</ref><ref name="Tipping Points" /> first receiving identification in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'', which was written by [[Adam Bray]], [[Tricia Barr]] and [[Cole Horton]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> and released on [[April 4]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-galaxy-guides-get-a-first-look-at-dks-star-wars-visual-encyclopedia-and-the-amazing-book-of-lego-star-wars|text=New Galaxy Guides: Get a First Look at DK's ''Star Wars Visual Encyclopedia'' and ''The Amazing Book of LEGO Star Wars''}}</ref>
