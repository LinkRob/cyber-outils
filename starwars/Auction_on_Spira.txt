Around [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''auction on Spira'''<ref name="Star Wars 29 preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-29-exclusive-preview|text=The Rebel Alliance Looks to the Nihil for Help in Marvel's ''Star Wars'' #29 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> was hosted by a [[Unidentified Troig auctioneer|Troig auctioneer]]. It was one of two antiques auctions held a [[Standard year|year]] on the [[planet]] [[Spira]]. During the auction, a [[Nihil]] [[strikeship]] came up for sale, which [[Captain]] [[Amilyn Holdo]] hoped to get the [[Path engine]] of so she and other members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] could find the lost [[Kezarat Convoy|''Kezarat'' Convoy]]. The bidding was opened at twenty-five thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] by the [[Unidentified curator|curator]] of the [[Galactic Museum|Imperial Museum]]. Holdo then bid twenty-six thousand, to which the [[curator]] responded with a thirty thousand bid. As the bidding almost came to a close, Holdo raised a bid of thirty-five, but the curator again countered, this [[time]] with fifty thousand credits, which ultimately won him the [[Starship|ship]]. Despite the curator's victory, it did not stop the rebels from promptly stealing the Path engine from the ship and escaping with it.<ref name="Star Wars 29" />
