An '''aeromagnifier''' was a type of illuminated magnifier that hung from a [[Repulsorlift|repulsor]] hood. The [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[human]] [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi Skywalker]] was gifted an aeromagnifier by the [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]]. She used it while cleaning [[computer]] memory devices in her [[Mos Espa]] [[Skywalker home|home]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. The magnifier was on Skywalker's workstation when she bid farewell to her [[Gender|son]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], as he was leaving Tatooine in [[32 BBY]]. In [[9 BBY]], aeromagnifiers hung in both [[Dendra's Antiquities]] and [[Pyloon's Saloon]] in [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]].

==Description==
An aeromagnifier was a [[Repulsorlift|repulsor]]-powered, floating magnifier that consisted of a transparent magnifier disc surrounded by yellow illuminator rings hanging from a [[Color|silver]] dome-shaped repulsor hood. The device produced a soothing thrum when in use.<ref name="Objects" />

==History==
By [[32 BBY]], the [[human]] [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi Skywalker]], who was [[Slavery|enslaved]] by the [[junk dealer]] [[Watto]], had been given an aeromagnifier in a rare show of kindness by Watto as recognition for all her hard work. She kept the tool on the workstation in her [[Skywalker home|home]]<ref name="Objects" /> in [[Slave Quarters Row]] in the [[city]] of [[Mos Espa]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="CVD" />

[[File:ShmiWorkstation-TPM.png|thumb|right|300px|Shmi Skywalker used an aeromagnifier at her workstation.]]
Skywalker used the aeromagnifier at the workstation to work long [[Standard hour|hours]] cleaning [[computer]] memory devices in order to earn extra [[wupiupi]]. Although the Skywalker home was poorly lit at [[Standard day|night]], the magnifier and light from the illuminator rings meant she could continue working on the devices long after Tatooine's [[Tatoo I|twin]] [[Tatoo II|suns]] set and her [[eye]]s had become fatigued.<ref name="Objects" /> Shmi's aeromagnifier was floating above the workstation when her [[Gender|son]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], brought a group of off-worlders home during a [[sandstorm]]<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref> in 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="Objects" /> It was still above the station later when Anakin bid farewell to Shmi before leaving Tatooine.<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>

A device similar in appearance to an aeromagnifier was stood on a desk in the abandoned [[Hermit's Abode|home]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]]  on the planet [[Bogano]] when the [[Jedi]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Bogano|visited it]]<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> in [[14 BBY]].<ref name="JFODate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> The device had a lens and hood similar to an aeromagnifier, but these were mounted on a stand over a parts tray rather than floating.<ref name="JFO" /> In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the [[merchant]] [[Doma Dendra]] had a pair of aeromagnifiers hanging from a grill above the counter in her shop, [[Dendra's Antiquities]], in [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]] on the planet [[Koboh]]. In the same outpost, an aeromagnifier hung above a workbench in the basement of [[Pyloon's Saloon]] which was used by Kestis while he was a guest of the [[saloon]]'s owner, [[Greez Dritus]].<ref name="Survivor" /> At some point after [[35 ABY]], an entry on Shmi's aeromagnifier was included as one of a hundred in a [[Unidentified historical book|book]] detailing objects from throughout [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]].<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
An aeromagnifier appeared in the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="TPM" /> which was released on [[May 19]], [[1999]].<ref name="Year by Year">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the tool was identified in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> The name for the [[Aeromagnifier/Legends|tool]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: Episode I: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> and released on [[May 26]], 1999.<ref name="Amazon">{{CollectorCite|6|Books with Bite}}</ref> Aeromagnifiers can be collected as a scrap part in the [[Star Wars video games|mobile video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]'' and are used to upgrade characters' Relic Amplifiers,<ref name="GoH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> which were added to the game in late [[2019]].<ref name="Relics">{{EA|subdomain=forums.galaxy-of-heroes.starwars|url=discussion/212247/relic-amplifiers-the-gear-system-our-next-update|text=Relic Amplifiers, The Gear System & Our Next Update|author=CG_SBCrumb|date=2019-08-22|nolive=1}}</ref>
