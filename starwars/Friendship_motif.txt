A '''friendship motif''' was written by [[John Williams]] for the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. The [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]] features alongside the [[victory motif]] in the track [[The Rise of Skywalker (music)|''The Rise of Skywalker'']] in the film's [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker (soundtrack)|soundtrack]], with both motifs being in major key. The friendship motif is played in the tracks "[[The Rise of Skywalker (music)|The Rise of Skywalker]]" as well as "To Kijimi."

The motif is used in ''[[The LEGO Star Wars Holiday Special]]'', which was scored by [[Michael Kramer]] and released in [[2020]]. In the first scene, when [[Yoda]] begins to narrate, Williams' friendship motif is combined with Kramer's new [[connection motif]]&mdash;which represents the bonds formed between [[Sentience|sentients]] and the celebration of such life, [[Life Day]], as well as the [[key]] that enabled travel through [[time]] and [[Realspace|space]]. Where the two are played in succession, the connection motif is played preceding the frienship motif. The two motifs are played when [[Rey Skywalker]] speaks of her intentions to travel to [[Kordoku]] to help with the [[Jedi training|training]] of her friend, [[Finn]], and when [[Jedi Master]] Yoda teaches Rey Skywalker to be mindful of her connections with others, showing [[Timeline of galactic history|visions of the past]] of a master and an [[Padawan|apprentice]] being not only learners in [[the Force]], but also friends&mdash;the most important thing. The two motifs are played again for when Rey Skywalker creates a [[portal]] through time and space without the use of the key, and again when Rey Skywalker and [[Luke Skywalker]] part ways aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] after [[Battle of Endor|defeating]] the [[Sith]] together by adhering to the [[Jedi Code|Jedi tenet]] of letting go. The two motifs are again played combined in the final scene of the ''Holiday Special'', when Yoda narrates that Life Day was a time of joy&mdash;friendship and family&mdash;as celebrants gather together in a circle in the [[Standard day|day]] of life, before transitioning into the [[Star Wars Main Title|end credits]].

The motif is used again in ''[[LEGO Star Wars Summer Vacation]]'', which also was scored by Michael Kramer and released in [[2022]]. At first it was played during the scene in which Finn reminds to all friends that their summer vacation will be their final time spending together. Later the motif was heard whenever is Finn attepmting to talk to his friends and during his conversations with [[Force spirit|Force spirits]] of [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]. The most prominent usage of the motif was during the scene of Finn's reminiscence about the first time he met Poe, Rey and Rose and their deeper and important meanings that they gave to him and after that the friends all share a group hug as all the Force spirits from throughout the episode appear.
