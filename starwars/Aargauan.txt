'''Aargauans''' were a group of individuals that existed within [[the galaxy]]. The [[TSMEU-6 personal wheel bike]], which was designed by the Aargauans, took inspiration from the wheels equipped on the [[IG-227 Hailfire-class droid tank]].<ref name="Ships">{{EncyclopediaCite|Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Aargauans were first mentioned in [[Canon|''Star Wars'' canon]] in "[[Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)]],"<ref name="Ships" /> a booklet in a series of reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']], published by [[De Agostini]] on [[July 29]], [[2022]].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref> The [[Aargauun]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[Star Wars (1977) 48|forty-eighth issue]] of the [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book series]], which was written by [[Larry Hama]], illustrated by [[Carmine Infantino]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="SW48">[[Star Wars (1977) 48|''Star Wars'' (1977) 48]]</ref> on [[March 24]], [[1981]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53838/star_wars_1977_48|text=Star Wars (1977) #48}} {{C|First printing}}</ref>
