"'''American Graffiti: George Lucas' First Blockbuster'''" is an article' written by [[Kevin Fitzpatrick]] which appeared inside the magazine [[Star Wars Insider 30|''Star Wars Insider'' 30]]. The article narrated how the success of [[Wikipedia:American Graffiti|''American Graffiti'']] enabled [[George Lucas]] to make the ''[[Star Wars]]'' movies. The article also showed how the career of the stars of ''American Graffiti'' fared in the latter years.<ref name="sw30" />
