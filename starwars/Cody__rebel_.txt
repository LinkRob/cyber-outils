'''Cody''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[soldier]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Alliance and the [[Galactic Empire]], he fought Imperial troops on the moon [[Jedha]].<ref name="Boots" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Biography==
Cody was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who joined the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="Boots" /> sometime during or before the [[Standard year|year]] [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[1 BBY]]. Given that the events of ''[[Rogue One: Boots on the Ground]]'' take place around and during the events of the film, it can be assumed ''Boots on the Grounds'' takes place during 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Cody was a human with [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s and tan skin.<ref name="Boots" />

==Equipment==
Cody wore the fatigues of a [[Rebel trooper]].<ref name="Boots" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cody was created as a playable character for the online browser [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Rogue One: Boots on the Ground|Star Wars: Boots on the Ground]]''. Players could select Cody to be a member of their three-man rebel squad. The image used for the character in ''Boots on the Ground''<ref name="Boots" /> is also an image of [[Stordan Tonc]] from ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>
