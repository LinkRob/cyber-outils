At the height of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]] held a '''peace conference''' on the neutral [[Planet|world]] of [[Mandalore]]. Hoping to reach a diplomatic end to the war, delegations including three [[senator]]s from the [[Galactic Senate|Republic Senate]] as well as the [[Separatist Senate]] met in the [[Grand Salon|throne room]] of [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]'s [[Sundari Royal Palace|Royal Palace]] in the [[capital]] city of [[Sundari]]. However, during the conference the [[junior senator]] [[Lux Bonteri]] accused the Confederacy's leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]] to be responsible for the [[Attack on Confederate people|murder]] of [[Mina Bonteri|his mother]] and the negotiations quickly collapsed. On the order of Separatist senator [[Bec Lawise]], three [[Commando Droid Diplomat]]s escorted him out to [[C-9979 landing craft|a starship]], where they were ordered by Dooku through [[hologram]] to murder Bonteri, but were defeated by the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. The remaining droids followed Tano and Bonteri to the [[Sundari shipping docks]], where they were repelled by [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senate Guard]]s led by [[Captain]] [[Taggart (Senate Guard)|Taggart]].<ref name="A Friend in Need" />
