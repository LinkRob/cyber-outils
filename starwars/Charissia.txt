'''Charissia''' was a [[forest]] monastery [[moon]] located in the [[Bartahn sector]]<ref name="Adventures in Wookiee-Sitting" /> within the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that very few [[smuggler]]s knew about. Many creatures were [[Homeworld|native]] to Charissia, including a [[Unidentified Charissia creature|creature]] that lived in herds. Prior to the [[mission to the Ring of Kafrene]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] left [[K-2SO]] on Charissia to babysit three [[Wookiee]]s named [[Wyhyatt]], [[D'Koetaa]] and [[D'Lylaa]] whilst he undertook a [[Unidentified Rebel raid|raid]].<ref name="Adventures in Wookiee-Sitting" />
