'''Astralor Corp.''' was a corporation. It manufactured the [[Mk I Personal Repulsorlift System]], a type of [[repulsor pack]], and the [["Black Hole Box" T-11 Armored Databank]], a type of [[armored computer core]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Astralor Corp. was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
