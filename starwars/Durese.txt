'''Durese''', also known as '''Duros'''<ref name="Nexus" /> was the native [[language]] of the [[Duros]] [[species]]. The original name of [[Delia Leighton]]'s [[starship]], [[Serendipity|''Miss Fortune'']], was a Durese word that translated to "serendipity" in [[Galactic Basic Standard]].<ref name="SR" />
