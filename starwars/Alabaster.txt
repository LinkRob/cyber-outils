'''Alabaster''' was the name of a concept or color associated with "Alabaster Bioengineer" and "Alabaster Launcher," a [[Clothing|costume]] and [[weapon]] respectively, which were used by the [[Grand Arena]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Tuya]]<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date2">{{HuntersDate}}</ref> It shared similarities with [[Alabaster-7791]], a light shade of [[Color|gray]] found in the [[Lantillian paintworks coding system]].<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref>
