'''Arakyd Industries''', also known simply as '''Arakyd''', was an [[weapon|arms]] and [[droid]] manufacturer based on the [[Deep Core]] [[planet]] [[Vulpter]] that notably produced a number of [[probe droid]] models including [[dwarf probe droid]]s, [[ID9 seeker droid]]s, [[internal systems probe droid]]s, [[Viper probe droid]]s, and [[Ringneck recon droid]]s. Having forged close ties with the [[Galactic Empire]], Arakyd produced [[RA-7 protocol droid]]s specially for Imperial use and created [[KX-series security droid]]s to provide Imperial facilities with more efficient security, in the process classifying them as [[security droid]]s to bypass [[Imperial Senate]] prohibitions on [[battle droid]]s. Finally, Arakyd manufactured several [[starship]]-grade weapons, such as [[ST2 concussion missile]]s and [[ST2 concussion missile tube|tubes]].

==Description==
Arakyd Industries -simply known as Arakyd-<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> was one of the three largest [[droid]] manufacturers in the [[galaxy]], the other two being [[Industrial Automaton]]<ref name="Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Head to Head]]''</ref> and [[Cybot Galactica]].<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> It was based on the [[Deep Core]] [[planet]]<ref name="The Force Awakens Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[Vulpter]],<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> where it also maintained a [[Arakyd Industries data storehouse|data storehouse]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> as well as its [[Vulpter droid factory|factories]]<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> and specialized in the production of [[probe droid]]s,<ref name="probe droid" /> which they produced for a variety of [[army|militaries]] including the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Epic Battles" /> and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Build R2-D2 65" /> The company's contract work for the Empire, with whom it had very close ties<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> also included the production of [[protocol droid]]s -the [[RA-7 protocol droid|RA-7]]-,<ref name="Updated and Expanded" /> [[security droid]]s -the [[KX-series security droid|KX-series]]-<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> as well as weaponry such as [[concussion missile]]s.<ref name="Millennium Falcon" /> Arakyd's data storehouse was also trusted with the storage of top-secret information, including information on key [[Project Celestial Power]] personnel such as [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Products==
===Probe and spy droids===
[[File:Recon-Droid-SWCT.png|left|thumb|200px|A Ringneck recon droid, secretly manufactured by Arakyd for the CIS army]]
Arakyd Industries' largest sector of operation was the manufacture of probe droids. With the eruption of the [[Clone Wars]], Arakyd, along with most droid manufacturers, aided the [[Galactic Republic]] in the war effort by producing [[Observation droid (Clone Wars)|observation droids]]<ref name="Build R2-D2 65" /> for the [[Grand Army of the Republic|GAR]]<ref name="To Catch a Jedi">{{TCW|To Catch a Jedi}}</ref> hile also selling it to private entities such as the [[Pyke Syndicate]]<ref name="The Lost One">{{TCW|The Lost One}}</ref> and states such as the [[Zygerrian Slave Empire]] to maximize its profits.<ref name="Slaves of the Republic">{{TCW|Slaves of the Republic}}</ref> Later during the war<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> Arakyd also produced the more advanced [[Prowler 1000 seeker droid]]<ref name="BuildR2 27" /> which was built to be more durable than its observation predecessor, being able to transmit local surveillance data even when significantly damaged.<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

Despite these contracts with the Republic army,<ref name="Build R2-D2 65" /> Arakyd would also covertly support the Republic's rival, the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Assassin, Security, and Other Droids of War}}</ref> Confederate sympathizers inside of Arakyd assembled the spy droid [[4-A7]].<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11">{{BuildR2Cite|11|Droid Directory}}</ref> Arakyd also made the [[Ringneck recon droid]]<ref name="Epic Battles">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Visual Guide: Epic Battles]]''</ref> for the Confederacy,<ref name="Defenders of Peace">{{TCW|Defenders of Peace}}</ref> which used it across operations, including to guard the [[Citadel]] on [[Lola Sayu]]<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref> and the [[CIS mobile command center]] on [[Maridun]] during the [[Defoliator]]'s tests.<ref name="DOP Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/defenders-of-peace-episode-gallery|text="Defenders of Peace" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref> At the same time, Arakyd continued to upgrade their already produced [[Spelunker probe droid]]s,<ref name="Nexus of Power">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref> turning them into [[Chameleon droid]]s to provide reconnaissance and sabotage Republic installations for the CIS.<ref name="Force Users">{{EncyclopediaCite|Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force}}</ref>

===Imperial droid contracts===
[[File:K-2SO_Sideshow.png|thumb|left|150px|Arakyd exploited a loophole in Senate legislation to market KX-series droids as "security"]]
Following their successful cooperation in the production of probe droids, Arakyd continued producing a number of specialized droid models for the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids" /> First in the line of these special units was the KX-series<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> which was designed to be an agile [[battle droid]] on par with an athletic human's skill and dexterity. To bypass the [[Imperial Senate]]'s post-Clone Wars ban on battle droids, Arakyd reclassified it as a "security droid" despite having removed the standard programming restrictions against harming organics.<ref name="The Ultimate Visual Guide" /> KX-series droids became standard companions to occupation forces and were stationed on many worlds from [[Niamos]]<ref name="Niamos Databank">{{Databank|niamos|Niamos}}</ref> to [[Jedha]]'s [[Jedha City|Holy City]].<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> They were also part of the main attack wave during the [[Great Purge of Mandalore]], where they formed the ground forces against [[Mandalorian]] resistance accompanied with [[gunship]]s and Viper probes.<ref name="Return of the Mandalorian">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref>

Another model specifically commissioned by the Empire was the RA-7 droid. Being in urgent need of a nationalized protocol droid series to serve the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and Officer Corps, the Empire quickly assimilated Arakyd's [[Republic Era]] stock,<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11">{{BuildR2Cite|11|Droid Directory}}</ref> repurposing them into logistics<ref name="The Forgotten Droid">{{Rebels|The Forgotten Droid}}</ref> and [[Inventory droid|inventory]] work<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> on board [[Class four container transport]]s<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" /> until the Imperial-commissioned units were ready. As soon as Arakyd completed the development of the new RA-7 they were immediately adopted by the Empire and used in large quantities on board [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyers]] and the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]],<ref name="Build Your Own R2-D2 11" /> -resulting in the units becoming known as the "Death Star droids" despite most never having served on board the [[space station|station]].<ref name="Flight Log">''[[Poe Dameron: Flight Log]]''</ref> Nevertheless, Arakyd's association with the Empire resulted in the company gaining even stronger ties to the regime.<ref name="Assassin, Security, and Combat Droids">{{EncyclopediaCite|Assassin, Security, and Other Droids of War}}</ref>

===Weapons and ammunition===
Apart from their droid manufacturing, Arakyd also produced a number of [[starship]]-mounted armaments. These included [[cannon]]s such as the [[Caltrop-5 chaff gun|Caltrop-5 Chaff Gun]] mounted on [[Jadthu-class landing ship|'"Jadthu''-class landing ships]]<ref name="Knights of Fate" /> and the heavier [[Tomral RM-76 heavy laser cannon]] which could be equipped on [[YT-1300 light freighter]]s.<ref name="OM">''[[YT-1300 Millennium Falcon Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> The company also produced ordnance including the [[Flex Tube proton torpedo launcher]]<ref name="SWCT Y-wing">{{SWCT|set=Blueprints|cardname=Y-wing Starfighter}}</ref> which was equipped on [[BTL-A4 Y-wing assault starfighter/bomber|BTL-A4]] and [[BTA-NR2 Y-wing starfighter]]s<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> as well as the ST-series of concussion missiles which included the [[ST2 concussion missile|ST2]]<ref name="Incredibuilds Falcon">''[[Star Wars: Millennium Falcon Deluxe Book and Model Set]]''</ref> and [[ST7 concussion missile]]s<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> and the associated [[ST2 concussion missile tube|launch tube]].<ref name="Incredibuilds Falcon" />

Arakyd also specialized in the production of exotic [[blaster]] weaponry, which were popular with [[mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[bounty hunter]]s of all kinds. Those included the [[ACP Repeater Gun]], which became known as the "[[Trandoshan]] Chopper" and "the [[Nar Shaddaa]] Grinder,"<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> the [[LJ-50 concussion rifle]] and its [[LJ-40 concussion carbine|modified carbine version]]<ref name="Fully Operational" /> and the [[X8 xenotoxic fléchette]].<ref name="SG" /> The company also introduced a number of [[armor]]-mounted biological enhancements such as the [[Biosteady Smartmonitor]]<ref name="Rise" /> and the [[Mk II Utility Manipulator]].<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Secret subsidiary==
Similar to [[Sonn-Blas Corporation|many]] arms manufacturers during the [[New Republic Era]], Arakyd industries created a secret subsidiary called the [[Arakyd-Harch Technologies]] to covertly manufacture and supply droids to the [[First Order]]. One such droid was the [[Spider probe droid|spider probe]],<ref name="USWNE" /> which was used to survey [[Unidentified moon (Najra-Va)|the moon of]] [[Najra-Va]], one of the first testing sites for the [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="The Core Problem" /> Arakyd also manufactured [[KXFO-series security droid]]s for the new regime,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> which the First Order used to guard the [[refueling depot]] ''[[Titan]]''<ref name="Station to Station">{{Resistance|Station to Station}}</ref> and aboard the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Thunderer]]''.<ref name="The Escape">{{Resistance|The Escape (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Arakyd Industries was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in a post made on the official [[Wikipedia:Facebook|Facebook]] page for the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated series that provided information on the dwarf probe droid.<ref name="probe droid" /> It was originally created for the [[1987]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference book ''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]'' by [[Bill Slavicsek]] and [[Curtis Smith]].<ref name="The Star Wars Sourcebook">''[[The Star Wars Sourcebook]]''</ref>
