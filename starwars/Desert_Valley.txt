The '''Desert Valley''' was a region north of the [[Jundland Wastes]] on [[Tatooine]], a [[desert]] [[planet]] located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. Located west of [[Mos Eisley]] the region contained the [[city]] [[Bestine (city)|Bestine]] as well as the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Fort Sunfire]]. [[Boba Fett's Palace]] and the [[Great Mesra Plateau]] were located in the north of the region.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{Nature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Desert Valley was first identified in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].
