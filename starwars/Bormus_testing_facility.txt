A '''testing facility''' from [[Koensayr Manufacturing]] was active during the early [[Clone Wars]] for the [[Y-wing project]]. [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Ask Aak]] oversaw the facility,<ref name="Starfighters" /> placed in orbit above [[Bormus]]<ref name="BMF 19" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Starfighters" /> as it tested and developed the [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber]]s,<ref name="BMF 19" /> despite the Republic already owning BTL-B Y-wings as force projection prior to the outbreak of war.<ref name="Collapse">''[[Collapse of the Republic]]''</ref>
