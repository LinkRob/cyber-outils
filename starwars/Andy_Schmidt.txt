'''Andy Schmidt''' (born [[August 3]] in [[Wikipedia:Fairmont, West Virginia|Fairmont]], [[Wikipedia:West Virginia|West Virginia]], [[Wikipedia:United States|United States]]), also known as '''Peter Andrewes Schmidt''', is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] author and editor. For the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, he authored children's short stories in anthologies such as ''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]'' and ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''.

==Biography==
Andy Schmidt, also known as Peter Andrewes Schmidt,<ref name="Interview" /> was born on [[August 3]]<ref name="Birthday" /> in [[Wikipedia:Fairmont, West Virginia|Fairmont]], [[Wikipedia:West Virginia|West Virginia]], [[Wikipedia:United States|United States]]. In adulthood, he became an author and editor.<ref name="Interview" /> For the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, he authored short stories collected in children's books. He enjoyed being able to write the stories when his children were the proper age to read them.<ref name="Twitter Quote 1" /> Schmidt's first stories were "[[A Bad Feeling About This]]"<ref name="OTS Bad Feeling">{{StoryCite|story=A Bad Feeling About This|book=The Original Trilogy Stories}}</ref> and "[[AT-AT Attack! (short story)|AT-AT Attack!]]," adapting events from<ref name="OTS AT-AT">{{StoryCite|story=AT-AT Attack! (short story)|book=The Original Trilogy Stories}}</ref> the [[1977]] film<ref name="IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']] and<ref name="OTS Bad Feeling" /> the [[1980]] film<ref name="V">{{Film|V}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] respectively.<ref name="OTS AT-AT" /> Both stories were published in ''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]''<ref name="OTS">''[[The Original Trilogy Stories]]''</ref> on [[September 1]], [[2015]].<ref name="Disney OTS Date">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-the-original-trilogy-stories|text=Star Wars: The Original Trilogy Stories}}</ref> On [[December 18]] of the same year,<ref name="5-Min Date">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-5-minute-star-wars-stories|text=Star Wars: 5-Minute Star Wars Stories}}</ref> Schmidt's third story, "[[A Jedi, You Must Become]]," also adapting events from ''The Empire Strikes Back'', was published in the anthology ''[[5-Minute Star Wars Stories]]''.<ref name="5 Min Jedi">{{StoryCite|story=A Jedi, You Must Become|book=5-Minute Star Wars Stories}}</ref>

On [[September 26]], [[2017]], ''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]'', a follow-up to ''The Original Trilogy Stories'', was published.<ref name="Amazon PTS Date">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-prequel-trilogy-stories|text=Star Wars: The Prequel Trilogy Stories}}</ref> In the anthology, three stories by Schmidt are featured.<ref name="PTS">''[[The Prequel Trilogy Stories]]''</ref> The first story, "[[Short Negotiations (The Prequel Trilogy Stories)|Short Negotiations]]," adapts events from the opening of<ref name="PTS Short">{{StoryCite|story=Short Negotiations (The Prequel Trilogy Stories)|book=The Prequel Trilogy Stories}}</ref> the [[1999]] film<ref name="I">{{Film|I}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="PTS Short" /> The second story, "[[Ambushed!]]," adapts a sequence from<ref name="PTS Ambushed">{{StoryCite|story=Ambushed!|book=The Prequel Trilogy Stories}}</ref> the [[2002]] film<ref name="II">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="PTS Ambushed" /> Schmidt's final story, "[[Empire Ascendant]]," retells scenes from the third act of<ref name="PTS Empire">{{StoryCite|story=Empire Ascendant|book=The Prequel Trilogy Stories}}</ref> the [[2005]] film<ref name="III">{{Film|III}}</ref> [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="PTS Empire" />
