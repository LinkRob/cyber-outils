'''"Prince" Patren''' was a [[Bedlam Raiders]] [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] who wielded an [[Color|orange]] [[crossguard lightsaber]]. [[Rayvis]] sent him along with [[Urjef Mekor]] and [[Sebb Eshan]] in Phon'Qi's Grotto on the [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. They ambushed [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] and tried to [[Death|kill]] him. After a fierce fight, Cal killed all three Raiders.<ref name="Survivor" />
