{{Quote|The wealthy leader of the Crimson Dawn crime syndicate was extremely proud of his museum-grade collection of strange treasures from across the galaxy. Among his most prized possessions was a near-complete set of Mandalorian armor dating back to the age of the Old Republic; rare animals preserved in suspended animation; and an ark allegedly containing the ashes of Chancellor Contispex I.|A book's entry on Dryden Vos' collection|<ref name="100 Objects">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref>}}
'''Contispex I''' was an individual that served as a [[chancellor]] who [[Death|perished]]<ref name="TOG">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> by [[10 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Solo Date" /> Contispex's ashes were held in arks aboard the ''[[First Light]]'', the personal [[Yacht|star yacht]] of [[Crimson Dawn]] [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]].<ref name="TOG" /> Vos considered the arks among his most prized posessions. A [[Unidentified historical book|book]] describing objects relating to [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] included an entry on the [[Lost King of Duro]]'s [[Limb|hand]] in Vos' collection, which also mentioned the ark containing Contispex's ashes.<ref name="100 Objects" />

==Behind the scenes==
Contispex I's ashes first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology]] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> before being identified as Contispex I in the accompanying reference book, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TOG" /> [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Contispex I/Legends|Contispex I]] of the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity via the [[2005]] reference book ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and [[Kevin J. Anderson]].<ref name="TNEC">''[[The New Essential Chronology]]''</ref> He was later identified as Contispex I by the [[2012]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]'' by [[Jason Fry]] and [[Paul R. Urquhart]].<ref name="EGTW">''[[The Essential Guide to Warfare]]''</ref>
