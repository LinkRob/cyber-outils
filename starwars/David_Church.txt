'''David Church''' is a British actor who had small parts in [[1977]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']] and [[1983]]'s [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']]. In the former, he played a [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtrooper]]<ref name="Confirmation">{{EmailCite|email=File:Beck2.png|creator=David Church|date=2021-07-07}}</ref> and a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Technician/Legends|technician]], and in the latter, a Rebel [[Commando/Legends|commando]] seen on [[Home One/Legends|''Home One'']] during the [[Death Star II/Legends|Death Star]] briefing scene.<ref name="church1" /> In [[1999]], the [[Endor Limited]] expansion set of the [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game]] consolidated said extra with the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] character [[Lieutenant/Legends|Lieutenant]] [[Judder Page/Legends|Page]].<ref name="ccg">{{CCG|set=Endor Limited|cardname=Lieutenant Page}}</ref> Church later discovered that his ''Return of the Jedi'' character had been given a name during a signing at a Collectormania convention.<ref name="church1" />

Church's later work included standing in for [[Wikipedia:Warren Clarke|Warren Clarke]] on the BBC series [[Wikipedia:Dalziel and Pascoe (BBC TV series)|''Dalziel and Pascoe'']].<ref name="church1" />
