'''Aldic Nelleer''' was a [[human]] [[musician]] manager that represented the drummer for [[Skungy Gungus and the Refractors]], [[Remla Moonis]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> When the band was booked to perform for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] at [[Boba Fett's Palace|his palace]] on [[Tatooine]], Moonis got nervous about performing for Jabba's and ran away to [[Wayfar]] planning to focus on going solo.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Fearing for the band and herself, Nelleer requested help from the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] who tracked down Moonis and convinced him to come back to player with the band. Later that year, Vess found a [[datapad]] in the [[Wonto District market]] in [[Mos Eisley]] which stated that while the performance stared out well the band didn't jive together when trying new material which ultimately led to one member being dropped into Jabba's [[rancor]] pit.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}
