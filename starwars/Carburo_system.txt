The '''Carburo system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Itopol sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It was located in grid square K-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the astronomical object [[Carburo]].<ref name="Origin" />
