The '''CryoBan projector''', manufactured by [[Tagge Corporation]], was originally designed to [[Fire extinguisher|extinguish]] [[fire]]s aboard [[starship]]s using a supercooling chemical agent. However, independent operators soon discovered that removing the safety nozzles from [[CryoBan]] projectors could convert them into lethal [[weapon]]s.<ref name="Gadgets" />

==Behind the scenes==
The CryoBan projector was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Gadgets" /> It was originally introduced in ''[[Fly Casual]]'', a game supplement for the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' series.<ref name="Fly">''[[Fly Casual]]''</ref>
