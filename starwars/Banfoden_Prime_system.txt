The '''Banfoden Prime system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Alchenaut sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]. It lay in grid square L-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Banfoden Prime]].<ref name="shield" /> Between [[389 BBY]] and [[379 BBY]],<ref name="Date">[[Rooper Nitani]] is around eighteen [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of "[[Shield of the Jedi]]." In ''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]'', the events of which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]], Nitani is fifteen years old. Therefore, "Shield of the Jedi" must take place around [[379 BBY]]. The [[uprising on Banfoden Prime]] took place before the events of "Shield of the Jedi" and after Nitani became a [[Padawan]], which "Shield of the Jedi" establishes as having taken place ten years before its events.</ref> [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Rooper Nitani]] settled an [[uprising on Banfoden Prime]].<ref name="shield" />
