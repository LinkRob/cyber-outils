'''Fantasy Flight Games''' ('''FFG''') is the current license holder to ''Star Wars'' card, [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] and miniatures games.<ref name="InitialAnnouncement">{{FFGweb|url=en/news/2011/8/2/use-the-force|text=Use The Force}}</ref> When they made this announcement in [[August]] of [[2011]], they also announced that they were planning to release ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Miniatures Game]]'', a tactical miniatures game, and ''[[Star Wars: The Card Game]]'', a cooperative card game, in Spring 2012.<ref name="InitialAnnouncement" /> Fantasy Flight Games is based in [[Wikipedia:Roseville, Minnesota|Roseville, Minnesota]].<ref name="Fantasy Flight Games About" />

At Gen Con in 2012, it was announced that [[Sterling Hershey]] was one of the designers of Fantasy Flight Games' new ''Star Wars''&ndash;themed [[roleplaying game]] (RPG), ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]''. That year, he discussed on his blog the names of two future installments: a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] vs. [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Empire]] campaign named [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Age of Rebellion'']] in [[2014]] and a [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]-based campaign called [[Star Wars: Force and Destiny|''Force and Destiny'']] in [[2015]].<ref name="Beta Announced">{{WebCite|url=http://www.sterlinghershey.com/blog/2012/8/22/star-wars-wednesday-swrpg-edge-of-the-empire-beta-announced.html|text=Star Wars Wednesday - SWRPG: Edge of the Empire Beta Announced!|archivedate=20160401155951}}</ref>

However, on [[June 6]], [[2013]], he announced on his website the completion and upcoming release of the first direct expansion of the game, ''[[Beyond the Rim]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]''{{'s}} first full length adventure, scheduled for release in the third quarter of 2013.<ref name="blog">{{WebCite|url=http://www.sterlinghershey.com/blog/2013/6/6/star-wars-beyond-the-rim.html|text="Star Wars Beyond The Rim" (Thursday, June 6, 2013, 7:08PM)|archivedate=20160403155533}}</ref> In 2014, Fantasy Flight merged with the [[Asmodee Group]].<ref name="Who We Are">{{SWUweb|url=about|text=Who We Are}}</ref>

On [[November 3]], 2015, [[Star Wars: Rebellion (board game)|''Star Wars: Rebellion'']] was announced on Fantasy Flight Games' website.<ref name="SWR Announcement">{{FFGweb|url=en/news/2015/11/3/star-wars-rebellion|text=Star Wars: Rebellion Announcement}}</ref> It was released on [[March 31]], 2016.<ref name="SWR Released">{{FFGweb|url=en/news/2016/3/31/the-rebellion-begins-now|text=The Rebellion Begins Now}}</ref>

On [[July 29]], 2016, the collectible dice card game [[Star Wars: Destiny|''Star Wars'': Destiny]] was announced, with a release of [[November]] 2016.<ref name="Destiny">{{SW|url=news/characters-collide-in-star-wars-destiny-from-fantasy-flight-games-exclusive|text=Characters Collide in ''Star Wars: Destiny'' from Fantasy Flight Games - Exclusive!}}</ref>

At the 2019 Gen Con, an adventure for the RPG, "[[Begun, The Clone Wars Have!]]," was made available to attendees.

On [[November 16]], [[2020]], it was announced that distribution and manufacture of the ''Star Wars'' line of miniatures games [[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition|''X-Wing Second Edition'']], ''[[Star Wars: Armada]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' transitioned to [[Atomic Mass Games]], a subsidiary of [[Asmodee Group]] dedicated to miniatures games.<ref name="Special Announcement">{{AMGweb|url=special-announcement|text=Atomic Mass Games Takes Its First Steps Into The Star Wars Miniatures Games Galaxy|nolive=1}} {{C|"In a strategic reorganization of its Star Wars miniatures titles, Asmodee is announcing today that oversight of its three Star Wars miniatures titles will be moving to their dedicated miniatures games studio, Atomic Mass Games."}}</ref> ''Star Wars'' RPGs will now fall under the newly created division [[Edge Studio]], and FFG will continue to manufacture and distribute board games and trading card games for Asmodee.
