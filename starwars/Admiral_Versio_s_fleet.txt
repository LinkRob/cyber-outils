'''Admiral Versio's fleet''' of the [[Imperial Navy]] was led by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] in the last year of the Galactic Civil War. The fleet consisted of at least five [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]], including the [[flagship]] ''[[Eviscerator]]'' and [[Gideon Hask's Star Destroyer|one]] commanded by [[Gideon Hask]]. Three of the fleet's [[Star Destroyer]]s were destroyed near [[Chinook Station]] on [[Bespin]] while refueling, and the ''Eviscerator'' was destroyed during the [[Battle of Jakku]], the final engagement of the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Description==
The [[fleet]] led by [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Admiral]] [[Garrick Versio]] consisted of at least five [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyers]]. Among these was the Versio's [[flagship]], the ''[[Eviscerator]]'', as well as the [[Gideon Hask's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] of [[Inferno Squad]] [[Commander]] [[Gideon Hask]]. The fleet was supported by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s, [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s including one piloted by Hask during the Battle of Jakku and [[TIE/sa tactical bomber]]s. The TIE forces were divided in several units and at least one squadron. The fleet was sustained by supplies from [[Chinook Station]] on [[Bespin]] and an [[abandoned weapons depot]] on [[Sullust]].<ref name="Battlefront II" />

==History==
[[File:SWBF2_Eviscerator_Vardos.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The fleet's flagship, the ''Eviscerator'']]
Following a [[skirmish on Vardos]] during [[Operation: Cinder]] in which the ''Eviscerator'' and [[Dauntless (Imperial-class)|''Dauntless'']] participated in, ISB Admiral Versio eventually came to the command of a fleet.<ref name="Battlefront II" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date Note">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates [[Han Solo]] meeting with an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Ralsius Paldora|defector]] at [[Takodana Castle|Maz's castle]] to get information that would help in the [[liberation of Kashyyyk]] to twenty-nine [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the meeting and the resulting skirmish took place in [[5 ABY]]. As the mission to [[Chinook Station]] took place between Solo's meeting with the Imperial defector, and the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''Star Wars: Galactic Atlas'' dates to 5 ABY, the mission to Chinook Station also took place in 5 ABY.</ref> the [[New Republic]] took an operation to Chinook Station, located on [[Bespin]] in the [[Anoat sector]]. Despite the [[sector]]'s lockdown by [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] and his [[Ubrik Adelhard's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]], Versio and his forces were able to receive [[fuel]] supplies from a fuel depot near Chinook Station.<ref name="Battlefront II" />

During the mission, [[Commander]] [[Gideon Hask]] left aboard one of the fleet's Star Destroyers. As a result, the two New Republic operatives switched to target the fuel depot that was currently refueling three Star Destroyers. The successful chain reaction destroyed the three ships. Following this mission and the [[destruction of an abandoned weapons depot]] on Sullust, Versio's supplies were cut. Versio eventually joined the Imperial loyalists assembled on [[Jakku]] led by [[Counselor to the Empire]] [[Gallius Rax]]<ref name="Battlefront II" />&mdash; and rival to Grand Moff Adelhard<ref name="Last Stand 4">[[Battle of Jakku — Last Stand 4|''Battle of Jakku — Last Stand'' 4]]</ref>&mdash; with his ''Eviscerator''. Hask additionally joined Versio, and their forces participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]] against the New Republic. During the battle, the ''Eviscerator'' was destroyed and Versio was [[Death|killed]], and the battle eventually resulted in a victory for the Republic and the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]].
