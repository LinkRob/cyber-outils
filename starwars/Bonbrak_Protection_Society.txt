The '''Bonbrak Protection Society''' were a group tasked with protecting [[Bonbrak]]s, a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that had been classified as a protected species by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the [[Corellian]] [[bodyguard]] [[Dizcaro]] informed his rival [[Alys Ongwa|Alys "Crash" Ongwa]] that he was charging her for a [[comlink]] she had [[Thief|stolen]] from him, as well as the bail [[Currency|money]] for his client&mdash;the [[Shani]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]]&mdash;who Ongwa had had arrested. In response, Ongwa stated she would have the money sent to the Bonbrak Protection Society.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
