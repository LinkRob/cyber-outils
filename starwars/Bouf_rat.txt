A '''bouf rat''' was a type of [[rodent]] that was preyed upon by a [[lyxine]]. While waiting to inform the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] that the crew of the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] and [[flagship]] of the elite [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[fleet]] [[Death Squadron]], the ''[[Executor]]'', had located the [[YT-1300 light freighter]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', newly-made [[Admiral]] [[Firmus Piett]] compared himself to a lyxine watching a bouf [[rat]] as he detailed how he had risen through the ranks of the [[Imperial Navy]].<ref name="For the Last Time" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bouf rat was mentioned in the short story "[[For the Last Time]]," written by [[Beth Revis]] and published in [[2020]] as part of the anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="For the Last Time" />
