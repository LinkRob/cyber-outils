The '''flower of life''' was a stylized [[flower]] that was used as an emblem and a decorative design in the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] culture. When she was a [[senator]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] owned an [[orichalc]] [[Naboo crest headdress|headdress]] decorated with continuous flower of life patterns on its crest.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
