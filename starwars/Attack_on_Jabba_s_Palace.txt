The '''Attack on Jabba's Palace''' was a skirmish carried out by the [[Shadow Collective]] at [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]], after [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] ordered his [[bounty hunter]]s to kill [[Maul]] on [[Nal Hutta]]. Landing on the Hutt grounds, [[Death Watch]] warriors attacked Jabba's men, killing many. With the warriors doing the dirty work, the leaders walked right into the palace, opposed by none. Entering Jabba's main chamber, [[Pre Vizsla]] and Maul gave Jabba and [[Gorga Desilijic Aarrpo|Gorga]] one chance to join them. Jabba conceded defeat and gave the [[Hutt Clan]]'s backing to Death Watch's [[Takeover of Mandalore|coup]] on [[Mandalore]]; which was not part of the [[Galactic Republic]] and therefore, the Clan was not breaking the [[Hutt–Republic treaty|treaty]] or declaring war on it. The surviving guards and thugs were helped up by the Death Watch warriors.<ref name="Eminence" />
