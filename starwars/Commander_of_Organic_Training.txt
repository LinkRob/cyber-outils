'''Commander of Organic Training''' was a title used by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Horn Ambigene]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]]. The title was given to Ambigene by the Confederacy's [[Head of State|leader]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]], after he stationed Ambigene on the [[planet]] [[Pzandias]] following the [[Bryx sector]]'s secession from the Republic. While on Pzandias, Ambigene was tasked with teaching fellow Separatists how to best keep their [[homeworld]]s free from the Republic.<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title of Commander of Organic Training was mentioned in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" />
