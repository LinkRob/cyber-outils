'''Dubraib''' was a [[waterworld]]<ref name="Starlight" /> in the [[Cegul sector]]'s [[Dubraib system]],<ref name="Appendix" /> located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> and the [[Western Reaches]],<ref name="WesternReaches" /> It was situated in grid square M-20 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Galactic Republic]] designated a section of the Outer Rim<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]''</ref>&mdash;which included Dubraib<ref name="Starlight" />&mdash;as the [[Galactic Frontier]], [[Planet|worlds]] considered far removed enough from [[the galaxy]]'s center to send explorers to.<ref name="Chronicles" />  In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Starlight: Hidden Danger]]" to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Master]] [[Gil Jaretto]] traveled to the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] from Dubraib.<ref name="Starlight" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dubraib was first mentioned in part two of "[[Starlight: Hidden Danger]]," a short story written by [[Justina Ireland]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Starlight" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The story was published in the [[Star Wars Insider 204|204th]] issue of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine as part of the [[The High Republic: Starlight|''Star Wars: The High Republic: Starlight'']] serialized fiction<ref name="Starlight" /> on [[August 10]], [[2021]].<ref name="Titan">{{TitanComics|url=m/79-star-wars-insider-204|text=Star Wars Insider #204}}</ref>
