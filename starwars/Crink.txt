'''Crink''' was a swear word used in [[the galaxy]]. It was part of the phrase "crink it," used by people like [[Beilert Valance]]<ref name="BH1">[[Bounty Hunters 1|''Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref> and [[T'onga]] to express anger.<ref name="BH4">[[Bounty Hunters 4|''Bounty Hunters'' 4]]</ref> The variant '''crinking''' was used as an intensifier, by people like [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]],<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" /> [[T'ongor]],<ref name="BH1" /> Valance<ref name="BH3">[[Bounty Hunters 3|''Bounty Hunters'' 3]]</ref> and [[Dengar]].<ref name="BH16">[[Bounty Hunters 16|''Bounty Hunters'' 16]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The word "crink" originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] novel ''[[Allegiance (novel)|Allegiance]]'', where it was part of the phrase "go crink yourself" and the variant "crinking."<ref name="Allegiance">[[Allegiance (novel)|''Allegiance'']]</ref> The phrase "go crink yourself" also appears in the Legends junior novel ''[[Rebel Force: Renegade]]''.<ref name="Renegade">''[[Rebel Force: Renegade]]''</ref>

"Crink"<ref name="BH1" /> and its variant "crinking"<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" /> are phonetically similar to the real-world noun "frick"<ref name="Frick">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/frick|text=frick|quote=a euphemism for the word ''f*ck''.|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|''Dictionary.com'']]|archivedate=20250105164244}}</ref> and the corresponding intensifier "fricking,"<ref name="Fricking">{{WebCite|url=https://www.thefreedictionary.com/fricking|text=fricking|quote=''adj slang'' (intensifier): ''surrounded by fricking idiots.'' [C20: euphemism for f*cking]|work=[[Wikipedia:The Free Dictionary|''The Free Dictionary'']]|archivedate=20250105165300}}</ref> which are euphemisms for the swear words "f*ck"<ref name="Frick" /> and "f*cking."<ref name="Fricking" />
