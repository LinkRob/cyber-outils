During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Partisans]] led by [[Saw Gerrera]] undertook a series of '''attacks''' in the [[Arkanis sector]]. During the attacks the Partisans scored a number of victories against Imperial storehouses, [[convoy]]s and inspection stations, leaving no survivors. Sometime after, [[Cynabar's InfoNet]] reported on the attacks, stating that they had been making life miserable for the [[Unidentified territorial moff|territorial moff]] and that it was a rare display of ruthlessness from the growing anti-Empire resistance. Cynabar added that after the attacks, the [[bounty]] on Gerrera's head was likely to increase, so [[Bounty hunter|hunters]] willing to wait longer would get a bigger payout.<ref name="Rebel Files" />
