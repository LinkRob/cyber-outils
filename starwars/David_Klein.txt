'''David Klein''' is the director of photography for five episodes of the TV series [[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]], the [[2021]] series ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', the [[2024]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' and the [[2026]] film ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''.
