'''Donato Giancola''' is an artist who created concept art for ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided]]''. His art was included in the art book ''[[From Pencil to Pixel: The Art of Star Wars Galaxies]]'', which was included with every collector's edition of the game. He has created art for other fictional universes, including Middle-Earth and Star Trek.
