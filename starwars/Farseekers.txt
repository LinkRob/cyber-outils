The '''Farseekers''' were an organization of [[Force-sensitive]] [[navigator]]s that operated in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the [[Grysk]] coordinator "[[Jixtus]]" offered to assign a Farseeker or a [[Void Guides|Void Guide]], another type of navigator, to his [[Agbui]] agent [[Haplif]], who already had an [[Attendants|Attendant]] in his service (Jixtus did not want the Attendant to be seen by anyone in the Chiss Ascendancy) Instead of assigning a Farseeker or Void Guide, Jixtus assigned a [[Pathfinders (Navigators' Guild)|Pathfinder]], [[Qilori]], as Haplif said that they were used on the [[Chiss]] [[planet]] [[Celwis]], where he would try to manipulate the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] into a civil war.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Farseekers were mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second novel in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Ascendancy Trilogy'']].<ref name="Greater Good" />
