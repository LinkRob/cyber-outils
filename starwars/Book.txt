'''Books''' were written works bounded together in print. In the [[Galactic Empire]] they were rare, as most preferred other means of storage, such as [[data-tape]]s or [[flimsiplast]]. [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus|Kallus]] was surprised to observe a shelf of books when he visited the office of [[Captain]] [[Hiram Zataire|Zataire]] on the ''[[Lawbringer]]''.<ref name="begins">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> [[Beru Whitesun Lars]] read to her nephew, [[Luke Skywalker]], from storybooks.<ref name="ANH Illustrated">''[[A New Hope: The Princess, the Scoundrel, and the Farm Boy]]''</ref> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] adhered to the ''[[White Book]]'', a field guide detailing procedures and regulations set by [[Alliance High Command]].<ref name="TC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref> The [[Sacred Jedi texts]] were a collection of [[Jedi]] books present on [[Ahch-To]] in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Shelved along the wall, and sorted by topic and author, were book&mdash;not data-tapes or flimsiplast replicas, but collector's hard copies, ink printed on paper.|Agent Kallus observing the bookshelves of Captain Zataire|<ref name="begins" />}}
The concept of printed books being scarce in the world of ''[[Star Wars]]'' originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] in titles such as ''[[The New Jedi Order: Force Heretic II: Refugee]]''. Though these titles are now considered part of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] and not [[canon]], the idea of printed books being scarce remains in the new canon continuity.
