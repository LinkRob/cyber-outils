The '''First Order Hootenanny''' was an event hosted by the [[First Order]] that involved [[Music|singing]]. At one point, the [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] [[Finn|FN-2187]] witnessed [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] singing at the First Order Hootenanny. While [[Escape from the Finalizer|escaping]] from the ''[[Finalizer]]'', he recounted the experience to [[Poe Dameron]] as being "horrible," comparing it to the [[torture]] Dameron had experienced at Ren's [[hand]].<ref name="LEGOTSS">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>
