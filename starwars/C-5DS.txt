'''C-5DS''' was a [[droid]] in service to [[General]] [[Grievous]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]], in which the [[Galactic Republic]] fought against the Confederacy. Early during the war, they communicated with [[chief supervisor]] [[Poreni]] about [[Akiva production plant|droid production]] on the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. 

==Biography==
{{Quote|Supervisor Poreni. I am C-5DS, on behalf of General Grievous. Field data indicates that batch 4652-A of B1 battle droids have proven insufficient in terms of combat performance. Out of more than twenty-six thousand deployed droids, less than five percent have survived engagement with Republic forces. Before scheduling another order, we require an update as how the product will be improved.|C-5DS to chief supervisor Poreni|audio=FieldDataIndicatesThatBatch4652A-Outlaws.flac|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
[[File:GrievousStand-SWHC821.png|thumb|right|200|C-5DS served General Grievous (pictured) during the Clone Wars.]]
C-5DS was a [[droid]] in service of [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies|Supreme Commander of the Droid Army]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia UE">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> for the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a [[Galaxy|galactic]] conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the Confederacy<ref name="Outlaws" /> that took place from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Early during the war,<ref name="Rishi Station note">{{Databank|rishi-station|Rishi Station}} establishes that the events of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' episode "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]," which puts itself to be early in the BX-series droid commando's existence, occurs in the early period of the [[Clone Wars]]. If the BX-series was new during the early war, then the Clone Wars-era transmission about preliminary BX testing seen in ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' must have been from the early war period as well.</ref> C-5DS communicated with [[chief supervisor]] [[Poreni]] in a series of messages about the performance of a [[Batch 4652-A|batch]] of [[B1-series battle droid]]s produced at the [[Akiva production plant|droid factory]] that Poreni oversaw on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

During the exchange, C-5DS informed Poreni that the batch's combat performance proved insufficient, with less than five percent of the twenty-six thousand [[battle droid]]s deployed against [[Grand Army of the Republic|Galactic Republic forces]] surviving the engagement. In response, Poreni apologized for the poor performance and offered a twenty percent discount on the next batch of B1 battle droids as well as a promise to improve the droid's future performance. In the following message, C-5DS accepted the discount and went on to make an advance order of twelve thousand [[BX-series droid commando]]s, following the promising preliminary tests of the BX-series. Copies of at least four of these transmissions between C-5DS and Poreni were captured by equipment that remained in [[orbit]] over Akiva through the end of the war and into the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|successor]], the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
Four of the transmissions were later recovered by the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> Vess's companion [[ND-5]], a BX-series droid commando, was able to use the messages between C-5DS and Poreni along with several other Clone Wars&ndash;era transmissions to track down a Separatist [[Abandoned Hideout|base]] on Akiva.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Characteristics==
C-5DS was capable of speaking [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
C-5DS's voice appeared in four collectible transmissions in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The four collectible transmissions, titled "CIS 01" through "CIS 04," were part of the "Separatist Transmissions" expert intel quest, which eventually leads the player to [[MT-7]], another BX-series droid and one of nine experts who the player can complete quests for in order to gain various abilities.<ref name="Outlaws" />
