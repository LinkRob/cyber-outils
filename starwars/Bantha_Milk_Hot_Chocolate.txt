'''Bantha Milk Hot Chocolate''' was a variation of [[Bantha Chai]], the signature drink of [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]]. Tuggs created the new recipe for the holiday of [[Life Day]], keeping the familiar [[Blue milk|bantha milk]] but swapping the [[tea]] for [[chocolate]]. The result was a bright [[Color|blue]] beverage with a light brown chocolate swirl on top.<ref name="life day" />

After Tuggs published his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="life day" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> Tuggs included a recipe for Bantha Milk Hot Chocolate in his holiday [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', where he explained the background of creating a Bantha Chai variant and noted that he expected to be drinking much of the [[hot chocolate]] during the [[winter]] [[Standard month|months]]. He also described the drink as rich and perfect for the holidays due to the addition of chocolate.<ref name="life day" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bantha Milk Hot Chocolate was introduced in ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'',<ref name="life day" /> a recipe book authored by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]] and published on [[November 9]], [[2021]]. It was first revealed in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the book on [[July 27]] of that year.<ref name="life day preview">{{SW|url=news/the-life-day-cookbook-exclusive-preview|text=Prepare a Feast Fit for a Wookiee With ''The Life Day Cookbook'' - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>

The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the drink's [[Wikipedia:Cream cheese|cream cheese]] topping calls for cream cheese, [[Wikipedia:Confectioners' sugar|confectioners' sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], and [[Wikipedia:Heavy cream|heavy cream]]. The milk then requires [[Wikipedia:Whole milk|whole milk]], [[Wikipedia:Vanilla extract|vanilla extract]], [[Wikipedia:White chocolate|white chocolate]], and blue [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|food gel dye]].<ref name="life day" />
