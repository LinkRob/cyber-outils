'''Bardelberan 7''',<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> also known simply as '''Bardelberan''', was a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> located in the [[Bardelberan system]], within the [[Churnis sector]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]. It was situated in grid square J-6 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], Bardelberan 7 had an [[Imperial Garrison Bardelberan 7|Imperial Garrison]], where the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] was once involved in an incident.<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of the first part of ''[[Pirate's Price]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> upon leaving the planet [[Galagolos V]] the [[YT-1300 light freighter|light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was contacted by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/sh shuttle]], which informed the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] that they were harboring a suspect wanted for the Bardelberan 7 incident. Solo believed [[Mahjo Reeloo]] was the suspect, but she informed him that she had never visited Bardelberan 7 before, and Solo later deduced that it was actually Ohnaka who was the suspect.<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> The position of Bardelberan 7 was eventually noted on a [[map]] included in the [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', which was written by the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and published in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="TGDate">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bardelberan 7 was first mentioned in the junior novel ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', which was written by [[Lou Anders]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
