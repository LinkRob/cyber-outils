'''Batuv''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Build X-wing">{{BuildXWingCite|63|Starfighter Aces}}</ref> in the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was occupied and [[blockade]]d by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Prior to his meeting with [[Tobias Beckett]], [[Captain]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], and his co-[[pilot]] [[L3-37]] took the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to Batuv where Calrissian picked up a [[cape]] from [[Borkus (Trandoshan)|Borkus]].<ref name="Double or Nothing 1" />
