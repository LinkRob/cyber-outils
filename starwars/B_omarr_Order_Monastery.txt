The '''B'omarr Order Monastery''', also known as the '''Teth Monastery''', was a monastery built by the [[B'omarr Order]] on [[Teth]], a [[jungle]] [[Planet|world]] in [[Wild Space]], until its abandonment. As was common for such monasteries,<ref name="TCWfilm" /> [[Ziro the Hutt's criminal organization]] made use of the building.<ref name="Helmet Collection 58" /> During [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure]]'s alliance with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the monastery was taken over by [[Asajj Ventress]] and the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]], and they used it to hide [[Rotta]], the infant [[Gender|son]] of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]], as part of their plan to make the [[Hutt Clan]] believe that the [[Jedi Order]] had kidnapped the Huttlet. This led to the [[Battle of Teth]] between the Separatists and the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], with [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] leading Republic forces to take the monastery.<ref name="TCWfilm" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Rex]]'s [[Clone Underground]] used the monastery as their base of operations. However, it was destroyed and left in ruins when the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] discovered its location and sent a [[Recovery Strike Team|strike team]] to [[Battle on the B'omarr Order Monastery|capture]] the clone [[Omega]].<ref name="Infiltration">{{TBB|Infiltration (episode)}}</ref>
