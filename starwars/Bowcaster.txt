A '''bowcaster''', also known as a '''laser crossbow''',<ref name="TFAVD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> was a type of traditional, handcrafted projectile weapon commonly used by [[Wookiee]]s.

==Description==
[[File:Bowcaster-SW.png|thumb|left|200px|A side view of a bowcaster]]
Through the use of magnetic acceleration, bowcasters were more powerful and accurate than the average [[blaster]]. Designs differed through the materials used and the creator's artistic approach, although the end result was visually similar to a crossbow-like weapon.<ref name="Bowcaster" /> Bowcasters were usually handcrafted, however some popular models are mass-produced in workshops all over Kashyyyk.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

The weapon used metal [[quarrel]]s enveloped with [[plasma]] energy<ref name="Bowcaster" /> as ammunition. Two [[Alternating polarizer|polarizing orbs]], balanced on each end of the bow, created a [[magnetic field]] that boosted the quarrel's momentum. Once the cocking spring was pulled back, the trigger fired the quarrel, which was enveloped in plasma energy.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

The last (known) [[Chewbacca's bowcaster|bowcaster]] to be crafted by [[Chewbacca]], a Wookiee warrior and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] hero, was created during the [[Galactic Empire]] era. Chewbacca unconventionally crafted the weapon using the frame and [[Power cell|power pack]] of a standard [[stormtrooper]] blaster;<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> additionally, Chewbacca also fitted it with an automatic cocking system, a feature traditional models of the time lacked.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

[[File:Bowcaster_Wookiee_SWL.png|thumb|right|250px|A Wookiee warrior firing his bowcaster]]
Bowcasters were much more powerful than standard [[blaster rifle]]s, but also much heavier, meaning few [[human]]s were capable of carrying one around, let alone hold it level and steady for a precise shot.<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> To fire comfortably, the strength of a Wookiee was required.<ref name="TFAVD" /> However, this statistic was challenged by [[Han Solo]], who was in his sixties at this time, on numerous occasions during the [[cold war]], in which he became fond of the weapon and have somehow developed the strength to use the weapon despite his age.<ref name="Episode VII" />

==History==
Bowcasters were based on ancient weapons from Wookiee history that fired poisoned darts and arrows.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]] used a bowcaster as his favored weapon. Embo’s bowcaster could easily be folded away for travel.<ref name="Bounty Hunters">{{TCW|Bounty Hunters (episode)}}</ref> At the end of the war, bowcasters were also used in the [[Battle of Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> Chewbacca crafted several of them over time.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />

[[File:ILikeThisThing-TFA.png|thumb|left|250px|Han Solo used Chewbacca's bowcaster during the Cold War.]]
During a [[Skirmish aboard the Eravana|skirmish]] on board the ''[[Eravana]]'' and later in a [[Battle of Takodana|battle]] on [[Takodana]], Han Solo used Chewbacca's current bowcaster, which he quickly came to enjoy firing due to its strong firepower. After Solo was [[Death|killed]] during the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] by his [[Reproduction|son]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], Chewbacca fired at Ren in anger, shooting his bowcaster<ref name="Episode VII" /> with the intention of killing the [[dark side adept]]. Nevertheless, Ren managed to survive being hit.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
