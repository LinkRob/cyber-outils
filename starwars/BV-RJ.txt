'''BV-RJ''' was a [[Color|gray-plated]] [[2-1B surgical droid]] with yellow [[sensor]]s who was stationed in the [[Sublight Lounge]] aboard the<ref name="Summer" /> [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s<ref name="Chandrila">[[Star Wars: Datapad (Galactic Starcruiser)]]</ref> passenger liner ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Summer" /> no earlier than [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[LEGO Star Wars Summer Vacation]]'' after set after the events of {{Film|IX}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[35 ABY]].</ref> When the [[human]] [[Finn]] entered the lounge, BV-RJ introduced<ref name="Summer" /> itself<ref name="EasterEggs" /> with human-[[cyborg]] libations and offered him a drink while carrying a white towel with an orange stripe on its shoulder. Finn asked what the [[medical droid]] would recommend to someone who was ditched by his friends, and BV-RJ at first recommended better friends before pouring Finn a green [[Yerdua Poison Spitter Drop]] beverage.<ref name="Summer" /> As a 2-1B surgical droid, it was manufactured by [[Industrial Automaton]] and stood at 1.85 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]], 1in).<ref name="TCW" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Hello, I am BV-RJ, human-cyborg libations.|BV-RJ's play on C-3PO's quote|audio=HelloIAmBVRJ-LegoSummerVacation.flac|<ref name="EasterEggs" />}}
BV-RJ first appeared in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] animated special ''[[LEGO Star Wars Summer Vacation]]''.<ref name="Summer" /> The droid's quote of human-cyborg libations was a play on the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]]'s "human-cyborg relations" quote<ref name="EasterEggs" /> from the [[1977]]<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> canon film ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope]]''.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> BV-RJ's name was also a play on the word "beverage."<ref name="EasterEggs" /> In the special, the bartender droid was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]].<ref name="Summer" />
