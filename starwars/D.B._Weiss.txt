'''Daniel Brett Weiss''' is an television producer, writer, and novelist. Along with [[David Benioff]], he is the co-creator and showrunner of the critically acclaimed [[Wikipedia:HBO|HBO]] television series [[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|''Game of Thrones'']], an adaptation of [[Wikipedia:George R. R. Martin|George R. R. Martin's]] [[Wikipedia:A Song of Ice and Fire|''A Song of Ice and Fire'']] novels. On [[February 6]], [[2018]], it was announced that Weiss and Benioff would be writing and producing a new [[Untitled Star Wars film series|''Star Wars'' film series]], that would be separate from the Skywalker saga and [[Rian Johnson]]'s [[Untitled Star Wars trilogy (Rian Johnson)|''Star Wars'' trilogy]].<ref name="Star Wars series">{{SW|url=news/game-of-thrones-creators-david-benioff-and-d-b-weiss-to-write-and-produce-a-new-series-of-star-wars-films|text=''Game of Thrones'' Creators David Benioff and D.B. Weiss to Write and Produce a New Series of ''Star Wars'' Films}}</ref> On [[October 28]], [[2019]], however, Weiss and Benioff exited the project due scheduling conflicts with [[Netflix]].<ref name="No Films?">{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2019/10/star-wars-setback-game-of-thrones-duo-david-benioff-d-b-weiss-exit-trilogy-1202771184|text='Star Wars' Setback: 'Game Of Thrones' Duo David Benioff & D.B. Weiss Exit Trilogy|author=Geoff Boucher|date=2019-10-28|archivedate=20200721195000}}</ref><ref name="Guardian Oct 2019">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2019/oct/29/game-of-thrones-creators-benioff-and-weiss-drop-star-wars-movies-for-netflix|text=Game of Thrones creators Benioff and Weiss drop Star Wars movies for Netflix|date=2019-10-29|archivedate=20200617052509}}</ref><ref name="Verge 29 Oct 2019">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/29/20937689/benioff-weiss-star-wars-trilogy-cancelled-netflix|text=Game of Thrones showrunners quit Star Wars trilogy to work on Netflix projects|author=Sam Byford|date=2019-10-29|archivedate=20200610151817}}</ref>
