'''Cheryl Potter''' is an editor who cut the [[Destiny (The Acolyte)|third]], [[Teach / Corrupt|sixth]], [[Choice|seventh]], and [[The Acolyte (episode)|eighth]] episodes of the [[2024]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]''. Potter also served as an additional editor for the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' and cut [[2021]] series [[Wikipedia:The Nevers|''The Nevers'']] and the [[2022]] series [[Wikipedia:The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power|''The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power'']], plus numerous other television and movie titles.<ref name="Website">{{WebCite|url=https://www.editchez.com|text=CHERYL POTTER|archivedate=20240803140135}}</ref>
