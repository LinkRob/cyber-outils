The '''centimeter''' was an established unit of measurement of length, equal to one hundredth of a [[meter]].<ref name="Conversion">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]'' establishes that the height of [[R5-D4]] is 97 centimeters, while {{Databank|r5-d4|R5-D4}} gives the height as 0.97 [[meter]]s.</ref>
