'''Climbing spikes''', or '''climbing claws''', were tools that aided the user in ascending a vertical wall by penetrating and gripping its side. They were used by members of the [[Jedi Order]], the [[Nightbrothers]] and the [[Scyre]] and [[Claw (clan)|Claw]] clans.

== Description ==
[[File:ClimbingClaw.jpg|left|thumb|Climbing claws (pictured) were used by the Nighbrothers of Dathomir.]]
Climbing spikes, or climbing claws, sometimes referred to as hand claws when used on the hands, or boot spikes when used on the feet, were tools that aided the user in ascending a vertical wall by penetrating and gripping its side. The spikes could be used singly, as support for climbing ropes, harnesses and other rappelling gear or be worn in single pairs or sets of pairs over gloves and boots on the hands and feet; pushing one set of spikes into the wall allowed a user to remain stable while using another other set to reach for the next point in their journey up or down an obstacle.

The spikes could also be used to generally improve a user's control of movement over unsteady and dangerous terrain; they were thus used regularly by the [[Scyre]] clan to traverse their [[Scyre territory|territory]] on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]] in the form of boot spikes and hand claws tipping their gloves, although they did use a version consisting of two spikes attached by a handle as well for more intensive climbs.<ref name="Phasma" /><ref name="Captain Phasma 3">[[Captain Phasma 3|''Captain Phasma'' 3]]</ref> Sentries of the rival [[Claw (clan)|Claw]] clan also used climbing spikes for stability while guarding the border between their [[Claw territory|territory]] and that of the Scyre..<ref name="Phasma" />

Boot spikes were used by members of the [[Jedi Order]]<ref name="RedBlade" /> while a variant of climbing claws was used by the [[Nightbrothers]] to climb the walls of the planet [[Dathomir]], primarily those covered by [[fire lichen]].<ref name="JFO" /> These claws looked like fingerless [[Glove|gloves]], with sheaths containing small curved blades of metal located  between the fingers; the blades extended when retracting the fingers and retracted when extending the fingers.<ref name="JFO" />

== History ==

=== Jedi usage ===
{{Quote|Watch out for the slugs."<br>"What slugs?|Iskat Akaris and Josk Nivar, moments before the latter fell to his death|<ref name="RedBlade" />}}
[[File:IskatAkarisLightsaber.png|thumb|Iskat Akaris used climbing spikes on Thule.]]
In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="ThuleDate">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that the [[Mission to Thule|mission]] to [[Thule]] takes place concurrent to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi|Obi-Wan]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]]'s [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|mission to Cato Neimoidia]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> during a [[Mission to Thule|mission]] to the the planet [[Thule]], [[Jedi Knight]]s [[Josk Nivar]] and [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] used climbing spikes on their boots to scale one wall of a canyon while Akaris' friend and fellow Knight [[Tualon Yaluna]] climbed the far side. As they were climbing, Akaris noted to herself that the wall had plenty of nooks to serve as footholds and thus thought she didn't really need the spikes on her boots. She also observed a number of [[Unidentified slug species|slugs]] feeding on the rocky wall and attempted to caution Nivar to avoid them lest he pierce one.<ref name="RedBlade" />

However, before he could understand her warning, one of Nivar's boot spikes did indeed impale a [[Slug (creature)|slug]], which broke the spike's grip on the [[Stone|rock]]. When Nivar attempted to get a fingerhold on a hole in the rock, he roused a flock of [[Unidentified venomous bird|birds]] housed therein, which flew out and attacked his face. Nivar instinctively raised his hands to shield himself and thus lost any remaining hold on the wall, falling to his death. Akaris and Yaluna were ultimately able to reach the top and climb back down later, with the former warning the latter to avoid the slugs and nesting holes lest he suffer the same fate as Nivar.<ref name="RedBlade" /> Around [[20 BBY|two years later]],<ref name="Olgothon3Date">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that the [[Mission to Olgothon 3|mission]] to [[Olgothon 3]] took place approximately two [[Standard year|years]] after the [[mission to Thule]], which itself takes place a few [[Standard week|weeks]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle of Geonosis]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> when reunited with Yaluna on another [[Mission to Olgothon 3|mission]], at his request for a more detailed account then she had given in their report, Akaris explained what had happened with the climbing spikes that had led to Nivar's death, adding that Nivar had pushed away from the wall.<ref name="RedBlade"/>

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFODate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during the [[Imperial Era]], [[Jedi]] survivor [[Cal Kestis]] acquired climbing claws from a fallen [[Nightbrother]] while fending off the fearsome [[chirodactyl]] [[Gorgara]] on a [[Mission to Dathomir (Cal Kestis)|mission]] to the planet [[Dathomir]].  He used the claws to climb up certain rock faces best suited for it on various planets, but not before soon having to use them very quickly to evade another assault from the resurging Gorgara, which then seized him and flew up to the sky for a final, fatal confrontation.<ref name="JFO" /> Several [[Standard year|years]] later,<ref name="JBSDate">{{BattleScarsDate}}</ref> while [[Infiltration of a Haxion Brood base|infiltrating]] a [[Haxion Brood base]] located on an [[Unidentified asteroid (Haxion Brood base)|asteroid]], Kestis noted how he always found the feeling of [[gravity]] comforting, even if it could be his death if he missed a [[Force jump|jump]] or his climbing claws failed him. Not long after, as he attempted to escape the base, Kestis cursed himself for not thinking to bring the climbing claws he had "liberated" from the Nightbrother with him.<ref name="JBS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars]]''</ref>

=== Parnassos usage ===
{{Quote|Climbing spikes and rappelling gear. They traveled with long lines of woven rope strung between each person, the only safety net should someone slip and plummet toward the raging waves below. Considering the danger of cuts and abrasions, they wore thick gloves and boots of leather, tipped with claws to help dig into stone. |Vi Moradi, recounting to Cardinal Siv's tale of Phasma and the Scyre|<ref name="Phasma" />}}
By [[22 ABY]],<ref name="EgilDeathDate">[[Egil]]'s [[death]] is dated to [[22 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the Scyre clan was living in a territory consisting of rocky cliffs and caves bordering an [[ocean]] on the planet Parnassos and utilized climbing spikes in their daily lives. The warrior [[Phasma]], for example, wore climbing claw-tipped [[Glove|gloves]] and [[boot]] spikes to navigate the treacherous terrain of her home and to fight enemies of the Scyre, such as the Claw clan. She was wearing these when she forced [[Porr]], who had murdered the clan leader [[Egil]], to surrender and accept Phasma and her brother [[Keldo]] as the new leaders of the Scyre.<ref name="Phasma" />
[[File:Parnassos-CaptainPhasma3.png|left|thumb|Siv, Gosta and Torben, as recalled by Phasma.]]
In [[24 ABY]],<ref name="BalderBrendolDate">''[[Phasma (novel)|Phasma]]'' establishes that [[Phasma]] defeated [[Balder]] the same [[Standard year|year]] that she met [[Brendol Hux]], which is dated to [[24 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Phasma was wearing her spikes when she subdued [[Balder]], the leader of the Claws, with the [[Unidentified poisonous lichen|toxic lichen]] paste she had smeared on her blades. Her brother Keldo then negotiated a peace agreement with the Claws. Later that year, after observing a ship falling from the sky, a group of Scyre folk were led by Phasma on a scouting mission to the crash site. As they made their way across Scyre land, the warrior Gosta, being the smallest and most agile of the group, led the way, testing footholds and planting climbing spikes as she went. [[Torben]], the biggest, went last to secure their rear and provide his weight as an anchor point. As the Scyre had not been outside their territory in years, Phasma was especially particular about making sure her people were secured together in their harnesses.<ref name="Phasma" /> 

Ignoring what Keldo had said about respecting the border before her departure, Phasma opted for the most direct route to the downed ship, which would take her group across Claw territory, the boundary of which was marked by tall stone spires. Upon seeing a distant Claw sentry, Phasma ordered Gosta to take him down; the young warrior unclipped her harness and proceeded to leap from spire to spire towards the sentry. She cut him loose from the spike securing him to a spire, sending him plummeting to his death. When the rest of the Scyre warriors arrived, Phasma ordered one to take the dead man's place to keep up appearances for the Claws; the woman did so, tying her rappelling line to the spike.<ref name="Phasma" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
Climbing spikes first appeared in [[Delilah S. Dawson]]'s novel ''[[Phasma]]'', which was published on [[September 1]], [[2017]]; they were also contextually identified in the text as climbing claws, hand claws and boot spikes.<ref name="Phasma" /> Climbing spikes were first depicted in the [[Captain Phasma 3|third issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Captain Phasma|Captain Phasma]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]], published on [[October 4]], 2017, in a flashback to a scene from the novel.<ref name="Captain Phasma 3"/> A climbing claw variant was depicted in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''<ref name="JFO" /> and was later referenced in [[Sam Maggs]]' novel [[Star Wars Jedi: Battle Scars|''Jedi: Battle Scars'']], which was published on [[March 7]], [[2023]].<ref name="JBS"/> Climbing spikes worn on boots also appeared in Dawson's novel [[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade|''Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade'']], which was published on [[July 18]], 2023.<ref name="RedBlade" />
