'''Drya Thornne's lightsaber''' was an orange-bladed [[lightsaber]] with retractable [[Crossguard lightsaber|crossguards]]. It belonged to [[Drya Thornne]], a lieutenant of the [[Bedlam Raiders]], though it was initially a weapon wielded by an unknown member of the [[Jedi Order]] before they were killed and their weapon taken as a trophy by [[Rayvis]], who entrusted several lightsabers in his personal collection to his most trusted lieutenants.<ref name="Survivor-TacGuide" />

Thornne later used the lightsaber against [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]], only to fall victim to him in a [[Mission to the Shattered Moon|duel]] on the [[Shattered Moon]]. Cal later took the lightsaber to disassemble it and incorporate its crossguard emitter into [[Cal Kestis' second lightsaber|his own lightsaber]].<ref name="Survivor" />
