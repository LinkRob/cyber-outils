'''G3-R44''' was an [[astromech droid]] that served the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. After the Confederacy lost the war<ref name="LRM" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> he and fellow Separatist astromech [[R0-E0]] were confiscated and assigned as [[maintenance droid]]s to the [[Sector Central Utility Records]] archive on the [[planet]] [[Giju]], working in the [[Sector Central Utility Records building]] in the urban area of [[Kristom]]. Neither [[droid]] was properly [[memory wipe]]d and they came to believe they were serving as Separatist [[Spy|spies]] in the facility, unaware that the war was over.<ref name="LRM" />

In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date Part 3">{{LRMDate|3}}</ref> the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]], another former Separatist, encountered G3-R44 at the records building and recruited the astromech to help him locate files on the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]] [[Madel Nureth]]. After G3-R44 provided a [[datapad]] with the information ND-5 sought, the commando droid pretended to recieve orders from the long [[Death|dead]] Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] instructing G3-R44 and R0-E0 to remain undercover on Giju and keep watch for anything suspicious.<ref name="LRM" />
