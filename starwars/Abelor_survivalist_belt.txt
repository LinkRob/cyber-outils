An '''Abelor survivalist belt''' was a type of durable [[belt]] that was made on<ref name="Outlaws" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Abelor]]. The belt was [[Color|brown]] and connected in the front by a silver-colored buckle. On its right side, it had a [[holster]] that could carry a [[blaster pistol]]. Its front had space to store [[blaster module]]s and the back of the belt had an attached pouch as well as space to store [[bacta vial]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an Abelor survivalist belt was for sale in [[Sirei's Stall]], a shop on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]] that was operated by the [[human]] [[tailor]] [[Berek Keehro]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the human [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] purchased an Abelor survivalist belt from Keehro for 700 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
An Abelor survivalist belt appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The belt allows the player to carry an additional [[Bacta]] Vial when equipped.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Iuliia Misiul]] created concept art of the Abelor survivalist belt and other outfits used by Vess.<ref name="Art Station">{{ArtStation|username=Iuliia Misiul|url=/artwork/4N9Ve4|name=[[Iuliia Misiul]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws &mdash; Kay's Gearsets}}</ref>
