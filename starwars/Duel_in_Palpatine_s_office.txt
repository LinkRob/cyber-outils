{{Quote|In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Republic, you're under arrest, Chancellor.|Mace Windu, to Sheev Palpatine|<ref name="ROTS" />}}
With the [[Clone Wars]] reaching its end and the revelation that [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] was secretly [[Darth]] Sidious, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]], [[Mace Windu]] and fellow members of the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] engaged Sidious in a '''lightsaber duel''' in the Chancellor's office on [[Coruscant]]. Sidious quickly murdered [[Jedi Master]]s [[Agen Kolar]], and [[Saesee Tiin]] almost immediately, while [[Kit Fisto]] was murdered only a few seconds after, but Sidious had an extended confrontation with Windu. The [[lightsaber duel]] lasted until Windu disarmed the Sith Lord and knocked him to the floor.

As Windu held Sidious at bladepoint ready to kill him and end his madness once and for all, he was interrupted by the arrival of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]; although Windu had told Skywalker to remain at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]], he was seduced to the Chancellor's office by the promise that Palpatine could use the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side of]] [[the Force]] to save his wife, [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], from dying in childbirth, an event Skywalker saw in a vision. Ignoring Skywalker, Windu declared Palpatine and the [[Sith]] as a whole defeated, to which Sidious responded by firing [[Force lightning]] at the [[Jedi]] Master. Windu managed to block the streams of electricity and deflected most it back into Sidious's body, disfiguring the [[Sith Lord]] in the process.<ref name="ROTS" />

Skywalker attempted to convince Windu to arrest the Sith [[Lord]], as he at first intended, but Windu had realized that Palpatine controlled the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] and the judicial system, and would most likely be acquitted no matter what. Stating that Palpatine was just too dangerous to be left alive, Windu prepared to kill him, while Skywalker desperately claimed that doing so was not the Jedi way and finally blurting out "''I need him!''" When Windu tried to make the killing blow, Skywalker lashed out at him, severing his [[lightsaber]]-holding hand and allowing Sidious, who was feigning helplessness, to kill the Jedi Master.

In the aftermath of the duel, Skywalker pledged his loyalty to Sidious and became a Sith Lord called Darth Vader. The Jedi attempt on his life allowed Sidious to execute his plan to seize full control over [[the galaxy]]. He branded the Jedi as traitors and issued [[Order 66]] to the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], leading to the destruction of the [[Jedi Order]]. Shortly thereafter, he [[Proclamation of the New Order|declared himself]] [[Galactic Emperor]] and transformed the [[Galactic Republic]] into the [[Galactic Empire]], which ruled the galaxy for over two decades.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|I've just learned a terrible truth. I think Chancellor Palpatine is a Sith Lord.''"<br />"''A Sith Lord?''"<br />"''Yes, the one we've been looking for.''"<br />"''How do you know this?''"<br />"''He knows the ways of the Force. [[File:Palpatine_and_Anakin.png|thumb|left|250px|Anakin Skywalker had long considered Sheev Palpatine his mentor and friend, unaware that the Supreme Chancellor was the Dark Lord of the Sith.]]
Not long after Sidious betrayed and orchestrated the death of [[Dooku|Count Dooku]] in the [[Battle of Coruscant]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] began experiencing [[Force vision|visions]] of his [[Marriage|wife]], [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], [[Death|dying]] in [[Reproduction|childbirth]]. Disturbed, Skywalker sought the advice of [[Grand Master|Master]] [[Yoda]]; however, Yoda advised him to "let go of everything he feared to lose" which simply served to frustrate Skywalker, as he did not understand what Yoda was trying to say. [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] invited Skywalker to a show at the [[Galaxies Opera House]] under the pretense of providing him with [[Clone Intelligence|intelligence]] on the [[Utapau|location]] of [[General]] [[Grievous]]. While they sat in Palpatine's private box, he told Skywalker of a [[Sith]] "legend," "The [[Tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise|Tragedy]] of [[Darth Plagueis]] the Wise," emphasizing that Plagueis had discovered way to use the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] to influence [[midi-chlorian]]s, an ability which could be used to both create life and cheat death. Aware of Anakin's plight, Sidious planned to use this revelation as bait to draw Anakin to the dark side. Anakin, desperate for a way to prevent his prophetic visions from coming to pass, questioned Palpatine on how one might learn Plagueis's technique, but Palpatine simply told him that it was impossible to learn it from a [[Jedi]]. Later, in the [[Supreme Chancellor]]'s [[Emperor's office|office]], after Skywalker told the Supreme Chancellor about [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] currently [[Battle of Utapau|fighting]] Grievous, Palpatine finally revealed himself to be [[Darth]] Sidious, a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. Skywalker was torn between his desire to learn Sidious's power and his duty as a Jedi to turn him in.<ref name="ROTS" />

[[File:DuelonCoruscant.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Jedi Masters Mace Windu, Kit Fisto, Saesee Tiin, and Agen Kolar confronted Chancellor Palpatine upon learning of his secret identity as a Sith Lord.]]
Skywalker initially thought to kill Palpatine on sight, as another part of his duty to the Jedi was to eliminate the Sith, but as Palpatine was his friend, and had implied killing him would turn Skywalker to the dark side, he did not. After leaving the Chancellor's office, he went to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] and informed [[Mace Windu]] of Palpatine's true identity as a [[Sith Lord]]. Aware that the Jedi Order could be destroyed,<ref name="Mace Windu DB">{{Databank|mace-windu|Mace Windu}}</ref> Windu sought to prevent this outcome by leading a team of fellow [[Jedi Master]]s, [[Kit Fisto]], [[Saesee Tiin]], and [[Agen Kolar]],<ref name="ROTS" /> who were among the finest warriors in the Order,<ref name="HC 15" /> to arrest Palpatine, leaving Skywalker behind.<ref name="ROTS" /> The quartet was fully aware of the enormity of the burden ahead of them.<ref name="HC 15">{{HelmetCollectionCite|15|Highlights of the Saga}}</ref> The Jedi strode into Palpatine's private office, where the Chancellor feigned surprise at their abrupt entry and asked if General Grievous had been killed. As the four Jedi ignited their lightsabers, Windu declared Palpatine under arrest, saying that the [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] would decide his fate.<ref name="ROTS" />

==The duel==
===Engagement===
{{Quote|Master Windu.''"<br />"''In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Republic, you're under arrest, Chancellor.''"<br />"''Are you threatening me, Master Jedi?''"<br />"''The Senate will decide your fate.''"<br />"''I am the Senate!''"<br />"''Not yet.''"<br />"''It's treason then.|Darth Sidious and Windu|<ref name="ROTS" />}}
[[File:ItsTreasonThen-ROTS.png|thumb|left|250px|The Sith Lord who masterminded the Clone Wars, Darth Sidious, accused the Jedi Masters of treason.]]
Accusing the Jedi of [[treason]], Sidious deployed [[Darth Sidious' lightsabers|his lightsaber]] from his sleeve and ignited it. With a ferocious snarl, Sidious leapt over his desk and landed in front of the Jedi. Kolar and Tiin were cut down in seconds; Fisto managed to fend off Sidious's initial strikes, but he too was killed. With his entire strike force destroyed, Windu was left alone to contend with the Dark Lord.<ref name="ROTS" />

Sidious drove Windu back along the corridor to his public office, where Windu managed to pull Sidious into a bladelock. As their battle took them towards the center of the room, Anakin's [[airspeeder]] touched down outside. He had [[Meditation|meditated]] on his thoughts in the [[Jedi Council Chamber|Council Chambers]], and the young Jedi felt as though he could not bear to let Sidious meet his death at the hands of the Jedi; at least, not before teaching Skywalker the secrets of Darth Plagueis to save Amidala from death.<ref name="ROTS" />

[[File:Vaapad.png|thumb|right|250px|With his fellow masters slain by Sidious, Windu alone fought to save the Jedi Order from destruction.]]
As Skywalker ran through the halls of the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]], Sidious unleashed a rapid series of fruitless attacks against Windu, and was driven back into the office by Windu's steady offensive strikes. Windu guided the battle across the office towards the bay window, shattering the transparisteel with missed strikes. As they moved out onto the wind-blown ledge, the duelists engaged in a brief flurry of bladework, but Windu managed to end the bout with a swift and precise kick to the Sith Lord's jaw. Sidious was thrown off balance, staggering back and dropping his [[lightsaber]] which flew out the window, flailed for balance before retreating against the window frame. Sidious was at Windu's mercy, who held his [[Mace Windu's lightsaber|blade]] to the Sith Lord's throat.<ref name="ROTS" />

At that moment, Skywalker appeared, convinced that only the power of the dark side could save his wife's from the end seen in his visions.<ref name="ROTS" />

===Intervention===
{{Dialogue|Mace Windu|I am going to end this, once and for all!|Anakin Skywalker|You can't. He must stand trial.|Mace Windu|He has control of the senate and the courts! He's too dangerous to be left alive.|Darth Sidious|I'm too weak. Oh. Don't kill me. Please.|Anakin Skywalker|It's not the Jedi way. He must live!|Darth Sidious|Please don't.|Anakin Skywalker|I need him!|Darth Sidious|Please don't!|Anakin Skywalker|NO!|attr=Mace Windu, Anakin Skywalker, and Darth Sidious|src=<ref name="ROTS" />}}
With Sidious disarmed, Windu declared again that the Dark Lord was in Jedi [[Prisoner|custody]]. However, having arrived too late to see the Sith Lord's intent, Skywalker saw only a helpless Chancellor cowering before Windu. Sidious claimed the Jedi were in rebellion, while Windu countered, declaring the ultimate defeat of the Sith.<ref name="ROTS" />

[[File:Superconductingloop.png|thumb|left|250px|Windu and Sidious continued to fight, forcing Skywalker to choose between the Jedi and the Sith.]]
In response, Sidious hurled [[Force lightning]] at the Jedi Master, only for him to deflect it back at the Dark Lord. The redirected current hit Sidious, deforming his face, yet he continued to uphold the barrage. Eventually, the lightning subsided, and Sidious lay on the floor, feigning weakness and begging for mercy.<ref name="ROTS" />

[[File:MaceWinduDeath-ROTS.png|thumb|right|250px|Driven by the fear of losing Padmé Amidala, Skywalker chose Sidious, leading to Windu's death.]]
Windu announced that he had no choice but to kill Sidious, as he saw him as too great a threat to keep alive. Skywalker implored Windu to spare Sidious, reasoning that the best course of action was for Sidious to stand trial, but Windu refused, believing that Sidious's influence would sway the [[Supreme Court]]'s ruling in his favor. Windu raised his blade to deliver the killing blow. Skywalker unexpectedly intervened, [[Dismemberment|severing]] the master's [[sword]] hand. Windu could only howl in pain as his lightsaber plummeted out of the window, leaving him defenseless. Seizing the opportunity, Sidious ceased his weakened ruse and unleashed a barrage of Force lightning at Windu before using the Force [[Telekinesis|to blast]] the Jedi Master out of the broken window to his death.<ref name="ROTS" />

Skywalker fell to his knees, horrified at his contribution to Windu's defeat. Seizing upon Skywalker's emotional turmoil, Sidious congratulated him for taking steps to fulfill his destiny and invited the Jedi to become his [[Sith apprentice]]. Skywalker, blinded by his desire to save his wife's life, begged Sidious to help him. The Sith Lord claimed that together, they could discover the secret to controlling death and saving Amidala's life. Skywalker knelt before Sidious and swore his allegiance to the Sith, thereby betraying the Jedi Order.<ref name="ROTS" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|The attempt on my life has left me scarred and deformed. But, I assure you, my resolve has never been stronger.|Sheev Palpatine|<ref name="ROTS" />}}
Sidious named his new apprentice Darth Vader, replacing Tyranus, and tasked Vader with the [[Operation: Knightfall|storming]] of the [[Jedi temple]]. Furthermore, Sidious issued [[Order 66]] to every [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]; the clones were ordered to eliminate their [[Jedi Commander]]s and [[Jedi General|Generals]], as the Jedi was marked as enemies of the Republic. The Clones hidden [[Behavioral modification biochip|biochips]] that Tyranus had implanted in their [[brain]]s, in order for Sidious to force them to obey him at the right moment, then activated and brainwashed them into believing that the Jedi were traitors to the Republic that needed to be executed, thus beginning the [[Great Jedi Purge]].<ref name="ROTS" /> Skywalker's decision to betray Windu and his subsequent anguish were felt by his former Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] aboard the ''[[Tribunal]]'', but she was soon [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|distracted]] by the execution of Order 66.<ref name="Shattered" />

[[File:Declaration2.png|thumb|left|250px|After accusing the Jedi Order of treason, Sidious declared himself Emperor of the First Galactic Empire.]]
Vader and the [[501st Legion]] [[Operation: Knightfall|stormed the Temple]] and proceeded to slaughter nearly every Jedi within its walls, murdering both esteemed Masters and defenseless younglings. The Temple was set ablaze while all of [[Imperial City|Galactic City]] watched. Shortly thereafter, Sidious traveled to the [[Galactic Senate Chamber|Senate Rotunda]] and told the Senate of the "Jedi Rebellion" and his actions against it. Furthermore, under the false pretense of better security, Sidious delivered a [[Proclamation of the New Order|speech]] that declared that the Republic was no more, and in its place was a new [[Galactic Empire]], receiving thunderous applause as [[Mission to Mustafar|Vader killed Viceroy Gunray]].<ref name="ROTS" />

After becoming [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of [[the galaxy]], Sidious essentially disappeared from public life and left most of the Imperial day-to-day affairs to the [[Imperial Ruling Council]]. The citizenry of the Empire, as such, began to circulate rumors on his absence that the Jedi assassination attempt not only resulted in Sidious's disfigurement but also in the death of the sanguine politician that he had once been, having shredded his benevolent persona. The matter of how precisely Windu and his team were killed as well how Sidious's face was deformed, however, were never settled to everyone's satisfaction, leading [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to correctly guess while not confirm that Sidious and Vader were Sith Lords.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

[[File:Vader-ROTJHD.png|thumb|right|250px|Luke Skywalker was saved by his father, Darth Vader, who sacrificed his life by destroying the Emperor.]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Vader's son [[Luke Skywalker]] defeated him in the [[Battle of Endor]], but refused to obey the Emperor's order to kill him. The furious Emperor then proceeded to torture Luke for his defiance, leaving Vader confronted with the same choice he'd been faced with in regards to Sidious and Windu. Vader's love for Luke and fear of losing him caused him to do the opposite this time, as he saved his son and overthrew his master. The ensuing struggle between master and apprentice ended with Sidious' (temporary) destruction but Vader was critically damaged in the process. Having destroyed the Sith and sacrificed his life for his son, the redeemed Anakin Skywalker fulfilled his role as the [[Chosen One|Chosen one]] and made peace with Luke before passing into the Force.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

In the [[New Republic Era|years following]] Anakin Skywalker's redemption and death, Luke embarked on a search for Jedi knowledge and learned about Windu's life, including his failed attempt to arrest Palpatine. Holding Windu in high esteem, Skywalker concluded that had it not been for his father's intervention, Windu would have likely succeeded in defeating Palpatine.<ref name="Secrets Jedi">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Milestones===
{{Quote|This is where he makes the pact with the Devil. This is the very Faustian pact of trying to do something that he shouldn't be doing.|George Lucas in the ''Revenge of the Sith'' commentary|<ref name="ROTSc">{{FilmVideo|3}}</ref>}}
[[File:AlternatePalpyDuel-RotSDeletedScene.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The earlier version of the scene had Skywalker present from the start.]]
The Duel in Palpatine's office appears in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], the third installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]]. This duel explained some&mdash;until then&mdash;unknown aspects of the ''Star Wars'' universe. It explained how Darth Vader,<ref name="ROTS" /> an antagonist from the [[original trilogy]],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref><ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref><ref name="Episode VI" /> joined the Sith.<ref name="ROTS" /> In the DVD commentary, Director [[George Lucas]] described Skywalker's decision as a "very Faustian pact" made "with the Devil."<ref name="ROTSc" />

At the close of the duel, Darth Sidious's face is seemingly deformed from the lightning being redirected into his face, with Sidious himself claiming his appearance changed because of the Jedi attack later in the film during his proclamation of the Empire.<ref name="ROTS" /> However, in [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], Luke Skywalker is the victim of Sidious's Force lighting, yet his appearance remains the same.<ref name="Episode VI" /> [[John Knoll]] stated in the film's commentary that it was "the strain of all this exertion" that changed Sidious's appearance.<ref name="ROTSc" /> Furthermore, when asked about the alleged discrepancy, [[Matt Martin]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] reflected that, in his personal opinion, there had been more going on with Sidious than what the Sith Lord claimed, so perhaps it was not the lightning alone that changed Sidious; in Martin's mind, it could have been as simple as Sidious using a higher voltage of Force lightning during his duel in ''Revenge of the Sith'' or that,<ref name="Matt's Explanation">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1034554469596319746|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=This is not an official answer but I just always assumed there was more going on with Palps and it wasn't just a result of the lightning itself. Maybe he overexerted himself and that caused it? Maybe he had the voltage turned up higher? IDK, but I never saw it as an inconsistency|archiveurl=https://archive.is/790hW}}</ref> much like Knoll suggested,<ref name="ROTSc" /> Sidious had overexerted himself during the confrontation.<ref name="Matt's Explanation" /> Author [[Gary M. Sarli]] theorized that Sidious had already looked "deformed" and that he had been masking his true appearance until that moment.<ref name="Jedi Counseling 71">{{Wizards|url=default.asp?x=starwars/article/sw20050804jc71|series=Jedi Counseling|text=71}}</ref>

===The Boba Fett interlude===
Mace Windu's actor, [[Samuel L. Jackson]], had been waiting for this scene since he was hired. One of the requests he made during negotiations for the part was for his character to die spectacularly at the end and not die "like some punk." For a time, director [[George Lucas]] considered to include [[Boba Fett]] in the story and have him participate in the [[Great Jedi Purge]] by having him kill Windu in revenge of him killing his father [[Jango Fett|Jango]] during the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], but Lucas later felt Fett was "too young" and unnecessary to Anakin's fall.<ref name="TMoSWRotS">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref>

Elaborating on this, [[Daniel Logan]] was slated to reprise his role as Fett and was put on hold for a year by Lucasfilm to shoot his part, but he was ultimately let go after Jackson went to Lucas' office and told him that "a punk kid" shouldn't kill someone as powerful as Windu, leading Lucas to change plans and Windu's killer.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.thepopverse.com/movies-star-wars-mace-windu-death-boba-fett-daniel-logan|text=Star Wars creator George Lucas originally planned for Boba Fett to kill Mace Windu (and here's how & why Samuel L Jackson fought it)|author=Joshua Lapin-Berton|date=2025-02-24|work=[https://www.thepopverse.com Popverse]|archivedate=20250219161344}}</ref> While promoting the movie on [[Wikipedia:Late Night with Conan O'Brien|''Late Night with Conan O'Brien'']], Jackson revealed his request to [[Conan O'Brien]], who joked that perhaps Mace Windu would meet his death by slipping on some soap in the shower. During shooting, Jackson and the crew joked about having Windu surviving the duel and returning for a [[Wikipedia:Post-credits scene|post-credits scene]].<ref name="TMoSWRotS" />

===Filming and production changes===
[[Ian McDiarmid]], the actor who portrayed Darth Sidious, did not enjoy filming these scenes. As a Shakespearean actor, filming high-speed sword fighting did not appeal to him. He had only about five days to learn the choreography his stunt double would not perform from the stunt coordinator, [[Nick Gillard]]. As an older man, he wasn't as dexterous as his opponents and, occasionally, he would be hit over the day's filming. McDiarmid did, however, have some respect for [[Samuel L. Jackson]]'s abilities. Producer [[Rick McCallum]] was worried about how the scene would turn out because of this, but was very happy with the final result.<ref name="TMoSWRotS" />

[[File:Original_fight.jpg|thumb|right|250px|George Lucas watching the earlier version of the scene]]
The duel went through several changes during production. One of the earlier versions of the scene had Anakin Skywalker present in Palpatine's office from the beginning, witnessing the fight between Sidious and the Jedi until entering to succor Sidious from Windu's will. This version of the scene was filmed on [[July 15]], [[2003]] at Fox Studios in [[Wikipedia:Sydney|Sydney]], [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]] on Stage 7. However, the shoot was difficult as the gloves worn by the Jedi Knights made it difficult to grab the lightsaber hilts, leading to the scene's reshoot on [[August 27]], [[2004]].<ref name="Kit's Bts">{{SW|url=news/from-concept-to-screen-kit-fisto|text=Kit Fisto: From Concept to Screen|nolive=1}}</ref> Another version of the duel had Windu coming and fighting alone with Sidious, as both Saesee Tiin and Kit Fisto were meant to be killed on [[Mygeeto]] and [[Saleucami]] during Order 66. Rather than being thrown out the window, Windu just fell the ground dead after receiving Sidious's lightning in this version.<ref name="TMoSWRotS" />

According to the screenplay of Episode III, the Chancellor's [[Rodian]] [[aide]] [[Dar Wac]] and two members of the [[Senate Guard]] originally had a cameo appearance before the Jedi entered Palpatine's office, in which they would have tried to stop the Jedi only to be [[Force push]]ed into the walls by them. The screenplay also indicates Windu attempted to defend himself with his remaining hand after he was betrayed by Skywalker.<ref name="Illustrated Screenplay">[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith Illustrated Screenplay'']]</ref>
