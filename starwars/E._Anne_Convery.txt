'''Elizabeth Anne Convery (abbr: E. Anne Convery)''' or '''Anne Convery''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] author and the wife of [[Dave Filoni]]. Convery wrote the short story "[[Bug (short story)|Bug]]," the eleventh and final story in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]''. She is also the namesake of the [[convor]].

==Biography==
===Early life and Influence on ''Star Wars''===
{{Quote|Right, [the owls] actually called Convorees, named after Anne [Convery], but that was back on Clone Wars when I named them that.|Dave Filoni|<ref name="Filoni IGN">{{WebCite|url=https://uk.ign.com/articles/2016/03/31/star-wars-rebels-dave-filoni-on-ahsokas-fate-mauls-return-and-much-more?page=1|text=''Star Wars Rebels'': Dave Filoni on Ahsoka's Fate, Maul's Return and Much More|author=Goldman, Eric|date=2016-03-30|work=[[Wikipedia:IGN|IGN]]|archivedate=20200103221540}}</ref>}}
[[File:Convoree-SWCT.png|thumb|right|The convorees, birds native to the moon of Wasskah, are named after Anne Convery.]]
Elizabeth Anne Convery was born on [[September 18]],<ref name="Birth Day" /> [[1974]]<ref name="Birth Year" /> in [[Wikipedia:San Fernando Valley|San Fernando Valley]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]]. As a child, she owned the [[Star Wars home video releases|home video release]] of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. During her teenage years she watched a rerelease in theaters with her mother, with both instances indulging her into the world of ''[[Star Wars]]''.<ref name="Skytalkers" />

Years afterwards, Convery would get married to [[Dave Filoni]],<ref name="Filoni IGN" /> the current Executive Vice President / Chief Creative Officer at [[Lucasfilm]].<ref name="Position">{{Lucasfilm|url=leadership/dave-filoni|text=Dave Filoni}}</ref> When Filoni had been working on the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episodes "[[Padawan Lost]]" and "[[Wookiee Hunt]]," he decided to the name the [[Color|yellow]]-feathered [[bird]]s in which appeared in the episodes, [[convor]]s, based on the surname of his wife.<ref name="Filoni IGN" /> The birds would be officially given the name in the [[StarWars.com]] episode guide for "Padawan Lost."<ref name="Padawan Lost Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/padawan-lost-episode-guide|text="Padawan Lost" Episode Guide &ndash; ''The Clone Wars''}}</ref> During development on [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|season four]] episode "[[A Friend in Need]]," Filoni decided to add a character named [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] to the script for the episode. The name "Bo-Katan" was created by Filoni as a nod to the name of Convery's cat. Filoni derived "Bo-Katan" from "boogie-cat-Anne," as Convery calls the cat "boogie."<ref name="AFIN Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/episode-guide-a-friend-in-need|text="A Friend in Need" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

===Bug and The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark===
{{Quote|I love it because I think it's a story that manages, while still being a satisfying adventure, to not glorify war. It does this mainly by following through on the arcs of wonderful, terrifying, funny, fallible, and diverse characters. From the personal to the political, The Clone Wars redefines the ways, big and small, that we can be heroes.|E. Anne Convery|<ref name="Light and Dark">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-stories-of-light-and-dark-authors-reveal-their-chapters|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars &ndash; Stories of Light and Dark'' Authors Reveal Their Stories}}</ref>}}
[[File:StoriesOfLightAndDark.jpg|thumb|left|The cover of ''The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark'', featuring Convery's name]]
On [[April 16]], [[2020]], StarWars.com announced Convery's involvement in ''[[The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark]]'',<ref name="GetAFirstLook">{{SW|url=news/get-a-first-look-at-star-wars-the-clone-wars-stories-of-light-and-dark|text=Get a First Look at ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars &ndash; Stories of Light and Dark''}}</ref> an anthology book consisting of eleven short stories based on the era of the [[Clone Wars]]. Convery wrote the eleventh and final story, "[[Bug (short story)|Bug]]," the only original story with the rest being retellings of popular ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' stories.<ref name="Light and Dark" /> The story focuses on a [[Youngling|young]] [[Sídi]] [[Gender|girl]] known only as [[Bug (Sídi)|Bug]] and a [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]] of no [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] named [[Falta]] in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Dathomir]], a genocide of the [[Nightsisters]] as carried out by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Bug">{{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> Convery compares her short story to "[[Wikipedia:Cinderella|Cinderella]],"<ref name="TatooineTimes">{{WebCite|url=https://tatooinetimes.com/star-wars-interviews/bug-interview-with-e-anne-convery|text=Bug: Interview with E. Anne Convery|author=Fiori, Fabio|date=2020-10-01|work=[https://tatooinetimes.com tatooinetimes.com]|archivedate=20220605232621}}</ref> a [[Wikipedia:Folklore|folk tale]] of [[Wikipedia:Europe|European]] origin.<ref name="CinderellaVox">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vox.com/2015/3/15/8214405/cinderella-fairy-tale-history|text=Disney didn't invent Cinderella. Her story is at least 2,000 years old.|author=McKinney, Kelsey|date=2015-03-15|work=[[Wikipedia:Vox (website)|Vox]]|archivedate=20220526180740}}</ref> Just like Cinderella, Bug lives with her two mean parents [[Rank]] and [[Leera]] and becomes friends with [[Ash-beetle|creatures]] as a coping mechanism. Convery stated that Bug inspired her because she was making the best of her awful situation.<ref name="TatooineTimes" /> The book was released on [[August 25]], 2020.<ref name="BN-Hardcover">{{BN|url=w/star-wars-the-clone-wars-lou-anders/1136625308|text=Star Wars The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}} {{C|Hardcover}}</ref>
