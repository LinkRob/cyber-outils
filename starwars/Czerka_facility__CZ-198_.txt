A '''facility''' of the [[Czerka Arms/Legends|Czerka Corporation]] was located on the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] [[CZ-198]] during the time of the [[Galactic War]].<ref name="Daily" /> In [[3638 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TOR_updates|2.3}}</ref> the facility was [[Seizure of CZ-198|seized]] by both the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the [[Sith Empire (Post–Great Hyperspace War)|reconstituted Sith Empire]] due to Czerka's scandalous and corrupt practices by [[Special Executive]] [[Rasmus Blys]].<ref name="Daily" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Czerka facility appeared in the [[August]] [[2013]] Game Update 2.3 of ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Rise of the Hutt Cartel]]'', the first expansion to the [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' developed by [[BioWare]].<ref name="Daily" />
