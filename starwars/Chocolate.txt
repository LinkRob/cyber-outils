'''Chocolate''' was a sweet [[Foodstuff|food]]. [[Clone trooper]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] sometimes [[Diet|ate]] chocolate.<ref name="Battle Tales 4" /> Chocolate was rare in the parts of [[the galaxy]] where [[Anukara]] was located. Whilst [[Mission to Anukara|posing]] as a school student on a tour of a [[Unidentified munitions factory|munitions factory]] on Anukara, [[Seyn Marana]] was offered some chocolate by [[Anice]], who said she received some for her birthday.<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> [[Chocolate Popcorn with Crait Red Salt]] was a form of popped [[Surabat grain]] mixed with chocolate served at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]][[Batuu]].<ref name="KSK">{{GEAttr|Kat Saka's Kettle}}</ref>
