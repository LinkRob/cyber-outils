'''Blutopia''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Vensensor sector]]'s [[Blutopia system]], in the [[Expansion Region]] and the [[Trailing Sectors]]. The [[water]]y world was [[Homeworld|home]] to the [[Blutopian]] [[species]]. [[Blutopia pearl]]s were a dish native to the planet and were considered by the Blutopian [[Oga Garra]] one of her favorites.

In [[10 BBY]], the [[scoundrel]] [[Jaylen Vrax]] visited the [[Renner Family Winery]] to meet with its owner, [[Bischt Renner]]. Following a scuffle, Vrax's [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]] [[Death|killed]] Renner, prompting the scoundrel and his partner to depart Blutopia shortly afterward.

==Description==
{{Quote|This was an especially good crop. Blutopia doesn't have a lot of exportable resources, but nermaline fruit makes excellent wine.|Bischt Renner, to Jaylen Vrax|<ref name="LRM" />}}
[[File:Rekardia-AcolyteVG.png|thumb|right|250px|Blutopia's location in the Expansion Region]]
Blutopia was a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> located in the [[Blutopia system]], within the [[Vensensor sector]]<ref name="SWSystems" /> of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="Trailing Sectors" /> The world was situated in grid square K-16 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> and connected to the [[Rekardia system|Rekardia]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> and [[Ivarujar system]]s via the [[Corellian Trade Spine]] [[hyperspace route]].<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> The world had a breathable, Type I [[atmosphere]],<ref name="Atmosphere" /> a [[Temperature|temperate]] climate, and a [[Blutopia's sun|sun]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

The planet had primarily [[water]]y terrain broken up by islands, and its flora included [[tree]]s and [[plant]]s bearing [[nermaline fruit]]. Most of Blutopia's population resided in its [[Ocean|seas]], although some establishments were present on its islands.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> The world was home to a type of multi-[[eye]]d [[Unidentified Blutopia creature|creature]] with [[Limb|tentacles]].<ref name="EOTE" /> Blutopia had few exportable resources.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==History==
{{Quote|I bring credits&hellip;and gifts. Like this taste from home, from Blutopia, as a gesture of respect.|Rey, to Oga Garra|<ref name="EOTE" />}}
[[File:JaylenVrax-OutlawsVCG.png|thumb|left|225px|Jaylen Vrax (pictured) visited the Renner Family Winery on Blutopia to meet with Bischt Renner.]]
In the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Renner Family Winery]] operated on an [[Unidentified island (Blutopia)|island]] on Blutopia, making [[wine]] out of nermaline fruit. During that time, the establishment became a frequent vacation spot for many criminal syndicates. Upon learning that information<ref name="LRM" /> in [[10 BBY]],<ref name="LRMDate">{{LRMDate|2}}</ref> the [[scoundrel]] [[Jaylen Vrax]] visited the winery to meet with [[Bischt Renner]], the winery's owner, who was also secretly operating as an undercover [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]] for the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following the pair's introductory conversation, Renner left to acquire refreshments for the pair. In that time, Vrax [[Thief|stole]] Renner's [[datapad]] from his safe.<ref name="LRM" />

When confronted about the theft, the resulting skirmish resulted in Renner's [[death]] at the hands of Vrax's partner, the [[BX-series droid commando]] [[ND-5]]. Vrax then discovered Renner's secret occupation as an ISB agent, ordering his droid to help dispose of the winery owner's body. The scoundrel departed Blutopia with ND-5, intending to impersonate Renner as an agent to gather information on the celestial body [[Andara]] at a gathering the winery owner had initially intended to attend.<ref name="LRM" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Blutopia was part of the [[New Republic]]'s territories.<ref name="MandoVG" />

[[File:BlutopiaCreature-GalaxysEdgeEOTE1.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Rey brought Oga Garra a creature from Blutopia as a gift.]]
[[Standard year|Years]] later, in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{TravelersGuidetoBatuuDate}}</ref> the [[historian]] [[Eloc Throno]] included a [[map]] with the location of Blutopia in his [[book]] ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', a book that detailed the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet of [[Batuu]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> The same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="EOTEDate">The events of ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge – Echoes of the Empire]]'' take place while the [[Resistance]] is on [[Batuu]], which corresponds to [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] brought a creature from Blutopia to Batuu as a gift for the [[Blutopian]] [[Oga Garra]], hoping for the latter's assistance in acquiring a Jedi [[weapon]]. Garra quickly consumed the creature, expressing appreciation.<ref name="EOTE" />

[[Blutopia pearl]]s were a dish native to the planet and were considered by Garra one of her favorites. During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> between 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited Blutopia. In Tuggs' third cookbook, ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 ABY,<ref name="UltimateDate" /> he mentioned Blutopia when documenting his recipe for the Blutopia pearls [[Foodstuff|dish]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Most of the population is sea based. We get the islands to ourselves.|Bischt Renner about Blutopia, to Jaylen Vrax|<ref name="LRM" />}}
Blutopia was the native world of the Blutopian species.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> While most of Blutopia's population was sea-based, some residents&mdash;such as the [[human]] [[Renner family]]&mdash;resided on the world's islands.<ref name="LRM" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Blutopia I believe is mostly a water planet. So I kind of made this headcanon that the Renner Family Winery there is&mdash;well, so most of the life on Blutopia I believe is underwater. But the idea is that several mammals have kind of exploited and colonized the, like, the few islands on there, and that's why it makes for a perfect getaway for this winery/resort.|Mike Chen|<ref name="WookInterview">{{WookYouTube|2CL4jk9K2aE|Wookieepedia Interviews - Mike Chen (Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon)}}</ref>}}
[[File:OutlawsLowRedMoon-Cover.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Blutopia first appeared in ''Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon''.]]
Blutopia was first mentioned in the [[2020]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Cole Horton]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> The world made its first appearance in the [[2026]] novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'', written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="LRM" />

Chen picked the planet for use in the story due to its proximity to the Corellian Trade Spine and location in the Mid Rim, wanting to imply that Imperial Security Bureau agents like Bischt Renner covered a radius. The author mused that since most of Blutopia's inhabitants lived underwater, several other species exploited and colonized the world's few islands, making it an ideal getaway for a resort-style establishment like the Renner Family Winery. Chen surmised that the Renner family likely lived on Blutopia for several generations, and Bischt Renner was recruited to work for the Empire because of his winery's ability to serve as a discreet meeting place for syndicate heads. The author noted that Bischt likely received ample compensation for making data dumps around every six months and did not have to deal with the Empire disrupting the winery's operations as a result of his cooperation.<ref name="WookInterview" />
