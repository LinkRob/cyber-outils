'''Cohmac's lightsaber''' was a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] used by [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]]. The weapon's hilt was equipped with a golden emitter and a gold-accented end cap.<ref name="THR Show #3" /> Before departing the [[Jedi Order]], Vitus used the saber to [[Knighting Ceremony|knight]] his [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] and then gave him the weapon.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
