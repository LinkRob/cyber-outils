'''Almec's office'''<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-academy-concept-art-gallery|text="The Academy" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> was an office that was used by [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]] in [[Sundari]] on the [[planet]] [[Mandalore]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Corruption" /> It was nearly identical to [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]]'s throne room, but with darker lighting. The room was decorated with [[glass]] busts of previous [[Mandalorian]] prime ministers.<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/the-academy-trivia-gallery|text="The Academy" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref>
