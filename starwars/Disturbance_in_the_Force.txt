A '''disturbance in the Force''', also known as a '''disruption in the Force''', a '''Force Disturbance''', or a '''tremor in the Force''', was an anomaly in the currents of [[the Force]], the powerful energy field which bound all beings, that could be sensed by [[Force-sensitive]]s, such as [[Jedi]] and [[Sith]].

==Description==
{{Quote|It's like a flood of black water&hellip; &hellip;Rushing in&hellip; Clouding my senses&hellip; &hellip; I think&hellip; It's a ''disturbance in the Force<nowiki/>|Luke Skywalker, after feeling a disturbance in the Force|<ref name="Star Wars Annual (2015) 4">[[Star Wars Annual (2015) 4|''Star Wars Annual'' (2015) 4]]</ref>}}
[[The Force]] was a powerful energy field which bound all being and could be sensed by [[Force-sensitive]]s.<ref name="Episode IV" /> Feeling a disturbance in the Force let the one who felt it to know what was happening elsewhere in [[the galaxy]]. It channeled through the [[heart]] and mind.<ref name="Expert Guide">''[[Star Wars Expert Guide]]''</ref> Major disturbances could be caused by events of a cataclysmic nature, such as a large group of people [[Death|dying]] at the same [[time]]. One sensing a disturbance in the Force might react in pain and confusion, due to feeling empathetic mental anguish at the time.<ref name="Episode IV" /> Smaller scale disturbances also existed. When [[Jedi Knight]] [[Gella Nattai]] felt a disturbance caused by the death of [[Viceroy]] [[Ferrol]] at the [[Limb|hands]] of her friend [[Axel Greylark]], she mentally compared feeling the disturbance to a weight pressing on her heart for a brief moment.<ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|What is thy bidding, my master?''"<br />"''There is a great disturbance in the Force.''"<br />"''I have felt it.''"<br />"''We have a new enemy, the young rebel who destroyed the Death Star. I have no doubt this boy is the offspring of Anakin Skywalker.|Darth Vader and Emperor Palpatine discuss Luke Skywalker|<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>}}
In [[Erasmus Capital City]], shortly before the [[wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran]] that promised to end the [[Eiram and E'ronoh War]], [[Jedi]] Knight Gella Nattai felt a disturbance in the Force stemming from the struggle between Viceroy Ferrol and her friend Axel Greylark, who killed Ferrol before escaping his room with vials of [[Eirami poison|Erami poison]]. During the wedding, [[Jedi Master]] [[Creighton Sun]] felt a disturbance in the Force as [[Eirami guard]]s moved to defend the wedding from a group he had assumed to be late guests, only for him to realize they were actually a crowd of [[bounty hunter]]s after Greylark.<ref name="Convergence" />

[[Anakin Skywalker]]'s suffering due to his [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|mother]] being killed by [[Tusken Raider]]s and subsequent slaughter of an entire [[Unidentified Tusken village|Tusken village]] on [[Tatooine]] created a disturbance in the Force so powerful that [[Yoda]] felt it from halfway across the [[galaxy]] on [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref>

The resurgence of [[Darth]] [[Maul]] on [[Lotho Minor]], as willed by his brother [[Savage Opress]] and [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], caused a disturbance in the Force powerful enough to be sensed by Skywalker on [[Stobar]], [[Asajj Ventress]] on Tatooine, and Yoda and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on Coruscant. [[Count]] [[Dooku]] on [[Serenno]] also felt that something "sinister" was rising shortly before Maul's return, informing [[General]] [[Grievous]] as such and deducing Opress was part of it after feeling his rising power.<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref>

Upon the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]]' execution of [[Order 66]], the simultaneous murders of thousands of Jedi by [[clone trooper]]s that they had commanded caused a significant disturbance in the Force that was felt by Yoda, who had also reeled from a similar tremor caused by Anakin Skywalker's choice to side with Sidious against [[Mace Windu]]. His skill allowed him to escape the same fate and flee from the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] with the aid of friendly [[Wookiee]]s.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref><ref name="datayoda">{{Databank|yoda|Yoda}}</ref> Skywalker's former [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], sensed Skywalker's fall to the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] immediately prior, but did not realize what exactly had occurred, and had no time to think about it when she was thrown into a [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|fight for her life]] against the clones aboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref> ''[[Tribunal]]''.<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> Maul, who was a [[prisoner]] aboard the ship, also sensed what had happened, as well as the Jedi deaths, and asked her if she had felt it when she was forced to release him from the [[Mandalorian vault]] he had been held in as a diversion.<ref name="Shattered" /> The [[Lasat]] Jedi Master [[Jaro Tapal]] also felt a disturbance in the Force onboard the ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Albedo Brave]]'' over [[Bracca]], giving him enough time to strike down his [[Unidentified Clone Commander (13th Battalion)|Clone Commander]] and warn his [[Padawan]], [[Cal Kestis]], of their betrayal.<ref name="Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

Darth Sidious wondered why he did not feel a disruption in the Force when [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s [[starship]], the ''[[Carrion Spike]]'', was [[Mission to Murkhana|stolen]] by [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebels]].<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

Later, Darth Sidious sensed the death of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] as a disturbance in the Force that had [[Rescue of Hera Syndulla (Lothal)|altered]] the fate of the planet [[Lothal]], though he could not sense exactly how. [[Minister]] [[Veris Hydan]] felt that the seizure of the [[Lothal Jedi Temple]] might provide clarity into the matter.<ref name="Wolves and a Door">{{Rebels|Wolves and a Door}}</ref>

[[File:DisturbanceLINE.jpg|thumb|left|150px|Obi-Wan Kenobi sensing the destruction of Alderaan]]
Aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', Obi-Wan Kenobi felt a great disturbance in the Force when the planet [[Alderaan]] was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

Darth Sidious, in his role as [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and speaking via [[hologram]] with Darth Vader, described [[Luke Skywalker]]'s potential emergence as a [[Jedi]] as a "great disturbance in the Force."<ref name="Episode V" />

Both [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]] and [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] felt the emergence of [[Rey Skywalker|Rey's]] latent [[Force power]] as an "awakening" in the Force.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>

The [[First Order]]'s use of [[Starkiller Base]] to [[Hosnian Cataclysm|obliterate]] the [[Hosnian system]] resulted in a massive disturbance in the Force that was felt by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa Solo]] at the [[Resistance base]] on [[D'Qar]]. Organa also buckled at a shudder in the Force upon the murder of her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Han Solo]], at the hands of their son, Ben.<ref name="Awakens novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>

During the [[Mission to Kef Bir|fight]] on [[Kef Bir]], Ben felt a disturbance in the Force that his mother, from the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on [[Ajan Kloss]], was dying so as to sacrifice herself in order to bring her son back to the light side, and Rey felt it too, seeing it as the loss of her [[General]], even though Darth Sidious from [[Exegol]] sensed it as well and stated that she had disrupted his plan, "but her foolish act will be in vain". In the decisive victory of the [[Battle of Exegol]], [[Finn]] felt a disturbance in the Force that Rey was dying until she had been resurrected by Ben using the [[Force healing]] as he, along with his mother, became part of the Force.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
