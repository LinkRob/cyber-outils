{{Quote|&hellip;I'm just a ''priest'' now, in the Church of the Ganthic Enlightenment. I bring people peace. Officiate weddings and funerals. That's all.|Eeth Koth, to Darth Vader|<ref name="Darth Vader 19" />}}
The '''Church of the Ganthic Enlightenment''' was a [[Religion|religious]] organization that was active during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[Eeth Koth]], a former [[Jedi Master]], served as a [[priest]] in the church, and as such officiated [[Marriage|weddings]] and [[funeral]]s, and stated to bring people peace. Eventually, however, [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], [[Sith apprentice]] to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious]], [[Hunt for Eeth Koth|discovered]] Koth and [[Death|killed]] him in a [[lightsaber duel]].<ref name="Darth Vader 19" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Church of the Ganthic Enlightment first appeared in [[Darth Vader (2017) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 19]], a [[2017]] [[canon]] comic written by [[Charles Soule]] and illustrated by [[Giuseppe Camuncoli]].<ref name="Darth Vader 19" />
