'''Balada's Row''' was a location on [[Vespaara]], listed as one of the destinations offered by the [[Vespaara Tours]] [[transport]]ation service in association with the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]]. As detailed by a digital bulletin board found within the Vespaara Tours [[Loading Zone]] building in [[Vespaara Row]], a civilian district and part-time make-shift battle arena utilized by the [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]], Balada's Row could be accessed via "Transit 32," a [[VT-327 transport bus]] route. The location was named after [[Balada]] the [[Hutt]], a sponsor and owner of the [[Grand Arena]].<ref name="Hunters" />
