{{Quote|Ever since you and your brother were delivered to our care, I have watched you grow, and now I will finally be able to witness you complete your mission.|B-2ON, to Am|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
'''B-2ON''' was a [[protocol droid]] that served the [[twin]]s [[Am]] and [[Karre]], [[Dark Lord]]s of the [[TRIGGER Empire|T-Empire]]. At some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] of [[35 ABY]], B-2ON was with Am in her [[throne]] room aboard a [[Gemini-class Star Destroyer|''Gemini''-class Star Destroyer]]. He reported to her that the final adjustments to the [[Starship|ship]] and its [[hyper cannon]] [[superweapon]] were being made and that it would soon be operational. Just as Am gave the order to fire the [[weapon]], they received an alert that the [[reactor]] core's [[Kyber crystal|power source]] had been stolen. Am realized that Karre had [[Thief|stolen]] it, and B-2ON accompanied her to a [[hangar]], where they [[Escape from a Gemini-class Star Destroyer|confronted]] him. With the help of his [[R-series|astromech droid]] [[R-DUO]], Karre escaped the hangar and then [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] Am on the Star Destroyer's surface. After Karre defeated Am and left her floating unconscious, B-2ON used a [[jetpack]] to recover her.  

==Biography==
===Serving the twins===
{{Quote|Once the refined dark energy is unleashed through the ship's hyper cannon, it will easily be capable of destroying a planet or any resistant star systems. However, I am concerned about the effect this prototype dark armor could have on your physical body, as well as that of your brother.|B-2ON, to Am|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
[[File:B2ONAndAm-THETWINS.png|thumb|left|300px|B-2ON reports to Am.]]
B-2ON was a [[protocol droid]] who acted as a loyal [[servant]] to [[Am]] and [[Karre]], [[Force-sensitive]] [[twin]]s who served as [[Dark Lord]]s of the [[TRIGGER Empire|T-Empire]].<ref name="Art of Visions" /> He<ref name="Visions Droids" /> raised Am and acted as a [[Nanny droid|nanny]], [[teacher]], and companion to her.<ref name="Art of Visions" /> At some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] of [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{VisionsDate|103}}</ref> B-2ON was with Am in her [[throne]] room aboard a [[Gemini-class Star Destroyer|''Gemini''-class Star Destroyer]] and reported to her that the final adjustments to the [[Starship|ship]] had been made. The [[droid]] remembered how he had watched Am and Karre grow and became emotional upon realizing that he would soon be able to watch them complete their mission of bringing order to [[the galaxy]].<ref name="The Twins" /> 

After Am declared they would do so, B-2ON informed her that the [[Star Destroyer]]'s [[hyper cannon]] [[superweapon]] would soon be ready to fire. Am asked if the [[weapon]] would be at full strength, and B-2ON confirmed that it would be, then explained the process of how the hyper cannon used [[dark energy]] to destroy [[planet]]s or [[star system]]s. B-2ON then noted that the hyper cannon used the same dark energy as the prototype [[dark armor]] used by Am and Karre, and expressed concern about the effects it could have on their bodies. Am told him not to worry, as she was prepared to sacrifice everything for the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]]. The droid found her response inspiring, and stated he would forever be a humble servant to her and Karre.<ref name="The Twins" />

===Karre's betrayal===
{{Quote|Master Karre, this is quite disappointing.|B-2ON, to Karre|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
[[File:B2ONAm-TheTwins.png|thumb|right|250px|B-2ON rescues Am.]]
Am then gave the order to ready the hyper [[cannon]], but just as she did, they received an alert that the [[reactor]] core's [[Kyber crystal|power source]] had been stolen. B-2ON was surprised, as only Am and Karre were able to access the reactor core chamber. Am and B-2ON noticed Karre was not in his throne room, and while the droid wondered where he had gone, Am realized that the power core had been stolen by Karre&mdash;who secretly had done so to save Am's life.<ref name="The Twins" />

B-2ON accompanied Am and a group of [[T-Stormtrooper]]s to confront Karre in a [[hangar]] aboard the ship, where he planned to steal a [[T-X-wing]] [[starfighter]] and [[Escape from a Gemini-class Star Destroyer|escape]]. Am attempted to convince Karre to hand over the power core, and B-2ON expressed disappointment at Karre's actions. However, Karre refused to return it, and Am prepared to attack him. Before she could, Karre's [[R-series|astromech droid]] [[R-DUO]] fired at Am from the [[X-wing starfighter|X-wing]]<ref name="The Twins" /> he<ref name="Visions Droids" /> had stolen, allowing Karre to escape the hangar. Am pursued him, and the two [[Lightsaber duel|dueled]] on the surface of the Star Destroyer. Karre defeated Am, destroying her [[Am's Dark Lord armor|armor]] and part of the Star Destroyer, then left her floating unconscious among the debris. Wearing a [[jetpack]] and [[helmet]], B-2ON flew to Am and rescued her.<ref name="The Twins" /> 

==Characteristics==
{{Quote|I will forever be the humble servant to you and your brother until the day my functions cease.|B-2ON, to Am|<ref name="The Twins" />}}
B-2ON had a [[humanoid]] form with [[Color|black]] plating and red [[sensor]]s.<ref name="The Twins" /> He was experienced and reserved, but also had an emotional side, which came from his dedication and devotion. He had an unwavering determination to protect Am and his masters' mission.<ref name="Visions Droids" />

==Equipment==
While retrieving Am, B-2ON wore a helmet and jetpack.<ref name="The Twins" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The way B-2ON carries himself and the way he talks reminds you of an old butler. He's experienced and usually reserved. Yet sometimes he shows a surprisingly emotional side that comes from devotion and dedication, leading to an unwavering determination to protect Am and his masters' mission at any cost.|Kanako Shirasaki|<ref name="Visions Droids" />}}
[[File:B2ON-Concept-ArtofVisions.png|thumb|right|300px|Concept art of B-2ON]]
B-2ON first appeared in "[[THE TWINS]]," a short film released as part of the [[Star Wars: Visions Volume 1|first volume]] of the [[Canon|non-canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' series. The animated short, produced by [[Trigger (animation studio)|Studio Trigger]],<ref name="The Twins" /> was released on [[September 22]], [[2021]].<ref name="Cast">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-trailer-and-cast|text=Stunning New ''Star Wars: Visions'' Trailer Debuts}}</ref> He was voiced by [[Tokuyoshi Kawashima]] in Japanese and by [[Jonathan Lipow]] in the English dub.<ref name="The Twins" />  Prior to the short's release, B-2ON was first was depicted in a video uploaded to the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel on [[July 3]], 2021.<ref name="Special Look">{{SWYouTube|b9Gr9gKFxpE|STAR WARS: VISIONS &#124; SPECIAL LOOK &#124; DISNEY+|sw_url=video/special-look-star-wars-visions}}</ref> B-2ON then appeared in the original Japanese and English dub trailers for the series, which were both uploaded to the official ''Star Wars'' YouTube channel on [[August 17]] of that year.<ref name="JP Trailer">{{SWYouTube|lle0NNmvIyU|Star Wars: Visions &#124; Original Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/original-trailer-star-wars-visions}}</ref><ref name="English Trailer">{{SWYouTube|jtAsl-0o3O0|Star Wars: Visions &#124; English Dub Trailer &#124; Disney+|sw_url=video/english-dub-trailer-star-wars-visions}}</ref>

The droid was first named in a [[StarWars.com]] article posted on August 17, 2021, although his name was incorrectly spelled as B-20N.<ref name="Cast" /> The correct spelling of B-2ON was first provided in another article published on [[September 15]] of that year, although it repeated the spelling error in its first mention of the character.<ref name="Visions Droids" /> The same spelling error occurred again in another StarWars.com article published on [[October 31]], [[2025]].<ref name="Favorite Droid Sidekicks">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-visions-droids|text=Our Favorite Droid Sidekicks from ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref>

B-2ON is a reference to the [[3PO-series protocol droid|droid]] [[C-3PO]], and B-2ON's name was created by shifting each of the letters and numbers in C-3PO's name one space. B-2ON's role is similar to that of<ref name="Art of Visions" /> the [[Wikipedia:DC Comics|DC comics]] superhero<ref name="Batman">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dc.com/characters/batman|text=Batman|work=[https://www.dc.com DC.com]|archivedate=20250222131749}}</ref> [[Wikipedia:Batman|Batman's]]<ref name="Art of Visions" /> butler<ref name="Batman" /> [[Wikipedia:Alfred Pennyworth|Alfred]],<ref name="Art of Visions" /> and ''Visions'' producer [[Kanako Shirasaki]] compared the way he talks to an old butler.<ref name="Visions Droids" /> Director [[Hiroyuki Imaishi]] said that he liked the contrast between ''Star Wars'' droids looking like robots but having human personalities.<ref name="Art of Visions" />
