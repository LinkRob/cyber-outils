'''William V. Tiller''' is an artist who worked for several [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]]s.

==Biography==
Bill Tiller studied character animation at the [[Wikipedia:California Institute of the Arts|California Institute of the Arts]] under artists who had been employed in [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney]].<ref name="dig">[[Jo Ashburn]], ''The Dig Official Player's Guide''</ref>

His work was noted by [[Collette Michaud]] in the school's annual Producers' Show and hired Tiller to [[LucasArts]]. His first task was background art on the first version of ''The Dig'' that was eventually cancelled. Tiller worked in the following iterations of the project.<ref name="dig" />

Tiller with [[Leonard Robel]] worked on the backgrounds and live-action character animations in the cutscenes of ''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault]]''.<ref>''[[Star Wars: Rebel Assault: The Official Insider's Guide]]''</ref><ref name="dig" /> He also worked for the ''[[Super Star Wars: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="dig" />
