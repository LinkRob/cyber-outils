'''B-Little''' was a specialized program that made droids entertain their masters. The [[Koorivar]] [[merchant]] [[Sheb Valaad]]'s [[protocol droid]], [[Blue (protocol droid)|Blue]] was equipped with it and [[Adul-8]], which combined to make him particularly obsequious to Sheb and his customers including the undercover [[Jedi]] [[Quinlan Vos]].<ref name="DD">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref>
