{{Quote|Native to Kashyyyk, the trakkrrrn's meat has a natural spiciness that is brought to the surface when cooked low and slow. Serve a slab of these at your next gatherin', and you're bound to hear endless growls of joy.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="LDCookbook" />}}
'''Barbecued Trakkrrrn Ribs''' was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] made from the ribs of the [[trakkrrrn]], a creature [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] with meat that had a natural spiciness. The ribs were served on the [[bone]] with [[sauce]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] claimed that he had never seen [[Wookiee]]s&mdash;[[Hair|furry]] natives of Kashyyyk known for their appetites&mdash;devour anything as quickly as when he had served them Barbecued Trakkrrrn Ribs.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

At some point after Tuggs published his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> the chef included a recipe for the ribs in the main courses section of his second cookbook, ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]''. He claimed that the spiciness of trakkrrrn meat was best brought to the surface when cooked slowly at a low heat and promised it would leave any Wookiee diner satisfied with nothing but bones left on their plate, though they might need to clean sauce from their fur when they were done.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Barbecued Trakkrrrn Ribs was created as a recipe for the [[2021]] ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the ribs uses [[Wikipedia:Pork ribs|baby back pork ribs]], [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|extra virgin olive oil]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|packed light brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Garlic powder|garlic powder]], and [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]]. The recipe for the sauce uses extra virgin olive oil, minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], [[Wikipedia:Chipotle|chipotle]] in [[Wikipedia:Adobo|adobo sauce]], [[Wikipedia:Ketchup|ketchup]], [[Wikipedia:Sweet chili sauce|sweet chili sauce]], packed light brown [[Wikipedia:Sugar|sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Apple cider vinegar|apple cider vinegar]], ground [[Wikipedia:Cumin|cumin]], [[Wikipedia:Onion powder|onion powder]], salt, and black pepper.<ref name="LDCookbook" /> [[Barbecued trakkrrrn ribs]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they appeared in the [[1998]] novel ''[[Rebel Dawn]]'', which was written by [[A. C. Crispin]].<ref name="RD">''[[Rebel Dawn]]''</ref>
