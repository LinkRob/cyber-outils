The '''BKX-4 "Shockwave" Grenade''', known simply as the '''BKX-4''', was a model of [[grenade]] manufactured by [[Baktoid Personal Ordnance]], a subsidiary of [[Baktoid Armor Workshop]]. Rather than causing damage with shrapnel, the BKX-4 caused damage with over-pressure and shockwaves, which could disorient or knock down opponents. Its detonation caused a broad blast that was strong enough to [[Death|kill]] multiple [[Armor|unarmored]] targets, and also damage structures, machinery, and material. They were popular among [[combat engineer]]s who used them to clear bunkers and cause large amounts of chaos.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
The BKX-4 "Shockwave" Grenade was first mentioned in [[Fully Operational (sourcebook)|''Fully Operational'']],<ref name="Fully Operational" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Fully Operational Is Online">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/3/29/fully-operational-is-online|text=Fully Operational Is Online}}</ref>
