A '''bar''' was located aboard the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[battle station]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], prior to the [[superweapon]]'s destruction in the [[Battle of Yavin]]. According to [[Sinjir Rath Velus]], it was an ugly, austere place with little to select in terms of [[alcohol]]. However, those who knew [[Pilkey]] would be given some of his [[Pilkey's "special batch"|"special batch"]].<ref name="ALD" />
