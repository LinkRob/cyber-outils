'''Cantina Roll-Call: Shedding Light on Some Alien Aliases''' is an article appearing on the [[StarWars.com]] website. It reveals the behind-the-scenes details of the [[Alien/Legends|aliens]] and other characters who appear in the [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina/Legends|cantina]] scene of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. The article was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and published on [[May 2]], [[2003]], though it was later reposted under the same name on [[October 27]], [[2008]].
