'''''Andor'': Vol. 1 (Episodes 1-4) [Original Score]''' is the soundtrack for the first four episodes of ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''&mdash;"[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," "[[That Would Be Me]]," "[[Reckoning (episode)|Reckoning]]," and "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]]"&mdash;and was released on digital platforms on [[October 21]], [[2022]]. The show's score was composed by Nicholas Britell,<ref name="Apple Music" /> who announced the track's release two days prior on Twitter.<ref name="Britell">{{Twitter|NicholasBritell|status/1582827798850203648|Nicholas Britell|date=2022-10-19|quote=Out this Friday!|archivedate=20221020025102}}</ref> The three Main Title Theme tracks were released early on [[September 21]] alongside the episodes themselves.

The show's composer, [[Nicholas Britell]], described each title theme as a variation of the first, changing sound to fit the mood of each episode depending on its preceding or following storyline.<ref name="Interview">{{SW|url=news/nicholas-britell-andor-interview|text=How Composer Nicholas Britell Created New Sounds for ''Andor''}}</ref>
