The '''Auxiliary Bay''' was a docking bay on the [[Achra Station]], a [[space station]] located in the [[Akiva system]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[docking clamp]]s in the bay were fried and the whole system needed to be reset. A [[Unidentified Pantoran engineer|Pantoran engineer]] working on the station during that [[Standard year|year]] complained that no one was clearing the bay because of the clamps and mentioned that they would need to get [[Andava]], a [[Rodian]] [[contract broker]] that operated on the station, to perform the system reset.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]] listened the [[Pantoran]] [[engineer]] talk to themselves and mention the Bay while Vess was searching the station for [[pirate]] named [[Procyon Wy]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Auxiliary Bay was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was mentioned by the [[Pantoran]] [[engineer]] during the "Passenger" [[syndicate]] quest while the player is searching for Procyon Wy.<ref name="Outlaws" />
