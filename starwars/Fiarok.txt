'''Fiarok''' was a [[Nautolan]] [[diplomat]] who led the [[Nautolan peace delegation]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Jango Fett 2" /> At that [[time]], the [[Anselmi–Nautolan war]] had come to a ceasefire with a [[Anselmi–Nautolan treaty|treaty]] set to end the conflict. To support that treaty, [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum]] decided to release the [[Hope of Glee Anselm]], an artifact of both species, to both the Nautolan and Aselmi delegations at a ceremony on [[Jaloria]].<ref name="Jango Fett 1" /> In secret, however, Fiarok was approached by the [[Mirialan]]<ref name="Jango Fett 4" /> [[Huijari]], an [[agent]] who served [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Clarifications">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1804310763495924024|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=[Gif that says "Yes Definitely"] {{C|In response to: "So just to have everything confirmed outright or at least your intentions, The "mirialan" is Huijari and the "benefactor" is Dooku, with Dooku also therefore being the non-republic figure it was mentioned Huijari was working for"}}|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/Gm0sm}}</ref> Unaware of Dooku's true identity and just knowing the Mirialan was working on behalf of a benefactor, Fiarok agreed to a plot to [[Heist of the Hope of Glee Anselm|steal the statue]] and swapped it with a fake. He then further hired a [[Unidentified thief group|crew of Nautolan theives]] to steal the fake.<ref name="Jango Fett 4" />

At the ceremony, where Fiarok was accompanied by two associates and stood alongside an [[Unidentified Anselmi official|Anselmi representative]] and<ref name="Jango Fett 1" /> [[Governor]] [[Tok]]<ref name="Jango Fett 3">[[Jango Fett 3|''Jango Fett'' 3]]</ref> from the [[Galactic Republic]], the artifact was presented and then stolen by the thieves, threatening to bring Glee Anselm back to war,<ref name="Jango Fett 1" /> all while Fiarok kept the real statue in his own guest quarters.<ref name="Jango Fett 4" /> Fiarok was later present, alongside the Anselmi, when the Republic official hired the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to retrieve it.<ref name="Jango Fett 1" /> When Fett revealed the truth, Fiarok tried to reveal the wider conspiracy but failed to convince the Republic.<ref name="Jango Fett 4" />
