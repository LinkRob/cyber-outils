The '''Empire Shuttle Yard''' was an outpost of the [[Galactic Empire]] on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]]. [[Captain]] [[Kosh]] used it as a depot for his [[Kosh's private smuggling operation|smuggling operation]] until the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] under [[Saponza's partner]] and [[Colonel]] [[Jennica Pierce]] [[Attack on the Empire Shuttle Yard|destroyed]] it.<ref name="Commander" />
