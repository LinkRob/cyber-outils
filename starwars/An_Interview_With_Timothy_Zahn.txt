"'''An Interview With Timothy Zahn'''" is a short interview with ''[[Star Wars]]'' author [[Timothy Zahn]] included at the end of the ''[[Fool's Bargain]]'' e-book. In it, Zahn is asked about his prolific ''Star Wars'' writing career, ''Fool's Bargain'', his then-upcoming novels ''[[Survivor's Quest]]'' and [[Outbound Flight (novel)|''Outbound Flight'']], and various other matters.
