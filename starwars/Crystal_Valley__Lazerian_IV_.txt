The '''Crystal Valley''' was a region on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Lazerian IV]]. The [[King/Legends|King]] of [[Kingdom of Devit|Devit]] had a private estate near it, and it was one of the world's best kept secrets. The [[Empeth Crystal]]s were grown in the Crystal Valley.<ref name="TSoK" />
