The '''Antiquities Institute''' was a small [[museum]] within the [[Tion Hegemony]] from which the sacred vase known as the [[ou'tranoi]] was stolen by [[Hylobon]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] in around [[11 BBY|7966]] [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] The act of theft was caught on [[Hologram|holographic]] security footage, which was reviewed by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], whom suspected that the theft had been orchestrated by the [[Crimson Dawn]] leader [[Dryden Vos]], who often employed Hylobons. Imperial Security Bureau [[agent]] [[Andressa Divo]] included the case in a file she maintained on a variety of museum thefts that she suspected were linked to Vos.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Antiquities Institute was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />
