A '''flight instructor''' was an individual who trained [[pilot]]s. During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protector]] [[Fenn Rau]] served as a flight instructor for the [[Grand Army of the Republic]].<ref name="Fenn Rau" /> [[Goran]]<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" /> and [[Vult Skerris]]<ref name="Vult Skerris">{{Databank|vult-skerris|Vult Skerris}}</ref> served as instructors at [[Skystrike Academy]], an [[Imperial Academy]]<ref name="The Antilles Extraction" /> on [[Montross]].<ref name="Skystrike Academy">{{Databank|skystrike-academy|Skystrike Academy}}</ref> [[Jake Farrell]] served as a flight instructor for the [[Galactic Empire]] before retiring on [[Derango 4]].<ref name="Incredibuilds">''[[Star Wars: A-wing Deluxe Book and 3D Wood Model]]''</ref> [[Yurib Nakan]] served as a flight instructor at the [[Carida Academy|Imperial Academy]] on [[Carida]].<ref name="Imperial Cadet 3">[[Han Solo - Imperial Cadet 3|''Han Solo - Imperial Cadet'' 3]]</ref> [[Lieutenant]] [[Galek]] served as a flight instructor in the [[First Order Academy]], notably training cadets [[Jace Rucklin]] and [[Tamara Ryvora]].<ref name="Live Fire" />
