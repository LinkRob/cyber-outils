'''Deaons''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the astronomical object of [[Ord Gimmel]]. Members of the species possessed long [[Limb|fingers]] and manufactured a variety of makes and models of the short, squat [[variable holdout blaster]]s, which were shaped to fit their hands.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
