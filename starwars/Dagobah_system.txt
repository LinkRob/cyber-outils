The '''Dagobah system''' was a [[star system]] that contained the swamp-covered [[planet]] of [[Dagobah]]. In his final [[Standard year|years]], the former [[Grand Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] [[Yoda]] called the Dagobah system his [[Yoda's Hut|home]].
