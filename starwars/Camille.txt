'''Camille''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Gloovan]] [[bodyguard]] who, alongside her almost-identical counterpart [[Delphine]], worked at the [[Jedha City]] [[Cantina|tapbar]] [[Enlightenment]] as security during the [[High Republic Era]]. The duo were known as the [[Twinkle Sisters]].<ref name="New Prospects" />
