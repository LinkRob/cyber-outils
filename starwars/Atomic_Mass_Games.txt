'''Atomic Mass Games''', abbreviated '''AMG''', is a miniature manufacturer under the parent company [[Asmodee Group]], which also owns [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. The manufacturing rights for the miniature games ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Armada]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'' were moved from Fantasy Flight Games to AMG on [[November 16]], [[2020]].<ref name="AMG Special" /> The company also is releasing the ''[[Star Wars: Shatterpoint]]'' miniature game, launched in [[2023]].<ref name="Shatterpoint Announced">{{AMGweb|url=shatterpoint|text=Star Wars: Shatterpoint|date=2021-11-9}}</ref>
