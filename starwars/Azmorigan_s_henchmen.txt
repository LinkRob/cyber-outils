'''Azmorigan's henchmen'''<ref name="Episode Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/idiots-array-episode-gallery|text="Idiot's Array" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 16}}</ref> were a cadre of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] and [[bodyguard]]s regularly hired by the [[Jablogian]] [[crime lord]] [[Azmorigan]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" />

==Description==
Azmorigan's henchmen were a group of [[humanoid]] mercenaries that served the criminal [[Azmorigan]] as his bodyguards. They wielded [[stock]]less [[773 Firepuncher sniper rifle]]s and black [[armor]] with an [[insect]]-like [[helmet]].<ref name="Brothers of the Broken Horn" />

==History==
[[File:Armorigans_henchmen.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Azmorigan, flanked by two of his henchmen]]
The henchmen accompanied Azmorigan on his [[starship]] [[Merchant One|''Merchant One'']] and ambushed [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and the [[Spectres]] at [[Lando Calrissian's farm]], but were defeated, with two henchmen retreating from the confrontation along with Azmorigan.<ref name="Idiots Array" />

The henchmen later traveled with Azmorigan to [[Nixus Hub 218]] when the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] and rebel [[Ezra Bridger]] showed up with five [[power generator]]s. When Azmorigan restrain Ohnaka and Bridger, the henchmen took the pair's weapons and one of the henchmen took off Bridger’s helmet, Azmorigan recognized him. Realizing one of the generators was missing, the henchmen got into a firefight with Bridger's [[astromech droid]] companion [[C1-10P|Chopper]]. After Azmorigan knocked out Bridger and Ohnaka, Chopper used one of the henchman’s [[blaster rifle]]s and Ohnaka’s [[blaster pistol]] to defeat Azmorigan, who promptly left in his [[Personal Luxury Yacht 3000]], leaving his unconscious henchmen behind.<ref name="Brothers of the Broken Horn" />
