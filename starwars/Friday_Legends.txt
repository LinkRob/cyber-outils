'''Friday''',<ref name="Friends">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref> sometimes shortened to '''Fri''', was a [[Standard day/Legends|day]] of the [[Standard week/Legends|week]].<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref>

At the time of the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]], the [[Musician/Legends|singer]] [[Sy Snootles/Legends|Sy Snootles]] performed a series of concerts on "Fri and [[Saturday/Legends|Sat]]," on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], according to [[The Sy Snootles Show (poster)/Legends|a poster]] written in [[Aurebesh/Legends|Aurebesh]].<ref name="Deception">{{TCW|Deception}}</ref>

A [[Painting/Legends|poster]] in the [[Bilbousa Bazaar/Legends|Bilbousa Bazaar]] showed the silhouette of a [[Gender/Legends|feminine]] looking [[Twi'lek/Legends|Twi'lek]] doing a pole dance, with a [[Huttese (alternative writing system)/Legends|Huttese]] caption reading "Dancers every FRIDAY [[Standard day/Legends|NIGHT]]!!".<ref name="Friends" /> The Twi'lek poster also appeared on the door leading out of the [[Refresher/Legends|latrine]] at the [[79's/Legends|79's]] cantina on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]].<ref name="Orders">{{TCW|Orders (episode)}}</ref>
