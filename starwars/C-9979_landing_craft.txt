The '''C-9979 landing craft''', also known as the '''Trade Federation landing ship''', was a heavy [[transport]] [[starship]] used by the [[Trade Federation]],<ref name="Episode I" /> and later the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The [[Trade Federation armaments committee]] chose the ship.<ref name="Complete Vehicles">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> The C-9979 was an elegantly designed transport manufactured by [[Haor Chall Engineering Corporation]] and was controlled by a central computer. One C-9979 could subjugate an entire susceptible [[Planet|world]].<ref name="Starfighters" />

==Characteristics==
The C-9979 was a heavily armed multi-vehicle and [[Troop transport|troop]] [[transport]] and [[landing craft]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" /> utilized by the [[Trade Federation]]<ref name="Episode I" /> and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Episode III" /> to deploy invasion [[Army|armies]] to [[planet]]s it intended to occupy<ref name="Episode I" /> or for massive offensives against heavy enemy forces.<ref name="Massacre" /> It was manned by a small crew of [[droid]]s, in keeping with the Federation's policy of reliance on automation; these manned the topside control center and [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Control Ship]] signal reception antennas, operated the ship's wingtip and hull weapons and repair shops as well the maintenance bays where individual [[B1-series battle droid]]s were realigned after every battle.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" />

[[File:C9979aft-MMSWSB.png|thumb|left|250px|Aft view of a C-9979 landing craft]]
The entirety of a C-9979's internal volume, save for the wingtips which housed the [[tensor field generator]]s and [[Antigrav|anti-gravity stabilizers]], was dedicated to vehicle and trooper storage. Hundreds of [[Multi-Troop Transport]]s, [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I|Armored Assault Tanks]] and [[Platoon Attack Craft]]s were stored in special garages on the tensor-field supported wings<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" /> while a few were kept in battle-ready positions behind the main deployment doors.<ref name="Starfighters" /> Upon landing on a designated landing zone, special [[Perimeter sensor|perimeter field sensors]] on the main doors scanned the surrounding area to detect [[land mine]]s or other potential hazards to the troop contingent.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" /> After giving the all clear, the doors opened, allowing the [[Entry ramp|drop ramp]] to be deployed.<ref name="Episode I" /> Then, as the first combat-ready units (usually MTTs accompanied by two AATs each) exited the craft, the [[Repulsor field|repulsor tracks]] lining the staging garages were activated, guiding the vehicles from their storage to a staging area directly above the main gate; this assistance was required because the larger vehicles couldn't maneuver in the tight confines of the C-9979's internal tunnels. From the staging area, specialized transport clamps lowered the vehicles to the exit tunnel while these powered their on-board repulsors<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" /> to exit the landing craft and begin their operations.<ref name="Massacre" /> Due to this complex procedure, the deployment of a full C-9979 contingent could take up to 45 minutes.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" />

Due to its extreme size, the C-9979 was disassembled after every mission, having their wings removed<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" /> and stored separately<ref name="Massacre" /> to prevent unnecessary strain to the body-wing connections. Whenever they were required, the wings were reassembled and bound to the body by use of specialized tensor fields which prevented them from sagging or breaking mid-flight.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2020" />

==History==
===Invasion of Naboo===
<div style="float:right">[[File:Trade_Federation_Invasion.png|thumb|250px|Landing craft, escorted by droid starfighters, approach Naboo.]]</div>
During the [[Invasion of Naboo]], the [[Trade Federation]] used many of these [[landing craft]] to deploy their [[Trade Federation Droid Army|Droid Army]], including [[Multi-Troop Transport]]s.<ref name="Episode I" />

===The Clone Wars===
As part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] effort to recruit the [[planet]] [[Toydaria]] to their side, [[Sith apprentice]] [[Asajj Ventress]] and a full [[battalion]] of droids arrived on the planet in a C-9979 landing craft.<ref name="Ambush" />

When the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Battle of Ryloth|invaded]] the planet [[Ryloth]], the ship was used at some point during the battle, with a few of them being destroyed.<ref name="Liberty">{{TCW|Liberty on Ryloth}}</ref>

During the [[Battle of Mon Cala]], a C-9979 [[landing craft]] was used to transport eight [[Hydroid Medusa]]s under its wings from the [[planet]] [[Karkaris]].<ref name="Water War" />

After [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] used [[Nightsisters]] [[magick]] on the [[Sith Lord]] [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], he sent the [[General]] [[Grievous]] and the [[Separatist Droid Army]] to [[Dathomir]] to eradicate the Nightsisters. C-9979 landing craft deployed a large compliment of droids as well as [[defoliator]] [[tank]]s, which slaughtered all of the Nightsisters save for Talzin and Ventress, who had been outcast by her former master Dooku, who escaped the battle.<ref name="Massacre">{{TCW|Massacre}}</ref>

Heading to meet [[Boss]] [[Lyonie]] after [[Jar Jar Binks]] disguised as Lyonie called off the attack, [[General]] [[Grevious]] met with "Lyonie"  and [[Roos Tarpals]] in one of the transports. Following a negotiation between the two, Binks gave Tarpals instructions to leave while he stalled for time. When Grevious became impatient, he chased Binks out of the ship, where the two [[Skirmish on Naboo|dueled]] just outside.<ref name="Shadow Warrior">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]'' establishes that the C-9979 landing craft is 210 meters in length.<ref name="Starfighters" /> The [[Databank (2014-)|databank]] and the [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|StarWars.com Encyclopedia]] had previously established that the wingspan was 149 meters.<ref name="Databank" /><ref name="SWE" /> Visibly, the wingspan appears to be greater than the bow-to-stern length of the vessel. It is unclear whether the ''Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'' refers to wingspan or bow-to-stern length.<ref name="Starfighters" />

The ''Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'' also establishes that the C-9979 landing craft can carry up to 361 [[battle droid]]s,<ref name="Starfighters" /> while the [[2019]] source book ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'' establishes that the C-9979 can carry up to 2,500 battle droids and [[droideka]]s, depending on its configuration.<ref name="Rise" /> As ''Rise of the Separatists'' is the more recent source, the article makes the assumption that the newer source is correct.
