'''Bruno Denturri''' was an [[enslaver]] who was an enemy of the [[Zygerrian]] [[Queen of Zygerria|Queen]] [[Miraj Scintel]]. As part of his [[Mission to Zygerria|mission]] to rescue [[Conquest of Kiros|captured]] [[Togruta]] colonists, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] went under the name "Lars Quell" and claimed he had [[Death|killed]] Denturri. Skywalker further claimed that his disguised [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] was a [[Slavery|slave]] he had captured from Denturri's [[Bruno Denturri's palace|palace]] as a gift for the [[queen]]. Denturri was a skilled warrior, as Scintel was highly impressed when "Quell" claimed to have defeated him in combat. Scintel also considered him to have an excellent eye for beautiful [[Gender|female]] slaves.<ref name="SOTR" />
