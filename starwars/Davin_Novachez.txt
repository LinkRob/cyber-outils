'''Davin Novachez''', known by the nickname "'''Dav'''", was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] native of [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]]. He was the big brother of [[Roan Novachez]] and [[Oliver Novachez|Oliver "Ollie" Novachez]]. He attended [[Pilot Academy Middle School]] to become a [[Pilot/Legends|pilot]] like [[Roan Novachez's father|his father]] and [[Roan Novachez's grandfather|grandfather]]. Although his age is unknown, he was an older teenager. He wrote to Roan almost as much as his Dad did, and showed an extent of interest in his career. He also had a girlfriend named [[Enowyn]], who he likely met at the Academy.<ref name="Jedi Academy" />

==Personality and traits==
Dav was optimistic, hard-working, and supportive, especially of his little brother, Roan. He was also something of a tease, but this was only seen with Roan and it wasn't above average for their relationship. He was also kind-hearted.<ref name="Jedi Academy" />
