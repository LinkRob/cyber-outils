The '''destruction of Tah'Nuhna''', also known as the '''sacking of Tah'Nuhna''', was an assault launched by the [[First Order]] on the neutral world of [[Tah'Nuhna]] for assisting the [[Resistance]].

==Prelude==
The people of the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Tah'Nuhna]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], the [[Tah'Nuhna (species)|Tah'Nuhna]] were a peaceful [[species]] who had always taken a neutral stance in [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[politics]] since the days of the [[Hundred-Year Darkness]], which managed to keep their planet out of [[the galaxy]]'s major wars. In fact, even the [[Galactic Empire]] never invaded the planet. As per their tradition of helping those in need, they welcomed the [[Resistance]] to their planet<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> to take refuge after the [[Battle of Crait]]. However, the First Order traced a Resistance transmission to the planet and dispatched<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> an [[Tah'Nuhna assault group|assault group]]<ref name="Ships of FO and Resistance" /> under [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] to punish the Tah'Nuhna for daring to help the Resistance, which was not seen as a neutral act by the First Order.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />

Intending to ransack the planet instead of invading it, the [[Battle group|assault group]] was composed of several [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]], including the ''[[Finalizer]]'', a [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]], and a compliment of [[TIE/se Bomber]]s and [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]]. After the First Order [[fleet]] arrived over a Tah'Nuhna [[city]], it was spotted by a Tah'Nuhna working on one of the world's glass palaces, which were built across generations. The sight of the First Order fleet made one Tah'Nuhna realize their world would soon have a new legacy, instead of their one of peace and neutrality. A [[Unidentified Tah'Nuhnan leader|Tah'Nuhan leader]] did contact Hux and pleaded that he not fo through with the attack, but her words fell on deaf [[ear]]s.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />

==Destruction==
[[File:Grudge-SWZ87.png|thumb|left|250px|First Order TIE Bombers attack the city]]
The ''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought opened the attack with a shot to the city, which was followed by the deployment of [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]] under orders kill any civilians who tried to flee from the besieged metropolis. As the snowtroopers fired on the populace, [[TIE/se Bomber]]s were deployed and attacked the city from above in a [[bomb]]ing run.<ref name="Allegiance 1" /> One [[First Order TIE fighter pilot]] involved in the operation as a [[bomber]] [[pilot]] was known by the nickname "[["Grudge"|Grudge]]."<ref name="Grudge">{{FFGXW2|set=Fury of the First Order Squadron Pack|cardname="Grudge"}}</ref> As explosions and destruction spread through the city and closer to her tower, the Tah'Nuhna leader sent out a message to anyone who could hear them asking for aid, only for several [[missile]]s to destroy the tower. The leader was killed in the explosion.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|We've&hellip; we've just received a general transmission from Tah'Nuhna&hellip; The First Order&hellip; They just wiped out the entire planet!''"<br />"''That must be a mistake&hellip; the species is known across the sector for their neutrality. Why, they welcomed us when we needed supplies after our escape from Crait&ndash;''"<br />"''And they've paid the ultimate price for that kindness.|Kaydel Ko Connix, [[C-3PO]], and Leia Organa|<ref name="Allegiance 1" />}}
[[File:AnoatResistanceBase-Allegience1.png|thumb|right|175px|Kaydel Ko Connix running to inform Leia of the destruction]]
At the new [[Resistance Camp]] on [[Anoat]], [[Kaydel Ko Connix]] watched as Tah'Nuhna was destroyed. She then ran over to General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo]] and reported the destruction of the people to her. Leia was very sad to hear the news, noting that the Tah'Nuhna paid the ultimate price for kindness. Knowing that it was now or never, Leia assembled a team to travel to the [[water]] planet of [[Mon Cala]] in order to [[Mission to Mon Cala|plead]] with the [[Mon Calamari]] for ships to rebuild the Resistance.<ref name="Allegiance 1" />
