The '''Binog Mesa''' was a [[grass]]y [[mesa]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Bogano]] that shared its name with the [[amphi-mammalian]] [[binog]] [[species]]. The mesa was visited by [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]] and by fugitive Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Standard year|years]] after. Cordova's discovery of a mural of a binog as drawn by the ancient [[Zeffonian]] species led him to believe that the binog was the reason why the Zeffonians had built their [[Bogano Vault|vault]] on Bogano. When Kestis was present on the mesa, he discovered the same mural and witnessed a nearby binog.

==Description==
[[File:BinogMesa-BogRatandSplox.jpg|left|thumb|250px|Splox and Bog rats on the Binog Mesa]]
Located on the<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|bogano|Bogano}}</ref> [[planet]] [[Bogano]], the Binog Mesa was a [[grass]]-filled [[mesa]] that shared its name with the [[amphi-mammalian]] [[binog]] [[species]]. It was bordered by the [[Fractured Plain]] and [[Great Divide]] regions. It contained short<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> [[Color|white]] [[limestone]] and red [[clay]]<ref name="ArtFallenOrder">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> cliffs with hanging [[Plant|vegetation]] off the sides and slopes between the flat areas of grass. A massive [[sinkhole]] led down into the [[Subterranean Refuge]], an area of [[cave]]s. The location was inhabited by binogs as well as the [[insect]]ile [[splox]], quadrupedal [[bog rat]]s, and [[Hair|furry]] [[bogling]]s.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

==History==
Long before the [[Imperial Era]], members of the [[Zeffonian]] [[species]] drew a mural of a binog on the side of one of the Binog Mesa's cliffs.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> By [[14 BBY]],<ref name="JFODate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Eno Cordova]], who had settled on Bogano, traveled to the mesa, where he discovered the Zeffonian's mural. He theorized that the presence of the binog was the reason why the Zeffonians built the [[Bogano Vault]] on the planet.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" /> In 14 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="JFODate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during his [[secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order]], the fugitive [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Bogano|journeyed]] to Bogano.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
While on Bogano, Kestis visited the Binog Mesa, where he encountered a resting binog and examined the Zeffonian binog mural. Kestis obtained the necessary material for the "[[Pathfinder (outfit)|Pathfinder]]" [[Clothing|outfit]] from a crate within a narrow crevasse, and fought several splox and bog rats to retrieve it. He also used the [[Force power]] of [[psychometry]] in order to discover the events that led to the [[death]] of a splox within the region.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
{{Endgame}}
