During the [[Imperial Era]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] had a number of '''Coruscant operatives''', which consisted of [[senator]]s, [[aide]]s, [[ambassador]]s, and [[Page (occupation)|pages]], that worked within the [[Galactic Empire]]. After the [[Dissolution of the Imperial Senate]], [[General]] [[Airen Cracken]] sent a message to [[Commander-in-Chief|Commander]] [[Mon Mothma]] informing her of the fact the operatives no longer held any [[Politics|political]] power within the government, and that the Alliance had been silenced.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
