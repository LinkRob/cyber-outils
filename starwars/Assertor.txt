The '''''Assertor''''' was an [[Assertor-class Star Dreadnought|''Assertor''-class Star Dreadnought]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], serving the [[Imperial Navy]] as the lead [[starship]] of its class. Both the ''Assertor'' and its sister ship, the [[Wrath (Assertor-class)|''Wrath'']], were laid down for construction at [[Kuat Drive Yards]]' [[Kuat shipyards]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. At some point after that battle, the ''Assertor'' was deployed in a large [[Assertor battle group|battle group]], which was thought to have consisted of dozens of other ships, along the border between the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] and [[Outer Rim Territories]] to support operations against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Lead by Example" />

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Assertor'' was mentioned in the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' sourcebook ''[[Lead by Example]]'', which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2016]] for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Lead by Example" />
