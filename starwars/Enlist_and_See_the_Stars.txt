'''''Enlist and See the Stars'''''<ref name="SW">{{SW|url=series/spark-of-rebellion-concept-art-gallery|text="Spark of Rebellion" Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 12}}</ref> was the name of several<ref name="Empire Day">{{Rebels|Empire Day (episode)}}</ref> [[graffiti]] [[Painting|murals]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. It featured an illustration of a ringed [[planet]] with the word "ENLIST" written [[Aurebesh]] above it, "AND SEE THE" beneath it,<ref name="SW" /> and "[[Star|STARS]]" printed in large letters below the illustration.<ref name="Spark" /> The mural came in several different [[color]] palettes and appeared in the [[city]] streets,<ref name="Empire Day" /> and [[LothalNet comm tower E-272|Ezra's tower]].<ref name="Spark" />
