'''Droidekas''', also known as ''' destroyer droids''' ('''destroyers''' for short),<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> or as '''rollies''' in [[clone trooper]] [[List of phrases and slang|slang]],<ref name="Ambush">{{TCW|Ambush}}</ref> were a type of [[battle droid]]<ref name="Databank Droideka" /> used by the [[Trade Federation]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]] and, later, by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Manufactured by the [[Colicoid]]s<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> on [[Colla IV]], droidekas were dangerous and deadly, designed to exterminate their adversaries with extreme prejudice. They could transform their shape by curling into a ball and moving up to 75 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]]<ref name="SWJTPM" /> across a surface, or stand on three legs and utilize a shield generator while firing at a target.<ref name="Episode I" />

==Description==
{{Quote|Destroyers are quick, pack twin blasters, and come with their own shield generators. Nothing short of a cannon will pierce it, but they do have two weaknesses, and you will need to work together to exploit them. The shields deflect high velocity attacks. What the shields don't stop are slow or stationary objects. They're designed to absorb them so nothing hinders their movement.''"<br />"''Destroyers are blind from behind. A distraction combined with a rear assault will give you a kill. The trick is to get the right speed on the droid popper.|[[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]], on how to take out a Droideka|<ref name="A War on Two Fronts">{{TCW|A War on Two Fronts}}</ref>}}
[[File:Droideka-USC.png|thumb|left|250px|Droidekas were equipped with a shield generator.]]
Shelled in [[bronzium]]<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> armor, droidekas came equipped with numerous armaments, both defensive and offensive. In particular, they made use of twin repeating [[blaster]] cannons and personal [[deflector shield generator]]s, which meant that when clones conducted mass attacks, they were stopped or suppressed by the droideka's twin repeating blaster cannons, and nothing could be done, unless the clones could get an EMP through the shield, use anti armor weapons, ion grenades or artillery could be called in.<ref name="A War on Two Fronts" /> Some destroyers were referred to with he/him pronouns<ref name="Aphra 17">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 17|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 17]]</ref> while others were referred to with she/her. Additionally, a droideka could be referred to with it/its.<ref name="Hunters Battle" />

A droideka's [[Deflector shield|shields]] were so strong and powerful that they could easily repel or deflect short-range, high-velocity attacks; rendering sidearms or blasters ineffective. The droideka was able to fire through its shield because the shield was polarized (meaning that it was phased) to allow the droideka's blaster bolts to travel out, but would stop any projectiles from coming in. Most hand-held weapons did not have enough power to pierce it. Although powerful, they featured several flaws that could be exploited to guarantee a kill; droideka were blind from behind and their shields were ineffective against slow and stationary objects. Rear assaults, coupled with a distraction and a well-thrown [[Electromagnetic pulse grenade|Electro Magnetic Pulse grenade]], were an extremely effective combination.<ref name="A War on Two Fronts" /> Another effective way to destroy a droideka was to crush it with a large, heavy object. As seen during the [[Battle of Naboo]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] was able to penetrate a droideka's shields with the blasters of an [[N-1 starfighter]],<ref>Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|Great. Rolling death balls.|Ahsoka Tano, on droidekas|<ref name="The Clone Wars">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>}}
[[File:Rex-vs-droidekas.png|thumb|right|280px|Droidekas fought against the clone army during the Clone Wars.]]
Before the [[battle droid]]-ground invasion of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]], [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan|apprentice]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] had to [[Escape from the Saak'ak|face]] droidekas on a [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|Droid Control Ship]]. During the [[Battle of Naboo]], droidekas cut a path through the [[Gungan]] formation with their deadly repeating blaster cannons and then shot the shield generators that the [[Fambaa]] were carrying.<ref name="Episode I" /> Later, they served as part of the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Ambush" /> Droidekas were also the subject of an article within the [[Republic Naval Institute]]-published ''[[Space Battles]]'' magazine.<ref name="ADE">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> After the [[Executive Separatist Council]] was massacred by the newly-anointed [[Sith apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], the droidekas were ordered to shut down.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

After the war, a droideka known as "[[The Unwinder]]," was champion in a [[Unidentified gladiatorial arena|gladiatorial arena]],<ref name="Always Bet on Chop">{{RebelsMagCite|23|Always Bet on Chop}}</ref> and another droideka was in a scrap pile operated by the [[Bidvel]] and [[Davil]], but was activated by, and was able to save, [[C1-10P]] from the two. When a [[B2-series super battle droid]] questioned what they should do now that they were safe, C1-10P suggested forming a [[Unidentified droid group|group]] to help other droids.<ref name="EtSP">{{RebelsMagCite|19|Escaping the Scrap Pile}}</ref> On the planet [[Koboh]], droidekas and other former Separatist forces were reprogrammed and used as soliders by the [[Bedlam Raiders]].<ref name="Survivor" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Droideka Oppressor]] and [[Droideka Sentinel]] variants were used by [[smuggler]]s, and were also used by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref> At least one droideka was used as a [[security droid]] by [[Quarantine World III]], and chased after [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] when she attempted to [[Theft of the Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix|steal]] the [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]].<ref name="Vader III" /> Aphra later reprogrammed a droideka mark one, [[Dek-Nil]], making him into a [[probability droid]].<ref name="Aphra 15" /> The [[Scarabi]], a [[mercenary]] group, used a droideka to guard one of their [[Safe House One|safe houses]].<ref name="Star Wars 46" />

==Behind the scenes==
When [[Doug Chiang]] designed the droideka in concept art, he did not do so with a walk cycle in mind; in his mind, the droideka was only able to move via rolling up and would stay in place when ready to fire, becoming a de facto weapons platform. However, [[George Lucas]] wanted the droideka to have the feel of a [[gunslinger]] thanks to its two large blasters one each side, and, thus, it was decided that the unit could indeed walk. Figuring out how to make the droideka unfurl and walk fell to [[Hal Hickel]] of [[Industrial Light & Magic]], who had a difficult time getting a walk cycle out of a design originally not meant to move in such a way.<ref name="Watto Ya Know">{{InsiderCite|225|Hal Hickel: Well, Watto Ya' Know?}}</ref>

A massive, [[Unidentified droideka (Zonus Corporation)|golden-plated droideka model]] was seen in the non-[[canon]] ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short "[[The Bounty Hunters]]," which was produced by [[WIT Studio]]<ref name="TBH">{{Visions|The Bounty Hunters}}</ref> and debuted on [[Disney+]] on [[October 29]], [[2025]]. The droideka is capable of being piloted by a human operator and was used as a guard by the [[Zonus Corporation]].<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=news/why-we-love-star-wars-visions|text=Why We Love the Unique Stories of ''Star Wars: Visions''}}</ref>
