"'''A Trip to the Country'''" is a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure outline in the [[September]] [[1996]] ''[[Shadows of the Empire Planets Guide]]'', published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire]]'' multimedia project. The adventure takes [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] characters on a [[Hunting/Legends|hunting]] expedition to [[Rodia/Legends|Rodia]] while attempting to finalize an important business deal with local [[Rodian/Legends|Rodians]].
