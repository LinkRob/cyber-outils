'''Cethis''' was a terrestrial astronomical object that was home to a settlement known as [[Haven (Cethis)|Haven]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. After the [[blight]] emerged on Cethis, the [[Jedi Order]] and [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[Mission to Cethis|evacuated]] Haven's citizens away to a settlement established on an [[Unidentified island (Cethis)|island]].<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
