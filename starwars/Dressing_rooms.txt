The '''dressing rooms''' were a series of rooms found within the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] where the contestants known as [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] could store all of their belongings, and prepare for demonstrations. The [[human]] Hunter [[Rieve]], for example, had a nook for her [[Clothing|costumes]], a wall rack for her [[lightsaber]]s, a medical table and wall-mounted [[medical droid]], a living space equipped with a kitchen area, and a dressing table and mirror screen that displayed the Hunters' current rankings (which Rieve had broken at some point out of frustration).<ref name="Kay" />

==Behind the scenes==
These dressing rooms were designed by concept artist [[Anna Kay]]<ref name="Kay" /> for the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''.<ref name="Hunters">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref> They were inspired by both [[Wikipedia:Broadway theatre|Broadway theatre]] and the [[Wikipedia:WWE|WWE]], intended to reveal elements of each Hunter's personality, showcasing their items, equipment, and other personalized aspects. The idea of shared dressing rooms were also considered, though the concept as a whole ended up going unused in the final version of the game.<ref name="Kay" />
