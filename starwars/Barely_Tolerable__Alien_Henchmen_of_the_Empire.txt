'''Barely Tolerable: Alien Henchmen of the Empire''' was a three-part<ref name="Pena on FB">{{Facebook|username=abelgpena|url=abelgpena/posts/10151638571077286|name=[[Abel G. Peña]]|date=2013-09-13|quote=The grand finale in my Galactic Empire aliens series with Rich Handley!|nobackup=1}}</ref> series of reference articles featured on the [[StarWars.com]] blogs. Written by [[Abel G. Peña]] and [[Rich Handley]], it is in the line of the ''[[Aliens in the Empire]]'' article that was written as an online supplement to [[Star Wars Insider 96|''Star Wars Insider'' 96]] and ''[[Star Wars Insider 97|97]]''. Part 1 was released on [[August 8]], [[2013]], Part 2 was released on [[August 22]], [[2013]] and Part 3 was released on [[September 12]], [[2013]].

It is also particularly notable as being the first source to briefly reference the events of ''[[Perfect Evil]]'', a manga that had been previously been listed under the Infinities label, regarding the character [[KkH'Oar'Rrhr]].
