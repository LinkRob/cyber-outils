"'''Dark Horse: Into the Future'''" is an article about the ''[[Star Wars]]'' comics published by [[Dark Horse Comics|Dark Horse]] after the release of the [[prequel trilogy]]. It was written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 116|''Star Wars Insider'' 116]]. The article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2013|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2013]].
