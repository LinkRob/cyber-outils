'''Anolo''' was a [[Muun]] and a [[Vice Chairman]] of the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] on [[Mygeeto]] during the [[Imperial Era]].

==Biography==
[[File:AnoloDeath-GalaxysEdge2.png|thumb|left|300px|Anolo lies unconscious and dies after being shot by Greedo]]
On [[Mygeeto]], [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] [[Mission to Mygeeto|sent enforcers]] to capture a [[Unidentified Givin codebreaker|Givin codebreaker]] whose brain was able to decode Imperial security codes. Anolo attempted to flee with the codebreaker, but was caught by [[Greedo]] and [[Takvaa]], two of Jabba's enforcers. In the ensuing fight, Anolo was shot in the chest and killed by Greedo.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 2" />
