The '''First Order military''' was the armed forces of the [[First Order]]. It consisted of the [[First Order Army]], the [[First Order Navy]], the [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]], and the [[First Order Special Forces|Special Forces]].

Its origins may have been from the surviving [[Imperial Military]] assets that escaped into the [[Unknown Regions]] during the [[Galactic Civil War|collapse]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], where the First Order built up a vast militarization program and developed a technologically superior military while in hiding.

Organized into formal military structures with established command hierarchies by [[First Order High Command]], along with [[Snoke]], who was the [[Supreme Leader]] until [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] killed him and took his place. The First Order fleet stood almost unchallenged due to the [[New Republic]]'s push for galactic disarmament.

==History==
===Origins===
{{Main|Imperial Military}}
{{Quote|We must now wonder if perhaps when the founders of the First Order escaped the known galaxy, they took the bulk of their surviving military equipment with them.|Beaumont Kin, The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire|<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
[[File:JakkuLitterbugs-TFAT2.png|thumb|left|250px|The forces of the First Order came from the surviving military assets that escaped into the Unknown Regions following the Galactic Civil War.]]
Over the years following the [[Battle of Jakku|defeat]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], former [[officer]]s, [[noble]]s, [[Scientist|technologists]] founded the [[First Order]], located in the far corners of the [[Unknown Regions]],<ref name="Before the Awakening">''[[Before the Awakening]]''</ref> and began rapidly rearming&mdash;intending to eventually reclaim [[the galaxy]] from the [[New Republic]]&mdash;in clear violation of the [[Galactic Concordance]] and Republic laws.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> Keeping its build up secret within the Unknown Regions,<ref name="POV 220">{{InsiderCite|220|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> military bases and [[shipyard]]s were constructed in those regions to churn out some of the technologically superior weapons and [[starfighter]]s utilized by the military, with many of the Old Empire's greatest secrets falling into the hands of the First Order.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

To avoid treaty restrictions banning the sale of weaponry to the First Order, galactic arms manufacturers [[BlasTech Industries]] and [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] spun off a subsidiary known as the [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]], which manufactured much of the First Order's weaponry within First Order [[Realspace|space]], while [[Sienar-Jaemus Army Systems]] and [[Aratech-Loratus Corporation]] provided spacecraft and vehicles to the Order.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> As the regime lacked the same resources of the Old Empire, the First Order defined its military focus on mobility and increased firepower per [[starship]].<ref name="POV 220" />

===The road to rearmament===
{{Quote|They're flying state-of-the-art TIEs, they're using commando boarding parties and latest generation attack shuttles in clear violation of the Galactic Concordance.|Poe Dameron|<ref name="Before the Awakening" />}}
[[File:Starkiller_Base_parade_grounds.png|thumb|right|250px|The First Order carried out a policy of rearmament in spite of the Galactic Concordance peace treaty.]]
In violation of New Republic laws regarding [[capital ship]]s, the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] was developed by [[Kuat-Entralla Engineering]]. It featured more than 1,500 [[turbolaser]]s, point-defense laser cannons and [[ion cannon]]s, and was specifically designed to evoke the fearful image of the Old Empire's [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> Although the First Order's military might had been suspected to be significantly large, not even General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] worst-case estimates conceived of just how large the First Order built up their military.<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> Part of the secret military build up involved the mining of [[dedlanite]] at [[Station Theta Black]] to create blasters.<ref name="Theta Black">{{Resistance|Station Theta Black (episode)}}</ref>

Thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Episode VII" /> one of the military's primary bases of operations was the [[Starkiller Base]], an [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] converted into a First Order stronghold. Utilizing it as their unofficial headquarters, the base was armed with a [[superweapon]] capable of destroying entire [[star system]]s by firing a planet-destroying beam through [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Starkiller DB">{{Databank|starkiller-base|Starkiller Base}}</ref> By then, the military was led by a triumvirate of [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] and [[Captain]] [[Phasma]].<ref name="Phasma DB">{{Databank|captain-phasma|Captain Phasma}}</ref>

===First Order-Resistance War===
====A new galactic war====
{{Quote|Today is the end of the Republic! The end of a regime that acquiesces to disorder! At this very moment, in a system far from here the New Republic lies to the galaxy while secretly supporting the treachery of the loathsome Resistance. This fierce machine which you have built, upon which we stand, will bring an end to the Senate! To their cherished fleet! All remaining systems will bow to the First Order! And will remember this&hellip; as the last day of the Republic!|Armitage Hux|<ref name="Episode VII" />}}
Shortly after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]], Starkiller Base was [[Battle of Starkiller Base|destroyed]] in a Resistance attack. Before its destruction, the Supreme Leader ordered Hux to evacuate the base and return to him with Ren.<ref name="Episode VII" />

[[File:ScorchZetaLeader-SWZ.png|thumb|left|250px|The First Order military struck back at the Resistance following the destruction of Starkiller Base.]]
A First Order fleet then attacked the Resistance base at D'Qar. Although their dreadnought, the ''[[Fulminatrix]]'', was destroyed, the First Order annihilated the base, destroyed the Resistance's entire bombing fleet, and forced them to flee to the [[Crait system]]. General Hux then used new [[hyperspace tracker]]s to confront the Resistance with their massive flagship, the ''[[Supremacy]]''. Attacks by Ren and his squad of TIE fighters destroyed the Resistance's starfighter squadrons, including Poe Dameron's own ''[[Black One]]'', and its entire leadership team with the exception of General Leia Organa. They also destroyed their medical frigate and the ''[[Ninka]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

Although former stormtrooper [[Finn]] and Resistance mechanic [[Rose Tico]] tried to deactivate the main hyperspace tracker on the ''Supremacy'', with the help of the thief [[DJ]], they were captured by First Order security bureau officers and stormtroopers. DJ later told General Hux about the Resistance's plan to flee in cloaked transports in exchange for money, who, upon which confirming the information, ordered the ''Supremacy'' to destroy the transports.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

However, Admiral [[Amilyn Holdo]] turned the cruiser [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] towards the ''Supremacy'' and made a hyperspace jump that cleaved the ship in half. This also stopped the execution of Finn and Rose, who later escaped on a stolen shuttle after defeating Captain Phasma and a few dozen stormtroopers. This sacrifice allowed the remaining Resistance transports to flee to the old Rebel outpost of Crait.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

[[File:FirstOrderAtCrait-TheLastJedi5.png|thumb|right|250px|In a display of military might, the First Order deployed its forces against the Resistance in the Battle of Crait.]]
Meanwhile, Ren brought the [[Force-sensitive]] scavenger [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] to [[Snoke]] in his Throne Room, upon which Snoke tried to get the location of [[Luke Skywalker]] out of her. Despite her resistance to his methods, including torture, Snoke ordered Ren to kill the restrained scavenger. However, Ren ignited Luke's lightsaber into Snoke's side and pulled it towards Rey, thus killing him and chopping his body in half. After defeating Snoke's guard, Rey rejected Ren's offer of ruling the galaxy together, instead trying to take Luke's lightsaber. The lightsaber split in two, but Rey recovered both parts and escaped the ''Supremacy''. Meanwhile, Ren declared himself new Supreme Leader after Force-choking Hux. He then ordered the First Order to assault the Resistance base on Crait.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

The First Order brought a massive [[superlaser siege cannon]] to destroy the massive door. Despite a desperate Resistance counterattack with ski-speeders, the door was destroyed. However, a [[Force projection]] of Luke Skywalker delayed First Order forces by confronting Ren, which allowed time for the Resistance to escape on the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Meanwhile, the [[Battle of Ikkrukk|Battle of Grail City]] began when a First Order [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|Maxima-A class heavy cruiser]] known as the ''[[Fortitude]]'' attacked the world of [[Ikkrukk]], which was caught in a civil conflict between [[Ikkrukk's government]] and [[Ikkrukk First Order sympathizers|local First Order sympathizers]]. [[Black Squadron]] engaged the First Order [[cruiser]] and its [[flight]] of TIE fighters, destroying the attacking forces and keeping the planet safe.<ref name="Poe Dameron 31">[[Poe Dameron 31|''Poe Dameron'' 31]]</ref>

====Final Order====
{{Quote|Forgive me, sir, but these allies on Exegol&hellip;they sound like a cult. Conjurers and soothsayers.''"<br />"''They've conjured legions of Star Destroyers. The Sith fleet will increase our resources ten-thousandfold. Such range and power will correct the error of Starkiller Base.|[[Domaric Quinn]], to Kylo Ren, and Enric Pryde, to Domaric Quinn|<ref name="Episode IX" />}}
[[File:Xyston-engines.png|thumb|left|250px|The Final Order was created as a fleet of warships that would enable the First Order to be a true Empire.]]
In 35 ABY,<ref name="35 ABY Date">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' to one [[Standard year|year]] after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[34 ABY]]. Therefore, the events of ''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'' must take place in [[35 ABY]].</ref> [[Darth Sidious]]&mdash;the architect and leader of the "Old Empire," who had returned from the dead via [[cloning]] a new body for his spirit<ref name="TROS novel">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>&mdash;issued a broadcast to the galaxy, vowing revenge. Supreme Leader Kylo Ren tracked Sidious to the hidden Sith world of [[Exegol]] where the Sith Lord and his [[Sith Eternal]] cultists had built a massive armada of [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] known as the [[Final Order]]. The Emperor offered the Sith fleet and his position to Ren in return for killing his granddaughter Rey.<ref name="Episode IX" />

Since Ren's flagship ''[[Finalizer]]'' had sustained damage over [[Batuu]], another Resurgent Star Destroyer [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] became Ren's flagship.<ref name="TROS VD" /> First Order military forces attempted to capture a Resistance team led by Dameron on two occasions at the [[Sinta Glacier Colony]] and [[Pasaana]]. Though they failed to prevent the Resistance from learning about the Emperor's return, they captured [[Chewbacca]] on Pasaana after sustaining several casualties. Later, Chewbacca's comrades include Finn, Poe, and Rey rescued him with the aid of the disaffected General Hux, who resented Ren's leadership.<ref name="Episode IX" />

[[File:The_Uprising_on_Jakku.jpg|thumb|right|250px|The Resistance victory on Exegol inspired the galaxy to overthrow the First Order's military rule.]]
After Ren returned to the [[light side of the Force]] and embraced his former identity as Ben Solo, [[Allegiant General]] [[Enric Pryde]] pledged his allegiance and the First Order to the Sith Lord. To demonstrate his loyalty, Pryde dispatched the ''Xyston''-class Star Destroyer ''[[Derriphan]]'' to [[Destruction of Kijimi|destroy]] the planet [[Kijimi]] as a show of military force. The Sith Eternal's [[Sith Eternal military|forces]] saw action against the Resistance and the [[Citizens' Fleet]] during the [[Battle of Exegol]], which saw the deaths of the Emperor and the redeemed Solo. The ''Steadfast'' along with the entire Sith armada was destroyed during the battle. Following the Battle of Exegol, various worlds, including [[Endor]], [[Jakku]], and [[Bespin]] [[Uprising against the First Order|rose]] up in rebellion against the First Order, leading to the destruction of several ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers.<ref name="Episode IX" />

==Organization==
===Overview and leadership===
{{Quote|We have 10,000 troops ready to fight.|Captain Phasma, during the war against the Resistance|<ref name="TLJ Season Trailer">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' : [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n0-ye4LXWts The Last Jedi Season Trailer]</ref>}}
[[File:CaptainPhasma-3-MayhewVariant_textless.png|thumb|left|250px|Kylo Ren and Captain Phasma were major leaders of the First Order's military might.]]
Although smaller than its predecessor, the Imperial Military, the First Order military's individual units and groupings nevertheless followed the same [[Order of Battle]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> By the time of the Battle of Crait, the First Order's army branch alone had 10,000 troops ready for combat.<ref name="TLJ Season Trailer" /> Overall command of the military fell to the Supreme Leader, with the likes of General Hux and Captain Phasma operating under the current head of state. The alien named Snoke commanded the might of the First Order until his execution at the hands of his own apprentice, Kylo Ren.<ref name="Episode VIII" /> Ren already exercised great control within the First Order<ref name="TCfT">{{Resistance|The Children from Tehar}}</ref> but was always subservient himself to Snoke. Following Snoke's execution, Ren assumed command of the First Order and its military forces despite Hux's objections.<ref name="Episode VIII" />

During his rule, Ren assembled the [[First Order Supreme Council]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In secret, Snoke had been a pawn of the resurrected Darth Sidious. Through Snoke, Sidious had controlled the First Order and its military.<ref name="Secrets of the Sith">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Sith]]''</ref> Following Ren's return to the light side of the Force, Allegiant General Pryde continued leading First Order forces from Ren's former flagship, the ''Steadfast''. Pryde himself, however, pledged his loyalty to Sidious,<ref name="Episode IX" /> cementing an alliance between the Sith Eternal and First Order.<ref name="Sidious BioGal28">{{Databank|darth-sidious-biography-gallery|Darth Sidious Biography Gallery}} {{C|Image 28 caption}}</ref> The Battle of Exegol, however, would see the destruction of the Sith Eternal and the ''Steadfast'', with Sidious and Pryde falling with each, respectively.<ref name="Episode IX" />

===Ground forces===
{{Main|First Order Army}}
{{Quote|Project Resurrection. Kidnapping children.''"<br />"''Rescuing. Forging them into an army the likes of which this galaxy has never seen.|[[Iden Versio]] and [[Gideon Hask]]|<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>}}
[[File:First_Order_Military.jpg|thumb|right|250px|First Order stormtroopers were modeled on the original stormtroopers that served the Galactic Empire.]]
Like its predecessor, the First Order employed [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]], including variants such as [[First Order snowtrooper|snowtroopers]]. It also utilized specialists including [[First Order heavy trooper|megablaster heavy assault troopers]], [[First Order flametrooper|flametroopers]] and [[First Order riot control stormtrooper|riot control stormtroopers]] and selectively had one of the three variants placed in every ten-member stormtrooper squad. Unlike the inconsistent academy standards of the previous Galactic Empire, training of the new generation of stormtroopers emphasized improvisation and counterinsurgency operations, including guerrilla tactics. With the First Order lacking the numerical superiority of the Galactic Empire, better-equipped and -trained soldiers resulted in higher trooper proficiency than the previous Galactic Empire. Soldiers were often trained on capital ships and underwent vivid simulations to create the most loyal soldiers, which was an extension of the ideas of [[Commandant]] [[Brendol Hux]] and his [[Commandant's Cadets]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

[[File:SuperlaserSiegeCanonAdvance-TLJ.png|thumb|left|250px|The First Order Army maintained the requisite vehicles and equipment necessary for military operations.]]
In addition to its stormtroopers and ground tactics like [[maneuver 0927]],<ref name="Pest Control">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=3|story=Pest Control}}</ref> the First Order utilized differing vehicles to help their infantry in various environments. For snowy terrain the Order deployed [[Light Infantry Utility Vehicle]]s and fast [[Speeder|snowspeeders]],<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> while they used [[125-Z treadspeeder bike]]s for desert environments.<ref name="Episode IX" /> More armored were the [[All Terrain Mobile Artillery]] used, for example, on Starkiller Base.<ref name="Episode VII" />

===Naval forces===
{{Main|First Order Navy}}
{{Quote|You will see how the First Order has created state-of-the-art TIE fighters, attack craft, and new classes of Star Destroyers that not only enforce its will in First Order space, but also threaten neutral worlds in the galactic borderlands.|Leia Organa|<ref name="The Last Jedi: Bomber Command">''[[The Last Jedi: Bomber Command]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Might_is_All.jpg|thumb|right|220px|The First Order Navy fielded next-generation Star Destroyers, TIE fighters, and other military vessels derived from the Imperial Navy.]]
The First Order Navy was mainly based on battle cruisers like its Imperial predecessor, with the Navy at its height constructing capital ships numbering well into the hundreds through [[Project Resurrection]]. ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers were the threatening capital ships deployed against the First Order's targets, but the Resistance believed that vessels even larger than them existed.<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" /> The First Order also used older ''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers and smaller ships compared to ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers, as [[Lancer-class frigate|''Lancer''-class frigates]], [[Maxima-A-class heavy cruiser|Maxima-A class heavy cruisers]], [[Dissident-class light cruiser|''Dissident''-class light cruisers]] and [[Nebulon-K frigate]]s.<ref name="Before the Awakening" />

For ground assaults the Order made a great use of [[AAL-1971/9.1 Troop Transport|Atmospheric Assault Landers]]; a hundred of them could be carried by ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers. They also used [[Upsilon-class command shuttle|''Upsilon''-class command shuttles]]. To achieve space superiority the Order deployed [[TIE series|TIEs]] in two variants: [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s and its two-seated version, the [[TIE/sf space superiority fighter]], this one used by the First Order's [[First Order Special Forces|Special Forces]].<ref name="TFA Visual Dictionary" />

===Other===

We will mobilize every bounty hunter and informant and turn over every rock to find ''all'' of them&hellip;and wipe every one of them out of existence!|Kylo Ren, to Armitage Hux|<ref name="Allegiance 1">[[Allegiance 1|''Allegiance'' 1]]</ref>}}
Although its military acted as the face of the First Order, proxy elements such as double agents, hired thugs, and spies helped to spread its influence to an even greater extent.<ref name="TROS VD" />
