The '''Abyssal Approach''' was a tunnel system underneath the surface of [[Shu-Torun]] that led to the [[Abyssal Rooms]]. [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and her [[Heroes of Yavin|team]] boarded [[Lord]] [[Or-Tar]]'s [[Starship|ship]] as it flew through the Abyssal Approach, in order to kidnap and impersonate the [[ore-duke]].<ref name="Star Wars 63" />
