'''''Colortivity''''' is a series of children's coloring and painting books published by [[Dreamtivity]]. Titles in the series cover a variety of franchises licensed to Dreamtivity<ref name="Colortivity">{{Dreamtivity|url=product/colortivity|text=Colortivity|archivedate=20200213083459}}</ref> including ''[[Star Wars]]''. Several books in the [[The Mandalorian publishing program|''The Mandalorian'' publishing program]], [[The Mandalorian: May the Force Be with You|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian: May the Force Be with You'']]<ref name="Edelweiss Force" /> and [[The Mandalorian: Good Luck with the Child|''Star Wars: The Mandalorian Colortivity: Good Luck with the Child'']],<ref name="Edelweiss Good Luck">{{Dreamtivity|url=books/star-wars-the-mandalorian-colortivity-good-luck-with-the-child-9781645883739|text=Star Wars The Mandalorian Colortivity: Good Luck with the Child}}</ref> were part of the Colortivity series.
