'''Ego''' was an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] officer who was stationed at an Imperial garrison on [[Cilpar]] and held the rank of [[Major/Legends|Major]] in [[4 ABY/Legends|4 ABY]]. When [[Vance Rego]], a [[Cilpari Resistance]] fighter who was [[Spy/Legends|spying]] for the Empire, learned where the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel Alliance]] operative "[[Winter Celchu|Targeter]]" was hiding, he informed his handlers, who dispatched Major Ego and a group of [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] to assist him in capturing Targeter. Major Ego and a group of [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]] took an injured [[Wes Janson/Legends|Wes Janson]] and the operative [[Winter Celchu|Winter]]&mdash;who, at the time, they believed to be [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]]&mdash;into custody and transported them to their headquarters at [[Kiidan]].

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Ego.jpg|thumb|right|The original version of [[Vance Rego]]'s dialogue, mentioning Major Ego.]]
The first and only mention of Major Ego occurred in the [[X-Wing Rogue Squadron 3|third part of ''The Rebel Opposition'' arc]], when Vance Rego betrayed Winter and Janson. However, this mention of the character was removed from the version of [[Star Wars: X-Wing Rogue Squadron: The Rebel Opposition|''The Rebel Opposition'']] published in [[Star Wars Omnibus: X-Wing Rogue Squadron Volume 1|''Omnibus: X-Wing Rogue Squadron Volume 1'']].
