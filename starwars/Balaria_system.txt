The '''Baleria system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It was situated in grid square M-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the origin point<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Balarian pie]]|system=Baleria [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> of a type of [[pie]], [[Balarian pie]].<ref name="DangerousBusiness-Subtitles">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}} {{Subtitles|Disney+}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Valeria indeed came from the plot summary mentioned; I suspect I checked it shortly after the episode aired, recorded it in my notes and never saw the correction. May delete it or it may live on.|Jason Fry|<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-09.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-09}}</ref>}}
[[File:StarWarsResistance-SeasonOne.jpg|thumb|right|220px|The Baleria system was created by Jason Fry in reference to Balarian pie, which was first introduced in ''Star Wars Resistance''{{'s}} first season.]]
The Baleria system was introduced as the Valeria system in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="AppendixOld">{{SW|full_url=https://cdnvideo.dolimg.com/cdn_assets/ff2066584bf86e5376fdfc3b26f0479b8795c403.pdf|text=Star Systems of the Galaxy|int=Star Systems of the Galaxy|oldversion=20251014003840}}</ref> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> Based on a misspelling, Fry created the system to serve as the origin point of<ref name="Fry" /> Balarian pie, introduced via mention in "[[Dangerous Business]],"<ref name="DangerousBusiness">{{Resistance|Dangerous Business}}</ref> the thirteenth<ref name="DVD">''[[Star Wars Resistance: Complete Season One]]''</ref> episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. The episode aired on [[January 20]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney Press">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref>

Following the appendix's release, Fry worked with contributors from the free-to-edit, unofficial online ''[[Star Wars]]'' encyclopedia known as [[Wookieepedia]] to confirm the intended origins of several star systems created for the appendix. One such system was the Baleria system&mdash;listed in the appendix's first version as Valeria system&mdash;which the author confirmed originated from<ref name="Fry" /> a [[January 21]], 2019 revision of the plot summary for "Dangerous Business" on Wookieepedia, which misspelled Balarian pie as "Valerian pie."<ref name="WookSummary">{{WookCite|url=Dangerous_Business?oldid=8056162|text=January 21, 2019 revision of the "Dangerous Business" article}}</ref> He further clarified that he had likely checked the plot summary shortly after it was posted and never updated his notes, indicating that the Valeria system may be removed from future versions of the appendix or may continue to remain present.<ref name="Fry" /> On the [[October 13]], [[2025]],<ref name="Substack2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> update to the appendix, the listing was renamed to the Balaria system.<ref name="Substack2" />
