'''Domed cities''' were [[City|cities]] that were contained within a [[Bio-cube|bio-dome]].<ref name="Children">{{TCW|Children of the Force}}</ref> [[Sundari]], along with other [[Mandalorian]] cities, was a domed city.<ref name="BOBF">[[The Book of Boba Fett Junior Novel|''The Book of Boba Fett'' Junior Novel]]</ref> The bio-domes on [[Mandalore]] allowed the city to exist on the harsh planet, which had been [[Annihilation|rendered]] a desert after [[Standard year|years]] of Mandalorian wars.<ref name="The Mandalore Plot">{{TCW|The Mandalore Plot}}</ref> Many of [[Rodia]]'s cities were also domed.<ref name="Children" />
