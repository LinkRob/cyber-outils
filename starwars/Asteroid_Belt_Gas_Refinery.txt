The '''Asteroid Belt Gas Refinery''' was a [[Clouzon-36]] processing refinery owned and maintained by the [[Mining Guild]]. During the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Spectres]] [[Heist at the Asteroid Belt Gas Refinery|raided]] the facility to steal [[fuel]] for [[Phoenix Cell]], blowing it up with the assistance of a pod of [[purrgil]], spacefaring creatures who fed on Clouzon-36 gas.<ref name="The Call" />
