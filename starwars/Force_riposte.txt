'''Riposte''' was a [[Force power|Force skill]] where practitioners utilized [[the Force]] to increase the effectiveness of non-critical strikes, temporarily turning what would generally be ineffective attacks from [[melee weapon]]s such as [[lightsaber]]s into crippling blows, though the user would then need to go on the defensive to restore their strength after pulling off this technique.<ref name="Jedi Challenges" />
