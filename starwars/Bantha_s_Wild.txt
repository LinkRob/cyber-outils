'''Bantha's Wild''' was a possible winning hand in the "[[Corellian Spike]]" variant of [[Sabacc]]. Amounting to zero, it included three cards of a kind (e.g., +4, +4, +4, -3, -9). The lower integer won (eg., three 4s beat three 5s).<ref name="Galaxy's Edge Deck">[[Sabacc (Galaxy's Edge)|Sabacc at ''Galaxy's Edge'']]</ref>
