'''Councillor''' was a title held by members of a council. The [[Alliance High Command]] was composed of multiple councillors.<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Ghorman]]'s capital [[city]] [[Palmo]] used city councillors as part of its governance, one being [[Carro Rylanz]].<ref name="Ever Been">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> [[Kith Alaytia]] &mdash; a former spy within the Imperial forces of the late [[Grand Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] &mdash; was a councillor within the [[New Republic]].<ref name="Star Wars 2025 1">[[Star Wars (2025) 1|''Star Wars'' (2025) 1]]</ref>
