'''Che Amanwe Papanoida''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Pantoran]] and a daughter of [[Baron]] [[Notluwiski Papanoida]], who served as [[Supreme Chairman]] of [[Pantora]].

==Biography==
[[File:CheAmanweBadass-SoI.png|thumb|left|200px|Che Amanwe uses an appropriated blaster to defend her family]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], Che Amanwe and her [[Gender|sister]] [[Chi Eekway Papanoida|Chi Eekway]] were [[Kidnapping of the Papanoida daughters|kidnapped]] by [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]] at their [[Coruscant]] home. He was aided by a [[Unidentified Gotal bounty hunter|Gotal]] [[Aide|assistant]], who grabbed an unsuspecting Papanoida, followed by her sister. The two were separated, and with the help of [[Pantoran]] [[Senator]] [[Riyo Chuchi]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], Chi Eekway was [[Rescue on the Pantora Droid Control Ship|rescued]] from [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] clutches.<ref name="Sphere of Influence" />
