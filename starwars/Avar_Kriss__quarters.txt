[[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] lived in a set of personal '''quarters''' in the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. In [[228 BBY]], as Kriss headed to her quarters to finish proposals for a [[Search for Marchion Ro|search]] for [[Nihil]] [[Eye of the Nihil|leader]] [[Marchion Ro]], she stopped and spoke with Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]]. Afterward, Kriss left the [[Jedi temple|temple]] to speak with the [[San Tekka clan]] member [[Cair San Tekka]] about the plague known as the [[blight]].

Later that [[Standard year|year]], Kriss and Mann&mdash;who had since become lovers&mdash;spent a [[Local day|night]] together in Kriss' bed. After [[Sleep|waking]] up the next [[Standard day|day]], both [[Jedi]] received a message from the [[Jedi High Council]] requesting them to participate in a [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|mission]] believed not to be survivable. Kriss and Mann agreed to take part in the mission, which they did not return from.

==Description==
A set of personal quarters in the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] was resided in by the [[human]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> The chamber had a smooth [[stone]] wall, a [[Viewport|window]], and a bed located within. Not far from Kriss' bed was a small and simple desk upon which the Jedi Master put her [[datapad]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==History==
[[File:PhaseIII-AvarArt.png|thumb|right|250px|Avar Kriss resided in the quarters.]]
During the [[High Republic Era]], Avar Kriss studied at the [[Jedi temple|Jedi Temple]] for over three [[Standard decade|decades]].<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Temptation Date">{{THRContent228BBY|TOTF}}</ref> she had come to live in the quarters.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Temptation Date" /> as Kriss headed to her quarters to finish her write-up for proposals for the [[Jedi]] to [[Search for Marchion Ro|search]] for [[Marchion Ro]]&mdash;the [[Eye of the Nihil|leader]] of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]&mdash;she encountered Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]], who asked her if she was going to her quarters. In response, Kriss confirmed she was and then informed him of her intent to finish the proposals to [[Nihil conflict|search]] for Ro. Following their conversation, however, Kriss left the temple to speak with the [[San Tekka clan]] member [[Cair San Tekka]] about the plague known as the [[blight]] after learning from Mann that the first sample of the blight the Jedi had received had been San Tekka's [[Dismemberment|severed]] [[Limb|hand]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

Later that year,<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|1}}</ref> after Kriss and Mann had become lovers, they began to spend as many [[Local day|nights]] as they could together. One [[Standard day|day]], Mann had been researching an esoteric and near-forgotten [[Unidentified Force technique|technique]] of the Force, with the two Masters ending the day together in Kriss' bed. The following morning, after Kriss [[Sleep|awoke]], her datapad received a message from the [[Jedi High Council]] to ask her to be part of a [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|mission]] to address the Blight and [[Shrikarai|Nameless]]&mdash;[[Living Force|Force]]-[[Diet|consuming]] creatures that the Nihil were using against the Jedi. After reading the message, her reaction woke Mann up.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

[[File:AvarElzar-TotF.png|thumb|left|280px|Avar Kriss (left) and Elzar Mann (right) spent a night together in Kriss' quarters before their mission.]]
The pair then conversed about the mission, with Kriss noting that it was not believed to be survivable. Afterward, Mann retrieved his own datapad, noting he did not have the same message. As Mann told Kriss that she had to accept the mission, the two looked at each other while the [[Coruscant Prime|sun]] glowed into Kriss' chamber. Mann's datapad then chimed, indicating he had received the message as well, with both Jedi then confirming their acceptance of the mission. Within [[second]]s, they both received another message with the location and timing of the mission's full briefing. After noting they did not have much [[time]], Mann told Kriss they should get ready, though Kriss did not want to do so yet and reached for him. Both Jedi attended the briefing and ultimately took part in the mission, from which they did not return, instead remaining on<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> the [[Wild Space]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> planet [[Sophros]] to hold back the spread of the Blight.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avar Kriss' quarters were first mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide}}</ref> The quarters later appeared in ''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]'', a [[2025]] novel written by [[Charles Soule]]<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" /> which was also part of the multimedia project's Phase III.<ref name="Phase" />
