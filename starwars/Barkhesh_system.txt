The '''Barkhesh system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Seitia sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western" /> It fell within grid square K-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and contained [[Barkhesh]], the [[homeworld]] of the [[human]] [[Antidar Williams]], a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Judicial]] who [[Death|perished]] in the [[Naboo system]] in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Naboo">{{EncyclopediaCite|The Heroes of Naboo and Other Characters of the Galaxy}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Barkhesh system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Barkhesh system/Legends|Barkhesh system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1998]] [[LucasArts]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']].<ref name="Rogue">[[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']]</ref>
