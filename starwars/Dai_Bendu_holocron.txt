'''Dai Bendu holocrons''' were [[Holocron|devices]] used to store tracts of the [[Dai Bendu]]. Although their function and appearance were similar to the [[Jedi holocron]]s, unlike them, they could be accessed even by the most unskilled [[Force-sensitive]]s. Each Dai Bendu holocron contained six tracts, subdivided into four lessons apiece.<ref name="UP" />
