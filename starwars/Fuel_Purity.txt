'''Fuel Purity''' was a sector within the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Bureau of Standards]]. The disgraced [[Tac-Corpo|corpo]] [[Syril Karn]] was hired to fill an immediate vacancy in Fuel Purity<ref name="Announcement" /> in [[5 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' dates the events of "[[Announcement]]" to [[5 BBY]].</ref> after his influential [[Gender|uncle]] [[Harlo]] arranged for him to attend an interview with the functionary [[Flob]] at the bureau's [[Imperial Bureau of Standards Coruscant office|office]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. In his new<ref name="Announcement" /> mediocre office job,<ref name="Space Moms">{{InsiderCite|221|Space Moms}}</ref> Karn sat in a cubicle pod in the office and monitored two screens of data.<ref name="Announcement" /> He earned a promotion soon after starting after the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] gave him a "clean bill of health" following an interrogation.<ref name="Listening">{{Andor|Nobody's Listening!}}</ref>
