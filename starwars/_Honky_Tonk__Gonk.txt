'''"Honky Tonk" Gonk''' was a [[GNK-series power droid]] who served the [[Galactic Republic]], and was assigned to [[Colonel]] [[Meebur Gascon]] to be part of [[D-Squad]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Insider" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Honky Tonk" Gonk is featured in a [[Canon|non-canonical]] section of [[Launchpad]] in [[Star Wars Insider 206|''Star Wars Insider'' 206]] that was written by [[Jamie Cosley]].<ref name="Insider" />
