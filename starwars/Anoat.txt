'''Anoat''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Anoat system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was once a sprawling [[ecumenopolis|city-world]] that, as seat of the [[Nothoiin]] [[Noble Court]], was the [[capital]] of the [[Anoat sector]]. However, after being [[Gassing of Anoat|gassed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]], the planet was polluted and largely abandoned.

==Description==
Anoat was connected to the planet [[Sullust]] by the [[Incisor Sidestep]] [[hyperspace route]] and the [[Ring of Kafrene]] [[mining]] colony by the [[Corellian Trade Spine]], which also continued on from Anoat out toward [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==History==
{{Quote|This wasn't an accident. This was the Empire's doing.''"<br />"''Come on. The Empire wouldn't&mdash;I mean, this was years before Alderaan and the Death Star&hellip;''"<br />"''Now you understand why our Rebellion keeps fighting.|Tam Bastion and a young smuggler|<ref name="Uprising" />}}
[[File:Anoat_City.png|thumb|left|250px|Anoat was a city-sprawled planet.]]
Once a sprawling [[city]]-[[Planet|world]], Anoat served as the [[capital]] of the entire [[Anoat sector]]. However, [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Uprising" /> [[Captain]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]] was sent to deal with a base of [[Anoat dissidents|dissidents]] on Anoat. He chose to have his forces [[Gassing of Anoat|gas]] the entire planet,<ref name="Jakku">[[Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising 1|''Battle of Jakku — Insurgency Rising'' 1]]</ref> and the [[atmosphere]] became toxic and [[poison]]ous, forcing much of the population to flee from the polluted world. Some people, however, did not leave the planet. Those who did not [[Death|die]] were driven insane by the toxins, evolving over time into savage and feral creatures referred to as "[[Lurker (Anoat)|Lurkers]]." During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the planet was under the control of the [[Galactic Empire]] and contained the sector's largest concentration of [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]].<ref name="Uprising" />

The [[Resistance]] maintained a [[Resistance Camp|camp]] on Anoat during its [[First Order-Resistance War|fight]] against the [[First Order]], which could not detect the rebels with long-range sensors thanks to the pollution on the world. Sometime after the [[Battle of Crait]], General [[Jonato]] warned that a transmission from the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] world of [[Tah'Nuhna]] threatened to expose the Resistance presence on Anoat, but he was overruled by [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]].<ref name="Allegiance 1" />
