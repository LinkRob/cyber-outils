'''Dorius Drevin''' was a [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] who accepted a [[Bounty/Legends|bounty]] on the [[Sullustan/Legends|Sullustan]] [[Loro Ecls]]. Posing as a pilgrim of the [[Fellowship of Kooroo]], Drevin joined a group lead by [[Honarius Hanast]] who hired a group of [[free-trader]]s to take them to [[Gelgelar Free Port/Legends|Gelgelar Free Port]]. On [[Gelgelar/Legends|Gelgelar]], Drevin attempted to kill Ecls with a bomb but was stopped by the same group of free-traders.
