A '''skirmish''' took place in a [[Unidentified Nightbrother warehouse|Nightbrother warehouse]] in a [[Nightbrother village]]. The robbery was planned by [[Arzo Suun]], a run-away [[Nightbrothers|Nightbrother]], as an effort gain medical supplies, basic technology, and [[Comm unit|long-range comms system]] for his former village leader [[Mak Eak]]. Inside the building, Mak Eak met with fellow Nightbrother [[Ruul]]. While stealing the supplies, they were caught by the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Nelsel]] when she sensed their presence through [[the Force]].<ref name="Collapse" />
