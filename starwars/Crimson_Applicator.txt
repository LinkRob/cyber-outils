The '''Crimson Applicator'''<ref name="Hunters" /> was a long-range [[dart gun]]<ref name="Reveal" /> owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Applicator|standard applicator]]. This applicator featured a [[Color|red]], black, white, and silver color scheme.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Crimson Applicator was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Skora's Underworld Applicator, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Crimson Surgeon]].<ref name="Hunters" />
