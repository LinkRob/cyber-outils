'''Elishe''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Pantoran]] individual who served as a [[Jedi Master|Master]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the later [[High Republic Era]]. She was the Master of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Yord Fandar]].<ref name="Crystal Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> Elishe and Fondor, together with Master [[Sol]] and his Padawan [[Jecki Lon]], went [[Mission to Siline|on a mission to clean up]] a [[Politics|political]] situation on the [[planet]] [[Siline]].<ref name="Crystal Crown" />

==Personality and traits==
Elishe was a Pantoran with dark blue skin, vivid golden [[eye]]s and pastel blue [[hair]]. She had delicate golden [[tattoo]]s on her cheeks and she had her hair pulled back in a high slouchy tail. In contrast to her stern Padawan, Elishe had a more light-hearted and fun personality.<ref name="Crystal Crown" />
