The '''Filar-Nitzan''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] in [[the galaxy]]. The [[High Republic Era]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Nubarron]], who looked like a [[Color|pink-and-purple]] [[cloud]] shot through with lightning, refused to identify their [[Nubarron's species|species]], leading to speculation they were a member of a previously unknown race of Filar-Nitzan.<ref name="OOTS" />

In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[Mirialan]] [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] mentally recalled the speculation regarding Nubarron's species, including the possbility of them being an unknown race of Filar-Nitzan, as she spoke with the [[Jedi]] Master on the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]].<ref name="OOTS" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Filar-Nitzan were mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="OOTS" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Chronological Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Filar-Nitzan/Legends|Filar-Nitzan]] originated in the [[1995]] [[West End Games]] roleplaying sourcebook ''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]'', where they were a [[Gas/Legends|gaseous]] [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] [[Species/Legends|species]].<ref name="Alliance Intelligence Reports">''[[Alliance Intelligence Reports]]''</ref>
