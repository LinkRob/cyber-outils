'''Fadros Angon''' was a [[human]] who held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[major]] in the [[Resistance]]. A former [[botanist]], Angon had to focus on his duties while at the Resistance [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|base]] on [[Ajan Kloss]] and not be distracted by the wealth of [[plant]] life. Angon served as a [[scout]], searching the perimeters for samples that could be used in equipment, [[Foodstuff|food]], or medicine.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==History==
A former [[botanist]] and member of the Resistance High Command, Major Fadros Angon was on Ajan Kloss where he participated in the preparations for the [[Battle of Exegol|assault]] on [[Exegol]].
