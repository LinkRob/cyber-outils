The '''aviation regulator''' was a component of the [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter|TIE striker]], a streamlined variant of the [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] [[starfighter]]s used by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Edelweiss">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=1942556411&page=1|text=Rogue One: A Star Wars Story: The Official Visual Story Guide}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TIE_Striker_Plan.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The TIE striker plan created for ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story: The Official Visual Story Guide''.]]
The aviation regulator was first revealed on [[May 17]], [[2016]], in a preview for ''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story: The Official Visual Story Guide'', a canceled reference book for the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|film]] ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Edelweiss" />
