"'''Envoy to the Queen'''" is an adventure seed contained in the ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]''. The players are caught between the factions on a neutral planet, and must retrieve a ring to prevent a disaster. Possible alternate storylines that could be adapted by a gamemaster were also included. It was written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]].
