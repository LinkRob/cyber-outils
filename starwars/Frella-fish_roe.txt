'''Frella-fish roe''' was an ingredient made from the [[Egg|roe]] of a [[frella fish]]. When whipped, the roe could be turned into a [[cream]]y [[meringue]] with stiff, [[cloud]]like peaks, but for most [[species]], eating too much of it dulled one's senses and put them in a happy, dazed state.<ref name="FACPOV" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">"[[But What Does He Eat?]]" takes place concurrently with {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Ugnaught]] [[sous-chef]] [[Gersolik]] perfectly prepared frella-fish roe by whipping it into the appropriate peaks for her head [[chef]], the [[Devaronian]] [[Torro Sbazzle]], while working in [[Cloud City]] of the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]]. When Sbazzle asked if she was finished, wanting to fold the meringue into a curd, Gersolik confirmed so but trailed off, her mind elsewhere, leading the Devaronian to think that her sous-chef had consumed too much of the roe when tasting it.<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Behind the scenes==
Frella-fish roe appeared in "[[But What Does He Eat?]]," a short story authored by [[S.A. Chakraborty]] and published in the [[2020]] anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="FACPOV" />
