The '''Belgaroth asteroid field''' was an [[asteroid field]]<ref name="Droidography">''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''</ref> located in the [[Belgaroth system]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> The field was known to have [[ore]] and to employ [[DLC-13 mining droid]]s to extract it. At some point, during the [[Imperial Era]] the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR]] visited the field and wrote on his autobiography ''[[From Trenches to Wrenches: The Roger Story]]'' that the field was a horrible [[hutt]] hideaway.<ref name="Droidography" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Rancors_Fist_TMOG.jpg|200px|thumb|The ''[[Rancor's Fist]]'' in the Belgaroth asteroid field]]
The Belgaroth asteroid field first appeared in the ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season One|Season One]] episode "[[The Mines of Graballa]],"<ref name="Mines of Graballa">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Mines of Graballa}}</ref> but was later [[canon]]ized in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]''.<ref name="Droidography" /> In the episode, the asteroid belt is where [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]] the [[Hutt]] had mines and where [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]] discovered one of the [[crystal]]s for the [[Kyber Saber]].<ref name="Mines of Graballa" />
