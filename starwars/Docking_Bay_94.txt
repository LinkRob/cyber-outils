'''Docking Bay 94''' was a [[Mos Eisley]] [[Hangar|docking bay]] that was used by [[Han Solo]] during at least one of his visits to [[Tatooine]]. The ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was docked here when [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[R2-D2]], and [[C-3PO]] came to [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] to find passage to [[Alderaan]] in order to deliver the secret [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[Death Star plans|plans]] to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]. It was there that they found Solo and his [[First Mate]] [[Chewbacca]], and booked passage aboard his [[Starship|ship]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

Moments after Kenobi, Skywalker, R2-D2, and C-3PO arrived and boarded the ''Falcon'', a [[squad]] of [[stormtrooper]]s arrived and immediately opened fire on the ship. Han was able to fire a few shots before running up the ''Falcon''{{'s}} gangway, yelling for Chewbacca to launch the ship. The ''Falcon'' barely escaped the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] and headed off into [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Episode IV" />

By [[3 ABY]], [[Kay Vess]] came to meet [[Hoss]] before going to Chalmun's Cantina.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
