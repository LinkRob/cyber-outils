The '''Battle of Molopolis''' was a battle on the [[city]] [[Molopolis]] on [[Molos]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and the [[Galactic Empire]]. Following a civilian [[uprising on Molos]], [[King]] [[Amand]] and his [[Molos army|army]] fought against the [[Imperial Army]]'s reinforcements. Ultimately, King Amand and the Rebel Alliance were victorious.<ref name="Lead" />
