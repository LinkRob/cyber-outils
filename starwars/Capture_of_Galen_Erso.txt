In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> renowned [[scientist]] [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]] was '''captured''' by [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]], [[Director]] of the [[Imperial Military Department of Advanced Weapons Research]] agency, and his personal [[squad]] of [[death trooper]]s. Krennic had previously manipulated Erso into researching [[crystallography]] for [[Project Celestial Power]], a scientific program ostensibly meant to promote sustainable energy. In reality, the ambitious [[Imperial officer]] sought Erso's [[Synthetic kyber crystal|synthetic]] [[kyber crystal]]s for a [[Project Stardust|secret enterprise]]&mdash;the construction of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a [[planet]]-destroying [[superweapon]]. Erso, upon learning the true nature of his work, fled the project and hid [[Erso family|his family]] on the [[Farm|agrarian]] world of [[Lah'mu]] with the help of the [[Partisans|rebel]] [[Saw Gerrera]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

In Erso's absence, work on the weapon's crystal-powered [[superlaser]] began to stagnate, and Krennic knew he would need to recover the valuable scientist for any hope of success. Eventually, Krennic traced Erso to Lah'mu<ref name="Rogue One" /> and ventured there aboard a [[Star Destroyer]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> Traveling to the surface aboard his [[ST-149|personal]] [[Delta-class T-3c shuttle|''Delta''-class T-3c shuttle]], Krennic was joined by his [[Krennic's guard squadron|guard squadron]]. The Ersos, composed of Galen, his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Lyra Erso|Lyra]], and their young daughter [[Jyn Erso|Jyn]], were surprised by the sudden [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] presence, but were well-prepared with a plan to save at least Jyn from capture. Krennic landed a short distance from the [[Erso homestead]], prompting the family to contact Gerrera with news of his appearance. Lyra guided their child to a secret bunker while Krennic and his [[trooper]] escorts approached. Galen confronted the [[cloak]]ed [[Director]] alone and attempted to convince him that Lyra had [[Death|died]], or alternatively that his mind had weakened. Krennic, acute in his own right, dismissed the lies and insisted that Erso return to work on the project. Lyra appeared, producing a [[blaster]] in an effort to prevent Galen's abduction, but she was executed in the encounter. When Krennic's troopers were unable to find Jyn in the surrounding area, the Imperials settled for Galen as their single [[prisoner]]<ref name="Rogue One" /> and took him aboard the [[destroyer]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" />
