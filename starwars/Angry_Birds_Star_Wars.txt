'''''Angry Birds Star Wars''''' was a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] released for [[Wikipedia:iOS|iOS]], [[Wikipedia:Android (operating system)|Android]], [[Wikipedia:Kindle Fire|Amazon Kindle Fire]], [[Wikipedia:Mac (computer)|Mac]], [[Wikipedia:Personal computer|personal computer]], and [[Wikipedia:Windows Phone|Windows Phone]] on [[November 8]], [[2012]].<ref name=".com" /> The game features various characters that resemble ''[[Star Wars]]'' characters, such as [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]], [[Princess/Legends|Princess]] [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa]], [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]], [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]], [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]], [[Tusken Raider/Legends|Tusken Raiders]], [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]], and other characters. It is a game in the popular [[Wikipedia:Angry Birds|''Angry Birds'']] app series, and the second based on another franchise.<ref name="USAtoday">{{WebCite|url=https://eu.usatoday.com/story/tech/2012/10/05/angry-birds-meets-star-wars/1613941|text=Star Wars invades Angry Birds|author=Snider, Mike|date=2012-10-08|archivedate=20191003145051}}</ref>

On [[October 29]], [[2013]], the game was released on gaming consoles.<ref name="NBCNews" /> Along with ''[[Angry Birds Star Wars II]]'', Angry Birds Star Wars was discontinued in March [[2020]].<ref name="Thank you" />

==Publisher's summary==
''A long time ago in a galaxy far, far away... a group of desperate rebel birds faced off against a galactic menace: the Empire's evil Pigtroopers!''

''Rebel birds, striking from a hidden base, have won their first victory against the evil Imperial Pigs. During the battle, Rebel spies managed to steal secret plans to the Empire's ultimate weapon, the PIG STAR, and are racing to deliver the plans to the Rebel birds. Now they need your help!''

''Join an epic adventure with the Angry Birds in the legendary ''Star Wars'' universe! Use the Force, wield your Lightsaber, and blast away Pigtroopers on an intergalactic journey from the deserts of Tatooine to the depths of the Pig Star -- where you'll face off against the terrifying Darth Vader, Dark Lord of the Pigs! Can you become a Jedi Master and restore freedom to the galaxy?''

==Development==
===Closure===
After a message published in early February [[2020]]<ref name="Forum">{{WebCite|url=https://www.angrybirdsnest.com/forums/topic/rio-and-star-wars-ending-support|text=Rio and Star Wars ending support|date=2020-02-04|work=angrybirdsnest.com|archivedate=20211104164052}}</ref>, the game closed its ingame shop and advertising in March 2020, and was removed from app stores. Player support continued to be provided until the end of 2020.<ref name="Thank you" />

==Legacy==
On [[July 15]], [[2013]], ''[[Angry Birds Star Wars II]]'' was announced.<ref name="SW-Join the Pork Side in Angry Birds Star Wars II">{{SW|url=news/join-the-pork-side-in-angry-birds-star-wars-ii|text=Join the Pork Side in ''Angry Birds Star Wars II''|nolive=1}}</ref> It was released on [[September 18]], 2013.<ref name="SW-Angry Birds Star Wars II Now Available">{{SW|url=news/angry-birds-star-wars-ii-now-available|text=Angry Birds Star Wars II Now Available|nolive=1}}</ref>
