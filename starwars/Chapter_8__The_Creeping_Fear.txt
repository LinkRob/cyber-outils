"'''Chapter 8: The Creeping Fear'''" is the eighth episode of the television series ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]]'' and was released on [[April 27]], [[2026]] alongside "[[Chapter 7: Call to Oblivion]]". Its title was revealed on [[March 17]], 2026 by the official ''[[Star Wars]]'' Twitter account.<ref name="Twitter" />

==Official description==
''A hasty escape plan proves dangerous, while an isolated Maul regroups.''

==Plot summary==
===Under the Imperial boot===
Following the cave-in, [[Maul]] plunges into the depths of the cave system near the waterfall. [[Marrok]] and the [[Eleventh Brother]] survive the cave-in and emerge from the rocks and debris. They are picked up by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] (LAAT/i) gunship. Other gunships circle the battle-scarred bridge. [[Spybot]] circles the waterfall. Meanwhile, Lady [[Rook Kast]], [[Icarus]], [[Looti Vario]] and the surviving [[Mandalorian]]s gather in the tunnels near the bridge. Unable to find Maul, they decide to leave the sector and hope that Maul finds them. She puts a consoling hand on the grieving Icarus, who has lost his brother [[Scorn]] at the hands of the [[Inquisitor]]s. 

Elsewhere, Maul finds himself in an underground tunnel. Staring at a reflection of himself in a pool, he says he hates himself. Due to his damaged mechanical legs, Maul struggles to walk and to navigate through the rocks. He is haunted by memories from his dark past. 

At the [[Janix Civil Defense HQ]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Blake]] ushes [[Rylee Lawson]] into an interrogation room. Rylee tells the [[Imperial officer]] that he told him everything but Blake doesn't believe him and says that the Inquisitor will get answers out of him. Shortly later, the Eleventh Brother enters the interrogation room through another door. Rylee says that he doesn't know anything but the cloaked Inquisitor continues his advance. 

===The rescue plan===
Outside the building, [[Brander Lawson]] tells his Jedi allies [[Eeko-Dio Daki]] and [[Devon Izara]] that he is growing impatient and wants to rescue his son Rylee. Shortly later, his [[comlink]] buzzes again. Branden's underworld contact [[Rheena Sul]] tells him that she has found a [[Rheena Sul's transport|transport]] to escape offworld but that it would leave in two [[standard hour|hour]]s. Brander tells her to give them more time before switching off the comlink. He tells the Jedi that they will proceed with his rescue plan. 

Inside the building, Blake leads a squad of [[TK stormtrooper]]s. They walk past [[2B0T]] ("Two Boots") who offers the Imperial officer a mug but Blake ignores him. After the Imperials pass, Blake encounters [[Tactical Defense Force]] [[Officer]] [[Wade]], who remarks that the droid is acting out of protocol. Two Boots responds that the Imperials are acting out of protocol and insists on speaking with [[Chief]] [[Klyce]]. Wade reveals that Klyce is dead. When Two Boots says that standard protocol is for the chief to be suspended while under investigation, Wade counters that the Imperials were never going to hold an investigation. 

Shortly later, Rylee is escorted down the corridor by a pair of stormtroopers. Two Boots looks with concern before answering his comlink. He apologizes for not returning his call and proceeds to update Brander about Rylee in Imperial custody. Before Two Boots can prattle about protocol, Brander tells him to tell the Imperials that he is willing to trade his son's freedom for himself. When Two Boots says this is not standard protocol, Brander replies that he is more concerned about doing the right thing and tells Two Boots to bring Rylee to loading dock three. 

Following the comlink conversation, Izara opines that Brander's plan is terrible while [[Jedi Master|Master]] Daki warns him that Two Boots has betrayed him once before. Brander concedes that his plan is highly risky but believes this is the only way. While avoiding an Imperial [[All Terrain Defense Pod]] (AT-DP) walker near the police precinct, Brander hands Master Daki a [[code cylinder]] containing the coordinates to Sul's ship. He tells Daki to make sure that Rylee gets onboard. Daki promises to look after Rylee and sends Izara to obtain a getaway vehicle. Putting the plan into action, Two Boots enters Rylee's cell and tells the teenager that he is rescuing him. 

===Maul's tribulation===
While walking through the tunnels, Maul is tormented by a manifestation of the Eleventh Brother, who taunts him. Through the fog, Maul experiences a flashback of his brother [[Savage Opress]] attempting to warn a younger Maul not to go with a cloaked [[Darth Sidious]]. The younger Maul ignores him, prompting Opress to ask the [[Sith Lord]] what he has done to his brother. Maul then experiences another flashback of training in lightsaber combat as a [[Sith apprentice]] under Sidious, who punishes the adolescent by striking him with [[Force lightning]].

Maul then experiences a flashback of being cut in half by Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] before ending up as a cyborg with a spidery form on the junkyard world of [[Lotho Minor]]. Maul is then rediscovered by his brother Opress. Maul then experiences a flashback of Darth Sidious killing Opress in lightsaber combat on [[Mandalore]]. The apparition of Opress tells Maul to seek revenge. Maul mourns the loss of his home, family and brother and blames Darth Sidious for the trauma and deaths of loved ones that he suffered. 

===Rescuing Rylee===
Meanwhile, Izara eyes a fast [[speeder]] but is confronted by the owners, three rough-looking blue-skinned [[humanoid]]s. She uses her [[The Force|Force powers]] and combat training to take them out. Izara steals their speeder and exits the parking lot. Two Boots escorts Rylee through the corridors and pretends to be taking him for processing. Reaching the exit to loading dock three, the two are met by two stormtrooper sentries. Two Boots claims he is taking the prisoner for transfer. When one of the stormtroopers proceeds to contact his superiors for confirmation, the droid stuns him with a [[blaster]] and knocks out his comrade. Two more stormtroopers arrive to investigate the commotion at the door but Two Boots also stuns them.

Two Boots and Rylee enter loading dock three where they reunite with Brander and Daki. Brander hugs his son and thanks Two Boots. When Two Boots asks how he knew it was him despite him lacking his unique boots, Brander replies that he would know that it was him any day. Brander tells Rylee to follow Daki while he surrenders himself to the Imperials. When the alarms ring and stormtroopers rush out, Two Boots shoots two of the approaching stormtroopers and tells Brander that the "Deal is off." 

The group flees as stormtroopers pursue them out of the building. Two stormtroopers on [[speeder bike]]s attempt to block their escape but Izara arrives in her stolen speeder and rams one of the speeder bikes. She then swipes the second speeder bike and its rider into the path of several stormtroopers. The four flee with Izara in her airspeeder as an AT-DP walker and more stormtroopers on speeder bikes arrive. Izara takes the speeder through an underground highway. Stormtroopers on speeder bikes give chase but Brander shoots two of of the pursuers.

Daki expresses disagreement with his Padawan for not choosing an inconspicuous vehicle. Izara responds that she selected the vehicle due to its powerful rear thrusters, which she accelerates to escape three more speeder bikes. Brander warns that the speeder can't outrun an LAAT/i gunship which strafes them. To avoid the gunship, Izara plunges the speeder down a barrier into another highway. The three mounted stormtroopers give chase but one of them hits a road structure. The other two continue the pursuit. Daki uses the Force to shove one of the speeder bikes and to hurl the second vehicle and its rider into the path of an LAAT gunship blocking their path, taking out the two pursuers. Izara jets past the stricken gunship.

===Resolve and reunion===
Elsewhere, the weakened Maul falls face first into a pool of water due to his malfunctioning mechanical legs. He speaks to an apparition of his younger self, who apologizes for following Darth Sidious. Maul accepts his younger self's apology and vows not to let Darth Sidious groom and exploit anyone else. Mustering his remaining strength, Maul continues his journey through the tunnel. 

Meanwhile, Spybot returns from a scouting mission and tells Kast that he has seen no sign of Maul. Kast and the surviving Shadow Collective members have regrouped in a derelict part of Janix city. Vario expresses impatience at waiting for Maul but is startled when Maul emerges out of an alleyway. 

===Springing the trap===
After abandoning their stolen speeder, the Jedi and the Lawsons head towards their rendezvous point with Sul. Izara apologizes to Rylee for failing to protect him. Rylee says that it is not her fault because he allowed himself to be captured by the Empire. He tells Izara that he was questioned by a second Inquisitor, who demanded to know the whereabouts of the Jedi fugitives. Rylee says that he didn't know anything and admits that he could have broken under interrogation. Izara reassures him that fear is natural but says that they cannot allow it to control them. 

Brander tells the younglings to hurry as they approach a warehouse. After entering the building, they take the lift down to their destination. As the lift descends, Izara clutches [[Devon Izara's lightsaber|her lightsaber]] and says that she senses they are walking into a trap. Daki expresses agreement. Brander responds that they have nowhere else to go and says that Rheena dislikes the Empire as well and would never double cross them. Izara suggests that Sul was coerced by the Empire into luring them into a trap. Daki agrees with his Padawan while Rylee suggests his father contact Sul via [[comlink]]. 

A nervous Sul tells Brander that she is alright and says the [[transport]] will get them to [[Catalor]]. Based on her nervous voice and choice of words, Brander suspects she had been compromised. Two Boots attempts to stop the lift but the controls have been overridden. Below, a nervous Sul watches as Imperial stormtroopers led by Marrok, the Eleventh Brother and Lieutenant Blake converge around the lift bay. When the lift lands, the Imperials discover that the fugitives have sliced a hole through the roof of the lift. Just as Blake starts to question where the fugitives are, Sul grabs a blaster from a stormtrooper and starts a gunfight. 

When she has her sights on Blake, she instead fires into one of the rear thrusters of her transport, causing an explosion that takes out Blake and several stormtroopers. The Inquisitors duck as the blast approaches the lift bay. The Lawsons, Jedi and Two Boots watch the explosion from a walkway above and flee.

===A crimson dawn===
Elsewhere, Maul and his followers reach [[Nico Deemis]]' [[Nico Deemis' headquarters|headquarters]]. Kast is afraid that the Empire will find them her but Vario reassures her that Deemis is dead because he killed him. He says that the Empire will be searching his office but not Deemis. Maul watches the window while Vario picks up an encoded call on his [[transmitter]]. Vario informs Maul that he has been contacted by a representative of the [[Crimson Dawn]], whose leader [[Dryden Vos]] requests an audience with Maul. He reassures Maul that things are "looking up."
