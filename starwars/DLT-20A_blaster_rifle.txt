The '''DLT-20A blaster rifle''', also known as the '''DLT-20A laser rifle''', was a model of [[blaster rifle]] and [[longblaster]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]. It was equipped with several operational modes, including a rapid-fire option and [[sniper rifle]] capabilities. The DLT-20A saw usage by several groups throughout its history, including the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and outlaws.

==Description==
Produced by [[BlasTech Industries]],<ref name="RVG" /> the DLT-20A blaster rifle was a model of [[blaster rifle]]<ref name="HC2" /> and [[longblaster]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref> It was also identified as the DLT-20A laser rifle,<ref name="RVG" /> although [[Laser rifle|Laser Rifles]] were a distinct weapon from [[blaster]]s.<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> DLT-20As also earned the nickname of the "twenties." As was generally the case with blaster rifles, the DLT-20A had a greater recoil for the user than the average [[blaster pistol]] and, if held too close to the individual's face, could leave severe burns. The DLT-20A also came with several extra operational modes, including a rapid fire sequence<ref name="Twilight Company" /> and [[Sniper|sniping]] capabilities. DLT-20As fired with either [[Color|red]]<ref name="Who Are You">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref> or green blaster bolts.<ref name="DLT-20Card">{{Legion|set=Rebel Troopers Upgrade Expansion|cardname=DLT-20A Trooper}}</ref> Another type of [[rifle]], the [[pulse cannon]], utilized the frame assembly of a DLT-20A,<ref name="BF">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref> due to being a modified DLT-20A blaster rifle with enhanced range, accuracy, and firepower.<ref name="Secrets Bounty Hunters">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]''</ref> 

==History==
{{Quote|One transmit five. One calling five.''"<br/>"''Five online. Standing by.''"<br/>"''Fire at will. Repeat. Fire at will.|Captain Kaido provides orders to a DLT-20A-equipped sniper|<ref name="Who Are You" />}}
[[File:WhoAreYou-Sniper.png|thumb|right|300px|A DLT-20A-equipped Imperial sniper on Ghorman takes aim at fellow Imperials, knowing that those below will assume it is the Ghormans who shot first.]]
The DLT-20A had been introduced by BlasTech Industries<ref name="RVG" /> by the [[Standard year|year]] [[33 BBY]]. In that year,<ref name="Introduction Date" /> an [[IG lancer droid]] made use of a DLT-20A during the [[Skirmish on the moon of Drazkel|hunt to find]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Eldra Kaitis]],<ref name="Darth Maul III">[[Darth Maul (2017) 3|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 3]]</ref> who had been captured and put up for [[Auction of Eldra Kaitis|auction]] by the [[Xrexus Cartel]]. While the auction was won by the [[Moogan]] [[Jee Kra]],<ref name="Darth Maul II">[[Darth Maul (2017) 2|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2]]</ref> Xrexus Cartel leader [[Xev Xrexus]] had deduced that an apparent rescue mission was in motion<ref name="Darth Maul III" /> and thus had an explosive wired into Kra's [[starship]], causing it to crash to the [[Moon of Drazkel]] below.<ref name="Darth Maul II" /> The IG lancer droid and two other [[IG-series assassin droid]]s took part in the hunt for Kaitis after Xrexus offered a buy-in to reach the moon, only for Kaitis' supposed rescuer&mdash;actually the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Maul|Darth Maul]], who had only rescued Kaitis to finally have a chance at killing a Jedi&mdash;to join forces with the Padawan and cut down all who opposed them, including the DLT-20A-equipped [[assassin droid]].<ref name="Darth Maul III" />

During the [[Imperial Era]], [[Stormtrooper|members]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]] sometimes used DLT-20As instead of their standard issue [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]]s.<ref name="RVG" /> An [[Unidentified Imperial sniper|Imperial sniper]] used a DLT-20A during the [[Ghorman Massacre]]<ref name="Who Are You" /> of [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> wherein the sniper was stationed on a rooftop overlooking a large-scale [[Ghorman (people)|Ghorman]] protest in [[Palmo Plaza]]. Upon being given clearance by [[Captain]] [[Kaido]], the sniper used the DLT-20A to kill an [[Imperial security trooper]] below, panicking the rest of the [[soldier]]'s unit; just as was planned, the Imperial soldiers believed the shot had come from the Ghormans and began firing into the crowd on the belief the Ghormans had shot first.<ref name="Who Are You" /> With the Empire having needed Ghorman aggression to justify the total takeover of the planet [[Ghorman]],<ref name="Messenger">{{Andor|Messenger}}</ref> the sniper's undercover actions help cement Imperial control over the world<ref name="Who Are You" /> and paint the Ghormans as aggressors.<ref name="Welcome">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}}</ref> As the massacre unfolded, the sniper continued to use the DLT-20A against the panicking Ghormans below.<ref name="Who Are You" />

[[File:DLT-20ATrooper-SWL.png|thumb|left|300px|A Rebel trooper takes aim with a DLT-20A in a volcanic environment.]]
[[Rebel trooper|Soldiers]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] who wielded DLT-20A rifles were identified as "DLT-20A Troopers." One such [[trooper]], a [[Theelin]], once took aim with a DLT-20 while on a balcony within a volcanic environment.<ref name="DLT-20Card" /> The [[Hutt Clan]]-hired [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]] used a DLT-20A when he was [[Mission to Mygeeto|hired]] to kidnap a [[Givin]] [[Unidentified Givin codebreaker|codebreaker]] from the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] on the [[planet]] [[Mygeeto]].<ref name="GE 2">[[Galaxy's Edge 2|''Galaxy's Edge'' 2]]</ref> Rebel-aligned [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] used a DLT-20A as a [[sniper rifle]] during the [[Battle of Vrogas Vas]].<ref name="SW 13">[[Star Wars (2015) 13|''Star Wars'' (2015) 13]]</ref> During the [[Mid Rim Retreat]], [[First Sergeant]] [[Hazram Namir]] of the [[61st Mobile Infantry]] introduced a group of new rebel recruits to the DLT-20A.<ref name="Twilight Company" /> IG-assassin droid [[IG-11]] carried this model of rifle, but without the [[scope]] attached on [[Arvala-7]]. IG-11 shot [[Din Djarin]] in the shoulder under the assumption he was part of the [[Nikto]] [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] [[Unidentified Nikto mercenary gang|group]] who had "[[Din Grogu|the Child]]".<ref name="Chapter 1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> On Nevarro, IG-11 wielded a scopless DLT-20A rifle along with an E-11 rifle while riding a [[74-Z speeder bike]] to take out Stormtroopers of [[Moff]] [[Gideon's Imperial remnant]].<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>

Many [[Imperial Remnant AT-RT Driver]]s and [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtroopers]] serving under [[Commander]] [[Barro]], an Imperial [[warlord]] on based out of [[Unidentified ice planet|a snow planet]], wielded DLT-20As during the [[New Republic Era]]. Several stormtroopers working for [[Janu Coin's Imperial remnant]] owned several rifles, with Djarin wielding one upon confronting Commander [[Janu Coin]] at [[Janu Coin's estate|his estate]]. Although he resorted to using his [[IB-94 blaster]] pistol and his weapons against Coin's stormtroopers and his second-in-command the [[Iktotchi]] [[Hogsbreth]].<ref name="MaG" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:DLT20ABlasterRifle-JtR.png|thumb|right|300px|A card in the ''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]
The DLT-20A laser rifle's name was brought into the current [[canon]] by the [[2014]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Rebels: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Adam Bray]].<ref name="RVG" />

A card released in [[2023]] in the [[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]] incorrectly identifies a image of the [[A300 blaster rifle]] as the DLT-20A laser rifle.<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Join the Rebellion!|subset=Weapons Elegant & Otherwise|cardname=DLT-20A Blaster Rifle}}</ref>

In ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', the sniper rifle model is named '''DLT-20A Sniper Rifle''' in-game and is among the secondary weapons dropped by [[Scout trooper]]s which [[Kay Vess]] can use though it will be discarded after using up all its shots or if she picks up another secondary weapon.<ref name="SWO">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>
