'''Daynaal''' was a [[Trandoshan]] [[Gender|male]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was the nephew of the [[pirate]] [[Sellaccc Orryak]], leader of the [[Ganguls]]. Orryak tried to teach him how to be a pirate by having him assist her in ruining the [[Life Day]] celebrations of the [[Wookiee]]s on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], although Daynaal was reluctant. After the Ganguls shot down a [[shuttle]] carrying [[Foodstuff|food]] for the Life Day feast, Orryak had Daynaal transport the supplies. Daynaal was pursued to the Ganguls' shuttle by the [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]], as well as the Wookiee [[Johgeshakka]], whose brother [[Rudi]] had been [[pilot]]ing the [[starship]] and had been taken [[prisoner]] by Orryak. The Ganguls captured the [[Jedi]], but Daynaal freed them while Orryak and the other pirates departed to cut down the [[Life Day tree]]. As the Jedi attempted to stop the pirates, Daynaal and Rudi gathered Wookiees to assist them, leading Orryak and the Ganguls to flee. Daynaal was subsequently invited by the Wookiees to celebrate Life Day.<ref name="The Missing Life Day Feast" />
