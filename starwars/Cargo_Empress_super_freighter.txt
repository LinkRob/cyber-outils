The '''Cargo Empress super freighter''' was a model of [[transport]] produced by [[Ghtroc Industries]]. The super [[freighter]] class was a commercial failure and led to the respected [[starship]] company going defunct by the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="S&S" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Cargo Empress super freighter was first mentioned in the [[2020]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' supplement ''[[Starships and Speeders]]''.<ref name="S&S" /> The [[Cargo Empress-class super freighter|vehicle]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was created in [[West End Games]]' [[roleplaying game]] in "[[Droids Defiant]]," an article written by [[Thomas Bowling]] and published in the [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 9|ninth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Adventure Journal]]'' magazine in [[February]] [[1996]].<ref name="DroidsDefiant">{{JournalCite|9|Droids Defiant}}</ref>
