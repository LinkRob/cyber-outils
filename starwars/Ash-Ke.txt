'''Ash-Ke''' was a [[Sith Eternal]] cultist on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. Their [[Sith]] training was almost complete when they told a [[protocol droid]] that they were concerned that their rival, [[Gar-Ee]], would overshadow their achievements, and asked for assistance in beating them in a fight. Ash-Ke explained to the protocol droid that fighting a fearsome warrior was the last [[Sith Trial]], and enlisted the droid's help, as the droid was a "mightier" warrior than Gar-Ee. Gar-Ee was not actually the warrior they were supposed to defeat; as such, Ash-Ke distracted their intended opponent while the protocol droid defeated Gar-Ee on their behalf, which subsequently occurred. Seeing Gar-Ee defeated was not as satisfying as Ash-Ke thought it would be; they even felt sorry for Gar-Ee, though they reprimanded themselves for saying such a "[[Jedi]] thing". They then revealed to the protocol droid that the physical fight was not, in fact, their final test, and that they still had to pass the theory exam.<ref name="LEGOTSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ash-Ke appeared in the non-[[canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', which was released in [[2022]]. They featured in the side quest "Sith Training Sabotage."<ref name="LEGOTSS" />
