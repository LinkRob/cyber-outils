An '''axe''' (alternatively spelled '''ax''') was a [[melee weapon]] that consisted of a heavy edged head fixed to a handle.<ref name="RotJ" /> Some axes had two blades.<ref name="Lost Treasure">"[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]"</ref> Although regular axes were intimidating weapons in themselves, their [[Death|lethality]] and cutting ability could be increased by the addition of a vibration generator, which produced a variant known as a [[vibro-ax]].<ref name="DB">{{Databank|vibro-ax|Vibro Axe}}</ref>

During the [[time]] of the [[First Order]], [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] who were assigned the duty of serving as [[First Order stormtrooper executioner|executioner troopers]] used the [[BL-155 Laser ax]] for [[Death penalty|capital punishment]] via beheading.<ref name="Laser Ax">{{Databank|laser-ax|Laser Ax}}</ref> [[Gamorrean]]s used [[Gamorrean war ax|war axes]].<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref>
