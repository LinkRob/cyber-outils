== Description ==
The criminal syndicate [[Black Sun]] operated a base on the astronomical object [[Smarab]]. The base was situated in a desert-like plain surrounded by canyon walls. The base was utilized as a [[prison]] for those who were caught harming the syndicate.<ref name="TSC" />

The base consisted of four large walls at least four levels high in a square pattern with towers in each of the corners. Atop the towers were [[laser cannon]] turrets. From the front and rear of the building there were pathways, with two water pools in the front, flanking the front door, over which the symbol of the crime syndicate was hung. To either side and the rear of the fortress were smaller guard buildings, one to each side and two on the rear, flanking the path.<ref name="TSC" />
