'''121-247''' was a [[security droid]] who resided on [[Anoat]] at the [[time]] of the [[Iron Blockade]]. During this time, [[Tam Bastion]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] sent a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] to disable the [[droid]], and the [[smuggler]] succeeded in doing so.<ref name="Uprising" />

121-247 was similar in design to the [[mining droid]]s [[RN-18]],<ref name="RN-18">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "RN-18"</ref> [[STALKER-1]]<ref name="stalker">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "STALKER-1"</ref> and [[Igniter]].<ref name="igniter">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Igniter"</ref>
