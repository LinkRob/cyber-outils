A [[Jedi]] served as '''master''' to the [[Padawan]] [[Fvorn]] during the [[Republic Era]]. In [[22 BBY]], the two participated in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], during which Fvorn was [[Death|killed]] by [[Geonosian|Geonosians]].

==Biography==
[[File:Mace_Windu_Jedi_Champion_EaW.png|thumb|260x260px|Fvorn's master fought at Geonosis under the command of Master Mace Windu (pictured).]]
A [[Force-sensitive]] individual became a [[Jedi]] and served as master to the [[Duros]] [[Padawan]] [[Fvorn]] during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''</ref> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[First Battle of Geonosis]] to [[22 BBY]].</ref> like many other Jedi, this individual and Fvorn were called back to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] and sent to the planet [[Geonosis]] as part of an [[Jedi assault team|assault team]] tasked with rescuring [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], Padawan [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] from the [[Petranaki Arena]], where they were held [[prisoner]] by the [[Geonosian|Geonosians]].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />

The two Jedi were taken to Geonosis aboard a [[shuttle]] with a group of other masters and Padawans led by [[Jedi Master]] [[Klefan Opus]]. As they approached the planet, Opus received their orders from the assault team's [[Commanding officer|commander]],<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" /> [[Master of the Order|Master]]<ref name="SWAEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> [[Mace Windu]]. The detachment was sent by Windu to secure the perimeter of the arena. As the group made its way there through a hallway in the complex's outer catacombs, Fvorn and his friend [[Zeeth]] kept together, accompanied by their masters. Soon after, the group was attacked by Geonosian guards and Fvorn was [[Death|killed]] via electrocution by a guard's [[static pike]].<ref name="Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade" />
