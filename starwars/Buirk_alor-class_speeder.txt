The '''''Buirk'alor''-class speeder''', also referred to as the '''Mandalorian police speeder''', was a model of [[speeder]] used by the [[Mandalorian Guard]], the [[New Mandalorians|New Mandalorian]] [[law enforcement agency]] that [[Law enforcement agency|policed]] [[Mandalore]].

==Characteristics==
The ''Buirk'alor''-class speeder,<ref name="Starfighters" /> or [[Mandalorian]] police speeder,<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep305|text="Corruption" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref> was a type of [[speeder]] manufactured by [[MandalMotors]] that measured at 10.1 [[meter]]s and could reach speeds of 280 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]]. The speeder had frontal and rear wraparound viewports that slid apart for easy access, as well as gull-wing doors for entry. Emergency lights were situated at the top of the speeder, and "[[Law enforcement agency|POLICE]]" was written in the front in the [[Mandalorian (writing system)|Mandalorian alphabet]], while the emblem of the [[Mandalorian Guard]] was featured on the front and sides of the speeder. The phrase "TO PROTECT AND SERVE," written in Mandalorian, was proudly displayed on the windshield.<ref name="Starfighters" />

==Role==
The ''Buirk'alor''-class speeder was utilized by the Mandalorian Guard as a law enforcement vehicle, utilized for patrol of [[Sundari]]'s [[skylane]]s and other policing duties.<ref name="Starfighters" />

==History==
The ''Buirk'alor''-class speeder was employed by the Mandalorian Guard of the [[New Mandalorians]] during the pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]]. Two speeders were used by [[Duchess]] of [[Mandalore]] [[Satine Kryze]] and the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Royal Guard]] during a [[skirmish at the Sundari docks]] when a [[Mandalorian black market conspiracy|black market conspiracy]] led to the [[poison]]ing of children.<ref name="Corruption" />
