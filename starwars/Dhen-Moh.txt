{{Quote|He may be mythologized, but he's earned his reputation. I could tell tales of how he slaughtered children, the Dhen-Moh genocides&mdash;|Everi Chalis, to Hazram Namir, on Darth Vader|<ref name="BTC">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>}}
'''Dhen-Moh''' was an [[Inner Rim Territories]] astronomical object where the [[Galactic Empire]], including the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] carried out [[Dhen-Moh genocides|genocides]]. Multiple individuals reflected on the atrocity later on, particularly in respect to Vader's involvement.

==Description==
Dhen-Moh was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Rise" /> in the [[Inner Rim Territories]]'s [[Dhen-Moh system]] at the coordinates K-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" />

==History==
{{Quote|Chemical weapons may have killed many Geonosians but Darth Vader's lightsaber proved just as effective on Dhen-Moh. The motivation behind all these appalling events was the Imperial principle that mass violence was the simplest solution to almost any problem.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Beaumont_Kin.png|thumb|right|200px|The historian Beaumont Kin recalled the Dhen-Moh genocides in ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''.]]
During its [[Imperial Era|reign]], the Empire ordered [[Dhen-Moh genocides|genocides]] on Dhen-Moh.<ref name="Alphabet">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref> The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] notably took part in the atrocity, [[Death|killing]] masses with his [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]].<ref name="Rise" /> The defecting Imperial [[Governor]] [[Everi Chalis]] mentioned the genocides as an example of Vader's reputation to [[Hazram Namir]], a [[First Sergeant]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]],<ref name="BTC" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref>

In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date 2">{{AlphabetSquadronDate}}</ref> [[Yrica Quell]], an Imperial [[pilot]] who had defected to the Alliance's successor, the [[New Republic]], reflected on [[Operation: Cinder]], a genocide ordered by the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor]] himself. Quell considered the difference between the Dhen-Moh genocides and Cinder as that between ruthlessness and cruelty.<ref name="Alphabet" /> In<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[35 ABY]]<ref name="Date 3">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', the [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] commented that Darth Vader and his [[lightsaber]] on Dhen-Moh was just as effective of a mass killing as the Empire's [[Sterilization of Geonosis|extermination]] of the [[Geonosian]] [[species]] with [[Bioweapon|chemical weapons]]<ref name="Rise" /> around [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Dhen-Moh was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="BTC" />
