"'''Anchorhead: The Lost Scenes'''" is a report by [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds]] in [[Star Wars Insider 35|''Star Wars Insider'' 35]].

The author writes about the [[Content cut from Star Wars|deleted scenes]] focusing on [[Luke Skywalker]]'s friends at [[Anchorhead]]. The article transcribes the scenes and includes commentary on their creation and eventual culling.
