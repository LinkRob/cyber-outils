'''Erstin Deez''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Imperial Royal Guard]], with the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[sergeant]] and later [[major]]. Deez accompanied [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Perilous]]'', when the group became the target of the [[Free Ryloth Movement]]. Although the other guardsmen were killed, Deez, the Emperor, and Vader all survived. [[Standard year|Years]] later, Deez was assigned to the [[Emperor's throne room (Imperial Palace)|throne room]] in the [[Imperial Palace]] alongside a cadre of other guardsmen. When the Emperor and [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] entered the room, Deez and all other guardsmen were [[poison]]ed and [[Assassination of the Emperor's Royal Guard|killed]].

==Biography==
By the [[Imperial Era]], Erstin Deez served as a [[sergeant]] in the [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guard]] of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]. He accompanied the Emperor and his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] on a [[Mission to Ryloth|visit]] to the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] in the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Perilous]]''. When the ''Perilous'' was attacked and destroyed above Ryloth by the rebellious [[Free Ryloth Movement]], Deez evacuated with the Emperor and Vader in a [[Vader's shuttle (Mission to Ryloth)|huttle]], but their craft crash-landed on Ryloth, leaving them stranded in the wilderness. Deez, Vader, Palpatine and a [[Unidentified Captain of the Royal Guard|captain of the Royal Guard]] made their way across the terrain searching for civilization, encountering a nest of native [[lylek]]s which they destroyed [[Death|at the cost]] of the captain's life. They then came upon a [[Twi'lek]] [[Drua's Village|village]], where they were welcomed and rested before being attacked by Free Ryloth movement members. The trio fought off the attackers, aided by [[Imperial Military|Imperial reinforcements]] who soon arrived to collect them.<ref name="LOTS" />

[[File:OchiKillsDeez-CrimsonReign2.png|thumb|right|225px|Deez's killer Ochi of Bestoon.]]
Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Deez served as an [[Imperial officer]] with the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[major]], as well as still part of the Royal Guard. The [[assassin]] [[Ochi]], acting on the orders of [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] of [[Crimson Dawn]], [[Assassination of the Emperor's Royal Guard|traveled]] to [[Coruscant]] where he acquired a number of [[poison]]s, [[Yurfloo|one]] of which he used to infect Deez in an attempt to take out the members of Palpatine's guard. Sometime later, when Deez and the other guards stood before Palpatine and [[Grand Vizier]] [[Mas Amedda]] in Palpatine's [[Emperor's throne room (Imperial Palace)|throne room]], they all suddenly dropped dead, to the surprise of Amedda.<ref name="Crimson Reign 2" />
