'''Dekien''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Dekien system]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]].<ref name="Ronin" /> At the [[time]] of the [[Sith rebellion]], it was not heavily populated or developed. [[The Ronin]], formerly a [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] who had led the rebellion, brought a shard from the [[kyber mirror of Shinsui Temple]] to [[Seikara Caverns]] and hid it within a grotto.<ref name="Ronin" />

During a twenty [[Standard year|year]] span after the war ended, the [[Empire (Rei'izu)|Empire]] took interest in the world. The tour company [[Eternity Enterprises]] developed tours that departed from the capital city, [[Dazenma]], to visit Seikara Caverns.<ref name="Ronin" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dekien appeared in the [[2021]] non-[[canon]] novel ''[[Ronin: A Visions Novel]]'', by [[Emma Mieko Candon]].<ref name="Ronin" /> According to Candon, the industry on the planet was inspired by the [[Wikipedia:Edo period|Edo period]]'s "[[Wikipedia:Ukiyo|floating world]]" lifestyle.<ref name="InspirationsThread">{{Twitter|username=EmmaCandon|url=status/1455604994845990912|name=[[Emma Mieko Candon]]|date=2021-11-03|quote=The industry of Dekien is inspired by a sort of Edo-era Floating World [...] Reds and golds, bridges to every imaginable pleasure. 8/|archivedate=20220628114639}}</ref>
Like all names in the novel, Dekien's name is comprised of [[Wikipedia:Phoneme|phonemes]] used in the [[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese language]],<ref name="Phonemes">{{Twitter|username=@EmmaCandon|url=/status/1442659690144354306|name=[[Emma Mieko Candon]]|date=2021-09-27|quote=Yup! Every name in Ronin uses Japanese phonemes.|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/51Gsj}}</ref> though the author does not remember the inspiration behind the name.<ref name="Podralorian">{{WebCite|url=https://notsafforwork.com/podcasts/the-podralorian-playing-gay-chicken-with-lucasfilm-with-emma-mieko-candon|text=The Podralorian: Playing Gay Chicken with Lucasfilm with Emma Mieko Candon|date=2021-11-13|work=Not Saf For Work|archivedate=20250929211952}}</ref>
