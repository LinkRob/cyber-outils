The '''Archive of the Great Library of Alderaan''' was a data archive that contained a copy of all the information held within the [[Royal Library of Alderaan|Royal Library]] of [[Alderaan]].

==History==
{{Quote|A Kudon trader named Mek Nu'Tiv has acquired, through nefarious means, a certain data archive.|[[C-3PO]]|<ref name="Poe Annual 2" />}}
After the archive's creation, it was [[Smuggler|smuggled]] offworld by [[librarian]]s who feared the [[Galactic Empire]] would take over the [[planet]].<ref name="Poe Annual 2" /> While the Empire never conquered Alderaan, the planet was [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]],<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> and the [[library]] itself was lost.<ref name="Poe Annual 2" />

During the [[Cold War]] between the [[Resistance]] and the [[First Order]], the [[Kudon]] [[Merchant|trader]] [[Mek Nu'Tiv]] acquired the archive, catching the attention of both [[General]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the Resistance and the smuggler [[Han Solo]]. Posing as an [[First Order officer|officer]] of the [[First Order]], Solo arranged to buy the archive. At the same time, Organa assigned [[Poe Dameron]] with a [[Heist of the Alderaanian data archive|mission]] to infiltrate [[Mek Nu'Tiv's cruiser|Nu'Tiv's cruiser]] and take the archive. Solo found the data archive while aboard Nu'Tiv's [[cruiser]], but upon realizing what it was, allowed the Resistance to leave with it. Once [[Black Squadron (Resistance)|Black Squadron]] returned to [[D'Qar]] with the archive, Organa planned to copy its contents and sell the physical archive to collectors who would pay high prices for it.<ref name="Poe Annual 2" />
