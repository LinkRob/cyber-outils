'''Emita''' was a settlement on the<ref name="Ghosts 2" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> [[Chandrila]]. [[New Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] directed [[Captain]] [[Lina Graf]] to a [[Unidentified bar (Emita)|bar]] in Emita when Lina sought transport to [[Mustafar]] in search of her missing brother [[Milo Graf|Milo]] and the [[droid]] [[CR-8R]]. When Lina arrived in Emita, she found the [[Lepi]] [[smuggler]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] at the [[Cantina|bar]].<ref name="Ghosts 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emita first appeared in the comic book [[Star Wars Adventures: Ghosts of Vader's Castle 2|''Star Wars Adventures: Ghosts of Vader's Castle'' 2]],<ref name="Ghosts 2" /> written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published on [[September 29]], [[2021]],<ref name="Date">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-Ghosts-of-Vader-s-Castle-2/digital-comic/951941|text=Star Wars Adventures: Ghosts of Vader's Castle #2|nolive=1}}</ref> though it was included in a preview of the issue published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[September 23]].<ref name="Preview">{{SW|url=news/ghosts-of-vaders-castle-2-exclusive-preview|text=Nightmares and a Job for Jaxxon in IDW's ''Ghosts of Vader's Castle'' #2 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Emita/Legends|Emita]] was first introduced in the [[2003]] reference book ''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''.<ref name="Coruscant">''[[Coruscant and the Core Worlds]]''</ref>
