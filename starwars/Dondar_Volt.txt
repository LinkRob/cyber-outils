{{Quote|Put on my own makeup? It's easy, really. Takes some practice, but you get the hang of it. Dondar Volt taught me. She's incredible.|Aytar, to Kantam Sy|<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />}}
'''Dondar Volt''' was an individual who taught the [[acrobat]] [[Aytar]] how to put on his own makeup. At some point before<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> when Aytar spoke with the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] and his lover [[Kantam Sy]], they told him they didn't know how he did it. Aytar thought they were referring to putting on his makeup and told them about Volt teaching him. Sy told Aytar that whilst that was fascinating, it was not what they meant, before informing him that what they actually meant was how Aytar could perform every night, how he could be someone else, and how he could smile and feel the crowds [[eye]]s on him, before eventually admitting they admired him for it.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
