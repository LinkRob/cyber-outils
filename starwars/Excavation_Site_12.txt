'''Excavation Site 12''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] dig site operated by the [[Imperial Department of Military Research]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> on an island in [[Lake Amari]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. The goal of the site was to excavate a [[meteorite]] impact site and research the meteorite's material for potential military applications. A set of ruins were also preset on the island as such the Imperials collected a number of artifacts including a miniature spinning artifact believed to have been used in ceremonial dances. The site also contained a copy of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]'s report on the [[Zerek Besh]] leader [[Sliro Barsha|Sliro Ruback]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During the same year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] visited the island and stole a [[NL-02 Charge Emitter]], the ISB report and the spinning artifact.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Excavation Site 12 first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The location contained the NL-02 Charge Emitter which was a key part in unlocking the [[Electro-shock prod]] tool as part of [[Temmin Wexley]]'s expert upgrade track. The site also included the "Cylcone Relic" major charm, which give the player a high chance to gain a free [[bacta vial]] or [[grenade]] when used.<ref name="Outlaws" />
