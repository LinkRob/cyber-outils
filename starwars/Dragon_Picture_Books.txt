'''Dragon Picture Books''' was a London-based children's book company that published storybooks based on the [[Ewoks (television series)|''Ewoks'']] and [[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO|''Droids'']] cartoon series for distribution in the UK, Australia, and New Zealand in [[1987]].<ref name="WWbbc">[[Wicket's Wagon (book)|''Wicket's Wagon'']], back cover.</ref> Dragon was an imprint of the Children's Division of the [[Collins Publishing Group]].<ref name="WWb32">[[Wicket's Wagon (book)|''Wicket's Wagon'']], p. 32.</ref> Unlike the Ewok storybooks from [[Random House]], which feature original illustrations, Dragon's volumes use artwork borrowed directly from the cartoons.<ref>[[Wicket's Wagon (book)|''Wicket's Wagon'']].</ref>
