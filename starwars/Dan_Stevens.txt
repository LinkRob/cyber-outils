'''Dan Stevens''' is a [[Wikipedia:British people|British]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actor]] best known for his role on the television series [[Wikipedia:Downton Abbey|''Downton Abbey'']] and for portraying the [[Wikipedia:Beast (Disney character)|Beast]] in [[The Walt Disney Company|Disney's]] live-action [[Wikipedia:Beauty and the Beast (2017 film)|''Beauty and the Beast'']]. Stevens voiced [[Pab Solitus]] in the [[2025]] animated series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy - Pieces of the Past]]''.<ref name="Announcement" />
