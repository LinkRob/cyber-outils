'''Felkor''' was a [[Sith Lord]] who was depicted in a statue chiseled into one of the interior [[Stone|rock]] walls of the [[Sith Citadel]] on [[Exegol]], a [[Sith]] [[planet]] in the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="TROS junior" /> By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="35 ABY">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel]]'' to [[35 ABY]].</ref> [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]]&mdash;former [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Initiate|trainee]],<ref name="TROS junior" /> [[Ren|Master of the Knights of Ren]],<ref name="TFA">{{Film|VII}}</ref> and the [[Supreme Leader]] of the [[First Order]]&mdash;had become familiar with Felkor as part of his studies. That [[Standard year|year]], when Ren [[Search for Exegol|arrived]] at the Sith Citadel in order to investigate a [[Palpatine's broadcast|mysterious transmission]] from the supposedly-[[Death|deceased]] [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], he recognized Felkor and other Sith Lords in the statues of the edifice. As he advanced into the interior of the citadel, Ren heard unintelligible whispers from Felkor's effigy and the others despite their [[mouth]]s not moving.<ref name="TROS junior" />

==Behind the scenes==
Felkor first appeared in the form of a statue in [[Michael Kogge]]'s [[2020]] [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: A Junior Novel|junior novelization]] of<ref name="TROS junior" /> the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref>
