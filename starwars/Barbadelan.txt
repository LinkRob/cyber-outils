'''Barbadelans''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]]. [[Dobarn Tren]] and [[Karamu]] were members of this species.

==Biology and appearance==
The Barbadelan were a [[species]] of [[Color|orange]] or tan colored skin.<ref name="The Official Guide" />

==Barbadelans in the galaxy==
[[Dobarn Tren]], a [[Gender|male]] Barbadelan, served as a [[priest]] of the [[Sacred Way]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="The Official Guide" />

[[Karamu]], a male Barbadelan, was a [[pilot]] who met [[Chewbacca]] and [[Han Solo]] on [[Barbadel]].<ref name="TFVC 3">[[Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle 3|''Star Wars Adventures: Tales from Vader's Castle'' 3]]</ref>
