'''Bastila Shan's motif''' is a music piece written by [[Jeremy Soule]] for the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''. The piece, which includes "[[The Force Theme]]," is used in [[musical score]] as the [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]] of Bastila Shan.

The piece is also played in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic: Onslaught]]''. If [[Alliance Commander|the Commander]] is romanced with [[Theron Shan]], a descendant of Bastila Shan, the music plays when Theron's [[Parent/Legends|mother]], [[Satele Shan|Satele]], discuss with the Commander about the latter's relationship with Theron.

Outside of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, this piece has been repurposed as neutral music in ''[[Wikipedia:The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim|The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim]]'', the music of which was composed by Jeremy Soule.
