{{Quote|The earliest known trophies of this variety date back to the ancient Krath that conquered the carbonite mines of the Empress Teta system, thousands of years ago in the galaxy's dark past.|Andressa Divo, ''Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious''|<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>}}
The '''Empress Teta system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Koros sector]]<ref name="NOP" /> of the [[Deep Core]], containing<ref name="TFABG" /> the [[planet]] [[Empress Teta]] and its [[moon]], [[Atale]].<ref name="NOP" /> [[Carbonite]] [[Mining|mines]] within the system were conquered by a group known as the [[Krath]] thousands of [[Standard year|years]] before the [[New Republic Era]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> At some point they also [[Conquest of the Empress Teta system|conquered]] the system itself,<ref name="A&S">{{EncyclopediaCite|Accessories and Structures}}</ref> and later the [[Galactic Republic]] reclaimed the system from the Krath.<ref name="NOP" />

According to the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], the system had once been known as the '''Koros system''', but the name was changed after the system's [[Unification Wars (Koros)|unification]] by the legendary [[Empress]] [[Teta]].<ref name="NOP" />
