The '''Batonn insurgents''' were a group of insurgents led by [[Nevil Cygni]] (or "Nightswan") who operated on the planet [[Batonn]] fought against [[Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. They worked with other insurgent groups in the [[Batonn sector]] including the [[Denash insurgents]] and the [[Sammun insurgents]] during the [[Batonn sector insurgency]]. The Batonn insurgents were wiped out during the [[Batonn insurgency]] As a result of Imperial operations on Batonn, [[Denash]], and [[Sammun]],<ref name="Thrawn" /> there were no more insurgents in the Batonn sector by [[2 BBY]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow" />

==Equipment==
The Batonn insurgents had a fleet of at least thirty mid-sized [[starship]]s, comprising of [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruisers]], [[GR-75 medium transport]]s, [[Dreadnought-class heavy cruiser|Imperial Support Vessels]], at least one [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|G9 ''Rigger''-class light freighter]],<ref name="Thrawn 6" /> [[LAAT/le patrol gunship]]s,<ref name="Thrawn" /> several other mid-sized ships, as well as [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]s, and [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttles]].<ref name="Thrawn 6" />
