The '''C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruiser''', also known as the '''C-ROC Carrier Ship''', with the [[shipyard]] custom configuration known as the '''Configuration-Restored Operational Capacity''', was an official modified version of the [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] that was manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] prominently during the [[Clone Wars]] and the [[Imperial Era]].

==Characteristics==
===Manufacturing===
The C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class [[light cruiser]],<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> also referred to as the C-ROC carrier [[Starship|ship]],<ref name="SW Kids">{{SWKidsYouTube|series=Star Wars By the Numbers|text=Every Type of Ship in Rebels|video=iasSt3JSV4Q|nolive=1}}</ref> was a class of light cruiser that was equipped with the Configuration-Restored Operational Capacity upgrade,<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> a [[shipyard]] custom configuration of the standard civilian [[Gozanti-class cruiser|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]], which was manufactured by [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> and meant to be a midlife upgrade program to reinvigorate the ''Gozanti''-class and create more profitability.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

===Design===
[[File:C-ROC_Cruiser_Expansion_Pack_box_art.png|thumb|left|270px|A C-ROC being escorted by a M3-A Scyk fighter.]]
The C-ROC was designed to be bulkier than the original ''Gozanti''-class, and had many cosmetic and mechanical modifications, which diverged it from the standard cruiser. The C-ROC's [[hull]] design was considered softer and more rounded, in contrast to the bold and square-shouldered lines of the standard cruiser. The front two-thirds of the C-ROC's hull had a sloping, bell-shaped cross-section that was narrow on the dorsal side, while it flared out below the midship line.<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> Although the C-ROC was not as large as a true [[cruiser]], its tight corridors and compartments could make for intense close-quarters combat, and had consumables capacity for two [[Standard month|months]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

The design of the new hull provided more protection as the slope of the hull deflected blasts more efficiently than the original ''Gozanti''-class. Outrigger cargo trays that were designed to carry modular cargo containers similar to the [[GR-75 medium transport]] replaced the two cranked delta wings. The outriggers, in concert with the complete redesign of the ship's interior, provided the C-ROC with exceptional hauling capability with the cost of the ship's combat and fleet support utility.<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> The outrigger racks, along with the extended prow, and the [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] module softened the original cruiser's sharp design edges.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

===Equipment===
[[File:BrokenHorn-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|The ''Broken Horn'' was a modified C-ROC cruiser owned by Cikatro Vizago.]]
Although surpassing or meeting the original ''Gozanti''-class cruiser in most metrics, the C-ROC was deprived of military-grade [[sensor]]s and ordnance launchers, though it was equipped with a medium-range sensor. With the original cruiser was originally kept slow to appease to [[Galactic Republic]] inspectors who were concerned the ship could be used for [[Pirate|piracy]], the C-ROC was equipped with three additional [[sublight engine]] turbines that brought its top speed back in line with other starships of the same category.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> The two original [[Girodyne]] [[Ter58 ion engine]]s were replaced with five [[Novaldex]] [[JV-80 ion drive]]s that were newer and faster. Two each were placed on port and starboard, while another was mounted on the dorsal spar.<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> The C-ROC could be crewed by a single [[pilot]],<ref name="Brothers" /> but had a typical crew of twelve. The crew included one pilot and one co-pilot, one [[engineer]], one communications and sensor operator, two loadmasters, and six [[gunner]]s.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

[[File:CorsairRefitC-ROC-SWZ.png|thumb|right|270px|A C-ROC cruiser Corsair Refit with a turbolaser battery firing on an enemy fighter.]]
Despite the C-ROC's [[armor]] and [[deflector shield]]s remaining untouched from the original design, the weapons loadout was drastically reduced to two retractable [[quad laser cannon]] [[turret]]s&mdash;one ventral and one dorsal&mdash;which provided good defense against smaller ships, such as [[Patrol ship|patrol boats]] and [[starfighter]]s. Additionally, each of the C-ROC's broad flanks were equipped with a two retractable twin heavy [[laser cannon]]s that kept pirates at bay. Although it did not carry the original firepower of the original design, the C-ROC was capable of defending itself from most foreseeable threats.<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> Many owners routinely relied on the C-ROC's speed rather than its firepower to escape danger.<ref name="Databank Broken Horn" /> The C-ROC could be modified to carry four [[M3-A Scyk interceptor]]s in its ventral hull.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

===Corsair Refit===
The C-ROC Cruiser was often the heaviest vessel available to many criminal operations, cartels, and syndicates in the Outer Rim, and was fully capable of carrying massive weapons batteries that kept it one step ahead of the law.<ref name="C-ROCExpansion">{{FFGXW|set=C-ROC Cruiser Expansion Pack}}</ref> The dangerous Corsair Refit fitted the C-ROC with additional fixed and turreted weapon mounts, along with ordnance tubes and a revamped power source.<ref name="CORSAIRREFIT" /> This allowed the craft to be fitted with additional [[heavy laser cannon]]s and [[quad laser cannon]] [[turret]]s, or for heavier weaponry such as [[turbolaser]]s batteries and [[ion cannon]] batteries. Beyond this, the C-ROC could be configured with adaptive shielding, sensor bafflers, optimized power cores, and minelayers.<ref name="HUGESHIP">{{FFGXW2|set=Huge Ship Conversion Kit}}</ref> Flexible and dangerous, the Corsair Refit made the C-ROC a force to be reckoned with whether it was hauling illicit cargo or marauding the hyperlanes in search of easy targets.<ref name="C-ROCExpansion" />

==Role==
The C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruiser was designed as an armed cargo [[transport]] rather than for outright military combat.<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> Despite this, the C-ROC's improved speed suited pirates and [[mercenary]] groups, and with significant space for customization, thick [[armor]], and rough-terrain landing capabilities, the ship was considered all-purpose.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> The C-ROC could be further militarized through the popular Corsair Refit,<ref name="CORSAIRREFIT" /> while the ship's spacious cargo bays could be modified to hold hundreds of mercenaries, making it a popular option with criminals and marauders. With its heavy armor, large crew complement, and passenger accommodations, the C-ROC also popularly served as a mobile base.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
[[File:CIS-C-ROC.png|thumb|left|270px|A Separatist privateer C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruiser during the Clone Wars, on the planet Zygerria.]]
Based off of the standard ''Gozanti''-class cruiser that had seen service by the time of the [[Invasion of Naboo]]<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> in [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruiser entered service by the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Separatist Privateer" /> ([[22 BBY]]&mdash;[[19 BBY]])<ref name="Atlas" /> seeing usage by [[privateer]]s in service to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]<ref name="Separatist Privateer" /> that fought against the Galactic Republic,<ref name="Secret Weapons">{{TCW|Secret Weapons}}</ref> with one being active on the [[planet]] [[Zygerria]] being equipped with [[Color|blue]] Confederacy livery. In addition to the C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class being used by the Confederacy,<ref name="Separatist Privateer" /> the Confederacy also used another [[Separatist Gozanti-class cruiser|modified ''Gozanti''-class cruiser]].<ref name="TIE Manual">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>

===Age of the Empire===
[[File:Merchant_One_XWM_Mariusz_Gandzel.png|thumb|right|270px|''Merchant One'', a C-ROC cruiser owned by the criminal Azmorigan.]]
With the rise of the [[Galactic Empire]] following the Clone Wars, [[Corellian]] Engineering Corporation initially offered the upgraded class of C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruisers to the [[Imperial Navy]] as an armed long-range [[transport]], with the benefits of common spare parts and crew training to that of the [[Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|''Gozanti''-class Assault Carrier]]. Despite the pitch by the CEC lobbyists, they failed to convince the top leaders of the Imperial [[Navy]] and the self-defending logistics platform.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> It was rejected as it was inappropriate to the Imperial Navy's [[Order of Battle]]<ref name="No Disintegrations" /> since it did not adhere with the Galactic Empire's new logistics doctrine of large, escorted transport ships such as the [[Eta-class supply barge|''Eta''-class supply barge]]. As such, the CEC turned to civilian buyers, with the aim being high-risk transportation companies.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

The C-ROC then became popular in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], as the C-ROC's capabilities were greatly satisfactory to pirates and [[smuggler]]s. This resulted in C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruisers fighting against [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruisers]] during the [[Imperial Era]] as criminal groups challenged Imperial patrols.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> While the C-ROC was not considered a wildly successful product, sales of the ship were steady and as such, CEC continued to produce updates and replacements to various systems. With the increased lawlessness in the Outer Rim, many C-ROC cruisers were inducted into service by rebel cells as artificial or substitute [[capital ship]]s. Though not suited for serving as frontline [[warship]]s, C-ROCs with a cunning crew were capable enough for dealing with older vessels used by the Imperial Navy to maintain law and order on the fringes of society.<ref name="No Disintegrations" />

[[File:C-ROC_Cruiser_SWX.png|thumb|left|270px|A C-ROC cruiser flying through a planetary asteroid field]]
In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Corellia]] events of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref> one such ship delivered supplies to the [[Santhe Shipyards|Corellian shipyards]] on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> By [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas note">"[[Rebel Resolve]]" directly follows "[[Call to Action]]," which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[4 BBY]].</ref> the C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class cruiser ''[[Broken Horn]]'' was used by [[Cikatro Vizago]], the criminal leader of the [[Broken Horn Syndicate]].<ref name="RebelResolve">{{Rebels|Rebel Resolve}}</ref> An associate of Vizago, the [[crime lord]] [[Azmorigan]], similarly used a C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class freighter, named ''[[Merchant One]]''.<ref name="Idiot's Array">{{Rebels|Idiot's Array (episode)}}</ref>

At one point, a C-ROC cruiser was attacked by [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and used its [[quick-release cargo lock]]s to dump its cargo.<ref name="Cargo Locks" /> In another instance, a C-ROC light cruiser was near the [[Ring of Kafrene]] when a [[YT-1300 light freighter]] was chased by Imperial TIE fighters through [[asteroid]]s.<ref name="Outer Rim">[[Star Wars: Outer Rim|''Star Wars'': Outer Rim]]</ref> At one time, the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] used a C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class to ferry a vault of priceless art from one planet to another, and was under heavy escort. Additionally, the C-ROC ''Gozanti''-class light cruiser ''[[Kimoliga's Maw]]'' served as a pirate ship, and was modified in a way that was similar of the Imperial ''Gozanti''-class cruiser, carrying four [[M3-A Scyk interceptor]]s. It was considered to be a plague on civilian shipping in the [[sector]] it operated in.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />
