"'''A Letter from Chancellor Valorum'''" is a short in-universe letter from [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Finis Valorum/Legends|Finis Valorum]] to [[Jedi Master/Legends|Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] that was published in the [[2001]] ''[[Secrets of Naboo]]'' sourcebook. The letter serves as a briefing to Jinn during his mission to [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]].
