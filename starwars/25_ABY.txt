The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''25 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3302 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''8002''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Greer Sonnel]] is diagnosed with [[bloodburn]] syndrome.<ref name="Bloodburn">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/652893780236988420|[[Del Rey]]|quote=So excited to have @claudiagray writing a #StarWars novel with us. SW: New Republic: Bloodline coming 2016. Set 6 years before TFA.|archivefile=File:New Republic Bloodline Twitter Post.jpg}} The tweet in question states that the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']] take place six [[Standard year|years]] before the events of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' establishes that the events of ''The Force Awakens'' take place in the year [[34 ABY]] and so using simple math it can be concluded that the events of ''Bloodline'' take place in [[28 ABY]]. In ''Bloodline'', it is stated that Greer Sonnel was diagnosed with bloodburn three years prior to the novel's events, setting her diagnosis in 25 ABY.</ref>
*Ten year old [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] crafted a toy [[doll]] of a [[Rebel pilot]] using debris she found in the junkfields of [[Jakku]].<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
*[[Phasma]] returns to [[Parnassos]] and forges her [[Phasma's armor|armor]] at [[Cleo Station]].
