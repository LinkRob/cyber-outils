It's practically a full meal&mdash;eggs, meat, grains, and veg&mdash;all packed into the perfect portable parcel. '''Byblos Squares''' were a convenient [[Foodstuff|food]] item available on the [[Colonies]] [[planet]] [[Byblos]], located along the [[Corellian Run]] [[Hyperspace route|hyperlane]]. The squares consisted of a [[Color|purple]] [[vegetable]] wrap filled with blue [[Kerkoidia Grains]], green [[Pickled Paddy Flower]]s, red meat, and yellow [[egg]]s. They were assembled by placing the four individual fillings on separate quarters of the wrap, which itself would be slit halfway down the middle from one side. The wrap would then be folded over itself three times, covering all the fillings.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

Upon completing a tour of [[the galaxy]] and its many food cultures <ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref>, [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for Byblos Squares in ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', his third [[Book|cookbook]], published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> Tuggs believed the popularity of the squares on Byblos was likely due to their convenient nature, making them perfect [[smuggler]]s on the run who needed a quick meal.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Byblos Squares were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the wrap requires [[Wikipedia:Lunch meat|luncheon meat]], [[Wikipedia:Soy sauce|tamari]], [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|eggs]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame oil|sesame oil]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|white pepper]], and [[Wikipedia:Nori|nori sheets]], as well as two other recipes&mdash;Kerkoidia Grains and Pickled Paddy Flowers&mdash;included in the book.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
