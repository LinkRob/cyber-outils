'''Crocins''' were a [[Reptile|reptilian]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Croce]]. [[Karjj]] was a member of this species.

==Biology and appearance==
Crocins had short bodies and [[Tooth|teeth]] that protruded from the tops of their [[mouth]].<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Society and culture==
Crocins built their [[City|cities]] in caverns near [[water]] on their homeworld of [[Croce]], and were unused to bright sunlight. When traveling to [[Temperature|warmer]] climates, they wore protective [[goggles]] to prevent damage to their sensitive [[eye]]sight.<ref name="Alien Archive" />

==Crocins in the galaxy==
[[Scarspike]] was a Crocin [[Gender|male]] who held the rank of [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] in the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization during the [[High Republic Era]]. [[Udi Dis]], a [[Talortai]] [[Strike]], would later [[Death|kill]] Scarspike by slashing his throat with a [[wingblade]] after a botched raid.<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref>

Karjj was a Crocin who lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], and could play the card game [[sabacc]]. He was present during [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Han Solo]]'s first sabacc game.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
