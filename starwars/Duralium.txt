'''Duralium''' was a [[Color|blue-colored]] material that could be used to reinforce [[steel]] or to construct [[lightsaber]]s.

==Description==
Duralium was a [[Color|blue]] material which could be combined with a [[steel]] alloy to enforce it.<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> It could also be used in [[lightsaber]] hilts.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==History==
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Veil speeder]]s, which were produced by [[Valkeri-Kuat Consolidated Enterprises]] for the [[Jedi Order]], used duralium frames.<ref name="Light of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' takes place five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Great Jedi Purge]], which is dated to [[19 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, ''Fallen Order'' is set in [[14 BBY]].</ref> the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] discovered some duralium within the [[Origin Lake]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] and kept it for making modifications to his [[Jaro Tapal|former master's]] [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|lightsaber]], which he received from his [[Jedi Master]] Jaro Tapal shortly before his [[death]] during [[Order 66]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Order66">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>
{{EndGame}}

Four [[Standard month|months]] before the [[Liberation of Erso]]<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jyn Erso]], daughter of [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[scientist]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]], was [[Prison|imprisoned]] at the [[Imperial Detention Center & Labor Camp LEG-817|Wobani Labor Camp]] on the planet [[Wobani]]. Inside the [[labor camp]], Jyn picked up a duralium-enforced [[steel]] alloy panel as she worked. However, she dropped it and the panel shattered. Noticing that the panel was too brittle, an [[RA-7 protocol droid]] notified the nearby [[engineer]] that 400 panels had been made from the same defective batch. In response, that section of the facility was shut down and every panel from that batch was pulled and remade.<ref name="Rebel Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], duralium first appeared in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Rebel Rising]]'', written by [[Beth Revis]].<ref name="Rebel Rising" /> It was first pictured in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, it is one of the twenty-four materials which players can collect in order to customize the appearance of Cal Kestis' lightsaber.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Duralium/Legends|duralium]] was first mentioned in the [[2005]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|Star Wars: Complete Locations]]'', in the {{Film|III}} section written by [[Kerrie Dougherty]].<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> It first appeared in the [[2011]] video game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', developed by [[BioWare]] Austin.<ref name="TORCite">{{TORcite|type=item|Duralium Combat Suit|the mission "Looters"}}</ref>
