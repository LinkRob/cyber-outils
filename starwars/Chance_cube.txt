{{Quote|I just happen to have a chance-cube here. Blue&mdash;it's the boy. Red&mdash;his mother.|Watto, deciding whether to wager [[Anakin Skywalker]] or [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|his mother]] in a bet|<ref name="TPM" />}}
A '''chance cube''', also formatted as '''chance-cube''', or alternatively known as a '''die''', was a [[gambling]] device in the form of a small cube. Some types of chance-cube could only give two results after being thrown, as three of their sides were one [[color]], while the other three were another color. [[Toydarian]]s like [[Watto]]<ref name="TPM" /> or [[King]] [[Katuunko]] were known for using such chance-cubes.<ref name="Ambush" /> [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Imperial cadet|cadets]] [[Pandak Symes]] and [[Nazhros Oleg]] used a chance cube to decide who would get the bottom bunk in their [[barracks]], with Symes winning the roll.<ref name="Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks">''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]''</ref>

Another variant had from one to six dots marked on each side of the cube. [[Han Solo]] had a [[Han Solo's dice|pair of such cubes]] decorating the cockpit of his [[starship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="ANH" /> It was Solo's copilot, [[Chewbacca]], who had hung the cubes as a joke inside the ship's cockpit several years before the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="Smugglers">''[[Smuggler's Run: A Han Solo & Chewbacca Adventure]]''</ref>

In [[grav-ball]], the team that won the chance-cube toss got to start at the center of the game's grid.<ref name="edge">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

During the [[Battle of Eriadu]], [[Abediah Viess]] gave three golden chance cubes to her crew so they use them to decide who would pick the next target for orbital bombardment.<ref name="Trials">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref>
