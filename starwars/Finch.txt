'''Finch''' was a [[Duros]] who served the [[New Republic]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. While onboard the [[CR90 corvette]]<ref name="SW 2025 1" /> ''[[Nechrim's Wake]]''<ref name="SW 2025 3">[[Star Wars (2025) 3|''Star Wars'' (2025) 3]]</ref> in the [[Fenril sector]], the Duros&mdash;alongside [[Captain]] [[Gauvis]]&mdash;were fired on by a [[Nagai rebellion|pirate crew]] that operated in the [[sector]].<ref name="SW 2025 1" />
