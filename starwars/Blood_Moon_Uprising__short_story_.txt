"'''Blood Moon Uprising'''" is a short story published in the ''[[Stories of Jedi and Sith]]'' short story collection. The story was written by [[Vera Strange]], illustrated by [[Jake Bartok]], and published on [[June 7]], [[2022]]. The story features [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].
