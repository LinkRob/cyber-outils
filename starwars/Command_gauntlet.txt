'''Command gauntlets''' were a type of [[Color|white]] [[gauntlet]] used by [[Alliance officer|officers]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] while they were stationed on the icy [[planet]] [[Hoth]].<ref name="Bust Announcement" /> In a pair of the gauntlets, the left glove was a equipped with a temperature gauge that allowed for control of internal electrical heating systems.<ref name="Eaten" /> While stationed in [[Echo Base]], [[General]]s [[Carlist Rieekan]] and [[Pharl McQuarrie]], [[Major]] [[Bren Derlin]], [[Lieutenant]]s [[Cal Alder]] and [[Tigran Jamiro]], the [[Jedi]] [[Luke Skywalker]], and the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] all wore command gauntlets.<ref name="ESB" />
