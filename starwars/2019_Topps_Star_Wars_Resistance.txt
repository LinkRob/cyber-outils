The '''2019 Topps ''Star Wars Resistance''''' trading card set was released by [[Topps]]. It is based on the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of the television series ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. The base set, released in [[2019]], featured 100 cards, 25 inserts, 12 danglers, 10 pop-up cards, 10 temporary tattoos, and 4 binders.<ref name="Release" />
