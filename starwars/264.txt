'''264''' was a readapted [[R4 astromech droid|R4]] [[astromech droid]] working for the [[Imperial Press Corps]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] as a communications [[R4 courier droid|courier]]. The droid was taken prisoner by the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' so their own droid, [[C1-10P|Chopper]], could pose as a courier. The rebels considered keeping him, but he was thrown out of the ''Ghost'' by Chopper.

==Biography==
[[File:ChopperDisguise-BYOR2D2-26.png|thumb|250px|left|Chopper was painted to like a communications droid to replace 264.]]
After the destruction of the [[Imperial Communications Center]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Lothal]], the [[Galactic Empire]] resorted to using [[R4 courier droid|droids]] to relay communication across the planet and into [[orbit]]. One such [[droid]] was 264, who was tasked with bringing messages from Lothal to a [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|communications]] [[cruiser]]. The [[Spectres|rebel crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'', hoping to find out the location of their [[Raid on the communications tower|captured]] leader, [[Kanan Jarrus]], decided to disguise their own droid, [[C1-10P]]&mdash;also known as Chopper&mdash;as a communications droid. They stole 264 from the Empire as the droid was being [[transport]]ed to the [[Sentinel-class landing craft|Imperial shuttle]] [[Lagos (Sentinel-class)|''Lagos'']], and Chopper was made to look like 264.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

Later, when Chopper was attempting to escape from the cruiser after having discovered Jarrus' location, the ''Ghost'' opened [[fire]] on the cruiser. The ''Ghost'' was hit as well, and a fire sparked inside of the [[Starship|ship]]. 264 helped put the fire out. The crew considered keeping the droid once the mission was over, but, while in the [[atmosphere]], a jealous Chopper threw 264 out of the ''Ghost''. The droid landed unharmed in a field on Lothal and found itself with the curious company of two [[Loth-cat]]s.<ref name="Rebel Resolve" />

A little time elapsed, until a pair of alien farmers found 264 on the prairie, still sitting where he landed, a Loth-cat curled up on his dome. The droid then had a brief chance at a new life, pulling vegetable carts at the alien's farm. However, the farmers ended up being ostensibly arrested for possession of Imperial property, and 264 was removed from Lothal. He was sent to a bleak Imperial Outpost on a cold, rocky, inhospitable world. There, 264 felt abused, taken for granted and forlorn, and he often dreamed of what his life could have been with the Spectres.<ref name="Ballad">{{RebelsMagCite|25|The Ballad of 264}}</ref>

One day, the whole base went on alert when a Rebel prisoner was brought in. When the courier droid realized that the prisoner was none other than Sabine Wren, he perked up, and started to hope the rest of the Spectres would show up soon to rescue her. Realizing that their daring rescue could also be his ticket to freedom, 264 decided to help. He opened Wren's cell, and followed her to the hangar where Kanan Jarrus, [[Garazeb Orrelios]], and Chopper were involved in a shootout with Imperials. The Rebels and the courier droid all boarded the Spectres' shuttle, the ''[[Phantom]]'', huddling in the crew section. While Jarrus questioned the presence of a droid with Imperial markings, Orrelios recognized him from their earlier adventure. The conversation, however, was cut short when the Rebels realized their shuttle was caught in a [[tractor beam]].<ref name="Ballad" />

[[File:264OutInTheCold-Balladof264.jpg|thumb|250px|right|Following his heroic sacrifice, 264 was left to freeze outside the Imperial base.]]
264 knew that he could deactivate the beam using a computer socket at the other side of the hangar, but that would make it impossible for him to get back to the ''Phantom'' on time for takeoff. A decision had to be made: stay with the Spectres, like he had always wanted, or leave them behind so he could save them. 264 ultimately chose the latter option, raced across the hangar and plugged into the manual override control. The courier droid beamed with pride as the ''Phantom'' pulled away from the hangar, Orrelios cracking him a smile in the distance. Almost immediately, the outpost's commander entered the premises with a few stormtroopers. Glowering at 264, the commander ordered his men to find out what he knew. Soon thereafter, 264 was thrown outside the outpost, put on a junk heap of other droids and equipment that were left there to freeze. Despite his predicament, the courier droid kept hoping that, now that his Rebel friends knew where he was, they would come back for him someday.<ref name="Ballad" />

==Behind the scenes==
264 first appeared in "[[Rebel Resolve]]," the twelfth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. In a [[Rebels Recon]] interview, [[Dave Filoni]] said that they discussed about 264's fate after he got thrown out of the ''Ghost''. They decided that maybe his battery runs out, leaving him standing on the plains like a monument, although not specifically dead, but turned off.<ref name="ResolveRecon">{{RebelsRecon|AATSzoxTC1g|#1.13: Inside "Rebel Resolve" &#124; Star Wars Rebels|sw_url=video/rebels-recon-inside-rebel-resolve}}</ref>
