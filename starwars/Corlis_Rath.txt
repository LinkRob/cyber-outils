'''Corlis Rath''', also known as '''Davros Hex''', was a [[Sinsaran]] [[Gender|male]] who lived during the late [[Republic Era]] and tried to [[Assassin|assassinate]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]].

==Biography==
===Meeting Qui-Gon Jinn===
Davros Hex was a [[Sinsaran]] [[Gender|male]] individual from the world [[Sinsara]], who lived during the late [[Republic Era]]. He was associated with a bandit group on [[Sinsara]] known as the [[The Banished|Behru]], or later as "The Banished". After the Behru had raided the local villages for a considerable time, the Sinsarans called for the help of the [[Jedi]] and [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Dooku]] and [[Padawan]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] [[Mission to Sinsara (Qui-Gon and Dooku)|were sent to deal with the issue]]. After arriving on Sinsara, the Jedi met the Behru at [[The Banished's base|their base]], but were unsuccessful at negotiating and were forced to retreat. Being of the opinion that their situation had confronted the limitations of the [[Jedi Code]], Dooku urged his apprentice to change to a more violent strategy. Upon their return to the bandits, Jinn, who had studied on the Sinsaran culture's sense of honor, proposed a duel between himself and the [[Unidentified Behru leader|Behru's leader]]. The conditions being, if Jinn lost, the Jedi would leave Sinsara, but if the Sinsaran lost the Behru would stop their raids. The leader agreed to the duel, but refused to fight "children" and instead put forward Davros Hex as a surrogate.<ref name="Jedi Knights 9" /> During the ensuing fighting Jinn slashed a wound into Hex's chest with [[Qui-Gon Jinn's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] while his Master tried to stop him.<ref name="MoonofDeath" />

In the following [[Standard decade|decades]], Hex began using the name Corlis Rath and altered his appearance, with [[Jedi librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]] suspecting that he underwent the highly dangerous Sinsaran ritual known as "[[Chrysalis]]", something which would also explain his superior fighting skills.<ref name="Jedi Knights 9" /> At some point, a [[hologram]] of Corlis Rath was included together with his name in the [[Jedi Archives]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" />

===Attempts to assassinate Qui-Gon Jinn===
{{Quote|What is your quarrel with me? Why are you hunting me? I don't even know you, Corlis Rath. Oh yes, since last we fought, I've learned your name. What I haven't learned is why you want me dead. The first time I even met you was on Vekura. Why are you doing this? Why?!''"<br />"''Sinsara.''"<br />"''What--? What did you say?|Qui-Gon Jinn and Corlis Rath during their duel on Mina-Rau|<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" />}}
[[File:QuiGonJinnAttacked-JediKnights1.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Corlis Rath attacks Qui-Gon Jinn on Vekura]]
Between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]]<ref name="Date">The events of [[Jedi Knights 1|''Jedi Knights'' 1]], which include the duel on [[Vekura]], take place between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:JediKnightsDate|here]].</ref> during the [[Invasion of Syrinx Prime]], Rath attacked Jinn &ndash; now a Jedi Master &ndash; on the [[planet]] on [[Vekura]]. Jinn and fellow [[Jedi Knight]] [[Berem Khana]] were on their way to their [[T-6 shuttle|shuttle]] to return to [[Syrinx Prime]] after they had unsuccessfully tried to negotiate a ceasefire with the [[Vekuran]] [[general]] [[Lafco]]. Rath jumped at Jinn and tried to stab him<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> with two energy-edged [[Sword|blades]],<ref name="MoonofDeath" /> and when Khana tried to assist Jinn, Rath propelled him back against a [[tree]] with a small<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> concussive-based weapon<ref name="FCBD 2025" /> on his right upper arm. Eventually Jinn managed to [[Force push]] Rath against a boulder, but before Jinn could remove Rath's mask and question him, he activated a [[smoke grenade]] and fled.<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" />

Back in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], Bhana showed Jinn a [[microdust transmitter]] sown into his [[Jedi apparel|robe]] that his attacker must have used to track him. Meanwhile Jinn's [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] got a [[Force vision]] of Rath killing Jinn<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> and, after investigation in the Jedi Archives, found out his identity and showed his master the hologram of Rath, who didn't recognize him.<ref name="Jedi Knights 2" /> Fellow Padawan [[Tensu Run]] began tracking Rath. Together with Jinn, Run tried [[Mission to K'shar|to meet]] with an informant named [[Aquint Garva]] on the [[moon]] [[K'shar]], but found Garva to have already been murdered by Rath.<ref name="MoonofDeath" /> At some point later, Jinn was on [[Robbery of the Galactic Bank of Coruscant|a mission]] on the planet [[Nar Shaddaa]] together with fellow Jedi Master [[Shaak Ti]], when he [[Force sense|sensed]] the presence of Rath, who was surveilling them from atop the roof of a nearby building, but didn't make a move to attack them.<ref name="Jedi Knights 4">[[Jedi Knights 4|''Jedi Knights'' 4]]</ref>

To lure out Jinn, Rath [[poison]]ed and [[Death|killed]] the Jedi Master [[Fondar Etzis]] with a fast acting toxin,<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> [[emubara]],<ref name="Jedi Knights 7">[[Jedi Knights 7|''Jedi Knights'' 7]]</ref> on the [[agriworld]] of [[Mina-Rau]]. [[Duel on Mina-Rau|Arriving]] on Mina-Rau as part of an honor guard with fellow Master [[Mace Windu]] and Knight [[Aayla Secura]], Jinn put together that Etzis had been murdered and sensed the assassin's approach when he finally revealed himself, whereas Windu and Secura were caught off guard and knocked out via darts. Rath dueled Jinn's lightsaber with his blades<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> in a challenging battle<ref name="Jedi Knights 7" /> and revealed his vendetta against the Jedi had something to do with Sinsara, something that shocked Jinn and gave Rath and opening to stab him through the chest.<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> Jinn was left poisoned but was saved from death by Windu and Secura, who made their way to Sinsara to [[Mission to Sinsara|recover]] the antidote.<ref name="Jedi Knights 7" /> 

After his recovery, Jinn recognized Corlis Rath as Davros Hex. Having researched how he could have altered his appearance in the Jedi Archives, Jinn, accompanied by his Padawan Kenobi, traveled to Sinsara to confront Rath once and for all.<ref name="Jedi Knights 9" />

==Personality and traits==
Corlis Rath had light-[[color]]ed skin<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> and yellow-colored [[eye]]s. He had a personal vendetta against Jedi Master Qui-Gon Jinn<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> and tried to kill him, but used only non-lethal weapons against other enemies.<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" />

==Skills and abilities==
Rath was skilled in the use of dual blades, able to push Qui-Gon Jinn on the defensive during their fight.<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> During their duel on Mina-Rau, Rath showed extraordinary jumping abilities comparable to [[Force jump|these]] of [[Force-sensitive]]s.<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" />

==Equipment==
During his duel with the young Qui-Gon Jinn, Rath wore only dark [[Clothing|pants]] and [[boot]]s.<ref name="Jedi Knights 9" /> During his later hunt for Jinn, Rath used two two energy-edged blades,<ref name="MoonofDeath" /> as well as smoke grenades,<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" /> a concussive-based weapon,<ref name="FCBD 2025" /> and [[poison dart]]s,<ref name="Jedi Knights 6" /> and wore mostly black clothing going up to his head, with red parts around his upper arms, and he concealed his face with a red [[metal]]ic mask and a grey [[goggles]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 1" />
