'''Celanon''' was an astronomical object located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], situated along the [[Hydian Way]] which connected it to [[Junction system (Outer Rim Territories)|Junction]] and [[Botajef]].<ref name="The Force Awakens Beginner Game">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> It was home to the [[Astarte line]].<ref name="DV6" /> The [[Dressellian]] [[smuggler]] [[Has Obitt]] was arrested on Celanon for [[blockade]] running, but was bailed out by the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> It shared its name with the [[Celanon Spur]],<ref name="BMF 48">{{BuildFalconCite|48|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> the [[Spurs of Celanon]] [[hyperspace route]]s,<ref name="BMF 70">{{BuildFalconCite|70|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> and the [[Celanon Circuit]] racing event.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref>
