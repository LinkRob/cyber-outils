'''Fighting droids''' were small and [[color]]ed spherical entertainment [[droid]]s that were made to fight each other for amusement and [[gambling]] purposes in a game known as [[droid ball fighting]]. Spheres of the same color could to clump together in order to form fiercer fighters. Around 30 [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="TFAVD" /> several patrons of the [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] were watching a fight between several fighting droids on one of the venue's tables.<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fighting droids first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released in [[2015]].<ref name="TFA" /> They were not identified in the film, but received a name in the ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released to coincide with the film.<ref name="TFAVD" />
