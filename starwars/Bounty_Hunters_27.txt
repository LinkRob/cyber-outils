'''''Bounty Hunters'' 27''' is the twenty-seventh issue of the [[2020]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Paolo Villanelli]] and [[Arif Prianto]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[September 14]], [[2022]].

==Publisher's summary==
''A DEADLY MISSION ON THE EDGE OF A BLACK HOLE! Desperate for credits, T'onga and the bounty hunters take a dangerous job for the Pykes. But is Tasu Leech ready to take on&hellip;the Kanjiklub? Valance makes a decision that will change everything&hellip;''

Valance is also treading on unstable ground, handpicked by Darth
Vader to lead an elite team ferreting out Crimson Dawn moles
within the Imperial military. He may have started out serving
the Empire against his will, but despite his protestations, the
mantle is starting to grow disturbingly comfortable&hellip;.}}
