'''Annisoukaline Kalfus''', or simply "'''Anni'''", was a [[Gender|female]] [[Twi'lek]] [[racer]] and wannabe pilot. At one point in time, she was involved in a [[parent]]-[[Youngling|child]] [[starfighter]] race alongside her mother, [[Kalina Kalfus]]. She had [[Color|blue]] skin with pink blotches&mdash;though her [[nose]] was orange&mdash;and short [[lekku]]. Her [[eye]]s were yellow, and she had brown [[Hair|eyebrows]]. Kalfus was equipped with an orange and white [[flight suit]], as well as a brown head wrap and yellow [[goggles]].<ref name="IAYM" />
