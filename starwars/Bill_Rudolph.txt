He has worked on many Star Wars titles including, ''[[LEGO Star Wars Summer Vacation]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Empire]]'', ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'', ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy]]''. ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'', and ''[[Star Wars: Maul - Shadow Lord]].
