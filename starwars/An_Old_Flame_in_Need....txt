"'''An Old Flame in Need...'''" is a short narrative published in the ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]''.

==Plot summary==
[[Imperial Advisor/Legends|Imperial Advisor]] [[Kren Blista-Vanee/Legends|Kren Blista-Vanee]] is hosting a gala to celebrate the opening night of ''[[The Kallea Cycle]]'' on [[Coruscant/Legends|Imperial Center]]. Various Imperial dignitaries, including [[Grand Admiral/Legends|Grand Admiral]] [[Miltin Takel]] and [[Lady/Legends|Lady]] [[Amber Comark|Comark]] are present to witness this event. Among the crowd is spice-runner, [[Wendell Wright-Sims]], a dealer that has contracts with many of the Imperial elite. While mingling with his numerous contacts, Wright-Sims spies his old flame, [[Mayli Weng]] among the crowd. Wendell attempts to put his charms on the woman, but she is uninterested, much more concerned with networking the wealthy crowd around her. Wendell persists, offering to take her mind off of her worries with a no-strings-attached night together.
