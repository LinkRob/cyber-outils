The '''Everon exodus'''<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> took place in [[500 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> when the last members of the [[Evereni]] [[species]]&mdash;known as [[the Wanderers]]&mdash;remaining after the [[Great Storm]] and [[Standard century|centuries]] of [[Evereni conflicts|conflicts]] banded together and abandoned their [[homeworld]] [[Everon]]<ref name="EotS1" /> to escape [[death]] under the lead of the [[Xouri family]].<ref name="Closed Fist" />

{{Event-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Everon exodus first appeared, albeit unidentified, in a flashback in the [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|first issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Eye of the Storm]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="EotS1" /> on [[January 12]], 2022, as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase I.<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> It was later identified as Evereni exodus in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.<ref name="Timelines" />
