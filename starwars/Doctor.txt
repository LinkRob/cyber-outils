{{Quote|Do not be afraid, your friend is in ''quite'' capable hands. Doctor Nala Se is the Chief Medical Scientist on Kamino. She was one of the primary engineers of ''all'' the clones.|[[Shaak Ti]], to [[Fives]]|<ref name="TCWConspiracy" />}}
'''Doctor'''<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> (abbreviated as '''Dr.''')<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref> was a title used by physicians<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> or individuals who held an advanced degree in a medical or scientific field. They were generally considered an expert on a specific subject of study.<ref name="POV">''[[The High Republic: Path of Vengeance]]''</ref>

Individuals who held the title of doctor included the [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]] [[Nala Se]],<ref name="TCWConspiracy" /> [[Sionver Boll]] of the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="TCWZilloBeast">{{TCW|The Zillo Beast}}</ref> the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Nuvo Vindi]],<ref name="TCWBlueShadow" /> the [[Mandalorian]] [[Zak Zaz]],<ref name="Corruption">{{TCW|Corruption (episode)}}</ref> and the [[Consular Jedi|Jedi Consular]] [[Rig Nema]].<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[clone medic]]s&mdash;[[clone trooper]]s who were trained as [[medic]]s&mdash;were sometimes referred to as doctors.<ref name="TCWConspiracy" /> [[Harter Kalonia]] was a doctor active with the [[Resistance]] during the [[Cold War]] who also held the rank of [[major]].<ref name="Episode VII" />

Aside from attending to physical and mental ailments, doctors of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] medical center were trained to aid [[Jedi]] who had lost their [[Force-sensitive|connection]] to [[the Force]].<ref name="Jedi Temple gallery 14">{{Databank|url=jedi-temple-location-gallery|title=Jedi Temple Location Gallery}} {{C|Image 14 caption}}</ref>

At one point, Doctor [[Chelli Lona Aphra]] had her doctorate revoked by her father, [[Korin Aphra]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 1|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 1]]</ref> After the [[twilight of the Ordu Aspectu]], he had her doctorate restored.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 6">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 6|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 6]]</ref>
