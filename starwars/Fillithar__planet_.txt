'''Fillithar''' was a [[planet]] in [[the galaxy]] that was the [[homeworld]]<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> of the [[snake]]-like<ref name="BL" /> [[Fillithar]] [[species]].<ref name="Princess of Alderaan" /> Twenty [[Standard year|years]] into the reign of the [[New Republic]], it was represented in the [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]].<ref name="BL" />

{{Planet-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the first iteration of the "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix published on [[June 24]], [[2025]], Fillithar was placed in the Inner Rim in grid square N-10;<ref name="Appendix" /> however, the ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' map instead placed it in the Colonies in M-9.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> The [[October 13]] iteration of the appendix updated Fillithar to use the Colonies placement.<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref>
