'''Buck Vancto''', known simply as "'''the Marshal'''," was a [[Surpossian]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[marshal]] active during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]]<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> Vancto hunted the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] as he undertook a [[Heist on Corellia|heist]] for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]].<ref name="Han Chewie 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|At the same time, I always like to make some contributions of my own, so the series will also introduce a few new faces, including a "Marshal" — think the corporate equivalent of a bounty hunter — who is going to be hunting Han and Chewie and be a real thorn in their side. That character's my tip of the hat to the "super posse" in Butch and Sundance and they're simply a blast to write. David Messina has come up with an incredible, iconic design for them that's going to blow people away.|Marc Guggenheim|<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/marvel-han-solo-chewbacca|text=Marvel's ''Han Solo & Chewbacca'' Series Coming March 2022 – Exclusive}}</ref>}}
Buck Vancto is a character created for the [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' written by [[Marc Guggenheim]]. Vancto appeared in the [[Han Solo & Chewbacca 1|first issue]]<ref name="Han Chewie 1" /> published on [[March 9]], [[2022]] and illustrated by [[David Messina]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96849/star_wars_han_solo_chewbacca_2022_1|text=Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca (2022) #1}}</ref> Vancto was created by Guggenheim as a reference to the relentless posse lawman [[Wikipedia:Joe Lefors|Joe Lefors]] in [[Wikipedia:William Goldman|William Goldman's]] 1969 film [[Wikipedia:Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid|''Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid'']], directed by [[Wikipedia:George Roy Hill|George Roy Hill]]. Guggenheim credited Messina for Vancto's design.<ref name="Reveal" />
