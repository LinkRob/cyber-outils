'''Bright Tree Village''' was home to an [[Bright Tree tribe|eponymous tribe]] of [[Ewok]]s on [[Endor]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].

==Characteristics==
The village was built within the evergreen [[tree]]s<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> of the [[Happy Grove]] Forest<ref name="BMF 55" /> on [[Endor]]<ref name="VE" /> and fifteen [[meter]]s above the ground, where nest-like bunches of thatched huts housing nearly 200 [[Ewok]]s clung to evergreen trees.<ref name="AbEverything" /> The village center included communal meeting areas, along with homes for tribal elders and [[Chief]] [[Chirpa]]'s family, including the [[Council of Elders (Ewok)|Council of Elders]].<ref name="Village Databank">{{Databank|bright-tree-village|Bright Tree Village}}</ref> Large huts for extended families huddled along the outer edges of the village. The village was connected through a series of rope ladders, bridges, vines, and catwalks. Despite the vast majority of villagers inhabiting the upper forest canopy, bachelors maintained small huts below the village proper, where they kept watch for even greater dangers.<ref name="UltimateSW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

[[File:Bright_tree.jpg|thumb|left|200px|The village at night]]
The village's upper canopy was utilized by watchers to alert the village of marauding [[gorax]] and [[condor dragon]]s, and was illuminated by [[fire]]s and torchlights during the night. The bark of the [[conifer]] trees surrounding Bright Tree village was not only fire resistant, but made for a good [[insect]] repellent. Their boughs were used to make excellent [[spear]]s, [[bow]]s, [[slingshot]]s, glider frames, and catapult arms. Gliders could also be launched from the canopy to patrol the lands and valleys beyond the immediate confines of the settlement. Following traditional Ewok houses, huts in Bright Tree Village were warm and cozy inside. A cooking fire was located at the center, where meat would be roasted on a spit and soups boiled in clay pots. Storerooms of [[Foodstuff|food]] and kindling were layered under the floor, while sleeping mats and furs were stacked in the lofts above. [[Wood]]en stools and baskets often littered the area, and Ewok hoods, [[cape]]s, and tools hung on the walls.<ref name="UltimateSW" />

Despite spending much of their time in the trees, inhabitants of Bright Tree Village had to descend to the [[forest]] floor to forage for [[Berry|berries]], gather herbs, and [[Hunting|hunt]]. Some Ewoks, such as the [[Warrick family]] maintained huts and lodges on the ground.<ref name="UltimateSW" />

==History==
{{Quote|Princess Leia wassi waba Artoo&hellip;|C-3PO explains the Rebel Alliance's plight against the Galactic Empire.|audio=ThreepioStory.flac|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
[[File:C3POTheStoryteller-RotJ40-Prouse.png|thumb|right|220px|C-3PO recounts the tale of the Rebel Alliance to the Ewoks.]]
Sometime before the [[Battle of Endor]], the Ewok [[Romba]] became a [[refugee]] following the destruction of his [[Unidentified Ewok tribe|own]] village to make way for a [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] [[Death Star's shield generator|complex]] utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]] for the construction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. He was eventually accepted into Bright Tree village by Chief Chirpa, who would rule Bright Tree Village for 42 seasons by the time of the battle.<ref name="Beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref><ref name="AbEverything" />

In the lead-up to the Battle of Endor, the Ewoks captured [[Han Solo]], [[Luke Skywalker]], [[Chewbacca]], [[C-3PO]], and [[R2-D2]] near the village after Chewbacca tried to eat a [[Death|dead]] [[verkle]] used as hunting bait. Lone Ewok [[Wicket Wystri Warrick|Wicket W. Warrick]] also befriended [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], who had gotten separated from the main group, and was brought back to the village on friendly terms bring Leia to [[Bright Tree Village|their village]], and as repayment for her heroics they give her a [[Clothing|dress]].<ref name="Ewok Escape">{{FOD|Ewok Escape}}</ref> The other group of [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebels]] were taken captive within the village and were nearly roasted alive for a feast, but [[C-3PO]]&mdash;whom the Ewoks believed to be a [[deity]]&mdash;convinced the Ewoks to free them after Luke utilized [[the Force]] to [[Telekinesis|lift]] Threepio off the ground, scaring many Ewoks into submission.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Telling the story of the Alliance's [[Galactic Civil War|battles]] against the Galactic Empire, Threepio eventually secured the group's admission as members of the tribe after a debate between Chirpa, the [[shaman]] [[Logray]], and his two best hunters, [[Teebo]] and [[Asha Fahn]]. In the middle of the night and unbeknownst to the Rebels, the Ewoks held a council of war in the village, where Chirpa recalled the tribe members killed and villages burned by the Empire, convincing the village to take up arms against the Empire, as did one of his best hunters, Asha, whereas Romba argued the upcoming struggle included all Ewok tribes. Chirpa soon sent messengers to the other tribes to ask for their help.<ref name="Beware" />

[[File:Ewok_village.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Bright Tree Village the night after the Battle of Endor]]
The Ewoks later joined the fight against the Galactic Empire.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Shortly after the battle was won, a pair of surviving [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|Imperial TIE Fighter]]s attempted to attack the village, but were intercepted and shot down by the ''[[Ghost]]''.<ref name="Chopper and Friends">{{FOD|Chopper and Friends}}</ref> That night, the village hosted the festivities celebrating the death of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the defeat of the Empire.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Decades after the Battle of Endor, the village continued to still be made up primarily of a few simple scattered huts, with scavenged Imperial gear remaining as the only visible trace of the village's role in the battle.<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref>

Chief Chirpa's daughter, Princess [[Kneesaa a Jari Kintaka|Kneesaa]], eventually succeeded the late chief.<ref name="AbEverything" />

In [[35 ABY]], following the [[Battle of Exegol|Battle of]] [[Exegol]], Wicket and his son [[Pommet Warrick]] watched as a [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] was [[Uprising on Endor|destroyed]] in orbit.<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
