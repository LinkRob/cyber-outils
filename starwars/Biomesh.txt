'''Biomesh''' was a material that could be used to filter [[Poison|toxins]]. During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], laborers working for the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate in an [[Crimson Dawn ore hub|ore hub]] on the [[planet]] [[Athus Klee]] were [[Cybernetics|cybernetically]] modified by the surgeon [[Doctor]] [[Cornelius Evazan]] in order to work more efficiently.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

These modifications included the replacement of some workers [[nose]]s and [[mouth]]s with biomesh. The [[Besalisk]] [[Dexter Jettster]] eventually discovered the ore hub and witnessed the modifications that had been made to the workers, taking scans of them and making a record in the [[book]] that would become known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Biomesh was first mentioned in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
