'''Douglas Chaffee''' is an American artist who illustrated [[Timothy Zahn]]'s three-part short story ''[[Hero of Cartao]]'' in ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' ''[[Star Wars Insider 68|68]]'', ''[[Star Wars Insider 69|69]]'', and ''[[Star Wars Insider 70|70]]''. He lives in Greenville, South Carolina.
