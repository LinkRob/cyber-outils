The '''Battleworn Chemist''' was a costume owned by the [[Rodian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Skora]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Underworld Chemist|standard costume]]. It featured a [[Color|pink]], purple, teal, brown, and cream colored aesthetic, with teal knee and ankle [[Armor|pads]], as well as a teal chest plate covered in technological components connected to a large [[backpack]]-like device of the same color via shoulder pads. The outfit almost included a hood with holes cut out to accommodate Skora's [[Antenna|antennae]].<ref name="Hunters" />

==Behind the scenes==
Battleworn Chemist was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Skora, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Battleworn Applicator]].<ref name="Hunters" />
