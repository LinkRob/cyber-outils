'''Droq'l''' was the [[Bartender/Legends|bartender]] at [[Shanko's Hive]] on [[Borgo Prime/Legends|Borgo Prime]]. He had three arms, two eyes centered in the middle of his head, another in the back, and one on top of his bald, blue skull. He also had glossy black teeth, and the ability to smile. He gave [[Zekk]] his first [[Bounty hunter/Legends|bounty hunter]] assignment. The assignment was to capture [[Fonterrat]] and discover what became of the cargo of [[ronik shells]] the rodent-like creature was supposed to sell for the bartender.
