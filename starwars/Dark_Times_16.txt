'''''Dark Times'' 16''', also titled with the legacy numbering '''''Republic'' 99''', is the sixteenth issue of the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]''. It was written by [[Randy Stradley]], illustrated by [[Douglas Wheatley]] and [[Dan Jackson]], and first published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[April 14]], [[2010]].

==Publisher's summary==
''Former Jedi Dass Jennir has woven a tangled web of lies in his quest to topple two opposing gangs. Now, all the threads are about to break, and the gangs will tear the town apart in a war that may doom those Jennir sought to save&mdash;but not before they take their vengeance out on him!''

==Opening crawl==
{{OpeningCrawl
|name=BLUE HARVEST<br />PART FOUR (of FIVE)
|text=Hired by a local businesswoman to rid a town on
Telerath of a gang of Chagrian slave traders, ex-Jedi
Dass Jennir soon discovered that there were really
two gangs in town&mdash;their rivalry held in check by a
shaky truce and an agreement to limit conflicts to
one-on-one swordfights.

But destroying one gang would leave the
townspeople at the mercy of the other&mdash;a group of
T'surri spice runners. So Jennir manipulated both
gangs&mdash;playing on their suspicions of one another
until they reached the brink of war.

While Jennir awaits the coming battle, across the
galaxy his friends, Bomo Greenbark and the crew
of the Uhumele, are hiding out after their recent
disastrous endeavor. But their sojourn may be
nearing its end&hellip;}}
