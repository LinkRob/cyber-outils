A '''Crystalline macrobinocular lens''' was a type of [[telescope|optical instrument]] originally developed to observe distant galaxies.<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> they had started being used to enhance analysis of [[starship]] [[sensor]]s to better determine weak points.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> [[Kay Vess]] was awarded a set of lens by the [[Miyuki Trade League]] after completing a [[smuggler|smuggling]] contract for them.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The crystalline macrobinocular lens first appeared in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> They were a collectable "passive system" ship upgrade that players would be awarded by completing quests for Miyuki Trade League. The upgrade increased the damage that the player's ship weapons did to weapoints on enemy ships.<ref name="Outlaws" />
