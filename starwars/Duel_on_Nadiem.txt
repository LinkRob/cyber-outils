A '''duel''' between [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[General]] [[Grievous]] was fought on the astronomical object [[Nadiem]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], only a few [[Standard month|months]]<ref name="Mace Windu Encyclopedia" /> after the [[Battle near the Kaliida Nebula|destruction]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[warship]], the [[Subjugator-class heavy cruiser|''Subjugator''-class heavy cruiser]] ''[[Malevolence]]''.<ref name="Destroy Malevolence">{{TCW|Destroy Malevolence|Destroy ''Malevolence''}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the duel was first mentioned in [[Mace Windu and Other Users of the Force]] in the ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'' booklet series published by [[De Agostini|Altaya]] on [[September 14]], [[2021]].<ref name="Mace Windu Encyclopedia" />

The event originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Stars Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was instead a full [[Battle of Nadiem|battle]] pitting the forces of [[Grievous/Legends|Grievous]] against those of [[Luminara Unduli/Legends|Luminara Unduli]] and other [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]]. It first appeared in "[[Hide in Plain Sight]]," a comic story written by [[Randy Stradley|Welles Hartley]], penciled by the [[Fillbach Brothers]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Hide">{{StoryCite|story=Hide in Plain Sight|book=Free Comic Book Day|bformatted=Free Comic Book Day 2004 Special}}</ref> as part of [[Free Comic Book Day]] on [[July 3]], [[2004]].<ref name="FCBD2004">{{WebCite|url=https://freecomicbookday.com/Article/189955-FCBD-2004-Spider-Man-Archie-Teen-Titans-Mickey-Mouse-and-Star-Wars|text=FCBD 2004: Spider-Man, Archie, Teen Titans, Mickey Mouse, and Star Wars!|author=[[Wikipedia:Diamond Comic Distributors|Diamond Comic Distributors]]|archivedate=20251114050123}}</ref> It was later identified as the Battle of Nadiem in the [[2008]] reference book ''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="CSWE">''[[The Complete Star Wars Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
