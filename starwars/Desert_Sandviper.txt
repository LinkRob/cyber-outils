The '''Desert Sandviper''' was a modified [[blaster rifle]] utilized by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Imara Vex]] that she could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition in the [[Grand Arena]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Firestorm Rifle|standard rifle]]. This [[blaster]] [[rifle]]'s frame was primarily [[Color|dark gray]], though weathering had exposed a silver underlayer. It also featured a foldable brown foregrip, and was wrapped with various tan strips of cloth.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Desert Sandviper was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Imara Vex's Firestorm Rifle, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Desert Isolation]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing the Desert Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
