The '''Destroyer-I ion engine''' was a model of [[ion engine]] manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] for use in the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] in service of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SWCT">[[Star Wars: Card Trader|''Star Wars'': Card Trader]]</ref> Such served the same purpose aboard the [[Xyston-class Star Destroyer|''Xyston''-class Star Destroyers]] of the [[Sith Eternal]]'s [[fleet]], the [[Final Order]].<ref name="SWCT Xyston" />
