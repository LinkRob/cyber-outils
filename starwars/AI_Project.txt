The '''AI Project''' was a [[Droid/Legends|droid]] construction project led by the [[Koroli Masters]] sect of the [[Machinist Theocracy]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Karlatt]]. After the AI had become deviant and indestructible, the project was condemned and the Machinists fled to [[Temple Maudit]] in a desolate, sterile peninsula of Karlatt's eastern continent.
