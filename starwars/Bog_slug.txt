The '''bog slug''' was a type of [[Slug (creature)|slug]] that lived in [[Swamp|bogs]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] swamp [[planet]] [[Dagobah]]. Whenever they were available, the [[Yoda's species|alien]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]] used bog slugs as an ingredient in the [[Mud and Bug Soup]] [[Foodstuff|meal]]&mdash;which he admitted did not [[Diet|taste]] good&mdash;during his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] on the planet<ref name="Atlas" /> from [[19 BBY]] to [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Yoda eventually included a menu including the Mud and Bug Soup&mdash;and noting the bog slugs&mdash;in a [[Yoda's Guide to the Galaxy|travel guide]] he [[author]]ed.<ref name="Atlas" />

==Behind the scenes==
The bog slug was mentioned in the [[2021]] [[Canon|non-canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Yoda's Galaxy Atlas]]''.<ref name="Atlas" />
