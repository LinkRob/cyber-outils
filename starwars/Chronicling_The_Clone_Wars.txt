'''Chronicling The Clone Wars''' was a series of reference articles that were on the [[Star Wars Blog|''Star Wars'' Blog]] from September 14, 2012 to May 30, 2013. They were authored by [[Leland Chee]] to reconcile the chronology of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', as well as how they fit into the overall [[Star Wars Legends|Expanded Universe]] due to several questions asked about them by various Star Wars fans.<ref name="Part 1">{{SW|url=news/chronicling-the-clone-wars|text=Chronicling ''The Clone Wars''}}</ref>

The blog series ended on May 30, 2013, in preparation for the June 3 release of Jason Fry's ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''. However, Chee did not dismiss the possibility of creating a subsequent blog that dealt with the bonus arcs in the series.<ref name="Part 11">{{SW|url=news/chronicling-the-clone-wars-11|text=Chronicling ''The Clone Wars'' #11}}</ref>
