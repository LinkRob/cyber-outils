"'''At Your Service'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year three of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was a stand-alone adventure, written by [[Ronald A. Heintz]] and edited by [[Margaret van Poelgeest-Heintz]] and [[Jae Walker]].
