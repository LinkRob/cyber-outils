'''''Brotherhood''''' is a [[canon]] adult novel by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]. Published by [[Del Rey]] on [[May 10]], [[2022]], it focuses on [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. An [[Brotherhood (audiobook)|unabridged audiobook]] version of the novel was released simultaneously.

==Publisher's summary==
'''''Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker must stem the tide of the raging Clone Wars and forge a new bond as Jedi Knights.'''''

''The Clone Wars have begun. Battle lines are being drawn throughout the galaxy. With every world that joins the Separatists, the peace guarded by the Jedi Order is slipping through their fingers.''

''After an explosion devastates Cato Neimoidia, the jewel of the Trade Federation, the Republic is blamed and the fragile neutrality of the planet is threatened. The Jedi dispatch Obi-Wan Kenobi, one of the Order's most gifted diplomatic minds, to investigate the crime and maintain the balance that has begun to dangerously shift. As Obi-Wan investigates with the help of a heroic Neimoidian guard, he finds himself working against the Separatists who hope to draw the planet into their conspiracy&mdash;and senses the sinister hand of Asajj Ventress in the mists that cloak the planet.''

''Amid the brewing chaos, Anakin Skywalker rises to the rank of Jedi Knight. Despite the mandate that Obi-Wan travel alone–and his former master's insistence that he listen this time&mdash;Anakin's headstrong determination means nothing can stop him from crashing the party, and bringing along a promising but conflicted youngling.''

''Once a Padawan to Obi-Wan, Anakin now finds himself on equal&mdash;but uncertain&mdash;footing with the man who raised him. The lingering friction between them increases the danger for everyone around them. The two knights must learn a new way to work together&mdash;and they must learn quickly, to save Cato Neimoidia and its people from the fires of war. To overcome the threat they face they must grow beyond master and apprentice. They must stand together as brothers.''<ref name="Penguin" />

==Development==
''Brotherhood'' was known as "Playground Fight" during production.<ref name="Production">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/678652/playground-fight-by-ballantine|text=Playground Fight}}</ref> It was announced by [[StarWars.com]] on [[October 7]], [[2021]], along with other upcoming titles.<ref name="New Books">{{SW|url=news/new-books-revealed|text=New Books Starring Luke and Lando, Obi-Wan and Anakin, and More Revealed &ndash; Exclusive}}</ref> During its development, Chen worked closely with fellow ''[[Star Wars]]'' author [[E. K. Johnston]], whose novel ''[[Queen's Hope]]'' is set slightly prior to ''Brotherhood''. They decided to focus specifically on 2 clone battalions, the 555th and the 302nd. The two authors ensured their stories synced together and created ties between them.<ref name="Mike Chen Queen's Hope">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1488172719170416640|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|quote=I'm just saying, Queen's Hope has some ties to Brotherhood and takes place immediately before my book (@ek_johnston and I did some homework to sync things up) so you'll definitely want to preorder!|archivedate=20220131154523|nolive=1}}</ref> Chen wanted the novel to feel like an episode of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="TCW Inspired">{{Bluesky|mikechenwriter.bsky.social|post/3lmdmpu5od22h|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|date=2025-04-08|quote=I thought there were some during the part when Anakin and Obi-Wan escape to the rooftops but I could have just blended my "write a book that feels like Clone Wars" with actual Clone Wars episodes. {{C|In response to: "Alright! This is a big help I will say, I’m not sure I can find any mention of droidekas/destroyer droids in the text, so maybe you need to write a sequel to ensure you have your chance to write them :P"}}|archivefile=File:MikeChen-feellikeTCW.png}}</ref>

==Release and reception==
Some fans of the character [[Mace Windu]] rejected to his characterization in ''Brotherhood'', accusing the novel of being overly negative towards the character. For his part, Chen disagrees with this reading of the book, pointing out how Windu is still supportive towards Count Dooku at this stage of the war in one scene and noting that most 'negative' feelings towards Windu are scenes from the perspective of Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="ChenWindu">{{Bluesky|username=mikechenwriter.bsky.social|author=[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|url=post/3jzl6vnemm526|date=2023-07-02|quote=Hot take #2: I've been accused of shitting on Mace a bit in BROTHERHOOD but I'm playing off 3 equally true things: he's the badass who took down droids on Dantooine, he says "it's not in his nature" about Dooku, he says "I don't trust him" about Anakin. Also my book is Anakin POV biased.|archivefile=File:MikeChen-WinduBrotherhood-Bluesky.png}}</ref>

==Continuity==
===Relation to previous stories===
{{Quote|So, if you want to treat ''Labyrinth of Evil'' as 'that business,' nothing that happens in my book will negate that. They take place at two different points in the timeline, so they can totally co-exist. It's totally cool.|Mike Chen|<ref name="Chen interview">{{WebCite|url=https://www.talkingbay94.com/2022/05/11/mike-chen-author-of-star-wars-brotherhood|text=Mike Chen: Author of Star Wars - BROTHERHOOD|accessdate=2022-05-12|work=''Talking Bay 94''|archivedate=20220511161716}}</ref>}}
The [[Mission to Cato Neimoidia (Kenobi and Skywalker)|mission to Cato Neimoidia]] featured in the novel is the same incident first mentioned in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Cover reveal">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-brotherhood-cover|text=Anakin and Obi-Wan Are Ready for Battle on the Cover of ''Star Wars: Brotherhood'' &ndash; Exclusive Reveal}}</ref> "[[Anakin Skywalker (reference book)|Anakin Skywalker]]," a [[2021]] issue of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|Star Wars Encyclopedia]]'' series, had previously stated that Kenobi and Skywalker undertook an [[Assault on Cato Neimoidia|operation on Cato Neimoidia]] shortly before the [[Battle of Yerbana]], which it claimed to be the event referenced in ''Revenge of the Sith''.<ref name="Anakin">{{EncyclopediaCite|Anakin Skywalker}}</ref> The encyclopedia's account roughly corresponds to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity's depiction of the event from the [[2005]] novel ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]'', written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Labyrinth of Evil">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref> Due to his love of ''Labyrinth of Evil'' and because his book takes place much earlier in the timeline, author Mike Chen has stated fans are free to continue to view the events seen in ''Labyrinth'' as "that business," even stating the two stories can co-exist.<ref name="Chen interview" />

Another connection with a Legends novel comes with a tie to the [[Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (novelization)|''Revenge of the Sith'' novelization]] by [[Matthew Stover]]: while that novelization sees a mission to an [[unidentified dead star system]] from Anakin Skywalker's point of view, Chen included a re-canonization of the scene, but now from Kenobi's perspective.<ref name="Connection to ROTS">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1526649691995111425|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|quote=#10: 'even stars burned out' -- that whole flashback with Anakin and Obi-Wan fleshes out a moment from Matthew Stover's Revenge of the Sith novelization of a 'minor mission' where Anakin witnessed the death of a star.|archivedate=20220517214211|nolive=1}}</ref> The image of Anakin and Obi-Wan running together on the inside cover of the book is sourced from the cover of the [[2002]] Legends [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Practice Makes Perfect (comic book)|''Practice Makes Perfect'']].<ref name="Practice Makes Perfect comic">[[Practice Makes Perfect (comic book)|''Practice Makes Perfect'']]</ref>

[[File:VentressVsSkywalkerRain-Chapter19.jpg|thumb|left|300px|A portion of the ''Clone Wars'' duel between Skywalker and Ventress in which the rain hammers against both opponents, a key part of Mike Chen's assumption of how the duel could occur in canon.]]
With the personal mission to reference it as much as possible,<ref name="Sneak in">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1721948902495273379|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|quote=I love Genndy as much as you, so I wanted to sneak in as much as possible!|archiveurl=https://archive.li/5GxaG|nolive=1}}</ref> Chen also included a number of references to the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' microseries.<ref name="CW references">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1721944833500299344|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|quote=Things I brought over from Genndy into BROTHERHOOD: "Like fire across the galaxy" Mace Windu on Dantooine General Grievous vs Ki-Adi-Mundi Anakin vs Ventress (it's very subtle and comes with some caveats but it's there: "I've seen that ship before." "It's you, isn't it?)"|archiveurl=https://archive.li/PsVkF|nolive=1}}</ref> Chen considered that the microseries could be seen as representing a HoloNet-dramatized versions of actual events&mdash;meaning that while liberties were taken for audiences, the bones of what had occurred were still accurate. He felt this was partially due to the stylized way action is portrayed in the ''Clone Wars'' series feeling disconnected to the films. The author also noted that his personal approach helped him consolidate what had happened in the films to what had happened in the series effectively during the novel's planning process.<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:MikeChenEmail3.png|creator=Mike Chen (author)|date=2023-12-14}}</ref>

The phrase "Like fire across the galaxy" is from ''Clone Wars''. Windu's role in the fighting on Dantooine and [[Grievous]]'s battle with [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]] were also referenced in the novel.<ref name="CW references" /> Chen purposely alluded to the [[Duel on Yavin 4|duel]] on [[Yavin 4/Legends|Yavin 4]] between Skywalker and Ventress shown in ''Clone Wars'',<ref name="Mike Chen Twitter">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1674180634078597121|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|date=2023-06-28|quote=Note that my Anakin/Ventress encounter at the end of the book is written to work with Genddy! Anakin says he's seen her ship before and Ventress knows who he is. This assumes that Yavin was dark, fast, and rainy, so Anakin knows he was attacked on Yavin but not by who.|archivedate=20230717232827|nolive=1}}</ref> with, in his words, "some caveats."<ref name="CW references" /> The mention he included that agents of Count Dooku had been sent after Skywalker previously referenced the duel.<ref name="Inverse">{{WebCite|url=https://www.inverse.com/entertainment/star-wars-anakin-skywalker-clone-wars-canon-twist-brotherhood-book|text=Star Wars writer reveals a new Anakin canon twist|author=[https://www.inverse.com/profile/ryan-britt-18846684 Britt, Ryan]|date=2022-05-11|archivedate=20220512022248}}</ref> Assuming that<ref name="Mike Chen Twitter" /> the ''Clone Wars'' duel occurred in a dark, rainy environment, Chen posited that Skywalker could have fought Ventress previously without realizing who she was or getting a good look at her.<ref name="Inverse" /> Skywalker also presumed he had bested the enemy.<ref name="Mike Chen one of minions">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1721946372583350388|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|date=2023-11-07|quote=Re: Anakin v Ventress, the idea is that Yavin 4 was rainy, dark, and quick, so he doesn't get a good look at her. He figures he defeated "one of Dooku's minions" and doesn't put together that she survived and is back. We decided not being explicit bc it's a LOT of exposition.|archiveurl=https://archive.li/MFqP2|nolive=1}}</ref> Still, he recognizes her [[Ginivex-class fanblade starfighter|''Ginivex''-class fanblade starfighter]], while Ventress is more than aware of who the Jedi is.<ref name="Mike Chen Twitter 2">{{Twitter|mikechenwriter|status/1674181911986728963|[[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]]|date=2023-06-28|quote=Anakin says he recognizes her ship and he's dealt with Dooku's agents before. Ventress just laughs at him and clearly knows who he is. We didn't want too much more expo to bog the story down but those are the clues.|archivedate=20230717233214|nolive=1}}</ref> The book did not go into more detail because it would have taken too much exposition.<ref name="Mike Chen one of minions" />

===Contradictions and connections===
[[File:AnakinBrotherhoodCover.png|thumb|right|300px|Anakin Skywalker's hair on the cover of ''Brotherhood'' is shorter than what is described in the novel.]]
The cover of ''Brotherhood'' depicts Kenobi and Skywalker in appearances<ref name="Brotherhood">[[Brotherhood (novel)|''Brotherhood'']]</ref> derived from [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="aotc">{{Film|II}}</ref> with Skywalker's design updated to reflect his gloved right hand. However, the novel itself also describes "his once-short hair" as having started to grow out, implying a bridge<ref name="Brotherhood" /> between his ''Attack of the Clones'' hair<ref name="aotc" /> and his ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' style<ref name="Cat and Mouse">{{TCW|Cat and Mouse}}</ref> and meaning his hair would be longer than what is seen on the cover. In the novel, after [[Padmé Amidala]] makes note of his growing hair, Skywalker muses that he will allow it to grow out even more,<ref name="Brotherhood" /> foreshadowing the yet longer hair to come by the time of ''The Clone Wars''.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> Kenobi's hair in the novel is also noted to have grown out since ''Attack of the Clones'' despite its appearance on the cover.<ref name="Brotherhood" />

The end of ''Brotherhood'' sees Kenobi and Skywalker adopt appearances<ref name="Brotherhood" /> that match their ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' designs,<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> including Kenobi cutting his hair and parting it<ref name="Brotherhood" /> to match his ''The Clone Wars'' appearance<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> and both looking over pieces of body armor,<ref name="Brotherhood" /> which both have donned by the events of ''The Clone Wars''.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> Such suggests that all appearances that stray closer to their ''Attack of the Clones'' designs are before ''Brotherhood'', while ''The Clone Wars''-inspired designs come after the novel. However, the novel also claims itself as the first time Kenobi and Amidala have seen each other since the Battle of Geonosis.<ref name="Brotherhood" /> Such would suggest that the events of [[Hyperspace Stories 1|''Hyperspace Stories'' 1]]&mdash;in which Kenobi and Skywalker, who is a Jedi Knight judging by his lack of Padawan braid, are closer to their ''Attack of the Clones'' designs&mdash;must occur after the events of ''Brotherhood'' due to Kenobi and Amidala's interactions.<ref name="HS1">[[Hyperspace Stories 1|''Hyperspace Stories'' 1]]</ref>

Additionally, [[Hyperspace Stories 5|''Hyperspace Stories'' 5]] depicts Skywalker as familiar with Ventress, thereby placing it after ''Brotherhood'' despite using the ''Attack of the Clones'' inspired design.<ref name="HS5">[[Hyperspace Stories 5|''Hyperspace Stories'' 5]]</ref> "[[Practice Makes Perfect (episode)|Practice Makes Perfect]]" depicts Kenobi with his longer hair and Skywalker with a shorter haircut than normal during [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s apprenticeship to Skywalker.<ref name="Practice Makes Perfect Talkes">{{TOTJ|Practice Makes Perfect (episode)}}</ref> As such, Kenobi and Skywalker must adjust their clothing and hairstyles several times over the course of the Clone Wars. Indeed, the end of the book sees Skywalker remark that he only wants to try the armor as a trial period instead of committing to it full time<ref name="Brotherhood" /> as he has by the time of ''The Clone Wars''.<ref name="Cat and Mouse" /> The claim that Kenobi and Amidala had not seen each other since the Battle of Geonosis<ref name="Brotherhood" /> would later be contradicted by ''[[Star Wars: My First Comic Reader 1]]'', which, judging by Skywalker's Padawan braid, is set during his apprenticeship to Kenobi.<ref name="Galactic Adventures">[[Star Wars: My First Comic Reader 1|''Star Wars: My First Comic Reader'' 1]]</ref> As the events of ''Brotherhood'' pick up sometime after Skywalker's apprenticeship is over, Kenobi and Amidala cannot meet for the first time since Geonosis during the novel.
