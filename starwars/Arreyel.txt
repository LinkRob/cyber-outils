'''Arreyel''' was a small [[Color|blue]]-and-white [[planet]] located in the [[Arreyel system]] within the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]. 

==History==
Arreyel joined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. However, after the [[Proclamation of the New Order|establishment]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], Arreyel fell into disfavor with [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] and the planet fell into a steep economic depression and had little chance of recovering due to lack of resources.<ref name="Leia" />

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] birth in [[19 BBY]]. The events of ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'' commence just after Organa's sixteenth [[birthday]], which places the events of the novel in [[3 BBY]].</ref> the [[Apprentice Legislature]] was presented with four systems, including Arreyel, to be considered as a site of an academy of aeronautical [[engineer]]ing and design. [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] championed for the planet to be chosen<ref name="Leia" /> on the basis of how the academy would benefit Arreyel's economy and population. However, after the decision was made, Imperial scans discovered that the planet had a powerful radiation source under its crust,<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> and Organa suspected the Legislature's entire process had been a ruse to enable the Empire to run the scans needed to detect the radiation source that they had already suspected was present.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> The Empire planned to build planet-wide factories, and gave the residents six weeks to evacuate with no compensation.<ref name="Leia" />
