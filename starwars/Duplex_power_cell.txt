A '''duplex power cell''' was a type of [[power cell]] available during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] brought a pair of [[Stuncuffs|electronic binders]] with a shielded duplex power cell in the hinge<ref name="AcolyteVG" /> with her on a [[Mission to Khofar|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Khofar]].<ref name="Night">{{Acolyte|Night}}</ref>
