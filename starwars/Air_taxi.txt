'''Air taxis''', also sometimes called '''repulsor taxis'''<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> or '''air cabs''',<ref name="Encyclopedia booklet">{{EncyclopediaCite|Miscellaneous Ships and Vehicles (2)}}</ref> were a type of small, [[airspeeder]] [[taxi]] used as passenger [[transport]]s on [[planet]]s with air traffic. On the [[ecumenopolis]] [[Coruscant]], air taxis were a popular means of transportation for passengers<ref name="2018Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles]]''</ref> and one of the planet's most characteristic sites. They were allowed unrestricted "free travel" that allowed them to leave the official autonavigated skylanes that confined most other vehicles on long journeys along defined corridors to prevent unmanageable traffic chaos<ref name="2020Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and also utilized [[airbus]] lanes.<ref name="SWCL" /> To be rated "free travel", air taxi [[pilot]]s had to pass demanding tests to proved their skills and their abilities to navigate the cityscape with safety. The vehicle's receivers also monitored the many channels of the [[Coruscant Air Traffic Control]] to allow the taxi drivers increased flexibility.<ref name="2020Vehicles" /> Some air taxis on Coruscant were also [[Taxi droid|piloted by droids]].<ref name="Convert">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}}</ref>

Air taxi models used on Coruscant included the open-air [[EasyRide passenger airspeeder]] and the larger [[ComfortRide passenger airspeeder]], both manufactured by [[Hyrotii Corporation]] and colloquially known as Coruscant air taxis;<ref name="2018Vehicles" /> a closed model known as a [[Coruscant cab]]<ref name="Orders concept">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/orders-concept-art-gallery|text="Orders" Concept Art Gallery}}</ref> and the small [[hover taxi]]s.<ref name="Lethal Trackdown Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lethal-trackdown-trivia-gallery|text="Lethal Trackdown" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref>
