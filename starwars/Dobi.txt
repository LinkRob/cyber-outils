'''Dobi''' was [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Sergeant/Legends|sergeant]] who served as a Pop-Dog, a soldier loyal to corrupt [[Galactic Federation of Free Alliances|Galactic Alliance]] [[General/Legends|General]] [[Stavin Thaal]] by [[44 ABY]] on [[Vandor-3]]. She and her partner, [[Corporal/Legends|Corporal]] [[Vitkins]], were called in to a small dwelling to investigate the reports of domestic disturbance. They soon found themselves caught in a trap by the [[Quad-Linked Militant Pacifists]], a fictitious terrorist organization that served as a front for [[Wraith Squadron]]. Dobi and Vitkins were both tied up after they fell for the trap, though they were later discovered by later parties of Pop-Dogs who freed them.<ref name="MK" />
