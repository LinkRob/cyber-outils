"'''A Question of Survival'''" is the third episode of [[The Empire Strikes Back (radio)|''The Empire Strikes Back'' radio drama]]. It first aired on [[National Public Radio]] on [[February 28]], [[1983]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The episode adapts events of {{Film|V}} taking place on the planet [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]]. It depicts the rescue of [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]] and [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and the discovery of [[Echo Base/Legends|Echo base]] by an [[Viper probe droid/Legends|imperial probe droid]].

==Plot summary==
[[File:RogueFlight-ESBRadio.flac|thumb|Rieekan and Leia watch the speeders of Rogue Flight depart on their mission.]]
In Echo Base, [[Carlist Rieekan/Legends|General Rieekan]] oversees the launch of the effort to find and rescue the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] heroes [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo/Legends|Han Solo]], who have been lost all night in the frozen wastes of [[Hoth/Legends|Hoth]]. As he and [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Princess Leia]] look on, the base's [[shield door]] opens and the [[Snowspeeder/Legends|snowspeeders]] of [[Rogue Squadron/Legends|Rogue Flight]] take off. Han Solo and Luke Skywalker are awake in their [[emergency shelter]]. They have survived the freezing night and are trying to make contact with the base. They learn that rescue is coming when they hear a comm signal from [[Rogue Two/Legends|Rogue Two]], who has detected the [[Homing beacon/Legends|homing beacon]] that Han brought along.

Back at the base, the [[Medical droid/Legends|medical droid]] [[2-1B/Legends|Too-Onebee]] tends to Luke. He is recovering quickly thanks to his time in a [[Bacta tank/Legends|regeneration tank]], and he can return to duty soon. Luke's first visitors are [[R2-D2/Legends|Artoo-Detoo]] and [[C-3PO/Legends|See-Threepio]], followed quickly by Han and [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]]. All are overjoyed to see him. Threepio express worry about the strange energy source detected by the base's new network of warning sensors. Leia arrives next, and Han tries to flirt with her again. Leia responds by giving Luke a long kiss to welcome him back. Then she departs for the command center, leaving Han annoyed, Chewie amused, and Luke with an elevated heart rate that concerns the droids. 

In the command center, General Rieekan discusses the unidentified blip with Leia, soon joined by the others coming from Luke's room. A [[Controller/Legends|controller]] picks up an audio signal which Threepio suspects to be an imperial code. Han volunteers to go out with Chewbacca to investigate; Rieekan orders speeders to fly in close formation nearby. Han and Chewbacca see an [[Unidentified probe droid (Hoth)|unidentified droid]] floating across the terrain and recognize it as a danger. Han decides to attack it right away rather than wait for the speeders. Chewbacca creates a diversion and Han shoots. The droid's response is to immediately destroy itself, confirming it to be an [[Viper probe droid/Legends|imperial probe droid]]. Rieekan and Leia decide to begin the base's evacuation immediately.

Far away, [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] strides through a starship buzzing with the sounds of officers and techs. He approaches [[Kendal Ozzel/Legends|Admiral Ozzel]] and asks about the status of the search for the Rebels. Ozzel responds proudly that the search proceeds ahead of schedule&mdash;but Vader impatiently criticizes the lack of results. While Ozzel protests that more ships are needed, [[Firmus Piett/Legends|Captain Piett]] interrupts with news. Ozzel orders him to stand by, but Vader insists on hearing what the captain has to say. A probe droid has found a piece of heavy equipment in the barren [[Hoth system/Legends|Hoth system]]. The droid was destroyed quickly, suggesting that someone attacked it. Ozzel dismisses this as just another unproven lead, but through [[The Force/Legends|the Force]], Vader is confident that it is the Rebel base. He orders Ozzel to take the fleet to Hoth and [[Maximilian Veers/Legends|General Veers]] to prepare his [[Stormtrooper/Legends|Stormtroopers]] for a cold-weather ground assault.

==Continuity==
"A Question of Survival" very closely follows the events of {{Film|V}}. The opening, depicting the launch of the Rogue Flight speeders, is the only entirely new scene (the radio dramas consistently refer to "flights" rather than "groups" or "squadrons" of fighters). Some additional dialogue shows Ozzel trying to put Piett in his place, further developing the rivalry between the two officers. Luke and Leia's kiss lasts fourteen seconds, far longer than in the film.<ref name="Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back">{{Film|V}}</ref>
