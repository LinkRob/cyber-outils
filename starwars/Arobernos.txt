The '''Arobernos''' were a large, [[Sentience|sentient]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Color|red]]-skinned [[species]] with pointed [[ear]]s. They could survive being engulfed in [[Magma|lava]].<ref name="Vader7" /> One [[Unidentified blind Arobernos|member]] of the species was blind and locked in symbiosis with the [[Eye of Webbish Bog]] on the [[planet]] [[Mustafar]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Arobernos first appeared in [[Rae Carson]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: Expanded Edition]]'', the novelization of the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']], the final installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[sequel trilogy]].<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> It was later identified in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''.<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> The species was originally created for ''The Rise of Skywalker'' itself, though the scene in which it appeared was cut before release.<ref name="Eye Cut">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/rise-of-skywalker-eye-of-webbish-bog-scene-was-filmed-why-it-was-cut|text=Rise of Skywalker': The Eye of Webbish Bog Scene Was Filmed&mdash;Here's Why It Was Cut|author=Chitwood, Adam|date=2020-03-06|archivedate=20200307020625}}</ref>
