'''Fhaen''' was a [[Imperial officer|officer]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Army]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Fhaen held the [[military rank]] of [[lieutenant]] and commanded a [[Fhaen's company|company]] of [[stormtrooper]]s on [[New Valis]] fresh out of the [[Imperial Academy]]. During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[liberation of New Valis]], Lieutenant Fhaen and his forces were defeated by the Alliance [[Marine Corps]].<ref name="Lead" />
