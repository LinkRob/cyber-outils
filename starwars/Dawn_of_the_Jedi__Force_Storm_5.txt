'''''Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm'' 5''' is the fifth issue in the [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: Force Storm|''Force Storm'']] story arc of the ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series and the fifth issue of the series in total. Written by [[John Ostrander]] and [[Jan Duursema]], the issue was scripted by Ostrander, pencilled by Duursema, colored by [[Wes Dzioba]], inked by [[Dan Parsons]], and lettered by [[Michael Heisler]], and it was released on [[June 20]], [[2012]]. The issue sees the end of the confrontation between the [[Force Hound]] [[Tau (individual)|Xesh]] and the [[Je'daii Order]] in the [[Rift]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Tython/Legends|Tython]], as well as the end of the titular [[Force Storm (Tython)|Force Storm]]. The end of the issue sees Xesh [[Exile (punishment)/Legends|exiled]] to the [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Bogan]], setting up the events of [[Star Wars: Dawn of the Jedi: The Prisoner of Bogan|''The Prisoner of Bogan'']] arc.

==Publisher's summary==
''On ancient Tython, the Force is out of balance, causing a massive Force storm which could destroy the entire planet!''

''As three mighty Je'daii Masters strive to subdue the storm, three of their students struggle to forge an uneasy alliance with the dark stranger whose presence created the storm in the first place.''

''As the first arc of the epic tale comes to a close, powers beyond even the Je'daii's understanding will be revealed, and a threat to the galaxy at large will be unveiled!''

==Plot summary==
The [[Force Hound]] [[Tau (individual)|Xesh]] abandoned the [[Je'daii Journeyer]]s who saved him from the [[saarl]] in the [[Rift]], but he remains behind to watch them fight. However, [[Shae Koda]]'s demonstration of the [[Light side of the Force/Legends|light side of the Force]] and her determination to protect her friends intrigues the Force Hound, who returns to join Koda in her fight as [[Je'daii Ranger]]s [[Rori Fenn]] and [[Hawk Ryo]] arrive to aid them. Xesh succeeds in killing the saarl, but the [[Force Storm (Tython)|Force Storm]] continues to grow in power with the Force Hound at its center. With the aid of the other [[Je'daii Temple Master]]s, [[Quan-Jang]] shifts the Force back into balance and ends the storm, though his apparent [[Death/Legends|death]] in doing so leads an enraged Koda to activate Xesh's [[Forcesaber]]&mdash;a feat only the Force Hound had been capable of&mdash;and to try and kill Xesh in revenge before she comes to her senses. Xesh, Quan-Jang, and the others are healed of their injuries at [[Mahara Kesh]], the Temple of Healing, but despite the Journeyers' protests, the Je'daii Council exiles Xesh to [[Bogan]] so that he can find balance.<ref name="Storm 5" />
