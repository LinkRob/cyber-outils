The '''Executor-50.x engine''' was a type of [[engine]] manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]].<ref name="BMF 27" /> With thirteen equipped to the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnoughts]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|naval forces]],<ref name="Episode V" /> the engine gave the ''Executor''-class a maximum acceleration speed of 1,230 [[G (unit)|G]], and a top speed of 40 [[Megalight per hour|MGLT]].<ref name="BMF 27" />
