'''Barayfe''' was an astronomical object<ref name="The Living Force" /> in the [[Barayfe system]] in the [[Alchenaut sector]] of the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> where the [[Jedi Order]] maintained a [[Jedi outpost]] during the [[Republic Era]]. It was one of several, alongside [[Janaus]], [[Lesser Tontakoh]], and [[Kwenn]], that were included on a list of locations no longer frequented by the [[Jedi]] that could be closed down to balance additional presence in places where the [[Galactic Senate]] expected growth. The list was presented to the [[Jedi High Council]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Adi Gallia]], upon which Master [[Yarael Poof]] made a remark on the low population of Barayfe and the other locations.<ref name="The Living Force" />
