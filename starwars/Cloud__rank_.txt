'''Cloud''' was a rank within the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization. Clouds served under a [[Tempest]] and a [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] and above [[Strike]]s. Clouds were identified by two lightning bolt symbols.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> Individuals that held the rank of Cloud included the [[Crocin]] [[Scarspike]],<ref name="The Rising Storm">''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]''</ref> the [[human]]s [[Vark Tarpalin]]<ref name="EFV">''[[The High Republic: Escape from Valo]]''</ref> and [[Dent (Nihil)|Dent]], the [[Devaronian]] [[Belial]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> the [[Gloovan]] [[Sarn Starbreaker]],<ref name="The Rising Storm" /> and the [[Bivall]] [[Quin Carree]].<ref name="Tempest Runner">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>
