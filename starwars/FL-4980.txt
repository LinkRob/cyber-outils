'''FL-4980''' was a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] of the [[First Order]] who was stationed aboard the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|warship]] ''[[Supremacy]]'' at some point between [[27 ABY]] and [[28 ABY]]. Aboard the [[starship]], they met the newly arrived [[trooper]] [[Jannah|TZ-1719]] and gave her some helpful advice. However, once they learned TZ-1719 was part of [[Company 77]], FL-4980 warned her of the [[company]]'s reputation for [[blood]]thirst. Later, FL-4980 themself was part of the company during a [[Battle of Ansett Island|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Epo]]. There, the trooper followed TZ-1719 and the rest of the company in refusing to carry out an order to [[Death|execute]] a group of captured [[smuggler]]s, with the stormtroopers laying down their [[weapon]]s and ultimately [[Desertion|deserting]] the First Order.

==Biography==
===Welcoming the new arrival===
{{Quote|I prefer not to think about it.|FL-4980 first speaks to TZ-1719|<ref name="TLO" />}}
[[File:Supremacy-USWNE.png|thumb|right|320px|FL-4980 was stationed aboard the ''Supremacy''.]]
FL-4980<ref name="TLO" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[squad leader]] in the [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]] of the [[First Order]] in service<ref name="TLO" /> at some point between [[27 ABY]] and [[28 ABY]],<ref name="FlashbackDate">{{TLOFlashbackDate}}</ref> For a time they were based out of the ''[[Supremacy]]'',<ref name="TLO" /> a vast [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|warship]] that served as the mobile [[capital]] of the First Order,<ref name="Supremacy DB">{{Databank|the-supremacy|The Supremacy}}</ref> becoming able to recognize [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] that were newly arrived aboard the [[starship]]. At one point, they were waiting in a line with other [[trooper]]s to board a [[magnet]]ic [[troop transport]] [[tram]] heading to the ship's staging area when they noticed the similarly recently-arrived trooper [[Jannah|TZ-1719]] taking in the large dimensions of the ''Supremacy''{{'s}} interior. Addressing the other [[squad]] leader, FL-4980 advised her to not pay too much attention to it. They also briefly informed TZ-1719 of the nature of the staging area before bluntly cutting off the trooper [[FL-8007]] when he brought up the topic of [[Death penalty|executions]] of First Order personnel.<ref name="TLO" />

When the stormtroopers arrived at the staging area, FL-4980 noticed that TZ-1719's squad was part of [[Company 77]], which had a reputation of being [[blood]]thirsty and volunteering for the duty of executing other troopers. TZ-1719, wishing to thank the other trooper for their help, noticed their reaction of intense dislike. When the perplexed TZ-1719 revealed that the [[company]] had shipped in from [[Locke Station]], FL-4980 eventually relayed that one of the First Order [[morale report]]s broadcast to stormtroopers had claimed that a squad leader of Company 77 aboard that [[space station]] had [[Death|killed]] a [[pirate]] and then used their body as a lure to kill other pirates&mdash;with TZ-1719 in actuality being said squad leader and the claim being false. After a brief moment during which TZ-1719 thought FL-4980 was studying her and before the latter departed to join their own formation, they gave a warning to TZ-1719 about her company's reputation.<ref name="TLO" />

===Joining in refusal===
{{Quote|COMPANY. About face!|Sergeant Teena Calcon to Company 77, immediately before her and FL-4980 refusal to carry out their orders|<ref name="TLO" />}}
[[File:Epo-GalaxyMap.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Company 77, including FL-4980, was dispatched on a mission to Epo (depicted on map).]]
Not long thereafter, FL-4980 themself was transferred to Company 77 for an [[Battle of Ansett Island|operation]] on [[Ansett Island]] of the [[planet]] [[Epo]]<ref name="TLO" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Ciutric sector]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}} &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Epo system]]</ref> During the mission, the company's [[Captain]] [[Shikra]] ordered the execution of a group of captured [[smuggler]]s who were transporting [[Youngling|infants]] orphaned by a previous [[Merchant Uprising|massacre]] carried out on Shikra's orders.<ref name="TLO" />

Affected by the captain's violent actions and attitude, TZ-1719 and her squad refused to carry out the order and laid down their [[weapon]]s. The company's [[Sergeant]] [[Teena Calcon]] followed suit, with FL-8007 and then FL-4980 doing the same, after which the entire company likewise carried out the act of insubordination. Ultimately, the company went on to [[Desertion|desert]] the First Order and, under TZ-1719 and Cancon's leadership, leave Epo aboard the commandeered [[light cruiser]] ''[[Culverin]]''.<ref name="TLO" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Stuff it, Knives. Ignore him. He's going to find himself on the wrong end of a laser ax if he doesn't ''shut up''.|FL-4980, to TZ-1719|<ref name="TLO" />}}
FL-4980 was respected by their fellow stormtroopers. They espoused the belief that a First Order stormtrooper should concentrate on survival and other concerns more pressing than contemplating the large size of the ''Supremacy'', and they were willing to provide helpful advice to newly arrived troopers on the warship. They also disapproved of the casualness with which their fellow trooper FL-8007 brought up in a conversation the topic of stormtrooper executions, expressing their belief that FL-8007 was thus endangering himself.<ref name="TLO" />

[[File:Company77-TheRiseOfSkywalker3.png|thumb|right|300px|FL-4980 followed TZ-1719 (center) in refusing to carry out their orders on Ansett Island.]]
In addition, FL-4980 harbored a deep dislike for Company 77's reputation for being casual and even eager about killing others, to the point where, upon learning that TZ-1719 was part of the company, they adopted a stiff posture and held their [[Limb|hands]] at their sides while clenched into fists. Later, after they had themself been transferred to the company, FL-4980&mdash;like TZ-1719 and the rest of the company's troopers&mdash;were motivated by Captain Shikra's open disregard for life to refuse an order to kill a group of captured smugglers, which ultimately led to their and the company's desertion from the First Order.<ref name="TLO" />

==Equipment==
FL-4980 donned [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] with a [[Color|white]] squad leader [[pauldron]] and wielded an [[F-11D blaster rifle]].<ref name="TLO" />
