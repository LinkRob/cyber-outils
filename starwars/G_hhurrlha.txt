'''G'hhurrlha''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Wookiee/Legends|Wookiee]] [[Big game hunter/Legends|Big-game hunter]]. He owned an [["Explorer" Scout Ship]] and customized the [[Hull/Legends|hull]] with skulls, [[Bone/Legends|bones]] and [[Chitin/Legends|chitin]] shells. The main room was decorated with [[Krayt dragon/Legends|Krayt dragon]], [[Acklay/Legends|acklay]], [[Lylek/Legends|lylek]], [[Rancor/Legends|rancor]] and [[swamp wampa]] trophies.<ref name="ETO" />
