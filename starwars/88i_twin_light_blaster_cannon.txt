The '''88i twin light blaster cannon''' was a model of light [[blaster cannon]] manufactured by [[Yaove Gunsmiths]]. It was part of the standard armaments on [[All Terrain Scout Transport]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Military|military forces]]. Mounted on the port side of the [[walker]]'s command module, it was capable of rotating in a 360 degree arc.<ref name="AT-ACT Deluxe Book" />
