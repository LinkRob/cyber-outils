The '''assault on Starlight Beacon'''<ref name="THR 6" /> occurred during the [[High Republic Era]] when the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] came under attack by the [[Drengir]], a [[species]] of [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s. The attack was a result of the [[Jedi Order]] bringing aboard the [[Death|corpse]]<ref name="THR 4" /> of a [[Unidentified Hutt trader|Hutt trader]] whose [[starship]] had been [[Attack in the Kazlin system|attacked]] by [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]] after departing the [[planet]] [[Sedri Minor]] with a cargo of [[Vratixia renanicus|Vratixian barley]].<ref name="THR 2" /> The [[Hutt]]'s system had been infected by the Drengir, who burst from the corpse while the Jedi were performing an autopsy. [[Jedi Master]] [[Estala Maru]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] proceeded to fend off the Drengir as Maru attempted to contact [[Marshal]] [[Avar Kriss]] for assistance; Kriss, however, was occupied with [[Mission to Sedri Minor|events]] on Sedri Minor.<ref name="THR 4" />
