A '''capital ship''' was any large [[Warship|military]] [[starship]]<ref name="SW EOSAOV (SE)">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)]]''</ref> greater than 100 [[meter]]s in length, and designed for [[Realspace|deep-space]] [[Space warfare|warfare]].<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> Boasting numerous heavy weapons and [[Deflector shield|shields]], capital ships had crews that numbered in the hundreds, if not the thousands. In their vast [[hangar]] bays, they often carried [[shuttle]]s, [[starfighter]]s, and other spacecraft.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> Types of capital [[Starship|ships]] included [[Star Destroyer]]s, [[Star Cruiser]]s, and [[battleship]]s.<ref name="VE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> Capital ships were commonly classified by the [[Anaxes War College System]] depending on their size, role, and firepower.<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref>

Although imposing, capital ships were also slow and ponderous in spaceflight. Due to this, the [[New Republic]], following the [[Galactic Civil War]], was able to, over [[time]], pick off most of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s capital ships by overwhelming them with smaller, nimbler vessels, like [[bomber]]s.<ref name="lifedebt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref> [[Sub-capital ship]]s were starships smaller than capital ships.<ref name="Dawn">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref>
