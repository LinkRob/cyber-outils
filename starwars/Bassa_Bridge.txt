'''Bassa Bridge''' was a [[Color|tan]]-colored man-made bridge constructed to cross the [[Solleu River]] in the [[city]] of [[Theed]], [[capital]] of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] culture on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="SWCL2016" /> By [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of {{Film|I}}, as depicted in [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']], to [[32 BBY]].</ref> [[Pergola's Bridge]], which was located further upstream, had become the main crossing point on the Solleu, taking some of the pressure off the more fragile Bassa Bridge.<ref name="SWCL2016" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Bassa Bridge was first mentioned in "Theed: Naboo's Capital City," an article in the "[[Guide to the Galaxy]]" department of the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 24|twenty fourth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BMF24">{{BuildFalconCite|24|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> which was released on around [[June 17]], [[2015]]<ref name="BMFdate">{{BMFdates|24}}</ref> Pergola's Bridge was then pictured in canon in the [[2016]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]. It was part of material reprinted<ref name="SWCL2016" /> from the [[2000]] reference book ''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]'', which was written by [[Kristin Lund]], illustrated by [[Richard Chasemore]] and [[Hans Jenssen]], and marked the first mention of the [[Bassa Bridge/Legends|bridge]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Inside">''[[Inside the Worlds of Star Wars: Episode I]]''</ref>
