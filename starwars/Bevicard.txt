'''Bevicard''' was a [[senator]] in the [[New Republic]]'s [[New Republic Senate|Galactic Senate]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> in [[28 ABY]].<ref name="Timeframe">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']], including the mention of Bevicard, to [[28 ABY]].</ref> At around this [[time]], a reception was thrown for him on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], and Senator [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] received an invitation. Since Bevicard was a member of the [[Centrists]] faction within the Senate, Organa knew that as a member of the opposing [[Populists]] faction she had only received the invitation as a symbolic gesture, and that Bevicard would be mortified if she actually attended. Organa had her [[Aide|assistant]] [[Greer Sonnel]] send a refusal the [[Standard day|day]] after receiving the invitation, making sure it was extra courteous since the senator considered Bevicard to be honest about what he was despite comparing him to a [[snake]].<ref name="Bloodline" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bevicard was first mentioned in [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']], a novel written by [[Claudia Gray]] and released in [[2016]].<ref name="Bloodline" />
