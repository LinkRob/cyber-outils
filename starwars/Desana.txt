'''Desana''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who was the daughter of [[Aryssha Starros|Aryssha]] of the [[Starros Clan]] and [[Cerasus Ehllo]], an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[captain]]. They were [[Reproduction|born]], along with their [[twin]] [[Thevera]], on the [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvette]] ''[[King's Ransom]]''<ref name="Sana Starros 5" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Their name was a mix of the first names of [[Deva Lompop]], a member of the Starros Clan, and Aryssha's cousin [[Sana Starros]].<ref name="Sana Starros 5" />
