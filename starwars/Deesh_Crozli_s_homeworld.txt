The '''homeworld''' of the [[Deesh Crozli's species|alien]] [[Deesh Crozli]] had an [[atmosphere]] that differed from that of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ueda]], which was breathable to [[human]]s. As such, Crozli needed to periodically immerse her soft tissues in an atmospheric mixture that matched that of her [[homeworld]] when on Ueda and so wore a [[pressure-mask]] and [[gas mixture monitor]] while there<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> in [[132 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Deesh Crozli's homeworld was mentioned in the [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />
