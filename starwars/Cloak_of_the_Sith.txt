The '''Cloak of the Sith''' was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Abrion sector]], with the [[Death Wind Corridor]] passing through. The [[smuggler]] [[Tryphon Leo]] marked it as a label for the Death Wind in the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]'' [[book]].

==Description==
[[File:Cloak-of-the-Sith-SG.jpg|thumb|right|The Cloak of the Sith lay along the Death Wind Corridor.]]
The Cloak of the Sith was located in the [[Abrion sector]] within the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Slice" /> Situated at the coordinates S-15 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was passed through by the [[Death Wind Corridor]] [[hyperspace route]], which linked the [[Ryloth system|Ryloth]] and [[Roon system]]s and formed part of the [[Spice Triangle]] trade network.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> The location shared its name with the [[Sith]], an ancient order of [[Force-sensitive]] adherents of the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="Sith DB">{{Databank|sith|Sith}}</ref>

==History==
By [[10 BBY]],<ref name="10 BBY">The entries in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'' that involve the [[Spice Triangle]] and the Cloak of the Sith are placed [[Time|chronologically]] prior to an entry written by [[Tobias Beckett]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates Beckett's [[Showdown on Savareen|death]] to [[10 BBY]], and so his entry in the guide and those chronologically before it must all be set at some point by that date.</ref> the [[Pyke Syndicate]] controlled underworld trade in the Spice Triangle, which was noted by the former [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] in a [[book]] that was eventually named the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. After the [[smuggler]] [[Tryphon Leo]] stole the book, they planned to lock down trade along the network. Leo wrote about their plans in the book and in the entry included a [[map]] of the Spice Triangle with the Cloak of the Sith marked as the label for the Death Wind Corridor. Leo's plans were cut short, however, when they [[Death|died]] trying to reach one of Kanata's [[Star map|mapped]] treasure caches in order to fund their plans.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Cloak of the Sith was first introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] through the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]]. In the book, "Cloak of the Sith" was labeled on a hyperspace route implied in the prose to be the Death Wind Corridor.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> However, the Cloak of the Sith was treated as a location in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Systems" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]&mdash;both works being written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> Via email, Fry specified his treatment of the Cloak of the Sith and suspected the label in the ''Smuggler's Guide'' to be an "art error."<ref name="Fry email">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-07-07.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-07-07}}</ref> This article therefore treats the Cloak of the Sith as a location.

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Cloak of the Sith/Legends|Cloak of the Sith]] was a [[Cosmic dust/Legends|cosmic dust]] [[Cloud/Legends|cloud]], in which the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Roon/Legends|Roon]] was located. It first appeared in "[[Tail of the Roon Comets]]," the tenth episode of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: Droids: The Adventures of R2-D2 and C-3PO]]''<ref name="Roon Comets">{{DroidsCite|Tail of the Roon Comets}}</ref> that aired on [[November 7]], [[1985]].<ref name="CELibration">{{InsiderCite|27|A Star Wars CELibration|A ''Star Wars'' CELibration}}</ref>
