'''Attacker One''' was the designation provided to a [[heavy cruiser]] by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]. Accompanied by three other [[heavy cruiser]]s, Attacker One was sent by the [[Lioaoin Regime]] to [[Attack at Primea|attack]] the [[Chiss]] [[warship]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']] on behalf of the [[Nikardun Destiny]] when the ''Vigilant'' visited the [[planet]] [[Primea]] to collect [[Senior Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]]. Attackers One and [[Attacker Two|Two]] provided flanking fire upon the ''Vigilant''{{'s}} starboard [[hull]]. However, with Thrawn damaging [[Attacker Four]] in a [[Thrawn's patrol boat|commandeered]] [[Patrol ship|Vak patrol boat]], the ''Vigilant'' was able to use Four as cover to escape the [[Primea system]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
