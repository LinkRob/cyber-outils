'''Fish Crackers''' were a type of [[Cheese|cheesy]], [[fish]]-shaped [[cracker]] that was suitable for [[human]] consumption. The snack was served on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="GS" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GSDate">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Fish Crackers were available as a snack in the [[In-universe|real world]] to guests staying at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="GS" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref> They were last served at the hotel during the [[September 28]][[September 29|&ndash;]][[September 30|30]] voyage of [[2023]], the final voyage before the closure of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser.<ref name="Official website">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=star-wars-galactic-starcruiser/overview|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser - Overview}}</ref> Fish Crackers are in actuality the real-world snack [[Wikipedia:Goldfish_(cracker)|Goldfish]].<ref name="SpaceSnacks">{{WebCite|url=https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/2022/02/25/review-why-space-snacks-look-awfully-familiar-at-the-star-wars-hotel-in-disney-world|text=REVIEW: Why Space Snacks Look Awfully Familiar at the Star Wars Hotel in Disney World|author=McOmber, Sara|date=2022-02-25|work=[https://www.disneyfoodblog.com/ disneyfoodblog.com]|archivedate=20220703202120}}</ref>
