'''Chalcedon''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> located in the [[Chalcedon system]] in the [[Tashtor sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], in grid square I-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Located on the [[Great Gran Run]] between [[Cerea]] and [[Takodana]], it was the center of the [[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Slavery|slave trade]].<ref name="TFA Locations" /> Chalcedon also housed a [[shadowport]] and was marked on a map included by the former [[pirate]] [[Queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] in the [[book]] that became known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''. The [[Enslaver|slavemaster]] [[Krastic Tarta]] filled his ship, the ''[[Arkanian Dawn]]'', with slaves that he intended to sell on Chalcedon, but upon landing at the [[Cyklo market]] the slaves staged a revolt and seized the ship instead.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
