The '''''Cobalt Hammer''''' was an [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] in the [[Resistance]]'s [[Cobalt Squadron]].

==History==
[[File:PaigeTicoMG100-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|The ''Cobalt Hammer'' participated in the evacuation of D'Qar]]
The ''Cobalt Hammer'' took part in the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]]<ref name="ICS" /> participated in the bombing run on a [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]]. Several [[bomber]]s were destroyed by [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] and debris, leaving only the ''Cobalt Hammer'' to drop bombs in the last hope to destroy the [[Dreadnought|Dreadnaught]], the ''[[Fulminatrix]]''. However, [[Nix Jerd]], the [[bombardier]], was knocked unconscious. This meant [[Paige Tico]], a [[gunner]], had to activate the bombs. [[Gender|She]] climbed up the ladder and was preparing to activate them when a TIE fighter blew up the [[cockpit]], [[Death|killing]] the [[pilot]], [[Finch Dallow]], and the [[flight engineer]]. The resulting explosion threw Tico off the ladder and down to a catwalk. After kicking the access ladder several times the [[detonator]] fell down. She caught it and dropped the bombs on the Dreadnaught. The resulting explosion destroyed both the Dreadnaught and the ''Cobalt Hammer'', killing all aboard the two doomed ships.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
