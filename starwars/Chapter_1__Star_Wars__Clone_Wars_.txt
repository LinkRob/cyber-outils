"'''Chapter 1'''" is the first episode of the [[2003]]&ndash;[[2005]] award-winning animated microseries ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''. First aired on [[Cartoon Network]] on [[November 7]], [[2003]] and released online on the Cartoon Network website and [[StarWars.com]], the episode served as the [[Wikipedia:Television pilot|pilot episode]] for the series. The episode ran over three minutes and filled a five-minute television slot. "Chapter 1" was directed by famous animator [[Genndy Tartakovsky]] and written by Tartakovsky, [[Darrick Bachman]], [[Bryan Andrews]], and [[Paul Rudish]]. The episode featured both [[Wikipedia:Computer animation|computer animation]] and [[Wikipedia:Traditional animation|traditional animation]], with characters stylized after the actors who portrayed them in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] ([[2002]]), though it featured none of the original film actors as voice actors for their characters. The episode was later released on [[Wikipedia:DVD|DVD]] with the rest of [[Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]] and [[Clone Wars: Season Two|Season Two]] on the [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume One]] collection. The pilot helped usher in a massive [[Clone Wars multimedia project|multimedia project]] that produced numerous comics and novels, a [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|video game]], action figures, and short stories. The show would later go on to inspire the [[2008]] computer-generated television show ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.

==Plot summary==
{{Quote|Like fire across the galaxy, the Clone Wars spread. In league with the wicked Count Dooku more and more planets slip. Against this threat, upon the Jedi Knights falls the duty to lead the newly formed army of the Republic. And as the heat of war grows, so too grows the prowess of one most gifted student of the Force.|Yoda's introductory narration|<ref name="Episode" />}}
[[File:V-19.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Kenobi's forces depart from Coruscant.]]
[[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Grand Master/Legends|Grand Master]] [[Yoda/Legends|Yoda]] explains the situation of the [[The galaxy/Legends|galaxy]]-spanning [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]] and the rebelling [[Confederacy of Independent Systems/Legends|Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. Battles rage across [[Planet/Legends|planets]] such as [[Dantooine/Legends|Dantooine]], [[Dac/Legends|Dac]], and many others. Members of the [[Jedi Order/Legends|Jedi Order]] such as Yoda, [[Shaak Ti/Legends|Shaak Ti]], [[Saesee Tiin/Legends|Saesee Tiin]], [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]], [[Luminara Unduli/Legends|Luminara Unduli]], [[Barriss Offee/Legends|Barriss Offee]], [[Kit Fisto/Legends|Kit Fisto]], [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi/Legends|Obi-Wan Kenobi]] lead [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] forces into battle across the galaxy, the [[Quarren Isolation League/Legends|Quarren Isolation League]] pledges its support to [[Count/Legends|Count]] [[Dooku/Legends|Dooku]] and the [[Separatist Droid Army/Legends|Separatist Droid Army]], and Anakin Skywalker's abilities in [[The Force/Legends|the Force]] continue to grow.<ref name="Episode" />

Yoda, Kenobi, and Skywalker have a meeting with [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] in [[Chancellor's Suite/Legends|his suite]] on the Republic [[Capital/Legends|capital]] of [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. Kenobi informs the Chancellor that the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan/Legends|InterGalactic Banking Clan]] has built huge hidden factories on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Muunilinst/Legends|Muunilinst]] and is producing massive [[Droid/Legends|droid]] armies and [[Warship/Legends|warships]], going on to state that they must act quickly. Palpatine concurs, but asks who would be available to lead the mission, seeing as Windu was fighting the [[Battle of Dantooine (Clone Wars)|Battle of Dantooine]]. Yoda suggests Kenobi lead the mission, and Kenobi states his army is ready and able to leave immediately. Palpatine then suggests Skywalker take command of Kenobi's space forces during the [[Battle of Muunilinst/Legends|battle]] due to his exceptional skills, but Kenobi feels Skywalker isn't ready for the responsibility, and Yoda states that a [[Padawan/Legends|Padawan's]] place is with his [[Jedi Master/Legends|master]]. The Grand Master admits Skywalker is powerful, and begins to make another statement, but he is interrupted by Palpatine, who decides for the group that Skywalker will indeed go, much to Yoda's and Kenobi's frustration.<ref name="Episode" />

Kenobi's forces begin loading into several [[Acclamator-class transgalactic military assault ship/Legends|''Acclamator''-class assault ships]] and preparing their [[V-19 Torrent starfighter/Legends|V-19 Torrent starfighters]] as the Jedi considers the challenge ahead, doubting even his old master [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]] could have prepared someone for it. Young Skywalker enters his ''[[Azure Angel]]'' and takes off with the rest of Kenobi's army, waving goodbye to his secret love [[Padmé Amidala/Legends|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Episode" />

==Development==
{{Quote|Lucas was interested in doing an animated project, but they even told us, they said, like, George wanted our interpretation of it, to a degree.|Genndy Tartakovsky|<ref name="VolumeOne" />}}
[[File:CW1_artwork.png|thumb|right|200px|Artwork for "Chapter 1"]]
[[George Lucas]] approached [[Genndy Tartakovsky]] regarding his interest in a ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Wikipedia:Animation|animated]] show to bridge the gap between [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] ([[2002]]) and [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']] ([[2005]]), partially due to the lack of action figure sales [[Hasbro]] was experiencing under the ''[[Star Wars]]'' brand.<ref name="BDH">{{WebCite|url=http://www.browndailyherald.com/2008/11/17/cartoon-network-animator-traces-his-path-to-the-top|text=Cartoon Network animator traces his path to the top|author=Sedano, Caroline|date=2008-11-17|work=[http://www.browndailyherald.com The Brown Daily Herald]|archivedate=20190313201032}}</ref> Telling Tartakovsky that he wanted the animator's own interpretation, to a degree, Lucas felt it was important that the team leave their own signature on the saga by coming up with their own stories. They decided on dividing the story up into twenty three-minute episodes of one continuous story with a few sidetracks. Regarding animation, the team decided to stylize the look of the film actors from ''Attack of the Clones,'' but without being too close to the original. The idea the team came up with for the main title was that of a transmission sent out from [[The galaxy/Legends|the galaxy]] that people on Earth were able to pick up on their television sets.<ref name="VolumeOne" />

The first scene of the chapter, with Yoda riding a creature across a desert into battle, was the first idea Tartakovsky thought of when asked about ''Star Wars''. One of the most difficult aspects of animating the show was attempting to create massive armies. The team decided to use [[Wikipedia:Computer animation|computer-generated imagery]] for the ships in order to properly handle complicated moves, but went ahead with [[Wikipedia:Traditional animation|traditional animation]] on the rest. So as to avoid making the computer-animation stand out, the team decided against over-rendering the non-traditional parts. In order to correctly showcase large battles and scenes with Jedi, Tartakovsky studied the battle scenes from the films, comparing those with the fighting styles of [[Wikipedia:Samurai|samurai]]. The team also decided against covering Skywalker's mechanical hand, as they felt it looked more impressive to have the golden hand visible. In order to create Kenobi's large army, the team took single clones and multiplied them using computer programs.<ref name="VolumeOne" />

Initially, Tartakovsky was worried about the voice acting for the episodes, though this concern did not last, as he regarded the actors as extremely talented and was pleased with how they portrayed the characters. The team was able to "cartoon up" the voices, making the process more enjoyable for the team, rather than long and enduring. The thoughts of Kenobi regarding his master were added in later in the process than usual for voiceovers since the team thought it would be interesting to have Kenobi reflect back on Qui-Gon Jinn.<ref name="VolumeOne" />

"Chapter 1" aired on the [[Cartoon Network]] on [[November 7]], [[2003]], running over three minutes long and filling a five-minute time slot, and was released on the Cartoon Network website and [[StarWars.com]] as the first installment of [[Clone Wars: Season One|Season One]] in the twenty-five-episode microseries ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="Episode" /> The chapter was included in the [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume One]] DVD released on [[March 22]], [[2005]].<ref name="VolumeOne" /> It was also collected in a bonuys DVD released specially marked [[Hasbro]] [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']] lightsabers.

==Release and reception==
[[File:SWCloneWars-VolumeOne.jpg|thumb|right|150px|The ''Volume One'' DVD]]
According to Tartakovsky, the first twenty episodes were well-received, though there were complaints about the short length of the chapters.<ref name="VolumeOne" /> Season One won the Emmy Award for "Outstanding Animated Program (For Programming One Hour or More)" the same year.<ref name="emmys1">{{WebCite|url=http://www.emmys.com/shows/star-wars-clone-wars|text=Star Wars: Clone Wars|work=[http://www.emmys.com Emmys.com]|archivedate=20181106235733}}</ref>

==Legacy==
As the pilot episode for ''Clone Wars'', "Chapter 1" started a massive [[Clone Wars multimedia project|multimedia project]] that produced not only the television series, but numerous comics and novels, a [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (video game)|video game]], action figures, and short stories. The show would later go on to inspire the [[2008]] computer-generated television show ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.
