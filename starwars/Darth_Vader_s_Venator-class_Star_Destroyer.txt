'''Darth Vader's ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer''' was a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] owned by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. He [[Death|murdered]] several of the [[Starship|ship's]] [[Imperial officer|crew]], including [[Admiral]] [[Mummert]], after being interrupted while meditating in his [[Darth Vader's meditation chamber|chamber]].<ref name="No Good Deed" /> By [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> it had been replaced as Vader's [[flagship]] by the ''[[Perilous]]''.<ref name="Lords of the Sith">''[[Lords of the Sith]]''</ref>
