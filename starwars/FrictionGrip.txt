'''FrictionGrip''' was a special coating manufactured and patented by [[Ayelixe/Krongbing Textiles]]. It was composed of thousands of micro­scopic filaments designed to grip surfaces in a manner similar to [[Spider|arachnids]]. The coating was used on Ayelixe/Krongbing's [[Climbsuit]], a special suit designed for climbing,<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> and on their [[mountaineer armor]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
FrictionGrip was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Cyphers and Masks]]'',<ref name="Cyphers and Masks" /> a [[2018]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Accept Your Mission">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/8/16/accept-your-mission|text=Accept Your Mission}}</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity where it was first mentioned in ''[[Rules of Engagement: The Rebel SpecForce Handbook]]'', a [[1997]] sourcebook for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', written by [[Timothy S. O'Brien]] and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="SpecForce">''[[Rules of Engagement: The Rebel SpecForce Handbook]]''</ref>
