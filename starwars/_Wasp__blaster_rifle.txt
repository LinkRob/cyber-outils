The '''"Wasp" blaster rifle''' was the name [[Razoo Qin-Fee]] gave to his customized [[blaster rifle]]. It was [[Color|black]] with an external accelerator barrel cage crudely painted in yellow and used a pump action [[gas]] compressor to reload.<ref name="TFAVD" />
