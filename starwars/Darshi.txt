The '''Darshi''', known as '''Thinfaces''' to residents of [[Batuu]], were a wealthy [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were employed by the warlike [[Grysk]] from the [[Unknown Regions]]. The Darshi themselves were believed by [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] to be a border society that traveled both [[Wild Space]] and the Unknown Regions. The culture of the Darshi were not deeply studied by the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], and stories did not portray them as violent. As such, according to the stories told, the Darshi were not generally believed to show violent tendencies to outsiders. However, their bodies were evolutionarily built in such a way that suggested they were a violent people: Darshi had long claws that would do well in combat, while their thin bodies and heads would keep them well-defended.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

Around 174 [[Standard day|days]] before a [[Mission to Batuu (Galactic Empire)|skirmish on Batuu]] where a group of Darshi attacked Thrawn and [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], a group of Darshi arrived on the [[planet]] Batuu and claimed they were on a [[pilgrim]]age. The group of Darshi built houses for themselves to the east of [[Black Spire Outpost]] due to claims that their journey required them to remain on Batuu for an extended period of [[time]]. After the construction of the houses, they spent three days [[Meditation|meditating]] in the woods to the north, and filled Black Spire Outpost's with their ships, constantly arriving and leaving to distant locales on unknown errands.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

As a result, the Darshi drove away [[merchant]]s, ships were discouraged from landing, and others abandoned Batuu entirely. The Darshi had also paid handsomely for the best food and resources, although some Batuu residents were not content with this, in particular business owners. Eighteen days before the skirmish, a new group of Darshi arrived with ten "[[Hibernation chamber|coffins]]<nowiki/>".<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />
