'''Eurus''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[witch]] who served as [[ensign]] for the [[Brendok coven]] on the [[planet]] [[Brendok]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Destiny" /> Her duties included acting as an ornamentalist; creating fobs, jewelry and talismans for the coven in order to help their gestural channeling of the [[The Force|Thread]],<ref name="Visual guide" /> and acting as an [[Advisor|adviser]] to [[Aniseya]].<ref name="Destiny" /> Eurus was one of the witches who took control of [[Kelnacca]] and was accidentally killed by [[Indara]] upon freeing him from their control.<ref name="Choice">{{Acolyte|Choice}}</ref>

{{Char-stub}}

==Personality and traits==
Eurus was a human [[Gender|woman]] with [[Color|brown]] [[hair]] and light skin. Eurus respected Mother [[Aniseya]]'s wisdom<ref name="Visual guide" /> and told [[Koril]] to remain calm when expressing a desire to retaliate against the [[Jedi]] <ref name="Destiny" /> but ultimately disagreed with [[Aniseya]] on allowing the scrutiny of the Jedi<ref name="Visual guide" /> and urged her not to allow [[Verosha Aniseya|Osha]] to leave with [[Sol]] and his companions.<ref name="Choice" />

==Equipment==
Like the other adult members of the Coven, Eurus wore purple robes.<ref name="Destiny" /> Eurus' outfit included a segmented plastron necklace and a reinforcing welt cord.<ref name="Visual guide" />
