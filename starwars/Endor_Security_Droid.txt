The '''Endor Security Droid''' was a body configuration owned by the [[KX-series security droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[K-A0S]] that she could swap for her standard [[KX-Series Security Droid (K-A0S)|frame]] during the the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> This frame resembled the [[armor]] donned by [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[scout trooper]]s.<ref name="RotJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Endor Security Droid was a "Legendary" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter K-A0S, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 5.0.0 alongside Season 5 on [[January 27]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Endor Grafted Shields]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
