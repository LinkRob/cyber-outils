'''Anakin's flagship''' was a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Navy|naval forces']] [[Open Circle Fleet]]. It served as the [[flagship]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Deal No Deal EG" /> Sometime after the destruction of his original [[flagship]], the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Resolute]]'',<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref> Skywalker used the Venator as his second [[command ship]] for his [[Skywalker's fleet|task force]].<ref name="Deal No Deal EG" />

==Description==
As a [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]],<ref name="The Unknown" /> the [[flagship]] was equipped with a dorsal [[hangar]] bay, which held [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry]] gunships, as well as the personal [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptors]] of [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> It was also armed with eight [[DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret]]s and two medium dual [[turbolaser]] [[cannon]]s.<ref name="Equipment" />
