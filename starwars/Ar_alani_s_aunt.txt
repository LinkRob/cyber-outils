{{Quote|My aunt enjoyed occasional sewing projects when I was growing up, but nothing this elaborate.|translated=1|Commodore Ar'alani|<ref name="Chaos Rising" />}}
A [[Chiss]] [[Gender|female]] was a member of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Ruling Families|ruling]] [[Irizi family]] and the '''aunt''' of [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]] of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. They enjoyed sewing projects while Ar'alani&mdash;known as Irizi'ar'alani, [[core name]] "Ziara," in her [[Youngling|youth]]&mdash;was growing up.<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date note">The events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' take place in [[19 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As the flashbacks of the novel, which include the mention of [[Ar'alani]]'s aunt, take place prior to the novel's main events, they must take place by 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> then-[[Commodore]] Ar'alani and [[Senior Commander]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo]] "Thrawn" were invited to the [[Garwian Unity]] [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Solitair]]. After viewing the extensive craftsmanship of the [[Garwian]] [[Species|people]] there, Ar'alani mentioned that her aunt enjoyed sewing projects, though they were not as elaborate as the Garwian crafts.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ar'alani's aunt was mentioned in flashbacks of the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
