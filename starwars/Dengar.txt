'''Dengar''', also known as '''Dengar the Demolisher''', was a [[Corellia]]n [[human]] [[bounty hunter]] operating since the early stages of the [[Clone Wars]]. Dengar participated in an [[Safe cargo objective|operation]] on [[Quarzite]]&mdash;during which he served as a member of [[Krayt's Claw]], a syndicate of hunters including [[Boba Fett]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], and [[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;to secure property valuable to Lord [[Otua Blank]] of the planet Quarzite. Dengar also took jobs for clients such as the [[Grand Hutt Council]], fighting against [[Maul]] and his [[Shadow Collective]] on [[Nal Hutta]].

Dengar remained active through the [[Galactic Civil War]], during which he was one of several bounty hunters hired by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to capture [[Han Solo]], captain of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. Though Dengar ultimately lost Vader's bounty to his rival and partner Boba Fett, he was later present with Bossk and Fett in [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on Tatooine during Solo's rescue by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]].

Dengar's impressive longevity led him to continue his work as a bounty hunter even after the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] was defeated at the [[Battle of Endor]]. Around a year later, he teamed up with other tenured hunters such as [[Embo]] when he joined [[Jas Emari]] and her [[Jas Emari's crew|crew]] after the [[Battle of Jakku]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Dengar was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] from the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> However, little was known of his early life.<ref name="Dengar fact file">[[:File:StarWars2020-18-Handbook.png]]</ref> As a [[Youngling|child]], he raced [[swoop]] [[speeder bike]]s in Corellia's [[swoop racing]] scene. He had a friend named [[Hill Purpura]], with whom he competed in swoop racing. Both would later make differing claims as to which of them won more often.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 10">[[Bounty Hunters 10|''Bounty Hunters'' 10]]</ref> Dengar would later claim he had been a professional [[swoop]] bike [[racer]].<ref name="Dengar fact file" /> Before Dengar became a [[bounty hunter]], he served as a [[gladiator]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref> It was during the earliest [[Standard day|days]] of that war that he began [[Hunter|hunting]] [[Bounty|bounties]].<ref name="Dengar fact file" />

At some point Dengar took part in a [[Swoop racing|swoop race]] on Corellia, where a kid attempted to upstage him. As the kid speeded up, Dengar realized his swoop is slowing down. Pulling a knife, Dengar attempted to distract the kid to do whatever it takes to win, which the kid grabbed his knife arm and directed him to crash into a [[moisture vaporator]], where the kid, Han Solo, won the race to get the credits to buy a ship with his lover [[Qi'ra]].<ref name="Bounty Hunters 23" />

===The Clone Wars===
====Safe cargo objective====
{{Quote|So... what do you think is in that box?''"<br />"''Whatever it is, it better be worth all this effort.|Dengar and Asajj Ventress contemplate the contents of the box|<ref name="Bounty" />}}
As part of [[Krayt's Claw]], Dengar was part of an [[Safe cargo objective|operation]] alongside fellow hunters [[Boba Fett]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], [[Latts Razzi]], [[C-21 Highsinger]], and [[Oked]]. The hunters were hired to escort a [[subtram]] containing [[Pluma Sodi|cargo]] valuable to [[Otua Blank]], the leader of the [[Belugan]] people of [[Quarzite]]. While preparing for the mission in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]], Oked was [[Death|murdered]] by the wayward [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] user [[Asajj Ventress]], who promptly agreed to take the dead hunter's place on the mission roster.<ref name="Bounty" />

[[File:DengarKageFight-Bounty.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Dengar fights a Kage Warrior]]
Dengar and company set off in Bossk's [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Hound's Tooth]]'', to Quarzite, where the Belugan [[Major]] [[Rigosso]] transported the team to the train's subterranean loading dock via a space [[Turbolift|elevator]] from an orbital skyhook. Dengar, along with Ventress, were assigned to guard the rear of the train. After unsuccessfully flirting with Ventress, a team of skilled [[Kage Warriors]] boarded the train in an attempt to retrieve the cargo. Dengar managed to fight off many of the attackers due to his skilled [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]] techniques, and even managed to kill two using [[sticky explosive]]s. Dengar was eventually overwhelmed by the attackers and was thrown from the train, landing safely on the subterranean surface. Dengar and his allies, who were likewise thrown from the train, returned to the skyhook to await Ventress, who had betrayed the mission objective by swapping the cargo with Boba Fett, who was bound and gagged. Before departing, she hands over the rest of the team's share of unmarked [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Bounty" />

====Working for the Hutts====
{{Quote|Come on, let's get out of here.|Latts Razzi, to her team after their job for the Hutts went south|<ref name="Eminence" />}}
[[File:Crime_Lord_SWDConv.png|thumb|right|250px|Dengar was on the payroll of the Hutts]]
Dengar was later employed by the [[Hutt]]s alongside Latts, [[Embo]], and [[Sugi (bounty hunter)|Sugi]] as protection on [[Nal Hutta]]. They were present when the renegade [[Sith Lord]]s [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]] as well as the [[Death Watch]] leader [[Pre Vizsla]] [[Mission to Nal Hutta|came]] to ally with the Hutts. When the plan went sour, the four bounty hunters attacked, though they were pushed by the [[Mandalorian]]'s [[flamethrower]]. The trio soon received reinforcements and attacked. When the battle was clearly lost, Dengar and Latts saved the beaten Embo and Sugi by covering their escape with [[Smoke grenade|smoke bombs]]. The group of mercenaries fled the Death Watch and their [[Sith]] allies.<ref name="Eminence" />

====War's end====
Around the time of the end of the Clone Wars, Dengar disappeared from circulation for a brief period of time, and was believed to have been recovering from a near-fatal injury.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

===Galactic Civil War===
====The Hidden Hand====
{{Quote|The Hidden Hand.''"<br />"''Told you everything I know about those maniacs. And I ''guarantee'' what I said is more than you'll ''ever'' get out of ''him''. Valence won't talk. In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Target Vader Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Target Vader'' to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Dengar joined a [[Beilert Valance's crew|team]] of bounty hunters under [[Beilert Valance]] who were hired by the [[Hidden Hand]] to assassinate Darth Vader,<ref name="TV 1" /> as a secret informant for Vader himself.<ref name="TV 4" /> Dengar and the others went with Valance aboard the ''[[Broken Wing]]'' and got weapons from [[Fetya]] on [[Arvina]] However, Fetya double crossed them, but were saved when Vader's star destroyer opened fired on them. Dengar and the group survived and stole a [[K79-S80 Imperial Troop Transport|ITT]]. They used it to return to the ''Broken Wing''.<ref name="TV 2">[[Target Vader 2|''Target Vader'' 2]]</ref> Dengar and the others went to [[Heva]] and asked about the Hidden Hand. However, Vader and his destroyer arrived. Dengar went with [[Honnah]] to prepare a trap for Vader. He watched as the Broken Wing took on Vader's [[Darth Vader's TIE Advanced|TIE Advanced]] and several other TIEs. After Valance crashed his ship, Dengar and Honnah took aim at Vader<ref name="TV 3">[[Target Vader 3|''Target Vader'' 3]]</ref> and waited for Valance to give the signal to eliminate him. Dengar opened fire on Vader while Honnah took Vader head on with her battle-ax. After Honnah and the others were killed by Vader, Dengar revealed he was working for Vader by stunning Valance as stormtroopers arrived.<ref name="TV 4">[[Target Vader 4|''Target Vader'' 4]]</ref>

Aboard Vader's destroyer, Dengar told Vader everything he knew and learned about the Hidden Hand before he left<ref name="TV 5" /> for Coruscant. He was then confronted by Valance. Dengar was punched hard by Valance, but was allowed to live despite his betrayal of Valance and his crew.<ref name="TV 6">[[Target Vader 6|''Target Vader'' 6]]</ref>

====The Smuggler's Moon====
{{Quote|You know what? Maybe I ''don't'' want you so alive after all. Just tell me one thing, and I'll put you out of your filthy, stinking misery. ''Where is Han Solo?!"<br />"''Right ''here'', ugly.|Dengar, interrogating a fallen Chewbacca, is ambushed by Han Solo|<ref name="SW 11">[[Star Wars (2015) 11|''Star Wars'' (2015) 11]]</ref>}}
Though weathered and grizzled by [[Standard year|years]] of bounty hunting, Dengar was still active around the time of the [[Assault on Cymoon|assault]] on [[Cymoon 1]]. He was present in [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba's]] court when [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], on a mission to negotiate with the Hutt, arrived at the [[crime lord]]'s [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]]. Jabba was briefly [[Force choke]]d by the [[Sith Lord]] and after this Jabba ordered Dengar out the of room so that "negotiations" could commence.<ref name="Darth Vader 1">[[Darth Vader (2015) 1|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 1]]</ref>

Following this, Dengar visited a cantina on [[Nar Shaddaa]], the notorious [[moon]] of the planet [[Nal Hutta]], where he was informed by a [[Gungan]] that [[Chewbacca]] was present.<ref name="Star Wars 10" /> Dengar soon located the Wookiee, before shocking him as he and [[C-3PO]] were about to storm [[Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi|Grakkus]] the Hutt's [[Palace of Grakkus the Hutt|palace]]. He greeted Chewbacca, claiming that Jabba sent his regards, before turning to 3PO and demanding to know where Han Solo was. 3PO told the bounty hunter that Solo was not with them, prompting Dengar to order him to contact the smuggler. 3PO agreed to comply, but first attempted to save Chewbacca. Before Dengar could intervene, the protocol droid had shorted out the shock bolt, and Chewbacca rose up to his feet. The two then began to fight on the rooftops. Dengar told Chewbacca that he could throw a punch just as well as the Wookiee, before being knocked backwards by a successful hit. Dengar then grabbed a blaster, before Chewbacca crushed it, and grabbed the corellian by the neck, raising him off the ground.<ref name="SW 11" />

[[File:SoloShotsDengar-2015StarWars11.jpg|thumb|left|250x250px|Dengar is shot in the back by Han Solo]]
Dengar ignited an explosive, knocking Chewbacca to the floor, before threatening to use his vibroblade against him. Dengar again knocked Chewbacca back, taunting him claiming he defied the reputation Wookiees had for being mighty warriors, before being punched up the chin. Fed up, Dengar threatened to kill the Wookiee, but first demanded the location of Solo. In that moment, Solo shot Dengar in the back revealing himself to be behind the bounty hunter, before ordering that he take his hands off of Chewbacca.<ref name="SW 11" /> Solo stated that Dengar was outgunned, and once again ordered him to step away from the Wookiee. Solo friend Princess Leia Organa of [[Alderaan]] advised Dengar to listen to Solo, stating that his wife would be eager to shoot him. Though Dengar was confused, his rage took priority, and he held up a thermal detonator, stating that they would all die if he were to let go, and stated that he and Solo step aside to settle it like men. Before he could finish his sentence however, Chewbacca raised the bounty hunter off of the ground and launched him off the building with his thermal detonator, which proceeded to explode.<ref name="SW 12">[[Star Wars (2015) 12|''Star Wars'' (2015) 12]]</ref> Despite this however, Dengar managed to survive the encounter.<ref name="ESB" />

====In Vader's employ====
{{Quote|There will be a substantial reward for the one who finds the ''Millennium Falcon''. You are free to use any methods necessary, but I want them alive.|Darth Vader to Dengar and five other bounty hunters|<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>}}
Having survived his encounter with Chewbacca, Dengar was later secretly hired by [[Darth]] Vader along with [[Krrsantan]], [[Zuckuss]], [[Beebox]], [[IG-90]], and C-21 Highsinger to find and return [[Doctor|Dr.]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]].<ref name="Darth Vader 16">[[Darth Vader (2015) 16|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 16]]</ref>

Dengar attempted to collect a bounty on [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] on [[Rekelos]] by using a [[Concussion grenade]]. He later caught up with Jaxxon and his ex-girlfriend [[Amaiza Foxtrain]] on [[Valtos]]. Dengar was knocked out by Amaiza. Despite that, he was able to pick himself and decided to collect on Foxtrain's [[Holopuck|bounty puck]].<ref name="Rabbit Hunt">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|annual=2020|story=Rabbit Hunt}}</ref> At some point, Dengar began dating an [[Aruzan]] woman named [[Manaroo]],<ref name="S&S">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> who became indebted to Jabba.<ref name="BH 16">[[Bounty Hunters 16|''Bounty Hunters'' 16]]</ref> As such, she was imprisoned in Jabba's palace, leaving her to be used as leverage against Dengar.<ref name="BH 14">[[Bounty Hunters 14|''Bounty Hunters'' 14]]</ref>

[[File:BountyHunters.jpg|thumb|right|220px|Dengar on the bridge of the ''Executor'' in 3 ABY]]
In [[3 ABY]], Dengar was one of the bounty hunters, alongside Boba Fett, Bossk, [[IG-88B|IG-88]], [[4-LOM]], and Zuckuss, summoned to the ''[[Executor]]'', the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[flagship]] of Darth Vader, who resorted to using hunters to track down the ''Millennium Falcon'', the ship of [[Captain]] Han Solo, who slipped away from Vader during the evacuation of [[Hoth]]. Dengar lost out on the Solo bounty to Fett, who delivered the now [[carbonite]]-encased Solo to the Hutt crime lord Jabba on [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ESB" />

====Reunion with Valance and the quest for Solo====
Dengar was later captured by the reformed [[Ohnaka Gang]], to whom he owed a debt.<ref name="BH9">[[Bounty Hunters 9|''Bounty Hunters'' 9]]</ref> Soon reunited with and put at the mercy of Beilert Valance during an operation involving the rebel transport ''[[Spirit of Jedha]]'' under his old friend Hill Purpura,<ref name="Bounty Hunters 10" /> Dengar agreed to help Valance track down Han Solo, repayment for selling out Valance to Darth Vader the last time they met.<ref name="BH11">[[Bounty Hunters 11|''Bounty Hunters'' 11]]</ref> As they approached Nar Shaddaa, Dengar and Valance came under attack by [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]]'s ship. Dengar then held Valance at gunpoint when he refused to reveal the location of [[Cadeliah]]. He then knocked Valance out when he ran into several [[Exogorth]]s. Dengar told them that the girl was not aboard the ship, but he would them information on Fett's current whereabouts.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 12">[[Bounty Hunters 12|''Bounty Hunters'' 12]]</ref>

The two bounty hunters then went to the arms market on Nar Shadaa to find Fett. However, Fett was gone by the time Dengar and Valance arrived. Dengar then spoke with [[Devono Vix]]. Valance punched Vix and Dengar tried to convince him to reveal Fett's whereabouts. Vix refused and ordered both him and Valance to be killed. The two of them fought Vix's thugs and then pursued him. When Valance ran into Chewbacca, Dengar went into a tavern for a drink. He was then strangled by Chewbacca, due to their previous encounter on Nar Shadaa. He tried to shoot the Wookiee, but was tossed at C-3PO. He woke up when Chewbacca left Valance to continue his search for Solo. Due to Valance's interrogation of Vix, Dengar and Valance were able to learn that Crimson Dawn had Solo and Fett was going after them. Dengar refused to believe him about Crimson Dawn's return until Vix was killed. Dengar saved Valance from an assassin.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 13">[[Bounty Hunters 13|''Bounty Hunters'' 13]]</ref>

[[File:BountyHunters15-textless.png|thumb|left|250px|Dengar and Valance in the crosshairs of Deathstick]]
Dengar and Valance were able to lose the assassin. Dengar took Valance to mechanic [[Mama Stammoch]] and her crew. As Valance was being fixed up, Dengar contacted [[Bib Fortuna]] about the status of [[Manaroo]], his girlfriend. Fortuna threatened to have her fed to the Rancor if he did not collect the bounty on Fett. Dengar learned about Crimson Dawn's origins from Stammoch. After Valance recovered, Dengar showed Stammoch a hologram of the assassin, who she recognized as [[Deathstick]]. Dengar then came under attack by Deathstick who killed Stammoch and her crew. The two bounty hunters were able to make their escape in a speeder by throwing a thermal detonator at Deathstick. Unfortunately, the speeder was not fixed and Dengar was saved from the crash when Valance grabbed him and jumped to safety. However, Deathstick followed them.<ref name="BH 14" />

Dengar and Valance took a [[Star Commuter 2000]] to [[Canto Bight]] despite it being a two day journey to get the ''[[Punishing One]]'' back. On Canto Bright, Dengar was able to get his back from [[Just Lucky]] and his boss the [[Sixth Kin]]'s leader [[Wen Delphis]]. As Dengar and Valance returned to the ''Punishing One'', they came under attack once again by Deathstick. The two fought Deathstick pushed Valance into a casino. When Valance returned, Dengar revealed that he stole Deathstick's Crimson Dawn invitation during the fight.<ref name="BH 15">[[Bounty Hunters 15|''Bounty Hunters'' 15]]</ref> Dengar and Valance was able to catch up with Fett on [[Jekara]]. Unfortunately, Fett was able to escape them and Dengar was trapped on a iceberg drifting away. Valance chose to leave Dengar drifting away and joined Fett in claiming Solo.<ref name="WotBH4">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 4|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 4]]</ref>

He watched as Valance left with Fett aboard ''Slave I''. He then came under attack by a [[Zugorak]]. He only had a knife to defend himself with. However, he was saved from the creature by Deathstick, who offered to pay off Manaroo's debt to Jabba. Dengar also provided her with Valance's transmission to [[Yuralla Vega]] so she could find [[Cadeliah]] at her rebel cell base.<ref name="BH 16" />

====Revnog rampage====
{{Quote|You are suggesting that your client, who according to my notes is often known by the sobriquet ''Dengar the Demolisher''&hellip;did ''not'' simply become deeply intoxicated, steal a ''speeder'' and go for a joyride that caused immense ''vehicular mayhem''&hellip;but was instead ''hypnotized'' by a ''Jedi Knight'' who ''forced'' him to do these things?''"<br />"''Well, your honor, the galaxy's a big place. My understanding is they have a near-infinite supply of large, heavy boulders that need '' demolishing'' in those mines. I'm sure he'll fit in well.''"<br />"''Oh, come ''on''.|A judge and Advocate Salli Georgio, and Dengar's reaction|<ref name="A Trick of the Mind">{{StoryCite|story=A Trick of the Mind|book=Revelations (2023) 1}}</ref>}}
On [[D'Assem]], Dengar visited the<ref name="A Trick of the Mind" /> planet's<ref name="Bounty Hunters 35">[[Bounty Hunters 35|''Bounty Hunters'' 35]]</ref> [[Black-Market District]]. Ingesting two bottles of [[revnog]], the bounty hunter stole a speeder and destroyed half of the port district before he was stopped by the authorities. Brought before [[Unidentified judge (Dengar)|a judge]], Dengar was represented by Advocate [[Salli Georgio]], who considered him lucky that she had agreed to work on his behalf. The trial commenced at the [[Imperial Court of Criminal Justice]] on [[Samaria]].<ref name="A Trick of the Mind" />

Georgio argued to the judge that Dengar was approached by a [[Jedi]] and put under the influence of a [[mind trick]], inciting Dengar's destructive rampage as a distraction from the Jedi's own activities. The judge, noting Dengar's epithet of "the Demolisher," found it unlikely that the bounty hunter had not simply become intoxicated and acted of his own accord. When Georgio lobbied that the scenario was not impossible and ultimately could not be disproven, the judge presented the Empire's legal position that both the Jedi and the [[Jedi Order]] no longer existed and sentenced Dengar to a 50,000 credit fine and 60 days of hard labor in the [[orgonium mines]], the frustrated defendant placing his head in his hands.<ref name="A Trick of the Mind" />

Imprisoned, Dengar later berated Georgio's choice of arguing with the Jedi mind trick. Georgio reminded Dengar of the severity of what had transpired, remarking that his case had required out-of-the-box thinking. Calling Georgio the worst lawyer in the galaxy and warning her to watch herself upon his release, Dengar was dragged away by the bailiff. Georgio considered sending Dengar's bill to the mine and, contacted by a [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|prospective client]], said that she was now free.<ref name="A Trick of the Mind" />

Dengar was present at Jabba's palace when a trader from [[Mos Elrey]] under the Hutt's protection approached with a dispute, claiming that a Trandoshan clan stole from the [[Snivvian]]'s stores. Jabba sentenced the Trandoshan to his torture chambers, though prepared to have the Snivvian killed to secure an offered ten-percent bonus from the clan.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" />

Interrupted by the arrival of Beilert Valance, Jabba offered his good graces and 50,000 credits to whomever captured Valance for further torture. Ignoring Manaroo's warning, an eager Dengar confronted Valance, intending to brutalize the cyborg for abandoning him on Jekara. Valance apologized to Dengar who, claiming to have killed an apexathor and been trained in twelve forms of bladed combat, was quickly knocked unconscious in one punch, exasperating Manaroo.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" />

Dengar later stole a shipment of spice wine from an Imperial officer, attracting the Empire's attention.<ref name="Bounty Hunters 42" />

Dengar, Fett, and Bossk were in the palace when chaos broke loose after the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] arrived to [[Rescue of Han Solo|free]] Solo.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> When Jabba's court left to the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]], Dengar decided not to go, a decision that saved his life because<ref name="Helmet Collection 14">{{HelmetCollectionCite|14|Databank A-Z}}</ref> the crime lord's [[sail barge]], the ''[[Khetanna]]'', was destroyed by Skywalker and his allies.<ref name="ROTJ" />

===New Republic===
====Skirmish on Corellia====
{{Quote|Things are changing now. Our profession is about to get marginalized, too. Those rebels won't put up with our special brand of sauce for too long, will they? It's why we gotta band together. Form a proper union. We'll be a force to reckon with.|Dengar, to [[Mercurial Swift]]|<ref name="Aftermath">[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']]</ref>}}
[[File:MercurialVsDengar-GalacticAtlasUpdated.png|thumb|right|300px|Dengar comes to blows with the younger bounty hunter Mercurial Swift.]]
Some time after the [[Battle of Endor]], which saw the death of Emperor Palpatine and allowed the suddenly winning Rebel Alliance a chance to become the [[New Republic]], Dengar got into a fight with the young bounty hunter [[Mercurial Swift]], in a holoplex rooftop on [[Coronet City]], Corellia. Dengar offered guidance, trying to convince him to join a bounty hunter alliance, but Mercurial refused, claiming he was old and ineffective. Dengar managed to have the advantage, aiming his [[particle array gun]] at his opponent, but was easily distracted and taken down when the hunter pretended to see Boba Fett standing behind him. Mercurial let him live, claiming to have no reason to kill him without a [[bounty]] to his head.<ref name="Aftermath" />

The two hunters went on their separate paths, but not before Dengar gave him one last warning, saying times would be different for their kind with the New Republic in power, with the new government potentially forcing bounties on other bounty hunters.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Dengar's prediction began to come true when the New Republic began denying services to bounty hunters due to the paranoia following the [[terrorist]] [[Attack on Chandrila|attacks]] that took place on [[Liberation Day (New Republic)|Liberation Day]].<ref name="Life Debt">''[[Aftermath: Life Debt]]''</ref>

====Working for Mercurial Swift====
{{Quote|You forgot one part.''"<br />"''What?''"<br />"''We don't really like you.|Dengar turning against Mercurial Swift|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
[[File:Dengar_SaV.png|thumb|left|200px|Dengar stands over two victims]]
In the days leading up to the [[Battle of Jakku]], Dengar along with his fellow bounty hunters Embo and the [[Rodian]] [[Jeeta]] accepted work as Mercurial Swift's crew. Swift wanted to collect the [[Black Sun]] crime boss [[Gyuti]]'s bounty on the [[Zabrak]] [[Jas Emari]], the niece of the hunter Sugi. In the past, Dengar had once worked with Sugi, who had always hated Dengar. Dengar in turn was unpopular among many of his fellow bounty hunters including Swift. After learning that the Hutt crime lady [[Niima]] had taken Jas captive, Dengar and his fellow bounty hunters traveled to Niima's temple of the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] world of [[Jakku]] to take Jas into custody.<ref name="Empire's End" />

However, Jas escaped from her captors and wounded Swift. She then managed to convince Embo, who was a friend of her aunt Sugi, to allow her to escape in Swift's [[Corellian shuttle]]. Dengar rushed back to the ship to apprehend her but was unable to stop Jas from escaping. After Jas returned with her friend [[Norra Wexley]] and the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[Mister Bones]] to Niima's temple, Dengar and his fellow hunters cornered Niima and Jas. Niima had earlier agreed to provide Jas and Norra with Imperial uniforms, an Imperial shuttle, and clearance codes to enter the [[Main Imperial base]] on Jakku out of gratitude for ferrying her back to her temple.<ref name="Empire's End" />

Niima initially refused to hand over Jas and grabbed Swift by the neck. However, Dengar pointed his blaster at Niima's throat and forced the Hutt to give up Jas. While the hunters were preoccupied, Norra and Bones escaped on the Imperial shuttle.<ref name="Empire's End" />

====Teaming up with Jas Emari====
{{Quote|Times, they are a-changing, my little gompers. We're gonna need to watch each others' backs, eh?|Dengar on striking a deal with Jas Emari|<ref name="Empire's End" />}}
[[File:Dengar-Conv.png|thumb|right|180px|Dengar worked with Jas Emari for the New Republic]]
After taking Jas captive, Dengar and his fellow hunters returned to Swift's shuttle. Swift wanted to depart for Nar Shaddaa to hand Jas to Gyuti but was forced to delay his departure due to the Battle of Jakku. While Jeeta landed the ship in a canyon, Jas tormented Swift. Dengar then demanded that Swift tell them his plan.<ref name="Empire's End" />

When an enraged Swift struck Jas, Dengar mocked Swift, who had sustained a facial scar from Jas' horns. Despite Swift's objections, Dengar indicated that he was willing to listen to Jas' counter-offer. Dengar and Embo reacted positively to Jas' offer that the New Republic would reward them for helping to capture the Imperial [[Grand Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]], the alleged mastermind behind the Liberation Day attacks. When Dengar opined that working for the New Republic was not exactly "cozy-making," Jas promised to secure them full pardons if they worked with her. Jas also promised to share the money with Dengar and Embo.<ref name="Empire's End" />

Swift tried to reassert his authority but Dengar countered that he and Embo did not like him. Dengar clubbed Swift in the head with his blaster. Swift managed to grab Jas by the neck and take her hostage. However, the Zabrak headbutted Swift and then punched him in the middle before throwing him out of the shuttle's door. Disliking Swift and accepting Jas' offer, Dengar complimented her. Embo also expressed agreement. Jeeta, who was piloting the ship, also agreed to to work for Jas. Jas and [[Jas Emari's crew|her new crew]] then flew to the [[Jakku Observatory]] where they evacuated Norra.<ref name="Empire's End" />

The Battle of Jakku ultimately ended the [[Galactic Civil War]] as a New Republic victory. Following the signing of the [[Galactic Concordance]], Jas managed to secure Dengar, Embo, and Jeeta full pardons and some money from the New Republic. While it was not a huge sum, the money was enough to stop them from killing her and to convince Dengar and his fellow hunters to continue working as Jas' crew. Dengar was particularly pleased by this turn of events and remarked that they needed to watch each others' backs because times were changing.<ref name="Empire's End" />

===Cybernetic surgery===
At some point after the Galactic Civil War, Dengar underwent [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] surgery in an attempt to remain competitive. The surgery left him barely recognizable.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> Some rumors claimed Dengar in fact became the hunter [[Rothgar Deng]].<ref name="DDaOBHaC">{{EncyclopediaCite|Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld}}</ref>

During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] listed Deng along with other bounty hunters who had attempted to capture [[Moebin Faltus]]. But when repeating the names, the bounty hunter [[Kief Varris]] called Deng, "Dengar."<ref name="Repair">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=14|story=Repair Stop, Part II}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|You made the ''wrong'' call, Valance! I can't believe you betrayed me, you veermok spore! Not that I wasn't planning to betray ''you''. But ''that's not the point''.|Dengar|<ref name="BH 16" />}}
[[File:Dengar-TCW.png|thumb|left|180px|Dengar wore plated armor and carried a heavy blaster rifle.]]
Though considered by others to be crude and slovenly, Dengar thought highly of himself, often flirting with females, such as Asajj Ventress. His confidence translated into his battle tactics, often taking the fight to the enemy head on with sheer firepower and destruction, which contrasted with the more cunning tactics of hunters like [[Cad Bane]] and Boba Fett. Dengar was known to be quite brutal in combat, going so far as to rush his enemies and beat them to death with his [[heavy blaster rifle]].<ref name="SWE" /> He was known to rush into battle with a joy that made veteran mercenaries wonder about his life expectancy.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

Thanks to his brutality and cunning, Dengar was able to regularly work with Hutt contracts, even often working alone on these missions.<ref name="Helmet Collection 14" /> In addition, he was held in high regard by Fett for his excellent track record of hunting down his marks.<ref name="SWE" /> Despite working for crime lords like Jabba, Dengar did not trust them, knowing one failure could lead to his end.<ref name="Reputation" />

While Dengar worked with several bounty hunters, including Sugi and Mercurial Swift, he was widely disliked by them. Dengar in turn disliked Swift, regarding the younger bounty hunter as arrogant and opportunistic. Jas Emari managed to convince Dengar and his companions Embo and Jeeta to turn against Swift with the promise of financial rewards from the New Republic for hunting down Grand Admiral Sloane and securing full pardons. Dengar knew how to fire a blaster and was also familiar with melee combat.<ref name="Empire's End" />

Dengar was a human male who was 1.87 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]], 2 inches) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> He had [[Color|brown]] [[hair]],<ref name="Bounty" /> blue [[eye]]s and light skin.<ref name="SWE" />
Much of Dengar's body was covered in cybernetics.<ref name="The Star Wars Book" />

==Skills and abilities==
{{Quote|I'm not just a ''pretty face,'' you know. I can throw a ''punch'' as well as you ca--|Dengar, to Chewbacca|<ref name="SW 11" />}}
Dengar was capable in melee combat,<ref name="Bounty" /> as well as armed combat and infiltration.<ref name="Join the Battle" />  He had an impressive track record of hunting down his bounties.<ref name="SWE" />

===Development===
{{Quote|Who doesn't love a good punch to Dengar's face?|Ethan Sacks|<ref name="Sacks Punch">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1748127792166780978|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=Who doesn't love a good punch to Dengar's face?|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/LNZU2}}</ref>}}
Dengar was played by British actor [[Morris Bush]] in [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="ESB" /> In two episodes of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' TV series, Dengar was voiced by British actor [[Simon Pegg]], who went on to reprise the role in the video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''.<ref name="Bounty" /><ref name="Eminence" /><ref name="Battlefront">''[[Star Wars Battlefront]]''</ref>

In the fourth draft of ''The Empire Strikes Back'' screenplay, Dengar was described, along with Zuckuss, as "battle-scarred, mangy human types."<ref name="SW:TAS">''[[Star Wars: The Annotated Screenplays]]''</ref> Author [[Ethan Sacks]] of ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]'' confessed that, if Dengar was not confirmed to survive after ''Return of the Jedi'' by ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy|The Aftermath Trilogy]]'' of novels, he would have killed the character.<ref name="Sacks Dengar">{{Twitter|ethanjsacks|status/1685058324776062976|[[Ethan Sacks]]|quote=If I could have done that, I would have. But we know he’s alive in Aftermath era. {{C|In response to: "dengar death !"}}|archivedate=20230731162152}}</ref>

While no explicit connection was made, Rothgar Deng's name, background, and [[armor]] resemble that of Dengar.<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> The [[2020]] reference text ''[[The Star Wars Book]]'' provided further similarities, stating that Dengar became almost unrecognizable after undergoing extreme surgeries following the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="The Star Wars Book" /> The [[2021]] reference book "[[Din Djarin and Other Characters of the Underworld]]" went even further, adding that an [[in-universe]] rumor stated that Deng was in fact Dengar.<ref name="DDaOBHaC" />

===The Freemaker Adventures===
[[File:Dengar_and_company_Showdown.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Dengar, flanked by Baash and Raam, in the ''Freemaker Adventures'']]
Dengar is a secondary antagonist in the non-[[canon]] [[Disney XD]] animated television series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'', which ran between [[June 20]] and [[August 29]], [[2016]] portrayed by [[James Patrick Stuart]]. In the series, he works for the Hutt crime lord [[Graballa/LEGO|Graballa]], who is obsessed with finding the protagonist [[Rowan Freemaker/LEGO|Rowan Freemaker]]. The [[Force-sensitive]] Rowan is on a quest to find the scattered [[Kyber crystal]] that make up the [[Kyber Saber]], a powerful ancient [[Jedi]] weapon.<ref name="Lost Treasure">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Lost Treasure of Cloud City}}</ref> Graballa wants to obtain these kyber crystals in order to finance a beach resort and café. In the series, Dengar is aided by the [[Iktotchi]] bounty hunters [[Baash]] and [[Raam]].<ref name="Race on Tatooine">{{FreemakerAdventures|Race on Tatooine}}</ref> Graballa later teams up with the Sith acolyte [[Naare]] in exchange for sharing half of the Kyber Saber crystals.<ref name="The Test">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Test}}</ref> However, Naare double crosses them and drives them away.<ref name="Duel of Destiny">{{FreemakerAdventures|Duel of Destiny}}</ref> Graballa and his henchmen then exact their revenge by freezing Naare in carbonite.<ref name="ROTKS">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return of the Kyber Saber}}</ref> Dengar also appeared as a downloadable character within the video game ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''.

Dengar resurfaces as a secondary antagonist in the [[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures Season Two|second season]] of the ''Freemaker Adventures'', which aired between [[June 17]] and [[August 16]], [[2017]]. In the series, Dengar is still serving Graballa and becomes one of the Freemakers' obstacles during their [[Mission to obtain Embersteel wings]]. Graballa is depicted as having a tense relationship with Dengar, regarding him as a "freeloader."<ref name="The Embersteel Blade">{{FreemakerAdventures|The Embersteel Blade}}</ref> Under Graballa's orders, Dengar later leads a team of bounty hunters consisting of IG-88, Bossk, 4-LOM, and Zuckuss to apprehend the Freemakers at the Wheel. However, the Freemakers managed to elude the bounty hunters and also [[Mission to obtain an energy matrix activator|obtain]] the energy matrix activator needed to build the [[Arrowhead (Freemakers)|''Arrowhead'']].<ref name="Return to the Wheel">{{FreemakerAdventures|Return to the Wheel}}</ref>
