During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Separatist Droid Army|droid army]] maintained a '''unit''' of [[battle droid]]s on the [[planet]] [[Lola Sayu]] within the [[Citadel]] [[prison]]. The unit was commanded by [[Commander]] [[Osi Sobeck]] and consisted of several different models of [[battle droid]]s, including [[B1-series battle droid]]s, [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, [[droideka]]s, [[BX-series droid commando]]s, [[OOM-series security droid]]s,<ref name="The Citadel" /> [[LM-432 crab droid]]s,<ref name="Citadel Rescue" /> [[Ringneck recon droid]]s,<ref name="Counterattack" /> and several packs of [[anooba]]s.<ref name="Citadel Rescue" /> It also used [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]]s and had at least ten [[squad]]s and several [[droid]] [[battalion]]s within it.<ref name="Counterattack" />

In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of ''[[Ghosts of Mortis]]'' and "[[Water War]]" to [[20 BBY]]. As the episode "[[The Citadel (episode)|The Citadel]]" takes place between those two episodes according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, the events of the episode must also take place in 20 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Republic]] dispatched a strike team to Lola Sayu to [[Battle of Lola Sayu|break]] into the Citadel, where they were to retrieve [[Jedi General]] [[Even Piell]] and his Republic forces from the prison, who had been brought there by Separatist forces intent on gaining the coordinates for the strategic [[Nexus Route]]. However, while inside the Citadel, the Republic strike team had several run-ins with the unit as they attempted to keep them from fleeing the planet with the desired information and as a result, the Citadel forces, under orders from [[Citadel Command]], activated all security protocols within the prison in order to prevent their escape.<ref name="The Citadel" />
