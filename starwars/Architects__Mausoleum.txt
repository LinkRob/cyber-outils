The '''Architects' Mausoleum''' was a crypt located beneath the [[city]] of [[Vaale]] on the [[planet]] [[Dianth]]. It was the final resting place of the [[Ring of Fortune]], one of the [[Rings of Vaale]], artifacts created by the [[Architects of Vaale]]. While searching for the Rings as part of the crew of the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]], [[Eustacia Okka]] discovered the Architects' Mausoleum, taking the Ring of Fortune.<ref name="Aphra 3" />
