The '''ARC Troopers Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ133''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the earlier [[ARC Troopers Unit Expansion]], it will be released on [[January 16]], [[2026]] as part of Atomic Mass Games' overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''Advanced Recon Commandos, or ARC troopers, are clone troopers that have courage, strategy, and skill that exceeds that of other troopers and are identifiable by their unique armor. With exceptional skills, ARC troopers are deployed on critical missions that demand a specialized force. This expansion contains 2 units: 1 Arc Trooper Unit and 1 Arc Trooper Strike Team.''

==Contents==
*7 Miniatures
*2 Unit Cards
*1 Upgrade Card
