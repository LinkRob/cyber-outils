'''Beekmonkeys''' were a type of [[monkey]]. While trapped aboard the [[Galactic Republic]] [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]]<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> in [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the [[pilot]] [[Leox Gyasi]] mentally compared fellow pilot [[Koley Linn]]&mdash;who he had a history of unpleasant encounters with&mdash;to a beekmonkey.<ref name="The Fallen Star" />

==Behind the scenes==
Beekmonkeys were mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2022]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]'', written by [[Claudia Gray]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> [[Beek-monkey]]s originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they were first mentioned in the [[Star Wars (1977) 10|tenth issue]] of the [[1977]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Star Wars 10">[[Star Wars (1977) 10|''Star Wars'' (1977) 10]]</ref> on [[January 10]], [[1978]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/53800/star_wars_1977_10|text=Star Wars (1977) #10}}</ref>
