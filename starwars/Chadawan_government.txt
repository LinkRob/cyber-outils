The '''Chadawan government''' was a rogue faction of the [[Galactic Empire]] formed following the [[Battle of Endor]] in the [[Galactic Civil War]], and was located in the [[Chadawa system]] and led by [[Governor]] [[Bordanivaux]]. [[Colonel]] [[Madrighast]] and his [[Madrighast's unit|unit]] aboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Unyielding]]'' defended the [[Imperial Remnants|remnant]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
