A '''cortosis shield''' was a model of shield that was made of alloys or synthetic compounds and laced with a [[cortosis]] weave, which allowed it to block [[lightsaber]]s and [[blaster]] bolts. With a cost of 900 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], the shields were strong enough that they could be used as melee weapons, although it was not their primary function.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cortosis shields were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
