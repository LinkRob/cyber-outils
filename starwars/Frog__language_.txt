"'''Frog'''" was a [[language]] spoken by the two [[Querm Rybet]] "[[Frog Lady]]" and her [[Frog Man|husband]].<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> It consisted of various clicks and groans. In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mandalorian Date">{{MandoDate}}</ref> when the [[human]] [[Technician|mechanic]] [[Peli Motto]] hired the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] to transport "Frog Lady" to the [[water]] [[moon]] [[Trask]], Motto spoke Frog to "Frog Lady" and acted as a translator to Djarin. During their [[Mission to Trask|journey]], Djarin expressed that he couldn't speak Frog.<ref name="ThePassenger" />

{{Language-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Frog first appeared in the [[Chapter 10: The Passenger]], the second episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season Two|''The Mandalorian'' Season Two]]. Though in the episode itself it is unclear whether "Frog" is the official name of the language or simply what [[Din Djarin]] nicknamed it, the subtitles used it as the name, confirming "Frog" is the proper term.<ref name="ThePassenger" />
