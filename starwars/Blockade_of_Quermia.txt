During the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the [[planet]] [[Quermia]] was extensively '''blockaded''' by the Separatists. The world was loyal to the Republic but surrounded by the Confederacy due to its location in Separatist space. The [[blockade]] was lifted with the fall of the Confederacy at the [[Mission to Mustafar|end]] of the Clone Wars, after which Quermia fell under the rule of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Savage Spirits" />
