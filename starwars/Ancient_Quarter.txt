The '''Ancient Quarter''' was a historical preservation district in the [[Gungan]] [[capital]] [[city]] [[Otoh Gunga]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. It encompassed the original foundations laid by [[Boss]] [[Gallo]] over the foundations of [[Spearhead]], an ancient [[fortress]] city. Spearhead had been the home of the [[warlord]] Boss [[Rogoe]], whom Gallo had [[Destruction of Spearhead|defeated]] at the end of a [[Gungan tribal war|conflict]] between the warring Gungan tribes [[Millennium|millennia]] before the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Nexus" />

The Ancient quarter contained ancient buildings and machinery, which were maintained by a small but devoted group of volunteer preservationists. Each structure, street, platform, and support membrane contained historical significance for the Gungans, and as such it was a popular tourist destination for Gungans from all over Naboo. The residents of the Ancient Quarter were among the most prestigious and expensive in Otoh Gunga, and many families lived in homes built by their ancestors in previous [[Standard century|centuries]]. The existing residents would clash with newer residents as well as visiting Gungans from distant cities.<ref name="Nexus" />
