The '''Bilzen system''' was a [[star system]] located in [[Wild Space]] that contained the [[planet]] [[Bilzen]]. In [[3 BBY]], the [[Spectres]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel cell]] traveled to Bilzen for the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] to instruct his [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]] in [[Jedi training]]. They were followed and [[Duel on Bilzen|confronted]] by the [[Inquisitor]]s [[Fifth Brother]] and [[Seventh Sister]], members of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Jedi hunter|Jedi-hunting]] [[Inquisitorius]].

==Description==
The Bilzen system was located in [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[star system]] was situated in the grid square U-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]] and contained<ref name="Appendix" /> [[Bilzen]], a [[planet]] with a [[Temperature|frozen]] environment on its surface.<ref name="Breaking" />

==History==
In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{Twitter|username=tronprogram2|url=status/712746787216424960|name=[[Martin Fisher]]|archiveurl=https://ghostarchive.org/archive/nC3Uy|archivefile=File:MartinFisher-IceBreaking-Twitter.png}} places "[[Ice Breaking]]" between "[[The Future of the Force]]" and "[[Legacy (episode)|Legacy]]," both of which are placed in [[3 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, the events of "Ice Breaking" must occur in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel cell]] named the [[Spectres]], traveled to the [[planet]] [[Bilzen]] aboard their [[VCX-series auxiliary starfighter]], the ''[[Phantom]]''. There, they continued Bridger's [[Jedi training]] before they were interrupted by the [[Fifth Brother]] and [[Seventh Sister]],<ref name="Breaking" /> [[Inquisitor]]s of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Jedi hunter|Jedi-hunting]] [[Inquisitorius]],<ref name="SWE">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> who were pursuing the Jedi duo.<ref name="Breaking" />

[[File:EscapingBilzen-IceBreaking.png|thumb|right|250px|The Spectres flee Bilzen aboard the ''Ghost''.]]
The Jedi [[Duel on Bilzen|escaped]] the hunters aboard the ''Phantom'' and [[Comm unit|called]] for help from<ref name="Breaking" /> fellow Spectre member<ref name="Spectre">{{EncyclopediaCite|Freedom Fighters From Lothal and Other Characters}}</ref> [[Hera Syndulla]]. The latter arrived in the ''[[Ghost]]''<ref name="Breaking" />&mdash;a modified [[VCX-100 light freighter]] that acted as a home for the Spectres<ref name="SWE" />&mdash;trapped the Inquisitors by causing a [[cave]]-in, and picked the ''Phantom'' up with its [[magnetic lock]]. Aboard the ''Ghost'', the Spectres fled the planet<ref name="Breaking" /> and the system.<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref> The Inquisitors eventually escaped their confines within the cave<ref name="Breaking" /> and left the [[star]] system as well.<ref name="Legacy" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bilzen system first appeared, albeit unidentified, in "[[Ice Breaking]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic story]] included in<ref name="Breaking" /> the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 16|sixteenth issue]] of the [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine]],<ref name="Mag">[[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 16|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine 16]]</ref> which was published on [[March 16]], [[2016]].<ref name="Star Wars Rebels Magazin 16">{{Panini|shop=1|url=star-wars-tv-und-film-science-fiction-magazine/star-wars-rebels-magazin-16-ydswrm016|text=Star Wars - Rebels - Magazin 16|nolive=1}}</ref> The story was written by [[Martin Fisher]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]].<ref name="Breaking" /> The system was first identified as the "Bilzen system" in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. The map and appendix were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
