'''Flight QS-14''' was a transport that was scheduled to depart from [[Mirogana Spaceport]]'s [[gate N1]] on [[Toshara]], a [[moon]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]], bound for [[Gand (planet)|Gand]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The departure was however cancelled after [[Thorden]], the moon's [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Governor]],  closed down a section of the spaceport while he met with [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] after a successful Imperial [[raid on the Starfaller's workshop]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flight QS-14 was in [[Aurebesh]] on a departures sign that players can read during the "All In" quest as part of the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref>
