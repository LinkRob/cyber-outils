{{Quote|As a result of its growth and activities the sheer number of files and datacards produced by COMPNOR during a standard year could feasibly have filled the Death Star. The fact that even some of these records exist and are available to us is through the dedicated work of Amn Fos-er and the New Republic's Post-Imperial Administration and Ephemeral Preservation Network.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>}}
'''Amn Fos-er''' was an individual who helped preserve some of the large number of records produced by the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] during the [[Galactic Empire|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. Thanks to Fos-er and the [[Post-Imperial Administration and Ephemeral Preservation Network]] of the [[New Republic]], some of the materials were available to [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]], who mentioned Fos-er's dedicated work in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''<ref name="Rise" /> which was published in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Amn Fos-er was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
