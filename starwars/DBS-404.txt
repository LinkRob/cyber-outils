'''DBS-404''' was a [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bomber]] that served in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. At one point, DBS-404 was damaged while in a battle on [[Geonosis]] and conducted a suicidal run on a [[Galactic Republic]] [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer]] after its preservation protocols were not found. DBS-404 was painted in [[Color|yellow]] markings.<ref name="X-wing" />
