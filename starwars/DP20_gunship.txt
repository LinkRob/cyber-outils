The '''DP20 gunship''', also known as the '''DP20 Frigate''' or simply as the '''DP20''' and colloquially referred to as the '''Corellian Gunship''', was a [[warship]] model manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]]. It saw service in the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Galactic Empire]], and then notably in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].

==Characteristics==
The DP20 [[Frigate]], also known colloquially as the "[[Corellian]] [[Gunship]]," was a class of frigate that was manufactured by the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation]] and cost 3,200,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Its [[hull]] was composed of three hull sections that were strung along a single, reinforced multi-decked corridor.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> It had a linear design, with a cockpit shaped like a truncated cone.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The DP20 was equipped with an oversized [[Solar ionization reactor|ionization reactor]] which gave it significant speed. It was also equipped with a class 2 primary [[hyperdrive]] and a class 16 secondary.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

DP20 Frigates had powerful [[targeting computer]]s and were heavily [[Deflector shield generator|shielded]]. It had a long-range [[sensor]] and a [[navigation computer]]. DP20s were heavily armed, boasting eight [[turret]]-mounted twin light [[turbolaser]] batteries, six retractable turret-mounted quad [[laser cannon]]s, and two port and two starboard fast-loading [[concussion missile launcher]]s which could target starfighters, enemy [[Patrol ship|patrol boats]], and [[corvette]]s. During combat, DP20s kept their shields balanced when encountered by enemy starfighters, but angled them in concentration to the enemy's angle of attack. It was operated by ninety-one [[officer]]s and crew and had consumables for eight [[Standard month|months]].<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==Role==
The DP20 Frigate was designed as a pure, single-purpose military vessel. During the [[Clone Wars]], it served the [[Republic Navy]] as a vessel to respond to the increased number of [[droid]] [[starfighter]]s and [[pirate]] groups. Their targeting systems and armament allowed them to fulfill anti-starfighter and anti-[[capital ship]] roles, and during the [[Imperial Era]], the Imperial DP20s were relegated down to operate in distant and low-value sectors.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==History==
{{Quote|The Corellian gunships were there at Endor. The Kesselian X4s, too. It didn't even take much convincing.|[[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]]|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:RebelFleet-2015StarWars23.png|thumb|left|250px|At least two DP20 Frigates were used in the Alliance Fleet around the time of the siege on Tureen VII.]]
The DP20 Frigate was manufactured for the [[Galactic Republic]] and saw action during the Clone Wars as part of the Republic Navy, often being dispatched against pirate groups and droid starfighters. After the [[Galactic Empire]] was formed, however, most of the ships were retired from active service by the [[Imperial Quartermaster Corps]] and sent to boneyards to be scrapped. The few remaining DP20s in the Empire's arsenal were then sent to remote sectors of [[the galaxy]]. This large supply of starships meant they would be scrapped, but were often stolen by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and other independent organizations.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

By [[0 ABY]],<ref name="SW 23 Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars (2015) 23|''Star Wars'' (2015) 23]] to [[0 ABY]].</ref> at least two DP20s were used by the Alliance shortly before the Imperial [[Siege on Tureen VII]]. Those DP20s traveled in concert with an [[EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate]] that housed members of [[Alliance High Command]], namely [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]], [[General]] [[Jan Dodonna]], and [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]].<ref name="SW23" /> A DP20 Frigate was later stationed as part of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] above the [[planet]] [[Hoth]] while [[Echo Base]] was being constructed.<ref name="Echo of Victory">{{StoryCite|story=An Echo of Victory|book=Empire Ascendant 1}}</ref>

At some point during the war, the Galactic Empire laid a trap in a starship boneyard by leaking information that a DP20 Frigate was awaiting decommissioning and was vulnerable to theft. Aboard the DP20, however, was a [[Unidentified elite squad|squad]] of elite [[stormtrooper]]s behind shielded compartments that were ready to engage any shipjackers. At another point, a DP20 Frigate took part in a battle, and its rebel [[captain]] was [[assassin]]ated by a double agent working for the Empire, sending the ship into chaos after the agent fled the bridge.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

When Admiral Ackbar later sought to build up his forces for the upcoming attack on the Galactic Empire's [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] above [[Endor]], the former [[Baron Administrator]] of [[Cloud City]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] introduced him to various individuals who would be willing to join the fight, including a group of [[Alderaanian]] [[Exile (punishment)|exiles]] that had four DP20 Frigates in their arsenal. Eventually, those four gunships participated in the [[Battle of Endor]], where the Alliance won its most crucial victory over the Empire.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Blockade_Runner_Sketchbook.jpg|thumb|180px|The DP20 began as a concept for the ''Millennium Falcon'' and later, the Rebel Blockade Runner.]]
The DP20 Frigate first appeared in the [[2016]] [[canon]] [[Marvel Comics|Marvel comic]] [[Star Wars (2015) 23|''Star Wars'' (2015) 23]], which was written by [[Jason Aaron]] and penciled by [[Jorge Molina]]. The shot of the Alliance fleet in which two DP20s appear seems to have been based<ref name="SW23" /> on a very similar shot from the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] video game ''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]''.<ref name="TFU2vg">''[[Star Wars: The Force Unleashed II]]''</ref>

The DP20 Frigate was subsequently identified in ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', a reference book and boxed set released in [[2017]] and authored by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />

The DP20 frigate first appeared in ''Star Wars'' Legends in the Marvel comic [[Star Wars (1977) 12|''Star Wars'' (1977) 12]], released in [[1978]] and written by [[Archie Goodwin]] and penciled by [[Carmine Infantino]].<ref name="SW12 Legends">[[Star Wars (1977) 12|''Star Wars'' (1977) 12]]</ref>
