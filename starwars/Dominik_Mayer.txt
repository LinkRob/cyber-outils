'''Dominik Mayer''' (born [[January 30]], [[1991]] in [[Wikipedia:Germany|Germany]]) is a [[Wikipedia:Germans|German]] concept artist and illustrator. For the ''[[Star Wars]]'' franchise, he created loading screens of [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Darth Sidious]] for the [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Fortnite]]'' and covers for ''[[The Essential Legends Collection]]'' reprints of four books in the [[Star Wars: The Old Republic (novel series)|''Star Wars: The Old Republic'']] novel series, as well as for the novel ''[[Knight Errant]]''.

==Biography==
[[File:Fortnite_Sith_Ascension_loading_screen.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Mayer's "Sith Ascension" loading screen art]]
Dominik Mayer was born on [[January 30]],<ref name="Deviant" /> [[1991]]<ref name="Interview" /> in [[Wikipedia:Germany|Germany]].<ref name="Deviant" /> His<ref name="Deviant" /> first ''[[Star Wars]]'' project was loading screen art for Chapter 3: Season 3 of the [[2017]]<ref name="Fornite Release Date">{{WebCite|url=https://dotesports.com/fortnite/news/when-did-fortnite-come-out|text=When did Fortnite come out?|author=[https://dotesports.com/author/jorgeaguilar Aguilar, Jorge]|date=2022-07-08|work=[https://dotesports.com Dot Esports]|archivedate=20221117041743}}</ref> [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Fortnite]]''.<ref name="Fortnite">''[[Fortnite]]''</ref> The art, titled "Sith Ascension," was released in ''Fortnite'' on<ref name="Fortnite" /> [[June 5]], [[2022]]<ref name="Fortnite S3 Trailer">{{FortniteYouTube|jexE8vm8q8E|text=Fortnite Chapter 3 Season 3 Gameplay Trailer}}</ref> as part of a cosmetics set featuring the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. Players could purchase the loading screen and have it appear while they load into matches.<ref name="Fortnite" /> On [[June 6]], Mayer published his art on social media, saying that it was a great pleasure to work on the piece. He found it incredibly cool to paint Vader in his personal art style,<ref name="ArtStation" /> and later indicated that it was his favorite piece of work for the franchise.<ref name="">{{Instagram|username=dominikmayerart|url=p/C6jqtPWMaQZ|name=Dominik Mayer|date=2016-07-29|quote=My favourite official #StarWars artwork I did in the last years for Epic Games. It is a Fortnite loading screen!|archiveurl=https://archive.is/LMWlg}}</ref>

Mayer also created the cover art for ''[[The Essential Legends Collection]]'' reprints<ref name="Essential Twitter">{{Twitter|DelReyStarWars|status/1539345861590888448|[[Del Rey]]|date=2022-06-21|quote=The covers keep getting better and better... new wave of #EssentialLegends novels, coming Nov 1, 2022. Each edition will have a new cover with original artwork.|archivedate=20220621230608}}</ref> of the [[2011]]<ref name="Deceived">''[[The Old Republic: Deceived]]''</ref><ref name="Revan">''[[The Old Republic: Revan]]''</ref> [[Star Wars: The Old Republic (novel series)|''The Old Republic'']] novels [[The Old Republic: Deceived|''Deceived'']]<ref name="Deceived" /> and [[The Old Republic: Revan|''Revan'']],<ref name="Revan" /> written by [[Paul S. Kemp]]<ref name="Deceived" /> and [[Drew Karpyshyn]] respectively.<ref name="Revan" /> Both of Mayer's designs were also used for the audiobook adaptations of each novel.<ref name="Deceived audiobook">[[The Old Republic: Deceived (audiobook)|''The Old Republic: Deceived'' audiobook]]</ref><ref name="Revan audiobook">[[The Old Republic: Revan (audiobook)|''The Old Republic: Revan'' audiobook]]</ref> Publisher [[Del Rey]] announced Mayer's involvement and revealed his cover art on [[June 21]], 2022. The reprinted novels were published on [[November 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Essential Twitter" />

In [[2023]],<ref name="Edelweiss Fatal" /><ref name="Edelweiss Annihilation" /> Mayer created the cover artwork for ''The Essential Legends Collection'' reprints of the novels ''[[The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance]]''<ref name="Fatal Alliance">''[[The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance]]''</ref> and ''[[The Old Republic: Annihilation]]'',<ref name="Annihilation">''[[The Old Republic: Annihilation]]''</ref> as well as their [[The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance (audiobook)|audiobook]] [[The Old Republic: Annihilation (audiobook)|adaptations]].<ref name="Fatal Alliance Audiobook">[[The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance (audiobook)|''The Old Republic: Fatal Alliance'' audiobook]]</ref><ref name="Annihilation Audiobook">[[The Old Republic: Annihilation (audiobook)|''The Old Republic: Annihilation'' audiobook]]</ref> The novels were authored by [[Sean Williams]]<ref name="Fatal Alliance" /> and Drew Karpyshyn, respectively.<ref name="Annihilation" /> The [[2024|following year]],<ref name="Edelweiss Knight Errant">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/203232/knight-errant-star-wars-legends-by-john-jackson-miller|text=Knight Errant: Star Wars Legends}}</ref> the artist also completed the reprint cover artwork for the novel [[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant]], authored by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="Knight Errant">[[Knight Errant (novel)|''Knight Errant'']]</ref> In [[2025]],<ref name="FortniteGB">{{FortniteYouTube|RKZPMyAvEXs|text=Fortnite Galactic Battle Teaser Trailer}}</ref> another of Mayer's pieces featured as a loading screen for ''Fortnite''{{'s}} Galactic Battle, this time being a piece focusing on [[Darth Sidious]].<ref name="Fortnite" />
