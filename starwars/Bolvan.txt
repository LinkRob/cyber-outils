'''Bolvan''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] who served as the [[gunnery captain]] aboard the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]''<ref name="A New Hope" /> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="A New Hope Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1}}</ref> After the [[Secret mission to Tatooine|capture]] of the [[CR90 corvette|corvette]] ''[[Tantive IV]]'', he ordered his subordinate [[Lieutenant]] [[Hija]] not to fire at an [[escape pod]] that launched from the captured [[Starship|ship]] because there were no life signs aboard.<ref name="A New Hope" /> Due to new regulations designed to counter rebel [[propaganda]] about Imperial accuracy, the Empire had ordered that all promotions would be tied to an individual's kill ratio. Therefore, firing on the pod would have been a waste of lasers and hurt his rise through the ranks.<ref name="The Sith of Datawork">{{StoryCite|story=The Sith of Datawork|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

While the [[corvette]] was being searched, and the [[Death Star plans]], which [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] suspected the rebel ship of carrying, still had not been found, Bolvan came to suspect that they were hidden aboard the escape pod. In order to avoid getting in trouble for failing to realize this earlier, he asked logistics officer [[Arvira]] for assistance. Bolvan disliked filling out paperwork, as he felt it took up too much of his time, but agreed to follow through all the datawork Arvira suggested to save himself from Vader's wrath. In return, he promised to lose in the next gambling game they both attended.<ref name="The Sith of Datawork" />
