In [[0 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Chelli Aphra]] '''assaulted''' the [[Naboo]] [[Commodex Tahn's villa|villa]] of the retired [[mortician]] [[Commodex Tahn]] on behalf of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. She stormed the villa with<ref name="Darth Vader 10" /> a [[platoon]]<ref name="AphraBook" /> of [[battle droid]]s, and although Tahn tried to fight back with a [[DC-15A blaster rifle]], he was injured and captured. [[0-0-0]] began torturing him to learn information about [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. After learning that she had indeed given birth to a [[Luke Skywalker|son]], 0-0-0 electrocuted Tahn to death.<ref name="Darth Vader 10" />
