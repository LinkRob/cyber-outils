The [[Aki-Aki]] '''Fadda-Rarb Arda''' worked as a tribal guardian of communally raised [[Youngling|children]] on the [[planet]] [[Pasaana]]. Having reached adulthood,<ref name="TROS VD" /> Arda attended the [[Festival of the Ancestors]] in the [[Forbidden Valley]]<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> At the festival, stood by some of the festival's [[tent]]s with fellow guardian [[Jesba-Be Issa]], both holding infant Aki-Aki. While Arda cooed over the infant, [[Resistance]] agents [[Mission to Pasaana (Resistance)|searching]] for a [[Sith wayfinder]] passed by the guardians.<ref name="TROS" /> Arda wore a [[Color|red]] [[Clothing|robe]] and had gray skin and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fadda-Rarb Arda was created for the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS" /> and was identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> Creature and droid puppeteer [[Barbara Fadden]]<ref name="TROS" /> portrayed the character, with Fadda-Rarb Arda being a partial anagram of the performer's name. Fadden also portrayed [[Faddaff Davenspon]] and [[Odla Sur]] in the film.<ref name="Fadden">{{WebCite|url=https://www.barbarafadden.com|text=Barbara Fadden &ndash; Creature Performer|author=Fadden, Barbara|archivedate=20220404171843}}</ref>
