"'''Buggle's Day Out'''" is an animated short that is part of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season One|first season]] of ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]''. It was released on the Disney Channel's YouTube channel on [[December 31]], [[2018]], and is the tenth of twelve shorts.

==Official description==
''Kaz gets in trouble when he tries to take care of Buggles!''<ref name="Buggle's Day Out" />

==Plot summary==
[[Torra Doza]] drops her pet [[Voorpak]], [[Buggles]], off at the [[Jarek Yeager's Repair Station|repair shop]], where [[Kazuda Xiono]] has agreed to watch him while she's busy. After Torra leaves, Kaz looks down to discover the animal has vanished. He quickly finds Buggles on the other side of the ''[[Fireball]]'', eating from a bowl of food, and attempts to collect him, only for the Voorpak to run outside. Kaz thinks Buggles has fallen into the ocean, but sees him pestering a [[Speagull|bird]] on the precarious ledges on the edge of the ''[[Colossus]]''. He goes after Buggles, putting himself in danger, but the pet bounds off again. Back in the repair shop, Kaz is unsuccessfully searching for Buggles when Torra returns and asks him where he is. Just then Buggles, inside the ''Fireball'', presses some buttons, causing the ship to take off. Torra asks Kaz who's flying the ship, and Kaz nervously admits that it's Buggles, causing Torra to look alarmed.

==Continuity==
The short's title misspells [[Buggles]]' name while adding a possessive apostrophe.
