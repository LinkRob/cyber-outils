The '''BE-29 blaster rifle''' was a [[blaster rifle]] with a [[Color|black]] [[barrel]], a black [[scope]], a black [[trigger]], and a brown, petrified [[rochiawood]] shoulder [[stock]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[moisture farmer]] [[Beru Whitesun Lars]] owned<ref name="Kenobi VI">{{Kenobi|Part VI}}</ref> a sawed off BE-29 blaster rifle<ref name="Dawn" /> that acted as a [[shotgun]] [[blaster]]<ref name="Kenobi VI AD">{{Kenobi|Part VI}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> that she kept hidden behind a false pipe in the [[Lars homestead|homestead]] she lived in with her [[Marriage|husband]] [[Owen Lars]] and their adopted [[Gender|son]], [[Luke Skywalker]].<ref name="Kenobi VI" />

In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Part VI]]" to [[9 BBY]].</ref> she retrieved the blaster and threw it to a surprised Owen after he informed her that the former [[Inquisitor]] [[Reva Sevander]] was one her way to the homestead in search of Skywalker. Owen then used the BE-29 to shoot at Sevander from cover once she entered the homestead, but she deflected the bolts with her [[Reva's lightsaber|lightsaber]] and eventually caused Owen to drop the gun by [[Telekinesis|hurling]] a piece of equipment at him.<ref name="Kenobi VI" />

==Behind the scenes==
A BE-29 blaster rifle appeared in "[[Part VI]],"<ref name="Kenobi VI" /> the final episode of the [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'', which was released on [[June 22]], [[2022]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-june-2022|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in June 2022}}</ref> The weapon was then identified in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="Dawn" />
