'''Bibs''' was a [[Bravaisian]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. A member of the class of Padawans training aboard the ''[[Star Hopper]]'', Bibs was, along with the rest of the class, dispatched to aid [[Trymant IV]] when it fell under threat from the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]].<ref name="HRA 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bibs first appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|first issue]] of the [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2021)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]], and released by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="HRA 1" /> on [[February 3]], [[2021]].<ref name="Disney Books">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/star-wars-the-high-republic-adventures-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures #1}}</ref>

[[Cole Horton]]'s reference book ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Chronicles of the Jedi]]'',<ref name="Chronicles" /> released on [[March 21]], [[2023]],<ref name="AmazonChronicles">{{TitanBooks|url=71311-star-wars-the-high-republic-chronicles-of-the-jedi-an-illustrated-guide-to-the-galaxys-golden-age|text=Star Wars: The High Republic – Chronicles of the Jedi: An Illustrated Guide to the Galaxy's Golden Age}}</ref> uses "they/them" pronouns for Bibs.<ref name="Chronicles" /> [[Megan Crouse]] and [[Amy Richau]]'s reference book ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'',<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> released on [[December 5]] of the same year,<ref name="Amazon US">{{DK|url=us/book/9780744093339-star-wars-the-high-republic-character-encyclopedia|text=Star Wars The High Republic Character Encyclopedia}}</ref> uses "he/him" pronouns in the character's entry.<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia" /> Older, the author who created<ref name="Email">{{EmailCite|email=File:DanielJoseOlderEmailBibs.png|creator=Daniel José Older|date=2023-11-27}}</ref> and first introduced Bibs into the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]],<ref name="HRA 1" /> was unaware of the differences between the publications.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|djolder|status/1727427233386832021|[[Daniel José Older]]|date=2023-11-22|quote=Thanks for pointing that out, I actually didn't know. He's always been he/him in the scripts, but I guess this canonically makes him he/they?|archiveurl=https://archive.is/mKkz1}}</ref> The author noted that the character had been created with<ref name="Email" /> and used "he/him" in scripts until that point, but surmised that the inclusion of both in sources could meant that Bibs went by "he/they" pronouns in canon.<ref name="Twitter" /> Older then indicated that he felt the best way to think of the Bibs was to use "he/they" pronouns when referring to them.<ref name="Email" />
