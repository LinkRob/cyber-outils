'''Ansett Island'''<ref name="TROS VD" /> was an island located on the [[planet]] [[Epo]]<ref name="TLO" /> within the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> At some point between approximately [[10 ABY]] and [[33 ABY]],<ref name="Dates">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' establishes that [[Jannah]], a former [[Desertion|deserter]] member of [[Company 77]], was in her early twenties by the [[time]] of the events of {{Film|IX}} and that Jannah and other deserters of the [[company]] had [[Colonist|settled]] on [[Kef Bir]] [[Standard year|years]] prior to that film's events. Furthermore, ''The Visual Dictionary'' describes the state of [[the galaxy]] up to and during the events of ''The Rise of Skywalker'', which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[35 ABY]], thereby placing the desertion of Company 77 to some point between [[10 ABY]] and [[33 ABY]].</ref> the [[First Order]] was involved in the [[Battle of Ansett Island]] there. During the battle, [[Company 77]] of [[First Order stormtrooper]]s&mdash;a training unit filled with teenaged [[trooper]]s&mdash;was ordered to [[Death|kill]] a group of civilians.<ref name="TROS VD" /> The entire [[company]] refused to carry out the order and subsequently [[Desertion|deserted]], escaping aboard a [[cruiser]] that members of the company took offworld.<ref name="TROS" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|'Twas at the battle fought<br />On Ansett Island&mdash;there we mutinied ''[&hellip;]|Jannah, as presented in ''William Shakespeare's The Merry Rise of Skywalker: Star Wars Part the Ninth''|<ref name="Shakespeare">''[[William Shakespeare's The Merry Rise of Skywalker: Star Wars Part the Ninth]]''</ref>}}
Ansett Island was first mentioned as part of the name of the Battle of Ansett Island that the former Company 77 member [[Jannah]] mentions to the former stormtrooper [[Finn]] in<ref name="TROS" /> the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']].<ref name="YearByYear">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, New Edition]]''</ref> It was also directly mentioned in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> The [[2020]] [[Canon|non-canon]] adaptation ''[[William Shakespeare's The Merry Rise of Skywalker: Star Wars Part the Ninth]]'' by [[Ian Doescher]] contains a different version of the line from ''The Rise of Skywalker'' mentioning Ansett Island that likewise directly refers to the location.<ref name="Shakespeare" />
