'''Cylo's base'''<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was made up of many large, [[armor]]ed [[Whale-ship]]s and was the headquarters of [[Doctor]] [[Cylo]], an [[agent]] working for [[Darth Sidious]], the [[Galactic Emperor|ruler]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. It was on this base that Cylo conducted his research into creating new forms of life, combining various alien parts and [[cybernetics]]; the doctor's ultimate goal was to convince the Emperor to replace his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], with his creations.<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />

Cylo's cyborgs were also trained for combat in this facility; they included, [[Morit Astarte|Morit]] and [[Aiolin Astarte]], [[Tulon Voidgazer]], and [[Commander]] [[Karbin]]. A permanent garrison was also maintained on the base to protect Cylo's operation from attackers.<ref name="Darth Vader 5" /><ref name="Darth Vader 6">[[Darth Vader (2015) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 6]]</ref>

After discovering Cylo's mission for the Emperor and his [[Sith Master|Master's]] plans to replace him, Vader led a [[Skirmish in Cylo's research base|mission]] to the base which he had discovered from Cylo-IV to investigate his potential replacements.<ref name="Darth Vader 5" />
