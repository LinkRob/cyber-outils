The '''First Order insignia''',<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> also known as the '''First Order symbol''', or '''hexagonal sigil''',<ref name="TSWB" /> was a sixteen-rayed symbol inscribed within a hexagon,<ref name="TFA VD" /> denoting an explosive force pushing against attempts to contain it.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> It was the emblem of the [[First Order]], a movement seeking to reclaim the legacy of the [[Galactic Empire]]. [[First Order officer|Officers]] of the First Order wore the insignia on their [[Hat|caps]], and also as a patch on their [[First Order military uniforms|uniforms]], in the region of the left upper [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="TFA VD" />
