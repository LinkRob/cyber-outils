'''Bota''' was a type of flowering [[plant]] with helpful properties. Shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Inquisitor" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] discovered bota growing in the flower beds of a small walled garden accessible from the [[Jedi Archives]] in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. She recognised the plant and remembered its name from readings that [[Jedi Master]] [[Sember Vey]] had assigned to her.<ref name="Inquisitor" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bota appeared in [[Delilah S. Dawson]]'s [[2023]] novel ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]''.<ref name="Inquisitor" /> The [[Bota/Legends|plant]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[2004]] novel ''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]'', which was written by [[Michael Reaves]] and [[Steve Perry]].<ref name="MedStar">''[[MedStar I: Battle Surgeons]]''</ref>
