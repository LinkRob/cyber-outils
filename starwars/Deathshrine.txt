A '''deathshrine''' of the [[Central Isopter]] was located on [[Ash Moon 1]] in the [[Kartovian Formation]]. [[Pilgrim]]s would visit the deathshrine and pray by shooting [[blaster]]s into the sky. When a [[probe droid]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Project Swarm]] was shot down over Ash Moon 1, an [[Archaeologist|archaeological]] team led by [[Professor]] [[Rupo Ud|Ud]] visited the shrine.<ref name="Aphra 37" />
