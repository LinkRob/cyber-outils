'''Claro''' was an [[Ugnaught]] [[thief|bandit]] active during the [[High Republic Era]]. Working with fellow Ugnaughts [[Flip (Ugnaught)|Flip]] and [[Daro (Ugnaught)|Daro]], Claro acted in a leadership role among the trio. During a spree of robberies in which Claro stole the [[power converter]] off a [[Jedi shuttle (High Republic Era)|Jedi shuttle]], he and his associates were pursued by [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Wes Vinik]] and [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Nubs]], who eventually apprehending the thieves, forcing them to return their plunder and clean up the messes they made along the way.<ref name="Nubs and the Bumbling Bandits" />
