During the [[Clone Wars]], a '''flotilla''' was [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], the [[Head of State|leader]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. In [[22 BBY]], at the same [[time]] as the Confederacy's [[Battle of Ryloth|conquest]] of the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]], the [[flotilla]] was active in [[realspace]]. It also participated in the Separatists' [[invasion of Scipio]], in which resulted in a temporary [[blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Scipio]] and led to a brief battle between the [[warship]]s of Dooku's [[fleet]] and the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Skywalker's fleet|fleet]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Shortly after the Republic's arrival, however, the flotilla fled the [[Scipio system/Legends|system]] at Dooku's command.

==Description==
The [[flotilla]]<ref name="Crisis EG" /> served as the personal [[fleet]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Head of State|leader]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]]. Flagged by Dooku's [[Dooku's Providence-class carrier/destroyer|personal]] [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], it consisted of at least six [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]] and two [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]].<ref name="Supply Lines" /> Later during the [[time]] of the [[Scipio]] [[Invasion of Scipio|invasion]], the flotilla was in possession of [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel]]s.<ref name="Crisis" />
