==Description==
{{Quote|Look here, clone, you speak to me with respect.''"<br/>"''In my experience, respect is something to be earned.''"<br/>"''Yet the Empire assigned you to this desolate rock, where you let the majority of your squad get killed.|[[Nolan]] and [[Mayday]]|<ref name="The Outpost">{{TBB|The Outpost}}</ref>}}
[[File:Mayday_dies.png|thumb|250px|left|Mayday was denied medical support by Nolan due to the latter treating clones as disposable.]]
A sentiment against [[Cloning|cloned]] individuals existed in [[the galaxy]] for varying forms of reason, which ultimately acknowledged them as nothing more than replaceable products. For some, it was a form of prejudice that was held against, referring to them derogatorily as "clones" and ignoring their individuality as a person. While others may see clones as a cost prohibitive operation to maintain.<ref name="Replacements" /> Many propagandas<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> and manipulations also helped push the sentiment further.<ref name="Truth">{{TBB|Truth and Consequences}}</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|We don't want your kind around here. We don't ''serve'' your kind around here!|A Quarren bartender, to Hunter|<ref name="Ghost Agents 1">[[Hyperspace Stories: The Bad Batch—Ghost Agents 1|''Hyperspace Stories: The Bad Batch—Ghost Agents'' 1]]</ref>}}
During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Kaminoan]]s treated their clones as if they were machines to be discarded if they malfunctioned.<ref name="Fugitive">{{TCW|Fugitive}}</ref> Around the same time, sentiment against clones grew as propaganda [[chassisplast]] were spread around during the war.<ref name="Propaganda" /> Clone troopers also had few individual liberties under Republic law despite being deployed as the defenders of Republic [[democracy]].<ref name="SWEasy">''[[Star Wars Made Easy]]''</ref> In the beginning of the [[Imperial Era]], the Kaminoans still uphold the same value and opinion, seeing clones as their properties to maintain operation with the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Replacements" />

The end of the Clone Wars and the [[Proclamation of the New Order|birth]] of the new Galactic Empire<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> marked the end of the cloning program as the Empire attempted to recruit common soldiers en masse, which would be less costly<ref name="Replacements">{{TBB|Replacements}}</ref> and ensure the loyalty of a new army of troopers who were not created with the now-dissolved Republic in mind.<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref> This left many veteran clones unemployed<ref name="K3">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref> due to their accelerated aging and the inability to create more soldiers.<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TBB|The Clone Conspiracy}}</ref> Clones didn't have the same rights or representation as other organic beings and the Empire viewed them as military assets instead of real people.<ref name="Secrets of the Clone Troopers">''[[Star Wars: The Secrets of the Clone Troopers]]''</ref> Many of these clones became subjects of the [[Advanced Science Division]] experiments in [[Mount Tantiss]], where [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]], who viewed them as nothing more than pieces of Imperial property, imprisoned them so they could be experimented on.<ref name="Tipping Point">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref> Other clones continued to serve the Empire as [[Purge Trooper]]s of the [[Inquisitorius|Imperial Inquisition]].<ref name="DV 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref>

During the [[New Republic]], all experiments related to the field of [[cloning]] were banned by the [[Coruscant Accords]].<ref name="Convert">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 19: The Convert}}</ref>
