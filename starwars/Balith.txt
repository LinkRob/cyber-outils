{{Quote|There's a large-scale civil war on Balith. They've requested our assistance. Anakin, you will leave immediately to command the Third Legion.|[[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], to Anakin Skywalker|audio=CivilWarOnBalith-TCW-Assassin.flac|<ref name="Assassin" />}}
'''Balith''' was an inhabited [[planet]] located in the [[Balith system]] that was in the midst of a [[Balith's Civil War|civil war]] in [[21 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]], at the request of Balith's people, the [[Galactic Republic]] dispatched its [[Third Legion]], under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]], to join the conflict.

==Description==
Balith was a terrestrial [[planet]]<ref name="Bug">{{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> in the [[Balith system]], which was situated within the [[Inner Rim Territories]] and in grid square N-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

==History==
[[File:NuClassTransportWithCaptainRexAnakinAndAhsoka-Assassin.png|thumb|right|300px|Ahsoka Tano watches as Anakin Skywalker and Captain Rex prepare to leave for Balith on a ''Nu''-class transport.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Assassin" /> a [[galaxy]]-spanning conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] which occurred from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Balith was in the midst of its own [[Balith's Civil War|civil war]].<ref name="Assassin" /> In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="AssassinDate">{{TCWcanondates|307}}</ref> the people of Balith requested help from the Republic. The Republic and the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Jedi High Council|High Council]] responded by sending [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex|CT-7567]] "Rex" to [[Commanding officer|command]] the [[Third Legion]]<ref name="Assassin" />&mdash;a [[clone trooper]] [[legion]] with the Galactic Republic's [[Grand Army of the Republic|army]]<ref name="Timelines" />&mdash;and assist the people of Balith. Though not part of the mission, Skywalker's [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] noted that she had not been to Balith before escorting her master to a waiting [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]].<ref name="Assassin" /> 

At some point between [[20 BBY]] and 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="BugDate">"[[Bug (short story)|Bug]]" is set during the [[Clone Wars]] and is stated to take place several [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Dathomir]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the battle to [[20 BBY]] and the end of the Clone Wars to [[19 BBY]], the short story must take place between 20 and 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> a group of [[refugee]]s from Balith ended up on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planetoid]] [[Sídi]]. While there, the refugees paid to use an abandoned [[Abandoned military relay tower|communications tower]] in order to receive news of their war-torn planet. Despite their hopes, none of the news they received was good.<ref name="Bug" />
