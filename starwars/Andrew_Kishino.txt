I've always been a huge fan of ''Star Wars''.|Andrew Kishino|<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/interview-andrew-kishino-saw-gerrera-on-the-clone-wars|text=Interview: Andrew Kishino &ndash; Saw Gerrera on ''The Clone Wars''}}</ref>}}
'''Andrew Kishino''' is a voice actor who portrayed [[Saw Gerrera]] in ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''<ref name="SW.com" /> as well as the characters of [[Izuma]] and [[Juro]] for the series ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]''. He has also provided his voice to [[Wikipedia:Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles (2012 TV series)|''Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles'']] and [[Wikipedia:The Lion Guard|''The Lion Guard'']].<ref name="OffWeb">{{WebCite|url=http://www.andrewkishino.com/#!animation/cxzy|text=Andrew Kishino Animation VO|work=[https://www.andrewkishino.com andrewkishino.com]|archivedate=20180817204711}}</ref> Kishino was married to [[Vanessa Marshall]],<ref name="BackRoom">{{WebCite|url=http://www.backroominterviews.net/vanessa-marshall-interview-june-2008|text=Vanessa Marshall Interview&mdash;June 2008|work=BackRoomInterviews.net|archivedate=20090220185237}}</ref> who voices [[Hera Syndulla]] in ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''.<ref name="SWYouTube">{{SWYouTube|video=RzopG0SGJYI|text=Meet Hera, the Pilot &#124; ''Star Wars Rebels''}}</ref>
