"'''Dear 2-1B'''" was a humorous "advice" column printed in [[Star Wars Insider]] around the time of the [[prequel trilogy]] releases. It featured [[2-1B/Legends|2-1B]], the [[Medical droid/Legends|medical droid]] who saved [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] at the beginning of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']], as a bitter columnist. He alternates between answering reader questions and ranting that he is the unsung hero of the entire ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga.

Dear 2-1B evolved from an editorial in [[Star Wars Insider 47|''Star Wars Insider'' 47]] in which the droid laments the relative unpopularity of his character among Star Wars fans. The editorial was co-written by editors [[Scott Chernoff]] and [[Jon Bradley Snyder]], with all subsequent columns written by Scott Chernoff.
