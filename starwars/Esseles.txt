'''Esseles''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Squadrons" /> located in the [[Esseles system]] in the [[Darpa sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="SWSystems" /> that had been contested territory throughout the [[Galactic Civil War]] and had a secret [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Esseles listening post|listening post]] concealed in its icy [[Planetary ring|rings]].<ref name="Squadrons" /> The [[Carrack-class light cruiser|''Carrack''-class light cruiser]] was designed and manufactured on Esseles by the [[Damorian Manufacturing Corporation]].<ref name="BMF 52">{{BuildFalconCite|52|Starship Fact File}}</ref>
