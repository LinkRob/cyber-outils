A '''fuel-air bomb''' was an anti-personnel weapon that used [[Atmosphere|atmospheric]] [[oxygen]] as an oxidizer. This created a powerful explosion and created a temporary vacuum in the blast zone.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fuel-air bombs were mentioned in ''[[Gadgets and Gear]]'', a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplementary sourcebook for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
