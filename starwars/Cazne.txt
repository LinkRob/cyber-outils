'''Cazne''' was an area of cratered flatlands located on the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]]. The site of a major battle during the [[Battle of Ryloth]], it was marked by a number of simple [[Twi'lek]] graves, while the [[Twi'lek freedom fighters hideout|underground hideout]] of the [[Twi'lek Resistance]] was hidden beneath the wreckage of a [[C-9979 landing craft]].<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/liberty-on-ryloth-episode-guide|text="Liberty on Ryloth" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
