{{Quote|Mostly desert. Nothing like here. Going too far away into nature meant you might end up a well-buffed skeleton if a storm came up suddenly. Small cities grew up around deep wells and then became tall cities. We had the best fried palm fruit.|Vi Moradi, to [[Salju]] on Chaaktil|<ref name="Black Spire" />}}
'''Chaaktil''' was a [[desert]] [[planet]]<ref name="Perfect Weapon" /> located in the [[Chaaktil system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]], in grid square N-14 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It had many [[City|cities]], such as [[Chaako City]]. The [[bounty hunter]] [[Bazine Netal]] grew up on Chaaktil, where she learned a range of deadly [[Unarmed combat|martial arts]] at [[Delphi Kloda]]'s [[Delphi Kloda's combat school|combat school]].<ref name="Perfect Weapon" /> Chaaktil was also the [[homeworld]] of [[Vi Moradi]].<ref name="Black Spire" />
