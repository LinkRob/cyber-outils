A [[Gender|female]] [[Artisan|jeweler]] was the '''sister''' of<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> the [[human]]<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] female [[Cordé|Corydn]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> In [[28 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' to [[28 BBY]].</ref> Cordyn began working as a [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] to [[Galactic Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and took on the name Cordé. When Amidala and her [[handmaiden]]s visited their [[homeworld]]&mdash;the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Naboo]]&mdash;for a holiday before the second session of the [[Galactic Senate]], Cordé had her sister design and make pins that could be used within the [[hair]]styles for the senator that could double as [[weapon]]s or tools, yet be small enough to pass most security scans. As she packed before leaving Naboo to return to the Senate, Amidala mentally noted about the pins Cordé's sister had made for her.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cordé's sister was mentioned in ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'', a [[2019]] young-adult novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]] as the first installment in the ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
