The '''Aristocra''' was an organization within the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s legislative and executive body, the [[Chiss Syndicure]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> Its members formed part of the [[Noble|ruling class]] of the [[Chiss]] [[species]] in the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Thrawn" /> The Aristocra was a part of the Syndicure which included representation from the Forty [[Great Families]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

The term ''[[Aristocra]]'' was also a title and rank among Chiss families. While the rank of Aristocra specifically referred to midlevel officials of a [[Chiss family]], the title Aristocra was also used less formally to refer to any official within the Syndicure of rank Aristocra proper or higher.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
