"'''For Every Action: Counterstrike'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in year five of the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was the second adventure in the [[For Every Action trilogy]] and was written by [[Rob Edwards]] and edited by [[Ronald A. Heintz|Ron Heintz]] and [[Margaret van Poelgeest-Heintz|Margaret Heintz]].

==Plot summary==
The heroes thwart the efforts of [[Riboga]] the [[Hutt/Legends|Hutt]] by destroying his new [[Starship/Legends|ship]], the ''[[Rightful Dominion]]'', and his [[Spice/Legends|spice]] operation on [[Kemix/Legends|Kemix]].
