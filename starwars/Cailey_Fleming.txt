'''Cailey Fleming''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Actor|actress]] who played young [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']]. Her scene was filmed at the headquarters of [[Bad Robot Productions]] in [[Wikipedia:Burbank|Burbank]], [[Wikipedia:California|California]].<ref name="Fleming Interview" /> She also portrayed [[W:c:walkingdead:Judith Grimes (TV Series)|Judith Grimes]] in ''[[Wikipedia:The Walking Dead (TV series)|The Walking Dead]]'' and young [[W:c:mcu:Sylvie Laufeydottir|Sylvie Laufeydottir]] in the ''[[Wikipedia:Loki (TV series)|Loki]]'' episode "[[Wikipedia:The Nexus Event|The Nexus Event]]."
