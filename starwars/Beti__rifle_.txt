'''Beti''' was a modified snub-[[barrel]]led [[blaster rifle]] owned by [[Sylvestri Yarrow]]. A gift from her [[Parent|mother]] [[Chancey Yarrow|Chancey]], Sylvestri named the rifle after her [[Beti|childhood doll]] as a joke.<ref name="OotS" /> The blaster rifle resembled<ref name="Art of High Republic Phase 1">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The High Republic: Volume I]]''</ref> the [[E-5 blaster rifle]] later used by the [[Trade Federation]]<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] in the late [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

Beti the blaster rifle first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''.<ref name="OotS" /> The rifle's name was spelled "Betty" in an excerpt of the novel published with ''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''.<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref>
