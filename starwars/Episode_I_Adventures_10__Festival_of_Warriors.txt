'''''Episode I Adventures 10: Festival of Warriors''''' was the tenth installment of ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Adventures]]''.

{{Book-stub}}

==Publisher's summary==
''The [[Festival of Warriors]] is the most challenging athletic competition in [[Otoh Gunga/Legends|Otoh Gunga]]. Hundreds of Gungans, including Boss [[Rugor Nass/Legends|Nass]] and [[Jar Jar Binks/Legends|Jar Jar Binks]], participate in its main event, the [[Big Nasty Free-For-All]]. This year's Free-For-All is the nastiest yet, because of the unexpected arrivals of some very unfriendly droids.''

''Who will win the race&hellip;and save the Gungans?''
