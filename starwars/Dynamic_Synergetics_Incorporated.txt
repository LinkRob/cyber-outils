'''Dynamic Synergetics Incorporated''',<ref name="Episode Guide" /> also known by the abbreviated name '''Dyn Synerge''',<ref name="An Old Friend" /> was a subsidiary to the highly important [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]] commerce guild.<ref name="InterGalactic Banking Clan">{{Databank|intergalactic-banking-clan|InterGalactic Banking Clan}}</ref> By [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[mission to Scipio]], during which the Dyn Synerge's bankruptcy was discovered, to [[19 BBY]].</ref> the conclusive [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]], a state of embezzlement within the Banking Clan had rendered Dyn Synerge bankrupt alongside several of the company's other subsidiaries.<ref name="An Old Friend" />

The bankruptcy was later discovered by Republic [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and Banking Clan member [[Rush Clovis]] during their [[Mission to Scipio|mission]] to<ref name="An Old Friend" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="HC 11">{{HelmetCollectionCite|11|Databank A-Z}}</ref> [[Scipio]]<ref name="An Old Friend" /> in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="HC 11" /> to uncover the commerce guild's suspected embezzlement. Upon their discovery, the Republic senator understood that the Banking Clan's state had to be immediately revealed to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], which had been a result of the embezzlement.<ref name="An Old Friend" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dynamic Synergetics Incorporated was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a [[datascreen]] in [[Aurebesh]] in "[[An Old Friend]],"<ref name="An Old Friend" /> the fifth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series' [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]],<ref name="Episode Guide" /> which aired on [[March 7]], [[2014]].<ref name="TCW on Netflix">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-the-lost-missions-now-on-netflix|text=Star Wars: The Clone Wars &ndash; The Lost Missions Now on Netflix|nolive=1}}</ref> In the episode, the subsidiary was identified as "Dyn Synerge" on the datascreen.<ref name="An Old Friend" /> It was later given its full name in the corresponding episode guide on [[StarWars.com]].<ref name="Episode Guide" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Dynamic Synergetics, Inc.]] was introduced in ''[[Planet of the Mists]]'', a [[1992]] adventure supplement authored by [[Nigel D. Findley]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' and published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="Planet">''[[Planet of the Mists]]''</ref>
