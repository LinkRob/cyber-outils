"'''Bright-Eyes'''" was a [[dragonsnake]] who lived on [[Dagobah]]. She was an inquisitive creature and often observed [[Yoda]], noting how peaceful and unafraid he was. At one point she witnessed [[Unidentified dragonsnake|another dragonsnake]] attempt to eat the [[droid]] [[R2-D2]]. Following Yoda's [[death]], Bright-Eyes sensed a rise in the [[dark side of the Force]] emanating from the [[Cave of Evil]].<ref name="FACPOV" />
