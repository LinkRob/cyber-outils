"'''Conflict and Mastery'''" is an article written by [[August Hahn]] and [[Cynthia Hahn]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[March 11]], [[2004]] and form of a meeting of the [[Almas Academy]] leadership with [[Darrus Jeht/Legends|Darrus Jeht]] who delivered orders from the [[Jedi Council/Legends|Jedi Council]] moving all non-essential staff of the rank of [[Jedi Knight/Legends|Jedi Knight]] into active duty in the [[Clone Wars/Legends|Clone Wars]].
