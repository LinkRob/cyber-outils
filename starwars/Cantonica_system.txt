The '''Cantonica system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="TLJ Junior novel" /> located in the [[Corporate Sector]] of [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]].<ref name="TLJ Junior novel" />

==Behind the scenes==
According to the map in ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', Cantonica is located in an area that [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] identifies as the grid square R-5,<ref name="Coordinates">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'' places Cantonica in what [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] identifies as grid square R-5 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].</ref> however the grid square is far from the Corporate Sector's position on the map.<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> Jason Fry has stated to "disregard that placement".<ref name="JCF2023-06-12">{{JCFCite|url=posts/56701760|board=Literature|thread=The Essential Atlas and Galactic Cartography: Official Discussion|user=[[Jason Fry|jasonfry]]|date=2020-06-12|time=9:38 AM|quote=Yeah, looks like they "made Cantonica fit." I'd disregard that placement.|archivedate=20230822012518}}</ref> [[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] places Cantonica in the Corporate Sector at grid square S-4.<ref name="SWGalaxyMap" />
