"'''Black Spire Outpost'''" is the fifty-second episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com|''Star Wars Kids'']] YouTube channel on [[August 27]], [[2020]].

==Plot summary==
The narrator announces that at ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|Galaxy's Edge]]'', [[Black Spire Outpost]] is full of danger, mystery, and wonder. The episode opens with a wide landscape shot of [[Black Spire Outpost]]'s [[Spaceport (Batuu)|spaceport]]. [[Chewbacca]] exits the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' with a [[porg]] on top of a box.

The porg flies away and explores the town, visiting [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]], where it avoids being trampled by the patrons. It flies to the enclosed encounter and eyes [[Dok-Ondar]], who waves him away. Meanwhile, the DJ [[droid]] [[R-3X]] plays music at [[Oga's Cantina]]. The porg briefly dances on his keyboard before mingling under the drink dispensers. The porg then flies through the streets of [[Merchant Row (Batuu)|Merchant Row]], hiding behind a pillar while two patrolling [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] march past. It sees the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] hiding in a corner but she motions for the porg not to expose her position.

The porg visits [[Ronto Roasters]] in the [[Black Spire Outpost Market|Market]] where the [[8D-series smelter droid]] [[8D-J8]] is roasting some meat on grills. The porg tries to taste some meat but is picked up by a disapproving Chewbacca. A [[Kowakian monkey-lizard]] laughs maniacally in the background.
