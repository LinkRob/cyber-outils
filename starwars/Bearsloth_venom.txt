'''Bearsloth venom''' was the virulent [[Poison|venom]] of the [[bearsloth]], an arboreal [[Diet|predator]] found on the celestial body [[Arbooine]]. The venom [[Death|killed]] the bearsloths' prey slowly, sapping their health the harder they exerted themselves. This meant that those who fled after being bitten by a bearsloth often inadvertently caused their own demise by hastening the venom's effect.<ref name="A&A" />

The predators were known best for their venomous bite, even moreso than their ferocity, and local [[Sathari]] [[Hunting|hunters]] on Arbooine used the venom to [[poison]] their bladed [[weapon]]s, known as [[diiro]]s, as the venom retained its potency and lethality well after a bearsloth's death. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], this made it a hot commodity with [[arms dealer]]s<ref name="A&A" /> and the [[glitterstim]] [[Spice dealer|dealer]] [[Savas|Savas the Dreaded]] sold some of the venom aquired on Arbooine  to the [[Hutt]] [[Mahon]].<ref name="ND">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref>

In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], bearsloth venom was first mentioned in the [[2017]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] sourcebook [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], which was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: Edge of the Empire]]'' system.<ref name="ND" /> The [[Bearsloth venom/Legends|venom]] originated in the [[2015]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]'', which was also released by Fantasy Flight Games but as apart of the ''[[Star Wars: Force and Destiny]]'' system.<ref name="CotG">''[[Chronicles of the Gatekeeper]]''</ref>
