"'''Brian Blessed: A Blessed Event'''" is an interview with [[Brian Blessed]], the actor who portrayed Boss [[Rugor Nass|Nass]], that was written by [[Scott Chernoff]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 43|''Star Wars Insider'' 43]]. It was republished in [[Star Wars: The Official Magazine 20|''Star Wars: The Official Magazine'' 20]], an issue of the UK version of ''Insider'', under the title "Boss Man."
