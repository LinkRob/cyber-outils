Between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] was involved in a '''battle''' on<ref name="Pilots">{{EncyclopediaCite|Pilots of the Rebel Alliance}}</ref> the [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> [[Bakura]] during the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]].
