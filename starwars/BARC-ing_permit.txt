A '''BARC-ing permit''' was a permit issued to [[Biker Advanced Recon Commando]]s. It made sure that their [[BARC speeder]]s were not towed, ticketed, or destroyed by a [[proton torpedo]]. One of these was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles">''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The BARC-ing permit was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
