'''Emma Powell''' is [[Wikipedia:London|London]]-based female voice actress who specializes in voice-overs for trailers, commercials, corporate training programs, and audio books, though she also sometimes branches out into video games projects and documentaries. Powell has lent her voice for [[2017]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]'' augmented/virtual reality experience and its subsequent expansions, serving as one of two voices for the game's exclusive character, [[The Archivist (Jedi Order)|The Archivist]], portraying their faux "[[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] vision persona,<ref name="Clients" /> while [[Julia Norton]] voiced the character for the rest of game.<ref name="Website">{{WebCite|url=https://julianorton.com|text=Julia Norton - British Voice Actor & Singer in California|work=[https://julianorton.com/ Julia Norton Voice]|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/GkVgX}}</ref> Powell also voiced the [[protocol droid]] [[SD-89]] in the themed voice "[[Wikipedia:Amazon Alexa|Amazon Alexa]] Skill" [[Star Wars video games|game]] extension, ''[[Star Wars: C-3PO Translates!]]'',<ref name="Amazon">{{Amazon|url=Disney-Star-Wars-C-3PO-Translates/dp/B08QQCZ1DX|text=Star Wars: C-3PO Translates!}}</ref> which launched on [[February 3]], [[2021]].<ref name="Announcement">{{Twitter|DisneyParks|status/1356967832504909826|[[The Walt Disney Company]]|quote=Turn up the volume with two brand new voice skills, Frozen Sing! and Star Wars: C-3PO Translates!, available exclusively on Amazon Kids+ on compatible Echo devices. Kids can put their karaoke skills to the test or chat with Chewbacca!|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/d9QdK}}</ref>
