Between around [[50 BBY]] and [[48 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''ambush''' occurred on the [[planet]] [[Raxus Secundus]], which led to the [[death]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Katri]] at the hands of the [[Raxus guard]]. Following the event, the [[Jedi High Council]] sent Jedi Masters [[Mace Windu]] and [[Dooku]] on a [[Incident on Raxus Secundus|mission]] to recover Katri's body.<ref name="Choices" />
