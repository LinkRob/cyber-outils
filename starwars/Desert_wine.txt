The '''desert wine''' was a sacred [[alcohol]]ic beverage produced by the [[Tusken Raider]]s of [[Tatooine]], who distilled it during their night prayers at high moon. Although the Tuskens refused to sell their sacred wine, they did offer some to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]]. The Hutt, having discovered that the beverage had an exceptional taste, started selling it in small, hard to come by bottles under the name '''Tusken Wind'''. At its peak, the price for a single measure rose to 12,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. Soon thereafter, however, a cartel on [[Coruscant]] figured out how to synthesize the desert wine, making it commercially worthless.<ref name="AoR Jabba" />
