'''Ashla''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Togruta]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] who trained in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] in the waning [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Jedi Order]].

==Biography==
Ashla was a [[Jedi]] [[Jedi Initiate|youngling]] during the waning [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. Along with many of her fellow younglings, she received [[Jedi training|instruction]] from [[Jedi Master]] [[Yoda]]. During a session on [[lightsaber]] instruction, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] visited Ashla's class to talk with Yoda about the [[planet]] [[Kamino]], a planet he was investigating that was missing from the [[Jedi Archives]]. One of Ashla's peers, [[J. K. Burtola]], correctly guessed that someone had erased the files, explaining Kamino's absence. Kenobi later [[Mission to Kamino (Separatist Crisis)|tracked]] Kamino down and found that a [[Clone trooper|clone army]] had been created for the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Episode II" /> which the Jedi ultimately led during the ensuing [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref> Ashla shared her name with an ancient term for [[the Force]],<ref name="ForceCollection" /> and sometimes specifically the [[light side of the Force]].<ref name="Steps Into Shadow">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ashla appeared in the [[2002]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" /> Her name was first given August 2004 in [[The Official Star Wars Fact File 138|''The Official Star Wars Fact File'' 138]],<ref name="Fact File">[[The Official Star Wars Fact File 138|''The Official Star Wars Fact File'' 138]]</ref> and it was later canonized by September 2015 in the official [[StarWars.com]] Databank.<ref name="Togruta Databank">{{Databank|togruta|Togruta}}</ref>

[[Leland Chee]] of [[Lucasfilm]], addressing fan speculation, confirmed in [[February]] [[2008]] that Ashla and [[Ahsoka Tano]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' were not the same character because their ages did not match up.<ref name="SWBoards">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=1755#17143245|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2008-02-18|time=1:47 PM}}</ref> Dave Filoni revealed they had considered making the character Ahsoka, but decided otherwise because it would have made her too young.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2020/04/ahsoka-tano-clone-wars-finale-star-wars|text=Ahsoka Tano—A ''Star Wars'' Oral History|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2020-04-24|work=Vanity Fair|archivedate=20200527044226}}</ref>
