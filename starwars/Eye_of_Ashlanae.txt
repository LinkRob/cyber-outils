The '''Eye of Ashlanae''' was, according to the [[Qel-Droma Epics]], a powerful [[The Force|Force]] [[vergence]] on one of the peaks of the [[Eocho Mountains]], that was a place of pilgrimage for the [[Jedi]]. Both the [[First Temple|Jedi Praxeum]] and [[Knossa Spaceport]] were originally built to support the pilgrims visiting the vergence.<ref name="NoP" />
