'''Ashley Park''' is an actress and singer who voiced [[Ara]] in the English dub of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' short film, "[[Journey to the Dark Head]]."<ref name="JDH" /> Park is best known for her award-nominated [[Wikipedia:Broadway theatre|Broadway]] performances in [[Wikipedia:The King and I|''The King and I'']] and the stage musical adaptation of [[Wikipedia:Mean Girls (musical)|''Mean Girls'']], as well as her roles on the television shows [[Wikipedia:Emily in Paris|''Emily in Paris'']] and [[Wikipedia:Girls5eva|''Girls5eva'']].<ref name="Cosmo" />
