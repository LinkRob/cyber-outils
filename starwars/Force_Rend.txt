'''Force Rend''' was a [[Force power|Force ability]] that enabled a [[Force-sensitive|Force user]] to [[Telekinesis|telekinetically]] lift and violently contort another individual. At one point within a hallway, an individual in the garb of [[Alderaanian consular security]]<ref name="Legacies">{{Destiny|set=Legacies|cardname=Force Rend|link=https://swdestinydb.com/card/05007}}</ref>&mdash;marking them as either a member of that security force or a [[rebel trooper]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]<ref name="HC 20">{{HelmetCollectionCite|20|Helmets}}</ref>&mdash;was attacked with Force Rend, throwing them into the air and making them drop their [[DH-17 blaster pistol]].<ref name="Legacies" />
