Van captured a [[hunanetre]] on the wastes of [[Rentalles]] and brought the [[Species|creature]] to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] on [[Tatooine]] so that Crakka could give it to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]] as a gift.<ref name="RTVC 4" />
