'''Eeth Koth's lightsaber''' was the [[lightsaber]] belonging to [[Zabrak]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Eeth Koth]] during the final [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]. It produced a [[Color|green]]-blade when ignited. During the [[Clone Wars]], Eeth Koth used his lightsaber when he [[Capture of Eeth Koth|fought]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]. Koth was briefly relieved of his [[weapon]] during his brief tenure as Grievous' [[prisoner]]<ref name="Lightsabers" /> but was able to recover it after he was [[Battle of Saleucami|freed]].<ref name="Grievous Intrigue" />

Koth was later dismissed from the [[Jedi High Council]] and renounced his [[Jedi]] ties. Koth kept his lightsaber and used it when [[Darth]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Vader]] and members of the [[Inquisitorius]] [[Hunt for Eeth Koth|tracked]] the [[Force-Sensitive Outcast|former Jedi]] down. During the duel, Koth was distracted when he saw the [[Inquisitor]]s had captured his [[Eeth Koth's daughter|newborn daughter]] and was stabbed from behind by [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|Vader's red-blade]], causing his lightsaber to fall from his limp hand onto the ground.<ref name="Dark Lord 19">[[Darth Vader (2017) 19|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 19]]</ref>
