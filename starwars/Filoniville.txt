'''Filoniville''' was a destination on the [[subtram]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld]]. It was listed on travel maps inside the subtram [[train]]s, along with [[Sunset]], [[Valley Village]], [[North Hollywood]], [[Beverly Blvd]], [[Burbank]], [[Lucasville]], [[LAAC]], [[Laurel Canyon]], [[Coach House]], [[Skinwalker]], [[RGC]], [[Letterman]], [[Skywalker Ranch (Coruscant)|Skywalker Ranch]], [[Big Rock Ranch]] and [[Skinwalker Ranch]].<ref name="To Catch a Jedi trivia gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/to-catch-a-jedi-trivia-gallery|text="To Catch a Jedi" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Filoniville was inserted onto a [[subtram]] map as an in-joke by the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' production team along with the other destinations on the map,<ref name="To Catch a Jedi trivia gallery" /> referencing series showrunner [[Dave Filoni]].
