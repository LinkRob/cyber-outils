'''Cerea''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="Cerean" /> located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It was a paradise<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> largely unspoiled by the ravages of technology.<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Cerean]]s,<ref name="Cerean" /> including the [[Jedi Master]] [[Ki-Adi-Mundi]], who served as a member of the [[Jedi High Council]] during the [[Republic Era|waning]] [[Standard day|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="SWE">{{SWE|characters|kiadimundi|Ki-Adi-Mundi}}</ref> Cerea was home to big bipedal [[bird]]s known as [[aryx]], which the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] communications specialist [[Kidi Aleri]] rode.<ref name="Moving Target" />

==History==
Prior to being arrested on the [[Five Points system]], [[Jyn Erso]] spent [[time]] bouncing around the Mid Rim on planets such as Cerea and [[Coyerti]] before settling on [[Takodana]] for almost a [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="Rebel Rising">''[[Rebel Rising]]''</ref> 

During the [[Cold War]] and the subsequent [[First Order-Resistance War]], the [[Resistance]] ran what they called a "peaceful and nature-led deprogramming protocol" in the "beautiful and ancient forests" of Cerea, meant to rehabilitate defectors and prisoners from the [[First Order]] and counter the FO's ideological "brainwashing". Participants practiced daily [[meditation]], stretching, and dance; ate an entirely plant-based diet; and abstained from most uses of technology. One such participant was First Order defector [[Archex]], formerly Captain Cardinal.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE:BS date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] picked up Archex from Asphodar 3 to accompany her on a [[Battle of Batuu|mission to establish a Resistance base]] on the planet [[Batuu]] on the edge of [[Wild Space]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|A beautiful world, largely unspoiled by the ravages of technology. It is home to the Cerean people, whose towering cone-shaped skulls contain a binary brain.|The ''[[Galactic Atlas]]''|<ref name="Galactic Atlas" />}}
The native Cereans were very protective of their homeworld's ecology, and thus only allowed off-world visitors and immigrants to enter and reside city-sized structures called [[Outsider Citadel]]s. One such structure was [[Asphodar 3]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />
