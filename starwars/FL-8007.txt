'''FL-8007''' was an [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> who served as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] for the [[First Order]]'s [[Company 77]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. On the ''[[Supremacy]]'', FL-8007 talked to [[Jannah|TZ-1719]] about what a recent morale report said about her [[Mission to Locke Station| recent heroic mission]]. Later, during the [[Battle of Ansett Island]], Knives dropped his weapon, refusing to kill civilians and defecting with the rest of Company 77.<ref name="TLO" />
