The '''Factory Arms Cantina and Gambling House''' was a [[cantina]] and [[gambling]] parlor located on the edge of the [[Kristom's manufacturing district|manufacturing section]] of the [[Kristom]] urban district on the [[planet]] [[Giju]]. The building included a bar, [[fathier table]], a number of booths, a [[refresher]] and a side hallway that included a [[Raven 4]] [[arcade machine]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Low Red Moon date">{{LRMDate|3}}</ref> the [[Trandoshan]] [[contract broker]] [[Kranash]] meet a [[Unidentified human male (Kranash)|contact]] at a booth in the cantina. Before the meeting, the upcoming criminal [[Jaylen Vrax]] intercepted a communique about the meeting and traveled to the cantina to establish contact with Kranash. Vrax listened in to the Trandoshan's conversation with his contact from the fathier table while both were in a nearby booth. Part of the conversation consisted of the pair complaining about the low quality of drinks served at the cantina.<ref name="Low Red Moon" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Factory Arms Cantina and Gambling House appeared in the [[2026]] novel ''[[Star Wars Outlaws: Low Red Moon]]'', written by [[Mike Chen (author)|Mike Chen]].<ref name="Low Red Moon" />
