'''Clatriffe''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Togruta]] [[archaeologist]] who led an [[Dallenor Excavation|excavation]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dallenor]]. She discovered a [[Jedi holocron]], and the [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] sent [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]] to verify and retrieve it.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Let's hope we didn't all waste our time here&hellip;and that this little toy of yours still works.|Clatriffe, to Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="Age of Republic" />}}
An [[archaeologist]], Clatriffe led the [[Dallenor Excavation]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] [[Dallenor]], searching for ancient artifacts. The first objects to be discovered included broken [[Standard century|century]]-old [[droid]] components, but nothing of any interest to non-academic individuals. During the early stages of the excavation, local [[pirate]]s, including the [[Krypder Riders]], caused difficulty for Clatriffe and her team, but stopped upon realizing that none of the artifacts being uncovered were worth any value to them.<ref name="Age of Republic" />

[[File:KenobiTakesHolocron-AORObiWan1.jpg|thumb|left|210px|Kenobi takes the holocron from Clatriffe as the pirates attack.]]
Eventually, Clatriffe's team uncovered what they thought was a [[Jedi holocron]]. The pirates heard of the news, resuming their attacks on the excavation in an attempt to steal it. Clatriffe was forced to hire some of the planet's natives to guard the [[holocron]], which was hidden among the other less valuable finds. The [[Jedi High Council|Jedi Council]] were alerted, and [[Mission to Dallenor|sent]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] to see for themselves. Clatriffe met the two [[Jedi]] when they arrived, giving the holocron to Kenobi. Before the Jedi could leave, the Krypder Riders, led by [[Hudso Shaku]], blocked their path, demanding that the holocron be handed over to them. Kenobi defended himself by holding up [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's second lightsaber|his lightsaber]], and Shaku decided to steal the weapon instead. The Jedi managed to disarm and incapacitate the Krypder Riders, leaving Dallenor with the holocron.<ref name="Age of Republic" />
