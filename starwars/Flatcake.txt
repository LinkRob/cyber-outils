'''Flatcakes''' were a type of flat, round breakfast [[pancake]] favored by multiple [[species]] in [[the galaxy]], including [[human]]s. Shortly before the [[Clone Wars]], passengers traveling aboard an [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]] between the [[planet]]s [[Coruscant]] and [[Naboo]] were served flatcakes. [[Standard year|Years]] later, during the [[Cold War]], a variety known as [[Trandoshani flatcake]]s was served in the [[Canto Bight]] [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|casino]] on the planet [[Cantonica]]. The [[Suerton]] [[Wodibin]], who was particularly fond of the food, served himself a plate of flatcakes and sautéed [[fruit]] at the [[casino]].

==Description==
{{Quote|I said I wanted flatcakes, the ''food''!|Kaljach Sonmi|audio=Sonmi-Flatcakes.flac|<ref name="Ride" />}}
Flatcakes were a type of flat, round, and [[Color|brown]]<ref name="AOTC" /> [[pancake]]<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> that could be prepared using a [[flatcake turner]]<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref> and served as a breakfast [[Foodstuff|food]]. Flatcakes could be eaten by [[human]]s, [[Twi'lek]]s,<ref name="AND" /> [[Suerton]]s, and [[Heptooinian]]s.<ref name="Ride" />

==History==
===Starship snack===
{{Quote|Cozy back here. We could send out for flatcakes.|Kanan Jarrus|audio=Kanan-Flatcakes.flac|<ref name="AND" />}}
[[File:COO-2180_serving_flatcakes.png|thumb|left|200px|Flatcakes served aboard a Coruscant freighter prior to the Clone Wars]]
Shortly before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="AOTC" /> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a [[COO-series cook droid]] stationed in a cafeteria aboard an [[AA-9 Freighter-Liner]] traveling from the [[planet]]s [[Coruscant]] to [[Naboo]] prepared flatcakes for the [[Starship|ship's]] passengers. While carrying four platters of flatcakes and flipping one of them, the [[droid]] shooed away the [[R2-series astromech droid|astromech droid]] [[R2-D2]] from the ship's food counter.<ref name="AOTC" />

In [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Visual Guide Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']] to [[11 BBY]].</ref> the human [[Jedi]]-in-hiding [[Kanan Jarrus]] joked that he and the Twi'lek [[pilot]] [[Hera Syndulla]] could order flatcakes while they were cramped together in the cargo hold of the [[transport]] [[Moonglow-72]] during a [[Gorse Conflict|conflict]] on the planet [[Gorse]].<ref name="AND" />

===Time for Flatcakes===
{{Quote|It's always time for flatcakes.|Wodibin|audio=Wodi-Flatcakes1.flac|<ref name="Ride" />}}
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date_note">{{SW|url=news/4-reasons-canto-bight-is-an-essential-read|text=4 Reasons ''Canto Bight'' Is an Essential Read}} states that the [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] anthology takes place [[Standard day|days]] before [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']], which corresponds to the timeline of events for the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], as ''The Last Jedi'' begins immediately in the latter's aftermath. Given that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of ''The Force Awakens'' to [[34 ABY]], the events of ''Canto Bight'' must also take place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> a racing [[fathier]] kept at the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack|Canto Bight racetrack]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] was named "[[Time for Flatcakes]]." When the trio of Suerton [[Gambling|gamblers]] known as the [[Lucky Three]] and their Heptooinian friend [[Kaljach Sonmi]] arrived at the track looking to bet, they learned the fathier's name from the Canto Casino employee [[Joris (Heptooinian)|Joris]].<ref name="Ride" />

[[File:Wodi.png|thumb|right|200px|The Suerton Wodibin enjoyed flatcakes.]]
This prompted the Suerton [[Wodibin]] to claim it truly was always [[time]] for flatcakes, and a hungry Sonmi absentmindedly mumbled that he would like some flatcakes. The Suertons misunderstood Sonmi's meaning and purchased "Time for Flatcakes" for him, causing the trio and Sonmi to temporarily fall out. Wodibin, who had previously unsuccessfully tried to buy flatcakes during the late [[Standard hour|hours]] of the [[Standard day|night]] in [[Canto Bight]], later found a buffet at the Canto Casino serving some of what Sonmi considered the most delicious [[Trandoshani flatcake]]s in [[the galaxy]] and served himself a plate of flatcakes and sautéed [[fruit]] to eat.<ref name="Ride" />

Before the Seurton could eat them all, he collided with the [[bartender]] [[Ganzer]], causing the plate of food and a tray of drinks that Ganzer was carrying to spill all over the professional gambler [[Orisha Okum]], who was [[Gambling|playing]] a game of [[pazaak]] with Sonmi. The spill revealed an alterable pazaak card that Okum was using to cheat in the game, but Sonmi allowed her to play it anyway. The card then malfunctioned because of the spilled food and drink, causing Okum to lose the game.<ref name="Ride" />

==Behind the scenes==
Flatcakes first appeared in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="AOTC" /> and were first named in the [[2007]] [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel [[Allegiance (novel)|''Allegiance'']], written by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Allegiance">[[Allegiance (novel)|''Allegiance'']]</ref> The name was then brought into the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] by the [[2014]] novel [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], which was written by [[John Jackson Miller]].<ref name="AND" />
