'''Core Worlds Classical Culture''', simply known as '''Core Worlds Culture''', was a course for [[Imperial cadet|cadets]] at the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Royal Imperial Academy]] on [[Coruscant]]. One of the subjects discussed in the course was ''[[Chalice and Altar]]'', the best-known work of the [[opera]] composer [[Igern]]. During their time at the Royal Academy, both [[Ciena Ree]] and [[Thane Kyrell]] attended that course. At one point, Ciena resolved to listen to the ''Chalice and Altar'' opera every night to prepare for the upcoming exam for the class.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>
