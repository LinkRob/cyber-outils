'''Bantha sludge stew''' was a type of [[stew]] found on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="JTC 3" /> It shared its name with the large [[bantha]] [[Species|creatures]] also [[Homeworld|native]] to the world.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>

At some point, the [[Yuzzum]] [[Pako (Yuzzum)|Pako]] played a prank on the [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Yates]] by filling her [[oil]] can with bantha sludge stew.<ref name="JTC 3" />
