'''Dalgos''' were a creature native to the dense [[jungle]]s of the [[planet]] [[Onderon]]. Unlike their smaller [[Diet|herbivore]] cousins, they were carnivorous predators. Despite this, they could still be domesticated, filling many of the same roles.<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

Along with their cousins, they were also found on the [[desert]] [[moon]] [[Zardossa Stix]].<ref name="The Disappeared, Part II">{{TCW|The Disappeared, Part II}}</ref>

[[Onderon rebels]] used dalgos as beasts of burden and battle mounts during the [[Onderonian Civil War]].<ref name="AWOTF" />
