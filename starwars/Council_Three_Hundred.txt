'''Council Three Hundred''' was a [[farm]]ing [[city|settlement]] on the agricultural world [[Mina-Rau]]. While discussing the movements of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] conducting an [[Imperial Supply Census]]<ref name="Sagrona Teema" /> in [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Sagrona Teema Date">{{AndorDate|202}}</ref> [[Kellen]] informed [[Bix Caleen]], [[Talia (Mina-Rau)|Talia]], [[Brasso]] and [[Wilmon Paak]] over a meal that it looked like the [[Krole's contingent|contingent]] was headed to Three Hundred next.<ref name="Sagrona Teema" />
