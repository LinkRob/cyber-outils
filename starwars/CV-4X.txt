'''CV-4X''' was a [[droid]] that was scheduled to depart on a [[shuttle]] from [[Hauler's Keep]] in [[Wild Space]] to [[Dennogra]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> A final boarding call was broadcast over the [[space station]]'s [[speaker]] system notifying CV-4X and another passenger named [[Magret Windtester]] of the shuttle's imminent departure.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
CV-4X was first mentioned in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> CV-4X is mentioned in one of several ambient announcements that players can overhear while on Hauler's Keep.<ref name="Outlaws" />
