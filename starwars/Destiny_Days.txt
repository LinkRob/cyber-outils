"'''Destiny Days'''" was a work written by [[Skreen]], the technical director of the [[Manifold Mollusk Matrix and Matrimony Association]]. It was a fictional account based on true events that had transpired on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Lusaanda]] after the [[Author/Legends|author]] himself had [[Death/Legends|died]]. Skreen planned on writing [[Destiny Days sequels|three sequels]] to the work for the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] following his attendance of a [[Unidentified seminar|seminar]].<ref name="MAGL" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Destiny Days" was mentioned in an entry for Skreen in a segment named "The Seminar Participants," written by [[Bob Carrau]] for the [[1993]] book ''[[Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas]]'', a publication that collects concept designs and photographs from various [[George Lucas]] projects.<ref name="MAGL" /> [[Lucasfilm]] employee [[Leland Chee]], who maintains the [[Holocron continuity database]], has indicated that information contained in the book is [[Canon|non-canonical]] with respect to the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="Chee">{{SWBoards|url=thread.jspa?threadID=152583&start=690#14724917|thread=Holocron continuity database questions|user=[[Leland Chee|Leland Y Chee]]|date=2006-05-03|time=5:45 PM|quote=And is information from Monsters and Aliens from George Lucas canon? Unless referred to by a source that is in-continuity, the information is non-continuity.}}</ref>
