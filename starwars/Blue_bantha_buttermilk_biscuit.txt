'''Blue bantha buttermilk biscuits''' were a type of [[biscuit]] made with [[Blue milk|buttermilk]] from [[bantha]]s. When the [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] was in possession of the [[light freighter]] ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', he found that the [[starship]]'s refrigerator unit contained blue bantha buttermilk biscuits, [[blue Bespin breakfast bar]]s, and [[Blue Puff Cube|blue puff cubes]], which led him to wonder if all the edible items were [[Color|blue]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blue bantha buttermilk biscuits first appeared in the [[canon]] junior novel ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', which was written by [[Lou Anders]] and published in [[2019]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" />
