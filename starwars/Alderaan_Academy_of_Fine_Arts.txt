The '''Alderaan Academy of Fine Arts''' was an institution on [[Alderaan]] where the [[Artist|painter]] and [[sculptor]] [[Dashira Dobeq]] of [[Ryloth]] exhibited her art.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Alderaan Academy of Fine Arts was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Propaganda" />
