The '''Celadon Sea''' was a [[poison]]ous mist that covered the [[Water|icy]] [[mountain]] ranges on the [[planet]] [[Carnelion IV]]. [[Grecker]], a member of the [[Closed]], blamed the [[Open]] for destroying their world and creating the Celadon Sea.<ref name="Part II" /> In [[29 BBY]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], crash-landed in the Celadon Sea while investigating a [[distress signal]]. Located beneath it was "[[The World That Was]],"<ref name="Part I" /> the remains of an advanced civilization destroyed in a war between the Open and the Closed. Except for individuals with [[breath mask]]s, the Celadon Sea transformed the planet's [[human]] population into beasts known as [[Fisher (Carnelion IV)|Fishers]], who lived in the [[city]]'s ruins, forcing the survivors to take refuge above the [[Ocean|Sea]] in the freezing [[mountain]]s.<ref name="Part IV">[[Obi-Wan and Anakin 4|''Obi-Wan and Anakin'' 4]]</ref>
