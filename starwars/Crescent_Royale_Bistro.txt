The '''Crescent Royale Bistro''' was a restaurant that<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> offered [[Derrech Wren]]'s nineteen course [[goblosh]] dinner.<ref name="Outlaws" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> an advertisement for the dinner was broadcast over the [[speaker]] system of the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" />
