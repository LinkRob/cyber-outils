The '''Azure sector''' was a [[sector]] located<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]].<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" />

{{Sec-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Azure sector originated from the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, in which [[Anaxes/Legends|Anaxes]] was located in the [[Core Worlds/Legends|Core Worlds]].<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> In the modern [[canon]] continuity, Anaxes was moved to the Outer Rim, and sources such as ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', and ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' repeated the Azure sector placement along with the new Outer Rim placement.<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" /> However, the [[2025]] "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]" appendix instead listed the [[Anaxes system]] under the [[Antemeridian sector]],<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> lining up with Anaxes' new placement on the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> as it falls in the part of space where the [[Antemeridian sector/Legends|Antemeridian sector]] was located in Legends.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
