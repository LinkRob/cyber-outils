The '''Class 5 restraining bolt''' was a type of [[restraining bolt]] that could be affixed to a droid. The device was requested to be attached onto the reprogrammed [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[droid]] [[K-2SO]], as apart of a series of recommended repairs whilst stationed at [[Great Temple|Base One]]. Despite this, [[Captain]] [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] objected and vetoed its implementation.<ref name="TRF" />
