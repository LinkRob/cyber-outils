'''Andava''' was a [[Rodian]] [[contract broker]] who operated out of the [[Achra Station cantina|cantina]] on [[Achra Station]], a [[space station]] in the [[Akiva system]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> they offered contracts from the criminal groups [[Crimson Dawn]], the [[Pyke Syndicate]], and the [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] while sat at a table in the [[cantina]] wearing a [[Color|purple]] [[jumpsuit]], a black [[belt]], black [[glove]]s, black [[boot]]s, and a metal right [[Limb|arm]] [[Bracer/Legends|bracer]]. Andava had green skin, black [[eye]]s, and spoke [[Huttese]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> A [[Unidentified Pantoran engineer|Pantoran engineer]] that worked on Achra Station complained to themselves that they would need to have Andava reset a system on the station in order reset the fried the [[docking clamp]]s in the station's [[Auxiliary Bay]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics}}
A [[thief]] named [[Kay Vess]] overheard the [[engineer]]'s complaints about needed Andava's help. During the same [[Standard year|year]], Vess accepted contracts from Andava.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Andava appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Accepting contracts from Andava are an optional activity which rewards the players with valuables, materials, [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], and/or increasing the player's reputation with the contract's [[syndicate]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
