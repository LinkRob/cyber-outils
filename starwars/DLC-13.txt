'''DLC-13''' was a [[DLC-13 mining droid]] with [[Gender|masculine programming]] who once helped the [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR]] save his friend [[Rowan Freemaker]] from certain doom. After the incident, DLC-13 stayed in contact with Roger, and even sent him cards on [[Life Day]].<ref name="Droidography" />
