The '''A-180 blaster''', also known as the '''A180 pistol''' or simply the '''A-180''', was a [[Reconfigurable blaster|modular blaster]] manufactured by [[BlasTech Industries]]. It could be configured to serve as a [[rifle]], [[pistol]], [[sniper rifle]], and [[Ion cannon|ion launcher]].

==Description==
{{Quote|These things are great. You could turn them into rifles or snipers. Perfect for unexpected situations.|Zaina|<ref name="Hunters" />}}
[[File:A180sniper-BF.png|thumb|250px|left|The A-180 in sniper rifle configuration]]
The A-180 blaster,<ref name="UVG" /> or simply the A-180,<ref name="Hunters" /> was a [[Reconfigurable blaster|modular blaster]] produced by [[BlasTech Industries]]<ref name="UVG" /> that had a highly versatile design. The weapon had multiple configurations<ref name="BF2" /> that made it perfect for unexpected situations.<ref name="Hunters" /> It could be easily reconfigured from a [[blaster pistol]]<ref name="BF2" /> identified as the A180 pistol<ref name="UVG" /> to a [[blaster rifle]], a [[sniper rifle]]/[[longblaster]],<ref name="BF2" /> or a portable [[Ion cannon|ion launcher]] depending on the situation.<ref name="Battlefront Scarif" />

The pistol, at the core of the weapon, featured a crescent-shaped trigger and a cylindrical-shaped upper design. To expand to a blaster rifle, a top-fitting lock was added to hold a separate, larger assembly to the front. The rifle configuration accepted a side-loading magazine, reminiscent of an [[E-11 medium blaster rifle|E-11]]. A barrel extension and extendable [[stock]] could be fitted to complete the sniper configuration.<ref name="BF2" />

==History==
[[File:Jyn_Ersos_Blaster_AtG.png|thumb|250px|right|Jyn Erso's blaster]]
Used during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Jyn Erso's blaster|one]] such pistol was wielded by [[Jyn Erso]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Rogue One" /> [[Zaina]], who had fought as a member of the Rebel Alliance, thought highly of the A-180 and had experience with the weapon. She was able to use two A-180s&mdash;identified as the "Signal A-180" and "Infiltration A-180," respectively&mdash;within the [[Grand Arena]] as part of the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]].<ref name="Hunters" /> By [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|6}}</ref> the A-180 was used by the [[New Republic soldier]]s like [[Lieutenant]] [[Lant Davan]],<ref name="Chapter 6" /> and, decades later, it was used in its sniper configuration by Resistance [[Sniper|forces]].<ref name="BF2" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[Matthew Savage]] took part in the design of the concept art for the blaster and expansions.<ref name="Szostak twitter">{{Twitter|PhilSzostak|status/1330203119406501895|[[Phil Szostak]]|quote="Jyn's blaster from #RogueOne. It didn't make it in the movie but we did design some add on components to turn her pistol into a sniper rifle. The physical prop was beautiful." - concept artist Matthew Savage|archivedate=20220222082533}}</ref>

The core pistol prop was developed from the lower and upper receiver of the German [[Wikipedia:Luger pistol|P-08 Luger]].<ref name="Inverse">{{WebCite|url=https://www.inverse.com/article/25087-star-wars-rogue-one-blasters|text=The Blasters of Rogue One Are Real Life Fire Arms in Sci-Fi Drag|archivedate=20180626011149}}</ref>

A version of the reconfigurable blaster appeared in the [[Star Wars: The Vintage Collection|Vintage-collection]] 3.75" Jyn Erso action figure and the "Sergeant Jyn Erso" [[Star Wars: The Black Series|Black Series]] release (2016).<ref name="Star Wars: The Black Series">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Black Series|pack=Sergent Jyn Erso|link=products/star-wars-the-black-series-rogue-one-sergeant-jyn-erso}}</ref>
