'''Alercite''' was a [[mineral]] found on the [[Agriworld|farming planet]] [[Septra]].<ref name="Wayseeker" /> Around [[160 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{WayseekerDate}}</ref> while aboard the [[VKCE-7100x Starcutter|starship]] ''[[Cantaros]]'', [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] informed [[Jedi Knight]] [[Indara]] that the [[arms dealer]] [[Nilsson Summach]], who the two were [[Investigation into the production of 47 lightsaber nullifiers|searching]] for, had manufacturing facilities on Septra. After Indara wondered why he would have facilities on a [[farm]]ing [[planet]], the [[droid]] [[J-6]] explained Septra was known for having abundant deposits of minerals such as alercite and [[hynaxium]], adding that that information was among the semi-useless knowledge her [[databank]]s were filled with due to being partially programmed by the [[Youngling|child]] genius [[Avon Sunvale]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />

==Behind the scenes==
