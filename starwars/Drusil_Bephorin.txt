'''Drusil Bephorin''' was a brilliant [[Givin]] [[Gender|female]] [[cryptologist]].<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref>

==Biography==
For a time, Bephorin worked for the [[Galactic Empire]], which had captured her family, against her will. She was later rescued by [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Nakari Kelen]] in a mission spearheaded by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and agreed to work for them.<ref name="Heir" />

==Personality and traits==
Like other members of her [[Givin|race]], she enjoyed solving complex mathematical problems, but she was particularly skilled in areas of probability analysis and [[Cryptologist|cryptography]]. Skywalker, in his discussions with her, often found that her keen insights gave him a new perspective on matters. She displayed a great affection for her family and frequently during her time with Skywalker and Kelen urged that every effort be made to verify that the mission to rescue her family had succeeded.<ref name="Heir" />
