'''Daqu''' was a [[human]] [[gambler]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> who, during [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> played [[Kessel Sabacc]] with the [[Rodian]] [[Razafi]] and the human [[Tanhi]] in [[Mirogana]], the capital city of [[Toshara]]. They claimed to have played sabacc with the [[Hutt]] crime lord [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], who according to Daqu played fairly.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daqu first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. They were one of several sabacc players that could be played against by [[Kay Vess]] in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity.<ref name="Outlaws" />
