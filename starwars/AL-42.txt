'''AL-42'''<ref name="Mandalorian Experience">{{GameSpot|url=gallery/the-mandalorian-experience-74-photos-from-the-star-wars-celebration-exhibit/2900-4118|text=The Mandalorian Experience: 74 Photos From The Star Wars Celebration Exhibit|date=2022-05-31}}</ref> was a [[train operator droid]]<ref name="SWECG" /> who operated a [[Tatooine repulsor train|train]] for the [[Pyke Syndicate]].<ref name="Chapter 2" /> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Flashback Chap 2 Date">{{InsiderCite|225|A Certain Point of View}} states that flashback events of "[[Chapter 2: The Tribes of Tatooine]]" took place five [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'' Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], corresponding to [[5 ABY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> the [[droid]] [[Pilot droid|operated]] a [[train]] that made its way across the [[Dune Sea]], killing various [[Tusken Raider]]s and their [[bantha]]s along the way. [[Boba Fett]] and a group of Tuskens [[Raid on a Pyke repulsortrain|attacked]] the train and stopped it during its course, the droid attempted to accelerate the train to loose the intruders but the former [[bounty hunter]] manage to break in and demand him to stop the train, but the droid jumps out the window and managed to escape soon before the train was stopped.<ref name="Chapter 2" />
