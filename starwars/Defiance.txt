The '''''Defiance''''' was an [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] in service to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] by the closing stages of the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. It participated in the decisive [[Battle of Endor]] and other important battles against the Empire.

==Description==
The ''Defiance'' was an [[MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80A ''Home One'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]]<ref name="Galactic Ambitions" /> produced by [[Mon Calamari Shipyards]],<ref name="UltimateSW" /> and as such was equipped with a class one [[hyperdrive]], ten [[ion engine]]s,<ref name="Starfighters" /> and [[deflector shield generator]]s. It was armed with [[turbolaser]]s, [[ion cannon]]s and [[tractor beam projector]]s.<ref name="Databank" />

==History==
{{Quote|Once the shield is down, our cruisers will create a perimeter while the fighters fly into the superstructure and attempt to knock out the main reactor.|Admiral Gial Ackbar, during a briefing before the Battle of Endor|<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>}}
The ''Defiance'' was considered to be a powerful ship in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], and served in many important battles against the [[Galactic Empire]]. In once instance, the ''Defiance'' was attacked by Imperial [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and defended itself, destroying several fighters.<ref name="Galactic Ambitions" /> By [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date note">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Battle of Endor]] to [[4 ABY]]. Because the ''Defiance'' participated in the Battle of Endor, it must have been part of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] by that [[time]].</ref> the ''Defiance'' was part of the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. Along with other MC80 Star Cruisers such as ''[[Home One]]'',<ref name="Episode VI" /> ''[[Nautilian]]'',<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> and ''[[Independence]]'', the ''Defiance'' participated in the climactic [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Starfighters" /> where the Rebel Alliance sought to destroy the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] and defeat [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].<ref name="Episode VI" />

Rebel Alliance [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]]'s strategy for success was for the [[capital ship]]s and Star Cruisers to form a perimeter around the second [[Death Star]] while starfighters moved into the superstructure of the incomplete [[battle station]] and destroy it from the inside. However, the battle was in actuality a trap by Emperor Palpatine to finally destroy the Rebel Alliance, and [[Imperial Navy|Imperial naval forces]] managed to block the rebel ships from escaping as the second Death Star was revealed to be fully operational, destroying several Rebel Alliance capital ships including the ''Nautilian'' and the ''[[Liberty]]''.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Believing that the Rebel Alliance would not have another opportunity, [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] convinced Admiral Ackbar to attack Imperial [[warship]]s head-on, confident that they would have a form of protection against the second Death Star's [[superlaser]], and also believed that rebel forces could inflict more Imperial casualties. By the end of the battle, the second Death Star was destroyed, Emperor Palpatine was [[Death|killed]] by his own [[Sith apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]],<ref name="Episode VI" /> and Imperial forces retreated by order of Imperial [[Vice Admiral]] [[Rae Sloane]].<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
