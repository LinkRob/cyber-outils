'''FN-2003''', nicknamed "'''Slip'''," was a [[First Order]] [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[Standard decade|decades]] following the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He served under [[Finn|FN-2187]] and was considered the weakest link of their group. His ineptitude was symbolically used in a speech by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] to 2187, in which she reinforced that the weakness of individuals contributed to the potential weakness of the First Order as a whole. Despite his failures, Slip continued to serve on the front lines until an [[Attack on Tuanul|assault]] on the [[Sacred Villages|village]] [[Tuanul]] on [[Jakku]], thirty years after the [[Battle of Endor]].

==Biography==
FN-2003 was a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served in the [[First Order]] in the [[Standard decade|decades]] after the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Part of a [[fire team]] led by [[Finn|FN-2187]] in the [[FN Trooper Corps]], FN-2003 often fell behind the rest of the group during their training simulations, leading to his nickname of "Slip." FN-2187 often had to rescue Slip in those simulations, including in one where the team assaulted a [[New Republic]] bunker with the objective of destroying a [[heavy repeating blaster]]. Slip was reprimanded by [[Captain]] [[Phasma]] after the simulation ended, and Phasma further ordered FN-2187 to stop rescuing Slip. As Phasma explained, Slip was the weakest link and helping him would weaken the First Order. During a [[Strike at Pressy's Tumble|strike]] at [[Pressy's Tumble]], Slip redeemed himself in Phasma's [[eye]]s by [[Death|executing]] an [[Abednedo]] negotiator.<ref name="BTA" />

During a First Order [[Attack on Tuanul|raid]] on a [[Tuanul|sacred village]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], Slip was hit by [[blaster]] [[fire]] from [[Poe Dameron]]. FN-2187 went to help his squadmate, but FN-2003's wound proved fatal. Slip wiped his [[blood]]y hand on FN-2187's [[First Order stormtrooper armor|helmet]] before dying. His sudden and violent death contributed to FN-2187's decision to escape from First Order service.<ref name="TFA VD" /><ref name="Episode VII" />

==Behind the scenes==
FN-2003 first appeared in the [[2015]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Episode VII" /> He was not named in the film itself, only in the novel ''[[Before the Awakening]]'', written by [[Greg Rucka]] and released the same day as the movie.<ref name="BTA" /> FN-2003's appearance in ''The Force Awakens'' was confirmed in the ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''.<ref name="TFA VD" /> He was portrayed by [[Pip Andersen]] in the film, listed in the end credits as "Lead Stormtrooper."

Although FN-2003 was killed by blaster fire from Poe Dameron in the film,<ref name="Episode VII" /> [[Alan Dean Foster]]'s [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|novelization]] depicts FN-2003 being killed by blaster fire from one of the villagers.<ref name="TFA Novel">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' novelization]]</ref>
