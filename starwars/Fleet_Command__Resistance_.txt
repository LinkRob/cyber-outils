The '''Fleet Command''' referred to the command section of the [[Resistance]] [[Resistance navy|fleet]].<ref name="TLJ VD" /> During the [[Battle of Oetchi]],<ref name="Episode VIII" /> the fleet command staff was redistributed among three [[Starship|ships]]. [[Vice Admiral]] [[Resdox]] took command of the [[Nebulon-C escort frigate]] [[Anodyne (Nebulon-C)|''Anodyne'']], Vice Admiral [[Jotis]] took command of the [[Vakbeor-class cargo frigate]] ''[[Vigil]]'', and Vice Admiral [[Amilyn Holdo]] retained command of her ship, the [[Free Virgillia-class Bunkerbuster|''Free Virgillia''-class Bunkerbuster]]<ref name="TLJ ICS">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> ''[[Ninka]]''.<ref name="TLJ VD" />

During the attack, several Resistance leaders including Holdo, [[Gial Ackbar]] and [[Idrosen Gawat]] [[Death|died]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
