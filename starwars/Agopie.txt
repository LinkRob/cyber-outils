'''Agopies''' were a [[water]]-dwelling creature that lived on the [[planet]] [[Eiram]]. They resembled [[horse]]s and could be ridden. Wild agopies living near the city of [[Erasmus Capital City]] were very docile and would let any of the locals ride them.<ref name="Convergence" />
