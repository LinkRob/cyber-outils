'''Farks''' was a [[Patrolian]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]]. A friend of the [[pirate]] [[queen]] [[Maz Kanata]], Farks gave her a [[vonduun crab]] [[Bone|skull]] from [[Zav Alox]] which Kanata kept in her [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]]. Later, after an [[Qort|infant]] [[Aloxian]] came into Kanata's care and began wearing the skull, Farks travelled to Takodana on the pirate's request and told her about the origin of the skull and the Aloxians.<ref name="HRA 9" />
