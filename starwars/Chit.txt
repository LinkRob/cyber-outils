A '''chit''' was a card which could be scanned for identification. The [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] used a chit to enter the [[Imperial Remnant safe house]], also known as a safe house, on [[Nevarro]] to receive his job by direct commission. He also used it when he returned and collected his payment.

==History==
Upon his return to Nevarro to collect his most recent bounty, [[Din Djarin]], a Mandalorian bounty hunter known to most as "the Mandalorian," collected his reward at the [[Nevarro school|cantina]] in the local [[Nevarro City|town]] from an [[agent]] of the [[Bounty Hunters' Guild]], [[Greef Karga]], and received his next [[bounty]], one that Djarin accepted when he learned of the high reward being offered by the patrons. After receiving the chit to enter the meeting location, Djarin left the cantina to meet with [[the Client]].<ref name="Chapter 1" />

Djarin returned to the Client with [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], the quarry, and used a chit to enter the [[Imperial Remnant safe house|safe house]] once again. Although he learned the Client had meet with other hunters.<ref name="Chapter 3">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 3: The Sin}}</ref>
