The '''Dhanis''',<ref name="Axe">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}}</ref> also known as '''Aldhanis''', were a cultural group of [[human]]s who called the [[planet]] of [[Aldhani]] [[Homeworld|home]]. Centuries prior to [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] takeover, the hills of the planet were covered by hundreds of settlements that housed at least 40,000 Dhanis across the highlands. However, after about a decade of the Empire's presence, the Dhanis were cleared out and relocated to an [[Enterprise Zone]] in the [[Lowlands]] in the southern region of the planet. The Enterprise Zone was comprised of factories and new Imperial housing. By then, the only people who remained living in the hills were a few shepherds, nature lovers, mystics, and dead-enders. Once every three years, the Dhanis gathered for a celestial event called the [[Eye of Aldhani]].<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref>
