'''Fleecs''' was a [[Weequay]] who was part of a [[Telsla's crew|crew]] led by the [[Abednedo]] [[Telsla]]. She often got into arguments with fellow crewman, [[Belk]]. After taking over [[Tomarian's Annual Festival of Light Gala]] at [[Tomarian's theater|his theater]], Telsla sent Fleecs to go search for anyone else in the theater. Fleecs came back empty handed.The Abednedo then ordered Fleecs to tie up Tomarian.<ref name="Adventures 5" />
