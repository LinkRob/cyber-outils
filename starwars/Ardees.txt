'''Ardees''', also known as '''Jawa Juice''', was a beverage that originated on [[Tatooine]] and was made of [[bantha]] hides mashed with fermented grains.<ref name="AbEverything" /> When [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] attended [[Dex's Diner]] on [[Coruscant]] shortly before the start of the [[Clone Wars]], he was offered a cup of ardees by [[FLO]], the [[WA-7 waitress droid|waitress droid]]. Kenobi shared his drink with his old friend [[Dexter Jettster]], the [[Restaurant|diner's]] owner.<ref name="AotC" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Nothing like a tall cup of Jawa Juice. Where's that waitress gone?|A human patron of Dex's Diner|<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]''</ref>}}
Ardees first appeared in the [[2002]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="AotC" /> It was first [[canon]]ically identified as "Ardees" in the [[2015]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''.<ref name="AbEverything" /> The drink was named after [[Ardees Rabang Jundis]], an assistant to [[Rick McCallum]].<ref name="Databank: WA-7 (Behind the scenes)">{{DB|droid|wa7|FLO (WA-7)}}</ref>

In the original script and pre-release cuts of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Attack of the Clones'']], FLO offers Obi-Wan Kenobi a cup of Ardees, although the name was changed to Jawa Juice in the final version of the film.<ref name="Databank: WA-7 (Behind the scenes)" />
