'''Chakham''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[General/Legends|General]] of the [[Galactic Alliance Army]]. When [[Stavin Thaal]] defected from the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] to the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]], Chakham was one of his supporters.
