'''Brad Rau''' is a television director. He has directed episodes of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'', ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny]]'', ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' and ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.

The ''Rebels'' character [[Fenn Rau]] is [[Tuckerization|named after]] Rau, who directed the episode ("[[The Protector of Concord Dawn]]") in which Fenn Rau first appeared.<ref name="BTSname">{{WebCite|url=http://fullofsith.com/archives/2100|text=Episode CCXXVI: Brad Rau and Forces of Destiny|date=2017-08-30|work=Full of Sith|archivedate=20191231234719}}</ref>
