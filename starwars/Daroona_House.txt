'''Daroona House''', or '''Ays' hideout''' was a two building home built in the [[South Jundland Wastes]] region of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a [[human]] named [[Ays Varga]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]] as a [[superweapon]]s [[engineer]] [[Desertion|defected]] from the Empire, intending to join the [[Crimson Dawn]]. During which she used Daroona House as her hideout. However, Varga instead made a deal with [[pirate]]s, who then sold her out to the Empire.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
After Varga stopped answering communications, [[EE'zok]], an operative of Crimson Dawn, dispatched the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] to find Varga at the house. Vess searched the house and discovered the meetup location between Varga and the pirates. After rescuing Varga and delivering her to Crimson Dawn, the Dawn began using Daroona house as a base. During which Vess stole the material necessary to apply a Crimson Dawn paint scheme to her [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Daroona House appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' published by [[Ubisoft]]. The buildings of the house are only accessible to the player after they complete the "defector" side quest and Crimson Dawn starts using the location as a base. Located in the building is an unlockable paint scheme that players can use to customize the appearance of their speeder bike.<ref name="Outlaws" />
