'''Dorwin Corvax''', also known as the '''Black Bishop''', was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] individual<ref name="Vader VR 2" /> [[Reproduction|born]] before [[1032 BBY]]<ref name="Dates" /> who was the [[Marriage|husband]] of [[Lady]] [[Corvax]].<ref name="Vader VR 2" /> At some point, he [[Death|fell]] in [[Attack on Mustafar|battle]], and Lady Corvax attempted to use the [[Bright Star]] to bring him back to life but instead turned [[Mustafar]] into a [[Chaos|hellish]] wasteland,<ref name="Vader VR 1" /> leaving her husband caught between the world of the living and what came after. Due to his situation, Lord Corvax had the ability to both suspend [[time]]<ref name="Vader VR 2" /> and teleport. At some point during the [[Imperial Era]], he helped [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] in his quest to bring [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] back to life,<ref name="Vader VR 1" /> though his true intentions were to retrieve the Bright Star from Vader and return it to the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Mustafarian]]s, freeing himself from his curse.<ref name="Vader VR 2" />

His [[Corvax descendant|descendant]] assisted in returning the Bright Star to the Mustafarians.<ref name="Vader VR 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dorwin Corvax first appeared in the first chapter of ''[[Vader Immortal: A Star Wars VR Series]]'', a three-part virtual reality series created by [[ILM Immersive|ILMxLAB]]. Named the Black Bishop in certain portions of the game, he is referred by the [[Priestess (Mustafarian)|Priestess]] in a flashback cutscene as "Sir Dorwin."<ref name="Vader VR 1" /> [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Matt Martin]] later clarified the spelling of this name on Twitter.<ref name="Martin Twitter">{{Twitter|missingwords|status/1307111705965723648|[[Matt Martin]]|quote=Lord Dorwin Corvax.|archiveurl=https://archive.is/PEapi}}</ref>
