'''Backwards Snoke''' was a [[humanoid]] [[Gender|male]] [[Cloning|clone]] of [[Snoke]] who was created by the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Religion|cult]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]]. His cloning process was unsuccessful which left him with a reversed head and backwards [[Language|speech]].<ref name="TSS" />

==Behind the scenes==
Backwards Snoke first appeared in the [[2022]] [[canon|non-canon]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga]]'', in the side mission "Attack of the Clones (Again)".<ref name="TSS" />
