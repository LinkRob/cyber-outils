'''Enanan Supa''' was a [[human]] [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] for the [[Resistance]]. A friend of the fallen [[pilot]] [[Nix Jerd]], she swapped from [[pilot]]ing [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17s]] to a [[B-wing Mark III]]. She specialized in making ungainly craft seem graceful, and had superb situational awareness. In [[35 ABY]], she attended a meeting at the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on the [[moon]] [[Ajan Kloss]] where pilot [[Poe Dameron]] revealed the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] had returned, and later raced through the base during preparations for an [[Battle of Exegol|attack]] on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].

==Biography==
Enanan Supa was a [[human]] who flew as a [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] for the [[Resistance]] during its [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]]. She at one point flew [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17s]] for the Resistance and was a friend of [[Nix Jerd]],<ref name="TROS VD" /> a [[bombardier]] serving in [[Cobalt Squadron]]<ref name="TLJ:CS">''[[The Last Jedi: Cobalt Squadron]]''</ref> who perished during the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] of the [[Resistance base]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]]<ref name="TLJ">{{Film|VIII}}</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="VIIIDate">{{FilmDate|VIII|the [[Battle of D'Qar]]}}</ref> By [[35 ABY]],<ref name="IXDate">{{FilmDate|IX|Enanan Supa's appearance}}</ref> Supa had changed to flying a [[B-wing Mark III]] and begun using a [[flight helmet]] that had belonged to Jerd.<ref name="TROS VD" />

Stationed at the [[Resistance base (Ajan Kloss)|Resistance base]] on the [[jungle]] [[moon]] of [[Ajan Kloss]], she was amongst those who attended a meeting in which fellow pilot [[Poe Dameron]] revealed that the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] had returned and created the [[Sith Eternal]]'s fleet, the [[Final Order]], on the [[planet]] [[Exegol]] in order to establish a new [[Sith Empire]]. After a later meeting to plan an [[Battle of Exegol|attack]] on Exegol, she ran through the base past [[controller]] [[Vober Dand]] as pilots boarded their craft and launched for the assault, where they ultimately defeated the First Order and the [[Sith]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==Personality and traits==
Enanan Supa specialized in making ungainly craft appear graceful, possessing exceptional situational awareness that allowed her to fly in crowded or confined battle conditions without crashing. She had [[Color|black]] [[hair]], dark skin, and [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Equipment==
[[File:Nix-Jerd-helmet-TLJVD.png|thumb|right|200px|Supa uses Nix Jerd's recovered flight helmet]]
Supa wore a yellow [[flight suit]] with a white [[flight harness]]. Her [[Armor|helmet]] bore red, yellow and gray markings with a white [[Rebel Alliance starbird]], as it had when its previous owner Nix Jerd<ref name="TROS VD" /> had worn it as part of Cobalt Squadron.<ref name="TLJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Enanan Supa first appeared in the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']],<ref name="TROS" /> and was first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" />
