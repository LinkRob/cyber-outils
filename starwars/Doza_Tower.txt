'''Doza Tower''', also referred to as '''High Tower''', was a tower located on the ''[[Colossus]]'', a [[Fuel|refueling]] platform on the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Castilon]].<ref name="The Recruit" /> It was considered a luxurious space, and as such, most ''Colossus'' inhabitants dreamed of living there. The [[Aces' Lounge]] was located within Doza Tower.<ref name="Doza Tower DB" /> The top of the tower was occupied by [[Captain]] [[Imanuel Doza]]'s [[Doza's office|office]] and the ''Colossus''{{'}} long-disused [[Colossus command bridge|command bridge]].<ref name="Into the Unknown" /><ref name="The High Tower" /> Doza reopened the command bridge after the ''Colossus'' was relaunched into space to [[Battle of Castilon|escape]] the [[First Order]].<ref name="Into the Unknown" />

The [[Aces|Ace]] [[pilot]] [[Hype Fazon]] had a personal hangar designated [[Hype Fazon's hangar bay|Hangar C2]] located in the tower.<ref name="The Disappeared" />
