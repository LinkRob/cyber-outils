'''Drabatese''' was the [[language]] spoken by the [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[Drabatan]]s of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Pipada]]. The language combined small words and syllables to form large words with detailed meanings with multiple layers. Sonic volume determined much subtext and grammar; as a result, the language was difficult to [[Writing|write]] out. When situations required it, Drabatans could switch to a softer [[Drabatan secondary language|secondary language]] by passing air through their [[vocal pouch]]es. Drabatans used their loud voices to create Drabatese [[music]]; their songs were part of their everyday lives, ranging from education to entertainment.

During the [[Imperial Era]], the Drabatan [[Musician|singer]] [[Sa'Kalla]] was [[Death penalty|executed]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] for encouraging Drabatan [[Sa'Kalla (movement)|resistance]] against Imperial rule. Her name was then used by Drabatans who fought the Empire as a Drabatese war cry. During the [[Battle of Scarif]] in [[1 BBY]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] Drabatan [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek]] used the war cry.

===Characteristics===

[[File:Pipada-SWT.png|thumb|right|300px|The Drabatans of Pipada (pictured) spoke Drabatese]]
Drabatese was a loud [[language]] used by the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Amphibian|amphibious]] [[Drabatan]]s of<ref name="DoR" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="Galaxy Map">[[Star Wars Galaxy Map (poster)|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map poster]]  &mdash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Pipada system]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Pipada]]. The language combined small words and syllables to form large words with multilayered ideas and very specific meanings. Drabatese was difficult to [[Writing|write]] out, as sonic volume determined much of the subtext and grammar.<ref name="DoR" /> When necessary, Drabatans switched to a [[Drabatan secondary language|secondary language]] by passing air through their [[vocal pouch]]es to emit croaks of varied pitch.<ref name="Visual Guide" />

Drabatans often used their language to create [[music]]. Critics described Drabatese songs as hauntingly beautiful and insisted that the songs required no translation to appreciate the ethereal, melancholy tones. Drabatese music was used extensively in Drabatans' day-to-day lives, spanning from entertainment to education. Drabatan families each had unique songs that detailed their lineage and were performed at family gatherings by the family's best [[Musician|vocalist]]. School curriculums taught everything through Drabatese song, and students used jingles as mnemonic devices to help with memorization.<ref name="DoR" />

==History==
{{Quote|Karabast! AT-AT!|audio=PaoKarabast-RO.flac|Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek alerts his comrades of the threat in Drabatese|<ref name="Rogue" />}}
[[File:Drabatan-DoR.png|thumb|left|230px|Drabatans (pictured) used the Drabatese war cry of "Sa'Kalla."]]
By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'' states that [[Sa'Kalla]]'s name was used as a battle cry after her [[death]]. As [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek|Pao]] uses her name as a battle cry during the [[Battle of Scarif]] in ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''&mdash;which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of to [[1 BBY]]&mdash;she must have passed away by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the Drabatans protested the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Era|rule]] of [[the galaxy]]. One of the most vocal protesters was the singer Sa'Kalla, who wrote protest songs speaking out against Imperial tyranny. The Empire heard of these songs and enacted a [[crackdown on Pipada]] and captured Sa'Kalla, which sparked a [[Sa'Kalla (movement)|movement]] in her name that spread across the planet. The singer was brought to the [[Pipada Auditorium]] by an [[Unidentified Imperial governor (Pipada)|Imperial governor]] in an effort to force the movement to submit. Sa'Kalla called out to her people to continue their resistance and was [[Death penalty|killed]] on stage. The enraged crowd [[Death|tore apart]] the [[Imperial Military|Imperial security forces]] and briefly took back their world. Drabatans who fought the Empire adapted the young singer's name as a Drabatese war cry.<ref name="DoR" />

The Drabatan [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Paodok'Draba'Takat Sap'De'Rekti Nik'Linke'Ti' Ki'Vef'Nik'NeSevef'Li'Kek]] spoke Drabatese while serving the Rebellion, often uttering oaths and curses in the language. His fellow [[Rebel trooper|soldiers]] did not mind him speaking Drabatese as relaying instructions was not one of his responsibilities.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> During the [[Battle of Scarif]]<ref name="Rogue" /> in 1 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Rogue One|rebel forces]] were faced against an [[All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport]].<ref name="Rogue" /> Pao exclaimed the Drabatese words "Karabast! AT-AT"<ref name="SWkids" /> to alert the other rebel [[soldier]]s of the threat.<ref name="Rogue" /> Pao also used the Drabatese war cry "Sa'Kalla" during the battle.<ref name="Visual Guide" /> 

Drabatese was mentioned in an [[Unidentified artist's journal|artist's journal]] written<ref name="Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> no earlier than [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Year">{{AlienArchiveJournalDate}}</ref> The language was mentioned in an entry on the Drabatan species. The journal eventually came into the possession of the [[Graf Archive]]&mdash;a vast depository owned by the [[Graf family]] that contained documents that held historical importance.<ref name="Archive" />
