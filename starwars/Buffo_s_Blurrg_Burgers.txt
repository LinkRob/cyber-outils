'''Buffo's Blurrg Burgers''',<ref name="Plan 99" /> also known as '''Boffo's Blurrg Burgers'''<ref name="Sinister Schemes"/> and '''Boffo's''',<ref name="Inquisition">{{MSL|Chapter 5: Inquisition}}</ref> was a [[restaurant]] which existed in several different locations across [[the galaxy]]: There was one in [[Ord Mantell City]] on the [[planet]] [[Ord Mantell]],<ref name="Plan 99" /> another was located on [[Suria space station]],<ref name="Territory" /> a third restaurant could be found at the [[Unidentified lunar base|lunar base]] where [[Phee Genoa]] landed to refuel her [[Providence|ship]];<ref name="Point" /> while a fourth one, known as B'''o'''ffo's Blurrg Burgers,<ref name="Sinister Schemes"/> or just B'''o'''ffo's,<ref name="Inquisition" /> was located in [[Janix city]] on the planet [[Janix]].<ref name="Sinister Schemes"/>

The eatery's specialty was [[burger]]s made from [[blurrg]] meat.<ref name="99 Trivia" /> Signs and advertisements were used to attract customers:<ref name="Plan 99" /> An [[Aurebesh]] sign on a building in Ord Mantell City read "BUFFO'S BLURRG BURGERS" and "THE BEST BLURRGER IN TOWN."<ref name="99 Trivia">{{SW|url=plan-99-trivia-gallery|text="Plan 99" Trivia Gallery &#124; The Bad Batch}} {{C|Slide 4}}</ref> An advertisement in [[Tanda's Cantina]] on Suria space station merely showed an illustration of the eatery's signature blurrgers, in the form of a blurrg between two pieces of [[bread]].<ref name="Territory" /> The sign in Ord Mantell City<ref name="Plan 99" /> was also featured as an advertisement in a hangar on the lunar base.<ref name="Point" /> The name of the eatery<ref name="Sinister Schemes" /> and its slogan were also advertised on billboards in Janix [[city]].<ref name="Call to Oblivion" />
