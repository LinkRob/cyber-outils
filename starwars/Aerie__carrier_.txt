The '''''Aerie''''' was a [[carrier]] utilized by the [[Cloud-Riders]] to [[transport]] them and their [[swoop]] bikes during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Starship|ship]] rarely landed, as the swoops could launch from altitudes of 400 [[kilometer]]s.<ref name="Official Guide" /> It served as the nomadic [[pirate]] group's home base.<ref name="Tales from Vandor">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
During the development of ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', the Cloud-Riders' carrier ship was going to be called the ''Buckshot''. Its design was made from kitbashing a [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]].<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The [[Razor Assault Ship]] from the [[2019]] [[sequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] had a similar design to the ''Buckshot''.<ref name="EpIX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
