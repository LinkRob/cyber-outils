The '''Coruscant Industrial Zone Detention Center''' was a [[Prison|detention center]] located in the [[Industrial District]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]'s [[Imperial City|Galactic City]]. The structure was abandoned by the [[Standard year|year]] [[21 BBY]] and became a secret hideout for the [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]]. [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] were captured and brought to the detention center, where Bane's modified [[8D-series smelter droid]] [[J0-N0]] [[torture]]d them for information on the [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]].<ref name="Evil Plans" />
