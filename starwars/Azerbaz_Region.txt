The '''Azerbaz Region''' was an area of the [[Desert Valley]] region on [[Tatooine]], a [[desert]] [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. It was situated east of the [[city]] [[Bestine (city)|Bestine]] and west of [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Miyuki Trade League]] had left a [[Smuggler Cache 81|cache]] of materials and equipment.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|option=1}}
That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a [[Imperial dispatcher|dispatcher]] with the [[Galactic Empire]] reported the last seen location of the [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] after she was involved in a disturbance on Tatooine.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Attention units. The suspect was seen in the Azerbaz Region|Imperial dispatcer reporting Kay Vess's location|audio=SuspectWasSeenInTheAzerbazRegion-Outlaws.flac|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
The Azerbaz Region appeared in the [[canon]] continuity  in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] continuity  in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. It was only identified by the Imperial dispatcher comm messages as part of the game's wanted system. As such the exact bounds and location of the Azerbaz Region are not clearly defined.<ref name="Outlaws" />

The [[Azerbaz Region/Legends|Azerbaz Region]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity where it appeared in the comic "[[Luke Skywalker: Detective]]" written by [[Rick Geary]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Tales 20">{{Tales|20|Luke Skywalker: Detective}}</ref> on [[June 30]], [[2004]]<ref name="Tales 20 Release Date">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/12-583/Star-Wars-Tales-20-Photo-cover|text=Star Wars Tales 20}}</ref> in the [[Star Wars Tales 20|twentieth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Tales]]'' comic series.<ref name="Tales 20" />
