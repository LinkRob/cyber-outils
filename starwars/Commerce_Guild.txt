The '''Commerce Guild''', also known as the '''Commerce Guilds''', was a large-scale interstellar conglomerate active in [[the galaxy]]. It included agricorps, retailers, service providers, and the [[Mining Guild]], among other commercial entities, and was a primary competitor of the [[Corporate Alliance]]. The Guild was headquartered on [[Felucia]], but was largely controlled by the [[Gossam]]s of [[Castell]].

During the [[Clone Wars]], the Commerce Guild, like many other interstellar conglomerates, lent its covert support to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], earning it [[Amita Fonti|representation]] in the [[Separatist Senate]] and the quasi-executive [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]]. Nevertheless, after the Separatists were defeated and [[Presidente]] [[Shu Mai]] was [[Death|killed]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Mission to Mustafar|on]] [[Mustafar]], it reversed course and supported the [[Galactic Empire]].

==History==
===Establishment and activity===
The Commerce Guild, sometimes referred to as the Commerce Guilds,<ref name="Episode II" /> was a conglomerate formed when<ref name="Build R2 36">{{BuildR2Cite|36|Droid Directory}}</ref> several galactic-scale corporate organizations&mdash;whose number would include agricorps, retailers, arms manufacturers, shipbuilders, service providers, and the [[Mining Guild]]<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" />&mdash;banded together in a desire for safety and to ease trade.<ref name="Build R2 36" /> It became headquartered on [[Felucia]] but was controlled by the [[Gossam]]s of [[Castell]].<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> In [[time]],<ref name="Episode II" /> it became one of the five most powerful companies in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="OF84">{{GermanFanCite|84|Database}}</ref>

The Commerce Guild used [[Vangaard Pathfinder]] [[scout ship]]s to discover valuable planets.<ref name="Tales">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref> [[Shu Mai]]&mdash;a Gossam who was only concerned with power, wealth, and status&mdash;used aggressive and unscrupulous tactics to increase her power in the Guild. Ultimately, no one stood in her path to become [[presidente]] of the Commerce Guild.<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition]]''</ref>

===Clone Wars===
Despite being a competitor to the [[Corporate Alliance]],<ref name="Rise of the Separatists" /> the Commerce Guild joined the Alliance as well as several other conglomerates in pledging its support and its [[Commerce Guild Punitive Security Forces|army]] to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and his [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] shortly before the outbreak of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Episode II" /> joining in their effort against the [[Galactic Senate]] as long as they were not openly involved in the Confederacy's affairs during the early period.<ref name="OF84" /> Presidente Mai sat on the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] for the duration of the conflict,<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> while [[Amita Fonti]] represented the Guild in the [[Separatist Senate]].<ref name="Heroes on Both Sides Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode310.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Heroes on Both Sides}}</ref>

Like many other major corporations that lifted up the Separatist cause,<ref name="MOF">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref> the Guild was not an open backer of the Confederacy<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition" /> and instead declared neutrality. Like its fellow Separatist-aligned corporations, however, its true loyalty was an open secret.<ref name="MOF" /> Indeed, Mai's [[OG-9 homing spider droid]]s were deployed to fight in the war.<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia, New Edition" /> Guild employee [[Yadoska Nir]] collaborated with the Confederacy while the company purchased the loyalty of entire planets, swaying them to the Separatist cause.<ref name="MOF" /> During the war, the planet [[Mooga]] was controlled by the Commerce Guild.<ref name="Corruption" />

===Imperial Era===
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="19 Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> the Separatist Alliance was defeated and Mai was [[Death|killed]] by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Mission to Mustafar|on]] [[Mustafar]]. Despite her death,<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> the Guild continued to operate after the war, working with the new [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" /> Nir was found by the Empire and interviewed for collaborating with the CIS.<ref name="MOF" />

The Commerce Guild's [[Unidentified Commerce Guild senator|representative]] in the [[Imperial Senate]] was a major supporter of the [[Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill]], believing that the people of the galaxy required a [[Imperial Military|conscripted military]] comprised of their [[TK stormtrooper|own people]] to fight for them while simultaneously advocating that the [[Clone trooper|clone soldiers]] that comprised the majority of their [[Grand Army of the Republic|present forces]] be slated for decommissioning, given that their accelerated age process meant that, with the [[Attack on Kamino|destruction]] of the [[Kaminoan]] [[Tipoca City|facilities]], could not be sustain.<ref name="The Clone Conspiracy" /> Agents of the Guild attempted to purchase the [[Phylanx Redux Transmitter]] during an [[Auction for the Phylanx Redux Transmitter|auction on Cantonica]].<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref>
