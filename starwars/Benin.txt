'''Benin''' was a [[human]] who was an [[administrator]]. They were in charge of [[Uveron City security|security]] at [[Uveron City]] and was responsible for providing security for rebel leader [[Mon Mothma]], during a [[Funding mission for the Alliance|funding mission]],<ref name="SW45" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars (2020)|''Star Wars'']] take place after the [[Escape from Cloud City]] and before the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which are dated to [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]], respectively, by ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref> During the mission, Mothma was [[Kidnapping of Mon Mothma|kidnapped]] by the [[Scarabi]] mercenary group, who attacked the security guards and took her hostage.<ref name="SW45" />

Afterwards, Benin was visited by Rebel Alliance [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|pathfinders]] who were [[Mission to rescue Mothma|rescuing]] Mothma. Benin apologized for the security failures and took full responsibility. Leader of the pathfinders, [[Kes Dameron]] assured Benin that they were not at fault and there was no security concerns. Benin then praised their fighters and believed that the perpetrators of the kidnapping were highly skilled.<ref name="SW45" />
