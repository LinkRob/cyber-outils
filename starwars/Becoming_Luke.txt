"'''Becoming Luke'''" is an interview with [[Mark Hamill]] conducted by [[Charles Lippincott]] in [[January]] [[1977]], a few months before the release of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']]. This article also includes a Top 5 of [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker's]] best actions through the [[original trilogy]] and a review of his costumes. It was published in [[Star Wars Insider 137|''Star Wars Insider'' 137]] and later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2014|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2014]].
