'''Daubcrete''' was a building material that could be used to make walls.<ref name="AEE" /> By [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] bunker on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]] had been built with daubcrete walls. At some point, one of the [[Slavery|enslaved]] [[Wookiee]]s trapped in the compound had attempted to scale its walls in an effort to escape.<ref name="AEE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Daubcrete was mentioned in ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', a [[2017]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the third installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="AEE" />
