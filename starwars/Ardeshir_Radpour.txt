'''Ardeshir Radpour''' is an actor, stuntman, and performance artist who has appeared across multiple live-action ''[[Star Wars]]'' series on [[Disney+]]. In ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', Radpour portrayed two separate [[Klatooinian]] characters, one being a [[Unidentified Klatooinian don|crime boss]] of [[Mos Espa]]<ref name="Boba Chapter 4" /> and the other being [[Kaba Baiz]], a [[bounty]] [[Hunting|hunted]] by [[Din Djarin]] in the show's [[Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian|fifth episode]].<ref name="Boba Chapter 5" /> He later appeared in several episodes of ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'', including the [[Part III|third]], in which he portrays a [[Unidentified stormtrooper (Mapuzo checkpoint)|stormtrooper]] at an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] checkpoint on [[Mapuzo]].<ref name="Kenobi Part III">{{Kenobi|Part III}}</ref>
