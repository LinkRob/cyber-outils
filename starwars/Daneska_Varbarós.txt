'''Daneska Varbarós''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[pilot]] in the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]], and the successor to [[Ric Olié]]. She served [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]], former [[queen]] of [[Naboo]], during her [[time]] in the [[Galactic Senate]]. After Amidala's [[Mid Rim Cooperation Motion]] was passed, Varbarós led the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Hazard Squadron]] to defend [[starship]] [[convoy]]s being sent to the ailing world of [[Bromlarch]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
