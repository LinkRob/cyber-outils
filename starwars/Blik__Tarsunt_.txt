'''Blik''' was a [[Tarsunt]] [[droid]] fight promoter at a [[Unidentified gladiatorial arena|gladiatorial arena]]. During the [[Imperial Era]], the individual helped organize an arena duel involving the [[Astromech droid|astromech]] "[[C1-10P|Chopper]]," mistakenly believing the [[Lasat]] [[Garazeb Orrelios]] had brought him to compete. When Orrelios witnessed Chopper battling a [[droideka]], he banged Blik's and a [[Unidentified Twi'lek bookie|Twi'lek bookie]]'s heads together before escaping with his droid companion.

==Biography==
{{Quote|Effor will prep your droid while you and I fill out your registration.|Blik, to Garazeb Orrelios|<ref name="ABoC" />}}
A member of the [[Tarsunt]] [[species]], Blik had started to work as a fight prompter by the [[Imperial Era]], recruiting contestants for the [[gladiator droid]] fights at a [[Unidentified gladiatorial arena|gladiatorial arena]].<ref name="ABoC" />

At some point between [[5 BBY]] and [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"Always Bet on Chop" takes place during the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and includes Rex, placing it after the events of "[[The Lost Commanders]]." ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "The Lost Commanders" to [[4 BBY]] and the ending of ''Star Wars Rebels'' to [[1 BBY]], placing "Always Bet on Chop" between these [[Standard year|years]]. Therefore, "Always Ben on Chop" must be taking place between [[4 BBY]] and 1 BBY. Since the arena incident is narrated in flashbacks, it might have taken place as early as [[5 BBY]], given that, as per ''Star Wars Timelines'', the [[Spectres|Spectre cell]]&mdash;which both Orrelios and Chopper are members of&mdash;was established in that year.</ref> the arena complex was visited by the [[Lasat]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Garazeb Orrelios|Garazeb "Zeb" Orrelios]] and the [[Astromech droid|astromech]] [[C1-10P]] "Chopper". Unbeknownst to Blik, the pair were on a covert mission to steal critical [[Galactic Empire|imperial]] bank codes. The duo split up before they met up again. As the Lasat hurried<ref name="ABoC" /> his<ref name="zeb-orrelios">{{Databank|zeb-orrelios|Garazeb "Zeb" Orrelios}}</ref> droid compation, he was interrupted mid-sentence by Blik. The Tarsunt informed him that the next fight was about to begin and gave him a shove to hurry him forward. They made their way through a corridor leading to the registration area, where Blik escorted Orrelios to the registration desk and had the [[Verpine]] [[Effor]] prepare Chopper and guide him to the arena floor. Unbeknownst to Zeb, his droid companion was to take part in a [[gladiator]] [[droid]] fight.<ref name="ABoC" />

[[File:TellingOnZeb.png|thumb|right|250px|Blik reports on Zeb's whereabouts.]]
At the registration desk, a [[Unidentified Twi'lek bookie|Twi'lek bookie]] noticed that Zeb's name did not appear in any database and summoned security. Meanwhile, a nearby [[human]] shouted for Blik, pointing out that the "little" astromech Chopper was causing chaos with "[[The Unwinder]]," a [[droideka]] champion in the arena. Blik, the bookie, and Orrelios rushed to a large [[viewport]], just in [[time]] to see Chopper dodging a barrage of [[laser]] bolts. When Blik asked if the droid belonged to him, Zeb denied it, as he instead treated Chopper as a family member. A moment later, he slammed Blik's and the bookie's heads together, using the confusion to make his escape. As Zeb raced to help his droid companion, Blik encountered a squad of Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s displaying a hologram of the wanted Lasat. Although the Tarsunt pointed the soldiers in Zeb's direction, the rebels ultimately managed to outsmart their pursuers and slip away from the arena complex.<ref name="ABoC" />

Some time later, Orrelios recounted the events at the gladiator arena to the veteran [[clone trooper]] [[Rex]] aboard the ''[[Ghost]]''. During their conversation, Zeb discovered that Chopper had already downloaded the Imperial data upon their arrival to the arena complex, which meant that the entire commotion at the arena could have been avoided altogether.<ref name="ABoC" />

==Personality and traits==
Blik was a [[Color|brown]]-skinned [[Tarsunt]] with a partially bald head, whose sides featured long strands of brown [[hair]] extending into a double-braided beard.<ref name="ABoC" />

==Equipment==
Blik wore a red and tan [[Clothing|outfit]] with a sleeveless vest featuring orange pockets.<ref name="ABoC" />
