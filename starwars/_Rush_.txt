"'''Rush'''" was the nickname of a<ref name="X-wing" /> [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> individual who served as a [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] in the [[First Order]] during the [[New Republic Era]]. Rush [[pilot]]ed a [[TIE/vn space superiority fighter]], and at one point attacked and destroyed another [[starship]] above a [[Water|snowy]] terrestrial body. Rush thrived with adrenaline while in combat.<ref name="X-wing" />
