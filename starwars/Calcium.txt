'''Calcium''' was a substance which could be used as [[fuel]] in demolitions. The [[Mandalorian]] [[artist]] and demolitions expert [[Sabine Wren]] used calcium in as fuel in some of her explosives as it created [[orange]] explosions, which she used in her "art." She listed the substance and the color it produced along with other fuels which produced colored explosions in her [[Sabine Wren's journal|journal]] about five [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="SMRS" />

Calcium also comprised certain [[starship]] fuels during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. It was present on [[Jakku]] in the [[Starship Graveyard|Graveyard of Ships]] as a hazard to [[scavenger]]s on the [[planet]]'s surface. It possessed an [[orange]] color in the sand, identifying it and warning those of its presence. [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] identified the substance in her [[Rey's survival guide|journal]] about twenty-nine years after the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Survival" />
