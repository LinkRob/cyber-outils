The '''Databank''' is the official [[StarWars.com]] [[canon]]ical encyclopedia. It was launched on [[July 1]], [[2014]], following a design overhaul of the StarWars.com site. The Databank replaced the StarWars.com [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]], which in turn replaced a previous encyclopedia also called the [[Databank (original)|Databank]].

{{Website-stub}}

==Continuity==
*[[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]'s entry for many years stated that his species were [[Hutt]] and "[[B'omarr Order|B'omarr Monk]]."<ref name="Jabba-old">{{Databank|jabba-the-hutt|Jabba the Hutt|oldversion=20220928232208}}</ref> However, Jabba was only established as a Hutt in other sources.<ref name="TCW CE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> It has since been updated to not list any species for him at all.<ref name="Jabba">{{Databank|jabba-the-hutt|Jabba the Hutt}}</ref>
*The "Biography Gallery" featured in [[Orn Free Taa]]'s entry contains a picture from ''The Phantom Menace'' depicting Taa in the [[Galactic Senate Chamber]] with two [[human]] [[aide]]s.<ref name="Taa Bio">{{Databank|url=orn-free-taa-biography-gallery|title=Orn Free Taa Biography Gallery}}; image #1 {{C|of 11}}</ref> Although his aides were indeed human in the theatrical release of the movie (played by [[Chrissie England]] and [[Jim Morris]]), they were replaced by a duo of female [[Twi'lek]] for the DVD release.<ref name="Taa's Aides">{{WebCite|url=http://www.dvdactive.com/editorial/articles/star-wars-the-changes-part-four.html|text=Star Wars: The Changes - Part Four|archivedate=20170708120829}}</ref>
*[[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] entry lists his height as 1.91 [[meter]]s.<ref name="Bail Organa Old">{{Databank|bail-organa|Bail Organa}}</ref> However, the ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'' lists Organa's height as 1.99 meters.<ref name="Updated and Expanded">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref> The entry was later updated to reflect this.<ref name="Bail Organa">{{Databank|bail-organa|Bail Organa}}</ref>
*Despite being a [[LR-57 combat/retail droid|Retail Caucus droid]], [[Battle droid 513]] is listed as being nearly a meter taller than the Retail Caucus [[droid]].<ref name="Retail Caucus Droid">{{Databank|retail-caucus-droid|Retail Caucus Droid}}</ref><ref name="Battle Droid 513">{{Databank|battle-droid-513|Battle droid 513}}</ref>
*[[Elan Sel'Sabagno]]'s entry featured an image of Sleazebaggano with [[ear]]s.<ref>{{Databank|elan-sleazebaggano|Elan Sleazebaggano}}</ref> Though Sleazebaggano actually had ears in reference photography, [[George Lucas]] changed Sleazebaggano's ears with antennapalps during post-production, causing early toys to feature the character with ears.<ref name="Hidalgo tweet #2">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/981652528931549186|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=Today's Star Wars Show reminds me: Elan Sleazebaggano was altered in postproduction, too late for toys in the works to change. The proper Elan has no ears. Reference photography didn't have this digital alteration. Toys had a running change to remove his ears.|archivefile=File:HidalgoSleazebaggano.png}}</ref>
*[[Ord Mantell]]'s entry states that it's the homeworld of the [[Mikkian]] species.<ref name="DB Ord Mantell">{{Databank|ord-mantell|Ord Mantell}}</ref> However, [[Leland Chee]] has stated since then that the Mikkians' homeworld is [[Mikkia]].<ref name="Mikkia Twit">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/808776406691786752|[[Leland Chee]]|quote=@LelalMekha Looks to me like an error. Mikkians are from Mikkia.|archivefile=File:Mikkia_tweet.png}}</ref>
*[[Tiplee]]'s entry incorrectly states that her and Obi-Wan Kenobi's mission to capture Darth Maul occurred at Ord Mantell,<ref name="DB Tiplee">{{Databank|tiplee|Tiplee}}</ref> instead of at [[Vizsla Keep 09]].<ref name="Son of Dathomir, Part Three">[[Darth Maul&mdash;Son of Dathomir 3|''Darth Maul&mdash;Son of Dathomir'' 3]]</ref>
*Although both [[Ponda Baba]]'s and [[Cornelius Evazan]]'s entries claim that the [[lightsaber]] wounds Obi-Wan Kenobi inflicted on Evazan were fatal,<ref name="DB Ponda Baba">{{Databank|ponda-baba|Ponda Baba}}</ref> the ''Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded'' stated that Evazan survived the encounter.<ref name="Updated and Expanded" />
*[[Derek Klivian]]'s entry says that he managed to eject from the cockpit of his [[T-47 airspeeder]] before it crashed into the "head" of an [[All Terrain Armored Transport]].<ref name="Derek Klivian">{{Databank|derek-hobbie-klivian|Derek "Hobbie" Klivian}}</ref> The reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'' claims he died. The author of the book, [[Daniel Wallace]], clarified that it was an idea of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] to kill Klivian based on a planned scene from the movie.<ref name="11 38">{{WebCite|url=http://eleven-thirtyeight.com/2017/07/on-the-front-lines-an-interview-with-dan-wallace|text=On The Front Lines – An Interview with Dan Wallace|author=Nicholas W. Adams|date=2017-07-24|archivedate=20190701072711}}</ref> Klivian's death was later reiterated in the short story "[[Rendezvous Point (short story)|Rendezvous Point]]," published in the anthology ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' in [[2020]].<ref name="FACPOV ESB">{{StoryCite|story=Rendezvous Point (short story)|book=From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back}}</ref>
*The entry for [[Nala Se's private lab]] is instead illustrated with a picture of the [[Tipoca City Training Facility]]'s storage room.<ref name="Nala Lab">{{Databank|nala-se-s-private-lab|Nala Se's private lab}}</ref>
*Both the entries for [[Sim Aloo]] and [[Janus Greejatus]] say that they were presumed dead after the [[Battle of Endor]] due to possibly being still aboard the [[Death Star II/Legends|Death Star II]], but otherwise their fates are unknown.<ref name="Sim Aloo">{{Databank|sim-aloo|Sim Aloo}}</ref><ref name="Janus Greejatus">{{Databank|janus-greejatus|Janus Greejatus}}</ref> The [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 10|tenth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' has since confirmed both of their respective deaths there.<ref name="HC10">{{HelmetCollectionCite|10|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref>
*In the gallery for [[Rebel pilot]]s, the entry on [[Sila Kott]] incorrectly lists her callsign as "[[Red Two]]" when she has been identified as "[[Red Three]]" in other sources. Another error is that the image used for [[Ekelarc Yong]] is actually that of [[Gureni Telsij]].
