==Continuity==
The cards for each character states that [[Aldhani]] was the [[homeworld]] of [[Arvel Skeen]], [[Cinta Kaz]], [[Karis Nemik]], and [[Taramyn Barcona]]. When asked if this was correct, [[Pablo Hidalgo]] of the [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] stated none of the characters were from Aldhani. He theorized the set had made a "poor choice of description" because the characters had all happened to stay in Aldhani for a time.<ref name="Pablo Twitter">{{Twitter|pabl0hidalgo|status/1693067125491912721|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2023-08-20|quote=They're not. I doubt they'd call it home either. It sounds like a poor choice of description of a planet they happen to be associated with.|archivedate=20230820012834}} {{C|In response to: "Hey @pabl0hidalgo @missingwords, in the spirit of recent card game discussion, topps recently dropped a new set but I think these homeworlds don't add up? I guess it's not impossible but I never got a sense all were from aldhani"}}</ref>
