'''AD-2508''' was the designation of a<ref name="Datapad" /> [[human]]<ref name="Human" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] in the [[709th Legion]] of the [[First Order]]. During the First Order's [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] of the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Datapad" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">AD-2508 was stationed on [[Batuu]] during the [[First Order]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|occupation]] there, which took place in [[34 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> AD-2508 was stationed in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on Batuu. The [[trooper]] conversed via [[datapad]] with fellow stormtrooper [[CF-8011]], who questioned what [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] meant when he claimed to [[Force sense|sense something]]. As a response, AD-2508 complained that Ren needed to get a sense of humor. AD-2508 wore standard [[First Order stormtrooper armor]] with a [[Color|white]] [[pauldron]],<ref name="Datapad" /> which denoted the role of [[squad leader]] among the [[Stormtrooper Corps (First Order)|Stormtrooper Corps]].<ref name="TLJVD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
