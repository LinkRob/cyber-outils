'''''Cadence''''' was the name given to a type of [[lightsaber]] drill in ancient [[Jedi]] lore. While [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] was [[Jedi training|training]] with [[Luke Skywalker]] on [[Ahch-To]], her overly energetic practice of ''cadences'' led to the destruction of a rock formation known locally as the "[[Windwailer]]."<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>

{{Stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], cadences were first mentioned in the [[2019]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="TROS VD" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Cadence/Legends|cadences]] were first mentioned in the ''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]'', a supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', published by [[West End Games]]<ref name="GG9">''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]''</ref> on [[May 13]], [[1993]].<ref name="BN">{{BN|url=w/star-wars-the-roleplaying-game-west-end-games-incorporated/1018175932|text=Star Wars the Roleplaying Game: Galaxy Guide #9: Fragments from the Rim}}</ref>
