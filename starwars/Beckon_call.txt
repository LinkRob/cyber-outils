A '''beckon call''' was a portable device that allowed its user to remotely access the controls of a slave-rigged [[starship]].<ref name="Thrawn" />

[[Duja]], a former [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] of [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] of [[Naboo]] owned a [[Duja's brooch|amateurishly-handmade brooch]] that served as a beckon call for her [[transport]] [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Possibility]]''.<ref name="Thrawn" />

The [[pirate]] [[Maz Kanata]] wore a beckon call [[transmitter]] that was connected to her [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Epoch Swift]]'', around the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="TROS VD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>}}
