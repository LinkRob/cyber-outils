'''Fracni''' was a small [[planet]] located near the [[Juinkwy Triangle]], a confluence of [[hyperspace route]]s. The [[Jedi]] [[Wayseeker]]s [[Ty Yorrick]] and [[Vernestra Rwoh]] once stopped [[pirate]]s from scamming Fracni's populace with bootleg [[bacta]], and Rwoh later reminisced that their conversation while resting in one of Fracni's [[City|cities]] afterwards was when Yorrick first told Rwoh that she was considering leaving the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
