'''Eleanor Ehrhardt''' was the author of three children's books. She was not given writing credit in any of these books.

Along with [[Gloria Katz]], who did uncredited editing on the script for [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|the original film]], and [[Carol Titelman]], who edited 1979's [[The Art of Star Wars, Episode IV - A New Hope]], Ehrhardt was one of the first women to create original story content in the ''Star Wars'' universe.
