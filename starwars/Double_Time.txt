'''Double Time''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[fathier]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] around [[1 BBY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> with [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|fur]] and black [[eye]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> they took part in the [[Canto Cup]], a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]] that was held in the [[city]] [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]]. By the time of the race, Double Time was 282 [[centimeter]]s tall, weighed 874 [[kilogram]]s, and was considered to be an old favorite by one [[Unidentified Canto Cup announcer (Basic)|Canto Cup announcer]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
Double Time appeared in a hologram in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. They are one of the fathiers that can randomly feature in the optional Canto Fathier Racing activity. In most races, the six fathiers are assigned a random [[jockey]] and a random symbol. As such, this article does not assume one pairing or symbol color to be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Outlaws" />
