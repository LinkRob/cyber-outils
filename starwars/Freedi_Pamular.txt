'''Freedi Pamular''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Freedi Pamular's species|alien]] from the [[moon]] [[Narkina 5]] who worked as a [[fisher]] with his brother [[Dewi Pamular]]. In [[5 BBY]], the pair were [[fishing]] by a quarry [[Water|lake]] when they captured the fugitives [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] and [[Ruescott Melshi]], who had been trying to [[Thief|steal]] the brothers' [[TUG-b13|quadjumper]].

Dewi and Freedi discussed turning the [[prisoner]]s over to the [[Galactic Empire]] but decided to help them escape instead, for the Empire's [[prison]]s had [[poison]]ed the waters and [[Death|killed]] the [[fish]] that the fishers usually caught. The brothers agreed to take Andor and Melshi to the [[Planet|world]] [[Niamos]] and flew them there in the quadjumper.

==Biography==
===An eventful fishing trip===
{{Quote|Mysie my.''"<br />[Freedi speaks Narkinian]<br />"''Prison, ''haye?'' Escapers. Toosie-two. A thousand each the offer be. One for each of us, Freedi.''"<br />[Freedi speaks Narkinian]|Dewi and Freedi discuss Andor and Melshi|audio=Dewi-TwosieTwo.flac|<ref name="Andor11" />}}
[[File:PamularsRunningPrisoners.png|thumb|right|300px|Dewi (right) and Freedi (left) watched Andor (center left) and Melshi (center right) unsuccessfully try and steal their ship.]]
Freedi Pamular was a [[fisher]]<ref name="Databank" /> from<ref name="Episode8Guide" /> the [[moon]] [[Narkina 5]],<ref name="8Audio">{{Andor|Narkina 5 (episode)}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> where he spent his [[Standard day|days]] on [[fishing]] expeditions with his<ref name="Databank" /> [[Keredian]] brother, [[Dewi Pamular]]. The pair caught [[Foodstuff|edible]] [[fish]] in the moon's quarry [[Water|pools]],<ref name="Dewi">{{Databank|dewi-pamular|Dewi Pamular}}</ref> but their quarry began to [[Death|die]] out after the water was [[poison]]ed by [[prison]]s that the [[Galactic Empire]] had constructed on Narkina 5.<ref name="Andor11" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|111}}</ref> the brothers traveled in a [[TUG-b13|quadjumper]] to a lake in one of the quarries and set up on the edge of the water with their equipment and fishing nets.<ref name="Andor11" />

While the pair talked and tended to their equipment, they were being watched by two [[human]] fugitives, [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] and [[Ruescott Melshi]], who had escaped from [[Narkina 5 Imperial Prison Complex|one]] of the Imperial facilities. Hoping to flee Narkina 5, the former [[prisoner]]s then rushed past the brothers toward the parked quadjumper in an attempt to [[Thief|steal]] it, but they were thrown to the floor when they triggered a [[sensor]] that caused sticky netting to be launched over them. Having simply watched the humans being captured, the brothers chuckled and then made their way over to the constricted men, who were reeled in toward them.<ref name="Andor11" />

===No friends of the Empire===
{{Quote|Kill anyone they would. Kill the water. Kill the squigglies. Care not a ''snod'' who they kill, ''haye?"<br />[Freedi speaks Narkinian]<br />"''Scob the Empire. They not be killing ye. ''Naye'' today. Wise to be jumping quickly, though. Where ye be looking to run now, eh?|Dewi and Freedi decide not to turn in the fugitives|<ref name="Andor11" />}}
[[File:PamularBrothers.png|thumb|left|300px|Freedi and Dewi worked together on Narkina 5 and decided to help Andor and Melshi escape from the moon.]]
Dewi pointed out that the Empire offered a [[Currency|reward]] of a thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for each prisoner returned to them and bemoaned the fact that the prisons had been spoiling the water, with Freedi regularly agreeing as his brother spoke. Claiming that they meant the brothers no harm, Melshi and Andor then pointed out that it was the Empire who had killed the fish, not them. Freedi then stated that the Empire would give the reward for the prisoners either dead or alive, causing Dewi to chuckle as Freedi extended a blade from his [[Limb|arm]].<ref name="Andor11" />

The prisoners began to plead with the brothers, but Dewi announced that the Empire would not be killing them today, so Freedi used his blade to deactivate the sensor, which released the humans from the netting. After Dewi suggested they leave quickly, Andor asked that they be taken to<ref name="Andor11" /> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Niamos">{{Databank|niamos|Niamos}}</ref> [[Niamos]].<ref name="Andor11" /> The brothers agreed to fly them there, with the four<ref name="Episode8Guide" /> taking off in the quadjumper and successfully escaping the moon before going their separate ways once they reached their destination.<ref name="Andor11" /> The [[artist]] [[Gammit Chond]] included a captioned illustration of Freedi and Dewi on a map of Narkina 5 that he created, which was published by the [[Graf Archive]] in an updated version of its ''[[Galactic Atlas]]'' at some point after the [[Battle of Exegol]] in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="AtlasUpdated">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|What? What is he saying? What is he saying?''"<br />"''He's saying, alive or dead, the offer goes.|Andor asks Dewi to translate Freedi's Narkinian words|audio=Andor-Dewi-AliveOrDead.flac|<ref name="Andor11" />}}
Freedi was a hulking<ref name="Trivia2" /> [[Color|beige]]-skinned [[Freedi Pamular's species|alien]]<ref name="Andor11" /> male with beefy limbs,<ref name="Databank" /> black [[eye]]s, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Andor11" /> He was the less chatty of the two brothers, who were not related by [[blood]],<ref name="Trivia2" /> and spoke [[Narkinian]] with Dewi.<ref name="Andor11Audio">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> Freedi shared his brother's dislike of the Empire as its prisons had caused the fish to die.<ref name="Episode8Guide" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|Looks like it's just these two.''"<br />"''They didn't walk here.|Melshi and Andor discuss the fact that Dewi and Freedi must have a starship|audio=Melshi-Andor-DidntWalk.flac|<ref name="Andor11" />}}
[[File:FreediRelease.png|thumb|right|250px|Freedi had an extendable blade.]]
Freedi had heavy implements capping his limbs, with his right hand being replaced by an extendable blade. These gave him the appearance of a formidable fighter, but in actual fact the blades were mostly used for chopping up the fish he and his brother caught.<ref name="Databank" />

The alien had a metal [[Bone|skull]] plate on the top of his head and wore a brown [[Clothing|shawl]], beige leggings, a gray [[belt]], a [[necklace]], and black-and-gray sandals. On their fishing trip, Freedi and his brother used various fishing equipment, a quadjumper, and a sensor that could launch retractable,<ref name="Andor11" /> sticky, organic-looking netting.<ref name="Andor11Audio" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|The most successful creatures I wrote were in season one when they escaped from Narkina 5, and then Cassian and Melshi see the two fisherman. I wrote this whole alien language for them, and they’re so great. People were like, "If you can do that, why aren't you doing more of it?" But it was a lot of work.|Tony Gilroy|<ref name="Vulture">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vulture.com/article/tony-gilroy-andor-star-wars-rogue-one-interview.html|text='Before Anyone Else Defines It, I'm Going to Define It' ''Andor'' creator Tony Gilroy reflects on five years inside the ''Star Wars'' universe.|author=VanArendonk, Kathryn|date=2025-05-16|work=[[Wikipedia:Vulture (website)|''Vulture'']]|archivedate=20250519093736}}</ref>}}
Freedi Pamular was portrayed by [[Liam Cook]] and voiced by [[Damian Farrell]] in the television show ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', appearing in the [[Andor Season 1|first season's]] eleventh episode, "[[Daughter of Ferrix]],"<ref name="Andor11" /> which aired on [[November 16]], [[2022]].<ref name="Release dates">{{Twitter|starwars|status/1557442379975573504|[[Star Wars]]|date=2022-8-10|quote=This is what revolution looks like. Watch #Andor, a Star Wars Original Series, streaming only on @DisneyPlus September 21.|archivedate=20220810191942}}</ref> The character's surname was not spoken in the episode but was listed in the credits.<ref name="Andor11" />

[[File:Freedi-Pamular-Sculpt.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Freedi Pamular's design was originally created for ''Rogue One''.]]
Freedi's design was originally created for<ref name="Trivia2" /> the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|Anthology Series]] film,<ref name="RO">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', but it did not make it into the final cut. There were also plans to show off the design during the [[Kessel]] sequence of<ref name="Trivia2" /> the [[2018]] Anthology Series film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', but ultimately it only appeared in in the film very briefly.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> The [[StarWars.com]] trivia gallery for "Daughter of Ferrix" erroneously states that Freedi's species did not make it into ''Solo'' at all.<ref name="Trivia2" /> Freedi's scene was photographed in Middle Peak Quarry in [[Wikipedia:Wirksworth|Wirksworth]], [[Wikipedia:Derbyshire|Derbyshire]] in the [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|United Kingdom]].<ref name="Trivia3">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e11-trivia-gallery|text="Daughter of Ferrix" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref>

The scene required more production meetings than almost any other in the series in order to establish how, when, and why they were going to shoot it and whether the tone could be as funny as they intended. Executive producer [[Tony Gilroy]] wanted it to be "[[Wikipedia:Sergio Leone|Sergio Leone]] meets the [[Wikipedia:Coen brothers|Coen brothers]]" and loved the final result despite the complexity that went into making it.<ref name="Gilroy">{{SWYouTube|_qP3CnChXgk|Andor Season 1 with Tony Gilroy LIVE &#124; Season 2 Coming April 22 on Disney+}}</ref> Gilroy wrote the brothers' dialogue for the scene, including the alien language elements. He was pleased with the result, considering it his best alien dialogue in the series, but felt that it took a lot of work.<ref name="Vulture" />
