The '''Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation''', or simply the '''Pre-Mor Authority''' or '''Preox-Morlana''', was a conglomerate based on the [[planet]] [[Morlana One]] in the [[Free Trade sector]] in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. 

During the [[Imperial Era]], it was aligned with the [[Galactic Empire]], and permitted to police areas under its jurisdiction with its private security assets. In [[5 BBY]], the Empire [[Takeover of the Preox-Morlana Corporate Sector|took over]] the [[Morlani system]] and liquidated the Preox-Morlana following an [[Ambush on Ferrix|incident]] on Ferrix.

==Description==
===Structure===
{{Quote|You may remind them that there's a Territorial Forum once a month where they are free to make official complaints.|[[Linus Mosk]]|<ref name="That Would Be Me" />}}
[[File:CorporateSecurityHQ-Andor-Kassa.png|thumb|left|250px|Corporate Security Headquarters was located on Morlana One.]]
The Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation was a corporate entity<ref name="Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> that maintained a degree of independence from the [[Galactic Empire]], but was still ultimately subservient to it.<ref name="Kassa" /> However, the Empire would encounter several issues when requesting a warrant in areas it operated in due to the Preox-Morlana corporate web.<ref name="Aldhani" /> The company's logo was written in [[Aurebesh]] and consisted of a stylized vertical P and M, the initials of the corporation.<ref name="Kassa" />

[[File:Preox-morlana-bulletin.png|thumb|right|250px|Preox-Morlana issued bulletins requesting information from its workers.]]
The Preox-Morlana Authority operated all services for its employees,<ref name="Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide" /> and required them to have an identification card at all times, so to allow private security forces to look up details about their background. Official complaints against the company could be filed once a [[Standard month|month]] through territorial forums as part of employee engagement, although in reality the organization was aware that they were too intimidated to do so and not even Pre-Mor Enforcement themselves took the forums seriously.<ref name="Reckoning" />

===Security===
{{Main|Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces}}
{{Quote|I've been saying all along we need a stronger hand with these affiliated planets. There's fomenting out there, sir. Pockets of fomenting. Corporate Tactical Forces are the Empire's first line of defense, and the best way to keep the blade sharp is to use it.|Linus Mosk|<ref name="That Would Be Me">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref>}}
[[File:SWU-SyrilWouldCryIfHeSawThis.png|thumb|left|250px|The Preox-Morlana Corporation maintained its own tactical forces for security and enforcement.]]
Preox-Morlana maintained the [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces]], which were [[Corporate Tactical Forces]] that operated across the [[Free Trade sector]] and protected its corporate assets. As part of [[Corporate Tactical Forces]], Pre-Mor Enforcement served as part of the Galactic Empire's overall defense. It consisted of [[officer]]s and security employees called [[Tac-Corpo]]s,<ref name="That Would Be Me">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref> known colloquially as "Blues."<ref name="Reckoning" /> and had a number of checkpoints across the [[sector]].<ref name="Kassa" /> Corpos acting in a guard role were identified as [[Preox-Morlana sentry guard]]s.<ref name="On Program">{{InsiderCite|220|On Program}}</ref>

Corporate-issue weapons, such as the [[B1-NA blaster pistol]], typically did not find their way into the hands of non-Preox-Morlana personnel, creating a sense of intrigue if one such weapon was claimed and actively used by a non-corporate actor.<ref name="The Axe Forgets">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}}</ref> The Pre-Mor Enforcement utilized armed [[BTY-180 carrier|personnel carriers]] and [[RK-392 mobile tac-pod|Mobile Tac-Pods]] to [[transport]] [[soldier]]s to different territories and [[star system]]s under the jurisdiction of Preox-Morlana.<ref name="Reckoning" /> Pre-Mor Enforcement [[Sergeant]] [[Linus Mosk]] viewed Pre-Mor Enforcement and other Corporate Tactical Forces as the [[Galactic Empire]]'s first line of defense, believing that a stronger hand with the [[planet]]s under Preox-Morlana jurisdiction was needed.<ref name="That Would Be Me" />

==History==
===Republic lobbying===
The Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation was founded by the [[High Republic Era]]. During this time of relative peace, the corporation became a notable charter member of the [[Galactic Corporate Policy League]], helping establish the [[Corporate Sector]].<ref name="AcolyteVG" />

===A rare calamity===
{{Quote|Two dedicated Pre-Mor employees caught in the sad orbit of a rare calamity.|[[Chief Inspector]] [[Hyne]]|<ref name="Kassa" />}}
After an unsuccessful attempt to [[Search for Kerri|gather information]] about his lost [[Kerri|sister]] in a [[Leisure Zone brothel|brothel]] in the [[Leisure Zone]] of [[Morlana One]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] was followed by Corpos [[Verlo Skiff]] and [[Kravas Drezzer]] into an alleyway, where an altercation occurred that resulted in the accidental [[death]] of Skiff, and intentional murder of Drezzer by Andor. Andor then fled the planet on a [[Breon HOY-39 Dayvan|Breon Dayvan]] [[transport]].<ref name="Kassa" />

[[File:Andor_Season_One_Episode_3.png|thumb|right|270px|Syril Karn's team of Corpos deploying to Ferrix.]]
Soon after, [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] took up the case and began investigating leads, creating a report for [[Chief Inspector]] [[Hyne]]. However, Hyne ordered Karn to drop the case and instead conjure it as a more heroic accident to minimize any Imperial attention to Preox-Morlana, and to benefit the corporation's public image. Hyne then left for the [[Imperial Regional Command]] for a report on Preox-Morlana crime rates. Despite the order to halt the investigation and generate an accident report, Karn decided to continue investigating the case, using different elements of Pre-Mor Security Inspection Team to track down the assailant. After filtering through [[starship]] data, Karn learned the suspect had gone to Ferrix, a world under Preox-Morlana jurisdiction but without heavy Pre-Mor presence, and commanded his underlings to search for a [[Kenari (people)|Kenari]] male on the planet.<ref name="Kassa" />

In a drunken bout of jealousy, [[Timm Karlo]] reported Andor to Preox-Morlana, alerting the Inspection Team to his criminal record. Karn brought a [[Unidentified brothel hostess|woman]] from the brothel in to verify the suspect, and was later visited by [[Sergeant]] [[Linus Mosk]], who joined the investigation with a keen intent of exercising the corporation's authority and power in catching Andor. The two assembled three Corpo teams, setting up an [[ambush on Ferrix]], designating the teams [[East Team]], [[North Team]], and [[West Team]]. The Corpos then deployed from their personnel carrier in Mobile Tac-Pods after a less-than-rousing speech from Karn.<ref name="That Would Be Me" />

Landing in different areas of the settlement, each team spread out to cover more ground. West Team moved to serve a warrant and search the [[Andor household|house]] of and question [[Maarva Carassi Andor|Maarva Andor]], Cassian Andor's adoptive [[Parent|mother]], where they learned of the location Andor was meeting with [[Luthen Rael]]. Moving out, the Corpos attempted to corner Andor and Rael but were fought and chased through the [[city]]. The Corpos also held up [[Bix Caleen]] and [[North-3]] killed Timm Karlo as he attempted to approach them. After a narrow escape, the ambush went awry, resulting in the death of multiple Corpos, the destruction of a Mobile Tac-Pod, and civilian unrest.<ref name="Reckoning">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>

===Independence revoked===
{{Quote|Take solace in this. You will not be replaced. You've rung the final bell on Corporative independence.|Supervisor Blevin, to Syril Karn|<ref name="Aldhani" />}}
[[File:Morlana-Gone-Andor104.png|thumb|left|290x290px|Inspector Blevin reprimands the Pre-Mor officers responsible for the Ferrix disaster]]
Following the incident, the Galactic Empire used the debacle as a pretext for the [[Takeover of the Preox-Morlana Corporate Sector|revocation]] of corporative independence and liquidated Preox-Morlana. All workers were ordered to surrender their [[comlink]]s, [[weapon]]s, and [[scandoc]]s to the Empire.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref> The upper ranks of the Preox-Morlana company were outright arrested<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> while Hyne, Mosk, Karn were reprimanded by [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[Inspector]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Blevin]] on Morlana One and told that they were to be dispatched to a transfer center, where they would have their personal effects handed to them. Blevin also ordered them to certify confirmation of the official ISB [[after-action report]] detailing their involvement in the "Ferrix Incident." Karn himself was further stripped of his duties and left utterly disgraced by his superiors.<ref name="Aldhani" />

On Ferrix, Imperial Security Bureau began to clear damage caused by the fighting during the incident, and clear out guests and furniture from a hotel, which the ISB had requisitioned as their new headquarters.<ref name="The Axe Forgets">{{Andor|The Axe Forgets}}</ref>

===Imperial transition===
{{Quote|Still a few Pre-Mor holdouts working in the office helping with the Imperial transition. My old partner is still working the HQ night desk.|Linus Mosk|<ref name="Andor11">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref>}}
The Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation was undergoing a transition from private to state ownership by the time [[Maarva's funeral procession]] was being planned, with some employees remaining at their posts to aid in the transition, including Linus Mosk's former partner who still retained their position at night desk at the HQ.<ref name="Andor11">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref>

==Astrography==
{{Quote|I've been saying all along we need a stronger hand with these affiliated planets. There's fomenting out there, sir. Pockets of fomenting. Corporate Tactical Forces are the Empire's first line of defense, and the best way to keep the blade sharp is to use it.|Linus Mosk|<ref name="That Would Be Me">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref>}}
Preox-Morlana controlled the [[Free Trade sector]] under the Galactic Empire supervision, and was [[Capital|headquartered]] on Morlana One and [[Morlana Four|Four]] and had a number of checkpoints across the [[sector]]. While Ferrix was under Preox-Morlana control, it was not regularly patrolled, rather being left to its own devices.<ref name="Kassa" />
