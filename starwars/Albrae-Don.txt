'''Albrae-Don''' was a [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> in the [[Albrae-Don system]] in the [[Sern sector]] of the [[Colonies]], in grid square L-13 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the [[Imperial Era]], Albrae-Don was a rebellious world along with other worlds in the Sern [[sector]] such as [[Ghorman]].<ref name="Dawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Albrae-Don was mentioned in the [[2023]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and [[Emily Shkoukani]].<ref name="Dawn" /> The [[Albrae-Don/Legends|world]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the "[[Galaxywide NewsNets]]" feature of [[Star Wars Adventure Journal 10|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 10]], which was written by [[Paul Sudlow]] and published by [[West End Games]]<ref name="GNN">{{JournalCite|10|Galaxywide NewsNets}}</ref> in [[May]] [[1996]].<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars Adventure Journal 10|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 10]]</ref>
