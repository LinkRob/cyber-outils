'''Candied seaweed chips''' were a type of [[seaweed]] [[chips]]<ref name="TOTF" />&mdash;a snack [[Foodstuff|food]]<ref name="PlanetX">''[[The High Republic: Quest for Planet X]]''</ref>&mdash;that was [[Diet|edible]] to [[human]]s. While in the [[San Tekka compound]] on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]<ref name="TOTF" /> in [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> the chips were served at a meal for [[Nihil]] [[General]] [[Abediah Viess]]&mdash;who had forcibly taken over the residence following the departure of its [[Gender|patriarchs]], [[Vellis San Tekka|Vellis]] and [[Marlowe San Tekka|Marlowe]]&mdash;and her [[Prisoner|captive]], [[Cair San Tekka]], a younger member of the [[San Tekka clan|family]] who engaged in anti-Nihil resistance activities. Though Cair was reluctant to dine with Viess, he [[Brain|mentally]] lamented that refusing the food would not be a smart decision for his physical well-being.<ref name="TOTF" />
