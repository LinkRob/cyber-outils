'''Agia''' was the [[Wookiee]] [[Deity|goddess]] of nature. In the settlement of [[Kachirho]], on the Wookiee home [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], there was a shrine to Agia,  situated near the top of [[Tree Vikkilynn]] and maintained by the local [[Tree Carers]].<ref name="CLNE" />
