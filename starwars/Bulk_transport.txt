'''Bulk transports''' were [[transport]] [[starship]]s capable of moving large amounts of cargo. The early [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|navy]] consisted of bulk transports that had previously been used by the various member corporations to move their wares across [[the galaxy]]. However, as the [[Clone Wars]] continued, [[Pammant Docks]] and other facilities manufactured [[capital ship]]s to make a credible navy.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Bulk transports were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Rise of the Separatists]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''Star Wars'' roleplaying products.<ref name="Rise" /> They were originally created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in [[Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)|''Twin Stars of Kira'']], a [[1993]] supplement to ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]].<ref name="TSOK">[[Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)|''Twin Stars of Kira'']]</ref>
