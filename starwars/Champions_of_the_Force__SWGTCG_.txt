'''''Champions of the Force''''' is the first set of [[Star Wars Galaxies Trading Card Game]] released on [[August 26]], [[2008]], which includes more than 300 cards of all types, including 30 Loot cards.

The story mode of this set, represented in [[Wookieepedia:SWGTCG/scenarios#CHAMPIONS_OF_THE_FORCE|'''Scenarios''']], revolves around four '''avatar''' characters and their quests to find an ancient [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] [[Holocron/Legends|holocron]], the [[Codex of Tython]]. The Jedi [[Rachi Sitra]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Coret Bhan]] are trying to find the pieces of the artifact, while the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Jeffren Brek]] and the [[Sith/Legends|Sith]] [[Namman Cha]] are trying to find it so as to use it as a weapon.

The '''Heroic encounter''' mode of this set recreates the [[Battle of Nym's Factory Compound]], revolving around a group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] trying to shut down the droid factory overrun by [[IG-88B|IG-88]], who sends [[Battle droid/Legends|Battle droids]] and [[Droideka/Legends|Droidekas]] to deal with them and ensure the success of his [[Droid Revolution]].
