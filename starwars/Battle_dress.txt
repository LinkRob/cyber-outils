'''Battle dress''' was a type of [[Clothing|combat attire]] worn by the [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen]] of [[Naboo]] and her [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]]. They could be layered with another dress or worn over trousers. The dresses had protective features, such as [[Blaster|blast]]-damping fabric and [[concussive absorbing material]], but when Naboo was peaceful, the garments were largely ceremonial.

[[Queen]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] had multiple styles of battle dress in her extensive [[Padmé Amidala's wardrobe|wardrobe]]. During the [[Invasion of Naboo]] in [[32 BBY]], uniform [[Color|maroon]] and gold battle dresses were worn by Amidala and four of her handmaidens; the black and red royal battle dress was worn by the Queen's decoy, [[Sabé]], along with traditional royal makeup. Although Sabé initially presented herself as the Queen using this attire in the first diplomatic meeting with the [[Gungan]]s, Amidala soon stepped forward to reveal the truth. Thanks in part to their battle dresses, Amidala and Sabé fooled [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]] long enough to secure the [[Throne room (Theed Royal Palace)|throne room]] of [[Theed Royal Palace]] during the [[Battle of Naboo]].

Amidala continued to wear the handmaiden's battle dress during her reign. While serving in the [[Galactic Senate]] during the [[Clone Wars]], [[Senator]] Amidala wore a modified version for a [[Skirmish on Naboo|skirmish]] on her [[homeworld]] and in other events.

==Description==
[[File:PadmeDecoy-SWI176.png|thumb|left|200px|Padmé Amidala in the handmaiden's style of battle dress]]
Battle dress was a type of [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] [[clothing]] that was worn by certain members of the [[Royal House of Naboo]],<ref name="TCVD NE" /> as the dress offered mobility while in combat.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> It became largely ceremonial during many [[Standard year|years]] of peace on the [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="TCVD NE" />

The [[Monarch of Naboo|Queen's]] royal battle dress<ref name="TCVD NE" /> included a broad, [[Color|black]] waistband fastened over a textured black surcoat that was layered over a long, red dress made of [[Blaster|blast]]-damping fabric.<ref name="CE UE" /> It was worn with a sturdy pair of [[boot]]s and a headdress held in place with pins. The incorporated [[wig]]<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> was split into two large bundles of rolled [[hair]] on either side of the head, with each encircled by a pair of thick red bands, extending from a large [[metal]] headband.<ref name="Episode I" />

[[Naboo Royal Handmaidens]] wore battle dresses as their uniforms in combat. The uniform included a maroon dress<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> made of dark [[velvet]] with a lighter-colored [[satin]] finish on the lining. The side seams of the dress, the hem of the interior lining, and the trousers had gold trim stripes<ref name="Shadow Warrior Concept Art" /> that were bright enough to see at night.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> The coat-like dress had billowing sleeves layered over ones that were fitted, and the trousers were tucked into knee-high black boots. The handmaidens also wore a belt with a metal buckle and armed themselves with blasters.<ref name="Episode I" />

==History==
{{Quote|Viceroy! Your occupation here has ended.|Sabé, tricking Viceroy Nute Gunray by wearing the royal battle dress|<ref name="Episode I" />}}
[[File:PadmeAmidala-TCWCEJtB.png|thumb|right|180px|As a senator, Amidala wore a modified handmaiden's style of battle dress.]]
[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s extensive [[Padmé Amidala's wardrobe|wardrobe]] included both the royal attire and the version worn by her handmaidens.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> Amidala's multiple battle dresses became necessary when she was [[Queen]] during the [[Invasion of Naboo]]<ref name="Episode I" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> For the first diplomatic meeting with the [[Gungan]]s at the [[Gungan Sacred Place]], [[Sabé]] acted as the Queen's decoy, wearing the royal battle dress with the Queen's [[scar of remembrance]] and other makeup, while Amidala wore the same uniform as the other handmaidens:<ref name="Episode I" /> [[Eirtaé]], [[Rabé Tonsort|Rabé]], [[Saché]], and [[Yané]]. Rabé added hair ties made from [[concussive absorbing material]] that was taken from the lining of another dress to provide the handmaiden version with limited protection from a heavy blow or a falling debris.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

Although Amidala revealed her true identity to secure [[Boss]] [[Rugor Nass]]'s trust,<ref name="Episode I" /> Sabé and Amidala had a serious discussion about continuing to wear the disguises to take back [[Theed]] from the [[Trade Federation]] with the full knowledge that Sabé would be risking her life.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> During the [[Battle of Naboo]], the battle dresses successfully confused the [[Neimoidian]] [[Viceroy]] [[Nute Gunray]]. When Sabé arrived outside the [[Throne room (Theed Royal Palace)|throne room]] of [[Theed Royal Palace]], looking more like the image of the Queen in that moment than the actual Amidala, she drew attention to herself to lure away the [[battle droid]]s. Amidala was then able to secure her throne room alongside the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]].<ref name="Episode I" />

Later during her reign, Amidala used the handmaiden's battle dress when a [[sando aqua monster]] disturbed the [[royal boathouse]].<ref name="Monster Misunderstanding">{{FOD|Monster Misunderstanding}}</ref> It remained in her wardrobe as the [[Senator]] of Naboo. During the [[Clone Wars]], she wore it for a mission with her [[Marriage|husband]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], that was unexpectedly joined by his [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]].<ref name="Unexpected Company">{{FOD|Unexpected Company}}</ref> She also donned a version with sleeves gathered at the wrists for a [[Skirmish on Naboo|skirmish]] on her [[homeworld]].<ref name="Shadow Warrior">{{TCW|Shadow Warrior}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Battle dresses were created for the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="Episode I" /> Artist [[Iain McCaig]] conceptualized the designs and the finished costumes were realized by costume designer [[Trisha Biggar]] and her department. McCaig's initial concept featured straps across the belly, but they were removed from subsequent concepts as they would be awkward and uncomfortable to wear in reality.<ref name="The Art of Episode I">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref>

The Queen's battle dress was initially designed for Amidala herself, but was instead worn by [[Keira Knightley]], who was cast as Sabé based on her resemblance to [[Natalie Portman]].<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]''</ref> It took one person over one month to complete the finished costume. [[Wikipedia:Bombyx mori|Silkworm]] [[Wikipedia:Pupa#Cocoon|pods]] from [[Wikipedia:India|India]] were woven into a [[Wikipedia:Silk|silk]] net, removed at the top of the costume, and then individually stitched back on, creating the shape of the shoulders.<ref name="Costume Design">{{SWArchive|url=episode-i/feature/19990501/indexp10.html|text=''Star Wars'' Episode I: Production Notes: Costume Design}}</ref>

The [[in-universe]] clothing items were both identified as "battle dress" in [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] reference materials such as ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]'',<ref name="Legends TCVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> but the new [[canon]] version of ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]'' only identifies the version worn by Sabé as "battle dress" and omits<ref name="TCVD NE" /> the page that had explicitly identified the handmaiden version.<ref name="Legends TCVD" /> The novel ''[[Queen's Peril]]'' uses the descriptions "maroon dresses" and "maroon and gold battle uniform" for the handmaidens.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> ''[[Queen's Shadow]]'' mentions "the battle dress," but does not specify which outfit the term is referring to.<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref> For the purposes of this article, it has been assumed that the Legends term still applies to both sets of attire, and an erroneous hyphen used in ''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]'' has been omitted.
