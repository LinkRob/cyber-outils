'''''Clone Trooper Blaster Game''''' is a [[Star Wars video games|video game]] created by [[Tiger Electronics]], developed by [[Santa Cruz Games]], and released in [[2008]]. The game comes with a controller in the style of a [[DC-15S/Legends|DC-15S]] [[Blaster carbine/Legends|blaster carbine]] that plugs into the television.<ref name="CTBG" />
