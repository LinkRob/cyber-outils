'''Ferrix Depot''' was a business located on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]]. An [[Aurebesh]] sign on the building stated the business' name,<ref name="Andor 12" /> with the words "[[Speeder|SPEEDERS]] AVAILABLE<ref name="Andor 1" /> HERE"<ref name="Andor 3" /> written in smaller letters underneath.<ref name="Andor 12" /> The top of a wall on the outside of the building was painted with text written in [[Bazeese script]], but it only produced gibberish when transliterated.<ref name="Andor 1" /><ref name="Andor 12" /> The depot was located on [[Rix Road]], next to the [[Ferrix bell tower]]<ref name="Andor 12" /> and [[Brasso]]'s workplace;<ref name="Andor 1" /> near [[Hotel Rix|the hotel]], [[Gavalox Communications]], and a [[Unidentified chair store|store selling chairs]].<ref name="Andor 12" />
