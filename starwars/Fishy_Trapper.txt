The '''Fishy Trapper''' was a costume owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Cunning Trapper|standard costume]]. A [[fish]] [[Bone|skeleton]] was suspended around the chest of this outfit.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fishy Trapper was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Charr, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Fishy Scatter Gun]].<ref name="Hunters" />
