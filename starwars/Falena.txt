'''Falena''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[human]] from [[Alderaan]].<ref name="Eclipse" /> By [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> she was the youngest attendant to Her Majesty [[Breha Organa]], [[Alderaanian monarchy|Queen]] of Alderaan. As such, she was allowed in the [[queen]]'s apartments, where she would notably practice at her lute.<ref name="Eclipse" />
