'''Felucian Gardenleaf''' was a type of [[plant]] from<ref name="Cruiser" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Felucia]]. Its [[Color|green]] leaves were used in [[Smoked Redfish Littletubes]], a [[Foodstuff|dish]] that was edible to [[human]]s and served in the [[Sublight Lounge]] on board the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''<ref name="Cruiser" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] are dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Felucian Gardenleaf is part of a dish available at the [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] hotel in [[Wikipedia:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]], Florida,<ref name="Cruiser" /> which opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]].<ref name="DPB First Voyages">{{DPB|url=2021/09/first-voyages-on-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-begin-march-1-2022-general-bookings-open-oct-28-2021|text=First Voyages on Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser Begin March 1, 2022; General Bookings Open Oct. 28, 2021}}</ref>
