The '''Dolgarak temple'''<ref name="TriviaGallery5">{{SW|url=journey-to-the-dark-head-trivia-gallery|text="Journey to the Dark Head" Trivia Gallery &#124; Star Wars Visions Volume 2}} {{C|Slide 5}}</ref> was a structure located on the [[planet]] [[Dolgarak]]. It was situated at the base of the planet's two [[statues of prophecy]], which represented the [[Light side of the Force|light]] and [[Dark side of the Force|dark]] aspects of [[the Force]].<ref name="JDH" /> It contained the Dolgarak [[library]]<ref name="JDH Focus" /> that wrapped around the hexagonal base of the temple, documenting visions that<ref name="JDH" /> the [[Force Oracles]]<ref name="JDH Focus" /> such as [[Ara]] saw in the stones at the temple base when they were exposed to [[Water|rainfall]].<ref name="JDH" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:TripitakaKoreana.jpg|thumb|right|250px|In writing "Journey to the Dark Head," Chung Se Rang was inspired by the real-world ''Tripiṭaka Koreana'', which bears similarities to the library at the Dolgarak temple.]]
Screenwriter [[Chung Se Rang]] of "[[Journey to the Dark Head]]" was particularly inspired by the [[Wikipedia:Tripitaka Koreana|''Tripiṭaka Koreana'']],<ref name="JDH Focus" /> a collection of more than eighty thousand 13th-century [[Wikipedia:Woodblock printing|wooden blocks]] engraved by [[Wikipedia:Buddhist canons|Buddhist scriptures]] that is stored at the [[Wikipedia:Haeinsa|Haeinsa]] temple in [[Wikipedia:South Korea|South Korea]],<ref name="TripitakaBritannica">{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/topic/Haein-Temple|text=Haein Temple &#124; South Korea, Buddhist, & Architecture|author=The Editors of Encyclopædia Britannica|date=2024-03-29|work=[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|''Encyclopædia Britannica'']]|archivedate=20250717073731}}</ref> with the temple on Dolgarak in the finished film also featuring a library of [[wood]]en blocks with [[writing]] on them.<ref name="JDH" />
