An '''ambush''' occurred at the [[Jedi Temple (Ilum)|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Ilum]] against [[Jedi Master]] [[Luminara Unduli]] and [[Padawan]] [[Barriss Offee]] by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. Four [[Standard month|months]] after the start of the [[Clone Wars]] between the Separatists and the [[Galactic Republic]], Unduli temporarily relinquished command of her [[army]] to bring Offee to Ilum in a [[meditation]] exercise to build Offee's [[Barriss Offee's lightsaber|last]] [[lightsaber]]. During the exercise, Separatist [[chameleon droid]]s attacked and almost destroyed [[Jedi temple|Temple]] there, burying the two under rubble.<ref name="Force Users" />

Offee and Unduli were rescued by [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]. After the ambush, Offee conducted more and more solo missions, working on occasion with her master.<ref name="Force Users" />
