The '''''Arquitens''-class 546''', also known as the '''Class 546 Cruiser''', was a modified model of [[Arquitens-class command cruiser|''Arquitens''-class command cruiser]] primarily designed for greater speed under the [[Class 546 program]].<ref name="Battles that Changed the Galaxy" /> A Class 546 [[Cruiser]] was used by an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]]. Although similar to the command cruiser model, the Class 546 housed a large internal [[hangar]] bay that could be accessed by port and starboard hangar doors and a launch tube at the center of the vessel, along the spine of the vessel<ref name="Chapter 16" />  Designated as a [[Lightspeed Cruiser]]<ref name="Chapter 15" />, its [[Bridge (starship)|bridge]] was also different from the standard ''Arquitens''-class models,<ref name="Chapter 16" /> and was equipped with three primary and four secondary [[sublight engine]]s.<ref name="Chapter 14" />

The [[Gideon's light cruiser|light cruiser]] that served as the command ship of Moff Gideon was a Class 546 Cruiser.<ref name="Chapter 15" /> The vessel participated in the [[Battle of Tython (9 ABY)|Battle of Tython]] where it destroyed the ''[[Razor Crest]]'', a [[gunship]] owned by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="Chapter 14" /> It was later boarded by a team led by Djarin and it included [[Lady]] [[Bo-Katan Kryze]], her companion [[Koska Reeves]], [[Carasynthia Dune]], and [[Fennec Shand]] in an effort to [[Second rescue of Grogu|rescue]] the [[Force-sensitive]] child, [[Din Grogu|Grogu]].<ref name="Chapter 16" />
