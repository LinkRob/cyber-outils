A type of '''scream''' of [[the Force]] was a [[Force power|Force exertion]] that could unleash a negative psychic and physiological impact on other beings and was thus associated with the [[dark side of the Force]]. Sometime between [[19 BBY]] and [[14 BBY]], [[Tualon Yaluna]], a [[Torture|broken]] former [[Jedi]] recently inducted into the [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitorus]], involuntarily used a Force scream on fellow [[Inquisitor]] [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] during a sparring session.

==Description==
A Force scream took the form of a physical scream of strong frustration or anger emitted [[Force power|through]] [[the Force]]. The former [[Jedi Knight]] [[Tualon Yaluna]]'s use of a Force scream caused his opponent, [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]], to stumble the ground with her whole body shaking violently and her organs feeling as if they were exploding. Such an ability was thus associated with the [[dark side of the Force]].<ref name="Inquisitor" />

==History==
[[File:RiseofRedBlade-Inquisitor-Iskat.png|thumb|Iskat Akaris (pictured) was subjected to a Force scream by Tualon Yaluna.]]
Sometime between [[19 BBY]] and [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'' establishes that [[Tualon Yaluna]] and [[Thirteenth Sister|Iskat Akaris]] sparred at the original [[Inquisitorius Headquarters]] between the events of [[Darth Vader (2017) 7|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 7]] and [[Darth Vader (2017) 19| ''Darth Vader'' (2017) 19]], which corresponds to between [[19 BBY]] and [[14 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Yaluna was [[Torture|broken]] in a [[Interrogation chair|torture chair]] by the [[Inquisitorius]] and inducted into their ranks. While sparring with his former friend and ex-Jedi Knight, the [[Inquisitor]] Iskat Akaris, at [[Inquisitorius Headquarters]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], Yaluna's shoulder was injured by her [[Thirteenth Sister's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. In response, he unleashed a Force scream that caused his opponent to collapse to the ground in a catatonic state. Akaris could still feel the physical effects sometime after she managed to stand back up while her counterpart took some time to recover enough mentally to respond to her questions.<ref name="Inquisitor" />

Yaluna explained that the scream felt like it had been pent up inside him his whole life and marked the first time that he had let go. Akaris affirmed the sentiment, declaring that doing so had allowed Yaluna to embrace his true self and that it felt good to let go. She noted to herself that Yaluna's ability to use the scream must have been unlocked by his newfound connection to the dark side of the Force and how she liked the aggressive change it marked in his personality and dueling style.<ref name="Inquisitor" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:InquisitorRiseoftheRedBlade-cover.jpg|thumb|right|180px|''Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade'' marked the first appearance of a scream of the Force in the current ''Star Wars'' canon.]]
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], a scream of the Force appeared in [[Delilah S. Dawson]]'s novel ''[[Inquisitor: Rise of the Red Blade]]'', which was published in [[2023]].<ref name="Inquisitor" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Force scream/Legends|ability]] first appeared in ''[[The Last Command]]'', the final novel of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s original [[Star Wars: The Thrawn Trilogy|Thrawn Trilogy]], which was published in [[1993]].<ref name="The Last Command">''[[The Last Command]]''</ref> It was first identified as a Force scream in ''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]'', a reference book written by [[Eric S. Trautmann|Eric Trautmann]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''<ref name="TLCSourcebook">''[[The Last Command Sourcebook]]''</ref> that was published in [[March]] [[1994]].<ref name="Adventure Journal ">[[Star Wars Adventure Journal 1|''Star Wars Adventure Journal'' 1]]</ref> A Force scream was later depicted for the first time in the [[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 3|third issue]] of the 1994 [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith]]''. The issue was written by [[Tom Veitch]] and [[Kevin J. Anderson]], illustrated by [[Chris Gossett]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="DLotS3">[[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 3|''Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith'' 3]]</ref> on [[December 13]], 1994.<ref name="PublishDate">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54573/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_-_dark_lords_of_the_sith_1994_3|text=Star Wars: Tales Of The Jedi - Dark Lords Of The Sith (1994) #3}}</ref>
