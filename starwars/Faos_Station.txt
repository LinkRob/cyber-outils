'''Faos Station''' was a civilian [[space station]]<ref name="ITNOTR" /> in the [[Belsmuth sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text="Narkina 5" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref> an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[freighter]] carrying a large [[kyber crystal]], posing as a standard civilian freighter, was [[Mission to Faos Station|infiltrated]] by [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] [[Saw Gerrera]], [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] during a brief stop at Faos Station.<ref name="ITNOTR" />

{{Station-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The station was originally going to be named Taos Station. The name change came too late to alter the surface texture of the model seen in [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[hologram]], which has "TAOS" printed in [[Aurebesh]] on its surface.<ref name="Trivia Gallery">{{SW|url=series/star-wars-rebels/in-the-name-of-the-rebellion-trivia-gallery|text="In the Name of the Rebellion" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 9}}</ref>
