A '''con artist''' was an individual who tricked other individuals out of their [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]], secrets or information by way of lying, cheating or defrauding.<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>

During the [[Republic Era|waning]] [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Dannl Faytonni]] was a con artist who sometimes wore fake military gear to disguise [[Gender|himself]].<ref name="Complete Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> [[Tak]] was as a con artist who [[Thief|robbed]] the elderly on [[Coruscant]] before he tried to rob the [[Unidentified Princess of Kessel|Princess]] of [[Kessel]] and was sent to work at the [[spice mines of Kessel]].<ref name="Solo Guide">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> [[Ezra Bridger]] used to be a con artist and ran scams for survival on [[Lothal]] before joining the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]''. [[Haja Estree]] was a con artist operating on [[Daiyu]]. While he did help [[Force-sensitive]] individuals escape the [[Galactic Empire]] by way of the [[Hidden Path]], he conned them out of credits while pretending to be a [[Jedi]].<ref name="OWK">{{Kenobi|Part II}}</ref> The [[Ubdurian]] brothers [[Prashee]] and [[Cratinus]], who frequented [[Takodana Castle]], were con artists who used their identical appearances to their advantage whilst [[gambling]].<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" />
