The '''Contrast Chair''' was the collective name of a custom [[Repulsorlift chair|repulsorlift]] [[Command Chair]] and a pair of [[Blaster Remote]]s owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Command Chair (Sprocket)|standard chair and remote set]]. Both the chair and the [[renote]]s shared a [[Color|cream]], red, black, dark gray, and silver color scheme, with visible wear on the chair, while the remotes had blue tips on their [[Antenna|antennae]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Contrast Chair was an "Epic" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Sprocket's Command Chair, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Slick Contrast]].<ref name="Hunters" />
