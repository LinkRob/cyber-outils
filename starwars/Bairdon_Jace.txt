'''Bairdon Jace''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] and was the [[Padawan]] of [[Khaat Qiyn]]. In [[22 BBY]], Jace fought in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the opening battle of the [[Clone Wars]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Bairdon Jace<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who was the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Khaat Qiyn]].

[[File:KhaatBairdon-2017DarthMaul1.png|thumb|left|220px|Bairdon Jace with his master Khaat Qiyn on Coruscant]]
Prior to the [[Invasion of Naboo]], Jace was touring [[Coruscant]] with Qiyn when they both sensed the cold presence of [[Maul|Darth Maul]], who was secretly tracking them from afar. Jace was unsure if what they sensed through [[the Force]] was true, and he asked his master if their senses could have been betraying them. As the two [[Jedi]] walked away together, Qiyn warned her Padawan that the shadows they had sensed held great rage that could swallow him whole in the mind, and that he should always trust in the Force.<ref name="DM1" />

===Battle of Geonosis===
In 22 BBY, he [[First Battle of Geonosis|accompanied]] his master to [[Geonosis]] as part of the [[Jedi assault team]] to rescue [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. He stormed the arena along with his master, and many comrades.<ref name="Episode II" />

==Personality and traits==
Bairdon Jace had [[Color|gray]] [[hair]], brown [[eye]]s, and light skin. After sensing [[Darth]] Maul, he thought he could not trust the Force.<ref name="DM1" />

==Powers and abilities==
Bairdon Jace was skilled in [[lightsaber combat]].<ref name="Episode II" />

==Equipment==
Bairdon Jace wielded a blue-bladed [[lightsaber]] and wore [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]].<ref name="Episode II" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bairdon Jace first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']].<ref name="Episode II" />

Jace was identified in the [[2018]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''.<ref name="CVD" />
