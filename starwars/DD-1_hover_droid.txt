The '''DD-1 hover droid'''<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was a type of [[training droid]], sometimes customized and utilized by the [[Jedi Order]] to teach [[Jedi Initiate]]s on [[Tenoo]]. Younglings training at the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]] dueled against these fast-moving [[droid]]s to hone their acrobatics and fighting skills against the droids' quadruple [[electroblade training sword]]s.<ref name="AotTD" />
