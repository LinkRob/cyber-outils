'''Corellian rules''' were a rule-set for the game of [[grav-ball]] originating from [[Corellia]]. Under these rules, the game was played indoors, but like on [[Lothal]], [[hover boots]] were not worn during play. Corellian rules were used on [[Corulag]].<ref name="EotG" />
