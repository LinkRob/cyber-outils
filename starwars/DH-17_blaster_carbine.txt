The '''DH-17 blaster carbine''' was a model of [[blaster carbine]], and a heavier version of the [[DH-17 blaster pistol]]. It was heavily used during the [[Galactic Civil War]] by the [[Imperial Navy]] and the [[Rebel Alliance Navy]]'s [[Alderaanian consular security|marines]] during [[Starship|shipboard]] skirmishes. It was known for its versatility, durability, and simplicity.<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

The DH-17 carbine was capable of penetrating most [[Armor|personal armors]], but it couldn't penetrate a ship's [[hull]], which made it ideal for battles onboard starships. It could fire automatically and its parts were easily found on the [[black market]]. Rebel [[marine]]s would often install [[spread barrel]]s onto the weapon, turning it into a deck sweeper known as the [[DH-17 short carbine/Canon|DH-17 short carbine]].<ref name="Forged in Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
The DH-17 blaster carbine was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Forged in Battle]]'',<ref name="Forged in Battle" /> a [[2016]] sourcebook for the [[Star Wars: Age of Rebellion (roleplaying game)|''Star Wars: Age of Rebellion'']] [[roleplaying game]] published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Soldier On!">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2016/8/17/soldier-on-1|text=Soldier On!}}</ref> The blaster carbine originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']], a [[Roleplaying game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] developed by [[Sony]] and published by [[LucasArts]],<ref name="SWG">{{GalaxiesAED}}</ref> prior to its closure on [[December 15]], [[2011]].<ref name="Shutdown">{{SonyCite|url=playerinfo|text=IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT ''STAR WARS'' GALAXIES™}}</ref>
