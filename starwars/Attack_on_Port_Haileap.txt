The '''Attack on Port Haileap''' was carried out by [[Nihil]] [[pirate]]s from [[Kara Xoo's Tempest]] who destroyed, raided, and looted the [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Haileap]]. The marauders also broke into a [[Glenna Kip's lab|lab]] and kidnapped kid inventor [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]] who was working there with [[scientist]] [[Glenna Kip]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Jorinda Boffrey]] led Jedi and civilians in fighting against the attackers, with [[Jedi Knight]] [[Kohta Jarik]] flying a [[Jedi Vector]] in the fight, but the Nihil quickly departed, though not before causing massive damage to [[Port Haileap]].<ref name="Mission to Disaster />
