"'''Dream on Space Pirate'''" was a [[music|song]] performed by the [[band]] [[The Wizards Are Alright]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> During [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> it was performed on [[Renpalli Station]] in the [[Toshaal system]].<ref name="Outlaws" />  By around the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> the song was available on the jukebox at [[Hauler's Keep]], a [[space station]] in the [[Wild Space]] [[Khepi system]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Dream on Space Pirate" was created as an [[in-universe]] song by [[Cody Matthew Johnson]] for the [[2024]] [[Ubisoft]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''.<ref name="Outlaws" /> It was released in the game's soundtrack ''[[Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)]]''.<ref name="Songs from the Underworld">{{OST|Songs from the Underworld (Original Music from Star Wars Outlaws)}}</ref>
