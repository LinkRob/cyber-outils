'''Aurra Sing''', also known by the name "'''Nashtah'''," was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Gender|female]] [[Palliduvan]] who left the [[Jedi Order]] to take up the life of a [[bounty hunter]]. Operating prior to and during the [[Clone Wars]], she was present on [[Tatooine]], where she watched the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]], during the [[Trade Federation]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. During this [[time]], she also became acquainted with [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[captain]], [[Hondo Ohnaka]]. After the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars and the [[death]] of fellow [[bounty]] hunter [[Jango Fett]], Sing trained Fett's son, [[Boba Fett]], and helped him in an unsuccessful attempt to kill [[Jedi Master]] [[Mace Windu]], who killed Jango on [[Geonosis]]. After parting ways with Boba, Sing took jobs from [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro the Hutt]] and [[Cad Bane]]. Sing's long and storied career, however, ultimately came to an end with her death at the hands of [[Tobias Beckett]].

===The Hunt for Phaedra===
{{Quote|You stole from the wrong mark this time, Phaedra.|Aurra to Phaedra|<ref name="Jedi Knights 4">[[Jedi Knights 4|''Jedi Knights'' 4]]</ref>}}
[[File:ZamWesellAurraSingBaneMalarPhaedraNarShadaa-JediKnights4.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Aurra, Zam, and Bane cornering Phaedra]]
Prior to [[34 BBY]],<ref name="JediKnightsDate">The events of ''Jedi Knights'' 4 take place between [[40 BBY]] and [[34 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:JediKnightsDate|here]].</ref> the criminal mastermind [[Phaedra]] [[Robbery of the Galactic Bank of Coruscant|stole]] 10,000 [[InterGalactic Banking Clan|IBC]] [[Credits]] from [[Senator]] [[Beka]], unaware the funds were being held illegally for the crime lord [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]]. Upon discovering the theft, Jabba placed a large dead-or-alive bounty. Sing, with fellow bounty hunters [[Zam Wesell]] and [[Bane Malar]], pursued Phaedra to [[Nar Shaddaa]]. Before cornering her, the trio agreed to split the bounty equally.<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />

After Phaedra threatened to detonate [[Phaedra's shuttle|her shuttle]] with remote-controlled [[Thermal detonator|thermal detonators]], the bounty hunters were interrupted by the arrival of [[Jedi Master|Jedi Masters]] [[Shaak Ti]] and [[Qui-Gon Jinn]]. Seizing the distraction, Phaedra boarded a nearly identical [[Phaedra's decoy shuttle|decoy]] of her shuttle. As her droid [[Arjee-Entoo]] piloted the original shuttle away, the bounty hunters rushed back to their starships. The Jedi, recognizing the deception, abandoned their pursuit.<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />

While chasing Phaedra into space, Wesell destroyed the fleeing decoy. After reminding the group that the mission wasn't complete until they received their reward from Jabba, Sing and the group traveled to [[Tatooine]], unaware Phaedra had fled to [[Ord Mantell]].<ref name="Jedi Knights 4" />
===Xav Xrexus's Auction===
{{Quote|I have a bad feeling about this, Bane. Our employer seems capable enough. Why does he need us?|Aurra, to Cad Bane on Darth Maul|<ref name="Darth Maul II" />}}
[[File:MaulSingBaneVorhdeilo-2017DarthMaul2.png|thumb|left|180px|Sing meets the Sith Lord Darth Maul]]
On Nar Shaddaa in [[33 BBY]],<ref name="mauldate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Darth Maul'' (2017) 2 to [[33 BBY]].</ref> she and fellow bounty hunters Cad Bane and [[Vorhdeilo]] met [[Sith Lord]], [[Maul|Darth Maul]]. Sing picked up a transmission that Jedi were on their way to [[Hutta Town]] to investigate. Together with [[Cad Bane's group|Bane's group]], despite Sing's suspicions about Maul, they attended [[Xev Xrexus]]'s [[Xrexus Cartel|Cartel's]] [[Auction of Eldra Kaitis|auction]] of the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Eldra Kaitis]].<ref name="Darth Maul II" />

During the auction, Sing, Bane, and Vorhdeilo spoke with Maul aboard their stolen [[Shekelesh-class freight gunship]]. Together, they planned to take Kaitis from [[Jee Kra]], who had successfully won the padawan in the auction. Sing and her compatriots [[Death|killed]] Kra's crew. Sing, Maul, and the others met Kra and Kaitis at his ship. After Maul subdued Kra and his remaining crew, Sing spoke with Bane about about leaving or killing Maul after their job was done. Having discovered the theft, Xrexus ordered the [[gunship]] shot down.<ref name="Darth Maul III">[[Darth Maul (2017) 3|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 3]]</ref>

After the group crashed onto the [[moon of Drazkel]] below, Xrexus allowed the rest of the bidders to [[Skirmish on the moon of Drazkel|hunt]] them down for sport and offered Kaitis to anyone who captured her. Maul and Kaitis split off from the group to distract the hunters, instructing the bounty hunters to steal a ship. Spotting several ships, Sing suggested while the Trandoshan's ship; [[HCT-2001 Dragonboat-class Reugeot 905 freighter|HCT-2001 Dragonboat-class Reugeot 905]], the Weequay's ship has more valuable cargo. Turning the tables on the hunters, Bane, Sing, and Vorhdeilo set a trap. Bane lured a group of [[Trandoshan]] hunters into a kill zone, where he used smoke bombs and killed them while Sing sniped the Weequay hunters from a distance and Vorhdeilo attacked them at close range.<ref name="Darth Maul IV">[[Darth Maul (2017) 4|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 4]]</ref> The group then stole a [[Flarestar-class attack shuttle|''Flarestar''-class attack shuttle]] from the [[Ohnaka Gang]]; with Sing and Vorhedilo boarded the [[shuttle]] with their Weequay [[prisoner]] - who Vorhdeilo wanted to suck his blood out- and Sing pilot the vessel, lowering the [[entry ramp]] so Bane can get onboard and destroyed the other starships on the moon and picked up Maul, who had killed Kaitis and promised they would be compensated.<ref name="Darth Maul V">[[Darth Maul (2017) 5|''Darth Maul'' (2017) 5]]</ref>

===Boonta Eve===
[[File:Aurra_sing_ep1.png|thumb|right|230px|Aurra at the Boonta Eve Classic]]
In [[32 BBY]],<ref name="BoontaDate">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], which includes the Boonta Eve Classic, to [[32 BBY]].</ref> ten [[Standard year|years]] before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|start]] of the [[Clone Wars]], Sing was on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of Tatooine during the [[time]] of the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]]. Sing watched the podrace from a balcony on [[Beggar's Canyon]],<ref name="Episode I" /> and became impressed by the flying skills displayed by Anakin Skywalker.<ref name="Jedi Master 2" /> A [[hologram]] image depicting Jabba the Hutt paying Sing as two of his underlings dragged the injured [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Neva Kee]] away from his crashed [[Podracer]] was acquired some time later by the [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Law enforcement agency|police]] [[inspector]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo|Tan Divo]]. All identifying authentimarks were scrubbed from the hologram, making it impossible to confirm its legitimacy, but if true, the image suggested Sing had a hand in Kee's demise. Divo developed a theory that [[Farwan & Glott]], the original manufacturers of Kee's podracer, hired Sing to take out Kee so that they could acquire the podracer customization secrets that he refused to reveal.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

===Halcyon Legacy===
{{Quote|My name is Colman Vestig, and I'm looking to--"<br />"That's a lot of credits. So, who do you need "taken care of"?|Colman Vestig and Aurra Sing|<ref name="HL2">[[Halcyon Legacy 2|''Halcyon Legacy'' 2]]</ref>}}
[[File:ColramVestigAurraSing-HalcyonLegacy2.jpg|thumb|left|265px|Colram Vestig hires Aurra Sing]]
At some point prior to [[22 BBY]],<ref name="HL2 flash date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] to the year [[22 BBY]]. Because the death of [[Zam Wesell]] is depicted in that film, and a living Wesell appears in flashbacks in [[Halcyon Legacy 2|''Halcyon Legacy'' 2]], those flashbacks must have taken place before 22 BBY.</ref> Sing was vacationing aboard the ''[[Halcyon]]'', along with the [[Clawdite]] bounty hunter, Zam Wesell, where she was hired by a [[Gender|man]] named [[Colram Vestig]] for protection against the [[Hutt Clan|Hutts]], whom he owed a great sum of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. As Vestig was negotiating with Sing, his son [[Wilmar]] approached to pester his father about his promises to let him join a safari to the [[planet]] [[Numidian Prime]] to see the native [[Predavorexx]]es. Vestig shooed his child away, and soon after, they were approached by a representative of the Hutts aboard the ''Halcyon''. Sing attacked the man, but he informed her and Vestig that his bosses now had possession of the boy, and they followed him to Numidian Prime to [[Rescue of Wilmar|retrieve]] him. As they were departing the ship, the [[droid]] [[D3-O9]] warned Sing that the ''Halcyon'' was approaching a [[solar storm]], which had caused all other passenger excursions from departing as scheduled. This, however, did not deter the bounty hunter.<ref name="HL2" />

They were led to a band of [[Weequay]]s, who had Wilmar held at gunpoint. They let loose a wild Predavorexx to attack Sing, much to the alarm of the representative who had been sent to collect Sing, who was still down on the ground with her when the creature attacked. The Hutts began to gamble on Sing's chances of success, but to their collective dismays, Aurra came out victorious in the match. With the help of Wesell, who had taken on the form of a Weequay in order to sneak behind the child's captors, Sing defeated the Weequays. She then returned to the transport and flew back to the ''Halcyon'', where she returned Wilmar to Vestig. The ''Halcyon'' narrowly avoided the solar storm. Shortly after, the Hutts finalized their purchase of the pleasure vessel, and so the bounty hunter pair decided to cut their vacation short and slink off at the next [[spaceport]].<ref name="HL2" />

===The Clone Wars===
====The Urubai job====
[[File:SlegAndSing-JarJar1.png|thumb|right|265px|Rum Sleg and Aurra Sing were hired to kill Mira Bridger by an unknown client.]]
During the beginning of the Clone Wars around 22 BBY, Sing and her fellow bounty hunter [[Rum Sleg]] were hired to kill [[Mira Bridger]], a member of the [[Unseen]] faction on the planet [[Urubai]] who had created a [[fractal radio]] network. Although not illegal, the existence of the private and secure communications network threatened a lot of powerful individuals and displeased [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] of the Republic. Despite learning of Sleg and Aurra's assignment to kill her, Bridger refused to leave her work and remained on Urubai until Jedi Master [[Kelleran Beq]] and [[Junior Representative]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] of the Republic's Senate convinced her to leave with them.<ref name="JarJar">[[Jar Jar 1|''Jar Jar'' 1]]</ref>

[[File:SlegShot-JarJar1.png|thumb|left|265px|Despite Sleg's injury, Aurra Sing mange to catch up to their targets.]]
The two hunters located Bridger as she, Beq, Binks, and her [[Urubaian]] ally [[Coralisa Senig]] approached Binks' [[H-type Nubian yacht]], the ''[[Heyblibber Duck]]''. The two bounty hunters attacked the group, but Beq held them at bay with his [[Kelleran Beq's lightsaber|lightsaber]], providing cover for the others as they ran toward the [[starship]]. While Senig was shot in the leg, she decided to cover the group firing back at the bounty hunters and stay help her people while Beq, Binks, and Bridger safely made it to the ''Heyblibber Duck'' and boarded, but Sing was able to leap to the [[entry ramp]] and get aboard before they took off.<ref name="JarJar" />

As Beq and Binks saw Sleg's own [[Rum Sleg's starship|ship]] which gave chase into [[orbit]], Sing wounded Bridger and almost killed her with her blaster but Beq fought her. As Sleg opening fire on the ''Heyblibber Duck'', it injured the [[pilot]] [[Ric Olié]]. While fighting Beq, the Jedi Master demanded to know who hired SIng but she responded that the information is confidential. The two lost their balance when an inexperienced Binks struggled to control the ship. Sing used this to her advantage, as Beq was knocked to the ground and gets up Sing points one of her blasters at the Jedi but he [[Force push]]es her to the wall, knocking her out and Binks managed to jump to [[lightspeed]] on Beq's orders. The ''Heyblibber Duck'' got Bridger safely to [[Coruscant]]. Once there, a recovered Olié assumed that it had been Beq who outflew Sleg, but the [[Jedi]] assured him it was Binks.<ref name="JarJar" />

====Joining Boba Fett====
{{Quote|I just wanted to kill the Jedi that murdered my father!''"<br />"''Well, that will have to wait. Grow up. You'll get your revenge in time.|Boba Fett and Aurra Sing|<ref name="Death Trap">{{TCW|Death Trap}}</ref>}}
[[File:Slave_I_airlock.png|thumb|right|290px|Aurra on board ''Slave I'' with Bossk]]
Sing's work as an expert sniper and the skills she displayed in in that field around the time of the [[Clone Wars]] earned her fame.<ref name="Complicated Profession">{{Topps|set=2022 Topps Star Wars Masterwork|subset=Bounty Hunting: A Complicated Profession|cardname=Aurra Sing|link=https://www.tcdb.com/ViewCard.cfm/sid/399146/cid/23267443}}</ref> During the Clone Wars, Sing joined up with [[Boba Fett]] to kill [[Mace Windu]] for revenge for Boba's [[Parent|father]], who Windu had killed on [[Geonosis]]. Boba, under the name of "Lucky," boarded the ''[[Endurance]]'' as a [[clone cadet]]. After his first attempt on Windu's life failed, he contacted Sing to ask for advice. Sing told him to blow up the [[reactor]]s, much to Boba's surprise. After Boba successfully destroyed the ''Endurance''{{'s}} reactors, sending it crashing towards [[Vanqor]], he got into one of the [[escape pod]]s along with several of the other [[cadet]]s. Once they were away from the plummeting vessel, the pod was docked by ''[[Slave I]]''. [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]] and Sing entered the pod to confront Boba. After telling him that the other cadets must be launched into [[Realspace|space]], due to the fact that they were "living witnesses," Boba reluctantly came aboard ''Slave I'' and jettisoned the pod from ''Slave I''{{'s}} docking area. Although the group of [[bounty]] hunters escaped, the cadets were saved by the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and Windu, who had escaped the ''Endurance'' in their [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Jedi starfighters]].<ref name="Death Trap" />

Sing and her crew reached the crashed cruiser before the Jedi. There, she encountered and executed multiple clone survivors, shooting them in the back or chest. She later abducted [[Admiral]] [[Shoan Kilian|Kilian]], [[Clone Commander|Commander]] [[Ponds]], and a [[Clone navigation officer|naval officer]] from the crash site of the ''Endurance'' on Vanqor. After Skywalker and Windu triggered their bomb in the crash site, Boba [[Mission to Vanqor|convinced]] Sing to go back to get the two Jedi's heads to bring to [[Count]] [[Dooku]], who might pay them for their trouble. Sing, along with Boba and [[Castas]], went to the crash site. Due to the ship falling apart, and [[R2-D2]]'s interference, Sing decided to blow up the crash site. As they left, Bossk contacted Sing, telling that a explosion happened near where the Jedi landed. Sing then ordered Bossk to jam all transmissions from the planet. When Sing and her team returned to ''Slave I'', they saw Windu's Jedi [[starfighter]] taking off. Unbeknownst to Sing, only R2-D2 was aboard. Though ''Slave I'' took out the Jedi fighter's communications, Artoo reached the [[Hyperspace transport ring|hyperspace rings]] and escaped. Knowing that Windu would not hold a grudge against them, she decided to motivate Windu by using the hostages.<ref name="R2CH">{{TCW|R2 Come Home}}</ref>

[[File:AhsokaGotsSing-LT.png|thumb|left|250px|Aurra Sing caught by Ahsoka Tano]]
When contacting Windu to come and find her, Sing shot Ponds and later ejected his body into space after Boba wouldn't do it. The group of bounty hunters then traveled to the planet [[Florrum]] for a visit with Sing's old flame Hondo Ohnaka, hoping for advice. During a visit in the [[Cantina|bar]] with Ohnaka, Sing,<ref name="LT" /> using her [[biocomputer]],<ref name="Databank" /> overheard Castas communicating via hologram to a friend on [[Coruscant]]. After hearing him calling her an "old hag" and offer up information about her, Sing took out her [[blaster]], called Castas' name, and shot him in the face when he turned around, killing him. Jedi Master [[Plo Koon]] and the Padawan [[Ahsoka Tano]] later [[Second mission to Florrum|caught up]] with her, having learned of her location via Castas' killing, and a standoff ensued with Boba holding a blaster to Koon's head and Tano a lightsaber to Sing's throat. Though Koon attempted to defuse the situation peacefully by promising no harm would come to the two if they yielded, Sing refused to do so and tried to kill the Jedi Master with darts concealed in her boot. The attempt failed and the situation turned violent; Sing took advantage of a bomb triggered by Boba to escape, but abandoned her protege despite his pleas after Koon managed to capture him. Stealing a speeder bike from a pirate, Sing led a pursuing Tano on a chase away from where the hostages were located but when Tano broke off pursuit to head in the correct direction after being informed of this, Sing took another path and ambushed the Jedi just as she freed the hostages. The Palliduvan boarded ''Slave I'' and powered it up, intending to flee, but the ship ended up crashing after a wing was sliced off by the Jedi Padawan. Sing was presumed dead by the Jedi.<ref name="LT" /> Unknown to them, Hondo salvaged her out of ''Slave I''{{'s}} wreckage.<ref name="Assassin" />

====Working for Ziro====
{{Quote|Honey, this isn't about your cause. This is about revenge. My employer wants to get even with you. Simple as that.|Aurra Sing, to Padmé Amidala|<ref name="Assassin" />}}
[[File:AurraSing-Assassin.png|thumb|right|270px|Sing facing Ahsoka Tano]]
Quickly returning to work after her apparent death, Sing was later hired by [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure|Ziro]] the [[Hutt]] to assassinate Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] in retaliation for [[Raid on Ziro's Palace|imprisoning]] him. She tracked the Senator to [[Alderaan]], where Amidala was due for a [[Alderaan Refugee Conference|conference]] on [[refugee]] concerns. Infiltrating the conference through the vents and snapping the neck of several [[Senate Commando]]s, Sing set up a sniper position. Only the timely intervention of Tano saved Amidala from suffering a fatal wound, forcing Sing to flee.<ref name="Assassin" />

Sing later made a second attempt on Amidala's life when she tracked down the Senator, not being fooled by the [[BD-3000 luxury droid]] acting in her place, but was once again foiled by Tano. Sing outdrew Tano as she grazed the Padawan, but was caught off-guard as Amidala pulled a hidden blaster and hit her with a [[stun]] blast, knocking her out. Sing was then arrested and brought to Coruscant; her failure to assassinate her target was exploited to garner an accidental confession from Ziro, guaranteeing her employer an extended [[prison]] sentence.<ref name="Assassin" />

====Senate hostages====
{{Quote|Help me&hellip;no&hellip;n-|A Senate Commando, before being killed by Aurra Sing|<ref name="HC">{{TCW|Hostage Crisis}}</ref>}}
[[File:Aura_Cad_Bane.png|thumb|left|240px|Sing working with Cad Bane]]
Later in the Clone Wars, Sing escaped [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] captivity and teamed up with [[Cad Bane]]'s bounty hunter team for the [[Senate hostage crisis|raid]] on the Republic [[Imperial Senate Building|Senate Building]], with the object of taking hostages to free Ziro the Hutt from his imprisonment. During the hostage siege Aurra covered Bane's entrance to the building by positioning herself at the top of a nearby building and taking out the Senate Commandos who attempted to stop him with a [[sniper rifle]]. After Bane eliminated the remaining Senate Commandos, Sing shot a dying trooper who was lying on the ground. Later she aided [[Shahan Alama]] in searching for Anakin Skywalker, whose presence in the Senate Building threatened to thwart Bane's plan. Eventually, with the help of [[Robonino]], the hunters managed to render Skywalker unconscious. When the Hutt [[crime lord]] was freed, Sing successfully left the crime scene along with the rest of the group.<ref name="HC" /> While escaping, Sing and the others were pursued by the Jedi into one of Coruscant's [[underworld portal]]s. Bane shot the engine of an ascending [[transport]] ship, which caused it to spin out of control and force the Jedi to steered it clear of a populated landing platform, allowing Sing and the rest of Bane's crew to escape with their bounty.<ref name="Dangerous Debt">{{TCW|Dangerous Debt}}</ref>

===Further work and death===
{{Quote|You're Tobias Beckett. You killed Aurra Sing."<br />"Pushed her. Pretty sure the fall killed her.|Lando Calrissian and Tobias Beckett|<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
[[File:TobiasBeckett-SASWSOCE.png|thumb|Tobias Beckett (pictured) killed Aurra Sing.]]
As the Clone Wars continued, Sing eventually took to [[Commanding officer|commanding]] forces from the [[Confederacy military]]. The Jedi agreed to personally capture her. The Republic put out a warning to its citizens about the bounty hunter, asking its people not engage her and instead immediately report any sighting to the [[Jedi High Council]]. However, the citizen's alert also<ref name="Fugitives Aurra Sing" /> incorrectly<ref name="Jedi Master 2" /> stated her career in bounty [[hunting]] had lasted seven [[Standard decade|decades]] by that point.<ref name="Fugitives Aurra Sing" />

At some point, the [[smuggler]] and entrepreneur [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] accrued a large debt to Sing.<ref name="Solo" /> Around [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Tobias Beckett]] took a job for [[Crimson Dawn]] crime lord [[Dryden Vos]] and eventually killed Sing by pushing her from a substantial height.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>

===Legacy===
Sing's death pleased Vos, and he gave Beckett a bonus for the act.<ref name="Expanded Edition" /> Sing's death was later recalled to Beckett while talking with Calrissian, who was also pleased by her demise due to the sum that he had owed her, though Beckett claimed that he had merely only pushed Sing and that it was, in fact, the fall that had killed her.<ref name="Solo" />

In 3 ABY, [[Chewbacca]] imagines Sing alongside other bounty hunters like [[4-LOM]], Boba Fett, Bossk, [[Dengar]], and [[Zuckuss]] while using training droids to simulate Jabba's Palace, though she had been long dead.<ref>[[Star Wars (2020) 22]]</ref>

Sing's former flame Hondo Ohnaka included her in his book ''[[The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters]]'' and reflected on his past with her. While he believed the galaxy was safer with her dead, he found that there were times when he missed his old lover.<ref name="Secrets of the Bounty Hunters" /> Sing was also mentioned in Ohnaka's book the ''[[Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''.<ref name="Galactic Explorer's Guide">''[[Star Wars: The Galactic Explorer's Guide]]''</ref> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Halcyon Legacy 2|''Halcyon Legacy'' 2]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], the droid D3-O9 told the tale of her exploits with Zam Wesell during their stay aboard the ''Halcyon'', reflecting that both bounty hunters had met their fates.<ref name="HL2" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Next time I tell you to pull the trigger, you do it.|Aurra Sing, to Boba Fett|<ref name="LT" />}}
[[File:Aurra_Sing_takes_aim.png|thumb|270px|Aurra takes aim|left]]
Aurra Sing was a Palliduvan female who stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> with [[Color|auburn]] [[hair]], green [[eye]]s and white skin.<ref name="Episode I" /> Sing was known as one of the most powerful and ruthless bounty hunters in [[the galaxy]]. She had no qualms about killing, whether it be executing wounded [[clone trooper]]s,<ref name="HC" /> former partners like Castas or setting adolescent clones adrift in a disabled escape pod. Despite her ruthlessness, Sing was also somewhat of a mother figure to Boba Fett during his teenage years, sometimes addressing him as "Honey" and stopping him from drinking [[alcohol]] in Hondo Ohnaka's bar. Despite this, she was also selfish, and she abandoned Fett to save herself after the boy was captured by Plo Koon.<ref name="LT" />

Sing also tried to serve as a mentor to Fett, as shown by when she tried to make him murder the helpless Clone Commander Ponds and when he hesitated, she simply shoved him aside and killed Ponds herself. Aurra Sing also had a romantic relationship with Hondo Ohnaka, and though they were no longer lovers, Ohnaka still referred to her as "My Dear." Consequently, after Sing and Boba arrived at Ohnaka's base, he asked if Boba was his son. Even with their past, Ohnaka refused to help Sing when she tried to lure Ahsoka Tano and Plo Koon into a trap.<ref name="LT" /> Like [[General]] [[Grievous]],<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> Sing kept lightsabers as trophies from the Jedi she hunted.<ref name="Women of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Women of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

==Skills and abilities==
[[File:AurraSing-BHUSC.png|thumb|200px|Sing was equally deadly with a blaster pistol or sniper rifle.]]{{Quote|You're a dangerous woman. Yes, very dangerous!|Hondo Ohnaka's flattering, yet very accurate, description of Sing|<ref name="LT" />}}
Sing was a very skilled markswoman,<ref name="HC" /> an expert sniper and a master assassin.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Her skills as a vicious bounty hunter included being Force-sensitive. Sing's specialty was in political assassinations,<ref name="Fugitives Aurra Sing" /> and she counted raids, abductions and assassinations on the politicians, such as Padmé Amidala as a fast route to a big paycheck.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Sing effortlessly shot several Senate Commandos with a sniper rifle without being seen and shot Alama's blaster out of Skywalker's hand. She possessed a considerable amount of strength as she snapped the neck of a Senate Commando<ref name="HC" /> and was even able to completely close her large hand around the smaller Ahsoka Tano's throat.<ref name="LT" /> Sing also was skilled at armed and unarmed combat, infiltration, surveillance and starship piloting.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Aurra Sing was known for her built-in comlink antenna.<ref name="Databank" /> Her skull contained<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> a [[Rhen-Orm]]<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> biocomputer that allowed her to track multiple threats simultaneously and to line up distant shots with her sniper rifle.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It also gave her an early warning that authorities were closing in on her position. Sing wielded a pair of [[DX-13 blaster pistol]]s. Sing's [[Adventurer slugthrower rifle|Czerka Adventurer rifle]] enabled her to hit targets from incredible distances. She also wore a pair of well-worn boots made from rancor hide.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> The sniper rifle Sing used to attempt to assassinate Senator Amidala was compact enough to fit in a case, and when assembled, each piece can slot into each other.<ref name="Assassin" />

==Behind the scenes==
The character of Aurra Sing was first conceived by concept artist [[Doug Chiang]] in a sketch labeled "Babe Fett" made as a result of ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]]' request to have more characters fill out the podracing sequence in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. In the film, Sing was played by model [[Michonne Bourriague]].<ref name="DB old">{{DB|character|aurrasing|Sing, Aurra}}</ref> She later reappeared in the animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', first appearing in the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|season one]] finale episode "[[Hostage Crisis]]". The character was voiced by [[Jaime King]] in the series.<ref name="HC" /> Concept art of her model for the series was done by [[Kilian Plunkett]].<ref name="Art of TCW">''[[The Art of Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''</ref>

A new model of Aurra Sing in a black "sniper outfit" was later created by Plunkett for the ''The Clone Wars'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|season three]] episode "[[Assassin (episode)|Assassin]]".<ref name="Tumblr">{{Tumblr|subdomain=starwars|name=''Star Wars''|url=post/90790686408/auura-sing-concept-by-killian-plunkett|text=Auura Sing concept by Killian Plunkett.}} [sic]</ref> The dark strip of makeup across her eyes in the episode is a homage to a similar look worn by [[Wikipedia:Daryl Hannah|Daryl Hannah]] in the [[1982]] film [[Wikipedia:Blade Runner|''Blade Runner'']], which also starred ''Star Wars'' actor [[Harrison Ford]].<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/assassin-trivia-gallery|text="Assassin" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref>

Aurra Sing was meant to appear in a<ref name="Celeb15">{{SWYouTube|7yKM6RExLlI|The Untold Clone Wars Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Anaheim}}</ref> four-episode arc<ref name="Michnovetz">{{Twitter|MattMichnovetz|status/341245477838266368|[[Matt Michnovetz]]|quote=You bet!|archivedate=20190904234219|archivefile=File:TCWBountyHunterArc-MichnovetzTwitter.png}} {{C|In response to: "Thanks Matt. Is your bounty hunter arc with Boba Fett, Cad Bane, & Aurra Sing a 4-episode arc too? Plz reply"}}</ref> starring Boba Fett and Cad Bane, with concept art of the three drawn by series supervising director [[Dave Filoni]]. [[Kyle Newman]], the husband of Jaime King, would have also voiced a character in a cancelled episode<ref name="Celeb15" /> of the [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|sixth season]]<ref name="Steele Wars">{{YouTube|channel=SteeleWars|name=Steele Wars|video=DU6WwLoQaqs|text=Solo Live Reaction|date=2018-05-31|quote=You know, she actually died befo... There were episodes of The Clone Wars season 6 that I was in, which were deleted and she was in that too and she died.}} {{C|c. 1:21:15}}</ref> of ''The Clone Wars'' and his role consisted of a member of the [[Toong]] species.<ref name="Celeb15" /> In May [[2018]], Newman revealed that Aurra Sing would have died in the episode in which he appeared.<ref name="Steele Wars" /> Sing would later be revealed in the 2018 [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to have been killed by Tobias Beckett.<ref name="Solo" />

===Inconsistencies===
While [[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 2|''Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine'' 2]] and [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 76|''Star Wars Helmet Collection'' 76]] state Sing was half-[[human]],<ref name="JMM2">[[Star Wars Jedi Master Magazine 2|''Star Wars Jedi Master'' Magazine 2]]</ref><ref name="HC76">{{HelmetCollectionCite|76|Weapons & Uniforms}}</ref> her [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry,<ref name="Databank" /> ''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]'',<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> and ''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''<ref name="CharUpdated">''[[Star Wars: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> say she was a [[Palliduvan]]. This article assumes ''Jedi Master Magazine'' 2 and ''Helmet Collection'' 76 are in error. The "Sith Fugitives" card for Sing claimed she had a seven-decade long career in bounty hunting by the Clone Wars,<ref name="Fugitives Aurra Sing" /> but ''Jedi Master Magazine'' 2 states she was only born in [[51 BBY]].<ref name="Jedi Master 2" /> As the "Sith Fugitives" card represents an in-universe piece of [[propaganda]],<ref name="Fugitives Aurra Sing" /> this article assumes it as an in-universe error.
