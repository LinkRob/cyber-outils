The '''''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser''', also designated as the '''''Cantwell''-class 410 cruiser''' and shortened as the '''''Cantwell''-class cruiser''', was a type of [[cruiser]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]] at the request of the [[Department of Imperial Justice]] and the [[Imperial Navy]], it featured three powerful [[tractor beam projector]]s which it used to ensnare suspect vessels and steer traffic away from construction zones. One Arrestor was notably featured in an Imperial recruitment film that was displayed in the [[Coronet City Spaceport]]. Several Arrestors were deployed to the construction site of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] over [[Scarif]] along with an [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyer]].

==Characteristics==
[[File:Andor-Arrestor.png|thumb|left|250px|A ''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser had three large dish-shaped tractor beam emitters.]]
The ''Cantwell''-class, named after famous [[starship]] designer [[Walex Cantwell Blissex I]]<ref name="DRVG" /> was an [[Arrestor cruiser]] utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Department of Imperial Justice]]. Like some other [[warship]]s used by the [[Galactic Empire]], the ''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser was shaped like a narrow arrowhead. However, the tip of the arrow was located at the stern of the ship, unlike the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial''-class Star Destroyers]] for example. The inverted triangle-frame ended midway along the hull, leaving the front half of the ship long and narrow. Arrestor cruisers were uniformly painted a light shade of [[Color|gray]],<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> in keeping with the Empire's preference for muted colors.<ref name="newhero">''[[Star Wars Rebels: A New Hero]]''</ref>

Vessels of the ''Cantwell''-class were equipped with three turret-mounted twin heavy [[ion cannon]]s (one port, one starboard, and one ventral), and twelve light [[laser cannon]]s (three forward-, three port-, three starboard-, and three aft-mounted). They were also equipped with three powerful, dish-shaped [[RT-17 repulsor-tractor beam emitter]]s: one at the bow, one at starboard, and one at port. They not only enabled the Arrestor to capture and draw in other [[starship]]s, but also to push them away to an ideal distance for the Arrestor's ion cannons to disable the suspect ship's systems.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

The warship had two large [[Hangar|docking bays]] situated amidships<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> but only carried a handful of [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s.<ref name="POV 220">{{InsiderCite|220|A Certain Point of View}}</ref> Each hangar housed a [[squadron]] of [[starfighter]]s&mdash;24 fighters in total&mdash;as well as [[shuttle]]s and TIE Boarding Craft, which allowed the ship's complement of [[stormtrooper]]s to board a captured craft.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==Role==
[[File:CantwellBridge-DoF.png|thumb|left|250px|The bridge of a ''Cantwell'']]
The ''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser was designed to ensnare and incapacitate any suspect starship, be it a [[freighter]], a [[frigate]] or even another [[cruiser]]. During a capture operation, it pursued its target, only needing to maintain a long range distance to bring its tractor beams to bear. Once the target was trapped, the Arrestor could either reel it in to disable it with ion cannons, or keep it at long range, allowing starfighter squadrons to destroy it.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> The Arrestor was also used to steer traffic away from construction zones.<ref name="Ultimate Sticker Collection">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story Ultimate Sticker Collection]]''</ref>

==History==
In the early [[Standard year|years]] of the Galactic Empire's [[Imperial Era|rule]], the [[Department of Imperial Justice]] and the [[Imperial Navy]] campaigned for the creation of a warship designed to capture [[pirate]]s, [[smuggler]]s and other dissidents so they could be tried publicly. The program was approved, and the contract was awarded to [[Kuat Drive Yards]]. After three years of development and testing, the first ''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser was commissioned into service.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

[[File:Cantwell_hangar_location.png|thumb|right|300px|Cantwell-class cruisers featured two hangar bays, one in the port and one in the starboard side]]
In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="13 BBY" /> the Arrestor Cruiser was in use in the [[Imperial Navy]]. At this [[time]], the [[White Worms]] members [[Han Solo]] and [[Qi'ra]] attempted to escape<ref name="Solo film">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> the [[Core Worlds]] [[planet]]<ref name="Corellia">{{Databank|corellia|Corellia}}</ref> of [[Corellia]] by boarding a [[Starship|ship]] at the [[Coronet City]] [[Coronet City Spaceport|spaceport]].<ref name="Solo guise">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]''</ref> Whilst being pursued by [[stormtrooper]]s, Solo saw an Imperial recruitment film that notably featured an Arrestor Cruiser flanked by a pair of [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyers]].<ref name="Solo film" />

Three years later, Solo was training at the [[Carida Academy]] as a pilot.<ref name="Solo film" /> He was part of [[Onyx Squadron]] of [[TIE/rb heavy starfighter|TIE brutes]] which was assigned to an Arrestor Cruiser. At one point, they engaged with [[pirate]] [[Z-95 Headhunter|Z-95s]], with Solo's starfighter becoming damaged. He guided it into the hangar, but knocked out two more starfighters. In the aftermath, he was sentenced at a [[Tribunal of Han Solo|tribunal]]<ref name="Solo novel">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> and reassigned as an [[Imperial Army trooper]] in the [[Mimban Campaign]].<ref name="Solo film" />

[[File:HaulcraftArrestor-DaughterofFerrix.png|thumb|300px|left|Luthen Rael's Fondor Haulcraft was temporarily stopped by a Arrestor Cruiser over Segra Milo.]]
In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"Daughter of Ferrix" takes place shortly after "[[Narkina 5 (episode)|Narkina 5]]," which {{SW|url=andor-s1-e8-trivia-gallery|text="Narkina 5" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}} dates to [[5 BBY]].</ref> [[Luthen Rael]] was briefly stopped by [[Captain]] [[Elk]]'s Arrestor cruiser while entering the [[orbit]] of [[Segra Milo]] aboard his [[V-21.1 Chevlex light haulcraft|Fondor Haulcraft]]. Although he gave the Imperials a convincing&mdash;if fake&mdash;[[Transponder code|transponder signal]], Rael soon found his ship trapped into the Arrestor's tractor beam. However, Rael broke away, and his haulcraft's powerful countermeasures allowed him to damage the Arrestor's main beam emitter. An irate Captain Elk then launched an air wing from the cruiser's docking bays, but Rael managed to defeat the enemy starfighters and jump to hyperspace.<ref name="Episode 11">{{Andor|Daughter of Ferrix}}</ref> At least two Cantwell cruisers were deployed to the construction area of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]] above [[Scarif]] around the same time.<ref name="Episode 12">{{Andor|Rix Road (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Notable appearances===
The ''Cantwell''-class Arrestor Cruiser first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', in an Imperial recruitment film.<ref name="Solo film" /> However, in the script for ''Solo'', the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was at one point to be captured by a tractor beam ship, which was developed into the Arrestor Cruiser.<ref name="The Art of">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> This full appearance in the film was eventually cut,<ref name="Cut scene">{{Instagram|username=colin_cantwell|url=p/BjAy0TkDhsc|name=[[Colin Cantwell]]|date=May 20, 2018|quote=Bad news everyone. The #ArrestorCruiser scene has been cut from #solo. I wasn't told the reasoning for this decision, but my ship will still be featured in a Solo book to be released soon. I hope they include the scene in the director's cut as I was very much looking forward to seeing her fly. #colincantwell|archivefile=File:Cantwell_cut.png}}</ref> but the cruiser was featured in the Onyx Squadron sequence in the film's deleted scenes.

In 2018, [[Leland Chee]] stated on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that the cruiser's name is the ''Cantwell''-class, although this wasn't confirmed by any officially licensed material<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/1002239233682161664|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2018-05-31|quote=I will share with you the tidbit that it's a Cantwell-class.|archivedate=20211111004731|nolive=1}}</ref> until ''[[Starships and Speeders]]'', a [[2020]] sourcebook for [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[roleplaying game]] system.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

===Design origins===
[[Colin Cantwell]] originally pitched the design of what would become the Imperial Arrestor Cruiser directly to [[George Lucas]],<ref name="Cantwell design">{{Instagram|username=colin_cantwell|url=p/BfJ_lBEDCnA|name=[[Colin Cantwell]]|date=February 13, 2018|quote=The creation of the #Stardestroyer - I asked George about the scenes. We had a few words in which he described the scenes. I then asked "Is it bigger than Burbank?" to determine the size of the Imperial Cruiser and the opening scenes of the movie.|archivefile=File:Cantwell_origins.png}}</ref> for use in the [[1977]] movie [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], although it was ultimately discarded in favor of a completely different design.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> The design had been derived from [[Wikipedia:World War II|World War II]] [[Wikipedia:aircraft carrier|aircraft carriers]]. Originally called the [[Sith]] [[Carrier]], the ship was to launch [[Imperial attack craft|Sith Fighters]], and the dishes on its sides were intended to be used to destroy various ships. This idea was eventually adapted for the [[superlaser]] [[Mk I Superlaser|weapon]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]].<ref name="Cantwell Collection website">{{WebCite|url=http://www.cantwellcollection.com|text=Birth of the X-Wing & Star Destroyer|author=[[Colin Cantwell]]|archivedate=20180721133629}}</ref>

The production crew for ''Solo'' discovered Cantwell's design while reviewing [[Lucasfilm]]'s archives. [[Industrial Light & Magic]] then created a high-fidelity CG model of the vessel for use in the film.<ref name="The Art of" />
