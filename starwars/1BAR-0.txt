'''1BAR-0''' was first [[foot droid]] to [[High Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] of the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]], serving alongside his second foot [[droid]], [[2MAS-N]]. In [[9 ABY]], the droids held up Karga's [[Mantle of Office]] [[cape]] while he greeted his allies [[Din Djarin]] and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] in [[Nevarro City]] and later when the trio confronted a [[Pirate Nation|group]] of [[pirate]]s in the [[city]]. They also accompanied the High Magistrate and held his cape when he took Djarin and Grogu to the [[Droidsmith workshop|workshop]] of the [[Anzellan Droidsmiths]].

==Biography==
1BAR-0 was the designation of a [[foot droid]] who served as first foot [[droid]] to [[High Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]], the [[human]] steward of the [[planet]] [[Nevarro]].<ref name="MandoVG" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|17}}</ref> 1BAR-0 and Karga's second foot droid, [[2MAS-N]], shadowed the High Magistrate and held up the train of his [[Mantle of Office]] [[cape]] to keep it free of Nevarro dust<ref name="MandoVG" /> while Karga greeted his allies [[Din Djarin]] and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] in a courtyard in [[Nevarro City]]. With 1BAR-0 holding the right corner of the cape, the two droids bounced down the courtyard's steps as Karga descended then followed when the High Magistrate and his allies headed for the [[Office of the Magistrate of Nevarro]].<ref name="Apostate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref>

1BAR-0 and 2MAS-N shadowed Karga again shortly after, when he and Djarin emerged to confront a [[Pirate Nation|group]] of [[pirate]]s led by the [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[Vane]] who were trying to enter the [[Nevarro school|schoolhouse]] in the courtyard. The pirates were trying to drink at the school, as it had previously been a [[cantina]], but Karga told them they could not and tried to diffuse the situation. When this failed, Vane and Karga dueled, with the High Magistrate beating Vane to the draw and shooting his [[blaster pistol]] from his hand. The two droids remained behind Karga, still holding his robe, as he and Djarin then shot the rest of the pirates when they drew their [[weapon]]s. Vane fled, and 1BAR-0 and 2MAS-N followed Karga and Djarin as they walked to the [[Statue of IG-11]] at the center of the courtyard.<ref name="Apostate" />

As Djarin wished to re-activate [[IG-11]], an [[assassin droid]] who had been [[Death|destroyed]] aiding Karga, Djarin, and Grogu, the two foot droids once again trailed Karga and held his cape as they made their way to the [[Droidsmith workshop|workshop]] of a group of [[Anzellan Droidsmiths]] who lived in the [[city]].<ref name="Apostate" />

==Characteristics==
1BAR-0 was a tiny<ref name-"MandoAD">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> wheeled foot droid with [[Color|red]] plating, a gray face plate, and a white back plate. The droid's wheel was black and had a unitread disc at the center.<ref name="MandoVG" />
