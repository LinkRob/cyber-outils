'''''Ewoks'' 4''' is the fourth and final issue of the [[2024]] [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] limited series [[Star Wars: Ewoks (2024)|''Star Wars: Ewoks'']]. The issue was written by [[Steve Orlando]], illustrated by [[Laura Braga]], [[Álvaro López]], and [[Antonio Fabela]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[January 29]], [[2025]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE IMPERIALS MAKE THEIR FINAL PLAY! The Ewoks make a final stand against the Empire! The giant GORAX enters the fray &mdash; can anyone survive its mighty rage?!''

==Plot summary==
On the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]], [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[General]] [[Tern Koyatta]] and his team, consisting of the former [[scout trooper]] [[Khu Fornot]], the [[bounty hunter|bounty hunting]] duo [[Zuckuss]] and [[4-LOM]], the [[scavenger]] duo [[Koll Orvenk]] and [[E-X77]], and their [[prisoner]]-guide [[Wicket Wystri Warrick]], are horrified by the [[Great Devourer]], the gargantuan [[Gorax]] who emerges from the [[cave]] storing an [[Imperial weapons cache]]. Koyatta calls for his team to scatter, but Wicket is frozen in fear. The [[Dowutin]] Orvenk charges at the Gorax and delivers a series of massive body slams to give Wicket a chance to escape. As he sprints to find some sort of help, Wicket slams into 4-LOM's leg. The [[droid]] expresses his disdain for Wicket, but the [[Ewok]] retorts with a scathing derision of the droid's evocation of the [[deity]] the [[Adversary]]. 

4-LOM threatens to [[death|kill]] Wicket, but he is stopped by the arrival of the Ewok loners [[Meedro]] and [[Asha Fahn]]. The two [[soldier|warriors]] pounce on the droid and batter his right knee joint. Meanwhile, their [[condor dragon]] [[Unidentified condor dragon|friend]] attacks the Gorax. Wicket flees to cover, but the other two Ewoks are thrown off 4-LOM by the arrival of Zuckuss. Meanwhile, the Great Devourer manages to seize the condor dragon's neck and forcefully slams it into the ground. As Zuckuss and 4-LOM recover, they wonder where Fornot and Koyatta went. 

The pair is answered by the fire of an [[All Terrain Scout Transport|AT-ST]] and a [[74-Z speeder bike]] emerging from the cave. The Great Devourer attacks Koyatta's AT-ST while Fornot chases after Asha and Meedro with his [[speeder bike]]. The [[xenophobia|xenophobic]] scout trooper grazes Meedro with a laser bolt, and circles back to try and kill the Ewoks. Asha is prepared, however, and leaps to strike Fornot off of his bike with her [[spear]]. Wicket recovers the bike and zooms away as Asha continues to fight Fornot. 

As Orvenk and Koyatta fight the Great Devourer, the mighty Gorax gains the advantage knocking the Dowutin back and rending the laser cannons off the AT-ST. Koyatta flings open the hatch on the AT-ST and aims his [[EPS-6 superheavy blaster cannon]] at the Gorax's heart, killing the Great Devourer. The general leaps out of the [[walker]] and uses his [[blaster pistol]] to blast Wicket off of the stolen speeder bike. He then asks Orvenk to kill their prisoner. The scavenger refuses to further disrespect the forest and walks away from the job despite the protests of the general. With Koyatta so distracted with the Dowutin, Wicket sneaks away with an idea for potential backup.

Asha and Meedro continue to fight the bounty hunters and Fornot. The scout trooper draws a [[knife]] and lurches at Meedro, hoping to finally kill an Ewok. At the sound of a massive rumbling, the fighters are alerted to a new threat. Wicket has arrived with a massive army of [[Yuzzum]]s and [[rakazzak beast]]s. Koyatta's party stop their fighting and speak to Wicket. Koyatta argues that all of the weapons in the cache are Imperial property, and thus his. Therefore, they must allow him to take it lest they are considered [[thief|thieves]]. 

Wicket calls out this lie, and he claims that Koyatta is a lying about being a general. The man gets defensive, allowing 4-LOM to pick up on bio-signs of deceit. This is enough for Fornot to turn on the "general", making Koyatta even more panicked. Fornot draws a [[blaster]] and compels Koyatta to come clean. The man reveals that he was a [[technician|maintenance tech]] on Endor. After the [[Battle of Yavin|destruction]] of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first Death Star]], he manipulated the Imperial bureaucracy to stockpile weapons in case the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] managed to destroy the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Second Death Star]] as well. During the [[Battle of Endor]], he stole an [[officer|officer's]] [[Imperial military uniforms|uniform]] off a corpse, including his [[code cylinder]] and impersonated the role of a general. 

Koyatta's confession only angers Fornot further, since the man is only a grunt preying on his own kind. Koyatta orders Zuckuss and 4-LOM to take out Fornot, but the [[Gand]] refuses to work with the liar any more, believing that abandoning the pretender will better maintain their reputation. Wicket blasts through the weapons of all three and calls for the end of the fighting. Koyatta then fatally blasts Fornot in the sternum. Meedro pounces on the murderer, a knife at his neck. Wicket talks his friend down.

Wicket tells the rest of the story to his younger brother, [[Widdle Warrick|Widdle]]. He tells how Orvenk and E-X77 communed with the forest, how Zuckuss and 4-LOM arrested Koyatta to turn him in to [[Moff]] [[Ubrik Adelhard]], and how the Yuzzums repaired their [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle|''Lambda''-class shuttle]] so the bounty hunters would never return to Endor. Asha, Wicket, and Meedro return to the cache and decide to destroy its contents. Asha and Meedro, decide to be loners together. When Koyatta is delivered to Adelhard, he is forced to clean out the [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer's]] waste chambers of [[dianoga]]s by hand. At [[Bright Tree Village]], the Ewoks host a massive celebration with their many [[species]] of neighbors so they can start building the forest of the future together.
