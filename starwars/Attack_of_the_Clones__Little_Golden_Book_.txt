'''''Attack of the Clones''''' is a [[canon]] young-readers adaptation of the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. It was written by [[Geof Smith]] and published by [[Golden Books]] on [[July 28]], [[2015]].<ref name="Penguin" /> An [[Star Wars Classic Stories: Attack of the Clones|audio version]] of the book was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]] on [[September 19]], [[2024]].<ref name="Audiobook">''[[Star Wars Classic Stories: Attack of the Clones]]'' audiobook</ref>

==Publisher's summary==
''Just in time for the release of'' Star Wars: ''Episode VII'' The Force Awakens''&hellip;the first six ''Star Wars'' films are finally retold in ''Little Golden Books''!

''The epic space saga, ''Star Wars: Attack of the Clones'', is finally retold in the iconic ''Little Golden Book'' format! Padawan Anakin Skywalker must choose between his Jedi duty and love, while Obi-Wan Kenobi uncovers a sinister plot that may lead to war. Featuring stunning retro illustrations, this book is perfect for ''Star Wars''—and ''Little Golden Book''—fans of all ages!<ref name="Penguin" />
