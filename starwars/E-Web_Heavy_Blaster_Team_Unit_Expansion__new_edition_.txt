The '''E-Web Heavy Blaster Team Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ206''') is an expansion to the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]].'' An updated version of the [[E-Web Heavy Blaster Team Unit Expansion]], it was announced on [[March 28]], [[2025]] and released on [[May 15]], [[2026]].

==Publisher summary==
''Imperial troops are known for their relentlessness, pressing the attack even in the face of the strongest Rebel forces. Bridging the gap between lightly armed trooper units and the heavy AT-ST, an E-Web Heavy Blaster Team gives the Galactic Empire fearsome firepower to take down any resistance.''

*''HEAVY IMPERIAL FIREPOWER: Reinforce your Galactic Empire army with the E-Web Heavy Blaster Team, delivering devastating ranged attacks against Rebel forces.''
*''SUPPORT WEAPON UNIT: Bridges the gap between standard Stormtrooper squads and larger vehicles like the AT-ST, adding powerful battlefield control.''
*''DETAILED MINIATURE: Includes 1 highly detailed E-Web Heavy Blaster Team miniature with base, ideal for hobby assembly and painting.''
*''ENHANCED TACTICAL OPTIONS: Features 1 Unit Card to integrate this heavy support weapon seamlessly into your Star Wars: Legion army strategy.''
*''COMPLETE EXPANSION CONTENTS: Includes 1 miniature, 1 base, 1 unit card, 1 punch sheet, and 1 insert; requires the Star Wars: Legion Core Set to play.''
