The '''Battle of Mimban''' was a battle in which the [[Mud Jumpers]] of [[224th Imperial Armored Division|224th Clone Division]] and [[clone trooper]]s of the [[501st Legion]] fought on [[Mimban]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="501 Plus One" />

==The battle==
The [[501st Legion]] and the [[224th Imperial Armored Division|224th Clone Division's]] [[Mud Jumpers]] under [[Jedi General]] [[Laan Tik]] fought on [[Mimban]].<ref name="501 Plus One" /> In addition the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] trained and assisted [[Iasento's tribe]] in repelling the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]' [[Separatist Droid Army|Droid Army]].<ref name="TOG" /> In addition to the [[clone trooper]] units,<ref name="501 Plus One" /> [[Junior Representative]]<ref name="JarJarDB">{{Databank|jar-jar-binks|Jar Jar Binks}}</ref> [[Jar Jar Binks]] was sent to interact with the [[Mimbanese]]. During the fight, [[General]] Tik was killed and the [[Cloning|clones]] were forced to fall back. Representative Binks was left in command.<ref name="501 Plus One" />

[[File:RexFightsAlone-501PlusOne.png|thumb|left|300px|Captain Rex stands alone against the Droid Army.]]
At the Republic camp, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] learned that the [[droid]]'s [[deflector shield generator]] was three [[Kilometer|klicks]] away. Rex decided to contact command, mark the landing zone and have a exfiltration [[shuttle]] sent to pull his men out. However, Rex decided to take out the shield generator by himself. One of his [[trooper]]s and Binks followed him. Rex then fought several [[battle droid]]s, but was shot. Binks, using General Tik's [[lightsaber]], was able to save Rex. Rex took the lightsaber away from Binks for his safety and continued on his own to destroy the [[deflector shield]] generator. The two pushed forward, and Rex had handed Binks his [[DC-15A blaster rifle]].<ref name="501 Plus One" />

While in combat on Mimban, the 224th's Mud Jumpers were given a song by a [[Hologram VJ]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] [[Grand Army of the Republic broadcast|broadcast]].<ref name="Rookies" />

==Aftermath==
Despite the Republic's promise for their freedom, the Mimbanese were denied this when the Republic was reorganized into the [[Galactic Empire]]. The 224th's [[Imperial swamp trooper]]s fought their former allies for the next twelve years.<ref name="TOG" /> [[Lieutenant]] [[Alayus Bolandin]] told his fellow [[officer]]s and [[Imperial Army|men]] that their mission was install a regime loyal to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Solo SW Story">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
