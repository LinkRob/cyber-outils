'''Dytrew''' was a location associated with the [[Keepers of Dytrew]]. [[Jedi Master]] [[Vernestra Rwoh]] visited Dytrew and helped the Keepers repel attacks from a vicious creature attacking their [[City|villages]]. In return, the Keepers gave Rwoh a [[Sunstones of Dytrew|sunstone]].<ref name="Wayseeker" />
