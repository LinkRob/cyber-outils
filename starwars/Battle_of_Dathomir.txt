{{Quote|The time has come at last to take revenge on Asajj Ventress and the witch Mother Talzin. I have learned Ventress has returned to Dathomir. Go there, and wipe the witches out. All of them.|Count Dooku, to General Grievous|<ref name="Massacre" />}}
The '''Battle of Dathomir''', also known as the '''Massacre of the Nightsisters''', was a genocidal battle that took place in [[20 BBY]], during the [[Clone Wars]], on the [[planet]] of [[Dathomir]]. The [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] launched an invasion that resulted in the destruction of the [[Nightsisters]] with few survivors. The assault was led by the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies]], [[General]] [[Grievous]], on the orders of Confederate [[Head of State]] [[Count]] [[Dooku]].

==Prelude==
{{Quote|Assemble the fleet! The time to exact vengeance against the traitor Ventress has arrived! We go to Dathomir, to battle!|General Grievous before the battle|<ref name="Massacre" />}}
[[File:Baptism-Massacre.png|left|thumb|250x250px|Asajj Ventress is baptized, becoming a full member of the Nightsisters.]]
[[Asajj Ventress]] had been the [[Sith apprentice]] of [[Count]] [[Dooku]] since the [[22 BBY|beginning]] of the [[Clone Wars]]. She successfully led [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Confederacy military|forces]] against the [[Galactic Republic]] during multiple battles and engagements. However, during the [[Battle of Sullust]], Count Dooku was contacted by his [[Sith]] master [[Darth Sidious]]. Sidious believed that Ventress had grown too powerful, and believed that she was being trained to be used against him so Dooku could take power, in line with the Sith [[Rule of Two]]. While Dooku denied this, Sidious ignored him and ordered the Count to eliminate Ventress to prove that his allegiance did belong to the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] alone. Dooku was forced to agree to kill Ventress. While the battle raged on between the [[Republic Navy|Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederate fleets]], he called off her reinforcements, and ordered the [[T-series military strategic analysis and tactics droid|T-series tactical droid]] [[TJ-912]] to fire on the ship Ventress was on, where she was dueling Dooku's nemesis [[Jedi General]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Jedi Master|Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Following this, Dooku informed Ventress by [[hologram]] that she was no longer his apprentice and retracted her reinforcements because of her failing him for the last time and would [[Death|die]].<ref name="Nightsisters">{{TCW|Nightsisters (episode)}}</ref>

Despite Dooku's scheme, Ventress managed to survive and escaped from both the Jedi and the Confederacy, hiding for a time.<ref name="Massacre" /> Having left Dooku and the Separatist cause, she returned to her [[homeworld]] of [[Dathomir]], where she rejoined the [[Nightsisters]] and began to make plans to kill Count Dooku as her revenge.<ref name="Nightsisters" />

Dooku, having learned that Ventress had returned to Dathomir, sent the [[Supreme Martial Commander of the Separatist Droid Armies]], [[General]] [[Grievous]], and a Separatist fleet carrying an army of [[battle droid]]s to finally exterminate Ventress, Mother [[Talzin]], and the Nightsisters for vengeance against them as well as Lord Sidious seeing Ventress' kinfolk as a threat to his new evil [[Galactic Empire|Empire]].<ref name="Massacre" />

Upon the arrival of General Grievous and his forces, Ventress had completed her initiation, and had become a full Nightsister.<ref name="Massacre" />

==The battle==
===The landing===
{{Quote|Prepare yourselves, sisters. The war has come to Dathomir!|[[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]], to the Nightsisters|<ref name="Massacre" />}}
[[File:BattleofDathomir-AAT.png|thumb|left|300px|The Separatist forces land and deploy their forces.]]
Grievous' squadron of [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighters]] and [[Hyena-class Droid Fighter/Bomber|''Hyena''-class Droid Bombers]] bombed the Nightsisters' fortress and force them out of hiding.<ref name="Massacre" />

His fleet of four [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]], two [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyers]], two [[Dual Hemisphere-Omni Support Vessel]]s, and his personal [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]], Grievous and his forces traveled to and exited hyperspace in the [[Dathomir system]], and landed on the planet.<ref name="Massacre" />

===Battle at the front lines===
[[File:VentressAdvance-Massacre.jpg|thumb|250px|A newly baptized Asajj Ventress leads her fellow Nightsisters on an offensive to drive back the battle droids.]]
To aid her fellow Nightsisters at the front lines, [[Daka|Old Daka]] recited the [[Chant of Resurrection]] to rise the [[Army of the Dead]], gaining an army of undead Nightsisters.<ref name="Massacre" />

Upon arrival, Grievous ordered his crew of [[OOM-series security droid]]s to launch the invasion by beginning to land their troops on the planet below. Grievous sent out a force of [[BX-series droid commando]]s, [[B1-series battle droid]]s, [[B2-series super battle droid]]s, [[IG-100 MagnaGuard]]s, a [[Defoliator Deployment Tank]], and [[Multi-Troop Transport]]s.<ref name="Massacre" />

===Storming the Nightsister fortress===

After controlling the front lines, Grievous' forces pushed the Nightsisters back to their fortress. After killing most of the Nightsisters, Grievous stormed the [[Nightsister fortress|fortress]] and knocked down the wall to get into the [[Hidden Cave]]. He impaled Daka with one of his lightsabers and knocked over her [[ichor cauldron]], stopping Dooku's [[voodoo]] [[torture]]. With Daka's defeat, the spell sustaining the zombie Nightsisters was severed, and they became immobile corpses once more; leaving the living sisters to be massacred by the Droid Army.<ref name="Massacre" /> Although the Nightsisters had fought bravely<ref name="Bug">{{StoryCite|story=Bug (short story)|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> and furiously against the droids,<ref name="SW Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>, more witches lay on the battlefield dead than droids<ref name="Bug" /> and even the undead could not withstand the attack,<ref name="SW Book" /> leaving no one to care for the dead.<ref name="Bug" />

===Last stand of the Nightsisters===
[[File:NightsistersLastStand-TPoF.png|thumb|left|250px|The Nightsisters make their last stand against General Grievous and his forces.]]
With Ventress on the run and the loss of their undead reinforcements,<ref name="Massacre" /> the remaining Nightsisters were scattered throughout the forests, pursued by hordes of battle droids. Many, including [[Morgan Elsbeth]] and her mother [[Selena]] were among a group of Nightsisters being pursued by many of Grievous' droids. As the sisters prepared to make their final stand, they were ambushed by Grievous himself, who chose to face Selena alone while commando droids faced down Morgan and the group of Nightsisters. After striking down Selena and mockingly yelling at Morgan to run, Grievous ordered his forces to annihilate the remaining Nightsisters, leaving the landscape a burning wasteland.<ref name="Path of Fear" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|After the massacre I buried my mothers, my friends&hellip;Ilyana.|Merrin|<ref name="JFO">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>}}
[[File:BattleofDathomirAftermath-TPoF.png|thumb|right|300px|The Mountain Clan views the aftermath of the battle from their village.]]
With the battle over and her fellow Nightsisters murdered, Ventress moved through the forest before being met by Talzin, appearing as a spirit. Ventress blamed herself for the destruction, and Talzin told her that, though the Nightsisters would always be linked to her, she now had her own path. Ventress begged Talzin not to leave her, but the Nightsister mother did just that.<ref name="Massacre" /> Thus, Ventress made her way off Dathomir.<ref name="Bounty" /> Talzin remained on the planet and kept in contact with her sons Sidious’ first Darth [[Maul]] and [[Savage Opress]].<ref name="Brothers">{{TCW|Brothers (episode)}}</ref> A dying Daka sent the nightsister [[Yenna]] off-planet in an [[escape pod]].<ref name="Bug" /> A young [[Merrin]] and [[Jerserra]] mourned the death of their sisters, with the former burying the clan's members including her best friend/romantic interest, [[Ilyana]].<ref name="JFO" /><ref name="GoD">''[[Ghosts of Dathomir]]''</ref> At least one Nightsister completely avoided the devastation of her people by being located offworld: [[Shelish]], who had left Dathomir in her youth years prior, sought to honor their memory after the massacre.<ref name="Uprising">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''</ref><ref name="Myths & Fables">{{StoryCite|story=The Witch & the Wookiee|book=Myths & Fables}}</ref>

Following the events of the battle, Ventress felt that her connection to the Force was scattered.<ref name="The Lost Nightsister">{{StoryCite|story=The Lost Nightsister|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> In pursuit of the new life Talzin had foretold, Ventress travelled far into the Outer Rim, eventually ending up on the [[desert]] world of [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Bounty" /> When Ventress arrived in a [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|cantina]] on Tatooine, she realized no one in the building knew that her people had been slaughtered, with Ventress considering the Battle of Dathomir to be the effective end of her homeworld. After taking part in [[safe cargo objective]], she remembered Talzin's final words to her, though now without the same feeling of pain. Instead, she believed she had always had a path and that, after losing it for some time and becoming so many things throughout her life, she was herself, with the entire galaxy waiting for her.<ref name="The Lost Nightsister">{{StoryCite|story=The Lost Nightsister|book=The Clone Wars: Stories of Light and Dark}}</ref> She proceeded to take up the life of a [[bounty hunter]].<ref name="Revenge">{{TCW|Revenge (episode)}}</ref>

[[File:GunshipAttack-TPoF.png|thumb|left|300px|Droid gunships were sent to hunt down any surviving Nightsisters.]]
To root out any surviving Nightsisters, Grievous deployed patrols of [[HMP droid gunship]]s, [[Ringneck recon droid|probe droids]] and battle droids across the forests and mountains following the battle to hunt down anyone that might have survived the Separatist invasion.<ref name="Path of Fear" />

No Nightsister was thought to have survived the atrocities of the Clone Wars.<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]''</ref> As [[Standard year|years]] and [[Standard decade|decades]] passed, those who survived<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> had watched their sisters<ref name="Ahsoka Tano">{{EncyclopediaCite|Ahsoka Tano and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> slaughtered by an armored warrior brandishing a lightsaber<ref name="JFO" />—although no one knew whose identity<ref name="Ahsoka Tano" />—waited for the right opportunity<ref name="Battles" /> to swear to seek vengeance<ref name="SW Book" /> to avenge their fallen sisters.<ref name="Battles" /> Stories were circulated about the Nightsisters being wiped out during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref> During the [[Imperial Era]], Jerserra was found by a [[Jerserra's master|female]] [[Inquisitorius|Inquisitor]] who trained her as a dark-sided Force user.<ref name="GoD" /> While showing the young [[Jedi]] [[Ezra Bridger]] the ruins of the Nightsister lair, Maul stated the Nightsisters would have been a threat to the Empire and that was why they were killed during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Visions and Voices">{{Rebels|Visions and Voices}}</ref> Some Nightsister spirits were bound to an [[Nightsisters' altar|altar]] and when Maul and Bridger conducted a [[Magick|ritual]], they were awakened and demanded payment in the form of host bodies. However, when Bridger destroyed the altar, the spirits lost their power and were vanquished.<ref name="Visions and Voices">{{Rebels|Visions and Voices}}</ref>

Morgan Elsbeth was another survivor, witnessing her mother's death, then staying low after defeating the droids in her vicinity.<ref name="Path of Fear" /> She later pledged allegiance to the Empire, helping to build and expand the Imperial Starfleet at the expense of the worlds she plundered for their resources and pursue power and revenge after the genocide of her people left her embittered.<ref name="Chapter 13">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 13: The Jedi}}</ref> Although her heritage as a Nightsister was questioned, Elsbeth deemed herself a survivor and would endeavor into the [[New Republic Era|era]] of the [[New Republic]], where she found her lost master [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], and restored him from his [[Exile (punishment)|exile]] in a [[far galaxy]] that was the original home of her family.<ref name="PartOne">{{Ahsoka|Part One: Master and Apprentice}}</ref> Stories about the massacre of the Nightsisters being wiped out during the Clone Wars were told.<ref name="Ultimate Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Rest assured, the Nightsisters will not go down without a fight. Prepare yourselves for the Battle of Dathomir.|Leland Chee, via Twitter|<ref>{{Twitter|HolocronKeeper|status/172562644451078145|[[Leland Chee]]|date=2012-02-22|archivedate=20200406095014}}</ref>}}
[[File:Concept-NightsisterLairDamaged.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the battle's aftermath]]
The Battle of Dathomir was created for the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series and was featured in the episode "[[Massacre]],"<ref name="Massacre" /> which aired on [[February 24]], [[2012]] as part of the series' [[The Clone Wars: Season Four|fourth season]].<ref name="Insider 131: Launch Pad">{{InsiderCite|131|Launchpad}}</ref> In [[December]] 2012, [[StarWars.com]] ranked the Battle of Dathomir as the third most memorable moment of the television series from 2012.<ref name="SW Blog: Top TCW 2012">{{SW|url=news/the-clone-wars-top-5-moments-of-2012|text=''The Clone Wars'': Top 5 Moments of 2012}}</ref>

The Battle of Dathomir also receives a mention within the [[2017]] video game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' through its character DLC. When a player is able to spawn into an ongoing match as Grievous, there is a chance the general will remark "this will be easier than Dathomir!" as his first line.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
