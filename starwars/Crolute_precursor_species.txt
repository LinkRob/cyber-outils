A '''species''' of [[Amphibian|amphibious]] creatures were native to the [[planet]] [[Crul]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], where they eventually evolved into the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Crolute]] [[species]] millions of [[Standard year|years]] later. Members of the species used to come ashore during [[summer]]s, with [[Gender|males]] (known as "bulls") fighting for females (or "cows") and guarding [[Reproduction|breeding]] colonies on the beaches.<ref name="AotG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The precursor species to the Crolutes were first mentioned in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', which was written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="AotG" /> and released on [[August 23]], [[2016]].<ref name="Amazon">{{StudioFun|url=books/star-wars-aliens-of-the-galaxy-9780794437312|text=Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy}}</ref>
