The '''20m-cp Strobe/C-Beam Lamp''', '''20m-cp Strobe/c-beam floodlights''' or simply the '''C-beam lamp''', was a model of [[starship]] [[floodlight]] manufactured by [[Gelieg]]. [[Han Solo]]'s [[YT-1300 light freighter]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', had two such floodlights on its forward mandibles.<ref name="IncrediBuilds" />
