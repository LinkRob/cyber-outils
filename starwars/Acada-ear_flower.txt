'''Acada-ear flowers''' were a type of [[flower]] found on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. They had leaves with thick veins that were liked by [[shadow leech]]es.<ref name="Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> during the [[survival level]] of the [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|Crowning Convocation]] coming-of-age ritual on Siline, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Yord Fandar]] became covered in shadow [[leech]]es. Padawan [[Jecki Lon]] asked how the leeches could be avoided, and the [[Silinese]] [[Rhos Arthyst]] told her to avoid acada-ear flowers and [[slee-sucker tree]]s, both of which had leaves that shadow leeches liked.<ref name="Crown" />

==Behind the scenes==
Acada-ear flowers were mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]'', a [[2025]] young-adult novel by [[Tessa Gratton]].<ref name="Crown" />
