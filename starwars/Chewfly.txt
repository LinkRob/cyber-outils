'''Chewflies''' were a type of [[fly]] on the [[Fruited Moon]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Hetzal Prime]]. They flew in [[season]]al swarms on the [[moon]] that destroyed the [[Plant|crops]] planted there, plaguing the [[Hetzalian]]s residing there. At some point by the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era]], the flies were [[gene]]tically modified, resulting in their extinction.<ref name="LOTJ" />

In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> the [[Hetzal system]]'s [[Minister]] [[Zeffren Ecka]] worked to save his home from the effects of the incoming [[Emergences|objects]] savaging locations in his [[Star system|system]] during the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. Ecka compared the incoming threats as similar to the seasonal chewfly swarms that used to plague the Fruited Moon prior to the flies' extinction, thinking in frustration that nothing could be done other than rebuild after the damage was done.<ref name="LOTJ" />
