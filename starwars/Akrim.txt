'''Akrim''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] [[Morling]] criminal, who operated as a [[Pirate/Legends|pirate]] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].

==Biography==
Akrim was a clan leader of the [[Morling]] population at the pirate lair of [[Providence (lair)|Providence]]. Thus, he sat on the Council but did not take an active part in it, since people on the station usually did not take the Morling's position into account.

==Personality and traits==
Akrim was an average Morling, brutal and ferocious. He had a penchant for alcohol and was drunk most of the time.

He generally wore an orange pilot suit and many talismans and trophies, never leaving behind his [[Heavy blaster pistol/Legends|heavy blaster]] and his mace.

==Behind the scenes==
Akrim was created by [[Jean-Michel Ringuet]] for the ambiguously [[canon]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] aid "[[Providence (Avalon)|Providence]]," which appeared in the French magazine [[Avalon 1|''Avalon'' 1]]. He was illustrated by [[Jean-Marc Emy]].
