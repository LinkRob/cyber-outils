'''Ango Croom''' was a [[Falleen]] [[Gender|male]] who led the [[Varluk Organization]].<ref name="PP" />

== Biography ==
At some point, Croom became the leader of the [[Varluk Organization]]. He lent the [[gambler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] some [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for one of his business ventures. Croom believed that Calrissian had swindled him, but Calrissian intended to pay him back. In [[4 BBY]], Croom ordered his minions to steal [[Lando Calrissian's puffer pig|Calrissian]]'s [[puffer pig]] from his [[Lando Calrissian's farm|farm]] on [[Lothal]]. He had the puffer pig brought to the [[Varluk Organization headquarters]] on [[Zyzar]].<ref name="PP" />

Once Calrissian was informed of the puffer pig's theft, he immediately traveled to Zyzar where he infiltrated the headquarters. After finding his pig, he was apprehended by guards and he and the pig were brought before Croom. Croom explained to Calrissian that either the puffer pig was to find a new source of minerals for the Organization as it was running out of their previous supply or Calrissian could repay the credits he owed. If he didn't, either dead or alive he was leaving the planet without the puffer pig.<ref name="PP" />

Calrissian refused to leave without his puffer pig and in the ensuing grappling, the scared puffer pig expanded rapidly knocking the weapons from the guards' hands. Calrissian grabbed one of the [[Blaster|blasters]] and shot the guards, absconding with the puffer pig. Croom ordered the rest of his guard to intercept Calrissian and recover the puffer pig. While fleeing, Calrissian stopped to throw a device through the window of the location where he had found his puffer pig. He escaped the facility, fleeing aboard the freighter [[Stellar Sun|''Stellar Sun'']]. Croom found the device that Calrissian had thrown and discovered it was a map created by the puffer pig showing a large vein of material that could be mined, keeping the organization running for years. Croom considered Calrissian's debt paid.<ref name="PP" />

== Personality and traits ==
Croom was a male [[Falleen]] with black hair and green skin. He wore a brown armless tunic with shoulder pads displaying the Varluk Organization's emblem, over which he had a red belt with a silver buckle, and blue, red, and silver [[Bracer|bracers]]. He wore brown pants with silver shin guard armor over black boots. 

Croom was ruthless, sending his guards to die without care to recover his possessions. He also believed in vigilante justice and theft, stealing Calrissian's property as repayment for credits he believed stolen from him.<ref name="PP" />
