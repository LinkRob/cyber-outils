The '''Dark Awakening''' was a costume owned by the [[human]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Rieve]] that she could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to her [[Sith Apprentice (Rieve)|standard costume]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Dark Awakening was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Rieve, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.17.0 update on [[July 18]], [[2023]], during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Awakening Saber Pattern]]. This costume could be unlocked by purchasing it individually within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
