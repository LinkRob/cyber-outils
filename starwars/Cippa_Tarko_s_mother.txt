A [[Gender|female]] individual was the '''mother''' of the [[Arkanian]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Cippa Tarko]]. After [[Jedi Master]] [[Yaddle]] felt a [[disturbance in the Force]] [[Mission to Dalna (Sun and Forte)|whilst]] on [[Dalna]], Tarko noticed Yaddle's reaction and asked her what was wrong. After Yaddle told the [[youngling]] she was all right, Tarko remarked that she had heard about these kind of situations, and noted her [[Tarkos family|mom]] called them doddery moments, which Tarko took to mean Yaddle was a lot older than she had realized. Sometime later, during the [[Night of Sorrow]], Tarko saw the [[Great Leveler|Leveler]], a [[Shrikarai|creature]] which consumed the life from [[Force-sensitive]]s, and she became overwhelmed by fear and had several [[Force vision]]s, including one of her parents screaming in torment.<ref name="Cataclysm" />
