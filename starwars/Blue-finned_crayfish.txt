The '''blue-finned crayfish''' was a species of [[crustacean]] endemic to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. The species was particularly elusive. At one point, [[priorite]] prospectors thought to capture the rare creature to sell at a high price. Their failures made many declare the species a myth. However, the blue-finned crayfish simply evaded capture by clinging to the underside of rocks and disguising itself by flowing with the [[water]]. If spotted, the creature still evaded capture due to its hydrodynamic body, which allowed it to be extremely slippery, and its sharp claws.

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
In [[9 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> the [[Sakavian]] [[fisher]]man [[Skoova Stev]] who resided on Koboh, caught a blue-finned crayfish at a bygone settlement in [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]] by covering his suit in [[grease]] as a way to combat the slipperiness of the creature. The crustacean was later added to the [[aquarium]] in [[Pyloon's Saloon]].<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}
