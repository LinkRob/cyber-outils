The '''Ferrix Clubs''' were a pair of [[Melee weapon|clubs]] owned and utilized by the [[Wookiee]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Grozz]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his standard [[KX Clubs]].<ref name="Hunters" /> They resembled traditional [[Ferrixian]] [[Hammer (tool)|hammer]]s such as those wielded by the [[Time Grappler]] of [[Ferrix City]] on the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]].<ref name="Time Grappler">{{Andor|That Would Be Me}}</ref><ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ferrix Clubs was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Grozz's KX Clubs, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Ferrix Fighter]]. This wrap could be unlocked by purchasing the Ferrix Bundle from within the Hunters in-game shop.<ref name="Hunters" />
