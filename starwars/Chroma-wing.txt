{{Quote|As you well know, there are ways that larvae do not become chroma-wings. Rather, larvae die so that chroma-wings can be born.|Woolif|<ref name="The Glass Abyss">''[[Mace Windu: The Glass Abyss]]''</ref>}}
'''Chroma-wings''' were the final life stage of the [[Ruurian]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. At an advanced age, Ruurians would shed their [[larva]]l forms and spin a cocoon around themselves, transforming into beautiful chroma-wings whose sole desires were [[Diet|eating]], [[Reproduction|mating]], and flying along the riverbanks of [[Ruuria]]. Although the Ruurians considered the transformation to be paradise, it was possible for them to prevent themselves from becoming chroma-wings through ointments, prescriptions, and special treatments.<ref name="The Rebellion Begins">''[[The Rebellion Begins]]''</ref> Ruurians could also receive [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] augmentations as larvae order to retain their memories as a chroma-wing. The Ruurian [[doctor]] [[Woolif]] received such augmentations in order to keep his medical knowledge after becoming a chroma-wing.<ref name="The Glass Abyss" /> [[Slyyth]] was a Ruurian criminal who feared becoming a chroma-wing, wishing to continue his work, and he thus took treatment to prevent the transformation.<ref name="The Rebellion Begins" />
