During the early [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] used a '''''Rho''-class transport shuttle''' as his personal craft. It was heavily guarded including by [[Emperor's Royal Guard]]s and, during a visit to [[Tantiss Base]] on<ref name="SOT" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="KL EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/kamino-lost-episode-guide|text="Kamino Lost" Episode Guide &#124; The Bad Batch}}</ref> [[Wayland]], accompanied by four [[Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighter|Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighters]]. Unlike most [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttles]], it lacked the ventral cargo pod and was [[gray]]-[[color]]ed with red highlights on the wings.<ref name="SOT" />
