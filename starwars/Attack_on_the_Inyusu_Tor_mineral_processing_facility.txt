In the year [[3 ABY]], an operative of the [[Sullustan resistance]] '''attacked''' the [[Inyusu Tor mineral processing facility]], on [[Sullust]], by stuffing a pipe with rags soaked in chemical cocktail, causing the extractors to grind to a halt and the magnetic separators to plunge into the magma flow. The event kick-started a series of roundups in the nearby [[city]] of [[Pinyumb]] in search of the saboteur. [[Luko Oorn]], the facility's [[administrator]], logged the attack in his records.<ref name="Battlefront" />

==Behind the scenes==
The attack was first mentioned in the [[2015]] [[canon]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Battlefront" />
