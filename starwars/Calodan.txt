{{Quote|Take the foundling to the city of Calodan on the forest planet of Corvus. There you will find Ahsoka Tano.|Bo-Katan Kryze instructs Din Djarin|<ref name="Chapter 11" />}}
'''Calodan''' was the walled<ref name="Chapter 13" /> capital [[city]] of the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Corvus]].<ref name="Ahsoka and Others">{{EncyclopediaCite|Ahsoka Tano and Other Users of the Force}}</ref> After the [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]] helped [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] [[Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter|seize]] weapons from an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial]] [[Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser|cruiser]], Kryze told him that the former [[Jedi]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] could be found in the city.<ref name="Chapter 11" />

Upon arrival, Djarin and his foundling were escorted by members of the [[scout guard]]s, [[Magistrate]] [[Morgan Elsbeth]]'s [[Morgan Elsbeth's forces|brigade]] of faceless enforcers in the city led by the [[mercenary]] [[Lang]]. Elsbeth tasked Djarin with killing Tano, who had killed several members of the scout guards and who sought the location of Elsbeth's "master." Promising her [[Beskar spear|spear]] of pure [[beskar]] to the Mandalorian as payment, Elsbeth provided the bounty hunter with coordinates of the Jedi's location.<ref name="Chapter 13" />

Djarin and Tano, having met in the wilds of Corvus and made a pact regarding the training of the [[foundling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] as a Jedi, [[Liberation of Calodan|liberated]] the city from Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth and her enforcers. Following this, [[Wing]] took up the mantle of leadership, wearing Elsbeth's cloak.<ref name="Chapter 13" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|In all honesty, [that] just comes out of my own personal experience living in an area [in Northern California] where there are fires. My wife and I have been evacuated three of the last four years, every fall. So I guess I'm just telling a story and can some way control it for myself. But yeah, I've known a lot of people that are terribly affected by it, and it's a powerful, powerful thing.|Dave Filoni, discussing why he made Calodan a scorched wasteland|<ref name="Tells all">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2020/11/rosario-dawson-dave-filoni-mandalorian-ahsoka-tano-grogu|text=Star Wars The Mandalorian: Rosario Dawson Tells All About Ahsoka Tano|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2020-11-30|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']]|archivedate=20201207031817}}</ref>}}
Calodan was first mentioned in ''[[Chapter 11: The Heiress]]''<ref name="Chapter 11" /> and first appeared in the ''[[Chapter 13: The Jedi]]'' of the [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''.<ref name="Chapter 13" /> When [[Dave Filoni]] was writing the setting for "The Jedi," he drew inspiration from the [[fire]]s in [[Wikipedia:California|Northern California]] where he lived. Filoni thought the setting would be really haunting and have a foreboding feeling.<ref name="Tells all">{{WebCite|url=https://www.vanityfair.com/hollywood/2020/11/rosario-dawson-dave-filoni-mandalorian-ahsoka-tano-grogu|text=Star Wars The Mandalorian: Rosario Dawson Tells All About Ahsoka Tano|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2020-11-30|work=[[Wikipedia:Vanity Fair (magazine)|''Vanity Fair'']]|archivedate=20201207031817}}</ref> The name of the city sounds similar to the name of [[Wikipedia:Dune universe|''Dune'']] planet [[w:c:dune:Caladan|Caladan]], the ancestral home of [[w:c:dune:House Atreides|House Atreides]].
