'''Black Spire Outpost''' ('''BSO'''), commonly known as '''Black Spire''', was an outpost [[city|settlement]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. Due to Batuu's location on the edge of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], Black Spire Outpost was one of the last stops before [[Wild Space]] and the [[Unknown Regions]]. The outpost was named for the giant [[petrified tree]]s that towered over it, and one in particular that was colored [[Color|black]], the [[Black Spire]].

==Description==
[[File:BSOlogo-TGTB.png|thumb|right|200px|The emblem of Black Spire Outpost]]
{{Quote|This outpost is rife with unsavory scum.|Shakel, on Black Spire Outpost|<ref name="Star Wars: Datapad">[[Star Wars: Datapad|''Star Wars'': Datapad]]</ref>}}
Black Spire Outpost was located on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and was considered one of the last stops before [[Wild Space]] and the [[Unknown Regions]].<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> Its name was derived from the petrified remains of ancient [[tree]]s turned into [[Color|gray]] and brown [[stone]] spires that were large enough to tower over the outpost, with the eponymous [[Black Spire]] standing out due to its black hue and smooth texture reminiscent of [[Volcano|volcanic]] [[glass]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire" />

==History==
{{Quote|You couldn't get from here to Black Spire without me, now you're going to make the Kessel Run? Ha!|L3-37, to Lando Calrissian|<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>}}
===Early history===
[[File:GalaxysEdge-Slider.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Black Spire Outpost]]
Before the invention of [[hyperspace]] travel, Batuu was a busy stop for travelers<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> to trade or refuel as they passed through the Outer Rim or journeyed into Wild Space. However, with the rise of the [[hyperdrive]] and new [[hyperspace route]]s that bypassed the planet, it instead became home to those who did not want to be found.<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> The outpost evolved into a thriving [[spaceport]] for smugglers, rogue traders, and adventurers traveling between the [[The galaxy|galactic]] frontier and uncharted space. Eventually, [[Chandrila Star Line]] began operating luxury cruises in the region for travelers who sought adventure and comfort on their expeditions.<ref name="The Star Wars Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref>

===High Republic era===
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi Order]] built a [[Batuu Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] near Black Spire Outpost.<ref name="Temple of Darkness">''[[Star Wars: Tales from the Galaxy's Edge]]''</ref>

Between [[233 BBY]] and [[232 BBY]],<ref name="YJA date">{{YJADate|122A}}</ref> [[Jedi Initiate]]s [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]] of the [[Tenoo Jedi Temple]] traveled to Black Spire Outpost with [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Celesta Kami]] of the [[Batuu Jedi temple|Batuu Jedi Temple]]. While in search of tools to purchase for Kami's master, [[Carver Drow]], the group found property developer [[Ozon Nimbee]] presenting to Black Spire residents on potential developments. Nimbee sought to construct buildings with unobstructed views of the Batuuan skies, which would necessitate cutting down very spires central to the outpost's identity. Despite of the refusal of the outpost residents, Nimbee tested using [[Pistoeka sabotage droid|sabotage droids]] to cut down several spires near the outpost. The Jedi, witnessing the test, confronted Nimbee, who was berated by the townsfolk. Believing the only way to get buy-in for his plans was to cut down the spires within the outpost without permission, Nimbee released a swarm of his buzz droids on Black Spire Outpost. The Jedi and townsfolk fought the swarm of droids, destroying many of them. Soon, Drow arrived to destroy several more with his [[lightsaber]] and [[the Force]]. One droid, however, escaped notice before completely transecting the spire overlooking the [[Milk Stand|milk stand]]. As the upper portion of the enormous spire fell, the Jedi used the Force to prevent it from crushing the milk stand and set it gently on the ground nearby. At some point, the fallen spire was placed upon its base once more, leaving behind the evidence of the clean cut. Following the defeat of Nimbee, Drow decided to promote relations with the local residents.<ref name="Tale of Small Spire">{{YJA|The Tale of Short Spire}}</ref>

To foster friendly relations with the residents of Black Spire Outpost, the Jedi hosted at least two public training days outside the Temple.<ref name="Caves of Batuu">{{YJA|The Caves of Batuu}}</ref><ref name="Lost Lightsaber">{{YJA|Lys' Lost Lightsaber}}</ref> During a later visit to Batuu, Brightstar duelled with Master Drow during a public training day but experienced fears and doubts. At the advice of Drow, Brightstar later retreated to a cave where he experienced [[Force vision]]s which helped him to overcome his doubts and insecurity.<ref name="Caves of Batuu" /> Following a second public training day, local [[Gender|girl]] [[Sadie Bolter]] stole [[Lys's lightsaber]] in order to overcome her own insecurities. However, the lightsaber was stolen by the local bully [[Pak Relda]]. Seeking to make amends with Solay and her friends, Bolter helped the Jedi to recover Lys's lightsaber and defeated Relda in a sword duel.<ref name="Lost Lightsaber" />

Later, the Jedi Order abandoned the Batuu Jedi Temple after the facility was [[Batuu Jedi temple incident|contaminated]] by an [[Ancient Sith Runestone|ancient Sith runestone]], that claimed the lives of several Jedi including Master [[Sylwin]]. The sole survivor was Padawan [[Ady Sun'Zee]]. In response Master [[Yoda]] sealed the Batuu Temple with [[The Force]], declaring it lost.<ref name="Temple of Darkness" />

===Imperial era===
[[File:Mando-and-Grogu-GalaxysEdge.png|thumb|right|250px|Din Djarin and Grogu in Black Spire Outpost]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date Note">The timeline provided in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'' places [[Han Solo]]'s [[Kessel Run]] nine [[Standard year|years]] after the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']]. As that film takes place in [[19 BBY]], we can deduce that the mission to [[Kessel]] and the subsequent Kessel Run took place in [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[pilot droid]] [[L3-37]] questioned [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] ability to make the [[Kessel Run]] when, according to L3-37, he could not successfully fly the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from "[[Vandor|here]] to Black Spire" without her.<ref name="Solo" /> 

===New Republic era===
Following the [[war against the Pykes]], [[Daimyo]] [[Boba Fett]] and [[Master Assassin]] [[Fennec Shand]] traveled to Black Spire<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref> on a mission<ref name="212 Launchpad">{{InsiderCite|212|Launchpad}}</ref> with the aim of finding recruits for the [[Fett gotra]] and tracking down members of the [[Pyke Syndicate]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> 

The [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] and his [[foundling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] also traveled to Black Spire at some point.<ref name="Mando and Grogu to Land">{{SW|url=news/d23-expo-2022-the-mandalorian-and-grogu-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=D23 Expo 2022: Mando and Grogu to Land at ''Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge'' at Disneyland Park}}</ref>

===First Order era===
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE Date">The events within the themed land [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] and in associated [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge (media project)|media]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the outpost attracted beings who sought to avoid the expanding reaches of the [[First Order]] and its [[Cold War|conflict]] with the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> which had erupted into a [[First Order-Resistance War|war]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> One such being was the [[Weequay]] [[smuggler]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]], who brought the modified [[YT-1300 light freighter]] named the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' to the outpost and established [[Ohnaka Transport Solutions]], a shipping company that was a front for his smuggling operations.<ref name="Pirate's Price">''[[Pirate's Price]]''</ref> Based out of [[Docking Bay 5]], the company frequently recruited flight crew members among the outpost's new arrivals.<ref name="Smuggler's Run">[[Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run|''Millennium Falcon'': Smuggler's Run]]</ref>

The human [[mercenary]] [[Bazine Netal]] had been hired to search for the ''Millennium Falcon'' and purchase it from whomever had it in their possession. At [[Oga's Cantina]], the smuggler told her stories of his history with the ship to stall for time as a [[Yarkora]]n [[sabacc]] player secretly contacted [[Mahjo Reeloo]], who owed Ohnaka a favor after an incident in the [[Undervaults of Gwongdeen]]. Netal paid Ohnaka for the ''Falcon'' and delivered it to [[Beixander 9]], where Reeloo posed as Netal's contact to accept delivery of the ship before returning it to the outpost. Ohnaka notified [[Chewbacca]], a [[Wookiee]] and the ship's former [[Pilot|co-pilot]], that the ''Falcon'' was safe from Netal's employers. Though Ohnaka continued to use it for his business, he also expressed certainty that the ship would continue to aid "the good guys."<ref name="Pirate's Price" />

[[File:GalaxysEdgeBlackSpireOutpost-main.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A view of the outpost]]
[[Black Spire Ruins|Ancient ruins]] just outside of the outpost housed a Resistance base around that same time. Eventually, [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] arrived at the outpost and took command of the First Order garrison, having learned that [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] was with a group of Resistance fugitives on Batuu.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" /> A [[Battle of Batuu|battle]] between the factions took place in the outpost and above the planet.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance">{{GEAttr|Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance}}</ref>

==Inhabitants==
The population of Black Spire Outpost was around four to five hundred [[Sentience|sentient beings]] by the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], although the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] observed that it did not appear large enough to support a population of that size. By the time of the [[Galactic Empire]], the outpost was populated by [[human]]s, [[Darshi]], [[Jablogian]]s, and [[Shistavanen]]s.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Star_Wars_land_aerial_view.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Concept art of the outpost]]
At the D23 Expo on [[August 15]], [[2015]], [[The Walt Disney Company]] CEO Bob Iger first announced that the company would be building a [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars''-themed land]] in [[Disneyland]] Park at the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim, California and in [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]] at the Walt Disney World Resort in Orlando, Florida. The announcement stated that these lands would be fourteen acres in size, making them the largest single land expansions in company history. The two attractions announced for the land included a ride that puts guests behind the controls of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' and a ride that throws guests into the middle of a battle between the First Order and the Resistance. it was also announced that the lands' setting would be on a yet-to-be-seen planet on the edge of the Outer Rim Territories, just before Wild Space.<ref name="Original announcement">{{DPB|url=2015/08/star-wars-themed-lands-coming-to-walt-disney-world-and-disneyland-resorts|text=Star Wars-Themed Lands Coming to Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resorts}}</ref> During the Q1 FY17 Earnings call for The Walt Disney Company on [[February 7]], 2017, Iger announced that the new lands would open in the year [[2019]].<ref name="Earnings call announcement">{{DPB|url=2017/02/star-wars-themed-lands-at-disney-parks-set-to-open-in-2019|text=Star Wars-Themed Lands at Disney Parks Set to Open in 2019}}</ref> During the "Star Wars and Disney Parks: A Galaxy in the Making" panel at [[Celebration Orlando]], Asa Kalama of Walt Disney Imagineering stated that what they were building would be [[canon]]ical.<ref name="Celebration panel">{{YouTube|video=tx0ofXtfXhY|text=FULL Star Wars Land panel - New details, artwork revealed at Star Wars Celebration 2017|channel=@InsideTheMagic|name=Inside the Magic}}</ref>

[[File:SWGE_Merchant_Food_Guide_map.jpg|thumb|right|250px|A map of Disneyland's ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]
During ''Galactic Nights'', a set of promotional trading cards were issued that hinted at what would be found at the attraction regarding Black Spire Outpost. The front of the cards depicted certain scenes of what was likely to occur, such as an encounter with Kylo Ren, bargaining with Dok-Ondar, and Hondo and Chewbacca agreeing to a deal, among others. The back of each card depicted part of a nine-panel map depicting the various locales of the outpost as well as some details about the specific locations. Eight of the cards had a phrase in Aurebesh describing the location which, thanks to Twitter user Nick Tierce, was revealed to say in full "At the edge of the galaxy so far away, black was the spire that called me to stay, a beacon for drifters forgotten and lost, the spire summoned those broken and tossed, come stay here forever or just pass on through, the spirit of the black spire will forever change you."<ref name="BlogMickey">{{WebCite|url=http://blogmickey.com/2018/05/star-wars-galaxys-edge-trading-cards-provide-map-of-black-spire-outpost|text=Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Trading Cards Provide Map of Black Spire Outpost|date=2018-05-29|archiveurl=https://archive.is/tQWIy}}</ref>
