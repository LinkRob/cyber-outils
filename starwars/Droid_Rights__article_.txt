"'''Droid Rights'''" is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] adventure ''[[Inside-out: Uffel's Prisoners]]''. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[July 1]], [[2004]] and took the form of a paper written by [[San Herrera]] and [[Nia Reston]] entitled "[[Droid Rights]]." Its thesis was that [[Droid/Legends|droids]] are [[Sentience/Legends|sentient]] and deserve freedom. The authors, therefore, called on the owning of droids or "synthetic persons" to be outlawed throughout the [[Galactic Republic/Legends|Galactic Republic]]. They sent their paper as a press release to every news agency and government office in the Cularin system.
