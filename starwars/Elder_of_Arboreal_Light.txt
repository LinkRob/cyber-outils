The '''Elder of Arboreal Light''' was a [[Priest|religious figure]] among the [[Wookiee]]s, the [[Homeworld|native]] [[species]] of the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. Carrying a curved staff from which the [[Beacon of Caalbeth]] hung, the Elder presided over the [[Life Day]] ceremony in the [[Sacred Grove]] near [[Rwookrrorro]].<ref name="Tree of Life" />
