'''Flestic Crupp''' was a bulky [[Rodian]] [[arms dealer]] and criminal who led a small gang of other Rodians in the [[Cantonica system]]. Wanted by the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] for some time, Crupp was finally connected to a robbery that gave the [[Law enforcement agency|police]] a case against Crupp, making him eligible for arrest. However, Crupp happened to be another victim of the same perpetrator of the other robbery. Crupp later escaped from [[prison]] with the help of the [[gangster]] [[Denel Strench]] and tracked down the [[slicer]] "[[DJ]]," whom Crupp believed was responsible for his troubles. Crupp was detained by the [[security guard]]s of the [[Vesustrian]] resort before he could harm DJ, though. As it happened, "Denel Strench" was actually a fake personality DJ created sometime prior.<ref name="DJ-MW" />
