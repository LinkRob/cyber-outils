'''Freighter 2716''' was an [[Orca-class freighter|''Orca''-class freighter]] used by the [[Galactic Empire]] to [[transport]] a giant [[kyber crystal]] to the [[Tonnis sector]]. It was disguised as a civilian [[freighter]] to avoid notice.<ref name="ITNOR" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Chronology Note" /> it was [[Mission to Faos Station|infiltrated]] by rebels [[Saw Gerrera]], [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Sabine Wren]], and [[C1-10P|Chopper]] during a brief stop at [[Faos Station]]. When Gerrera discovered the freighter's cargo, he wired the crystal up to the ship's [[hyperdrive]] to create a bomb, which destroyed both the freighter and the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class]] [[Slavin's Star Destroyer|Star Destroyer]] it was rendezvousing with when it went off.<ref name="ITNOR" />
