'''Construction droids''', also known as '''constructor droids''', or '''builder droids''' were [[droid]]s that could be utilized for construction. Builder droids were responsible for building the [[Trade Federation laboratory]].<ref name="Altaya">{{EncyclopediaCite|Naboo}}</ref> While working as a co-[[pilot]] and [[navigator]] for the [[Wookiee]] [[Lohgarra]], [[Thane Kyrell]] and his crew once delivered a shipment of constructor droids to the cold [[planet]] of [[Zeitooine]].<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref>

==History==
===Kamino===
After the [[great flood]]<ref name="Rise of the Separatists">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> of [[Kamino]], the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Kaminoan]]s built their modern cities with '''[[repulsorlift]] construction droids'''. One such [[city]] was their [[planet]]ary [[capital]], [[Tipoca City]], which rested upon large stilts that extended to the [[water]]world's seabed. Building and repair work mostly took place during the [[Time|short]] [[Standard year|annual]] break of Kamino's otherwise unceasing [[Atmosphere|rain storms]].<ref name="CL2016">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

===Lothal===
When the [[Galactic Empire]] took over the [[jogan fruit]] orchards of [[Beck Ollet]]'s family on [[Lothal]], they utilized massive construction droids and other equipment for strip-[[mining]] that was so outdated that [[Zare Leonis]] described them as "primitive stuff that should have been melted down before the [[Clone Wars]]." More precise equipment could have left the surrounding land intact, but the Empire was unwilling to spend anything more than bare-bottom [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] for the job.<ref name="edge">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

While serving as a [[Imperial cadet|cadet]] at the [[Academy for Young Imperials]], Leonis observed construction droids ruining [[grass]]lands in the [[Easthills]] in order to construct a new [[BlasTech Industries]] weapons lab.<ref name="justice">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref>

During his time at the [[Arkanis Academy]], Leonis was given the task of [[Death|killing]] [[Penn Zarang]] as an initiation into the [[Commandant's Cadets]]. With the help of [[Lieutenant]] [[Chiron]] and Zarang himself, he buried a side of [[nerf]] as a stand-in for the body. [[Orman le Hivre]], doubtful that the body was real, suggested using a construction droid to unearth it, but [[Anya Razar]] didn't feel it worth the trouble, and the group accepted that Leonis had in fact killed Zarang.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>
