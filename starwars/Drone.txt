A '''drone''' was an unmanned craft that could be remotely [[pilot]]ed. [[Bot-drone]]s were an experimental type of drone developed for the [[Galactic Empire]], but abandoned due to unstable technology.<ref name="Target Vader 3">[[Target Vader 3|''Target Vader'' 3]]</ref> Drones could also be [[droid]]-automated, such as the drones that came equipped aboard [[Moraysian cruiser]]s.<ref name="Free Fall" /> The [[First Order]] trained its [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] using [[Microraptor-class target drone|''Microraptor''-class target drones]] designed after [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]].<ref name="Live Fire">{{Resistance|Live Fire}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Drones first appeared in ''[[Star Wars]]'' in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] continuity's "[[Bring Me the Children]]" comic strip in [[1980]]. In the current canon continuity, drones were first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]'' and first appeared in the [[2017]] novel ''[[Adventures in Wild Space: The Cold]]''.

In the real world, drones typically refer to [[Wikipedia:Unmanned aerial vehicle|unmanned aerial vehicles]] (UAVs). During Russia's [[Wikipedia:Russo-Ukrainian war (2022–present)|full-scale invasion]] of Ukraine, [[Mark Hamill]]&mdash;the actor of [[Luke Skywalker]] in ''Star Wars''&mdash;joined Ukraine's [[Wikipedia:United24|United24]] fundraising campaign in September [[2022]] as an ambassador to acquire an "Army of Drones," with [[Wikipedia:President of Ukraine|Ukrainian President]] [[Wikipedia:Volodymyr Zelenskyy|Volodymyr Zelenskyy]] thanking Hamill's support and echoing ''Star Wars''{{'}} theme that "good will triumph over evil and light will overcome darkness."<ref name="U24">{{WebCite|url=https://apnews.com/article/mark-hamill-ukraine-drones-46cfb2c53d43e7e1b4e270d0292a5f32|text=Mark Hamill talks fundraising for Ukraine, love of politics|author=Martina Rebecca Inchingolo|date=2022-12-14|work=[[Wikipedia:Associated Press|Associated Press]]|archivedate=20221214175707}}</ref> Over 500 drones were acquired via Hamill's campaign by October.<ref name="500drones">{{WebCite|url=https://www.foxnews.com/entertainment/star-wars-actor-mark-hamill-donated-500-drones-ukraine-forces-charity|text='Star Wars' actor Mark Hamill donated 500 drones to Ukraine forces through charity|author=Peter Aitken|date=2022-10-21|work=[[Wikipedia:Fox News|Fox News]]|archivedate=20221104152507}}</ref> From [[February 17]] to [[March 24]], 2023, United24 and Hamill ran a raffle for ten autographed copies of ''Resist'', a poster by Justin Aram depicting Ukraine as one X-wing facing a Russian fleet of [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]]. The partnership raised over $1.5 million USD from ''Star Wars'' fans, which bought RQ-35 Heidrun reconnaissance drones for Ukrainian defenders.<ref name="ResistPosterPortfolio">{{WebCite|url=https://justinaram.com/portfolio/resist|text=Resist Poster|work=[https://justinaram.com Portfolio of Justin Aram]|archivedate=20241211090752}}</ref><ref name="ResistPosterU24">{{WebCite|url=https://u24.gov.ua/news/hamill_posters|text=Mark Hamill Raffles Autographed Star Wars Posters in Exchange for a Donation to Support Ukraine|date=2023-02-17|work=[[Wikipedia:United24|United24]]|archivedate=20230219065228}}</ref>

Russian drone attacks continued targeting Ukrainian civilians, and in March [[2023]], Mark Hamill provided his voice for the Ukrainian "Air Alert" app's English-language version.<ref name="Hamill air alert">{{WebCite|url=https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-star-wars-luke-hamill-app-08ec03bf1a2c9c0378857090079f00f9|text=Mark Hamill lends 'Star Wars' voice to Ukrainian air-raid app|author=John Leicester|date=2023-03-28|work=[[Wikipedia:Associated Press|Associated Press]]|archivedate=20260226000941}}</ref> In August [[2024]], Hamill and historian [[Wikipedia:Timothy Snyder|Timothy Snyder]] helped United24 launch the "Safe Terrain" fundraiser for ground drones, including thirty Zmiy Ground Demining Complexes, UGV SIRKO-S1 Remote Controlled Platforms, and KNLR-E Multi-purpose Tracked Platforms to neutralize Russian mines&mdash;"these are the robots you are raising money for."<ref name="Safe Terrain">{{WebCite|url=https://u24.gov.ua/safeterrain|text=Safe Terrain|work=[[Wikipedia:United24|United24]]|archivedate=20251218060032}}</ref>
