The '''B'ankora division''' was an organization of the [[Galactic Empire]] that was part of [[Project Celestial Power]]. Following the [[Vulpter data raid]], [[General]] [[Davits Draven]] mentioned the division in a classified correspondence titled "RI 467-X1 Vulpter findings" to [[Chief of Intelligence|Chief of Rebel Intelligence]] [[Airen Cracken]] that was included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
