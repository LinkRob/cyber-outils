'''Athena Yvette Portillo''' is a line producer of several animated series from [[Lucasfilm Animation]] including ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]''. Portillo also served as an executive producer of ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]''.<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-bad-batch-series-announce|text=Star Wars: The Bad Batch, An All-New Animated Series, to Debut on Disney+ in 2021}}</ref>
