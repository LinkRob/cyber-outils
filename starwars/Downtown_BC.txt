'''Downtown BC''' was an area of [[Bralanak City]] on<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="High Republic Adventures 1">[[The High Republic Adventures (2021) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2021) 1]]</ref> [[planet]] [[Trymant IV]].<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Race to Crashpoint Tower]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> [[Zeen Mrala-Talisola|Zeen Mrala]], a former resident of Trymant, [[Jedi Master]] [[Kantam Sy]], and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Lula Talisola]] traveled to Trymant IV to locate the [[Elders of the Path]]<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" /> [[Religion|religious]] group<ref name="High Republic Adventures 1" /> to invesigate information sought by the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization. While searching for the group's [[Elders of the Path compound|compound]] in the [[city]], Mrala believed the three were a little south of the downtown, which Sy confirmed. With this, Mrala knew that they were close to the compound, and immediately after they were found by members of the Elders of the Path and taken to the compound.<ref name="Race to Crashpoint Tower" />
