During the [[Imperial Era|era]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], many individuals belonging to the '''family''' of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]] [[Arliz Hadrassian]] served alongside her within the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial Starfleet]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> before its dissolution in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the collapse of the [[Galactic Empire]] and the events of [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']], including the [[death]] of Arliz Hadrassian on [[Hosnian Prime]], to [[5 ABY]] and [[28 ABY]] respectively.</ref> Hadrassian also had a [[Arliz Hadrassian's brother|brother]] that worked as a member of the [[Emperor's Royal Guard|Royal Guard]] that protected [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] until his [[death]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]], which left his sister in possession of his [[helmet]]. When the Empire [[Galactic Concordance|capitulated]] to the [[New Republic]] after its final stand on [[Jakku]], Hadrassian became a small-time businesswoman before later becoming leader of the [[Amaxine warriors (New Republic Era)|Amaxine warriors]], a paramilitary outfit formed from surviving [[Imperial Remnants|Imperial loyalists]] like herself that possessed ties with the [[First Order]], a secretive [[First Order military|military]] [[First Order High Command|junta]] that was itself formed from [[Unknown Regions Imperial remnant|ashes]] of the Empire's [[Imperial Military|war machine]] that had retreated [[Unknown Regions|beyond]] [[The galaxy|known]] [[Realspace|space]] after its collapse.<ref name="Bloodline" />

In [[28 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> Arliz Hadrassian mentioned how various members of her family had worked within the Imperial [[Fleet|armada]] while discussing her own past services with [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]], who she later sold her brother's helmet to in hopes of converting him as a sympathiser for the First Order. During that same year, Hadrassian was responsible for [[Tai-Lin Garr's assassination|assassinating]] the [[Populists|Populist]] candidate for [[First Senator]], [[Tai-Lin Garr]], then proceeded to commit suicide, enabling her handler, [[Lady]] [[Carise Sindian]], to frame the Amaxines for being the [[Amaxine warrior crisis|sole threat]] to the New Republic and discourage investigations that could interfere with future plans her allies had devised to [[First Order secession|withdraw]] the [[Centrists|Centrist]]-represented [[star system]]s from the Republic, allowing such [[planet]]s to instead establish a legitimate basis for the First Order.<ref name="Bloodline" />
