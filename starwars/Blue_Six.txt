'''Blue Six''' was the [[callsign]] of the sixth member of [[Blue Squadron]].

==History==
[[Tenedra]] flew as Blue Six for the [[Republic Defense Coalition]]'s [[Blue Squadron (Republic Defense Coalition)|Blue Squadron]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

[[File:Swoop-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Clone pilot "Swoop" as Blue Six at the Battle of Ryloth]]
[[Clone trooper pilot]] "[[Swoop (clone trooper)|Swoop]]" flew as Blue Six at the [[Battle of Ryloth]].<ref name="X-Wing" />

[[Vangos Grek]] was a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] who used this callsign around the time of the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Visual Guide" />

During the [[Battle of Jakku]], a member of the New Republic's [[77 Squadron]] (which flew as Blue Squadron in that battle) flew under the callsign.<ref name="Hyperspace Mountain" />
