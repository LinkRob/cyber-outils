'''Ezra's broadcast''' was a message of rebellion delivered shortly after the [[raid on the communications tower]]in the [[Standard year|year]] [[4 BBY]]. Inspired by his [[parent]]s, [[Mira Bridger|Mira]] and [[Ephraim Bridger|Ephraim]], [[Ezra Bridger]]'s broadcast encouraged bravery against the [[Galactic Empire]], calling for [[the galaxy]] to resist its oppressive rule. Bridger's message of rebellion was transmitted from the hacked [[Imperial Communications Center]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]], and it was sent across the [[galaxy]] until the center was destroyed by the order of [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]].

==The broadcast==
{{ExcerptIU|''We have been called criminals, but we are not. We are rebels, fighting for the people, fighting for you. I'm not that old, but I remember a time when things were better on Lothal. Maybe not great, but never like this. See what the Empire has done to your lives, your families, and your freedom? It's only gonna get worse&hellip;unless we stand up and fight back. It won't be easy. There will be loss and sacrifice. But we can't back down just because we're afraid. That's when we need to stand the tallest. That's what my parents taught me. That's what my new family helped me remember. Stand up together. Because that's when we're strongest&mdash;as one.''|attrib=[[Ezra Bridger]]<ref name="Call to Action" />}}

==Legacy==
Despite the attempts of the [[Galactic Empire]] to quash the message, it was heard by thousands on [[Lothal]]<ref name="Call to Action" /> and in the surrounding [[star system]]s before spreading across [[the galaxy]], reaching every corner and giving thousands of people hope in their darkest moments. Resistance against the Empire grew from a collection of cells to a full-blown rebellion, the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Secret Cargo">{{Rebels|Secret Cargo}}</ref>

Ezra's message<ref name="Battle to the End">''[[Battle to the End]]''</ref> also managed to reach the very people who had inspired him to make it, his [[parent]]s [[Mira Bridger|Mira]] and [[Ephraim Bridger]], who had been arrested and [[Prison|imprisoned]] for treason. Hearing their son's message, and recognizing his voice, the Bridgers were inspired to [[Revolt in an Imperial prison|break]] themselves and dozens of other [[prisoner]]s out. Whilst they managed to help everyone else escape, the Bridgers themselves [[Death|perished]].<ref name="Legacy">{{Rebels|Legacy (episode)}}</ref>

In [[4 ABY]], shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]], [[C-3PO]] told the [[Ewok]]s on [[Endor]] about the broadcast Ezra sent to the galaxy, telling them how a boy was able to spark the rebellion with a message broadcast across the worlds.<ref name="RotJ:BtPotDS">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref>
