'''Adiana Caton''' served as the [[pilot]] of the [[Corvus (Raider II-class)|''Corvus'']], a [[Raider II-class corvette|''Raider II''-class corvette]] used by [[Inferno Squad]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. [[Weston Morro]] was Caton's copilot.<ref name="Inferno Squad" /> When [[Iden Versio]] and [[Del Meeko]] [[Skirmish on Vardos|defected]] from the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] after witnessing [[Operation: Cinder]], Adiana stayed loyal to their comrades and allowed a number of fleeing civilians aboard the ''Corvus'' in accordance with Iden's wishes before fleeing Vardos and the Empire.<ref name="Battlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
