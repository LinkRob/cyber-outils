'''1984 Kellogg's ''Star Wars'' Series''', also known as '''''Star Wars Sticker/Trading Cards''''', was a trading card set released by Kellogg's in specifically marked boxes of [[C-3PO's]] breakfast cereal. Each box contained two cards from the full ten-card set.<ref name="Tales" /> The stickers on the front of the cards peeled off to reveal another photo on the trading card below, while text on the back describes characters and scenes from the original ''[[Star Wars]]'' films.<ref name="Commercial 2">[[Kellogg's C-3PO's cereal commercial 2]]</ref>

The cards are in the format of Stick'r Trading Cards, which featured other subjects and were included with various Kellogg's cereals over the years.<ref name="TCDB" />
