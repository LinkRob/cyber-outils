'''Antar 4''', also known simply as '''Antar''' and the '''Gotal moon''', was a [[moon]] that [[orbit]]ed [[Antar]], a [[planet]] located in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]], that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Gotal]] [[species]]. It was taken over by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. As a result, it was later targeted by an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] operation that came to be known as the [[Antar Atrocity]].

==Description==
An [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]]<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> [[moon]], Antar 4<ref name="Tarkin" /> [[orbit]]ed [[Antar]], a [[planet]] located in grid square N-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Beginner Game" />

==History==
{{Quote|Not all of us were Separatist.|A [[Unidentified Koorivar captain|Koorivar captain]]|<ref name="Tarkin" />}}
In [[620 BBY]], a [[Law enforcement agency|security force]] known as the [[Antarian Rangers]] was created on the [[planet]] to aid the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="HC 2" />

A member of the [[Galactic Republic]] almost from its inception, the [[Separatist Crisis]] created a [[Antar 4 schism|schism]] among Antar 4's [[Gotal]]s that gave rise to [[terrorist]] groups aligned with the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Tarkin" /> such as the [[Roshu Sune]], a radical splinter group of the [[Gotal Assembly for Separation]]. Despite a Roshu Sune [[Roshu Sune bombing campaign|terrorist bombing campaign]] in the final [[Standard month|months]] before the eruption of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> Gotal loyalists, shored up by the Republic, managed to retain power. However, shortly after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> the moon fell to Separatist forces, and for a brief period, became a headquarters for its leader, [[Count]] [[Dooku]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

As a result, Antar 4 became a political imbroglio that spawned one of the first resistance groups, made up of loyalist [[Koorivar]] and Gotals whom the Republic supported with tactical advisers and secret shipments of arms and materiel. Though the resistance was successful in carrying out hundreds of acts of sabotage, the moon remained under Separatist control for the conflict's duration.<ref name="Tarkin" /> At some point, a [[Battle of Antar 4]] was fought, with at least one [[BX-series droid commando]] seeing use and proving itself a valuable fighter.<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref>

After the war,<ref name="Tarkin" /> in [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Republic's successor, was ordered to make an example of Antar 4 for supporting the Confederacy of Independent Systems<ref name="Tarkin" /> as a warning to former Separatists.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> No attempt was to be made at repatriation, nor was he to waste [[time]] sorting Separatists from resistance fighters and intelligence operatives waiting to be exfiltrated to safety. Instead, many Koorivar and Gotal loyalists were swept up in the ensuing arrests, [[Death|executions]], and massacres that came to be known as the [[Antar Atrocity]]. As a result of its actions on Antar 4, former [[Republic Intelligence]] [[captain]] [[Berch Teller]], who lost many operatives in the Imperial reprisal, created a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] to combat the Empire within a year of the attack.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Inhabitants==
Antar 4 was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] Gotal [[species]].<ref name="Gotal" /> When it was taken over by the Separatists, tens of millions of Gotal [[refugee]]s fled to their colony world, [[Atzerri]], replaced by an influx of Koorivar, [[Gossam]]s, and other species whose homeworlds had joined the Separatists.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==Behind the scenes==
Antar 4 was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], a novel written by [[James Luceno]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> and released on [[November 4]], [[2014]].<ref name="Tarkin release date">{{SW|url=news/disney-publishing-worldwide-and-random-house-announce-relaunch-of-star-wars-adult-fiction-line|text=Disney Publishing Worldwide and Random House Announce Relaunch of Star Wars Adult Fiction Line}}</ref> It was originally created for ''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]'', a [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] supplement written by [[Troy Denning]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''<ref name="Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races">''[[Galaxy Guide 4: Alien Races]]''</ref> and released in late [[1989]].<ref name="GG4-Date">''Galaxy Guide 4'' is listed in the merchandise section of [[The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 10|''The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine'' 10]], which was published at the start of [[1990]]. [[The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine 9|''The Lucasfilm Fan Club Magazine'' 9]], which was published in fall [[1989]], only lists ''Galaxy Guide 1'' and ''Galaxy Guide 2'', suggesting ''Galaxy Guide 4'' was published afterwards.</ref>

''Tarkin'' mistakenly claimed that Antar IV was located in the galaxy's [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> In addition, Antar 4 was mistakenly labelled simply as "Antar" on ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''{{'s}} galaxy map.<ref name="Galactic Atlas" />
