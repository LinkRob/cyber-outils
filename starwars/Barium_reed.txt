'''Barium reeds''' were a type of [[Plant|reed]]<ref name="TRS" /> that partially shared their name with [[barium]], a [[poison]]ous substance that was present in [[starship]] [[fuel]]s.<ref name="RSG">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref> In [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[Nihil]] member [[Udi Dis]] accompanied [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] to the [[Temperature|cold]], lifeless [[planet]] of [[Rystan]] to [[Mission to Rystan|meet]] the latter's [[Ro family|cousin]], a [[Gender|woman]] named [[Kufa]]. Dis was skeptical of the woman's frail appearance, thinking to himself that Ro could snap her like a barium reed.<ref name="TRS" />

==Behind the scenes==
