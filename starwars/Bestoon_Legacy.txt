The '''''Bestoon Legacy''''' was a modified [[WTK-85A interstellar transport]] and the personal [[starship]] of [[Sith cultist|Sith loyalist]] [[Ochi]] of [[Bestoon]].

==History==
[[File:BestoonLegacy-CGSWG.png|thumb|250px|left|Aft view of the engines of the ''Bestoon Legacy'']]
Ochi used the ''Bestoon Legacy'' throughout his [[assassin]] career, including during the [[Quest for Exegol|quest to find]] the [[Youngling|child]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], granddaughter of the resurrected [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]]. During the quest, Ochi allied with the [[Corporate Sector Authority]] and their liaison [[Enric Pryde]], a former [[Imperial officer]]. The craft was briefly stolen by Rey's parents, [[Miramir]] and [[Dathan]], to return Rey to the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], only for a drunken Ochi to [[Death|kill]] them and reclaim his ship. Traveling to the planet [[Pasaana]] on a false lead on Rey's location,<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> Ochi died after sinking in the [[Shifting Mires]], leaving his ship behind.<ref name="Episode IX" /> For years the ship the intakes and thrusters began to fill with sand-clogs and nomadic [[solif-spider]]s left tons of silk strands.<ref name="TROS VD" />

[[File:LurchCanyon.png|thumb|250px|left|The ''Bestoon Legacy'' on Pasaana.]]
During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], the ship was used by [[Poe Dameron]], [[Finn]], Rey, [[C-3PO]], and BB-8 after the [[First Order]] captured the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. After Rey apparently killed [[Chewbacca]] by accident, the group escaped Pasaana in the ship, with Poe hiding the ship in an [[Asteroid field|asteroid belt]]. Though they lost [[Blade of Ochi of Bestoon|the]] [[dagger]], its inscription was in C-3PO's memory. So, the group decided to head to [[Kijimi]] so that [[Babu Frik]] can perform a memory bypass, unaware that the ''[[Night Buzzard]]'', the [[Knights of Ren]]'s ship, was following them. The ship made planetfall and landed in [[Kijimi City]], which was cracking down under the First Order's occupation.

[[File:FirstOrderDataMedallion-TROS.jpg|thumb|250px|right|Poe using a data-medallion in the ''Bestoon Legacy''.]]
After Babu Frik successfully retrieved the translation at the cost of C-3PO's memories, the group returned to the ''Bestoon Legacy'' and flew to the [[Steadfast (Resurgent-class)|''Steadfast'']] hovering above the city to rescue Chewbacca. Poe used [[Zorii Bliss]]' [[First Order transit data-medallion|First Order Captain's Medallion]] to get clearance to land aboard one of the ship's hangars, [[Hangar 12]]. [[Allegiant General]] Pryde,<ref name="Episode IX" /> the very same former Imperial whom had met Ochi and seen his ship up close,<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> and a detachment of [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] went into the hangar, where Pryde was surprised to see the vessel and asked one of his subordinates who the ship belonged to. Poe Dameron's group later recovered the ''Falcon'' and rescued Chewbacca, and the First Order [[Battle of Exegol|met its demise]] soon after.<ref name="Episode IX" />
