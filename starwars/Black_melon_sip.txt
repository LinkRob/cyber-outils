'''Black melon sip''' was a [[Foodstuff|beverage]] advertised and sold in the [[Grand Arena]] during the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] sporting event. This [[melon]]-based drink was priced at six [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] on the menu of the [[The Restraining Bolt]], an [[Restaurant|eatery]] found on the bottom level of the [[Mos Espa Waystation]], one of the Grand Arena's battlefields located on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> [[Black melon]]s, a [[plant]] found among the [[desert]]s of [[Tatooine]],<ref name="SW7" /> served as the beverage's namesake.<ref name="Hunters" />
