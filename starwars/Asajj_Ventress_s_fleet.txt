During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Commander]] [[Asajj Ventress]] was in [[Commanding officer|command]] of a '''fleet''' of [[warship]]s as part of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|naval forces]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The [[fleet]] participated in the [[Battle of Sullust]], where it engaged [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Navy|naval forces]] led by [[Jedi General]]s [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]], as well as [[Admiral]] [[Wullf Yularen]].<ref name="Nightsisters" />

The fleet included at least two [[starfighter]] [[squadron]]s, [[Trident group one]] and [[Trident group two]]. The fleet consisted of a [[Unidentified Providence-class carrier/destroyer (Battle of Sullust)|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] that served as the [[flagship]], a [[TJ-912's Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class light destroyer]] that was helmed by Commander [[TJ-912]], another ''Recusant''-class light [[destroyer]], and six [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]]. The flagship was destroyed by TJ-912's vessel after the fleet received orders to [[Death|kill]] Asajj Ventress.<ref name="Nightsisters" />
