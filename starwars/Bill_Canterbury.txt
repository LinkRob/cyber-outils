'''Bill Canterbury''' is a writer for ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', a [[Wikipedia:Computer-generated imagery|CGI]]-based television series that debuted [[October 3]], [[2008]] on [[Cartoon Network]] as a continuation of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|film with the same name]], released two months prior. Bill also has written for TV shows such as [[Wikipedia:The Simpsons|''The Simpsons'']] ([[1993]]-[[1994]]), [[Wikipedia:Scooby-Doo, Where Are You?|''Scooby-Doo, Where Are You?'']] ([[2004]]-[[2005]]), and [[Wikipedia:Phil of the Future|''Phil of the Future'']] (2005).
