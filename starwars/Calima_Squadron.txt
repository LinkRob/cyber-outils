'''Calima Squadron''' was a secretive [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] formed by [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] as part of one of the earliest [[Bail Organa's resistance movement|rebel cells]] created on the [[planet]] [[Crait]]. The squadron undertook missions to sabotage outposts of the [[Galactic Empire]] across [[the galaxy]]. The squadron's [[starship]]s were [[Color|red]] with white stripes.<ref name="Squadrons" />

==Behind the scenes==
The colors of Calima Squadron is one of the cosmetic options available to customize [[New Republic]] starfighters in the [[2020]] game ''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''.<ref name="Squadrons" />
