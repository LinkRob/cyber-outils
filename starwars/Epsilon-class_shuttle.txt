The '''''Epsilon''-class shuttle''' was a transport class used by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Challenge">{{ChallengeCite|38|Starfighters Down}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The ''Epsilon''-class shuttle was created for the [[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game|''Star Wars'' D6]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] adventure "[[Starfighters Down]]" by [[William W. Connors]], published in the magazine [[Challenge 38|''Challenge'' 38]] in [[1989]].<ref name="Challenge" />

In the adventure, the class was only mentioned in the adventure's overview as being present on [[Phaylenn]]. Subsequently, all references to stealing a [[Hyperdrive/Legends|hyperdrive]] from a shuttle mention the [[Lambda-class T-4a shuttle/Legends|''Lambda''-class shuttle]], so this article assumes the target is of the latter class.<ref name="Challenge" />
