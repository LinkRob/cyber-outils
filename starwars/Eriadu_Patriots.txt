The '''Eriadu Patriots''' were a sports team of some sort within the [[Galactic Empire]], based on [[Eriadu]]. [[Merei Spanjaf]] was once bored by a broadcast describing the [[Galactic League]] [[Galactic League commissioner|commissioner's]] review of a proposed trade between the Patriots and the [[Shad (city)|Shad]] [[Shad Furies|Furies]].<ref name="Ranks" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the continuity of [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the Patriots were a [[Shockball/Legends|shockball]] team.<ref name="essential">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-guide-to-warfare-authors-cut|series=Star Wars: The Essential Guide to Warfare Author's Cut|text=Part 8: Tales of the Clone Wars}}</ref> The Patriots, however, are only mentioned in passing in ''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]'', and shockball is not specifically mentioned.
