'''Chalactans''' were [[human]]s<ref name="SWCEUE" /> [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Chalacta]].<ref name="HC 9" /> Two prominent Chalactans were [[Jedi Master]] [[Depa Billaba]]<ref name="databank">{{Databank|depa-billaba|Depa Billaba}}</ref> and her sister [[Sar Labooda]].<ref name="Ultimate">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chalactans made their first appearance in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first film of the [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace">{{Film|I}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] Chalactans were presented as a [[Near-Human]] [[Species/Legends|species]].<ref name="The Essential Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>{{Species-stub}}
