'''Adratharpe 7''' was an astronomical object<ref name="Lost Treasure">"[[The Crimson Corsair and the Lost Treasure of Count Dooku]]"</ref> in the [[Adratharpe system]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]], in grid square H-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[pirate]] [[Sidon Ithano]] and his crew sought the "Lost Treasure of Adratharpe 7,"<ref name="Smuggler">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> a quest that resulted in [[Skirmish of Adratharpe 7|fighting]] and saw Ithano become known as the "Scourer of Adratharpe 7."<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy">''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
When asked about the similarly-named [[Adrathorpe]] that appeared on a map in ''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]'', [[Jason Fry]], the author of the [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]] appendix, agreed that Adrathorpe was likely a misspelling but that it would be kept regardless as a case similar to [[Alderaan]] and [[Aldereen system|Aldereen]].<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-09-15.png|author=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-09-15}}</ref>
