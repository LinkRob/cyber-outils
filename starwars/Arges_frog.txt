The '''arges frog''',<ref name="Bestiary" /> also known as '''Sorgan frogs'''<ref name="Merch">{{SW|url=news/the-child-merch-from-the-mandalorian-is-here|text=UPDATED: 'The Child' Merch from ''The Mandalorian'' is Here!}}</ref> were a [[species]] of one-[[eye]]d [[frog]]s named for the [[forest]] [[planet]] [[Sorgan]],<ref name="Sanctuary" /> though they could be found on a number of other worlds. The [[Mandalorian]] [[foundling]] [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] had a particular taste for these [[amphibian]]s and often tried to [[Hunting|hunt]] them during his travels across [[the galaxy]].<ref name="The Child" /><ref name="Comes a Stranger" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Mando Date">{{MandoDate|4|the appearance of [[Sorgan]] [[frog]]s}}</ref> the [[Youngling|child]] consumed a Sorgan frog on an [[Arvala-7]] [[Kuiil's moisture farm|moisture farm]],<ref name="The Child" /> and later, while visiting a [[Unidentified village (Sorgan)|village]] on the animal's namesake planet, attempted to do the same to another frog. Grogu halted, however, when the [[Gender|girl]] [[Winta]] and several other children reacted in disgust, allowing the frog to escape into a nearby trough.<ref name="Sanctuary" />

Around the same [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="BOBF Date">{{BoBFDate}}</ref> on [[Tatooine]], several Sorgan frogs were boiled alive and prepared as a [[Foodstuff|meal]] for [[Daimyo]] [[Bib Fortuna]] at [[Boba Fett's Palace|his palace]] by an [[Unidentified EV-series sous-chef droid|EV-series sous-chef]]. The amphibians escaped, however, when the former [[bounty hunter]] [[Boba Fett]] chased an [[Unidentified LEP-series service droid|LEP-series ratcatcher]] through the palace kitchen.<ref name="Gathering Storm">{{BOBF|Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm}}</ref> Sometime later, Grogu left his caretaker [[Din Djarin]] to [[Jedi training|study]] the ways of [[the Force]] with [[Luke Skywalker]]. While meditating with his [[Jedi Master]] on [[Ossus]], Grogu used his [[Telekinesis|Force powers]] to pull a Sorgan frog from a pond. In an effort to show his [[Padawan]] what was truly possible with these [[Force power|powers]], Skywalker effortlessly lifted dozens of frogs from the pond before gently returning them to their home.<ref name="Comes a Stranger" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Hasbro's Star Wars: The Black Series The Child figure stands at just 1.12-inches tall, with premium detail, multiple points of articulation, and accessories including a bone broth cup, a ball, and a tasty Sorgan frog.|StarWars.com description of the figure|<ref name="Merch" />}}
[[File:The_child_eats_arvala_7_frog.png|thumb|left|200px|The Sorgan frog's first appearance]]
Sorgan frogs first appeared in "[[Chapter 2: The Child]]," an episode of the [[The Mandalorian Season One|first season]] of [[Jon Favreau]]'s television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]''. The episode was directed by [[Rick Famuyiwa]] and aired on [[Disney+]]<ref name="The Child" /> on [[November 15]], [[2019]].<ref name="Airdate">{{WebCite|url=https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf|text=The Mandalorian Media Kit|date=2019-09-24|format=[[Wikipedia:PDF|PDF]]|work=[[Wikipedia:Walt Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International|Walt Disney Direct-to-Consumer & International]]|pages=[https://lumiere-a.akamaihd.net/v1/documents/the_mandalorian_project_one-sheet_10_ec1e005f.pdf#page=1 p. 1]|archivedate=20210623202717}}</ref> On [[December 23]] of that year, the creatures were subsequently identified as Sorgan frogs in a [[StarWars.com]] article detailing merchandise from the show. The article revealed that a frog figure would come as an accessory for Grogu, then known as "the Child" in the ''[[Star Wars: The Black Series]]'' toyline.<ref name="Merch" />
