'''Carni chips''' were a type of [[Foodstuff|food]] item that could be served with [[glaze sauce]]. The [[Wermal]] [[Merchant|salesbeing]] [[Kedpin Shoklop]] noticed carni chips with glaze [[sauce]] being served during [[midmeal]] at the [[Canto Casino Hotel]] in the [[city]] of [[Canto Bight]] on the [[planet]] [[Cantonica]] during his stay there<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{Film|VII}} is set in [[34 ABY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'' states that the events of [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']] take place in the same [[Standard year|year]] as the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|Starkiller Incident]], which is depicted in ''The Force Awakens''. Since the events of the [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] collection, which include the events of "[[Rules of the Game]]," take place between ''The Force Awakens'' and ''The Last Jedi'', they must take place in 34 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]] as well.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Carni chips were mentioned in [[Saladin Ahmed]]'s [[2017]] short story "[[Rules of the Game]]," written for the novella collection [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']].<ref name="Rules of the Game" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Carni chip/Legends|carni chips]] were mentioned in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[2013]] novel ''[[Scoundrels]]''.<ref name="Scoundrels">''[[Scoundrels]]''</ref>
