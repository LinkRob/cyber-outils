The '''A43 Hushabye''', or shortened to '''A43''', was a model of [[blaster pistol]]. It was owned by [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Lieutenant]] [[Tanivos Exantor Divo]].<ref name="Blasters" /> He wore the [[blaster]] on the left side of his police [[utility belt]] with the handle facing forward.<ref name="Senate M">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref> The criminal [[Lauli Wahlo]] also owned a A43 Hushabye pistol around that same time, although he added a silencer.<ref name="LL Ep Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/lightsaber-lost-episode-guide|text="Lightsaber Lost" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The A43 Hushabye pistol first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season Two|Season Two]] episode "[[Lightsaber Lost]]",<ref name="Lightsaber Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref>  which aired on [[January 22]], [[2010]].<ref name="LL Ep Guide old">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode211.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> It was created for the character Tan Divo in the later episode<ref name="LL Ep Guide" /> "[[Senate Murders]]",<ref name="Senate M">{{TCW|Senate Murders}}</ref> but was repurposed for Lauli Wahlo in "Lightsaber Lost". It was fully shown in concept art of Wahlo's model done by<ref name="LL Ep Guide" /> [[Wayne Lo]]<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" /> on [[May 19]], [[2008]].<ref name="LL Ep Guide" /> It was later first identified in the current canon in the [[2015]] article "''Star Wars'' Blasters: So Uncivilized, But So Awesome" on [[StarWars.com]] by [[Tim Veekhoven]]<ref name="Blasters" /> and had previously been first identified in the 2010 ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="TCWE">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
