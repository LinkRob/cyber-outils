The '''Aquatic Programmer''' was a costume owned by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Sprocket]] that he could don for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Prodigal Programmer|standard costume]]. This [[Clothing|costume]] consisted of a [[Color|pink]], purple, and orange uniform, with dark brown [[boot]]s. It also included a purple [[Sprocket's datapad|datapad]] with gray components and an orange decal.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Aquatic Programmer was an "Epic" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Sprocket, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 3.0.0 alongside Season 3 on [[September 23]], [[2024]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Aquatic Chair]].<ref name="Hunters" />
