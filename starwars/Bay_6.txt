'''Bay 6''' was an location on one side of the [[Vandor Railyard]], a battlefield of the [[Grand Arena]] utilized by the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]]. This area could be accessed via two large sets of [[blast door]]s, which remained opened during matches and were marked by large bold [[Aurebesh]] text that read "Bay 6." This area housed a number of features, including spare [[20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport]] [[train]]s, as well as a sealed room containing a [[Holoprojector|holographic]] display. Bay 6 also doubled as a starting point for one of the two teams of [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunters]] at the beginning of each match.<ref name="Hunters" />
