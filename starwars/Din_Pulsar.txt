The '''Din Pulsar'''<ref name="BTM">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> was situated in the [[Moddell sector]],<ref name="TRF" /> a [[Sector|subsector]] of the [[Zuma sector]]<ref name="Appendix" /> of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="TRF" /> It lay in grid square H-16 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="TFABG" /> Located near the [[Din Nebula]],<ref name="BTM" /> the [[pulsar]] was depicted on a [[map]] of the Moddell [[sector]] included with the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] collection of non-electronic documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="TRF" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Din Pulsar was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a mention in the [[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon 18|eighteenth issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Build the Millennium Falcon]]'' magazine,<ref name="BTM" /> which was published around [[May 6]], [[2015]].<ref name="BMF18date">{{BMFdates|18}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Din Pulsar/Legends|Din Pulsar]] was introduced in the article "[[Endor and the Moddell Sector]]," which was published in the [[Star Wars Gamer 9|ninth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Gamer]]'' magazine<ref name="Endor">{{GamerCite|9|Endor and the Moddell Sector}}</ref> on [[February 22]], [[2002]].<ref name="GamerDate">{{SWArchive|url=gaming/other/rpg/news20020222.html|text=Starfighters Soar in Gamer #9}}</ref>
