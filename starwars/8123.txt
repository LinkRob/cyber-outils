'''8123''' was an individual who served as a guard of the [[Benelex Marshal Service]] at a [[Gulhadar prison|prison]] on the [[planet]] [[Gulhadar III|Gulhadar]].<ref name="Han Chewie 7" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> 8123 was present in the [[prison]]'s mess hall when the [[Wookiee]] [[prisoner]] [[Chewbacca]] collapsed to the floor after taking pills created by fellow prisoner [[Phaedra]]. As they made their way to Chewbacca, the guard informed other prisoners who had gathered around the Wookiee to move out of the way. 8123 then pulled out a [[comlink]] and informed medical that a prisoner was down. The guard then checked Chewbacca's pulse and updated their report to medical, informing them that the prisoner was actually [[Death|deceased]]. In reality, however, Chewbacca was not dead, and was just made to seem that way as part of Phaedra's plan to escape the prison.<ref name="Han Chewie 7" />
