The '''Corbos system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Esstran sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> At one point, it was protected by a [[Jedi Watchman]]. However, the [[Unidentified Jedi Watchman|Watchman]] became corrupted after being anonymously gifted a [[Neer's Gauntlet|gauntlet]] crafted by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Neer]], leading his fellow [[Jedi]] to strike him down.<ref name="NOP">''[[Nexus of Power]]''</ref>
