During the early [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], a '''bombing''' was the result of simultaneous missions to [[Raven's Peak]] on the [[planet]] [[Eriadu]] by [[Clone Force 99]] and [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]]. The two groups independently infiltrated Raven's Peak, and each side had different objectives. Clone Force 99 sought to locate [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]]'s whereabouts, and in turn, find and rescue their brother [[Crosshair]], who had been held [[Prisoner|captive]] by Hemlock. The Partisans on the other hand sought to [[Assassin|assassinate]] [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] and used the [[Cloning|clones]] in order to do so.<ref name="The Summit" />

==History==
===Prelude===
During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a [[Unidentified Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser (Balmorra)|''Gozanti''-class cruiser]] in the [[Balmorra system]] was raided by a group of rogue [[clone trooper]]s. The cruiser, commanded by [[Captain]] [[Pearce]], was successfully taken by the clones and its logs were taken by [[Echo]]. Echo brought the data logs to [[Pabu]], hoping that [[Tech]] could decipher it. Tech's efforts proved successful, and he informed Clone Force 99 that Doctor Royce Hemlock of the Galactic Empire was experimenting on clones. These clones happened to include [[Crosshair|CT-9904]] "Crosshair," a former member of Clone Force 99. Not long after, Crosshair attempted to escape from [[Mount Tantiss]] after [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] failed to interrogate the clone in the [[Tantiss Base]].<ref name="Tipping Point" />

==The mission==
===Infiltration===
When Bad Batch infiltrated Eriadu on their ship the ''[[Marauder (Omicron-class)|Marauder]]'', he used the transport sky rail to infiltrate the heavily guarded Raven's Peak. After the hunters took control of the hangar's control room, [[Omega]] and [[Wrecker]] attached a tracking device to Hemlock's shuttle. However, Echo noticed something suspicious in the facility, prompting [[Hunter]] and Tech to investigate. The two encountered [[Saw Gerrera]] in Sector 4 and learned about their plans.<ref name="The Summit" />

===Sabotage and Escape===
Hunter and Tech tried to persuade them to stop the bombing plan, but Gerrera insisted on assassinating the empire's dignitaries and refused to listen, even though Tech argued it would be more prudent to gather intelligence on the Empire's plans and that the Empire would merely replace the leadership he wipes out. In the end, they were found by TK Trooper guards, forcing Gerrera to partially abandon their plan and withdraw. The partisans took the low-class transport shuttle and escaped, activating the bombs they had planted. As a result, Hemlock's ship, which was equipped with a tracking device, was also destroyed. Meanwhile, the Bad Batch escaped on the cable car,<ref name="The Summit" /> but the vehicle broke down in the middle of the rail and stopped.<ref name="Plan 99" />

===Tech's Death===
Although Tech managed to restore power, the rear vehicle was shot off the rails by the V-Wing Starfighter. Hanging from the rear vehicle, Tech shot through the connecting part of the vehicle to let even his comrades in the front vehicle escape. Due to Tech's self-sacrifice, the Bad Batch managed to reach the ''Marauder'' and escape from Eriadu.<ref name="Plan 99" />
