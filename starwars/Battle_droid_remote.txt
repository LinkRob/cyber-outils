One model of '''remotes''' were [[training droid]]s designed to simulate [[battle droid]]s. They shot [[Laser|blaster bolts]] from its [[sensor]] and had four extra [[blaster]]s hidden inside. Many [[Jedi]], including [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], trained with these [[droid]]s as part of a test.

==Characteristics==
[[File:BattleDroidRemote.png|thumb|270px|left|A battle droid remote in closed form]]
[[Battle droid]] [[remote]]s were [[training droid]]s designed to simulate battle [[droid]]s,<ref name="PMP" /> the main forces of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> The droids were spherical in shape and shot [[Color|yellow]] [[Laser|blaster bolts]] from its [[sensor]] that could be deflected by a [[lightsaber]]. The droids also had four<ref name="PMP" /> extra [[blaster]]s<ref name="Audio" /> hidden inside. They could hover high above the ground and move rapidly. They had gray and brown plating and a green sensor.<ref name="PMP" />

==History==
{{Quote|It's a lousy test.''"<br />"''But it's what every Jedi does.''"<br />"''Exactly.|Anakin Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="PMP" />}}
[[File:AhsokaTrainingBattleDroidRemotes.png|thumb|right|300px|Ahsoka Tano training with four battle droid remotes]]
Battle droid remotes were considered new<ref name="PMP" /> during or after [[21 BBY]].<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|211}} As [[Ahsoka Tano]] interacts with [[Tera Sinube]] at the beginning of "[[Practice Makes Perfect (episode)|Practice Makes Perfect]]," it must be set after "Lightsaber Lost".</ref> At the [[time]], every [[Jedi]] trained with the remotes as part of a test. [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]] trained with four of these droids in the [[Jedi Temple dojo|dojo]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] under the tutelage of<ref name="PMP" /> [[Jedi Master]]<ref name="Tera Sinube DB">{{Databank|tera-sinube|Tera Sinube}}</ref> [[Tera Sinube]]. Tano used [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|her lightsaber]] to deflect blasts from the droids until the exercise ended and they hovered upwards. As Sinube summoned a [[force field]] to surround Tano, the droids hovered around Tano and revealed their extra blasters. Sinube tapped [[Tera Sinube's lightsaber|his lightsaber]] on the field, which activated the exercise. While the force field decreased in size, Tano deflected the droids' blasts, managing to destroy two. [[Gender|She]] then flipped in the air and sliced the remaining droids with her lightsaber. The force field then receded, ending the exercise. Afterwards, Tano's master, [[Anakin Skywalker]], explained that the exercise was a lousy test because it was done by every Jedi.<ref name="PMP" />
