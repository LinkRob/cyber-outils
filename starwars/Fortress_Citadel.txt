The '''Fortress Citadel''' was a location within [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on the [[ocean]] [[moon]] of [[Nur]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> in the [[Mustafar system]].<ref name="Art of Fallen Order">''[[The Art of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> It was connected to the highly secured [[Interrogation Chamber]] by a long [[bridge]], and had two [[turret]] emplacements outside the door to the chamber.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> during his [[Secret mission to rebuild the Jedi Order|mission]] to restore the [[Jedi Order]], Jedi Knight [[Cal Kestis]] [[Mission to Nur|infiltrated]] the [[Inquisitorius]] [[fortress]] in search of a [[holocron]] that was stolen by the [[Inquisitor]] [[Second Sister]] on the [[planet]] [[Bogano]]. On his mission, Kestis traveled through the Fortress Citadel in order to reach the Interrogation Chamber, and traveled through once more while being pursued by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] after his [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] with the Second Sister.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Fortress Citadel appeared in the [[2019]] video game ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]] and released by [[Electronic Arts]].<ref name="Fallen Order" />
