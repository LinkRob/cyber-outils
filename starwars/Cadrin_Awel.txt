'''Cadrin Awel''' was a [[Gender/Legends|female]] [[Human/Legends|Human]] who was romantically linked to [[General/Legends|General]] [[Stavin Thaal]]. As a teenager, Awel performed as a singer but was unable to make an impact on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] after winning local contests on her homeworld. During the [[Yuuzhan Vong War]], Awel and her family hid on [[Vandor-3]] to evade notice from the [[Yuuzhan Vong]]. Before the [[Second Galactic Civil War]], Awel established a boot camp on Vandor-3 for people to learn nature skills. Her relationship with Thaal went undocumented and she was last recorded as having married a fellow wartime survivor in [[42 ABY]].<ref name="Mercy Kill" />
