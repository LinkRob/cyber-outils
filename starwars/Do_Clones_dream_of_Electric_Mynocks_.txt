"'''Do Clones dream of Electric Mynocks?'''" is the thirteenth in a series of comics on the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] website. It is a continuation of the story-arc started with "[[A droid in the hand is worth two in the asteroid belt|A droid in the hand is worth]]." It was placed on the website [[June 11]], [[2009]].

==Plot summary==
"Do clone troopers dream of electric mynocks?"

The story begins with a [[TIE Advanced x1/Legends|TIE Advanced x1]] flying through [[Realspace/Legends|space]]. This confuses the narrator since it is off track from the ending of the [[Clones don't even know the meaning of the word surrender|last story]]. The flight turns out to be a [[Dream/Legends|dream]] of the [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone trooper]] [[1137]], who is woken up by [[1139|his buddy]]. The two are in a crater along with the remains of their hijacked [[Armored Assault Tank/Legends|tank]]. Outside the crater, the group's [[Clone Commander/Legends|commander]] is shown berating their pilot for leaving them after taking off and then bombing their tank when the pilot mistook it as an enemy. Meanwhile, the team's [[Gunner/Legends|gunner]] asks for help to assemble a [[Walker/Legends|walker]]. 1139 eagerly volunteers, but 1137 decides to do some wandering. As the two build the walker, two [[V-19 Torrent starfighter/Legends|V-19 Torrent starfighters]] land nearby. The [[Pilot/Legends|pilots]] had heard that there were some [[Droid/Legends|droid]] tanks to blow up. This news upsets the commander, who furiously shouts that no tanks are to be blown up. This brings relief to several [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]] that just arrived in [[Armored Assault Tank/Legends|Armored Assault Tanks]]. The commander pulls outs his blasters and a fight breaks out. Elsewhere, 1137 continues to wander, unaware of the skirmish and the Torrent starfighters taking off behind him. As he remembers his dream, [[Ahsoka Tano/Legends|Ahsoka Tano]] lands her [[Delta-7B Aethersprite-class light interceptor/Legends|Jedi starfighter]] in front of 1137 and ask him if he's seen [[R2-D2/Legends|R2-D2]].

"TO BE CONTINUED!"
