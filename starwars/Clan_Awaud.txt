'''Clan Awaud''' was a [[Mandalorian clan]] that splintered during the [[Clone Wars]]. The splinter faction of Clan Awaud was part of the [[Old Mandalorians]] and was based on [[Vlemoth Port]]. It was led by [[Chief]]tain [[Nam Beroya]] until his supposed [[death]], and was succeeded by his [[Foundling|adoptive]] daughter, [[Vera Beroya]].<ref name="Friends" />

Clan Awaud allied themselves with the residents of [[Foundry Four]] on [[Xorrn]] and sympathizers of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], and fought against the [[Galactic Empire]] in the [[Battle of Xorrn]]. The clan led a [[Vlemoth Port battalion|battalion]] of 800 [[Mandalorian warrior]]s from Vlemoth Port alone, as other warriors were out on missions.<ref name="Friends" />
