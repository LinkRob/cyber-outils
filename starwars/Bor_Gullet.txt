{{Quote|Bor Gullet can feel your thoughts. No lie is safe. ''[&hellip;]'' Bor Gullet will know the truth. The unfortunate side-effect is that one tends to lose one's mind.|Saw Gerrera, to Bodhi Rook|<ref name="Rogue One" />}}
'''Bor Gullet''' was a [[Color|purple]]-skinned [[Mairan]] with the [[Telepathy|ability to read thoughts]]. [[Saw Gerrera]] used it to [[torture]] [[Bodhi Rook]] on [[Jedha]] to ascertain the truth behind his claim of being in contact with [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen Erso]]. Afterwards, Rook was for a time left a gibbering wreck, unable to speak a coherent thought and acting very panicky. Despite this, Rook showed some resilience, as he recovered upon [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]] referring to him to as "the [[pilot]]" and mentioning Erso. Rook was able to recover enough of his senses to escape from Jedha when [[Jedha City]] was targeted by the [[superlaser]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="Rogue One" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Bor has this incredible mind but is hampered by body, so we used that design as inspiration. He's a bit like Jabba the Hutt.|Neal Scanlan|<ref name="Rogue One Mission Debrief">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story - The Official Mission Debrief]]''</ref>}}
Bor Gullet first appeared in the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars'' Anthology]] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One" /> The creature was illustrated by [[Ivan Manzella]] and designed by [[Neal Scanlan]], and it was sculpted at full size, which had never been attempted before. Two and a half tons of silicone were used in the process, and Bor Gullet was ultimately realized as a life-size, 10 by 6 by 6 foot puppet operated by a crew of fifteen puppeteers.<ref name="Rogue One Mission Debrief" />
