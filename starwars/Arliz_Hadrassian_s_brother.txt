A [[Gender|male]] '''human<ref name="Human" /> ''' was the brother of the human [[Arliz Hadrassian]]. Hadrassian's brother served as a member of the [[Emperor's Royal Guard]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. He was intended to accompany [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] to the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], but was unable to due to an illness, thus missing out on the [[Battle of Endor]]<ref name="Bloodline" /> in [[4 ABY]]. In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> He was among the guards who had [[Death|perished]] at the [[Battle of Jakku]]. Hadrassian remembered her brother through his guardsmen [[helmet]], although she later sold the item to [[New Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Ransolm Casterfo]] to gain his trust. While conversing with Casterfo, Hadrassian told him about her brother and remarked that had he been present, her brother could have changed history at the Battle of [[Endor]]. Casterfo thought it would be unlikely for a single [[guard]] to thwart the entire [[Rebel Alliance Navy|rebel fleet]], though he did not tell Hadrassian this.<ref name="Bloodline" /> As a member of the Emperor's Royal Guard, Arliz Hadrassian's brother was at least 1.83 [[meter]]s tall.<ref name="EmpRoyGuard" />
