The '''formation''' of the [[Byrnum Maw]] [[nebula]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]] occurred [[Standard century|centuries]] before [[40 BBY]]. After the event, the [[hyperspace route]]s near the nebula were disrupted and rendered unusable, isolating neighboring [[Star system|systems]], such as the [[Pijal system]], from the wider [[The galaxy|galaxy]]. In 40 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[scientist]]s charted a path through the Byrnum Maw and used two [[hyperspace anchor]]s to stabilize a central portion of the nebula allowing for the establishment of the [[hyperspace]] route known as the [[Pijal Hyperspace Corridor]].

==Prelude==
[[Millennium|Millennia]] before the [[Republic Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], [[Colonist|settlers]] began colonizing several [[star system]]s in the [[Inner Rim Territories]], including the [[Pijal system]]. Over [[time]], the [[Planet|worlds']] citizens helped establish several [[hyperspace route]]s, leading to the rise of trade and economic prosperity in that region of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="MA" />

==Nebula formation==
[[Standard century|Centuries]] before [[40 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> a dying [[Unidentified star (Byrnum Maw)|star]] exploded and formed the [[Byrnum Maw]] [[nebula]]. The [[hyperspace]] routes connecting the surrounding region of [[Realspace|space]] were disrupted by the nebula's emergence, rendering them unusable.<ref name="MA" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|&hellip;scientists have determined that two hyperspace anchors&mdash;one on the planet Pijal, and one on its moon&mdash;could together generate a field capable of stabilizing a central section of the maw. With that stabilized, several traffic pathways become possible. The worlds on those routes have been left behind, shut out of progress, for far too long. Now that can all change.|Supreme Chancellor Kirames Kaj to Qui-Gon Jinn and Obi-Wan Kenobi|<ref name="MA" />}}
[[File:Pijal-SWT.jpg|thumb|left|280px|The Byrnum Maw's formation disrupted the hyperspace traffic of Pijal and nearby worlds.]]
After the event, Republic [[scientist]]s attempted to find a stable path through the nebula to restore hyperspace traffic but were unsuccessful. Over time, the worlds relying on the disrupted routes became isolated from the wider [[galaxy]]. One of the planets near the nebula's center, [[Pijal]], had its economic progress stalled due to a lack of trading traffic. In the following centuries, Pijal remained minimally in contact with the Republic, hoping scientists would someday [[Star map|chart]] a path through the Byrnum Maw.<ref name="MA" />

In 40 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> scientists working under the Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Kirames Kaj]] discovered that placing a [[hyperspace anchor]] on both Pijal and its [[Pijal's moon|moon]] could stabilize a portion of the Byrnum Maw, which would allow the establishment of several new hyperspace routes. During his time [[Mission to Pijal (Rael Averross)|serving]] as the planet's [[lord]] [[regent]], the [[Jedi]] [[Rael Averross]], worked with [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] officials to draft the [[Governance Treaty]]. The initiative would strip the [[Pijali monarchy]] of its authority, instead granting it to a [[Democracy|democratic]] [[Assembly (Pijal)|Assembly]] that would be able to make legally binding agreements with Republic officials.<ref name="MA" />

Kaj signed off on the initiative, hopeful to reconnect systems near the Byrnum Maw with other Republic worlds. The [[Chancellor]] sent the [[Jedi Master]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], on a [[Mission to Pijal|mission]] to Pijal to ratify the Governance Treaty after its signing. However, the [[Pijali]] [[Queen]] [[Fanry]] refused to sign the initiative, for it would take away her authority and leave her world under the control of the Czerka Corporation. Eventually, her successor [[Lamia]] signed it after the [[Monarchy|queen's]] abdication, which allowed for the establishment of the hyperspace route known as the [[Pijal Hyperspace Corridor]] and the restoration of traffic to the region.<ref name="MA" />
