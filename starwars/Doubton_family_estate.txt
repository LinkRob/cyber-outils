The '''Doubton family estate''' was a family estate on the Outer Rim planet Eriadu. It was owned by Byno Doubton and after Doubton became an instructor in the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]]'s [[Sub-Adult Group]] during the early [[Imperial Era]], he eventually retired to the family estate.<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
This location was first mentioned in the reference book [[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]], which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]]<ref name="Propaganda" /> and released on [[October 25]], [[2016]].<ref name="Harper">{{Harper|url=products/star-wars-propaganda-pablo-hidalgo?variant=32210373345314|text=Star Wars Propaganda}}</ref>
