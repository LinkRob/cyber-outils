The '''Bounty Hunter Hang Out''' was a building in the city [[Mos Eisley]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] which was used by [[Krayt's Claw|Boba Fett's syndicate]].

==History==
During the [[Clone Wars]], the building was used as a base for [[Krayt's Claw|Boba Fett's syndicate]]. The [[bounty hunter]]s that frequented the building was [[C-21 Highsinger]], [[Latts Razzi]], [[Embo]], [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk|Bossk]], [[Dengar]] and [[Oked]]. Before heading to a [[Safe cargo objective|job]] on [[Quarzite]] they lost Oked in a [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina|bar]] mishap. Embo would not join their mission so Bossk and Razzi brought [[Asajj Ventress|Oked's killer]] who had agreed to fill in the position on the job. She said she had no name and was at first irritated at the young leadership of Fett but soon set out to Quarzite with them.<ref name="Bounty" />

==Behind the scenes==

The building was scheduled to appear in an unfinished four-episode arc starring [[Cad Bane]] and Boba Fett.
