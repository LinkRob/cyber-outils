The '''B22''' was a mechanically-activated [[blaster pistol]] produced by [[Merr-Sonn Munitions, Inc.]] and used during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. During the [[Siege of Inyusu Tor]]<ref name="bftc" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Captain]] [[Tabor Seitaron]] owned one of these [[pistol]]s as a sidearm and used it in an attempt to break up a fight between [[Prelate]] [[Verge]] and the Imperial-turned&ndash;[[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] asset, [[Everi Chalis]] aboard<ref name="bftc" /> the [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref> ''[[Herald]]''. Although Seitaron intended to shoot Chalis, he accidentally shot and [[Death|killed]] Verge instead.<ref name="bftc" />

==Behind the scenes==
The B22 first appeared in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="bftc" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[B22 Hold-Out Blaster]] was introduced in [[West End Games]]' [[1997]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying Game|roleplaying]] supplement ''[[Gundark's Fantastic Technology: Personal Gear]]''.<ref name="GFTPG">''[[Gundark's Fantastic Technology: Personal Gear]]''</ref>
