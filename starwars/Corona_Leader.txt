'''Corona Leader''' was the [[callsign]] of the leader of [[Corona Squadron]].

==History==
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[The Contessa]] flew as Corona Leader. After the [[Battle of Endor]], The Contessa retired from the [[fleet]] and [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]] became Corona Leader.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
