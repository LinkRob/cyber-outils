'''Corellia's nearest moon''' was the nearest [[moon]] to the<ref name="Most Wanted" /> [[Core Worlds|Core]]<ref name="Corellia" /> [[planet]] [[Corellia]]. When traveling into [[Realspace|space]] aboard a [[shuttle]], [[Han Solo|Han]] noted that he could see everything from the [[Starship|ship's]] [[hull]] to [[Qi'ra]]'s face to Corellia's nearest moon in bright relief and perfect detail.<ref name="Most Wanted" />
