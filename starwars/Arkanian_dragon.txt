'''Arkanian dragons''' were a [[species]] of [[Dragon (creature)|dragon]] capable of flight.<ref name="Thrawn" /> The creatures shared their name with the [[Arkanian]]s, the [[Homeworld|native]] species of the [[Colonies]] [[planet]] [[Arkania]].<ref name="DoH">''[[Disciples of Harmony]]''</ref> Around [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The sections of [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']] involving [[Batonn insurgency]] take place shortly before the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]'', which took place in [[2 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[Governor of the Lothal sector|governor]] of the planet [[Lothal]], [[Arihnda Pryce]], and [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[agent]] [[Gudry]] went to the planet [[Batonn]] in order to gather intelligence on the [[Batonn insurgency|insurgency]] there, as well as to extract Pryce's [[parent]]s, [[Talmoor Pryce|Talmoor]] and [[Elainye Pryce]]. While on the planet, Gudry informed the [[governor]] that he would not bring her parents with them, sarcastically adding that they could not come even if they became Arkanian dragons who could fly their group out.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Arkanian dragons were mentioned in the [[2017]] novel [[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']], authored by [[Timothy Zahn]] as the first installment of ''[[Star Wars: The Imperial Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Thrawn" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, Arkanian dragons were first mentioned in the [[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 5|fifth issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi - Dark Lords of the Sith'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic series]],<ref name="Leg">[[Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith 5|''Tales of the Jedi – Dark Lords of the Sith'' 5]]</ref> released on [[February 14]], [[1995]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54575/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_-_dark_lords_of_the_sith_1994_5|text=Star Wars: Tales Of The Jedi - Dark Lords Of The Sith (1994) #5}}</ref>
