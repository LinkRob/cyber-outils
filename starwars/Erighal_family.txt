The '''Erighal family''' was a [[Chiss family]] belonging to the [[Chiss Ascendancy]]'s [[Great Families]]. It possessed a [[fleet]] named the [[Expeditionary Force Alpha]]. The Erighal family was allied with the [[Ruling Families|Ruling]] [[Dasklo family]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

The Erighal family was one of three Great Families involved in [[Haplif]] of the [[Agbui]] and "[[Jixtus]]"'s [[Jixtus's plan|conspiracy]] to draw the Chiss Ascendancy into civil war. The other two families were the [[Pommrio family]], allied with the Ruling [[Plikh family]]; and the [[Xodlak family]], allied with the Ruling [[Irizi family]].<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Erighal family first appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second book of [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''The Ascendancy Trilogy'']] by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Greater Good" />
