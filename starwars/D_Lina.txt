'''D'Lina'''<ref name="Twit">{{Twitter|Jeremy_Barlow|status/1005881155566989312|[[Jeremy Barlow]]|quote=@LelalMekha They do! From left to right: D'Lina, Prince Baj, and Rakan. That's a "Dealer Droid", and the train is a luxury passenger vessel called the Exas Jewell.|archivefile=File:OTR_char_tweet.png}}</ref> was a [[Gender|female]] socialite [[Fosh]], a [[bird]]-like [[species]]. 

== Biography ==
D'Lina was a socialite who at some point, gave birth to the designer [[Shen Kanton]]. In [[3 BBY]], she embarked on the maiden voyage of the [[luxury train]] ''[[Exas Jewell]]'', which had been designed by her son. During the voyage, she took part in a high-stakes card game with Prince [[Baj]], [[Rakan]], and purportedly Kanton, but in reality, it was [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] impersonating him. Calrissian won the card game, but before he could depart with his winnings, the train was attacked by [[Pirate|pirates]]. D'Lina was robbed of her jewellry by [[Grillo]], who also damaged the train. After Calrissian, Rakan, Baj, and the train's engineer slowed the train down, D'Lina confronted Calrissian revealing that she was the mother of the individual Calrissian was impersonating and told him to make better choices in life.<ref name="Off the Rails" />

== Traits and personality ==
D'Lina was wealthy and dressed in a long flowing purple dress with a blue shawl. She had purple feathering, yellow eyes and an orange bill. She wore a small pair of glasses atop her beak. She was very stern.<ref name="Off the Rails" />

==Behind the scenes==
D'Lina appeared in "[[Off the Rails (comic story)|Off the Rails]]," a short comic written by [[Jeremy Barlow]] and illustrated by [[Bob Molesworth]].<ref name="Off the Rails" /> Although her name does not appear in the story proper, it is mentioned in the script.<ref name="Twit" />
