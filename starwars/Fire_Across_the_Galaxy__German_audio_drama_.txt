"'''Fire Across the Galaxy'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]]: "'''Galaxis in Flammen'''") is an episode of the [[German Rebels audio dramas]] adapting the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Fire Across the Galaxy]]". It was released on [[March 11]], [[2016]] by [[Universal Music Group]] as part of the album [[Star Wars Rebels Episode 7 - Fire Across the Galaxy / The Siege of Lothal, Part 1 & 2]]. The episode was written by [[Gabriele Bingenheimer]], directed by [[Marian Szymczyk]] and includes narration by [[Hanns Jörg Krumpholz]].<ref name="Kiddinx" />
