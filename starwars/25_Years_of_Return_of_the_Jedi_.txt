"'''25 Years of ''Return of the Jedi''!'''" is an article appearing in [[Star Wars Insider 105|''Star Wars Insider'' 105]], written by [[Daniel Wallace]]. The article offers visual commentary celebrating 25 years since the [[1983]] release of [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']].

It was later republished under the title "'''Return to Endor!'''" in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2010|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2010]].
