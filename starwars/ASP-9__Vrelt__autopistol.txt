The '''ASP-9 "Vrelt" autopistol''' was a type of [[slugthrower pistol]] manufactured by [[Talus Ironworks]], and was a common weapon used throughout the [[Corellian system]], intended for pest control in rural areas. Engineers built the ASP-9 specifically to hunt [[vrelt]]s: large, [[hair]]less [[rodent]]s that scurried and hopped around on two large hind [[Limb|legs]], and scavenged for crops and were known to even gnaw on sleeping campers. The ASP-9 fired slugs in short bursts to improve the odds of hitting the evasive creatures.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Because the ASP-9 was so readily available, was easy to conceal, and had a high rate of fire, it was a favorite of [[swoop gang]]s and street toughs across the Corellian system. It was not uncommon for [[Corellian Security Forces|CorSec]] [[inspector]]s to be called to crime scenes with slug-riddled bodies in the back alleys of [[Coronet City]]'s [[Blue Sector]] or the slums of [[Dearic]].<ref name="Gadgets" />
