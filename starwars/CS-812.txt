'''CS-812'''<ref name="The New Trooper" /> was a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] who served the [[First Order]] during the waning [[Standard month|months]] of the [[New Republic Era]]. A [[Soldier|member]] of [[CS-Squad]], CS-812 held the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[sergeant]] by the [[time]] of their [[Occupation of the Colossus|deployment]] to the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform, located on the [[planet]] [[Castilon]],<ref name="The New Trooper" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref>

Following the arrival of [[Commander]] [[Pyre]], one of CS-812's [[trooper]]s, [[CS-515]], was [[Theft of First Order data rod|impersonated]] by the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Kazuda Xiono]]. CS-812 became suspicious of Xiono due to the latter's behavior, which was considered unorthodox for a stormtrooper, although they did not suspect that "CS-515" was, in fact, an impostor. The [[squad]] attempted to apprehend Xiono, but the spy succeeded in switching places with the real CS-515, who was taken into [[First Order military|military]] [[Prisoner|custody]] to undergo [[Brain|mental]] [[Memory wipe|reconditioning]].<ref name="The New Trooper" />
