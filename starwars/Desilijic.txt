'''Desilijic''',<ref name="absolutely">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> also known as the '''Desilijic Tiure clan''',<ref name="BMF 94">{{BuildFalconCite|94|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> or the '''Tiure family''',<ref name="Super Graphic">''[[Star Wars Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away]]''</ref> was an influential<ref name="BMF 94" /> [[Hutt]] [[kajidic]]. [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]], head of his crime family, had the Desilijic's [[tattoo]] carved on his [[Limb|right arm]].<ref name="absolutely" />

The matriarch and patriarch of the Tiure family were [[Mama]]<ref name="Super Graphic" /> the Hutt<ref name="HFZ">{{TCW|Hunt for Ziro}}</ref> and [[Papa]]<ref name="Super Graphic" /> the Hutt,<ref name="HFZ" /> respectively. They had three [[Reproduction|children]], [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure]], [[Ebor]], and [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure's father|another son]], who went on to be the father of Jabba.<ref name="Super Graphic" /> Ziro had a son named [[Hiro]],<ref name="GEStaff" /> and was great-uncle<ref name="RottaDB">{{Databank|rotta-the-huttlet|Rotta the Huttlet}}</ref> to Jabba's own son, [[Rotta]].<ref name="TCW film" /> Jabba was uncle to [[Gorga Desilijic Aarrpo]]<ref name="Super Graphic" /> and to [[Graballa]],<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|209|Crime and Punishment}}</ref> and was cousins with [[Crakka]]<ref name="RTVC 4">[[Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle 4|''Star Wars Adventures: Return to Vader's Castle'' 4]]</ref> as well as a [[Unidentified Hutt brother|brother]] and [[Unidentified Hutt sister|sister]] duo known collectively as [[The Twins|the Twins]].<ref name="Chapter 2" />
