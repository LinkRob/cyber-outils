[[Corporal]] '''Beezer'''<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="SWE" /> who served as a [[Communications officer|communications operative]] in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] against the [[Galactic Empire]]. She was trained by veteran [[Special Forces Trackers Unit|Pathfinder]] [[sergeant]] [[Brooks Carlson]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the [[Battle of Endor]] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> both Beezer and Carlson were selected as members of a [[commando]] [[Endor strike team|strike team]] that was [[Battle of Endor|sent]] to the [[moon]] of [[Endor]] by the Alliance with the objective of disabling an Imperial [[Death Star's shield generator|shield generator]] built there to protect the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|Death Star II]] [[superweapon]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

Using the stolen Imperial [[shuttle]] ''[[Tydirium]]'', the team landed on the moon and proceeded to the rear of the generator, where a security bunker acted as a backdoor. Expecting the Alliance attack, the Imperials ambushed the rebels at the bunker, but they were defeated when the local [[Ewok]] population came to the aid of the strike team. The rebels then destroyed the [[Deflector shield generator|generator]], allowing the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] to destroy the [[Death Star]]. Beezer had light skin, [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, and brown [[hair]]. She wore a [[Color|green]] [[helmet]], and a brown [[jerkin]] with a white [[Clothing|shirt]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Beezer was pictured in an entry for [[Rebel trooper]]s on the [[StarWars.com]] [[Encyclopedia (StarWars.com)|Encyclopedia]].<ref name="SWE" /> The character was then identified in the English release of "[[The Battle of Endor and the Fall of the Empire]]," an entry in [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] series<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> that was originally released in Spanish on [[June 29]], [[2021]].<ref name="De Agostini">{{DeAgostini|url=libros-comics/enciclopedia-star-wars|text=Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>

The image used in the Encyclopedia entry was a promotional photo taken for the [[1983]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="BtM">''[[Star Wars: Behind the Magic]]''</ref> although Beezer does not appear in the film itself.<ref name="ROTJ">{{Film|VI}}</ref> The photo depicts Beezer,<ref name="BtM" /> portrayed by stuntwoman [[Tracey Eddon]],<ref name="Aficionado">{{Blogspot|subdomain=starwarsaficionado|url=2010/09/classic-image-rebel-assembly.html|name=Star Wars Aficionado|text=CLASSIC IMAGE: REBEL ASSEMBLY!}}</ref> and other commandos stood with protagonist [[Luke Skywalker]] as their prisoner in one of the Death Star II's corridors.<ref name="BtM" /> The name Beezer originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first used in the short story ''[[Night Attack]]'', published in ''[[Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="Night Attack 1st edition">{{WEGCite|story=Night Attack|book=Galaxy Guide 5: Return of the Jedi}}</ref> A card in the [[1999]] ''[[Endor Limited]]'' expansion set of the [[Decipher]] [[Star Wars Customizable Card Game|''Star Wars'' Customizable Card Game]] then linked the name to Eddon's character.<ref name="CCG">{{CCG|set=Endor Limited|cardname=Corporal Beezer}}</ref> The [[2008]] [[StarWars.com]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive short story ''[[Fists of Ion]]'' provided the character's first name as Dansra. The story's author [[Edward M. Erdelac]],<ref name="Fists of Ion">{{Hyperspace|url=hyperspace/fiction/feature20081031.html|text="Fists of Ion"|int=Fists of Ion|newhost=Suvudu}}</ref> stated that the name was a [[tuckerization]] of his wife's first name.<ref name="Endnotes">{{Blog|caledre25|23|Endnotes For Fists Of Ion: Memoirs Of A Champion Shockboxer by Lobar Aybock, Chapter VII: Hard Raine}}</ref>
