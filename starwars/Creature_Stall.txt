'''Creature Stall''' was a [[Merchant|shop]] in the [[Black Spire Outpost Market]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]].  In [[34 ABY]]<ref name="GE Date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>, the store was operated by [[Bina]], an [[Amani]] from [[Utapau]] and featured a number of caged animals for purchase. She adorned the store with Amani staves, blankets, and other decorations, so not only did it collect her exotic finds, it also was a home base.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==History==
In [[232 BBY]], [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi Initiates]] [[Lys Solay]], [[Kai Brightstar]], and [[Nubs]] visited the shop with [[Padawan]] [[Celesta Kami]] and encountered a therri among other creatures in various cages.<ref name="Lys" /> 
[[File:Creature Stall Entrance 232BBY.png|thumb|Creature Stall in  232 BBY|left|264x264px]]
Centuries later around 34 ABY, Bina ran the store in the Black Spire Outpost Market. She used it as storage for her personal favorite creatures from offworld, a shop to sell those she could part with, and also as an opportunity to educate the children that stopped by her store.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> Bina wrote a booklet for [[Youngling|younglings]] called ''[[Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu]]'' which catalogued some of the creatures that lived in Black Spire from the [[Bantha]] tracks to the baby [[Sarlacc|sarlaac]] in [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]].<ref name="Creature Stall">{{GEAttr|Creature Stall}}</ref>

In the same time, a human [[Volt Vescuso]] worked at the stall who often met [[Julen Rakab]] at the shop. One day, Jules brought [[Izal Garsea|Izzy]] into the stall, but through a series of mishaps, freed a cage of [[Loralora bird|loralora birds]]. Another disaster struck the stall when the [[Wayuning]] [[Topi Topi]] impersonated a caretaker and stole some number of [[Kowakian monkey-lizard|Kowakian monkey-lizards]]; a bounty was issued for their arrest.<ref name="Bounty Hunter">''[[Star Wars: Batuu Bounty Hunters]]''</ref> When [[Vi Moradi]] first explored the Market, she also traveled to the stall, but determined that its owner, Bina, would not support the [[Resistance]] efforts due to her business.<ref name="Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]</ref> She often left the shop in the care of her assistants while she journeyed off-world, such as when [[Eloc Throno]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> or when [[Unidentified boy (Black Spire)|a young boy]] and his [[Unidentified boy (Black Spire)|mother]] and [[Unidentified boy (Black Spire)|father]] visited the Outpost and bought a blue tooka.<ref name="Batuu Adventure">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: A Batuu Adventure!]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Creature_Stall_art_2_SWGE.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Concept art of the shop]]
Bina's Creature Stall is one of the gift shops at [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]], selling interactive plushies and toys of various ''Star Wars'' animals in the guise of being a pet shop in the context of the land's story. It opened on [[May 31]], [[2019]] in [[Disneyland]] Park and on [[August 29]] at [[Disney's Hollywood Studios]].<ref name="A to Z">{{D23|url=a-to-z/creature-stall|text=Disney A to Z: Creature Stall}}</ref> ''Bina's Guide to the Creatures of Batuu'' is free to children under 10 years old, and contains in-universe information and scavenger hunt activities within the theme park.<ref name="Creature Stall" /> The merchandise sold at the store is branded as the [[Creatures (toy line)|''Creatures'' toy line]].
