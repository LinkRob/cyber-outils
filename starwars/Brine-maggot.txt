'''Brine-maggots''' were a type of [[maggot]] that lived in groups known as schools.<ref name="Aftermath" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> worry gnawed at the [[New Republic]] [[Mon Calamari]] [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]] like a school of brine-maggots after he learned that the [[Galactic Empire]] was [[Blockade|blockading]] the [[planet]] [[Akiva]] and enacting a communications blackout there for [[Akiva Summit|reasons]] he did not know.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Brine-maggots were mentioned in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
