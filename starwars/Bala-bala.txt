'''Bala-balas''' were small [[speeder]]s used on [[Akiva]], a [[planet]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. They were used by both citizens of the world and the [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]] stationed. They helped navigate the narrow streets and channels of the [[Myrra]], the [[capital]] [[city]] of Akiva.<ref name="Aftermath" />

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bala-bala speeders appeared in [[2015]] novel ''[[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']] '', written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" /> Bala-balas were pictured in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The speeder is not ride-able in the game but is frequently driven by non-playable characters.<ref name="Outlaw">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> The game's Art Director, [[Marthe Jonkers]], created [[Wikipedia:Concept Art|concept art]] for the bala-bala speeders.<ref name="Jonkers ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=martheart|url=projects/5WvEKJ|name=[[Marthe Jonkers]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws // Akiva Explorations|quote=Here are some early explorations of Akiva! We had some ideas of what you could find in the jungle, ranging from strange otherworldly plants to ancient orbital ruins. I also worked on some sketches for the bala bala speeder, the way to get around in the streets of Myrra, Akiva's main city! |date=2024-09-30}}</ref>
