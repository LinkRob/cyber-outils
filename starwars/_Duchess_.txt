"'''Duchess'''" was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter]] [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|pilot]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. She had light skin and shoulder-length brown hair.<ref name="Starfighter Missions" /> Duchess had been stationed on [[Scarif]], and during the [[Battle of Scarif|resulting battle]] between Imperial and Rebel forces, she provided aerial support, flying over an [[All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport]].<ref name="X-Wing" />
