The '''Beacon network''' was a planned [[The galaxy|galaxy-wide]] network of [[space station]]s, one of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s [[Great Works]]. The stations were intended to improve galaxy-wide communications and increase the [[Galactic Republic]]'s presence in far-flung regions. The [[Starlight Beacon]],<ref name="LotJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref> launched in [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> was intended as the first station in the network.<ref name="LotJ" />
