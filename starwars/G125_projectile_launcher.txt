The '''G125 projectile launcher''' was a weapon used by [[First Order jet trooper]]s in combat during the [[First Order-Resistance War]].

==Description==
[[File:G125ProjectileLauncher-CGSWG.png|thumb|250px|left|Profile view of a G125 projectile launcher]]
Designed by the [[Sonn-Blas Corporation]], the G125 was capable of firing a standard [[laser]] bolt from its central barrel or more powerful explosive rounds from the three outside barrels. Although it was a small weapon, it had significant stopping power and was effective against [[Resistance]] forces on [[Pasaana]].<ref name="TROS">{{Film|IX}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:FirstOrder-JetTrooper.png|thumb|300px|right|The G125 projectile launcher sported three barrels.]]
The G125 projectile launcher was designed by the Sonn-Blas Corporation and issued to [[First Order jet trooper]]s<ref name="TROS VD" /> in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of {{Film|IX}} to [[35 ABY]].</ref> Launchers were deployed by two [[First Order]] jet troopers on Pasaana when Resistance operative [[Poe Dameron]] hotwired two local [[skimmer]]s to escape [[First Order Army|First Order]] patrols on the planet. The G125's explosive rounds proved powerful enough to destroy the skimmers, although the two jet troopers were eventually shot down and killed, allowing Dameron and the rest of the [[Resistance Army|Resistance]] forces to escape.<ref name="TROS VD" />

==Behind the scenes==
The G125 projectile launcher first appeared in the Rise of Skywalker DLC on the game ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]'' as a playable weapon, shortly before the film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars: ''Episode IX'' The Rise of Skywalker's'']] release.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
