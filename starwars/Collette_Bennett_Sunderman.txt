'''Collette Bennett Sunderman''' (born [[October 25]], [[1950s#1959|1959]]) was a casting director for the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars]]'' television series.<ref name="CW1">[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Volume One|''Star Wars: Clone Wars'' Volume One]]</ref> She is the older sister of voice actor [[Jeff Bennett]].
