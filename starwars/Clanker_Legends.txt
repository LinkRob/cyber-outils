[[File:Fandex-B1BattleDroid.png|thumb|right|250px|The B1 battle droid, a unit often called a "clanker"]]
'''Clanker''' was a slang term used by [[Clone trooper/Legends|clone troopers]] to describe [[B1-series battle droid/Legends|B1-series battle droids]]<ref name="SWRC">''[[Star Wars: Republic Commando]]''</ref> and other [[Battle droid/Legends|battle droids]]<ref name="Warfare" /> characterized by a distinctive "clanking" sound produced by their joints and metallic frames during movement.<ref name="Voyage">{{TCW|Voyage of Temptation}}</ref> The term was occasionally used to denote to other [[droid]]s as well; for example, [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex/Legends|Rex]] referred to [[4A-7/Legends|4A-7]] as a "clanker."<ref name="TCW novel">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (novelization)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' novelization]]</ref> The term "clanker" was used to describe all types of droids within the [[Separatist Droid Army]] in ''[[Geonosian Debriefings, Vol. III]]''.<ref name="Warfare" />
