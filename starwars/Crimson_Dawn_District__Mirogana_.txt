The '''Crimson Dawn District''' was a small area of the [[capital]] [[city]] [[Mirogana]] on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] that was under the control of the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate<ref name="Outlaws" /> during [[3 ABY]].<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> The district was located next to the [[Market District (Mirogana)|Market District]] and could be accessed through the kitchen of the [[Hot Pot]] [[Restaurant|eatery]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

{{Loc-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The Crimson Dawn district first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The district was one of several areas in the game that was more accessible depending on the players reputation with the owning faction, in this case with Crimson Dawn<ref name="Outlaws" />
