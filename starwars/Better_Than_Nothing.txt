"'''Better Than Nothing'''" is a short story appearing in the [[June]] [[1996]] ''[[Shadows of the Empire Sourcebook]]'', written by [[Peter M. Schweighofer]] for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The story centers around [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]] [[Platt Okeefe/Legends|Platt Okeefe]] and [[Gender/Legends|her]] unsophisticated [[ASP-7 labor droid/Legends|ASP-7 labor droid]], [[SeeVee]].

{{Shortstory-stub}}

==Plot summary==
Platt Okeefe, having previously being forced by an Imperial picket to ditch her ship in the [[Dorajan]] [[Jungle/Legends|jungle]], attempts to enlist the assistance of SeeVee. The droid is unhelpful in delivering a [[Hydrospanner/Legends|hydrospanner]], much to Okeefe's annoyance.
