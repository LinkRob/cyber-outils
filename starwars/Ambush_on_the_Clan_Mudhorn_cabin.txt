The '''ambush on the Clan Mudhorn cabin''' was an attack on the [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]]'s [[Clan Mudhorn cabin|cabin]] by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]]. Djarin had broken a deal he made with [[The Twins]] regarding the rescue of [[Rotta|their nephew]], and in response they sent Embo to capture Djarin and bring him to them on [[Nal Hutta]].<ref name="TMAG" />

The Mandalorian's [[Mandalorian apprentice|apprentice]] and adopted son, [[Din Grogu]], was present at the cabin, as was a group of [[Anzellan]]s. Embo successfully apprehended Djarin, while his pet [[anooba]] [[Keibu]] grabbed the Anzellan [[Minch]]. Grogu and the remaining Anzellans managed to escape.<ref name="TMAG" />
