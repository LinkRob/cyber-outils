'''Crystal City''' was the name of a [[city]] on a [[Unidentified planet (The Pit)|planet]] built and occupied by the [[Galactic Empire]]. A [[The Pit (kyber crystals)|large pit]] where [[Slavery|enslaved]] individuals were forced by the Empire to dig for [[kyber crystal]]s was located near the city.<ref name="Pit" />

{{City-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The name of the city is identified via the plaque at the city square, which reads "CRYSTAL CITY" in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="Pit" />
