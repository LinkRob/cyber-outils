The '''''Errant Venture''''' was a [[starship]] with a dedicated [[casino]].<ref name="Shadow" /> By [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> the [[gambler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] had made one of his best hauls playing in the casino, which he considered to have a good gambling hall.<ref name="Shadow" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the ''Errant Venture'' was mentioned in the [[2022]] novel ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'', which was written by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="Shadow" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the ''[[Errant Venture/Legends|Errant Venture]]'' was a [[Star Destroyer/Legends|Star Destroyer]] originally named the ''Virulence'' before its capture by the [[Smuggler/Legends|smuggler]] [[Booster Terrik]], first appearing in the [[1997]] novel ''[[X-Wing: The Bacta War]]'' by [[Michael A. Stackpole]], the fourth book in the [[Star Wars: X-Wing (novel series)|''Star Wars: X-Wing'']] series.<ref name="X-Wing">''[[X-Wing: The Bacta War]]''</ref>
