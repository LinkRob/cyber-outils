'''Bacta Slush''' was an [[Water|icy]] and [[cream]]y beverage that consisted of a [[fruit]]-filled slush. Its ingredients also included a scoop of [[Fondor Ice]] [[ice cream]] and the [[Ithor Float]] [[Foodstuff|dessert]]. It was made on the [[Inner Rim Territories|Inner Rim]] [[planet]] [[Thyferra]], the origin point of the medicinal substance [[bacta]], by its natives. While not medicinal, Bacta Slush instead improved the drinker's state of mind and helped them to feel better.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for Bacta Slush in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', following a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bacta Slush was created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the beverage makes use of [[Wikipedia:Shaved ice|shaved ice]], [[Wikipedia:Evaporated milk|evaporated milk]], diced [[Wikipedia:Mango|mango]], sweetened [[Wikipedia:Adzuki bean|red beans]], [[Wikipedia:Corn flakes|corn flakes]], and shredded [[Wikipedia:Coconut|coconut]]. It also uses Fondor Ice and Ithor Float, two other recipes from the book, as ingredients.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
