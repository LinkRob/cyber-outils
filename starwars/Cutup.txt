'''Cutup''', designated '''CT-4040''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. A member of [[Domino Squad]], Cutup was trained on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] before being stationed on a [[Rishi Station|listening post]] on the [[Rishi Moon]]. In [[22 BBY]], [[Rishi Station]] was [[Defense of Rishi Station|attacked]] by [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]] and the [[trooper]]s were forced to evacuate. During the escape, Cutup was attacked and [[Death|eaten]] by a giant [[Rishi eel]].

==Biography==
===Training on Kamino===
{{Quote|You're a real cutup, aren't you?!|Bric unintentionally giving CT-4040 his name|<ref name="Clone Cadets" />}}
[[File:CutupFivesBunks-CC.png|thumb|left|220px|Cutup and Fives in the [[barracks]] on Kamino.]]
A [[Cloning|clone]] of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]],<ref name="Clone Troopers DB" /> CT-4040<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]].<ref name="Clone Troopers DB" />

A member of [[Domino Squad]], CT-4040 and his fellow [[clone trooper]]s, [[Droidbait|CT-00-2010]], [[Fives|CT-5555]], [[Hevy|CT-782]], and [[Echo|CT-1409]], were on the verge of being assigned sanitation duty as a result of poor performance in training. Although each member of the squad had their shortcomings, one of their drill instructors, the [[Siniteen]] [[Bric]], blamed CT-4040 for the squad's failure. One evening, before their final attempt at the [[Citadel Challenge]] training course, CT-4040 met with Bric in the [[hangar]], where Bric berated him, saying that everything was a joke to him and that he could not even take his own training seriously. However, CT-4040 only laughed and decided to embrace the nickname "Cutup" after Bric used it to derogatorily refer to him. Despite seemingly not being affected by the conversation, Cutup and his brothers, who each had their own perspectives refreshed in various ways, banded together and were determined to complete the challenge. They progressed very well in the early stages, but eventually, Cutup and his squad learned that they had been sabotaged and did not have their [[ascension cable]]s, which they needed to scale to the top of the citadel and complete the assessment. Although Bric had been the one to sabotage them, the squad used their ingenuity and teamwork to find a way to complete the challenge nonetheless. Cutup and his squad were ultimately commissioned to fight in the ongoing [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

===Rishi Station===
{{Quote|You wanted excitement, Hevy.''"<br />"''Right. Oo-ooh, meteor shower.|Cutup and Hevy, with Hevy being sarcastic to Cutup's remark|audio=YouWantedExcitement-Rookies.flac|<ref name="Rookies" />}}
The escalating war demanded more [[troop]]s and many inexperienced clones&mdash;including Cutup&mdash;were pushed into service before their intensive training could be completed.<ref name="Rookies" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> Cutup and his Domino Squad mates were stationed at [[Rishi Station]], a [[listening post]] located on the [[Rishi Moon]]. As part of their plan to attack the clone troopers' [[homeworld]] Kamino, the Confederacy of Independent Systems launched a [[Defense of Rishi Station|surprise assault]] on Rishi Station. As [[BX-series droid commando]]s stormed the listening post, Cutup and CT-5555, nicknamed "Fives," CT-1409, known as "Echo," and CT-782 "Hevy" were forced to flee through a ventilation shaft. After they escaped the outpost, they emerged onto the surface underneath the station. However, a [[Rishi eel]] appeared and attacked Cutup, killing him. The remaining clone troopers eventually managed to alert the [[Galactic Republic]] about the Separatist attack, thwarting a possible invasion of Kamino.<ref name="Rookies" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:Cutup-TCW.png|thumbnail|180px|Cutup was a clone trooper during the Clone Wars.]]
As a clone cadet, Cutup showed qualities of being the clown of Domino Squad, telling jokes, teasing his fellow cadets, making light-hearted comments about other cadet squads, and mocking his trainer, Bric. Nevertheless, Cutup cared greatly for his fellow cadets, stopping to check on a wounded Droidbait when the clone was shot from behind.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

After being shipped out to the Rishi Moon, Cutup, like most of the other clones there, was an inexperienced rookie trooper and he was bored with working at the station. He challenged Hevy a lot, but they got along well, participating in idle entertainment like arm wrestling. He would also join Hevy in teasing Echo, because the trooper was focused on protocols and manuals. Cutup sported a goatee while he served on the Rishi Moon, perhaps embracing his own individuality like many of the other clones during the Clone Wars.<ref name="Rookies" />

A [[human]] clone of Jango Fett, Cutup was 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> and weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" /> He had [[Color|brown]] [[eye]]s, tan skin, and black [[hair]].<ref name="Rookies" />

==Skills and abilities==
As a clone trooper, CT-4040 was trained in military tactics, as well as both armed and [[unarmed combat]].<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Cutup was equipped with unmarked white [[Phase I clone trooper armor]]<ref name="Rookies" /> and was armed with a [[DC-15A blaster carbine]].<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> Like other clone troopers, Cutup had an [[behavioral modification biochip|inhibitor chip]] implanted in his [[brain]] by [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s, ensuring his obedience.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
"Cutup" first appeared in "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]," the fifth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars|The Clone Wars TV series]], aired on [[October 24]], [[2008]].<ref name="Old EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode005.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rookies}}</ref> He also appeared in the first episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]], titled "[[Clone Cadets]],"<ref name="Clone Cadets" /> which chronologically took place before "Rookies."<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of "[[Clone Cadets]]" to [[22 BBY]], and the events of "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]" to later that same [[Standard year|year]].</ref> In the episodes, he was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]], who voices all clone troopers through the series.<ref name="Clone Cadets" />

In the young reader book ''[[Star Wars: I Am a Clone Trooper]]'', Cutup is seen sharing a meal with [[Dogma]], who is donning the gear of the [[501st Legion]].<ref name="I am a Clone Trooper">''[[Star Wars: I Am a Clone Trooper]]''</ref> However, Dogma did not join the frontline of the Clone Wars as part of the 501st until later in the war,<ref name="Dogma">{{Databank|dogma|Clone Trooper Dogma}}</ref> by which point Cutup had already been killed.<ref name="Rookies" /> As such, the image cannot be correct.<ref name="Date information">{{Databank|rishi-station|Rishi Station}} establishes that the events of "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]," which includes the [[death]] of [[Cutup/Legends|Cutup]], to early in the [[Clone Wars]]. ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' also dates that episode's events to [[22 BBY]] and places the [[Battle of Umbara]] in [[20 BBY]].</ref>
