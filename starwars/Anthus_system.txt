The '''Anthus system''' was a [[star system]] situated in grid square M-17 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], in the [[Brema sector]], which was located in the regions known as [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Western Reaches]].<ref name="Western Reaches" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Anthus system was mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via the appendix [[Star Systems of the Galaxy]], published on the [[StarWars.com]] website<ref name="Appendix" /> as part of [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Galaxy">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Anthus system/Legends|Anthus system]] originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was mentioned in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion|Online Companion]] to the [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="EAOC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-essential-atlas-online-companion|text=''Star Wars: The Essential Atlas'' Online Companion|int=Star Wars: The Essential Atlas Online Companion}}</ref>
