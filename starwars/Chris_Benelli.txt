'''Chris Benelli''' is an [[Wikipedia:Singer|singer]] and [[Wikipedia:Audio Engineer|audio engineer]] who has worked on the post-production for hundreds of audiobooks for [[Random House]]. Benelli's first ''[[Star Wars]]'' work was the Post-production editing for the [[Victory's Price (audiobook)|''Victory's Price'' audiobook]].<ref name="Victory's Price">[[Victory's Price (audiobook)|''Victory's Price'' audiobook]]</ref>
