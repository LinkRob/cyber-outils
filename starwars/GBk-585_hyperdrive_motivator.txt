The '''GBk-585 hyperdrive motivator''' was a [[hyperdrive motivator]] used on the [[UT-60D U-wing starfighter/support craft]]<ref name="ROUVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> and the [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]].<ref name="IncrediBuilds X-wing" /> One was mounted in each of the four [[4J.7 fusial thrust engine]]s of the UT-60D.<ref name="ROUVG" />
