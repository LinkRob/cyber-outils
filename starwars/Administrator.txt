'''Administrator''' was a title used in business and [[politics]] to denote an individual with authority over or within an organization. [[Finis Valorum]] served as an administrator to [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Skor Kalpana]] before being elected Chancellor himself,<ref name="Tarkin" /> while [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] was the [[Baron Administrator]] of [[Cloud City]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Databank Lando">{{SWE|characters|landocalrissian|Lando Calrissian}}</ref> The [[Symeong]] [[Dathan (Symeong)|Dathan]] held the position of administrator during his servitude to the [[Sith Eternal]] [[Sith cult|cult]] on the planet [[Exegol]] and therefore enjoyed a level of autonomy, although he remained as much a [[Slavery|slave]] as the other captives.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
