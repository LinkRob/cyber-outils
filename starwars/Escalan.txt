'''Escalan''' was a primitive [[planet]]<ref name="Han Chewie 7" /> located in the [[Escalan system]] in the [[Cademimu sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that was home to a [[Unidentified Escalan species|species]].<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> Between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars: Han Solo & Chewbacca]]'' to between around [[8 BBY]] and [[2 BBY]].</ref> the [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]] was dumped onto Escalan by the [[Khel Tanna's crew|crew]] of [[Khel Tanna]] after he was shot by the [[Rodian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Greedo]].<ref name="Han Chewie 7" /> Solo was taken to a [[Aber|local]] [[doctor]] by two members of the [[species]], and awoke one [[Standard week|week]] later.<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> During his [[time]] on Escalan, Solo went for a drink, but was unable to communicate with the natives as they did not speak [[Galactic Basic Standard|basic]]. As he made his way out of the building, he saw an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] news report that mentioned upcoming [[Death penalty|executions]] to be carried out by the [[Benelex Marshal Service]], including his friend, the [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]]. Solo then went to a acquire a [[Starship|ship]] to get off-world and rescue his friend, and came across a [[Unidentified merchant (Han Solo)|merchant]] who had a ship. The pair then left Escalan.<ref name="Han Chewie 7" />
