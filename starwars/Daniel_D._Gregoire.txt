'''Daniel D. Gregoire''' is a pre-visualization supervisor who worked on dozens of films, television series, and video games. The founder of pre-visualization and animatics studio Halon Entertainment, he had collaborated with [[George Lucas]] at [[Skywalker Ranch]] as part of [[Lucasfilm|JAK Films]]<ref name="Halon">{{WebCite|url=https://www.halon.com/team/daniel-gregoire|text=Daniel Gregoire - Halon Entertainment|work=[https://www.halon.com HALON]|archivedate=20211114122335}}</ref> and served as the supervisor of the pre-visualization/effects department for the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="ROTS doc">[[Star Wars home video releases#Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'' DVD (''Within a Minute'')]]</ref> Gregoire also worked on [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Halon" />
