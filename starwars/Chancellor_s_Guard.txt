The '''Chancellor's guard''' was a position created for the [[Supreme Chancellor/Legends|Supreme Chancellor's]] protection. They were to guard the politician and preserve their life. [[Johun Othone]] once served at this position in [[1000 BBY/Legends|1000 BBY]], protecting [[Tarsus Valorum/Legends|Tarsus Valorum]], the Supreme Chancellor at the time.
