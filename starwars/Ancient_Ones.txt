The '''Ancient Ones''' were an organization of [[human]]s from a [[Vagadarr Prime|planet]] in the [[Vagadarr system]] who were the ancestors of the [[Old Ones (Vagadarr system)|Old Ones]]. The Old Ones were the [[parent]]s of the two tribes known as the [[Mud dwellers]] and [[Rockhawkers]].
