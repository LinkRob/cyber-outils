'''Bhatiqu''' was the lone [[city]] on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] [[Thelj]], situated in a temperate zone created by the planet's original settlers. It was covered by a dome. Thelj had been settled by the [[Galactic Republic]] as a potential colony<ref name="POD" /> after approximately [[682 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> but froze over shortly after upon the sudden collapse of its [[Thelj's sun|sun]]. Nearly everyone unable to escape was [[Death|killed]]. Bhatiqu and other settlements were made habitable after [[scientist]]s discovered a method of using Thelj's planetary core to create vents of heated air through the use of [[atmospheric generator]]s to create the temperate zones. The [[Graf family compound]] was located near Bhatiqu<ref name="POD" /> around [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Date" />
