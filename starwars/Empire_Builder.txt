"'''Empire Builder'''" is an interview with [[Irvin Kershner]], the director of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. It was conducted by [[Calum Waddell]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 119|''Star Wars Insider'' 119]]. This article was later reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2012|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2012]].
