The '''Concord Dawn system''' was a [[star system]] within the [[Mandalore sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> under the control of a [[Mandalorian]] squad known as the [[Journeyman Protector|Mandalorian Protectors]]. It was home to the [[planet]] [[Concord Dawn]], which had at least three [[moon]]s, the [[Third moon of Concord Dawn|third moon]] being the location of the [[Protectors Camp]].<ref name="Camp" /> In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="ProtectorDate">{{RebelsDate|213}}</ref> the rebellion against the [[Galactic Empire]] sought safe passage through the system, and were granted such by [[Fenn Rau]], leader of the Protectors.<ref name="Protector" />
