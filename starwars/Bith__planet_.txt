'''Bith''',<ref name="Bith" /> also known as '''Clak'dor VII'''<ref name="FO" /> and simply '''Clak'dor''',<ref name="StarshipsAndSpeeders">''[[Starships and Speeders]]''</ref> was a [[planet]]<ref name="FO" /> located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] and situated along the [[Rimma Trade Route]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Bith]] [[species]].<ref name="Bith" />

Clak'dor existed in ecological collapse as chemical, radioactive, and biological effects from the [[Nozho–Weogar War|ancient civil war]] continually ravaged the environment. The poisoned [[jungle]]s and [[swamp]]s were filled with rapidly mutating flora and fauna.<ref name="FO" />

Bith lived in domed [[City|cities]], the majority of which lay nestled in the crags of Clak'dor's [[mountain]] ranges. They relied on subterranean engineering and urban architectural advances to burrow deeper and build higher to alleviate the cramped conditions. Many Bith, including offworld Bith, found Clak'dor harsh and depressing.<ref name="FO" />

Clak'dor was listed on a map in the ''[[Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]'', written by [[Eloc Throno]].<ref name="TGtB">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref>
