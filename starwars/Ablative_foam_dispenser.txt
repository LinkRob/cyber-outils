An '''ablative foam dispenser''' was a device that could be installed on a [[starship]] to protect its cargo by deploying [[ablative foam]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> [[Kay Vess]] paid a mechanic a thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and provided them with ten [[cargo signature scrambler]]s to install ablative foam dispensers in the hold of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Ablative foam dispenses first appeared in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> They were mentioned in the reactive plating ship upgrade that players could purchase for their ship that would reduce the damage taken by fragile cargo during [[smuggler|smuggling]] contracts.<ref name="Outlaws" />
