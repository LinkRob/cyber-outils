'''Cicadas''' were a type of [[insect]] found in [[the galaxy]]. Various kinds of the bug could be found on different planets, such as the giant [[hundred-year cicada]]s of [[Thies IV]] and at least two kinds on the [[planet]] [[Siline]]. The reproductive cycles of the creature were variable depending on the variety of cicada. Those of Thies IV, who reproduced every [[standard century|century]], were contingent upon geothermal cycling. At least two varieties of Silinese cicadas based their mating cycles around large [[water|rainfall]]. Cicadas were notable for their loud mating calls, which, when in chorus among the population, could make it hard for [[sentience|sentient beings]] to hear their surroundings.<ref name="TCC" /> 

During the Siline [[Crowning Convocation]] in [[134 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref>, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]] thought that the local apex predator, [[ericadi]], reminded her of the hundred-year cicadas of Thies IV. Later, during the [[survival level]] of the [[Crowning Convocation (Jecki and Yord)|Convocation]], Jecki had trouble assessing her surroundings due to the noise created by cicadas.<ref name="TCC" />
