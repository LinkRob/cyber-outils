The '''Droid Gotra''' was a [[droid]] group that supported [[droid rights]]. It was originally formed by a band of lethal repurposed [[battle droid]]s with grievances against the [[Galactic Empire]] for having been abandoned after their service during the [[Clone Wars]]. At one [[time]], the group was confined to an [[Droid Gotra industrial complex|industrial complex]] in the [[Coruscant Underworld|bowels]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], but it later managed to expand. They were known to support [[droid]] emancipation and served as muscle for the [[Crymorah Syndicate]] and the [[Hutt Clan]]. The group continued to operate into the [[New Republic Era]].

==History==
===Under the Empire===
{{Quote|You must be a powerful group to bid a billion credits for something.''"<br />"''That bid stretched our resources. But it would have been worth it. We started as a loosely connected group of battle droids, beings who were abandoned after the Clone Wars or repurposed against their will for other functions. But now our organization is large, consisting of all manner of droids, even a few organic sympathizers. We have been working to establish a cell here on Corellia.|[[Qi'ra]] and TD-H4|<ref name="Most Wanted" />}}
[[File:Battle droids-TSW.png|left|thumb|300px|The Droid Gotra was formed from battle droids leftover from the Clone Wars.]]
The Droid Gotra was formed during the early [[Imperial Era]] by a band of repurposed [[battle droid]]s<ref name="Tarkin" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[19 BBY]].<ref name="GotraDate" /> These combat units had what some considered legitimate grievances against the [[Galactic Empire]] for having been abandoned after their service during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The Gotra recruited [[Q2-B3KO]], an [[articulated holographic color separator]] who produced propaganda supporting the [[Separatist Droid Army]] during the Clone Wars, to disseminate messages of mechanical emancipation.<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> 

[[File:StarWars55.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Due to Vader, the Droid Gotra moved their operations to the Outer Rim.]]
Originally confined to an [[Droid Gotra industrial complex|industrial complex]] located in the [[Coruscant Underworld|bowels]] of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]], they served as muscle for the [[Crymorah Syndicate]] in robberies, kidnapping, illegal salvage, and extortion.<ref name="Tarkin" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the Empire's [[Sentinel Base|base]] on the [[Sentinel moon]] came under [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attack]] by [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|insurgents]] using Separatist equipment and droids. The installation's commander, [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]], initially suspected that the Gotra was responsible, but found it unlikely that they would make use of obsolete weapons to send a message to the Empire. [[Phoca Soot]], the [[Coruscant Security Force]] [[Prefect (Coruscant police)|prefect]] of [[level 1331]], hired [[Gender|himself]] out to the Droid Gotra instead of controlling crime within his sector. However, his duplicity did not go unnoticed and he was [[Death|executed]] by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] as an example to Coruscant's criminal elements, advising them to relocate their operations to [[sector]]s in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Outer Rim Territories]], lest their activities infringe upon [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]'s designs.<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:Vanguard axis.png|thumb|left|270px|The Vanguard Axis was an offshoot of the Droid Gotra.]]
Between 19 BBY and [[18 BBY]],<ref name="DateTBB">{{TBBDate|206}}</ref> a [[Smuggler|smuggling]] cartel known as the [[Vanguard Axis]] emerged as an offshoot of the Droid Gotra crime family.<ref name="DRVG" /> At some point, the Droid Gotra was involved in a [[Gang dispute on Eshan|gang dispute]] on the planet [[Eshan]], which resulted in the death of many innocents.<ref name="Solo OG" /> Around [[13 BBY]], the Droid Gotra were attempting to place a foothold on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Most Wanted" />

===Amid open war===
{{Quote|I was ''abstractly'' meant to be delivering these two to the Droid Gotra. They had another mission lined up&hellip;|Aphra, in reference to [[BT-1]] and [[0-0-0]]|<ref name="Vader, Part III">[[Darth Vader (2015) 3|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 3]]</ref>}}
[[File:BT-1 and 0-0-0 Villain Pack art.png|right|thumb|200px|Triple Zero and BT-1 were originally going to the Droid Gotra but served Vader for a time.]]
In [[0 ABY]], the Droid Gotra hired the rogue [[archaeologist]] [[Doctor]] [[Chelli Lona Aphra|Aphra]] to [[Theft of the Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix|recover]] the [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]], which could turn a [[protocol droid]] into a [[torture]] specialist, and the prototype [[BT-1 assassin droid]] on their behalf.<ref name="Vader, Part III" /> They also meant to have her liberate a [[Portable droid factory|droid factory]]<ref name="Vader, Part IV">[[Darth Vader (2015) 4|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 4]]</ref> from [[Karina]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> a [[Geonosian]] [[queen]] who survived the Empire's [[Sterilization of Geonosis|sterilization]] of [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Vader, Part IV" /> Although successful in retrieving the droids, she instead turned them over to [[Darth]] Vader<ref name="Vader, Part III" /> and helped him secure Karina's droid factory,<ref name="Vader, Part IV" /> as he needed personal troops for his own ends without the Empire knowing.<ref name="Vader, Part III" />

The [[Cyban Front]], an organization who worked with the Droid Gotra, were hired to participate in the [[Auction of Rur|auction]] of the [[Rur Crystal]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 9" /> so it could be destroyed due to its [[technopathy]] abilities.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 10">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 10|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 10]]</ref> Their attempt was unsuccessful, and the two Cyban Front droids were destroyed during a [[Skirmish aboard the Sorca Retreat|skirmish]] on the [[Sorca Retreat]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 12">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 12|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 12]]</ref><ref name="Doctor Aphra 13">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 13|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 13]]</ref>

[[File:Qira-HiddenEmpire1.png|thumb|left|250px|Palpatine ordered the Droid Gotra to hunt Qi'ra down when she started a war.]]
As part of the machinations of [[Qi'ra]] and her [[agent]]s in the [[Crimson Dawn]], the group was soon pulled into the [[Syndicate War]] along with other organizations such as the Hutt Clan, Black Sun and the Pyke Syndicate. The Droid Gotra and the other organizations were eventually contacted jointly by Emperor [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] himself. Palpatine informed them of Qi'ra's real reason for starting the war, while also reminding them that their power and influence only existed due to his due diligence. Thus he ordered all of them to seek out and destroy Crimson Dawn, stating that whoever managed to bring Qi'ra's corpse to the Emperor would rise high in his estimation.<ref name="Hidden Empire 1">[[Hidden Empire 1|''Hidden Empire'' 1]]</ref>

===New Republic Era===
The Droid Gotra continued to operate after the [[Battle of Jakku|fall]] of the Empire<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" /> in [[5 ABY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> Sometime after [[Supreme Leader]] [[Snoke]]'s assumption of leadership within the [[First Order]], the Gotra attacked and eliminated an armed [[Ubrikkian Industries|Ubrikkian]] freighter commanded by [[Red Key Raiders]] at the [[Perlemian Spur Waypoint 643Besh]]. After [[Gamma-bit 669753]] sweeped the wreckage, they found [[Smuggler's Guide|''Maz Kanata's Journal'']] and flagged valuable datapoints from the contents within. They then began to transmit orders to all members of the Gotra and the Cyban Front stationed in the Rim Territories, ordering them to target the [[Terminus|closest]] [[Little Petrovi|data]] [[Corbett Cluster|cache]]. The transmission was promptly intercepted by the [[Guavian Death Gang]], who then broadcast their own counteracting broadcast and somehow removed Kanata's Journal from the Gotra's possession.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />

[[File:Xrexus Cartel SaV.png|right|thumb|270px|Exantor discovered KRONOS-841 attended an auction hosted by Xev Xrexus before joining the Droid Gotra.]]
Despite the Gotra only being formed after the Clone Wars,<ref name="Most Wanted" /> the police files of [[Tanivos Exantor Divo]], who worked during the late [[Republic Era]], made reference to the Gotra, but those files were only released by his grandson, [[Exantor Divo]], during the New Republic Era. The files claimed that [[KRONOS-841]] and other members of the Gotra attended a [[Xrexus Cartel]] [[Auction of Eldra Kaitis|auction]] of a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] named [[Eldra Kaitis]] before the [[Invasion of Naboo]]. The group was unsuccessful in acquiring the Padawan.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

[[File:Twins dais Huttese lightened.jpg|thumb|left|270px|The Droid Gotra served as security for the Hutt Twins on Nal Hutta.]]
The Droid Gotra were employed by the [[The Twins]] as security for [[Hutt Palace|their fortress]] on [[Nal Hutta]] alongside a group of [[Amani]] warriors, utilising [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]]s. The Droid Gotra [[sentry droid]]s also piloted [[HH-87 Starhopper]] [[starfighter]]s to escort incoming vessels which entered Nal Hutta's [[atmosphere]]. When [[Din Djarin]] betrayed his agreement with the Twins, they sent [[Embo]] who captured him and one of the droids took off his helmet on orders of the Hutt Twins. When Grogu helped Djarin escape [[Hutt's dragonsnake|their dragonsnake]], they sent their makeshift droid army, along with their [[Amani]] warriors to hunt the [[Mandalorian]]. Seeing the [[Anzellan Starship]], was too small for him, Djarin decided to hold the Hutt forces off before succumbing to his poison. After a few days the droids couldn't find Djarin, and neither did Embo, after he asked [[Unidentified species (Grogu)|an]] [[alien]] [[fisher]] named [[Gatori]], who secretly hid Grogu from the [[bounty hunter]]'s [[anooba]] [[Keibu]] and created an [[antidote]] for the [[dragonsnake]] [[poison]]. After Djarin recovered, he and Grogu found the [[TR-RL3 Terrapin-class bulk tanker]] of the [[Smuggler|gunrunner]] he hired to help [[Rotta]]. After the two decided to fight back, the Hutt Twins sent out three starfighters to stop the Turtle Tanker but the transport crashes into their palace and Djarin used a [[Single Trooper Aerial Platform]] to destroy several Droid Grota patrols. Djarin later goes on foot causing the wall to collapse on another patrol. Before heading into the throne room, Djarin fought two large droids [[Arges (droid)|Arges]] and [[Bullethead]], succeeded in using his new [[Amban phase-pulse blaster]] to damage Arges while Bullethead blew up from the [[grenade]]s on its torso. After defeating the Twins and rescuing Rotta, Djarin used his Amban rifle to finish off the rest fo the Droid Gotra, before receiving a comlink from [[Colonel]] [[Ward]] leading the [[Adelphi Rangers]] to rescue Djarin and Grogu, destroying the [[turbolaser]] towers, launching tubes, and the palace, destroying all the sentry droids in the process.<ref name="Mandalorian and Grogu" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Droid Gotra was set to appear in the cancelled [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] video game ''[[Star Wars: 1313]]''. After ''1313''{{'s}} cancellation,<ref name="Interview">{{YouTube|video=CK8_Wh2rkoQ|text=The Authors of Star Wars Timelines On How They Created One of the BEST Star Wars Reference Books!|name=StarWarsExplained|date=2023-09-04}}</ref> the Droid Gotra was first mentioned in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], written by [[James Luceno]]. Despite ''Tarkin''<ref name="Tarkin" /> and the novel ''[[Most Wanted]]'' affirming that the gotra was formed after the Clone Wars,<ref name="Most Wanted" /> the reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'' stated that the Gotra was active before the Invasion of Naboo. As ''Scum and Villainy'' is framed as an in-universe text,<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> this article assumes it to be an in-universe error as well.

According to ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'', the Gotra was formed circa [[18 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> However, information provided by ''Scum and Villainy'' narrows the formation date down to 19 BBY.<ref name="GotraDate" /> As ''Timelines'' only provided an approximate date with room for flexibility,<ref name="Timelines" /> this article assumes ''Scum and Villainy'' is correct.
