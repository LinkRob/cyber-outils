'''Eeusu Estornii''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Senator]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]]. She was an ally of fellow Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]],<ref name="SWVE" /> and was one of the many mourners to attend her [[Padmé Amidala's funeral|funeral]] on Amidala's [[homeworld]] of [[Naboo]]<ref name="RotS">{{Film|III}}</ref> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], including Estornii's appearance, to [[19 BBY]].</ref> where Estornii with other senators in the procession behind the casket.<ref name="RotS" /> She had [[Color|white]] skin, black [[eye]]s and blonde [[hair]]. While attending the [[funeral]], she wore a dark red [[Clothing|dress]] and a black piece of jewellery on her forehead.<ref name="SWVE" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eeusu Estornii appeared in the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Revenge of the Sith'']]. The procession scene she appears in was shot on Stage 3 at [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] on [[September 16]], [[2003]]; day 57 of filming. Estornii was played by [[Jacqui Louez]], assistant to director [[George Lucas]] and producer [[Rick McCallum]].<ref name="MakingOf">''[[The Making of Star Wars Revenge of the Sith]]''</ref> Executive editor of [[Lucas Licensing]],<ref name="Retire">{{SWArchive|url=vault/books/rostoni_retirement/index.html|text=Sue Rostoni to Retire as ''Star Wars'' Editor}}</ref> [[Sue Rostoni]], named the character after herself when given the chance to pick one of the unnamed characters from ''Revenge of the Sith'' and name them.<ref name="TFN Rostoni">{{JCFCite|url=posts/31762425|board=Literature|thread=Sue Rostoni Retiring on July 2011|user=SueRostoni|date=2011-06-14|time=3:14 PM|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20200406052330/https://boards.theforce.net/threads/sue-rostoni-retiring-on-july-2011.31755274/page-3}}</ref> In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the name was used in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]'',<ref name="SWVE" /> while in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, it was first used in the 2005 reference book ''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]''.<ref name="Chronicles">''[[Star Wars Chronicles: The Prequels]]''</ref>
