'''Bowling pin''' was an object used in the [[bowling]] game, where several of them would be stacked together, with the goal of being knowcked down by the player by rolling a ball towards them.<ref name="PnF">''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]''</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{PnFDate|1}}</ref> the game was available at the [[bowling alley]]s onboard the ''[[Death Star]]'' space station, which was destroyed during the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Before its explosion, the bowling alley employees were evacuated from the space station by the [[Firestar Girls]] squadron, some of whom took the bowling pins with them.<ref name="PnF" />
==Behind the scenes==
Bowling pins first appeared in the non-canonical television special ''[[Phineas and Ferb: Star Wars]]'', which aired in 2014, where they can be seen being held by the bowling alley employees onboard the [[Firestar Girls]] squadron ship.<ref name="PnF"/>
