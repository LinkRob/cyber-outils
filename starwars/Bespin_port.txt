'''Bespin port''' was an [[alcohol]]ic beverage. [[Jai Cirri]] ended up nursing a [[hangover]] in his living quarters in [[Cloud City]] after consuming it.<ref name="Clouds" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bespin port originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] roleplaying supplement ''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]'', published in [[1993]]. In Legends continuity, "port" is spelled with a capital letter.<ref name="Guide">''[[Galaxy Guide 9: Fragments from the Rim]]''</ref>
