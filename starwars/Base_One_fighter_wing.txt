A '''fighter wing''' of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]'s [[Massassi Group]] was active during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The [[fighter wing]] consisted of all of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]]s stationed at [[Great Temple|Base One]], the headquarters for the [[Alliance High Command]] and the Massassi Group's base. [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Command]] [[General]] [[Antoc Merrick]] was in command of the fighter wing when its squadrons were scrambled. When the fighter wing was better deployed operationally from a ground-based dispatch or if General Merrick were absent, General [[Jan Dodonna]] of [[Sector Command (Alliance)|Sector Command]] and the Massassi Group would take command.<ref name="UVG" />
