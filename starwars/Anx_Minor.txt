'''Anx Minor''' was an astronomical object<ref name="MandoVG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian Visual Guide]]''</ref> located in the [[Anx Minor system]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Raioballo sector]].<ref name="Systems" /> It lay along the [[Veragi Trade Route]]<ref name="Route" /> between the [[Dantooine system|Dantooine]] and [[Ord Trasi system]]s.<ref name="MandoVG" /> It was the site of a [[Massacre at Anx Minor|massacre]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Shadow Fall">''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref> In [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{ShadowFallDate}}</ref> the massacre was among several events reviewed by [[New Republic Intelligence]] [[agent]] [[Caern Adan]] and [[Alphabet Squadron]] [[New Republic pilot|pilot]] [[Yrica Quell]] in search of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] after the [[New Republic]] [[Intelligence]] flagged it as possible 204th activity. Quell felt that the massacre at Anx Minor shared similarities with the [[fighter wing]]'s [[raid on Beauchen]], but the location was distant from other possible 204th sightings on her and Adan's map.<ref name="Shadow Fall" />

==Behind the scenes==
Anx Minor was mentioned in [[canon]] in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Shadow Fall: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Shadow Fall" /> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], [[Anx Minor/Legends|Anx Minor]] was first mentioned in the reference book ''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]] and published in [[2002]].<ref name="NEGTC">''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''</ref>
