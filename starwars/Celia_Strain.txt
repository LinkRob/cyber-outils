'''Celia Strain''' is an artist who specializes in animal portraiture and scientific illustration. In the 1980s she produced several promotional posters for [[National Public Radio]], including one for the [[Star Wars (radio)|''Star Wars'' radio drama]].<ref name="About">{{WebCite|url=http://www.celiastrainportraits.com/ABOUT/About.html |text=About |work=Celia Strain Portraits |archivedate=20211220001500 }}</ref> The poster depicts [[C-3PO/Legends|C-3PO]] wearing headphones and standing in front of an old-fashioned microphone: this alludes to NPR's intention to revive a lost art form from the golden age of radio - updating it to feature recognizable imagery from {{Film|IV}}.<ref name="Brandt">{{BookCite|author=Jessica K. Brandt |container=An Elegant Weapon for a More Civilized Age: Star Wars, Public Radio, and Middlebrow Cold War Culture. |title=A Galaxy Here and Now: Historical and Cultural Readings of Star Wars |contributor=edited by Peter W. Lee |publisher=McFarland |date=2016 |isbn=9781476662206}}</ref>
