'''Bullgar''' was a hulking [[Dowutin]] member of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> they were stationed at a [[Nihil base|base]] on the [[planet]] [[Angoth]]. When the base was [[Mission to Angoth|attacked]] by [[Jedi Master]] [[Mirro Lox]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Amadeo Azzazzo]], the Dowutin emerged to fight the Jedi. As the other marauders chanted Bullgar's name, the Dowutin picked up a prone [[human]] Nihil and used them as a [[club]]. Lox and Azzazzo performed a [[Flip-and-push maneuver|maneuver]] that allowed the Padawan to leap over Bullgar, using his [[Azzazzo's lightsaber|lightsaber]] to pierce the Dowutin's neck and [[Death|kill]] them.<ref name="Tears of the Nameless" />
