The '''''Crimson Smiter''''' was an [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] in the [[Resistance]]'s [[Crimson Squadron]]. When a [[First Order]] [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighter]] crashed into its bomb bay, it set off a chain reaction which resulted in the ''[[Cobalt Hammer]]'' being the final [[bomber]] left.<ref name="JN" />
