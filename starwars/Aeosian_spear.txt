A type of '''vibro-blade spear''' was used by [[Aeosian]]s on the [[planet]] of [[Aeos Prime]]. When these [[spear]]s were activated, the connecting point between the spearhead and the staff glowed, and electricity began to arc around the blade. When activated, the spears were capable of disabling an aged [[B2-series super battle droid]], and blocking blaster bolts. The spears had a second point above that main blade, the tip of which glowed [[Color|blue]] when the spear was activated. These spears had a gold material fashioned onto them, mostly around the point between the staff and the spearhead.<ref name="New World" />

A variant of this spear was used by the the [[Unidentified Aeosian Queen|tribe's queen]], which had more gold material fashioned onto it, and two points from the side edges of the main blade, giving it the appearance more akin to a [[trident]].<ref name="New World" />

==Behind the scenes==
To illustrate the Aeosians' balance of technology and nature, the design team added veins of [[gold]] in the sea rock of the [[Aeos Prime|planet's]] tunnels, visually tying into the gold metal of the Aeosians' [[armor]] and weapons.<ref name="The New World Bucket's List">{{SW|url=news/buckets-list-extra-the-new-world-star-wars-resistance|text=Bucket's List Extra: 7 Fun Facts from "The New World" - ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref>
