'''Bakkar Spire''' was a spire in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]] that acted as the location of a [[Spaceport (Batuu)|spaceport]].<ref name="Trading Card" /> During the [[Cold War|conflict]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]],<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref> the spaceport served as a base of operations for [[Hondo's complex|Hondo Ohnaka]], who frequently recruited crew members<ref name="Trading Card" /> after securing the use of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' from [[Chewbacca]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bakkar Spire was identified on a map card distributed at the ''Star Wars'': Galactic Nights event on [[May 27]], [[2018]].<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|WDWscope|status/1000962657707614208|WDWscope|quote=Hondo and Chewie make a deal / Bakkar Spire|archiveurl=https://archive.is/dBzpr}}</ref> Its location corresponds to the entrance area for the [[Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run|''Millennium Falcon'': Smuggler's Run]] attraction at the [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] themed land.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge" />
