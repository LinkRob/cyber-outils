{{Quote|I'm not sick! I just&hellip; I get things wrong. I'm selfish. I&hellip; I ''use'' people. But&hellip;I don't expect to ''sleep well''. Ever.|Chelli Lona Aphra|<ref name="Doctor Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 19]]</ref>}}
'''Chelli Lona Aphra''', also known as "'''Smallfry'''," was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] [[archaeologist]]. Born in [[24 BBY]] as the daughter of [[Korin Aphra|Korin]] and [[Lona Aphra]], she learned much about [[the galaxy]] due to Korin's [[scholar]]ly ambitions. Korin was particularly interested in the [[Jedi Order]] as well as the ancient [[Ordu Aspectu]], but when his work began to consume his life, the neglected Lona took Aphra with her to live on the [[planet]] [[Arbiflux]]. When Lona was [[Death|killed]] by [[Pirate|raiders]] who had been instructed by the [[Galactic Empire]] to cause a chaos that would result in an Imperial takeover, Aphra was taken by the Empire and returned to her father's residence. Wanting no part in her neglectful parent's life, Aphra attempted to burn down his house before striking out on her own.

She later became a student at the [[University of Bar'leth]], making strides towards an [[archaeology]] doctorate. During her time at [[university]], she would make connections with individuals like [[Sana Starros]], who she was briefly romantically involved with, [[Eustacia Okka]], a future [[Shadow University]] professor, and [[Kho Phon Farrus]]. Aphra would fraudulently achieve her degree after blackmailing her hated [[professor]], [[Sava]] [[Toob-Nix]], who had also developed a hatred for her. After Aphra went on an unremarkable research trip to [[Boothi XII]] and Toob-Nix taunted Aphra with his illegal, mind-controlling [[abersyn symbiote]] [[parasite]]s, Aphra stole the symbiotes and re-located them to the [[Boothi XII vault]] to make it appear as if she had made a breakthrough discovery. Toob-Nix was unable to expose her trickery without exposing his own illegal activities, and thus, Aphra achieved her doctorate.

After the [[Battle of Yavin]], Aphra would be recruited by the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] after he witnessed her [[droid]] customization handiwork while [[Attack on an unidentified pirate base|slaughtering]] a group of [[Crymorah-backed pirates|pirates]]. Aphra, along with her [[assassin droid]] companions, the [[protocol droid]] [[assassin]] [[0-0-0]] and the [[BT-1 assassin droid]] "Blastomech" prototype [[BT-1]], fulfilled several missions for the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. She helped him build an army [[Artificial intelligence|artificially intelligent]] [[BX-series droid commando|droid commandos]], assisted in [[Skirmish in Cylo's research base|combating]] Doctor [[Cylo]], an Imperial [[scientist]] who sought to replace Vader at the [[Darth Sidious|Emperor's]] side, and even thwart [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] forces on [[Vrogas Vas]]. However, as her work became increasingly dangerous and Vader became a less reliable ally, Aphra plotted to find a way out of their partnership. However, it was Vader who betrayed her first. Wanting to eliminate her due to how much knowledge she had, Vader Force-choked Aphra and sent her out an [[airlock]] to her death. However, Aphra was rescued by Triple-Zero, Beetee, and the [[Wookiee]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Krrsantan|Black Krrsantan]], whom Aphra had built a tenuous trust with during her adventures.

Now an enemy of the Empire, Aphra resumed her treasure-hunting and archaeological quests. Sometime after a mission to locate the [[Sword of Khashyun]], she was approached by her father, who wanted to help her get her doctorate, which had recently been suspended following the truth of her symbiote plot being revealed, restored. Aphra reluctantly joined forces with Korin, and after discovering the [[Citadel of Rur]], claimed the [[Rur Crystal]], a computer core that contained the consciousness of the ancient [[Force-sensitive]] [[Rur]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
====Family issues====

Chelli Lona Aphra was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]]<ref name="Databank" /> [[Reproduction|born]] in the [[Standard year|year]] [[24 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> to [[Korin Aphra|Korin]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> and [[Lona Aphra]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 5|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 5]]</ref>&mdash;her [[Parent|father and mother]], respectively. [[Aphra family|The Aphra's]] retained a [[Aphra family home|house]]<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 2|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 2]]</ref> in the [[University Quarter]] on the [[Second Moon of Thrinittik]], a peaceful world<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> free from the strife that had broken out across [[the galaxy]] during the [[Separatist Crisis]]<ref name="Propaganda">''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]''</ref> and the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> At some point, Chelli also gained a sibling in [[Dellen Aphra]], her half-sister via her father.<ref name="Chaos Agent 5" />

[[File:SecondMoonOfThrinittik-2016DoctorAphra32.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The Aphra family home]]
Young Chelli&mdash;known affectionately as "Little Boop" to her mother<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> and father&mdash;often observed the latter's work<ref name="Doctor Aphra 3">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 3|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 3]]</ref> as a dedicated [[scholar]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars New">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> She absorbed information as he spoke about subjects, such as the extinct [[Massassi]] [[species]] and their [[Massassi piece]] [[crystal]]s. Korin specialized in researching the ancient [[Ordu Aspectu]], a nearly-forgotten splinter group of the [[Jedi Order]] that disappeared with little trace [[Millennium|millennia]] before Chelli's birth during the [[Republic Era]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 3" />

After the [[Great Jedi Purge]], Korin began neglecting his family to focus on his work, believing it would make the [[galaxy]] safer for his daughter by restoring the [[Light side of the Force|light]] that had died with the [[Jedi]].<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5" /> The Jedi were a group of spiritual followers of [[the Force]] who had served as the primary [[Law enforcement agency|peacekeepers]] of the Republic for thousands of generations. While the Jedi were dedicated to the light,<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> they had counterparts in the [[dark side of the Force]] called the [[Sith]]. The existence of the Sith was doubted by the time Chelli was born,<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> but the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] was secretly responsible for the galactic strife.<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> The Sith's long-last artifacts were said to be deadly and destructive, but Chelli found her father's stories of them much more interesting than those of the Jedi. It seemed to Chelli that Korin only paid attention to her when she pretended to be interested in his Jedi research.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />

Lona struggled in her [[marriage]] due to her husband's obsession<ref name="Doctor Aphra 3" /> with locating the Ancient Aspects of the Jedi<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5" /> and what she saw as his need to achieve greatness.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> Although she never outright hated him, they disagreed on what was best for Chelli. Lona would not compromise on that, and thought her family should both live without fear of consequences and make a new home in the middle of nowhere.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 5" /> The couple would argue about Korin's research when they thought their daughter was [[Sleep|asleep]]. Even when she was only six years old, Chelli understood that she could get them to stop fighting if they paid attention to her instead.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />

One night, Chelli interrupted her parents while they argued about Korin's plan to secure records from the university library on the [[Fifth Moon of Thrinittik]] before they could be destroyed by the Republic's successor state,<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> the totalitarian [[Galactic Empire]] that was ruled by Sidious in his public guise as [[Emperor]] Palpatine.<ref name="Episode III" /> Chelli's father asked her if she would like to stay home and play with her [[Aphra family's tooka|pet tooka]] or join him on an adventure. Wanting her father's attention and the thrill of possible danger, she agreed. It turned out he invited her in part because she was small enough to slip into narrow spaces to locate hidden materials, cute enough to distract the librarians while her father gathered up the materials, and able to help carry them after he bribed a librarian. She was later certain that was the event that made her mother decide to leave her husband and take their child with her.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />

[[File:AphraFamilyValues-2016DoctorAphra32.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Distracted by his research, Korin Aphra did not notice that Lona was about to leave him and take Chelli with her.]]
Examining his findings from the library, Korin realized his collection of stolen [[memory crystal]]s that the Imperials had marked for the [[garbage compactor]] were indeed genuine; he had recovered Jedi heresiologes, including an account of [[Oo'ob]] called "The Crimes of Jedi Renegade Oo'ob the Apostate." Korin was excited about the findings and their potential to help him restore the Ordu Aspectu or find the Lost Citadel of [[Garn]]. Preoccupied with his discovery, he did not notice Chelli cut her finger on one of the crystals. Lona did not share his excitement, as his research could get the family marked by censors for a firing squad, and Chelli did not understand why her father did not seem to care about her injury. Lona told Chelli to pack some of her things for a trip,<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> and they left Korin behind.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 3" />

====Arbiflux====
{{Quote|I'm here because the nice Imperials dropped me home! Mom's ''dead in a ditch'' on that alien world she took us to when she left you for being obsessed by old dumb stuff! Oh&mdash;the psychologist says I may have some issues. Fair to warn you.|Chelli Lona Aphra, to her father|<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2" />}}
[[File:Arbiflux-2016DoctorAphra32.jpg|thumb|left|300px|The homestead on Arbiflux]]
Lona Aphra moved Chelli to a [[Lona Aphra's house|homestead]] on [[Arbiflux]], a [[forest]] world in the largely primitive [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Darth Vader 10" /> Their new homestead, however, was experiencing a drought, leaving it surrounded by dry grass and dying trees.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />

At their new homestead, they farmed [[nuna]]s, which Chelli hated and called dumb, evil fartbirds. Her mother admonished her not to call things evil, even the Sith. Lona said it was called the dark side because they kept ''in the dark'' to be secretive and more easily impose order in their belief that it was the right thing. Idealists like Korin and the Jedi had a simplistic perspective of good and evil that involved purists shouting at each other while labeling the other side heretics; such idealism was useless in the shifting galaxy and just made people fight.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 33">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 33|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 33]]</ref> Idealists just brought trouble down on themselves and others.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> She told Chelli, "Evil's just a measure of how much ''your'' choices take away other's peoples," and that all anyone could hope for was to do right by the people they loved. Young Chelli pointed out that her mother may be evil for taking her away without asking her or Korin, and Lona acknowledged that while saying it was for Chelli's safety.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 33" />

Over the years, Chelli's mother would also tell her everyone needed to make mistakes and learn from them, but she would not let the girl take risks with her life. Despite emphasizing the need to stay sharp and learn, Lona also thought ambition and intellect would quickly get people killed. Chelli would later see her mother's actions as finding any excuse to keep her safe, and that taking her to a place where nothing ever changed was a way to do that in a galaxy where nothing could be controlled.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 34">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 34|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 34]]</ref>

For two years, Chelli and her mother were alone with no other sentient beings around.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 34" /> One day, they were attempting to prime a group of [[chatterplant]]s as an alarm system for predators, something that Chelli was never successful at doing. Just as she was complaining about how boring and safe it was on Arbiflux, a group of [[Pirate|raiders]] arrived,<ref name="Doctor Aphra 32" /> under orders from Imperial [[Coalition for Progress]] Minister [[Pitina Mar-Mas Voor]] to stage attacks and create chaos that would make planetary beg for Imperial intervention.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 36">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 36|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 36]]</ref> Chelli saw her mother's arm shot off with a [[blaster]], causing rapid blood loss.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> Lona gave her own blaster to Chelli, and urged her to run ahead and not look back. While Chelli fired the blaster at the raiders as she ran, and Lona sent a transmission to the Empire, calling for them to help her daughter before she died.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 34" />

Traumatized by her mother's death,<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2" /> Chelli would later have conflicting memories regarding the event. She remembered looking back and witnessing her mother collapse and die;<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> she thought that after Imperial [[scout trooper]]s found her in a ditch, she wanted to stay with her mother and turned to see that her mother had died without her noticing;<ref name="Doctor Aphra 34" /> she also recalled that she returned after the attack to find her mother's body lying in the ditch.<ref name="Darth Vader 10" />

[[File:AphraFamilyHome-2016DoctorAphra2.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Korin Aphra races to his home after his daughter set fire to it.]]
The troopers brought Chelli to an encampment, where an [[Imperial officer]] told her that the planet's natives had formally invited the Empire and she would be returned to civilization, away from "nuisances" like the raiders that had killed her mother.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 35">[[Doctor Aphra (2016) 35|''Doctor Aphra'' (2016) 35]]</ref> At some point, she was evaluated by a [[psychologist]]. Later, the Empire returned Chelli to her former home on the Second Moon of Thrinittik where her father still lived. She set [[fire]] to the house, attempting to burn up all his research with it. He returned to find his home burning and raced inside. As he expressed his surprise at seeing her and distractedly patted out the flames<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2" /> to save his life's work,<ref name="Doctor Aphra 3" /> she screamed at him that his wife had been killed, and informed him that a psychologist had said she may have some issues.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2" />

====Early university years====
{{Quote|Isn't that all the more reason to do it? Isn't that what being an archaeologist is about? Making new discoveries, finding treasures of the past, being able to see the&hellip;the fullness of the history of the entire galaxy? If we want to make ''real'' discoveries, it's always going to be a little dangerous&mdash;|Chelli Lona Aphra, arguing with Sava Toob-Nix in favor of unsanctioned expeditions|<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />}}
As a young adult, Aphra studied [[Timeline of galactic history|galactic history]] and ancient civilizations while working toward a [[doctor]]ate degree in [[archaeology]] at the [[University of Bar'leth]],<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" /> located on the [[planet]] [[Bar'leth]] in the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Lando 1">[[Lando 1|''Lando'' 1]]</ref> At the time, she thought attending [[university]] was a necessary step to becoming a proper, professional archaeologist, and she had not yet perfected her own personal archaeological methods. She immediately developed a mutual hatred with one of her instructors, [[Sava]] [[Toob-Nix]],<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> who held strong opinions on what qualified as an [[archaeologist]] that Aphra did not share.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

[[File:SanaStarros-2015StarWars42.png|thumb|left|200px|Sana Starros was also studying at the University of Bar'leth.]]
Early in the program, Aphra frequently ran late to Toob-Nix's lectures, which she found frightfully dull, and would contradict him mid-lecture. He threatened to begin the expulsion process and would lock the door to keep the latecomer out of his lecture hall. At one point, Aphra had two lovers, a second-year student and a third-year student. She had neglected to tell either of them about the other and they confronted her together, which caused her to be late for Toob-Nix's class again. After successfully sneaking into Toob-Nix's classroom through a window, she gave herself away by interrupting him as he told her fellow [[cadet]]s that all of the artifacts once stored at the university library on the Fifth Moon of Thrinittik had been destroyed, along with the entire structure, in a cataclysmic blast; the Empire had destroyed the library sometime after Aphra's childhood visit there with her father. She protested that while the library had been attacked and much of it had decayed, enough of the structure remained that some of the artifacts may remain buried and preserved, awaiting an expedition to uncover them. The Sava dismissed her ideas as a fairy tale that would never be authorized. As they argued about her belief that unsanctioned expeditions and taking dangerous risks were worth it and what archaeology was about, another student named [[Sana Starros]] spoke up and agreed with her. Starros further pointed out that there was research in their university library that supported Aphra's assertions. Aphra had not previously noticed the quiet and non-disruptive Starros, but she was immediately enamored with her voice and the way Starros had taken her side.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />

Despite the two students humiliating him in his own class, Toob-Nix did not begin the expulsion process as Aphra expected, and she was unsure for years after why he had not. Unbeknownst to Aphra, Starros approached him after class and blackmailed him. She warned him that if he expelled Aphra, she would end his career by reporting how he furthered his own academic agenda through teaching his class incorrect information.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />

During her time at the university, Aphra nearly got a new student, [[Kho Phon Farrus]], expelled from the university on the day of their enrollment,<ref name="Aphra 19">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 19|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 19]]</ref> and according to Starros, continued to get Farrus in trouble all the time.<ref name="Aphra 18">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 18|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 18]]</ref> Over time, they came to consider themself Aphra's archnemesis.<ref name="Aphra (2020) 17">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 17|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 17]]</ref>

====Doctoral candidacy====
{{Quote|Aphra, you are flash. Archaeology isn't about flash. [&hellip;] All those years mocking me, you never thought to ask me why I selected you for my doctoral supervision. Because I was never going to let someone like you become a doctor. Not because of my standards. Not because your work is sloppy. Just because I hate you.|Sava Toob-Nix, to Chelli Aphra|<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />}}
[[File:AphraInUniversity-2016DoctorAphra1.jpg|thumb|right|300px|Aphra in Sava Toob-Nix's class at the University of Bar'leth]]
Towards the end of her schooling, Toob-Nix selected Aphra as his candidate for doctoral [[Supervisor|supervision]]. Despite requiring his approval for her degree, she continued to mock him on a self-described weekly basis.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" /> Aphra would sometimes fall asleep during a class she shared with [[Eustacia Okka]], and one day she noticed Okka reading "[[Lost Legends of the High Republic]]" on her datapad. When she asked Okka about it after class, Okka was initially embarrassed before Aphra reassured her with a kiss and told her she worried too much about what other people thought.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 2020 2">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 2|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 2]]</ref>

On one occasion, Aphra sat in with a class of the Sava's undergraduates. When Toob-Nix inquired as to why Aphra, a senior doctoral candidate, was sitting in with the less-experienced students, Aphra [[Slicer|sliced]] into the classroom's [[holoprojector]] and made the two [[Hologram|holographic]] historical beings kiss, rather than stand still. Toob-Nix angrily expelled Aphra from his classroom. Aphra later told the [[Standard day|day's]] events to her friend [[Susina]], who urged Aphra not to antagonize the Sava. Aphra, however, wasn't worried about receiving Toob-Nix's approval because her graduation project, she believed, would be too important for the Sava to dismiss.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

[[File:AphraWithFlashlight-2016DoctorAphra1.png|thumb|left|170px|Dressed in her archaeological gear, Aphra explores the vault on Boothi XII.]]
Aphra's project was to travel to a backwater world of [[farmer]]s, [[Boothi XII]]. There, Aphra hoped to find evidence of a [[Precursor species|precursor race]] in a [[Boothi XII vault|subterranean vault]]. Unfortunately for the aspiring archaeologist, when Aphra later explored every [[centimeter]] of the vault in her archaeological gear&mdash;a [[leather]] [[hat]], [[greatcoat]], [[Glowrod|flashlight]], and [[blaster pistol]]&mdash;she found nothing but an empty [[cave]] system.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

After returning to Bar'leth, Aphra wrote a report on her underwhelming findings on Boothi XII. Toob-Nix confronted Aphra after reading the report and sarcastically chided her for "finding an old shed on a planet known as being full of old sheds." Aphra retorted that she believed Toob-Nix a far inferior Sava compared to the former,<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" /> the [[Ugnaught]] [[Korin Pers]].<ref name="Lando 1" /> Instead of replying with another insult, Toob-Nix led her to a secret room in the university where he stored several [[abersyn symbiote]]s which he had collected over [[Standard decade|decades]], and made quiescent by exposure to [[plasma]] venting. Horrified that the Sava was concealing the highly illegal [[Brain|mind]]-controlling [[parasite]]s, Aphra listened as Toob-Nix revealed to Aphra the true reason he had chosen her for his personal supervision: not because he saw Aphra as a poor student who cared more about excitement than archaeology, but simply because he hated her. Aphra threatened to blackmail him, but he assured her that her inexperienced word wouldn't be able to touch his professional reputation.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

Aphra knew Toob-Nix was correct, so she instead called Starros via [[comlink]] and enlisted the aid of one Starros's [[alien]] friends who owned a lot of "hardware." Within the next two days, Aphra, with the help of Starros and her alien friend, sliced into Toob-Nix's secret room, preventing alarms from sounding due to the hack, and removed all of the dormant abersyn symbiotes in their respective capsules, returned to the empty vault on Boothi XII, and placed the symbiotes in the vault; this made it appear that the symbiotes had been left untouched for thousands of years. Aphra soon published her findings, and her "find" was hailed as the greatest archeoxenobiology find in centuries.<ref name="Doctor Aphra 1" />

===Adventures on Dantooine===
{{Quote|If you're feeling philosophical, you'll add something like, 'All innocence died that day', and people will nod, and know that just because you did this really bad thing, it doesn't make you a bad person.|Aphra to a stormtrooper|<ref name="The Trigger">{{StoryCite|story=The Trigger|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>}}
Aphra was staying in [[Dantoo Town]] while trying to reactivate and upgrade some war-surplus [[droideka]]s. In need of a 3.23 [[colicoid]]ic pulse modulator, she attempted to loot the abandoned rebel base on [[Dantooine]], believing it to be salvage-rich. While working her way through a stripped [[Central Computer/Canon|central comm]], Aphra was alerted by the [[Ark Angel II|''Ark Angel'']] to the presence of [[TIE series|TIE-fighters]]. While retreating to the ''Ark Angel'', Aphra encountered three [[stormtrooper]]s doing a perimeter sweep in an area that she had covered in a layer of [[micromine]]s. Knowing that escaping the nearby [[Star Destroyer]] would be difficult, coupled with not wanting to let people die and a reluctance to abandon the droidekas she'd been working on, Aphra decided peacefully surrender herself to the stormtroopers.<ref name="The Trigger">{{StoryCite|story=The Trigger|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref>

[[File:GenCassioTagge-ANH.png|thumb|left|200px|General Cassio Tagge (pictured) encountered Aphra on Dantooine.]]
The stormtroopers questioned and searched Aphra before bringing her into the new Imperial base on Dantooine. The stormtroopers presented Aphra to [[General]] [[Cassio Tagge]], and informed him they were looking for Aphra's hidden [[speeder]], which she had lied to them about to keep them from discovering the ''Ark Angel''. Tagge questioned Aphra about why she had been at the Rebel base, not believing that Aphra was simply scavenging for supplies. Aphra attempted to persuade Tagge of her innocence. Aphra stated that she was unaware of a Rebel base on Dantooine, because, due to the planet's primitiveness, no one there would think to resist the Galactic Empire. Amused, Tagge then revealed to Aphra that the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] had recently [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed Alderaan]]. Curious as to how the Death Star's weapon worked, Aphra enthusiastically questioned Tagge about it, though he did not reveal any details. Tagge then declared that he did not think Aphra was a Rebel, but that she was "trouble," and sentenced her to execution.<ref name="The Trigger" />

Tagge assigned a stormtrooper to take Aphra out into the woods and execute her, while they continued to search for her alleged hidden speeder. To buy herself time to plan an escape, Aphra conversed with the stormtrooper. After asking him if it was his first execution, Aphra spoke with him about how firing squads on some worlds and the Death Star were the same; as they involved a variety of people, no one person would have to shoulder the responsibility for taking people's lives—there was no one person to pull "the trigger." Aphra then attempted to guilt-trip the stormtrooper, by illustrating the true horror and historical impact of the destruction of Alderaan, saying the stormtrooper would always remember it as the day he executed Aphra.<ref name="The Trigger" />

While distracting the stormtrooper with her talk, Aphra was able to secretly jam the frequency on his Imperial-model blaster for a few seconds; disarmed him and held him captive with his own weapon. She informed the stormtrooper that while ''he'' had never shot anyone in cold blood, she had. Threatening to kill him, Aphra ordered the stormtrooper to remove his helmet. Aphra stated that it was easy to kill a stormtrooper, but harder to kill another human. Aphra then philosophized that the Death Star did indeed have "a trigger," as it was easy to kill a planet because it was more abstract than personal. Although Aphra had always defended the Empire—believing it brought peace and order to the galaxy—she realized that what had happened to Alderaan changed her opinion.<ref name="The Trigger" />

Aphra decided to release the young stormtrooper, asking him to tell his superiors that he followed through on his orders. Abandoning the blaster, Aphra was able to successfully board the ''Ark Angel'' and get into orbit. She then managed to avoid the Star Destroyer before jumping into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="The Trigger" />

===Servant to a Sith Lord===
====Enlisted by Darth Vader====
=====Odd jobs building to something greater=====
Sometime after parting ways with Starros,<ref name="Star Wars 19">[[Star Wars (2015) 19|''Star Wars'' (2015) 19]]</ref> Aphra reactivated and customized a pair of Clone Wars-era Separatist [[droideka]] [[security droid]]s with [[missile launcher]]s. Aphra then sold them to a small group of [[Crymorah-backed pirates|Crymorah Syndicate-funded pirates]] who operated from a [[Unidentified pirate space station|space station]] in the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]. However, in the year [[0 ABY]], the pirates gained the attention of the Galactic Empire and were [[Skirmish over an unidentified green planet|hunted]] down and [[Attack on an unidentified pirate base|eradicated]] personally by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]].<ref name="Darth Vader 2">[[Darth Vader (2015) 2|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 2]]</ref> Vader was a member of the ancient [[Sith Order]], and second in the Imperial hierarchy only to the [[Galactic Emperor]] himself, Sheev Palpatine.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> Having accessed the pirates' main [[computer]] while aboard,<ref name="Darth Vader 2" /> Vader learned of Doctor Aphra's involvement in upgrading the droidekas, which greatly impressed him. As such, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] set out to recruit her to his secret list of allies.<ref name="Darth Vader 3" />

Aphra, meanwhile, was hired by the [[Droid Gotra]],<ref name="Darth Vader 3" /> which was a militarized organization of Separatist battle droids who were not disabled<ref name="Propaganda" /> by the droid shutdown order<ref name="The Last Battle">{{Rebels|The Last Battle}}</ref> at the conclusion of the Clone Wars. Aphra, as an archaeologist who specialized in droid and weapons technologies, was hired for work on several missions; which included stealing the ancient [[Triple-Zero protocol personality matrix]], to locate and activate the unique [[BT-1 assassin droid]] "Blastomech" prototype, and to recover a forgotten [[portable droid factory]] on the [[desert]] world of [[Geonosis]].<ref name="Darth Vader 3" />

Aboard her prized ship, the ''Ark Angel'', Aphra located the "Blastomech" prototype, designated [[BT-1]]. BT-1 was floating in [[Realspace|space]] near the remains of an Imperial-built [[Tarkin Initiative]] [[Tarkin Initiative base|base]], where BT-1 was kept before he destroyed it. However, BT-1 had gone dormant and would not wake up&mdash;the only languages the droid spoke were seemingly lost to history when the Tarkin Initiative base was destroyed.<ref name="Darth Vader 3" />

At some point after, Aphra was tracked and attached by Maz Kanata's gang, who managed to take down the Ark Angel's hyperdrive and damage her life support systems. Maz Kanata herself communicated with the ship and angrily berated Aphra for having apparently stolen stealth microdroid dust which Kanata's gang had taken great lengths to procure. Kanata believed Aphra had something in mind for its use and so Aphra tried to calm Kanata down, envisioning her and Kanata having a drink together, but Kanata refused to budge, continually firing upon the Ark Angel. Aphra continued to put on a confident front, buying time as she weathered through Kanata's rising accusations, even criticizing Maz for essentially misusing the potential of the artifacts Maz acquired. At a crucial point, Aphra then revealed that she had "liberated" a few astromech scraps from Maz Kanata which she now used to fix her ship's operating system, enhance its weapon system and use as fuel for the ship's modified engines. This allowed her to disable Kanata's ships, with Kanata ordering a withdrawal shortly thereafter.<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" />

Aphra's next stop was a large [[asteroid]]-turned-space station: [[Quarantine World III]] in [[Kallidahin Space]]&mdash;which was where the Triple-Zero matrix was held in quarantine.<ref name="Darth Vader 3" />

[[File:SalvadorLarrocaTextless-Vader3.png|thumb|right|250px|While attempting to steal the Triple-Zero matrix, Aphra accepted the invitation of Darth Vader after his surprise appearance.]]
Aphra stealthily made her way into the quarantine facilities and located the safe where the droid matrix was kept. Fearing security measures, Aphra released a cloud of [[stealth microdroid dust]], which revealed a web of [[laser]]s that would trigger and alert the facility to Aphra's trespass if eclipsed. The doctor crawled prone to the safe and disconnected the personality matrix and stowed it on her body without incident. However, on her return journey crawling under the lasers, she fluked and triggered them. Aphra was forced to sprint to the [[hangar]] of the quarantine world as the facility [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destructed]] around her. After outrunning a [[Deflector shield|shielded]] droideka and diving under a closing doorway, Aphra was confronted by Quarantine World III's [[curator]] and her old adversary, [[Utani Xane]]. Before Aphra could be escorted away back to prison, an uninvited [[TIE series|TIE fighter series]] [[starfighter]] approached and released none other than [[Darth]] Vader. Mouth agape at the arrival of Vader himself, Aphra watched as the dark lord systematically eradicated the B2 battle droids using the Force and his red-bladed [[lightsaber]]. Aphra took advantage of the chaos and tackled Xane, who was attempting to flee with the personality matrix. In the tumble, Aphra barely managed to catch the matrix and herself as she and it went over a large ledge and nearly into a deep chasm. ===Recovering the Sword of Khashyun===
====Hired by Dok Ondar====

She wished to auction the sword<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 4">[[Galaxy's Edge 4|''Galaxy's Edge'' 4]]</ref> and brought Triple-Zero and BT-1 to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]], where she met with the pirate Hondo Ohnaka to pitch her idea and request for a starship.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge 3" /> However, Aphra was instead hired by the [[Ithorian]] [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]], who sought to acquire the ancient artifact for his [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|collection]]. Aphra then analysed the Sword of Khashyun, recalling its legend. After a brief interruption by Ondar, she added about the stolen maps she had with her. After some more traveling, Aphra and her companions reached the mummified body of a [[Unidentified Dowutin Sith|Dowutin Sith]], with the Sword of Khashyun before it. The Shistavanen removed the sword, when the rogue archaeologist noticed a wind noise, which BT-1 identified as an ultrasonic noise. Aphra guessed that it was an animal call, and that was when a [[Tuk'ata]], a Sith [[Dog|hound]] was released. She and her cohorts ran from the hound, and the rogue archaeologist made BT-1 trip the final Shistavanen, leaving the mercenary to the Tuk'ata. ==Behind the scenes==
Chelli Lona Aphra was created by writer [[Kieron Gillen]], illustrator [[Salvador Larroca]], and editor [[Heather Antos]] for the [[Marvel Comics]]' ''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader (2015)|Star Wars: Darth Vader]]'' comic book series, which spawned a spin-off comic book series entitled [[Star Wars: Doctor Aphra (2016)|''Star Wars: Doctor Aphra'']]. According to Gillen, Aphra was conceived as the archaeological antithesis of [[Indiana Jones]], the title character of [[Lucasfilm]]'s [[Wikipedia:Indiana Jones (franchise)|''Indiana Jones'' franchise]].<ref name="Darth Vader Volume 2">''[[Star Wars: Darth Vader Vol. 2]]''</ref> Indeed, [[Star Wars - Das offizielle Magazin 91|''Star Wars - The Official Magazine'' 91]] stated as such, noting her only similarities to Jones' are an interest in archaeology and a habit to get into "absurd situations" while in search of artifacts.<ref name="Issue 91">{{GermanFanCite|91|Database}}</ref>

[[January LaVoy]] voiced Aphra in the audiobook adaptation of the short story "[[The Trigger]]."<ref name="From a Certain Point of View">[[From a Certain Point of View (audiobook)|''From a Certain Point of View'' audiobook]]</ref> In the [[2020]] audio drama ''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]'', Aphra was voiced by [[Emily Woo Zeller]].<ref name="Aphra Audiobook" /> At the New York Comic Con [[2018]], [[Hasbro]] revealed that an action figure of Doctor Aphra was slated for release in Spring [[2019]].<ref name="Hasbro">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2018-star-wars-hasbro-panel|text=NYCC 2018: 6 Things We Learned at the ''Star Wars'' Hasbro Panel}}</ref>

When asked about Aphra's specific sexuality, Kieron Gillen said, "I normally say Aphra's a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesbian lesbian]. I've never written her with any romantic interest in men." He and co-writer [[Simon Spurrier]] noted that some readers believed Aphra flirted with Darth Vader, but Gillen wrote her as "primarily romantically interested in women." He added that the ''Star Wars'' universe does not have the same terminology used in the real world, nor does it have the same [[Wikipedia:Homophobia|homophobia]], so characters would likely have different ways of processing and identifying their sexuality.<ref name="KGSS Int">{{SW|url=news/doctor-aphra-creator-kieron-gillen-co-writer-si-spurrier-discuss-whats-next-for-the-fan-favorite-rogue|text=''Doctor Aphra'' Creator Kieron Gillen, Co-Writer Si Spurrier Discuss What's Next for the Fan Favorite Rogue}}</ref> [[Sana Starros]] stated [[in-universe]] that "guys and Aphra don't exactly mix" in Gillen's [[Star Wars (2015) 61|''Star Wars'' 61]].<ref name="Star Wars 61">[[Star Wars (2015) 61|''Star Wars'' (2015) 61]]</ref> On [[March 18]], [[2021]], [[StarWars.com]] announced that ''Star Wars'' comic books released by [[Marvel Comics]] in [[June]] 2021 would have Pride Month variant covers that feature LGBTQIA+ artists paired with LGBTQIA+ characters.<ref name="SW.com Marvel Celebrates">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-pride-month-covers|text=Marvel Celebrates Sana Starros and More in ''Star Wars'' Pride Month Comics Covers this June}}</ref> As one of the prominent LGBTQIA+ characters in the ''Star Wars'' canon, Aphra was featured on the [[War of the Bounty Hunters 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 1]] variant cover by [[Babs Tarr]].<ref name="SW.com Pride Month Covers">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-pride-month-covers-exclusive|text=Check Out All of Marvel's ''Star Wars'' Pride Month Covers - Exclusive}}</ref>
