'''Demiloch''' was a terrestrial astronomical object<ref name="Twilight" /> located in an [[Demiloch system|eponymous star system]] in [[Lannik Space]] within an intersecting portion of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Systems" /> and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Slice" /> Positioned at the coordinates R-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was connected by a [[Bothawui–Daalang hyperlane|hyperspace route]] to the astronomical objects [[Daalang]] and [[Lannik]].<ref name="Galaxy" /> Sometime by the [[Standard year|year]] [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|Demiloch's mention}}</ref> the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[61st Mobile Infantry]] held a recruitment drive on Demiloch.<ref name="Twilight" />

During the recruitment, an [[Unidentified Imperial spy (Demiloch)|Imperial spy]] who had pretended to be a recruit shot [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], the [[commanding officer]] of the [[company]]. Evon survived the shot and was unconscious for two [[Standard day|days]], eventually recovering. As a result of the incident, [[Lieutenant]] [[Sairgon]], Evon's second-in-command, cut the recruitment drive short. The incident on Demiloch was later brought up during Evon's [[funeral]]<ref name="Twilight" /> in 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> by the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldier]] and former [[bounty hunter]] [[Brand]].<ref name="Twilight" />

==Behind the scenes==
Demiloch was first mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Twilight" />
