{{Quote|I called it. Those outlaws are mountin' an attack!|Saponza warns his partners of the incoming hostiles|<ref name="Star Wars: Commander">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''</ref>}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], a group of outlaws working for [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] '''attacked''' a [[Saponza's homestead|homestead]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]], belonging to the [[mercenary]] [[Saponza]]. The homestead's [[autoturret]]s shot down all of the mercenaries, after which the [[majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]] contacted the [[Tatooine rebel cell|mercenaries]] and informed them Jabba was angry in what had become a dispute.

==Prelude==
{{Quote|There are lots of outlaws out here. We should build up our defences.|Saponza advises his partner to oversee the construction of a autoturret|<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />}}
At some point between the [[Battle of Yavin]] and the [[Battle of Hoth]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[mercenary]] [[Saponza]] returned after an extended period of absence to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] with his [[Saponza's partner|partner]] and a [[Tatooine rebel cell|mercenary group]]. The group established a new base of operations in a [[Saponza's homestead|homestead]] on the planet, where Saponza advised his partner to construct a new [[autoturret]], since the homestead only had one.<ref name="SWC" />

==The attack==
After the autoturret's construction was complete, six [[blaster]]-wielding outlaws serving the [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] arrived and attacked the homestead. The two autoturrets opened fire and swiftly gunned down all of the criminals.<ref name="SWC" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Those men worked for my master, the mighty Jabba the Hutt. He is most displeased with your hostility.|Bib Fortuna contacts the mercenaries following the battle|<ref name="Star Wars: Commander" />}}
Following the battle, Jabba's [[majordomo]] [[Bib Fortuna]] contacted the mercenaries via [[hologram]] and informed them that they had angered his master. Worried the powerful Hutt would mount another attack, the gang bought another [[Homestead droid|construction droid]] and built an alloy refinery and an alloy depot. They were then asked to aid the Empire in [[Attack on a Tusken Raider camp|attacking]] a [[Tusken Raider]] [[Unidentified Tusken Raider camp|camp]], and offered the opportunity to ally with the Imperials. The Alliance also contacted the mercenaries and asked for their aid in [[Mission to recover medical supplies|recovering]] medical supplies from a [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] [[Unidentified Cartel den|den]] on the planet [[Dandoran]], also earning them an offer of allegiance from the Rebels. While the mercenaries were off-world aiding the Alliance, Jabba's men destroyed their homestead in vengeance for the outlaws deaths. Realizing they couldn't face Jabba alone the gang then chose to accept<ref name="SWC" /> the Rebellions's offer.<ref name="Starwars.com">In [[Kekit]]'s Databank entry, it is stated that Saponza and his partner played an integral part in the campaign that liberated a Jawa clan from the Tusken Raiders. It also states that the plan was orchestrated by Jennica Pierce of the Rebel Alliance. This scenario does not occur if the player aligns with the Empire, so it can be deduced that Saponza's gang joined the Rebellion.</ref> The mercenary gang would later work with Jabba and Fortuna in order to obtain intel on the Empire; however, the arrangement eventually broke down and conflict between Saponza's men and the Hutt's errupted once more.<ref name="SWC5">''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', Chapter 5: Servant of Two Masters</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The attack first appeared in ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]'', a mobile strategy game released in [[2014]] by [[Disney Interactive]]. The attack acts as part of the game's tutorial and always has the same outcome. Shortly after the battle players must chose to either side with the Empire or the Alliance.
