'''Blevins''' was a [[captain]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> who was part of [[Tempest Force]].<ref name="Tempest Force" /> He fought and [[Death|died]] during the [[Battle of Endor|battle]] on the [[moon]] [[Endor]] against the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]<ref name="Aftermath" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> After the battle's conclusion, Blevins's colleague [[Sinjir Rath Velus]] found the captain's [[blood]]y-faced body among many other corpses in the moon's [[Tree|woods]]. As Velus had considered Blevins a bully and a braggart who had truly believed in the Empire's ideals, Velus was glad to see the captain among the fallen.<ref name="Aftermath" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blevins appeared as a corpse in a flashback in [[Aftermath (novel)|''Aftermath'']], a [[2015]] novel written by [[Chuck Wendig]] as the first installment in ''[[Star Wars: The Aftermath Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Aftermath" />
