The '''''Avandor''-class fighter/transport''', also known as the '''Guavian Ship''', was a model of [[starship]] that was used by the [[Guavian Death Gang]] during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]].

==Characteristics==
The ''Avandor''-class fighter/transport was a sleek and menacing starship that functioned as a [[scout ship]] and [[Starfighter|fighter]]. The Guavians painted their ships bright [[Color|red]]. Despite its sleek design, it could still adeptly hide in the safety of an [[asteroid field]], lurking in the shadows to survey oncoming threats. Then, long before they were spotted, a swarm of ships could launch a fierce attack against a much larger opponent.<ref name="Databank" /> Although the Guavian ships have trouble maneuvering around [[asteroid]]s at close range.<ref name="Kaz's Curse" />

==History==
One ''Avandor''-class ship landed on the planet [[Nevarro]] during the [[New Republic Era]] around [[9 ABY]].<ref name="The Apostate">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 17: The Apostate}}</ref>

One ''Avandor''-class vessel was present within<ref name="Barrier">{{SkeletonCrew|Way, Way Out Past the Barrier}}</ref> the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[shadowport]]<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> [[Borgo Prime]]<ref name="Barrier" /> around [[9 ABY]].<ref name="9 ABY Date">{{SkeletonCrewDate}}</ref> 

An Guavian ship was used to transport, five [[Guavian security soldier]]s with [[Bala-Tik]] and five [[Kanjiklub]] members lead by [[Tasu Leech]] to boarded [[Han Solo]]'s [[Baleen-class heavy freighter|''Baleen''-class heavy freighter]], the ''[[Eravana]]''. Aboard the freighter, Solo caught only the Guavian ship but assume it was only the Guavian Death Gang.<ref name="Awakens" />

A contingent of Guavian [[starship]]s fought in a [[skirmish in Guavian Death Space]] after the ''[[Colossus]]'' intruded on their territory, though most were destroyed due to the [[Ace Squadron]] using the asteroids to help maneuver around them, leaving only two ships before the station jumped into [[hyperspace]].<ref name="Kaz's Curse" />

In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="TROS Date">{{FilmDate|IX}}</ref> Guavian starships were part of a [[Citizens' Fleet|fleet]] assembled by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] to assist the Resistance during the [[Battle of Exegol|battle]] against the [[Sith Eternal military|Sith Eternal forces]] over the [[planet]] [[Exegol]].<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
