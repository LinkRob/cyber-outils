The '''Alderaanian crown jewels''',<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride" /> also known as the '''Crown Jewels of Alderaan''',<ref name="Halcyon Legacy 4">[[Halcyon Legacy 4|''Halcyon Legacy'' 4]]</ref> were objects of jewellery that were part of the [[Alderaanian monarchy|monarch]] of [[Alderaan]]'s regalia.<ref name="S&S" /> [[Brea Tonnika|Brea]] and [[Senni Tonnika]] once broke into the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]] to [[Thief|steal]] the jewels, but their attempted heist failed, forcing them to hide in a dumpster for two [[Standard day|nights]].<ref name="You Owe Me a Ride" />

Although the [[Galactic Empire]] [[Destruction of Alderaan|destroyed]] Alderaan<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref> elements of the crown jewels survived, inaccessibly locked aboard the [[Nau'ur-class yacht|''Nau'ur''-class yacht]] ''[[Memory]]''. At one point, the ''Memory'' was set to moor on [[Pantora]] for one night, hosting an exclusive charity ball for the Alderaanian diaspora. This, however, again presented an opportunity for the jewels to be stolen.<ref name="S&S" />
