'''Blue Five''' was the [[callsign]] of the fifth member of the [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] [[Blue Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Blue Squadron]].

{{Alias-stub}}

==History==
===Clone Wars===
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[clone trooper pilot]] "[[Tucker]]" flew as Blue Five during the [[Battle of Ryloth]]. Serving in [[Blue Squadron (Galactic Republic)|Blue Squadron]] and under command of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], Tucker flew his [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] blockade until he was [[Death|killed]] by [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]].<ref name="SOR" />

===Galactic Civil War===
[[File:Ten_Numb_-_SW_Card_Trader.png|thumb|left|200px|Ten Numb flew as Blue Five at the Battle of Endor.]]
[[Farns Monsbee]] flew as Blue Five on a [[Operation Fracture|mission]] to [[Eadu]] and soon after at the [[Battle of Scarif]].<ref name="Visual Guide">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Ten Numb]] flew a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel]] [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter|B-wing]] as Blue Five during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="SWCT" />

During the [[Battle of Jakku]], a member of the New Republic's [[77 Squadron]] (which flew as Blue Squadron in that battle) flew under the callsign.<ref name="Hyperspace Mountain" />

===First Order-Resistance War===
A [[Blue Five (Batuu)|pilot]] used the callsign Blue Five while escorting an [[Intersystem Transport Ship]] leaving [[Batuu]] carrying new recruits for the [[Resistance]], alongside [[Poe Dameron]] and another pilot "[[Red Two (Batuu)|Red Two]]." The transport was attacked by [[First Order]] [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] and "Blue Five" died when her fighter was destroyed.<ref name="Rise" />
