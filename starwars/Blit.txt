'''Blit''' was a member of the [[Nihil]], possessing the title of [[Strike]] in the [[Lourna Dee's Tempest|Tempest]] of [[Lourna Dee]]. She served under the [[Storm (rank)|Storm]] [[Zagyar]]. Zagyar and his crew, including Blit, were part of the Nihil force that [[Attack on the Garello convoy|attacked]] a [[Garello Technologies]] [[convoy]] escaping [[Ab Dalis]]. The Nihil intended to tear the [[freighter]]s apart to loot them, but the freighter that Zagyar's crew was assigned to was destroyed in an explosion when Blit miscalculated a [[harpoon]] shot and hit a [[fuel]] tank. Blit and most of Zagyar's crew were [[Death|killed]] in the resulting explosion.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
