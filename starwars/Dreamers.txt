The '''Dreamers''' were an [[Terrorist|extremist]] group consisting of surviving members of [[Saw Gerrera]]'s [[Partisans]] that were not present on [[Jedha]] when [[Jedha City]] was [[Destruction of Jedha City|destroyed]] by the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], [[Inferno Squad]] was sent to [[Dreamers Infiltration|infiltrate]] and eliminate the Dreamers.<ref name="Inferno Squad" />
