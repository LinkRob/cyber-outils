The '''barbed hookfish''' was a [[species]] of [[fish]] endemic to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]]. The species evolved out of necessity into a brutal predator. Unlike other fish in its taxonomy who fed on [[Krill (creature)|krill]] and water-based nutrients, the barbed hookfish developed a hard exoskeleton and hooked fins ideal for attacking other fish and even [[bird]]s.<ref name="Survivor" /> 

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Survivor}}
[[Skoova Stev]], a [[fisher]]man who resided on Koboh, caught a barbed hookfish in the smuggler's tunnels beneath [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]]<ref name="Survivor" /> in [[9 BBY]]<ref name="Date">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> and put it in [[Pyloon's Saloon]].<ref name="Survivor" />
{{EndGame}}
