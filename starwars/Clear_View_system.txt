The '''Clear View system''' was a [[star system]] located in [[Wild Space]]. It was located in grid square U-13 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the site of [[Clear View]], a colony of dissidents on a remote and virtually uninhabited [[planet]] who fled the wrath of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Savage" />
