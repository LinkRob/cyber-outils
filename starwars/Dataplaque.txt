'''Dataplaques''' were ancient information storage devices.<ref name="TOG" /> Made up of storage rods built into a capacitance board made of precious metal, they were notably used to contain [[astrogation]]al data, in which case they were called '''navigational dataplaques'''.<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" />

The [[gangster]] [[Dryden Vos]] kept three finely adorned dataplaques in his [[Dryden's study|personal museum]] onboard the [[Yacht|star yacht]] ''[[First Light]]''.<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> At least two of them were navigational dataplaques. [[Ancient navi-computer dataplaque|One]] allegedly pinpointed the location of the long-lost ''[[Queen of Ranroon]]'' ship.<ref name="TOG" /> The other navigational dataplaque, which he had stolen from the [[Bureau of Ships and Services]] [[Bureau of Ships and Services Heritage Museum|Heritage Museum]] on [[Coruscant]] and replaced with a forgery, was thought to have come from an old [[Jedi]] [[scout ship]], the ''[[Permondiri Explorer]]''.<ref name="Scum and Villainy Case Files" />

One form of dataplaque was used as records of sentencing on the [[planet]] [[Niamos]] during the [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=andor-s1-e7-trivia-gallery|text="Announcement" Trivia Gallery &#124; Andor}} {{C|Slide 6}}</ref>

Around [[34 ABY]], the [[Ithorian]] [[collector]] [[Dok-Ondar]] kept four dataplaques on display at his [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities|store]] on [[Batuu]], three of which appeared nearly identical to those that had belonged to Dryden Vos.<ref name="Galaxy'sEdge">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref>
