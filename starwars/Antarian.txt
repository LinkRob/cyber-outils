'''Antarian''' was a [[language]] spoken by the [[Gotal]] [[species]],<ref name="Language" /> which hailed from the [[moon]] [[Antar 4]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|gotal|Gotal}}</ref> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{FilmDate|IV|1|[[Cobb Sonbepol]]'s use of Antarian}}</ref> the Gotal [[Cobb Sonbepol]]<ref name="Tweet" /> spoke Antarian<ref name="Language" /> while conversing with the [[Ithorian]] [[Momaw Nadon]] and the [[Stennes Shifter]] [[Trinto Duaba]]<ref name="Complete locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> in [[Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina]] in [[Mos Eisley]] [[spaceport]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="ANH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Antarian first appeared in the [[1977]] [[original trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="ANH" /> It was first identified as Antarian in the video "Every Language in ''Star Wars''" which released in [[2019]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars By the Numbers]]'' series on the ''Star Wars'' Kids [[Wikipedia:YouTube|YouTube]] channel.<ref name="Language" /> The name was first used in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[1998]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="EGtPaM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref>
