The '''Chiss shuttle''', also known as a '''Chiss courier ship''', was a type of [[shuttle]] utilized by the [[Chiss]]. During a [[battle against Grysk forces]], a Chiss shuttle was present and escaped, although not before [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] saw it. After the battle, Vader confronted Thrawn about the shuttle and questioned his loyalty to the Empire.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" />
