'''Al Williamson''' was a comic-book illustrator. He worked on the ''[[Star Wars]]'' daily [[Star Wars (comic strip)|comic strips]], as well as the comic-book adaptations for [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''The Empire Strikes Back'']] and [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Return of the Jedi'']] published by [[Marvel Comics]]. He also did the inking for the [[Dark Horse Comics]] adaptation of [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''The Phantom Menace'']].
