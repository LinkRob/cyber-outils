'''Ben Teene''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[pilot]]<ref name="TLJ" /> of the [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] ''[[Crimson Bolide]]''<ref name="XWing" /> during the [[Battle of D'Qar|Evacuation of D'Qar]]. He spotted incoming [[First Order]] [[starfighter]]s approaching the [[Resistance]] Bomber [[squadron]]s and advised the rest of the unit. He was subsequently [[Death|killed]] along with the rest of the squadron during the attack on the [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought|''Mandator IV''-class Siege Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]''.<ref name="TLJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ben Teene was portrayed by [[Ben Morris]] in the [[2017]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]. The character was identified only as "Resistance Bomber Pilot Teene" in the film's credits, although "BEN" is visibly written in [[Aurebesh]] on the side of his helmet in the film.<ref name="TLJ" /> The ''[[Resistance Conversion Kit]]'', a [[2018]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: X-Wing Second Edition]]'', then confirmed Ben to also be the character's first name.<ref name="XWing" />
