'''Andelm beetles''' were a [[species]] of [[beetle]] native to [[Andelm IV]]. Their larvae contained certain rare organics that could be used to power [[blaster]]s.<ref name="Chewbacca II" /> Andelm beetles traveled in a swarm and were dangerous to sentient beings in large numbers.<ref name="Chewbacca III">[[Chewbacca (2015) 3|''Chewbacca'' (2015) 3]]</ref> Following the [[Battle of Yavin]], the crime lord [[Jaum]] ran a [[mining]] operation at the [[Andelm beetle caverns]]. He used debtors as [[Slavery|slaves]] to work his mines.<ref name="Chewbacca II" />
