A '''musical motif''' composed by [[John Williams]] and performed by the [[London Symphony Orchestra]] first appeared in the [[1980]] film [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']]. The motif is played as an [[Wikipedia:Ostinato|ostinato]], with the [[Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back (soundtrack)|film score's]] "To Hyperspace" track featuring the motif prominently. The same track was incorporated into the track "Snowspeeder Rescue," which plays in the film when [[Rogue Squadron]], led by [[Wedge Antilles]], search for [[Han Solo]] and [[Commander]] [[Luke Skywalker]] with modified [[T-47 airspeeder]]s on [[Hoth]]. Later in the film, the [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] ostinato is played as [[Luke Skywalker]] hangs from a [[weather scanner vane]] of [[Cloud City]] and as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' escapes from the [[city]] and the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Death Squadron]] on [[Bespin]].<ref name="ESB" />

A similar [[Wikipedia:Figure (music)|musical figure]] is played in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], as Luke Skywalker races towards his burning [[Lars Homestead|homestead]].<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> A rendition of the dark side ostinato appears in the [[Star Wars Episode VI: Return of the Jedi (soundtrack)|soundtrack]] to [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']], with the music being played at the start of the concert suite "[[The Forest Battle]],"<ref name="Returns" /> which refers to the [[Ewok]] ground battles of the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Return">{{Film|VI}}</ref> Every two intances of the motif is followed by a transition to the [[Wikipedia:Dies irae|dies irae]] [[Wikipedia:Leitmotif|leitmotif]].<ref name="Returns" />

[[File:Scraplands-MPQ.png|thumb|left|180px|The Episode V motif is played during the [[Train/Legends|train]] level in ''Shadows of the Empire''.]]
The dark side motif in "The Forest Battle" was adapted by [[Paul Webb]] for being played in the [[Endor/Legends|Endor]] levels of the [[1995]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Super Star Wars: Return of the Jedi]]''.<ref name="ReturnSuper" /> The ostinato is also played during levels&mdash;particularly [[Snowspeeder/Legends|snowspeeder]] levels&mdash;of the [[Star Wars: Rogue Squadron (video game)|''Star Wars: Rogue Squadron'']], composed by [[Chris Hülsbeck]] and released in [[1998]]. The tracks "TIE Fighter Attack" and "Save the city" in the game's Concert Hall feature contain the motif, with the track "Snowspeeder theme" being composed of the ostinato.<ref name="RogueS" /> The [[1996]] video game [[Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire (video game)|''Star Wars: Shadows of the Empire'']] makes use of the "To Hyperspace" track for the [[Hovertrain/Legends|hovertrain]] [[Mission to Ord Mantell (Dash Rendar)|level]] on [[Ord Mantell/Legends|Ord Mantell]].<ref name="SOTE" />

While ''The Empire Strikes Back''{{'s}} "To Hyperspace" is included in ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]'', a [[2002]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] tie-in to the film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']],<ref name="BH" /> the ''Return of the Jedi'' ostinato is played in the beginning of the Seduction TV Spot for the [[2005]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], where [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]] seduces [[Anakin Skywalker]] to the dark side of [[the Force]].<ref name="Seduction" />

The dark side ostinato reappears in the track "[[Rogue One (theme)|Rogue One]]" of the [[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (soundtrack)|soundtrack]] to<ref name="RogueOs">[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story (soundtrack)|''Rogue One: A Star Wars Story'' soundtrack]]</ref> ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]'', a [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|anthology]] film composed by [[Michael Giacchino]]. In the film, the track accompanies the sequence of rebels readying to [[Battle of Scarif|sacrifice]] themselves on the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Scarif]].<ref name="RogueO" /> The dark side motif is played twice during the [[Battle of Crait]] sequence in the [[2017]] [[sequel trilogy]] [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']]. It is featured in the track "The Battle of Crait" of the accompanying [[Star Wars: The Last Jedi (soundtrack)|soundtrack]].<ref name="TLJ" />

The Episode V "To Hyperspace" track is repurposed for the [[Resistance]]'s [[Battle of Batuu|escape from the ''Finalizer'']] in the [[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|''Rise of the Resistance'']] ride, which opened in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[2019]].<ref name="RoR" /> The [[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker (soundtrack)|score]] of the 2019 sequel film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']] also contains the Episode V dark side ostinato by tracking from "To Hyperspace."<ref name="TROS" />

The dark side motif in ''The Rise of Skywalker'' is played as the ''Millennium Falcon'' hyperspace jumps through [[the galaxy]], where the ostinato is interspersed with dies irae and overlayed with "[[March of the Resistance]]" and the [[Rebel Fanfare]], which transitions to the new [[heroics motif]] before returning to the [[Resistance]] motif. The "To Hyperspace" track then plays alongside the Rebel Fanfare before the music ends with<ref name="TROS" /> the climatic music to the [[Battle of Yavin]] in 1977's ''A New Hope''.<ref name="ANH" />
