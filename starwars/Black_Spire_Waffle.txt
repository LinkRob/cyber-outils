'''Black Spire Waffle''' was a [[foodstuff|food]] sold at [[Kat Saka's Kettle]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Kat" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> The snack was suitable for [[human]] consumption.<ref name="Kat" />
