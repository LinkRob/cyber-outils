The '''''Alpha''-class Xg-1 Star Wing''' was an Imperial<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> [[starship]] model in service during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. During the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] defeat in the [[skirmish in the Recluse's Nebula]], in which the Alliance suffered a net loss of [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] assets, one stolen Imperial Star Wing was lost among others with a loss of 125,000 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. The lost Star Wing, along with other lost assets, were listed in an official report filed by [[General]] [[Baccam Grafis]] of [[Ordnance and Supply]] to [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]].<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>

The report to Mothma would be included in ''[[The Rebel Files]]'', a central data repository that contained hard copies of sensitive information. ''The Rebel Files'' were eventually uncovered<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in [[34 ABY]]<ref name="Date">Since ''[[The Rebel Files]]'' were buried on the same [[Standard day|day]] as the [[Battle of Endor]], which according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' takes place in [[4 ABY]], and because ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'' establishes that ''The Rebel Files'' were rediscovered 30 [[Standard year|years]] after the battle, it can be determined that ''The Rebel Files'' were uncovered in [[34 ABY]].</ref> in the ruins of the [[Tak-Beam complex]] on the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] [[planet]] of [[Durkteel]], and eventually found its way to General [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Rebel Files" />
