"'''517'''" was an individual who served the [[Galactic Empire]] aboard its first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] [[battle station]]. Even as [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo]] were infiltrating the station to try and [[Rescue of Princess Leia|free]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] from [[Detention Block AA-23]], "517" was asked to report to scanner control.<ref name="Episode IV" />

==Behind the scenes==
The first and only mention of "517" was in the [[1977]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="Episode IV" />
