The '''224th Imperial Armored Division''', previously the '''224th Clone Division''' or '''224th Division''' under the [[Galactic Republic]], was an [[Imperial Army]] unit in service during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During its service within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], the 224th participated in the [[Clone Wars]], with its [[Mud Jumpers]] fighting in the [[Battle of Mimban]] alongside the local [[Mimbanese]]. With the rise of the Empire, the 224th was reorganized and fought against their former allies during the [[Mimban Campaign]].

==Description==
===Republic service===
The 224th Imperial Armored Division was previously designated<ref name="TOG" /> as the 224th Clone Division<ref name="BC 51">{{BustCollectionCite|51|Character}}</ref> and 224th Division while under the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. The [[division]] consisted of [[clone trooper]]s known as the [[Mud Jumpers]].<ref name="TOG" />

===Imperial service===
{{Quote|How do we fight? Trenches and TIES!|A slogan chanted by 224th-assigned pilots|<ref name="Squadrons">''[[Star Wars: Squadrons]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Mimban_Stormtroopers_SWI181.png|thumb|left|220px|The 224th made use of wet-weather gear stormtroopers on Mimban.]]
Reorganized as the 224th Imperial Armored Division,<ref name="TOG" /> the division gained an infamous reputation<ref name="Squadrons" /> and fell under the command of [[General]] [[Ire Falk]],<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> with their overall operations directed by [[Moff]] [[Bin Essada]] along with other Imperial resources on [[Mimban]]. [[Major]] [[Staz]] served in the 224th,<ref name="TOG" /> as did [[Captain]] [[Alayus Bolandin]] following his promotion from [[lieutenant]].<ref name="Battles" /> Another captain served in the division, whose uniform was stolen by [[Tobias Beckett]] during his infiltration.<ref name="Solo" /> The division prided itself on its use of [[trench warfare]],<ref name="Squadrons" /> even if the actual soldiers who fought in the trenches hated their deployment conditions<ref name="Solo" /> or suffered under them.<ref name="TOG" />

Division ground personnel included [[Imperial swamp trooper]]s, a subset of [[Imperial Army trooper]]s, and [[wet-weather gear stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="TOG" /> Also part of the division were [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot]]s<ref name="Squadrons" /> and [[medic]]s. In line with Imperial interpretations of medical oaths, the division's medics only treated [[human]]s and had a reputation for firing upon Mimbanese natives during the Mimban campaign. On Mimban, the division was headquartered at [[Imperial Forward Field Operations]], with its [[Camp Forward airfield|airfield]] receiving shipments from [[Y-45 armored transport hauler]]s and supporting [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s. [[All Terrain Defense Turret]]s were deployed by the 224th as mobile [[artillery]] units, with the support of swamp troopers.<ref name="TOG" />

==History==
===Clone Wars===
During the [[Clone Wars]], the unit was known as the 224th Division<ref name="TOG" /> of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. A detachment of the 224th known as the [[Mud Jumpers]] fought on [[Mimban]] alongside the [[501st Legion]], under [[Jedi General]] [[Laan Tik]]. During the fight, General Tik was killed and the [[Clone trooper|clones]] were forced to fall back.<ref name="501st Plus One">{{StoryCite|story=501 Plus One|book=Age of Republic Special 1}}</ref>

While in combat on [[Mimban]], the 224th's Mud Jumpers were given a song by a [[Hologram VJ]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic broadcast|Grand Army of the Republic's broadcast]].<ref name="Rookies" /> The 224th Division fought alongside [[Iasento's tribe]] against [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] [[Confederacy military|forces]], which had invaded the planet to exploit its mineral resources.<ref name="TOG" />

===Galactic Empire===
[[File:BustCollection51-Army-grunts.png|thumb|right|300px|Soldiers of the 224th in the trenches of Mimban]]
When the Republic reorganized into the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]], the planet once again experienced an incursion from an external enemy: their former allies. The Imperials returned to the once-allied planet as conquerors, seeking to procure the abundant mineral resources that existed on the swampy world. By [[10 BBY]], the 224th Imperial Armored Division consisted of swamp troopers who had been conscripted from local planetary forces and integrated into the [[Imperial Army]]. While these were gradually being replaced with stormtroopers, regular army and stormtrooper personnel fought together. The 224th Armored Division took part in trench warfare and skirmishes against the [[Mimbanese]].<ref name="TOG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 224th was first mentioned in "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]]," the fifth episode of the [[canon]] animated series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]],<ref name="Rookies" /> which originally aired on [[October 24]], [[2008]].<ref name="Rookies air date">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode005.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rookies}}</ref> It was later depicted as an Imperial military unit in the [[2018]] film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="TOG" />
