The '''Carannian hills'''<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> were a group of hills on the [[planet]] [[Serenno]] located outside the [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Carannia]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> By [[42 BBY]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>, a lookout was established in view of Carannia. In 42 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]], [[Jenza]], [[Dooku]]'s sister took [[Jedi Master]]s Dooku, [[Lene Kostana]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]] to the lookout and showed them Carannia from a distance<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" /> which was burning.<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost script" /> This angered Dooku leading to him planning an assault on an Abyssin Outpost holding a comm tower that blocked transmissions during the [[Incident on Serenno]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost" />
