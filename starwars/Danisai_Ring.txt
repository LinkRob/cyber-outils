The '''Danisai Ring''' was the middle of the three concentric districts of [[Salis D'aar/Legends|Salis D'aar]], [[Capital/Legends|capital]] of the [[Wild Space/Legends|Wild Space]] [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Bakura/Legends|Bakura]]. The district's [[Color/Legends|yellow]] lights gave it the name "'''Yellow District'''." The Ring was primarily used for residential and commercial purposes, including the [[Tiarest University]].<ref name="GatORW" />
