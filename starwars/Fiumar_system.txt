The '''Fiumar system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Quanta sector]] of the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square M-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia" /> the [[Fiumarian]] [[species]].<ref name="Free Fall" />
