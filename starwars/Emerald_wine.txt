'''Emerald wine''' was a beverage made from the juice of [[emerald grape]]s. It was notably produced on the [[planet]]s of [[Alderaan]], [[Naboo]], and [[Lothal]]. During the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Captain]] [[Hiram Zataire]] served [[Imperial Security Bureau|ISB]] [[Agent]] [[Alexsandr Kallus]] emerald wine from Alderaan aboard the ''[[Lawbringer]]''<ref name="The Rebellion Begins" />
