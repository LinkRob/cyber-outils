'''Bolivar Denai'''<ref name="DI3.0">''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]''</ref> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who spent time in [[Niima Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]]. At this time, he was amongst a group who gathered to watch the [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] fight off a pair of [[Unkar's thugs|thugs]] that had been sent by the [[junk dealer]] [[Unkar Plutt]] to capture the [[astromech droid]] [[BB-8]]. Rey successfully defeated her attackers with her [[quarterstaff]], causing them to flee and the crowd of watchers to disperse. Shortly after the fight ended, the [[First Order]] [[Escape from Jakku|attacked]] Niima in search of BB-8. While in Niima, Denai wore [[Color|brown]] [[clothing]] and a red hat with a large peak.<ref name="TFA" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bolivar Denai first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']], which was released in [[2015]]. The actor playing the character was uncredited and the character did not receive a name in the film,<ref name="TFA" /> but was identified by the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Disney Infinity 3.0]]'', which was released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="DI3.0" /> Despite the game being non-[[canon]], story group member [[Pablo Hidalgo]] confirmed on [[Wikipedia:Twitter|Twitter]] that the names provided by the game for previously unidentified characters, including Denai, were considered canon.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/749616406245699584|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|date=2016-07-03|quote=@LelalMekha Yeah, same with Infinity.|archivefile=File:PabloHidalgo-LegoName-Twitter.png|nolive=1}} {{C|In response to: "@pablohidalgo <nowiki>youtube.com/watch?v=JwxAeHPLpPg</nowiki> The Lego game identifies a few BG characters from TFA, but are those names canonical?"}}</ref>
