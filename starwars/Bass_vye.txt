A '''bass vye''' was a type of musical instrument. In the [[cockpit]] of her [[A/SF-01 B-wing starfighter]], the [[Theelin]] [[pilot]] [[Chass na Chadic]] sometimes listened to a song about agony and revolution played on bass vye and [[synthtone]] with singing by a [[Herglic]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />

==Behind the scenes==
A bass vye first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[2019]] novel [[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']], which was written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" /> The instrument originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it appeared in the short story "[[Rebel Bass]]," which was written by [[Kathy Tyers]] and published in [[Star Wars Gamer 6|''Star Wars Gamer'' 6]] in [[2001]].<ref name="Gamer">{{GamerCite|6|Rebel Bass}}</ref>
