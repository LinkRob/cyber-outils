The '''Firebird of Colsassa''' was a sculpture<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> that depicted a [[starbird]]<ref name="The High Republic 2" /> and dated back to the [[Third Cataclysm]].<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> By [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic (2022) 2|''The High Republic'' (2022) 2]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> it had been in a 2,304 collection of sacred art<ref name="The High Republic 2" /> kept by the [[Guardians of the Whills]] in the [[Temple of the Kyber]] on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]] for over a [[millennium]].<ref name="The High Republic 3" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> it was stolen from the Temple and ended up with the [[Condluran]] [[Briff]].<ref name="The High Republic 2" />
