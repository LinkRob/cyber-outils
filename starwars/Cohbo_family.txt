The '''Cohbo family''' was one of the [[Chiss family|families]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]], the government of the [[Chiss]] [[species]]. The Chiss [[Gender|male]] [[Bomarmo]] was a member of the family.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> At some point before [[18 BBY]],<ref name="LE Date">[[Cohbo'rik'ardok|Borika]] [[Marriage|married]] [[Bomarmo]] at some point prior to the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]'', which take place in [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Cohbo'rik'ardok|Irizi'rik'ardok]], a member of the [[Irizi family|Irizi ruling family]], [[Rematching|joined]] the Cohbo after [[Marriage|marrying]] Bomarmo. The couple settled on the [[planet]] [[Ool]], where they ran the [[Ardok Ranch]].<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
