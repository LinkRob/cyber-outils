'''Cam droids''', also called '''hovercams''' and '''holocam droids''', were [[repulsorlift]] [[droid]]s used as self-motivated recording devices. The proceedings of the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] were constantly recorded by such [[Senate hovercam droid|droids]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The [[Hologlide J57 cam droid|Hologlide J57]] was a model of cam droid used on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] to record the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[podracing]] event.<ref name="AbEverything">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Cam droids equipped with [[Holocamera|holocams]] were known as '''holocam droids''', and were used in the [[New Republic]] [[New Republic Senate|Senate]].<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref> Cam droids were used during [[grav-ball]] matches on [[Lothal]].<ref name="Edge of the Galaxy">''[[Servants of the Empire: Edge of the Galaxy]]''</ref>

During or after [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{GOCDate}}</ref> [[CAM]] was a cam droid who was a member of the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] alongside the [[Safari droid]] [[SF-R3]]. They traveled [[the galaxy]] in a [[Aree's ship|starship]] and learned about several [[species]] of wildlife. CAM broadcast footage of SF-R3's interactions with creatures<ref name="Porgs">{{GoC|Porgs}}</ref> across the [[HoloNet]] and sent their findings back to the Society.<ref name="Activity Sheet">[[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts Activity Journal]]</ref> Another cam droid affiliated with the Society was [[CAM-E]] who was stationed on the [[Youngling Care Space Station]] alongside the Safari droid [[M1-RE]], also known as Miree.<ref name="Learn to care">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-pals|text=Learn to Care for Creatures and Aliens in ''Star Wars Galactic Pals''}}</ref> CAM-E recorded Miree as she cared for and studied several species of [[youngling]]s, and CAM-E broadcasted footage<ref name="Wookiee">{{GalacticPals|Wookiee}}</ref> across the Holonet.<ref name="Activity Sheet" />
