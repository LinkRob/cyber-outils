'''Ashley Beck''' is a British an actor, stunt performer, martial artist and motion capture artist who portrayed the [[Knights of Ren|Knight of Ren]] [[Ushar]]<ref name="Twitter" /> in the [[2019]] film [[Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker|''Star Wars'': Episode IX ''The Rise of Skywalker'']]. He also served as a stunt double for the [[2018]] anthology film ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''.

Beck is a professional stunt performer who has trained all his life in different martial arts styles and acrobatics. He is a three times World Champion in forms and weapons, and an experienced motion capture artist. Between 2003 and 2011, he served as a motion capture artist 30 movies and video games. In 2011, he qualified for the British Stunt Register.<ref name="Official" />
