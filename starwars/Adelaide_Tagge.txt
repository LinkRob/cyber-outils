'''Adelaide Tagge''' was a [[human]] member of the [[House of Tagge]] who served as one of the [[Tagge Corporation]]'s two [[Head of Finance|Heads of Finance]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The other Head of Finance was [[Felix Tagge]], who referred to Adelaide as "dear." Adelaide was the cousin of [[Domina Tagge]], the leader of the Tagge Corporation.<ref name="WOTBH Boushh" /> Adelaide and Felix were the [[parent]]s of [[Ronen Tagge]].<ref name="Aphra 28" />
