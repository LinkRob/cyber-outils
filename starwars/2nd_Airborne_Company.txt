The '''2nd Airborne Company'''<ref name="Clone Paratrooper" /> was an elite<ref name="Sideshow" /> aerial [[infantry]] [[company]]<ref name="Clone Paratrooper" /> of the [[212th Attack Battalion]]<ref name="Sideshow" /> in the [[7th Sky Corps]]. The company consisted of [[clone paratrooper]]s and participated in the [[Clone Wars]], taking part in the [[Battle of Utapau]].<ref name="Episode III" /> The 2nd Airborne were considered a rapid deployment drop unit within the [[Grand Army of the Republic]], and like all [[clone trooper]]s, received elite training, although they were hardened by the battles fought against [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] [[General]] [[Grievous]]'s forces.<ref name="Clone Paratrooper" />
