'''''Bounty Hunters'' 18''' is the eighteenth issue of the [[canon]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series ''[[Star Wars: Bounty Hunters]]''. It was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Ramón F. Bachs|Ramon Bachs]] and [[Bryan Valenza]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[December 1]], [[2021]].

==Publisher's summary==
''THE GALAXY'S GREATEST! In the wake of the shocking events of ''WAR OF THE BOUNTY HUNTERS'', the underworld has become more dangerous than ever. T'ONGA has assembled the greatest team of bounty hunters in the galaxy &ndash; including BOSSK, ZUCKUS [sic] and TASU LEECH for a special mission! Can she keep them from killing each other long enough to become a real team while she grieves the loss of an old friend?''
