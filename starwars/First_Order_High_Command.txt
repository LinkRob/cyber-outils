'''First Order High Command''',<ref name="TIE Manual">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> also known as '''First Order Command''',<ref name="Robot Resistance">{{StoryCite|story=Robot Resistance|book=Age of Resistance Special 1}}</ref> was the [[First Order military|military]] leadership council of the [[First Order]]. [[General]] [[Armitage Hux]] was a member of the High Command. Twice a [[Standard day|day]], the High Command would broadcast recorded speeches, most often from General Hux himself, to their [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtroopers]] through [[holoprojector]]s detailing the deplorable conditions of the [[New Republic]] and the First Order's alleged many victories over the Republic.<ref name="BTA" />

The First Order High Command would shift into a new incarnation under the rule of [[Supreme Leader]] [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]], who formed the [[First Order Supreme Council]] as a new command organization.<ref name="TROS VD" />
