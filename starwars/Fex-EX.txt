'''Fex-EX''', or '''Blue Harvest''' as it was also known, was a highly concentrated derivative of the [[Fex-M3/Legends|Fex-M3]] nerve toxin. It was so powerful that contact with the skin was enough to cause a massive systemic overload that could shut down the body. The [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Assassin/Legends|assassin]] [[Cabe Maquez|Cabe]] purchased some in a bar called ''[[The Hold-Out]]'' and used it to kill the [[New Republic/Legends|New Republic]] [[Senator/Legends|Senator]] [[Luralon Odaay]] and later, the [[Prostitute/Legends|prostitute]] who had witnessed his [[Death/Legends|death]], [[Kandi]].

It had a sweet and exotic smell.
