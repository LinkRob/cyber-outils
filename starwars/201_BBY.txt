The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''201 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3076 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''182 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7776''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*On Fire, the [[Silver Knights]], led by [[Li Yu]], clashes with the Blue Dragon, led by its leader, the [[dragon head]]. Li lets the dragon head escape with his life in the belief that it would be more profitable for the head to be alive than dead.<ref name="Vow">''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]''</ref><ref name="Dragon head">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that the clash on [[Fire (city)|Fire]] involving the [[dragon head]] escaping with his life occurred one [[Standard year|year]] ago, the event must have taken place around [[201 BBY]].</ref>
*Nan Moyou and Nan Holi go on a short vacation at the [[Nan family villa]].<ref name="Nan vacation">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Nan Moyou]] and [[Nan Holi]] last went on a short vacation at the [[Nan family villa]] one [[Standard year|year]] ago, the vacation must have taken place around [[201 BBY]].</ref>
*Mostima and Sean study the Outer Rim's [[sector]]s, including [[Oplovis sector]]. In particular, the two Jedi study its Aakaash system.<ref name="Oplovis study">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that [[Mostima]] and [[Sean]] studied the [[Oplovis sector]] a [[Standard year|year]] ago, it can be calculated that they studied the [[sector]] around [[201 BBY]].</ref>
*The industrial output of the distant, loosely Republic-affiliated Aakaash system exceeds that of the famous industrial world [[Metalorn]].<ref name="Vow" /><ref name="Metalorn">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates ''[[The Vow of Silver Dawn]]'' to [[200 BBY]]. Since the webnovel states that the industrial output of the [[Aakaash system]] exceeded that of [[Metalorn]]'s a [[Standard year|year]] ago, it can be calculated that the event occurred around [[201 BBY]].</ref>
