The '''''Blue Range''''' was a [[Republic Defense Coalition]] [[transport]] in the service of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="TOTF" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''The High Republic: Temptation of the Force''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] and the [[pilot]] [[Friielan]] used the ''Blue Range'' and a [[Sunvale device]] to move through the [[Nihil]]'s [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier.<ref name="TOTF" /> The ship would later be used by the [[Ugnaught]] pilot [[Belin]] to bring the [[journalist]] [[Rhil Dairo]] to the ''[[Overtone]]'' for an exclusive interview with [[Lourna Dee]], former Nihil and current leader of the [[Tempest Breakers]].<ref name="TB">''[[The High Republic: Tempest Breaker]]''</ref>
