An '''arms dealer''', also known as a '''weapons dealer''', or as a '''weapons smuggler''', was an individual who sold weapons.<ref name="Wayseeker">''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]''</ref>

==History==
At the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Sugi]]s were reputed to be the premier arms dealers in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Sugi Databank">{{Databank|sugi-species|Sugi (Species)}}</ref> Those who specialized in weapons adapted to frigid environments were called sub-zero arms dealers.<ref name="SZAD">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Sub-Zero Arms Dealer"</ref> A [[Weequay]] criminal named [[Car Affa]] sold [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] arms to the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] until he was arrested by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]. <ref name="Lightsaber Lost">{{TCW|Lightsaber Lost}}</ref> A [[Rodian]] named [[Pablo]] sold weapons and armor in his [[Pablo's pawnshop|pawnshop]] on [[Nal Hutta]].<ref name="TCW: Friends and Enemies">{{TCW|Friends and Enemies}}</ref>

The [[Devaronian]] [[crime lord]] [[Cikatro Vizago]] sold stolen weapons like the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]]s, which were stolen b the [[Spectres]].<ref name="Spark of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]''</ref> 

The [[Aqualish]] weapon designer, [[Amda Wabo]] made a deal with the Empire to sell them a large supply of [[T-7 ion disruptor rifle]]s, despite the [[Imperial Senate]] banning their use. The Spectres stole the [[disruptor rifle]]s and sold them to Vizago but when the Empire track them, he only tool half of the [[disruptor]] shipment while the rest were overload and destroyed.<ref name="Distress">{{Rebels|Droids in Distress}}</ref>

[[Fetya]] was an arms dealer working with the crime syndicate known as the [[Hidden Hand]].<ref name="Target Vader 2">[[Target Vader 2|''Target Vader'' 2]]</ref>

The [[Gender|male]] [[Ortolan]] known as "[[the Dragon]]" was an infamous arms dealer who operated on the [[planet]] [[Anthan Prime]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Darth Vader 9">[[Darth Vader (2015) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 9]]</ref> 

[[Nien Nunb]] often operated as a independent [[pilot]], who worked as an arms dealer and [[smuggler]] for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]].<ref name="Leia 4">[[Princess Leia 4|''Princess Leia'' 4]]</ref> 

The pirate [[Lassa Rhayme]] and her [[Blood Bone Order]] became a food and weapons supplier for a subgroup of the mainly extinct [[Partisans]] known as the [[Dreamers]].<ref name="Inferno Squad">''[[Battlefront II: Inferno Squad]]''</ref> 

After stealing the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', [[Gannis Ducain]] became a [[Smuggler|gunrunner]], gaining help of a mysterious contact, whom he worked for until he met with his contact on the world of [[Vardos]].<ref name="Grand Theft Falcon">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=18|story=Flight of the Falcon, Part 5: Grand Theft Falcon}}</ref>

The [[Melitto]] [[ronin]], [[Sarco Plank]] became an arms dealer on [[Jakku]].<ref name="VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> 

The [[pirate]] [[captain]] [[Sidon Ithano]] worked as an arms dealer where he gave [[Kragan Gorr]], a sache of [[B2-series super battle droid]]s.<ref name="The Mutiny">{{Resistance|The Mutiny}}</ref>

[[Korfé Bennux-Ai]] was an arms dealer who sold weapons, [[walker]]s, and [[starship]]s like [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] to both bad guys and good. Many of his sales was on the [[databank]]s in his custom [[Yacht|Star Yacht]], the ''[[Libertine]]''.<ref name="Episode 8">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
