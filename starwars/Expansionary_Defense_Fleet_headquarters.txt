The '''Expansionary Defense Fleet headquarters''' where the headquarters of the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] on the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Naporar]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

Prior to his promotion to the rank of [[Supreme General]] of the [[Chiss Defense Force]], [[General]] [[Ba'kif]] had direct command over the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet from his office at the headquarters.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

As part of the ceremony to become a [[Patriarch]], the Patriarch's credentials must be presented at the headquarters.<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> In around [[118 BBY]], the credentials must be presented in person by the Patriarch, but by [[18 BBY]]<ref name="Date" /> it could be sent by aide instead.<ref name="Lesser Evil" /> In 18 BBY<ref name="Date" /> [[Mitth'iv'iklo|Thivik]] made a trip there to present [[Mitth'urf'ianico|Thurfian's]] credentials as a Patriarch.<ref name="Lesser Evil" />
