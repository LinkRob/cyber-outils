'''Cosian Space''' was a region within the [[Deep Core]] of [[the galaxy]] that contained a knot of [[star system]]s,<ref name="AotG" /> including the [[Cosia system]] but also the [[planet]] [[Cos Secundu]].<ref name="USW" /> It was the home of both the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Cosian]] [[species]] and the closely related [[Nu-Cosian]]s, who were genetically engineered offshoots of the Cosians.<ref name="AotG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cosian Space was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy]]'', a reference book written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="AotG" /> and released on [[August 23]], [[2016]].<ref name="Amazon">{{StudioFun|url=books/star-wars-aliens-of-the-galaxy-9780794437312|text=Star Wars: Aliens of the Galaxy}}</ref>
