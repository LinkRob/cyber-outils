The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''14 BBY''',<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3263 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7963''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] has [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] [[Hunt on Chandar's Folly|hunt]] him on [[Chandar's Folly]].<ref name="Vader 18">[[Darth Vader (2017) 18|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 18]]</ref>
*[[Fortress Vader]] begins construction on Mustafar.<ref name="Vader 22">[[Darth Vader (2017) 22|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 22]]</ref>
*[[Cal Kestis]] begins repairing his connection to [[the Force]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
*Kestis is given another message by Cordova which tells him to search for an [[astrium]], which can open the [[Bogano Vault]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
*Kestis is kidnapped by the [[Haxion Brood]] crime syndicate and forced to fight in a [[Haxion Fight Pit|gladiator ring]] until the ''Mantis'' crew arrives.<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
*While on [[Kashyyyk]], [[Tarfful]] sends Kestis to the [[Origin Tree]], where he finds a recording telling him that the astrium he needs is in [[Kujet]]'s [[Tomb of Kujet|tomb]] on [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
*While on Dathomir, Kestis meets the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
*Cal Kestis is promoted to [[Jedi Knight]].<ref name="Jedi Fallen Order" />
