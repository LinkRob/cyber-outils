Following the [[Battle of Kur]], the [[Nihil]] [[pirate]] organization '''attacked'''<ref name="TRS" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="AcolyteVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' &ndash; Based on corresponding data for the [[Salissia system]]</ref> location [[Salissia]]<ref name="TRS" /> at some point between [[232 BBY|232]] and [[231 BBY]]. In 231 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Senator]] [[Vaadu]] attended a [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] meeting concerning the upcoming [[Valo Republic Fair|Republic Fair]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Valo]]. He argued that the attack on Salissia was proof that the Nihil still posed a threat to the [[Republic Fair]] but was assured by [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] and the event's [[coordinator]], [[Samera Ra-oon]], that enough security would be present at the event to ensure its safety. Republic [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]] agreed with their assessment, continuing the meeting despite Vaadu's concerns.<ref name="TRS" />
