The '''2GS-91E20 system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]. According to the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it was in grid square M-11.<ref name="GMA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The 2GS-91E20 system was first mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix on [[StarWars.com]] listing star systems of [[the galaxy]].<ref name="GMA" /> It was published on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], the system was first mentioned in ''[[The Essential Atlas]]'', a guide containing [[planet]] profiles and star charts,<ref name="Essential Atlas">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref> which was released on [[August 18]], [[2009]].<ref name="Penguin">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/185066/the-essential-atlas-star-wars-by-daniel-wallace-and-jason-fry|text=The Essential Atlas: Star Wars}}</ref>
