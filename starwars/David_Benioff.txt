'''David Benioff''' is an [[Wikipedia:Emmy Award|Emmy Award]]&ndash;winning [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] [[Wikipedia:Television producer|television producer]] and [[Wikipedia:Screenwriter|screenwriter]] best known for creating the critically acclaimed television series [[Wikipedia:Game of Thrones|''Game of Thrones'']] with collaborator [[D.B. Weiss]]. Benioff and Weiss were developing an upcoming [[Untitled Star Wars film series|''Star Wars'' film series]], and the first installment was set to premiere in [[2022]].<ref name="2022-Verge">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theverge.com/2019/5/14/18623128/star-wars-movie-2022-game-of-thrones-benioff-weiss-bob-iger-rian-johnson-disney|text=The Next ''Star Wars'' Movie Hitting Theaters in 2022 Will Be from ''Game of Thrones''{{'}} Benioff and Weiss|accessdate=2019-05-19|author=Chaim Gartenberg|date=2019-05-14|work=[https://www.theverge.com The Verge]|archivedate=20190520043256}}</ref>

The collaborators would have co-produced and co-written the series, which would be separate from the episodic [[Star Wars saga|Skywalker saga]]. According to [[Lucasfilm]] president [[Kathleen Kennedy]], Benioff and Weiss were also working closely with writer-director [[Rian Johnson]], who is creating an upcoming [[Untitled Star Wars trilogy (Rian Johnson)|''Star Wars'' trilogy]].<ref name="Kennedy-Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://ew.com/movies/2019/04/13/lucasfilm-star-wars-movies-hiatus|text=Lucasfilm Putting the Star Wars Movies 'On Hiatus' After This Year|author=Breznican, Anthony|date=2019-04-13|work=[https://ew.com Entertainment Weekly]|quote=As [David Benioff and D.B. Weiss] finish ''Game of Thrones'', they're going to segue into ''Star Wars''. They're working very closely with Rian.|archivedate=20190503042236}}</ref> On [[October 28]], [[2019]], however, Benioff and Weiss exited the project due scheduling conflicts with [[Netflix]]<ref name="No Films?">{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2019/10/star-wars-setback-game-of-thrones-duo-david-benioff-d-b-weiss-exit-trilogy-1202771184|text='Star Wars' Setback: 'Game Of Thrones' Duo David Benioff & D.B. Weiss Exit Trilogy|author=Geoff Boucher|date=2019-10-28|archivedate=20200721195000}}</ref><ref name="Guardian Oct 2019">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theguardian.com/film/2019/oct/29/game-of-thrones-creators-benioff-and-weiss-drop-star-wars-movies-for-netflix|text=Game of Thrones creators Benioff and Weiss drop Star Wars movies for Netflix|date=2019-10-29|archivedate=20200617052509}}</ref><ref name="Verge 29 Oct 2019">{{WebCite|url=https://www.theverge.com/2019/10/29/20937689/benioff-weiss-star-wars-trilogy-cancelled-netflix|text=Game of Thrones showrunners quit Star Wars trilogy to work on Netflix projects|author=Sam Byford|date=2019-10-29|archivedate=20200610151817}}</ref>
