A '''cooking droid''', also called a '''kitchen droid''', a '''galley droid''' or '''cafeteria droid''', was a [[droid]] whose job was to aid [[Sentience|sentients]] in the kitchen with the making of [[Foodstuff|food]]. Notable models included the Solo family's [[BX-778]] a [[Class three droid|class 3]] [[Culinary septoid droid]].<ref name="Last Shot">''[[Last Shot]]''</ref> One was used in a cafeteria in [[Terpsichore Station]] on [[Parnassos]] as well as aboard the [[First Order]] [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Absolution]]''.<ref name="Phasma">[[Phasma (novel)|''Phasma'']]</ref> Some of these droids worked on the ''[[Buried Treasure]]''.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>
