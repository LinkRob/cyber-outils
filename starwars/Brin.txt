'''Brin''' was the hero of the [[holodrama]] ''[[The Octave Stairway]]''. The holo recounted the adventures of Brin, who along with his friends sought to return to his home, by ascending the fabled [[Octave Stairway]]. When she was a [[Youngling|child]], [[Jyn Erso]] gave her [[Parent|father]] [[Galen Walton Erso|Galen]] a picture of Brin using Galen's likeness to depict the hero. The drawing was a stairway spiraling down eight levels to a conclave area where Brin received the magical powers that would enable him to return home.<ref name="Catalyst" />
