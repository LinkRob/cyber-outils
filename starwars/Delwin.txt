'''Delwin''' was a [[Weequay]] who was an [[Elder (Path of the Open Hand)|Elder]] in the [[Path of the Open Hand]]<ref name="POD" /> in [[382 BBY]].<ref name="Path of Deceit date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> When [[Morton San Tekka]], the mediator for the [[Eiram–E'ronoh peace treaty]] was [[Death|killed]], the Path put forward Delwin as a replacement mediator, but was rejected. During the [[Battle of Jedha]], Delwin preached the ideas of the Path and was [[Death|killed]] by a [[blaster]] shot.<ref name="Battle of Jedha">''[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha]]''</ref>
