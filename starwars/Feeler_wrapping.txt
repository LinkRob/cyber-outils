A '''feeler wrapping''' was a type of [[clothing]] used to cover or protect a [[species]]'s feelers.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Merqaal]] [[Nix]] had a set of [[color|blue]] feeler wrappings while living on [[Canto Bight]] with his companion [[Kay Vess]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
During the same [[Standard year|year]], Vess found several other feeling wrappings for Nix during their travels. These included ones in black, brown, tan, green, orange and grey tail wrappings.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Feeler wrappings appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game feeler wrappings were one type of collectible accessory players could find and use to customize Nix's appearance.<ref name="Outlaws" /> [[Wikipedia:Concept_art|Concept art]] for various colored feeler wrappings were created by the Concept Artist [[Tatiana Vetrova]].<ref name="Vetrova ArtStation">{{ArtStation|subdomain=northernchild|url=projects/JrvWld|name=[[Tatiana Vetrova]]|text=Star Wars Outlaws - Nix in-game gear|date=2024-09-30|quote=Some of the Nix in-game gear concepts. Yes, that's me who's responsible for that fruit hat!}}</ref>
