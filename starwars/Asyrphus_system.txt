The '''Asyrphus system''' was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Perkell sector]] in grid square Q-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Asyrphus]], where the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]]'s [[61st Mobile Infantry]] was [[Massacre at Asyrphus|deployed]]<ref name="BTC" /> at some point between [[1 BBY]] and [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate|the mention of the [[massacre at Asyrphus]]}} In the novel, [[Hazram Namir]], who was present for the massacre, joined the [[61st Mobile Infantry]] shortly after the [[Battle of the Tower]], which occurs nineteen [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Clone Wars]] in the novel, and before the [[Battle of Yavin]]. Since ''Star Wars: Timelines'' places the end of the Clone Wars in [[19 BBY]], the massacre at [[Asyrphus]] must have occurred sometime between [[1 BBY]] and 3 ABY.</ref> A bombardment massacred many members of the 61st during its deployment.<ref name="BTC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Asyrphus [[Star system|system]] was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix released alongside the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been authored by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
