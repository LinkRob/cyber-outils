"'''30 Years of an Ewok Adventure!'''" is an article published in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2015|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2015]]. It features interviews with [[Warwick Davis]], [[Eric Walker]], and [[Aubree Miller]] about the filming of ''[[Caravan of Courage: An Ewok Adventure]]'' and ''[[Ewoks: The Battle for Endor]]''. It was released for the 30th anniversary of ''Caravan of Courage''.
