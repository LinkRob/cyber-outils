'''Donald F. Glut''' is a freelance writer and movie director. He wrote some [[Star Wars (1977)|''Marvel Star Wars'']] comics, as well as the [[Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back novelization'']].
