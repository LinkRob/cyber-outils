'''Farsin Kappehl'''<ref name="UVG" /> was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Rebel trooper|soldier]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Early rebellion against the Galactic Empire|early rebellion]] against the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Serving as a [[private]], Kappehl was one of several volunteers who joined an improvised squad of rebel fighters, codenamed "[[Rogue One]]," who took it upon themselves to [[Battle of Scarif|steal]] the [[Death Star plans|plans]] of the Imperial [[superweapon]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. While the mission was a success, Kappehl and the rest of his team lost their lives in the opening battle of the war.<ref name="Rogue One" />
