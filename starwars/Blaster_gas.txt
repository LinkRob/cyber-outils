'''Blaster gas''' was a chemically-enhanced<ref name="RITR" /> energy-rich fluid that was used in [[blaster]]s. Stored in cartridges<ref name="Cross" /> or ampoules,<ref name="ROVG">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref> blaster gas was converted to a [[plasma]]-based particle beam which was then shot against targets to pierce or melt them.<ref name="Cross">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref> Types of blaster gas included [[tibanna]],<ref name="Blasters and Firearms">{{EncyclopediaCite|Blasters and Ranged Weapons}}</ref> [[irolunn]],<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']]</ref>[[eleton]]<ref name="ROVG" /> and [[kesium gas]].<ref name="JoJ">''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]''</ref> More exotic blaster gases produced specialized effects, such as the [[armor]]-piercing [[sig]] -which forced the [[Galactic Republic]] to form the [[Bureau of Sig, Spice and Slavery]] to regulate its use.<ref name="Rise">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref>

The [[Imperial cadet]] [[Jai Kell]] once quipped that [[Sergeant]] [[Currahee]] lived on blaster gas and molten [[carbonite]].<ref name="RITR" />
