A special model of '''behavioral collar''' was used by the [[Galactic Empire]]. The collar was kept on [[lurca hound]]s under the Empire's control, and could be used&mdash;via the external control of a [[Unidentified Imperial labor droid|droid]]&mdash;to influence the behavior and movements of the hounds.<ref name="DB" /> A group of lurca hounds on the [[planet]] [[Wayland]] wore the collars<ref name="Confined">{{TBB|Confined}}</ref> during or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TBBDate|301}}</ref> which were controlled by the [[droid]] [[K-9X1]]. The hound [[Batcher]] was marked for termination after the [[Cloning|clone]] [[Omega]] domesticated her. To save Batcher, Omega freed her from the collar's control and released her into the wild.<ref name="Confined" />
