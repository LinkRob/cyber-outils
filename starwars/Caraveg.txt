'''Caraveg''' was a nightclub on [[Coruscant]]. [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Daneska Varbarós]] discreetly met with [[Sabé]] and [[Captain]] [[Tonra]] in Caraveg while [[Cordé]] was standing in for the [[Senator]] at a function being held that same night. Amidala then had a run-in with fellow Senator [[Rush Clovis]] at the club, for he had left the function early and gone to the nightclub, where he recognized her and assumed she had also left early.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />
