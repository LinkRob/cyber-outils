'''Bartokan V''' was a [[planet]] located in the [[Bartokan system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim's]] [[Tamarin sector]] that was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Bonbrak]] [[species]]. The [[Svor Clan]] that lived on Bartokan V revered the Bonbraks&mdash;known to them as "barat-karabak"&mdash;as [[Deity|gods]], and at some point requested that the [[Galactic Republic]] classify the species as a protected species, which it complied with.

In [[230 BBY]], the [[Shani]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]] told [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]] about the planet. [[228 BBY|Two years]] later, Bartokan V became part of the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]]' [[Occlusion Zone]] region of [[Realspace|space]] after the Nihil [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] its [[Stormwall]] [[hyperspace]] barrier. Later that [[Standard year|year]], the Occlusion Zone was dissolved after the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]].

==Description==
Bartokan V was a terrestrial [[planet]] that occupied the fifth [[orbit]]al position in<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> the [[Bartokan system]] within the [[Tamarin sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]. It was situated in grid square N-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" />

==History==
[[File:Bonbraks.jpg|thumb|right|210px|Bartokan V was the homeworld of the the Bonbrak species (members of the species depicted).]]
By [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Midnight Horizon]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> the [[Svor Clan]] who resided on Bartokan V requested the [[Galactic Republic]] classify the [[Bonbrak]]&mdash;a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the planet whom the clan knew as the "barat-karabak"&mdash;as a protected species. The Republic ultimately complied with the clan's request.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Shani]] [[Musician|singer]] [[Crukibolt Nart Adeen|Crufeela]] was to take part in the annual [[Finance Ball]] on the [[Core Worlds]] planet [[Corellia]]. As part of a plan to have Crufeela prevented from participating in the ball, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ram Jomaram]]&mdash;who was friends with several Bonbraks&mdash;purchased a barat-karabak to take to the Shani to [[Diet|eat]].<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

[[File:RamJomaramDB.png|thumb|left|240px|Ram Jomaram (pictured) was informed about Bartokan V by the singer Crufeela.]]
As he made his way to where he was to meet with Crufeela, Jomaram mentally noted that he had not heard of barat-karabaks before, believing that to be due to the fact that they were supposedly endangered, unaware that they were actually Bonbraks. After discovering he was actually taking a Bonbrak named [[Teetak]] to the Shani, the Padawan reluctantly carried on with his plan and met with Crufeela, who told him about the species' homeworld and the Svor Clan who lived there. Before the Shani could eat Teetak, however, she was arrested.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />

Later that year, the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] organization [[Deployment of the stormseeds|formed]] the [[Occlusion Zone]], a region of [[Realspace|space]] stranded behind a [[hyperspace]] barrier known as the [[Stormwall]].<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Expansion date">{{THRContent228BBY}}</ref> the Nihil [[Expansion of the Stormwall|expanded]] the Stormwall after a failed Republic and [[Jedi Order]] [[Assault on the Stormwall|assault]] on the barrier,<ref name="Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref> which resulted in Bartokan V falling within Nihil territory.<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> Later that year,<ref name="TrialsDate">{{TrialsoftheJediDate|23|the [[Nihil]]'s surrender}}</ref> the Nihil surrendered to the Republic following the [[Mission to Planet X (Jedi Order)|Stormwall's fall]], which dissolved the Occlusion Zone as a territory. The worlds of the Occlusion Zone,<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> which included Bartokan V,<ref name="Acolyte Visual Guide" /> were welcomed back into the Republic if they wished. However, some opted to retain their independence, while others remained under the control of individual Nihil [[warlord]]s.<ref name="Trials of the Jedi" />

==Inhabitants==
Bartokan V was the home planet of the sentient Bonbrak species, who spoke the [[Bonbreez]] [[language]]. The giant Svor Clan also resided on Bartokan V and worshipped the Bonbraks&mdash;whom they called the "barat-karabak"&mdash;as [[Deity|gods]]. At some point, the Svor Clan requested the Bonbraks be classified as a protected species, which the Galactic Republic agreed to do.<ref name="Midnight Horizon" />
