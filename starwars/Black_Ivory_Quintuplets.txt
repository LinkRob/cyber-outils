The '''Black Ivory Quintuplets''' were a group of criminals active during the [[Imperial Era]]. The group's members were [[Death|killed]] by the [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]], who chose not to immediately claim the [[bounty]] on them. After capturing IG-88 and discovering the bounties stored in his head, a group of criminals led by [[Kaluub Akjee]] attempted to claim the bounty on the Black Ivory Quintuplets, among several others.<ref name="The Long Game" />
