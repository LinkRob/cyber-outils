The '''Army Air Corps''', often abbreviated as '''AAC''', was the [[Starfighter Corps]] of the [[Imperial Army]]. It consisted of [[airspeeder]]s and [[TIE series]] [[starfighter]]s detached from the [[Imperial Starfighter Corps]] of the [[Imperial Navy]]. The AAC was primarily concerned in ground attack and air support missions to support [[Army]] operations.<ref name="Dawn" /> [[Imperial Starfighter Pilot|TIE fighter pilots]] who flew "[[List of phrases and slang#I|in the blue]]" for the Air Corps were nicknamed "[[List of phrases and slang#G|Groundhogs]]."<ref name="LCZA" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Army Air Corps debuted in "[[Last Call at the Zero Angle]]" in [[Star Wars Insider 156|''Star Wars Insider'' 156]], written by [[Jason Fry]], albeit identified as "Groundhogs".<ref name="LCZA" /> The Army Air Corps was first identified in ''[[Dawn of Rebellion]]'', a [[2018]] [[canon]] roleplaying supplementary sourcebook that is based on ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' and ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''. The book was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] and authored by [[Tim Huckelberry]], [[John Dunn]], [[Sterling Hershey]], [[Jonathan Julius]], [[Keith Kappel|Keith Ryan Kappel]], [[Jason Marker]], [[Tom Sorenson]], and [[James M. Spahn]].<ref name="Dawn" />
