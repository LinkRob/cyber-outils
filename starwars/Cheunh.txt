'''Cheunh''' was the main [[language]] spoken by the [[Chiss]].<ref name="Thrawn: Treason" /> The title of [[Sky-walker|ozyly-esehembo]] was a Cheunh word for [[Force-sensitive]] [[Chiss]] [[navigator]]s, which translated to [[Galactic Basic Standard]] as "sky-walker." [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], a Chiss, recounted his confusion when meeting [[Anakin Skywalker]] during the [[Clone Wars]] after he had asked [[Darth]] Vader to use [[the Force]] to navigate the ''[[Chimaera]]''.<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances">''[[Thrawn: Alliances]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Cheunh was first seen in the novel [[Thrawn: Alliances]] by [[Timothy Zahn]].<ref name="Thrawn: Alliances" /> It is later seen more prominently in the [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|Thrawn Ascendancy]] trilogy which follows the [[Chiss Ascendancy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
