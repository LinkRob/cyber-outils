'''Benji Samit''' is a [[Wikipedia:Screenwriter|screenwriter]] and [[Wikipedia:Film producer|producer]] who has worked on various ''[[Star Wars]]'' projects. Samit worked on the [[2024]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy]]''<ref name="Rebuild" /> as well as its [[2025]] sequel ''[[LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy - Pieces of the Past]]'' as both producer and screenwriter.<ref name="Announcement">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2025-lego-star-wars-rebuild-the-galaxy-pieces-of-the-past|text=''LEGO Star Wars: Rebuild the Galaxy: Pieces of the Past'' Will Feature LEGO BrickHeadz Ahsoka and&hellip; Jaxxon?!}}</ref> Along with ''Star Wars'', Samit is currently working on the upcoming film ''[[Wikipedia:Spaceballs 2|Spaceballs 2]]'', a sequel to the [[1987]] ''Star Wars'' parody ''[[Wikipedia:Spaceballs|Spaceballs]]''.<ref name="Instagram">{{Instagram|username=benjisamit|name=[[Benji Samit]]|quote=writer/producer - SPACEBALLS 2|archiveurl=https://archive.is/HQX41}}</ref>
