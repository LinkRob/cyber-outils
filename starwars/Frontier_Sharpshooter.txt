The '''Frontier Sharpshooter''' was the standard outfit owned by the [[Miraluka]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Diago Velaar]] that he wore for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Frontier Sharpshooter was a "Base" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Diago, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within the 0.17.0 update on [[July 18]], [[2023]], during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Frontier Slugthrower]]. This costume was unlocked by default when Diago became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
