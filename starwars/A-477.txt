{{Quote|As instructed, we have observed the Quarren Ultus at the Sabacc tables. Behavior analysis reveals that he is indeed a habitual cheater.|Data dispatch by A-477|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
'''A-477''' was a [[security droid]] that worked for the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]''<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> A-477 dispatched a report to the [[Starship|ship's]] database which detailed the cheating habits of a [[Quarren]] [[gambler]] named [[Ultus]]. The report noted that Ultus was a habitual cheater who [[Gambling|played]] with his chips to distract other players when he was palming a card with his other hand.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] [[Slicer|sliced]] into the ship's database and viewed A-477's report on Ultus. She later used this information to have Ultus removed from the [[Okala Five]] [[Kessel Sabacc]] tournament after she witnessed him cheating.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
A-477 was mentioned in a datapad entry in the "Wild Card" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[November 21]], [[2024]].<ref name="Wild Card released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/1Q4y5lMJwdUUt6qXzbWKPI/star-wars-outlaws-game-update-showcase-wrapup|text=Star Wars Outlaws Game Update Showcase Wrap-Up}}</ref> Viewing the report is an optional interaction in the expansion and rewards the player with the ability to call out Ultus's cheating during the tournament which results in his removal from the tournament.<ref name="Outlaws" />
