"'''Empire vs. Empire: Disloyalty'''" is a scenario for ''[[Legacy of the Force (Star Wars Miniatures)|Legacy of the Force]]'' miniatures written by [[Sterling Hershey]], that was added to [[Wizards.com]] on [[May 6]], [[2008]].

==Plot summary==
Early in the [[Second Imperial Civil War]], a group of soldiers loyal to Emperor [[Roan Fel]] are attempting to escape [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. [[One Sith]] troops are sent to destroy them.
