'''Flight regulations''' were required to be followed by [[CSA pilot|pilots]] that flew for the [[Corporate Sector Authority]]. CSA [[pilot]]s that broke flight regulations with an unapproved pass in a [[starship]] would eventually find themselves attached to a chain gang in a [[Mining|spice mine]].<ref name="SotS">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Flight regulations were mentioned in the [[2022]] novel ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' and its [[Shadow of the Sith (audiobook)|audiobook]], both authored by [[Adam Christopher]].<ref name="SotS" />
