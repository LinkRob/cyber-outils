The '''Aldera Alps''' were a chain of [[Water|snow]]-capped [[mountain]]s on the [[planet]] [[Alderaan]]. The [[city]] of [[Aldera]], which notably housed the [[Royal Palace of Alderaan]], was nestled between those mountains and a lake.<ref name="Dawn" /> In [[1 BBY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> the Alps were destroyed lost along with the rest of Alderaan when [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] used the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]] to [[Destruction of Alderaan|fire upon]] the planet.<ref name="A New Hope" />
