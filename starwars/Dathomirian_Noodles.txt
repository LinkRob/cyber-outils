{{Quote|This traditional Dathomirian noodle bowl has a burnin' broth that grows red like the planet's swampy surface. Add in a slab of rancor meat and a few Valon Clusters and you're in for a fiery feast that's simply magick.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Dathomirian Noodles''' was a spicy [[noodle]] bowl dish made on the [[Outer Rim Territories]] [[planet]] [[Dathomir]] by the [[Nightsisters]], a [[Dathomiri clan|coven]] of [[Homeworld|native]] [[Dathomirian]] [[witch]]es. Before they were [[Battle of Dathomir|wiped out]] during the [[Clone Wars]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> the witches brewed the noodles in their [[Ichor cauldron|cauldron]]. Alongside the [[Color|red]] noodles, the dish also included pink meat of the native [[rancor]]s, sliced yellow [[fruit]], small yellow [[grain]]s, larger green grains, and [[Valon Cluster]]s, all contained within a brown [[broth]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

After a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he made between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for Dathomirian Noodles in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which had been published<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> by 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" /> In the book, he described the noodles as "[[magick]],"<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in reference to the form of sorcery the Nightsisters were known to utilize.<ref name="TSWB">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> He believed it was spicier than another recipe, the [[Mustafar Hot Pot]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Dathomirian Noodles were mentioned in the [[2023]] canon cookbook ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', which was written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The book provided a recipe that consisted of [[Wikipedia:Water|water]], [[Wikipedia:Gochujang|gochujang]], red [[Wikipedia:Miso|miso]], [[Wikipedia:Instant noodles|instant ramen noodles]], [[Wikipedia:Eggs as food|egg]], [[Wikipedia:Maize|corn]], shelled [[Wikipedia:Edamame|edamame]], [[Wikipedia:Lime (fruit)|lime wedges]], [[Wikipedia:Spam (food)|Spam meat]], and green [[Wikipedia:Onion|onions]], as well as the book's ingredients for Valon Clusters.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
