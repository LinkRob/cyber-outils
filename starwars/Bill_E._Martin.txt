'''Bill E. Martin''' was a voice actor who was credited with voicing "[[Bith/Legends|Bith]] Merchant, [[Gungan/Legends|Gungan]] Guard, Fisherman, [[Lizard boy]], and [[Naboo/Legends|Naboo]] Soldier 2" in [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (video game)|''Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace video game'']].
