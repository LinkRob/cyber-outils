'''Antidotes''' were counteracting substances to [[poison]]s or viruses, typically injected through the skin. 

The root of the [[reeksa]] [[plant]] contained the antidote to the [[Blue Shadow Virus]].<ref name="Mystery of a Thousand Moons">{{TCW|Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref>

[[Rush Clovis]] forced [[Senator]] [[Lott Dod]] to hand over the antidote for the [[Manax root]] poison he gave to [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Senate Spy">{{TCW|Senate Spy}}</ref>

[[Dragonsnake]]s could poison their prey by biting them, and the [[Dragonsnake venom|venom]] could be fatal to [[human]]s unless a specific [[Dragonsnake venom antidote|antidote]] made from local [[plant]]s found on [[Nal Hutta]], was administered. During the [[New Republic Era]], [[Mandalorian]] [[Din Djarin]] was critically injured and rendered unconscious on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]] by [[Hutt's dragonsnake|a dragonsnake]] owned by the [[The Twins]]. Eventually, the [[Unidentified species (Grogu)|alien]] [[fisher]] [[Gatori]] created an antidote for Djarin's adoptive son, [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], to give him after learning from the [[Kyuzo]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Embo]] that Djarin had been infected by a dragonsnake. Gatori warned Gorgu that it may be too late to save Djarin, but the antidote worked, causing Djarin to awaken from his coma after Grogu fed it to him.<ref name="Mandalorian and Grogu">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>
