'''Armand Isard''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Director of COMPNOR|Director]] of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]] and member of the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military]]. Prior to his membership in the [[Galactic Empire]], he was the [[SBI Director|Director]] of the [[Senate Bureau of Intelligence]] as part of the preceding [[Galactic Republic]]. Isard was present at a meeting of the Joint Chiefs in [[14 BBY]], where he attempted to speak but was cut off by a [[junior officer]] signaling news of a [[Attack on Lucazec|rebel attack]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|However, within other holdings that did not exist on Coruscant there are records of figures like Grand Moff Tarkin communicating to COMPNOR. A good number of these communications, though bland in content, were addressed to Crueya Vandron, Armand Isard, and Kinman Doriana.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:COMPNOR_Recruitment-SW_Propaganda.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Armand Isard served as the Director of the Galactic Empire's COMPNOR (recruitment poster pictured).]]
Armand Isard was a [[Gender|male]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> who served in the [[Galactic Republic]] prior to and during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict between the Republic and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], and he eventually served as the [[SBI Director|director]] of the [[Senate Bureau of Intelligence]].<ref name="Sep" /> When the Republic [[Proclamation of the New Order|transitioned]] into the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> he<ref name="Den of Geek 2014 interview" /> became part of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine's]] inner circle. Isard served as the [[Director of COMPNOR|Director]] of the [[Commission for the Preservation of the New Order]]<ref name="Tarkin" /> and represented the commission as part of the [[Joint Chiefs of the Imperial Military]]. Transmissions from [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] to Isard were not located in the [[Imperial Archives]] on the Empire's [[homeworld]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Rise" /> Later that [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="mask-date">{{MOFdate}}</ref> when speaking with [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]] about his [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|search]] for evidence proving the [[Jedi Order]] had been framed for [[treason]] in the transitional period from the Republic to the Empire, [[Imperial Intelligence]] agent [[Haki Zeophrine]] considered that a successful acquisition of evidence would potentially cause Isard to disgracefully resign from his position.<ref name="Mask of Fear">''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]''</ref>

In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> when a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] operated by former Imperial Intelligence agent [[Berch Teller]] began to cause problems, Isard attended a meeting of the Joint Chiefs of the [[Imperial Military]] on Coruscant. During the meeting, Isard attempted to speak but was interrupted by a [[junior officer]] who reported a rebel [[attack on Lucazec]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> In<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'',<ref name="Rise" /> published in [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned Isard's role as the director of the Commission for the Preservation of the New Order and position among the Joint Chiefs. Despite finding the content within bland, Kin was able to cite transmissions between Tarkin and Isard and claimed that their absence from the Imperial Archives meant that they were some of the only COMPNOR records to have survived the purge of Imperial records. As COMPNOR's director, Kin believed that Isard inevitably served within the secretive COMPNOR entity known as the [[Select Committee]].<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Armand Isard first appeared in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[James Luceno]]'s [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']].<ref name="Tarkin" /> Luceno stated that he included Isard in ''Tarkin'' as he fit into a role that was part of the story Luceno was writing, hence his appearance rather than the invention of a new character.<ref name="Den of Geek 2014 interview" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Armand Isard/Legends|Armand Isard]] first appeared in the short story "[[Interlude at Darkknell]]," published in the [[1999]] anthology book ''[[Tales from the New Republic]]'' and written by [[Timothy Zahn]] and [[Michael A. Stackpole]].<ref name="IaD">{{StoryCite|story=Interlude at Darkknell|book=Tales from the New Republic}}</ref> He was first pictured in the twenty-fifth strip of ''[[Reversal of Fortune]]'', a series of webstrips<ref name="Fortune">''[[Reversal of Fortune]]''</ref> published on [[Hyperspace]] between October [[2004]] and June [[2005]].<ref name="Reversal Release Date">{{SWArchive|url=hyperspace/about/news/news20050729.html|text=Prepare to Take Evasive Action}}</ref> The strips were written by [[Paul Ens]] and illustrated by [[Tom Hodges]].<ref name="Fortune" />
