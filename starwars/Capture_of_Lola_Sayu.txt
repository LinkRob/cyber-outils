In the early stages of the [[Clone Wars]], sometime between [[22 BBY]] and [[20 BBY]], the [[planet]] [[Lola Sayu]] was '''captured''' by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. The Confederacy also took control over the [[Citadel]], a [[prison]] facility constructed by the [[Galactic Republic]] [[522 BBY|five-hundred years]] before the war's [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]], and populated the prison with a [[Citadel droid unit|unit]] of [[battle droid]]s under the [[Commanding officer|command]] of [[Phindian]] Separatist [[warden]], [[Commander]] [[Osi Sobeck]].

==Prelude==
For [[Standard century|centuries]] prior to the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]], fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the Republic maintained a form of governance over the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]] of [[Lola Sayu]]. In the Republic's governance,<ref name="BMF 75">{{BuildFalconCite|75|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> [[522 BBY|five-hundred years]]<ref name="Construction">{{SWE|locations|thecitadel|The Citadel}} states that the [[Citadel]] was constructed by the [[Galactic Republic]] five-hundred [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Clone Wars]]. As the war began in [[22 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'', it can be deduced that the Citadel was constructed in [[522 BBY]].</ref> before the war's inception, the democratic government constructed the [[Citadel]], a [[prison]] facility, on the planet's surface. However, sometime after the war's beginning,<ref name="BMF 75" /> between [[22 BBY]] and [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> which succeeded the formation of the Confederacy, Lola Sayu was placed deep within the confederate government's territory, which resulted in efforts to claim the planet early on in the conflict.<ref name="BMF 75" />

==The capture==
With the outbreak of the Clone Wars, which saw Lola Sayu located deep within the Confederacy of Independent Systems' territory,<ref name="BMF 75" /> the planet was taken under the confederate government's control. Along with their efforts to take the world, the Confederacy also aimed to claim governance over the Citadel for their uses.<ref name="Citadel" />

==Aftermath==
[[File:TheCitadel-TC.png|thumb|left|300px|The Confederacy took control of the Citadel in their capture of Lola Sayu.]]
Following the capture of both the planet and the prison, the Citadel was updated by the Confederacy with modern security measures so that Republic assaults could be repelled, while also establishing a planetary [[Lola Sayu defense fleet|blockade]]<ref name="BMF 75" /> of one [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] accompanied by two [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]]<ref name="The Citadel">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref> in Lola Sayu's [[orbit]].<ref name="BMF 75" /> Upon acquisition of the Citadel, the confederate government came to realize the prison's value in holding and interrogating members of the [[Jedi Order]], as well as other Republic [[Prisoner|captives]].<ref name="Citadel" /> Furthermore, the government appointed the [[Phindian]], [[Commander]] [[Osi Sobeck]], as the prison's [[warden]], who directed the facility with unending cruelty,<ref name="BMF 75" /> and was supplemented by a [[Citadel droid unit|unit]] of [[battle droid]]s that the Confederacy occupied the Citadel with.<ref name="Citadel" />

Sometime after its capture by the Confederacy, in [[20 BBY]],<ref name="20 BBY">According to [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], the events of "[[The Citadel (episode)|The Citadel]]" occur a year after a [[Battle of Murkhana|battle]] on [[Murkhana]], which itself took place nine [[Standard month|months]] after the [[First Battle of Geonosis]]. As a result, "The Citadel" takes place one year and nine months into the [[Clone Wars]]. Additionally, ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the events of "[[Ghosts of Mortis]]" and "[[Water War]]" to [[20 BBY]]. As this episode takes place between those two episodes according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, "The Citadel" and its events, including the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]] must also take place in 20 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> the planet served as the site of an [[Battle of Lola Sayu|engagement]] between both the Republic and Confederacy. When the Citadel had become the site of detainment for imprisoned allies, who had discovered vital information regarding the coordinates of the [[Nexus Route]], a [[hyperspace route]] that extended through both government's territories and could further either's war efforts, the Republic dispatched a rescue team to retrieve their allies<ref name="The Citadel" /> and return them to safety following a prolonged struggle on the world.<ref name="Citadel Rescue">{{TCW|Citadel Rescue}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The capture of Lola Sayu was first mentioned, albeit indirectly, in "[[Counterattack]],"<ref name="Counterattack" /> the nineteenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' television series, which aired on [[March 4]], [[2011]].<ref name="Counterattack Ep Guide">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode319.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Counterattack}}</ref>
