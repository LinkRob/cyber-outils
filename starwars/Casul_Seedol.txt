'''Casul Seedol''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Mikkian]] from the remote [[planet]] [[Lenahra]]. He was the brother of [[Audj Seedol]] and the son of [[Ampher]] and [[Chemi]]. After the [[death]]s of their parents and all of the adults who remained on Lenahra after abusing [[the Power]] stolen from the planet, Audj became the leader of her small community after the [[death]]s of all of the adults who remained on Lenahra after abusing [[the Force]]-like [[the Power|Power]] stolen from the planet and<ref name="Padawan" /> by [[41 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Padawan (novel)|''Padawan'']] to [[41 BBY]].</ref> he was one of the oldest of the twelve remaining residents of the planet.<ref name="Padawan" />

That year the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] [[Mission to Lenahra|arrived]] on Lenahra in search for information about the [[High Republic Era]] [[Wayseeker]] [[Orla Jareni]] who had seemingly visited the planet [[Standard century|centuries]] earlier. After Audj first distrusted the new arrival, they quickly accepted him into their [[Lenahran's home base ship|home]], after seeing that he wasn't a threat. Casul quickly found a liking to the young Jedi and began to befriend him and jokingly flirted with him, although he and Audj didn't like his questions about them and their ways. After Kenobi saw them hurting the planet by stealing it's the Power and tried to convince them to leave the planet in peace, he and his sister instead began to become antagonistic towards the Padawan and after further arguments with the group, Casul left their home base and went to their newly returned uncle [[Loegrib]].

After Audj saw Kenobi in harmony with the planet's seemingly hostile [[plant]]s and [[Species|animal]]s she had a change of heart and she and the other remaining members of their family returned their stolen pieces of the Power and assisted Kenobi in stopping Loegrib in stealing all of Lenahra's remaining power. During the confrontation of the group with Loegrib, he used Casul as a hostage and sent out his [[Loegrib's mining-turned-enforcer droid|enforcer droids]] before he descended down the [[great gash]] into the [[power cave]] to steal and consume a large amount of the power. Although Kenobi eventually managed to kill Loegrib, the thefts of the power had enraged Lenahra too much as that they could still hope to be able to live in peace with it and as such Casul and his family evacuated with the help of Loegrib's [[Loegrib's crew|former crew]].<ref name="Padawan" />
