The '''Beauchen system''' was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Vensori sector]] at the coordinates P-8 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Systems" /> It contained the terrestrial astronomical object [[Beauchen]], where the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] conducted a [[Raid on Beauchen|raid]]<ref name="Alphabet" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Date">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']] establishes that the [[raid on Beauchen]] took place within the between the [[Battle of Endor]] and the formation of [[Alphabet Squadron]], which are both dated to [[4 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Beauchen [[Star system|system]] was introduced into the current [[canon]] through "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix released alongside the article [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]]<ref name="Systems" />&mdash;both works having been authored by [[Jason Fry]] and published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Beauchen system/Legends|Beauchen system]] was first mentioned in Jason Fry and [[Daniel Wallace]]'s [[2009]] reference book ''[[The Essential Atlas]]''.<ref name="TEA">''[[The Essential Atlas]]''</ref>
