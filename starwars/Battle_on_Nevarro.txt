At some point by [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> a '''battle''' took place on<ref name="Chapter 7" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> [[Nevarro]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In the battle, [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Alliance Military|forces]] landed on the planet's surface and fought with [[Imperial Military|forces]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], making their way to the planet's [[Nevarro City|city]]. Due to the [[city]] being located deep within the terrain of Nevarro, which provided no viable cover, the rebels suffered heavy casualties. Thus, the Empire retained control of the planet until the [[Battle of Jakku|end]] of the war<ref name="Chapter 7" /> in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Atlas" /> though an [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|Imperial remnant]] operated on Nevarro after the war's end.<ref name="Chapter 7" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{MandoDate|7}}</ref> the [[Mandalorian]]<ref name="Chapter 7" /> [[Din Djarin]]<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> asked the former rebel [[shock trooper]] [[Carasynthia Dune|Cara Dune]] if she had ever been to Nevarro while the two were traveling to the planet. Dune detailed the history of the battle while revealing she had never visited the planet before.<ref name="Chapter 7" />
