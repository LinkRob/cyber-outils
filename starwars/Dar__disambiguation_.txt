===Mandalorian culture===
*'''''dar''''' meant "gone, temporary, or no longer" in the [[Mando'a/Legends|Mandalorian language]]. Various Mandalorian terms and names were derived from this:
**'''''[[Cuy'val Dar]]''''', a Mandalorian group whose name literally meant "those who no longer exist"
**''dar'buir'', a term in the Mandalorian language meaning "no longer a parent"
**''dar'jetii'', a term in the Mandalorian language meaning "no longer a Jedi"
**''dar'manda'', a term in the Mandalorian language meaning "no longer a Mandalorian"
***'''[[Darman Skirata]]''', a [[Clone commando/Legends|clone commando]] affectionately known as '''Dar''' whose name was taken from the term "dar'manda"
***The '''[[Dar'manda]]''', a group of ex-Mandalorian [[Smuggler/Legends|gun runners]] who formed one of the five ruling parties on [[Mek-Sha]] after the [[Huttbreaker's Rebellion]]
**''dar'tome'', a term in the Mandalorian language meaning "parted" as opposed to ''tome'' for "together"
**''dar'yaim'', a term in the Mandalorian language meaning [[Chaos/Legends|hell]] or a place one would want to forget
