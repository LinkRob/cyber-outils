'''Forcehold''' or alternatively spelt as '''Force Hold''' was a dimension beyond [[time]] and [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="Part One">{{PiecesOfThePast|Part One}}</ref> where pieces of past versions of the [[The galaxy|galaxy]] go to when they no longer fit the new version.<ref name="Part Two">{{PiecesOfThePast|Part Two}}</ref> Once Forcehold was destroyed by being forcefully fed a [[galaxy]] worth of matter it became a black abyss of destruction.<ref name="Part Four">{{PiecesOfThePast|Part Four}}</ref> Forcehold acted a prison for [[Bobarian Afol|Bobarian Afol's]] former master, [[Solitus]] for countless eons,<ref name="Part Two" /> however, through manipulating [[Sig Greebling]] and [[Darth Devastator]], Solitus freed himself by [[Rift control|opening a rift]]; a gateway between the galaxy and Forcehold.<ref name="Part One"/>
