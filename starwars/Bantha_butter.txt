'''Bantha butter''', or '''blue butter''', was a [[Color|blue]]-hued [[butter]] made with the [[blue milk]] produced by [[bantha]]s. The mellow butter tolerated [[Temperature|heating]] and was useful for cooking [[Foodstuff|foods]], such as [[dumpling]]s and [[bantha-butter pancake]]s, but the results could be bland in flavor. On [[Starlight Beacon]], it was served with [[yellow chime fruit preserve]] on [[seed bread]]. The [[human]] [[Tasu Leech]] suggested pairing sautéed [[Trandoshan]] [[liver]] with bantha butter.

==Description==
Bantha butter,<ref name="BWDHE" /> also known as blue butter, was made with [[blue milk]] produced by [[bantha]]s,<ref name="Galactic Baking" /> a type of creature that was [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Bantha DB">{{Databank|bantha|Bantha}}</ref> The [[milk]] gave bantha [[butter]] its [[Color|blue]] hue.<ref name="Galactic Baking" /> A common variety of butter, its mellow flavor and tolerance for being [[Temperature|heated]] made it useful for cooking.<ref name="BWDHE" />

==History==
===Republic and Imperial Eras===
[[File:Bantha-TMH-Photoroom.png|thumb|right|300px|Banthas produced the blue milk used to make bantha butter.]]
While the [[Starlight Beacon]] [[space station]] was in operation<ref name="OOTS">''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]''</ref> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date: OOTS">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Out of the Shadows]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> the [[Cooking droid|kitchen droids]] in its dining facility served [[Foodstuff|foods]] that included freshly baked [[seed bread]] with thick blue butter and [[yellow chime fruit preserve]]. During a visit to the station, the [[human]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] got that [[bread]].<ref name="OOTS" />

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date: FACPOV">"[[But What Does He Eat?]]" from ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' takes place concurrently with {{Film|V}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates the events of to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Devaronian]] [[Chef|executive chef]] [[Torro Sbazzle]] was asked by [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]], [[Baron Administrator]] of the [[gas]] [[mining]] colony [[Cloud City]] on the [[gas giant]] [[Bespin]], to prepare food while under occupation by the [[Galactic Empire]]. Calrissian's involuntary "guests" included the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], who Sbazzle presumed was human like most Imperials. When Sbazzle chose to make [[dumpling]]s, she considered using bantha butter to cut her midnight-hued [[Flour|pastry flour]] because it was less likely to burn than [[zaffa oil]] and had less flavor. As it might have been the last meal she ever cooked, she decided she would not [[Death|die]] for bland dumplings and chose the [[oil]] instead.<ref name="BWDHE" />

In the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date: BH16">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Bounty Hunters 16|''Bounty Hunters'' 16]] to [[3 ABY]].</ref> the [[Trandoshan]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Bossk'wassak'Cradossk]], known as Bossk, appeared to have frozen on the [[Water|ice]] planet [[Jekara]] before he was discovered by a [[T'onga's crew|group]] that included the human [[Fighting pit|pit]] [[Gladiator|fighter]] [[Tasu Leech]]. After Leech suggested to his companions that they abandon or kill Bossk, he asked if they had ever tasted sautéed Trandoshan [[liver]] with bantha butter, at which point the Trandoshan leapt up, put a [[knife]] to Leech's throat, and threatened to flay the human.<ref name="BH16" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Dune Sea Songs Date">The main story of "Dune Sea Songs of Salt and Moonlight" takes place during the [[Rescue of Han Solo]], which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref> the [[musician]] [[Jess]] [[Diet|ate]] pieces of [[haroun bread]] smeared with bantha butter for breakfast in the [[Boba Fett's Palace|palace]] of the [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="Dune Sea Songs">{{StoryCite|story=Dune Sea Songs of Salt and Moonlight|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>

===Later times===
{{Quote|In addition to serving as benign beasts of burden, they produce a distinctly blue milk that can be consumed directly or used as an ingredient in foods like butter, yogurt, or ice cream.|Ardis San Tekka, in her journal|<ref name="Bestiary">''[[Star Wars Bestiary, Vol. 1: Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref>}}
[[File:Halcyon-GalacticStarcruiserConceptArt.jpg|thumb|left|300px|Bantha butter was served aboard the ''Halcyon'' (pictured).]]
In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date: GE GS">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> aboard the ''[[Halcyon]]'', an [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|MPO-1400 ''Purgill''-class star cruiser]], spiraled discs of blue bantha butter were part of a pastry board available to cruise guests during breakfast service in the [[Crown of Corellia Dining Room]].<ref name="Crown">{{GSAttr|Crown of Corellia Dining Room}}</ref>

[[Bantha-butter pancake]]s were a flat food that the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] once compared with the result of a [[turlossus]] stepping on something with its gigantic [[foot]]. He witnessed that scenario on the [[moon]] [[Dhandu 3]] when two [[Pakiphantos]] members of [[Trunc Adurmush]]'s [[Trunc Adurmush's gang|gang]] got flattened by a turlossus.<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> At some point no earlier than [[35 ABY]],<ref name="BestiaryDate">{{BestiaryVol1Date}}</ref> [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]]' [[Director of Creature Cartography]], [[Ardis San Tekka]], mentioned bantha butter in her [[Ardis San Tekka's journal|journal]] within an entry about banthas.<ref name="Bestiary" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|When a bantha gives blue milk, you get blue butter.|Recipe for Bantha Blue Butter Sandwich Cookies|<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-baking-cookbook|text=Go on a Journey of Deliciousness with the ''Galactic Baking'' Cookbook}}</ref>}}
[[File:SWPiratesPriceFinal.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Cover art by [[Annie Wu]] for ''Pirate's Price'', in which bantha butter was first mentioned]]
Bantha butter was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Pirate's Price]]'', a [[2019]] novel by [[Lou Anders]].<ref name="Pirate's Price" /> It first appeared in "[[But What Does He Eat?]]," a short story by [[S.A. Chakraborty]] published in the [[2020]] anthology book ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]''.<ref name="BWDHE" /> On [[February 22]], [[2021]], [[StarWars.com]] previewed the Bantha Blue Butter Sandwich Cookies recipe from the cookbook ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Baking]]''; the recipe first identified blue butter as an alternative name. The cookbook, which [[Insight Editions]] published on [[May 4]] of that year,<ref name="SW.com" /> referred to the substance as both "Bantha butter" and "blue butter."<ref name="Galactic Baking" />

A StarWars.com preview of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] that was published on November 17, 2021 mentioned blue bantha butter as one of the foods inspired by ''Star Wars'' storytelling. The immersive experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]],<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and had its final voyage from [[September 28]] to [[September 30|30]], [[2023]].<ref name="StarcruiserClosure">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref> Guests could consume the butter during breakfast in the Crown of Corellia Dining Room.<ref name="Crown" />

In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Bantha butter/Legends|bantha butter]] was first mentioned in the short story "[[Soup's On: The Pipe Smoker's Tale]]," written by [[Jennifer Roberson]] and published in the [[1995]] anthology ''[[Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina]]''.<ref name="Soup's On">{{StoryCite|story=Soup's On: The Pipe Smoker's Tale|book=Tales from the Mos Eisley Cantina}}</ref> Unlike the canon version, the Legends version of bantha butter was described as [[Color/Legends|green]] in the [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] [[Legacy (2006) 4|''Legacy'' 4]], which was written by [[John Ostrander]] and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="Legacy 4">[[Legacy (2006) 4|''Legacy'' (2006) 4]]</ref> on [[October 11]], [[2006]].<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54722/star_wars_legacy_2006_4|text=Star Wars: Legacy (2006) #4}}</ref> In Legends, [[blue butter]] was first mentioned in the [[2002]] ''[[Star Wars: The New Jedi Order]]'' novel [[The New Jedi Order: Enemy Lines II: Rebel Stand|''Rebel Stand'']], which was written by [[Aaron Allston]] as the second book of the [[Enemy Lines duology|''Enemy Lines'' duology]],<ref name="Rebel Stand">''[[The New Jedi Order: Enemy Lines II: Rebel Stand]]''</ref> but it was not explicitly associated with [[Bantha/Legends|banthas]].<ref name="Rebel Stand" /><ref name="Exile">''[[Legacy of the Force: Exile]]''</ref><ref name="Mercy Kill">''[[X-Wing: Mercy Kill]]''</ref>
