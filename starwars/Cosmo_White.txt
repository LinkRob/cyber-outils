'''Cosmo White''' is a [[Wikipedia:Brighton|Brighton]]-based [[Wikipedia:United Kingdom|British]] freelance artist specializing in comics, illustration and character design.<ref name="Facebook ITW" /> He penciled the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' short comics "[[Never Far Behind]]"<ref name="Never Far Behind">{{SWRMCite|39|Never Far Behind}}</ref> and "[[A Trooper's Worth]],"<ref name="ATW">{{SWRACite|1|A Trooper's Worth}}</ref> as well as the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' comics "[[Fuel for the Fire (comic story)|Fuel for the Fire]]"<ref name="FFTF">{{SWResACite|4|Fuel for the Fire (comic story)}}</ref> and "[[The High Tower (comic story)|The High Tower]]."<ref name="THT">{{StoryCite|story=The High Tower (comic story)|book=Star Wars Fun & Action 1|bformatted=''Star Wars Fun & Action'' 1}}</ref> Additionally, he did the colors on "[[The Recruit - Part 1]]"<ref name="TR1">{{SWResACite|1|The Recruit - Part 1}}</ref> and "[[The Recruit - Part 2]]."<ref name="TR2">{{SWResACite|2|The Recruit - Part 2}}</ref>
