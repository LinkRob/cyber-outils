A '''decree of exile''' was issued to the [[Mandalorian]] [[Apostate (religion)|apostate]] [[Din Djarin]] that belonged to the<ref name="Chapter 17" /> [[Children of the Watch]]<ref name="Chapter 11">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 11: The Heiress}}</ref> and broke the [[Mandalorian Creed]]. After Djarin revealed that he removed his helmet, [[Paz Vizsla]] and [[The Armorer]] demanded him to leave the [[Mandalorian covert|covert]] on [[Glavis Ringworld]].<ref name="Return">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> Djarin later saved [[the Tribe]] from a [[Lake monster|Dinosaur turtle]], but still was not accepted by the tribe, and left the [[Mandalorian secret covert|covert]] on a desert astronomical object. According to Creed, the decree of exile can be lifted only after bathing in the [[Living Waters]] beneath the [[mines of Mandalore]].<ref name="Chapter 17" />
