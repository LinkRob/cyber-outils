'''Cephalosaurs''' were a type of creature with [[Limb|tentacle]]-like arms that could be found on the astronomical object [[Regnio III]]. During his youth, the [[Evereni]] [[Marchion Ro]] [[Hunting|hunted]] the creatures while on Regnio III.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon and its events span a few [[Standard month|months]]|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> while on a [[Unidentified desert planet (Marchion Ro)|desert planet]], Ro was reminded of the cephalosaurs he had hunted when he saw [[Color|blue-green]] [[Cactus|cacti]] that sprouted spindly [[bush]]es with long tentacle-like arms.<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cephalosaurs were mentioned in the [[2024]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Temptation of the Force" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref> The word "cephalosaur" is a combination of "cephalo-," meaning head,<ref name="Cephalo">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/cephalo|text=Cephalo- Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|Dictionary.com, LLC]]|quote=a combining form meaning "head," used in the formation of compound words|archivedate=20240716183347}}</ref> and "-saur," used in the Anglicized [[Wikipedia:Taxonomy (biology)|taxonomic]] names of certain extinct real-world [[Wikipedia:Reptile|reptiles]].<ref name="Saur">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/saur|text=-Saur Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|Dictionary.com, LLC]]|quote=a combining form used in the names of extinct reptiles, especially archosaurs, usually Anglicized forms of Latin taxonomic names|archivedate=20240716183409}}</ref>
