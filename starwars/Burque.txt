'''Burque''' was a [[human]] who served as a [[captain]] in the [[Imperial Navy]] by [[14 BBY]]. He [[Commanding officer|commanded]] the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Electrum (Venator-class)|''Electrum'']] while it was on loan from a [[Ryloth deepdock|deepdock]] at the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] to an Imperial facility named [[Sentinel Base]].

When Sentinel Base received a fake [[Distress signal|call for aid]] from the [[Space station|space outpost]] [[Rampart Station]], [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] realized it was a trick and ordered Burque to [[Microjump|take]] the ''Electrum'' to the edge of Sentinel Base's [[Sentinel Base system|star system]] instead of sending it to Rampart. This allowed the ''Electrum'' to quickly return when a [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]] controlled by a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] attacked Sentinel Base; however, Burque was not able to disable the rebel [[Starship|ship]] before it fled.

==Biography==
===Captain of the ''Electrum''===
{{Quote|Are you up to speed on what is occurring at Rampart Station, Captain Burque?''"<br />"''We are, sir. The ''Electrum'' is prepared to jump to Rampart on your command.|Wilhuff Tarkin and Burque|audio=Burque-Rampart.flac|<ref name="Trkn" />}}
[[File:Moff-Tarkin.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Burque took orders from Governor Wilhuff Tarkin.]]
Burque was a [[human]] who served the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy]] as [[captain]] of the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Electrum (Venator-class)|''Electrum'']].<ref name="Trkn" /> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Tarkin date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']] to [[14 BBY]].</ref> his [[Starship|vessel]] was on loan from a [[Ryloth deepdock|deepdock]] above the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]] to the [[Sentinel Base's flotilla|flotilla]] defending [[Sentinel Base]], an Imperial facility commanded by [[Governor]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] that was secretly overseeing the [[Project Stardust|construction]] of a [[superweapon]] known as the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]]. While his ship was stationed at the base, Tarkin and his [[Gender|men]] received a [[Distress signal|distress call]] from [[Rampart Station]], a [[space station]] that acted as a marshaling depot for ships carrying supplies for the [[Death Star]].<ref name="Trkn" />

Tarkin suspected that the feed from Rampart was a fake and ordered Burque via a [[holoprojector]] to keep the coordinates for a [[hyperspace]] jump to Rampart Station ready, but to only [[Microjump|send]] the ''Electrum'' to the edge of the [[Sentinel Base system|star system]] they were currently in and find concealment there if possible. Burque followed the orders and moved the ''Electrum'', after which a hostile [[Berch Teller's warship|warship]] constructed from a number of [[Clone Wars]]&ndash;era [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] vessels arrived in the [[Star system|system]], proving Tarkin's suspicions correct. The enemy vessel then released a number of [[droid tri-fighter]]s and [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droids]].<ref name="Trkn" />

===Skirmish at Sentinel Base===
{{Quote|I trust that this is the trouble you were expecting, Governor.''"<br />"''Actually, Captain, most of this is quite unexpected. Therefore, I hope you'll do your best to incapacitate the carrier rather than destroy it. No doubt we can glean something by interrogating the crew.''"<br />"''I'll be as gentle with it as I can, Governor.|Burque and Wilhuff Tarkin discuss the Sentinel Base attack|audio=Burque-Trouble.flac|<ref name="Trkn" />}}
Tarkin ordered Burque to bring the ''Electrum'' back to Sentinel Base to engage the vessel, as the [[Droid starfighter|starfighters]] were [[Attack on Sentinel Base|attacking]] the Imperial facility. The captain complied, jumping through hyperspace and receiving orders to disable the [[warship]] once he arrived. Burque sent out his vessel's [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter]]s to attack, but before the ''Electrum'' could fire a disabling shot, the warship fled the system.<ref name="Trkn" />

Annoyed, Tarkin ordered Burque to collect debris from the battle, which included several of the [[droid]] [[Starfighter|fighters]], but warned the captain that the enemy ships might be rigged to explode. The ''Electrum''{{'s}} salvage team successfully recovered one vulture droid whose [[self-destruct mechanism]] had been damaged and delivered it to the base for analysis. Tarkin later discovered that a [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] that had become aware of the Death Star's construction had been behind the attack and had them [[Death|executed]].<ref name="Trkn" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|Keep those hyperspace coordinates at the ready, Captain. But right now I want you to execute a microjump to the Rimward edge of this system. Do you understand?''"<br />"''Understood, Governor.|Burque follows Wilhuff Tarkin's orders despite being confused by them|audio=Burque-Microjump.flac|<ref name="Trkn" />}}
Burque was a tall and gangly man with a strong jaw, which was lined with a well-clipped [[Color|brown]] [[Hair|beard]]. He was confused when Governor Wilhuff Tarkin ordered him to jump to the edge of the system containing Sentinel Base instead of jumping to aid Rampart Station while it was under attack, but he followed the orders nonetheless.<ref name="Trkn" />

==Behind the scenes==
Burque appeared, as a [[hologram]], in the [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']], which was written by [[James Luceno]].<ref name="Trkn" />
