The '''Ferrix occupation''' occurred when the [[Galactic Empire]] occupied the [[planet]] [[Ferrix]] in [[5 BBY]]. Following the [[ambush on Ferrix]], [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]]'s authority over the [[Morlani system]] was rescinded. In its place, an [[Imperial Military|Imperial]] [[Ferrix garrison|garrison]] was established on Ferrix, headquartered in [[Hotel Rix]].

The occupation was initially overseen by [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Blevin]], who had [[Chief Inspector]] [[Hyne]], [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] and [[Sergeant]] [[Linus Mosk]] transferred away from Ferrix. Control of the Morlani [[Star system|system]] was transferred to [[Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]], Blevin's colleague in the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], by order of [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz]].

==Pre-occupation==
In [[5 BBY]], [[Cassian Jeron Andor]] [[Death|killed]] two [[Pre-Mor Security Inspection team|Pre-Mor Security officers]], [[Verlo Skiff]] and [[Kravas Drezzer]], while searching for his sister [[Kerri]] on [[Morlana One]]. [[Deputy Inspector]] [[Syril Karn]] investigated the murder of Skiff and Drezzer despite [[Chief Inspector]] [[Hyne]]'s orders to cover up the incident.<ref name="Episode 1">{{Andor|Kassa (episode)}}</ref> Karn tracked Andor to his [[homeworld]] of [[Ferrix]], where he [[Commanding officer|commanded]] a contingent of the [[Pre-Mor Authority Security Forces|Pre-Mor Enforcement]] with the support of [[Sergeant]] [[Linus Mosk]]. However, they failed to capture Andor who escaped with [[Luthen Rael]]'s help. The [[Ambush on Ferrix|Ferrix incident]] ultimately resulted in the deaths of several Pre-Mor [[officer]]s.<ref name="Episode 3">{{Andor|Reckoning (episode)}}</ref>

==The occupation==
Control of the [[Morlani system]] was [[Takeover of the Preox-Morlana Corporate Sector|taken]] from [[Consolidated Holdings of Preox-Morlana Corporation|Preox-Morlana]] by the [[Galactic Empire]] in consequence of the Ferrix incident.<ref name="Aldhani" /> In the wake of the chaotic Ferrix incident,<ref name="Announcement" /> the [[Imperial Army]] established a robust [[Ferrix garrison|garrison]]<ref name="SecondEdition">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'' (second edition)]]</ref> to establish Imperial authority<ref name="Announcement" /> and ward off potential uprisings.<ref name="SecondEdition" /> In the process, the Empire commandeered a [[Hotel Rix|hotel]] on [[Rix Road]] to use as their headquarters.<ref name="TAF" /> [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Blevin]], a member of the [[Imperial Security Bureau]], was dispatched from [[Coruscant]] to oversee Imperial rule in the Morlani [[Star system|system]]. Hyne, Karn, and Mosk were accused of incompetence by Blevin, who ordered the trio to report to a transfer center while he oversaw the establishment of permanent Imperial rule in the [[Free Trade sector]].<ref name="Aldhani" /> However, [[Major]] [[Lio Partagaz|Partagaz]] later transferred the system to the jurisdiction of ISB [[Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]], who convinced the major that the incident on Ferrix was connected to a [[Axis network|rebel network]].<ref name="N5" />
