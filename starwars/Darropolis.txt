'''Darropolis''' was a [[city]] on the [[planet]] [[Hosnian Prime]]. A [[New Republic]] [[Unidentified New Republic veteran hospital|veteran hospital]] was located in the city, and the hospital was where [[Dade Hetkins]] received treatment and a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] [[Limb|leg]] to replace the one lost in the aftermath of the [[Battle of Endor]]. [[Doctor]] [[Arsad]] supervised Hetkins' recovery, and assigned him the prototype [[therapy droid]] [[QT-9]]. Dome-style Hosnian homes were built on [[crystal]] dunes on the outskirts of the city.<ref name="ALD" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Hosnian Prime was [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] by the [[First Order]] using the [[superweapon]] built within [[Starkiller Base]].<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref>
