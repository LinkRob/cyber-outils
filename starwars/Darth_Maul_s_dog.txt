A '''dog''' belonged to the [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Maul/Legends|Darth Maul]].<ref name="LSW: TESO" /> In [[0 ABY/Legends|0 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Out]]'' takes place immediately after {{Film|IV}}, which ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' dates the end of to [[0 ABY]].</ref> Maul constructed the [[Darth Star]] for his [[Sith Master/Legends|Sith Master]] [[Galactic Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]] aboard an [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Imperial]] [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought/Legends|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]]. Soon after the Darth Star was complete, it was destroyed when Palpatine's other [[Sith apprentice/Legends|apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]], collided with the [[Space station/Legends|space station]], causing an explosion that destroyed a section of the [[Star Dreadnought/Legends|Star Dreadnought's]] [[Bridge (starship)/Legends|bridge]]. Maul's [[Limb/Legends|legs]] were [[Dismemberment/Legends|detached]] from his body and he was pulled into [[Realspace/Legends|space]]. As he floated away he called out to Palpatine, asking the [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] to feed his [[Dog/Legends|dog]].<ref name="LSW: TESO" />
