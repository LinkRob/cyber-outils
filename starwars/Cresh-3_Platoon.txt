'''Cresh-3 Platoon''' was an [[infantry]] [[platoon]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Stormtrooper Corps]], serving as a component of [[Company Cresh]] alongside three other platoons. After being rotated off active duty for training, Company Cresh and other [[stormtrooper]] units were placed under [[Red Battalion]], an [[Imperial cadet]] training formation for several [[Standard day|days]] of training exercises on [[Sirpar]]. There, the young [[cadet]]s would command the stormtrooper units to demonstrate the value of discipline and training. The Imperial cadet [[Zare Leonis]] served as the platoon's [[commanding officer]] as a [[lieutenant]], while [[Bedal Cerroux]] served as the [[sergeant major]].<ref name="Secrets" />

During one of the exercises, Leonis personally led one of the platoon's four [[squad]]s and were directed to serve as an [[artillery]] unit. There, they provided fire support for the rest of the company during the exercise.<ref name="Secrets" />
