The '''Crescent Royale Casino''' was a [[casino]] located in<ref name="Aphra 4" /> the [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]]<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|canto-bight|Canto Bight}}</ref> on the [[planet]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> [[Cantonica]].<ref name="Aphra 4" /> As one of the city's main attractions and oldest venues. In [[3 ABY]], it was the site of a shootout between [[Kay Vess' crew]] and the syndicate [[Zerek Besh]] as Vess' crew escaped the Canto sewers after their successful heist on [[Westhill Palace]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

In [[34 ABY]], during their search for [[Maz Kanata]]'s associate [[Master Codebreaker]] in their attempt to help the [[Resistance]] to escape from the [[First Order]]'s clutches, [[Finn]], [[Rose Tico]], and [[BB-8]] [[Mission to Canto Bight|traveled]] to the Canto Casino at [[Canto Bight]], which was located near the Canto Bight [[racetrack]], where they witnessed a [[Fathier racing|fathier race]]. Although they were incapacitated with [[stun]] blasts by the [[Canto Bight Police Department]] and hauled off to the [[Canto Bight police headquarters]], Finn and Rose managed to escape from the jail to the racetrack thanks to the help of [[DJ]] and BB-8.<ref name="Episode VIII">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>
