'''Changeling'''<ref name="Episode II" /> or '''shapeshifter''' were terms used for the few [[species]] that could assume different forms.<ref name="IJ">''[[Servants of the Empire: Imperial Justice]]''</ref> The [[Clawdite]]s of [[Zolan]] were shapeshifters.<ref name="Ultimate SW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> Thanks to those abilities, changelings made almost undetectable impostors, which posed security risks. Notably, the Clawdite [[bounty hunter]] [[Cato Parasitti]] helped [[Cad Bane]] infiltrate the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]] by impersonating [[Death|deceased]] [[Jedi Master]] [[Ord Enisence]], and then [[Chief Librarian]] [[Jocasta Nu]].<ref name="Holocron Heist">{{TCW|Holocron Heist}}</ref> During the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], the [[Imperial Intelligence]] of his [[Galactic Empire]] kept a careful eye on shapeshifters with underworld ties.<ref name="IJ" />

Transforming through the Force was possible. The [[Force wielder]]s had this ability, with the [[Son]] being able to transform into a large, winged [[gargoyle]] while the [[Daughter]] could become a [[griffin]].<ref name="Overlords">{{TCW|Overlords}}</ref>
