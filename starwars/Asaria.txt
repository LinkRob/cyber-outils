'''Asaria''' was a [[Trandoshan]] member of [[Lourna Dee's Tempest]] and a [[Strike]] working under [[Cloud (rank)|Cloud]] [[Dent (Nihil)|Ultident Margrona]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> she was a member of a team sent to the [[planet]] [[Elphrona]] to [[Raid on Elphrona|capture]] the [[Blythe family]] so they could be held for ransom. While the [[pirate]]s were transporting their captives to their [[starship]]s, Asaria, sitting in the cart to watch the prisoners, was violently flung out when [[Ottoh Blythe]], who had been feigning unconsciousness, whistled to startle the captured [[steelee]]s the Nihil had been using as transportation. Asaria then [[Death|died]] upon hitting the ground, with her head being split open.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
