{{Quote|I've seen that hangar of yours in back with that old starfighter you're restoring.''"<br />"''An antique, hauled out of scrap&mdash;and I haven't spent a credit on it in a year.|[[Teeler]] and Fraxa|<ref name="The Living Force" />}}
An antique '''ARC-8 starfighter''' was owned by [[Fraxa]], owner of [[Kwikhaul Livery Corporation]] on the [[planet]] [[Kwenn]]. The [[ARC-8 starfighter]] was originally from the [[High Republic Era]], during which it had seen extensive use. Although Fraxa attempted to restore the [[starfighter]], she was mainly limited to cosmetic work. During the [[Jedi High Council]]'s [[Mission to Kwenn|mission]] to Kwenn, [[Jedi Master]] [[Saesee Tiin]] helped to repair the starfighter in exchange for being able to take it for a test flight. Tiin flew the starfighter to [[Defense of Kwenn|defend]] Kwenn against a [[pirate]] attack orchestrated by [[Zilastra]] of the [[Riftwalkers]].<ref name="The Living Force" />
