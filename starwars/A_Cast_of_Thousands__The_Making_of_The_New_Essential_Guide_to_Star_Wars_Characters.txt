"'''A Cast of Thousands: The Making of The New Essential Guide to ''Star Wars'' Characters'''" is an article which appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 59|''Star Wars Insider'' 59]]. It was written by [[Daniel Wallace]], illustrated by [[Michael Sutfin]], and focused on the production and creation of ''[[The New Essential Guide to Characters]]''. Also included within the article were two character entries that were not included within the final published product of ''The New Essential Guide to Characters'': entries for [[Wes Janson/Legends|Wes Janson]] and [[Poggle the Lesser/Legends|Poggle the Lesser]].
