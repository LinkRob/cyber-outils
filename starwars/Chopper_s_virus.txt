'''Chopper's virus''' was a [[computer virus]] programmed and uploaded by the [[C1-series astromech droid|C1-series]] [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P]], or Chopper, to an [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]]. It was used to disrupt the Star Destroyer's systems and distract its crew while Chopper and [[Ezra Bridger]] of the rebel crew known as the [[Spectres]] carried out a [[mission to steal Imperial codes|mission]] to steal Imperial codes that would allow them to land safely on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. The result of the virus included disruptions to the ship's stabilizers, as well as sending waste into the halls of the ship.<ref name="Virus Alert!">{{LSWCite|5|Virus Alert!}}</ref>
