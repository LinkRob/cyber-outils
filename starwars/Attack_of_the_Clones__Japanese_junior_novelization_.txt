'''''Attack of the Clones''''' ([[Wikipedia:Japanese language|Japanese]]: '''クローンの攻撃''') is a Japanese junior novel adaptation of  [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']]. It was written by [[Ella Patrick]] and published by [[Kaiseisha]] on [[December 9]], [[2017]].<ref name="Amazon" />
