'''Dan Norton''' is a comic book penciller who pencilled the interiors of the [[Star Wars Infinities: Return of the Jedi 2|second issue]] of the [[Dark Horse Comics]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars Infinities: Return of the Jedi]]'' alongside [[Ryan Benjamin]] and [[J.J. Kirby|Juvaun Kirby]]
The issue was published on [[January 28]], [[2004]].<ref name="Dark Horse" />
