'''Alaphani''' was a [[language]] spoken by the [[Alaphani]] [[species]]. The [[3PO-series protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] was able [[Translator|translate]] and speak the language.<ref name="SW50" /> In [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''Star Wars'' (2015) 50 to [[1 ABY]].</ref> C-3PO assisted<ref name="SW50" /> the [[smuggler]]<ref name="CGSWG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Han Solo]] on the [[Ring of Kafrene]]<ref name="SW50" /> [[space station]]<ref name="CGSWG" /> when<ref name="SW50" /> he<ref name="CGSWG" /> made a deal with an [[Unidentified Alaphani|Alaphani]] speaking the language in exchange for [[transponder code]]s. When Solo told C-3PO to tell the Alaphani that it was good doing business with him, the [[protocol droid]] told the [[alien]] "our regurgitated mouth mucus proved easy to mix" in Alaphani.<ref name="SW50" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Alaphani language appeared in the [[Star Wars (2015) 50|fiftieth issue]] of the [[2015]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars (2015)|''Star Wars'']]. The issue was written by [[Kieron Gillen]], lettered by [[Clayton Cowles]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="SW50" /> on [[July 4]], [[2018]].<ref name="Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/69553/star_wars_2015_50|text=Star Wars (2015) #50}}</ref>
