'''Balnab''' was a temperate [[planet]] located in the [[Bright Jewel sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]], near [[Patitite Pattuna]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], it served as a home to a group of [[Unidentified tentacle-faced species|alien]] castaways.

==Description==
[[File:BalnabLandscape-ND.png|thumb|left|200px|The landscape of Balnab]]
Balnab was a [[planet]]<ref name="SWE" /> located in the [[Balnab system]] in the [[Bright Jewel sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> near [[Patitite Pattuna]]. At the time of the [[Clone Wars]], the planet was still in its early stages of development, made by a [[Star|stellar]] remnant, and was covered in [[swamp]]s and rocky canyons. Balnab's temperate [[Temperature|climate]] was the home to [[hesten]], small, four-armed [[primate]]s<ref name="SWE" /> who lived in troops in the native [[umbrella tree]]s.<ref name="TVE" />

==History==
At some point prior to the Clone Wars, an [[Unidentified tentacle-faced species|alien]] [[Gozanti-class cruiser|cruiser]] crashed on Balnab, and the surviving crew became the lone [[Sentience|intelligence]] on the primitive world. The [[Balnab (group)|Balnab]] denizens were tricked into serving a group of [[DUM-series pit droid|pit droids]] who took the [[Hologram|holographic]] persona of "[[Albee Dewaw]]." During the war, [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] inadvertently visited Balnab, where they revealed the truth about the self-appointed [[Politics#Dictatorship|dictator]].<ref name="ND" />

In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="10 BBY">The events of ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' takes place in [[9 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:KenobiDate|here]]. As "[[Part IV]]" establishes that a transmission on [[Balnab]] was intercepted a [[Standard year|year]] before, it must have been intercepted in [[10 BBY]].</ref> the [[Hidden Path]] was rumored to have an established presence on Balnab. This was due to a transmission that was intercepted by the Empire regarding communications of a secret network. Initially, the network was disregarded as a lie, but safehouses in two other [[Star system|systems]] were identified.<ref name="Kenobi IV">{{Kenobi|Part IV}}</ref>

Following the [[Battle of Yavin]] and the [[Cyrkon Extraction]], [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] camped out on Balnab aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''. They counted their reward money while planning their next venture, but were convinced by [[Luke Skywalker]] to undertake a [[Mission to Calumdarian|mission]] to deliver crates to [[Calumdarian]]. Solo reluctantly agreed, and took the ''Falcon'' to [[Balnab Spaceport]] where he became embroiled in a bar fight between members of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] and the [[smuggler]] [[Akko]] and his gang. Han helped his old smuggling friends ship [[spice]] off Balnab, and departed that night for [[Troiken]].<ref name="Age of Rebellion" />
