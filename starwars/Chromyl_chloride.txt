'''Chromyl chloride''' was a chemical which was found in high concentrations in the oceans that entirely covered the surface of the lifeless [[planet]] [[Calcoraan]].<ref name="AND">[[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chromyl chloride was first mentioned in [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']], a novel written by [[John Jackson Miller]] and released in [[2014]].
