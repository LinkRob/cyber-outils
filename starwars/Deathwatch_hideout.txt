'''Deathwatch hideout''' was a hideout used by the [[Mandalorian]] [[terrorist]] group [[Death Watch]] on [[Mandalore]]'s [[moon]], [[Concordia]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. Initially constructed by Mandalorian [[miner]]s, the group used the base until it was discovered by [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Duchess]] [[Satine Kryze]] in [[21 BBY]]. As the two discovered the group of Mandalorian warriors there, they fought, with Kenobi eventually dueling [[Pre Vizsla]], the former [[governor]] of Concordia who was secretly the leader of Death Watch.<ref name="TMP" />
