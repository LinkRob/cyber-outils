'''1977 Topps ''Star Wars'' Series 2''' was a trading card set released by [[Topps]] in [[1977]].<ref name="Volume One" />

The set was based on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']], and featured stills from the film, promotional photos and behind the scenes photos, all within a red border. Backs of the cards featured "Actor's Profile" or "Movie Facts" themes, or included a portion of a larger image that could be pieced together with other cards like a puzzle. A sub-set of sticker cards was included.<ref name="Volume One" />

Card numbering in the set continued from the [[1977 Topps Star Wars Series 1|previous series]]. Three more series of the set followed.<ref name="Volume One" />

==Continuity==
Some cards use images from deleted scenes from the film: Card 74 uses an image from the scene featuring [[Luke Skywalker/Legends|Luke Skywalker]] and [[Biggs Darklighter/Legends|Biggs Darklighter]] on [[Tatooine/Legends|Tatooine]], Card 85 is from the deleted scene of Luke repairing a [[Moisture vaporator/Legends|moisture vaporator]], Card 101 shows [[Chewbacca/Legends|Chewbacca]] from the deleted, later restored, scene of [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure/Legends|Jabba the Hutt]] in [[Docking Bay 94/Legends|Docking Bay 94]]. Card 132 uses an image from a deleted scene featuring [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Darth Vader]] and [[Moradmin Bast/Legends|Moradmin Bast]].<ref name="blu-ray">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Saga]]''</ref>

Like in the previous card series, lightsabers are referred to as "light sabres."
