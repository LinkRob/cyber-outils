'''Clone trooper flight crews'''<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> were [[clone trooper]]s in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] who were trained to help maintain the [[Starship|ships]] and [[walker]]s. They served aboard ships such as the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="TCW" />

Members of [[Cloning|clone]] trooper flight crews wore [[Color|blue]] suits with yellow vests. They wore [[helmet]]s often equipped with radio communication.<ref name="TCW" />
