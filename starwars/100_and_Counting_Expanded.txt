"'''100 and Counting Expanded'''" is an article by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] that appeared in [[Star Wars Insider 150|''Star Wars Insider'' 150]]. It serves as a guide for episodes of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', starting with "[[Eminence (episode)|Eminence]]," and [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|The Lost Missions]], the series' final season [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|at the time]]. This article is a follow-up to ''[[100 and Counting]]'' published in [[Star Wars Insider 139|''Star Wars Insider'' 139]], also listing the episodes in production order.
