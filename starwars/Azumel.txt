The '''Azumels''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] six-[[eye]]d [[species]] [[Homeworld|native]] to [[Azum]]. [[Argus Panox]] was a member of this species.

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Eyes on your own cards, buddy. All of them.|Han Solo, to Argus Panox|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:Argus_Panox_Topps_Countdown_to_Solo.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Argus Panox was a Azumel sabacc player.]]
The Azumel<ref name="FA" /> were a [[species]] of [[Color|gray]],<ref name="Age of Republic" /> mottled brown,<ref name="Solo" /> red,<ref name="Han Chewie 6" /> tan,<ref name="Adventures 12" /> or yellow colored skin.<ref name="MoTP 1" /> They had six eyes which varied in color from blue<ref name="Solo" /> to green<ref name="Droid Hunters" /> or orange.<ref name="Age of Republic" /> They also had four fingers on each hand.<ref name="Solo" />

==Azumel in the galaxy==
During the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Cibaba]] was an Azumel serving the [[Jedi Order]] at the the [[Jedi temple]] on [[Alaris Prime]], where he trained his [[Padawan]], [[Ty Yorrick|Tylera]].<ref name="MoTP 1" /> An Azumel was present in a bar on the [[planet]] [[Telerath]] when [[Jango Fett]] and [[Boba Fett]] met with [[bounty hunter]]s.<ref name="Age of Republic" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], an Azumel was a stagehand aboard [[Risha Synata's ship]].<ref name="Adventures 12" />

[[Argus Panox]] was a [[Gender|male]] Azumel who played during [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Han Solo]]'s first [[sabacc]] game on the planet [[Vandor]].<ref name="Solo" /> [[Makkeer]] was an Azumel who discovered the location of hidden treasure by the [[pirate]] [[queen]] [[Rane Mahal]].<ref name="Droid Hunters" />
