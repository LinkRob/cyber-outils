The '''Almagest''' was a [[nebula]]<ref name="ALD" /> located within the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Timelines" /> with [[Color|red]] clouds. Portions of the [[Imperial Navy]] hid in it after the [[Battle of Endor]], creating an [[Almagest Imperial fleet|Imperial fleet]]. The [[fleet]] there was not as big as the one in the [[Vulpinus Nebula]], but there were still hundreds of [[Star Destroyer]]s and thousands of smaller [[Starship|craft]].<ref name="ALD" />
