'''''Dark Times—Fire Carrier'' 3''' is the twenty-fifth issue of the ''[[Star Wars: Dark Times]]'' comic series and the third in the [[Star Wars: Dark Times—Fire Carrier|''Fire Carrier'']] story arc. Counted as a continuation of ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'', this issue established the title as the longest-running Star Wars comic, passing the original [[Star Wars (1977)|''Star Wars'' (1977)]].

==Publisher's summary==
''As Jedi Master K'Kruhk and a band of Padawans distance themselves from the Empire's refugee camp, they discover a deadly secret the local militia is keeping from their Imperial masters!''

''Now, marked for death by both factions, they must find a place of safety. But how does one escape the dark times?''
