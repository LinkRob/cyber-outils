The '''Alliance pilot doll'''<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> was a [[doll]] created by the [[desert]] [[scavenger]] [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]]. When Rey was a young [[Gender|girl]], she [[dream]]ed up stories about [[Dosmit Ræh]], a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[captain]] who fought in the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Her fascination with the Rebel captain inspired young Rey to create a doll using fabric from a [[flight suit]] she recovered<ref name="Survival Guide">''[[Rey's Survival Guide]]''</ref> from a [[New Republic]]<ref name="Visual Dictionary" /> cargo container. Rey eventually grew out of playing with the doll, but she kept the memory of Dosmit Ræh alive by keeping the doll and Ræh's old flight helmet in her possession throughout her [[Standard year|years]] on Jakku, to remind herself of how far she had come.<ref name="Survival Guide" />
