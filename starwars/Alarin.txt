'''Alarin''' was a [[language]] that [[Cassian Jeron Andor|Cassian Andor]]<ref name="Aldhani" />&mdash; a [[human]] originally from the [[planet]] [[Kenari]] but raised on the planet [[Ferrix]]<ref name="Dawn">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>&mdash;could speak<ref name="Aldhani" /> by [[5 BBY]]. That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]'' dates the events of "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]]" to [[5 BBY]].</ref> speaking Alarin was one of Andor's skills that the [[Axis network|rebel]] [[Luthen Rael]] listed when trying to convince his compatriot [[Vel Sartha]] that Andor would be an asset to her [[Aldhani rebel infiltration team|infiltration team]] for an upcoming [[Aldhani heist|heist]] on the planet [[Aldhani]], although that was not enough to convince Sartha.<ref name="Aldhani" />

==Behind the scenes==
Alarin was mentioned in "[[Aldhani (episode)|Aldhani]],"<ref name="Aldhani" /> the fourth episode of the [[Andor Season 1|first season]] of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]'', which aired on [[September 28]], [[2022]].<ref name="Companion">{{InsiderCite|217|Star Wars: Andor Season One Companion|''Star Wars: Andor'' Season One Companion}}</ref>
