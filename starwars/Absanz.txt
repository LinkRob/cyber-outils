'''Absanz''' was a [[desert]] [[planet]] that hosted a [[Sienar Fleet Systems factory|factory]] belonging to [[Sienar Fleet Systems]]. The [[Spectres]] [[Mission to Absanz|traveled]] to Absanz to retrieve intelligence on Sienar's newest [[TIE series|TIE]] prototypes. They attempted to capture lead designer [[Dron Ryall]], but were forced to let him go in order to escape.

==Description==
A [[planet]] with flat and rocky [[desert]] terrain, Absanz had an [[atmosphere]] that was breathable and filled with clouds.<ref name="Secrets" />

==History==
{{Quote|This factory contains intel on Sienar's new bombs and updates on their most recent TIE prototypes. We're to find the information and bring it back to the fleet.|Kanan Jarrus, to Garazeb Orrelios|<ref name="Secrets" />}}
[[Sienar Fleet Systems]] operated a [[Sienar Fleet Systems factory|factory]] on Absanz that was eventually controlled by the [[Galactic Empire]]. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the rebel cell known as the [[Spectres]] [[Mission to Absanz|infiltrated]] the factory in an attempt to find information on new developments, including [[bomb]]s and [[TIE series|TIE]] prototypes.<ref name="Secrets" />

[[File:Escape_from_Absanz.jpg|thumb|left|250px|The Spectres attempt to escape Absanz]]
While the rest of the crew waited elsewhere in the ''[[Ghost]]'', [[Kanan Jarrus]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] entered the factory. They encountered [[Dron Ryall]], one of the lead designers at Sienar. The rebels kidnapped Ryall to interrogate him for information, but they were chased by several [[stormtrooper]]s. Jarrus and Orrelios took Ryall aboard a [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]], but fire from an Imperial [[cruiser]] caused Ryall to fall out of the [[Starship|ship]]. Orrelios managed to catch Ryall, but decided to let him escape to prevent the destruction that the Empire would cause in pursuit of him.<ref name="Secrets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Absanz first appeared in "[[Secrets of Sienar]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] written by [[Martin Fisher]], illustrated by [[Eva Widermann]] and published in [[Star Wars Rebels Magazine 13|''Star Wars Rebels'' Magazine 13]].<ref name="Secrets" />
