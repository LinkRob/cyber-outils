'''Arvel Crynyd''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] who served in the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. In the war's [[4 ABY|fourth]] [[Standard year|year]], [[Commander]] Crynyd participated in the Rebellion's attack on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] in the [[Battle of Endor]], flying an [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]] in [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]] as [[Green Leader|leader]] of the squadron. Crynyd was [[Death|killed]] during the battle in a [[Death|suicide]] run to destroy the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]''. The battle ended soon after, seeing the destruction of the Death Star and death of [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]].

==Biography==
===Joining the Rebellion===
Before joining the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]], Arvel Crynyd flew as a [[Z-95 Headhunter]] [[pilot]] for a [[Thyferra]]n [[bacta]] [[Bacta Cartel|cartel]]<ref name="Incredibuilds" /><ref name="FACPOV" /> In approximately [[0 ABY]],<ref>"[[The Impossible Flight of Ash Angels]]" states that this encounter occurred 4 [[Standard year|years]] before the [[Battle of Endor]] which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[4 ABY]].</ref>, Crynyd was working under contract with the Bacta Cartel when he came across a group of rookie Rebel pilots training near an abandoned spacedock on the edge of the [[Western Reaches]]. Although he typically tried to stay out of the civil war and keep his head down, Crynyd was intrigued by the lovingly maintained [[A-wing]]s the pilots were training in. Although [[Pieter (Rebel)|Pieter]], the more experienced pilot of the group, was initially suspicious of Crynyd and urged him to go on his way, Crynyd impressed the pilots by performing a barrel roll. Without knowing exactly why, Crynyd offered to teach the other pilots, including [[Raf (Rebel)|Raf]] the trick. This encounter led to Crynyd becoming a flight instructor for the Rebel Alliance.<ref name="FACPOV" />

One year later, Crynyd met with a contact on [[Koda Station]] and learned that his former student Raf had died. While there, he met [[Demms Ryx]], a [[Volpai]] bartender. Ryx comforted Crynyd, and the two met again when Pieter and Crynyd returned to Koda Station for a shipment of A-Wing parts. Sometime later, Ryx arrived on the abandoned spacedock where Crynyd trained pilots, saying that he wanted to make a difference. Ryx became a cook for the Alliance, and eventually he and Crynyd became romantic partners. The two raised a daughter, [[Starr]], who was a toddler at the time of the Battle of Endor. Starr and Ryx lived on [[Cerea]], and Crynyd visited them a few days prior to the battle.<ref name="FACPOV" />

===Battle of Endor===
====Commander at Endor====
{{Quote|Three of them, coming in. 20 degrees.<br />Cut to the left, I'll take the lead.|Crynyd to [[Wedge Antilles|Antilles]]|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
Holding the [[Military rank|rank]] of [[commander]],<ref name="On the Front Lines">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> Crynyd flew as a [[Rebel pilot|pilot]] in [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green Squadron]], operating an [[RZ-1 A-wing interceptor]]<ref name="Episode VI" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> after the [[Bothan Spynet]] discovered that [[Galactic Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] would be aboard the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]] to oversee its construction, [[Alliance High Command]] determined that launching an immediate [[Battle of Endor|assault]] on the [[space station]] would be their only chance to cripple the Empire.<ref name="Episode VI" />

[[File:ArvelCrynyd-SWZ.png|thumb|left|270px|Arvel Crynyd, flying as Green Leader at the Battle of Endor]]
With the full [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]], Crynyd, under the [[callsign]] of [[Green Leader]], exited [[hyperspace]] above the [[Endor|forest moon]] of [[Endor (planet)|Endor]], approaching the [[superweapon]] in his A-wing. He was the last squadron leader to report stand-by status and prepared to attack the Death Star, but pulled up at the last second on [[General]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian's]] orders when it was revealed that the shield was still up. Green Squadron was ordered to stick close to [[Sector MV-7]] as a massive flotilla of Star Destroyers emerged from the other side of the moon. The Alliance had walked right into a trap, but were determined to go down fighting.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Initially, both sides fought with only their starfighters to the rebels' confusion, but this turned to horror when the Death Star proved itself to be fully operational, firing its superlaser on the Mon Calamari Star Cruiser ''Liberty'' and reducing it to atoms. Following the advice of Calrissian, the remaining Rebel forces moved up alongside the Star Destroyers and fought at point-blank range, hoping the Death Star wouldn't dare shoot into its own fleet.<ref name="Episode VI" />

====Sacrifice====
{{Quote|'''Intensify forward firepower!'''<br />'''AAAAAAAGGGGHHHHH...!!!'''<br />TOO LATE!|Piett and Gherant's last watch on Crynyd's A-Wing's last out of control spin|<ref name="Episode VI" />}}
When the shield protecting the Death Star was disabled, several fighters broke off and headed for it to destroy the reactor. To buy them time, Crynyd and his squadron attacked the Imperial flagship, the mighty Super Star Destroyer ''Executor'', with two A-wings knocking out its bridge deflector shields.<ref name="Episode VI" />

[[File:ArvelCrynydsSacrifice-BTMF29.png|thumb|right|300px|Crynyd's last action during the Battle of Endor was crashing his A-wing into the command bridge of the ''Executor'', causing the Super Star Destroyer to smash into the Death Star.]]
In response, Admiral [[Firmus Piett]] ordered the forward batteries to increase forward firing to prevent any window of opportunity from being exploited. As Crynyd attempted a trench run through the ship's superstructure, his fighter was struck by a laser bolt and spun wildly. Realizing he was doomed, Crynyd managed to wrestle what little control he had over his starfighter and put it on a collision course with the ''Executor''{{'s}} unshielded bridge tower. Letting out one last cry of defiance, Crynyd's ship crashed through the bridge windows and exploded, killing himself alongside Piett and the entire bridge crew.<ref name="Episode VI" />

Without its command structure, the ''Executor'' lost primary power and plunged into the Death Star's gravity well, colliding with the station and detonating in a massive fireball. Crynyd's sacrifice tipped the battle in the Alliance's favor, with the Death Star being destroyed shortly after.<ref name="Episode VI" />

===Legacy===
After his death, Crynyd was posthumously memorialized during the celebrations on [[Endor]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> He was awarded the [[Medal of Bravery|Medal of Alderaan]], a special Rebel Alliance award that conveyed similar honor to the [[Kalidor Crescent]], for his actions during the battle.<ref name="Lead by Example">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> With Crynyd's death, command of Green Squadron was transferred to [[Shara Bey]].<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" />

==Personality and traits==
[[File:ArvelCrynyd-SWZ99.png|thumb|300px|right|Crynyd sacrificed his life for the Rebellion, crashing his A-wing into the ''Executor''.]]
Crynyd was a human<ref name="Episode VI" /> male<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel" /> with brown eyes and light skin.<ref name="Episode VI" /> Being a veteran [[pilot]] and instructor, Crynyd spent more time in an A-wing than anyone else in the [[Rebel Alliance Starfighter Corps|Alliance Starfighter Corps]]. He praised the starfighter and maintained the notion that skilled A-wing pilots could easily outfly the [[TIE/IN interceptor|TIE interceptor]]&mdash;the Imperial counterpart to the A-wing. Having also recognized that the lightly shielded A-wings couldn't stand up to [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer]]s, Crynyd pushed them into reconnaissance and escort missions where speed meant strength.<ref name="On the Front Lines" /> He was also brave and dedicated to the Alliance cause to the extent that he was willing to sacrifice his life for it, crashing his crippled starfighter into the ''Executor''.

Before joining the Alliance, Crynyd tried to avoid believing in anything except his starships and the experience of flying them. He was uncomfortable with vulnerability and sharing his inner feelings. Observing the dedication of the Rebels, and learning to believe in something through his relationship with Demms Ryx and their daughter, Crynyd almost reluctantly became a dedicated Rebel. By the time he sacrificed himself at the Battle of Endor, Crynyd believed in the people of the galaxy and the power of hope in the face of impossibility.<ref name="FACPOV" />

==Skills and abilities==
Crynyd possessed hyper-quick reflexes and an uncanny spatial sense.<ref name="Incredibuilds" />

==Equipment==
As a pilot of the Alliance, Arvel Crynyd wore a green [[flight suit]] and [[Flight helmet|helmet]], while operating an RZ-1 A-wing interceptor.<ref name="Episode VI" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Mad_Maxx_storyboard_ROTJ.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Storyboard art of "Mad Maxx"]]
Arvel Crynyd first appeared in the [[1983]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']],<ref name="Episode VI" /> and first identified in the [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|a-wing-fighter|A-wing Fighter}}</ref> He was portrayed by [[Hilton McRae]] in an uncredited role.<ref name="Hilton McRae 1">{{WebCite|url=http://aveleyman.com/ActorCredit.aspx?ActorID=11773|text=Hilton McRae|work=Aveleyman|archivedate=20130429000650}}</ref><ref name="Hilton McRae 2">{{WebCite|url=http://let-the-wookie-win.com/index.php?showimage=535|text=My Hilton McRae Star Wars Autograph|work=SW Autograph Collecting|archivedate=20131114003323}}</ref> The character's name during the production of ''Return of the Jedi'' was "Mad Maxx" — an homage to the film ''[[wikipedia:Mad Max (film)|Mad Max]]'' — as seen in storyboards for the film.<ref name="Making">''[[The Making of Return of the Jedi]]''</ref>
