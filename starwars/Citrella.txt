'''Citrella''' were a type of tropical [[fruit]] that could be made into a [[Color|pale orange]] drink known as [[sour citrella]], which was [[Diet|potable]] by [[human]]s.<ref name="PATS" /> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]'' to [[4 ABY]].</ref> after [[Prince]]ss [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[General]] [[Han Solo]]'s [[Wedding of Han Solo and Leia Organa|wedding]], they enjoyed their honeymoon on the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]''. Upon arriving in their suite, Organa noticed a basket of fresh fruit in the cabin, which included some tropical citrella.<ref name="PATS" />

==Behind the scenes==
