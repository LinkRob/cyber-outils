The '''Beskar MT-97''' was a pair of [[beskar]] [[MT-97]] [[blaster pistol]]s owned by the [[Mandalorian]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Aran Tal]] that he could wield for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on [[Vespaara]] as an alternative to his [[Clan Tal MT-97|standard blaster pistols]]. These ornate [[pistol]]s were [[Color|gold]], black, silver, red, and pale dark green in coloration, with circular grip ornaments, and yellow tassels dangling from the bottoms of the barrels.<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Weapon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Beskar MT-97 was a "Legendary" rarity "Weapon Wrap" for the Hunter Aran Tal's Clan Tal MT-97, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 6.0.0 alongside Season 6 on [[April 15]], [[2025]]. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Beskar Warrior]]. This wrap was unlocked immediately for all players at the start of Season 6.<ref name="Hunters" />
