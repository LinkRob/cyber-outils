==Behind the scenes==
Droids' Paradise will appear in ''[[Star Wars: Visions: Tsukumo]]'', a non-[[canon]] manga part of the ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'' multimedia project, set to be written by [[Eiichi Shimizu]] and published by [[Viz Media]] on [[July 14]], [[2026]]. The location was first mentioned at the [[Wikipedia:New York Comic Con|New York Comic Con]] on [[October 11]], [[2025]].<ref name="NYCC 2025" />
