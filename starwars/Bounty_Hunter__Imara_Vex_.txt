The '''Bounty Hunter''' was the standard outfit and [[armor]] set owned by the [[human]] [[bounty hunter]]<ref name="WotB" /> and [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] competitor<ref name="Hunters" /> [[Imara Vex]] that she wore prior to her involvement with,<ref name="WotB" /> as well as for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" /> Bounty Hunter included a helmet with glowing visor, as well as a [[shoulder-mounted armament]] that could fire tracking [[missile]]s.<ref name="WotB" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bounty Hunter first appeared in [[War of the Bounty Hunters 5|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 5]], written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]] and [[David Messina]],<ref name="WotB" /> and published by [[Marvel Comics]],<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/92295/star_wars_war_of_the_bounty_hunters_2021_5|text=Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters (2021) #5}}</ref> before being featured in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added when the game's early access went live via update 0.11.0 on [[November 16]], [[2021]]. Within the game, Bounty Hunter was a "Base" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Imara Vex, having served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Firestorm Rifle]]. This costume was unlocked by default when Imara Vex became playable, as early as the initial in-game tutorial.<ref name="Hunters" /> It was initially planned to be called "Arena Collaboration."<ref name="Hunters Panel">{{SW|url=news/swca-2022-star-wars-hunters-panel|text=SWCA 2022: 7 Things We Learned from the ''Star Wars: Hunters'' Panel}}</ref>
