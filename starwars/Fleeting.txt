The '''''Fleeting''''' was a [[starship]] owned by [[Ariole Yu]], a [[Lieutenant (criminal)|lieutenant]] in the [[Sixth Kin]] criminal syndicate. Yu and [[Just Lucky]] used the vessel to travel to [[Crimson Dawn]]'s [[Auction for Han Solo|auction]] of [[Han Solo]] in order to track down [[Gallin Crae]], a supposedly traitorous Sixth Kin lieutenant.<ref name="Aphra 11" />

The [[cockpit]] of the ''Fleeting'' included two chairs and [[hologram]] displays.<ref name="Aphra 11" />
