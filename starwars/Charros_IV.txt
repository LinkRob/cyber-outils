'''Charros IV''' was a [[planet]] in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]' [[Numesira sector]]. It was the site of the [[Xi Char Cathedral factories]], where members of the [[Xi Charrian]] [[species]] produced the [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighter]] "vulture [[droid]]." During the [[Galactic Civil War]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] was forced to [[Mid Rim Retreat|retreat]] from Charros IV, expecting the [[Galactic Empire]] to soon retake the world.

==Description==
Charros IV,<ref name="Complete Vehicles /> also known simply as Charros, was a [[planet]]<ref name="Rebel Files" /> in the fourth [[orbit]]al<ref name="Complete Vehicles /> position of the [[Charros system]],<ref name="Systems" /> located in [[the Slice]] portion<ref name="Slice" /> of the [[Mid Rim Territories]]. Situated in grid square R-9 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]], it lay in the [[Numesira sector]]<ref name="Systems" /> and was connected by [[hyperspace route]]s to the [[Bimmisaari system|Bimmisaari]] and [[Togoria system]]s,<ref name="TFABG" /> with the [[Great Kashyyyk Branch]] hyperroute<ref name="Branch" /> also linking it to the celestial body [[Balamak]].<ref name="TFABG" />

==History==
{{Quote|With regret, I must report that the Mid Rim Offensive has collapsed around several flash points, necessitating the withdrawal of Alliance troops to better-fortified holdings.|Carlist Rieekan, in a document addressed to Mon Mothma|<ref name="Rebel Files" />}}
[[File:CISVultureDroid-TCW.png|thumb|left|290px|Charros IV was the producer of vulture droids.]]
During the [[Republic Era|days]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Xi Char Cathedral factories]] at Charros IV produced<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> the [[Vulture-class starfighter|''Vulture''-class starfighter]] "vulture [[droid]]," an [[Droid starfighter|automated starfighter]] that saw use by the [[Trade Federation]] corporation and eventually the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]] against the Republic.<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> The Clone Wars [[Mission to Mustafar|ended]] in [[19 BBY]] with the fall of the Separatists and the Republic's [[Proclamation of the New Order|transition]] to the [[Galactic Empire]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> which came to control Charros IV.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

During the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the Empire and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]], the Alliance carried out its [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim Offensive]] from around [[1 ABY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> with Charros IV being liberated from Imperial control.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> The campaign then collapsed in [[2 ABY]]<ref name="Timelines" /> due to Imperial pushback. The Empire's counteroffensive necessitated the retreat of [[Rebel trooper|Alliance troops]] to more fortified holdings, and thus [[Alliance Military|Alliance forces]] prepared to retreat from Charros IV as well as the planets [[Durkteel]], [[Jeyell]], [[Ord Tiddell]], [[Pothor]], and [[Trasse]]&mdash;all of which the Empire was expected to retake imminently.<ref name="Rebel Files" />

[[File:Charros-NR-MandoVG.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Charros IV was part of the New Republic's territories (marked in dark blue) in 9 ABY.]]
The rebel [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]] made a summary of the situation in a classified document for the Alliance [[Commander-in-Chief]] [[Mon Mothma]], with Charros IV and the other five worlds being mentioned. Rieekan added that while a few local [[Mid Rim revolutionaries|revolutionaries]] were set to evacuate with the Alliance, the rest were set to stay and harass the Empire. [[Archivist]] [[Hendri Underholt]] soon collected the document and included it in their compilation of non-electronic documents known as ''[[The Rebel Files]]''.<ref name="Rebel Files" /> In [[9 ABY]],<ref name="MandoDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> Charros IV was part of the [[New Republic]] territory.<ref name="MandoVG" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Starcruiserdate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> a number of [[Viewscreen|screens]] on [[Chandrila Star Line]]'s [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser|Star Cruiser]] ''[[Halcyon]]'' showed a partial [[Star map|map]] of [[the galaxy]] that indicated Charros IV's position.<ref name="GS">[[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]]</ref>

==Inhabitants==
{{Quote|Look at the curve of the combat pods. The shape of the stripes, the positions of the blaster barrels. Weapons such as this not only are functional, but also incorporate the artistry of their creators. The beings who created and built these fighters believe in short, quick answers to questions and problems.|Imperial [[Captain]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]], commenting on the Xi Charrian design of vulture droids|<ref name="Thrawn">[[Thrawn (novel)|''Thrawn'']]</ref>}}
On Charros IV, the [[Xi Char]],<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> a [[Religion|religious cult]]<ref name="BMF 45">{{BuildFalconCite|45|Starship Fact File}}</ref> that founded<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> the fanatical [[Haor Chall Engineering Corporation]],<ref name="BMF 45" /> operated its cathedral [[Factory|factories]],<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> where members of<ref name="USWNE" /> the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[insectoid]] [[Xi Charrian]] [[species]]<ref name="EoS">[[Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles (second edition)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia of Starfighters and Other Vehicles'', second edition]]</ref> produced vulture droids.<ref name="USWNE" /> The Xi Char believed in bespoke production,<ref name="BMF 45" /> and ultra-precision manufacturing was a religious act of worship at the factories<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>&mdash;as well as the Haor Chall Engineering company as a whole&mdash;that was followed by thousands. Initiates in the workforce did not concern themselves with what came to use of the products.<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> Most Xi Charrian vehicle designs, like the vulture droid, had an [[insect]]oid appearance.<ref name="BMF 45" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:CrossSecEp1.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Charros IV originated in the ''Star Wars'' Legends reference book ''Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections''.]]
Charros was first introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a map included in the [[2016]] [[Fantasy Flight Games]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying]] boxed set [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]].<ref name="TFABG" /> It was fully identified as Charros IV in the [[2018]] reference book [[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'']], specifically in a section<ref name="Complete Vehicles" /> reprinted from [[David West Reynolds (real person)|David West Reynolds']] [[1999]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections]]'' of the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity.<ref name="EP1CS">''[[Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections]]''</ref>

The first [[in-universe]] appearance of Charros IV came via depiction on a map viewable in [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]], an immersive roleplaying experience at [[W:c:disney:Walt Disney World Resort|Walt Disney World Resort]] in Florida<ref name="GS" /> that was first opened to the public on [[March 1]], [[2022]],<ref name="31 Things">{{SW|url=news/31-things-we-learned-about-star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=31 Things We Learned in an Exclusive Preview of ''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser|nolive=1}}</ref> and had its final voyage from [[September 28]] to [[September 30|30]], [[2023]].<ref name="StarcruiserClosure">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyworld|domain=go.com|url=destinations/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser|text=''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser}}</ref> ''Star Wars: Episode I Incredible Cross-Sections'' served as the first mention of<ref name="EP1CS" /> the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Charros IV/Legends|Charros IV]] in Legends, and it subsequently had its first full appearance in [[James Luceno]]'s [[2005]] novel ''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''.<ref name="LoE">''[[Labyrinth of Evil]]''</ref>
