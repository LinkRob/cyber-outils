'''Betancy''' was an [[Imperial cadet]] who trained at the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] in [[5 BBY]]. They were a member of the promising [[cadet]] [[squad]] [[LRC-077|LRC077]] with [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Zare Leonis]] and were part of the squad's [[Unit Besh]]. When squad LRC077 was summoned to [[the Well]] training facility, Betancy was one of two cadets ordered not to participate in the exercise by [[Taskmaster]] [[Myles Grint]].

==Biography==
Betancy entered the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Academy for Young Imperials]] on the [[planet]] [[Lothal]] as a [[Imperial cadet|cadet]]<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" /> [[5 BBY|five]] years before the [[Battle of Yavin]],<ref name="Star Wars: Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Betancy was a member of the promising [[cadet]] squad [[LRC-077|LRC077]] with [[Zare Leonis]] and "[[Ezra Bridger|Dev Morgan]]," and served in the squad's [[Unit Besh]].<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" />

When Unit Besh and [[Unit Aurek]], the other unit in LRC077, were summoned to [[the Well]] training facility in the academy, Betancy and fellow Besh cadet [[Thurgos]] were ordered by [[Taskmaster]] [[Myles Grint]] to not take part in the exercise and instead stand with [[Sergeant]] [[Currahee]] and Units [[Unit Cresh|Cresh]] and [[Unit Dorn|Dorn]]. Betancy and Thurgos's shoulders slumped when given they were given the order, but the cadets obeyed and removed their helmets before walking away from their squad.<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" />

==Personality and traits==
As a member of Unit Besh, who were among the best cadets at the Academy for Young Imperials, Betancy was a promising cadet and considered [[officer]] material.<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" />

[[File:Unit_Besh.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Betancy with Units Besh and Aurek]]
They were of similar stature and build to [[Zare Leonis]] and [[Ezra Bridger]] and wore the standard [[Color|white]] and gray Imperial cadet [[Clothing|uniform]] with black [[boot]]s and [[glove]]s. Betancy's [[helmet]] was white and had no unique identifying symbols, unlike the helmets belonging to the Unit Aurek cadets.<ref name="Breaking Ranks" />

==Behind the scenes==
Betancy first appeared in the [[2014]] ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Breaking Ranks]]."<ref name="Breaking Ranks" /> They were first identified in the [[2015]] junior novel, ''[[Servants of the Empire: Rebel in the Ranks]]'', although it was not specified which of the four Unit Besh cadets seen in "Breaking Ranks" was Betancy.<ref name="Rebel in the Ranks" />

Their [[gender]] was never stated, but Fry's comment that there were no female cadets seen in "Breaking Ranks" indicates he considered Betancy male.<ref name="Gender note">{{Tumblr|subdomain=jasonfry|url=post/156687285821/notes-rebel-in-the-ranks-pt-1|name=Jason Fry's Dorkery|text=Notes: Rebel in the Ranks, Pt. 1|quote=Note Unit Forn is all-female. We don't see female cadets in "Breaking Ranks," but we know they exist – Dhara was one, after all.|archivefile=File:Jason_Fry_cadets_gender.png}}</ref>
