'''Chezdeze''' was an establishment on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. It was located in [[Slum district G17]] and was situated in the same building as the [[Trueping's]] cantina and a [[Unidentified massage parlor|massage parlor]]. A luminous sign showed the name of the establishment, written in large, [[Aurebesh]] characters. A red "open" sign on the façade signaled when the business was ready to receive visitors.<ref name="Deception" />
