{{Quote|Amidala. I was hoping to ask you to take the position, and to represent Naboo and the surrounding worlds of the Chommel ''[sic]'' sector in the Senate.|[[Réillata]], to Padmé Amidala|<ref name="Queen's Shadow" />}}
The '''Chommell sector''',<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> also known as the '''Naboo sector''',<ref name="Wayseeker">''[[The Acolyte: Wayseeker]]''</ref> was a [[The galaxy|galactic]] [[sector]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> Lightly populated, it comprised 36 full-member worlds, over 40,000 settled dependencies, and 300,000,000 barren [[star]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> It notably included the [[Naboo system]]<ref name="Ultimate SW" /> and [[Karlinus]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> The sector was [[Senator|represented]] in the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] by [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]]<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref> and later [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>

The sector took its name from the [[Chommell Minor system]].<ref name="BMF 18">{{BuildFalconCite|18|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">Per the reasoning [[:Template:StarlightDate|here]], the events of ''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]'' and ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'', which take place at least one [[Standard year|year]] and two [[Standard week|weeks]] and one year and a few [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of [[Starlight Beacon]], respectively, must take place in [[228 BBY]]. ''Defy the Storm'' and ''Temptation of the Force'' establish that the [[Stormwall]] expansion which engulfed most of the Chommell sector occurred between the two novels, therefore meaning it must taken place that year.</ref> [[Eye of the Nihil]] [[Marchion Ro]] began haphazardly expanding the border of his [[Stormwall]] barrier that prevented [[hyperspace]], with one such expansion engulfing most of the Chommell sector, including [[Naboo]].<ref name="Temptation of the Force" />
