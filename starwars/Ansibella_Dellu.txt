'''Ansibella Dellu''' was an [[artist]] [[Homeworld|from]] [[Raxus Secundus]]. Dellu served as a student government official at [[Reena University]], where she worked on a [[Count Dooku: Words of Truth|poster]] advertising a lecture series at the school by former [[Jedi Master]] [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]], a harsh critic of the [[Galactic Republic]]. Dooku went on to form the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and fought against the Republic in the [[Clone Wars]], prompting Dellu to become a [[Senator|member]] of the [[Separatist Senate|Separatist Parliament]] and oversee Separatist [[shadowfeed]]s in Republic [[Realspace|space]]. Dellu eventually surrendered to the Republic, which at war's end [[Proclamation of the New Order|became]] the [[Galactic Empire]], and she was considered an enemy of the state.

==Biography==
[[File:CountDooku-WordsofTruth.png|thumb|left|190px|Dellu's work-in-progress poster, ''Count Dooku: Words of Truth'']]
Ansibella Dellu was an [[artist]] [[Homeworld|from]] the [[Planet|world]] [[Raxus Secundus]] who was critical of the [[Core Worlds]]' [[politics]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> Between [[42 BBY]] and [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date 1">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates [[Dooku]]'s departure from the [[Jedi Order]] to [[42 BBY]]. Furthermore, ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'' states that Dooku's [[Reena University]] lectures occurred before the [[Clone Wars]], which began in [[22 BBY]], according to ''Timelines''. Therefore, ''[[Count Dooku: Words of Truth]]'', which advertised Dooku's Reena University lectures prior to the war, according to ''Star Wars Propaganda'', must have been made between the years 42 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] and 22 BBY.</ref> Dellu was a student government official at [[Reena University]], where she worked on a [[Painting|poster]] titled ''[[Count Dooku: Words of Truth]]''. It advertised a series of lectures at the university's [[Panos Lecture Hall]] by the leader of the [[Independent Movement for Self-Determination]] and former [[Jedi Master]], [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]]. As stated on the poster, Dooku stood as a "voice of reason" amid the [[Galactic Republic]]'s "corruption and bureaucracy."<ref name="Propaganda" />

As tensions heightened from the [[Separatist Crisis]], during which Dooku encouraged thousands of [[star system]]s to secede from the Republic, the [[count]] and other like-minded individuals formed the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Separatist Droid Army|took up arms]] against the Republic,<ref name="Episode II">{{Film|II}}</ref> starting the [[Clone Wars]] in 22 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Dellu became a [[Senator|member]] of the [[Separatist Senate|Separatist Parliament]] and was responsible for supervising the Separatist [[shadowfeed]]s in Republic [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> These [[propaganda]] broadcasts relied upon the [[HoloNet]] to report on the Confederacy's victories in battle and alleged Republic war crimes.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref>

Before the Clone Wars came to an end, Dellu surrendered to the Republic,<ref name="Propaganda" /> which [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] into the [[Galactic Empire]] in [[19 BBY]] as the Separatists were defeated.<ref name="Timelines" /> Dellu was deemed an enemy of the state under the new [[The galaxy|galactic]] government, although a rare specimen of the former senator's work-in-progress poster, ''Count Dooku: Words of Truth'', managed to survive the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]'s loyalty purges.<ref name="Propaganda" /> Circa [[34 ABY]], shortly after the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]],<ref name="Date 2">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the [[Hosnian Cataclysm]] to [[34 ABY]]. As ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'' indicates that the [[in-universe]] [[book]] ''[[A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'' was published shortly after the Hosnian Cataclysm, its release must have occurred around that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> that piece of Dellu's work was included in ''[[A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', a [[book]] that collected many different articles of propaganda from the [[time]] of the Republic to the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Propaganda" />

==Behind the scenes==
Ansibella Dellu was mentioned in the [[2016]] reference book ''[[Star Wars Propaganda: A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy]]'', written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="Propaganda" /> In an early preview for the book, Dellu's surname was spelled as "Delu,"<ref name="Preview">{{LegacyEdelweiss|url=#sku=9780062466822&page=1|text=Star Wars Propaganda : A History of Persuasive Art in the Galaxy}}</ref> although this was changed for the final release.<ref name="Propaganda" />
