A '''bed and breakfast''' was operated by the [[human]] [[gender|woman]] [[Alma (Ah Re Won)|Alma]] in [[Unidentified town (Ah Re Won)|a town]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Ah Re Won]]. Alma was an early settler to the area and the town grew around her modest inn, which was located by ancient [[Ah Re Won Sith temple|Sith temple ruins]]. Eventually, the settlement gained a [[mayor]]ship, a position which became filled by a [[Unidentified Ah Re Won town mayor|Sullustan businessman]] intent on building a [[Unidentified hotel (Ah Re Won)|luxurious hotel]] on the [[beach]]front to attract tourism. Even so, Alma only charged two [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] a night for her lodging, which included [[foodstuff|breakfast]] and dinner. The [[mayor]] disliked Alma and her bed and breakfast since the structure supposedly blocked visitors' view of the ruins.<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" />

At some point between around [[40 BBY]] and [[32 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Sith apprentice]] [[Darth]] [[Maul]] arrived to the [[city|town]] to [[meditation|meditate]] at the temple ruins and elected to stay at Alma's place hoping to find quietude. When the mayor's attempts to oust Alma with excessive taxation and noise failed, he resorted to [[fire|arson]] to raze the structure. His attempts was thwarted by Maul. Later, after the [[Pirate King of Sector Nine]] arrived to the town in search of [[Afolonkin|his son]] who had recently been [[death|killed]] while [[hunting]] near the ruins, the furious [[pirate]] blamed Alma, a mere bystander, for being complicit in his son's death. He ordered his [[tank]] to bombard the inn, which set it ablaze. Alma was able to escape in a [[starship]] she kept on the inn's roof.<ref name="DM-BW&R 4" />
