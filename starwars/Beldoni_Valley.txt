The '''Beldoni Valley''' was a valley on<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="One Year Later" /> [[Ghorman]]. The valley was home to a [[Unidentified webberie co-op|co-op]] of nine [[webberie]] estates,<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> a type of [[farm]] where the [[Ghorman silk|silk]] produced by [[Ghorlectipod]]s was harvested,<ref name="One Year Later" /> that, in [[3 BBY]], had been in business for around a [[millennium]]. That [[Standard year|year]], the webberies were subject to ground inspections, freight penalties, and new shipping codes by the [[Galactic Empire]], making all nine webberies wonder, for the first time in their history, if it was worth opening their hives the following [[spring]]. The [[Local day|day]] after an abrupt change in Imperial shipping codes that same year, a broker for the webberie co-op, [[Sunif Brecanti]], represented the co-op at a town hall with several Ghorman electors, during which time he mentioned Beldoni Valley with regard to who he represented.<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> The valley shared its name with the [[Beldoni loom]], a device used to weave [[robe]]s worn by [[Sern sector]] [[senator]] [[Bal-Cobor Sampurn]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="SWTATVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref>
