The '''74-Z Speeder Bikes Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ207''') is an expansion to the miniatures game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]].'' An updated version of the [[74-Z Speeder Bikes Unit Expansion]], it was announced on [[March 28]], [[2025]] and released on [[May 15]], [[2026]].

==Publisher summary==
''The howl of a 74-Z Speeder Bike racing past is not something that’s easily forgotten. When deployed onto the battlefield, their sheer speed often provides tactical advantage, preventing the enemy from taking a clean shot. This expansion for Star WarsTM: Legion contains 2 74-Z Speeder Bike miniatures, enough for 1 Support unit as well as cards to support gameplay.''

*''HIGH-SPEED IMPERIAL ASSAULT: Deploy 74-Z Speeder Bikes to outmaneuver enemy forces and strike quickly across the battlefield.''
*''SUPPORT UNIT EXPANSION: Includes 2 Speeder Bike miniatures, enough to field 1 fast-moving Support unit in your Galactic Empire army.''
*''TACTICAL MOBILITY ADVANTAGE: Utilize superior speed to reposition, flank opponents, and reduce incoming fire with evasive maneuvers.''
*''DETAILED STAR WARS MINIATURES: Features 2 finely sculpted 74-Z Speeder Bike models with bases, ideal for assembly and painting.''
*''READY FOR GAMEPLAY: Includes 1 Unit Card, 1 punch sheet, and 1 insert; requires the Star Wars: Legion Core Set to play.''

==Contents==
*2 Miniatures
*1 Unit Card
