The '''First Order Short-Range Evacuation Vehicle''', also known as '''SREV''', was a type of [[escape pod]] found on board [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers]]. The craft were large enough to accommodate a single [[First Order Fleet Transport]].<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />

A group of [[Resistance]] recruits and [[Lieutenant]] [[Bek]] once fled a destroyer in SREVs following their ship's capture after leaving [[Batuu]]. They escaped during a [[Battle of Batuu|battle]] between [[First Order]] forces and Resistance ships led by [[Poe Dameron]]. The pods made a rough landing back at the Resistance base in the [[Black Spire Ruins|ancient ruins]] on Batuu.<ref name="Rise of the Resistance" />

==Behind the scenes==
The First Order Short-Range Evacuation Vehicle first appeared in the [[Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance|Star Wars: ''Rise of the Resistance'']] attraction, where it utilizes two different ride systems to create the attraction's finale in the form of a motion simulator base inside of a freefall drop elevator. The Fleet Transport that guests are riding in parks itself inside the elevator and drops to return to the first floor, after which the flight simulator goes into motion to create the action for the return to Batuu.<ref name="OCR">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ocregister.com/2019/12/13/disney-reveals-how-the-rise-of-the-resistance-escape-pod-works-in-star-wars-galaxys-edge|text=Disney reveals how the Rise of the Resistance escape pod works in Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|author=MacDonald, Brady|date=2019-12-13|work=[[Wikipedia:Orange County Register|The Orange County Register]]|archivedate=20201201124750}}</ref> The vehicles resemble an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] escape pod seen in the [[2017]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''.<ref name="RBattlefront II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>
