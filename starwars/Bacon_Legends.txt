'''Bacon''' was a type of [[Foodstuff/Legends|food]]<ref name="Dex" /> the [[Besalisk/Legends|Besalisk]]<ref name="Star Wars Character Encyclopedia" /> [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]] enjoyed and served in [[Gender/Legends|his]] [[Restaurant/Legends|restaurant]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] of [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]], [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]], before<ref name="Dex" /> the establishment's destruction during the [[19 BBY/Legends|first]] [[18 BBY/Legends|two]] [[Standard year/Legends|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire's]] [[Imperial Era/Legends|rule]].<ref name="DexEra">''[[The Last of the Jedi: Underworld]]'' establishes that [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] was destroyed by the [[Galactic Empire/Legends|Galactic Empire]]. ''[[The Essential Reader's Companion]]'' dates that novel to the [[Standard year/Legends|year]] [[18 BBY/Legends|18 BBY]]. ''[[The New Essential Chronology]]'' states that the Empire was formed in [[19 BBY/Legends|19 BBY]], so [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dex's]] Diner was destroyed either during 19 [[Galactic Standard Calendar|BBY]] or 18 BBY.</ref> Varieties included [[tailring bacon]], which Jettster used as a topping on his [[Socorro slider]], and [[nuna bacon]]. The latter was served on its own as a breakfast side dish, with [[Anoat oat]]s as the second [[Pantoran Plate Specials|Pantoran Plate Special]] breakfast, in [[Shawda Clubb sandwich]]es, and in [[Besh-Leth-Trill sandwich]]es.<ref name="Dex" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bacon was mentioned in a [[2009]] [[Hyperspace: The Official Star Wars Fan Club|Hyperspace]]-exclusive release, "[[Dining at Dex's]]," which was written by [[Gregory Walker]] in the style of an [[in-universe]] menu for Dex's Diner.<ref name="Dex" /> The varieties of bacon named on the menu were intentional references to various bits of ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars Legends|lore]],<ref name="Blog-narbflick|11">{{Blog|narbflick|11|Dining at Dex's: The Story Behind the Story}}</ref> but it did not necessarily specify any connection between those pre-existing elements and the bacon. For example, there is no explicit connection between the [[tailring]] or [[nuna]] and the varieties named for them.<ref name="Dex" />

A [[2013]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars: Tiny Death Star]]'', included an item called the [[Krayt bacon burger]], but did not provide an explicit connection to the pre-existing bacon.<ref name="Tiny Death Star">''[[Star Wars: Tiny Death Star]]''</ref>
