By around that year,<ref name="Date" /> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Rooper Nitani]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Silandra Sho]], and the [[church droid]] [[P3-7A]] went on a [[Mission to Penumbra|mission]] to the floating [[city]] [[Penumbra (city)|Penumbra]].<ref name="Missing Pieces">{{StoryCite|book=Star Wars Insider: The High Republic: Tales of Enlightenment|story=Tales of Enlightenment: Missing Pieces}}</ref> Around that year<ref name="Date" /> Nitani made a [[pilgrim]]age to the [[Jedha City|Holy City]] on [[Jedha]] and visited the [[Cantina|bar]] [[Enlightenment]].<ref name="Missing Pieces" />
