'''Batuuan Beef Pot Roast''' was a [[Foodstuff|meat]] dish served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] at [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="GE date">The events within [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] take place in [[34 ABY]], per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> It was suitable for consumption by [[human]]s.<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] made it with [[Batuuan]] [[cow]]s that were grass-fed on [[Naboo]], along with [[mushroom]]s, [[Plant|greens]], and pasta.<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

==Behind the scenes==
Batuuan Beef Pot Roast was introduced in the [[In-universe|real world]] as [[Braised Shaak Roast]] at the Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo eatery in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]],<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/docking-bay-7-food-and-cargo/menus/lunch-and-dinner|text=Disneyland: Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo: Lunch And Dinner Menu}}</ref> where it has been available with differing names since the opening in [[2019]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=galaxys-edge-announcements|text=Everything You Need to Know About ''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge}}</ref> The dish consists of a beef pot roast with cavatelli pasta, wilted kale, and mushrooms.<ref name="Menu" /> A recipe for the dish as Braised Shaak Roast is included in the 2019 recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]]. Unlike the in-universe version of Braised Shaak Roast,<ref name="Cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref> Batuuan Beef Pot Roast does not use [[shaak]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />
