The '''''Berenko''-class speeder''',<ref name="Starfighters" /> also known as '''gondola speeders''', were a type of [[Water|aquatic]] vehicle used in the [[Lake Country]] region of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. [[Paddy Accu]], the caretaker of the [[Varykino]] island retreat,<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> used a [[Color|yellow]] gondola speeder to [[transport]] [[senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] and [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] to Varykino shortly before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|outbreak]] of the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref>

Those gondola speeders shared their name<ref name="Starfighters" /> with the [[noble]] [[House Berenko]]<ref name="TTaT">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=4|story=The Trouble at Tibrin}}</ref> and the [[poet]] [[Omar Berenko]].<ref name="Complete Locations">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>

{{Veh-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
A ''Berenko''-class gondola speeder first appeared in the [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones (novelization)|novelization]] of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] by [[R. A. Salvatore]] in [[2002]], shortly before the film itself was released. The vehicle's name was confirmed to be [[canon]] by the reference book ''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]'', which was released in [[2015]].
