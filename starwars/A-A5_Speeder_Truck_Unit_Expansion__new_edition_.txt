The '''A-A5 Speeder Truck Unit Expansion''' ('''SWQ92''') is an expansion to the tabletop game ''[[Star Wars: Legion]]'' by [[Atomic Mass Games]]. An updated version of the [[A-A5 Speeder Truck Unit Expansion]], it contains one miniature of an [[A-A5 Speeder Truck]] and will be released on [[August 15]], [[2025]] as part of the overhaul of the game.

==Publisher's summary==
''The A-A5 Speeder Truck provides Star WarsTM: Legion players with a robust and versatile heavy transport. Rebel players will find that this vehicle greatly improves the mobility of their troops across the battlefield while also being able to provide covering fire with its mounted weapons. Additionally, its strong armor plating can provide cover for rebel troops during intense blaster fights.''

''The A-A5 Speeder Truck can be assembled with its side door open or closed and outfitted with either a quad laser or a heavy laser retrofit, allowing players to personalize the way they want their truck to look on the battlefield. In addition to these cosmetic customizations, the included upgrade cards allow players to decide how the A-A5 Speeder Truck operates in battle!''
