During the early [[Clone Wars]], a '''battle''' occurred on the [[moon]] of [[Caliban]] after [[General]] [[Grievous]] [[Invasion of Caliban|invaded]] it. Captured by Grievous, [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and members of his [[212th Attack Battalion]] allied with the native [[Qubey]]s. The [[Jedi]] and his [[clone trooper]]s were able to escape after reuniting a Qubey family.<ref name="Battle Tales 5" />
