The '''Bratric''' were a species mentioned by [[Jod Na Nawood]] when introducing the [[youngling]]s [[Wim]], [[Neel]], [[KB]], and [[Fern]] while they were disguised as the "Wise Elders" in order to gain entry to the [[Skull Ridge Mountain Hotel & Spa]]. According to Na Nawood the Bratric physiology did not allow them to speak or understand [[Galactic Basic Standard|Galactic Basic]]. In order to reinforce this, the [[Skeleton Crew|group]] spoke gibberish to each other while around the [[Unidentified Lanupa hotelier|Lanupa hotelier]].<ref name="Learn About Pirates" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The Bratrics were first mentioned in "[[You Have a Lot to Learn About Pirates]]," the fifth episode of the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'', which aired on [[December 24]], [[2024]].<ref name="Learn About Pirates" /> According to the episode's subtitles the crew speaks gibberish when the impersonating the Bratric Wise Elders however the episode doesn't specify if Bratrics were a real species in [[the galaxy]] or entirely made up as part of the crew's con.
