The '''Coxixia system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Core Worlds]]. It was located in grid square M-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]],<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|other=[[Coxixian Prayer for the Departed]]|system=Coxixia [[Star system|system]]}}</ref> the [[Coxixian Prayer for the Departed]].<ref name="GotW">[[Guardians of the Whills (novel)|''Guardians of the Whills'']]</ref>

{{System-stub}}
