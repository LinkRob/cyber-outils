'''Bladder-bugs''' were a type of hissing [[insect]] that lived in the [[forest]]s and canyons of the [[planet]] [[Akiva]].

While on [[Kashyyyk]], [[Norra Wexley]], a native of Akiva, contrasted how the forests of Kashyyyk were silent after the [[Galactic Empire]]'s devastation, whereas the [[Great Basin|Canyon of Akar]] on her [[homeworld]] was loud with the sounds of [[atele]]s, [[clever-bird]]s, and bladder-bugs.<ref name="LD" />
