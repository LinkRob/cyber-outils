'''C1-1KR''', nicknamed "'''Checker'''," was a [[C1-series astromech droid]] belonging to the [[Royal Naboo Security Forces]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], Checker was assigned by [[Captain]] [[Talsma]] to escort [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] a short distance when she walked from a [[hangar]] to the location where [[Tomarian]] was throwing [[Tomarian's Annual Festival of Light Gala|his annual Festival of Light gala]]. When Amidala arrived at the party, Checker was unable to ascend the steps of the building, but gave Amidala a [[comlink]] so she could call the [[droid]] later.<ref name="Incident Part 1" />
