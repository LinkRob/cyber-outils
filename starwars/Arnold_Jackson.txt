'''Arnold "Arnie" Jackson''' was a prop builder who worked at the [[Wikipedia:Metro-Goldwyn-Maye|Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer]] studios in [[Wikipedia:Borehamwood|Borehamwood]] in the [[1940s]]. By the time the original [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'']] movie was being produced, Jackson was retired. Nevertheless, he assisted [[John Mollo]] with many costume accessories for the movie. He notably made two belts worn by [[Carrie Fisher]] as [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], one for her diplomatic gown and the other for her ceremonial attire.
