'''Akar Duel''' was a [[Duros]] who lived on [[Lotho Minor]]. He created many [[Monster droid|junk droids]] from [[droid]] parts, forcing them to serve him with the use of [[restraining bolt]]s. With the help of the droids [[Teetee]] and [[Doc]], along with the arrival of [[C-3PO]] aboard the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', the droids fought back against Duel and were freed from him.<ref name="Misfit Droids" />
