"'''Broken Orbits: Depths of Dorumaa'''" was a roleplaying-game adventure in the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|''Living Force'']] campaign. It was Episode I of the [[Broken Orbits trilogy]] and was written by [[Michael S. Webster]].
