The '''Axis desk''' was an investigative desk opened by the [[Imperial Security Bureau]]'s [[Investigations]] branch to focus on movements of a rebel leader that the bureau dubbed "[[Luthen Rael|Axis]]".<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" /> Originally run by [[ISB Supervisor]] [[Dedra Meero]] of the [[ISB Board]] in [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Sagrona Teema">{{Andor|Sagrona Teema}}</ref> it was later handed off to Supervisor [[Avril Heert]], Meero's former aid turned fellow member of the board, who ran it during [[3 BBY]]. In the same year, Heert took an interest in some of the weapons recovered from a number of raids the ISB had conducted in Supervisor [[Lagret]]'s sector.<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Axis desk was mentioned in the [[Andor Season 2|second season]] of the live-action series ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''. It was first indirectly mentioned in the season's second episode "[[Sagrona Teema]]" when Meero was transfered from working it to working on the ISB's Ghorman project.<ref name="Sagrona Teema" /> It was later identified as the Axis Desk in the season's fourth episode, "[[Ever Been to Ghorman?]]."<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman" />
