A '''clone medic''' was a [[clone trooper]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] that served as a [[medic]]. [[Kix]] was a [[Cloning|clone]] medic in the [[501st Legion]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|kix|Clone Trooper Kix}}</ref> and wore the symbol of the [[Republic Medic Corps]] on his left shoulder.<ref name="Join the Battle">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==Description==
===Role===
[[Cloning|Clone]] [[medic]]s<ref name="Saleucami">{{SWE|locations|saleucami|Saleucami}}</ref> were [[clone trooper]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] created by the [[Kaminoan]]s of the [[planet]] [[Kamino]], trained specifically to treat fellow clones of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]].<ref name="SWGOH">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes]]''</ref> When the health of troopers is in question, clone medics outrank all other troopers in a team, including [[captain]]s.<ref name="The Deserter" /> They were organized under the [[Republic Medic Corps]]<ref name="Join the Battle" /> in addition to whatever [[infantry]] unit they were assigned.<ref name="Darkness">{{TCW|Darkness on Umbara}}</ref>

===Equipment===
[[File:KixFull-SWE.png|thumb|right|200px|Clone medic Kix, who donned the blue markings of the 501st along with the red symbol of the Republic Medic Corps.]]
Medics were equipped with portable [[medical droid]]s, a medical [[crate]] of specialized field equipment, and a [[backpack]] of medical equipment. Among the medical equipment was a variety of [[bacta]] supplies<ref name="SWGOH" /> including [[bacta grenade]]s<ref name="Journeys" /> and [[bacta patch]]es.<ref name="The Deserter" /> Aside from bacta gear, medics carried [[medpac]]s<ref name="Journeys" /> and slings for injured [[Limb|arms]].<ref name="The Deserter" /> While prioritizing the medical well-being of their brothers, clone medics were also equipped with [[DC-15A blaster carbine]]s for combat, and later utilizing [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster]]s.<ref name="Journeys" />

At the start of the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and Galactic Republic, standard clone medics were distinguished by [[Color|red]] markings similar to [[Clone Captain]]s on their [[phase I clone trooper armor]] and the symbol of the [[Republic Medic Corps]] on their left shoulder plate.<ref name="SWGOH" /> As the war continued, clone medics donned more personalized gear, such as with unit markings.<ref name="The Deserter" /> Later in the war, clone medics, like all other clones, transitioned into [[phase II clone trooper armor]]. In their standard kit, medics kept the red markings from their former armor but also received a [[kama]] with red stripes on the bottom.<ref name="Journeys" /> The trend of personalization also continued, however, including the continued implementation of unit markings.<ref name="Darkness" />

In the field, clone medics could make use of stretchers capable of being attached to the side of [[BARC speeder]]s.<ref name="The Deserter" />

==History==
{{Dialogue|Hardcase|Commando droids took a potshot that would have gone straight through your heart had it been two inches to the left.|Rex|I can't move my arm.|Kix|You have some nerve damage.|attr=Clones discuss Rex's injuries|src=<ref name="The Deserter" />}}
[[File:KixExaminesRex.png|thumb|left|300px|Kix assesses Rex's blaster wound]]
When pursuing the Separatist [[General]] [[Grievous]] on the [[planet]] [[Saleucami]]<ref name="The Deserter" /> in [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> the clone medic [[Kix]] of the [[501st Legion]] accompanied [[Jedi General]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. Under the command of Clone Captain [[Rex]], Kix searched for Grievous on the planet alongside [[Jesse]] and [[Hardcase]]. While searching on BARC [[speeder]]s, Rex sustained a [[blaster]] wound to the chest dealt by a pair of [[BX-series droid commando]]s. After destroying the droids, Kix checked on Rex and informed the others that he would need to remove Rex's armor to assess the full extent of the damage. Jesse suggested finding a [[farmer]]s homestead to avoid more combat and the troopers lifted their captain to a [[speeder bike]]-attached stretcher. The troopers eventually arrived at [[Lawquane's Farm]], where they were confronted by the [[Twi'lek]] [[Suu Lawquane]], who allowed the clones to use the benches in the barn.<ref name="The Deserter" />

Kix rested Rex on one of the benches and applied a bacta patch to the captain's chest. After Rex awoke, the clones explained to Rex what happened to him, stating the blast would have gone straight through his [[heart]] had it been two inches to the left. Upon struggling to move his arm, Kix informed the captain that the blast caused some nerve damage. Rex ordered the troopers to continue the search but Kix shut down the captain's request, using his medical authority to let Rex heal from his wound. After receiving [[fruit]] from Lawquane, Rex told the clones to instead continue the search without him, leaving Jesse in command. The clones left Rex at the homestead to heal and joined Kenobi in a skirmish against Grievous and his [[battle droid]]s.<ref name="The Deserter" />
