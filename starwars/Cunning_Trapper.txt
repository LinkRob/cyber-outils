The '''Cunning Trapper''' was the standard outfit owned by the [[Trandoshan]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[Charr]] that he wore for the [[Hunters of the Outer Rim]] competition on the [[planet]] [[Vespaara]].<ref name="Hunters" />

{{Clothes-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cunning Trapper was a "Base" rarity "Costume" for the Hunter Charr, which first appeared in the competitive [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]'', specifically added within update 0.18.0 during the game's soft launch. It served as half of a cosmetic set alongside the [[Cunning Scatter Gun]]. This costume was unlocked by default when Charr became playable.<ref name="Hunters" />
