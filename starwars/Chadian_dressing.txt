Now the only time diners whip out their blasters is when a bottle of this stuff is empty!|Strono Tuggs, ''The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook''|<ref name="Cookbook" />}}
'''Chadian dressing''' was a zesty,<ref name="Cookbook" /> [[Color|blue]]-colored [[dressing]].<ref name="TFAVD" /> It originated on the [[homeworld]] of the [[Chadra-Fan]] [[species]],<ref name="Cookbook" /> the [[planet]] [[Chad]].<ref name="Chadra-Fan">{{Databank|chadra-fan|Chadra-Fan}}</ref> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] was introduced to the condiment when a Chadra-Fan outlaw requested a taste of home during the time that Tuggs managed the kitchen at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]] on [[Takodana]]. Tuggs was impressed that such a simple [[sauce]] was so flavorful and that beings of diverse occupations and temperaments enjoyed it.<ref name="Cookbook" /> Bottles of the dressing were available for use by patrons dining at the castle.<ref name="Episode VII">{{Film|VII}}</ref> Sometime after the castle was destroyed during the [[Battle of Takodana]]<ref name="Cookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Tuggs published a [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]] which included a recipe for the dressing and used it as an ingredient in [[Xizor Salad]].<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chadian dressing first appeared as a prop in the [[2015]] [[sequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']].<ref name="Episode VII" /> It was first identified in the reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'' which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released the day after the film.<ref name="TFAVD" />

The dressing was included in the [[2019]] recipe book ''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Chelsea Monroe-Cassel]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe uses the ingredients [[Wikipedia:Vinegar#Spirits|white wine vinegar]], [[Wikipedia:Olive oil|light olive oil]], water, [[Wikipedia:Garlic|minced garlic]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|pepper]], dried mixed herbs such as [[Wikipedia:Oregano|oregano]] and [[Wikipedia:Marjoram|marjoram]], [[Wikipedia:Blue cheese|crumbled blue cheese]], and blue [[Wikipedia:Food coloring|food coloring]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
