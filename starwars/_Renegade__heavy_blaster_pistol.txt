The '''"Renegade" heavy blaster pistol''' was a type of [[heavy blaster pistol]] manufactured by [[SoroSuub Corporation]]. It occupied a niche similar in design to that of the [[T-6 heavy blaster pistol/Canon|T-6 "Thunderer"]] of [[BlasTech Industries]] and the [[C-10 heavy blaster pistol|C-10 "Dragoneye Reaper"]] of [[Czerka Arms]], and it featured the highest energy output of any SoroSuub [[blaster pistol]] model. More lightweight and better priced than its competition, the Renegade dominated the super-heavy [[blaster]] [[pistol]] market, however, most enthusiasts eschewed the mass-market weapon due to its poor beam cohesion and awkwardly angeled grip, and preferred pricier but higher-quality options.<ref name="Gadgets" />

Common throughout the more dangerous locales of the [[Outer Rim Territories]], the Renegade was a favored weapon of [[pirate]]s and outlaws as a result of its massive and intimidating barrel housing. It was also commonly issued to [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] [[Rebel Alliance SpecOps|SpecOps]] [[troop]]s, though most who survived a few missions were known to replace it. Many smaller species, like [[Sullustan]]s, added a folding stock and used the Renegade as a [[Blaster carbine|carbine]] instead.<ref name="Gadgets" />
