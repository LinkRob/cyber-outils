The '''citrom''' was a [[fruit]]. Its juice was both sour and sweet, with a burst of heat upon consumption. It spoiled easily. A [[bartender]] at [[Oga's Cantina]] on [[Batuu]] came up with a combination of spices and juices that perfectly mimicked the taste of citrom juice, which was turned into a beverage called [[Blurrgfire]].<ref name="cookbook">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook]]''</ref>
