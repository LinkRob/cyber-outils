'''Darth Sidious' Sith Chalice''' was an ancient [[Sith]] [[meditation]] [[incense]] [[Censer|burner]] [[Expedition to Malachor|taken]] by [[Darth Sidious]] and displayed before guests by his public persona, Sheev Palpatine&mdash;[[Supreme Chancellor]] of the [[Galactic Republic]]&mdash;in his [[Emperor's office|official suite]], where the [[Sith artifact]] formed a part of a [[Darth Sidious' collection#Under the nose of the Jedi|collection]] viewable by [[Jedi]] yet which left Palpatine's [[Sith Lord|Sith]] identity hidden.

==Description==
Like other [[Sith Chalice]]s,<ref name="Hidden Treasures">{{SW|url=news/andor-luthens-gallery-easter-eggs|text=''Andor'': 10 Easter Eggs We Found in Luthen's Gallery}}</ref> Sidious' artifact was an [[incense]] [[Censer|burner]] used by the ancient [[Sith]] in certain [[meditation]] rituals. Despite the Chalice's Sith origins, [[Jedi]] did not question its [[Darth Sidious|owner]]&mdash;who then held the [[Supreme Chancellor|leading position]] of the [[Office of the Chancellor]] and openly displayed the [[Sith artifact]]&mdash;regarding the incense burner's ties to the Sith.<ref name="Complete Locations" />

==History==
The Sith Chalice was [[Expedition to Malachor|retrieved]] from the [[planet]] [[Malachor]] by [[Darth]] Sidious in one of his earlier expeditions. Later, during the [[Republic Era|tenure]] of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]'s public persona, Sheev Palpatine, as the Supreme Chancellor of the [[Galactic Republic]], the [[Sith Lord]] placed the Chalice in the [[Emperor's office|Chancellor's Suite]] of the [[Imperial Executive Building|Senate Office Building]].<ref name="Complete Locations" />

By the [[time]] of the [[Battle of Coruscant]], Palpatine's Chancellor's Suite was furnished with several other aesthetically matching Sith artifacts from his [[Darth Sidious' collection|personal collection]]. Despite the suite's reflection of Sith culture,<ref name="Complete Locations" /> the Chancellor's Sith tendencies went away unnoticed by even<ref name="ROTS">{{Film|III}}</ref> the [[Jedi High Council]],<ref name="Complete Locations" /> whose members stepped foot into the chambers throughout the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="ROTS" /><ref name="TCW film">[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Dok-Ondars_Den-SWGE_video.jpg|thumb|right|260px|A replica chalice is present on the wall of Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities.]]
A replica of the Sith Chalice is available for purchase by guests at [[Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities]] in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]].<ref name="Den of Antiquities">{{GEAttr|Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities}}</ref> While "[[Coruscant (reference book)|Coruscant]]," a [[2021]] issue of [[De Agostini]]'s [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']] line of reference magazines, establishes that Sith Chalice came from [[Yavin 4]],<ref name="Coruscant">{{EncyclopediaCite|Coruscant}}</ref> which was the case in the ''Star Wars'' Legends storytelling,<ref name="SWCL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2005)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> [[2016]]'s [[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] established Malachor as the original site of the Sith Chalice. This article assumes the latter source is correct.<ref name="Complete Locations" />
