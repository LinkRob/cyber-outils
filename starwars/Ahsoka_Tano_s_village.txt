A '''village''' located on the [[planet]] [[Shili]] was inhabited by members of the [[Togruta]] [[species]], including a young [[Ahsoka Tano]] and her mother, [[Pav-ti Tano]].<ref name="Life and Death" /> It was located at the edge of a [[forest]]ed wilderness.<ref name="Children">{{InsiderCite|225|Children of the Galaxy (article)}}</ref>

==Description==
The village was built on a gradual slope amidst an extensive [[forest]] of [[pine]] [[tree]]s that was home to animals such as [[kybuck]]s and [[raxshir]]. A main dirt road ran through the middle of the village, which was illuminated by lamps in the evening. The village was composed of over two dozen stone buildings that had wooden shingle roofs as well as wooden windows.<ref name="Life and Death" />
