'''Forch Gomou''' was a reactor tender working in [[22 BBY]] on [[Coruscant]]. He wore a Burgundy Bodysuit.<ref name="SWVE" /> Gomou was a patron in the [[Outlander Club]], in the [[Uscru Entertainment District]], in the last years of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="AotC">{{Film|II}}</ref> Gomou had [[Color|black]] [[hair]] and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="SWVE" />
