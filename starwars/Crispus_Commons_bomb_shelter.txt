A '''bomb shelter''' was located in [[Crispus Commons]] within [[Gorse City]] on the [[planet]] [[Gorse]]. It was built by the [[Galactic Republic]] as part of the Crispus project in the event that [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] leaders [[Count of Serenno|Count]] [[Dooku]] or [[General]] [[Grievous]] ever destroyed the commons facility.<ref name="AND" /> In [[11 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[A New Dawn (novel)|''A New Dawn'']] to [[11 BBY]].</ref> the [[Twi'lek]] [[Hera Syndulla]] tracked the demolitions expert [[Skelly]] to the [[bomb]] shelter and spoke with him about combating the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="AND" />
