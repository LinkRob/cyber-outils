The '''Fifth Brother's lightsaber''' was a [[Double-bladed spinning lightsaber|double-bladed spinning]] [[lightsaber]] wielded by the [[Jedi hunter]] known as [[Fifth Brother]], similar to other double-bladed spinning lightsabers wielded by [[Inquisitorius|Imperial Inquisitors]].

==Specifications==
The [[weapon]] featured a ringed emitter which could partially detach from the cylindrical handgrip in the center, allowing the twin blades to rotate rapidly without effort from the wielder.<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.comicbookmovie.com/fansites/nailbiter111/news/?a=104334|text=The Inquisitor's Lightsaber Skills In First STAR WARS REBEL Clip|work=ComicBookMovie.com|archivedate=20140729201113}}</ref> For a [[Jedi hunter]] like an [[Inquisitor]], this function likely served as a way to intimidate opponents, as well as deflect any form of frontal attack. However, this was also the weapon's primary weakness, as a [[lightsaber]]-wielding opponent could simply destroy the emitter itself and cut the weapon in half, as was demonstrated by [[Kanan Jarrus]] during his [[Action at Mustafar|final duel]] with [[the Grand Inquisitor]] aboard the ''[[Sovereign]]''.

It could also be used to fly, spinning in a helicopter-like motion strong enough to lift the wielder off the ground.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice">{{Rebels|Twilight of the Apprentice}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Fifth_threatens_Sabine.png|thumb|left|250px|The Fifth Brother threatens [[Sabine Wren]].]]
The [[Fifth Brother]] was trained in the use of this lightsaber alongside his fellow Inquisitors in [[The Works]] on [[Coruscant]] under the tutelage of [[the Grand Inquisitor]].<ref name="Dark Lord of the Sith 6">[[Darth Vader (2017) 6|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 6]]</ref>

This lightsaber saw recurring use by the Fifth [[Brother (Inquisitorius)|Brother]] as he was dispatched by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] alongside the [[Seventh Sister]] to hunt down the [[Spectres]]. This hunt would bring the pair into several [[Lightsaber duel|duels]] with [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] [[Jedi]], engaging in [[Lightsaber combat|combat]] against the cell's Jedi members: Kanan Jarrus and his [[Padawan]] [[Ezra Bridger]], as well as the [[Phoenix Cell]]'s former Jedi leader, [[Ahsoka Tano]].

In [[3 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> whilst on [[Malachor]], Tano struck and damaged the Fifth Brother's lightsaber with one of [[Ahsoka Tano's white lightsabers|her white lightsabers]], knocking it to the ground and deactivating it. Disarmed, the Fifth Brother was immediately [[Death|slain]] by the former [[Sith Lord]], [[Maul]], falling next to his lightsaber.<ref name="Twilight of the Apprentice" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Here we have the Fifth Brother's lightsaber hilt. One of the really interesting things about this hilt is that it is interactive. So this is a hero hilt that was used by the actor, but it does also light up, which is really fun.''"<br />"''And looks very similar to what we saw in ''Star Wars Rebels''.''"<br />"''That's right.|A member of the [[Lucasfilm Archives]] and [[Kristin Baver]] discussing the lightsaber prop used in ''Obi-Wan Kenobi''|<ref name="Dark side props">{{ThisWeek|5MmJiU_fiA4|Obi-Wan Kenobi Dark Side Props, Emmy Nominations, and More!|sw_url=video/obi-wan-kenobi-dark-side-props-emmy-nominations-and-more}}</ref>}}
The Fifth Brother's lightsaber first appeared in "[[Relics of the Old Republic]]," the fourth episode of the [[Star Wars Rebels Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' animated series,<ref name="Relics">{{Rebels|Relics of the Old Republic}}</ref> which premiered at [[Wikipedia:New York Comic Con|New York Comic Con]] on [[October 8]], [[2015]].<ref name="NYCC Preview">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-rebels-season-two-preview-at-new-york-comic-con|text=''Star Wars Rebels'' Season Two Preview at New York Comic Con &ndash; Updated!|nolive=1}}</ref>

[[File:FifthBrotherLightsaberProp.jpg|thumb|right|280px|Lightsaber prop used in ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'']]
In the ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]'' television series, the lightsaber was used by [[Sung Kang]], the actor who played the Fifth Brother. In an early meeting attended by [[Dave Filoni]] and propmaster [[Brad Elliott]], they discussed how to translate the animated Inquisitor lightsabers into realistic props. Filoni advised the propmaster to imagine the live-action came first, and the animated material was an exaggerated version of that. This allowed Elliott to keep the form factor of the props established in animation and to bring them in line with props made for [[human]] [[Limb|hands]].<ref name="Weapons of Inquis">{{SW|url=news/inside-the-lucasfilm-archive-obi-wan-kenobi-props-2|text=Inside the Lucasfilm Archive: Weapons of the Inquisitorius from the ''Obi-Wan Kenobi'' Limited Series}}</ref> The prop making team did a substantial amount of research to make sure the lightsaber matched its appearance in ''Star Wars Rebels''.<ref name="Dark side props" /> The lightsaber was shifted to accommodate Kang's hands. Elliott and his team were careful to minimize the more fragile looking parts of the animated designs and they made sure that the size of the rings and handles would fit their intended character.<ref name="Weapons of Inquis" /> The hero hilt used by Kang was interactive and could light up.<ref name="Dark side props" />
