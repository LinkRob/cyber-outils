'''CoruscInfos''' was a news agency at the time of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]]. In [[0 BBY/Legends|0 BBY]], when the [[Destruction of Alderaan/Legends|destruction of Alderaan]] occurred, CoruscInfos interrupted its scheduled broadcast to transmit an official announcement of [[Emperor/Legends|Emperor]] [[Palpatine/Legends|Palpatine]].
