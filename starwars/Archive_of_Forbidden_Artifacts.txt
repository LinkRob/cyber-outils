The '''Archive of Forbidden Artifacts''' was a [[Bogan Collection]], a hidden vault of prohibited objects associated with the [[dark side of the Force]], that lay within the [[Jedi Archives|archives]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. Despite being accessible only by specific members of the [[Jedi Order]], several [[Jedi]], including [[Klias Teradine]], [[Lene Kostana]], [[Yula Braylon]], [[Dooku]], and [[Sifo-Dyas]], had broken into the vault. After the fall of the [[Galactic Republic]] in [[19 BBY]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Darth Sidious]] inherited the Bogan Collection.

The Archive of Forbidden Artifacts was a [[Bogan Collection]], a hidden vault located in the [[Jedi Archives]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]],<ref name="Dooku" /> accessible only by specific members of the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="ASotC">{{InsiderCite|199|A Seat on the Council}}</ref> [[Kaneer's window]], a fake window that served as a door, hid a corridor which contained several [[spinner-web]]s and led to the vault. Inside the Bogan Collection were several cabinets that contained artifacts connected to the [[dark side of the Force]], including the [[Saber of Darth Krall]], scrolls,<ref name="Dooku" /> the [[Mask of Lord Momin|mask]] of the [[Sith Lord]] [[Darth Momin]], diverse [[lightsaber]]s,<ref name="SW" /> a [[parang]], and other artifacts relating to the [[Sorcerers of Tund]] and the [[Yacombe]]. Some of the relics were suspended on [[repulsor pad]]s, while others were kept behind [[glass]] or protected by [[force field]]s.<ref name="Dooku" /> "Bogan" was another name for the dark side of [[the Force]].<ref name="SIS">''[[Star Wars Rebels: Steps Into Shadow]]''</ref>

==History==
{{Quote|And it's not just Sith. Look at this. The Sorcerers of Tund. The Yacombe. Where did all this come from?|Sifo-Dyas, to Dooku while exploring the Bogan Collection|audio=TundYacombe.flac|<ref name="Dooku" />}}
During the [[High Republic Era]], the [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Klias Teradine]] entered the Archive of Forbidden Artifacts and documented what he saw in his [[Teradine's holojournal|holojournal]].<ref name="Dooku" /> Though Teradine [[Death|died]] in [[241 BBY]] on [[Mount Bikja]],<ref name="Date-Klias">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the flashback events of ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures — The Monster of Temple Peak]]'', including [[Klias Teradine]]'s [[death]] on [[Mount Bikja]], to [[241 BBY]].</ref> he was subsequently believed by many Jedi to have been expelled from the Jedi Order.<ref name="Dooku" /> In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[Jedi Master]] [[Cohmac Vitus]] and [[Jedi Knight]] [[Orla Jareni]] brought a [[Binding statues|set of arcane statues]] that caused a [[disturbance in the Force]] back to Coruscant to store them in the Bogan Collection.<ref name="SW" /> However, the statues were discovered to have been restraining a [[Drengir|dark side presence]] instead of containing one during a cleansing ritual in the [[Shrine in the Depths]], leading Vitus, Jareni, and Padawan [[Reath Silas]] to covertly attempt to [[Mission on the Amaxine station|return]] them to the [[Amaxine Space Station]] where they had been found.<ref name="ITD">''[[The High Republic: Into the Dark]]''</ref> Sometime later, when they were [[Jedi Initiate]]s, [[Yula Braylon]] and [[Lene Kostana]] broke into the Bogan Collection through Kaneer's window, but were eventually discovered by Jedi [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]]. As punishment, Yoda forced them to translate the [[Lothal Papyri]], a series of [[Farm|agricultural]] treatises.<ref name="Dooku" />

[[File:EmperorAmused-Orientation.png|thumb|right|260px|Darth Sidious inherited the Archive of Forbidden Artifacts after the Republic's fall.]]
A while later,<ref name="Dooku" /> in [[88 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Jedi Initiates [[Dooku]] and [[Sifo-Dyas]] snuck into the vault using the [[Shifting Sand]] [[Force power]] to open Kaneer's window and saw several artifacts. Dooku's attention was captured by a piece of [[metal]], and he soon started to hear the growl of the [[Tirra'Taka]], a creature that, according to a legend, held the planet [[Serenno]] together. Dooku had a [[Force vision|vision]] of the beast and, to protect himself, [[Force push]]ed everything around him, including the cabinets and Sifo-Dyas. Shortly afterward, the two Initiates were rescued by [[Jedi librarian]] [[Restelly Quist]], who brought them to the [[Jedi Temple medical bay|Temple infirmary]], where they were healed and questioned by Yoda and Braylon. Braylon informed Kostana that, because of Dooku, many artifacts were ruined and needed to be incinerated. Dooku and Sifo-Dyas also received the translation of the Lothal Papyri as punishment for their actions. Dooku later recounted the incident to his [[Gender|sister]] [[Jenza]] via [[holo-letter]].<ref name="Dooku" />

Around [[23 BBY]],<ref name="DJL-JournalsDate">{{DookuJediLostDate}}</ref> Dooku gave [[Datacard|data disks]] of his conversations with Jenza to his recently recruited [[Sith assassin]]&mdash;[[Asajj Ventress]]&mdash;for her [[Mission to assassinate Jenza|investigation]] into Jenza's location, and she listened to the message mentioning the Bogan Collection.<ref name="Dooku" /> After the fall of the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="SW" /> in [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Sith Master|Master]] of the [[Sith]] Order, [[Darth Sidious]], inherited the Archive of Forbidden Artifacts<ref name="SW" /> and turned the Jedi Temple into the Imperial Palace.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> The [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] later gifted his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]], with the mask of [[Darth]] Momin.<ref name="SW" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Archive of Forbidden Artifacts appeared in a flashback in the audio drama ''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]]<ref name="Dooku" /> and published on [[April 30]], [[2019]].<ref name="Penguin-Download">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/607498/dooku-jedi-lost-star-wars-by-cavan-scott/9780593102817|text=Dooku: Jedi Lost (Star Wars)}}</ref> The capitalization of the location was confirmed by the audio drama's [[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|script release]]<ref name="DJL script">[[Dooku: Jedi Lost (script)|''Dooku: Jedi Lost'' script]]</ref> on [[October 1]] of the same year.<ref name="Penguin-Download" />
