'''Alpha One''' was the highest security level in use by the [[Imperial Military]] on the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|DS-1 Death Star Mobile Battle Station]]<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men">{{StoryCite|story=Of MSE-6 and Men|book=From a Certain Point of View}}</ref> in [[1 BBY]].<ref name="Galactic Atlas">"[[Of MSE-6 and Men]]" occurs simultaneously with the events of {{Film|IV}} that ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]'' dates its beginning to [[1 BBY]].</ref> Alpha-One-level clearance empowered an [[Imperial officer]] to supersede all other active commands, directives, and subroutines in a [[droid]], and utilize [[Imperial Protocol Alpha One]] to establish a new priority protocol. As the [[commander]] of the Death Star [[battle station]], [[Grand Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] held that clearance level.<ref name="Of MSE-6 and Men" />
