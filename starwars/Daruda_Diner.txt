'''Daruda Diner''' was a [[Restaurant|diner]] in the [[city|town]] of [[Jaunta's Hope]] on the [[planet]] [[Toshara]] in [[3 ABY]].

==Description==
Daruda Diner was a [[Restaurant|diner]] in the [[city|town]] of [[Jaunta's Hope]] on the [[planet]] [[Toshara]] in [[3 ABY]]. It was a round building with six booths and a line bar. The building had a basement that became a [[Cantina|speakeasy]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

==History==
The diner was originally run by a retired mechanic however it eventually became an interest for criminals that wanted to conduct business away from the [[Pyke Syndicate]] in [[Mirogana]]. By 3 ABY, the diner's basement had been converted into a speakeasy where [[Galactic Empire|imperials]] and criminals would make profitable deals. 

During 3 ABY the diner's menu consisted of [[Fompa bread]], [[Cushnip]], [[Borgola stew]], [[Cerulean yam stew]], and [[Empona noodles]]. The [[Hutt Clan|Hutt Cartel]] [[contract broker]], [[Verrick]] operated out of the diner during that same year.

==Behind the scenes==
Daruda Diner was created for the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. Prior to the game's release, the diner was shown during a gameplay preview released on [[June 13]], [[2023]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
