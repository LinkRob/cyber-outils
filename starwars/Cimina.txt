[[Lady]] '''Cimina''' was a [[Tholothian]] [[Gender|female]] who visited the ''[[Halcyon]]'' with her grandfather, [[Shorr Komrrin]]. When they arrived onboard the [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser]] was [[Attack on the Halcyon|attacked]] by the [[pirate]] [[Crimson Jack]].<ref name="HL1" />

==Biography==
During the [[First Order-Resistance War]], Cimina and her grandfather, [[Shorr Komrrin]], took a trip onboard the [[Corellian]] [[MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruiser]] known as the [[Halcyon]]. Once on the ship, Cimina met [[D3-O9|Deethree-Ohnine]], a droid that served aboard the [[Star Cruiser]], and asked to see the [[Lightsaber Training Pod]] before being reminded by her grandfather that they should probably go to their cabin first in order to settle in. The Halcyon was eventually attacked by a group of pirates lead by [[Crimson Jack]]. Seeing [[Crimson Jack's starship]] on her window, Cimina started to get worried but D3-O9 comfort her by telling her that the Halcyon had already faced way more dangerous situations before, the droid told the young lady about another attack on the Halcyon that occurred during the [[High Republic Era]]. After hearing the story, Cimina realized that Jack's ship started firing at them.<ref name="HL1" />

Despite it only being a warning shot, Cimina still expressed her fears to her grandfather. Crimson Jack contacted [[Captain]] [[Riyola Keevan]] and asked her to allow them to take a [[resistance]] spy that was inside the Halcyon. The Captain seemingly accepted when in reality she wanted the pirate ship to get closer so they could fire at it while they traveled right into a massive solar storm to avoid counter attacks. As the Halcyon approached the solar storm, Deethree-Ohnine told Cimina and her grandfather that they needed to move towards a muster station in order to avoid the radiation. The droid told Cimina another story from the [[Republic Era]] related to the star cruiser but once again all it did was confuse her.<ref name="HL2">[[Halcyon Legacy 2|''Halcyon Legacy'' 2]]</ref>

While the Halcyon passed through the solar storm, Cimina told D3-O9 that the next story she tells should be one with action, romance and a [[Jedi]]. The droid then told the young lady a story about [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]. After the story, Cimina's grandfather told her that he'll be disembarking from the ship in order to deal with the pirates.<ref name="HL3">[[Halcyon Legacy 3|''Halcyon Legacy'' 3]]</ref>
