An '''attack''' took place on a [[pirate]] [[Unidentified asteroid mining station|base]] by the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The proximity of the base to the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet's]] [[Unidentified rendezvous point|rendezvous point]] following the [[Battle of Hoth|evacuation of Hoth]] threatened their safety. [[Commander]] [[Wedge Antilles]] led the newly reformed [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]] in attack to destroy the base and all its pirates.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />

==Prelude==
{{Quote|The pirates have compromised our security at a time when we're still waiting for high-value Alliance personnel to arrive. That gives us two alternatives. The first is we jump to the backup muster point and hope the missing can find us. The second is we destroy the pirates. We've chosen to destroy them.|Wedge Antilles, briefs the squadron|<ref name="Date" />}}
Shortly after the [[Battle of Hoth|evacuation of Hoth]] in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Seventh Division]]<ref name="Star Wars 8">[[Star Wars (2020) 8|''Star Wars'' (2020) 8]]</ref> of [[Rebel Alliance Navy]] had regrouped at a [[Unidentified rendezvous point|predetermined rendezvous point]] and were still waiting for high-value personnel to return. Fearing that leaders such as [[Luke Skywalker]] was never going to return, [[the Contessa]] promoted [[Wedge Antilles]] to the rank of [[commander]] and tasked him with rebuilding his squadron. Antilles, along with his newly appointed [[executive officer]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Wes Janson]], recruited pilots into a reformed [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red Squadron]]. They were given only days to complete their training before being assigned a mission. A nearby asteroid housing an [[Unidentified asteroid mining station|old mining station]] was being used as a [[pirate]] base, which threatened the safety of the Alliance's rendezvous point, and therefore had to be destroyed. Red Squadron initially launched a scouting mission to gather intelligence and plan the mission over the following day.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />

==Battle==
{{Quote|Landing field's locked in, watch your vectors&mdash;Wes's and Bela's flights will be coming in at twelve o'clock. Stay out their flight path.|Wedge Antilles, to his flights|<ref name="Date" />}}
Antilles, led the squadron, in a pincer maneuver on the pirate base, using two flights of three X-wings that converged from opposite directions with the objective of total destruction. The squadron was split into four flights: Antilles' and Janson's flights hit the targets on the landing field, while Scotian's and Elar's flights ran interference for Antilles' and Janson's flights respectively. [[Sila Kott]] and [[Tomer Darpen]] formed Antilles's wingmates, [[Keyser Salm]] and [[Barlon Hightower]] were Janson's, [[Ix Ixstra]] and [[Cinda Tarheel]] were [[Will Scotian]]'s, and finally [[Grizz Frix]] and [[Penn Zowlie]] were [[Bela Elar]]'s. The pirates launched [[Z-95 Headhunter]]s, a [[Nighthawk fighter]] and an interceptor, resulting in a dogfight with the squadron's X-wing cover. Janson's torpedos took out the hangar, however Elar spotted a modified [[freighter]], the pirate mothership, that tried to escape. Antilles, however, managed to destroy it before it was able to make the jump to hyperspace.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|Your bravery has given us something precious in wartime&mdash;time. To gather those who have yet to find us. To recoup our strength. And to ensure that those we've lost will not have died in vain.|Mon Mothma, to Red Squadron|<ref name="Date" />}}
Of the twelve pilots that participated, only nine returned from the mission to the fleet's flagship ''[[Home One]]'', after the deaths of Barlon Hightower, Ix Ixstra and Penn Zowlie. However, the mission was ultimately successful and the Alliance's rendezvous point remained safe.<ref name="Rendezvous Point" /> Ultimately, Organa, Skywalker, and the crew of the ''Millennium Falcon'' would reunite with the [[Fourth Division]]<ref name="Star Wars 1">[[Star Wars (2020) 1|''Star Wars'' (2020) 1]]</ref> instead of Antilles' group, only reuniting with Antilles, Red Squadron, and the Seventh Division during the [[battle at Elessia]].<ref name="Star Wars 8" /> Mothma, meanwhile, was part of the [[Eleventh Division]] by the time of the [[Battle of Ab Dalis]].<ref name="SW 15">[[Star Wars (2020) 15|''Star Wars'' (2020) 15]]</ref>
