The '''durga berry''' was a type of [[berry]] found on the astronomical object [[Hon-Tallos]]<ref name="SWA14" /> and the [[planet]] [[Dalna]].<ref name="MtD" /> During the [[High Republic Era]], durga berries grew on Hon-Tallos in [[Hon-Tallos groves|groves]]. The [[Nautolan]] [[Vroma]] took a durga berry from a grove, but she was chased through the city and pushed down by the [[human]] [[Gender|boy]] Ghenli, crushing the fruit. Vroma wanted the berry for her [[Vroma's mothers|mothers]] because it was their favorite. She then ran into [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauder]] [[Deva Lompop]], who helped her come up with a plan to get payback on the bully.<ref name="SWA14" />

At the groves, Vroma picked a durga berry from a vine as Lompop created her plan. Vroma was skeptical of the plan but went along anyways. As Ghenli and his friends chased Vroma, each one was picked off by Lompop until Ghenli remained. Lompop claimed to be the leader of the [[Grove Guardians]] and extended her mouth to scare Ghenli. Ghenli confessed to stealing the fruit and promised to never do it again and ran off. Vroma thanked Lompop for the help and ran off with the durga berry to her [[Parent|mothers]].<ref name="SWA14" />

The [[Evereni]] [[Religion|cultist]] from the [[Path of the Open Hand]] [[Marda Ro]] enjoyed a durga berry [[Snow cone|flavored ice]] with [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kevmo Zink]] from a stall on the streets of [[Ferdan]].<ref name="POD">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The durga berry first appeared in the middle grade novel ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and the e-book was published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]<ref name="Disaster">''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]''</ref> on [[January 4]], [[2022]].<ref name="Ebook & audio">{{Twitter|justinaireland|status/1478377755934867462|[[Justina Ireland]]|quote=Surprise! The ebook and audio book of Mission to Disaster released today because why not? For those wondering, Mission Disaster occurs BEFORE Fallen Star. No major spoilers if you read them out of order, though!|archivefile=File:JustinaIreland-MissiontoDisaster.png}}</ref> The berry was first pictured in "[[Tales of Villainy: A Very Nihil Interlude]]," the back story of the [[Star Wars Adventures (2020) 14|fourteenth]] and final issue of the [[2020]] [[Star Wars Adventures (2020)|''Star Wars Adventures'']] anthology [[Star Wars comic books|''Star Wars'' comic book]] series. The comic story was also written by Ireland, illustrated by [[Nick Brokenshire]] and published by [[IDW Publishing]]<ref name="SWA14" /> on [[February 9]], [[2022]]<ref name="Amazon">{{Comixology|url=Star-Wars-Adventures-2020-14/digital-comic/973690|text=Star Wars Adventures (2020-) #14|nolive=1}}</ref>

Although ''Mission to Disaster'' describes durga berries as round and purple,<ref name="MtD" /> "A Very Nihil Interlude" depicts the fruit as oblong and green with fuzzy skin.<ref name="SWA14" />
