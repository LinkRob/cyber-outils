The '''Claatuvac Guild Ceremonia Hall''' was a room used by the [[Claatuvac Guild]] for ceremonial purposes. Housed in the lower part of [[Tree Vikkilynn]], in the [[city]] of [[Kachirho]], [[Kashyyyk]], it featured a balcony and a tall, decorative arch carrying Guild symbols.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref>
