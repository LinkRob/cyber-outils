'''Alberto Santos Editor''' (or '''Alberto Santos Publishing''' in English) is the publisher for Spanish adaptations of many Star Wars novels. They publish works in Spain, which are also distributed in South America. They are the successor to [[Martínez Roca]], the publishing company from whom they bought the rights to distribute ''Star Wars'' materials in [[2001]]. Alberto Santos Editor also publishes Spanish adaptions of ''Buffy the Vampire Slayer'' as well as their own titles.
