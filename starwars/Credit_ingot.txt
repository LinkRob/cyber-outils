'''Credit ingots''' were a physical form of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]],<ref name="DV 8" /> the standard [[currency]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]<ref name="Episode I">{{Film|I}}</ref> and later the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Episode IV">{{Film|IV}}</ref> They were small, flat, rectangular bars of [[gold]]- and [[silver]]-[[color]]ed metal, the silver ones being a bit longer than the gold ones. They had symbols stamped onto their surface, including the credit symbol ({{Cr}}).<ref name="Corruption">{{TCW|Corruption (episode)}}</ref> Credit ingots could be stacked into blocks that held themselves together for transportation.<ref name="Bounty">{{TCW|Bounty (episode)}}</ref>

Another means of carrying credits was through [[credit chip]]s.<ref name="whatwookiee">[[Star Wars: What is a Wookiee? (2015)|''What is a Wookiee?'']]</ref>

== Denominational Values ==
While the values of the credits are not stated directly, mathematical deduction can provide a likely estimate using information from "[[Evil Plans]]." In the episode, [[C-3PO]] and [[R2-D2]] were given 40 credits in the form of two gold ingots and a silver ingot. Assuming that gold ingots are more valuable than the silver ingots, this leaves six possible integer value pairs: 14 and 12, 15 and 10, 16 and 8, 17 and 6, 18 and 4, and 19 and 2.

A useful currency system should be well-divisible, so 14, 17, 18, and 19 are unlikely values for the gold ingot. In the episode, C-3PO is not shown to be given change, but it is inferred that change is given as C-3PO states they have 8 credits left. This rules out 16 and 8 as the denomination values as they would have had perfect change for the vendor, yet they gave the vendor all three credit ingots which [[Anakin Skywalker|Anakin]] had given them.

This leaves the only logical value conclusion as follows:

Bronze - 1 Credit (Base)

Silver - 10 Credits

Gold - 15 Credits{{Stub}}
