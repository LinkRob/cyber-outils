The '''brora flower''' was a [[plant]] that was known for its ability to spread prolifically and cross-breed with other flowering plants. Its [[seed]]s could survive the cold [[vacuum]] of space and it was hypothesized that they needed [[hyperspace]] to be viable. [[Churo]] was performing research to see if crossing brora flowers with plants such as [[biri biri tree]]s or [[miri flower]]s would allow the new hybrids to grow more rapidly and in places the original plants could not.<ref name="VV" />
