'''Aaaaaalt''' was one of the nine months constituting a specific [[Unidentified calendar (Raspar system)|calendar]] used on a [[Unidentified planet (West Fissure)|planet]] inhabited by [[dust farmer]]s and [[Grain/Legends|grain]] distributors. It followed [[Bababbb]], preceded [[Ensi]], and, in one weather forecast for the [[Planet/Legends|planet's]] [[West Fissure]] region, was presented as the last month of the calendar. Bababbb and Aaaaaalt also coincided with the onset of [[Winter/Legends|winter]] in the West Fissure.<ref name="Monsters" />

Similarly to Bababbb, Aaaaaalt could sometimes be [[Color/Legends|pink]]-colored. Weather phenomena that could be observed during the month included [[glacial cyst]]s, which presented a danger to the West Fissure's inhabitants. At one point, a weather forecast for the region prognosticated that Bababbb and Aaaaaalt of that [[Local year|year]] would be pinker than usual and that they would become somewhat chartreuse in tone by the [[Time/Legends|time]] winter commenced. The forecast also warned of the possibility of [[Water/Legends|glacial]] cysts and recommended [[overnothing]]s for that time.<ref name="Monsters" />
