'''Bracront''' was a remote terrestrial [[moon]] in the [[Tarabba sector]] where the [[Warlord]] [[Lula Mrala-Talisola|Tartak Vil]] operated during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="THRA 1" />

{{Moon-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Bracront first appeared in the [[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 1|first issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'']]. The issue was written by [[Daniel José Older]], illustrated by [[Harvey Tolibao]] and [[Nick Brokenshire]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="THRA 1" /> Phase III on [[December 6]], 2023.<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3013-790/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III #1}}</ref> The planet was first mentioned in a [[StarWars.com]] post discussing details about the then-upcoming comic series on [[September 18]] of the same year.<ref name="FirstDetails">{{SW|url=news/dark-horse-star-wars-the-high-republic-adventures-phase-iii|text=First Details on Dark Horse's ''Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures'' Phase III}}</ref>
