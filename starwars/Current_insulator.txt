A '''current insulator''' was a circuit that would absorb and channel ion build-up. They could be installed in [[starship]]s and be used to protect a ship's cargo from ion energy.<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] paid a starship [[Technician|mechanic]] two thousand [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and supplied them with twenty [[Cargo signature scrambler]]s in order to install a current insulator in the hold of the ''[[Trailblazer]]'', her [[EML-850 light freighter]]. This upgrade allowed her and her companion [[ND-5]] to more easily [[smuggler|smuggle]] ionized cargo for the [[Miyuki Trade League]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Current insulators appeared as an upgrade in the "A Pirate's Fortune" expansion for the [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'',<ref name="Outlaws" /> which was released on [[May 15]], [[2025]].<ref name="Pirate's Fortune released">{{Ubisoft|url=en-us/game/star-wars/outlaws/news-updates/7ugpLQ1cukTrYcWgmIekyA/watch-the-new-trailer-for-star-wars-outlaws-a-pirates-fortune-releasing-may-15|text=Watch The New Trailer For Star Wars Outlaws: A Pirate's Fortune, Releasing May 15}}</ref> In the expansion, players could purchase the "current insulator" upgrade to reduce the ion build-up their ship would received while their ion cargo was unstable.
