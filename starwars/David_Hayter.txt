'''David Hayter '''(born [[February 6]], [[1960s#1969|1969]]) is a Canadian-American voice and screen actor and screenwriter. He is best known for providing the English voices of [[w:c:metalgear:Solid Snake|Solid Snake]] and [[w:c:metalgear:Big Boss|Big Boss]] (Naked Snake) in the ''[[Wikipedia:Metal Gear|Metal Gear]]'' video game series, and for writing the screenplay for [[Wikipedia:X-Men (film)|''X-Men'']] and co-writing the screenplays for [[Wikipedia:The Scorpion King|''The Scorpion King'']], [[Wikipedia:X2 (film)|''X2'']], and [[Wikipedia:Watchmen (film)|''Watchmen'']].

In [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]], he is known for the voice of the male [[Hero of Tython|Jedi Knight]] character in the [[2011]] MMORPG ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'' and its expansions.
