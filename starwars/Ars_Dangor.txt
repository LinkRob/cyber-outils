'''Ars Dangor''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[human]] official who served on the [[Imperial Ruling Council|Ruling Council]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], he served as a [[Senior Advisor]] of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]] who sometimes acted as a [[bureaucrat]], [[emissary]], or a dignitary in Palpatine's name<ref name="RotS">''[[Rise of the Separatists]]''</ref> and one time accompanied [[Janus Greejatus]], [[Sly Moore]], [[Sate Pestage]], and [[Lieutenant Commander]] [[Orson Callan Krennic|Orson Krennic]] to [[Geonosis]] to oversee [[Poggle the Lesser]]'s homecoming. As Poggle gave a speech to his fellow [[Geonosian]]s, Dangor sat alongside the other [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] emissaries in the [[Petranaki Arena]]. During the speech, Dangor expressed worries to Krennic that he felt they could become appetizers at the drop of a [[hat]].<ref name="Catalyst" />

After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the Galactic Empire, Dangor served in the Imperial Ruling Council. As such, he was an [[Emperor's adviser|adviser]] to [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] Sheev Palpatine. He wore long, braided [[Hair|mustachios]] and had broad, furrowy [[eye]]brows. Despite his mostly glum countenance, Dangor's unusual facial [[hair]] gave him a bit of élan. Like all the other members of the Imperial Ruling Council, he donned [[color]]ful [[robe]]s and floppy [[hat]]s.<ref name="Tarkin" />

In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Date">"Aldhani" takes place shortly after "[[Kassa (episode)|Kassa]]," which dates itself to [[5 BBY]].</ref> he was on the guest list for a dinner party hosted by [[Perrin Fertha]], to his [[Marriage|wife]] [[Mon Mothma]]'s chagrin, as he and [[Sly Moore]] had cut off the [[Ghorman]] shipping lanes the day before, which Mothma claimed would lead to mass starvation.<ref name="Aldhani">{{Andor|Aldhani (episode)}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Ars Dangor was introduced to the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[James Luceno]]'s [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The [[Ars Dangor/Legends|character]] was originally created for the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] short story "[[Imperial Spin]]" of the [[1989]] sourcebook ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''.<ref name="IS-IS">{{WEGCite|story=Imperial Spin|book=Imperial Sourcebook}}</ref>

In [[2003]], a production photo from five of the Advisors seen in the [[1983]] film [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] was included in [https://web.archive.org/web/20031008010828/www.starwars.com/community/askjc/jocasta/askjc20030602.html an article] published on [[StarWars.com]], accompanied by text that read "Among the members of Palpatine's Inner Circle are: [[Sate Pestage]], [[Janus Greejatus]], [[Sim Aloo]], Ars Dangor, and [[Kren Blista-Vanee]]."<ref name="Ars in RotJ?">{{SWArchive|url=community/askjc/jocasta/askjc20030602.html|text=Ask the Lucasfilm Jedi Council: Who are the gentlemen with the Emperor?}}</ref> The short story "[[Kernels and Husks]]" confirmed that Dangor was one of the advisors seen in the movie.<ref name="FACPOV">{{StoryCite|story=Kernels and Husks|book=From a Certain Point of View: Return of the Jedi}}</ref>
