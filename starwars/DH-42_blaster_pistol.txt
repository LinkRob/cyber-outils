The '''DH-42 blaster pistol''' was a model of [[blaster pistol]]. Visually similar to the [[DH-17 blaster pistol|DH-17]], the DH-42 was smaller and more compact, capable of burst firing blaster bolts in rapid succession with a great deal of accuracy. Despite this, the blaster was prone to overheating, its tip eventually becoming molten red&mdash;when this happened, the user had to vent the blaster of excess heat by racking a heat release slide on the top of the blaster before being able to fire again.<ref name="TALES" />
