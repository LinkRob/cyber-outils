'''Deak Starkiller''' was a [[Gender/Legends|male]] human and the son of [[Kane Starkiller]].

==Biography==
After the [[Showdown on Coruscant|Jedi Rebellion]], the young [[Annikin Starkiller]] and his brother Deak settled on the [[Fourth Moon of Utapau]] with their father [[Kane Starkiller]]. However, a [[Unidentified Knight of the Sith|Sith Knight]] discovered their hiding place, forcing the family to flee to [[Aquilae (The Star Wars)|Aquilae]]. He and his father had been warned earlier by Annikin, and had solved a [[Corbet dictum]] problem cube with some effort, but before they could steal the Sith Knight's ship, a Banta Four, the Sith Knight ambushed and killed Deak.
