'''Anthan Prime''', also known as '''Anthan''', was a [[gas giant]] located in the [[Anthan system]] of<ref name="Vader 9" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Braxant sector]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> It was connected by [[hyperspace route]] to the [[planet]]s [[Bastion]] and [[Bescane system|Bescane]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Above the planet floated the [[Anthan Spire]], a [[city]] used as a vacation resort for the incredibly rich.<ref name="DV9">[[Darth Vader (2015) 9|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 9]]</ref>

Anthan Prime was orbited by several [[moon]]s. [[Anthan 1]] was close enough to sit within the [[Thanteen Substrata]], a part of Anthan's own [[gas]]eous [[atmosphere]].<ref name="DV12">[[Darth Vader (2015) 12|''Darth Vader'' (2015) 12]]</ref>
