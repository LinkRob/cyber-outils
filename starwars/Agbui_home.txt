The [[Agbui]] [[species]] possessed a '''home'''.<ref name="GG" /> Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' take place around [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the Agbui agent [[Haplif]] asked his employer, the [[Grysk]] operative "[[Jixtus]]," who had tasked Haplif and other Agbui with [[Sunrise's civil war|destroying]] the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] "[[Sunrise]]," if he could return to the Agbui home. The Grysk then [[Commanding officer|commanded]] Haplif's Agbui to [[Agbui attempt to precipitate a Chiss Ascendancy civil war|destroy]] the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] before they could go home. The Agbui, however, were unable to complete their task, and Haplif was [[Death|killed]] on the Ascendancy planet [[Celwis]].<ref name="GG" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Agbui home was mentioned in the second volume of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s [[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy|''Thrawn Ascendancy Trilogy'']], [[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good|''Greater Good]]''.<ref name="GG" />
