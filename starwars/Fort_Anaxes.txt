'''Fort Anaxes''' was a [[Galactic Republic]] [[Republic Military|military]] base built inside a [[cave]] on the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. After the planet's [[Destruction of Anaxes|destruction]], the facility remained intact on [[PM-1203]], one of the [[asteroid]]s in the newly created [[Anaxes asteroid belt|asteroid belt]].

==Description==
Fort Anaxes was located in a valley on the [[planet]] [[Anaxes]], mostly built into a cavern inside a sheer cliff face, with a landing pad and outbuildings outside.<ref name="The Bad Batch">{{TCW|The Bad Batch (episode)}}</ref> After Anaxes' [[Destruction of Anaxes|destruction]] early in the [[Imperial Era]], the fort miraculously survived, remaining intact on an [[asteroid]] eventually designated [[PM-1203]].<ref name="Databank" />

==History==
{{Quote|This base was abandoned years ago, after the Clone Wars.|Hera Syndulla|<ref name="OOD">{{Rebels|Out of Darkness}}</ref>}}
[[File:Hunter_on_Anaxes.png|left|240px|thumb|Hunter on Anaxes]]
During the [[Battle of Anaxes]], the fort was the base for [[Jedi General]]s [[Mace Windu]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and [[Anakin Skywalker]]. After getting permission to infiltrate a [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[Cyber Center]] to discover why the [[Separatist Droid Army]] had been consistently maintaining the upper hand, [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]], [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Jesse]], and [[clone medic]] [[Kix]] departed from the fort along with the recently arrived [[clone commando]] squad [[Clone Force 99]]. The infiltration led to the discovery that [[Echo]],<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> an ARC trooper believed [[Death|killed]] during the [[Battle of Lola Sayu]],<ref name="Counterattack">{{TCW|Counterattack}}</ref> was still alive, with the Separatists using him for a [[Rex's battle strategy algorithm|strategy algorithm]] that accounted for their success on Anaxes.<ref name="The Bad Batch" /> [[Captain]] Rex, [[General]] Skywalker, and Clone Force 99 subsequently departed from the fort on an [[Mission to Skako Minor (Clone Wars)|unauthorized mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Skako Minor]] to rescue Echo.<ref name="A Distant Echo">{{TCW|A Distant Echo}}</ref> After his rescue, Echo went through medical treatment at Fort Anaxes before proving instrumental in a plan to defeat the Separatist forces besieging Anaxes once and for all.<ref name="Unfinished Business">{{TCW|Unfinished Business (episode)}}</ref> 

After the war, Anaxes was subjected to a cataclysm that broke apart the planet, but the fort remained preserved, becoming a [[rhydonium]] [[fuel]] depot and a popular choice for secret meetings.<ref name="Databank" /> At least four [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], a colony of [[fyrnock]]s had nested in the remains of the base, which were encountered by [[Hera Syndulla]] and [[Sabine Wren]] when they had planned to meet "[[Ahsoka Tano|Fulcrum]]."<ref name="OOD" />

A minor skirmish between the Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Kanan Jarrus]], his apprentice [[Ezra Bridger]] and a small force of [[stormtrooper]]s led by [[the Grand Inquisitor]] took place here roughly four years before the Battle of Yavin. Although the Inquisitor was able to defeat Kanan, his apprentice used the [[dark side of the Force]] to summon a [[Unidentified massive fyrnock|giant fyrnock]] to combat the Inquisitor. Both Jedi were able to escape on the ''[[Phantom]]'', destroying the Inquisitor's [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class shuttle]] in the process to prevent pursuit.<ref name="Gathering Forces" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fort Anaxes first appeared in the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[Out of Darkness]]."<ref name="OOD" /> It was conceived for the "Bad Batch" arc of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which had been in production prior to the series' initial cancellation in [[2013]].<ref name="ADE EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/a-distant-echo-episode-guide|text="A Distant Echo" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
