'''Admiral Snackbar''' was a restaurant in the [[city]] of [[Pons Ora]] on the [[planet]] [[Abafar]]. The diner was marked with a sign showing a steaming cup and the words "ADMIRAL SNACKBAR" in [[Aurebesh]].<ref name="MIA" /> Above the sign was a [[Hologram|holographic]] image of a [[Mon Calamari]] [[chef]] saying "IT'S A FRAP!"<ref name="Trivia" /> Admiral Snackbar was located next to [[Game Go]] and was across the street from [[Dr. Evazan Cosmetic Surgery]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], the building was marked with a sign which read "OUT OF BUSINESS."<ref name="MIA" /> Decades later, the motif of the Mon Calamari chef was used to decorate the [[flight suit]] of [[Hype Fazon]], a [[racer]] [[pilot]] on ''[[Colossus]]'' [[Space station|station]].<ref name="Bucket's">{{SW|url=news/designing-star-wars-resistance|text=Designing ''Star Wars'': ''Star Wars Resistance''|date=2018-10-29|quote=Nearly all of the logos on Hype's ship, racing outfit, and droid can be found in episodes of ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' and ''Star Wars Rebels''. The "79's" cantina logo comes from ''The Clone Wars'' episode "Orders"; the "Power Sliders" and "Admiral Snackbar" logos were first seen on the planet Abafar in ''The Clone Wars'' droid arc; and several of the others can be found in background advertisements throughout the two series.}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Admiral Snackbar first appeared in "[[Missing in Action]]," the twelfth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''.<ref name="MIA" /> It is a spoof of the [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VI ''Return of the Jedi'']] character [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar|Ackbar]], who is known for the iconic quote "It's a trap!"<ref name="Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi">{{Film|VI}}</ref>
