'''Cin Drallig''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|male]] [[Jedi Master]] who served the [[Jedi Order]] as [[Battlemaster]] and [[Chief of Security]] of the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. He was the head of the [[Jedi Temple Guard]] and a [[lightsaber]] instructor who was disrespectfully nicknamed "'''the Troll'''" by members of the [[Jedi]]. Drallig was killed by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] during his [[Operation: Knightfall|attack]] on the Jedi Temple.

==Biography==
===Early life and Jedi career===
[[Homeworld|Hailing]] from [[Lavisar]],<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Cin Drallig, a gifted [[Force-sensitive]] [[human]] [[Gender|male]], was [[Padawan|apprenticed]] to the [[Jedi High Council]]'s [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]].<ref name="CVDNE" /> Faithfully honing his skills and attunement to the Force, Drallig garnered many years of knowledge and experience, and eventually became the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Battlemaster]].<ref>{{ForceCollection|Cin Drallig|star=4}}</ref> Drallig also achieved the rank of [[Jedi Master]], and led the [[Jedi Temple Guard]] during the [[Clone Wars]] as the temple [[Chief of Security]]. As a Master, he was a teacher of [[lightsaber]] combat who passed his skills on to new generations of [[Jedi]],<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|cin-drallig|Cin Drallig}}</ref> such as [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="CVDNE" /> Although he was regarded as perhaps the finest duelist in the Order,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> he earned the unflattering nickname "the [[Troll]]" from other members of the Jedi Order.<ref name="Databank" />

===The Clone Wars===
====Guarding the Temple====
{{Quote|Stay with them. Keep an eye out for those mini-drone explosives.''"<br />"''Yes, Master Drallig.|Cin Drallig orders Jedi Temple Guards to stay with a group of younglings|<ref name="Kanan 8" />}}
[[File:Jedi_guards_at_rally.png|thumb|left|250px|Drallig guards the Jedi Temple during a rally.]]
In [[21 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places "[[Liberty on Ryloth]]" and "[[Children of the Force]]" in [[21 BBY]]. As this episode takes place between these two episodes according to {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}}, "[[Holocron Heist]]" must also take place in 21 BBY.</ref> [[Cad Bane]] slipped past Drallig and the Temple's security forces using specialized technology and his intelligence and managed to slip into the [[Holocron Vault]]. A Jedi [[holocron]] was stolen.<ref name="SWE" />

During the [[Cloning|Clone]] Wars, the [[Bombing of the Jedi Temple hangar|Jedi Temple was bombed]] by [[Padawan]] [[Barriss Offee]]. Before the perpetrator had been caught, the Jedi led an investigation to determine who was behind the attack.<ref name="Sabotage">{{TCW|Sabotage (episode)}}</ref> As the bombing caused great alarm, Drallig being the head of Temple security, was most concerned at how vulnerable the Jedi Order had become.<ref name="NoLive Sabotage Ep Guide">{{SW|url=explore/the-clone-wars/ep517|text="Sabotage" - ''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide|nolive=1}}</ref> Skywalker approached Drallig, who was standing guard with the Temple Guard at the gates of the Jedi Temple, where protesters were demonstrating against the war and the Jedi Order's involvement in it. Skywalker showed Drallig a [[Hologram|holographic]] image of their then-suspect, [[Jackar Bowmani]], and asked the Jedi Master to be on alert for him. This led to the suspect's wife, [[Letta Turmond]], being taken into custody after spotting the holographic image,<ref name="Sabotage" /> and the Jedi ultimately learned that Turmond was part of the plot to attack the Jedi Temple.<ref name="Knew Too Much">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref>

[[File:CinDrallig-Kanan8.png|thumb|right|250px|Drallig defends younglings from Separatist explosives, aided by two Temple Guards.]]
The Temple was later [[Bombing at the Jedi Temple|attacked]] by [[Captain]] [[Rackham Sear]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the attack, Drallig led two Temple Guards in securing the quarters of the Jedi [[youngling]]s. He arrived just in time to cut a small explosive drone in half before it detonated in a room with several younglings. He ordered the guards to stay with the younglings and destroy any more explosives that entered the area. Drallig then joined Masters [[Mace Windu]] and Kenobi, reporting to them that the younglings were safe, but that there were explosions throughout the Temple and that the source was unknown. Master [[Depa Billaba]] and [[Jedi Initiate|Initiate]] [[Kanan Jarrus|Caleb Dume]] ended the attack by confronting Sear on the roof of the Temple, where he committed suicide by leaping off the building.<ref name="Kanan 8" />

====Fall of the Jedi Temple====
[[File:CinDrallig.png|thumb|264x264px|left|Drallig dueled Darth Vader alongside Padawans Whie and Bene.]]
At the end of the war, Skywalker turned to the [[dark side of the Force]] and became Darth Vader, apprentice to [[Darth Sidious]], the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. Vader [[Operation: Knightfall|attacked the Jedi Temple]] with the [[501st Legion]] of [[clone trooper]]s, while Sidious issued [[Order 66]] to force the clones to turn against the Jedi Order. Drallig fought to defend the Jedi Temple<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref> alongside Padawans [[Whie Malreaux]] and [[Bene]], both of whom perished in [[Lightsaber duel|lightsaber combat]] with an enraged Darth Vader.<ref name="SWE" /> Drallig confronted Vader himself, but proved unable to put an end to the Sith Lord's rampage.<ref name="Episode III" />

===Legacy===
Shortly after Drallig's death, Masters Yoda and Kenobi returned to the Temple, where they witnessed a security hologram recording depicting Drallig's final moments dueling Darth Vader alongside Padawans Bene and Whie.<ref name="Episode III" />

Sometime after his death, the [[holocron of Cin Drallig]] was sold in an auction on [[Denon]] to the [[noble]] [[Rin Praji]], who kept the holocron safe at [[Rin Praji's estate|his estate]] on [[Kaikielius]].<ref name="Knights of Fate">''[[Knights of Fate]]''</ref>

==Personality and traits==
[[File:VaderDrallig-TheSecretsOfCloneTroopers.png|thumb|right|275px|Cin Drallig takes a stand against Darth Vader, his former comrade Anakin Skywalker.]]
Cin Drallig was a stoic and resolved Jedi Master, a noted leader who presided over the Temple Guard at the Jedi Temple on Coruscant. He stood at 1.74 [[meter]]s tall, or 5 [[Foot|feet]] 9 inches. He had [[Color|dark blond]] hair, blue eyes, and light skin.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
Drallig wore classic Jedi robes and boots and kept his hair long. From time to time he had protective gauntlets on his arms and hands.<ref name="Join the Battle" /> Drallig wielded a single green lightsaber.<ref name="Kanan 8" />

==Behind the scenes==
For his brief appearance in the [[2005]] film [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']], Cin Drallig was portrayed by [[prequel trilogy]] stunt coordinator [[Nick Gillard]], whose name was the [[Tuckerization|basis for the character's name]] (Cin is Nick backwards without the K, and Drallig is Gillard backwards).<ref name="Episode III" /> Drallig later reapeared in [[2013]] in "[[Sabotage (episode)|Sabotage]]," the seventeenth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season Five|fifth season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', where he was voiced by [[Robin Atkin Downes]].<ref name="Sabotage" /> For the episode a clay marquette of the character' head was modeled by [[Darren Marshall]]<ref name="Sabotage EG">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/sabotage-episode-guide|text="Sabotage" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> and Marshall also created his final model for the episode, which was finished on [[August 2]], [[2011]]. Drallig's model used the previous model of [[Jedi Knight]] [[Ky Narec]].<ref name="Marshall">{{WebCite|url=https://dmarshallart.com/portfolio/the-clone-wars-season-5/|text=The Clone Wars – Season 5|work=[[Darren Marshall]]'s website|archivedate=20260123054301}}</ref>
