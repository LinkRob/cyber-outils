The '''Frizznoth''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that lived in [[the galaxy]]. They were short in stature, and at least some members utilized giant [[mechanized battle armor]] to compensate for their small size. [[Mozeen Parapa]], head of the [[Parapa Cartel]], was a [[Gender|male]] Frizznoth.<ref name="Last Shot" />
