The '''Bool family''' was the family of the well-connected and underhanded [[Caskadag]] [[racer]] [[Kestar Bool]].<ref name="trailer 2 article">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-galactic-racer-gameplay-trailer|text=Welcome to the Galactic League with the Latest Trailer for ''Star Wars: Galactic Racer''}}</ref> Bool dominated the unsanctioned [[Galactic League (racing)|Galactic League]] racing circuit that emerged in the lawless [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[New Republic Era|following]] the [[Battle of Jakku|fall of the]] [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="SecretModeNews">{{WebCite|url=https://wearesecretmode.com/news/star-wars-galactic-racertm-launches-in-2026|text=Star Wars: Galactic Racer™ Launches in 2026|work=[[Secret Mode]]'s official website|archivedate=20251213044840}}</ref> [[Darius Pax]], who ran the League, recruited the mysterious racer [[Shade]], who held a grudge against the Bool family, in order to take Kestar Bool down.<ref name="trailer 2 article" /> Bool stood in the way of Shade's dream of glory and revenge, harboring his own power-hungry plans for the League, and becoming a ruthless racing rival.<ref name="SecretModeNews" />
