The '''Ashu''' were a prominent family of [[Mahran]] on the [[planet]] [[Mahranee]] during the [[Clone Wars]], a conflict between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], comprised of many noted [[warrior]]s and [[Politics|statesmen]].

==Description==
{{Quote|I am walking the warrior path, Mother!|Ashu-Nyamal to Rakshu on her familial path|<ref name="Dark Disciple" />}}
From the [[planet]] [[Mahranee]], this [[Mahran]] family was comprised of a [[warrior]] and [[Politics|statesmen]] path, of which family members could choose one to follow. Its members included [[Rakshu]] and her three children: Firstborn [[Ashu-Nyamal]] who followed the warrior path, Secondborn [[Teegu]] who easily soothed quarrels, and Thirdborn [[artist]]-to-be [[Kamu]].<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==History==
{{Quote|I will ''not'' die in huddled fear!|Nya's complaints to a clone trooper|<ref name="Dark Disciple" />}}
In [[19 BBY]] during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="DD Date" /> the Separatists targeted Mahranee for its plentiful resources and Republic support. They attempted to win over the planet with false promises, which the [[Mahranee government|government]] refused. The Separatists launched a mass genocide of the Mahran known as the [[Atrocity on Mahranee]]. Sent by the Republic, the family was evacuated on [[Chubor's Republic frigate|the]] [[Consular-class cruiser (Charger c70 retrofit)|''Consular-class'' cruiser]] of [[Jedi General]] [[Chubor]], crewed by [[clone trooper]]s younger than Nya. However, the [[Starship|ship]] was attacked by a Separatist [[fleet]] commanded by [[Count]] [[Dooku]], Separatist [[Head of State]] who Nya was familiar with. Chubor pleaded with Dooku to spare the lives of all the innocent families and passengers. However, the Separatists continued their attack, [[Death|killing]] the entire family. A transmission of the attack was broadcast to the [[Jedi Order]], aware of this Dooku stated that their deaths were the fault of the [[Jedi]] Order and the Galactic Republic.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />
