'''Bill Bailey''' was cast as a character who would have been called "Montross" during the production of [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="Making of">''[[The Making of Star Wars: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film]]''</ref> According to an e-mail from [[Tom Spina]], Montross was meant to be [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt's]] second-in-command, but although he tested out his outfit, he never filmed his scenes.<ref name="Tom Spina Bill Bailey confirmation">{{EmailCite|email=File:BillBaileyNOTINFILM.png|creator=Tom Spina|date=2021-06-27}}</ref>
