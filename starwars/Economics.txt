==Behind the scenes==
Economics was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in [[James Luceno]]'s [[2014]] novel [[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']].<ref name="Tarkin" /> The science's first full appearance was marked by the [[economist]] [[Kriz Tray]]'s featuring in [[Beth Revis]]'s [[2022]] novel ''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''.<ref name="Princess">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref> [[Economics/Legends|Economics]] was originally introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the ''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]'', a [[1989]] supplement written by [[Greg Gorden]] and published by [[West End Games]] for use with ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="ISB">''[[Imperial Sourcebook]]''</ref> In the real world, [[Wikipedia:Economics|economics]] is a [[Wikipedia:Social science|science]] that studies the general concepts of [[Wikipedia:Production (economics)|production]], [[Wikipedia:Distribution (economics)|distribution]], and [[Wikipedia:Consumption (economics)|consumption]] of [[Wikipedia:Goods and services|goods and services]].<ref name="Economics">{{WebCite|url=https://www.dictionary.com/browse/economics|text=ECONOMICS Definition & Meaning|work=[[Wikipedia:Dictionary.com|Dictionary.com, LLC]]|archivedate=20241204011848}}</ref>
