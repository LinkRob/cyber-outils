'''Food dispensers''' were automated feeding machines used to feed both sentient beings and non-sapient creatures. The adolescent [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lords]] [[Dromika]] and [[Quillan (Sith)|Quillan]] were known to be fed by food dispensers while residing at [[the Loft]] in the city of [[Hestobyll]] on [[Byllura]]. Food dispensers were also used to feed the thousands of children confined with the Sith Lord [[Odion]]'s [[Odionate cloister|cloisters]], a network of planet-sized mega orphanages within the [[Odionate]].
