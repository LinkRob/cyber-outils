The '''Bygone Settlement''' was a dilapidated habitation located near the [[Rambler's Reach Valley]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Koboh]] home to a group of [[Jawa]]s [[scavenger]]s.

==History==
Long before the [[Galactic Republic]] initially settled Koboh, earlier groups constructed [[stone]] and mud structures directly into the cliff sides. This settlement was eventually abandoned, and their structures were considered the oldest on the planet. By [[9 BBY]],<ref name="JSDate">{{JediSurvivorDate}}</ref> a group of Jawas had settled in the same area and built a [[Sandcrawler|digger crawler]], reminiscent of those on Tatooine.<ref name="Survivor" />

The Bygone Settlement was also the home to many varieties of [[Nekko]] and other Koboh wildlife. This included several [[bogling]]s, an invasive [[species]].<ref name="Survivor" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Bygone Settlement first appeared in the [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]'' by [[Respawn Entertainment]] as a playable area. Although first visited early in the game, after developing further skills by progressing, players had the option to follow a rumor and return to the area to explore further. Using late-game movement mechanics, players could traverse the ancient structures and approach the digger crawler. To complete the rumor, players had to fight off a team of [[Bedlam Raiders]] and [[battle droid]]s to save the Jawas. Once victorious, the door to the digger crawler would open, and players could speak to a Jawa, inviting them to set up a shop in [[Rambler's Reach Outpost]]. Then, players could climb atop the digger crawler to unlock a [[The Force|Force]] Essence, which gave them significant experience points.<ref name="Survivor" />

Other collectibles available in the Bygone Settlement included the [[Geonosian]] legs and head cosmetics for [[BD-1]], the Wanderer Jacket and the Choppy Forward hair cosmetics for [[Cal Kestis|Cal]], five [[priorite]] shards, two [[databank]] entries, and three [[seed]] pods. Additionally, one of the collectible [[fish]], the [[blue-finned crayfish]] could be collected by speaking with [[Skoova Stev]] here.<ref name="Survivor" />

The Bygone Settlement was also the home of a unique process to acquire a priorite shard, necessary to purchase every item in [[Dendra's Antiquities]]. At a random chance, one of several potential rare [[color]]s of nekko would spawn in the Bygone Settlement. In order to acquire the priorite shard, players needed to have one of the special nekko spawn, ride it back to Rambler's Reach Outpost and stable it. After a passage of [[time]], the priorite shard could be found in the droppings of the nekko.<ref name="Survivor" />
