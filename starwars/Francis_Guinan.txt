'''Francis Guinan''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] actor. He voiced the [[Minister of Intelligence (Galactic War)|Keeper]] and additional voices in the computer game ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''. He is best known for his role as the patriarch Edgar Teller in the television series [[Wikipedia:Eerie, Indiana|''Eerie, Indiana'']].

Guinan has also worked in a number other films and television shows including [[Wikipedia:Speed 2: Cruise Control|''Speed 2: Cruise Control'']], [[Wikipedia:The Last Airbender|''The Last Airbender'']], [[Wikipedia:Constantine (film)|''Constantine'']], ''[[Wikipedia:Star Trek: Enterprise]]'', and [[Wikipedia:Star Trek: Voyager|''Star Trek Voyager'']].
