'''Amy Vincent''', better known by her pen name '''Claudia Gray''',<ref name="Harper">{{Harper|url=cr-103298/claudia-gray|text=Claudia Gray}}</ref> is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] author who has written multiple novels in the ''Star Wars'' [[canon]]. Gray's previous titles include the book series [[Wikipedia:Evernight (series)|''Evernight'']], ''Spellcaster'', and ''Firebird''.<ref name="Claudia">{{WebCite|url=http://www.claudiagray.com/books|text=Books|work=[http://www.claudiagray.com ClaudiaGray.com]|archivedate=20200208121608}}</ref>

She namely wrote the novels ''[[Lost Stars]]'', [[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']], ''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]'', and ''[[Master & Apprentice]]''. She worked on ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' with [[Cavan Scott]], [[Charles Soule]], [[Daniel José Older]], and [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="Celebration">{{StarWarsShow|sP6SaKn2WuM|Star Wars Celebration Chicago 2019 Live Stream - Day 4 &#124; The Star Wars Show LIVE!}}</ref>
