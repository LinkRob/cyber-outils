During the [[Galactic Civil War]], eleven [[Standard month|months]] after the [[Battle of Endor]] in [[5 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> an '''attack''' led by [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] was launched on the [[planet]] [[Dybbron III]] as part of [[Operation: Cinder]]. The attack resulted in the acquisition of a [[pirate]] [[gunship]] from the evidence yards on the planet, as well as the recruitment of [[Heirorius]] and [[Starzha]] into the [[fighter wing]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />
