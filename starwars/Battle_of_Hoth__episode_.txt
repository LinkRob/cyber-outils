"'''Battle of Hoth'''" is the forty-seventh episode of ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures]]''. It premiered on the [[StarWarsKids.com|''Star Wars Kids'']] YouTube channel on the 40th anniversary of the release of [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']] on [[May 21]], [[2020]]. It was later compiled into an omnibus YouTube video called "The Empire Strikes Back 40th Anniversary", that was released on [[June 19]], [[2020]].<ref name="TESB">{{SWKidsYouTube|n2IF0DMFKxg|The Empire Strikes Back 40th Anniversary &#124; Star Wars Galaxy of Adventures}}</ref>

==Plot summary==
The [[Tom Kane|narrator]] announces that the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] strikes back as [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] leads an attack on [[Prince]]ss [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebels]] on [[Hoth]]. Rebel communications officer [[Romas Navander]] informs [[General]] [[Carlist Rieekan]] that a fleet of [[Star Destroyer]]s is exiting [[hyperspace]] in [[Sector Four (Hoth system)|Sector Four]]. Leia bends down beside Navander while [[Han Solo]] watches. Leia replies that it is risky but that they can't hold out much longer.

[[Rebel trooper]]s man the trenches as a phalanx of [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT walkers]] approaches [[Echo Base]]. As the walkers blast the rebel ground forces and artillery, [[Rogue Squadron]] flies their [[T-47 airspeeder|snowspeeders]] into action against the walkers. [[Luke Skywalker]] convinces [[Wedge Antilles]] and his gunner [[Wes Janson]] to use their snowspeeder to wrap a tow cable around one of the walkers, causing it to trip over and explode. Luke Skywalker smiles but his joy is shot-lived as a walker destroy's Echo Base's [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]].

As debris rains down on the command center, Leia orders the command crew to give the evacuation signal and to get to their transports. As Navander warns that Imperial troops have entered the base, Darth Vader uses [[the Force]] to break through a wall. Rebel troopers exchange fire with [[Cold assault stormtrooper|snowtroopers]] but are quickly mown down.

Han, Leia, [[Chewbacca]], and [[C-3PO]] flee up the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} gangplank as snowtroopers converge around the freighter. The snow troopers set up an [[E-Web heavy repeating blaster/Legends|E-Web heavy repeating blaster]] but the ''Falcon'' uses one of its [[laser cannon]]s to mow them down. Han tells Chewbacca to punch it as the ''Falcon'' rockets out of the hangar, leaving Lord Vader and his snowtroopers behind.

Meanwhile, Skywalker boards his [[T-65B X-wing starfighter]] with [[R2-D2]]. He tells the [[astromech droid]] that they are not going to regroup with the others but are going to the [[Dagobah system]] instead.

==Development==
The episode was originally titled "Battle on Hoth," but was later changed to "Battle of Hoth" on the [[Star Wars Kids]] YouTube channel.<ref name="Battle of Hoth" />
