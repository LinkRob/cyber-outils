'''Burryaga Agaburry''',<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> known as '''Tall Friend''' to the [[charhound]] [[Ember]],<ref name="Trials of the Jedi">''[[The High Republic: Trials of the Jedi]]''</ref> was a [[Gender|male]] [[Wookiee]] [[Jedi Knight]]<ref name="Reunite">{{SW|url=news/inside-the-high-republic-phase-3|text=Inside ''Star Wars: The High Republic'': Reunite with the Jedi and the Nihil in Phase III}}</ref> of the [[Jedi Order]] who was active during the [[High Republic Era]]. The apprentice of [[Jedi Master]] [[Nib Assek]], his master learned [[Shyriiwook]] so she could communicate with him.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Biography==
===Early life===
[[File:AgaburryParents-JediBrave.png|250px|left|thumb|The parents of Burryaga Agaburry]]
When Burryaga Agaburry was very young, he was afraid to climb the trees of his [[homeworld]]. [[Burryaga Agaburry's parents|His parents]] encouraged the young Wookiee to persevere as they climbed with him, teaching him a valuable lesson on bravery.<ref name="Brave" />

===Padawan of the Jedi Order===
Burryaga joined the [[Jedi Order]] and became the [[Padawan]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Nib Assek]]. In [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Agaburry and his master participated in the rescue effort to save the [[Hetzal system]] during the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. He was the one who discovered that several of the [[Hyperspace anomaly|hyperspace anomalies]] bearing down on the worlds of the [[Star system|system]] were [[starship]] fragments with people still alive onboard. While Jedi pilots attempted to destroy the starship fragments, Burryaga felt the survivors' terror so deeply that it radiated through the Jedi [[Song of the Force|Force network]]. Facing a [[language]] barrier, the other Jedi believed that they were feeling Burryaga's own emotions and initially attempted to remove him from the network. Burryaga's efforts ultimately made the other Jedi realize that the starship fragments had survivors aboard.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

[[231 BBY|A year]] after the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]] Burryaga and his master [[Nib Assek]] attended the [[Republic Fair]] on [[Valo]]. During the [[Nihil]] attack on the fair, Burryaga helped where he could.<ref name="The Rising Storm" />

Burryaga and Nib went to [[Kashyyyk]] for [[Life Day]] and fought Drengir that had been dormant there.<ref name="Deck the Halls">{{StoryCite|story=Deck the Halls|book=Life Day 1}}</ref>

Burryaga and Nib took part in the [[Battle of Galov]]. They helped disable the ''[[Lourna Dee (cruiser)|Lourna Dee]]'' and then boarded the ship, taking Nihil prisoners.<ref>''[[The High Republic: Tempest Runner]]''</ref>

Burryaga was present on [[Starlight Beacon]] while Jedi Master [[Torban Buck]] was baking [[sweet story custard cake]].<ref name="Bake Off">''[[The High Republic Adventures: Galactic Bake-Off Spectacular]]''</ref>

[[File:Burryaga-CGSWG.png|thumb|200px|right|Burryaga as a Jedi Padawan]]
Burryaga was on Starlight Beacon when the [[Nihil]] sabotaged the station. He became trapped on the bottom half of [[Starlight Beacon]] and later discovered, with his friend, [[Bell Zettifar]], the body of his master, [[Nib Assek]], who was attacked by a [[Shrikarai|Nameless]]. During the evacuation of the lower half of Starlight, Burryaga went missing while fighting a [[rathtar]] and was proclaimed dead,<ref name="The Fallen Star">''[[The High Republic: The Fallen Star]]''</ref> though he had actually survived.<ref name="THR Show">{{HighRepublicShow|5CdcCHZoHA0|The Fate of Burryaga Revealed, The High Republic Heads to San Diego Comic-Con, and More!|sw_url=video/the-fate-of-burryaga-revealed-the-high-republic-heads-to-san-diego-comic-con-and-more}}</ref> Bell felt sure that his friend had survived and resolved to find him.<ref name="The Fallen Star" /> Bell remained on the [[planet]] of [[Eiram]] and mounted a search for many weeks, eventually finding Burryaga trapped in an underwater cave.<ref name="All Jedi">{{StoryCite|story=All Jedi Walk Their Own Path|book=The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life}}</ref>

===Jedi Knight===
Two months after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|fall of Starlight Beacon]], Burryaga and Bell arrived at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on [[Coruscant]], where they were greeted by the [[charhound]] [[Ember]] and Bell's master, [[Indeera Stokes]]. The two Padawans were then [[Knighting Ceremony|knighted]].<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3">[[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 3|''The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight'' 3]]</ref>

Four months after the fall, Burryaga checked in on Bell, who was looking into the leader of the Nihil, [[Marchion Ro]], whom Bell said was responsible for the deaths of both Jedi's masters. Five months after the fall, Burryaga spoke with [[Jedi Council]] member [[Pra-Tre Veter]] about Burryaga's concern for Bell. Veter believed that Bell needed a task to put his energy into. He would bring Bell into [[Jedi-Republic task force|a task force]] to fight the Nihil on the borders of the [[Occlusion Zone]]. Veter invited Burryaga to join as well, but Burryaga did not accept. Six months after the fall, Burryaga was drawing along with some [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi younglings]]. He drew a picture of Bell looking angry. He went to a landing pad where Bell and Master Veter were leaving to join the task force, deciding to go with them.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3" />

Nine months after the fall, Burryaga attended a meeting aboard the [[Emissary-class Republic Cruiser|''Emissary''-class Cruiser]] ''[[Third Horizon]]'' led by [[Admiral]] [[Pevel Kronara]], regarding a Nihil attack on a settlement on [[Asternin]]. Kronara asked the Jedi to deliver aid to the planet. Veter, Bell, and Burryaga met with the settlement's mayor, but they were spotted by Nihil scouts. A Nihil [[Stormship]], the ''[[Cacophony]]'', destroyed the ship the Jedi had used and activated its [[47 lightsaber nullifier]], deactivating the Jedi's weapons. On Veter's insistence, Burryaga and Bell rounded up the townspeople and fled while the [[Grand Master]] fought the Nihil.<ref name="Shadows of Starlight 3" />

Nearly a year after the fall of Starlight, Burryaga and Bell were patrolling the border of the Occlusion Zone on the ''[[Tractate]]'' when they received a distress signal from the planet [[Ribento]], which was being [[Bombardment of Ribento|bombarded]] by Nihil ships, including the ''Cacophony''. [[Melis Shryke]] launched two [[Drill ship|drill ships]] that tore into the ''Tractate''<nowiki/>'s hull and delivered [[Enforcer droid|enforcer droids]] into the ship. Burryaga and Bell split up to fight the droids. RDC reinforcements arrived, but the ''Cacophony'' escaped.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness">''[[The High Republic: The Eye of Darkness]]''</ref>

Later, Burryaga and Bell arrived on the planet [[Phrill]] with relief ships from the RDC to lend aid to the town of [[Perseverance (town)|Perseverance]], which had been destroyed by Shryke's Nihil forces. Burryaga sensed people trapped under a collapsed building, and the two Jedi worked together to free them.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

At the RDC base on [[Hintis IV]], Burryaga and Bell studied a map of places where Nihil ships had exited the Stormwall, unable to determine a pattern to predict their emergence. The ''Tractate''<nowiki/>'s captain, [[Amaryl Pel]], informed them that the ship had been sufficiently repaired to resume service.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

At the behest of Jedi Master [[Elzar Mann]], a fleet of RDC and Jedi ships, including the ''Tractate'', assembled at the border of the Occlusion Zone to [[Assault on the Stormwall|attempt to pierce the Stormwall]]. The assault destroyed several of the [[Stormseed|stormseeds]] making up the wall, but as some of the Republic vessels tried to move through the breach, the Stormwall repaired itself. Burryaga watched in anguish as the ships were destroyed.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

After the failed attack on the Stormwall, the ''Tractate'' resumed its patrol of the Occlusion Zone's border. The ship's crew noticed that [[Expansion of the Stormwall|the Stormwall was expanding]], and Captain Pel gave the order to get clear of it so that they would not be trapped in the Occlusion Zone. Bell took the ship's controls and attempted to take them toward the approaching Stormwall, hoping for a chance to take the fight to the Nihil, but Burryaga talked him down and the ''Tractate'' made its escape.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

At the Hintis IV base, Burryaga and Bell spoke with the technician [[Harlak]] about the seemingly random locations of raids carried out by the Nihil. Burryaga came up with the idea to analyze the locations of the ''Cacophony''<nowiki/>'s raids specifically. Harlak identified a pattern to Melis Shryke's attacks and predicted that the ''Cacophony''<nowiki/>'s next emergence from the Stormwall could be near a planet called [[Reventine]].<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

The ''Tractate'' waited with a large force of RDC ships for the ''Cacophony'' near the point where it was predicted to appear in the [[Seswenna sector]]. The ''Cacophony'' emerged from the Stormwall badly damaged. Bell hailed the Nihil ship and was surprised to hear the voice of Jedi Master [[Avar Kriss]], who had been trapped in the Occlusion Zone. Burryaga and Avar each realized that the other was alive. Avar informed the ''Tractate''<nowiki/>'s crew that she had taken Melis Shryke prisoner.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

When Avar returned to Coruscant, Burryaga was among the Jedi to welcome her back.<ref name="The Eye of Darkness" />

==Personality and traits==
Burryaga disliked social gatherings due to the fact that most people could not understand him. However, when he was onboard the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Hospital ship|medical frigate]] ''[[Panacea]]'' with the many patients from the ''[[Legacy Run]]'', he was able to help comfort the [[human]] boy [[Serj Ukkarian]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Powers and abilities==
Burryaga had exceptional empathic abilities. He was capable of piloting a [[Jedi Vector]].<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Equipment==
Burryaga [[Burryaga's lightsaber|wielded]] a [[Color|blue]]-bladed [[lightsaber]] with a physical [[Crossguard lightsaber|crossguard]] on the hilt.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Burryaga_Agaburry_Concept_Art.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Concept art of Burryaga Agaburry]]
Burryaga Agaburry was created for the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' publishing project. He appeared in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]'' by [[Charles Soule]],<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> who also named the character. Soule revealed he chose the name in large part because he found "Burryaga Agaburry" fun to say.<ref name="Fun to Say">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1233497729780850689|Charles Soule|quote=This is - no joke - a big part of why I gave Burryaga Agaburry, the Wookiee Jedi from my upcoming novel STAR WARS: THE HIGH REPUBLIC: LIGHT OF THE JEDI, the name Burryaga Agaburry.<br />It's fun to say.<br />(Star Wars is, among other things, very fun.)|archivedate=20200228210553}}</ref> He is described as a "very sensitive Jedi" and wields a blue crossguard lightsaber, with the crossguard design being inspired by Arthurian myths, such as the sword Excalibur.<ref name="SlashFilm">{{WebCite|url=https://www.slashfilm.com/star-wars-the-high-republic-revealed|text='Star Wars: The High Republic' Revealed: Everything You Need To Know About Project Luminous|author=Sciretta, Peter|date=2020-02-25|work=SlashFilm|archivedate=20200314051057}}</ref>
