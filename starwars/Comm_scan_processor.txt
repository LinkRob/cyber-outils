'''Comm/scan''', also known as '''ComScan''' or '''Com-scan''', was a form of [[sensor]] technology used to detect energy readings and approaching [[starship]]s. Both the [[Galactic Empire]] and the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] as well as their respective successors, the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]], made use of comm/scan technology.

==History==
{{Quote|Comm/scan reports three ''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyers and a larger capital ship. Unknown class, Dreadnought-sized. Preliminary estimated length seven thousand five hundred meters.|[[PZ-4CO]], to [[Kaydel Ko Connix]]|<ref name="TLJ EE">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>}}
The [[Galactic Empire]] utilized ComScan technology to detect the [[deflector shield]] protecting [[Echo Base]] on [[Hoth]] prior to the [[Battle of Hoth]].<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>

The [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] utilized comm/scan technology on [[Endor]]. Shortly following the [[Battle of Endor]], [[Hera Syndulla]] learnt that comm/scan detected a pair of surviving [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|Imperial TIE Fighter]]s heading to attack the [[Bright Tree Village|Ewok village]], and so took the ''[[Ghost]]'' to intercept them.<ref name="Chopper and Friends">{{FOD|Chopper and Friends}}</ref>

The [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing|204th Imperial Fighter Wing's]] [[flagship]]  after the [[Battle of Troithe]], a modified [[bulk freighter]] called the ''[[Yadeez]]'', was equipped with a comscan station, which was  manned by [[Lieutenant]] [[Heirorius]]. During the prelude to the [[Destruction of Fedovoi End]] as part of a 'second' [[Operation: Cinder]], Heirorius reported no replies or signals from the threatened [[Yomo Council|breakaway]] [[Fedovoi End|world]], but rather spiking energy readings indicative of [[ion cannon]]s being powered up, when asked by Shadow Wing's [[Colonel]], [[Soran Keize]]. The [[New Republic]] [[Star Destroyer]] [[Deliverance]], which was under the command of Syndulla during the [[Hunt for Shadow Wing]], was also equipped with a comscan station, manned by a [[Cathar]] [[gender|girl]] named [[Dhina]]. The vessel was destroyed, whilst the ''Yadeez'' was crippled and set adrift, during the climactic [[Battle of Jakku]] at the end of the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

The [[Resistance]] had comm/scan technology on [[D'Qar]], using it to determine the quantity and size of [[starship]]s comprising the [[First Order]]'s [[First Order Navy|attack force]] during the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation of D'Qar]]. The First Order later used comm/scan to home in on the signatures of emissions given off by fleeing [[U-55 orbital loadlifter]]s attempting to reach [[Crait]], and [[gunner]]s were able to shoot down several of the escaping craft.<ref name="TLJ EE" />
