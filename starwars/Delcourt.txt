'''Delcourt''' is a [[Wikipedia:France|French]] publishing house specialized in [[Star Wars comic books|comics]] and manga. Founded in 1986, Delcourt is responsible for publishing the [[Wikipedia:France|French]] printings of all ''Star Wars'' comics and manga since it took up [[Dark Horse France]]'s job in 1999.

Since 2015, the new line of [[Marvel]] comic books based on Star Wars are published in French by another company, [[Wikipedia:Panini Comics|Panini Comics]]. Delcourt kept the rights to the previous comics, in addition to the comics from ''[[Star Wars Rebels Magazine]]''.
