'''Antennae''' were long, thin [[sensor]]y appendages situated on the heads of certain [[species]] including the [[crustacean]] creature known as [[the Sleeper]], which inhabited the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]].<ref name="Dark Disciple">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> The antenna implanted into [[Aurra Sing]]'s skull supposedly allowed her to sense fear in her enemies.<ref name="HNTGEBEAOGSS">''[[Star Wars: How Not to Get Eaten by Ewoks and Other Galactic Survival Skills]]''</ref> The [[OG-9 homing spider droid]] had two [[comlink]] antennae, and the [[NR-N99 Persuader-class droid enforcer|NR-N99 ''Persuader''-class droid enforcer]] had two communication antennae.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> The [[R5 series astromech droid]] was also equipped with a dome-mounted antenna.<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref>
