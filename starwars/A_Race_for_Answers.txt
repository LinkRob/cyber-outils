"'''A Race for Answers'''" is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story that was published as the lead story of [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 23|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 23]] on [[July 3]], [[2019]].

==Plot summary==
===Joining the race===
{{Quote|Oh? You've never heard of battle-racing? It's just like regular racing. Just with&hellip; well&hellip; live ammo.|Lando Calrissian, to Chewbacca|<ref name="A Race for Answers">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=23|story=A Race for Answers}}</ref>}}
After the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="Date" /> [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Chewbacca]] were sent on a [[Mission to Neskar Station|mission]] to stop [[pirate]]s from raiding [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] supply lines. Chewbacca was reluctant to stay back on [[Endor]] to keep partying but Calrissian insisted he come along to leave [[Han Solo]] and [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] alone. Calrissian got a lead on a former [[podracer]] who had a connection to the pirate problem. The podracer was going to be part of a [[Battle-racing|battle race]] and Calrissian planned to join to intercept him.

Calrissian and Chewbacca&mdash;under the pseudonyms "Rizzo Landian" and "Crunch" respectively&mdash;then arrived at [[Neskar Station]] for the race with the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', which was disguised as the "''Centennial Buzzard''." Calrissian located the informate's [[starship]], the [[Widowmaker (racer)|''Widowmaker'']] piloted by the [[Dug]] [[Sebulba]]. The announcer called all the racer to the start and began the countdown. He shouted for the race to began and the ships took many opening fire on each other.

===Imperial interruption===
The [[Starfighter combat|dogfighting]] continued all around Calrissian as he tried to follow Sebulba. But then a [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyer]] appeared and stopped the fight. The [[Imperial officer]] onboard the [[Star Destroyer]] ordered the racers to surrender and join the [[Galactic Empire]].

Calrissian was having none of this and came up with a plan to stop the Empire. He called into the other racers and introduced himself as the [[Gold Leader|leader]] of [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold Squadron]]. He convinced the other racers to join him and take on the incoming Imperial forces. They all rally behind him an charge toward the incoming [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]].

All around the star destroyer, the racers were chasing down the TIE fighters as Calrissian barked orders to them. They took out the [[Deflector shield generator|shield generator]] and the Imperials were in full retreat. The racers all celebrated but Calrissian then asked them that he knew one of them had information he needed. Realizing that the rebel [[general]] was referring to him, Sebulba attempted to make a quick dash out of the area but was stopped by the ''Falcon''. Sebulba was angry that he was captured but Calrissian was happy that his plan worked out.

==Continuity==
"A Race for Answers" features the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''{{'s}} [[rectenna]], which was destroyed during the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="Endor">{{Film|VI}}</ref> However, this comic takes place after the Battle of [[Endor]], as it refers to the [[Galactic Empire]] as having been defeated.
