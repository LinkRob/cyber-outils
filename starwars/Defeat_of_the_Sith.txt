The '''defeat of the Sith''',<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> also known as the '''decimation of the Sith'''<ref name="Bane Galactic Files" /> or the '''fall of the Sith''',<ref name="Star Wars 35">[[Star Wars (2020) 35|''Star Wars'' (2020) 35]]</ref> was an engagement fought at<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> the [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> of [[Ruusan]]<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> in the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Visual Encyclopedia" /> during the [[Standard year|year]] [[1032 BBY]].<ref name="Date" /> The incident marked the end of the [[Jedi-Sith War]] by<ref name="Secrets of the Jedi" /> appearing to be the destruction of the [[Sith]] Order.<ref name="Sacrifice" />

Weakened after their greed and lust for power caused infighting amongst themselves,<ref name="Sacrifice" /> the Sith were all but destroyed<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> by [[Jedi Knight]]s.<ref name="Bane Galactic Files" /> [[Darth Bane]] was left the sole Sith survivor of the war.<ref name="USW">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> He had helped bring about the destruction of his own order<ref name="Dark Side">''[[Star Wars: The Dark Side]]''</ref> because he had come to believe that, for the Sith to survive, they needed to limit their numbers so as to prevent infighting.<ref name="Sacrifice" /> The victory began a [[Republic Era|new era]] for the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="Helmet Collection" /> Following the defeat of the Sith, [[Form II]] "Makashi" fell out of fashion in the [[Jedi Order]] and was gradually seen as a mere [[Jedi training|training exercise]].<ref name="Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment">{{EncyclopediaCite|Lightsabers and Jedi Equipment}}</ref>
