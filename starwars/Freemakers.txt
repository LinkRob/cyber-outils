The '''Freemakers''' were a family that lived during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The family consisted of at least [[Pace Freemaker|Pace]], [[Kordi Freemaker|Kordi]], [[Rowan Freemaker|Rowan]], and [[Moxie Freemaker]]. They had a servant [[B1-series battle droid]], [[R0-GR]] ("Roger"), who considered himself both a Freemaker and a [[droid]].<ref name="Droidography" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{SeeAlso|Freemakers/LEGO}}
[[File:Freemakers_Showdown.jpg|thumb|250px|The Freemakers in the TV series ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'']]
The Freemakers are the main cast of the non-[[canon]] ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures]]'' series. The four characters Rowan, [[Zander Freemaker|Zander]], Kordi Freemaker, and Roger were voiced by [[Nicolas Cantu]], [[Eugene Byrd]], [[Vanessa Lengies]], and [[Matthew Wood]] respectively.<ref name="Premiere Announcement">{{SW|url=news/lego-star-wars-the-freemaker-adventures-premieres-june-20-on-disney-xd|text=''LEGO Star Wars: The Freemaker Adventures'' Premieres June 20 on Disney XD}}</ref> The Freemakers would later be introduced to the canon continuity in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Droidography]]'', written by [[Marc Sumerak]].<ref name="Droidography" />
