'''Fawn Moonraiser''' was an elderly [[Human/Legends|Human]] [[Gender/Legends|female]] who lived during the [[Time/Legends|time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War/Legends|Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="AED" />

==Behind the scenes==
Fawn Moonraiser appears in the directory tree files for the [[2003]] [[Roleplaying game|massively multiplayer online role-playing game]] [[Star Wars Galaxies|''Star Wars Galaxies: An Empire Divided'']]. However, she is not known to have appeared on the [[Star Wars video games|video game]]'s live servers as a proper [[Wikipedia:Non-player character|non-player character]]. Nevertheless, an emulated depiction pulled from this character's Interchange File Format reveals what she would have looked like if she were added to the game.<ref name="AED" />
