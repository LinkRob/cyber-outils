'''Edon Kappehl'''<ref name="ICC" /> was a fair-skinned [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who served in the [[Resistance]] during their [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] against the [[First Order]]. During the [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuation]] of the [[Resistance base]] on the [[planet]] [[D'Qar]]<ref name="TLJ" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> he flew as the [[bombardier]] for ''[[Crimson Hailstorm]]'', a [[MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 heavy bomber|MG-100 StarFortress SF-17]] [[bomber]] serving in [[Crimson Squadron]].<ref name="ICC" /> He was [[Death|killed]] [[Ileenium system|above D'Qar]] along with the rest of the [[squadron]] during an attack run on the First Order's<ref name="TLJ" /> [[Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought]] ''[[Fulminatrix]]'', when a burning enemy [[Starfighter|fighter]] crashed into the bomber he was on, starting a chain reaction and destroying the bomber.<ref name="ICC" />

==Behind the scenes==
Edon Kappehl first appeared in the film [[Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi|''Star Wars'': Episode VIII ''The Last Jedi'']],<ref name="TLJ" /> which was released on [[December 15]], [[2017]].<ref name="Release Date">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-episode-viii-to-open-december-15-2017|text=''Star Wars: Episode VIII'' to Open December 15, 2017}}</ref> He was portrayed by actor [[Dan Euston]]. The character was not named in the film,<ref name="TLJ" /> but received identification in ''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Incredible Cross-Sections]]'', a reference book written by [[Jason Fry]]<ref name="ICC" /> and released in conjunction with the film.<ref name="Release date">{{SW|url=news/new-star-wars-the-last-jedi-books-and-more-revealed-at-sdcc-2017|text=New ''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Books and More Revealed at SDCC 2017}}</ref>
