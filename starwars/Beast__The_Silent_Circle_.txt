The '''beasts''' were an ancient [[species]]. The myth "[[The Silent Circle]]" tells the story of how the [[Tree (The Silent Circle)|trees]], the [[fae]] and the beasts once lived in harmony on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Silent Circle" />

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|They were great hulking beasts with mossy backs and tusks designed to worry at the thick bark of the trees.|[[The Silent Circle]]|<ref name="Silent Circle" />}}
The beasts were hulking creatures. Some [[fae]]s used the beasts' mossy backs as their home.<ref name="Silent Circle" />
