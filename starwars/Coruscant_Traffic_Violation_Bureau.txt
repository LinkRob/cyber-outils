The '''Coruscant Traffic Violation Bureau''' was a [[Galactic Republic]] agency which dealt with traffic violations on the Republic [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. A special division of the [[Commission for the Protection of the Republic]] serving as part of the Traffic Violation Bureau during the [[Clone Wars]] scanned [[Hologram|holographic]] records of [[speeder]]s on the planet to keep track of and account for troublesome [[chassisplast]]s that were in opposition to the Republic.<ref name="Propaganda" />
