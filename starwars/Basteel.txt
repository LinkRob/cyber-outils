'''Basteel''' was a [[Temperature|frigid]] and [[mountain]]ous [[planet]] located in the [[Corva sector]]. It had almost no flat ground and little [[Plant|vegetation]]. Its inhabitants lived in subterranean [[City|cities]], like its [[capital]], [[Eladro City]].

==History==
Basteel was considered not very important and had a token [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] presence. [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and her team visited this [[planet]] during [[Operation Yellow Moon]] to place a beacon and meet with [[Bon Yoth]]. Several [[Standard hour|hours]] after they left, the [[Star Destroyer]] ''[[Shieldmaiden]]'' arrived and sent troops into the [[city]] to arrest the locals.<ref name="MT" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]'', Basteel is misspelled as "Bastille" on page 153.<ref name="Rebel Files">''[[Star Wars: The Rebel Files]]''</ref>
