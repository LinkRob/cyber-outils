In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> after escaping [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] forces at the [[Battle of Hoth]], the [[Sixth Division]] of the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance's]] [[Rebel Alliance Navy|fleet]] was '''destroyed'''. The cell was discovered by [[Commander]] [[Ellian Zahra]], who was able to locate the rebels with the help of a signal analysis protocol she had developed. Zahra's [[flagship]], the ''[[Tarkin's Will]]'', decimated the Sixth Division, destroying all ships and leaving no survivors.<ref name="SW 3" />
