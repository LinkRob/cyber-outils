'''Andrea Detwiler''' worked as a puppeteer on six episodes of the [[2022]] live-action television series ''[[Star Wars: Obi-Wan Kenobi]]''.<ref name="PartI" /> Later, Detwiler portrayed the [[Unidentified Tarsunt X-wing pilot|Tarsunt X-wing pilot]] in "[[The Real Good Guys]]" the eighth episode of the ''[[Star Wars: Skeleton Crew]]'' series.<ref name="SC8" />
