'''Carbon-based life forms''' were organisms whose biochemistry was based on [[carbon]] compounds, with [[water]] as a solvent. The majority of [[Sentience|sentient]] (and other) [[species]] in [[the galaxy]] fell into that category. Such life forms typically evolved in [[atmosphere]]s mostly made up of a mixture of [[oxygen]], [[nitrogen]], and [[hydrogen]]. Alternative biochemistries included [[Silicon-based organism|one]] based on [[silicon]],<ref name="BMF 43">{{BuildFalconCite|43|Secrets of Spaceflight}}</ref> and another based on [[Mercury/Legends|mercury]].<ref name="Sanctuary">''[[Sanctuary: A Bad Batch Novel]]''</ref>
