'''Area Null''' was a tower within the [[Arkanis Academy]] that was restricted to most [[Imperial officer]]s and [[Imperial cadet|cadets]]. Several [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] operations were based at Area Null including [[Project Harvester]], a secret program for identifying [[Force-sensitive]] [[Imperial Academy]] recruits, and [[Project Unity]], a program for rehabilitating former Imperial dissenters.<ref name="Secret Academy" />

Several [[Standard year|years]] prior to the [[Battle of Yavin]], the rebellious [[cadet]] [[Zare Leonis]] and his [[Lothalite]] friend [[Beck Ollet]] infiltrated Area Null in order to [[Rescue of Dhara Leonis|rescue]] Zare's sister [[Dhara Leonis]]. They were aided by their friend [[Merei Spanjaf]] and several members of the [[Spectres]] rebel cell.<ref name="Secret Academy" />
