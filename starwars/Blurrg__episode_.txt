"'''Blurrg'''" is the third episode of the webseries ''[[Star Wars Galaxy of Creatures]]''. It follows the [[Safari droid]] [[SF-R3]] as he interacts with a herd of [[blurrg]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]]. The episode was released on [[StarWarsKids.com]] on [[October 21]], [[2021]],<ref name="YouTube" /> the same day as the series' fourth episode, "[[Voorpak (episode)|Voorpak]]."<ref name="Voorpak" />

==Official description==
''Aree has to learn how to wrangle some rowdy blurrgs back into their pen, before the local Ewoks find out!''

==Plot summary==
On the [[Endor|Forest Moon of Endor]], [[SF-R3]] ("Aree") and [[CAM]] crash their [[74-Z speeder bike]] while approaching a herd of [[blurrg]]s. After tending to CAM's damaged visual receptor, Aree explains that he has been sent on a mission by the [[Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts]] to herd blurrgs before the local [[Ewok]]s find out that someone has set them free.

Riding his speeder bike, Aree attempts to lasso a blurrg but ends up tied up around a tree. Aree later tries to use signaling lights to herd them into a pen but they run past him. During the third attempt, Aree runs circles around them with his speeder bike. However, the bike breaks down and he lends in the middle of the herd. Aree discovers that he can herd them by whistling a tune. Aree leads them into a pen but the blurrgs follow him as he whistles on his way out. Aree lists several fun facts including that male blurrgs are eaten by female blurrgs after mating.
