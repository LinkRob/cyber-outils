In the [[19 BBY|final year]] of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] assembled an attack '''fleet''' to besiege the [[Galactic Republic]] capital world [[Coruscant]] and kidnap [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]].<ref name="Battles" /> The Confederate [[fleet]] massed to over 1,000 [[warship]]s [[Flagship|flagged]] by the [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] ''[[Invisible Hand]]''.<ref name="BMF 75">{{BuildFalconCite|75|Starship Fact File}}</ref> The fleet consisted of various ship classes, including other ''Providence''-class Dreadnoughts such as the ''[[Prosperous]]'',<ref name="BMF 58" /> [[Lucrehulk-class battleship|''Lucrehulk''-class Battleships]], [[Recusant-class light destroyer|''Recusant''-class destroyers]], [[Munificent-class star frigate|''Munificent''-class star frigates]], and many [[droid starfighter]]s.<ref name="Episode III" />
