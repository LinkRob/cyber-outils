'''Cedric Williams''', whose full professional name is '''Cedric L. Williams''', is an actor who provided additional voices in [[Respawn Entertainment|Respawn Entertainment's]] [[2023]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''.<ref name="Credits" /> Among these additional voices was that of [[Soont Madas]].<ref name="Soont Madas">{{Instagram|username=cedriclwilliams|url=p/Cr1AlRcrm_2/?igsh=b202NTM5a3oxOTky|name=Cedric L. Williams Jr.|date=2023-05-04|archiveurl=https://archive.is/HOpKj}} This Instagram post from the voice actor features a video highlighting many [[Soont Madas]] dialog sequences from the game, and the post is about his contributions to the game.</ref>
