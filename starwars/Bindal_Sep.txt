'''Bindal Sep''' was a [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] of the [[Jedi Order]] during the [[High Republic Era]] and was a member of the ''[[Star Hopper]]'' crew.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 2" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="High Republic Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> when the [[space station]] [[Starlight Beacon]] was [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destroyed]] by the [[Nihil]], Sep was presumed [[Death|dead]] along with several others. A [[229 BBY|year]] later, the [[Galactic Republic]] file on Sep was closed as the Padawan had been presumed dead for over a [[Standard year|year]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 2" /> Sep, however, was in fact alive but had been [[Prisoner|imprisoned]] on the [[Korvix Vorn]], a Nihil [[prison ship]], for the past year alongside [[Jedi Master]] [[Torban Buck]], [[Jedi Knight]] [[Farzala Tarabal]], and [[Kajain'sa'Nikto]] [[Ishnar Ti-Kharatal]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 4">[[The High Republic Adventures (2023) 4|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2023) 4]]</ref>
