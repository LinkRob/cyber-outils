The '''Doza Dive''' was a [[starfighter combat]] maneuver that two [[pilot]]s could perform. In [[34 ABY]], [[Venisa Doza|Venisa]] and [[Torra Doza]] opted to use a Doza Dive after encountering a [[First Order]] [[Unidentified First Order TIE squadron|TIE fighter squadron]] during a [[Blockade of Dantooine|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]]. The pair successfully completed the maneuver by destroying the two [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]] following them and congratulated each other for their efforts.

==Description==
The Doza Dive was a [[starfighter combat]] maneuver that shared its name with the [[Doza family]]. The tactic was used to misdirect enemy pursuers, leading them into an easier position to take out. The Doza Dive could be performed by two [[pilot]]s diving their [[starfighter]]s in opposite directions, both downward and away from their pursuers, until the combatants followed them. Afterward, both individuals would swing their [[Starship|vessels]] in the opposite direction to surprise and engage their opposition, destroying their first combatant. One starfighter would then loop around and fire at their second pursuer, completing the maneuver.<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance" />

==History==
{{Quote|Great shot, Mom.''"<br />"''Nicely done yourself.|Torra and Venisa Doza|audio=DozaDive2-RebuildingTheResistance.flac|<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance" />}}
[[File:TheDozaDive-RebuildingTheResistance.png|thumb|left|300px|Venisa and Torra Doza executing a Doza Dive]]
At some point, the pilots [[Venisa Doza]] and [[Torra Doza]] learned how to complete the Doza Dive. After encountering a [[First Order]] [[Unidentified First Order TIE squadron|TIE fighter squadron]] during a [[Blockade of Dantooine|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Dantooine]]<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance" /> in [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> Torra and Venisa opted to use a Doza Dive while combatting two [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter|TIE fighters]]. Both pilots steered their starfighters, the ''[[Blue Ace]]'' and an [[Venisa Doza's X-wing|X-wing]], respectively, toward Dantooine's surface.<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance" />

After ensuring one TIE was following each ship, Torra fired at and destroyed the enemy fighter following Venisa's ship. Afterward, Venisa flew in a loop to position herself behind the ''Blue Ace'', taking out the second TIE fighter following her partner. Upon completing the maneuver and destroying both enemy combatants, they congratulated each other before focusing on the remaining First Order forces.<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance" />
