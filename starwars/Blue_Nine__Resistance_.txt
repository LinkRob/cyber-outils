"'''Blue Nine'''" was an individual who served as a [[Resistance pilot|pilot]] in the [[Resistance]] as a member of [[Blue Squadron (Resistance)|Blue Squadron]]. During the Resistance's [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] with the [[First Order]], Blue Nine participated in the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]]. During the battle, [[Blue Nine]] was saved from a pair of [[Seeker (missile)|seekers]] when [[Ello Asty]] redirected the [[missile]].<ref name="On the Front Lines" />

{{Char-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Nine is mentioned in Ello Asty's Tales of Valor profile in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]'', written by [[Daniel Wallace]].
