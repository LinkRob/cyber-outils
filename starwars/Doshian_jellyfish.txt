The '''Doshian jellyfish''' was a type of [[jellyfish]] from the [[planet]] [[Trandosha]]. The claws of the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[Trandoshan]] [[species]] could cut and flay [[wroshyr wood]] as easily as a they could a Doshian jellyfish.<ref name="FACPOV" />

{{Creature-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Doshian jellyfishes were first mentioned in "[[Tooth and Claw]]," a short story written by [[Michael Kogge]] and published in the ''[[From a Certain Point of View: The Empire Strikes Back]]'' anthology in [[2020]].<ref name="FACPOV" /> They originally appeared in [[2005]] as a [[Hologram/Legends|hologram]] in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Rage of the Wookiees]]''.<ref name="SWG">''[[Star Wars Galaxies: Rage of the Wookiees]]''</ref>
