During or after [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> a '''diplomatic mission to Endor''' was undertaken by the [[Lepi]] [[Jaxxon T. Tumperakki]] of the [[New Republic]] to help resolve a crisis on the [[moon]] [[Endor]]. Tumperakki was accompanied by the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]], who the [[Homeworld|native]] [[Ewok]]s saw as a [[Golden One|god]]. After the two arrived, they ended up in the middle of an [[Ewok Civil War]].<ref name="Reveal" />
