During the [[Imperial Era]], a [[Unidentified pontiff|pontiff]]<ref name="Vader 18" /> of a [[Unidentified religious cult|religious cult]]<ref name="Bounty Hunters 24">[[Bounty Hunters 24|''Bounty Hunters'' 24]]</ref> on [[Vincorba]] in the [[Expansion Region]] who was allied with the [[Crimson Dawn]] criminal syndicate was '''assassinated''' by three members of the [[Assassin's Guild|Assassins Guild]]. The assassins&mdash;[[G-90|Gee-Ninety]], [[Loriach]], and [[Chilla Zin]]&mdash;were hired by the [[Sith assassin]] [[Ochi|Ochi of Bestoon]], a servant of the [[Dark Lord of the Sith|Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]. The purpose of the mission was to test the three assassins in order for them to serve Vader in his quest against Crimson Dawn. Once the assassins successfully [[Death|killed]] the [[pontiff]], their escape aboard a [[landspeeder]] was stopped by Vader and Ochi, who recruited them.<ref name="Vader 18" />
