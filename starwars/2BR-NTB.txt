'''2BR-NTB''',<ref name="2BR-NTB Databank">{{Databank|2br-ntb|2BR-NTB}}</ref> known as '''2B''', was a [[BB-series astromech droid]] who served the [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Norath Kev]]. 2B accompanied Kev to the [[planet]] [[Varkana]], where he was discovered by the [[bounty hunter]] [[Ax Tagrin]], working for the [[First Order]]. Tagrin shot 2B, capturing Kev.<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> The droid had a distinct [[Color|green]] and red paint scheme and [[sensor]]s attuned to impending danger.<ref name="2BR-NTB Databank" />

==Behind the scenes==
2BR-NTB first appeared in "[[The Missing Agent]],"<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> the twelfth episode of the [[Star Wars Resistance Season Two|second season]] of the ''[[Star Wars Resistance]]'' animated television series, which aired on [[December 22]], [[2019]].<ref name="Disney release">{{DisneyPress|wdtv=1|url=disneychannel/shows/star-wars-resistance/episodes|text=Star Wars Resistance}}</ref> The droid was referred to as "2B" within the episode's dialogue,<ref name="The Missing Agent" /> but received their full designation, 2BR-NTB, in their [[StarWars.com]] [[Databank (2014-)|Databank]] entry.<ref name="2BR-NTB Databank" /> The droid's concept art was developed by [[Stephan A. Carey|Stephanie Carey]] and based off an existing model titled "BB8A,"<ref name="Concept Art Gallery">{{SW|url=the-missing-agent-concept-art-gallery|text=The Missing Agent Concept Art Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> which is [[BB-8]]'s model for ''Star Wars Resistance'',<ref name="BB-8 Concept Art">{{SW|url=news/designing-star-wars-resistance|text=Designing ''Star Wars'': ''Star Wars Resistance''}}</ref> with changes made to the head and colors.<ref name="Concept Art Gallery" />
