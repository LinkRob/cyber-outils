'''Argai Minor''' was a terrestrial [[planet]]. The [[Galactic Empire]] had forces within the [[Treinhaus Citadel]] on Argai Minor, and when the [[New Republic]] [[Skirmish on Argai Minor|attacked]] the citadel, the [[Barma Battle Group]] sent [[Meteor Squadron]], [[Hail Squadron]], and [[Vanguard Squadron]] to support their ground forces.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
