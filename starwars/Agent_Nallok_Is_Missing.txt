"'''Agent Nallok Is Missing'''" is a mini three-part [[roleplaying game]] adventure that appears in ''[[Instant Adventures]]'', published by [[West End Games]] in December [[1996]].

==Plot summary==
[[Alliance to Restore the Republic/Legends|Rebel]] [[Agent/Legends|agent]] [[Nallok]] has gone missing. A team of operatives is assigned to locate her, and their only lead is [[Bennbus Polag]], who guides the agent toward a salt mine.

In the mine, they meet a [[Unidentified booby-trapped R2 unit|booby-trapped droid]] which reveals Pollag's treachery, and then they are discovered by [[Stormtrooper/Legends|stormtroopers]].

The operatives manage to defeat the Imperial troops and return to the settlement to confront Pollag and retrieve Nallok.
