The '''Flooded Cave''' was a cave in the mountains in [[Mirage (Toshara)|Mirage]] region on the [[moon]] [[Toshara]] that overlooked [[Kadùa Village]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> an [[camtono]] had been left in cave.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws|canon}}
During 3 ABY,<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] retrieved the camtono.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 
{{Endgame}}

==Behind the scenes==
The flooded cave appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. The camtono found in the cave can be used as a trophy to customize the appearance of the player's [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
