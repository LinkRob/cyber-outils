'''Bal'demnic''',<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-10-24.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-10-24}}</ref> also known simply as the '''Unknown Planet''', was an [[ocean]] [[planet]]<ref name="Acolyte VG" /> in the [[Bal'demnic system]] in the [[Auril sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During the later [[Standard year|years]] of the [[High Republic Era]], the planet was yet to be explored by [[the galaxy]] at large, with the [[star system]] known simply as XA-1958JHR under the [[Columi catalog system]].<ref name="Acolyte VG" /> By the time of the [[Galactic Empire]], the planet had been given the proper name of Bal'demnic.<ref name="Map">{{StarWarsGalaxyMap}}</ref>

The planet was the home of "[[the Stranger]]," a former [[Jedi]] turned [[Sith]].<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> [[Verosha Aniseya]] was brought to the [[planet]] by the Stranger after the [[Mission to Khofar]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> The world was known for its high tides and was secluded from other planets, which in turn made it the perfect hiding spot for one to train in the arts of the dark side.<ref name="DB">{{Databank|unknown-world|Unknown World}}</ref> [[Darth Plagueis]] watched from a cave as the Stranger and Aniseya departed from the [[The Stranger's island|island]] and left the planet on the ''[[Exile II]]''.<ref name="The Acolyte" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Unknown Planet first appeared in the live-action [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte]]'' in the episode "[[Lost / Found]]" on [[June 4]], [[2024]].<ref name="Lost / Found">{{Acolyte|Lost / Found}}</ref> It was first identified in the episode "[[Teach / Corrupt]]" where the label "Unknown Planet" appeared onscreen.<ref name="Teach / Corrupt" /> Scenes on this planet were shot on the [[wikipedia:Portugal|Portuguese]] island of [[wikipedia:Madeira|Madeira]].<ref name="Trivia">{{SW|url=teach-corrupt-trivia-gallery-the-acolyte|text="Teach / Corrupt" Trivia Gallery &#124; The Acolyte}} {{C|Slide 2}}</ref> According to [[Leslye Headland]], the planet was meant to be similar to [[Ahch-To]].<ref name="Collider">{{WebCite|url=https://collider.com/the-acolyte-episode-6-explained-leslye-headland|text=Leslye Headland Says It's "So Clear" What's Going On Between Osha and Qimir in 'The Acolyte'|work=[[Wikipedia:Collider (website)|Collider]]|archivedate=20240703220047}}</ref> 

While not explicitly stated as such, the Unknown Planet resembled the planet [[Bal'demnic/Legends|Bal'demnic]], which was featured in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] novel [[Darth Plagueis (novel)|''Darth Plagueis'']] as the site of both a cortosis mine and where the Sith Lord killed his master [[Darth Tenebrous/Legends|Darth Tenebrous]]. The [[2025]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]'' further hinted at the connection, placing the Unknown Planet in the [[Auril sector]] like Bal'demnic in the Legends continuity, and mentioning an unnamed hostile reptilian native species as a reference to the [[Kon'me]] species from Legends.<ref name="Acolyte VG" /> The [[October 13]], 2025 update<ref name="Substack 2">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/star-wars-galaxy-map-update|text=Star Wars Galaxy Map Update|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-10-13}}</ref> to the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] solidified the connection, replacing the Unknown Planet label with Bal'demnic,<ref name="Map" /> and author [[Jason Fry]] confirmed the implication that the Unknown Planet was given a proper name by the [[Imperial Era|time]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Fry">{{EmailCite|email=File:AtlasEmail-2025-10-24.png|creator=[[Jason Fry]]|date=2025-10-24}}</ref>
