The '''DAS-430 Neural Inhibitor''',<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> also known as the '''Neural Inhibitor Projectile Launcher''', was a projectile launcher<ref name="Droid Factory" /> used by the [[bounty hunter]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]] that fired [[Color|green]] blasts which slowed down and damaged its targets.<ref name="Galactic Defense" /> It had a very similar design to the [[E-11 medium blaster rifle]].
