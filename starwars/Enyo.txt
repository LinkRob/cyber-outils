'''Enyo''' was an [[Color|orange]]-skinned [[Twi'lek]] [[Gender|female]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> In [[21 ABY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Shadow of the Sith]]'' to [[21 ABY]].</ref> they served as an [[instructor]] for the [[Jedi Order]] at [[Jedi Master]] [[Luke Skywalker]]'s [[Jedi Temple of Luke Skywalker|Academy]] on the [[planet]] [[Ossus]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Enyo was briefly spotted by Skywalker as they led a group of [[Jedi Initiate|younglings]] through a series of exercises with [[Training lightsaber|training blades]]. The session was conducted close to a cluster of temple buildings situated apart from Skywalker's [[Luke's hut (Ossus)|hut]]. Subsequently, Enyo passed their duties to Jedi [[Padawan]] [[Ben Solo]], the [[Skywalker family|nephew]] of Skywalker, who started leading the training session just in [[time]] to catch the attention of Skywalker and the [[Scout|explorer]] [[Lor San Tekka]].<ref name="Shadow of the Sith" />
