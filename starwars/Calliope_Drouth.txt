'''Calliope Drouth''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|female]] who worked as a [[Journalist|reporter]] for [[HoloNet News]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="Voice of the Empire" /> She embedded coded messages to the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebellion]] into her society broadcasts with the help of Zox, the [[X-0X unit|X-0X]] unit gifted to her by her former mentor, [[Mandora Catabe]].
