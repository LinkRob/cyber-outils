The '''Celantes'''<ref name="TAVG">''[[Star Wars: The Acolyte: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that existed in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="LF" /> [[Chippin Jat]] was a member of this species.<ref name="TAVG" />

==Biology and appearance==
This bipedal [[humanoid]] [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] was distinguished by their [[hair]]less orange skin of varying shades, with dark mottled spots. They had elongated heads, with low-set [[mouth]]s surrounded by pointed tendrils, as well as large, bulging, glassy black [[eye]]s, and a variety of short tube-like growths spread across their [[Bone|craniums]].<ref name="LF" /> Infant Celantes hatched instead of being produced via live birth.<ref name="TAVG" />

==Species in the galaxy==
In [[132 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TheAcolyteDate}}</ref> a member of this species,<ref name="LF" /> [[Chippin Jat]],<ref name="TAVG" /> was a [[youngling]] within the [[Jedi Order]], being instructed by the [[Jedi Master]] known as [[Sol]].<ref name="LF" />
