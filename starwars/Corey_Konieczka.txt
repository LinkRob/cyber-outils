'''Corey Konieczka''' is a tabletop-game designer at [[Fantasy Flight Games]]. He and [[Lukas Litzsinger]] co-led the creation of the game ''[[Star Wars: Destiny]]''. He was also part of the design team for [[Star Wars: Outer Rim|''Star Wars'': Outer Rim]].
