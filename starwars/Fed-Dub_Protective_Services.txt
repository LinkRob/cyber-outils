'''Fed-Dub Protective Services''' was an arms manufacturer based on the [[Corellian system|Corellia system]]<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" /> [[planet]]s<ref name="Tales from Vandor">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Tales from Vandor]]''</ref> of [[Talus]] and [[Tralus]]. They manufactured the [[Spukami pocket blaster]], a [[hold-out blaster]] made for home-defense.<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />
