'''Alan Oppenheimer''' is an actor and [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]] who voiced [[Feltipern Trevagg/Legends|Feltipern Trevagg]] in the [[1995]] [[Nightlily: The Lovers' Tale (audio drama)|audio dramatization]] of ''[[Nightlily: The Lovers' Tale]]''.<ref name="Nightlily" /> Oppenheimer is best known for his voice work in various classic cartoons, having voiced such characters as [[Wikipedia:Skeletor|Skeletor]], [[Wikipedia:Ming the Merciless|Ming the Merciless]], and [[Wikipedia:Mighty Mouse|Mighty Mouse]].<ref name="SummerCon Bio">{{WebCite|url=https://www.wasummercon.com/alan-oppenheimer|text=Washington State SummerCon: Alan Oppenheimer|archivedate=20240226212146}}</ref>
