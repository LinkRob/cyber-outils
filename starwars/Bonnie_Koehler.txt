'''Bonnie Koehler''' is an Emmy nominated editor. She is credited as being an assistant film editor on [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']],<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> as well as being a sound editor on [[Star Wars: Episode V The Empire Strikes Back|''Star Wars'': Episode V ''The Empire Strikes Back'']].<ref name="ESB">{{Film|V}}</ref>
