'''Arfit''' was a [[Gender|male]] individual who worked for [[Jabba's criminal empire]]. He was one of the guard in Jabba's Palace during [[4 ABY]].<ref name="FACPOV" />

The crew was on security detail when Jabba sentenced the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] and the [[smuggler]]s [[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] to be executed in the [[Great Pit of Carkoon]]. When the prisoners tried to escape, Arfit led the charge to try and apprehend Skywalker. He was killed while Skywalker knocked back the laser projectile.<ref name="FACPOV" />
