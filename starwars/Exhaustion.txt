'''Exhaustion''' was a type of [[Shift Token]] that was used in the card game [[Kessel Sabacc]]. When played, choose another player and that player has to discard their hand and draw new cards.<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics}}
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the scoundrel [[Kay Vess]] won a Exhaustion token from the [[Crimson Dawn District (Kijimi)|Crimson Dawn District]] cardroom in [[Kijimi City]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]]. Kess also stole another copy from the [[Ashiga Clan District]] vault in Kijimi City.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Shift tokens were created for Kessel Sabacc which first appeared in the [[2024]] [[Massive Entertainment]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''. Exhaustion was one of several types of Shift Tokens that could be collected and used in Kessel Sabacc, an optional activity in the game.<ref name="Outlaws" />
