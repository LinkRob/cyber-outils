'''Crodium''' was a [[Color|maroon-colored]] material that could be used in [[lightsaber]] construction.<ref name="Fallen Order" />

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order}}
In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'' to [[14 BBY]].</ref> the [[Jedi Order|Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Cal Kestis]] traveled to the [[Kyyyalstaad Falls]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]], where he discovered some crodium and kept it for making modifications to the [[Jaro Tapal's lightsaber|lightsaber]] of his former master, [[Jedi Master]] [[Jaro Tapal]], who gave the lightsaber to him shortly before his [[death]] during [[Order 66]]<ref name="Fallen Order" /> in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Order66">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas: Updated Edition]]''</ref>
{{EndGame}}

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> three shipments of crodium were scheduled to be sent to the [[moon]] [[Toshara]]. The cargo was ultimately transported by the [[Pyke Syndicate]] as a [[Currency|bribe]] to to the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[governor]] [[Thorden]]. Its delivery was documented in [[Shipment log 459|shipment log #459]], which was stored on a [[datapad]] on [[Fuel Station Visavi]], stationed in Toshara's [[orbit]].<ref name="SWO">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
The outlaw [[Kay Vess]] infiltrated Fuel Station Visavi and read the datapad while evading Imperial personnel.<ref name="SWO" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], crodium first appeared in the [[2019]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]'', developed by [[Respawn Entertainment]]. In the game, it is one of the twenty-four materials players can collect in order to customize the appearance of Cal Kestis' lightsaber.<ref name="Fallen Order" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, [[Crodium/Legends|crodium]] was a metal that first appeared in [[2006]]'s ''[[Legacy of the Force: Tempest]]'', written by [[Troy Denning]] as the third novel in the ''[[Star Wars: Legacy of the Force]]'' series.<ref name="Tempest">''[[Legacy of the Force: Tempest]]''</ref> It was first pictured in the [[2010]] [[Wikipedia:Massively multiplayer online game|massively multiplayer online game]] [[Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures (video game)|''Star Wars: Clone Wars Adventures'']], developed by [[Sony]] Online Entertainment.<ref name="SWCA">{{TCWA|coll=Crodium}}</ref>
