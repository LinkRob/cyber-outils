A '''unit''' of [[clone trooper]]s served under [[Jedi General]] [[Chiata]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. They were outfitted with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] customized in light [[Color|yellow]] markings, identical to the [[13th Battalion]] under [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Jaro Tapal]]. In the final [[Standard day|days]] of the conflict, Chiata and her forces [[Battle against a Separatist dreadnought|engaged]] a [[Providence-class Dreadnought|Separatist dreadnought]], although [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|their]] [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] [[Crash Site|crashed]] on [[Zeffo]]. After the crash, the [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Zeffo)|Clone Commander]] of the unit received [[Order 66]] from [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Darth Sidious|Sheev Palpatine]], culminating in a [[Skirmish on Zeffo|skirmish]] that led to the [[death]] of some clone forces and Chiata and her [[Padawan]] [[Marseph]].<ref name="JFO" />

==Description==
{{Quote|All squads on red alert.&mdash;Aaaahh!!|The Clone Commander, before being killed by a lightsaber|<ref name="JFO" />}}
The unit was part of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] and participated in the pan-[[Galaxy|galactic]] [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. The unit was under the command of [[Jedi General]] [[Chiata]] and a [[Unidentified Clone Commander (Zeffo)|Clone Commander]]. The unit consisted of several [[squad]]s of [[clone trooper]]s that were headquartered aboard a [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]. The [[trooper]]s of the unit donned [[Phase II clone trooper armor]] in standard white markings, although several members contained identical markings to those of the [[13th Battalion]] under the command of [[Jedi]] [[General]] [[Jaro Tapal]].<ref name="JFO" />

==History==
{{Quote|The Villagers built this memorial to the clones on the Venator. If only they knew the truth.|[[Cal Kestis]] upon discovering the shrine|<ref name="JFO" />}}
After the [[Unidentified Venator-class Star Destroyer (Zeffo)|unit]]'s [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] crashed on [[Zeffo]], the [[Abandoned Village|local villagers]] collected the clone trooper helmets from the [[Crash Site|crash site]] and built a shrine to honor the Republic [[soldier]]s.<ref name="JFO" />
