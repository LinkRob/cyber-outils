The '''Cusver system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Deep Core]]. It was located in grid square M-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and was the [[Homeworld|home]]<ref name="Planetnamia" /> of the [[Cusverian]] [[species]].<ref name="Origin" />
