A '''675 test zone''' was a code utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]].<ref name="Stealth" /> In [[4 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">"[[Stealth Strike]]" takes place in [[4 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the Empire employed a 675 test zone in the [[Del Zennis system]], while [[Admiral]] [[Brom Titus]] tested the [[Interdictor (Interdictor-class)|''Interdictor'']], a prototype [[Interdictor-class heavy cruiser|''Interdictor''-class heavy cruiser]], and its [[Gravity well projector|gravity well system]] to ensnare passing rebel organizations. Around that [[time]], the test zone remained restricted to unauthorized Imperial personnel, such as the original crew of [[Sentinel-class landing craft|''Sentinel''-class landing craft]] [[Shuttle S257]].<ref name="Stealth" />

While testing the [[starship]], the ''Interdictor'' and its test zone were breached by vessels of [[Phoenix Cell]], including the [[CR90 corvette]] [[Liberator (CR90 corvette)|''Liberator'']] commanded by rebel [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]]. After his capture, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Kanan Jarrus]] and former [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] covertly entered the 675 test zone aboard Shuttle S257. Bombarding the Interdictor's crew with various codes in order to board, using [[priority protocol 7-10-niner-5]] to gain emergency access, the two were able to get aboard the Star Destroyer and free the rebel prisoners.<ref name="Stealth" />
