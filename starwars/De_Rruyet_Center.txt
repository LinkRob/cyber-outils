'''De'Rruyet Center''',<ref name="Aphra 9" /> also known as '''De'Rruyet HQ''', was the headquarters of the company [[De'Rruyet Industries]], led by [[Beol De'Rruyet]]<ref name="Aphra 6" /> and based on [[Midarr]].<ref name="Aphra 9" /> When De'Rruyet planned to unveil the [[Path engine]], a [[Nihil]] artifact that supposedly provided an ancient method of [[lightspeed]] travel, the [[journalist]] [[Vella]] was situated at De'Rruyet HQ to give a live report.<ref name="Aphra 6" />
