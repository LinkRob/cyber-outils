'''Björn Weckström''' is a famous jewelry artist and sculptor of [[Wikipedia:Finland|Finnish]] nationality. In [[1977]], he was hired by ''[[Star Wars]]'' creator [[George Lucas]] to design an original piece of jewelry that would become [[Leia Organa Solo/Legends|Leia Organa's]] [[Organa's Ceremonial Necklace|Ceremonial Necklace]] in [[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|''Star Wars'': Episode IV ''A New Hope'']].<ref name="starwars.com">{{SWArchive|url=community/event/museum/news20010724.html|text=Museum Tours: Hutchinson & Helsinki}}</ref> However, due to time constraints, Lucas was forced to buy a ready piece of jewelry from Weckström's catalogue, and he chose the "Planetary Valleys" necklace.<ref name="Vapaa Sana">{{WebCite|url=http://www.vapaasana.com/weckstrom.htm|text=Suunnittelija Björn Weckströmin korut ovat pieniä veistoksia|work=Vapaa Sana|archivedate=20051220115009}}</ref>
