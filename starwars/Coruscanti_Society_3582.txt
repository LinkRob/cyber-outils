'''Coruscanti Society #3582''' was an issue of the periodical social publication ''[[Coruscanti Society]]''. It featured a section titled "The Bright Lights of [[Sienar Fleet Systems|Sienar]]," which reported on the various Sienar Fleet Systems [[engineer]]s and chief financial officers that were considered part of [[Coruscant]]'s elite and spotted in major social events. The issue was used by [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]] as a historical source for his book ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'' and mentioned in the chapter covering [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] economy and industry.<ref name="Rise">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref>
