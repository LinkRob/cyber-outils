'''Dianoga cream-filled donuts''' were a type of [[Doughnut/Legends|donut]] which contained [[dianoga cream]]. They were served on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]] in [[Dex's Diner/Legends|Dex's Diner]] where they were made by a [[Crisp-E-O donut droid]] in a traditional [[Adarian/Legends|Adarian]] style every morning. They were personally recommended as the best donuts available by [[Dexter Jettster/Legends|Dexter Jettster]].
