A '''relay system''' was created by the [[Amaxine warriors]] for transportation between the [[Amaxine Space Station]] and stations on various worlds. The system launched [[hyperdrive pod]]s with no internal controls between the relay stations. The network's hub was located on the [[space station]]. The system was abandoned by the Amaxines after contact with the [[Drengir]], [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s who infiltrated the station. Much later, during the [[High Republic Era]], the system was discovered in the aftermath of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]]. [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Reath Silas]] destroyed the system by launching the remaining pods into deep space to prevent the Drengir and [[Nihil]] from making use of it.<ref name="ItD" />
