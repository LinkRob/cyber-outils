A '''church droid''', also known as a '''processional droid''', a '''ceremonial droid''',<ref name="Battle of Jedha script">[[The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha (script)|''The High Republic: The Battle of Jedha'' script]]</ref> or as a '''Temple droid''',<ref name="TENP" /> was a model of [[droid]] that was used by the [[Church of the Force]] at the [[Temple of the Kyber]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Battle of Jedha" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The events of "[[Tales of Enlightenment: New Prospects]]" take place during the events of [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|''The High Republic'' Phase II]], which ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Keth Cerepath owned a [[P3-7A|droid]] that used the [[Vocabulator|vocoder]] of an [[Unidentified Temple droid|old Temple droid]].<ref name="TENP" />
