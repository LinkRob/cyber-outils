'''Clone Comms Technicians''' were [[clone trooper]] [[technician]]s of the [[Galactic Republic]] that were active during the [[Clone Wars]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. [[Cloning|Clone]] Comms Technicians were responsible for proper communication, making them essential for any [[army]]. They were equipped with specialized [[Phase I clone trooper armor]] and later [[Phase II clone trooper armor]]. During the war, the [[501st Legion]] deployed Clone Comms Technicians.<ref name="Standing Out" />
