An '''ascension cable''' was a type of [[grappling hook]] that was used during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[DC-15A blaster carbine]] and [[DC-15A blaster rifle]]s, which saw widespread usage by the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]'s [[clone trooper]]s, were equipped with ascension cables.<ref name="TCW movie" /> A grappling extension could be equipped to a [[DC-17 repeater hand blaster|DC-17 hand blaster]], another standard issue weapon in the Republic Army, to provide an ascension cable to a smaller weapon. 

During [[Domino Squad]]'s training, [[Master Chief]] [[Bric]] attempted to sabotage their final attempt at the [[Citadel Challenge]] by stealing the squad's ascension cables. However, this backfired as it provided a final incentive for the formerly bickering clones to pull together as a team to find an alternate way to complete the challenge.<ref name="Clone Cadets">{{TCW|Clone Cadets}}</ref>

[[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]] used the extension during the [[Battle of Ringo Vinda]].<ref name="The Unknown">{{TCW|The Unknown}}</ref>

[[Clone commando]] [[Crosshair]] used a different ascension cable that can be fired but instead fires a cable onto each of the [[Armored Assault Tank Mk I]] tanks, attaching them to one another, allowing [[Wrecker]] to push them off.<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> He later used this same cable to save [[Omega]], who was in her [[medical pod]] and pull her up.<ref name="Return to Kamino">{{TBB|Return to Kamino}}</ref>

[[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] also used a similar ascension cable when he attached it to his DC-15A blaster carbine when infiltrating [[Desix Separatist holdout]].<ref name="SolitaryClone">{{TBB|The Solitary Clone}}</ref>
