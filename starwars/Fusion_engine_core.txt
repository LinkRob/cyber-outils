A '''fusion engine core''' was a type of [[power core]] used to power the [[fusion engine]]s of a [[sandcrawler]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Jawa]] [[merchant]] [[Pinota Pakinota]] offered a fusion engine core for trade while on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws" />

{{GameMechanics}}
In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the [[Scoundrel/Legends|scoundrel]] [[Kay Vess]] traded Pakinota ten units of [[coarseweave]], six [[accu-accelerator]]s, and five [[MG-2 power exceeder]]s in exchange for a fusion engine core, which she later traded for a [[BT-P7 ion accelerator]] in order to upgrade her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Fusion engine cores appeared as a quest item in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. The player must obtain a fusion engine core to as part of the process for upgrading their [[blaster]]'s ion surge module to level four.<ref name="Outlaws" />
