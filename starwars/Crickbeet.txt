'''Crickbeets''' were a type of nocturnal [[Species|critter]] known for their constant chirping, which would suddenly stop if they sensed danger. One type of crickbeet was the '''Lothalian crickbeet. '''It''' '''could be found in [[Capital City]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Lothal]]. One night, [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[stormtrooper]] [[TK-626]] was patrolling a [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] airfield, listening to the crickbeets. When they stopped chirping, he sensed something was amiss, alerting his partner, [[MB-223]], before the airfield was [[Attack on a Lothal airfield|attacked]] by [[Mandalorian]] [[Sabine Wren]].<ref name="RotR" />
