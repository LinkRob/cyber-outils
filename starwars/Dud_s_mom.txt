'''Dud's mom''' was a [[Gender|female]] individual who was the [[Parent|mother]] of<ref name="BeyondVictory" /> the [[Vulptereen]]<ref name="Force Collection">{{ForceCollection|Dud Bolt|star=1}}</ref> [[Podracer (profession)|podracer]] [[Dud Bolt]],<ref name="BeyondVictory" /> who was [[Homeworld|from]] the [[planet]] [[Vulpter]].<ref name="Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> She was [[poison]]ed by Bolt's rival [[Ark Roose]] after the two [[racer]]s began feuding over a collision between their [[Podracer|vehicles]] at the [[Boonta Eve Classic]] [[Podracing|podrace]]<ref name="BeyondVictory" /> held on the planet [[Tatooine]] in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> The poison was intended for Bolt himself, but his mother survived and recovered.<ref name="BeyondVictory" /> Around [[10 BBY]],<ref name="BVDate">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> the [[Snivvian]] podracer [[Coy Vrizh]] and the [[Radio DJ|host]] of the [[Subspace transceiver|radio]] show ''[[Track Talk]]''  discussed Bolt and Roose's rivalry, including the poisoning of Bolt's mother, during an [[In the Pits|interview]].<ref name="BeyondVictory" />
