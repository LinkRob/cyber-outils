'''Dilan''' was a [[human]] who resided in [[Ghorman]]'s capital city of [[Palmo]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. He was the boyfriend of [[Enza Rylanz]],<ref name="EnzaDatabank" /> and alongside Enza, was a member of her father [[Carro Rylanz]]'s emerging rebel group known as the [[Ghorman Front]].<ref name="Ever Been to Ghorman">{{Andor|Ever Been to Ghorman?}}</ref> Dilan monitored and vetted potential allies to their cause.<ref name="DilanDB" /> During the [[Ghorman Massacre]] in [[2 BBY]], Dilan was wounded by an Imperial and collapsed onto [[Palmo Plaza]]. After limping with [[Lezine]], he eventually slid out of Lezine's grasp and [[Death|passed away]].<ref name="Who Are You">{{Andor|Who Are You?}}</ref>
