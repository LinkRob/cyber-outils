The '''Baroo Coast''' was a [[podracing]] [[Racetrack|course]] that shared its name<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> with [[Baroo]], a location that had artificially created [[beach]] resorts<ref name="Convergence">''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]''</ref> by [[382 BBY]]<ref name="ConvergenceDate">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Convergence" /> The twin [[Rodian]] [[Podracer (profession)|podracers]] [[Staka]] and [[Raka]] had won a race on the Baroo Coast by the [[time]] they participated in the [[Hydian-Hutta Podracing Circuit]]<ref name="Beyond Victory">[[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]]</ref> around [[10 BBY]].<ref name="BVDate">{{BeyondVictoryDate}}</ref> The win was recorded on their downloadable [[Racer|racing]] profile.<ref name="Beyond Victory" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Baroo Coast was mentioned in the [[2025]] [[Industrial Light & Magic]] [[Star Wars video games|virtual reality game]] [[Star Wars: Beyond Victory - A Mixed Reality Playset|''Star Wars: Beyond Victory'' - A Mixed Reality Playset]].<ref name="Beyond Victory" /> The [[Baroo Coast/Legends|course]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']], a [[1999]] video game released by [[LucasArts]].<ref name="Racer">[[Star Wars: Episode I Racer|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''Racer'']]</ref>
