"'''Atari Wars: A ''Star Wars'' Video Game History'''" is an article about the history of ''[[Star Wars]]'' video games. It was written by [[Matt Yeo]] and [[David Kelsall]] and was published in [[Star Wars Insider 40|''Star Wars Insider'' 40]], though it was originally published as "'''Game On'''" in [[August]] [[1997]]'s [[Star Wars: The Official Magazine 9|''Star Wars: The Official Magazine'' 9]].
