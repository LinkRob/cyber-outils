'''Amy Hennig''' was the creative director and a co-writer of the canceled video game ''[[Project Ragtag]]'' by [[Visceral Games]].<ref name="Amy Hennig">{{EA|url=news/amy-hennig-joins-visceral-games|text=Amy Hennig Joins Visceral Games}}</ref> She left EA in January 2018,<ref>{{WebCite|url=http://www.ign.com/articles/2018/06/28/amy-hennig-confirms-she-left-ea-starting-new-studio|text=Amy Hennig Confirms She Left EA, Starting New Studio|date=2018-06-30|archivedate=20180701022654}}</ref> and became the president of [[Paramount Games Studio|Skydance New Media]]. On [[April 19]], [[2022]], it was announced that Hennig and Skydance were working on a [[Untitled Paramount video game|new ''Star Wars'' game]] with [[Lucasfilm Games]], which Hennig will be directing.<ref name="New Game" /> She is best known for her work on the first three ''Uncharted'' games by Naughty Dog and the ''Legacy of Kain'' series by Crystal Dynamics.
