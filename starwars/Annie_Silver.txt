'''Annie Silver''' is an [[Wikipedia:Americans|American]] voice actress. She voiced [[Sola (Outrider)|Sola]] and additional voices in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]''. She has also worked as a voice actor in the English&ndash;dubbed version of the [[Wikipedia:Pokémon (anime)|''Pokémon'']] animated television series.
