Centuries before [[4 ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> the [[Sith]] [[Darkseekers]] carried out an '''attack''' on the [[Ascendant temple]] on [[Pyrr IX]]. During the attack, [[Ilith]], leader of a [[Ilith's Group|group]] of the [[Ascendant]] used his [[Spark Eternal]] to wipe out a number of Sith, though he was caught by surprise and stabbed in the chest by a surviving Darkseeker. As he lay [[Death|dying]], he told [[Miril]], the leader of another [[Bar'leth Group|group]] of the [[Religion|cult]], to destroy her own Spark Eternal and prevent it from falling into the hands of the Sith.<ref name="Aphra 29" /> Sometime after, the Sith [[Slaughter of the Ascendant|attacked]] Miril's group on [[Bar'leth]], where they wiped out the group. Miril sacrificed herself to seal away the Spark, preventing it from falling into the hands of the Sith.<ref name="Aphra 22">[[Doctor Aphra (2020) 22|''Doctor Aphra'' (2020) 22]]</ref>
