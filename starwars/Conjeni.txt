The '''Conjeni''' were a star-shaped [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]. During [[Lina Soh]]'s [[time]] as [[Supreme Chancellor]] during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Force-sensitive]] Conjeni [[youngling]]s were part of the [[Jedi Order]]'s [[Clan Kowak]]. On one particularly hot [[Standard day|day]], the clan was chaperoned by [[Jedi Initiate]] [[Reina Bilass]] to a lesson at a swimming pool near the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. On the return journey to the temple, some of the rambunctious group wore their [[Jedi apparel|Jedi robes]] while others remained in [[swimsuit]]s.<ref name="What?" />

Upon reaching [[Processional Way]] in front of the temple, Bilass silenced the Conjeni and other younglings with a snap of her fingers, causing them to fall into a straight line as they approached the steps to the temple. [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] and [[Jedi Master]] [[Avar Kriss]] then emerged from the structure to greet the group, and when Yoda asked how their swimming lesson had gone, the Conjeni younglings shouted back gleefully in response along with their [[human]] and [[Snivvian]] companions. Kriss then led the group into the temple to lead them in [[meditation]].<ref name="What?" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Conjeni first appeared in "[[What a Jedi Makes]]," a short story written by [[Michael Kogge]] and published in the [[2022]] anthology collection ''[[Stories of Jedi and Sith]]''.<ref name="What?" /> The [[Conjeni/Legends|species]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they appeared in "[[Treasure Hunt (adventure)|Treasure Hunt]]," an adventure written by [[Todd Quigley]] and published in [[Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)|''Twin Stars of Kira'']], a [[1993]] supplement produced by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="TwinStars">{{WEGCite|story=Treasure Hunt (adventure)|book=Twin Stars of Kira (sourcebook)}}</ref>
