'''Fantafly''' was a [[casino]] [[Chance cube|dice]] game that could be played in [[Canto Bight]].<ref name="Smuggler">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref> It shared its name with [[Fantafly polyhedral dice]] that were also used in games at [[Canto Bight]]'s [[Canto Casino and Racetrack]].<ref name="TLJ VD">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
