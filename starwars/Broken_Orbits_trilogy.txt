The '''Broken Orbits trilogy''' was a trilogy of adventures for the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. The first was written by [[Michael S. Webster]], while the final two were written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]].

The adventures were supported by some articles on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website.
