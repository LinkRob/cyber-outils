'''1132''' was a [[clone trooper]] created to serve in an [[army]] for the [[Jedi]]. In [[22 BBY]], 1132 attempted to express their individuality amongst their fellow [[trooper]]s. This was met with another [[Cloning|clone]] telling 1132 to be silent.

==Biography==
1132 was a [[human]] [[clone trooper]] made on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]] from the [[Clone template|genetic template]] of [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]] to serve in an [[army]] created for the [[Jedi]], [[Grand Army of the Republic|one]] that would eventually be used by the [[Galactic Republic]] to fight the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="100Scenes" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]'' establishes the scene with 1132 takes place during the events of {{Film|II}}, which ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates to [[22 BBY]].</ref> many [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s were in formation, including 1132, and were overseen by [[Jedi Knight]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. One of the troopers in formation stated that they were all the same. Upon hearing this, 1132 expressed their uniqueness by disagreeing, to which 1132 was told by another trooper to be quiet.<ref name="100Scenes" />

==Personality and traits==
As a clone of Fett,<ref name="100Scenes" /> 1132 stood at 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|ft]]) tall and had [[Color|tan]] skin.<ref name="Clones" /> They also had a weight of 80 [[kilogram]]s.<ref name="Weight" /> 1132 had a sense of individuality which led to them standing out among their fellow troopers.<ref name="100Scenes" />

==Equipment==
1132 wore the standard white [[Phase I clone trooper armor]], and while in formation they carried a [[blaster rifle]] over their shoulder.<ref name="100Scenes" /> Like all other clone troopers, 1132 had a [[behavioral modification biochip]] implanted in them by [[Kaminoan]] [[Scientist|scientists]] to ensure that they would not resist certain [[Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic|orders]].<ref name="Clones" />

==Behind the scenes==
1132 was mentioned in the [[Canon|non-canon]] [[2015]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars in 100 Scenes]]''.<ref name="100Scenes" />
