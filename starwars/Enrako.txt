The '''enrako'''<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref> was a [[Realspace|space]]&ndash;faring creature found in the [[Kijimi Nebula]] located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]'s [[Kijimi system]].<ref name="Art of Outlaws">''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]''</ref> The creature's name was mentioned in the [[Music|song]] "[[Wake Up the Enrako]]" by the [[band]] [[Arctic Nobaki]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the song was available on the [[jukebox]] at [[the Domak Refectory]], a [[cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Kijimi]] located in the same [[star system]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Behind the scenes==
The enrako was mentioned in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> Concept art for the enrako's design and its iteration from a more intricate design to a more simplified design released in the [[2025]] ''[[The Art of Star Wars: Outlaws]]'' reference book. During the game's development, the team envisioned that the enrako would feed on the [[ice nebula]] until nothing was left, while its [[ice shard]]s would emit vapor that would replenish the [[nebula]], thus giving it a transient nature. A visual depiction of the enrako and an explanation of its effect on the nebula was ultimately not included in the game to keep it a mystery.<ref name="Art of Outlaws" />
