'''Burial''' was a [[funeral]] rite used across [[the galaxy]] to honorarily dispose of the [[Death|corpse]] of a [[Sentience|sentient]] being by placing it into the ground. After his [[Parent|mother]] [[Shmi Skywalker Lars|Shmi]] was [[torture]]d to death by a band of [[Tusken Raider]]s, [[Anakin Skywalker]] buried her in the sand at [[Cliegg Lars|her husband's]] [[Lars homestead|homestead]].<ref name="AOTC">{{Film|II}}</ref> During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Gran]] [[senator]] [[Kharrus]] was killed on [[Florrum]] and buried there under a heap of rocks.<ref name="The Gungan General">{{TCW|The Gungan General}}</ref> On [[Jelucan]], the inhabitants would practice sky burial, putting bodies in open cairns at high-altitude for birds to devour. They believed the birds took the soul as well as the flesh, into the sky, forever.<ref name="Lost Stars">''[[Lost Stars]]''</ref> The [[bone]]s of the [[Serennian]] ancestors of [[Dooku]] were buried on [[Mantero]], the [[Funeral]] [[Moon]] of [[Serenno]].<ref name="Dooku: Jedi Lost">''[[Dooku: Jedi Lost]]''</ref> After the [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|skirmish]] aboard<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref> the ''[[Tribunal]]'',<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref> [[Ahsoka Tano]] and [[Rex]] buried the the [[332nd Company]] on the [[Unidentified moon|moon]] the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] had crashed on.<ref name="Victory and Death" /> After he perished during the [[Clash on Nevarro]], the [[Ugnaught]] [[Kuiil]] was buried by [[Mandalorian]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Din Djarin]].<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref>
