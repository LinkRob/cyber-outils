'''Blackroot''' was a rejuvenating beverage from the [[planet]] [[Dathomir]] which could be summoned through the use of [[magick]].<ref name="Monster" /> In [[20 BBY]],<ref name="MonsterDate">{{TCWcanondates|313}}</ref> [[Mother (title)|Mother]] [[Talzin]] of the [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Dathomiri clan|clan]] of Dathomiri [[witch]]es once offered blackroot to [[Count]] [[Dooku]] during his search for a new [[Savage Opress|apprentice]] to replace [[Asajj Ventress]], whom he had presumed [[Death|dead]].<ref name="Monster" />

==Behind the scenes==
Blackroot appeared in "[[Monster]]," the thirteenth episode in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Three|third season]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'' animated television series,<ref name="Monster" /> which first aired on [[January 14]], [[2011]].<ref name="TCWEG">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Episode Guide]]''</ref> Blackroot is a reference to the [[W:c:willowufgood:Blackroot|substance of the same name]] from [[George Lucas]]' [[1988]] film [[Willow (film)|''Willow'']].<ref name="Episode Guide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/monster-episode-guide|text="Monster" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref>
