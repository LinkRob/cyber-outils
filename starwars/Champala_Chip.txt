'''Champala Chips''' were a small crispy [[Foodstuff|dish]] invented by the [[Chagrian]]s of the [[planet]] [[Champala]]. Created in response to poor [[fishing]] conditions, the dish used to be made from a [[crustacean]] [[Homeworld|native]] to the world, but the recipes were later changed to be [[plant]]-based and came to be offered in most dockside [[Cantina|pubs]] on Champala. During a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] he took<ref name="Cookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] tried the chips while on Champala. He was unable to tell that it was plant-based, and in his<ref name="Cookbook" /> 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]]<ref name="Date" /> [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', he praised the "seaworthy flavors" he found in each bite of the dish.<ref name="Cookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Champala Chips were introduced in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Marc Sumerak]] and [[Jenn Fujikawa]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] ingredients for the recipe are [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]], [[Wikipedia:Rice paper|rice paper]], [[Wikipedia:Furikake|furikake]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], and [[Wikipedia:Kosher salt|kosher salt]].<ref name="Cookbook" />
