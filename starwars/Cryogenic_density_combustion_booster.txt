A '''cryogenic density combustion booster''' was a part used on [[Din Djarin's N-1 starfighter]].<ref name="Chapter 5">{{BOBF|Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian}}</ref> [[Han Solo]] once used one to brace against the walls of<ref name="ANH">{{Film|IV}}</ref> [[garbage masher 3263827]]<ref name="ANH Read-Along">[[A New Hope Read-Along Storybook and CD|''A New Hope'' Read-Along Storybook and CD]]</ref> of the [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|first]] [[Death Star]].<ref name="ANH" />

[[Din Djarin]] was given a combustion booster that was stolen by [[Jawa]]s from underneath a [[Tatooine repulsor train|Pyke repulsortrain]]<ref name="Chapter 5" /> around [[9 ABY]].<ref name="BoBFDate">{{BoBFDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Despite having appeared in {{Film|IV}} in [[1977]], the cryogenic density combustion booster wasn't identified as such until [[Chapter 5: Return of the Mandalorian|Chapter 5]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Book of Boba Fett]]'', nearly [[2022|45 years later]].
