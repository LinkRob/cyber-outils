'''Doowan''' was a [[Nautolan]] [[Gender|male]] who was an associate of the [[arms dealer]] known as [[the Dragon]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. Shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]], Doowan was at a [[droid]]-fighting den on [[Anthan Prime]], where he became involved in a [[Mission to the Anthan system|skirmish]] between the [[Galactic Empire|Empire]] and a group of spectators at the fighting den. During the fight, he was isolated and interrogated by [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] and [[Inspector]] [[Thanoth]] about the location of the Dragon. After some forceful negotiations, Doowan gave the two men the location of the Dragon before being handed into the custody of [[Morit Astarte|Morit]] and [[Aiolin Astarte]] to be used in their investigation into arms dealing. Rather than continuing to use him as a source, Morit immediately [[Dismemberment|decapitated]] the Nautolan, claiming the act to be a show of force on behalf of the Empire.<ref name="Shadows and Secrets, Part III" />
