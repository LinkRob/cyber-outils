'''Crystal bats''' were a type of [[bat]] found in the [[Triple Moon Caves]] on the [[planet]] [[Siline]].<ref name="Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> while visiting the [[cave]]s, the [[Silinese ]] [[Rhos Arthyst]] told [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Yord Fandar]] to watch out for both crystal bats and endangered [[dry-rock salamander]]s. Although Fandar wanted to see the crystal bats, he resisted the urge to immediately crane his neck upward and instead relied on swift glances, noticing channels that led into the cave ceiling where he assumed the bats lived.<ref name="Crown" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crystal bats were mentioned in ''[[The Acolyte: The Crystal Crown]]'', a [[2025]] young-adult novel by [[Tessa Gratton]].<ref name="Crown" />
