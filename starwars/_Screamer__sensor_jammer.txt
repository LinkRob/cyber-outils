The '''"Screamer" sensor jammer'''<ref name="RSOWM">''[[Rebel Starfighters Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref> was a [[sensor jammer]] manufactured by [[Bertriak]] and was commonly installed on the [[T-65B X-wing starfighter|T-65 X-wing starfighter]]. It created noise on the electromagnetic spectrum that could confuse the tracking systems of [[TIE series|TIE fighters]] or seeker missiles.<ref name="IncrediBuilds X-wing" />
