The '''Egaria system''' was located in the [[Svivreni sector]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[Trailing Sectors]].<ref name="TrailingSectors" /> The [[star system]] was situated in grid square O-19 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> It was the origin point of<ref name="Planetnamia">{{Planetnamia|system=Egaria [[Star system|system]]|other=[[Egarian silk]]}}</ref> [[Egarian silk]], a type of [[silk]] with high durability and fluctuating elasticity.<ref name="LOTJ">''[[The High Republic: Light of the Jedi]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Egaria system was mentioned in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]]. Both works were authored by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>
