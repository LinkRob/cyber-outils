'''BD-3000 luxury droids''', also known as '''attendant droids''', '''BD units''',<ref name="Droidography" /> and nicknamed '''"Bettie Bots"''',<ref name="RookiesEpGuide">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rookies-episode-guide|text="Rookies" Episode Guide &#124; The Clone Wars}}</ref> were a model of high-end "[[secretary droid]]"<ref name="Droidography" /> used by the [[Galactic Republic]],<ref name="Rookies" /> [[Trade Federation]],<ref name="SS" /> and [[Hutt Clan]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="HFZ" />

==History==
[[File:LottDodBD3000droids-SenateSpy.png|thumb|250px|left|Senator Lott Dod made use of gold colored BD-3000s at his palace on Cato Neimoidia.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], a BD-3000 known as the [[Hologram VJ]] hosted a [[music]] program which broadcast across [[the galaxy]]. Her show was popular among the [[Galactic Republic|Republic's]] [[clone trooper]]s, and she sometimes dedicated songs to particular units, such as the [[Mud Jumpers|Mud-Jumpers]] of the [[224th]].<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> Another [[Unidentified BD-3000 luxury droid|luxury droid]] was used as a cloaked decoy for Senator [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]].<ref name="Assassin">{{TCW|Assassin (episode)}}</ref> [[Trade Federation]] [[Senator]] [[Lott Dod]] owned a pair of [[Color|golden]] BD-3000s.<ref name="SS" />

Two BD-3000 droids, [[Carly]] and [[J'Nell]] once occupied [[Takodana Castle]] on [[Takodana]], serving as judges in [[Strono Tuggs]]' cooking competition. The pair were also responsible for the murder of Chef [[Robbs Ely]].<ref name="Recipe">{{StoryCite|story=A Recipe for Death|book=Tales from a Galaxy Far, Far Away: Aliens: Volume I}}</ref>
