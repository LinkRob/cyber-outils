'''Caretaker of First Knowledge''' was a title given to the leader of the [[Council of First Knowledge]], a [[Jedi Council|council]] within the [[Jedi Order]] that maintained the [[Jedi Archives]]<ref name="SWcom" /> in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]].<ref name="Databank">{{Databank|coruscant|Coruscant}}</ref> In [[19 BBY]], the rank became defunct with the Order's [[Order 66|fall]].<ref name="Timelines" />

==Behind the scenes==
The title was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in an article written by [[Kevin Beentjes]] that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[November 6]], [[2015]].<ref name="SWcom" /> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared&mdash;albeit identified as [[Caretaker of First Knowledge/Legends|Caretaker of the Temple of First Knowledge]]&mdash;in the [[Star Wars (1998) 33|thirty-third issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars: Republic]]'' comic series, written by [[John Ostrander]]<ref name="Republic 33">[[Star Wars (1998) 33|''Star Wars'' (1998) 33]]</ref> and released by [[Dark Horse Comics]] on [[August 22]], [[2001]].<ref name="DarkHorse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/00-464/Star-Wars-33Darkness-Part-2-of-4|text=Star Wars #33: Darkness (Part 2 of 4)}}</ref> In the [[2009]] [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] sourcebook ''[[The Clone Wars Campaign Guide]]'' published by [[Wizards of the Coast]] for use with the ''[[Saga Edition]]'' system, the rank is identified as Caretaker of the Tower of First Knowledge.<ref name="CWCG">''[[The Clone Wars Campaign Guide]]''</ref> The name Caretaker of First Knowledge originates in the [[2010]] reference book [[The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force (real-life book)|''The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force'']], written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="TJP">[[The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force (real-life book)|''The Jedi Path: A Manual for Students of the Force'']]</ref>
