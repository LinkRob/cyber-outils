'''8D-8D''', or simply referred as '''Eights''', was a [[training droid]] who served the [[Jedi Order]] on [[Starlight Beacon]] during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="Brave" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
8D-8D first appear, albeit unidentified, in the [[The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight 1|first issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic – Shadows of Starlight]]'', written by [[Charles Soule]], illustrated by [[Ibraim Roberson]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="SoS 1" /> on [[October 4]], 2023.<ref name="SoS 1 Release">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/112950/star_wars_the_high_republic_-_shadows_of_starlight_2023_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic &mdash; Shadows of Starlight (2023) #1}}</ref> The droid was later identified in the children's book ''[[The High Republic: Jedi Brave in Every Way]]'', written by Charles Soule and [[Rosemary Soule]], and published by [[Disney–Lucasfilm Press]]<ref name="Brave" /> on [[October 10]] of the same year.<ref name="Brave Release">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/727560/star-wars-the-high-republic-jedi-brave-in-every-way-by-rosemary-soule|text=Star Wars: The High Republic: Jedi Brave in Every Way}}</ref>

8D-8D had two different designs between ''Jedi Brave in Every Way'' and the first issue of ''Shadows of Starlight'', though they kept the same color scheme. Soule later confirmed on Twitter that the two droids are indeed 8D-8D.<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|CharlesSoule|status/1717539420608274788|[[Charles Soule]]|date=2023-10-26|quote=It is!|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/zPpuW|archivefile=File:CharlesSoule-8D8D-Twitter.png}} {{C|In Response to: "Hey, @CharlesSoule. I have a question. Is this droid seen in SoS 1 supposed to be 8D-8D from Jedi Brave in Every Way? Wondering cause the two droids share similar color schemes."}}</ref>
