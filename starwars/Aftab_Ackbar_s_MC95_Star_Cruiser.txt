The [[Mon Calamari]] [[Gender|male]] [[Aftab Ackbar]] utilized an '''MC95 Star Cruiser''' during the [[First Order-Resistance War|war]] between the [[First Order]] and the [[Resistance]] in [[34 ABY]]. The [[MC95 Star Cruiser|Star Cruiser]] was the [[flagship]] of the [[Mon Cala fleet]] when the First Order arrived to [[Mission to Mon Cala|destroy]] a group of fleeing Resistance operatives and make an example of the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]]. Onboard the [[Star Cruiser|cruiser]], Ackbar ordered the Mon Cala [[fleet]] to enter [[hyperspace]] immediately in order to avoid losing the battle. Together with the rest of the fleet, the [[cruiser]] was instrumental in rebuilding the [[Resistance navy]].

==Description==
The [[MC95 Star Cruiser]] featured a [[hyperdrive]] and an [[engine]]. The [[Star Cruiser|cruiser]] also had two [[Hangar|docking bays]].<ref name="Allegiance 4" />

==History==
{{Quote|We must hit the hyperdrive now or else this will be the shortest battle in the history of Mon Cala!|Ackbar ordering the fleet to escape from the First Order|<ref name="Allegiance 4" />}}
[[File:AftabInCruiser-Allegiance4.png|thumb|left|155px|Aftab Ackbar ordered the fleet to escape Mon Cala from inside the cruiser.]]
The Star Cruiser used by the [[Mon Calamari]] [[Gender|male]] [[Aftab Ackbar]] was the [[flagship]] of a [[Mon Cala fleet|fleet]] of [[starship]]s located on the [[planet]] [[Mon Cala]], serving under the rule of the [[Mon Calamari monarchy]].<ref name="Allegiance 4" /> In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="Date1">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Allegiance 4|''Allegiance'' 4]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> during the [[First Order-Resistance War]], Ackbar's starship participated in an escape from the planet during the closing stages of a [[Mission to Mon Cala|mission]] on the planet by the [[Resistance]].<ref name="Allegiance 4" />

After the [[First Order]] arrived to destroy the Resistance operatives before they could escape and punish the inhabitants of Mon Cala for siding with the Resistance and giving them the starships needed to rebuild the [[Resistance navy]], Ackbar led the [[fleet]] from aboard his [[cruiser]]. [[Chief]]tain [[Nossor Ri]] [[Death|sacrificed]] his life by distracting the First Order with his [[Mon Calamari fighter]], buying the fleet vital [[time]]. Noting that they had a short [[Viewport|window]] of escape, Ackbar ordered the ships to immediately enter [[hyperspace]] or risk participating in the "shortest battle in the history of Mon Cala." The fleet subsequently joined the Resistance and its [[navy]] and became an integral part of its rebuilding.<ref name="Allegiance 4" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|General Nossor Ri bought us a window&hellip; but the rest of the First Order will be on us in seconds!|Aftab Ackbar realizing the fleet could escape|<ref name="Allegiance 4" />}}
The cruiser was [[Commanding officer|commanded]] by Aftab Ackbar,<ref name="Allegiance 4" /> who was the [[Youngling|son]] of the late veteran [[Admiral]] [[Gial Ackbar]].<ref name="Allegiance 2">[[Allegiance 2|''Allegiance'' 2]]</ref> When the Mon Cala fleet prepared to escape the planet from the attacking First Order forces, Ackbar realized that they had a window of escape after Ri bought the fleet time. Commanding the fleet from his chair inside the cruiser, Ackbar ordered the vessels to escape before the remaining First Order forces arrived.<ref name="Allegiance 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:AftabsFlagshipMisdrawn-Allegiance4.png|thumb|right|230px|Ackbar's starship misdrawn as an MC85 Star Cruiser]]
The MC95 Star Cruiser appeared in the [[Allegiance 4|fourth issue]] of the [[2019]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: Allegiance]]''. The issue was written by [[Ethan Sacks]], illustrated by [[Luke Ross]], and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Allegiance 4" /> on [[October 30]], 2019.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/76705/journey_to_star_wars_the_rise_of_skywalker_-_allegiance_2019_4|text=Journey to Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker - Allegiance (2019) #4}}</ref> In one panel, the ship was erroneously depicted as an [[MC85 Star Cruiser]].<ref name="Allegiance 4" /> ''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]'' definitively confirmed that Aftab's cruiser was an MC95 Star Cruiser.<ref name="Comprehensive">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref>
