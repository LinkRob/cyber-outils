The '''Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing starfighter''', also known as the '''Alpha-3 Nimbus''', '''V-wing starfighter''', or '''''Nimbus'' fighter''', was a [[starfighter]] model manufactured by [[Kuat Systems Engineering]] near the end of the [[Clone Wars]]. It was the primary starfighter model employed by the [[Imperial Navy]] during the early years of the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Characteristics==
===Design===
[[File:Nimbus-class_V-wing_TFOWM.png|thumb|left|187x187px|The Alpha-3 Nimbus was a wedge-shaped and fast starfighter.]]
The Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class [[starfighter]]<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> was a sharp and wedge-shaped fighter, manufactured by [[Kuat Systems Engineering]], the same company that made [[Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Jedi starfighters.]] V-wings were agile and fast,<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref> with the ship having a top speed of 52,000 [[Kilometer per hour|kilometers per hour]]<ref name="CV NE" /> and maximum acceleration of 5,200G's.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> Cutting-edge technology was used to give the V-wing maneuverability, and the [[starship]] had two vertically placed<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> [[ion engine]]<ref name="TOWM" /> thrusters, allowing for surprisingly fast pitch-turnings. Thrusters were placed at the end of the starfighter, but, like a number of ships made for the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]], the V-wing was too compact to hold a [[hyperdrive]]. However, the V-wing did possess a powerful [[reactor]].<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" />

To maximize power for speed and agility, Kuat produced the starfighter without a pressurized cockpit. This required [[pilot]]s to wear sealed [[flight suit]]s equipped with life-support systems.<ref name="TOWM" /> This flight suit also allowed a pilot to eject. A single [[clone trooper pilot]], [[clone trooper]], [[TK stormtrooper]], and/or [[conscription trooper pilot]] flew the ship, and an independent [[Q7-series astromech droid]] backed up the [[Cloning|clone]]. This [[Astromech droid|astromech]] helped in-flight maintenance and navigation. Close to the astromech were two [[deflector shield]] heat sinks. Also included in the ship were [[repulsorlift]] vanes, at least one reactant tank, shield projector units, the power lines to the shield projectors, and forward [[sensor]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" />

[[File:JinataVwing.png|thumb|left|200px|A Jinata Security V-wing]]
Much like the larger [[Star Courier]] of [[Sienar Fleet Systems]], the V-wing had folding radiator wings and [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="TOWM" /> The wings, which were located at either side of the V-wing and reached above and below the craft, were the ship's most distinguishing feature<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> and on articulated collars, which let the wings move ninety degrees for landing. During intense activity, the radiator panels on the wings helped heat disposal. The wings radiators remained unfolded while the ship was in flight position. The [[laser]] [[cannon]]s could swivel on and had a rapid fire capability by being placed<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> on both of the wing struts<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" />/hubs. The cannons had a power feed and [[capacitor]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> There were at least two sub-models, the [[Alpha-3B "Besh"]]<ref name="V-wing 3B">{{FFGXW2|set=Nimbus-class V-Wing Expansion Pack|cardname=Alpha-3B "Besh"}}</ref> and [[Alpha-3E "Esk"|Alpha-3E "Esk."]]<ref name="V-wing 3E">{{FFGXW2|set=Nimbus-class V-Wing Expansion Pack|cardname=Alpha-3E "Esk"}}</ref>

===Role in combat===
[[File:JediCommander-SWZ.png|thumb|right|270px|A trio of V-wings escort General Plo Koon's Delta-7 interceptor on Cato Neimoidia.]]
The V-wing starfighter served as a support ship in the [[Republic Navy]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. The [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[warship]]s and [[carrier]]s deployed furious swarms of V-wings, and the fighters were used to defend [[fortress world]]s and part of [[fleet]] actions.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> Because of the ships's lack of a built-in hyperdrive, the V-wing required transportation into combat zones by carriers such as the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]].<ref name="TOWM" /> Because of the V-wing's maneuverability, it was more agile than the Republic's [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter]]. The ship was also a [[clone trooper]] pilot's best choice for a dogfight against the [[Vulture-class starfighter|vulture droid starfighters]] and [[droid tri-fighter]]s<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> used in the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|Confederacy navy]].<ref name="Episode III">{{Film|III}}</ref>

Because of its twin laser cannons, the V-wing was a deadly and surprising enemy. They were also a frustratingly elusive target and could be part of a mixed strike-force when used with [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptors]] and Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighters.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" />

==History==
===Republic service===
[[File:V-wing_SWDConv.png|thumb|left|250px|An Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wing flying over the [[Federal District]] during the Battle of Coruscant.]]
Alpha-3 ''Nimbus''-class V-wings were first deployed by the Galactic Republic in the final [[Standard day|days]] of the Clone Wars.<ref name="V-wing Fighter" /> Becoming a staple of the Republic military machine<ref name="Rise and Fall">''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''</ref> despite its late introduction,<ref name="V-wing Fighter" /> streamlined mass productions techniques let this ship take the [[V-19 Torrent starfighter]]'s place as the preferred compact space superiority starfighter.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018" /> At a point when he was working towards the rank of [[admiral]], [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] flew a V-wing as part of a group of three. [[Clone trooper pilot]] [[Klick (clone trooper)|Klick]] piloted a V-wing and flew in a space battle against vulture droids.<ref name="Stay Sharp">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2020/10/15/stay-sharp|text=Stay Sharp}}</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> V-wings served in the [[Battle of Coruscant]] against the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy]].<ref name="Episode III" /> Clone pilot [[Contrail]] flew a V-wing over the burning [[Federal District]] during the battle.<ref name="Stay Sharp" /> During [[Order 66]], two V-wings chased after [[Kelleran Beq]] and [[Din Grogu|Grogu]] in an effort to prevent them from leaving Coruscant, though the starfighters' lack of a hyperdrive cost them the chance to shoot down the fugitive Jedi as they fled into hyperspace.<ref name="The Foundling">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 20: The Foundling}}</ref>

===Imperial service===
====Early service====
With the conclusion of the Clone Wars and the aftermath of Order 66, V-wings continued to see extensive use under the [[Galactic Empire]] during throughout its reign.<ref name="Episode III" /> Notably, a V-wing [[Unidentified V-wing variant|variant]] was utilized as escort fighter for [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]'s personal [[Theta-class T-2c shuttle|''Theta''-class T-2c shuttle]],<ref name="Altaya" /> Some even took the V-19 Torrent starfighter's place in guarding [[Kamino]]'s cloning facilities.<ref name="Aftermath">{{TBB|Aftermath (episode)}}</ref> Others provided security for the Imperial-occupied Confederacy capital [[Raxus Secundus|Raxus]],<ref name="Common Ground">{{TBB|Common Ground}}</ref> and were even seen on [[Ryloth]].<ref name="Rescue on Ryloth">{{TBB|Rescue on Ryloth (episode)}}</ref> On [[Daro]], numerous V-wings chased [[Clone Force 99]]'s [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']] after the squad had rescued clone commando [[Gregor]] after he defected from training the first batch of [[TK stormtrooper]]s.<ref name="War-Mantle">{{TBB|War-Mantle}}</ref> On [[Serenno]], V-wings escorted several [[Class four container transport]]s in carrying [[Count]] [[Dooku]]'s [[Dooku's war chest|war chest]] to fund various projects in the works, and would provide air support for [[Clone Captain]] "[[Wilco]]" and [[Wilco's company|his company]] during their pursuit of Clone Force 99 through a [[Unidentified Serenno city|ruined Serennian city]].<ref name="Spoils of War">{{TBB|Spoils of War}}</ref>

When [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] and Clone Force 99 members [[Hunter]], [[Echo]], [[Tech]], and [[Wrecker]] infiltrated [[Vice Admiral]] [[Edmon Rampart|Rampart]]'s [[VZ-114|''Venator'' Star Destroyer]] in the [[Coruscant shipyard]], Tech and Echo activated the ship, which sounded an alarm. A [[Clone trooper|clone stormtrooper]] noted that there was a security breach, and he ordered patrol squads to trench Alpha-7. A [[squadron]] of six V-wings then took off from a nearby hangar and flew around the ''Venator''. Echo took control of the ship's [[DBY-827 heavy turbolaser turret|turbolasers]] and used them to shoot down two V-wings, which crashed into the shipyard. Four V-wings were still flying above the ''Venator'' when the clones ejected in an [[escape pod]].<ref name="Truth">{{TBB|Truth and Consequences}}</ref>

A squadron of V-wings and [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/Is]] were deployed from a ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer to [[Silla]] to assist in the recapture of a cloned [[Zillo Beast]] that had escaped Imperial captivity.<ref name="Metamorphis">{{TBB|Metamorphosis (episode)}}</ref> During or after [[18 BBY]],<ref name="SanctuaryDate">{{SanctuaryDate}}</ref> 3 V-wings were deployed from the ''[[Jurant]]'', ISB Officer [[Sendril Crane]]'s [[Gozanti-class cruiser]] during the [[Skirmish on Dallow]] where they chased the ''Marauder'' in an intense dogfight, though the [[Unidentified V-wing pilot|pilot]] of the last remaining fighter wisely ejected from it when [[Sohi]] used the shuttle's deflector shield to destroy it.<ref name="Sanctuary">''[[Sanctuary: A Bad Batch Novel]]''</ref> Several V-wings were deployed from another ''Venator-''class Star Destroyer to stop rogue clones Echo, Gregor, [[Fireball (clone trooper)|Fireball]], and [[Nemec]] from rescuing [[Howzer]] and two other rogue clones from being transported on an [[Unidentified Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser (Balmorra)|Imperial cruiser]].<ref name="Tipping Point">{{TBB|Tipping Point}}</ref> Later this same year, a squadron of V-wings were assigned to [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s personal compound, the [[Raven's Peak]], on the planet of [[Eriadu]]. After the rogue commandos of Clone Force 99 had [[Bombing of Raven's Peak|infiltrated]] the base<ref name="The Summit">{{TBB|The Summit}}</ref> and attempted to escape using the installation's [[Raven's Peak sky train|sky train]], numerous V-wings proceeded to strafe the train's [[Raven's Peak Sky Rail|trail line]], causing it to detach and ultimately lead to the [[death]] of Tech.<ref name="Plan 99">{{TBB|Plan 99 (episode)}}</ref>

V-wings were later utilized on [[Wayland]] to escort Palpatine's [[Emperor Palpatine's shuttle (Rho-class transport shuttle)|shuttle]] as he arrived to the [[Tantiss Base]] to check up on the status of [[Project Necromancer]], which was of great importance to the [[Emperor]]. Later, four V-wings were sent to stop rogue clones [[Omega]] and [[Crosshair]], as well as [[Batcher]], a [[Lurca hound]] rescued from termination, from escaping Wayland on a hijacked [[Rho-class transport shuttle|''Rho''-class transport shuttle]]. [[Doctor]] [[Royce Hemlock]] had ordered the starfighters to shoot the clones down, though he called them off when [[Emerie Karr]] informed him that Omega's blood had a substantial [[Midi-chlorian|M-count]].

V-wings also flew around [[Imperial Station 003]] on patrol, while three more were sent from Tantiss Base to shoot down [[Alpha-44]], which housed Hunter, Wrecker, Crosshair, and former Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart during their mission to infiltrate Tantiss to rescue Omega and all the Clone prisoners that were held there. After Tantiss was destroyed by the cloned [[Specimen A|Zillo Beast]] and Hemlock's death, V-wings, along with numerous [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle]]s and [[Rho-class transport shuttle]]s, collected the various resources during Tarkin's cleanup of Mount Tantiss.

====Persistence====
[[File:ChorinCityVWingFlyBy-BountyHunters18.png|thumb|right|300px|V-wings fly over [[Chorin City]] after it was [[Liberation of Chorin City|liberated]] by the Empire]]
The Empire would increasingly grow to rely on the new [[TIE series]] produced by [[Sienar Fleet Systems]],<ref name="Rise and Fall" /> with the infamous [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighter]] already seeing deployment by the time of the [[invasion of Mon Cala]]<ref name="Dark Lord 14">[[Darth Vader (2017) 14|''Darth Vader'' (2017) 14]]</ref> in 18 BBY.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Nonetheless, the V-wing would continue to see extensive use within the Empire.<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> In [[14 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> [[Moff]] Wilhuff Tarkin utilized V-wing starfighters in the [[Berch Teller campaign]].<ref name="Tarkin" /> In [[2 BBY]], Imperial Moff [[Ahr]] deployed numerous V-wings from his [[Ahr's Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] during the [[Attack on Namzor]]. After the V-wings had obliterated rebel positions, Ahr recalled the starfighters back to his ''Venator'' so that the rebel's [[coaxium]] supplies could be secured.<ref name="Age Vader 1">[[Age of Rebellion - Darth Vader 1|''Age of Rebellion - Darth Vader'' 1]]</ref>

By the time of the [[Galactic Civil War]], V-wings had largely been replaced by the TIE fighter.<ref name="Tarkin" /> Some would eventually end up in the hands of [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement agencies]], such as [[Jinata Security]].<ref name="Star Wars Battlefront 2" /> In [[5 ABY]], [[Wild Squadron]] of the [[New Republic]] made use of V-wings at the [[Battle of Jakku]].<ref name="Victory's Price">''[[Victory's Price: An Alphabet Squadron Novel]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
V-wings first appeared in [[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith|''Star Wars'': Episode III ''Revenge of the Sith'']].<ref name="Episode III" />

In "[[THE TWINS]]," an episode of ''[[Star Wars: Visions]]'', V-wings are used by [[TRIGGER Empire|Am's Galactic Empire]].<ref name="The Twins">{{Visions|THE TWINS}}</ref> The [[Neimoidian]] [[pirate]] [[Billow]] also used a V-Wing in the non-canon comic "[[Trouble on Takodana]]."<ref name="LEGO 55">{{LSWCite|55|Trouble on Takodana}}</ref>

In 2023, Jazwares revealed a V-Wing Starfighter as part of their Micro Galaxy Squadrons line. A second currently unknown variant was also revealed as part of the Series 5 line-up during [[Celebration Europe (2023)]]. This second variant was a “chase” and limited to 5,000 pieces worldwide. The Micro Galaxy Squadron ship is also the first toy/collectible V-Wing with correctly positioned wings during landing, unlike the [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] and [[Hasbro]] versions.
