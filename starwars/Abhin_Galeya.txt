'''Abhin Galeya''' is an actor who portrayed [[Salman Paak]] in ''[[Star Wars: Andor]]''.<ref name="Kassa" /> He is best known for playing police constable [[W:c:thebill:Arun Ghir|Arun Ghir]] in the British police procedural television series ''[[Wikipedia:The Bill|The Bill]]'', and Ash in the [[2012]] spy thriller film ''[[Wikipedia:Cleanskin (film)|Cleanskin]]''.
