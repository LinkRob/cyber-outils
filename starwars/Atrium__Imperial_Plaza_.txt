A vast '''atrium''' with [[glass]] walls<ref name="Welcome AD">{{Andor|Welcome to the Rebellion}} {{AD|Disney+}}</ref> rose in the center of a large reflecting pool on [[Imperial Plaza]], on the [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]]. The semi-spherical structure boasted a curved, elongated shell, with a smaller dome at its core that glowed at night.<ref name="Welcome" /> In [[2 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{AndorDate|209}}</ref> [[Senator]] [[Mon Mothma]] visited the deserted atrium to rehearse what would ultimately become her final address before the [[Imperial Senate]]. Shortly thereafter, she was forced to [[Escape from the Senate|flee]], prompting a swift and covert extraction.<ref name="Welcome" />

The atrium, along with other Senate District areas, was shot on location in [[Wikipedia:Valencia|Valencia]]'s [[Wikipedia:City of Arts and Sciences|City of Arts and Sciences]] complex in [[Wikipedia:Spain|Spain]], originally designed by architect [[Wikipedia:Santiago Calatrava|Santiago Calatrava]].<ref name="Worldbuilding">{{SW|url=news/andor-season-2-world-building-production-design-feature|text=The Secrets of Ghorman, Mina-Rau, and More: Creating the Worlds of Andor Season 2 - Updated}}</ref>
