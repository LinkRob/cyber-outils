A '''Fleet Commander''',<ref name="Victory's Price" /> or a '''fleet group commander''',<ref name="HC 45" /> was a [[Assignment|position]] given to an [[officer]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Captain]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]] served as the fleet commander of [[Jedi General]] [[Even Piell]]'s [[Even Piell's fleet group|fleet group]].<ref name="HC 45" /> During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Hellip]] served as a fleet commander and took over local operations following [[General]] [[Hera Syndulla]]'s [[Attack on Chadawa|battle]] with the loyalist forces of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]].<ref name="Victory's Price" /> [[Unidentified Mandalorian fleet commander|A Mandalorian]] of the [[Mandalorian resistance]] was a captain and held the position of fleet commander for the [[Mandalorian fleet]].<ref name="Chapter 23" />
