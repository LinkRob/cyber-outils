A [[Color|pink]] '''moon''' [[orbit]]ed the [[planet]] [[Eroudac]], which was located in the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and [[the Slice]] regions. While growing up at the [[Eroudac Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on Eroudac during the [[High Republic Era]], [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Jamil Sollis]] was able to see the [[moon]] from his dormitory.

By [[3 ABY]], a member of the [[Rokana Raiders]] defeated a [[squadron]] of [[drift-skipper]]s off the moon. In [[34 ABY]], the [[pilot]] [[Izal Garsea]] mentioned Eroudac's moon to her childhood friend [[Julen Rakab]] while inviting him to a date on Eroudac.

==Description==
A [[Color|pink]] [[moon]] [[orbit]]ed [[Eroudac]],<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> a [[planet]]<ref name="Traveler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge: Traveler's Guide to Batuu]]''</ref> located in the [[Eroudac system]] within the [[Doldur sector]] of<ref name="Appendix" /> the [[Mid Rim Territories]] and [[the Slice]] regions.<ref name="Location" /> It was situated in grid square P-15 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Coordinates" />

==History==
===High Republic Era===
[[File:ChuroSulNathRuneEroudac-THRAAnnualPhaseIII.jpg|thumb|right|260px|Eroudac's moon, rising above the Quiraawa Mountains]]
By [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Beware the Nameless date">Per the reasoning [[:Template:StarlightDate|here]], the events of ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', which take place a [[Standard year|year]] and a half after the [[Destruction of Starlight Beacon|destruction]] of [[Starlight Beacon]], must take place in [[228 BBY]]. As ''Beware the Nameless'' establishes that [[Jamil Sollis]] grew up at the [[Eroudac Jedi temple]] prior to the [[Jedi Order]]'s recall to [[Coruscant]], an event ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates to [[230 BBY]], he must have been stationed there by 230 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[human]] [[Jedi Initiate|Jedi youngling]] [[Jamil Sollis]] grew up at the [[Eroudac Jedi temple|Jedi temple]] on Eroudac. While at the [[Jedi temple|temple]], Sollis was always able to see the moon through his dormitory [[Viewport|skylight]].<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Beware the Nameless date" /> while stationed at the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] on<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> the [[Core Worlds]] planet<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Coruscant]], the [[youngling]] recalled being able to see Eroudac's pink moon when thinking about how he felt lost at the Coruscant Temple in comparison to his [[time]] on Eroudac.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

The [[Hutt]] [[Churo]] traveled to Eroudac<ref name="Heart of Eroudac">{{StoryCite|book=The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual|story=The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac}}</ref> later that [[Standard year|year]]<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}} "[[The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac]]" establishes its events occur after the events of ''Beware the Nameless'', therefore meaning they must take place no earlier than 228 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. ''[[The High Republic: A Valiant Vow]]'' depicts [[Churo]] working as the protégé of [[Glenna Kip]], who he had not heard of during "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac." Therefore, "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac" must be set before ''A Valiant Vow''. The events of ''A Valiant Vow'' take place nearly two years after the destruction of Starlight Beacon, meaning they and the events of "The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac" must also take place in 228 BBY per the aforementioned reasoning.</ref> to find a [[quiraawa flower]] to heal<ref name="Heart of Eroudac" /> his [[homeworld]]&mdash;the [[Hutt Space]]<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> planet [[Nal Hutta]], which had been [[Attack on Nal Hutta|devastated]] by<ref name="Heart of Eroudac" /> the [[Sentience|sentient]] [[carnivorous plant]]s known as<ref name="High Republic Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> the [[Drengir]]. While on the planet, Churo came across [[Jedi Knight]] [[Sul-Nath-Rune]]. As the two headed toward the [[Quiraawa Mountains]] to locate one of the [[flower]]s, the light from the moon's crescent shape shone down from the [[Local day|night]] sky.<ref name="Heart of Eroudac" />

===Later incidents===
{{Quote|I once bested a whole squadron of drift-skippers off the moon of Eroudac.|A Rokana Raider|audio=Rokana-MoonOfEroudac.flac|<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' {{C|''A Pirate's Fortune'' DLC}}</ref>}}
[[File:Batuu-system.png|thumb|left|260px|While on Batuu (right), Izal Garsea mentioned Eroudac's moon to her friend Julen Rakab]]
By [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a member of the [[Rokana Raiders]] bested an entire [[squadron]] of [[drift-skipper]]s off the moon.<ref name="Outlaws" /> That year,<ref name="Outlaws Date" /> while the individual was patrolling the [[Khepi Tomb]] in the [[Khepi system]], they boasted to another raider about their defeat of the squadron. Their companion grunted in response.<ref name="Outlaws" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="A Crash of Fate date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref> the [[pilot]] [[Izal Garsea]]&mdash;who had lived for a time on Eroudac&mdash;reunited with her childhood friend [[Julen Rakab]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet [[Batuu]] and invited him to meet her on Eroudac for a date. Rakab was at first confused about the location choice, and asked if it was the place where she had dropped out of an [[Eroudac academy|academy]]; Garsea replied affirmatively and explained to him that the planet had also been the last place she had lived with her [[parent]]s, noting that it also had a pink moon.<ref name="A Crash of Fate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eroudac's moon was first mentioned in the [[2019]] young-adult novel ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]].<ref name="A Crash of Fate" /> The moon later made its first appearance in "[[The Adventures of Churo the Hutt and the Heart of Eroudac]]," a [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] story included in the anthology ''[[The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual]]''. The story was written by Córdova, illustrated by [[Juan Samu]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]<ref name="Heart of Eroudac" /> on [[January 29]], [[2025]].<ref name="Dark Horse">{{DarkHorse|url=Comics/3015-809/Star-Wars-The-High-Republic-Adventures-Phase-III-Annual|text=Star Wars: The High Republic Adventures Phase III Annual}}</ref> Prior to the Annual's release, an uncolored depiction of the moon was revealed during the Dark Horse panel at [[Wikipedia:New York Comic Con|New York Comic Con]] on [[October 19]], [[2024]].<ref name="NYCC">{{SW|url=news/nycc-2024-lucasfilm-publishing-reveals|text=NYCC 2024: All the ''Star Wars'' News and Reveals from New York Comic-Con}}</ref>
