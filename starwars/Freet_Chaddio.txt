'''Freet Chaddio''' was an [[Abednedo]] individual who operated as a [[miner]] on the [[planet]] [[Tenoo]] during the [[High Republic Era]]. He was the owner of the [[droid]] [[MB-4]] and programmed the locations of all his [[gemstone]] mines into the droid's [[memory bank]], making MB-4 a target of a group of thieves.<ref name="A Mission to Remember" />
