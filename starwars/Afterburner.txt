An '''afterburner''' was a component used in atmospheric vehicles and [[starship]]s. The customized [[Rey's speeder|speeder]] built by [[Rey Skywalker|Rey]], a scavenger on the [[desert]] [[planet]] of [[Jakku]], was customized with racing-[[swoop]] afterburners.<ref name="TFA VD">''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref>
