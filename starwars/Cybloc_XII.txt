'''Cybloc XII''' was an astronomical object<ref name="TCW:CE" /> situated in the [[Cybloc system]] of the [[Outer Rim Territories]]' [[Meridian sector]].<ref name="Revival" /> It was the [[homeworld]] of the [[Snivvian]] [[Morlimur Snugg]], a [[superintendent]] on the [[InterGalactic Banking Clan]]'s [[Cybloc Transfer Station]]<ref name="TCW:CE" /> in the same [[star system]].<ref name="Revival" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Cybloc XII was first mentioned in the [[2021]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="TCW:CE" /> [[Cybloc XII/Legends|Cybloc XII]] was first introduced in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity in the [[1997]] novel ''[[Planet of Twilight]]'', written by [[Barbara Hambly]].<ref name="Planet of Twilight">''[[Planet of Twilight]]''</ref>
