This [[Color|blue]]-bladed '''lightsaber''' was the personal [[lightsaber]] of [[Jedi Master]] [[Adi Gallia]]. It replaced her [[Adi Gallia's first lightsaber|first]] lightsaber after it was destroyed on a mission.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref> She used it during many campaigns in the [[Clone Wars]] against foes, such as [[General]] [[Grievous]], [[Savage Opress]], and many others. [[Jedi]] Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] used it [[Skirmish on Florrum|briefly]] along with [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's third lightsaber|his own saber]] to hold his own against [[Darth]] [[Maul]] and his brother, Savage Opress after Gallia's [[death]] at the horns of the latter, using it to [[Dismemberment|cut]] off Opress' left arm.<ref name="Revival" />
