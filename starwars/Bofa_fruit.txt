'''Bofa fruit''' was a type of [[fruit]] produced on the [[planet]] [[Omaka]]. The bofa fruit was consumed by [[farmer]]s on the planet, who picked the wild fruit off of [[tree]]s. Bofa fruit could be prepared into [[Foodstuff|food]] in several manners, including being jellied or sliced. On [[Dunkutu Day]] in [[232 BBY]], Omakan farmers, with the assistance of [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Wes Vinik]] and [[Jedi Initiate|Initiates]] [[Kai Brightstar]], [[Lys Solay]], and [[Nubs]], prepared [[cracker]]s topped with bofa fruit jelly and slices. The [[Pooba]] [[youngling]] Nubs thought that the [[bofa fruit cracker]]s tasted better than [[gomgourd pie]], which he loved.<ref name="The Great Gomgourd Quest" />
