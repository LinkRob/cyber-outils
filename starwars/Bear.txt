'''Bears''' were a type of creature that lived in [[the galaxy]]. Upon seeing [[Lieutenant]] [[Wulfgar Kath]] of the [[First Order]], [[Resistance]] [[spy]] [[Vi Moradi]] noted he was built like a bear.<ref name="Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref> A [[species]] of bear was the [[garu-bear]], a type of hibernating bear.<ref name="Shadow of the Sith">''[[Shadow of the Sith]]''</ref> The [[droid]] [[Hunter (Grand Arena)|Hunter]] [[K-A0S]] referred to the [[Ewok]] Hunter [[Pilbush]] as a small bear.<ref name="SWH">''[[Star Wars: Hunters]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The memorable voice of [[Chewbacca]] was designed by [[Ben Burtt]], sound designer for all nine films. Creating Chewbacca's voice was Burtt's first job for ''[[Star Wars: Episode IV A New Hope|A New Hope]]''. He mixed together various animal growls for different emotions, including those of bears.<ref name="Much to Learn">{{SW|url=news/much-to-learn-you-still-have-8-things-you-might-not-know-about-wookiees|text=Much to Learn You Still Have: 8 Things You Might Not Know About Wookiees}}</ref> The most heavily used sound came from a cinnamon bear<ref name="Familiar Sounds">{{SW|url=news/drawing-from-the-present-familiar-creatures-in-a-galaxy-far-far-away|text=Drawing from the Present: Familiar Creatures in a Galaxy Far, Far Away|int=Back from the Drawing Board|nolive=1}}</ref> named [[Pooh]].<ref name="Much to Learn" /> Burtt also used a black bear named [[Tarik]],<ref name="Familiar Sounds" /> who died of congestive heart failure, liver disease, and cancer in [[October]], [[1994]] at the age of 16.<ref name="Insider 24">[[Star Wars Insider 24|''Star Wars Insider'' 24]]</ref>

In ''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', [[Mi'no Teest]] indirectly mentioned [[Ewok]]s as "teddy bears."<ref name="LEGO">''[[LEGO Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]''</ref>
