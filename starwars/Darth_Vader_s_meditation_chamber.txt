'''Darth Vader's meditation chamber''', also known as the '''Qabbrat''', was a life support pod that allowed the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] to survive for extended periods without the [[helmet]] and [[Armor|mask]] of [[Darth Vader's armor|his suit]]. The injuries Vader sustained during his [[Duel on Mustafar|duel]] against [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] on [[Mustafar]] normally made this impossible.

==Description==
{{Quote|Yes. There is power here.''"<br />"''The dark lord would enter this space when he required&hellip;''clarity''. He would spend hours inside&hellip;sometimes ''days''.|Kylo Ren and Vaneé|<ref name="LoV 10">[[Legacy of Vader 10|''Legacy of Vader'' 10]]</ref>}}
[[File:Darth_Vader_Dark_Lord_of_the_Sith_13_Textless.jpg|thumb|left|220px|Vader in his chamber]]
Some three [[meter]]s in diameter,<ref name="Tarkin" /> the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]]'s [[meditation chamber]]<ref name="sw100">''[[Star Wars 100 Objects]]''</ref> was flattened on the bottom, designed to nestle in a hexagonal dais. It had neither a proper hatch nor a control panel; and had two longitudinal seams which allowed the device to open into two hemispheres.<ref name="Tarkin" /> The chamber, also known as the "Qabbrat," was part of Darth Vader's personal quarters on his [[flagship]] ''[[Executor]]''<ref name="ManWarriorCommander">''[[Star Wars Master Models: Darth Vader]]''</ref> or placed within other locations the Sith Lord found himself based.<ref name="Tarkin" />

The chamber supplied Vader with high pressure and sterilized hyper-[[oxygen]], which, combined, allowed Vader a chance to safely remove his [[Darth Vader's armor|life-support armor]], even his [[helmet]],<ref name="ManWarriorCommander" /> while the chamber was enclosed around him.<ref name="Episode V" /> While within the life support pod,<ref name="LOS" /> Vader would also meditate.<ref name="Tarkin" />

==History==
Five [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Proclamation of the New Order]], [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] was in possession of a mobile meditation chamber which he ordered to be transferred from a [[Victory-class Star Destroyer|''Victory''-class]] [[Star Destroyer]] to [[Moff]] [[Wilhuff Tarkin]]'s ''[[Carrion Spike]]'' for their [[mission to Murkhana]]. During the transferral, Vader selected a dozen [[stormtrooper]]s to accompany him and had them load the chamber onto Tarkin's [[corvette]]; during the maneuver, the sphere collided briefly with the edge of the [[Starship|ship's]] cargo hold hatch, for which Vader grew angry at [[Sergeant]] [[Crest]], the one controlling the [[repulsorlift]] crane.<ref name="Tarkin" />

When the ''Carrion Spike'' was stolen by [[Berch Teller]]'s [[Berch Teller's rebel cell|rebel cell]] on [[Murkhana]], Vader used the meditation chamber as a [[homing beacon]] of sorts, allowing him to track and pinpoint the rebels' exact location despite the corvette's [[Cloaking device|stealth system]]. Thanks to it, the [[Sith Lord]] and Tarkin pursued the ship from Murkhana to the [[Fial system|Fial]] and [[Galidraan system|Galidraan]] [[Star system|systems]]. During the Imperial [[Attack on Galidraan Station|attack]] on [[Galidraan Station]], Teller and [[Artoz]] decided to jettison the device just as they made their escape to [[Lucazec]].<ref name="Tarkin" />

[[File:VaderChamber.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Vader communicates with the bridge of ''[[Executor]]'' from his meditation chamber.]]
Vader acquired a new meditation chamber, which he kept on his [[flagship]], the [[Executor-class Star Dreadnought|''Executor''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Executor]]''. In the prelude to the [[Battle of Hoth]], Vader was using the chamber when [[General]] [[Maximilian Veers]] informed him that [[Admiral]] [[Kendal Ozzel]] had [[Battle of Hoth|emerged]] from [[lightspeed]] too close to [[Hoth]], alerting the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Alliance's]] [[Echo Base]]. Vader gave Veers orders to prepare his strike force for a surface attack on Echo Base. After Veers had exited the room, Vader activated a viewscreen mounted outside the chamber and spoke with both Ozzel and [[Captain]] [[Firmus Piett]]. During the conversation, Ozzel was suddenly overcome with the agonies of death throes as Vader [[Force choke]]d him to [[death]] for his incompetence, and then appointed Piett the new Admiral on the spot.<ref name="Episode V" />

Piett later entered before Vader had finished putting on his [[Darth Vader's armor|mask]], and informed him that the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' had entered an [[Hoth asteroid field|asteroid field]]. Vader then ordered the entirety of [[Death Squadron]] to begin sweeping the [[Asteroid field|field]] so as to find [[Han Solo]]'s [[freighter]].<ref name="Episode V" />
