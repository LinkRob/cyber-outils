The '''Aradonti Ravine''' was a gap between building blocks in [[Imperial City|Galactic City]], the [[ecumenopolis]] [[city]] that covered the surface of the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. It was situated south west of the planet's [[Federal District]] and close to the [[CoCo Town|Collective Commerce District]]. During the [[Clone Wars]], it housed [[Ziro's Palace]], a [[nightclub]] run by the [[crime lord]] [[Ziro Desilijic Tiure]] that was anchored into one of the ravine's walls.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of the [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]], which includes the appearance of the ravine, to [[22 BBY]].</ref> [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] [[pilot]]ed her [[J-type Naboo star skiff]] through the ravine and landed upon the landing pad outside Ziro's Palace in order to visit him and ask for his aid. After Amidala was captured by Ziro, her [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] traveled through the ravine with [[Clone Commander]] [[Fox|CC-1010]] "Fox" and a [[squad]] of [[Coruscant Guard]] [[clone trooper]]s on board the senator's [[H-type Nubian yacht]], landing on the same pad Amidala had and then [[Raid on Ziro's Palace|raiding]] the palace, arresting Ziro and rescuing Amidala.<ref name="TCWf" />

==Behind the scenes==
Aradonti's Ravine first appeared in the [[2008]] film, [[Star Wars: The Clone Wars (film)|''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' film]].<ref name="TCWf" /> It was first identified in the [[2018]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].
