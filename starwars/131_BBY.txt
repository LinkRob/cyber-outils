The following events took place in or around the [[Standard year|year]] '''131 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3146 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''7846''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Behind the scenes==
''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' dates the [[B'ankora homeworld cataclysm]] and the tenure of [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Chasen Piian]] to 131 BBY,<ref name="Rebel Dossier">According to ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' the [[B'ankora]] were granted refuge 112 [[Standard year|years]] before the end of the [[Clone Wars]]. As ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' places the end of the Clone Wars in [[19 BBY]], it can be deduced that the [[B'ankor Refuge]] was established on [[Coruscant]] in 131 BBY.</ref> but ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the event in [[1300 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> In the novel ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', the [[B'ankora]] had lived in the [[B'ankor Refuge]] "for more than fifty generations" and had planted a copse of gargantuan trees "centuries earlier" by the time of the novel.<ref name="Catalyst">''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]''</ref> Because 1300 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] lines up with what is established in ''Catalyst'', this article assumes ''Timelines'' is correct.
