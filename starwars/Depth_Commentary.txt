'''Depth Commentary''' is a web-exclusive feature included on the [[Star Wars home video releases#Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'' DVD]].<ref name="DVD">{{FilmVideo|2}}</ref> By inserting the DVD on a computer DVD-ROM, users were able to access dvd.starwars.com, a subdomain of [[StarWars.com]], which was home to several features, including the Depth Commentary. The feature consisted on a software from [[InterActual]] running along the movie and provided text-based commentaries.<ref name="HB">{{HBCite|73}}</ref>
