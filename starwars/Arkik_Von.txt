'''Arkik Von''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Force-sensitive]] [[Geonosian]] who lived during the [[High Republic Era]].<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" /> He was formerly a member of the [[Sorcerers of Tund]].<ref name="THRCE" /> [[Standard decade|Decades]] before<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of [[The High Republic Adventures (2022) 1|''The High Republic Adventures'' (2022) 1]] to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Von was conned out of the [[planet]] [[Takodana]] by the [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]] [[Maz Kanata]] and sought revenge.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" /> Around [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> he led the criminal group known as the [[Dank Graks]]<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" /> and had plans to acquire a devastating weapon<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 3">{{Previews|url=Catalog/NOV220432|text=STAR WARS HIGH REPUBLIC ADVENTURES #3 (OF 8)}}</ref> in order to rid Takodana of the [[Jedi Order]] and Kanata.<ref name="The High Republic Adventures 1" />
