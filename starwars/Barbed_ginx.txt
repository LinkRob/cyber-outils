The '''Barbed Ginx''' was a relatively harmless, curious [[Amphibian/Legends|amphibian]] that frequented [[Makeb]]'s marshes.<ref name="Barbed ginx" /> They were first discovered by scientist [[Dep Aila]] on Makeb when he found the creatures surrounding his speeder having pulled the exterior casing off in search of food. The "barbed" in their name refers to the barbed "thumb" which allows them to peel bark from trees or fallen logs when foraging for insects. Ginx have a genetic relation to the more aggressive [[Vrake (species)|Vrakes]].<ref name="Barbed ginx">"{{TORcite|type=codex|Barbed ginx}}</ref> A dark [[Color/Legends|green]] variety of Swamp Ginx was common amid the [[Endless Swamp]] of the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Zakuul]]. The species was also prominent on the [[Yavin 4/Legends|fourth]] [[Moon/Legends|moon]] of [[Yavin Prime/Legends|Yavin]].<ref name=":0" /> Some of them were also present on the [[Chiss/Legends|Chiss]] world of [[Copero/Legends|Copero]].<ref name=":0" /> 

The small Ginx were of a relatively harmless nature, the large Ginx were aggressive.<ref name=":0" /> Ginx could be trained by a skilled handler to perform some tasks.<ref name="cartel">[[Cartel Market|''Star Wars: The Old Republic'' Cartel Market]]</ref>
