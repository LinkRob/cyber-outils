'''Alxa Kress''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Anomid]] [[Jedi Master]] who at one point served in the [[Jedi High Council|High Council]] of the [[Jedi Order]]. He had a fondness for [[Rodian Theater]], and even taught [[Mace Windu]] how to perform the works of [[Harido Kavila]] in the native [[Rodian (language)|Rodese]].<ref name="Obi-Wan & Anakin" />
