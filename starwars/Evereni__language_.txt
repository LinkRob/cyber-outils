'''Evereni''' was the [[language]] of the [[species]] of the [[Evereni|same name]] that originated from their [[homeworld]] [[Everon]]. The Evereni species named their families for actions, and their name for themselves&mdash;"Evereni"&mdash;meant "caretakers." After the species [[Everon exodus|abandoned]] their world in [[500 BBY]], the Evereni continued to use the language for [[Standard century|centuries]]. The Evereni [[pirate]] leader [[Marda Ro]] discovered the language during her [[Marda Ro's hunt|encounter]] with another Evereni who called himself "[[Isren]]," who taught her some phrases in Evereni. Ro thought the language sounded slippery and later mentioned it in [[Marda Ro's journal|journal entries]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]].

By [[252 BBY]], her [[Gender|granddaughter]] [[Shalla Ro]] taught her own grandson [[Marchion Ro]]&mdash;who had listened to his great-great-grandmother's messages mentioning Evereni by that same [[Standard year|year]]&mdash;about the value of things connected to the name Ro. He recalled this lesson [[228 BBY|years]] later while digging through his family's records.

[[File:EvereniCaretaker-THREOTS1.png|thumb|right|320px|An Evereni taking care of Everon's nature]]
Evereni was the [[language]] of the tempest [[planet]] [[Everon]]'s native [[species]], the [[humanoid]] [[Evereni]]. The Evereni embraced their [[homeworld]]'s nature and took care of Everon, calling themselves "caretakers" in their language.<ref name="EotS1" /> Evereni families were named for actions and passed these names down as commands.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

[[Marda Ro]]&mdash;the Evereni<ref name="Closed Fist" /> founder of a [[Pirate|marauder]] group who would eventually become the [[Nihil]]<ref name="GrattonInterview">{{YouTube|video=llnvoEG0CjE|text=Interview with Tessa Gratton: "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" (TALES OF LIGHT & LIFE)|channel=@FriendsOfTheForce|name=[[Friends of the Force]]|date=2023-10-06}}</ref>&mdash;considered the sound of the language to be slippery the first time she heard it.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

==History==
{{Quote|Isren. You can call me that.''"<br />"''My name is Marda Ro. I am looking for more of us.|"Isren" and Marda Ro|<ref name="Closed Fist" />}}
The form of speech originated on Everon and was in use by the [[time]] its inhabitants, the [[The Wanderers|last of the Evereni]], [[Everon exodus|abandoned]] the planet<ref name="EotS1" /> in [[500 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> It continued to be used by surviving Evereni in the [[Standard century|centuries]] after the departure from Everon.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

[[File:MardaRo-PathOfDeceit.png|thumb|left|215px|Marda Ro, whose family name meant "to look, to see"]]
At some point no earlier than [[379 BBY]],<ref name="IsrenDate">The segment of "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]" depicting a [[Unidentified Jedi (Marda Ro)|Jedi's]] [[death]] onboard the ''[[Gaze Electric]]'' takes place around [[379 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:ClosedFistJediDeathDate|here]]. [[Marda Ro]] met "[[Isren]]" after the [[Jedi]]'s death; therefore, she must have met him no earlier than 379 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref> Marda Ro [[Marda Ro's hunt|tracked]] down "[[Isren]]," an Evereni who, instead of his name, used the Evereni word meaning "old uncle" to refer to himself. Isren was on<ref name="Closed Fist" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="RylothLocation">{{Databank|ryloth|Ryloth}}</ref> planet [[Ryloth]], [[hunting]] the creatures known as [[lylek]]s in [[Ryloth's equatorial forest]]s. Raised outside of the Evereni culture, Ro desired to know more about<ref name="Closed Fist" /> her<ref name="Temptation">''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]''</ref> species, and Isren told her about the Evereni and introduced her to their language, teaching her some phrases in Evereni. However, he refused to tell Ro the meaning of her last name.<ref name="Closed Fist" />

Ro eventually located other Evereni, the [[Faers|siblings]] [[Vika Faer|Vika]] and [[Velya Faer]]. They taught Ro more about the Evereni and their language, including Evereni naming customs and her last name's meaning. Ro mentioned both of the encounters and some of the words she had learned in [[Marda Ro's journal|messages]] she recorded for her [[Ro family|descendants]], whom she affectionately refered to as her "Riferi," the Evereni word for "legacy."<ref name="Closed Fist" />

By [[252 BBY]],<ref name="ShallaDate">"The Lie" chapter in [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|''The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' 1]], which includes [[Shalla Ro]]'s [[death]], features [[Asgar Ro]]'s ascension to [[Eye of the Nihil]], an event dated to [[252 BBY]] by ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''. As ''[[The High Republic: Temptation of the Force]]'' establishes she introduced [[Marchion Ro]] to [[Marda Ro]]'s [[Marda Ro's journal|journal]], he must have listened to it by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Ro's [[Gender|granddaughter]]<ref name="Temptation" /> [[Shalla Ro]]<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]''</ref> had taught her own grandson [[Marchion Ro]] the usefulness of anything connected to the name Ro, including Marda's journal.<ref name="Temptation" /> By that same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="ShallaDate" /> Marchion had listened to the memoirs, including the entries where Evereni was mentioned.<ref name="Temptation" /> In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> Marchion&mdash;who had by then become the incumbent [[Eye of the Nihil]]&mdash;was familiar with the language and knew at least one of its words,<ref name="EotS1" /> and [[228 BBY|two years]] later,<ref name="TemptationDate">{{StarlightDate|''The High Republic: Temptation of the Force''|one [[Standard year|year]] and six weeks after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> he recalled the lesson he was taught by his grandmother while digging through his family's records.<ref name="Temptation" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|It's really hard to find naming conventions in ''Star Wars'' and that's one of my fundamental, like, first things I do when I'm writing a book. ''[&hellip;] [It]'' is a really fundamental worldbuilding block for me and so I was like 'Oh my gosh, that's the first thing I'm gonna do! I'm gonna give the Evereni a naming convention!'|Tessa Gratton talking about the Evereni naming convention in an interview|<ref name="GrattonInterview" />}}
[[File:TessaGratton.jpg|thumb|right|200px|Tessa Gratton, creator of the Evereni language's naming convention]]
Evereni was indirectly mentioned in the [[The High Republic: Eye of the Storm 1|first issue]] of the [[2022]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] miniseries ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic: Eye of the Storm]]''. The issue was written by [[Charles Soule]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="EotS1" /> on [[January 12]], 2022, as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase I.<ref name="Reader's Guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> Prior to the comic's release, the language was first indirectly mentioned in a preview of the issue that was published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[January 5]] of the same year.<ref name="SWcomPreview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-the-high-republic-eye-of-the-storm-1-exclusive-preview|text=Embrace the Tempest in Marvel's ''Star Wars: The High Republic: Eye of the Storm'' #1 - Exclusive Preview}}</ref>

The language later appeared in and was identified by name in the short story "[[A Closed Fist Has No Claws]]," written by [[Tessa Gratton]] and published in the [[2023]] young-adult anthology ''[[The High Republic: Tales of Light and Life]]''.<ref name="Closed Fist" /> In an interview, Gratton revealed that<ref name="GrattonInterview" /> they<ref name="GrattonPronouns">{{Bluesky|username=tessagratton.bsky.social|name=[[Tessa Gratton]]|quote=she/he/they|archivedate=20251206043541}}</ref> are frustrated by the absence of naming conventions in ''[[Star Wars]]'', which motivated her decision to create one for the Evereni. The naming convention was one of the first parts of the story he created. Gratton also mentioned that expansions on Evereni lore introduced in ''Eye of the Storm''<ref name="GrattonInterview" />&mdash;such as the language, which "A Closed Fist Has No Claws" provided more details for<ref name="Closed Fist" />&mdash;were her second favorite part of writing the short story.<ref name="GrattonInterview" />
