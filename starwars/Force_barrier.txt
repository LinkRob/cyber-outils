'''Force barrier''' was a defensive [[Force power]] composed of [[The Force|Force]] energy, used to block out any potentially deadly elements.

==Description==
[[File:GroguForceBarrier-Ch24TheReturn.png|thumb|300px|left|Grogu created a Force barrier to protect himself, Din Djarin and Bo-Katan Kryze from the flames of an exploding light cruiser.]]
Force barrier was a defensive [[Force power|technique]] within [[the Force]] composed of Force energy, used to block out any potentially deadly elements. The technique itself was an offshoot of [[telekinesis]] and was aligned to neither the [[Light side of the Force|light]] nor [[Dark side of the Force|dark side]] but was a neutral ability any [[Force-sensitive|Force user]] could learn and deploy.<ref name="The Zillo Beast Strikes Back" /> Depending on the individual, the barrier could manifest in the form of a tangible, as well as potentially visible surface in a similar manner to [[deflector shield]]s,<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref> or, more commonly, possess no physical form at all, instead manifesting as an immaterial field of telekinetically-derived force used to [[Force Stasis|impede]], [[Force push|repel]], or [[Force deflection|redirect]] intended targets away from the user.<ref name="The Zillo Beast Strikes Back" /> While normally only able to withstand a few attacks before collapsing, at its most powerful, this technique could temporarily insulate its user against lethal impacts, explosions and even the vacuum of space.<ref name="IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref><ref name="Star Wars: Episode VIII The Last Jedi">{{Film|VIII}}</ref>

==Users==
[[File:ForceBarrier-ZBSB.png|thumb|300px|right|Yoda, Aayla Secura, and Anakin Skywalker used a Force barrier to block a cloud of poison gas during the Zillo Beast incident.]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]], [[Jedi Master]] [[Aayla Secura]], and [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker, used this power to stop a poisonous gas from reaching them as it was [[Zillo Beast incident|being deployed]] against the [[Zillo Beast]].<ref name="The Zillo Beast Strikes Back" /> [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] used this ability on several occasions to protect himself from environmental hazards like falling debris, oceanic pressure from being submerged in the depths of [[Mon Cala]], and explosions of fire or shock-waves.<ref name="Jedi Challenges">''[[Star Wars: Jedi Challenges]]''</ref> [[Kanan Jarrus]] used a barrier of Force energy to hold back an explosion, saving his [[Spectres|fellow rebels]] before his [[death]].<ref name="Jedi Night">{{Rebels|Jedi Night}}</ref> [[Ezra Bridger]], [[Ahsoka Tano]],<ref name="AWBW">{{Rebels|A World Between Worlds}}</ref> [[Tenth Brother]], [[Sixth Brother]], and [[Ninth Sister]] also used this power.<ref name="Dark Lord 14" />

While escaping from [[Fortress Inquisitorius]] on [[Nur]], [[Cere Junda]] projected a barrier to prevent [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] from harming her and [[Cal Kestis]] with [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|his]] [[lightsaber]]. Vader attempted to stab through it, partly succeeding, until Junda and Kestis escaped. Shortly thereafter, Vader used a barrier to stop seawater from engulfing him.<ref name="Jedi: Fallen Order">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order]]''</ref>

[[File:TheHandThing-SWL98.png|thumb|300px|left|Grogu holds back wave of fire from a flamethrower.]]
In [[9 ABY]], [[Din Grogu|Grogu]], an infant Force-sensitive member of [[Yoda's species]], applied a form of the technique to hold back and deflect a blast of fire coming from an [[Incinerator stormtrooper|incinerator trooper]].<ref name="Chapter 8">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 8: Redemption}}</ref> He also used the technique to protect [[Din Djarin]] and [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] during the [[Liberation of Mandalore|destruction]] of the [[Mandalore Imperial base|Imperial base]] on [[Mandalore]].<ref name="Chapter 24">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 24: The Return}}</ref>

In [[34 ABY]], [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]] instinctively used a version of this technique and his [[Force Stasis]] ability to stop [[Chewbacca]]'s [[bowcaster]] [[Quarrel|bolt]] from causing fatal damage and killing him.<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> He used Force barrier during his ship crash on [[Pasaana]] and also on a planet in a ship with [[Armitage Hux|Hux]], which in both cases left him unharmed.<ref name="Age of Resistance - General Hux 1">[[Age of Resistance - General Hux 1|''Age of Resistance - General Hux'' 1]]</ref><ref name="Star Wars: Episode IX The Rise of Skywalker">{{Film|IX}}</ref>
