'''Bweena''' was a [[Gender|female]] [[Quarren]] who was present at the [[Frozen Kova]] [[cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]]. She played [[Sabacc]] with [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and [[Zel Gris]].

==Biography==
Bweena was a [[Gender|female]] [[Quarren]] who lived in the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. During that time, she visited the [[Frozen Kova]], the only [[cantina]] on the [[planet]] [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]]. There, Bweena engaged in several hands of [[Sabacc]] with the [[smuggler]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and the [[Lynna]] [[Zel Gris]], [[strategy instructor]] to the [[Hynestian]] [[Princess]] [[Rinetta Gan]]. Early in their play, Calrissian lost multiple hands in a row to Bweena and Gris. Later his luck changed and the Quarren lost a substantial amount of [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] to him. As her credits dwindled, she became visibly worried and irritated, and ultimately folded to avoid further loss of [[Currency|money]]. Because she was a an excellent player, Calrissian, who always enjoyed playing Sabacc against others with skill, greatly enjoyed their game. As Bweena left, he told Bweena if she was ever in that area of [[the galaxy]] againt to stop and say hello. She responded with a blooping noise that may have been an agreement or a rude retort.<ref name="LL" />

==Personality and traits==
Bweena had facial [[Limb|tendrils]] [[Color|blue-green]] skin that glistened in the light. Losing at Sabacc made her worried and irritated.<ref name="LL" />

==Skills and abilities==
Bweena was an excellent Sabacc player, earning her the respect of Lando Calrissian.<ref name="LL" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bweena first appeared in the [[2018]] middle-grade novel ''[[Lando's Luck]]'' written by [[Justina Ireland]].<ref name="LL" />
