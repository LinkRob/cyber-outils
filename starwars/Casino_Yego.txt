'''''Casino Yego''''' was a casino ship that was located above [[Glee Anselm]]<ref name="Inquisitors 3" /> between [[12 BBY]] and [[9 BBY]].<ref name="Date">The events of [[Inquisitors 3|''Inquisitors'' 3]] take place between [[12 BBY]] and [[9 BBY]] per the reasoning [[Template:InquisitorsDate|here]].</ref> In this time, the [[Seventh Sister|Seventh]] and [[Ninth Sister]]s met [[Eorgus]] aboard the ship to collect information regarding the whereabouts of [[Tensu Run]]. After successfully extracting the information, the [[Inquisitor]]s executed Eorgus.<ref name="Inquisitors 3" />
