{{Quote|Took me more time, effort, and mileage than I'd hoped to finally get my hands on a loralora, but there's no doubt that these fragrant, sweet-glazed wings were worth the wait.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
'''Aromatic Loralora Wings''' were the [[Limb|wings]] of a [[loralora bird]] made into a fragrant and sweet-glazed [[Foodstuff|dish]]. After failing to purchase a loralora [[bird]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]] in [[34 ABY]], the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] visited the birds' [[homeworld]], [[Ketz]], during a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] and declared that the wings had been worth the additional effort, including a recipe for the dish in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', in [[35 ABY]].

==Description==
{{Quote|One of the animals in her menagerie happened to be a loralora bird, an avian species that I'd always heard was particularly succulent.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
Aromatic Loralora Wings were a type of fragrant, sweet-glazed [[Foodstuff|wings]] made using the [[limb]]s of the [[loralora bird]] [[Homeworld|from]] the [[Colonies]] [[jungle]] [[moon]] [[Ketz]]. The wings were white meat while the glaze was [[Color|brown]], with a sliced, green garnish scattered over the top. They were edible to members of the [[Artiodac]] [[species]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|But before I could buy it to find out, the bird's cage got opened in some sorta street-side scuffle and it flew off to freedom. (Guess the little guy was lucky.) Fortunately, I managed to convince Bina to tell me where I could find more of 'em.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Ultimate Cookbook''|<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />}}
[[File:Creature_Stall_TGTB.jpg|thumb|right|295px|alt=The interior of the Creature Stall contains a central blue crate with a caged Tooka on top surrounded by floor to ceiling shelves filled with toys of various animals.|Strono Tuggs failed to buy a loralora bird at the Creature Stall (pictured), but tracked them down elsewhere to make Aromatic Loralora Wings.]]
Having heard that loralora birds were particularly succulent, the Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] intended to purchase one such [[Lucky (loralora bird)|bird]] being sold at the [[Creature Stall]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the planet [[Batuu]].<ref name="ACoF">''[[A Crash of Fate]]''</ref> However, before he got the chance, the [[bird]] was freed from its cage<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> by the local [[Julen Rakab]] as part of a distraction and escaped<ref name="ACoF" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[A Crash of Fate]]'' to [[34 ABY]].</ref>

Tuggs then convinced the stall's owner, [[Bina]], to tell him where he could find more of the birds and then made sure to include Ketz on a culinary tour of [[the galaxy]] that he took after publishing<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]''.<ref name="LifeDay">''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]''</ref> Despite the amount of [[time]], effort, and mileage it had taken him to taste a loralora bird, Tuggs believed the wings were worth the wait and included a recipe for them in his third cookbook, ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in [[35 ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Aromatic Loralora Wings were created as a recipe for the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]] with photography by [[Ted Thomas]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the wings makes use of [[Wikipedia:Chicken as food|chicken wings]], [[Wikipedia:Sodium bicarbonate|baking soda]], [[Wikipedia:Vegetable oil|vegetable oil]], minced [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], minced [[Wikipedia:Ginger|ginger]], [[Wikipedia:Soy sauce#Traditional|tamari]], [[Wikipedia:Mirin|mirin]], [[Wikipedia:Honey|honey]], [[Wikipedia:Sesame oil|sesame oil]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], and diced [[Wikipedia:Scallion|green onions]].<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
