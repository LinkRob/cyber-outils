The '''Andor family''' was a [[human]] family that included [[Clem Andor]], [[Maarva Carassi Andor]], and their adopted son, [[Cassian Jeron Andor]].<ref name="That Would Be Me" /> In [[5 BBY]],<ref name="Kassa" /> the family resided in a [[Andor household|house]] on [[Ferrix]].<ref name="That Would Be Me" /> The [[droid]] [[B2EMO]] was also part of the family,<ref name="B2EMO">{{Databank|b2emo|B2EMO}}</ref> as were [[Jezzi]] and [[Sammo]].<ref name="That Would Be Me" />
