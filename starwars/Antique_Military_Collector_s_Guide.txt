The '''''Antique Military Collector's Guide''''' was a written guide for [[Collector|collecting]] military antiques, such as [[stormtrooper armor]]. [[Karr Nuq Sin]], a [[Force-sensitive]] teenager who collected such items in the hopes they would trigger glimpses of the antiques' history with his [[Force power|power]] of [[psychometry]], owned a copy of the Guide on a [[datapad]]. The guide gave details about how much military antiques could be bought and sold for, and revealed that he had been overpaying and spending too many [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]]. When [[Maize Raynshi]], a new friend who attended Nuq Sin's [[Unidentified school (Merokia)|school]], discovered his copy on a datapad in his home and asked what the publication told him, he admitted that was what he had learned.<ref name="Force Collector" />
