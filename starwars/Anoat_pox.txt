The '''Anoat pox''' was a [[disease]] that shared its name with the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Anoat]]. The disease resulted in [[color]]ed marks on the victim's skin, among other symptoms.<ref name="Daxxus" /> Sometime before [[5 ABY]],<ref name="time">''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''&mdash;Crew Member: "Daxxus" describes the deterring of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] inspection in the past tense. The [[Star Wars video games|game]] is set during [[Ubrik Adelhard]]'s [[Iron Blockade]]. According to ''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]'', Adelhard was defeated just prior to the [[Battle of Jakku]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[5 ABY]]. As such, the events of ''Uprising'' must take place by that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[Blarina]] [[Daxxus]], with [[berry]] stains on their face, described the symptoms suffered due to contraction of the Anoat pox to avoid an inspection of their cargo by [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]]. The Blarina later explained how the inspection was avoided to [[the Smuggler]], the leader of the [[Uprising]] with whom the Blarina had agreed to work for.<ref name="Daxxus" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Anoat pox was mentioned in [[Kabam]]'s [[2015]] [[Star Wars video games|mobile game]] ''[[Star Wars: Uprising]]''<ref name="Daxxus" /> prior to the game's shutdown in [[2016]].<ref name="Shutting down">{{Kabam|subdomain=community|url=forums/showthread.php?675751-Star-Wars-Uprising-is-Shutting-Down|text=Discussion Thread: Star Wars Uprising is Shutting Down|date=2016-09-22}}</ref> The pox was mentioned in conversation with Daxxus, a character that could be obtained by players in-game.<ref name="Daxxus" />
