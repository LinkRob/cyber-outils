The '''Aurodium Sword''' was a [[company]] of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] active during the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]],<ref name="Collapse" /> fought from [[22 BBY]] to [[19 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Relatively new by the Clone Wars, the company was secretly headed by a [[Genetically defective clone|defective]] [[Cloning|clone]] [[Advanced Recon Commando]] from the Republic, [[Muzzle]], while largely incorporating [[Wookiee warrior]]s. In the war, they preferred assignments that provided security for Republic corporate members such as [[Raith Sienar]],<ref name="Collapse" /> the [[Chief Executive Officer]] of [[Sienar Fleet Systems]].<ref name="TTFOWM">''[[TIE Fighter Owners' Workshop Manual]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The Aurodium Sword was introduced in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] via a mention in ''[[Collapse of the Republic]]'', a [[2019]] supplement to [[Fantasy Flight Games]]' series of ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' products.<ref name="Collapse" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Aurodium Sword/Legends|Aurodium Sword]] was introduced in "[[Underworld: A Galaxy of Scum and Villainy]]," an article authored by [[Ryan Kaufman (real person)|Ryan Kaufman]] and [[Abel G. Peña]] and published in the [[Star Wars Insider 89|eighty-ninth issue]] of the ''[[Star Wars Insider]]'' magazine<ref name="Underworld">{{InsiderCite|89|Underworld: A Galaxy of Scum and Villainy}}</ref> on [[August 9]], [[2006]].<ref name="Date">[[Star Wars Insider 88|''Star Wars Insider'' 88]]</ref>
