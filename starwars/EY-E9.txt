'''EY-E9''', or '''EY''', was a [[protocol droid]] that ran the [[casino cruiser]] ''[[Morenia]]'' during [[3 ABY]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|I was there when EY-E9 threw the captain out the airlock. Never liked him myself, I have to say. EY spaced a lot of the crew back then. Wanted her own people. All droids. But I told her, "I'm the best cleaner you've got, and you'll need clean floors if you want to attract the high rollers." And she respected that. She had a vision, you see. Always admired someone who could things her way. So she put a duct scrubber in my hand and said, "Demonstrate your worth."|Morenia cleaner talking to Kay Vess|<ref name="Outlaws" />}}
Sometime before [[3 ABY]]<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> EY worked aboard the ''Morenia'' under its [[Morenia's original captain|previous captain]]. At some point, the captain tasked EY with increasing the ships profit margins, resulting in EY throwing the captain out an airlock and taking over the ''Morenia'' herself. As part of her take over, she spaced a number of the crew, wanting to replace them with droids. However, at least one [[human]] [[Unidentified cleaner (Morenia)|cleaner]] aboard the ship convinced EY to let them keep working for her.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

After taking over, EY turned the ''Morenia'' into a lucrative underworld casino which drew in some of the best players of the card game [[Sabacc]] in [[the galaxy]]. To avoid the [[Galactic Empire]], the ship spent most of its time in [[Hyperspace]].<ref name="Outlaws" /> In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]], EY held a [[Morenia's Sabacc tournament|sabacc tournament]] where the grand prize was the coordinates to the [[moon]] [[Okala Five]], which was said to contain two point eight trillion tons of pure [[cyphrite]] ore.<ref name="Outlaws" /> 

==Characteristics==
EY-E9 was a [[protocol droid]] with a [[Color|copper color]] panels and green [[photoreceptor]]s.<ref name="Outlaws" />
