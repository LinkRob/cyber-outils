"'''Fett's Flying Lesson'''" is a [[Canon|non-canon]] animated [[LEGO Star Wars|LEGO ''Star Wars'']] short released on [[June 12]], [[2025]]<ref name="Fett's Flying Lesson" /> as part of ''[[LEGO STAR WARS: Celebrate the Season]]''.<ref name="SWKids" />

==Official description==
''Caught in a heated escape, Jango Fett must give his son Boba an impromptu flying lesson.''<ref name="Fett's Flying Lesson" />

==Plot summary==
[[Jango Fett]] and his son [[Boba Fett]] are cornered by [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] as they attempt to flee aboard ''[[Slave I]]'' at a [[Kamino]]an landing pad. While Jango fires upon the [[lightsaber]]-wielding Kenobi, Boba enters the ship's cockpit and fires its [[laser cannon]]s at Kenobi. Jango is caught in the crossfire. Fiddling with the controls, Jango fires two rockets which narrowly miss his father. With Kenobi distracted, Jango flees aboard the ship. 

Kenobi attempts to use [[the Force]] to reach out to ''Slave I''. The ship flies through a flock of [[aiwha]]. Kenobi gives chase in his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Delta-7 Aethersprite-class light interceptor|Jedi starfighter]]. Under Jango's guidance, an inexperienced Boba flies the ship into space, evading Kenobi's laser bolts. 

Kenobi pursues the ''Slave I'' in his Jedi starfighter through the rings of [[Geonosis]]. Jango guides his son as he pilots the ship through the [[asteroid field]]. Kenobi's [[homing beacon]] glows red on the rear of the ''Slave I''. The ship narrowly avoids colliding with an asteroid. Boba flies the ship through the asteroid's tunnel. When Kenobi catches up with them, Jango releases a [[seismic charge]], that unleashes a shock wave. Kenobi is forced to dodge the debris, allowing the Fetts to escape. Father and son cheer.
