'''CT-327''' was a [[clone trooper]] who served as a [[deck officer]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic|Grand Army]] of the [[Galactic Republic]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[22 BBY]], CT-327 was member of a small [[Rishi Station unit|unit]] of [[Cloning|clones]] stationed at an [[Rishi Station|outpost]] on the [[Rishi Moon]]. He served as the base's deck officer, guarding the [[listening post]]'s grounds. As the sentry patrolled the base, he observed an incoming [[meteor shower]]. Unbeknownst to him, [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] [[BX-series droid commando|commando droids]] traveling in a [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ship]] were hidden among the [[meteorite]]s. The [[droid]]s ambushed the deck officer, striking him with a [[stun baton]]. The [[commando]] droids then [[Defense of Rishi Station|infiltrated]] the base in preparation for the Separatists' planned surprise attack on the clone [[homeworld]] of [[Kamino]].

==Biography==
===Guarding the Rishi Moon===
{{Quote|This is the deck officer checking in. Nothing going on&hellip;as usual.|CT-327 reports to Rishi Station command from his post|<ref name="Rookies" />}}
[[File:Rishi_Station.png|thumb|left|250px|CT-327 served as the deck officer of Rishi Station.]]
CT-327 was a [[Gender|male]] [[clone trooper]]<ref name="Rookies" /> who was [[Reproduction|born]] on the [[planet]] [[Kamino]].<ref name="Join the Battle" /> In [[22 BBY]], during first [[Standard year|year]] of the [[Clone Wars]],<ref name="Date" /> the [[Cloning|clone]] served as a [[deck officer]] at [[Rishi Station]], a [[Galactic Republic]] outpost on the barren [[Rishi Moon]],<ref name="Rookies" /> located on the wispy borders of Republic [[Realspace|space]]<ref name="Rookies episode gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rookies-episode-gallery|text="Rookies" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 1}}</ref> not far from Kamino's cloning facilities.<ref name="Rookies" />

While stationed at the [[listening post]], CT-327 and the rest of the [[Rishi Station unit]]'s primary mission was to monitor incoming transmissions and sound the alarm if it was determined the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]] were planning an attack on<ref name="Rishi Station DB">{{Databank|rishi-station|Rishi Station}}</ref> their [[Homeworld|home planet]]. Therefore, it was crucial CT-327 and his comrades guarded the outpost, since, if the Separatist [[droid]]s got past the station,<ref name="Rookies" /> the rest of the clones who were still in gestation on Kamino would be vulnerable to an attack.<ref name="Rookies Rewatch">{{SW|url=news/the-clone-wars-rewatch-rookies|text=''The Clone Wars'' Rewatch: Sacrifices and Mistakes in "Rookies"}}</ref>

===Commando droid infiltration===
{{Quote|Sir, incoming meteor shower.|Hevy reported to Sergeant O'Niner in the Rishi Outpost's command center|<ref name="Rookies" />}}
[[File:CT-327_electrocuted.png|thumb|right|250px|CT-327 is electrocuted by commando droids.]]
As the other clone [[trooper]]s of the Rishi Station unit were stationed inside the listening post, CT-327 was conducting his duties and patrolling the base's [[hangar|observation platform]], where he reported that there were no signs of activity. During one of CT-327's patrols of the perimeter, a [[meteor shower]] occurred, with [[Clone Sergeant|Sergeant]] [[O'Niner]] ordering [[Hevy]] to raise the base's [[Deflector shield|shields]]. From the observation platform, CT-327 observed several [[meteorite]]s crash into the base's deflector shield and the craters surrounding the station. Following the event, CT-327 used his [[electrobinoculars]] to [[scout]] the area and discovered a [[Droch-class boarding ship|''Droch''-class boarding ship]] had crashed onto the surface of the [[moon]]. A Separatist [[BX-series droid commando]] soon appeared in front of him and caught the sentry off-guard. CT-327 was simultaneously attacked from behind by the droid [[Sparky|captain]], who used a [[stun baton]] to [[Death|kill]] him.<ref name="Rookies" />

After CT-327's death, more droid [[commando]]s were deployed from the boarding ship and [[Defense of Rishi Station|infiltrated the base]]. [[Sergeant]] O'Niner, the [[commanding officer]] of the base, made several unsuccessful attempts to contact CT-327 with his [[comlink]]. Once [[Fives]] was unable to locate the deck officer on the base's [[computer]]s, O'Niner ordered [[Droidbait]] and [[Nub]] to head outside the station and find the unresponsive sentry. However, as Droidbait and Nub approached the base's [[blast door]]s, the droid commandos entered the listening post and killed both [[Shiny|rookies]], as well as the sergeant. The four remaining clones, Fives, Echo, Hevy, and [[Cutup]], escaped through the ventilation shaft, while the droid commandos captured the Republic base.<ref name="Rookies" />

===Legacy===
{{Quote|This is not good. I don't see the deck officer anywhere.''"<br />"''These boys are sloppy. There should always be an officer on duty.|Commander Cody and Captain Rex upon arriving at the Rishi outpost|<ref name="Rookies" />}}
[[File:RishiStationBoom-TCW-Rookies.png|thumb|left|250px|Rishi Station explodes, alerting the Republic of the Separatist invasion.]]
When the clone inspection team consisting of [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]] and [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]] landed their [[Obex|''Nu''-class shuttle]] on the Rishi Station's landing platform, Cody noted he was not impressed that the rookies stationed there did not have a deck officer on duty. In response, Rex assumed the clones stationed on the Rishi Moon were sloppy, without the knowledge CT-327 had been killed by the commando droids. However, after Hevy fired a [[droid attack flare]], Rex determined droids had infiltrated the base.<ref name="Rookies" /> 

After Cody and Rex evacuated the platform, the clone [[officer]]s rendezvoused with the three surviving "shinies." Cody, Rex, and the rookies worked together to destroy the base and drive the Separatists away from the moon. The destruction of the Rishi outpost alerted the [[Republic Navy]] and denied the Separatists their stepping-stone for a secret invasion of Kamino.<ref name="Rookies" />

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|What the&mdash;|CT-327 upon discovering the commando droids' boarding ship|<ref name="Rookies" />}}
As a [[human]] clone of the [[bounty hunter]] [[Jango Fett]], CT-327 was 1.83 [[meter]]s (6 [[Foot|feet]]) tall,<ref name="Join the Battle" /> weighed eighty [[kilogram]]s,<ref name="Weight" /> and had [[Color|tan]] skin.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Skills and abilities==
Similar to most clone troopers, CT-327 was proficient in both armed and [[unarmed combat]], as well as military tactics.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Equipment==
[[File:CT327SeesHisHouseFromHere-Rookies.png|thumb|right|250px|CT-327 wore standard Phase I clone armor with a visor attached to his helmet.]]
The clone deck officer wore unmarked<ref name="Rookies" /> [[plastoid]] [[Phase I clone trooper armor]], sealed to a temperature-controlled black [[body glove]] via [[Magnatomic grip panel|magnatomic gription panels]].<ref name="SWECG">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Attached to his white [[helmet]] was an [[Binoculars|electrobinocular]] visor.<ref name="Rookies" /> Like all clone troopers, CT-327 had a [[behavioral modification biochip]] implanted in his [[brain]] by [[Kaminoan]] [[scientist]]s to ensure obedience.<ref name="Join the Battle" />

==Behind the scenes==
CT-327 was introduced in "[[Rookies (episode)|Rookies]],"<ref name="Rookies" /> the fifth episode of the [[The Clone Wars: Season One|first season]] of the animated television series ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', which premiered on [[October 24]], [[2008]].<ref name="Old EG">{{SWArchive|url=theclonewars/guide/episode005.html|text=''The Clone Wars'' Episode Guide: Rookies}}</ref> His designation number is another appearance of the digits "327," a number that regularly appears throughout the ''[[Star Wars]]'' saga.<ref name="Rookies trivia gallery">{{SW|url=series/clone-wars/rookies-trivia-gallery|text="Rookies" Trivia Gallery}} {{C|Slide 3}}</ref> The sentry was voiced by [[Dee Bradley Baker]],<ref name="Old EG" /> who voiced all the clones in the show.<ref name="Baker">{{SW|url=news/dee-bradley-baker-the-bad-batch-season-3-interview|text=Dee Bradley Baker Looks Ahead to the Final Season of ''Star Wars: The Bad Batch''}}</ref>
