The [[Ewok]] [[species]] of the [[forest]] [[moon]] [[Endor]] produced a '''wine''' that was simpler and sweeter than the overly potent [[Yuzzum wine|wine]] produced by the [[Yuzzum]], another species found on Endor. The Ewoks' [[wine]] could be combined with that of the Yuzzums in order to make the latter safely [[Diet|consumable]] for other species.<ref name="Wine in Dreams" />

==Behind the scenes==
The wine was mentioned in "[[The Wine in Dreams]]," a novella written by [[Mira Grant]] and published in the [[Canto Bight (novella collection)|''Canto Bight'']] anthology in [[2017]].<ref name="Wine in Dreams" />
