'''Batarikans''' were a type of [[Snake|serpent]] found on<ref name="TRS" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> [[planet]]<ref name="Tarkin">[[Tarkin (novel)|''Tarkin'']]</ref> [[Galidraan]]. At some point during the [[High Republic Era]], the [[mercenary]] [[Ty Yorrick]] was hired to [[Hunt on Galidraan|hunt]] a batarikan on the world. However, she found that the snake had been infested by a [[Drengir]]&mdash;a type of [[Sentience|sentient]], [[carnivorous plant]]&mdash;with [[Plant|roots]] crammed into its [[eye]]s and [[Nose|nasal]] cavities, and its body leeched of nutrients. Knowing that many of Galidraan's residents had met the same fate, the mercenary opted to flee the scene despite having not completed the job, barely escaping with her life. While on the [[swamp]] planet [[Safrifa]] for a [[Extraction on Safrifa|rescue mission]]<ref name="TRS" /> in [[231 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'' to [[231 BBY]].</ref> Yorrick thought about her [[time]] on Galidraan for the batarikan [[Hunting|hunt]] as she fought another Drengir.<ref name="TRS" />
