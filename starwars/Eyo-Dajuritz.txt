'''Eyo-Dajuritz''' was a [[planet]]<ref name="MOF" /> in the [[Eyo-Dajuritz system]] in the [[Inner Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> During its heyday, Eyo-Dajuritz was a sprawling [[ecumenopolis]], housing trillions of people in huge metal towers, while accomplishing marvelous feats of architecture, geo-[[engineer]]ing, and materials [[science]]. By the time of the early [[Republic Era]], it had become a fossilized city planet. This was due to shifting [[ocean]]s and a change of the [[atmosphere]], submerging and subsequently eroding the framework and lower levels of the towers, causing many to collapse.<ref name="MOF" />

By the time of the [[Imperial Era]], Eyo-Dajuritz was a [[galactic heritage world]] and on the [[protected planets list]], considered an ancient wonder. It was the planet of one of the oldest cultures known in the galaxy, but the name and nature of its constructors were lost to time. Eyo-Dajuritz was located far from major trade lanes. It hosted a dozen floating settlements, where [[scientist]]s worked to uncover the planet's many secrets. Although the planetary surface was forbidden to the public due to the building's volatile nature, many tourists came to visit the site. Thousands of documentaries and fictional works alike depicted Eyo-Dajuritz. To prevent unauthorized people from arriving there, a sensor net was installed.<ref name="MOF" />

During [[19 BBY]], [[Saw Gerrera]] and his [[Partisans]] [[Attack on Eyo-Dajuritz|detonated explosives]] destroying an ancient dam, resulting in the flooding of an empty lakebed and the destruction of numerous ancient towers.<ref name="MOF" />

In 9 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="MandoVGDate">{{MandoVGDate}}</ref> the planet fell within the borders of the [[New Republic]].<ref name="MandoVG" />
