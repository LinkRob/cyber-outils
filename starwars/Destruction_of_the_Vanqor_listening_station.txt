During the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Galactic Republic]] launched a mission to '''destroy''' a [[Vanqor listening station|Confederate listening post]] on the [[planet]] [[Vanqor]]. However, the [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]], who was secretly working for the Separatist leader [[Count]] [[Dooku]], leaked intelligence about the operation to the Separatists. As a result, the Separatist were able to evacuate the listening post well before Republic forces arrived. When Republic forces and Jedi forces arrived, they found that the listening post had already been abandoned. As a result, the Jedi Order came to suspect that they had a traitor within their ranks.

==Prelude==
[[File:Vos_DD.png|thumb|left|300px|Quinlan Vos (pictured) found himself a double agent in the Clone Wars.]]
Following a failed [[Jedi]] and [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[Destruction of the Separatist supply storage base|operation]] to extract supplies from a [[Separatist supply storage base]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Quinlan Vos]] was praised for saving his Jedi comrades by warning them about the presence of [[bomb]]s in the [[asteroid]] base. Deeming him worthy to fight the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatists]], the [[Jedi High Council]] sent Vos to lead an operation to destroy a [[Vanqor listening station|Separatist listening post]] on the [[planet]] [[Vanqor]]. Quinlan had been captured earlier during a [[Mission to Raxus Secundus|mission]] to [[assassin]]ate [[Count]] [[Dooku]] and had spent several [[Standard month|months]] in [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist]] captivity. As "[[Admiral]] Enigma", Vos gained an extensive knowledge about [[Confederacy military|Separatist military]] strategy.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

For this mission, Master Vos was accompanied by Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] and the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]]. Unknown to the Jedi however, Vos had embraced the [[dark side of the Force]] and become Dooku's [[Sith apprentice]]. In reality, this was merely a ruse for Vos to get Dooku to lead him to his [[Sith Master|master]] [[Darth Sidious]] so that he could destroy the [[Sith]] once and for all. To perpetuate the ruse that he was still loyal to Dooku, Vos leaked information about the planned operation to the Separatists. As a result, Dooku was able to evacuate the [[listening post]] before [[Grand Army of the Republic|Republic forces]] arrived.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==The mission==
Expecting heavy resistance from Separatist [[Vulture-class starfighter|droid fighters]], Kenobi, Vos, and Skywalker flew three [[Eta-2 Actis-class light interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptors]]. They were accompanied by twenty-four [[Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter|ARC-170 starfighters]] which formed the first attack wave. As they descended into Vanqor's [[atmosphere]] and approached their target, the three Jedi were baffled to encounter no resistance. While the Jedi and the first wave approached the listening post, Kenobi ordered Admiral [[Block]] to dispatch the second attack wave because he suspected that the Separatists had set another trap.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

As the Jedi [[starfighter]]s approached the listening post, Vos "observed" that it had been abandoned. To be sure that this was the case, the three Jedi starfighters embarked on a strafing run in order to flush out any stragglers. While their blast destroyed the listening post's main dish and tower, their scanners were unable to detect any [[Starship|ships]], vehicles, people, [[droid]]s, or animals below. Later, the Jedi landed at the listening post where they were joined by several [[Clone trooper]]s led by [[Clone Commander]] [[Cody]]. After inspecting the interior, the Jedi and [[Cloning|clones]] deduced that Count Dooku had received intelligence about the operation and evacuated the facility. As a result, Kenobi and Skywalker came to rightfully suspect that there was a [[Treason|traitor]] within their ranks leaking information to the Separatists.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

==Aftermath==
Following the failed Vanqor operation, the [[Jedi Order]] came to suspect that a traitor was leaking information to the Separatists. Due to Master Vos' captivity under Dooku, suspicions fell on Quinlan Vos. After meeting with Vos in private, Master [[Yoda]] was able to deduce that Quinlan had indeed fallen to the dark side. Unsure whether this meant that he had committed himself to Count Dooku, the Jedi Council assigned Vos with a mission to assassinate Dooku. If he succeeded, he would be considered innocent. But if he failed, this was enough evidence to prove that he was a traitor.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />

After receiving intelligence that Dooku's [[Providence-class Dreadnought|''Providence''-class Dreadnought]] was visiting [[Christophsis]], Vos and his lover [[Asajj Ventress]] traveled there on her ship, the [[Banshee (starship)|''Banshee'']]. Unknown to Vos, the Jedi had sent Kenobi and Skywalker to tail him. Using a stolen Separatist [[shuttle]], the two Jedi managed to follow Vos aboard the dreadnought. There, they discovered Vos trying to force Dooku to lead him to his master [[Darth]] Sidious. Confronted with evidence of Vos' treason, the two Jedi promptly arrested him. However, Vos and Dooku managed to escaped, leading to a [[Second battle of Christophsis|Republic assault]] on Christophsis.<ref name="Dark Disciple" />
