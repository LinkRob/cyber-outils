There were two battles which occurred on [[Skarpos]] during the [[Republic Dark Age]] in [[1032 BBY/Legends|1032 BBY]].
*'''[[Capture of Skarpos]]'''&mdash;an invasion of Lord [[Malakite]]'s [[The Menagerie|Menagerie]] by [[Sith Lord/Legends|Sith Lord]] [[Odion]]'s [[Odionate]] forces
*'''[[Massacre on Skarpos]]'''&mdash;a three-way battle involving Odionate, Menagerie reinforcements, and Lord [[Daiman]].
