'''Broo''' was an elderly [[Talz]] with grey [[Hair|fur]] and a hulking physique that lived in the [[Imperial Elderblock 26-0640]] on the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. They were friends with the fellow resident [[Loos]] and were always close by his side. At some point, during the Imperial Era, when Broo was over the age of 73, their neighbor [[Armen Sim]] died and Broo tooted at a joke about him made by Loos.<ref name="Elderblock" />
