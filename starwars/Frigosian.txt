'''Frigosians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that were [[Homeworld|native]] to the [[planet]] [[Tansyl 5]]. Small for the average humanoid, they had sensitive eyes that meant they had to wear goggles to protect them from the light in certain environments. The Frigosian [[Opeepit]] was a janitor on the ''[[Colossus]]'' platform, while the Frigosians [[Thromba]] and [[Laparo]] were [[cryptosurgeon]]s working at [[Maz Kanata]]'s [[Takodana Castle|castle]].

==Biology and appearance==
{{Quote|Jibb! Jibb jhu-woo!"<br />"Jeeba!|Laparo and Thromba speak in the Frigosian language|<ref name="TFOE" />}}
[[File:Opeepit-CGSWG.png|thumb|left|150px|The Frigosian janitor Opeepit]]
Frigosians were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] hailing from the [[planet]] [[Tansyl 5]]. They had dome-shaped heads as wide as their shoulders and were covered in a thick layer of fluffy [[Color|yellowish]] [[hair]]. By [[humanoid]] standards, they were regarded as short-sized. Although their fur usually concealed it, they had a large [[mouth]] filled with [[dagger]]-like [[Tooth|teeth]].<ref name="TFOE" />

A nocturnal species, the Frigosians had sensitive [[eye]]s, and, in certain environments, they were forced to wear heavy black goggles to protect them from the light. On worlds such as [[Takodana]]<ref name="TFOE" /> and [[Castilon]],<ref name="Fuel for the Fire">{{Resistance|Fuel for the Fire}}</ref> the [[atmosphere]] was so different from that of Tansyl 5 that Frigosians had to use breathing apparatuses. They had a [[language]] of [[Frigosian language|their own]], which consisted of a series of chirps.<ref name="TFOE" />

==Frigosians in the galaxy==
[[Opeepit]] was a Frigosian and the sole janitor of the [[Fuel|refueling]] station ''[[Colossus]]''.<ref name="Fuel for the Fire" /> [[Thromba]] and [[Laparo]] were a pair of Frigosian [[cryptosurgeon]]s who worked at [[Takodana Castle|the castle]] of [[Maz Kanata]].<ref name="TFOE" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Had to be reminded I sorta named the Frigosians after the Fry Guys.|Pablo Hidalgo|<ref name="Twitter">{{Twitter|pablohidalgo|status/1162429484701605889|[[Pablo Hidalgo]]|quote=Had to be reminded I sorta named the Frigosians after the Fry Guys.|archivefile=File:Twitter-Frigosians.png}}</ref>}}
The Frigosian species first appeared in "[[The Face of Evil]]," a short story written by [[Landry Q. Walker]] as part of the ''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'', a multi-media campaign leading up to the release of the film [[Star Wars: Episode VII The Force Awakens|''Star Wars'': Episode VII ''The Force Awakens'']] later that year in [[2015]]. They then appeared as minor characters in the film itself.<ref name="TFOE" /> The species was originally named by [[Lucasfilm Story Group]] member [[Pablo Hidalgo]], who named them after the [[Wikipedia:McDonaldland|McDonaldland]] "Fry Guys."<ref name="Twitter" />
