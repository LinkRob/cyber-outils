'''Bubble sharks''' were a type of [[shark]] that was on display at [[Wild Wollina's Animal Adventures]] [[zoo]] on<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[Planet|world]]<ref name="Path of Deceit">''[[The High Republic: Path of Deceit]]''</ref> [[Hon-Tallos]]<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> between [[3 ABY]] and [[4 ABY]]. During that [[time]],<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref> while at the zoo to [[Mission to Hon-Tallos|liberate]] a [[Nussi|rancor]], the [[Shani]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Deva Lompop]] told the [[Starros Clan]] member [[Sana Starros]] to look as though she was excited to see the zoo's bubble sharks&mdash;so they could hide their intent to cause a scene at the zoo as part of their plan to free the [[rancor]].<ref name="Sana Starros 3" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bubble sharks were mentioned in the [[Sana Starros 3|third issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series ''[[Star Wars: Sana Starros]]''. The issue was written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]]<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> on [[April 19]], 2023.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/96862/star_wars_sana_starros_2023_3|text=Star Wars: Sana Starros (2023) #3}}</ref>
