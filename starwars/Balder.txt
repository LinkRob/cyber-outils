'''Balder''' was a [[Dug]] [[Gender|male]] who was the leader of the [[Claw (clan)|Claw]] clan on the [[planet]] [[Parnassos]]. After helping [[Brendol Hux]] of the [[First Order]] onto a plateau after their [[Escape pod|emergency pod]] crash-landed on the planet, Balder pushed Hux aside and reached out to help a [[First Order stormtrooper|figure]] wearing [[First Order stormtrooper armor|white armor]] onto the plateau.<ref name="Phasma" />
