'''Detonite''' was an explosive material used in [[bomb]]s.

The [[C-25 fragmentation grenade]] consisted of a sphere of [[baradium]]-impregnated detonite encased in a conductive shell.<ref name="r1vg">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]''</ref>

During the [[Galactic Civil War]], some [[Stormtrooper|members]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Empire's]] [[Stormtrooper Corps]] lost [[limb]]s when their [[Stormtrooper armor|armor]] was pierced by shrapnel from [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] bombs, made of detonite.<ref name="bftc">''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]''</ref>

In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> the Empire had a supply of compact detonite at their [[Imperial Outpost 51|outpost]] in the [[North Jundland Wastes]] region of the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Tatooine]].<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Detonite was first mentioned in [[2015]] [[canon]] reference book ''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]'' by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="Sabine Sketchbook">''[[Sabine My Rebel Sketchbook]]''</ref>

It was first mentioned in the [[Star Wars Legends]] in the [[1987]] roleplaying game, ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'' published by [[West End Games]]<ref name="Roleplaying Game">''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''</ref>

In the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' detonite is shows as having a compact form which was a quest item used to increased the radius and damage of the players grenades by fifty percent.<ref name="Outlaws" />
