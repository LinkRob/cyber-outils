'''Blaster spears''' were a [[Mon Calamari]] weapon, a [[blaster rifle]] that not only resembled a [[spear]] but could also be used as one in melee combat.

==Description==
A large [[blaster rifle]] of [[Mon Calamari]] design, the blaster [[spear]] was standard-issue among the [[Mon Calamari Guard]]. While it had only average energy output and range, it could fire underwater, making it ideal for amphibious assault units and beings in submerged cities like those on [[Mon Cala]]. Although the weapon was rare outside [[Calamari sector|Mon Calamari Space]], a number of Mon Calamari wielded their traditional weapons with pride.<ref name="Water War" />

Where most rifles had a buttstock, the spear blaster had a large [[blade]], limiting the weapon to hip fire on land. However, underwater, it was typically fired while swimming toward the enemy. While this underarm shooting stance was not particularly accurate, it left the weapon readied to make an upward slash attack in close quarters.<ref name="GaG" />
