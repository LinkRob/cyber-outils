An '''attack''' on the rebel cell [[Phoenix Cell]] fleet was launched by [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]]. The fleet was waiting for [[fuel]] to be delivered by the [[Spectres|crew]] of the ''[[Ghost]]'' before it could jump into [[hyperspace]] in route to the [[Yost system]], where the rebels intended to set up a base.

They soon learned, however, that the Yost system was under Imperial control and the rebels would be jumping into a trap. With the help of an Imperial [[inventory droid]] named [[AP-5]], the rebel [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] was able to discover a new location: [[Atollon]]. The rebels jumped away from the attack and arrived safely in Atollon.

==Prelude==
Following the [[Mission to Ryloth (Phoenix Squadron)|theft]] of the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Quasar Fire-class cruiser-carrier|fighter carrier]] above the [[planet]] [[Ryloth]], the [[Phoenix Cell]] continued their search for a permanent rebel base. By cross-referencing several [[Galactic Republic|Old Republic]] and [[Lasat]] star maps, the rebels had decided to settle on [[Berzite's moon]] in the [[Yost system]] at the urging of [[Ketsu Onyo]]; [[Sabine Wren]]'s friend. However, the rebel fighter carrier lacked sufficient fuel to travel to the Yost system.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" />

Consequently, the [[Spectres]] were dispatched on a [[Heist at Horizon Base|mission]] to steal [[fuel]] from the Imperial depot at [[Horizon Base]]. The rebels managed to steal the fuel but the [[astromech droid]] [[C1-10P|Chopper]] was left stranded after he was distracted by a new [[Ambulatory strut|droid leg]]. Despite being pursued by Imperial [[stormtrooper]]s, Chopper managed to stow aboard an [[Class four container transport|Imperial cargo ship]] called [[Imperial Cargo Transport 241|Cargo transport 241]]. There, he struck an unlikely friendship with an Imperial [[inventory droid]] named [[AP-5]], who had been a veteran of the [[Clone Wars]]. Working together, the two droids knocked out the ship's [[Unidentified Imperial cargo ship captain|captain]] and seized control of the vessel. They also jettisoned the ship's cargo compartment and the stormtrooper garrison in space.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" />

==The battle==
[[File:GhostHardLanding.png|thumb|right|250px|The ''Ghost'' landing within ''Phoenix Nest''.]]
As soon as the Spectres returned to the rebel fleet, they discovered that it was under attack by an Imperial fleet. Recognizing the rebel starship ''[[Ghost]]'' as the same vessel that had raided the Imperial depot at Horizon Base, [[Admiral]] [[Kassius Konstantine]] realized that the ''Ghost'' was carrying fuel for the rebels and ordered his ships to focus their fire on the ''Ghost''. The ''Ghost'' was pursued by several [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] but Onyo came to the rebels' aid in her starship ''[[Shadow Caster]]''. After breaking through the Imperial ships, the ''Ghost'' managed to land inside the rebel fighter carrier.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" />

While [[Ezra Bridger]] and [[Garazeb Orrelios]] were unloading fuel, Chopper managed to contact the ''Ghost'' and informed [[Captain]] [[Hera Syndulla]] that he had managed to steal an Imperial cargo ship. When Syndulla mentioned that the rebel fleet was preparing to depart for the Yost system, AP-5 quickly warned the rebel captain that an Imperial fleet was lying in ambush there. After Chopper vouched for AP-5, Syndulla agreed to trust the former Imperial inventory droid. After cross-referencing both Chopper and Imperial data, AP-5 identified the planet [[Atollon]] as a safe haven with no Imperial presence.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" />

Using AP-5's information, Syndulla warned [[Commander]] [[Jun Sato]] and the other rebels that the Yost system was unsafe. Before AP-5 could transmit the new [[hyperspace]] coordinates for Atollon, he and Chopper were accosted by the Imperial ship captain, who had woken up. Following a struggle, Chopper managed to subdue the Imperial but not before the captain shot AP-5 twice with his blaster; severely damaging the droid. Chopper then completed AP-5's work by transmitting the rest of the coordinates to Syndulla, who in turn relayed them to Commander Sato. Due to AP-5's information, the Phoenix rebels were able to escape the Imperial trap and reached Atollon.<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" />

==Aftermath==
After the rebel fleet reached Atollon, they were rejoined by Chopper and AP-5. After Wren repaired AP-5,<ref name="The Forgotten Droid" /> the inventory droid decided to join the rebellion at the advice of Chopper. While the rebel fleet remained in orbit, rebel forces under Captain [[Rex]] began building a base on Atollon. In honor of Chopper, the new base was named "[[Chopper Base]]." Rebel efforts to establish the base encountered a major obstacle in the form of several predatory spider-like creatures known as [[krykna]]. The krykna devoured [[Lieutenant]] [[Dicer]] and kidnapped Rex, who was subsequently rescued by the Spectres. Unwilling to abandon the base, the rebels constructed a perimeter fence consisting of sensor markers; a security technology that irritated the krykna and kept them away.<ref name="Mystery">{{Rebels|The Mystery of Chopper Base}}</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
The attack on the rebel fleet served as the climax of the ''[[Star Wars Rebels]]'' episode "[[The Forgotten Droid]]," which first premiered on [[Disney XD]] on [[March 16]], [[2016]]. It introduced several new characters and plot elements including the inventory droid AP-5 (who was voiced by [[Stephen Stanton]]), the Imperial cargo ship captain ([[James Adomian]]), and the planet Atollon.
