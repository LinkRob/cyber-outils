The [[First Order]] [[starfighter]] [[ace]] known as the [[Banshee of Bestine]] flew a black '''TIE/ba Baron Space Superiority Interceptor'''<ref name="Codebreaker">[[Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker 1|''Hyperspace Stories—Codebreaker'' 1]]</ref> between [[31 ABY|31]] and [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">In this issue, [[Poe Dameron]] is already a member of the [[Resistance]] and is subject to the [[New Republic]]'s authority. According to ''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]'', Poe Dameron joined the Resistance three years prior to the events of {{Film|VII}}, which corresponds to [[31 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. As the New Republic is destroyed in ''The Force Awakens'', the events must take place between these two events, meaning it must take place between 31 and [[34 ABY]].</ref> The TIE Baron was destroyed and the Banshee was killed in battle by [[Resistance]] pilot [[Poe Dameron]] above the planet [[Bothawui]].
