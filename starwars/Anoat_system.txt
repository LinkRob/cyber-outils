The '''Anoat system''' was a [[Star system|solar system]] of [[the galaxy]] that was located between the [[planet]]s [[Hoth]] and [[Bespin]]<ref name="ESB" /> in the [[Anoat sector]].<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars" /> It notably contained the planets [[Anoat]] and [[Pujool]].<ref name="Uprising" />

==History==
{{Quote|We've uncovered a Separatist plan of attack. We shall travel to the Anoat system here, then move across to Saleucami.''"<br />"''So far out of our way?''"<br />"''Unfortunately, we must avoid these neutral systems.|Obi-Wan Kenobi and [[Mace Windu]]|<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much">{{TCW|The Jedi Who Knew Too Much}}</ref>}}
Toward the end of the [[Clone Wars]], the [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]] proposed sending a [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[fleet]] through the [[Anoat]] [[Star system|system]] and then to [[Saleucami]] to counter a planned attack by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]].<ref name="The Jedi Who Knew Too Much" />

[[Standard year|Years]] later, while running from the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[Imperial Navy|fleet]] following the [[Battle of Hoth]], the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] heroes [[Han Solo]], [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] and [[Chewbacca]] considered hiding in the Anoat system. However, Organa pointed out how empty the system was, and Solo took their [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', to [[Bespin]] instead.<ref name="ESB" />
