'''Adikaria''' was a terrestrial<ref name="JoJ" /> astronomical object in the [[Adikaria system]] of the [[Core Worlds]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Interior]].<ref name="Interior" /> It fell within grid square K-12 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> The [[Tallimarian]] [[Centada Ressad]] attended a ball on Adikaria and was pictured in a news report covering the event. After seeing a [[Hologram|holo]] of the report, the Tallimarian [[scavenger]] [[Velorie Sarcon]]&mdash;who thought the ball looked fancy&mdash;began telling others on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]] that Ressad was her [[Gender|aunt]]. Sarcon mentioned Adikaria when revealing that she was not actually related to Ressad to fellow Jakkuvian [[Leesa Chaffni]]<ref name="JoJ" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]].<ref name="Date">The events of ''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]'' take place between the events of {{Film|VIII}} and {{Film|IX}}, which corresponds to between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> Chaffni then relayed the information to [[Dai's Cafe]] patron [[Naldri Zuvio]] soon after.<ref name="JoJ" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], Adikaria was mentioned in ''[[The Jaws of Jakku]]'', a [[2025]] audio book written by [[Cavan Scott]] and narrated by [[Helen Sadler]].<ref name="JoJ" /> The Adikaria star system had previously been first mentioned in the current canon in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," written by [[Jason Fry]] and published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref> The [[Adikaria/Legends|Adikaria]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned indirectly in [[Clone Wars Gambit: Stealth|''Stealth'']], a [[2010]] novel by [[Karen Miller]] that was the first book in the ''[[Star Wars: Clone Wars Gambit]]'' duology.<ref name="Stealth">''[[Clone Wars Gambit: Stealth]]''</ref> It was then first identified as part of a Scavenging Crew Skill mission in ''[[Star Wars: The Old Republic]]'', a [[2011]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] developed by [[BioWare]], though the information regarding the mission's outcome was later removed from the game.<ref name="SWTOR">{{TORcite|type=crewskill|Scavenging|Cleansing the Seas}}</ref>
