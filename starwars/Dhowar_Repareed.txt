'''Dhowar Repareed''' was a [[human]] [[Gender|male]] who worked as an air traffic controller in a [[Pnakotic Coast village|village]] on the [[Pnakotic Coast]] of the [[planet]] [[Savareen]]. He and all the other villagers had their [[tongue]]s cut out by a [[mercenary]] group who would become the [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate when the [[Savarian]]s refused to pay further tribute to the criminals. Repareed worked as the air traffic controller for the village, which allowed him [[time]] to daydream.

In [[10 BBY]], Repareed received word over [[Comlink|comms]] from the criminal [[Han Solo]] that the [[YT-1300 light freighter]] known as the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'' was about to arrive in the village with [[coaxium]] to unload. The air traffic controller then watched it make an emergency landing, after which he sent a message to the refinery to send all the help it could to the ''Falcon''. The vessel's coaxium was then unloaded and processed by the villagers.

==Biography==
===Muted daydreamer===
{{Quote|My mother once told me about a band of mercenaries that came to a peaceful planet. They had a resource there these men coveted, so they took it. They kept coming back, taking more. Until finally, the people resisted. When they returned demanding their tribute, the people shouted back in one voice, "No more!" The mercenaries didn't like the sound of that, so they cut off the tongue of every last man, woman, and child.|Enfys Nest|audio=Enfys-Tongues-Solo.flac|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:SavariansResist-SoloAdaptation6.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Four Savarians raise weapons or fists against a humanoid mercenary in a purple hood, who raises an arm after being struck.|Repareed and other Savarians lost their tongues for opposing Crimson Dawn.]]
Dhowar Repareed was a [[human]]<ref name="SoloOG" /> [[Gender|male]]<ref name="SoloAD" /> who lived in a [[Pnakotic Coast village|village]] constructed in the ruins of the [[Bis Refinery]] on the [[Pnakotic Coast]] of his [[homeworld]] [[Savareen]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of the [[Galactic Empire]]. The [[Savarian]]s revived some of the refinery's machinery and allowed criminals to use it, with the village becoming a [[shadowport]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> When a group of [[Mercenary|mercenaries]] repeatedly demanded tribute from the village's occupants, the Savarians eventually resisted and refused to provide more and so the mercenaries cut out all their [[tongue]]s, including Repareed's. The mercenary group eventually evolved into the criminal [[Crimson Dawn]] syndicate.<ref name="Solo" />

Repareed worked for a [[time]] at the refinery<ref name="Junior">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: A Junior Novel]]''</ref> but eventually took on the role of the village's air traffic controller instead, overseeing the small number of landing pads around the refinery. As the skies of the Pnakotic Coast were usually clear, Repareed spent a lot of [[time]] daydreaming and the other villagers joked that he had the easiest job of them all. Occasionally a stricken [[starship]] would arrive and require the air traffic controller's attention, leading to more panicked moments.<ref name="SoloOG" />

===The ''Falcon'' arrives===
{{Quote|We're here now&hellip; see you. I&hellip;|Han Solo's distorted radio message to Repareed|audio=Solo-SavareenRadio2-Solo.flac|<ref name="Solo" />}}
[[File:DhowarRepareedRelaxed-Solo.png|thumb|left|300px|alt=Dhowar Repareed sits with his feet up on a counter in the Bis Refinery control tower.|Dhowar Repareed daydreamed while working as air traffic controller.]]
In [[10 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Savareen]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[10 BBY]].</ref> Repareed's [[Standard day|days]] in the refinery were long done and he intended to spend what time he had left reclining in the relative peace and quiet of the refinery's control tower. He spent an entire [[Local day|day]] in the tower marking time while lost in thought, staring out across the coastline and to the [[Emerald Sea]] beyond it, which made for a far better view then the sight of the long-decaying refinery also visible from the tower.<ref name="Junior" /> He was sat with his [[Limb|feet]] up in the tower when the light on<ref name="Solo" /> the tower's antiquated radio<ref name="Junior" /> began blinking [[Color|red]].<ref name="Solo" />

The criminal [[Han Solo]] then began speaking over partially distorted comms, alerting Repareed to the imminent arrival of the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', a [[YT-1300 light freighter]] that Solo was [[pilot]]ing that had unrefined [[coaxium]] on board that needed to be immediately offloaded.<ref name="Solo" /> Although Solo's voice wavered in and out, the urgency and near-panic in his tone were clear<ref name="Junior" /> and so Repareed sat up<ref name="Solo" /> and began typing out a response<ref name="Junior" /> by pressing levers on the control tower's terminal. The ''Falcon'' then arrived, blowing a swell of dust through the tower<ref name="Solo" /> and shaking it on its foundations as the [[Viewport|windows]] rattled,<ref name="Junior" /> and the air traffic controller<ref name="Solo" /> whipped his head around<ref name="SoloAD" /> to watch the [[freighter]] make an emergency landing.<ref name="Solo" />

===Revenge on the Dawn===
{{Quote|Send help. All you've got.|Repareed's message to the refinery|<ref name="Junior" />}}
Turning his attention from the ''Falcon'' to the refinery, Repareed tapped out a message for the village to send all the help it could muster to the ''Falcon''.<ref name="Junior" /> The Savarans were more than willing to aid with the coaxium,<ref name="SoloEE">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story: Expanded Edition]]''</ref> and villagers helped offload the [[fuel]] and began processing it while the [[Beckett's gang|ship's crew]] waited for the arrival of their employer: Crimson Dawn [[crime lord]] [[Dryden Vos]]. However, before Vos arrived, Solo and his companions were confronted by the [[Cloud-Riders]], a [[Swoop|biker]] gang who opposed the Crimson Dawn. The gang's leader, [[Enfys Nest]], explained what the crime syndicate had done to the people of Repareed's village, convincing Solo and most of his allies to give them the coaxium so that they could oppose the criminal syndicates.<ref name="Solo" /> 

[[File:FalconDepartsSavareen-Solo.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=The ''Millennium Falcon'' flies away from the Savareen village while the Cloud-Riders and Beckett's gang face off.|Repareed watched the arrival of the ''Millennium Falcon'' on Savareen, although the ship departed soon after.]]
Although Solo's companion [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] departed the village in the ''Falcon'', the group came up with a scheme to fool Crimson Dawn, with some villagers disguising themselves as the Cloud-Riders so that the real gang members could ambush Crimson Dawn's [[Hylobon Enforcers|enforcers]] when they arrived. When Vos and his associates did arrive, the Cloud-Riders succeeded in taking out the enforcers and Solo and his allies [[Death|killed]] Vos, allowing the Cloud-Riders to take the coaxium as agreed.<ref name="Solo" />

==Personality and traits==
Due to the lax nature of his work, Repareed was a fairly mellow individual,<ref name="SoloOG" /> although by 10 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]] he was grizzled and old, with Solol considering him an old fossil.<ref name="Junior" /> He had tan skin, black [[hair]], and brown [[eye]]s.<ref name="SoloOG" />

==Equipment==
When the ''Falcon'' arrived on the Pnakotic Coast, Repareed was wearing black [[boot]]s, brown pants,<ref name="Solo" /> and a brown apron with an orange, black, and yellow skirt.<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=Solo: A Star Wars Story - Parallax|cardname=Dhowar Repareed}}</ref> He was also wearing a tan [[Clothing|shirt]] under a darker brown [[jacket]] and a brown vest, a red [[belt]] with pockets full of [[Flare|signal flares]], a red headband, a yellow [[scarf]], and a tap-code [[comlink]] on his vest for emergencies.<ref name="SoloOG" />
