'''Chris Kirkpatrick''' is a singer and actor best known as a member of the boy band [[Wikipedia:NSYNC|NSYNC]]. Kirkptrick was originally set to appear as a [[Jedi]] in the [[2002]] [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] alongside fellow NSYNC members [[Joey Fatone]] and [[Joshua Scott Chasez|JC Chasez]], as well as Fatone's brother [[Steven Fatone]].<ref name="EW1">{{WebCite|url=http://ew.com/article/2002/06/03/behind-n-sync-cameos-episode-ii|text=Behind the 'N Sync cameos in "Episode II"|author=Fonseca, Nicholas|date=2002-06-03|archivedate=20170625163749}}</ref> However, Fatone later confirmed that their scene had been cut as a result of fan backlash. Despite this, [[Lucasfilm]] spokesperson [[Lynne Hale]] could not corroborate that their roles had been cut, stating it was ultimately the decision of director and franchise creator [[George Lucas]].<ref name="E">{{WebCite|url=http://www.eonline.com/news/article/index.jsp?uuid=2f676e77-703c-4e86-bf06-cf5c0104f0a1|text='N Sync Cut from "Clones"?|author=Armstrong, Mark|date=2002-01-10|archivedate=20080503072749}}</ref> Ultimately, Kirkpatrick's character and scene were not present in ''Attack of the Clones''.<ref name="EpII">{{Film|II}}</ref>
