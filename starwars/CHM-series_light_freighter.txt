The '''CHM-series''' was a type of [[light freighter]] manufactured by [[Sublights Products Corporation]]. It was heavily [[armor]]ed and equipped with two side [[weapon]]s,<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> a [[hyperdrive]] and a long-range [[comlink]]. It could be manned by a skeleton crew of a single [[pilot]].<ref name="Cornered" /> A CHM-series freighter named the ''[[Outcast]]'' was used by [[bounty hunter]] [[Fennec Shand]] during the early [[Imperial Era]].<ref name="Encyclopedia" />
