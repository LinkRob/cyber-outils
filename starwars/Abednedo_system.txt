The '''Abednedo system''' was a [[star system]] located in the [[Colonies]]<ref name="Appendix" /> that contained the [[planet]] [[Abednedo (planet)|Abednedo]].<ref name="Aliens of the Galaxy" /> It was situated at the coordinates N-12 on the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" />

The Abednedo system was mentioned in the appendix "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], [[2025]].
