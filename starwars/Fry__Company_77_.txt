'''Fry''' was a [[human]]<ref name="Nightmares" /> that was conscripted into the [[First Order]] during his childhood<ref name="Episode IX">{{Film|IX}}</ref> and proceeded to serve within [[First Order military|its forces]] as a [[First Order stormtrooper|stormtrooper]] belonging to [[Company 77]] until his [[Desertion|defection]] at the [[Battle of Ansett Island|Battle]] of [[Ansett Island]], where his entire [[company]] refused to comply with their orders to fire on civilians and subsequently deserted, subsequently settling on the [[ocean]] [[moon]] of [[Kef Bir]]. Fry, however, would suffer from nightmares where he imagined the First Order coming for Company 77 to take them back, causing him to doubt his own bravery, despite insistence to the contrary by his leader [[Jannah]]. To prove his courage, Fry attempted to enter the [[Death Star ruins|ruins]] of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station]], but was approached by the [[Espirion (species)|Espirion]] [[Saan]], who attempted to discourage him from his foolhardy endeavour. Fry ignored these warnings, however, and entered the remains of the [[superweapon]], only to be attacked by a creature that Saan proceeded to save him from, revealing her identity, then disappearing after Jannah arrived and scolded her fellow Company 77 member. While speaking with Fry, Jannah told him that all of their company suffered from the same fears of being captured by the First Order as he was.<ref name="Nightmares" />
