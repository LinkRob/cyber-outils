The '''Ab Dalis system''' was a [[star system]] situated in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] that contained the [[planet]] [[Ab Dalis]]. The system was located past the [[Hetzal system]] on<ref name="Light of the Jedi" /> the [[Corellian Run]],<ref name="Galaxy Map" /> the [[hyperspace route]] along which the [[freighter]] ''[[Legacy Run]]'' was traveling when it was destroyed in [[hyperspace]], and as such was subject to the [[First Emergence|first]] [[Emergences|Emergence]] of the [[Great Hyperspace Disaster]], where fragments of the [[Starship|vessel]] entered the system at just below [[lightspeed]]. Six of the seven fragments passed through the system without hitting any objects, but the seventh hit a glancing blow on Ab Dalis, the most densely populated planet, [[Death|killing]] around twenty million people in the process.<ref name="Light of the Jedi" />
