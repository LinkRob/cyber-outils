'''Crucrarocha beetle salad''' was a type of [[salad]] made with [[crucrarocha beetle]]s that was [[Diet|edible]] by [[Hutt]]s.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> In [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{StarlightDate|''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]''|one and a half [[Standard year|years]] after the destruction of Starlight Beacon|[[228 BBY]]}}</ref> in the [[Hutt]] [[Devirsivik Clan]]'s [[Devirsivik Clan's palace|palace]] on the [[planet]] [[Nal Hutta]], a [[Toydarian]] [[Unidentified Toydarian cook|cook]] served the Hutt [[Churo]] crucrarocha [[beetle]] salad and [[fried worrt]] with his [[green juice]] for breakfast, despite Churo having previously told the [[Chef|cook]] he was a [[vegetarian]]. Churo's [[Churo and Dahara's father|father]] made him eat his meal regardless and he was mocked by his cousin [[Lorko]], who shoveled an extra helping onto Churo's plate. Later that [[Local day|day]], when Churo's [[Gender|sister]] [[Dahara Devirsivik Norri]] told him to stop grinning, Churo attempted to twist his face to look stretchy and mad like his sister's, but felt that he only managed to look like he was trying to not throw up the crucrarocha beetles he had eaten.<ref name="Beware the Nameless" />

==Behind the scenes==
Crucrarocha beetle salad was mentioned in the [[2024]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: Beware the Nameless]]'', written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] and published as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Beware the Nameless" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]].<ref name="Phase">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2024}}</ref>
