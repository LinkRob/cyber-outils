'''Desert rose incense''' was a type of [[incense]] made from [[desert rose]]s on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]].<ref name="Convergence" /> In [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] [[envoy]] [[Axel Greylark]] boarded his [[Starship|ship]], the ''[[Eventide]]'', and smelled a scent similar to desert rose incense inside the vessel's interior.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Desert rose incense was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a [[2022]] novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's [[Phase II: Quest of the Jedi|Phase II]].<ref name="Convergence" />
