"'''A Jedi Trio'''" is an interview with actors [[Liam Neeson]], [[Samuel L. Jackson]] and [[Jake Lloyd]], who respectively portrayed [[Qui-Gon Jinn/Legends|Qui-Gon Jinn]], [[Mace Windu/Legends|Mace Windu]] and nine-year-old [[Anakin Skywalker/Legends|Anakin Skywalker]] in the [[prequel trilogy]]. It was conducted by [[Kevin Fitzpatrick]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 46|''Star Wars Insider'' 46]].
