The '''Empire''', also known as the '''first Empire''', was created [[Standard century|centuries]] before the [[Sith rebellion]]. The capital of the first Empire was [[Yojou]] on the [[planet]] [[Rei'izu]]. The Empire had existed nominally long before the Sith rebellion and had once been strong and centralized, but for hundreds of [[Standard year|years]], it was united in name only. Feuding [[lord]]s had since become the real rulers of their own local territories in regions of [[Realspace|space]].<ref name="Ronin" />

When a sect of former [[Jedi]] decided to rebel and control their own destiny as the [[Sith]], rival lords [[Imperial unification|united]] to oppose them instead of warring with each other. The rebellion failed and the Empire was strengthened by the new alliances, becoming a ''de facto'' centralized Empire once again. The Jedi acted as the Empire's most loyal servants. The revitalized Empire practiced the same [[Feudalism|system of government]] as it had before the Sith rebellion.<ref name="Ronin" />
