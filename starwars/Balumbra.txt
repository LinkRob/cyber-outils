'''Balumbra''' was the eighth [[moon]] of the [[gas giant]] [[Balagash]].<ref name="MOF" /> Around [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{MOFdate|23}}</ref>  [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa]] and the the [[Imperial Intelligence]] operative [[Haki Zeophrine]] traveled to Balumbra while Organa was [[Investigation into the framing of the Jedi|investigating]] the [[Galactic Empire]]'s framing of the [[Jedi Order]] for [[treason]].<ref name="MOF" />

==Behind the scenes==
Balumbra appeared in the [[2025]] novel ''[[Reign of the Empire: The Mask of Fear]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]] as the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars: Reign of the Empire]]'' trilogy.<ref name="MOF" />
