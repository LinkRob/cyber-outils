An ornate, long-bodied [[blaster pistol]] of impeccable craftmanship, the [[Coronet Arms]] model was a typical design of '''dueling pistols'''. Often ordered in pairs, dueling [[pistol]]s possessed a stopping power that made it equal to [[Blaster carbine|carbines]] and [[rifle]]s, with a long frame that made them accurate. However, dueling pistols lost beam cohesion quickly, which limited their range and preventing them from carrying anymore than enough energy for a single blast.<ref name="Gadgets" />
