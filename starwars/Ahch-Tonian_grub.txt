An '''Ahch-Tonian grub''' was a type of creature [[Homeworld|native]] the the remote [[planet]] of [[Ahch-To]]. The [[Wookiee]] [[Chewbacca]] once attempted to use these grubs as bait for [[fishing]] on the planet, only to find that there were no [[fish]] to catch because of it being the [[winter]] season. [[Porg]]s enjoyed these grubs as [[Foodstuff|food]], though Chewbacca hated the grubs, as did Wookiees in general.<ref name="Chewie and the Porgs" />
