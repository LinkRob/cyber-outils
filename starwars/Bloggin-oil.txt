'''Bloggin-oil''' was a type of [[oil]] which could be harvested from members of the [[bloggin]] [[species]] native to the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. The occupants of [[Tuanul]] village on Jakku used bloggin-oil as [[fuel]] in their [[Bloggin-oil lamp|lamps]] around thirty [[Standard year|years]] after the [[Battle of Endor]].<ref name="TFAVD" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bloggin-oil was first mentioned in ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens: The Visual Dictionary]]'', a reference book written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]] and released in [[2015]].
