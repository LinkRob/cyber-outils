'''Charlotte Chen''' is a concept artist who provided conceptual artwork for ''[[Star Wars: The Bad Batch]]'', where she created concept art for an [[astromech droid]],<ref name="Cut and Run EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/cut-and-run-episode-guide|text="Cut and Run" Episode Guide}}</ref> spare parts aboard the [[Marauder (Omicron-class)|''Marauder'']], metal pieces aboard the ''Marauder'',<ref name="Replacements EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/replacements-episode-guide|text="Replacements" Episode Guide}}</ref> [[Raspar Six]],<ref name="Cornered EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/cornered-episode-guide|text="Cornered" Episode Guide}}</ref> and Omega's [[comlink]].<ref name="Rampage EG">{{SW|url=series/the-bad-batch/rampage-episode-guide|text="Rampage" Episode Guide}}</ref>
