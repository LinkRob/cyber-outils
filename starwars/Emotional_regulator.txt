'''Emotional regulators''' were among the components of the [[AJ^6 cyborg construct]],<ref name="Ezra's Duel with Danger">''[[Ezra's Duel with Danger]]''</ref> a [[Cybernetics|cybernetic]] implant designed to increase the user's productivity in exchange for their personality.<ref name="Databank Lobot-Tech Headgear">{{Databank|lobot-tech-headgear|Lobot-Tech Headgear}}</ref> Although the regulators were meant to suppress the emotional responses of the implant's wearer, they could be overwhelmed by strong waves of raw emotions.<ref name="Ezra's Duel with Danger" />
