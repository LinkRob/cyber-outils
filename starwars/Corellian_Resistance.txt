The '''Corellian Resistance''' was a resistance movement against the [[Galactic Empire]], [[Corellian insurgency|operating]] on [[Corellia]] during the [[Imperial Era|reign]] of [[Galactic Emperor|Emperor]] [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]]. During this time, one of their members, [[Miru Nadrinakar]], attempted to warn them of an upcoming attack by [[Imperial Military|Imperial forces]].<ref name="TeoH" />
