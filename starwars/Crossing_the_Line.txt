"'''Crossing the Line'''" ([[Wikipedia:German language|German]] title "'''Grenzüberschreitung'''") is a [[Star Wars comic books|comic strip]] written by [[Alec Worley]], drawn by [[Ingo Römling]] and edited by [[Gunther Nickel]]. The story was published in the 4th issue of the [[Wikipedia:Germany|German]] ''[[Star Wars Rebels Animation]]'', which was released on [[October 10]], [[2018]]. It was first published in English in the December 2019 issue of the Australian magazine ''K-Zone''.
