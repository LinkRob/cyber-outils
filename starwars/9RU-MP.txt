'''9RU-MP''' was a gruff [[Color|red]]-plated [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] [[astromech droid]] in the service of the [[Resistance]]. At some point 9RU-MP met [[B1-series battle droid]] [[R0-GR]] at the repair bay aboard the [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']]. R0-GR would later include 9RU-MP in his [[book]] ''[[Droidography]]''.<ref name="Droidography" />

==Equipment==
9RU-MP had multiple cicular [[tool-bay disk]]s. Its head stayed on its body with the use of [[magnet]]ic balancing, it had an [[antenna]] and a black [[sensor]].<ref name="Droidography" /> 9RU-MP also had an [[internal orbiculate motivator]] powered by a [[selenium]] drive.<ref name="Droid Repair Bay">''[[Star Wars: Droid Repair Bay]]''</ref>
