"'''Design Classic!'''" is an interview with ''[[Star Wars]]'' production designer [[Norman Reynolds]] about his work on the [[original trilogy]]. The interview was conducted by [[Jonathan Wilkins]] and published in [[Star Wars Insider 125|''Star Wars Insider'' 125]].<ref name="sw125" /> It was reprinted in [[Star Wars Insider Special Edition 2013|''Star Wars Insider'' Special Edition 2013]].<ref name="swse2013" />
