During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Count]] [[Dooku]] dispatched his [[Sith apprentice|apprentice]], [[Sith assassin]] [[Asajj Ventress]], to '''capture''' [[Outpost 716]], which even [[General]] [[Grievous]] had failed to [[Attempt to capture Outpost 716|conquer]]. Ventress quickly cut down three [[clone trooper]]s outside before being subdued by [[Jedi General]] [[Mayanka Catrine]] and [[Jedi Commander]] [[Agustin]], unknowingly playing into her hands: Ventress was held captive in the outpost for several [[Standard day|days]] until she confirmed, by overhearing a [[Cloning|clone]] say as such, that Catrine had departed for a [[Unidentified mission (Mayanka Catrine)|new mission]]. Deducing that both Jedi were gone, Ventress escaped, overcame the base's defenses, and captured it for the Confederacy.<ref name="HS 5" />
