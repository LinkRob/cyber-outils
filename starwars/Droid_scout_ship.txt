This '''[[droid]] [[scout ship]]''' was used to [[scout]] the [[planet]] [[Jakku]], and was scheduled to leave on the afternoon following the [[attack on Tuanul]]. The [[Labor droid|loading droid]] [[F3-ZK]] was scheduled to be picked up at the [[Starship Graveyard]] by the droid ship, but fell into a trap set by [[Teedo (Niima Outpost)|Teedo]]. Fez was saved by [[BB-8]], and after a series of misadventures reached his ship. BB-8 distracted Teedo while Fez boarded, and the vessel departed Jakku safely.<ref name="Run" />

==Behind the scenes==
The droid scout ship first appeared in the [[2017]] young readers book ''[[BB-8 on the Run]]'', written by [[Drew Daywalt]] and illustrated by [[Matt Myers]].<ref name="Run" /> The physical appearance of the vessel closely resembles that of the [[Light freighter/Legends|light freighter]] ''[[Dusty Duck]]'',<ref name="DB">{{DB|starship|dustyduck|Dusty Duck}}</ref> which appeared in a deleted scene of the [[1999]] [[prequel trilogy]] film, [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']].<ref name="TPM">{{Film|I}}</ref>
