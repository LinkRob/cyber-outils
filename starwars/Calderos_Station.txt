'''Calderos Station''' was an [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[space station]] that served as a deep-space waypoint<ref name="Leia" /> in the [[Bryx sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Due to local Imperial travel restrictions, ships traveling to or from certain worlds, such as [[Wobani]], were required to pass through the station. In [[3 BBY]], the station was [[Attack on Calderos|attacked]] by the rebellion in order to cover up rebel shipping to their [[Crait outpost|outpost]] on the planet [[Crait]]. Returning from a diplomatic mission to Wobani, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] witnessed the damaged station.<ref name="Leia" />
