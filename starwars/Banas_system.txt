The '''Banas system''' was located in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] region of [[the galaxy]]. The [[star system]] was home to the [[planet]] [[Banas]] and [[Unidentified Banas species|its species]], although most of the [[Species|population]] lived on other planets in the system. During the [[Galactic Civil War]], [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] [[Mission to Banas|traveled]] to Banas to determine if the planet was suitable for a base for the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]]. During the mission, the [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] presence on Banas convinced Organa the system was to be avoided by the Rebellion.<ref name="HS2" />
