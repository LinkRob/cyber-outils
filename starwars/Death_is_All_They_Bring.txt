'''''Death is All They Bring''''' was an anti-[[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[propaganda]] [[Painting|poster]] created by the [[Bith]] [[artist]] [[Janyor]] at the [[time]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]]. The poster depicts the visage of the Imperial enforcer Darth Vader's helmet against a space backdrop, as well as the Death Star firing its superlaser on a planet below, with red lights emitting from the planet, with the word up top being "Death" with the individual letters being spaced out, and the words on the bottom declaring "is All They Bring."<ref name="Propaganda" />
