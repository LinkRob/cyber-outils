'''Star Wars Celebration Anaheim''' of [[2022]] was the fourteenth [[Celebration]] convention. It was held from [[May 26]] to [[May 29|29]] at the Anaheim Convention Center in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]]. Originally scheduled for [[August 27]]&ndash;[[August 30|30]], [[2020]], it was canceled due to the [[Wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]], and rescheduled for the same location in 2022, the year marking the 45th anniversary of the ''Star Wars'' franchise.

==History==
[[File:StarWarsCelebrationAnaheim2020.png|thumb|left|250px|The convention's original 2020 logo]]
Celebration Anaheim was announced as the next official ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Celebration]] on the fourth day of [[2019]]'s [[Celebration Chicago]].<ref name="Announcement">{{StarWarsShow|sP6SaKn2WuM|Star Wars Celebration Chicago 2019 Live Stream - Day 4 &#124; The Star Wars Show LIVE!}}</ref> It was originally scheduled to be held [[August 27]]&ndash;[[August 30|30]], [[2020]], at the Anaheim Convention Center in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]].<ref name="Dates Announced" /> A trailer was released,<ref name="Trailer">{{SWYouTube|yPR1aJKDf6E|Star Wars Celebration Anaheim 2020 Trailer}}</ref> and tickets went on sale on [[June 21]], 2019.<ref name="Tickets 2020">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-2020-tickets-on-sale-now|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim 2020 Tickets On Sale Now!}}</ref>

In early 2020, the [[Wikipedia:COVID-19 pandemic|COVID-19 pandemic]] struck. As a result, [[Lucasfilm]] postponed Celebration Anaheim two years,<ref name="Postponement">{{SW|url=news/an-update-on-star-wars-celebration-2020|text=An Update on ''Star Wars'' Celebration 2020}}</ref> marking the first cancellation or postponement of any Celebration.<ref name="Deadline">{{WebCite|url=https://deadline.com/2020/06/star-wars-celebration-canceled-will-return-2022-1202959943|text=Star Wars Celebration Canceled, Set To Return In 2022|author=Ramos, Dino-Ray|date=2020-06-16|work=[[Wikipedia:Deadline Hollywood|Deadline Hollywood]]|archivedate=20200616042022}}</ref><ref name="Variety">{{WebCite|url=https://variety.com/2020/tv/news/star-wars-celebration-2020-canceled-coroanvirus-1234635590|text=Star Wars Celebration Canceled for First Time, Sets New Date for 2022|author=Murphy, J. Kim|date=2020-06-15|work=[[Wikipedia:Variety (magazine)|Variety]]|archivedate=20200616065020}}</ref> The convention was rescheduled for [[August 18]]&ndash;[[August 21|21]], [[2022]], at the same location. Fans who had already purchased tickets were offered a choice of either a refund or transferring their tickets to the new dates.<ref name="Postponement" />

On [[August 28]], 2020, exclusive merchandise created for the convention was placed on sale at an online store, initially open only to those who had purchased tickets for the original dates of the convention. The store became open to the general public on August 31.<ref name="Exclusives">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2020-exclusives-revealed|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration 2020 Exclusives Revealed}}</ref> The merchandise store was opened again for early access on [[November 13]], and to the general public on [[November 20]].<ref name="New Merch">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-merchandise|text=New ''Star Wars'' Celebration Merch Heading to Our Galaxy}}</ref> Exclusive artwork and prints intended for the convention were placed on sale in an art show beginning [[December 15]], 2020.<ref name="Art Show">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-2020-art-show-revealed-exclusive|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration 2020 Art Show Revealed &ndash; Exclusive}}</ref>

On [[May 12]], [[2021]], [[StarWars.com]] announced that the Celebration was being moved up from August, and would now be held [[May 26]]&ndash;[[May 29|29]], 2022.<ref name="Preponement" /> Tickets went on sale on [[March 15]], 2022, along with merchandise pre-orders for ticketholders.<ref name="Tickets 2022">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-2022-tickets|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim 2022 Tickets Back On Sale Soon}}</ref> The Celebration's first guests were announced on [[March 24]].<ref name="Guests">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-2022-guest-announce-1|text=''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim Announces First Celebrity Guests}}</ref> The convention's first panel was announced on [[April 6]],<ref name="Live-Action">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-2022-filmmakers-special-guests|text=Join Lucasfilm's Filmmakers and Special Guests for a Look at What's Coming Soon to a Galaxy Near You&hellip;}}</ref> and the full schedule was unveiled on [[April 27]].<ref name="Panels">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-celebration-anaheim-panel-schedule-2022|text=Check Out the ''Star Wars'' Celebration Anaheim Panel Schedule}}</ref>
