'''Fbinti Fith''' was a [[Chadra-Fan]] [[Gender|female]] who served as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Republic]]'s [[Galactic Senate|senate]]. After she retired, the Republic was [[Proclamation of the New Order|replaced]] by the autocratic [[Galactic Empire]], and Fith sought to quietly build local resistance against the new regime early on. When the [[planet]] of [[Anoat]] was [[Gassing of Anoat|devastated]] by the Empire, her determination was shaken.<ref name="Uprising" />
{{GameMechanics|Star Wars: Uprising}}
Several [[Standard month|months]] into the Imperial [[Iron Blockade|blockade]] of the entire [[Anoat sector]], the ex-senator decided to fight again. She joined the crew of a [[The Smuggler|young smuggler]] from [[Burnin Konn]], using her skills on various missions throughout the [[sector]].<ref name="Uprising" />
