'''Brun Obatsun''' was an individual who served in the [[First Order military|military branch]] of the [[First Order]]. Obatsun worked on the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]'', the [[flagship]] and headquarters for the First Order, acting as one of the flagship's [[First Order fleet gunner|gunners]]. Obatsun's [[Armor|helmet]] had the data feed of their targets fed through the visor.<ref name="TLJ:TVD" />
