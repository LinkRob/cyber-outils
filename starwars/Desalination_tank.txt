'''Desalination tanks''' were devices designed to extract [[salt]] from [[Water|seawater]] for industrial use. Four blocky [[Color|gray]] [[desalination]] tanks were built along the side of the [[factory]] designated [[Coronet-SFS-14]] in [[Coronet City]] on the [[planet]] [[Corellia]].<ref name="SoloOG" />

In [[13 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events on [[Corellia]] in ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'' to [[13 BBY]].</ref> a [[human]] worker took shelter between the tanks when the criminals [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] raced past in a [[M-68 landspeeder]] while fleeing from the [[White Worms]] gang's<ref name="Solo">''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> [[A-A4B truckspeeder]],<ref name="SoloOG" /> which also passed the tanks.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
Desalination tanks first appeared in the [[2018]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Anthology Series'']] film, ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'',<ref name="Solo" /> and were first identified in the accompanying reference book ''[[Solo: A Star Wars Story The Official Guide]]'', which was written by [[Pablo Hidalgo]].<ref name="SoloOG" /> The Corellia [[speeder]] chase locations in ''Solo'' were filmed at [[Wikipedia:Fawley Power Station|Fawley Power Station]] or [[Wikipedia:Tilbury|Tilbury]] in [[Wikipedia:England|England]].<ref name="Making of">''[[Industrial Light & Magic Presents: Making Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
