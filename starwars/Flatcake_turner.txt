{{Quote|Are you mocking me, Commander? Because if you are, as the Emperor is my witness, I'll take you down so hard and so fast they'll have to scrape up what's left of your career with a flatcake turner.|Admiral Carlou Gendling threatens Commander Alfren Cheno|<ref name="Thrawn" />}}
'''Flatcake turners''' were a device which could be used to prepare [[flatcake]]s. During an argument with [[Galactic Empire|Imperial]] [[Commander]] [[Alfren Cheno]] after a [[Battle over Umbara|battle]] over the [[planet]] [[Umbara]], [[Admiral]] [[Carlou Gendling]] threatened Cheno for mocking him and showing him up during the battle, claiming the commander would need to metaphorically scrape up his career with a flatcake turner once the admiral was done with him.<ref name="Thrawn" />

==Behind the scenes==
