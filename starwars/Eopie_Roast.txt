{{Quote|There are lots of worlds where eopie is considered a delicacy. Personally, I never quite understood the appeal of gnawin' on tough meat from such an ill-tempered, flatulent creature. But a visit to Bakkar over at Ronto Roasters in Black Sire Outpost changed my tune.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="LDCookbook" />}}
'''Eopie Roast''' was a dish created by the [[human]] [[chef]] [[Bakkar]], proprietor of [[Ronto Roasters]]. It consisted of the seasoned roast rump of an [[eopie]]. After Bakkar allowed him to sample some Eopie Roast, the [[Artiodac]] chef [[Strono Tuggs]] traded one of his best [[Vibroknife|vibroknives]] to Bakkar in exchange for the recipe. Tuggs then published the recipe in his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]''.

==Description==
{{Quote|[Bakkar] lemme sample a slice of roasted eopie rump so tender that it practically melted in my big ol' mouth. The way his special seasonin' blend permeated the meat was pure magic.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="LDCookbook" />}}
Eopie Roast was a [[Foodstuff|dish]] consisting of the rump of an [[eopie]] flavored with a special seasoning and roasted in an oven. It was edible to [[Artiodac]]s and [[human]]s and was served as a main course. The Artiodac [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] described the dish as so tender that it melted in his [[mouth]] and called the way that the seasoning permeated the meat magical.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==History==
{{Quote|Bakkar ain't usually one to share his trade secrets, though, even with an ol' friend like me. Had to trade one of my best vibroknives to get the recipe from him, but I still think I came out on top in that deal.|Strono Tuggs, ''The Life Day Cookbook''|<ref name="LDCookbook" />}}
[[File:Bakkar.png|thumb|right|250px|alt=A monochrome photo of a smiling light-skinned man with a neat haircut and a dark jacket over a shirt.|Eopie Roast was a creation of Bakkar.]]
Eopie Roast was created, along with the special seasoning used in the dish, by the human chef [[Bakkar]], proprietor of [[Ronto Roasters]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[planet]] [[Batuu]]. At one point, he gave a slice of roasted eopie rump to the Strono Tuggs as a sample while Tuggs was dining at Ronto Roasters. The Artiodac, who had always been dubious of eating tough eopie meat due to the ill-tempered and flatulent nature of the creature, was so impressed by the tenderness and flavor of the sample that he asked Bakkar for the recipe. Although Bakkar was usually unwilling to part with his trade secrets, Tuggs convinced his fellow chef to share by trading him one of his best [[Vibroknife|vibroknives]] in exchange for it.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

Believing that he had gotten the better end of the deal, Tuggs then included the recipe in the main courses section of his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', which he released at some point after his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]] was published<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref> He suggested that readers could not go wrong with Eopie Roast at any special event, including the [[Life Day]] festival.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Eopie Roast was created as a recipe for the [[2021]] ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe uses [[Wikipedia:Garlic|garlic cloves]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|packed light brown sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Paprika|paprika]], [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]], [[Wikipedia:Black pepper|black pepper]], [[Wikipedia:Pork|pork loin]], and fresh [[Wikipedia:Rosemary|rosemary]].<ref name="LDCookbook" />
