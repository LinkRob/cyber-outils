'''Deva Lompop''' was a [[Shani]] [[Gender|female]] [[bounty hunter]], [[Pirate|marauder]], and [[mercenary]] who operated from the [[time]] of the [[High Republic Era|High Republic]] into the [[Imperial Era]]. By the time of the [[Galactic Empire]], Lompop had a debt to [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba the Hutt]] that amounted to more than [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]] and caused Lompop to be routinely summoned by the [[Hutt]] gangster for various missions. Eventually, Lompop worked with the criminal organization [[Crimson Dawn]].

==Biography==
===With the Nihil===
In [[230 BBY]],<ref name="Mission to Disaster date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Mission to Disaster]]'' to [[230 BBY]].</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], Deva Lompop was a member of the marauders known as the [[Nihil]] and served under [[Tempest Runner]] [[Kara Xoo]]. She was placed in charge of handling the children kidnapped by Xoo's [[Tempest]], including [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]], and caught Starros when she first tried to escape, delivering her to [[Zadina Mkampa]] and threatening to eat the girl if she tried again.<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> While with the Nihil, she helped the [[Nautolan]] [[Youngling|girl]] [[Vroma]] stop bullies from taking a [[durga berry]] meant for the girl's [[Vroma's mothers|mothers]], and then launched an [[Attack on Hon-Tallos|attack]] on [[Hon-Tallos]].<ref name="SWA14">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2020|issue=14|story=Tales of Villainy: A Very Nihil Interlude}}</ref>

=== Alliance with Avon ===
After the destruction of Starlight Beacon, Lompop became the bodyguard of Avon Starros while the girl was being held in the [[Nihil main base|Nihil base]] on [[Hetzal Prime]] by  [[Ghirra Starros|her mother]], and Lompop and Avon became close. Lompop protected Avon from an attack by a [[Unidentified Nautolan Nihil|member of the Nihil]], and then the two left Hetzal in order to rescue Avon's Jedi friend [[Imri Cantaros]].<ref name="Defy the Storm">''[[The High Republic: Defy the Storm]]''</ref>

Lompop and Avon split up, and Avon went to find Cantaros's master, [[Vernestra Rwoh]]. Using [[Maz Kanata]] as an intermediary, Lompop hired [[Sylvestri Yarrow]] and [[Jordanna Sparkburn]] to kidnap [[Xylan Graf]]. Lompop met them at a location in the Seswenna system and destroyed the ship of [[Corax Oreli]], Graf's Nihil bodyguard who had followed him. Avon and Rwoh arrived and boarded Lompop's ship, the ''[[Dreadful Day]]''. Sparkburn decided to go with them into the [[Occlusion Zone]], and Lompop used Graf's codes to cross the [[Stormwall]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

After the group landed on [[Seswenna]], Avon absconded with Graf to destroy the [[Lightning Crash]], while Sparkburn left to find her family. Lompop and Rwoh traveled to [[Aricho]] to find Cantaros. There, they fought a pair of Nihil who were trying to abduct a child for [[Kara Xoo]]. They reached the apparently abandoned city of [[Ytrecht]], and Lompop left to find out what Xoo was up to, telling Rwoh to stay put. By the time Lompop returned, Rwoh had reunited with Cantaros and the Jedi had defeated Xoo.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

Cantaros chose to stay in the Occlusion Zone to continue helping people, and Lompop and Rwoh returned to Seswenna. As they approached the planet, their ship was attacked by Nihil [[Scav droid|scav droids]], and they were forced to take an escape pod. They fought their way through the Nihil and reunited with Avon and Sparkburn on the ''Dreadful Day''.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

Thanks to Avon's work on the hyperdrive and Rwoh's navigating, the ''Dreadful Day'' was able to cross the Stormwall. On [[Takodana]], Rwoh asked Lompop about the [[Path]] she was given by [[Mari San Tekka]]. Lompop told her that it led to a place beyond the edges of [[Wild Space]] and that in old Shani stories, it was said to be full of treasure.<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

After parting ways with Avon and the others, Lompop tried to borrow a ship in order to return to Aricho to acquire glaka root, which was highly valued by [[Hutt|Hutts]].<ref name="Defy the Storm" />

===Dangerous debt===
{{Quote|&laquo;This is not a matter of ''money'', Deva, and I know you know that.&raquo;|Jabba the Hutt|<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />}}
At some point, [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] the [[Hutt]] saved Lompop's life, leaving her in his debt that transcended monetary compensation.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" /> At one point, Lompop employed a group of at least six bounty hunters to assassinate the [[Culisetto]] warlord [[Cribiriz Idollax]], offering a lump sum to whichever individual was responsible for the kill. The group of hunters included [[4-LOM]] and [[Zuckuss]], who eliminated who they thought was Cribiriz but was actually their sibling [[Sribiriz]], and emerged from the mission as a team.<ref name="4&Z">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – 4-LOM & Zuckuss'' 1]]</ref> At another time, Lompop became romantically involved with the [[Zabrak]] [[arms dealer]] [[Jarm Brock]].<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />

By the time of the [[Imperial Era]], Lompop had moved on from bounty hunting to other work as an information broker operating out of the [[Dalnan sector]]. Despite her change in career, she was frequently pulled away from her work by Jabba's request. On one occasion, she was invited to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]] and coerced into joining the bounty hunter [[Boba Fett]] in a hunt for Brock, who Jabba believed was attacking his men under the orders of [[Bokku]], who, like Jabba, was a member of the [[Grand Hutt Council]]. Jabba promised to finally lift Lompop's debt to him, although Lompop had her doubts that the Hutt was telling the truth. Fett and Lompop tracked down Brock in the town of [[Mos Entha]] and engaged in a firefight, which ended with Fett cornering Brock and Lompop killing Brock's men. However, Lompop betrayed Fett, stabbing him with a poisoned dagger and knocking him unconscious, though not before he burned her with his [[flamethrower]]. Lompop interrogated Brock, revealing that the Zabrak had in fact been hired by [[Crimson Dawn]], rather than by Bokku. Lompop proceeded to unhinge her jaw and eat Brock alive, leaving only his head, which she delivered to Jabba. The Hutt refused to clear Lompop's debt as he had promised, citing the fact that Brock had not been returned alive.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />

[[File:DevaBartenderDeal-WOTBHJabbaTheHutt1.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Lompop subdued Doc Ragon for information]]
Some time later, Jabba called Lompop in to aid in the hunt for the [[carbonite]]-frozen [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], though she secretly had contacted Crimson Dawn with the hope of killing Jabba. Tracking Boba Fett to the moon of [[Nar Shaddaa]], she learned from a bystander that an individual with similar armor to Fett had contacted the [[Besalisk]] [[Doctor|Doc]] [[Ragon]]. She found Ragon at a bar and seduced him into getting drunk and revealing that he had been paid to inspect by Fett to fix the carbonite matrix containing Solo. Lompop informed both Jabba and [[Crimson Dawn]] of Fett's location, after observing him enter a tavern, for which she was paid. The Crimson Dawn official [[Margo]] promised Lompop a chance to kill the Hutt sometime in the future.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />

===Working for Crimson Dawn===
Lompop later traveled to [[Ord Mantell]] and completed a bounty for [[Gyuti]], where she also encountered the [[Ubese]] bounty hunter [[Boushh]]. Lompop warned Boushh that his bad blood with [[Black Sun]] would cost him, and that if he was unlucky she would be sent to deal with him.<ref name="Boushh 1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Boushh'' 1]]</ref> Lompop continued to work in the employ of Crimson Dawn, hiring [[RB-919]] on their behalf to restore the destroyed [[IG-series assassin droid]] [[IG-88B|IG-88]]. After the rebuilt IG-88 killed RB-919, the droid contacted Lompop, who gave him a mission to pursue Boba Fett.<ref name="War of the Bounty Hunters IG-88" />

===Working with the Starros clan===
Circa [[3 ABY]]Y<ref name="Date">{{SanaStarrosDate}}</ref>, Lompop was hosting a party in the Dalnan sector when [[Sana Starros]], [[Mevera Starros|Mevera]], and [[Thea Starros|Thea]] showed up.<ref name="Sana Starros 2">[[Sana Starros 2|''Sana Starros'' 2]]</ref> She met with them as they asked for her help in retrieving the data cube containing [[Avon's journals|Avon Starros' research]] that was taken by [[Phel Starros]]. After Sana left angrily, she found her talking to [[Lanitra]] by the bar. Lompop informed Lanitra that her favor was being granted and that they would leave for Hon-Tallos shortly. Lanitra asked why Lompop had changed her mind, and she responded that she had found some help for the job. Sana asked Lompop why she cared about the Starros Clan. The Shani told her that a long time ago she made a promise to watch over the clan like it was her own.<ref name="Sana Starros 3" />
[[File:Hragscythe-SanaStarros3.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Lompop and Sana Starros face down a hragscythe]]
Lompop travelled to Hon-Tallos along with the Starros women and Lanitra to [[Wild Wollina's Animal Adventures]]. She purchased admission to the zoo and waited for Lanitra to start a distraction. A [[hragscythe]] was released in the park, and Sana and Lompop went to prevent it from harming innocents. While Sana saved [[Outrie]], Lompop jumped onto the creature and stabbed it with her poisoned blades. After subduing the creature, they went to the [[rancor]] breeding pens. Lompop instructed Sana to pour juvenile rancor urine over herself in order to trick the rancor they were stealing into thinking Sana was her cub. As they left, Sana asked Lompop why she didn't have to douse herself in urine and the Shani told her that the rancor recognized her as a predator and would not attack her. On their way out, the pair were confronted by [[Wollina]] and [[Marl Jibs]]. Lompop attacked Wollina for breaking their contract and breeding the rancor named [[Nussi]] that Lompop had provided to the zoo. Afterwards, she met up with Sana and the [[Genetian Collective]] as they prepared to take the animals from the zoo and transport them to freedom. She teased Sana about smelling badly. When Sana got a call from Mevera telling her that her grandmother was taken hostage by Jibs, Lompop observed that the Starroses had a knack for getting kidnapped.<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> They returned to the ''[[Vector Bundle]]'' and travelled to [[Hynestia Prime|Hynestia]]. Lompop checked with her [[Soikan]] contacts and determined they were not responsible for Jibs' kidnapping of Thea. Sana and Mevera argued about how to rescue their matriarch until Lompop interrupted them and told them her plan. They landed on Hynestria and Sana questioned Lompop about her plan. Lompop said it would probably fail and was mostly there just to get more information on the situation. Realizing that Sana was extremely stressed, Lompop instigated a fight with her to relax her. They proceeded to an old [[Temple on Hynestia|Jedi temple]] that was currently being used as a bar. Jibs sprung his ambush on them, causing Lompop and Sana to dive behind a bar to avoid fire. While there, Lompop helped herself to some [[Durga berry schnapps|durga berry schnapp]]. She laid down covering fire so that Sana could purse her grandmother's kidnappers. Outside, Sana was confronted by her brother who shot at her. Lompop knocked Sana aside, taking the blaster bolt. After the Imperials escaped, she consumed a downed [[Cold weather assault trooper|snowtrooper]] to help herself recover.<ref name="Sana Starros 4">[[Sana Starros 4|''Sana Starros'' 4]]</ref>
[[File:DevaPreparesToEat-SanaStarros4.png|thumb|left|250px|Deva prepares to eat a snowtrooper]]
Lompop and Sana returned to space to rescue their family from the ''[[King's Ransom]]''. They were joined by [[Hondo Ohnaka]] who had been contacted by Lompop to provide backup. When he attacked two [[TIE/IN interceptor]]s, she took control of the ''Vector Bundle''{{'s}} ion cannons and disabled the starfighters for salvaging. The ''Vector Bundle'' landed in the hangar bay of the ''King's Ransom'' and Lompop vented the [[knockout gas]] that Lanitra had released on the ship. The pair stumbled across a group of [[stormtrooper]]s and Lompop ordered Sana to go find her family while she worked on rescuing Lanitra who was pinned down by enemy fire. She made her way to Lanitra and her crew and helped eliminate the [[fire team]] when they were overwhelmed by Sana playing [[Chadra-Fan opera]] through the [[speaker|comm speakers]] in the helmets of the stormtroopers. She captured [[Cerasus Ehllo]] and forced him to take them to the Starros data cube. She stood outside while Sana greeted [[Aryssha Starros]] and her [[Desana|new]] [[Thevera|babies]]. She unlocked and gave the data cube to Sana who decided it was too dangerous to have around and destroyed it despite it being priceless. She confirmed when Sana guessed that Lompop already had a buyer lined up for the captured [[Raider-class corvette|''Raider''-class corvette]]. When Lanitra showed up and informed her that the galley was stocked with [[alcohol]], she left to go get a drink.<ref name="Sana Starros 5">[[Sana Starros 5|''Sana Starros'' 5]]</ref>

==Personality and traits==
{{Quote|I hate cold food.|Deva Lompop, prior to eating Jarm Brock|<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />}}
Deva Lompop was sardonic and tenacious bounty hunter, renowned amongst those who knew her,<ref name="Boushh 1" /> though some of her colleagues held her in contempt.<ref name="4&Z" /> Lompop's remarks were often either threatening or joking, depending on her company. She enjoyed violence, once relishing in picking a fight with several individuals on Nar Shaddaa who cat-called her, going so far as to slay one with her retractable blade.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" /> However, despite her lethal and menacing tendencies, she had an occasional sense of humor, and was shown to have at least some moral standards, having once aided the young Nautolan girl Vroma with her bully problem without seeking any payment,<ref name="SWA14" /> and, while on a job with Boba Fett, offhandedly called Jabba a monster for his use of [[Slavery|slave]] pits.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" /> Lompop was also shown to be a faithful person when she helped the [[Starros Clan|Starros family]], whom she had promised to look after as if they were her own,<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> risking her life alongside them on their adventure. Though she would occasionally not take dire situations seriously, at one point even enjoying a drink while under gunfire.<ref name="Sana Starros 4" /> As a cunning information broker, she had many employees and connections.<ref name="Sana Starros 3" />

Deva's motives for pursuing the bounty hunter's occupation likely stemmed from her enjoyment of her high class lifestyle, owning her own manor with attendants, and hosting parties for her many guests, of whom she considered to be working for her, whether they knew it or not.<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> When on the job, she was shown to be a keen strategist and leader, and a fearless combatant in the use of her blades and blaster, being able to match or even exceed the ability of Boba Fett, having apparently fought and taken down several of Jarm Brock's armed men while Boba Fett chased after three. She had a rivalry with Fett, despite reluctantly working together on a mission for Jabba. She regularly referred to Fett as "Pup."<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />

Like other members of the Shani people, Deva had sharp teeth and the ability to unhinge her jaw. It was rumored that her species could swallow sentients whole. She was unable to completely consume Jarm Brock's head due to his horns, but was seemingly able to eat a dead stormtrooper whom had been killed by Sana Starros. She was not shy about bringing up her species' reputation as man-eaters, remarking to Boba Fett that she usually ate her former romantic partners,<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" /> threatening to eat Avon Starros,<ref name="Mission to Disaster" /> scaring a young bully who had been tormenting Vroma,<ref name="SWA14" /> and once remarking that she'd look for someone to eat on [[Naboo]] after a job. She preferred her meals to be alive, once saying that she had a distaste for "cold" food, though she also enjoyed roast [[ghara bird]] served at Jabba's Palace.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />

==Equipment==
{{Quote|&laquo;The Lady Deva is quite the hunter.&raquo;|[[Bib Fortuna]]|<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />}}
Lompop carried a variety of weapons, including [[Knife|knives]] and [[blaster]]s, which she could dual-wield with ease. During missions, she wore plated [[armor]]. Her gauntlets contained hidden blades, which on at least one occasion were coated with a nonfatal but incapacitating [[poison]]. She was able to drive a [[speeder]]. In addition to being a skilled fighter in [[Unarmed combat|hand-to-hand combat]], she was familiar with more elaborate weaponry, including flesh-melting [[grenade]]s.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />

When not on a job, she would don an elaborate blue gown and a turban when relaxing in her home, tended to by her maids and workers.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" /> For special occasions, she would dress in a luxurious white robe,<ref name="Sana Starros 3" /> and when seducing targets while on a job, she would wear an alluring purple, feathered dress.<ref name="Jabba the Hutt" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|Deva was the product of my absolute love of feathered lizards and badass women. There is definitely something awesome about getting a chance to bring a High Republic character to the ''War of the Bounty Hunters'', and for High Republic fans they should watch for Deva in upcoming works.|Justina Ireland|<ref name="Deva" />}}
[[File:WarOfTheBountyHuntersJabba1-Coello.png|thumb|right|200px|The 2nd Printing cover for ''War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt 1'' featuring Deva Lompop.]]
Deva Lompop was created by writer [[Justina Ireland]] and artist [[Iban Coello]] for the ''[[Star Wars: War of the Bounty Hunters]]'' [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] crossover event. She debuted in [[War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters – Jabba the Hutt'' 1]]. The character was first revealed in an article published on [[StarWars.com]] on [[June 24]], [[2021]].<ref name="Deva" />
