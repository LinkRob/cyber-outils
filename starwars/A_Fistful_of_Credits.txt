"'''A Fistful of Credits'''" is an adventure outline written by [[Bill Olmesdahl]] and published in [[1992]] as part of [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars Gamemaster Screen]]'' for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game, Second Edition]]''. The adventure sends a group of [[Spacer/Legends|spacers]] to resist a [[Jauxson's pirate gang|gang]] of [[Pirate/Legends|pirates]] led by a [[Gotal/Legends|Gotal]] named [[Jauxson]] as they raid the [[Domed city/Legends|domed]] [[City/Legends|city]] of [[Buerhoz]] on the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Clak'dor VII/Legends|Clak'dor VII]].
