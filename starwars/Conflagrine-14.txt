'''Conflagrine-14''' was an extremely flammable gel used in [[First Order]] [[First Order flametrooper|flametrooper]] [[D-93 Incinerator flamethrower]]s.<ref name="Visual" />

It was supplied via twin [[fuel]] tanks on the flametrooper's backpack and fed with an additional central pressurized propellant tank.<ref name="Visual" /> Ignited electrically, the gel was launched by a separate propellant gas and could reach a distance of 75 meters.<ref name="Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Character Encyclopedia: Updated and Expanded]]''</ref>
