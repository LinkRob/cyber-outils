==Events==
*The [[Pacification operations in the Western Reaches|Western Reaches pacification operations]] are brought to a close.<ref name="Tarkin" />
*[[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] of [[Alderaan]] turns four and shortly after receives her "big girl bed" making her decide that she is too old to sleep in her parents bed anymore.<ref name="Princess of Alderaan">''[[Leia, Princess of Alderaan]]''</ref>

==Births==
*[[Kitwarr]] on [[Kashyyyk]].<ref name="Kitwarr">{{HelmetCollectionCite|33|Databank A-Z}} states that [[Kitwarr]] was ten [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of ''[[Star Wars Rebels: Spark of Rebellion]]'', which takes place in [[5 BBY]] according to ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''. Therefore, Kitwarr was born in 15 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]].</ref>
*[[Wyl Lark]] on [[Polyneus]].<ref name="Alphabet Squadron">[[Alphabet Squadron (novel)|''Alphabet Squadron'']] states that [[Wyl Lark]] is nineteen [[Standard year|years]] old during the events of the novel, which is placed in [[4 ABY]] due to the fact that it takes place shortly after [[Star Wars: Episode VI Return of the Jedi]], which is dated to [[4 ABY]] in ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''.</ref>
*[[Asara Deyn]]<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>

==Deaths==
*[[Tanith Ponta]]<ref name="15 BBY note">''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: The Ultimate Visual Guide]]'' states that [[Jyn Erso]] was eight [[Standard year|years]] old in [[13 BBY]]. When Erso is sixteen in ''[[Rebel Rising]]'', [[Hadder Ponta]] states that Tanith Ponta [[Death|died]] a [[Standard decade|decade]] prior. Using simple math it can be deduced that Tanith Ponta died in [[15 BBY]].</ref>
