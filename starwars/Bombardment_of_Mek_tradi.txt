The '''bombardment of Mek'tradi''' was a massacre carried out by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] prior to the [[Battle of Endor]] in an attempt to draw out a [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|rebel]] cell. After participating in the [[Orbital bombardment|bombardment]], [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]] was horrified at her part in the event.<ref name="Alphabet Squadron" />
