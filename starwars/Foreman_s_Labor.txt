The '''''Foreman's Labor''''' was a [[Hardcell-class interstellar transport|''Hardcell''-class interstellar transport]] used by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems Navy|CIS Navy]] of the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. ''Foreman's Labor'' was considered legendary for its ability to hold out under the heaviest of enemy firepower.<ref name="FFG" />

It participated in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the battle that began the war, alongside three other ''Hardcell''-class interstellar [[transport]]s as they attempted to escape the [[planet]] [[Geonosis]]. During the escape, the transports fought against [[Galactic Republic]] [[starship]]s, resulting in the destruction of one transport, the critical damaging of another, the damage to a third, and the ''Foreman's Labor''{{'s}} [[deflector shield]]s receiving enemy fire. It was also equipped with six [[sublight engine]]s and at least three forward [[laser cannon]]s.<ref name="FFG" />
