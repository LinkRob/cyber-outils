The '''Endorian carrot''' was a [[plant]] found on the [[moon]] [[Endor]]. While making a culinary tour of the [[galaxy]]<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> between [[34 ABY]] and [[35 ABY]],<ref name="UltimateDate">{{UltimateCookbookDate}}</ref> the [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] ate a [[Bright Tree Village Salad]] that was made from endorian carrots during an festival that celebrated the [[Golden One]]. Chef Tuggs included a recipe for the salad in his third [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Ultimate Cookbook]]'',<ref name="UltimateCookbook" /> which was published in 35 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]].<ref name="UltimateDate" />

==Behind the scenes==
Endorian carrots were mentioned as an ingredient in the [[Bright Tree Village Salad]] recipe in the [[2023]] ''[[Star Wars: The Ultimate Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the dish makes use of [[Wikipedia:Daikon|daikon radishes]] and [[Wikipedia:Carrot|carrots]] as the out-of-universe substitute for the ingredient.<ref name="UltimateCookbook" />
