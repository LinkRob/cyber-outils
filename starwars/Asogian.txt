'''Asogians''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]]<ref name="JC">{{InsiderCite|161|Jedi Challenge!}}</ref> that partly shared their name with both the [[Asog system]] and the [[Brodo Asogi system]].<ref name="Appendix">{{GalaxyMapAppendix}}</ref> At least three Asogians were affiliated with the [[Galactic Senate]] of the [[Galactic Republic|Republic]] at the [[time]] of [[Naboo]]'s [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]].<ref name="TPM" />

{{Species-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
Asogians first appeared in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']], the first installment of the ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[prequel trilogy]].<ref name="TPM" /> Their appearance was a homage to the title character of [[Steven Spielberg]]'s [[1982]] classic film [[Wikipedia:E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial|''E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial'']] to make good on a promise&mdash;after Spielberg included a [[Yoda]] cameo and ''Star Wars'' toys such as [[Boba Fett]] in ''E.T.'', [[George Lucas]] promised to include an ''E.T.'' cameo in the next ''Star Wars'' film he made&mdash;''The Phantom Menace''.<ref name="Insider">{{InsiderCite|161|Jedi Challenge!}}</ref>
