'''Contraband Trade Ports''' were structures utilized by both the [[Galactic Empire]] and [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] in association with the [[Hutt Clan]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]]. They operated as [[spaceport]]s where [[Hutt Clan]]-affiliated [[smuggler]]s flying small [[freighter]]s such as the [[Corellian Engineering Corporation|Corellian]] [[G9 Rigger-class light freighter|G9 ''Rigger''-class light freighter]] could dock and deliver contraband. This contraband would then be uploaded by the port's staff, which usually included at least one [[Weequay]], and would subsequently be transferred to a [[Contraband Safehouse]] for storage.<ref name="Commander" />
