The '''Central Ridge Mining Company''' was a [[mining]] company based in the [[Otomok system]]. [[Hue Tico|Hue]] and [[Thanya Tico]] worked for Central Ridge Mining when their daughters were young. The [[Tico family]]'s eldest daughter, [[Paige Tico|Paige]], began flight training with the company when she turned thirteen.<ref name="RTRF" />
