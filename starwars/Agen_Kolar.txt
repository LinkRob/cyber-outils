'''Agen Kolar''' was a [[Force-sensitive]] [[Iridonian]] [[Zabrak]] [[Gender|male]] and was a [[Jedi Master]] in the era of the [[Galactic Republic]]. At his time in the order, he was considered to be a notable [[Lightsaber duel|duelist]], and sat on the [[Jedi High Council]]. He, [[Saesee Tiin]], and [[Kit Fisto]] were almost immediately [[Death|killed]] by [[Darth Sidious]] in a [[lightsaber]] [[Duel in Palpatine's office|duel]] when they confronted him for being the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. Kolar and Tiin, with Fisto were the first casualties of the [[Great Jedi Purge]] that [[Order 66|wiped out]] most of the members of the [[Jedi Order]].

==Biography==
===Early life===
Agen Kolar was [[Reproduction|born]] on [[Coruscant]] prior to the [[Separatist Crisis]]. When he was found to be [[Force-sensitive]], he was brought to the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] to train in the ways of [[the Force]], where he eventually rose up to the rank of [[Jedi Master]].<ref name="ECUC" /> He soon trained a [[Padawan]] named [[Tan Yuster]].<ref name="Lightsabers" />

===Geonosis===
[[File:Kolar-Geonosis.jpg|thumb|left|200px|Kolar during the First Battle of Geonosis]]
Kolar participated in the [[First Battle of Geonosis]], the conflict that sparked the [[Clone Wars]] between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems|Separatist Alliance]]. As a participant, he was a member of the [[Jedi assault team]] assembled by fellow [[Jedi High Council]] member [[Mace Windu]] to rescue [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]] and [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] from being [[Death|executed]] by the Separatists.<ref name="Attack of the Clones" /> During the battle, Kolar's [[Padawan]] [[Tan Yuster]] was overwhelmed by [[B2-series super battle droid]]s and [[Death|killed]]. The Jedi Master recovered the blue [[kyber crystal]] from his fallen apprentice's [[lightsaber]] and later added it to [[Agen Kolar's lightsaber|his own]] as a homage.<ref name="Lightsabers" />

===Yoda's vision===
In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{TCWcanondates|611}}</ref> [[Grand Master]] [[Yoda]] experienced a [[Force vision|vision]] of Kolar's death at the hands of [[Darth Sidious]] whilst on the [[planet]] [[Dagobah]] to learn the secrets of immortality.<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref>

===Joining the Council===
[[File:CouncilReport-Yoda8.png|thumb|Yoda and Anakin Skywalker reported to Kolar and the Council on Operation: Titan.]]
Later that year,<ref name="Yoda Date">The third arc of ''[[Star Wars: Yoda]]'' features [[Agen Kolar]] on the [[Jedi High Council]]. "[[A Seat on the Council]]" establishes that Kolar replaced [[Eeth Koth]], who served on the Council during [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|''The Clone Wars'': The Lost Missions]]. ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates the events of "[[The Wrong Jedi]]" to [[19 BBY]]. ''Galactic Atlas'' also dates the beginning of [[Great Jedi Purge]], which includes the events of "[[Victory and Death]]," to 19 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]]. {{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-clone-wars-chronological-episodeorder|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' Chronological Episode Order}} places ''The Lost Missions'' between "The Wrong Jedi" and "Victory and Death", dating the events of ''The Lost Missions'' to 19 BBY. Kolar was later seen on the Council during {{Film|III}}, the events of which ''Galactic Atlas'' also dates to 19 BBY, including the [[Operation: Knightfall|destruction]] of the [[Jedi Order]], limiting Kolar's tenure, and ''[[Star Wars: Yoda: Size Matters Not]]'', to that year.</ref> Kolar replaced fellow Zabrak [[Eeth Koth]] on the Jedi High Council after Koth was forced to step down.<ref name="Insider 199">{{InsiderCite|199|A Seat on the Council}}</ref> Kolar was present [[holocomm]] when the two reported to the Council that Separatist "[[megadroid]]s" were under construction on the planet [[Golatha]] and informed them that they planned to [[Mission to Golatha|destroy]] the [[Golatha factory|factory]] there before the megadroids could be deployed on the battlefield,<ref name="Yoda 8">[[Yoda 8|''Yoda'' 8]]</ref> a task that they were ultimately successful in completing.<ref name="Yoda 9">[[Yoda 9|''Yoda'' 9]]</ref> Kolar took part in many other important discussions such as when [[Supreme Chancellor]] Palpatine requested that Skywalker be appointed to the Council. He was also present when Palpatine reported that [[General]] [[Grievous]] was hiding on [[Utapau]] and suggested that they dispatch [[Jedi Knight]] Anakin Skywalker. The Council instead [[Battle of Utapau|dispatched]] Jedi Master [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]].<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

===Death===
[[File:AgenKolarDies-ROTS.jpg|thumb|280px|Agen Kolar is struck down by Darth Sidious.|left]]
During the last [[Standard day|days]] of the war, Kolar was part of another team assembled by Windu, this [[time]] to [[Duel in Palpatine's office|arrest]] Palpatine&mdash;who was secretly [[Darth]] Sidious, the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]]. Of the four Jedi Masters sent to [[Duel in Palpatine's office|arrest]] the Dark [[Lord]], Kolar and [[Saesee Tiin]] were near effortlessly killed and the first two to die in the [[Great Jedi Purge|Jedi Purge]]. The other two Masters, [[Kit Fisto]] and Windu, also perished. Afterwards, Sidious [[Proclamation of the New Order|transformed]] the Republic into the [[Galactic Empire]], had the [[Executive Separatist Council|Separatist Council]] [[Mission to Mustafar|slain]] and [[Order 66|destroyed]] the [[Jedi Order]].<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

==Personality and traits==
Agen Kolar's impulsive personality often led him to leap into battle without thinking<ref name="Pocket" /> and he was known to strike first and ask questions later. He was also considered a valuable member of the Jedi Council. Windu had a high opinion of Kolar's combat skills, which led him to recruit the Zabrak in the attempt to arrest Supreme Chancellor Palpatine.<ref name="ECUC" /> Sidious, however, easily killed Kolar and Tiin.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" /> He stood 1.9 [[meter]]s (6 foot 4 inches) tall.<ref name="Pocket" />

==Powers and abilities==
[[File:AgenKolar-Databank.png|thumb|180px|Jedi Master Agen Kolar]]
A master swordsman of the Jedi Order, Kolar's lightsaber skills were widely celebrated and renowned.<ref name="ECUC" /> Kolar was confident he could defeat anyone,<ref name="Pocket" /> but despite his reputation, Darth Sidious was able to slay the [[Zabrak]] Jedi with a single blow before doing the same to Tiin.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

==Equipment==
Kolar built his lightsaber in such a way that it used dual [[Kyber crystal|crystals]], which allowed the [[color]] of the lightsaber blade to ignite with either his green blade or his deceased student Yuster's blue blade.<ref name="ECUC" /> The latter's blade was used during Kolar's mission to help arrest [[Darth Sidious|Palpatine]], who quickly killed him before he could use it.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

==Behind the scenes==
Agen Kolar was originally intended to be Jedi Master Eeth Koth, who had been portrayed by [[Hassani Shapi]] in the [[1999]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']]. As the majority of [[Star Wars: Episode II Attack of the Clones|''Star Wars'': Episode II ''Attack of the Clones'']] was being shot in [[Fox Studios, Sydney]] [[Wikipedia:Australia|Australia]], local actor [[Tux Akindoyeni]] was brought in to replace the original actor. However, Akindoyeni appearance was deemed noticeably different from Shapi's version of the character and so a new character was created for Akindoyeni, which became known as Agen Kolar.<ref name="Old databank">{{DB|character|agenkolar|Kolar, Agen}}</ref> Akindoyeni later reprised his role in the [[2005]] film ''[[Star Wars: Episode III Revenge of the Sith]]''.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />

In January [[2013]], ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]''' supervising director [[Dave Filoni]] mentioned that they soon needed to introduce Agen Kolar as the series moved closer to ''Revenge of the Sith''.<ref name="Filoni RFR">{{WebCite|url=https://www.rebelforceradio.com/shows/2015/9/28/rebel-force-radio-january-19-2013-premiere|text=Rebel Force Radio: January 19, 2013 (Premiere)|work=''[[Rebel Force Radio]]''|archivedate=20230828084324}} {{C|46:15}}</ref> However, Eeeth Koth later still appeared in [[The Clone Wars: The Lost Missions|the sixth season]] of the series, released in [[2014]],<ref name="Conspiracy">{{TCW|Conspiracy}}</ref> and the unfinished episode "[[The Big Bang]]".<ref name="BB">{{TCW|The Big Bang}}</ref> The model for Koth was also used in the Season Six episode "[[Voices]]" during a [[Force vision]] to Yoda at the [[Cave of Evil]] on [[Dagobah]] to depict<ref name="Voices">{{TCW|Voices}}</ref> the death of Agen Kolar from ''Revenge of the Sith''.<ref name="Revenge of the Sith" />
