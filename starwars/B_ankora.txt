The '''B'ankora''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] of [[ruminant]]s in [[the galaxy]]. Their [[B'ankora homeworld|homeworld]] suffered a [[B'ankora homeworld cataclysm|cataclysmic collision]] with a [[meteor]]<ref name="Rebel Dossier" /> at some point before around [[1300 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> which led the [[Galactic Republic]] to give them a small sanctuary, that eventually became the [[B'ankor Refuge]], on their [[capital]] [[planet]] [[Coruscant]]. [[Chasen Piian]], a [[Supreme Chancellor]] at the time of the Republic granted the land in perpetuity to the surviving members of the species so they could resume their sedentary lifestyle with humble homes, fields, and trails.<ref name="Catalyst" />

The B'ankora continued to live on the land for over fifty generations. After the [[Proclamation of the New Order|rise]] of the [[Galactic Empire]], their Coruscant allotment was seized after an Imperial edict citing eminent domain was enacted and an [[Project Celestial Power complex|Imperial energy research center]] affiliated with [[Project Celestial Power]] was build on their former lands. The entire species, around ten thousand individuals, was [[Relocation of the B'ankora|relocated]] to [[Parau VI]], a dying world located in the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]] which featured similar characteristics to those of the B'ankor Refuge.<ref name="Catalyst" /> Not more than nineteen years later, the B'ankora had become extinct as a result of the Empire's actions.<ref name="Rebel Dossier" />

==Behind the scenes==
The B'ankora first appeared in ''[[Catalyst: A Rogue One Novel]]'', written by [[James Luceno]]<ref name="Catalyst" /> and published on [[November 15]], [[2016]]<ref name="Penguin-Hardcover">{{PenguinRandomHouse|url=books/7879/catalyst-star-wars-by-james-luceno/9780345511492|text=Catalyst (Star Wars)}}</ref> as a prequel to the film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Catalyst" />

The 2016 reference book ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'', written by [[Jason Fry]], originally dated the species being granted the B'ankor Refuge to [[131 BBY]],<ref name="Rebel Dossier date">According to ''[[Star Wars: Rogue One: Rebel Dossier]]'' the B'ankora were granted the [[B'ankor Refuge]] 112 [[Standard year|years]] before the end of [[Proclamation of the New Order|the end]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], which ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates to [[19 BBY]]. As such it could be deduced that the B'ankor Refuge was established in [[131 BBY]].</ref> but in ''Catalyst'' it is stated that they had lived in the Refuge "for more than fifty generations" and had planted a copse of gargantuan [[tree]]s "centuries earlier" by the time of the novel.<ref name="Catalyst" /> [[2023]]'s ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' later instead placed the event to [[1300 BBY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> Because ''Timelines'' is the newer source and it's date lines up better with what is established in ''Catalyst'', this article assumes it to be correct.
