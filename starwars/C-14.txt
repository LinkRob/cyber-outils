A '''C-14''' was a type of [[droid]]. Shortly after losing his [[R2-series astromech droid]], [[R2-D2]], during the [[Clone Wars]]' [[Battle of Bothawui]]<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" /> in the [[Standard year|year]] [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and his [[Padawan]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], returned to the [[Fervse'dra asteroid belt|site of the battle]] in search of the missing droid. The two [[Jedi]] and Skywalker's replacement [[Astromech droid|astromech]], [[R3-S6]], boarded the [[Vulture's Claw|freighter]] of the [[Trandoshan]] [[scavenger]] [[Gha Nachkt]], who was combing through debris from the battle. Nachkt, eyeing Skywalker's [[R3-series astromech droid|R3 unit]], offered to trade them a C-14 in exchange for the astromech; however, Tano refused.<ref name="Downfall of a Droid" />
