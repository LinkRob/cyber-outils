The '''Cloud Paradise''' was a tall residential building constructed from black [[Granite/Legends|granite]] which was located on [[Coruscant/Legends|Coruscant]]. In [[44 ABY]], [[Wraith Squadron]] leader [[Garik Loran|Face Loran]] visited [[Zehrinne Thaal]] in her [[Zehrinne Thaal's apartment|apartment]] on the building to discuss her [[Marriage/Legends|husband]] [[General/Legends|General]] [[Stavin Thaal]].<ref name="MK">''[[X-Wing: Mercy Kill]]''</ref>
