'''Daniel Ross''' is a [[Wikipedia:Voice acting|voice actor]]. He voiced the [[Unidentified Inquisitor (Asajj Ventress)|Inquisitor]] in "[[A Way Forward]],"<ref name="Way">{{TOTU|A Way Forward}}</ref> the first episode of ''[[Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld]]'', the third installment of the ''[[Star Wars Tales (television series)|Star Wars Tales]]'' anthology series.<ref name="Article">{{SW|url=news/tales-of-the-underworld-announce|text=Two Favorite ''Star Wars'' Bounty Hunters are Back for ''Tales of the Underworld''|date=2025-04-02}}</ref> The episode aired [[May 2]], [[2025]] in the [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Fortnite]]''{{'s}} "''Star Wars'' Watch Party" event, two days prior to the full series' [[May 4]] premiere on [[Disney+]].<ref name="Fortnite">{{FortniteWeb|url=news/get-a-new-stormtrooper-in-fortnite-and-watch-two-episodes-of-star-wars-tales-of-the-underworld|text=Get a New Stormtrooper in Fortnite & Watch Two Episodes of Star Wars: Tales of the Underworld!|date=2025-04-29}}</ref> On [[May 5]], Ross expressed joy about joining the ''[[Star Wars]]'' universe and thanked<ref name="Facebook" /> series caster<ref name="Way" /> [[Lindsay Halper]],<ref name="Facebook" /> executive producers<ref name="Article" /> [[Athena Yvette Portillo]] and [[Dave Filoni]], and his team at CESD Talent Agency.<ref name="Facebook" />
