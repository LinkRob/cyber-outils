The '''attack on Jabiim''', or the '''Jabiim incident''', was an attack conducted by the [[Galactic Empire]] on the base of the [[Hidden Path]] on the [[planet]] [[Jabiim]]. The assault was led by [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] from [[orbit]] aboard the [[Imperial I-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial I''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Devastator]]'', while the ground assault was commanded by the newly-promoted [[Grand Inquisitor]], the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]]. In truth, she intended to betray Vader to avenge her class of [[Jedi Initiate]]s, whom Vader had slain [[Standard year|years]] earlier during the [[Operation: Knightfall|siege of the Jedi Temple]], but the [[Sith Lord]] was well aware of her planned treachery. The [[The Grand Inquisitor|real Grand Inquisitor]] was alive and with Vader, waiting for a chance to reveal himself to the Third Sister.

The assault occurred as part of the Imperial [[Hunt for the Hidden Path|hunt for the Path]], but also the [[Hunt for Obi-Wan|hunt]] for surviving [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. With the sacrifice of several Path members, including Kenobi's new close ally [[Tala Durith]], Kenobi and the Path were able to escape after Vader landed. The Third Sister took that as her moment to strike, but she was easily struck down and left for dead by Vader and the Grand Inquisitor. Vader then commanded the ''Devastator'' to chase down the Path's [[Unidentified freight transport|escape ship]], but he focused solely on Kenobi's presence aboard instead of the wider rebel group.

==The attack==
===Imperial assault===
{{Quote|My lord, we're approaching Jabiim.''"<br />"''Lock down the facility.''"<br />"''If we seal them in now, they can hold out for days. If we cannot break them&mdash;''"<br />"''It is not them we need to break.|The Third Sister and Darth Vader as they arrive in the Jabiim system|<ref name="Part V" />}}
[[File:IntoTheBreach-OWKpV.png|thumb|left|250px|Stormtroopers charge into the Path base.]]
Led to the planet via a [[Homing beacon|tracking device]] planted on [[Price|Princess]] of [[Alderaan]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa's]] [[toy droid]] [[L0-LA59]], the ground assault&mdash;led by the [[Reva Sevander|Third Sister]] after the [[Sith Lord]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] promoted her to be the [[Grand Inquisitor]]&mdash;had the main objective of hunting down and capturing [[Jedi Master]] [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], and a secondary objective of capturing or eliminating all present members of the Path. As the Third Sister commanded the ground attack of multiple [[stormtrooper]] [[squad]]s, Vader remained aboard the ''[[Devastator]]'' in [[orbit]].<ref name="Part V" />

During the assault, the Path was trapped in the base by L0-LA59, who was being controlled by the Galactic Empire via a [[restraining bolt]]. While fortifying and preparing to evacuate, Kenobi stalled for time by attempting to convince the Third Sister to help him defeat Vader, then surrendering himself. Kenobi later escaped and boarded a [[JT-731 Broadhorn transport]] alongside the other members of the Path, while [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] [[Telekinesis|Force pulled]] [[Kawlan Roken's freight transport|another transport]].<ref name="Part V" />

===Duel with Reva===
{{Quote|Hello Third Sister. Revenge does wonders for the will to live, don't you think? Your rage was useful. Now it is tiresome. We will leave you where we found you. In the gutter, where you belong. Goodbye&hellip; Grand Inquisitor.|The Grand Inquisitor reveals his survival to Reva|<ref name="Part V" />}}
[[File:ObiWanKenobi5-variantart-Lashley.jpg|thumb|right|300px|The Third Sister proved no match for Darth Vader.]]
The Third Sister then attempted to assassinate Vader, only for him to anticipate her strike and engage her in a "[[lightsaber duel]]," during which Vader effortlessly deflected her attacks without even igniting his own [[Darth Vader's lightsaber|lightsaber]]. Eventually, Vader would claim half of the Third Sister's lightsaber and used it to overwhelm her, gravely wounding her with the same stabbing attack he had used against her as a [[Jedi Initiate]]. Revealing all along he had known she was trying to overthrow him to avenge his attack on the Jedi in her youth, Vader was soon joined by [[the Grand Inquisitor]], who reclaimed his title and goodbye to the Third Sister as someone who was no longer of use to the Empire.<ref name="Part V" />

After Vader, the Grand Inquisitor, and the remaining troopers departed, she then eyed Kenobi's holoprojector and intercepted [[Viceroy]] [[Bail Prestor Organa]]'s message, in which he discussed [[Luke Skywalker]],<ref name="Part V" /> boy living on the Outer Rim planet [[Tatooine]] who was, in secret, Vader's own son.<ref name="CompEncyc">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> Armed with the knowledge that the boy was of some importance, the Third Sister would depart from Jabiim and made her way to Tatooine, intending to murder Skywalker as a new way to enact her revenge.<ref name="Part VI" />

==Aftermath==
{{Quote|We cannot prioritize one lone Jedi.''"<br />"''He is not just any Jedi. Follow Kenobi.|The Grand Inquisitor and Darth Vader as they pursue Kenobi|<ref name="Part VI" />}}
[[File:Devastator_pursues_Obi-Wan.png|thumb|left|300px|The ''Devastator'' pursues Kenobi in his escape craft.]]
Aboard Roken's ship, Kenobi and Leia sat among the refugees. Roken told them that the ship's hyperdrive was not working and that the Empire was still pursuing them. Kenobi realized his holoprojector was missing but told Roken that nothing was wrong.<ref name="Part V" /> The ''Devastator'' chased after the ship and fired upon it until Kenobi, realizing that Vader would prioritize his hunt for him instead of going after the Path, fled from the freighter aboard an escape craft, escaping to a [[Unidentified barren moon|barren moon]] that Vader followed him to.<ref name="Part VI" /> Following the attack on Jabiim's Path safe house and a subsequent<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion">''[[Star Wars: Dawn of Rebellion: The Visual Guide]]''</ref> [[Raid on the Archive|raid]] on [[The Archive]], a Path base on the [[moon]] [[Jedha]],<ref name="Survivor">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> [[Jedi Knight]] [[Cal Kestis]] and [[Nightsisters|Nightsister]] [[Merrin]] prepared to use the hidden planet [[Tanalorr]] as a new home for the Path.<ref name="Dawn of Rebellion" />
