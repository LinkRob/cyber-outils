The '''Coruscant Skyways Authority''' or '''CSA''' was a group that regulated the skyways of [[Coruscant]]. At some point, they sent a Charge Payment Order to [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], as his [[Obi-Wan Kenobi's Eta-2 Actis-class interceptor|Eta-2 ''Actis''-class light interceptor]] did not display identification. After Kenobi sent a response letter, they told him that they didn't need to see his identification, and he could move along. This notice was collected by [[Poe Dameron]] in a [[Unidentified book|vehicle guidebook]].<ref name="Vehicles" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Coruscant Skyways Authority was mentioned in the [[2022]] non-[[canon]] reference book ''[[LEGO Star Wars Awesome Vehicles]]''.<ref name="Vehicles" />
