"'''Static'''", known by their title "'''Omega Ace'''," was the nickname of a [[human]]<ref name="ECUC" /> [[First Order]] [[ace]] [[First Order TIE fighter pilot|pilot]] from [[Omega Squadron]], a [[squadron]] of [[TIE/fo space superiority fighter]]s. They were a part of the [[First Order military|military]] of the [[First Order]]. At one point, Omega Ace proceeded to enter a skirmish with a holdout, with their blaster fire hitting an encampment.<ref name="FFG" />

==Behind the scenes==
Static is a character introduced in the X-Wing Miniatures series. Static's gender is debatable: Although the article I am Their Father Part 2 identifies Static as male,<ref name="FFG Site">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2017/6/14/x-wing-101-i-am-their-father-part-2|text=X-Wing™ 101: I Am Their Father, Part Two}}</ref> the Advanced Terror article covering the TIE/fo Fighter Expansion Pack for the second edition referred to Static as female.<ref name="Advanced Terror">{{FantasyFlightGames|url=en/news/2018/11/7/advanced-terror|text=Advanced Terror&ndash;Preview the TIE/fo Fighter Expansion Pack for X-Wing}}</ref>
