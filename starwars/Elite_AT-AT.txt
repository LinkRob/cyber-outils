The '''Elite AT-AT''' was a modified variant of the [[All Terrain Armored Transport]] and crewed by an elite crew who were among the best [[AT-AT pilot|pilots]] in the [[Galactic Empire]].

==Characteristics==
The Elite AT-AT was modified from the base model of [[All Terrain Armored Transport]],<ref name="SWC" /> that was manufactured by [[Kuat Drive Yards]]<ref name="Complete Vehicles">''[[Star Wars Complete Vehicles, New Edition]]''</ref> and as such, was equipped with thick [[Color|black]] [[armor]] and more powerful [[laser cannon]]s. The vehicle had built-in saws on its legs, making it difficult to be taken down by [[Magnetic harpoon|magnetic harpoon tow cables]], but were susceptible to ion explosives. The Elite AT-AT's design was modified design of stronger firepower and defenses were modified by the Empire for various uses. The [[walker]]'s viewport was colored red.<ref name="SWC" />

==History==
The Elite AT-AT was deployed by the [[Imperial Army]] during the [[Galactic Civil War]], participating in [[Operations on Tatooine|operations]] on [[Tatooine]] under the [[68th Legion]], the [[Dandoran Campaign]], the [[Er'Kit Campaign]], a [[campaign on Yavin IV]], and other conflicts shortly after the [[Battle of Yavin]].<ref name="SWC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Elite AT-AT debuted in [[canon]] in the [[2014]] mobile [[video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Commander]]''.<ref name="SWC" /> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the Elite AT-AT is known as the [[Shadow AT-AT]], was created for [[Hasbro]]'s [[2007]] ''[[Star Wars: Titanium Series]]'' line of die-cast figurine vehicles.<ref name="Titanium Series">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: Titanium Series|pack=Shadow AT-AT|altlink=https://www.rebelscum.com/SW07TSatat.asp}}</ref>
