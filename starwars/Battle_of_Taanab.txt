The '''Battle of Taanab''' was a famous battle<ref name="Geektionary">''[[Star Wars: Geektionary: The Galaxy from A - Z]]''</ref> that took place in [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Date" /> between [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] and a [[pirate]] [[fleet]] in [[orbit]] of the [[planet]] [[Taanab]]. It was won by a daring and successful maneuver pulled by Calrissian. Shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]], Calrissian mentioned the battle to fellow [[smuggler]] [[Han Solo]], and decided his promotion to [[General]] in the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet]] had been due to his success at the battle.<ref name="Episode VI" />
