'''Achra Station cantina''' was a [[Cantina|drinking and dining establishment]] located on the [[Achra Station]] [[space station]] in [[orbit]] around the [[planet]] [[Akiva]]. The cantina consisted on a square room with booths around the edge and a circular bar in the center. The [[Rodian]] [[contract broker]] [[Andava]] operated from the cantina<ref name="Outlaws" /> in [[3 ABY]].<ref name="Outlaws Date">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> In the same [[Standard year|year]], the [[pirate]] [[Hondo Ohnaka]] visited the cantina after he and [[Kay Vess]] escaped from the [[Rokana Raiders]]. There Ohnaka fested while planning his and Vess's return to the [[Khepi Tomb]] the raiders were using as a base in order to recover the treasure of the Khepi.<ref name="Outlaws" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Achra Station cantina appeared in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'', developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]].<ref name="Outlaws" />
