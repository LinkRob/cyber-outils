'''BB-00''', nicknamed '''Boo''', was a [[BB-series astromech droid|BB-series]] [[astromech droid]] that belonged to the [[cartographer]] [[Emil Graf]]. The droid helped keep their [[scout ship]] ''[[Star Herald]]'' operational.<ref name="Stop, Thief" />

{{Droid-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
[[File:Emil_Graf_BB-00_concept_art.jpg|thumb|left|210x210px|[[Jon Sommariva]]'s concept art of Emil Graf and BB-00.]]
BB-00 first appeared in the ''Tales of Wild Space'' back-up story of [[Star Wars Adventures (2017) 1|''Star Wars Adventures'' (2017) 1]], "[[Tales from Wild Space: Stop, Thief!|Stop, Thief!]]," written by [[Cavan Scott]] with art by [[Jon Sommariva]], [[Sean Parsons]], and [[Charlie Kirchoff]].

Scott named BB-00 after his youngest daughter, who also goes by the nickname Boo.<ref name="Name">{{WebCite|url=http://cavanscott.com/2017/09/19/naming-bb-00-for-star-wars-adventures|text=Naming BB-00 for Star Wars Adventures|author=[[Cavan Scott]]|date=2017-09-19|archivedate=20190315161442}}</ref>
