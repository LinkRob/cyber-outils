'''Beszilov''' was a continent, located in the south-eastern hemisphere of [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Garnib]], the [[Balinaka]] [[Homeworld/Legends|homeworld]]. It was the location of the [[City/Legends|cities]] of [[Feszum]] and [[Tettilov]].
