'''Cols''' was an astronomical object<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> located in the [[Cols system]] within the [[Kastolar sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]].<ref name="The Slice" /> It was situated in grid square Q-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> At one point, Cols was under the control of the [[Galactic Republic]].<ref name="The High Republic 1" />

By [[228 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{THRContent228BBY|THR1}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], however, Cols had come under the control of the [[Hutt Clan]].<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> Republic [[Commander]] [[Velko Jahen]] notified [[Jedi Master]] [[Elzar Mann]] of the clan's recent losses and told him that the [[Republic Defense Coalition]] expected to retake Cols within a matter of [[Standard week|weeks]]. In response, Mann informed Jahen they could possibly no longer have that amount of [[time]] due to the potential of an alliance being formed by the Hutt Clan and the [[Nihil]] [[Pirate|marauders]].<ref name="The High Republic 1" />

==Behind the scenes==
Cols was first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[The High Republic (2023) 1|first issue]] of the [[2023]] [[Star Wars comic books|comic]] series [[Star Wars: The High Republic (2023)|''Star Wars: The High Republic'']], released as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="The High Republic 1" /> [[Phase III: Trials of the Jedi|Phase III]]. The issue was written by [[Cavan Scott]] and published by [[Marvel Comics]] on [[November 8]], 2023.<ref name="Marvel">{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/110346/star_wars_the_high_republic_phase_iii_2023_1|text=Star Wars: The High Republic &lsqb;Phase III&rsqb; (2023) #1}}</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, the [[Planet/Legends|planet]] [[Cols/Legends|Cols]] was first mentioned in ''[[Creatures of the Galaxy]]'', a [[1994]] supplement for [[West End Games]]' ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''. The section of the book mentioning the planet was authored by [[Bill Smith]].<ref name="Creatures of the Galaxy">''[[Creatures of the Galaxy]]''</ref>
