"'''Aftershock / Feather Frenzy'''" is the twentieth episode of the [[Young Jedi Adventures Season One|first season]] of the animated series ''[[Star Wars: Young Jedi Adventures]]''. It first aired on [[Disney+]] on [[February 14]], [[2024]],<ref name="More Episode 2024" /> and later on [[Disney Junior]] on [[March 15]], 2024.<ref name="Futon" />

==Official description==
''The Jedi help a prince serve his people. Large creatures take over the landing pad at Nash's garage.''
