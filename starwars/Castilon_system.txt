The '''Castilon system''' was a [[star system]]<ref name="USWNE">''[[Ultimate Star Wars, New Edition]]''</ref> in [[the galaxy]]'s [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> It contained the [[ocean]] [[planet]] [[Castilon]],<ref name="The Recruit" /> as well as an [[Castilon asteroid field|asteroid field]].<ref name="Signal from Sector Six" /> It was a ways out in the Outer Rim, and considered to be on the edge of [[Wild Space]].<ref name="The Recruit" />
