A [[Gender|male]] individual was the '''father''' of the [[human]] [[scientist]] [[Avon Sunvale|Avon Starros]]. Prior to Avon being sent to the outpost [[Port Haileap]] on the [[planet]] [[Haileap]] by her [[Parent|mother]]&mdash;the [[Galactic Republic]] [[senator]] [[Ghirra Starros]]<ref name="A Test of Courage" />&mdash;by [[232 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'' to [[232 BBY]].</ref> Avon's father left the [[Starros Clan|family]] to chase a [[dream]] of [[map]]ping [[hyperspace route]]s and to live on the edges of [[Wild Space]]. Afterward, Ghirra did not say anything except to tell her daughter that her father would not be joining them for dinner ever again.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

That [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="Date" /> while on the [[moon]] [[Wevo]], Avon was asked by the [[Dalnan]] [[Honesty Weft]] why she had been sent to Port Haileap. The question prompted Avon to mentally recall that she been told by an [[Unidentified emotional-support droid|emotional-support droid]] to talk about her experience of being [[Kidnapping of Avon Starros|kidnapped]]&mdash;the reason why she had been sent to Haileap&mdash;though also noted it conflicted with the way her family lived their lives, thinking back to when her father left and her mother's reaction.<ref name="A Test of Courage" />

==Behind the scenes==
Avon Starros' father was mentioned in the [[2021]] junior novel ''[[The High Republic: A Test of Courage]]'', written by [[Justina Ireland]] and published as part of<ref name="A Test of Courage" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]] of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="High Republic Reader's guide">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref>
