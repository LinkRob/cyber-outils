The '''Black Hole Gang''' was a crew of [[pirate]]s that operated out of the [[Delphon system]] and terrorized the shipping lanes of the [[Anoat sector]]. Originally founded and led by the [[Corellian]] [[Hethra Mcgrrrr]] as the '''Mcgrrrr Gang''', the crew frequently changed leadership due to violent disagreements between its members. Whenever the gang fell on hard times, however, ex-captain Mcgrrrr reappeared to help reorganize the crew before returning to semiretirement.<ref name="Collapse" />

At one point during the height of the [[Galactic Empire]], the Black Hole Gang was led by a [[Pantoran]] named [[Cru Devilar]], who employed a number of [[Trandoshan]] goons. [[Cage (Mikkian)|Cage]], a [[Mikkian]] tech-monger from [[Delphon City]], had ties to the gang.<ref name="ASM">''[[Choose Your Destiny: A Smuggler Mission]]''</ref>

During the Empire's [[Iron Blockade]], whenever the criminal [[Trade Spine League]] wanted to move a shipment of [[crystal]]s quietly from the [[planet]] [[Burnin Konn]] to the planet [[Delphon]], they had to first deal with the Black Hole Gang.<ref name="CC" />
