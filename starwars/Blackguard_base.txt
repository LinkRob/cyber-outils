A '''base''' on the [[planet]] [[Pijal]] was used by the [[blackguard]]s, [[Treason|traitorous]] members of the [[Royal guard (Pijal)|Pijal royal guard]] who planned to follow [[Princess]] [[Fanry]] in revolting against the [[Czerka Arms|Czerka Corporation]] during her [[Coronation of Queen Fanry|coronation]]. The base, constructed from a renovated [[Celestial Watchtower]], was discovered by the [[Opposition]], a political group who also opposed Czerka, who reported its existence to [[Regent|Lord Regent]] [[Rael Averross]], along with the [[Jedi]] [[Qui-Gon Jinn]] and [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]]. The Jedi chose to investigate the base, accompanied by Aveross and [[Captain]] [[Deren]] of the royal guard, who, as the leader of the blackguards, warned the outpost. When the Jedi arrived, they found the base hurriedly abandoned, though they managed to uncover communication records that led them to guess Deren's treachery.<ref name="MA" />
