'''Charnoqs''' were a [[species]] native to the [[planet]] [[Maridun]]. They were [[Hunting|hunted]] by the [[Amani]]n, a [[Sentience|sentient]] species from the same planet.<ref name="Mustafar and Other Planets" />

==Behind the scenes==
Charnoqs originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, and were first mentioned in ''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]'', a [[roleplaying game]] supplement for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]'', published by [[West End Games]] in [[1995]].<ref name="GG12">''[[Galaxy Guide 12: Aliens — Enemies and Allies]]''</ref> They were first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in "[[Mustafar and Other Planets in the Outer Rim]],"<ref name="Mustafar and Other Planets" /> a [[2022]] booklet in a series of reference booklets titled the [[Star Wars Encyclopedia (series)|''Star Wars Encyclopedia'']].<ref name="Altaya">{{Altaya|lang=fr|url=livres-bd/encyclopedie-star-wars|text=Official Star Wars Encyclopedia}}</ref>
