'''''Clone Wars Quarterly''''' was a periodical publication which had an interview with [[Imperial Army]] [[Colonel]] [[Toln Yurak]] regarding the [[Clone Wars reenactment movement]] during the [[Imperial Era]]. Colonel Yurak spoke with the publication about their belief that such reenactments provided young individuals with the skills and training for military service. ''Clone Wars Quarterly'' was used as a reference source in [[historian]] [[Beaumont Kin]]'s ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]''.<ref name="Rise and Fall" />
