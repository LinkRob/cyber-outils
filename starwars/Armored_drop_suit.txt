The '''armored drop suit''' was a type of light body armor constructed from fabric and [[laminate]]. The suits were built around a [[repulsorlift]] unit that allowed the wearer to fly. Because of their material composition, the suits were able to minimize air resistance and improve the wearer's maneuverability while also providing protection against attack. The suits sold for 7,500 [[Galactic Credit Standard|credits]].<ref name="Gadgets and Gear" />

==Behind the scenes==
Armored drop suits were first mentioned in [[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']], a [[Roleplaying game|roleplaying]] supplement for the ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' series that was published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]] in [[2017]].<ref name="No Disintegrations">[[No Disintegrations (Fantasy Flight Games)|''No Disintegrations'']]</ref>
