'''Deputy''' was a rank or title held by some [[Law enforcement agency|law enforcement]] officers, inferior in rank to a [[constable]]<ref name="High Noon" /> or [[marshal]]. [[Scott]] served as the deputy of [[Freetown]] on [[Tatooine]] under Marshal [[Cobb Vanth]] prior to his [[death]] at the hands of [[bounty hunter]] [[Cad Bane]]<ref name="From the Desert">{{BOBF|Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger}}</ref> circa [[9 ABY]].<ref name="BOBF Date">{{BoBFDate}} Therefore, Scott's death must have also taken place around that year.</ref> The [[Kyuzo]]s [[Drego]] and [[Streehn]] were the deputies under Constable [[Zuvio]] of [[Niima Outpost]], a frontier trading post located on the [[planet]] [[Jakku]].<ref name="High Noon" />
