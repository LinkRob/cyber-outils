The '''''Cargo Sky''''' was a [[Wayfarer-class medium freighter|''Wayfarer''-class medium transport]] that was part of [[Senator]] [[Bail Prestor Organa|Bail Organa's]] resistance movement During the [[Blockade of Ibaar|blockade]] of the [[planet]] [[Ibaar]], Organa ordered the ''Cargo Sky'' and the ''[[Cargo Comet]]'' to be loaded with supplies and military equipment and to deliver it to the [[Ibaar rebel network|rebel network]] operating there.<ref name="Dawn" />

While Bail Organa's adoptive daughter, [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]], oversaw the ''Cargo Comet'' and the ''Cargo Sky'' being loaded with the cargo in [[Sanctuary Coast Starport]] on [[Alderaan]], six [[agent]]s from the [[Imperial Security Bureau]] and [[Imperial Office of Customs]] arrived at the landing bay to inspect the shipment, and subsequently ordered licenses and manifests for review.<ref name="Dawn" />
