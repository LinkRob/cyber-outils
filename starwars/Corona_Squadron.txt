'''Corona Squadron''' was a [[starfighter]] [[squadron]] that served the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic]] and later the [[New Republic]]. It was designated for reconnaissance duty but also served in the [[Battle of Hoth]] and the [[Battle of Endor]]. The squadron flew [[X-wing starfighter]]s.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

==History==
During the early [[Standard year|years]] of the [[Galactic Civil War]], Corona [[Squadron]] included [[the Contessa]] ([[Corona Leader]]), [[Yendor Brethen|Yendor]] ([[Corona Two]]), [[Smikes]] ([[Corona Three]]), [[Thane Kyrell]] ([[Corona Four]]), and [[Kendy Idele]] ([[Corona Five]]). They were a ragtag group that came together from across [[the galaxy]] with vastly different backgrounds.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

===Rebel Alliance===
====Battle of Hoth====
{{Quote|Haul your Jelucani ass up here! We've got multiple Imperial walkers marching in.|Yendor to Kyrell|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
[[File:Thane_Kyrell-SWGA.jpg|thumb|left|250px|Thane Kyrell pilots a snowspeeder]]
In [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> Corona Squadron was stationed on the [[Water|ice]] [[planet]] of [[Hoth]] when the [[Galactic Empire]] discovered the location of the hidden [[Echo Base|rebel base]] there. When the [[Imperial Navy|Imperial fleet]] arrived on Hoth sooner than was expected, the job of Corona Squadron was to delay the oncoming [[Imperial Army|army]] until the rebel transports could [[Battle of Hoth|evacuate]] the base. Two members of Corona Squadron, Thane Kyrell and Yendor, piloted a [[T-47 airspeeder|snowspeeder]] together and managed to take down an Imperial [[All Terrain Armored Transport|AT-AT]] on their own by shooting at its joints, where walkers were most vulnerable. Once the transports had taken off, the squadron took their [[X-wing starfighter|X-wings]] into space, where they rendezvoused with the rest of the rebel fleet.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====Rendezvouz on ''Liberty''====
After the Battle of Hoth, they were personally thanked for their service by [[Mon Mothma]], the leader of the Rebel Alliance. She then gave them a special mission &mdash; to do reconnaissance for the Rebel Alliance &mdash; in which they were to be based on the [[MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruiser|MC80 ''Liberty'' Type Heavy Star Cruiser]] ''[[Liberty]]''. In the coming months, they participated in many intelligence missions.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====Scouting D'Qar====
{{Quote|We should list this planet as a potential base in future. The Empire's not interested, not much space lane traffic in this area, and there's plenty of water.|Thane Kyrell, to his squadron|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
Corona Squadron took part in a scouting mission to the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] planet of [[D'Qar]] for any signs of an Imperial outpost. The Alliance had received reports from deep-cover agents on [[Coruscant]] that the massive amounts of material was being processed for the Imperial fleet, including rumors of a new large-ship construction facility, but nobody knew for certain. While the Squadron found no sign of artificial power sources or Imperial activity, they identified D'Qar as a potential rebel base for the future. With nothing further, the squadron returned to the ''Liberty'' for maintenance on their X-wings.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====New base on 5251977====
Some time later, Corona Squadron relocated to the [[Rebel Alliance Navy|Alliance Fleet's]] new base, a small uninhabited no-name planet, designation [[5251977]]. The Alliance had built a much larger hangar than usual, to accommodate a fleet that was double the size it was two months previous.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====Skirmish in the Hudalla system====
{{Quote|All we have now is the element of surprise. Let's take the fight to them.|Corona Leader to Corona Squadron|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
[[File:HudallaSyst.jpg|thumb|right|250px|Scouting mission to the Hudalla system.]]
Ten hours after reaching 5251977, the squadron were informed of a large gathering of ships in the [[Hudalla system]], and were sent on an intelligence mission to investigate. While there, they found a large fleet of Imperial ships and learned of the construction of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]]. Despite hiding in the rings of the [[planet]] in low power mode, they were detected by the Imperial fleet and a squadron of four [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] was sent to root them out. In the [[Dogfight in the Hudalla system|battle]], Smikes (Corona Three) was shot down before the X-wings could escape.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====Battle of Endor====
{{Quote|I'm going in close on the engines, who's with me?"<br />"Corona Four, right behind you."<br />"Corona Five, too. Let's do it!|Corona Leader to Corona Squadron|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
[[File:Yendor-SWZ99.png|thumb|left|220px|Corona Squadron flying near a Mon Calamari cruiser during the Battle of Endor.]]
In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="Galactic Atlas" /> after the discovery of the [[DS-2 Death Star II Mobile Battle Station|second Death Star]], Corona Squadron was assigned to cover the starfighters of the [[Gold Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gold]], [[Red Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Red]], [[Green Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Green]], and [[Gray Squadron (Rebel Alliance)|Gray]] squadrons, as they penetrated the Death Star. Due to [[Endor]]'s shields being up when they pulled out of hyperspace, they were forced to face off with a large number of TIE fighters and [[Imperial-class Star Destroyer|Imperial Star Destroyers]]. On orders from [[Gold Leader]] in the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]'', they moved in close to the Star Destroyers, working as a team and successfully disabling the engines of the [[Subjugator (Star Destroyer)|''Subjugator'']]. When the shields surrounding the second Death Star fell, they protected the other squadrons, as they made their entry into the Death Star. By the conclusion of the battle, the squadron had suffered no casualties, however they did lose their headquarters, the ''Liberty'', which was destroyed in an instant from a single blast of the Death Star's [[superlaser]].<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====Celebrations after Endor====
{{Quote|&hellip;Corona Squadron? Anyone? Has anyone seen&hellip;|A rebel after the Battle of Endor|<ref name="Shattered Empire">[[Shattered Empire 1|''Shattered Empire'' 1]]</ref>}}
After surviving starfighters landed on ''[[Home One]]'', a rebel was asking about the squadron's status.<ref name="Shattered Empire" /> Members of the squadron, including Kyrell and Idele, participated in the celebrations on the forest moon of Endor.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

===New Republic===
====Naboo invasions====
{{Quote|Come on&hellip;You've taken worse than that.''"<br />"''Yeah, and I'm sick and tired of it!|Kyrell and Idele, about her damaged X-wing after a battle with TIE fighters over Naboo|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
Corona Squadron continued to participate in a full-out war almost a year after the Battle of Endor, now serving the New Republic, but their composition had changed. The Contessa had left the squadron to run for presidency on her [[homeworld]], resulting in Yendor becoming the new Corona Leader. They also acquired two new members, an exile from [[Coruscant]] and the other a rookie from [[Nea Dajanam]]. As the Empire sought to regain control of [[Naboo]], the squadron [[Invasions of Naboo|fought]] on the planet in several separate battles.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

====Battle of Jakku====
{{Quote|We need people like you and Lieutenant Idele who have served on Imperial ships in the past, it's this simple &ndash; we need more vessels, and we need them faster than they can be built, especially while the Empire still holds most of the main construction facilities. The only way we're going to get those ships is by capturing them from the Empire.|General Rieekan, briefs Kyrell and Idele on their orders for Jakku|<ref name="Lost Stars" />}}
Shortly after, Corona Squadron participated in the [[Battle of Jakku]]. Due to their experience as ex-Imperials and knowledge of ship design and procedures, General [[Carlist Rieekan]] tasked Kyrell,<ref name="Lost Stars" /> recently promoted to commander,<ref name="OntFL">''[[Star Wars: On the Front Lines]]''</ref> and Lieutenant Idele, to lead a boarding party to capture the Imperial Star Destroyer ''[[Inflictor]]''. The boarding party disabled the ship's engines and [[Self-destruct mechanism|self-destruct system]], and so the Star Destroyer's captain &mdash; Kyrell's childhood friend and lover [[Ciena Ree]] &mdash; ordered her crew to abandon ship while she remained on board to scuttle it on the surface of [[Jakku]]. Thane recognised her voice over the intercom, so Kyrell made his way to the bridge to persuade Ree to leave with him, but she refused, fearing reprisal against her parents from the Empire. After a brief struggle, Kyrell was forced used his blaster to stun Ree and they evacuated in an escape pod. After crash-landing on the planet's surface, New Republic soldiers found them and took Ree, a high-ranking Imperial officer, into custody.<ref name="Lost Stars" />

===Legacy===
Two decades later, Yendor had become the ambassador from his homeworld of [[Ryloth]] to the Republic, and [[Senator]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] mentioned meeting the squadron to him during her investigation on the planet.<ref name="Bloodline">[[Bloodline (novel)|''Bloodline'']]</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Corona Squadron first appeared in [[Claudia Gray]]'s [[2015]] young adult novel ''[[Lost Stars]]'', which was released as part of the ''[[Journey to Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'' multimedia project.<ref name="Lost Stars" />
