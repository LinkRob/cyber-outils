The '''A-A4B truckspeeder''',<ref name="Solo Official Guide" /> also known as the '''A-A4B landspeeder'''<ref name="Nissan" /> or the '''A-A4B speeder truck''',<ref name="Starships and Speeders" /> was a model of [[Speeder truck|truckspeeder]] manufactured by [[Trast Heavy Transports]]. The [[gangster]] [[Moloch]] owned one such vehicle.

==Details==
[[File:AA4BSpeederTruck-SaS.png|thumb|left|220px|An A-A4B truckspeeder]]
[[Trast Heavy Transports|Trast's]] A-A4B truckspeeder was a popular short-haul [[cargo speeder]] typically used by small businesses for local deliveries. The square-shouldered vehicle had a long, narrow, rectangular forward section housing the powerful and noisy [[T-RA950 MagnaDrive engine]]. The [[cockpit]] lay at the rear of this section in front of a larger, reinforced cargo box mounted in the rear. From the factory, the cargo box had a single door with an extendable ramp which was reinforced. It could also be equipped with a hydraulic lift for heavy cargo, smaller side doors that were lockable, and even armor to protect cargo. The cargo box included a modular cargo-handling system to organize pallets, crates, or loose cargo so that loading and unloading the vehicle could be done quickly and efficiently. Passengers could also ride in the cargo section.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

The A-A4B proved to be an excellent short-haul cargo truck and attracted the favor of [[smuggler]]s, crime syndicates, and other individuals who wished to move large amounts of precious freight in semi-anonymity. The vehicle was known to be sturdy and relatively fast, resembling a [[tank]], with a favorable capacity. It could also be customized and had plenty of room in the hull for hidden compartments, retractable weapons, battering rams, shield generators, and other less-than legal modifications.<ref name="Starships and Speeders" />

==History==
The [[Grindalid]] gangster Moloch owned a modified A-A4B while working for [[Lady]] [[Proxima]] on [[Corellia]].<ref name="Solo Official Guide" /> As his [[species]] was sensitive to light, the henchman would don protective armor and travel in his truckspeeder when conducting business on the planet's surface.<ref name="Alien Archive">''[[Star Wars: Alien Archive]]''</ref> Moloch used his truckspeeder to chase down [[Han Solo|Han]] and [[Qi'ra]] when the [[scrumrat]]s escaped with a vial of [[coaxium]]. He rammed Han's stolen [[M-68 landspeeder]] but was unable to run them off the concourses leading to the [[Coronet City Spaceport|Coronet Spaceport]], and eventually gave chase on foot with his [[Corellian hound]]s.<ref name="Solo" />

==Behind the scenes==
As shown in ''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]'', the real-world Baja [[Wikipedia:Desert racing|desert-racing]] [[Wikipedia:Trophy truck|trophy trucks]] with their powerful suspensions and engines were an early source of inspiration for the A-A4B.<ref name="Art of Solo">''[[The Art of Solo: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref>
