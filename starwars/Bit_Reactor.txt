'''Bit Reactor''' is a [[Wikipedia:United States|American]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] studio.<ref name="BitReactor" /> On [[January 25]], [[2022]] it was announced that Bit Reactor would develop a new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[Star Wars video games|video game]] in collaboration with [[Electronic Arts|EA]]'s [[Respawn Entertainment]].<ref name="Reveal">{{SW|url=news/ea-lucasfilm-games|text=Electronic Arts and Lucasfilm Games Announce New ''Star Wars'' Titles in Development from Respawn Entertainment}}</ref> On [[April 14]], [[2025]],<ref name="swcom original">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-zero-company|text=Join the Ranks of ''Star Wars'' Zero Company|archiveurl=https://archive.ph/msqyK|oldversion=1}}</ref> the game was revealed as ''[[Star Wars Zero Company]]'', which will release in [[2026]].<ref name="swcom Update">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-zero-company|text=Join the Ranks of ''Star Wars Zero Company'' - Update}}</ref>
