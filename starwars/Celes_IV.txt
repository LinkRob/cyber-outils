{{Quote|I fought beside the Jedi once, on Celes IV, defending my home. A hard rock, a miserable rain, endless bloodshed, a great dark Separatist fortress too shielded to reduce from orbit.|Altadan Igar, to [[Luke Skywalker]]|<ref name="TMWCLS" />}}
'''Celes IV''', also written as '''Celes Four''', was a terrestrial astronomical object that was home to a [[Separatist fortress]] during the [[Clone Wars]].<ref name="TMWCLS" /> It was located in the [[Celes system]] in the [[Herdessa sector]] of the [[Mid Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A [[Battle of Celes IV|battle]] was fought there between the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] and forces of the [[Galactic Republic]], which included [[Altadan Igar]] and were lead by a [[Unidentified Jedi (Altadan Igar)|Jedi]]. The forces of the Republic won and took the fortress, but the death toll was immense. Igar later mentioned the events on Celes IV to [[Jedi Knight]] [[Luke Skywalker]] on the [[forest]] [[moon]] of [[Endor]] shortly before the [[Battle of Endor]],<ref name="TMWCLS" /> in [[4 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
