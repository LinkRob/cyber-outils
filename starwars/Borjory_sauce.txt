'''Borjory sauce''' was a type of [[Foodstuff|sauce]] known to the [[Chiss]] [[species]].<ref name="Greater Good" /> Around [[18 BBY]],<ref name="Date">The main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' take place around [[18 BBY]] per ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> [[Senior Warrior]] [[Yopring]], who served aboard the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] [[Nightdragon man-of-war]] [[Vigilant (Nightdragon man-of-war)|''Vigilant'']], was sent to [[Scout|explore]] the [[Unknown Regions]] [[planet]] [[Sunrise]]. While investigating the world's surface, Yopring located an active [[Mining|mine]]. The ''Vigilant''{{'s}} [[Executive officer|first officer]], [[Senior Captain]] [[Kiwu'tro'owmis]], [[core name]] "Wutroow," believed that mines were not run unless their owners' people had access to food and homes. Yopring then stated that if so, Sunrise had to be filled with borjory sauce.<ref name="Greater Good" />

==Behind the scenes==
The borjory sauce was mentioned in the [[2021]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'', the second installment in [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Greater Good" />
