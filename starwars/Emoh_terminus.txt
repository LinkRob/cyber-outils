'''Emoh terminus''' was a location<ref name="QH">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref> in the [[Ploo sector]]'s [[Emoh system]], within the [[Expansion Region]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and the [[New Territories]].<ref name="NewTerritories" /> It was situated in grid square N-7 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> In [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of ''[[Queen's Hope]]'' to [[22 BBY]].</ref> while on a [[Siege on Hebekrr Minor|mission]] to the [[planet]] [[Hebekrr Minor]], [[Senator]] [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] traveled aboard a [[shuttle]] and disembarked when she reached Emoh terminus. From there, she and her companions boarded the ''[[Namrelllew]]'', a [[Wookiee]] [[starship]], to transport them the rest of the way to Hebekrr Minor.<ref name="QH" />

==Behind the scenes==
Emoh terminus was mentioned in ''[[Queen's Hope]]'',<ref name="QH" /> a [[2022]] young-adult novel written by [[E. K. Johnston]]<ref name="Disney">{{Disney|subdomain=books|url=book/queens-hope|text=Queen's Hope}}</ref> as the third installment of ''[[Star Wars: Queen's Series]]''.<ref name="QH" />
