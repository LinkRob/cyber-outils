The '''Coruscant blockade''' was an [[Imperial Security Bureau]] [[blockade]]<ref name="Life Debt" /> [[fleet]] that served the [[Galactic Empire]] in the final moments of the [[Galactic Civil War]], defending the Imperial [[capital]] [[planet]] of [[Coruscant]]. While the bulk of the [[Imperial Navy]] were stationed at [[Jakku]] with other loyalist forces, the Coruscant blockade defended the [[star system]] from [[New Republic]] attack. The force consisted of dozens of Imperial [[starship]]s, including the ''[[Panaka]]'', and their [[TIE/ln space superiority starfighter|TIE fighters]] and blockade [[commander]]s. The blockade intercepted [[Colonel]] [[Soran Keize]] of the [[204th Imperial Fighter Wing]] and [[Lieutenant]] [[Yrica Quell]] of the New Republic as they participated in an [[attack on Coruscant's Verity District]].<ref name="Victory's Price" />

Concurrently, Imperial forces participated in a [[Coruscant civil war|civil war]] on the surface, with ISB [[Patrol ship|patrol craft]] and starships<ref name="Empire's End">''[[Aftermath: Empire's End]]''</ref> and [[TIE/sk x1 experimental air superiority fighter|TIE/sk striker]] [[squadron]]s bolstering the Imperial presence on the capital world.<ref name="Victory's Price" />
