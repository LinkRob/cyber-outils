'''Dart guns''' were a ranged weapon-type used to fire physical [[dart]] projectiles. These darts were highly customizable, able to be converted into special-use ammunition such as [[Stim Dart]]s<ref name="Reveal" /> and [[poison dart]]s.<ref name="Ultimate Star Wars">''[[Ultimate Star Wars]]''</ref> The [[assassin droid]] [[IG-88B|IG-88's]] [[needle dart gun]] was a type of dart gun.<ref name="The Visual Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref>
