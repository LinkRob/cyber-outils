The '''Carbon Ridge'''<ref name="RSG" /> was a ridge located on the [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Jakku]]. It held a [[Imperial research base|secret research facility]] owned by the [[Galactic Empire]].<ref name="RSG" /><ref name="DB">{{Databank|jakku|Jakku}}</ref> During the [[Battle of Jakku]], the [[New Republic]] took notice of this facility and attempted to attack it. A crashed [[Imperial II-class Star Destroyer|''Imperial II''-class Star Destroyer]] attempted to send [[TIE/sa tactical bomber|TIE bomber]] reinforcements to back up the Imperial troops there, but the remains of the [[Starship|ship]] were destroyed by [[Iden Versio]] and [[Shriv Suurgav]]. [[Lindsey's CR90 corvette|One]] [[CR90 corvette]] commanded by [[Captain]] [[Lindsey]] was shot down while making its way to Carbon Ridge.<ref name="Battlefront II" />
