'''Alchemists''' were individuals who practiced [[alchemy]], such as [[Sith alchemy|Force alchemy]],<ref name="Unlimited Power" /> which was also known as "Sith alchemy."<ref name="TROS VD" />

An alchemist might [[Force-sensitive|use]] [[the Force]] to create powerful objects by channeling the Force into physical form.<ref name="Unlimited Power" /> Sith-aligned alchemists were known as [[Sith alchemist]]s.<ref name="TROS VD" />
