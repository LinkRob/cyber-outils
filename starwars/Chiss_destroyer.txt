A class of '''destroyer''' was utilized by the [[Unknown Regions]] [[Chiss Ascendancy]] nation's [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet|Expansionary Defense Fleet]]. [[Chiss]] [[destroyer]]s were equipped with [[engine]]s and a [[hyperdrive]], and possessed [[electrostatic barrier]]s for [[Deflector shield|shields]]. Its [[weapon]]ry included [[spectrum laser]]s, [[plasma sphere]]s and [[breacher]] [[missile]]s<ref name="Chaos Rising" /> and the [[hull]] was constructed of [[nyix-alloy]].<ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref> In [[19 BBY]],<ref name="CR Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'' in [[19 BBY]].</ref> several of these [[Starship|ships]] participated in a [[Battle over Primea|battle]] over the [[Vak Combine]] nation [[planet]] of [[Primea]] against the [[Nikardun Destiny]] hegemony as part of [[Admiral]] [[Ar'alani]]'s [[Picket Force Six]].<ref name="Chaos Rising" />

By [[18 BBY]],<ref name="GG Date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' places the main events of ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]'' in [[18 BBY]].</ref> the [[Unidentified Midsummer pilot|pilot]] of the [[Xodlak family]] [[frigate]] ''[[Midsummer]]'' had served aboard a Chiss destroyer as its third [[pilot]] before being [[Family emergency|recalled]] by the [[Chiss family|family]] to serve aboard the family [[warship]].<ref name="Greater Good">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Greater Good]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Chiss destroyers first appeared in the [[2020]] novel ''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Chaos Rising]]'', the first installment of [[Timothy Zahn]]'s ''[[Star Wars: The Ascendancy Trilogy]]''.<ref name="Chaos Rising" />
