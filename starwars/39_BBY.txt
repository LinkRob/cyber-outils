The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''39 BBY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3238 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]],<ref name="RVG" /> '''20 BFE''' in the [[Imperial calendar]],<ref name="Rebel Files" /> and year '''7938''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Naberrie]] and her [[Ruwee Naberrie|father]] help relocate the entire populace of [[Shadda-Bi-Boran]]. The populace later [[Death|dies]] after they are unable to replicate Shadda-Bi-Boran's environment at their new home.<ref name="SBB date" />
*The [[Trade Federation]] starts encroaching on [[Kashyyyk]] and its colony worlds, beginning a 20-year-long conflict with the [[Wookiee]]s.<ref name="Complete Vehicles 2018">[[Star Wars: Complete Vehicles (2018)|''Star Wars: Complete Vehicles'' (2018)]]</ref>
*[[General]] [[Ba'kif]] of the [[Chiss Ascendancy]] founds the [[Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet]] {{Circa}}.<ref name="When Thrass met Thrawn" /><ref name="Chaos Rising" />
*The [[Chiss]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Kivu'raw'nuru]] "Vurawn" is [[Rematching|rematched]] as a [[merit adoptive]] of the [[Mitth family]], changing his name to Mitth'raw'nuru and his [[core name]] to Thrawn.<ref name="When Thrass met Thrawn" /><ref name="Chaos Rising" />
**Thrawn arrives on the [[planet]] [[Naporar]] for his tenure as a [[cadet]] at the Chiss Expansionary Defense Fleet's [[Taharim Academy]].<ref name="When Thrass met Thrawn" /><ref name="Chaos Rising" />
*[[Sky-walker]] [[navigator]] [[Mitth'ali'astov|Al'iastov]] loses her powers of [[precognition]], known as "[[The Force|Third Sight]]."<ref name="When Thrass met Thrawn" /><ref name="Chaos Rising" />
**Concurrently, Thrawn meets Al'iastov aboard the [[Chiss Defense Force Transport]] ''[[Tomra]]''.<ref name="When Thrass met Thrawn" /><ref name="Chaos Rising" />
*Thrawn meets the future [[Syndic]] [[Mitth'ras'safis]] "Thrass" at his [[rematching dinner]] on [[Avidich]].<ref name="When Thrass met Thrawn" /><ref name="Lesser Evil">''[[Thrawn Ascendancy: Lesser Evil]]''</ref>
*Cadet Thrawn is [[Mitth'raw'nuru's cheating charge|accused and acquitted]] of cheating on an exam at Taharim Academy {{Circa}}.<ref name="When Thrass met Thrawn" /><ref name="Chaos Rising" />
*[[Sifo-Dyas]] joins the [[Jedi High Council]]. {{Circa}}.<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref>
