'''DefenStar Ltd.''' was a company that manufactured the [[PSF Mk III power fence]], a popular small-scale [[power fence]] model.<ref name="Fully Operational" />

==Behind the scenes==
DefenStar Ltd. was first mentioned in the new ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in ''[[Lead by Example]]'', a [[2016]] [[Star Wars Roleplaying|roleplaying game]] sourcebook published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="Lead">''[[Lead by Example]]''</ref> It originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it was first mentioned in ''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]'', a [[1993]] roleplaying guide published by [[West End Games]] for ''[[Star Wars: The Roleplaying Game]]''.<ref name="Han Solo">''[[Han Solo and the Corporate Sector Sourcebook]]''</ref>
