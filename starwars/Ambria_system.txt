The '''Ambria system''' was a [[star system]] in the [[Airon sector]] of the [[Inner Rim Territories]]<ref name="Appendix" /> and [[the Slice]]<ref name="Slice" /> that fell within grid square O-10 of the [[Standard Galactic Grid]].<ref name="Appendix" /> A [[hyperspace route]] connected it to the [[Japrael system|Onderon system]]<ref name="TFABG">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]]</ref> and the [[Merson system]].<ref name="Merson">[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]] places the Ambria system on a [[hyperspace route]] in a position adjacent to where ''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]'' places the [[Merson system]] on the same route.</ref> The Ambria system contained the unpleasant [[desert]] [[planet]] [[Ambria]], which was in tune with the [[Dark side of the Force]].<ref name="HC 2" />

==Behind the scenes==
In the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]], the Ambria system was first mentioned as just "Ambria" on a map of [[the galaxy]] included in [[Star Wars: The Force Awakens Beginner Game|''Star Wars: The Force Awakens'' Beginner Game]], a [[2016]] ''[[Star Wars Roleplaying]]'' set published by [[Fantasy Flight Games]].<ref name="TFABG" /> The system was first pictured in an image of Ambria in the [[Star Wars Helmet Collection 2|second issue]] of [[De Agostini]]'s ''[[Star Wars Helmet Collection]]'' magazine series,<ref name="HC 2" /> published on [[September 2]], [[2014]].<ref name="France">{{HCdatesFR|2}}</ref> It was first identified as the Ambria system in "[[Star Systems of the Galaxy]]," an appendix published with the [[Star Wars Galaxy Map|''Star Wars'' Galaxy Map]] on [[StarWars.com]]<ref name="Appendix" /> on [[June 24]], 2025. Both were written by [[Jason Fry]].<ref name="Substack">{{Substack|subdomain=jasonfry|url=p/about-that-new-star-wars-galaxy-map|text=About That New Star Wars Galaxy Map&hellip;|name=[[Jason Fry]]|username=jasonfry|date=2025-06-24}}</ref>

The [[Ambria system/Legends|Ambria system]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where it first appeared in the [[Tales of the Jedi 3|third issue]] of the [[Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (comic series)|''Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] series, which was written by [[Tom Veitch]], illustrated by [[Janine Johnston]], and published by [[Dark Horse Comics]]<ref name="TOTJ3">[[Tales of the Jedi 3|''Tales of the Jedi'' 3]]</ref> on [[December 21]], [[1993]].<ref>{{Marvel|url=comics/issue/54568/star_wars_tales_of_the_jedi_1993_3|text=Star Wars: Tales of the Jedi (1993) #3}}</ref> The system was initially known as the Stenness system<ref name="TOTJ3" /> but was identified as the Ambria system in the [[1998]] reference book ''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]'', which was written by [[Daniel Wallace]].<ref name="EGtPaM">''[[The Essential Guide to Planets and Moons]]''</ref>
