'''Captain Terro's listening post''', was a [[listening post]] on the [[planet]] [[Tatooine]] that was utilized by the [[Galactic Empire]]'s [[stormtrooper]] [[Captain]] [[Terro]]. The post's presence on Tatooine resulted in the capture of many [[Starship|ships]] affiliated with the local [[Hutt]] [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]].<ref name="LotA" />
