'''Cordra''' was an individual that climbed a rock formation south west of an [[amberine]] mound called [[Typhon's Rock]] in the [[Lost Steppe]] region of the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[moon]] [[Toshara]]<ref name="Outlaws" /> by [[3 ABY]]. By the same [[Standard year|year]],<ref name="OutlawsDate">{{OutlawsDate}}</ref> a group of [[smuggler]]s established a [[mountain hideout]] on the rock formation and had stored a [[HD05-G circuit]] there.<ref name="Outlaws" /> In 3 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|ABY]],<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> a message entry which mentioned Cordra's climb and her belief that the rock formation would a good place to stash loot was stored on a [[datapad]] in the [[Crimson Dawn]] [[Crimson Dawn District (Mirogana)|district]] of [[Mirogana]], Toshara's [[capital]] [[city]].<ref name="Outlaws" />  

{{GameMechanics|Star Wars Outlaws}}
That year,<ref name="OutlawsDate" /> the [[human]] [[thief]] [[Kay Vess]] read the datapad in the Crimson Dawn district and later used her [[S-57 Cardinal]] [[speeder bike]] to jump a gab near the formation and land near the hideout. She then used the [[Ion blaster|ion]] module of her [[VM-19 heavy blaster pistol]] to power up the hideout door and recover the HD05-G circuit.<ref name="Outlaws" />
{{EndGame}}

==Behind the scenes==
Cordra was mentioned on a datapad entry title: "I'm here" which players can find in the [[2024]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]], ''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]'' developed by [[Massive Entertainment]] and published by [[Ubisoft]]. In the game, reading the datapad begins the "Smuggler's Hideout" intel quest which guides the player to the mountain hideout.<ref name="Outlaws" />
