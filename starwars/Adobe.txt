'''Adobe''' was a building material. [[1044 Chapel]], an old warehouse in the industrial district of [[Lothal]]'s [[Capital City]] later used as a safe house by the [[Gray Syndicate]], was made of adobe and expanded with synthetic stone. Capital City also sported a number of single-story adobe buildings.<ref name="secret">''[[Servants of the Empire: The Secret Academy]]''</ref>
