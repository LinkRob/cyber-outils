{{Quote|We took so much that the train of freighters stretches all the way back to Foran Tutha, with each new cargo offered at the feet of topboss Tasu Leech.|Razoo Qin-Fee, ''Smuggler's Guide''|<ref name="Smuggler's Guide">''[[Star Wars: Smuggler's Guide]]''</ref>}}
'''Foran Tutha''' was a terrestrial [[Planet|world]] located in the [[Tion Cluster]]'s<ref name="Nexus of Power" /> [[Cronese Mandate]] in the [[Foran Tutha system]], within the [[Outer Rim Territories]].<ref name="Appendix" /> Like other nearby worlds such as [[Belderone]], Foran Tutha was host to a cottage industry that supported treasure hunters for the ancient [[Jedi]] planet [[Ossus]], located nearby [[Cron Drift]].<ref name="Nexus of Power" />

After the [[Kanjiklub]] gang defeated forces of the [[Guavian Death Gang]] during the [[skirmish in the Cronese Mandate]], the gang member [[Razoo Qin-Fee]] claimed that the train of [[freighter]]s containing items they [[Thief|stole]] stretched back to Foran Tutha when writing about the events in the [[book]] known as the ''[[Smuggler's Guide]]''.<ref name="Smuggler's Guide" />
