"'''53'''" was an individual who served the [[Galactic Empire]] aboard the first [[DS-1 Orbital Battle Station|Death Star]], a [[planet]]-killing [[battle station]]. "53" was told to report to the Death Star's upper bay door even as [[Luke Skywalker]] and [[Han Solo]] were infiltrating the station to try and [[Rescue of Princess Leia|rescue]] [[Princess]] [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] from [[Detention Block AA-23]].<ref name="Episode IV" />
