'''Chaos''',<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> also known as '''the Void''',<ref name="Aphra">''[[Doctor Aphra: An Audiobook Original]]''</ref> was a mythological place associated with disorder.<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> When referred to as '''Hell''', it hinted at an unpleasant afterlife&mdash;[[deck officer]] [[Tigran Jamiro]] once told [[Han Solo]] he would probably freeze to [[death]] on the icy plains of [[Hoth]], before rescuing [[Luke Skywalker]], to which Solo replied "Then I'll see you in Hell!"<ref name="Star Wars Episode V: The Empire Strikes Back">{{Film|V}}</ref> The term could also be used as an [[List of phrases and slang|expletive]], such as when [[clone trooper]] [[Hevy]] asked "What the hell was that?" after a [[Rishi eel]] killed [[Cutup]] on the [[Rishi Moon]], and when Hevy claimed a [[BX-series droid commando|droid commando]] kill and [[Echo]] responded "Like hell you did."<ref name="Rookies">{{TCW|Rookies (episode)}}</ref> [[Owen Lars]] once said that Luke better have the units in the south range repaired by midday, "or there'll be hell to pay."<ref name="A New Hope">{{Film|IV}}</ref> After escaping from her binds and fleeing onto [[Polan GX-8]]'s escape ship, [[Mae-ho Aniseya]] remarks to Jedi Master [[Sol]], "See you in hell, Jedi."<ref name="The Acolyte (episode)">{{Acolyte|The Acolyte (episode)}}</ref>

According to a legend of the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] people, Chaos was a pit held back by six impenetrable gates. In a direct reference to that legend, the virtually fathomless core of [[Theed]]'s [[Theed power generator|power generator]] was kept behind six [[laser gate]]s.<ref name="CL" />

==Behind the scenes==
The word "Chaos" originates in the [[Star Wars Legends|Legends]] novel [[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Skywalker'']], where [[Anakin Skywalker|Darth Vader]] growls angrily at [[Raymus Antilles|Captain Antilles]], "Chaos take your mission!"<ref name="Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)">[[Star Wars: A New Hope (novelization)|''Star Wars: From the Adventures of Luke Skywalker'']]</ref> The term "hell" was first used in the film {{Film|IV}}.<ref name="A New Hope" />

In [[Wikipedia:Greek mythology|Greek mythology]], [[Wikipedia:Chaos (cosmogony)|''Chaos'']] refers to [[Wikipedia:Tartarus|Tartarus]], the Greek version of Hell; and the primeval emptiness of the universe before things came into being.<ref>{{WebCite|url=https://www.britannica.com/topic/Chaos-ancient-Greek-religion|text=Chaos|quote='''Chaos''', (Greek: 'Abyss') in early Greek cosmology, either the primeval emptiness of the universe before things came into being or the abyss of Tartarus, the underworld.|work=''[[Wikipedia:Encyclopædia Britannica|Encyclopædia Britannica]]''|archivedate=20201124051557}}</ref> It also refers to the primordial goddess of the same name (Kaos/Χαος in Greek, sometimes spelled Kháos, with Chaos being the common English spelling).
