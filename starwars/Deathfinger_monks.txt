The '''Deathfinger monks''' were a group of [[monk]]s. Shortly before the [[First Battle of Geonosis|Battle of Geonosis]]<ref name="CLNE">''[[Star Wars: Complete Locations, New Edition]]''</ref> in [[22 BBY]],<ref name="Timeline">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> a pair of [[humanoid]] Deathfinger monks drank at a table on the upper level of the [[Outlander Club]] on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] while plotting revenge against [[Greez Dritus]],<ref name="CLNE" /> a [[Latero]] [[pilot]] and [[Gambling|gambler]].<ref name="JS">''[[Star Wars Jedi: Survivor]]''</ref> One monk wore an [[Color|orange]] [[Clothing|shirt]], gray pants, and gray [[boot]]s. The other wore a blue dress, white boots, and a piece of blue headwear.<ref name="CLNE" />
