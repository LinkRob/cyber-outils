'''Bannamu''' was a [[Gender|male]] [[Patrolian]] [[Thief|pickpocket]] living on the [[planet]] [[Coruscant]] during the [[Clone Wars]]. In [[21 BBY]], Bannamu managed to steal a [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers|lightsaber]] from [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s belt and subsequently sold it to the [[Trandoshan]] [[Nack Movers]]. Soon, Tano, with the help of [[Jedi Master]] [[Tera Sinube]], tracked Bannamu down to his hideout in the [[Spider Arms Hostel]] and forced him to reveal the whereabouts of her weapon.<ref name="Lightsaber Lost" />
