The '''Carida incident''' was the result of a plot by the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]] to load the ''[[Renown]]'' with enough [[rhydonium]] to destroy the [[space station]] [[Valor (space station)|''Valor'']], which was the site of a [[Republic strategy conference]]. The plot was discovered and foiled by [[D-Squad]].<ref name="PONR" />
