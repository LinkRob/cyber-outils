The following events took place in the [[Standard year|year]] '''34 ABY''',<ref name="GA">''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]''</ref> also known as '''3311 LY''' according to the [[Lothal Calendar]]<ref name="RVG" /> and year '''8011''' in the [[Coruscant reckoning calendar|C.R.C.]] calendar.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

==Events==
*[[Temmin Wexley]] and [[Karé Kun]] are [[Snap and Karé's wedding|married]].<ref name="JTR 2">''[[Join the Resistance: Escape from Vodran]]''</ref>
*[[Kazuda Xiono]] is recruited by [[Poe Dameron]] for the [[Resistance]] and is sent on a [[Mission on the Colossus platform|mission]] with [[BB-8]].
*After [[Hunt for Lor San Tekka|searching]] the galaxy, the Resistance and [[First Order]] discover that [[Lor San Tekka]] is living on [[Jakku]].<ref name="Episode VII" />
*Lor San Tekka is [[Death|killed]] by [[Ben Solo|Kylo Ren]].<ref name="Episode VII" />
*[[Commander]] Poe Dameron receives a piece of the [[Map to Skywalker|map]] that could lead to [[Luke Skywalker]] and gives it to BB-8 before being captured by the First Order and imprisoned on the [[Resurgent-class Star Destroyer|''Resurgent''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Finalizer]]''.<ref name="Episode VII" />
*[[Finn]] defects from the First Order and [[Escape from the Finalizer|rescues]] Poe Dameron.<ref name="Episode VII" />
*[[Han Solo]] and [[Chewbacca]] reacquire the ''[[Millennium Falcon]]''.<ref name="Episode VII" />
*The First Order uses their new [[superweapon]] [[Starkiller Base]] to [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroy]] the [[Hosnian system]] and the [[New Republic]]'s capital [[Hosnian Prime]].<ref name="Episode VII" />
*[[Rey Skywalker|Rey]] is captured by Kylo Ren during the [[Battle of Takodana|battle]] on [[Takodana]].<ref name="Episode VII" />
*The Resistance commences the [[Battle of Starkiller Base]] and ultimately destroys the superweapon.<ref name="Episode VII" />
*Han Solo is killed by Kylo Ren.<ref name="Episode VII" />
*[[Captain]] [[Phasma]] begins a [[Hunt for Rivas|hunt]] for [[Sol Rivas]].<ref name="Phasma 1">[[Captain Phasma 1|''Captain Phasma'' 1]]</ref>
*Rey finds Luke Skywalker on [[Ahch-To]].<ref name="Episode VII" />
*The [[Cold War]] comes to an end.<ref name="Episode VII" />
*[[First Order-Resistance War|Open warfare]] begins between the First Order and the Resistance.<ref name="TLJ SDCC">{{SW|url=news/star-wars-the-last-jedi-props-and-costumes-on-display-at-sdcc|text=''Star Wars: The Last Jedi'' Props and Costumes on Display at SDCC}}</ref>
*The [[funeral of Han Solo]] is held on [[D'Qar]].<ref name="Expanded Edition">''[[Star Wars: The Last Jedi: Expanded Edition]]''</ref>
*Following the destruction of Starkiller Base, the Resistance [[Battle of D'Qar|evacuates]] their [[Resistance base|base]] on D'Qar.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
*[[Amilyn Holdo]] sacrifices herself by ramming the [[MC85 Star Cruiser]] [[Raddus (MC85 Star Cruiser)|''Raddus'']] through the [[Mega-class Star Dreadnought|''Mega''-class Star Dreadnought]] ''[[Supremacy]]''.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
*Snoke is killed by Kylo Ren, thus Ren becomes the new Supreme Leader of the First Order.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
*A [[Battle of Crait|battle]] is fought at [[Crait]]. Resistance forces suffer heavy losses, but core leadership escapes.<ref name="Episode VIII" />
*Luke Skywalker becomes one with the Force on [[Ahch-To]].<ref name="Episode VIII" />
*A manhunt against the surviving members of the Resistance is conducted by the First Order under Kylo Ren, with their trail eventually leading them to [[Black Spire Outpost]] on [[Batuu]].<ref name="Galaxy's Edge">[[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]]</ref><ref name="GEDate">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref>
*Resistance [[commander]] [[Venisa Doza]] and the ''[[Colossus]]''<nowiki/>' [[Ace Squadron]] breaks the First Order's [[blockade of Dantooine]], evacuating several Resistance recruits.<ref name="Rebuilding the Resistance">{{Resistance|Rebuilding the Resistance}}</ref>
*[[Tamara Ryvora]] is rescued from the [[First Order]] by Kazuda Xiono and [[Jarek Yeager]] leading to a skirmish that results in the execution of [[Agent]] [[Tierny]] and the death of [[Commander]] [[Pyre]].
