'''Blue Milk with Golden Lichen Paddy Frog Eggs''' was a beverage made from [[blue milk]], [[Golden Lichen]], and [[Klatooine paddy frog]] [[egg]]s found at the [[Milk Stand]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]] on the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[Batuu]].<ref name="SWGE" /> It was suitable for [[human]] consumption and sold in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] and [[Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser|''Star Wars'': Galactic Starcruiser]] to [[34 ABY]]. As this beverage was sold at Galaxy's Edge, it was therefore sold in 34 ABY.</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
Blue Milk with Golden Lichen Paddy Frog Eggs was available in the [[In-universe|real world]] at the Milk Stand in [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] in [[Disneyland]] in [[Wikipedia:Anaheim, California|Anaheim, California]]. It was a seasonal offering which was first sold in [[November]] of [[2024]].<ref name="WDWNT">{{WebCite|url=https://wdwnt.com/2024/12/review-life-day-2024-swge-milk-stand-dlr|text=REVIEW: Wookiee Ookiees and Blue Milk Paddy Frog Egg Drink Honor Life Day 2024 at Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge Milk Stand|work=[https://www.wdwnt.com WDW News Today]|author=Brit Tuttle|archivedate=20241207232308}}</ref> The menu describes the beverage as "Blue Milk with Strawberry Puree and Lychee Popping Spheres."<ref name="Menu">{{Disney|subdomain=disneyland|domain=go.com|url=dining/disneyland/milk-stand|text=Disneyland: Milk Stand}}</ref> This beverage is similar to the [[Green Milk with Klatooine Paddy Frog Eggs]] sold at the Milk Stand at Disneyland in summer 2024, however the green milk iteration was served with blueberry popping spheres. The beverage returned on [[November 14]], [[2025]] as part of the park's [[Life Day]] celebration, which corresponded with the real-world holiday season.<ref name="LaughingPlace">{{WebCite|url=https://www.laughingplace.com/disney-parks/star-wars-life-day-menu-disneyland/|text=Celebrate Life Day With Special Menu Items Coming to Disneyland's Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|author=Betti, Tony|date=2025-11-07|work=[https://laughingplace.com Laughing Place]|archivedate=20251107203118}}</ref>
