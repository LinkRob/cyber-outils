'''Cardogian honey''' was a type of [[honey]]. [[Marlaa Jinara]] and [[Maz Kanata]] caught several [[giant buzzer bee]]s on [[Borlax 14]] by using Cardogian honey as bait. Later, when the pair attempted to locate and stop a rogue jetpack on [[Tenoo]], Jinara reminded Kanata about how they caught the giant buzzer bees, but Kanata pointed out that they did not have any have Cardogian honey as they searched for the jetpack, and that even if they did have it, it would not have been useful in the search for a rogue jetpack.<ref name="TheJumpingJetpack">{{YJA|The Jumping Jetpack}}</ref>
