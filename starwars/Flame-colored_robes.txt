'''Flame-colored robes''' were worn by the [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaidens]] of [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]] when she served as [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]] of the [[planet]] [[Naboo]]. The [[robe]]s were designed by the [[handmaiden]] [[Yané]] to complement several of the [[queen]]'s [[Clothing|outfits]] and help the handmaidens fade to the background. They were worn by the handmaidens throughout Amidala's term as Queen of Naboo, including during the [[Invasion of Naboo]], when Amidala escaped the planet hidden among her handmaidens.

The robes enjoyed an enduring life as a wearable garment: robes in the same style were also worn by Amidala's handmaidens during her tenure as a [[senator]] in the [[Galactic Republic]] [[Galactic Senate|Senate]] and by the handmaidens of a later [[Gender|queen]] of Naboo, [[Sosha Soruna]]. Flame-colored robes were immortalized in [[Painting|artworks]] depicting Amidala after her term as queen ended, and their image reached beyond Naboo in [[Handmaiden pin-up|pin-up]] posters displayed in [[cantina]]s, [[barracks]], and other public locations from the [[Core Worlds]] to the [[Outer Rim Territories]].

==Description==
The flame-colored robes featured a [[Color|yellow]]-to-orange ombré outer [[robe]]<ref name="Episode I" /> over an orange under-robe made of [[Karlini silk]]. The lighter under-robe was made to help prevent sweat stains, as the outer robe was difficult to clean.<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> The [[Clothing|ensemble]] included a two-piece hood that covered the wearer's [[hair]] and neck and shadowed their face. To complete the outfit, a sash matching the orange under-robe was wrapped wide around the back, crossed, and tucked at the front of the waist to form [[Flower|petal]]-like shapes over the hips.<ref name="Episode I" /> The [[Star|sunrise]]-like appearance of the robes symbolized the rebirth of<ref name="EncyclopediaPadme">{{EncyclopediaCite|Padmé Amidala}}</ref> the [[Mid Rim Territories]]<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> [[planet]] [[Naboo]].<ref name="EncyclopediaPadme" />

==History==
===The subtle flame===
{{Quote|I wasn't talking about just blankets. Can you only make heavy yarn do what you want?|Quarsh Panaka to Yané, inquiring about her skill with fabrics|<ref name="Queen's Peril" />}}
[[File:TPM_PurpleFlame.png|thumb|left|300px|Sabé and Rabé wear flame-colored robes while attending the queen of Naboo.]]
The [[Naboo Royal Handmaidens|handmaiden]] [[Yané]] designed the [[Fire|flame]]-colored robes for [[Padmé Amidala Naberrie|Padmé Amidala]]'s [[handmaiden]]s during her first term as [[Monarch of Naboo|queen]] of the planet Naboo<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> in [[32 BBY]].<ref name="Date I" /> The handmaidens often wore hoods, which quickly touched off a trend for wearing similarly styled hooded garments among the [[Naboo (people)|Naboo]] people that rendered the flame-colored robes unremarkable in silhouette. Flame, however, quickly became the handmaidens' signature color, and they found that the saturated hues drew attention away from the individual wearer.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

The handmaidens typically wore matching robes, chosen to complement whichever outfit was selected for the [[queen]] from her extensive [[Padmé Amidala's wardrobe|wardrobe]].<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> During Amidala's first term, the flame-colored robes were paired with at least two different gowns: they provided a stark contrast with a [[black traveling gown]] and matching feathered headdress, and they complemented the rich hues of a somber [[dark purple traveling dress]].<ref name="Episode I" />

===Fire of defiance===
{{Quote|We'll weave a code with the rest of the pieces. It'll be something simple. ''[&hellip;]'' Something that the people will understand and the droids will overlook.|Yané, determining how best to use scraps of the handmaidens' robes|<ref name="Queen's Peril" />}}
[[File:SabeRabePadme-TPM.jpg|thumb|right|270px|Padmé Amidala wears the flame-colored robe of a handmaiden while Sabé plays the queen.]]
As part of its [[Invasion of Naboo|invasion]] of Naboo<ref name="Queen's Peril" /> in 32 [['ABY-BBY' dating system|BBY]],<ref name="Timelines" /> the [[Trade Federation]] occupied the [[city]] of [[Theed]] in an attempt to force the [[Gender|queen]] into signing a treaty. As soon as Amidala's [[Head of Security]], [[Quarsh Panaka]], noticed something amiss, the handmaiden [[Saché]] used the pre-arranged code word "flame" to signal all the handmaidens to gather in the [[Throne room (Theed Royal Palace)|throne room]] of the [[Theed Royal Palace]] dressed in the flame-colored robes.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

Later, hidden among her handmaidens, Amidala escaped the planet and the Federation, wearing the robes as a disguise. She continued to wear them and go by the name "Padmé" while on the [[Naboo Royal Starship|royal starship]] alongside her handmaidens [[Rabé Tonsort|Rabé]] and [[Eirtaé]] while [[Sabé]] posed as the queen.<ref name="Episode I" /> The robes also proved useful while Yané and Saché were trapped in the Trade Federation's [[Camp Four|prison camp]]; Yané shredded their under-robes and used them to [[Weaver|weave]] coded messages. Using the pattern of a [[Comlink|comm code]] commonly learned by Naboo [[Youngling|children]], the colorful [[silk]] threads outlined guard rotations and key strategic locations for the [[Naboo resistance|resistance]] against the Trade Federation.<ref name="Queen's Peril" />

===Remembered in light===
{{Quote|Is one of them holding the canopy? I don't think the artist understands what it is we do.|Saché, criticizing a stained glass window depicting Padmé Amidala's handmaidens|<ref name="Queen's Shadow">''[[Queen's Shadow]]''</ref>}}
[[File:StainedGlassHandmaiden.jpg|thumb|left|250px|A stained glass window in the Theed Royal Palace depicted a flame-colored robe.]]
The flame-colored robes saw continued use after the Invasion. When the [[Gungan]] [[Jar Jar Binks]] brought news to Queen Amidala of a [[map]] to a powerful Gungan relic, her handmaidens were again wearing the flame-colored robes.<ref name="Raiders">{{IDWAdventuresCite-2017|issue=18|story=Raiders of the Lost Gundark}}</ref>

A stained [[glass]] [[Viewport|window]] in the Theed Royal Palace commissioned during the reign of [[Réillata]], Amidala's successor, depicted Amidala taking part in a royal procession, surrounded by many handmaidens wearing flame-colored, hooded outfits.<ref name="Queen's Shadow" /> By the [[time]] of the [[Clone Wars]] fought between the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]], the palace also had a stained glass window picturing a lone figure in profile wearing the flame-colored robe and a [[painting]] of a group of eight figures dressed in flame-colored robes crossing a courtyard.<ref name="BF II">''[[Star Wars Battlefront II]]''</ref>

A [[Handmaiden pin-up|pin-up]] poster featuring a woman wearing the robes was also widely available in [[the galaxy]] during the Clone Wars. Copies of the poster hung in places as far flung as a [[cantina]] on the [[Coruscant Underworld|lower levels]] of the [[Ecumenopolis|city-world]] [[Coruscant]]<ref name="LethalTrackdown">{{TCW|Lethal Trackdown}}</ref> in the [[Core Worlds]] and locations in the [[Outer Rim Territories]] such as<ref name="TVE" /> a [[Weequay]] [[pirate]] [[Hondo's complex|lair]] on the planet [[Florrum]],<ref name="GunganGeneral">{{TCW|The Gungan General}}</ref> Republic [[clone trooper]] [[barracks]] on the world of [[Christophsis]],<ref name="HiddenEnemy">{{TCW|The Hidden Enemy}}</ref> and the outside of a building on the planet [[Iego]].<ref name="ThousandMoons">{{TCW|Mystery of a Thousand Moons}}</ref>

===Banked embers===
[[File:KarteImpersonated-FODAhsokaPadme.png|thumb|right|250px|Cato Parasitti impersonates the handmaiden Karté.]]
During Amidala's time as a [[senator]] of the Republic's [[Galactic Senate|Senate]], shortly after the outbreak of the Clone Wars, Sabé wore the outfit once again when she greeted Amidala following her return from the planet [[Hebekrr Minor]] and helped her prepare for bed. Sabé also told Amidala that she had learned of her secret [[marriage]] to the [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]] and mentioned that she had decided to leave her position as Amidala's handmaiden, not able to keep up with all the changes in Amidala's life and wishing to be her own person rather than simply Amidala's shadow.<ref name="Queen's Hope">''[[Queen's Hope]]''</ref>

The disguise was used against Amidala when the [[Clawdite]] [[bounty hunter]] [[Cato Parasitti]] shapeshifted into the form of Amidala's handmaiden [[Karté]] and attempted to disrupt a delicate negotiation with the [[Arthurian delegates]]. When Amidala and her friend, [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], discovered the deception and fought off the [[bounty]] hunter, Parasitti was dressed in a flame-colored robe to complete her handmaiden disguise.<ref name="Imposter Inside">{{FOD|The Imposter Inside}}</ref> Handmaidens wearing flame-colored robes also attended Amidala as she met up with Skywalker for a mission.<ref name="Unexpected Company">{{FOD|Unexpected Company}}</ref>

The retinue of at least one other queen of Naboo also wore similar robes.<ref name="SE3">[[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]]</ref> In [[4 ABY]],<ref name="DateSE">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Shattered Empire 3|''Shattered Empire'' 3]] to [[4 ABY]].</ref> an [[Lerr Duvat|agent]] of the remnant [[Galactic Empire]] executed [[Operation: Cinder]] on Naboo, activating a [[climate disruption array]] which caused [[hurricane]]s and dangerous electrical storms across the planet. As Queen [[Sosha Soruna]] prepared to launch an attack on the array, she was aided by two handmaidens who wore robes identical to the flame-colored robes worn by Amidala's handmaidens.<ref name="SE3" /> No earlier than [[34 ABY]],<ref name="34 ABY">''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny: Volume 2]]'' and ''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny: Volume 3]]'' feature [[Maz Kanata]] narrating the first eight episodes from ''[[Forces of Destiny: Season One]]'' and ''[[Forces of Destiny: Season Two]]''. One of the stories Kanata recounts is "[[Shuttle Shock]]," which takes place during {{Film|VIII}}, dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''. Therefore, her narration must occur sometime no earlier than that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> the [[Pirate Queen|pirate queen]]<ref name="Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Star Wars Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Maz Kanata]] was looking for<ref name="Volume 3">''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny: Volume 3]]''</ref> her<ref name="Encyclopedia" /> [[Foodstuff|dinner]] when an individual and the [[Flurrg]] [[Roz (Flurrg)|Roz]] visited her. Kanata recounted various stories to her visitors as they related to her search, including two in which Amidala's handmaidens donned flame-colored robes.<ref name="Volume 3" /><ref name="Volume 2">''[[Star Wars Forces of Destiny: Volume 2]]''</ref> Also during this time,<ref name="Epic Adventures Date">The narration in ''[[Star Wars Epic Adventures: The Phantom Menace]]'' takes place after the events of {{Film|VIII}}, dated to [[34 ABY]] by ''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''.</ref> the [[protocol droid]] [[C-3PO]] recounted to [[BB-8]] about the events leading up to and during the [[Battle of Naboo]], which included the handmaidens wearing flame-colored robes.<ref name="Epic Adventures">''[[Star Wars Epic Adventures: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref>

==Behind the scenes==
===Continuity and name===
Flame-colored robes were created for the [[prequel trilogy]] film [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace|''Star Wars'': Episode I ''The Phantom Menace'']],<ref name="Episode I" /> released [[May 19]], [[1999]].<ref name="YbY">''[[Star Wars Year By Year: A Visual History, Updated and Expanded Edition]]''</ref> In the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, they appeared prior to the film in its [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|novelization]],<ref name="TPM novel">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (novelization)|''Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace'' novelization]]</ref> which was written by [[Terry Brooks]] and published on [[April 21]], 1999.<ref name="YbY" />

The robes were not named in the film.<ref name="Episode I" /> However, in the Legends [[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (junior novelization)|junior novelization]], written by [[Patricia C. Wrede]]<ref name="TPMJr">[[Star Wars: Episode I The Phantom Menace (junior novelization)|''Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace'' junior novelization]]</ref> and released in [[May]] of the same year,<ref name="TPMJrDate">{{CollectorCite|6|The Padawan Reading List}}</ref> they were identified as "[[Flame-colored robes/Legends|flame-colored robes]]."<ref name="TPMJr" /> That name was first used in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2017]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''.<ref name="TVE">''[[Star Wars: The Visual Encyclopedia]]''</ref> From [[2015]] to [[2018]],<ref name="Itinerary">{{WebCite|url=http://www.powerofcostume.si.edu/intinerary.html|text=''Itinerary''|author=[https://www.sites.si.edu Smithsonian Institution Traveling Exhibition Service]|partialdate=[[2017]]|archivedate=20201111181401}}</ref> the [[Wikipedia:Smithsonian Institution|Smithsonian Institution Traveling Exhibition Service]] exhibited the costume as "Handmaiden, Ombréd Travel Gown" in ''[[Rebel, Jedi, Princess, Queen: Star Wars and the Power of Costume]]''.<ref name="All Costumes">{{WebCite|url=http://www.powerofcostume.si.edu/allCostumes.html|text=''All Costumes''|author=[https://www.sites.si.edu Smithsonian Institution Traveling Exhibition Service]|partialdate=[[2017]]|archivedate=20200928162529}}</ref>

===Conception and construction===
[[File:Flame_sleeve_detail_2.png|thumb|right|250px|Detail of the sleeve shows red under-sleeve with guipure lace trim]]
When designing for the handmaidens, concept artist [[Iain McCaig]] drew simpler hairstyles and tried to evoke a more [[Wikipedia:Pre-Raphaelite Brotherhood|Pre-Raphaelite]] or [[Wikipedia:Art Nouveau|art nouveau]] flavor than the queen's more elaborate costumes. His Naboo costume designs deliberately obscured details like size, shape, and age because they would be used for disguise. Working with McCaig, costume designer [[Trisha Biggar]] turned the sketches into outfits that would work onscreen.<ref name="ArtTPM">''[[The Art of Star Wars Episode I: The Phantom Menace]]''</ref> Biggar envisioned a dress code and a specific style for ceremonial regalia for queens and handmaidens, which included common silhouettes and motifs.<ref name="Dressing">''[[Dressing a Galaxy: The Costumes of Star Wars]]''</ref> In addition, Biggar sought to keep the handmaidens in petite, vertical costumes to contrast with Amidala's larger, diagonal ensembles.<ref name="Souvenir Guide">''[[The Official Star Wars: Episode I Souvenir Magazine]]''</ref> The handmaidens' outfits were dyed to complement the queen's ensembles.<ref name="Dressing" />

The hooded dresses were originally made in white [[Wikipedia:Silk|silk]] and [[Wikipedia:Rayon|viscose]] [[Wikipedia:Velvet|velvet]] and then disassembled and [[Wikipedia:Ombré|ombré-dyed]] so that the depths and levels of the colors matched when the handmaidens stood side by side. The red sashes, sleeves, and inner hoods were sewn from [[Wikipedia:Grain (textile)#Bias|bias-cut]] silk [[Wikipedia:Crêpe (textile)|crepe]]. Antique [[Wikipedia:Guipure|guipure]] [[Wikipedia:Lace|lace]] motifs were dyed to match and used to trim the sleeves.<ref name="Dressing" />
