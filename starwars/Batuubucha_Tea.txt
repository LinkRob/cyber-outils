'''Batuubucha Tea''' was a [[fruit]]-flavored [[tea]] from [[Batuu]] that was fermented and bubbly, and was rumored to have health benefits. After [[Standard century|centuries]] of enjoyment by local [[Batuuan]]s, it gained popularity among the wealthy of the [[Core Worlds]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" />

In [[34 ABY]],<ref name="GE date">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]'' dates the events of [[Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge|''Star Wars'': Galaxy's Edge]] to [[34 ABY]].</ref> Batuubucha tea was served at [[Docking Bay 7 Food and Cargo]] in [[Black Spire Outpost]].<ref name="Docking Bay 7" /> When [[Strono Tuggs]] was the visiting [[chef]], he sold it at his mobile [[restaurant]], [[Tuggs' Grub]].<ref name="Traveler's Guide" /> [[Ylena]], who was a member of the [[Gatherers]] on Batuu and a [[Resistance]] supporter, drank the tea when she had lunch with [[Dolin]] before he assisted with sabotaguing the [[First Order]]'s [[MHU-6e Mobile Habitation Unit|mobile habitat]].<ref name="GE:BS">''[[Galaxy's Edge: Black Spire]]''</ref>
