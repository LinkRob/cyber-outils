'''Chalm Plesk''' was a [[University of Coruscant]] [[historian]] who was embedded with the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] during the [[Battle of Peridea|return]] of [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] to [[the galaxy]] around [[9 ABY]]. Plesk was then blocked from publishing his work, ''[[The Last War Against the Galactic Empire: On Deployment with the New Republic Fleet]]'', for [[Standard year|years]] and [[Death|perished]] during the [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destruction]] of the Republic's [[capital]], [[Hosnian Prime]], in [[34 ABY]] while there to argue his case before the [[New Republic Senate]].

==Biography==
{{Quote|Plesk eventually took his case to the New Republic Senate on Hosnian Prime but, tragically, was on the world when it was destroyed by the First Order.|Beaumont Kin, ''The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire''|<ref name="Rise" />}}
[[File:Thrawn-AhsokaPart8.png|thumb|right|300px|alt=Blue-skinned and red-eyed, Thrawn stands in his white Grand Admiral's uniform on board the Eye of Sion.|Chalm Plesk was embedded with the New Republic Defense Fleet when Grand Admiral Thrawn returned to the galaxy.]]
Chalm Plesk was a [[historian]] from the [[University of Coruscant]] who was embedded with the [[New Republic Defense Fleet]] when [[Grand Admiral]] [[Mitth'raw'nuruodo|Thrawn]] [[Battle of Peridea|returned]] to [[the galaxy]]<ref name="Rise" /> around [[9 ABY]]<ref name="Ahsoka Date">{{AhsokaSeriesDate}}</ref> after being trapped in a [[far galaxy]].<ref name="Ahsoka">{{Ahsoka|Part Eight: The Jedi, the Witch, and the Warlord}}</ref> Plesk then spent [[Standard year|years]] trying to publish his planned work: ''[[The Last War Against the Galactic Empire: On Deployment with the New Republic Fleet]]'', which was highly anticipated, but various figures in the [[New Republic Defense Force|military]] refused to allow its publication.<ref name="Rise" />

A file relating to Plesk's work titled [[Notice Prohibiting Publication 1310|Notice Prohibiting Publication #1310]] was stored in the "Military Intelligence" section of the [[New Republic Archives]]. Plesk eventually took his case to the [[New Republic Senate]] on the Republic's [[capital]], [[Hosnian Prime]], but [[Death|perished]] there when the [[First Order]] [[Hosnian Cataclysm|destroyed]] the [[Hosnian system]]<ref name="Rise" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="DeathDate" /> In [[35 ABY]],<ref name="RiseDate">{{RiseAndFallDate}}</ref> the historian [[Beaumont Kin]] mentioned Plesk in<ref name="Rise" /> his<ref name="TROSVD">''[[Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker: The Visual Dictionary]]''</ref> own [[book]] ''[[The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', calling the fellow's historian's death a tragedy and stating that he hoped copies of Plesk's planned work still existed somewhere so that they could be released.<ref name="Rise" />

==Behind the scenes==
Chalm Plesk was mentioned in the [[2024]] reference book ''[[Star Wars: The Rise and Fall of the Galactic Empire]]'', which was written by Dr. [[Chris Kempshall]].<ref name="Rise" />
