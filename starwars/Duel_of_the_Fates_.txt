"'''Duel of the Fates!'''" is a short comic story published in [[Totally: The Phantom Menace|''Totally 20: The Phantom Menace'']].

==Publisher's summary==
''An awesome comic strip in eye-popping 3D. Anakin, still a slave boy, shows his speedy Podracing on Tatooine, while Darth Maul crushes Battle droids and is trained by the dreadful Darth Sidious!''
