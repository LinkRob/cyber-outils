The '''battle on Phorsa Gedd''' took place during the [[Galactic Civil War]] between the [[Alliance to Restore the Republic|Rebel Alliance]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] as part of the former's [[Mid Rim Retreat|Mid Rim offensive]]<ref name="TC" /> of circa [[1 ABY]]&ndash;[[2 ABY]].<ref name="Timelines" /> The Alliance's [[61st Mobile Infantry]], led by [[Captain]] [[Micha Evon]], fought in the [[factory]]-[[desert]]s of the [[Mid Rim Territories|Mid Rim]]<ref name="TC" /> [[planet]]<ref name="Odyssey">''[[The Odyssey of Star Wars: An Epic Poem]]''</ref> [[Phorsa Gedd]]. Among the [[Rebel trooper|rebel soldiers]] who participated were [[Hazram Namir]], [[Carver]], [[Roja]] and [[Beak (individual)|Beak]], with Namir remembering the event vividly.<ref name="TC" />

While on the planet [[Hoth]]<ref name="TC" /> in [[3 ABY]],<ref name="Date">{{BTCdate}}</ref> Namir, Roja, and Beak recounted the fighting on Phorsa Gedd. Later that [[Standard year|year]], following the 61st's [[Siege of Inyusu Tor|victory]] against the Empire on the planet [[Sullust]], Carver retold the events of the Phorsa Gedd battle while members of the 61st gathered at a [[cantina]] in Sullust's [[capital]] [[city]] of [[Pinyumb]].<ref name="TC" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle on Phorsa Gedd was mentioned in the [[2015]] novel ''[[Battlefront: Twilight Company]]'', written by [[Alexander Freed]].<ref name="TC" />
