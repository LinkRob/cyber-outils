'''Christopher Patrick Nolan''' is a stage and screen actor and an alumnus of the London Academy of the Performing Art.<ref name="CPN AHA">{{WebCite|url=http://www.ahatalent.co.uk/actor/christopher-patrick-nolan|text=Christopher Patrick Nolan|work=[https://www.ahatalent.co.uk AHA Talent]|archivedate=20201025185642}}</ref> He portrayed the [[Alderaanian consular security]] [[Security guard|guard]] [[Toshma Jefkin]] in the the [[2016]] [[Star Wars Anthology Series|''Star Wars Anthology'']] film ''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''.<ref name="Rogue One">''[[Rogue One: A Star Wars Story]]''</ref> Nolan was at [[Pinewood Studios]] for four days, during additional photography and reshoots, and his part was filmed in three days.<ref name="Interview">{{WebCite|url=https://starwarsinterviews.com/star-wars-anthology/rogue-one/christopher-patrick-nolan-toshma-jefkin|text=Christopher Patrick Nolan (Toshma Jefkin)|author=[[Dennis Pellegrom]]|date=2017-02|work=[[Star Wars Interviews]]|archivedate=20230205201720|nolive=1}}</ref>

Outside of ''Star Wars'', Nolan has appeared in many theater productions in [[Wikipedia:London|London's]] [[Wikipedia:West End of London|West End]] and [[Wikipedia:New York City|New York City's]] [[Wikipedia:Broadway theatre|Broadway]], including ''1984'', ''War Horse'', ''Stones in his Pockets'', ''Macbeth'' (with Patrick Stewart) and ''The Seagull'' (with Carey Mulligan, Kristen Scott Thomas, Chiwetel Ejiofor and Peter Sarsgaard).<ref name="CPN CV">{{WebCite|url=https://www.ahatalent.co.uk/cv/nolan-christopher-patrick.pdf|text=Christopher Patrick Nolan CV|work=[https://www.ahatalent.co.uk AHA Talent]|archivedate=20210304121202}}</ref>
