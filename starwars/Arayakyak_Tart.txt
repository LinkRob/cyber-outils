'''Arayakyak Tarts''' were a type of tiny [[Foodstuff|dessert]] [[Cake|tart]] that paid homage to [[Mount Arayakyak]] on the [[planet]] [[Kashyyyk]]. The tarts were made entirely from [[fruit]]s and [[nut]]s that could be found growing in the [[jungle]] orchards on the [[mountain]]. The [[Artiodac]] [[chef]] [[Strono Tuggs]] included a recipe for the tarts in his second [[Book|cookbook]], ''[[The Life Day Cookbook]]'', which he released at some point after publishing his first [[The Official Black Spire Outpost Cookbook|cookbook]]<ref name="LDCookbook" /> in [[34 ABY]].<ref name="Date">{{BSOCookbookDate}}</ref>

Tuggs, who considered the fruits of Mount Arayakyak to be some of the freshest and most delicious on all of Kashyyyk, suggested serving the tarts during the [[Life Day]] festival. However, he stated that anyone celebrating Life Day away from Kashyyyk could instead substitute the ingredients from the mountain orchards for local [[Berry|berries]] and still achieve an equally delicious result.<ref name="LDCookbook" />

==Behind the scenes==
Arayakyak Tart was created as a recipe for the [[2021]] ''[[Star Wars: The Life Day Cookbook]]'', a recipe book written by [[Jenn Fujikawa]] and [[Marc Sumerak]]. The [[In-universe|out-of-universe]] recipe for the topping uses shelled raw [[Wikipedia:Pistachio|pistachios]], [[Wikipedia:Brown sugar|packed light brown sugar]], and [[Wikipedia:Water|water]]. The recipe for the filling uses [[Wikipedia:White sugar|granulated sugar]], [[Wikipedia:Lemon#Culinary_uses|lemon juice]], lemon [[Wikipedia:Zest (ingredient)|zest]], large [[Wikipedia:Egg as food|eggs]], an [[Wikipedia:Yolk|egg yolk]], cubed and unsalted [[Wikipedia:Butter|butter]], and [[Wikipedia:Salt|salt]]. The crust recipe uses [[Wikipedia:Graham cracker|graham cracker crumbs]], chopped shelled raw pistachios, melted and unsalted butter, packed light brown sugar, salt, [[Wikipedia:Raspberry|raspberries]], and [[Wikipedia:Mentha|mint leaves]].<ref name="LDCookbook" />
