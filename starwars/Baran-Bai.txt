'''Baran-Bai''' was a location that was home to the [[lesser leaf viper]] and its cousin, the [[elder viper]]. According to the [[Theelin-human hybrid|Theelin-human]] [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Jecki Lon]], the lesser leaf viper was significantly more deadly than the elder viper, though had an appearance that made it no more interesting than that of a [[rock worm]].<ref name="Crystal Crown" /> In [[134 BBY]],<ref name="Date">{{CrystalCrownDate}}</ref> during the [[High Republic Era]], before Lon took part in the [[dueling tournament]] portion of the [[Crowning Convocation]] on the [[planet]] [[Siline]], her [[Jecki Lon's opponent|opponent]] told her she looked even smaller when he was up close to her. In response, the Padawan told him about the [[Snake|vipers]] of Baran-Bai.<ref name="Crystal Crown" />
