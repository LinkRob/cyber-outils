The '''Assault on the Imperial Gozanti freighter'''<ref name="SWCT">{{SWCT|set=The Mandalorian - Box Set|cardname=Assault on the Imperial Gozanti Freighter!}}</ref> was a [[Mandalorian resistance]] operation<ref name="Chapter 11" /> in [[9 ABY]].<ref name="Date" /> Four [[Mandalorian]]s led by [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] went on a mission to steal weapons from an [[Unidentified Gozanti-class Assault Carrier|Imperial ''Gozanti''-class Assault Carrier]] which flew for a [[Gideon's Imperial remnant|remnant]] of the [[Galactic Empire]] led by [[Moff]] [[Gideon]].<ref name="Chapter 11" />

{{Battle-stub}}

==Behind the scenes==
The mission appeared in "[[Chapter 11: The Heiress]]" of [[Jon Favreau]]'s [[2019]] [[Disney+]] television series ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'', directed by [[Bryce Dallas Howard]]<ref name="Chapter 11" /> and released on [[November 13]], [[2020]].<ref name="D23">{{D23|url=everything-new-you-can-stream-on-disney-in-november-2020|text=Everything New You Can Stream on Disney+ in November 2020}}</ref>
