'''Carbonite''' was a liquid substance that was made from [[carbon]] gas and could change into a solid through rapid freezing. Goods could be encased in carbonite for preservation, through a process known as [[carbon-freezing]],<ref name="SYWtBaJ?">''[[The Empire Strikes Back: So You Want to Be a Jedi?]]''</ref> and an example was condensed [[tibanna]] gas because carbon-freezing was used as a way to condense it for transport.<ref name="CVD">''[[Star Wars: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition]]''</ref>

Carbonite blocks could also be used to place people in hibernation.<ref name="ESB" /> Before the invention of the [[hyperdrive]], some early [[spacer]]s would use carbonite to endure long voyages. That technique, however, had brutal side effects, collectively referred to as [[hibernation sickness]].<ref name="beware">''[[Return of the Jedi: Beware the Power of the Dark Side!]]''</ref> Droids such as the [[DLC-13 mining droid]] could be made out of heat-resistant carbonite.<ref name="Absolutely Everything You Need to Know">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref>

==History==
===Ancient history===
[[File:Uprising_UI_Prop_Crystal_Balanced_07.png|thumb|left|300px|Crystallized carbonite]]
The discovery<ref name="BMF 36">{{BuildFalconCite|36|Guide to the Galaxy}}</ref> of the peculiar stasis qualities of carbonite<ref name="Scum and Villainy" /> by the [[scientist]]s from the [[planet]] [[Empress Teta|Koros Major]] saw the end of [[generation ship]]s and development of [[sleeper ship]]s. Ushering a new age of exploration<ref name="BMF 36" /> that expanded the pre-[[Old Republic-era|Republic]] borders, carbonite freezing was employed aboard ancient sleeper ships to keep crews alive in extended periods of hibernation as they traversed interstellar space.<ref name="Scum and Villainy" />

Over time the use of carbon freezing dropped off as the advent of [[hyperdrive]]s eliminated the need for such extended voyages, and [[bio-entropic field generators]] became standard for medical stasis applications. Only industrial applications remained in common usage, typically for freezing [[coaxium]], [[tibanna]], and other volatile cargoes for transport. However, with some modification industrial grade freezing chambers could be modified into a crude and indeed painful means of bio-stasis sometimes described as "a big wide-awake nothing." At various times the likes of [[Galactic Empire|Imperials]], [[bounty hunter]]s, and underworld gangsters were known to use this cruel but effective method. The earliest known use of carbon freezing living victims to be displayed as trophies dated back thousands of [[Standard year|years]] to the ancient [[Krath]] and their conquest of the carbonite mines of the [[Empress Teta system]].<ref name="Scum and Villainy">''[[Star Wars: Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious]]''</ref>

According to legend, [[Darth Siberus]] froze herself in carbonite and spread the false story of a [[Unidentified Princess (Siberus)|wealthy princess]] to lure [[Treasure hunting|treasure hunters]] to her [[Darth Siberus' Tomb|tomb]], where they hoped to find the riches of the princess's empire. Instead, they would find the slumbering Siberus, who would be awoken from her carbonite "imprisonment" and sap the life from those who discovered her. With that, Siberus hoped to live forever and retain her beauty. On one occasion, so said the legend, the [[archaeologist]] [[Loreth (archaeologist)|Loreth]] discovered the tomb and become increasingly drawn towards Siberus's carbonite slab. Coming to believe it was the princess upon finding herself touching its controls, Loreth instead became a victim of Siberus and was hit by a wave of lightning from the awoken [[Sith Lord]].<ref name="Sleep of Ages">{{StoryCite|story=The Sleep of Ages|book=Dark Legends}}</ref>

===Late Republic Era===
{{Quote|Are, uh, we sure this thing is safe? I don't wanna end up a wall decoration.|[[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC trooper]] [[Fives]], before the Battle of Lola Sayu|<ref name="TC">{{TCW|The Citadel (episode)}}</ref>}}
[[File:CarboniteClones-TC.png|thumb|right|300px|Clone troopers frozen in carbonite for the mission to the Citadel]]
During the [[Clone Wars]], [[Obi-Wan Kenobi]], [[Anakin Skywalker]], [[Ahsoka Tano]], and the [[clone trooper]]s under their command embarked on an [[Battle of Lola Sayu|extraction mission]] to the [[Citadel]] on [[Lola Sayu]]. They used a large facility housed in the [[Imperial Palace|Jedi Temple]] to freeze themselves in carbonite, as a means to smuggle their team past the prison facility's life-form scanners.<ref name="TC" />

===Imperial Era===
{{Quote|Does It hurt?"<br />"It's pure agony. Imagine every one of your cells being frozen in stasis. I'd rather be shot."<br />"At least you'd still be alive."<br />"If you ever get unfrozen, and that's a big IF. Not everyone survives the process. Heard it can paralyze you. Some people go blind, and those are the lucky ones."<br />"So, what did he do? Must've been pretty bad, if Jabba turned him into wall décor."<br />"He was a smuggler. Even did some work for Jabba. Was too busy playing rebel and never paid Jabba the debt he owed.|[[Kay Vess]] and Hutt Enforcer, translating the Enforcer's Huttese dialogue, on Han Solo|<ref name="Outlaws">''[[Star Wars Outlaws]]''</ref>}}
During the [[Galactic Civil War]], Skywalker, now [[Darth]] Vader, intended to trap his son [[Luke Skywalker|Luke]] in carbonite on [[Bespin]]. Having blackmailed [[Baron Administrator]] [[Landonis Balthazar Calrissian|Lando Calrissian]] into letting him use [[Cloud City]]'s crude industrial carbon-freezing facility, he tested the device out on [[Han Solo]] to ensure the chamber would be safe. Once Solo was confirmed to be in perfect hibernation, Solo's carbonite block<ref name="Episode V">{{Film|V}}</ref>&mdash;which in time would be dubbed the "[[Hansicle]]"<ref name="Star Wars 50">{{WebCite|url=https://www.gamesradar.com/comics/marvel-comics/star-wars-50-series-finale-preview|text=Star Wars #50 ends the current era of Marvel's Star Wars comics with a confrontation between Luke Skywalker and Ben Solo|archivedate=20240905221125}}</ref>&mdash;was turned over to [[Boba Fett]] for transport to [[Boba Fett's Palace|Jabba's Palace]], where [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure|Jabba]] would display it as a trophy in his main audience chamber.<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref> When Solo later recalled being frozen in carbonite, he noted that the creeping silence and darkness still haunted him, and it was a claustrophobic feeling of being utterly trapped in his own body.<ref name="The Princess and the Scoundrel">''[[The Princess and the Scoundrel]]''</ref>

[[File:QiraHanSolo-WOTBH1.png|thumb|left|250px|Qi'ra and Solo, while the latter was in carbonite]]
A few days after Solo's capture, [[Leia Skywalker Organa Solo|Leia Organa]] along with several inhabitants of Cloud City, was also frozen in carbonite, having returned to Bespin and been subsequently apprehended by the Empire,<ref name="SW 3">[[Star Wars (2020) 3|''Star Wars'' (2020) 3]]</ref> Leia and the prisoners were quickly rescued by Skywalker and Calrissian, who were able to free them before the Empire could transport them off-world.<ref name="SW 4">[[Star Wars (2020) 4|''Star Wars'' (2020) 4]]</ref> Although [[Lady]] [[Qi'ra]] had [[Crimson Dawn]] steal Solo to use the frozen smuggler as bait for a trap,<ref name="WotBH 1">[[War of the Bounty Hunters 1|''War of the Bounty Hunters'' 1]]</ref> Fett came to regain possession of Solo,<ref name="Boba Bio Gallery 13">{{Databank|boba-fett-biography-gallery|Boba Fett Biography Gallery}} {{C|slide 13}}</ref> giving the carbonite block to [[crime lord]] [[Jabba Desilijic Tiure]]. Eventually, Solo was freed by Organa as part of a [[Rescue of Han Solo|rescue effort]].<ref name="Episode VI">{{Film|VI}}</ref>

===New Republic Era===
[[File:Mythrol_Frozen_The_Mandalorian.png|thumb|right|275px|A Mythrol being frozen in carbonite]]
Around [[9 ABY]],<ref name="9 ABY Note">The events of ''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian]]'' take place in or around 9 ABY, per the reasoning [[Template:MandoDate|here]].</ref> the [[bounty hunter]] known as [[Din Djarin]] used a small carbon freezing unit installed aboard his [[Razor Crest|gunship]] to transport several prisoners at a time, including a [[The Fledgling Mythrol|Mythrol]].<ref name="mando1">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 1: The Mandalorian}}</ref> After being freed and entering the service of [[Magistrate]] [[Greef Karga]] to pay off his debts, the Mythrol had issues seeing out of his left eye due to his time in carbonite. Upon reuniting with Djarin, the Mythrol expressed no interest in running off so as to avoid being frozen by Djarin once more.<ref name="Chapter 12">{{TheMandalorian|Chapter 12: The Siege}}</ref>
