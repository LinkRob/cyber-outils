The '''Dalnan military''' was the armed forces of the [[Dalnan government]], and was active during the [[High Republic Era]]. Young [[Dalnan]]s underwent [[Metamorphosis Trials]] to test skills that were required in their pursued field. For those joining the military, Dalnans would focus on hand-to-hand combat. The Dalnan military used [[medic]]s, and was located on a [[military compound]] on [[Dalna]].<ref name="A Test of Courage" />
