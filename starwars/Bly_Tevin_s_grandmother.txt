{{Quote|M-my gran used to say when ''she'' was small, they measured time not by the moon, but by when Eiram's ships flew over the city. I&mdash;I think she was exaggerating but it was ''ages'' ago.|Bly Tevin, to Thylefire Squadron|<ref name="Convergence" />}}
The '''grandmother''' of the [[pilot]]s [[Bly Tevin]] and [[Lina]] was an [[E'roni]] [[Gender|woman]] who [[Homeworld|hailed]] from the [[Outer Rim Territories|Outer Rim]] [[planet]] [[E'ronoh]]. At some point when she was [[Youngling|young]], she watched [[Starship|ships]] from the Outer Rim planet [[Eiram]] fly over [[the Rook]], E'ronoh's [[capital]] [[city]]. She later told Tevin that she and others used the vessels' flyovers to keep track of [[time]] instead of using their [[Eiram system|system's]] [[Eirie|Timekeeper Moon]]. While flying on an escort [[Skirmish in the Eiram system|mission]] with [[Thylefire Squadron]] of [[E'ronoh's fleet]]<ref name="Convergence" /> in [[382 BBY]],<ref name="Date">''[[Star Wars: The High Republic Character Encyclopedia]]'' dates the events of ''[[The High Republic: Convergence]]'' to [[382 BBY]].</ref> Tevin relayed his [[Bly Tevin's family|grandmother's]] tale to his colleagues but noted that she might have exaggerated her story.<ref name="Convergence" />

==Behind the scenes==
Bly Tevin's grandmother was mentioned in [[The High Republic: Convergence|''Convergence'']], a novel written by [[Zoraida Córdova]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's Phase II<ref name="Convergence" /> that was published on [[November 22]], [[2022]]. Prior to its release, her mention was included in a [[StarWars.com]] preview for the novel on [[October 4]] of that year.<ref name="SW.com">{{SW|url=news/the-high-republic-convergence-excerpt|text=A Mysterious Mission Begins in ''The High Republic: Convergence'' &ndash; Exclusive Excerpt}}</ref>
