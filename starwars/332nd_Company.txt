The '''332nd Company''', with its members informally known as '''Ahsoka's Clone Troopers''' or '''Ahsoka Loyalist Troopers''', was a [[company]] within the [[332nd Division]] of the [[501st Legion]] of the [[Galactic Republic]], and was characterized by the distinctive [[helmet]] design of its [[Clone trooper|troopers]]. It was active during the late [[Clone Wars]], forming in response to a proposed [[Siege of Mandalore|invasion]] of [[Mandalore]].

When [[Bo-Katan Kryze]] and former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] [[Ahsoka Tano]], requested the help of [[Republic Military|Republic forces]] to retake Mandalore from former [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]]'s [[Shadow Collective]], Tano's friend, [[Clone Commander]] [[Rex]] formed the new company out a sense of respect and loyalty. Since Tano no longer held an official position in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] after leaving the [[Jedi Order]], Rex was formally placed in command with Tano serving as a civilian advisor. Despite this, all of the clones, Rex included, deferred to her and painted their helmets in imitation of her facial markings.

The company reinforced the Republic [[troop]]s in the invasion, successfully defeating [[Maul]] and the [[Shadow Collective]]. However, on the way back to [[Coruscant]] onboard the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]] ''[[Tribunal]]'', [[Order 66]] was enacted and the 332nd Company [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|attacked]] Ahsoka Tano and Maul, held [[prisoner]]. They were unsuccessful in [[Death|killing]] either [[Force-sensitive|Force-user]], and all of the [[trooper]]s except Rex were killed when the ship crashed on a [[Unidentified moon|moon]].

==Description==
{{Quote|Loyalty means everything to the clones.|Anakin Skywalker, to Ahsoka Tano|audio=LoyaltyMeansEverything-OFNF.flac|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
The 332nd [[Company]] was an [[infantry]] company of [[clone trooper]]s<ref name="SWCT" /> within the [[501st Legion]] of the [[Grand Army of the Republic]]. It was [[Commanding officer|led]] by [[Clone Captain]] [[Rex]], who was [[Military rank|promoted]] to [[Clone Commander]] in order to lead the newly established<ref name="Old Friends" /> [[332nd Division]],<ref name="SW Book">''[[The Star Wars Book]]''</ref> which was being created for the [[Siege of Mandalore]]. The 332nd Company was part of the division<ref name="Old Friends" /> and shared its numerical designation of 332.<ref name="SW Book" /> As a company of the Republic, it consisted of four [[platoon]]s,<ref name="CL">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']]</ref> including a [[Unidentified platoon (332nd Company)|platoon]] that was present on Ahsoka's return.<ref name="Old Friends" />

[[File:332ndCustomPaintjob-OFNF.png|thumb|left|300px|The troopers' helmets were customized to resemble Ahsoka Tano's face.]]
The [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s of the company were equipped with [[Phase II clone trooper armor]], with the standard [[Color|blue]] markings of the 501st.<ref name="Old Friends" /> Their [[helmet]]s, however, were painted red-orange in a stylized version of [[Ahsoka Tano]]'s [[Tattoo|facial markings]] and skin tone to represent their friendship with Tano,<ref name="SWCT" /> a heroic<ref name="Insider 223">{{InsiderCite|223|Launchpad}}</ref> [[Force-Sensitive Outcast|former]] [[Jedi Commander]], and as such they were informally referred to as "Ahsoka's Clone Troopers"<ref name="SWCT" /> or "Ahsoka Loyalist Troopers."<ref name="Hasbro">{{HasbroCite|set=Star Wars: The Vintage Collection|packtype=4-Pack|pack=Phase II Clone Trooper|link=products/star-wars-the-vintage-collection-phase-ii-clone-trooper-2}}</ref>

Throughout the missions and skirmishes that the 332nd Company participated in, they employed the use of infantry weapons, jetpacks,<ref name="Old Friends" /> and certain military vehicles, mainly [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/infantry|LAAT/i gunships]], which served as their primary method of transportation,<ref name="Old Friends" /><ref name="Phantom Apprentice">{{TCW|The Phantom Apprentice}}</ref> and the [[All Terrain Tactical Enforcer|AT-TE walkers]], which were present with the company at [[time]]s.<ref name="Phantom Apprentice" /><ref name="Shattered" /> The company was stationed on the [[Venator-class Star Destroyer|''Venator''-class Star Destroyer]]<ref name="Shattered" /> ''[[Tribunal]]''.<ref name="TCW Character Encyclopedia">''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle!]]''</ref>

==History==
===Return of Ahsoka Tano===
{{Quote|As soon as Rex and the guys knew you were back they got to work.''"<br />"''The paint job's a little crude but we think it gets the idea across. Glad to have you back, Commander.|audio=WelcomeBackCmdr-OFNF.flac|Anakin Skywalker and Clone Captain Rex to Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
[[File:TCW_Tano_unit.png|thumb|right|270px|The company stands at attention.]]
After the former [[Jedi]] [[Padawan]] Ahsoka Tano's [[Trial of Ahsoka Tano|departure]] from the [[Jedi Order]], she later returned to serve the Galactic Republic in [[19 BBY]].<ref name="19 BBY">Since ''[[Star Wars: Galactic Atlas]]'' dates both Tano's departure and the [[Great Jedi Purge]] to [[19 BBY]], Tano's return and the Siege of Mandalore must have also taken place in that [[Standard year|year]].</ref> Tano, along with members of the [[Mandalore resistance]], had arrived to propose a joint operation in order to oust the [[Shadow Collective]] and renegade [[Sith Lord]] [[Maul]] from [[Mandalore]]. After the meeting, Tano met with her former master, [[Jedi Knight]] [[Anakin Skywalker]].<ref name="Old Friends" />

Skywalker led her to a hangar, where [[Captain]] Rex stood with the company and called them to attention. Tano walked among them in surprise, noting that their helmets were painted in a stylized version of her face. Skywalker told her that as soon as they knew she was returning, they had customized their helmets. Captain Rex continued that, although the paint job was crude, it was effective, and they were glad she had returned.<ref name="Old Friends" />

Since Tano was no longer a Jedi Commander within the Grand [[Army]] of the Republic, and therefore wasn't officially permitted to lead a unit of [[Republic Military|Republic forces]], Captain Rex was promoted from Captain to [[Commander]] so that he could lead the new division,<ref name="Old Friends" /> which was created with the same designation as the company,<ref name="SW Book" /> and take Tano as an [[advisor]].<ref name="Old Friends" />

===Siege of Mandalore===
====Initial assault====
{{Quote|There are a small number of firefights still happening in sector 11. But for the most part, your clones have been as effective as promised.|Bo-Katan Kryze, on the clones' effectiveness|audio=EffectiveAsPromised-TPA.flac|<ref name="Phantom Apprentice" />}}
[[File:MandaloreSiegeLanding-OFNF.png|thumb|left|270px|The Republic-Mandalorian joint attack force approaches the planet's surface.]]
Preparing for the siege, three ''Venator''-class [[Star Destroyer]]s arrived [[Atmosphere|above]] Mandalore and deployed a large contingent of [[Low Altitude Assault Transport/carrier|LAAT carriers]] and LAAT/i [[gunship]]s containing infantry forces, including the 332nd Company. The Republic [[Troop transport|ships]] were escorted by a force of [[Kom'rk-class fighter/transport|''Kom'rk''-class fighter/transports]].<ref name="Old Friends" />

Mandalorian [[Prime Minister]] [[Almec]], a puppet of Maul, prepared his forces of [[Mandalorian super commando]]s and readied [[Sundari]]'s defenses. As the Republic-Mandalorian attack force neared the [[capital]] [[city]], several gunships and [[transport]]s were downed by incoming [[missile]]s. Shadow Collective commandos used their jetpacks to intercept the gunships in the sky, and troopers along with Mandalore resistance warriors flew out to repel them. The surviving [[Starship|ships]] touched down at Sundari, deploying the infantry [[troop]]s, and a battle ensued.<ref name="Old Friends" />

====Pursuit in the Undercity====
{{Quote|What's down there?''"<br />"''A system of tunnels. Sewage ports, mainly.|Ahsoka and Vaughn as they enter the Undercity|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
[[File:Vaughn-332nd-Sideshow.png|thumb|right|270px|Captain Vaughn leads 332nd Company troops in the siege.]]
The Shadow Collective commandos were pushed back by the invaders, and they retreated into the [[Undercity]]&mdash;with a squad of 332nd Company troopers in pursuit. Ahsoka Tano and Captain [[Vaughn]] led the group through the tunnels, but having lost track of the Shadow Collective, they were left vulnerable trying to find their bearings. Mandalorian super commando [[Rook Kast]] then revealed herself and fired a missile at them, which knocked Tano to the ground. She called after the clones to wait as they ran past her, but Vaughn didn't hear her warning. By the time she reached the clones, they had been ambushed and were all [[Death|killed]], including Vaughn.<ref name="Old Friends" />

The Mandalorian super commandos then emerged from the tunnels and surrounded Tano, along with Maul.<ref name="Old Friends" /> He was surprised that she had come instead of Kenobi or Skywalker. While he was talking, Tano covertly transmitted a beacon from her [[Wrist comm|wristcomm]] to alert Commander Rex of her location. He led a squad of 332nd Company troopers into the Undercity to rescue her, following the signal. Rex and his men rescued Tano, but Maul and his followers escaped.<ref name="Phantom Apprentice" />

[[File:Siege_of_Mandalore_rages.png|thumb|left|270px|The aftermath of the siege]]
Shortly after, a detachment of the 332nd Company was sent to reinforce [[C-deck]]. They were ambushed and massacred by the Shadow Collective. Maul took [[Advanced Recon Commando|ARC Trooper]] [[Jesse]] hostage in order to torture him for information. A while later, after a pivotal [[Lightsaber duel|duel]] between Tano and Maul,<ref name="Phantom Apprentice" /> the Shadow Collective surrendered and the siege ended with a Republic victory.<ref name="Shattered">{{TCW|Shattered}}</ref>

===Order 66===
====Returning to Coruscant====
{{Quote|You're a good soldier, Rex. So is every one of those men down there. They may be willing to die, but I am not the one who is going to kill them.|Ahsoka tells Rex she is not willing to kill to escape|audio=GoodSoldierRex-VaD.flac|<ref name="Victory and Death">{{TCW|Victory and Death}}</ref>}}
Following the battle, with Maul in custody inside a [[Mandalorian vault]], Republic forces returned to the ''Tribunal'' to transport Maul to [[Coruscant]]&mdash;although on the way, [[Order 66]] was enacted. All of the clone troopers, including the 332nd Company, suddenly [[Skirmish aboard the Tribunal|became hostile]] towards Tano. They were determined to kill her and Maul.<ref name="Shattered" />

[[File:Escape_from_Order_66.png|thumb|right|270px|Forced to fight their friends, Rex and Tano made their way to the hangar.]]
When Order 66 was given, Rex opened fire on Tano. She deflected the shots with her lightsabers, and narrowly escaped from the room while under fire from multiple troopers of the 332nd Company. Rex ordered the troopers to search the ship for her. The 332nd Company split up into squads and hunted the ship. During the search, Ahsoka abducted Rex with the help of onboard astromech droids, knocking the commander out and taking him to the medical bay. She held off hostile clone troopers as Rex's inhibitor chip was surgically removed.<ref name="Shattered" /> After the removal of his chip, Rex was no longer affected by Order 66. He and Tano worked their way to the hangar, fending off troopers of the 332nd along the way.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

Meanwhile, Maul, who had been released by Tano as a diversion, fought his way to the [[hyperdrive]] room, killing many 332nd Company troopers, and collapsed the hyperdrive mid-jump, causing the ''Venator''-class Star Destroyer to fall out of [[hyperspace]] in pieces. He then walked over to the hangar, and was confronted by more clone troopers. He used the Force to pick up metal siding to impale or decapitate the clones. A group of 332nd Company troopers, including [[Ridge]], attempted to intercept Maul as he moved towards the hangar, but were unsuccessful. Ridge was knocked down and another clone was impaled by a piece of the wall.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

====Faceoff in the hangar====
{{Quote|Commander Rex, you're in violation of Order 66. I accuse you of treason against the Grand Army of the Republic. You'll be demoted in rank from Commander and subject to execution along with the traitor, Ahsoka Tano.|Jesse threatens Commander Rex |audio=JesseDemotesRex-VaD.flac|<ref name="Victory and Death" />}}
[[File:AhsokaRexFeint-VaD.png|thumb|left|270px|Rex pretends to have captured Ahsoka in order to buy time for their plan.]]
When Ahsoka and Rex reached the main hangar, a formation of the remaining clone troopers was waiting for them, led by the ARC Trooper Jesse. The formation included the remaining troops of the 332nd Company as well as other 501st units. Rex and Ahsoka came up with a plan, in which Rex brought Ahsoka down to the clone troopers at gunpoint, and claimed that she was no longer a Jedi so they had no reason to kill her. Jesse and Rex argued about the matter for a while, and then Jesse accused Rex of treason.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

However, the debate was a diversion to buy time, and at that moment Tano's group of astromechs dropped the hangar lifts which some of the troopers were standing on. This caught the clones by surprise, but they recovered and opened fire on Tano and Rex. The droids kept changing the elevation of the hangar lifts, to disorient them. By then, the vessel they were all on was about to crash into a [[Unidentified moon|nearby moon]]. Ahsoka and Rex escaped in a [[BTL-B Y-wing starfighter/bomber|Y-wing]], and Maul in a [[Nu-class attack/transport shuttle|''Nu''-class attack/transport shuttle]], but the clones on board were all killed when the ship crashed on the surface of the [[moon]]. Rex and Tano visited the crash site and [[Burial|buried]] the clone troopers they could find.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

===Legacy===
[[File:Reflection-VaD.png|thumb|270px|Darth Vader is reflected in the visor of a division trooper, years after the clones' death.]]
[[Standard year|Years]] later,<ref name="VaD episode guide trivia gallery">{{SW|url=victory-and-death-trivia-gallery|text=Tech's Notes: "Victory and Death" Trivia Gallery}}</ref> during the early reign of the [[Galactic Empire]]<ref name="Victory and Death Episode Gallery 20">{{SW|url=victory-and-death-episode-gallery|text="Victory and Death" Episode Gallery}} {{C|Slide 20}}</ref> around [[17 BBY]],<ref name="Timelines">''[[Star Wars: Timelines]]''</ref> Skywalker&mdash;who had fallen to the [[dark side of the Force]] and become the [[Dark Lord of the Sith]] [[Darth]] Vader&mdash;visited the site of the crash during an Imperial excavation. He observed the helmet of a 332nd Company trooper in the [[Water|snow]] and retrieved one of [[Ahsoka Tano's lightsabers]] which lay in front of the helmet. While he kept the lightsaber, he left the helmet abandoned in the snow.<ref name="Victory and Death" />

==Commanders and crew==
{{Quote|Commander Rex, this is CT-0292. I think we have something.''"<br />"''I'll be right there, Captain.|Vaughn, and Ahsoka Tano|<ref name="Old Friends" />}}
[[File:TCW_Tano_unit_2.png|thumb|left|300px|Captain-turned-Commander Rex was a leading member of the 332nd.]]
As part of the 501st Legion, the 332nd Company fell under the command of Jedi General Anakin Skywalker and Clone Captain Rex, who had both previously been joined in command by Jedi Commander Ahsoka Tano before she departed from the Jedi Order. When the 332nd became part of the newly formed 332nd Divison, Rex was named its Clone Commander. Aside from Rex, officers within the company included Captain Vaughn.<ref name="Old Friends" /> Clone Lieutenant Jesse also held command in the unit and overall division. Clone trooper Ridge was also assigned to the 332nd Company.<ref name="Shattered" /> As a company within the Grand Army of the Republic, the unit consisted of 144 clone troopers organized into four [[platoon]]s when at full strength.<ref name="CL 144">[[Star Wars: Complete Locations (2016)|''Star Wars: Complete Locations'']] establishes that a [[company]] in the [[Grand Army of the Republic]] consisted of 144 [[clone trooper]]s divided into four [[platoon]]s. Since this company is within the Grand [[Army]], it must also have 144 [[Cloning|clone]] [[trooper]]s that are divided into four platoons.</ref> While still respected by the clones of the 332nd and commonly addressed as commander, Ahsoka Tano's position in the company and overall 332nd Division was that of an advisor.<ref name="Old Friends" /> Still, she was permitted to attend new briefings and meetings.<ref name="Shattered" />

==Behind the scenes==
{{Quote|What happens is that he says that 'I'm not gonna leave you on this mission, I'm protective,' and these hangar doors open up and reveals all these clones standing there from half the 501st. And they're all now wearing orange helmets and orange striped helmets like her, and Rex is there and Anakin has given Rex his command unit to Ahsoka and attack Mandalore&hellip;|Dave Filoni in Celebration Europe on the clone troopers of the 332nd|<ref name="Ahsoka's Untold Tales">{{SWYouTube|lS4sclln5J4|Ahsoka's Untold Tales Panel &#124; Star Wars Celebration Europe 2016}}</ref>}}
[[File:332nd_Dave_Filoni_sketch.png|thumb|left|100px|A sketch by Dave Filoni.]]
The 332nd Company first appeared in the [[The Clone Wars: Season Seven|seventh season]] of ''[[Star Wars: The Clone Wars]]'', in the episode "[[Old Friends Not Forgotten]]."<ref name="Old Friends" /> When [[The Walt Disney Company]] cancelled ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'', a four-episode arc about the Siege of Mandalore was still in production.<ref name="Ahsoka's Untold Tales" /> The company was first identified as the 332nd Company on [[April 14]], [[2020]] in a [[StarWars.com]] blog post by Brendan Nystedt.<ref name="9 Helmets">{{SW|url=news/9-of-the-best-helmets-in-star-wars|text=9 of the Best Helmets in ''Star Wars''}}</ref>

The arc had production numbers 7.21, 7.22, 7.23, and 7.24, and was to depict Ahsoka Tano and this company of clone troopers attacking Mandalore. As part of the production process, [[Dave Filoni]] drew a sketch of the helmet worn by the company, and was shown in [[Celebration Europe (2016)|Celebration Europe]] in [[2016]]. The sketch was labelled as the "332nd."<ref name="Ahsoka's Untold Tales" /> A portion of the arc was adapted into the prologue of the 2016 [[canon]] novel [[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']].<ref name="Ahsoka novel">[[Ahsoka (novel)|''Ahsoka'']]</ref>

[[File:Star_Wars_The_Clone_Wars_revival_poster.png|thumb|right|150px|The helmet of the company as seen in the revival poster.]]
On [[July 19]], [[2018]], it was announced that ''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' was returning with twelve new episodes, which would include the Siege of Mandalore. The helmet of the company was featured on the poster for the revival season,<ref name="New Season">{{SW|url=news/sdcc-2018-the-clone-wars-to-return|text=''Star Wars: The Clone Wars'' to Return with New Episodes}}</ref> and finally appeared in completed animation in a trailer shown during [[Celebration Chicago]] in [[2019]].<ref name="Clone Wars Panel">{{SWYouTube|I0XlB2EJZuA|Star Wars: The Clone Wars Panel at Star Wars Celebration 2019}}</ref>
