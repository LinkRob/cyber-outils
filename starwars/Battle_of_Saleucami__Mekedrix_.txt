Between [[21 BBY]] and [[20 BBY]],<ref name="Date" /> following the [[Battle of Ryloth]] and before the [[Devaron massacre]], a '''battle''' was fought on<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> the [[planet]]<ref name="Battles">''[[Star Wars: Battles that Changed the Galaxy]]''</ref> [[Saleucami]]. The [[91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps]] and [[Commander]] [[Mekedrix]],<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> who served in the [[Galactic Republic]]-aligned [[Roon's military defense forces|defense forces]] of the astronomical object [[Roon]],<ref name="Obi-Wan 3">[[Obi-Wan 3|''Obi-Wan'' 3]]</ref> were deployed into the battle, which was waged during the [[Clone Wars]] between the Republic and [[Confederacy of Independent Systems]]. During the engagement, a [[clone trooper officer]] and two other [[clone trooper]]s rode into combat on [[BARC speeder]]s. As he had in previous deployments, Mekedrix fought with distinction and honor during the battle.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" />

==Behind the scenes==
The battle of Saleucami first appeared through flashback in the [[Obi-Wan 4|fourth issue]] of the [[Marvel Comics|Marvel]] [[Star Wars: Obi-Wan (comic series)|''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'']] [[Star Wars comic books|comic book]] mini-series. The issue was written by [[Christopher Cantwell]] and featured art by [[Madibek Musabekov]].<ref name="Obi-Wan 4" /> While the comic itself was released on [[August 31]], [[2022]], the flashback panel was included within the [[StarWars.com]] preview for the comic released on [[August 24]], 2022.<ref name="Obi-Wan 4 preview">{{SW|url=news/marvel-star-wars-obi-wan-4-exclusive-preview|text=Flashback to a Top-Secret Mission in Marvel's ''Star Wars: Obi-Wan'' #4 – Exclusive Preview}}</ref>
