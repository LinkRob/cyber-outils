A '''fish''' was a type of [[Water|aquatic]] [[Species|creature]] that could be found throughout [[the galaxy]]. Some types of fish included the [[burra fish]] of [[Dathomir]]<ref name="DD">''[[Dark Disciple]]''</ref> and the [[scalefish]] of [[Naboo]].<ref name="AEYNTK">''[[Star Wars: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know]]''</ref> Fish was also considered a staple of Naboo.<ref name="Queen's Peril">''[[Queen's Peril]]''</ref>

An [[Unidentified species (Grogu)|alien]] on [[Nal Hutta]] cooked several [[Unidentified Nal Hutta fish|native fish]], which he caught in a river, on a small, portable stove on his porch. When [[Grogu|Grogu's]] adaptive father, [[Din Djarin]], was rendered unconscious by the posion of a [[Dragonsnake]], Djarin's adoptive son, [[Grogu]], stole one of the cooked fish from the stove on the porch, and ate it in the nearby forest by the mound he had contructed to keep Djarin safe in.<ref name="TMAG">''[[Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu]]''</ref>
