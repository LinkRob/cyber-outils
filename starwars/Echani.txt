The '''Echani''' were a [[Sentience|sentient]] [[species]] that lived in [[the galaxy]].<ref name="Rising Storm" /> They shared their name with a form of [[Echani (martial art)|martial art]].<ref name="Perfect Weapon">"[[The Perfect Weapon]]"</ref> During the [[High Republic Era]], when the [[Nihil]] [[Valo Atrocity|attacked]] [[Valo]], an Echani man who had come to attend the [[Valo Republic Fair]] had a run-in with [[Supreme Chancellor]] [[Lina Soh]]'s pet [[targon]]s, nearly being mauled before [[Jedi Master]] [[Stellan Gios]] held the large [[cat]]s off so the man could flee.<ref name="Rising Storm" />

==Behind the scenes==
The Echani were first mentioned in the current ''[[Star Wars]]'' [[canon]] in the [[2021]] novel ''[[The High Republic: The Rising Storm]]'', written by [[Cavan Scott]] as part of the ''[[Star Wars: The High Republic]]'' multimedia project's<ref name="Rising Storm" /> [[Phase I: Light of the Jedi|Phase I]].<ref name="Phase I">{{HighRepublicReaderGuide|2020}}</ref> The [[Echani/Legends|Echani]] originated in the [[Star Wars Legends|''Star Wars'' Legends]] continuity, where they first appeared in the [[2003]] [[Star Wars video games|video game]] ''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''.<ref name="KOTOR">''[[Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic]]''</ref>
