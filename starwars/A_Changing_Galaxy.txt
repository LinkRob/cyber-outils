'''A Changing Galaxy''' is an article written by [[Morrie Mullins (real person)|Morrie Mullins]] as a tie in to the [[Living Force (roleplaying campaign)|Living Force]] campaign. It was published on the [[Wizards of the Coast]] website on [[July 25]] [[2002]] in the form of a report by [[Jedi/Legends|Jedi]] historian [[Oden Malksch]] summarizing the events of the first 19 months of the campaign.
